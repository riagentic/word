- 1 Sam 2:29 — Hebrew mixes plural (*tiv'atu*, *lehavri'akhem*) and singular (*vatekhabbed*); Czech mirrors „pohrdáte … ty ctíš … vykrmujete se“
- Ruth 2:10–Notes — EN Notes say Ruth is "twice noted … Moabitess (v2, 21) and foreigner (v10)"; Moabitess also in 2:6 („mladá Moabka“) — minor count issue (EN kept)
