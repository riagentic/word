- 1Sam 14 — ethnonym convention for batch: Philistines → „Philistinci" (adj. „philistinský"), Hebrews → „Hebrejové", Arameans/Syrians → „Aramejci", Israelites → „Israelci"; -ah place names (Gibeah, Ramah, Keilah) left indeclinable
- 1Sam 14:21 — EN note "Ephraimites return (v21)" but v21 speaks of Hebrews with the Philistines; Ephraim hill-country men are v22 (EN kept)
- 2Sam 6:5 — EN "all manner of songs made of fir wood" conflates MT (instruments of cypress/fir wood) with 1 Chr 13:8 (with all might and songs); rendered literally „písněmi z jedlového dřeva" (EN kept)
