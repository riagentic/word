# Ephesians 3

[1] For this cause I, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, the prisoner of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ on behalf of you, the nations —

[2] if indeed you heard of the stewardship of the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that was given to me for you,

[3] that according to revelation the mystery was made known to me, just as I wrote before in brief,

[4] in view of which you are able, when reading, to perceive my understanding in the mystery of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[5] which in other generations was not made known to the sons of men as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit:

[6] that the nations are fellow heirs and of the same body and fellow partakers of the promise in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ through the gospel,

[7] of which I became a servant according to the gift of the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that was given to me according to the working of his power —

[8] to me, the less than least of all saints, this grace was given — to preach to the nations the gospel of the unsearchable riches of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[9] and to enlighten all as to what is the stewardship of the mystery that has been hidden from the ages in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who created all things,

[10] that now to the principalities and the authorities in the heavenlies the manifold wisdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ might be made known through the church,

[11] according to the purpose of the ages which he made in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our Lord,

[12] in whom we have the boldness and access in confidence through the faith of him.

[13] Therefore I ask not to grow weary in my afflictions on behalf of you, which is your glory.

[14] For this cause I bend my knees toward the Father,

[15] from whom every family in the heavens and on earth is named,

[16] that he may give you, according to the riches of his glory, to be made mighty with power through his Spirit unto the inner man,

[17] that ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ may dwell through the faith in your hearts in love; having been rooted and founded,

[18] that you may have full strength to grasp with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth,

[19] and to know the love of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ that exceeds knowledge, that you may be filled unto all the fullness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] Now to him who is able to do beyond all things, superabundantly beyond what we ask or think, according to the power that is working in us,

[21] to him be the glory in the church and in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ unto all the generations of the age of the ages. Amen.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 7, 9, 10, 19) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"the prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles" → "the prisoner of the *Mashiach* *Yeshua* on behalf of you, the nations"** (v1) — Greek ὁ δέσμιος τοῦ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ, a plain genitive with the article (Phlm 1), not "a prisoner for"; ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν τῶν ἐθνῶν. The sentence breaks off (dash) and is taken up in v14 with the same "For this cause" (Τούτου χάριν).
- **"If ye have heard of the dispensation of the grace of God which is given me to you-ward" → "if indeed you heard of the stewardship of the grace of *Elohim* that was given to me for you"** (v2) — Greek εἴ γε ἠκούσατε; οἰκονομία as 1:10 and Col 1:25. "That was given" (τῆς δοθείσης) agrees with "grace", not with "stewardship".
- **"How that by revelation he made known unto me the mystery" → "that according to revelation the mystery was made known to me"** (v3) — Greek ὅτι κατὰ ἀποκάλυψιν ἐγνωρίσθη μοι, passive. "That" (ὅτι) is read by WH, Treg, NA28 and RP; SBLGNT (with the WH margin) omits it and begins a new clause. "In brief": ἐν ὀλίγῳ.
- **"Whereby, when ye read, ye may understand my knowledge" → "in view of which you are able, when reading, to perceive my understanding"** (v4) — Greek πρὸς ὃ δύνασθε ἀναγινώσκοντες νοῆσαι τὴν σύνεσίν μου; σύνεσις "understanding" as Col 1:9; 2:2; νοέω again in v20 ("think").
- **"by the Spirit" → "in the Spirit"** (v5) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι, without the article. "Has now been revealed": ἀπεκαλύφθη, aorist with "now".
- **"That the Gentiles should be fellowheirs, and of the same body, and partakers of his promise" → "that the nations are fellow heirs and of the same body and fellow partakers of the promise"** (v6) — Greek εἶναι τὰ ἔθνη συγκληρονόμα καὶ σύσσωμα καὶ συμμέτοχα, three words with συν-; "his" is not in the printed text. The Greek does not repeat "this mystery is".
- **"Whereof I was made a minister" → "of which I became a servant"** (v7) — Greek ἐγενήθην διάκονος, as Col 1:23, 25.
- **"by the effectual working of his power" → "according to the working of his power"** (v7) — Greek κατὰ τὴν ἐνέργειαν, as 1:19. The printed dashes mark v8a as an insertion.
- **"who am less than the least of all saints" → "the less than least of all saints"** (v8) — Greek τῷ ἐλαχιστοτέρῳ, a comparative built on a superlative; no "though I am".
- **"that I should preach among the Gentiles" → "to preach to the nations the gospel of"** (v8) — Greek τοῖς ἔθνεσιν εὐαγγελίσασθαι, as 2:17; the printed text has no "among". ἀνεξιχνίαστον, "not to be tracked out" (Rom 11:33).
- **"to make all men see what is the fellowship of the mystery" → "to enlighten all as to what is the stewardship of the mystery"** (v9) — Greek φωτίσαι πάντας, the verb of 1:18; οἰκονομία ("fellowship" is the later reading κοινωνία). Not "plan".
- **"which from the beginning of the world hath been hid in God, who created all things by Jesus Christ" → "that has been hidden from the ages in *Elohim*, who created all things"** (v9) — Greek ἀπὸ τῶν αἰώνων, as Col 1:26; "by Jesus Christ" is not in the critical text.
- **"unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places" → "to the principalities and the authorities in the heavenlies"** (v10) — Greek ἀρχαῖς, ἐξουσίαις, as 1:21; see 1:3.
- **"According to the eternal purpose which he purposed" → "according to the purpose of the ages which he made"** (v11) — Greek κατὰ πρόθεσιν τῶν αἰώνων ἣν ἐποίησεν: a genitive; ἐποίησεν is "made" (formed the purpose) or "carried out".
- **"boldness and access with confidence by the faith of him" → "the boldness and access in confidence through the faith of him"** (v12) — Greek διὰ τῆς πίστεως αὐτοῦ, a genitive: "faith in him" or "his faith / faithfulness" (as Phil 3:9). προσαγωγή as 2:18.
- **"Wherefore I desire that ye faint not at my tribulations for you" → "Therefore I ask not to grow weary in my afflictions on behalf of you"** (v13) — Greek αἰτοῦμαι μὴ ἐγκακεῖν: the Greek names neither the one asked nor the one who is not to grow weary ("I ask you not to …" or "I ask *Elohim* that I may not …"). ἐγκακέω as 2 Thess 3:13; θλίψεσιν "afflictions".
- **"I bow my knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ" → "I bend my knees toward the Father"** (v14) — Greek κάμπτω … πρὸς τὸν πατέρα; "of our Lord Jesus Christ" is not in the critical text.
- **"Of whom the whole family in heaven and earth is named" → "from whom every family in the heavens and on earth is named"** (v15) — Greek πᾶσα πατριά, without the article; πατριά (a group descended from one father) is formed from πατήρ, "father" (v14).
- **"to be strengthened with might by his Spirit in the inner man" → "to be made mighty with power through his Spirit unto the inner man"** (v16) — Greek δυνάμει κραταιωθῆναι: the verb is from κράτος "might" (1:19), the noun is δύναμις "power"; εἰς τὸν ἔσω ἄνθρωπον, "unto", not "in your inner being".
- **"That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith; that ye, being rooted and grounded in love" → "that the *Mashiach* may dwell through the faith in your hearts in love; having been rooted and founded,"** (v17) — the printed text puts the stop after "in love", so the words go with "dwell". Punctuation is the editors' work: the words can also be read "in love having been rooted and founded". The participles are nominative and stand loose in the sentence. τεθεμελιωμένοι as Col 1:23.
- **"May be able to comprehend" → "that you may have full strength to grasp"** (v18) — Greek ἐξισχύσητε, from ἰσχύς "strength" (1:19); καταλαβέσθαι, "lay hold of".
- **"which passeth knowledge, that ye might be filled with all the fulness of God" → "that exceeds knowledge, that you may be filled unto all the fullness of *Elohim*"** (v19) — Greek ὑπερβάλλουσαν (1:19); εἰς πᾶν τὸ πλήρωμα, "unto", not "with".
- **"exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think" → "beyond all things, superabundantly beyond what we ask or think"** (v20) — Greek ὑπὲρ πάντα ποιῆσαι ὑπερεκπερισσοῦ ὧν αἰτούμεθα ἢ νοοῦμεν.
- **"the power that worketh in us" → "the power that is working in us"** (v20) — Greek ἐνεργουμένην, as Col 1:29.
- **"Unto him be glory in the church by Christ Jesus throughout all ages, world without end" → "to him be the glory in the church and in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua* unto all the generations of the age of the ages"** (v21) — the printed text has "and in"; εἰς πάσας τὰς γενεὰς τοῦ αἰῶνος τῶν αἰώνων. "Be" is supplied.

### Notes
- **v2–13** — a digression: the sentence begun in v1 is taken up again in v14.
- **v6** — the three words (συγκληρονόμα, σύσσωμα, συμμέτοχα) all begin with συν-, "together with"; σύσσωμα, "of the same body", is not found before this letter.
