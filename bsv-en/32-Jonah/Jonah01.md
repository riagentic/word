# Jonah 1

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧ son of Amittai, saying,

[2] "Arise, go to ⟦nineveh|Ninveh|Nineveh⟧, the great city, and call out against it; for their evil has come up before me."

[3] And ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧ rose to flee to Tarshish from the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and he went down to ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ and found a ship going to Tarshish; and he gave its fare and went down into it, to go with them to Tarshish, from the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ hurled a great wind to the sea, and there was a great storm on the sea, and the ship thought it would be broken up.

[5] And the sailors feared, and they cried out, each man to his god; and they hurled the articles that were in the ship into the sea, to make it lighter for them. And ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧ had gone down into the far parts of the vessel, and he lay down and fell fast asleep.

[6] And the chief of the sailors came near to him and said to him, "What is it with you, sleeper? Arise, call to your god; perhaps the god will give a thought to us, and we shall not perish."

[7] And they said, each man to his neighbor, "Come, and let us cast lots, that we may know on whose account this evil is on us." And they cast lots, and the lot fell on ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧.

[8] And they said to him, "Tell us, please, on whose account this evil is on us. What is your work? And from where do you come? What is your land? And from which people are you?"

[9] And he said to them, "I am ⟦hebrew|an Ivri|a Hebrew⟧; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the heavens, I fear, who made the sea and the dry land."

[10] And the men feared a great fear, and they said to him, "What is this you have done?" For the men knew that from the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he was fleeing, because he had told them.

[11] And they said to him, "What shall we do to you, that the sea may be quiet for us?" For the sea was growing more and more stormy.

[12] And he said to them, "Lift me up and hurl me into the sea, and the sea will be quiet for you; for I know that on my account this great storm is on you."

[13] And the men rowed hard to get back to the dry land, and they could not; for the sea was growing more and more stormy against them.

[14] And they called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "Ah, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, please let us not perish for the soul of this man, and do not lay on us innocent blood; for you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, as it pleased you, you have done."

[15] And they lifted up ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧ and hurled him into the sea; and the sea stood still from its raging.

[16] And the men feared ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with a great fear, and they sacrificed a sacrifice to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and vowed vows.

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appointed a great fish to swallow ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧; and ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧ was in the inward parts of the fish three days and three nights.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **"Now the word of the LORD came unto Jonah" → "And the word of Yahweh came to Jonah"** (v1) — וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֶל … לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula ("saying" kept); the book opens with וַ.
- **"that great city … for their wickedness is come up before me" → "the great city … for their evil has come up before me"** (v2) — הָעִיר הַגְּדוֹלָה "the great city"; קָרָא עַל "call out against"; רָעָתָם "their evil".
- **"But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD … so he paid the fare thereof, and went down into it" → "And Jonah rose to flee to Tarshish from the face of Yahweh … and he gave its fare and went down into it"** (v3) — וַ "and" (each clause); מִלִּפְנֵי "from the face of" (twice; of fleeing, the מִפְּנֵי row); נָתַן שְׂכָרָהּ "gave its fare" (שָׂכָר "hire", the price of the passage); יָרַד "go down", three times in the story (v3, 5), kept.
- **"But the LORD sent out a great wind into the sea, and there was a mighty tempest in the sea, so that the ship was like to be broken" → "But Yahweh hurled a great wind to the sea, and there was a great storm on the sea, and the ship thought it would be broken up"** (v4) — the subject first marks the turn ("But"); הֵטִיל "hurl" (as v5, 12, 15); גָּדוֹל "great" both times; סַעַר "storm" (as v12); חִשְּׁבָה לְהִשָּׁבֵר "thought to be broken": the ship is said to think ("it would" supplied).
- **"Then the mariners were afraid, and cried every man unto his god, and cast forth the wares … to lighten it of them. But Jonah was gone down into the sides of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep" → "And the sailors feared, and they cried out, each man to his god; and they hurled the articles … to make it lighter for them. And Jonah had gone down into the far parts of the vessel, and he lay down and fell fast asleep"** (v5) — אִישׁ אֶל־אֱלֹהָיו "each man to his god" (the plural form, of a god of the nations); כֵּלִים "articles"; לְהָקֵל מֵעֲלֵיהֶם lit. "to make light from upon them" ("it" supplied); יַרְכְּתֵי "the far parts"; סְפִינָה "vessel" here only, beside אֳנִיָּה "ship"; וַיֵּרָדַם "fell fast asleep" (nifal of רָדַם, a deep sleep).
- **"So the shipmaster came to him, and said unto him, What meanest thou, O sleeper? arise, call upon thy God, if so be that God will think upon us, that we perish not" → "And the chief of the sailors came near to him and said to him, What is it with you, sleeper? Arise, call to your god; perhaps the god will give a thought to us, and we shall not perish"** (v6) — רַב הַחֹבֵל "the chief of the sailors" (lit. "of the rope-puller"); מַה־לְּךָ "what is it with you"; no "O"; הָאֱלֹהִים with the article in the captain's mouth is "the god" he has just named ("your god"); the narrator's הָאֱלֹהִים of 3:5–10 is the plain Elohim token; יִתְעַשֵּׁת, only here, "give a thought" (uncertain); וְלֹא נֹאבֵד "and we shall not perish".
- **"And they said every one to his fellow … for whose cause this evil is upon us" → "And they said, each man to his neighbor … on whose account this evil is on us"** (v7) — אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ "each man to his neighbor"; לְכוּ "come" (lit. "go"); הָרָעָה הַזֹּאת לָנוּ "this evil is to us"; רָעָה "evil" (of the harm that has come; as v8).
- **"Tell us, we pray thee, for whose cause this evil is upon us; What is thine occupation? and whence comest thou? what is thy country? and of what people art thou?" → "Tell us, please … What is your work? And from where do you come? What is your land? And from which people are you?"** (v8) — נָא "please"; מְלָאכָה "work"; אֶרֶץ "land"; אֵי־מִזֶּה עַם "from which people".
- **"I am an Hebrew; and I fear the LORD, the God of heaven, which hath made the sea and the dry land" → "I am a Hebrew; and Yahweh, the Elohim of the heavens, I fear, who made the sea and the dry land"** (v9) — the object stands first in the Hebrew ("and Yahweh … I fear"); שָׁמַיִם "heavens" (always plural).
- **"Then were the men exceedingly afraid, and said unto him, Why hast thou done this?" → "And the men feared a great fear, and they said to him, What is this you have done?"** (v10) — יָרְאוּ יִרְאָה גְדוֹלָה, the verb with its own noun ("feared a great fear", as v16); מַה־זֹּאת עָשִׂיתָ is a question (a reproach); כִּי־מִלִּפְנֵי יְהוָה הוּא בֹרֵחַ "that from the face of Yahweh he was fleeing".
- **"that the sea may be calm unto us? for the sea wrought, and was tempestuous" → "that the sea may be quiet for us? For the sea was growing more and more stormy"** (v11) — וְיִשְׁתֹּק הַיָּם מֵעָלֵינוּ lit. "be quiet from upon us"; הוֹלֵךְ וְסֹעֵר lit. "going and storming" (= growing ever more stormy; as v13).
- **"Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you" → "Lift me up and hurl me into the sea, and the sea will be quiet for you; for I know that on my account this great storm is on you"** (v12) — שָׂא "lift up" (as v15); בְשֶׁלִּי "on my account" (as בְּשֶׁלְּמִי v7).
- **"Nevertheless the men rowed hard to bring it to the land; but they could not" → "And the men rowed hard to get back to the dry land, and they could not"** (v13) — וַיַּחְתְּרוּ lit. "they dug" (of oars driven into the water: "rowed hard" is the sense; "dug" would say something else in English); לְהָשִׁיב "to bring back" (hifil, no object: "get back"); הַיַּבָּשָׁה "the dry land" (as v9; 2:10).
- **"We beseech thee, O LORD, we beseech thee, let us not perish for this man's life, and lay not upon us innocent blood: for thou, O LORD, hast done as it pleased thee" → "Ah, Yahweh, please let us not perish for the soul of this man, and do not lay on us innocent blood; for you, Yahweh, as it pleased you, you have done"** (v14) — אָנָּה "Ah" (a cry of entreaty, its row) and נָא "please": two words (KJV "we beseech thee" twice); no "O"; בְּנֶפֶשׁ "for the soul" (= at the cost of his life); כַּאֲשֶׁר חָפַצְתָּ עָשִׂיתָ in the Hebrew order.
- **"So they took up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased from her raging" → "And they lifted up Jonah and hurled him into the sea; and the sea stood still from its raging"** (v15) — וַיַּעֲמֹד "stood (still)"; זַעַף "raging".
- **"Then the men feared the LORD exceedingly, and offered a sacrifice unto the LORD, and made vows" → "And the men feared Yahweh with a great fear, and they sacrificed a sacrifice to Yahweh and vowed vows"** (v16) — each verb with its own noun (יִרְאָה, זֶבַח, נְדָרִים), as the Hebrew.
- **"Now the LORD had prepared a great fish … in the belly of the fish" → "And Yahweh appointed a great fish … in the inward parts of the fish"** (v17) — וַיְמַן "appointed" (מִנָּה piel; the same verb 4:6, 7, 8); דָּג גָּדוֹל "a great fish" (the kind is not named; not "whale", which is the Greek κῆτος of Matt 12:40, "sea creature"); מְעֵי "inward parts" (מֵעִים; בֶּטֶן "belly" is 2:2).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 1:17 is Heb 2:1.
- **"Three days and three nights"** (v17) — cited as the sign of Yeshua (Matt 12:40).
