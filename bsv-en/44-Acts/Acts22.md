# Acts 22

[1] "Men, brothers and fathers, hear my defense to you now."

[2] And having heard that he was addressing them in the Hebrew language, they offered the more quietness. And he says:

[3] "I am a man, a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, born in ⟦tarsus|Tarsos|Tarsus⟧ of ⟦cilicia|Kilikia|Cilicia⟧, but brought up in this city at the feet of ⟦gamaliel-pharisee|Gamliel|Gamaliel⟧, trained according to the accuracy of the ancestral ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, being a zealot for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, just as you all are today,

[4] who persecuted this Way unto death, binding and delivering up into prisons both men and women,

[5] as also the high priest testifies to me, and all the eldership; from whom also having accepted letters to the brothers, I was going to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, to lead also those who were there bound to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, that they might be punished.

[6] "And it came to pass to me, as I was going and drawing near to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, about midday, that suddenly out of heaven a considerable light flashed around me,

[7] and I fell to the ground and heard a voice saying to me, '**⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, why are you persecuting me?**'

[8] And I answered, 'Who are you, Lord?' And he said to me, 'I am ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧, whom you are persecuting.'

[9] And those who were with me beheld the light indeed, but they did not hear the voice of the one speaking to me.

[10] And I said, 'What shall I do, Lord?' And the Lord said to me, 'Having risen, go into ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and there it will be spoken to you about all the things which have been set in place for you to do.'

[11] And as I was not seeing, from the glory of that light, being led by the hand by those who were with me I came into ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧.

[12] "And a certain ⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧, a man reverent according to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, having testimony borne to him by all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ dwelling there,

[13] having come to me and stood by, said to me, '⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, brother, receive your sight.' And I in that very hour received my sight and looked at him.

[14] And he said, 'The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our fathers appointed you to know his will and to see the Righteous One and to hear a voice out of his mouth,

[15] because you will be a witness for him to all men of the things which you have seen and heard.

[16] And now, why do you delay? Having risen, be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ and wash away your sins, having called upon his name.'

[17] "And it came to pass to me, having returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and as I was praying in the temple, that I came to be in a trance

[18] and saw him saying to me, 'Hasten and go out quickly from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, because they will not accept your testimony about me.'

[19] And I said, 'Lord, they themselves know that I was imprisoning and beating, synagogue by synagogue, those who believe upon you;

[20] and when the blood of ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧ your witness was being poured out, I myself also was standing by and consenting and guarding the garments of those who were doing away with him.'

[21] And he said to me, 'Go, because I will send you out far away to nations.'"

[22] And they were listening to him up to this word, and they lifted up their voice, saying, "Take away such a one from the earth, for it was not proper that he live!"

[23] And as they were shouting and throwing the garments and casting dust into the air,

[24] the chiliarch commanded him to be brought into the barracks, saying that he be interrogated with scourges, so that he might come to know for what cause they were calling out against him thus.

[25] But when they stretched him forward for the thongs, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ said to the centurion standing by, "Is it permitted for you to scourge a man who is a Roman and uncondemned?"

[26] And having heard, the centurion, having approached the chiliarch, reported, saying, "What are you about to do? For this man is a Roman."

[27] And the chiliarch, having approached, said to him, "Tell me, are you a Roman?" And he said, "Yes."

[28] And the chiliarch answered, "I acquired this citizenship for a great sum." And ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ said, "But I have even been born one."

[29] Immediately therefore those who were about to interrogate him departed from him; and the chiliarch also was afraid, having come to know that he is a Roman and that he had bound him.

[30] And on the morrow, wishing to know the certainty, what he is accused of by the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, he loosed him and commanded the high priests and all the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧ to come together, and having brought ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ down he set him among them.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 14) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v8) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"Holy Ghost" / Spirit** — no explicit *Ruach HaQodesh* occurrence in this chapter.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v16) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering. βάπτισαι is a middle form: "have yourself immersed".
- **"the Way"** (v4) — Greek τὴν ὁδόν; Paul says he "persecuted this Way unto death"; he himself uses the term for the movement he once opposed.
- **"law of the fathers" → "the ancestral Torah"** (v3, also v12) — Greek τοῦ πατρῴου νόμου, an adjective, "ancestral"; no "our".
- **"Hebrew tongue" → "Hebrew language"** (v2) — Greek τῇ Ἑβραΐδι διαλέκτῳ, as 21:40: the Greek says Hebrew; many take it to be Aramaic.
- **Roman citizenship** (v25–29) — the Greek says "a Roman" (Ῥωμαῖος) each time; "citizen" is not in these verses; "citizenship" (πολιτεία) stands in v28.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
- **"Men, brethren, and fathers, hear ye my defence which I make now unto you" → "Men, brothers and fathers, hear my defense to you now"** (v1) — Greek Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοὶ καὶ πατέρες, as 7:2; μου τῆς πρὸς ὑμᾶς νυνὶ ἀπολογίας, with no verb "make".
- **"they kept the more silence: and he saith" → "they offered the more quietness. And he says"** (v2) — Greek μᾶλλον παρέσχον ἡσυχίαν (ἡσυχία "quietness"; "silence" is σιγή, 21:40); φησίν, a present.
- **"I am verily a man which am a Jew, born in Tarsus, a city in Cilicia" → "I am a man, a Jew, born in Tarsus of Cilicia"** (v3) — Greek ἀνὴρ Ἰουδαῖος; "verily" (μέν) is Byzantine; γεγεννημένος, the passive of "beget".
- **"brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, and taught according to the perfect manner of the law" → "brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, trained according to the accuracy of the ancestral Torah"** (v3) — Greek ἀνατεθραμμένος, as 7:20; πεπαιδευμένος, as 7:22; κατὰ ἀκρίβειαν, the noun of "accurately" (18:25). The printed comma stands after "Gamaliel"; with the comma after "city" the words run "brought up in this city, trained at the feet of Gamaliel according to …".
- **"was zealous toward God" → "being a zealot for God"** (v3) — Greek ζηλωτὴς ὑπάρχων τοῦ θεοῦ, as 21:20.
- **"delivering into prisons" → "delivering up into prisons"** (v4) — Greek παραδιδοὺς εἰς φυλακάς, plural. The sentence runs on from v3 ("who").
- **"doth bear me witness, and all the estate of the elders: from whom also I received letters … and went" → "testifies to me, and all the eldership; from whom also having accepted letters … I was going"** (v5) — Greek μαρτυρεῖ, a present, no "can"; πρεσβυτέριον, as 1 Tim 4:14; δεξάμενος; ἐπορευόμην, an imperfect. "For to be punished": ἵνα τιμωρηθῶσιν.
- **"about noon, suddenly there shone from heaven a great light round about me" → "about midday, that suddenly out of heaven a considerable light flashed around me"** (v6) — Greek περὶ μεσημβρίαν; περιαστράψαι, as 9:3; φῶς ἱκανόν. "It came to pass to me" is Ἐγένετο δέ μοι.
- **"unto the ground" kept as "to the ground"** (v7) — Greek εἰς τὸ ἔδαφος (in 9:4 ἐπὶ τὴν γῆν). "A voice" is genitive here (φωνῆς); in v9 it is accusative (τὴν φωνήν).
- **"Jesus of Nazareth" → "Yeshua the Nazorean"** (v8) — Greek Ἰησοῦς ὁ Ναζωραῖος, as 2:22.
- **"saw indeed the light, and were afraid; but they heard not the voice" → "beheld the light indeed, but they did not hear the voice"** (v9) — Greek ἐθεάσαντο; τὴν δὲ φωνὴν οὐκ ἤκουσαν: "hear", the verb of v7. In 9:7 the men are "hearing the voice (τῆς φωνῆς, genitive) but beholding no one"; the verse says what the Greek says and does not turn "hear" into "understand". "And were afraid" is Byzantine.
- **"Arise, and go … it shall be told thee of all things which are appointed for thee" → "Having risen, go … it will be spoken to you about all the things which have been set in place for you"** (v10) — Greek Ἀναστὰς πορεύου; λαληθήσεται; τέτακται, as 13:48.
- **"when I could not see for the glory of that light" → "as I was not seeing, from the glory of that light"** (v11) — Greek οὐκ ἐνέβλεπον ἀπὸ τῆς δόξης; δόξα is "glory", not "brightness".
- **"a devout man according to the law, having a good report of all the Jews which dwelt there" → "a man reverent according to the Torah, having testimony borne to him by all the Jews dwelling there"** (v12) — Greek εὐλαβής (WH, Treg, NA28), as 2:5; μαρτυρούμενος, as 10:22; "there" is supplied.
- **"stood, and said unto me, Brother Saul, receive thy sight. And the same hour I looked up upon him" → "stood by, said to me, 'Saul, brother, receive your sight.' And I in that very hour received my sight and looked at him"** (v13) — Greek ἐπιστάς; ἀνάβλεψον … ἀνέβλεψα εἰς αὐτόν: one verb, which means both "see again" and "look up"; with εἰς αὐτόν it is "I looked up at him", seeing again. The verse gives both; "and looked" stands for the one verb.
- **"hath chosen thee … and see that Just One, and shouldest hear the voice of his mouth" → "appointed you … and to see the Righteous One and to hear a voice out of his mouth"** (v14) — Greek προεχειρίσατο, as 3:20; τὸν δίκαιον, as 7:52; φωνὴν ἐκ τοῦ στόματος αὐτοῦ, without article.
- **"his witness unto all men" → "a witness for him to all men"** (v15) — Greek μάρτυς αὐτῷ, a dative.
- **"why tarriest thou? … calling on the name of the Lord" → "why do you delay? … having called upon his name"** (v16) — Greek τί μέλλεις, the verb "be about to" used alone; ἐπικαλεσάμενος, an aorist participle; τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"when I was come again to Jerusalem, even while I prayed in the temple, I was in a trance" → "having returned to Jerusalem and as I was praying in the temple, that I came to be in a trance"** (v17) — Greek ὑποστρέψαντι; γενέσθαι με ἐν ἐκστάσει, as 10:10.
- **"Make haste, and get thee quickly out" → "Hasten and go out quickly"; "receive" → "accept"** (v18) — Greek Σπεῦσον, as 20:16; ἐν τάχει, as 12:7; παραδέξονται, as 15:4.
- **"I imprisoned and beat in every synagogue them that believed on thee" → "I was imprisoning and beating, synagogue by synagogue, those who believe upon you"** (v19) — Greek ἤμην φυλακίζων καὶ δέρων; κατὰ τὰς συναγωγάς; τοὺς πιστεύοντας ἐπὶ σέ, a present participle.
- **"thy martyr Stephen was shed … consenting unto his death, and kept the raiment of them that slew him" → "Stephen your witness was being poured out … consenting and guarding the garments of those who were doing away with him"** (v20) — Greek μάρτυρος, "witness"; ἐξεχύννετο, an imperfect; συνευδοκῶν, as 8:1 ("unto his death" is Byzantine); φυλάσσων; ἀναιρούντων.
- **"Depart: for I will send thee far hence unto the Gentiles" → "Go, because I will send you out far away to nations"** (v21) — Greek Πορεύου; ἐξαποστελῶ; εἰς ἔθνη, without article.
- **"they gave him audience … lifted up their voices … Away with such a fellow from the earth: for it is not fit that he should live" → "they were listening to him … lifted up their voice … Take away such a one from the earth, for it was not proper that he live"** (v22) — Greek Ἤκουον; τὴν φωνήν, singular; Αἶρε, as 21:36; τὸν τοιοῦτον; καθῆκεν, an imperfect (WH, Treg, NA28), the verb of Rom 1:28: he ought not to have been left alive.
- **"cried out, and cast off their clothes, and threw dust" → "were shouting and throwing the garments and casting dust"** (v23) — Greek κραυγαζόντων (WH, Treg, NA28), as John 19:15; ῥιπτούντων τὰ ἱμάτια, "throwing" (off, or about); βαλλόντων.
- **"The chief captain commanded him to be brought into the castle, and bade that he should be examined by scourging; that he might know wherefore they cried so against him" → "the chiliarch commanded him to be brought into the barracks, saying that he be interrogated with scourges, so that he might come to know for what cause they were calling out against him thus"** (v24) — Greek χιλίαρχος; παρεμβολήν, as 21:34; μάστιξιν ἀνετάζεσθαι (not ἀνακρίνω "examine"); ἐπιγνῷ; διʼ ἣν αἰτίαν; ἐπεφώνουν, as 21:34.
- **"as they bound him with thongs" → "when they stretched him forward for the thongs"** (v25) — Greek προέτειναν αὐτὸν τοῖς ἱμᾶσιν (WH, Treg, NA28): "for the thongs" (the lashes of the scourge) or "with the thongs" (the straps that held him).
- **"Is it lawful … a man that is a Roman" → "Is it permitted … a man who is a Roman"** (v25–27, 29) — Greek ἔξεστιν; ἄνθρωπον Ῥωμαῖον, with no word "citizen"; μαστίζειν, of one root with μάστιξ.
- **"he went and told the chief captain, saying, Take heed what thou doest" → "having approached the chiliarch, reported, saying, 'What are you about to do?'"** (v26) — Greek προσελθὼν … ἀπήγγειλεν; Τί μέλλεις ποιεῖν (WH, Treg, NA28); "Take heed" is Byzantine.
- **"With a great sum obtained I this freedom … But I was free born" → "I acquired this citizenship for a great sum … But I have even been born one"** (v28) — Greek πολλοῦ κεφαλαίου; πολιτείαν, as Eph 2:12; ἐκτησάμην; Ἐγὼ δὲ καὶ γεγέννημαι, "but I have even been born"; "one" is supplied.
- **"they departed from him which should have examined him … after he knew that he was a Roman, and because he had bound him" → "those who were about to interrogate him departed from him … having come to know that he is a Roman and that he had bound him"** (v29) — Greek ἀπέστησαν, as 12:10; οἱ μέλλοντες … ἀνετάζειν; ἐπιγνούς; the name "Paul" is not in the Greek.
- **"because he would have known the certainty wherefore he was accused of the Jews, he loosed him from his bands, and commanded the chief priests and all their council to appear … and set him before them" → "wishing to know the certainty, what he is accused of by the Jews, he loosed him and commanded the high priests and all the Sanhedrin to come together … he set him among them"** (v30) — Greek βουλόμενος; τὸ ἀσφαλές, as 21:34; ἔλυσεν αὐτόν ("from the chains" is Byzantine); συνελθεῖν (WH, Treg, NA28); πᾶν τὸ συνέδριον, without "their"; ἔστησεν εἰς αὐτούς, "set him into their midst".
