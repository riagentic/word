# Acts 9

[1] But ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, still breathing threat and murder against the disciples of the Lord, having approached the high priest,

[2] asked from him letters to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, to the synagogues, so that if he found any who were of the Way, both men and women, he might lead them bound to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[3] Now as he was going, it came to pass that he drew near to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and suddenly a light out of heaven flashed around him,

[4] and falling to the ground he heard a voice saying to him, "**⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, why are you persecuting me?**"

[5] And he said, "Who are you, Lord?" And he: "I am ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, whom you are persecuting;

[6] but rise and enter into the city, and it will be told you what you must do."

[7] And the men who were journeying with him stood speechless, hearing indeed the voice but beholding no one.

[8] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ arose from the ground, and though his eyes were opened he was seeing nothing; and leading him by the hand they brought him into ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧.

[9] And he was three days not seeing, and he did not eat nor drink.

[10] Now there was a certain disciple in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, ⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧ by name, and the Lord said to him in a vision, "⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧." And he said, "Behold, I am here, Lord."

[11] And the Lord to him: "Rise and go to the street called Straight, and seek in the house of ⟦jude|Yehudah|Judas⟧ one of ⟦tarsus|Tarsos|Tarsus⟧, ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ by name; for behold, he is praying,

[12] and he has seen a man in a vision, ⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧ by name, coming in and laying his hands on him, so that he might receive his sight."

[13] But ⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧ answered, "Lord, I have heard from many about this man, how many bad things he did to your saints in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;

[14] and here he has authority from the high priests to bind all who call upon your name."

[15] But the Lord said to him, "Go, for this one is to me a vessel of election, to bear my name before both nations and kings, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[16] for I will show him how many things he must suffer for my name."

[17] And ⟦ananias|Chananyah|Ananias⟧ went away and entered into the house, and laying his hands upon him he said, "⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, brother, the Lord has sent me — ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, who appeared to you on the road by which you were coming — so that you may receive your sight and be filled with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧."

[18] And immediately there fell off from his eyes something like scales, and he received his sight, and rising he was ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧,

[19] and taking food he gained strength.

And he was with the disciples in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ some days,

[20] and immediately in the synagogues he was proclaiming ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, that this one is the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[21] And all who heard were amazed and were saying, "Is not this the one who ravaged in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ those who call upon this name, and had come here for this, that he might lead them bound to the high priests?"

[22] But ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was being empowered all the more and was confounding the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who dwelt in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, proving that this one is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[23] Now when a considerable number of days were being fulfilled, the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ took counsel together to do away with him;

[24] but their plot became known to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧. And they were also watching the gates both day and night, so that they might do away with him;

[25] but his disciples, taking him by night, let him down through the wall, lowering him in a large basket.

[26] And having arrived in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, he was trying to be joined to the disciples; and all were afraid of him, not believing that he was a disciple.

[27] But ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, laying hold of him, led him to the apostles and related to them how on the road he saw the Lord, and that he spoke to him, and how in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ he spoke boldly in the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[28] And he was with them, going in and going out at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, speaking boldly in the name of the Lord,

[29] and he was speaking and disputing with the Hellenists; but they were attempting to do away with him.

[30] But the brothers, having come to know it, brought him down to Caesarea and sent him out to ⟦tarsus|Tarsos|Tarsus⟧.

[31] So then the church throughout the whole of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ and ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ and ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧ had peace, being built up; and going in the fear of the Lord and in the encouragement of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, it was being multiplied.

[32] Now it came to pass that ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, going through all, came down also to the saints who dwelt at ⟦lydda|Lod|Lydda⟧.

[33] And he found there a certain man, ⟦aeneas|Aineas|Aeneas⟧ by name, lying on a pallet for eight years, who was paralyzed.

[34] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said to him, "⟦aeneas|Aineas|Aeneas⟧, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ heals you; rise and spread your bed for yourself." And immediately he rose.

[35] And all who dwelt at ⟦lydda|Lod|Lydda⟧ and the Sharon saw him, and they turned to the Lord.

[36] Now in ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ there was a certain woman disciple, ⟦tabitha|Tavita|Tabitha⟧ by name, which, being interpreted, is called ⟦dorcas|Dorkas|Dorcas⟧; she was full of good works and of alms which she was doing.

[37] And it came to pass in those days that she became sick and died; and having bathed her, they laid her in an upper room.

[38] Now since ⟦lydda|Lod|Lydda⟧ was near ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧, the disciples, having heard that ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was in it, sent two men to him, appealing, "Do not hesitate to come through to us."

[39] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ rose and went with them; and when he arrived, they led him up into the upper room, and all the widows stood beside him, weeping and showing tunics and garments, as many as ⟦dorcas|Dorkas|Dorcas⟧ was making while she was with them.

[40] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, casting them all outside and kneeling down, prayed; and turning to the body he said, "⟦tabitha|Tavita|Tabitha⟧, rise." And she opened her eyes, and seeing ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ she sat up.

[41] And giving her his hand he raised her up; and calling the saints and the widows, he presented her living.

[42] And it became known throughout the whole of ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧, and many believed upon the Lord.

[43] And it came to pass that he abode a considerable number of days in ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧ with a certain ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, a tanner.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v20) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v22, 34) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v17, 31) — consistent rendering. In v17 the Greek has no article (πλησθῇς πνεύματος ἁγίου); in v31 it has (τοῦ ἁγίου πνεύματος).
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v32–43) — Aramaic name used consistently; the Greek here has Πέτρος.
- **"the Way"** (v2) — Greek ἡ ὁδός *hē hodos* ("the Way"); this is the earliest name for the *Yeshua* movement in Acts (also 19:9, 23; 22:4; 24:14, 22). Retained as "the Way" — a significant identifier for the early community. "Any of this way" → "any who were of the Way": τῆς ὁδοῦ ὄντας.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v18) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"breathing out threatenings and slaughter" → "breathing threat and murder"** (v1) — Greek ἐμπνέων ἀπειλῆς καὶ φόνου, two singulars.
- **"desired of him letters to Damascus to the synagogues" → "asked from him letters to Damascus, to the synagogues"** (v2) — Greek ᾐτήσατο παρ' αὐτοῦ ἐπιστολὰς εἰς Δαμασκὸν πρὸς τὰς συναγωγάς. "Bring" → "lead": ἀγάγῃ (also v21).
- **"there shined round about him a light from heaven" → "a light out of heaven flashed around him"** (v3) — Greek περιήστραψεν, from the word for lightning; ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀπό).
- **"Saul, Saul"** (v4, 17) — Greek Σαοὺλ Σαούλ, the Hebrew form of the name; elsewhere in the chapter the Greek form Σαῦλος.
- **"And the Lord said, I am Jesus" → "And he: 'I am Yeshua'"** (v5) — Greek ὁ δέ· Ἐγώ εἰμι Ἰησοῦς, without a verb and without "the Lord" (WH Treg NA28; RP adds κύριος εἶπεν). The words "it is hard for thee to kick against the pricks. And he trembling and astonished said, Lord, what wilt thou have me to do? And the Lord said unto him" (KJV v5–6) are in none of the four editions nor in RP.
- **"Arise" → "rise"** (v6, 11, 34, 40) — Greek ἀνάστηθι, ἀναστάς (ἀνίστημι); "arose" in v8 is ἠγέρθη (ἐγείρω), literally "was raised".
- **"stood speechless, hearing a voice, but seeing no man" → "stood speechless, hearing indeed the voice but beholding no one"** (v7) — Greek ἀκούοντες μὲν τῆς φωνῆς, a genitive: "the voice" or "the sound"; θεωροῦντες.
- **"he saw no man" → "he was seeing nothing"** (v8) — Greek οὐδὲν ἔβλεπεν (WH Treg NA28; RP οὐδένα, "no one").
- **"Behold, I am here, Lord" kept** (v10) — Greek Ἰδοὺ ἐγώ, κύριε, "Behold, I", the Hebrew *hinneni*; "am here" is supplied for English.
- **"And the Lord said unto him" → "And the Lord to him"** (v11) — Greek ὁ δὲ κύριος πρὸς αὐτόν, without a verb. "Enquire … for one called Saul, of Tarsus" → "seek … one of Tarsus, Saul by name": ζήτησον … Σαῦλον ὀνόματι Ταρσέα, "a Tarsian".
- **"putting his hand on him, that he might receive his sight" → "laying his hands on him, so that he might receive his sight"** (v12) — the verse follows τὰς χεῖρας, "the hands" (WH NA28); Treg χεῖρας, "hands"; RP χεῖρα, "a hand". ἀναβλέψῃ: "look up", also "see again"; so in v17, 18.
- **"how much evil he hath done" → "how many bad things he did"** (v13) — Greek ὅσα κακὰ … ἐποίησεν. "I have heard": ἤκουσα (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀκήκοα).
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v14, 21) — Greek ἀρχιερέων, the plural of the word for the high priest (v1).
- **"he is a chosen vessel unto me" → "this one is to me a vessel of election"** (v15) — Greek σκεῦος ἐκλογῆς ἐστίν μοι οὗτος: a vessel of choosing, a chosen vessel. "Before the Gentiles, and kings, and the children of Israel" → "before both nations and kings, and the sons of Israel": ἐνώπιον ἐθνῶν τε καὶ βασιλέων υἱῶν τε Ἰσραήλ (Treg NA28 RP without article; WH τῶν ἐθνῶν).
- **"how great things he must suffer for my name's sake" → "how many things he must suffer for my name"** (v16) — Greek ὅσα … ὑπὲρ τοῦ ὀνόματός μου.
- **"Brother Saul, the Lord, even Jesus, that appeared unto thee" → "Saul, brother, the Lord has sent me — Yeshua, who appeared to you"** (v17) — Greek order kept: ὁ κύριος ἀπέσταλκέν με, Ἰησοῦς ὁ ὀφθείς σοι (WH Treg NA28; RP has no "Jesus").
- **"as it had been scales" → "something like scales"** (v18) — Greek ὡς λεπίδες. "Forthwith" is not in the critical text.
- **"when he had received meat, he was strengthened" → "taking food he gained strength"** (v19) — Greek ἐνίσχυσεν (Treg NA28 RP; WH ἐνισχύθη, "was strengthened"). "Then was Saul certain days" → "And he was … some days": the Greek does not name Saul (WH Treg NA28; RP adds ὁ Σαῦλος).
- **"he preached Christ in the synagogues, that he is the Son of God" → "in the synagogues he was proclaiming Yeshua, that this one is the Son of Elohim"** (v20) — Greek ἐκήρυσσεν τὸν Ἰησοῦν (WH Treg NA28; RP τὸν χριστόν) ὅτι οὗτός ἐστιν: reported speech, not a quotation.
- **"destroyed them which called on this name in Jerusalem" → "ravaged in Jerusalem those who call upon this name"** (v21) — Greek ὁ πορθήσας, as in Gal 1:13, 23; ἐν Ἰερουσαλήμ (WH Treg RP and the SBLGNT; NA28 εἰς). "Came hither" → "had come here": ἐληλύθει, a pluperfect.
- **"increased the more in strength" → "was being empowered all the more"** (v22) — Greek ἐνεδυναμοῦτο. "Proving that this is very Christ" → "proving that this one is the Mashiach": συμβιβάζων, literally "bringing together"; οὗτος, "this one": the Greek does not repeat the name.
- **"after that many days were fulfilled" → "when a considerable number of days were being fulfilled"** (v23) — Greek ἐπληροῦντο ἡμέραι ἱκαναί, "considerable days"; so in v43. "To kill him" → "to do away with him": ἀνελεῖν (v23, 24, 29).
- **"their laying await was known of Saul" → "their plot became known to Saul"** (v24) — Greek ἐγνώσθη … ἡ ἐπιβουλή. "They watched the gates" → "they were also watching the gates": παρετηροῦντο δὲ καί.
- **"Then the disciples took him" → "but his disciples, taking him"** (v25) — Greek οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ, "his disciples" (WH Treg NA28; RP αὐτὸν οἱ μαθηταί, "the disciples … him"). "By the wall in a basket" → "through the wall … in a large basket": διὰ τοῦ τείχους, ἐν σπυρίδι.
- **"he assayed to join himself" → "he was trying to be joined"** (v26) — Greek ἐπείραζεν κολλᾶσθαι; the verb is "tempt" elsewhere, here in its plain sense "try". The Greek does not name Saul here (RP adds ὁ Σαῦλος).
- **"took him, and brought him … and declared" → "laying hold of him, led him … and related"** (v27) — Greek ἐπιλαβόμενος, ἤγαγεν, διηγήσατο. The Greek does not say who related (Barnabas or Saul), nor who spoke to whom in "that he spoke to him". "Preached boldly" → "spoke boldly": ἐπαρρησιάσατο, from παρρησία "boldness"; the Greek has no "preached".
- **"coming in and going out at Jerusalem" → "going in and going out at Jerusalem"** (v28) — Greek εἰς Ἰερουσαλήμ, "into Jerusalem"; "and going out" is in WH Treg NA28, not in RP. "Of the Lord Jesus" → "of the Lord": WH Treg NA28.
- **"Grecians" → "Hellenists"** (v29) — Greek Ἑλληνιστάς, as in 6:1. "Went about to slay him" → "were attempting to do away with him": ἐπεχείρουν ἀνελεῖν.
- **"when the brethren knew" → "the brothers, having come to know it"** (v30) — Greek ἐπιγνόντες. "Sent him forth" → "sent him out": ἐξαπέστειλαν.
- **"Then had the churches rest … and were edified; and walking in the fear of the Lord, and in the comfort of the Holy Ghost, were multiplied" → "So then the church … had peace, being built up; and going in the fear of the Lord and in the encouragement of the Holy Spirit, it was being multiplied"** (v31) — Greek ἡ ἐκκλησία, a singular, with singular verbs (WH Treg NA28; RP has the plural throughout); εἰρήνην "peace"; οἰκοδομουμένη; πορευομένη, "going" (another verb than "walk", περιπατέω); παρακλήσει, "encouragement", also "exhortation".
- **"as Peter passed throughout all quarters" → "Peter, going through all"** (v32) — Greek διερχόμενον διὰ πάντων, "through all": all the places, or all of them.
- **"which had kept his bed eight years, and was sick of the palsy" → "lying on a pallet for eight years, who was paralyzed"** (v33) — Greek ἐξ ἐτῶν ὀκτώ, "for eight years", also possible "from the age of eight"; κραβάττου; παραλελυμένος.
- **"Jesus Christ maketh thee whole: arise, and make thy bed" → "Yeshua the Mashiach heals you; rise and spread your bed for yourself"** (v34) — Greek ἰᾶταί σε, a present; στρῶσον σεαυτῷ, "spread for yourself", without an object: "your bed" is supplied (the same verb is used of preparing a couch for a meal).
- **"all that dwelt at Lydda and Saron saw him, and turned to the Lord" → "all who dwelt at Lydda and the Sharon saw him, and they turned to the Lord"** (v35) — Greek τὸν Σαρῶνα with the article: the plain of Sharon; οἵτινες ἐπέστρεψαν.
- **"Lydda" / "*Lod*"** (v32, 35) — Hebrew לוֹד *Lod*; Greek Λύδδα *Lydda*; first occurrence notes Hebrew form.
- **"Joppa" / "*Yaffo*"** (v36, 38, 42, 43) — Hebrew יָפוֹ *Yafo* (modern Jaffa); first occurrence in this chapter gives Hebrew form alongside Joppa.
- **"Tabitha" / "*Tavitha*" / "*Dorkas*"** (v36) — Aramaic טְבִיתָא *Tavitha* and Greek Δορκάς *Dorkas* (both meaning "gazelle"); text preserves both forms.
- **"a certain disciple … which by interpretation is called Dorcas … almsdeeds which she did" → "a certain woman disciple … which, being interpreted, is called Dorcas … alms which she was doing"** (v36) — Greek μαθήτρια, the feminine of "disciple"; διερμηνευομένη; ἐλεημοσυνῶν ὧν ἐποίει, deeds of mercy, alms.
- **"whom when they had washed" → "having bathed her"** (v37) — Greek λούσαντες, washing of the whole body.
- **"desiring him that he would not delay to come to them" → "appealing, 'Do not hesitate to come through to us'"** (v38) — Greek παρακαλοῦντες· Μὴ ὀκνήσῃς διελθεῖν ἕως ἡμῶν, direct speech (WH Treg NA28; RP reported speech, "to them"). "Two men" is in WH Treg NA28, not in RP.
- **"coats and garments" → "tunics and garments"** (v39) — Greek χιτῶνας καὶ ἱμάτια; ὅσα ἐποίει, "as many as she was making".
- **"put them all forth" → "casting them all outside"** (v40) — Greek ἐκβαλὼν ἔξω πάντας, as in Mark 5:40. "Kneeled down": θεὶς τὰ γόνατα, as in 7:60.
- **"lifted her up" → "raised her up"** (v41) — Greek ἀνέστησεν αὐτήν, the transitive of "rise" (v40). "Presented her alive" → "living": ζῶσαν.
- **"believed in the Lord" → "believed upon the Lord"** (v42) — Greek ἐπὶ τὸν κύριον.
- **"he tarried many days" → "he abode a considerable number of days"** (v43) — Greek ἡμέρας ἱκανὰς μεῖναι.
- **"Sharon"** (v35) — Greek Σαρῶνα *Sarōna*; the Plain of Sharon; retained.
