# Acts 6

[1] Now in these days, as the disciples were multiplying, there arose a murmuring of the Hellenists against the ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧, because their widows were being overlooked in the daily service.

[2] And the twelve, calling the multitude of the disciples to themselves, said, "It is not pleasing that we, leaving the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, should serve tables.

[3] But look out, brothers, seven men from among you who have testimony borne to them, full of Spirit and wisdom, whom we will appoint over this need;

[4] but we will persevere in prayer and in the service of the word."

[5] And the word was pleasing before the whole multitude, and they chose ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧, a man full of faith and of ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧, and ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ and ⟦prochorus|Prochoros|Prochorus⟧ and ⟦nicanor|Nikanor|Nicanor⟧ and Timon and Parmenas and ⟦nicolaus|Nikolaos|Nicolaus⟧, a proselyte of ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧,

[6] whom they set before the apostles; and having prayed, they laid their hands on them.

[7] And the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was growing, and the number of the disciples in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ was multiplying greatly, and a great crowd of the priests were obeying the faith.

[8] And ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧, full of grace and power, was doing great wonders and signs among the people.

[9] But there rose up some of those from the synagogue called that of the Freedmen and ⟦cyrenians|Kyrenaioi|Cyrenians⟧ and ⟦alexandrians|Alexandreis|Alexandrians⟧, and of those from ⟦cilicia|Kilikia|Cilicia⟧ and Asia, disputing with ⟦stephen|Stephanos|Stephen⟧,

[10] and they had no strength to resist the wisdom and the Spirit with which he was speaking.

[11] Then they suborned men who said, "We have heard him speaking blasphemous words against ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[12] And they stirred up the people and the elders and the scribes, and coming upon him they seized him and led him to the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧,

[13] and they set up false witnesses who said, "This man does not cease speaking words against this holy place and the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧,

[14] for we have heard him saying that this ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧ will tear down this place and will change the customs which ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ delivered to us."

[15] And gazing at him, all those sitting in the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧ saw his face as the face of an angel.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 7, 11) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"when the number of the disciples was multiplied" → "as the disciples were multiplying"** (v1) — Greek πληθυνόντων τῶν μαθητῶν; the Greek has no "number" here. The same verb stands in v7 (ἐπληθύνετο ὁ ἀριθμός, "the number … was multiplying").
- **"murmuring" kept** (v1) — Greek γογγυσμός, a low muttering of discontent; "Grecians" → "Hellenists": Ἑλληνισταί, Greek-speaking Jews.
- **"were neglected in the daily ministration" → "were being overlooked in the daily service"** (v1) — Greek παρεθεωροῦντο (an imperfect), διακονία: the same word as "the service of the word" in v4, and its verb is "serve tables" in v2. The Greek does not say what the service was; "distribution" is an interpretation.
- **"It is not reason" → "It is not pleasing"** (v2) — Greek οὐκ ἀρεστόν, "pleasing, acceptable"; the same root as "was pleasing" (ἤρεσεν) in v5. "Leaving the word": καταλείψαντας τὸν λόγον; the Greek has no word "preaching".
- **"Wherefore … look ye out" → "But look out"** (v3) — Greek δέ (WH NA28; Treg RP οὖν, "therefore").
- **"of honest report" → "who have testimony borne to them"** (v3) — Greek μαρτυρουμένους, "being testified to": men of whom others speak well.
- **"full of the Holy Ghost and wisdom" → "full of Spirit and wisdom"** (v3) — Greek πλήρεις πνεύματος καὶ σοφίας, without article and without "holy" (WH Treg NA28; RP adds ἁγίου).
- **"over this business" → "over this need"** (v3) — Greek ἐπὶ τῆς χρείας ταύτης.
- **"give ourselves continually" → "persevere"** (v4) — Greek προσκαρτερήσομεν.
- **"the saying pleased the whole multitude" → "the word was pleasing before the whole multitude"** (v5) — Greek ἤρεσεν ὁ λόγος ἐνώπιον παντὸς τοῦ πλήθους; "multitude" is the same word as in v2.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v5) — Greek πνεύματος ἁγίου, without article, as "faith" beside it.
- **"a proselyte of Antioch" kept** (v5) — Greek προσήλυτον Ἀντιοχέα, "a proselyte, an Antiochian".
- **"and when they had prayed, they laid their hands on them" kept** (v6) — the Greek does not name who prayed and laid on hands; by its grammar the subject is the same as of "they set", by the sense it is the apostles. The verse leaves it as the Greek does.
- **"increased" → "was growing"** (v7) — Greek ηὔξανεν, an imperfect, as are "was multiplying" and "were obeying".
- **"a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith" → "a great crowd of the priests were obeying the faith"** (v7) — Greek πολύς τε ὄχλος τῶν ἱερέων ὑπήκουον τῇ πίστει.
- **"full of faith and power" → "full of grace and power"** (v8) — Greek χάριτος (WH Treg NA28; RP πίστεως, "faith").
- **"the synagogue, which is called the synagogue of the Libertines" → "the synagogue called that of the Freedmen"** (v9) — Greek Λιβερτίνων, the Latin *libertini*, freed slaves or their sons. The Greek can be read as one synagogue of Freedmen, Cyrenians and Alexandrians, or as several groups; the verse keeps its order.
- **"Cyrene" / "*Cyrenians*"** (v9) — Greek Κυρηναῖοι *Kyrēnaioi*; first occurrence noted.
- **"they were not able to resist" → "they had no strength to resist"** (v10) — Greek οὐκ ἴσχυον ἀντιστῆναι. "The Spirit": τῷ πνεύματι; it can also be read "the spirit with which he was speaking".
- **"suborned" kept** (v11) — Greek ὑπέβαλον, to put forward secretly. "Against Moses": εἰς, "with regard to".
- **"came upon him, and caught him, and brought him to the council" → "coming upon him they seized him and led him to the Sanhedrin"** (v12) — Greek ἐπιστάντες συνήρπασαν … ἤγαγον εἰς τὸ συνέδριον.
- **"ceaseth not to speak blasphemous words against this holy place" → "does not cease speaking words against this holy place"** (v13) — "blasphemous" is not in the critical text (WH Treg NA28 λαλῶν ῥήματα; RP ῥήματα βλάσφημα λαλῶν). "This holy place": the verse follows τοῦ ἁγίου τούτου (WH NA28); Treg RP and the SBLGNT have no "this".
- **"Torah" / "the law"** (v13) — Greek τοῦ νόμου; rendered "Torah" as it refers to Moses' instruction.
- **"Jesus of Nazareth" → "Yeshua the Nazorean"** (v14) — Greek Ἰησοῦς ὁ Ναζωραῖος, as 2:22.
- **"shall destroy" → "will tear down"** (v14) — Greek καταλύσει, the verb used of pulling down a building.
- **"customs which Moses delivered us" → "customs which Moshe delivered to us"** (v14) — Greek ἔθη = "customs"; παρέδωκεν, "deliver", the verb of handing down a tradition.
- **"looking stedfastly on him" → "gazing at him"** (v15) — Greek ἀτενίσαντες.
- **"as it had been the face of an angel" → "as the face of an angel"** (v15) — Greek ὡσεὶ πρόσωπον ἀγγέλου; the Greek has no "that his face was".
- **"Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, Nicolas" → "Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, Nicolaus"** (v5) — Greek Πρόχορος, Νικάνωρ, Τίμων, Παρμενᾶς, Νικόλαος; the familiar Latinized forms are kept, with "Nicolaus" matching the Greek *Nikolaos* more closely than KJV "Nicolas."
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
