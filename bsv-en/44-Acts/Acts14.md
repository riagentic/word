# Acts 14

[1] And it came to pass in ⟦iconium|Ikonion|Iconium⟧ that they entered together into the synagogue of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and spoke in such a way that a great multitude of both ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ believed.

[2] But the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who disobeyed roused the souls of the nations and made them evil against the brothers.

[3] So they stayed a considerable time, speaking boldly upon the Lord, who testified to the word of his grace, giving signs and wonders to be done through their hands.

[4] But the multitude of the city was split, and some were with the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and some with the apostles.

[5] And when there came a rush of both the nations and ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, with their rulers, to treat them shamefully and to stone them,

[6] having become aware of it, they fled to the cities of ⟦lycaonia|Lykaonia|Lycaonia⟧, Lystra and Derbe, and the surrounding country,

[7] and there they were preaching the gospel.

[8] And a certain man in Lystra, powerless in the feet, was sitting, lame from his mother's womb, who never walked.

[9] This man heard ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ speaking; who, having gazed at him and seen that he has faith to be saved,

[10] said with a loud voice, "Rise upright upon your feet." And he leaped up and was walking.

[11] And the crowds, seeing what ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ did, lifted up their voice, saying in Lycaonian, "The gods, made like men, came down to us."

[12] And they were calling ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ Zeus, and ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ Hermes, since he was the one leading the word.

[13] And the priest of the Zeus who was before the city, having brought bulls and garlands to the gateways, wanted to sacrifice with the crowds.

[14] But the apostles ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having heard, tore their garments and leaped out into the crowd, crying out

[15] and saying, "Men, why do you do these things? We also are men of like nature with you, preaching the gospel to you, to turn from these vain things to a living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who made the heaven and the earth and the sea and all the things in them;

[16] who in the generations gone by let all the nations go in their ways;

[17] and yet he did not leave himself without witness, working good, giving you rains from heaven and fruit-bearing seasons, filling up your hearts with food and gladness."

[18] And saying these things, they scarcely restrained the crowds from sacrificing to them.

[19] But there came ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ from ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧ and ⟦iconium|Ikonion|Iconium⟧, and having persuaded the crowds and stoned ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, they were dragging him outside the city, supposing him to have died.

[20] But when the disciples had encircled him, having risen he entered into the city. And on the morrow he went out with ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ to Derbe.

[21] And having preached the gospel to that city and made a considerable number disciples, they returned to Lystra and to ⟦iconium|Ikonion|Iconium⟧ and to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧,

[22] making stable the souls of the disciples, exhorting them to abide in the faith, and that through many afflictions we must enter into the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[23] And having chosen elders for them church by church, having prayed with fastings, they entrusted them to the Lord in whom they had believed.

[24] And having gone through Pisidia they came to Pamphylia,

[25] and having spoken the word in ⟦perga|Perge|Perga⟧ they went down to ⟦attalia|Attaleia|Attalia⟧,

[26] and from there they sailed away to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, from where they had been given over to the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for the work which they fulfilled.

[27] And having arrived and gathered the church together, they were announcing all that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did with them, and that he opened to the nations a door of faith.

[28] And they stayed no little time with the disciples.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15, 22, 26, 27) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" / Spirit** — no explicit occurrence of the formula "Holy Spirit" in this chapter; consistent policy.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** — no direct occurrence in narrative of this chapter (referenced as "the Lord"); consistent policy.
- **"baptize" → "immerse"** — no direct occurrence in this chapter.
- **"the gods" / Zeus / Hermes** (v11–13) — Greek pagan deities retained by name; no Hebrew substitution appropriate here.
- **"ordained them elders" → "chosen elders for them"** (v23) — Greek χειροτονήσαντες *cheirotonēsantes*, literally "stretching out the hand": to choose by a show of hands, and so to appoint; the same verb as 2 Cor 8:19. "Ordained" is a later ecclesiastical term.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
- **"they went both together" → "they entered together"** (v1) — Greek κατὰ τὸ αὐτό, "together"; it can also mean "in the same way" (as at Antioch). "The synagogue of the *Yehudim*" is a noun in Greek, not "Jewish".
- **"the unbelieving Jews stirred up the Gentiles, and made their minds evil affected" → "the *Yehudim* who disobeyed roused the souls of the nations and made them evil"** (v2) — Greek ἀπειθήσαντες, "having disobeyed" (not ἀπιστέω "disbelieve"); ἐπήγειραν καὶ ἐκάκωσαν τὰς ψυχὰς τῶν ἐθνῶν: both verbs have "the souls" as object. κακόω is "make bad"; with a person as object it is "mistreat" (12:1), which does not fit "against the brothers" here.
- **"long time" → "a considerable time"; "in the Lord" → "upon the Lord"; "gave testimony" → "testified"** (v3) — Greek ἱκανὸν χρόνον; ἐπὶ τῷ κυρίῳ, "resting upon the Lord"; μαρτυροῦντι. "Granted" → "giving": διδόντι.
- **"was divided" → "was split"** (v4) — Greek ἐσχίσθη, the verb of σχίσμα; no word "sided".
- **"an assault" → "a rush"; "to use them despitefully" → "to treat them shamefully"** (v5) — Greek ὁρμή, an impulse or onset; ὑβρίσαι, as 1 Thess 2:2.
- **"they were ware of it" → "having become aware of it"** (v6) — Greek συνιδόντες, as 12:12; "it" is supplied.
- **"impotent in his feet … a cripple" → "powerless in the feet … lame"** (v8) — Greek ἀδύνατος τοῖς ποσίν; χωλός. "From his mother's womb" kept: ἐκ κοιλίας μητρὸς αὐτοῦ; no "birth".
- **"who stedfastly beholding him" → "who, having gazed at him"** (v9) — the name "Paul" is not repeated in the Greek. "To be healed" → "to be saved": σωθῆναι, the verb "save"; of a sick man it is "be made well".
- **"Stand upright on thy feet" → "Rise upright upon your feet"** (v10) — Greek Ἀνάστηθι. "Walked" → "was walking": περιεπάτει, imperfect.
- **"in the likeness of men" → "made like men"** (v11) — Greek ὁμοιωθέντες ἀνθρώποις, a participle. "Their voice" is singular.
- **"the chief speaker" → "the one leading the word"** (v12) — Greek ὁ ἡγούμενος τοῦ λόγου.
- **"the priest of Jupiter, which was before their city" → "the priest of the Zeus who was before the city"** (v13) — Greek τοῦ Διὸς τοῦ ὄντος πρὸ τῆς πόλεως; no word "temple"; "their" is Byzantine. "Oxen" → "bulls": ταύρους. "Gates" → "gateways": πυλῶνας, as 12:13.
- **"ran in" → "leaped out"** (v14) — Greek ἐξεπήδησαν (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"of like passions" → "of like nature"** (v15) — Greek ὁμοιοπαθεῖς, as Jas 5:17. "And preach unto you that ye should turn" → "preaching the gospel to you, to turn": εὐαγγελιζόμενοι ὑμᾶς … ἐπιστρέφειν. "The living God" → "a living *Elohim*": no article (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"in times past suffered all nations to walk" → "in the generations gone by let all the nations go"** (v16) — Greek ἐν ταῖς παρῳχημέναις γενεαῖς; πορεύεσθαι, not περιπατέω "walk"; no "own".
- **"he left not himself without witness, in that he did good" → "he did not leave himself without witness, working good"** (v17) — Greek ἀγαθουργῶν (WH, Treg, NA28). "Himself": the editions differ only in the breathing, which the old manuscripts do not have: αὐτόν (Treg, NA28; the reading the verse follows) or αὑτόν (WH, SBLGNT), RP ἑαυτόν; the sense is "himself" in each. "Us … our" → "you … your": ὑμῖν, ὑμῶν (WH, Treg, NA28). "Fruitful" → "fruit-bearing": καρποφόρους.
- **"restrained" kept** (v18) — Greek κατέπαυσαν, "made to rest, to cease" (Heb 4:8 "give rest"). "The people" → "the crowds": τοὺς ὄχλους. No word "even".
- **"drew him out" → "were dragging him outside"; "had been dead" → "to have died"** (v19) — Greek ἔσυρον, imperfect; τεθνηκέναι.
- **"stood round about him" → "had encircled him"; "the next day" → "on the morrow"** (v20) — Greek κυκλωσάντων; τῇ ἐπαύριον.
- **"had taught many" → "made a considerable number disciples"** (v21) — Greek μαθητεύσαντες ἱκανούς.
- **"confirming" → "making stable"; "to continue in" → "to abide in"; "tribulation" → "afflictions"** (v22) — Greek ἐπιστηρίζοντες; ἐμμένειν, as Gal 3:10; θλίψεων, plural. The Greek passes into direct speech with ὅτι; there is no "saying".
- **"in every church" → "church by church"; "with fasting" → "with fastings"; "commended" → "entrusted"** (v23) — Greek κατʼ ἐκκλησίαν; νηστειῶν; παρέθεντο.
- **"recommended" → "given over"** (v26) — Greek παραδεδομένοι, the verb "deliver up" (as 15:26); "delivered up to the grace" would say the opposite in English, so "give over" here and in 15:40.
- **"rehearsed" → "were announcing"; "had done with them" kept** (v27) — Greek ἀνήγγελλον, imperfect (WH, Treg, NA28); μετʼ αὐτῶν is "with them", not "through them". "Gentiles" → "nations".
- **"abode long time" → "stayed no little time"** (v28) — Greek διέτριβον … χρόνον οὐκ ὀλίγον; "there" is Byzantine.
