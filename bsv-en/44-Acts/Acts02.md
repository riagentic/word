# Acts 2

[1] And when the day of ⟦shavuot|Shavuot|Pentecost⟧ was being fulfilled, they were all together at the same place,

[2] and suddenly there came out of heaven a sound as of a violent wind borne along, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting,

[3] and there appeared to them tongues as of fire, being divided, and it sat upon each one of them,

[4] and they were all filled with ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|the Holy Spirit⟧ and began to speak in other tongues, as the Spirit was giving them to speak forth.

[5] Now there were dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, reverent men from every nation of those under heaven;

[6] and when this voice came, the multitude came together and was confounded, because each one was hearing them speaking in his own language.

[7] And they were amazed and marveled, saying, "Behold, are not all these who are speaking Galileans?

[8] And how do we hear, each in our own language in which we were born?

[9] ⟦parthians|Parthoi|Parthians⟧ and ⟦medes|Madai|Medes⟧ and ⟦elamites|Elmaye|Elamites⟧, and those dwelling in Mesopotamia, both ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ and ⟦cappadocia|Kappadokia|Cappadocia⟧, ⟦pontus|Pontos|Pontus⟧ and Asia,

[10] both Phrygia and Pamphylia, ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and the parts of ⟦libya-nt|Libye|Libya⟧ toward ⟦cyrene|Kyrene|Cyrene⟧, and the sojourners from ⟦rome|Roma|Rome⟧,

[11] both ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and proselytes, ⟦cretans|Kretes|Cretans⟧ and ⟦arabians|Arvim|Arabians⟧ — we hear them speaking in our tongues the great things of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[12] And they were all amazed and utterly at a loss, saying one to another, "What does this mean?"

[13] But others, mocking, were saying, "They are full of sweet wine."

[14] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, standing with the eleven, lifted up his voice and spoke forth to them: "Men, ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and all you who dwell in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, let this be known to you, and give ear to my words.

[15] For these are not drunk, as you suppose, for it is the third hour of the day;

[16] but this is what has been spoken through the prophet ⟦joel|Yoel|Joel⟧:

[17] "'**And it shall be in the last days, says ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
I will pour out from my Spirit upon all flesh,
and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy,
and your young men shall see visions,
and your old men shall dream dreams;
[18] and even upon my male slaves and upon my female slaves
in those days I will pour out from my Spirit, and they shall prophesy.
[19] And I will give wonders in the heaven above
and signs upon the earth below,
blood and fire and vapor of smoke;
[20] the sun shall be turned into darkness
and the moon into blood,
before the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ comes, the great and notable day.
[21] And it shall be that everyone who calls upon the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ shall be saved.**'

[22] "Men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, hear these words: ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧, a man shown forth to you from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ by powers and wonders and signs which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did through him in your midst, even as you yourselves know —

[23] this one, given up by the determined counsel and foreknowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you fastened up through the hand of lawless men and did away with,

[24] whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised up, having loosed the birth pains of death, because it was not possible for him to be held by it.

[25] For David says with regard to him,

"'**I saw ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ before me always,
for he is at my right hand, that I may not be shaken;
[26] therefore my heart was glad and my tongue exulted;
and moreover my flesh also will dwell in hope,
[27] for you will not forsake my soul unto Hades,
nor will you give your devout one to see corruption.
[28] You made known to me ways of life;
you will fill me with gladness with your face.**'

[29] "Men, brothers, it is permitted to say to you with boldness concerning the patriarch David that he both died and was buried, and his grave is among us until this day.

[30] Being therefore a prophet, and knowing that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ swore to him with an oath to seat one from the fruit of his loins upon his throne,

[31] foreseeing, he spoke concerning the resurrection of the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, that neither was he forsaken unto Hades nor did his flesh see corruption.

[32] This ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised up, of which we all are witnesses.

[33] Being therefore lifted up by the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and having received from the Father the promise of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, he poured out this which you both see and hear.

[34] For David did not go up into the heavens, but he himself says,

"'**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ said to my Lord,
"Sit at my right hand,
[35] until I set your enemies as a footstool of your feet."**'

[36] "Assuredly therefore let all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ know that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ made him both Lord and ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, this ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ whom you crucified."

[37] Now having heard, they were pierced in the heart, and said to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and the rest of the apostles, "What shall we do, men, brothers?"

[38] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said to them, "**Repent, and let each of you be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ upon the name of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ unto forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧;**

[39] for the promise is to you and to your children and to all those far off, as many as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall call to himself."

[40] And with many other words he solemnly testified, and was exhorting them, saying, "Be saved from this crooked generation."

[41] So then those who accepted his word were ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧, and there were added in that day about three thousand souls.

[42] And they were persevering in the teaching of the apostles and in the fellowship, in the breaking of the bread and in the prayers.

[43] And fear was coming upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were being done through the apostles.

[44] And all who believed were together and had all things common,

[45] and they were selling their possessions and goods and dividing them to all, according as anyone had need;

[46] and day by day, persevering with one accord in the temple and breaking bread from house to house, they were partaking of food in exultation and singleness of heart,

[47] praising ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and having favor with all the people. And the Lord was adding together day by day those who were being saved.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v31, 36, 38) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v4, 33, 38) — consistent rendering throughout Acts. In v4 the Greek has no article (πνεύματος ἁγίου).
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v14, 37, 38) — Aramaic name used consistently; the Greek has Πέτρος.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v38, 41) — Greek *baptizo* = "to immerse"; consistent rendering.
- **"hell" → "Hades"** (v27, 31) — Greek ᾅδης *Hadēs*, the realm of the dead; in Ps 16:10 it renders Hebrew שְׁאוֹל *Sheol*. The verse gives the Greek word; "hell" is misleading in modern English.
- **"when the day of Pentecost was fully come" → "when the day of *Shavuot* was being fulfilled"** (v1) — Greek ἐν τῷ συμπληροῦσθαι, a present infinitive. τῆς πεντηκοστῆς is "of the fiftieth (day)".
- **"with one accord in one place" → "together at the same place"** (v1) — Greek ὁμοῦ ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό (the four critical editions; ὁμοθυμαδόν "with one accord" RP).
- **"a rushing mighty wind" → "a violent wind borne along"** (v2) — Greek φερομένης πνοῆς βιαίας; πνοή is "breath, blast" (17:25), not the usual word for wind.
- **"cloven tongues like as of fire, and it sat" → "tongues as of fire, being divided, and it sat"** (v3) — Greek διαμεριζόμεναι, "being divided, distributed" (the verb of v45): tongues shared out among them, or tongues that parted. ἐκάθισεν is singular with no stated subject.
- **"to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance" → "to speak in other tongues, as the Spirit was giving them to speak forth"** (v4) — Greek λαλεῖν ἑτέραις γλώσσαις, a dative as in 1 Cor 14; ἀποφθέγγεσθαι as v14.
- **"devout" → "reverent"** (v5) — Greek εὐλαβεῖς; "devout" renders ὅσιος (v27).
- **"when this was noised abroad" → "when this voice came"** (v6) — Greek γενομένης δὲ τῆς φωνῆς ταύτης; φωνή is "voice, sound": the sound of v2 (there ἦχος) or the speaking of v4.
- **"every man heard them speak in his own language" → "each one was hearing them speaking in his own language"** (v6) — Greek ἤκουον εἷς ἕκαστος, plural verb with "each one" (ἤκουσεν WH); διάλεκτος "language" as 1:19, beside γλῶσσα "tongue" (v4, 11).
- **"wherein we were born" → "in which we were born"** (v8) — Greek ἐν ᾗ ἐγεννήθημεν; no word "native".
- **"strangers of Rome" → "the sojourners from *Roma*"** (v10) — Greek οἱ ἐπιδημοῦντες Ῥωμαῖοι, "the Romans staying here".
- **"the wonderful works of God" → "the great things of *Elohim*"** (v11) — Greek τὰ μεγαλεῖα.
- **"were in doubt" → "utterly at a loss"** (v12) — Greek διηπόρουν (NA28; διηποροῦντο WH Treg). "What does this mean?" is literally "What does this wish to be?" (Τί θέλει τοῦτο εἶναι).
- **"full of new wine" → "full of sweet wine"** (v13) — Greek γλεύκους μεμεστωμένοι, "having been filled with sweet wine"; a different verb from "filled" in v2 and v4.
- **"Ye men of Judaea" → "Men, *Yehudim*"** (v14) — Greek Ἄνδρες Ἰουδαῖοι, the word of v5 and v11; it can also be heard as "Judeans".
- **"hearken to my words" → "give ear to my words"** (v14) — Greek ἐνωτίσασθε τὰ ῥήματά μου (ῥήματα; λόγους in v22).
- **"I will pour out of my Spirit" → "I will pour out from my Spirit"** (v17, 18) — Greek ἀπὸ τοῦ πνεύματός μου, as the Septuagint; the Hebrew of Joel 2:28 has "my Spirit" as the object.
- **"your old men" kept** (v17) — Greek οἱ πρεσβύτεροι ὑμῶν, the word for "elders", here beside "young men". ἐνυπνίοις ἐνυπνιασθήσονται is "shall dream with dreams".
- **"on my servants and on my handmaidens" → "upon my male slaves and upon my female slaves"** (v18) — Greek δούλους, δούλας; "my" both times, which the Hebrew of Joel lacks.
- **"I will shew wonders in heaven above, and signs in the earth beneath" → "I will give wonders in the heaven above and signs upon the earth below"** (v19) — Greek δώσω; "above", "signs" and "below" are not in Joel.
- **"that great and notable day of the Lord" → "the day of the Lord …, the great and notable day"** (v20) — Greek ἡμέραν κυρίου τὴν μεγάλην καὶ ἐπιφανῆ; ἐπιφανής is "conspicuous, splendid", the root of ἐπιφάνεια "appearing". The Hebrew of Joel has "fearful". The verse omits ἤ after πρίν (WH Treg NA28; RP and the printed SBLGNT have it): no change in translation.
- **"Jesus of Nazareth" → "Yeshua the Nazorean"** (v22) — Greek Ἰησοῦν τὸν Ναζωραῖον; the place name is not in the verse.
- **"a man approved of God among you by miracles" → "a man shown forth to you from *Elohim* by powers"** (v22) — Greek ἀποδεδειγμένον ἀπὸ τοῦ θεοῦ εἰς ὑμᾶς δυνάμεσι.
- **"being delivered by the determinate counsel" → "given up by the determined counsel"** (v23) — Greek ἔκδοτον, an adjective, "given out, surrendered" (only here); τῇ ὡρισμένῃ βουλῇ, from ὁρίζω "mark out, determine" (Rom 1:4).
- **"ye have taken, and by wicked hands have crucified and slain" → "you fastened up through the hand of lawless men and did away with"** (v23) — Greek διὰ χειρὸς ἀνόμων προσπήξαντες ἀνείλατε. προσπήγνυμι is "fasten to" (only here); "crucify" is a different verb (v36). ἀναιρέω "do away with" means "kill". "Have taken" (λαβόντες) is the Byzantine reading.
- **"having loosed the pains of death" → "having loosed the birth pains of death"** (v24) — Greek τὰς ὠδῖνας τοῦ θανάτου. In Ps 18:4–5 the Septuagint has ὠδῖνες for Hebrew *chevlei*, which can mean "cords" or "pangs".
- **"raised up"** (v24, 32) — Greek ἀνέστησεν, the verb of ἀνάστασις "resurrection" (v31); not ἐγείρω.
- **"I foresaw the Lord always before my face" → "I saw the Lord before me always"** (v25) — Greek Προορώμην … ἐνώπιόν μου, "I kept seeing before me"; the same verb is "foreseeing" in v31. "With regard to him" is εἰς αὐτόν.
- **"my tongue was glad" → "my tongue exulted"** (v26) — Greek ἠγαλλιάσατο; the Hebrew of Ps 16:9 has "my glory".
- **"shall rest in hope" → "will dwell in hope"** (v26) — Greek κατασκηνώσει ἐπ' ἐλπίδι, "will pitch its tent upon hope".
- **"thou wilt not leave my soul in hell" → "you will not forsake my soul unto Hades"** (v27) — Greek οὐκ ἐγκαταλείψεις … εἰς ᾅδην.
- **"thine Holy One" → "your devout one"** (v27) — Greek τὸν ὅσιόν σου (Hebrew *chasid*), not ἅγιος "holy".
- **"corruption" kept** (v27, 31) — Greek διαφθορά; the Hebrew of Ps 16:10 has *shachat*, "pit".
- **"with thy countenance" → "with your face"** (v28) — Greek μετὰ τοῦ προσώπου σου.
- **"let me freely speak" → "it is permitted to say … with boldness"** (v29) — Greek ἐξὸν εἰπεῖν μετὰ παρρησίας.
- **"his sepulchre" → "his grave"** (v29) — Greek τὸ μνῆμα.
- **"that of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne" → "to seat one from the fruit of his loins upon his throne"** (v30) — Greek ἐκ καρποῦ τῆς ὀσφύος αὐτοῦ καθίσαι ἐπὶ τὸν θρόνον αὐτοῦ; "one" is supplied. The longer wording is the Byzantine reading.
- **"that his soul was not left in hell" → "that neither was he forsaken unto Hades"** (v31) — the four critical editions have no "his soul" here.
- **"whereof we all are witnesses" → "of which we all are witnesses"** (v32) — Greek οὗ, "of which" or "of whom".
- **"by the right hand of God exalted" → "lifted up by the right hand of *Elohim*"** (v33) — Greek τῇ δεξιᾷ … ὑψωθείς, a bare dative: "by the right hand" or "to the right hand". ὑψόω is "lift up, exalt".
- **"which ye now see and hear" → "which you both see and hear"** (v33) — the verse follows ὑμεῖς καὶ βλέπετε (WH NA28; 2–2, NA28 decides; Treg, RP and the printed SBLGNT have no καί). "Now" is the Byzantine reading.
- **"until I make thy foes thy footstool" → "until I set your enemies as a footstool of your feet"** (v35) — Greek θῶ … ὑποπόδιον τῶν ποδῶν σου.
- **"they were pricked in their heart" → "they were pierced in the heart"** (v37) — Greek κατενύγησαν τὴν καρδίαν. The Greek has no "this" after "heard".
- **"Then Peter said unto them" → "And *Kefa* said to them"** (v38) — the printed text has no verb (Πέτρος δὲ πρὸς αὐτούς, WH Treg); "said" is supplied (NA28 adds φησίν).
- **"be baptized … in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins" → "let each of you be immersed upon the name of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* unto forgiveness of your sins"** (v38) — Greek βαπτισθήτω, third person; ἐπὶ τῷ ὀνόματι (NA28, SBLGNT; 2–2, NA28 decides; ἐν "in" WH Treg); εἰς ἄφεσιν as Mark 1:4.
- **"even as many as the Lord our God shall call" → "as many as the Lord our *Elohim* shall call to himself"** (v39) — Greek προσκαλέσηται.
- **"did he testify and exhort" → "he solemnly testified, and was exhorting them"** (v40) — Greek διεμαρτύρατο, παρεκάλει; παρακαλέω is "exhort" where it is an urging, as here.
- **"Save yourselves" → "Be saved"** (v40) — Greek Σώθητε, a passive.
- **"they that gladly received his word" → "those who accepted his word"** (v41) — Greek ἀποδεξάμενοι; "gladly" is the Byzantine reading.
- **"they continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine" → "they were persevering in the teaching of the apostles"** (v42) — Greek προσκαρτεροῦντες τῇ διδαχῇ, as 1:14.
- **"fear came" → "fear was coming"** (v43) — Greek ἐγίνετο, imperfect; φόβος is "fear".
- **"possessions and goods" kept; "parted them" → "dividing them"** (v45) — Greek τὰ κτήματα καὶ τὰς ὑπάρξεις … διεμέριζον αὐτά; no word "proceeds".
- **"with gladness and singleness of heart" → "in exultation and singleness of heart"** (v46) — Greek ἐν ἀγαλλιάσει καὶ ἀφελότητι καρδίας; ἀφελότης, "plainness", occurs only here. κατ' οἶκον is "from house to house" or "at home".
- **"having favour" kept** (v47) — Greek ἔχοντες χάριν, the word for "grace".
- **"the Lord added to the church daily" → "the Lord was adding together day by day"** (v47) — Greek προσετίθει … ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό, "to the same place, together" (the four critical editions); "to the church" is the Byzantine reading. The Greek has no "to their number".
- **v17-21** — OT quotation: Joel 2:28-32 (LXX), the *Shavuot* Pentecost sermon.
- **v25-28** — OT quotation: Ps 16:8-11 (LXX), applied to *Yeshua's* resurrection.
- **v34-35** — OT quotation: Ps 110:1 ("The Lord said to my Lord…").

### Notes
- **v1** — *Shavuot* ("Weeks") = Pentecost, fiftieth day after Passover; celebrates the giving of the Torah at Sinai.
- **v17-21** quotes Joel 2:28-32.
- **v25-28** quotes Ps 16:8-11.
- **v34-35** quotes Ps 110:1.
