# Acts 21

[1] And when it came to pass that we put out to sea, having been drawn away from them, having run a straight course we came to ⟦kos|Kos|Cos⟧, and on the next day to ⟦rhodes|Rhodos|Rhodes⟧, and from there to Patara;

[2] and having found a ship crossing over to ⟦phoenicia|Phoinike|Phoenicia⟧, having gone aboard we put out to sea.

[3] And having sighted ⟦cyprus|Kypros|Cyprus⟧ and left it behind on the left, we were sailing to Syria, and we came down to ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, for there the ship was unloading the cargo.

[4] And having found the disciples, we remained there seven days; who were saying to ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ through the Spirit not to set foot in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[6] we took leave of one another, and we went on board the ship, and those returned to their own.

[7] And we, having finished the voyage from ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, arrived at Ptolemais, and having greeted the brothers we abode one day with them.

[8] And on the morrow, having gone out, we came to Caesarea, and having entered into the house of ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ the evangelist, who was of the seven, we abode with him.

[9] And this man had four daughters, virgins, prophesying.

[10] And as we remained many days, there came down from ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ a certain prophet named ⟦agabus|Agabos|Agabus⟧,

[11] and having come to us and taken ⟦paul|Paulus's|Paul's⟧ belt, having bound his own feet and hands he said, "These things says the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧: 'The man whose belt this is, thus will the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ bind in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and deliver up into the hands of nations.'"

[12] And when we heard these things, both we and those of the place were appealing to him not to go up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] Then ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ answered, "What are you doing, weeping and breaking my heart? For I am ready not only to be bound but also to die in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ for the name of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧."

[14] And as he was not persuaded, we became quiet, having said, "**Let the will of the Lord be done.**"

[15] And after these days, having made preparations, we were going up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;

[16] and there went with us also some of the disciples from Caesarea, bringing us to one with whom we should lodge, a certain Mnason of ⟦cyprus|Kypros|Cyprus⟧, a disciple of old.

[17] And when we had come to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the brothers accepted us gladly.

[18] And on the following day ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ was going in with us to ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧, and all the elders arrived.

[19] And having greeted them, he was declaring one by one each of the things which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did among the nations through his service.

[20] And they, having heard, were glorifying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they said to him, "You behold, brother, how many myriads there are among the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ of those who have believed, and they are all zealots for the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧;

[21] and they were instructed about you, that you teach all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who are among the nations apostasy from ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, telling them not to circumcise the children nor to walk by the customs.

[22] What then is it? They will certainly hear that you have come.

[23] Do therefore this which we say to you: we have four men having a vow upon themselves;

[24] having taken these along, be purified with them and spend upon them, so that they may shave the head, and all will know that of the things of which they have been instructed about you there is nothing, but that you yourself also walk in line, guarding the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[25] But concerning the nations who have believed, we sent, having judged that they guard themselves from what is sacrificed to idols and blood and what is strangled and sexual immorality."

[26] Then ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, having taken the men along, on the day after, having been purified with them, was going into the temple, declaring the fulfillment of the days of the purification, until the offering was offered for each one of them.

[27] And when the seven days were about to be consummated, the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ from Asia, having beheld him in the temple, were confounding the whole crowd, and they laid hands on him,

[28] crying out, "Men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, help! This is the man who teaches all everywhere against the people and the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and this place; and further he also brought ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ into the temple and has made this holy place common."

[29] For they had previously seen ⟦trophimus|Trophimos|Trophimus⟧ the ⟦ephesians|Ephesios|Ephesian⟧ in the city with him, whom they were supposing that ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ brought into the temple.

[30] And the whole city was moved, and there came a running together of the people, and having laid hold of ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ they were drawing him outside the temple, and immediately the doors were shut.

[31] And as they were seeking to kill him, a report went up to the chiliarch of the cohort that the whole of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ is being confounded,

[32] who at once, having taken along soldiers and centurions, ran down upon them; and they, having seen the chiliarch and the soldiers, ceased striking ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧.

[33] Then the chiliarch, having drawn near, laid hold of him and commanded him to be bound with two chains, and he was inquiring who he might be and what he has done.

[34] But some in the crowd were calling out one thing, others another; and as he was not able to know the certainty because of the commotion, he commanded him to be led into the barracks.

[35] And when he came upon the steps, it happened that he was borne by the soldiers because of the force of the crowd,

[36] for the multitude of the people was following, crying out, "Take him away!"

[38] Are you not then the ⟦egyptian|Mitzri|Egyptian⟧ who before these days unsettled and led out into the wilderness the four thousand men of the Sicarii?"

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v18) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v11) — consistent rendering.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v13) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"church" / assembly** — no church occurrence in this chapter.
- **"Agabus" / "*Agabus*"** (v10) — Greek Ἄγαβος; consistent with ch. 11 first introduction.
- **"Torah"** (v20, 24, 28) — Greek νόμος *nomos*; rendered "Torah" as it refers to Moses' teaching.
- **"Trophimus the Ephesian" / "*Trophimos*"** (v29) — Greek Τρόφιμος *Trophimos* ("nourishing"); first occurrence gives Greek form.
- **"the Way"** — no direct occurrence in this chapter.
- **The vow and purification** (v23–26) — the vow with shaving of the head points to the Nazirite vow (Num 6); the text itself does not name it.
- **"in the Hebrew tongue" → "in the Hebrew language"** (v40) — Greek τῇ Ἑβραΐδι διαλέκτῳ, "in the Hebrew language" (διάλεκτος as 1:19; 2:6): the Greek says Hebrew, the language of the Hebrews; many take it to be Aramaic, the spoken language of Judea. "Spake unto them" → "addressed them": προσεφώνησεν; "them" is supplied.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
- **"after we were gotten from them, and had launched" → "that we put out to sea, having been drawn away from them"** (v1) — Greek ἀναχθῆναι, as 13:13; ἀποσπασθέντας, a passive of the verb of 20:30, "drawn, torn away". "With a straight course" → "having run a straight course": εὐθυδρομήσαντες, as 16:11. "The day following" → "on the next day": τῇ ἑξῆς.
- **"sailing over" → "crossing over"; "set forth" → "put out to sea"** (v2) — Greek διαπερῶν; ἀνήχθημεν. "Went aboard" is ἐπιβάντες, the verb of v4 ("set foot in").
- **"when we had discovered Cyprus, we left it on the left hand … and landed at Tyre: for there the ship was to unlade her burden" → "having sighted Cyprus and left it behind on the left, … we came down to Tyre, for there the ship was unloading the cargo"** (v3) — Greek ἀναφάναντες (WH, NA28, SBLGNT; Treg, RP ἀναφανέντες, the same sense); καταλιπόντες; κατήλθομεν (WH, Treg, NA28); ἦν ἀποφορτιζόμενον. "Sailed" → "were sailing": ἐπλέομεν.
- **"finding disciples, we tarried" → "having found the disciples, we remained"; "that he should not go up to" → "not to set foot in"** (v4) — Greek ἀνευρόντες, "having found by searching", τοὺς μαθητάς with the article; ἐπεμείναμεν, as 10:48; μὴ ἐπιβαίνειν εἰς (WH, Treg, NA28), as 20:18; RP ἀναβαίνειν "go up".
- **"when we had accomplished those days, we departed and went our way; and they all brought us on our way, with wives and children" → "when it came to pass that we completed the days, having gone out we were going on our way, all of them sending us forward, with women and children"** (v5) — Greek ἐξαρτίσαι; ἐξελθόντες ἐπορευόμεθα; προπεμπόντων, as 20:38; γυναιξί is "women" or "wives". "We kneeled down … and prayed" → "having knelt down … and prayed": participles (WH, Treg, NA28); the sentence runs on into v6.
- **"taken our leave one of another, we took ship; and they returned home again" → "we took leave of one another, and we went on board the ship, and those returned to their own"** (v6) — Greek ἀπησπασάμεθα, the verb "greet" with ἀπό, a parting greeting; ἐνέβημεν (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28 ἀνέβημεν, "we went up"); εἰς τὰ ἴδια, as John 19:27.
- **"finished our course" → "finished the voyage"; "came" → "arrived"; "saluted" → "greeted"** (v7) — Greek τὸν πλοῦν διανύσαντες; κατηντήσαμεν; ἀσπασάμενοι. "From Tyre" can go with "voyage" or with "arrived". "Abode" kept (also v8): ἐμείναμεν.
- **"we that were of Paul's company departed, and came" → "having gone out, we came"** (v8) — Greek ἐξελθόντες ἤλθομεν (WH, Treg, NA28); "those around Paul" is Byzantine. "Which was one of the seven" → "who was of the seven": ὄντος ἐκ τῶν ἑπτά.
- **"four daughters, virgins, which did prophesy" kept as "four daughters, virgins, prophesying"** (v9) — Greek θυγατέρες τέσσαρες παρθένοι προφητεύουσαι; παρθένος is "virgin", not "unmarried".
- **"as we tarried there many days" → "as we remained many days"** (v10) — Greek ἐπιμενόντων δὲ ἡμέρας πλείους, literally "more days"; "we" is supplied (ἡμῶν only in RP), and there is no "there".
- **"girdle" → "belt"; "Thus saith" → "These things says"; "So shall the Jews at Jerusalem bind the man that owneth this girdle, and shall deliver him into the hands of the Gentiles" → "The man whose belt this is, thus will the Jews bind in Jerusalem and deliver up into the hands of nations"** (v11) — Greek ζώνην; Τάδε λέγει; the Greek order is kept; παραδώσουσιν, "deliver up"; εἰς χεῖρας ἐθνῶν, without article. "Feet and hands": the Greek order (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"they of that place, besought him" → "those of the place were appealing to him"** (v12) — Greek οἱ ἐντόπιοι; παρεκαλοῦμεν, an imperfect; "him" is supplied.
- **"What mean ye to weep and to break mine heart?" → "What are you doing, weeping and breaking my heart?"** (v13) — Greek Τί ποιεῖτε κλαίοντες καὶ συνθρύπτοντες, "breaking in pieces, crushing". "To be bound" is δεθῆναι, the verb of v11 (not "imprisoned").
- **"when he would not be persuaded, we ceased" → "as he was not persuaded, we became quiet"** (v14) — Greek μὴ πειθομένου; ἡσυχάσαμεν, as 11:18. "The will of the Lord be done": Τοῦ κυρίου τὸ θέλημα γινέσθω, with "of the Lord" first (WH, Treg, NA28).
- **"we took up our carriages, and went up" → "having made preparations, we were going up"** (v15) — Greek ἐπισκευασάμενοι, "having equipped ourselves, packed"; ἀνεβαίνομεν, an imperfect.
- **"brought with them one Mnason of Cyprus, an old disciple, with whom we should lodge" → "bringing us to one with whom we should lodge, a certain Mnason of Cyprus, a disciple of old"** (v16) — Greek ἄγοντες παρʼ ᾧ ξενισθῶμεν Μνάσωνί τινι Κυπρίῳ: "bringing [us] to Mnason, with whom we should lodge"; the words can also be taken "bringing Mnason, with whom we should lodge" (KJV). "Us" is supplied. ἀρχαίῳ is "of old, from the beginning" (15:7), not "aged"; Κυπρίῳ is "a Cypriot".
- **"received us gladly" → "accepted us gladly"** (v17) — Greek ἀπεδέξαντο (WH, Treg, NA28), as 2:41.
- **"the day following" → "on the following day"; "went in" → "was going in"; "were present" → "arrived"** (v18) — Greek τῇ ἐπιούσῃ; εἰσῄει, an imperfect; παρεγένοντο.
- **"he declared particularly what things God had wrought among the Gentiles by his ministry" → "he was declaring one by one each of the things which God did among the nations through his service"** (v19) — Greek ἐξηγεῖτο καθʼ ἓν ἕκαστον; ἐν τοῖς ἔθνεσιν; διὰ τῆς διακονίας.
- **"glorified the Lord" → "were glorifying God"** (v20) — Greek ἐδόξαζον τὸν θεόν (WH, Treg, NA28); RP "the Lord".
- **"Thou seest … how many thousands of Jews there are which believe; and they are all zealous of the law" → "You behold … how many myriads there are among the Jews of those who have believed, and they are all zealots for the Torah"** (v20) — Greek Θεωρεῖς; μυριάδες, "tens of thousands"; ἐν τοῖς Ἰουδαίοις (WH, Treg, NA28); πεπιστευκότων, a perfect; ζηλωταί, a noun, as Gal 1:14.
- **"they are informed of thee, that thou teachest all the Jews which are among the Gentiles to forsake Moses" → "they were instructed about you, that you teach all the Jews who are among the nations apostasy from Moses"** (v21, also v24) — Greek κατηχήθησαν, the verb of Gal 6:6 ("instruct"), here of what they were told; ἀποστασίαν … ἀπὸ Μωϋσέως, the noun of 2 Thess 2:3. "Their children … the customs": τὰ τέκνα, τοῖς ἔθεσιν, with the article only; no "our".
- **"the multitude must needs come together: for they will hear" → "They will certainly hear"** (v22) — the critical text (WH, Treg, NA28) has only πάντως ἀκούσονται; the rest is Byzantine. "What is it therefore?" kept as "What then is it?": τί οὖν ἐστιν.
- **"a vow on them" → "a vow upon themselves"** (v23) — ἐφʼ ἑαυτῶν, "upon themselves" (Treg, NA28, RP); WH and the printed SBLGNT read ἀφʼ ἑαυτῶν, "of themselves, of their own accord".
- **"Them take, and purify thyself with them, and be at charges with them, that they may shave their heads" → "having taken these along, be purified with them and spend upon them, so that they may shave the head"** (v24) — Greek παραλαβών; ἁγνίσθητι, a passive form; δαπάνησον ἐπʼ αὐτοῖς; τὴν κεφαλήν, singular.
- **"and all may know … are nothing; but that thou thyself also walkest orderly, and keepest the law" → "and all will know that … there is nothing, but that you yourself also walk in line, guarding the Torah"** (v24) — Greek γνώσονται, a future (WH, Treg, NA28); στοιχεῖς, as Gal 6:16; φυλάσσων, "guarding".
- **"we have written and concluded that they observe no such thing, save only that they keep themselves from things offered to idols" → "we sent, having judged that they guard themselves from what is sacrificed to idols"** (v25) — ἀπεστείλαμεν, "we sent" (WH, Treg, SBLGNT); NA28 and RP read ἐπεστείλαμεν, "we wrote" (as 15:20). κρίναντες as 15:19; "that they observe no such thing, save only" is Byzantine. φυλάσσεσθαι is the verb of v24; τὸ εἰδωλόθυτον and πνικτόν are singular.
- **"the next day purifying himself with them entered into the temple, to signify the accomplishment of the days of purification, until that an offering should be offered" → "on the day after, having been purified with them, was going into the temple, declaring the fulfillment of the days of the purification, until the offering was offered"** (v26) — Greek τῇ ἐχομένῃ ἡμέρᾳ, which can go with "having taken" or with "having been purified"; εἰσῄει; διαγγέλλων, as Rom 9:17; ἐκπλήρωσιν; ἕως οὗ προσηνέχθη … ἡ προσφορά, an aorist indicative with the article: literally "until the offering was offered".
- **"were almost ended" → "were about to be consummated"; "when they saw him" → "having beheld him"; "stirred up all the people" → "were confounding the whole crowd"** (v27) — Greek ἔμελλον συντελεῖσθαι, as Mark 13:4; θεασάμενοι; συνέχεον, as 2:6; ὄχλον.
- **"hath polluted this holy place" → "has made this holy place common"** (v28) — Greek κεκοίνωκεν, as 10:15. "All men every where": πάντας πανταχῇ.
- **"had seen before" → "had previously seen"; "an Ephesian" → "the Ephesian"; "had brought" → "brought"** (v29) — Greek προεωρακότες (WH, Treg, NA28); τὸν Ἐφέσιον; εἰσήγαγεν.
- **"the people ran together: and they took Paul, and drew him" → "there came a running together of the people, and having laid hold of Paul they were drawing him"** (v30) — Greek ἐγένετο συνδρομὴ τοῦ λαοῦ; ἐπιλαβόμενοι, as 9:27 (also v33); εἷλκον, an imperfect, as 16:19. "Doors" kept: θύραι.
- **"as they went about to kill him, tidings came unto the chief captain of the band, that all Jerusalem was in an uproar" → "as they were seeking to kill him, a report went up to the chiliarch of the cohort that the whole of Jerusalem is being confounded"** (v31) — Greek ζητούντων; ἀνέβη φάσις; χιλιάρχῳ, the commander of a thousand, a military tribune; σπείρης, as 10:1; συγχύννεται (WH, Treg, NA28), a present, the verb of v27.
- **"immediately took" → "at once, having taken along"; "ran down unto them" → "ran down upon them"; "left beating of Paul" → "ceased striking Paul"** (v32) — Greek ἐξαυτῆς; κατέδραμεν ἐπʼ αὐτούς; ἐπαύσαντο τύπτοντες, "strike" as 23:2 (δέρω is "beat").
- **"came near, and took him" → "having drawn near, laid hold of him"; "demanded who he was, and what he had done" → "was inquiring who he might be and what he has done"** (v33) — Greek ἐγγίσας ἐπελάβετο; ἐπυνθάνετο τίς εἴη (an optative) καὶ τί ἐστιν πεποιηκώς.
- **"some cried one thing, some another, among the multitude … for the tumult … to be carried into the castle" → "some in the crowd were calling out one thing, others another … because of the commotion … to be led into the barracks"** (v34) — Greek ἐπεφώνουν (WH, Treg, NA28), as 12:22; ὄχλῳ; θόρυβον, as 20:1; ἄγεσθαι; παρεμβολήν, "camp" (Heb 13:11), here the quarters of the Roman soldiers. "The certainty" kept: τὸ ἀσφαλές.
- **"so it was, that he was borne of the soldiers for the violence of the people" → "it happened that he was borne by the soldiers because of the force of the crowd"** (v35) — Greek συνέβη; βίαν, as 5:26; ὄχλου. "Stairs" → "steps": ἀναβαθμούς.
- **"Away with him" → "Take him away!"** (v36) — Greek Αἶρε αὐτόν, as John 19:15.
- **"as Paul was to be led into the castle, he said … May I speak unto thee? … Canst thou speak Greek?" → "being about to be brought into the barracks, Paul says … Is it permitted for me to say something to you? … Do you know Greek?"** (v37) — Greek Μέλλων εἰσάγεσθαι; λέγει, a present; Εἰ ἔξεστίν μοι εἰπεῖν τι (τι: WH, Treg, NA28); Ἑλληνιστὶ γινώσκεις.
- **"Art not thou that Egyptian, which … madest an uproar, and leddest out into the wilderness four thousand men that were murderers?" → "Are you not then the Egyptian who … unsettled and led out into the wilderness the four thousand men of the Sicarii?"** (v38) — Greek οὐκ ἄρα σὺ εἶ, which can also be a statement: "Then you are not the Egyptian …". ἀναστατώσας, as Gal 5:12; τοὺς τετρακισχιλίους, with the article; σικαρίων, "dagger-men" (Latin *sicarii*), a name for armed assassins.
- **"I am a man which am a Jew of Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, a citizen of no mean city … suffer me" → "I am a man, a Jew, of Tarsus of Cilicia, a citizen of no insignificant city … permit me"** (v39) — Greek ἄνθρωπος … Ἰουδαῖος, Ταρσεὺς τῆς Κιλικίας ("a Tarsian of Cilicia"); οὐκ ἀσήμου; ἐπίτρεψον (also v40).
- **"beckoned" → "motioned"; "there was made a great silence" → "a great silence had come"** (v40) — Greek κατέσεισε, as 13:16; πολλῆς σιγῆς γενομένης.
