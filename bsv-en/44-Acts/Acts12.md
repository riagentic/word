# Acts 12

[1] Now about that time ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ the king laid on his hands to mistreat some of those from the church.

[2] And he did away with ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the brother of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ with the sword.

[3] And seeing that it is pleasing to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, he went on to apprehend ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ also (and they were days of the ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧),

[4] whom also having seized, he put in prison, delivering him up to four squads of four soldiers to guard him, wishing after the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to bring him up to the people.

[5] So ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was kept in the prison; but prayer was being earnestly made by the church to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ concerning him.

[6] And when ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ was about to bring him forth, on that night ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was asleep between two soldiers, bound with two chains, and guards before the door were keeping the prison.

[7] And behold, an angel of the Lord stood by, and a light shone in the cell; and having struck the side of ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, he roused him, saying, "Rise quickly." And his chains fell off from his hands.

[8] And the angel said to him, "Gird yourself and bind on your sandals." And he did so. And he says to him, "Throw your garment around you and follow me."

[9] And going out, he was following, and he did not know that what was happening through the angel is true, but thought he was seeing a vision.

[10] And having gone through a first guard and a second, they came to the iron gate that leads into the city, which opened to them of itself, and having gone out they went forward one street, and immediately the angel departed from him.

[11] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, having come to himself, said, "Now I know truly that the Lord sent out his angel and delivered me out of the hand of ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ and of all the expectation of the people of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧."

[12] And having become aware of it, he came to the house of ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the mother of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ who is called ⟦mark|Marcus|Mark⟧, where a considerable number were assembled and praying.

[13] And when he knocked at the door of the gateway, a slave woman came to answer, named ⟦rhoda|Rhode|Rhoda⟧,

[14] and recognizing the voice of ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, from joy she did not open the gateway, but running in she reported that ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ stood before the gateway.

[15] But they said to her, "You are mad." But she kept insisting that it was so. And they were saying, "It is his angel."

[16] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ continued knocking; and having opened, they saw him and were amazed.

[17] And having motioned to them with the hand to be silent, he related to them how the Lord brought him out of the prison, and he said, "Report these things to ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and to the brothers." And going out he went to another place.

[18] Now when day came, there was no small stir among the soldiers, what then had become of ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧.

[19] And ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, having sought after him and not found him, having examined the guards, commanded them to be led away, and going down from ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ to Caesarea he stayed there.

[20] Now he was furiously angry with the people of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧; and with one accord they came to him, and having persuaded ⟦blastus|Blastos|Blastus⟧, who was over the bedchamber of the king, they were asking for peace, because their country was nourished from the king's country.

[21] And on a set day ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, having put on royal apparel and sat down upon the judgment seat, was making a public speech to them.

[22] And the populace was calling out, "The voice of a god, and not of a man!"

[23] And instantly an angel of the Lord struck him, because he did not give the glory to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and having become eaten by worms he expired.

[24] But the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ grew and multiplied.

[25] And ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ returned, having fulfilled the service unto ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, taking along with them ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ who was called ⟦mark|Marcus|Mark⟧.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v2, 17) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 23, 24) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" / Spirit** — no explicit *Ruach HaQodesh* occurrence in this chapter; consistent policy maintained.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** — no direct occurrence in narrative text of this chapter (referenced implicitly as "the Lord"); consistent policy.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v3–18) — Aramaic name used consistently throughout the chapter.
- **"Easter" → "Passover"** (v4) — KJV's mistranslation of Greek πάσχα *pascha* as "Easter"; the text refers to the Jewish Passover festival. One of the most notorious translation errors in KJV; corrected.
- **"days of unleavened bread"** (v3) — Greek ἡμέραι τῶν ἀζύμων; retained; refers to the seven-day festival following Passover (Chag HaMatzot). "Days" has no article in the printed text (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28 and RP add αἱ, "the days").
- **"Herod Agrippa I"** — retained as proper historical identification; grandson of Herod the Great; died 44 CE.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** modernized throughout.
- **"stretched forth his hands to vex certain of the church" → "laid on his hands to mistreat some of those from the church"** (v1) — Greek ἐπέβαλεν … τὰς χεῖρας κακῶσαί τινας τῶν ἀπὸ τῆς ἐκκλησίας; no word "violent", no "belonged to".
- **"killed" → "did away with"** (v2) — Greek ἀνεῖλεν (ἀναιρέω, "take up, do away with"; as Heb 10:9); "kill" is ἀποκτείνω.
- **"it pleased" → "it is pleasing"; "proceeded further to take" → "went on to apprehend"** (v3) — Greek ἀρεστόν ἐστιν, a present; προσέθετο συλλαβεῖν, literally "he added to apprehend", a Hebrew turn of phrase.
- **"apprehended" → "seized"; "delivered" → "delivering him up"; "quaternions" → "squads of four"; "intending" → "wishing"; "bring him forth" → "bring him up"** (v4) — Greek πιάσας, παραδούς, τετραδίοις, βουλόμενος, ἀναγαγεῖν.
- **"prayer was made without ceasing" → "prayer was being earnestly made"** (v5) — Greek ἐκτενῶς (WH, Treg, NA28, SBLGNT), an adverb; "for him" is περὶ αὐτοῦ, "concerning him".
- **"bring him forth" kept** (v6) — Greek προαγαγεῖν (NA28, SBLGNT); WH προσαγαγεῖν "bring him to them", Treg προάγειν. "Was asleep" is κοιμώμενος. "That very night": Greek "on that night".
- **"prison"** (v7) — rendered "cell": Greek οἴκημα, "a room, a dwelling", a different word from φυλακή "prison" (v4–6, 17).
- **"raised him up" → "roused him"; "Arise up quickly" → "Rise quickly"** (v7) — Greek ἤγειρεν of a sleeper; Ἀνάστα ἐν τάχει.
- **"saith" → "says"** (v8) — Greek λέγει, a present, kept. "Garment" is ἱμάτιον.
- **"the first and the second ward" → "a first guard and a second"** (v10) — Greek πρώτην φυλακὴν καὶ δευτέραν, without article; φυλακή is here a guard post, the same word as "prison". "Of its own accord" → "of itself": αὐτομάτη.
- **"of a surety" → "truly"; "delivered" kept** (v11) — Greek ἀληθῶς; ἐξείλατο (ἐξαιρέω, as Gal 1:4), not ῥύομαι "rescue". "The people of the *Yehudim*" is τοῦ λαοῦ τῶν Ἰουδαίων, a noun, not "Jewish".
- **"when he had considered the thing" → "having become aware of it"** (v12) — Greek συνιδών; "it" is supplied. "Many" → "a considerable number": ἱκανοί. "Whose surname was" → "who is called": ἐπικαλουμένου.
- **"a damsel came to hearken" → "a slave woman came to answer"** (v13) — Greek παιδίσκη, as John 18:17; ὑπακοῦσαι is "to listen, to obey", used of answering a door.
- **"Thou art mad" kept; "constantly affirmed" → "kept insisting"** (v15) — Greek Μαίνῃ; διϊσχυρίζετο.
- **"astonished" → "amazed"** (v16) — Greek ἐξέστησαν.
- **"declared" → "related"; "Go shew" → "Report"** (v17) — Greek διηγήσατο, Ἀπαγγείλατε.
- **"commanded that they should be put to death" → "commanded them to be led away"** (v19) — Greek ἐκέλευσεν ἀπαχθῆναι, "to be led away", that is, to execution; the Greek does not name it. "There" is supplied.
- **"Herod was highly displeased" → "he was furiously angry"** (v20) — the name is not in the Greek (only the Byzantine text adds it); θυμομαχῶν is "fighting in rage". "The people of Tyre and Sidon" is Τυρίοις καὶ Σιδωνίοις, "Tyrians and Sidonians".
- **"the king's chamberlain" → "who was over the bedchamber of the king"** (v20) — Greek τὸν ἐπὶ τοῦ κοιτῶνος τοῦ βασιλέως. "Was nourished from the king's country": τρέφεσθαι … ἀπὸ τῆς βασιλικῆς; no word "depended" or "food".
- **"upon his throne" → "upon the judgment seat"; "made an oration" → "was making a public speech"** (v21) — Greek ἐπὶ τοῦ βήματος (as 2 Cor 5:10); ἐδημηγόρει.
- **"the people" → "the populace"** (v22) — Greek δῆμος, the assembled citizens; a different word from λαός "people" (v4, 11).
- **"immediately" → "instantly"; "smote" → "struck"** (v23) — Greek παραχρῆμα (not εὐθέως, v10); ἐπάταξεν, the verb of v7. "Gave up the ghost" → "expired": ἐξέψυξεν.
- **"the word of God" kept** (v24) — θεοῦ (Treg, NA28, SBLGNT, RP); WH κυρίου, "of the Lord".
- **"returned from Jerusalem, when they had fulfilled their ministry" → "returned, having fulfilled the service unto Jerusalem"** (v25) — the reading is εἰς Ἰερουσαλήμ, "unto Jerusalem" (WH, NA28, SBLGNT, RP); Treg ἐξ, "from", others ἀπό. With εἰς the words can be read "returned to Jerusalem", which does not fit 11:30 and 13:1; the printed text sets a comma after "returned" and joins "unto Jerusalem" to "having fulfilled the service" (the service of 11:29). The verse follows the printed punctuation.
