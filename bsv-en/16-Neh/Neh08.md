# Nehemiah 8

[1] And all the people gathered as one man to the square that was before the Water Gate. And they said to Ezra the scribe to bring the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And Ezra the priest brought the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ before the assembly, from man to woman, and all who had understanding to hear, on the first day of the seventh month.

[3] And he read in it before the square that was before the Water Gate, from the light until the middle of the day, in front of the men and the women and those who had understanding; and the ears of all the people were to the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧.

[4] And Ezra the scribe stood on a tower of wood that they had made for the purpose; and beside him stood ⟦mattithiah|Mattityah|Mattithiah⟧, Shema, ⟦anaiah|Anayah|Anaiah⟧, ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧, ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ and ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ on his right; and on his left ⟦pedaiah|Pedayah|Pedaiah⟧, Mishael, ⟦malchijah|Malkiyah|Malchijah⟧, ⟦hashum|Chashum|Hashum⟧, ⟦hashbaddanah|Chashbaddanah|Hashbaddanah⟧, ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧, Meshullam.

[5] And Ezra opened the book before the eyes of all the people, for he was above all the people; and as he opened it, all the people stood.

[6] And Ezra blessed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the great ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. And all the people answered, "Amen, Amen," with the lifting up of their hands; and they bowed the head and bowed down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, faces to the ground.

[7] And ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ and Bani and ⟦sherebiah|Sherevyah|Sherebiah⟧, ⟦jamin|Yamin|Jamin⟧, ⟦akkub|Aqquv|Akkub⟧, ⟦shabbethai|Shabbetai|Shabbethai⟧, ⟦hodiah|Hodiyah|Hodiah⟧, ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧, ⟦kelita|Qelita|Kelita⟧, ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧, ⟦jozabad|Yozavad|Jozabad⟧, ⟦hanan|Chanan|Hanan⟧, ⟦pelaiah|Pelayah|Pelaiah⟧, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, were making the people understand the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧; and the people were in their place.

[8] And they read in the book, in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, distinctly, and setting the sense, and they gave understanding in the reading.

[9] And ⟦nehemiah|Nechemyah|Nehemiah⟧, he is the Tirshatha, and Ezra the priest, the scribe, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ who were making the people understand, said to all the people, "The day is holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; do not mourn and do not weep." For all the people were weeping as they heard the words of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧.

[10] And he said to them, "Go, eat fat things and drink sweet things, and send portions to him for whom nothing is prepared, for the day is holy to our Lord. And do not be grieved, for the joy of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, it is your stronghold."

[11] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ were quieting all the people, saying, "Hush, for the day is holy, and do not be grieved."

[12] And all the people went to eat and to drink and to send portions and to make great gladness, for they had understood the words that they had made known to them.

[13] And on the second day the heads of the fathers of all the people, the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, gathered to Ezra the scribe, and that to gain insight into the words of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧.

[14] And they found written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ should dwell in booths in the feast in the seventh month,

[15] and that they should make it heard and make a voice pass through all their cities and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying, "Go out to the hill country and bring leaves of olive and leaves of oil tree and leaves of myrtle and leaves of palms and leaves of leafy tree, to make booths, as it is written."

[16] And the people went out and brought, and they made for themselves booths, each on his roof, and in their courts, and in the courts of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and in the square of the Water Gate, and in the square of the Gate of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[17] And all the assembly, those who had returned from the captivity, made booths and dwelt in the booths; for from the days of ⟦joshua|Yeshua|Joshua⟧ son of Nun until that day the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had not done so. And there was very great gladness.

[18] And he read in the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ day by day, from the first day until the last day. And they kept the feast seven days, and on the eighth day a solemn assembly, according to the ordinance.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 6, 9, 10, 14) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6, 8, 9, 16, 18) — Hebrew *Elohim*; with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים) in v6, 8, 16, 18.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"gathered themselves together … into the street" → "gathered … to the square"** (v1, 3, 16) — Hebrew הָרְחוֹב, the broad open place of the town. **"that was before"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לִפְנֵי, no verb; "was" supplied.
- **"the book of the law" / "the Scroll" → "the book of the Torah"** (v1, 3, 5, 8, 18) — Hebrew סֵפֶר, "book", any written document; it was a scroll, but the word is not מְגִלָּה "scroll".
- **"both men and women" → "from man to woman"** (v2) — Hebrew מֵאִישׁ וְעַד־אִשָּׁה. **"all that could hear with understanding" → "all who had understanding to hear"** — Hebrew וְכֹל מֵבִין לִשְׁמֹעַ, "everyone understanding to hear". The one verb בִּין runs through the chapter: "those who had understanding" (v3), "were making … understand" (v7, 9), "gave understanding" (v8), "had understood" (v12).
- **"from the morning until midday" → "from the light until the middle of the day"** (v3) — Hebrew מִן־הָאוֹר עַד־מַחֲצִית הַיּוֹם: "the light" = daybreak. **"before the men" → "in front of the men"** — Hebrew נֶגֶד (לִפְנֵי "before" stands earlier in the verse). **"were attentive unto the book" → "were to the book"** — Hebrew וְאָזְנֵי כָל־הָעָם אֶל־סֵפֶר הַתּוֹרָה, no verb; "were" supplied.
- **"a pulpit of wood" → "a tower of wood"** (v4) — Hebrew מִגְדַּל־עֵץ: מִגְדָּל "tower", here a raised stand of timber. **"for the purpose"** — Hebrew לַדָּבָר, "for the matter". **"Hashbadana, Zechariah, and Meshullam" → "Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, Meshullam"** — no "and" before the last names in the Hebrew.
- **"in the sight of" → "before the eyes of"** (v5) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי. **"stood up" → "stood"** — Hebrew עָמְדוּ.
- **"the great God" (v6)** — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים הַגָּדוֹל, with the article. **"bowed their heads, and worshipped" → "bowed the head and bowed down"** — Hebrew וַיִּקְּדוּ וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲוֻּ: קָדַד "bow the head", הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" to the ground. **"with their faces to the ground" → "faces to the ground"** — Hebrew אַפַּיִם אָרְצָה, literally "nostrils to the earth".
- **"Also Jeshua … and the Levites, caused the people to understand the law" → "And Jeshua and Bani and … and the Levites, were making the people understand the Torah"** (v7) — Hebrew מְבִינִים, a participle; וְהַלְוִיִּם has "and": the names and the Levites, or (if the "and" means "that is") the names as the Levites. The Hebrew has "and" only before Bani, Sherebiah and the Levites. **"stood in their place" → "were in their place"** — Hebrew וְהָעָם עַל־עָמְדָם, "upon their standing", no verb.
- **"So they read in the book in the law of God distinctly, and gave the sense, and caused them to understand the reading" → "And they read in the book, in the Torah of Elohim, distinctly, and setting the sense, and they gave understanding in the reading"** (v8) — word for word. Hebrew מְפֹרָשׁ, a passive participle of פָּרַשׁ "make distinct, declare distinctly" (Lev 24:12; Num 15:34): "made clear", read section by section and explained; the Aramaic form of the same word is used of a letter "translated" or "read clearly" in Ezra 4:18, and Jewish tradition took the verse to speak of translation into Aramaic (the Targum). Both senses are held; the word itself says only "made distinct". וְשׂוֹם שֶׂכֶל, an infinitive, "and setting / putting sense (insight)": who sets it, and how, is not said. וַיָּבִינוּ בַּמִּקְרָא, the hifil of בִּין without an object, "and they caused (them) to understand the reading", or "and they understood the reading" (the people); "gave understanding" keeps the form open. מִקְרָא "reading" (from קָרָא).
- **"Nehemiah, which is the Tirshatha" → "Nehemiah, he is the Tirshatha"** (v9) — Hebrew נְחֶמְיָה הוּא הַתִּרְשָׁתָא: תִּרְשָׁתָא is a Persian title of a governor (its exact sense uncertain; also Ezra 2:63; Neh 7:65, 70; 10:1); פֶּחָה "governor" (5:14) is another word, so the title is kept. **"said"** — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר, singular, before three subjects. **"Ezra the priest the scribe" → "Ezra the priest, the scribe"** — two titles. **"This day is holy" → "The day is holy"** (v9, 10, 11) — Hebrew הַיּוֹם קָדֹשׁ־הוּא, "the day, holy it is"; הַיּוֹם is "the day" or "today". **"mourn not, nor weep" → "do not mourn and do not weep"** — two prohibitions, אַל … וְאַל.
- **"Go your way, eat the fat, and drink the sweet" → "Go, eat fat things and drink sweet things"** (v10) — Hebrew לְכוּ אִכְלוּ מַשְׁמַנִּים וּשְׁתוּ מַמְתַקִּים, plurals without the article. **"unto them for whom nothing is prepared" → "to him for whom nothing is prepared"** — Hebrew לְאֵין נָכוֹן לוֹ, singular. **"holy unto our Lord"** — Hebrew לַאֲדֹנֵינוּ, "to our Lord", of Yahweh; not the form אֲדֹנָי. **"neither be ye sorry" → "do not be grieved"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew תֵּעָצֵבוּ, עָצַב nifal. **"the joy of the LORD is your strength" → "the joy of Yahweh, it is your stronghold"** — Hebrew כִּי־חֶדְוַת יְהוָה הִיא מָעֻזְּכֶם: מָעוֹז is a strong place, a refuge (Judg 6:26; Ps 27:1), not "strength" (עֹז); חֶדְוָה "joy" is a late word (1 Chr 16:27). "The joy of Yahweh" may be the joy he gives or joy in him.
- **"stilled all the people" → "were quieting all the people"** (v11) — Hebrew מַחְשִׁים, a participle. **"Hold your peace" → "Hush"** — Hebrew הַסּוּ.
- **"to make great mirth" → "to make great gladness"** (v12, 17) — Hebrew שִׂמְחָה. **"the words that were declared unto them" → "the words that they had made known to them"** — Hebrew הוֹדִיעוּ, active, plural; the subject is not named.
- **"the chief of the fathers" → "the heads of the fathers"** (v13) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת. **"even to understand the words of the law" → "and that to gain insight into the words of the Torah"** — Hebrew וּלְהַשְׂכִּיל אֶל, הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight"; the וּ before the infinitive ("and that", "namely") is kept.
- **"by Moses" → "by the hand of Moses"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּיַד־מֹשֶׁה. **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** (v14, 17). **"dwell in booths in the feast of the seventh month" → "dwell in booths in the feast in the seventh month"** — Hebrew בַּסֻּכּוֹת בֶּחָג בַּחֹדֶשׁ הַשְּׁבִיעִי; סֻכָּה "booth" (Lev 23:42, 43), חַג "feast". The name of the feast (חַג הַסֻּכּוֹת, Lev 23:34) does not stand here, so the booths are plain words.
- **"they should publish and proclaim" → "they should make it heard and make a voice pass"** (v15) — Hebrew יַשְׁמִיעוּ וְיַעֲבִירוּ קוֹל, as Exod 36:6 "they made a voice pass through the camp". **"Go forth unto the mount" → "Go out to the hill country"** — Hebrew הָהָר, "the mountain", of the hill country around Jerusalem. **"olive branches, and pine branches, and myrtle branches, and palm branches, and branches of thick trees" → "leaves of olive and leaves of oil tree and leaves of myrtle and leaves of palms and leaves of leafy tree"** — Hebrew עֲלֵי, "leaves (foliage) of", five times. עֵץ שֶׁמֶן "oil tree" (1 Kgs 6:23; Isa 41:19): a tree other than the olive, perhaps the oleaster (wild olive) or a resin tree (uncertain); not "pine". עֵץ עָבֹת "leafy tree", as Lev 23:40. **"as it is written"** — Hebrew כַּכָּתוּב; the list is not the list of Lev 23:40, which has "the fruit of a tree of splendor, fronds of palm trees, a bough of a leafy tree, and willows".
- **"every one upon the roof of his house" → "each on his roof"** (v16) — Hebrew אִישׁ עַל־גַּגּוֹ; "of his house" is not written. **"in the street of the gate of Ephraim" → "in the square of the Gate of Ephraim"**.
- **"all the congregation" → "all the assembly"** (v17) — Hebrew כָּל־הַקָּהָל. **"sat under the booths" → "dwelt in the booths"** — Hebrew וַיֵּשְׁבוּ בַסֻּכּוֹת, יָשַׁב "dwell", as v14. **"Jeshua the son of Nun" → "Joshua son of Nun"** — Hebrew יֵשׁוּעַ בִּן־נוּן: the late spelling of the name יְהוֹשֻׁעַ, Moses' successor (in v7 the same spelling names a Levite, Jeshua). **"had not … done so" (v17)** — Hebrew לֹא־עָשׂוּ … כֵּן: booths were made before (Lev 23; 2 Chr 8:13; Ezra 3:4); what had not been done "so" is not explained.
- **"he read" (v18)** — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא, singular; the subject (Ezra, or one) is not named. **"they kept the feast" (v18)** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲשׂוּ־חָג, literally "they made / did a feast". **"a solemn assembly"** — Hebrew עֲצֶרֶת (Lev 23:36; Num 29:35): the word means a day of holding back or a closing gathering (uncertain); "solemn" is customary English, not a word of the Hebrew. **"according unto the manner" → "according to the ordinance"** — Hebrew כַּמִּשְׁפָּט.

### Notes
- **First day of the seventh month** (v2) — the day of shouting (Lev 23:24; Num 29:1). A public reading of the Torah to the whole people.
- **The square before the Water Gate** — an open place outside the temple courts, open to men and women and all who could understand.
- **Ezra on a tower of wood** — raised above the people (v5). Reading from daybreak to midday.
- **The Levites** (v7–8) — they read, made distinct and gave understanding; Jewish tradition sees here the beginning of the Targum (the Aramaic rendering read beside the Hebrew).
- **"The joy of Yahweh is your stronghold"** (v10) — the holy day is kept with feasting, and with portions sent to those who have nothing.
- **Booths** (v14–17) — the feast of the seventh month (Lev 23:33–43), kept "as it is written" after the reading.
- **Daily reading** (v18) — the Torah read on each day of the feast (cf. Deut 31:10–13).
