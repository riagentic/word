## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v11, 16, 22 with the article, בֵּית הָאֱלֹהִים "the house of the Elohim".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses and its list members with וַ / וְ "and"; it is kept, also in the towns of v25–29 ("and in … and in").
- **Names and figures** — the lists are given as the Hebrew prints them; they are not levelled with the parallel list of 1 Chr 9:2–22, which differs in many names and in all the figures (see Notes). Numbers are in words.
- **"dwelt" kept** (throughout) — Hebrew יָשַׁב "dwell" (v1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 25); in v21 the participle יֹשְׁבִים "were dwelling".
- **"the rulers of the people" → "the princes of the people"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי־הָעָם. **"one of ten" → "one of the ten"; "nine parts to dwell in other cities" → "the nine parts in the cities"** — Hebrew אֶחָד מִן־הָעֲשָׂרָה … וְתֵשַׁע הַיָּדוֹת בֶּעָרִים, literally "the nine hands" (as Gen 47:24 "four parts"); "other" and "to dwell" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"willingly offered themselves"** (v2) — Hebrew הַמִּתְנַדְּבִים, hitpael of נָדַב, kept.
- **"the chief of the province" → "the heads of the province"** (v3) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הַמְּדִינָה. **"every one in his possession" → "each man in his possession"** — Hebrew אִישׁ בַּאֲחֻזָּתוֹ. **"the children of Solomon's servants" → "the sons of the servants of Solomon"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי עַבְדֵי שְׁלֹמֹה.
- **"certain of the children of" → "some of the sons of"** (v4) — Hebrew מִבְּנֵי, as 1 Chr 9:3. **"son of"** (throughout the lists) — Hebrew בֶּן־, as 1 Chr 9.
- **"the son of Shiloni" → "the son of the Shilonite"** (v5) — Hebrew בֶּן־הַשִּׁלֹנִי, with the article: a man of Shiloh, or (with other vowels) of the clan of Shelah son of Judah (Num 26:20); the pointing is kept, as 1 Chr 9:5.
- **"valiant men" → "men of ability"** (v6) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי־חָיִל. **"mighty men of valour" → "mighty ones of ability"** (v14) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרֵי חַיִל, as 1 Chr 9:13.
- **"Gabbai, Sallai"** (v8) — Hebrew גַּבַּי סַלָּי, kept as names; a change of letters to גִּבֹּרֵי חַיִל "mighty ones of ability" is often proposed (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"was their overseer: and Judah … was second over the city" → "was overseer over them, and Judah … was over the city, second"** (v9) — Hebrew פָּקִיד עֲלֵיהֶם … עַל־הָעִיר מִשְׁנֶה, no verbs ("was" supplied twice). מִשְׁנֶה is "second": second in rank over the city, or over the Second quarter of the city (2 Kgs 22:14); the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"Jedaiah the son of Joiarib, Jachin"** (v10) — so the Masoretic Text; 1 Chr 9:10 has "Jedaiah and Jehoiarib and Jachin". Kept.
- **"the ruler of the house of God" → "the leader of the house of Elohim"** (v11) — Hebrew נְגִד, as 1 Chr 9:11 (where the name is Azariah).
- **"that did the work of the house" → "who did the work for the house"** (v12) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂי הַמְּלָאכָה לַבַּיִת.
- **"chief of the fathers" → "heads of fathers"** (v13) — Hebrew רָאשִׁים לְאָבוֹת, "heads for fathers".
- **"the son of one of the great men" → "the son of Haggedolim"** (v14) — Hebrew בֶּן־הַגְּדוֹלִים, literally "son of the great ones" (with the article); a name, or "son of the great men" (one of the leading families). The token `haggedolim` is kept.
- **"had the oversight of the outward business" → "were over the outer work"** (v16) — Hebrew עַל־הַמְּלָאכָה הַחִיצֹנָה, "over the outer work": the work outside the house. **"of the chief of the Levites" → "of the heads of the Levites"** — Hebrew מֵרָאשֵׁי, standing at the end of the verse.
- **"the principal to begin the thanksgiving in prayer" → "the head of the beginning, who would give thanks for the prayer"** (v17) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ הַתְּחִלָּה יְהוֹדֶה לַתְּפִלָּה, difficult: הַתְּחִלָּה "the beginning"; a change of two letters gives הַתְּהִלָּה "the praise", "the head of the praise" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. יְהוֹדֶה "he would give thanks", the imperfect of הוֹדָה. **"the second among his brethren" → "second among his brothers"** — Hebrew מִשְׁנֶה מֵאֶחָיו. **"Jeduthun"** — the written text (ketiv) has ידיתון, the qere יְדוּתוּן; the same name.
- **"were two hundred fourscore and four"** (v18) — Hebrew without a verb: "All the Levites in the holy city: two hundred and eighty-four".
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"; "that kept the gates" → "who kept guard at the gates"** (v19) — Hebrew הַשּׁוֹעֲרִים … הַשֹּׁמְרִים בַּשְּׁעָרִים, "those keeping guard in the gates".
- **"every one in his inheritance" → "each man in his inheritance"** (v20) — Hebrew אִישׁ בְּנַחֲלָתוֹ; the verse has no verb ("were" supplied).
- **"the Nethinims dwelt in Ophel" → "the Netinim were dwelling in the Ophel"** (v21) — Hebrew יֹשְׁבִים בָּעֹפֶל, a participle; the Ophel ("the swelling, the hill") with the article.
- **"over the business of the house of God" → "before the work of the house of Elohim"** (v22) — Hebrew לְנֶגֶד מְלֶאכֶת, "in front of, over against the work": in charge of it, or standing before it in their service.
- **"it was the king's commandment concerning them, that a certain portion should be for the singers, due for every day" → "the commandment of the king was upon them, and a firm agreement upon the singers, the matter of a day in its day"** (v23) — Hebrew מִצְוַת הַמֶּלֶךְ עֲלֵיהֶם וַאֲמָנָה עַל־הַמְשֹׁרְרִים דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ. אֲמָנָה is the word of 9:38 ("a firm agreement"), not "portion"; "the matter of a day in its day" as Exod 5:13; 16:4 (what is due each day).
- **"was at the king's hand in all matters concerning the people" → "was at the hand of the king for every matter of the people"** (v24) — Hebrew לְיַד הַמֶּלֶךְ לְכָל־דָּבָר לָעָם, as 1 Chr 18:17 "at the hand of the king".
- **"for the villages, with their fields" → "as for the villages in their fields"** (v25) — Hebrew וְאֶל־הַחֲצֵרִים בִּשְׂדֹתָם, "and to the villages in their fields". **"the villages thereof" → "its daughters"** (v25, 27, 28, 30, 31) — Hebrew בְּנֹתֶיהָ, the towns dependent on a city, as 1 Chr 7:28; "its villages" stays for חֲצֵרֶיהָ (v25, 30). **"Kirjath-arba"** — the Hebrew has the article, קִרְיַת הָאַרְבַּע "the town of the Arba". The towns of v25–29 are each joined by "and in" (וּבְ).
- **"they dwelt from Beer-sheba" → "they camped from Beersheba"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיַּחֲנוּ, of חָנָה "camp", not יָשַׁב. **"the valley of Hinnom"** — Hebrew גֵּיא־הִנֹּם.
- **"The children also of Benjamin from Geba dwelt at Michmash" → "And the sons of Benjamin from Geba: Michmash"** (v31) — Hebrew וּבְנֵי בִנְיָמִן מִגָּבַע מִכְמָשׂ, no verb; "from Geba" may begin the list ("from Geba on") or name where they came from. The towns of v31 are joined by "and".
- **"Lod, and Ono, the valley of craftsmen" → "Lod and Ono, the valley of the craftsmen"** (v35) — Hebrew לֹד וְאוֹנוֹ גֵּי הַחֲרָשִׁים, the place called Ge-harashim in 1 Chr 4:14.
- **"of the Levites were divisions in Judah, and in Benjamin" → "of the Levites: divisions of Judah for Benjamin"** (v36) — Hebrew וּמִן־הַלְוִיִּם מַחְלְקוֹת יְהוּדָה לְבִנְיָמִין, no verb: some Levite divisions that had been in Judah were for (assigned to) Benjamin.

### Notes

- **Repopulation of Jerusalem** — one in ten chosen by lot to dwell in the city; the "nine parts" stay in the cities.
- **"Offered themselves willingly"** (v2) — leaving ancestral lands for the city.
- **Classes** — Judah, Benjamin, priests, Levites, gatekeepers, Netinim; as Ezra 2.
- **Territorial extent** (v25–36) — south to Beersheba, north to Michmash and Bethel; smaller than pre-exilic Judah.
- **"The commandment of the king"** (v23) — a royal provision for the singers; the king is not named (Artaxerxes is the king of the book, 2:1).
- **1 Chr 9:2–22** — a parallel list of those who dwelt in Jerusalem, with other names and other figures (Judah 690 against 468, Benjamin 956 against 928, priests 1,760 against 822 + 242 + 128, gatekeepers 212 against 172).
