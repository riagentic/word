## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; with the article in v24, 36, 40 (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses and many of its names with וְ "and"; it is kept where it stands (v6, 19, 33, 34, 42) and not added where it does not (v8, 41).
- **"Ginnetho" → "Ginnethon"** (v4); **"Miamin" → "Mijamin"** (v5) — minor spelling variants of the names.
- **"and Mattaniah, which was over the thanksgiving" → "Mattaniah; over the thanksgivings was he and his brothers"** (v8) — Hebrew עַל־הַהֻיְּדוֹת הוּא וְאֶחָיו; the noun הֻיְּדוֹת is found only here, from ידה "give thanks": songs or choirs of thanksgiving (uncertain). No "and" before Mattaniah.
- **"were over against them in the watches" → "were opposite them by watches"** (v9) — Hebrew לְנֶגְדָּם לְמִשְׁמָרוֹת; no verb ("were" supplied), so also v24 "opposite them". **"Unni"** — the qere וְעֻנִּי is translated; the written form (ketiv) is וענו "Unno".
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew הוֹלִיד. **"and Eliashib Joiada"** (v10) — the Hebrew has no verb in the third link; nothing is supplied.
- **"chief of the fathers" → "heads of the fathers"** (v12, 23; v22 without the article "heads of fathers") — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת: the heads of the fathers' houses.
- **"Melicu"** (v14) — the qere לִמְלִיכוּ "of Melicu" is translated, as KJV has it; the written form (ketiv) is למלוכי "of Malluchi" (cf. Malluch, v2). The previous text followed the ketiv.
- **"of Iddo"** (v16) — the qere לְעִדּוֹא is translated; the ketiv is לעדיא "Iddi".
- **"of Miniamin, of Moadiah, Piltai" → "of Miniamin; of Moadiah, Piltai"** (v17) — the Hebrew has לְמִנְיָמִין with no name after it: the head of that house has dropped out of the list. Nothing is supplied, and the previous "and of" joining the two houses is removed.
- **"were recorded … also the priests, to the reign of" → "were written … and the priests, in the reign of"** (v22) — Hebrew כְּתוּבִים "written"; עַל־מַלְכוּת, literally "upon the reign": "during" or "up to" (both are possible); the sentence is loose in the Hebrew and is kept so.
- **"were written in the book of the chronicles" → "were written on the book of the chronicles, even until"** (v23) — Hebrew עַל־סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, literally "upon the book of the matters of the days" (as the regnal formulas of Kings); וְעַד "and until".
- **"Jeshua the son of Kadmiel"** (v24) — so the Masoretic Text (בֶּן־קַדְמִיאֵל); v8 has Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel side by side, and many read "Binnui" for בֶּן "son" here (a conjecture: not followed). **"to praise and to give thanks" → "to praise, to give thanks"** — no "and". **"ward over against ward" → "watch alongside watch"** — Hebrew מִשְׁמָר לְעֻמַּת מִשְׁמָר.
- **"keeping the ward at the thresholds of the gates" → "keeping a watch at the stores of the gates"** (v25) — Hebrew בַּאֲסֻפֵּי הַשְּׁעָרִים: אֲסֻפִּים "stores, gatherings", as the "house of the stores" of 1 Chr 26:15, 17 (uncertain); "thresholds" was a guess (that word is סַף). **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v25, 45, 47) — שֹׁעֲרִים.
- **"Ezra the priest, the scribe"** (v26) — two nouns side by side.
- **"sought the Levites" → "sought the Levites out of all their places"** (v27) — בִּקֵּשׁ "seek". **"to keep the dedication with gladness, both with thanksgivings, and with singing" → "to make a dedication and gladness, and with thanksgivings and with song"** — Hebrew לַעֲשֹׂת חֲנֻכָּה וְשִׂמְחָה וּבְתוֹדוֹת וּבְשִׁיר, word for word. **"psalteries" → "harps"; "harps" → "lyres"** — נְבָלִים, כִּנֹּרוֹת.
- **"the plain country round about" → "the circle around"** (v28) — Hebrew הַכִּכָּר "the round (district)", the word of the circle of the Jordan (Gen 13:10). **"gathered themselves together" → "gathered themselves"** — nifal. **"the villages of Netophathi"** — the Hebrew is singular, "of the Netophathite" (a collective).
- **"the house of Gilgal" → "Beth-gilgal"** (v29) — the token as the lexicon has it; the Hebrew is מִבֵּית הַגִּלְגָּל "from the house of the Gilgal".
- **"purified" → "cleansed"** (v30) — Hebrew טָהֵר hitpael / piel, "cleanse oneself", "cleanse"; so "the cleansing" in v45 (טָהֳרָה).
- **"princes"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי יְהוּדָה; "officials" of the previous text is not the word.
- **"appointed two great companies of them that gave thanks, whereof one went on the right hand" → "made stand two great thanksgivings and processions, to the right"** (v31) — Hebrew וָאַעֲמִידָה שְׁתֵּי תוֹדֹת גְּדוֹלֹת וְתַהֲלֻכֹת לַיָּמִין: תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving" stands for a choir that gives thanks (so v38, 40); תַּהֲלֻכֹת is found only here, "processions" (from הָלַךְ "go"). "Whereof one went" is not in the Masoretic Text; it is a common correction (וְהָאַחַת הֹלֶכֶת "and the one going"): not followed. **"upon the wall"** (v31, 38) — מֵעַל לַחוֹמָה, literally "from above to the wall".
- **"some of the priests' sons with trumpets"** (v35) — no verb ("carried" removed); חֲצֹצְרוֹת "trumpets", the straight metal trumpet.
- **"with the musical instruments" → "with the instruments of song"** (v36) — כְּלֵי־שִׁיר. **"before them"** — לִפְנֵיהֶם.
- **"at the fountain gate, which was over against them" → "at the Spring Gate, and straight before them"** (v37) — Hebrew וְעַל שַׁעַר הָעַיִן וְנֶגְדָּם; עַיִן "spring". **"even unto the water gate eastward" → "and to the Water Gate eastward"**.
- **"went over against them" → "was going to the opposite side"** (v38) — Hebrew הַהוֹלֶכֶת לְמוֹאל, from מוּל "in front, opposite": the other direction around the wall. **"I after them" → "I after it"** — אַחֲרֶיהָ, feminine: the thanksgiving choir.
- **"the old gate" → "the Gate of the Old"** (v39) — שַׁעַר הַיְשָׁנָה, "the gate of the old (city)" (the article favours the adjective) or a proper name (Yeshanah); as 3:6. Gate names as Neh 3 (Gate of the Ash Heaps, Spring Gate, Flock Gate). **"from above the gate of Ephraim, and above the old gate, and above the fish gate" → "above the Gate of Ephraim, and over the Gate of the Old, and over the Fish Gate"** — מֵעַל (above) then עַל (over, by). **"prison gate" → "Gate of the Guard"** — שַׁעַר הַמַּטָּרָה. **"stood still"** — עָמְדוּ.
- **"rulers" → "prefects"** (v40) — Hebrew סְגָנִים, an Akkadian title of officers under a governor; one word in Ezra–Nehemiah (2:16; 13:11).
- **"sang loud, with Jezrahiah their overseer" → "sounded, and Jezrahiah the overseer"** (v42) — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁמִיעוּ הַמְשֹׁרְרִים, "made (it) heard"; "loud" and "their" are not written; פָּקִיד "overseer".
- **"offered great sacrifices" → "sacrificed great sacrifices"** (v43) — זָבַח זְבָחִים, verb and noun of one root. **"joy" → "gladness"** — שִׂמְחָה (so v27, 44). **"children"** — יְלָדִים.
- **"for the treasures" → "for the treasuries"** (v44) — אוֹצָרוֹת. **"offerings" → "contributions"** — תְּרוּמוֹת. **"firstfruits" → "the first"** — רֵאשִׁית (not בִּכּוּרִים). **"out of the fields of the cities the portions of the law" → "by the fields of the cities, the portions of the torah"** — לִשְׂדֵי הֶעָרִים, literally "for the fields": field by field (or "from"; uncertain); מְנָאוֹת הַתּוֹרָה "the portions of the torah", that is, set by it. **"Judah rejoiced for the priests … that waited" → "the gladness of Judah was over the priests … who stood"** — a noun, no verb; הָעֹמְדִים "who stood" (= served).
- **"the ward of their God, and the ward of the purification" → "the charge of their Elohim and the charge of the cleansing"** (v45) — מִשְׁמֶרֶת. **"of David, and of Solomon his son" → "of David, of Solomon his son"** — no "and" in the Hebrew.
- **"of old there were chief of the singers" → "of old, there were heads of the singers"** (v46) — the qere רָאשֵׁי "heads" is translated; the ketiv ראש "head" (singular) would make Asaph "head of the singers". **"and songs of praise and thanksgiving" → "and song of praise and thanksgivings"** — שִׁיר singular, הֹדוֹת plural (or an infinitive, "giving thanks").
- **"gave the portions … every day his portion" → "were giving the portions … the matter of a day in its day"** (v47) — participle; דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ, as 2 Chr 31:16. **"sanctified holy things unto the Levites" → "were sanctifying for the Levites"** — מַקְדִּשִׁים, no object.

### Notes
- **Priestly and Levitical lineages** — three generations: Zerubbabel/Jeshua era, Joiakim era, Eliashib-Johanan-Jaddua era. Spans roughly 150 years (late 6th to mid-4th century BC).
- **Jaddua** (v11, 22) — last high priest mentioned; his era overlaps Alexander the Great (ca. 330 BC) per Josephus. Indicates Nehemiah contains some later editorial notes.
- **Wall dedication procession** — two choirs circuit wall in opposite directions, meeting at temple. Symbolic encircling ritual.
- **Nehemiah with one choir** ("I after it") — memoir-style; v31, 38, 40 preserve first-person voice.
- **Tithe infrastructure** (v44-47) — storehouse system organized; tithes flow from fields to temple.
- **David/Solomon's ordinance** — Chronistic pattern of Davidic temple-music and temple-administration as enduring institutional foundation.
