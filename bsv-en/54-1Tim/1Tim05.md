# 1 Timothy 5

[1] Do not upbraid an elder, but exhort him as a father, younger men as brothers,

[2] elder women as mothers, younger women as sisters, in all purity.

[3] Honor widows who are really widows.

[4] But if any widow has children or descendants, let them learn first to show godliness toward their own house and to render returns to their forebears, for this is acceptable before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] But she who is really a widow and left alone has hoped upon ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and remains in the supplications and the prayers night and day;

[6] but she who lives in indulgence is dead while living.

[7] And these things charge, that they may be irreproachable;

[8] but if anyone does not provide for his own, and especially those of his house, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.

[9] Let a widow be enrolled, being not less than sixty years old, a wife of one husband,

[10] testified to in good works: if she brought up children, if she received strangers, if she washed saints' feet, if she relieved the afflicted, if she followed after every good work.

[11] But younger widows refuse; for when they grow wanton against ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, they want to marry,

[12] having a sentence, because they set aside the first faith;

[13] and at the same time they also learn to be idle, going about the houses, and not only idle but also babblers and busybodies, speaking the things they must not.

[14] I wish therefore that younger ones marry, bear children, rule the house, give no occasion to the one who opposes, for the sake of reviling;

[15] for already some were turned aside after Satan.

[16] If any faithful woman has widows, let her relieve them, and let the church not be burdened, that it may relieve those who are really widows.

[17] Let the elders who lead well be deemed worthy of double honor, especially those who labor in word and doctrine;

[18] for the Scripture says, "**You shall not muzzle a threshing ox**," and, "**The worker is worthy of his wage**."

[19] Against an elder do not admit an accusation, except upon two or three witnesses;

[20] those who sin reprove before all, that the rest also may have fear.

[21] I solemnly testify before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and the elect angels, that you guard these things without prejudgment, doing nothing according to partiality.

[22] Lay hands quickly on no one, nor share in the sins of others; keep yourself pure.

[23] No longer be a drinker of water, but use a little wine because of the stomach and your frequent weaknesses.

[24] The sins of some people are plainly evident, going before unto judgment, but some they also follow after;

[25] likewise also the good works are plainly evident, and those that are otherwise cannot be hidden.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 5, 21) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v21) — systematic name restoration; "the Lord" (KJV) is not in the critical text.
- **"church"** (v16) — Greek ἐκκλησία *ekklēsia*, "assembly/congregation."
- **"Rebuke not an elder, but intreat him" → "Do not upbraid an elder, but exhort him"** (v1) — Greek μὴ ἐπιπλήξῃς, "strike at" with words, only here; "rebuke" renders ἐπιτιμάω (2 Tim 4:2). παρακάλει, "exhort", as 1:3. "Him" is supplied.
- **"elder" kept; "the elder women" → "elder women"** (v1, 2) — Greek πρεσβυτέρῳ, πρεσβυτέρας, the same word as "elders" in v17, 19; beside "younger" it speaks of age.
- **"widows that are widows indeed" → "widows who are really widows"** (v3, 5, 16) — Greek τὰς ὄντως χήρας; ὄντως again in 6:19.
- **"nephews" → "descendants"** (v4) — Greek ἔκγονα, offspring; in 1611 "nephews" meant grandchildren.
- **"to shew piety at home, and to requite their parents" → "to show godliness toward their own house and to render returns to their forebears"** (v4) — Greek τὸν ἴδιον οἶκον εὐσεβεῖν, the verb of "godliness"; ἀμοιβὰς ἀποδιδόναι τοῖς προγόνοις, the word of 2 Tim 1:3 ("forefathers"), here of living parents and grandparents. "Let them learn": the children and descendants, or such widows.
- **"good and acceptable" → "acceptable"** (v4) — Greek ἀπόδεκτον, as 2:3; "good and" is not in the critical text.
- **"desolate, trusteth in God, and continueth in" → "left alone has hoped upon *Elohim* and remains in"** (v5) — Greek μεμονωμένη; ἤλπικεν ἐπὶ θεόν, perfect, as 4:10; προσμένει, as 1:3. "The supplications and the prayers": both with the article.
- **"she that liveth in pleasure" → "she who lives in indulgence"** (v6) — Greek σπαταλῶσα, as Jas 5:5.
- **"give in charge" → "charge"; "blameless" → "irreproachable"** (v7) — Greek παράγγελλε; ἀνεπίλημπτοι, as 3:2. "They" can be the widows or all those named.
- **"for those of his own house" → "those of his house"; "worse than an infidel" → "worse than an unbeliever"** (v8) — Greek οἰκείων; ἀπίστου.
- **"Let not a widow be taken into the number under threescore years old" → "Let a widow be enrolled, being not less than sixty years old"** (v9) — Greek καταλεγέσθω, "be put on the list".
- **"having been the wife of one man" → "a wife of one husband"** (v9) — Greek ἑνὸς ἀνδρὸς γυνή, the counterpart of 3:2; there is no verb.
- **"well reported of for good works" → "testified to in good works"** (v10) — Greek ἐν ἔργοις καλοῖς μαρτυρουμένη.
- **"lodged strangers" → "received strangers"; "diligently followed" → "followed after"** (v10) — Greek ἐξενοδόχησεν; ἐπηκολούθησεν, the same verb as in v24.
- **"when they have begun to wax wanton against Christ, they will marry" → "when they grow wanton against the *Mashiach*, they want to marry"** (v11) — Greek καταστρηνιάσωσιν τοῦ Χριστοῦ, only here: to be driven by strong desire against someone; γαμεῖν θέλουσιν.
- **"having damnation, because they have cast off their first faith" → "having a sentence, because they set aside the first faith"** (v12) — Greek κρίμα, as 3:6; ἠθέτησαν; πίστις is "faith", and also a pledge given. ὅτι is "because" or "that".
- **"they learn to be idle"** (v13) — Greek ἀργαὶ μανθάνουσιν, "idle, they learn"; "to be" is supplied.
- **"wandering about from house to house" → "going about the houses"; "tattlers" → "babblers"** (v13) — Greek περιερχόμεναι τὰς οἰκίας; φλύαροι.
- **"the younger women" → "younger ones"** (v14) — Greek νεωτέρας, feminine: younger women, or younger widows (v11).
- **"I will" → "I wish"** (v14) — Greek βούλομαι, as 2:8; not θέλω "want" (v11).
- **"guide the house" → "rule the house"** (v14) — Greek οἰκοδεσποτεῖν, "be master of a house" (δεσπότης, 6:1).
- **"the adversary" → "the one who opposes"; "to speak reproachfully" → "for the sake of reviling"** (v14) — Greek τῷ ἀντικειμένῳ, as 1:10; λοιδορίας χάριν.
- **"If any man or woman that believeth have widows" → "If any faithful woman has widows"** (v16) — Greek εἴ τις πιστή; "man or" is not in the critical text; πιστή is "faithful" or "believing". The verse has no word for "relatives".
- **"relieve" kept** (v10, 16) — Greek ἐπαρκέω, three times.
- **"the elders that rule well be counted worthy" → "the elders who lead well be deemed worthy"** (v17) — Greek προεστῶτες, as 3:4; ἀξιούσθωσαν; "count" renders ἡγέομαι (6:1). τιμή is "honor" and also "price, pay".
- **"in the word and doctrine" → "in word and doctrine"** (v17) — Greek ἐν λόγῳ καὶ διδασκαλίᾳ, no article.
- **"the ox that treadeth out the corn" → "a threshing ox"; "The labourer is worthy of his reward" → "The worker is worthy of his wage"** (v18) — Greek Βοῦν ἀλοῶντα; ἐργάτης, as 2 Tim 2:15; μισθοῦ.
- **"receive not an accusation, but before two or three witnesses" → "do not admit an accusation, except upon two or three witnesses"** (v19) — Greek μὴ παραδέχου; ἐπὶ δύο ἢ τριῶν μαρτύρων, the wording of Deut 19:15 in the Septuagint.
- **"Them that sin rebuke" → "those who sin reprove"** (v20) — Greek τοὺς ἁμαρτάνοντας, present participle; ἔλεγχε, "reprove", as Titus 1:9.
- **"I charge thee" → "I solemnly testify"** (v21) — Greek διαμαρτύρομαι, as 2 Tim 2:14; 4:1; "charge" renders παραγγέλλω (v7).
- **"observe these things without preferring one before another" → "guard these things without prejudgment"** (v21) — Greek φυλάξῃς; χωρὶς προκρίματος, a judgment made beforehand.
- **"by partiality" → "according to partiality"** (v21) — Greek κατὰ πρόσκλισιν, "leaning toward".
- **"Lay hands suddenly on no man, neither be partaker of other men's sins" → "Lay hands quickly on no one, nor share in the sins of others"** (v22) — Greek ταχέως; κοινώνει, the verb of "fellowship".
- **"Drink no longer water" → "No longer be a drinker of water"** (v23) — Greek ὑδροπότει, one verb, "be a water-drinker".
- **"for thy stomach's sake and thine often infirmities" → "because of the stomach and your frequent weaknesses"** (v23) — Greek διὰ τὸν στόμαχον, without "your" in the critical text; ἀσθενείας, "weaknesses", here of bodily sickness.
- **"open beforehand" → "plainly evident"; "manifest beforehand" → "plainly evident"** (v24, 25) — Greek πρόδηλοι, πρόδηλα, one word: evident before all, or evident beforehand.
- **"going before to judgment; and some men they follow after" → "going before unto judgment, but some they also follow after"** (v24) — Greek προάγουσαι, as 1:18; τισὶν δὲ καὶ ἐπακολουθοῦσιν: "some" is some people.

### Notes
- **v18** quotes Deut 25:4 + Luke 10:7.
