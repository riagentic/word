# Jeremiah 46

[1] That which came as the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet concerning the nations.

[2] For ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧: concerning the force of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ ⟦neco|Necho|Neco⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, which was by the river ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧ at ⟦carchemish|Karkemish|Carchemish⟧, which ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ struck in the fourth year of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[3] Set in order shield and large shield,
and draw near to the battle.

[4] Harness the horses,
and mount up, horsemen,
and take your stand in helmets;
polish the spears,
put on the armor.

[5] Why have I seen?
They are dismayed, turning back behind,
and their mighty ones are beaten down,
and a flight they have fled, and they have not turned;
terror on every side, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] Let not the swift flee,
and let not the mighty one escape;
northward, alongside the river ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧,
they have stumbled and fallen.

[7] Who is this that comes up like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧,

[8] ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ comes up like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧,
and like the rivers the waters heave;
and he said, I will go up, I will cover the earth,
I will make a city perish and those who dwell in it.

[9] Go up, horses,
and rave, chariots,
and let the mighty ones go out:
⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ and Put, who handle the shield,
and the Ludim, who handle, who tread the bow.

[10] And that day is for ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts
a day of vengeance, to avenge himself on his adversaries;
and the sword shall devour and be sated
and drink its fill of their blood;
for there is a sacrifice for ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts
in the land of the north, by the river ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧.

[11] Go up to ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and take balm,
virgin daughter of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
in vain you have multiplied remedies;
healing there is none for you.

[12] Nations have heard your disgrace,
and of your cry the earth is full;
for mighty one against mighty one they have stumbled,
together they have fallen, both of them.

[13] The word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet about the coming of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ to strike the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[14] Tell in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and make heard in Migdol,
and make heard in ⟦memphis|Nof|Memphis⟧ and in ⟦tahpanhes|Tachpanches|Tahpanhes⟧;
say, Take your stand and prepare yourself,
for the sword has devoured around you.

[15] Why are your mighty ones swept away?
He did not stand,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ pushed him.

[16] He has made the stumbling one many;
also each man has fallen upon his neighbor,
and they said, Arise, and let us return to our people
and to the land of our kindred,
from the face of the oppressing sword.

[17] They called out there:
⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ is a tumult;
he has let the appointed time pass.

[18] As I live, declares the King,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name:
surely like ⟦tabor|Tavor|Tabor⟧ among the mountains,
and like ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ by the sea, he shall come.

[19] Make yourself gear for exile,
dweller, daughter of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
for ⟦memphis|Nof|Memphis⟧ shall become a thing that appalls,
and shall be laid in ruins, without inhabitant.

[20] A heifer, very beautiful, is ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
a gadfly from the north has come, has come.

[21] Also her hired men in her midst
are like calves of the stall;
for they too have turned, they have fled together,
they did not stand;
for the day of their calamity has come upon them,
the time of their visitation.

[22] Her sound goes like the serpent;
for in force they go,
and with axes they come to her,
like hewers of wood.

[23] They have cut down her forest, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for it will not be searched out;

and there is no number to them.

[24] The daughter of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ has been put to shame;
she has been given into the hand of a people of the north.

[25] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, has said: Behold, I am visiting upon Amon of ⟦thebes|No|Thebes⟧, and upon ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and upon ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and upon her gods and upon her kings, and upon ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and upon those who trust in him.

[26] And I will give them into the hand of those who seek their soul, and into the hand of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and into the hand of his servants; and afterward it shall abide as in the days of old, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[27] And you, do not fear, my servant ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,

[28] You, do not fear, my servant ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

and of you I will not make a full end;

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v18, 25) — יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"Lord GOD of hosts" → "the Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v10, twice) — אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת, both names.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v25) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Layout** — v1–2, 13, 25–26 prose; v3–12, 14–24, 27–28 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents. The quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where the speech of the prophet, of Yahweh and of the fleeing men begins and ends.
- **"The word of the LORD which came to Jeremiah the prophet against the Gentiles" → "That which came as the word of Yahweh to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the nations"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הָיָה דְבַר־יְהוָה, "what came (as) the word of Yahweh" (the heading form of 14:1; 47:1; 49:34); עַל "concerning" (or "against"); גּוֹיִם "nations".
- **"Against Egypt, against the army of Pharaohnecho … which Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon smote" → "For Egypt: concerning the force of Pharaoh Neco … which Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon struck"** (v2) — Hebrew לְמִצְרַיִם "for / of Egypt" (a heading); חַיִל "force" (of an army, its row); הִכָּה "strike". The battle of Carchemish, 605 BC.
- **"Order ye the buckler and shield, and draw near to battle" → "Set in order shield and large shield, and draw near to the battle"** (v3) — Hebrew עָרַךְ "set in order" (as עָרַךְ מִלְחָמָה "draw up for battle"); מָגֵן "shield", צִנָּה "large shield" (as 1 Sam 17:7; 2 Chr 11:12); נָגַשׁ "draw near"; לַמִּלְחָמָה with the article.
- **"get up, ye horsemen, and stand forth with your helmets; furbish the spears, and put on the brigandines" → "and mount up, horsemen, and take your stand in helmets; polish the spears, put on the armor"** (v4) — Hebrew עֲלוּ "go up" (on the horses: "mount up"); פָּרָשִׁים "horsemen" (the word may also mean "steeds": "mount the steeds"); הִתְיַצֵּב "take one's stand"; בְּכוֹבָעִים "in helmets"; מָרַק "polish" (scour); רְמָחִים "spears"; סִרְיֹנֹת plural of סִרְיוֹן (= שִׁרְיוֹן) "armor".
- **"Wherefore have I seen them dismayed and turned away back? and their mighty ones are beaten down, and are fled apace, and look not back: for fear was round about" → "Why have I seen? They are dismayed, turning back behind, and their mighty ones are beaten down, and a flight they have fled, and they have not turned; terror on every side"** (v5) — Hebrew מַדּוּעַ רָאִיתִי, "why have I seen?", set off by the accents from הֵמָּה חַתִּים "they are dismayed" (חָתַת "be dismayed", lit. "be shattered"); נְסֹגִים אָחוֹר "turning back behind"; גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones" (its row; also v6, 9, 12); יֻכַּתּוּ "are beaten down" (כָּתַת "beat in pieces"); וּמָנוֹס נָסוּ, "and a flight they have fled" (noun and verb of one root, נוּס); הִפְנוּ "turn" (to face the enemy, or to look back); מָגוֹר מִסָּבִיב "terror on every side" (its row; as 6:25; 20:3, 10).
- **"Let not the swift flee away, nor the mighty man escape; they shall stumble, and fall toward the north by the river Euphrates" → "Let not the swift flee, and let not the mighty one escape; northward, alongside the river Euphrates, they have stumbled and fallen"** (v6) — Hebrew אַל + jussive twice, "let not" (a wish or a forbidding, not "cannot"); נוּס "flee", נִמְלַט "escape"; צָפוֹנָה "northward"; עַל־יַד "alongside" (lit. "on the hand of"); כָּשְׁלוּ וְנָפָלוּ perfects, "they have stumbled and fallen".
- **"Who is this that cometh up as a flood, whose waters are moved as the rivers?" → "Who is this that comes up like the Nile, like the rivers his waters heave?"** (v7–8) — Hebrew עָלָה "come / go up" (of the Nile in flood, and of Egypt: one verb in v7, 8, 9, 11); יְאֹר "the Nile" (token); הִתְגָּעֵשׁ "heave" (as 2 Sam 22:8; 5:22 of the sea); "his waters" (v7: מֵימָיו), "the waters" (v8: מָיִם). **"I will go up, and will cover the earth; I will destroy the city and the inhabitants thereof" → "I will go up, I will cover the earth, I will make a city perish and those who dwell in it"** (v8) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ without article ("land" or "earth"; "earth" for the flood that covers); הֶאֱבִיד "make perish" (its row); עִיר singular "a city", וְיֹשְׁבֵי בָהּ "and those who dwell in it".
- **"Come up, ye horses; and rage, ye chariots; and let the mighty men come forth; the Ethiopians and the Libyans, that handle the shield; and the Lydians, that handle and bend the bow" → "Go up, horses, and rave, chariots, and let the mighty ones go out: Cush and Put, who handle the shield, and the Ludim, who handle, who tread the bow"** (v9) — Hebrew הִתְהֹלְלוּ "rave" (act madly, as 1 Sam 21:13; Nah 2:4); רֶכֶב "chariots" (collective); יֵצְאוּ jussive "let … go out"; כּוּשׁ וּפוּט "Cush and Put" (the lands, as the Hebrew names them; KJV "Ethiopians", "Libyans"); לוּדִים "the Ludim" (a people named in Gen 10:13; KJV "Lydians"); תֹּפְשֵׂי "who handle" (תָּפַשׂ "seize, grasp", as Gen 4:21 "all who handle the lyre"), and in the last line twice, "who handle, who tread the bow": דָּרַךְ קֶשֶׁת "tread the bow" (its row: the bow is bent with the foot).
- **"For this is the day of the Lord GOD of hosts, a day of vengeance, that he may avenge him of his adversaries: and the sword shall devour, and it shall be satiate and made drunk with their blood: for the Lord GOD of hosts hath a sacrifice" → "And that day is for the Lord Yahweh of Hosts a day of vengeance, to avenge himself on his adversaries; and the sword shall devour and be sated and drink its fill of their blood; for there is a sacrifice for the Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהַיּוֹם הַהוּא "and that day" (not "this is"); לְ "for, belonging to" (twice); נְקָמָה "vengeance", לְהִנָּקֵם מִן "to avenge himself on" (lit. "from"); צָרִים "adversaries" (its row); שָׂבַע "be sated"; רָוָה "drink its fill" (be saturated; the piel "drench", Isa 16:9); זֶבַח "sacrifice"; בְּאֶרֶץ צָפוֹן "in the land of the north"; אֶל "by" (lit. "to").
- **"in vain shalt thou use many medicines; for thou shalt not be cured" → "in vain you have multiplied remedies; healing there is none for you"** (v11) — the qere הִרְבֵּית "you have multiplied" (feminine, to the "virgin daughter"); the written הרביתי is the older form of the same ending (or "I have multiplied"); לַשָּׁוְא "in vain" (lit. "for emptiness", as the שָׁוְא row); רְפֻאוֹת "remedies"; תְּעָלָה "healing" (the new flesh over a wound; the word is rare: "healing" is the best-supported sense); אֵין לָךְ "there is none for you". בְּתוּלַת בַּת־מִצְרַיִם, lit. "virgin of the daughter of Egypt": "virgin daughter" kept; no "O".
- **"The nations have heard of thy shame, and thy cry hath filled the land: for the mighty man hath stumbled against the mighty" → "Nations have heard your disgrace, and of your cry the earth is full; for mighty one against mighty one they have stumbled, together they have fallen, both of them"** (v12) — Hebrew גּוֹיִם without article; קָלוֹן "disgrace" (reviewer row); צְוָחָה "cry" (a loud cry of distress; not צְעָקָה "outcry"); מָלְאָה "is full"; כָּשָׁלוּ plural "they have stumbled"; יַחְדָּיו נָפְלוּ שְׁנֵיהֶם "together they have fallen, both of them".
- **"how Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon should come and smite the land of Egypt" → "about the coming of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon to strike the land of Egypt"** (v13) — Hebrew לָבוֹא … לְהַכּוֹת, two infinitives, "for the coming … to strike".
- **"Declare ye in Egypt, and publish in Migdol … Stand fast, and prepare thee; for the sword shall devour round about thee" → "Tell in Egypt, and make heard in Migdol … Take your stand and prepare yourself, for the sword has devoured around you"** (v14) — Hebrew הִגִּיד "tell" (its row), הִשְׁמִיעַ "make heard" (its row; twice); נֹף "Memphis" (token; Hebrew *Nof*); the commands to Egypt are singular, הִתְיַצֵּב "take your stand" (its row), וְהָכֵן לָךְ "and prepare for yourself"; אָכְלָה perfect "has devoured" (the prophet sees it done); סְבִיבֶיךָ "around you" (or "those around you").
- **"Why are thy valiant men swept away? they stood not, because the LORD did drive them" → "Why are your mighty ones swept away? He did not stand, for Yahweh pushed him"** (v15) — Hebrew נִסְחַף "swept away" (singular verb); אַבִּירֶיךָ "your mighty ones" as printed (the plural form of אַבִּיר "mighty one", which is also said of bulls, Ps 22:12; 50:13: its row); the verbs and the suffix after it are singular, עָמַד "he did not stand", הֲדָפוֹ "pushed him" (הָדַף "push", its row): the number is left as printed. Many manuscripts read the singular "your mighty one" (from memory). The Septuagint divides נִסְחַף as נָס חַף, "Apis has fled", and reads "your chosen calf" (from memory: the bull of Memphis); the MT is kept.
- **"He made many to fall, yea, one fell upon another … let us go again to our own people, and to the land of our nativity, from the oppressing sword" → "He has made the stumbling one many; also each man has fallen upon his neighbor … let us return to our people and to the land of our kindred, from the face of the oppressing sword"** (v16) — Hebrew הִרְבָּה כּוֹשֵׁל, "he has multiplied the one stumbling" (the subject is Yahweh of v15; כּוֹשֵׁל participle, singular collective); נָפַל אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ "each man has fallen upon his neighbor" (its row); קוּמָה "arise" (the long form); אֶרֶץ מוֹלַדְתֵּנוּ "the land of our kindred" (its row); מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (its row: of fleeing); חֶרֶב הַיּוֹנָה "the oppressing sword" (יוֹנָה participle of יָנָה "oppress", as 25:38; 50:16; the same letters spell "the dove").
- **"They did cry there, Pharaoh king of Egypt is but a noise; he hath passed the time appointed" → "They called out there: Pharaoh king of Egypt is a tumult; he has let the appointed time pass"** (v17) — Hebrew קָרְאוּ שָׁם "they called out there" as the MT points it; the versions read שֵׁם, "call the name of Pharaoh …" (from memory), which the old text followed ("Call the name of Pharaoh 'Noisy One Who Missed His Chance'"); the MT is kept. שָׁאוֹן "tumult" (reviewer row); הֶעֱבִיר "make / let pass over" (its row); מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (its row).
- **"As I live, saith the King, whose name is the LORD of hosts, Surely as Tabor is among the mountains, and as Carmel by the sea, so shall he come" → "As I live, declares the King, Yahweh of Hosts is his name: surely like Tabor among the mountains, and like Carmel by the sea, he shall come"** (v18) — Hebrew נְאֻם "declares" (its row); יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת שְׁמוֹ "Yahweh of Hosts is his name" (a clause of its own; not "whose"); כִּי after the oath "surely"; no "so"; יָבוֹא "he shall come" (the one coming is not named).
- **"O thou daughter dwelling in Egypt, furnish thyself to go into captivity: for Noph shall be waste and desolate without an inhabitant" → "Make yourself gear for exile, dweller, daughter of Egypt; for Memphis shall become a thing that appalls, and shall be laid in ruins, without inhabitant"** (v19) — Hebrew כְּלֵי גוֹלָה "gear of exile" (כְּלִי "article, gear": what a man carries; as Ezek 12:3); יוֹשֶׁבֶת בַּת־מִצְרַיִם "dweller, daughter of Egypt" (feminine, singular); שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (its row); נִצְּתָה "be laid in ruins" (נָצָה, as 4:7; or "be burned", from יָצַת, as 2:15: uncertain); מֵאֵין יוֹשֵׁב "without inhabitant".
- **"Egypt is like a very fair heifer, but destruction cometh; it cometh out of the north" → "A heifer, very beautiful, is Egypt; a gadfly from the north has come, has come"** (v20) — Hebrew יְפֵה־פִיָּה, a doubled form of יָפֶה "beautiful": "very beautiful"; קֶרֶץ only here: "gadfly" (a stinging insect that drives cattle, set against the heifer: the best-supported sense; uncertain); בָּא בָא "has come, has come" as printed (many manuscripts read בָּא בָהּ "has come upon her", from memory).
- **"Also her hired men are in the midst of her like fatted bullocks … because the day of their calamity was come upon them, and the time of their visitation" → "Also her hired men in her midst are like calves of the stall; for they too have turned, they have fled together, they did not stand; for the day of their calamity has come upon them, the time of their visitation"** (v21) — Hebrew שְׂכִירֶיהָ "her hired men" (its row); עֶגְלֵי מַרְבֵּק "calves of the stall" (fattened); כִּי־גַם הֵמָּה "for they too"; אֵיד "calamity" (its row); פְּקֻדָּה "visitation" (its row).
- **"The voice thereof shall go like a serpent; for they shall march with an army, and come against her with axes, as hewers of wood" → "Her sound goes like the serpent; for in force they go, and with axes they come to her, like hewers of wood"** (v22) — Hebrew קוֹלָהּ "her sound" (קוֹל of a movement: its row); כַּנָּחָשׁ "like the serpent" (hissing as it slips away); בְּחַיִל "in force" (its row); קַרְדֻּמּוֹת "axes"; חֹטְבֵי עֵצִים "hewers of wood" (its row).
- **"They shall cut down her forest … though it cannot be searched; because they are more than the grasshoppers, and are innumerable" → "They have cut down her forest … for it will not be searched out; for they are more than the locusts, and there is no number to them"** (v23) — Hebrew כָּרְתוּ perfect; כִּי לֹא יֵחָקֵר "for (or "though") it will not be searched out" (חָקַר "search out", reviewer row: the forest is too thick to be gone through); אַרְבֶּה "locusts" (its row); וְאֵין לָהֶם מִסְפָּר "and there is no number to them".
- **"The daughter of Egypt shall be confounded; she shall be delivered into the hand of the people of the north" → "The daughter of Egypt has been put to shame; she has been given into the hand of a people of the north"** (v24) — Hebrew הֹבִישָׁה, perfect "has been put to shame" (בּוֹשׁ, its row); נִתְּנָה בְּיַד "has been given into the hand of"; עַם־צָפוֹן without article.
- **"Behold, I will punish the multitude of No, and Pharaoh, and Egypt …" → "Behold, I am visiting upon Amon of No, and upon Pharaoh …"** (v25) — Hebrew הִנְנִי פוֹקֵד אֶל … וְעַל, participle "I am visiting upon" (פָּקַד "visit", its row; "punish" is the sense); אָמוֹן מִנֹּא "Amon of No", the god of Thebes (KJV "the multitude", reading הָמוֹן); נֹא "No" (token: Thebes); every "upon" kept; Pharaoh stands twice, as printed.
- **"into the hand of those that seek their lives … and into the hand of his servants: and afterward it shall be inhabited, as in the days of old" → "into the hand of those who seek their soul … and into the hand of his servants; and afterward it shall abide as in the days of old"** (v26) — Hebrew מְבַקְשֵׁי נַפְשָׁם "those who seek their soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul", its row); עֲבָדָיו "his servants" (of a king's men, the עֶבֶד row); תִּשְׁכֹּן "it shall abide" (שָׁכַן "abide", its row; the subject is the land, feminine, or "you shall abide"); כִּימֵי־קֶדֶם "as in the days of old".
- **"But fear not thou, O my servant Jacob, and be not dismayed, O Israel … and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and be in rest and at ease, and none shall make him afraid" → "And you, do not fear, my servant Jacob, and do not be dismayed, Israel … and your seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return and be quiet and at ease, and there is none who makes him tremble"** (v27) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה "and you"; עֶבֶד of Yahweh "servant" (its row); חָתַת "be dismayed" (as v5); מוֹשִׁיעֲךָ participle "I am saving you"; זֶרַע "seed" (its row); שְׁבִי "captivity"; שָׁקַט "be quiet" (its row), שַׁאֲנָן "at ease" (its row); מַחֲרִיד "one who makes tremble". No "O". v27–28 stand again, with small differences, in 30:10–11.
- **"for I will make a full end of all the nations whither I have driven thee … but correct thee in measure; yet will I not leave thee wholly unpunished" → "for I will make a full end of all the nations where I have driven you, and of you I will not make a full end; and I will discipline you in judgment, and I will surely not clear you"** (v28) — Hebrew עָשָׂה כָלָה בְּ "make a full end of"; הִדִּיחַ "drive"; יִסֵּר "discipline" (its row); לַמִּשְׁפָּט "in (lit. "to the") judgment" (in due measure); וְנַקֵּה לֹא אֲנַקֶּךָּ, infinitive absolute with the verb, lit. "and clearing I will not clear you" (נִקָּה piel "clear", as Exod 34:7: "surely not").

### Notes
- **v25 Amon of No** — Amon, the chief god of Thebes; נֹא "No" is Thebes in Upper Egypt (Nah 3:8 "No-amon").
- **Carchemish (605 BC)** — Nebuchadnezzar's victory over Pharaoh Neco on the Euphrates; v13–24 look ahead to an invasion of Egypt itself.
