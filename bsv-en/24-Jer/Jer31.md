# Jeremiah 31

[1] At that time, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to all the clans of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and they, they shall be a people to me.

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

There found favor in the wilderness
a people of survivors of the sword,
going to give him rest — ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] From afar ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to me:
And with an everlasting love I have loved you;
therefore I have drawn you with ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧.

[4] Again I will build you, and you shall be built,
virgin of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
again you shall adorn yourself with your timbrels,
and go out in the dance of those who make merry.

[5] Again you shall plant vineyards
on the mountains of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧;
the planters have planted and put them to common use.

[6] For there is a day
the watchers have called on the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧:
"Arise, and let us go up to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[7] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

Ring out for ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ with gladness,
and shout aloud at the head of the nations;
make heard, praise, and say,
"Save, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your people,
the remnant of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[8] Behold, I am bringing them from the land of the north,
and I will gather them from the remote parts of the earth,
among them the blind and the lame,
the woman with child and she who gives birth, together;
a great assembly, they shall return here.

[9] With weeping they shall come,
and with supplications I will bring them;
I will make them walk to wadis of water,
in a straight way, they shall not stumble in it;
for I have become a father to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, he is my firstborn.

[10] Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, nations,
and tell it in the coastlands from afar,
and say, "He who strews ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ will gather him,
and keep him as a shepherd his drove."

[11] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has ransomed ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
and redeemed him from the hand of one stronger than he.

[12] And they shall come and ring out on the height of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and they shall stream to the goodness of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for grain and for new wine and for fresh oil,
and for the young of the flock and of the herd;
and their soul shall be like a watered garden,
and they shall not languish any more.

[13] Then the virgin shall rejoice in the dance,
and young men and old men together;
and I will turn their mourning to joy,
and I will comfort them and gladden them out of their sorrow.

[14] And I will water the soul of the priests with fatness,
and my people shall be satisfied with my goodness,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

A voice is heard in Ramah,
wailing, weeping of bitterness;
Rachel weeping for her sons;
she refuses to be comforted for her sons,
for he is not.

[16] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

Hold back your voice from weeping,
and your eyes from tears;
for there is a reward for your work, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and they shall return from the land of the enemy.

[17] And there is hope for your latter end, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and sons shall return to their territory.

[18] I have surely heard ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ bemoaning himself:
"You have disciplined me, and I was disciplined,
like a calf not trained;
bring me back, and let me turn back,
for you are ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[19] For after my turning back I was sorry,
and after I was made to know I struck upon my thigh;
I was ashamed, and also I was put to shame,
for I have borne the reproach of my youth."

[20] Is ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ a precious son to me,
or a child of delight?
For as often as I speak against him,
I surely remember him still;
therefore my inward parts moan for him;
I will surely have compassion on him,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[21] Set up markers for yourself,
make signposts for yourself;
set your heart to the highway,
the way you went;
turn back, virgin of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
turn back to these your cities.

[22] How long will you turn this way and that,
turning-away daughter?
For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has created a new thing in the land:
a female shall surround a mighty man.

[23] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Again they shall say this word in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in its cities, when I restore their fortunes:

"May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ bless you, pasture of righteousness,
mountain of holiness!"

[24] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and all its cities shall dwell in it together, farmers, and they shall journey with the drove.

[25] For I have watered the weary soul, and every languishing soul I have filled.

[26] On this I awoke and looked, and my sleep was sweet to me.

[27] Behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will sow the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ with the seed of man and the seed of beast.

[28] And it shall be, as I have kept awake over them to pluck up and to break down and to throw down and to make perish and to do evil, so I will keep awake over them to build and to plant, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[29] In those days they shall no more say:

"Fathers have eaten sour grapes,
and the teeth of sons are set on edge."

[30] But each man shall die in his iniquity; every man who eats the sour grapes, his teeth shall be set on edge.

[31] Behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and I will cut with the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and with the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ a ⟦berit-chadashah|berit chadashah — a new covenant|new covenant⟧,

[32] not like the covenant that I cut with their fathers

to bring them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
in that they, they broke my covenant,
and I, I was husband to them, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[33] For this is the covenant that I will cut
with the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ after those days, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
I have put my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ in their inward part,
and on their heart I will write it;
and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them,
and they, they shall be a people to me.

[34] And they shall no more teach, each man his neighbor and each man his brother, saying,
"Know ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,"

[35] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who gives the sun for light by day, the statutes of the moon and the stars for light by night, who stirs up the sea, and its waves roar — ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name:

[38] Behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the city shall be built for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the tower of ⟦hananel|Chananel|Hananel⟧, the gate of the corner.

[39] And the measuring line shall go out again straight before it over the hill of ⟦gareb|Garev|Gareb⟧, and it shall turn round to Goah.

[40] And all the valley of the corpses and of the fat ashes, and all the terraces as far as the wadi of ⟦kidron|Qidron|Kidron⟧, as far as the corner of the gate of the horses toward the east, shall be holiness to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it shall not be plucked up, and it shall not be thrown down any more forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v23, 35) — plain words, as the prophets' row. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v1, 6, 18, 23, 33). נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v1, 14, 16, 17, 20, 27, 28, 31–34, 36–38), literally "the declaration of Yahweh". No "O" before a name or title (v4, 7, 10, 21, 22, 23; the reviewer's vocative row).
- **Layout** — poetry v1–22, 29b, 31–34, 36–37, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order; prose v23a, 24–28, 30, 35, 38–40, as the existing layout. Lines changed where they divided against the accents or against the Hebrew order: v5 (three lines: the last line is one colon), v6 (the first colon ends at "a day"), v31 (two lines, "a new covenant" last, as in the Hebrew). The outer quotation marks are dropped.
- **"I will be the God of all the families of Israel, and they shall be my people" → "I will be Elohim to all the clans of Israel, and they, they shall be a people to me"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶהְיֶה לֵאלֹהִים לְכֹל מִשְׁפְּחוֹת יִשְׂרָאֵל וְהֵמָּה יִהְיוּ־לִי לְעָם, literally "I will be for Elohim to all the clans … and they, they shall be to me for a people" (the covenant formula as Jer 30:22; also v33); מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan" (its row); הֵמָּה written. The Hebrew counts this verse with chapter 31 (some editions join it to 30:24).
- **"The people which were left of the sword found grace in the wilderness; even Israel, when I went to cause him to rest" → "There found favor in the wilderness a people of survivors of the sword, going to give him rest — Israel"** (v2) — the Hebrew order of the lines: מָצָא חֵן בַּמִּדְבָּר "found favor in the wilderness" (the חֵן row: "favor", not "grace"), then עַם שְׂרִידֵי חָרֶב "a people of survivors of the sword" (שָׂרִיד "survivor", its row). הָלוֹךְ לְהַרְגִּיעוֹ יִשְׂרָאֵל: an infinitive absolute "going" with no subject, and לְהַרְגִּיעוֹ "to give him rest" (hifil of רָגַע): either Yahweh going to give Israel rest, or Israel going to find rest. The verse leaves both open; nothing is supplied.
- **"The LORD hath appeared of old unto me, saying, Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee" → "From afar Yahweh appeared to me: And with an everlasting love I have loved you; therefore I have drawn you with chesed"** (v3) — Hebrew מֵרָחוֹק "from afar" (of place; "of old" is a guess at time); נִרְאָה לִי "appeared to me" (the נִרְאָה אֶל row; the Septuagint reads "to him", from memory; MT kept). "saying" is not written: the colon marks the turn to Yahweh's words. וְאַהֲבַת עוֹלָם אֲהַבְתִּיךְ, literally "and a love of the age I have loved you" ("everlasting", the עוֹלָם genitive row; "with" supplied); מְשַׁכְתִּיךְ חָסֶד "I have drawn you, chesed": chesed is the manner ("with chesed") or the object ("I have drawn out chesed to you"); token `steadfast-love`. "you" is feminine (v3–5, 21–22: the virgin of Israel).
- **"Again I will build thee … O virgin of Israel: thou shalt again be adorned with thy tabrets, and shalt go forth in the dances of them that make merry" → "Again I will build you … virgin of Israel; again you shall adorn yourself with your timbrels, and go out in the dance of those who make merry"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּתוּלַת יִשְׂרָאֵל "virgin of Israel" (also v21; as Jer 18:13); תַּעְדִּי, qal of עָדָה "adorn yourself" (the עֲדִי "ornaments" root), with תֻפַּיִךְ "your timbrels" (the תֹּף row); מָחוֹל "dance" (singular); מְשַׂחֲקִים as Jer 30:19.
- **"the planters shall plant, and shall eat them as common things" → "the planters have planted and put them to common use"** (v5) — Hebrew נָטְעוּ נֹטְעִים וְחִלֵּלוּ, perfects: the prophet sees it done (future in sense). חִלֵּל of a vineyard "put to common use" (its row, which names this verse: literally "profaned" — the first ordinary use of the fruit after the years it was forbidden, Lev 19:23–25).
- **"For there shall be a day, that the watchmen upon the mount Ephraim shall cry" → "For there is a day the watchers have called on the hill country of Ephraim"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי יֶשׁ־יוֹם "for there is a day"; קָרְאוּ perfect "have called" (the same perfect of vision as v5); נֹצְרִים "watchers" (from נָצַר "guard", its row; שֹׁמֵר is "watchman"); הַר אֶפְרַיִם "the hill country of Ephraim" (the הַר row). Ephraim, the north, goes up to Zion.
- **"Sing with gladness for Jacob, and shout among the chief of the nations: publish ye, praise ye, and say, O LORD, save thy people" → "Ring out for Jacob with gladness, and shout aloud at the head of the nations; make heard, praise, and say, Save, Yahweh, your people"** (v7) — Hebrew רָנּוּ "ring out" (cry with joy), שִׂמְחָה "gladness" (its row); צַהֲלוּ "shout aloud" (the cry of neighing, used of joy); בְּרֹאשׁ הַגּוֹיִם "at the head of the nations" (= the foremost of the nations, Israel; or "at the head" of a procession); הַשְׁמִיעוּ "make heard" (its row); הוֹשַׁע an imperative, "Save" (the Septuagint reads "Yahweh has saved", from memory; MT kept).
- **"Behold, I will bring them from the north country, and gather them from the coasts of the earth, and with them the blind and the lame, the woman with child and her that travaileth with child together: a great company shall return thither" → "Behold, I am bringing them … from the remote parts of the earth, among them … the woman with child and she who gives birth, together; a great assembly, they shall return here"** (v8) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, a participle; יַרְכְּתֵי־אָרֶץ "the remote parts of the earth" (the Ps 48:2 row); הָרָה "with child" (its row), יֹלֶדֶת "she who gives birth"; קָהָל "assembly"; הֵנָּה "here" (not "thither").
- **"They shall come with weeping, and with supplications will I lead them: I will cause them to walk by the rivers of waters" → "With weeping they shall come, and with supplications I will bring them; I will make them walk to wadis of water"** (v9) — the Hebrew order; תַּחֲנוּנִים "supplications" (pleas for favor); אוֹבִילֵם "I will bring them", אוֹלִיכֵם "I will make them walk" (two verbs); אֶל־נַחֲלֵי מַיִם "to wadis of water" (the נַחַל row); הָיִיתִי "I have become" (perfect); וְאֶפְרַיִם בְּכֹרִי הוּא "and Ephraim, he is my firstborn".
- **"Hear the word of the LORD, O ye nations, and declare it in the isles afar off … He that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock" → "Hear the word of Yahweh, nations, and tell it in the coastlands from afar … He who strews Israel will gather him, and keep him as a shepherd his drove"** (v10) — Hebrew הִגִּיד "tell" (its row; "it" supplied); אִיִּים "coastlands" (its row); מִמֶּרְחָק "from afar"; מְזָרֵה, piel participle of זָרָה "strew" (its row, as grain is scattered to the wind; הֵפִיץ is "scatter"); עֵדֶר "drove" (its row); שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"redeemed him from the hand of him that was stronger than he"** (v11) — Hebrew פָּדָה "ransom" and גָּאַל "redeem" (their rows); מִיַּד "from the hand" (singular).
- **"Therefore they shall come and sing in the height of Zion, and shall flow together to the goodness of the LORD, for wheat, and for wine, and for oil, and for the young of the flock and of the herd: and their soul shall be as a watered garden; and they shall not sorrow any more at all" → "And they shall come and ring out on the height of Zion, and they shall stream to the goodness of Yahweh, for grain and for new wine and for fresh oil … and they shall not languish any more"** (v12) — Hebrew וּבָאוּ "And they shall come" (no "therefore"); רִנְּנוּ as v7; נָהֲרוּ "stream" (as rivers flow: Isa 2:2; or "be radiant", Isa 60:5); דָּגָן "grain", תִּירוֹשׁ "new wine", יִצְהָר "fresh oil" (their rows); בְּנֵי־צֹאן וּבָקָר, literally "sons of the flock and of the herd" (the young; צֹאן and בָּקָר, their rows); רָוֶה "watered" (one root with רִוָּה, v14, 25); לְדַאֲבָה "to languish" (the דַּאֲבוֹן root; also v25).
- **"Then shall the virgin rejoice in the dance, both young men and old together: for I will turn their mourning into joy, and will comfort them, and make them rejoice from their sorrow" → "Then the virgin shall rejoice in the dance, and young men and old men together; and I will turn their mourning to joy, and I will comfort them and gladden them out of their sorrow"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּתוּלָה "the virgin", singular; וְ "and" (no "for"); שָׂשׂוֹן "joy" (the reviewer row: מָשׂוֹשׂ is "exultation"); נִחֵם "comfort"; שִׂמַּח "gladden" (its row); יָגוֹן "sorrow" (its row).
- **"And I will satiate the soul of the priests with fatness" → "And I will water the soul of the priests with fatness"** (v14) — Hebrew רִוֵּיתִי, piel of רָוָה "drink one's fill": "water" (the same root as "watered garden", v12, and v25); דֶּשֶׁן "fatness"; שָׂבַע "be satisfied".
- **"A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, and bitter weeping; Rahel weeping for her children refused to be comforted for her children, because they were not" → "A voice is heard in Ramah, wailing, weeping of bitterness; Rachel weeping for her sons; she refuses to be comforted for her sons, for he is not"** (v15) — the Hebrew as it stands: נִשְׁמָע a participle, "is heard"; נְהִי "wailing" (the Jer 9–12 row); בְּכִי תַמְרוּרִים "weeping of bitternesses"; מְבַכָּה participle "weeping"; בָּנֶיהָ "her sons" (the standing decision); מֵאֲנָה "she refuses"; כִּי אֵינֶנּוּ "for he is not", singular (each one of them; or the people as one). Matt 2:18 quotes the verse in Greek: Φωνὴ ἐν Ῥαμὰ ἠκούσθη, κλαυθμὸς καὶ ὀδυρμὸς πολύς· Ῥαχὴλ κλαίουσα τὰ τέκνα αὐτῆς … ὅτι οὐκ εἰσίν ("a voice in Ramah was heard … her children … because they are not", plural); the verse keeps the Hebrew.
- **"Refrain thy voice from weeping … for thy work shall be rewarded … and they shall come again from the land of the enemy" → "Hold back your voice from weeping … for there is a reward for your work … and they shall return from the land of the enemy"** (v16) — Hebrew מִנְעִי "hold back"; יֵשׁ שָׂכָר לִפְעֻלָּתֵךְ "there is a reward for your work" (שָׂכָר of what God gives, its row; פְּעֻלָּה "work", the toil that earns a wage: its row); שׁוּב "return" (also v17).
- **"And there is hope in thine end … that thy children shall come again to their own border" → "And there is hope for your latter end … and sons shall return to their territory"** (v17) — Hebrew לְאַחֲרִיתֵךְ "for your latter end" (as Jer 29:11); בָנִים "sons" (no "your"); גְּבוּלָם "their territory" (the גְּבוּל row: "border", here the land within it).
- **"I have surely heard Ephraim bemoaning himself thus; Thou hast chastised me, and I was chastised, as a bullock unaccustomed to the yoke: turn thou me, and I shall be turned" → "I have surely heard Ephraim bemoaning himself: You have disciplined me, and I was disciplined, like a calf not trained; bring me back, and let me turn back"** (v18) — Hebrew שָׁמוֹעַ שָׁמַעְתִּי, the infinitive absolute ("hearing I have heard": the standing decision); מִתְנוֹדֵד, hitpolel of נוּד "rock oneself" (grief: the נוּד לְ row); יִסַּר "discipline" (its row; "chastise" in KJV); עֵגֶל "calf", לֹא לֻמָּד "not trained" ("to the yoke" is not written); הֲשִׁיבֵנִי וְאָשׁוּבָה "bring me back, and let me turn back" (one root שׁוּב, a cohortative; "turn back" in the calls to turn, the Jer 1–4 row), or "turn me, and I will turn".
- **"Surely after that I was turned, I repented; and after that I was instructed, I smote upon my thigh: I was ashamed, yea, even confounded" → "For after my turning back I was sorry, and after I was made to know I struck upon my thigh; I was ashamed, and also I was put to shame"** (v19) — Hebrew כִּי "For"; שׁוּבִי "my turning back" (שׁוּב: "away" or "back", the word does not say which); נִחַמְתִּי "I was sorry" (the נִחַם row, not "repent"); הִוָּדְעִי, nifal of יָדַע, "my being made to know"; סָפַק עַל־יָרֵךְ "struck upon the thigh" (a gesture of grief); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", נִכְלַם "be put to shame" (its row); נָשָׂא "bear"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (its row).
- **"Is Ephraim my dear son? is he a pleasant child? for since I spake against him, I do earnestly remember him still: therefore my bowels are troubled for him; I will surely have mercy upon him" → "Is Ephraim a precious son to me, or a child of delight? For as often as I speak against him, I surely remember him still; therefore my inward parts moan for him; I will surely have compassion on him"** (v20) — Hebrew הֲבֵן יַקִּיר לִי, a question, "a precious son to me"; יֶלֶד שַׁעֲשֻׁעִים "a child of delight" (the שַׁעֲשֻׁעִים row, which names this verse: a plural rendered singular); מִדֵּי דַבְּרִי בּוֹ "as often as I speak against him" (בְּ "against", or "of him"); זָכֹר אֶזְכְּרֶנּוּ and רַחֵם אֲרַחֲמֶנּוּ, infinitives absolute ("remembering I remember", "having compassion I will have compassion": the standing decision); הָמוּ מֵעַי "my inward parts moan" (הָמָה of a person "moan", the Ps 75–77 row; plain words, as Isa 16:11); רִחַם "have compassion".
- **"Set thee up waymarks, make thee high heaps: set thine heart toward the highway, even the way which thou wentest: turn again, O virgin of Israel" → "Set up markers for yourself, make signposts for yourself; set your heart to the highway, the way you went; turn back, virgin of Israel"** (v21) — Hebrew צִיֻּנִים "markers" (the צִיּוּן row, which names this verse); תַּמְרוּרִים "signposts" (from תָּמָר "palm, pillar": posts; the same letters as "bitterness" in v15, another word); מְסִלָּה "highway" (its row); הָלָכְתְּ "you went" is the qere (the ketiv הלכתי is an old spelling of the same feminine form, or "I went"); שׁוּבִי … שֻׁבִי "turn back … turn back" (the Jer 1–4 row for the calls to turn).
- **"How long wilt thou go about, O thou backsliding daughter? for the LORD hath created a new thing in the earth, A woman shall compass a man" → "How long will you turn this way and that, turning-away daughter? For Yahweh has created a new thing in the land: a female shall surround a mighty man"** (v22) — Hebrew תִּתְחַמָּקִין, hitpael of חָמַק "turn away": "turn this way and that" (waver); הַבַּת הַשּׁוֹבֵבָה "the turning-away daughter" (שׁוֹבֵב, from שׁוּב as v18–19: the reviewer row "turning-away", KJV "backsliding"); בָּרָא "create" (its row); בָּאָרֶץ "in the land" (or "on the earth": the אֶרֶץ row by sense). נְקֵבָה תְּסוֹבֵב גָּבֶר word for word: נְקֵבָה "female" (as Gen 1:27 "male and female"; not אִשָּׁה "woman"), תְּסוֹבֵב, polel of סָבַב, "shall surround" (go around, encompass, or protect), גֶּבֶר "mighty man" (the standing decision; not אִישׁ). What the saying means is not known: the woman protects the man, seeks him out, is turned into a man, or a woman conceives a man (a reading of the church fathers, from memory); the verse keeps the words.
- **"As yet they shall use this speech … when I shall bring again their captivity; The LORD bless thee, O habitation of justice, and mountain of holiness" → "Again they shall say this word … when I restore their fortunes: May Yahweh bless you, pasture of righteousness, mountain of holiness"** (v23) — Hebrew עוֹד "again"; הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה "this word"; בְּשׁוּבִי אֶת־שְׁבוּתָם, literally "when I turn back their captivity / their turning": the reviewer row "restore the fortunes"; יְבָרֶכְךָ a jussive, "May … bless you" (masculine: the mountain); נְוֵה־צֶדֶק "pasture of righteousness" (נָוֶה, a pasture or abode of flocks: "habitation" in Changes; צֶדֶק "righteousness", its row); הַר הַקֹּדֶשׁ "mountain of holiness" (the קֹדֶשׁ row).
- **"And there shall dwell in Judah itself, and in all the cities thereof together, husbandmen, and they that go forth with flocks" → "And Judah and all its cities shall dwell in it together, farmers, and they shall journey with the drove"** (v24) — Hebrew וְיָשְׁבוּ בָהּ יְהוּדָה וְכָל־עָרָיו "and Judah and all its cities shall dwell in it" (the land); אִכָּרִים "farmers"; וְנָסְעוּ בַּעֵדֶר "and they shall journey with the drove" (a finite verb: the MT as pointed; a participle "and those who journey" is commonly read; נָסַע "journey", עֵדֶר "drove", their rows).
- **"For I have satiated the weary soul, and I have replenished every sorrowful soul" → "For I have watered the weary soul, and every languishing soul I have filled"** (v25) — Hebrew הִרְוֵיתִי "I have watered" (as v14); עֲיֵפָה "weary"; דָּאֲבָה "languishing" (as v12); מִלֵּאתִי "I have filled" (the מָלֵא row); perfects.
- **"Upon this I awaked, and beheld; and my sleep was sweet unto me" → "On this I awoke and looked, and my sleep was sweet to me"** (v26) — Hebrew עַל־זֹאת; עָרְבָה "was sweet" (עָרֵב, another root than "be surety"). Who speaks is not named.
- **"that I will sow the house of Israel … with the seed of man, and with the seed of beast" → "and I will sow the house of Israel … with the seed of man and the seed of beast"** (v27) — Hebrew וְזָרַעְתִּי "and I will sow" (וְ "and", no "that"); זֶרַע "seed" (its row) of אָדָם "man" and בְּהֵמָה "beast" (their rows).
- **"And it shall come to pass, that like as I have watched over them, to pluck up, and to break down, and to throw down, and to destroy, and to afflict; so will I watch over them, to build, and to plant" → "And it shall be, as I have kept awake over them to pluck up and to break down and to throw down and to make perish and to do evil, so I will keep awake over them to build and to plant"** (v28) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "And it shall be"; שָׁקַד "keep awake" (its row, as Jer 1:12); the verbs of Jer 1:10 by their rows: נָתַשׁ "pluck up", נָתַץ "break down", הָרַס "throw down", הֶאֱבִיד "make perish", and הֵרַע "do evil" (the הֵרַע row), then בָּנָה "build", נָטַע "plant".
- **"The fathers have eaten a sour grape, and the children's teeth are set on edge" → "Fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the teeth of sons are set on edge"** (v29) — Hebrew אָבוֹת … בָנִים without the article; בֹּסֶר "unripe (sour) grapes"; תִּקְהֶינָה "are set on edge" (קָהָה, literally "become dull, blunt"; also v30). Ezek 18:2 has the same proverb.
- **"But every one shall die for his own iniquity: every man that eateth the sour grape" → "But each man shall die in his iniquity; every man who eats the sour grapes"** (v30) — Hebrew כִּי אִם "but" (after a negative, one thing put in place of another: the reviewer row); אִישׁ "each man", בַּעֲוֹנוֹ "in his iniquity" ("own" is not written); כָּל־הָאָדָם "every man" (אָדָם, its row); הַבֹּסֶר with the article.
- **"Behold, the days come … that I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel, and with the house of Judah" → "Behold, days are coming … and I will cut with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah a berit chadashah — a new covenant"** (v31) — the Hebrew as it stands, word for word: הִנֵּה יָמִים בָּאִים (no article, "days are coming"); וְכָרַתִּי "and I will cut" (כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant", the בְּרִית row; also v32, 33); the Hebrew order, with בְּרִית חֲדָשָׁה last (token `berit-chadashah`). Heb 8:8 quotes in Greek: καὶ συντελέσω ἐπὶ τὸν οἶκον Ἰσραὴλ … διαθήκην καινήν ("and I will complete with the house of Israel … a new covenant"); the verse keeps the Hebrew.
- **"Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day that I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt; which my covenant they brake, although I was an husband unto them" → "not like the covenant that I cut with their fathers in the day I took hold of their hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt, in that they, they broke my covenant, and I, I was husband to them"** (v32) — Hebrew לֹא כַבְּרִית "not like the covenant"; הֶחֱזִיקִי בְיָדָם "my taking hold of their hand" (singular); אֲשֶׁר־הֵמָּה הֵפֵרוּ אֶת־בְּרִיתִי "in that they broke my covenant" (אֲשֶׁר with the pronoun written, as Jer 1:16 "in that"; הֵפֵר בְּרִית "break a covenant", its row); וְאָנֹכִי בָּעַלְתִּי בָם "and I, I was husband to them": בָּעַלְתִּי as pointed, the verb of בַּעַל "owner, husband" (the בָּעַל row: "become the husband of", literally "owner"), with בְּ "over / to them"; וְ "and" (not "although"): the two pronouns הֵמָּה … אָנֹכִי are set against each other. Heb 8:9 reads in Greek ὅτι αὐτοὶ οὐκ ἐνέμειναν ἐν τῇ διαθήκῃ μου, κἀγὼ ἠμέλησα αὐτῶν ("for they did not remain in my covenant, and I disregarded them"), as the Septuagint (from memory; perhaps reading בָּחַלְתִּי "I loathed", or another sense of בָּעַל); the verse keeps the MT.
- **"But this shall be the covenant … After those days, saith the LORD, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people" → "For this is the covenant that I will cut … after those days, declares Yahweh: I have put my Torah in their inward part, and on their heart I will write it; and I will be Elohim to them, and they, they shall be a people to me"** (v33) — Hebrew כִּי "For"; נָתַתִּי, a perfect, "I have put" (literally "I have given"; the promise seen as done: future in sense) beside אֶכְתֲּבֶנָּה imperfect "I will write it"; תּוֹרָתִי "my Torah" (token `torah`); בְּקִרְבָּם "in their inward part" (singular; קֶרֶב "inward part, midst", its row); עַל־לִבָּם "on their heart" (singular); the covenant formula as v1. Heb 8:10 reads in Greek διδοὺς νόμους μου εἰς τὴν διάνοιαν αὐτῶν, καὶ ἐπὶ καρδίας αὐτῶν ἐπιγράψω αὐτούς ("giving my laws into their mind, and on their hearts I will write them": plural laws, "mind" for קֶרֶב); the verse keeps the Hebrew.
- **"And they shall teach no more every man his neighbour, and every man his brother … for they shall all know me, from the least of them unto the greatest of them … for I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more" → "And they shall no more teach, each man his neighbor and each man his brother … from the least of them and to the greatest of them … for I will pardon their iniquity, and their sin I will remember no more"** (v34) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶת־רֵעֵהוּ וְאִישׁ אֶת־אָחִיו "each man his neighbor and each man his brother" (the אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ row); לְמִקְטַנָּם וְעַד־גְּדוֹלָם "from their least and unto their greatest"; סָלַח "pardon" (its row: said only of God); וּלְחַטָּאתָם לֹא אֶזְכָּר־עוֹד "and their sin I will not remember again", in the Hebrew order (חַטָּאת singular). Heb 8:11–12 reads in Greek τὸν πολίτην αὐτοῦ "his fellow citizen" for רֵעֵהוּ and ἵλεως ἔσομαι ταῖς ἀδικίαις αὐτῶν, καὶ τῶν ἁμαρτιῶν αὐτῶν οὐ μὴ μνησθῶ ἔτι ("I will be merciful to their unrighteousnesses, and their sins I will remember no more", plural); the verse keeps the Hebrew.
- **"which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar" → "who gives the sun for light by day, the statutes of the moon and the stars for light by night, who stirs up the sea, and its waves roar"** (v35) — Hebrew נֹתֵן participle; חֻקֹּת "statutes" (the חֻקָּה row; also v36 חֻקִּים); רֹגַע הַיָּם "who stirs up the sea" (רָגַע "stir up, disturb", as Isa 51:15; KJV "divideth"); וַיֶּהֱמוּ גַלָּיו "and its waves roar" (הָמָה "roar", the reviewer's row beside שָׁאוֹן).
- **"If those ordinances depart from before me … then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever" → "If these statutes depart from before me … also the seed of Israel shall cease from being a nation before me all the days"** (v36) — Hebrew הַחֻקִּים הָאֵלֶּה "these statutes"; מוּשׁ "depart"; גַּם "also"; זֶרַע "seed" (its row); שָׁבַת "cease"; כָּל־הַיָּמִים "all the days" (not עוֹלָם).
- **"If heaven above can be measured, and the foundations of the earth searched out beneath, I will also cast off all the seed of Israel" → "If the heavens above can be measured, and the foundations of the earth below searched out, I also will reject all the seed of Israel"** (v37) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם "heavens" (plural, its row); חָקַר "search out" (the reviewer row); גַּם־אֲנִי "I also"; מָאַס בְּ "reject" (its row).
- **"Behold, the days come … that the city shall be built to the LORD from the tower of Hananeel unto the gate of the corner" → "Behold, days are coming … and the city shall be built for Yahweh from the tower of Hananel, the gate of the corner"** (v38) — Hebrew בָּאִים "are coming" is the qere (the ketiv has no verb: "Behold, days, declares Yahweh"); וְנִבְנְתָה "and … shall be built" (no "that"); שַׁעַר הַפִּנָּה "the gate of the corner" with no עַד "unto" written: the line runs from the tower to the Corner Gate (2 Kgs 14:13); nothing is supplied.
- **"And the measuring line shall yet go forth over against it upon the hill Gareb, and shall compass about to Goath" → "And the measuring line shall go out again straight before it over the hill of Gareb, and it shall turn round to Goah"** (v39) — Hebrew קָו הַמִּדָּה "the line of the measure" (qere קָו; ketiv קוה, the same word fully written; the קָו "line" row); עוֹד "again"; נֶגְדּוֹ "straight before it"; גִּבְעַת גָּרֵב "the hill of Gareb"; וְנָסַב גֹּעָתָה "and it shall turn round toward Goah" (the place is not known).
- **"And the whole valley of the dead bodies, and of the ashes, and all the fields unto the brook of Kidron … shall be holy unto the LORD; it shall not be plucked up, nor thrown down any more for ever" → "And all the valley of the corpses and of the fat ashes, and all the terraces as far as the wadi of Kidron … shall be holiness to Yahweh; it shall not be plucked up, and it shall not be thrown down any more forever"** (v40) — Hebrew הַפְּגָרִים "the corpses" (the פֶּגֶר row); הַדֶּשֶׁן "the fat ashes" (its row: the ashes of the altar); הַשְּׁדֵמוֹת qere "the terraces" (uncertain: fields or terraced slopes; the ketiv השרמות is a miswriting of the same word); נַחַל "wadi" (its row); שַׁעַר הַסּוּסִים "the gate of the horses"; מִזְרָחָה "toward the east"; קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה "holiness to Yahweh" (the קֹדֶשׁ row, as Exod 28:36); נָתַשׁ nifal "be plucked up", הָרַס "be thrown down" (as v28); לְעוֹלָם "forever".

### Notes
- **v15** — quoted in Matt 2:18 (the Greek in Changes).
- **v22** — the meaning of "a female shall surround a mighty man" is not known (see Changes).
- **v31–34** — the new covenant, quoted in Heb 8:8–12 and in part Heb 10:16–17 (the Greek in Changes).
- **v38–40** — the bounds of the rebuilt city: the tower of Hananel and the Corner Gate on the north wall (Neh 3:1; 12:39; 2 Kgs 14:13), the valley of Hinnom to the south (the corpses and the fat ashes), the Horse Gate on the east (Neh 3:28).
