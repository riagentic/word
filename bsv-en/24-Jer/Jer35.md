# Jeremiah 35

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the days of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying,

[2] Go to the house of the ⟦rechabites|Rechavim|Rechabites⟧, and you shall speak with them, and you shall bring them to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, into one of the chambers, and you shall give them wine to drink.

[3] And I took ⟦jaazaniah|Yaazanyahu|Jaazaniah⟧ son of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, son of ⟦habazziniah|Chavatzinyah|Habazziniah⟧, and his brothers and all his sons and all the house of the ⟦rechabites|Rechavim|Rechabites⟧;

[4] and I brought them to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, into the chamber of the sons of ⟦hanan|Chanan|Hanan⟧ son of ⟦igdaliah|Yigdalyahu|Igdaliah⟧, the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which was beside the chamber of the princes, which was above the chamber of ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ son of Shallum, the keeper of the threshold.

[5] And I set before the sons of the house of the ⟦rechabites|Rechavim|Rechabites⟧ goblets full of wine, and cups; and I said to them, Drink wine.

[6] And they said, We will not drink wine; for ⟦jonadab|Yonadav|Jonadab⟧ son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧, our father, commanded us, saying, You shall not drink wine, you and your sons, forever;

[7] and a house you shall not build, and seed you shall not sow, and a vineyard you shall not plant, and you shall not have one; but in tents you shall dwell all your days, so that you may live many days on the face of the ground where you are sojourning.

[8] And we listened to the voice of ⟦jehonadab|Yehonadav|Jehonadab⟧ son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧, our father, in all that he commanded us, not to drink wine all our days, we, our wives, our sons and our daughters,

[9] and not to build houses for our dwelling; and vineyard and field and seed we do not have.

[10] And we dwelt in tents, and we listened, and we did according to all that ⟦jonadab|Yonadav|Jonadab⟧ our father commanded us.

[11] And it came to pass, when ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came up into the land, that we said, Come, and let us go into ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from the face of the force of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ and from the face of the force of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧. And we dwelt in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[12] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, saying,

[13] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Go, and you shall say to the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: Will you not take discipline, to listen to my words? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] The words of ⟦jehonadab|Yehonadav|Jehonadab⟧ son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧ have been established, which he commanded his sons, not to drink wine; and they have not drunk to this day, for they have heard the commandment of their father. And I, I have spoken to you, rising early and speaking, and you have not listened to me.

[15] And I sent to you all my servants the prophets, rising early and sending, saying, Turn back, please, each man from his evil way, and make your doings good, and do not go after other gods to serve them, and dwell on the ground that I have given to you and to your fathers. But you have not turned your ear, and you have not listened to me.

[16] For the sons of ⟦jehonadab|Yehonadav|Jehonadab⟧ son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧ have established the commandment of their father that he commanded them, and this people have not listened to me.

[17] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I am bringing to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to all the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ all the evil that I have spoken against them; because I have spoken to them and they have not listened, and I have called to them and they have not answered.

[18] And to the house of the ⟦rechabites|Rechavim|Rechabites⟧ ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Because you have listened to the commandment of ⟦jonadab|Yonadav|Jonadab⟧ your father, and you have kept all his commandments, and you have done according to all that he commanded you,

[19] therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: There shall not be cut off for ⟦jonadab|Yonadav|Jonadab⟧ son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧ a man standing before me all the days.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 13, 17–19). **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v13, 18, 19); **"the LORD God of hosts" → "Yahweh, the Elohim of Hosts"** (v17) — אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת. נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v13). **Quotation marks** — the Hebrew has none; the speeches stand without them.
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose.
- **"Go unto the house of the Rechabites, and speak unto them, and bring them … and give them wine to drink" → "Go to the house of the Rechabites, and you shall speak with them, and you shall bring them … and you shall give them wine to drink"** (v2) — הָלוֹךְ, an infinitive absolute as command, then three verbs "you shall"; דִּבַּרְתָּ אוֹתָם "speak with them"; לִשְׁכָּה "chamber" (its row; also v4); הִשְׁקָה "give to drink" (the root of מַשְׁקֶה). בֵּית הָרֵכָבִים "the house of the Rechabites" is the family (also v3, 5, 18).
- **"Then I took Jaazaniah" → "And I took Jaazaniah"** (v3) — וָאֶקַּח; the Jeremiah named here is not the prophet.
- **"into the chamber of the sons of Hanan … a man of God, which was by the chamber of the princes, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah the son of Shallum, the keeper of the door" → "into the chamber of the sons of Hanan … the man of Elohim, which was beside the chamber of the princes, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah son of Shallum, the keeper of the threshold"** (v4) — אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים "the man of Elohim" (its row; the article of הָאֱלֹהִים as the reviewer's row); אֵצֶל "beside" (its row); שָׂרִים "princes" (the שַׂר row); שֹׁמֵר הַסַּף "keeper of the threshold" (סַף "threshold"; the office of 2 Kgs 25:18; Jer 52:24).
- **"And I set before the sons of the house of the Rechabites pots full of wine, and cups" → "And I set before the sons of the house of the Rechabites goblets full of wine, and cups"** (v5) — וָאֶתֵּן לִפְנֵי "and I set before" (the reviewer's נָתַן לִפְנֵי row: the verb is "give"); גְּבִעִים "goblets" (the גָּבִיעַ row, which names Jer 35:5), כֹּסוֹת "cups"; בְּנֵי בֵית־הָרֵכָבִים "the sons of the house of the Rechabites".
- **"But they said, We will drink no wine: for Jonadab the son of Rechab our father commanded us, saying, Ye shall drink no wine, neither ye, nor your sons for ever" → "And they said, We will not drink wine; for Jonadab son of Rechab, our father, commanded us, saying, You shall not drink wine, you and your sons, forever"** (v6) — וַיֹּאמְרוּ "And they said"; צִוָּה עָלֵינוּ "commanded us" (literally "upon us"); עַד־עוֹלָם "forever". The name is written יוֹנָדָב in v6, 10, 18, 19 and יְהוֹנָדָב in v8, 14, 16 (two tokens, `jonadab` and `jehonadab`, as the Hebrew spells it; one man: 2 Kgs 10:15).
- **"Neither shall ye build house, nor sow seed, nor plant vineyard, nor have any: but all your days ye shall dwell in tents; that ye may live many days in the land where ye be strangers" → "and a house you shall not build, and seed you shall not sow, and a vineyard you shall not plant, and you shall not have one; but in tents you shall dwell all your days, so that you may live many days on the face of the ground where you are sojourning"** (v7) — each object first, as in the Hebrew; וְלֹא יִהְיֶה לָכֶם, literally "and it shall not be to you"; זֶרַע "seed" (its row; also v9); יָשַׁב "dwell" (its row; also v9–11, 15); לְמַעַן "so that" (its row); עַל־פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה "on the face of the ground"; גָּרִים "sojourning" (גֵּר "sojourner").
- **"Thus have we obeyed the voice of Jonadab … in all that he hath charged us" → "And we listened to the voice of Jehonadab … in all that he commanded us"** (v8) — וַנִּשְׁמַע בְּקוֹל "and we listened to the voice of" (the שָׁמַע row: not "obey"; also v10, 13–18; with אֶל "listen to", v13–17; with עַל "listen to", literally "upon", v18); the wayyiqtol forms of v8, 10 are rendered past ("we listened", "we dwelt", "we did").
- **"neither have we vineyard, nor field, nor seed" → "and vineyard and field and seed we do not have"** (v9) — לְשִׁבְתֵּנוּ "for our dwelling"; לֹא יִהְיֶה־לָּנוּ, literally "shall not be to us", the objects first.
- **"But it came to pass, when Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon came up into the land, that we said, Come, and let us go to Jerusalem for fear of the army of the Chaldeans, and for fear of the army of the Syrians: so we dwell at Jerusalem" → "And it came to pass … that we said, Come, and let us go into Jerusalem from the face of the force of the Chaldeans and from the face of the force of Aram. And we dwelt in Jerusalem"** (v11) — וַיְהִי "And it came to pass" (its row); בֹּאוּ וְנָבוֹא "Come, and let us go in"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (its row: of fleeing), not "for fear of"; חֵיל "force" (the exile row); אֲרָם is the land or people, Aram (token `aram`; "Syria" traditional), not the gentilic; וַנֵּשֶׁב "and we dwelt", past.
- **"Go and tell the men of Judah … Will ye not receive instruction to hearken to my words?" → "Go, and you shall say to the men of Judah … Will you not take discipline, to listen to my words?"** (v13) — הָלֹךְ וְאָמַרְתָּ as v2; אִישׁ יְהוּדָה, a collective singular, "the man of Judah" (also Jer 36:31); לָקַח מוּסָר "take discipline" (the מוּסָר row); שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to".
- **"The words of Jonadab the son of Rechab, that he commanded his sons not to drink wine, are performed; for unto this day they drink none, but obey their father's commandment: notwithstanding I have spoken unto you, rising early and speaking; but ye hearkened not unto me" → "The words of Jehonadab son of Rechab have been established, which he commanded his sons, not to drink wine; and they have not drunk to this day, for they have heard the commandment of their father. And I, I have spoken to you, rising early and speaking, and you have not listened to me"** (v14) — שָׁמְעוּ אֵת "they have heard" (שָׁמַע with an object: "hear", beside שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to" in the same verse); הוּקַם, hofal of the הֵקִים "establish" row (as v16; Jer 33:14; 34:18), singular with אֶת: "it has been established, the words"; לֹא שָׁתוּ perfect "they have not drunk"; מִצְוָה "commandment" (its row; also v16, 18); וְאָנֹכִי stressed; הַשְׁכֵּם וְדַבֵּר "rising early and speaking" (as the 2 Chr 36:15 row "rising early and sending", which v15 has).
- **"I have sent also unto you all my servants the prophets, rising up early and sending them, saying, Return ye now every man from his evil way, and amend your doings … and ye shall dwell in the land which I have given to you … but ye have not inclined your ear, nor hearkened unto me" → "And I sent to you all my servants the prophets, rising early and sending, saying, Turn back, please, each man from his evil way, and make your doings good … and dwell on the ground that I have given to you and to your fathers. But you have not turned your ear, and you have not listened to me"** (v15) — עֲבָדַי הַנְּבִאִים "my servants the prophets" (עֶבֶד of Yahweh's men: "servant"); הַשְׁכֵּם וְשָׁלֹחַ, no object (its row); שׁוּבוּ־נָא "turn back, please" (the שׁוּב row; נָא "please"); הֵיטִיבוּ מַעַלְלֵיכֶם "make your doings good"; הָלַךְ אַחֲרֵי אֱלֹהִים אֲחֵרִים "go after other gods" (its row); וּשְׁבוּ, an imperative of יָשַׁב, "and dwell", one of the commands; אֲדָמָה "ground"; הִטָּה אֹזֶן "turn the ear" (as Jer 34:14).
- **"Because the sons of Jonadab … have performed the commandment of their father, which he commanded them; but this people hath not hearkened unto me" → "For the sons of Jehonadab … have established the commandment of their father that he commanded them, and this people have not listened to me"** (v16) — כִּי "for" (or "indeed"); הֵקִימוּ "they have established"; וְהָעָם הַזֶּה … שָׁמְעוּ, plural verb ("have").
- **"I will bring upon Judah … all the evil that I have pronounced against them: because I have spoken unto them, but they have not heard; and I have called unto them, but they have not answered" → "Behold, I am bringing to Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the evil that I have spoken against them; because I have spoken to them and they have not listened, and I have called to them and they have not answered"** (v17) — הִנְנִי מֵבִיא participle; אֶל "to" (twice); רָעָה "evil" (its row), not "disaster"; דִּבַּרְתִּי עֲלֵיהֶם "I have spoken against them" (not "pronounced"); יַעַן "because"; וְ "and" both times, not "but".
- **"Because ye have obeyed the commandment of Jonadab your father, and kept all his precepts, and done according unto all that he hath commanded you" → "Because you have listened to the commandment of Jonadab your father, and you have kept all his commandments, and you have done according to all that he commanded you"** (v18) — יַעַן אֲשֶׁר "because"; שְׁמַעְתֶּם עַל, literally "listened upon"; שָׁמַר "keep"; מִצְוֺתָיו "his commandments" (מִצְוָה, the same word as "commandment" in the verse; not "precepts", which is פִּקּוּדִים).
- **"Jonadab the son of Rechab shall not want a man to stand before me for ever" → "There shall not be cut off for Jonadab son of Rechab a man standing before me all the days"** (v19) — לֹא־יִכָּרֵת אִישׁ לְ, as Jer 33:17, 18 and the Kings row; עֹמֵד לְפָנַי "standing before me" (the phrase of service before Yahweh); כָּל־הַיָּמִים "all the days", not "forever".

### Notes

- **Rechabites** — the house of Jehonadab son of Rechab (2 Kgs 10:15–23; 1 Chr 2:55), held up against Judah: they keep the word of a father, Judah does not keep the word of Yahweh. The time is Jehoiakim's reign, before Jer 34 in time (v1, 11).
