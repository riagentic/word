# Jeremiah 5

[1] Roam through the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
and see, please, and know,
and seek in her squares,
if you can find a man,
if there is one doing judgment,
seeking faithfulness,
and I will pardon her.

[2] And if they say, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives,"
therefore to falsehood they swear.

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your eyes, are they not to faithfulness?
You have struck them, and they were not in anguish;
you have made an end of them, they have refused to take discipline;
they have made their faces stronger than a crag,
they have refused to turn back.

[4] And I said, "Only the poor are they;
they are foolish,
for they do not know the way of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the judgment of their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] Let me get myself to the great
and speak with them,
for they know the way of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the judgment of their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."
But they together have broken the yoke,
they have torn off the bonds.

[6] Therefore a lion from the forest has struck them,
a wolf of the steppes lays them waste,
a leopard keeps watch over their cities;
everyone who goes out from them is torn to pieces;
for their transgressions are many,
their turnings away are mighty.

[7] How, for this, shall I pardon you?
Your sons have forsaken me
and sworn by no-gods;
and I fed them full, and they committed adultery,
and to the house of a prostitute they troop.

[8] Well-fed, lusty horses they have been;
each man to the wife of his neighbor they neigh.

[9] Shall I not visit for these things?
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
or on a nation such as this
shall my soul not avenge itself?

[10] Go up into her vine rows and ruin,
but a full end do not make;
remove her tendrils,
for they are not ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧.

[11] For they have surely dealt treacherously with me,
the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] They have lied about ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and said, "Not he!
And evil shall not come upon us,
and sword and famine we shall not see.

[13] And the prophets shall become wind,
and the word is not in them;
thus shall it be done to them."

[14] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts:
Because you have spoken this word,
behold, I am making my words in your mouth fire,
and this people wood,
and it shall devour them.

[15] Behold, I am bringing upon you a nation from afar,
house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
an enduring nation it is,
a nation from of old it is,
a nation whose tongue you do not know,
and you will not understand what it speaks.

[16] Its quiver is like an open grave;
all of them are mighty ones.

[17] And it shall eat your harvest and your bread;
they shall eat your sons and your daughters;
it shall eat your flock and your herd;
it shall eat your vine and your fig tree;
it shall shatter your fortified cities,
in which you trust, with the sword.

[18] And even in those days, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, I will not make a full end with you.

[19] And it shall be, when you say, "Why has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ done all these things to us?" then you shall say to them, "As you have forsaken me and served foreign gods in your land, so you shall serve strangers in a land that is not yours."

[20] Declare this in the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and make it heard in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying:

[21] Hear this, please,
foolish people and without heart:
eyes they have, and they do not see;
ears they have, and they do not hear.

[22] Me — do you not fear? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
or from my face do you not writhe?
I who have set the sand a border for the sea,
an everlasting limit, and it does not pass over it;
and they toss, and they do not prevail,
and its waves roar, and they do not pass over it.

[23] And this people has a heart stubborn and rebellious;
they have turned aside and gone.

[24] And they have not said in their heart,
"Let us fear, please, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
who gives rain, the early rain and the latter rain, in its season;
the weeks, the statutes of harvest, he keeps for us."

[25] Your iniquities have turned these away,
and your sins have held back the good from you.

[26] For wicked ones are found among my people;
one watches, as fowlers crouch;
they set up a destroyer,
men they capture.

[27] Like a cage full of birds,
so their houses are full of deceit;
therefore they have become great and grown rich.

[28] They have grown fat, they are sleek;
also they have passed over the matters of evil;
the cause they have not judged,
the cause of the fatherless, that they might prosper,
and the judgment of the needy they have not judged.

[29] Shall I not visit for these things?
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
or on a nation such as this
shall my soul not avenge itself?

[30] A thing that appalls and a horrible thing
has come to be in the land:

[31] the prophets prophesy in falsehood,
and the priests rule by their hands,
and my people love it so;
and what will you do at its end?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"O LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"LORD God of hosts" → "Yahweh, Elohim of Hosts"** (v14) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת, both names; "Hosts" plain, as the prophets' "Yahweh of Hosts".
- **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v9, 11, 15, 18, 22, 29) — נְאֻם יְהוָה, literally "the declaration of Yahweh".
- **Layout** — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; v18–19 are prose, as the Hebrew runs on without short lines. No outer quotation marks: the chapter is Yahweh's and the prophet's speech throughout; only the words quoted inside it (v2, 4–5, 12–13, 19, 24) are marked.
- **"Run ye to and fro … see now … the broad places thereof" → "Roam … see, please … her squares"** (v1) — שׁוֹטְטוּ "roam, go to and fro"; נָא "please"; רְחוֹבוֹת "squares" (the open places of a town; חוּצוֹת is "streets"). **"executeth judgment, that seeketh the truth" → "doing judgment, seeking faithfulness"** — עֹשֶׂה מִשְׁפָּט, מְבַקֵּשׁ אֱמוּנָה: participles; אֱמוּנָה is "faithfulness" (אֱמֶת is "truth"), also v3. **"and I will pardon it" → "and I will pardon her"** — סָלַח; the suffix is feminine: the city.
- **"surely they swear falsely" → "therefore to falsehood they swear"** (v2) — וְאִם … לָכֵן: "And if … therefore"; לָכֵן is "therefore" (KJV "surely" gives the sense: even their oath by Yahweh is false). לַשֶּׁקֶר "to falsehood".
- **"are not thine eyes upon the truth?" → "your eyes, are they not to faithfulness?"** (v3) — הֲלוֹא לֶאֱמוּנָה, word for word. **"they have not grieved" → "they were not in anguish"** — חָלוּ, of חִיל "writhe, be in anguish" (also v22 "writhe"). **"consumed them" → "made an end of them"** — כִּלָּה with persons. **"receive correction" → "take discipline"** — לָקַחַת מוּסָר, "take" (לָקַח). **"harder than a rock" → "stronger than a crag"** — חִזְּקוּ "made strong" (not the verb "harden"); סֶלַע "crag". **"return" → "turn back"** — שׁוּב.
- **"Therefore I said, Surely these are poor" → "And I said, Only the poor are they"** (v4) — וַאֲנִי אָמַרְתִּי "And I, I said"; אַךְ "only". **"they are foolish"** — נוֹאֲלוּ, kept. **"nor the judgment" → "the judgment"** — no "nor" in the Hebrew: the second line names the way again.
- **"I will get me unto the great men" → "Let me get myself to the great"** (v5) — אֵלֲכָה־לִּי, cohortative with לִּי, as the reviewer's "Get yourself" row; "men" is not written. **"speak unto them" → "speak with them"** — אוֹתָם, here "with them". **"these have altogether broken" → "they together have broken"** — אַךְ הֵמָּה יַחְדָּו: אַךְ "but", יַחְדָּו "together". **"burst the bonds" → "torn off the bonds"** — נִתְּקוּ, "tear off, tear away".
- **"shall slay them … shall spoil them … shall watch" → "has struck them … lays them waste … keeps watch"** (v6) — הִכָּם a perfect ("has struck": what is sure is told as done), יְשָׁדְדֵם an imperfect, שֹׁקֵד a participle; English keeps the change of forms. הִכָּה "strike"; שָׁדַד "lay waste"; שָׁקַד "keep watch, keep awake". **"a wolf of the evenings" → "a wolf of the steppes"** — זְאֵב עֲרָבוֹת: the pointing is the plural of עֲרָבָה "steppe, desert plain"; KJV and others read עֶרֶב "evening" (as Hab 1:8; Zeph 3:3, where the pointing is עֶרֶב). **"their backslidings are increased" → "their turnings away are mighty"** — the qere מְשׁוּבוֹתֵיהֶם (the ketiv is the same word, defectively written); מְשׁוּבָה is a noun of שׁוּב, "turning away" (also 8:5); עָצְמוּ "be mighty".
- **"How shall I pardon thee for this?" → "How, for this, shall I pardon you?"** (v7) — אֵי לָזֹאת; the qere אֶסְלַח (the ketiv אסלוח is the same verb, fully written); "you" feminine singular, the city. **"thy children" → "Your sons"** — בָּנַיִךְ. **"by them that are no gods" → "by no-gods"** — בְּלֹא אֱלֹהִים, plain text, as the Song of Moses' "no-El" (Deut 32:17, 21). **"when I had fed them to the full, they then committed adultery" → "and I fed them full, and they committed adultery"** — וָאַשְׂבִּעַ … וַיִּנְאָפוּ, two clauses with "and"; the pointing is שָׂבַע "satisfy" (a reading "I made them swear", from שָׁבַע, is defended: the two roots are spelled alike). **"assembled themselves by troops in the harlots' houses" → "to the house of a prostitute they troop"** — בֵּית זוֹנָה, singular; זוֹנָה "prostitute"; יִתְגֹּדָדוּ "gather in troops" (the same form also means "cut themselves", Jer 16:6; 41:5: uncertain).
- **"They were as fed horses in the morning" → "Well-fed, lusty horses they have been"** (v8) — סוּסִים מְיֻזָּנִים מַשְׁכִּים הָיוּ: no "as" in the Hebrew; both adjectives are uncertain. מְיֻזָּנִים is taken from זוּן "feed"; מַשְׁכִּים from a root "be heavy, be lusty" (KJV "in the morning" takes it from הִשְׁכִּים "rise early"). **"every one neighed after" → "each man to the wife of his neighbor they neigh"** — אִישׁ אֶל־אֵשֶׁת רֵעֵהוּ יִצְהָלוּ.
- **"Shall I not visit … and shall not my soul be avenged" → "Shall I not visit … or on a nation such as this shall my soul not avenge itself?"** (v9, 29) — פָּקַד "visit"; אִם opens the second question ("or"); v9 has וְאִם, with "and", which English cannot add to "or"; v29 has אִם alone. תִּתְנַקֵּם נַפְשִׁי, hitpael "avenge itself"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul". The two verses are otherwise the same and are rendered alike.
- **"Go ye up upon her walls … take away her battlements" → "Go up into her vine rows … remove her tendrils"** (v10) — שָׁרוֹת is found only here (uncertain: "vine rows" or "terraced walls", from שׁוּר "wall"); נְטִישׁוֹת "tendrils, shoots" (Isa 18:5); with "for they are not Yahweh's" the picture is the vine. שַׁחֵתוּ "ruin"; כָּלָה "a full end" (also v18); הָסִירוּ "remove".
- **"have dealt very treacherously against me" → "have surely dealt treacherously with me"** (v11) — בָּגוֹד בָּגְדוּ בִּי, infinitive absolute with the verb (literally "dealing treacherously they have dealt treacherously"); the subject comes after, as in the Hebrew.
- **"They have belied the LORD" → "They have lied about Yahweh"** (v12) — כִּחֲשׁוּ בַּיהוָה, "lie about, deny"; **"It is not he" → "Not he!"** — לֹא־הוּא. **"neither shall evil … neither shall we see" → "And evil … and sword and famine we shall not see"** — each clause with "and"; רָעָה "evil".
- **"thus shall it be done unto them"** (v13) — kept; it may close the people's words (the prophets will get what they foretell) or be the prophet's answer; the setumah after v13 and "Because you have spoken this word" (v14) suggest the people's words. הַדִּבֵּר "the word", a form found only here.
- **"Because ye speak this word, behold, I will make" → "Because you have spoken this word, behold, I am making"** (v14) — דַּבֶּרְכֶם, "your speaking", plural "you"; הִנְנִי נֹתֵן, a participle: "I am making" (literally "giving … for fire"); "your mouth" is singular: the prophet.
- **"it is a mighty nation" → "an enduring nation it is"** (v15) — גּוֹי אֵיתָן, "lasting, never failing" (as the "ever-flowing" wadi, Deut 21:4); **"an ancient nation" → "a nation from of old"** — מֵעוֹלָם. **"whose language" → "whose tongue"** — לָשׁוֹן. **"neither understandest what they say" → "and you will not understand what it speaks"** — שָׁמַע of a language is "understand" here (literally "hear": the tongue is heard, not understood); the verb is singular: the nation.
- **"an open sepulchre … mighty men" → "an open grave … mighty ones"** (v16) — קֶבֶר "grave"; גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones".
- **"they shall eat up thine harvest, and thy bread, which thy sons and thy daughters should eat" → "it shall eat your harvest and your bread; they shall eat your sons and your daughters"** (v17) — the accents set יֹאכְלוּ בָּנֶיךָ וּבְנוֹתֶיךָ as its own clause, "they shall eat your sons and your daughters"; KJV joins it to the bread as a relative clause ("which … should eat"): the Hebrew allows both, the verse follows the accents. The verbs are singular, then plural, then singular: kept. **"thy flocks and thine herds … vines … fig trees" → "your flock and your herd … your vine and your fig tree"** — singular, צֹאן "flock", בָּקָר "herd". **"impoverish thy fenced cities" → "shatter your fortified cities"** — יְרֹשֵׁשׁ "beat down, shatter"; עָרֵי מִבְצָרֶיךָ "your fortified cities" (literally "the cities of your fortifications").
- **"Nevertheless in those days" → "And even in those days"** (v18) — וְגַם.
- **"And it shall come to pass, when ye shall say" → "And it shall be, when you say"** (v19) — וְהָיָה כִּי. **"Wherefore" → "Why"** — תַּחַת מֶה, literally "in return for what". **"then shalt thou answer them" → "then you shall say to them"** — וְאָמַרְתָּ "and you shall say", singular: the prophet. **"strange gods" → "foreign gods"** — אֱלֹהֵי נֵכָר (literally "gods of foreignness"); זָרִים "strangers" is another word.
- **"publish it" → "make it heard"** (v20) — הַשְׁמִיעוּהָ.
- **"Hear now this, O foolish people, and without understanding" → "Hear this, please, foolish people and without heart"** (v21) — נָא "please"; no "O"; וְאֵין לֵב "and there is no heart" (the heart as the seat of understanding). **"which have eyes, and see not" → "eyes they have, and they do not see"** — עֵינַיִם לָהֶם, no relative; "they" plural, after the singular "people".
- **"Fear ye not me?" → "Me — do you not fear?"** (v22) — הַאוֹתִי לֹא־תִירָאוּ, "me" first. **"will ye not tremble at my presence" → "or from my face do you not writhe?"** — אִם "or"; מִפָּנַי "from my face"; תָחִילוּ, of חִיל (as v3). **"for the bound of the sea by a perpetual decree" → "a border for the sea, an everlasting limit"** — גְּבוּל "border"; חָק־עוֹלָם: חֹק of the sea's bound is "limit" (as Job 38:10), עוֹלָם as a genitive "everlasting". **"though the waves thereof toss themselves, yet can they not prevail; though they roar" → "and they toss, and they do not prevail, and its waves roar"** — each clause with וְ, no "though"; יוּכָלוּ "they are able" (here "prevail"); הָמָה "roar".
- **"a revolting and a rebellious heart; they are revolted and gone" → "a heart stubborn and rebellious; they have turned aside and gone"** (v23) — סוֹרֵר וּמוֹרֶה "stubborn and rebellious" (as Deut 21:18); סָרוּ "turned aside" (סוּר).
- **"Neither say they" → "And they have not said"** (v24) — perfect. **"Let us now fear" → "Let us fear, please"** — נָא. **"both the former and the latter" → "the early rain and the latter rain"** — the qere יוֹרֶה (the ketiv וירה is the same word); "rain" is in both words. **"he reserveth unto us the appointed weeks of the harvest" → "the weeks, the statutes of harvest, he keeps for us"** — שְׁבֻעוֹת חֻקּוֹת קָצִיר, word for word (the weeks fixed for the harvest); שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"have withholden good things" → "have held back the good"** (v25) — מָנְעוּ הַטּוֹב, singular with the article. עֲוֹנוֹת "iniquities", חַטֹּאות "sins".
- **"wicked men … they lay wait, as he that setteth snares; they set a trap" → "wicked ones … one watches, as fowlers crouch; they set up a destroyer"** (v26) — רְשָׁעִים "wicked ones" ("men" is not written); יָשׁוּר, singular, "watch" (שׁוּר); כְּשַׁךְ יְקוּשִׁים is uncertain (שַׁךְ perhaps "crouching", from שָׁכַךְ); מַשְׁחִית "a destroyer" (= a trap, what ruins); הִצִּיבוּ "set up"; לָכַד "capture".
- **"they are become great, and waxen rich" → "they have become great and grown rich"** (v27) — גָּדְלוּ וַיַּעֲשִׁירוּ. מִרְמָה "deceit".
- **"they shine" → "they are sleek"** (v28) — עָשְׁתוּ, found only here (uncertain: "be smooth, sleek"). **"yea, they overpass the deeds of the wicked" → "also they have passed over the matters of evil"** — גַּם עָבְרוּ דִבְרֵי־רָע: עָבַר "pass over" (they go beyond the bounds of evil, or overlook evil deeds: uncertain); דְּבָרִים "matters". **"they judge not the cause" → "the cause they have not judged"** — דִּין לֹא־דָנוּ: noun and verb of one root ("judge" is also שָׁפַט, the last line: English has one word). **"yet they prosper" → "that they might prosper"** — וְיַצְלִיחוּ, a non-narrative form: "that they (the fatherless, or the judges) might prosper"; the subject is not clear. **"the right of the needy" → "the judgment of the needy"** — מִשְׁפַּט; אֶבְיוֹנִים "needy".
- **"A wonderful and horrible thing is committed" → "A thing that appalls and a horrible thing has come to be"** (v30) — שַׁמָּה (root of שָׁמֵם "be appalled") וְשַׁעֲרוּרָה; נִהְיְתָה "has come to be".
- **"prophesy falsely" → "prophesy in falsehood"** (v31) — בַשֶּׁקֶר. **"bear rule by their means" → "rule by their hands"** — עַל־יְדֵיהֶם, literally "upon their hands" (at the prophets' direction, or by their own hand: uncertain). **"love to have it so" → "love it so"** — אָהֲבוּ כֵן. **"in the end thereof" → "at its end"** — לְאַחֲרִיתָהּ.
