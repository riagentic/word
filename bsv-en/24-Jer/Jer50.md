# Jeremiah 50

[1] The word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke concerning ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, concerning the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, by the hand of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet:

[2] Declare among the nations and make heard, and lift up a banner;
make heard, do not hide;
say: ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ is captured,
Bel is put to shame, Merodach is dismayed;
her images are put to shame, her idols are dismayed.

[3] For a nation has come up against her from the north;
it shall make her land a thing that appalls,
and there shall be none dwelling in it;
from man to beast they have wandered off, they are gone.

[4] In those days and in that time, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall come, they and the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ together;
going and weeping they shall go,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ they shall seek.

[5] They shall ask the way to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, their faces hither:
Come! And they shall join themselves to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
in an everlasting covenant that shall not be forgotten.

[6] A perishing flock my people have been;
their shepherds have caused them to wander;
on the mountains they turned them back;
from mountain to hill they have gone;
they have forgotten their resting place.

[7] All who found them devoured them,
and their adversaries said: We are not guilty,
because they have sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the habitation of righteousness,
and the hope of their fathers, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] Wander away from the midst of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and from the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ go out,
and be like he-goats before the flock.

[9] For behold, I am stirring up and bringing up against ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧
an assembly of great nations from the land of the north,
and they shall set themselves in array against her;
from there she shall be captured;
his arrows are like a skilled mighty one;
he shall not return empty.

[10] And ⟦chaldea|Kasdim|Chaldea⟧ shall be for spoil;
all who spoil her shall be sated, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] Because you rejoice, because you exult,
pillagers of my inheritance,
because you frisk like a heifer threshing,
and neigh like the mighty ones,

[12] your mother is very ashamed,
she who bore you is abashed;
behold, the latter end of the nations:
a wilderness, a dry land, and a desert plain.

[13] Because of the wrath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ she shall not be inhabited,
and she shall become a desolation, all of her;
everyone who passes by ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ shall be appalled
and shall hiss over all her blows.

[14] Set yourselves in array against ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ all around,
all who tread the bow;
shoot at her, do not spare an arrow,
for against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ she has sinned.

[15] Shout against her all around;
she has given her hand;
her foundations have fallen,
her walls are thrown down;
for it is the vengeance of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
take vengeance on her;
as she has done, do to her.

[16] Cut off the sower from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and him who holds the sickle in the time of harvest;
from the face of the oppressing sword
they shall turn each man to his people,
and each man to his land they shall flee.

[17] A scattered sheep is ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; lions have driven him away;
the first, the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, devoured him,
and this last, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, has broken his bones.

[18] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:
Behold, I am visiting the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and his land,
as I visited the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.

[19] And I will bring ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ back to his habitation,
and he shall feed on the ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ and the Bashan;
and on the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and the ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧
his soul shall be sated.

[20] In those days and in that time, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the iniquity of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be sought, and there is none,
and the sins of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they shall not be found;
for I will pardon those whom I leave over.

[21] Against the land of Merathaim, go up against it,
and against the inhabitants of ⟦pekod|Peqod|Pekod⟧;
lay waste and devote to destruction after them, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and do according to all that I have commanded you.

[22] A sound of war in the land,
and a great breaking!

[23] How is hewn off and broken
the hammer of all the earth!
How ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ has become a thing that appalls
among the nations!

[24] I laid a snare for you, and you were also captured, ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and you did not know;
you were found and also seized,
for you have provoked ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[25] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has opened his treasury
and brought out the weapons of his indignation;
for it is a work for ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts
in the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧.

[26] Come to her from the end;
open her granaries;
heap her up like heaps, and devote her to destruction;
let her have no remnant.

[27] Lay waste all her bulls;
let them go down to the slaughter.
Woe to them, for their day has come,
the time of their visitation.

[28] A voice of those who flee and those who escape from the land of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
to declare in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ the vengeance of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the vengeance of his temple.

[29] Make the archers heard against ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, all who tread the bow;
camp against her all around;
let there be no escape for her;
repay her according to her work;
according to all that she has done, do to her;
for against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ she has acted presumptuously,
against the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[30] Therefore her young men shall fall in her squares,
and all her men of war shall be silenced in that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] Behold, I am against you, Presumption,
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts,
for your day has come,
the time that I visit you.

[32] And Presumption shall stumble and fall,
and there is none to raise him up;
and I will kindle a fire in his cities,
and it shall devour all around him.

[33] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:
Oppressed are the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧
and the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ together;
and all who took them captive have held them fast;
they have refused to let them go.

[34] Their Redeemer is strong;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name;
he will surely contend their case,
so that he may give rest to the earth,
and give unrest to the inhabitants of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[35] A sword against the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and against the inhabitants of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and against her princes and against her wise men.

[36] A sword against the empty talkers, and they shall become fools;
a sword against her mighty ones, and they shall be dismayed.

[37] A sword against his horses and against his chariots,
and against all the mixed people who are in her midst,
and they shall become women;
a sword against her treasures, and they shall be plundered.

[38] A drought against her waters, and they shall be dried up;
for it is a land of carved images,
and over terrors they act madly.

[39] Therefore desert dwellers shall dwell with hyenas,
and daughters of the ostrich shall dwell in her;
and she shall not be inhabited again forever,
nor be dwelt in to generation and generation.

[40] As at the overthrow by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧ and her neighbors, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
no man shall dwell there,
and no son of man shall sojourn in her.

[41] Behold, a people is coming from the north,
and a great nation and many kings
are stirred up from the remote parts of the earth.

[42] Bow and javelin they hold fast;
they are cruel and will not have compassion;
their voice roars like the sea,
and on horses they ride,
set in array like a man for the battle,
against you, daughter of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[43] The king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ has heard the report of them,
and his hands have grown slack;
distress has seized him,
anguish like a woman giving birth.

[44] Behold, like a lion he shall come up from the pride of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧
to a lasting habitation;
for in a moment I will make them run from her,
and whoever is chosen, I will appoint him over her.
For who is like me? And who will summon me?
And who is this shepherd who will stand before me?

[45] Therefore hear the counsel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he has counseled against ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and his plans that he has devised against the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧:
Surely they shall drag them away, the little ones of the flock;
surely a habitation shall be made desolate over them.

[46] At the sound "⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ is seized" the earth is shaken,
and an outcry is heard among the nations.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת "Yahweh of Hosts" (v18, 33, 34); אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת "the Lord Yahweh of Hosts" (v25, 31); אֱלֹהִים token (v4, 18, 28, 40).
- **Layout and speech marks** — v1 is prose, v2–46 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents. The outer quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where the words of Yahweh begin and end. No "O" before names and titles (v11, 24, 31, 42).
- **"by Jeremiah the prophet" → "by the hand of Jeremiah the prophet"** (v1) — בְּיַד.
- **"Declare ye among the nations, and publish, and set up a standard; publish, and conceal not: say, Babylon is taken, Bel is confounded, Merodach is broken in pieces; her idols are confounded, her images are broken in pieces" → "Declare among the nations and make heard, and lift up a banner; make heard, do not hide; say: Babylon is captured, Bel is put to shame, Merodach is dismayed; her images are put to shame, her idols are dismayed"** (v2) — הִשְׁמִיעַ "make heard" (twice); נָשָׂא נֵס "lift up a banner"; כִּחֵד "hide"; לָכַד "capture"; הֹבִישׁ "is put to shame" (hifil of בּוֹשׁ, said of the gods); חַת · חַתּוּ "dismayed" (lit. "shattered"); עֲצַבִּים "images" (lit. "shaped things"), גִּלּוּלִים "idols" (a word of contempt). Bel ("lord") and Merodach are names of the god of Babylon (Marduk).
- **"which shall make her land desolate, and none shall dwell therein: they shall remove, they shall depart, both man and beast" → "it shall make her land a thing that appalls, and there shall be none dwelling in it; from man to beast they have wandered off, they are gone"** (v3) — שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls"; מֵאָדָם וְעַד־בְּהֵמָה "from man to beast"; נָדוּ הָלָכוּ perfects, "they have wandered off, they are gone" (נוּד).
- **"the children of Israel shall come, they and the children of Judah together, going and weeping: they shall go, and seek the LORD their God" → "the sons of Israel shall come, they and the sons of Judah together; going and weeping they shall go, and Yahweh their Elohim they shall seek"** (v4) — בְּנֵי "sons of" (also v33); הָלוֹךְ וּבָכוֹ יֵלֵכוּ "going and weeping they shall go" (two infinitives absolute: the going goes on with weeping); בִּקֵּשׁ "seek".
- **"They shall ask the way to Zion with their faces thitherward, saying, Come, and let us join ourselves to the LORD in a perpetual covenant that shall not be forgotten" → "They shall ask the way to Zion, their faces hither: Come! And they shall join themselves to Yahweh in an everlasting covenant that shall not be forgotten"** (v5) — הֵנָּה "hither"; no "saying"; בֹּאוּ imperative "Come!"; וְנִלְווּ is pointed as a perfect with וְ, "and they shall join themselves" (נִלְוָה "be joined to"), not "let us join" (that would be וְנִלְוֶה): the versions and many translations read the cohortative (from memory); "in" before בְּרִית עוֹלָם is supplied (an accusative of manner).
- **"My people hath been lost sheep: their shepherds have caused them to go astray, they have turned them away on the mountains: they have gone from mountain to hill, they have forgotten their restingplace" → "A perishing flock my people have been; their shepherds have caused them to wander; on the mountains they turned them back; from mountain to hill they have gone; they have forgotten their resting place"** (v6) — the qere הָיוּ "have been" (plural; written היה) and שׁוֹבְבוּם "they turned them back" (polel of שׁוּב); the written שובבים gives "mountains that turn back"; צֹאן אֹבְדוֹת "a perishing (lost) flock"; הִתְעָה "cause to wander"; רֵבֶץ "resting place" (where the flock lies down).
- **"and their adversaries said, We offend not, because they have sinned against the LORD, the habitation of justice, even the LORD, the hope of their fathers" → "and their adversaries said: We are not guilty, because they have sinned against Yahweh, the habitation of righteousness, and the hope of their fathers, Yahweh"** (v7) — צָרִים "adversaries"; לֹא נֶאְשָׁם "we are not guilty" (אָשֵׁם); תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר "because"; נְוֵה־צֶדֶק "habitation of righteousness" (נָוֶה, also v19, 44, 45); מִקְוֵה "hope" (also "pool, gathering": from קָוָה); the Hebrew order is kept, Yahweh last.
- **"Remove out of the midst of Babylon, and go forth out of the land of the Chaldeans, and be as the he goats before the flocks" → "Wander away from the midst of Babylon, and from the land of the Chaldeans go out, and be like he-goats before the flock"** (v8) — נֻדוּ "wander away" (נוּד, as v3; 49:30); the qere צְאוּ "go out" is read (the written text has יצאו "they go out"); עַתּוּדִים "he-goats".
- **"an assembly of great nations from the north country: and they shall set themselves in array against her; from thence she shall be taken: their arrows shall be as of a mighty expert man; none shall return in vain" → "an assembly of great nations from the land of the north, and they shall set themselves in array against her; from there she shall be captured; his arrows are like a skilled mighty one; he shall not return empty"** (v9) — מֵעִיר וּמַעֲלֶה participles "stirring up and bringing up"; קְהַל גּוֹיִם "an assembly of nations"; עָרַךְ "set in array"; חִצָּיו "his arrows" (singular: the enemy as one); כְּגִבּוֹר מַשְׁכִּיל "like a skilled (wise-acting) mighty one", as printed; many manuscripts read מְשַׁכֵּל "bereaving" (from memory); רֵיקָם "empty".
- **"And Chaldea shall be a spoil: all that spoil her shall be satisfied" → "And Chaldea shall be for spoil; all who spoil her shall be sated"** (v10) — שָׁלָל · שֹׁלְלִים one root; שָׂבַע "be sated".
- **"Because ye were glad, because ye rejoiced, O ye destroyers of mine heritage, because ye are grown fat as the heifer at grass, and bellow as bulls" → "Because you rejoice, because you exult, pillagers of my inheritance, because you frisk like a heifer threshing, and neigh like the mighty ones"** (v11) — the four verbs are read with the qere (plural "you"; the written forms are feminine singular); שָׂמַח "rejoice", עָלַז "exult"; שֹׁסִים "pillagers" (שָׁסָה); נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; פּוּשׁ "frisk"; עֶגְלָה דָשָׁה "a heifer threshing", as pointed (KJV "at grass" reads דֶּשֶׁא); צָהַל "neigh"; אַבִּירִים "mighty ones" (stallions are meant).
- **"Your mother shall be sore confounded; she that bare you shall be ashamed: behold, the hindermost of the nations shall be a wilderness, a dry land, and a desert" → "your mother is very ashamed, she who bore you is abashed; behold, the latter end of the nations: a wilderness, a dry land, and a desert plain"** (v12) — בּוֹשָׁה perfect "is ashamed"; חָפְרָה "is abashed" (חָפֵר); אַחֲרִית "latter end"; צִיָּה "dry land"; עֲרָבָה "desert plain".
- **"Because of the wrath of the LORD it shall not be inhabited, but it shall be wholly desolate … and hiss at all her plagues" → "Because of the wrath of Yahweh she shall not be inhabited, and she shall become a desolation, all of her … and shall hiss over all her blows"** (v13) — קֶצֶף "wrath"; לֹא תֵשֵׁב, qal of יָשַׁב "dwell", of a city "be inhabited" (also v39); שְׁמָמָה כֻּלָּהּ "a desolation, all of her"; וְ "and" (not "but"); מַכּוֹת "blows" (as 49:17).
- **"all ye that bend the bow, shoot at her, spare no arrows" → "all who tread the bow; shoot at her, do not spare an arrow"** (v14, 29) — דֹּרְכֵי קֶשֶׁת "treaders of the bow" (the bow is strung by treading on it); יָדָה "shoot"; חָמַל "spare"; חֵץ singular.
- **"Shout against her round about: she hath given her hand: her foundations are fallen, her walls are thrown down: for it is the vengeance of the LORD: take vengeance upon her; as she hath done, do unto her" → "Shout against her all around; she has given her hand; her foundations have fallen, her walls are thrown down …"** (v15) — הֵרִיעַ "shout" (of voices); נָתְנָה יָדָהּ "she has given her hand" (= she has surrendered: the sense, not the words); the qere אָשְׁיוֹתֶיהָ "her foundations" (only here; buttresses or foundations: uncertain; the written אשויתיה the same word); נֶהֶרְסוּ "are thrown down"; הִנָּקְמוּ "take vengeance".
- **"for fear of the oppressing sword they shall turn every one to his people, and they shall flee every one to his own land" → "from the face of the oppressing sword they shall turn each man to his people, and each man to his land they shall flee"** (v16) — מִפְּנֵי "from the face of"; חֶרֶב הַיּוֹנָה "the oppressing sword" (יוֹנָה participle of יָנָה "oppress"; the same letters are "the dove": also Jer 46:16); תֹּפֵשׂ מַגָּל "who holds the sickle".
- **"Israel is a scattered sheep; the lions have driven him away: first the king of Assyria hath devoured him; and last this Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon hath broken his bones" → "A scattered sheep is Israel; lions have driven him away; the first, the king of Assyria, devoured him, and this last, Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, has broken his bones"** (v17) — שֶׂה "sheep" (not "hunted"); עִצֵּם, only here, "break the bones".
- **"I will punish the king of Babylon … as I have punished the king of Assyria" → "I am visiting the king of Babylon … as I visited the king of Assyria"** (v18) — פֹּקֵד אֶל participle, "visit (upon)": פָּקַד "visit", for punishment here; also v27 פְּקֻדָּה "visitation", v31.
- **"And I will bring Israel again to his habitation, and he shall feed on Carmel and Bashan, and his soul shall be satisfied upon mount Ephraim and Gilead" → "And I will bring Israel back to his habitation, and he shall feed on the Carmel and the Bashan; and on the hill country of Ephraim and the Gilead his soul shall be sated"** (v19) — שׁוֹבֵב polel of שׁוּב; נָוֶה "habitation" (a pasture-dwelling); רָעָה "feed" (of the flock); the articles of הַכַּרְמֶל, הַבָּשָׁן, הַגִּלְעָד kept; הַר אֶפְרַיִם "the hill country of Ephraim".
- **"the iniquity of Israel shall be sought for, and there shall be none; and the sins of Judah, and they shall not be found: for I will pardon them whom I reserve" → "the iniquity of Israel shall be sought, and there is none, and the sins of Judah, and they shall not be found; for I will pardon those whom I leave over"** (v20) — עָוֹן "iniquity", חַטֹּאת "sins" (plural, with a plural verb); סָלַח "pardon"; הִשְׁאִיר "leave over" ("as a remnant" was added).
- **"Go up against the land of Merathaim, even against it, and against the inhabitants of Pekod: waste and utterly destroy after them" → "Against the land of Merathaim, go up against it, and against the inhabitants of Pekod; lay waste and devote to destruction after them"** (v21) — מְרָתַיִם "Merathaim" (a name for the land by the Gulf; heard as "double rebellion", from מָרָה) and פְּקוֹד "Pekod" (a people of Babylonia; heard as "visitation", from פָּקַד): the names are kept, the plays named here; חֲרֹב "lay waste" (as pointed; "put to the sword", from חֶרֶב, is also read; also v27); הַחֲרֵם "devote to destruction".
- **"A sound of battle is in the land, and of great destruction" → "A sound of war in the land, and a great breaking!"** (v22) — מִלְחָמָה "war"; שֶׁבֶר "breaking".
- **"How is the hammer of the whole earth cut asunder and broken! how is Babylon become a desolation among the nations!" → "How is hewn off and broken the hammer of all the earth! How Babylon has become a thing that appalls among the nations!"** (v23) — נִגְדַּע "be hewn off"; שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls".
- **"I have laid a snare for thee, and thou art also taken, O Babylon, and thou wast not aware: thou art found, and also caught, because thou hast striven against the LORD" → "I laid a snare for you, and you were also captured, Babylon, and you did not know; you were found and also seized, for you have provoked Yahweh"** (v24) — יָקֹשְׁתִּי "I laid a snare"; וְגַם twice "also"; לָכַד "capture", תָּפַשׂ "seize" (also v46); הִתְגָּרָה בְּ "provoke" (stir oneself up against).
- **"The LORD hath opened his armoury, and hath brought forth the weapons of his indignation: for this is the work of the Lord GOD of hosts in the land of the Chaldeans" → "Yahweh has opened his treasury and brought out the weapons of his indignation; for it is a work for the Lord Yahweh of Hosts in the land of the Chaldeans"** (v25) — אוֹצָר "treasury" (also v37 "treasures"); כְּלֵי "weapons" (the word is "articles, gear"); זַעַם "indignation"; מְלָאכָה הִיא לַאדֹנָי "it is a work for the Lord".
- **"Come against her from the utmost border, open her storehouses: cast her up as heaps, and destroy her utterly: let nothing of her be left" → "Come to her from the end; open her granaries; heap her up like heaps, and devote her to destruction; let her have no remnant"** (v26) — מִקֵּץ "from the end" (from every far quarter); מַאֲבוּסִים, only here, "granaries"; סָלַל "heap up"; עֲרֵמִים "heaps"; הֶחֱרִים "devote to destruction"; שְׁאֵרִית "remnant".
- **"Slay all her bullocks; let them go down to the slaughter: woe unto them! for their day is come, the time of their visitation" → "Lay waste all her bulls; let them go down to the slaughter. Woe to them, for their day has come, the time of their visitation"** (v27) — חִרְבוּ, the root of v21 (pointed "lay waste"; "put to the sword" also read); פָּרִים "bulls" (the young men are meant); טֶבַח "slaughter"; הוֹי "Woe".
- **"The voice of them that flee and escape … the vengeance of his temple" → "A voice of those who flee and those who escape … the vengeance of his temple"** (v28) — נָסִים "those who flee", פְּלֵטִים "those who escape" (two words); הֵיכָל "temple".
- **"Call together the archers against Babylon: all ye that bend the bow, camp against it round about; let none thereof escape: recompense her according to her work … for she hath been proud against the LORD" → "Make the archers heard against Babylon, all who tread the bow; camp against her all around; let there be no escape for her; repay her according to her work … for against Yahweh she has acted presumptuously"** (v29) — הַשְׁמִיעוּ "make heard" (= summon); רַבִּים "archers" (from רָבַב "shoot", as Job 16:13; the same letters are "many": uncertain); חָנָה עַל "camp against"; the qere adds לָהּ "for her"; פְּלֵטָה "escape"; שִׁלֵּם "repay"; פֹּעַל "work"; זָד "act presumptuously" (also v31–32 זָדוֹן).
- **"and all her men of war shall be cut off in that day" → "and all her men of war shall be silenced in that day"** (v30) — as 49:26: יִדַּמּוּ "be silenced"; here מִלְחַמְתָּהּ "her (men of) war"; "declares Yahweh" (49:26 "Yahweh of Hosts").
- **"Behold, I am against thee, O thou most proud … And the most proud shall stumble and fall, and none shall raise him up" → "Behold, I am against you, Presumption … And Presumption shall stumble and fall, and there is none to raise him up"** (v31–32) — זָדוֹן is the noun "presumption" used as a name for Babylon (no "proud one"); מֵקִים "one to raise up"; סְבִיבֹתָיו "all around him".
- **"The children of Israel and the children of Judah were oppressed together: and all that took them captives held them fast; they refused to let them go" → "Oppressed are the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah together; and all who took them captive have held them fast; they have refused to let them go"** (v33) — עֲשׁוּקִים "oppressed" (the passive of עָשַׁק "extort", which in English needs a thing taken: the person is here the one pressed); הֶחֱזִיק בְּ "hold fast"; שִׁלַּח "let go".
- **"he shall throughly plead their cause, that he may give rest to the land, and disquiet the inhabitants of Babylon" → "he will surely contend their case, so that he may give rest to the earth, and give unrest to the inhabitants of Babylon"** (v34) — גֹּאֵל "Redeemer"; רִיב יָרִיב אֶת־רִיבָם "contending he will contend their case"; הִרְגִּיעַ "give rest", הִרְגִּיז "give unrest" (a play of sound); הָאָרֶץ "the earth" (or "the land").
- **"A sword is upon the Chaldeans … and upon her princes, and upon her wise men" → "A sword against the Chaldeans … and against her princes and against her wise men"** (v35–37) — no verb: "A sword against …"; שָׂרִים "princes".
- **"A sword is upon the liars; and they shall dote: a sword is upon her mighty men; and they shall be dismayed" → "A sword against the empty talkers, and they shall become fools; a sword against her mighty ones, and they shall be dismayed"** (v36) — בַּדִּים "empty talkers" (as Isa 16:6; Jer 48:30; "diviners" of the ESV is the sense taken from Isa 44:25); נוֹאֲלוּ "become fools"; חַתּוּ "be dismayed" (as v2).
- **"and upon all the mingled people that are in the midst of her; and they shall become as women" → "and against all the mixed people who are in her midst, and they shall become women"** (v37) — סוּסָיו, רִכְבּוֹ "his horses, his chariots" (masculine, beside "her"); הָעֶרֶב "the mixed people" (foreign troops: the sense); no "as": לְנָשִׁים "women"; בֻּזָּזוּ "be plundered".
- **"A drought is upon her waters … for it is the land of graven images, and they are mad upon their idols" → "A drought against her waters … for it is a land of carved images, and over terrors they act madly"** (v38) — חֹרֶב "drought" as pointed (the same letters as חֶרֶב "sword" of v35–37: the play is the Hebrew's); פְּסִלִים "carved images"; אֵימִים "terrors" (the idols as dreadful things); הִתְהוֹלֵל "act madly".
- **"Therefore the wild beasts of the desert with the wild beasts of the islands shall dwell there, and the owls shall dwell therein: and it shall be no more inhabited for ever; neither shall it be dwelt in from generation to generation" → "Therefore desert dwellers shall dwell with hyenas, and daughters of the ostrich shall dwell in her; and she shall not be inhabited again forever, nor be dwelt in to generation and generation"** (v39) — צִיִּים "desert dwellers" (beasts or men: uncertain); אִיִּים "hyenas" (uncertain: "howlers"); בְּנוֹת יַעֲנָה "daughters of the ostrich"; לָנֶצַח "forever"; שָׁכַן "be dwelt in"; עַד־דּוֹר וָדוֹר "to generation and generation". "In Babylon" was added.
- **"As God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah and the neighbour cities thereof … so shall no man abide there" → "As at the overthrow by Elohim of Sodom and Gomorrah and her neighbors … no man shall dwell there"** (v40) — מַהְפֵּכַת אֱלֹהִים "the overthrow of Elohim" (as Amos 4:11; Isa 13:19); the rest as 49:18 (there "says Yahweh", here "declares Yahweh"; here no "so").
- **"Behold, a people shall come from the north, and a great nation, and many kings shall be raised up from the coasts of the earth" → "Behold, a people is coming from the north, and a great nation and many kings are stirred up from the remote parts of the earth"** (v41) — בָּא participle "is coming"; יֵעֹרוּ "be stirred up"; יַרְכְּתֵי־אָרֶץ "the remote parts of the earth". Jer 6:22–23 has nearly the same words of Zion.
- **"They shall hold the bow and the lance: they are cruel, and will not shew mercy: their voice shall roar like the sea, and they shall ride upon horses, every one put in array, like a man to the battle, against thee, O daughter of Babylon" → "Bow and javelin they hold fast; they are cruel and will not have compassion; their voice roars like the sea, and on horses they ride, set in array like a man for the battle, against you, daughter of Babylon"** (v42) — כִּידוֹן "javelin"; הֶחֱזִיק "hold fast"; אַכְזָרִי "cruel"; רִחַם "have compassion"; הָמָה "roar"; עָרוּךְ "set in array" (singular; no "every one").
- **"The king of Babylon hath heard the report of them, and his hands waxed feeble: anguish took hold of him, and pangs as of a woman in travail" → "The king of Babylon has heard the report of them, and his hands have grown slack; distress has seized him, anguish like a woman giving birth"** (v43) — רָפוּ "have grown slack" (as 49:24); צָרָה "distress"; הֶחֱזִיק "seize"; חִיל "anguish".
- **v44–45** — the words of 49:19–20 of Babylon, translated the same way; the qere אֲרִיצֵם "I will make them run" is read (the written ארוצם); v45 ends with נָוֶה without "their": "a habitation". See the Changes of 49:19–20 for גְּאוֹן הַיַּרְדֵּן "the pride of the Jordan", נְוֵה אֵיתָן "a lasting habitation", אַרְגִּיעָה "in a moment", יוֹעִדֵנִי "summon me", and the open subject of יִסְחָבוּם and יַשִּׁים.
- **"At the noise of the taking of Babylon the earth is moved, and the cry is heard among the nations" → "At the sound 'Babylon is seized' the earth is shaken, and an outcry is heard among the nations"** (v46) — מִקּוֹל נִתְפְּשָׂה בָבֶל, "from the sound: Babylon is seized" (a clause, not a noun); נִרְעֲשָׁה "is shaken"; זְעָקָה "outcry" (as 49:21, there צְעָקָה).

### Notes
- **v39–40** — beside Isa 13:19–22; **v40, 44–46** — 49:18–21 of Edom.
