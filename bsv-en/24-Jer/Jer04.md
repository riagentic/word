# Jeremiah 4

[1] If you turn back, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
to me you shall turn back;
and if you remove your detestable things from my face,
and do not wander,

[2] and you swear, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives,"
in truth, in judgment, and in righteousness,
then nations shall bless themselves in him,
and in him they shall boast.

[3] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧:

Break up for yourselves fallow ground,
and do not sow among thorns.

[4] Be circumcised to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and remove the foreskins of your heart,
men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and those who dwell in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
lest my fury go out like fire,
and burn, and there is none who quenches,
because of the evil of your doings.

[5] Tell in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ make it heard,
and say, Blow the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ in the land;
call aloud and say,
Gather yourselves, and let us go
into the fortified cities.

[6] Lift up a banner toward ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
bring to safety, do not stand still;
for evil I am bringing from the north,
and a great breaking.

[7] A lion has gone up from his thicket,
and a destroyer of nations has set out;
he has gone out from his place
to make your land a thing that appalls;
your cities shall be laid in ruins,
without one who dwells.

[8] For this gird on sackcloth,
lament and wail;
for the burning of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
has not turned back from us.

[9] And it shall be in that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the heart of the king shall perish, and the heart of the princes; and the priests shall be appalled, and the prophets shall be astounded.

[10] And I said, "Alas, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! Surely you have greatly deceived this people and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying, '⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧ shall be to you,' and the sword has touched to the soul."

[11] At that time it shall be said to this people and to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧:
A glaring wind of the bare heights in the wilderness,
the way of the daughter of my people,
not to winnow and not to cleanse;

[12] a wind more full than these shall come for me.
Now I also, I will speak judgments with them.

[13] Behold, like clouds he comes up,
and like the tempest his chariots;
his horses are swifter than eagles.
Woe to us, for we are laid waste!

[14] Wash your heart from evil, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
so that you may be saved.
Until when shall your thoughts of wickedness
lodge within you?

[15] For a voice is telling from Dan,
and making trouble heard from the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[16] Make mention to the nations, behold;
make it heard against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧:
Watchers are coming from the land of the distance,
and they have given their voice against the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[17] Like keepers of a field they have been against her all around,
for she has rebelled against me,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] Your way and your doings
have done these things to you.
This is your evil, for it is bitter,
for it has touched to your heart.

[19] My inward parts, my inward parts! I writhe!
The walls of my heart!
My heart roars to me; I cannot keep silent;
for the sound of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ you have heard, my soul,
the blast of war.

[20] Breaking upon breaking is called,
for the whole land is laid waste;
suddenly my tents are laid waste,
in a moment my curtains.

[21] Until when shall I see a banner,
shall I hear the sound of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧?

[22] For my people is senseless;
me they do not know;
foolish sons are they,
and not understanding are they;
wise are they to do evil,
and to do good they do not know.

[23] I saw the earth, and behold, waste and void;
and to the heavens, and their light was not.

[24] I saw the mountains, and behold, they were quaking,
and all the hills rocked to and fro.

[25] I saw, and behold, there was no man,
and all the birds of the heavens had fled.

[26] I saw, and behold, the garden land was the wilderness,
and all its cities were broken down
from the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, from the face of the burning of his anger.

[27] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
The whole land shall be a desolation;
and a full end I will not make.

[28] For this the land shall mourn,
and the heavens above shall be dark;
because I have spoken, I have devised,
and I have not been sorry, and I will not turn back from it.

[29] From the sound of the horseman and the shooter of the bow
every city is fleeing;
they have gone into the thickets,
and onto the rocks they have gone up;
every city is forsaken,
and there is not a man dwelling in them.

[30] And you, devastated one, what will you do?
Though you clothe yourself in scarlet,
though you adorn yourself with ornaments of gold,
though you tear open your eyes with paint,
in vain you make yourself beautiful;
lovers have rejected you;
they seek your soul.

[31] For a voice as of a woman in labor I have heard,
distress as of one bearing her first child,
the voice of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧; she gasps for breath,
she spreads out her palms:
"Woe to me, now! for my soul is weary before killers."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v10) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names (token `adonai-yhwh`).
- **Layout** — the chapter is poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line, except v9–10 (prose) and the openings of v3, 11 and 27. The outer quotation marks are dropped: the prophet gives Yahweh's words and his own (v19–21) without a change of speaker being marked; quotation marks stand only for words cited (v2, 10, 31).
- **"If thou wilt return, O Israel, saith the LORD, return unto me" → "If you turn back, Israel, declares Yahweh, to me you shall turn back"** (v1) — Hebrew אִם־תָּשׁוּב … אֵלַי תָּשׁוּב, the one verb twice (the שׁוּב row: "turn back", as Jer 3); the second is an imperfect; no "O". **"and if thou wilt put away thine abominations out of my sight, then shalt thou not remove" → "and if you remove your detestable things from my face, and do not wander"** — שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things" (not תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination"); מִפָּנַי "from my face"; וְלֹא תָנוּד "and do not wander" (נוּד, root of the land of Nod): either part of the condition or the promise "then you shall not wander": open.
- **"And thou shalt swear, The LORD liveth, in truth, in judgment, and in righteousness; and the nations shall bless themselves in him, and in him shall they glory" → "and you swear, As Yahweh lives, in truth, in judgment, and in righteousness, then nations shall bless themselves in him, and in him they shall boast"** (v2) — Hebrew וְנִשְׁבַּעְתָּ continues the condition; חַי־יְהוָה, literally "Yahweh lives", the oath; אֱמֶת "truth", מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", צְדָקָה "righteousness" (their rows); הִתְבָּרְכוּ, hitpael, "bless themselves" (the הִתְבָּרֵךְ row; also read as a passive); גּוֹיִם "nations" without the article; יִתְהַלָּלוּ, hitpael of הִלֵּל, "boast" (the word of praise turned on oneself).
- **"to the men of Judah and Jerusalem, Break up your fallow ground, and sow not among thorns" → "to the men of Judah and to Jerusalem: Break up for yourselves fallow ground, and do not sow among thorns"** (v3) — Hebrew לְאִישׁ יְהוּדָה "to the man of Judah" (collective) וְלִירוּשָׁלִַם "and to Jerusalem"; נִירוּ לָכֶם נִיר "break up for yourselves fallow ground" (verb and noun of one root; the נִיר row).
- **"Circumcise yourselves to the LORD, and take away the foreskins of your heart, ye men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem: lest my fury come forth like fire, and burn that none can quench it, because of the evil of your doings" → "Be circumcised to Yahweh, and remove the foreskins of your heart, men of Judah and those who dwell in Jerusalem, lest my fury go out like fire, and burn, and there is none who quenches, because of the evil of your doings"** (v4) — Hebrew הִמֹּלוּ, nifal, "be circumcised" (the מוּל row: the stem kept); עָרְלוֹת plural, לְבַבְכֶם singular; חֵמָה "fury" (its row); וְאֵין מְכַבֶּה "and there is none who quenches" (as Isa 1:31; no object); רֹעַ "evil" (noun of רָעַע); מַעַלְלִים "doings" (as Isa 1:16).
- **"Declare ye in Judah, and publish in Jerusalem; and say, Blow ye the trumpet in the land: cry, gather together, and say, Assemble yourselves" → "Tell in Judah, and in Jerusalem make it heard, and say, Blow the shofar in the land; call aloud and say, Gather yourselves"** (v5) — Hebrew הַגִּידוּ "tell" (its row); הַשְׁמִיעוּ "make heard"; תִּקְעוּ is the qere, "Blow" (the written form ותקעו has "and"); קִרְאוּ מַלְאוּ, literally "call, fill": call with a full voice; הֵאָסְפוּ "gather yourselves" (nifal); עָרֵי הַמִּבְצָר "the fortified cities" (the מִבְצָר row).
- **"Set up the standard toward Zion: retire, stay not: for I will bring evil from the north, and a great destruction" → "Lift up a banner toward Zion; bring to safety, do not stand still; for evil I am bringing from the north, and a great breaking"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׂאוּ־נֵס "lift up a banner" (the נֵס row); הָעִיזוּ "bring to safety" (its row, Jer 4:6 named); רָעָה "evil" first, as written; מֵבִיא, a participle; שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as Isa 1:28; also v20).
- **"the destroyer of the Gentiles is on his way … to make thy land desolate; and thy cities shall be laid waste, without an inhabitant" → "a destroyer of nations has set out … to make your land a thing that appalls; your cities shall be laid in ruins, without one who dwells"** (v7) — Hebrew נָסַע "set out" (the נָסַע row: "pull up" the tent pegs); שַׁמָּה (as 2:15); תִּצֶּינָה, from נָצָה "be laid in ruins"; מֵאֵין יוֹשֵׁב.
- **"gird you with sackcloth, lament and howl: for the fierce anger of the LORD is not turned back from us" → "gird on sackcloth, lament and wail; for the burning of the anger of Yahweh has not turned back from us"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׂקִּים "sackcloth" (plural; the שַׂק row); סָפַד "lament" (its row); הֵילִיל "wail"; חֲרוֹן אַף "the burning of anger" (its row); שָׁב "turned back" (שׁוּב).
- **"And it shall come to pass at that day … that the heart of the king shall perish, and the heart of the princes; and the priests shall be astonished, and the prophets shall wonder" → "And it shall be in that day … the heart of the king shall perish, and the heart of the princes; and the priests shall be appalled, and the prophets shall be astounded"** (v9) — Hebrew וְהָיָה בַיּוֹם־הַהוּא (the prophets' row); לֵב "heart" (not "courage"); שַׂר "prince"; נָשַׁמּוּ, שָׁמֵם "be appalled" (of persons, its row); תָּמַהּ "be astounded".
- **"Then said I, Ah, Lord GOD! surely thou hast greatly deceived this people … saying, Ye shall have peace; whereas the sword reacheth unto the soul" → "And I said, Alas, Lord Yahweh! Surely you have greatly deceived this people … saying, Shalom shall be to you, and the sword has touched to the soul"** (v10) — Hebrew וָאֹמַר "And I said"; אֲהָהּ "Alas" (as 1:6); אָכֵן "surely"; הַשֵּׁא הִשֵּׁאתָ, infinitive absolute with its verb, "deceiving you have deceived": "greatly" (the force of the doubling; "surely" is already אָכֵן); שָׁלוֹם token (the reviewer row); וְנָגְעָה חֶרֶב עַד־הַנָּפֶשׁ "and the sword has touched to the soul" (נָגַע "touch"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; "whereas" is not written).
- **"A dry wind of the high places in the wilderness toward the daughter of my people, not to fan, nor to cleanse" → "A glaring wind of the bare heights in the wilderness, the way of the daughter of my people, not to winnow and not to cleanse"** (v11) — Hebrew רוּחַ צַח "a glaring wind" (צַח "dazzling, clear": the white heat of the desert wind); שְׁפָיִים "bare heights" (as 3:2); דֶּרֶךְ "the way of" (toward); זָרָה "winnow" (the זָרָה row, of grain); הָבַר "cleanse" (sift out).
- **"Even a full wind from those places shall come unto me: now also will I give sentence against them" → "a wind more full than these shall come for me. Now I also, I will speak judgments with them"** (v12) — Hebrew רוּחַ מָלֵא מֵאֵלֶּה "a wind full from these" = fuller than these (a winnowing wind); יָבוֹא לִי "shall come for me" (at my bidding); גַּם־אֲנִי, the pronoun written; אֲדַבֵּר מִשְׁפָּטִים אוֹתָם "speak judgments with them" (as 1:16).
- **"his chariots shall be as a whirlwind … Woe unto us! for we are spoiled" → "and like the tempest his chariots … Woe to us, for we are laid waste!"** (v13) — Hebrew סוּפָה "tempest" (its row); מַרְכְּבוֹת "chariots"; קַלּוּ "are swift" (lit. "light"); אוֹי לָנוּ "Woe to us" (the אוֹי row); שֻׁדָּדְנוּ, pual of שָׁדַד, "we are laid waste" (its row; also v20, 30).
- **"O Jerusalem, wash thine heart from wickedness, that thou mayest be saved. How long shall thy vain thoughts lodge within thee?" → "Wash your heart from evil, Jerusalem, so that you may be saved. Until when shall your thoughts of wickedness lodge within you?"** (v14) — Hebrew כַּבְּסִי "wash" (the כִּבֵּס row); רָעָה "evil"; no "O"; לְמַעַן "so that"; עַד־מָתַי "until when"; מַחְשְׁבוֹת אוֹנֵךְ "your thoughts of wickedness" (אָוֶן, its row).
- **"For a voice declareth from Dan, and publisheth affliction from mount Ephraim" → "For a voice is telling from Dan, and making trouble heard from the hill country of Ephraim"** (v15) — Hebrew מַגִּיד · מַשְׁמִיעַ, participles (the verbs of v5); אָוֶן "trouble" (the row's other sense; v14 has "wickedness": one word, two senses); הַר אֶפְרָיִם "the hill country of Ephraim" (the הַר row of a region).
- **"Make ye mention to the nations; behold, publish against Jerusalem, that watchers come from a far country, and give out their voice against the cities of Judah" → "Make mention to the nations, behold; make it heard against Jerusalem: Watchers are coming from the land of the distance, and they have given their voice against the cities of Judah"** (v16) — Hebrew הַזְכִּירוּ "make mention" (the הִזְכִּיר row); הִנֵּה "behold"; נֹצְרִים "watchers" (participle of נָצַר "guard, watch": besiegers who keep watch on a city); מֵאֶרֶץ הַמֶּרְחָק "from the land of the distance"; וַיִּתְּנוּ "and they have given".
- **"As keepers of a field, are they against her round about; because she hath been rebellious against me" → "Like keepers of a field they have been against her all around, for she has rebelled against me"** (v17) — Hebrew הָיוּ "they have been"; שֹׁמְרֵי שָׂדַי "keepers of a field" (שָׂדַי, an old form of שָׂדֶה); מָרָה אֶת "rebel against" (the מָרָה row).
- **"Thy way and thy doings have procured these things unto thee; this is thy wickedness, because it is bitter, because it reacheth unto thine heart" → "Your way and your doings have done these things to you. This is your evil, for it is bitter, for it has touched to your heart"** (v18) — Hebrew עָשׂוֹ, infinitive absolute standing for the verb, "have done"; רָעָתֵךְ "your evil"; כִּי מָר … כִּי נָגַע "for it is bitter … for it has touched" (or "how bitter … how it has touched").
- **"My bowels, my bowels! I am pained at my very heart; my heart maketh a noise in me; I cannot hold my peace, because thou hast heard, O my soul, the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war" → "My inward parts, my inward parts! I writhe! The walls of my heart! My heart roars to me; I cannot keep silent; for the sound of the shofar you have heard, my soul, the blast of war"** (v19) — Hebrew מֵעַי "my inward parts" (the מֵעִים row); אוֹחִילָה is the qere, "I writhe" (חִיל; the written form is spelled otherwise); קִירוֹת לִבִּי "the walls of my heart", a cry by itself; הֹמֶה "roars" (the הָמָה row); לֹא אַחֲרִישׁ "I cannot keep silent" (the הֶחֱרִישׁ row); שָׁמַעַתְּ is the qere, "you have heard" (feminine: the soul is addressed; the written form reads "I have heard"); no "O"; תְּרוּעַת מִלְחָמָה "the blast of war" (the reviewer's תְּרוּעָה row: the shofar stands beside it; "the shout of war" if the war cry of voices is meant).
- **"Destruction upon destruction is cried; for the whole land is spoiled: suddenly are my tents spoiled, and my curtains in a moment" → "Breaking upon breaking is called, for the whole land is laid waste; suddenly my tents are laid waste, in a moment my curtains"** (v20) — Hebrew שֶׁבֶר עַל־שֶׁבֶר (as v6); נִקְרָא "is called" (proclaimed; read from קָרָה, "meets"); שֻׁדְּדָה · שֻׁדְּדוּ (as v13); פִּתְאֹם "suddenly" (its row); יְרִיעוֹת "curtains" (of a tent).
- **"How long shall I see the standard, and hear the sound of the trumpet?" → "Until when shall I see a banner, shall I hear the sound of the shofar?"** (v21) — Hebrew עַד־מָתַי (as v14); נֵס "banner" (as v6), without the article; אֶשְׁמְעָה, the second verb without "and".
- **"For my people is foolish, they have not known me; they are sottish children, and they have none understanding" → "For my people is senseless; me they do not know; foolish sons are they, and not understanding are they"** (v22) — Hebrew אֱוִיל "senseless" (as Isa 19:11) and סְכָלִים "foolish" (the סָכָל row), two words; אוֹתִי first; בָּנִים "sons"; נְבוֹנִים "understanding" (the נָבוֹן row). **"they are wise to do evil, but to do good they have no knowledge" → "wise are they to do evil, and to do good they do not know"** — לְהָרַע · לְהֵיטִיב "to do evil · to do good"; לֹא יָדָעוּ, the verb of the first line again.
- **"I beheld the earth, and, lo, it was without form, and void; and the heavens, and they had no light" → "I saw the earth, and behold, waste and void; and to the heavens, and their light was not"** (v23) — Hebrew רָאִיתִי "I saw" (רָאָה; also v24–26); הִנֵּה "behold"; תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ "waste and void", the words of Gen 1:2, rendered as there (the תֹּהוּ · בֹּהוּ row): the verse undoes the creation of Gen 1:2–3 (earth waste and void, no light). The `tohu-va-vohu` token is removed so that the verse reads as Gen 1:2 reads (plain words there; the token form carried an explanation inside it). וְאֶל־הַשָּׁמַיִם "and to the heavens"; וְאֵין אוֹרָם "and their light was not".
- **"I beheld the mountains, and, lo, they trembled, and all the hills moved lightly" → "I saw the mountains, and behold, they were quaking, and all the hills rocked to and fro"** (v24) — Hebrew רֹעֲשִׁים "quaking" (the רָעַשׁ row); הִתְקַלְקָלוּ, hitpalpel of קלל "be light": "rocked to and fro".
- **"there was no man, and all the birds of the heavens were fled" → "there was no man, and all the birds of the heavens had fled"** (v25) — Hebrew הָאָדָם "mankind / the man" with the article (the אָדָם row); עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם "the birds of the heavens" (its row); נָדָדוּ "had fled" (the נָדַד row).
- **"lo, the fruitful place was a wilderness, and all the cities thereof were broken down at the presence of the LORD, and by his fierce anger" → "behold, the garden land was the wilderness, and all its cities were broken down from the face of Yahweh, from the face of the burning of his anger"** (v26) — Hebrew הַכַּרְמֶל "the garden land" (as 2:7) and הַמִּדְבָּר "the wilderness", both with the article; נִתְּצוּ, nifal of נָתַץ "break down" (the reviewer row); מִפְּנֵי … מִפְּנֵי "from the face of … from the face of" (the מִפְּנֵי row; "because of" is the sense of the second); חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ (as v8).
- **"The whole land shall be desolate; yet will I not make a full end" → "The whole land shall be a desolation; and a full end I will not make"** (v27) — Hebrew שְׁמָמָה "desolation" (its row; a noun); וְכָלָה "and a full end" (וְ "and"; "yet" is the sense).
- **"For this shall the earth mourn … because I have spoken it, I have purposed it, and will not repent, neither will I turn back from it" → "For this the land shall mourn … because I have spoken, I have devised, and I have not been sorry, and I will not turn back from it"** (v28) — Hebrew אָבַל "mourn" (its row); הָאָרֶץ "the land" (as v27; "earth" in v23); קָדַר "be dark"; עַל כִּי "because"; זַמֹּתִי, זָמַם "devise" (its row: Jer 4:28 named); לֹא נִחַמְתִּי "I have not been sorry" (the נִחַם row: not "repent"), a perfect; אָשׁוּב "turn back" (שׁוּב). The objects "it" are not written in the first two verbs.
- **"The whole city shall flee for the noise of the horsemen and bowmen; they shall go into thickets, and climb up upon the rocks: every city shall be forsaken, and not a man dwell therein" → "From the sound of the horseman and the shooter of the bow every city is fleeing; they have gone into the thickets, and onto the rocks they have gone up; every city is forsaken, and there is not a man dwelling in them"** (v29) — Hebrew קוֹל "sound" (the קוֹל row); פָּרָשׁ "horseman" (its row), singular; רֹמֵה קֶשֶׁת "shooter of the bow" (רָמָה "throw, shoot"); כָּל־הָעִיר "all the city" (every city); בֹּרַחַת, a participle, "is fleeing" (the בָּרַח row); בָּאוּ · עָלוּ, perfects; עָבִים "thickets" (the same letters as "clouds"); כֵּפִים "rocks"; עֲזוּבָה "forsaken"; וְאֵין־יוֹשֵׁב בָּהֵן אִישׁ "and there is not a man dwelling in them".
- **"And when thou art spoiled, what wilt thou do? Though thou clothest thyself with crimson, though thou deckest thee with ornaments of gold, though thou rentest thy face with painting, in vain shalt thou make thyself fair; thy lovers will despise thee, they will seek thy life" → "And you, devastated one, what will you do? Though you clothe yourself in scarlet, though you adorn yourself with ornaments of gold, though you tear open your eyes with paint, in vain you make yourself beautiful; lovers have rejected you; they seek your soul"** (v30) — Hebrew וְאַתְּ is the qere, "and you" (feminine; the written form ואתי is an old spelling); שָׁדוּד "devastated" (passive of שָׁדַד; masculine in form, addressed to a woman: the form is not explained); כִּי … כִּי … כִּי "though"; שָׁנִי "scarlet"; עֲדִי "ornaments" (its row); תִּקְרְעִי בַפּוּךְ עֵינַיִךְ "tear open your eyes with paint" (קָרַע "tear"; פּוּךְ, the dark paint round the eyes); "face" is not written. עֹגְבִים "lovers" (those who lust), without "your"; מָאֲסוּ "have rejected" (מָאַס, its row); נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (its row).
- **"For I have heard a voice as of a woman in travail, and the anguish as of her that bringeth forth her first child, the voice of the daughter of Zion, that bewaileth herself, that spreadeth her hands, saying, Woe is me now! for my soul is wearied because of murderers" → "For a voice as of a woman in labor I have heard, distress as of one bearing her first child, the voice of the daughter of Zion; she gasps for breath, she spreads out her palms: Woe to me, now! for my soul is weary before killers"** (v31) — Hebrew כְּחוֹלָה "as of one in labor" (from חִיל "writhe"); צָרָה "distress" (its row); מַבְכִּירָה "one bearing her first child" (root of בְּכוֹר "firstborn"); תִּתְיַפֵּחַ "gasps for breath"; תְּפָרֵשׂ כַּפֶּיהָ "spreads out her palms" (the פָּרַשׂ כַּפַּיִם row); אוֹי־נָא לִי "Woe to me, now" (the אוֹי row; נָא kept); עָיְפָה נַפְשִׁי "my soul is weary" (the עָיֵף row); לְהֹרְגִים "before killers" (הֹרְגִים, participle of הָרַג "kill"; not "murderers": רָצַח).

### Notes
- **v23–26** — four times "I saw, and behold": the land returns to the state of Gen 1:2 — waste and void, no light, no man, no birds.
