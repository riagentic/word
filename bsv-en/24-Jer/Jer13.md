# Jeremiah 13

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to me, "Go, and buy yourself a linen loincloth, and put it on your loins, and into water you shall not bring it."

[2] And I bought the loincloth according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and put it on my loins.

[3] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me a second time, saying,

[4] "Take the loincloth that you have bought, which is on your loins, and arise, go to ⟦euphrates|Perat|the Euphrates⟧, and hide it there in a cleft of the crag."

[5] And I went and hid it at ⟦euphrates|Perat|the Euphrates⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded me.

[6] And it came to pass at the end of many days, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Arise, go to ⟦euphrates|Perat|the Euphrates⟧, and take from there the loincloth that I commanded you to hide there."

[7] And I went to ⟦euphrates|Perat|the Euphrates⟧, and dug, and took the loincloth from the place where I had hidden it. And behold, the loincloth was ruined; it was good for nothing.

[8] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[9] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Thus will I ruin the pride of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the great pride of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] This evil people, who refuse to hear my words, who walk in the stubbornness of their heart and have gone after other gods to serve them and to bow down to them — let it be like this loincloth, which is good for nothing.

[11] For as the loincloth clings to the loins of a man, so I made cling to me the whole house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the whole house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to be to me for a people and for a name and for praise and for beauty; but they did not hear.

[12] "And you shall say to them this word: 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Every jar shall be filled with wine.' And they will say to you, 'Do we not surely know that every jar shall be filled with wine?'

[13] And you shall say to them, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am filling all the inhabitants of this land, and the kings who sit for David on his throne, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with drunkenness.

[14] And I will shatter them, each man against his brother, and the fathers and the sons together, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. I will not spare, and I will not pity, and I will not have compassion, so as not to ruin them.'"

[15] Hear and give ear; do not be haughty,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[16] Give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ glory
before he brings darkness,
and before your feet stumble
on the mountains of twilight;
and you wait for light,
and he makes it the shadow of death,
sets it as dark cloud.

[17] And if you will not hear it,
in secret places my soul shall weep because of pride;
and my eye shall surely shed tears and run down with tears,
for the drove of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has been taken captive.

[18] Say to the king and to the great lady,
"Make yourselves low, sit down;
for there has come down from your heads
the crown of your beauty."

[19] The cities of the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ are shut up,
and there is none who opens;
⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ is carried into exile, all of it,
carried into exile wholly.

[20] Lift up your eyes and see
those who come from the north.
Where is the drove that was given to you,
the flock of your beauty?

[21] What will you say when he visits upon you?
And you yourself have taught them against you, chiefs as head.
Will not pangs take hold of you,
like a woman giving birth?

[22] And when you say in your heart,
"Why have these things befallen me?"
For the greatness of your iniquity
your skirts have been uncovered,
your heels have suffered violence.

[23] Can a ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ change his skin,
or a leopard his spots?
You too can do good,
who are taught to do evil.

[24] And I will scatter them like stubble passing on
to the wind of the wilderness.

[25] This is your lot,
the portion of your measures from me,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
because you have forgotten me
and trusted in falsehood.

[26] And I too have stripped your skirts up over your face,
and your disgrace shall be seen.

[27] Your adulteries and your neighings,
the depravity of your prostitution,
on the hills in the field,
I have seen your detestable things.
Woe to you, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!
You will not be clean — after how long yet?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv12, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — vv15–27 set as verse, one Hebrew line per printed line in the Hebrew order; the prose of the loincloth sign-act (vv1–14) stays prose.
- **"And"** (vv2–8) — the narrative joins its clauses with וַ "and"; kept (not "So", "Then").
- **"Go and get thee a linen girdle, and put it upon thy loins, and put it not in water" → "Go, and buy yourself a linen loincloth, and put it on your loins, and into water you shall not bring it"** (v1) — Hebrew כֹּה אָמַר "Thus says" (the prophets' formula, kept also where the narrative opens with it); הָלוֹךְ, infinitive absolute used as a command "Go"; קָנִיתָ לְּךָ "buy yourself" (קָנָה "get / buy"; לְּךָ kept as the reflexive); מָתְנַיִם "loins" (also vv2, 4, 11); וּבַמַּיִם לֹא תְבִאֵהוּ "and into water you shall not bring it" (not "dip"; whether it is not to be washed or not to be wetted is not said). **"loincloth"** — אֵזוֹר, a cloth bound about the loins (v11: it "clings" to them); the same word is "belt" in 2 Kgs 1:8 (row): here "loincloth" is kept in vv1–11, because a linen cloth that clings to the loins is meant.
- **"a second time, saying"** (v3) — Hebrew שֵׁנִית לֵאמֹר; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept (also v8, the prophets' formula).
- **"go to Euphrates" → "go to Perat / the Euphrates"** (vv4–7) — Hebrew פְּרָתָה / בִּפְרָת, as printed: the name of the Euphrates (token), without "river" (Jer 51:63 the same). Many take it here as פָּרָה Parah near Anathoth (Josh 18:23), or the spring of Wadi Farah, because the Euphrates is some 500 km away and the journey is made twice; the Hebrew has only the name, so the verse keeps it. **"in a hole of the rock" → "in a cleft of the crag"** (v4) — Hebrew נְקִיק הַסָּלַע; סֶלַע "crag" (row). **"by Euphrates" → "at Perat"** (v5) — בִּ "in, at".
- **"after many days" → "it came to pass at the end of many days"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיְהִי מִקֵּץ יָמִים רַבִּים (וַיְהִי kept).
- **"the girdle was marred, it was profitable for nothing" → "the loincloth was ruined; it was good for nothing"** (v7) — Hebrew נִשְׁחַת "was ruined" (nifal of שָׁחַת; the same root as "I will ruin" in v9, so the sign and its meaning have one verb); לֹא יִצְלַח לַכֹּל, literally "it does not prosper for anything" (צָלַח "prosper": "good for nothing" gives its sense; also v10). **"I digged"** — וָאֶחְפֹּר "and dug".
- **"After this manner will I mar the pride of Judah" → "Thus will I ruin the pride of Judah"** (v9) — Hebrew כָּכָה "thus"; אַשְׁחִית "I will ruin" (hifil of שָׁחַת, as v7); גָּאוֹן "pride" (row, of men); הָרָב "the great (pride)".
- **"which walk in the imagination of their heart" → "who walk in the stubbornness of their heart"** (v10) — Hebrew שְׁרִרוּת לֵב "stubbornness of the heart" (row). **"to worship them" → "to bow down to them"** — הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" (row). **"shall even be as this girdle" → "let it be like this loincloth"** — וִיהִי is the short (jussive) form: "let it be"; it may also be read as a result, "so that it becomes". The sentence opens with the people as its head and is not finished before the dash: kept.
- **"so have I caused to cleave unto me" → "so I made cling to me"** (v11) — Hebrew יִדְבַּק … הִדְבַּקְתִּי, one verb: "clings … made cling" (דָּבַק "cling", row). **"that they might be unto me for a people, and for a name, and for a praise, and for a glory" → "to be to me for a people and for a name and for praise and for beauty"** — לִהְיוֹת לִי לְעָם וּלְשֵׁם וְלִתְהִלָּה וּלְתִפְאָרֶת; תְּהִלָּה "praise", תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (row: Deut 26:19; Jer 13:11). **"but they would not hear" → "but they did not hear"** — וְלֹא שָׁמֵעוּ, a plain perfect (שָׁמַע "hear", row).
- **"bottle" → "jar"** (v12) — Hebrew נֵבֶל, a vessel for wine: a jar or a skin (uncertain; row "jar"). **"Do we not certainly know" → "Do we not surely know"** — הֲיָדֹעַ לֹא נֵדַע, literally "knowing do we not know" (infinitive absolute). The setumah mark in the middle of the verse in the printed text is a scribal paragraph sign, not a word.
- **"I will fill all the inhabitants … with drunkenness" → "I am filling all the inhabitants … with drunkenness"** (v13) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מְמַלֵּא, a participle after "behold" (what is about to happen: "I am filling"); the Hebrew names all the groups first and puts שִׁכָּרוֹן "drunkenness" last: the order is kept. **"the kings that sit upon David's throne" → "the kings who sit for David on his throne"** — הַיֹּשְׁבִים לְדָוִד עַל־כִּסְאוֹ, literally "who sit for David on his throne" (in David's line, on David's throne). Each group has its own "and", as printed.
- **"I will dash them one against another, even the fathers and the sons together" → "I will shatter them, each man against his brother, and the fathers and the sons together"** (v14) — Hebrew נִפַּצְתִּים "I will shatter them" (נָפַץ, row); אִישׁ אֶל־אָחִיו "each man against his brother" (row). **"I will not pity, nor spare, nor have mercy, but destroy them" → "I will not spare, and I will not pity, and I will not have compassion, so as not to ruin them"** — three verbs: חָמַל "spare" (the "have pity (spare)" row), חוּס "pity" (row), רִחַם "have compassion" (row); מֵהַשְׁחִיתָם, literally "from ruining them" (nothing will hold him back from it; שָׁחַת as vv7, 9).
- **"be not proud" → "do not be haughty"** (v15) — Hebrew אַל־תִּגְבָּהוּ, of גָּבַהּ "be high". **"give ear"** — הֶאֱזִין (row).
- **"Give glory to the LORD your God" → "Give to Yahweh your Elohim glory"** (v16) — the Hebrew order (כָּבוֹד last). **"before he cause darkness"** — יַחְשִׁךְ, hifil "make dark", with no subject named: "he brings darkness" by the person of the verb. **"upon the dark mountains" → "on the mountains of twilight"** — הָרֵי נָשֶׁף; נֶשֶׁף "twilight" (row). **"and, while ye look for light, he turn it into the shadow of death, and make it gross darkness" → "and you wait for light, and he makes it the shadow of death, sets it as dark cloud"** — וְקִוִּיתֶם "and you wait for" (קִוָּה, row; "while" is not written); וְשָׂמָהּ לְצַלְמָוֶת "and he makes it the shadow of death" (צַלְמָוֶת as pointed, row; the reading צַלְמוּת "deep darkness" is also defended); qere וְשִׁית "and sets" (the written ketiv יָשִׁית "he will set": the same sense); עֲרָפֶל "dark cloud" (row).
- **"But if ye will not hear it" → "And if you will not hear it"** (v17) — Hebrew וְאִם; תִשְׁמָעוּהָ "hear it" (feminine object: the word just spoken). **"my soul shall weep in secret places for your pride" → "in secret places my soul shall weep because of pride"** — the Hebrew order; מִפְּנֵי גֵוָה "because of pride" ("your" is not written; whose pride is not said). **"mine eye shall weep sore, and run down with tears" → "my eye shall surely shed tears and run down with tears"** — וְדָמֹעַ תִּדְמַע, literally "and shedding tears it shall shed tears" (infinitive absolute); דָּמַע "shed tears" kept apart from בָּכָה "weep"; וְתֵרַד עֵינִי דִּמְעָה "and my eye shall run down with tears" (literally "go down tears"); עֵינִי "my eye", singular. **"the LORD's flock" → "the drove of Yahweh"** — עֵדֶר "drove" (row; also v20, beside צֹאן "flock"); נִשְׁבָּה "has been taken captive" (row).
- **"the queen" → "the great lady"** (v18) — Hebrew גְּבִירָה, a rank at court (row; the word says neither "queen" nor "mother": the king's mother is commonly meant, cf. Jer 22:26; 29:2). **"Humble yourselves, sit down" → "Make yourselves low, sit down"** — הַשְׁפִּילוּ שֵׁבוּ, two imperatives (הִשְׁפִּיל "make low", row). **"for your principalities shall come down, even the crown of your glory" → "for there has come down from your heads the crown of your beauty"** — יָרַד, a perfect; מַרְאֲשׁוֹתֵיכֶם as pointed: "from your head-places" (the place at the head, as the מְרַאֲשׁוֹת row), here "from your heads"; KJV "principalities" reads another word; עֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאַרְתְּכֶם "the crown of your beauty" (תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty", as v11).
- **"and none shall open them" → "and there is none who opens"** (v19) — Hebrew וְאֵין פֹּתֵחַ, a participle, no object. **"Judah shall be carried away captive all of it, it shall be wholly carried away captive" → "Judah is carried into exile, all of it, carried into exile wholly"** — הָגְלָת, a perfect (passive) of גָּלָה "go into exile", twice; שְׁלוֹמִים "wholly" (literally "completenesses", of the root of *shalom*: no token, the noun שָׁלוֹם is not written).
- **"Lift up your eyes, and behold them that come from the north" → "Lift up your eyes and see those who come from the north"** (v20) — qere שְׂאוּ וּרְאוּ, plural imperatives; the ketiv שְׂאִי וּרְאִי is feminine singular, as the rest of the verse ("given to you", "your beauty" are feminine singular: Jerusalem addressed as a woman). **"thy beautiful flock" → "the flock of your beauty"** — צֹאן תִּפְאַרְתֵּךְ (תִּפְאֶרֶת as vv11, 18).
- **"What wilt thou say when he shall punish thee? for thou hast taught them to be captains, and as chief over thee" → "What will you say when he visits upon you? And you yourself have taught them against you, chiefs as head"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּי־יִפְקֹד עָלַיִךְ "when he visits upon you" (פָּקַד עַל, as the visitation rows; no subject named); וְאַתְּ לִמַּדְתְּ אֹתָם עָלַיִךְ אַלֻּפִים לְרֹאשׁ, word for word: "and you, you taught them, against (or "over") you, chiefs (or "friends") for a head". The line is difficult: אַלּוּף is "chief" (row) and also "close friend" (Jer 3:4); whether she taught them to be her friends, who will now be set over her, or taught them to rule, is not settled: kept word for word. **"sorrows" → "pangs"** — חֲבָלִים (the pains of birth); אֵשֶׁת לֵדָה "a woman of giving birth".
- **"Wherefore come these things upon me?" → "Why have these things befallen me?"** (v22) — Hebrew קְרָאֻנִי, of קָרָא in the sense of קָרָה "befall" (row). **"For the greatness of thine iniquity are thy skirts discovered, and thy heels made bare" → "For the greatness of your iniquity your skirts have been uncovered, your heels have suffered violence"** — נִגְלוּ "have been uncovered" (גָּלָה, as the גִּלָּה "uncover" row); נֶחְמְסוּ עֲקֵבָיִךְ "your heels have suffered violence" (of חָמָס "violence"; "heels" may be a modest word for the body: in Changes only). The current "and you suffer violence" had dropped "your heels".
- **"Can the Ethiopian change his skin" → "Can a Cushite change his skin"** (v23) — Hebrew כּוּשִׁי, a man of Kush (token, traditional form "Cushite"); no article ("a Cushite … a leopard"); הָפַךְ "turn, change"; חֲבַרְבֻּרֹת "spots" (stripes or spots: only here). **"then may ye also do good, that are accustomed to do evil" → "You too can do good, who are taught to do evil"** — Hebrew גַּם־אַתֶּם תּוּכְלוּ לְהֵיטִיב: "then" is not written (the answer to the question is left to the hearer); לִמֻּדֵי הָרֵעַ "taught (ones) of doing evil" (לִמֵּד "teach", row; "accustomed" gives the sense).
- **"Therefore will I scatter them as the stubble that passeth away by the wind of the wilderness" → "And I will scatter them like stubble passing on to the wind of the wilderness"** (v24) — Hebrew וַאֲפִיצֵם "and I will scatter them" (3rd person plural; the verse had "you"); קַשׁ "stubble" (row; not "chaff"); עוֹבֵר "passing on" (עָבַר); לְרוּחַ מִדְבָּר "to the wind of the wilderness" (מִדְבָּר "wilderness", row).
- **"This is thy lot, the portion of thy measures from me" → "This is your lot, the portion of your measures from me"** (v25) — Hebrew מְנָת־מִדַּיִךְ מֵאִתִּי, word for word (מַד "measure"; not "the portion I have measured out to you"). **"trusted in falsehood"** — שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (row; not "lies"); גּוֹרָל "lot" (row).
- **"Therefore will I discover thy skirts upon thy face" → "And I too have stripped your skirts up over your face"** (v26) — Hebrew וְגַם־אֲנִי חָשַׂפְתִּי, a perfect, kept as a perfect (the judgement spoken as done; "will" in other versions); חָשַׂף "strip bare" (as Isa 20:4 "bared"); "up" is English for עַל־פָּנָיִךְ "over your face". **"that thy shame may appear" → "and your disgrace shall be seen"** — וְנִרְאָה "and shall be seen"; קָלוֹן "disgrace" (row).
- **"I have seen thine adulteries, and thy neighings, the lewdness of thy whoredom, and thine abominations on the hills in the fields" → "Your adulteries and your neighings, the depravity of your prostitution, on the hills in the field, I have seen your detestable things"** (v27) — the Hebrew order (the verb comes in the fourth line); זִמָּה "depravity" (row), זְנוּת "prostitution" (row), שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things" (as 2 Kgs 23:13, 24); בַּשָּׂדֶה "in the field", singular. **"Woe unto thee, O Jerusalem!"** — אוֹי "Woe" (row); no "O" (reviewer). **"wilt thou not be made clean? when shall it once be?" → "You will not be clean — after how long yet?"** — Hebrew לֹא תִטְהֲרִי אַחֲרֵי מָתַי עֹד, literally "you will not be clean; after when yet?" (טָהֵר qal "be clean"; the first words may also be a question).

### Notes
- **The loincloth** — the sign-act (vv1–11) is told as Jeremiah's own report; the meaning is given in vv9–11.
