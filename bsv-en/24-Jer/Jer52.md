# Jeremiah 52

[1] ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and eleven years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦hamutal|Chamutal|Hamutal⟧ the daughter of ⟦jeremiah-libnah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, from ⟦libnah|Livnah|Libnah⟧.

[2] And he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to all that ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ had done.

[3] For on account of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ it came to be in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, until he had thrown them from before his face. And ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ rebelled against the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[4] And it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth of the month, that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came, he and all his force, against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they camped against it, and they built against it a siege wall all around.

[5] And the city came into the siege, until the eleventh year of King ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧.

[6] In the fourth month, on the ninth of the month, the famine was strong in the city, and there was no bread for the people of the land.

[7] And the city was split open, and all the men of war fled and went out from the city by night, by the way of the gate between the two walls, which was by the garden of the king — and the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ were against the city all around — and they went by the way of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧.

[8] And the force of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ pursued after the king and overtook ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ in the plains of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, and all his force was scattered from him.

[9] And they seized the king and brought him up to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ in the land of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧, and he spoke judgments with him.

[10] And the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ slaughtered the sons of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ before his eyes, and also all the princes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ he slaughtered at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧.

[11] And the eyes of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ he blinded, and he bound him in bronze fetters, and the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ brought him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and put him in the house of the visitations until the day of his death.

[12] And in the fifth month, on the tenth of the month — it was the nineteenth year of King ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ — ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards, who stood before the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, came into ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] And he burned the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the house of the king; and all the houses of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and every great house, he burned with fire.

[14] And all the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ all around, all the force of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ that was with the chief of the guards broke down.

[15] And of the poor of the people, and the rest of the people who were left in the city, and the deserters who had fallen away to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and the rest of the craftsmen, ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards carried into exile.

[16] And of the poor of the land ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards left some, as vinedressers and as field workers.

[17] And the pillars of bronze that belonged to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the bases and the sea of bronze that were in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ broke in pieces, and they carried all their bronze to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[18] And the pots and the shovels and the snuffers and the bowls and the pans, and all the utensils of bronze with which they ministered, they took.

[19] And the basins and the firepans and the bowls and the pots and the lampstands and the pans and the libation bowls, what was of gold as gold, and what was of silver as silver, the chief of the guards took.

[20] The pillars, two; the sea, one; and the twelve of the herd, of bronze, that were under the bases, which King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had made for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: there was no weight for their bronze, all these utensils.

[21] And the pillars: eighteen cubits was the height of the one pillar, and a thread of twelve cubits went around it, and its thickness was four fingers; it was hollow.

[22] And a capital of bronze was upon it, and the height of the one capital was five cubits, and a lattice and pomegranates were upon the capital all around, all of it bronze; and like these the second pillar had, and pomegranates.

[23] And the pomegranates were ninety-six, toward the wind; all the pomegranates were a hundred upon the lattice all around.

[24] And the chief of the guards took ⟦seraiah|Serayah|Seraiah⟧ the head priest and ⟦zephaniah|Tzefanyah|Zephaniah⟧ the second priest and the three keepers of the threshold.

[25] And from the city he took one eunuch who was overseer over the men of war, and seven men of those who saw the face of the king, who were found in the city, and the scribe of the commander of the host, who mustered the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the midst of the city.

[26] And ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards took them and led them to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧.

[27] And the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ struck them down and put them to death at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ in the land of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧. And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went into exile from upon its ground.

[28] This is the people whom ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ carried into exile: in the seventh year, ⟦judeans|Yehudim|Judeans⟧ three thousand and twenty-three;

[29] in the eighteenth year of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, eight hundred and thirty-two souls;

[30] in the twenty-third year of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards carried into exile of the ⟦judeans|Yehudim|Judeans⟧ seven hundred and forty-five souls; all the souls were four thousand and six hundred.

[31] And it came to pass in the thirty-seventh year of the exile of ⟦jehoiachin|Yehoyachin|Jehoiachin⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-fifth of the month, that Evil-merodach king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, in the year of his kingship, lifted up the head of ⟦jehoiachin|Yehoyachin|Jehoiachin⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and brought him out from the house of confinement.

[32] And he spoke good things with him and set his throne above the throne of the kings who were with him in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[33] And he changed his garments of confinement, and he ate bread continually before him all the days of his life.

[34] And his allowance, a continual allowance, was given to him from the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, the matter of a day in its day, until the day of his death, all the days of his life.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **Beside 2 Kgs 24:18–25:30** — the wording of the finished 2 Kings chapters is used where the Hebrew is the same; where Jeremiah's Hebrew differs, it is translated as printed here, and each difference is named below. Jeremiah has no counterpart of 2 Kgs 25:22–26 (Gedaliah; told in Jer 40–41), and v28–30 are not in Kings.
- **"was one and twenty years old when he began to reign … and his mother's name was Hamutal the daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah" → "was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and eleven years he reigned … And the name of his mother was Hamutal the daughter of Jeremiah, from Libnah"** (v1) — as 2 Kgs 24:18 (בֶּן־… שָׁנָה "a son of twenty-one years"); the qere חֲמוּטַל is read (the written text has חמיטל).
- **"And he did that which was evil in the eyes of the LORD" → "And he did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v2) — הָרַע with the article, as 2 Kgs 24:19.
- **"For through the anger of the LORD it came to pass in Jerusalem and Judah, till he had cast them out from his presence" → "For on account of the anger of Yahweh it came to be in Jerusalem and in Judah, until he had thrown them from before his face"** (v3) — as 2 Kgs 24:20: עַל־אַף "on account of the anger"; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; מֵעַל פָּנָיו "from before his face"; מָרַד בְּ "rebel against".
- **"Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon came, he and all his army, against Jerusalem, and pitched against it, and built forts against it round about" → "… came, he and all his force, against Jerusalem, and they camped against it, and they built against it a siege wall all around"** (v4) — as 2 Kgs 25:1 (חַיִל "force", דָּיֵק "siege wall", uncertain), except וַיַּחֲנוּ plural "they camped" (Kings: וַיִּחַן "he camped"); Jeremiah spells the name נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר (with r; also v12, 28–30).
- **"So the city was besieged unto the eleventh year" → "And the city came into the siege, until the eleventh year"** (v5) — as 2 Kgs 25:2.
- **"And in the fourth month, in the ninth day of the month, the famine was sore in the city, so that there was no bread" → "In the fourth month, on the ninth of the month, the famine was strong in the city, and there was no bread"** (v6) — Jeremiah has בַּחֹדֶשׁ הָרְבִיעִי "in the fourth month" (Kings has no month: 2 Kgs 25:3); וַיֶּחֱזַק "was strong", its וַ after the time phrase not rendered; לֶחֶם "bread".
- **"Then the city was broken up, and all the men of war fled, and went forth out of the city by night … and they went by the way of the plain" → "And the city was split open, and all the men of war fled and went out from the city by night … and they went by the way of the Arabah"** (v7) — as 2 Kgs 25:4, but Jeremiah has the verbs that Kings lacks: יִבְרְחוּ וַיֵּצְאוּ מֵהָעִיר "fled and went out from the city", and the plural וַיֵּלְכוּ "they went" (Kings: "he went"). "Though" is not in the Hebrew: the clause about the Chaldeans is a parenthesis.
- **"But the army of the Chaldeans pursued after the king, and overtook Zedekiah" → "And the force of the Chaldeans pursued after the king and overtook Zedekiah"** (v8) — as 2 Kgs 25:5, with the name (Kings: "him"); עַרְבוֹת יְרֵחוֹ "the plains of Jericho".
- **"where he gave judgment upon him" → "and he spoke judgments with him"** (v9) — Jeremiah has "in the land of Hamath", the singular וַיְדַבֵּר "he spoke" and the plural מִשְׁפָּטִים "judgments" (2 Kgs 25:6: "they spoke judgment"); תָּפַשׂ "seize".
- **"Then the king of Babylon slew the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes: he slew also all the princes of Judah in Riblah" → "And the king of Babylon slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah before his eyes, and also all the princes of Judah he slaughtered at Riblah"** (v10) — שָׁחַט "slaughter"; שָׂרִים "princes"; the second half is not in Kings.
- **"Then he put out the eyes of Zedekiah; and the king of Babylon bound him in chains, and carried him to Babylon, and put him in prison till the day of his death" → "And the eyes of Zedekiah he blinded, and he bound him in bronze fetters, and the king of Babylon brought him to Babylon and put him in the house of the visitations until the day of his death"** (v11) — עִוֵּר "blind"; נְחֻשְׁתַּיִם "bronze fetters" (a dual), as 2 Kgs 25:7; the qere בֵּית is read; בֵּית הַפְּקֻדֹּת, only here, "the house of the visitations" (פְּקֻדָּה "visitation": a house of punishment, a prison; or "of the musterings", a guardhouse: uncertain); the end of the verse is not in Kings.
- **"in the tenth day of the month … came Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, which served the king of Babylon, into Jerusalem" → "on the tenth of the month … Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards, who stood before the king of Babylon, came into Jerusalem"** (v12) — Jeremiah has "the tenth" (2 Kgs 25:8: "the seventh"); רַב־טַבָּחִים "the chief of the guards" (lit. "of the slaughterers"; also v14–16, 19, 24, 26, 30); עָמַד לִפְנֵי "who stood before" (= served; Kings has עֶבֶד "a servant of").
- **"and all the houses of Jerusalem, and all the houses of the great men, burned he with fire" → "and all the houses of Jerusalem, and every great house, he burned with fire"** (v13) — as 2 Kgs 25:9; Jeremiah writes בֵּית הַגָּדוֹל with the article ("the great house", or "the house of the great one": uncertain; KJV "of the great men").
- **"And all the army of the Chaldeans, that were with the captain of the guard, brake down all the walls of Jerusalem round about" → "And all the walls of Jerusalem all around, all the force of the Chaldeans that was with the chief of the guards broke down"** (v14) — as 2 Kgs 25:10, with כָּל־ "all" the walls; the Hebrew order (the object first) kept; נָתַץ "break down".
- **"Then Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carried away captive certain of the poor of the people, and the residue of the people that remained in the city, and those that fell away, that fell to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the multitude" → "And of the poor of the people, and the rest of the people who were left in the city, and the deserters who had fallen away to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the craftsmen, Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards carried into exile"** (v15) — as 2 Kgs 25:11, but Jeremiah begins with וּמִדַּלּוֹת הָעָם "and of the poor of the people" (not in Kings; it stands against v16, where "the poor of the land" are left), and has הָאָמוֹן "the craftsmen" (אָמוֹן "master workman", Prov 8:30; collective) where Kings has הֶהָמוֹן "the multitude": "craftsmen" as printed, "multitude" (a form of הָמוֹן) is also defended; הֶגְלָה "carry into exile"; הַנֹּפְלִים אֲשֶׁר נָפְלוּ "the deserters who had fallen away".
- **"But Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard left certain of the poor of the land for vinedressers and for husbandmen" → "And of the poor of the land Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards left some, as vinedressers and as field workers"** (v16) — as 2 Kgs 25:12, with the name (Kings lacks it); "some" is supplied (מִן "of"); יֹגְבִים "field workers" (uncertain).
- **"Also the pillars of brass that were in the house of the LORD, and the bases, and the brasen sea … and carried all the brass of them to Babylon" → "And the pillars of bronze that belonged to the house of Yahweh, and the bases and the sea of bronze that were in the house of Yahweh … and they carried all their bronze to Babylon"** (v17) — as 2 Kgs 25:13, but Jeremiah has אֲשֶׁר לְבֵית "that belonged to the house" (Kings: בֵית "in the house") and כָּל־נְחֻשְׁתָּם "all their bronze"; מְכֹנוֹת "bases"; שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces".
- **"The caldrons also, and the shovels, and the snuffers, and the bowls, and the spoons, and all the vessels of brass wherewith they ministered, took they away" → "And the pots and the shovels and the snuffers and the bowls and the pans, and all the utensils of bronze with which they ministered, they took"** (v18) — as 2 Kgs 25:14, with הַמִּזְרָקֹת "the bowls" (not in Kings here); סִירוֹת "pots", יָעִים "shovels", מְזַמְּרוֹת "snuffers", כַּפּוֹת "pans"; שֵׁרֵת "minister".
- **"And the basons, and the firepans, and the bowls, and the caldrons, and the candlesticks, and the spoons, and the cups; that which was of gold in gold, and that which was of silver in silver, took the captain of the guard away" → "And the basins and the firepans and the bowls and the pots and the lampstands and the pans and the libation bowls, what was of gold as gold, and what was of silver as silver, the chief of the guards took"** (v19) — 2 Kgs 25:15 names only the firepans and the bowls; Jeremiah's list as printed: סִפִּים "basins" (as 1 Kgs 7:50), מַחְתּוֹת "firepans", מִזְרָקוֹת "bowls", סִירוֹת "pots", מְנֹרוֹת "lampstands", כַּפּוֹת "pans", מְנַקִּיּוֹת "libation bowls" (the bowls of Exod 25:29 for the drink offering; uncertain; "libation" keeps it apart from מִזְרָק "bowl" in the same verse); אֲשֶׁר זָהָב זָהָב "what was of gold as gold".
- **"The two pillars, one sea, and twelve brasen bulls that were under the bases, which king Solomon had made in the house of the LORD: the brass of all these vessels was without weight" → "The pillars, two; the sea, one; and the twelve of the herd, of bronze, that were under the bases, which King Solomon had made for the house of Yahweh: there was no weight for their bronze, all these utensils"** (v20) — Jeremiah names וְהַבָּקָר שְׁנֵים־עָשָׂר נְחֹשֶׁת "and the twelve of the herd, of bronze" (the cattle under the sea, 1 Kgs 7:25; 2 Kgs 16:17), not in 2 Kgs 25:16; אֲשֶׁר־תַּחַת הַמְּכֹנוֹת "that were under the bases" as printed (1 Kgs 7 sets them under the sea: the MT is kept); "King" Solomon; לֹא־הָיָה מִשְׁקָל "there was no weight".
- **"And concerning the pillars, the height of one pillar was eighteen cubits; and a fillet of twelve cubits did compass it; and the thickness thereof was four fingers: it was hollow" → "And the pillars: eighteen cubits was the height of the one pillar, and a thread of twelve cubits went around it, and its thickness was four fingers; it was hollow"** (v21) — the qere קוֹמַת "the height of" is read; חוּט "thread" (as 1 Kgs 7:15); סָבַב "go around"; עֳבִי "thickness"; נָבוּב "hollow"; the circumference, thickness and hollowness are not in 2 Kgs 25:17.
- **"And a chapiter of brass was upon it; and the height of one chapiter was five cubits, with network and pomegranates upon the chapiters round about … The second pillar also and the pomegranates were like unto these" → "And a capital of bronze was upon it, and the height of the one capital was five cubits, and a lattice and pomegranates were upon the capital all around, all of it bronze; and like these the second pillar had, and pomegranates"** (v22) — "five cubits", as printed (2 Kgs 25:17 "three cubits"; 1 Kgs 7:16 "five"); כֹּתֶרֶת "capital", שְׂבָכָה "lattice"; וְרִמּוֹנִים "and pomegranates" at the end (Kings: "upon the lattice").
- **"And there were ninety and six pomegranates on a side; and all the pomegranates upon the network were an hundred round about" → "And the pomegranates were ninety-six, toward the wind; all the pomegranates were a hundred upon the lattice all around"** (v23) — not in Kings; רוּחָה "toward the wind" (= on the sides, facing out: uncertain); numbers in words.
- **"Seraiah the chief priest, and Zephaniah the second priest, and the three keepers of the door" → "Seraiah the head priest and Zephaniah the second priest and the three keepers of the threshold"** (v24) — as 2 Kgs 25:18: כֹּהֵן הָרֹאשׁ "the head priest", כֹּהֵן הַמִּשְׁנֶה "the second priest", סַף "threshold".
- **"He took also out of the city an eunuch, which had the charge of the men of war; and seven men of them that were near the king's person … and the principal scribe of the host … and threescore men of the people of the land, that were found in the midst of the city" → "And from the city he took one eunuch who was overseer over the men of war, and seven men of those who saw the face of the king … and the scribe of the commander of the host … and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the midst of the city"** (v25) — as 2 Kgs 25:19, but Jeremiah has "seven men" (Kings: "five") and בְּתוֹךְ הָעִיר "in the midst of the city"; סָרִיס "eunuch"; פָּקִיד "overseer"; רֹאֵי פְנֵי־הַמֶּלֶךְ "those who saw the face of the king"; סֹפֵר שַׂר הַצָּבָא "the scribe of the commander of the host" (uncertain: "the scribe, the commander of the host"); הַמַּצְבִּא "who mustered".
- **"So Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard took them, and brought them" → "And Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards took them and led them"** (v26) — as 2 Kgs 25:20.
- **"And the king of Babylon smote them, and put them to death in Riblah in the land of Hamath. Thus Judah was carried away captive out of his own land" → "And the king of Babylon struck them down and put them to death at Riblah in the land of Hamath. And Judah went into exile from upon its ground"** (v27) — as 2 Kgs 25:21: וַיִּגֶל qal "went into exile"; אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"This is the people whom Nebuchadrezzar carried away captive: in the seventh year three thousand Jews and three and twenty: In the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar he carried away captive from Jerusalem eight hundred thirty and two persons" → "This is the people whom Nebuchadrezzar carried into exile: in the seventh year, Judeans three thousand and twenty-three; in the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar, from Jerusalem, eight hundred and thirty-two souls"** (v28–30) — not in Kings; הֶגְלָה "carry into exile"; v29 has no verb ("he carried away" was supplied); נֶפֶשׁ "soul(s)", counted as persons (as Num 31:35); numbers in words; כָּל־נֶפֶשׁ "all the souls".
- **"in the five and twentieth day of the month, that Evilmerodach king of Babylon in the first year of his reign lifted up the head of Jehoiachin king of Judah, and brought him forth out of prison" → "on the twenty-fifth of the month, that Evil-merodach king of Babylon, in the year of his kingship, lifted up the head of Jehoiachin king of Judah and brought him out from the house of confinement"** (v31) — Jeremiah has "the twenty-fifth" (2 Kgs 25:27: "the twenty-seventh"), בִּשְׁנַת מַלְכֻתוֹ "in the year of his kingship" (Kings: בִּשְׁנַת מָלְכוֹ "in the year that he began to reign"), and וַיֹּצֵא אוֹתוֹ "and brought him out" (not in Kings); נָשָׂא אֶת־רֹאשׁ "lift up the head of"; the qere הַכְּלוּא is read (written הכליא); בֵּית הַכְּלוּא "the house of confinement".
- **"And spake kindly unto him, and set his throne above the throne of the kings that were with him in Babylon" → "And he spoke good things with him and set his throne above the throne of the kings who were with him in Babylon"** (v32) — as 2 Kgs 25:28: דִּבֶּר אִתּוֹ טֹבוֹת "spoke good things with him"; the qere הַמְּלָכִים "the kings" is read (written מלכים).
- **"And changed his prison garments: and he did continually eat bread before him all the days of his life" → "And he changed his garments of confinement, and he ate bread continually before him all the days of his life"** (v33) — as 2 Kgs 25:29; בִּגְדֵי כִלְאוֹ "his garments of confinement"; תָּמִיד "continually".
- **"And for his diet, there was a continual diet given him of the king of Babylon, every day a portion until the day of his death, all the days of his life" → "And his allowance, a continual allowance, was given to him from the king of Babylon, the matter of a day in its day, until the day of his death, all the days of his life"** (v34) — as 2 Kgs 25:30, but Jeremiah has "the king of Babylon" and עַד־יוֹם מוֹתוֹ "until the day of his death"; אֲרֻחָה "allowance"; דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ "the matter of a day in its day" (each day's due).

### Notes
- **v1–27, 31–34** — beside 2 Kgs 24:18–25:21, 27–30 (finished); the same wording where the Hebrew is the same, the differences in Changes.
- **v28–30** — the three deportations by year of Nebuchadrezzar's reign; they stand only here.
