# Jeremiah 36

[3] Perhaps the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ will hear all the evil that I am devising to do to them, so that they may turn back, each man from his evil way, and I will pardon their iniquity and their sin.

[5] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ commanded Baruch, saying, I am shut up; I am not able to go into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] Perhaps their plea for favor will fall before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they will turn back, each man from his evil way; for great is the anger and the fury that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken against this people.

[8] And Baruch son of ⟦neriah|Neriyah|Neriah⟧ did according to all that ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet commanded him, reading in the book the words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] And Baruch read in the book the words of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in the chamber of ⟦gemariah|Gemaryahu|Gemariah⟧ son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe, in the upper court, at the entrance of the new gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in the ears of all the people.

[11] And ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ son of ⟦gemariah|Gemaryahu|Gemariah⟧, son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧, heard all the words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from upon the book,

[13] And ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ told them all the words that he had heard, when Baruch read in the book in the ears of the people.

[14] And all the princes sent to Baruch ⟦jehudi|Yehudi|Jehudi⟧ son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧, son of ⟦shelemiah|Shelemyah|Shelemiah⟧, son of ⟦cushi|Kushi|Cushi⟧, saying, The scroll in which you read in the ears of the people, take it in your hand and go. [16] And it came to pass, when they heard all the words, they turned in dread, each man to his neighbor; and they said to Baruch, We will surely tell the king all these words.

[17] And they asked Baruch, saying, Tell us, please, how did you write all these words from his mouth?

[18] And Baruch said to them, From his mouth he would call out to me all these words, and I was writing on the book with ink.

[19] And the princes said to Baruch, Go, hide yourself, you and ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, and let no man know where you are.

[20] And they went in to the king, to the court, and the scroll they deposited in the chamber of Elishama the scribe; and they told all the words in the ears of the king.

[21] And the king sent ⟦jehudi|Yehudi|Jehudi⟧ to take the scroll, and he took it from the chamber of Elishama the scribe; and ⟦jehudi|Yehudi|Jehudi⟧ read it in the ears of the king and in the ears of all the princes who were standing over the king.

[27] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, after the king had burned the scroll and the words that Baruch had written from the mouth of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, saying,

[28] Again take yourself another scroll, and write on it all the former words that were on the first scroll, which ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ burned.

[31] And I will visit upon him and upon his seed and upon his servants their iniquity; and I will bring upon them and upon the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and to the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ all the evil that I have spoken against them; and they did not listen.

[32] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of ⟦neriah|Neriyah|Neriah⟧, the scribe; and he wrote on it from the mouth of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ all the words of the book that ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ had burned in the fire; and there were added to them yet many words like them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved; v8 has the second יְהוָה with another vowel sign (יְהֹוָה), the same name. **Quotation marks** — the Hebrew has none; the speeches stand without them.
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose.
- **"And it came to pass in the fourth year … that this word came unto Jeremiah from the LORD, saying"** (v1) — וַיְהִי "And it came to pass" (its row; also v9, 16, 23); לֵאמֹר "saying" (also v5, 14, 17, 27, 29).
- **"Take thee a roll of a book, and write therein all the words that I have spoken unto thee against Israel" → "Take yourself a scroll of a book, and write on it all the words that I have spoken to you concerning Israel … until this day"** (v2) — קַח־לְךָ, the imperative with לְךָ (the reviewer's row: "get yourself"; here "take yourself", also v28); מְגִלַּת־סֵפֶר "a scroll of a book" (מְגִלָּה "scroll", the rolled sheet; סֵפֶר "book", the written document, its row; also v4). In this chapter מְגִלָּה is always "scroll" (v2, 4, 6, 14, 20, 21, 23, 25, 27–29, 32) and סֵפֶר alone always "book" (v8, 10, 11, 13, 18, 32): the one roll is meant by both. כָּתַב אֶל, literally "write to"; עַל "concerning" (or "against": the preposition allows both, three times); הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה "this day".
- **"It may be that the house of Judah will hear all the evil which I purpose to do unto them; that they may return every man from his evil way; that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin" → "Perhaps the house of Judah will hear all the evil that I am devising to do to them, so that they may turn back, each man from his evil way, and I will pardon their iniquity and their sin"** (v3) — אוּלַי "perhaps" (its row; also v7); חֹשֵׁב participle of חָשַׁב "devise" (of the mind); רָעָה "evil" (also v7, 31); לְמַעַן "so that"; שׁוּב "turn back" (its row; also v7); וְסָלַחְתִּי "and I will pardon" (weqatal; the earlier סָלַח row).
- **"Baruch wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the LORD … upon a roll of a book"** (v4) — kept: מִפִּי "from the mouth of" (also v6, 17, 18, 27, 32; "at the dictation of" is the sense, not the word); the Hebrew order puts "on a scroll of a book" last.
- **"I am shut up; I cannot go into the house of the LORD" → "I am shut up; I am not able to go into the house of Yahweh"** (v5) — עָצוּר "shut up" (as Jer 33:1; why he is shut up the verse does not say: "banned" is an interpretation); לֹא אוּכַל "I am not able".
- **"Therefore go thou, and read in the roll, which thou hast written from my mouth … upon the fasting day" → "And you, you shall go in, and you shall read in the scroll that you have written from my mouth the words of Yahweh, in the ears of the people in the house of Yahweh on a day of fasting"** (v6) — וּבָאתָ אַתָּה, וְ "and" with אַתָּה stressed; קָרָא בְּ "read in" (also v8, 10, 13); בְּאָזְנֵי "in the ears of" (its row; also v10, 13–15, 20, 21); בֵּית יְהוָה without a preposition ("in" supplied, also v8, 10); בְּיוֹם צוֹם "on a day of fasting"; כָּל־יְהוּדָה "all Judah", "the men" not written.
- **"It may be they will present their supplication before the LORD … for great is the anger and the fury that the LORD hath pronounced against this people" → "Perhaps their plea for favor will fall before Yahweh … for great is the anger and the fury that Yahweh has spoken against this people"** (v7) — תִּפֹּל תְּחִנָּתָם "their plea for favor will fall" (תְּחִנָּה, the row of Solomon's prayer); אַף "anger", חֵמָה "fury"; דִּבֶּר אֶל "spoken to / against".
- **"And Baruch … did according to all that Jeremiah the prophet commanded him, reading in the book"** (v8) — kept; לִקְרֹא בַסֵּפֶר "reading in the book".
- **"they proclaimed a fast before the LORD to all the people in Jerusalem" → "they proclaimed a fast before Yahweh, all the people in Jerusalem and all the people who came …"** (v9) — כָּל־הָעָם is the subject of קָרְאוּ ("they proclaimed"), standing after the verb; "to" is not written: the people proclaimed the fast; who called them to it the verse does not say.
- **"in the chamber of Gemariah the son of Shaphan the scribe, in the higher court, at the entry of the new gate" → "in the chamber of Gemariah son of Shaphan the scribe, in the upper court, at the entrance of the new gate"** (v10) — הַסֹּפֵר "the scribe" may belong to Gemariah or to Shaphan (2 Kgs 22:3: Shaphan was the scribe): the order kept; פֶּתַח "entrance" (its row); שַׁעַר … הֶחָדָשׁ "the new gate" (as Jer 26:10).
- **"heard out of the book all the words of the LORD" → "heard all the words of Yahweh from upon the book"** (v11) — מֵעַל הַסֵּפֶר, literally "from upon the book".
- **"Then he went down into the king's house, into the scribe's chamber: and, lo, all the princes sat there" → "and he went down to the house of the king, to the chamber of the scribe; and behold, all the princes were sitting there"** (v12) — עַל־לִשְׁכַּת, literally "upon the chamber"; וְהִנֵּה "and behold"; שָׂרִים "princes" (the שַׂר row; also v14, 19, 21); יוֹשְׁבִים participle; "and" before each name, as in the Hebrew.
- **"when Baruch read the book in the ears of the people" → "when Baruch read in the book in the ears of the people"** (v13) — בִּקְרֹא בָרוּךְ בַּסֵּפֶר; הִגִּיד "tell" (its row).
- **"Take in thine hand the roll wherein thou hast read in the ears of the people, and come" → "The scroll in which you read in the ears of the people, take it in your hand and go"** (v14) — הַמְּגִלָּה first, as in the Hebrew; וָלֵךְ "and go" (הָלַךְ; "come" is the sense of the summons, not the word).
- **"Sit down now, and read it in our ears" → "Sit down, please, and read it in our ears"** (v15) — שֵׁב נָא, נָא "please".
- **"they were afraid both one and other, and said unto Baruch, We will surely tell the king of all these words" → "they turned in dread, each man to his neighbor; and they said to Baruch, We will surely tell the king all these words"** (v16) — פָּחֲדוּ אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ, literally "they were in dread, each man to his neighbor" ("turned" supplied for אֶל; the אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ row); פָּחַד "be in dread" (as Jer 33:9; also v24); הַגֵּיד נַגִּיד, literally "telling we will tell" (the "surely" form).
- **"Tell us now, How didst thou write all these words at his mouth?" → "Tell us, please, how did you write all these words from his mouth?"** (v17) — one question; "Was it at his dictation?" of the earlier text is not in the Hebrew; הַגֶּד־נָא "tell, please".
- **"He pronounced all these words unto me with his mouth, and I wrote them with ink in the book" → "From his mouth he would call out to me all these words, and I was writing on the book with ink"** (v18) — מִפִּיו first; יִקְרָא, imperfect of קָרָא "call / read out" (the same verb as "read" in v6–15), of repeated action; אֲנִי כֹּתֵב participle "I was writing"; דְּיוֹ "ink" (only here).
- **"Go, hide thee, thou and Jeremiah; and let no man know where ye be"** (v19) — kept: לֵךְ הִסָּתֵר; אִישׁ אַל־יֵדַע "let no man know".
- **"And they went in to the king into the court, but they laid up the roll in the chamber of Elishama the scribe, and told all the words in the ears of the king" → "And they went in to the king, to the court, and the scroll they deposited in the chamber of Elishama the scribe; and they told all the words in the ears of the king"** (v20) — חָצֵרָה "to the court"; הִפְקִידוּ "they deposited" (hifil of פָּקַד, of a thing left in safe keeping; against the "appoint over" row, which is of persons); the object first.
- **"and in the ears of all the princes which stood beside the king" → "who were standing over the king"** (v21) — הָעֹמְדִים מֵעַל הַמֶּלֶךְ, literally "standing from upon the king": standing by him as he sat, attending (as Gen 18:2).
- **"Now the king sat in the winterhouse in the ninth month: and there was a fire on the hearth burning before him" → "And the king was sitting in the winter house, in the ninth month, and the brazier was burning before him"** (v22) — יוֹשֵׁב participle; אָח "brazier" (a fire pot; also v23); וְאֶת־הָאָח … מְבֹעָרֶת, literally "and the brazier before him kindled" (pual of בָּעַר "kindle / burn"); אֶת before a subject is unusual: kept as subject.
- **"when Jehudi had read three or four leaves, he cut it with the penknife, and cast it into the fire that was on the hearth, until all the roll was consumed" → "when Jehudi had read three columns or four, he would tear it with the razor of the scribe and throw it into the fire that was on the brazier, until all the scroll came to an end on the fire"** (v23) — שָׁלֹשׁ דְּלָתוֹת וְאַרְבָּעָה, literally "three doors and four" (columns of writing); יִקְרָעֶהָ, imperfect of repeated action, "he would tear it": the subject is not named (the king, by the sense); קָרַע "tear", the verb of v24 "tear their garments" (they tear the scroll, not their clothes); תַּעַר הַסֹּפֵר "the razor of the scribe" (the תַּעַר "razor" row; a small knife for trimming the reed pen and the sheet); וְהַשְׁלֵךְ infinitive absolute "and throw"; עַד־תֹּם "until … came to an end" (the תַּם row); אֶל־הָאָח … עַל־הָאָח "on the brazier" both times.
- **"Yet they were not afraid, nor rent their garments, neither the king, nor any of his servants that heard all these words" → "And they were not in dread, and they did not tear their garments, the king and all his servants who heard all these words"** (v24) — the Hebrew order: the subjects at the end; עֲבָדָיו "his servants" (the עֶבֶד row: a king's men).
- **"Nevertheless Elnathan and Delaiah and Gemariah had made intercession to the king that he would not burn the roll: but he would not hear them" → "And also Elnathan and Delaiah and Gemariah interceded with the king not to burn the scroll, and he did not listen to them"** (v25) — וְגַם "and also"; הִפְגִּיעַ בְּ "intercede with" (the Isa 52–55 row: without an object "intercede"); שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to".
- **"Jerahmeel the son of Hammelech" → "Jerahmeel the son of the king"** (v26) — בֶּן־הַמֶּלֶךְ "the son of the king" (a royal son, or a title of an officer: not a name, as the Hebrew article shows); לָקַחַת "to take" (לָקַח; "seize" is the sense); וַיַּסְתִּרֵם יְהוָה "and Yahweh hid them", with "and".
- **"Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah, after that the king had burned the roll, and the words which Baruch wrote at the mouth of Jeremiah" → "And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah, after the king had burned the scroll and the words that Baruch had written from the mouth of Jeremiah, saying"** (v27) — וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה; וְאֶת־הַדְּבָרִים "and the words" (not "with").
- **"Take thee again another roll" → "Again take yourself another scroll"** (v28) — שׁוּב קַח־לְךָ, שׁוּב with a second verb = "again" (the שׁוּב row); עַל "on" the first scroll.
- **"Thou hast burned this roll, saying, Why hast thou written therein, saying, The king of Babylon shall certainly come and destroy this land, and shall cause to cease from thence man and beast?" → "You, you have burned this scroll, saying, Why have you written on it, saying, The king of Babylon shall surely come and ruin this land and make cease from it man and beast?"** (v29) — אַתָּה stressed; לֵאמֹר twice, kept; בֹּא־יָבוֹא, literally "coming he shall come" (the "surely" form); הִשְׁחִית "ruin" (the שָׁחַת row); הִשְׁבִּית "make cease" (its row); אָדָם וּבְהֵמָה "man and beast".
- **"He shall have none to sit upon the throne of David: and his dead body shall be cast out in the day to the heat, and in the night to the frost" → "He shall not have one sitting on the throne of David; and his dead body shall be thrown out to the heat by day and to the frost by night"** (v30) — לֹא־יִהְיֶה־לּוֹ יוֹשֵׁב, literally "there shall not be to him one sitting"; נְבֵלָה "dead body" (the row for a dead man); מֻשְׁלֶכֶת "thrown out" (hofal of הִשְׁלִיךְ, as v23 "throw"); חֹרֶב "heat" (as Gen 31:40; the Judg 6 row has "dryness" for the fleece: another sense of the one word); קֶרַח "frost".
- **"And I will punish him and his seed and his servants for their iniquity … and upon the men of Judah, all the evil that I have pronounced against them; but they hearkened not" → "And I will visit upon him and upon his seed and upon his servants their iniquity; and I will bring upon them … and to the men of Judah all the evil that I have spoken against them; and they did not listen"** (v31) — פָּקַד עַל … עָוֹן "visit upon … iniquity" (the פָּקַד row; "punish" is the sense); זֶרַע "seed"; אִישׁ יְהוּדָה, a collective singular, "the men of Judah" (as Jer 35:13), with אֶל "to" where the others have עַל "upon"; וְלֹא שָׁמֵעוּ "and they did not listen" (שָׁמַע without an object, as Jer 35:17).
- **"Then took Jeremiah another roll … who wrote therein from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the book which Jehoiakim king of Judah had burned in the fire: and there were added besides unto them many like words" → "And Jeremiah took another scroll and gave it to Baruch son of Neriah, the scribe; and he wrote on it from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the book … and there were added to them yet many words like them"** (v32) — וַיִּתְּנָהּ "and gave it"; הַסֹּפֵר "the scribe" stands after Neriah, as v26 has "Baruch the scribe"; וְעוֹד נוֹסַף עֲלֵיהֶם דְּבָרִים רַבִּים כָּהֵמָּה, literally "and yet there was added to them many words like them" (נוֹסַף, nifal of יָסַף "add").

### Notes

- **The scroll** — מְגִלַּת־סֵפֶר "a scroll of a book" (v2, 4; Ps 40:7; Ezek 2:9): a written sheet rolled up; the chapter calls it "the scroll" and "the book" by turns. Baruch son of Neriah is a scribe (v26, 32); a scribe Elishama, a chamber of the scribe and Gemariah son of Shaphan stand in the royal house (v10–12, 20, 21).
