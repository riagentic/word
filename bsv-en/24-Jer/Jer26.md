# Jeremiah 26

[1] In the beginning of the reign of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, this word came from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying:

[2] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Stand in the court of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and speak to all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who come to bow down in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ all the words that I have commanded you to speak to them; do not diminish a word.

[3] Perhaps they will listen and turn back, each man from his evil way, and I will relent concerning the evil that I am devising to do to them because of the evil of their doings.

[4] And you shall say to them, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: If you will not listen to me, to walk in my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ that I have set before you,

[5] to listen to the words of my servants the prophets, whom I am sending to you, rising early and sending, and you have not listened,

[6] then I will make this house like Shiloh, and this city I will make a curse to all the nations of the earth.'"

[7] And the priests and the prophets and all the people heard ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ speaking these words in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] And it came to pass, when ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ had finished speaking all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded to speak to all the people, that the priests and the prophets and all the people seized him, saying, "You shall surely die!

[9] Why have you prophesied in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, 'Like Shiloh this house shall be, and this city shall be laid waste, without inhabitant'?" And all the people were assembled to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] And the princes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ heard these words, and they came up from the house of the king to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they sat at the entrance of the New Gate of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] And the priests and the prophets said to the princes and to all the people, saying, "This man has a judgment of death, for he has prophesied to this city, as you have heard with your ears."

[12] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said to all the princes and to all the people, saying, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ sent me to prophesy to this house and to this city all the words that you have heard.

[13] And now, make good your ways and your doings, and listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will relent concerning the evil that he has spoken against you.

[14] And I, behold, I am in your hand; do to me as is good and as is right in your eyes.

[15] Only, you shall surely know that if you put me to death, innocent blood you are putting upon yourselves and on this city and on its inhabitants; for in truth ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has sent me to you to speak in your ears all these words."

[16] And the princes and all the people said to the priests and to the prophets, "This man has no judgment of death, for in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ he has spoken to us."

[17] And men of the elders of the land arose and said to all the assembly of the people, saying,

[18] "⟦micah-prophet|Michah|Micah⟧ of ⟦moresheth|Moreshet|Moresheth⟧ was prophesying in the days of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he said to all the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ shall be plowed as a field,
and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall become heaps,
and the mountain of the house high places of a forest.'

[19] Did ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ surely put him to death? Did he not fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧? And he besought the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ relented concerning the evil that he had spoken against them. And we are doing great evil against our souls."

[20] And also a man was prophesying in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ son of ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ from ⟦kiriath-jearim|Qiryat Yearim|Kiriath-jearim⟧, and he prophesied against this city and against this land according to all the words of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧.

[21] And King ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ and all his mighty men and all the princes heard his words, and the king sought to put him to death. And ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ heard, and he was afraid and fled and came to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[22] And King ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ sent men to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧: ⟦elnathan|Elnatan|Elnathan⟧ son of Achbor, and men with him, to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[23] And they brought ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and brought him to King ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧, and he struck him down with the sword and threw his dead body into the graves of the sons of the people.

[24] But the hand of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧ son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ was with ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, so as not to give him into the hand of the people to put him to death.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v18) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v13, 16).
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose; the words of Micah in v18 are set as three lines, as the Hebrew lines (cola) of Mic 3:12. The speech of the elders (v18–19) is one quotation.
- **"In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim … came this word from the LORD, saying" → "… this word came from Yahweh, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּרֵאשִׁית מַמְלְכוּת "in the beginning of the reign (kingdom)"; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept.
- **"speak unto all the cities of Judah, which come to worship in the LORD's house" → "speak to all the cities of Judah who come to bow down in the house of Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew עַל־כָּל־עָרֵי, literally "upon / concerning all the cities" (the men of the cities are meant: הַבָּאִים "who come"); הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down". **"all the words that I command thee" → "all the words that I have commanded you"** — צִוִּיתִיךָ perfect. **"diminish not a word" → "do not diminish a word"** — גָּרַע "diminish" (take away).
- **"If so be they will hearken, and turn every man from his evil way, that I may repent me of the evil, which I purpose to do unto them" → "Perhaps they will listen and turn back, each man from his evil way, and I will relent concerning the evil that I am devising to do to them"** (v3) — Hebrew אוּלַי "perhaps"; שׁוּב "turn back"; וְנִחַמְתִּי אֶל־הָרָעָה "and I will relent concerning the evil" (נִחַם: not "repent"; רָעָה "evil", the harm planned, not "disaster"); חֹשֵׁב participle of חָשַׁב "devise". **"because of the evil of their doings"** — מִפְּנֵי רֹעַ מַעַלְלֵיהֶם; מַעֲלָלִים "doings" (also v13).
- **"And thou shalt say unto them … If ye will not hearken to me, to walk in my law, which I have set before you" → "And you shall say to them … If you will not listen to me, to walk in my Torah that I have set before you"** (v4) — Hebrew תּוֹרָתִי (token `torah`); נָתַתִּי לִפְנֵיכֶם "I have set before you" (the verb is "give").
- **"To hearken to the words of my servants the prophets, whom I sent unto you, both rising up early, and sending them, but ye have not hearkened" → "to listen to the words of my servants the prophets, whom I am sending to you, rising early and sending, and you have not listened"** (v5) — Hebrew שֹׁלֵחַ participle "I am sending"; וְהַשְׁכֵּם וְשָׁלֹחַ, the row of 2 Chr 36:15 (no object); "and" kept. The sentence of v4–5 is a condition whose answer follows in v6.
- **"Then will I make this house like Shiloh, and will make this city a curse to all the nations of the earth" → "then I will make this house like Shiloh, and this city I will make a curse to all the nations of the earth"** (v6) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי … כְּשִׁלֹה, literally "I will give this house as Shiloh" (Shiloh's sanctuary destroyed: 7:12–14; Ps 78:60); the object "this city" placed first in the Hebrew; qere הַזֹּאת (ketiv הזאתה, a longer spelling); קְלָלָה "curse".
- **"So the priests and the prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah" → "And the priests and the prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah"** (v7) — וַיִּשְׁמְעוּ.
- **"Now it came to pass, when Jeremiah had made an end of speaking … that the priests … took him, saying, Thou shalt surely die" → "And it came to pass, when Jeremiah had finished speaking … that the priests … seized him, saying, You shall surely die!"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיְהִי; כְּכַלּוֹת "when … had finished"; צִוָּה יְהוָה לְדַבֵּר "Yahweh had commanded to speak" ("him" is not written); תָּפַשׂ "seize"; מוֹת תָּמוּת, literally "dying you shall die".
- **"This house shall be like Shiloh, and this city shall be desolate without an inhabitant" → "Like Shiloh this house shall be, and this city shall be laid waste, without inhabitant"** (v9) — the Hebrew order; תֶּחֱרַב "be laid waste" (of water "dry up", one root with חָרְבָּה "ruin", 25:9); מֵאֵין יוֹשֵׁב "without inhabitant". **"And all the people were gathered against Jeremiah" → "And all the people were assembled to Jeremiah"** — וַיִּקָּהֵל … אֶל, "to" (against him or around him: the Hebrew does not decide).
- **"When the princes of Judah heard these things" → "And the princes of Judah heard these words"** (v10) — וַיִּשְׁמְעוּ שָׂרֵי יְהוּדָה, a chain of clauses with "and"; שָׂרִים "princes" (not "officials"; also v11, 12, 16, 21); הַדְּבָרִים "words". **"and sat down in the entry of the new gate of the LORD's house" → "and they sat at the entrance of the New Gate of Yahweh"** — Hebrew בְּפֶתַח שַׁעַר־יְהוָה הֶחָדָשׁ, literally "in the entrance of the gate of Yahweh, the new one"; "house" is not in the MT (some Hebrew manuscripts and versions add בֵּית, from memory; as 36:10). יָשַׁב "sit" (of the body), פֶּתַח "entrance".
- **"This man is worthy to die" → "This man has a judgment of death"** (v11) — Hebrew מִשְׁפַּט־מָוֶת לָאִישׁ הַזֶּה, literally "a judgment of death is for this man" (as Deut 19:6; also v16: "This man has no judgment of death"). **"for he hath prophesied against this city" → "for he has prophesied to this city"** — נִבָּא אֶל "to" (also v12); what he prophesied was against it. **"as ye have heard with your ears"** — בְּאָזְנֵיכֶם.
- **"The LORD sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city" → "Yahweh sent me to prophesy to this house and to this city"** (v12) — אֶל twice, "to"; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept.
- **"Therefore now amend your ways and your doings, and obey the voice of the LORD your God" → "And now, make good your ways and your doings, and listen to the voice of Yahweh your Elohim"** (v13) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה "and now"; הֵיטִיב "make good"; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of" (not "obey"). **"and the LORD will repent him of the evil that he hath pronounced against you" → "and Yahweh will relent concerning the evil that he has spoken against you"** — וְיִנָּחֵם (or "that Yahweh may relent"); דִּבֶּר "spoken". Also v19.
- **"As for me, behold, I am in your hand" → "And I, behold, I am in your hand"** (v14) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי הִנְנִי בְיֶדְכֶם; יָד singular. **"do with me as seemeth good and meet unto you" → "do to me as is good and as is right in your eyes"** — כַּטּוֹב וְכַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינֵיכֶם, "as" twice; יָשָׁר "right" (of a thing) and "in your eyes" kept.
- **"But know ye for certain, that if ye put me to death, ye shall surely bring innocent blood upon yourselves" → "Only, you shall surely know that if you put me to death, innocent blood you are putting upon yourselves"** (v15) — Hebrew אַךְ "only"; יָדֹעַ תֵּדְעוּ, literally "knowing you shall know" (an imperfect: "you must know" is its force); מְמִתִים and נֹתְנִים participles; דָּם נָקִי "innocent blood", placed first. **"and upon this city, and upon the inhabitants thereof" → "and on this city and on its inhabitants"** — וְאֶל … וְאֶל. **"for of a truth the LORD hath sent me unto you" → "for in truth Yahweh has sent me to you"** — בֶּאֱמֶת "in truth"; עֲלֵיכֶם, literally "upon you"; the Hebrew order "to speak in your ears all these words".
- **"This man is not worthy to die: for he hath spoken to us in the name of the LORD our God" → "This man has no judgment of death, for in the name of Yahweh our Elohim he has spoken to us"** (v16) — אֵין־לָאִישׁ הַזֶּה מִשְׁפַּט־מָוֶת (as v11); the Hebrew order.
- **"Then rose up certain of the elders of the land, and spake to all the assembly of the people" → "And men of the elders of the land arose and said to all the assembly of the people, saying"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲנָשִׁים מִזִּקְנֵי הָאָרֶץ "men of the elders" ("certain" is not written); קְהַל הָעָם "the assembly of the people"; וַיֹּאמְרוּ … לֵאמֹר "said … saying".
- **"Micah the Morasthite prophesied" → "Micah of Moresheth was prophesying"** (v18) — qere מִיכָה "Micah" (ketiv מיכיה "Micaiah", a longer form of the same name); הַמּוֹרַשְׁתִּי "the Moreshethite" (= of Moresheth, Mic 1:1, 14); הָיָה נִבָּא "was prophesying". **"and spake to all the people of Judah, saying" → "and he said to all the people of Judah, saying"** — וַיֹּאמֶר … לֵאמֹר.
- **"Zion shall be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of a forest" → "Zion shall be plowed as a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house high places of a forest"** (v18) — the words of Mic 3:12, which has the same Hebrew (Micah adds לָכֵן בִּגְלַלְכֶם "Therefore because of you" before them and spells עִיִּין for עִיִּים; Micah's chapter is to be worded the same). שָׂדֶה תֵחָרֵשׁ "shall be plowed a field": "as" supplied for the accusative; עִיִּים "heaps" (of ruins); לְבָמוֹת יָעַר "for high places of a forest" (בָּמוֹת "high places"; the verb "shall become" carries over); הַר הַבַּיִת "the mountain of the house" (the temple hill).
- **"Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah put him at all to death?" → "Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah surely put him to death?"** (v19) — Hebrew הֶהָמֵת הֱמִתֻהוּ, a question with the infinitive absolute ("putting to death did they put him to death?"). **"did he not fear the LORD, and besought the LORD, and the LORD repented him of the evil" → "Did he not fear Yahweh? And he besought the face of Yahweh, and Yahweh relented concerning the evil"** — הֲלֹא יָרֵא asks only "did he not fear"; then two narrative verbs follow, וַיְחַל … וַיִּנָּחֶם, as statements; חִלָּה אֶת־פְּנֵי "beseech the face of" (lit. "make the face soft"). **"Thus might we procure great evil against our souls" → "And we are doing great evil against our souls"** — וַאֲנַחְנוּ עֹשִׂים, participle; נַפְשׁוֹתֵינוּ "our souls".
- **"And there was also a man that prophesied in the name of the LORD" → "And also a man was prophesying in the name of Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew וְגַם־אִישׁ הָיָה מִתְנַבֵּא; the hitpael מִתְנַבֵּא beside the nifal וַיִּנָּבֵא, one verb "prophesy". **"according to all the words of Jeremiah"** — כְּכֹל דִּבְרֵי, "all" kept.
- **"And when Jehoiakim the king, with all his mighty men, and all the princes, heard his words" → "And King Jehoiakim and all his mighty men and all the princes heard his words"** (v21) — וַיִּשְׁמַע, "and"; גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty men". **"he was afraid, and fled, and went into Egypt" → "and he was afraid and fled and came to Egypt"** — בָּרַח "flee"; וַיָּבֹא מִצְרָיִם "and came to Egypt".
- **"And Jehoiakim the king sent men into Egypt, namely, Elnathan the son of Achbor, and certain men with him into Egypt" → "And King Jehoiakim sent men to Egypt: Elnathan son of Achbor, and men with him, to Egypt"** (v22) — "to Egypt" twice, as the Hebrew; "certain" is not written.
- **"And they fetched forth Urijah out of Egypt" → "And they brought Uriah out from Egypt"** (v23) — וַיּוֹצִיאוּ. **"who slew him with the sword, and cast his dead body into the graves of the common people" → "and he struck him down with the sword and threw his dead body into the graves of the sons of the people"** — וַיַּכֵּהוּ "struck (down)"; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; נִבְלָתוֹ "his dead body" (נְבֵלָה, of an animal "carcass": the word is harsh); קִבְרֵי בְּנֵי הָעָם "the graves of the sons of the people" (= of the common people).
- **"that they should not give him into the hand of the people to put him to death" → "so as not to give him into the hand of the people to put him to death"** (v24) — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי תֵּת־אֹתוֹ בְיַד־הָעָם, no subject named; אַךְ "But".

### Notes
- **v18** — the words of Mic 3:12, quoted by the elders with the prophet's name and the king's days (Mic 1:1).
- **Ahikam** (v24) — son of Shaphan (2 Kgs 22:12); father of Gedaliah (39:14; 40:5); Shaphan's family stands by Jeremiah again in 36:10–12, 25.
