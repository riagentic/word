[11] Do not fear the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, whom you fear; do not fear him, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for I am with you, to save you and to deliver you from his hand.

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v15, 18) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2–6, 9, 13, 15, 18–22) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved throughout.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; the remnant's inquiry and Jeremiah's word against going to Egypt.
- **"came near … from the least even unto the greatest" → "drew near … from small to great"** (v1, 8) — Hebrew נָגַשׁ "draw near"; מִקָּטֹן וְעַד־גָּדוֹל, as 2 Kgs 25:26. **"Jezaniah"** — the Hebrew has the short form יְזַנְיָה; the name token is kept.
- **"Let, we beseech thee, our supplication be accepted before thee" → "Please let our plea for favor fall before you"** (v2, 9) — Hebrew תִּפָּל־נָא תְחִנָּתֵנוּ לְפָנֶיךָ, literally "let our plea for favor fall before you" (נָא "please"); in v9 the hifil לְהַפִּיל, literally "to make … fall": "to lay … before him" (both as the Jer 37–40 row, 37:20; 38:26). תְּחִנָּה "plea for favor" (noun of חָנַן "show favor"). **"for we are left but a few of many"** — נִשְׁאַרְנוּ מְעַט מֵהַרְבֵּה "we are left a few out of many"; no "but".
- **"That the LORD thy God may shew us the way wherein we may walk" → "and may Yahweh your Elohim tell us the way in which we should walk"** (v3) — Hebrew וְיַגֶּד, a wish with "and" (הִגִּיד "tell", also v4, 20, 21).
- **"I have heard you; … whatsoever thing the LORD shall answer you, I will declare it unto you; I will keep nothing back" → "I have heard. … every word that Yahweh answers you I will tell you; I will not hold back a word from you"** (v4) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי, no object; הִנְנִי מִתְפַּלֵּל "behold, I (am) praying": a participle of what is about to be done; כְּדִבְרֵיכֶם "according to your words"; וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; כָּל־הַדָּבָר "every word"; מָנַע "hold back".
- **"The LORD be a true and faithful witness between us, if we do not even according to all things" → "May Yahweh be a true and faithful witness against us, if we do not do according to all the word"** (v5) — Hebrew בָּנוּ "against us" (literally "in us"); אֱמֶת וְנֶאֱמָן, literally "of truth and faithful"; אִם־לֹא … כֵּן נַעֲשֶׂה, literally "if not according to all the word … so we will do": with the curse named in the first half, the "if" is kept (the oath formula).
- **"Whether it be good, or whether it be evil, we will obey … that it may be well with us, when we obey" → "Whether good or whether evil, we will listen to … so that it may go well with us, because we listen"** (v6, 13, 21) — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of" (no word "obey"); אֲנַחְנוּ, the qere (ketiv אנו, the short form of "we": the meaning is the same); לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר יִיטַב־לָנוּ "so that it may go well with us"; כִּי "because".
- **"after ten days" → "And it came to pass at the end of ten days that"** (v7) — וַיְהִי מִקֵּץ … וַיְהִי.
- **"Then called he Johanan … and all the captains … and all the people" → "And he called to Johanan … and to all the commanders … and to all the people"** (v8) — וַיִּקְרָא אֶל … וְאֶל … וּלְ, "to" each time.
- **"If ye will still abide in this land, then will I build you, and not pull you down … for I repent me of the evil" → "If you will surely dwell in this land, then I will build you and not throw you down … for I relent concerning the evil"** (v10) — Hebrew אִם־שׁוֹב תֵּשְׁבוּ: שׁוֹב is taken as the infinitive absolute of יָשַׁב "dwell" with its first letter dropped ("surely dwell"; literally "dwelling you dwell"); read as שׁוּב it would be "if you will return and dwell". בָּנָה "build", הָרַס "throw down", נָטַע "plant", נָתַשׁ "pluck up" (as Jer 1:10). נִחַמְתִּי אֶל־הָרָעָה "I relent concerning the evil" (נִחַם, as Jer 18:8; the evil here is already done, so the sense may be "I am sorry for the evil": both in the one verb).
- **"Be not afraid of the king of Babylon, of whom ye are afraid" → "Do not fear the king of Babylon, whom you fear"** (v11) — Hebrew יָרֵא מִפְּנֵי, literally "fear from the face of"; נְאֻם־יְהוָה "declares Yahweh"; יָשַׁע "save", הִצִּיל "deliver".
- **"I will shew mercies unto you, that he may have mercy upon you, and cause you to return to your own land" → "I will give you compassion, and he will have compassion on you and bring you back to your ground"** (v12) — Hebrew נָתַן רַחֲמִים "give compassion", רִחַם "have compassion", one root; וְהֵשִׁיב "and he will bring back" (some read וְהוֹשִׁיב "make you dwell", from memory; the MT is kept); אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"But if ye say … neither obey the voice" → "But if you are saying … so as not to listen to the voice"** (v13) — Hebrew אֹמְרִים, a participle; לְבִלְתִּי שְׁמֹעַ "so as not to listen".
- **"No; but we will go into the land of Egypt, where we shall see no war, nor hear the sound of the trumpet, nor have hunger of bread" → "No, but the land of Egypt we will enter, where we shall not see war, and the sound of the shofar we shall not hear, and for bread we shall not be hungry"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹא כִּי "No, but"; the Hebrew puts "the land of Egypt", "the sound of the shofar" and "for bread" first; בּוֹא "enter" (also v15, 17, 18, 19, 22). רָעֵב "be hungry".
- **"And now therefore hear the word of the LORD, ye remnant of Judah … If ye wholly set your faces to enter into Egypt, and go to sojourn there" → "and now, therefore, hear the word of Yahweh, remnant of Judah. … If you surely set your faces to enter Egypt, and you enter to sojourn there"** (v15) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה לָכֵן "and now, therefore" opens the answer to the "if" of v13 (no "then" is written); no vocative "O"; שׂוֹם תְּשִׂמוּן, the infinitive absolute ("surely set"; literally "setting you set"); וּבָאתֶם לָגוּר "and you enter to sojourn" (גּוּר "sojourn", also v17, 22).
- **"Then it shall come to pass, that the sword, which ye feared … shall follow close after you there in Egypt" → "then it shall be that the sword which you fear, there it shall overtake you … and the famine about which you are anxious, there it shall cling after you, in Egypt"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהָיְתָה; the participles "you fear", "you are anxious" (דָּאַג "be anxious"); each half has שָׁם "there" before its verb; דָּבַק אַחֲרֵי "cling after" (the דָּבַק "cling" row).
- **"So shall it be with all the men … and none of them shall remain or escape from the evil" → "And it shall be that all the men … shall die … and there shall be no survivor or fugitive for them from the face of the evil"** (v17) — Hebrew וְיִהְיוּ כָל־הָאֲנָשִׁים … יָמוּתוּ, literally "and all the men … shall be: they shall die"; בַּחֶרֶב בָּרָעָב וּבַדָּבֶר with the article, no "and" before the second (also v22); שָׂרִיד "survivor", פָּלִיט "fugitive"; מִפְּנֵי, of fleeing; מֵבִיא "am bringing", a participle.
- **"As mine anger and my fury hath been poured forth … and ye shall be an execration, and an astonishment, and a curse, and a reproach; and ye shall see this place no more" → "As my anger and my fury were poured out … and you shall become a curse and a thing that appalls and a cursing and a reproach, and you shall not see this place again"** (v18) — Hebrew אַף "anger", חֵמָה "fury" (literally "heat"); נִתַּךְ "be poured out"; אָלָה and קְלָלָה stand side by side, and English has one word "curse": אָלָה "a curse" (the curse called down by an oath: its row), קְלָלָה "a cursing" (noun of קִלֵּל); שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls", חֶרְפָּה "reproach".
- **"The LORD hath said concerning you, O ye remnant of Judah; Go ye not into Egypt: know certainly that I have admonished you this day" → "Yahweh has spoken concerning you, remnant of Judah: Do not enter Egypt. You shall surely know that I have warned you today"** (v19, 22) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר "spoken"; יָדֹעַ תֵּדְעוּ, the infinitive absolute with the imperfect, "you shall surely know" (literally "knowing you shall know"); הֵעִיד בְּ "warn" (literally "testify against").
- **"For ye dissembled in your hearts" → "For you have caused yourselves to wander at the cost of your souls"** (v20) — Hebrew הִתְעֵיתֶם, the qere (ketiv התעתים, the same verb spelled with a different ending), hifil of תָּעָה "wander": "you have caused to wander" with no object written ("yourselves" supplied: you led yourselves astray); בְּנַפְשׁוֹתֵיכֶם "at the cost of your souls" (literally "with / in your souls", as 2 Sam 23:17); KJV "hearts" is not the word. **"declare unto us" → "so tell us"** — כֵּן הַגֶּד־לָנוּ.
- **"And now I have this day declared it to you; but ye have not obeyed … nor any thing for the which he hath sent me" → "And I have told you today, and you have not listened … or to anything with which he has sent me to you"** (v21) — Hebrew וָאַגִּד; no "now", no "it"; וּלְכֹל "and to all", under the negation "or to anything".
- **"in the place whither ye desire to go and to sojourn" → "in the place where you delight to enter to sojourn there"** (v22) — Hebrew חֲפַצְתֶּם לָבוֹא "you delight (have delight) to enter" (the חָפֵץ row); לָגוּר שָׁם "to sojourn there".
