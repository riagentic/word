## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v10) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1–3, 10) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; sign-act of the stones at Tachpanches.
- **"when Jeremiah had made an end of speaking … all these words" → "And it came to pass, when Jeremiah had finished speaking … all these words, that"** (v1–2) — Hebrew וַיְהִי כְּכַלּוֹת … וַיֹּאמֶר; כִּלָּה "finish"; "all these words" stands again at the end, after "with which Yahweh their Elohim had sent him to them".
- **"and all the proud men, saying unto Jeremiah, Thou speakest falsely" → "and all the presumptuous men said, saying to Jeremiah, You are speaking falsehood"** (v2) — Hebrew הַזֵּדִים "the presumptuous" (its row); וַיֹּאמֶר … אֹמְרִים "said … saying" (both kept); שֶׁקֶר אַתָּה מְדַבֵּר "falsehood you are speaking". **"Go not into Egypt" → "Do not enter Egypt"** — בּוֹא "enter", as 42:14–22; לָגוּר "to sojourn".
- **"But Baruch … setteth thee on against us, for to deliver us into the hand of the Chaldeans, that they might put us to death, and carry us away captives into Babylon" → "but Baruch … is inciting you against us, in order to give us into the hand of the Chaldeans, to put us to death and to carry us into exile to Babylon"** (v3) — Hebrew מַסִּית, a participle of הֵסִית "incite"; לְמַעַן תֵּת "in order to give"; the two infinitives לְהָמִית "to put to death" and לְהַגְלוֹת "to carry into exile" have no subject ("they might" is not written).
- **"obeyed not the voice of the LORD" → "did not listen to the voice of Yahweh"** (v4, 7) — שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל; לָשֶׁבֶת "to dwell".
- **"that were returned from all nations, whither they had been driven, to dwell in the land of Judah" → "who had returned from all the nations to which they had been driven, to sojourn in the land of Judah"** (v5) — Hebrew הַגּוֹיִם with the article; נִדְּחוּ "had been driven" (literally "thrust"); לָגוּר "to sojourn" (the same verb as in v2; not לָשֶׁבֶת of v4).
- **"Even men, and women, and children, and the king's daughters, and every person" → "the grown men and the women and the little ones and the daughters of the king, and every soul"** (v6) — Hebrew הַגְּבָרִים, adult males beside women and little ones (the גֶּבֶר row), each noun with the article; טַף "little ones"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; הִנִּיחַ אֶת "left with"; "the chief of the guards" (רַב־טַבָּחִים) as 2 Kgs 25:8; "and Jeremiah … and Baruch" go on the list of the objects of "took" (v5), with no "also".
- **"So they came into the land of Egypt … thus came they even to Tahpanhes" → "And they entered the land of Egypt … and they came as far as Tahpanhes"** (v7) — וַיָּבֹאוּ … וַיָּבֹאוּ עַד. The name stands in v7, 8, 9 as תַּחְפַּנְחֵס (token `tahpanhes`).
- **"Then came the word of the LORD unto Jeremiah in Tahpanhes" → "And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying"** (v8) — the prophets' formula וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֶל … לֵאמֹר.
- **"Take great stones in thine hand, and hide them in the clay in the brickkiln, which is at the entry of Pharaoh's house … in the sight of the men of Judah" → "Take in your hand great stones, and hide them in the mortar, in the brickwork that is at the entrance of the house of Pharaoh … before the eyes of men, Judeans"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּיָדְךָ, singular "hand"; טָמַן "hide (in the ground)", also v10. בַּמֶּלֶט, found only here: "mortar" or "clay" (uncertain); בַּמַּלְבֵּן, from לְבֵנָה "brick": "brickwork" — a brick terrace or pavement; elsewhere a brick mould or kiln (2 Sam 12:31; Nah 3:14); uncertain. בֵּית־פַּרְעֹה "the house of Pharaoh" (not "palace"); פֶּתַח "entrance"; לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of"; אֲנָשִׁים יְהוּדִים "men, Judeans" (the noun stands beside "men"; the Hebrew does not say "of Judah").- **"Behold, I will send and take Nebuchadrezzar … and will set his throne upon these stones that I have hid; and he shall spread his royal pavilion over them" → "Behold, I am sending, and I will take Nebuchadrezzar … and I will set his throne above these stones that I have hidden, and he shall spread his canopy over them"** (v10) — Hebrew הִנְנִי שֹׁלֵחַ, a participle (as 25:9); מִמַּעַל לְ "above"; שַׁפְרִירוֹ, the qere (ketiv שפרורו, the same word spelled with ו), found only here: "canopy" (a spread cover or carpet; uncertain); "royal" is not written. **"and say unto them" → "and you shall say to them"** — וְאָמַרְתָּ, a perfect with "and" (the command goes on).
- **"And when he cometh, he shall smite the land of Egypt, and deliver such as are for death to death" → "And he shall come and strike the land of Egypt: those who are for death, to death"** (v11) — Hebrew וּבָא, the qere (ketiv ובאה, the same verb with a final ה, read by some as "and she shall come"); הִכָּה "strike"; אֲשֶׁר לַמָּוֶת לַמָּוֶת, literally "who for the death, to the death": no verb "deliver" or "give over" is written; מָוֶת "death" (not "pestilence": that is דֶּבֶר; death by disease is meant, as 15:2); שְׁבִי "captivity" (the captives).
- **"And I will kindle a fire in the houses of the gods of Egypt … and he shall array himself with the land of Egypt, as a shepherd putteth on his garment" → "And I will kindle a fire in the houses of the gods of Egypt … and he shall wrap himself in the land of Egypt as the shepherd wraps himself in his garment"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהִצַּתִּי "and I will kindle", first person (the Septuagint and others "he shall kindle", from memory; the MT is kept); בָּתֵּי "houses" (not "temples", also v13); אֱלֹהֵי מִצְרַיִם "the gods of Egypt", plain; שָׁבָם "take them captive"; עָטָה "wrap oneself" (as a garment is put on, quickly and wholly); others take עָטָה here from a word "pick clean (of lice)" (uncertain; ESV "clean … of vermin"); בֶּגֶד "garment"; בְּשָׁלוֹם, the shalom token.
- **"He shall break also the images of Bethshemesh … and the houses of the gods of the Egyptians" → "And he shall break in pieces the pillars of Beit Shemesh … and the houses of the gods of Egypt"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces"; מַצְּבוֹת "pillars" (standing stones: here the obelisks); בֵּית שֶׁמֶשׁ "house of the sun", the Hebrew name of the Egyptian city On / Heliopolis (Gen 41:45), not the Beth-shemesh of Judah; the token form now gives the name alone ("(Heliopolis)" removed). The Hebrew puts "the houses of the gods of Egypt" first.
