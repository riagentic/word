# Jeremiah 48

[1] For ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:
Woe to ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧, for it is laid waste;
⟦kiriathaim|Qiryatayim|Kiriathaim⟧ is put to shame, it is captured;
the secure height is put to shame and dismayed.

[2] There is no more the praise of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
in ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ they have devised evil against her:
Come, and let us cut her off from being a nation.
Also you, Madmen, shall be silenced;
after you the sword shall go.

[3] A voice of outcry from ⟦horonaim|Choronayim|Horonaim⟧:
havoc and great breaking.

[4] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is broken;
her little ones have made an outcry heard.

[5] For the ascent of ⟦luhith|Luchit|Luhith⟧,
with weeping weeping goes up;
for on the descent of ⟦horonaim|Choronayim|Horonaim⟧
the adversaries have heard an outcry of breaking.

[6] Flee, rescue your soul,
and you shall be like Aroer in the wilderness.

[7] For because you have trusted in your works and in your treasuries,
you also shall be captured;
and ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧ shall go out into exile,
his priests and his princes together.

[8] And the one who lays waste shall come to every city,
and no city shall escape;
and the valley shall perish,
and the tableland be destroyed,
as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has said.

[9] Give a wing to ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
for flying she shall go out;
and her cities shall become a thing that appalls,
without anyone dwelling in them.

[10] Cursed is he who does the work of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with slackness,
and cursed is he who holds back his sword from blood.

[11] At ease has ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ been from his youth,
and he has been quiet on his lees,
and he has not been emptied from vessel to vessel,
and into exile he has not gone;
therefore his taste has stood in him,
and his smell has not changed.

[12] Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and I will send to him tilters, and they will tilt him;
and his vessels they will empty,
and their jars they will shatter.

[13] And ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be ashamed of ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧,
as the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were ashamed of ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, their trust.

[14] How do you say, Mighty ones are we,
and men of ability for war?

[15] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is laid waste, and up into her cities he has gone,
and the choice of his young men have gone down to the slaughter,
declares the King, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name.

[16] Near is the calamity of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ to come,
and his evil hastens greatly.

[17] Show grief for him, all who are around him,
and all who know his name;
say, How is the staff of strength broken,
the rod of beauty!

[18] Come down from glory,
and sit in thirst,
dweller, daughter of ⟦dibon|Divon|Dibon⟧;
for the one who lays waste ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has come up against you,
he has ruined your fortresses.

[19] Stand by the way and watch,
dweller of Aroer;
ask him who flees and her who escapes,
say, What has happened?

[20] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is put to shame, for it is dismayed;
wail and cry out;
tell in the Arnon
that ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is laid waste.

[21] And judgment has come to the land of the tableland: to ⟦holon|Cholon|Holon⟧ and to ⟦jahaz|Yahtzah|Jahzah⟧ and upon ⟦mephaath|Mefaat|Mephaath⟧,

[22] and upon ⟦dibon|Divon|Dibon⟧ and upon ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧ and upon ⟦beth-diblathaim|Beit Divlatayim|Beth-diblathaim⟧,

[23] and upon ⟦kiriathaim|Qiryatayim|Kiriathaim⟧ and upon ⟦beth-gamul|Beit Gamul|Beth-gamul⟧ and upon ⟦beth-meon|Beit Meon|Beth-meon⟧,

[24] and upon ⟦kerioth|Qeriyot|Kerioth⟧ and upon ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧, and upon all the cities of the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, those far and those near.

[25] The horn of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is hewn off,
and his arm is broken, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[26] Make him drunk, for against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he has made himself great;
and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall splash in his vomit,
and he too shall become a derision.

[27] Or was not ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ the derision to you?
Was he found among thieves,
that as often as you speak of him you shake yourself?

[28] Leave cities and abide in the crag,
dwellers of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
and be like a dove that nests
in the sides of the mouth of the pitfall.

[29] We have heard of the pride of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
very proud;
his loftiness and his pride and his haughtiness
and the height of his heart.

[30] I know, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, his raging, and it is not right;
his empty talk, not right have they done.

[31] Therefore over ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ I will wail,
and for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, all of it, I will cry out;
for the men of ⟦kir-heres|Qir Cheres|Kir-heres⟧ he will moan.

[32] More than with the weeping of ⟦jazer|Yazer|Jazer⟧ I weep for you,
vine of ⟦sibmah|Sivmah|Sibmah⟧;
your tendrils have passed over the sea,
to the sea of ⟦jazer|Yazer|Jazer⟧ they reached;
upon your summer fruit and upon your vintage
the one who lays waste has fallen.

[33] And gladness and rejoicing are gathered away from the garden land
and from the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
and wine from the presses I have made to cease;
he does not tread with the shout;
the shout is no shout.

[34] From the outcry of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ to ⟦elealeh|Elaleh|Elealeh⟧,
to ⟦jahaz|Yahatz|Jahaz⟧ they have given their voice,
from ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧ to ⟦horonaim|Choronayim|Horonaim⟧,
⟦eglath-shelishiyah|Eglat Shelishiyah|Eglath-shelishiyah⟧;
for also the waters of Nimrim
shall become desolations.

[35] And I will make cease for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
him who offers up on a high place
and him who makes smoke to his god.

[36] Therefore my heart for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧
murmurs like flutes,
and my heart for the men of ⟦kir-heres|Qir Cheres|Kir-heres⟧
murmurs like flutes;
therefore the abundance he has made has perished.

[37] For every head is baldness,
and every beard is clipped;
upon all hands are gashes,
and upon loins sackcloth.

[38] On all the roofs of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and in her squares,
all of it is lamentation;
for I have broken ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧
like a vessel in which there is no delight, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[39] How it is dismayed! They have wailed!
How ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has turned the neck, ashamed!
And ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has become a derision and a terror
to all who are around him.

[40] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
Behold, like the eagle he shall swoop,
and spread his wings over ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

[41] The towns are captured,
and the strongholds are seized;
and the heart of the mighty ones of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be in that day
like the heart of a woman in distress.

[42] And ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be destroyed from being a people,
for against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he has made himself great.

[43] Dread and pitfall and trap
are upon you, inhabitant of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!

[44] He who flees from the face of the dread
shall fall into the pitfall,
and he who comes up from the pitfall
shall be captured in the trap;
for I will bring upon her, upon ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
the year of their visitation, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[45] In the shade of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ they have stood,
without strength, those who flee;
for fire went out from ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧,
and flame from between ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧,
and it devoured the edge of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧
and the crown of the head of the sons of tumult.

[46] Woe to you, ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧!
The people of ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧ has perished;
for your sons have been taken into captivity,
and your daughters into captivity.

[47] And I will restore the fortunes of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧
in the latter days, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
Thus far is the judgment of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v1, 15) — יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Layout** — the heading "For Moab" and the words "Thus says Yahweh of Hosts …" are the first part of v1 in the Hebrew (they stood before the verse number); "Thus far is the judgment of Moab" is the last part of v47 (it stood after it). v1–20, 25–47 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents; v21–24 (the list of cities) prose. The quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark them. Place names keep their tokens.
- **Beside Isa 15–16; 24:17–18** — v29–38 have much of Isa 15:2–7; 16:6–11, and v43–44 Isa 24:17–18; v45–46 have Num 21:28–29; 24:17. Where the Hebrew is the same the wording of Isaiah is kept; each is translated as printed, and the differences are named at their verse.
- **"Woe unto Nebo! for it is spoiled: Kiriathaim is confounded and taken: Misgab is confounded and dismayed" → "Woe to Nebo, for it is laid waste; Kiriathaim is put to shame, it is captured; the secure height is put to shame and dismayed"** (v1) — Hebrew לְמוֹאָב "for Moab" (a heading, as 46:2); הוֹי אֶל "Woe to" (the prophets' row); שֻׁדָּדָה pual of שָׁדַד "be laid waste" (as Isa 15:1; also v8, 15, 18, 20, 32); הֹבִישָׁה "is put to shame" (בּוֹשׁ, as 46:24; also v20); לָכַד "capture" (its row; also v7, 41, 44); הַמִּשְׂגָּב "the secure height" (its row; KJV took it as a name, "Misgab"); חָתָּה "dismayed" (חָתַת, lit. "be shattered": its row; also v20, 39).
- **"There shall be no more praise of Moab: in Heshbon they have devised evil against it … Also thou shalt be cut down, O Madmen; the sword shall pursue thee" → "There is no more the praise of Moab; in Heshbon they have devised evil against her: Come, and let us cut her off from being a nation. Also you, Madmen, shall be silenced; after you the sword shall go"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵין עוֹד "there is no more"; תְּהִלָּה "praise"; חָשַׁב "devise" (its row: of the mind), a play on חֶשְׁבּוֹן; הִכְרִית "cut off" (its row); מִגּוֹי "from (being) a nation"; תִּדֹּמִּי "you shall be silenced" (דָּמַם, a play on מַדְמֵן; "be still" in 47:6); אַחֲרַיִךְ תֵּלֶךְ "after you shall go".
- **"A voice of crying shall be from Horonaim, spoiling and great destruction" → "A voice of outcry from Horonaim: havoc and great breaking"** (v3) — Hebrew צְעָקָה "outcry" (its row); שֹׁד "havoc" (its row); שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as Isa 15:5; also v5).
- **"her little ones have caused a cry to be heard" → "her little ones have made an outcry heard"** (v4) — the qere צְעִירֶיהָ "her little ones" (the young); the written צעוריה may be read "to Zoar" (as the Septuagint has it, from memory); הִשְׁמִיעַ "make heard" (its row); זְעָקָה "outcry" (its row: one rendering for both spellings).
- **"For in the going up of Luhith continual weeping shall go up; for in the going down of Horonaim the enemies have heard a cry of destruction" → "For the ascent of Luhith, with weeping weeping goes up; for on the descent of Horonaim the adversaries have heard an outcry of breaking"** (v5) — the qere הַלּוּחִית "Luhith" (the written הלחות "the tablets"); מַעֲלֵה "the ascent" (as Isa 15:5); בִּבְכִי יַעֲלֶה־בֶּכִי, "with weeping weeping goes up" (Isa 15:5 has "with weeping he goes up on it"); מוֹרַד "descent" (Isa 15:5 "the way of"); צָרֵי "the adversaries of" (צַר, its row), in construct before "outcry": "the adversaries have heard an outcry of breaking", or "the distresses of the outcry … they have heard" (uncertain); צַעֲקַת־שֶׁבֶר "an outcry of breaking" (as Isa 15:5).
- **"Flee, save your lives, and be like the heath in the wilderness" → "Flee, rescue your soul, and you shall be like Aroer in the wilderness"** (v6) — Hebrew מַלְּטוּ נַפְשְׁכֶם "rescue your soul" (מִלֵּט "rescue", נֶפֶשׁ "soul" singular: their rows); וְתִהְיֶינָה "and you shall be" (feminine plural; also read "and they shall be"); כַּעֲרוֹעֵר spelled as the town Aroer (v19); the same word is taken by many as עַרְעָר "a juniper" (as 17:6) or "a destitute one" (Ps 102:17); the Septuagint has "a wild ass" (from memory). As printed: "Aroer".
- **"For because thou hast trusted in thy works and in thy treasures … and Chemosh shall go forth into captivity with his priests and his princes together" → "For because you have trusted in your works and in your treasuries … and Chemosh shall go out into exile, his priests and his princes together"** (v7) — the qere כְמוֹשׁ "Chemosh" and יַחְדָּיו "together" (the written כמיש, יחד: other spellings, same meaning); בִּטְחֵךְ "your trusting" (feminine: Moab as a woman); מַעֲשִׂים "works" (or "deeds"); אוֹצָרוֹת "treasuries" (its row); יָצָא בַּגּוֹלָה "go out into exile"; שָׂרִים "princes" (its row); no "with".
- **"And the spoiler shall come upon every city … the plain shall be destroyed, as the LORD hath spoken" → "And the one who lays waste shall come to every city, and no city shall escape; and the valley shall perish, and the tableland be destroyed, as Yahweh has said"** (v8) — Hebrew שֹׁדֵד "one who lays waste" (as Isa 16:4; also v18, 32); אֶל "to"; נִמְלַט "escape"; אָבַד "perish" (its row); מִישׁוֹר "tableland" (its row; also v21); נִשְׁמַד "be destroyed" (its row); אֲשֶׁר אָמַר "as (lit. "which") Yahweh has said" (אָמַר "say", its row).
- **"Give wings unto Moab, that it may flee and get away: for the cities thereof shall be desolate, without any to dwell therein" → "Give a wing to Moab, for flying she shall go out; and her cities shall become a thing that appalls, without anyone dwelling in them"** (v9) — Hebrew צִיץ: the meaning is uncertain here ("a wing", the sense taken by the old versions and the verb after it; also read "salt", to make the land barren, or "a marker", a gravestone; elsewhere צִיץ is "blossom", "plate"); נָצֹא תֵּצֵא, infinitive absolute with the verb, "flying she shall go out" (נָצָא, only here, taken as "fly"; the literal form kept: the infinitive is a different verb from יָצָא "go out"); שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (its row); מֵאֵין יוֹשֵׁב בָּהֵן "without anyone dwelling in them".
- **"Cursed be he that doeth the work of the LORD deceitfully, and cursed be he that keepeth back his sword from blood" → "Cursed is he who does the work of Yahweh with slackness, and cursed is he who holds back his sword from blood"** (v10) — Hebrew אָרוּר + participle "Cursed is he who" (its row: no verb "be"); מְלֶאכֶת יְהוָה "the work of Yahweh"; רְמִיָּה "slackness" (the word also means "deceit": the slack hand, Prov 10:4; 12:24); מָנַע "hold back" (its row); מִדָּם "from blood".
- **"Moab hath been at ease from his youth, and he hath settled on his lees … therefore his taste remained in him, and his scent is not changed" → "At ease has Moab been from his youth, and he has been quiet on his lees … therefore his taste has stood in him, and his smell has not changed"** (v11) — Hebrew שַׁאֲנַן "at ease" (its row; no verb in the Hebrew); שֹׁקֵט "quiet" (שָׁקַט, its row); שְׁמָרִים "lees" (the dregs of wine); הוּרַק "be emptied"; כְּלִי "vessel" (its row); הָלַךְ בַּגּוֹלָה "go into exile"; עָמַד "stand" (remain); טַעַם "taste"; רֵיחַ "smell" (its row); נָמָר "changed" (a rare form of מוּר).
- **"I will send unto him wanderers, that shall cause him to wander, and shall empty his vessels, and break their bottles" → "and I will send to him tilters, and they will tilt him; and his vessels they will empty, and their jars they will shatter"** (v12) — Hebrew הִנֵּה יָמִים בָּאִים "behold, days are coming" (no "the"); וְשִׁלַּחְתִּי "and I will send" (וְ kept; no "when"); צֹעִים … וְצֵעֻהוּ "tilters … and they will tilt him" (צָעָה "tip, tilt", of a jar poured off its lees; KJV "wanderers"); הֵרִיק "empty"; נְבָלִים "jars" (its row); נִפֵּץ "shatter" (its row).
- **"And Moab shall be ashamed of Chemosh, as the house of Israel was ashamed of Bethel their confidence" → "… as the house of Israel were ashamed of Bethel, their trust"** (v13) — Hebrew וּבֹשׁ "and shall be ashamed" (בּוֹשׁ, its row), בֹּשׁוּ plural; מִבְטָח "trust" (its row). בֵּית אֵל may be the town of Jeroboam's calf (1 Kgs 12:29) or "the house of El"; the token keeps the name.
- **"We are mighty and strong men for the war" → "Mighty ones are we, and men of ability for war"** (v14) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones" (its row); אַנְשֵׁי־חַיִל "men of ability" (its row); לַמִּלְחָמָה "for war".
- **"Moab is spoiled, and gone up out of her cities, and his chosen young men are gone down to the slaughter" → "Moab is laid waste, and up into her cities he has gone, and the choice of his young men have gone down to the slaughter"** (v15) — Hebrew שֻׁדַּד מוֹאָב וְעָרֶיהָ עָלָה, "Moab is laid waste, and her cities he has gone up": the destroyer has gone up into (against) her cities ("into" supplied), or "her cities have gone up" in smoke (the verb is singular; uncertain); מִבְחַר בַּחוּרָיו "the choice of his young men"; יָרְדוּ לַטָּבַח "have gone down to the slaughter"; the closing formula as 46:18 ("Yahweh of Hosts is his name": no "whose").
- **"The calamity of Moab is near to come, and his affliction hasteth fast" → "Near is the calamity of Moab to come, and his evil hastens greatly"** (v16) — Hebrew אֵיד "calamity" (its row); רָעָתוֹ "his evil" (רָעָה, its row; not "affliction"); מִהַר "hasten" (its row); מְאֹד "greatly".
- **"All ye that are about him, bemoan him … How is the strong staff broken, and the beautiful rod!" → "Show grief for him, all who are around him … How is the staff of strength broken, the rod of beauty!"** (v17) — Hebrew נוּדוּ לוֹ "show grief for him" (lit. "sway for him": its row, as Job 2:11); אֵיכָה "how" (the cry of a lament); מַטֵּה־עֹז "the staff of strength" (עֹז "strength", its row); מַקֵּל תִּפְאָרָה "the rod of beauty" (מַקֵּל "rod", beside מַטֶּה "staff": its row; תִּפְאָרָה "beauty", the reviewer row).
- **"Thou daughter that dost inhabit Dibon, come down from thy glory, and sit in thirst; for the spoiler of Moab shall come upon thee, and he shall destroy thy strong holds" → "Come down from glory, and sit in thirst, dweller, daughter of Dibon; for the one who lays waste Moab has come up against you, he has ruined your fortresses"** (v18) — the qere וּשְׁבִי "and sit" (the written ישבי without "and"); כָּבוֹד "glory" (no "your"); צָמָא "thirst" (its row: the dry ground); יוֹשֶׁבֶת בַּת־דִּיבוֹן "dweller, daughter of Dibon" (as 46:19); עָלָה בָךְ perfect "has come up against you"; שִׁחֵת "ruin" (its row); מִבְצָרִים "fortresses" (its row).
- **"stand by the way, and espy; ask him that fleeth, and her that escapeth, and say, What is done?" → "Stand by the way and watch, dweller of Aroer; ask him who flees and her who escapes, say, What has happened?"** (v19) — Hebrew אֶל־דֶּרֶךְ "by (lit. "to") the way"; צָפָה "watch"; נָס "one who flees" (masculine), נִמְלָטָה "one who escapes" (feminine); מַה־נִּהְיָתָה "what has come to be". No "O".
- **"Moab is confounded; for it is broken down: howl and cry; tell ye it in Arnon, that Moab is spoiled" → "Moab is put to shame, for it is dismayed; wail and cry out; tell in the Arnon that Moab is laid waste"** (v20) — the qere הֵילִילוּ וּזְעָקוּ "wail and cry out" (plural; the written forms are feminine singular, to Aroer); הֵילִיל "wail" (its row; also v31, 39); זָעַק "cry out"; הִגִּיד "tell" (its row); בְאַרְנוֹן "in (by) the Arnon".
- **"And judgment is come upon the plain country; upon Holon, and upon Jahazah, and upon Mephaath" → "And judgment has come to the land of the tableland: to Holon and to Jahzah and upon Mephaath"** (v21–24) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ הַמִּישֹׁר "the land of the tableland"; אֶל "to" (three times) and עַל "upon" (the rest) kept apart; the qere מֵיפָעַת "Mephaath" (the written מופעת, another spelling); every "and" kept; הָרְחֹקוֹת וְהַקְּרֹבוֹת "those far and those near".
- **"The horn of Moab is cut off" → "The horn of Moab is hewn off"** (v25) — Hebrew נִגְדְּעָה, nifal of גִּדַּע "hew down" (its row); זְרוֹעַ "arm"; נִשְׁבָּרָה "is broken".
- **"Make ye him drunken: for he magnified himself against the LORD: Moab also shall wallow in his vomit, and he also shall be in derision" → "Make him drunk, for against Yahweh he has made himself great; and Moab shall splash in his vomit, and he too shall become a derision"** (v26, 42) — Hebrew הִשְׁכִּיר "make drunk" (its row); עַל־יְהוָה הִגְדִּיל, "against Yahweh he has made great" (hifil without object: he has made himself great, acted great; also v42); סָפַק "splash" (the verb is "clap, strike": he falls into it with a smack; uncertain); קִיא "vomit" (its row); שְׂחֹק "derision" (also v27, 39); וְהָיָה … גַּם־הוּא "and he too shall become".
- **"For was not Israel a derision unto thee? was he found among thieves? for since thou spakest of him, thou skippedst for joy" → "Or was not Israel the derision to you? Was he found among thieves, that as often as you speak of him you shake yourself?"** (v27) — Hebrew וְאִם לוֹא, "and if not" (a question: "or was not"); הַשְּׂחֹק with the article, "the derision"; the qere נִמְצָא "he was found" (masculine, to Israel; the written נמצאה feminine); מִדֵּי דְבָרֶיךָ בּוֹ "as often as your words of him"; תִּתְנוֹדָד "you shake yourself" (hitpael of נוּד, as v17: the shaking of the head in scorn, 18:16; KJV "skippedst for joy").
- **"O ye that dwell in Moab, leave the cities, and dwell in the rock, and be like the dove that maketh her nest in the sides of the hole's mouth" → "Leave cities and abide in the crag, dwellers of Moab; and be like a dove that nests in the sides of the mouth of the pitfall"** (v28) — Hebrew עִזְבוּ "leave" (עָזַב, its row: of a place); עָרִים without article; שָׁכַן "abide" (its row), beside יֹשְׁבֵי "dwellers" (יָשַׁב); סֶלַע "crag" (its row); יוֹנָה "dove"; קִנֵּן "nest"; עֶבְרֵי "the sides (lit. "the far sides") of"; פִּי־פָחַת "the mouth of the pitfall" (פַּחַת, as v43–44: a chasm, a gorge).
- **"We have heard the pride of Moab, (he is exceeding proud) his loftiness, and his arrogancy, and his pride, and the haughtiness of his heart" → "We have heard of the pride of Moab, very proud; his loftiness and his pride and his haughtiness and the height of his heart"** (v29) — as Isa 16:6 where the Hebrew is the same: גְּאוֹן "pride", גֵּאֶה "proud" (Isa גֵּא), גַּאֲוָה "haughtiness" (the Isa 13–16 row: four words of one root kept apart); Jeremiah has his own גָּבְהוֹ "his loftiness" (גֹּבַהּ "height") and רֻם לִבּוֹ "the height of his heart" (רוּם "be high"), where Isaiah has עֶבְרָתוֹ "his raging" and the clause that Jeremiah puts in v30.
- **"I know his wrath, saith the LORD; but it shall not be so; his lies shall not so effect it" → "I know, declares Yahweh, his raging, and it is not right; his empty talk, not right have they done"** (v30) — Hebrew אֲנִי יָדַעְתִּי "I know" (the pronoun stresses "I"); עֶבְרָה "raging" (as Isa 16:6); וְלֹא־כֵן "and (it is) not right" (lit. "not so"), set off by the accents; בַּדָּיו "his empty talk" (as Isa 16:6; the plural may also mean "his empty talkers"); לֹא־כֵן עָשׂוּ "not right have they done" (plural). Isa 16:6 has only "not right is his empty talk".
- **"Therefore will I howl for Moab, and I will cry out for all Moab; mine heart shall mourn for the men of Kirheres" → "Therefore over Moab I will wail, and for Moab, all of it, I will cry out; for the men of Kir-heres he will moan"** (v31) — Hebrew עַל־כֵּן "therefore"; אֲיֵלִיל "I will wail" (Isa 16:7 "Moab will wail for Moab"); כֻּלֹּה "all of it" (as Isa 16:7); אֶזְעָק "I will cry out"; יֶהְגֶּה third person, "he will moan" (הָגָה, as Isa 16:7 "you will moan"; the versions have "I will moan", from memory; "mine heart" is not in the Hebrew); אַנְשֵׁי קִיר־חֶרֶשׂ "the men of Kir-heres" (Isa 16:7 "the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth"); the token's traditional form corrected to "Kir-heres", as Isa 16:11 (also v36).
- **"O vine of Sibmah, I will weep for thee with the weeping of Jazer: thy plants are gone over the sea, they reach even to the sea of Jazer: the spoiler is fallen upon thy summer fruits and upon thy vintage" → "More than with the weeping of Jazer I weep for you, vine of Sibmah; your tendrils have passed over the sea, to the sea of Jazer they reached; upon your summer fruit and upon your vintage the one who lays waste has fallen"** (v32) — Hebrew מִבְּכִי יַעְזֵר "more than with (lit. "from") the weeping of Jazer" (Isa 16:9 בִּבְכִי "with the weeping"); אֶבְכֶּה־לָּךְ "I weep for you"; הַגֶּפֶן שִׂבְמָה "vine of Sibmah" (the article marks the address); נְטִישׁוֹת "tendrils" (lit. "what is let loose"; Isa 16:8 has שְׁלֻחוֹת "shoots" and the verb נִטְּשׁוּ "spread out"); עָבְרוּ יָם "passed over the sea" and נָגָעוּ "reached" (as Isa 16:8); עַד יָם יַעְזֵר "to the sea of Jazer" as printed (Isa 16:8 "to Jazer"; "sea" may be repeated from the line before); קַיִץ "summer fruit"; בָּצִיר "vintage" (its row; Isa 16:9 קָצִיר "harvest"); שֹׁדֵד "the one who lays waste" (Isa 16:9 הֵידָד "the shout").
- **"And joy and gladness is taken from the plentiful field, and from the land of Moab; and I have caused wine to fail from the winepresses: none shall tread with shouting; their shouting shall be no shouting" → "And gladness and rejoicing are gathered away from the garden land and from the land of Moab; and wine from the presses I have made to cease; he does not tread with the shout; the shout is no shout"** (v33) — as Isa 16:10 where the Hebrew is the same: נֶאֶסְפָה "are gathered away", שִׂמְחָה וָגִיל "gladness and rejoicing", כַּרְמֶל "garden land" (without article here), יֶקֶב "press", דָּרַךְ "tread", הֵידָד "shout" (its row), הִשְׁבַּתִּי "I have made to cease"; Jeremiah's own: "and from the land of Moab", "wine … I have made to cease" (Isa: "the shout"), לֹא־יִדְרֹךְ הֵידָד "he does not tread, (with) the shout" (no subject named; Isa "the treader does not tread"), and הֵידָד לֹא הֵידָד "the shout is no shout" (the shout of the treaders has become the shout of war: 25:30; 51:14).
- **"From the cry of Heshbon even unto Elealeh, and even unto Jahaz, have they uttered their voice … for the waters also of Nimrim shall be desolate" → "From the outcry of Heshbon to Elealeh, to Jahaz they have given their voice, from Zoar to Horonaim, Eglath-shelishiyah; for also the waters of Nimrim shall become desolations"** (v34) — beside Isa 15:4–6: זְעָקָה "outcry" (its row); נָתְנוּ קוֹלָם "they have given their voice" (its row; Isa 15:4 "their voice is heard"); עֶגְלַת שְׁלִשִׁיָּה a place name (token), as Isa 15:5; לִמְשַׁמּוֹת יִהְיוּ "shall become desolations" (Isa 15:6 "will be desolations"); גַּם "also".
- **"Moreover I will cause to cease in Moab, saith the LORD, him that offereth in the high places, and him that burneth incense to his gods" → "And I will make cease for Moab, declares Yahweh, him who offers up on a high place and him who makes smoke to his god"** (v35) — Hebrew הִשְׁבַּתִּי לְ "I will make cease for"; מַעֲלֶה בָמָה "him who offers up (on) a high place" (הֶעֱלָה "offer up", its row; בָמָה without article); מַקְטִיר "him who makes smoke" (הִקְטִיר without an object: the reviewer row); לֵאלֹהָיו "to his god" (the plural form אֱלֹהִים of the one god of Moab, Chemosh; "his gods" is also possible).
- **"Therefore mine heart shall sound for Moab like pipes, and mine heart shall sound like pipes for the men of Kirheres: because the riches that he hath gotten are perished" → "Therefore my heart for Moab murmurs like flutes, and my heart for the men of Kir-heres murmurs like flutes; therefore the abundance he has made has perished"** (v36) — beside Isa 16:11: הָמָה "murmur" (as Isa 16:11: the sound of an instrument); לִבִּי "my heart" twice (Isa "my inward parts … my inmost part"); חֲלִלִים "flutes" (its row; Isa "a lyre"); עַל־כֵּן "therefore" (not "because"); יִתְרַת עָשָׂה "the abundance he has made" (as Isa 15:7), with the plural verb אָבָדוּ "have perished" (its parts).
- **"For every head shall be bald, and every beard clipped: upon all the hands shall be cuttings, and upon the loins sackcloth" → "For every head is baldness, and every beard is clipped; upon all hands are gashes, and upon loins sackcloth"** (v37) — beside Isa 15:2: קָרְחָה "baldness" (a noun: no verb), גְּרֻעָה "clipped" (as Isa 15:2); גְּדֻדֹת "gashes" (noun of the 47:5 "gash yourself"); מָתְנַיִם "loins"; שַׂק "sackcloth" (its row).
- **"There shall be lamentation generally upon all the housetops of Moab, and in the streets thereof: for I have broken Moab like a vessel wherein is no pleasure" → "On all the roofs of Moab and in her squares, all of it is lamentation; for I have broken Moab like a vessel in which there is no delight"** (v38) — beside Isa 15:3: גַּגּוֹת "roofs", רְחֹבוֹת "squares", כֻּלֹּה "all of it" (as Isa 15:3); מִסְפֵּד "lamentation" (noun of סָפַד "lament", its row); כְּלִי "vessel"; חֵפֶץ "delight" (its row).
- **"They shall howl, saying, How is it broken down! how hath Moab turned the back with shame! so shall Moab be a derision and a dismaying to all them about him" → "How it is dismayed! They have wailed! How Moab has turned the neck, ashamed! And Moab has become a derision and a terror to all who are around him"** (v39) — Hebrew אֵיךְ "how" twice; חַתָּה "is dismayed" (as v1, 20); הֵילִילוּ "they have wailed" (or the command "wail!", as v20: the same letters); הִפְנָה עֹרֶף "turned the neck" (the back of the neck, in flight: its row); בּוֹשׁ "ashamed" (an infinitive: "in shame"); וְהָיָה "and has become" (the weqatal after the perfects; or "and shall become"); מְחִתָּה "terror" (noun of חָתַת).
- **"Behold, he shall fly as an eagle, and shall spread his wings over Moab" → "Behold, like the eagle he shall swoop, and spread his wings over Moab"** (v40) — Hebrew כַנֶּשֶׁר "like the eagle" (נֶשֶׁר "eagle" or "vulture"); דָּאָה "swoop" (as Deut 28:49); פָּרַשׂ כְּנָפָיו "spread his wings"; אֶל "over (toward)". v40–41 stand again of Edom in 49:22.
- **"Kerioth is taken, and the strong holds are surprised, and the mighty men's hearts in Moab at that day shall be as the heart of a woman in her pangs" → "The towns are captured, and the strongholds are seized; and the heart of the mighty ones of Moab shall be in that day like the heart of a woman in distress"** (v41) — Hebrew הַקְּרִיּוֹת with the article, "the towns" (קִרְיָה "town", its row; the same letters without the article are the city Kerioth, v24: KJV took it so); נִלְכְּדָה … נִתְפָּשָׂה singular verbs with the plural subjects, "captured … seized" (לָכַד, תָּפַשׂ: their rows); מְצָדוֹת "strongholds" (its row); בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" (the prophets' row); לֵב singular "the heart"; מְצֵרָה "in distress" (pressed: a woman in labor).

No "O".

- **"Yet will I bring again the captivity of Moab in the latter days … Thus far is the judgment of Moab" → "And I will restore the fortunes of Moab in the latter days, declares Yahweh. Thus far is the judgment of Moab"** (v47) — Hebrew וְשַׁבְתִּי שְׁבוּת־מוֹאָב, literally "and I will turn back the captivity / the turning of Moab" (שְׁבוּת from שָׁבָה "take captive" or from שׁוּב "turn"), traditional "bring again the captivity": "restore the fortunes" (the reviewer row: one idiom, one rendering); וְ "and" (not "yet"); בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים "in the latter days" (its row); עַד־הֵנָּה "thus far" (lit. "until here"); מִשְׁפַּט מוֹאָב "the judgment of Moab", the closing note of the collection, part of the verse.

### Notes
- **Chemosh** — the god of Moab (Num 21:29; 1 Kgs 11:7; the Mesha stone).
- **v47** — the oracle ends with Moab's fortunes restored "in the latter days" (as 49:6 of Ammon, 49:39 of Elam).
