[32] therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am visiting upon ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ the ⟦nehelam|Nechelamite|Nehelamite⟧ and upon his seed; he shall not have a man dwelling in the midst of this people, and he shall not see the good that I am doing for my people, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he has spoken defection against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4, 8, 17, 21, 25) — יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, plain words as the prophets' row. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v4, 8, 21, 25). נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v9, 11, 14, 19, 23, 32), literally "the declaration of Yahweh".
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose (a letter, v4–23, and the word against Shemaiah, v24–32); the outer quotation marks are dropped, quoted speech inside the letter is marked.
- **"the residue of the elders which were carried away captives … whom Nebuchadnezzar had carried away captive" → "the rest of the elders of the exiles … whom Nebuchadnezzar had carried into exile"** (v1) — Hebrew יֶתֶר זִקְנֵי הַגּוֹלָה: יֶתֶר "rest" (its row), הַגּוֹלָה "the exile", a collective noun for the exiled body (the exile row; "the exiles" here and v4, 20, 31). הֶגְלָה "carry into exile" (also v4, 7, 14). Each of the four groups has its own אֶל "to". סֵפֶר "letter" (the word is "book", any written document: the סֵפֶר row; also v25, 29). The verse opens with וְ "And".
- **"(After that Jeconiah the king, and the queen, and the eunuchs, the princes … and the carpenters, and the smiths, were departed …)" → "after Jeconiah the king had gone out, and the great lady, and the eunuchs, the princes … and the craftsmen and the smiths"** (v2) — Hebrew צֵאת "going out"; הַגְּבִירָה "the great lady" (a rank at court, the king's mother: the גְּבִירָה row; the word says neither "queen" nor "mother"); סָרִיס "eunuch" (its row: also a court officer); שָׂרֵי "princes"; הֶחָרָשׁ וְהַמַּסְגֵּר "the craftsmen and the smiths" (the exile row; מַסְגֵּר uncertain, perhaps "locksmith" or "fortifier").
- **"which Zedekiah king of Judah sent unto Babylon to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon" → "sent to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, to Babylon, saying"** (v3) — the Hebrew order; בְּיַד "by the hand of"; לֵאמֹר "saying" opens the letter.
- **"Build ye houses, and dwell in them; and plant gardens, and eat the fruit of them" → "Build houses and dwell, and plant gardens and eat their fruit"** (v5; also v28) — Hebrew וּשְׁבוּ "and dwell" (the יָשַׁב row), "in them" is not written; פְּרִי "fruit".
- **"and beget sons and daughters … and give your daughters to husbands, that they may bear … that ye may be increased there, and not diminished" → "and beget … and give your daughters to men, and let them bear … and multiply there, and do not become few"** (v6) — Hebrew הוֹלִידוּ "beget" (its row); לַאֲנָשִׁים "to men"; וְתֵלַדְנָה "and let them bear"; רְבוּ "multiply" (the רָבָה row); וְאַל־תִּמְעָטוּ "and do not become few" (מָעַט).
- **"seek the peace of the city … for in the peace thereof shall ye have peace" → "seek the shalom of the city … for in its shalom there shall be shalom for you"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם three times (token `shalom`, the reviewer row; also v11); יִהְיֶה לָכֶם "there shall be for you". בַעֲדָהּ "on its behalf".
- **"Let not your prophets and your diviners, that be in the midst of you, deceive you, neither hearken to your dreams which ye cause to be dreamed" → "Let not your prophets who are in your midst and your diviners deceive you, and do not listen to your dreams which you cause to be dreamed"** (v8) — the Hebrew order; קֹסְמֵיכֶם "your diviners" (the קֶסֶם row); הִשִּׁיא "deceive"; חֲלֹמֹתֵיכֶם "your dreams" (not "their"), אֲשֶׁר אַתֶּם מַחְלְמִים hifil "which you cause to dream": you make the prophets dream for you (uncertain; perhaps "which you dream" with the hifil weakened).
- **"For they prophesy falsely unto you in my name" → "For in falsehood they are prophesying to you in my name"** (v9) — Hebrew בְשֶׁקֶר, the שֶׁקֶר row "falsehood" (also v21, 23, 31), stands first; נִבְּאִים participle.
- **"That after seventy years be accomplished at Babylon I will visit you, and perform my good word toward you, in causing you to return" → "When seventy years are fulfilled for Babylon, I will visit you, and I will make my good word stand upon you, to bring you back"** (v10) — Hebrew לְפִי מְלֹאת, literally "according to the mouth of the filling" = when they are full; לְבָבֶל "for Babylon" (Babylon's seventy years, Jer 25:11–12); פָּקַד "visit" (its row; also v32); וַהֲקִמֹתִי … אֶת־דְּבָרִי הַטּוֹב "and I will make my good word stand" (the הֵקִים דָּבָר row); עֲלֵיכֶם "upon you". The כִּי after "Thus says Yahweh" only opens the words spoken.
- **"For I know the thoughts that I think toward you … thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end" → "For I, I know the thoughts that I am thinking toward you … thoughts of shalom and not for evil, to give you a latter end and a hope"** (v11) — the Hebrew as it stands: אָנֹכִי written twice ("I, I know … that I am thinking"), חֹשֵׁב a participle; מַחֲשָׁבֹת "thoughts" (from חָשַׁב "think, devise": "plans" is the sense); מַחְשְׁבוֹת שָׁלוֹם "thoughts of shalom" but וְלֹא לְרָעָה "and not for evil": the two members differ in the Hebrew and are kept so; אַחֲרִית "latter end" (what comes after: as Prov 23:18, where it also stands with תִּקְוָה "hope"; "a future" is the sense, "an expected end" is KJV's pairing of the two nouns).
- **"Then shall ye call upon me, and ye shall go and pray unto me, and I will hearken unto you" → "And you shall call me, and go, and pray to me, and I will listen to you"** (v12) — Hebrew וּקְרָאתֶם אֹתִי, the object marker, "call me"; וַהֲלַכְתֶּם "and you shall go"; שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to" (its row).
- **"And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart" → "And you shall seek me and find, for you shall seek me with all your heart"** (v13) — Hebrew בִּקֵּשׁ and דָּרַשׁ, both "seek" (their rows: English has one word); וּמְצָאתֶם has no object; כִּי "for" (or "when": both are possible).
- **"And I will be found of you … and I will turn away your captivity" → "And I will be found by you … and I will restore your fortunes"** (v14) — Hebrew וְנִמְצֵאתִי לָכֶם (the נִמְצָא לְ row); וְשַׁבְתִּי אֶת־שְׁבוּתְכֶם (qere; ketiv שביתכם, the other form of the noun), literally "and I will turn back your captivity / your turning": the reviewer row "restore the fortunes" (traditional "turn the captivity"). הִדִּיחַ "drive"; הֲשִׁבֹתִי "bring back".
- **"Because ye have said" → "For you have said"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי. The verse seems to look ahead to v21 (the prophets in Babylon); the Septuagint has v14b–20 differently or not at all (from memory). The MT order is kept.
- **"Know that thus saith the LORD of the king … and of all the people … your brethren that are not gone forth with you into captivity" → "For thus says Yahweh concerning the king … your brothers who did not go out with you into the exile"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי "For"; אֶל "concerning" here (they are spoken of, not to: "against them", v17); אָחִים "brothers" (its row); בַּגּוֹלָה "into the exile". The sentence breaks off; v17 takes it up again with "thus says".
- **"I will send upon them the sword, the famine, and the pestilence, and will make them like vile figs, that cannot be eaten, they are so evil" → "I am sending against them the sword, the famine and the pestilence, and I will make them like the horrid figs that cannot be eaten for badness"** (v17) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מְשַׁלֵּחַ, a participle, with בָּם "against / among them"; the three nouns with the article (also v18); דֶּבֶר "pestilence" (its row); וְנָתַתִּי … כְּ "and I will give them like" = make; הַשֹּׁעָרִים only here: "horrid", "burst" or "rotten" (uncertain); מֵרֹעַ "from badness" (Jer 24:2–3 has רָעוֹת "bad" for the same figs).
- **"and will deliver them to be removed to all the kingdoms of the earth, to be a curse, and an astonishment, and an hissing, and a reproach" → "and I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, a curse and a thing that appalls and a hissing and a reproach"** (v18) — Hebrew לְזַעֲוָה (qere; ketiv לזועה, the same word spelled זְוָעָה) "horror" (the זַעֲוָה row: from זוּעַ "tremble"; KJV "removed" reads another root); אָלָה "curse", שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls", שְׁרֵקָה "hissing", חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (their rows); רָדַף אַחֲרֵי "pursue after".
- **"Because they have not hearkened … which I sent unto them by my servants the prophets, rising up early and sending them; but ye would not hear" → "because they have not listened … which I sent to them by my servants the prophets, rising early and sending; and you have not listened"** (v19) — Hebrew תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר "because" (literally "in place of that"); שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to" (both times; "would not" is not written). אֲשֶׁר שָׁלַחְתִּי אֲלֵיהֶם אֶת־עֲבָדַי: "by" is supplied, the Hebrew sets the servants as the object of "sent". עֶבֶד "servant" (of Yahweh's men: the עֶבֶד row; the word is "slave"). הַשְׁכֵּם וְשָׁלֹחַ, two infinitives absolute, "rising early and sending" (the הִשְׁכִּים row) = again and again. The turn from "they" to "you" is in the Hebrew.
- **"Hear ye therefore the word of the LORD, all ye of the captivity, whom I have sent from Jerusalem" → "And you, hear the word of Yahweh, all the exiles whom I have sent away from Jerusalem"** (v20) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם "And you"; שִׁלַּחְתִּי, piel of שָׁלַח, "sent away".
- **"which prophesy a lie unto you in my name; Behold, I will deliver them … and he shall slay them" → "who are prophesying to you in my name falsehood: Behold, I am giving them into the hand … and he shall strike them down"** (v21) — the Hebrew order; הִנְנִי נֹתֵן, a participle; הִכָּה "strike" (its row; "strike down": they are killed, v22). The name is written נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר here (the token's r form); אֶל "concerning" both times.
- **"And of them shall be taken up a curse by all the captivity of Judah … The LORD make thee like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire" → "And from them a curse shall be taken up by all the exiles of Judah … May Yahweh make you like Zedekiah and like Ahab …"** (v22) — Hebrew וְלֻקַּח מֵהֶם קְלָלָה "and a curse shall be taken from them" (קְלָלָה "curse", the noun of קִלֵּל: its row); לְכֹל גָּלוּת יְהוּדָה "for all the exile of Judah" (גָּלוּת, the exile row); יְשִׂמְךָ a jussive, "May … make you". The curse plays on קָלָם "roasted them" (קָלָה) and קְלָלָה: English cannot carry it.
- **"Because they have committed villany in Israel, and have committed adultery with their neighbours' wives, and have spoken lying words in my name … even I know, and am a witness" → "because they have done an outrage in Israel, and have committed adultery with the wives of their neighbors, and have spoken a word in my name, falsehood … and I am he who knows, and a witness"** (v23) — Hebrew יַעַן אֲשֶׁר "because"; עָשׂוּ נְבָלָה בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל "done an outrage in Israel" (the נְבָלָה row); נָאַף "commit adultery"; דָּבָר … שֶׁקֶר "a word … falsehood", in the Hebrew order. הַיּוֹדֵעַ "he who knows" is the qere; the ketiv הו ידע reads "he, knowing" (the same sense); וָעֵד "and a witness" (the עֵד row).
- **"Thus shalt thou also speak to Shemaiah the Nehelamite, saying" → "And to Shemaiah the Nehelamite you shall say, saying"** (v24) — Hebrew וְאֶל … תֹּאמַר לֵאמֹר, "saying" kept (its row). הַנֶּחֱלָמִי "the Nehelamite" (also v31, 32): the token form now gives the Hebrew word (a clan or place; the lexicon's lemma), not "of Nehelam".
- **"Because thou hast sent letters in thy name … saying" → "Because you, you have sent letters in your name … saying"** (v25) — Hebrew יַעַן אֲשֶׁר אַתָּה, the pronoun written; לֵאמֹר "saying" stands twice in the verse (after "Thus says" and before the letter's words). The "because" has no main clause: v26–28 give the letter, and the sentence is never finished (the reply comes in v31–32). Kept unfinished. "Zephaniah son of Maaseiah the priest": the priest is Zephaniah (as Jer 21:1).
- **"The LORD hath made thee priest in the stead of Jehoiada the priest, that ye should be officers in the house of the LORD, for every man that is mad, and maketh himself a prophet, that thou shouldest put him in prison, and in the stocks" → "Yahweh has given you as priest in place of Jehoiada the priest, for there to be overseers in the house of Yahweh over every man who is mad and prophesies, and you shall put him into the stocks and into the collar"** (v26) — Hebrew נְתָנְךָ כֹהֵן "has given you (as) priest"; לִהְיוֹת פְּקִדִים "for there to be overseers" (plural, no subject); לְכָל־אִישׁ "for / over every man"; מְשֻׁגָּע "mad" (the מְשֻׁגָּע row) and מִתְנַבֵּא, hitpael of נבא, "prophesies" (one English for the stems: the נִבָּא row; also v27); הַמַּהְפֶּכֶת "the stocks" (its row, uncertain), הַצִּינֹק only here, "collar" (uncertain).
- **"why hast thou not reproved Jeremiah of Anathoth, which maketh himself a prophet to you?" → "why have you not rebuked Jeremiah of Anathoth, who prophesies to you?"** (v27) — Hebrew גָּעַר בְּ "rebuke"; הָעֲנְּתֹתִי "the Anathothite" (the token gives the place name); הַמִּתְנַבֵּא as v26.
- **"For therefore he sent unto us in Babylon, saying, This captivity is long" → "For on this account he has sent to us in Babylon, saying, It is long"** (v28) — Hebrew כִּי עַל־כֵּן "for on this account"; אֲרֻכָּה הִיא "it is long", feminine, with no noun written ("captivity" is supplied by KJV; the exile or the time is meant).
- **"read this letter in the ears of Jeremiah the prophet"** (v29) — Hebrew בְּאָזְנֵי "in the ears of", kept literal.
- **"Then came the word of the LORD unto Jeremiah, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah, saying"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְּבַר־יְהוָה אֶל־יִרְמְיָהוּ לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula with the name for "me".
- **"Because that Shemaiah hath prophesied unto you, and I sent him not, and he caused you to trust in a lie" → "Because Shemaiah has prophesied to you, and I did not send him, and he has made you trust in falsehood"** (v31) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי לֹא שְׁלַחְתִּיו "and I, I did not send him"; וַיַּבְטַח … עַל־שָׁקֶר "made you trust on falsehood". עַל־כָּל־הַגּוֹלָה "to all the exiles" (literally "upon").
- **"I will punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite, and his seed: he shall not have a man to dwell among this people … because he hath taught rebellion against the LORD" → "I am visiting upon Shemaiah the Nehelamite and upon his seed; he shall not have a man dwelling in the midst of this people … for he has spoken defection against Yahweh"** (v32) — Hebrew הִנְנִי פֹקֵד עַל "I am visiting upon" (the פָּקַד row: for punishment); זֶרַע "seed" (its row); יוֹשֵׁב "dwelling"; בְתוֹךְ "in the midst of"; עֹשֶׂה participle "am doing"; סָרָה דִבֶּר עַל־יְהוָה "spoken defection against Yahweh" (the סָרָה row, as Deut 13:5; Jer 28:16).

### Notes
- **v1–23** — the letter to the exiles of 597 BC (Jeconiah's exile, 2 Kgs 24:12–16).
- **v10** — the seventy years: Jer 25:11–12; Dan 9:2; 2 Chr 36:21.
- **v24–32** — the answer to Shemaiah's letters; his own letter (v26–28) is quoted inside Yahweh's word.
