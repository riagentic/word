# Jeremiah 39

[1] In the ninth year of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the tenth month, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came, and all his force, to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they besieged it.

[2] In the eleventh year of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧, in the fourth month, on the ninth of the month, the city was split open.

[3] And all the princes of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came and sat in the middle gate: ⟦nergal-sharezer|Nergal-Sar-Etzer|Nergal-sar-ezer⟧, ⟦samgar-nebo|Samgar-Nevu|Samgar-nebo⟧, ⟦sarsechim|Sar-Sechim|Sarsechim⟧ ⟦rab-saris|Rav-Saris|Rab-saris⟧, ⟦nergal-sharezer|Nergal-Sar-Etzer|Nergal-sar-ezer⟧ ⟦rab-mag|Rav-Mag|Rab-mag⟧, and all the remnant of the princes of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[4] And it came to pass, when ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and all the men of war saw them, that they fled and went out by night from the city by the way of the garden of the king, by the gate between the two walls; and he went out by the way of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧.

[5] And the force of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ pursued after them and overtook ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ in the plains of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧; and they took him and brought him up to ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ in the land of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧, and he spoke judgments with him.

[6] And the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ slaughtered the sons of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ in ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ before his eyes; and all the nobles of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ slaughtered.

[7] And the eyes of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ he blinded, and he bound him in bronze fetters, to bring him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[8] And the house of the king and the house of the people the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ burned with fire, and the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ they broke down.

[9] And the rest of the people who were left in the city, and the deserters who had fallen away to him, and the rest of the people who were left, ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards carried into exile to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[10] And of the people, the poor who had not anything, ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards left in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he gave them vineyards and fields in that day.

[11] And ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ commanded concerning ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ by the hand of ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards, saying,

[12] "Take him, and set your eyes on him, and do not do to him anything evil; but as he speaks to you, so do with him."

[13] And ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards sent, and ⟦nebushazban|Nevushazban|Nebushazban⟧ ⟦rab-saris|Rav-Saris|Rab-saris⟧, and ⟦nergal-sharezer|Nergal-Sar-Etzer|Nergal-sar-ezer⟧ ⟦rab-mag|Rav-Mag|Rab-mag⟧, and all the chiefs of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧;

[14] and they sent and took ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from the court of the guard, and gave him to ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ son of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧ son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧, to bring him out to the house; and he dwelt in the midst of the people.

[15] And to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came, while he was shut in, in the court of the guard, saying,

[16] "Go, and say to ⟦ebed-melech|Eved-Melech|Ebed-melech⟧ the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧, saying, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I am bringing my words upon this city for evil and not for good, and they shall be before you in that day.

[17] And I will deliver you in that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and you shall not be given into the hand of the men of whom you are afraid.

[18] For I will surely rescue you, and by the sword you shall not fall; and your soul shall be yours as spoil, because you have trusted in me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v16) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת (plain "of Hosts", as the prophets' formula row). **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v16). נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v17, 18), literally "the declaration of Yahweh".
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose. **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Jer 39:1–10 and 2 Kgs 25:1–12** — the same events, told in other Hebrew. The wording of 2 Kgs 25 is kept where the Hebrew is the same ("the city was split open", "the men of war", "between the two walls", "the plains of Jericho", "slaughtered", "he blinded", "bronze fetters", "the deserters who had fallen away", "the chief of the guards", "carried into exile"); every difference is translated as Jeremiah has it and listed below; nothing is levelled.
- **"In the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, came Nebuchadrezzar … and all his army against Jerusalem, and they besieged it" → "… came, and all his force, to Jerusalem, and they besieged it"** (v1) — Hebrew בָּא … וְכָל־חֵילוֹ אֶל־יְרוּשָׁלִַם וַיָּצֻרוּ עָלֶיהָ. Jeremiah has "of Zedekiah king of Judah" (Kings "of his reign"), no day of the month (Kings "on the tenth of the month"), אֶל "to" (Kings עַל "against"), and "they besieged it" (צוּר עַל) where Kings has "he camped against it, and they built against it a siege wall all around". חַיִל "force", as Kings. The name is written נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר, with r (Kings נְבֻכַדְנֶאצַּר). 39:1–2 stand between 38:28b ("And it came to pass, when Jerusalem was captured") and 39:3, which completes it.
- **"and in the fourth month, the ninth day of the month, the city was broken up" → "in the fourth month, on the ninth of the month, the city was split open"** (v2) — Hebrew בַּחֹדֶשׁ הָרְבִיעִי בְּתִשְׁעָה לַחֹדֶשׁ: Jeremiah names "the fourth month" (Kings 25:3 has only "on the ninth of the month", of the famine); "day" is not written. הָבְקְעָה, the hofal of בָּקַע (Kings וַתִּבָּקַע, nifal): the same sense, "was split open". The siege and the famine of 2 Kgs 25:2–3 are not in Jeremiah.
- **"and sat in the middle gate, even Nergal-sharezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsechim, Rab-saris, Nergal-sharezer, Rab-mag" → "and sat in the middle gate: Nergal-sharezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsechim Rab-saris, Nergal-sharezer Rab-mag"** (v3; only in Jeremiah) — Hebrew יָשַׁב of taking a seat, "sit"; שַׁעַר הַתָּוֶךְ "the gate of the middle". The names and titles stand as printed, with their tokens: נֵרְגַל שַׂר־אֶצֶר (Babylonian Nergal-šarri-uṣur, "Nergal, protect the king", from memory) twice, the second with the title רַב־מָג; סַמְגַּר־נְבוּ and שַׂר־סְכִים (the division is uncertain: some read "Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebu-sarsechim", from memory); רַב־סָרִיס "Rab-saris", literally "chief eunuch / chief officer" (as 2 Kgs 18:17); רַב־מָג "Rab-mag", a Babylonian title whose sense is uncertain (the word מָג is not Hebrew; "chief of the magi" is a guess). The titles have no article, as names (as 2 Kgs 18). **"with all the residue of the princes" → "and all the remnant of the princes"** — שְׁאֵרִית "remnant"; שָׂרֵי "princes" (the שַׂר row).
- **"when Zedekiah … saw them, and all the men of war, then they fled, and went forth out of the city by night, by the way of the king's garden, by the gate betwixt the two walls: and he went out the way of the plain" → "And it came to pass, when Zedekiah king of Judah and all the men of war saw them, that they fled and went out by night from the city by the way of the garden of the king, by the gate between the two walls; and he went out by the way of the Arabah"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְהִי כַּאֲשֶׁר רָאָם, "saw them": the princes of v3 (not in Kings); וַיִּבְרְחוּ וַיֵּצְאוּ "fled and went out" (Kings has no verb for the men of war); לַיְלָה "by night" (Kings הַלַּיְלָה); דֶּרֶךְ גַּן הַמֶּלֶךְ "by the way of the garden of the king" (Kings: "the gate … which was by the garden of the king"); בְּשַׁעַר "by the gate"; וַיֵּצֵא "and he went out", singular (Kings וַיֵּלֶךְ "and he went"). The clause "and the Chaldeans were against the city all around" of Kings is not here.
- **"But the Chaldeans' army pursued after them, and overtook Zedekiah … and when they had taken him, they brought him up … and he gave judgment upon him" → "And the force of the Chaldeans pursued after them and overtook Zedekiah … and they took him and brought him up to Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon at Riblah in the land of Hamath, and he spoke judgments with him"** (v5) — Hebrew אַחֲרֵיהֶם "after them" (Kings "after the king"); "Zedekiah" named (Kings "him"); the scattering of his force (Kings 25:5) is not here; וַיִּקְחוּ "and they took" (לָקַח; Kings תָּפַשׂ "seized"); וַיַּעֲלֻהוּ "brought him up"; the king of Babylon is named, and בְּאֶרֶץ חֲמָת "in the land of Hamath" is added; רִבְלָתָה "to Riblah". וַיְדַבֵּר אִתּוֹ מִשְׁפָּטִים, a singular verb and a plural noun, "he spoke judgments with him" (= passed sentence; as Jer 1:16; Kings: plural verb, singular noun, "they spoke judgment with him").
- **"Then the king of Babylon slew the sons of Zedekiah in Riblah before his eyes: also the king of Babylon slew all the nobles of Judah" → "And the king of Babylon slaughtered the sons of Zedekiah in Riblah before his eyes; and all the nobles of Judah the king of Babylon slaughtered"** (v6) — Hebrew שָׁחַט "slaughter", twice, singular, with "the king of Babylon" named twice (Kings: plural "they slaughtered", once); "in Riblah" and the nobles (חֹרֵי יְהוּדָה, the חֹרִים "nobles" row) are not in Kings.
- **"Moreover he put out Zedekiah's eyes, and bound him with chains, to carry him to Babylon" → "And the eyes of Zedekiah he blinded, and he bound him in bronze fetters, to bring him to Babylon"** (v7) — Hebrew עִוֵּר "blinded"; נְחֻשְׁתַּיִם, a dual, "bronze fetters"; לָבִיא אֹתוֹ, an infinitive, "to bring him" (Kings וַיְבִאֵהוּ "and brought him").
- **"And the Chaldeans burned the king's house, and the houses of the people, with fire, and brake down the walls of Jerusalem" → "And the house of the king and the house of the people the Chaldeans burned with fire, and the walls of Jerusalem they broke down"** (v8) — Hebrew בֵּית הָעָם "the house of the people", singular as printed (a collective for the people's houses, or a public building: uncertain); the house of Yahweh is not named here (Kings 25:9 "the house of Yahweh"); the Chaldeans are the subject of both verbs (Kings: Nebuzaradan burns, the force breaks down); נָתַץ "break down". The Hebrew order is kept: the objects first.
- **"Then Nebuzar-adan the captain of the guard carried away captive into Babylon the remnant of the people that remained in the city, and those that fell away, that fell to him, with the rest of the people that remained" → "And the rest of the people who were left in the city, and the deserters who had fallen away to him, and the rest of the people who were left, Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards carried into exile to Babylon"** (v9) — Hebrew יֶתֶר הָעָם הַנִּשְׁאָרִים twice (Kings: "the rest of the multitude", הֶהָמוֹן, the second time); עָלָיו "to him" (Kings "to the king of Babylon"); בָּבֶל "to Babylon" (not in Kings 25:11); רַב־טַבָּחִים "the chief of the guards" (literally "chief of the slaughterers"; as Kings).
- **"But Nebuzar-adan … left of the poor of the people, which had nothing, in the land of Judah, and gave them vineyards and fields at the same time" → "And of the people, the poor who had not anything, Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards left in the land of Judah, and he gave them vineyards and fields in that day"** (v10) — Hebrew וּמִן־הָעָם הַדַּלִּים אֲשֶׁר אֵין־לָהֶם מְאוּמָה (Kings מִדַּלַּת הָאָרֶץ "of the poor of the land"); "Nebuzaradan" and "in the land of Judah" named (not in Kings); וַיִּתֵּן לָהֶם כְּרָמִים וִיגֵבִים "and he gave them vineyards and fields" (Kings: "as vinedressers and as field workers"); יְגֵבִים is found only here, of the root of Kings' יֹגְבִים: "fields" is uncertain (perhaps "plowed lands"). בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" (the prophets' formula row).
- **"Now Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon gave charge concerning Jeremiah to Nebuzar-adan" → "And Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon commanded concerning Jeremiah by the hand of Nebuzaradan"** (v11) — Hebrew צִוָּה עַל "command concerning"; בְּיַד "by the hand of".
- **"Take him, and look well to him, and do him no harm; but do unto him even as he shall say unto thee" → "Take him, and set your eyes on him, and do not do to him anything evil; but as he speaks to you, so do with him"** (v12) — Hebrew וְעֵינֶיךָ שִׂים עָלָיו, literally "and your eyes set on him" (as 40:4; Gen 44:21); מְאוּמָה רָע "anything evil" (רַע "evil"); כִּי אִם "but": the אם is written and not read (no qere is given); עָשָׂה עִם "do with".
- **"So Nebuzar-adan the captain of the guard sent, and Nebushasban, Rab-saris, and Nergal-sharezer, Rab-mag, and all the king of Babylon's princes" → "And Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards sent, and Nebushazban Rab-saris, and Nergal-sharezer Rab-mag, and all the chiefs of the king of Babylon"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁלַח "and he sent", singular, before a list of subjects; the titles as v3; רַבֵּי "the chiefs" (רַב "chief, great one", the word of the titles; not שָׂרִים "princes" of v3). The sentence runs on into v14.
- **"Even they sent, and took Jeremiah out of the court of the prison, and committed him unto Gedaliah … that he should carry him home: so he dwelt among the people" → "and they sent and took Jeremiah from the court of the guard, and gave him to Gedaliah … to bring him out to the house; and he dwelt in the midst of the people"** (v14) — Hebrew נָתַן אֶל "give to"; לְהוֹצִאֵהוּ אֶל־הַבָּיִת "to bring him out to the house" ("home" is one reading; which house is not said: Gedaliah's, or Jeremiah's own); בְּתוֹךְ "in the midst of"; יָשַׁב "dwell". 40:1–6 tells Jeremiah's release otherwise (taken in chains to Ramah): the two accounts stand as printed.
- **"Now the word of the LORD came unto Jeremiah, while he was shut up in the court of the prison, saying" → "And to Jeremiah the word of Yahweh came, while he was shut in, in the court of the guard, saying"** (v15) — Hebrew וְאֶל־יִרְמְיָהוּ הָיָה, "to Jeremiah" first; עָצוּר "shut in" (עָצַר "restrain"); this word was given before the fall (38:7–13).
- **"Go and speak to Ebed-melech the Ethiopian … Behold, I will bring my words upon this city for evil, and not for good; and they shall be accomplished in that day before thee" → "Go, and say to Ebed-melech the Cushite, saying, Thus says … Behold, I am bringing my words upon this city for evil and not for good, and they shall be before you in that day"** (v16) — Hebrew הָלוֹךְ, an infinitive, "Go"; וְאָמַרְתָּ … לֵאמֹר "and say … saying" (kept); the reading (qere) מֵבִיא "bringing", a participle (the written text מבי, the same word written short); אֶל "upon"; רָעָה "evil", set against טוֹבָה "good"; וְהָיוּ לְפָנֶיךָ "and they shall be before you" ("accomplished" is not written).
- **"But I will deliver thee … and thou shalt not be given into the hand of the men of whom thou art afraid" → "And I will deliver you … the men of whom you are afraid"** (v17) — Hebrew הִצִּיל "deliver"; יָגוֹר מִפְּנֵיהֶם, literally "you are afraid from their face" (גּוּר "be afraid", not יָרֵא "fear").
- **"For I will surely deliver thee, and thou shalt not fall by the sword, but thy life shall be for a prey unto thee: because thou hast put thy trust in me" → "For I will surely rescue you, and by the sword you shall not fall; and your soul shall be yours as spoil, because you have trusted in me"** (v18) — Hebrew מַלֵּט אֲמַלֶּטְךָ, literally "rescuing I will rescue you" (מִלֵּט "rescue", beside הִצִּיל "deliver" in v17); נַפְשְׁךָ "your soul" (as 21:9; 38:2); בָּטַח בְּ "trust in".

### Notes

- **586 BC** — the city falls in the eleventh year of Zedekiah (v2), after a siege begun in his ninth (v1).
