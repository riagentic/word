# Jeremiah 27

[1] In the beginning of the reign of ⟦jehoiakim-zedekiah|Yehoyaqim|Zedekiah⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, this word came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying:

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to me: "Make yourself bonds and yoke-bars, and put them on your neck.

[3] And send them to the king of Edom, and to the king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and to the king of the sons of Ammon, and to the king of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, and to the king of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, by the hand of the messengers who are coming to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[4] And command them to their lords, saying, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Thus you shall say to your lords:

[5] I, I have made the earth, man and beast that are on the face of the earth, with my great power and with my outstretched arm, and I give it to whom it is right in my eyes.

[6] And now I, I have given all these lands into the hand of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, my servant; and also the living things of the field I have given to him to serve him.

[7] And all the nations shall serve him and his son and his son's son, until the time of his own land comes, it also; and many nations and great kings shall make him serve.

[8] And it shall be: the nation and the kingdom that will not serve him, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and that will not put its neck into the yoke of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, with the sword and with the famine and with the pestilence I will visit upon that nation, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, until I have brought them to an end by his hand.

[9] And you, do not listen to your prophets, and to your diviners, and to your dreams, and to your soothsayers, and to your sorcerers, who are saying to you, saying, You shall not serve the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[10] For falsehood they are prophesying to you, in order to remove you far from upon your ground, and I will drive you out, and you shall perish.

[11] And the nation that will bring its neck into the yoke of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and serve him, I will leave it on its ground, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and it shall work it and dwell in it.'"

[12] And to ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ I spoke according to all these words, saying, "Bring your necks into the yoke of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and serve him and his people, and live.

[13] Why will you die, you and your people, by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken to the nation that will not serve the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧?

[14] And do not listen to the words of the prophets who are saying to you, saying, 'You shall not serve the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,' for falsehood they are prophesying to you.

[15] For I have not sent them, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they are prophesying in my name falsely, in order that I may drive you out and you perish, you and the prophets who are prophesying to you."

[16] And to the priests and to all this people I spoke, saying, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Do not listen to the words of your prophets who are prophesying to you, saying, 'Behold, the utensils of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are being brought back from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ now, quickly,' for falsehood they are prophesying to you.

[17] Do not listen to them; serve the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and live. Why should this city become a ruin?

[18] And if they are prophets, and if the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is with them, let them entreat, please, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, that the utensils that remain in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and in the house of the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ may not come to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[19] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts concerning the pillars, and concerning the sea, and concerning the bases, and concerning the rest of the utensils that remain in this city,

[20] which ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ did not take when he carried into exile ⟦jeconiah|Yechonyah|Jeconiah⟧ son of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and all the nobles of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[21] for thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, concerning the utensils that remain in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and in the house of the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧:

[22] To ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ they shall be brought, and there they shall be until the day of my visiting them, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and I will bring them up and bring them back to this place."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4, 18, 19, 21). **"God" → "Elohim"** (v4, 21).
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose. The words for the kings (v4b–11) are one quotation inside Yahweh's speech (v2–11).
- **"In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah"** (v1) — the MT reads יְהוֹיָקִם "Jehoiakim", as printed (token `jehoiakim-zedekiah`: original form "Yehoyaqim"). The chapter itself names Zedekiah (v3, 12) and 28:1 dates "in that year, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah"; a few Hebrew manuscripts and the Syriac read "Zedekiah" (from memory), and the Septuagint lacks the verse (from memory). The traditional form of the token gives "Zedekiah"; the MT is kept. מַמְלֶכֶת "reign" (lit. "kingdom"); אֶל־יִרְמְיָה, the short form of the name; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept.
- **"Thus saith the LORD to me; Make thee bonds and yokes, and put them upon thy neck" → "Thus says Yahweh to me: Make yourself bonds and yoke-bars, and put them on your neck"** (v2) — Hebrew כֹּה־אָמַר יְהוָה אֵלַי (as 13:1); עֲשֵׂה לְךָ "make yourself" (the English reflexive carries לְךָ); מוֹסֵרוֹת "bonds" (the straps that tie the yoke on); מֹטוֹת "yoke-bars" (מוֹטָה, the wooden bar of a yoke; plural here; also 28:10, 12, 13); עֹל "yoke" (v8, 11, 12) is another word. וּנְתַתָּם "and put them".
- **"And send them to the king of Edom" → "And send them to the king of Edom"** (v3) — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּחְתָּם "and send them" (the bonds and bars: the sign is sent; the current "send word" was an explanation); each "and to the king of" kept. **"by the hand of the messengers which come to Jerusalem" → "by the hand of the messengers who are coming to Jerusalem"** — מַלְאָכִים "messengers"; הַבָּאִים participle.
- **"And command them to say unto their masters" → "And command them to their lords, saying"** (v4) — Hebrew וְצִוִּיתָ אֹתָם אֶל־אֲדֹנֵיהֶם לֵאמֹר; אָדוֹן "lord" (also "to your lords"). **"Thus shall ye say" → "Thus you shall say"** — כֹּה תֹאמְרוּ.
- **"I have made the earth, the man and the beast that are upon the ground, by my great power and by my outstretched arm" → "I, I have made the earth, man and beast that are on the face of the earth, with my great power and with my outstretched arm"** (v5) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי עָשִׂיתִי, the pronoun stressed; הָאָדָם … הַבְּהֵמָה "man … beast" (collectives); עַל־פְּנֵי הָאָרֶץ "on the face of the earth" (אֶרֶץ, not אֲדָמָה); בְּ "with" before both, as the "with a strong hand" ruling. **"and have given it unto whom it seemed meet unto me" → "and I give it to whom it is right in my eyes"** — וּנְתַתִּיהָ, a weqatal: what he does (or "and I will give it"); יָשַׁר בְּעֵינָי "it is right in my eyes".
- **"And now have I given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar" → "And now I, I have given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar"** (v6) — אָנֹכִי stressed again; עַבְדִּי "my servant" (as 25:9). **"and the beasts of the field have I given him also to serve him" → "and also the living things of the field I have given to him to serve him"** — חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה "the living things of the field" (חַיָּה, not "beasts": that is בְּהֵמָה of v5); the Hebrew order.
- **"and his son, and his son's son, until the very time of his land come" → "and his son and his son's son, until the time of his own land comes, it also"** (v7) — Hebrew עַד בֹּא־עֵת אַרְצוֹ גַּם־הוּא, literally "until the time of his land comes, it too" (גַּם־הוּא: the land, or "he too"). **"and then many nations and great kings shall serve themselves of him" → "and many nations and great kings shall make him serve"** — וְעָבְדוּ בוֹ, עָבַד בְּ "make serve" (as 25:14). The Septuagint lacks the verse (from memory).
- **"And it shall come to pass, that the nation and kingdom which will not serve the same Nebuchadnezzar … and that will not put their neck under the yoke … that nation will I punish" → "And it shall be: the nation and the kingdom that will not serve him, Nebuchadnezzar … and that will not put its neck into the yoke … I will visit upon that nation"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהָיָה; לֹא־יַעַבְדוּ אֹתוֹ אֶת־נְבוּכַדְנֶאצַּר "will not serve him, Nebuchadnezzar" (the object said twice); נָתַן צַוָּאר בְּעֹל "give the neck into the yoke"; פָּקַד עַל "visit upon" (not "punish"). **"with the sword, and with the famine, and with the pestilence"** — each with בְּ, placed first. **"until I have consumed them by his hand" → "until I have brought them to an end by his hand"** — עַד־תֻּמִּי אֹתָם, of תָּמַם "come to an end" (not אָכַל "consume").
- **"Therefore hearken not ye to your prophets, nor to your diviners, nor to your dreamers, nor to your enchanters, nor to your sorcerers" → "And you, do not listen to your prophets, and to your diviners, and to your dreams, and to your soothsayers, and to your sorcerers"** (v9) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם "and you"; each וְאֶל "and to"; קֹסְמִים "diviners", עֹנְנִים "soothsayers", כַּשָּׁפִים "sorcerers" (the words of Deut 18:10); חֲלֹמֹתֵיכֶם "your dreams" as the MT has it (the current "dreamers" read חֹלְמֵיכֶם, as the versions, from memory). **"which speak unto you, saying" → "who are saying to you, saying"** — אֹמְרִים … לֵאמֹר, both kept (also v14).
- **"For they prophesy a lie unto you, to remove you far from your land" → "For falsehood they are prophesying to you, in order to remove you far from upon your ground"** (v10) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" placed first (also v14, 16); לְמַעַן הַרְחִיק "in order to remove far": what comes of their words is spoken of as their purpose (also v15); מֵעַל אַדְמַתְכֶם "from upon your ground". **"and that I should drive you out, and ye should perish" → "and I will drive you out, and you shall perish"** — וְהִדַּחְתִּי … וַאֲבַדְתֶּם, weqatal.
- **"But the nations that bring their neck under the yoke … those will I let remain still in their own land" → "And the nation that will bring its neck into the yoke … I will leave it on its ground"** (v11) — Hebrew הַגּוֹי singular; יָבִיא … בְּעֹל "bring into the yoke"; וְהִנַּחְתִּיו "I will leave it (to rest)", hifil of נוּחַ. **"and they shall till it, and dwell therein" → "and it shall work it and dwell in it"** — עָבַד of the ground "work" (the same verb as "serve" before it); יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"I spake also to Zedekiah king of Judah according to all these words" → "And to Zedekiah king of Judah I spoke according to all these words, saying"** (v12) — the Hebrew order; כְּכָל "according to all"; לֵאמֹר kept. **"and live" → "and live"** — וִחְיוּ.
- **"Why will ye die, thou and thy people" → "Why will you die, you and your people"** (v13) — תָמוּתוּ plural, אַתָּה וְעַמֶּךָ singular: the king and his people. **"as the LORD hath spoken against the nation" → "as Yahweh has spoken to the nation"** — אֶל "to".
- **"Therefore hearken not" → "And do not listen"** (v14) — וְאַל.
- **"yet they prophesy a lie in my name; that I might drive you out" → "and they are prophesying in my name falsely, in order that I may drive you out"** (v15) — Hebrew לַשָּׁקֶר, literally "for falsehood"; לְמַעַן הַדִּיחִי "in order that I drive you out" (as v10). **"ye, and the prophets that prophesy unto you" → "you and the prophets who are prophesying to you"** — participles.
- **"Also I spake to the priests and to all this people" → "And to the priests and to all this people I spoke"** (v16) — the Hebrew order. **"Behold, the vessels of the LORD's house shall now shortly be brought again from Babylon" → "Behold, the utensils of the house of Yahweh are being brought back from Babylon now, quickly"** — כְּלֵי בֵית־יְהוָה "the utensils of the house of Yahweh" (כְּלִי of the house: "utensils"; also v18–21); מוּשָׁבִים, hofal participle "are being brought back"; עַתָּה מְהֵרָה "now, quickly".
- **"wherefore should this city be laid waste?" → "Why should this city become a ruin?"** (v17) — לָמָּה תִהְיֶה … חָרְבָּה, the noun "ruin" (25:9, 11).
- **"let them now make intercession to the LORD of hosts" → "let them entreat, please, Yahweh of Hosts"** (v18) — Hebrew יִפְגְּעוּ־נָא בַּיהוָה צְבָאוֹת: פָּגַע בְּ "entreat" (as 7:16), נָא "please". **"that the vessels which are left … go not to Babylon" → "that the utensils that remain … may not come to Babylon"** — לְבִלְתִּי־בֹאוּ "that they not come"; הַנּוֹתָרִים "that remain" (נוֹתַר; also v19, 21).
- **"concerning the pillars, and concerning the sea, and concerning the bases, and concerning the residue of the vessels" → "concerning the pillars, and concerning the sea, and concerning the bases, and concerning the rest of the utensils"** (v19) — Hebrew אֶל … וְעַל … וְעַל … וְעַל, "concerning" each time; the bronze pillars, the sea and the bases of 1 Kgs 7:15–39; 2 Kgs 25:13; יֶתֶר "rest".
- **"which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took not, when he carried away captive Jeconiah … and all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem" → "which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon did not take when he carried into exile Jeconiah … from Jerusalem to Babylon, and all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem"** (v20) — Hebrew בַּגְלוֹתוֹ "in his carrying into exile"; the Hebrew order; חֹרִים "nobles" (lit. "free men"). The sentence that began in v19 is taken up again in v21 with a second "for thus says".
- **"Yea, thus saith the LORD of hosts" → "for thus says Yahweh of Hosts"** (v21) — כִּי כֹה אָמַר, "for", as v19. **"in the house of the LORD" → "in the house of Yahweh"** — בֵּית without "in", the "in" of v18 carried over.
- **"They shall be carried to Babylon, and there shall they be until the day that I visit them" → "To Babylon they shall be brought, and there they shall be until the day of my visiting them"** (v22) — the Hebrew order; יוּבָאוּ "they shall be brought"; יוֹם פָּקְדִי אֹתָם "the day of my visiting them" (פָּקַד "visit"). **"then will I bring them up, and restore them to this place" → "and I will bring them up and bring them back to this place"** — הֶעֱלָה "bring up", הֵשִׁיב "bring back". The Septuagint has a shorter text of v19–22 (from memory); the MT is kept.

### Notes
- **27–28 the yoke** — מוֹטָה "yoke-bar" (the bar worn by Jeremiah, 27:2; 28:10, 12, 13) and עֹל "yoke" (27:8, 11, 12; 28:2, 4, 11, 14) are two words: the sign is the bar, what it stands for is the yoke of the king of Babylon.
- **v20** — Jeconiah's exile in 597 BC: 2 Kgs 24:12–16.
