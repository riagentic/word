# Jeremiah 14

[1] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ concerning the matters of the droughts:

[2] "⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has mourned,
and her gates have languished;
they are darkened to the ground,
and the outcry of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ has gone up.

[3] And their nobles have sent their young ones for water;
they have come to the pits;
they have not found water;
they have returned, their vessels empty;
they are ashamed and put to shame,
and have covered their head.

[4] Because of the ground, which is dismayed,
for there has been no rain in the land,
the farmers are ashamed;
they have covered their head.

[5] For even the doe in the field
has given birth and forsaken,
for there has been no vegetation.

[6] And wild donkeys have stood on the bare heights;
they have panted after wind like jackals;
their eyes have failed,
for there is no plant.

[7] Though our iniquities answer against us,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, act on account of your name;
for our turnings away have been many;
against you we have sinned.

[8] Hope of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
his savior in a time of distress,
why should you be like a sojourner in the land,
and like a wayfarer who turns in to lodge?

[9] Why should you be like a man dumbfounded,
like a mighty one who cannot save?
And you are in our midst, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and your name is called upon us;
do not leave us."

[10] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to this people:

"So they have loved to roam;
their feet they have not withheld;
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is not pleased with them;
now he will remember their iniquity
and visit their sins."

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Do not pray on behalf of this people for good.

[12] When they fast, I am not listening to their cry; and when they offer up burnt offering and grain offering, I am not pleased with them; for by the sword and by famine and by pestilence I am making an end of them."

[13] And I said, "Alas, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! Behold, the prophets are saying to them, 'You shall not see the sword, and famine shall not be yours, for ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ of truth I will give you in this place.'"

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Falsehood the prophets are prophesying in my name. I did not send them, and I did not command them, and I did not speak to them. A vision of falsehood, and divination, and worthlessness, and the deceit of their heart, they are prophesying to you.

[15] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the prophets who prophesy in my name, and I did not send them, and they are saying, 'Sword and famine shall not be in this land': By the sword and by famine those prophets shall come to an end.

[16] And the people to whom they are prophesying shall be thrown out in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ because of the famine and the sword, and there shall be none to bury them — them, their wives, and their sons, and their daughters; and I will pour out upon them their evil.

[17] "And you shall say to them this word:

'Let my eyes run down with tears night and day,
and let them not cease,
for with a great breaking the virgin daughter of my people has been broken,
with a blow very sore.

[18] If I go out into the field,
then behold, the slain of the sword!
And if I come into the city,
then behold, the sicknesses of famine!
For both prophet and priest have gone about to a land,
and they have not known.'"

[19] Have you surely rejected ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?
Or has your soul abhorred ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧?
Why have you struck us,
and there is no healing for us?
Waiting for ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and there is no good;
and for a time of healing, and behold, terror!

[20] We know, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, our wickedness,
the iniquity of our fathers,
for we have sinned against you.

[21] Do not spurn, on account of your name;
do not treat with contempt the throne of your glory;
remember, do not break your covenant with us.

[22] Are there among the vapors of the nations those who cause rain?
Or do the heavens give showers?
Are you not he, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?
And we wait for you,
for you have made all these.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v22) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — vv2–9 the lament over the drought, v10 the answer, vv17–22 the lament and plea set as verse; the prose of vv1, 11–16 stays prose.
- **Vocative** (vv7, 9, 20, 22) — no "O" before the name: the Hebrew has none.
- **"concerning the dearth" → "concerning the matters of the droughts"** (v1) — Hebrew עַל־דִּבְרֵי הַבַּצָּרוֹת, "the matters (words) of the droughts", plural. The heading begins אֲשֶׁר הָיָה דְבַר־יְהוָה, literally "That which came, the word of Yahweh": given in the usual English order.
- **"Judah mourneth, and the gates thereof languish; they are black unto the ground" → "Judah has mourned, and her gates have languished; they are darkened to the ground"** (v2) — Hebrew perfects kept as perfects (also vv3–6); אֻמְלְלוּ "languished" (row); קָדְרוּ לָאָרֶץ "they are darkened (in mourning) to the ground" (קֹדֵר row; no subject is named: "her people" is not in the Hebrew; the gates may be meant); צְוָחָה "outcry".
- **"their nobles have sent their little ones to the waters" → "their nobles have sent their young ones for water"** (v3) — Hebrew אַדִּירִים "nobles" (row); qere צְעִירֵיהֶם "their young ones" (the ketiv צעוריהם is another spelling of the same word); גֵּבִים "pits" (row); רֵיקָם "empty"; בֹּשׁוּ וְהָכְלְמוּ "they are ashamed and put to shame" (בּוֹשׁ · נִכְלַם, row); חָפוּ רֹאשָׁם "have covered their head" (singular, as printed; also v4).
- **"Because the ground is chapt" → "Because of the ground, which is dismayed"** (v4) — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר הָאֲדָמָה חַתָּה: בַּעֲבוּר here gives a cause, "because of" (the "for the sake of" row would say a purpose); חַתָּה "is dismayed" (חָתַת, as the "dismayed" of persons; "cracked" is the sense many give the ground: in Changes only); גֶּשֶׁם "rain" (row); אִכָּרִים "farmers".
- **"the hind also calved in the field, and forsook it, because there was no grass" → "even the doe in the field has given birth and forsaken, for there has been no vegetation"** (v5) — Hebrew יָלְדָה וְעָזוֹב, a perfect and an infinitive absolute: "has given birth and forsaken"; no object is written ("her young" is understood); דֶּשֶׁא "vegetation" (row).
- **"the wild asses did stand in the high places, they snuffed up the wind like dragons" → "wild donkeys have stood on the bare heights; they have panted after wind like jackals"** (v6) — Hebrew פְּרָאִים "wild donkeys" (row); שְׁפָיִם "bare heights"; שָׁאַף "pant after" (row); רוּחַ "wind" (or "breath": one word); תַּנִּים "jackals" (row; not "dragons"); כָּלוּ "have failed" (literally "come to an end"); עֵשֶׂב "plant" (row).
- **"though our iniquities testify against us" → "Though our iniquities answer against us"** (v7) — Hebrew עָנוּ בָנוּ "answer against us" (= testify against, row). **"for thy name's sake" → "on account of your name"** — לְמַעַן before a noun (row; also v21). **"backslidings" → "turnings away"** — מְשׁוּבֹת, a noun of שׁוּב "turn back" (new row). **"we have sinned against thee" → "against you we have sinned"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"O the hope of Israel, the saviour thereof in time of trouble" → "Hope of Israel, his savior in a time of distress"** (v8) — Hebrew מִקְוֵה "hope" (row); מוֹשִׁיעוֹ "his savior"; צָרָה "distress" (row). **"stranger" → "sojourner"** — גֵּר (row). **"a wayfaring man that turneth aside to tarry for a night" → "a wayfarer who turns in to lodge"** — אֹרֵחַ "wayfarer" (row); נָטָה "turn (in)" (row); לוּן "lodge" (row).
- **"a man astonied" → "a man dumbfounded"** (v9) — Hebrew נִדְהָם, only here: the sense "be astounded, at a loss" is taken from Arabic and is uncertain. **"a mighty man" → "a mighty one"** — גִּבּוֹר (row). **"Yet thou … art in the midst of us" → "And you are in our midst"** — וְאַתָּה. **"we are called by thy name" → "your name is called upon us"** — וְשִׁמְךָ עָלֵינוּ נִקְרָא, word for word (they belong to him). **"leave us not" → "do not leave us"** — אַל־תַּנִּחֵנוּ, of הִנִּיחַ "set down, leave" (row).
- **"concerning this people" → "to this people"** (v10) — Hebrew לָעָם הַזֶּה. **"Thus have they loved to wander" → "So they have loved to roam"** — כֵּן "so"; נוּעַ qal of persons "roam" (row). **"they have not refrained their feet" → "their feet they have not withheld"** — the Hebrew order; חָשַׂךְ "withhold" (row). **"the LORD doth not accept them" → "Yahweh is not pleased with them"** — רָצָה "be pleased with" (row; also v12). **"visit their sins"** — פָּקַד "visit" (row).
- **"for their good" → "for good"** (v11) — Hebrew לְטוֹבָה; בְּעַד "on behalf of".
- **"When they fast, I will not hear their cry" → "When they fast, I am not listening to their cry"** (v12) — Hebrew אֵינֶנִּי שֹׁמֵעַ אֶל, a participle: "I am not listening to" (שָׁמַע אֶל, row); רִנָּה "cry" (a ringing cry, here of prayer, as 1 Kgs 8:28). **"when they offer burnt offering and an oblation" → "when they offer up burnt offering and grain offering"** — יַעֲלוּ עֹלָה, literally "make go up a going-up" (row); מִנְחָה "grain offering" (row). **"I will consume them" → "I am making an end of them"** — אָנֹכִי מְכַלֶּה, a participle; כִּלָּה with persons "make an end of" (row). **"and by the famine"** — רָעָב "famine"; דֶּבֶר "pestilence" (row).
- **"Ah, Lord GOD!" → "Alas, Lord Yahweh!"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲהָהּ, as Josh 7:7; Judg 6:22; Jer 1:6. **"neither shall ye have famine; but I will give you assured peace in this place" → "and famine shall not be yours, for shalom of truth I will give you in this place"** — וְרָעָב לֹא־יִהְיֶה לָכֶם; כִּי "for" (or "but": after a negative both are possible); שְׁלוֹם אֱמֶת "shalom of truth" (שָׁלוֹם token, reviewer; אֱמֶת "truth", row).
- **"The prophets prophesy lies in my name" → "Falsehood the prophets are prophesying in my name"** (v14) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (row; also v14b), first for emphasis; נִבְּאִים, nifal participle, and מִתְנַבְּאִים, hitpael (one English for both stems, row). **"neither have I commanded them, neither spake unto them" → "and I did not command them, and I did not speak to them"** — each clause with its וְ. **"a false vision and divination, and a thing of nought, and the deceit of their heart" → "A vision of falsehood, and divination, and worthlessness, and the deceit of their heart"** — חֲזוֹן שֶׁקֶר "a vision of falsehood"; קֶסֶם "divination" (row); qere וֶאֱלִיל "and worthlessness" (row; the ketiv ואלול is another form of the same word); qere וְתַרְמִית "and the deceit" (ketiv ותרמות, another form); לִבָּם "their heart" (not "minds").
- **"yet they say" → "and they are saying"** (v15) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי לֹא־שְׁלַחְתִּים וְהֵמָּה אֹמְרִים, two clauses with "and". **"shall not be in this land"** — לֹא יִהְיֶה בָּאָרֶץ הַזֹּאת. **"shall be consumed" → "shall come to an end"** — יִתַּמּוּ, of תַּם (row).
- **"shall be cast out" → "shall be thrown out"** (v16) — Hebrew מֻשְׁלָכִים, of הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw" (row). **"because of the famine and the sword"** — מִפְּנֵי "because of" (row). **"and they shall have none to bury them, them, their wives" → "and there shall be none to bury them — them, their wives"** — וְאֵין מְקַבֵּר לָהֵמָּה הֵמָּה, the pronoun repeated, as printed. **"for I will pour their wickedness upon them" → "and I will pour out upon them their evil"** — וְשָׁפַכְתִּי "and I will pour out" (שָׁפַךְ, row); רָעָתָם "their evil" (row: the evil they did, which comes back on them).
- **"Let mine eyes run down with tears" → "Let my eyes run down with tears"** (v17) — Hebrew תֵּרַדְנָה עֵינַי דִּמְעָה, literally "let my eyes go down tears"; וְאַל־תִּדְמֶינָה "and let them not cease" (דָּמָה "be still"). **"for the virgin daughter of my people is broken with a great breach, with a very grievous blow" → "for with a great breaking the virgin daughter of my people has been broken, with a blow very sore"** — שֶׁבֶר גָּדוֹל נִשְׁבְּרָה, one root twice: "with a great breaking … has been broken" (שֶׁבֶר "breaking", row); בְּתוּלָה "virgin"; מַכָּה "blow" (row); נַחְלָה מְאֹד "very sore" (literally "made very sick", nifal of חָלָה).
- **"If I go forth into the field, then behold the slain with the sword!"** (v18) — Hebrew perfects after אִם (a condition: "If I go out … if I come"); חַלְלֵי־חֶרֶב "the slain of the sword" (row); תַּחֲלוּאֵי רָעָב "the sicknesses of famine" (row). **"both the prophet and the priest go about into a land that they know not" → "both prophet and priest have gone about to a land, and they have not known"** — סָחֲרוּ אֶל־אֶרֶץ וְלֹא יָדָעוּ, word for word: סָחַר "go about" (as a trader goes from place to place; the noun סַחַר "trade", row); "that they know not" joins the clauses into one; "and they have not known" may also mean they do not understand: kept open.
- **"Hast thou utterly rejected Judah?" → "Have you surely rejected Judah?"** (v19) — Hebrew הֲמָאֹס מָאַסְתָּ, literally "rejecting have you rejected" (infinitive absolute); אִם "or" in the second question; גָּעַל "abhor" (row). **"and there is no healing for us?"** — וְאֵין לָנוּ מַרְפֵּא (מַרְפֵּא "healing", row). **"We looked for peace, and there is no good; and for the time of healing, and behold trouble!" → "Waiting for shalom, and there is no good; and for a time of healing, and behold, terror!"** — קַוֵּה, an infinitive absolute standing alone (no subject; "we looked for" in other versions; as Jer 8:15); קִוָּה "wait for" (row); שָׁלוֹם token (reviewer); בְעָתָה "terror" (noun of בִּעֵת "terrify", row).
- **"We acknowledge, O LORD, our wickedness, and the iniquity of our fathers" → "We know, Yahweh, our wickedness, the iniquity of our fathers"** (v20) — Hebrew יָדַעְנוּ "we know" (יָדַע, row); רֶשַׁע "wickedness" (row); no "and" before "the iniquity": the two stand side by side, as printed.
- **"Do not abhor us, for thy name's sake" → "Do not spurn, on account of your name"** (v21) — Hebrew אַל־תִּנְאַץ "do not spurn" (נִאֵץ, row); no object is written ("us" is not in the Hebrew). **"do not disgrace the throne of thy glory" → "do not treat with contempt the throne of your glory"** — אַל־תְּנַבֵּל; the row has "treat as a fool" for נִבֵּל (Deut 32:15, of a person), which cannot be said of a throne: here "treat with contempt" (to make it a thing of no worth). **"remember, break not thy covenant with us" → "remember, do not break your covenant with us"** — זְכֹר אַל־תָּפֵר, two verbs without "and"; הֵפֵר בְּרִית "break a covenant" (row).
- **"Are there any among the vanities of the Gentiles that can cause rain?" → "Are there among the vapors of the nations those who cause rain?"** (v22) — Hebrew הַבְלֵי הַגּוֹיִם "the vapors of the nations" (הֶבֶל "vapor", row: their gods, things of breath); מַגְשִׁמִים "those who cause rain" (hifil of the root of גֶּשֶׁם); גּוֹיִם "nations" (row). **"or can the heavens give showers?" → "Or do the heavens give showers?"** — יִתְּנוּ, a plain yiqtol; רְבִבִים "showers" (row); שָׁמַיִם "heavens" (row). **"art not thou he" → "Are you not he"** — הֲלֹא אַתָּה־הוּא. **"therefore we will wait upon thee: for thou hast made all these things" → "And we wait for you, for you have made all these"** — וּנְקַוֶּה־לָּךְ "and we wait for you" (קִוָּה, row; also read "let us wait"); עָשִׂיתָ "you have made" (עָשָׂה "make", row); אֵלֶּה "these" (the rain and the showers).

### Notes
- **The drought** — the lament (vv2–9, 19–22) is the people's or the prophet's, the answers (vv10–12, 14–16) are Yahweh's; the Hebrew does not mark the speakers.
