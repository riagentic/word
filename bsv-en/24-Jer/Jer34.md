# Jeremiah 34

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, while ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and all his force and all the kingdoms of the earth under the dominion of his hand and all the peoples were fighting against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and against all its cities, saying,

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Go, and you shall say to ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and you shall say to him, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am giving this city into the hand of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and he shall burn it with fire.

[3] And you, you shall not escape from his hand, for you shall surely be seized, and into his hand you shall be given; and your eyes shall see the eyes of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and his mouth shall speak with your mouth, and to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ you shall go.

[4] Yet hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧: Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning you: You shall not die by the sword.

[5] In ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ you shall die; and as the burnings for your fathers, the former kings who were before you, so they shall burn for you, and "Woe, lord!" they shall lament for you; for a word I myself have spoken, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet spoke to ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ all these words in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[7] and the force of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ was fighting against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and against all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ that remained, against Lachish and against ⟦azekah|Azeqah|Azekah⟧; for these were left among the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, fortified cities.

[8] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, after King ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ had cut a covenant with all the people who were in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to proclaim liberty to them,

[9] that each man should let his slave and each man his maidservant, the ⟦hebrew|Ivri|Hebrew⟧ man and the ⟦hebrew|Ivri|Hebrew⟧ woman, go free, so that no man should make them serve, a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ his brother.

[10] And all the princes and all the people who had come into the covenant heard that each man should let his slave and each man his maidservant go free, so that they should not make them serve any more; and they heard, and they let them go.

[11] And afterward they turned back, and they brought back the slaves and the maidservants whom they had let go free, and they subdued them as slaves and as maidservants.

[12] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying,

[13] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: I myself cut a covenant with your fathers in the day that I brought them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, out of the house of slaves, saying,

[14] At the end of seven years you shall let go each man his brother, the ⟦hebrew|Ivri|Hebrew⟧, who is sold to you; and he shall serve you six years, and you shall let him go free from you. But your fathers did not listen to me, and did not turn their ear.

[15] And you, you turned back today and did what is right in my eyes, to proclaim liberty each man to his neighbor; and you cut a covenant before me in the house over which my name is called.

[16] And you turned back and profaned my name, and you brought back each man his slave and each man his maidservant, whom you had let go free to their soul, and you subdued them to be to you as slaves and as maidservants.

[17] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: You, you have not listened to me, to proclaim liberty each man to his brother and each man to his neighbor. Behold, I am proclaiming liberty to you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to the sword, to the pestilence and to the famine; and I will make you a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

[18] And I will make the men who overstepped my covenant, who did not establish the words of the covenant that they cut before me, the calf that they cut in two and passed between its halves:

[19] the princes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the princes of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the eunuchs and the priests and all the people of the land, who passed between the halves of the calf.

[20] And I will give them into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their soul; and their dead bodies shall be food for the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth.

[21] And ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and his princes I will give into the hand of their enemies and into the hand of those who seek their soul, and into the hand of the force of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ who are going up from upon you.

[22] Behold, I am commanding, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will bring them back to this city, and they shall fight against it and capture it and burn it with fire; and the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ I will make a desolation without inhabitant.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 13). נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v5, 17, 22), literally "the declaration of Yahweh". **Quotation marks** — the Hebrew has none; the prophet's message (v2–5, 13–22) stands without them; only the lament "Woe, lord!" (v5) is marked.
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose.
- **"when Nebuchadnezzar … and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth of his dominion, and all the people, fought against Jerusalem" → "while Nebuchadnezzar … and all his force and all the kingdoms of the earth under the dominion of his hand and all the peoples were fighting against Jerusalem and against all its cities, saying"** (v1) — וּ with the participle נִלְחָמִים: a clause of circumstance ("while"); חַיִל "force" (the exile row); מֶמְשֶׁלֶת יָדוֹ "the dominion of his hand" stands after "the earth" without a preposition ("under" supplied); הָעַמִּים plural "peoples"; לֵאמֹר "saying" (also v12, 13). The name is written נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר, with r (the token's original form).
- **"Go and speak to Zedekiah" → "Go, and you shall say to Zedekiah … and you shall say to him"** (v2) — הָלֹךְ, an infinitive absolute as command; וְאָמַרְתָּ twice; הִנְנִי נֹתֵן "behold, I am giving" (participle); שָׂרַף בָּאֵשׁ "burn with fire".
- **"And thou shalt not escape out of his hand, but shalt surely be taken, and delivered into his hand; and thine eyes shall behold the eyes of the king of Babylon, and he shall speak with thee mouth to mouth" → "And you, you shall not escape from his hand, for you shall surely be seized, and into his hand you shall be given; and your eyes shall see the eyes of the king of Babylon, and his mouth shall speak with your mouth, and to Babylon you shall go"** (v3) — וְאַתָּה stressed; כִּי "for"; תָּפֹשׂ תִּתָּפֵשׂ, literally "being seized you shall be seized" (the "surely" form; תָּפַשׂ "seize"); וּפִיהוּ אֶת־פִּיךָ יְדַבֵּר "his mouth shall speak with your mouth"; the Hebrew order kept.
- **"Yet hear the word of the LORD, O Zedekiah" → "Yet hear … Zedekiah"** (v4) — אַךְ "yet"; no "O" (the reviewer's vocative row); עָלֶיךָ "concerning you".
- **"But thou shalt die in peace: and with the burnings of thy fathers … so shall they burn odours for thee; and they will lament thee, saying, Ah lord! for I have pronounced the word" → "In shalom you shall die; and as the burnings for your fathers … so they shall burn for you, and 'Woe, lord!' they shall lament for you; for a word I myself have spoken"** (v5) — בְּשָׁלוֹם with the token; מִשְׂרְפוֹת "burnings": what was burned is not named ("spices" / "odours" are supplied in other versions); יִשְׂרְפוּ־לָךְ "they shall burn for you", subject not named; הוֹי "Woe" (the prophets' row; KJV "Ah"); אָדוֹן "lord" (its row), no article; סָפַד לְ "lament for" (its row); כִּי־דָבָר אֲנִי־דִבַּרְתִּי, "a word" first and אֲנִי "I" stressed.
- **"Then Jeremiah the prophet spake all these words unto Zedekiah" → "And Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Zedekiah … all these words"** (v6) — וַיְדַבֵּר "And … spoke", the Hebrew order.
- **"When the king of Babylon's army fought against Jerusalem, and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish, and against Azekah: for these defenced cities remained of the cities of Judah" → "and the force of the king of Babylon was fighting … against all the cities of Judah that remained, against Lachish and against Azekah; for these were left among the cities of Judah, fortified cities"** (v7) — וְחֵיל … נִלְחָמִים, again a clause with "and" and a participle; הַנּוֹתָרוֹת "that remained" (the נוֹתַר row) beside נִשְׁאֲרוּ "were left" (שָׁאַר nifal): two verbs; אֶל … וְאֶל "against … and against"; עָרֵי מִבְצָר "fortified cities" (the מִבְצָר row); "the only" is not written.
- **"after that the king Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people … to proclaim liberty unto them" → "after King Zedekiah had cut a covenant with all the people who were in Jerusalem to proclaim liberty to them"** (v8) — כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant" (its row; also v13, 15, 18, where the verb comes back in "cut the calf in two"); קָרָא דְּרוֹר "proclaim liberty" as Lev 25:10 (the דְּרוֹר row; literally "a flowing free", release; also v15, 17). The heading of v8 is not followed by the word itself until v12–22.
- **"That every man should let his manservant, and every man his maidservant, being an Hebrew or an Hebrewess, go free; that none should serve himself of them, to wit, of a Jew his brother" → "that each man should let his slave and each man his maidservant, the Hebrew man and the Hebrew woman, go free, so that no man should make them serve, a Jew his brother"** (v9) — שִׁלַּח … חָפְשִׁים "let go free" (the שִׁלַּח row: "let go" of one who is held; as Deut 15:12, which v14 quotes; also v10, 11, 14, 16); עֶבֶד "slave" (its row), שִׁפְחָה "maidservant" (its row); הָעִבְרִי וְהָעִבְרִיָּה "the Hebrew man and the Hebrew woman" with the token; עָבַד בְּ "make serve" (its row; literally "serve by means of"; also v10); בִּיהוּדִי אָחִיהוּ "a Jew his brother" with the `jew` token; אִישׁ "a man" at the end ("no man").
- **"Now when all the princes, and all the people … heard that every one should let his manservant … go free, that none should serve themselves of them any more, then they obeyed, and let them go" → "And all the princes and all the people who had come into the covenant heard … and they heard, and they let them go"** (v10) — שָׁמַע twice, "hear" both times (the שָׁמַע row: "obey" is not a Hebrew word here); שָׂרִים "princes" (the שַׂר row; also v19, 21); בָּאוּ בַבְּרִית "had come into the covenant".
- **"But afterward they turned, and caused the servants and the handmaids, whom they had let go free, to return, and brought them into subjection for servants and for handmaids" → "And afterward they turned back, and they brought back the slaves and the maidservants whom they had let go free, and they subdued them as slaves and as maidservants"** (v11) — וַיָּשׁוּבוּ … וַיָּשִׁבוּ, qal and hifil of one root, שׁוּב "turn back" / "bring back" (also v15, 16; v15 is the turning to the good, v16 the turning back from it); כָּבַשׁ "subdue" (its row; also v16). The reading (qere) וַיִּכְבְּשׁוּם (qal) is followed; the written text (ketiv) is a causative form of the same verb, the same sense.
- **"I made a covenant with your fathers … out of the house of bondmen" → "I myself cut a covenant … out of the house of slaves"** (v13) — אָנֹכִי stressed; בְּיוֹם "in the day"; בֵּית עֲבָדִים "house of slaves" (עֶבֶד "slave").
- **"At the end of seven years let ye go every man his brother an Hebrew, which hath been sold unto thee; and when he hath served thee six years, thou shalt let him go free from thee: but your fathers hearkened not unto me, neither inclined their ear" → "At the end of seven years you shall let go each man his brother, the Hebrew, who is sold to you; and he shall serve you six years, and you shall let him go free from you. But your fathers did not listen to me, and did not turn their ear"** (v14) — the words of Deut 15:1, 12; מִקֵּץ, literally "from the end"; תְּשַׁלְּחוּ "you (plural) shall let go", then the singular "you" of the law; יִמָּכֵר nifal "is sold" (or "sells himself": the form allows both); וַעֲבָדְךָ "and he shall serve you", not "when"; שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to" (its row; also v17); הִטָּה אֹזֶן "turn the ear" (נָטָה; "incline", as the Josh 24:23 row).
- **"And ye were now turned, and had done right in my sight … and ye had made a covenant before me in the house which is called by my name" → "And you, you turned back today and did what is right in my eyes … and you cut a covenant before me in the house over which my name is called"** (v15) — אַתֶּם stressed; הַיּוֹם "today"; שׁוּב "turn back" (not "repent": the שׁוּב row); הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינַי "what is right in my eyes" (the reviewer's row); אִישׁ לְרֵעֵהוּ "each man to his neighbor" (its row); אֲשֶׁר־נִקְרָא שְׁמִי עָלָיו, literally "over which my name is called".
- **"But ye turned and polluted my name … whom he had set at liberty at their pleasure" → "And you turned back and profaned my name … whom you had let go free to their soul"** (v16) — חִלֵּל "profane" (its row); לְנַפְשָׁם, literally "to their soul" (= to go where they would; as Deut 21:14); שִׁלַּחְתֶּם "you had let go" (plural; "he had" of the KJV is not the Hebrew); לִהְיוֹת לָכֶם "to be to you".
- **"Ye have not hearkened unto me … behold, I proclaim a liberty for you, saith the LORD, to the sword, to the pestilence, and to the famine; and I will make you to be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth" → "You, you have not listened to me … Behold, I am proclaiming liberty to you, declares Yahweh, to the sword, to the pestilence and to the famine; and I will make you a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth"** (v17) — אַתֶּם stressed; הִנְנִי קֹרֵא, participle; the same דְּרוֹר "liberty" turned against them; אֶל "to" three times; דֶּבֶר "pestilence", רָעָב "famine"; וְנָתַתִּי אֶתְכֶם לְ, literally "I will give you for" (= make you); זַעֲוָה "horror" (its row). The reading (qere) לְזַעֲוָה is followed; the written text (ketiv) has the letters לזועה, the same word.
- **"And I will give the men that have transgressed my covenant, which have not performed the words of the covenant which they had made before me, when they cut the calf in twain, and passed between the parts thereof" → "And I will make the men who overstepped my covenant, who did not establish the words of the covenant that they cut before me, the calf that they cut in two and passed between its halves"** (v18) — וְנָתַתִּי אֶת־הָאֲנָשִׁים, literally "I will give the men"; הָעֵגֶל "the calf" follows with nothing to join it: "I will make the men (as) the calf they cut in two" (the reading followed, "make" as in v17, 22), or "the covenant they cut before me — the calf they cut in two" (the calf as the covenant rite, KJV "when"); both in the one Hebrew; "like" is not written. עָבַר בְּרִית "overstep" (its row; "transgress" is פֶּשַׁע); הֵקִים "establish" the words (as Jer 33:14); כָּרַת לִשְׁנַיִם "cut in two": the same verb as "cut a covenant"; בְּתָרָיו "its halves" (בֶּתֶר "half", the Gen 15:10 row; "pieces" of Gen 15:17 is גְּזָרִים; also v19). The rite of Gen 15:9–18.
- **"The princes of Judah … the eunuchs, and the priests, and all the people of the land, which passed between the parts of the calf"** (v19) — kept, with "princes" and "halves"; סָרִיס "eunuch" (its row: also a court officer); עַם הָאָרֶץ "the people of the land" (its row). Verse 19 names the men of v18; v20 goes on with "and I will give them".
- **"those that seek their life: and their dead bodies shall be for meat unto the fowls of the heaven, and to the beasts of the earth" → "those who seek their soul; and their dead bodies shall be food for the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth"** (v20) — מְבַקְשֵׁי נַפְשָׁם "who seek their soul" (also v21); נִבְלָתָם, singular, "their dead body" (the נְבֵלָה row for a dead man), rendered plural for the many; עוֹף "birds" (its row); בֶּהֱמַת הָאָרֶץ "the beasts of the earth" (בְּהֵמָה "beast").
- **"which are gone up from you" → "who are going up from upon you"** (v21) — הָעֹלִים מֵעֲלֵיכֶם, a plural participle (the force as many men): the army that is withdrawing (Jer 37:5, 11).
- **"Behold, I will command … and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation without an inhabitant" → "Behold, I am commanding … and the cities of Judah I will make a desolation without inhabitant"** (v22) — הִנְנִי מְצַוֶּה participle; לָכַד "capture" (its row); אֶתֵּן שְׁמָמָה, literally "I will give a desolation" (= make; שְׁמָמָה "desolation", its row); the object first.

### Notes

- **v8–22** — the release of Hebrew slaves under the law of Deut 15:12 (seventh year), proclaimed with the word of the jubilee (Lev 25:10 דְּרוֹר), and the covenant rite of Gen 15:9–18: the calf cut in two and the parties passing between the halves.
