# Jeremiah 10

[1] Hear the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken to you, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"The way of the nations do not learn,
and at the signs of the heavens do not be dismayed,
for the nations are dismayed at them;

[3] for the ⟦chuqqot|chuqot|customs⟧ of the peoples are vapor;
for a tree from the forest one has cut down,
the work of the hands of a craftsman with the cutting tool.

[4] With silver and with gold he makes it beautiful;
with nails and with hammers they make them strong,
and it does not totter.

[5] Like a palm post in a cucumber field are they,
and they do not speak;
they are surely carried,
for they do not march.
Do not fear them,
for they do no evil,
and also to do good is not in them."

[6] There is none at all like you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
great are you, and great is your name in might.

[7] Who would not fear you, King of the nations?
For to you it is fitting;
for among all the wise of the nations
and in all their kingdom
there is none at all like you.

[8] And at once they are brutish and stupid;
the discipline of vapors — it is wood.

[9] Silver beaten out is brought from Tarshish,
and gold from ⟦uphaz|Ufaz|Uphaz⟧,
the work of a craftsman and of the hands of a refiner;
blue and purple is their clothing;
the work of wise men are they all.

[10] But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the true ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
he is the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the everlasting King.
At his wrath the earth quakes,
and nations cannot endure his indignation.

[11] Thus you shall say to them: "The gods who did not make the heavens and the earth shall perish from the earth and from under the heavens, these."

[12] The maker of the earth by his power,
who establishes the world by his wisdom,
and by his understanding stretched out the heavens.

[13] When he gives voice, there is a multitude of waters in the heavens,
and he made mists go up from the end of the earth;
lightnings for the rain he made,
and he brought out wind from his treasuries.

[14] Every man is brutish, without knowledge;
every refiner is put to shame by the carved image,
for his molten image is falsehood,
and there is no breath in them.

[15] Vapor are they, a work of mockeries;
at the time of their visitation they shall perish.

[16] Not like these is the share of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
for he is the former of all things,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is the tribe of his inheritance;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name.

[17] Gather up your bundle from the land,
you who dwell in the siege!

[18] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
"Behold, I am slinging out the inhabitants of the land
at this time,
and I will distress them,
so that they may find."

[19] Woe to me for my breaking!
My blow is grievous.
And I said, "Surely this is a sickness,
and I will bear it."

[20] My tent is laid waste,
and all my cords are snapped;
my sons have gone out from me, and they are not;
there is none pitching my tent any more
and raising up my curtains.

[21] For the shepherds have become brutish,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they have not sought;
therefore they have not had insight,
and all their pasturing flock is scattered.

[22] The sound of a report! Behold, it comes,
and a great quaking from the land of the north,
to make the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ a desolation,
a lair of jackals.

[23] I know, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, that not to man is his way;
not to a man who walks, to establish his step.

[24] Discipline me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, but in judgment;
not in your anger, lest you make me few.

[25] Pour out your fury on the nations
that have not known you,
and on clans
that have not called on your name;
for they have devoured ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
they have devoured him and made an end of him,
and his habitation they have laid waste.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v16). **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10).
- **Layout** — v2–10 and v12–25 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order; v1 and v11 are prose.
- **Vocative** (v1, 6, 7, 23, 24) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"which the LORD speaketh unto you" → "that Yahweh has spoken to you"** (v1) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר, perfect; עֲלֵיכֶם, literally "upon you" (to you, or concerning you).
- **"Learn not the way of the heathen" → "The way of the nations do not learn"** (v2) — Hebrew אֶל־דֶּרֶךְ הַגּוֹיִם אַל־תִּלְמָדוּ, the object first, with אֶל "to, toward" (= do not get used to); גּוֹיִם "nations". **"be not dismayed at the signs of heaven; for the heathen are dismayed at them"** — חָתַת nifal "be dismayed", twice; אוֹתוֹת הַשָּׁמַיִם "the signs of the heavens".
- **"For the customs of the people are vain" → "for the chuqqot of the peoples are vapor"** (v3) — Hebrew חֻקּוֹת "statutes" (the word of חֹק / חֻקָּה, "statute"; here the fixed ways of the peoples), the existing token kept with its forms; הָעַמִּים plural "peoples"; הֶבֶל הוּא, literally "it is vapor" (breath: what has nothing in it; "vanity" is the Latin). **"for one cutteth a tree out of the forest, the work of the hands of the workman, with the axe" → "for a tree from the forest one has cut down, the work of the hands of a craftsman with the cutting tool"** — כְּרָתוֹ, perfect, no subject named; חָרָשׁ "craftsman"; מַעֲצָד "cutting tool" (as Isa 44:12; kind uncertain).
- **"They deck it with silver and with gold; they fasten it with nails and with hammers, that it move not" → "With silver and with gold he makes it beautiful; with nails and with hammers they make them strong, and it does not totter"** (v4) — Hebrew יְיַפֵּהוּ singular "he makes it beautiful", יְחַזְּקוּם plural "they make them strong" (חִזֵּק, as Isa 41:7 "he makes it strong with nails"); וְלוֹא יָפִיק "and it does not totter".
- **"They are upright as the palm tree, but speak not" → "Like a palm post in a cucumber field are they, and they do not speak"** (v5) — Hebrew כְּתֹמֶר מִקְשָׁה: תֹּמֶר "palm (tree), post"; מִקְשָׁה is "cucumber field" (Isa 1:8) or, with the same letters, "hammered work" (Exod 25:18: "like a palm of hammered work"); a scarecrow in a cucumber field is how many take it (Letter of Jeremiah 70, from memory). Uncertain; the plainest sense of the two nouns is kept. "Their idols" is not in the Hebrew. **"they must needs be borne, because they cannot go" → "they are surely carried, for they do not march"** — נָשׂוֹא יִנָּשׂוּא, infinitive absolute ("being carried they are carried"); צָעַד "march, step". **"for they cannot do evil, neither also is it in them to do good" → "for they do no evil, and also to do good is not in them"** — לֹא יָרֵעוּ (hifil of רָעַע "do evil"); הֵיטֵיב "do good".
- **"Forasmuch as there is none like unto thee" → "There is none at all like you"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew מֵאֵין כָּמוֹךָ, literally "from there being none like you" (an intensive form). **"thy name is great in might"** — גְּבוּרָה "might".
- **"for to thee doth it appertain" → "For to you it is fitting"** (v7) — Hebrew יָאָתָה (only here). **"among all the wise men of the nations, and in all their kingdoms" → "among all the wise of the nations and in all their kingdom"** — מַלְכוּתָם, singular ("their kingship, their realm").
- **"But they are altogether brutish and foolish" → "And at once they are brutish and stupid"** (v8) — Hebrew וּבְאַחַת "and in one", "at once, together"; בָּעַר "be brutish" (also v14, 21), כָּסַל "be stupid" (the root of כְּסִיל). **"the stock is a doctrine of vanities" → "the discipline of vapors — it is wood"** — מוּסַר הֲבָלִים עֵץ הוּא: מוּסָר "discipline"; הֲבָלִים "vapors", of the idols; the instruction one gets from them is a piece of wood.
- **"Silver spread into plates" → "Silver beaten out"** (v9) — Hebrew מְרֻקָּע (רָקַע piel "beat out"). **"the work of the workman, and of the hands of the founder" → "the work of a craftsman and of the hands of a refiner"** — חָרָשׁ, צוֹרֵף (as Isa 41:7). **"blue and purple is their clothing"** — תְּכֵלֶת וְאַרְגָּמָן. **"they are all the work of cunning men" → "the work of wise men are they all"** — חֲכָמִים "wise men", the Hebrew order.
- **"But the LORD is the true God" → "But Yahweh is the true Elohim"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיהוָה אֱלֹהִים אֱמֶת, literally "Elohim, truth" (= Elohim in truth). **"he is the living God" → "he is the living Elohim"** — אֱלֹהִים חַיִּים, the adjective plural with the plural noun (the one God; "living gods" would misread the form). **"and an everlasting king" → "and the everlasting King"** — מֶלֶךְ עוֹלָם, literally "King of the age". **"at his wrath the earth shall tremble" → "At his wrath the earth quakes"** — קֶצֶף "wrath", רָעַשׁ "quake". **"and the nations shall not be able to abide his indignation" → "and nations cannot endure his indignation"** — גוֹיִם without the article; זַעַם "indignation".
- **Aramaic** (v11) — the verse is in Aramaic, not Hebrew; v10 and v12 are Hebrew. **"The gods that have not made the heavens and the earth, even they shall perish from the earth, and from under these heavens" → "The gods who did not make the heavens and the earth shall perish from the earth and from under the heavens, these"** — אֱלָהַיָּא "the gods", plain (the plural of other gods; the `elah-aram` token is for the one God); אֵלֶּה "these" stands at the end and refers to the gods, not to the heavens. Two forms for "earth", אַרְקָא and אַרְעָא; a sound play עֲבַדוּ "made" / יֵאבַדוּ "shall perish".
- **"He hath made the earth by his power, he hath established the world by his wisdom, and hath stretched out the heavens by his discretion" → "The maker of the earth by his power, who establishes the world by his wisdom, and by his understanding stretched out the heavens"** (v12) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה אֶרֶץ, participle in the construct ("maker of"); מֵכִין, participle; נָטָה, perfect; כֹּחַ "power", תֵּבֵל "world", תְּבוּנָה "understanding". v12–16 stand again in Jer 51:15–19; one wording for both.
- **"When he uttereth his voice, there is a multitude of waters in the heavens" → "When he gives voice, there is a multitude of waters in the heavens"** (v13) — Hebrew לְקוֹל תִּתּוֹ, literally "at the voice of his giving"; הֲמוֹן "multitude" (also "roar"). **"and he causeth the vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth" → "and he made mists go up from the end of the earth"** — וַיַּעֲלֶה, a past form, and so עָשָׂה "he made", וַיּוֹצֵא "he brought out" (the Hebrew mixes the timeless participles with past forms; kept); נְשִׂאִים, literally "things lifted up" ("vapor" is kept for הֶבֶל, v3, 8, 15); מִקְצֵה (singular) "from the end"; qere הָאָרֶץ, ketiv אֶרֶץ without the article: the sense is the same. **"he maketh lightnings with rain" → "lightnings for the rain he made"** — לַמָּטָר "for the rain". **"out of his treasures" → "from his treasuries"** — אֹצְרֹתָיו.
- **"Every man is brutish in his knowledge" → "Every man is brutish, without knowledge"** (v14) — Hebrew נִבְעַר כָּל־אָדָם מִדַּעַת, "brutish from knowledge" (= too brutish to know). **"every founder is confounded by the graven image" → "every refiner is put to shame by the carved image"** — הֹבִישׁ, צוֹרֵף, פֶּסֶל. **"for his molten image is falsehood, and there is no breath in them"** — נִסְכּוֹ "his molten image" (נֶסֶךְ, as Isa 41:29; elsewhere "drink offering"); רוּחַ "breath".
- **"They are vanity, and the work of errors" → "Vapor are they, a work of mockeries"** (v15) — Hebrew הֶבֶל הֵמָּה מַעֲשֵׂה תַּעְתֻּעִים (root תעע "mock"). **"in the time of their visitation they shall perish"** — פְּקֻדָּה "visitation".
- **"The portion of Jacob is not like them" → "Not like these is the share of Jacob"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא־כְאֵלֶּה חֵלֶק יַעֲקֹב, the Hebrew order; חֵלֶק "share". **"for he is the former of all things"** — יוֹצֵר הַכֹּל (יָצַר "form"). **"and Israel is the rod of his inheritance" → "and Israel is the tribe of his inheritance"** — שֵׁבֶט "rod", of a kin group "tribe".
- **"Gather up thy wares out of the land, O inhabitant of the fortress" → "Gather up your bundle from the land, you who dwell in the siege!"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּנְעָתֵךְ (only here; "bundle" is the likely sense); qere יֹשֶׁבֶת "you who dwell" (feminine), ketiv יֹשַׁבְתִּי, an older form of the same; בַּמָּצוֹר "in the siege".
- **"I will sling out the inhabitants of the land at this once" → "Behold, I am slinging out the inhabitants of the land at this time"** (v18) — Hebrew הִנְנִי קוֹלֵעַ, participle with הִנֵּה; בַּפַּעַם הַזֹּאת "at this time". **"and will distress them, that they may find it so" → "and I will distress them, so that they may find"** — הֲצֵרוֹתִי (the root of צָרָה "distress"); יִמְצָאוּ has no object ("find it", "be found", "feel it" are guesses: uncertain).
- **"Woe is me for my hurt! my wound is grievous" → "Woe to me for my breaking! My blow is grievous"** (v19) — Hebrew אוֹי לִי; שֶׁבֶר "breaking"; מַכָּה "blow"; נַחְלָה "grievous" (nifal of חָלָה "be sick"). **"but I said, Truly this is a grief, and I must bear it" → "And I said, Surely this is a sickness, and I will bear it"** — וַאֲנִי אָמַרְתִּי; אַךְ "surely"; חֳלִי "sickness" (as נַחְלָה); וְאֶשָּׂאֶנּוּ.
- **"My tabernacle is spoiled" → "My tent is laid waste"** (v20) — Hebrew אָהֳלִי שֻׁדָּד (שָׁדַד "lay waste"). **"all my cords are broken" → "all my cords are snapped"** — נִתָּקוּ, nifal of נָתַק "tear away" (of a cord pulled apart; "drawn out" of the Josh 4:18 row is said of feet from water). **"my children are gone forth of me" → "my sons have gone out from me"** — בָּנַי "my sons". **"there is none to stretch forth my tent any more, and to set up my curtains" → "there is none pitching my tent any more and raising up my curtains"** — נָטָה אֹהֶל "pitch a tent"; הֵקִים "raise up".
- **"For the pastors are become brutish, and have not sought the LORD" → "For the shepherds have become brutish, and Yahweh they have not sought"** (v21) — Hebrew רֹעִים "shepherds"; the object first. **"therefore they shall not prosper" → "therefore they have not had insight"** — לֹא הִשְׂכִּילוּ, perfect; הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight, act wisely" (also "prosper": the one word). **"and all their flocks shall be scattered" → "and all their pasturing flock is scattered"** — מַרְעִיתָם "their pasturing" (the flock at pasture); נָפוֹצָה, perfect.
- **"Behold, the noise of the bruit is come" → "The sound of a report! Behold, it comes"** (v22) — Hebrew קוֹל שְׁמוּעָה הִנֵּה בָאָה. **"a great commotion out of the north country" → "a great quaking from the land of the north"** — רַעַשׁ "quaking" (the verb of v10). **"desolate, and a den of dragons" → "a desolation, a lair of jackals"** — שְׁמָמָה מְעוֹן תַּנִּים, as 9:11.
- **"O LORD, I know that the way of man is not in himself: it is not in man that walketh to direct his steps" → "I know, Yahweh, that not to man is his way; not to a man who walks, to establish his step"** (v23) — Hebrew לָאָדָם (אָדָם "man, mankind") … לְאִישׁ (אִישׁ "a man"), two words; לְ "to, belonging to"; הָכִין "establish" (make firm, direct); צַעֲדוֹ singular.
- **"O LORD, correct me, but with judgment; not in thine anger, lest thou bring me to nothing" → "Discipline me, Yahweh, but in judgment; not in your anger, lest you make me few"** (v24) — Hebrew יִסֵּר "discipline"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; אַף "anger"; תַּמְעִטֵנִי, hifil of מָעַט "be few, little".
- **"Pour out thy fury upon the heathen that know thee not, and upon the families that call not on thy name" → "Pour out your fury on the nations that have not known you, and on clans that have not called on your name"** (v25) — Hebrew חֵמָה "fury"; perfects; מִשְׁפָּחוֹת "clans", without the article. Ps 79:6–7 has nearly the same words (מַמְלָכוֹת "kingdoms" there, and a singular "he has devoured"); each as printed, the shared words in one wording. **"they have eaten up Jacob, and devoured him, and consumed him, and have made his habitation desolate" → "they have devoured Jacob, they have devoured him and made an end of him, and his habitation they have laid waste"** — אָכְלוּ … וַאֲכָלֻהוּ, the same verb twice; כִּלָּה "make an end of"; נָוֶה "habitation"; הֵשַׁמּוּ (hifil of שָׁמֵם), as Ps 79:7.

### Notes
- **v11** — the only verse in Jeremiah written in Aramaic.
- **v12–16** — the same Hebrew stands in Jer 51:15–19.
