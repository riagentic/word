# Jeremiah 21

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when King ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ sent to him ⟦pashhur|Pashchur|Pashhur⟧ son of ⟦malchiah|Malkiyah|Malchiah⟧ and ⟦zephaniah|Tzefanyah|Zephaniah⟧ son of ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧, the priest, saying,

[2] "Seek, please, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on our behalf, for ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ is fighting against us. Perhaps ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will deal with us according to all his wonderful deeds, and he will go up from us."

[3] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said to them, "Thus you shall say to ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧:

[4] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I am turning back the weapons of war that are in your hand, with which you are fighting the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ who are besieging you outside the wall; and I will gather them into the midst of this city.

[5] And I myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a strong arm, and with anger and with fury and with great wrath.

[6] And I will strike the inhabitants of this city, both man and beast; by a great pestilence they shall die.

[7] And afterward, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, I will give ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and his servants and the people, and those who are left in this city from the pestilence, from the sword and from the famine, into the hand of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those who seek their soul; and he shall strike them with the edge of the sword. He shall not pity them, and he shall not spare, and he shall not have compassion."

[8] And to this people you shall say: "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am setting before you the way of life and the way of death.

[9] He who dwells in this city shall die by the sword and by the famine and by the pestilence; but he who goes out and falls away to the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ who are besieging you shall live, and his soul shall be his as spoil.

[10] For I have put my face against this city for evil and not for good, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; into the hand of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ it shall be given, and he shall burn it with fire."

[11] And to the house of the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧: Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[12] house of David! Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

Judge judgment in the morning,
and deliver the robbed
from the hand of the extortioner,
lest my fury go out like fire
and burn, and there is none who quenches,
because of the evil of your doings.

[13] Behold, I am against you, inhabitant of the valley,
rock of the tableland, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who say, "Who shall come down upon us,
and who shall come into our abodes?"

[14] And I will visit upon you according to the fruit of your doings,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
and I will kindle a fire in her forest,
and it shall devour all that is around her.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4). נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v7, 10, 13, 14), literally "the declaration of Yahweh".
- **Layout** — v1–11 are prose; v12 (after the opening words) to v14 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order (v12 "deliver the robbed / from the hand of the extortioner" in the Hebrew order of the words).
- **"The word which came unto Jeremiah from the LORD" → "The word that came …"** (v1) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר אֲשֶׁר־הָיָה; "This is" was supplied. **"Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah the priest"** — הַכֹּהֵן "the priest" stands after Maaseiah; Zephaniah is the priest (Jer 29:25; 37:3; 52:24).
- **"Enquire, I pray thee, of the LORD for us" → "Seek, please, Yahweh on our behalf"** (v2) — Hebrew דְּרָשׁ־נָא, דָּרַשׁ "seek" with נָא; בַּעֲדֵנוּ "on our behalf". **"maketh war against us" → "is fighting against us"** — נִלְחָם, a participle of נִלְחַם "fight" (also v4, 5). The name is written נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר, with r (the token's original form). **"according to all his wondrous works, that he may go up from us" → "according to all his wonderful deeds, and he will go up from us"** — נִפְלְאֹתָיו "his wonderful deeds"; וְיַעֲלֶה מֵעָלֵינוּ, literally "and he will go up from upon us": the king of Babylon will withdraw. The subject is not named.
- **"I will turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands" → "I am turning back … that are in your hand"** (v4) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מֵסֵב, a participle; בְּיֶדְכֶם singular "hand". **"wherewith ye fight against the king of Babylon, and against the Chaldeans, which besiege you without the walls" → "with which you are fighting the king of Babylon and the Chaldeans who are besieging you outside the wall"** — the verb takes them as direct object (אֶת); הַצָּרִים עֲלֵיכֶם "besieging you" (צוּר עַל, also v9); לַחוֹמָה singular. **"and I will assemble them" → "and I will gather them"** — אָסַף "gather"; "them" may be the weapons or the Chaldeans: the Hebrew leaves it open.
- **"I myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a strong arm, even in anger, and in fury, and in great wrath" → "And I myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a strong arm, and with anger and with fury and with great wrath"** (v5) — Hebrew וְנִלְחַמְתִּי אֲנִי, "And … I" stressed; the formula of the exodus ("a strong hand and an outstretched arm", Deut 4:34; 26:8) is turned round here: נְטוּיָה goes with יָד "hand", חֲזָקָה with זְרוֹעַ "arm", and it is turned against Israel. Each member has בְּ "with" (the reviewer's "with a strong hand" row). אַף "anger", חֵמָה "fury", קֶצֶף "wrath".
- **"I will smite" → "I will strike"** (v6, 7) — הִכָּה. **"they shall die of a great pestilence" → "by a great pestilence they shall die"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"such as are left in this city from the pestilence" → "those who are left in this city from the pestilence, from the sword and from the famine"** (v7) — הַנִּשְׁאָרִים "those who are left" (nifal of שָׁאַר). **"those that seek their life" → "those who seek their soul"** — נַפְשָׁם (נֶפֶשׁ "soul"). **"with the edge of the sword"** — לְפִי־חֶרֶב, literally "to the mouth of the sword". **"he shall not spare them, neither have pity, nor have mercy" → "He shall not pity them, and he shall not spare, and he shall not have compassion"** — three verbs: חוּס "pity", חָמַל "spare" (have pity), רִחַם "have compassion" (root of רַחֲמִים).
- **Quotation marks** (v3–10) — Jeremiah's answer to the envoys (v3–7) is closed at v7; v8–10 are a second word, with "you" singular in v8 ("you shall say"): Jeremiah is addressed. The Hebrew has no quotation marks.
- **"Behold, I set before you" → "Behold, I am setting before you"** (v8) — Hebrew הִנְנִי נֹתֵן לִפְנֵיכֶם, a participle; the verb is "give" (the reviewer's "set before" row; as Deut 30:15, 19).
- **"He that abideth in this city" → "He who dwells in this city"** (v9) — Hebrew הַיֹּשֵׁב, יָשַׁב "dwell". **"he that goeth out, and falleth to the Chaldeans" → "he who goes out and falls away to the Chaldeans"** — נָפַל עַל, of going over to the enemy (as 2 Kgs 25:11). **"shall live"** — the written text (ketiv) is יחיה "shall live", the reading (qere) וְחָיָה "and shall live": the same sense; the qere is followed. **"his life shall be unto him for a prey" → "his soul shall be his as spoil"** — Hebrew וְהָיְתָה־לּוֹ נַפְשׁוֹ לְשָׁלָל, literally "his soul shall be to him for spoil": he shall carry off his life and nothing else (also Jer 38:2; 39:18; 45:5).
- **"I have set my face against this city for evil" → "I have put my face against this city for evil"** (v10) — שַׂמְתִּי פָנַי בָּעִיר, שִׂים פָּנִים "put one's face" (as Lev 20:5; Jer 44:11; נָתַן פָּנִים is "set"), literally "in this city"; רָעָה "evil", set against טוֹבָה "good". **"it shall be given into the hand of the king of Babylon" → "into the hand of the king of Babylon it shall be given"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"And touching the house of the king of Judah, say, Hear ye the word of the LORD" → "And to the house of the king of Judah: Hear the word of Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew וּלְבֵית מֶלֶךְ יְהוּדָה שִׁמְעוּ: "say" is not in the Hebrew. לְ may be "to" or "concerning" (a heading over 21:11–23:8).
- **"O house of David" → "house of David"** (v12) — no "O": the Hebrew has the bare vocative (the reviewer's vocative row; also v13). **"Execute judgment in the morning" → "Judge judgment in the morning"** — Hebrew דִּינוּ … מִשְׁפָּט, verb and noun of two roots: דִּין "judge" with מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; לַבֹּקֶר "in the morning" (or "morning by morning"). **"deliver him that is spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor" → "deliver the robbed from the hand of the extortioner"** — גָזוּל "one robbed" (גָּזַל "rob"); עוֹשֵׁק "extortioner" (the עָשַׁק row; as Jer 22:3). **"lest my fury go out like fire, and burn that none can quench it" → "lest my fury go out like fire and burn, and there is none who quenches"** — חֵמָה "fury"; וְאֵין מְכַבֶּה, a clause. **"because of the evil of your doings"** — the reading (qere) מַעַלְלֵיכֶם "your doings"; the written text (ketiv) has מעלליהם "their doings".
- **"O inhabitant of the valley, and rock of the plain" → "inhabitant of the valley, rock of the tableland"** (v13) — יֹשֶׁבֶת, a feminine singular: the city is addressed as a woman (also "her forest", v14); מִישֹׁר "tableland" (level land: the מִישׁוֹר row). Which place is meant is not said. **"which say" → "who say"** — הָאֹמְרִים is plural. **"Who shall come down against us? or who shall enter into our habitations?" → "Who shall come down upon us, and who shall come into our abodes?"** — יֵחַת (from נָחַת "come down") עָלֵינוּ "upon us"; וּמִי "and who"; מְעוֹנוֹת "abodes" (also of the dens of beasts).
- **"But I will punish you according to the fruit of your doings" → "And I will visit upon you according to the fruit of your doings"** (v14) — פָּקַד עַל "visit upon" (the פָּקַד row; "punish" is the sense); the verse opens with וּ "And". **"in the forest thereof … round about it" → "in her forest … all that is around her"** — the feminine suffixes: the city. "Forest" may be the houses built of cedar (Jer 22:6–7, 23; the House of the Forest of Lebanon, 1 Kgs 7:2).
