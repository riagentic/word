# Hosea 11

[1] **When ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was a boy, then I loved him,
and out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ I called my son.**

[2] They called to them;
so they went from their face;
to the Baals they sacrifice,
and to the carved images they make smoke.

[3] And I, I taught ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ to walk,
taking them upon his arms;
and they did not know that I healed them.

[4] With cords of man I drew them,
with ropes of love;
and I was to them like those who lift up a yoke upon their jaws,
and I bent down to him; I fed him.

[5] He shall not return to the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, he is his king,
for they have refused to return.

[6] And the sword shall whirl in his cities,
and make an end of his bars, and devour,
because of their counsels.

[7] And my people are hung upon turning away from me;
and to the One above they call him;
together he does not lift him up.

[8] How shall I give you up, ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧,
deliver you up, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?
How shall I give you up like Admah,
set you like ⟦zeboiim|Tzevoyim|Zeboiim⟧?
My heart is overturned upon me;
together my relentings grow warm.

[9] I will not do the burning of my anger;
I will not return to ruin ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧;
for ⟦el|El|God⟧ I am, and not a man,
in your midst the Holy One,
and I will not come into a city.

[10] After ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they shall go;
like a lion he shall roar;
for he, he shall roar,
and sons shall come trembling from the sea.

[11] They shall come trembling like a bird from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and like a dove from the land of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧;
and I will make them dwell in their houses, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ has surrounded me with lying,
and the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ with deceit;
and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ still roams with ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and with the Holy One is faithful.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "El"** (v9, 12) — Hebrew אֵל, not אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** — poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line, as the accents divide them.
- **"When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt" → "When Israel was a boy, then I loved him, and out of Egypt I called my son"** (v1) — Hebrew כִּי נַעַר יִשְׂרָאֵל וָאֹהֲבֵהוּ: כִּי "when" (also "for"); נַעַר "boy" (its row); וָאֹהֲבֵהוּ "and I loved him" (the וָ given as "then", as KJV). וּמִמִּצְרַיִם קָרָאתִי לִבְנִי: "out of Egypt" first, as the Hebrew; קָרָא לְ "call" (to call him to come; also "call him my son": the לְ can mark the name given). The verse speaks of Israel as Yahweh's son (Exod 4:22) and of the exodus; Matt 2:15 quotes it of Yeshua, with the Hebrew "my son" (the Greek of the Septuagint has "his children": from memory). The verse is translated as the Hebrew stands.
- **"As they called them, so they went from them" → "They called to them; so they went from their face"** (v2) — Hebrew קָרְאוּ לָהֶם, "they called to them" (the callers are not named: the prophets, or Yahweh's messengers; the Greek has "as I called them": from memory, the MT kept); כֵּן "so"; מִפְּנֵיהֶם "from their face" (the מִפְּנֵי row: of leaving a person). **"they sacrificed unto Baalim, and burned incense to graven images" → "to the Baals they sacrifice, and to the carved images they make smoke"** — imperfects (what they kept doing), the objects first; זִבֵּחַ piel "sacrifice"; פְּסִלִים "carved images" (its row); קִטֵּר without an object "make smoke" (its row; not "burn incense").
- **"I taught Ephraim also to go, taking them by their arms" → "And I, I taught Ephraim to walk, taking them upon his arms"** (v3) — וְאָנֹכִי, the pronoun stressed; תִרְגַּלְתִּי, from רֶגֶל "foot": "I taught to walk" (found only here); קָחָם, the infinitive "taking them" (or a perfect "he took them"); עַל־זְרוֹעֹתָיו "upon his arms" (the Hebrew has "his": Ephraim's arms; the versions read "my arms": from memory). **"but they knew not that I healed them"** — וְלֹא יָדְעוּ, "and" (no "but"); רָפָא "heal" (its row).
- **"I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love" → "With cords of man I drew them, with ropes of love"** (v4) — חַבְלֵי אָדָם "cords of man" (אָדָם "man, mankind": cords fit for a man, not for a beast; "kindness" is not written); עֲבֹתוֹת "ropes" (its row); מָשַׁךְ "draw"; the means first, as the Hebrew. **"and I was to them as they that take off the yoke on their jaws" → "and I was to them like those who lift up a yoke upon their jaws"** — כִּמְרִימֵי עֹל, "like those who raise a yoke" (הֵרִים its row; lifting it up off the jaws, or "a little child" by another pointing, עוּל: uncertain); עֹל "yoke" (its row). **"and I laid meat unto them" → "and I bent down to him; I fed him"** — וְאַט אֵלָיו אוֹכִיל: אַט taken from נָטָה "bend" (or the adverb "gently": "and gently to him I gave food"; uncertain); אוֹכִיל "I fed" (hifil of אָכַל; "him" supplied for English); אֵלָיו "to him" singular.
- **"He shall not return into the land of Egypt, but the Assyrian shall be his king" → "He shall not return to the land of Egypt, and Assyria, he is his king"** (v5) — לֹא "not" as printed (some read לוֹ "to him / surely": the MT kept; 8:13; 9:3 say he shall return to Egypt: the tension is left); וְאַשּׁוּר הוּא מַלְכּוֹ, "and Assyria, he is his king" (no "but"; the name, not "the Assyrian"). **"because they refused to return"** — מֵאֵן "refuse" (its row); "to me" is not written.
- **"And the sword shall abide on his cities, and shall consume his branches, and devour them" → "And the sword shall whirl in his cities, and make an end of his bars, and devour"** (v6) — חָלָה, from חוּל "whirl, dance" (also "writhe"); בְּעָרָיו "in his cities"; כִּלָּה "make an end of" (its row); בַּדָּיו "his bars" (בַּד, the "pole" row; of a city the bars of its gates; the same letters are "his empty talkers", Jer 50:36: uncertain); וְאָכָלָה "and devour", no object. **"because of their own counsels"** — מִמֹּעֲצוֹתֵיהֶם "from their counsels".
- **"And my people are bent to backsliding from me: though they called them to the most High, none at all would exalt him" → "And my people are hung upon turning away from me; and to the One above they call him; together he does not lift him up"** (v7) — the line is hard and is kept as the MT has it. תְלוּאִים "hung" (= bent upon, held fast to); מְשׁוּבָתִי "my turning away" (מְשׁוּבָה its reviewer row; = turning away from me). וְאֶל־עַל "and to above" (עַל "height", taken as a name, "the One above"; or read as "the yoke", עֹל: uncertain); יִקְרָאֻהוּ "they call him" (who calls whom is not said); יַחַד לֹא יְרוֹמֵם, "together he does not raise (him) up" (רוֹמֵם "raise high"; "him" supplied: uncertain).
- **"How shall I give thee up, Ephraim? how shall I deliver thee, Israel?" → "How shall I give you up, Ephraim, deliver you up, Israel?"** (v8) — אֵיךְ "how" once over both lines; נָתַן "give (up)"; מִגֵּן "deliver up" (its row, which names this verse); no "O". **"how shall I make thee as Admah? how shall I set thee as Zeboim?" → "How shall I give you up like Admah, set you like Zeboiim?"** — the same נָתַן as the line before ("give you up"); שִׂים "set"; Admah and Zeboiim, overthrown with Sodom (Deut 29:23). **"mine heart is turned within me, my repentings are kindled together" → "My heart is overturned upon me; together my relentings grow warm"** — נֶהְפַּךְ, the verb of the overthrow of the cities (the הָפַךְ row, literally "turned over"): the heart is overturned in place of Ephraim; עָלַי "upon me"; נִחוּמִים "relentings" (noun of the נִחַם row, "relent" where a planned act is given up); נִכְמְרוּ "grow warm" (as Gen 43:30).
- **"I will not execute the fierceness of mine anger, I will not return to destroy Ephraim" → "I will not do the burning of my anger; I will not return to ruin Ephraim"** (v9) — עָשָׂה "do"; חֲרוֹן אַף "the burning of anger" (as the חֳרִי אַף row); לֹא אָשׁוּב לְשַׁחֵת, literally "I will not return to ruin" (= I will not ruin again: the שׁוּב row); שִׁחֵת "ruin" (its row). **"for I am God, and not man; the Holy One in the midst of thee" → "for El I am, and not a man, in your midst the Holy One"** — כִּי אֵל אָנֹכִי, El first; אִישׁ "a man" (its row); קָדוֹשׁ "Holy One", the order kept. **"and I will not enter into the city" → "and I will not come into a city"** — וְלֹא אָבוֹא בְּעִיר as printed: עִיר "city", no article (some take it from another root, "in anger" or "in terror": uncertain; the plain word kept).
- **"They shall walk after the LORD: he shall roar like a lion: when he shall roar, then the children shall tremble from the west" → "After Yahweh they shall go; like a lion he shall roar; for he, he shall roar, and sons shall come trembling from the sea"** (v10) — הָלַךְ אַחֲרֵי "go after" (its row), the name first; כִּי־הוּא "for he", stressed; שָׁאַג "roar"; חָרַד "tremble" (its row) with מִן "from": "come" is supplied; בָּנִים "sons" (its row), no article; מִיָּם "from the sea" (= the west).
- **"They shall tremble as a bird out of Egypt, and as a dove out of the land of Assyria: and I will place them in their houses, saith the LORD" → "They shall come trembling like a bird from Egypt, and like a dove from the land of Assyria; and I will make them dwell in their houses, declares Yahweh"** (v11) — חָרַד as v10; צִפּוֹר "bird" (its row); יוֹנָה "dove"; הוֹשִׁיב "make dwell" (hifil of יָשַׁב "dwell"); עַל "in / upon"; נְאֻם "declares".
- **"Ephraim compasseth me about with lies, and the house of Israel with deceit" → "Ephraim has surrounded me with lying, and the house of Israel with deceit"** (v12) — סְבָבֻנִי perfect; כַּחַשׁ "lying" (as 10:13); מִרְמָה "deceit" (its row). **"but Judah yet ruleth with God, and is faithful with the saints" → "and Judah still roams with El, and with the Holy One is faithful"** — וִיהוּדָה "and Judah" (no "but"); עֹד רָד, from רוּד "roam, be restless" (KJV "rule" reads רָדָה; whether Judah is praised or blamed is not clear: uncertain); קְדוֹשִׁים is plural, "the Holy One" taken as a plural of majesty beside אֵל (as Prov 9:10; 30:3), or "holy ones"; נֶאֱמָן "faithful" (its row).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 11:12 = Hebrew 12:1.
- **v1** — quoted Matt 2:15 (Yeshua's return from Egypt).
