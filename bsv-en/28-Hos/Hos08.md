# Hosea 8

[1] To your palate a ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧!
Like the eagle over the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
because they have overstepped my covenant
and against my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ they have transgressed.

[2] To me they cry out,
"My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, we know you" — ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has cast off good;
an enemy shall pursue him.

[4] They, they have made kings reign, and not from me;
they have made princes, and I did not know.
Their silver and their gold they have made for themselves into idols,
so that it may be cut off.

[5] He has cast off your calf, ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧;
my anger has burned against them.
How long will they not be capable of innocence?

[6] For from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is it also:
a craftsman made it, and it is not ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
for splinters shall it become,
the calf of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[7] For wind they sow,
and tempest they shall reap.
Standing grain — it has no sprout;
it shall not make meal;
perhaps it will make it:
strangers shall swallow it.

[8] Swallowed up is ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
now they have become among the nations
like a vessel in which there is no delight.

[9] For they, they have gone up to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧,
a wild donkey alone by himself;
⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ have hired loves.

[10] Even though they hire among the nations,
now I will gather them;
and they have begun to be few
from the burden of the king of princes.

[11] For ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ has multiplied altars for sinning;
they have become to him altars for sinning.

[12] I write for him the myriads of my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧;
like a strange thing they are reckoned.

[13] The sacrifices of my gifts:
they sacrifice flesh and they eat;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is not pleased with them.
Now he will remember their iniquity
and visit their sins;
they, to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ they shall return.

[14] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ forgot his Maker
and built palaces,
and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has multiplied fortified cities;
and I will send a fire into his cities,
and it shall devour her citadels.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 6) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים (v2 with "my": אֱלֹהַי).
- **Layout** — poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line, as the accents divide them.
- **"Set the trumpet to thy mouth. He shall come as an eagle against the house of the LORD" → "To your palate a shofar! Like the eagle over the house of Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶל־חִכְּךָ שֹׁפָר, "to your palate, a shofar": no verb (the cry is a command without "set"); חֵךְ "palate" (its row; "mouth" is פֶּה). כַּנֶּשֶׁר עַל־בֵּית יְהוָה, "like the eagle over the house of Yahweh": no verb, no "he shall come"; נֶשֶׁר "eagle" (its row: the word also covers the great vultures); עַל "over" or "against". **"transgressed my covenant, and trespassed against my law" → "overstepped my covenant and against my Torah they have transgressed"** — עָבַר בְּרִית "overstep" (its row; lit. "pass over"); פָּשַׁע עַל "transgress against" (root of פֶּשַׁע "transgression"), with the object first as the Hebrew has it.
- **"Israel shall cry unto me, My God, we know thee" → "To me they cry out, 'My Elohim, we know you' — Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew לִי יִזְעָקוּ, "to me" first; יִזְעָקוּ imperfect "they cry out" (זָעַק its row). יִשְׂרָאֵל stands last: "we, Israel, know you" (apposition to "we"), or the name of those who cry; the Hebrew order kept and the sense left open.
- **"Israel hath cast off the thing that is good: the enemy shall pursue him" → "Israel has cast off good; an enemy shall pursue him"** (v3) — זָנַח "cast off" (its row); טוֹב "good" without the article; אוֹיֵב "an enemy" without the article.
- **"They have set up kings, but not by me: they have made princes, and I knew it not" → "They, they have made kings reign, and not from me; they have made princes, and I did not know"** (v4) — Hebrew הֵם הִמְלִיכוּ, the pronoun stressed; הִמְלִיךְ "make reign" (its row: no object written, "kings" supplied for English); וְלֹא מִמֶּנִּי "and not from me"; הֵשִׂירוּ "they have made princes" (hifil of שָׂרַר, root of שַׂר "prince"; also read from סוּר "they have removed"): uncertain, the Masoretic form taken as "made princes"; "it" is not written after "know". **"of their silver and their gold have they made them idols, that they may be cut off" → "Their silver and their gold they have made for themselves into idols, so that it may be cut off"** — עָשׂוּ לָהֶם עֲצַבִּים "they made for themselves idols" (עֲצַבִּים its row); לְמַעַן יִכָּרֵת singular "so that it may be cut off": Israel (as one), or the silver and gold; the result put as purpose.
- **"Thy calf, O Samaria, hath cast thee off" → "He has cast off your calf, Samaria"** (v5) — Hebrew זָנַח עֶגְלֵךְ שֹׁמְרוֹן: זָנַח is a perfect, third person, with no object suffix; עֶגְלֵךְ "your calf" (feminine "your": Samaria). Taken as "He has cast off your calf" (Yahweh, unnamed, as the next line "my anger" shows); the KJV makes the calf the subject and supplies "thee". No "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it). **"mine anger is kindled against them" → "my anger has burned against them"** — חָרָה אַפִּי בָּם, a perfect; אַף "anger" (its row). **"how long will it be ere they attain to innocency?" → "How long will they not be capable of innocence?"** — עַד־מָתַי לֹא יוּכְלוּ נִקָּיֹן, literally "until when will they not be able (for) innocence"; נִקָּיוֹן "innocence" (its row).
- **"For from Israel was it also: the workman made it; therefore it is not God: but the calf of Samaria shall be broken in pieces" → "For from Israel is it also: a craftsman made it, and it is not Elohim; for splinters shall it become, the calf of Samaria"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי מִיִּשְׂרָאֵל וְהוּא, "for from Israel — and it", no verb; חָרָשׁ "craftsman" (its row), no article; וְלֹא אֱלֹהִים הוּא "and not Elohim is it" ("and", not "therefore"; the sense is "it is no god": the token kept for אֱלֹהִים, "a god" in the plain sense). כִּי־שְׁבָבִים יִהְיֶה "for splinters it shall be" (כִּי "for", not "but"); שְׁבָבִים is found only here: "splinters" or "fragments", uncertain (some take it as "flames"); the calf of Samaria named last, as the Hebrew.
- **"For they have sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind" → "For wind they sow, and tempest they shall reap"** (v7) — Hebrew רוּחַ יִזְרָעוּ, imperfect, the object first; רוּחַ "wind" (its row) and סוּפָתָה "tempest" (סוּפָה its row, with the old poetic ending ־ָה) both without the article. **"it hath no stalk: the bud shall yield no meal: if so be it yield, the strangers shall swallow it up" → "Standing grain — it has no sprout; it shall not make meal; perhaps it will make it: strangers shall swallow it"** — קָמָה "standing grain" (its row) אֵין־לוֹ "it has none"; צֶמַח "sprout" (its row) and קֶמַח "meal" (its row) rhyme in the Hebrew; עָשָׂה "make" (as the עָשָׂה פְּרִי row: not "yield"); אוּלַי "perhaps" (its row); זָרִים "strangers" without the article; בָּלַע "swallow" (its row).
- **"Israel is swallowed up: now shall they be among the Gentiles as a vessel wherein is no pleasure" → "Swallowed up is Israel; now they have become among the nations like a vessel in which there is no delight"** (v8) — נִבְלַע first, as the Hebrew; הָיוּ, a perfect, "they have become"; גּוֹיִם "nations" (its row); כְּלִי "vessel"; חֵפֶץ "delight" (the noun of the חָפֵץ בְּ row).
- **"For they are gone up to Assyria, a wild ass alone by himself: Ephraim hath hired lovers" → "For they, they have gone up to Assyria, a wild donkey alone by himself; Ephraim have hired loves"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי־הֵמָּה עָלוּ, the pronoun stressed; פֶּרֶא "wild donkey" (as Jer 2:24; 14:6), sounding like אֶפְרַיִם "Ephraim"; בּוֹדֵד לוֹ "alone by himself". אֶפְרַיִם הִתְנוּ, plural verb with the name (kept: "have"); הִתְנוּ hifil of תָּנָה "hire", the verb of אֶתְנַן "hire" (its row: a prostitute's fee) — so here they pay; אֲהָבִים "loves" (the plural noun of love: love affairs, or love-gifts), not מְאַהֲבִים "lovers".
- **"Yea, though they have hired among the nations, now will I gather them" → "Even though they hire among the nations, now I will gather them"** (v10) — גַּם כִּי־יִתְנוּ "even though they hire" (imperfect); קִבֵּץ "gather". **"and they shall sorrow a little for the burden of the king of princes" → "and they have begun to be few from the burden of the king of princes"** — וַיָּחֵלּוּ is pointed as the hifil of חלל "begin" (as the הֵחֵל row), a narrative form: "and they have begun"; מְּעָט "a little, few" ("to be" is supplied). The KJV "sorrow" reads the verb from חִיל "writhe" (pointed differently); the Masoretic pointing kept. מַשָּׂא "burden" (what is lifted up: the load laid on them); מֶלֶךְ שָׂרִים "king of princes" (no "and" in the Hebrew: the Assyrian title "king of kings"?, uncertain).
- **"Because Ephraim hath made many altars to sin, altars shall be unto him to sin" → "For Ephraim has multiplied altars for sinning; they have become to him altars for sinning"** (v11) — כִּי "for"; הִרְבָּה "multiply" (as v14); לַחֲטֹא "for sinning" both times, as the Hebrew repeats it; הָיוּ־לוֹ "they have become to him" (a perfect).
- **"I have written to him the great things of my law, but they were counted as a strange thing" → "I write for him the myriads of my Torah; like a strange thing they are reckoned"** (v12) — the written text has אכתוב (a fuller spelling of the same verb) and רבו; the qere אֶכְתָּב, רֻבֵּי is translated: "I write" (imperfect: also "should I write", a supposition), "the myriads of" (רִבּוֹא "ten thousand"; the ketiv may be read "the many things of"). כְּמוֹ־זָר "like a strange thing" (זָר "stranger, strange", as v7); נֶחְשָׁבוּ "they are reckoned" (חָשַׁב its row); no "but".
- **"They sacrifice flesh for the sacrifices of mine offerings, and eat it" → "The sacrifices of my gifts: they sacrifice flesh and they eat"** (v13) — Hebrew זִבְחֵי הַבְהָבַי, found only here: "the sacrifices of my gifts" (from יָהַב "give", doubled), uncertain; the Hebrew order kept (the phrase stands first and alone); זֶבַח · זָבַח "sacrifice" (their rows); בָּשָׂר "flesh" (its row); "it" is not written after "eat". **"but the LORD accepteth them not" → "Yahweh is not pleased with them"** — רָצָה "be pleased with" (its row; not "accept"); no "but". **"visit their sins" → "visit their sins"** — פָּקַד "visit" (its row). **"they shall return to Egypt" → "they, to Egypt they shall return"** — הֵמָּה stressed, Egypt first.
- **"For Israel hath forgotten his Maker, and buildeth temples … but I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour the palaces thereof" → "And Israel forgot his Maker and built palaces … and I will send a fire into his cities, and it shall devour her citadels"** (v14) — וַיִּשְׁכַּח, וַיִּבֶן narrative forms with "and"; עֹשֵׂהוּ "his Maker" (participle of עָשָׂה "make"); הֵיכָלוֹת "palaces" (the הֵיכָל row: "temple", of a king "palace"; here the buildings of a nation's pride beside fortified cities: "temples" is possible, said here). עָרִים בְּצֻרוֹת "fortified cities" (בָּצוּר its row). וְשִׁלַּחְתִּי "and I will send" (no "but"); בְּעָרָיו "into his cities"; אַרְמְנֹתֶיהָ "her citadels" (feminine "her": of the cities, or of the land; as Jer 49:27).

### Notes
- **Ephraim** — the name of the northern kingdom in Hosea, beside "Israel"; v9 plays פֶּרֶא "wild donkey" against אֶפְרַיִם.
