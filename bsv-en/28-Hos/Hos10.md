# Hosea 10

[1] An emptying vine is ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
fruit he sets for himself;
according to the abundance of his fruit
he has multiplied the altars;
according to the goodness of his land
they have made the pillars good.

[2] Their heart is divided;
now they shall be guilty.
He, he will break the neck of their altars;
he will lay waste their pillars.

[3] For now they will say,
"There is no king for us,
for we did not fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
and the king — what would he do for us?"

[4] They have spoken words,
swearing empty oaths, cutting a covenant;
and judgment buds like poison
on the furrows of the field.

[5] For the heifers of ⟦beth-aven|Beit Aven|Beth-aven⟧
the dwellers of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ shall be afraid;
for its people shall mourn over it,
and its idol-priests shall exult over it,
over its glory, for it has gone into exile from it.

[6] Also it, to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ it shall be brought,
an offering to King Jareb;
shame shall ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ take,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be ashamed of his counsel.

[7] Undone is ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ — her king,
like a twig on the face of the waters.

[8] And the high places of Aven shall be destroyed, the sin of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
thorn and thistle shall come up on their altars;
and they shall say to the mountains, "Cover us,"
and to the hills, "Fall on us."

[9] From the days of ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ you have sinned, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
there they stood;
war shall not overtake them in ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧
against the sons of injustice.

[10] In my desire — and I will discipline them;
and peoples shall be gathered against them
when they are bound to their two iniquities.

[11] And ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is a taught heifer,
loving to thresh;
and I, I passed over upon the goodness of her neck;
I will make ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ ride;
⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall plow;
⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall harrow for himself.

[12] Sow for yourselves for righteousness;
reap according to ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧;
break up for yourselves untilled ground;
and it is time to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
until he comes and rains righteousness for you.

[13] You have plowed wickedness;
injustice you have reaped;
you have eaten the fruit of lying;
for you trusted in your way,
in the multitude of your mighty ones.

[14] And a tumult shall rise among your people,
and all your fortresses shall be laid waste,
like the havoc of Shalman on ⟦beth-arbel|Beit Arbel|Beth-arbel⟧ in the day of war:
a mother was dashed in pieces upon sons.

[15] Thus has ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ done to you,
because of the evil of your evil;
in the dawn the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧
shall surely be undone.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** — poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line, as the accents divide them.
- **"Israel is an empty vine, he bringeth forth fruit unto himself" → "An emptying vine is Israel; fruit he sets for himself"** (v1) — Hebrew גֶּפֶן בּוֹקֵק, participle of בָּקַק "empty" (its row): a vine that empties, or is emptied (many take it from another root, "luxuriant": uncertain; the row kept); the vine first, as the Hebrew. פְּרִי יְשַׁוֶּה־לּוֹ, literally "fruit he makes equal / sets for himself" (שִׁוָּה; uncertain). **"according to the multitude of his fruit he hath increased the altars; according to the goodness of his land they have made goodly images" → "according to the abundance of his fruit he has multiplied the altars; according to the goodness of his land they have made the pillars good"** — כְּרֹב "according to the abundance"; הִרְבָּה "multiply" (as 8:11); הֵיטִיבוּ "they have made good"; מַצֵּבוֹת "pillars" (its row), not "images".
- **"Their heart is divided; now shall they be found faulty" → "Their heart is divided; now they shall be guilty"** (v2) — חָלַק "divide" (also read "be smooth": a false heart; uncertain); אָשֵׁם "be guilty" (its row). **"he shall break down their altars, he shall spoil their images" → "He, he will break the neck of their altars; he will lay waste their pillars"** — Hebrew הוּא "he", stressed; the name Yahweh is not written (it stood in the verse before: taken out); עָרַף "break the neck of" (its row, used of the altar's horns); שָׁדַד piel "lay waste" (its row); מַצֵּבוֹת "pillars".
- **"what then should a king do to us?" → "and the king — what would he do for us?"** (v3) — וְהַמֶּלֶךְ מַה־יַּעֲשֶׂה־לָּנוּ, "and the king, what would he do for us": the king named first, with the article. **"We have no king, because we feared not the LORD" → "There is no king for us, for we did not fear Yahweh"** — אֵין מֶלֶךְ לָנוּ; יָרֵא "fear" (its row). The quotation marks mark what they will say; whether it runs to the end of the verse is the reader's.
- **"They have spoken words, swearing falsely in making a covenant" → "They have spoken words, swearing empty oaths, cutting a covenant"** (v4) — אָלוֹת שָׁוְא כָּרֹת בְּרִית, two infinitives absolute: "swearing (oaths) of emptiness, cutting a covenant" (אָלָה "swear with a curse", its row; שָׁוְא "empty", its row; כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant", its row). **"thus judgment springeth up as hemlock in the furrows of the field" → "and judgment buds like poison on the furrows of the field"** — וּפָרַח "and … buds" (פָּרַח its row), no "thus"; רֹאשׁ "poison" (its row: a poisonous plant, kind unknown); עַל "on"; שָׂדָי, the poetic form of "field".
- **"The inhabitants of Samaria shall fear because of the calves of Bethaven" → "For the heifers of Beth-aven the dwellers of Samaria shall be afraid"** (v5) — לְעֶגְלוֹת first, as the Hebrew; עֶגְלוֹת is the feminine plural "heifers" (עֶגְלָה its row; the calf of 8:5–6 so named, perhaps in contempt; uncertain); שְׁכַן "the dweller(s) of" (a collective; noun of שָׁכַן "abide"); גּוּר "be afraid" (the גּוּר מִן row). Beth-aven, "house of wickedness", Hosea's name for Bethel (4:15). **"for the people thereof shall mourn over it, and the priests thereof that rejoiced on it, for the glory thereof, because it is departed from it" → "for its people shall mourn over it, and its idol-priests shall exult over it, over its glory, for it has gone into exile from it"** — כְּמָרִים "idol-priests" (its row: another word for "priest", only of other cults); יָגִילוּ, imperfect of גִּיל "be glad, exult" (as the Psalms rows; the sense beside "mourn" is not clear: "who used to exult", or a word for wailing; uncertain, the Hebrew kept); גָּלָה "go into exile" (its row: "glory has gone into exile", 1 Sam 4:21).
- **"It shall be also carried unto Assyria for a present to king Jareb" → "Also it, to Assyria it shall be brought, an offering to King Jareb"** (v6) — גַּם־אוֹתוֹ, the object first; יוּבָל "it shall be brought"; מִנְחָה "offering" (its row; "tribute" is the sense); מֶלֶךְ יָרֵב as in 5:13 ("Jareb", "he contends"; read by some as מַלְכִּי רַב "the great king": uncertain). **"Ephraim shall receive shame, and Israel shall be ashamed of his own counsel" → "shame shall Ephraim take, and Israel shall be ashamed of his counsel"** — בָּשְׁנָה "shame" (found only here, a form beside בֹּשֶׁת); לָקַח "take"; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"; עֵצָה "counsel" (its row).
- **"As for Samaria, her king is cut off as the foam upon the water" → "Undone is Samaria — her king, like a twig on the face of the waters"** (v7) — נִדְמֶה, nifal of דָּמָה "cease, be undone" (as Jer 47:5; 4:6 has "are destroyed" for the same verb: the reviewer aligns); the accents put the break after מַלְכָּהּ: "Samaria, her king" (Samaria is undone, its king with it). קֶצֶף here "twig" or "splinter" (another word than קֶצֶף "wrath"; KJV "foam": uncertain); עַל־פְּנֵי "on the face of".
- **"The high places also of Aven, the sin of Israel, shall be destroyed: the thorn and the thistle shall come up on their altars" → "And the high places of Aven shall be destroyed, the sin of Israel; thorn and thistle shall come up on their altars"** (v8) — וְנִשְׁמְדוּ "and … shall be destroyed" first, as the Hebrew; בָּמוֹת "high places" (its row); אָוֶן "Aven" ("wickedness", the אָוֶן row; short for Beth-aven); קוֹץ וְדַרְדַּר "thorn and thistle" (as Gen 3:18; חוֹחַ 9:6 has the same English: another word). **"they shall say to the mountains, Cover us; and to the hills, Fall on us"** — גִּבְעָה "hill" (its row).
- **"O Israel, thou hast sinned from the days of Gibeah" → "From the days of Gibeah you have sinned, Israel"** (v9) — the time first, as the Hebrew; no "O"; הַגִּבְעָה with the article (as 9:9). **"there they stood: the battle in Gibeah against the children of iniquity did not overtake them" → "there they stood; war shall not overtake them in Gibeah against the sons of injustice"** — עָמָדוּ "they stood"; לֹא־תַשִּׂיגֵם imperfect "shall not overtake them" (read as a question, "shall not war overtake them?", by many: the Hebrew has no question word; the statement kept); מִלְחָמָה "war" (its row); בְּנֵי עַלְוָה "the sons of injustice" (עַלְוָה is עַוְלָה with two letters exchanged, as the KJV takes it).
- **"It is in my desire that I should chastise them" → "In my desire — and I will discipline them"** (v10) — בְּאַוָּתִי וְאֶסֳּרֵם, "in my desire, and I will discipline them" (אַוָּה "desire", its row; אֶסֳּרֵם pointed from יָסַר "discipline"; the "and" kept). **"and the people shall be gathered against them, when they shall bind themselves in their two furrows" → "and peoples shall be gathered against them when they are bound to their two iniquities"** — עַמִּים "peoples" (plural); אָסַף pual "be gathered"; בְּאָסְרָם "when they are bound" (from אָסַר "bind": a play on אֶסֳּרֵם); the written text has עינתם ("their two eyes", or "springs, furrows"), the qere עוֹנֹתָם "their iniquities": the qere translated, "two" as written.
- **"And Ephraim is as an heifer that is taught, and loveth to tread out the corn" → "And Ephraim is a taught heifer, loving to thresh"** (v11) — no "as"; עֶגְלָה "heifer" (its row); מְלֻמָּדָה "taught" (pual of the לִמֵּד "teach" row); אֹהַבְתִּי "loving" (participle with an old ending); דּוּשׁ "thresh". **"but I passed over upon her fair neck" → "and I, I passed over upon the goodness of her neck"** — וַאֲנִי עָבַרְתִּי עַל־טוּב צַוָּארָהּ word for word (= I laid the yoke on her fine neck? or I spared it; uncertain); no "but". **"I will make Ephraim to ride; Judah shall plow, and Jacob shall break his clods" → "I will make Ephraim ride; Judah shall plow; Jacob shall harrow for himself"** — הִרְכִּיב "make ride" (its row: to set in harness, or to mount; uncertain); חָרַשׁ "plow"; שִׂדֵּד לוֹ "harrow for himself".
- **"Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in mercy; break up your fallow ground" → "Sow for yourselves for righteousness; reap according to chesed; break up for yourselves untilled ground"** (v12) — לִצְדָקָה "for righteousness" (צְדָקָה its row); לְפִי־חֶסֶד "according to chesed" (literally "at the mouth of"; חֶסֶד its token); נִירוּ לָכֶם נִיר, verb and noun of one root, "break up for yourselves ground not yet broken". **"for it is time to seek the LORD, till he come and rain righteousness upon you" → "and it is time to seek Yahweh, until he comes and rains righteousness for you"** — וְעֵת "and (it is) time" (no "for"); דָּרַשׁ "seek"; וְיֹרֶה, from יָרָה hifil "rain" (as the יוֹרֶה "early rain" row) or "teach" (the same letters: "and teaches you righteousness"; both in the Hebrew, the rain kept for the field picture); צֶדֶק "righteousness" (its row); לָכֶם "for you".
- **"Ye have plowed wickedness, ye have reaped iniquity" → "You have plowed wickedness; injustice you have reaped"** (v13) — רֶשַׁע "wickedness" (its row); עַוְלָתָה "injustice" (the עָוֶל row; with the old poetic ending), first, as the Hebrew. **"ye have eaten the fruit of lies" → "you have eaten the fruit of lying"** — כַּחַשׁ "lying" (noun of the כִּחֵשׁ row). **"because thou didst trust in thy way, in the multitude of thy mighty men" → "for you trusted in your way, in the multitude of your mighty ones"** — the address turns to the singular, as the Hebrew; בָּטַח "trust"; גִּבּוֹר "mighty one" (its row); בְּדַרְכְּךָ "in your way" (the Greek has "in your chariots": from memory).
- **"Therefore shall a tumult arise among thy people, and all thy fortresses shall be spoiled" → "And a tumult shall rise among your people, and all your fortresses shall be laid waste"** (v14) — וְקָאם "and … shall rise" (written with an א), no "therefore"; שָׁאוֹן "tumult" (its reviewer row); מִבְצָר "fortress" (its row); יוּשַּׁד hofal of שָׁדַד "be laid waste" (its row). **"as Shalman spoiled Betharbel in the day of battle" → "like the havoc of Shalman on Beth-arbel in the day of war"** — כְּשֹׁד, the noun "havoc" (its row): "like the havoc of Shalman (of / on) Beth-arbel" (who Shalman was, and the event, are not known); מִלְחָמָה "war". **"the mother was dashed in pieces upon her children" → "a mother was dashed in pieces upon sons"** — אֵם עַל־בָּנִים, no articles, no "her"; רֻטָּשָׁה perfect.
- **"So shall Bethel do unto you because of your great wickedness" → "Thus has Bethel done to you, because of the evil of your evil"** (v15) — כָּכָה עָשָׂה, a perfect: "thus he / it has done"; Bethel taken as the subject (the place of their worship has brought it on them; the Greek has "house of Israel": from memory); מִפְּנֵי "because of" (its row); רָעַת רָעַתְכֶם, literally "the evil of your evil" (= your utter evil). **"in a morning shall the king of Israel utterly be cut off" → "in the dawn the king of Israel shall surely be undone"** — בַּשַּׁחַר "in the dawn"; נִדְמֹה נִדְמָה, infinitive absolute with the verb of v7, literally "being undone shall be undone" ("surely", the infinitive absolute row).

### Notes
- **v8** — "say to the mountains, Cover us": quoted in Luke 23:30; Rev 6:16.
- **Beth-aven / Aven** (v5, 8) — "house of wickedness", Hosea's name for Bethel ("house of El"), v15.
