# Hosea 6

[1] "Come, and let us return to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for he has torn, and he will heal us;
he has struck, and he will bind us up.

[2] He will make us live after two days;
on the third day he will raise us up,
and we shall live before him.

[3] And let us know, let us pursue to know ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
like the dawn his going out is established,
and he will come like the rain to us,
like the latter rain, the early rain of the land."

[4] What shall I do to you, ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧?
What shall I do to you, ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?
And your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is like a cloud of the morning,
and like the dew that goes away early.

[5] Therefore I have hewn by the prophets,
I have killed them by the sayings of my mouth;
and your judgments: light goes forth.

[6] **For ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ I delight in, and not sacrifice,
and knowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ more than burnt offerings.**

[7] And they, like Adam, have overstepped the covenant;
there they have dealt treacherously with me.

[8] ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ is a town of workers of wickedness,
tracked with blood.

[9] And as raiding bands wait for a man,
so is the company of priests;
on the way they murder, toward Shechem,
for they have done depravity.

[10] In the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ I have seen a horrible thing;
there is prostitution for ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is defiled.

[11] Also, ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, he has set a harvest for you,
when I restore the fortunes of my people.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order (v10 now three lines, by the accents). The words of v1–3 are the people's (no "saying" in the Hebrew; the quotation marks are ours); with v4 Yahweh answers.
- **"Come, and let us return"** (v1) — kept: לְכוּ וְנָשׁוּבָה, a cohortative. **"he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up"** — טָרָף "he has torn" (no object), יַךְ "he has struck" (a short form; also "he strikes"); רָפָא "heal", חָבַשׁ "bind up".
- **"After two days will he revive us" → "He will make us live after two days"** (v2) — יְחַיֵּנוּ מִיֹּמָיִם, piel of חָיָה "make live"; מִיֹּמָיִם "from two days" (= after two days). **"we shall live in his sight" → "we shall live before him"** — לְפָנָיו, literally "before his face".
- **"Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the LORD" → "And let us know, let us pursue to know Yahweh"** (v3) — וְנֵדְעָה נִרְדְּפָה, two cohortatives; רָדַף "pursue". **"his going forth is prepared as the morning" → "like the dawn his going out is established"** — כְּשַׁחַר נָכוֹן מוֹצָאוֹ, the Hebrew order; נָכוֹן "established"; מוֹצָא "going out" (of the sunrise). **"he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth" → "he will come like the rain to us, like the latter rain, the early rain of the land"** — Hebrew כַגֶּשֶׁם … כְּמַלְקוֹשׁ יוֹרֶה אָרֶץ, as pointed: יוֹרֶה may be the noun "early rain" or the verb "that waters (the land)"; the Hebrew has no "and".
- **"O Ephraim … O Judah" → "Ephraim … Judah"** (v4) — no "O". **"What shall I do unto thee" → "What shall I do to you"** — מָה אֶעֱשֶׂה־לְּךָ. **"your goodness is as a morning cloud" → "your chesed is like a cloud of the morning"** — חַסְדְּכֶם, the token for חֶסֶד (as v6); וְ "and" kept; כַּעֲנַן־בֹּקֶר. **"as the early dew it goeth away" → "like the dew that goes away early"** — כַטַּל מַשְׁכִּים הֹלֵךְ, literally "like the dew, rising early, going".
- **"Therefore have I hewed them by the prophets" → "Therefore I have hewn by the prophets"** (v5) — חָצַבְתִּי בַּנְּבִיאִים, no object ("them" is not in the Hebrew; בְּ "by" or "among, against" the prophets). **"slain them by the words of my mouth" → "killed them by the sayings of my mouth"** — הֲרַגְתִּים בְּאִמְרֵי־פִי; אֵמֶר "saying". **"thy judgments are as the light that goeth forth" → "your judgments: light goes forth"** — Hebrew וּמִשְׁפָּטֶיךָ אוֹר יֵצֵא, as printed ("light" the subject of the singular verb; "your" is singular). Dividing the letters as וּמִשְׁפָּטִי כָאוֹר gives "and my judgment goes forth like the light" (so the versions, from memory); not followed.
- **"For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings" → "For chesed I delight in, and not sacrifice, and knowledge of Elohim more than burnt offerings"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי חֶסֶד חָפַצְתִּי וְלֹא־זָבַח וְדַעַת אֱלֹהִים מֵעֹלוֹת: חֶסֶד first; חָפֵץ "delight in" (a perfect of state); מֵעֹלוֹת "more than burnt offerings" (מִן of comparison; "rather than" is also read). Quoted Matt 9:13; 12:7 from the Greek ("mercy").
- **"But they like men have transgressed the covenant" → "And they, like Adam, have overstepped the covenant"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהֵמָּה כְּאָדָם עָבְרוּ בְרִית; כְּאָדָם without the article: "like Adam" (as Job 31:33); "like a man / like mankind" is the other reading, and a place Adam (Josh 3:16; "there" follows) a third — the Hebrew does not decide. עָבַר בְּרִית "overstep the covenant" (literally "pass over"). **"there have they dealt treacherously against me"** — בָּגְדוּ בִי.
- **"a city of them that work iniquity" → "a town of workers of wickedness"** (v8) — קִרְיַת פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן; קִרְיָה "town". **"is polluted with blood" → "tracked with blood"** — עֲקֻבָּה מִדָּם, from the root of עָקֵב "heel": "marked with heel-prints (footprints) from blood". The word list has "uneven / crooked" for עָקֹב here; that would say something else of a town, so the verse keeps the footprints (the reviewer decides).
- **"And as troops of robbers wait for a man, so the company of priests" → "And as raiding bands wait for a man, so is the company of priests"** (v9) — Hebrew וּכְחַכֵּי אִישׁ גְּדוּדִים חֶבֶר כֹּהֲנִים, literally "and as the waiting for a man of raiding bands, a company of priests": "so is" is supplied. **"murder in the way by consent" → "on the way they murder, toward Shechem"** — דֶּרֶךְ יְרַצְּחוּ־שֶׁכְמָה, שְׁכֶם with ה of direction (the KJV takes שְׁכֶם as "shoulder": "with one shoulder, by consent"). **"they commit lewdness" → "they have done depravity"** — כִּי זִמָּה עָשׂוּ, "for" kept.
- **"I have seen an horrible thing"** (v10) — the qere שַׁעֲרוּרִיָּה is translated; the ketiv שעריריה is another spelling of the same word. **"there is the whoredom of Ephraim" → "there is prostitution for Ephraim"** — שָׁם זְנוּת לְאֶפְרַיִם. **"defiled"** — נִטְמָא.
- **"Also, O Judah, he hath set an harvest for thee" → "Also, Judah, he has set a harvest for you"** (v11) — שָׁת, no subject; לָךְ "for you" (feminine, as the land or the people); whether the harvest is judgment or blessing is not said. **"when I returned the captivity of my people" → "when I restore the fortunes of my people"** — בְּשׁוּבִי שְׁבוּת עַמִּי, literally "in my turning back the captivity / the turning of my people"; traditional "turn the captivity". The clause ends 6:11 by the verse division; many join it to 7:1.
