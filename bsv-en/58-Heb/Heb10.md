# Hebrews 10

[1] For the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, having a shadow of the good things to come, not the very image of the things, can never, year by year with the same sacrifices which they offer perpetually, perfect those who approach.

[2] Otherwise would they not have ceased to be offered, because the worshipers, once cleansed, would no longer have any conscience of sins?

[3] But in them there is a remembrance of sins year by year,

[4] for it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.

[5] Therefore, entering into the world, he says,

"**Sacrifice and offering you did not will,
but a body you fitted for me;
[6] whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin
you took no pleasure in.
[7] Then I said, 'Behold, I have come — in the roll of the book it is written of me —
to do, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, your will.'**"

[8] Saying above, "Sacrifices and offerings and whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin you did not will, nor did you take pleasure in them" — which are offered according to ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ —

[9] then he has said, "Behold, I have come to do your will." He does away with the first that he may establish the second.

[10] In which will **we have been sanctified through the offering of the body of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ once for all**.

[11] And every priest stands day by day ministering and offering many times the same sacrifices, which can never strip away sins.

[12] But this one, having offered one sacrifice for sins perpetually, sat down at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[13] henceforth waiting until his enemies are set as a footstool of his feet,

[14] for by one offering he has perfected perpetually those who are being sanctified.

[15] And the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ also testifies to us; for after having said,

[16] "**This is the covenant that I will covenant with them
after those days, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧:
giving my laws upon their hearts,
upon their mind also I will write them**,"

[17] "**and their sins and their acts of lawlessness I will remember no more**."

[18] Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.

[19] Having therefore, brothers, boldness for the entrance of the holy places in the blood of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[20] which he inaugurated for us, a way new and living, through the veil, that is, his flesh,

[21] and a great priest over the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[22] let us approach with a true heart in full conviction of faith, having our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience and our body washed with pure water;

[23] let us hold fast the confession of the hope unwavering, for he who promised is faithful;

[24] and let us observe one another unto a stirring up of love and of good works,

[25] not forsaking the gathering together of ourselves, as is the custom of some, but encouraging, and so much the more as you see the day drawing near.

[26] For if we sin willingly after receiving the full knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins,

[27] but a certain fearful expectation of judgment and a zeal of fire that is about to eat the adversaries.

[28] Anyone who has set aside the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ dies without compassions upon two or three witnesses;

[29] of how much worse punishment, do you think, will he be counted worthy who has trampled underfoot the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and has counted common the blood of the covenant in which he was sanctified, and has insulted the Spirit of grace?

[30] For we know him who said, "**Vengeance is mine; I will repay**." And again, "**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ will judge his people**."

[31] It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[32] But call to remembrance the former days, in which, having been enlightened, you endured a great contest of sufferings,

[33] partly being made a spectacle both by reproaches and by afflictions, and partly having become partners of those who were so conducting themselves;

[34] for you both sympathized with the prisoners and accepted with joy the seizing of your possessions, knowing that you yourselves have a better possession and one that remains.

[35] Do not therefore throw away your boldness, which has great recompense,

[36] for you have need of endurance, that, having done the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you may carry off the promise;

[37] for

"**yet a very little while,
he who is coming will come and will not delay;
[38] but my righteous one will live from faith,
and if he draws back,
my soul takes no pleasure in him**."

[39] But we are not of drawing back unto destruction, but of faith unto the obtaining of the soul.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v10, 19) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v10) — systematic. The Greek has no name in v5 ("entering into the world, he says") and οὗτος, "this one", in v12 (KJV "this man").
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v15) — systematic.
- **"the law" → "the Torah"** (v1, 8, 28) — Mosaic-law context; systematic. In v8 the printed text has κατὰ νόμον without the article.
- **"the very image of the things" kept** (v1) — Greek αὐτὴν τὴν εἰκόνα τῶν πραγμάτων; πράγματα also in 11:1. The Greek has no "but" or "only" with "shadow", and "having" (ἔχων) is a participle.
- **"year by year continually" → "year by year … perpetually"; "the comers thereunto" → "those who approach"** (v1) — Greek κατʼ ἐνιαυτόν stands before "with the same sacrifices" and can also go with "can never"; εἰς τὸ διηνεκές is "perpetually" in v1, 12, 14 and 7:3; προσέρχομαι is "approach" (v22; 4:16; 7:25; 11:6; 12:18, 22).
- **"conscience of sins" kept** (v2) — Greek συνείδησιν ἁμαρτιῶν; the same word as "conscience" in v22; 9:9, 14; 13:18.
- **"remembrance" kept; "call to remembrance" kept** (v3, 32) — Greek ἀνάμνησις, ἀναμιμνῄσκεσθε: one root.
- **"take away" kept; "take away" → "strip away"; "taketh away" → "does away with"** (v4, 11, 9) — three Greek verbs: ἀφαιρεῖν, περιελεῖν ("take off from around"), ἀναιρεῖ.
- **"when he cometh into the world" → "entering into the world"** (v5) — Greek εἰσερχόμενος, a participle.
- **"thou wouldest not" → "you did not will"** (v5, 8) — Greek οὐκ ἠθέλησας, the verb of θέλημα, "will" (v7, 9, 10): what he did not will stands against the will that is done.
- **"a body hast thou prepared me" → "a body you fitted for me"** (v5) — Greek κατηρτίσω, "fit together, make complete" (11:3; 13:21); "prepare" is ἑτοιμάζω (11:16). The quotation follows the Septuagint (Ps 40:6–8 = 39:7–9 LXX); the Hebrew of Ps 40:6 has "ears you have dug for me".
- **"burnt offerings and sacrifices for sin" → "whole burnt offerings and offerings for sin"** (v6, 8) — Greek ὁλοκαυτώματα καὶ περὶ ἁμαρτίας: "for sin" has no noun in Greek ("that which is for sin", the Septuagint's name for the sin offering); "offerings" is supplied. Both are objects of "you took no pleasure in".
- **"Lo, I come (in the volume of the book it is written of me,) to do thy will, O God" → "Behold, I have come — in the roll of the book it is written of me — to do, *Elohim*, your will"** (v7) — Greek ἥκω, "I have come, I am here"; κεφαλίς is a roll; the Greek order is kept, and ὁ θεός is an address without "O".
- **"then said he" → "then he has said"** (v9) — Greek εἴρηκεν, perfect.
- **"By the which will" → "In which will"** (v10) — Greek ἐν ᾧ θελήματι.
- **"we are sanctified" → "we have been sanctified"** (v10) — Greek ἡγιασμένοι ἐσμέν, perfect; in v14 ἁγιαζομένους is present, "those who are being sanctified".
- **"daily ministering" → "day by day ministering"** (v11) — Greek λειτουργῶν, as λειτουργός "minister" (8:2).
- **"after he had offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, sat down" → "having offered one sacrifice for sins perpetually, sat down"** (v12) — Greek εἰς τὸ διηνεκές stands between "having offered one sacrifice" and "sat down" and can go with either; the printed text has no comma.
- **"From henceforth expecting" → "henceforth waiting"; "be made his footstool" → "are set as a footstool of his feet"** (v13) — Greek ἐκδεχόμενος (11:10); τεθῶσιν … ὑποπόδιον τῶν ποδῶν αὐτοῦ (Ps 110:1).
- **"is a witness" → "testifies"** (v15) — Greek μαρτυρεῖ.
- **"after that he had said before" → "after having said"; no "then he adds"** (v15–17) — the printed text has no words between the two parts of the quotation; the sentence that begins with "after having said" is not finished. It can also be read with "says the Lord" as its main clause ("after having said, 'This is the covenant …', the Lord says, 'giving my laws …'").
- **"that I will make" → "that I will covenant"** (v16) — Greek διαθήκη ἣν διαθήσομαι: noun and verb of one root.
- **"saith the Lord" → "says *Yahweh*"** (v16) — Greek λέγει κύριος; the quotation is Jer 31:33, where the Hebrew has the Name.
- **"I will put my laws into their hearts, and in their minds will I write them" → "giving my laws upon their hearts, upon their mind also I will write them"** (v16) — Greek διδοὺς … ἐπὶ καρδίας, a participle; τὴν διάνοιαν is singular (printed text).
- **"iniquities" → "acts of lawlessness"** (v17) — Greek ἀνομιῶν, plural of ἀνομία.
- **"remission" → "forgiveness"** (v18) — Greek ἄφεσις.
- **"boldness to enter into the holiest" → "boldness for the entrance of the holy places"** (v19) — Greek παρρησίαν εἰς τὴν εἴσοδον τῶν ἁγίων: a noun, "entrance", and a plural, "the holy places" (9:8, 12, 24–25; 13:11). **"by the blood" → "in the blood"** — Greek ἐν τῷ αἵματι.
- **"By a new and living way, which he hath consecrated for us" → "which he inaugurated for us, a way new and living"** (v20) — Greek ἣν ἐνεκαίνισεν: the relative refers to "entrance"; the same verb in 9:18. πρόσφατος is "recent, fresh", a different word from "new" in "new covenant".
- **"that is to say, his flesh" → "that is, his flesh"** (v20) — Greek τοῦτʼ ἔστιν τῆς σαρκὸς αὐτοῦ, a genitive: in apposition to "the veil", or depending on "way" ("the way of his flesh").
- **"having an high priest" → "a great priest"** (v21) — Greek ἱερέα μέγαν; it depends on "having" in v19.
- **"draw near" → "approach"; "full assurance of faith" → "full conviction of faith"** (v22) — Greek προσερχώμεθα; πληροφορία (6:11). "Assurance" is kept for ὑπόστασις (11:1).
- **"our bodies" → "our body"** (v22) — Greek τὸ σῶμα, singular.
- **"the profession of our faith without wavering" → "the confession of the hope unwavering"** (v23) — Greek τὴν ὁμολογίαν τῆς ἐλπίδος ἀκλινῆ: "hope", without "our"; "unwavering" is an adjective with "confession".
- **"consider one another to provoke unto love" → "observe one another unto a stirring up of love"** (v24) — Greek κατανοῶμεν (3:1); παροξυσμός is a noun; "provocation" is kept for παραπικρασμός (3:8, 15).
- **"Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together" kept as "not forsaking the gathering together of ourselves"** (v25) — Greek ἐγκαταλείποντες (13:5), τὴν ἐπισυναγωγὴν ἑαυτῶν.
- **"exhorting one another" → "encouraging"** (v25) — Greek παρακαλοῦντες without an object; παρακαλέω is "encourage" here and in 3:13, "appeal" in 13:19, 22; παράκλησις is "encouragement" (12:5; 13:22).
- **"approaching" → "drawing near"** (v25) — Greek ἐγγίζουσαν.
- **"if we sin wilfully" → "if we sin willingly"** (v26) — Greek ἑκουσίως ἁμαρτανόντων ἡμῶν, "we sinning willingly": a present participle (a continuing action), without "if".
- **"knowledge" → "full knowledge"** (v26) — Greek ἐπίγνωσιν.
- **"a certain fearful looking for of judgment and fiery indignation, which shall devour" → "a certain fearful expectation of judgment and a zeal of fire that is about to eat"** (v27) — Greek ἐκδοχή (as ἐκδέχομαι, v13); πυρὸς ζῆλος, "zeal of fire"; ἐσθίειν μέλλοντος.
- **"despised" → "has set aside"; "without mercy" → "without compassions"; "under two or three witnesses" → "upon two or three witnesses"** (v28) — Greek ἀθετήσας (as ἀθέτησις, 7:18; 9:26); οἰκτιρμῶν, plural (Phil 2:1); ἐπὶ δυσὶν ἢ τρισὶν μάρτυσιν (Deut 17:6).
- **"hath counted the blood of the covenant … an unholy thing" → "has counted common the blood of the covenant"** (v29) — Greek κοινὸν ἡγησάμενος; κοινός is "common", not set apart. **"wherewith" → "in which"** — Greek ἐν ᾧ. **"hath done despite unto" → "has insulted"** — Greek ἐνυβρίσας.
- **"Vengeance belongeth unto me, I will recompense, saith the Lord" → "Vengeance is mine; I will repay"** (v30) — the printed text has no "says the Lord" (Deut 32:35). **"The Lord shall judge his people" → "*Yahweh* will judge his people"** — Deut 32:36, where the Hebrew has the Name.
- **"a great fight of afflictions" → "a great contest of sufferings"** (v32) — Greek ἄθλησιν, an athletic contest; παθημάτων, "sufferings"; "affliction" is θλῖψις (v33).
- **"ye were made a gazingstock" → "being made a spectacle"; "companions of them that were so used" → "partners of those who were so conducting themselves"** (v33) — Greek θεατριζόμενοι (from θέατρον); κοινωνοί (Phlm 17); ἀναστρεφομένων, "conducting themselves, living", is not a passive.
- **"ye had compassion of me in my bonds" → "you sympathized with the prisoners"** (v34) — printed text τοῖς δεσμίοις; KJV follows the reading "my bonds". Greek συνεπαθήσατε (4:15).
- **"took joyfully the spoiling of your goods" → "accepted with joy the seizing of your possessions"** (v34) — Greek προσεδέξασθε; ἁρπαγή; τῶν ὑπαρχόντων and ὕπαρξιν ("possession") are one root.
- **"knowing in yourselves that ye have in heaven a better and an enduring substance" → "knowing that you yourselves have a better possession and one that remains"** (v34) — the printed text has ἑαυτούς ("yourselves", the subject of "have") and no "in heaven"; μένουσαν is a participle of μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a thing: "remaining".
- **"confidence" → "boldness"; "great recompence of reward" → "great recompense"** (v35) — Greek παρρησίαν, as v19; μισθαποδοσίαν (2:2; 11:26).
- **"patience" → "endurance"; "receive" → "carry off"** (v36) — Greek ὑπομονῆς; κομίσησθε, "carry off for oneself" (11:19, 39), a different verb from "receive" (λαμβάνω, v26; 11:13).
- **"For yet a little while" → "for yet a very little while"** (v37) — Greek μικρὸν ὅσον ὅσον (Isa 26:20 LXX); there is no "and" before "he who is coming".
- **"he that shall come" → "he who is coming"; "tarry" → "delay"** (v37) — Greek ὁ ἐρχόμενος, present; χρονίσει (Hab 2:3 LXX).
- **"Now the just shall live by faith" → "but my righteous one will live from faith"** (v38) — printed text ὁ δὲ δίκαιός μου ἐκ πίστεως; "by faith" is kept for the dative πίστει (ch. 11).
- **"if any man draw back" → "if he draws back"; "shall have no pleasure" → "takes no pleasure"** (v38) — Greek ἐὰν ὑποστείληται has no new subject; εὐδοκεῖ is present, the verb of v6, 8.
- **"we are not of them who draw back unto perdition; but of them that believe to the saving of the soul" → "we are not of drawing back unto destruction, but of faith unto the obtaining of the soul"** (v39) — Greek οὐκ ἐσμὲν ὑποστολῆς … ἀλλὰ πίστεως: two nouns in the genitive, no "those who"; ἀπώλειαν; περιποίησιν, "obtaining, gaining, preserving" (1 Thess 5:9).

### Notes
- **v5-7** quotes Ps 40:6-8 (LXX).
- **v16-17** quotes Jer 31:33-34.
- **v30** quotes Deut 32:35-36.
- **v37-38** quotes Hab 2:3-4 (LXX), with words of Isa 26:20; in the Septuagint the two lines of v38 stand in the opposite order.
- **v29** — the printed text ends the sentence with a full stop; it is a question in sense ("of how much worse punishment, do you think …").
