# Hebrews 3

[1] Hence, holy brothers, partakers of a heavenly calling, observe the apostle and high priest of our confession, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[2] being faithful to him who made him, as also ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ in all his house.

[3] For this one has been counted worthy of more glory than ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, by as much as he who built it has more honor than the house.

[4] For every house is built by someone, but he who built all things is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ indeed was faithful in all his house as an attendant, for a testimony of the things that were to be spoken,

[6] but ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ as a son over his house; whose house are we, if we hold fast the boldness and the boast of the hope.

[7] Therefore, as the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ says,

"**Today, if you hear his voice,**

**[8] do not harden your hearts as in the provocation,
according to the day of the trial in the wilderness,**

**[9] where your fathers tempted by testing
and saw my works**

**[10] forty years.
Therefore I was angry with this generation
and said, 'They always go astray in the heart;
but they themselves did not know my ways';**

**[11] as I swore in my wrath,
'They shall not enter into my rest.'**"

[12] See, brothers, that there will not be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, in departing from the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[13] but exhort one another each day, as long as it is called "Today," that no one of you be hardened by deceit of sin;

[14] for we have become partakers of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, if indeed we hold fast the beginning of the assurance firm until the end,

[15] while it is said,

"**Today, if you hear his voice,
do not harden your hearts as in the provocation**."

[16] For who, having heard, provoked? But was it not all those who came out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ through ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧?

[17] And with whom was he angry forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose corpses fell in the wilderness?

[18] And to whom did he swear that they would not enter into his rest, if not to those who disobeyed?

[19] And we see that they were not able to enter because of unbelief.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 12) — systematic. In v2, 5, 6 the Greek has "his house" (τῷ οἴκῳ αὐτοῦ, in v2 and v5 "all his house"), not "God's house"; "his" can be *Elohim*'s (Num 12:7) or, in v6, the son's own.
- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v1) — the printed text has Ἰησοῦν only, and the name stands last in the verse. In v3 the Greek has "this one" (οὗτος), not the name.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ *Mashiach*, "anointed one"; Greek Χριστός *Christos* is a translation of the Hebrew; restored to the original. In v6 the Greek has no article, in v14 it has one.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v7) — systematic; Greek τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον.
- **"Wherefore" → "Hence"; "partakers of the heavenly calling" → "partakers of a heavenly calling"** (v1) — Greek ὅθεν, as 2:17; κλήσεως ἐπουρανίου has no article. μέτοχοι as v14 and 1:9.
- **"consider" → "observe"** (v1) — Greek κατανοήσατε, "fix the mind on, look closely at" (as Jas 1:23).
- **"profession" → "confession"** (v1) — Greek ὁμολογίας; also 4:14; 10:23.
- **"Who was faithful to him that appointed him" → "being faithful to him who made him"** (v2) — Greek πιστὸν ὄντα, a present participle; τῷ ποιήσαντι αὐτόν, "the one who made him": made him what he is (apostle and high priest), so "appointed"; the Greek word is the plain "make".
- **"in all his house" kept** (v2) — the verse follows the reading with ὅλῳ, "all" (WH, Treg, NA28); the SBLGNT (Holmes, with the WH margin) leaves it out in v2. All have it in v5.
- **"this man" → "this one"; "he who hath builded the house" → "he who built it"** (v3) — Greek οὗτος; ὁ κατασκευάσας αὐτόν. κατασκευάζω is "build, fit out, prepare" (9:2, 6; 11:7 "prepare").
- **"he that built all things is God" kept** (v4) — Greek θεός without the article, as predicate. The verse is not in brackets in the Greek.
- **"servant" → "attendant"** (v5) — Greek θεράπων, one who attends on another by free service (Num 12:7 in the Septuagint); only here in the New Testament, and not "slave" (δοῦλος) or "servant" (διάκονος).
- **"those things which were to be spoken after" → "the things that were to be spoken"** (v5) — Greek τῶν λαληθησομένων, a future passive participle; no "later" in the Greek.
- **"But Christ as a son over his own house" → "but the *Mashiach* as a son over his house"** (v6) — the Greek has no verb; "was faithful" (v5) carries over.
- **"whose house are we" kept** (v6) — the verse follows οὗ οἶκός ἐσμεν ἡμεῖς, "whose house we are" (WH, Treg, NA28); the SBLGNT (Holmes) alone reads ὃς οἶκος, "which house we are".
- **"if we hold fast the confidence and the rejoicing of the hope firm unto the end" → "if we hold fast the boldness and the boast of the hope"** (v6) — Greek παρρησίαν, "boldness, open speech" (4:16; 10:19, 35); καύχημα, "boast". The verse has no "firm unto the end" here (SBLGNT, NA28; WH and Treg have μέχρι τέλους βεβαίαν; on the tie NA28 decides; the words stand in v14) and plain "if" (ἐάν: SBLGNT, WH, Treg; NA28 ἐάνπερ, "if indeed").
- **"if ye will hear his voice" → "if you hear his voice"** (v7, 15) — Greek ἐὰν … ἀκούσητε.
- **"in the provocation" → "as in the provocation" (kept)** (v8, 15) — Greek παραπικρασμός, "embittering" (from πικρός, "bitter"), the Septuagint's rendering of the place name Meribah; the verb in v16 ("provoked").
- **"in the day of temptation" → "according to the day of the trial"** (v8) — Greek κατὰ τὴν ἡμέραν τοῦ πειρασμοῦ, the Septuagint's rendering of Massah. πειρασμός is "trial" and its verb πειράζω "tempt" (v9; 2:18; 4:15): one root in Greek.
- **"When your fathers tempted me, proved me" → "where your fathers tempted by testing"** (v9) — the printed text reads οὗ ἐπείρασαν οἱ πατέρες ὑμῶν ἐν δοκιμασίᾳ, "where your fathers tried in a testing"; it has no "me". The KJV's "tempted me, proved me" follows the later text (and the Septuagint).
- **"forty years"** (v9–10) — in the letter "forty years" goes with "saw my works" and "Therefore" (διό) begins a new sentence; in the Psalm it goes with "I was angry" (so the author himself in v17).
- **"I was grieved with that generation" → "I was angry with this generation"** (v10) — Greek προσώχθισα, "be angry with, loathe"; the printed text has "this" (ταύτῃ).
- **"They do alway err in their heart" → "They always go astray in the heart"** (v10) — Greek πλανῶνται, middle or passive of the verb "deceive, lead astray": "they go astray" or "they are led astray". The Greek has "the heart", without "their".
- **"and they have not known my ways" → "but they themselves did not know my ways"** (v10) — Greek αὐτοὶ δὲ οὐκ ἔγνωσαν.
- **"So I sware in my wrath, They shall not enter into my rest" → "as I swore in my wrath, 'They shall not enter into my rest.'"** (v11) — Greek ὡς ὤμοσα … Εἰ εἰσελεύσονται, literally "if they shall enter into my rest". This is the Hebrew oath formula of Ps 95:11 (אִם), kept word for word in the Greek: "if" with the consequence left unsaid ("if they enter, may …"). It is a sworn "they shall not enter", and the author says so in his own words in v18–19. The verse gives that meaning, because a literal "if" would tell the English reader the opposite of what is said (also 4:3, 5).
- **"Take heed, brethren, lest there be" → "See, brothers, that there will not be"** (v12) — Greek βλέπετε … μήποτε ἔσται (as Col 2:8).
- **"an evil heart of unbelief" kept; "in departing from" kept** (v12) — Greek καρδία πονηρὰ ἀπιστίας, a plain genitive; ἐν τῷ ἀποστῆναι, "in standing away from". "Living *Elohim*" has no article in the Greek.
- **"exhort" kept** (v13) — Greek παρακαλεῖτε; "exhort" in this letter where it urges, "encouragement" for the noun. ἑαυτούς is "yourselves", here "one another".
- **"through the deceitfulness of sin" → "by deceit of sin"** (v13) — Greek ἀπάτῃ τῆς ἁμαρτίας.
- **"we are made partakers of Christ" → "we have become partakers of the *Mashiach*"** (v14) — Greek μέτοχοι τοῦ Χριστοῦ γεγόναμεν; the genitive is "sharers in the *Mashiach*" or "partners of the *Mashiach*".
- **"the beginning of our confidence stedfast unto the end" → "the beginning of the assurance firm until the end"** (v14) — Greek τὴν ἀρχὴν τῆς ὑποστάσεως: ὑπόστασις, "what stands under", is the word of 1:3 ("substance") and 11:1; here the firm standing, the assurance, we began with. No "our" in the Greek.
- **"For some, when they had heard, did provoke: howbeit not all" → "For who, having heard, provoked? But was it not all those"** (v16) — the printed text has the question word τίνες, and ἀλλʼ οὐ begins a second question; the KJV read τινές, "some".
- **"carcases" → "corpses"** (v17) — Greek κῶλα, "limbs", in the Septuagint dead bodies (Num 14:29).
- **"to them that believed not" → "to those who disobeyed"** (v18) — Greek ἀπειθήσασιν, "refuse to be persuaded, disobey"; "unbelief" in v19 is a different word (ἀπιστία).

### Notes
- **v7-11** quotes Ps 95:7-11, in the Greek wording of the letter.
- **v12–15** — one sentence in Greek; v15 can also begin a new sentence that runs into v16.
