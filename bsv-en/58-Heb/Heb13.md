# Hebrews 13

[1] Let brotherly love abide.

[2] Do not forget hospitality, for through this **some have entertained angels unawares**.

[3] Be mindful of the prisoners, as bound with them; of the ill-treated, as being yourselves also in a body.

[4] Let marriage be honorable among all, and the bed undefiled, for the sexually immoral and adulterers ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will judge.

[5] Let the manner of life be without love of money, being content with the things at hand; for he himself has said, "**I will by no means let you go, nor will I by any means forsake you**";

[6] so that we, being of good courage, say,

"**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ is my helper,
and I will not fear;
what will man do to me?**"

[7] Remember those who lead you, who spoke to you the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; contemplating the outcome of their conduct, imitate their faith.

[8] **⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is the same yesterday and today, and unto the ages.**

[9] Do not be carried away by various and strange teachings; for it is good that the heart be confirmed by grace, not by foods, in which those who walk were not profited.

[10] We have an altar from which those who serve the tent have no authority to eat.

[11] For the bodies of those animals whose blood is brought for sin into the holy places through the high priest are burned up outside the camp;

[12] therefore ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ also, that he might sanctify the people through his own blood, suffered outside the gate.

[13] So then let us go out to him outside the camp, bearing his reproach;

[14] for we do not have here a city that remains, but we seek after the one to come.

[15] Through him therefore let us offer up a sacrifice of praise at all times to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that is, the fruit of lips confessing to his name.

[16] But do not forget doing good and fellowship, for with such sacrifices ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is well pleased.

[17] Be persuaded by those who lead you, and yield, for they themselves keep watch on behalf of your souls as those who will render an account, that they may do this with joy and not groaning, for this would be unprofitable for you.

[18] Pray for us, for we are persuaded that we have a good conscience, wanting to conduct ourselves well in all things.

[19] And more abundantly I appeal to you to do this, that I may be restored to you sooner.

[20] Now the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of peace, who brought up from the dead the shepherd of the sheep, the great one, in the blood of an eternal covenant, our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[21] may he fit you in every good thing to do his will, doing in us what is well-pleasing before him through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, to whom be the glory unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

[22] Now I appeal to you, brothers, bear with the word of encouragement, for indeed I have written to you in few words.

[23] Know that our brother ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ has been released, with whom, if he comes sooner, I will see you.

[24] Greet all those who lead you and all the saints. Those from ⟦italy|Italia|Italy⟧ greet you.

[25] Grace be with you all.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v8, 12, 20, 21) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v8, 21) — systematic dual substitution.
- **"continue" → "abide"** (v1) — Greek μενέτω; the same verb of a thing is "remain": "a city that remains" (v14).
- **"Be not forgetful to entertain strangers" → "Do not forget hospitality"** (v2) — Greek τῆς φιλοξενίας μὴ ἐπιλανθάνεσθε: φιλοξενία, "love of strangers", stands beside φιλαδελφία, "brotherly love" (v1); the same verb "forget" in v16. **"thereby" → "through this"; "some have entertained angels unawares" kept** — Greek ἔλαθόν τινες ξενίσαντες, "some escaped notice in entertaining".
- **"Remember them that are in bonds" → "Be mindful of the prisoners"** (v3) — Greek μιμνῄσκεσθε (2:6), a different verb from "remember" in v7; τῶν δεσμίων (10:34). **"them which suffer adversity" → "the ill-treated"** — Greek κακουχουμένων (11:37).
- **"as being yourselves also in the body" → "as being yourselves also in a body"** (v3) — Greek ἐν σώματι, without the article.
- **"Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled" → "Let marriage be honorable among all, and the bed undefiled"** (v4) — the Greek has no verb: it can be a statement ("is") or a call ("let … be"); the commands around it and "for" favor the call. ἐν πᾶσιν is "among all" or "in all things". The Greek has κοίτη, "bed", without "marriage".
- **"whoremongers" → "the sexually immoral"** (v4) — Greek πόρνους (12:16).
- **"Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have" → "Let the manner of life be without love of money, being content with the things at hand"** (v5) — Greek ἀφιλάργυρος ὁ τρόπος, without a verb and without "your"; τρόπος is "manner, way"; ἀρκούμενοι τοῖς παροῦσιν, a participle.
- **"I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee" → "I will by no means let you go, nor will I by any means forsake you"** (v5) — Greek οὐ μή σε ἀνῶ οὐδʼ οὐ μή σε ἐγκαταλίπω: a doubled and a tripled negative; ἀνίημι, "let go, loosen"; ἐγκαταλείπω as 10:25. "He himself" is αὐτός.
- **"So that we may boldly say" → "so that we, being of good courage, say"** (v6) — Greek θαρροῦντας (2 Cor 5:6), not the word "boldness" (παρρησία).
- **"The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me" → "*Yahweh* is my helper, and I will not fear; what will man do to me?"** (v6) — the verse follows καὶ οὐ φοβηθήσομαι (Treg NA28 RP); the SBLGNT prints οὐ φοβηθήσομαι without "and" (WH). ποιήσει is future. The quotation is Ps 118:6 (117:6 LXX), where the Hebrew has the Name.
- **"them which have the rule over you" → "those who lead you"** (v7, 17, 24) — Greek τῶν ἡγουμένων ὑμῶν, a participle of ἡγέομαι, "lead"; in its other sense the verb is "count" (10:29; 11:11, 26).
- **"whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation" → "contemplating the outcome of their conduct, imitate their faith"** (v7) — Greek ἀναθεωροῦντες; ἔκβασιν; ἀναστροφῆς (as ἀναστρέφεσθαι, v18); μιμεῖσθε (as μιμητής "imitator", 6:12).
- **"the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever" → "is the same yesterday and today, and unto the ages"** (v8) — Greek ἐχθὲς καὶ σήμερον ὁ αὐτός, καὶ εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας: no verb ("is" is supplied); the plural "ages", as in v21.
- **"Be not carried about with divers and strange doctrines" → "Do not be carried away by various and strange teachings"** (v9) — Greek παραφέρεσθε; διδαχαῖς (6:2); ξέναις, as "strangers" in 11:13.
- **"that the heart be established with grace; not with meats, which have not profited them that have been occupied therein" → "that the heart be confirmed by grace, not by foods, in which those who walk were not profited"** (v9) — Greek βεβαιοῦσθαι, "be made firm" (2:3; βέβαιος "firm"); βρώμασιν (9:10); ἐν οἷς … οἱ περιπατοῦντες, "those who walk in them".
- **"they have no right to eat which serve the tabernacle" → "those who serve the tent have no authority to eat"** (v10) — Greek ἐξουσίαν; τῇ σκηνῇ λατρεύοντες (8:5): λατρεύω, elsewhere "worship", with a thing in the dative.
- **"the bodies of those beasts, whose blood is brought into the sanctuary by the high priest for sin" → "the bodies of those animals whose blood is brought for sin into the holy places through the high priest"** (v11) — Greek ζῴων; περὶ ἁμαρτίας (10:6, 18), without "sacrifice"; εἰς τὰ ἅγια, plural (9:8, 12, 24–25; 10:19); διὰ τοῦ ἀρχιερέως.
- **"without the camp" / "without the gate" → "outside the camp" / "outside the gate"** (v11–13) — Greek ἔξω.
- **"Let us go forth therefore unto him" → "So then let us go out to him"; "bearing his reproach" kept** (v13) — Greek ἐξερχώμεθα; τὸν ὀνειδισμὸν αὐτοῦ φέροντες (11:26): "his reproach", without "that he endured".
- **"no continuing city, but we seek one to come" → "a city that remains, but we seek after the one to come"** (v14) — Greek μένουσαν, a participle of μένω, of a thing "remain" (as 10:34; 12:27); τὴν μέλλουσαν, with the article; ἐπιζητοῦμεν (11:14).
- **"let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually" → "let us offer up a sacrifice of praise at all times to *Elohim*"** (v15) — Greek ἀναφέρωμεν (7:27); διὰ παντός, a different expression from "perpetually" (10:1).
- **"giving thanks to his name" → "confessing to his name"** (v15) — Greek ὁμολογούντων τῷ ὀνόματι αὐτοῦ; ὁμολογέω is "confess" (11:13; ὁμολογία 10:23).
- **"to do good and to communicate forget not" → "do not forget doing good and fellowship"** (v16) — Greek εὐποιΐας καὶ κοινωνίας, two nouns; κοινωνία is "fellowship", here the sharing of what one has.
- **"with such sacrifices God is well pleased" kept** (v16) — Greek εὐαρεστεῖται, passive of the verb of 11:5–6.
- **"Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves" → "Be persuaded by those who lead you, and yield"** (v17) — Greek πείθεσθε, the verb of "we are persuaded" in v18, in this form also "obey"; ὑπείκετε, "give way", only here in the New Testament (ὑποτάσσομαι is "be subject").
- **"they watch for your souls, as they that must give account" → "they themselves keep watch on behalf of your souls as those who will render an account"** (v17) — Greek αὐτοί; ἀγρυπνοῦσιν, "stay awake"; λόγον ἀποδώσοντες, a future participle; λόγος is here "account" (4:13).
- **"with grief" → "groaning"** (v17) — Greek στενάζοντες.
- **"we trust we have a good conscience, in all things willing to live honestly" → "we are persuaded that we have a good conscience, wanting to conduct ourselves well in all things"** (v18) — printed text πειθόμεθα; καλήν … καλῶς, one word twice ("good … well"); ἀναστρέφεσθαι.
- **"I beseech you the rather to do this" → "more abundantly I appeal to you to do this"** (v19) — Greek περισσοτέρως (2:1); παρακαλῶ, "appeal" here and in v22; the Greek has no "you" as object. **"the sooner" → "sooner"** — Greek τάχιον, as in v23.
- **"that brought again from the dead our Lord Jesus, that great shepherd of the sheep, through the blood of the everlasting covenant" → "who brought up from the dead the shepherd of the sheep, the great one, in the blood of an eternal covenant, our Lord *Yeshua*"** (v20) — the Greek order is kept: "in the blood" (ἐν αἵματι) can go with "brought up" or with "the great shepherd"; διαθήκης αἰωνίου has no article; ἀναγαγών, "lead up".
- **"Make you perfect in every good work to do his will, working in you" → "may he fit you in every good thing to do his will, doing in us"** (v21) — Greek καταρτίσαι, a wish (optative), "fit together, make complete" (10:5; 11:3), not the verb "perfect" (τελειόω); printed text ἐν παντὶ ἀγαθῷ, without "work", and ἐν ἡμῖν, "in us" (WH NA28; Treg RP: "work", "in you"); ποιῆσαι … ποιῶν is one verb twice.
- **"that which is wellpleasing in his sight" → "what is well-pleasing before him"** (v21) — Greek τὸ εὐάρεστον ἐνώπιον αὐτοῦ.
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v21) — the verse follows εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων (WH Treg NA28 RP); the SBLGNT prints εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας only (Holmes).
- **"suffer the word of exhortation" → "bear with the word of encouragement"** (v22) — Greek ἀνέχεσθε; τοῦ λόγου τῆς παρακλήσεως (12:5), without "my". **"I have written a letter unto you in few words" kept as "I have written to you in few words"** — Greek ἐπέστειλα, "sent a letter"; διὰ βραχέων.
- **"is set at liberty" → "has been released"; "if he come shortly" → "if he comes sooner"** (v23) — Greek ἀπολελυμένον, "released" or "sent off"; τάχιον. γινώσκετε is a command ("know") or a statement ("you know").
- **"They of Italy salute you" → "Those from *Italia* greet you"** (v24) — Greek οἱ ἀπὸ τῆς Ἰταλίας: people from Italy, whether in Italy or away from it.
- **"Grace be with you all. Amen." → "Grace be with you all."** (v25) — Greek ἡ χάρις, with the article; the verse has no "Amen" (WH NA28; Treg RP add it).

### Notes
- **v5** quotes Deut 31:6, 8 (compare Josh 1:5); the wording is not that of the Septuagint in any one place.
- **v6** quotes Ps 118:6 (LXX 117:6).
- **v11** echoes Lev 16:27.
- **v20–21** — one sentence in Greek, a wish.
