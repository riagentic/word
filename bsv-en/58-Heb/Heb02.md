# Hebrews 2

[1] Because of this we must give heed the more abundantly to the things that were heard, lest we drift away.

[2] For if the word spoken through angels became firm, and every transgression and disobedience received a just recompense,

[3] how shall we escape, having neglected so great a salvation, which, having had its beginning in being spoken through the Lord, was confirmed unto us by those who heard,

[4] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ also bearing witness with them, both by signs and wonders and various powers and distributions of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ according to his own will?

[5] For not to angels did he subject the inhabited earth to come, about which we are speaking.

[6] But someone testified somewhere, saying,

"**What is man, that you remember him,
or a son of man, that you visit him?
[7] You made him a little lower than angels;
with glory and honor you crowned him,
[8] you subjected all things under his feet**."

For in subjecting all things to him he left nothing unsubjected to him. But now we do not yet see all things subjected to him;

[9] but the one made a little lower than angels we look upon, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, because of the suffering of death crowned with glory and honor, so that by the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ he might taste death for everyone.

[10] For it befitted him, on account of whom are all things and through whom are all things, in bringing many sons unto glory, to perfect the author of their salvation through sufferings.

[11] For both he who sanctifies and those who are sanctified are all of one; for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brothers,

[12] saying,

"**I will declare your name to my brothers;
in the midst of the church I will sing praise to you**."

[13] And again,

"**I will be confident in him**."

And again,

"**Behold, I and the young children whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave me**."

[14] Since then the young children have shared in blood and flesh, he himself also in like manner partook of the same things, that through death he might bring to nothing him who has the might of death, that is, the devil,

[15] and release those, as many as by fear of death were through all their living liable to slavery.

[16] For surely it is not angels he lays hold of, but he lays hold of the seed of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[17] Hence he ought in all things to be made like the brothers, that he might become a merciful and faithful high priest in the things pertaining to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to make propitiation for the sins of the people;

[18] for in that he himself has suffered, having been tempted, he is able to help those who are being tempted.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 9, 13, 17) — systematic; see heb01. In v5 the Greek has no "God" ("did he subject").
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v9) — Hebrew יֵשׁוּעַ *Yeshua*, "YHWH saves"; Greek Ἰησοῦς is a transliteration; restored to the original Hebrew form throughout.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v4) — Greek πνεύματος ἁγίου, here without the article.
- **"we ought to give the more earnest heed to the things which we have heard, lest at any time we should let them slip" → "we must give heed the more abundantly to the things that were heard, lest we drift away"** (v1) — Greek δεῖ ("it is necessary"), περισσοτέρως ("more abundantly"), τοῖς ἀκουσθεῖσιν (a passive: "the things heard"); παραρυῶμεν is "flow past, drift away", with no "from it".
- **"was stedfast" → "became firm"; "confirmed" kept** (v2, 3) — Greek ἐγένετο βέβαιος, ἐβεβαιώθη: one root; also 3:14.
- **"disobedience" kept** (v2) — Greek παρακοή, "hearing amiss, refusing to hear" (it answers "the things that were heard" in v1); a different word from the "disobedience" of 3:18; 4:6, 11 (ἀπείθεια).
- **"a just recompence of reward" → "a just recompense"** (v2) — Greek μισθαποδοσίαν, one word, "payment of wages".
- **"if we neglect" → "having neglected"** (v3) — Greek ἀμελήσαντες, a participle; the question runs to the end of v4.
- **"at the first began to be spoken by the Lord" → "having had its beginning in being spoken through the Lord"** (v3) — Greek ἀρχὴν λαβοῦσα λαλεῖσθαι διὰ τοῦ κυρίου, literally "having received a beginning of being spoken"; διά is "through". The Greek has "those who heard", with no "him".
- **"God also bearing them witness" → "*Elohim* also bearing witness with them"** (v4) — Greek συνεπιμαρτυροῦντος, "bearing witness in addition, together with"; "them" renders the συν- of the verb.
- **"divers miracles, and gifts of the Holy Ghost" → "various powers and distributions of the *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v4) — Greek δυνάμεσιν, "powers" (deeds of power; δύναμις as 1:3); μερισμοῖς, "apportionings, distributions" (the same word in 4:12, "division"). The genitive can mean that the Spirit is distributed or that the Spirit distributes.
- **"the world to come" → "the inhabited earth to come"** (v5) — Greek τὴν οἰκουμένην τὴν μέλλουσαν, as 1:6.
- **"put in subjection" → "subject"** (v5, 8) — Greek ὑποτάσσω, five times in v5–8; "unsubjected" (v8) is ἀνυπότακτον, of the same root (elsewhere, of persons, "insubordinate").
- **"one in a certain place testified" → "someone testified somewhere"** (v6) — Greek διεμαρτύρατο δέ πού τις, "solemnly testified".
- **"that thou art mindful of him" → "that you remember him"; "visitest" → "visit"** (v6) — Greek μιμνῄσκῃ; ἐπισκέπτῃ, "look upon, visit, look after" (as Jas 1:27). "Son of man" has no article in the Greek.
- **"a little lower than the angels" → "a little lower than angels"** (v7, 9) — Greek βραχύ τι παρʼ ἀγγέλους. βραχύ τι is "some little": a little in degree, or for a little while; English cannot keep both, and "a little" is the plain wording. In the Hebrew of Ps 8:5 the word is one of degree.
- **"and didst set him over the works of thy hands" not in the verse** (v7) — the SBLGNT and NA28 do not have this clause; WH and Treg have it (καὶ κατέστησας αὐτὸν ἐπὶ τὰ ἔργα τῶν χειρῶν σου), as does the Septuagint of Ps 8. On the tie NA28 decides.
- **"For in that he put all in subjection under him" → "For in subjecting all things to him"** (v8) — the verse follows the reading with αὐτῷ, "to him" (WH, Treg, NA28); the SBLGNT (Holmes, with the WH margin) leaves it out.
- **"But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower" → "but the one made a little lower than angels we look upon, *Yeshua*"** (v9) — the Greek order is kept: the name comes after the description. "Look upon" is βλέπομεν, a different verb from "see" in v8 (ὁρῶμεν). "Because of the suffering of death" stands between "made lower" and "crowned" and can be read with either.
- **"by the grace of God" kept as "by the grace of *Elohim*"** (v9) — the verse follows χάριτι θεοῦ (WH, Treg, NA28, and the Byzantine text). The SBLGNT (Holmes) alone reads χωρὶς θεοῦ, "apart from *Elohim*" / "without *Elohim*". ὑπὲρ παντός is "for everyone" or "for everything".
- **"it became him" → "it befitted him"** (v10) — Greek ἔπρεπεν (as Titus 2:1). "Bringing" (ἀγαγόντα) is a participle that agrees with the unexpressed subject of "to perfect" (he "on account of whom are all things") or with "the author".
- **"for whom … by whom" → "on account of whom … through whom"** (v10) — Greek διʼ ὅν … διʼ οὗ; the Greek has no verb ("are" is supplied).
- **"to make the captain of their salvation perfect" → "to perfect the author of their salvation"** (v10) — Greek ἀρχηγόν, "one who leads the way, originator" (also 12:2); τελειῶσαι, "bring to the goal, make complete". "Sufferings" is plural.
- **"all of one" kept** (v11) — Greek ἐξ ἑνὸς πάντες; "one" can be a person or a thing, and no noun is added.
- **"in the midst of the church will I sing praise unto thee" kept** (v12) — Greek ἐν μέσῳ ἐκκλησίας, "assembly"; ἐκκλησία is "church" in every book. ὑμνήσω σε is "I will hymn you".
- **"I will put my trust in him" → "I will be confident in him"** (v13) — Greek ἔσομαι πεποιθὼς ἐπʼ αὐτῷ, "I will be one who has put his confidence upon him".
- **"the children" → "the young children"** (v13, 14) — Greek παιδία, as in 1 John 2:14, 18. "Gave" is ἔδωκεν, a past tense.
- **"are partakers of flesh and blood" → "have shared in blood and flesh"** (v14) — Greek κεκοινώνηκεν αἵματος καὶ σαρκός, in that order; the verb is of the root of "fellowship" (κοινωνία). "Partook" is μετέσχεν, a different verb.
- **"destroy him that had the power of death" → "bring to nothing him who has the might of death"** (v14) — Greek καταργήσῃ, "make powerless, bring to nothing"; elsewhere "abolish", but of a person as in 2 Thess 2:8 "bring to nothing"; κράτος, "might"; ἔχοντα is a present.
- **"deliver them who … were all their lifetime subject to bondage" → "release those, as many as … were through all their living liable to slavery"** (v15) — Greek ἀπαλλάξῃ; διὰ παντὸς τοῦ ζῆν; ἔνοχοι δουλείας, "held in, liable to slavery".
- **"he took not on him the nature of angels" → "it is not angels he lays hold of"** (v16) — Greek ἐπιλαμβάνεται, "take hold of" (to help, or to take to oneself), a present tense; the Greek has no "nature" and no "help". "Seed" is σπέρματος.
- **"it behoved him" → "he ought"; "his brethren" → "the brothers"** (v17) — Greek ὤφειλεν, "he owed, was obliged"; τοῖς ἀδελφοῖς.
- **"in things pertaining to God" → "in the things pertaining to *Elohim*"** (v17) — Greek τὰ πρὸς τὸν θεόν (also 5:1); no "service" in the Greek.
- **"to make reconciliation for the sins" → "to make propitiation for the sins"** (v17) — Greek ἱλάσκεσθαι τὰς ἁμαρτίας, the verb of ἱλασμός (1 John 2:2); "the sins" is its direct object.
- **"tempted" kept** (v18) — Greek πειρασθείς, πειραζομένοις; the verb is also "test, try" (3:9), and its noun is "trial" (3:8). ἐν ᾧ is "in that" or "in that in which".

### Notes
- **v2–4** — one sentence in Greek, a question.
- **v6-8** quotes Ps 8:4-6.
- **v12** quotes Ps 22:22.
- **v13** quotes Isa 8:17-18.
