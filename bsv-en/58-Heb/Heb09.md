# Hebrews 9

[1] Now the first also indeed had ordinances of worship, and the sanctuary, a worldly one.

[2] For a tent was prepared, the first one, in which were both the lampstand and the table and the setting forth of the loaves, which is called the Holy Place;

[3] and after the second veil, a tent which is called the Holy of Holies,

[4] having a golden altar of incense and the ark of the covenant covered on all sides with gold, in which were a golden jar having the manna, and ⟦aaron|Aharon's|Aaron's⟧ rod that budded, and the tablets of the covenant,

[5] and above it ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ of glory overshadowing the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧; concerning which things it is not possible now to speak part by part.

[6] Now these things having been thus prepared, into the first tent the priests go in continually, accomplishing the services of worship,

[7] but into the second, once a year, the high priest alone, not without blood, which he offers on behalf of himself and of the people's sins of ignorance,

[8] the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ making this plain, that the way of the holy places has not yet been manifested while the first tent still has standing,

[9] which is a parable unto the present time, according to which both gifts and sacrifices are offered that are not able, as to conscience, to perfect the one who worships,

[10] being only upon foods and drinks and various washings, ordinances of flesh imposed until a time of setting right.

[11] But ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, having arrived as high priest of the good things that have come, through the greater and more perfect tent, not made with hands, that is, not of this creation,

[12] nor through the blood of goats and calves, but through his own blood, entered once for all into the holy places, having found eternal redemption.

[13] For if the blood of goats and bulls and the ashes of a heifer sprinkling those who have been defiled sanctifies unto the cleanness of the flesh,

[14] how much more will the blood of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, who through eternal Spirit offered himself without blemish to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, cleanse our conscience from dead works unto worshiping the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[15] And because of this he is mediator of a new covenant, so that, a death having taken place for redemption of the transgressions upon the first covenant, those who have been called may receive the promise of the eternal inheritance.

[16] For where there is a covenant, it is necessary that the death of the one who made it be brought;

[17] for a covenant is firm upon the dead, since it never has force while the one who made it lives.

[18] Whence not even the first has been inaugurated without blood.

[19] For every commandment having been spoken according to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ by ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ to all the people, he took the blood of the calves and the goats, with water and scarlet wool and hyssop, and sprinkled both the book itself and all the people,

[20] saying, "**This is the blood of the covenant which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded unto you**."

[21] And the tent also and all the vessels of the ministry he sprinkled likewise with the blood.

[22] And **almost all things are cleansed in blood according to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, and without shedding of blood there comes no forgiveness**.

[23] It was necessary, then, that the copies of the things in the heavens be cleansed with these, but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these.

[24] For ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ did not enter into holy places made with hands, counterparts of the true ones, but into heaven itself, now to appear before the face of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ on our behalf;

[25] nor that he should offer himself often, as the high priest enters into the holy places year by year with blood of another,

[26] since he would have had to suffer often from the foundation of the world; but now once, at the consummation of the ages, he has been manifested for the setting aside of sin through his sacrifice.

[27] And inasmuch as it is laid up for men to die once, and after this judgment,

[28] so also ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, having been offered once to bear the sins of many, will be seen a second time, without sin, by those who eagerly await him, unto salvation.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v11, 14, 24, 28) — systematic. In v11 and v24 the Greek has Χριστός without the article.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v8) — systematic.
- **"the law" → "the Torah"** (v19, 22) — Greek κατὰ τὸν νόμον; systematic.
- **"Then verily the first covenant had also ordinances of divine service, and a worldly sanctuary" → "Now the first also indeed had ordinances of worship, and the sanctuary, a worldly one"** (v1) — Greek ἡ πρώτη, without a noun (the covenant of 8:13); δικαιώματα λατρείας (λατρεία, the noun of λατρεύω "worship", v9, 14); τό τε ἅγιον κοσμικόν: the singular τὸ ἅγιον, "the sanctuary", with κοσμικόν, "belonging to the world", set after it. The verse follows οὖν καὶ ἡ πρώτη, "the first also" (WH, Treg, NA28, RP); without καί (SBLGNT, WH margin).
- **"there was a tabernacle made; the first" → "a tent was prepared, the first one"** (v2) — Greek σκηνὴ … κατεσκευάσθη ἡ πρώτη; σκηνή is "tent" throughout, and the two rooms are called "the first tent" (v6, 8) and a second "tent" (v3). The Greek has no "section".
- **"the shewbread" → "the setting forth of the loaves"** (v2) — Greek ἡ πρόθεσις τῶν ἄρτων, the Septuagint's term (2 Chr 13:11). The Greek does not say "bread of the Presence": that is the Hebrew לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים.
- **"which is called the sanctuary" → "which is called the Holy Place"; "the Holiest of all" → "the Holy of Holies"** (v2, 3) — Greek Ἅγια and Ἅγια Ἁγίων, neuter plurals: "Holies", "Holies of Holies"; the same word as "the holy places" in v8, 12, 24, 25.
- **"after the second veil" kept** (v3) — Greek μετὰ … τὸ δεύτερον καταπέτασμα; καταπέτασμα is "veil" as 6:19; 10:20. The verse has no verb.
- **"Which had the golden censer" → "having a golden altar of incense"** (v4) — Greek θυμιατήριον, "a thing for burning incense": the word is used both of a censer (2 Chr 26:19; Ezek 8:11 in the Septuagint) and of the altar of incense (Philo, Josephus). The verse says only that the Holy of Holies "has" it.
- **"the golden pot that had manna, and Aaron's rod that budded, and the tables of the covenant" → "a golden jar having the manna, and *Aharon's* rod that budded, and the tablets of the covenant"** (v4) — Greek στάμνος, "jar"; ῥάβδος, "rod" (1:8); πλάκες, flat stones.
- **"the cherubims of glory shadowing the mercyseat" → "*keruvim* of glory overshadowing the *kapporet*"** (v5) — Greek Χερουβὶν δόξης, without the article; τὸ ἱλαστήριον, "the place of propitiation", the Septuagint's word for כַּפֹּרֶת (Ex 25:17).
- **"of which we cannot now speak particularly" → "concerning which things it is not possible now to speak part by part"** (v5) — Greek οὐκ ἔστιν … κατὰ μέρος; the Greek has no "we".
- **"when these things were thus ordained, the priests went always into the first tabernacle, accomplishing the service of God" → "these things having been thus prepared, into the first tent the priests go in continually, accomplishing the services of worship"** (v6) — Greek κατεσκευασμένων, the verb of v2; εἰσίασιν, present; τὰς λατρείας, plural. ἐπιτελέω is "complete" in 8:5; here, of rites, "accomplish, carry out". The word "God" is not in the Greek.
- **"once every year, not without blood, which he offered for himself, and for the errors of the people" → "once a year, the high priest alone, not without blood, which he offers on behalf of himself and of the people's sins of ignorance"** (v7) — Greek ἅπαξ τοῦ ἐνιαυτοῦ; προσφέρει, present; ἀγνοημάτων, things done in ignorance (the root of "ignorant", 5:2). The verse has no verb "goes" and no "taking".
- **"The Holy Ghost this signifying, that the way into the holiest of all was not yet made manifest, while as the first tabernacle was yet standing" → "the *Ruach HaQodesh* making this plain, that the way of the holy places has not yet been manifested while the first tent still has standing"** (v8) — Greek δηλοῦντος; τὴν τῶν ἁγίων ὁδόν, a genitive: the way into (or of) the holy places; πεφανερῶσθαι, perfect of φανερόω "manifest" (v26); ἐχούσης στάσιν.
- **"Which was a figure for the time then present" → "which is a parable unto the present time"** (v9) — Greek ἥτις παραβολὴ εἰς τὸν καιρὸν τὸν ἐνεστηκότα; παραβολή is "parable" (11:19). "Which" can refer to the first tent or to the whole arrangement; the printed text has no parenthesis. The printed text has καθʼ ἥν, "according to which" (the parable, or the tent).
- **"that could not make him that did the service perfect, as pertaining to the conscience" → "that are not able, as to conscience, to perfect the one who worships"** (v9) — Greek μὴ δυνάμεναι κατὰ συνείδησιν τελειῶσαι τὸν λατρεύοντα; τελειόω is "perfect" throughout Hebrews.
- **"Which stood only in meats and drinks, and divers washings, and carnal ordinances, imposed on them until the time of reformation" → "being only upon foods and drinks and various washings, ordinances of flesh imposed until a time of setting right"** (v10) — Greek μόνον ἐπὶ βρώμασιν …, "only on the basis of" ("being" is supplied); the verse reads δικαιώματα σαρκός in apposition (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); καὶ δικαιώματα, "and ordinances" (Treg); καὶ δικαιώμασιν (RP); βαπτισμοῖς, "washings" (6:2); διορθώσεως, "a making straight"; καιροῦ is without the article.
- **"But Christ being come an high priest of good things to come" → "But the *Mashiach*, having arrived as high priest of the good things that have come"** (v11) — Greek παραγενόμενος, "having come to be present"; the verse reads γενομένων, "that have come" (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); the KJV follows μελλόντων, "to come" (Treg, RP; as 10:1).
- **"by a greater and more perfect tabernacle" → "through the greater and more perfect tent"** (v11) — Greek διὰ τῆς μείζονος καὶ τελειοτέρας σκηνῆς; the same διά, "through", three times in v11–12.
- **"not of this building" → "not of this creation"** (v11) — Greek κτίσεως.
- **"Neither by the blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood he entered in once into the holy place, having obtained eternal redemption for us" → "nor through the blood of goats and calves, but through his own blood, entered once for all into the holy places, having found eternal redemption"** (v12) — Greek ἐφάπαξ (7:27); τὰ ἅγια, plural; εὑράμενος, "having found"; λύτρωσιν, "a ransoming" (ἀπολύτρωσις in v15 is a compound of it). The Greek has no "for us".
- **"the ashes of an heifer sprinkling the unclean, sanctifieth to the purifying of the flesh" → "the ashes of a heifer sprinkling those who have been defiled sanctifies unto the cleanness of the flesh"** (v13) — Greek κεκοινωμένους, "made common"; καθαρότητα, the state of being clean (the root of "cleanse", v14, 22, 23).
- **"through the eternal Spirit" → "through eternal Spirit"** (v14) — Greek διὰ πνεύματος αἰωνίου, without the article; it can be read of the Holy Spirit or of his own eternal spirit.
- **"without spot" → "without blemish"; "purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God" → "cleanse our conscience from dead works unto worshiping the living *Elohim*"** (v14) — Greek ἄμωμον; καθαριεῖ; the verse reads ἡμῶν, "our" (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); ὑμῶν, "your" (Treg, RP); λατρεύειν is "worship", the service given to God (v9); θεῷ ζῶντι is without the article.
- **"for this cause he is the mediator of the new testament, that by means of death, for the redemption of the transgressions that were under the first testament, they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance" → "because of this he is mediator of a new covenant, so that, a death having taken place for redemption of the transgressions upon the first covenant, those who have been called may receive the promise of the eternal inheritance"** (v15) — Greek διαθήκης καινῆς, without the article; θανάτου γενομένου; ἐπὶ τῇ πρώτῃ διαθήκῃ, "upon, on the basis of"; κεκλημένοι, perfect. "Of the eternal inheritance" stands last and can also be joined to "those who have been called".
- **"testament" → "covenant"; "testator" → "the one who made it"** (v16–17) — Greek διαθήκη and ὁ διαθέμενος, the noun and verb of 8:10. In ordinary Greek διαθήκη is a will, and v16–17 are commonly read of a will and the one who made it; in the Septuagint and everywhere else in Hebrews it is "covenant". The Greek uses the one word through v15–20, so the verse keeps "covenant" and leaves the two readings open: (a) a will has force only when its maker has died; (b) a covenant is made firm over slain victims, which stand for the death of the one who makes it.
- **"there must also of necessity be the death of the testator" → "it is necessary that the death of the one who made it be brought"** (v16) — Greek θάνατον ἀνάγκη φέρεσθαι, "be brought, be brought forward"; not "be established".
- **"a testament is of force after men are dead: otherwise it is of no strength at all while the testator liveth" → "a covenant is firm upon the dead, since it never has force while the one who made it lives"** (v17) — Greek ἐπὶ νεκροῖς, a plural: "upon dead ones", "over the dead"; βεβαία, "firm" (6:19). The clause can also be read as a question: "since does it ever have force while the one who made it lives?"
- **"neither the first testament was dedicated without blood" → "not even the first has been inaugurated without blood"** (v18) — Greek ἡ πρώτη, without a noun; ἐγκεκαίνισται, perfect (10:20).
- **"when Moses had spoken every precept to all the people according to the law, he took the blood of calves and of goats" → "every commandment having been spoken according to the *Torah* by *Moshe* to all the people, he took the blood of the calves and the goats"** (v19) — Greek λαληθείσης … πάσης ἐντολῆς κατὰ τὸν νόμον, a passive; "according to the *Torah*", not "of the *Torah*". The verse follows τῶν μόσχων καὶ τῶν τράγων, "of the calves and the goats" (WH, Treg, NA28; RP καὶ τράγων); τῶν μόσχων alone (SBLGNT).
- **"This is the blood of the testament which God hath enjoined unto you" → "This is the blood of the covenant which *Elohim* commanded unto you"** (v20) — Ex 24:8 as the Greek prints it: ἐνετείλατο πρὸς ὑμᾶς. The Hebrew has "Behold the blood of the covenant which *Yahweh* has cut with you".
- **"he sprinkled with blood both the tabernacle, and all the vessels of the ministry" → "the tent also and all the vessels of the ministry he sprinkled likewise with the blood"** (v21) — Greek τῆς λειτουργίας, "of the ministry" (8:6); a different word from "worship" (λατρεία, v1, 6).
- **"almost all things are by the law purged with blood; and without shedding of blood is no remission" → "almost all things are cleansed in blood according to the *Torah*, and without shedding of blood there comes no forgiveness"** (v22) — Greek ἐν αἵματι; καθαρίζεται; οὐ γίνεται ἄφεσις: the Greek has no "of sins".
- **"the patterns of things in the heavens should be purified with these" → "the copies of the things in the heavens be cleansed with these"** (v23) — Greek ὑποδείγματα (8:5); τούτοις, "with these [things]": the Greek has no "rites". The clause has no verb; "it was" is supplied.
- **"the holy places made with hands, which are the figures of the true" → "holy places made with hands, counterparts of the true ones"** (v24) — Greek ἀντίτυπα τῶν ἀληθινῶν: things answering to a pattern (τύπος, 8:5).
- **"now to appear in the presence of God for us" → "now to appear before the face of *Elohim* on our behalf"** (v24) — Greek ἐμφανισθῆναι τῷ προσώπῳ τοῦ θεοῦ. Three verbs of appearing stand in v24–28: ἐμφανισθῆναι "appear" (v24), πεφανέρωται "has been manifested" (v26), ὀφθήσεται "will be seen" (v28).
- **"Nor yet that he should offer himself often" kept; "with blood of others" → "with blood of another"** (v25) — Greek πολλάκις, "often" (6:7; also v26); ἐν αἵματι ἀλλοτρίῳ, "in blood that is another's".
- **"once in the end of the world hath he appeared to put away sin by the sacrifice of himself" → "once, at the consummation of the ages, he has been manifested for the setting aside of sin through his sacrifice"** (v26) — Greek ἅπαξ, "once" ("once for all" is ἐφάπαξ, v12); συντελείᾳ τῶν αἰώνων; εἰς ἀθέτησιν (7:18) τῆς ἁμαρτίας, with the article (WH, Treg, NA28; without it SBLGNT, RP); διὰ τῆς θυσίας αὐτοῦ, "through his sacrifice" or "through the sacrifice of himself".
- **"as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment" → "inasmuch as it is laid up for men to die once, and after this judgment"** (v27) — Greek καθʼ ὅσον (7:20); ἀπόκειται, "is laid up, is in store"; τοῖς ἀνθρώποις, plural; κρίσις without the article and without a verb.
- **"Christ was once offered to bear the sins of many" → "the *Mashiach*, having been offered once to bear the sins of many"** (v28) — Greek προσενεχθείς, passive of "offer"; ἀνενεγκεῖν, elsewhere "offer up" (7:27), here "bear", the wording of Isa 53:12 in the Septuagint.
- **"unto them that look for him shall he appear the second time without sin unto salvation" → "will be seen a second time, without sin, by those who eagerly await him, unto salvation"** (v28) — Greek ὀφθήσεται; χωρὶς ἁμαρτίας, "without sin", "apart from sin" (χωρίς as v7, 18, 22): the Greek does not say "not to deal with sin". "Unto salvation" stands last and can go with "will be seen" or with "await".

### Notes
- **v20** quotes Ex 24:8.
- **v28** takes up Isa 53:12.
- **v6–10** and **v11–12** — each one sentence in Greek.
