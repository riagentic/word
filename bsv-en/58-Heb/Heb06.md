# Hebrews 6

[1] Therefore, leaving the word of the beginning of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, let us be borne on to perfection, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith upon ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[2] of a teaching of washings and of laying on of hands, of resurrection of the dead and of eternal sentence.

[3] And this we will do, if indeed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ permits.

[4] For it is impossible, as to those who were once enlightened and tasted of the heavenly gift and became partakers of ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧

[5] and tasted the good word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the powers of the age to come,

[6] and fell away, to renew them again unto repentance, crucifying again for themselves the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and putting him to open shame.

[7] For land which has drunk the rain coming often upon it, and bears vegetation fit for those on account of whom it is also tilled, receives a share of blessing from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[8] but bringing forth thorns and thistles, it is unapproved and near to a curse, whose end is unto burning.

[9] But we are persuaded concerning you, beloved, of the better things and things belonging to salvation, even if we speak thus;

[10] for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is not unrighteous, to forget your work and the love which you demonstrated unto his name, having served the saints and serving.

[11] But we desire each of you to demonstrate the same diligence toward the full conviction of the hope until the end,

[12] that you may not become sluggish, but imitators of those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

[13] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, having promised to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, since he had no one greater to swear by, swore by himself,

[14] saying: "**Surely blessing I will bless you, and multiplying I will multiply you**";

[15] and so, having been patient, he obtained the promise.

[16] For men swear by the greater, and for them the oath is an end of all contradiction, for confirmation;

[17] in which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, wishing more abundantly to show to the heirs of the promise the unchangeableness of his counsel, mediated with an oath,

[18] that through two unchangeable things, in which it is impossible for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to lie, we may have strong encouragement, we who fled for refuge to take hold of the hope set before us;

[19] which we have as an anchor of the soul, both secure and firm and entering into that which is within the veil,

[20] where a forerunner on our behalf entered, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, having become, according to the order of ⟦melchizedek|Malki-Tzedeq|Melchizedek⟧, a high priest forever.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v20) — systematic.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v4) — systematic. The Greek is πνεύματος ἁγίου, without the article.
- **"leaving the principles of the doctrine of Christ" → "leaving the word of the beginning of the *Mashiach*"** (v1) — Greek τὸν τῆς ἀρχῆς τοῦ Χριστοῦ λόγον: λόγος is "word", ἀρχή "beginning" (5:12); the genitive "of the *Mashiach*" is left as it stands.
- **"let us go on unto perfection" → "let us be borne on to perfection"** (v1) — Greek φερώμεθα, passive or middle: "be carried". τελειότης, "perfection", is the root of "the perfect" (5:14).
- **"faith toward God" → "faith upon *Elohim*"** (v1) — Greek πίστεως ἐπὶ θεόν.
- **"Of the doctrine of baptisms" → "of a teaching of washings"** (v2) — the verse follows the genitive διδαχῆς, "of a teaching" (Treg, NA28, RP); the accusative διδαχήν, "a teaching", standing beside "foundation" (SBLGNT, WH). βαπτισμῶν is the plural of βαπτισμός, "washing" (9:10; Mark 7:4), not βάπτισμα. The genitives that follow can depend on "teaching" or on "foundation".
- **"eternal judgment" → "eternal sentence"** (v2) — Greek κρίματος; κρίμα is the sentence passed, κρίσις the judging (9:27).
- **"if God permit" → "if indeed *Elohim* permits"** (v3) — Greek ἐάνπερ.
- **"For it is impossible for those who were once enlightened … to renew them again unto repentance" → "For it is impossible, as to those who were once enlightened … to renew them again unto repentance"** (v4–6) — Greek ἀδύνατον … πάλιν ἀνακαινίζειν, an active infinitive with no subject named; "impossible" stands first. All the participles are aorists: "were enlightened", "tasted", "became", "fell away".
- **"tasted of the heavenly gift" kept; "tasted the good word of God" kept** (v4, 5) — the first has the genitive (τῆς δωρεᾶς), the second the accusative (καλὸν … θεοῦ ῥῆμα); the latter can also be read "tasted that the word of *Elohim* is good". "Word" here is ῥῆμα, not λόγος.
- **"were made partakers" → "became partakers"** (v4) — Greek μετόχους γενηθέντας; μέτοχος as 3:1, 14.
- **"the world to come" → "the age to come"** (v5) — Greek μέλλοντος αἰῶνος.
- **"If they shall fall away" → "and fell away"** (v6) — Greek καὶ παραπεσόντας, one more aorist participle in the row; the Greek has no "if".
- **"seeing they crucify to themselves the Son of God afresh" → "crucifying again for themselves the Son of *Elohim*"** (v6) — Greek ἀνασταυροῦντας ἑαυτοῖς, a present participle: it can give the reason ("since they crucify") or the time ("while they crucify"). Outside the New Testament ἀνασταυρόω is simply "crucify" (ἀνά = "up"); the Greek fathers and the old versions read ἀνά here as "again". ἑαυτοῖς is "for themselves" or "to themselves"; the Greek has no "to their own harm".
- **"put him to an open shame" kept** (v6) — Greek παραδειγματίζοντας, "making a public example of"; "him" is supplied.
- **"the earth which drinketh in the rain that cometh oft upon it" → "land which has drunk the rain coming often upon it"** (v7) — Greek γῆ … ἡ πιοῦσα, an aorist participle.
- **"bringeth forth herbs meet for them by whom it is dressed" → "bears vegetation fit for those on account of whom it is also tilled"** (v7) — Greek διʼ οὕς, "on account of whom"; καί, "also".
- **"receiveth blessing" → "receives a share of blessing"** (v7) — Greek μεταλαμβάνει.
- **"that which beareth thorns and briers is rejected, and is nigh unto cursing; whose end is to be burned" → "bringing forth thorns and thistles, it is unapproved and near to a curse, whose end is unto burning"** (v8) — Greek ἀδόκιμος, "unapproved" (failing the test); εἰς καῦσιν is a noun. "Whose" (ἧς) can refer to the land or to the curse.
- **"things that accompany salvation" → "things belonging to salvation"** (v9) — Greek ἐχόμενα σωτηρίας, "things that hold close to salvation".
- **"though we thus speak" → "even if we speak thus"** (v9) — Greek εἰ καὶ οὕτως λαλοῦμεν; it stands last in the Greek.
- **"your work and labour of love" → "your work and the love"** (v10) — the printed text has no "labour" (τοῦ κόπου is a later reading).
- **"toward his name" → "unto his name"; "in that ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister" → "having served the saints and serving"** (v10) — Greek εἰς τὸ ὄνομα; διακονήσαντες … καὶ διακονοῦντες, two participles.
- **"which ye have shewed" → "which you demonstrated"; "do shew" → "to demonstrate"** (v10, 11) — Greek ἐνεδείξασθε, ἐνδείκνυσθαι, one verb.
- **"diligence" kept; "to the full assurance of hope" → "toward the full conviction of the hope"** (v11) — Greek σπουδήν; πρὸς τὴν πληροφορίαν τῆς ἐλπίδος: πληροφορία is fullness, full conviction (10:22); "assurance" stands for ὑπόστασις (3:14). The Greek has no "to have".
- **"That ye be not slothful, but followers" → "that you may not become sluggish, but imitators"** (v12) — Greek νωθροὶ γένησθε (νωθρός as 5:11); μιμηταί.
- **"when God made promise to Abraham" → "*Elohim*, having promised to *Avraham*"** (v13) — Greek ἐπαγγειλάμενος, a participle.
- **"Surely blessing I will bless thee, and multiplying I will multiply thee" kept (thee → you)** (v14) — Gen 22:17 as the Greek prints it: εὐλογῶν εὐλογήσω, πληθύνων πληθυνῶ. The Hebrew has "your seed" for the second "you".
- **"after he had patiently endured" → "having been patient"** (v15) — Greek μακροθυμήσας, the verb of "patience" (v12); "endure" is ὑπομένω. The Greek does not repeat the name Abraham.
- **"men verily swear by the greater: and an oath for confirmation is to them an end of all strife" → "men swear by the greater, and for them the oath is an end of all contradiction, for confirmation"** (v16) — Greek ἀντιλογίας, "contradiction" (7:7; 12:3). "The greater" can be masculine or neuter; the Greek has no "than themselves".
- **"Wherein God, willing more abundantly to shew" → "in which *Elohim*, wishing more abundantly to show"** (v17) — Greek ἐν ᾧ, "in which" (the oath) or "on which account"; ἐπιδεῖξαι is a different verb from "demonstrate" (ἐνδείκνυμαι) in v10–11.
- **"the immutability of his counsel, confirmed it by an oath" → "the unchangeableness of his counsel, mediated with an oath"** (v17) — Greek ἐμεσίτευσεν, the verb of μεσίτης "mediator" (8:6; 9:15): he came in between as a guarantor; no object "it" in the Greek. βουλή, "counsel", is the root of βουλόμενος, "wishing".
- **"two immutable things" → "two unchangeable things"** (v18) — Greek ἀμεταθέτων, the word of v17.
- **"a strong consolation" → "strong encouragement"** (v18) — Greek παράκλησιν; παράκλησις is "encouragement" in Hebrews (12:5; 13:22).
- **"who have fled for refuge to lay hold upon the hope" → "we who fled for refuge to take hold of the hope"** (v18) — Greek οἱ καταφυγόντες κρατῆσαι; "to take hold" can go with "fled" or with "encouragement".
- **"both sure and stedfast" → "both secure and firm"** (v19) — Greek ἀσφαλῆ τε καὶ βεβαίαν; βέβαιος is "firm" (3:14; 9:17). The Greek does not repeat "hope" in this verse.
- **"that within the veil" → "that which is within the veil"** (v19) — Greek τὸ ἐσώτερον τοῦ καταπετάσματος, "the inner side of the veil" (Lev 16:2 in the Septuagint).
- **"Whither the forerunner is for us entered, even Jesus, made an high priest for ever after the order of Melchisedec" → "where a forerunner on our behalf entered, *Yeshua*, having become, according to the order of *Malki-Tzedeq*, a high priest forever"** (v20) — Greek πρόδρομος without the article; εἰσῆλθεν, aorist; γενόμενος, "having become". The Greek order is kept: "according to the order of *Malki-Tzedeq*" stands first, "forever" last.

### Notes
- **v14** quotes Gen 22:17.
- **v4–6** — one sentence in Greek.
- **v20** takes up Ps 110:4 (5:6, 10).
