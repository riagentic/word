# Hebrews 7

[1] For this ⟦melchizedek|Malki-Tzedeq|Melchizedek⟧, king of ⟦salem|Shalem|Salem⟧, priest of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ Most High, who met ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ returning from the slaughter of the kings and blessed him,

[2] to whom also ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ apportioned a tenth from all — first, being interpreted, king of righteousness, and then also king of ⟦salem|Shalem|Salem⟧, which is king of peace,

[3] without father, without mother, without genealogy, having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but made like the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ — abides a priest perpetually.

[4] Now look how great this one is, to whom ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ gave a tenth out of the choicest spoils — the patriarch.

[5] And those of the sons of Levi who receive the priestly office have a commandment to tithe the people according to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, that is, their brothers, though these have come out of the loins of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧;

[6] but the one whose genealogy is not traced from them has tithed ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and has blessed the one who has the promises.

[7] And without any contradiction the lesser is blessed by the better.

[8] And here men who die receive tithes, but there one who is testified to, that he lives.

[9] And, so to speak, through ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ Levi also, who receives tithes, has been tithed,

[10] for he was still in the loins of his father when ⟦melchizedek|Malki-Tzedeq|Melchizedek⟧ met him.

[11] If then perfection was through the Levitical priesthood — for upon it the people has been given the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ — what need was there still for a different priest to arise according to the order of ⟦melchizedek|Malki-Tzedeq|Melchizedek⟧, and not to be named according to the order of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧?

[12] For when the priesthood is changed, of necessity there comes a change of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ also.

[13] For he of whom these things are said has partaken of a different tribe, from which no one has attended to the altar.

[14] For it is plainly evident that our Lord has risen out of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, as to which tribe ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke nothing concerning priests.

[15] And it is still more abundantly evident, if according to the likeness of ⟦melchizedek|Malki-Tzedeq|Melchizedek⟧ there arises a different priest,

[16] who has become one not according to a law of a fleshly commandment but according to the power of an indestructible life.

[17] For it is testified:

"**You are a priest forever, according to the order of ⟦melchizedek|Malki-Tzedeq|Melchizedek⟧**."

[18] For there comes a setting aside of a preceding commandment because of its weakness and unprofitableness

[19] (for the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ perfected nothing), and a bringing in besides of a better hope, through which we draw near to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] And inasmuch as it was not without oath-taking (for they have become priests without oath-taking,

[21] but he with oath-taking, through the one saying to him:

"**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ swore and will not regret:
'You are a priest forever'**"),

[22] by so much has ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ become the surety of a better covenant also.

[23] And they have become priests in greater number, because of being hindered by death from remaining;

[24] but he, because he abides forever, has the priesthood inviolable;

[25] whence also **he is able to save to the uttermost those who approach ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through him, always living to intercede on their behalf**.

[26] For such a high priest was also fitting for us: devout, guileless, undefiled, separated from sinners, and having become higher than the heavens;

[27] who does not have daily need, as the high priests, to offer up sacrifices first for his own sins, then for those of the people (for this he did once for all, having offered up himself).

[28] For the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ appoints as high priests men having weakness, but the word of the oath-taking, which was after the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, a Son, perfected forever.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v22) — systematic.
- **"the most high God" → "*Elohim* Most High"** (v1) — Greek τοῦ θεοῦ τοῦ ὑψίστου, the Septuagint's wording for אֵל עֶלְיוֹן *El Elyon* (Gen 14:18).
- **"who met Abraham … and blessed him" kept** (v1) — Greek ὁ συναντήσας … καὶ εὐλογήσας, participles; v1–3 are one sentence whose verb is "abides" (v3).
- **"gave a tenth part of all" → "apportioned a tenth from all"** (v2) — Greek ἐμέρισεν, "divided, apportioned".
- **"first being by interpretation King of righteousness, and after that also King of Salem" → "first, being interpreted, king of righteousness, and then also king of *Shalem*"** (v2) — Greek ἑρμηνευόμενος; the Greek has no "his name" and no "he is".
- **"Without father, without mother, without descent" → "without father, without mother, without genealogy"** (v3) — Greek ἀπάτωρ, ἀμήτωρ, ἀγενεαλόγητος, three single words; the third is "without a recorded genealogy" (v6).
- **"made like unto the Son of God" kept** (v3) — Greek ἀφωμοιωμένος, a perfect passive participle, "having been made like"; not "resembling".
- **"abideth a priest continually" → "abides a priest perpetually"** (v3) — Greek μένει (μένω, "abide", also v24); εἰς τὸ διηνεκές, "perpetually" (10:1, 12, 14), a different phrase from "forever" (εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, v17, 21, 24, 28).
- **"Now consider how great this man was, unto whom even the patriarch Abraham gave the tenth of the spoils" → "Now look how great this one is, to whom *Avraham* gave a tenth out of the choicest spoils — the patriarch"** (v4) — Greek θεωρεῖτε; πηλίκος οὗτος has no verb and no "man"; ἀκροθίνια is "the top of the heap", the best of the spoils; "the patriarch" stands last. The verse reads ᾧ, "to whom" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg); ᾧ καί, "to whom even" (NA28, RP).
- **"they that are of the sons of Levi, who receive the office of the priesthood" → "those of the sons of Levi who receive the priestly office"** (v5) — Greek ἱερατεία; "priesthood" stands for ἱερωσύνη (v11, 12, 24).
- **"to take tithes of the people according to the law" → "to tithe the people according to the *Torah*"** (v5) — Greek ἀποδεκατοῦν τὸν λαὸν κατὰ τὸν νόμον; "according to the *Torah*" is not "a commandment in the *Torah*".
- **"though they come out of the loins of Abraham" → "though these have come out of the loins of *Avraham*"** (v5) — Greek ἐξεληλυθότας ἐκ τῆς ὀσφύος; "loins" again in v10.
- **"he whose descent is not counted from them received tithes of Abraham, and blessed him that had the promises" → "the one whose genealogy is not traced from them has tithed *Avraham* and has blessed the one who has the promises"** (v6) — Greek γενεαλογούμενος (the root of v3); δεδεκάτωκεν, εὐλόγηκεν are perfects; τὸν ἔχοντα is present.
- **"without all contradiction the less is blessed of the better" → "without any contradiction the lesser is blessed by the better"** (v7) — Greek ἀντιλογίας (6:16); τὸ ἔλαττον, neuter; κρείττονος, "better" as throughout Hebrews.
- **"men that die receive tithes; but there he receiveth them, of whom it is witnessed that he liveth" → "men who die receive tithes, but there one who is testified to, that he lives"** (v8) — Greek ἀποθνῄσκοντες ἄνθρωποι, "dying men"; μαρτυρούμενος, passive of μαρτυρέω "testify"; the second half has no verb.
- **"as I may so say, Levi also, who receiveth tithes, payed tithes in Abraham" → "so to speak, through *Avraham* Levi also, who receives tithes, has been tithed"** (v9) — Greek διʼ Ἀβραάμ, "through"; δεδεκάτωται, perfect passive of the verb of v6.
- **"in the loins of his father" kept** (v10) — Greek τοῦ πατρός; not "ancestor".
- **"If therefore perfection were by the Levitical priesthood" → "If then perfection was through the Levitical priesthood"** (v11) — Greek τελείωσις, the act of perfecting (τελειόω, v19, 28). The Greek has no "attainable".
- **"for under it the people received the law" → "for upon it the people has been given the *Torah*"** (v11) — Greek ἐπʼ αὐτῆς νενομοθέτηται, perfect passive of νομοθετέω, "give law, enact as law" (8:6): "on the basis of it".
- **"another priest should rise" → "a different priest to arise"; "called" → "named"** (v11, 15) — Greek ἕτερον, "another of a different kind"; λέγεσθαι, "to be said".
- **"the priesthood being changed, there is made of necessity a change also of the law" → "when the priesthood is changed, of necessity there comes a change of *Torah* also"** (v12) — Greek μετατιθεμένης … μετάθεσις, one root; νόμου without the article.
- **"pertaineth to another tribe, of which no man gave attendance at the altar" → "has partaken of a different tribe, from which no one has attended to the altar"** (v13) — Greek μετέσχηκεν (μετέχω, 2:14; 5:13), προσέσχηκεν: two perfects that sound alike.
- **"it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda; of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood" → "it is plainly evident that our Lord has risen out of *Yehudah*, as to which tribe *Moshe* spoke nothing concerning priests"** (v14) — Greek πρόδηλον; ἀνατέταλκεν, perfect, "has risen" as a star or a shoot rises (Num 24:17; Zech 6:12 in the Septuagint); the printed text has περὶ ἱερέων, "concerning priests".
- **"it is yet far more evident: for that after the similitude of Melchisedec there ariseth another priest" → "it is still more abundantly evident, if according to the likeness of *Malki-Tzedeq* there arises a different priest"** (v15) — Greek περισσότερον ἔτι κατάδηλον; εἰ is "if".
- **"Who is made, not after the law of a carnal commandment, but after the power of an endless life" → "who has become one not according to a law of a fleshly commandment but according to the power of an indestructible life"** (v16) — Greek κατὰ νόμον, without the article; γέγονεν, "has become" ("one", that is, priest, is supplied); σαρκίνης, "made of flesh"; ἀκαταλύτου, "that cannot be dissolved". The Greek does not say "bodily descent".
- **"For he testifieth" → "For it is testified"** (v17) — the printed text has the passive μαρτυρεῖται; the Greek has no "of him".
- **"there is verily a disannulling of the commandment going before for the weakness and unprofitableness thereof" → "there comes a setting aside of a preceding commandment because of its weakness and unprofitableness"** (v18) — Greek ἀθέτησις (9:26); γίνεται as in v12. The Greek has two nouns, "setting aside" and "bringing in" (v19), with one verb.
- **"the law made nothing perfect, but the bringing in of a better hope did" → "(for the *Torah* perfected nothing), and a bringing in besides of a better hope"** (v19) — Greek ἐτελείωσεν, "perfected"; ἐπεισαγωγή, "a bringing in in addition"; the Greek has no "did".
- **"we draw nigh unto God" → "we draw near to *Elohim*"** (v19) — Greek ἐγγίζομεν; a different verb from "approach" (προσέρχομαι, v25).
- **"not without an oath" → "not without oath-taking"** (v20, 21, 28) — Greek ὁρκωμοσία, the swearing of an oath; "oath" stands for ὅρκος (6:16–17). The clause "inasmuch as … not without oath-taking" has no verb in Greek ("it was" is supplied); it is completed by v22.
- **"those priests were made without an oath" → "they have become priests without oath-taking"** (v20) — Greek εἰσὶν … γεγονότες, "are having-become"; the Greek has no "formerly".
- **"The Lord sware and will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchisedec" → "*Yahweh* swore and will not regret: 'You are a priest forever'"** (v21) — Greek ὤμοσεν, aorist; μεταμεληθήσεται, "will regret, will change his mind" (not μετανοέω, "repent"). The printed text ends at "forever"; the words "after the order of Melchisedec" are a later addition here.
- **"By so much was Jesus made a surety of a better testament" → "by so much has *Yeshua* become the surety of a better covenant also"** (v22) — Greek γέγονεν ἔγγυος; διαθήκη is "covenant" throughout Hebrews. The verse follows καὶ κρείττονος, "of a better … also" (WH, NA28); κρείττονος without καί (SBLGNT, Treg, RP). The name stands last in the Greek sentence.
- **"they truly were many priests, because they were not suffered to continue by reason of death" → "they have become priests in greater number, because of being hindered by death from remaining"** (v23) — Greek πλείονές εἰσιν γεγονότες; παραμένειν, a compound of "abide" (v24). The Greek has no "former" and no "in office".
- **"because he continueth ever, hath an unchangeable priesthood" → "because he abides forever, has the priesthood inviolable"** (v24) — Greek μένειν; ἀπαράβατον, "not to be overstepped": inviolable, permanent; some take it as "that does not pass over to another". It is not the "unchangeable" of 6:17–18.
- **"Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them" → "whence also he is able to save to the uttermost those who approach *Elohim* through him, always living to intercede on their behalf"** (v25) — Greek ὅθεν; εἰς τὸ παντελές is "completely" or "for all time"; προσερχομένους, "approaching" (4:16; 10:1, 22); πάντοτε ζῶν, a participle; ἐντυγχάνειν ὑπέρ.
- **"such an high priest became us" → "such a high priest was also fitting for us"** (v26) — Greek ἡμῖν καὶ ἔπρεπεν; not "that we should have".
- **"holy, harmless, undefiled" → "devout, guileless, undefiled"** (v26) — Greek ὅσιος, "devout" ("holy" is ἅγιος); ἄκακος, "without evil"; ἀμίαντος.
- **"made higher than the heavens" → "having become higher than the heavens"** (v26) — Greek ὑψηλότερος τῶν οὐρανῶν γενόμενος; not "exalted above".
- **"Who needeth not daily, as those high priests, to offer up sacrifice" → "who does not have daily need, as the high priests, to offer up sacrifices"** (v27) — Greek ἀναφέρειν, "offer up" (also "having offered up himself"); θυσίας, plural; the Greek has no "those".
- **"this he did once" → "this he did once for all"** (v27) — Greek ἐφάπαξ (9:12; 10:10); "once" is ἅπαξ (9:26–28).
- **"the law maketh men high priests which have infirmity; but the word of the oath, which was since the law, maketh the Son, who is consecrated for evermore" → "the *Torah* appoints as high priests men having weakness, but the word of the oath-taking, which was after the *Torah*, a Son, perfected forever"** (v28) — Greek καθίστησιν, "appoints" (5:1; 8:3), said once; ἀσθένειαν, "weakness" (5:2); υἱόν without the article; τετελειωμένον, perfect passive participle of τελειόω, "perfect" (5:9).

### Notes
- **v1–2** take up Gen 14:17–20.
- **v17, 21** quote Ps 110:4 (Ps 109:4 in the Septuagint).
- **v20–22** — one sentence in Greek; the parenthesis is that of the printed text.
