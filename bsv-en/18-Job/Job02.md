# Job 2

[1] And it came to pass on the day that the sons of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to take their stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the adversary also came among them to take his stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the adversary, "From where, then, do you come?" And the adversary answered ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "From roaming on the earth and from walking about on it."

[3] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the adversary, "Have you set your heart on my servant ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧? For there is none like him on the earth, a man blameless and upright, fearing ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and turning aside from evil; and he is still holding fast his integrity, and you incited me against him, to swallow him up for nothing."

[4] And the adversary answered ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "Skin for skin! And all that a man has he will give for his soul.

[5] But stretch out, please, your hand and touch his bone and his flesh: he will surely bless you to your face."

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the adversary, "Behold, he is in your hand; only keep his soul."

[7] And the adversary went out from the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and struck ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ with evil boils from the sole of his foot even to the crown of his head.

[8] And he took for himself a potsherd to scrape himself with it, while he sat in the midst of the ashes.

[9] And his wife said to him, "You are still holding fast your integrity! Bless ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and die."

[10] And he said to her, "As one of the foolish women speaks, you speak. The good, too, we accept from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the evil we shall not accept?" In all this ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ did not sin with his lips.

[11] And the three friends of ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ heard of all this evil that had come upon him, and they came each from his place, ⟦eliphaz|Elifaz|Eliphaz⟧ the ⟦temanite|Temani|Temanite⟧ and Bildad the ⟦shuhite|Shuchi|Shuhite⟧ and ⟦zophar|Tzofar|Zophar⟧ the ⟦naamathite|Naamati|Naamathite⟧; and they met together by appointment to come to show him grief and to comfort him.

[12] And they lifted up their eyes from afar and did not recognize him; and they lifted up their voice and wept, and they tore each his robe and tossed dust on their heads toward the heavens.

[13] And they sat with him on the earth seven days and seven nights, and there was none speaking a word to him, for they saw that the pain was very great.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v10 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of וַ / וְ kept; "answered … and said" (v2, 4).
- **"Again there was a day" → "And it came to pass on the day"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הַיּוֹם, the words of 1:6; there is no "again". **"sons of God" → "sons of *Elohim*"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים, as 1:6. **"present himself" → "take his stand"** — לְהִתְיַצֵּב עַל, lit. "over / by" (as 1:6).
- **"Satan" → "the adversary"** (v1–4, 6, 7) — Hebrew הַשָּׂטָן *ha-satan*, a common noun with the article; traditional "Satan" (see 1:6).
- **"From whence comest thou?" → "From where, then, do you come?"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵי מִזֶּה, lit. "where, from this?", another form than 1:7 (מֵאַיִן). **"going to and fro … walking up and down" → "roaming … walking about"** — as 1:7.
- **"Hast thou considered" → "Have you set your heart on"** (v3) — Hebrew הֲשַׂמְתָּ לִבְּךָ אֶל (1:8 has עַל). **"perfect and upright … escheweth evil" → "blameless and upright, fearing *Elohim* and turning aside from evil"** — the words of 1:1, 8.
- **"he holdeth fast his integrity, although thou movedst me against him, to destroy him without cause" → "he is still holding fast his integrity, and you incited me against him, to swallow him up for nothing"** (v3) — Hebrew וְעֹדֶנּוּ מַחֲזִיק בְּתֻמָּתוֹ וַתְּסִיתֵנִי בוֹ לְבַלְּעוֹ חִנָּם: the clauses are joined by "and" ("although" is not written); הֵסִית "incite"; בִּלַּע "swallow (up)"; חִנָּם "for nothing", the word of the adversary's question in 1:9 ("without cause" in sense).
- **"for his life" → "for his soul"** (v4) — Hebrew נַפְשׁוֹ. **"Skin for skin"** — עוֹר בְּעַד עוֹר; the meaning of the saying is not certain (see Notes).
- **"touch his bone and his flesh, and he will curse thee" → "touch his bone and his flesh: he will surely bless you"** (v5) — Hebrew בֵּרֵךְ "bless" where cursing is meant, as 1:5, 11; the oath form אִם־לֹא, lit. "if he will not bless you …", by its meaning. **"put forth thine hand now" → "stretch out, please, your hand"** — שְׁלַח־נָא יָדְךָ.
- **"but save his life" → "only keep his soul"** (v6) — Hebrew אַךְ אֶת־נַפְשׁוֹ שְׁמֹר, "only his soul keep" (= do not take his life).
- **"sore boils" → "evil boils"** (v7) — Hebrew שְׁחִין רָע, a singular for the whole outbreak (as Deut 28:35); רָע "evil" (here of a disease: "bad, malignant"). Ketiv עד "to", qere וְעַד "and to": "even to". **"from the presence of" → "from the face of"** — מֵאֵת פְּנֵי.
- **"a potsherd to scrape himself withal; and he sat down among the ashes" → "a potsherd to scrape himself with it, while he sat in the midst of the ashes"** (v8) — Hebrew חֶרֶשׂ "earthenware" (a broken piece of it); וְהוּא יֹשֵׁב, a participle: he was already sitting; בְּתוֹךְ "in the midst of".
- **"Dost thou still retain thine integrity? curse God, and die" → "You are still holding fast your integrity! Bless *Elohim* and die."** (v9) — Hebrew עֹדְךָ מַחֲזִיק בְּתֻמָּתֶךָ has no question word; it may be read as a question. בָּרֵךְ "bless" where cursing is meant (as 1:5).
- **"as one of the foolish women speaketh" → "As one of the foolish women speaks, you speak"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּדַבֵּר אַחַת הַנְּבָלוֹת תְּדַבֵּרִי; נָבָל is the fool who is base and godless, not only unwise. **"Shall we receive good … and shall we not receive evil?" → "The good, too, we accept …, and the evil we shall not accept?"** — Hebrew גַּם אֶת־הַטּוֹב נְקַבֵּל … וְאֶת־הָרָע לֹא נְקַבֵּל, a question without a question word; קִבֵּל "accept".
- **"Job's three friends" → "the three friends of Job"** (v11). **"made an appointment together to come to mourn with him and to comfort him" → "met together by appointment to come to show him grief and to comfort him"** — Hebrew וַיִּוָּעֲדוּ (nifal of יָעַד "appoint"); לָנוּד־לוֹ, lit. "to sway for him" (the shaking of the head in grief); נִחַם piel "comfort".
- **"they knew him not" → "did not recognize him"** (v12) — Hebrew הִכִּיר. **"rent every one his mantle, and sprinkled dust" → "tore each his robe and tossed dust"** — מְעִיל "robe"; זָרַק of dust "toss". **"toward heaven" → "toward the heavens"** — הַשָּׁמַיְמָה.
- **"none spake a word unto him: for they saw that his grief was very great" → "there was none speaking a word to him, for they saw that the pain was very great"** (v13) — Hebrew וְאֵין־דֹּבֵר; הַכְּאֵב "the pain", with the article ("his" is not written).

### Notes
- **Second divine council** — Yahweh points to Job's integrity, still unbroken. The adversary escalates to physical attack.
- **"Skin for skin"** — proverbial; disputed meaning. Perhaps: "a man gives up outer goods to preserve his life."
- **The boils head-to-foot** — the disease cannot be identified from the text (leprosy, pemphigus or smallpox have been proposed).
- **Potsherd scraping** — sitting on ashes with potsherd: image of total abasement, classic ANE mourning.
- **Job's wife** — gets one line. Mediaeval tradition often harsh on her; modern reading: a fellow-sufferer voicing despair. She too has lost everything.
- **Three friends** named with their regions: Eliphaz (Teman, Edom), Bildad (Shuah, east), Zophar (Naamah, location debated). All non-Israelite; universal wisdom setting.
- **Seven-day silent vigil** — matches mourning custom for the dead (Gen 50:10). Treating Job as already dead.
