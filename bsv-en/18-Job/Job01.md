# Job 1

[1] There was a man in the land of ⟦uz|Utz|Uz⟧ whose name was ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧; and that man was blameless and upright, fearing ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and turning aside from evil.

[2] And there were born to him seven sons and three daughters.

[3] And his livestock was seven thousand of the flock and three thousand camels and five hundred yoke of the herd and five hundred she-donkeys, and a very great body of slaves; and that man was greater than all the sons of the east.

[4] And his sons would go and make a feast in the house of each one on his day; and they would send and call their three sisters to eat and to drink with them.

[5] And it came to pass, when the days of the feast had gone around, that ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ would send and sanctify them; and he would rise early in the morning and offer up burnt offerings according to the number of them all, for ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ said, "Perhaps my sons have sinned and blessed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in their hearts." Thus ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ did all the days.

[6] And it came to pass on the day that the sons of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to take their stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the adversary also came among them.

[7] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the adversary, "From where do you come?" And the adversary answered ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "From roaming on the earth and from walking about on it."

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the adversary, "Have you set your heart on my servant ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧? For there is none like him on the earth, a man blameless and upright, fearing ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and turning aside from evil."

[9] And the adversary answered ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "Is it for nothing that ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ fears ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[10] Have you not put a hedge around him and around his house and around all that he has, on every side? The work of his hands you have blessed, and his livestock has spread abroad in the land.

[11] But stretch out, please, your hand and touch all that he has: he will surely bless you to your face."

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the adversary, "Behold, all that he has is in your hand; only against him do not stretch out your hand." And the adversary went out from the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And it came to pass on the day, when his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their brother the firstborn,

[14] and a messenger came to ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ and said, "The herd were plowing, and the she-donkeys grazing beside them,

[15] and ⟦sabeans|Sheva|the Sabeans⟧ fell on them and took them, and the young men they struck with the edge of the sword; and only I alone have escaped to tell you."

[16] While this one was still speaking, another came and said, "The fire of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ fell from the heavens and burned among the flock and among the young men and consumed them; and only I alone have escaped to tell you."

[17] While this one was still speaking, another came and said, "The ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ set three companies and made a raid on the camels and took them, and the young men they struck with the edge of the sword; and only I alone have escaped to tell you."

[18] While this one was speaking, another came and said, "Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their brother the firstborn,

[19] and behold, a great wind came from across the wilderness and struck the four corners of the house, and it fell on the young people, and they died; and only I alone have escaped to tell you."

[20] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ arose and tore his robe and sheared his head, and fell to the earth and bowed down.

[21] And he said:

"Naked I came out of my mother's belly,
and naked I shall return there.
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ gave, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has taken;
let the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be blessed."

[22] In all this ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ did not sin, and he did not ascribe unseemliness to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ; the chain is kept, as are the repeated "and" of the lists (v3) and the speech formula "answered … and said" (v7, 9).
- **"perfect and upright, and one that feared God, and eschewed evil" → "blameless and upright, fearing *Elohim* and turning aside from evil"** (v1, 8) — Hebrew תָּם וְיָשָׁר יְרֵא אֱלֹהִים וְסָר מֵרָע: תָּם "whole, complete" (also Gen 25:27), יָשָׁר "upright", יְרֵא and סָר participles ("fearing … turning aside"); סוּר מִן is "turn aside from" (not "shun", "eschew"). One wording in 1:1, 8; 2:3.
- **"seven thousand sheep … five hundred yoke of oxen" → "seven thousand of the flock … five hundred yoke of the herd"** (v3) — Hebrew צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats, one collective word), בָּקָר "herd" (cattle); אֲתֹנוֹת "she-donkeys".
- **"a very great household" → "a very great body of slaves"** (v3) — Hebrew עֲבֻדָּה, from עָבַד "serve": the whole of the slaves (as Gen 26:14).
- **"the greatest of all the men of the east" → "greater than all the sons of the east"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־קֶדֶם "sons of the east"; קֶדֶם is "front, east".
- **"feasted in their houses, every one his day" → "make a feast in the house of each one on his day"** (v4) — Hebrew וְעָשׂוּ מִשְׁתֶּה בֵּית אִישׁ יוֹמוֹ; the forms are frequentative ("would go … would send"). Ketiv אחיתיהם, qere אַחְיוֹתֵיהֶם "their sisters": the same word in two spellings.
- **"were gone about" → "had gone around"** (v5) — Hebrew הִקִּיפוּ, "came round" (a full round of the feast days). **"offered … according to the number of them all"** — הֶעֱלָה עֹלוֹת, lit. "made go up goings-up" (one root). **"continually" → "all the days"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים.
- **"cursed God" → "blessed *Elohim*"** (v5); **"curse thee to thy face" → "bless you to your face"** (v11) — Hebrew בֵּרֲכוּ / יְבָרֲכֶךָּ, the verb "bless". "Curse" is meant; the text does not set a word of cursing beside the divine name, and the word that stands is translated (as 1 Kgs 21:10, 13; Job 2:5, 9).
- **"Now there was a day" → "And it came to pass on the day"** (v6, 13) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הַיּוֹם, with the article: "the day" (= the day of the gathering; "one day" in sense).
- **"sons of God" → "sons of *Elohim*"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים, as Gen 6:2, 4; Job 2:1; 38:7. Who they are (members of the heavenly council, "angels" in the Septuagint, cited from memory) the verse does not say.
- **"present themselves before" → "take their stand before"** (v6) — Hebrew לְהִתְיַצֵּב עַל־יְהוָה, the hitpael "take one's stand"; עַל is "over / by" (standing by one who is seated).
- **"Satan" → "the adversary"** (v6–9, 12) — Hebrew הַשָּׂטָן *ha-satan*, a common noun with the article: "the adversary, the accuser". The Hebrew does not make it a name here; traditional "Satan".
- **"going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it" → "roaming on the earth and from walking about on it"** (v7) — Hebrew מִשּׁוּט … וּמֵהִתְהַלֵּךְ, both with מִן "from"; הִתְהַלֵּךְ is "walk about".
- **"Hast thou considered" → "Have you set your heart on"** (v8) — Hebrew הֲשַׂמְתָּ לִבְּךָ עַל, "set your heart on" (= pay attention to). עַבְדִּי "my servant" (the word is "slave").
- **"Doth Job fear God for nought?" → "Is it for nothing that Job fears *Elohim*?"** (v9) — Hebrew הַחִנָּם stands first. חִנָּם "for nothing, gratis"; the same word in 2:3, where Yahweh says he was incited against Job "for nothing" ("without cause").
- **"made an hedge about him" → "put a hedge around him"** (v10) — Hebrew שַׂכְתָּ בַעֲדוֹ, with בְּעַד "around" three times. Ketiv את, qere אַתָּה "you": no difference in sense. **"increased" → "spread abroad"** — Hebrew פָּרַץ, "break out".
- **"put forth thine hand now" → "stretch out, please, your hand"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew שְׁלַח־נָא יָדְךָ. **"and he will curse thee" → "he will surely bless you"** (v11) — the oath formula אִם־לֹא, lit. "if he will not bless you …", by its meaning.
- **"in thy power" → "in your hand"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּיָדֶךָ. **"from the presence of" → "from the face of"** — Hebrew מֵעִם פְּנֵי, lit. "from with the face of".
- **"their eldest brother's house" → "the house of their brother the firstborn"** (v13, 18) — Hebrew הַבְּכוֹר "the firstborn".
- **"The oxen … the asses" → "The herd … the she-donkeys"** (v14) — Hebrew הַבָּקָר with a feminine plural participle (חֹרְשׁוֹת), הָאֲתֹנוֹת; עַל־יְדֵיהֶם "beside them", lit. "on their hands". מַלְאָךְ "messenger".
- **"the Sabeans" → token** (v15) — Hebrew שְׁבָא "Sheba", the land or people, with a feminine singular verb ("Sheba fell"). **"servants" → "young men"** (v15–17) — Hebrew הַנְּעָרִים "the young men" (attendants). **"edge of the sword"** — lit. "the mouth of the sword". **"I only am escaped alone" → "only I alone have escaped"** — Hebrew רַק־אֲנִי לְבַדִּי, both words kept.
- **"While he was yet speaking, there came also another" → "While this one was still speaking, another came"** (v16–18) — Hebrew עוֹד זֶה מְדַבֵּר וְזֶה בָּא, "yet this one speaking, and this one came". In v18 the Hebrew has עַד "while" instead of עוֹד "yet" (no "still").
- **"from heaven" → "from the heavens"** (v16) — Hebrew הַשָּׁמַיִם (plural). **"burned up the sheep" → "burned among the flock"** — Hebrew וַתִּבְעַר בַּצֹּאן "burned among the flock".
- **"made out three bands, and fell upon" → "set three companies and made a raid on"** (v17) — Hebrew שָׂמוּ שְׁלֹשָׁה רָאשִׁים, lit. "set three heads"; פָּשַׁט עַל "make a raid on".
- **"the young men … they are dead" → "the young people, and they died"** (v19) — Hebrew הַנְּעָרִים, the word of v15–17; here it covers the sons and the daughters (a masculine plural for both), so "young people". וַיָּמוּתוּ "and they died".
- **"rent his mantle, and shaved his head, and fell down upon the ground, and worshipped" → "tore his robe and sheared his head, and fell to the earth and bowed down"** (v20) — Hebrew מְעִיל "robe", גָּזַז "shear" (the verb of shearing sheep), אַרְצָה "to the earth", הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down".
- **"my mother's womb" → "my mother's belly"** (v21) — Hebrew בֶּטֶן "belly". Ketiv יצתי, qere יָצָאתִי "I came out": one word, two spellings. **"hath taken away" → "has taken"** — Hebrew לָקָח "took". **"blessed be" → "let … be blessed"** — Hebrew יְהִי … מְבֹרָךְ, a jussive.
- **"nor charged God foolishly" → "and he did not ascribe unseemliness to *Elohim*"** (v22) — Hebrew וְלֹא־נָתַן תִּפְלָה לֵאלֹהִים, lit. "and did not give unseemliness to *Elohim*". תִּפְלָה (also 24:12; Jer 23:13) is "what is unseemly, tasteless"; "give … to" = ascribe.

### Notes
- **Uz** — location uncertain; Edom/Arabia area. Job is not an Israelite; the setting is the age of the patriarchs, told without a date.
- **Prose prologue (1-2)** + poetic dialogues (3:3–42:6) + prose epilogue (42:7–17). Sandwich structure.
- ***Ha-satan*** — in Job, not yet the developed "Satan" figure of intertestamental and NT. He's a role in the divine council — the "adversary/prosecutor." Acts with Yahweh's permission throughout.
- **"Bless" for "curse"** (v5, 11) — the Hebrew writes בֵּרֵךְ where cursing is meant, whether by the author or by later scribes: no word of cursing stands beside the divine name.
- **Seven / three pairing** (sons/daughters; sheep/camels thousands) — complete/perfect numbers.
- **"Fire of *Elohim* from the heavens"** (v16) — perhaps lightning; the Hebrew says "the fire of *Elohim*".
- **Four disasters, four messengers** — symmetrical catastrophe.
- **"Naked I came out... Yahweh gave, Yahweh has taken"** (v21) — the words are said at countless funerals.
