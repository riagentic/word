# Job 9

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Truly I know that it is so;
and how can mortal man be righteous with ⟦el|El|God⟧?

[3] If he delights to contend with him,
he will not answer him one out of a thousand.

[4] Wise of heart and mighty in power —
who has made himself hard against him and come out whole?

[5] He who removes mountains, and they do not know,
who overthrows them in his anger;

[6] who makes the earth quake out of its place,
and its pillars shudder;

[7] who says to the sun, and it does not rise,
and seals up around the stars;

[8] who stretches out the heavens by himself,
and treads on the high places of the sea;

[9] who makes ⟦bear|Ash|the Bear⟧, ⟦orion|Kesil|Orion⟧, and ⟦pleiades|Kimah|the Pleiades⟧,
and the chambers of the south;

[10] who does great things past searching out,
and wonderful deeds past numbering.

[11] Behold, he passes by me, and I do not see;
and he passes on, and I do not discern him.

[12] Behold, he snatches away — who can turn him back?
Who will say to him, 'What are you doing?'

[13] ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ does not turn back his anger;
under him the helpers of ⟦rahab-monster|Rahav|Rahab⟧ have bowed down.

[14] How much less I — that I should answer him,
should choose my words with him!

[15] Whom, if I were righteous, I would not answer;
with my judge I would plead.

[16] If I called and he answered me,
I would not believe that he gives ear to my voice,

[17] he who bruises me with a tempest
and multiplies my wounds without cause.

[18] He does not let me bring back my breath,
but he sates me with bitter things.

[19] If it is of power — behold, a mighty one!
And if of judgment — who will summon me?

[20] If I am righteous, my mouth will condemn me;
blameless I am — and it will declare me crooked.

[21] Blameless I am; I do not know my soul;
I reject my life.

[22] It is one thing; therefore I have said,
'The blameless and the wicked he makes an end of.'

[23] If the whip kills suddenly,
he mocks at the testing of the innocent.

[24] The earth is given into the hand of the wicked;
the faces of its judges he covers —
if not, then who is it?

[25] And my days are swifter than a runner;
they flee, they have not seen good.

[26] They pass on with ships of reed,
like an eagle that swoops upon food.

[27] If I say, 'Let me forget my complaint,
let me forsake my face and brighten up,'

[28] I am afraid of all my pains;
I know that you will not clear me.

[29] I — I shall be wicked;
why then do I labor for vapor?

[30] If I wash myself with water of snow
and make my palms pure with lye,

[31] then you will dip me in the pit,
and my garments will abominate me.

[32] For he is not a man like me, that I should answer him,
that we should come together into judgment.

[33] There is not between us one who decides,
who might set his hand on us both.

[34] Let him take away his rod from upon me,
and let not his dread terrify me.

[35] Let me speak and not fear him,
for not so am I with myself."

## Changes

- **"Then … answered and said" → "And … answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the speech headings in Job.
- **"God" → "El"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*. **"God" → "Eloah"** (v13) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ.
- **"but how should man be just with God?" → "and how can mortal man be righteous with El?"** (v2) — Hebrew וּמַה "and how"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; יִצְדַּק "be righteous" (not "just"); עִם "with".
- **"If he will contend with him, he cannot answer him one of a thousand" → "If he delights to contend with him, he will not answer him one out of a thousand"** (v3) — Hebrew יַחְפֹּץ "delights, desires"; רִיב "contend" (in law). Who is "he" and who "him" is not named: a man who would take El to law, or El who answers him; the ambiguity is kept. אַחַת "one" (a thing).
- **"He is wise in heart, and mighty in strength: who hath hardened himself against him, and hath prospered?" → "Wise of heart and mighty in power — who has made himself hard against him and come out whole?"** (v4) — Hebrew has no "he is"; כֹּחַ "power"; הִקְשָׁה, hifil of קָשָׁה, "make hard", without an object: "himself" is supplied (the neck is meant, as Deut 10:16). וַיִּשְׁלָם "and was whole, unharmed" (שָׁלֵם).
- **"Which removeth the mountains … which overturneth them in his anger" → "He who removes mountains … who overthrows them in his anger"** (v5–10) — participles with and without the article, "the one who …", in a row from v5 to v10. הָפַךְ "overthrow" (lit. "turn over"); אַף "anger" (lit. "nose"). "It" after "know" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Which shaketh the earth … and the pillars thereof tremble" → "who makes the earth quake … and its pillars shudder"** (v6) — Hebrew הִרְגִּיז "make quake"; יִתְפַלָּצוּן "shudder".
- **"Which commandeth the sun … and sealeth up the stars" → "who says to the sun … and seals up around the stars"** (v7) — Hebrew הָאֹמֵר לַחֶרֶס "who says to the sun" (אָמַר "say"; חֶרֶס is a rare word for the sun; "commands" is the sense); וּבְעַד כּוֹכָבִים יַחְתֹּם, "and seals around / behind the stars".
- **"Which alone spreadeth out the heavens, and treadeth upon the waves of the sea" → "who stretches out the heavens by himself, and treads on the high places of the sea"** (v8) — Hebrew נֹטֶה "stretches out"; לְבַדּוֹ "by himself"; בָּמֳתֵי יָם "the high places (backs) of the sea", not "waves".
- **"Which maketh Arcturus, Orion, and Pleiades, and the chambers of the south" → "who makes Ash, Kesil, and Kimah, and the chambers of the south"** (v9) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה־עָשׁ כְּסִיל וְכִימָה. The three names are given as the Hebrew has them (tokens: traditional "the Bear, Orion, the Pleiades"); which stars they are is not certain. עָשׁ (38:32 עַיִשׁ) has no article. The earlier text set the traditional names into the verse a second time inside the token; that is taken out. חַדְרֵי תֵמָן "the chambers of the south" (תֵּימָן "south", lit. "the right-hand side"; also the name Teman): a part of the sky, which one is not known.
- **"Which doeth great things past finding out; yea, and wonders without number" → "who does great things past searching out, and wonderful deeds past numbering"** (v10) — Hebrew עַד־אֵין חֵקֶר "until there is no searching", עַד־אֵין מִסְפָּר "until there is no number"; נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds". "Yea" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Lo, he goeth by me, and I see him not: he passeth on also, but I perceive him not" → "Behold, he passes by me, and I do not see; and he passes on, and I do not discern him"** (v11) — Hebrew הֵן "behold"; יַעֲבֹר עָלַי "passes by me"; וְלֹא אֶרְאֶה "and I do not see" ("him" is not written); יַחֲלֹף "passes on"; אָבִין לוֹ "discern him".
- **"Behold, he taketh away, who can hinder him?" → "Behold, he snatches away — who can turn him back?"** (v12) — Hebrew יַחְתֹּף "snatches"; יְשִׁיבֶנּוּ "turn him (or it) back".
- **"If God will not withdraw his anger, the proud helpers do stoop under him" → "Eloah does not turn back his anger; under him the helpers of Rahav have bowed down"** (v13) — Hebrew has no "if"; אַפּוֹ "his anger" (lit. "nose"). The qere is תַּחְתָּיו "under him" (ketiv תחתו, the same meaning in a shorter spelling). שָׁחֲחוּ, perfect, "have bowed down". עֹזְרֵי רָהַב "the helpers of Rahav" (token; רַהַב is "arrogance, storming", the name of the sea monster); KJV "the proud helpers" translates the name. The gloss "the sea-monster" inside the token is taken out.
- **"How much less shall I answer him, and choose out my words to reason with him?" → "How much less I — that I should answer him, should choose my words with him!"** (v14) — Hebrew אַף כִּי־אָנֹכִי "how much less I"; אֶבְחֲרָה, cohortative; עִמּוֹ "with him" ("to reason" is not written).
- **"Whom, though I were righteous, yet would I not answer, but I would make supplication to my judge" → "Whom, if I were righteous, I would not answer; with my judge I would plead"** (v15) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר "whom", joined to "him" of v14; לִמְשֹׁפְטִי "to my judge" (poel of שָׁפַט, "the one who judges me", or "my opponent at law"); אֶתְחַנָּן "plead" (lit. "seek favor for myself"); "for mercy" is not written.
- **"yet would I not believe that he had hearkened unto my voice" → "I would not believe that he gives ear to my voice"** (v16) — Hebrew יַאֲזִין "gives ear".
- **"For he breaketh me with a tempest" → "he who bruises me with a tempest"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר "who", not "for"; יְשׁוּפֵנִי "bruises me" (meaning uncertain: also "crushes"; Gen 3:15); בִּשְׂעָרָה "with a tempest" (the letters also give "with a hair"). פְּצָעַי "my wounds"; חִנָּם "without cause" (lit. "for nothing").
- **"He will not suffer me to take my breath, but filleth me with bitterness" → "He does not let me bring back my breath, but he sates me with bitter things"** (v18) — Hebrew הָשֵׁב רוּחִי "bring back my breath" (רוּחַ "breath, spirit"); יַשְׂבִּעַנִי "sates me"; מַמְּרֹרִים "bitter things" (plural).
- **"If I speak of strength, lo, he is strong: and if of judgment, who shall set me a time to plead?" → "If it is of power — behold, a mighty one! And if of judgment — who will summon me?"** (v19) — Hebrew אִם־לְכֹחַ אַמִּיץ הִנֵּה, literally "if for power — a mighty one: behold!"; "he is" is not written. יוֹעִידֵנִי "will summon me (appoint me a time)": the Masoretic Text has "me"; the earlier rendering "summon him" follows the Septuagint and other versions (cited from memory): the Masoretic Text is kept. In this verse Job may be giving El's own words.
- **"mine own mouth shall condemn me: if I say, I am perfect, it shall also prove me perverse" → "my mouth will condemn me; blameless I am — and it will declare me crooked"** (v20) — Hebrew יַרְשִׁיעֵנִי "condemn me"; "own" and "if I say" are not written: תָּם־אָנִי "blameless I am". וַיַּעְקְשֵׁנִי "and it (or he) declares me crooked" (עָקַשׁ hifil); the subject may be the mouth or El.
- **"Though I were perfect, yet would I not know my soul: I would despise my life" → "Blameless I am; I do not know my soul; I reject my life"** (v21) — Hebrew תָּם "blameless"; נַפְשִׁי "my soul" (= myself); אֶמְאַס "I reject".
- **"This is one thing, therefore I said it, He destroyeth the perfect and the wicked" → "It is one thing; therefore I have said, 'The blameless and the wicked he makes an end of'"** (v22) — Hebrew מְכַלֶּה, piel of כָּלָה, "makes an end of" (not "destroy").
- **"If the scourge slay suddenly, he will laugh at the trial of the innocent" → "If the whip kills suddenly, he mocks at the testing of the innocent"** (v23) — Hebrew שׁוֹט "whip"; לְמַסַּת "at the testing" (מַסָּה, noun of נִסָּה "test"); it may also be taken from מָסַס "melt", "the despair" (so the earlier rendering): the word is uncertain. נְקִיִּם "the innocent"; יִלְעָג "mocks".
- **"he covereth the faces of the judges thereof; if not, where, and who is he?" → "the faces of its judges he covers — if not, then who is it?"** (v24) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא אֵפוֹא מִי־הוּא "if not, then who is he / it?"; "if it is not he" supplies words.
- **"Now my days are swifter than a post" → "And my days are swifter than a runner"** (v25) — Hebrew וְיָמַי "and my days"; רָץ "runner". **"they see no good" → "they have not seen good"** — perfect.
- **"They are passed away as the swift ships" → "They pass on with ships of reed"** (v26) — Hebrew חָלְפוּ "pass on"; עִם "with" (= like); אֳנִיּוֹת אֵבֶה: אֵבֶה is found only here; "reed, papyrus" is the most likely sense (uncertain). **"as the eagle that hasteth to the prey" → "like an eagle that swoops upon food"** — Hebrew נֶשֶׁר (the word also covers the great vultures); אֹכֶל "food".
- **"I will forget my complaint, I will leave off my heaviness, and comfort myself" → "Let me forget my complaint, let me forsake my face and brighten up"** (v27) — Hebrew אִם־אָמְרִי "if my saying is"; three cohortatives: אֶשְׁכְּחָה "let me forget", אֶעֶזְבָה פָנַי "let me forsake my face" (the sad face is meant), אַבְלִיגָה "let me brighten up". שִׂיחַ "complaint, musing" (10:1).
- **"I am afraid of all my sorrows, I know that thou wilt not hold me innocent" → "I am afraid of all my pains; I know that you will not clear me"** (v28) — Hebrew יָגֹרְתִּי "I am afraid"; עַצְּבֹתַי "my pains"; תְנַקֵּנִי "clear me" (hold guiltless).
- **"If I be wicked, why then labour I in vain?" → "I — I shall be wicked; why then do I labor for vapor?"** (v29) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי אֶרְשָׁע, "I, I shall be wicked" (= I shall be held guilty), with no "if"; הֶבֶל "vapor, breath"; אִיגָע "I labor".
- **"If I wash myself with snow water, and make my hands never so clean" → "If I wash myself with water of snow and make my palms pure with lye"** (v30) — the qere is בְמֵי "with water of" (ketiv במו "with": "with snow"). הֲזִכּוֹתִי "make pure" (the root of זַךְ "pure"); בְּבֹר "with lye" (the same letters give "purity", 2 Sam 22:21); כַּפַּי "my palms".
- **"Yet shalt thou plunge me in the ditch, and mine own clothes shall abhor me" → "then you will dip me in the pit, and my garments will abominate me"** (v31) — Hebrew אָז "then"; תִּטְבְּלֵנִי "dip me"; שַׁחַת "pit"; תִּעֲבוּנִי "abominate me" (תִּעֵב); שַׂלְמוֹתַי "my garments". "Own" is not written.
- **"For he is not a man, as I am" → "For he is not a man like me"** (v32) — Hebrew אִישׁ "man"; "he is" supplied. נָבוֹא בַּמִּשְׁפָּט "come into judgment".
- **"Neither is there any daysman betwixt us, that might lay his hand upon us both" → "There is not between us one who decides, who might set his hand on us both"** (v33) — Hebrew לֹא "not"; some read לוּ "would that there were" (so the Septuagint and Syriac, cited from memory): the Masoretic Text is kept. מוֹכִיחַ, participle of הוֹכִיחַ "reprove, decide between two"; יָשֵׁת "set".
- **"Let him take his rod away from me, and let not his fear terrify me" → "Let him take away his rod from upon me, and let not his dread terrify me"** (v34) — Hebrew מֵעָלַי "from upon me"; אֵמָתוֹ "his dread"; תְּבַעֲתַנִּי "terrify me".
- **"Then would I speak, and not fear him; but it is not so with me" → "Let me speak and not fear him, for not so am I with myself"** (v35) — Hebrew אֲדַבְּרָה, cohortative "let me speak"; כִּי לֹא־כֵן אָנֹכִי עִמָּדִי, "for not so am I with myself" (= I am not, in myself, the guilty one I am taken for; the sense is debated).

### Notes
- **Cosmic-judicial struggle** — Job's dilemma: how can mortal man contend with El, who controls everything?
- **Cosmic poetry** (v5–10) — mountains, earth, sun, stars all at El's command.
- **Rahav** — the sea monster (Ps 89:10, Isa 51:9). Chaos-beings bow under Eloah.
- **"The blameless and the wicked he makes an end of"** (v22) — direct denial of the friends' retribution theology. Complaint at divine indiscrimination.
- **No one who decides** (v33) — Job longs for one who could stand between him and El. Later Christian reading sees messianic anticipation.
- **"Wash with water of snow"** (v30) — no amount of purification can make Job righteous before one who counts him guilty in advance.
