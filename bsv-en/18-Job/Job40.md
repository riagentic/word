# Job 40

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ and said:

[2] "Shall a corrector contend with ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧?
One who reproves ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧, let him answer it."

[3] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said:

[4] "Behold, I am slight; what shall I reply to you?
My hand I have put to my mouth.

[5] Once I have spoken, and I will not answer;
and twice, and I will not do it again."

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ out of the storm and said:

[7] "Gird up, please, your loins like a man;
I will ask you, and you make me know.

[8] Will you even make void my judgment?
Will you condemn me so that you may be righteous?

[9] Or have you an arm like ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and with a voice like his can you thunder?

[10] Adorn yourself, please, with exaltation and loftiness,
and with majesty and splendor clothe yourself.

[11] Scatter the overflowings of your anger,
and see every proud one and make him low.

[12] See every proud one, bring him low,
and tread down the wicked in their place.

[13] Hide them in the dust together;
bind their faces in the hidden place.

[14] And I also will acknowledge you,
that your right hand can save you.

[15] Behold, please, ⟦behemoth|Behemot|Behemoth⟧, which I made with you;
he eats grass like the herd.

[16] Behold, please, his power is in his loins,
and his strength in the sinews of his belly.

[17] He bends his tail like a cedar;
the sinews of his thighs are knit together.

[18] His bones are channels of bronze,
his limbs like a bar of iron.

[19] He is the first of the ways of ⟦el|El|God⟧;
the one who made him brings near his sword.

[20] For the mountains bear produce for him,
and all the living things of the field play there.

[21] Under the lotus bushes he lies down,
in the covert of reed and marsh.

[22] The lotus bushes cover him, as his shade;
the willows of the wadi surround him.

[23] Behold, a river oppresses, he is not alarmed;
he trusts, though ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ bursts forth to his mouth.

[24] By his eyes can one take him?
With snares can one pierce the nose?"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v2) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי; **"God" → "Eloah"** (v2) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ; **"God" → "El"** (v9, 19) — Hebrew אֵל. Three names, none levelled.
- **"Moreover the LORD answered Job, and said" → "And Yahweh answered Job and said"** (v1; also v3, 6) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the book.
- **"Shall he that contendeth with the Almighty instruct him?" → "Shall a corrector contend with Shaddai?"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲרֹב עִם־שַׁדַּי יִסּוֹר: רֹב is the infinitive of רִיב "contend"; יִסּוֹר is found only here, a noun of the pattern of an agent from יָסַר "correct, discipline" ("a corrector, faultfinder"); it is also read as a verb, "shall one who contends … correct?" (so KJV). Uncertain. **"he that reproveth God" → "One who reproves Eloah"** — Hebrew מוֹכִיחַ, the participle of הוֹכִיחַ "reprove". יַעֲנֶנָּה "let him answer it" (or "he shall answer it").
- **"I am vile; what shall I answer thee? I will lay mine hand upon my mouth" → "I am slight; what shall I reply to you? My hand I have put to my mouth"** (v4) — Hebrew קַלֹּתִי "I am light, slight" (not "vile"); אֲשִׁיבֶךָּ "return to you" (= reply; עָנָה "answer" is the word of v5); שַׂמְתִּי a perfect, "I have put". The two cola of the Hebrew are printed as two lines.
- **"but I will proceed no further" → "and I will not do it again"** (v5) — Hebrew וְלֹא אוֹסִיף, literally "and I will not add". Each half has its "and".
- **"out of the whirlwind" → "out of the storm"** (v6) — Hebrew מִן סְעָרָה (qere; the written form joins and spells it otherwise, the same words), as 38:1; סְעָרָה is "storm", no "whirl" in the word.
- **"Gird up thy loins now like a man: I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me" → "Gird up, please, your loins like a man; I will ask you, and you make me know"** (v7) — Hebrew אֱזָר־נָא (נָא "please"); גֶּבֶר "man" (alone in the verse; the word of the mighty, strong man); אֶשְׁאָלְךָ "I will ask you"; וְהוֹדִיעֵנִי an imperative, "and make me know". The same words as 38:3; 42:4.
- **"disannul my judgment" → "make void my judgment"** (v8) — Hebrew הֵפֵר "make void, break"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (the right I give). **"that thou mayest be righteous"** — לְמַעַן תִּצְדָּק, "so that you may be righteous"; הִרְשִׁיעַ "condemn" (declare wicked).
- **"Hast thou an arm like God? or canst thou thunder" → "Or have you an arm like El, and with a voice like his can you thunder?"** (v9) — Hebrew וְאִם "or" in a question; Hebrew order kept.
- **"Deck thyself now with majesty and excellency; and array thyself with glory and beauty" → "Adorn yourself, please, with exaltation and loftiness, and with majesty and splendor clothe yourself"** (v10) — Hebrew גָּאוֹן "exaltation" (the root of גֵּאֶה "proud", v11–12: God's own height, which in man is pride), גֹּבַהּ "height, loftiness"; הוֹד "majesty", הָדָר "splendor".
- **"Cast abroad the rage of thy wrath: and behold every one that is proud, and abase him" → "Scatter the overflowings of your anger, and see every proud one and make him low"** (v11) — Hebrew הָפֵץ "scatter"; עֶבְרוֹת "overflowings" (the word for fury); אַף "anger" (literally "nose"); רְאֵה "see"; הַשְׁפִּילֵהוּ "make him low".
- **"in their place"** (v12) — Hebrew תַּחְתָּם, "under them / where they stand". הִכְנִיעַ "bring low"; הָדַךְ "tread down" is found only here (uncertain).
- **"bind their faces in secret" → "bind their faces in the hidden place"** (v13) — Hebrew בַּטָּמוּן, from the same verb as "hide" (טָמַן) in the first line; the grave is meant.
- **"Then will I also confess unto thee that thine own right hand can save thee" → "And I also will acknowledge you, that your right hand can save you"** (v14) — Hebrew אוֹדֶךָּ, hifil of ידה (elsewhere "give thanks to", "confess"): here praise given to Job in acknowledgment; וְגַם "and also".
- **"Behold now behemoth … he eateth grass as an ox" → "Behold, please, Behemoth … he eats grass like the herd"** (v15) — Hebrew הִנֵּה־נָא (also v16); בְּהֵמוֹת is the plural of בְּהֵמָה "beast", here used of one animal ("the beast of beasts"); the name is kept. כַּבָּקָר "like the herd" (cattle). עִמָּךְ "with you": made as Job was made.
- **"his force is in the navel of his belly" → "his strength in the sinews of his belly"** (v16) — Hebrew אוֹן "strength, vigor"; כֹּחַ "power"; שְׁרִירֵי, found only here, "muscles, sinews" (uncertain).
- **"He moveth his tail like a cedar: the sinews of his stones are wrapped together" → "He bends his tail like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are knit together"** (v17) — Hebrew יַחְפֹּץ: the verb is otherwise "delight in"; here "bend, let hang" or "make stiff" is taken (uncertain). פַּחֲדָיו qere "his thighs" (ketiv singular "his thigh"); פַּחַד in this sense only here (Aramaic "thigh"; "stones" = testicles is the old guess); יְשֹׂרָגוּ "are intertwined".
- **"His bones are as strong pieces of brass; his bones are like bars of iron" → "His bones are channels of bronze, his limbs like a bar of iron"** (v18) — Hebrew אֲפִיקֵי "channels" (stream beds; tubes of bronze are meant), the word of 41:15; עֲצָמָיו and גְּרָמָיו are two words for "bones" (the second is given "limbs" to keep them apart); מְטִיל, only here, a forged bar.
- **"He is the chief of the ways of God: he that made him can make his sword to approach unto him" → "He is the first of the ways of El; the one who made him brings near his sword"** (v19) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית "first, beginning" (as Prov 8:22); הָעֹשׂוֹ "the one who made him"; יַגֵּשׁ חַרְבּוֹ "brings near his sword": only his maker can come at him with a sword; "his" may be the beast's own sword (his teeth). Not a wish ("let …").
- **"Surely the mountains bring him forth food" → "For the mountains bear produce for him"** (v20) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; בּוּל "produce, yield"; יִשְׂאוּ "lift up, bear". **"the beasts of the field" → "the living things of the field"** — Hebrew חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה; שִׂחֵק "play".
- **"He lieth under the shady trees, in the covert of the reed, and fens" → "Under the lotus bushes he lies down, in the covert of reed and marsh"** (v21) — Hebrew צֶאֱלִים (v21, 22 only): the thorny lotus is the usual sense (uncertain); סֵתֶר "covert, hiding place"; בִּצָּה "marsh".
- **"cover him with their shadow; the willows of the brook" → "cover him, as his shade; the willows of the wadi"** (v22) — Hebrew צִלֲלוֹ "his shade", without a preposition ("as" supplied); עַרְבֵי־נָחַל "willows of the wadi" (the kind of tree is uncertain).
- **"Behold, he drinketh up a river, and hasteth not: he trusteth that he can draw up Jordan into his mouth" → "Behold, a river oppresses, he is not alarmed; he trusts, though Jordan bursts forth to his mouth"** (v23) — Hebrew יַעֲשֹׁק נָהָר: עָשַׁק is "oppress, extort", here of a river in flood (the river is the subject); לֹא יַחְפּוֹז "he is not alarmed" (חָפַז "hurry away in alarm"); יִבְטַח "he trusts"; כִּי "though / when"; יָגִיחַ "bursts forth" (as 38:8); יַרְדֵּן is without the article: the Jordan, or any river like it.
- **"He taketh it with his eyes: his nose pierceth through snares" → "By his eyes can one take him? With snares can one pierce the nose?"** (v24) — Hebrew בְּעֵינָיו יִקָּחֶנּוּ בְּמוֹקְשִׁים יִנְקָב־אָף. The Hebrew has no question word; the verse is read as a question, as 41:1 (also read "in his sight", while he watches). אַף "nose" has no "his". מוֹקְשִׁים "snares".

### Notes
- **The Hebrew numbering** — the Hebrew chapter 40 runs on to verse 32: our 41:1–8 are Heb 40:25–32 (see Job 41).
- **Yahweh's second speech** (40:6–41:34) — it opens with the same words as the first (38:1, 3) and puts the question of v8: whether Job will make God wicked so as to be righteous himself.
- **Behemoth** (v15–24) — the beast of the river and the marsh, made "with you" by the same maker; commonly taken as the hippopotamus; the "first of the ways of El" sets it at the head of what El made.
