# Job 12

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Truly, you are the people,
and with you wisdom will die!

[3] I too have a heart like you;
I do not fall below you.
And with whom are there not things like these?

[4] A laughingstock to his friend I am,
one who called to ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧, and he answered him;
a laughingstock, one righteous, blameless!

[5] For disaster, contempt, in the thought of one at ease;
it is ready for those whose foot slips.

[6] The tents of destroyers are untroubled,
and security is for those who agitate ⟦el|El|God⟧,
for him who has brought ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ in his hand.

[7] And yet, ask the beasts, please, and they will teach you,
and the birds of the heavens, and they will tell you;

[8] or talk to the earth, and it will teach you,
and the fish of the sea will recount to you.

[9] Who does not know among all these
that the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done this,

[10] in whose hand is the soul of every living thing
and the spirit of all the flesh of man?

[11] Does not the ear test words,
and the palate taste food for itself?

[12] With the aged is wisdom,
and length of days is understanding.

[13] With him are wisdom and might;
to him belong counsel and understanding.

[14] Behold, he throws down, and it is not built;
he shuts upon a man, and it is not opened.

[15] Behold, he restrains the waters, and they are dried out;
and he lets them go, and they overturn the earth.

[16] With him are strength and sound wisdom;
to him belong the one who errs and the one who makes him err.

[17] He makes counselors walk stripped,
and judges he makes fools.

[18] The discipline of kings he loosens,
and he binds a belt on their loins.

[19] He makes priests walk stripped,
and the enduring he overturns.

[20] He removes the lip from the faithful,
and the sense of elders he takes away.

[21] He pours contempt on nobles,
and the girdle of the strong he slackens.

[22] He uncovers deep things out of darkness,
and brings out to light the shadow of death.

[23] He makes the nations great, and does away with them;
he spreads out the nations, and leads them.

[24] He removes the heart of the heads of the people of the land,
and makes them wander in a waste without a way.

[25] They feel about in darkness, and not light,
and he makes them wander like a drunkard.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v9) — Tetragrammaton preserved; the only יהוה in the speeches of Job and his friends (3–37).
- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v4, 6) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ. **"God" → "El"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵל. Verse 6 has both names.
- **"Then Job answered" → "And Job answered"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן אִיּוֹב וַיֹּאמַר, the heading of every speech.
- **"No doubt but ye are the people" → "Truly, you are the people"** (v2) — Hebrew אָמְנָם כִּי אַתֶּם־עָם; עָם has no article ("you are people"). **"wisdom shall die with you"** — the Hebrew order is "and with you wisdom will die".
- **"I have understanding as well as you" → "I too have a heart like you"** (v3) — Hebrew לֵבָב "heart". **"I am not inferior to you" → "I do not fall below you"** — Hebrew לֹא־נֹפֵל אָנֹכִי מִכֶּם, literally "I am not falling from you" (so 13:2). **"who knoweth not such things as these?" → "And with whom are there not things like these?"** — Hebrew וְאֶת־מִי אֵין כְּמוֹ־אֵלֶּה.
- **"I am as one mocked of his neighbour" → "A laughingstock to his friend I am"** (v4) — Hebrew שְׂחֹק לְרֵעֵהוּ אֶהְיֶה: "his friend", third person, though Job speaks of himself. **"who calleth upon God, and he answereth him"** — a participle and a narrative verb: "one who called … and he answered him". **"the just upright man is laughed to scorn" → "a laughingstock, one righteous, blameless"** — Hebrew שְׂחוֹק צַדִּיק תָּמִים, without verb or article.
- **"He that is ready to slip with his feet is as a lamp despised in the thought of him that is at ease" → "For disaster, contempt, in the thought of one at ease; it is ready for those whose foot slips"** (v5) — Hebrew לַפִּיד בּוּז לְעַשְׁתּוּת שַׁאֲנָן נָכוֹן לְמוֹעֲדֵי רָגֶל. לַפִּיד is read as לְ + פִּיד "disaster" with the article (as 30:24; 31:29); the same letters and pointing also give לַפִּיד "a torch" (KJV "lamp"). Which is meant is uncertain. נָכוֹן is "ready" here (niphal of כּוּן; "right / established" of its other places would not fit). עַשְׁתּוּת "thought" is found only here.
- **"The tabernacles of robbers prosper, and they that provoke God are secure; into whose hand God bringeth abundantly" → "The tents of destroyers are untroubled … for him who has brought Eloah in his hand"** (v6) — Hebrew יִשְׁלָיוּ "are untroubled" (שָׁלָה, as 3:26; שַׁאֲנָן "at ease" is v5); שֹׁדְדִים "destroyers" (שָׁדַד "devastate"); בַּטֻּחוֹת "securities"; מַרְגִּיזֵי אֵל "those who agitate El" (hifil of רָגַז "be agitated"). The last line, לַאֲשֶׁר הֵבִיא אֱלוֹהַּ בְּיָדוֹ, is literally "to whom brought Eloah in his hand": either Eloah is the object (one who carries his god in his hand, as the verse gives it) or Eloah is the subject ("whom Eloah brought into his hand", KJV). "Abundantly" is not in the Hebrew. The divine name is kept, not "their god".
- **"But ask now" → "And yet, ask … please"** (v7) — Hebrew וְאוּלָם שְׁאַל־נָא; "you" is singular in v7–8. **"the fowls of the air" → "the birds of the heavens"** — Hebrew עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם. "Beasts" is בְּהֵמוֹת, the word that is a name in 40:15.
- **"speak to the earth" → "talk to the earth"** (v8) — Hebrew שִׂיחַ לָאָרֶץ; the same letters are the noun "shrub" ("the shrubs of the earth"), which some prefer. **"shall declare unto thee" → "will recount to you"** — Hebrew וִיסַפְּרוּ.
- **"Who knoweth not in all these" → "Who does not know among all these"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּכָל־אֵלֶּה. Verses 9–10 are one sentence (אֲשֶׁר "in whose").
- **"the breath of all mankind" → "the spirit of all the flesh of man"** (v10) — Hebrew רוּחַ כָּל־בְּשַׂר־אִישׁ; רוּחַ is "spirit" and "breath": one word.
- **"and the mouth taste his meat?" → "and the palate taste food for itself?"** (v11) — Hebrew וְחֵךְ אֹכֶל יִטְעַם־לוֹ.
- **"With the ancient is wisdom; and in length of days understanding" → "… and length of days is understanding"** (v12) — the Hebrew has no "in" in the second line. Some read the verse as a question or as the friends' claim, which v13 answers; the Hebrew does not mark it.
- **"strength" → "might"** (v13) — Hebrew גְּבוּרָה (עֹז "strength" is v16).
- **"he breaketh down" → "he throws down"** (v14) — Hebrew הָרַס. **"it cannot be built again … there can be no opening" → "it is not built … it is not opened"** — Hebrew passives, "again" is not written. **"he shutteth up a man" → "he shuts upon a man"** — Hebrew יִסְגֹּר עַל־אִישׁ.
- **"he withholdeth the waters" → "he restrains the waters"** (v15) — Hebrew עָצַר; וְיִבָשׁוּ "and they are dried out". **"he sendeth them out" → "he lets them go"** — Hebrew שִׁלַּח piel, of what was held back. **"overturn"** — Hebrew הָפַךְ.
- **"wisdom" → "sound wisdom"** (v16) — Hebrew תּוּשִׁיָּה, not חָכְמָה (v13); the meaning is uncertain (sound counsel, success, help that endures). **"the deceived and the deceiver" → "the one who errs and the one who makes him err"** — Hebrew שֹׁגֵג וּמַשְׁגֶּה, qal and hifil of שָׁגַג / שָׁגָה "err"; "him" is supplied.
- **"He leadeth counsellors away spoiled" → "He makes counselors walk stripped"** (v17, 19) — Hebrew מוֹלִיךְ … שׁוֹלָל; שׁוֹלָל "stripped" or "barefoot" (Mic 1:8), uncertain. **"maketh the judges fools"** — Hebrew יְהוֹלֵל, "makes mad / foolish".
- **"He looseth the bond of kings" → "The discipline of kings he loosens"** (v18) — the MT points מוּסַר, construct of מוּסָר "discipline" (the authority of kings); the consonants also allow מוֹסֵר "bond, fetter" (KJV), which most modern versions follow. The MT pointing is kept; uncertain. **"girdeth their loins with a girdle" → "he binds a belt on their loins"** — Hebrew וַיֶּאְסֹר אֵזוֹר בְּמָתְנֵיהֶם; אָסַר "bind", אֵזוֹר "belt".
- **"princes" → "priests"** (v19) — Hebrew כֹּהֲנִים. **"overthroweth the mighty" → "the enduring he overturns"** — Hebrew אֵתָנִים "enduring, ever-flowing ones"; סִלֵּף "overturn".
- **"He removeth away the speech of the trusty" → "He removes the lip from the faithful"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׂפָה "lip", here of speech; נֶאֱמָנִים "the faithful, trusted ones". **"the understanding of the aged" → "the sense of elders"** — Hebrew טַעַם, literally "taste".
- **"weakeneth the strength of the mighty" → "the girdle of the strong he slackens"** (v21) — Hebrew מְזִיחַ אֲפִיקִים רִפָּה. מְזִיחַ "girdle" (as מֵזַח Ps 109:19); אֲפִיקִים is elsewhere "channels" (2 Sam 22:16); "the strong" is uncertain.
- **"the shadow of death" kept** (v22) — Hebrew צַלְמָוֶת, pointed as צֵל "shadow" + מָוֶת "death". Many read צַלְמוּת "deep darkness"; the MT pointing is kept. **"discovereth" → "uncovers"** — Hebrew גִּלָּה.
- **"He increaseth the nations, and destroyeth them" → "He makes the nations great, and does away with them"** (v23) — Hebrew מַשְׂגִּיא; אִבֵּד piel. **"straiteneth them again" → "leads them"** — Hebrew וַיַּנְחֵם, from נָחָה "lead" (whether away into exile or to rest is not said).
- **"He taketh away the heart of the chief of the people of the earth" → "He removes the heart of the heads of the people of the land"** (v24) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי עַם־הָאָרֶץ; לֵב "heart". **"in a wilderness where there is no way" → "in a waste without a way"** — Hebrew בְּתֹהוּ לֹא־דָרֶךְ; תֹּהוּ as Gen 1:2.
- **"They grope in the dark without light" → "They feel about in darkness, and not light"** (v25) — Hebrew יְמַשְׁשׁוּ־חֹשֶׁךְ וְלֹא־אוֹר. **"he maketh them to stagger" → "he makes them wander"** — Hebrew וַיַּתְעֵם, the same verb as v24.

### Notes
- **Irony** (v2) — "with you wisdom will die".
- **Raiders at ease** (v6) — against the friends: those who agitate El live secure.
- **"Ask the beasts"** (v7–10) — the creatures themselves know that Yahweh's hand did this.
- **The reversals** (v13–25) — counselors, judges, kings, priests, elders, nobles, nations: each brought low by him.
