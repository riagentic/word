# Job 35

[1] And Elihu answered and said:

[2] "Do you reckon this as judgment?
You have said, 'My righteousness is from ⟦el|El|God⟧.'

[3] For you say, 'What does it profit you?
What shall I gain from my sin?'

[4] I will return utterances to you,
and to your friends with you.

[5] Look at the heavens and see,
and watch the skies; they are higher than you.

[6] If you have sinned, what do you work against him?
And your transgressions are many, what do you do to him?

[7] If you are righteous, what do you give him?
Or what does he take from your hand?

[8] For a man like you is your wickedness,
and for a son of man your righteousness.

[9] From the multitude of extortions they cry out;
they cry for help from the arm of the many.

[10] And he does not say, 'Where is ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ my Maker,
who gives songs in the night,

[11] who teaches us from the beasts of the earth,
and from the birds of the heavens makes us wise?'

[12] There they cry out, and he does not answer,
because of the pride of evil ones.

[13] Surely ⟦el|El|God⟧ does not hear emptiness,
and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ does not watch it.

[14] How much less when you say you do not watch him;
the cause is before him, and you wait for him!

[15] And now, because his anger has not visited,
and he does not greatly know of the folly,

[16] and ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ opens his mouth with vapor;
without knowledge he makes many utterances."

## Changes

- **"God" → "El"** (v2, 13) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*. **"God" → "Eloah"** (v10) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v13) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי.
- **"Elihu spake moreover, and said" → "And Elihu answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the formula of every speech in the book.
- **"Thinkest thou this to be right, that thou saidst, My righteousness is more than God's?" → "Do you reckon this as judgment? You have said, 'My righteousness is from El'"** (v2) — Hebrew חָשַׁבְתָּ לְמִשְׁפָּט "reckon for judgment"; צִדְקִי מֵאֵל, literally "my righteousness from El": מִן may be "more than" (KJV "more than God's") or "from", or "before El"; left open. "That" is not written: two clauses.
- **"What advantage will it be unto thee? and, What profit shall I have, if I be cleansed from my sin?" → "What does it profit you? What shall I gain from my sin?"** (v3) — Hebrew יִסְכָּן "profit" (as 34:9); אֹעִיל "gain"; מֵחַטָּאתִי "from my sin" (= more than if I sinned); "if I be cleansed" is not written.
- **"I will answer thee, and thy companions with thee" → "I will return utterances to you, and to your friends with you"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲשִׁיבְךָ מִלִּין, "I will return you utterances" (= answer); אֲנִי "I" stands first; רֵעַ "friend". Two Hebrew lines, two lines.
- **"Look unto the heavens … and behold the clouds which are higher than thou" → "Look at the heavens … and watch the skies; they are higher than you"** (v5) — Hebrew הַבֵּט "look at"; שׁוּר "watch" (also v13, 14); שְׁחָקִים "skies"; גָּבְהוּ "they are high" with מִן "than".
- **"what doest thou against him? or if thy transgressions be multiplied" → "what do you work against him? And your transgressions are many"** (v6) — Hebrew תִּפְעָל "work"; וְרַבּוּ "and (if) they are many": the "if" carries over.
- **"what receiveth he of thine hand?" → "what does he take from your hand?"** (v7) — Hebrew יִקָּח "take".
- **"Thy wickedness may hurt a man as thou art; and thy righteousness may profit the son of man" → "For a man like you is your wickedness, and for a son of man your righteousness"** (v8) — Hebrew לְאִישׁ־כָּמוֹךָ רִשְׁעֶךָ, a clause without a verb ("is" supplied); "hurt" and "profit" are not written.
- **"By reason of the multitude of oppressions they make the oppressed to cry: they cry out by reason of the arm of the mighty" → "From the multitude of extortions they cry out; they cry for help from the arm of the many"** (v9) — Hebrew עֲשׁוּקִים "extortions" (עָשַׁק "extort"); יַזְעִיקוּ "they cry out" (hifil, without an object); יְשַׁוְּעוּ "cry for help"; רַבִּים "the many" (or "the great"; the root of רֹב "multitude").
- **"But none saith" → "And he does not say"** (v10) — Hebrew וְלֹא־אָמַר, singular, without a subject (the one who cries out). עֹשָׂי "my Maker" (plural form, as Isa 54:5). זְמִרוֹת "songs".
- **"Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and maketh us wiser than the fowls of heaven?" → "who teaches us from the beasts of the earth, and from the birds of the heavens makes us wise?"** (v11) — Hebrew מַלְּפֵנוּ (= מְאַלְּפֵנוּ, the verb "teach" of 33:33) with מִן "from" twice: "more than" or "by means of"; left open.
- **"but none giveth answer, because of the pride of evil men" → "and he does not answer, because of the pride of evil ones"** (v12) — Hebrew וְלֹא יַעֲנֶה "and he does not answer"; מִפְּנֵי "because of"; רָעִים "evil ones" ("men" is not written).
- **"Surely God will not hear vanity, neither will the Almighty regard it" → "Surely El does not hear emptiness, and Shaddai does not watch it"** (v13) — Hebrew שָׁוְא "emptiness" (what has nothing in it; "an empty cry" in the earlier wording supplied "cry").
- **"Although thou sayest thou shalt not see him, yet judgment is before him; therefore trust thou in him" → "How much less when you say you do not watch him; the cause is before him, and you wait for him!"** (v14) — Hebrew אַף כִּי "how much less" (also "even though", KJV "although"); דִּין "cause"; תְּחוֹלֵל לוֹ "you wait for him" (חוּל polel, also "writhe"); "therefore" is not written. Two Hebrew lines, two lines.
- **"But now, because it is not so, he hath visited in his anger; yet he knoweth it not in great extremity" → "And now, because his anger has not visited, and he does not greatly know of the folly"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי־אַיִן פָּקַד אַפּוֹ, literally "because there is not — his anger has visited" (KJV "because it is not so, he hath visited"); פָּקַד "visit" (to call to account). פַּשׁ is found only here: "folly" (uncertain; also "arrogance"; many read פֶּשַׁע "transgression", the earlier wording; MT kept). יָדַע "know" (= take note of).
- **"Therefore doth Job open his mouth in vain" → "and Iyov opens his mouth with vapor"** (v16) — Hebrew וְ "and" ("therefore" is not written); הֶבֶל "vapor, breath" (KJV "in vain"); פָּצָה פֶּה "open the mouth". **"he multiplieth words" → "he makes many utterances"** — Hebrew מִלִּין יַכְבִּר, hifil of כָּבַר "be much" (34:37 has another verb, יֶרֶב "multiplies").

### Notes
- **Elihu's third speech** — neither sin nor righteousness reaches Eloah (v5–8); they reach a man like Job.
- **"Songs in the night"** (v10) — said of Eloah, the Maker.
- **The cry that is not heard** (v12–13) — El does not hear emptiness.
- **Charge of speaking without knowledge** (v16) — repeats 34:35–37.
