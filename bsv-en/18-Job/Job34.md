# Job 34

[1] And Elihu answered and said:

[2] "Hear my utterances, you wise,
and you who know, give ear to me.

[3] For the ear tries utterances,
and the palate tastes food.

[4] Judgment, let us choose it for ourselves;
let us know among ourselves what is good.

[5] For ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ has said, 'I am righteous,
and ⟦el|El|God⟧ has removed my judgment.

[6] Concerning my judgment I lie;
my arrow is incurable, without transgression.'

[7] What man is like ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧,
who drinks mockery like water,

[8] and travels for company with the workers of wickedness,
and to walk with men of wickedness?

[9] For he has said, 'A man does not profit
when he is pleased with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'

[10] Therefore, men of heart, listen to me:
far be wickedness from ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and injustice from ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧.

[11] For the work of a human he repays to him,
and according to the path of a man he makes it find him.

[12] Yes, truly, ⟦el|El|God⟧ does not do wickedly,
and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ does not pervert judgment.

[13] Who appointed the earth upon him?
And who set the whole world?

[14] If he sets his heart to him,
his spirit and his breath he gathers to him,

[15] all flesh expires together,
and man returns to dust.

[16] And if you have understanding, hear this;
give ear to the voice of my utterances.

[17] Shall even one who hates judgment bind?
And will you condemn the righteous mighty one,

[18] — does one say to a king, '⟦belial|Beliyyaal|Worthlessness⟧!',
'Wicked!' to nobles? —

[19] who does not lift up the face of princes,
and does not recognize the rich before the poor,
for they are all the work of his hands?

[20] In a moment they die,
and at midnight a people is shaken, and they pass on,
and they remove the mighty one, not by hand.

[21] For his eyes are on the ways of a man,
and he sees all his steps.

[22] There is no darkness and there is no shadow of death
for the workers of wickedness to hide themselves there.

[23] For he does not set on a man any more,
to go to ⟦el|El|God⟧ in judgment.

[24] He shatters mighty ones without examining,
and makes others stand in their place.

[25] Therefore he recognizes their deeds,
and he overturns by night, and they are crushed.

[26] As wicked ones he strikes them,
in the place of those who see,

[27] because they turned aside from after him,
and had no insight into any of his ways,

[28] so as to bring upon him the outcry of the poor,
and the outcry of the afflicted he hears.

[29] And he gives quiet, and who will condemn?
And he hides the face, and who will watch him? —
both over a nation and over a man together,

[30] from the reigning of a godless man,
from the snares of the people.

[31] For has one said to ⟦el|El|God⟧,
'I have borne; I will not act corruptly;

[32] apart from what I behold, you teach me;
if I have worked injustice, I will add no more'?

[33] Shall he repay it from with you,
because you have rejected?
For you shall choose, and not I;
and what you know, speak.

[34] Men of heart will say to me,
and a wise mighty man who hears me:

[35] '⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ speaks without knowledge,
and his words are without insight.'

[36] Would that ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ were tried to the end,
for answers among men of wickedness.

[37] For he adds transgression to his sin;
among us he claps,
and multiplies his sayings to ⟦el|El|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"God" → "El"** (v5, 10, 12, 23, 31, 37) — Hebrew אֵל *El*. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים, the only *Elohim* in the chapter. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v10, 12) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי.
- **"Furthermore Elihu answered and said" → "And Elihu answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the formula of every speech in the book.
- **"Hear my words" → "Hear my utterances"** (v2, 3, 16) — Hebrew מִלָּה "utterance" (beside דָּבָר "word", v35, and אֵמֶר "saying", v37). **"O ye wise men"** — Hebrew חֲכָמִים "wise ones"; "men" is not written.
- **"the ear trieth words, as the mouth tasteth meat" → "the ear tries utterances, and the palate tastes food"** (v3) — Hebrew וְ "and", not "as"; חֵךְ "palate"; לֶאֱכֹל, literally "to eat".
- **"Let us choose to us judgment"** (v4) — kept; Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" stands first, as the object.
- **"God hath taken away my judgment" → "El has removed my judgment"** (v5) — Hebrew הֵסִיר "remove"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (= my right, my case; so v6).
- **"Should I lie against my right? my wound is incurable" → "Concerning my judgment I lie; my arrow is incurable"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־מִשְׁפָּטִי אֲכַזֵּב, "concerning (against) my judgment I lie": that is, I must give the lie to my own case; it may also be read as a question, as KJV, but the Hebrew does not mark one. חִצִּי "my arrow" (the arrow in me); KJV "wound" is the sense. אָנוּשׁ "incurable".
- **"who drinketh up scorning" → "who drinks mockery"** (v7) — Hebrew לַעַג. **"What man"** (also v9, 34) — Hebrew גֶּבֶר: "man" where it stands alone (v7, 9); "mighty man" in v34, where אַנְשֵׁי (plural of אִישׁ) stands in the same verse.
- **"Which goeth in company with the workers of iniquity, and walketh with wicked men" → "and travels for company with the workers of wickedness, and to walk with men of wickedness"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאָרַח לְחֶבְרָה, "and travels for company"; וְלָלֶכֶת, an infinitive, "and to walk". פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness" (אָוֶן; עָוֹן is "iniquity") and אַנְשֵׁי־רֶשַׁע "men of wickedness" (רֶשַׁע): two Hebrew words, one English word (CZ keeps them apart).
- **"It profiteth a man nothing that he should delight himself with God" → "A man does not profit when he is pleased with Elohim"** (v9) — Hebrew לֹא יִסְכָּן־גָּבֶר "a man does not profit"; בִּרְצֹתוֹ עִם "in his being pleased with" (רָצָה; also read "when he is in favor with").
- **"ye men of understanding" → "men of heart"** (v10, 34) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי לֵבָב; the heart is the seat of understanding. **"far be it from God, that he should do wickedness; and from the Almighty, that he should commit iniquity" → "far be wickedness from El, and injustice from Shaddai"** — Hebrew חָלִלָה לָאֵל מֵרֶשַׁע וְשַׁדַּי מֵעָוֶל, literally "far be it to El from wickedness, and Shaddai from injustice"; "that he should do" is not written. עָוֶל "injustice" (also v32).
- **"For the work of a man shall he render unto him, and cause every man to find according to his ways" → "For the work of a human he repays to him, and according to the path of a man he makes it find him"** (v11) — Hebrew פֹעַל אָדָם … אִישׁ: two words for man, kept apart ("human" for אָדָם, as 38:26); וּכְאֹרַח אִישׁ יַמְצִאֶנּוּ; אֹרַח "path" (singular). שִׁלֵּם "repay".
- **"God will not do wickedly" → "El does not do wickedly"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא־יַרְשִׁיעַ, without an object ("condemn" with an object, v17, 29). עִוֵּת "pervert".
- **"Who hath given him a charge over the earth? or who hath disposed the whole world?" → "Who appointed the earth upon him? And who set the whole world?"** (v13) — Hebrew מִי־פָקַד עָלָיו אָרְצָה, "who appointed upon him the earth" (= put it in his charge); שָׂם "set", without "in place".
- **"If he set his heart upon man, if he gather unto himself" → "If he sets his heart to him, his spirit and his breath he gathers to him"** (v14) — Hebrew אֵלָיו twice, "to him": "upon man" is not written, and "to him" may be "to himself"; whose spirit and breath (his own or man's) is not said. רוּחַ "spirit", נְשָׁמָה "breath".
- **"All flesh shall perish together" → "all flesh expires together"** (v15) — Hebrew יִגְוַע "expire"; יַחַד "together".
- **"If now thou hast understanding" → "And if you have understanding"** (v16) — Hebrew וְאִם־בִּינָה, "and if understanding": "you have" is supplied (בִּינָה may also be read "understand!").
- **"Shall even he that hateth right govern? and wilt thou condemn him that is most just?" → "Shall even one who hates judgment bind? And will you condemn the righteous mighty one"** (v17) — Hebrew יַחֲבוֹשׁ "bind" (to bind up, or to bind in, rule: KJV "govern"); צַדִּיק כַּבִּיר "a righteous one, a mighty one" (not a superlative).
- **"Is it fit to say to a king, Thou art wicked? and to princes, Ye are ungodly?" → "does one say to a king, 'Worthlessness!', 'Wicked!' to nobles?"** (v18) — Hebrew הַאֲמֹר, a question with the infinitive, "is it to say"; בְּלִיָּעַל "worthlessness" (the word of "sons of Beliyyaal"); רָשָׁע "wicked"; נְדִיבִים "nobles" (lit. "willing ones").
- **"How much less to him that accepteth not the persons of princes, nor regardeth the rich more than the poor" → "who does not lift up the face of princes, and does not recognize the rich before the poor"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר "who"; "how much less" is not written. The question opened in v17 ("will you condemn the righteous mighty one … who …?") closes at the end of v19; v18 stands between. נָשָׂא פְּנֵי "lift up the face of" (= show regard to); נִכַּר "recognize"; שׁוֹעַ "rich" (uncertain: "noble, free", Isa 32:5); דַּל "poor".
- **"the people shall be troubled at midnight, and pass away: and the mighty shall be taken away without hand" → "a people is shaken, and they pass on, and they remove the mighty one, not by hand"** (v20) — Hebrew יְגֹעֲשׁוּ "are shaken" (the verb "heave" of 2 Sam 22:8); וְיָסִירוּ אַבִּיר "and they remove a mighty one" (the subject is not named); לֹא בְיָד "not by hand" ("human" is not written). The lines follow the accents: the first break falls after רֶגַע יָמֻתוּ "in a moment they die", the main one after וְיַעֲבֹרוּ.
- **"nor shadow of death, where the workers of iniquity may hide themselves" → "and there is no shadow of death for the workers of wickedness to hide themselves there"** (v22) — Hebrew צַלְמָוֶת, as the Masoretes point it "shadow of death"; many derive it from a root "be dark", "deep darkness".
- **"For he will not lay upon man more than right; that he should enter into judgment with God" → "For he does not set on a man any more, to go to El in judgment"** (v23) — Hebrew כִּי לֹא עַל־אִישׁ יָשִׂים עוֹד; the object of "set" is not written ("more than right" is KJV's supply). Many read מוֹעֵד "an appointed time" for עוֹד "any more" (earlier wording "appointed a time"); MT kept.
- **"He shall break in pieces mighty men without number" → "He shatters mighty ones without examining"** (v24) — Hebrew יָרֹעַ "shatter"; לֹא־חֵקֶר "with no examining" (חֵקֶר, noun of חָקַר "examine"). וַיַּעֲמֵד "and makes stand".
- **"he knoweth their works, and he overturneth them in the night, so that they are destroyed" → "he recognizes their deeds, and he overturns by night, and they are crushed"** (v25) — Hebrew יַכִּיר "recognize"; מַעְבָּדֵיהֶם, an Aramaic word, only here, "their deeds"; "them" is not written after הָפַךְ "overturn"; דָּכָא "crush". The second half is one Hebrew line.
- **"He striketh them as wicked men in the open sight of others" → "As wicked ones he strikes them, in the place of those who see"** (v26) — Hebrew תַּחַת־רְשָׁעִים "in place of wicked ones" (= as wicked ones); סְפָקָם "strikes them" (סָפַק, "clap" in v37: the one verb, struck with the open hand); בִּמְקוֹם רֹאִים "in the place of those who see".
- **"Because they turned back from him, and would not consider any of his ways" → "because they turned aside from after him, and had no insight into any of his ways"** (v27) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עַל־כֵּן "because"; סָרוּ מֵאַחֲרָיו "turned aside from after him"; הִשְׂכִּילוּ "have insight".
- **"So that they cause the cry of the poor to come unto him, and he heareth the cry of the afflicted" → "so as to bring upon him the outcry of the poor, and the outcry of the afflicted he hears"** (v28) — Hebrew לְהָבִיא עָלָיו "to bring upon him"; צְעָקָה "outcry"; עָנִי "afflicted".
- **"When he giveth quietness, who then can make trouble? and when he hideth his face, who then can behold him? whether it be done against a nation, or against a man only" → "And he gives quiet, and who will condemn? And he hides the face, and who will watch him? — both over a nation and over a man together"** (v29) — Hebrew יַשְׁקִט "give quiet"; יַרְשִׁעַ "condemn" (as v17); פָּנִים "the face", without "his"; יְשׁוּרֶנּוּ "watch him"; יָחַד "together".
- **"That the hypocrite reign not, lest the people be ensnared" → "from the reigning of a godless man, from the snares of the people"** (v30) — Hebrew מִמְּלֹךְ אָדָם חָנֵף מִמֹּקְשֵׁי עָם, word for word (= that a godless man may not reign, that there be no snares for the people); חָנֵף "godless" (lit. "profane, polluted"; KJV "hypocrite").
- **"Surely it is meet to be said unto God, I have borne chastisement, I will not offend any more" → "For has one said to El, 'I have borne; I will not act corruptly'"** (v31) — Hebrew הֶאָמַר, a question, "has one said"; נָשָׂאתִי "I have borne" without an object ("chastisement" is not written); אֶחְבֹּל "act corruptly" (חָבַל, not the חָבַל "take in pledge").
- **"That which I see not teach thou me: if I have done iniquity, I will do no more" → "apart from what I behold, you teach me; if I have worked injustice, I will add no more"** (v32) — Hebrew בִּלְעֲדֵי אֶחֱזֶה "apart from what I behold"; אַתָּה הֹרֵנִי "you, teach me"; לֹא אֹסִיף "I will not add".
- **"Should it be according to thy mind? he will recompense it, whether thou refuse, or whether thou choose; and not I: therefore speak what thou knowest" → "Shall he repay it from with you, because you have rejected? For you shall choose, and not I; and what you know, speak"** (v33) — Hebrew הַמֵעִמְּךָ יְשַׁלְמֶנָּה, "is it from with you he shall repay it" (= on your terms); כִּי־מָאַסְתָּ "because you have rejected", without an object ("his way" in the earlier wording is not written).
- **"Let men of understanding tell me, and let a wise man hearken unto me" → "Men of heart will say to me, and a wise mighty man who hears me"** (v34) — Hebrew יֹאמְרוּ לִי "will say to me"; שֹׁמֵעַ לִי "hearing me"; what they say follows in v35.
- **"his words were without wisdom" → "his words are without insight"** (v35) — Hebrew בְהַשְׂכֵּיל "with insight" (the verb of v27).
- **"My desire is that Job may be tried unto the end because of his answers for wicked men" → "Would that Iyov were tried to the end, for answers among men of wickedness"** (v36) — Hebrew אָבִי is uncertain: as written "my father"; also taken as a word of wishing (KJV "my desire is", as from אָבָה "be willing"). עַל־תְּשֻׁבֹת בְּאַנְשֵׁי־אָוֶן "for answers among (or: like) men of wickedness".
- **"he addeth rebellion unto his sin, he clappeth his hands among us, and multiplieth his words against God" → "he adds transgression to his sin; among us he claps, and multiplies his sayings to El"** (v37) — Hebrew פֶּשַׁע "transgression"; יִסְפּוֹק "he claps" ("his hands" is not written); אֲמָרָיו "his sayings"; לָאֵל "to El" (= against El).

### Notes
- **Elihu's second speech** — defends the justice of El and Shaddai.
- **Job's words quoted** (v5–6, 9) — Elihu cites Job as saying El has removed his judgment.
- **Impartiality** (v18–19) — kings, nobles, the rich and the poor are all the work of his hands.
- **"Without examining"** (v24) — the verse does not say why no examining is needed; v21 says his eyes are on every way.
- **The words a man might say to El** (v31–32) — given as a question.
- **Harsh verdict on Job** (v35–37) — speaking without knowledge, adding transgression to sin.
