# Job 4

[1] And ⟦eliphaz|Elifaz|Eliphaz⟧ the ⟦temanite|Temani|Temanite⟧ answered and said:

[2] "If one attempts a word toward you, will you be weary?
And to hold back utterances, who is able?

[3] Behold, you have disciplined many,
and slack hands you made strong.

[4] The stumbling one your utterances raised up,
and bowing knees you strengthened.

[5] But now it comes to you, and you are weary;
it touches you, and you are dismayed.

[6] Is not your fear your confidence,
your hope, and the integrity of your ways?

[7] Remember, please: who that was innocent perished?
And where were the upright effaced?

[8] As I have seen: those who plow wickedness
and those who sow toil reap it.

[9] From the breath of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ they perish,
and from the wind of his nose they come to an end.

[10] The roar of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion,
and the teeth of the young lions are broken out.

[11] The old lion perishes for lack of prey,
and the sons of the lioness are parted.

[12] And to me a word was brought by stealth,
and my ear took a whisper of it.

[13] In disquieting thoughts from visions of the night,
when deep sleep falls on men,

[14] dread met me, and trembling,
and the multitude of my bones it made to dread.

[15] And a spirit passed over my face;
the hair of my flesh bristled.

[16] It stood, and I did not recognize its appearance;
a form was before my eyes;
stillness, and a voice I heard:

[17] 'Is mortal man more righteous than ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧?
Or is a mighty man purer than his Maker?

[18] Behold, in his servants he does not believe,
and his angels he charges with error.

[19] How much more those who abide in houses of clay,
whose foundation is in the dust:
they crush them before the moth.

[20] From morning to evening they are beaten in pieces;
without anyone heeding, forever they perish.

[21] Is not their cord pulled up within them?
They die, and not in wisdom.'"

## Changes

- **"Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said" → "And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one heading formula of the book.
- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v9, 17) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*.
- **"If we assay to commune with thee, wilt thou be grieved? but who can withhold himself from speaking?" → "If one attempts a word toward you, will you be weary? And to hold back utterances, who is able?"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲנִסָּה דָבָר אֵלֶיךָ, a question, literally "Has one attempted a word to you?"; נִסָּה is the verb "test", here of attempting (as Deut 4:34; 28:56 "tried"). עָצַר בְּמִלִּין, literally "restrain in utterances". מִלָּה "utterance" (Job's frequent word, Aramaic plural מִלִּין) is kept apart from דָּבָר "word" here and in v4.
- **"instructed" → "disciplined"** (v3) — Hebrew יִסַּרְתָּ, the verb of מוּסָר "discipline" (5:17): to train by correction. **"the weak hands" → "slack hands"** — Hebrew רָפוֹת. **"strengthened … strengthened" → "made strong … strengthened"** (v3, 4) — two verbs, חִזֵּק and אִמֵּץ. The imperfects of v3b–4 describe what Job used to do.
- **"him that was falling" → "the stumbling one"**, **"feeble knees" → "bowing knees"** (v4) — Hebrew כּוֹשֵׁל, כֹּרְעוֹת (כָּרַע "bow").
- **"But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest … thou art troubled" → "But now it comes to you, and you are weary … you are dismayed"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי עַתָּה, literally "for now"; וַתֵּלֶא, the verb of v2 ("be weary"); נִבְהַל "be dismayed".
- **"Is not this thy fear, thy confidence, thy hope, and the uprightness of thy ways?" → "Is not your fear your confidence, your hope, and the integrity of your ways?"** (v6) — Hebrew הֲלֹא יִרְאָתְךָ כִּסְלָתֶךָ תִּקְוָתְךָ וְתֹם דְּרָכֶיךָ. The MT names no object of "fear" ("of God" in other versions is supplied). The second line may also be read "and your hope the integrity of your ways"; the Hebrew order is kept and the reading left open. כִּסְלָה "confidence" (the same word is "folly" in Ps 85:8). תֹּם "integrity".
- **"Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished, being innocent? or where were the righteous cut off?" → "Remember, please: who that was innocent perished? And where were the upright effaced?"** (v7) — Hebrew זְכָר־נָא; מִי הוּא נָקִי אָבָד, literally "who is he, innocent, perished?"; "ever" is not written. יְשָׁרִים "upright"; נִכְחָדוּ, nifal of כָּחַד "hide": "be effaced, made to vanish".
- **"they that plow iniquity, and sow wickedness, reap the same" → "those who plow wickedness and those who sow toil reap it"** (v8) — Hebrew חֹרְשֵׁי אָוֶן וְזֹרְעֵי עָמָל יִקְצְרֻהוּ: אָוֶן "wickedness" (also "trouble", as 5:6), עָמָל "toil"; the suffix "it" is written.
- **"By the blast of God they perish, and by the breath of his nostrils are they consumed" → "From the breath of Eloah they perish, and from the wind of his nose they come to an end"** (v9) — Hebrew מִנִּשְׁמַת … וּמֵרוּחַ אַפּוֹ: נְשָׁמָה "breath", רוּחַ "wind, breath, spirit" (one word), אַף "nose" (also "anger"); יִכְלוּ from כָּלָה "come to an end" (so 7:6, 9).
- **Lions** (v10, 11) — five words: אַרְיֵה "lion", שַׁחַל "fierce lion", כְּפִיר "young lion", לַיִשׁ "old lion", לָבִיא "lioness". What kind of lion שַׁחַל and לַיִשׁ name is uncertain; the renderings are KJV's, kept one word for one word. **"are broken" → "are broken out"** (v10) — Hebrew נִתָּעוּ, found only here, taken as a by-form of נָתַץ "break down"; the first line has no verb of its own.
- **"the stout lion's whelps are scattered abroad" → "the sons of the lioness are parted"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּנֵי לָבִיא; יִתְפָּרָדוּ, hitpael of פָּרַד "part".
- **"Now a thing was secretly brought to me, and mine ear received a little thereof" → "And to me a word was brought by stealth, and my ear took a whisper of it"** (v12) — Hebrew דָּבָר "word"; יְגֻנָּב, pual of גָּנַב "steal", literally "was stolen"; וַתִּקַּח "took". שֵׁמֶץ "whisper" (only here and 26:14; uncertain: "a trace").
- **"In thoughts" → "In disquieting thoughts"** (v13) — Hebrew שְׂעִפִּים (also 20:2; uncertain: thoughts that divide or trouble). חֶזְיֹנוֹת "visions" (also 7:14).
- **"Fear came upon me … which made all my bones to shake" → "dread met me … and the multitude of my bones it made to dread"** (v14) — Hebrew פַּחַד "dread" (not יִרְאָה "fear", v6); קְרָאַנִי, the קָרָא of meeting (= קָרָה "befall"); רֹב "multitude", not "all"; הִפְחִיד, the verb of פַּחַד.
- **"Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up" → "And a spirit passed over my face; the hair of my flesh bristled"** (v15) — Hebrew וְרוּחַ, which may also be "a wind"; עַל־פָּנַי "over my face". The imperfects of the vision (v15, 16) are given as past.
- **"It stood still, but I could not discern the form thereof: an image was before mine eyes, there was silence, and I heard a voice, saying" → "It stood, and I did not recognize its appearance; a form was before my eyes; stillness, and a voice I heard"** (v16) — Hebrew מַרְאֵהוּ "its appearance"; תְּמוּנָה "form"; דְּמָמָה וָקוֹל "stillness and a voice" (as 1 Kgs 19:12 "a sound of thin stillness"; others "a still voice"). "Saying" is not written.
- **"Shall mortal man be more just than God? shall a man be more pure than his maker?" → "Is mortal man more righteous than Eloah? Or is a mighty man purer than his Maker?"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man", גֶּבֶר "mighty man" (two words). מִן may be "than" or "before, in the sight of" ("righteous before Eloah"); both readings are possible.
- **"he put no trust in his servants; and his angels he charged with folly" → "in his servants he does not believe, and his angels he charges with error"** (v18) — Hebrew הֵן "behold"; עֲבָדָיו, plural of עֶבֶד "slave" (of Eloah's own: "servant"); לֹא יַאֲמִין, the verb "believe"; מַלְאָךְ, the word is "messenger". יָשִׂים תָּהֳלָה, literally "in his angels he puts error": תָּהֳלָה is found only here; "error" is uncertain ("folly").
- **"How much less in them that dwell in houses of clay … which are crushed before the moth?" → "How much more those who abide in houses of clay … they crush them before the moth"** (v19) — Hebrew אַף, literally "also, even", here the step from the greater to the lesser; שֹׁכְנֵי from שָׁכַן "abide"; יְדַכְּאוּם, active, "they crush them" (no subject named). לִפְנֵי־עָשׁ "before the moth": "like a moth" or "sooner than a moth"; uncertain.
- **"They are destroyed from morning to evening: they perish for ever without any regarding it" → "From morning to evening they are beaten in pieces; without anyone heeding, forever they perish"** (v20) — Hebrew יֻכַּתּוּ, from כָּתַת "beat in pieces"; מִבְּלִי מֵשִׂים, literally "without one setting" (the heart, or the mind); לָנֶצַח "forever" (נֶצַח "perpetuity", another word than עוֹלָם).
- **"Doth not their excellency which is in them go away? they die, even without wisdom" → "Is not their cord pulled up within them? They die, and not in wisdom"** (v21) — Hebrew נִסַּע "be pulled up" (the verb of pulling up tent pegs); יִתְרָם is "their cord" (as יֶתֶר Judg 16:7) or "their remainder, their abundance" (יֶתֶר "rest"); uncertain. "Tent" is not written. וְלֹא בְחָכְמָה "and not in wisdom".

### Notes
- **Eliphaz first** — opens the cycles of speeches. Tactful opening; moves to theological challenge.
- **"Who that was innocent perished?"** (v7) — retributive orthodoxy: suffering = guilt, prosperity = righteousness. The challenge of Job is precisely this equation.
- **Lion imagery** (v10-11) — wicked are as predators now fallen.
- **Visionary experience** (v12-16) — Eliphaz reports a nocturnal revelation. *Ruach* passing; voice speaking.
- **"Is mortal man more righteous than Eloah?"** — rhetorical; points to universal human inadequacy. But Job's question is different: can a just person suffer without cause?
- **Angels charged with error** — even the heavenly servants are not trusted; certainly not humans.
- **"Houses of clay" / "foundation in dust"** — human mortality theme.
