# Job 23

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Even today my complaint is rebellion;
my hand is heavy on my sighing.

[3] Oh that I knew, and could find him,
that I could come to his set place!

[4] I would arrange judgment before him
and fill my mouth with arguments.

[5] I would know the utterances he would answer me
and understand what he would say to me.

[6] Would he contend with me in the greatness of power?
No; he would only give heed to me.

[7] There an upright one could argue with him,
and I would come away safe forever from my judge.

[8] Behold, I go forward, and he is not there,
and backward, and I do not perceive him;

[9] to the left, where he works, and I do not take hold;
he covers himself to the right, and I do not see.

[10] For he knows the way that is with me;
he has tried me: as gold I shall come out.

[11] My foot has held fast to his step;
his way I have kept and have not turned.

[12] The commandment of his lips — I have not departed from it;
more than what is allotted me I have stored up the sayings of his mouth.

[13] But he is one, and who can turn him back?
And his soul desires, and he does it.

[14] For he completes what is allotted me,
and many such things are with him.

[15] Therefore I am dismayed before his face;
I consider, and I am in dread of him.

[16] And ⟦el|El|God⟧ has made my heart faint,
and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ has dismayed me.

[17] For I have not been destroyed because of darkness,
and from my face he has covered gloom."

## Changes

- **"Then Job answered and said" → "And Job answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר.
- **"God" → "El"** (v16) — Hebrew אֵל *El*; **"Shaddai"** (v16) — שַׁדַּי, traditionally "the Almighty".
- **"my complaint is bitter: my stroke is heavier than my groaning" → "my complaint is rebellion; my hand is heavy on my sighing"** (v2) — Hebrew אַנְחָתִי "my sighing" (as 3:24); מְרִי שִׂחִי, as pointed "my complaint is rebellion" (מְרִי "rebellion", as the מֶרִי row; many read מַר "bitter", from memory); שִׂיחַ "complaint" (as 7:13; 10:1). יָדִי "my hand" (some versions "his hand", from memory); כָּבְדָה עַל "is heavy on".
- **"Oh that I knew where I might find him! that I might come even to his seat!" → "Oh that I knew, and could find him, that I could come to his set place!"** (v3) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן, literally "who will give", a wish; "where" is not written. תְּכוּנָה "set place" (from כּוּן "establish").
- **"I would order my cause before him" → "I would arrange judgment before him"** (v4) — Hebrew אֶעֶרְכָה … מִשְׁפָּט, "arrange a judgment" (a case brought to be judged: "case", as Job 13:18). תוֹכָחוֹת "arguments", the noun of הוֹכִיחַ "reprove"; its nifal is "argue" in v7.
- **"the words which he would answer me" → "the utterances he would answer me"** (v5) — Hebrew מִלִּים (beside דָּבָר "word").
- **"Will he plead against me with his great power? No; but he would put strength in me" → "Would he contend with me in the greatness of power? No; he would only give heed to me"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּרָב־כֹּחַ "in greatness of power"; לֹא אַךְ־הוּא יָשִׂם בִּי, literally "no, only he would set on me" (= set his mind on me); "strength" is not written.
- **"There the righteous might dispute with him; so should I be delivered for ever from my judge" → "There an upright one could argue with him, and I would come away safe forever from my judge"** (v7) — Hebrew יָשָׁר "upright"; נוֹכָח "argue" (nifal of the root of v4); אֲפַלְּטָה, piel of פלט "bring to safety", with no object: "I would bring (myself) to safety". מִשֹּׁפְטִי "from my judge".
- **"forward … backward … On the left hand … on the right hand" kept** (v8–9) — Hebrew קֶדֶם, אָחוֹר, שְׂמֹאל, יָמִין are also the words for east, west, north and south (WORDS-HE.md "Directions"); here the speaker's own body may be meant as well, so the plain words are kept and the compass sense is given here.
- **"but I cannot behold him" → "and I do not take hold"** (v9) — Hebrew וְלֹא־אָחַז, as pointed of אָחַז "take hold" (a common conjecture reads אֶחֱזֶה "I behold"). **"he hideth himself on the right hand, that I cannot see him" → "he covers himself to the right, and I do not see"** — יַעְטֹף, of עָטַף "cover, wrap" (also taken as "turn aside"); "him" is not written in either line.
- **"the way that I take" → "the way that is with me"** (v10) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ עִמָּדִי. **"when he hath tried me" → "he has tried me"** — בְּחָנַנִי, a perfect, with no "when"; בָּחַן "try" (נִסָּה is "test").
- **"held his steps … not declined" → "held fast to his step … have not turned"** (v11) — Hebrew בַּאֲשֻׁרוֹ "to his step"; אָט, of נָטָה "turn".
- **"Neither have I gone back from the commandment of his lips; I have esteemed the words of his mouth more than my necessary food" → "The commandment of his lips — I have not departed from it; more than what is allotted me I have stored up the sayings of his mouth"** (v12) — Hebrew מִצְוַת שְׂפָתָיו וְלֹא אָמִישׁ, "the commandment of his lips, and I do not depart": "from it" supplied. מֵחֻקִּי "more than my statute": חֹק is "statute", here what is fixed for a person, his allotted share (as Prov 30:8); some versions read "in my bosom" (from memory). צָפַן "store up" (elsewhere "hide": here what is kept as treasure, as Ps 119:11). אִמְרֵי־פִיו "the sayings of his mouth".
- **"But he is in one mind, and who can turn him? and what his soul desireth, even that he doeth" → "But he is one, and who can turn him back? And his soul desires, and he does it"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהוּא בְאֶחָד, literally "and he is in one": "one, alone, of one mind" (uncertain); יְשִׁיבֶנּוּ "turn him back"; וְנַפְשׁוֹ אִוְּתָה וַיָּעַשׂ "and his soul desired, and he did".
- **"he performeth the thing that is appointed for me" → "he completes what is allotted me"** (v14) — Hebrew יַשְׁלִים חֻקִּי, the word of v12.
- **"Therefore am I troubled at his presence" → "Therefore I am dismayed before his face"** (v15) — Hebrew מִפָּנָיו "from his face"; נִבְהַל "be dismayed" (also v16, hifil "dismay"). **"I am afraid of him" → "I am in dread of him"** — פָּחַד.
- **"maketh my heart soft" → "has made my heart faint"** (v16) — Hebrew הֵרַךְ לִבִּי, literally "made my heart soft, tender".
- **"Because I was not cut off before the darkness, neither hath he covered the darkness from my face" → "For I have not been destroyed because of darkness, and from my face he has covered gloom"** (v17) — Hebrew נִצְמַתִּי, of צָמַת "destroy, put an end to" (also taken as "be silenced"); מִפְּנֵי־חֹשֶׁךְ "because of darkness" or "from before darkness"; כִּסָּה־אֹפֶל "he has covered gloom" (or "gloom has covered", "from my face"; אֹפֶל is of one root with אֲפֵלָה "gloom"); whether the "not" of the first line rules the second is not said. The verse is obscure; its words are kept.

### Notes
- **The case at law** (v3–7) — Job wants to stand before Eloah as at a trial: "arrange judgment", "arguments", "argue", "my judge".
- **He cannot be found** (v8–9) — four directions, no sight of him.
- **Tried as gold** (v10) — Job expects to come out of the trial pure.
- **Kept his way** (v11–12) — Job holds to his blamelessness.
- **One will** (v13–14) — what is allotted to Job will be completed; Job's dread follows from it (v15–17).
