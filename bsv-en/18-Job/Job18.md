# Job 18

[1] And Bildad the ⟦shuhite|Shuchi|Shuhite⟧ answered and said:

[2] "How long will you set snares for words?
Understand, and afterward we will speak.

[3] Why are we reckoned as the beast,
stupid in your eyes?

[4] One tearing his soul in his anger —
on your account shall the earth be forsaken,
and a rock be moved from its place?

[5] Also the light of the wicked goes out,
and the flame of his fire does not shine.

[6] The light has grown dark in his tent,
and his lamp above him goes out.

[7] The steps of his strength are narrowed,
and his counsel throws him down.

[8] For he has been sent into a net by his feet,
and upon a lattice he walks about.

[9] A trap seizes him by the heel;
a noose holds fast upon him.

[10] His cord is hidden in the earth,
and his snare upon the path.

[11] All around, terrors have terrified him,
and they have scattered him at his feet.

[12] Let his strength be hungry,
and calamity is ready for his side.

[13] It devours the parts of his skin;
the firstborn of death devours his parts.

[14] His trust is drawn out from his tent,
and it makes him march to the king of terrors.

[15] There abides in his tent what is none of his;
sulfur is strewn upon his habitation.

[16] Beneath, his roots are dried out,
and above, his bough is cut off.

[17] His remembrance has perished from the earth,
and he has no name on the face of the street.

[18] They push him from light into darkness,
and from the world they chase him away.

[19] He has no offspring and no posterity among his people,
and there is no survivor in his sojournings.

[20] At his day those of the west are appalled,
and those of the east have taken hold of horror.

[21] Surely these are the dwellings of the unjust one,
and this is the place of him who did not know ⟦el|El|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said" → "And Bildad the Shuhite answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the book.
- **"God" → "El"** (v21) — Hebrew אֵל *El*. The chapter has no *Elohim*.
- **"How long will it be ere ye make an end of words? mark" → "How long will you set snares for words? Understand"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּשִׂימוּן קִנְצֵי לְמִלִּין: קִנְצֵי is found only here; "snares" (uncertain; KJV "an end", from קֵץ). The verbs are plural ("you" of more than one), as v3 "your eyes"; תָּבִינוּ "understand".
- **"Wherefore are we counted as beasts, and reputed vile in your sight?" → "Why are we reckoned as the beast, stupid in your eyes?"** (v3) — Hebrew נֶחְשַׁבְנוּ "we are reckoned" (חָשַׁב); כַבְּהֵמָה "as the beast" (singular); נִטְמִינוּ, of טָמָה "be stupid" (uncertain; from טָמֵא it is "unclean"); בְּעֵינֵיכֶם "in your eyes".
- **"He teareth himself in his anger: shall the earth be forsaken for thee? and shall the rock be removed out of his place?" → "One tearing his soul in his anger — on your account shall the earth be forsaken, and a rock be moved from its place?"** (v4) — Hebrew טֹרֵף נַפְשׁוֹ בְּאַפּוֹ, a participle in the third person ("he who tears his soul"), said of Job; טָרַף "tear to pieces"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; אַף "anger" (literally "nose"). הַלְמַעַנְךָ "on your account"; עָזַב "forsake"; צוּר "rock".
- **"Yea, the light of the wicked shall be put out, and the spark of his fire shall not shine" → "Also the light of the wicked goes out, and the flame of his fire does not shine"** (v5) — Hebrew גַּם "also"; רְשָׁעִים plural, then "his fire"; שְׁבִיב "flame" (only here).
- **"The light shall be dark in his tabernacle, and his candle shall be put out with him" → "The light has grown dark in his tent, and his lamp above him goes out"** (v6) — Hebrew חָשַׁךְ, perfect; אֹהֶל "tent"; נֵר "lamp"; עָלָיו "above him".
- **"The steps of his strength shall be straitened, and his own counsel shall cast him down" → "The steps of his strength are narrowed, and his counsel throws him down"** (v7) — Hebrew אוֹנוֹ "his strength" (אוֹן, not אָוֶן "wickedness"); יֵצְרוּ "are narrowed"; עֲצָתוֹ "his counsel" ("own" is not written).
- **"For he is cast into a net by his own feet, and he walketh upon a snare" → "For he has been sent into a net by his feet, and upon a lattice he walks about"** (v8) — Hebrew שֻׁלַּח, pual of שָׁלַח "send"; רֶשֶׁת "net"; שְׂבָכָה "lattice" (a netted covering over a pit); יִתְהַלָּךְ "walks about".
- **"The gin shall take him by the heel, and the robber shall prevail against him" → "A trap seizes him by the heel; a noose holds fast upon him"** (v9) — Hebrew פַּח "trap" (the fowler's); צַמִּים "noose" (uncertain; KJV "robber", taken from another word).
- **"The snare is laid for him in the ground, and a trap for him in the way" → "His cord is hidden in the earth, and his snare upon the path"** (v10) — Hebrew טָמוּן "hidden" (in the ground); חַבְלוֹ "his cord" (= the cord set for him); מַלְכֻּדְתּוֹ "his snare" (of לָכַד "capture"); מוֹקֵשׁ, "snare" in WORDS-HE.md, is not in this passage, and English has no third plain word beside "net", "trap"; נָתִיב "path".
- **"Terrors shall make him afraid on every side, and shall drive him to his feet" → "All around, terrors have terrified him, and they have scattered him at his feet"** (v11) — Hebrew בִּעֲתֻהוּ, הֱפִיצֻהוּ, perfects; בַּלָּהוֹת "terrors"; הֵפִיץ "scatter"; לְרַגְלָיו "at his feet" (= at every step).
- **"His strength shall be hungerbitten, and destruction shall be ready at his side" → "Let his strength be hungry, and calamity is ready for his side"** (v12) — Hebrew יְהִי, the jussive "let it be"; רָעֵב "hungry"; אֹנוֹ "his strength" (as v7); אֵיד "calamity"; נָכוֹן "ready" (as 15:23); לְצַלְעוֹ "for his side" (צֶלַע "stumbling" is also read).
- **"It shall devour the strength of his skin: even the firstborn of death shall devour his strength" → "It devours the parts of his skin; the firstborn of death devours his parts"** (v13) — Hebrew יֹאכַל twice; בַּדֵּי עוֹרוֹ "the parts of his skin" and בַּדָּיו "his parts" (בַּד "part, limb"; the "bars" of 17:16 are the same letters). The subject of the first line is not named; it may be "the firstborn of death" of the second. בְּכוֹר מָוֶת "the firstborn of death".
- **"His confidence shall be rooted out of his tabernacle, and it shall bring him to the king of terrors" → "His trust is drawn out from his tent, and it makes him march to the king of terrors"** (v14) — Hebrew יִנָּתֵק מֵאָהֳלוֹ מִבְטַחוֹ: נִתַּק "be drawn out"; מִבְטָח "trust" (what he trusts in). וְתַצְעִדֵהוּ "and it makes him march": the form is feminine third person, or second person ("you make him march"); the subject is not named. מֶלֶךְ בַּלָּהוֹת "the king of terrors".
- **"It shall dwell in his tabernacle, because it is none of his" → "There abides in his tent what is none of his"** (v15) — Hebrew תִּשְׁכּוֹן (feminine: "it abides"; or "you abide"); מִבְּלִי־לוֹ, literally "from without-to-him": "what is none of his" (uncertain). **"brimstone shall be scattered upon his habitation" → "sulfur is strewn upon his habitation"** — גָּפְרִית "sulfur"; זָרָה "strew"; נָוֶה "habitation".
- **"His roots shall be dried up beneath, and above shall his branch be cut off" → "Beneath, his roots are dried out, and above, his bough is cut off"** (v16) — Hebrew יִבָשׁוּ "are dried out"; יִמַּל, from מָלַל, "is cut off" or "withers" (uncertain; "cut off" as 14:2, the same verb); קְצִירוֹ "his bough" (קָצִיר, the word for "harvest", of a tree "bough", as 14:9).
- **"he shall have no name in the street" → "he has no name on the face of the street"** (v17) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי־חוּץ "on the face of the street" (= out of doors, abroad). זִכְרוֹ "his remembrance"; אָבַד "perished" (perfect).
- **"He shall be driven from light into darkness, and chased out of the world" → "They push him from light into darkness, and from the world they chase him away"** (v18) — Hebrew יֶהְדְּפֻהוּ … יְנִדֻּהוּ, active, plural, with no subject named; הָדַף "push"; תֵּבֵל "world".
- **"He shall neither have son nor nephew among his people, nor any remaining in his dwellings" → "He has no offspring and no posterity among his people, and there is no survivor in his sojournings"** (v19) — Hebrew נִין … נֶכֶד "offspring … posterity" (as Gen 21:23); שָׂרִיד "survivor"; מְגוּרָיו "his sojournings".
- **"They that come after him shall be astonied at his day, as they that went before were affrighted" → "At his day those of the west are appalled, and those of the east have taken hold of horror"** (v20) — Hebrew אַחֲרֹנִים "those behind" and קַדְמֹנִים "those in front": the west and the east (one faces east), or "those who come after" and "those who were before" (KJV). נָשַׁמּוּ, of שָׁמֵם "be appalled"; אָחֲזוּ שָׂעַר "have taken hold of horror" (שַׂעַר "shuddering"); "as" is not written.
- **"the wicked" → "the unjust one"** (v21) — Hebrew עַוָּל (רָשָׁע "wicked" is v5). מִשְׁכְּנוֹת "dwellings"; לֹא־יָדַע, perfect.

### Notes
- **Bildad's second speech** — it does not take up Job's words of ch 16–17; it gives again the fate of the wicked.
- **"The firstborn of death" / "the king of terrors"** (v13, 14) — death named as a figure; Canaanite texts know a god Mot ("Death").
- **The light put out** (v5–6) — as 21:17; Prov 13:9; 24:20.
- **Net, lattice, trap, noose, cord, snare** (v8–10) — six words for the hunter's gear.
- **Sulfur** (v15) — as on Sodom (Gen 19:24).
- **Remembrance and offspring gone** (v17, 19).
