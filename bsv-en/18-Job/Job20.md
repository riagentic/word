# Job 20

[1] And ⟦zophar|Tzofar|Zophar⟧ the ⟦naamathite|Naamati|Naamathite⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Therefore my disquieting thoughts make me answer,
and because of my haste within me.

[3] The discipline of my shame I hear,
and a spirit from my understanding answers me.

[4] Do you know this from of old,
since man was placed upon the earth,

[5] that the ringing cry of the wicked is short,
and the gladness of the godless for a moment?

[6] If his height goes up to the heavens,
and his head reaches to the thick cloud,

[7] like his dung he perishes forever;
those who see him will say, 'Where is he?'

[8] Like a dream he flies away, and they do not find him;
and he is chased away like a vision of the night.

[9] An eye glimpsed him and will not again,
and no more will his place watch him.

[10] His sons will seek the favor of the poor,
and his hands will give back his strength.

[11] His bones are full of his youth,
and with him it will lie down on the dust.

[12] If evil is sweet in his mouth,
he hides it under his tongue,

[13] he spares it and does not leave it,
and holds it back in the midst of his palate,

[14] his bread in his inward parts is turned;
the gall of cobras is within him.

[15] Wealth he swallowed, and he vomits it up;
from his belly ⟦el|El|God⟧ drives it out.

[16] The poison of cobras he sucks;
the tongue of the viper kills him.

[17] Let him not look on the streams,
rivers of torrents of honey and curds.

[18] He gives back toil and does not swallow it;
as the wealth of his exchange, and he does not exult.

[19] For he crushed, he forsook the poor;
a house he seized, and he will not build it.

[20] For he knew nothing untroubled in his belly;
of what he desires he will save nothing.

[21] There is no survivor of his eating;
therefore his good will not endure.

[22] In the fullness of his sufficiency he will be in distress;
every hand of the toiling one will come upon him.

[23] Let it be, to fill his belly —
he will send into him the burning of his anger,
and he will rain upon him with his food.

[24] He will flee from the weapon of iron;
a bow of bronze will pass through him.

[25] He draws, and it goes out of the back,
and lightning from his gall goes;
upon him are terrors.

[26] All darkness is hidden for his treasured things;
a fire not blown will devour him;
it will be evil for the survivor in his tent.

[27] The heavens will uncover his iniquity,
and the earth rises up against him.

[28] The produce of his house will depart,
poured away in the day of his anger.

[29] This is the share of a wicked man from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and the inheritance of his saying from ⟦el|El|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"Then answered Zophar … and said" → "And Zophar … answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of every speech heading.
- **"Therefore do my thoughts cause me to answer, and for this I make haste" → "Therefore my disquieting thoughts make me answer, and because of my haste within me"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׂעִפַּי "my disquieting thoughts" (as 4:13); יְשִׁיבוּנִי, hifil of שׁוּב, "make me return (an answer)"; וּבַעֲבוּר חוּשִׁי בִי, "and because of my hastening in me" (בַּעֲבוּר is "for the sake of" elsewhere: here of a cause). The line has no verb of its own.
- **"I have heard the check of my reproach, and the spirit of my understanding causeth me to answer" → "The discipline of my shame I hear, and a spirit from my understanding answers me"** (v3) — Hebrew מוּסַר כְּלִמָּתִי, "the discipline of my shame" (= a correction that shames me); רוּחַ מִבִּינָתִי "a spirit from my understanding" (רוּחַ also "wind": Zophar's answer may be called wind).
- **"Knowest thou not this of old" → "Do you know this from of old"** (v4) — Hebrew הֲזֹאת יָדַעְתָּ, a question with no "not"; מִנִּי־עַד "from of old" (lit. "from perpetuity"). "Thou" is singular: to Job.
- **"the triumphing of the wicked is short, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment" → "the ringing cry of the wicked is short, and the gladness of the godless for a moment"** (v5) — Hebrew רְנָנָה "ringing cry"; מִקָּרוֹב, literally "from near"; שִׂמְחָה "gladness"; חָנֵף "godless" (profane; not "hypocrite"); עֲדֵי־רָגַע "until a moment".
- **"Though his excellency mount up … unto the clouds" → "If his height goes up … to the thick cloud"** (v6) — Hebrew אִם "if"; שִׂיא, only here, "height"; עָב "thick cloud".
- **"Yet he shall perish for ever like his own dung: they which have seen him" → "like his dung he perishes forever; those who see him"** (v7) — Hebrew כְּגֶלֲלוֹ "like his dung" (first in the line); "yet" is not written; רֹאָיו is a participle, "those who see him".
- **"and shall not be found" → "and they do not find him"** (v8) — Hebrew וְלֹא יִמְצָאוּהוּ, active.
- **"The eye also which saw him shall see him no more" → "An eye glimpsed him and will not again"** (v9) — Hebrew עַיִן (no article) שְׁזָפַתּוּ, from שָׁזַף "glimpse, catch sight of"; וְלֹא תוֹסִיף "and will not add (to do so)". שׁוּר "watch".
- **"His children shall seek to please the poor, and his hands shall restore their goods" → "His sons will seek the favor of the poor, and his hands will give back his strength"** (v10) — Hebrew בָּנָיו "his sons"; יְרַצּוּ: uncertain — piel of רָצָה "seek the favor of", or of רָצַץ "crush" ("his sons will crush the poor"). אוֹנוֹ "his strength" (אוֹן "vigor", and of goods "wealth", Hos 12:8); "their goods" is not written: the suffix is "his". The same written form is read "his wickedness" in 21:19.
- **"His bones are full of the sin of his youth, which shall lie down" → "His bones are full of his youth, and with him it will lie down on the dust"** (v11) — Hebrew עֲלוּמָיו "his youth" (qere; the ketiv עלומו is a shorter spelling of the same word); "the sin of" is not in the Hebrew. וְעִמּוֹ "and with him".
- **"Though wickedness be sweet … though he hide it" → "If evil is sweet … he hides it"** (v12) — Hebrew אִם "if"; רָעָה "evil"; יַכְחִידֶנָּה "he hides it" (no second "though"). v12–14 are one sentence: "if … he hides … spares … holds back, his bread … is turned".
- **"Though he spare it, and forsake it not; but keep it still within his mouth" → "he spares it and does not leave it, and holds it back in the midst of his palate"** (v13) — Hebrew חָמַל "spare"; עָזַב "forsake", of a thing kept back "leave"; מָנַע "hold back"; חֵךְ "palate".
- **"Yet his meat in his bowels … the gall of asps" → "his bread in his inward parts … the gall of cobras"** (v14) — Hebrew לֶחֶם "bread"; מֵעִים "inward parts"; מְרוֹרָה "gall" (as v25); פְּתָנִים "cobras" (the kind of snake is not certain).
- **"He hath swallowed down riches, and he shall vomit them up again: God shall cast them out of his belly" → "Wealth he swallowed, and he vomits it up; from his belly El drives it out"** (v15) — Hebrew חַיִל "wealth" (singular); וַיְקִאֶנּוּ; יֹרִשֶׁנּוּ, hifil of יָרַשׁ (elsewhere "dispossess" of peoples): "drives it out". אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"He shall suck the poison of asps: the viper's tongue" → "The poison of cobras he sucks; the tongue of the viper"** (v16) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ "poison" (the same letters as "head"); לְשׁוֹן "tongue" (not "fangs"); אֶפְעֶה "viper".
- **"He shall not see the rivers, the floods, the brooks of honey and butter" → "Let him not look on the streams, rivers of torrents of honey and curds"** (v17) — Hebrew אַל־יֵרֶא, a negative wish; רָאָה בְּ "look on"; פְּלַגּוֹת "streams" (channels); נַהֲרֵי נַחֲלֵי "rivers of torrents" (נַחַל "wadi", here the water itself); חֶמְאָה "curds".
- **"That which he laboured for shall he restore, and shall not swallow it down: according to his substance shall the restitution be, and he shall not rejoice therein" → "He gives back toil and does not swallow it; as the wealth of his exchange, and he does not exult"** (v18) — Hebrew מֵשִׁיב יָגָע, "giving back toil" (יָגָע: what toil has gained); כְּחֵיל תְּמוּרָתוֹ "as the wealth of his exchange" (תְּמוּרָה: what is given in exchange; uncertain); עָלַס "exult". Two Hebrew lines, two printed lines (the verse had three).
- **"because he hath violently taken away an house which he builded not" → "a house he seized, and he will not build it"** (v19) — Hebrew רִצַּץ עָזַב, two verbs side by side ("crushed, forsook"); גָּזַל "seize"; וְלֹא יִבֶנֵהוּ "and he will not build it" (= build it up; "which he did not build" is a reading).
- **"Surely he shall not feel quietness in his belly, he shall not save of that which he desired" → "For he knew nothing untroubled in his belly; of what he desires he will save nothing"** (v20) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; לֹא־יָדַע שָׁלֵו, literally "he did not know untroubled" (שָׁלֵו "untroubled", as 21:23); בַּחֲמוּדוֹ לֹא יְמַלֵּט, literally "in his desired thing he will not save": no object is written (or "with his treasure he will not escape").
- **"There shall none of his meat be left; therefore shall no man look for his goods" → "There is no survivor of his eating; therefore his good will not endure"** (v21) — Hebrew אֵין־שָׂרִיד לְאָכְלוֹ, "there is no survivor to his eating" (nothing escaped his appetite); לֹא־יָחִיל "will not endure" (from חִיל "be firm"); טוּבוֹ "his good".
- **"he shall be in straits: every hand of the wicked" → "he will be in distress; every hand of the toiling one"** (v22) — Hebrew יֵצֶר לוֹ, "it will be narrow for him"; שֵׂפֶק "sufficiency" (only here); עָמֵל "toiling one" (adjective of עָמָל "toil": the wretched, those he crushed), not "wicked".
- **"When he is about to fill his belly, God shall cast the fury of his wrath upon him, and shall rain it upon him while he is eating" → "Let it be, to fill his belly — he will send into him the burning of his anger, and he will rain upon him with his food"** (v23) — Hebrew יְהִי "let it be" (jussive); לְמַלֵּא בִטְנוֹ "to fill his belly" (whose filling is not said). The Masoretic Text names no subject for "he will send": "God" is not in the Hebrew (KJV and ESV supply it; the context points to El of v15, 29). חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ "the burning of his anger"; וְיַמְטֵר עָלֵימוֹ "and he will rain upon him", no object. בִּלְחוּמוֹ: uncertain — "with his food" (from לֶחֶם), "into his flesh" (Zeph 1:17 לְחוּמָם "their flesh"), or "with his weapons" (from לָחַם "fight").
- **"the bow of steel shall strike him through" → "a bow of bronze will pass through him"** (v24) — Hebrew קֶשֶׁת נְחוּשָׁה "a bow of bronze" (its arrow is meant; "arrow" is not written); חָלַף "pass through". נֶשֶׁק "weapon".
- **"It is drawn, and cometh out of the body; yea, the glittering sword cometh out of his gall" → "He draws, and it goes out of the back, and lightning from his gall goes"** (v25) — Hebrew שָׁלַף "he draws" (active; who draws is not said); גֵּוָה "back, body"; בָּרָק "lightning" (the flashing point; no article); מְרֹרָה "gall" (as v14); יַהֲלֹךְ "it goes" (הָלַךְ, not the יָצָא "go out" of the first line), in the Hebrew order. אֵמִים "terrors".
- **"All darkness shall be hid in his secret places … it shall go ill with him that is left in his tabernacle" → "All darkness is hidden for his treasured things … it will be evil for the survivor in his tent"** (v26) — Hebrew טָמוּן לִצְפּוּנָיו "hidden for his treasured (lit. hidden) things"; לֹא־נֻפָּח "not blown"; יֵרַע "it will be evil" (or "it will graze on", from רָעָה: uncertain); שָׂרִיד "survivor" (as v21).
- **"The heaven shall reveal … shall rise up" → "The heavens will uncover … rises up"** (v27) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם (plural); גִּלָּה "uncover"; מִתְקוֹמָמָה is a participle.
- **"The increase of his house shall depart, and his goods shall flow away in the day of his wrath" → "The produce of his house will depart, poured away in the day of his anger"** (v28) — Hebrew יְבוּל "produce"; יִגֶל from גָּלָה (of glory "go into exile", 1 Sam 4:21): here "depart"; נִגָּרוֹת, a participle, "poured away" ("his goods" is not written); אַפּוֹ "his anger" (whose is not said).
- **"the portion of a wicked man from God, and the heritage appointed unto him by God" → "the share of a wicked man from Elohim, and the inheritance of his saying from El"** (v29) — Hebrew חֵלֶק "share"; אֱלֹהִים *Elohim* and אֵל *El*: two names; נַחֲלַת אִמְרוֹ "the inheritance of his saying" (= the inheritance decreed for him; whose saying is not said).

### Notes
- **Zophar's second speech** — the fate of the wicked, told to Job.
- **Dream imagery** (v8) — the wicked man as fleeting as a dream.
- **Food turned to poison** (v12–16) — the evil he savours becomes gall within him.
- **Rivers of honey and curds denied** (v17) — the blessings of a good land withheld.
- **Indirect charge** — Zophar casts Job as the wicked man.
