# Job 6

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Oh that my vexation were surely weighed,
and my calamity they would lift in the balances together!

[3] For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas;
therefore my words have been rash.

[4] For the arrows of ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ are with me,
whose poison my spirit drinks;
the terrors of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ array themselves against me.

[5] Does the wild donkey bray over vegetation?
Or does the ox low over its fodder?

[6] Is what is tasteless eaten without salt?
Or is there taste in the slime of the mallow?

[7] My soul refuses to touch;
they are like the sickness of my bread.

[8] Oh that my request would come,
and my hope ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ would give,

[9] and that ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ would be willing and crush me,
would let loose his hand and cut me off!

[10] And it would still be my comfort,
and I would leap in anguish — he does not spare —
for I have not hidden the sayings of the Holy One.

[11] What is my power, that I should hope?
And what is my end, that I should be patient?

[12] Is my power the power of stones?
Or is my flesh bronze?

[13] Is it that my help is not in me,
and success is driven away from me?

[14] To the one who melts away, from his friend, ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧;
and the fear of ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ he forsakes.

[15] My brothers have dealt treacherously like a wadi,
like the channel of wadis that pass away,

[16] which are dark from the ice;
upon them the snow hides itself.

[17] In the time they are scorched, they are ended;
in its heat they are put out from their place.

[18] The caravans of their way turn aside;
they go up into the waste and perish.

[19] The caravans of Tema looked;
the companies of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ waited for them.

[20] They were ashamed, for one had trusted;
they came up to it and were abashed.

[21] For now you have become it;
you see a dread, and you fear.

[22] Is it because I said, 'Give to me',
and 'From your power bribe for me',

[23] and 'Rescue me from the hand of the adversary',
and 'From the hand of the ruthless ransom me'?

[24] Teach me, and I, I will be silent;
and how I have erred, make me understand.

[25] How forceful are sayings of uprightness!
But what does reproving from you reprove?

[26] Do you mean to reprove utterances,
and as wind the sayings of one who despairs?

[27] Even over the fatherless you would cast lots,
and you would bargain over your friend.

[28] And now, be willing: turn to me;
and to your face I will not lie.

[29] Return, please; let there be no injustice;
and return: still my righteousness is in it.

[30] Is there injustice on my tongue?
Or can my palate not discern calamities?"

## Changes

- **"But Job answered and said" → "And Job answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the heading formula of the book.
- **"Oh that my grief were throughly weighed, and my calamity laid in the balances together!" → "Oh that my vexation were surely weighed, and my calamity they would lift in the balances together!"** (v2) — Hebrew לוּ שָׁקוֹל יִשָּׁקֵל, literally "weighing it were weighed"; כַּעְשִׂי "my vexation", the word of Eliphaz in 5:2. "My calamity" is the qere הַוָּתִי (ketiv והיתי, without a clear sense); יִשְׂאוּ "they would lift" (no subject named).
- **"For now it would be heavier than the sand of the sea: therefore my words are swallowed up" → "For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas; therefore my words have been rash"** (v3) — Hebrew יַמִּים "seas"; לָעוּ, taken from לוּעַ "speak rashly" (Prov 20:25); KJV derived it from לָעַע "swallow"; uncertain.
- **"For the arrows of the Almighty are within me, the poison whereof drinketh up my spirit: the terrors of God do set themselves in array against me" → "For the arrows of Shaddai are with me, whose poison my spirit drinks; the terrors of Eloah array themselves against me"** (v4) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי *Shaddai*, אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*; עִמָּדִי "with me"; חֲמָתָם "their poison" (חֵמָה is "heat, fury": here of venom, as Deut 32:24, 33); בִּעוּתֵי "the terrors of"; יַעַרְכוּנִי, from עָרַךְ "arrange (for battle)".
- **"Doth the wild ass bray when he hath grass? or loweth the ox over his fodder?" → "Does the wild donkey bray over vegetation? Or does the ox low over its fodder?"** (v5) — Hebrew עֲלֵי־דֶשֶׁא "over vegetation"; גָּעָה "low"; בְּלִיל "fodder" (mixed feed).
- **"Can that which is unsavoury be eaten without salt? or is there any taste in the white of an egg?" → "Is what is tasteless eaten without salt? Or is there taste in the slime of the mallow?"** (v6) — Hebrew רִיר, elsewhere "spittle" (1 Sam 21:13); חַלָּמוּת is found only here: "mallow" (a plant with slimy juice) or the white or yolk of an egg; uncertain.
- **"The things that my soul refused to touch are as my sorrowful meat" → "My soul refuses to touch; they are like the sickness of my bread"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵאֲנָה לִנְגּוֹעַ נַפְשִׁי, with no object; הֵמָּה כִּדְוֵי לַחְמִי, word for word; דְּוַי "sickness" (as Ps 41:3); the sense ("loathsome food") is uncertain. מֵאֵן "refuse"; לֶחֶם "bread".
- **"Oh that I might have my request; and that God would grant me the thing that I long for!" → "Oh that my request would come, and my hope Eloah would give"** (v8) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן, literally "who will give", the form of a wish; שְׁאֵלָה "request" (from שָׁאַל "ask"); תִּקְוָתִי "my hope".
- **"Even that it would please God to destroy me; that he would let loose his hand, and cut me off!" → "and that Eloah would be willing and crush me, would let loose his hand and cut me off"** (v9) — Hebrew וְיֹאֵל, הוֹאִיל "be willing"; דִּכָּא "crush"; יַתֵּר יָדוֹ "let loose his hand"; בִּצַּע "cut off" (as a weaver cuts the thread, Isa 38:12).
- **"Then should I yet have comfort; yea, I would harden myself in sorrow: let him not spare; for I have not concealed the words of the Holy One" → "And it would still be my comfort, and I would leap in anguish — he does not spare — for I have not hidden the sayings of the Holy One"** (v10) — Hebrew אֲסַלְּדָה, found only here: "leap" (for joy or in pain); uncertain. חִילָה "anguish" (writhing); לֹא יַחְמוֹל "he does not spare" (masculine: of Eloah, not of the anguish), a plain statement, not "let him not". כִּחֵד "hide" (the verb of 4:7, nifal "be effaced"); אִמְרֵי "sayings of".
- **"What is my strength, that I should hope? and what is mine end, that I should prolong my life?" → "What is my power, that I should hope? And what is my end, that I should be patient?"** (v11) — Hebrew כֹּחַ "power" (so v12); אֲיַחֵל "hope"; אַאֲרִיךְ נַפְשִׁי, literally "make my soul long", that is, be patient.
- **"or is my flesh of brass?" → "Or is my flesh bronze?"** (v12) — Hebrew נָחוּשׁ.
- **"Is not my help in me? and is wisdom driven quite from me?" → "Is it that my help is not in me, and success is driven away from me?"** (v13) — Hebrew הַאִם אֵין עֶזְרָתִי בִי, literally "is it that there is not my help in me?"; תֻשִׁיָּה "success" (as 5:12; uncertain); נִדְּחָה "is driven away".
- **"To him that is afflicted pity should be shewed from his friend; but he forsaketh the fear of the Almighty" → "To the one who melts away, from his friend, chesed; and the fear of Shaddai he forsakes"** (v14) — Hebrew לַמָּס מֵרֵעֵהוּ חָסֶד: no verb; לַמָּס, from מָסַס "melt" (one who despairs). Who "forsakes" is not said: the despairing man (if chesed fails him), or the friend who withholds it. "Should be shown" is not written.
- **"My brethren have dealt deceitfully as a brook, and as the stream of brooks they pass away" → "My brothers have dealt treacherously like a wadi, like the channel of wadis that pass away"** (v15) — Hebrew בָּגַד "deal treacherously"; נַחַל "wadi" (a stream bed that runs dry); אָפִיק "channel".
- **"Which are blackish by reason of the ice, and wherein the snow is hid" → "which are dark from the ice; upon them the snow hides itself"** (v16) — Hebrew הַקֹּדְרִים (קָדַר "be dark", as 5:11); עָלֵימוֹ "upon them"; יִתְעַלֶּם, hitpael, "hides itself".
- **"What time they wax warm, they vanish: when it is hot, they are consumed out of their place" → "In the time they are scorched, they are ended; in its heat they are put out from their place"** (v17) — Hebrew יְזֹרְבוּ, found only here (uncertain: "be scorched", "flow away"); נִצְמָתוּ "they are ended" (silenced, destroyed); בְּחֻמּוֹ "in its heat"; נִדְעֲכוּ, the verb of a lamp going out.
- **"The paths of their way are turned aside; they go to nothing, and perish" → "The caravans of their way turn aside; they go up into the waste and perish"** (v18) — Hebrew אָרְחוֹת, the same word and pointing as v19: "caravans", or "paths" (of the streams' course); one rendering for both verses, the other reading here. תֹּהוּ "waste" (as Gen 1:2); יַעֲלוּ "go up".
- **"The troops of Tema looked, the companies of Sheba waited for them" → "The caravans of Tema looked; the companies of Sheba waited for them"** (v19) — Hebrew הִבִּיטוּ "looked"; הֲלִיכֹת "goings, companies"; קִוּוּ "waited for".
- **"They were confounded because they had hoped; they came thither, and were ashamed" → "They were ashamed, for one had trusted; they came up to it and were abashed"** (v20) — Hebrew בֹּשׁוּ "they were ashamed"; בָטָח, singular, "he (each one) had trusted"; עָדֶיהָ "up to it"; וַיֶּחְפָּרוּ from חָפֵר "be abashed" (another root than חָפַר "dig").
- **"For now ye are nothing; ye see my casting down, and are afraid" → "For now you have become it; you see a dread, and you fear"** (v21) — the verse follows the qere לוֹ: הֱיִיתֶם לוֹ "you have become it" (הָיָה לְ "become": like the wadi); the ketiv לא reads "you have become nothing" (so KJV). חֲתַת "a dread" (only here; root of חַת "dread"); תִּרְאוּ … וַתִּירָאוּ "see … fear", a play on sound. "My casting down" is not written.
- **"Did I say, Bring unto me? or, Give a reward for me of your substance?" → "Is it because I said, 'Give to me', and 'From your power bribe for me'"** (v22) — Hebrew הֲכִי "is it because"; הָבוּ "give"; מִכֹּחֲכֶם "from your power" (here of means, wealth); שִׁחֲדוּ "bribe". Each line has "and", not "or" (v22–23).
- **"Or, Deliver me from the enemy's hand? or, Redeem me from the hand of the mighty?" → "and 'Rescue me from the hand of the adversary', and 'From the hand of the ruthless ransom me'"** (v23) — Hebrew מַלְּטוּנִי "rescue me"; צָר "adversary"; עָרִיצִים "ruthless ones" (root עָרַץ "terrify"); פָּדָה "ransom".
- **"Teach me, and I will hold my tongue: and cause me to understand wherein I have erred" → "Teach me, and I, I will be silent; and how I have erred, make me understand"** (v24) — Hebrew הוֹרוּנִי; וַאֲנִי אַחֲרִישׁ, the "I" written; שָׁגִיתִי "I have erred".
- **"How forcible are right words! but what doth your arguing reprove?" → "How forceful are sayings of uprightness! But what does reproving from you reprove?"** (v25) — Hebrew נִמְרְצוּ (also "be painful, grievous", 1 Kgs 2:8): "how painful" is the other reading; אִמְרֵי־יֹשֶׁר; יוֹכִיחַ הוֹכֵחַ מִכֶּם, literally "what does a reproving from you reprove".
- **"Do ye imagine to reprove words, and the speeches of one that is desperate, which are as wind?" → "Do you mean to reprove utterances, and as wind the sayings of one who despairs?"** (v26) — Hebrew תַּחְשֹׁבוּ "mean" (חָשַׁב); מִלִּים "utterances"; לְרוּחַ "as / for wind"; נוֹאָשׁ "one who despairs".
- **"Yea, ye overwhelm the fatherless, and ye dig a pit for your friend" → "Even over the fatherless you would cast lots, and you would bargain over your friend"** (v27) — Hebrew תַּפִּילוּ "you make fall"; "lots" is supplied (as הִפִּיל גּוֹרָל, 1 Sam 14:42); תִכְרוּ עַל from כָּרָה "bargain" (as 41:6 [Heb 40:30]); KJV took it as כָּרָה "dig" (a pit, not written).
- **"Now therefore be content, look upon me; for it is evident unto you if I lie" → "And now, be willing: turn to me; and to your face I will not lie"** (v28) — Hebrew הוֹאִילוּ "be willing"; פְנוּ־בִי "turn to me"; וְעַל־פְּנֵיכֶם אִם־אֲכַזֵּב, the oath form "and to your face, if I lie": it means "I will not lie".
- **"Return, I pray you, let it not be iniquity; yea, return again, my righteousness is in it" → "Return, please; let there be no injustice; and return: still my righteousness is in it"** (v29) — Hebrew שֻׁבוּ־נָא; עַוְלָה "injustice" (so v30); "and return" is the qere וְשׁוּבוּ; the ketiv ושבי may be read "and my return"; עוֹד "still".
- **"Is there iniquity in my tongue? cannot my taste discern perverse things?" → "Is there injustice on my tongue? Or can my palate not discern calamities?"** (v30) — Hebrew חִכִּי "my palate"; הַוּוֹת "calamities", the word of v2.

### Notes
- **Job's first response** — directed at Eliphaz's comfort. He takes up Eliphaz's word "vexation" (5:2) in v2.
- **Heavier than sand** — classic hyperbole for grief.
- **Arrows of *Shaddai*** (v4) — divine-assault imagery.
- **Wanting death** — wishes Eloah would crush him and cut him off (v8–9).
- **"Brothers treacherous as a wadi"** — seasonal streambeds dry when travelers most need them. Caravans of Tema (Arabia) and Sheba reach the wadi in vain. Image for friends' failure.
- **Accuses friends of casting lots over the fatherless and bargaining over a friend** (v27) — harsh rhetoric, reflects pain.
- **"To your face I will not lie"** — refuses their implied diagnosis that he has sinned.
