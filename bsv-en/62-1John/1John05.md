# 1 John 5

[1] Everyone who believes that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ has been begotten of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and everyone who loves the one who begot loves also the one who has been begotten of him.

[2] By this we know that we love the children of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, when we love ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and do his commandments.

[3] For this is the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that we keep his commandments; and his commandments are not heavy,

[4] because everything that has been begotten of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ overcomes the world. And this is the victory that overcame the world: our faith.

[5] And who is the one who overcomes the world, if not the one who believes that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[6] This is the one who came through water and blood, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧; not in the water only, but in the water and in the blood. And the Spirit is that which testifies, because the Spirit is the truth.

[7] For there are three that testify,

[8] the Spirit and the water and the blood, and the three are unto the one.

[9] If we receive the testimony of men, the testimony of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is greater, because this is the testimony of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that he has testified concerning his Son.

[10] The one who believes in the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has the testimony in himself; the one who does not believe ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has made him a liar, because he has not believed in the testimony which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has testified concerning his Son.

[11] And this is the testimony, that **⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.**

**[12] The one who has the Son has the life; the one who does not have the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does not have the life**.

[13] These things I wrote to you, that you may know that you have eternal life, you who believe in the name of the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[14] And this is the boldness which we have toward him, that if we ask anything according to his will, he hears us.

[15] And if we know that he hears us, whatever we ask, we know that we have the requests which we have asked from him.

[16] If anyone sees his brother sinning a sin not unto death, he shall ask, and he will give him life, to those who sin not unto death. There is a sin unto death; I do not say that he should request concerning that.

[17] All unrighteousness is sin, and there is a sin not unto death.

[18] We know that everyone who has been begotten of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does not sin, but the one who was begotten of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ keeps him, and the evil one does not touch him.

[19] We know that we are of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the whole world lies in the evil one.

[20] And we know that the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has come, and he has given us understanding, that we may know the true one; and we are in the true one, in his Son ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧. This is the true ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and eternal life.

[21] Little children, guard yourselves from the idols.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v1, 5, 6, 20) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v6, 20) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v1) — systematic.
- **"Holy Ghost" (KJV v7, *Comma Johanneum*) — omitted** — the *Comma Johanneum* ("in heaven: the Father, the Word, and the *Ruach HaQodesh*; and these three are one") is not present in this translation, following the earliest and most reliable Greek manuscripts. See Notes.
- **"is born of God: and every one that loveth him that begat loveth him also that is begotten of him" → "has been begotten of *Elohim*, and everyone who loves the one who begot loves also the one who has been begotten of him"** (v1, 4, 18) — Greek γεγέννηται … τὸν γεννήσαντα … τὸν γεγεννημένον: one verb, γεννάω, three times (see 2:29). The Greek does not say "the Father" here. "Also" is καί (SBLGNT, Treg, NA28, RP); WH omits it.
- **"keep his commandments" → "do his commandments"** (v2) — NA28 ποιῶμεν, "do"; "keep" (τηρῶμεν) follows in v3.
- **"the love of God" kept** (v3) — Greek ἡ ἀγάπη τοῦ θεοῦ, as in 2:5: his love or love for him.
- **"his commandments are not grievous" → "his commandments are not heavy"** (v3) — Greek βαρεῖαι, "heavy, weighty". The sentence runs on into v4 ("because").
- **"whatsoever is born of God" → "everything that has been begotten of *Elohim*"** (v4) — Greek πᾶν τὸ γεγεννημένον, neuter.
- **"the victory that overcometh the world" → "the victory that overcame the world"** (v4) — Greek ἡ νίκη ἡ νικήσασα, an aorist participle; "victory" (νίκη) and "overcome" (νικάω) are one root in Greek.
- **"Who is he that overcometh" → "And who is the one who overcomes"** (v5) — Τίς δέ ἐστιν (SBLGNT, Treg, NA28; WH ἐστιν δέ); RP has no δέ.
- **"came by water and blood … not by water only, but by water and blood" → "came through water and blood … not in the water only, but in the water and in the blood"** (v6) — Greek δι' ὕδατος καὶ αἵματος, then ἐν τῷ ὕδατι … ἐν τῷ αἵματι: the preposition changes from διά to ἐν and the nouns take the article.
- **"it is the Spirit that beareth witness, because the Spirit is truth" → "the Spirit is that which testifies, because the Spirit is the truth"** (v6) — Greek τὸ πνεῦμά ἐστιν τὸ μαρτυροῦν … ἡ ἀλήθεια, with the article; "that which" follows the neuter participle, and the gender is grammatical. μαρτυρέω, μαρτυρία are "testify", "testimony" throughout (v6–11; 1:2; 4:14).
- **"these three agree in one" → "the three are unto the one"** (v8) — Greek οἱ τρεῖς εἰς τὸ ἕν εἰσιν, literally "the three are into the one": they point to one and the same thing. "Those who testify" (v7) is masculine in Greek, though Spirit, water and blood are neuter nouns.
- **"which he hath testified of his Son" → "that he has testified concerning his Son"** (v9) — NA28 ὅτι, "that" or "because"; KJV follows ἥν, "which".
- **"hath the witness in himself" → "has the testimony in himself"** (v10) — the editions differ only in the breathing mark: ἐν αὐτῷ (Treg, NA28, RP), ἐν αὑτῷ (SBLGNT, WH); NA27 ἐν ἑαυτῷ. The old manuscripts have no breathing marks, so the letters are the same. The verse reads it reflexively, "in himself"; "in him" (the Son) is the other possible sense.
- **"he believeth not the record that God gave of his Son" → "he has not believed in the testimony which *Elohim* has testified concerning his Son"** (v10) — Greek οὐ πεπίστευκεν εἰς τὴν μαρτυρίαν ἣν μεμαρτύρηκεν: perfects, and the same noun and verb as in v9. "Believe *Elohim*" (τῷ θεῷ, dative) and "believe in" (εἰς) are kept apart.
- **"hath life" → "has the life"** (v12) — Greek τὴν ζωήν, with the article both times: the life just named in v11.
- **"These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know … and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God" → "These things I wrote to you, that you may know that you have eternal life, you who believe in the name of the Son of *Elohim*"** (v13) — NA28 has "you who believe" once, at the end; ἔγραψα, an aorist.
- **"the confidence that we have in him" → "the boldness which we have toward him"** (v14) — Greek παρρησία … πρὸς αὐτόν, as in 3:21.
- **"the petitions that we desired of him" → "the requests which we have asked from him"** (v15) — Greek τὰ αἰτήματα ἃ ᾐτήκαμεν ἀπ' αὐτοῦ, noun and verb of one root.
- **"he shall ask, and he shall give him life for them that sin not unto death" → "he shall ask, and he will give him life, to those who sin not unto death"** (v16) — Greek αἰτήσει, καὶ δώσει αὐτῷ ζωήν, τοῖς ἁμαρτάνουσιν μὴ πρὸς θάνατον. The subject of "will give" is not named: it can be *Elohim*, or the one who asks; the name is not in the Greek. "Him" is singular, "those who sin" plural.
- **"a sin unto death" kept** (v16, 17) — Greek ἁμαρτία πρὸς θάνατον, "sin toward death"; Greek has no indefinite article ("a sin" or "sin").
- **"I do not say that he shall pray for it" → "I do not say that he should request concerning that"** (v16) — Greek ἐρωτήσῃ, a different verb from "ask" (αἰτέω) in v14–16.
- **"All unrighteousness is sin" kept** (v17) — Greek ἀδικία, as in 1:9; καί is "and".
- **"sinneth not; but he that is begotten of God keepeth himself, and that wicked one toucheth him not" → "does not sin, but the one who was begotten of *Elohim* keeps him, and the evil one does not touch him"** (v18) — Greek οὐχ ἁμαρτάνει, a plain present (see 3:6); ὁ γεννηθείς, an aorist participle beside the perfect γεγεννημένος; τηρεῖ "keeps", the verb used of the commandments. The text has αὐτόν, "him" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA27): "the one who was begotten of *Elohim*" is then most naturally the Son, who keeps the believer. NA28 and RP: τηρεῖ ἑαυτόν, "keeps himself" — then it is the believer.
- **"the whole world lieth in wickedness" → "the whole world lies in the evil one"** (v19) — Greek ἐν τῷ πονηρῷ κεῖται; the form can be masculine ("the evil one", as in v18) or neuter ("evil"); there is no word for "power".
- **"hath given us an understanding, that we may know him that is true" → "he has given us understanding, that we may know the true one"** (v20) — Greek διάνοιαν, the faculty of thinking; τὸν ἀληθινόν … ἐν τῷ ἀληθινῷ, "the true one", the word of 2:8.
- **"This is the true God, and eternal life" kept** (v20) — Greek οὗτός ἐστιν ὁ ἀληθινὸς θεὸς καὶ ζωὴ αἰώνιος. "This" can refer to the nearest name, *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, or to "the true one" (the Father) of the clause before.
- **"keep yourselves from idols" → "guard yourselves from the idols"** (v21) — Greek φυλάξατε, "guard", a different verb from "keep" (τηρέω); τῶν εἰδώλων has the article. The KJV's "Amen" is not in the critical text.

### Notes
- **v7-8** — the *Comma Johanneum* ("in heaven: the Father, the Word, and the *Ruach HaQodesh*; and these three are one. And there are three that bear witness on earth:") is absent from all Greek MSS before the 14th century and is widely regarded as a Latin interpolation. The earliest Greek and the most reliable MSS read only "the Spirit and the water and the blood."
