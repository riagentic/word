## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"; "Lord" → "Adonai"; "God" → "El"** (v18, 22, 24, 25, 26, 40, 50, 55, 59, 61, 64, 66 · v31, 36, 37, 58 · v41) — Hebrew יהוה, אֲדֹנָי and אֵל, each as written; עֶלְיוֹן "Most High" (v35, 38) keeps its token. No vocative "O" (v55, 58, 59, 61, 64).
- **"I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath" → "I am the man who has seen affliction by the rod of his raging"** (v1) — Hebrew גֶּבֶר, the man in his strength (the standing decision "mighty man"; where it stands alone, "man", as in Job; v27, 35; beside אָדָם in v39 "mighty man"); עֳנִי "affliction"; שֵׁבֶט "rod"; עֶבְרָה "raging" (its row, as 2:2). The pronoun "his" has no name before it in the chapter: Yahweh is first named in v18.
- **"He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, but not into light" → "Me he has driven and made walk in darkness and not light"** (v2) — Hebrew אוֹתִי first ("me"); נָהַג "drive"; וַיֹּלַךְ "and made walk" (הוֹלִיךְ, its row); חֹשֶׁךְ וְלֹא־אוֹר "darkness and not light" ("into" is not written).
- **"Surely against me is he turned; he turneth his hand against me all the day" → "Surely against me he again turns his hand all the day"** (v3) — Hebrew יָשֻׁב יַהֲפֹךְ, "he turns back, he turns over": שׁוּב with a second verb = "do again" (its row); the hand is named once.
- **"My flesh and my skin hath he made old; he hath broken my bones" → "He has worn out my flesh and my skin; he has broken my bones"** (v4) — Hebrew בִּלָּה "wear out" (as Ps 32:3 בָּלוּ); שִׁבַּר "break" (piel).
- **"He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail" → "He has built against me and encompassed me with poison and hardship"** (v5) — Hebrew בָּנָה עָלַי "built against me" (siege works); הִקִּיף "encompass"; רֹאשׁ "poison" (a bitter plant, also v19; "gall" in other versions); תְּלָאָה "hardship" (its row).
- **"He hath set me in dark places, as they that be dead of old" → "In dark places he has made me dwell like the everlasting dead"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּמַחֲשַׁכִּים הוֹשִׁיבַנִי כְּמֵתֵי עוֹלָם, the words of Ps 143:3, rendered as there ("dark places", "he has made me dwell", lit. "sit"); כְּמֵתֵי עוֹלָם, literally "like the dead of the age": "the dead of long ago" or "the dead for ever" (עוֹלָם looks out of sight both ways; "everlasting" is the row for עוֹלָם as a genitive). The Hebrew order is kept ("In dark places" first).
- **"He hath hedged me about, that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy" → "He has walled me in, and I cannot go out; he has made my bronze heavy"** (v7) — Hebrew גָּדַר בַּעֲדִי "walled around me" (also v9); וְלֹא אֵצֵא "and I shall not go out"; הִכְבִּיד "made heavy" (כָּבֵד); נְחָשְׁתִּי "my bronze" (= my bronze fetters, as נְחֻשְׁתַּיִם 2 Kgs 25:7).
- **"Also when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer" → "Even when I cry out and cry for help, he has shut out my prayer"** (v8) — Hebrew זָעַק "cry out"; שִׁוַּע "cry for help" (as שַׁוְעָה v56); שָׂתַם "shut out" (stop up), a perfect.
- **"He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked" → "He has walled in my ways with hewn stone; my tracks he has made crooked"** (v9) — Hebrew גָּדַר (v7); גָּזִית "hewn stone"; נְתִיבוֹת "tracks" (its row); עִוָּה "make crooked"; the Hebrew order of the second half kept.
- **"He was unto me as a bear lying in wait, and as a lion in secret places" → "A bear lying in ambush he is to me, a lion in hiding places"** (v10) — Hebrew order; no "as" (a metaphor); ketiv אריה, qere אֲרִי, both "lion"; אֹרֵב "lying in ambush" (its row); מִסְתָּרִים "hiding places".
- **"He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces: he hath made me desolate" → "My ways he has turned aside, and he has torn me in pieces; he has made me desolate"** (v11) — Hebrew סוֹרֵר, polel of סוּר "turn aside"; וַיְפַשְּׁחֵנִי, only here, "tore me in pieces" (uncertain); שָׂמַנִי שֹׁמֵם "set me desolate".
- **"He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow" → "He has trodden his bow and set me up like the mark for the arrow"** (v12) — Hebrew דָּרַךְ קֶשֶׁת "tread the bow" (as 2:4); הִצִּיב "set up" (its row); מַטָּרָא "mark"; כַּ "like the".
- **"He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins" → "He has made enter into my kidneys the sons of his quiver"** (v13) — Hebrew הֵבִיא "made come in"; כְּלָיוֹת "kidneys" (the inner man: its row); בְּנֵי אַשְׁפָּתוֹ "the sons of his quiver" (= arrows: kept); the Hebrew order kept.
- **"I was a derision to all my people; and their song all the day" → "I have become a laughingstock to all my people, their song all the day"** (v14) — Hebrew הָיִיתִי "I have become"; שְׂחֹק "laughingstock" (lit. "laughter"); לְכָל־עַמִּי "to all my people" (some manuscripts "all peoples", from memory: the MT is kept); נְגִינָה "song" (sung to strings: its row; a taunt is meant).
- **"He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath made me drunken with wormwood" → "He has sated me with bitter things; he has made me drink my fill of wormwood"** (v15) — Hebrew הִשְׂבִּיעַ "sate" (שָׂבַע); מְרוֹרִים "bitter things" (bitter herbs, as Exod 12:8); הִרְוָה "make drink one's fill" (not "make drunk"); לַעֲנָה "wormwood".
- **"He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes" → "And he has made my teeth grind on gravel; he has made me cower in ashes"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיַּגְרֵס "and made grind" (גָּרַס "crush, grind": uncertain), with "and"; חָצָץ "gravel"; הִכְפִּישׁ, only here, "made cower" (press down: uncertain); אֵפֶר "ashes".
- **"And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I forgat prosperity" → "And you have cast off my soul from shalom; I have forgotten good"** (v17) — Hebrew וַתִּזְנַח: "you cast off" (second person) or "it (my soul) was cast off / rejected" (third feminine; the same form): uncertain, the second person kept as in the earlier text; זָנַח "cast off" (its row; v31); שָׁלוֹם token; טוֹבָה "good" (not "prosperity").
- **"And I said, My strength and my hope is perished from the LORD" → "And I said, My eminence has perished, and my hope from Yahweh"** (v18) — Hebrew נִצְחִי "my eminence" (נֵצַח, its row: "eminence" or "endurance", uncertain; "strength" in other versions); תּוֹחֶלֶת "hope" (root יחל, as v21, 24); the verb אָבַד "perish" stands once; וָאֹמַר "And I said".
- **"Remembering mine affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the gall" → "Remember my affliction and my wandering, the wormwood and the poison"** (v19) — Hebrew זְכָר, imperative "remember" (or the noun "remembering of": uncertain); מְרוּדִי "my wandering" (as 1:7); לַעֲנָה וָרֹאשׁ (v15, 5).
- **"My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is humbled in me" → "My soul surely remembers, and it bows down within me"** (v20) — Hebrew זָכוֹר תִּזְכּוֹר, infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "remembering it remembers" (the form may also be "you surely remember": uncertain; the soul is the subject of the next verb); ketiv ותשיח "and it muses", qere וְתָשׁוֹחַ "and it bows down" (the qere in the verse); עָלַי "within me" (lit. "upon me"). An old scribal note (tiqqun sopherim, from memory) says the line once read "and your soul will bow down upon me".
- **"This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope" → "This I bring back to my heart; therefore I will hope"** (v21) — Hebrew אָשִׁיב אֶל־לִבִּי "I bring back to my heart"; אוֹחִיל, an imperfect, "I will hope" (hifil of יחל; קִוָּה "wait for" is another word, v25).
- **"It is of the LORD'S mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not" → "The chasadim of Yahweh: for we are not consumed, for his compassions are not spent"** (v22) — Hebrew חַסְדֵי יְהוָה, plural of *chesed* (token as Ps 89:1); כִּי … כִּי "for … for"; MT תָמְנוּ, first person plural of תָּמַם "come to an end": "we are not consumed" (literally "we have not come to an end"); the Targum and the Syriac read תַּמּוּ "they have not ceased" (from memory), so "the chasadim of Yahweh have not ceased": the MT is kept; כָּלוּ "are spent" (כָּלָה, as 2:11); רַחֲמִים "compassions" (plural kept).
- **"They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness" → "New for the mornings; great is your faithfulness"** (v23) — Hebrew חֲדָשִׁים לַבְּקָרִים, no verb and no subject ("they are" is not written; "every" is not written: לַבְּקָרִים "for the mornings", = morning by morning); אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness"; the first address to "you" in the chapter.
- **"The LORD is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him" → "My share is Yahweh, says my soul; therefore I will hope in him"** (v24) — Hebrew חֶלְקִי יְהוָה, in this order; חֵלֶק "share" (its row); אָמְרָה "says" (a perfect: "has said"); אוֹחִיל לוֹ (v21).
- **"The LORD is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him" → "Good is Yahweh to those who wait for him, to the soul that seeks him"** (v25) — Hebrew טוֹב first (v25–27 open with טוֹב); לְקֹוָו "those who wait for him" (קִוָּה, its row; the written form may be singular); דָּרַשׁ "seek".
- **"It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the LORD" → "Good it is that he hope, and in silence, for the salvation of Yahweh"** (v26) — Hebrew טוֹב וְיָחִיל וְדוּמָם, literally "good, and he hopes, and silently": "a man" is not written (the subject is unnamed: "he"); יָחִיל "hope" (of יחל, v21); דּוּמָם "in silence" (an adverb, root דָּמַם); תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation".
- **"It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth" → "Good it is for a man that he bear a yoke in his youth"** (v27) — Hebrew גֶּבֶר "man" (v1); עֹל "a yoke" (no article); נְעוּרִים "youth".
- **"He sitteth alone and keepeth silence, because he hath borne it upon him" → "Let him sit alone and be silent, for he has laid it on him"** (v28) — Hebrew יֵשֵׁב … וְיִדֹּם, jussive in sense with v29–30 ("let him"); בָּדָד "alone" (as 1:1); נָטַל עָלָיו "he has laid it on him" (נָטַל "lift, lay"; the subject is Yahweh or the man himself: left open).
- **"He putteth his mouth in the dust; if so be there may be hope" → "Let him put his mouth in the dust; perhaps there is hope"** (v29) — Hebrew יִתֵּן "let him give / put"; אוּלַי "perhaps" (its row); תִּקְוָה "hope".
- **"He giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him: he is filled full with reproach" → "Let him give his cheek to the one who strikes him; let him be sated with reproach"** (v30) — Hebrew לְמַכֵּהוּ "to the one who strikes him" (הִכָּה); לֶחִי "cheek"; יִשְׂבַּע "be sated"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach".
- **"For the Lord will not cast off for ever" → "For Adonai will not cast off forever"** (v31) — Hebrew יִזְנַח "cast off" (v17); לְעוֹלָם "forever"; אֲדֹנָי at the end of the line in the Hebrew.
- **"But though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according to the multitude of his mercies" → "For though he has grieved, he will have compassion according to the abundance of his chasadim"** (v32) — Hebrew כִּי אִם "for if / though"; הוֹגָה "he has grieved" (יָגָה hifil, as 1:5, 12; v33); רִחַם "have compassion"; ketiv חסדו "his chesed", qere חֲסָדָיו "his chasadim" (the qere in the verse; token plural as v22); רֹב "abundance".
- **"For he doth not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men" → "For he has not afflicted from his heart, nor grieved the sons of man"** (v33) — Hebrew עִנָּה "afflict"; מִלִּבּוֹ "from his heart" ("willingly" is the sense); וַיַּגֶּה "and grieved" (v32); בְּנֵי־אִישׁ "sons of man" (reviewer row; אִישׁ "a man").
- **"To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the earth" → "To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the earth"** (v34) — Hebrew לְדַכֵּא "to crush" (as Ps 143:3); v34–36 are three infinitives hanging on the question at the end of v36.
- **"To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the most High" → "to turn aside the judgment of a man before the face of the Most High"** (v35) — Hebrew לְהַטּוֹת "to turn aside" (הִטָּה); מִשְׁפַּט־גָּבֶר "the judgment of a man" (מִשְׁפָּט, its row; גֶּבֶר v1); נֶגֶד פְּנֵי "before the face of"; עֶלְיוֹן token.
- **"To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord approveth not" → "to make a man crooked in his case — does Adonai not see?"** (v36) — Hebrew לְעַוֵּת "to make crooked" (עִוֵּת, as עַוָּתָתִי v59); אָדָם "man"; רִיב "case" (its row in law); אֲדֹנָי לֹא רָאָה "Adonai has not seen": a question ("does he not see?") or a statement ("Adonai does not approve", lit. "does not look on"): uncertain; the question is kept with the dash, as the infinitives of v34–36 ask for it.
- **"Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the Lord commandeth it not?" → "Who is this who has said, and it came to be, when Adonai has not commanded?"** (v37) — Hebrew מִי זֶה "who is this"; אָמַר וַתֶּהִי "said, and it came to be" (as Ps 33:9); לֹא צִוָּה "has not commanded" ("it" is not written).
- **"Out of the mouth of the most High proceedeth not evil and good?" → "From the mouth of the Most High do they not go out, the evils and the good?"** (v38) — Hebrew לֹא תֵצֵא "does not go out" (a question by the sense); הָרָעוֹת plural "the evils" (רָעָה, its row), וְהַטּוֹב singular "and the good"; the Hebrew order kept.
- **"Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins?" → "Why should a living man complain, a mighty man, over his sins?"** (v39) — Hebrew אָדָם חָי "a living man" (אָדָם) and גֶּבֶר "mighty man", kept apart as the standing decision has it; הִתְאוֹנֵן "complain" (only here and Num 11:1); ketiv חטאו "his sin", qere חֲטָאָיו "his sins" (the qere in the verse); "the punishment of" is not written (חֵטְא can mean the sin and what it brings: in Changes only).
- **"Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the LORD" → "Let us search our ways and search them out, and let us return to Yahweh"** (v40) — Hebrew נַחְפְּשָׂה "let us search" (חָפַשׂ) and וְנַחְקֹרָה "let us search out" (חָקַר, reviewer row: Lam 3:40); "them" supplied; וְנָשׁוּבָה עַד "let us return as far as" (שׁוּב "return", not "turn again").
- **"Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the heavens" → "Let us lift up our heart with our palms to El in the heavens"** (v41) — Hebrew אֶל־כַּפָּיִם, literally "to the palms" (heart together with palms: "with" is the sense; some correct to עַל "upon", from memory); כַּפַּיִם "palms"; אֵל token; שָׁמַיִם "the heavens".
- **"We have transgressed and have rebelled: thou hast not pardoned" → "We, we have transgressed and rebelled; you, you have not pardoned"** (v42) — Hebrew נַחְנוּ and אַתָּה are written beside the verbs: the pronouns set "we" against "you"; פָּשַׁע "transgress"; מָרָה "rebel" (as 1:18, 20); סָלַח "pardon" (its row, said only of God).
- **"Thou hast covered with anger, and persecuted us: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied" → "You have covered yourself with anger and pursued us; you have killed, you have not spared"** (v43) — Hebrew סַכֹּתָה "you have covered (yourself)" (סָכַךְ, also v44; "yourself" from לָךְ in v44); בָאַף "with anger"; רָדַף "pursue"; הָרַג "kill"; חָמַל "spare" (as 2:21).
- **"Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, that our prayer should not pass through" → "You have covered yourself with a cloud, so that prayer cannot pass over"** (v44) — Hebrew לָךְ "for yourself"; עָנָן "cloud"; מֵעֲבוֹר "from passing over" (עָבַר, its row); תְּפִלָּה "prayer" (no "our").
- **"Thou hast made us as the offscouring and refuse in the midst of the people" → "Offscouring and refuse you make us in the midst of the peoples"** (v45) — Hebrew order; סְחִי, only here, "offscouring"; מָאוֹס "refuse" (what is rejected, מָאַס); תְּשִׂימֵנוּ, an imperfect, "you make us"; עַמִּים "peoples" (plural).
- **"All our enemies have opened their mouths against us" → "They have opened their mouth against us, all our enemies"** (v46) — Hebrew order, as 2:16; פֶּה singular. The letter פ comes before ע here (v46–51), as in ch. 2 and 4.
- **"Fear and a snare is come upon us, desolation and destruction" → "Dread and pitfall have come upon us, devastation and breaking"** (v47) — Hebrew פַּחַד וָפַחַת "dread and pitfall" (its row, Isa 24:17; the play of sound in Changes only); הָיָה לָנוּ "has been to us"; הַשֵּׁאת, only here, "devastation" (uncertain); שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as 2:11, 13).
- **"Mine eye runneth down with rivers of water for the destruction of the daughter of my people" → "With streams of water my eye runs down over the breaking of the daughter of my people"** (v48) — Hebrew פַּלְגֵי־מַיִם "streams of water" (reviewer row: Lam 3:48), first in the line; עַל־שֶׁבֶר "over the breaking" (as 2:11).
- **"Mine eye trickleth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission" → "My eye pours out and is not still, without respite"** (v49) — Hebrew נִגְּרָה "pours out"; תִדְמֶה "be still" (דָּמָה); מֵאֵין הֲפֻגוֹת "from there being no respites" (as פוּגַת 2:18).
- **"Till the LORD look down, and behold from heaven" → "until Yahweh looks down and sees from the heavens"** (v50) — Hebrew הִשְׁקִיף "look down"; רָאָה "see"; שָׁמַיִם "the heavens".
- **"Mine eye affecteth mine heart because of all the daughters of my city" → "My eye deals with my soul, because of all the daughters of my city"** (v51) — Hebrew עוֹלְלָה לְנַפְשִׁי "deals with my soul" (עָלַל, as 1:12, 22; 2:20); נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (not "heart"); מִכֹּל בְּנוֹת עִירִי "because of all the daughters of my city" (the women of the city, or its towns: uncertain).
- **"Mine enemies chased me sore, like a bird, without cause" → "They have surely hunted me like the bird, my enemies, without cause"** (v52) — Hebrew צוֹד צָדוּנִי, infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "hunting they have hunted me"; כַּצִּפּוֹר "like the bird"; חִנָּם "without cause" (its row).
- **"They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and cast a stone upon me" → "They have silenced my life in the pit, and they have thrown a stone on me"** (v53) — Hebrew צָמְתוּ "silenced" (put an end to, as Ps 143:12 הִצְמִית); בּוֹר "pit" (its row; also v55); וַיַּדּוּ "and they threw" (יָדָה); אֶבֶן singular.
- **"Waters flowed over mine head; then I said, I am cut off" → "Waters flowed over my head; I said, I am cut off"** (v54) — Hebrew צָפוּ "flowed over"; אָמַרְתִּי "I said" ("then" is not written); נִגְזַרְתִּי "I am cut off" (גָּזַר, as Isa 53:8).
- **"I called upon thy name, O LORD, out of the low dungeon" → "I have called your name, Yahweh, from the pit of the lowest places"** (v55) — Hebrew קָרָאתִי שִׁמְךָ "I have called your name" ("upon" is not written); בּוֹר תַּחְתִּיּוֹת "the pit of the lowest places".
- **"Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry" → "My voice you have heard; do not hide your ear at my relief, at my cry for help"** (v56) — Hebrew קוֹלִי שָׁמָעְתָּ, in this order; the main pause after it (the second line is longer); תַּעְלֵם "hide"; לְרַוְחָתִי "at my relief" (רְוָחָה, the breathing space asked for: uncertain); שַׁוְעָה "cry for help" (v8).
- **"Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee: thou saidst, Fear not" → "You came near in the day I called you; you said, Do not fear"** (v57) — Hebrew קָרַבְתָּ "you came near"; אֶקְרָאֶךָּ "I called you"; אַל־תִּירָא "do not fear".
- **"O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life" → "You have contended, Adonai, the cases of my soul; you have redeemed my life"** (v58) — Hebrew רַבְתָּ … רִיבֵי, one root: "contended … the cases" (רִיב, its row); גָּאַל "redeem"; חַיִּים "life".
- **"O LORD, thou hast seen my wrong: judge thou my cause" → "You have seen, Yahweh, the wrong done to me; judge my judgment"** (v59) — Hebrew רָאִיתָה "you have seen"; עַוָּתָתִי, only here, "the wrong done to me" (root of עִוֵּת v36); שָׁפְטָה מִשְׁפָּטִי "judge my judgment" (one root kept).
- **"Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their imaginations against me" → "You have seen all their vengeance, all their thoughts against me"** (v60) — Hebrew נְקָמָה "vengeance"; מַחְשְׁבוֹת "thoughts" (חָשַׁב, as 2:8); no "and"; לִי "against me" (lit. "to me").
- **"Thou hast heard their reproach, O LORD, and all their imaginations against me" → "You have heard their reproach, Yahweh, all their thoughts against me"** (v61) — Hebrew חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; no "and"; עָלָי "against me" (lit. "upon me").
- **"The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day" → "the lips of those who rise against me, and their murmuring against me all the day"** (v62) — Hebrew קָמַי "those who rise against me"; הֶגְיוֹן "murmuring" (root הָגָה: what is uttered low; "device" is the sense); the verse has no verb and hangs on "you have heard" in v61.
- **"Behold their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their musick" → "Their sitting down and their rising up — look: I am their song"** (v63) — Hebrew order (the objects first, then הַבִּיטָה "look"); מַנְגִּינָתָם, only here, "their song" (one root with נְגִינָה v14: a taunt song).
- **"Render unto them a recompence, O LORD, according to the work of their hands" → "You will return to them a recompense, Yahweh, according to the work of their hands"** (v64) — Hebrew תָּשִׁיב, an imperfect, "you will return" (v64–66 are imperfects; many render them as wishes: the form is kept, the wish in this note); גְּמוּל "recompense" (what is dealt back); מַעֲשֶׂה "work".
- **"Give them sorrow of heart, thy curse unto them" → "You will give them a covering of heart; your curse be on them"** (v65) — Hebrew תִּתֵּן "you will give"; מְגִנַּת־לֵב, only here, "a covering of heart" (from גנן "cover, shield": a heart shut tight; "sorrow", "anguish" in other versions: uncertain); תַּאֲלָתְךָ, only here, "your curse" (from אָלָה "curse"); לָהֶם "to them" (no verb: "be" supplied).
- **"Persecute and destroy them in anger from under the heavens of the LORD" → "You will pursue them in anger and destroy them from under the heavens of Yahweh"** (v66) — Hebrew תִּרְדֹּף … וְתַשְׁמִידֵם, imperfects (v64); בְּאַף "in anger"; הִשְׁמִיד "destroy"; שְׁמֵי יְהוָה "the heavens of Yahweh" (the name in the third person after "you": kept). The chapter ends with פ (an open paragraph).

### Notes
- **Triple acrostic** — 66 verses; each letter of the alphabet opens three verses in a row (v1–3 א, v4–6 ב … v64–66 ת), with פ before ע (v46–48 פ, v49–51 ע), as in ch. 2 and 4. The letters are printed in bold; the translation does not imitate the scheme.
- **Verse form** — each verse is one Hebrew line of two halves; the printed lines follow the halves.
- **v22–23** — "new for the mornings, great is your faithfulness": the turn to hope in the middle of the book.
- The numbering is the same in the Hebrew.
