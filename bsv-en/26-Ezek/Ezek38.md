# Ezekiel 38

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[2] "Son of man, put your face toward Gog, of the land of the Magog, chieftain, head of Meshech and ⟦tubal|Tuval|Tubal⟧, and prophesy against him,

[3] and say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am against you, Gog, chieftain, head of Meshech and ⟦tubal|Tuval|Tubal⟧.

[4] And I will turn you around, and put hooks in your jaws, and I will bring you out, and all your force, horses and horsemen, all of them clothed in full array, a great assembly, with large shield and shield, all of them handling swords;

[5] ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧, ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ and Put with them, all of them with shield and helmet;

[6] Gomer and all its troops; ⟦beth-togarmah|Beit Togarmah|Beth-togarmah⟧, the remote parts of the north, and all its troops — many peoples with you.

[7] Be ready, and make ready for yourself, you and all your assembly that are assembled to you, and you shall be a guard for them.

[8] After many days you shall be visited; in the latter years you shall come to a land brought back from the sword, gathered out of many peoples, on the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which had been a ruin continually; and it was brought out from the peoples, and they dwell in security, all of them.

[9] And you shall go up; like devastation you shall come; like the cloud to cover the land you shall be, you and all your troops, and many peoples with you.

[10] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: And it shall be in that day that things shall come up upon your heart, and you shall devise an evil design;

[11] and you shall say, I will go up against a land of open villages; I will come upon the quiet ones, who dwell in security, all of them dwelling without a wall, and bar and doors they have none;

[12] to take spoil and to plunder plunder, to turn your hand against ruins that are inhabited, and against a people gathered out of the nations, who has made livestock and property, who dwell on the navel of the earth.

[13] ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ and Dedan and the traders of Tarshish and all its young lions shall say to you, Is it to take spoil that you are coming? Is it to plunder plunder that you have assembled your assembly, to carry off silver and gold, to take livestock and property, to take great spoil?

[14] Therefore prophesy, son of man, and say to Gog, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: In that day, when my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ dwell in security, will you not know?

[15] And you shall come from your place, from the remote parts of the north, you and many peoples with you, riders on horses all of them, a great assembly and a numerous force.

[16] And you shall come up against my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ like the cloud to cover the land; in the latter days it shall be; and I will bring you against my land, in order that the nations may know me, when I am sanctified through you before their eyes, Gog.

[17] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Are you he of whom I spoke in former days by the hand of my servants the prophets of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who prophesied in those days, years, that I would bring you against them?

[18] And it shall be in that day, in the day when Gog comes against the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, my fury shall come up in my anger.

[19] And in my jealousy, in the fire of my wrath, I have spoken: surely in that day there shall be a great quaking on the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[20] and there shall quake before my face the fish of the sea and the birds of the heavens and the living things of the field and every creeping thing that creeps on the ground, and all mankind that is on the face of the ground; and the mountains shall be thrown down, and the steep places shall fall, and every wall shall fall to the earth.

[21] And I will call against him for a sword on all my mountains, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧; each man's sword shall be against his brother.

[22] And I will enter into judgment with him with pestilence and with blood; and flooding rain and stones of hail, fire and sulfur, I will rain upon him and upon his troops and upon the many peoples who are with him.

[23] And I will show myself great and show myself holy, and make myself known before the eyes of many nations; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both words; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. The quotation marks that opened new paragraphs inside the one speech (v7, 10, 14, 17) are dropped: the speech runs from v2 to v23.
- **"And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying"** (v1) — the prophets' formula, one wording; "saying" (לֵאמֹר) kept.
- **"set thy face against Gog" → "put your face toward Gog"** (v2) — שִׂים פָּנֶיךָ אֶל (the שִׂים פָּנִים row: "put"; as 6:2). **"the land of Magog" → "of the land of the Magog"** — אֶרֶץ הַמָּגוֹג stands beside "Gog" without a preposition ("Gog, the land of the Magog"); "of" is supplied, because "Gog, the land" would make Gog a land; הַ "the" before Magog is written (Magog is a son of Japheth in Gen 10:2, there without the article). **"the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal" → "chieftain, head of Meshech and Tubal"** (v2, 3) — נְשִׂיא רֹאשׁ מֶשֶׁךְ וְתֻבָל. The accents decide: נְשִׂיא carries a dividing accent (zaqef), so רֹאשׁ is not joined to it as "prince of Rosh"; רֹאשׁ "head" goes with "Meshech and Tubal" ("head of Meshech and Tubal"). The other reading, "prince of Rosh, Meshech and Tubal", takes רֹאשׁ as a third people (so the Septuagint, Ῥως, from memory); no people Rosh is named elsewhere in the Hebrew Bible. נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (its row; KJV "prince"; as 37:25); KJV "chief prince" joins the two words into one title.
- **"prophesy against him"** (v2) — עָלָיו "against / concerning him".
- **"O Gog" → "Gog"** (v3, 16) — vocative, no "O". **"Behold, I am against thee"** — הִנְנִי אֵלֶיךָ, literally "behold me toward you".
- **"I will turn thee back" → "I will turn you around"** (v4) — שׁוֹבַבְתִּיךָ, polel of שׁוּב. **"and put hooks into thy jaws" → "and put hooks in your jaws"** — חַחִים "hooks" (as 19:4; 29:4; the clasp of Exod 35:22 is the same word); לְחָיַיִם "jaws". **"all thine army" → "all your force"** — חַיִל "force" (the חַיִל row, of an army). **"all of them clothed with all sorts of armour" → "all of them clothed in full array"** — לְבֻשֵׁי מִכְלוֹל, literally "clothed in completeness" (as 23:12: fully and finely dressed; "armour" is a guess). **"a great company" → "a great assembly"** — קָהָל "assembly" (its row; also v7, 13, 15). **"with bucklers and shields" → "with large shield and shield"** — צִנָּה "shield" (the large one, its row) beside מָגֵן "shield"; English has one word: "large" says which. **"handling swords"** — תֹּפְשֵׂי, "those who handle" (תָּפַשׂ "seize, handle").
- **"Persia, Ethiopia, and Libya with them" → "Persia, Cush and Put with them"** (v5) — כּוּשׁ and פּוּט are names (as Gen 10:6), not "Ethiopia", "Libya". **"all of them with shield and helmet"** — כֻּלָּם מָגֵן וְכוֹבָע, literally "all of them shield and helmet": "with" supplied; כּוֹבַע "helmet" (its row).
- **"Gomer, and all his bands" → "Gomer and all its troops"** (v6) — אֲגַפִּים "troops" (as 12:14; 17:21: literally "wings" of an army; also v9, 22); the first "its" is feminine (of Gomer), the second masculine (of Beth-togarmah). **"of the north quarters" → "the remote parts of the north"** — יַרְכְּתֵי צָפוֹן (the "remote parts" row; also v15), without "from": the Hebrew sets it beside the name. **"and many people with thee" → "many peoples with you"** — עַמִּים plural (also v8, 9, 15, 22).
- **"Be thou prepared, and prepare for thyself" → "Be ready, and make ready for yourself"** (v7) — הִכֹּן וְהָכֵן לְךָ, nifal and hifil of one root. **"thy company that are assembled unto thee" → "your assembly that are assembled to you"** — קָהָל … נִקְהָלִים, one root. **"and be thou a guard unto them" → "and you shall be a guard for them"** — וְהָיִיתָ, a weqatal; לְמִשְׁמָר "for a guard" (the מִשְׁמָר "custody" row: "a keeping"; here the one who keeps watch over them, or their rallying point: uncertain).
- **"After many days thou shalt be visited" → "After many days you shall be visited"** (v8) — תִּפָּקֵד, nifal of פָּקַד (the פָּקַד "visit" row): "be visited" (attended to, for punishment), or "be mustered" (the פָּקַד "number" row); both senses fit. **"in the latter years" → "in the latter years"** — בְּאַחֲרִית הַשָּׁנִים, as the בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים row. **"into the land that is brought back from the sword, and is gathered out of many people" → "to a land brought back from the sword, gathered out of many peoples"** — מְשׁוֹבֶבֶת (polal of שׁוּב) and מְקֻבֶּצֶת, participles; חֶרֶב "sword" (its row; not "war"). **"which have been always waste" → "which had been a ruin continually"** — חָרְבָּה "ruin" (its row); תָּמִיד "continually" (its row). **"but it is brought forth out of the nations" → "and it was brought out from the peoples"** — עַמִּים "peoples". **"and they shall dwell safely all of them" → "and they dwell in security, all of them"** — לָבֶטַח "in security" (the בֶּטַח row; also v11, 14).
- **"Thou shalt ascend and come like a storm, thou shalt be like a cloud to cover the land" → "And you shall go up; like devastation you shall come; like the cloud to cover the land you shall be"** (v9) — the Hebrew order; שׁוֹאָה "devastation" (its row; KJV "storm"); כֶּעָנָן "like the cloud" (also v16).
- **"It shall also come to pass, that at the same time shall things come into thy mind" → "And it shall be in that day that things shall come up upon your heart"** (v10) — וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא (the formula row); יַעֲלוּ "come up"; דְּבָרִים "things" (the דָּבָר row: no speech is meant); עַל־לְבָבֶךָ "upon your heart". **"and thou shalt think an evil thought" → "and you shall devise an evil design"** — חָשַׁבְתָּ מַחֲשֶׁבֶת (the חָשַׁב מַחֲשָׁבֹת row: literally "think a thought").
- **"to the land of unwalled villages" → "against a land of open villages"** (v11) — עַל; פְּרָזוֹת "open country, open villages" (without walls; as Zech 2:4). **"I will go to them that are at rest" → "I will come upon the quiet ones"** — אָבוֹא הַשֹּׁקְטִים. **"and having neither bars nor gates" → "and bar and doors they have none"** — בְּרִיחַ "bar" (its row), דְּלָתַיִם "doors" (the two leaves of a gate).
- **"To take a spoil, and to take a prey" → "to take spoil and to plunder plunder"** (v12) — לִשְׁלֹל שָׁלָל, literally "to spoil spoil" (שָׁלָל "spoil", its row), and לָבֹז בַּז, "to plunder plunder" (the בָּזַז row): verb and noun of one root each time (also v13). **"to turn thine hand upon the desolate places that are now inhabited" → "to turn your hand against ruins that are inhabited"** — לְהָשִׁיב יָדְךָ עַל; חֳרָבוֹת "ruins"; נוֹשָׁבֹת "inhabited" ("now" is not written). **"which have gotten cattle and goods" → "who has made livestock and property"** — עֹשֶׂה, a singular participle of עָשָׂה "make" (as Gen 12:5; 31:1: "make" wealth = get it; "gotten" in KJV); מִקְנֶה "livestock", קִנְיָן "property" (their rows; also v13). **"that dwell in the midst of the land" → "who dwell on the navel of the earth"** — עַל־טַבּוּר הָאָרֶץ: טַבּוּר "navel" (its row; uncertain, as Judg 9:37: the high middle point); אֶרֶץ "earth" or "land".
- **"the merchants of Tarshish, with all the young lions thereof" → "the traders of Tarshish and all its young lions"** (v13) — סֹחֲרִים "traders" (the סֹחֵר row); כְּפִרֶיהָ "its young lions" (the MT pointing; a figure for its strong men, as 19:2–6; 32:2); "its villages" (כְּפָרִים) and "its merchants" are other readings (from memory). **"Art thou come to take a spoil?" → "Is it to take spoil that you are coming?"** — הֲלִשְׁלֹל שָׁלָל אַתָּה בָא, the question set on the purpose. **"hast thou gathered thy company" → "you have assembled your assembly"** — הִקְהַלְתָּ קְהָלֶךָ. **"to carry away" → "to carry off"** — לָשֵׂאת (נָשָׂא "lift, carry").
- **"In that day when my people of Israel dwelleth safely, shalt thou not know it?" → "In that day, when my people Israel dwell in security, will you not know?"** (v14) — הֲלוֹא … תֵּדָע, no object ("it" was supplied); the Septuagint has "will you not rouse yourself" (reading תֵּעוֹר, from memory): the MT is kept.
- **"out of the north parts" → "from the remote parts of the north"** (v15). **"all of them riding upon horses" → "riders on horses all of them"** — רֹכְבֵי סוּסִים (the רֹכֵב row: "rider"). **"a great company, and a mighty army" → "a great assembly and a numerous force"** — קָהָל גָּדוֹל וְחַיִל רָב; רַב "many, numerous" (as v4 קָהָל רָב "great": English needs two words for the one רַב, said here).
- **"it shall be in the latter days" → "in the latter days it shall be"** (v16) — בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים תִּהְיֶה (the row: "in the latter days"); תִּהְיֶה may be "it shall be" or "you shall be" (as v9). **"that the heathen may know me" → "in order that the nations may know me"** — לְמַעַן + infinitive. **"when I shall be sanctified in thee, O Gog, before their eyes" → "when I am sanctified through you before their eyes, Gog"** — בְּהִקָּדְשִׁי בְךָ (the נִקְדַּשׁ nifal row: "be sanctified", or "show myself holy"); the vocative stands last, as the Hebrew; no "O".
- **"Art thou he of whom I have spoken in old time by my servants the prophets of Israel" → "Are you he of whom I spoke in former days by the hand of my servants the prophets of Israel"** (v17) — בְּיָמִים קַדְמוֹנִים "in former days"; בְּיַד "by the hand of" (kept). **"which prophesied in those days many years" → "who prophesied in those days, years"** — שָׁנִים "years", without "many": it stands alone after "in those days" (= for years). **"that I would bring thee against them"** — לְהָבִיא אֹתְךָ עֲלֵיהֶם.
- **"And it shall come to pass at the same time when Gog shall come against the land of Israel" → "And it shall be in that day, in the day when Gog comes against the ground of Israel"** (v18) — the formula row; בְּיוֹם בּוֹא "in the day of the coming of"; אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל "the ground of Israel" (Ezekiel's own phrase; as 37:12; also v19). **"that my fury shall come up in my face" → "my fury shall come up in my anger"** — חֵמָה "fury" (its row); בְּאַפִּי, אַף "anger" (the Standing decision; literally "in my nose", the seat of anger).
- **"For in my jealousy and in the fire of my wrath have I spoken" → "And in my jealousy, in the fire of my wrath, I have spoken"** (v19) — וּ "and"; קִנְאָה "jealousy" (its row); בְּאֵשׁ־עֶבְרָתִי "in the fire of my wrath" without "and". **"Surely in that day there shall be a great shaking in the land of Israel" → "surely in that day there shall be a great quaking on the ground of Israel"** — אִם־לֹא, the oath form, literally "if not …" (the Standing decision: by meaning, "surely"); רַעַשׁ "quaking" (the Ezekiel row; of the ground, an earthquake: v20 says what it does).
- **"So that the fishes … shall shake at my presence" → "and there shall quake before my face the fish …"** (v20) — וְרָעֲשׁוּ, the verb of רַעַשׁ, first as the Hebrew; מִפָּנַי, literally "from before my face" (the פָּנִים of Yahweh row: "face", never "presence"). **"the fowls of the heaven" → "the birds of the heavens"** — עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם (its row). **"the beasts of the field" → "the living things of the field"** — חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה (the חַיָּה row). **"all the men" → "all mankind"** — כֹּל הָאָדָם. **"the steep places shall fall" → "the steep places shall fall"** — מַדְרֵגוֹת (only here and Song 2:14: stepped cliffs). **"to the ground" → "to the earth"** — לָאָרֶץ.
- **"And I will call for a sword against him throughout all my mountains" → "And I will call against him for a sword on all my mountains"** (v21) — וְקָרָאתִי עָלָיו לְכָל־הָרַי חֶרֶב: "him" (the earlier wording "against Gog" supplied the name); לְ "to, for, on". **"every man's sword shall be against his brother" → "each man's sword shall be against his brother"** — חֶרֶב אִישׁ בְּאָחִיו (the אִישׁ … אָחִיו row).
- **"And I will plead against him with pestilence and with blood" → "And I will enter into judgment with him with pestilence and with blood"** (v22) — וְנִשְׁפַּטְתִּי אִתּוֹ (the נִשְׁפַּט עִם row; as 17:20; 20:35–36); דֶּבֶר "pestilence", דָּם "blood" (its row). **"an overflowing rain, and great hailstones" → "flooding rain and stones of hail"** — גֶּשֶׁם שׁוֹטֵף (שָׁטַף "overflow, rinse"; as 13:11, 13); אַבְנֵי אֶלְגָּבִישׁ "stones of hail" (the Ezek 13–16 row; "great" is not written). **"fire, and brimstone" → "fire and sulfur"** — גָּפְרִית (its row). **"I will rain upon him"** — אַמְטִיר (the הִמְטִיר row); the objects stand first, as the Hebrew.
- **"Thus will I magnify myself, and sanctify myself" → "And I will show myself great and show myself holy"** (v23) — הִתְגַּדִּלְתִּי וְהִתְקַדִּשְׁתִּי, hitpael (the הִתְקַדֵּשׁ row: "sanctify oneself"; here of Yahweh, "show myself holy"); "Thus" was not written. **"and I will be known in the eyes of many nations" → "and make myself known before the eyes of many nations"** — וְנוֹדַעְתִּי, nifal ("be known, make myself known"); לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of" (the row). **"and they shall know that I am the LORD"** — the formula, one wording.

### Notes
- **Gog / Magog** — Gog is named only here and in ch. 39 (and 1 Chr 5:4, another man); Magog, Meshech, Tubal, Gomer and Togarmah are sons and grandsons of Japheth in Gen 10:2–3; Meshech and Tubal stand together also in 27:13; 32:26. Taken up in Rev 20:8 ("Gog and Magog").
- **v17** — no earlier prophecy names Gog; the question points to the prophets' words of an enemy from the north (as Jer 1:14–15; 4–6).
