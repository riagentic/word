# Ezekiel 11

[1] And a spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which faces eastward. And behold, at the entrance of the gate were twenty-five men; and I saw in their midst ⟦jaazaniah|Yaazanyah|Jaazaniah⟧ son of Azzur and ⟦pelatiah|Pelatyah|Pelatiah⟧ son of ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧, princes of the people.

[2] And he said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise wickedness and who counsel evil counsel in this city,

[3] who say, 'Not near is the building of houses; it is the pot, and we are the flesh.'

[4] Therefore prophesy against them; prophesy, son of man."

[5] And the Spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ fell upon me, and he said to me, "Say, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: So you have said, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and the things that come up in your spirit, I know them.

[6] You have multiplied your slain in this city, and you have filled its streets with the slain.

[7] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Your slain whom you have put in its midst, they are the flesh, and it is the pot; but you, I will bring out of its midst.

[8] The sword you have feared, and the sword I will bring upon you, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[9] And I will bring you out of its midst, and give you into the hand of strangers, and execute judgments among you.

[10] By the sword you shall fall; on the border of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ I will judge you; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] It shall not be a pot for you, nor shall you be flesh in its midst; at the border of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ I will judge you.

[12] And you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in whose statutes you have not walked, and my ordinances you have not done, but according to the ordinances of the nations that are around you you have done."

[13] And it came to pass, as I prophesied, that ⟦pelatiah|Pelatyah|Pelatiah⟧ son of ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ died. And I fell on my face and cried out with a loud voice and said, "Ah, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! A full end you are making of the remnant of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!"

[14] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[15] "Son of man, your brothers, your brothers, the men of your redemption, and all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, all of it, are those to whom the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ have said, 'Go far from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; to us it has been given, the land, for a possession.'

[16] Therefore say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Though I have removed them far among the nations, and though I have scattered them in the lands, yet I have been to them a sanctuary in small measure in the lands where they have come.

[17] Therefore say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: And I will gather you together from the peoples, and gather you from the lands where you have been scattered, and I will give you the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[18] And they shall come there, and they shall remove from it all its detestable things and all its abominations.

[19] And I will give them one heart, and a new spirit I will put within you; and I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh, and I will give them a heart of flesh,

[20] so that they may walk in my statutes, and keep my ordinances and do them; and they shall be a people to me, and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them.

[21] But to the heart of their detestable things and their abominations their heart walks: their way upon their head I have laid, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[22] And the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ lifted up their wings, and the wheels alongside them, and the glory of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was over them, above.

[23] And the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went up from upon the midst of the city and stood on the mountain that is east of the city.

[24] And a spirit lifted me up and brought me to ⟦chaldea|Kasdim|Chaldea⟧, to the exiles, in the vision, by the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and the vision that I had seen went up from upon me.

[25] And I spoke to the exiles all the words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he had shown me.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v7, 8, 13, 16, 17, 21) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both names.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v20, 22, 24) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"son of man"** (v2, 4, 15) — בֶּן־אָדָם, one wording through the book; no "O" before it (the Hebrew has no word for it).
- **"Moreover the spirit lifted me up" → "And a spirit lifted me up"** (v1) — Hebrew וַתִּשָּׂא אֹתִי רוּחַ: רוּחַ without the article ("spirit" or "wind"); "the Spirit" supplies an article (also v24). In v5 רוּחַ יְהוָה "the Spirit of Yahweh" and in v24 רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים "the Spirit of *Elohim*" stand with a name. **"unto the east gate … which looketh eastward" → "to the east gate … which faces eastward"** — הַקַּדְמוֹנִי הַפּוֹנֶה קָדִימָה. **"at the door of the gate" → "at the entrance of the gate"** — פֶּתַח "entrance". **"among whom I saw" → "and I saw in their midst"** — וָאֶרְאֶה בְתוֹכָם. **"princes of the people"** — שָׂרֵי הָעָם (שַׂר "prince").
- **"devise mischief, and give wicked counsel" → "devise wickedness and who counsel evil counsel"** (v2) — Hebrew הַחֹשְׁבִים אָוֶן וְהַיֹּעֲצִים עֲצַת־רָע: אָוֶן "wickedness"; verb and noun of one root, "counsel a counsel of evil".
- **"It is not near; let us build houses: this city is the caldron" → "Not near is the building of houses; it is the pot"** (v3) — Hebrew לֹא בְקָרוֹב בְּנוֹת בָּתִּים, literally "not in nearness the building of houses": a statement (the time to build houses is not near) or, read as a question, "Is not the building of houses near?" — the Hebrew has no question word; הִיא הַסִּיר "it is the pot" ("it" is the city, feminine; "this city" is not said); סִיר "pot" (also v7, 11); בָּשָׂר "flesh" (meat in the pot).
- **"Thus have ye said" → "So you have said"** (v5) — כֵּן אֲמַרְתֶּם: אָמַר "say" (not "think"). **"for I know the things that come into your mind, every one of them" → "and the things that come up in your spirit, I know them"** — וּמַעֲלוֹת רוּחֲכֶם אֲנִי יְדַעְתִּיהָ, literally "and the goings up of your spirit, I, I know it": רוּחַ "spirit" (not "mind"); the pronoun אֲנִי stands before the verb.
- **"ye have filled the streets thereof" → "you have filled its streets"** (v6) — חוּצוֹתֶיהָ; חָלָל "slain".
- **"but I will bring you forth out of the midst of it" → "but you, I will bring out of its midst"** (v7) — Hebrew וְאֶתְכֶם הוֹצִיא מִתּוֹכָהּ: הוֹצִיא is pointed as an infinitive absolute ("bringing out"), standing for the verb; the person is taken from v9 ("And I will bring you out"). Many Hebrew manuscripts and the versions read אוֹצִיא "I will bring out" (from memory). The MT is kept.
- **"Ye have feared the sword; and I will bring a sword upon you" → "The sword you have feared, and the sword I will bring upon you"** (v8) — the Hebrew order; חֶרֶב twice without article.
- **"deliver you into the hands of strangers" → "give you into the hand of strangers"** (v9) — וְנָתַתִּי … בְּיַד־זָרִים: נָתַן "give", יָד singular; זָר "stranger". **"execute judgments among you"** — וְעָשִׂיתִי בָכֶם שְׁפָטִים, שְׁפָטִים "judgments" (acts of judgment).
- **"in the border of Israel" → "on the border of Israel"** (v10) — עַל־גְּבוּל; v11 has אֶל־גְּבוּל "at the border". **"and ye shall know that I am the LORD"** — וִידַעְתֶּם כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה, one wording through the book (also v12).
- **"This city shall not be your caldron, neither shall ye be the flesh" → "It shall not be a pot for you, nor shall you be flesh in its midst"** (v11) — Hebrew הִיא לֹא־תִהְיֶה לָכֶם לְסִיר וְאַתֶּם תִּהְיוּ בְתוֹכָהּ לְבָשָׂר: the second clause has no לֹא; the negation of the first clause runs on into it (a literal "and you shall be flesh" would say the opposite); "this city" is not said.
- **"for ye have not walked in my statutes, neither executed my judgments, but have done after the manners of the heathen" → "in whose statutes you have not walked, and my ordinances you have not done, but according to the ordinances of the nations … you have done"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר בְּחֻקַּי לֹא הֲלַכְתֶּם: אֲשֶׁר is a relative ("I am Yahweh, in whose statutes …"; "because" is also possible); מִשְׁפָּט "ordinance" (a ruling), three times the one word; וּכְמִשְׁפְּטֵי … עֲשִׂיתֶם, the וְ is "but" here; גּוֹיִם "nations".
- **"when I prophesied" → "as I prophesied"** (v13) — כְּהִנָּבְאִי. **"Benaiah"** — here בְנָיָה, the short form (v1 בְּנָיָהוּ); the name keeps one form. **"Wilt thou make a full end of the remnant of Israel?" → "A full end you are making of the remnant of Israel!"** — Hebrew כָּלָה אַתָּה עֹשֶׂה אֵת שְׁאֵרִית יִשְׂרָאֵל: no question word (9:8 has one), a participle; it is often read as a question; שְׁאֵרִית "remnant"; קוֹל גָּדוֹל "a loud voice".
- **"Again the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v14) — וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula.
- **"thy brethren, even thy brethren, the men of thy kindred" → "your brothers, your brothers, the men of your redemption"** (v15) — Hebrew אַחֶיךָ אַחֶיךָ אַנְשֵׁי גְאֻלָּתֶךָ: אָח repeated; גְּאֻלָּה "redemption" (the right to redeem belongs to the nearest kin: the men whose redeemer you are); the Septuagint reads "the men of your exile" (גָּלוּת, from memory). **"and all the house of Israel wholly"** — כֻּלֹּה "all of it". **"Get you far from the LORD" → "Go far from Yahweh"** — רַחֲקוּ, pointed as an imperative (a perfect "they have gone far" would be pointed otherwise). **"unto us is this land given in possession" → "to us it has been given, the land, for a possession"** — לָנוּ הִיא נִתְּנָה הָאָרֶץ לְמוֹרָשָׁה, the Hebrew order; "this" is not said; מוֹרָשָׁה "possession".
- **"Although I have cast them far off … and although I have scattered them among the countries" → "Though I have removed them far … and though I have scattered them in the lands"** (v16) — כִּי הִרְחַקְתִּים … וְכִי הֲפִיצוֹתִים בָּאֲרָצוֹת: אֲרָצוֹת "lands" (also v17). **"yet will I be to them as a little sanctuary" → "yet I have been to them a sanctuary in small measure"** — וָאֱהִי לָהֶם לְמִקְדָּשׁ מְעַט, a past form; מְעַט "a little": "in small measure", "for a little while", or "a little sanctuary" — the Hebrew allows each. **"where they shall come" → "where they have come"** — בָּאוּ, perfect. The former text set "Thus says …" in inner quotes here and in v17; the quotation marks follow v2–12.
- **"I will even gather you … and assemble you" → "And I will gather you together … and gather you"** (v17) — וְקִבַּצְתִּי … וְאָסַפְתִּי: קִבֵּץ "gather together", אָסַף "gather" (as Isa 11:12); עַמִּים "peoples". **"the land of Israel" → "the ground of Israel"** — אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל (אֲדָמָה "ground"), Ezekiel's own phrase.
- **"they shall take away … all the detestable things thereof and all the abominations thereof" → "they shall remove from it all its detestable things and all its abominations"** (v18) — וְהֵסִירוּ (הֵסִיר "remove"); שִׁקּוּץ "detestable thing", תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" (also v21).
- **"one heart"** (v19) — לֵב אֶחָד; the Septuagint (from memory) and some Hebrew manuscripts have "another heart" (אַחֵר), and 36:26 has "a new heart": the MT is kept. **"I will put a new spirit within you"** — וְרוּחַ חֲדָשָׁה אֶתֵּן בְּקִרְבְּכֶם: "you" (plural) in the middle of "them": the change of person is the Hebrew's; נָתַן "give, put". **"I will take the stony heart out of their flesh, and will give them an heart of flesh" → "I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh, and I will give them a heart of flesh"** — וַהֲסִרֹתִי לֵב הָאֶבֶן מִבְּשָׂרָם וְנָתַתִּי לָהֶם לֵב בָּשָׂר: הֵסִיר "remove"; לֵב הָאֶבֶן "the heart of stone", לֵב בָּשָׂר "a heart of flesh" (36:26 has the same words, with "you").
- **"That they may walk" → "so that they may walk"** (v20) — לְמַעַן with a verb. **"and they shall be my people, and I will be their God" → "and they shall be a people to me, and I will be Elohim to them"** — וְהָיוּ־לִי לְעָם וַאֲנִי אֶהְיֶה לָהֶם לֵאלֹהִים, as Jer 30:22.
- **"But as for them whose heart walketh after the heart of their detestable things … I will recompense their way upon their own heads" → "But to the heart of their detestable things and their abominations their heart walks: their way upon their head I have laid"** (v21) — Hebrew וְאֶל־לֵב שִׁקּוּצֵיהֶם וְתוֹעֲבוֹתֵיהֶם לִבָּם הֹלֵךְ, word for word ("their heart walks to the heart of their detestable things"); the sentence is hard and is often emended (not followed here); דַּרְכָּם בְּרֹאשָׁם נָתַתִּי as 9:10.
- **"and the wheels beside them" → "and the wheels alongside them"** (v22) — לְעֻמָּתָם, as 10:19; מִלְמָעְלָה "above".
- **"went up from the midst of the city" → "went up from upon the midst of the city"** (v23) — מֵעַל תּוֹךְ הָעִיר. **"which is on the east side of the city" → "that is east of the city"** — מִקֶּדֶם לָעִיר, literally "from the front of the city" (קֶדֶם "front" = east).
- **"Afterwards the spirit took me up, and brought me in a vision by the Spirit of God into Chaldea, to them of the captivity" → "And a spirit lifted me up and brought me to Chaldea, to the exiles, in the vision, by the Spirit of Elohim"** (v24) — Hebrew וְרוּחַ נְשָׂאַתְנִי וַתְּבִיאֵנִי כַשְׂדִּימָה אֶל־הַגּוֹלָה בַּמַּרְאֶה בְּרוּחַ אֱלֹהִים: the Hebrew order; גּוֹלָה "exile, the exiles" (also v25); בַּמַּרְאֶה "in the vision". **"So the vision that I had seen went up from me" → "and the vision … went up from upon me"** — מֵעָלַי.
- **"Then I spake unto them of the captivity all the things that the LORD had shewed me" → "And I spoke to the exiles all the words of Yahweh that he had shown me"** (v25) — דִּבְרֵי יְהוָה "the words of Yahweh"; הֶרְאָנִי "he had shown me" (literally "made me see").

### Notes
- **v23** — the mountain east of the city is the Mount of Olives (the verse does not name it).
- **v19–20** — the same promise in 36:26–28.
