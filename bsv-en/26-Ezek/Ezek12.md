# Ezekiel 12

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, in the midst of the house of rebellion you dwell, who have eyes to see and have not seen, who have ears to hear and have not heard; for a house of rebellion they are.

[3] And you, son of man, make for yourself gear of exile, and go into exile by day before their eyes; and you shall go into exile from your place to another place before their eyes. Perhaps they will see, for a house of rebellion they are.

[4] And you shall bring out your gear, like gear of exile, by day before their eyes; and you shall go out in the evening before their eyes, like the goings out of exile.

[5] Before their eyes dig for yourself through the wall, and you shall bring out through it.

[6] Before their eyes on the shoulder you shall carry, in the thick darkness you shall bring out; your face you shall cover, and you shall not see the land; for a wonder I have given you to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[7] And I did so, as I was commanded: my gear I brought out, like gear of exile, by day; and in the evening I dug for myself through the wall with the hand; in the thick darkness I brought out; on the shoulder I carried, before their eyes.

[8] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me in the morning, saying,

[9] "Son of man, have not the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the house of rebellion, said to you, 'What are you doing?'

[10] Say to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: The chieftain is this burden in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who are in their midst.

[11] Say, I am your wonder: as I have done, so it shall be done to them; into exile, into captivity they shall go.

[12] And the chieftain who is in their midst shall carry on the shoulder in the thick darkness and go out; through the wall they shall dig, to bring out through it; his face he shall cover, because he himself shall not see the land with the eye.

[13] And I will spread my net over him, and he shall be caught in my net; and I will bring him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, and it he shall not see; and there he shall die.

[14] And all who are around him, his help, and all his troops, I will strew to every wind; and the sword I will draw out after them.

[15] And they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I scatter them among the nations and strew them in the lands.

[16] And I will leave over of them a few men from the sword, from the famine, and from the pestilence, so that they may recount all their abominations among the nations where they come; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[17] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[18] "Son of man, your bread with quaking you shall eat, and your water with trembling and with anxiety you shall drink.

[19] And you shall say to the people of the land, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ on the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Their bread with anxiety they shall eat, and their water in appallment they shall drink, so that her land may be desolate of its fullness, because of the violence of all who dwell in it.

[20] And the inhabited cities shall be laid waste, and the land shall be a desolation; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[21] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[22] "Son of man, what is this proverb that you have on the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, 'The days grow long, and every vision perishes'?

[23] Therefore say to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: I have made this proverb cease, and they shall no more speak it as a proverb in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. But speak to them: The days have drawn near, and the word of every vision.

[24] For there shall no more be any empty vision or smooth divination in the midst of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[25] For I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: I will speak the word that I will speak, and it shall be done; it shall no more be drawn out. For in your days, house of rebellion, I will speak a word and do it, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[26] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[27] "Son of man, behold, the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ are saying, 'The vision that he sees is for many days, and for times far off he prophesies.'

[28] Therefore say to them, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: None of my words shall be drawn out any more; the word that I speak, it shall be done, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v10, 19, 23, 25, 28) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both names.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"The word of the LORD also came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1, 8, 17, 21, 26) — וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula; in v8 with בַּבֹּקֶר "in the morning" after "to me". **"son of man"** (v2, 3, 9, 18, 22, 27) — בֶּן־אָדָם, one wording through the book; no "O".
- **"thou dwellest in the midst of a rebellious house" → "in the midst of the house of rebellion you dwell"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ בֵּית־הַמֶּרִי: מֶרִי "rebellion", a noun (בֵּית מְרִי "a house of rebellion", also v3, 9, 25). **"which have eyes to see, and see not; they have ears to hear, and hear not" → "who have eyes to see and have not seen, who have ears to hear and have not heard"** — וְלֹא רָאוּ … וְלֹא שָׁמֵעוּ, perfects.
- **"prepare thee stuff for removing" → "make for yourself gear of exile"** (v3) — Hebrew עֲשֵׂה לְךָ כְּלֵי גוֹלָה: כְּלִי "gear" (what a man has with him, as 1 Sam 25:13; also v4, 7); גּוֹלָה "exile". **"and remove by day in their sight" → "and go into exile by day before their eyes"** — וּגְלֵה … לְעֵינֵיהֶם (גָּלָה "go into exile"; לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of", also v4–7). **"it may be they will consider, though they be a rebellious house" → "Perhaps they will see, for a house of rebellion they are"** — אוּלַי יִרְאוּ: רָאָה "see"; כִּי "for" (or "though": both are possible).
- **"as stuff for removing" → "like gear of exile"** (v4) — כִּכְלֵי גוֹלָה (also v7). **"as they that go forth into captivity" → "like the goings out of exile"** — כְּמוֹצָאֵי גוֹלָה, literally "like the goings out of exile" (= as exiles go out); מוֹצָא "going out".
- **"Dig thou through the wall in their sight, and carry out thereby" → "Before their eyes dig for yourself through the wall, and you shall bring out through it"** (v5) — חֲתָר־לְךָ בַקִּיר וְהוֹצֵאתָ בּוֹ: no object for "bring out" (also v6, 7, 12).
- **"shalt thou bear it upon thy shoulders, and carry it forth in the twilight" → "on the shoulder you shall carry, in the thick darkness you shall bring out"** (v6) — עַל־כָּתֵף תִּשָּׂא בָּעֲלָטָה תוֹצִיא: no "it"; עֲלָטָה "thick darkness" (also v7, 12). **"that thou see not the ground" → "and you shall not see the land"** — וְלֹא תִרְאֶה אֶת־הָאָרֶץ. **"for I have set thee for a sign unto the house of Israel" → "for a wonder I have given you to the house of Israel"** — כִּי־מוֹפֵת נְתַתִּיךָ: מוֹפֵת "wonder" (beside אוֹת "sign"; a portent); נָתַן "give" (also v11).
- **"I digged through the wall with mine hand" → "I dug for myself through the wall with the hand"** (v7) — חָתַרְתִּי־לִי בַקִּיר בְּיָד. **"I brought it forth in the twilight, and I bare it upon my shoulder" → "in the thick darkness I brought out; on the shoulder I carried"** — no object.
- **"Hath not the house of Israel, the rebellious house, said unto thee" → "have not the house of Israel, the house of rebellion, said to you"** (v9).
- **"This burden concerneth the prince in Jerusalem" → "The chieftain is this burden in Jerusalem"** (v10) — Hebrew הַנָּשִׂיא הַמַּשָּׂא הַזֶּה בִּירוּשָׁלִַם, word for word (= the burden is about the chieftain); נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (literally "one lifted up") and מַשָּׂא "burden" ("oracle"; literally "what is lifted up") are from one root, נָשָׂא "lift up". **"and all the house of Israel that are among them" → "and all the house of Israel who are in their midst"** — אֲשֶׁר־הֵמָּה בְתוֹכָם; whose midst is meant is not said.
- **"I am your sign" → "I am your wonder"** (v11) — מוֹפֶתְכֶם. **"they shall remove and go into captivity" → "into exile, into captivity they shall go"** — בַּגּוֹלָה בַשְּׁבִי יֵלֵכוּ, the Hebrew order.
- **"the prince … shall bear upon his shoulder" → "the chieftain … shall carry on the shoulder"** (v12) — אֶל־כָּתֵף יִשָּׂא, no "his", no object. **"they shall dig through the wall to carry out thereby" → "through the wall they shall dig, to bring out through it"** — לְהוֹצִיא בוֹ. **"that he see not the ground with his eyes" → "because he himself shall not see the land with the eye"** — Hebrew יַעַן אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יִרְאֶה לַעַיִן הוּא אֶת־הָאָרֶץ: יַעַן אֲשֶׁר "because"; לַעַיִן "with the eye" (literally "to the eye"); הוּא "he", emphatic.
- **"he shall be taken in my snare" → "he shall be caught in my net"** (v13) — וְנִתְפַּשׂ בִּמְצוּדָתִי: נִתְפַּשׂ "be caught"; מְצוּדָה from צוּד "hunt", a hunter's net (as Eccl 9:12; the same word is "stronghold" in 2 Sam 22:2); the first "net" is רֶשֶׁת: two words, one English. **"yet shall he not see it" → "and it he shall not see"** — וְאוֹתָהּ לֹא־יִרְאֶה.
- **"all that are about him to help him" → "all who are around him, his help"** (v14) — the written text עזרה, the qere עֶזְרוֹ "his help" (עֵזֶר "help"). **"all his bands" → "all his troops"** — אֲגַפָּיו, a word found only in Ezekiel (17:21; 38–39), literally perhaps "wings" of an army (uncertain). **"I will scatter toward every wind" → "I will strew to every wind"** — אֱזָרֶה (זָרָה "strew", beside הֵפִיץ "scatter" in v15: two verbs); לְכָל־רוּחַ "to every wind". **"I will draw out the sword after them" → "the sword I will draw out after them"** — חֶרֶב אָרִיק, hifil of רִיק, literally "empty out" (the sheath); Hebrew order kept.
- **"when I shall scatter them among the nations, and disperse them in the countries" → "when I scatter them among the nations and strew them in the lands"** (v15) — בַּהֲפִיצִי … וְזֵרִיתִי … בָּאֲרָצוֹת. **"and they shall know that I am the LORD"** — וְיָדְעוּ כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה, one wording through the book (also v16, 20).
- **"But I will leave a few men of them" → "And I will leave over of them a few men"** (v16) — וְהוֹתַרְתִּי מֵהֶם אַנְשֵׁי מִסְפָּר: הוֹתִיר "leave over"; אַנְשֵׁי מִסְפָּר, literally "men of number" (= few, as יָמִים מִסְפָּר "a few days"). **"from the famine, and from the pestilence"** — מֵרָעָב וּמִדָּבֶר. **"that they may declare" → "so that they may recount"** — לְמַעַן יְסַפְּרוּ (סִפֵּר "recount").
- **"eat thy bread with quaking, and drink thy water with trembling and with carefulness" → "your bread with quaking you shall eat, and your water with trembling and with anxiety you shall drink"** (v18) — Hebrew לַחְמְךָ בְּרַעַשׁ תֹּאכֵל וּמֵימֶיךָ בְּרָגְזָה וּבִדְאָגָה תִּשְׁתֶּה: imperfects, the Hebrew order; רַעַשׁ "quaking" (the word for an earthquake); רָגְזָה, found only here (the root of רָגַז "be agitated, tremble"); דְּאָגָה "anxiety" (also v19).
- **"And say" → "And you shall say"** (v19) — וְאָמַרְתָּ. **"of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and of the land of Israel" → "of the inhabitants of Jerusalem on the ground of Israel"** — לְיוֹשְׁבֵי יְרוּשָׁלִַם אֶל־אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל: no second "of"; אֲדָמָה "ground" (also v22). **"drink their water with astonishment" → "their water in appallment they shall drink"** — בְּשִׁמָּמוֹן (as 4:16), from שָׁמֵם "be desolate, be appalled": the same root as תֵּשַׁם "be desolate" in the next clause. **"that her land may be desolate from all that is therein" → "so that her land may be desolate of its fullness"** — לְמַעַן תֵּשַׁם אַרְצָהּ מִמְּלֹאָהּ: מְלֹא "fullness" (what fills it); "her" is Jerusalem. **"because of the violence of all them that dwell therein"** — חָמָס "violence".
- **"the cities that are inhabited shall be laid waste" → "the inhabited cities shall be laid waste"** (v20) — הַנּוֹשָׁבוֹת (literally "that are dwelt in"); תֶּחֱרַבְנָה from חָרֵב "be dry, be laid waste" (the root of חָרְבָּה "ruin"). **"and the land shall be desolate" → "and the land shall be a desolation"** — שְׁמָמָה, a noun. **"and ye shall know that I am the LORD"** — וִידַעְתֶּם, "you" plural.
- **"what is that proverb that ye have in the land of Israel" → "what is this proverb that you have on the ground of Israel"** (v22) — הַמָּשָׁל הַזֶּה לָכֶם עַל־אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל: מָשָׁל, here a current saying, "proverb" (elsewhere "parable"; also v23). **"The days are prolonged, and every vision faileth" → "The days grow long, and every vision perishes"** — יַאַרְכוּ הַיָּמִים וְאָבַד כָּל־חָזוֹן: אָבַד "perish"; חָזוֹן "vision" (also v23, 24, 27).
- **"I will make this proverb to cease" → "I have made this proverb cease"** (v23) — הִשְׁבַּתִּי, a perfect, hifil of שָׁבַת "cease". **"they shall no more use it as a proverb" → "they shall no more speak it as a proverb"** — וְלֹא־יִמְשְׁלוּ אֹתוֹ עוֹד, the verb of מָשָׁל. **"but say unto them" → "But speak to them"** — כִּי אִם־דַּבֵּר אֲלֵיהֶם, דִּבֶּר "speak". **"The days are at hand, and the effect of every vision" → "The days have drawn near, and the word of every vision"** — קָרְבוּ, a perfect; דְּבַר "the word" ("effect", "fulfillment" are explanations).
- **"vain vision nor flattering divination" → "empty vision or smooth divination"** (v24) — חֲזוֹן שָׁוְא וּמִקְסַם חָלָק: שָׁוְא "empty"; מִקְסַם (only here and 13:7; the root of קֶסֶם "divination"); חָלָק "smooth".
- **"I will speak, and the word that I shall speak shall come to pass" → "I will speak the word that I will speak, and it shall be done"** (v25) — Hebrew אֲנִי יְהוָה אֲדַבֵּר אֵת אֲשֶׁר אֲדַבֵּר דָּבָר וְיֵעָשֶׂה, literally "I, Yahweh, will speak what I will speak, a word, and it shall be done" ("For I am Yahweh" or "For I, Yahweh, will speak": both readings are possible); נַעֲשָׂה "be done". **"it shall be no more prolonged" → "it shall no more be drawn out"** — לֹא תִמָּשֵׁךְ עוֹד, nifal of מָשַׁךְ "draw" (also v28). **"O rebellious house" → "house of rebellion"** — no "O"; **"will I say the word, and will perform it" → "I will speak a word and do it"** — אֲדַבֵּר דָּבָר וַעֲשִׂיתִיו.
- **"they of the house of Israel say" → "the house of Israel are saying"** (v27) — אֹמְרִים, a participle; "they of" is not in the Hebrew. **"is for many days to come" → "is for many days"** — לְיָמִים רַבִּים. **"and he prophesieth of the times that are far off" → "and for times far off he prophesies"** — וּלְעִתִּים רְחוֹקוֹת הוּא נִבָּא, the Hebrew order.
- **"There shall none of my words be prolonged any more, but the word which I have spoken shall be done" → "None of my words shall be drawn out any more; the word that I speak, it shall be done"** (v28) — לֹא־תִמָּשֵׁךְ עוֹד כָּל־דְּבָרָי אֲשֶׁר אֲדַבֵּר דָּבָר וְיֵעָשֶׂה: "but" is not in the Hebrew; אֲדַבֵּר imperfect "I speak"; וְיֵעָשֶׂה "and it shall be done".
