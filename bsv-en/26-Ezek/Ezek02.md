# Ezekiel 2

[1] And he said to me, "Son of man, stand on your feet, and I will speak with you."

[2] And a spirit came into me as he spoke to me, and it set me on my feet; and I heard the one speaking to me.

[3] And he said to me, "Son of man, I am sending you to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to nations that rebel, who have rebelled against me; they and their fathers have transgressed against me to this very day.

[4] And the sons are hard of face and strong of heart; I am sending you to them, and you shall say to them, 'Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.'

[5] And they, whether they hear or whether they cease — for they are a house of rebellion — and they shall know that a prophet has been in their midst.

[6] "And you, son of man, do not fear them, and do not fear their words, for briers and thorns are with you, and you dwell among scorpions. Do not fear their words, and do not be dismayed before their faces, for they are a house of rebellion.

[7] And you shall speak my words to them, whether they hear or whether they cease, for they are rebellion.

[8] "And you, son of man, hear what I am speaking to you: do not be rebellion like the house of rebellion; open your mouth and eat what I am giving you."

[9] And I looked, and behold, a hand stretched out to me, and behold, in it a scroll of a book.

[10] And he spread it out before me, and it was written front and back; and written on it were dirges and moaning and woe.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"the Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names (the `adonai-yhwh` token, the prophets' wording).
- **"Son of man" kept** (v1, 3, 6, 8) — Hebrew בֶּן־אָדָם, "son of man" (אָדָם "man, mankind"), one wording through the book.
- **"And the spirit entered into me when he spake unto me, and set me upon my feet, that I heard him that spake unto me" → "And a spirit came into me as he spoke to me, and it set me on my feet; and I heard the one speaking to me"** (v2) — Hebrew רוּחַ without the article: "a spirit" (or "wind"; no capital); the verbs are feminine, of the spirit. מִדַּבֵּר is pointed as a hitpael ("speaking with itself / making itself heard"), not the piel of 1:28 (as Num 7:89).
- **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** (v3) — בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל; **"to a rebellious nation" → "to nations that rebel"** — גּוֹיִם הַמּוֹרְדִים, plural "nations" with a participle, as printed. **"even unto this very day" → "to this very day"** — עַד־עֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "to the bone of this day". **"transgressed"** — פָּשַׁע בְּ.
- **"For they are impudent children and stiffhearted" → "And the sons are hard of face and strong of heart"** (v4) — Hebrew וְהַבָּנִים קְשֵׁי פָנִים וְחִזְקֵי־לֵב: קָשֶׁה "hard" of the face, חָזָק "strong" of the heart (the חָזַק row: never "harden", never "stubborn"). **"I do send thee" → "I am sending you"** (v3, 4) — שׁוֹלֵחַ, a participle.
- **"whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear" → "whether they hear or whether they cease"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew אִם־יִשְׁמְעוּ וְאִם־יֶחְדָּלוּ; חָדַל "cease" (its row): they leave off hearing. **"yet shall know" → "and they shall know"** (v5) — וְיָדְעוּ: the "and" opens the main clause after the condition and is kept; the formula "and they shall know that I am Yahweh" (6:7 and often) begins with the same verb.
- **"a rebellious house" → "a house of rebellion"** (v5, 6, 8) — Hebrew בֵּית מְרִי, a noun: "house of rebellion" (מֶרִי, its row). In v7 the Hebrew has only כִּי מְרִי הֵמָּה, "for they are rebellion"; in v8 אַל־תְּהִי־מֶרִי כְּבֵית הַמֶּרִי, "do not be rebellion like the house of rebellion" — the noun kept each time.
- **"be not afraid of them, neither be afraid of their words" → "do not fear them, and do not fear their words"** (v6) — one verb, יָרֵא, three times. **"though briers and thorns be with thee" → "for briers and thorns are with you"** — כִּי "for"; סָרָבִים and סַלּוֹנִים are found only here (סַלּוֹן also 28:24): the meaning is uncertain; סָרָבִים may come from a root "be stubborn". **"thou dost dwell among scorpions" → "you dwell among scorpions"** — אֶל־עַקְרַבִּים אַתָּה יוֹשֵׁב, a participle. **"nor be dismayed at their looks" → "and do not be dismayed before their faces"** — מִפְּנֵיהֶם אַל־תֵּחָת; חָתַת "be dismayed".
- **"what I say unto thee" → "what I am speaking to you"** (v8) — מְדַבֵּר, a participle; so also **"that I give thee" → "what I am giving you"** — נֹתֵן.
- **"a roll of a book" → "a scroll of a book"** (v9) — מְגִלַּת־סֵפֶר. **"an hand was sent unto me" → "a hand stretched out to me"** — יָד שְׁלוּחָה, the participle of שָׁלַח.
- **"within and without" → "front and back"** (v10) — פָּנִים וְאָחוֹר, literally "face and back". **"there was written therein lamentations, and mourning, and woe" → "written on it were dirges and moaning and woe"** — קִנִים "dirges" (plural of קִינָה, its row); הֶגֶה "moaning" (a low sound, as Job 37:2 "rumbling"); הִי "woe" is found only here (perhaps a short form of נְהִי "wailing", uncertain). The earlier "words of" is not in the Hebrew.

### Notes
- ***Ben-Adam*** — "Son of man" — Ezekiel is addressed by this title 93 times; emphasizes his humanity before the divine.
