# Ezekiel 25

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, set your face toward the sons of Ammon, and prophesy against them.

[3] And say to the sons of Ammon, Hear the word of ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧. Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you said, 'Aha!' over my sanctuary when it was profaned, and over the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ when it was made desolate, and over the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ when they went into exile,

[4] therefore behold, I am giving you to the sons of the east for a possession, and they shall set their encampments in you and put their dwellings in you; they shall eat your fruit, and they shall drink your milk.

[5] And I will make Rabbah a habitation of camels, and the sons of Ammon a lying place of the flock; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you clapped the hand and stamped with the foot, and rejoiced with all your contempt in soul over the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[7] therefore behold, I have stretched out my hand against you, and I will give you as plunder to the nations, and I will cut you off from the peoples, and I will make you perish from the lands; I will destroy you; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and Seir said, Behold, like all the nations is the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,

[9] therefore behold, I am opening the shoulder of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ from the cities, from its cities, from its end, the beauty of the land, ⟦beth-jeshimoth|Beit HaYeshimot|Beth-jeshimoth⟧, ⟦baal-meon|Baal Meon|Baal-meon⟧ and ⟦kiriathaim|Qiryatayim|Kiriathaim⟧,

[10] to the sons of the east, together with the sons of Ammon; and I will give it for a possession, so that the sons of Ammon shall not be remembered among the nations.

[11] And on ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ I will execute judgments; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because Edom acted in taking vengeance on the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they have surely been guilty and have avenged themselves on them,

[13] therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: And I will stretch out my hand against Edom and cut off from it man and beast, and I will make it a ruin from Teman; and toward Dedan they shall fall by the sword.

[14] And I will put my vengeance on Edom by the hand of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they shall do in Edom according to my anger and according to my fury; and they shall know my vengeance, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[15] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ acted in vengeance, and avenged themselves with vengeance with contempt in soul, to destroy, with everlasting enmity,

[16] therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am stretching out my hand against the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and I will cut off the ⟦cherethites|Keretim|Cherethites⟧, and I will make perish the remnant of the shore of the sea.

[17] And I will do great vengeances on them with rebukes of fury; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, when I put my vengeance on them."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 6, 8, 12–16) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names.
- **"The word of the LORD came again unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula; "again" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"set thy face against the Ammonites" → "set your face toward the sons of Ammon"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׂים פָּנֶיךָ אֶל, אֶל "toward" (also "against"); בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן "sons of Ammon" (also v3, 5, 10; as Jer 49:1).
- **"Because thou saidst, Aha, against my sanctuary … and against the land of Israel, when it was desolate … when they went into captivity" → "Because you said, 'Aha!' over my sanctuary … and over the ground of Israel when it was made desolate … when they went into exile"** (v3) — Hebrew אָמְרֵךְ, feminine "your saying" (Ammon addressed as a woman; v4–5 go on in the feminine, v6–7 turn to the masculine); הֶאָח "Aha" (a cry of glee); אֶל "over" (also "against"); נִחָל nifal "was profaned" (חִלֵּל "profane"); אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל "the ground of Israel" (Ezekiel's phrase, also v6; אֶרֶץ is "land"); נָשַׁמָּה nifal of שָׁמֵם "be made desolate"; הָלְכוּ בַּגּוֹלָה "went into exile" (lit. "went in the exile").
- **"Behold, therefore I will deliver thee to the men of the east for a possession, and they shall set their palaces in thee" → "therefore behold, I am giving you to the sons of the east for a possession, and they shall set their encampments in you"** (v4) — Hebrew לָכֵן הִנְנִי נֹתְנָךְ, "therefore" first, a participle; בְּנֵי־קֶדֶם "sons of the east" (קֶדֶם "east", lit. "front"); מוֹרָשָׁה "possession" (its row; also v10); טִירָה "encampment" (its row: a ring of tents; not "palaces"); נָתְנוּ בָךְ מִשְׁכְּנֵיהֶם "put their dwellings in you" (נָתַן "give, put"); הֵמָּה … וְהֵמָּה "they … and they" kept.
- **"And I will make Rabbah a stable for camels, and the Ammonites a couchingplace for flocks" → "And I will make Rabbah a habitation of camels, and the sons of Ammon a lying place of the flock"** (v5) — Hebrew נְוֵה גְמַלִּים, נָוֶה "habitation" (its row; of beasts the pasture where they stay); מִרְבַּץ־צֹאן "a lying place of the flock" (noun of רָבַץ "lie", its row; צֹאן "flock"); וִידַעְתֶּם plural "and you shall know".
- **"Because thou hast clapped thine hands, and stamped with the feet, and rejoiced in heart with all thy despite against the land of Israel" → "Because you clapped the hand and stamped with the foot, and rejoiced with all your contempt in soul over the ground of Israel"** (v6) — Hebrew מַחְאֲךָ יָד וְרַקְעֲךָ בְּרָגֶל, infinitives, "hand" and "foot" singular (מָחָא "clap", as the Ps 98 row); בְּכָל־שָׁאטְךָ בְּנֶפֶשׁ "with all your contempt in soul" (שְׁאָט "contempt", also v15; נֶפֶשׁ "soul", not "heart").
- **"Behold, therefore I will stretch out mine hand upon thee, and will deliver thee for a spoil to the heathen; and I will cut thee off from the people, and I will cause thee to perish out of the countries" → "therefore behold, I have stretched out my hand against you, and I will give you as plunder to the nations, and I will cut you off from the peoples, and I will make you perish from the lands"** (v7) — Hebrew הִנְנִי נָטִיתִי, a perfect after "behold me"; the written text (ketiv) has לבג, which gives no sense; the reading tradition (qere) לְבַז "as plunder" is translated (בַּז "plunder", its row); גּוֹיִם "nations"; עַמִּים plural "peoples"; הֶאֱבִיד "make perish" (also v16); אֲרָצוֹת "lands" (plural of אֶרֶץ); וְיָדַעְתָּ singular.
- **"Because that Moab and Seir do say, Behold, the house of Judah is like unto all the heathen" → "Because Moab and Seir said, Behold, like all the nations is the house of Judah"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲמֹר, an infinitive; the Hebrew order; "other" is not in the Hebrew. Seir is the hill country of Edom.
- **"Therefore, behold, I will open the side of Moab from the cities, from his cities which are on his frontiers, the glory of the country" → "therefore behold, I am opening the shoulder of Moab from the cities, from its cities, from its end, the beauty of the land"** (v9) — Hebrew הִנְנִי פֹתֵחַ, a participle; כֶּתֶף "shoulder" (its row; the slope of the land); מִקָּצֵהוּ "from its end" (קָצֶה "end": its outer edge, the frontier; "which are" is not in the Hebrew); צְבִי אֶרֶץ "the beauty of the land" (צְבִי "beauty", its row). **"and Kiriathaim"** — the qere וְקִרְיָתָיְמָה (ketiv וקריתמה, the same name spelled short) has the ending of direction, "to Kiriathaim"; English cannot carry it.
- **"Unto the men of the east with the Ammonites, and will give them in possession, that the Ammonites may not be remembered" → "to the sons of the east, together with the sons of Ammon; and I will give it for a possession, so that the sons of Ammon shall not be remembered"** (v10) — Hebrew לִבְנֵי־קֶדֶם עַל־בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן goes on from v9 ("opening … to the sons of the east"); עַל "together with" (lit. "upon", its row); וּנְתַתִּיהָ "I will give it" (feminine singular: the land, or Moab).
- **"And I will execute judgments upon Moab" → "And on Moab I will execute judgments"** (v11) — Hebrew וּבְמוֹאָב אֶעֱשֶׂה שְׁפָטִים, the Hebrew order; שְׁפָטִים "judgments" (its row); עָשָׂה "execute" (lit. "do").
- **"Because that Edom hath dealt against the house of Judah by taking vengeance, and hath greatly offended, and revenged himself upon them" → "Because Edom acted in taking vengeance on the house of Judah, and they have surely been guilty and have avenged themselves on them"** (v12) — Hebrew עֲשׂוֹת אֱדוֹם בִּנְקֹם נָקָם, lit. "Edom's doing in avenging vengeance"; וַיֶּאְשְׁמוּ אָשׁוֹם, the infinitive absolute with its verb, "surely been guilty" (lit. "were guilty, being guilty"; אָשֵׁם "be guilty", its row; not "offended"); the verbs are plural: "they" (Edom's people); נִקְּמוּ nifal "avenged themselves".
- **"I will also stretch out mine hand upon Edom … and I will make it desolate from Teman; and they of Dedan shall fall by the sword" → "And I will stretch out my hand against Edom … and I will make it a ruin from Teman; and toward Dedan they shall fall by the sword"** (v13) — Hebrew וְנָטִתִי, "And" kept; חָרְבָּה "ruin" (its row); the accents set "from Teman" with the ruin and וּדְדָנֶה "and toward Dedan" (the ending of direction) with the falling; "they of" is not in the Hebrew. Teman and Dedan stand for the south and the far edge of Edom's reach.
- **"And I will lay my vengeance upon Edom" → "And I will put my vengeance on Edom"** (v14) — Hebrew נָתַתִּי … בְּ, lit. "give in" (the Num 31:3 row "put … on"; also v17); אַף "anger" (lit. "nose"), חֵמָה "fury"; נְאֻם "declares" (lit. "declaration of").
- **"Because the Philistines have dealt by revenge, and have taken vengeance with a despiteful heart, to destroy it for the old hatred" → "Because the Philistines acted in vengeance, and avenged themselves with vengeance with contempt in soul, to destroy, with everlasting enmity"** (v15) — Hebrew עֲשׂוֹת פְּלִשְׁתִּים בִּנְקָמָה וַיִּנָּקְמוּ נָקָם בִּשְׁאָט בְּנֶפֶשׁ, the one root three times; בְּנֶפֶשׁ "in soul" (as v6); לְמַשְׁחִית "to destroy" (no "it" written; the "destroy" of its row); אֵיבַת עוֹלָם "everlasting enmity" (lit. "enmity of the age", the עוֹלָם row; not "old hatred").
- **"Behold, I will stretch out mine hand upon the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethims, and destroy the remnant of the sea coast" → "Behold, I am stretching out my hand against the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethites, and I will make perish the remnant of the shore of the sea"** (v16) — Hebrew הִנְנִי נוֹטֶה, a participle; וְהִכְרַתִּי אֶת־כְּרֵתִים, a play of sound (*hikratti … keretim*: "cut off the Cutters"); הֶאֱבִיד "make perish" (as v7); שְׁאֵרִית "remnant"; חוֹף הַיָּם "the shore of the sea" (as Jer 47:7).
- **"And I will execute great vengeance upon them with furious rebukes" → "And I will do great vengeances on them with rebukes of fury"** (v17) — Hebrew נְקָמוֹת גְּדֹלוֹת plural; בְּתוֹכְחוֹת חֵמָה "with rebukes of fury"; בְּתִתִּי "when I put" (lit. "in my giving").

### Notes
- **Ammon, Moab, Edom, Philistia** — the four neighbours east, southeast, south and west of Judah; Rabbah was the chief city of Ammon. Beth-jeshimoth, Baal-meon and Kiriathaim lie on the plateau north of the Arnon.
