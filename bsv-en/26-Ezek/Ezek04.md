# Ezekiel 4

[1] "And you, son of man, take yourself a brick, and put it before you, and engrave on it a city, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] And put a siege against it, and build a siege wall against it, and pour out a mound against it, and put camps against it, and place battering rams against it all around.

[3] And you, take yourself an iron griddle, and put it as an iron wall between you and the city, and fix your face toward it, and it shall be in the siege, and you shall besiege it. It is a sign for the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] "And you, lie on your left side, and place the iniquity of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ upon it; the number of the days that you lie upon it, you shall bear their iniquity.

[5] And I — I have given you the years of their iniquity as a number of days, three hundred and ninety days; and you shall bear the iniquity of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[6] And you shall complete these, and you shall lie on your right side a second time, and you shall bear the iniquity of the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ forty days; a day for the year, a day for the year, I have given it to you.

[7] And toward the siege of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ you shall fix your face, and your arm bared, and you shall prophesy against it.

[8] And behold, I have put cords upon you, and you shall not turn from your side to your side until you have completed the days of your siege.

[9] "And you, take yourself wheat and barley and beans and lentils and millet and spelt, and put them in one vessel, and make them for yourself into bread; the number of the days that you lie on your side, three hundred and ninety days, you shall eat it.

[10] And your food that you eat shall be by weight, twenty shekels for the day; from time to time you shall eat it.

[11] And water by measure you shall drink, the sixth of the hin; from time to time you shall drink.

[12] And as a cake of barley you shall eat it; and it — on the dung of what comes out of man you shall bake it, before their eyes."

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "Thus shall the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ eat their bread unclean among the nations where I will drive them."

[14] And I said, "Ah, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! Behold, my soul has not been defiled; and a carcass and a torn animal I have not eaten from my youth until now, and foul flesh has not come into my mouth."

[15] And he said to me, "See, I have given you the dung of the herd instead of the dung of man, and you shall make your bread on them."

[16] And he said to me, "Son of man, behold, I am breaking the staff of bread in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they shall eat bread by weight and with anxiety, and water by measure and in appallment they shall drink,

[17] so that they may lack bread and water, and be appalled, each man and his brother, and rot away in their iniquity."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names (the `adonai-yhwh` token, the prophets' wording); no "O" (the Hebrew has none).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("Then", "Moreover", "So long", "I will do this" removed).
- **"take thee a tile" → "take yourself a brick"** (v1, 3, 9) — קַח־לְךָ, the לְךָ kept as the reflexive (as Jer 16:2); לְבֵנָה "brick" (its row). **"lay it before thee" → "put it before you"** — נָתַן "give, put" (v1, 2, 3, 8); שִׂים "place" (v2, 4).
- **"lay siege against it, and build a fort against it, and cast a mount against it" → "put a siege against it, and build a siege wall against it, and pour out a mound against it"** (v2) — מָצוֹר "siege" (its row), דָּיֵק "siege wall" (uncertain; its row), סֹלְלָה "mound", שָׁפַךְ "pour out" (as 2 Kgs 19:32). **"battering rams"** — כָּרִים, literally "rams" (the animal word used of the engine).
- **"an iron pan" → "an iron griddle"** (v3) — מַחֲבַת (its row). **"set thy face" → "fix your face"** (v3, 7) — הֵכִין "make firm, set up". **"and it shall be besieged, and thou shalt lay siege against it" → "and it shall be in the siege, and you shall besiege it"** — וְהָיְתָה בַמָּצוֹר וְצַרְתָּ עָלֶיהָ (the צוּר עַל row).
- **"the punishment" → "the iniquity"** (v4–6, 17) — Hebrew עָוֹן, "iniquity", with נָשָׂא "bear" (its row: the guilt and what it brings; not "punishment"). The earlier text had "punishment" in every place.
- **"For I have laid upon thee the years of their iniquity, according to the number of the days" → "And I — I have given you the years of their iniquity as a number of days"** (v5) — וַאֲנִי נָתַתִּי לְךָ … לְמִסְפַּר יָמִים; "equal to", "so long" are not in the Hebrew. **"three hundred and ninety days"** (v5, 9) — the number in words (the reviewer's number row). The Septuagint has another number here (190, from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"And when thou hast accomplished them, lie again on thy right side" → "And you shall complete these, and you shall lie on your right side a second time"** (v6) — weqatal verbs, the chain kept; the qere הַיְמָנִי and the written form הימיני both mean "right". **"I have appointed thee each day for a year" → "a day for the year, a day for the year, I have given it to you"** — יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה יוֹם לַשָּׁנָה נְתַתִּיו לָךְ, the repetition kept (= each day for a year).
- **"and thine arm shall be uncovered, and thou shalt prophesy against it" → "and your arm bared, and you shall prophesy against it"** (v7) — וּזְרֹעֲךָ חֲשׂוּפָה, a clause without a verb; עָלֶיהָ "against it" (not "against the city").
- **"I will lay bands upon thee" → "I have put cords upon you"** (v8) — נָתַתִּי, a perfect (as 3:25); עֲבֹתִים "cords" (as Judg 15:13). **"from one side to another" → "from your side to your side"** — מִצִּדְּךָ אֶל־צִדֶּךָ.
- **"wheat, and barley, and beans, and lentiles, and millet, and fitches" → "wheat and barley and beans and lentils and millet and spelt"** (v9) — each with "and" as the Hebrew; כֻּסְּמִים "spelt" (its row; KJV "fitches"); פּוֹל "beans", דֹּחַן "millet" (both uncertain in kind). **"make thee bread thereof" → "make them for yourself into bread"** — וְעָשִׂיתָ אוֹתָם לְךָ לְלָחֶם. **"thou shalt lie" → "you lie"** — שׁוֹכֵב, a participle.
- **"twenty shekels a day" → "twenty shekels for the day"** (v10) — לַיּוֹם; the shekel is a weight, not a coin (about 230 grams in all; outside knowledge). **"from time to time" kept** (v10, 11) — מֵעֵת עַד־עֵת; the earlier "from day to day" is not the Hebrew.
- **"the sixth part of an hin" → "the sixth of the hin"** (v11) — שִׁשִּׁית הַהִין, with the article; מְשׂוּרָה "measure".
- **"thou shalt eat it as barley cakes, and thou shalt bake it with dung that cometh out of man" → "as a cake of barley you shall eat it; and it — on the dung of what comes out of man you shall bake it"** (v12) — עֻגַת שְׂעֹרִים, singular; גֶּלְלֵי "dung" (pellets, balls of dung); צֵאַת הָאָדָם "what comes out of man", the plain word for human excrement (צֵאָה, its row; as Deut 23:13). Not softened. בְּ "on, with": the dung is the fuel of the baking (v15 "on them"). **"in their sight" → "before their eyes"** — לְעֵינֵיהֶם (its row).
- **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** (v13) — בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"whither I will drive them" → "where I will drive them"** — אַדִּיחֵם, hifil of נָדַח.
- **"my soul hath not been polluted" → "my soul has not been defiled"** (v14) — מְטֻמָּאָה, pual of טָמֵא "be unclean" (the טִמֵּא row). **"that which dieth of itself, or is torn in pieces" → "a carcass and a torn animal"** — נְבֵלָה · טְרֵפָה (their row). **"abominable flesh" → "foul flesh"** — בְּשַׂר פִּגּוּל (the פִּגּוּל row: sacrificial flesh kept too long; exact sense uncertain). **"Ah"** — אֲהָהּ, a cry of dismay.
- **"cow's dung for man's dung" → "the dung of the herd instead of the dung of man"** (v15) — צְפִיעֵי הַבָּקָר, the qere (the written form צפועי, the same sense); בָּקָר "herd (cattle)", collective; תַּחַת "instead of". **"thou shalt prepare thy bread therewith" → "you shall make your bread on them"** — עֲלֵיהֶם, plural.
- **"I will break the staff of bread" → "I am breaking the staff of bread"** (v16) — הִנְנִי שֹׁבֵר מַטֵּה־לֶחֶם, a participle; "staff of bread" is the Hebrew (the earlier "supply" interprets it). **"with care" → "with anxiety"** — דְּאָגָה (its row). **"with astonishment" → "in appallment"** — שִׁמָּמוֹן, a noun of שָׁמֵם; **"be astonied one with another" → "be appalled, each man and his brother"** (v17) — וְנָשַׁמּוּ אִישׁ וְאָחִיו (the אִישׁ … אָחִיו row; "look at one another" is not in the Hebrew).
- **"consume away for their iniquity" → "rot away in their iniquity"** (v17) — נָמַק nifal "rot away" (its row); בַּעֲוֹנָם "in their iniquity". **"That they may want" → "so that they may lack"** — לְמַעַן (its row); חָסֵר "lack".
