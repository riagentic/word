# Ezekiel 17

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, riddle a riddle and speak a parable to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[3] and you shall say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

The great eagle, great of wings, long of pinion,
full of feathers, which had the many colors,
came to the ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧
and took the crown of the cedar.

[4] The top of its young shoots he plucked off,
and he brought it to a land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧;
in a city of traders he set it.

[5] And he took of the seed of the land
and put it in a field of seed;
he took it beside many waters,
as a willow he set it.

[6] And it sprouted and became a spreading vine, low of stature,
for its branches to turn toward him,
and its roots were to be under him;
and it became a vine,
and it made shoots and sent out limbs.

[7] And there was one great eagle,
great of wings and much of feathers;
and behold, this vine bent its roots toward him,
and its branches it sent out to him,
for him to water it from the beds of its planting.

[8] To a good field, to many waters it was planted,
to make boughs and to bear fruit,
to become a mighty vine.

[9] Say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Shall it prosper? Will he not tear out its roots and strip off its fruit, and it shall wither? All the plucked leaves of its sprouting shall wither — and not with a great arm and with a numerous people, to lift it up from its roots.

[10] And behold, it is planted: shall it prosper? Will it not, when the east wind touches it, surely wither? On the beds of its sprouting it shall wither."

[11] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[12] "Say, please, to the house of rebellion: Do you not know what these are? Say: Behold, the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and took her king and her princes and brought them to him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[13] And he took of the seed of the kingship and cut a covenant with him and brought him into an oath; and the leading men of the land he took,

[14] for it to be a low kingdom, so as not to lift itself up, to keep his covenant, so that it should stand.

[15] And he rebelled against him by sending his messengers to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, to give him horses and a numerous people. Shall he prosper? Shall he escape who does these things? And he has broken a covenant, and shall he escape?

[16] As I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, surely in the place of the king who made him king, whose oath he despised and whose covenant he broke, with him, in the midst of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, he shall die.

[17] And not with a great force and with a numerous assembly will ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ act with him in the war, when a siege mound is poured out and a siege wall is built, to cut off many souls.

[18] And he despised an oath by breaking a covenant; and behold, he gave his hand, and all these things he did; he shall not escape.

[19] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: As I live, surely my oath that he despised and my covenant that he broke — I will put it on his head.

[20] And I will spread my net over him, and he shall be caught in my snare; and I will bring him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and enter into judgment with him there for his unfaithfulness that he committed against me.

[21] And all his fugitives in all his troops shall fall by the sword, and those who are left shall be spread out to every wind; and you shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken.

[22] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: And I myself will take from the high crown of the cedar and will set it; from the top of its young shoots I will pluck off a tender one, and I myself will plant it on a high and lofty mountain.

[23] On the mountain of the height of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ I will plant it, and it shall bear boughs and make fruit and become a mighty cedar; and under it shall abide every bird of every wing; in the shade of its branches they shall abide.

[24] And all the trees of the field shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have brought low the high tree, have made high the low tree, have dried up the fresh tree and have made the dry tree bud. I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken, and I will do it."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 9, 16, 19, 22) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`). נְאֻם אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה "declares Lord Yahweh" (v16), literally "the declaration of".
- **Layout** — v3 (after "Thus says Lord Yahweh") to v8 are set as lines, one Hebrew colon per line in the Hebrew order; v9–24 stay prose, as the existing layout. The riddle (v3–10) is given word for word; its meaning is only what the chapter itself says in v12–21.
- **"The word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1, 11) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula (one wording); וַ "And" and לֵאמֹר "saying" kept.
- **"put forth a riddle, and speak a parable" → "riddle a riddle and speak a parable"** (v2) — Hebrew חוּד חִידָה, verb and noun of one root (as Judg 14:12); וּמְשֹׁל מָשָׁל, also verb and noun of one root, literally "parable a parable" (מָשָׁל "parable", a saying built on a likeness: the מָשָׁל row). "Son of man" — בֶּן־אָדָם, one wording through Ezekiel.
- **"And say" → "and you shall say"** (v3) — וְאָמַרְתָּ, a weqatal. **"A great eagle with great wings, longwinged, full of feathers, which had divers colours" → "The great eagle, great of wings, long of pinion, full of feathers, which had the many colors"** — הַנֶּשֶׁר הַגָּדוֹל has the article; אֶרֶךְ הָאֵבֶר "long of pinion"; מָלֵא הַנּוֹצָה "full of feathers"; אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ הָרִקְמָה "which had the many colors" (רִקְמָה, the word for variegated work). נֶשֶׁר "eagle" (the word also covers the great vultures: the Lev 11 row). **"came unto Lebanon, and took the highest branch of the cedar" → "came to the Lebanon and took the crown of the cedar"** — הַלְּבָנוֹן with the article; צַמֶּרֶת (only in Ezekiel, 17:3, 22; 31:3–14) "crown" of a tree, its top or tuft (sense not fully certain).
- **"He cropped off the top of his young twigs, and carried it into a land of traffick; he set it in a city of merchants" → "The top of its young shoots he plucked off, and he brought it to a land of Canaan; in a city of traders he set it"** (v4) — the Hebrew order; רֹאשׁ "top" (lit. "head"); יְנִיקוֹת "young shoots" (from יָנַק "suck": shoots that draw sap); אֶרֶץ כְּנַעַן is word for word "a land of Canaan": כְּנַעַן is also the word for "trader" (Hos 12:7; Zeph 1:11), and the line beside it has עִיר רֹכְלִים "a city of traders"; the land meant is shown by v12 (Babylon), as 16:29 calls Chaldea "the land of Canaan". The name is kept; the two senses in Changes.
- **"He took also of the seed of the land, and planted it in a fruitful field" → "And he took of the seed of the land and put it in a field of seed"** (v5) — וַיִּתְּנֵהוּ, נָתַן "give, put"; שְׂדֵה־זָרַע "a field of seed". **"he placed it by great waters, and set it as a willow tree" → "he took it beside many waters, as a willow he set it"** — קָח is found only here; it is commonly taken as a form of לָקַח "take" ("it" supplied); its sense is uncertain. צַפְצָפָה is found only here, "willow" (by a related Arabic word; uncertain); צַפְצָפָה שָׂמוֹ, literally "a willow he set it": "as" supplied. The old verse's "like a willow twig" and its note "the lowliest of the land's plants" (words not in this verse) are removed.
- **"And it grew, and became a spreading vine of low stature, whose branches turned toward him, and the roots thereof were under him" → "And it sprouted and became a spreading vine, low of stature, for its branches to turn toward him, and its roots were to be under him"** (v6) — וַיִּצְמַח "sprouted" (צָמַח "spring up"); סֹרַחַת "spreading" (overhanging); לִפְנוֹת, an infinitive, "for … to turn"; יִהְיוּ, an imperfect in the past: "were to be". תַּחְתָּיו "under him" — the eagle (the masculine suffix). דָּלִיּוֹת "branches", בַּדִּים "shoots" (lit. "parts"), פֹּארוֹת "limbs", עָנָף (v8, 23) "boughs": four words, each kept to one English word through the chapter.
- **"There was also another great eagle with great wings and many feathers" → "And there was one great eagle, great of wings and much of feathers"** (v7) — נֶשֶׁר־אֶחָד, literally "one eagle" ("another" is the sense). **"this vine did bend her roots toward him, and shot forth her branches toward him, that he might water it by the furrows of her plantation" → "this vine bent its roots toward him, and its branches it sent out to him, for him to water it from the beds of its planting"** — כָּפְנָה is found only here ("bent", or "turned in longing"; uncertain); לְהַשְׁקוֹת אוֹתָהּ, an infinitive without a named subject; מֵעֲרֻגוֹת מַטָּעָהּ "from the beds of its planting" (מַטָּע, from נָטַע "plant"), which may also be read "away from the beds".
- **"It was planted in a good soil by great waters" → "To a good field, to many waters it was planted"** (v8) — אֶל … אֶל "to … to"; שָׂדֶה "field"; שְׁתוּלָה, from שָׁתַל "plant, transplant" (also v10, 22, 23). **"that it might bring forth branches … that it might be a goodly vine" → "to make boughs … to become a mighty vine"** — לַעֲשׂוֹת עָנָף; גֶּפֶן אַדֶּרֶת, אַדִּיר "mighty" (the Exod 15 row; also v23 "a mighty cedar").
- **"Shall it prosper? shall he not pull up the roots thereof, and cut off the fruit thereof, that it wither?" → "Shall it prosper? Will he not tear out its roots and strip off its fruit, and it shall wither?"** (v9) — צָלַח "prosper"; נִתֵּק "tear out"; יְקוֹסֵס is found only here ("strip off"; uncertain). **"it shall wither in all the leaves of her spring" → "All the plucked leaves of its sprouting shall wither"** — טַרְפֵּי צִמְחָהּ, טֶרֶף "a freshly plucked leaf" (as Gen 8:11). **"even without great power or many people to pluck it up by the roots thereof" → "and not with a great arm and with a numerous people, to lift it up from its roots"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־בִזְרֹעַ גְּדוֹלָה וּבְעַם־רָב לְמַשְׂאוֹת אוֹתָהּ מִשָּׁרָשֶׁיהָ word for word (it will take no great arm or many people); זְרוֹעַ "arm"; מַשְׂאוֹת, an infinitive of נָשָׂא "lift up".
- **"Yea, behold, being planted, shall it prosper? shall it not utterly wither, when the east wind toucheth it?" → "And behold, it is planted: shall it prosper? Will it not, when the east wind touches it, surely wither?"** (v10) — וְהִנֵּה "And behold"; כְגַעַת בָּהּ "when … touches it" (נָגַע "touch"); תִּיבַשׁ יָבֹשׁ, literally "drying it shall dry": "surely wither". **"it shall wither in the furrows where it grew" → "On the beds of its sprouting it shall wither"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"Say now to the rebellious house, Know ye not what these things mean?" → "Say, please, to the house of rebellion: Do you not know what these are?"** (v12) — אֱמָר־נָא, with נָא "please"; בֵית הַמֶּרִי "the house of rebellion" (מֶרִי "rebellion"); מָה־אֵלֶּה "what these are" ("mean" supplied in the old verse). **"and hath taken the king thereof, and the princes thereof, and led them with him to Babylon" → "and took her king and her princes and brought them to him to Babylon"** — the feminine suffixes: Jerusalem; אֵלָיו "to him".
- **"And hath taken of the king's seed, and made a covenant with him, and hath taken an oath of him" → "And he took of the seed of the kingship and cut a covenant with him and brought him into an oath"** (v13) — זֶרַע הַמְּלוּכָה "the seed of the kingship"; כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant"; וַיָּבֵא אֹתוֹ בְּאָלָה, literally "brought him into an אָלָה": the word is "curse", of a sworn agreement "oath" (an oath that calls a curse on the breaker; also v16, 18, 19). **"he hath also taken the mighty of the land" → "and the leading men of the land he took"** — אֵילֵי הָאָרֶץ, literally "the rams of the land" (as 2 Kgs 24:15 qere).
- **"That the kingdom might be base, that it might not lift itself up, but that by keeping of his covenant it might stand" → "for it to be a low kingdom, so as not to lift itself up, to keep his covenant, so that it should stand"** (v14) — לִהְיוֹת מַמְלָכָה שְׁפָלָה "to be a low kingdom"; לְבִלְתִּי הִתְנַשֵּׂא "so as not to lift itself up"; לְעָמְדָהּ "for it to stand": the feminine suffix may be the kingdom or the covenant (both feminine): open.
- **"But he rebelled against him in sending his ambassadors into Egypt, that they might give him horses and much people" → "And he rebelled against him by sending his messengers to Egypt, to give him horses and a numerous people"** (v15) — מָרַד "rebel"; מַלְאָכִים "messengers"; לָתֶת־לוֹ, an infinitive without a named subject. **"Shall he prosper? shall he escape that doeth such things? or shall he break the covenant, and be delivered?" → "Shall he prosper? Shall he escape who does these things? And he has broken a covenant, and shall he escape?"** — נִמְלַט "escape" (both times: one verb); וְהֵפֵר, a perfect: "he has broken".
- **"surely in the place where the king dwelleth that made him king" → "surely in the place of the king who made him king"** (v16) — the oath אִם־לֹא, literally "if not": "surely" (the oath row); בִּמְקוֹם הַמֶּלֶךְ "in the place of the king": "dwells" was supplied. הִמְלִיךְ "made king" (lit. "made reign"). **"even with him in the midst of Babylon he shall die" → "with him, in the midst of Babylon, he shall die"** — אִתּוֹ בְתוֹךְ־בָּבֶל, the Hebrew order. בָּזָה "despise"; הֵפֵר בְּרִית "break a covenant".
- **"Neither shall Pharaoh with his mighty army and great company make for him in the war" → "And not with a great force and with a numerous assembly will Pharaoh act with him in the war"** (v17) — the Hebrew order; חַיִל "force"; קָהָל "assembly"; יַעֲשֶׂה אוֹתוֹ, literally "will do him": אוֹתוֹ is here taken as "with him" (as often in Ezekiel), "act with" = help him in the war (uncertain). **"by casting up mounts, and building forts, to cut off many persons" → "when a siege mound is poured out and a siege wall is built, to cut off many souls"** — בִּשְׁפֹּךְ סֹלְלָה "by the pouring out of a siege mound"; דָּיֵק "siege wall" (uncertain: the 2 Kgs 25 row); נְפָשׁוֹת רַבּוֹת "many souls" (נֶפֶשׁ). Whose siege is meant is not said: the Babylonians' against Jerusalem.
- **"Seeing he despised the oath by breaking the covenant, when, lo, he had given his hand" → "And he despised an oath by breaking a covenant; and behold, he gave his hand"** (v18) — no articles; וְהִנֵּה "and behold"; נָתַן יָדוֹ "gave his hand" (a pledge). **"and hath done all these things" → "and all these things he did"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"surely mine oath that he hath despised, and my covenant that he hath broken, even it will I recompense upon his own head" → "surely my oath that he despised and my covenant that he broke — I will put it on his head"** (v19) — אִם־לֹא "surely" (the oath); וּנְתַתִּיו בְּרֹאשׁוֹ, literally "and I will give it on his head". The oath and covenant sworn to the king of Babylon are called "my oath", "my covenant": as the Hebrew has it.
- **"And I will spread my net upon him, and he shall be taken in my snare" → "And I will spread my net over him, and he shall be caught in my snare"** (v20) — פָּרַשׂ "spread" (also v21 "spread out"); נִתְפַּשׂ "be caught"; מְצוּדָה "snare" (a hunter's net; of a place "stronghold"). **"and will plead with him there for his trespass that he hath trespassed against me" → "and enter into judgment with him there for his unfaithfulness that he committed against me"** — וְנִשְׁפַּטְתִּי אִתּוֹ "enter into judgment with"; מַעֲלוֹ אֲשֶׁר מָעַל־בִּי, noun and verb of one root, "commit unfaithfulness" (the מָעַל מַעַל row).
- **"And all his fugitives with all his bands" → "And all his fugitives in all his troops"** (v21) — the reading (qere) מִבְרָחָיו "his fugitives"; the written text (ketiv) מברחו may be read as a singular "his flight" or "his fugitive"; the sense is the same. אֲגַפִּים "troops" (an Ezekiel word, 12:14; 38:6–22; 39:4; lit. perhaps "wings" of an army; uncertain). **"they that remain shall be scattered toward all winds" → "those who are left shall be spread out to every wind"** — הַנִּשְׁאָרִים; לְכָל־רוּחַ singular. **"and ye shall know that I the LORD have spoken it" → "and you shall know that I, Yahweh, have spoken"** — כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה דִּבַּרְתִּי: the verb follows the name, so "I, Yahweh, have spoken" (not the formula "that I am Yahweh"; "that I am Yahweh; I have spoken", by the accents, is the other possible reading); "it" was supplied.
- **"I will also take of the highest branch of the high cedar, and will set it" → "And I myself will take from the high crown of the cedar and will set it"** (v22) — וְלָקַחְתִּי אָנִי, "I" stressed; הָרָמָה is feminine and goes with צַמֶּרֶת "crown"; וְנָתָתִּי "and I will set" ("it" supplied: the verb has no object). **"I will crop off from the top of his young twigs a tender one" → "from the top of its young shoots I will pluck off a tender one"** — רַךְ "tender"; קָטַף "pluck off" (as v4). **"and will plant it upon an high mountain and eminent" → "and I myself will plant it on a high and lofty mountain"** — וְשָׁתַלְתִּי אָנִי, "I" stressed again; תָּלוּל is found only here ("lofty"; uncertain).
- **"In the mountain of the height of Israel will I plant it: and it shall bring forth boughs, and bear fruit, and be a goodly cedar" → "On the mountain of the height of Israel I will plant it, and it shall bear boughs and make fruit and become a mighty cedar"** (v23) — נָשָׂא עָנָף "bear boughs" (נָשָׂא, also v8 "bear fruit"); עָשָׂה פְרִי "make fruit"; אֶרֶז אַדִּיר. **"and under it shall dwell all fowl of every wing; in the shadow of the branches thereof shall they dwell" → "and under it shall abide every bird of every wing; in the shade of its branches they shall abide"** — שָׁכַן "abide" (both times); כֹּל צִפּוֹר כָּל־כָּנָף, literally "every bird, every wing"; צֵל "shade". The old verse's "every kind of bird … of every sort" is removed.
- **"And all the trees of the field shall know that I the LORD have brought down the high tree" → "And all the trees of the field shall know that I, Yahweh, have brought low the high tree"** (v24) — perfects, kept as perfects ("have brought low … have made bud"); as in v21 the verb follows the name. לַח "fresh" (moist, full of sap); הִפְרִיחַ "make bud" (פָּרַח "bud"). **"I the LORD have spoken and have done it" → "I, Yahweh, have spoken, and I will do it"** — וְעָשִׂיתִי, a weqatal, "and I will do"; "it" supplied.

### Notes
- **v12–21** explain the riddle in the chapter's own words: the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and took her king and princes (v12; the cedar's crown, v3–4); he took one "of the seed of the kingship" and made a covenant with him (v13–14; the seed planted, v5–6); that one sent to Egypt (v15; the second eagle, v7); he shall die in Babylon, and Pharaoh will not help him (v16–17). The kings are not named in the chapter; 2 Kgs 24:10–20 names them Jehoiachin and Zedekiah.
- **v22–24** — Yahweh himself takes a shoot from the same cedar and plants it on the mountain of Israel; the chapter does not name who is meant (later readers took it of the house of David, cf. Isa 11:1; Jer 23:5).
