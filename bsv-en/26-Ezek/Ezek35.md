# Ezekiel 35

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "Son of man, set your face against Mount Seir, and prophesy against it,

[3] and say to it: Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am against you, Mount Seir, and I will stretch out my hand over you, and make you a desolation and a devastation.

[4] Your cities I will make a ruin, and you shall be a desolation; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] Because you have had an everlasting enmity, and have poured out the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on the hands of the sword in the time of their calamity, in the time of the iniquity of the end,

[6] therefore, as I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, surely for blood I will make you, and blood shall pursue you; since blood you have not hated, blood shall pursue you.

[7] And I will make Mount Seir a desolation and a desolation, and I will cut off from it the one who passes over and the one who returns.

[8] And I will fill its mountains with its slain; your hills and your valleys and all your channels — the slain of the sword shall fall in them.

[9] Everlasting desolations I will make you, and your cities shall not return; and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] Because you said, 'These two nations and these two lands shall be mine, and we will possess it' — and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was there —

[11] therefore, as I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, I will do according to your anger and according to your envy that you have shown out of your hatreds against them; and I will make myself known among them when I judge you.

[12] And you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: I have heard all your spurnings that you have spoken against the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, 'They are desolate; to us they have been given for food.'

[13] And you made yourselves great against me with your mouth, and you multiplied your words against me; I — I have heard.

[14] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: As all the earth rejoices, a desolation I will make you.

[15] As your gladness over the inheritance of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, because it was desolate, so I will do to you: a desolation you shall be, Mount Seir, and all Edom, all of it; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 6, 11, 14) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`).
- **"Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula.
- **"set thy face against mount Seir" → "set your face against Mount Seir"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׂים פָּנֶיךָ עַל, עַל "against" (as 13:17; אֶל "toward" in 25:2); הַר שֵׂעִיר, the hill country of Edom, here addressed as a person ("Mount Seir", v2, 3, 7, 15).
- **"Behold, O mount Seir, I am against thee" → "Behold, I am against you, Mount Seir"** (v3) — Hebrew הִנְנִי אֵלֶיךָ הַר־שֵׂעִיר, in the Hebrew order; no "O" (reviewer row; also v15). **"I will stretch out mine hand against thee" → "I will stretch out my hand over you"** — נָטָה יָד עַל (its row: "stretch out the hand over"). **"and I will make thee most desolate" → "and make you a desolation and a devastation"** — שְׁמָמָה וּמְשַׁמָּה, two nouns of one root (as 33:28).
- **"I will lay thy cities waste, and thou shalt be desolate" → "Your cities I will make a ruin, and you shall be a desolation"** (v4) — Hebrew עָרֶיךָ חָרְבָּה אָשִׂים (חָרְבָּה "ruin", its row), וְאַתָּה שְׁמָמָה תִהְיֶה (שְׁמָמָה "desolation", its row; also v7, 9, 14, 15). **"and thou shalt know that I am the LORD" → "and you shall know that I am Yahweh"** — Ezekiel's formula (here singular; v9 plural "you"; v15 "they").
- **"Because thou hast had a perpetual hatred" → "Because you have had an everlasting enmity"** (v5) — Hebrew הֱיוֹת לְךָ אֵיבַת עוֹלָם: אֵיבָה "enmity" (its row; שִׂנְאָה "hatred" is v11); עוֹלָם as a genitive "everlasting", literally "of the age" (its row; also v9). **"and hast shed the blood of the children of Israel by the force of the sword" → "and have poured out the sons of Israel on the hands of the sword"** — וַתַּגֵּר אֶת־בְּנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל עַל־יְדֵי־חָרֶב, as Jer 18:21 ("blood" is not written; עַל־יְדֵי "on the hands of"). **"in the time of their calamity, in the time that their iniquity had an end" → "in the time of their calamity, in the time of the iniquity of the end"** — אֵיד "calamity" (its row); בְּעֵת עֲוֺן קֵץ, word for word, as 21:25, 29.
- **"I will prepare thee unto blood" → "surely for blood I will make you"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי־לְדָם אֶעֶשְׂךָ: כִּי after "As I live" is "surely"; עָשָׂה "make". **"sith thou hast not hated blood, even blood shall pursue thee" → "since blood you have not hated, blood shall pursue you"** — אִם־לֹא דָם שָׂנֵאתָ וְדָם יִרְדֲּפֶךָ: the line is hard. Taken as the oath form (Standing decisions) it says "surely blood you have hated"; with KJV it is read "since you have not hated blood", which agrees with the sense of the verse and is kept; the Greek is said to read "you have sinned in blood" (from memory). The doubt is reported.
- **"Thus will I make mount Seir most desolate" → "And I will make Mount Seir a desolation and a desolation"** (v7) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי … לְשִׁמְמָה וּשְׁמָמָה: two forms of the one noun שְׁמָמָה, kept as the same word twice. **"him that passeth out and him that returneth" → "the one who passes over and the one who returns"** — עֹבֵר וָשָׁב (עָבַר "pass over", its row).
- **"And I will fill his mountains with his slain men: in thy hills, and in thy valleys, and in all thy rivers" → "And I will fill its mountains with its slain; your hills and your valleys and all your channels"** (v8) — Hebrew הָרָיו חֲלָלָיו "its mountains … its slain" (חָלָל "slain", its row), then "your": the change of person is the Hebrew's; גִּבְעָה "hill", גַּיְא "valley", אָפִיק "channel" (reviewer row); they stand first, "in them" closes the verse.
- **"I will make thee perpetual desolations, and thy cities shall not return" → "Everlasting desolations I will make you, and your cities shall not return"** (v9) — Hebrew שִׁמְמוֹת עוֹלָם אֶתֶּנְךָ; qere תָשֹׁבְנָה "shall return" (shall not be restored); ketiv תישבנה "shall (not) be inhabited". **"and ye shall know" → "and you shall know"** — וִידַעְתֶּם, plural.
- **"and we will possess it; whereas the LORD was there" → "and we will possess it — and Yahweh was there"** (v10) — Hebrew וִירַשְׁנוּהָ (יָרַשׁ "possess", its row; "it", feminine singular, the land), וַיהוָה שָׁם הָיָה, "and"; הָאֲרָצוֹת "the lands".
- **"I will even do according to thine anger, and according to thine envy which thou hast used out of thy hatred against them" → "I will do according to your anger and according to your envy that you have shown out of your hatreds against them"** (v11) — Hebrew וְעָשִׂיתִי כְּאַפְּךָ וּכְקִנְאָתְךָ אֲשֶׁר עָשִׂיתָה מִשִּׂנְאָתֶיךָ בָּם: אַף "anger" (Standing decisions; literally "nose"); קִנְאָה "envy" (of a rival: its row; elsewhere "jealousy"); עָשִׂיתָה, literally "you have done"; שִׂנְאָה plural "hatreds". **"and I will make myself known among them, when I have judged thee" → "and I will make myself known among them when I judge you"** — וְנוֹדַעְתִּי בָם, nifal "I will be known"; כַּאֲשֶׁר אֶשְׁפְּטֶךָ, imperfect.
- **"and that I have heard all thy blasphemies which thou hast spoken" → ": I have heard all your spurnings that you have spoken"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי, no "and that"; נָאָצוֹת "spurnings" (noun of נִאֵץ "spurn", its row; שְׁאָט "contempt" is another word, 36:5); אָמַרְתָּ "you have said". **"They are laid desolate, they are given us to consume" → "They are desolate; to us they have been given for food"** — qere שָׁמֵמוּ "they are desolate", ketiv שממה "a desolation"; לָנוּ נִתְּנוּ לְאָכְלָה, in the Hebrew order (אָכְלָה "food", as 34:5).
- **"Thus with your mouth ye have boasted against me" → "And you made yourselves great against me with your mouth"** (v13) — Hebrew וַתַּגְדִּילוּ עָלַי בְּפִיכֶם, "and", hifil of גָּדַל "make great" (plural). **"and have multiplied your words against me: I have heard them" → "and you multiplied your words against me; I — I have heard"** — הַעְתַּרְתֶּם (עָתַר hifil, "make abundant"); אֲנִי שָׁמָעְתִּי, the pronoun written, no "them".
- **"When the whole earth rejoiceth, I will make thee desolate" → "As all the earth rejoices, a desolation I will make you"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּשְׂמֹחַ כָּל־הָאָרֶץ שְׁמָמָה אֶעֱשֶׂה־לָּךְ: כְּ with the infinitive ("as / when"); שְׁמָמָה "desolation" set first; לָּךְ feminine. How v14 and v15 belong together (the rejoicing of the earth beside Edom's rejoicing) is not clear; the Hebrew is followed.
- **"As thou didst rejoice at the inheritance of the house of Israel" → "As your gladness over the inheritance of the house of Israel"** (v15) — Hebrew כְּשִׂמְחָתְךָ לְנַחֲלַת, the noun שִׂמְחָה "gladness" (its row); נַחֲלָה "inheritance". **"thou shalt be desolate, O mount Seir, and all Idumea, even all of it" → "a desolation you shall be, Mount Seir, and all Edom, all of it"** — שְׁמָמָה תִהְיֶה; אֱדוֹם "Edom" (KJV "Idumea" from the Greek). **"and they shall know" → "and they shall know"** — וְיָדְעוּ.
