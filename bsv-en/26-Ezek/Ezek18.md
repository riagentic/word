# Ezekiel 18

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "What is it to you, that you speak this proverb concerning the ground of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying,

'Fathers eat sour grapes,
and the teeth of the sons are set on edge'?

[3] As I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, you shall no more have occasion to speak this proverb in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] Behold, all the souls are mine; as the soul of the father, so the soul of the son, they are mine: **the soul that sins, it shall die.**

[5] And a man — if he is righteous and does judgment and righteousness:

[6] on the mountains he has not eaten, and his eyes he has not lifted up to the idols of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the wife of his neighbor he has not defiled, and to a woman in her impurity he does not come near;

[7] and a man he does not wrong; his pledge for a debt he returns; robbery he does not commit; his bread he gives to the hungry, and the naked he covers with a garment;

[8] on interest he does not give, and increase he does not take; from injustice he turns back his hand; judgment of truth he does between man and man;

[9] in my statutes he walks, and my ordinances he has kept, to do truth — he is righteous; he shall surely live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[10] And he begets a son, a violent one, a shedder of blood, and he does the like of any one of these;

[11] and he — all these he has not done, for he has even eaten on the mountains, and the wife of his neighbor he has defiled;

[12] the afflicted and the needy he has wronged; robberies he has committed; a pledge he does not return; and to the idols he has lifted up his eyes; abomination he has done;

[13] on interest he has given, and increase he has taken — and shall he live? He shall not live. All these abominations he has done; he shall surely be put to death; his blood shall be on him.

[14] And behold, he begets a son, and he sees all the sins of his father that he has done; and he sees, and he does not do the like:

[15] on the mountains he has not eaten, and his eyes he has not lifted up to the idols of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; the wife of his neighbor he has not defiled;

[16] and a man he has not wronged; a pledge he has not taken in pledge; and robbery he has not committed; his bread he has given to the hungry, and the naked he has covered with a garment;

[17] from the afflicted he has turned back his hand; interest and increase he has not taken; my ordinances he has done; in my statutes he has walked — he shall not die for the iniquity of his father; he shall surely live.

[18] His father — because he committed extortion, committed robbery against a brother, and what is not good he did in the midst of his peoples — behold, he dies in his iniquity.

[19] And you say, 'Why does the son not bear the iniquity of the father?' And the son has done judgment and righteousness; all my statutes he has kept, and he has done them; he shall surely live.

[20] **The soul that sins, it shall die.** A son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, and a father shall not bear the iniquity of the son; the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him.

[21] And the wicked — if he turns back from all his sins that he has done, and keeps all my statutes and does judgment and righteousness, he shall surely live; he shall not die.

[22] All his transgressions that he has done shall not be remembered to him; in his righteousness that he has done he shall live.

[23] Do I surely delight in the death of the wicked, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧? Is it not in his turning back from his ways, so that he lives?

[24] And when a righteous man turns back from his righteousness and does injustice — according to all the abominations that the wicked has done he does — and shall he live? All his righteous deeds that he has done shall not be remembered; for his unfaithfulness that he has committed and for his sin that he has sinned, in them he shall die.

[25] And you say, 'The way of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is not fair.' Hear, please, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Is my way not fair? Is it not your ways that are not fair?

[26] When a righteous man turns back from his righteousness and does injustice and dies for them — for his injustice that he has done he shall die.

[27] And when a wicked man turns back from his wickedness that he has done and does judgment and righteousness, he shall keep his soul alive.

[28] And he sees, and he turns back from all his transgressions that he has done; he shall surely live; he shall not die.

[29] And the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ say, 'The way of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is not fair.' Are my ways not fair, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧? Is it not your ways that are not fair?

[30] Therefore each man according to his ways I will judge you, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧. Turn back, and turn back from all your transgressions, and iniquity shall not be a stumbling block to you.

[31] Cast away from upon you all your transgressions by which you have transgressed, and make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit; and why should you die, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[32] For I do not delight in the death of the one who dies, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧; so turn back, and live."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 9, 23, 30, 32) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`); נְאֻם "declares" (v3, 9, 23, 30, 32), literally "the declaration of". **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v25, 29) — אֲדֹנָי alone (token `adonai`).
- **"The word of the LORD came unto me again, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — the prophets' formula (one wording); "again" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"What mean ye, that ye use this proverb concerning the land of Israel" → "What is it to you, that you speak this proverb concerning the ground of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew מַה־לָּכֶם, literally "what to you"; מֹשְׁלִים אֶת־הַמָּשָׁל, verb and noun of one root ("parable a parable", as 17:2): of a short current saying "proverb" (the מָשָׁל row); אַדְמַת יִשְׂרָאֵל "the ground of Israel" (אֲדָמָה, an Ezekiel phrase). **"The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge" → "Fathers eat sour grapes, and the teeth of the sons are set on edge"** — worded as Jer 31:29, where the Hebrew is the same; the differences are as printed: here יֹאכְלוּ, an imperfect, "eat" (Jer 31:29 the perfect אָכְלוּ "have eaten"), and הַבָּנִים "the sons" with the article. אָבוֹת without the article; בֹּסֶר "sour grapes" (unripe); קָהָה "be set on edge" (lit. "be blunted, dulled").
- **"ye shall not have occasion any more to use this proverb in Israel" → "you shall no more have occasion to speak this proverb in Israel"** (v3) — the oath אִם יִהְיֶה, literally "if there shall be to you any more the speaking of this proverb": the sense is "there shall not be" (the oath row).
- **"Behold, all souls are mine" → "Behold, all the souls are mine"** (v4) — הֵן "behold"; כָּל־הַנְּפָשׁוֹת with the article; כְּנֶפֶשׁ … וּכְנֶפֶשׁ "as … so". **"the soul that sinneth, it shall die" → "the soul that sins, it shall die"** (v4, 20) — הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַחֹטֵאת הִיא תָמוּת: נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (plain word, the reviewer's row; also v27), feminine, with the pronoun הִיא "it" kept.
- **"But if a man be just, and do that which is lawful and right" → "And a man — if he is righteous and does judgment and righteousness"** (v5) — וְאִישׁ כִּי, the man named first; צַדִּיק "righteous"; מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה "judgment and righteousness" (the rows; also v19, 21, 27).
- **Tenses (v6–19)** — the cases are drawn with perfects ("he has not eaten", "he has wronged") beside imperfects ("he does not come near", "he returns"); each is kept as the Hebrew has it. The objects stand first, as in the Hebrew.
- **"hath not eaten upon the mountains, neither hath lifted up his eyes to the idols" → "on the mountains he has not eaten, and his eyes he has not lifted up to the idols"** (v6) — אֶל־הֶהָרִים (v15 עַל) "on the mountains"; גִּלּוּלִים "idols" (a term of contempt). **"neither hath defiled his neighbour's wife, neither hath come near to a menstruous woman" → "and the wife of his neighbor he has not defiled, and to a woman in her impurity he does not come near"** — טִמֵּא "defile"; אִשָּׁה נִדָּה "a woman of impurity" (נִדָּה, the impurity of the monthly flow); קָרַב "come near".
- **"And hath not oppressed any, but hath restored to the debtor his pledge, hath spoiled none by violence" → "and a man he does not wrong; his pledge for a debt he returns; robbery he does not commit"** (v7) — אִישׁ "a man"; הוֹנָה "wrong" (also v12, 16); חֲבֹלָתוֹ חוֹב "his pledge of debt" (חוֹב is found only here: "debt"; KJV took it as "the debtor"); הֵשִׁיב "return"; גְּזֵלָה לֹא יִגְזֹל, literally "a robbery he does not rob" (also v12, 16, 18). **"hath given his bread to the hungry, and hath covered the naked with a garment"** — עֵירֹם "naked"; בֶּגֶד "garment".
- **"He that hath not given forth upon usury, neither hath taken any increase" → "on interest he does not give, and increase he does not take"** (v8) — נֶשֶׁךְ "interest" (lit. "a bite"); תַּרְבִּית "increase" (also v13, 17). **"withdrawn his hand from iniquity" → "from injustice he turns back his hand"** — עָוֶל "injustice" (also v24, 26), not עָוֹן "iniquity"; יָשִׁיב יָדוֹ "turns back his hand". **"executed true judgment" → "judgment of truth he does"** — מִשְׁפַּט אֱמֶת.
- **"Hath walked in my statutes, and hath kept my judgments, to deal truly" → "in my statutes he walks, and my ordinances he has kept, to do truth"** (v9) — חֻקּוֹת "statutes" (the חֻקָּה row); מִשְׁפָּטִים of Yahweh's rules "ordinances" (as Deut "statutes and ordinances"; also v17); לַעֲשׂוֹת אֱמֶת "to do truth". **"he shall surely live"** — חָיֹה יִחְיֶה, literally "living he shall live" (also v17, 19, 21, 28).
- **"If he beget a son that is a robber, a shedder of blood, and that doeth the like to any one of these things" → "And he begets a son, a violent one, a shedder of blood, and he does the like of any one of these"** (v10) — הוֹלִיד "beget"; פָּרִיץ "violent one"; וְעָשָׂה אָח מֵאַחַד מֵאֵלֶּה, literally "and does a brother from one of these": אָח is taken as "the like" (uncertain; often read as a slip for אַךְ "only"); whether "these" are the good deeds or the evil ones is debated.
- **"And that doeth not any of those duties, but even hath eaten upon the mountains" → "and he — all these he has not done, for he has even eaten on the mountains"** (v11) — וְהוּא אֶת־כָּל־אֵלֶּה לֹא עָשָׂה: "he" may be the son (who has not done the good deeds) or the father (who did none of the evil); "duties" was supplied; כִּי גַם "for … even".
- **"Hath oppressed the poor and needy" → "the afflicted and the needy he has wronged"** (v12) — עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן "afflicted and needy". **"hath committed abomination" → "abomination he has done"** — תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination".
- **"shall he then live? he shall not live" → "and shall he live? He shall not live"** (v13) — וָחָי, a question without a question word (also v24). **"he shall surely die; his blood shall be upon him" → "he shall surely be put to death; his blood shall be on him"** — מוֹת יוּמָת, literally "dying he shall be made to die" (the row).
- **"Now, lo, if he beget a son, that seeth all his father's sins which he hath done, and considereth, and doeth not such like" → "And behold, he begets a son, and he sees all the sins of his father that he has done; and he sees, and he does not do the like"** (v14) — וְהִנֵּה "And behold"; וַיַּרְא … וַיִּרְאֶה, one verb רָאָה twice (also v28), "considers" is the sense; כָּהֵן "like them".
- **"hath not taken to pledge" → "a pledge he has not taken in pledge"** (v16) — חֲבֹל לֹא חָבָל, noun and verb of one root ("take in pledge").
- **"That hath taken off his hand from the poor" → "from the afflicted he has turned back his hand"** (v17) — מֵעָנִי, the Masoretic text, "from the afflicted" (from oppressing him); v8 has מֵעָוֶל "from injustice", and the Greek has that here (from memory): the MT kept. **"he shall not die for the iniquity of his father"** — בַּעֲוֹן "in / for the iniquity".
- **"As for his father, because he cruelly oppressed, spoiled his brother by violence" → "His father — because he committed extortion, committed robbery against a brother"** (v18) — עָשַׁק עֹשֶׁק "extort extortion", גָּזַל גֵּזֶל אָח "rob a robbery of a brother": verb and noun of one root each; אָח "a brother", no "his". **"among his people" → "in the midst of his peoples"** — עַמָּיו plural (his kin). **"lo, even he shall die in his iniquity" → "behold, he dies in his iniquity"** — וְהִנֵּה־מֵת, a participle.
- **"Yet say ye, Why? doth not the son bear the iniquity of the father?" → "And you say, 'Why does the son not bear the iniquity of the father?'"** (v19) — מַדּוּעַ לֹא־נָשָׂא, one question; נָשָׂא בַּעֲוֹן "bear the iniquity" (the עָוֹן … נָשָׂא row; also v20). **"When the son hath done that which is lawful and right, and hath kept all my statutes, and hath done them" → "And the son has done judgment and righteousness; all my statutes he has kept, and he has done them"** — no "when"; וַיַּעֲשֶׂה אֹתָם "and he has done them".
- **"The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, neither shall the father bear the iniquity of the son" → "A son shall not bear … and a father shall not bear …"** (v20) — בֵּן … אָב without the article. **"the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him"** — the reading (qere) הָרָשָׁע "the wicked"; the written text (ketiv) רשע without the article: the same sense. עָלָיו "upon him" (twice).
- **"But if the wicked will turn from all his sins" → "And the wicked — if he turns back from all his sins"** (v21) — שׁוּב "turn back" (the row; "repent" is not the word; also v23, 24, 26, 27, 28, 30, 32); the reading (qere) חַטֹּאתָיו "his sins"; the written text (ketiv) חטאתו "his sin".
- **"they shall not be mentioned unto him: in his righteousness that he hath done he shall live" → "shall not be remembered to him; in his righteousness that he has done he shall live"** (v22) — לֹא יִזָּכְרוּ לוֹ, זָכַר "remember"; פְּשָׁעִים "transgressions" (also v28, 30, 31).
- **"Have I any pleasure at all that the wicked should die? … and not that he should return from his ways, and live?" → "Do I surely delight in the death of the wicked …? Is it not in his turning back from his ways, so that he lives?"** (v23) — הֶחָפֹץ אֶחְפֹּץ, literally "delighting do I delight" (the infinitive absolute: "surely"); חָפֵץ "delight" (also v32); בְּשׁוּבוֹ "in his turning back", וְחָיָה "and he lives".
- **"But when the righteous turneth away from his righteousness, and committeth iniquity, and doeth according to all the abominations that the wicked man doeth, shall he live?" → "And when a righteous man turns back from his righteousness and does injustice — according to all the abominations that the wicked has done he does — and shall he live?"** (v24) — עָוֶל "injustice"; the Hebrew order. **"All his righteousness that he hath done shall not be mentioned" → "All his righteous deeds that he has done shall not be remembered"** — the reading (qere) צִדְקֹתָיו, a plural: "righteous deeds"; the written text (ketiv) צדקתו "his righteousness". **"in his trespass that he hath trespassed" → "for his unfaithfulness that he has committed"** — מַעַל … מָעַל (the "commit unfaithfulness" row); חַטָּאת … חָטָא "sin … sinned"; בָּם "in them".
- **"The way of the Lord is not equal" → "The way of Adonai is not fair"** (v25, 29) — לֹא יִתָּכֵן, nifal of תָּכַן "measure, weigh out" (the תכן row): "is not weighed fairly"; "fair" in all six places. **"Hear now, O house of Israel" → "Hear, please, house of Israel"** — שִׁמְעוּ־נָא; no "O" (the reviewer's vocative row; also v29, 30, 31).
- **"and dieth in them" → "and dies for them"** (v26) — וּמֵת עֲלֵיהֶם, literally "and dies upon them" (on account of them).
- **"he shall save his soul alive" → "he shall keep his soul alive"** (v27) — הוּא אֶת־נַפְשׁוֹ יְחַיֶּה, piel of חָיָה (the "let live" row).
- **"Because he considereth, and turneth away" → "And he sees, and he turns back"** (v28) — וַיִּרְאֶה as v14; the reading (qere) וַיָּשָׁב "and he turns back", the written text (ketiv) וישוב "and he will turn back": the same sense. חָיוֹ יִחְיֶה, the infinitive written with ו.
- **"Yet saith the house of Israel" → "And the house of Israel say"** (v29) — וְאָמְרוּ plural. **"are not my ways equal? are not your ways unequal?"** — הַדְּרָכַי plural with a plural verb, then דַרְכֵיכֶם plural with a singular verb: "fair" in both.
- **"Therefore I will judge you … every one according to his ways" → "Therefore each man according to his ways I will judge you"** (v30) — the Hebrew order (the אִישׁ row). **"Repent, and turn yourselves from all your transgressions" → "Turn back, and turn back from all your transgressions"** — שׁוּבוּ וְהָשִׁיבוּ, qal and hifil of שׁוּב without an object: "turn back, and turn (yourselves / others) back"; "repent" is not the word. **"so iniquity shall not be your ruin" → "and iniquity shall not be a stumbling block to you"** — מִכְשׁוֹל "stumbling block" (as Ezek 3:20; 7:19; 14:3).
- **"Cast away from you all your transgressions, whereby ye have transgressed" → "Cast away from upon you all your transgressions by which you have transgressed"** (v31) — הִשְׁלִיךְ "cast away" (the "throw" row: "cast" of one's sins); מֵעֲלֵיכֶם "from upon you"; פְּשַׁעְתֶּם בָּם, verb and noun of one root. **"make you a new heart and a new spirit: for why will ye die" → "make for yourselves a new heart and a new spirit; and why should you die"** — לָכֶם "for yourselves"; לֵב "heart", רוּחַ "spirit"; וְלָמָּה "and why".
- **"For I have no pleasure in the death of him that dieth" → "For I do not delight in the death of the one who dies"** (v32) — הַמֵּת, a participle. **"wherefore turn yourselves, and live ye" → "so turn back, and live"** — וְהָשִׁיבוּ, the hifil as v30 (no object); וִחְיוּ "and live".

### Notes
- **v2** — the same proverb stands in Jer 31:29–30, with "eat" as a perfect; both prophets answer it.
- **v4, 20** — "the soul that sins, it shall die": the chapter sets each soul — father, son — before Yahweh on its own way (v5–20), and the wicked or the righteous who turns back (v21–28).
