# Ezekiel 42

[1] And he brought me out to the outer court, by the way, the way toward the north; and he brought me to the chamber that was opposite the separate place and that was opposite the building, toward the north.

[2] Facing the length of the hundred cubits was the entrance of the north; and the width was fifty cubits.

[3] Opposite the twenty that belonged to the inner court, and opposite the pavement that belonged to the outer court, was gallery facing gallery, in three stories.

[4] And before the chambers was a passage of ten cubits in width, to the inner side, a way of one cubit; and their entrances were toward the north.

[5] And the upper chambers were short, for galleries took away from them, more than from the lower and from the middle ones of the building.

[6] For they were in three stories, and they had no pillars like the pillars of the courts; therefore it was set back, more than the lower and the middle ones, from the ground.

[7] And the fence that was outside, alongside the chambers, toward the outer court, facing the chambers: its length was fifty cubits.

[8] For the length of the chambers that belonged to the outer court was fifty cubits; and behold, facing the temple, a hundred cubits.

[9] And below these chambers was what leads in from the east, as one comes into them from the outer court.

[10] In the width of the fence of the court toward the east, facing the separate place and facing the building, were chambers,

[11] and a way before them, like the appearance of the chambers that were toward the north; as their length, so their width, and all their goings out, and as their ordinances, and as their entrances.

[12] And as the entrances of the chambers that were toward the south was an entrance at the head of a way, the way facing the corresponding fence toward the east, as one comes into them.

[13] And he said to me, "The chambers of the north and the chambers of the south that are facing the separate place, they are the holy chambers, where the priests who are near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall eat the most holy things. There they shall lay up the most holy things, and the grain offering, and the sin offering, and the guilt offering; for the place is holy.

[14] When the priests come in, they shall not go out from the Holy to the outer court; and there they shall lay up their garments in which they minister, for they are holy; and they shall put on other garments and come near to what is for the people."

[15] And he finished the measures of the inner house, and he brought me out by the way of the gate whose face was toward the east, and he measured it all around, all around.

[16] He measured the east side with the measuring reed: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed, around.

[17] He measured the north side: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed, around.

[18] The south side he measured: five hundred reeds by the measuring reed.

[19] He turned to the west side; he measured five hundred reeds by the measuring reed.

[20] On the four sides he measured it. It had a wall all around, all around: the length five hundred and the width five hundred, to separate the holy from the common.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v13) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Building words** (throughout) — as Ezek 41 and 1 Kgs 6–7: לִשְׁכָּה "chamber", הַגִּזְרָה "the separate place" (41:12), בִּנְיָן "building", אַתִּיק "gallery" (uncertain, v3, 5), הֵיכָל "temple", פֶּתַח "entrance", חָצֵר "court". Many details of the plan are uncertain; the plainest sense is given and nothing is filled in from the Septuagint.
- **"the way toward the north" → "by the way, the way toward the north"** (v1) — Hebrew הַדֶּרֶךְ דֶּרֶךְ הַצָּפוֹן, the word repeated. **"the chamber"** — הַלִּשְׁכָּה, singular (a row of chambers is meant, v4). **"which was before the building" → "that was opposite the building"** — נֶגֶד twice.
- **"Before the length of an hundred cubits was the north door" → "Facing the length of the hundred cubits was the entrance of the north"** (v2) — Hebrew אֶל־פְּנֵי־אֹרֶךְ אַמּוֹת הַמֵּאָה פֶּתַח הַצָּפוֹן, word for word; the sentence has no verb. "The building whose door faced north" was an explanation.
- **"the twenty cubits" → "the twenty"** (v3) — Hebrew הָעֶשְׂרִים, without "cubits" (the twenty cubits of 41:10 are commonly meant). **"was gallery against gallery in three stories" → "was gallery facing gallery, in three stories"** — אַתִּיק אֶל־פְּנֵי־אַתִּיק בַּשְּׁלִשִׁים, literally "in the thirds" (v6 מְשֻׁלָּשׁוֹת "threefold").
- **"a walk of ten cubits breadth inward, a way of one cubit" → "a passage of ten cubits in width, to the inner side, a way of one cubit"** (v4) — Hebrew מַהֲלַךְ עֶשֶׂר אַמּוֹת רֹחַב אֶל־הַפְּנִימִית דֶּרֶךְ אַמָּה אֶחָת, as the Masoretic Text has it. The former text had "a hundred cubits long", which is the Septuagint's reading (from memory); not followed. **"their doors" → "their entrances"** — פִּתְחֵיהֶם.
- **"the upper chambers were shorter" → "the upper chambers were short"** (v5) — קְצֻרוֹת. **"for the galleries were higher than these" → "for galleries took away from them"** — Hebrew כִּי־יוֹכְלוּ אַתִּיקִים מֵהֵנָה: יוֹכְלוּ is commonly taken from אָכַל "eat, consume" ("took away from"); the form is uncertain. "More than" is the comparative מִן before "the lower" and "the middle".
- **"in three stories" kept** (v6) — מְשֻׁלָּשׁוֹת, literally "made threefold". **"therefore the building was straitened" → "therefore it was set back"** — Hebrew נֶאֱצַל, nifal of אָצַל "set aside, keep back"; "the building" is not in the Hebrew (no subject). **"from the ground"** — מֵהָאָרֶץ.
- **"the wall that was without" → "the fence that was outside"** (v7) — Hebrew גָּדֵר, a fence or low wall (as Num 22:24), not קִיר "wall" (also v10, 12 גֶּדֶר / גְּדֶרֶת). **"over against the chambers" → "alongside the chambers"** — לְעֻמַּת. **"on the forepart of the chambers" → "facing the chambers"** — אֶל־פְּנֵי הַלְּשָׁכוֹת.
- **"and, lo, before the temple were an hundred cubits" → "and behold, facing the temple, a hundred cubits"** (v8) — וְהִנֵּה עַל־פְּנֵי הַהֵיכָל מֵאָה אַמָּה; הִנֵּה "behold" kept; "nave" was the former text's word for הֵיכָל.
- **"And from under these chambers was the entry" → "And below these chambers was what leads in"** (v9) — qere וּמִתַּחַת הַלְּשָׁכוֹת הָאֵלֶּה הַמֵּבִיא; the written form is ומתחתה לשכות … המבוא "and below it chambers … the entry". The qere הַמֵּבִיא is the participle "that which brings in". **"on the east side" → "from the east"** — מֵהַקָּדִים. **"as one goeth into them"** — בְּבֹאוֹ לָהֵנָּה, literally "at his coming to them".
- **"in the thickness of the wall of the court toward the east" → "In the width of the fence of the court toward the east"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּרֹחַב גֶּדֶר הֶחָצֵר דֶּרֶךְ הַקָּדִים: the Masoretic Text has "east"; the former text had "south", which the Septuagint reads (from memory) and which v12–13 suggest. The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"was like the appearance of the chambers" → "like the appearance of the chambers"** (v11) — וְדֶרֶךְ לִפְנֵיהֶם כְּמַרְאֵה הַלְּשָׁכוֹת; no verb. **"as long as they, and as broad as they" → "as their length, so their width"** — כְּאָרְכָּן כֵּן רָחְבָּן. **"all their goings out were both according to their fashions" → "and all their goings out, and as their ordinances"** — מוֹצָאֵיהֶן "goings out" (מוֹצָא); מִשְׁפְּטֵיהֶן "their ordinances" (מִשְׁפָּט of a building, as 1 Kgs 6:38).
- **"even the way directly before the wall" → "the way facing the corresponding fence"** (v12) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ בִּפְנֵי הַגְּדֶרֶת הֲגִינָה: הֲגִינָה is found only here; "corresponding, fitting" is a guess, the sense is uncertain. **"as one entereth into them" → "as one comes into them"** — בְּבוֹאָן, literally "at their coming in".
- **"they be holy chambers" → "they are the holy chambers"** (v13) — לִשְׁכוֹת הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "chambers of holiness". **"that approach unto the LORD" → "who are near to Yahweh"** — אֲשֶׁר־קְרוֹבִים לַיהוָה, the adjective קָרוֹב "near". **"the most holy things"** — קָדְשֵׁי הַקֳּדָשִׁים, literally "the holy things of the holy things" (twice). **"there shall they lay" → "There they shall lay up"** — יַנִּיחוּ, hifil of נוּחַ "lay up" (also v14). **"the meat offering … the trespass offering" → "the grain offering … the guilt offering"** — מִנְחָה, אָשָׁם.
- **"When the priests enter therein" → "When the priests come in"** (v14) — בְּבֹאָם הַכֹּהֲנִים; "therein" is not in the Hebrew. **"out of the holy place" → "from the Holy"** — מֵהַקֹּדֶשׁ. **"wherein they minister"** — אֲשֶׁר־יְשָׁרְתוּ בָהֶן (שֵׁרֵת "minister"). **"for they are holy"** — כִּי־קֹדֶשׁ הֵנָּה, literally "for they are holiness". **"and shall put on"** — qere וְלָבְשׁוּ; the written form ילבשו "they shall put on" says the same. **"and shall approach to those things" → "and come near to what is"** — וְקָרְבוּ אֶל־אֲשֶׁר לָעָם.
- **"Now when he had made an end of measuring the inner house" → "And he finished the measures of the inner house"** (v15) — וְכִלָּה אֶת־מִדּוֹת הַבַּיִת הַפְּנִימִי. **"whose prospect is toward the east" → "whose face was toward the east"** — אֲשֶׁר פָּנָיו דֶּרֶךְ הַקָּדִים. **"round about" → "all around, all around"** — וּמְדָדוֹ סָבִיב סָבִיב: "it" is the whole area; סָבִיב סָבִיב is kept double (also v20), the single סָבִיב "around" (v16, 17).
- **"side"** (v16–20) — Hebrew רוּחַ, literally "wind": the four winds are the four quarters. **"the west side"** (v19) — רוּחַ הַיָּם, literally "the sea wind": the sea is the west.
- **"five hundred reeds"** (v16) — qere חֲמֵשׁ־מֵאוֹת קָנִים; the written form is חמש אמות קנים "five cubits, reeds". The former text had "500 cubits" in v16–20; the Hebrew has "reeds" in v16–19 (a reed is six long cubits, 40:5) and no unit in v20. Numbers are written in words.
- **"to make a separation between the sanctuary and the profane place" → "to separate the holy from the common"** (v20) — Hebrew לְהַבְדִּיל בֵּין הַקֹּדֶשׁ לְחֹל, literally "to separate between the holy and the common": חֹל "common", what is not holy (as 44:23; 22:26). **"five hundred broad" → "the width five hundred"** — no unit in the Hebrew.

### Notes
- **The priests' chambers** (v1–14) — two blocks of chambers north and south of the separate place, in three stories, where the priests eat the most holy things and leave their garments (as 44:19).
- **The square** (v15–20) — five hundred by five hundred reeds around the whole; the wall separates the holy from the common.
