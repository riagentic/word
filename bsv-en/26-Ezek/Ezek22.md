# Ezekiel 22

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "And you, son of man, will you judge, will you judge the city of bloodguilt? Then make her know all her abominations.

[3] And you shall say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: A city that sheds blood in her midst, that her time may come, and that has made idols upon herself, to become unclean!

[4] By your blood that you have shed you have become guilty, and by your idols that you have made you have become unclean; and you have brought your days near, and you have come to your years. Therefore I have given you as a reproach to the nations and a mocking to all the lands.

[5] Those near and those far from you shall mock at you, unclean of name, great in confusion.

[6] Behold, the chieftains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, each man according to his arm, have been in you, in order to shed blood.

[7] Father and mother they have made light of in you; to the sojourner they have dealt with extortion in your midst; fatherless and widow they have wronged in you.

[8] My holy things you have despised, and my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ you have profaned.

[9] Men of talebearing have been in you, in order to shed blood; and on the mountains they have eaten in you; depravity they have done in your midst.

[10] The nakedness of a father one has uncovered in you; her who is unclean in her impurity they have humbled in you.

[11] And a man has done abomination with the wife of his neighbor; and a man has defiled his daughter-in-law with depravity; and a man has humbled his sister, the daughter of his father, in you.

[12] A bribe they have taken in you, in order to shed blood; interest and increase you have taken, and you have made unjust gain of your neighbors by extortion; and me you have forgotten, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[13] "And behold, I have struck my palm at your unjust gain that you have made, and at your blood that has been in your midst.

[14] Will your heart stand, or will your hands be strong, in the days when I am dealing with you? I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken, and I will do it.

[15] And I will scatter you among the nations, and I will strew you in the lands, and I will bring your uncleanness to an end from you.

[16] And you shall be profaned in yourself before the eyes of nations, and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[17] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[18] "Son of man, the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has become dross to me; all of them are bronze and tin and iron and lead in the midst of a furnace; they have become the dross of silver.

[19] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you have all become dross, therefore, behold, I am gathering you into the midst of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[20] As a gathering of silver and bronze and iron and lead and tin into the midst of a furnace, to blow fire on it, to melt it, so I will gather in my anger and in my fury, and I will set you down and melt you.

[21] And I will gather you in, and I will blow on you with the fire of my raging, and you shall be melted in her midst.

[22] As silver is melted in the midst of a furnace, so you shall be melted in her midst; and you shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have poured out my fury on you."

[23] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[24] "Son of man, say to her, You are a land that is not cleansed; it has no rain in the day of indignation.

[25] A conspiracy of her prophets is in her midst, like a roaring lion tearing prey; soul they have devoured; store and precious things they take; her widows they have multiplied in her midst.

[26] Her priests have done violence to my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, and they have profaned my holy things; between holy and common they have not separated, and between the unclean and the clean they have not made known; and from my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ they have hidden their eyes, and I am profaned in their midst.

[27] Her princes within her are like wolves tearing prey, to shed blood, to do away with souls, in order to gain unjust gain.

[28] And her prophets have plastered for them with whitewash, beholding emptiness and divining a lie for them, saying, 'Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧,' and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not spoken.

[29] The people of the land have committed extortion and committed robbery, and the afflicted and the needy they have wronged, and from the sojourner they have extorted without judgment.

[30] "And **I sought from among them a man building a fence and standing in the breach before me on behalf of the land, so that I should not ruin it; and I found none**.

[31] And I have poured out on them my indignation; with the fire of my raging I have made an end of them; their way upon their head I have laid, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3, 12, 19, 28, 31) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`). נְאֻם "declares" (v12, 31).
- **"Moreover the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying,"** (v1, 17, 23) — the prophets' formula, לֵאמֹר kept.
- **"wilt thou judge, wilt thou judge the bloody city? yea, thou shalt shew her" → "will you judge, will you judge the city of bloodguilt? Then make her know"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲתִשְׁפֹּט הֲתִשְׁפֹּט אֶת־עִיר הַדָּמִים וְהוֹדַעְתָּהּ: the question twice, as printed; דָּמִים, literally "bloods" (the plural of blood shed and its guilt: the דָּמִים row, as 7:23; 24:6, 9); הוֹדִיעַ "make know" (hifil of יָדַע; also v26).
- **"The city sheddeth blood in the midst of it, that her time may come, and maketh idols against herself to defile herself" → "A city that sheds blood in her midst, that her time may come, and that has made idols upon herself, to become unclean!"** (v3) — עִיר שֹׁפֶכֶת … וְעָשְׂתָה גִלּוּלִים עָלֶיהָ לְטָמְאָה: a participle, then a perfect; עָלֶיהָ "upon herself"; לְטָמְאָה qal infinitive "to become unclean" (the טָמֵא qal row; also v4 טָמֵאת). גִּלּוּלִים "idols" (its row; also v4).
- **"Thou art become guilty in thy blood … and hast defiled thyself in thine idols" → "By your blood that you have shed you have become guilty, and by your idols that you have made you have become unclean"** (v4) — אָשַׁמְתְּ (the אָשֵׁם row), טָמֵאת (qal). **"and art come even unto thy years" → "and you have come to your years"** — וַתָּבוֹא עַד־שְׁנוֹתָיִךְ; the earlier "the appointed time of your years has come" adds and changes the subject. **"therefore have I made thee a reproach unto the heathen, and a mocking to all countries" → "Therefore I have given you as a reproach to the nations and a mocking to all the lands"** — נְתַתִּיךְ, literally "I have given you"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (its row), קַלָּסָה "mocking"; אֲרָצוֹת "lands" (also v15).
- **"which art infamous and much vexed" → "unclean of name, great in confusion"** (v5) — טְמֵאַת הַשֵּׁם רַבַּת הַמְּהוּמָה, word for word; מְהוּמָה "confusion" (its row). The near and far are feminine (the lands).
- **"every one were in thee to their power to shed blood" → "each man according to his arm, have been in you, in order to shed blood"** (v6) — אִישׁ לִזְרֹעוֹ, זְרוֹעַ "arm" kept (strength); לְמַעַן "in order to" (also v9, 12, 27). **"princes" → "chieftains"** — נָשִׂיא (its row, as 21:12); in v27 שָׂרִים "princes" (the שַׂר row): two words kept apart.
- **"In thee have they set light by father and mother" → "Father and mother they have made light of in you"** (v7) — הֵקַלּוּ, hifil of קָלַל "be light" (Deut 27:16); the Hebrew order. **"the stranger" → "the sojourner"** (v7, 29) — גֵּר (its row); **"dealt by oppression" → "dealt with extortion"** — עָשׂוּ בַעֹשֶׁק, עֹשֶׁק "extortion" (its row; also v12, 29). **"vexed the fatherless and the widow" → "fatherless and widow they have wronged"** — הוֹנוּ (the הוֹנָה row; also v29).
- **"Thou hast despised mine holy things" → "My holy things you have despised"** (v8; also v26) — קָדָשַׁי, the objects first as printed; חִלֵּל "profane" (its row; also v26).
- **"In thee are men that carry tales to shed blood" → "Men of talebearing have been in you, in order to shed blood"** (v9) — אַנְשֵׁי רָכִיל (the רָכִיל row). **"in thee they eat upon the mountains" → "on the mountains they have eaten in you"** — וְאֶל־הֶהָרִים אָכְלוּ, at the high places (as 18:6, 11; 18:15 has עַל); אֶל "to, at". **"lewdness" → "depravity"** (v9, 11) — זִמָּה (its row).
- **"In thee have they discovered their fathers' nakedness" → "The nakedness of a father one has uncovered in you"** (v10) — עֶרְוַת־אָב גִּלָּה, singular verb, no "their" (the עֶרְוָה · גִּלָּה row). **"in thee have they humbled her that was set apart for pollution" → "her who is unclean in her impurity they have humbled in you"** — טְמֵאַת הַנִּדָּה, literally "the unclean of the impurity" (the נִדָּה row: the monthly flow, as 18:6; Lev 18:19); עִנּוּ "humbled" (the reviewer's row for Ezek 22:10–11; the earlier "violate" is replaced: the word does not say force).
- **"And one hath committed abomination with his neighbour's wife; and another hath lewdly defiled his daughter in law; and another in thee hath humbled his sister" → "And a man has done abomination … and a man has defiled … with depravity; and a man has humbled his sister … in you"** (v11) — וְאִישׁ three times, "a man" each time (not "one … another"); עָשָׂה תּוֹעֵבָה "done abomination"; טִמֵּא "defiled" (its row); בְזִמָּה "with depravity"; כַּלָּה "daughter-in-law" (its row); עִנָּה "humbled" (the reviewer row).
- **"In thee have they taken gifts" → "A bribe they have taken in you"** (v12) — שֹׁחַד "bribe" (its row), singular. **"thou hast taken usury and increase" → "interest and increase you have taken"** — נֶשֶׁךְ "interest" (literally "a bite") וְתַרְבִּית "increase" (the rows, as 18:8, 13). **"thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by extortion" → "you have made unjust gain of your neighbors by extortion"** — וַתְּבַצְּעִי, piel of the root of בֶּצַע "unjust gain" (its row; also v13, 27). **"and hast forgotten me" → "and me you have forgotten"** — וְאֹתִי שָׁכַחַתְּ, "me" first.
- **"Behold, therefore I have smitten mine hand at thy dishonest gain" → "And behold, I have struck my palm at your unjust gain"** (v13) — וְהִנֵּה הִכֵּיתִי כַפִּי, a perfect; כַּף "palm" (as 21:14, 17). **"thy blood which hath been in the midst of thee" → "your blood that has been in your midst"** — דָּמֵךְ singular with the plural verb הָיוּ, as printed.
- **"Can thine heart endure" → "Will your heart stand"** (v14) — הֲיַעֲמֹד לִבֵּךְ, עָמַד "stand"; לֵב "heart" (the earlier "courage" interprets). **"in the days that I shall deal with thee" → "in the days when I am dealing with you"** — עֹשֶׂה, a participle. **"I the LORD have spoken it, and will do it" → "I, Yahweh, have spoken, and I will do it"** — אֲנִי יְהוָה דִּבַּרְתִּי וְעָשִׂיתִי, as 17:24 (the Ezekiel row: no "it" after "spoken"; by the accents also "I am Yahweh; I have spoken").
- **"and disperse thee in the countries" → "and I will strew you in the lands"** (v15) — זֵרִיתִיךְ, זָרָה "strew" (its row), beside הֵפִיץ "scatter". **"and will consume thy filthiness out of thee" → "and I will bring your uncleanness to an end from you"** — הֲתִמֹּתִי, hifil of תָּמַם (the הֵתֵם row "bring to an end"); טֻמְאָה "uncleanness" (its row).
- **"And thou shalt take thine inheritance in thyself in the sight of the heathen" → "And you shall be profaned in yourself before the eyes of nations"** (v16) — Hebrew וְנִחַלְתְּ בָּךְ: the pointing is the nifal of חָלַל "be profaned" (as v26 וָאֵחַל, "I am profaned"); KJV takes it from נָחַל "inherit". לְעֵינֵי גוֹיִם, "nations" without the article.
- **"is to me become dross" → "has become dross to me"** (v18) — the reading (qere) לְסִיג; the written text (ketiv) לסוג, the same word in another spelling. **"they are even the dross of silver" → "they have become the dross of silver"** — סִגִים כֶּסֶף הָיוּ. **"furnace"** (v18, 20, 22) — כּוּר, the smelting furnace (its row). בְּדִיל "tin", עוֹפֶרֶת "lead" (its row).
- **"behold, therefore I will gather you" → "behold, I am gathering you"** (v19) — הִנְנִי קֹבֵץ, a participle.
- **"As they gather silver … to blow the fire upon it, to melt it; so will I gather you in mine anger and in my fury" → "As a gathering of silver … to blow fire on it, to melt it, so I will gather in my anger and in my fury"** (v20) — קְבֻצַת, a noun "a gathering"; אֶקְבֹּץ has no object in the Hebrew ("you" stands only after the last verb); אַף "anger", חֵמָה "fury" (the rows; also v22). **"and I will leave you there, and melt you" → "and I will set you down and melt you"** — וְהִנַּחְתִּי, the hifil of נוּחַ with doubled נ (the הִנִּיחַ row: "set down / leave"); הִתִּיךְ "melt" (verb of נָתַךְ; also v21, 22).
- **"Yea, I will gather you" → "And I will gather you in"** (v21) — כִּנַּסְתִּי, כִּנֵּס beside קָבַץ "gather" (English has one word: "gather in" for this one). **"in the fire of my wrath" → "with the fire of my raging"** (v21, 31) — עֶבְרָה "raging" (its row, as 21:31). **"in the midst thereof" → "in her midst"** (v21, 22) — בְּתוֹכָהּ, feminine: the city, not the furnace (כּוּר is masculine).
- **"and ye shall know that I the LORD have poured out my fury upon you" → "and you shall know that I, Yahweh, have poured out my fury on you"** (v22) — כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה שָׁפַכְתִּי: "I, Yahweh" is the subject of the verb (as 21:5).
- **"Thou art the land that is not cleansed, nor rained upon in the day of indignation" → "You are a land that is not cleansed; it has no rain in the day of indignation"** (v24) — Hebrew לֹא מְטֹהָרָה הִיא לֹא גֻשְׁמָהּ: מְטֹהָרָה, pual of טִהַר "cleanse" (the Greek has "rained upon": from memory, not followed); גֻּשְׁמָהּ is pointed as the noun גֶּשֶׁם "rain" with "her" ("not her rain"), not a verb; זַעַם "indignation" (its row; also v31).
- **"There is a conspiracy of her prophets in the midst thereof" → "A conspiracy of her prophets is in her midst"** (v25) — קֶשֶׁר "conspiracy" (its row); MT נְבִיאֶיהָ "her prophets" (the Greek reads "whose chieftains": from memory, not followed). **"ravening the prey" → "tearing prey"** (v25, 27) — טֹרֵף טָרֶף (the טָרַף row). **"they have devoured souls" → "soul they have devoured"** — נֶפֶשׁ אָכָלוּ, singular collective, first (the נֶפֶשׁ row: plain "soul"; the earlier "human lives" interprets). **"they have taken the treasure and precious things" → "store and precious things they take"** — חֹסֶן "store" (its row), יְקָר "precious things"; יִקָּחוּ, an imperfect.
- **"Her priests have violated my law" → "Her priests have done violence to my torah"** (v26) — חָמְסוּ, verb of חָמָס "violence" (its row). **"they have put no difference between the holy and profane" → "between holy and common they have not separated"** — הִבְדִּיל "separate" (its row), חֹל "common" (its row; Lev 10:10). **"neither have they shewed difference between the unclean and the clean" → "and between the unclean and the clean they have not made known"** — לֹא הוֹדִיעוּ; "difference" is not written. **"and have hid their eyes from my sabbaths" → "and from my Shabbats they have hidden their eyes"** — הֶעְלִימוּ עֵינֵיהֶם. **"and I am profaned among them" → "and I am profaned in their midst"** — וָאֵחַל בְּתוֹכָם.
- **"Her princes in the midst thereof" → "Her princes within her"** (v27) — בְקִרְבָּהּ (קֶרֶב, beside תּוֹךְ "midst" of v25). **"to destroy souls, to get dishonest gain" → "to do away with souls, in order to gain unjust gain"** — לְאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד (the "do away with" row); לְמַעַן בְּצֹעַ בָּצַע, verb and noun of one root.
- **"And her prophets have daubed them with untempered morter" → "And her prophets have plastered for them with whitewash"** (v28) — טָחוּ לָהֶם תָּפֵל, as 13:10–15 ("for them", לָהֶם). **"seeing vanity, and divining lies unto them" → "beholding emptiness and divining a lie for them"** — חֹזִים שָׁוְא וְקֹסְמִים לָהֶם כָּזָב, as 13:6–9 and 21:29. **"when the LORD hath not spoken" → "and Yahweh has not spoken"** — וַיהוָה לֹא דִבֵּר.
- **"have used oppression, and exercised robbery" → "have committed extortion and committed robbery"** (v29) — עָשְׁקוּ עֹשֶׁק וְגָזְלוּ גָּזֵל, each verb with its own noun (as 18:18). **"and have vexed the poor and needy" → "and the afflicted and the needy they have wronged"** — עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן (the rows, as 18:12). **"yea, they have oppressed the stranger wrongfully" → "and from the sojourner they have extorted without judgment"** — עָשְׁקוּ בְּלֹא מִשְׁפָּט (the עָשַׁק row: of a person "extort from"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment").
- **"that should make up the hedge, and stand in the gap" → "a man building a fence and standing in the breach"** (v30) — גֹּדֵר־גָּדֵר, verb and noun ("fence", as 13:5; the גָּדֵר row); פֶּרֶץ "breach" (its row, as 13:5). **"for the land" → "on behalf of the land"** — בְּעַד. **"that I should not destroy it" → "so that I should not ruin it"** — שִׁחֵת "ruin" (its row). **"but I found none" → "and I found none"** — וְלֹא מָצָאתִי, "and I did not find".
- **"Therefore have I poured out" → "And I have poured out"** (v31) — וָאֶשְׁפֹּךְ, wayyiqtol: "and"; no "therefore" in the Hebrew. **"I have consumed them" → "I have made an end of them"** — כִּלִּיתִים, the כִּלָּה row with persons. **"their own way have I recompensed upon their heads" → "their way upon their head I have laid"** — דַּרְכָּם בְּרֹאשָׁם נָתַתִּי, literally "I have given" (the Ezekiel row for נָתַן of deeds laid back on the doer, 9:10; 11:21; 16:43); "head" singular.

### Notes
- **v30** — "standing in the breach": the one who stands before Yahweh for the land (as Ps 106:23, of Moses); none was found.
