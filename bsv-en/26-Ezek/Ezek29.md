# Ezekiel 29

[1] In the tenth year, in the tenth, on the twelfth of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[2] "Son of man, set your face against ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and prophesy against him and against all ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[3] Speak, and say, Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

Behold, I am against you,
⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
the great sea monster that lies
in the midst of his ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧,
who has said, 'My ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ is mine,
and I, I have made myself.'

[4] And I will put hooks in your jaws,
and I will make the fish of your ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧ cling to your scales;
and I will bring you up from the midst of your ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧,
and all the fish of your ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧,
that cling to your scales.

[5] And I will abandon you to the wilderness,
you and all the fish of your ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧;
on the face of the field you shall fall;
you shall not be gathered up and not be collected.
To the living things of the earth and to the birds of the heavens
I have given you as food.

[6] And all the inhabitants of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall know
that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
because they have been a staff of reed to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] When they seized you by the palm, you were crushed, and you split open every shoulder for them;
and when they leaned on you, you were broken, and you made all loins stand for them.

[8] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am bringing a sword upon you, and I will cut off from you man and beast.

[9] And the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall become a desolation and a ruin, and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. Because he said, 'The ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ is mine, and I, I have made it,'

[10] therefore, behold, I am against you and against your ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧, and I will make the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ ruins of waste, a desolation, from Migdol to ⟦syene|Seveneh|Syene⟧, and as far as the border of ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧.

[11] The foot of man shall not pass through it, and the foot of beast shall not pass through it, and it shall not be inhabited forty years.

[12] And I will make the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ a desolation in the midst of desolated lands, and her cities in the midst of ruined cities shall be a desolation forty years; and I will scatter the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ among the nations and strew them through the lands.

[13] For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: At the end of forty years I will gather the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ from the peoples where they were scattered.

[14] And I will restore the fortunes of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and I will bring them back to the land of ⟦pathros|Patros|Pathros⟧, to the land of their origin; and they shall be there a low kingdom.

[15] Lower than the kingdoms it shall be, and it shall not lift itself up any more above the nations; and I will make them few, so that they do not rule over the nations.

[16] And it shall no more be a trust for the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, bringing iniquity to remembrance when they turn after them; and they shall know that I am ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧."

[17] And it came to pass in the twenty-seventh year, in the first, on the first of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[18] "Son of man, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ made his force serve a great service against ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧; every head made bald, and every shoulder plucked bare; and wages there were none for him and for his force from ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ for the service that he served against it.

[19] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am giving to ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and he shall carry off her multitude, and spoil her spoil, and plunder her plunder; and it shall be wages for his force.

[20] As his wage for which he served against it I have given him the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, because they acted for me, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[21] In that day I will cause a horn to spring up for the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and to you I will give an opening of the mouth in their midst; and they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **Layout** — v3b–7 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; the rest stays prose. The quotation marks inside the oracle are dropped where the Hebrew runs on as one speech (v6, 8, 13, 21).
- **"in the tenth month, in the twelfth day of the month" → "in the tenth, on the twelfth of the month"** (v1; also v17 "in the first, on the first of the month") — Hebrew בָּעֲשִׂרִי בִּשְׁנֵים עָשָׂר לַחֹדֶשׁ: "month" and "day" are not written (as 1:1). **"came unto me, saying"** — לֵאמֹר kept.
- **"against all Egypt"** (v2) — וְעַל־מִצְרַיִם כֻּלָּהּ, literally "against Egypt, all of her".
- **"the great dragon" → "the great sea monster"** (v3) — Hebrew הַתַּנִּים, the form written with final ם for תַּנִּין (as 32:2; not תַּנִּים "jackals"), rendered by its row: "sea monster" where it is in the waters (the Nile is called a sea, Isa 19:5; 32:2 "in the seas"). The creature meant is uncertain: the crocodile of the Nile is the common explanation; the word itself names a great serpent-like creature. **"his rivers" → "his Niles"** (v3, 4, 5, 10) — יְאֹרָיו, plural of יְאֹר "Nile" (its arms and canals). **"which hath said, My river is mine own, and I have made it for myself" → "who has said, 'My Nile is mine, and I, I have made myself'"** — לִי יְאֹרִי, literally "to me is my Nile"; וַאֲנִי עֲשִׂיתִנִי, with the suffix "me": "I have made myself". Many take the suffix as "for myself", with "it" understood (as v9 עָשִׂיתִי "I have made"): uncertain.
- **"hooks"** (v4) — qere חַחִים; the ketiv חחיים is another spelling of the same word. **"I will cause the fish … to stick" → "I will make the fish … cling"** — הִדְבַּקְתִּי, hifil of דָּבַק "cling". **"I will bring thee up out of the midst of thy rivers, and all the fish of thy rivers shall stick unto thy scales" → "and I will bring you up … and all the fish of your Niles, that cling to your scales"** — וְאֵת כָּל־דְּגַת יְאֹרֶיךָ: אֵת marks the fish as a second object of "bring up"; תִּדְבָּק "it clings" stands without a relative word ("shall cling" as a clause of its own is the other reading).
- **"I will leave thee thrown into the wilderness" → "I will abandon you to the wilderness"** (v5) — Hebrew וּנְטַשְׁתִּיךָ הַמִּדְבָּרָה, נָטַשׁ "abandon". **"upon the open fields" → "on the face of the field"** — עַל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה. **"thou shalt not be brought together, nor gathered" → "you shall not be gathered up and not be collected"** — תֵאָסֵף (אָסַף, also of gathering the dead) and תִקָּבֵץ (קָבַץ, the "gather" of v13): two verbs kept apart. **"the beasts of the field" → "the living things of the earth"** — חַיַּת הָאָרֶץ. **"I have given thee"** — נְתַתִּיךָ, a perfect.
- **"because they have been a staff of reed"** (v6) — Hebrew יַעַן הֱיוֹתָם, "because of their being": "they", the Egyptians (the old text had "you"). **"Then … shall know" → "And … shall know"** — וְיָדְעוּ.
- **"When they took hold of thee by thy hand, thou didst break" → "When they seized you by the palm, you were crushed"** (v7) — qere בַכַּף "by the palm" (ketiv בכפך "by your palm"); תֵּרוֹץ, from רָצַץ "crush" (as 2 Kgs 18:21 "this crushed reed"). **"rend" → "split open"** — בָּקַע. **"madest all their loins to be at a stand" → "you made all loins stand for them"** — MT וְהַעֲמַדְתָּ, hifil of עָמַד "stand" (the loins made rigid). Many read וְהִמְעַדְתָּ "you made … shake" (from מָעַד, by the change of two letters; the Septuagint and Syriac are cited for it, from memory): the old text of this verse followed it; the MT is kept.
- **"I will bring a sword upon thee" → "I am bringing a sword upon you"** (v8) — הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, participle; עָלַיִךְ is feminine (the land is addressed).
- **"a waste" → "a ruin"** (v9) — חָרְבָּה; שְׁמָמָה "desolation". **"because he hath said" → "Because he said"** — יַעַן אָמַר, third person (the old text had "you said"); יְאֹר לִי "the Nile is mine"; וַאֲנִי עָשִׂיתִי "and I, I have made", "it" supplied (no object in the Hebrew).
- **"utterly waste and desolate, from the tower of Syene even unto the border of Ethiopia" → "ruins of waste, a desolation, from Migdol to Syene, and as far as the border of Cush"** (v10) — Hebrew לְחָרְבוֹת חֹרֶב שְׁמָמָה, three nouns of one field; מִמִּגְדֹּל סְוֵנֵה: מִגְדֹּל is a town in the north of Egypt (as Jer 44:1; 46:14; the word also means "tower"), סְוֵנֵה "to Syene" (with the ending of direction) in the far south: the whole land; כּוּשׁ "Cush" (KJV "Ethiopia").
- **"neither shall it be inhabited" → "and it shall not be inhabited"** (v11) — וְלֹא תֵשֵׁב, the qal "dwell" said of the land (as Isa 13:20).
- **"countries that are desolate … cities that are laid waste" → "desolated lands … ruined cities"** (v12) — אֲרָצוֹת נְשַׁמּוֹת, עָרִים מָחֳרָבוֹת; Hebrew order "and her cities in the midst of ruined cities shall be". **"the Egyptians"** — מִצְרַיִם with the plural pronoun "them" (וְזֵרִיתִים): one Hebrew word for "Egypt" and "the Egyptians". **"disperse" → "strew"** — זֵרָה.
- **"whither they were scattered" → "where they were scattered"** (v13) — אֲשֶׁר־נָפֹצוּ שָׁמָּה.
- **"I will bring again the captivity of Egypt" → "I will restore the fortunes of Egypt"** (v14) — Hebrew וְשַׁבְתִּי אֶת־שְׁבוּת מִצְרַיִם, literally "I will turn back the captivity / the turning of Egypt" (traditional "turn the captivity"). **"into the land of their habitation" → "to the land of their origin"** — מְכוּרָתָם (as 16:3; 21:30; uncertain: "origin, birth"). **"a base kingdom" → "a low kingdom"** — שְׁפָלָה.
- **"It shall be the basest of the kingdoms" → "Lower than the kingdoms it shall be"** (v15) — מִן־הַמַּמְלָכוֹת, comparative; Hebrew order. **"neither shall it exalt itself any more" → "and it shall not lift itself up any more"** — תִתְנַשֵּׂא, hitpael of נָשָׂא. **"I will diminish them, that they shall no more rule" → "I will make them few, so that they do not rule"** — הִמְעַטְתִּים; לְבִלְתִּי רְדוֹת has no "more" (עוֹד stands only in the first clause).
- **"the confidence of the house of Israel" → "a trust for the house of Israel"** (v16) — לְמִבְטָח, "for a trust". **"which bringeth their iniquity to remembrance, when they shall look after them" → "bringing iniquity to remembrance when they turn after them"** — מַזְכִּיר עָוֹן, no "their" (as Num 5:15); בִּפְנוֹתָם אַחֲרֵיהֶם "in their turning after them". **"that I am the Lord GOD"** — כִּי אֲנִי אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה: here the formula has both names.
- **"caused his army to serve a great service" → "made his force serve a great service"** (v18) — הֶעֱבִיד … עֲבֹדָה, the verb and noun of עָבַד; חַיִל "force". **"every head was made bald, and every shoulder was peeled" → "every head made bald, and every shoulder plucked bare"** — מֻקְרָח, מְרוּטָה: participles, no verb. **"yet had he no wages" → "and wages there were none for him"** — וְשָׂכָר לֹא־הָיָה לוֹ; "and" (וְ), Hebrew order. **"for the service that he had served against it"** — עָבַד עָלֶיהָ.
- **"he shall take her multitude" → "he shall carry off her multitude"** (v19) — נָשָׂא הֲמֹנָהּ, הָמוֹן "multitude" (her crowd of people, or her abundance of goods: "wealth" in many versions; uncertain). **"take her spoil, and take her prey" → "spoil her spoil, and plunder her plunder"** — שָׁלַל שְׁלָלָהּ, בָּזַז בִּזָּהּ: verb and noun of one root each time.
- **"I have given him the land of Egypt for his labour wherewith he served against it" → "As his wage for which he served against it I have given him the land of Egypt"** (v20) — פְּעֻלָּתוֹ "his wage" (lit. "his work"), first; עָבַד בָּהּ. **"because they wrought for me" → "because they acted for me"** — אֲשֶׁר עָשׂוּ לִי: אֲשֶׁר may be "because" or "(the work) which"; עָשָׂה "do, act". **"saith the Lord GOD" → "declares"** — נְאֻם, "the declaration of".
- **"In that day will I cause the horn of the house of Israel to bud forth" → "In that day I will cause a horn to spring up for the house of Israel"** (v21) — Hebrew אַצְמִיחַ קֶרֶן לְבֵית יִשְׂרָאֵל: הִצְמִיחַ "cause to spring up", קֶרֶן without the article, לְ "for". **"I will give thee the opening of the mouth" → "to you I will give an opening of the mouth"** — וּלְךָ אֶתֵּן פִּתְחוֹן־פֶּה, "to you" first; פֶּה "mouth" (the old text had "lips").
- **"Then shall they know" → "and they shall know"** (v9, 16, 21) — וְיָדְעוּ, one wording through the book.

### Notes
- **v1, 17** — the tenth year is that of the exile of Jehoiachin (1:2); v17–21, in the twenty-seventh year, is the latest dated word in the book.
- **v21** — "horn": strength and standing (as 1 Sam 2:1, 10; Ps 132:17).
