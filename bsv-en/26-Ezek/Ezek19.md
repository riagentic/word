# Ezekiel 19

[1] And you, lift up a dirge for the chieftains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[2] and you shall say:

What was your mother? A lioness!
Among lions she lay;
in the midst of young lions she reared her cubs.

[3] And she brought up one of her cubs;
a young lion he became,
and he learned to tear prey;
man he devoured.

[4] And nations heard of him;
in their pit he was caught,
and they brought him with hooks
to the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[5] And she saw that she had waited,
her hope had perished;
and she took one of her cubs;
a young lion she made him.

[6] And he walked about among lions;
a young lion he became,
and he learned to tear prey;
man he devoured.

[7] And he knew his widows,
and their cities he laid waste;
and the land was desolate, and its fullness,
at the sound of his roaring.

[8] And nations set against him
all around from the provinces,
and they spread their net over him;
in their pit he was caught.

[9] And they put him in the cage with hooks,
and they brought him to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧;
they brought him into the snares,
so that his voice should no more be heard
on the mountains of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[10] Your mother was like a vine in your blood,
planted by waters;
fruitful and full of boughs she was
from many waters.

[11] And she had rods of strength
for scepters of rulers;
and its stature rose high among the thick boughs,
and it was seen in its height
with the multitude of its branches.

[12] And she was plucked up in fury;
to the ground she was thrown,
and the east wind dried up her fruit;
they were torn off and withered, the rod of her strength;
fire devoured it.

[13] And now she is planted in the wilderness,
in a land of drought and thirst.

[14] And fire went out from the rod of her shoots;
her fruit it devoured;
and there was not in her a rod of strength,
a scepter to rule.

A dirge it is, and it has become a dirge.

## Changes

- **Layout** — the chapter is a קִינָה "dirge" (v1, 14), set as poetry: one Hebrew colon per line in the Hebrew order (v2 relaid at the atnach: "Among lions she lay; / in the midst of young lions she reared her cubs"; v11 and v12 by the accents). The note "blockquote indentation" was untrue: the file has none.
- **"Moreover take thou up a lamentation for the princes of Israel" → "And you, lift up a dirge for the chieftains of Israel"** (v1) — וְאַתָּה "And you"; שָׂא "lift up"; קִינָה "dirge" (the 2 Sam 1:17 row; also v14); נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (lit. "one lifted up"; a play with שָׂא "lift up").
- **"And say, What is thy mother? A lioness: she lay down among lions" → "and you shall say: What was your mother? A lioness! Among lions she lay"** (v2) — וְאָמַרְתָּ, a weqatal; מָה אִמְּךָ לְבִיָּא, literally "what your mother — a lioness" (no verb; "was" supplied); לָבִיא "lioness" (the gender is not certain); אֲרָיוֹת "lions"; רָבַץ "lie" (of an animal). **"she nourished her whelps among young lions" → "in the midst of young lions she reared her cubs"** — כְּפִירִים "young lions"; גּוּר "cub" (as Gen 49:9; also v3, 5); רִבְּתָה "reared" (made great).
- **"he learned to catch the prey; it devoured men" → "he learned to tear prey; man he devoured"** (v3, 6) — לִטְרָף־טֶרֶף, verb and noun of one root (טָרַף "tear to pieces"); אָדָם "man" (mankind), singular, first.
- **"The nations also heard of him; he was taken in their pit" → "And nations heard of him; in their pit he was caught"** (v4) — גּוֹיִם without the article (also v8); שַׁחַת "pit" (a trap pit; also v8); נִתְפַּשׂ "be caught". **"they brought him with chains" → "they brought him with hooks"** — חַחִים "hooks" (as 2 Kgs 19:28; Ezek 29:4; also v9).
- **"Now when she saw that she had waited, and her hope was lost" → "And she saw that she had waited, her hope had perished"** (v5) — וַתֵּרֶא, a wayyiqtol: "And she saw"; נוֹחֲלָה, nifal of יָחַל "wait" (only here in this form; "she had waited in vain", or "was disappointed": uncertain); אָבְדָה "perished" (אָבַד). **"then she took another of her whelps, and made him a young lion" → "and she took one of her cubs; a young lion she made him"** — אֶחָד "one" (as v3); the Hebrew order.
- **"And he went up and down among the lions" → "And he walked about among lions"** (v6) — הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about" (hitpael); אֲרָיוֹת without the article.
- **"And he knew their desolate palaces" → "And he knew his widows"** (v7) — וַיֵּדַע אַלְמְנוֹתָיו, the Masoretic text, "he knew his widows" (אַלְמָנָה "widow"; the suffix "his" is as printed); its sense is uncertain (taken as "violated", or "he knew [them as] his widows" after killing their men). KJV and others read אַרְמְנוֹתָיו "his citadels", one letter different: the MT kept; "seized" of the old verse is not the word (יָדַע "know"). **"he laid waste their cities" → "and their cities he laid waste"** — הֶחֱרִיב, the Hebrew order. **"and the land was desolate, and the fulness thereof" → "and the land was desolate, and its fullness"** — שָׁמֵם "be desolate" (of persons "be appalled": one root); מְלֹא "fullness" (all that fills it). **"by the noise of his roaring" → "at the sound of his roaring"** — קוֹל "sound"; שַׁאֲגָה "roaring".
- **"Then the nations set against him on every side from the provinces" → "And nations set against him all around from the provinces"** (v8) — וַיִּתְּנוּ עָלָיו, literally "gave against him" (no object: perhaps snares, or themselves); סָבִיב "all around"; מְדִינוֹת "provinces". **"and spread their net over him" → "and they spread their net over him"** — פָּרַשׂ "spread", רֶשֶׁת "net" (as 17:20).
- **"And they put him in ward in chains" → "And they put him in the cage with hooks"** (v9) — סוּגַר is found only here ("cage"; a neck-collar is also proposed: uncertain); בַּחַחִים "with hooks". **"they brought him into holds" → "they brought him into the snares"** — מְצֹדוֹת, the plural of the word of 17:20 ("snare", a hunter's net; of a place "stronghold": here either). **"that his voice should no more be heard"** — לְמַעַן "so that".
- **"Thy mother is like a vine in thy blood" → "Your mother was like a vine in your blood"** (v10) — Hebrew כַגֶּפֶן בְּדָמְךָ, the Masoretic text, word for word; the sense is not known (taken as "in your blood" = in your life, or as "in your likeness" from דָּמָה "be like"). The old verse's "in a vineyard" is a conjecture (כֶּרֶם); it is removed. **"planted by the waters: she was fruitful and full of branches by reason of many waters" → "planted by waters; fruitful and full of boughs she was from many waters"** — שְׁתוּלָה "planted" (as 17:8); עֲנֵפָה "full of boughs" (עָנָף, 17:8); מִמַּיִם רַבִּים "from many waters".
- **"And she had strong rods for the sceptres of them that bare rule" → "And she had rods of strength for scepters of rulers"** (v11) — מַטּוֹת עֹז "rods of strength" (מַטֶּה "rod, staff"; also v12, 14); שִׁבְטֵי מֹשְׁלִים "scepters of rulers" (שֵׁבֶט "rod", of one who rules "scepter": the row). **"and her stature was exalted among the thick branches" → "and its stature rose high among the thick boughs"** — קוֹמָתוֹ "its stature" (masculine: the rod or the vine); עֲבֹתִים "thick boughs" (also read "clouds", as 31:3; uncertain). **"and she appeared in her height with the multitude of her branches" → "and it was seen in its height with the multitude of its branches"** — וַיֵּרָא, nifal "was seen"; דָּלִיּוֹת "branches" (as 17:6).
- **"But she was plucked up in fury, she was cast down to the ground" → "And she was plucked up in fury; to the ground she was thrown"** (v12) — נָתַשׁ "pluck up"; חֵמָה "fury"; הֻשְׁלָכָה "she was thrown" (the הִשְׁלִיךְ row). **"her strong rods were broken and withered; the fire consumed them" → "they were torn off and withered, the rod of her strength; fire devoured it"** — הִתְפָּרְקוּ "were torn off" (the פָּרַק row); מַטֵּה עֻזָּהּ singular after the plural verbs, as printed; אֲכָלָתְהוּ "devoured it" (אָכַל "eat, devour"; also v14).
- **"in a dry and thirsty ground" → "in a land of drought and thirst"** (v13) — וְעַתָּה "And now"; בְּאֶרֶץ צִיָּה וְצָמָא, two nouns (צָמָא "thirst").
- **"fire is gone out of a rod of her branches, which hath devoured her fruit" → "fire went out from the rod of her shoots; her fruit it devoured"** (v14) — מַטֵּה בַדֶּיהָ "the rod of her shoots" (בַּד, as 17:6); the Hebrew order. **"so that she hath no strong rod to be a sceptre to rule" → "and there was not in her a rod of strength, a scepter to rule"** — שֵׁבֶט לִמְשׁוֹל. **"This is a lamentation, and shall be for a lamentation" → "A dirge it is, and it has become a dirge"** — וַתְּהִי, a wayyiqtol: "and it has become".

### Notes
- The dirge names no king. 2 Kgs 23:31–34 tells of a king taken to Egypt (Jehoahaz); 2 Kgs 24:8–15 and 25:6–7 of kings taken to Babylon (Jehoiachin; Zedekiah). The mother (v2, 10) is not named either: Judah, the royal house, or the queen mother are proposed.
