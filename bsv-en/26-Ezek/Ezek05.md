# Ezekiel 5

[1] "And you, son of man, take for yourself a sharp sword; as a razor of the barbers you shall take it for yourself, and you shall make it pass over your head and over your beard. And you shall take for yourself balances of weight and divide them.

[2] A third you shall burn in the fire in the midst of the city, when the days of the siege are fulfilled. And you shall take the third, you shall strike with the sword around it. And the third you shall scatter to the wind, and a sword I will draw out after them.

[3] And you shall take from there a few in number and bind them in your corners.

[4] And from them again you shall take, and throw them into the midst of the fire and burn them in the fire; from it a fire shall go out to all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] "Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: This is ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; in the midst of the nations I have set her, and around her are lands.

[6] And she rebelled against my ordinances into wickedness more than the nations, and against my statutes more than the lands that are around her; for my ordinances they rejected, and my statutes, they did not walk in them.

[7] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Because you are more turbulent than the nations that are around you — in my statutes you have not walked, and my ordinances you have not done, and according to the ordinances of the nations that are around you you have not done —

[8] therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, I am against you, even I; and I will execute judgments in your midst before the eyes of the nations.

[9] And I will do in you what I have not done, and the like of which I will not do again, because of all your abominations.

[10] Therefore fathers shall eat sons in your midst, and sons shall eat their fathers; and I will execute judgments in you, and I will scatter all your remnant to every wind.

[11] "Therefore, as I live, declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, surely, because you have defiled my sanctuary with all your detestable things and with all your abominations, I too will diminish, and my eye shall not pity, and I too will not have pity.

[12] A third of you shall die by pestilence and be consumed by famine in your midst; and the third shall fall by the sword around you; and the third I will scatter to every wind, and a sword I will draw out after them.

[13] "And my anger shall be spent, and I will make my fury rest on them, and I will be comforted; and they shall know that I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken in my jealousy, when I spend my fury on them.

[14] And I will make you a ruin and a reproach among the nations that are around you, before the eyes of all who pass by.

[15] And it shall be a reproach and a reviling, a discipline and a thing that appalls, to the nations that are around you, when I execute judgments in you in anger and in fury and in reproofs of fury — I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken —

[16] when I send against them the evil arrows of famine, which were to destroy, which I will send to destroy you; and famine I will add upon you, and I will break for you the staff of bread.

[17] And I will send upon you famine and an evil living thing, and they shall bereave you; and pestilence and blood shall pass through you, and a sword I will bring upon you. I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have spoken."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai Yahweh*, both words; Ezekiel's distinctive doubled divine title.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept, also where the commands run on in the "and you shall …" form (v1–4).
- **"And thou, son of man" → "And you, son of man"** (v1) — Hebrew בֶן־אָדָם "son of man"; no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it). **"take thee a sharp knife, take thee a barber's razor" → "take for yourself a sharp sword; as a razor of the barbers you shall take it for yourself"** — חֶרֶב is "sword" (as v2, 12, 17); קַח־לְךָ … תִּקָּחֶנָּה לָּךְ, the לְךָ "for yourself" twice; תַּעַר הַגַּלָּבִים "the razor of the barbers" stands beside the sword without a word "as" (supplied). **"cause it to pass" → "you shall make it pass"** — וְהַעֲבַרְתָּ, hifil of עָבַר. **"balances to weigh, and divide the hair" → "balances of weight and divide them"** — מֹאזְנֵי מִשְׁקָל; וְחִלַּקְתָּם "and divide them": "the hair" is not named.
- **"with fire" → "in the fire"** (v2) — בָּאוּר, literally "in the flame" (אוּר "light, fire"). **"when the days of the siege are fulfilled"** — כִּמְלֹאת, literally "as the filling of". **"smite about it with a knife" → "you shall strike with the sword around it"** — תַּכֶּה בַחֶרֶב סְבִיבוֹתֶיהָ; "it" is the city (feminine). **"I will draw out a sword" → "a sword I will draw out"** — אָרִיק, hifil of רִיק, literally "empty out" (the sheath); Hebrew order kept (also v12).
- **"a few in number" … "bind them in thy skirts" → "bind them in your corners"** (v3) — בִּכְנָפֶיךָ, literally "in your wings": the corners of the garment (the כָּנָף row).
- **"Then take of them again" → "And from them again you shall take"** (v4) — no object in the Hebrew. **"for thereof shall a fire come forth into" → "from it a fire shall go out to"** — מִמֶּנּוּ "from it" (the fire, or the hair burned in it); no "for".
- **"I have set it in the midst of the nations and countries that are round about her" → "in the midst of the nations I have set her, and around her are lands"** (v5) — שַׂמְתִּיהָ "I have set her"; וּסְבִיבוֹתֶיהָ אֲרָצוֹת, a clause of its own; אֲרָצוֹת "lands" (also v6).
- **"she hath changed my judgments into wickedness" → "she rebelled against my ordinances into wickedness"** (v6) — וַתֶּמֶר from מָרָה "rebel against" (the מָרָה row; KJV read it from מוּר "change"); לְרִשְׁעָה "into / for wickedness"; מִן־הַגּוֹיִם "more than the nations". מִשְׁפָּטַי "my ordinances" and חֻקּוֹתַי "my statutes" (the מִשְׁפָּט and חֻקָּה rows; also v7). **"for they have refused my judgments" → "for my ordinances they rejected"** — מָאַס "reject"; the subject changes to "they" in the Hebrew. **"and my statutes, they did not walk in them"** — Hebrew order kept.
- **"Because ye multiplied more than" → "Because you are more turbulent than"** (v7) — הֲמָנְכֶם, found only here: an infinitive with "your", commonly taken from הָמָה "roar, be in tumult" (or from הָמוֹן "multitude", hence KJV "multiplied"): uncertain. **"neither have done according to the judgments of the nations" → "and according to the ordinances of the nations that are around you you have not done"** — MT has the "not"; some Hebrew manuscripts and the Syriac lack it (from memory), and 11:12 says "you have done according to the ordinances of the nations": the MT is translated as it stands. The sentence is not finished; v8 takes it up with "therefore".
- **"Behold, I, even I, am against thee" → "Behold, I am against you, even I"** (v8) — הִנְנִי עָלַיִךְ גַּם־אָנִי, Hebrew order. **"in the sight of the nations" → "before the eyes of the nations"** — לְעֵינֵי (also v14). מִשְׁפָּטִים "judgments" here are acts of judgment (also v10, 15, שְׁפָטִים).
- **"I will do in thee that which I have not done" → "I will do in you what I have not done"** (v9) — בָךְ "in you"; "because of all your abominations" stands last, as in the Hebrew. תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" (also v11).
- **"the fathers shall eat the sons" → "fathers shall eat sons"** (v10) — אָבוֹת … בָנִים without article or "their"; "their fathers" (אֲבוֹתָם) in the second line. **"the whole remnant of thee will I scatter into all the winds" → "I will scatter all your remnant to every wind"** — שְׁאֵרִית "remnant"; לְכָל־רוּחַ "to every wind" (also v12).
- **"As I live … surely, because thou hast defiled" → "as I live, … surely, because you have defiled"** (v11) — חַי־אָנִי, literally "living am I"; אִם־לֹא, literally "if not": the oath, "surely". **"detestable things"** — שִׁקּוּצִים (תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" beside it). **"therefore will I also diminish thee" → "I too will diminish"** — וְגַם־אֲנִי אֶגְרַע, no object in the Hebrew (גָּרַע "diminish, take away"); some Hebrew manuscripts read אֶגְדַּע "I will cut down" (from memory): the MT is kept. **"neither shall mine eye spare, neither will I have any pity" → "and my eye shall not pity, and I too will not have pity"** — חוּס with עֵינִי; גַּם־אֲנִי again; חָמַל "have pity".
- **"A third part of thee shall die with the pestilence, and with famine shall they be consumed" → "A third of you shall die by pestilence and be consumed by famine"** (v12) — וּבָרָעָב יִכְלוּ, "and"; not "or". **"round about thee" → "around you"** (also v5–7, 14, 15).
- **"Thus shall mine anger be accomplished" → "And my anger shall be spent"** (v13) — וְכָלָה, qal of כָּלָה "come to an end"; בְּכַלּוֹתִי, the piel, "when I spend" (one root). **"I will cause my fury to rest upon them" → "I will make my fury rest on them"** — הֲנִחוֹתִי, hifil of נוּחַ "rest"; חֵמָה "fury" (also v15). **"I will be comforted"** — וְהִנֶּחָמְתִּי, of נִחַם: the relief of one whose wrong is avenged (the "be comforted" sense of the נִחַם row). **"and they shall know that I the LORD have spoken it in my zeal" → "and they shall know that I, Yahweh, have spoken in my jealousy"** — כִּי־אֲנִי יְהוָה דִּבַּרְתִּי: here the clause goes on with "have spoken", so it is not the formula "that I am Yahweh" (the reading "that I am Yahweh; I have spoken in my jealousy" is also possible by the accents); קִנְאָה "jealousy" ("zeal" as the other sense, the קִנֵּא row); no "it".
- **"Moreover I will make thee waste" → "And I will make you a ruin"** (v14) — וְאֶתְּנֵךְ לְחָרְבָּה, literally "I will give you to a ruin"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (also v15).
- **"So it shall be" → "And it shall be"** (v15) — וְהָיְתָה, third person feminine (the city); the Septuagint and others have "you shall be" (from memory): the MT is kept. **"a taunt" → "a reviling"** — גְּדוּפָה (only here; root גָּדַף "revile"). **"an instruction" → "a discipline"** — מוּסָר, noun of יִסֵּר "discipline": the lesson others learn from her. **"an astonishment" → "a thing that appalls"** — מְשַׁמָּה, of the root of שָׁמֵם "be appalled". **"in furious rebukes" → "in reproofs of fury"** — וּבְתֹכְחוֹת חֵמָה. **"I the LORD have spoken it" → "I, Yahweh, have spoken"** — אֲנִי יְהוָה דִּבַּרְתִּי (also v17); no "it".
- **"When I shall send upon them the evil arrows of famine" → "when I send against them the evil arrows of famine"** (v16) — בָּהֶם "against them": the Hebrew passes from "them" to "you" in the same verse; both kept. **"which shall be for their destruction" → "which were to destroy"** — הָיוּ לְמַשְׁחִית, a perfect, "which were for destroying" (the מַשְׁחִית "destroyer" form; one root with לְשַׁחֶתְכֶם "to destroy you"). **"I will increase the famine" → "famine I will add"** — אֹסֵף. **"break your staff of bread" → "break for you the staff of bread"** — מַטֵּה־לֶחֶם, "staff of bread": the supply of bread one leans on; לָכֶם "for you".
- **"evil beasts" → "an evil living thing"** (v17) — חַיָּה רָעָה, a collective singular (the חַיָּה "living thing" row: wild animals are meant); "they shall bereave you" is plural in the Hebrew (וְשִׁכְּלֻךְ, שִׁכֵּל "bereave"; "of children" is in the verb). **"and I will bring the sword upon thee" → "and a sword I will bring upon you"** — Hebrew order, no article.

### Notes
- **v1–4** — the sign of the hair: burned, struck, scattered; v12 gives its meaning.
- **v5–17** — the address passes between "her / it" (the city), "you" feminine singular, "you" plural and "them"; each verse keeps the person the Hebrew has.
