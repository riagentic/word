[2] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ made the divisions of the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ stand, by their divisions, each man according to his service, of the priests and of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, for the burnt offering and for the peace offerings, to minister and to give thanks and to praise in the gates of the camps of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] And the portion of the king from his goods was for the burnt offerings: for the burnt offerings of the morning and of the evening, and the burnt offerings for the ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ and for the new moons and for the appointed times, as it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] And he said to the people, to those dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to give the portion of the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, so that they might be strong in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] And when the word spread abroad, the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ multiplied the first of grain, new wine and fresh oil and honey and all the yield of the field, and the tithe of all they brought in abundance.

[6] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who dwelt in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, they also brought the tithe of herd and flock and the tithe of holy things that were sanctified to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they placed them heaps and heaps.

[7] In the third month they began to found the heaps, and in the seventh month they finished.

[8] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ and the princes came and saw the heaps, and they blessed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and his people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[9] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ sought of the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ concerning the heaps.

[10] And ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the head priest, of the house of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧, said to him, and he said, "Since they began to bring the contribution to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, there has been eating and being sated and leaving over in abundance, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has blessed his people, and what remains is this multitude."

[11] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said to prepare chambers in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they prepared them.

[12] And they brought the contribution and the tithe and the holy things in faithfulness. And over them as leader was ⟦conaniah|Kananyahu|Conaniah⟧ the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, and ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ his brother was second.

[13] And ⟦jehiel|Yechiel|Jehiel⟧ and ⟦azaziah|Azazyahu|Azaziah⟧ and ⟦nahath|Nachat|Nahath⟧ and Asahel and ⟦jerimoth|Yerimot|Jerimoth⟧ and ⟦jozabad|Yozavad|Jozabad⟧ and Eliel and ⟦ismachiah|Yismachyahu|Ismachiah⟧ and ⟦mahath|Machat|Mahath⟧ and ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ were overseers from the hand of ⟦conaniah|Kananyahu|Conaniah⟧ and ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ his brother, by the appointment of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ the king and ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the leader of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[14] And ⟦kore|Qore|Kore⟧ the son of ⟦imnah|Yimnah|Imnah⟧ the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, the gatekeeper toward the east, was over the freewill offerings of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to give the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the most holy things.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; with the article in v13, 14, 21.
- **"brake the images in pieces, and cut down the groves, and threw down" → "broke in pieces the pillars and hewed down the Asherim and broke down"** (v1) — מַצֵּבוֹת "pillars"; הָאֲשֵׁרִים, the word kept, plural; גִּדַּע "hew down"; נִתֵּץ "break down". 2 Kgs 18:4 has other words (the king as subject, "removed the high places", כָּרַת "cut down", "the Asherah" singular); each as printed.
- **"until they had utterly destroyed them all" → "until making an end"** (v1) — עַד־לְכַלֵּה, no object.
- **"appointed the courses" → "made the divisions … stand"** (v2) — הֶעֱמִיד; מַחְלְקוֹת "divisions", as 1 Chr 23–26.
- **"the tents of the LORD" → "the camps of Yahweh"** (v2) — מַחֲנוֹת, plural.
- **"the king's portion of his substance" → "the portion of the king from his goods"** (v3) — מְנָת; רְכוּשׁ.
- **"set feasts" → "appointed times"** (v3) — מוֹעֲדִים. **"new moons"** — חֳדָשִׁים, the word "month".
- **"commanded" → "said"** (v4, 11) — אָמַר.
- **"that they might be encouraged in the law" → "so that they might be strong in the torah"** (v4) — חָזַק "be strong".
- **"as soon as the commandment came abroad" → "when the word spread abroad"** (v5) — כִפְרֹץ הַדָּבָר, literally "as the word broke out".
- **"brought in abundance the firstfruits of corn, wine, and oil" → "multiplied the first of grain, new wine and fresh oil"** (v5) — הִרְבּוּ "made many" = gave in great quantity; רֵאשִׁית "the first" (not בִּכּוּרִים "firstfruits"); תִּירוֹשׁ, יִצְהָר. **"increase" → "yield"** — תְּבוּאָה.
- **"oxen and sheep" → "herd and flock"** (v6) — בָּקָר וָצֹאן. **"consecrated" → "sanctified"** — הַמְקֻדָּשִׁים.
- **"laid them by heaps" → "placed them heaps and heaps"** (v6) — וַיִּתְּנוּ עֲרֵמוֹת עֲרֵמוֹת, the noun twice; "them" is supplied.
- **"to lay the foundation of the heaps" → "to found the heaps"** (v7) — לְיִסּוֹד.
- **"questioned with" → "sought of"** (v9) — דָּרַשׁ עַל, the verb "seek": he asked them.
- **"the chief priest" → "the head priest"** (v10) — הַכֹּהֵן הָרֹאשׁ.
- **"the offerings" → "the contribution"** (v10, 12, 14) — תְּרוּמָה, what is lifted off for Yahweh.
- **"we have had enough to eat, and have left plenty" → "there has been eating and being sated and leaving over in abundance"** (v10) — three infinitives with no subject. **"that which is left is this great store" → "what remains is this multitude"** — הֶהָמוֹן.
- **"chambers"** (v11) — לְשָׁכוֹת. **"they prepared them"** — "them" is supplied.
- **"the dedicated things" → "the holy things"** (v12) — הַקֳּדָשִׁים. **"faithfully" → "in faithfulness"** (v12, 15, 18) — בֶּאֱמוּנָה; "in their office of trust" is the other reading (KJV "set office").
- **"ruler" → "leader"** (v12, 13) — נָגִיד. **v12, 13** — the qere "Conaniah" is translated (ketiv: Conaniahu).
- **"under the hand of" → "from the hand of"** (v13) — מִיַּד. **"at the commandment of" → "by the appointment of"** — בְּמִפְקַד.
- **"the porter toward the east" → "the gatekeeper toward the east"** (v14) — שׁוֹעֵר. **"to distribute the oblations of the LORD" → "to give the contribution of Yahweh"** — לָתֵת.
- **"the most holy things"** (v14) — literally "the holy things of the holy things".
- **"next him" → "at his hand"** (v15) — עַל־יָדוֹ.
- **"beside their genealogy of males" → "besides their enrollment by genealogy for males"** (v16) — הִתְיַחְשָׂם; so v17, 18, 19. **"three years old"** — literally "a son of three years"; so the Masoretic Text.
- **"his daily portion" → "the matter of a day in its day"** (v16) — דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ.
- **"in their charges"** (v16, 17) — מִשְׁמָרוֹת, what is to be kept.
- **"in their set office they sanctified themselves in holiness" → "in their faithfulness they sanctified themselves in holiness"** (v18) — יִתְקַדְּשׁוּ־קֹדֶשׁ. The syntax of v16–18 is loose in the Hebrew and is kept so.
- **"the suburbs" → "the pasture land"** (v19) — מִגְרָשׁ. **"in every several city" → "in every city"** — literally "in every city and city". **"expressed by name" → "designated by names"**.
- **"that which was good and right and truth" → "what was good and what was right and the truth"** (v20) — הַטּוֹב וְהַיָּשָׁר וְהָאֱמֶת; the third is the noun "truth".
- **"to seek his God"** (v21) — דָּרַשׁ with לְ.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 18:4–7** — only v1 (the pillars, the Asherim, the high places) and v20–21 ("he did … he prospered") touch Kings, and in other words; the rest is found only in Chronicles.
- **v1** — the breaking down reaches into Ephraim and Manasseh.
- **The portions** (v4–19) — the first of the produce, the tithe and the holy things are brought, stored in chambers and given out to priests and Levites by divisions. Compare Neh 10:35–39.
- **Third to seventh month** (v7) — from the grain harvest to the ingathering.
- **From three years old** (v16) — so the Hebrew; the Levites are counted from twenty years (v17).
