# 2 Chronicles 19

[1] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ returned to his house in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ the son of ⟦hanani|Chanani|Hanani⟧, the seer, went out to meet him and said to King ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "Is it for you to help the wicked, and do you love those who hate ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧? [4] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ dwelt in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And he returned and went out among the people, from ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧ to the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and he brought them back to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|the Elohim|the God⟧ of their fathers.

[6] And he said to the judges, "See what you are doing, for not for man do you judge, but for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he is with you in the word of judgment.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 4, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v3 with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"returned to his house in peace to Jerusalem" → "returned to his house in shalom, to Jerusalem"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּשָׁלוֹם: the word of Micaiah (18:16, 26, 27).
- **"went out to meet him"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֵּצֵא אֶל־פָּנָיו, literally "went out to his face". **"the seer"** — Hebrew הַחֹזֶה, "the one who beholds" (another word than רֹאֶה "seer", 16:7); by the accents it belongs to Jehu.
- **"Shouldest thou help the ungodly, and love them that hate the LORD?" → "Is it for you to help the wicked, and do you love those who hate Yahweh?"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲלָרָשָׁע לַעְזֹר וּלְשֹׂנְאֵי יְהוָה תֶּאֱהָב, literally "is it the wicked to help, and those who hate Yahweh you love?": the objects stand first. רָשָׁע "wicked" is singular (Ahab). **"therefore is wrath upon thee from before the LORD" → "And for this, wrath is upon you from before Yahweh"** — Hebrew וּבָזֹאת "and by this"; קֶצֶף "wrath"; no verb.
- **"Nevertheless there are good things found in thee" → "But good things have been found with you"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲבָל (in the late books "but"); עִמָּךְ "with you". **"taken away the groves" → "purged the Asheroth"** — Hebrew בִעַרְתָּ, "clear away, purge" (Deut 13:5; 1 Kgs 22:46); הָאֲשֵׁרוֹת, the feminine plural (as Judg 3:7), where 17:6 has the plural "Asherim": the word is kept. **"prepared thine heart" → "established your heart"** — Hebrew וַהֲכִינוֹתָ לְבָבְךָ, הֵכִין "make firm, establish" (12:14; 1 Sam 7:3). **"to seek"** — דָּרַשׁ.
- **"he went out again through the people" → "he returned and went out among the people"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיָּשָׁב וַיֵּצֵא: "return" with a second verb says that a thing is done again; the earlier going out is 17:7–9. **"brought them back"** — Hebrew וַיְשִׁיבֵם, the causative of the same verb "return". **"mount Ephraim" → "the hill country of Ephraim"** — Hebrew הַר אֶפְרַיִם.
- **"he set judges" → "he made judges stand"** (v5, 8) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד, the causative of "stand": he stationed, appointed them. **"fenced" → "fortified"**. **"city by city" kept** — Hebrew לְעִיר וָעִיר, literally "for city and city".
- **"Take heed what ye do" → "See what you are doing"** (v6) — Hebrew רְאוּ. **"ye judge not for man" → "not for man do you judge"** — Hebrew אָדָם. **"who is with you in the judgment" → "and he is with you in the word of judgment"** — Hebrew וְעִמָּכֶם בִּדְבַר מִשְׁפָּט, literally "and with you in the word (or matter) of judgment"; "he is" is supplied (Deut 17:9 "the word of the judgment").
- **"the fear of the LORD" → "the dread of Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew פַּחַד, as 17:10; in v9 the word is יִרְאָה "fear". **"take heed and do it" → "keep and do"** — Hebrew שִׁמְרוּ וַעֲשׂוּ, the "keep … do" of Deuteronomy (Deut 5:1); no object.
- **"no iniquity … nor respect of persons, nor taking of gifts" → "no injustice or lifting up of faces or taking of a bribe"** (v7) — Hebrew עַוְלָה (Deut 32:4 אֵין עָוֶל); מַשֹּׂא פָנִים "a lifting up of faces" and מִקַּח־שֹׁחַד "a taking of a bribe", the nouns of Deut 10:17, "who does not lift up a face and does not take a bribe" (cf. Deut 16:19): to lift up a man's face is to show him regard, to be partial.
- **"did Jehoshaphat set of the Levites, and of the priests, and of the chief of the fathers of Israel" → "made some of the Levites and the priests, and of the heads of the fathers of Israel, stand"** (v8) — Hebrew מִן־הַלְוִיִּם וְהַכֹּהֲנִים וּמֵרָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת. **"for controversies" → "for contention"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew רִיב, a quarrel brought to law (Deut 17:8 "matters of contention").
- **"when they returned to Jerusalem" → "and they returned to Jerusalem"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיָּשֻׁבוּ יְרוּשָׁלִָם, as the Masoretic Text is pointed. The sense is difficult (who returned?). With other vowels the same letters read "and they dwelt in Jerusalem" (the earlier text had "they resided"); the Septuagint is reported to read "and to judge the inhabitants of Jerusalem" (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"he charged them" → "he commanded them"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיְצַו. **"faithfully, and with a perfect heart" → "in faithfulness and with a whole heart"** — Hebrew בֶּאֱמוּנָה וּבְלֵבָב שָׁלֵם.
- **"what cause soever shall come to you of your brethren" → "every contention that comes upon you from your brothers"** (v10) — Hebrew יָבוֹא עֲלֵיכֶם. **"between blood and blood"** — the words of Deut 17:8: a case of bloodshed, where the kind of killing must be decided. **"between law and commandment, statutes and judgments" → "between torah and commandment, statutes and ordinances"** — Hebrew בֵּין־תּוֹרָה לְמִצְוָה לְחֻקִּים וּלְמִשְׁפָּטִים: which rule applies to the case; מִשְׁפָּטִים is the plural of "judgment" (v6, 8), of rulings "ordinances".
- **"ye shall even warn them that they trespass not against the LORD, and so wrath come upon you" → "you shall warn them, that they may not be guilty toward Yahweh and wrath be upon you"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהִזְהַרְתֶּם אֹתָם וְלֹא יֶאְשְׁמוּ לַיהוָה וְהָיָה־קֶצֶף, literally "and you shall warn them, and they shall not be guilty toward Yahweh, and wrath shall be upon you": the "not" runs on to the second clause in sense. אָשֵׁם "be guilty, incur guilt" (twice).
- **"Amariah the chief priest" → "Amariah the head priest"** (v11) — Hebrew כֹהֵן הָרֹאשׁ. **"in all matters of the LORD … for all the king's matters" → "for every matter of Yahweh … for every matter of the king"** — Hebrew לְכֹל דְּבַר, "word, matter". **"the ruler of the house of Judah" → "the leader"** — Hebrew הַנָּגִיד. **"the Levites shall be officers before you" → "the Levites are officers before you"** — Hebrew וְשֹׁטְרִים הַלְוִיִּם לִפְנֵיכֶם, no verb; שֹׁטֵר is a lower official who keeps order and lists.
- **"Deal courageously" → "Be strong and do"** (v11) — Hebrew חִזְקוּ וַעֲשׂוּ (1 Chr 28:10, 20). **"the LORD shall be with the good" → "may Yahweh be with the good"** — Hebrew וִיהִי, a wish (jussive); הַטּוֹב "the good" may be the good man or what is good.

### Notes
- **Unique to Chronicler** — no parallel in Kings. Jehoshaphat's judicial reforms, oracle of Jehu, national judicial system.
- **Jehu son of Hanani** — son of the seer who rebuked Asa (ch 16). Prophetic family, consistent ministry.
- **Judicial reform** — fortified cities get local judges; Jerusalem gets appellate Levite-priest-elder panel. Two-tier system.
- **Bipartite oversight** (v11) — Amariah (religious cases) and Zebadiah (civil/royal cases). Separation of domains.
- **"No lifting up of faces, no bribe"** — classic Deuteronomic judicial virtues (Deut 10:17, 16:19).
- **Chronicler's educational project** — Jehoshaphat = teaching king, judicial king. Models post-exilic theocratic governance structure.
