# 2 Chronicles 24

[1] ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ was seven years old when he began to reign, and forty years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦zibiah|Tzivyah|Zibiah⟧ of ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧.

[2] And ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ all the days of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest.

[3] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ took for him two wives, and he begot sons and daughters.

[4] And it came to pass after this that it was with the heart of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ to renew the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] And he gathered the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and said to them, "Go out to the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and gather from all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ silver to repair the house of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, year by year, and you, you shall hasten the matter." But the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ did not hasten.

[6] And the king called ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the head and said to him, "Why have you not required of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ to bring from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ the levy of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and of the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for the tent of the testimony?"

[7] For ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ the wicked woman, her sons had breached the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and also all the holy things of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they had made for the Baals.

[8] And the king said, and they made a chest and placed it in the gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, outside.

[9] And they gave a voice in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to bring to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the levy of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ upon ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the wilderness.

[10] And all the princes and all the people rejoiced, and they brought and threw into the chest until they had finished.

[11] And it came to pass, at the time when one would bring the chest to the oversight of the king by the hand of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and when they saw that the silver was much, that the scribe of the king and the overseer of the head priest would come and empty the chest and lift it up and return it to its place. Thus they did day by day, and they gathered silver in abundance.

[12] And the king and ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ gave it to the doer of the work of the service of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they were hiring hewers and craftsmen to renew the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and also to craftsmen in iron and bronze, to repair the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And the doers of the work worked, and healing went up for the work in their hand, and they made the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ stand upon its measure, and they strengthened it.

[14] And when they had finished, they brought before the king and ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the rest of the silver, and he made it into utensils for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, utensils of ministering and of offering up, and pans, and utensils of gold and silver. And they were offering up burnt offerings in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ continually, all the days of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧.

[15] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ grew old and was sated with days, and he died; he was a hundred and thirty years old at his death.

[16] And they buried him in the city of David with the kings, for he had done good in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and his house.

[17] And after the death of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the princes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ came and bowed down to the king; then the king listened to them.

[18] And they forsook the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers and served the Asherim and the idols, and there was wrath upon ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ for this guilt of theirs.

[19] And he sent prophets among them to bring them back to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they warned them, and they did not give ear.

[20] And the spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ clothed ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest, and he stood above the people and said to them, "Thus says ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: 'Why are you overstepping the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧? And you shall not prosper. For you have forsaken ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he has forsaken you.'"

[21] And they conspired against him and stoned him with stone by the commandment of the king, in the court of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] And ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ the king did not remember the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ that ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ his father had done with him, and he killed his son. And as he died he said, "May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ see and require."

[23] And it came to pass, at the turn of the year, that the force of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ came up against him, and they came to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and destroyed all the princes of the people from among the people, and all their spoil they sent to the king of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧.

[24] For with a small number of men the force of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ had come, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave into their hand a force in very great abundance, for they had forsaken ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers. And on ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ they did judgments.

[25] And when they went from him, for they left him in many sicknesses, his servants conspired against him for the blood of the sons of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest, and they killed him upon his bed, and he died. And they buried him in the city of David, and they did not bury him in the graves of the kings.

[26] And these are those who conspired against him: ⟦zabad|Zavad|Zabad⟧ the son of ⟦shimeath|Shimat|Shimeath⟧ the ⟦ammonites|Ammonit|Ammonitess⟧, and ⟦jehozabad|Yehozavad|Jehozabad⟧ the son of ⟦shimrith|Shimrit|Shimrith⟧ the ⟦moabites|Moaviyah|Moabitess⟧.

[27] And his sons, and how the burden upon him multiplied, and the founding of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, behold, they are written in the ⟦midrash|Midrash|Story⟧ of the book of the kings. And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים; with the article in v7, 9, 13, 16, 20, 27 (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"money" → "silver"** (v5, 11, 14) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף.
- **"Joash was seven years old when he began to reign"** (v1) — Hebrew בֶּן־שֶׁבַע שָׁנִים "a son of seven years"; מָלַךְ "reigned", here of the beginning. 2 Kgs 12:1 opens otherwise: "In the seventh year of Jehu, Jehoash began to reign"; from "and forty years" on the two verses are the same. Chronicles writes the short name יוֹאָשׁ "Joash" throughout.
- **"all the days of Jehoiada the priest"** (v2) — Hebrew כָּל־יְמֵי יְהוֹיָדָע הַכֹּהֵן. 2 Kgs 12:2 has "all his days, because Jehoiada the priest taught him".
- **"took for him two wives; and he begat" → "took for him two wives, and he begot"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּשָּׂא־לוֹ … נָשִׁים, literally "lifted up for him wives"; הוֹלִיד "beget". Who begot, Joash or Jehoiada, the Hebrew does not say.
- **"Joash was minded to repair" → "it was with the heart of Joash to renew"** (v4) — Hebrew הָיָה עִם־לֵב יוֹאָשׁ לְחַדֵּשׁ. חִדֵּשׁ (from חָדָשׁ "new"; also v12) is "renew"; "repair" is kept for חִזֵּק (v5, 12), as in 2 Kgs 12.
- **"gather of all Israel money to repair … from year to year, and see that ye hasten the matter. Howbeit the Levites hastened it not" → "gather from all Israel silver to repair … year by year, and you, you shall hasten the matter. But the Levites did not hasten"** (v5) — Hebrew קָבַץ "gather" twice (אָסַף in v11 has the same English); חִזֵּק, literally "make strong", of a building "repair"; מִדֵּי שָׁנָה בְּשָׁנָה, literally "as often as a year in a year"; וְאַתֶּם תְּמַהֲרוּ לַדָּבָר with the pronoun stressed; וְלֹא מִהֲרוּ has no object.
- **"Jehoiada the chief … Why hast thou not required of the Levites to bring in … the collection, according to the commandment of Moses … and of the congregation of Israel, for the tabernacle of witness?" → "Jehoiada the head … Why have you not required of the Levites to bring … the levy of Moses the servant of Yahweh and of the assembly of Israel, for the tent of the testimony?"** (v6) — Hebrew הָרֹאשׁ "the head"; דָּרַשׁ עַל, the verb "seek", here "require of" (as of a reckoning; the same verb in v22). מַשְׂאַת מֹשֶׁה "the levy of Moses": מַשְׂאֵת is "what is lifted / laid on", a contribution (commonly referred to Exod 30:12–16; the Hebrew has no "according to the commandment"). וְהַקָּהָל לְיִשְׂרָאֵל is literally "and the assembly, for / of Israel": taken here as a second genitive, "and (the levy) of the assembly of Israel"; "of" is supplied, and the words can also be read "and (which) the assembly (laid) upon Israel". Uncertain.
- **"the sons of Athaliah, that wicked woman, had broken up the house of God; and also all the dedicated things … did they bestow upon Baalim" → "Athaliah the wicked woman, her sons had breached the house of Elohim, and also all the holy things … they had made for the Baals"** (v7) — the Hebrew sets "Athaliah" first and takes it up with "her sons". הַמִּרְשַׁעַת, a noun found only here, "the wickedness" in person. פָּרַץ "breach, break into". קָדְשֵׁי "holy things". עָשׂוּ לַבְּעָלִים "they made for the Baals": used them for the Baals, or made them into things for the Baals.
- **"at the king's commandment they made a chest, and set it without at the gate" → "the king said, and they made a chest and placed it in the gate … outside"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר הַמֶּלֶךְ "and the king said"; אֲרוֹן אֶחָד "one chest"; נָתַן "place" (literally "give"), as 2 Kgs 12:9. There Jehoiada takes the chest, bores a hole in it and places it beside the altar.
- **"they made a proclamation through Judah … the collection that Moses the servant of God laid upon Israel" → "they gave a voice in Judah … the levy of Moses the servant of Elohim upon Israel"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנוּ־קוֹל "gave a voice": had it proclaimed. The Hebrew has no verb "laid".
- **"princes"** (v10, 17, 23) — Hebrew שָׂרִים.
- **"cast into the chest, until they had made an end" → "threw into the chest until they had finished"** (v10) — Hebrew עַד־לְכַלֵּה "until finishing": until all had given, or until it was full.
- **"at what time the chest was brought unto the king's office … the high priest's officer came and emptied the chest, and took it, and carried it to his place again" → "at the time when one would bring the chest to the oversight of the king … the overseer of the head priest would come and empty the chest and lift it up and return it to its place"** (v11) — Hebrew יָבִיא "one brings" (no subject named); פְּקֻדַּת הַמֶּלֶךְ "the oversight of the king": those whom the king set over it; פְּקִיד "overseer", one root; כֹּהֵן הָרֹאשׁ "the head priest" (2 Kgs 12:10 has "the great priest"). The verbs tell what was done each time. לָרֹב "in abundance".
- **"such as did the work … hired masons and carpenters to repair … and also such as wrought iron and brass to mend" → "the doer of the work … hiring hewers and craftsmen to renew … and also to craftsmen in iron and bronze, to repair"** (v12) — Hebrew עוֹשֵׂה is singular as printed (plural in v13); מְלֶאכֶת עֲבוֹדַת "the work of the service", as 1 Chr 28:20. חֹצְבִים "hewers" (of stone), חָרָשִׁים "craftsmen"; לְחַדֵּשׁ "to renew" beside לְחַזֵּק "to repair". וְגַם לְחָרָשֵׁי is "and also to / for craftsmen of iron and bronze".
- **"So the workmen wrought, and the work was perfected by them, and they set the house of God in his state" → "the doers of the work worked, and healing went up for the work in their hand, and they made the house of Elohim stand upon its measure"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲשׂוּ עֹשֵׂי הַמְּלָאכָה, verb and noun of one root ("the doers did"). וַתַּעַל אֲרוּכָה לַמְּלָאכָה: אֲרוּכָה is the new flesh that grows over a wound (Jer 8:22; 30:17; of walls Neh 4:7): the repair went forward. מַתְכֻּנְתּוֹ, from תכן "measure, fix": its fixed measure or plan ("amount" in Exod 5:8 would be wrong of a building). אִמֵּץ "strengthen".
- **"whereof were made vessels … vessels to minister, and to offer withal, and spoons" → "he made it into utensils … utensils of ministering and of offering up, and pans"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲשֵׂהוּ "and he made it" (singular; who is not said); כְּלִי "utensil, vessel"; כְּלֵי שָׁרֵת וְהַעֲלוֹת "utensils of ministering and of offering up"; כַּפּוֹת "pans" (literally "palms"). תָּמִיד "continually". 2 Kgs 12:13 says that no utensils were made from the silver brought to the house.
- **"waxed old, and was full of days when he died; an hundred and thirty years old was he" → "grew old and was sated with days, and he died; he was a hundred and thirty years old at his death"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂבַּע יָמִים; בֶּן־מֵאָה וּשְׁלֹשִׁים שָׁנָה בְּמוֹתוֹ.
- **"among the kings, because he had done good in Israel, both toward God, and toward his house" → "with the kings, for he had done good in Israel, and with Elohim and his house"** (v16) — Hebrew עִם "with" both times.
- **"made obeisance to the king. Then the king hearkened unto them" → "bowed down to the king; then the king listened to them"** (v17) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה; שָׁמַע אֶל.
- **"they left the house … served groves and idols: and wrath came upon … for this their trespass" → "they forsook the house … served the Asherim and the idols, and there was wrath upon … for this guilt of theirs"** (v18) — Hebrew עָזַב "forsake" (one word in v18, 20, 24; "left" in v25); אֲשֵׁרִים, the wooden objects of worship named after the goddess Asherah; עֲצַבִּים "idols"; קֶצֶף "wrath"; אַשְׁמָה "guilt".
- **"Yet he sent prophets to them … and they testified against them: but they would not give ear" → "And he sent prophets among them … and they warned them, and they did not give ear"** (v19) — the Hebrew names no subject for "sent"; הֵעִיד בְּ "warn", literally "testify against"; הֶאֱזִין "give ear".
- **"the Spirit of God came upon Zechariah" → "the spirit of Elohim clothed Zechariah"** (v20) — Hebrew לָבְשָׁה אֶת, literally "put on Zechariah" (as Judg 6:34; 1 Chr 12:18). **"which stood above the people"** — Hebrew מֵעַל לָעָם. **"Why transgress ye the commandments of the LORD, that ye cannot prosper? because ye have forsaken the LORD, he hath also forsaken you" → "Why are you overstepping the commandments of Yahweh? And you shall not prosper. For you have forsaken Yahweh, and he has forsaken you"** — Hebrew עֹבְרִים "passing over" (as Num 14:41, the same two clauses); וְלֹא תַצְלִיחוּ "and you shall not prosper", joined by "and"; no "also" in the Hebrew.
- **"stoned him with stones at the commandment of the king" → "stoned him with stone by the commandment of the king"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיִּרְגְּמֻהוּ אֶבֶן, singular, as Lev 20:2; קָשַׁר עַל "conspire against" (also v25, 26).
- **"the kindness which Jehoiada his father had done to him … The LORD look upon it, and require it" → "the *chesed* that Jehoiada his father had done with him … May Yahweh see and require"** (v22) — Hebrew הַחֶסֶד אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה … עִמּוֹ; "his father" is the father of the one killed. יֵרֶא יְהוָה וְיִדְרֹשׁ: two wishes (jussive) without an object; דָּרַשׁ "seek", here "require" as of blood (Gen 9:5); the verb of v6.
- **"at the end of the year, that the host of Syria came up" → "at the turn of the year, that the force of Aram came up"** (v23) — Hebrew לִתְקוּפַת הַשָּׁנָה (as Exod 34:22); חֵיל אֲרָם: חַיִל "force", of an army. **"destroyed all the princes of the people from among the people"** — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁחִיתוּ … מֵעָם. Damascus is written דַּרְמֶשֶׂק here, the later spelling. 2 Kgs 12:17–18 tells of Hazael's coming otherwise: Jehoash buys him off with the holy things and the gold.
- **"the army of the Syrians came with a small company of men … a very great host … So they executed judgment against Joash" → "with a small number of men the force of Aram had come … a force in very great abundance … And on Joash they did judgments"** (v24) — Hebrew בְּמִצְעַר אֲנָשִׁים "with a smallness of men"; אֲרָם is "Aram", the name of the people and land, not "Arameans"; חַיִל לָרֹב מְאֹד; עָשׂוּ שְׁפָטִים "did judgments" (plural, as Exod 12:12), with אֶת־יוֹאָשׁ "with / on Joash".
- **"when they were departed from him, (for they left him in great diseases,)" → "when they went from him, for they left him in many sicknesses"** (v25) — Hebrew עָזְבוּ, the verb "forsake" of v18, 20, 24; qere בְּמַחֲלוּיִם, a form found only here, from חָלָה "be sick": sicknesses, or the sufferings of wounds; the ketiv במחליים is another spelling of the same. **"for the blood of the sons of Jehoiada"** — Hebrew בִּדְמֵי בְּנֵי, "sons" plural as printed; the Greek and Latin have "son" (from memory). **"slew him on his bed … sepulchres of the kings" → "killed him upon his bed … graves of the kings"** — Hebrew הָרַג; קִבְרוֹת. 2 Kgs 12:20–21 has him struck down at Beth-millo and buried "with his fathers in the city of David".
- **"Zabad the son of Shimeath an Ammonitess, and Jehozabad the son of Shimrith a Moabitess"** (v26) — as printed; 2 Kgs 12:21 has "Jozabad the son of Shimeath and Jehozabad the son of Shomer".
- **"Now concerning his sons, and the greatness of the burdens laid upon him, and the repairing of the house of God … in the story of the book of the kings" → "And his sons, and how the burden upon him multiplied, and the founding of the house of Elohim … in the midrash of the book of the kings"** (v27) — the qere יִרֶב is a verb, "(the burden upon him) multiplied"; "how" is supplied to carry it in the list. The ketiv ורב is the noun "and the multitude of the burden upon him". הַמַּשָּׂא "the burden": either what was laid on him (the levy, v6, 9, or tribute) or the prophets' utterances against him (v19: "oracle", the other sense of the word); not decided. יְסוֹד "founding" (root יסד "found"): the restoring of the house. מִדְרַשׁ סֵפֶר הַמְּלָכִים "the midrash of the book of the kings": מִדְרָשׁ, from דָּרַשׁ "seek", is found only here and in 13:22: a searching out, a written study. הִנָּם כְּתוּבִים "behold, they are written". 2 Kgs 12:19 names "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 12** — the Chronicler tells much that Kings does not have: Jehoiada's age and burial, the forsaking of the house after his death, the killing of Zechariah, the defeat by Aram as judgment, the reason for the conspiracy. Kings alone has the high places (12:3), the first plan with the priests (12:4–8) and the payment to Hazael (12:18).
- **Jehoiada's burial with the kings** (v16) — an exceptional honor for a priest; Joash himself is not buried in the graves of the kings (v25).
- **Zechariah son of Jehoiada** — commonly identified with the Zechariah of Matt 23:35 and Luke 11:51 ("from the blood of Abel to the blood of Zechariah"); Matthew calls him "son of Barachiah". In the Hebrew order of the books Chronicles stands last, so Abel and Zechariah are the first and the last killing told.
- **Stoning in the court of the house** (v21) — hence the dying words "May Yahweh see and require", which v25 takes up ("for the blood of the sons of Jehoiada").
- **Chronicles' own words** — "forsake" (v18, 20, 24, and "left" v25) and "seek / require" (v6, 22) carry the chapter: as they forsook Yahweh, he forsook them (v20).
