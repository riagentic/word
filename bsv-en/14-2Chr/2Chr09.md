# 2 Chronicles 9

[1] And the queen of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ heard the report of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, and she came to test ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ with riddles in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, with a very heavy force, and camels carrying spices and gold in abundance and precious stone. And she came to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and spoke with him all that was with her heart.

[2] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ told her all her words; and there was not a word hidden from ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ that he did not tell her.

[3] And the queen of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ saw the wisdom of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, and the house that he had built,

[4] and the food of his table, and the seating of his servants, and the standing of his ministers, and their clothing, and his cupbearers, and their clothing, and his upper room by which he would go up to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and there was no more spirit in her.

[5] And she said to the king, "The word is truth that I heard in my land about your words and about your wisdom.

[6] And I did not believe their words until I came and my eyes saw; and behold, the half of the abundance of your wisdom was not told me. You have added to the report that I heard.

[7] Happy are your men, and happy are these your servants, who stand before you continually and hear your wisdom.

[8] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be blessed, who delighted in you, to place you on his throne as king for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Because your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ loves ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to make it stand forever, he has placed you over them as king, to do judgment and righteousness."

[9] And she gave the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, and spices in very great abundance, and precious stone. And there was no such spice as that which the queen of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ gave to King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[10] And also the servants of ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ and the servants of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, who brought gold from ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧, brought algum wood and precious stone.

[11] And the king made of the algum wood raised ways for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for the house of the king, and lyres and harps for the singers. And the like of them had not been seen before in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[12] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ gave to the queen of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ all her delight that she asked, besides what she had brought to the king. And she turned about and went to her land, she and her servants.

[13] And the weight of the gold that came to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,

[14] apart from what the men who went about and the traders were bringing; and all the kings of ⟦arabia|Arav|Arabia⟧ and the governors of the land were bringing gold and silver to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[15] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made two hundred large shields of beaten gold; six hundred of beaten gold went up on the one large shield.

[16] And three hundred shields of beaten gold; three hundred of gold went up on the one shield. And the king put them in the House of the Forest of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧.

[17] And the king made a great throne of ivory and overlaid it with pure gold.

[18] And the throne had six steps, and a footstool in gold, fastened to the throne, and arms on this side and on that upon the place of the seat, and two lions standing beside the arms.

[19] And twelve lions were standing there on the six steps, on this side and on that. The like was not made for any kingdom.

[20] And all the drinking vessels of King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ were gold, and all the vessels of the House of the Forest of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ were refined gold. Silver was not reckoned as anything in the days of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[21] For the king had ships going to Tarshish with the servants of ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧. Once in three years the ships of Tarshish would come, carrying gold and silver, ivory and apes and baboons.

[22] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ became greater than all the kings of the earth in riches and wisdom.

[23] And all the kings of the earth were seeking the face of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, to hear his wisdom, which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had put in his heart.

[24] And they were bringing, each man his offering, vessels of silver and vessels of gold and garments, weapons and spices, horses and mules, the matter of a year in a year.

[25] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had four thousand stalls of horses and chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen; and he placed them in the cities of the chariots and with the king in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[26] And he was ruling over all the kings from the River and to the land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and to the border of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[27] And the king made the silver in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ like the stones, and the cedars he made like the sycamores that are in the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧, for abundance.

[28] And they were bringing out horses from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ for ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, and from all the lands.

[29] And the remainder of the acts of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, the first and the last, are they not written in the words of ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ the prophet, and in the prophecy of ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧ the ⟦shilonite|Shiloni|Shilonite⟧, and in the visions of Iddo the seer concerning ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧?

[30] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ forty years.

[31] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ lay with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David his father, and ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v23 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **The parallel in Kings** (throughout) — v1–28 run beside 1 Kgs 10 and v29–31 beside 1 Kgs 11:41–43. Where the Hebrew of the two books is the same, the wording is that of Kings; where Chronicles has another word, form, number or order, the verse follows Chronicles. The differences that touch the meaning are named below; nothing is taken over from Kings.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when the queen of Sheba heard of the fame of Solomon" → "the queen of Sheba heard the report of Solomon"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׁמְעָה אֶת־שֵׁמַע שְׁלֹמֹה: a perfect, "heard" (Kings has the participle, "was hearing", and adds "for the name of Yahweh"). **"to prove Solomon with hard questions" → "to test Solomon with riddles"** — Hebrew נִסָּה; חִידוֹת.
- **"a very great company" → "a very heavy force"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּחַיִל כָּבֵד מְאֹד. **"gold in abundance"** — Hebrew לָרֹב (Kings: "very much gold"). **"precious stones" → "precious stone"** (v1, 9, 10) — singular in the Hebrew, of a kind. **"communed with him of all that was in her heart" → "spoke with him all that was with her heart"** — Hebrew עִמּוֹ "with him" (Kings: "to him"); עִם־לְבָבָהּ.
- **"told her all her questions: and there was nothing hid from Solomon which he told her not" → "told her all her words; and there was not a word hidden from Solomon that he did not tell her"** (v2) — Hebrew דְּבָרֶיהָ / דָּבָר, "word" or "matter". Chronicles has "from Solomon" (Kings: "from the king").
- **"the sitting of his servants, and the attendance of his ministers, and their apparel; his cupbearers also, and their apparel" → "the seating of his servants, and the standing of his ministers, and their clothing, and his cupbearers, and their clothing"** (v4) — Hebrew מוֹשַׁב, מַעֲמַד; מַשְׁקָיו may also be "his drinks". "And their clothing" stands twice in Chronicles, once in Kings.
- **"his ascent by which he went up into the house of the LORD" → "his upper room by which he would go up to the house of Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew וַעֲלִיָּתוֹ אֲשֶׁר יַעֲלֶה בֵּית יְהוָה: עֲלִיָּה is an upper room (Judg 3:20; 1 Kgs 17:19); with the verb "go up" it is commonly understood as a way up, "his ascent". Kings has וְעֹלָתוֹ "his burnt offering that he would offer up"; each is translated as printed. **"there was no more spirit in her"** — Hebrew רוּחַ, "spirit / breath".
- **"It was a true report which I heard" → "The word is truth that I heard"** (v5) — Hebrew אֱמֶת הַדָּבָר, without a verb (Kings: "was truth"). **"of thine acts" → "about your words"** — Hebrew דְּבָרֶיךָ, "words" or "matters".
- **"I believed not their words … the one half of the greatness of thy wisdom was not told me: for thou exceedest the fame that I heard" → "I did not believe their words … the half of the abundance of your wisdom was not told me. You have added to the report that I heard"** (v6) — Hebrew לְדִבְרֵיהֶם "their words" (Kings: "the words"); חֲצִי מַרְבִּית חָכְמָתֶךָ: מַרְבִּית is "increase, the greater part" (Lev 25:37; 1 Chr 12:29), here the greatness or abundance; Kings has only "the half". יָסַפְתָּ עַל־הַשְּׁמוּעָה (Kings: "you have added wisdom and good to the report").
- **"Blessed be the LORD thy God" → "May Yahweh your Elohim be blessed"** (v8) — Hebrew יְהִי … בָּרוּךְ, a wish with its verb.
- **"to set thee on his throne, to be king for the LORD thy God: because thy God loved Israel, to establish them for ever, therefore made he thee king over them" → "to place you on his throne as king for Yahweh your Elohim. Because your Elohim loves Israel, to make it stand forever, he has placed you over them as king"** (v8) — Hebrew לְתִתְּךָ עַל־כִּסְאוֹ לְמֶלֶךְ לַיהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ: "his throne" is the throne of Yahweh (Kings: "the throne of Israel"; 1 Chr 29:23). בְּאַהֲבַת אֱלֹהֶיךָ אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל לְהַעֲמִידוֹ לְעוֹלָם, literally "in your Elohim's loving Israel, to make him stand forever" ("him" is Israel; "to make it stand" is only in Chronicles). וַיִּתֶּנְךָ, "gave you", the verb of "to place you" (Kings: "set you"). **"judgment and justice" → "judgment and righteousness"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה.
- **"of spices great abundance … neither was there any such spice" → "spices in very great abundance … And there was no such spice"** (v9) — Hebrew לָרֹב מְאֹד; וְלֹא הָיָה כַּבֹּשֶׂם הַהוּא (Kings: "there came no more such spice for abundance").
- **"the servants also of Huram, and the servants of Solomon, which brought gold from Ophir, brought algum trees" → "also the servants of Churam and the servants of Solomon, who brought gold from Ophir, brought algum wood"** (v10) — "Churam" is the reading tradition (qere); the written form is "Chiram", the name as Kings has it. Kings has "the fleet of Hiram, which carried gold". עֲצֵי אַלְגּוּמִּים: the tree is not known; Kings writes אַלְמֻגִּים "almug".
- **"terraces to the house of the LORD" → "raised ways for the house of Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew מְסִלּוֹת, the word for a raised road, a "highway" (Num 20:19); what was made of the wood is not known (Kings has מִסְעָד "a support"). "Highways" would say roads through the land, so the sense of the word is given. **"harps and psalteries" → "lyres and harps"** — Hebrew כִּנּוֹר and נֵבֶל. **"there were none such seen before in the land of Judah" → "the like of them had not been seen before in the land of Judah"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־נִרְאוּ כָהֵם לְפָנִים בְּאֶרֶץ יְהוּדָה (Kings: "Such almug wood has not come and has not been seen, to this day").
- **"all her desire, whatsoever she asked, beside that which she had brought unto the king. So she turned" → "all her delight that she asked, besides what she had brought to the king. And she turned about"** (v12) — Hebrew חֶפְצָהּ; מִלְּבַד אֲשֶׁר־הֵבִיאָה אֶל־הַמֶּלֶךְ, word for word (Kings: "besides what he gave her according to the hand of King Solomon"); וַתַּהֲפֹךְ "turned about" (Kings has another verb, "turned").
- **"six hundred and threescore and six" → "six hundred and sixty-six"** (v13).
- **"Beside that which chapmen and merchants brought" → "apart from what the men who went about and the traders were bringing"** (v14) — Hebrew לְבַד מֵאַנְשֵׁי הַתָּרִים וְהַסֹּחֲרִים מְבִיאִים, literally "apart from the men of those who explore and the traders, bringing"; "what" is supplied. תָּרִים is the participle of תּוּר "explore, go about". Kings has "the trade of the merchants" and no "bringing".
- **"all the kings of Arabia" kept** (v14) — Hebrew מַלְכֵי עֲרַב, "Arabia", the word of Chronicles; 1 Kgs 10:15 points the same letters הָעֶרֶב, "the mixed people". Each book is translated as it is printed. "Were bringing gold and silver to Solomon" stands only in Chronicles.
- **"targets" / "shields" → "large shields" / "shields"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew צִנָּה and מָגֵן. **"beaten gold"** — Hebrew זָהָב שָׁחוּט; the meaning of שָׁחוּט is not certain. **"six hundred shekels of beaten gold went to one target … three hundred shekels of gold" → "six hundred of beaten gold went up on the one large shield … three hundred of gold"** — the Hebrew names no weight; Kings has "three minas of gold" for the shield.
- **"overlaid it with pure gold" kept** (v17) — Hebrew זָהָב טָהוֹר (Kings: זָהָב מוּפָז "fine gold"). "Ivory" is שֵׁן, literally "tooth".
- **"with a footstool of gold, which were fastened to the throne, and stays on each side of the sitting place" → "and a footstool in gold, fastened to the throne, and arms on this side and on that upon the place of the seat"** (v18) — Hebrew וְכֶבֶשׁ בַּזָּהָב לַכִּסֵּא מָאֳחָזִים: כֶּבֶשׁ in this sense is found only here ("footstool"; uncertain), and "fastened" is plural, of the steps and the footstool. Kings has "the throne had a round top behind it". יָדוֹת "arms" is literally "hands"; עַל "upon" (Kings: "at").
- **"pure gold: none were of silver; it was not any thing accounted of" → "refined gold. Silver was not reckoned as anything"** (v20) — Hebrew זָהָב סָגוּר, literally "shut-up gold" (uncertain: pure, solid or stored gold); אֵין כֶּסֶף נֶחְשָׁב … לִמְאוּמָה (Kings: "There was no silver; it was not reckoned").
- **"the king's ships went to Tarshish with the servants of Huram: every three years once came the ships of Tarshish bringing" → "the king had ships going to Tarshish with the servants of Churam. Once in three years the ships of Tarshish would come, carrying"** (v21) — Hebrew אֳנִיּוֹת לַמֶּלֶךְ הֹלְכוֹת תַּרְשִׁישׁ. Kings has "a fleet of Tarshish on the sea with the fleet of Hiram"; Chronicles says that the ships went to Tarshish.
- **"ivory, and apes, and peacocks" → "ivory and apes and baboons"** (v21) — Hebrew שֶׁנְהַבִּים וְקוֹפִים וְתוּכִּיִּים; the last is found only here and in 1 Kgs 10:22, and its meaning is not known: "baboons" or "peacocks".
- **"passed all the kings" → "became greater than all the kings"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּגְדַּל … מִכֹּל.
- **"all the kings of the earth sought the presence of Solomon" → "all the kings of the earth were seeking the face of Solomon"** (v23) — Hebrew מְבַקְשִׁים אֶת־פְּנֵי. Chronicles has "all the kings of the earth" (Kings: "all the earth").
- **"his present" → "his offering"** (v24) — Hebrew מִנְחָה, a gift brought to a superior. **"raiment, harness" → "garments, weapons"** — Hebrew שְׂלָמוֹת נֵשֶׁק, without "and" between them (Kings has it). **"a rate year by year" → "the matter of a year in a year"** — Hebrew דְּבַר־שָׁנָה בְּשָׁנָה: what was due each year.
- **"four thousand stalls for horses and chariots … whom he bestowed" → "four thousand stalls of horses and chariots … and he placed them"** (v25) — Hebrew אֻרְיוֹת "stalls" (or teams); וַיַּנִּיחֵם "placed them", as 1:14 (1 Kgs 10:26 is pointed "led them"). Kings has here "a thousand four hundred chariots"; four thousand stalls: compare 1 Kgs 4:26, which has forty thousand. Each number is as printed.
- **"he reigned over all the kings from the river" → "he was ruling over all the kings from the River"** (v26) — Hebrew מוֹשֵׁל, a participle of מָשַׁל "rule"; הַנָּהָר "the River" is the Euphrates, which the Hebrew does not name. The verse has its parallel in 1 Kgs 4:21.
- **"made silver in Jerusalem as stones, and cedar trees made he as the sycomore trees that are in the low plains in abundance" → "made the silver in Jerusalem like the stones, and the cedars he made like the sycamores that are in the Shephelah, for abundance"** (v27) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "gave"; שְׁפֵלָה, the lowland.
- **"they brought unto Solomon horses out of Egypt, and out of all lands" → "they were bringing out horses from Egypt for Solomon, and from all the lands"** (v28) — Hebrew וּמוֹצִיאִים, a participle of "bring out". Kings (10:28–29) has more: Kue, and the prices.
- **"the rest of the acts of Solomon, first and last" → "the remainder of the acts of Solomon, the first and the last"** (v29) — Hebrew וּשְׁאָר, another word than the יֶתֶר "rest" of the formula in Kings; דִּבְרֵי is literally "the words / matters of".
- **"in the book of Nathan the prophet" → "in the words of Nathan the prophet"** (v29) — Hebrew עַל־דִּבְרֵי, the same word as "acts" (1 Chr 29:29); the Hebrew has no "book". Kings names "the book of the acts of Solomon".
- **"the visions of Iddo the seer against Jeroboam" → "the visions of Iddo the seer concerning Jeroboam"** (v29) — Hebrew עַל "concerning" (or "against"). חֹזֶה is "seer", literally "one who gazes". The name is printed יֶעְדּוֹ, "Yedo" (qere; the written form is "Yedi"); he is commonly taken to be the Iddo (עִדּוֹ) of 12:15; 13:22, and the name is given in that form.
- **"slept with his fathers, and he was buried" → "lay with his fathers, and they buried him"** (v31) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכַּב; וַיִּקְבְּרֻהוּ, active and plural (Kings: "was buried").

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 10; 11:41–43** — Chronicles does not have 1 Kgs 11:1–40.
- **The queen of Sheba** — compare Matt 12:42.
- **"His throne"** (v8) — the throne on which Solomon sits is called the throne of Yahweh, as 1 Chr 28:5; 29:23.
- **The numbers** (v13, 25) — 666 talents of gold in one year; 4,000 stalls and 12,000 horsemen.
- **The three writings** (v29) — the words of Nathan, the prophecy of Ahijah, the visions of Iddo.
