# 2 Chronicles 28

[1] ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ was twenty years old when he began to reign, and sixteen years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And he did not do what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, like David his father.

[2] And he walked in the ways of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and also molten images he made for the Baals.

[3] And he made smoke in the valley of the son of Hinnom, and he burned his sons in the fire, according to the abominations of the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dispossessed from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] And he sacrificed and made smoke on the high places and on the hills and under every luxuriant tree.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave him into the hand of the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, and they struck him and took captive from him a great captivity and brought them to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧. And also into the hand of the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he was given, and he struck him a great blow.

[6] And ⟦pekah|Peqach|Pekah⟧ the son of ⟦remaliah|Remalyahu|Remaliah⟧ killed in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ a hundred and twenty thousand in one day, all of them sons of ability, because they had forsaken ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers.

[7] And Zichri, a mighty one of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, killed ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ the son of the king and ⟦azrikam|Azriqam|Azrikam⟧ the leader of the house and ⟦elkanah|Elqanah|Elkanah⟧ the second to the king.

[8] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ took captive from their brothers two hundred thousand, women, sons and daughters, and also much spoil they plundered from them, and they brought the spoil to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[9] And a prophet of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was there, Oded his name, and he went out before the host that was coming to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and said to them, "Behold, in the fury of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers against ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ he gave them into your hand, and you have killed among them in a rage that has reached to the heavens.

[10] And now, the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ you mean to subdue as slaves and as maidservants for yourselves. Is there not with you yourselves only guilt against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[11] And now, hear me and return the captives whom you have taken captive from your brothers, for the burning anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is upon you."

[12] And men from the heads of the sons of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ rose up, ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the son of ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧, ⟦berechiah|Berechyah|Berechiah⟧ the son of ⟦meshillemoth|Meshillemot|Meshillemoth⟧, and ⟦jehizkiah|Yechizqiyahu|Jehizkiah⟧ the son of Shallum, and Amasa the son of ⟦hadlai|Chadlai|Hadlai⟧, against those who were coming from the host.

[13] And they said to them, "You shall not bring the captives here, for you mean a guilt against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to be upon us, adding to our sins and to our guilt; for we have much guilt, and burning anger is upon ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[14] And the armed men left the captives and the plunder before the princes and all the assembly.

[15] And the men who were designated by names rose up and took hold of the captives, and all their naked ones they clothed from the spoil, and they clothed them and gave them sandals and gave them to eat and gave them to drink and anointed them and guided them on donkeys, every one who stumbled, and brought them to ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, the city of palm trees, beside their brothers, and they returned to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[16] At that time King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ sent to the kings of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ to help him.

[17] And again ⟦edomites|Edomim|Edomites⟧ came and struck ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and took captives.

[18] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ made a raid in the cities of the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧ and of the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ that belonged to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they captured ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧ and ⟦aijalon|Ayyalon|Aijalon⟧ and the ⟦gederoth|Gederot|Gederoth⟧ and ⟦soco|Socho|Soco⟧ and its daughters and Timnah and its daughters and Gimzo and its daughters, and they dwelt there.

[19] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ low because of ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for he had let loose in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and had committed unfaithfulness against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[20] And ⟦tiglath-pileser|Tillegat-Pilneser|Tiglath-pilneser⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ came against him and distressed him and did not strengthen him.

[21] For ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ portioned out the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the house of the king and of the princes and gave to the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, and it was not for help to him.

[22] And in the time of his distress he acted yet more unfaithfully against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he, King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧.

[23] And he sacrificed to the gods of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ who were striking him, and he said, "Because the gods of the kings of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, they are helping them, to them I will sacrifice, and they will help me." And they were to him to make him stumble, and to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[24] And ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ gathered the utensils of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and cut in pieces the utensils of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he shut the doors of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and made for himself altars in every corner in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[25] And in every city of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ he made high places to make smoke to other gods, and he vexed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of his fathers.

[26] And the rest of his acts and all his ways, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[27] And ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ lay with his fathers, and they buried him in the city, in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for they did not bring him to the graves of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v24 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"was twenty years old"** (v1) — literally "a son of twenty years". "Began to reign" is the verb מָלַךְ "reign", here of the beginning.
- **"molten images"** (v2) — מַסֵּכוֹת, cast images.
- **"he burnt incense" → "he made smoke"** (v3, 4, 25) — הִקְטִיר / קִטֵּר with no object: the verb says only that he made sacrificial smoke; "burned incense" would decide for incense.
- **"burnt his children in the fire" → "burned his sons in the fire"** (v3) — וַיַּבְעֵר, "burned", and בָּנָיו "his sons", plural. 2 Kgs 16:3 has other words ("also his son he made pass through the fire"); each book is translated as it is printed.
- **"heathen" → "nations"**, **"cast out" → "dispossessed"** (v3) — הוֹרִישׁ.
- **"green tree" → "luxuriant tree"** (v4) — רַעֲנָן, full of leaves; not a colour.
- **"smote him" → "struck him"** (v5, 17, 23) — הִכָּה with בְּ, literally "struck at / among him".
- **"a great multitude of them captives" → "a great captivity"** (v5) — שִׁבְיָה גְדוֹלָה, the body of captives; the same word is "the captives" in v11, 13, 14, 15. "Them" after "brought" is supplied.
- **"smote him with a great slaughter" → "struck him a great blow"** (v5) — מַכָּה, the noun of "strike".
- **"valiant men" → "sons of ability"** (v6) — בְּנֵי־חָיִל.
- **"because they had forsaken"** (v6) — literally "in their forsaking".
- **"the governor of the house" → "the leader of the house"** (v7) — נָגִיד. **"next to the king" → "the second to the king"** — מִשְׁנֶה.
- **"the army" / "the war" → "the host"** (v9, 12) — צָבָא both times.
- **"in wrath" → "in the fury"**, **"in a rage"** (v9) — חֵמָה "fury" (literally "heat"); זַעַף "rage, storming".
- **"ye purpose to keep under … for bondmen and bondwomen" → "you mean to subdue as slaves and as maidservants"** (v10) — אֹמְרִים "saying" = meaning to; כָּבַשׁ "subdue"; עֲבָדִים, שְׁפָחוֹת.
- **"are there not with you, even with you, sins" → "Is there not with you yourselves only guilt"** (v10) — literally "Is it not only you, with you, guilts toward Yahweh your Elohim?"; אֲשָׁמוֹת is the plural of "guilt"; רַק "only" may also go with "you".
- **"deliver the captives again" → "return the captives"** (v11) — הֵשִׁיב. **"fierce wrath" → "burning anger"** (v11, 13) — חֲרוֹן אַף, literally "the burning of the nose".
- **v13** — literally "for to a guilt of Yahweh upon us you are saying, to add upon our sins and upon our guilt"; "to be" is supplied. "Trespass" of the KJV is the same word אַשְׁמָה "guilt", three times in the verse.
- **"designated by names"** (v15) — נִקְּבוּ בְשֵׁמוֹת. **"took hold of"** — הֶחֱזִיק בְּ. **"anointed"** — סוּךְ, rubbing with oil; not the verb מָשַׁח. **"carried all the feeble of them upon asses" → "guided them on donkeys, every one who stumbled"** — נֵהֵל "guide"; כּוֹשֵׁל "stumbling". **"to their brethren" → "beside their brothers"** — אֵצֶל.
- **"the kings of Assyria"** (v16) — plural in the Masoretic Text; the Septuagint has the singular (from memory).
- **"had invaded" → "made a raid in"** (v18) — פָּשַׁט בְּ. **"low country" → "Shephelah"**, **"south" → "Negev"**. **"with the villages thereof" → "and its daughters"** — בְּנוֹתֶיהָ, the settlements that belong to a city.
- **"because of Ahaz"** (v19) — בַּעֲבוּר, elsewhere "for the sake of"; here it names the cause.
- **"king of Israel"** (v19) — so the Hebrew, of a king of Judah; kept.
- **"he made Judah naked" → "he had let loose in Judah"** (v19) — הִפְרִיעַ בִּיהוּדָה, hifil of פָּרַע "let loose": he let restraint go in Judah. No object stands in the Hebrew.
- **"transgressed sore" → "had committed unfaithfulness"** (v19) — מָעוֹל מַעַל, verb and noun of one root; **"trespass yet more" → "acted yet more unfaithfully"** (v22) — literally "he added to act unfaithfully". מָעַל is "act unfaithfully" through Chronicles.
- **"Tilgath-pilneser"** (v20) — the name as Chronicles spells it (Kings: Tiglath-pileser). **"distressed him, but strengthened him not" → "distressed him and did not strengthen him"** — וְלֹא חֲזָקוֹ, the qal of "be strong" with a suffix; also read "did not overpower him".
- **"took away a portion out of" → "portioned out"** (v21) — חָלַק "divide, portion out" ("divided the house" would say he split the building): he parted the goods of the house of Yahweh, of the king and of the princes. **"he helped him not" → "it was not for help to him"** — וְלֹא לְעֶזְרָה לוֹ.
- **"in the time of his distress"** (v22) — literally "in the time of distressing him".
- **"they were the ruin of him" → "they were to him to make him stumble"** (v23) — לְהַכְשִׁילוֹ.
- **"vessels" → "utensils"** (v24) — כְּלִי. **"cut in pieces"** — קִצֵּץ, as 2 Kgs 24:13.
- **"in every several city" → "in every city"** (v25) — literally "in every city and city". **"provoked to anger" → "vexed"** — הִכְעִיס.
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"**, **"sepulchres" → "graves"** (v27).
- **"Hezekiah"** (v27) — here written יְחִזְקִיָּהוּ, Yechizqiyahu; the same form is the name of the Ephraimite in v12.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 16** — v1–4 and v26–27 stand beside 2 Kgs 16:2–4, 19–20 and are worded like them where the Hebrew is the same. Chronicles differs in v1 (no "his Elohim"), v2 ("ways", plural; the molten images for the Baals), v3 (the valley of the son of Hinnom; "burned his sons"), and in the burial (v27). Only Chronicles has the captives and their return (v5–15) and the raids of Edom and the Philistines (v17–18).
- **Valley of the son of Hinnom** — the place of child sacrifice (2 Kgs 23:10); the later name Gehenna comes from it.
- **Oded** (v9–11) and the heads of Ephraim (v12–15) — the men of the north hear the prophet, clothe and feed the captives and bring them back. Luke 10:30–35 has the same acts (clothing, anointing, the beast, Jericho).
- **Assyria** — Chronicles says of the help Ahaz bought that it "distressed him and did not strengthen him".
- **The doors shut** (v24) — only in Chronicles; Hezekiah opens them (29:3).
- **The burial** (v27) — in the city, not in the graves of the kings.
