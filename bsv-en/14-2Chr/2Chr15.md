# 2 Chronicles 15

[1] And ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the son of Oded, the spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was upon him.

[2] And he went out before Asa and said to him, "Hear me, Asa, and all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧: ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is with you while you are with him; and if you seek him, he will be found by you; and if you forsake him, he will forsake you.

[3] And for many days ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was without ⟦elohim|an Elohim|a God⟧ of truth and without a teaching priest and without ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[4] And it returned, in its distress, to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they sought him, and he was found by them.

[5] And in those times there was no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ for him who went out and for him who came in, for many confusions were upon all the inhabitants of the lands.

[6] And nation was beaten in pieces by nation, and city by city, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ threw them into confusion with every distress.

[7] And you, be strong, and let not your hands go slack, for there is a reward for your work."

[8] And when Asa heard these words and the prophecy, Oded the prophet, he strengthened himself and made the detestable things pass away from all the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and from the cities that he had captured from the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and he renewed the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that was before the porch of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] And he gathered all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, and those sojourning with them from ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ and from ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧, for they had fallen away to him from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in abundance when they saw that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was with him.

[10] And they gathered at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ in the third month of the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.

[11] And they sacrificed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on that day from the spoil they had brought: seven hundred of the herd and seven thousand of the flock.

[12] And they came into the covenant to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers with all their heart and with all their soul.

[13] And everyone who would not seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was to be put to death, from small to great, from man to woman.

[14] And they swore to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with a loud voice and with shouting and with trumpets and with ⟦shofar|shofarot|horns⟧.

[15] And all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ rejoiced over the oath, for with all their heart they had sworn, and with all their good pleasure they had sought him, and he was found by them; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave them rest all around.

[16] And also ⟦maacah|Maachah|Maacah⟧ the mother of Asa the king, he removed her from being great lady, because she had made for the Asherah a horrid thing. And Asa cut down her horrid thing and crushed it and burned it in the wadi ⟦kidron|Qidron|Kidron⟧.

[17] And the high places did not depart from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; only, the heart of Asa was whole all his days.

[18] And he brought the holy things of his father and his own holy things into the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: silver and gold and vessels.

[19] And there was no war until the thirty-fifth year of the reign of Asa.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. "The house of Elohim" (v18) has the article in the Hebrew, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — every Hebrew clause that opens with וְ / וַ opens with "And"; "But", "So", "Now", "therefore" of the KJV are not in the Hebrew.
- **"the Spirit of God came upon Azariah" → "Azariah the son of Oded, the spirit of Elohim was upon him"** (v1) — Hebrew הָיְתָה עָלָיו "was upon him", with the name set first, as it stands.
- **"he went out to meet Asa" → "he went out before Asa"** (v2) — Hebrew לִפְנֵי "before (the face of)".
- **"while ye be with him … but if ye forsake him" → "while you are with him … and if you forsake him"** (v2) — Hebrew בִּהְיוֹתְכֶם עִמּוֹ, literally "in your being with him"; וְאִם "and if" both times. דָּרַשׁ "seek", נִמְצָא לְ "be found by", עָזַב "forsake": the same words as 1 Chr 28:9.
- **"Now for a long season Israel hath been without the true God … without law" → "And for many days Israel was without an Elohim of truth … without torah"** (v3) — Hebrew יָמִים רַבִּים "many days"; the sentence has no verb ("was" is supplied: the Hebrew does not say whether the time is past or to come; v4 goes on with past forms). אֱלֹהֵי אֱמֶת "an Elohim of truth" (= a true God). תּוֹרָה without the article.
- **"But when they in their trouble did turn unto the LORD" → "And it returned, in its distress, to Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיָּשָׁב בַּצַּר־לוֹ, singular (Israel), literally "and he returned, when it was distress to him"; then plural, "they sought him". בִּקֵּשׁ "seek" here and in v15: another verb than דָּרַשׁ "seek" (v2, 12, 13); English has one word.
- **"no peace to him that went out, nor to him that came in, but great vexations" → "no shalom for him who went out and for him who came in, for many confusions"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם; כִּי "for"; מְהוּמֹת רַבּוֹת "many confusions" (or "great").
- **"nation was destroyed of nation … for God did vex them with all adversity" → "nation was beaten in pieces by nation … for Elohim threw them into confusion with every distress"** (v6) — Hebrew כֻּתְּתוּ "they were beaten in pieces" (a plural verb); הָמַם "throw into confusion", the verb of "confusions" in v5; צָרָה "distress".
- **"Be ye strong therefore, and let not your hands be weak: for your work shall be rewarded" → "And you, be strong, and let not your hands go slack, for there is a reward for your work"** (v7) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם "and you"; רָפָה "go slack, sink"; יֵשׁ שָׂכָר לִפְעֻלַּתְכֶם, literally "there is a wage for your work".
- **"the prophecy of Oded the prophet" → "the prophecy, Oded the prophet"** (v8) — the Hebrew text as it stands: הַנְּבוּאָה "the prophecy" has the article and so cannot be joined to the name ("the prophecy of Oded"); the two stand side by side. The speaker of v1–7 is Azariah the son of Oded. Nothing is supplied.
- **"he took courage, and put away the abominable idols" → "he strengthened himself and made the detestable things pass away"** (v8) — Hebrew הִתְחַזֵּק "strengthen oneself" (as 16:9); הֶעֱבִיר "make pass away" (as 1 Kgs 15:12); שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things". **"which he had taken from mount Ephraim" → "that he had captured from the hill country of Ephraim"** — Hebrew לָכַד; הַר "mountain", of a region.
- **"the porch of the LORD"** (v8) — Hebrew אוּלָם.
- **"the strangers with them" → "those sojourning with them"** (v9) — Hebrew הַגָּרִים, a participle. **"they fell to him" → "they had fallen away to him"** — Hebrew נָפַל עַל, of going over to another lord.
- **"in the third month, in the fifteenth year" → "in the third month of the fifteenth year"** (v10) — Hebrew לִשְׁנַת "of the year"; "at" is supplied before "Jerusalem".
- **"they offered unto the LORD the same time … seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep" → "they sacrificed to Yahweh on that day … seven hundred of the herd and seven thousand of the flock"** (v11) — Hebrew זָבַח "sacrifice"; בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "on that day"; בָּקָר "herd" (cattle) and צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats), both collectives.
- **"they entered into a covenant" → "they came into the covenant"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיָּבֹאוּ בַבְּרִית, with the article.
- **"whether small or great, whether man or woman" → "from small to great, from man to woman"** (v13) — Hebrew לְמִן … וְעַד "from … and to". "Seek Yahweh" is here דָּרַשׁ לַיהוָה, with לְ; v12 has אֶת.
- **"with cornets" → "with shofarot"** (v14) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָרוֹת, rams' horns, beside חֲצֹצְרוֹת "trumpets". תְּרוּעָה "shouting": a shout or a blast; the word does not say which.
- **"sought him with their whole desire … rest round about" → "with all their good pleasure they had sought him … rest all around"** (v15) — Hebrew רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (will, desire: what one is pleased to do); the Hebrew sets "with all their heart" and "with all their good pleasure" first.
- **"concerning Maachah the mother of Asa the king, he removed her from being queen, because she had made an idol in a grove" → "Maacah the mother of Asa the king, he removed her from being great lady, because she had made for the Asherah a horrid thing"** (v16) — as 1 Kgs 15:13, where the Hebrew is the same: גְּבִירָה "lady, mistress", the rank of the king's mother at court (the word says neither "queen" nor "mother"); מִפְלֶצֶת, only here and in 1 Kgs 15:13, from a root "shudder": what the object was is not known. Chronicles has "the mother of Asa the king" for "his mother", and "for the Asherah" before "a horrid thing". אֵם is "mother" (also of a grandmother: 1 Kgs 15:2, 10 name the same Maacah as the mother of Abijam). The words can also be divided "the mother of Asa — the king removed her".
- **"cut down her idol, and stamped it, and burnt it at the brook Kidron" → "cut down her horrid thing and crushed it and burned it in the wadi Kidron"** (v16) — Hebrew כָּרַת; וַיָּדֶק "and crushed" (made fine), which 1 Kgs 15:13 does not have; בְּנַחַל "in the wadi"; "it" is supplied twice.
- **"the high places were not taken away out of Israel: nevertheless the heart of Asa was perfect" → "the high places did not depart from Israel; only, the heart of Asa was whole"** (v17) — Hebrew לֹא־סָרוּ "did not depart"; רַק "only"; שָׁלֵם "whole". 1 Kgs 15:14 has no "from Israel" and adds "with Yahweh".
- **"the things that his father had dedicated, and that he himself had dedicated" → "the holy things of his father and his own holy things"** (v18) — Hebrew קָדְשֵׁי אָבִיו וְקָדָשָׁיו; "into" is supplied (the Hebrew has the bare "the house of Elohim"). 1 Kgs 15:15 reads "the holy things of the house of Yahweh" in its reading tradition.
- **"there was no more war unto the five and thirtieth year" → "there was no war until the thirty-fifth year"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא הָיָתָה "there was not"; "more" is not in the Hebrew. The figure is translated as printed; 1 Kgs 15:16 says that there was war between Asa and Baasha all their days, and by 1 Kgs 16:6, 8 Baasha died in the twenty-sixth year of Asa.

### Notes

- **Azariah son of Oded** — a prophet named only in Chronicles. Not the Oded of 2 Chr 28:9.
- **"The prophecy, Oded the prophet"** (v8) — the Syriac and the Vulgate are commonly cited as reading "Azariah the son of Oded the prophet" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"A teaching priest"** (v3) — priests as teachers of torah (Deut 33:10). The verses are commonly read as a look back to the time of the judges.
- **The covenant** (v12–13) — a public oath of the whole people, with death for the one who will not seek Yahweh (compare Deut 13; 17).
- **Those who fell away to Asa** (v9) — men of the northern tribes who went over to Judah.
- **The thirty-fifth year** (v19) — leads to 16:1; see the Notes there.
