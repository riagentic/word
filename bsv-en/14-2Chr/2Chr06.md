# 2 Chronicles 6

"⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has said that he would abide in the dark cloud.

[2] And I, I have built a lofty house for you,
and an established place for your dwelling forever."

[3] And the king turned his face around and blessed all the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and all the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was standing.

[4] And he said, "Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who spoke with his mouth with David my father, and with his hands has fulfilled, saying,

[5] 'From the day that I brought out my people from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, I did not choose a city out of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to build a house, for my name to be there; and I did not choose a man to be leader over my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[6] And I chose ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for my name to be there; and I chose David, to be over my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'

[7] And it was with the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to David my father, 'Because it was with your heart to build a house for my name, you did well, that it was with your heart.

[9] Only, you, you shall not build the house, for your son who comes out from your loins, he shall build the house for my name.'

[10] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has established his word that he spoke; and I have risen in place of David my father, and I sit on the throne of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke, and I have built the house for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[11] And I have put there the ark, where the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is, which he cut with the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[12] And he stood before the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in front of all the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he spread out his palms.

[13] For ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had made a platform of bronze, and he placed it in the midst of the enclosure, five cubits its length and five cubits its breadth and three cubits its height; and he stood upon it, and he knelt on his knees in front of all the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he spread out his palms toward the heavens.

[14] And he said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, there is no ⟦elohim|elohim|god⟧ like you in the heavens and on the earth, keeping the covenant and the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ for your servants who walk before you with all their heart,

[15] you who have kept for your servant David my father what you spoke to him; and you spoke with your mouth, and with your hand you have fulfilled, as at this day.

[16] And now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, keep for your servant David my father what you spoke to him, saying, 'There shall not be cut off for you a man from before me, sitting on the throne of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, only if your sons keep their way, to walk in my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ as you have walked before me.'

[17] And now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, let your word be confirmed, which you spoke to your servant, to David.

[18] For will ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ indeed dwell with man on the earth? Behold, the heavens and the heavens of the heavens cannot contain you; how much less this house that I have built!

[19] And turn to the prayer of your servant and to his plea for favor, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to listen to the cry and to the prayer that your servant is praying before you,

[20] that your eyes may be open toward this house day and night, toward the place of which you said that you would put your name there, to listen to the prayer that your servant shall pray toward this place.

[21] And listen to the pleas for favor of your servant and of your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which they shall pray toward this place; and you, hear from the place of your dwelling, from the heavens; and hear and pardon.

[22] If a man sins against his neighbor, and he lays on him a curse, to put him under a curse, and the curse comes before your altar in this house,

[23] then you, hear from the heavens, and act, and judge your servants, to return to the wicked, to put his way on his head, and to justify the righteous, to give to him according to his righteousness.

[24] And if your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ are smitten before an enemy, because they sin against you, and they return and give thanks to your name and pray and plead before you in this house,

[25] then you, hear from the heavens, and pardon the sin of your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and bring them back to the ground that you gave to them and to their fathers.

[26] When the heavens are closed up and there is no rain, because they sin against you, and they pray toward this place and give thanks to your name, from their sin they turn back, because you afflict them,

[27] then you, hear in the heavens, and pardon the sin of your servants and of your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for you teach them the good way in which they should walk; and give rain on your land, which you gave to your people as an inheritance.

[28] Famine, when it is in the land; pestilence, when it is; blight and mildew, locusts and devourer, when it is; when his enemies distress him in the land of his gates; any plague and any sickness;

[29] any prayer, any plea for favor, that is made by any man and by all your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who know each man his plague and his pain, and he spreads out his palms toward this house,

[30] then you, hear from the heavens, the established place of your dwelling, and pardon, and give to each man according to all his ways, whose heart you know — for you, you alone, know the heart of the sons of man —

[31] so that they may fear you, to walk in your ways, all the days that they are alive on the face of the ground that you gave to our fathers.

[32] And also to the foreigner, who is not of your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he comes from a far land on account of your great name and your strong hand and your outstretched arm, and they come and pray toward this house,

[33] then you, hear from the heavens, from the established place of your dwelling, and do according to all that the foreigner calls to you for, so that all the peoples of the earth may know your name, and to fear you like your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and to know that your name is called upon this house that I have built.

[34] When your people go out to battle against their enemies, in the way that you send them, and they pray to you in the direction of this city that you have chosen and the house that I have built for your name,

[35] then hear from the heavens their prayer and their plea for favor, and do their judgment.

[36] When they sin against you — for there is no man who does not sin — and you are angry with them and set them before an enemy, and their captors take them captive to a land far or near,

[37] and they bring back to their heart in the land where they have been taken captive, and they return and plead with you in the land of their captivity, saying, 'We have sinned, we have done iniquity and we have done wickedly,'

[38] and they return to you with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity, where they took them captive, and they pray in the direction of their land that you gave to their fathers, and the city that you have chosen, and to the house that I have built for your name,

[39] then hear from the heavens, from the established place of your dwelling, their prayer and their pleas for favor, and do their judgment, and pardon your people who have sinned against you.

[40] Now, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, let your eyes, please, be open, and your ears attentive, to the prayer of this place.

[41] And now, arise, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to your rest,

and let your faithful ones rejoice in the good.

[42] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not turn back the face of your ⟦anointed|meshichim|anointed ones⟧;
remember the ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|steadfast love⟧ of David your servant."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v14 the word is generic ("there is no elohim like you"). In v41, 42 the Hebrew is יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים, "Yahweh Elohim", three times.
- **The parallel in Kings** (throughout) — the chapter runs beside 1 Kgs 8:12–53. Where the Hebrew of the two books is the same, the wording is that of 1 Kings 8; where Chronicles has another word, form or number, the verse follows Chronicles. The differences that touch the meaning are named below; nothing is taken over from Kings.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, also through the long sentences of the prayer.
- **"thick darkness" → "dark cloud"; "that he would dwell" → "that he would abide"** (v1) — Hebrew עֲרָפֶל (Exod 20:21); אָמַר לִשְׁכּוֹן, literally "said to abide"; שָׁכַן "abide".
- **"But I have built an house of habitation for thee, and a place for thy dwelling for ever" → "And I, I have built a lofty house for you, and an established place for your dwelling forever"** (v2) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי בָּנִיתִי "and I, I have built" (Kings: "I have surely built"); בֵּית זְבֻל "a house of loftiness"; וּמָכוֹן "and an established place" (Kings has no "and"). "Forever" is the plural עוֹלָמִים, "ages".
- **"congregation" → "assembly"** (v3, 12, 13) — Hebrew קָהָל.
- **"Blessed be" → "Blessed is"** (v4) — the Hebrew has no verb.
- **"who hath with his hands fulfilled that which he spake with his mouth to my father David" → "who spoke with his mouth with David my father, and with his hands has fulfilled"** (v4) — the Hebrew order; אֵת דָּוִיד "with David"; מִלֵּא "fill, fulfil" has no object. Chronicles has "with his hands" (plural; Kings "with his hand"; in v15 both have the singular).
- **"I chose no city … neither chose I any man to be a ruler" → "I did not choose a city … and I did not choose a man to be leader"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־בָחַרְתִּי בְעִיר … וְלֹא־בָחַרְתִּי בְאִישׁ לִהְיוֹת נָגִיד. Chronicles has "my people from the land of Egypt" (Kings: "my people Israel from Egypt"); the clause about the man and the choosing of Jerusalem (v6a) stand only in Chronicles.
- **"But I have chosen" → "And I chose"** (v6) — Hebrew וָאֶבְחַר, twice.
- **"it was in the heart of David" → "it was with the heart of David"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew עִם־לְבַב.
- **"Notwithstanding thou shalt not build the house; but thy son which shall come forth out of thy loins" → "Only, you, you shall not build the house, for your son who comes out from your loins"** (v9) — Hebrew רַק אַתָּה לֹא תִבְנֶה … כִּי בִנְךָ הַיּוֹצֵא. Chronicles has only כִּי, "for" (after a negative also "but"); Kings has כִּי אִם "but".
- **"hath performed his word … I am risen up … as the LORD promised" → "has established his word … I have risen … as Yahweh spoke"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיָּקֶם … וָאָקוּם: one root, "he made his word stand … and I stood up"; דִּבֶּר "spoke".
- **"in it have I put the ark, wherein is the covenant of the LORD, that he made with the children of Israel" → "I have put there the ark, where the covenant of Yahweh is, which he cut with the sons of Israel"** (v11) — Hebrew וָאָשִׂים שָׁם אֶת־הָאָרוֹן; כָּרַת "cut"; בְּנֵי "sons". Kings has "a place for the ark" and "with our fathers when he brought them out from the land of Egypt".
- **"in the presence of" → "in front of"; "spread forth his hands" → "spread out his palms"** (v12, 13, 29) — Hebrew נֶגֶד; כַּפָּיו "his palms". In v12 Chronicles has neither the name Solomon nor "to the heavens" (Kings has both); "toward the heavens" in v13 is הַשָּׁמָיְמָה, with the ending of direction.
- **"a brasen scaffold" → "a platform of bronze"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּיּוֹר נְחֹשֶׁת. The word is כִּיּוֹר, elsewhere "basin" (4:6; Exod 30:18); here it names a thing on which a man stands, five cubits square and three high. "Basin" would say a vessel for water, so the sense is given; the word may tell of its shape. The verse stands only in Chronicles.
- **"had set it in the midst of the court" → "placed it in the midst of the enclosure"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנֵהוּ, "gave" = placed. "Enclosure" is עֲזָרָה, as 4:9, a late word; חָצֵר is "court".
- **"kneeled down upon his knees" → "knelt on his knees"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּבְרַךְ עַל־בִּרְכָּיו, verb and noun of one root.
- **"there is no God like thee in the heaven, nor in the earth; which keepest covenant, and shewest mercy unto thy servants" → "there is no elohim like you in the heavens and on the earth, keeping the covenant and the chesed for your servants"** (v14) — Hebrew בַּשָּׁמַיִם וּבָאָרֶץ (Kings: "in the heavens above and on the earth beneath"); שֹׁמֵר הַבְּרִית וְהַחֶסֶד לַעֲבָדֶיךָ, the words of Deut 7:9; there is no second verb. "Servants" is עֲבָדִים, the word for slaves; of Yahweh's men "servant".
- **"that which thou hast promised him" → "what you spoke to him"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew דִּבַּרְתָּ. "Kept for" is שָׁמַר לְ.
- **"There shall not fail thee a man in my sight to sit upon the throne of Israel; yet so that thy children take heed to their way to walk in my law" → "There shall not be cut off for you a man from before me, sitting on the throne of Israel, only if your sons keep their way, to walk in my Torah"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא־יִכָּרֵת לְךָ אִישׁ מִלְּפָנַי יוֹשֵׁב; רַק אִם־יִשְׁמְרוּ; "keep" is the verb of v14–16 throughout. Chronicles has לָלֶכֶת בְּתוֹרָתִי "to walk in my Torah"; Kings has "to walk before me".
- **"let thy word be verified, which thou hast spoken unto thy servant David" → "let your word be confirmed, which you spoke to your servant, to David"** (v17) — Hebrew יֵאָמֵן; לְעַבְדְּךָ לְדָוִיד, the לְ twice. Chronicles has the name Yahweh here and neither "please" nor "my father" (Kings: "Elohim of Israel, let your word, please, be confirmed … David my father").
- **"But will God in very deed dwell with men on the earth? behold, heaven and the heaven of heavens" → "For will Elohim indeed dwell with man on the earth? Behold, the heavens and the heavens of the heavens"** (v18) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; אֶת־הָאָדָם "with man" (singular with the article: mankind), which Kings does not have. The first "heavens" has no article in Chronicles; English needs it.
- **"Have respect therefore to" → "And turn to"** (v19) — Hebrew וּפָנִיתָ אֶל. Chronicles has no "today" at the end.
- **"supplication" → "plea for favor"** (v19, 29, 35) — Hebrew תְּחִנָּה, the noun of חָנַן "show favor"; the verb הִתְחַנֵּן is "plead" (v24, 37). In v21 the word is another noun of the same root, תַּחֲנוּנִים, plural, "pleas for favor" (Kings has תְּחִנָּה, singular); in v39 תְּחִנֹּת is plural (Kings singular).
- **"hearken" → "listen to" / "hear"** (v19–21) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to"; שָׁמַע alone is "hear".
- **The requests of the prayer** (v19–39) — the Hebrew uses the forms "and you will turn", "and you, you will hear … and pardon" (perfect with וְ and imperfect), which in a prayer ask; they are rendered as requests, and the pronoun אַתָּה is kept ("you, hear").
- **"upon this house day and night, upon the place whereof thou hast said that thou wouldest put thy name there" → "toward this house day and night, toward the place of which you said that you would put your name there"** (v20) — Hebrew אֶל "toward"; יוֹמָם וָלַיְלָה "day and night" (Kings: "night and day"); אָמַרְתָּ לָשׂוּם שִׁמְךָ שָׁם, literally "you said to put your name there" (Kings: "you said, 'My name shall be there'").
- **"hear thou from thy dwelling place, even from heaven" → "hear from the place of your dwelling, from the heavens"** (v21) — Hebrew מִמְּקוֹם שִׁבְתְּךָ מִן־הַשָּׁמַיִם. Chronicles has "from" here and in v23, 25, 30, 33, 35, 39; Kings has "in the heavens" (literally "to" in 8:30). In v27 Chronicles too has the bare הַשָּׁמַיִם, "in the heavens". In v30, 33, 39 the word is מְכוֹן "established place" (v2).
- **"forgive" → "pardon"** (v21, 25, 27, 30, 39) — Hebrew סָלַח, said only of God.
- **"If a man sin against his neighbour, and an oath be laid upon him to make him swear, and the oath come" → "If a man sins against his neighbor, and he lays on him a curse, to put him under a curse, and the curse comes"** (v22) — Hebrew אִם "if" (Kings: "when"); אָלָה is a curse, of an oath that calls a curse on the one who breaks it; לְהַאֲלֹתוֹ is the verb of the same root.
- **"by requiting the wicked, by recompensing his way upon his own head; and by justifying the righteous, by giving him" → "to return to the wicked, to put his way on his head, and to justify the righteous, to give to him"** (v23) — Hebrew לְהָשִׁיב לְרָשָׁע, literally "to bring back to the wicked" (Kings: לְהַרְשִׁיעַ רָשָׁע "to condemn the wicked"); לָתֵת "give" both times.
- **"be put to the worse before the enemy, because they have sinned against thee; and shall return and confess thy name, and pray and make supplication before thee" → "are smitten before an enemy, because they sin against you, and they return and give thanks to your name and pray and plead before you"** (v24) — Hebrew וְאִם־יִנָּגֵף "and if … are smitten" (Kings: "when"); הוֹדָה "give thanks to, praise, acknowledge" (of sin "confess"); Chronicles has "return" without "to you" and לְפָנֶיךָ "before you" (Kings: "plead with you").
- **"the land which thou gavest to them and to their fathers" → "the ground that you gave to them and to their fathers"** (v25, 31) — Hebrew הָאֲדָמָה "ground"; "land" (אֶרֶץ) stands in v27, 28, 36–38. "To them and" stands only in Chronicles.
- **"When the heaven is shut up … when thou dost afflict them" → "When the heavens are closed up … because you afflict them"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּהֵעָצֵר; כִּי תַעֲנֵם, as pointed; the same letters could be read "when you answer them". Chronicles has no "and" before "from their sin".
- **"when thou hast taught them the good way" → "for you teach them the good way"** (v27) — Hebrew כִּי תוֹרֵם אֶל־הַדֶּרֶךְ, literally "teach them to the good way" (Kings has אֶת).
- **"blasting, or mildew, locusts, or caterpillers; if their enemies besiege them in the cities of their land; whatsoever sore or whatsoever sickness there be" → "blight and mildew, locusts and devourer, when it is; when his enemies distress him in the land of his gates; any plague and any sickness"** (v28) — Hebrew שִׁדָּפוֹן וְיֵרָקוֹן, אַרְבֶּה וְחָסִיל (Chronicles joins the pairs with "and"). חָסִיל is a kind or a stage of locust, named from חָסַל "devour"; which is not known. יָצַר־לוֹ אוֹיְבָיו בְּאֶרֶץ שְׁעָרָיו: "his enemies" is plural (Kings singular), the verb and "him" singular; "the land of his gates" as the Hebrew has it. נֶגַע "plague" (literally "a touch, a stroke").
- **"of any man, or of all thy people Israel, when every one shall know his own sore and his own grief" → "by any man and by all your people Israel, who know each man his plague and his pain"** (v29) — Hebrew אִישׁ נִגְעוֹ וּמַכְאֹבוֹ (Kings: "the plague of his heart"); מַכְאוֹב "pain".
- **"render unto every man" → "give to each man"** (v30) — Hebrew וְנָתַתָּה. Chronicles has neither "and act" nor "all" before "the sons of man" (Kings has both). "Sons of man" is בְּנֵי הָאָדָם.
- **"That they may fear thee, to walk in thy ways, so long as they live in the land" → "so that they may fear you, to walk in your ways, all the days that they are alive on the face of the ground"** (v31) — Hebrew לְמַעַן יִירָאוּךָ לָלֶכֶת בִּדְרָכֶיךָ; "to walk in your ways" stands only in Chronicles. עַל־פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"Moreover concerning the stranger … but is come from a far country for thy great name's sake, and thy mighty hand, and thy stretched out arm; if they come and pray in this house" → "And also to the foreigner … and he comes from a far land on account of your great name and your strong hand and your outstretched arm, and they come and pray toward this house"** (v32) — Hebrew וְגַם אֶל־הַנָּכְרִי; לְמַעַן "on account of"; חֲזָקָה "strong"; אֶל־הַבַּיִת "toward this house". The verb passes from the singular ("he comes") to the plural ("they come and pray"), as the Hebrew has it. Kings has here "for they shall hear of your great name …", which Chronicles does not have.
- **"that all people of the earth may know thy name, and fear thee" → "so that all the peoples of the earth may know your name, and to fear you"** (v33) — Hebrew עַמֵּי הָאָרֶץ, plural; וּלְיִרְאָה "and to fear" (Kings has no "and"). "Your name is called upon this house" is שִׁמְךָ נִקְרָא עַל.
- **"to war against their enemies … toward this city" → "to battle against their enemies … in the direction of this city"** (v34, 38) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ "the way of"; "toward" is אֶל. Chronicles has "their enemies" (plural), "pray to you" (Kings: "to Yahweh") and "this city".
- **"maintain their cause" → "do their judgment"** (v35, 39) — Hebrew עָשָׂה מִשְׁפָּט: give them their right.
- **"deliver them over before their enemies, and they carry them away captives unto a land far off or near" → "set them before an enemy, and their captors take them captive to a land far or near"** (v36) — Hebrew וּנְתַתָּם לִפְנֵי אוֹיֵב, literally "give them before"; וְשָׁבוּם שׁוֹבֵיהֶם. Chronicles has "a land far or near" (Kings: "the land of the enemy, far or near").
- **"Yet if they bethink themselves … and turn and pray unto thee in the land of their captivity" → "and they bring back to their heart … and they return and plead with you in the land of their captivity"** (v37) — Hebrew וְהֵשִׁיבוּ אֶל־לְבָבָם, of the root of "return" (שׁוּב); וְשָׁבוּ "return", not "repent"; הִתְחַנֵּן "plead". Chronicles has בְּאֶרֶץ שִׁבְיָם "the land of their captivity" (Kings: "of their captors"), also in v38 (Kings: "of their enemies").
- **"We have sinned, we have done amiss, and have dealt wickedly" → "We have sinned, we have done iniquity and we have done wickedly"** (v37) — Hebrew חָטָאנוּ הֶעֱוִינוּ וְרָשָׁעְנוּ; the "and" stands before the third verb (in Kings before the second).
- **"whither they have carried them captives, and pray toward their land … and toward the city … and toward the house" → "where they took them captive, and they pray in the direction of their land … and the city … and to the house"** (v38) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־שָׁבוּ אֹתָם; דֶּרֶךְ אַרְצָם … וְהָעִיר … וְלַבַּיִת: the last member has לְ "to". Chronicles has no "to you" after "pray".
- **"forgive thy people which have sinned against thee" → "pardon your people who have sinned against you"** (v39) — here the prayer of Chronicles leaves Kings: 1 Kgs 8:50b–53 has no counterpart, and v40–42 stand only in Chronicles.
- **"let, I beseech thee, thine eyes be open, and let thine ears be attent unto the prayer that is made in this place" → "let your eyes, please, be open, and your ears attentive, to the prayer of this place"** (v40) — Hebrew נָא "please"; לִתְפִלַּת הַמָּקוֹם הַזֶּה, "the prayer of this place"; "that is made" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Now therefore arise, O LORD God, into thy resting place, thou, and the ark of thy strength" → "And now, arise, Yahweh Elohim, to your rest, you and the ark of your strength"** (v41) — Hebrew לְנוּחֶךָ, "to your rest" (or "resting"); Ps 132:8 has לִמְנוּחָתֶךָ "to your resting place". The Hebrew has no "O".
- **"let thy saints rejoice in goodness" → "let your faithful ones rejoice in the good"** (v41) — Hebrew חֲסִידֶיךָ, those who keep *chesed*; יִשְׂמְחוּ בַטּוֹב. Ps 132:9 has "be clothed with righteousness" and "shout for joy"; Chronicles has "salvation" (תְּשׁוּעָה) and "rejoice in the good".
- **"turn not away the face of thine anointed" → "do not turn back the face of your anointed ones"** (v42) — Hebrew אַל־תָּשֵׁב פְּנֵי, to refuse one who asks (1 Kgs 2:16). מְשִׁיחֶיךָ is written as a plural, "your anointed ones"; Ps 132:10 has the singular, "your anointed". The plural form is translated as it is printed.
- **"remember the mercies of David thy servant" → "remember the chesed of David your servant"** (v42) — Hebrew זָכְרָה לְחַסְדֵי דָּוִיד: the plural of חֶסֶד (*chasadim*), with לְ after "remember". "Of David" is left as open as the Hebrew: the *chesed* shown to David (Isa 55:3), or the faithful deeds of David (as 32:32; 35:26). The words are not in Ps 132:10, which has "For the sake of David your servant".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 8:12–50** — Chronicles has the bronze platform (v13) and the closing lines v40–42, which stand also in Ps 132:8–10 in another form; it does not have 1 Kgs 8:50b–53.
- **"The dark cloud"** (v1) — as at Sinai (Exod 20:21; Deut 4:11).
- **Seven cases** — the curse between neighbors, defeat, no rain, famine and plague, the foreigner, battle, captivity.
- **Captivity** (v36–39) — the prayer looks ahead to a people taken captive who pray toward the land, the city and the house.
- **The foreigner** (v32–33) — the one who is not of Israel prays toward the house and is heard.
- **"The ark of your strength"** (v41) — as Ps 132:8; compare Num 10:35.
