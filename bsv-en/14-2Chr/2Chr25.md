# 2 Chronicles 25

[1] ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and twenty-nine years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦jehoaddan|Yehoaddan|Jehoaddan⟧ of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] And he did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, only not with a whole heart.

[3] And it came to pass, when the kingdom was strong upon him, that he killed his servants who had struck down the king his father.

[4] And their sons he did not put to death, but as it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, in the book of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded, saying, "Fathers shall not die on account of sons, and sons shall not die on account of fathers; but each man in his sin they shall die."

[5] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ gathered ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and made them stand by fathers' houses, by the commanders of the thousands and by the commanders of the hundreds, for all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧. And he numbered them from twenty years old and upward, and found them three hundred thousand chosen ones who go out to the host, taking hold of spear and large shield.

[6] And he hired from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a hundred thousand mighty ones of ability for a hundred talents of silver.

[7] And a man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to him, saying, "O king, let not the host of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ go with you, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is not with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, all the sons of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[8] But go, you; do, be strong for the battle: ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will make you stumble before an enemy, for there is power in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to help and to make stumble."

[9] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ said to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "And what is to be done about the hundred talents that I have given to the band of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?" And the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is able to give you much more than this."

[10] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ separated them, the band that had come to him from ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, to go to their place. And their anger burned greatly against ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they returned to their place in the burning of anger.

[11] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ strengthened himself and led his people, and he went to the Valley of Salt and struck down the sons of Seir, ten thousand.

[12] And ten thousand the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ took captive alive, and they brought them to the top of the crag and threw them from the top of the crag, and all of them were split open.

[13] And the sons of the band whom ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ had turned back from going with him to the battle made a raid in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, from ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and as far as ⟦beth-horon|Beit Choron|Beth-horon⟧, and they struck down three thousand of them and plundered much plunder.

[14] And it came to pass, after ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ came from striking down the ⟦edomites|Edomim|Edomites⟧, that he brought the gods of the sons of Seir and made them stand for himself as gods, and before them he would bow down, and to them he would make smoke.

[15] And the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned against ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧, and he sent to him a prophet, and he said to him, "Why have you sought the gods of the people, who did not deliver their people from your hand?"

[16] And it came to pass, as he was speaking to him, that he said to him, "Have we made you a counselor to the king? Cease, for your part! Why should they strike you?" And the prophet ceased, and he said, "I know that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has counseled to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my counsel."

[17] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ took counsel and sent to ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ the son of ⟦jehoahaz|Yehoachaz|Jehoahaz⟧ the son of ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "Come, let us look one another in the face."

[18] And ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sent to ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying, "The thistle that is in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ sent to the cedar that is in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son as a wife.' And a living thing of the field that is in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ passed by and trampled the thistle.

[19] You have said, 'Behold, you have struck down Edom,' and your heart has lifted you up to get glory. Now sit in your house. Why should you provoke evil and fall, you and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ with you?"

[20] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ did not listen, for it was from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in order to give them into a hand, because they had sought the gods of Edom.

[21] And ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went up, and they looked one another in the face, he and ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, at ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧, which belongs to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[22] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ was smitten before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they fled each man to his tents.

[23] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, the son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ the son of ⟦jehoahaz|Yehoachaz|Jehoahaz⟧, ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ seized at ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧; and he brought him to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and made a breach in the wall of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, from the Gate of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ as far as the gate that turns, four hundred cubits.

[24] And all the gold and the silver and all the vessels that were found in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with ⟦obed-edom|Oved-Edom|Obed-edom⟧, and the treasuries of the house of the king, and the hostages — and he returned to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[25] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ the son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ lived after the death of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ the son of ⟦jehoahaz|Yehoachaz|Jehoahaz⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ fifteen years.

[26] And the rest of the acts of ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧, the first and the last, are they not, behold, written in the book of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[27] And from the time that ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ turned aside from after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, they conspired a conspiracy against him in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and he fled to Lachish, and they sent after him to Lachish and put him to death there.

[28] And they carried him upon the horses and buried him with his fathers in the city of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים) in v7, 8, 9, 20, 24, without it in v8b, 16.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Beside 2 Kgs 14:1–20** — where the Hebrew of the two books is the same, word for word, the wording is that of 2 Kings 14 (v18, 21, 22, 25 and parts of v1–4, 17, 19, 23, 27, 28); where Chronicles has its own words, they are translated as they stand and the reading of Kings is named below.
- **"Amaziah was twenty and five years old when he began to reign"** (v1) — Hebrew בֶּן־עֶשְׂרִים וְחָמֵשׁ שָׁנָה מָלַךְ אֲמַצְיָהוּ, literally "a son of twenty and five years Amaziah reigned": מָלַךְ "reign", here of the beginning; with a number of years "reigned". Kings has "he was … when he began to reign" (הָיָה בְמָלְכוֹ) and does not name him here. The numbers are in ordinary English order.
- **"but not with a perfect heart" → "only not with a whole heart"** (v2) — Hebrew רַק "only"; לֵבָב שָׁלֵם "a whole heart". Kings has "only not like David his father; according to all that Joash his father had done, he did".
- **"when the kingdom was established to him, that he slew" → "when the kingdom was strong upon him, that he killed"** (v3) — Hebrew חָזְקָה הַמַּמְלָכָה עָלָיו: חָזַק "be strong"; עָלָיו "upon him" (Kings: בְּיָדוֹ "in his hand"), that is, firm under him. הָרַג "kill" (Kings: הִכָּה "strike down", the verb kept here for what the servants did). עֶבֶד is "slave", of a king's men "servant".
- **"But he slew not their children, but did as it is written in the law in the book of Moses, where the LORD commanded" → "And their sons he did not put to death, but as it is written in the Torah, in the book of Moses, which Yahweh commanded"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּנֵיהֶם "their sons" (Kings: "the sons of the strikers"); הֵמִית "put to death"; כִּי כַכָּתוּב "but as it is written", with no verb "did"; בַּתּוֹרָה בְּסֵפֶר מֹשֶׁה "in the Torah, in the book of Moses" (Kings: "in the book of the Torah of Moses"). אֲשֶׁר־צִוָּה יְהוָה "which Yahweh commanded" may also be taken "as Yahweh commanded".
- **"The fathers shall not die for the children, neither shall the children die for the fathers, but every man shall die for his own sin" → "Fathers shall not die on account of sons, and sons shall not die on account of fathers; but each man in his sin they shall die"** (v4) — the words of Deut 24:16, in the wording used there and in 2 Kgs 14:6, with the differences of the Hebrew: Chronicles has the verb "die" (יָמוּתוּ, three times), where Deuteronomy and the reading tradition of Kings have "be put to death" (יוּמְתוּ); the last verb is plural, as in Deuteronomy ("each man in his sin, they shall die"; Kings has the singular); and before the last clause stands כִּי "but" (Kings: כִּי אִם; Deuteronomy has nothing).
- **"gathered Judah together, and made them captains over thousands, and captains over hundreds, according to the houses of their fathers" → "gathered Judah and made them stand by fathers' houses, by the commanders of the thousands and by the commanders of the hundreds, for all Judah and Benjamin"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמִידֵם "made them stand"; the preposition לְ four times (לְבֵית־אָבוֹת לְשָׂרֵי … וּלְשָׂרֵי … לְכָל), "by … by … by … for": he set them in order under their commanders. שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander".
- **"three hundred thousand choice men, able to go forth to war, that could handle spear and shield" → "three hundred thousand chosen ones who go out to the host, taking hold of spear and large shield"** (v5) — Hebrew בָּחוּר "chosen" (or "young man": the same form); יוֹצֵא צָבָא "going out to the host" (as Num 1:3); אֹחֵז "taking hold of"; צִנָּה is the large shield (beside מָגֵן "shield"). All in the singular, of the whole number.
- **"mighty men of valour" → "mighty ones of ability"** (v6) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר חָיִל, singular, of the whole number.
- **"there came a man of God to him, saying, O king, let not the army of Israel go with thee … to wit, with all the children of Ephraim" → "a man of Elohim came to him, saying, 'O king, let not the host of Israel go with you … all the sons of Ephraim'"** (v7) — Hebrew צְבָא "host"; יָבֹא "come / go"; כֹּל בְּנֵי אֶפְרָיִם stands beside "Israel" with no word between: "Israel, (that is) all the sons of Ephraim". "O king": the Hebrew has "the king" as an address.
- **"But if thou wilt go, do it, be strong for the battle: God shall make thee fall before the enemy: for God hath power to help, and to cast down" → "But go, you; do, be strong for the battle: Elohim will make you stumble before an enemy, for there is power in Elohim to help and to make stumble"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־בֹּא אַתָּה עֲשֵׂה חֲזַק לַמִּלְחָמָה יַכְשִׁילְךָ הָאֱלֹהִים, translated word for word: three commands ("go", "do", "be strong") and then, with nothing between, "Elohim will make you stumble". The sentence is difficult. כִּי אִם after the "let not" of v7 is "but"; it can also be "for if" ("for if you go — do, be strong for the battle — Elohim will make you stumble"), though בֹּא as pointed is a command. Either way the commands are a challenge, not advice: if he goes so, he will fall. Many translations add "else" or a negative ("lest God …", "God will not …"); the Hebrew has neither. הִכְשִׁיל "make stumble", twice; כֹּחַ "power"; אוֹיֵב "an enemy", without the article; מִלְחָמָה is "war", of the single fight "battle" (also v13).
- **"what shall we do for the hundred talents which I have given to the army of Israel?" → "what is to be done about the hundred talents that I have given to the band of Israel?"** (v9) — Hebrew וּמַה־לַּעֲשׂוֹת "and what (is there) to do"; גְּדוּד (v9, 10, 13) is a band of fighting men, elsewhere a "raiding band" (1 Sam 30:8; 1 Chr 12:21): "raiding" is left out here, where a hired troop is meant, and the one word should be heard in v13, where they raid. **"The LORD is able to give thee much more than this"** — Hebrew יֵשׁ לַיהוָה לָתֶת לְךָ, literally "there is to Yahweh to give you".
- **"Amaziah separated them, to wit, the army that was come to him out of Ephraim, to go home again … they returned home in great anger" → "Amaziah separated them, the band that had come to him from Ephraim, to go to their place … they returned to their place in the burning of anger"** (v10) — Hebrew לְהַגְּדוּד, with לְ naming who "them" are; לִמְקוֹמָם "to their place", twice; וַיִּחַר אַפָּם "their anger burned" and בָּחֳרִי־אָף "in the burning of anger": אַף is "nose", of anger (also v15).
- **"strengthened himself, and led forth his people, and went to the valley of salt, and smote of the children of Seir ten thousand" → "strengthened himself and led his people, and he went to the Valley of Salt and struck down the sons of Seir, ten thousand"** (v11) — Hebrew הִתְחַזַּק "strengthened himself", the reflexive of חָזַק, one rendering through Chronicles (also 1:1; 17:1; 27:6); נָהַג "lead" (literally "drive"); הִכָּה "strike", here of killing; בְּנֵי "sons of". Kings has "He struck down Edom in the Valley of Salt, ten thousand, and he seized Sela in the battle".
- **"other ten thousand left alive did the children of Judah carry away captive, and brought them unto the top of the rock, and cast them down from the top of the rock, that they all were broken in pieces" → "ten thousand the sons of Judah took captive alive, and they brought them to the top of the crag and threw them from the top of the crag, and all of them were split open"** (v12) — the Hebrew has no "other"; שָׁבָה "take captive"; רֹאשׁ "top", literally "head"; הַסֶּלַע "the crag", with the article: it may be the name Sela, the stronghold that 2 Kgs 14:7 says he seized; נִבְקְעוּ "were split open" (nifal of בָּקַע "split").
- **"the soldiers of the army which Amaziah sent back, that they should not go with him to battle, fell upon the cities of Judah … and took much spoil" → "the sons of the band whom Amaziah had turned back from going with him to the battle made a raid in the cities of Judah … and plundered much plunder"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הַגְּדוּד "the sons of the band"; הֵשִׁיב (hifil of שׁוּב "return") "turned back"; וַיִּפְשְׁטוּ בְּ "made a raid in"; וַיָּבֹזּוּ בִּזָּה, verb and noun of one root. "From Samaria and as far as Beth-horon": so the Hebrew, though Samaria is not in Judah.
- **"after that Amaziah was come from the slaughter of the Edomites, that he brought the gods of the children of Seir, and set them up to be his gods, and bowed down himself before them, and burned incense unto them" → "after Amaziah came from striking down the Edomites, that he brought the gods of the sons of Seir and made them stand for himself as gods, and before them he would bow down, and to them he would make smoke"** (v14) — Hebrew מֵהַכּוֹת "from striking"; וַיַּעֲמִידֵם לוֹ לֵאלֹהִים "made them stand for himself as gods"; the last two verbs (יִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה, יְקַטֵּר) are in the form of repeated action, and "before them" and "to them" stand first. קִטֵּר has no object: "make smoke"; whether incense or the smoke of sacrifices is meant is not said.
- **"the anger of the LORD was kindled … which said unto him, Why hast thou sought after the gods of the people, which could not deliver their own people out of thine hand?" → "the anger of Yahweh burned … and he said to him, 'Why have you sought the gods of the people, who did not deliver their people from your hand?'"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר "and he said" (the prophet, or Yahweh through him); דָּרַשׁ "seek" (also v20), the word of Chronicles for turning to a god; לֹא־הִצִּילוּ "did not deliver", not "could not"; "the people" has the article: the people just named.
- **"as he talked with him, that the king said unto him, Art thou made of the king's counsel? forbear; why shouldest thou be smitten?" → "as he was speaking to him, that he said to him, 'Have we made you a counselor to the king? Cease, for your part! Why should they strike you?'"** (v16) — the Hebrew does not name the king as the speaker; נְתַנּוּךָ is literally "have we given you"; חֲדַל־לְךָ "cease", with לְךָ "for yourself", which turns the command back on the one addressed; יַכּוּךָ "they strike you" (הִכָּה; whether to death is not said). **"Then the prophet forbare, and said, I know that God hath determined to destroy thee … and hast not hearkened unto my counsel" → "And the prophet ceased, and he said, 'I know that Elohim has counseled to destroy you … and have not listened to my counsel'"** — one root three times: יוֹעֵץ "counselor", יָעַץ "has counseled", עֲצָתִי "my counsel" (and וַיִּוָּעַץ "took counsel", v17). הִשְׁחִית "destroy".
- **"took advice, and sent to Joash"** → **"took counsel and sent to Joash"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיִּוָּעַץ; Kings has "Then Amaziah sent messengers". The qere לְכָה "Come" is translated; the ketiv לך is the same word in a shorter form. Chronicles writes the king of Israel יוֹאָשׁ "Joash" throughout (Kings: "Jehoash" in these verses).
- **"let us look one another in the face"** (v17, 21) — Hebrew נִתְרָאֶה פָנִים, literally "let us see one another, faces": to meet face to face, here in battle.
- **"The thistle"** (v18) — Hebrew הַחוֹחַ, a thorny plant; "thistle" or "bramble", the kind is not certain. **"to wife" → "as a wife"**. **"there passed by a wild beast" → "a living thing of the field … passed by"** — Hebrew חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה; the word says "living", not "wild". **"trode down" → "trampled"** — Hebrew רָמַס. The verse is the same as 2 Kgs 14:9, word for word.
- **"Thou sayest, Lo, thou hast smitten the Edomites; and thine heart lifteth thee up to boast: abide now at home; why shouldest thou meddle to thine hurt, that thou shouldest fall" → "You have said, 'Behold, you have struck down Edom,' and your heart has lifted you up to get glory. Now sit in your house. Why should you provoke evil and fall"** (v19) — Hebrew אָמַרְתָּ "you have said"; הִכִּיתָ, as pointed, "you have struck down" (the king speaking to himself; many read "I have struck down"); Kings has "You have surely struck down Edom". לְהַכְבִּיד (hifil of כָּבֵד "be heavy, honored"), literally "to make heavy / to make glory", where Kings has the command "Be glorified". עַתָּה שְׁבָה בְּבֵיתֶךָ "now sit in your house"; תִתְגָּרֶה בְּרָעָה, literally "stir yourself up against evil".
- **"But Amaziah would not hear; for it came of God, that he might deliver them into the hand of their enemies, because they sought after the gods of Edom" → "And Amaziah did not listen, for it was from Elohim, in order to give them into a hand, because they had sought the gods of Edom"** (v20) — all after "did not listen" is Chronicles' own. Hebrew לְמַעַן תִּתָּם בְּיָד "in order to give them into a hand": the Hebrew does not say whose hand; "of their enemies" is supplied by the translations.
- **"was put to the worse before Israel, and they fled every man to his tent" → "was smitten before Israel, and they fled each man to his tents"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּגֶף (nifal of נָגַף "smite"); לְאֹהָלָיו "to his tents", plural.
- **"took Amaziah … and brought him to Jerusalem, and brake down the wall of Jerusalem from the gate of Ephraim to the corner gate" → "seized … and he brought him to Jerusalem and made a breach in the wall of Jerusalem, from the Gate of Ephraim as far as the gate that turns"** (v23) — the object stands first in the Hebrew; תָּפַשׂ "seize"; וַיְבִיאֵהוּ "brought him" (Kings qere: "he came"); וַיִּפְרֹץ בְּחוֹמַת "broke through in the wall"; מִשַּׁעַר "from the gate" (Kings: "at the gate"). שַׁעַר הַפּוֹנֶה is, as written, "the gate that turns" (or "that faces"); Kings has שַׁעַר הַפִּנָּה "the Gate of the Corner" (so also 2 Chr 26:9), which differs by one letter and is commonly read here. The Masoretic Text is kept. Chronicles calls Amaziah's grandfather "Jehoahaz" (Kings: "Ahaziah"): the same king under the other form of his name (2 Chr 21:17).
- **"And he took all the gold … and the hostages also, and returned to Samaria" → "And all the gold and the silver and all the vessels … and the hostages — and he returned to Samaria"** (v24) — the Hebrew of Chronicles has no verb "took" (Kings: וְלָקַח "and he took"): the things are named, and the sentence goes on with "and he returned". It is left so. "With Obed-edom" (עִם־עֹבֵד אֱדוֹם) is Chronicles' own: the vessels in the keeping of Obed-edom, the house of gatekeepers set over the stores (1 Chr 26:15). אֹצְרוֹת "the treasuries" is here an object (Kings: "and in the treasuries"); Kings has "the house of Yahweh". **"the hostages"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי הַתַּעֲרֻבוֹת, literally "the sons of the pledges" (from עָרַב "be surety"); found only here and in 2 Kgs 14:14.
- **"the rest of the acts of Amaziah, first and last, behold, are they not written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel?" → "the rest of the acts of Amaziah, the first and the last, are they not, behold, written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel?"** (v26) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי "the words / matters of Amaziah"; הֲלֹא הִנָּם "are they not, behold": both words stand. The source is named as Chronicles names it (Kings: "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah").
- **"Now after the time that Amaziah did turn away from following the LORD they made a conspiracy against him" → "And from the time that Amaziah turned aside from after Yahweh, they conspired a conspiracy against him"** (v27) — the time clause is Chronicles' own; Hebrew סָר מֵאַחֲרֵי "turned aside from after"; וַיִּקְשְׁרוּ … קֶשֶׁר, verb and noun of one root. **"slew him" → "put him to death"** — Hebrew הֵמִית.
- **"they brought him upon horses, and buried him with his fathers in the city of Judah" → "they carried him upon the horses and buried him with his fathers in the city of Judah"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיִּשָּׂאֻהוּ עַל־הַסּוּסִים, with the article. "The city of Judah" is what the Hebrew has; Kings has "he was buried in Jerusalem with his fathers, in the city of David", and "city of David" is commonly read here. The Masoretic Text is kept.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 14:1-20** — Chronicler expands on Edom campaign and hired-Israelite-army incident (unique content v5-13), and Amaziah's idolatry (v14-16).
- **Deut 24:16 cited directly** (v4) — same as 2 Kgs 14:6; individual-responsibility principle.
- **Hired northern mercenaries** — Amaziah pays 100 talents, the man of Elohim objects, Amaziah sends them back. But the dismissed band raids the cities of Judah.
- **Amaziah's fatal idolatry** — brings back the gods of the sons of Seir. Chronicler's distinct irony: he bows down to defeated gods.
- **Chronicler's theology** — defeat at Beth-shemesh is explicit judgment for seeking the gods of Edom (v20).
- **Crag-top execution** (v12) — Hebrew *sela* = crag; perhaps the stronghold Sela of 2 Kgs 14:7, later identified by many with Petra.
- **Assassination** — like his father Joash, Amaziah dies by conspiracy.
