# 2 Chronicles 20

[1] And it came to pass after this that the sons of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and the sons of Ammon, and with them some of the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧, came against ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ to battle.

[2] And they came and told ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, saying, "A great multitude is coming against you from across the sea, from ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧; and behold, they are in ⟦hazazon-tamar|Chatzatzon Tamar|Hazazon-tamar⟧" (that is ⟦en-gedi|Ein Gedi|Engedi⟧).

[4] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ gathered to seek from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; also from all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ they came to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ stood in the assembly of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, before the new court.

[6] And he said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our fathers, are you not ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the heavens? And you rule over all the kingdoms of the nations, and in your hand are power and might, and there is none to take his stand against you.

[7] Did not you, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, dispossess the inhabitants of this land from before your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and give it to the seed of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, who loved you, forever?

[8] And they dwelt in it, and they built for you in it a sanctuary for your name, saying,

[9] 'If evil comes upon us, the sword, judgment, and pestilence and famine, we will stand before this house and before you, for your name is in this house, and we will cry out to you from our distress, and you will hear and save.'

[10] And now, behold, the sons of Ammon and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and the hill country of Seir, among whom you did not let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ come when they came from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, for they turned aside from them and did not destroy them:

[11] and behold, they are dealing so with us, coming to drive us out from your possession, which you gave us to possess.

[12] Our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, will you not judge them? For there is no power in us before this great multitude that is coming against us, and we, we do not know what we shall do, but our eyes are upon you."

[13] And all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ were standing before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, also their little ones, their wives and their sons.

[14] And ⟦jahaziel|Yachaziel|Jahaziel⟧ the son of ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧, the son of ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧, the son of ⟦jeiel|Yeiel|Jeiel⟧, the son of ⟦mattaniah|Mattanyah|Mattaniah⟧, the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, of the sons of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧: the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon him in the midst of the assembly.

[15] And he said, "Give heed, all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and King ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧! Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to you: 'You, do not fear and do not be dismayed because of this great multitude, for the battle is not yours, but ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧.

[16] Tomorrow go down against them. Behold, they are coming up by the ascent of the ⟦ziz|Tzitz|Ziz⟧, and you will find them at the end of the wadi, in front of the wilderness of ⟦jeruel|Yeruel|Jeruel⟧.

[17] It is not for you to fight in this. Take your stand, stand, and see the salvation of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with you, ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. Do not fear and do not be dismayed. Tomorrow go out before them, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is with you.'"

[18] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ bowed with his face to the ground, and all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ fell before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to bow down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[19] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, of the sons of the ⟦kohathites|Qehatim|Kohathites⟧ and of the sons of the ⟦korahites|Qorchim|Korahites⟧, arose to praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|the Elohim|the God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, with a loud voice, exceedingly.

And as they went out, ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ stood and said, "Hear me, ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧! Believe in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall be made firm; believe in his prophets, and prosper."

[21] And he took counsel with the people, and he made singers to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ stand, and those giving praise to the splendor of holiness, as they went out before the armed men, and saying,
"Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever."

And the ships were broken, and they did not retain power to go to Tarshish.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v15 לֵאלֹהִים "Elohim's".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"and with them other beside the Ammonites" → "and with them some of the Ammonites"** (v1) — Hebrew וְעִמָּהֶם מֵהָעַמּוֹנִים, "and with them some of the Ammonites", after "the sons of Ammon" have been named. The Septuagint is reported to read "of the Meunites" (*me-ha-Me'unim*: two letters change places; cf. 26:7, and "Mount Seir" in v10, 22, 23), and the earlier text followed it. The Masoretic Text is kept. **"children" → "sons"** (v1, 10, 13, 22, 23) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"from beyond the sea on this side Syria" → "from across the sea, from Aram"** (v2) — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לַיָּם מֵאֲרָם; the sea is the Salt Sea. One Hebrew manuscript is reported to read "from Edom" (אדם for ארם: one letter), which fits the place; the Masoretic Text is kept. **"there cometh" → "is coming"** — Hebrew בָּא (a participle, or a perfect "has come"). "That is En-gedi" may belong to the speech or to the writer.
- **"set himself to seek the LORD" → "set his face to seek Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן … אֶת־פָּנָיו; the verb is "give". **"seek"** (v3, 4) — דָּרַשׁ in v3, בִּקֵּשׁ twice in v4: two verbs, one English word. **"to ask help of the LORD" → "to seek from Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew לְבַקֵּשׁ מֵיְהוָה; "help" is not written.
- **"congregation" → "assembly"** (v5, 14) — Hebrew קָהָל.
- **"art not thou God in heaven?" → "are you not Elohim in the heavens?"** (v6) — Hebrew הֲלֹא אַתָּה־הוּא אֱלֹהִים בַּשָּׁמַיִם, "are you not he — Elohim in the heavens". **"rulest not thou over all the kingdoms of the heathen?" → "And you rule over all the kingdoms of the nations"** — a statement in the Hebrew. **"power and might"** — כֹּחַ וּגְבוּרָה (1 Chr 29:12). **"so that none is able to withstand thee" → "and there is none to take his stand against you"** — Hebrew וְאֵין עִמְּךָ לְהִתְיַצֵּב: הִתְיַצֵּב "take one's stand" (the verb of v17); עִם is "with", here of one who stands up to another.
- **"didst drive out the inhabitants" → "dispossess the inhabitants"** (v7) — Hebrew הוֹרַשְׁתָּ, the causative of "possess"; the same verb ends v11. **"Abraham thy friend" → "Abraham, who loved you"** — Hebrew אֹהַבְךָ, the participle of "love": "your lover", one who loves you (Isa 41:8; Jas 2:23 "friend of God").
- **"the sword, judgment, or pestilence, or famine" → "the sword, judgment, and pestilence and famine"** (v9) — Hebrew חֶרֶב שְׁפוֹט וְדֶבֶר וְרָעָב: שְׁפוֹט "judgment" stands beside "sword" without "and"; it may be read "the sword of judgment". The prayer takes up 6:28–30. **"in our affliction" → "from our distress"** — Hebrew מִצָּרָתֵנוּ. **"help" → "save"** — Hebrew וְתוֹשִׁיעַ.
- **"mount Seir" → "the hill country of Seir"** (v10, 22, 23) — Hebrew הַר־שֵׂעִיר: הַר is "mountain", of a region "hill country". **"whom thou wouldest not let Israel invade" → "among whom you did not let Israel come"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא־נָתַתָּה לְיִשְׂרָאֵל לָבוֹא בָהֶם, literally "whom you did not give to Israel to come among them" (Deut 2:4–19).
- **"how they reward us" → "they are dealing so with us"** (v11) — Hebrew גֹּמְלִים עָלֵינוּ, "deal with, repay"; "so" is supplied. **"to cast us out of thy possession, which thou hast given us to inherit" → "to drive us out from your possession, which you gave us to possess"** — Hebrew לְגָרְשֵׁנוּ מִיְּרֻשָּׁתְךָ אֲשֶׁר הוֹרַשְׁתָּנוּ: noun and verb of one root.
- **"we have no might against this great company" → "there is no power in us before this great multitude"** (v12) — Hebrew כֹּחַ, the word of v6; הָמוֹן "multitude" (v2, 12, 15, 24). **"neither know we what to do" → "we, we do not know what we shall do"** — the pronoun is written out.
- **"their little ones, their wives, and their children" → "their little ones, their wives and their sons"** (v13) — Hebrew טַפָּם נְשֵׁיהֶם וּבְנֵיהֶם.
- **"came the Spirit of the LORD" → "the spirit of Yahweh was upon him"** (v14) — Hebrew הָיְתָה עָלָיו רוּחַ יְהוָה, the verb "be".
- **"Hearken ye" → "Give heed"** (v15) — Hebrew הַקְשִׁיבוּ (another verb than "hear", v20). **"Be not afraid nor dismayed by reason of" → "You, do not fear and do not be dismayed because of"** — Hebrew אַתֶּם "you", written out; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of, because of". **"the battle is not yours, but God's"** — Hebrew כִּי לֹא לָכֶם הַמִּלְחָמָה כִּי לֵאלֹהִים (1 Sam 17:47).
- **"the cliff of Ziz" → "the ascent of the Ziz"** (v16) — Hebrew מַעֲלֵה הַצִּיץ, with the article. **"the brook" → "the wadi"** — Hebrew הַנַּחַל. **"before" → "in front of"** — Hebrew פְּנֵי.
- **"Ye shall not need to fight in this battle: set yourselves, stand ye still, and see the salvation of the LORD" → "It is not for you to fight in this. Take your stand, stand, and see the salvation of Yahweh"** (v17) — Hebrew לֹא לָכֶם לְהִלָּחֵם בָּזֹאת; "battle" is not written. הִתְיַצְּבוּ … וּרְאוּ אֶת־יְשׁוּעַת יְהוָה are the words of Moses at the sea (Exod 14:13), with עִמְדוּ "stand" added. **"for the LORD will be with you" → "and Yahweh is with you"** — Hebrew וַיהוָה עִמָּכֶם, no verb.
- **"bowed his head with his face to the ground" → "bowed with his face to the ground"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיִּקֹּד … אַפַּיִם אַרְצָה: אַפַּיִם is "nostrils", the face. **"worshipping the LORD" → "to bow down to Yahweh"** — Hebrew לְהִשְׁתַּחֲוֺת.
- **"stood up" → "arose"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיָּקֻמוּ. **"with a loud voice on high" → "with a loud voice, exceedingly"** — Hebrew בְּקוֹל גָּדוֹל לְמָעְלָה, literally "with a great voice, upward" (as 17:12).
- **"Believe in the LORD your God, so shall ye be established; believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper" → "Believe in Yahweh your Elohim, and you shall be made firm; believe in his prophets, and prosper"** (v20) — Hebrew הַאֲמִינוּ בַּיהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם וְתֵאָמֵנוּ הַאֲמִינוּ בִנְבִיאָיו וְהַצְלִיחוּ. "Believe" and "be made firm" are one Hebrew verb, אמן "be firm", in two stems: the causative הֶאֱמִין, "hold firm to, trust, believe", and the passive-reflexive נֶאֱמַן, "be made firm, prove firm, endure". The play is that of Isa 7:9 (אִם לֹא תַאֲמִינוּ כִּי לֹא תֵאָמֵנוּ, "if you do not believe, surely you shall not be made firm"); English has no one word for both, and "believe" is kept for the first because it is the word of faith through both Testaments (Gen 15:6). Elsewhere the second form is "be sure" (of a house, a word: 2 Sam 7:16; 1 Chr 17:23); of persons that would say "be certain", so "be made firm" stands here. The first clause ends in a promise ("you shall be made firm"), the second in a command ("and prosper"). Both times the verb has בְּ "in".
- **"when he had consulted with the people, he appointed singers unto the LORD, and that should praise the beauty of holiness" → "he took counsel with the people, and he made singers to Yahweh stand, and those giving praise to the splendor of holiness"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֵד, as 19:5, 8; מְשֹׁרֲרִים לַיהוָה וּמְהַלְלִים לְהַדְרַת־קֹדֶשׁ: לְ stands before "Yahweh" and before "the splendor of holiness" alike. The sense of the second is uncertain: praise given to the holy splendor (of Yahweh), or praise given "in holy splendor", that is, in holy attire (1 Chr 16:29 has בְּ "in"). **"before the army" → "before the armed men"** — Hebrew הֶחָלוּץ (as 17:18).
- **"Praise the LORD; for his mercy endureth for ever" → "Give thanks to Yahweh, for his *chesed* is forever"** (v21) — Hebrew הוֹדוּ לַיהוָה כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ, the refrain of 1 Chr 16:34, 41; 2 Chr 5:13; 7:3 and Ps 136, here without "for he is good"; no verb in the second half.
- **"when they began to sing and to praise" → "at the time they began with a cry and praise"** (v22) — Hebrew הֵחֵלּוּ בְרִנָּה וּתְהִלָּה, two nouns: רִנָּה is a ringing cry. **"set ambushments" → "placed men in ambush"** — Hebrew נָתַן … מְאָרְבִים, a participle, "liers in wait"; who they were is not said. **"were smitten"** — Hebrew וַיִּנָּגֵפוּ.
- **"utterly to slay and destroy them … every one helped to destroy another" → "to devote them to destruction and to destroy … they helped, each man against his neighbor, for ruin"** (v23) — three Hebrew words: לְהַחֲרִים (devote to the ban), וּלְהַשְׁמִיד "destroy", לְמַשְׁחִית "for ruin / to destroy" (the root of "the destroyer", Exod 12:23). אִישׁ־בְּרֵעֵהוּ "each man against his neighbor". **"stood up against"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַמְדוּ … עַל.
- **"the watch tower in the wilderness" → "the lookout toward the wilderness"** (v24) — Hebrew הַמִּצְפֶּה לַמִּדְבָּר. **"dead bodies fallen to the earth, and none escaped" → "carcasses fallen to the ground, and there was no escape"** — Hebrew פְּגָרִים נֹפְלִים אַרְצָה וְאֵין פְּלֵיטָה: פְּלֵיטָה is the escaping or those who have escaped.
- **"to take away the spoil of them … both riches with the dead bodies, and precious jewels, which they stripped off for themselves, more than they could carry away" → "to plunder their spoil … both goods and carcasses and desirable utensils, and they stripped them off for themselves until there was no carrying"** (v25) — Hebrew לָבֹז אֶת־שְׁלָלָם; וּרְכוּשׁ וּפְגָרִים וּכְלֵי חֲמֻדוֹת: "carcasses" is what the Masoretic Text has; some Hebrew manuscripts are reported to read בְגָדִים "garments" (from memory), which the earlier text followed; the Masoretic Text is kept. בָהֶם "among them" may also be read with the Septuagint's "cattle" (בְּהֵמָה, from memory). כְּלֵי חֲמֻדוֹת "utensils (articles) of desirable things" (36:10); וַיְנַצְּלוּ "strip" (Exod 3:22; 12:36); לְאֵין מַשָּׂא, literally "to there being no burden / carrying". **"three days in gathering of the spoil" → "three days plundering the spoil"** — Hebrew בֹּזְזִים.
- **"the valley of Berachah" kept** (v26) — Hebrew עֵמֶק בְּרָכָה *Emeq Berakhah*, "valley of blessing": the name is explained in the verse by "there they blessed (בֵּרֲכוּ) Yahweh". **"assembled themselves in" → "assembled at"** — Hebrew נִקְהֲלוּ לְ. **"was called … unto this day" → "they called … until today"** — Hebrew קָרְאוּ … עַד־הַיּוֹם.
- **"in the forefront of them, to go again to Jerusalem with joy; for the LORD had made them to rejoice over their enemies" → "at their head, to return to Jerusalem with gladness, for Yahweh had gladdened them over their enemies"** (v27) — Hebrew בְּרֹאשָׁם; בְּשִׂמְחָה כִּי־שִׂמְּחָם, noun and verb of one root; מֵאוֹיְבֵיהֶם, literally "from their enemies". "Returned … to return": the verb stands twice.
- **"psalteries and harps" → "harps and lyres"** (v28) — Hebrew נְבָלִים וּכִנֹּרוֹת; the kinds are not certain.
- **"the fear of God was on" → "the dread of Elohim was upon"** (v29) — Hebrew פַּחַד, as 17:10; 19:7. **"fought against" → "fought with"** — Hebrew נִלְחַם … עִם.
- **"the realm of Jehoshaphat was quiet: for his God gave him rest round about" → "the kingdom of Jehoshaphat was quiet, and his Elohim gave him rest all around"** (v30) — Hebrew מַלְכוּת (kingdom, reign); וַיָּנַח "and … gave rest".
- **Parallel: 1 Kgs 22:41–49** (v31–37) — where the Hebrew is the same, the wording is that of Kings. Chronicles has no date by the year of Ahab (1 Kgs 22:41) and begins "And Jehoshaphat reigned over Judah". **"thirty and five years old"** (v31) — literally "a son of thirty-five years"; the verb "reign" is used here of the beginning ("when he began to reign").
- **"he walked in the way of Asa his father, and departed not from it" → "he walked in the way of his father Asa and did not turn aside from it"** (v32) — Kings has "in all the way of Asa his father; he did not turn aside from it". **"right in the sight of" → "right in the eyes of"**.
- **"Howbeit the high places were not taken away: for as yet the people had not prepared their hearts unto the God of their fathers" → "Only, the high places did not depart, and the people had still not established their heart toward the Elohim of their fathers"** (v33) — Hebrew לֹא־סָרוּ, the verb of v32 ("turn aside, depart"); וְעוֹד הָעָם לֹא־הֵכִינוּ לְבָבָם לְ, as 19:3. Kings has here "the people were still sacrificing and making smoke on the high places". 17:6 says that he removed the high places from Judah; the two statements stand side by side.
- **"first and last"** (v34) — Hebrew הָרִאשֹׁנִים וְהָאַחֲרֹנִים, not in Kings. **"written in the book of Jehu the son of Hanani, who is mentioned in the book of the kings of Israel" → "written in the words of Jehu the son of Hanani, which were taken up into the book of the kings of Israel"** — Hebrew בְּדִבְרֵי "in the words (acts) of", the same word as "the acts of Jehoshaphat"; no "book" is written with Jehu. אֲשֶׁר הֹעֲלָה עַל־סֵפֶר, literally "which was made to go up upon the book": the verb is singular and may also be read of Jehu ("who was taken up"). Kings names "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah".
- **"did Jehoshaphat … join himself with Ahaziah king of Israel, who did very wickedly" → "Jehoshaphat … joined himself with Ahaziah king of Israel; he did wickedly in his doing"** (v35) — Hebrew אֶתְחַבַּר (a late, Aramaic-like form); הוּא הִרְשִׁיעַ לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "he acted wickedly to do": "he" may be Ahaziah or Jehoshaphat, and the Hebrew does not decide; "his" is supplied, "very" is not written. Kings has no such verse here; it has "And Jehoshaphat made peace with the king of Israel" (1 Kgs 22:44).
- **"he joined himself with him to make ships to go to Tarshish" → "he joined him with himself to make ships to go to Tarshish"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיְחַבְּרֵהוּ עִמּוֹ, the causative with an object: one king joined the other with himself. Kings says: "Jehoshaphat made ships of Tarshish to go to Ophir for the gold" (1 Kgs 22:48): there "Tarshish" names the kind of ship, here the place they were to go. Each text is translated as it stands.
- **"Because thou hast joined thyself with Ahaziah, the LORD hath broken thy works" → "As you have joined yourself with Ahaziah, Yahweh has breached your works"** (v37) — Hebrew כְּהִתְחַבֶּרְךָ "as / when you joined yourself"; פָּרַץ "break through, make a breach in" (1 Chr 13:11; 15:13), another verb than "were broken" (וַיִּשָּׁבְרוּ) of the ships. **"were not able to go" → "did not retain power to go"** — Hebrew וְלֹא עָצְרוּ לָלֶכֶת, "did not retain … to go": "power" is supplied, as in 14:11 (the full phrase עָצַר כֹּחַ stands in 13:20; 1 Chr 29:14); the sense is "were not able". In Kings Ahaziah offers his servants for the ships after they are broken, and Jehoshaphat is not willing (1 Kgs 22:49); in Chronicles the joining comes first and the prophet gives it as the cause. Ahaziah is written אֲחַזְיָה in v35 and אֲחַזְיָהוּ in v37.
- **Numbers** (v31) — written out in the ordinary English order.

### Notes
- **Unique to Chronicler** — no parallel to this battle in Kings.
- **Jehoshaphat's prayer** (v6-12) — model public-crisis prayer. Appeals to creation-sovereignty, Abrahamic covenant, temple-dedication promise (ch 6), historical precedent, human weakness.
- **Jahaziel the Levite prophet** (v14) — spontaneous Asaphite oracle. Chronicler's theology: Levites prophesy.
- **Singers before army** (v21-22) — unique: worship as warfare. Enemies self-destruct while Judah praises.
- **Self-annihilation of enemies** — Ammon/Moab turn on Seir, then each other. Divine confusion like Midianites with Gideon (Judg 7).
- **Valley of Berakah** — near Tekoa; etymologized as "Blessing" from the post-victory worship.
- **Chronicler's paradigmatic battle** — faith + song + divine initiative; Yahweh alone does the fighting.
- **Late apostasy** — Jehoshaphat's final alliance with Ahaziah of Israel incurs prophetic rebuke. Chronicler never fully condones the Omride alliance.
