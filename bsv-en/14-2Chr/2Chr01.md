# 2 Chronicles 1

[1] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ the son of David strengthened himself over his kingdom, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was with him and made him exceedingly great.

[2] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ said to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to the commanders of the thousands and the hundreds, and to the judges, and to every chieftain of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the heads of the fathers.

[3] And they went, ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and all the assembly with him, to the high place that was in ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧, for there was the tent of meeting of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had made in the wilderness.

[4] But the ark of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ David had brought up from ⟦kiriath-jearim|Qiryat Yearim|Kiriath-jearim⟧ to where David had prepared for it, for he had pitched for it a tent in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[5] And the altar of bronze that ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ the son of Uri, the son of ⟦hur|Chur|Hur⟧, had made he had put before the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and the assembly sought it.

[6] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ went up there upon the altar of bronze before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which belonged to the tent of meeting, and he offered up on it a thousand burnt offerings.

[7] In that night ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ appeared to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and said to him, "Ask what I shall give you."

[11] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, "Because this was with your heart, and you have not asked riches, wealth and glory, and the soul of those who hate you, and also many days you have not asked, and you have asked for yourself wisdom and knowledge, that you may judge my people over whom I have made you king,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v3, 4 the Hebrew has the article (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"was strengthened in his kingdom" → "strengthened himself over his kingdom"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּתְחַזֵּק … עַל־מַלְכוּתוֹ: a reflexive verb and the preposition עַל "over". **"magnified him exceedingly" → "made him exceedingly great"** — Hebrew וַיְגַדְּלֵהוּ לְמָעְלָה, literally "made him great upward", as 1 Chr 29:25.
- **"spake unto all Israel" → "said to all Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר "said"; what he said is not given (he gave the word to go, v3). **"captains" → "commanders"** — Hebrew שָׂרֵי, the word "prince, chief", here of army units. **"governor" → "chieftain"** — Hebrew נָשִׂיא, "one lifted up". **"the chief of the fathers" → "the heads of the fathers"** — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת.
- **"congregation" → "assembly"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew קָהָל. **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (v3, 6, 13) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"the servant of Yahweh"** — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word "slave".
- **"to the place which David had prepared for it" → "to where David had prepared for it"** (v4) — Hebrew בַּהֵכִין לוֹ דָּוִיד; the Hebrew has no word "place". אֲבָל is "but" in the late books.
- **"the brasen altar … he put before the tabernacle of the LORD" → "the altar of bronze … he had put before the dwelling of Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling". The Masoretic Text points שָׂם "he put"; who put it is not said. The same letters pointed שָׁם are "was there", and so many manuscripts and the old versions read (from memory). The printed pointing is translated.
- **"sought unto it" → "sought it"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיִּדְרְשֵׁהוּ. The suffix can be "it" (the altar) or "him" (Yahweh); the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"went up thither to the brasen altar" → "went up there upon the altar of bronze"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיַּעַל … עַל, "went up upon". The same form can be read "offered up there on the altar"; the second וַיַּעַל has an object and is "offered up" (literally "made go up", one root with עֹלָה "burnt offering"). 1 Kgs 3:4 has another form, "would offer up".
- **"which was at the tabernacle" → "which belonged to the tent of meeting"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לְאֹהֶל מוֹעֵד, said of the altar.
- **"In that night did God appear unto Solomon"** (v7) — 1 Kgs 3:5 has "Yahweh appeared to Solomon in a dream of the night"; Chronicles has *Elohim* and names no dream.
- **"Thou hast shewed great mercy unto David" → "You have done with David my father great chesed"** (v8) — Hebrew עָשִׂיתָ עִם … חֶסֶד גָּדוֹל, the same words as 1 Kgs 3:6, which adds "your servant". **"hast made me to reign in his stead" → "have made me king in his place"** — Hebrew הִמְלַכְתַּנִי תַּחְתָּיו.
- **"let thy promise unto David my father be established" → "let your word with David my father be sure"** (v9) — Hebrew יֵאָמֵן דְּבָרְךָ עִם: דָּבָר is "word", נֶאֱמַן "be sure, firm", as 1 Chr 17:23. **"a people like the dust of the earth in multitude" → "a numerous people, like the dust of the earth"** — Hebrew עַם רַב כַּעֲפַר הָאָרֶץ.
- **"Give me now wisdom and knowledge" → "Now wisdom and knowledge give me"** (v10) — the Hebrew order, with the things asked first. מַדָּע is a late word for "knowledge", of one root with דַּעַת. **"for who can judge this thy people, that is so great?" → "for who shall judge this great people of yours?"** — Hebrew מִי יִשְׁפֹּט … הַגָּדוֹל. 1 Kgs 3:9 has "who is able to judge this heavy people of yours" (יוּכַל, הַכָּבֵד); Chronicles has neither word.
- **"this was in thine heart" → "this was with your heart"** (v11) — Hebrew עִם־לְבָבֶךָ. **"riches, wealth, or honour" → "riches, wealth and glory"** — Hebrew עֹשֶׁר נְכָסִים וְכָבוֹד; כָּבוֹד is "glory". **"the life of thine enemies" → "the soul of those who hate you"** — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ שֹׂנְאֶיךָ; 1 Kgs 3:11 has אֹיְבֶיךָ "your enemies". **"long life" → "many days"** — Hebrew יָמִים רַבִּים. The order of the things not asked differs from 1 Kgs 3:11, and "wealth" and "glory" are not there.
- **"that thou mayest judge my people"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר תִּשְׁפּוֹט, literally "which you shall judge": the wisdom by which he is to judge, or "so that you may judge".
- **"Wisdom and knowledge is granted unto thee" → "the wisdom and the knowledge are given to you"** (v12) — the nouns have the article; נָתוּן is "given". **"such as none of the kings have had that have been before thee, neither shall there any after thee have the like" → "the like of which the kings who were before you did not have, and after you there shall not be the like"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא־הָיָה כֵן לַמְּלָכִים … וְאַחֲרֶיךָ לֹא יִהְיֶה־כֵּן, literally "which was not so for the kings who were before you, and after you it shall not be so".
- **"Then Solomon came from his journey to the high place that was at Gibeon to Jerusalem" → "And Solomon came, to the high place that was in Gibeon, to Jerusalem"** (v13) — the Masoretic Text has לַבָּמָה "to the high place", which does not fit a journey away from Gibeon; the Septuagint and the Vulgate read "from the high place" (from memory), and so the verse is commonly corrected. The Masoretic Text is translated word for word; "to" before "Jerusalem" is supplied (the Hebrew has the bare name). **"from before the tabernacle of the congregation" → "from before the tent of meeting"** — Hebrew מִלִּפְנֵי.
- **"which he placed in the chariot cities" → "and he set them in the cities of the chariots"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיַּנִּיחֵם, from הִנִּיחַ "set down" (as 4:8). The parallel 1 Kgs 10:26 is pointed וַיַּנְחֵם "and he led them"; the rest of the verse is the same, word for word. רֶכֶב "chariots" is a collective.
- **"made silver and gold at Jerusalem as plenteous as stones" → "made the silver and the gold in Jerusalem like the stones"** (v15) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "gave"; the nouns have the article; "plenteous" is not in the Hebrew. 1 Kgs 10:27 has only "the silver". **"sycomore trees that are in the vale for abundance" → "sycamores that are in the Shephelah, for abundance"** — Hebrew שְׁפֵלָה, the lowland; the tree is the sycamore fig.
- **"Solomon had horses brought out of Egypt, and linen yarn: the king's merchants received the linen yarn at a price" → "the going out of the horses that Solomon had was from Egypt, and from Kue: the traders of the king, from Kue they would take them at a price"** (v16) — Hebrew וּמוֹצָא הַסּוּסִים … מִמִּצְרָיִם וּמִקְוֵא סֹחֲרֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ מִקְוֵא יִקְחוּ בִּמְחִיר, as 1 Kgs 10:28, with מִקְוֵא spelled with another last letter and the second "from Kue" before its verb. מוֹצָא is "going out": where they came from. מִקְוֵא is taken as "from Qeveh" (Kue, a land in the south-east of Asia Minor). The same letters can be read as a noun, "a gathering": "and the gathering of the king's traders would take a gathering (a drove) at a price"; "linen yarn" is an old guess. "Them" is supplied.
- **"they fetched up, and brought forth out of Egypt a chariot" → "they brought up and brought out from Egypt a chariot"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲלוּ וַיּוֹצִיאוּ, two plural verbs with the traders as subject (the first can also be read "they went up"). 1 Kgs 10:29 has "a chariot came up and went out". **"six hundred shekels of silver" → "six hundred of silver"** — no weight is named in the Hebrew. **"so brought they out horses for all the kings … by their means" → "and so for all the kings … by their hand they would bring them out"** — Hebrew בְּיָדָם יוֹצִיאוּ; "horses" is not in the Hebrew. 1 Kgs 10:29 has "and for the kings of Aram"; Chronicles has no second "for".
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲרָם.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 3:4–15 and 10:26–29** — Chronicles tells of Solomon's visit to Gibeon and his asking for wisdom in its own words. It names no dream; it presents an appearance in the night. Where the Hebrew is the same as in Kings, word for word, the wording is the same (v7b, v8a, v14, v15, v16).
- **Gibeon** — the place of the tent of meeting and the altar of bronze. Chronicles explains why Solomon worshiped away from Jerusalem: the tent that Moses made was there.
- **Two places of worship** — the ark in Jerusalem (v4), the tent of meeting in Gibeon (v3).
- **Solomon's request** — in Chronicles "wisdom and knowledge" to "go out before this people and come in"; in 1 Kgs 3:9 "a hearing heart, to judge your people".
- **Chariot cities, horse trade** — Deut 17:16 forbids the king to multiply horses and to make the people return to Egypt for horses. Chronicles does not comment.
- **Kue** — a land in Cilicia (south-east Asia Minor), known for horses.
- **Chronicles opens Solomon's reign** with Yahweh's favor, wisdom and wealth; the struggle with Adonijah (1 Kgs 1–2) is not told.
