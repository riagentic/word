# 2 Chronicles 13

[1] In the eighteenth year of King ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ began to reign over ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[2] Three years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦micaiah|Michayahu|Micaiah⟧ the daughter of Uriel, from ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧. And there was war between ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧.

[3] And ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ joined the battle with a force of mighty ones of war, four hundred thousand chosen men; and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ drew up for battle against him with eight hundred thousand chosen men, mighty ones of ability.

[4] And ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ rose up upon Mount ⟦zemaraim|Tzemarayim|Zemaraim⟧, which is in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and said, "Hear me, ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!

[5] Is it not for you to know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, gave a kingdom to David over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ forever, to him and to his sons — a ⟦berit-melach|berit melach|covenant of salt⟧?

[6] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧, the servant of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ the son of David, rose up and rebelled against his lord.

[7] And there gathered about him empty men, ⟦belial|sons of Beliyyaal|sons of worthlessness⟧, and they made themselves firm against ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ the son of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧; and ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ was a young man and soft of heart, and he did not strengthen himself before them.

[8] And now you mean to strengthen yourselves before the kingdom of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the hand of the sons of David; and you are a great multitude, and with you are the calves of gold that ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ made for you for gods.

[9] Have you not driven away the priests of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and made for yourselves priests like the peoples of the lands? Everyone who comes to fill his hand with a bull of the herd and seven rams, he becomes a priest to no-gods.

[10] And as for us, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and we have not forsaken him; and priests are ministering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ in the work.

[11] And they turn into smoke to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burnt offerings morning by morning and evening by evening, and fragrant incense, and an arrangement of bread on the pure table, and the lampstand of gold and its lamps, to burn evening by evening; for we are keeping the charge of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you have forsaken him.

[12] And behold, with us at the head is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and his priests, and the trumpets of the blast, to sound a blast against you. Sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, do not fight with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers, for you shall not prosper."

[13] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ brought the ambush around to come from behind them; and they were before ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and the ambush was behind them.

[14] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ turned, and behold, they had the battle front and behind; and they cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the priests were sounding the trumpets.

[15] And the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shouted; and it came to pass, when the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shouted, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ smote ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ before ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[16] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ fled from the face of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave them into their hand.

[17] And ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ and his people struck them a great blow, and there fell slain of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ five hundred thousand chosen men.

[18] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were brought low at that time, and the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ were firm, because they leaned on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers.

[19] And ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ pursued after ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ and captured cities from him: ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and its daughters, and ⟦jeshanah|Yeshanah|Jeshanah⟧ and its daughters, and Efrayin and its daughters.

[20] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ did not retain power any more in the days of ⟦abijah|Aviyahu|Abijah⟧; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ smote him, and he died.

[21] And ⟦abijah|Aviyahu|Abijah⟧ strengthened himself, and he took for himself fourteen wives and begot twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.

[22] And the rest of the acts of ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧, and his ways and his words, are written in the ⟦midrash|Midrash|Story⟧ of the prophet Iddo.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v12, 15 the Hebrew has the article (הָאֱלֹהִים), in v16 it has none; the verse has one form.
- **"Now in the eighteenth year … began Abijah to reign" → "In the eighteenth year … Abijah began to reign"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּמְלֹךְ, "and Abijah reigned"; the verb is "reign", here of the beginning. The "and" before the verb after the date cannot stand in English. 1 Kgs 15:1 has the same sentence with "And", "the son of Nebat" and the name Abijam; Chronicles has none of the three.
- **"He reigned three years … His mother's name also was Michaiah … of Gibeah" → "Three years he reigned … And the name of his mother was Michayahu the daughter of Uriel, from Gibeah"** (v2) — the Hebrew order and מִן "from". Chronicles prints מִיכָיָהוּ בַת־אוּרִיאֵל; 1 Kgs 15:2 and 2 Chr 11:20 have "Maacah the daughter of Abishalom / Absalom". Each is translated as printed.
- **"set the battle in array with an army of valiant men of war" → "joined the battle with a force of mighty ones of war"** (v3) — וַיֶּאְסֹר אֶת־הַמִּלְחָמָה, literally "bound the battle", as 1 Kgs 20:14; חַיִל "force"; גִּבּוֹרֵי מִלְחָמָה.
- **"set the battle in array against him … being mighty men of valour" → "drew up for battle against him … mighty ones of ability"** (v3) — עָרַךְ עִמּוֹ מִלְחָמָה, literally "with him"; גִּבּוֹר חָיִל and אִישׁ בָּחוּר are singular in Hebrew, said of the whole number. The numbers are as printed: four hundred thousand, eight hundred thousand, and in v17 five hundred thousand.
- **"stood up upon mount Zemaraim" → "rose up upon Mount Zemaraim"** (v4) — וַיָּקָם מֵעַל לְהַר, literally "rose up from above to the mount": up on it, above them. הַר אֶפְרַיִם "the hill country of Ephraim" is the same word "mount".
- **"Ought ye not to know" → "Is it not for you to know"** (v5) — הֲלֹא לָכֶם לָדַעַת, word for word.
- **"gave the kingdom over Israel to David for ever, even to him and to his sons by a covenant of salt" → "gave a kingdom to David over Israel forever, to him and to his sons — a covenant of salt"** (v5) — מַמְלָכָה has no article; בְּרִית מֶלַח stands beside the sentence with no "by". The phrase as in Num 18:19; the verse does not say what the salt stands for.
- **"Yet Jeroboam … is risen up, and hath rebelled" → "And Jeroboam … rose up and rebelled"** (v6) — two narrative verbs with "and". עֶבֶד "servant" (of a king's man; the word is "slave"). אֲדֹנָיו "his lord" is plural in form.
- **"vain men, the children of Belial" → "empty men, sons of Beliyyaal"** (v7) — רֵקִים "empty"; בְּנֵי בְלִיַּעַל, a common noun, "worthlessness", not a name. עָלָיו "about him", literally "upon him".
- **"have strengthened themselves against Rehoboam" → "made themselves firm against Rehoboam"** (v7) — וַיִּתְאַמְּצוּ, root אמץ; "strengthen himself" in the same verse is another root, הִתְחַזֵּק (also v8, 21). The two are kept apart; the same root אמץ is "were firm" in v18.
- **"when Rehoboam was young and tenderhearted, and could not withstand them" → "and Rehoboam was a young man and soft of heart, and he did not strengthen himself before them"** (v7) — נַעַר, the word "boy" (2 Chr 12:13 gives him forty-one years); רַךְ־לֵבָב "soft of heart", as Deut 20:8 ("tender of heart" would say "kind"); וְלֹא הִתְחַזַּק, "did not", not "could not".
- **"ye think to withstand the kingdom of the LORD" → "you mean to strengthen yourselves before the kingdom of Yahweh"** (v8) — אֹמְרִים לְהִתְחַזֵּק, literally "you are saying to strengthen yourselves".
- **"and there are with you golden calves, which Jeroboam made you for gods" → "and with you are the calves of gold that Jeroboam made for you for gods"** (v8) — לֵאלֹהִים; as with the calf elsewhere (Exod 32:4; 1 Kgs 12:28) the word is translated "gods"; it can also be read "for a god".
- **"cast out … after the manner of the nations of other lands" → "driven away … like the peoples of the lands"** (v9) — הִדַּחְתֶּם; כְּעַמֵּי הָאֲרָצוֹת, no "other", no "manner".
- **"whosoever cometh to consecrate himself with a young bullock" → "Everyone who comes to fill his hand with a bull of the herd"** (v9) — לְמַלֵּא יָדוֹ "to fill his hand" = to be installed as priest (Exod 28:41); פַּר בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "a bull, a son of the herd".
- **"the same may be a priest of them that are no gods" → "he becomes a priest to no-gods"** (v9) — לְלֹא אֱלֹהִים, "to not-gods"; also "to a no-god" (the same word as in v8).
- **"But as for us, the LORD is our God … and the priests, which minister unto the LORD, are the sons of Aaron, and the Levites wait upon their business" → "And as for us … and priests are ministering to Yahweh, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites in the work"** (v10) — the Hebrew has no "are" and no "their": כֹהֲנִים without the article, בַּמְלָאכֶת "in the work".
- **"they burn unto the LORD … burnt sacrifices and sweet incense: the shewbread also set they in order" → "they turn into smoke to Yahweh burnt offerings … and fragrant incense, and an arrangement of bread"** (v11) — one verb, מַקְטִרִים, carries the whole list; the Hebrew supplies no second verb for the bread and the lampstand. מַעֲרֶכֶת לֶחֶם "an arrangement of bread" (KJV "shewbread"; "the bread of the face" is another expression). "every morning" → "morning by morning": the word is doubled.
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"** (v11) — *menorah*.
- **"God himself is with us for our captain, and his priests with sounding trumpets to cry alarm against you" → "with us at the head is Elohim, and his priests, and the trumpets of the blast, to sound a blast against you"** (v12) — בָרֹאשׁ "at the head"; חֲצֹצְרוֹת הַתְּרוּעָה as Num 31:6.
- **"O children of Israel, fight ye not against the LORD … for ye shall not prosper" → "Sons of Israel, do not fight with Yahweh"** (v12) — no "O"; בְּנֵי "sons"; עִם "with".
- **"caused an ambushment to come about behind them" → "brought the ambush around to come from behind them"** (v13) — הֵסֵב; הַמַּאְרָב is here the men in ambush.
- **"when Judah looked back, behold, the battle was before and behind" → "Judah turned, and behold, they had the battle front and behind"** (v14) — וַיִּפְנוּ; לָהֶם הַמִּלְחָמָה, literally "to them was the battle".
- **"the priests sounded with the trumpets"** (v14) — the written text has מחצצרים, the reading tradition מַחְצְרִים; the meaning is the same.
- **"gave a shout" → "shouted"**; **"God smote"** (v15) — אִישׁ יְהוּדָה is singular, "the man of Judah", for all of them; נָגַף "smote" (also v20), another verb than "struck" in v17.
- **"fled before Judah: and God delivered them" → "fled from the face of Judah, and Elohim gave them"** (v16).
- **"slew them with a great slaughter: so there fell down slain" → "struck them a great blow, and there fell slain"** (v17) — וַיַּכּוּ בָהֶם … מַכָּה רַבָּה, literally "struck among them a great blow", as 1 Sam 19:8.
- **"were brought under … prevailed, because they relied upon" → "were brought low … were firm, because they leaned on"** (v18) — וַיִּכָּנְעוּ; וַיֶּאֶמְצוּ (root of v7); נִשְׁעֲנוּ עַל "leaned on", the verb of a man leaning on a staff or a hand (2 Sam 1:6; 2 Kgs 5:18), also 14:11; 16:7, 8.
- **"took cities from him, Bethel with the towns thereof … and Ephrain with the towns thereof" → "captured cities from him: Bethel and its daughters … and Ephrain and its daughters"** (v19) — בְּנוֹתֶיהָ "its daughters", the settlements that belong to a city. The reading tradition has עֶפְרַיִן, Ephrain, which the verse follows; the written text has עפרון, Ephron.
- **"Neither did Jeroboam recover strength again" → "And Jeroboam did not retain power any more"** (v20) — עָצַר כֹּחַ, as 1 Chr 29:14. The name is written אֲבִיָּהוּ, Abijahu, in v20 and 21.
- **"But Abijah waxed mighty, and married fourteen wives" → "And Abijahu strengthened himself, and he took for himself fourteen wives"** (v21) — וַיִּתְחַזֵּק; וַיִּשָּׂא־לוֹ, literally "lifted up for himself".
- **"his ways, and his sayings, are written in the story of the prophet Iddo" → "and his ways and his words, are written in the midrash of the prophet Iddo"** (v22) — דִּבְרֵי "the acts of" and דְבָרָיו "his words" are one Hebrew word. מִדְרַשׁ (only here and 24:27) is from דָּרַשׁ "seek": an inquiry, a study or exposition; what kind of writing it was is not known. 1 Kgs 15:7 names another book, "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah".

### Notes
- **Chronicles beside Kings** — 1 Kgs 15:1–8 gives Abijam a short, negative notice (1 Kgs 15:3); Chronicles calls him Abijah and tells of the battle and the speech, which Kings does not have.
- **"Covenant of salt"** — Num 18:19; Lev 2:13.
- **The priests of the north** — "to fill his hand with a bull of the herd and seven rams": the installation offering of Exod 29:1 names one bull and two rams; compare 1 Kgs 12:31; 13:33.
- **The numbers** — 400,000, 800,000 and 500,000 slain are translated as printed.
- **Bethel** — the place of one of the calves (1 Kgs 12:29).
- **Iddo** — also named as a source in 2 Chr 9:29; 12:15.
