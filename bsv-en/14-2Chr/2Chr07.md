# 2 Chronicles 7

[1] And when ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ finished praying, the fire came down from the heavens and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ filled the house.

[2] And the priests were not able to come into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ filled the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] And all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were seeing when the fire came down, and the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ upon the house; and they bent down, faces to the ground, upon the pavement, and bowed down, and gave thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he is good, for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever.

[4] And the king and all the people were sacrificing a sacrifice before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sacrificed the sacrifice of the herd, twenty-two thousand, and of the flock, a hundred and twenty thousand. And they dedicated the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the king and all the people.

[6] And the priests were standing at their charges, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ with the instruments of song of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which David the king had made to give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever, when David praised by their hand; and the priests were sounding the trumpets in front of them, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was standing.

[7] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sanctified the middle of the court that was before the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he offered there the burnt offerings and the fat parts of the peace offerings, for the altar of bronze that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had made was not able to hold the burnt offering and the grain offering and the fat parts.

[8] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made the feast at that time, seven days, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ with him, a very great assembly, from ⟦lebo-hamath|Levo Chamat|Lebo-hamath⟧ to the wadi of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[9] And on the eighth day they made a solemn assembly, for they made the dedication of the altar seven days, and the feast seven days.

[10] And on the twenty-third day of the seventh month he sent the people away to their tents, rejoicing and good of heart over the good that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had done for David and for ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his people.

[11] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ finished the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the house of the king; and all that came upon the heart of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ to do in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and in his house, he made prosper.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in the night and said to him, "I have heard your prayer, and I have chosen this place for myself as a house of sacrifice.

[13] If I close up the heavens and there is no rain, and if I command the grasshopper to consume the land, and if I send pestilence among my people,

[14] and my people, upon whom my name is called, bring themselves low and pray and seek my face and turn back from their evil ways, then I, I will hear from the heavens and will pardon their sin and will heal their land.

[15] Now my eyes shall be open and my ears attentive to the prayer of this place.

[16] And now I have chosen and sanctified this house, for my name to be there forever, and my eyes and my heart shall be there all the days.

[17] And you, if you walk before me as David your father walked, and to do according to all that I have commanded you, and my statutes and my ordinances you keep,

[18] then I will raise up the throne of your kingdom, as I cut a covenant for David your father, saying, 'There shall not be cut off for you a man ruling in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'

[19] And if you turn back, you, and forsake my statutes and my commandments that I have set before you, and you go and serve other gods and bow down to them,

[20] then I will pluck them up from upon my ground that I have given to them, and this house that I have sanctified for my name I will throw from before my face, and I will make it a proverb and a taunt among all the peoples.

[21] And this house, which was high — everyone who passes by it shall be appalled, and he shall say, 'Why has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ done thus to this land and to this house?'

[22] And they shall say, 'Because they forsook ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers, who brought them out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and they held fast to other gods and bowed down to them and served them; therefore he has brought upon them all this evil.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v5 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **The parallel in Kings** (throughout) — v4–10 run beside 1 Kgs 8:62–66 and v11–22 beside 1 Kgs 9:1–9. Where the Hebrew of the two books is the same, the wording is that of Kings; where Chronicles has another word, form or number, the verse follows Chronicles. The differences that touch the meaning are named below. V1–3, 6, 9, 13–15 stand only in Chronicles; Solomon's blessing of the people (1 Kgs 8:54b–61) is not in Chronicles.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Now when Solomon had made an end of praying" → "And when Solomon finished praying"** (v1) — Hebrew וּכְכַלּוֹת שְׁלֹמֹה לְהִתְפַּלֵּל. "The heavens" is always plural in Hebrew. "Consumed" is אָכַל "eat", as in v13.
- **"could not enter" → "were not able to come into"** (v2) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָכְלוּ … לָבוֹא אֶל, as 5:14; 1 Kgs 8:11.
- **"when all the children of Israel saw how the fire came down" → "all the sons of Israel were seeing when the fire came down"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּנֵי "sons"; רֹאִים is a participle.
- **"they bowed themselves with their faces to the ground upon the pavement, and worshipped, and praised the LORD, saying, For he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever" → "they bent down, faces to the ground, upon the pavement, and bowed down, and gave thanks to Yahweh, for he is good, for his chesed is forever"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּכְרְעוּ "bent down" (the knees); אַפַּיִם "faces", literally "nostrils"; וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּ "bowed down"; וְהוֹדוֹת לַיהוָה, an infinitive, "and to give thanks"; there is no "saying". The refrain is that of 1 Chr 16:34; 2 Chr 5:13; the Hebrew has no verb ("is", not "endures").
- **"offered sacrifices" → "were sacrificing a sacrifice"** (v4) — Hebrew זֹבְחִים זֶבַח, a participle. Chronicles has "all the people" (Kings: "all Israel with him").
- **"a sacrifice of twenty and two thousand oxen, and an hundred and twenty thousand sheep" → "the sacrifice of the herd, twenty-two thousand, and of the flock, a hundred and twenty thousand"** (v5) — Hebrew אֶת־זֶבַח הַבָּקָר … וְצֹאן, the collective words; צֹאן covers sheep and goats. Kings has "the sacrifice of peace offerings that he sacrificed to Yahweh", "the house of Yahweh" and "all the sons of Israel"; Chronicles has "King Solomon", "the house of Elohim" and "all the people".
- **"waited on their offices" → "were standing at their charges"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־מִשְׁמְרוֹתָם עֹמְדִים: מִשְׁמֶרֶת is what is given to one to keep, a charge or a watch.
- **"instruments of musick of the LORD" → "the instruments of song of Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew כְלֵי־שִׁיר יְהוָה, as 1 Chr 16:42.
- **"to praise the LORD, because his mercy endureth for ever, when David praised by their ministry" → "to give thanks to Yahweh, for his chesed is forever, when David praised by their hand"** (v6) — Hebrew לְהֹדוֹת "give thanks"; בְּהַלֵּל דָּוִיד בְּיָדָם, literally "in David's praising by their hand".
- **"sounded trumpets before them" → "were sounding the trumpets in front of them"** (v6) — the reading tradition (qere) has מַחְצְרִים; the written form (ketiv) is the fuller מחצצרים; the sense is the same. נֶגְדָּם "in front of them".
- **"hallowed" → "sanctified"** (v7, 16, 20) — Hebrew קִדַּשׁ / הִקְדִּישׁ.
- **"burnt offerings, and the fat of the peace offerings … the meat offerings, and the fat" → "the burnt offerings and the fat parts of the peace offerings … the burnt offering and the grain offering and the fat parts"** (v7) — Hebrew הָעֹלוֹת plural, then הָעֹלָה singular; מִנְחָה; חֲלָבִים plural. "He offered" is עָשָׂה "did, made". Kings has "the altar of bronze that was before Yahweh was too small to hold"; Chronicles has "that Solomon had made was not able to hold".
- **"kept the feast seven days" → "made the feast at that time, seven days"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ … אֶת־הֶחָג. Chronicles has "seven days" here and "a very great assembly"; it does not have "before Yahweh our Elohim, seven days and seven days, fourteen days" (Kings), but counts the days in v9.
- **"from the entering in of Hamath unto the river of Egypt" → "from Lebo-hamath to the wadi of Egypt"** (v8) — Hebrew מִלְּבוֹא חֲמָת עַד־נַחַל מִצְרַיִם.
- **"they made a solemn assembly" kept; "they kept the dedication of the altar" → "they made the dedication of the altar"** (v9) — Hebrew עֲצֶרֶת (Lev 23:36): a day of holding back, or a closing gathering; "solemn" is customary English, not a word of the Hebrew. עָשׂוּ "made", the verb of v8.
- **"on the three and twentieth day" → "on the twenty-third day"** (v10) — the number in ordinary order. Kings has "On the eighth day he sent the people away, and they blessed the king; and they went to their tents"; Chronicles gives the day of the month and has "to their tents" with "sent away".
- **"glad and merry in heart for the goodness that the LORD had shewed unto David, and to Solomon, and to Israel his people" → "rejoicing and good of heart over the good that Yahweh had done for David and for Solomon and for Israel his people"** (v10) — Hebrew שְׂמֵחִים וְטוֹבֵי לֵב עַל־הַטּוֹבָה אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה. "And for Solomon" stands only in Chronicles; "all" before "the good" and "his servant" after "David" stand only in Kings.
- **"all that came into Solomon's heart to make … he prosperously effected" → "all that came upon the heart of Solomon to do … he made prosper"** (v11) — Hebrew כָּל־הַבָּא עַל־לֵב שְׁלֹמֹה … הִצְלִיחַ. Kings has "all the longing of Solomon that he delighted to do".
- **"by night" → "in the night"** (v12) — Hebrew בַּלָּיְלָה. Kings has "a second time, as he had appeared to him at Gibeon", and "your plea for favor, with which you have pleaded before me"; Chronicles has "and I have chosen this place for myself as a house of sacrifice" (לְבֵית זָבַח).
- **"If I shut up heaven that there be no rain, or if I command the locusts to devour the land" → "If I close up the heavens and there is no rain, and if I command the grasshopper to consume the land"** (v13) — the first two clauses begin with הֵן, the word "behold", which in late Hebrew also stands for "if"; the third has אִם "if". עָצַר "close up" (6:26). חָגָב is "grasshopper" (Lev 11:22), a kind of locust; the word of 6:28 is אַרְבֶּה. Each clause is joined with וְ "and", not "or".
- **"If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin" → "and my people, upon whom my name is called, bring themselves low and pray and seek my face and turn back from their evil ways, then I, I will hear from the heavens and will pardon their sin"** (v14) — the Hebrew has no second "if": the verse goes on with וְ "and". אֲשֶׁר נִקְרָא־שְׁמִי עֲלֵיהֶם, as 6:33. נִכְנַע is "be brought low, bring oneself low" (1 Kgs 21:29). בִּקֵּשׁ "seek"; שׁוּב "turn back"; רָעִים "evil"; וַאֲנִי אֶשְׁמַע, with the pronoun; סָלַח "pardon", said only of God.
- **"attent unto the prayer that is made in this place" → "attentive to the prayer of this place"** (v15) — Hebrew לִתְפִלַּת הַמָּקוֹם הַזֶּה, as 6:40.
- **"For now have I chosen" → "And now I have chosen"** (v16) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה. "For my name to be there" is לִהְיוֹת־שְׁמִי שָׁם (Kings: "to put my name there"); "perpetually" → "all the days" (כָּל־הַיָּמִים). "I have chosen" stands only in Chronicles; "that you have built" only in Kings.
- **"and do according to all … and shalt observe my statutes and my judgments" → "and to do according to all … and my statutes and my ordinances you keep"** (v17) — Hebrew וְלַעֲשׂוֹת, an infinitive with "and"; חֻקַּי וּמִשְׁפָּטַי תִּשְׁמוֹר. מִשְׁפָּט of a ruling given as law is "ordinance". Kings has also "in integrity of heart and in uprightness".
- **"Then will I stablish the throne of thy kingdom, according as I have covenanted with David thy father, saying, There shall not fail thee a man to be ruler in Israel" → "then I will raise up the throne of your kingdom, as I cut a covenant for David your father, saying, 'There shall not be cut off for you a man ruling in Israel'"** (v18) — Hebrew וַהֲקִימוֹתִי, "make stand". "Kingdom" is מַלְכוּת here (Kings: מַמְלָכָה). כָּרַתִּי לְדָוִיד is only "I cut for David": "a covenant" is supplied, as the verb alone means this (5:10; 1 Sam 22:8); Kings has "as I spoke concerning David". לֹא־יִכָּרֵת לְךָ אִישׁ מוֹשֵׁל בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל (Kings: "a man from upon the throne of Israel"; compare Mic 5:2). Kings has also "over Israel forever".
- **"But if ye turn away" → "And if you turn back, you"** (v19) — Hebrew וְאִם־תְּשׁוּבוּן אַתֶּם: from here "you" is plural; in v17–18 it is singular. Kings has "If you surely turn back, you and your sons, from after me, and do not keep"; Chronicles has "and forsake" (עָזַב). "Set before you" is נָתַן לִפְנֵי; the verb is "give". "Worship" → "bow down" (הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה), also v22.
- **"Then will I pluck them up by the roots out of my land … will I cast out of my sight, and will make it to be a proverb and a byword among all nations" → "then I will pluck them up from upon my ground … I will throw from before my face, and I will make it a proverb and a taunt among all the peoples"** (v20) — Hebrew וּנְתַשְׁתִּים מֵעַל אַדְמָתִי (Deut 29:28); the verse passes from "you" to "them". אַשְׁלִיךְ מֵעַל פָּנָי "throw"; וְאֶתְּנֶנּוּ, "give it for"; שְׁנִינָה is literally "a sharp word"; עַמִּים "peoples". Kings has "I will cut off Israel from upon the face of the ground", "send away", and "Israel shall become a proverb"; in Chronicles it is the house that is made a proverb.
- **"And this house, which is high, shall be an astonishment to every one that passeth by it; so that he shall say" → "And this house, which was high — everyone who passes by it shall be appalled, and he shall say"** (v21) — Hebrew וְהַבַּיִת הַזֶּה אֲשֶׁר הָיָה עֶלְיוֹן לְכָל־עֹבֵר עָלָיו יִשֹּׁם: the sentence breaks off after "high" and begins again with לְ "as for everyone". Kings has "this house shall be high" and adds "and shall hiss". "He shall say" is singular (Kings plural).
- **"Why" kept** (v21) — Hebrew בַּמֶּה, literally "in what, by what"; Kings has עַל־מֶה "for what".
- **"laid hold on other gods" → "held fast to other gods"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיַּחֲזִיקוּ בְּ. Chronicles has "the Elohim of their fathers, who brought them out" (Kings: "their Elohim, who brought their fathers out") and does not name Yahweh again with "has brought".

### Notes
- **The fire** (v1–3) — fire from the heavens upon the offering, as Lev 9:24; 1 Chr 21:26; it stands only in Chronicles.
- **The refrain** (v3, 6) — "for he is good, for his *chesed* is forever": 1 Chr 16:34, 41; 2 Chr 5:13; 20:21; Ps 136.
- **The days** (v8–10) — the dedication of the altar seven days, the feast seven days, the solemn assembly on the eighth day (Lev 23:36), and the sending away on the twenty-third day of the seventh month.
- **V13–15** — the answer takes up the cases of the prayer (6:26–28, 40).
- **The house and the throne** (v17–22) — the promise to Solomon is bound to "if you walk before me"; the threat is against the people ("you", plural) and against the house.
