# Galatians 4

[1] Now I say: for as long a time as the heir is a minor, he differs in nothing from a slave, being lord of all,

[2] but he is under guardians and stewards until the time appointed beforehand by the father.

[3] So also we, when we were minors, were enslaved under the elements of the world;

[4] but when the fullness of the time came, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent forth his Son, come of a woman, come under ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧,

[5] that he might redeem those under ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, that we might receive the adoption as sons.

[6] And because you are sons, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent forth the Spirit of his Son into our hearts, crying, "***Abba*, Father!**"

[7] So you are no longer a slave but a son; and if a son, also an heir through ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] But then, indeed, not knowing ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you were slaves to those that by nature are not gods;

[9] but now, having come to know ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, or rather having been known by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, how do you turn back again to the weak and poor elements, to which you want to be slaves again anew?

[10] You observe days and months and seasons and years.

[11] I fear for you, lest somehow I have labored for you in vain.

[12] Become as I am, because I also am as you are, brothers, I beseech you. You wronged me in nothing;

[13] and you know that because of weakness of the flesh I preached the gospel to you the former time,

[14] and your trial in my flesh you did not despise nor spit out, but as an angel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you accepted me, as ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[15] Where then is your blessedness? For I testify to you that, if possible, you would have dug out your eyes and given them to me.

[16] So then, have I become your enemy by telling you the truth?

[17] They are zealous for you, not well, but they want to shut you out, that you may be zealous for them.

[18] But it is good to be the object of zeal in a good thing always, and not only when I am present with you,

[19] my children, with whom I am again in birth pains until ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is formed in you;

[20] and I could wish to be present with you now and to change my voice, because I am perplexed about you.

[21] Tell me, you who want to be under ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, do you not hear the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧?

[22] For it is written that ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ had two sons, one from the slave woman and one from the free woman;

[23] but the one from the slave woman has been begotten according to flesh, and the one from the free woman through promise.

[24] These things are spoken allegorically: for these are two covenants, one from Mount Sinai, bearing unto slavery, which is Hagar.

[25] Now this Hagar is Mount Sinai in ⟦arabia|Arav|Arabia⟧, and corresponds to the present ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for she is a slave with her children;

[26] but the ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ above is free, which is our mother.

[27] For it is written,

"**Rejoice, barren one who does not bear;
break forth and shout, you who are not in birth pains;
because many are the children of the desolate one,
more than of her who has the husband**."

[28] But you, brothers, like ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧, are children of promise.

[29] But just as then the one begotten according to flesh persecuted the one according to Spirit, so also now.

[30] But what does the Scripture say? "**Cast out the slave woman and her son, for the son of the slave woman shall by no means inherit with the son of the free woman**."

[31] Therefore, brothers, we are not children of a slave woman but of the free woman.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (vv14, 19) — systematic; order preserved from Greek *Christos Iēsous*.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 14) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Abba" retained** (v6) — Aramaic אַבָּא *Abba* = "Father/Daddy"; Paul preserves the Aramaic term as used in prayer (cf. Mark 14:36; Rom 8:15). The Greek adds ὁ πατήρ, "the Father".
- **"as long as he is a child" → "for as long a time as the heir is a minor"** (v1, 3) — Greek νήπιος, literally "infant", here of an heir not yet of age; a different word from "children" (τέκνα) in v19, 25, 27, 28, 31.
- **"though he be lord of all" → "being lord of all"** (v1) — Greek κύριος πάντων ὤν, a participle; the Greek has no "though".
- **"under tutors and governors until the time appointed of the father" → "under guardians and stewards until the time appointed beforehand by the father"** (v2) — Greek ἐπιτρόπους, οἰκονόμους, προθεσμίας.
- **"were in bondage under the elements of the world" → "were enslaved under the elements of the world"** (v3) — Greek ὑπὸ τὰ στοιχεῖα τοῦ κόσμου ἤμεθα δεδουλωμένοι; στοιχεῖα is "elements" as in Col 2:8, 20; 2 Pet 3:10.
- **"made of a woman, made under the law" → "come of a woman, come under law"** (v4) — Greek γενόμενον, "having come to be" (as Phil 2:7), twice; not the word "born" (γεννάω, v23, 29). ὑπὸ νόμον has no article.
- **"to redeem them that were under the law" → "that he might redeem those under law"** (v5) — Greek ἐξαγοράσῃ, as 3:13.
- **"the adoption of sons" → "the adoption as sons"** (v5) — Greek υἱοθεσίαν, "placing as a son".
- **"hath sent forth" → "sent forth"; "into your hearts" → "into our hearts"** (v6) — Greek ἐξαπέστειλεν, the same verb as v4; ἡμῶν, "our". "Crying" goes with the Spirit (κρᾶζον, neuter with πνεῦμα; the gender is grammatical).
- **"an heir of God through Christ" → "an heir through *Elohim*"** (v7) — Greek διὰ θεοῦ.
- **"ye did service unto them which by nature are no gods" → "you were slaves to those that by nature are not gods"** (v8) — Greek ἐδουλεύσατε; also "to be slaves" in v9 and "is a slave" in v25.
- **"after that ye have known God, or rather are known of God" → "having come to know *Elohim*, or rather having been known by *Elohim*"** (v9) — Greek γνόντες, γνωσθέντες.
- **"the weak and beggarly elements" → "the weak and poor elements"** (v9) — Greek πτωχά, the word of 2:10 ("the poor"); the Greek has no "of the world" here.
- **"again to be in bondage" → "to be slaves again anew"** (v9) — Greek πάλιν ἄνωθεν, "again, from the beginning".
- **"Ye observe days" kept** (v10) — the sentence can also be read as a question.
- **"I am afraid of you" → "I fear for you"** (v11) — Greek φοβοῦμαι ὑμᾶς.
- **"be as I am; for I am as ye are" → "Become as I am, because I also am as you are"** (v12) — Greek γίνεσθε ὡς ἐγώ, ὅτι κἀγὼ ὡς ὑμεῖς; both halves have no verb "to be".
- **"ye have not injured me at all" → "You wronged me in nothing"** (v12) — Greek οὐδέν με ἠδικήσατε.
- **"through infirmity of the flesh" → "because of weakness of the flesh"** (v13) — Greek διʼ ἀσθένειαν τῆς σαρκός; the same root as "weak" in v9. The Greek has no "ailment".
- **"at the first" → "the former time"** (v13) — Greek τὸ πρότερον: "the first time" (of two) or "formerly".
- **"my temptation which was in my flesh ye despised not, nor rejected" → "your trial in my flesh you did not despise nor spit out"** (v14) — Greek τὸν πειρασμὸν ὑμῶν, "your trial"; πειρασμός is "trial" or "temptation"; ἐξεπτύσατε is literally "spit out".
- **"received me" → "accepted me"** (v14) — Greek ἐδέξασθε.
- **"Where is then the blessedness ye spake of?" → "Where then is your blessedness?"** (v15) — Greek ποῦ οὖν ὁ μακαρισμὸς ὑμῶν; μακαρισμός is the calling of someone blessed.
- **"I bear you record" → "I testify to you"; "plucked out" → "dug out"** (v15) — Greek μαρτυρῶ, ἐξορύξαντες.
- **"because I tell you the truth" → "by telling you the truth"** (v16) — Greek ἀληθεύων ὑμῖν, a participle: "being truthful to you".
- **"They zealously affect you, but not well; yea, they would exclude you, that ye might affect them" → "They are zealous for you, not well, but they want to shut you out, that you may be zealous for them"** (v17) — Greek ζηλοῦσιν, ζηλοῦτε, and ζηλοῦσθαι in v18 ("to be the object of zeal", passive): one verb three times, "be zealous for, court".
- **"I preached the gospel unto you" kept** (v13) — Greek εὐηγγελισάμην, as 1:8.
- **"My little children" → "my children"** (v19) — the printed text has τέκνα, "children"; the reading τεκνία, "little children", is in other manuscripts. In the printed text v19 continues the sentence of v18.
- **"of whom I travail in birth again" → "with whom I am again in birth pains"** (v19) — Greek ὠδίνω, the verb of v27.
- **"until Christ be formed in you" kept** (v19) — Greek μορφωθῇ, from μορφή, "form" (Phil 2:6–7).
- **"I desire to be present" → "I could wish to be present"; "my voice" kept** (v20) — Greek ἤθελον, imperfect tense; φωνήν, "voice".
- **"I stand in doubt of you" → "I am perplexed about you"** (v20) — Greek ἀποροῦμαι ἐν ὑμῖν.
- **"ye that desire to be under the law, do ye not hear the law?" → "you who want to be under law, do you not hear the law?"** (v21) — Greek ὑπὸ νόμον without the article, τὸν νόμον with it.
- **"the one by a bondmaid, the other by a freewoman" → "one from the slave woman and one from the free woman"** (v22) — Greek παιδίσκης, a female slave; both nouns have the article.
- **"was born after the flesh" → "has been begotten according to flesh"** (v23, 29) — Greek γεγέννηται, γεννηθείς, from γεννάω, "beget"; κατὰ σάρκα has no article. In v24 the same verb is said of the mother: "bearing".
- **"by promise" → "through promise"** (v23) — the printed text has διʼ ἐπαγγελίας, without the article.
- **"Which things are an allegory" → "These things are spoken allegorically"** (v24) — Greek ἅτινά ἐστιν ἀλληγορούμενα; the Greek has no "may be interpreted".
- **"for these are the two covenants" → "for these are two covenants"** (v24) — Greek αὗται, feminine: the two women; δύο διαθῆκαι has no article.
- **"which gendereth to bondage" → "bearing unto slavery"** (v24) — Greek εἰς δουλείαν γεννῶσα; the Greek has no "children".
- **"For this Agar is mount Sinai in Arabia" → "Now this Hagar is Mount Sinai in *Arav*"** (v25) — Greek τὸ δὲ Ἁγὰρ Σινᾶ ὄρος ἐστίν: the neuter article points to the name "Hagar" as a word. Some manuscripts lack "Hagar".
- **"answereth to Jerusalem which now is, and is in bondage" → "corresponds to the present Jerusalem, for she is a slave"** (v25) — Greek συστοιχεῖ, "stand in the same row"; δουλεύει γάρ.
- **"which is the mother of us all" → "which is our mother"** (v26) — "all" is not in the critical text.
- **"for the desolate hath many more children than she which hath an husband" → "because many are the children of the desolate one, more than of her who has the husband"** (v27) — Greek ὅτι πολλὰ τὰ τέκνα τῆς ἐρήμου μᾶλλον ἢ τῆς ἐχούσης τὸν ἄνδρα.
- **"Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise" → "But you, brothers, like *Yitzchaq*, are children of promise"** (v28) — the printed text has ὑμεῖς … ἐστέ, "you are"; κατὰ Ἰσαάκ is "according to, after the manner of *Yitzchaq*".
- **"him that was born after the Spirit" → "the one according to Spirit"** (v29) — Greek τὸν κατὰ πνεῦμα, without a participle and without the article.
- **"shall not be heir" → "shall by no means inherit"** (v30) — Greek οὐ γὰρ μὴ κληρονομήσει, the strong negation.
- **"So then" → "Therefore"; "of the bondwoman" → "of a slave woman"** (v31) — the printed text has διό; παιδίσκης has no article, τῆς ἐλευθέρας has it.

### Notes
- **v27** quotes Isa 54:1.
- **v30** quotes Gen 21:10.
