# Malachi 3

[1] **Behold, I am sending my messenger, and he shall clear a way before me; and suddenly he shall come to his temple, the Lord whom you are seeking, and the messenger of the covenant in whom you delight, behold, he is coming, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.**

[2] And who can sustain the day of his coming, and who is the one who stands when he appears? For he is like the fire of a refiner, and like the lye of washers.

[3] And he shall sit refining and cleansing silver, and he shall cleanse the sons of Levi and purify them like the gold and like the silver; and they shall be to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ones who bring forward an offering in righteousness.

[4] And the offering of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall be pleasant to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as the days of old and as former years.

[5] And I will come near to you for the judgment, and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, and against the adulterers, and against those who swear to falsehood, and against those who extort the wage of the hired man, the widow and the fatherless, and who turn aside the sojourner, and do not fear me, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[6] For I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, have not changed; and you, sons of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, are not finished.

[7] From the days of your fathers you have turned aside from my statutes and have not kept them. Turn back to me, and I will turn back to you, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts. And you say, In what shall we turn back?

[8] Will a man rob ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? For you are robbing me. And you say, In what have we robbed you? The tithe and the contribution.

[9] With the curse you are cursed, and me you are robbing, the nation, all of it.

[10] **Bring all the tithe into the house of the treasury, and let there be food in my house; and try me, please, in this, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, whether I will not open for you the windows of the heavens, and empty out for you a blessing until there is no more enough.**

[11] And I will rebuke for you the devourer, and he shall not ruin for you the fruit of the ground, and the vine in the field shall not miscarry for you, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[12] And all the nations shall call you happy, for you, you shall be a land of delight, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[13] Your words have been strong against me, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And you say, What have we spoken together against you?

[14] You have said, It is empty to serve ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and what gain is it that we have kept his charge, and that we have walked in mourning before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts?

[15] And now we are calling the presumptuous happy; not only are the doers of wickedness built up, they have also tried ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and have escaped.

[16] Then those who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke together, each man with his neighbor; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave heed and heard, and a ⟦book-of-remembrance|sefer zikkaron|book of remembrance⟧ was written before him for those who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for those who think on his name.

[17] And they shall be to me, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, for the day when I act, a treasured possession; and I will spare them, as a man spares his son who serves him.

[18] And you shall return and see between the righteous and the wicked, between one who serves ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and one who has not served him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, plain "of Hosts" (no token).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8, 14, 15, 18) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **Layout** — prose, as the Hebrew; the quotation marks are dropped (the Hebrew marks none; the people's words in v13–15 are carried by "you say", "you have said"); v6 is one line (it was split as poetry).
- **"Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me: and the Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple, even the messenger of the covenant, whom ye delight in" → "Behold, I am sending my messenger, and he shall clear a way before me; and suddenly he shall come to his temple, the Lord whom you are seeking, and the messenger of the covenant in whom you delight"** (v1) — הִנְנִי שֹׁלֵחַ participle "I am sending"; מַלְאָכִי "my messenger" (מַלְאָךְ, its row; of a man "messenger"): the same letters as the name in 1:1 — the book's heading may take its name from this verse, or the verse plays on the name; no decision in the verse. פִּנָּה דֶרֶךְ "clear a way" (piel of פָּנָה "turn": clear away what stands in it; as Isa 40:3; 57:14; 62:10); דֶּרֶךְ without the article "a way"; the Hebrew order kept: the verb "come" first, then its subject הָאָדוֹן "the Lord" (אָדוֹן with the article, as "the Lord, Yahweh of Hosts" in Isaiah: plain "Lord", no token); מְבַקְשִׁים participle "are seeking"; וּמַלְאַךְ הַבְּרִית "and the messenger of the covenant" — whether "the Lord" and "the messenger of the covenant" are one or two, and whether either is "my messenger" of the first clause, the verse does not say; וְ "and" kept (KJV "even" decides). חֲפֵצִים "you delight in" (חָפֵץ). Mark 1:2 and Matt 11:10 quote the first clause in another form ("before your face … your way"); that difference belongs to the New Testament.
- **"But who may abide the day of his coming? and who shall stand when he appeareth? for he is like a refiner's fire, and like fullers' soap" → "And who can sustain the day of his coming, and who is the one who stands when he appears? For he is like the fire of a refiner, and like the lye of washers"** (v2) — מְכַלְכֵּל participle of כִּלְכֵּל "sustain" (its row: here "hold up under"; "can" supplied); הָעֹמֵד "the one who stands" (participle with the article); בְּהֵרָאוֹתוֹ "in his appearing" (niphal of רָאָה); מְצָרֵף "refiner" (צָרַף "refine", beside בָּחַן "try"); בֹּרִית "lye" (an alkali for washing; "soap" in Changes); מְכַבְּסִים "washers" (of cloth; KJV "fullers").
- **"he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver: and he shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the LORD an offering in righteousness" → "he shall sit refining and cleansing silver, and he shall cleanse the sons of Levi and purify them like the gold and like the silver; and they shall be to Yahweh ones who bring forward an offering in righteousness"** (v3) — מְצָרֵף וּמְטַהֵר participles "refining and cleansing" (no "as"); טִהַר piel "cleanse" (the טִהַר row of the rite); זִקֵּק "purify" (of metal filtered or smelted clean; "purge" in KJV); the articles "the gold … the silver" kept; וְהָיוּ לַיהוָה מַגִּישֵׁי מִנְחָה "and they shall be to Yahweh bringers forward of an offering" (הִגִּישׁ as 1:7, 11; 2:12); מִנְחָה "offering" (as 1:10).
- **"Then shall the offering of Judah and Jerusalem be pleasant unto the LORD, as in the days of old, and as in former years" → "And the offering … shall be pleasant to Yahweh, as the days of old and as former years"** (v4) — וְעָרְבָה "and shall be pleasant" (עָרֵב "be sweet, pleasant"; not the עָרַב "be surety" of its row); כִּימֵי עוֹלָם "as the days of old" (מֵעוֹלָם / עוֹלָם of the past; lit. "of the age"); קַדְמֹנִיּוֹת "former"; "in" not written.
- **"And I will come near to you to judgment … against those that oppress the hireling in his wages, the widow, and the fatherless, and that turn aside the stranger from his right" → "And I will come near to you for the judgment … against those who extort the wage of the hired man, the widow and the fatherless, and who turn aside the sojourner"** (v5) — קָרַבְתִּי weqatal "I will come near"; לַמִּשְׁפָּט "for the judgment" (article kept; מִשְׁפָּט row); עֵד מְמַהֵר "a swift witness" (lit. "a hastening witness"); מְכַשְּׁפִים "sorcerers" (its row); מְנָאֲפִים "adulterers" (נָאַף row); נִשְׁבָּעִים לַשָּׁקֶר "who swear to falsehood" (שֶׁקֶר row); עֹשְׁקֵי שְׂכַר־שָׂכִיר "who extort the wage of the hired man" (עָשַׁק "extort", שָׂכִיר "hired man", their rows); the widow and the fatherless stand as further objects of the same participle; מַטֵּי־גֵר "who turn aside the sojourner" (הִטָּה, its row: from his judgment; "from his right" of KJV supplied); גֵּר "sojourner" (its row).
- **"For I am the LORD, I change not; therefore ye sons of Jacob are not consumed" → "For I, Yahweh, have not changed; and you, sons of Jacob, are not finished"** (v6) — כִּי אֲנִי יְהוָה לֹא שָׁנִיתִי "for I, Yahweh, have not changed" (perfect; also read "I am Yahweh; I have not changed"); וְאַתֶּם "and you" (not "therefore"); כְלִיתֶם qal of כָּלָה "be finished" (its row; "consumed" of KJV; also read "you have not ceased", i.e. from your ways).
- **"Even from the days of your fathers ye are gone away from mine ordinances … Return unto me, and I will return unto you … Wherein shall we return?" → "From the days of your fathers you have turned aside from my statutes and have not kept them. Turn back to me, and I will turn back to you … In what shall we turn back?"** (v7) — לְמִימֵי "from the days of"; סַרְתֶּם מִן "turned aside from" (the סוּר row); חֻקִּים "statutes" (its row); שׁוּב "turn back" (the prophets' wording, as Jer 3; Ezek 18: not "return", "repent"), three times one verb.
- **"Will a man rob God? Yet ye have robbed me … In tithes and offerings" → "Will a man rob Elohim? For you are robbing me … The tithe and the contribution"** (v8) — קָבַע "rob" (rare: Prov 22:23; the sense is uncertain; the Greek has "trip / deceive", reading עָקַב, from memory: not followed); אָדָם "a man" (mankind); כִּי "for" (or "yet"); קֹבְעִים participle "are robbing"; הַמַּעֲשֵׂר "the tithe" (מַעֲשֵׂר row: the due of the law), וְהַתְּרוּמָה "and the contribution" (its row, "what is lifted off"; not "offerings"), singular with the article, without "in".
- **"Ye are cursed with a curse: for ye have robbed me, even this whole nation" → "With the curse you are cursed, and me you are robbing, the nation, all of it"** (v9) — בַּמְּאֵרָה "with the curse" (article; מְאֵרָה, as 2:2), נֵאָרִים "you are cursed" (niphal of אָרַר); וְאֹתִי "and me", the object first (not "for"); הַגּוֹי כֻּלּוֹ "the nation, all of it".
- **"Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in mine house, and prove me now herewith … if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there shall not be room enough to receive it" → "Bring all the tithe into the house of the treasury, and let there be food in my house; and try me, please, in this … whether I will not open for you the windows of the heavens, and empty out for you a blessing until there is no more enough"** (v10) — בֵּית הָאוֹצָר "the house of the treasury" (אוֹצָר row); וִיהִי טֶרֶף "and let there be food" (jussive; טֶרֶף "food", the Ps 109–112 row: lit. "prey"); בְּחָנוּנִי "try me" (the reviewer's בָּחַן row); נָא "please" (kept); אִם־לֹא "whether … not": the test, not the oath formula (the thing hoped for is said in the form "whether I will not"); אֲרֻבּוֹת הַשָּׁמַיִם "the windows of the heavens" (as Gen 7:11); הֲרִיקֹתִי "empty out" (hifil of רִיק, the "draw (lit. empty out)" of its row); עַד־בְּלִי־דָי "until there is no more enough" (lit. "until without sufficiency": past what is needed, or past what can hold it; uncertain).
- **"And I will rebuke the devourer for your sakes, and he shall not destroy the fruits of your ground; neither shall your vine cast her fruit before the time in the field" → "And I will rebuke for you the devourer, and he shall not ruin for you the fruit of the ground, and the vine in the field shall not miscarry for you"** (v11) — גָּעַרְתִּי בְּ "rebuke" (its row, as 2:3); הָאֹכֵל "the devourer" (lit. "the eater": the locust or any pest, not named); יַשְׁחִת "ruin" (the שָׁחַת row); פְּרִי הָאֲדָמָה "the fruit of the ground" (אֲדָמָה row; "your" is לָכֶם "for you"); תְשַׁכֵּל "miscarry" (piel of the שִׁכֵּל "bereave" row: of a vine losing its fruit before it ripens, said in Changes); הַגֶּפֶן "the vine" (not "your vine").
- **"And all nations shall call you blessed: for ye shall be a delightsome land" → "And all the nations shall call you happy, for you, you shall be a land of delight"** (v12) — אִשְּׁרוּ "call happy" (the אִשֵּׁר row; not "blessed", which is בָּרוּךְ); אַתֶּם stressed; אֶרֶץ חֵפֶץ "a land of delight".
- **"Your words have been stout against me … What have we spoken so much against thee?" → "Your words have been strong against me … What have we spoken together against you?"** (v13) — חָזְקוּ "have been strong" (the חָזַק row); נִדְבַּרְנוּ niphal "spoken together" (one with another: as v16; "so much" is not written).
- **"It is vain to serve God: and what profit is it that we have kept his ordinance, and that we have walked mournfully before the LORD of hosts?" → "It is empty to serve Elohim; and what gain is it that we have kept his charge, and that we have walked in mourning before the face of Yahweh of Hosts?"** (v14) — שָׁוְא "empty" (its row); בֶּצַע "gain" (the "unjust gain" of its row: here the people's word for profit, nothing unjust in it; said); שָׁמַרְנוּ מִשְׁמַרְתּוֹ "we have kept his charge" (the מִשְׁמֶרֶת row); קְדֹרַנִּית "in mourning" (only here; lit. "darkly", in the dark clothes of mourning); מִפְּנֵי "before the face of" (as 2:5).
- **"And now we call the proud happy; yea, they that work wickedness are set up; yea, they that tempt God are even delivered" → "And now we are calling the presumptuous happy; not only are the doers of wickedness built up, they have also tried Elohim, and have escaped"** (v15) — מְאַשְּׁרִים participle "we are calling happy"; זֵדִים "the presumptuous" (its row); גַּם … גַּם "not only … also" (lit. "also … also"); נִבְנוּ "are built up" (lit. "have been built": prosper, gain a house; as Jer 12:16); עֹשֵׂי רִשְׁעָה "doers of wickedness" (רִשְׁעָה row); בָּחֲנוּ "they have tried" (the reviewer's בָּחַן row; the people's own word of v10 turned back); וַיִּמָּלֵטוּ "and have escaped" (נִמְלַט row).
- **"Then they that feared the LORD spake often one to another: and the LORD hearkened, and heard it, and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared the LORD, and that thought upon his name" → "Then those who fear Yahweh spoke together, each man with his neighbor; and Yahweh gave heed and heard, and a book of remembrance was written before him for those who fear Yahweh and for those who think on his name"** (v16) — נִדְבְּרוּ "spoke together" (as v13: the same niphal, of the other party; "often" is not written); יִרְאֵי יְהוָה "those who fear Yahweh" (participle); אִישׁ אֶת־רֵעֵהוּ "each man with his neighbor" (its row); וַיַּקְשֵׁב "and gave heed" (the root of the קַשֻּׁב "attentive" row); סֵפֶר זִכָּרוֹן "a book of remembrance" (token `book-of-remembrance`; זִכָּרוֹן elsewhere "memorial", its row: here the lexicon's term, "remembrance", kept); לְ "for" (on behalf of, or "of": who is written in it is not said); חֹשְׁבֵי שְׁמוֹ "those who think on his name" (חָשַׁב of the mind, its row; "esteem" in modern versions).
- **"And they shall be mine, saith the LORD of hosts, in that day when I make up my jewels; and I will spare them" → "And they shall be to me … for the day when I act, a treasured possession; and I will spare them"** (v17) — the atnach stands on סְגֻלָּה: לַיּוֹם אֲשֶׁר אֲנִי עֹשֶׂה "for the day when I act" (participle "I am making / doing", as 4:3, where the same words stand alone), and סְגֻלָּה "a treasured possession" (its row: a king's own treasure; Exod 19:5) says what they shall be; "when I make my treasured possession" joins the words against the accents (in Changes). חָמַל "spare" (its row), twice; הָעֹבֵד אֹתוֹ "who serves him".
- **"Then shall ye return, and discern between the righteous and the wicked, between him that serveth God and him that serveth him not" → "And you shall return and see between the righteous and the wicked, between one who serves Elohim and one who has not served him"** (v18) — וְשַׁבְתֶּם וּרְאִיתֶם "and you shall return and see" (the שׁוּב + a second verb row: = see again; kept); רָאָה בֵּין "see between" (= tell apart); צַדִּיק · רָשָׁע "righteous · wicked" (their row); עֹבֵד participle, לֹא עֲבָדוֹ perfect "has not served him".

### Notes
- **v13–18** — the last dispute of the book: the people's complaint (v13–15) is answered by the writing of the book (v16) and the promise of the day (v17–18; 4:1–3, Heb 3:19–21, continue the same speech: the chapter division is not in the Hebrew, which runs on to 3:24).
