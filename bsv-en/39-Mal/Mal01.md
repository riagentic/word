# Malachi 1

[1] The burden of the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ by the hand of Malachi.

[2] I have loved you, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And you say, In what have you loved us? Is not ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ a brother to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And I loved ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,

[3] and ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ I hated; and I made his mountains a desolation, and his inheritance for the jackals of the wilderness.

[4] If Edom says, We are beaten down, and we will return and build the ruins, thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: They, they shall build, and I, I will tear down; and they shall call them the border of wickedness, and the people whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has denounced forever.

[5] And your eyes shall see, and you, you shall say, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be great above the border of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[6] A son honors a father, and a slave his lord. And if I am a father, where is my honor? And if I am lord, where is my fear? says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts to you, the priests, who despise my name. And you say, In what have we despised your name?

[7] Bringing forward upon my altar defiled bread. And you say, In what have we defiled you? In your saying, The table of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, it is despised.

[8] And when you bring forward the blind to sacrifice, there is no evil; and when you bring forward the lame and the sick, there is no evil. Bring it near, please, to your governor: will he be pleased with you, or will he lift up your face? says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[9] And now, beseech, please, the face of ⟦el|El|God⟧, that he may show favor to us. From your hand this has been: will he lift up faces from you? says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[10] Who is there also among you who would shut the doors, so that you would not light my altar for nothing? I have no delight in you, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and an offering I will not be pleased with from your hand.

[11] **For from the rising of the sun and to its setting great is my name among the nations, and in every place incense is brought forward to my name, and a pure offering; for great is my name among the nations**, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[12] And you are profaning it in your saying, The table of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, it is defiled, and its fruit, its food, is despised.

[13] And you say, Behold, what hardship! and you have puffed at it, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts; and you have brought what was robbed, and the lame, and the sick, and you have brought the offering. Shall I be pleased with it from your hand? says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] And cursed is the deceiver: and there is in his drove a male, and he vows, and sacrifices to ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ a ruined thing. For a great king am I, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and my name is fearsome among the nations.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, plain "of Hosts" (no token), as the earlier books.
- **Layout** — the quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where the words of Yahweh, of the people and of the priests begin and end; the dialogue (you say … in what …?) is carried by the words themselves.
- **"The burden of the word of the LORD to Israel by Malachi" → "The burden of the word of Yahweh to Israel by the hand of Malachi"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא "burden", "what is lifted up" (the formula row; "oracle" in modern versions names the sense, not the word); the conjunctive accent on מַשָּׂא joins it to what follows ("the burden of the word"; "A burden: the word of …" is also read, as Zech 9:1; 12:1). בְּיַד "by the hand of" (kept). מַלְאָכִי is "my messenger" (the same word as 3:1); whether it is a name or a title is not certain: the name kept, as the heading of the book.
- **"Wherein hast thou loved us?" → "In what have you loved us?"** (v2; also v6, 7) — Hebrew בַּמָּה / בַּמֶּה "in what", the question repeated through the book. **"Was not Esau Jacob's brother? … yet I loved Jacob, and I hated Esau" → "Is not Esau a brother to Jacob? … And I loved Jacob, and Esau I hated"** (v2–3) — הֲלוֹא־אָח עֵשָׂו לְיַעֲקֹב "is not a brother Esau to Jacob" (no verb); אָהַבְתִּי "I have loved" (perfect, v2a), then וָאֹהַב "and I loved" (wayyiqtol, past); וְאֶת־עֵשָׂו שָׂנֵאתִי, the object first, "and Esau I hated" (אָהַב · שָׂנֵא "love · hate", the row; no softening to "loved less"). Quoted in Rom 9:13 (the Greek follows the Hebrew order); that is a matter of the New Testament, not of this verse.
- **"laid his mountains and his heritage waste for the dragons of the wilderness" → "made his mountains a desolation, and his inheritance for the jackals of the wilderness"** (v3) — וָאָשִׂים "and I made" (wayyiqtol); הָרָיו "his mountains" (the plural, not "hill country"); שְׁמָמָה "desolation" (its row); נַחֲלָה "inheritance" (its row); תַּנּוֹת is found only here: taken as a feminine plural of תַּנִּים "jackals" (as Isa 34:13; Jer 9:11), uncertain; "dwellings" and the Greek "gifts" (from memory) are other readings.
- **"Whereas Edom saith, We are impoverished, but we will return and build the desolate places" → "If Edom says, We are beaten down, and we will return and build the ruins"** (v4) — כִּי "if / though"; רֻשַּׁשְׁנוּ pual of רָשַׁשׁ "beat down, shatter" (Jer 5:17); וְנָשׁוּב וְנִבְנֶה "and we will return and build" (the שׁוּב + a second verb row: = build again); חֳרָבוֹת "ruins" (its row). **"They shall build, but I will throw down; and they shall call them, The border of wickedness, and, The people against whom the LORD hath indignation for ever" → "They, they shall build, and I, I will tear down; and they shall call them the border of wickedness, and the people whom Yahweh has denounced forever"** — הֵמָּה … וַאֲנִי, the pronouns stressed; וְ "and" (not "but"); הָרַס "tear down" (as Jer 45:4); גְּבוּל "border" (the land within it: "territory", its row); רִשְׁעָה "wickedness" (its row); זָעַם "denounce" (its row: speak wrath against), perfect.
- **"The LORD will be magnified from the border of Israel" → "Yahweh shall be great above the border of Israel"** (v5) — Hebrew יִגְדַּל יְהוָה, imperfect "shall be great" (also read as a wish, "may Yahweh be great"); מֵעַל לִגְבוּל "above / over the border of" (as מֵעַל לָרָקִיעַ "above the vault", Gen 1:7): either "beyond Israel's border" or "over Israel's territory"; open. וְאַתֶּם "and you", stressed.
- **"A son honoureth his father, and a servant his master: if then I be a father, where is mine honour? and if I be a master, where is my fear?" → "A son honors a father, and a slave his lord. And if I am a father, where is my honor? And if I am lord, where is my fear?"** (v6) — כִּבֵּד "honor" (its row) with כְּבוֹדִי "my honor": the noun is the כָּבוֹד "glory" of its row; here "honor" to keep the one root of the verse; אָב without "his"; עֶבֶד "slave" (its row; of a king's men or of Yahweh "servant"); אֲדֹנָיו "his lord" (אָדוֹן row; not "master"); אֲדוֹנִים is plural ("lords", the plural of majesty, as of a master in Gen 39:2); מוֹרָאִי "my fear" (the fear due to me; its row). **"O priests, that despise my name" → "the priests, who despise my name"** — no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it); בּוֹזֵי שְׁמִי participle "despisers of my name"; בָּזָה "despise" (its row).
- **"Ye offer polluted bread upon mine altar … Wherein have we polluted thee? In that ye say, The table of the LORD is contemptible" → "Bringing forward upon my altar defiled bread … In what have we defiled you? In your saying, The table of Yahweh, it is despised"** (v7) — מַגִּישִׁים, a participle with no subject, the answer to "in what" of v6 ("you" supplied by KJV); הִגִּישׁ "bring forward" (its row, kept apart from הִקְרִיב "bring near", v8); לֶחֶם "bread" (its row; "food" in ESV); מְגֹאָל, גֵּאַלְנוּךָ from גָּאַל II "defile" (not the גָּאַל "redeem" of its row); נִבְזֶה הוּא "it is despised" (niphal participle of בָּזָה; also "despicable"), the pronoun הוּא kept as "it".
- **"And if ye offer the blind for sacrifice, is it not evil? … offer it now unto thy governor; will he be pleased with thee, or accept thy person?" → "And when you bring forward the blind to sacrifice, there is no evil; … Bring it near, please, to your governor: will he be pleased with you, or will he lift up your face?"** (v8) — אֵין רָע "there is no evil" twice: no question word; heard as the priests' own word (a mocking statement) or as a question by tone ("is it not evil?"); open. עִוֵּר "the blind", פִּסֵּחַ "the lame" (its row), חֹלֶה "the sick"; הַקְרִיבֵהוּ "bring it near" (הִקְרִיב row); נָא "please" (kept); פֶּחָה "governor" (its row); הֲיִרְצְךָ "will he be pleased with you" (רָצָה row; not "accept"); נָשָׂא פָנִים "lift up the face of" (its row: show regard to; kept).
- **"beseech God that he will be gracious unto us: this hath been by your means: will he regard your persons?" → "beseech, please, the face of El, that he may show favor to us. From your hand this has been: will he lift up faces from you?"** (v9) — חַלּוּ־נָא פְנֵי־אֵל "beseech, please, the face of El" (the row; lit. "make the face soft"); אֵל "El" (token); וִיחָנֵנוּ "that he may show favor to us" (חָנַן row; the "us" of the prophet among the people, kept); מִיֶּדְכֶם הָיְתָה זֹּאת "from your hand this has been"; הֲיִשָּׂא מִכֶּם פָּנִים "will he lift up faces from you" (מִכֶּם "from you": on your account, or "of any of you"); the call may be ironic: not decided in the verse.
- **"Who is there even among you that would shut the doors for nought? neither do ye kindle fire on mine altar for nought" → "Who is there also among you who would shut the doors, so that you would not light my altar for nothing?"** (v10) — מִי גַם־בָּכֶם וְיִסְגֹּר דְּלָתַיִם, a wish put as a question ("is there" supplied, a copula); חִנָּם "for nothing" (its row) belongs to the second clause by the accents; תָאִירוּ hifil of אוֹר "make light, light" ("fire" is not written). **"I have no pleasure in you … neither will I accept an offering at your hand" → "I have no delight in you … and an offering I will not be pleased with from your hand"** — חֵפֶץ "delight"; מִנְחָה here "offering" (the word for a gift: Malachi uses it of the animals brought, v13; 2:12, 13; 3:3, 4; the "grain offering" of its row is the Levitical sense); לֹא־אֶרְצֶה "I will not be pleased with" (רָצָה row); the object first, as the Hebrew.
- **"my name shall be great among the Gentiles; and in every place incense shall be offered unto my name, and a pure offering: for my name shall be great among the heathen" → "great is my name among the nations, and in every place incense is brought forward to my name, and a pure offering; for great is my name among the nations"** (v11) — as printed: גָּדוֹל שְׁמִי has no verb: "great is" (present; "shall be" of KJV is a reading of the sense); מֻקְטָר מֻגָּשׁ two hofal participles: מֻקְטָר "what is turned into smoke" (incense: the הִקְטִיר row), מֻגָּשׁ "is brought forward" (הִגִּישׁ, as v7); joined by the accents; also read "incense is offered, (and) brought forward". מְבוֹאוֹ "its setting" (lit. "its going in"); גּוֹיִם "nations" both times (KJV "Gentiles", "heathen"); מִנְחָה "offering" (as v10). Who brings it and when is not said in the verse.
- **"But ye have profaned it, in that ye say, The table of the LORD is polluted; and the fruit thereof, even his meat, is contemptible" → "And you are profaning it in your saying, The table of Adonai, it is defiled, and its fruit, its food, is despised"** (v12) — מְחַלְּלִים participle "are profaning" (חִלֵּל "profane", its row); אֲדֹנָי (token `adonai`); נִיבוֹ "its fruit" (נִיב, rare: "produce", uncertain); אָכְלוֹ "its food", in apposition; נִבְזֶה "despised" (as v7).
- **"Behold, what a weariness is it! and ye have snuffed at it … ye brought that which was torn, and the lame, and the sick; thus ye brought an offering: should I accept this of your hand?" → "Behold, what hardship! and you have puffed at it … and you have brought what was robbed, and the lame, and the sick, and you have brought the offering. Shall I be pleased with it from your hand?"** (v13) — מַתְּלָאָה = מַה־תְּלָאָה "what hardship" (תְּלָאָה row; lit. "weariness"); הִפַּחְתֶּם אוֹתוֹ "you have puffed at it" (the הֵפִיחַ row: = scorned); גָּזוּל "what was robbed" (גָּזַל "rob", its row; not "torn": that is טְרֵפָה); הַמִּנְחָה "the offering" with the article; וַהֲבֵאתֶם twice "and you have brought" (not "thus"); הַאֶרְצֶה "shall I be pleased with" (רָצָה row).
- **"But cursed be the deceiver, which hath in his flock a male, and voweth, and sacrificeth unto the Lord a corrupt thing" → "And cursed is the deceiver: and there is in his drove a male, and he vows, and sacrifices to Adonai a ruined thing"** (v14) — אָרוּר "cursed is" (the row: no verb in the Hebrew, "is", not "be"); נוֹכֵל participle of נָכַל "deal craftily" (the root of the נֵכֶל "plot" row); עֵדֶר "drove" (its row); מָשְׁחָת "a ruined thing" (the row "ruin": what is ruined, a blemished animal); אֲדֹנָי (token). **"for I am a great King … and my name is dreadful among the heathen" → "For a great king am I … and my name is fearsome among the nations"** — the predicate first, as the Hebrew; נוֹרָא "fearsome" (its row; participle, present).

### Notes
- **Structure** — the book is a series of disputes: Yahweh says a thing, the hearers answer "In what …?", and the answer follows (1:2, 6, 7; 2:14, 17; 3:7, 8, 13).
