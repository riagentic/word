# 1 Samuel 28

[1] And it came to pass in those days that the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ gathered their camps for the host, to fight against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. And Achish said to David, "You shall surely know that with me you shall go out in the camp, you and your men."

[2] And David said to Achish, "Therefore you yourself shall know what your servant will do." And Achish said to David, "Therefore I will make you the keeper of my head all the days."

[3] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ had died, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had lamented for him and buried him in Ramah, and in his city. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ had removed the ghosts and the knowing spirits from the land.

[4] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ were gathered and came and camped at Shunem, and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ gathered all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they camped at the Gilboa.

[5] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ saw the camp of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and he feared, and his heart trembled greatly.

[6] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ asked of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did not answer him, neither by the dreams nor by the Urim nor by the prophets.

[7] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to his servants, "Seek for me a woman who is mistress of a ghost, and let me go to her and seek by her." And his servants said to him, "Behold, a woman who is mistress of a ghost is at ⟦en-dor|Ein Dor|En-dor⟧."

[8] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ disguised himself and put on other garments, and he went, he and two men with him, and they came to the woman by night. And he said, "Practice divination for me, please, by the ghost, and bring up for me the one whom I shall say to you."

[9] And the woman said to him, "Behold, you know what ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ has done, that he has cut off the ghosts and the knowing spirit from the land. And why are you laying a snare for my soul, to put me to death?"

[10] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ swore to her by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, no iniquity shall befall you in this matter."

[11] And the woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?" And he said, "Bring up ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ for me."

[12] And the woman saw ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧, and she cried out with a loud voice. And the woman said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, saying, "Why have you deceived me? And you are ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧!"

[13] And the king said to her, "Do not fear; for what have you seen?" And the woman said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "I have seen ⟦elohim|elohim|gods⟧ coming up from the earth."

[14] And he said to her, "What is his form?" And she said, "An old man is coming up, and he is wrapped in a robe." And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ knew that he was ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧, and he bowed his head, face to the earth, and bowed down.

[15] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "Why have you disturbed me, to bring me up?" And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "I am in great distress, and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ are fighting against me, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has departed from me and has not answered me any more, neither by the hand of the prophets nor by the dreams; and I have called you, to make known to me what I shall do."

[16] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ said, "And why do you ask me, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has departed from you and has become your adversary?

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done for himself as he spoke by my hand, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has torn the kingdom from your hand and given it to your neighbor, to David.

[18] Because you did not listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and did not do the burning of his anger against ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧, therefore ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done this thing to you this day.

[19] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ also, with you, into the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me; the camp of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ also ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give into the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧."

[20] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ hastened and fell his full length to the earth, and he feared greatly because of the words of ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧; also there was no power in him, for he had not eaten bread all the day and all the night.

[21] And the woman came to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and saw that he was greatly dismayed, and she said to him, "Behold, your maidservant has listened to your voice, and I have put my soul in my palm and have heard your words that you spoke to me.

[22] And now, listen, please, you also, to the voice of your maidservant, and let me put before you a bit of bread, and eat, and let there be power in you, for you will go on the way."

[23] And he refused and said, "I will not eat." And his servants pressed upon him, and also the woman, and he listened to their voice, and he arose from the earth and sat on the bed.

[24] And the woman had a stall-fed calf in the house, and she hastened and slaughtered it, and she took meal and kneaded and baked it into unleavened bread.

[25] And she brought it near before ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and before his servants, and they ate. And they arose and went away in that night.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15) — Hebrew *Elohim*; for v13 see below.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"gathered their armies together for warfare" → "gathered their camps for the host"** (v1) — Hebrew מַחֲנֵיהֶם "their camps" (the word of v5, 19); לַצָּבָא "for the host", that is, for service in war.
- **"Know thou assuredly" → "You shall surely know"** (v1) — Hebrew יָדֹעַ תֵּדַע, the infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "knowing you shall know". **"go out with me to battle" → "with me you shall go out in the camp"** — Hebrew בַּמַּחֲנֶה.
- **"Surely thou shalt know what thy servant can do" → "Therefore you yourself shall know what your servant will do"** (v2) — Hebrew לָכֵן אַתָּה תֵדַע … יַעֲשֶׂה: "therefore", the pronoun stressed, "will do". David's answer says nothing of which side he will fight on.
- **"keeper of mine head for ever" → "the keeper of my head all the days"** (v2) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר לְרֹאשִׁי … כָּל־הַיָּמִים, kept word for word (= my bodyguard, for life).
- **"lamented him, and buried him in Ramah, even in his own city" → "had lamented for him and buried him in Ramah, and in his city"** (v3) — Hebrew בָרָמָה וּבְעִירוֹ: the Masoretic Text has "and in his city"; the "and" is commonly taken as "that is" (25:1 has "in his house, in Ramah"). The verbs look back to 25:1.
- **"those that had familiar spirits, and the wizards" → "the ghosts and the knowing spirits"** (v3, 9) — Hebrew הָאֹבוֹת … הַיִּדְּעֹנִים: אוֹב *ov* and יִדְּעֹנִי *yidde'oni* (from יָדַע "know") name the spirits that are asked, not the persons who ask them (Lev 19:31; 20:6, 27; Deut 18:11). The exact sense of both words is uncertain. "Mediums", "wizards", "spiritists" name the persons and are not what the Hebrew says. In v9 the second word is singular, "the knowing spirit".
- **"pitched" → "camped"** (v4) — Hebrew חָנָה. **"in Gilboa" → "at the Gilboa"** — the Hebrew has the article.
- **"he was afraid" → "he feared"** (v5, 20) — Hebrew יָרֵא.
- **"enquired of the LORD" → "asked of Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאַל … בַּיהוָה: the verb is שָׁאַל "ask" (the word of 22:10; 23:2 and of v16 here, "why do you ask me", and the root of the name Saul, שָׁאוּל "asked for"). 1 Chr 10:14 says that Saul "did not seek Yahweh" (another verb, דָּרַשׁ). **"neither by dreams, nor by Urim, nor by prophets" → "neither by the dreams nor by the Urim nor by the prophets"** — all three have the article; גַּם … גַּם … גַּם is "also … also … also".
- **"a woman that hath a familiar spirit" → "a woman who is mistress of a ghost"** (v7) — Hebrew אֵשֶׁת בַּעֲלַת־אוֹב, word for word "a woman, a mistress (owner) of an *ov*": בַּעֲלָה is the feminine of בַּעַל "owner, lord". The sense is a woman who has such a spirit at her call, by which the dead are asked. Not "medium", "witch", "necromancer".
- **"enquire of her" → "seek by her"** (v7) — Hebrew וְאֶדְרְשָׁה־בָּהּ: דָּרַשׁ "seek" with בְּ "by, through"; another verb than "asked" in v6.
- **"put on other raiment" → "put on other garments"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּגָדִים.
- **"divine unto me by the familiar spirit" → "Practice divination for me, please, by the ghost"** (v8) — Hebrew קָסֳמִי־נָא לִי בָּאוֹב (qere; the written text has the fuller form קסומי): קָסַם "practice divination" (Deut 18:10); נָא "please". **"whom I shall name unto thee" → "the one whom I shall say to you"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־אֹמַר אֵלָיִךְ.
- **"wherefore then layest thou a snare for my life, to cause me to die" → "why are you laying a snare for my soul, to put me to death"** (v9) — Hebrew מִתְנַקֵּשׁ בְּנַפְשִׁי; הֵמִית "put to death".
- **"there shall no punishment happen to thee for this thing" → "no iniquity shall befall you in this matter"** (v10) — Hebrew אִם־יִקְּרֵךְ עָוֺן, the oath form: literally "if iniquity befalls you …" = it shall not. עָוֺן is "iniquity": the guilt and what it brings.
- **"said … Why hast thou deceived me? for thou art Saul" → "said … saying, 'Why have you deceived me? And you are Saul!'"** (v12) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר kept; וְאַתָּה שָׁאוּל "and you are Saul". The verse does not say how the sight of Samuel made her know Saul.
- **"Be not afraid: for what sawest thou?" → "Do not fear; for what have you seen?"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי מָה רָאִית, a perfect; so also רָאִיתִי "I have seen".
- **"I saw gods ascending out of the earth" → "I have seen *elohim* coming up from the earth"** (v13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים רָאִיתִי עֹלִים מִן־הָאָרֶץ. אֱלֹהִים is the word used for God, for gods, and for beings not of this world; the participle "coming up" (עֹלִים) is plural, so the words can be read "gods coming up" (KJV), while Saul's question in v14 ("his form") and her answer ("an old man") speak of one figure, and so it is also read "a god", "a divine being", "a spirit". The verse gives the Hebrew word and does not decide; the traditional form of the token has "gods", after the plural participle. The earlier text had "a divine being". אֶרֶץ "earth" may be the ground or the land below; Sheol is not named in the chapter.
- **"What form is he of?" → "What is his form?"** (v14) — Hebrew מַה־תָּאֳרוֹ. **"covered with a mantle" → "wrapped in a robe"** — Hebrew עֹטֶה מְעִיל; מְעִיל is the robe that Samuel wore (2:19; 15:27).
- **"Saul perceived that it was Samuel" → "Saul knew that he was Samuel"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיֵּדַע. The narrator says "Samuel" in v12, 15, 16, 20 without any qualification; it is kept so. **"stooped with his face to the ground, and bowed himself" → "bowed his head, face to the earth, and bowed down"** — Hebrew וַיִּקֹּד אַפַּיִם אַרְצָה וַיִּשְׁתָּחוּ: אַפַּיִם is literally "nostrils".
- **"Why hast thou disquieted me, to bring me up?" → "Why have you disturbed me, to bring me up?"** (v15) — Hebrew הִרְגַּזְתַּנִי, the causative of רָגַז "be agitated, tremble": to rouse out of rest.
- **"I am sore distressed; for the Philistines make war against me" → "I am in great distress, and the Philistines are fighting against me"** (v15) — Hebrew צַר־לִי מְאֹד, literally "it is very narrow for me"; וּ "and"; נִלְחָמִים "fighting".
- **"God is departed from me, and answereth me no more, neither by prophets" → "*Elohim* has departed from me and has not answered me any more, neither by the hand of the prophets"** (v15) — Hebrew סָר מֵעָלַי, literally "has turned aside from upon me"; בְּיַד "by the hand of". Saul does not name the Urim here (v6).
- **"Wherefore then dost thou ask of me, seeing the LORD is departed from thee, and is become thine enemy?" → "And why do you ask me, and Yahweh has departed from you and has become your adversary?"** (v16) — Hebrew וְלָמָּה תִּשְׁאָלֵנִי וַיהוָה …: two clauses joined by "and". עָרֶךָ is a rare word, taken as "your adversary" (a form of צַר; cf. Ps 139:20); its sense is uncertain. The Septuagint is reported to have "and is with your neighbor" (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"the LORD hath done to him" → "Yahweh has done for himself"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ יְהוָה לוֹ: לוֹ is "to him / for him"; it is taken of Yahweh ("for himself"), and may also be read "for him", that is, for David. Some Hebrew manuscripts and the old versions have "to you" (cited from memory), which the earlier text followed; the Masoretic Text is kept. **"by me" → "by my hand"** — Hebrew בְּיָדִי. **"rent" → "torn"** — Hebrew קָרַע (15:28).
- **"obeyedst not the voice" → "did not listen to the voice"** (v18) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתָּ בְּקוֹל. **"nor executedst his fierce wrath upon Amalek" → "did not do the burning of his anger against Amalek"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־עָשִׂיתָ חֲרוֹן־אַפּוֹ, with the verb "do", which the verse uses twice more of Yahweh.
- **"Moreover the LORD will also deliver Israel with thee … and to morrow shalt thou and thy sons be with me: the LORD also shall deliver the host of Israel" → "And Yahweh will give Israel also, with you, … and tomorrow you and your sons will be with me; the camp of Israel also Yahweh will give"** (v19) — word for word: וְיִתֵּן יְהוָה גַּם אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל עִמְּךָ בְּיַד־פְּלִשְׁתִּים וּמָחָר אַתָּה וּבָנֶיךָ עִמִּי גַּם אֶת־מַחֲנֵה יִשְׂרָאֵל יִתֵּן יְהוָה בְּיַד־פְּלִשְׁתִּים. נָתַן "give"; מַחֲנֶה "camp". "With me" (עִמִּי) has no verb in the Hebrew and is not explained in the verse: with Samuel, among the dead.
- **"Then Saul fell straightway all along on the earth" → "And Saul hastened and fell his full length to the earth"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיְמַהֵר … וַיִּפֹּל מְלֹא־קוֹמָתוֹ, literally "hastened and fell, the fullness of his stature". **"strength" → "power"** (v20, 22) — Hebrew כֹּחַ.
- **"sore troubled" → "greatly dismayed"** (v21) — Hebrew נִבְהַל. **"thine handmaid hath obeyed thy voice, and I have put my life in my hand, and have hearkened unto thy words" → "your maidservant has listened to your voice, and I have put my soul in my palm and have heard your words"** — Hebrew שִׁפְחָה; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice", then שָׁמַע אֶת "hear"; נַפְשִׁי בְּכַפִּי, "my soul in my palm" (19:5; Judg 12:3): to risk one's life.
- **"a morsel of bread" → "a bit of bread"; "that thou mayest have strength, when thou goest on thy way" → "and let there be power in you, for you will go on the way"** (v22) — Hebrew פַּת־לֶחֶם; וִיהִי בְךָ כֹּחַ כִּי תֵלֵךְ בַּדָּרֶךְ.
- **"his servants, together with the woman, compelled him" → "his servants pressed upon him, and also the woman"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּפְרְצוּ־בוֹ: the Masoretic Text has the verb פָּרַץ "break out, break through" (literally "they broke out upon him"), which is difficult here; it is commonly corrected to פָּצַר "urge" (the same letters in another order; so also in 2 Sam 13:25, 27). The Masoretic Text is translated. **"sat upon the bed"** — Hebrew אֶל־הַמִּטָּה, "toward / on the bed".
- **"a fat calf" → "a stall-fed calf"; "killed it" → "slaughtered it"; "flour" → "meal"** (v24) — Hebrew עֵגֶל־מַרְבֵּק, "a calf of the stall"; וַתִּזְבָּחֵהוּ, the verb זָבַח, elsewhere "sacrifice", here of killing for food (Deut 12:15, 21); קֶמַח.
- **"she brought it before Saul" → "she brought it near before Saul"** (v25) — Hebrew וַתַּגֵּשׁ. **"went away that night" → "went away in that night"** — Hebrew בַּלַּיְלָה הַהוּא.

### Notes
- **Saul's final desperation** — denied prophetic access (v6), he turns to a woman who is mistress of a ghost — the very thing he himself had removed from the land (v3, 9).
- **The woman of En-dor** — often called "Witch of Endor" or "medium"; the Hebrew calls her "a woman, mistress of an *ov*". Asking the dead is forbidden (Lev 19:31, Deut 18:11).
- **Samuel** — the narrator says simply "Samuel" (v12, 14–16, 20), though some readings argue it is a necromantic deception. The content of the message is accurate; Samuel rebukes Saul and predicts his death.
- **"Tomorrow you and your sons will be with me"** (v19) — Samuel predicts Saul's death and his sons' deaths in the coming battle.
- **The woman's hospitality** (v21–25) — even the outlawed woman shows compassion. Ironic contrast: her kindness to the king who had outlawed her.
- **Saul's last supper** (v24–25) — grim parallel. The condemned man eats a final meal.
