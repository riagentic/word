# 1 Samuel 31

[1] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ were fighting against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ fled from the face of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and fell slain on Mount Gilboa.

[2] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ caught up with ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and his sons, and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ struck down ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ and ⟦abinadab|Avinadav|Abinadab⟧ and ⟦malchishua|Malki-Shua|Malchi-shua⟧, the sons of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[3] And the battle was heavy against ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and the archers found him, men with the bow, and he was in great anguish because of the archers.

[4] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to his weapon-bearer, "Draw your sword and pierce me with it, lest these uncircumcised come and pierce me and deal ruthlessly with me." And his weapon-bearer was not willing, for he feared greatly. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ took the sword and fell upon it.

[5] And his weapon-bearer saw that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was dead, and he also fell upon his sword and died with him.

[6] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ died, and his three sons and his weapon-bearer, also all his men, on that day together.

[7] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who were across the valley and who were across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ saw that the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had fled and that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and his sons had died, and they left the cities and fled, and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ came and dwelt in them.

[8] And it came to pass on the morrow that the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ came to strip the slain, and they found ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and his three sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.

[9] And they cut off his head and stripped off his gear, and they sent in the land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ all around, to bring the news to the house of their idols and to the people.

[10] And they put his gear in the house of ⟦ashtaroth-goddesses|Ashtarot|Ashtaroth⟧, and his body they fastened on the wall of ⟦beth-shan|Beit Shan|Beth-shan⟧.

[13] And they took their bones and buried them under the tamarisk in ⟦jabesh|Yavesh|Jabesh⟧, and they fasted seven days.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Now the Philistines fought against Israel" → "And the Philistines were fighting against Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew נִלְחָמִים, a participle: the story takes up 28:4 and 29:11. **"fled from before the Philistines" → "fled from the face of the Philistines"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי. **"fell down slain in mount Gilboa" → "fell slain on Mount Gilboa"** (v1, 8) — Hebrew חֲלָלִים, "pierced ones"; בְּהַר הַגִּלְבֹּעַ, "on the mountain of the Gilboa", with the article.
- **"followed hard upon Saul" → "caught up with Saul"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיַּדְבְּקוּ … אֶת, literally "clung to"; in 14:22 the same verb stands with אַחֲרֵי "after". **"slew" → "struck down"** — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike". The names are joined by "and".
- **"the battle went sore against Saul" → "the battle was heavy against Saul"** (v3) — Hebrew וַתִּכְבַּד הַמִּלְחָמָה אֶל, the verb "be heavy".
- **"the archers hit him" → "the archers found him, men with the bow"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּמְצָאֻהוּ הַמּוֹרִים אֲנָשִׁים בַּקָּשֶׁת: מָצָא "find"; הַמּוֹרִים "the shooters" (from יָרָה "shoot, cast"); "men with the bow" is in the Hebrew and was dropped in the earlier text.
- **"he was sore wounded of the archers" → "he was in great anguish because of the archers"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיָּחֶל מְאֹד מֵהַמּוֹרִים: as the Masoretic Text is pointed, the verb is from חִיל "writhe, be in anguish, tremble" (the root of "anguish", Exod 15:14): Saul is in dread of the archers; the verse does not say that an arrow hit him. The Septuagint is reported to have "he was wounded" (as from חָלַל or חָלָה; cited from memory), and so the KJV and the earlier text ("badly wounded"). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"armourbearer" → "weapon-bearer"** (v4, 5, 6) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא כֵלָיו, literally "the bearer of his gear": no armor is named. **"armour" → "gear"** (v9, 10) — the same word, כֵּלָיו: what a fighting man carries and wears, his weapons and equipment.
- **"thrust me through" → "pierce me"** (v4) — Hebrew דָּקַר, twice.
- **"abuse me" → "deal ruthlessly with me"** (v4) — Hebrew וְהִתְעַלְּלוּ־בִי: הִתְעַלֵּל בְּ is to treat a person as one pleases, wantonly, to make sport of him (Exod 10:2; Num 22:29; Judg 19:25; 1 Sam 6:6). "Torment" (the earlier text) says more than the word.
- **"would not; for he was sore afraid" → "was not willing, for he feared greatly"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא אָבָה; יָרֵא מְאֹד. **"took a sword" → "took the sword"** — the Hebrew has the article; the earlier text had "his sword", which stands only in v5 of the weapon-bearer.
- **"So Saul died … that same day together" → "And Saul died … also all his men, on that day together"** (v6) — Hebrew גַּם כָּל־אֲנָשָׁיו. 1 Chr 10:6 has "and all his house" in place of the weapon-bearer and the men.
- **"on the other side of the valley, and they that were on the other side Jordan" → "across the valley and who were across the Jordan"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הָעֵמֶק … בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן, the same words twice. **"were dead" → "had died"** — Hebrew מֵתוּ. **"forsook the cities" → "left the cities"; "dwelt in them"** — Hebrew עָזַב; יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"it came to pass on the morrow" kept** (v8) — Hebrew וַיְהִי מִמָּחֳרָת. **"fallen"** — Hebrew נֹפְלִים, a participle: lying fallen.
- **"stripped off his armour, and sent into the land of the Philistines round about, to publish it in the house of their idols, and among the people" → "stripped off his gear, and they sent in the land of the Philistines all around, to bring the news to the house of their idols and to the people"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיְשַׁלְּחוּ, "they sent", with no object: messengers, or the head and the gear. לְבַשֵּׂר is "bring news" (4:17). בֵּית עֲצַבֵּיהֶם "the house of their idols" (עֲצַבִּים, shaped images); 1 Chr 10:9 has "to their idols", without "house".
- **"in the house of Ashtaroth" kept** (v10) — Hebrew בֵּית עַשְׁתָּרוֹת: a plural form, "the Ashtoreths", the goddesses (7:3; 12:10); here without the article, as the name of a temple. The earlier text had "temple"; the word is "house".
- **"they fastened his body to the wall" → "his body they fastened on the wall"** (v10) — Hebrew תָּקְעוּ, the verb "thrust, drive in" (Judg 4:21): fixed there as with pegs or stakes. "Thrust", its usual rendering in this translation, would not say in English what was done; "fastened" is kept. גְּוִיָּה "body".
- **"when the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard of that which" → "the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard of it, what"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמְעוּ אֵלָיו … אֵת אֲשֶׁר: אֵלָיו is literally "to him / concerning him"; the phrase is difficult and is rendered "of it". The verse is a sentence of its own, joined to v12 by "and".
- **"All the valiant men arose" → "every man of ability arose"** (v12) — Hebrew כָּל־אִישׁ חַיִל; חַיִל is "ability, strength, force".
- **"under a tree" → "under the tamarisk"** (v13) — Hebrew הָאֶשֶׁל *ha-eshel*, "the tamarisk" (Gen 21:33; 1 Sam 22:6), with the article. This is Samuel's own word; 1 Chr 10:12 has another tree, הָאֵלָה "the terebinth". (The earlier note said the tamarisk came from Chronicles; it is the reverse.)
- **"burnt them there"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂרְפוּ אֹתָם שָׁם, the plain verb "burn"; 1 Chr 10:12 does not speak of burning.

### Notes
- **Battle of Gilboa** — Saul's predicted death (28:19) fulfilled. Israel routed; Saul and three sons killed. Jonathan, David's beloved friend, dies.
- **Saul's death** (v4–5) — refuses to be taken alive. Weapon-bearer will not strike the king; Saul falls on the sword. Weapon-bearer follows. Later 2 Sam 1 has an alternate death-tradition told by an Amalekite.
- **Philistine desecration** (v9–10) — Saul's head cut off; body fastened on the city wall. Gear in the house of Ashtaroth.
- **Jabesh-gilead's loyalty** (v11–13) — the city Saul rescued in ch 11 returns the favor. Night march, body recovery, burning (unusual for Israelites), burial, and seven-day fast.
- **End of Saul's reign** — 40 years (per Acts 13:21). Beginning marked by rescue of Jabesh-gilead (ch 11); ending marked by Jabesh-gilead's rescue of his body.
- **Transition to 2 Samuel** — with Saul dead, David's path to kingship clears. The next book begins with David learning of Saul's death.
