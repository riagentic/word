# 1 Samuel 22

[1] And David went from there and escaped to the cave of Adullam. And his brothers and all the house of his father heard, and they went down to him there.

[2] And there gathered to him every man in straits, and every man who had a creditor, and every man bitter of soul, and he became commander over them. And there were with him about four hundred men.

[3] And David went from there to ⟦mizpeh|Mitzpeh|Mizpeh⟧ of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and he said to the king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, "Let my father and my mother, please, come out with you, until I know what ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will do for me."

[4] And he led them before the face of the king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and they dwelt with him all the days that David was in the stronghold.

[5] And Gad the prophet said to David, "You shall not dwell in the stronghold. Go, and get yourself to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧." And David went and came to the forest of ⟦hereth|Cheret|Hereth⟧.

[6] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ heard that David had become known, and the men who were with him. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was sitting in ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ under the tamarisk on the height, and his spear was in his hand, and all his servants were standing by him.

[7] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to his servants who were standing by him, "Hear, please, ⟦benjaminite|sons of Yemini|sons of Benjamin⟧! Will the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧ also give to all of you fields and vineyards? Will he make all of you commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds,

[8] that all of you have conspired against me, and there is none who uncovers my ear when my son cuts a covenant with the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧, and there is none of you who is sick over me and uncovers my ear, that my son has raised up my servant against me as one lying in ambush, as at this day?"

[9] And Doeg the ⟦edomites|Edomi|Edomite⟧ answered — and he was standing by the servants of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ — and he said, "I saw the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧ coming to ⟦nob|Nov|Nob⟧, to ⟦ahimelech|Achimelech|Ahimelech⟧ the son of ⟦ahitub|Achituv|Ahitub⟧.

[10] And he asked of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for him, and provision he gave him, and the sword of ⟦goliath|Golyat|Goliath⟧ the ⟦philistines|Pelishti|Philistine⟧ he gave him."

[11] And the king sent to call ⟦ahimelech|Achimelech|Ahimelech⟧ the son of ⟦ahitub|Achituv|Ahitub⟧, the priest, and all the house of his father, the priests who were in ⟦nob|Nov|Nob⟧, and all of them came to the king.

[12] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Hear, please, son of ⟦ahitub|Achituv|Ahitub⟧." And he said, "Here I am, my lord."

[13] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to him, "Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧, in your giving him bread and a sword and asking of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for him, that he should rise against me as one lying in ambush, as at this day?"

[14] And ⟦ahimelech|Achimelech|Ahimelech⟧ answered the king and said, "And who among all your servants is like David, faithful, and the son-in-law of the king, and turning aside to your bidding, and honored in your house?

[15] Did I begin today to ask of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for him? Far be it from me! Let not the king put a thing on his servant, on all the house of my father, for your servant has not known in all this a thing small or great."

[16] And the king said, "You shall surely die, ⟦ahimelech|Achimelech|Ahimelech⟧, you and all the house of your father."

[17] And the king said to the runners who were standing by him, "Turn and put to death the priests of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for their hand also is with David, and because they knew that he was fleeing and did not uncover my ear." [19] And ⟦nob|Nov|Nob⟧, the city of the priests, he struck with the edge of the sword, from man to woman, from child to suckling, and ox and donkey and sheep, with the edge of the sword.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 13, 15) — Hebrew *Elohim*, without the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"every one that was in distress, and every one that was in debt, and every one that was discontented" → "every man in straits, and every man who had a creditor, and every man bitter of soul"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ מָצוֹק "a man of straits"; אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ נֹשֶׁא "who had a creditor"; מַר־נֶפֶשׁ "bitter of soul".
- **"captain over them" → "commander over them"** (v2) — Hebrew שַׂר "prince, chief"; the same word in v7 ("commanders of thousands").
- **"Let my father and my mother … come forth, and be with you" → "come out with you"** (v3) — Hebrew יֵצֵא … אִתְּכֶם, "go out with you": the expression is difficult ("come out and be with you" is the sense usually given; some versions have "dwell with you": from memory). "You" is plural.
- **"he brought them before the king of Moab" → "he led them before the face of the king of Moab"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיַּנְחֵם אֶת־פְּנֵי, pointed as the verb נָחָה "lead". With other vowels the same letters are "he left them", and so some versions read (from memory); the Masoretic pointing is translated.
- **"hold" → "stronghold"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew מְצוּדָה. **"dwelt" / "dwell"** (v4, 5, 23) — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"Abide not … depart, and get thee into" → "You shall not dwell … Go, and get yourself to"** (v5) — Hebrew לֵךְ וּבָאתָ־לְּךָ, literally "go, and come for yourself".
- **"David was discovered" → "David had become known"** (v6) — Hebrew נוֹדַע, the passive of יָדַע "know": it was known where he was.
- **"under a tree in Ramah" → "under the tamarisk on the height"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵשֶׁל "tamarisk" (the tree is fairly sure), with the article. בָּרָמָה is "on the height" or "in Ramah" (the same letters). **"Gibeah"** — Hebrew הַגִּבְעָה, "the hill".
- **"standing about him" → "standing by him"** (v6, 7, 9, 17) — Hebrew נִצָּב עַל, "stationed by / over". In v9 it may also say that Doeg was "set over" the servants of Saul.
- **"ye Benjamites" → "sons of Yemini"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יְמִינִי, "sons of the Yemini" (Benjaminites).
- **"will the son of Jesse give every one of you" → "Will the son of Jesse also give to all of you"** (v7) — Hebrew גַּם "also" (as I have done), and לְכֻלְּכֶם "to all of you" twice. The Hebrew has no question particle: the two sentences are statements said in scorn ("To all of you also the son of Jesse will give …!") or questions; v8 runs on from them with כִּי "that / for".
- **"sheweth me" → "uncovers my ear"** (v8, 17) — Hebrew גֹּלֶה אֶת־אָזְנִי, kept word for word (= tells me).
- **"hath made a league" → "cuts a covenant"** (v8) — Hebrew בִּכְרָת, "at the cutting": the verb of "cut a covenant" alone; the word "covenant" is supplied.
- **"is sorry for me" → "is sick over me"** (v8) — Hebrew חֹלֶה "sick": pained on my account. The Septuagint has "has pity" (from memory).
- **"hath stirred up my servant against me, to lie in wait" → "has raised up my servant against me as one lying in ambush"** (v8, 13) — Hebrew הֵקִים "make stand, raise up"; לְאֹרֵב, a participle, "for one lying in ambush".
- **"was set over" → "was standing by"** (v9) — see v6.
- **"enquired of the LORD for him" → "asked of Yahweh for him"** (v10, 13, 15) — Hebrew שָׁאַל בְּ "ask of", the plain verb "ask": to seek a word from God through the priest. Chapter 21 does not tell of it. **"victuals" → "provision"** — Hebrew צֵידָה.
- **"Here I am"** (v12) — Hebrew הִנְנִי, literally "behold me".
- **"which goeth at thy bidding" → "turning aside to your bidding"** (v14) — Hebrew וְסָר אֶל־מִשְׁמַעְתֶּךָ. Difficult: סָר is "turning aside", and מִשְׁמַעַת (from שָׁמַע "hear") is the hearing one gives, obedience, or the body of men who obey, a bodyguard (2 Sam 23:23). The Septuagint has "chief over all your command" (שַׂר for סָר; from memory). The Masoretic Text is translated as it stands.
- **"Did I then begin to enquire" → "Did I begin today to ask"** (v15) — Hebrew הַיּוֹם הַחִלֹּתִי "today I began …": no question particle marks it; it is a question by its sense (this was not the first time), or a statement.
- **"let not the king impute any thing unto his servant, nor to all the house" → "put a thing on his servant, on all the house"** (v15) — Hebrew שִׂים דָּבָר בְּ "put a word / thing in"; there is no "nor". **"less or more" → "small or great"**.
- **"Thou shalt surely die"** (v16) — Hebrew מוֹת תָּמוּת, "dying you shall die". So also "would surely tell" (v22), הַגֵּד יַגִּיד.
- **"footmen" → "runners"** (v17) — Hebrew רָצִים, "runners": the men who run before the king, his guard.
- **"slay" → "put to death"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew הֵמִית; "killed" (v21) is הָרַג; "struck" (v19) is הִכָּה.
- **"shewed it to me" → "uncover my ear"** (v17) — the written text (ketiv) has "his ear"; the reading tradition (qere) "my ear".
- **"would not put forth their hand to fall upon" → "were not willing to stretch out their hand to fall upon"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew אָבָה "be willing"; פָּגַע בְּ "meet, fall upon".
- **"that did wear a linen ephod" → "bearing a linen ephod"** (v18) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא "bearing, carrying". The name is written דויג in v18 and 22 and read "Doeg".
- **"with the edge of the sword"** (v19) — Hebrew לְפִי־חֶרֶב, literally "to the mouth of the sword". **"both men and women, children and sucklings, and oxen, and asses, and sheep" → "from man to woman, from child to suckling, and ox and donkey and sheep"** — all singular in the Hebrew.
- **"I have occasioned the death of all the persons" → "I myself have turned upon every soul"** (v22) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי סַבֹּתִי בְּכָל־נֶפֶשׁ: the verb סָבַב "turn" of v17–18 ("Turn and put to death", "Doeg turned"). Difficult: David seems to say that he was the turning point of it, the cause. The Septuagint has "I am guilty of the souls" (from memory), and a like word is often conjectured; the Masoretic Text is translated. נֶפֶשׁ is "soul".
- **"he that seeketh my life seeketh thy life" → "he who seeks my soul seeks your soul"** (v23) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ.
- **"with me thou shalt be in safeguard" → "you are a charge with me"** (v23) — Hebrew מִשְׁמֶרֶת, from שָׁמַר "keep": a thing given to be kept and guarded.

### Notes
- **David gathers 400 men** (v1–2) — men in straits, debtors, the bitter of soul.
- **Adullam cave** — refuge in the lowland of Judah. The headings of Psalms 57 and 142 speak of David "in the cave".
- **Parents to Moab** — David has Moabite ancestry through Ruth (Ruth 4:17).
- **Gad the prophet** (v5) — named here for the first time (cf. 2 Sam 24:11).
- **Saul's speech** (v6–8) — addresses Benjaminites (his own tribe). Accuses a conspiracy of which the story has told nothing.
- **Doeg** (v9–19) — the witness of 21:7 becomes the one who kills.
- **Nob** — 85 priests and the whole city. Cf. the word of Yahweh against Eli's house (1 Sam 2:31–36; 3:11–14). Abiathar alone escapes.
