# 1 Samuel 20

[1] And David fled from ⟦naioth|Nayot|Naioth⟧ in Ramah, and he came and said before ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, "What have I done? What is my iniquity, and what is my sin before your father, that he is seeking my soul?"

[2] And he said to him, "Far be it! You shall not die. Behold, my father does not do a great thing or a small thing and not uncover my ear; and why should my father hide this thing from me? This is not so."

[3] And David swore again and said, "Your father surely knows that I have found favor in your eyes, and he has said, 'Let not ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ know this, lest he be grieved.' But truly, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives and by the life of your soul, there is but a step between me and death."

[4] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said to David, "What your soul says, I will do for you."

[5] And David said to ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, "Behold, tomorrow is the new moon, and I should surely sit with the king to eat; and you shall let me go, and I will conceal myself in the field until the third evening.

[6] If your father surely misses me, then you shall say, 'David surely asked leave of me to run to ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧ his city, for the sacrifice of the days is there for all the clan.'

[7] If thus he says, 'Good,' there is ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ for your servant; and if he surely burns with anger, know that the evil is determined on his part.

[8] And you shall do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ to your servant, for into a covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ you have brought your servant with you. And if there is iniquity in me, put me to death yourself; and to your father why is it that you should bring me?"

[9] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said, "Far be it from you! For if I surely know that the evil is determined on the part of my father to come upon you, would I not tell it to you?"

[10] And David said to ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, "Who will tell me, or what hard thing your father answers you?"

[11] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said to David, "Come, and let us go out to the field." And the two of them went out to the field.

[12] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said to David, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧! When I examine my father about this time tomorrow, the third day, and behold, it is good toward David, shall I not then send to you and uncover your ear?

[13] Thus may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ do to ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, and thus may he add: if the evil upon you is good to my father, I will uncover your ear and let you go, and you shall go in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧. And may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be with you, as he was with my father.

[14] And will you not, if I am still alive, will you not do with me the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I shall not die?

[15] And you shall not cut off your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ from my house forever, and not when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cuts off the enemies of David, each man, from upon the face of the ground."

[16] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ cut a covenant with the house of David: "And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall require it from the hand of the enemies of David."

[17] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ again made David swear, because he loved him, for with the love of his own soul he loved him.

[18] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said to him, "Tomorrow is the new moon, and you will be missed, for your seat will be missed.

[19] And on the third day you shall go far down, and you shall come to the place where you concealed yourself on the day of the deed, and you shall sit beside the ⟦ezel|stone Ezel|stone heap⟧.

[20] And I will shoot the three arrows to its side, sending them for myself at a mark.

[21] And behold, I will send the boy: 'Go, find the arrows.' If I surely say to the boy, 'Behold, the arrows are on this side of you, take it,' then come, for there is ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ for you, and there is nothing, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives.

[22] And if thus I say to the lad, 'Behold, the arrows are beyond you,' go, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has sent you away.

[23] And the word that we have spoken, I and you, behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is between me and you forever."

[24] And David concealed himself in the field. And it was the new moon, and the king sat down to the bread to eat.

[25] And the king sat on his seat, as time upon time, on the seat by the wall, and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ arose, and ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ sat at the side of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and the place of David was missed.

[26] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ did not speak anything on that day, for he said, "It is a chance; he is not clean, for he is not clean."

[27] And it came to pass on the morrow of the new moon, the second day, that the place of David was missed. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ his son, "Why has the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧ not come, both yesterday and today, to the bread?"

[28] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ answered ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "David surely asked leave of me as far as ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧.

[29] And he said, 'Let me go, please, for we have a sacrifice of the clan in the city, and he, my brother, has commanded me; and now, if I have found favor in your eyes, let me escape, please, and see my brothers.' Therefore he has not come to the table of the king."

[30] And the anger of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ burned against ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, and he said to him, "Son of a perverse woman of rebellion! Do I not know that you are choosing the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧ to your shame and to the shame of the nakedness of your mother?

[31] For all the days that the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧ lives upon the ground, you shall not be established, you and your kingdom. And now, send and take him to me, for he is a son of death."

[32] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ answered ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ his father and said to him, "Why shall he be put to death? What has he done?"

[33] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ hurled the spear at him to strike him, and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ knew that it was determined on the part of his father to put David to death.

[34] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ arose from the table in burning anger, and he did not eat bread on the second day of the new moon, for he was grieved for David, for his father had put him to shame.

[35] And it came to pass in the morning that ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ went out to the field for the appointed time with David, and a small boy was with him.

[36] And he said to his boy, "Run, find, please, the arrows that I am shooting." The boy ran, and he shot the arrow to pass beyond him.

[37] And the boy came as far as the place of the arrow that ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ had shot, and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ called after the boy and said, "Is not the arrow beyond you?"

[38] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ called after the boy, "Quickly, hasten, do not stand!" And the boy of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ gathered the arrows and came to his lord.

[39] And the boy did not know anything; only ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ and David knew the matter.

[40] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ gave his gear to the boy that was his and said to him, "Go, bring it to the city."

[41] The boy went, and David arose from beside the south, and he fell on his face to the earth and bowed down three times. And they kissed, each man his neighbor, and they wept, each man with his neighbor, until David did greatly.

[42] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said to David, "Go in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, inasmuch as we have sworn, the two of us, we, in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ shall be between me and you, and between my seed and your seed, forever.'" And he arose and went, and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ came into the city.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"what is mine iniquity"** (v1, 8) — Hebrew עָוֹן. **"that he seeketh my life" → "that he is seeking my soul"** — Hebrew נַפְשִׁי. "From Naioth" is the reading tradition (qere), as 19:18.
- **"God forbid" → "Far be it"** (v2, 9) — Hebrew חָלִילָה; the Hebrew has no "God". **"my father will do nothing … but that he will shew it me" → "my father does not do … and not uncover my ear"** (v2, 12, 13) — Hebrew גָּלָה אֶת־אֹזֶן "uncover the ear": tell, let know. "Does not do" is the reading tradition (qere); the written text has "if he had done". **"it is not so" → "This is not so"** — Hebrew אֵין זֹאת, literally "this is not".
- **"David sware moreover" → "David swore again"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּשָּׁבַע עוֹד; the Septuagint has "answered" (from memory). **"certainly knoweth" → "surely knows"** — Hebrew יָדֹעַ יָדַע, the verb with its infinitive ("knowing he knows"); so also "surely sit" (v5), "surely misses", "surely asked leave" (v6, 28), "surely burns" (v7), "surely know" (v9), "surely say" (v21).
- **"as the LORD liveth, and as thy soul liveth" → "as Yahweh lives and by the life of your soul"** (v3) — Hebrew חַי־יְהוָה וְחֵי נַפְשֶׁךָ: two forms, חַי "living" of Yahweh and חֵי "the life of" of a man. **"there is but a step"** — Hebrew כְפֶשַׂע, "as it were a step".
- **"Whatsoever thy soul desireth" → "What your soul says"** (v4) — Hebrew מַה־תֹּאמַר נַפְשְׁךָ; "says", not "desires". It may also be a question: "What does your soul say?"
- **"new moon" kept** (v5, 18, 24, 27, 34) — Hebrew חֹדֶשׁ, "new moon" and "month" (the word list has "month"); here the day of the new moon is meant ("tomorrow is the month" would say something else). In v27, 34 "the second day of the new moon" is the second day of the month. **"I should not fail to sit with the king at meat" → "I should surely sit with the king to eat"** — the Hebrew has no negative. **"hide myself" → "conceal myself"** (v5, 19, 24) — Hebrew נִסְתַּר; "hide" in v2 is the causative of the same root. **"unto the third day at even" → "until the third evening"** — Hebrew עַד הָעֶרֶב הַשְּׁלִשִׁית; the form of "third" does not agree with "evening", and the words are difficult.
- **"If thy father at all miss me" → "surely misses me"** (v6, 18, 25, 27) — Hebrew פָּקַד, "visit, attend to, look for": here to look for one who is absent, and in the passive "be missed". **"a yearly sacrifice" → "the sacrifice of the days"** — Hebrew זֶבַח הַיָּמִים (1:21).
- **"thy servant shall have peace" → "there is shalom for your servant"** (v7, 13, 21, 42) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם. **"if he be very wroth" → "if he surely burns with anger"** — Hebrew חָרֹה יֶחֱרֶה לוֹ. **"evil is determined by him" → "the evil is determined on his part"** (v7, 9, 33) — Hebrew כָלְתָה הָרָעָה מֵעִמּוֹ, literally "the evil is finished from with him": settled.
- **"thou shalt deal kindly with thy servant" → "you shall do chesed to your servant"** (v8, 14, 15) — Hebrew חֶסֶד: faithful love within a bond; here with עַל "upon". **"slay me thyself" → "put me to death yourself"**.
- **"then would not I tell it thee?" → "would I not tell it to you?"** (v9) — Hebrew וְלֹא אֹתָהּ אַגִּיד לָךְ, "and not it shall I tell you", taken as a question.
- **"Who shall tell me? or what if thy father answer thee roughly?" → "Who will tell me, or what hard thing your father answers you?"** (v10) — Hebrew מִי יַגִּיד לִי אוֹ מַה־יַּעַנְךָ אָבִיךָ קָשָׁה, literally "who will tell me, or what your father answers you, a hard thing". The sentence is difficult.
- **"O LORD God of Israel, when I have sounded my father" → "Yahweh, the Elohim of Israel! When I examine my father"** (v12) — the Masoretic Text has the name alone, as a call or an oath; the old versions add "is witness" (from memory), and it is not put into the verse. **"about to morrow any time, or the third day" → "about this time tomorrow, the third day"** — Hebrew כָּעֵת מָחָר הַשְּׁלִשִׁית, literally "about the time tomorrow the third"; difficult.
- **"The LORD do so and much more to Jonathan" → "Thus may Yahweh do to Jonathan, and thus may he add"** (v13) — Hebrew כֹּה־יַעֲשֶׂה יְהוָה … וְכֹה יֹסִיף, the oath that calls down harm on the swearer. **"but if it please my father to do thee evil" → "if the evil upon you is good to my father"** — Hebrew כִּי־יֵיטִב אֶל־אָבִי אֶת־הָרָעָה עָלֶיךָ, as it stands; "to bring" is commonly supplied. **"as he hath been with my father" → "as he was with my father"**.
- **"And thou shalt not only while yet I live shew me the kindness of the LORD, that I die not" → "And will you not, if I am still alive, will you not do with me the chesed of Yahweh, and I shall not die?"** (v14) — Hebrew וְלֹא אִם־עוֹדֶנִּי חָי וְלֹא־תַעֲשֶׂה עִמָּדִי חֶסֶד יְהוָה וְלֹא אָמוּת, with "and not" three times. The verse is very difficult; it is taken here as a question, word for word. Many read the first two "and not" as "and would that" (the same letters, other vowels: a conjecture; so also some versions, from memory).
- **"thou shalt not cut off thy kindness from my house for ever: no, not when the LORD hath cut off the enemies of David every one from the face of the earth" → "… each man, from upon the face of the ground"** (v15) — Hebrew אִישׁ "each man"; אֲדָמָה "ground" (so also v31).
- **"So Jonathan made a covenant with the house of David, saying, Let the LORD even require it" → "And Jonathan cut a covenant with the house of David: 'And Yahweh shall require it'"** (v16) — the Hebrew has only וַיִּכְרֹת "and he cut": "a covenant" is supplied, as English needs it (11:2; 22:8). "Saying" is not in the Hebrew; וּבִקֵּשׁ is "and he will seek / require". The verse is difficult and the versions differ.
- **"Jonathan caused David to swear again, because he loved him: for he loved him as he loved his own soul" → "… for with the love of his own soul he loved him"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי־אַהֲבַת נַפְשׁוֹ אֲהֵבוֹ, literally "for the love of his soul he loved him". The Septuagint has "Jonathan swore again to David" (from memory).
- **"thy seat will be empty" → "your seat will be missed"** (v18, 25, 27) — the same verb as "you will be missed". מוֹשָׁב is the place where one sits.
- **"when thou hast stayed three days, then thou shalt go down quickly" → "on the third day you shall go far down"** (v19) — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּשְׁתָּ תֵּרֵד מְאֹד, literally "and you shall do a third, you shall go down greatly". The words are difficult and the versions differ. **"when the business was in hand" → "on the day of the deed"** — Hebrew בְּיוֹם הַמַּעֲשֶׂה; which deed is not said. **"remain by the stone Ezel" → "sit beside the stone Ezel"** — Hebrew הָאֶבֶן הָאָזֶל; the Septuagint has "beside that mound" (from memory).
- **"as though I shot at a mark" → "sending them for myself at a mark"** (v20) — Hebrew לְשַׁלַּח־לִי לְמַטָּרָה. "The three arrows" has the article.
- **"lad" → "boy" / "lad"** (v21, 22, 35–41) — Hebrew נַעַר "boy", an attendant; in v22 the word is עֶלֶם, a grown youth. **"take them; then come thou" → "take it,' then come"** (v21) — Hebrew קָחֶנּוּ, "take it" (singular); וָבֹאָה "and come" is said to David. **"no hurt" → "there is nothing"** — Hebrew וְאֵין דָּבָר, "and there is no matter".
- **"the LORD hath sent thee away"** (v22) — Hebrew שִׁלַּחֲךָ, the verb of "let me go" (v5, 13, 29).
- **"the LORD be between thee and me" → "Yahweh is between me and you"** (v23) — the Hebrew has no verb; the Septuagint adds "witness" (from memory).
- **"sat him down to eat meat" → "sat down to the bread to eat"** (v24, 27, 34) — Hebrew לֶחֶם "bread", for the meal.
- **"as at other times" → "as time upon time"; "Jonathan arose"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיָּקָם, so the Masoretic Text; the Septuagint has "went before" (from memory). **"David's place was empty" → "the place of David was missed"**.
- **"Something hath befallen him" → "It is a chance"** (v26) — Hebrew מִקְרֶה, a happening, a chance thing (6:9; Deut 23:10): something that has made him unclean. The clause "he is not clean" stands twice, with two different words for "not".
- **"on the morrow, which was the second day of the month" → "on the morrow of the new moon, the second day"** (v27) — Hebrew מִמָּחֳרַת הַחֹדֶשׁ הַשֵּׁנִי.
- **"asked leave of me to go to Bethlehem" → "asked leave of me as far as Bethlehem"** (v28) — the Hebrew has no verb of going.
- **"my brother, he hath commanded me to be there" → "he, my brother, has commanded me"** (v29) — "to be there" is not in the Hebrew. **"let me get away" → "let me escape"** — Hebrew אִמָּלְטָה, the verb of 19:10–18.
- **"Thou son of the perverse rebellious woman" → "Son of a perverse woman of rebellion!"** (v30) — Hebrew בֶּן־נַעֲוַת הַמַּרְדּוּת, literally "son of a perverse one of the rebellion": נַעֲוַת is a feminine form of עָוָה "be bent, twisted" ("woman" is in the form, not a second word), מַרְדּוּת is "rebellion" and stands only here. The insult strikes at Jonathan through his mother. The Septuagint has "son of deserting girls" (from memory). **"thou hast chosen" → "you are choosing"** — Hebrew בֹחֵר, a participle. **"unto the confusion of thy mother's nakedness" → "to the shame of the nakedness of your mother"** — Hebrew לְבֹשֶׁת עֶרְוַת אִמֶּךָ, the plain words.
- **"he shall surely die" → "he is a son of death"** (v31) — Hebrew בֶן־מָוֶת, one who belongs to death. **"kingdom"** — Hebrew מַלְכוּת. "Take him" is the Hebrew וְקַח.
- **"cast a javelin at him to smite him" → "hurled the spear at him to strike him"** (v33) — as 18:11.
- **"in fierce anger" → "in burning anger"** (v34) — Hebrew בָּחֳרִי־אָף, literally "in the burning of the nose". **"because his father had done him shame" → "for his father had put him to shame"** — Hebrew הִכְלִמוֹ; "him" may be David or Jonathan.
- **"at the time appointed with David" → "for the appointed time with David"** (v35) — Hebrew לְמוֹעֵד דָּוִד, "for the appointed time (or place) of David". **"a little lad" → "a small boy"**.
- **"he shot an arrow beyond him" → "he shot the arrow to pass beyond him"** (v36, 37) — Hebrew לְהַעֲבִרוֹ. In v38 "the arrows" is the reading tradition; the written text has "the arrow".
- **"Make speed, haste, stay not" → "Quickly, hasten, do not stand!"** (v38). **"his master" → "his lord"** — Hebrew אֲדֹנָיו.
- **"his artillery" → "his gear"** (v40) — Hebrew כֵּלָיו, as 17:54: his bow and arrows.
- **"David arose out of a place toward the south" → "David arose from beside the south"** (v41) — Hebrew מֵאֵצֶל הַנֶּגֶב, as it stands; the words are difficult. The Septuagint has "from the mound" (from memory; cf. v19). **"fell on his face to the ground"** — literally "to his nostrils, to the earth".
- **"they kissed one another, and wept one with another, until David exceeded" → "they kissed, each man his neighbor, and they wept, each man with his neighbor, until David did greatly"** (v41) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶת־רֵעֵהוּ twice; רֵעַ is "neighbor, friend, fellow". עַד־דָּוִד הִגְדִּיל is literally "until David made great": he wept the more, or beyond measure. The words are difficult.
- **"forasmuch as we have sworn both of us" → "inasmuch as we have sworn, the two of us, we"** (v42) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁבַּעְנוּ שְׁנֵינוּ אֲנַחְנוּ; אֲשֶׁר is "that which": also "as for what we have sworn". **"between my seed and thy seed"** — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **Verse numbers** — the last sentence of v42 ("And he arose and went …") is 21:1 in the Hebrew numbering.

### Notes
- **Jonathan's oath** (v14–17) — covenantal friendship with David includes an oath binding David to preserve Jonathan's line. Later fulfilled (2 Sam 9): David honors Mephibosheth.
- **New Moon feast** — monthly festival (Num 10:10, 28:11). David's absence requires explanation.
- **Saul's ritual cleanness thought** (v26) — assumes David may have contracted temporary ritual uncleanness. Day two forces the question.
- **Saul's spear at Jonathan** (v33) — now the king threatens his own son. Family destruction advances with kingdom disintegration.
- **Arrow signal** (v19–22, 35–39) — elaborate secret communication. Shows risk level: open communication impossible.
- **David's weeping more** (v41) — greater grief from David. His kingdom-promise is being paid in friendship-loss and homelessness.
