## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 17) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the child Samuel ministered unto the LORD before Eli" → "the boy Samuel was ministering to Yahweh before Eli"** (v1) — Hebrew נַעַר "boy" (v1, 8); here לִפְנֵי "before", where 2:11 has "before the face of".
- **"the word of the LORD was precious" → "the word of Yahweh was rare"** (v1) — Hebrew יָקָר *yaqar*, "precious, costly", and so "scarce". **"there was no open vision" → "there was no vision spread abroad"** — Hebrew אֵין חָזוֹן נִפְרָץ: נִפְרָץ is of פָּרַץ "break out, spread abroad"; the form stands only here. No vision was breaking through to the people.
- **"at that time" → "on that day"** (v2) — Hebrew בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא. The sentence that begins with "And it came to pass" runs through the circumstances of v2–3 to "And Yahweh called" (v4); the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"his eyes began to wax dim, that he could not see" → "his eyes had begun to grow dim; he was not able to see"** (v2) — Hebrew כֵהוֹת "dim"; the qere "his eyes" is translated (the written text has "his eye"). The same root returns in v13.
- **"ere the lamp of God went out" → "the lamp of Elohim had not yet gone out"** (v3) — it was still night: the lamp burned from evening to morning (Exod 27:21). **"was laid down to sleep" → "was lying"** — Hebrew שֹׁכֵב, as of Eli in v2; the Hebrew has no word "sleep". The accents set a pause after "was lying": "Samuel was lying — in the temple of Yahweh, where the ark of Elohim was".
- **"Here am I" → "Here I am"** (v4, 5, 6, 8, 16) — Hebrew הִנֵּנִי, literally "behold me".
- **"lie down again" → "go back, lie down"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew שׁוּב שְׁכָב, two commands: "return, lie down".
- **"the LORD called yet again, Samuel" → "Yahweh called yet again, 'Samuel!'"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף יְהוָה קְרֹא עוֹד שְׁמוּאֵל, literally "and Yahweh added to call again: Samuel"; so also v8 ("added to call Samuel, the third time") and v21 ("added to appear").
- **"neither was the word of the LORD yet revealed unto him" → "the word of Yahweh was not yet revealed to him"** (v7) — Hebrew יִגָּלֶה, "be uncovered".
- **"Eli perceived" → "Eli understood"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיָּבֶן.
- **"thy servant heareth" → "your servant is hearing"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵעַ, a participle; עַבְדֶּךָ is literally "your slave". In v10 Samuel does not say the name "Yahweh" that Eli gave him to say.
- **"came, and stood" → "came and took his stand"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיִּתְיַצַּב. **"as at other times" → "as at the other times"** — Hebrew כְפַעַם־בְּפַעַם, literally "as time upon time".
- **"I will do a thing" → "I am doing a thing"** (v11) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה, a participle: what is at hand. דָּבָר is "word" and "thing"; "thing" here, "word" in v1, 7, 17, 21.
- **"I will perform against Eli all things which I have spoken concerning his house: when I begin, I will also make an end" → "I will establish against Eli all that I have spoken about his house, beginning and finishing"** (v12) — Hebrew אָקִים "make stand" (as 1:23); אֶל is "to" both times; הָחֵל וְכַלֵּה, two verbal nouns, "beginning and finishing".
- **"For I have told him that I will judge his house" → "And I have told him that I am judging his house"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהִגַּדְתִּי; שֹׁפֵט is a participle.
- **"because his sons made themselves vile, and he restrained them not" → "that his sons were bringing a curse on themselves, and he did not rebuke them"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי־מְקַלְלִים לָהֶם בָּנָיו, literally "that his sons were cursing for themselves". The Masoretic Text is translated as it stands; the wording is unusual. Jewish tradition counts the place among the "corrections of the scribes": the text is said to have once read אֱלֹהִים "Elohim" for לָהֶם "for themselves" — "his sons were cursing Elohim" — and so the Septuagint has it (cited from memory). **"rebuke"** — Hebrew כִהָה, a form of the verb "grow dim" (v2); in this sense it stands only here, and the meaning ("rebuke", "restrain") is not certain.
- **"I have sworn … that the iniquity of Eli's house shall not be purged" → "I have sworn …: the iniquity of the house of Eli shall not be atoned for"** (v14) — the Hebrew is the oath formula, literally "if the iniquity of the house of Eli shall be atoned for …": a sworn "it shall not". **"with sacrifice nor offering" → "by sacrifice or by offering"** — Hebrew בְּזֶבַח וּבְמִנְחָה, the pair of 2:29.
- **"feared to shew Eli the vision" → "feared to tell the vision to Eli"** (v15) — Hebrew הַמַּרְאָה, "what was seen", another word than חָזוֹן (v1).
- **"What is the thing that the LORD hath said unto thee?" → "What is the word that he spoke to you?"** (v17) — the Hebrew does not name Yahweh. **"I pray thee hide it not" → "Do not, please, hide it"** — Hebrew אַל־נָא.
- **"God do so to thee, and more also" → "Thus may Elohim do to you, and thus may he add"** (v17) — Hebrew כֹּה יַעֲשֶׂה־לְּךָ אֱלֹהִים וְכֹה יוֹסִיף, the oath formula word for word; what "thus" is, is not said.
- **"told him every whit, and hid nothing from him" → "told him all the words and did not hide from him"** (v18).
- **"It is the LORD: let him do what seemeth him good" → "He is Yahweh; what is good in his eyes let him do"** (v18) — Hebrew יְהוָה הוּא, "Yahweh (is) he".
- **"did let none of his words fall to the ground" → "he let none of all his words fall to the ground"** (v19) — Hebrew וְלֹא־הִפִּיל, "he did not make fall". "He" and "his" are not named: Yahweh let none of Samuel's words (or of his own words) fail.
- **"from Dan even to Beer-sheba" → "from Dan and as far as Beersheba"** (v20).
- **"was established to be a prophet of the LORD" → "was confirmed as a prophet to Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew נֶאֱמָן, the word of 2:35 ("a faithful priest", "a sure house"): found firm, to be relied on. לְנָבִיא לַיהוָה is "for a prophet to / for Yahweh".
- **"the LORD appeared again in Shiloh" → "Yahweh appeared again in Shiloh"** (v21) — Hebrew לְהֵרָאֹה, "to be seen"; then נִגְלָה "revealed himself" (uncovered himself), as in v7.

### Notes
- **Samuel's call** — classical vocation narrative, a pattern for later prophets.
- **Revelation scarce** (v1) — prophetic silence in Israel. Samuel's emergence ends it.
- **Eli accepts the oracle** (v18) — he does not resist or try to bargain. "He is Yahweh."
- **"None of all his words fall to the ground"** (v19) — Hebrew idiom: what is spoken comes true.
- **Shiloh** (v21) — Samuel is known as a prophet from Dan to Beersheba.
- **Bridge into ch 4** — Samuel's prophetic authority set; ch 4 narrates Eli's predicted judgment.
