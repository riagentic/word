# Zechariah 8

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts came, saying:

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: I am jealous for ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ with great jealousy, and with great fury I am jealous for her.

[3] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: I have returned to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, and I will abide in the midst of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall be called the City of Truth, and the mountain of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts the Holy Mountain.

[4] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Again old men and old women shall sit in the squares of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and each man his staff in his hand because of the multitude of days.

[5] And the squares of the city shall be filled with boys and girls playing in its squares.

[6] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: If it is too wonderful in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, also in my eyes shall it be too wonderful? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[7] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Behold, I am saving my people from the land of the east and from the land of the setting of the sun;

[8] and I will bring them, and they shall abide in the midst of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and they shall be to me a people, and I will be to them ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in truth and in righteousness.

[9] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Let your hands be strong, you who are hearing in these days these words from the mouth of the prophets, who were on the day that the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts was founded, that the temple might be built.

[10] For before those days there was no wage for man, and a wage for beast there was none; and for him who went out and for him who came in there was no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ because of the adversary; and I let go all mankind, each man against his neighbor.

[11] And now, not as in the former days am I to the remnant of this people, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[12] For the seed of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧: the vine shall give its fruit, and the land shall give its produce, and the heavens shall give their dew; and I will make the remnant of this people inherit all these.

[13] And it shall be: as you were a curse among the nations, house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, so I will save you, and you shall be a blessing. Do not fear; let your hands be strong.

[14] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: As I devised to do evil to you when your fathers provoked me to wrath, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and I did not relent,

[15] so I have turned back, I have devised in these days to do good to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and to the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. Do not fear.

[16] These are the things that you shall do: Speak truth, each man with his neighbor; truth and the judgment of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ judge in your gates;

[17] and each man the evil of his neighbor, do not think it in your heart; and an oath of falsehood do not love; for all these are what I hate, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts came to me, saying:

[19] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: The fast of the fourth and the fast of the fifth and the fast of the seventh and the fast of the tenth shall be to the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ for joy and for gladness and for good appointed times; and love the truth and the ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[20] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: It shall yet be that peoples shall come, and the inhabitants of many cities;

[21] and the inhabitants of one shall go to another, saying: Let us surely go to beseech the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts; I will go, I also.

[22] And many peoples and mighty nations shall come to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and to beseech the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[23] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: In those days it shall be that **ten men from all the tongues of the nations shall take hold, and they shall take hold of the corner of a man, a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, saying: Let us go with you, for we have heard: ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is with you.**

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, plain "of Hosts" as the earlier books.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8, 23) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** — prose, as the Hebrew; the quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where the speeches begin and end.
- **"Again the word of the LORD of hosts came to me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh of Hosts came, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְּבַר־יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת לֵאמֹר: the verse has no "to me" (v18 has אֵלַי); וַיְהִי "And … came" (lit. "and it was").
- **"I was jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and I was jealous for her with great fury" → "I am jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and with great fury I am jealous for her"** (v2) — Hebrew קִנֵּאתִי (perfect of a state now held, as 1:14); the second half puts "great fury" first; חֵמָה "fury" (its row).
- **"I am returned … and will dwell … a city of truth … the holy mountain" → "I have returned … and I will abide … the City of Truth … the Holy Mountain"** (v3) — Hebrew שַׁבְתִּי perfect; שָׁכַן "abide" (its row, beside יָשַׁב "dwell"; also v8); עִיר־הָאֱמֶת "the city of the truth" (אֱמֶת "truth", its row; "Faithful City" would be אֱמוּנָה); הַר הַקֹּדֶשׁ "the mountain of holiness" (קֹדֶשׁ as a genitive "holy", its row).
- **"in the streets … every man with his staff in his hand for very age" → "in the squares … each man his staff in his hand because of the multitude of days"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew רְחֹבוֹת "squares" (its row; not "streets"); אִישׁ מִשְׁעַנְתּוֹ בְּיָדוֹ, no "with"; מִשְׁעֶנֶת "staff (to lean on)"; מֵרֹב יָמִים "from the multitude of days".
- **"boys and girls playing" → "boys and girls playing"** (v5) — Hebrew יְלָדִים וִילָדוֹת, the masculine and the feminine of יֶלֶד "child" (its row): English has no feminine of "child"; מְשַׂחֲקִים "playing".
- **"If it be marvellous in the eyes of the remnant … should it also be marvellous in mine eyes?" → "If it is too wonderful … also in my eyes shall it be too wonderful?"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי יִפָּלֵא בְּעֵינֵי … גַּם־בְּעֵינַי יִפָּלֵא: נִפְלָא "be too wonderful" (as Gen 18:14, said of what is too hard for someone); the second half has no question word: a question by tone, or a statement ("it shall be wonderful in my eyes also"): open.
- **"I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country" → "I am saving my people from the land of the east and from the land of the setting of the sun"** (v7) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מוֹשִׁיעַ, participle ("I am about to save"); מִזְרָח "east" (lit. "rising"); מְבוֹא הַשָּׁמֶשׁ, lit. "the going in of the sun".
- **"and they shall dwell … and they shall be my people, and I will be their God" → "and they shall abide … and they shall be to me a people, and I will be to them Elohim"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ־לִי לְעָם וַאֲנִי אֶהְיֶה לָהֶם לֵאלֹהִים (the covenant formula, as Ezek 14:11); בֶּאֱמֶת "in truth".
- **"ye that hear in these days these words by the mouth of the prophets, which were in the day that the foundation of the house … was laid, that the temple might be built" → "you who are hearing … who were on the day that the house … was founded, that the temple might be built"** (v9) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁמְעִים participle; אֲשֶׁר בְּיוֹם יֻסַּד, "who (were) on the day it was founded" ("were" supplied, as KJV); הַהֵיכָל לְהִבָּנוֹת, lit. "the temple, to be built" (הֵיכָל "temple", its row; Hag 1–2, Zech 4:9).
- **"there was no hire for man, nor any hire for beast; neither was there any peace to him that went out or came in because of the affliction: for I set all men every one against his neighbour" → "there was no wage for man, and a wage for beast there was none; … no shalom because of the adversary; and I let go all mankind, each man against his neighbor"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׂכָר "wage" (its row), אֵינֶנָּה "there was none" (the second clause has its own form); שָׁלוֹם token (reviewer row); הַצָּר "the adversary" (צַר, its row; KJV "affliction" reads צָרָה); וַאֲשַׁלַּח piel of שָׁלַח, "let go" (the שִׁלַּח row; with בְּ "against"); כָּל־הָאָדָם "all mankind" (אָדָם, the standing decision).
- **"But now I will not be unto the residue of this people as in the former days" → "And now, not as in the former days am I to the remnant of this people"** (v11) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה לֹא כַיָּמִים הָרִאשֹׁנִים אֲנִי, a verbless clause in the Hebrew order; שְׁאֵרִית "remnant" (its row; also v6, 12).
- **"For the seed shall be prosperous; the vine shall give her fruit, and the ground shall give her increase … and I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these things" → "For the seed of shalom: the vine shall give its fruit, and the land shall give its produce … and I will make the remnant of this people inherit all these"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי־זֶרַע הַשָּׁלוֹם הַגֶּפֶן: by the accents "the seed of the shalom" stands apart, and the vine is what that seed is ("the seed of peace, the vine, shall give its fruit"); KJV "shall be prosperous" supplies a verb; הָאָרֶץ "the land" (אֶרֶץ, not אֲדָמָה "ground"); יְבוּל "produce" (its row); הִנְחַלְתִּי "make inherit" (the נָחַל rows; not "possess", which is יָרַשׁ); "things" not in the Hebrew.
- **"as ye were a curse among the heathen … fear not, but let your hands be strong" → "And it shall be: as you were a curse among the nations … Do not fear; let your hands be strong"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהָיָה kept; קְלָלָה "curse" (its row; "byword" not in the Hebrew); no "O" before "house of Judah" (vocative row); no "but" in the Hebrew.
- **"As I thought to punish you … and I repented not" → "As I devised to do evil to you … and I did not relent"** (v14) — Hebrew זָמַמְתִּי "devise" (its row; also v15); לְהָרַע לָכֶם "do evil to" (the הֵרַע לְ row); בְּהַקְצִיף "when (they) provoked to wrath" (קֶצֶף "wrath"); וְלֹא נִחָמְתִּי "and I did not relent" (the נִחַם row: a planned act given up); the order of the Hebrew kept ("says Yahweh of Hosts" before "and I did not relent").
- **"So again have I thought in these days to do well unto Jerusalem" → "so I have turned back, I have devised in these days to do good to Jerusalem"** (v15) — Hebrew שַׁבְתִּי זָמַמְתִּי, two perfects: שׁוּב + a second verb = "I have again devised" (the שׁוּב row: kept, the sense said here); לְהֵיטִיב אֶת "to do good to" (its row).
- **"Speak ye every man the truth to his neighbour; execute the judgment of truth and peace in your gates" → "Speak truth, each man with his neighbor; truth and the judgment of shalom judge in your gates"** (v16) — Hebrew דַּבְּרוּ אֱמֶת אִישׁ אֶת־רֵעֵהוּ (the אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ row; אֶת "with"); אֱמֶת וּמִשְׁפַּט שָׁלוֹם שִׁפְטוּ, the objects first, "judge" the verb of מִשְׁפָּט "judgment".
- **"And let none of you imagine evil in your hearts against his neighbour; and love no false oath" → "and each man the evil of his neighbor, do not think it in your heart; and an oath of falsehood do not love"** (v17) — Hebrew וְאִישׁ אֶת־רָעַת רֵעֵהוּ אַל־תַּחְשְׁבוּ בִּלְבַבְכֶם, the object first; חָשַׁב of the mind "think" (its row; kept apart from זָמַם "devise", v14–15); "it" supplied for English; שְׁבֻעַת שֶׁקֶר "an oath of falsehood" (שֶׁקֶר, its row); כִּי אֶת־כָּל־אֵלֶּה אֲשֶׁר שָׂנֵאתִי "for all these are what I hate" ("are" supplied).
- **"The fast of the fourth month … cheerful feasts; therefore love the truth and peace" → "The fast of the fourth … for joy and for gladness and for good appointed times; and love the truth and the shalom"** (v19) — the Hebrew has no "month" with the ordinals: not supplied (the fasts of the fourth, fifth, seventh and tenth months: 7:3, 5; Jer 39:2; 52:6–13; 41:1–2; 2 Kgs 25:1); לְשָׂשׂוֹן וּלְשִׂמְחָה "for joy and for gladness" (their rows); מוֹעֲדִים "appointed times" (its row; the set feasts are meant); וְהָאֱמֶת וְהַשָּׁלוֹם אֱהָבוּ "and the truth and the shalom love" (objects first, with the article; "therefore" is not in the Hebrew).
- **"It shall yet come to pass, that there shall come people, and the inhabitants of many cities" → "It shall yet be that peoples shall come, and the inhabitants of many cities"** (v20) — Hebrew עֹד אֲשֶׁר יָבֹאוּ עַמִּים, "yet (it is) that peoples shall come"; עַמִּים plural.
- **"the inhabitants of one city shall go to another, saying, Let us go speedily to pray before the LORD … I will go also" → "the inhabitants of one shall go to another, saying: Let us surely go to beseech the face of Yahweh … I will go, I also"** (v21) — Hebrew יֹשְבֵי אַחַת אֶל־אַחַת, "of one to one" (feminine: the cities of v20; "city" not written); נֵלְכָה הָלוֹךְ, infinitive absolute: "let us surely go" (lit. "let us go, going"); לְחַלּוֹת אֶת־פְּנֵי יְהוָה "to beseech the face of Yahweh" (its row, as 7:2; also v22); בִּקֵּשׁ "seek" (its row); אֵלְכָה גַּם־אָנִי "let me go, I also" (a cohortative).
- **"strong nations" → "mighty nations"** (v22) — Hebrew עֲצוּמִים "mighty" (its row).
- **"In those days it shall come to pass, that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew" → "In those days it shall be that ten men from all the tongues of the nations shall take hold, and they shall take hold of the corner of a man, a Jew"** (v23) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יַחֲזִיקוּ … וְהֶחֱזִיקוּ, the verb twice; לְשֹׁנוֹת "tongues" (לָשׁוֹן, its row); בִּכְנַף "the corner" (כָּנָף of a garment, its row; lit. "wing"); אִישׁ יְהוּדִי "a man, a Jew"; כִּי שָׁמַעְנוּ אֱלֹהִים עִמָּכֶם, no "that" in the Hebrew: what was heard is given as said.
