# Zechariah 1

[1] In the eighth month, in the second year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ son of ⟦berechiah|Berechyah|Berechiah⟧, son of Iddo, the prophet, saying,

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ was wrathful against your fathers with wrath.

[3] And you shall say to them, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: **Return to me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and I will return to you**, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[4] Do not be like your fathers, to whom the former prophets called out, saying, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Return, please, from your evil ways and your evil doings. And they did not hear, and they did not give heed to me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] Your fathers, where are they? And the prophets, do they live forever?

[6] But my words and my statutes, which I commanded my servants the prophets, did they not overtake your fathers? And they returned and said, As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts devised to do to us, according to our ways and according to our doings, so he has done with us.

[7] On the twenty-fourth day of the eleventh month, that is the month of ⟦shevat|Shevat|Shebat⟧, in the second year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ son of ⟦berechiah|Berechyah|Berechiah⟧, son of Iddo, the prophet, saying,

[8] I saw in the night, and behold, a man riding on a red horse, and he was standing among the myrtles that were in the glen; and behind him were horses, red, sorrel, and white.

[9] And I said, "What are these, my lord?" And the angel who spoke with me said to me, "I will show you what these are."

[10] And the man who was standing among the myrtles answered and said, "These are those whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has sent to walk about in the earth."

[11] And they answered the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who was standing among the myrtles, and said, "We have walked about in the earth, and behold, all the earth is sitting and is quiet."

[12] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered and said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts, how long will you not have compassion on ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, against which you have been indignant these seventy years?"

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered the angel who spoke with me good words, comforting words.

[14] And the angel who spoke with me said to me, "Call out, saying, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: I have been jealous for ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and for ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ with a great jealousy.

[15] And with great wrath I am wrathful against the nations that are at ease; for I was wrathful a little, and they helped toward evil.

[16] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: I have returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with compassion; my house shall be built in it, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and a line shall be stretched out over ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[17] Again call out, saying, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: My cities shall again overflow with good, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall again comfort ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and shall again choose ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

[18] And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and behold, four horns.

[19] And I said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these?" And he said to me, "These are the horns that have strewn ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

[20] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ showed me four craftsmen.

[21] And I said, "What are these coming to do?" And he spoke, saying, "These are the horns that have strewn ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, so that no man lifted up his head; and these have come to make them tremble, to cast down the horns of the nations who lift up a horn against the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to strew it."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v3, 4, 6, 12, 14, 16, 17) — יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, the prophets' formula row. נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v3, 4, 16); כֹּה אָמַר "Thus says" (v3, 4, 14, 16, 17), אָמַר alone at the end of v3 "says".
- **Layout and quotation marks** — prose, as the Hebrew. The outer quotation marks of the earlier text (around the whole oracle and the whole vision report) are removed: the Hebrew has none; double quotation marks stand only for words spoken inside the vision (v9–21), and what a prophet is told to proclaim follows "saying" without further marks.
- **"came the word of the LORD unto Zechariah, the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet" → "the word of Yahweh came to Zechariah son of Berechiah, son of Iddo, the prophet"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew הָיָה דְבַר־יְהוָה אֶל־זְכַרְיָה בֶּן־בֶּרֶכְיָה בֶּן־עִדּוֹ הַנָּבִיא לֵאמֹר, a perfect (not the וַיְהִי of the formula row): "the word … came". "The prophet" stands last, as in the Hebrew; whether it names Zechariah or Iddo is not marked (Ezra 5:1; 6:14 call Zechariah "son of Iddo"). v7 writes the names בֶּרֶכְיָהוּ and עִדּוֹא, longer spellings of the same names. "In the second year" is literally "in year two" (also v7).
- **"The LORD hath been sore displeased with your fathers" → "Yahweh was wrathful against your fathers with wrath"** (v2) — קָצַף … קָצֶף, the verb with its own noun (the קָצַף · קֶצֶף row "be wrathful · wrath"; also v15 three times); "sore" is the earlier text's way of giving the doubled root.
- **"Therefore say thou unto them" → "And you shall say to them"** (v3) — וְאָמַרְתָּ, a weqatal. **"Turn ye unto me … and I will turn unto you" → "Return to me … and I will return to you"** — שׁוּב both times (the שׁוּב row; also v4, 6 "return", v16 "I have returned"). **"saith" → "declares … says"** — נְאֻם in the middle, אָמַר at the end: two words.
- **"Turn ye now from your evil ways, and from your evil doings" → "Return, please, from your evil ways and your evil doings"** (v4) — שׁוּבוּ נָא, נָא "please" (as Jer 25:5; 35:15); the written text (ketiv) ומעליליכם and the reading (qere) וּמַעַלְלֵיכֶם differ only in spelling, "doings" (also v6). **"cried" → "called out, saying"** — קָרְאוּ … לֵאמֹר (קָרָא "call", also v14, 17). **"but they did not hear, nor hearken unto me" → "And they did not hear, and they did not give heed to me"** — two clauses, each וְלֹא; הִקְשִׁיב "give heed" (its row).
- **"Your fathers, where are they?" kept** (v5) — the Hebrew order.
- **"But my words and my statutes … did they not take hold of your fathers?" → "… did they not overtake your fathers?"** (v6) — אַךְ "but"; הִשִּׂיג "overtake". **"servants"** — עֲבָדַי (the עֶבֶד row: "servant" of Yahweh's men). **"and they returned" kept** — וַיָּשׁוּבוּ, the שׁוּב of v3–4 (they turned back to Yahweh: "repented" in other versions). **"Like as the LORD of hosts thought to do unto us … so hath he dealt with us" → "As Yahweh of Hosts devised to do to us, according to our ways and according to our doings, so he has done with us"** — זָמַם "devise" (its row, which names this verse); כִּ "according to" twice; עָשָׂה twice, "do … done".
- **"Sebat" → "Shevat"** (v7) — שְׁבָט, the month name token. **"which is the month" → "that is the month"** — הוּא חֹדֶשׁ.
- **"I saw by night, and behold a man riding upon a red horse, and he stood among the myrtle trees that were in the bottom" → "I saw in the night, and behold, a man riding on a red horse, and he was standing among the myrtles that were in the glen"** (v8) — רָאִיתִי הַלַּיְלָה, literally "I saw the night" (= this night); עֹמֵד a participle "was standing" (also v10, 11); הֲדַסִּים "myrtles"; מְצֻלָה, elsewhere "the depth" (of the sea), here a hollow or glen: the sense is uncertain. **"red horses, speckled, and white" → "horses, red, sorrel, and white"** — סוּסִים אֲדֻמִּים שְׂרֻקִּים וּלְבָנִים; שָׂרֹק is a colour between red and brown (only here and Isa 16:8 of vines): uncertain.
- **"Then said I, O my lord, what are these?" → "And I said, What are these, my lord?"** (v9) — the Hebrew order; אֲדֹנִי "my lord" of the angel (lower case: no divine pointing); no "O" (vocative row). **"the angel that talked with me" → "the angel who spoke with me"** (v9, 13, 14, 19) — הַמַּלְאָךְ הַדֹּבֵר בִּי; מַלְאָךְ is "angel" (the WORDS-HE row and the finished books for the heavenly one; a human sent one is "messenger": Hag 1:13; Mal 2:7; 3:1); דִּבֶּר "speak" (its row); בִּי is literally "in me" (or "with me", "by me"): "with" as Num 12:2, 6, 8 (the דִּבֶּר בְּ row "speak with").
- **"These are they whom the LORD hath sent to walk to and fro through the earth" → "These are those whom Yahweh has sent to walk about in the earth"** (v10) — לְהִתְהַלֵּךְ, the hitpael "walk about" (its row; Job 1:7; 2:2), also v11 "We have walked about"; "patrol" of the earlier text says more than the word. **"answered and said"** — וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, both verbs kept (also v11, 12).
- **"the angel of the LORD" → "the angel of Yahweh"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה; the same noun as "the angel who spoke with me" (v9), so one English word. By the WORDS-HE row and the finished books (reviewer 2026-10-16); one rendering of מַלְאָךְ through Zechariah 1–6 (also 3:1–6). **"all the earth sitteth still, and is at rest" → "all the earth is sitting and is quiet"** — יֹשֶׁבֶת וְשֹׁקָטֶת, two participles; יָשַׁב "sit" (its row: "sit" of rest, "dwell" of settling); שָׁקַט "be quiet" (its row; not "at rest").
- **"O LORD of hosts, how long wilt thou not have mercy on Jerusalem … against which thou hast had indignation these threescore and ten years?" → "Yahweh of Hosts, how long will you not have compassion on Jerusalem … against which you have been indignant these seventy years?"** (v12) — no "O" (vocative row); רִחַם "have compassion" (the root of רַחֲמִים "compassion", v16); זָעַם "be indignant" (the Ps 7:11 row; with Yahweh as subject, not speech); זֶה שִׁבְעִים שָׁנָה "these seventy years" (numbers row).
- **"good words and comfortable words" → "good words, comforting words"** (v13) — דְּבָרִים טוֹבִים דְּבָרִים נִחֻמִים: "words" twice, no "and"; נִחֻמִים, the noun "comforts" (from נִחַם "comfort", v17).
- **"Cry thou" → "Call out, saying"** (v14) — קְרָא לֵאמֹר (also v17). **"I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with a great jealousy" → "I have been jealous …"** — קִנֵּאתִי, a perfect, with its own noun קִנְאָה (the קִנֵּא row "be jealous for" with לְ; "zealous" in other versions).
- **"And I am very sore displeased with the heathen that are at ease: for I was but a little displeased, and they helped forward the affliction" → "And with great wrath I am wrathful against the nations that are at ease; for I was wrathful a little, and they helped toward evil"** (v15) — וְקֶצֶף גָּדוֹל אֲנִי קֹצֵף, noun first, then the participle; גּוֹיִם "nations"; שַׁאֲנַנִּים "at ease" (its row); אֲשֶׁר here "for" (or "in that"); עָזְרוּ לְרָעָה "they helped toward evil" (רָעָה "evil", its row; "affliction" and "disaster" narrow it).
- **"I am returned to Jerusalem with mercies" → "I have returned to Jerusalem with compassion"** (v16) — רַחֲמִים "compassion" (its row). **"and a line shall be stretched forth" → "and a line shall be stretched out"** — the written text קוה, the reading (qere) קָו "line" (the same word: the 2 Kgs 21:13 row "line"); "measuring" of the earlier text is not in the Hebrew.
- **"My cities through prosperity shall yet be spread abroad" → "My cities shall again overflow with good"** (v17) — תְּפוּצֶינָה מִטּוֹב, פּוּץ "be scattered, spread abroad" (its row), here of cities so full that they run over: "overflow" (the literal "spread abroad from good" in Changes); טוֹב "good" (its row; not "prosperity"). עוֹד four times, "again" each time. **"the LORD shall yet comfort Zion" → "and Yahweh shall again comfort Zion"** — נִחַם piel "comfort" (the noun of v13); בָּחַר "choose" (its row).
- **"Then lifted I up mine eyes, and saw" → "And I lifted up my eyes and saw"** (v18) — וָאֶשָּׂא … וָאֵרֶא, wayyiqtol "And".
- **"These are the horns which have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem" → "These are the horns that have strewn Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem"** (v19, 21) — זֵרוּ, זָרָה "strew" (its row; Ezek 12:14, 15; 22:15 beside הֵפִיץ "scatter"); v21 לְזָרוֹתָהּ "to strew it" (the land, feminine).
- **"carpenters" → "craftsmen"** (v20) — חָרָשִׁים, workers in wood, stone or metal.
- **"Then said I" → "And I said"** (v21). **"And he spake, saying" kept** — וַיֹּאמֶר לֵאמֹר, "and he said, saying". **"so that no man did lift up his head" kept** — כְּפִי־אִישׁ לֹא־נָשָׂא רֹאשׁוֹ, literally "according to the mouth of: a man did not lift his head" (= so that). **"to fray them" → "to make them tremble"** — לְהַחֲרִיד, hifil of חָרַד "tremble"; "them" (masculine) is not named. **"to cast out the horns of the Gentiles, which lifted up their horn over the land" → "to cast down the horns of the nations who lift up a horn against the land"** — לְיַדּוֹת, piel of יָדָה "throw"; הַנֹּשְׂאִים קֶרֶן, a participle with "horn" singular.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — our 1:18–21 = Heb 2:1–4 (the Hebrew chapter 1 ends at our 1:17).
