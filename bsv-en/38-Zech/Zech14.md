# Zechariah 14

[1] Behold, a day is coming for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and your spoil shall be divided in your midst.

[2] And I will gather all the nations to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ for war, and the city shall be captured, and the houses pillaged, and the women lain with; and half of the city shall go out into exile, and the rest of the people shall not be cut off from the city.

[3] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall go out and fight against those nations, as the day he fought in the day of battle.

[4] **And his feet shall stand in that day upon ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧, which is facing ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ on the east**; and ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧ shall be split from its middle toward the east and toward the west, a very great valley; and half of the mountain shall move away toward the north, and half of it toward the south.

[5] And you shall flee to the valley of my mountains, for the valley of the mountains shall reach to ⟦atzel|Atzel|Azal⟧; and you shall flee as you fled from the face of the quaking in the days of ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall come, all the holy ones with you.

[6] And it shall be in that day, there shall not be light; splendors and congealing.

[7] And it shall be one day — it is known to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — not day and not night; and it shall be, at the time of evening there shall be light.

[8] And it shall be in that day, living waters shall go out from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, half of them to the eastern sea and half of them to the hinder sea; in summer and in winter it shall be.

[9] **And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be king over all the earth; in that day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be one and his name one.**

[10] All the land shall turn about like the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, from ⟦geba|Geva|Geba⟧ to Rimmon, south of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and she shall be lifted up and shall dwell in her place, from the Gate of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ to the place of the First Gate, to the Gate of the Corners, and from the Tower of ⟦hananel|Chananel|Hananel⟧ to the winepresses of the king.

[11] And they shall dwell in her, and there shall be no more a thing devoted to destruction; and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall dwell in security.

[12] And this shall be the plague with which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall smite all the peoples who have mustered against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: his flesh rots away while he stands on his feet, and his eyes shall rot away in their sockets, and his tongue shall rot away in their mouth.

[13] And it shall be in that day, a great confusion from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be among them; and they shall seize, each man the hand of his neighbor, and his hand shall go up against the hand of his neighbor.

[14] And also ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall fight in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and the wealth of all the nations round about shall be gathered, gold and silver and garments, in very great abundance.

[15] And so shall be the plague of the horse, the mule, the camel and the donkey, and all the beasts that shall be in those camps, like this plague.

[16] And it shall be, every one who is left of all the nations that came against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, they shall go up year after year to bow down to the King, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and to hold ⟦sukkot|the Feast of Sukkot|the Feast of Booths⟧.

[17] And it shall be, whoever of the clans of the earth does not go up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to bow down to the King, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, upon them there shall be no rain.

[18] And if the clan of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ does not go up and does not come in, then not upon them; there shall be the plague with which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ smites the nations that do not go up to hold ⟦sukkot|the Feast of Sukkot|the Feast of Booths⟧.

[19] This shall be the sin of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and the sin of all the nations that do not go up to hold ⟦sukkot|the Feast of Sukkot|the Feast of Booths⟧.

[20] In that day there shall be upon the bells of the horse, **Holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧**; and the pots in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be like the bowls before the altar.

[21] And every pot in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall be holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts; and all who sacrifice shall come and take of them and boil in them; and there shall no more be ⟦canaanites|a Kenaani|a Canaanite⟧ in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts in that day.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Layout** — prose, as the Hebrew; the quotation marks of v20 are dropped.
- **"the day of the LORD cometh, and thy spoil shall be divided in the midst of thee" → "a day is coming for Yahweh, and your spoil shall be divided in your midst"** (v1) — הִנֵּה "behold"; יוֹם־בָּא לַיהוָה "a day is coming for Yahweh" (no article, לְ "for"; not "the day of the LORD"); "you" is feminine singular (Jerusalem); וְ "and"; "taken from you" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"to battle … the houses rifled, and the women ravished … the residue of the people" → "for war … the houses pillaged, and the women lain with … the rest of the people"** (v2) — מִלְחָמָה "war" (its row); לָכַד "capture" (its row); נָשַׁסּוּ "pillaged" (the שָׁסָה row); qere תִּשָּׁכַבְנָה "lain with" (שָׁכַב, its row); the ketiv תשגלנה is a coarser word for the same act ("be ravished"), and the qere stands in the verse (reviewer row); גּוֹלָה "exile" (its row); יֶתֶר "rest" (its row); וְ "and", not "but".
- **"as when he fought in the day of battle" → "as the day he fought in the day of battle"** (v3) — כְּיוֹם הִלָּחֲמוֹ "as the day of his fighting"; קְרָב "battle" (another word than מִלְחָמָה "war" of v2); נִלְחַם בְּ "fight against" (its row).
- **"which is before Jerusalem on the east, and the mount of Olives shall cleave in the midst thereof toward the east and toward the west, and there shall be a very great valley" → "which is facing Jerusalem on the east; and the Mount of Olives shall be split from its middle toward the east and toward the west, a very great valley"** (v4) — the Hebrew order kept (the verb first: "And his feet shall stand in that day"); עַל־פְּנֵי "facing" (its row); מִקֶּדֶם "on the east" (lit. "from the front", the Directions row); נִבְקַע "be split" (בָּקַע row); מִזְרָחָה וָיָמָּה "toward the east and toward the west" (lit. "toward the sunrise and toward the sea"); גֵּיא גְּדוֹלָה מְאֹד "a very great valley", without a verb ("there shall be" not supplied); מָשׁ "move away"; צָפוֹנָה / נֶגְבָּה "toward the north / toward the south" (נֶגְבָּה lit. "toward the Negev").
- **"ye shall flee to the valley of the mountains … to Azal … from before the earthquake … and all the saints with thee" → "you shall flee to the valley of my mountains … to Azal … from the face of the quaking … all the holy ones with you"** (v5) — MT וְנַסְתֶּם "and you shall flee" (נוּס, three times as pointed; the versions read וְנִסְתַּם "and it shall be stopped up", the valley, from memory); גֵּיא־הָרַי "the valley of my mountains" (the suffix as printed); אָצַל "Azal" (a place not otherwise known; or "beside, near": uncertain); מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (its row); רַעַשׁ "quaking" (the Ezekiel row; of the land "earthquake"); עִמָּךְ "with you" (feminine singular: Jerusalem; the versions have "with him", from memory); קְדֹשִׁים "holy ones" (not "saints": angels may be meant); the clause has no verb, nothing supplied.
- **"the light shall not be clear, nor dark" → "there shall not be light; splendors and congealing"** (v6) — וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "And it shall be in that day" (the formula); לֹא־יִהְיֶה אוֹר "there shall not be light"; יְקָרוֹת "splendors" (lit. "precious / splendid ones", as יָרֵחַ יָקָר "the moon in splendor", Job 31:26); the qere וְקִפָּאוֹן "and congealing" (a noun of קָפָא "congeal", Exod 15:8); the ketiv יקפאון "they shall congeal" (a verb): "the splendid ones shall congeal". The verse is obscure as it stands; "cold and frost" (from the versions, from memory) is a common emendation. Translated as the qere.
- **"But it shall be one day which shall be known to the LORD, not day, nor night: but it shall come to pass, that at evening time it shall be light" → "And it shall be one day — it is known to Yahweh — not day and not night; and it shall be, at the time of evening there shall be light"** (v7) — יוֹם־אֶחָד "one day" ("unique" is a reading of "one"); הוּא יִוָּדַע "it is known" (or "shall be known"), set apart by the accents; וְהָיָה kept; וְ "and", not "but".
- **"living waters … toward the former sea, and half of them toward the hinder sea: in summer and in winter shall it be" → "living waters … to the eastern sea and half of them to the hinder sea; in summer and in winter it shall be"** (v8) — וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא kept; מַיִם חַיִּים "living waters" (its row; here plural "waters" with "half of them"); יֵצְאוּ "shall go out" (יָצָא); הַיָּם הַקַּדְמוֹנִי "the eastern sea" (lit. "the front sea": the Dead Sea); הַיָּם הָאַחֲרוֹן "the hinder sea" (its row: the Mediterranean; "western sea" in Changes only).
- **"And the LORD shall be king … in that day shall there be one LORD, and his name one" → "And Yahweh shall be king … in that day Yahweh shall be one and his name one"** (v9) — וְהָיָה יְהוָה לְמֶלֶךְ "Yahweh shall be king" (lit. "for king"); יִהְיֶה יְהוָה אֶחָד וּשְׁמוֹ אֶחָד "Yahweh shall be one and his name one", word for word (Deut 6:4 has אֶחָד of Yahweh too).
- **"All the land shall be turned as a plain … and it shall be lifted up, and inhabited in her place … unto the place of the first gate, unto the corner gate … unto the king's winepresses" → "All the land shall turn about like the Arabah … and she shall be lifted up and shall dwell in her place … the First Gate, to the Gate of the Corners … the winepresses of the king"** (v10) — יִסּוֹב "turn about" (סָבַב); כָּעֲרָבָה "like the Arabah" (with the article: the Jordan rift, token `arabah`; as a common noun "steppe", its row); נֶגֶב יְרוּשָׁלִַם "south of Jerusalem" (the Directions row; lit. "Negev of Jerusalem"); וְרָאֲמָה "and she shall be lifted up" (= רָמָה, spelled with א; of Jerusalem); וְיָשְׁבָה "shall dwell" (יָשַׁב row, as 12:6); שַׁעַר הָרִאשׁוֹן "the First Gate" ("former" is a reading); שַׁעַר הַפִּנִּים "the Gate of the Corners" (plural); יִקְבֵי "winepresses" (יֶקֶב row). The gates and the tower are named as places of the city; where each stood is not certain.
- **"And men shall dwell in it, and there shall be no more utter destruction; but Jerusalem shall be safely inhabited" → "And they shall dwell in her, and there shall be no more a thing devoted to destruction; and Jerusalem shall dwell in security"** (v11) — "men" not supplied (the subject is in the verb); חֵרֶם "a thing devoted to destruction" (its row: "curse" or "ban" in Changes only); לָבֶטַח "in security" (its row); וְ "and", not "but".
- **"Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth" → "his flesh rots away while he stands on his feet, and his eyes shall rot away in their sockets, and his tongue shall rot away in their mouth"** (v12) — מַגֵּפָה "plague" (its row) and נָגַף "smite" (its row); צָבְאוּ "have mustered" (צָבָא verb, its row: "fought" in Changes); הָמֵק בְּשָׂרוֹ, infinitive absolute of מָקַק (נָמַק row "rot away"), "his flesh rots away"; the singular "his" (each man) and the plural "their sockets" (the eyes), "their mouth" (the peoples) as printed; חֹרִים "holes, sockets".
- **"a great tumult from the LORD … they shall lay hold every one on the hand of his neighbour, and his hand shall rise up against the hand of his neighbour" → "a great confusion from Yahweh … they shall seize, each man the hand of his neighbor, and his hand shall go up against the hand of his neighbor"** (v13) — וְהָיָה kept; מְהוּמַת־יְהוָה "confusion of Yahweh" (מְהוּמָה, its row; "from" for the genitive); הֶחֱזִיקוּ "seize" (the הֶחֱזִיק row); אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ "each man … his neighbor" (its row); עָלְתָה "go up" (עָלָה).
- **"Judah also shall fight at Jerusalem; and the wealth of all the heathen round about shall be gathered together, gold, and silver, and apparel, in great abundance" → "And also Judah shall fight in Jerusalem; and the wealth of all the nations round about shall be gathered, gold and silver and garments, in very great abundance"** (v14) — תִּלָּחֵם בִּירוּשָׁלִַם "fight in Jerusalem" (בְּ "in"; with נִלְחַם "fight against" is also possible: whether Judah fights for the city or against it is open); חַיִל "wealth" (its row, of goods); בְּגָדִים "garments" (its row); לָרֹב מְאֹד "in very great abundance".
- **"so shall be the plague of the horse, of the mule … as this plague" → "so shall be the plague of the horse, the mule … like this plague"** (v15) — the Hebrew order kept (the comparison at the end); פֶּרֶד "mule" (its row), גָּמָל "camel", חֲמוֹר "donkey" (its row); בְּהֵמָה "beasts" (its row); מַחֲנוֹת "camps".
- **"every one that is left … shall even go up from year to year to worship the King, the LORD of hosts, and to keep the feast of tabernacles" → "every one who is left … they shall go up year after year to bow down to the King, Yahweh of Hosts, and to hold the Feast of Sukkot"** (v16, 18, 19) — וְהָיָה kept; הַנּוֹתָר "who is left" (נוֹתַר row); מִדֵּי שָׁנָה בְשָׁנָה "year after year" (lit. "as often as year in year"); הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" (as Isa 44–47 beside סָגַד); חָגַג "hold a feast" (its row) with חַג הַסֻּכּוֹת in the token `sukkot` (its row).
- **"whoso will not come up of all the families of the earth … even upon them shall be no rain" → "whoever of the clans of the earth does not go up … upon them there shall be no rain"** (v17) — וְהָיָה kept; מִשְׁפְּחוֹת "clans" (its row); גֶּשֶׁם "rain" (its row).
- **"And if the family of Egypt go not up, and come not, that have no rain; there shall be the plague" → "And if the clan of Egypt does not go up and does not come in, then not upon them; there shall be the plague"** (v18) — וְלֹא עֲלֵיהֶם word for word, "then not upon them", divided by the atnach from what follows: whether "no rain" is meant (from v17), or the plague is meant ("then upon them shall be the plague", from the Greek and Syriac without לֹא, from memory), is open; nothing supplied.
- **"This shall be the punishment of Egypt" → "This shall be the sin of Egypt"** (v19) — חַטָּאת "sin" (its row; the sin and what it brings: "punishment" is a reading).
- **"upon the bells of the horses, HOLINESS UNTO THE LORD; and the pots … shall be like the bowls before the altar" → "upon the bells of the horse, Holy to Yahweh; and the pots … like the bowls before the altar"** (v20) — מְצִלּוֹת "bells" (only here; uncertain); הַסּוּס singular "the horse" (collective); קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה "Holy to Yahweh", as the plate of the high priest (Exod 28:36: the קֹדֶשׁ-as-predicate row); סִירוֹת "pots" (its row); מִזְרָקִים "bowls" (its row).
- **"and seethe therein: and in that day there shall be no more the Canaanite" → "and boil in them; and there shall no more be a Canaanite"** (v21) — וְהָיָה kept; זֹבְחִים "who sacrifice" (זָבַח); בִּשֵּׁל "boil" (its row); "the meat of the sacrifice" is not in the Hebrew; כְנַעֲנִי "a Canaanite" (no article); the word is also used of traders (Job 41:6; Prov 31:24; Isa 23:8: "merchant"), and "trader" is a common reading here — the name kept, the sense open.

### Notes
- **v4** — Acts 1:11–12 has the ascent of Jesus from the Mount of Olives; the verse itself names only Yahweh's feet.
- **v8** — "living waters": see Ezek 47:1–12; John 7:38; Rev 22:1.
- **v9** — "one … one": the word of Deut 6:4.
