# Zechariah 6

[1] And I returned and lifted up my eyes and saw, and behold, four chariots going out from between the two mountains; and the mountains were mountains of bronze.

[2] In the first chariot were red horses, and in the second chariot black horses,

[3] and in the third chariot white horses, and in the fourth chariot dappled horses, strong ones.

[4] And I answered and said to the angel who spoke with me, "What are these, my lord?"

[5] And the angel answered and said to me, "These are the four winds of the heavens, going out from taking their stand by the Lord of all the earth.

[6] The one in which are the black horses is going out to the land of the north, and the white went out after them, and the dappled went out to the land of the south."

[7] And the strong ones went out, and they sought to go to walk about in the earth; and he said, "Go, walk about in the earth." And they walked about in the earth.

[8] And he called out to me and spoke to me, saying, "See, those who are going out to the land of the north have set my spirit at rest in the land of the north."

[9] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[10] Take from the exile, from ⟦heldai|Chelday|Heldai⟧ and from ⟦tobijah|Toviyah|Tobijah⟧ and from ⟦jedaiah|Yedayah|Jedaiah⟧; and you shall come, you, in that day, and you shall come to the house of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ son of ⟦zephaniah|Tzefanyah|Zephaniah⟧, who have come from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[11] And you shall take silver and gold, and make crowns, and set them on the head of ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the high priest.

[12] And you shall say to him, saying, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, saying:

Behold, a man, Sprout is his name,
and from his place he shall sprout,
and he shall build the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And he, he shall build the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and he, he shall bear majesty,
and he shall sit and rule upon his throne;
and there shall be a priest upon his throne,
and a counsel of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ shall be between the two of them.

[14] And the crowns shall be to ⟦helem|Chelem|Helem⟧ and to ⟦tobijah|Toviyah|Tobijah⟧ and to ⟦jedaiah|Yedayah|Jedaiah⟧ and to ⟦hen|Chen|Hen⟧ son of ⟦zephaniah|Tzefanyah|Zephaniah⟧ for a memorial in the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] And those who are far off shall come and build in the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and you shall know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has sent me to you. And it shall be, if you will surely listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ —

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v12, 15) — the prophets' formula row. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v15). וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying" (v9; the formula row).
- **Layout and quotation marks** — v1–11, 14–15 prose; v12b–13 lines as before (by the accents). Double quotation marks for the dialogue of the vision (v4–8); the word that came to the prophet (v10–15) stands without them, as 4:9–10.
- **"And I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked" → "And I returned and lifted up my eyes and saw"** (v1) — שׁוּב with a second verb, as 4:1; 5:1. **"there came four chariots out from between two mountains" → "four chariots going out from between the two mountains"** — יֹצְאוֹת a participle (also v5, 6, 8); שְׁנֵי הֶהָרִים with the article; מַרְכָּבָה "chariot" (its row); נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze" (its row).
- **"In the first chariot were red horses" kept; "and in the second chariot black horses"** (v2) — the Hebrew has no verb; "were" supplied once; "chariot" repeated as in the Hebrew (also v3).
- **"grisled and bay horses" → "dappled horses, strong ones"** (v3) — בְּרֻדִּים "dappled" (from בָּרָד "hail": spotted as with hail); אֲמֻצִּים, from אָמֵץ "be strong" (the אָמֵץ row "be firm"): "strong", or a colour ("bay", "piebald"), uncertain; the Hebrew has no "and" between the two words. v7 has הָאֲמֻצִּים alone, "the strong ones".
- **"Then I answered and said unto the angel that talked with me" → "And I answered and said to the angel who spoke with me"** (v4) — as 4:4.
- **"These are the four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth" → "These are the four winds of the heavens, going out from taking their stand by the Lord of all the earth"** (v5) — אַרְבַּע רֻחוֹת הַשָּׁמַיִם, "the four winds of the heavens" as 2:6 (the רוּחַ row: "wind", also "spirit"; v8 "my spirit": one word, said here); מֵהִתְיַצֵּב "from taking their stand" (the הִתְיַצֵּב row); עַל "by" (as 4:14); אֲדוֹן כָּל־הָאָרֶץ as 4:14.
- **"The black horses which are therein go forth into the north country" → "The one in which are the black horses is going out to the land of the north"** (v6) — אֲשֶׁר־בָּהּ "which in it" (the chariot, feminine); אֶרֶץ צָפוֹן "the land of the north" (as 2:6). **"and the white go forth after them; and the grisled go forth toward the south country" → "and the white went out after them, and the dappled went out to the land of the south"** — יָצְאוּ twice, a perfect after the participle; אֶל־אַחֲרֵיהֶם "after them" (literally "to behind them"; "to the west" is a reading by the Directions row, not followed); אֶרֶץ הַתֵּימָן "the land of the south" (תֵּימָן, literally "the right-hand side", the south; also the region Teman). The red horses of v2 are not named in v6–7.
- **"And the bay went forth, and sought to go that they might walk to and fro through the earth" → "And the strong ones went out, and they sought to go to walk about in the earth"** (v7) — וַיְבַקְשׁוּ "sought" (the בִּקֵּשׁ row); לְהִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about" (as 1:10, 11; three times here). **"Get you hence" → "Go"** — לְכוּ. **"So they walked" → "And they walked"** — wayyiqtol, feminine plural (the chariots or horses).
- **"Then cried he upon me, and spake unto me, saying" → "And he called out to me and spoke to me, saying"** (v8) — וַיַּזְעֵק אֹתִי (the הִזְעִיק row "call out, summon by a cry"); the speaker is not named. **"Behold, these … have quieted my spirit in the north country" → "See, those who are going out … have set my spirit at rest in the land of the north"** — רְאֵה "See"; הֵנִיחוּ, hifil of נוּחַ "give rest, set at rest" (רוּחַ "spirit": here the spirit of Yahweh, lower case as the finished books; "my anger" in many versions, since רוּחַ can be wrath, Judg 8:3; uncertain).
- **"Take of them of the captivity, even of Heldai, of Tobijah, and of Jedaiah, which are come from Babylon, and come thou the same day, and go into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah" → "Take from the exile, from Heldai and from Tobijah and from Jedaiah; and you shall come, you, in that day, and you shall come to the house of Josiah son of Zephaniah, who have come from Babylon"** (v10) — לָקוֹחַ, an infinitive absolute as a command; הַגּוֹלָה "the exile" (the Ezra row: the returned body); מֵ … וּמֵאֵת … וּמֵאֵת "from … and from … and from"; וּבָאתָ אַתָּה … וּבָאתָ, "come" twice with the stressed "you"; בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day" (the prophets' row; here "that same day"); אֲשֶׁר־בָּאוּ מִבָּבֶל "who have come from Babylon" stands last in the Hebrew, plural: the three men (or Josiah's house); the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"Then take silver and gold, and make crowns" → "And you shall take silver and gold, and make crowns"** (v11) — weqatal; עֲטָרוֹת "crowns", plural as printed (also v14 הָעֲטָרֹת with a singular verb: one crown of several rings, or several crowns; said here). **"and set them upon the head of Joshua" kept** — וְשַׂמְתָּ has no object in the Hebrew; "them" is supplied (English cannot stand without it).
- **"And speak unto him, saying, Thus speaketh the LORD of hosts, saying" → "And you shall say to him, saying, Thus says Yahweh of Hosts, saying"** (v12) — לֵאמֹר twice, kept. **"Behold the man whose name is The BRANCH; and he shall grow up out of his place" → "Behold, a man, Sprout is his name, and from his place he shall sprout"** — הִנֵּה־אִישׁ "a man" (no article); צֶמַח שְׁמוֹ, "Sprout is his name": a name, so with a capital (see 3:8); יִצְמָח, the verb of the same root (the צָמַח row "spring up": here "sprout" to keep the play); מִתַּחְתָּיו, literally "from under him" (the תַּחְתָּיו row "in his place"). הֵיכָל "temple" (its row; also v13–15).
- **"Even he shall build the temple of the LORD; and he shall bear the glory" → "And he, he shall build the temple of Yahweh, and he, he shall bear majesty"** (v13) — וְהוּא … וְהוּא, the pronoun stressed twice; הוֹד "majesty" (its row; "glory" is כָּבוֹד; "royal" of the earlier text is not in the word). **"and shall sit and rule upon his throne" kept** — מָשַׁל "rule" (its row). **"and he shall be a priest upon his throne" → "and there shall be a priest upon his throne"** — וְהָיָה כֹהֵן: "he shall be a priest" or "there shall be a priest" (the Hebrew allows both; "between the two of them" names two: kept as the earlier text, the other reading here). The Greek has "a priest at his right hand" (from memory); the MT is kept. **"and the counsel of peace shall be between them both" → "and a counsel of shalom shall be between the two of them"** — עֲצַת שָׁלוֹם, no article; שָׁלוֹם token (reviewer row).
- **"And the crowns shall be to Helem, and to Tobijah, and to Jedaiah, and to Hen the son of Zephaniah, for a memorial in the temple of the LORD" kept in the Hebrew order** (v14) — עֲטָרֹת plural (v11); זִכָּרוֹן "memorial" (its row). Helem (חֵלֶם) is Heldai of v10 and Hen (חֵן, "favor") is Josiah of v10: the names stand as printed (the Syriac reads "Heldai", from memory).
- **"And they that are far off shall come and build in the temple of the LORD" kept** (v15) — וּבָנוּ בְּהֵיכַל "build in the temple" (the earlier "help to build" adds a verb). **"And this shall come to pass, if ye will diligently obey the voice of the LORD your God" → "And it shall be, if you will surely listen to the voice of Yahweh your Elohim —"** — וְהָיָה אִם, "And it shall be, if" (the Deut row, kept; "this" is not in the Hebrew); שָׁמוֹעַ תִּשְׁמְעוּן, the infinitive absolute "surely" (standing decision; literally "hearing you shall hear"); שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of" (its row; "obey" is not the Hebrew word). The sentence has no main clause in the Hebrew: it stays unfinished (the standing decision), the dash marks it.

### Notes
- **v12–13** — the crowned man is Joshua the priest (v11), yet the words name "Sprout" (3:8) and a throne; whether one man holds both offices or a king and a priest sit side by side ("between the two of them") is not decided by the Hebrew. The verse is read of the Mashiach in later tradition (Heb 7 sets priest and king together).
