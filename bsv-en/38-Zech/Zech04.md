# Zechariah 4

[1] And the angel who spoke with me returned and woke me, like a man who is woken from his sleep.

[2] And he said to me, "What are you seeing?" And I said, "I have seen, and behold, a lampstand of gold, all of it, and its bowl on its top, and its seven lamps upon it, seven and seven pipes to the lamps that are on its top.

[3] And two olive trees by it, one on the right of the bowl and one on its left."

[4] And I answered and said to the angel who spoke with me, saying, "What are these, my lord?"

[5] And the angel who spoke with me answered and said to me, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, my lord."

[6] And he answered and said to me, saying, "This is the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧, saying,

**Not by might and not by power,
but by my spirit, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.**

[7] Who are you, great mountain? Before ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧, to level land! And he shall bring out the top stone, with shouts: Favor, favor to it!"

[8] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[9] The hands of ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ have founded this house, and his hands shall finish it; and you shall know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has sent me to you.

[10] For who has despised the day of small things? And they shall rejoice, and they shall see the stone, the tin, in the hand of ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧, these seven; the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, they are roaming through all the earth.

[11] And I answered and said to him, "What are these two olive trees on the right of the lampstand and on its left?"

[12] And I answered a second time and said to him, "What are the two ears of the olive trees that are beside the two pipes of gold, which empty the gold from upon them?"

[13] And he said to me, saying, "Do you not know what these are?" And I said, "No, my lord."

[14] And he said, "These are the two sons of fresh oil, who stand by the Lord of all the earth."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v6, 9) — the prophets' formula row. וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying" (v8; the formula row).
- **Layout and quotation marks** — prose, with v6b in two lines and bold as before. Double quotation marks for the dialogue of the vision (v2–7, 11–14); v9–10, the word that came to the prophet (v8), stand without them.
- **"And the angel that talked with me came again, and waked me" → "And the angel who spoke with me returned and woke me"** (v1) — וַיָּשָׁב … וַיְעִירֵנִי, שׁוּב with a second verb (the שׁוּב row: "return and …", = "again": kept); מַלְאָךְ "angel" and הַדֹּבֵר בִּי "who spoke with me" as chapters 1–3 (also v4, 5). **"as a man that is wakened out of his sleep" → "like a man who is woken from his sleep"** — יֵעוֹר, nifal of עוּר (as 2:13 "roused himself").
- **"What seest thou?" → "What are you seeing?"** (v2) — רֹאֶה, a participle. **"And I said, I have looked" → "And I said, I have seen"** — the written text (ketiv) ויאמר "and he said", the reading (qere) וָאֹמַר "and I said": followed; רָאִיתִי a perfect. **"a candlestick all of gold, with a bowl upon the top of it, and his seven lamps thereon" → "a lampstand of gold, all of it, and its bowl on its top, and its seven lamps upon it"** — מְנוֹרַת זָהָב כֻּלָּהּ, the Hebrew order; מְנוֹרָה "lampstand", נֵר "lamp" (their row); גֻּלָּהּ "its bowl" (the גֻּלָּה row: "bowl" in this verse); עַל־רֹאשָׁהּ "on its top" (literally "head"). **"and seven pipes to the seven lamps" → "seven and seven pipes to the lamps"** — שִׁבְעָה וְשִׁבְעָה מוּצָקוֹת: "seven and seven", seven to each lamp (forty-nine) or fourteen in all; the Hebrew does not say which; מוּצָקוֹת, from יָצַק "pour", "pipes" or "spouts" (uncertain).
- **"And two olive trees by it, one upon the right side of the bowl, and the other upon the left side thereof" → "And two olive trees by it, one on the right of the bowl and one on its left"** (v3) — עָלֶיהָ "by it" (literally "upon / over it"); אֶחָד … וְאֶחָד "one … and one"; no verb in the Hebrew (also v2).
- **"So I answered and spake to the angel that talked with me, saying" → "And I answered and said to the angel who spoke with me, saying"** (v4) — וָאַעַן וָאֹמַר … לֵאמֹר, all three kept (also v11, 12 "answered and said"; v6, 13 "said … saying"). **"my lord"** — אֲדֹנִי, of the angel (also v5, 13); no "O".
- **"Then the angel … answered" → "And the angel … answered"** (v5) — wayyiqtol "And" (also v6, 13, 14). **"Knowest thou not what these be?" → "Do you not know what these are?"** — הֲלוֹא יָדַעְתָּ, a perfect of knowing.
- **"Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit" → "Not by might and not by power, but by my spirit"** (v6) — לֹא בְחַיִל וְלֹא בְכֹחַ כִּי אִם־בְּרוּחִי: "not" twice with "and"; חַיִל "might" (the חַיִל row "ability", of an army "force": here strength of any kind, beside כֹּחַ "power", its row); כִּי אִם "but", one thing put in place of another (reviewer row); רוּחַ "spirit", lower case as the finished books (the רוּחַ row; "Spirit" with a capital decides a doctrine the word does not carry). אָמַר "says". **"This is the word … unto Zerubbabel, saying" kept** — לֵאמֹר.
- **"Who art thou, O great mountain? before Zerubbabel thou shalt become a plain" → "Who are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel, to level land!"** (v7) — no "O"; הַר־הַגָּדוֹל, "mountain" with the article on the adjective; לְמִישֹׁר, without a verb: "to level land" (the מִישׁוֹר row, literally "level land"; "tableland" of the row says a region, which is not meant here). **"and he shall bring forth the headstone thereof with shoutings, crying, Grace, grace unto it" → "And he shall bring out the top stone, with shouts: Favor, favor to it!"** — וְהוֹצִיא, a weqatal (the הוֹצִיא "bring out" row); הָאֶבֶן הָרֹאשָׁה "the top stone" (literally "the head stone"; "thereof" is not in the Hebrew); תְּשֻׁאוֹת "shouts" (reviewer row: of men "shouts"), without a preposition: "with" is supplied; חֵן "favor" (its row; not "grace").
- **"The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundations of this house; his hands shall also finish it" → "The hands of Zerubbabel have founded this house, and his hands shall finish it"** (v9) — יִסְּדוּ "have founded"; וְיָדָיו "and his hands" ("also" is not in the Hebrew); בִּצַּע "finish" (literally "cut off", complete). **"and thou shalt know" kept** — וְיָדַעְתָּ, singular (the prophet); "me" as 2:9, 11.
- **"For who hath despised the day of small things? for they shall rejoice, and shall see the plummet in the hand of Zerubbabel with those seven; they are the eyes of the LORD, which run to and fro through the whole earth" → "For who has despised the day of small things? And they shall rejoice, and they shall see the stone, the tin, in the hand of Zerubbabel, these seven; the eyes of Yahweh, they are roaming through all the earth"** (v10) — מִי בַז "who has despised": a question, or "whoever has despised" with the next clause (both readings stand); וְשָׂמְחוּ וְרָאוּ, weqatal "and they shall rejoice and see"; הָאֶבֶן הַבְּדִיל, "the stone, the tin" (בְּדִיל "tin", its row: a stone of tin or a tin weight; "plummet" interprets). The accents (atnach) close the first half with שִׁבְעָה־אֵלֶּה "these seven": so "these seven" may be the subject of "rejoice … see", and "the eyes of Yahweh, they are roaming" a clause of its own; the earlier text's "These seven are the eyes of Yahweh" joins across the break; the Hebrew order is kept and both readings named here. מְשׁוֹטְטִים "roaming" (as Job 1:7 שׁוּט; 2 Chr 16:9 the same words of the eyes of Yahweh); בְּכָל־הָאָרֶץ "through all the earth".
- **"upon the right side of the candlestick and upon the left side thereof" → "on the right of the lampstand and on its left"** (v11) — the Hebrew order; the written text שמאולה is a fuller spelling.
- **"What be these two olive branches which through the two golden pipes empty the golden oil out of themselves?" → "What are the two ears of the olive trees that are beside the two pipes of gold, which empty the gold from upon them?"** (v12) — שִׁבֲּלֵי, plural of שִׁבֹּלֶת "ear (of grain)": here the fruit-laden tips of the olive branches (the word is "ears"); בְּיַד "beside" (literally "in the hand of"); צַנְתְּרוֹת "pipes" (only here: uncertain); הַמְרִיקִים "which empty", the subject the pipes or the ears; הַזָּהָב "the gold" (the golden oil: "oil" is not written); מֵעֲלֵיהֶם "from upon them".
- **"These are the two anointed ones, that stand by the Lord of the whole earth" → "These are the two sons of fresh oil, who stand by the Lord of all the earth"** (v14) — שְׁנֵי בְנֵי־הַיִּצְהָר, "the two sons of the fresh oil" (the בְּנֵי standing decision; the יִצְהָר row "fresh oil", not the anointing oil שֶׁמֶן מִשְׁחָה): "anointed ones" interprets; עַל "by" (literally "upon / over", of those who stand before a master); אֲדוֹן כָּל־הָאָרֶץ "the Lord of all the earth" (as Josh 3:11, 13; Mic 4:13).

### Notes
- **v14** — the two "sons of fresh oil" are commonly taken to be Zerubbabel and Joshua (3:1–8; 4:6–10); the verse does not name them. Rev 11:4 takes up the two olive trees and the lampstands.
