# Exodus 34

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Hew for yourself two tablets of stone like the first ones, and I will write on the tablets the words that were on the first tablets, which you broke in pieces.

[2] And be ready for the morning, and you shall go up in the morning to Mount Sinai and stand for me there on the top of the mountain.

[3] And no man shall go up with you, and also let no man be seen on all the mountain; also let not the flock and the herd feed in front of that mountain."

[4] And he hewed two tablets of stone like the first ones, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ rose early in the morning and went up to Mount Sinai, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded him, and he took in his hand two tablets of stone.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came down in the cloud, and he took his stand with him there, and he called out the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ passed by before his face and called out, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦el|El|God⟧ compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth,

[7] guarding ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ for thousands, forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin; and he will surely not clear, visiting the iniquity of fathers upon sons and upon sons' sons, upon the third and upon the fourth generation."

[8] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ hastened and bowed his head to the earth and bowed down.

[9] And he said, "If, please, I have found favor in your eyes, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, let ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, please, go in our midst, for it is a stiff-necked people; and pardon our iniquity and our sin, and take us as your inheritance."

[10] And he said, "Behold, I am cutting a covenant. Before all your people I will do wonderful deeds that have not been created in all the earth and in all the nations, and all the people in whose midst you are shall see the work of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for it is fearsome, what I am doing with you.

[11] Keep for yourself what I am commanding you today. Behold, I am driving out from before you the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦hivites|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧.

[12] Take heed to yourself, lest you cut a covenant with the inhabitant of the land upon which you are coming, lest he become a snare in your midst.

[13] For their altars you shall break down, and their pillars you shall break in pieces, and his Asherim you shall cut down.

[14] For you shall not bow down to another ⟦el|el|god⟧, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, Jealous is his name; a jealous ⟦el|El|God⟧ is he.

[15] Lest you cut a covenant with the inhabitant of the land, and they play the prostitute after their gods and sacrifice to their gods, and he calls you, and you eat of his sacrifice,

[16] and you take of his daughters for your sons, and his daughters play the prostitute after their gods and make your sons play the prostitute after their gods.

[17] Molten gods you shall not make for yourself.

[18] The Feast of ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ you shall keep. Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, as I commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of ⟦abib|Aviv|Abib⟧, for in the month of ⟦abib|Aviv|Abib⟧ you came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[19] Every firstling of the womb is mine, and all your livestock that is male, the firstling of ox and sheep.

[20] And the firstling of a donkey you shall ransom with a sheep, and if you do not ransom it, you shall break its neck. Every firstborn of your sons you shall ransom. And they shall not appear before my face empty.

[21] Six days you shall labor, and on the seventh day you shall cease; in plowing and in harvest you shall cease.

[22] And a Feast of ⟦weeks|Shavuot|Weeks⟧ you shall make for yourself, the firstfruits of the wheat harvest, and the Feast of the Ingathering at the turn of the year.

[23] Three times in the year all your males shall appear before the face of ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[24] For I will dispossess nations from before you and enlarge your border, and no man shall covet your land when you go up to appear before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ three times in the year.

[25] You shall not slaughter the blood of my sacrifice upon what is leavened, and the sacrifice of the Feast of the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ shall not lodge until the morning.

[26] The first of the firstfruits of your ground you shall bring to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. You shall not boil a kid in its mother's milk."

[27] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Write for yourself these words, for according to these words I have cut a covenant with you and with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[28] And he was there with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ forty days and forty nights; bread he did not eat, and water he did not drink. And he wrote on the tablets the words of the covenant, the ten words.

[29] And it came to pass, when ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ came down from Mount Sinai, and the two tablets of the testimony were in the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ when he came down from the mountain, that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ did not know that the skin of his face shone in his speaking with him.

[30] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ saw ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, and behold, the skin of his face shone, and they feared to draw near to him.

[31] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ called to them, and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and all the chieftains in the congregation returned to him, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to them.

[32] And afterward all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ drew near, and he commanded them all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken with him on Mount Sinai.

[33] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ finished speaking with them, and he put a veil on his face.

[34] And when ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went in before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to speak with him, he would remove the veil until he came out. And he would come out and speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ what he was commanded.

[35] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would see the face of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, that the skin of the face of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ shone, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ would return the veil upon his face until he went in to speak with him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v23, 24, 26) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; of the gods of the peoples, "gods" (v15, 16, 17).
- **"God" → "El"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew אֵל *El*: אֵל רַחוּם וְחַנּוּן, אֵל קַנָּא. In v14 the same word stands twice: "another *el*" (אֵל אַחֵר) and "a jealous El".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Hew thee" → "Hew for yourself"** (v1, 4) — Hebrew פְּסָל־לְךָ; פָּסַל is "hew, carve" (the root of פֶּסֶל "carved image"). Moses hews these tablets; the first were "the work of *Elohim*" (32:16). **"two tables of stone"** — Hebrew לֻחֹת אֲבָנִים, literally "tablets of stones". **"brakest" → "broke in pieces"** — Hebrew שִׁבֵּר, as 32:19 and v13.
- **"present thyself there to me" → "stand for me there"** (v2) — Hebrew וְנִצַּבְתָּ לִי. **"in the top of the mount" → "on the top of the mountain"** — Hebrew רֹאשׁ "head".
- **"neither let the flocks nor herds feed before that mount" → "also let not the flock and the herd feed in front of that mountain"** (v3) — Hebrew גַּם "also" twice; צֹאן and בָּקָר are collective singulars.
- **"took in his hand the two tables" → "took in his hand two tablets"** (v4) — the Hebrew has no article here.
- **"descended … and stood with him there, and proclaimed the name of the LORD" → "came down … and he took his stand with him there, and he called out the name of Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיִּתְיַצֵּב עִמּוֹ שָׁם וַיִּקְרָא בְשֵׁם יְהוָה. After "Yahweh came down" the subjects are not named again: either Yahweh took his stand with Moses and called out the name (as he said he would, 33:19), or Moses took his stand with him there (33:21; 34:2) and called on the name of Yahweh. The words are those of "call on the name of Yahweh" (Gen 12:8). The English leaves "he" as the Hebrew does.
- **"passed by before him, and proclaimed" → "passed by before his face and called out"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־פָּנָיו, literally "over / before his face" (33:19, 22). The accents divide "and he called out: Yahweh, Yahweh"; without them the words can also be read "and Yahweh called out: Yahweh, El compassionate …".
- **"merciful and gracious" → "compassionate and gracious"** (v6) — Hebrew רַחוּם וְחַנּוּן. רַחוּם is the adjective of רַחֲמִים "compassion" (from רֶחֶם "womb"); חַנּוּן is the adjective of חֵן "favor": one who shows favor (33:19, where the two verbs stand). Both adjectives are said almost always of God.
- **"longsuffering" → "slow to anger"** (v6) — Hebrew אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם, literally "long of nostrils": אַף "nose" is the Hebrew word for anger (32:10–12), and one whose nostrils are "long" is long before his anger burns.
- **"abundant in goodness and truth" → "abounding in *chesed* and truth"** (v6) — Hebrew וְרַב־חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת, literally "and much of chesed and truth". חֶסֶד *chesed* is the love that keeps faith with those to whom one is bound; the word is kept. אֱמֶת is "truth, trustworthiness" (from אָמַן "be firm"); both nouns depend on "abounding".
- **"keeping mercy for thousands" → "guarding *chesed* for thousands"** (v7) — Hebrew נֹצֵר "guard, watch over" (not שָׁמַר "keep"). לָאֲלָפִים "for the thousands" has the article; set beside "the third and the fourth" it may mean thousands of generations (so Deut 7:9, "to a thousand generations").
- **"forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin" kept** (v7) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא "lifting, bearing, taking away": the same verb as "bear" in "bear iniquity"; said of the one wronged it is "forgive". Three nouns, each joined by "and": עָוֺן "iniquity" (crookedness, guilt), פֶּשַׁע "transgression" (rebellion), חַטָּאָה "sin" (missing the mark).
- **"and that will by no means clear the guilty" → "and he will surely not clear"** (v7) — Hebrew וְנַקֵּה לֹא יְנַקֶּה, infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "and clearing he will not clear", that is, he will not hold guiltless, not leave unpunished (the verb of 20:7). The Hebrew has no object: "the guilty" is supplied by KJV. The clause stands directly beside "forgiving", joined by "and".
- **"upon the children, and upon the children's children, unto the third and to the fourth generation" → "upon sons and upon sons' sons, upon the third and upon the fourth generation"** (v7) — Hebrew בָּנִים "sons"; שִׁלֵּשִׁים and רִבֵּעִים are "those of the third / of the fourth generation", with עַל "upon" each time (20:5). פָּקַד is "visit": attend to, here for punishment.
- **"made haste, and bowed his head toward the earth, and worshipped" → "hastened and bowed his head to the earth and bowed down"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיִּקֹּד … וַיִּשְׁתָּחוּ, two verbs.
- **"found grace in thy sight, O Lord, let my Lord" → "found favor in your eyes, Adonai, let Adonai"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי *Adonai* twice, not the name Yahweh; "O" and "my" are not separate words. נָא "please" stands twice.
- **"for it is a stiffnecked people" kept** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי "for" (or "though"); literally "a people hard of neck". **"pardon"** — Hebrew סָלַח, a verb used only of God's forgiving; another word than "forgiving" in v7. **"take us for thine inheritance" → "take us as your inheritance"** — Hebrew וּנְחַלְתָּנוּ, one verb: "and inherit us".
- **"I make a covenant" → "I am cutting a covenant"** (v10, 12, 15, 27) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית, "cut a covenant". The speaker of v10 is not named in the Hebrew.
- **"marvels, such as have not been done" → "wonderful deeds that have not been created"** (v10) — Hebrew נִפְלָאֹת; נִבְרְאוּ is the verb בָּרָא "create". **"a terrible thing" → "fearsome"** — Hebrew נוֹרָא, from יָרֵא "fear".
- **"Observe thou" → "Keep for yourself"** (v11) — Hebrew שְׁמָר־לְךָ. **"I drive out before thee" → "I am driving out from before you"** — Hebrew מִפָּנֶיךָ, literally "from your face". The six peoples are singulars with the article, each joined by "and".
- **"Take heed to thyself" → "Take heed to yourself"** (v12) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֶר לְךָ, literally "keep yourself": the same verb as "Keep" in v11. **"the inhabitants of the land" → "the inhabitant of the land"** (v12, 15) — Hebrew יוֹשֵׁב, singular for the whole people; so "he", "his" in v12–16, beside the plural "they", "their". **"make a covenant with"** — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית לְ, "cut a covenant for": grant terms to. **"whither thou goest" → "upon which you are coming"** — Hebrew בָּא עָלֶיהָ.
- **"But ye shall destroy their altars, break their images, and cut down their groves" → "For their altars you shall break down, and their pillars you shall break in pieces, and his Asherim you shall cut down"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; נָתַץ "break down" (Lev 14:45; Deut 7:5; 12:3) and שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces" are two verbs; the verbs are plural ("you" all). מַצֵּבָה is a stone set up, "pillar". אֲשֵׁרָיו is "his Asherim", with a singular suffix: the plural of אֲשֵׁרָה *Asherah*, a wooden object of worship that bears the name of the goddess Asherah. KJV "groves" follows the Septuagint (from memory).
- **"thou shalt worship no other god" → "you shall not bow down to another *el*"** (v14) — Hebrew לְאֵל אַחֵר, singular.
- **"for the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God" → "for Yahweh, Jealous is his name; a jealous El is he"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי יְהוָה קַנָּא שְׁמוֹ אֵל קַנָּא הוּא, word for word. קַנָּא is an adjective used only of God, from קִנְאָה "jealousy, zeal": one who does not bear a rival.
- **"they go a whoring after their gods" → "they play the prostitute after their gods"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew זָנָה; in v16 also the causative, "make … play the prostitute". The sentence of v15–16 is not finished in the Hebrew: "lest you …, and they …, and you …". **"one call thee" → "he calls you"** — Hebrew וְקָרָא לְךָ, singular.
- **"Thou shalt make thee no molten gods" → "Molten gods you shall not make for yourself"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי מַסֵּכָה, the words of the calf (32:4, 8: עֵגֶל מַסֵּכָה); also "a molten god".
- **"The feast of unleavened bread" → "The Feast of *Matzot*"** (v18) — Hebrew חַג הַמַּצּוֹת; חַג is a pilgrim feast. **"in the time of the month Abib" → "at the appointed time of the month of Aviv"** — Hebrew לְמוֹעֵד; אָבִיב is "the fresh ears" of grain.
- **"All that openeth the matrix is mine" → "Every firstling of the womb is mine"** (v19) — Hebrew פֶּטֶר רֶחֶם, literally "what breaks open the womb".
- **"and every firstling among thy cattle, whether ox or sheep, that is male" → "and all your livestock that is male, the firstling of ox and sheep"** (v19) — Hebrew וְכָל־מִקְנְךָ תִּזָּכָר פֶּטֶר שׁוֹר וָשֶׂה. The form תִּזָּכָר is found only here and is uncertain: as pointed it is a verb, "(that) is born male" (from זָכָר "male") or "shall be remembered" (from זָכַר). The old versions read הַזָּכָר "the male" (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept with the first sense; שֶׂה is one of the flock, sheep or goat.
- **"redeem" → "ransom"; "lamb" → "sheep"** (v20) — Hebrew פָּדָה; שֶׂה.
- **"none shall appear before me empty" → "they shall not appear before my face empty"** (v20) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יֵרָאוּ פָנַי רֵיקָם, literally "and they shall not be seen, my face, empty": the verb is pointed as a passive ("be seen, appear"), and "my face" stands beside it without a preposition. The same letters can be read as an active: "they shall not see my face empty". So also in v23, 24 (יֵרָאֶה … אֶת־פְּנֵי; לֵרָאוֹת אֶת־פְּנֵי).
- **"Six days thou shalt work, but on the seventh day thou shalt rest" → "Six days you shall labor, and on the seventh day you shall cease"** (v21) — Hebrew תַּעֲבֹד (עָבַד "serve, work") and תִּשְׁבֹּת twice (שָׁבַת "cease", the root of *Shabbat*; "rest" is another verb). **"in earing time" → "in plowing"** — Hebrew בֶּחָרִישׁ.
- **"thou shalt observe the feast of weeks" → "a Feast of *Shavuot* you shall make for yourself"** (v22) — Hebrew וְחַג שָׁבֻעֹת תַּעֲשֶׂה לְךָ, without the article; שָׁבֻעֹת is "weeks". The capital letters of the feast names are English only. **"at the year's end" → "at the turn of the year"** — Hebrew תְּקוּפַת הַשָּׁנָה, the coming round of the year.
- **"all your men children" → "all your males"** (v23) — Hebrew זְכוּרְךָ. **"before the Lord GOD, the God of Israel" → "before the face of the Lord Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Israel"** — Hebrew אֶת־פְּנֵי הָאָדֹן יְהוָה: הָאָדוֹן is "the lord, the master" with the article, not the word *Adonai* (as 23:17).
- **"cast out the nations" → "dispossess nations"** (v24) — Hebrew אוֹרִישׁ, the causative of יָרַשׁ "possess"; no article. **"neither shall any man desire thy land" → "no man shall covet your land"** — Hebrew חָמַד, the verb of 20:17.
- **"Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leaven" → "You shall not slaughter the blood of my sacrifice upon what is leavened"** (v25) — Hebrew לֹא־תִשְׁחַט עַל־חָמֵץ דַּם־זִבְחִי, literally so: שָׁחַט is "slaughter", עַל "upon, beside". **"neither shall … be left unto the morning" → "shall not lodge until the morning"** — Hebrew לֹא־יָלִין, "spend the night". **"the feast of the passover" → "the Feast of the *Pesach*"** — Hebrew חַג הַפָּסַח.
- **"of thy land" → "of your ground"** (v26) — Hebrew אַדְמָתְךָ. **"seethe a kid in his mother's milk" → "boil a kid in its mother's milk"** — Hebrew בִּשֵּׁל, גְּדִי.
- **"Write thou these words: for after the tenor of these words" → "Write for yourself these words, for according to these words"** (v27) — Hebrew כְּתָב־לְךָ; עַל־פִּי is literally "upon the mouth of".
- **"he did neither eat bread, nor drink water" → "bread he did not eat, and water he did not drink"** (v28) — the Hebrew order.
- **"he wrote upon the tables"** (v28) — the Hebrew does not name the subject. The nearest subject is Moses (v27 "Write for yourself"); in v1 Yahweh says "I will write on the tablets" (so also Deut 10:4).
- **"the ten commandments" → "the ten words"** (v28) — Hebrew עֲשֶׂרֶת הַדְּבָרִים; דָּבָר is "word", not מִצְוָה "commandment".
- **"wist not that the skin of his face shone while he talked with him" → "did not know that the skin of his face shone in his speaking with him"** (v29) — Hebrew בְּדַבְּרוֹ אִתּוֹ, "in / through his speaking with him": because he had spoken with him, or while he spoke; the two "he / him" are not named.
- **"shone" kept** (v29, 30, 35) — Hebrew קָרַן *qaran*, a verb made from קֶרֶן "horn"; it is found in this stem only in these three verses. Best supported is "sent out rays, shone": קֶרֶן is used of rays of light in Hab 3:4. Taken by the noun alone, the words are "the skin of his face had horns / was horned": so the Latin Vulgate (*cornuta*), from which the horned Moses of medieval and later art comes; the Septuagint has "was glorified" (both from memory). The subject of the verb is "the skin of his face".
- **"were afraid to come nigh him" → "feared to draw near to him"** (v30, 32) — Hebrew וַיִּירְאוּ מִגֶּשֶׁת אֵלָיו; נָגַשׁ "draw near".
- **"all the rulers of the congregation" → "all the chieftains in the congregation"** (v31) — Hebrew הַנְּשִׂאִים בָּעֵדָה.
- **"he gave them in commandment" → "he commanded them"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיְצַוֵּם.
- **"And till Moses had done speaking with them, he put a vail on his face" → "And Moses finished speaking with them, and he put a veil on his face"** (v33) — two narrative clauses; "till" is not in the Hebrew. מַסְוֶה is found only in v33–35 and its exact sense is not certain: a covering for the face. It is another word than the "veil" of the dwelling (פָּרֹכֶת) and the "veil" of a woman (צָעִיף).
- **"he took the vail off … and he came out, and spake" → "he would remove the veil … And he would come out and speak"** (v34, 35) — the Hebrew verbs are forms of repeated action. **"that which he was commanded"** — Hebrew יְצֻוֶּה, a passive.
- **"put the vail upon his face again" → "would return the veil upon his face"** (v35) — Hebrew וְהֵשִׁיב, the causative of שׁוּב "return".

### Notes

- **The attribute formula (v6–7)** — the words are taken up again and again in the Old Testament (Num 14:18; Neh 9:17; Ps 86:15; 103:8; 145:8; Joel 2:13; Jonah 4:2; Nah 1:3).
- **Covenant renewal** — the chapter parallels ch 20–23 in structure, after the golden-calf crisis. Some scholars call v11–26 a "ritual decalogue" beside the ten words of ch 20; the text itself does not number its commands.
- **Shining face of Moses** — Paul uses this in 2 Cor 3:7–18 to contrast the transient glory of Moses' face with the permanent glory of the new covenant.
