# Exodus 13

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Sanctify to me every firstborn, the firstling of every womb among the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, among man and among beast; it is mine."

[3] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to the people, "Remember this day in which you went out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the house of slaves, for by strength of hand ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought you out from here; and what is leavened shall not be eaten.

[4] Today you are going out, in the month of ⟦abib|Aviv|Abib⟧.

[5] And it shall be, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brings you to the land of the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦hivites|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧, which he swore to your fathers to give you, a land flowing with milk and honey, that you shall serve this service in this month.

[6] Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there shall be a feast to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] Unleavened bread shall be eaten the seven days; and what is leavened shall not be seen with you, and leaven shall not be seen with you in all your territory.

[8] And you shall tell your son on that day, saying, 'It is for the sake of this that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did for me when I went out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.'

[9] And it shall be for you a sign on your hand and a remembrance between your eyes, so that the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ may be in your mouth; for with a strong hand ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought you out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[10] And you shall keep this statute at its appointed time, from year to year.

[11] And it shall be, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brings you to the land of the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧, as he swore to you and to your fathers, and gives it to you,

[12] that you shall make every firstling of the womb pass over to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and every firstling, the offspring of a beast, that you have, the males, are ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧.

[13] And every firstling of a donkey you shall ransom with a sheep; and if you do not ransom it, you shall break its neck. And every firstborn of man among your sons you shall ransom.

[14] And it shall be, when your son asks you tomorrow, saying, 'What is this?', that you shall say to him, 'By strength of hand ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought us out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the house of slaves.

[15] And it came to pass, when ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ made it hard to let us go, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ killed every firstborn in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the firstborn of man to the firstborn of beast. Therefore I sacrifice to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ every firstling of the womb, the males, and every firstborn of my sons I ransom.'

[16] And it shall be for a sign on your hand and for frontlets between your eyes; for by strength of hand ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought us out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧."

[17] And it came to pass, when ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ let the people go, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not lead them by the way of the land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, for it was near; for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Lest the people be sorry when they see war, and return to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧."

[18] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ turned the people around by the way of the wilderness to the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧; and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went up in battle array from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[19] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ took the bones of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ with him, for he had surely made the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ swear, saying, "⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will surely visit you, and you shall bring up my bones from here with you."

[20] And they journeyed from ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ and camped in ⟦etham|Etam|Etham⟧, at the edge of the wilderness.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was going before them by day in a pillar of cloud to lead them on the way, and by night in a pillar of fire to give them light, to go by day and by night.

[22] The pillar of cloud by day and the pillar of fire by night did not depart from before the people.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17, 18, 19) — Hebrew *Elohim*. The chapter has Yahweh in v1–16, 21 and *Elohim* in v17–19.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 18, 19) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"Sanctify" kept** (v2) — Hebrew קַדֶּשׁ־לִי, the causative of "be holy": set apart as holy to me.
- **"whatsoever openeth the womb" / "all that openeth the matrix" → "the firstling of every womb" / "every firstling of the womb"** (v2, 12, 13, 15) — Hebrew פֶּטֶר רֶחֶם *peter rechem*. פֶּטֶר is a noun from פָּטַר "set free, open": literally "what breaks open the womb", the first young of a mother. One rendering, "firstling", stands for the noun in all four verses, also where it stands without "womb" ("every firstling, the offspring of a beast", v12; "every firstling of a donkey", v13). "Firstborn" (v2, 13, 15) is another word, בְּכוֹר. In v2 the Hebrew is "the firstling of every womb".
- **"both of man and of beast" → "among man and among beast"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּאָדָם וּבַבְּהֵמָה, literally "in man and in beast".
- **"the house of bondage" → "the house of slaves"** (v3, 14) — Hebrew בֵּית עֲבָדִים, a plural: "slaves". **"came out" → "went out"** (v3, 8) — Hebrew יָצָא; "brought out" (v3, 9, 14, 16) is its causative.
- **"by strength of hand" kept** (v3, 14, 16) — Hebrew בְּחֹזֶק יָד; in v9 the Hebrew has בְּיָד חֲזָקָה "with a strong hand". Both are from חָזַק "be strong", the verb used of Pharaoh's heart (11:10; 14:4).
- **"there shall no leavened bread be eaten" → "what is leavened shall not be eaten"** (v3, 7) — Hebrew חָמֵץ, what is leavened; "leaven" in v7 is שְׂאֹר, the sourdough. "Bread" is not in either word. "Territory" (v7) is גְּבוּל, "border" and the land within it.
- **"Abib" → "Aviv"** (v4) — Hebrew הָאָבִיב, with the article: "the *aviv*", the fresh ears of grain (9:31), the month in spring when the barley is in the ear. The first month (12:2), later called Nisan.
- **"the Canaanites, and the Hittites …" → "the Canaanite and the Hittite …"** (v5, 11) — the Hebrew names are singular, each with the article and "and". The Masoretic Text names five peoples here; the Samaritan text and the Greek have more (cited from memory).
- **"keep this service" → "serve this service"** (v5) — Hebrew וְעָבַדְתָּ אֶת־הָעֲבֹדָה, verb and noun of one root.
- **"a feast"** (v6) — Hebrew חַג, as in 12:14; "there shall be" is supplied.
- **"shew thy son" → "tell your son"** (v8) — Hebrew הִגִּיד.
- **"This is done because of that which the LORD did unto me" → "It is for the sake of this that Yahweh did for me"** (v8) — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר זֶה עָשָׂה יְהוָה לִי, literally "for the sake of this Yahweh did for me". Two ways of taking it: (1) "it is because of this that Yahweh did for me", that is, because of what Yahweh did; (2) "for the sake of this (this service) Yahweh acted for me". "It is" and "that" are supplied; the English leaves both open.
- **"a memorial" → "a remembrance"** (v9) — Hebrew זִכָּרוֹן, as in 12:14.
- **"the LORD's law" → "the Torah of Yahweh"** (v9) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת יְהוָה; תּוֹרָה is "instruction, teaching, law".
- **"ordinance in his season" → "statute at its appointed time"** (v10) — Hebrew חֻקָּה and מוֹעֵד. **"from year to year"** — Hebrew מִיָּמִים יָמִימָה, literally "from days to days".
- **"set apart unto the LORD" → "make … pass over to Yahweh"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהַעֲבַרְתָּ, the causative of עָבַר "pass over": hand over. **"every firstling that cometh of a beast" → "every firstling, the offspring of a beast"** — Hebrew פֶּטֶר שֶׁגֶר בְּהֵמָה; שֶׁגֶר is what an animal drops, its young. "Are Yahweh's" is literally "to Yahweh".
- **"redeem with a lamb" → "ransom with a sheep"** (v13) — Hebrew פָּדָה, to free by paying a price; "redeem" is kept for גָּאַל (15:13). שֶׂה is one animal of the flock, a sheep or a goat (12:3–5). **"the firstborn of man among thy children" → "every firstborn of man among your sons"** — Hebrew בְּכוֹר אָדָם בְּבָנֶיךָ.
- **"in time to come" → "tomorrow"** (v14) — Hebrew מָחָר, "tomorrow": at a later day.
- **"when Pharaoh would hardly let us go" → "when Pharaoh made it hard to let us go"** (v15) — Hebrew הִקְשָׁה פַרְעֹה לְשַׁלְּחֵנוּ, literally "Pharaoh made hard to let us go"; no object is named ("it" is supplied). This is the third of the three verbs used of Pharaoh's stubbornness, קָשָׁה "be hard" (7:3); the other two are "be strong" and "be heavy". **"slew" → "killed"** — Hebrew הָרַג.
- **"being males" → "the males"** (v12, 15) — Hebrew הַזְּכָרִים. **"I redeem" → "I ransom"** (v15).
- **"frontlets between thine eyes"** (v16) — Hebrew טוֹטָפֹת, found only here and in Deut 6:8; 11:18; its meaning is uncertain (a band or mark on the forehead). In v9 the same place has "a remembrance". Jewish practice takes v9 and v16 as the command of the *tefillin*. "On your hand" has an unusual long form of "your" (יָדְכָה).
- **"although that was near" → "for it was near"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי קָרוֹב הוּא; כִּי is "for, because", and is also taken as "although" here. The next clause begins with the same word.
- **"Lest peradventure the people repent" → "Lest the people be sorry"** (v17) — Hebrew יִנָּחֵם, to be sorry, to change one's mind; "led" in the same verse is נָחָם, from another verb (נָחָה) with the same letters.
- **"led the people about, through the way of the wilderness of the Red sea" → "turned the people around by the way of the wilderness to the Sea of Reeds"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיַּסֵּב … דֶּרֶךְ הַמִּדְבָּר יַם־סוּף. There is no word for "to" or "of": "the way of the wilderness, the Sea of Reeds". יַם־סוּף is "sea of reeds" (סוּף, the reeds of 2:3, 5); "Red Sea" comes from the Greek.
- **"harnessed" → "in battle array"** (v18) — Hebrew חֲמֻשִׁים, found four times (Josh 1:14; 4:12; Judg 7:11); the meaning is uncertain: "armed", "in battle array", or "in fifties / by fives" (from חָמֵשׁ "five").
- **"he had straitly sworn the children of Israel" → "he had surely made the sons of Israel swear"** (v19) — Hebrew הַשְׁבֵּעַ הִשְׁבִּיעַ, an infinitive absolute with its verb, as is פָּקֹד יִפְקֹד "will surely visit"; the words are those of Gen 50:25. The Hebrew does not repeat Joseph's name. "Visit" is פָּקַד, to attend to.
- **"took their journey" → "journeyed"; "encamped" → "camped"** (v20) — Hebrew נָסַע and חָנָה.
- **"went before them" → "was going before them"** (v21) — a participle. **"to go by day and night" → "to go by day and by night"** — an infinitive: so that they might go.
- **"He took not away the pillar" → "The pillar … did not depart"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹא־יָמִישׁ, an imperfect: it would not depart; the form can also be read "he would not remove". עַמּוּד is a standing column.

### Notes

- **Firstborn redemption** — Yahweh's claim on every firstborn is both commemorative (remembering the Egyptian firstborn) and theological (firstborn status as token of the whole). Human firstborn ransomed (not sacrificed); unclean-animal firstborn (donkey) ransomed or killed (not offered); clean-animal firstborn given to Yahweh.
- **Memory-shaping language** — the chapter is heavy with memorial / catechetical language: "remember this day" (v3), "tell your son" (v8, 14), "a sign on your hand" (v9, 16). The exodus is to become a perpetual identity-shaping memory.
- **"the way of the land of the Philistines" (v17)** — the coastal road, the shortest way to Canaan.
- **"pillar of cloud … pillar of fire" (v21–22)** — the presence of Yahweh by day and by night (Num 9:15–23; cf. 1 Kgs 8:10–11).
- **Divine presence** — the chapter transitions from deliverance (Passover) to journey (wilderness), and the pillar-theophany opens Israel's relationship with Yahweh as ever-present traveling-companion.
