# Exodus 38

[1] And he made the altar of the burnt offering of acacia wood: five cubits its length and five cubits its width, square, and three cubits its height.

[2] And he made its horns on its four corners; its horns were from it; and he overlaid it with bronze.

[3] And he made all the utensils of the altar: the pots and the shovels and the bowls, the forks and the firepans; all its utensils he made of bronze.

[4] And he made for the altar a grating, the work of a net, of bronze, under its ledge, from below, up to its half.

[5] And he cast four rings on the four ends of the grating of bronze, housings for the poles.

[6] And he made the poles of acacia wood, and he overlaid them with bronze.

[7] And he brought the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar, to carry it with them; hollow, of planks, he made it.

[8] And he made the basin of bronze and its stand of bronze, with the mirrors of the mustering women who mustered at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[9] And he made the court: for the south side, southward, the hangings of the court, of fine twined linen, a hundred cubits;

[10] their pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands, of silver.

[11] And for the north side a hundred cubits, their pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands, of silver.

[12] And for the west side hangings of fifty cubits, their pillars ten and their sockets ten; the hooks of the pillars and their bands, of silver.

[13] And for the east side, eastward, fifty cubits.

[14] Hangings of fifteen cubits for the shoulder, their pillars three and their sockets three.

[15] And for the second shoulder, on this side and on that side of the gate of the court, hangings of fifteen cubits, their pillars three and their sockets three.

[16] All the hangings of the court around were of fine twined linen.

[17] And the sockets for the pillars were of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands, of silver; and the overlay of their tops, of silver; and they were banded with silver, all the pillars of the court.

[18] And the screen of the gate of the court was the work of an embroiderer, of blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen; and twenty cubits the length, and the height in the width five cubits, alongside the hangings of the court.

[19] And their pillars four and their sockets four, of bronze; their hooks of silver, and the overlay of their tops and their bands, of silver.

[20] And all the pegs for the dwelling and for the court around were of bronze.

[21] These are the numberings of the dwelling, the dwelling of the testimony, which were numbered at the mouth of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧: the service of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, by the hand of ⟦ithamar|Itamar|Ithamar⟧ the son of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest.

[22] And ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ the son of Uri, the son of ⟦hur|Chur|Hur⟧, of the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, made all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧;

[23] and with him ⟦oholiab|Oholiav|Oholiab⟧ the son of ⟦ahisamach|Achisamach|Ahisamach⟧, of the tribe of Dan, a craftsman and a designer, and an embroiderer in the blue and in the purple and in the scarlet and in the fine linen.

[24] All the gold that was worked for the work, in all the work of the Holy: the gold of the wave offering was twenty-nine talents and seven hundred and thirty shekels, by the holy shekel.

[25] And the silver of those of the congregation who were numbered was a hundred talents and one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels, by the holy shekel:

[26] ⟦beka|a beqa|a beka⟧ a head, half the shekel, by the holy shekel, for everyone who passed over to those who are numbered, from twenty years old and upward, for six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty.

[27] And the hundred talents of silver were for casting the sockets of the Holy and the sockets of the veil: a hundred sockets for the hundred talents, a talent for a socket.

[28] And of the one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five he made hooks for the pillars, and he overlaid their tops and banded them.

[29] And the bronze of the wave offering was seventy talents and two thousand four hundred shekels.

[30] And he made with it the sockets of the entrance of the tent of meeting, and the altar of bronze and the grating of bronze that belongs to it, and all the utensils of the altar,

[31] and the sockets of the court around, and the sockets of the gate of the court, and all the pegs of the dwelling, and all the pegs of the court around.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the altar of burnt offering of shittim wood" → "the altar of the burnt offering of acacia wood"** (v1) — Hebrew מִזְבַּח הָעֹלָה, with the article; עֲצֵי שִׁטִּים, literally "woods of acacias". "Square" stands alone (רָבוּעַ). The command is 27:1–8.
- **"the horns thereof were of the same" → "its horns were from it"** (v2) — Hebrew מִמֶּנּוּ הָיוּ: of one piece with it. **"brass" → "bronze"** (throughout) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת.
- **"vessels" → "utensils"; "basons" → "bowls"; "fleshhooks" → "forks"** (v3) — Hebrew כֵּלִים (any made articles), מִזְרָקֹת (bowls for dashing the blood), מִזְלָגֹת.
- **"a brasen grate of network under the compass thereof beneath unto the midst of it" → "a grating, the work of a net, of bronze, under its ledge, from below, up to its half"** (v4) — Hebrew מִכְבָּר מַעֲשֵׂה רֶשֶׁת (רֶשֶׁת is the word "net"); כַּרְכֹּב is found only here and in 27:5, and its meaning is uncertain.
- **"to be places for the staves" → "housings for the poles"** (v5) — Hebrew בָּתִּים, literally "houses".
- **"to bear it withal; he made the altar hollow with boards" → "to carry it with them; hollow, of planks, he made it"** (v7) — Hebrew נְבוּב לֻחֹת; לוּחַ is the "tablet" of other places.
- **"laver … foot" → "basin … stand"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּיּוֹר, כֵּן.
- **"of the lookingglasses of the women assembling, which assembled at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "with the mirrors of the mustering women who mustered at the entrance of the tent of meeting"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּמַרְאֹת הַצֹּבְאֹת אֲשֶׁר צָבְאוּ. The verb is צָבָא, the verb of צָבָא "host": to come together as a host, to do service in ordered ranks. It is used of going to war and of the service of the Levites at the tent (Num 4:23; 8:24), and of the women at the entrance of the tent of meeting in 1 Sam 2:22 (הַנָּשִׁים הַצֹּבְאוֹת). Here the noun "women" is not written: it lies in the feminine form of the participle. What their service was the text does not say; "ministering" and "serving" are other Hebrew verbs. מַרְאֹת are mirrors (of polished metal); בְּ is "with" or "of". The tent of meeting has not yet been raised up at this point of the account (40:17).
- **"on the south side southward" → "for the south side, southward"** (v9) — Hebrew לִפְאַת נֶגֶב תֵּימָנָה; פֵּאָה is literally "edge", and the two words for "south" are "Negev" and "Teman". "West" (v12) is יָם "sea"; "east, eastward" (v13) is קֵדְמָה מִזְרָחָה, "toward the front, toward the sunrise". **"an hundred cubits"** — literally "a hundred by the cubit".
- **"fillets" → "bands"; "filleted" → "banded"** (v10–12, 17, 19, 28) — Hebrew חֲשׁוּקִים, מְחֻשָּׁקִים: uncertain, a ring or a rod joining the pillars (root "be attached").
- **"for the one side of the gate" → "for the shoulder"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew כָּתֵף "shoulder": the stretch beside the gate. **"on this hand and that hand"** → "on this side and on that side" (v15) — Hebrew מִזֶּה וּמִזֶּה.
- **"the overlaying of their chapiters" → "the overlay of their tops"** (v17, 19, 28) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁיהֶם, literally "their heads".
- **"needlework" → "the work of an embroiderer"** (v18) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה רֹקֵם. "Blue and purple and scarlet" are the dyes and stand for wool dyed with them. **"the height in the breadth was five cubits, answerable to the hangings" → "the height in the width five cubits, alongside the hangings"** — Hebrew וְקוֹמָה בְרֹחַב … לְעֻמַּת: the height of the screen is the width of its cloth, and it answers to the hangings of the court, which are five cubits high (27:18). The same word stands in 37:14 and 39:20.
- **"pins" → "pegs"** (v20, 31) — Hebrew יְתֵדֹת.
- **"This is the sum of the tabernacle, even of the tabernacle of testimony, as it was counted, according to the commandment of Moses" → "These are the numberings of the dwelling, the dwelling of the testimony, which were numbered at the mouth of Moses"** (v21) — Hebrew פְּקוּדֵי … אֲשֶׁר פֻּקַּד, noun and verb of one root (פָּקַד "number, muster"; the verb is singular); עַל־פִּי "at the mouth of". **"for the service of the Levites" → "the service of the Levites"** — the Hebrew has no "for": the numbering was the Levites' service.
- **"an engraver, and a cunning workman, and an embroiderer" → "a craftsman and a designer, and an embroiderer"** (v23) — Hebrew חָרָשׁ וְחֹשֵׁב וְרֹקֵם; the last two are the words of "the work of a designer" and "the work of an embroiderer".
- **"All the gold that was occupied for the work in all the work of the holy place" → "All the gold that was worked for the work, in all the work of the Holy"** (v24) — Hebrew הֶעָשׂוּי, the passive of "make"; הַקֹּדֶשׁ "the Holy": the sanctuary ("place" is not in the Hebrew). The sentence begins again with "and the gold … was" (וַיְהִי). **"the gold of the offering" → "the gold of the wave offering"** (v24, 29) — Hebrew תְּנוּפָה, what is "waved" before Yahweh (35:22).
- **"talents … shekels" kept; "after the shekel of the sanctuary" → "by the holy shekel"** (v24–29) — Hebrew כִּכָּר, literally "a round" (a round weight); שֶׁקֶל is a weight, not a coin; שֶׁקֶל הַקֹּדֶשׁ is literally "the shekel of holiness" or "the shekel of the Holy", the sanctuary's weight. The numbers are in words, in ordinary English order; the Hebrew has "nine and twenty talents", "a thousand and seven hundred and five and seventy".
- **"the silver of them that were numbered of the congregation" → "the silver of those of the congregation who were numbered"** (v25) — Hebrew פְּקוּדֵי הָעֵדָה; the command is 30:11–16.
- **"A bekah for every man, that is, half a shekel" → "a *beqa* a head, half the shekel"** (v26) — Hebrew בֶּקַע, from בָּקַע "split": a half. לַגֻּלְגֹּלֶת is literally "for the skull". "That is" and "men" are not in the Hebrew. **"every one that went to be numbered" → "everyone who passed over to those who are numbered"** — Hebrew הָעֹבֵר עַל־הַפְּקֻדִים. **"from twenty years old"** — literally "from a son of twenty years". At a half shekel each, the six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty give three hundred and one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five shekels: so the talent has three thousand shekels.
- **"the sockets of the sanctuary" → "the sockets of the Holy"** (v27) — Hebrew אַדְנֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ. "Casting" is יָצַק, "pour".
- **"the brasen altar, and the brasen grate for it" → "the altar of bronze and the grating of bronze that belongs to it"** (v30) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ, as 35:16 and 39:39.

### Notes

- **Parallels** — the altar (v1–7) is commanded in 27:1–8, the basin (v8) in 30:17–21, the court (v9–20) in 27:9–19. The mirrors of v8 are not named in the command.
- **Inventory (v21–31)** — the accounting of tabernacle materials. Around 30 talents of gold, 100 talents of silver, 70 talents of bronze — representing over 1 ton of gold, 3 tons of silver, 2 tons of bronze. Massive material offering.
- **Women's mirrors become the priestly washing basin** — gendered piety contribution. Later Talmudic tradition tenderizes this detail: Moses had initially resisted using mirrors (thinking them tainted by vanity), but Yahweh insisted because they had been used by Hebrew women to beautify themselves for their husbands in Egypt, sustaining family life under oppression.
