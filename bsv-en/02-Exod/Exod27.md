# Exodus 27

[1] "And you shall make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits the length and five cubits the width; the altar shall be square; and three cubits its height.

[2] And you shall make its horns on its four corners; its horns shall be from it; and you shall overlay it with bronze.

[3] And you shall make its pots to clear it of fat ashes, and its shovels and its bowls and its forks and its firepans; all its utensils you shall make of bronze.

[4] And you shall make for it a grating, the work of a net, of bronze; and you shall make on the net four rings of bronze on its four ends.

[5] And you shall place it under the ledge of the altar, from below, and the net shall be up to the half of the altar.

[6] And you shall make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with bronze.

[7] And its poles shall be brought into the rings, and the poles shall be on the two sides of the altar when it is carried.

[8] Hollow, of planks, you shall make it; as he showed you on the mountain, so they shall make.

[9] And you shall make the court of the dwelling: for the south side, southward, hangings for the court, of fine twined linen, a hundred cubits the length for the one side;

[10] and its pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands, of silver.

[11] And so for the north side in length: hangings a hundred in length, and its pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands, of silver.

[12] And the width of the court for the west side: hangings of fifty cubits, their pillars ten and their sockets ten.

[13] And the width of the court for the east side, eastward, fifty cubits.

[14] And fifteen cubits of hangings for the shoulder, their pillars three and their sockets three.

[15] And for the second shoulder fifteen of hangings, their pillars three and their sockets three.

[16] And for the gate of the court a screen of twenty cubits, of blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen, the work of an embroiderer, their pillars four and their sockets four.

[17] All the pillars of the court around shall be banded with silver, their hooks of silver and their sockets of bronze.

[18] The length of the court shall be a hundred cubits, and the width fifty by fifty, and the height five cubits, of fine twined linen, and their sockets of bronze.

[19] All the utensils of the dwelling in all its service, and all its pegs and all the pegs of the court, shall be of bronze.

[20] And you, you shall command the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that they take to you pure oil of olives, beaten, for the light, to set up a lamp continually.

[21] In the tent of meeting, outside the veil that is over the testimony, ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall arrange it from evening until morning before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: an everlasting statute for their generations, from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v21) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. The speech runs on from 25:1.
- **"an altar" → "the altar"** (v1) — the Hebrew has the article. **"foursquare" → "square"** — Hebrew רָבוּעַ.
- **"his horns shall be of the same" → "its horns shall be from it"** (v2) — Hebrew מִמֶּנּוּ "from it": of one piece with the altar, not fastened on. The horns (קַרְנֹת) are projections at the four corners (1 Kgs 1:50; Lev 16:18).
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v2, 3, 4, 6, 10, 11, 17, 18, 19) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת.
- **"pans to receive his ashes" → "pots to clear it of fat ashes"** (v3) — Hebrew לְדַשְּׁנוֹ, a verb from דֶּשֶׁן "fatness": the ashes soaked with the fat of the sacrifices; literally "to de-fat it". **"basons" → "bowls"** — Hebrew מִזְרָקֹת, from זָרַק "dash": the bowls from which the blood is dashed. **"fleshhooks" → "forks"** — Hebrew מִזְלָגֹת; "flesh" is not in the word. **"vessels" → "utensils"** (v3, 19) — Hebrew כְּלִי, any made article; literally "for all its utensils you shall make bronze".
- **"a grate of network" → "a grating, the work of a net"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew מִכְבָּר מַעֲשֵׂה רֶשֶׁת; רֶשֶׁת is the word "net": a mesh of bronze. **"corners" → "ends"** (v4) — Hebrew קְצוֹת, another word than "corners" (פִּנֹּת) in v2.
- **"compass" → "ledge"** (v5) — Hebrew כַּרְכֹּב, found only here and in 38:4; the meaning is uncertain (a rim or ledge running round the altar).
- **"staves" → "poles"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew בַּדִּים. **"to bear it" → "when it is carried"** (v7) — Hebrew בִּשְׂאֵת אֹתוֹ, "in the carrying of it".
- **"Hollow with boards" → "Hollow, of planks"** (v8) — Hebrew נְבוּב לֻחֹת; לוּחַ is elsewhere a "tablet". Not the word for the "boards" of the dwelling (קֶרֶשׁ, 26:15). **"as it was shewed thee" → "as he showed you"** — Hebrew הֶרְאָה אֹתְךָ, an active verb with no subject named.
- **"court"** (v9–19) — Hebrew חָצֵר, an enclosed yard. **"hangings"** — Hebrew קְלָעִים.
- **"for the south side southward"** (v9) — Hebrew לִפְאַת נֶגֶב־תֵּימָנָה, two words for south, as in 26:18. "West" (v12) is literally "sea"; "east side eastward" (v13) is קֵדְמָה מִזְרָחָה, "to the front, toward the sunrise".
- **"fillets" → "bands"** (v10, 11, 17) — Hebrew חֲשֻׁקִים, from a root "be attached": rings round the pillars or rods joining them; the meaning is uncertain.
- **"its pillars"** (v11) — the written text has ועמדו, the reading tradition וְעַמּוּדָיו; the sense is the same.
- **"on the one side of the gate" → "for the shoulder"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew כָּתֵף "shoulder": the stretch of the east side on each side of the gate. "Of the gate" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"hanging" → "screen"** (v16) — Hebrew מָסָךְ, as in 26:36. **"wrought with needlework" → "the work of an embroiderer"** — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה רֹקֵם; see 26:36. "Blue and purple and scarlet" are the dyes, standing for the dyed wool.
- **"filleted with silver" → "banded with silver"** (v17) — Hebrew מְחֻשָּׁקִים, the verb of the "bands".
- **"fifty every where" → "fifty by fifty"** (v18) — Hebrew חֲמִשִּׁים בַּחֲמִשִּׁים, word for word; what is meant is not certain (fifty at each end). "Hangings" is not in this verse: "of fine twined linen" stands alone.
- **"pins" → "pegs"** (v19) — Hebrew יְתֵדֹת, tent pegs. The verse begins with לְכֹל "as for all"; "shall be" is supplied.
- **"that they bring thee" → "that they take to you"** (v20) — Hebrew וְיִקְחוּ אֵלֶיךָ, the verb לָקַח "take". "And you" is emphatic (וְאַתָּה).
- **"pure oil olive beaten" → "pure oil of olives, beaten"** (v20) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן זַיִת זָךְ כָּתִית: oil from olives crushed in a mortar, not pressed.
- **"to cause the lamp to burn always" → "to set up a lamp continually"** (v20) — Hebrew לְהַעֲלֹת נֵר תָּמִיד, literally "to make a lamp go up": to set it up on the lampstand, or to make its flame go up (to light it). תָּמִיד is "continually, regularly"; v21 says "from evening until morning".
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (v21) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד, the tent of the appointed meeting (25:22; 29:42).
- **"which is before the testimony" → "that is over the testimony"** (v21) — Hebrew עַל־הָעֵדֻת "over, by": the veil hangs in front of the ark in which the testimony lies.
- **"shall order it" → "shall arrange it"** (v21) — Hebrew עָרַךְ "set in order, arrange".
- **"a statute for ever unto their generations on the behalf of the children of Israel" → "an everlasting statute for their generations, from the sons of Israel"** (v21) — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age"; מֵאֵת is "from": a due coming from the sons (בְּנֵי) of Israel.

### Notes

- **The bronze altar of sacrifice** — the altar of the outer court, for burnt offerings and the other sacrifices; the golden altar of incense stands inside (30:1–10).
- **Gradation by material** — bronze (court, altar) → silver (hooks and bands of the pillars, sockets of the boards) → gold (the furnishings inside).
- **The lamp** (v20–21) — Aaron and his sons arrange it from evening until morning. Later Jewish practice keeps a *ner tamid* ("continual lamp") in every synagogue.
