# Exodus 40

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "On the day of the first month, on the first of the month, you shall raise up the dwelling of the tent of meeting.

[3] And you shall put there the ark of the testimony, and you shall screen the ark with the veil.

[4] And you shall bring in the table and arrange its arrangement, and you shall bring in the lampstand and set up its lamps.

[5] And you shall place the altar of gold for incense before the ark of the testimony, and you shall put the screen of the entrance to the dwelling.

[6] And you shall place the altar of the burnt offering before the entrance of the dwelling of the tent of meeting.

[7] And you shall place the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and you shall place water there.

[8] And you shall put the court around, and you shall place the screen of the gate of the court.

[9] And you shall take the oil of anointing and anoint the dwelling and all that is in it, and you shall sanctify it and all its utensils, and it shall be holy.

[10] And you shall anoint the altar of the burnt offering and all its utensils, and you shall sanctify the altar, and the altar shall be most holy.

[11] And you shall anoint the basin and its stand, and you shall sanctify it.

[12] And you shall bring near ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting, and you shall wash them with water.

[13] And you shall clothe ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ with the holy garments, and you shall anoint him and sanctify him, and he shall be priest to me.

[14] And his sons you shall bring near, and you shall clothe them with tunics.

[15] And you shall anoint them as you anointed their father, and they shall be priests to me. And their anointing shall be to them for an everlasting priesthood, for their generations."

[16] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ did; according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded him, so he did.

[17] And it came to pass in the first month, in the second year, on the first of the month, that the dwelling was raised up.

[18] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ raised up the dwelling, and he placed its sockets and put its boards and placed its bars, and he raised up its pillars.

[19] And he spread the tent over the dwelling and put the covering of the tent on it above, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[20] And he took and placed the testimony into the ark, and he put the poles on the ark, and he placed the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ on the ark above.

[21] And he brought the ark into the dwelling and put the veil of the screen and screened the ark of the testimony, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[22] And he placed the table in the tent of meeting, on the flank of the dwelling northward, outside the veil.

[23] And he arranged on it an arrangement of bread before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[24] And he put the lampstand in the tent of meeting, opposite the table, on the flank of the dwelling southward.

[25] And he set up the lamps before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[26] And he put the altar of gold in the tent of meeting, before the veil.

[27] And he burned on it fragrant incense, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[28] And he put the screen of the entrance to the dwelling.

[29] And the altar of the burnt offering he put at the entrance of the dwelling of the tent of meeting, and he offered up on it the burnt offering and the grain offering, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[30] And he put the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and he placed water there for washing.

[31] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons would wash from it their hands and their feet.

[32] When they went into the tent of meeting and when they came near to the altar, they would wash, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[33] And he raised up the court around the dwelling and the altar, and he placed the screen of the gate of the court. And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ finished the work.

[34] And the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ filled the dwelling.

[35] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ was not able to go into the tent of meeting, for the cloud abode on it, and the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ filled the dwelling.

[36] And when the cloud was taken up from over the dwelling, the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would set out, in all their journeys.

[37] And if the cloud was not taken up, then they would not set out until the day that it was taken up.

[38] For the cloud of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was on the dwelling by day, and fire would be in it by night, before the eyes of all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, in all their journeys.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"On the first day of the first month" → "On the day of the first month, on the first of the month"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם־הַחֹדֶשׁ הָרִאשׁוֹן בְּאֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ, both phrases kept. In v17 "on the first of the month" again; the word "day" is not there.
- **"set up the tabernacle" → "raise up the dwelling"** (v2, 17, 18, 33) — Hebrew הֵקִים, literally "make stand" (in v17 the passive, הוּקַם); one rendering for the dwelling, its pillars and the court. מִשְׁכָּן is "dwelling", from שָׁכַן "abide" (v35). Here it is "the dwelling of the tent of meeting" (v2, 6, 29).
- **"tent of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"put therein" → "put there"; "set" → "place"** (v3–8, 18–33) — two Hebrew verbs alternate: שִׂים "put" and נָתַן, literally "give" ("place"). Of the water (v7, 30) נָתַן is "place" too, as in the command (30:18). In v5, 28 and v8, 33 the screens are "put" and "placed"; the Hebrew does not say "hang".
- **"cover the ark with the vail" → "screen the ark with the veil"** (v3, 21) — Hebrew סָכַךְ עַל, literally "screen over the ark"; the same root as מָסָךְ "screen" (v5, 8, 21, 28, 33). In v21 "the veil of the screen" (פָּרֹכֶת הַמָּסָךְ).
- **"set in order the things that are to be set in order upon it" → "arrange its arrangement"** (v4, 23) — Hebrew וְעָרַכְתָּ אֶת־עֶרְכּוֹ, verb and noun of one root; in v23 "an arrangement of bread".
- **"light the lamps" → "set up its lamps"** (v4, 25) — Hebrew הֶעֱלָה, literally "make go up": to set the lamps up on the lampstand, or to make their flame go up (light them). The same verb is "offered up" in v29.
- **"the altar of gold for the incense" → "the altar of gold for incense"** (v5) — Hebrew לִקְטֹרֶת, without the article.
- **"the hanging of the door to the tabernacle" → "the screen of the entrance to the dwelling"** (v5, 28) — Hebrew מָסַךְ הַפֶּתַח לַמִּשְׁכָּן; פֶּתַח is the opening, not a door.
- **"laver" → "basin"; "his foot" → "its stand"** (v7, 11, 30) — Hebrew כִּיּוֹר, כֵּן.
- **"the anointing oil" → "the oil of anointing"** (v9) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה.
- **"hallow" / "sanctify" → "sanctify"** (v9, 10, 11, 13) — one Hebrew verb, קִדֵּשׁ.
- **"all the vessels thereof" → "all its utensils"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew כֵּלִים, any made articles: the furniture of the dwelling and the tools of the altar.
- **"it shall be holy" kept; "an altar most holy" → "most holy"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ is a noun, "holiness"; "most holy" is literally "holiness of holinesses" (קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים). "The holy garments" (v13) is literally "the garments of holiness".
- **"bring" → "bring near"** (v12, 14) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב, the causative of "come near" (v32).
- **"that he may minister unto me in the priest's office" → "and he shall be priest to me"** (v13, 15) — Hebrew וְכִהֵן לִי, a verb made from כֹּהֵן "priest", joined by "and". "Minister" and "office" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"coats" → "tunics"** (v14) — Hebrew כֻּתֳּנֹת.
- **"their anointing shall surely be an everlasting priesthood" → "their anointing shall be to them for an everlasting priesthood"** (v15) — Hebrew וְהָיְתָה לִהְיֹת לָהֶם מָשְׁחָתָם לִכְהֻנַּת עוֹלָם, literally "and it shall be, to be to them, their anointing, for a priesthood of the age"; the doubled "be" cannot be kept in English. "Everlasting" is the noun עוֹלָם, time out of sight. **"throughout their generations" → "for their generations"** — Hebrew לְדֹרֹתָם.
- **"Thus did Moses" → "And Moses did"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ מֹשֶׁה; no "thus".
- **"it came to pass" kept** (v17) — Hebrew וַיְהִי.
- **"fastened his sockets, and set up the boards thereof, and put in the bars thereof, and reared up his pillars" → "placed its sockets and put its boards and placed its bars, and he raised up its pillars"** (v18) — the four Hebrew verbs are נָתַן, שִׂים, נָתַן, הֵקִים.
- **"spread abroad the tent" → "spread the tent"** (v19) — Hebrew פָּרַשׂ. **"as the LORD commanded Moses"** (v19, 21, 23, 25, 27, 29, 32) — seven times in the setting up, each time without a joining word before it.
- **"he took and put the testimony" → "he took and placed the testimony"** (v20) — two verbs with one object, as in the Hebrew.
- **"mercy seat" → "*kapporet*"** (v20) — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת, the cover of the ark; from כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (root "cover"): "atonement cover". "Seat" is not in the word; "mercy seat" comes from Luther's *Gnadenstuhl* through Tyndale.
- **"upon the side of the tabernacle northward" → "on the flank of the dwelling northward"** (v22, 24) — Hebrew יֶרֶךְ, literally "thigh"; the command (26:35) has צֶלַע "side". "Southward" is נֶגְבָּה, "toward the Negev".
- **"set the bread in order" → "arranged on it an arrangement of bread"** (v23) — see v4.
- **"sweet incense" → "fragrant incense"** (v27) — Hebrew קְטֹרֶת סַמִּים, "incense of fragrant substances"; הִקְטִיר, "burn incense" here, is literally "turn into smoke": the same verb is said of what is laid on the altar; plain "burn" is another verb (שָׂרַף).
- **"by the door of" → "at the entrance of"** (v29) — the Hebrew has פֶּתַח without a preposition. **"offered upon it" → "offered up on it"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַל … הָעֹלָה, verb and noun of one root ("made go up the going-up"). **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה.
- **"to wash withal" → "for washing"** (v30) — Hebrew לְרָחְצָה. "There" is שָׁמָּה, "to there".
- **"washed" → "would wash"** (v31, 32) — the Hebrew forms (וְרָחֲצוּ, יִרְחָצוּ) tell what was done each time, not once. "From it" is מִמֶּנּוּ: with water taken from the basin.
- **"So Moses finished the work" → "And Moses finished the work"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיְכַל; the same verb and noun as Gen 2:2.
- **"Then a cloud covered" → "And the cloud covered"** (v34) — Hebrew וַיְכַס הֶעָנָן, with the article.
- **"because the cloud abode thereon" → "for the cloud abode on it"** (v35) — Hebrew שָׁכַן, the verb from which מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling" is made. "Filled" (מָלֵא) is the same form in v34 and v35.
- **"went onward" → "would set out"** (v36, 37) — Hebrew נָסַע, literally "pull up" (the tent pegs); "journeys" (מַסְעֵיהֶם) is its noun. The verbs of v36–38 tell what happened each time. **"was taken up"** — Hebrew נַעֲלָה, the passive of "go up"; מֵעַל is "from upon".
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v36) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"fire was on it by night" → "fire would be in it by night"** (v38) — Hebrew בּוֹ "in it": in the cloud (or in the dwelling). **"in the sight of" → "before the eyes of"** — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי.

### Notes

- **The tabernacle's completion and Yahweh's dwelling** — the climactic moment of Exodus: Yahweh's glory fills the newly completed dwelling. The exodus goal — "I will dwell in their midst" (29:45) — is accomplished.
- **Moses cannot enter** (v35) — the glory is too great. The high-priestly office (not Moses') will mediate the glory thenceforth. Compare 1 Kgs 8:10–11 where the same happens at Solomon's temple dedication.
- **Chronology** (v17) — first day, first month, second year of the exodus. The tabernacle is erected about nine months after the arrival at Sinai (19:1); Israel sets out from Sinai some fifty days later (Num 10:11). Forty years of wilderness journey will follow, guided by the cloud/fire pillar.
- **Book ends with journey theme** — Exodus closes not with arrival but with the journey-image: the cloud by day, the fire by night, guiding Israel onward. The people are not yet home; they are on the way, with Yahweh in their midst.
