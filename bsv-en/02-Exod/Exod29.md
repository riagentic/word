# Exodus 29

[1] "And this is the thing that you shall do to them to sanctify them, to be priests to me: take one bull of the herd, and two rams without blemish,

[2] and unleavened bread, and unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil; of fine wheat flour you shall make them.

[3] And you shall place them on one basket, and you shall bring them near in the basket, and the bull and the two rams.

[4] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons you shall bring near to the entrance of the tent of meeting, and you shall wash them with water.

[5] And you shall take the garments, and you shall clothe ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ with the tunic and the robe of the ephod and the ephod and the breastpiece, and you shall bind the ephod on him with the band of the ephod.

[6] And you shall put the turban on his head, and you shall place the holy crown on the turban.

[7] And you shall take the oil of anointing, and you shall pour it on his head and anoint him.

[8] And his sons you shall bring near, and you shall clothe them with tunics.

[9] And you shall gird them with a sash, ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons, and you shall bind caps on them; and the priesthood shall be theirs for an everlasting statute. And you shall fill the hand of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and the hand of his sons.

[10] And you shall bring the bull near before the tent of meeting, and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the bull.

[11] And you shall slaughter the bull before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[12] And you shall take of the blood of the bull, and you shall place it on the horns of the altar with your finger; and all the blood you shall pour out at the base of the altar.

[13] And you shall take all the fat that covers the entrails, and the lobe on the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, and you shall turn them into smoke on the altar.

[14] And the flesh of the bull and its skin and its dung you shall burn with fire outside the camp; it is a sin offering.

[15] And the one ram you shall take, and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.

[16] And you shall slaughter the ram, and you shall take its blood and dash it against the altar around.

[17] And the ram you shall cut into its pieces, and you shall wash its entrails and its shanks, and you shall place them on its pieces and on its head.

[18] And you shall turn the whole ram into smoke on the altar; it is a burnt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; a soothing smell, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ it is.

[19] And you shall take the second ram, and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.

[20] And you shall slaughter the ram, and you shall take of its blood and place it on the lobe of the ear of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and on the lobe of the ear of his sons, the right one, and on the thumb of their right hand and on the great toe of their right foot; and you shall dash the blood against the altar around.

[21] And you shall take of the blood that is on the altar and of the oil of anointing, and you shall sprinkle it on ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and on his garments, and on his sons and on the garments of his sons with him; and he shall be holy, he and his garments, and his sons and the garments of his sons with him.

[22] And you shall take from the ram the fat and the fat tail, and the fat that covers the entrails, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, and the right leg, for it is a ram of filling;

[23] and one round loaf of bread, and one cake of bread with oil, and one wafer, from the basket of unleavened bread that is before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[24] And you shall put the whole on the palms of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and on the palms of his sons, and you shall wave them as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[25] And you shall take them from their hand, and you shall turn them into smoke on the altar, upon the burnt offering, for a soothing smell before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it is an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[26] And you shall take the breast from the ram of filling that is for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and you shall wave it as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and it shall be your portion.

[27] And you shall sanctify the breast of the wave offering and the leg of the contribution, which was waved and which was lifted up, from the ram of filling, from that which is for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and from that which is for his sons.

[28] And it shall be for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and for his sons as an everlasting statute from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for it is a contribution; and it shall be a contribution from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the sacrifices of their peace offerings, their contribution to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[29] And the holy garments that are for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall be for his sons after him, for anointing in them and for filling their hand in them.

[30] Seven days the priest in his place from among his sons shall put them on, he who goes into the tent of meeting to minister in the Holy.

[31] And the ram of filling you shall take, and you shall boil its flesh in a holy place.

[32] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram, and the bread that is in the basket, at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[33] And they shall eat those things by which atonement was made, to fill their hand, to sanctify them; and a stranger shall not eat, for they are holy.

[34] And if any of the flesh of the filling or of the bread remains until the morning, you shall burn what remains with fire; it shall not be eaten, for it is holy.

[35] And you shall do to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons thus, according to all that I have commanded you; seven days you shall fill their hand.

[36] And a bull of sin offering you shall offer each day, for atonement; and you shall purge the altar of sin when you make atonement for it, and you shall anoint it to sanctify it.

[37] Seven days you shall make atonement for the altar and sanctify it, and the altar shall be most holy; all that touches the altar shall be holy.

[38] And this is what you shall offer on the altar: lambs a year old, two each day, continually.

[39] The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer between the two evenings;

[40] and a tenth of fine flour mixed with a quarter of the hin of beaten oil, and a drink offering, a quarter of the hin of wine, for the one lamb.

[41] And the second lamb you shall offer between the two evenings; like the grain offering of the morning and like its drink offering you shall do for it, for a soothing smell, an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

[42] a continual burnt offering for your generations, at the entrance of the tent of meeting before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, where I will meet you, to speak to you there.

[43] And I will meet there with the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and it shall be sanctified by my glory.

[44] And I will sanctify the tent of meeting and the altar; and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons I will sanctify, to be priests to me.

[45] And I will abide in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them.

[46] And they shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought them out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ that I might abide in their midst. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v45, 46) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"hallow" → "sanctify"** (v1, 27, 33, 36, 37, 43, 44) — Hebrew קִדֵּשׁ, one verb; "be holy" (v21, 37) is its simple stem קָדַשׁ. **"to minister unto me in the priest's office" → "to be priests to me"** (v1, 44) — Hebrew לְכַהֵן לִי, the verb of כֹּהֵן "priest".
- **"one young bullock" → "one bull of the herd"** (v1) — Hebrew פַּר אֶחָד בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "one bull, a son of the herd"; the words do not say "young". **"without blemish"** — Hebrew תְּמִימִם, "whole, complete": the word that is "blameless" of a man.
- **"tempered with oil" → "mixed with oil"** (v2, 40) — Hebrew בָּלַל "mix". **"cakes"** — Hebrew חַלֹּת, pierced or ring-shaped loaves; **"wafers"** — רְקִיקִים, thin loaves. "Anointed with oil" is the verb מָשַׁח, as of the priests (v7).
- **"bring" → "bring near"** (v3, 4, 8, 10) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב, hifil of קָרַב "come near". **"door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "entrance of the tent of meeting"** (v4, 11, 32, 42) — Hebrew פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"gird him with the curious girdle of the ephod" → "bind the ephod on him with the band of the ephod"** (v5) — Hebrew וְאָפַדְתָּ לוֹ, a verb made from the noun "ephod": "you shall ephod for him". For חֵשֶׁב "band" see 28:8.
- **"the holy crown"** (v6) — Hebrew נֵזֶר הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the crown of holiness": נֵזֶר (root of "Nazirite") is the mark of one set apart. It is the gold plate of 28:36.
- **"the anointing oil" → "the oil of anointing"** (v7, 21) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה.
- **"girdles" → "a sash"** (v9) — the Hebrew is singular, אַבְנֵט. **"bonnets" → "caps"** — see 28:40. **"for a perpetual statute" → "for an everlasting statute"** (v9, 28) — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם (v9), חָק־עוֹלָם (v28), literally "a statute of the age". In v28 it is the due laid down for the priests.
- **"consecrate Aaron and his sons" → "fill the hand of Aaron and the hand of his sons"** (v9, 29, 33, 35) — Hebrew מִלֵּא יָד, literally so: the set expression for installing a priest ("ordain"). See 28:41. **"ram of consecration" → "ram of filling"** (v22, 26, 27, 31, 34) — Hebrew אֵיל (הַ)מִּלֻּאִים, the noun of the same root: the ram of the "filling" of the hand, parts of which are laid on the palms of Aaron and his sons (v24).
- **"put their hands upon" → "lay their hands on"** (v10, 15, 19) — Hebrew סָמַךְ "lean, press".
- **"kill" → "slaughter"** (v11, 16, 20) — Hebrew שָׁחַט.
- **"pour all the blood beside the bottom of the altar" → "all the blood you shall pour out at the base of the altar"** (v12) — Hebrew אֶל־יְסוֹד, "to the foundation".
- **"the inwards" → "the entrails"** (v13, 17, 22) — Hebrew קֶרֶב. **"the caul that is above the liver" → "the lobe on the liver"** (v13, 22) — Hebrew הַיֹּתֶרֶת עַל־הַכָּבֵד, literally "what is over on the liver": an appendage of the liver; the exact part is uncertain.
- **"burn" → "turn into smoke"** (v13, 18, 25); **"burn with fire" kept** (v14, 34) — two Hebrew verbs, kept apart. On the altar it is הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke" (the root of קְטֹרֶת "incense"). Outside the camp, and of what remains, it is שָׂרַף "burn".
- **"sin offering"** (v14, 36) — Hebrew חַטָּאת, the one word "sin".
- **"sprinkle it round about upon the altar" → "dash it against the altar around"** (v16, 20) — Hebrew זָרַק, to throw from a bowl; "sprinkle" (v21) is הִזָּה.
- **"legs" → "shanks"** (v17) — Hebrew כְּרָעַיִם, the lower legs. **"shoulder" → "leg"** (v22, 27) — Hebrew שׁוֹק, the upper hind leg.
- **"a sweet savour" → "a soothing smell"** (v18, 25, 41) — Hebrew רֵיחַ נִיחוֹחַ, from נוּחַ "rest"; as Gen 8:21. **"an offering made by fire" → "an offering by fire"** — Hebrew אִשֶּׁה, commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain.
- **"the tip of the right ear of Aaron … the thumb … the great toe"** (v20) — Hebrew תְּנוּךְ אֹזֶן, the lobe (or the upper rim) of the ear. "The right one" stands once in the Hebrew, after "the ear of his sons". "Thumb" and "great toe" are one word, בֹּהֶן.
- **"shall be hallowed" → "shall be holy"** (v21, 37) — Hebrew וְקָדַשׁ.
- **"rump" → "fat tail"** (v22) — Hebrew הָאַלְיָה, the heavy tail of the fat-tailed sheep.
- **"one loaf of bread" → "one round loaf of bread"** (v23) — Hebrew כִּכַּר לֶחֶם, "a round of bread". **"one cake of oiled bread" → "one cake of bread with oil"** — Hebrew חַלַּת לֶחֶם שֶׁמֶן, "a cake of bread of oil".
- **"hands" → "palms"** (v24) — Hebrew כַּף; in v25 "their hand" is יָד.
- **"wave them for a wave offering"** (v24, 26, 27) — Hebrew וְהֵנַפְתָּ … תְּנוּפָה, verb and noun of one root: to move to and fro (or to lift) before Yahweh.
- **"it shall be thy part" → "it shall be your portion"** (v26) — Hebrew לְמָנָה.
- **"heave offering" → "contribution"; "heaved up" → "lifted up"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew תְּרוּמָה, from הֵרִים "lift up": what is lifted off and set aside (25:2). "Which was waved and which was lifted up" has the verbs of the two nouns.
- **"to be anointed therein, and to be consecrated in them" → "for anointing in them and for filling their hand in them"** (v29) — Hebrew לְמָשְׁחָה בָהֶם וּלְמַלֵּא־בָם אֶת־יָדָם.
- **"that son that is priest in his stead" → "the priest in his place from among his sons"** (v30) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן תַּחְתָּיו מִבָּנָיו. **"the holy place" → "the Holy"** — Hebrew בַּקֹּדֶשׁ, as 26:33; in v31 "a holy place" is מָקֹם קָדֹשׁ.
- **"seethe" → "boil"** (v31) — Hebrew בִּשֵּׁל.
- **"wherewith the atonement was made" → "by which atonement was made"** (v33) — Hebrew כֻּפַּר, passive of כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (literally "cover"). **"a stranger"** — Hebrew זָר: here one who is not of the priests.
- **"offer"** (v36, 38, 39, 41) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "do, make", the usual verb for preparing a sacrifice.
- **"thou shalt cleanse the altar" → "you shall purge the altar of sin"** (v36) — Hebrew וְחִטֵּאתָ עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ, the piel of חָטָא "sin": literally "de-sin upon the altar". "For atonement" is עַל־הַכִּפֻּרִים, a plural noun.
- **"most holy"** (v37) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "holiness of holinesses". **"whatsoever toucheth" → "all that touches"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַנֹּגֵעַ: "everyone who" or "everything that"; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"lambs of the first year" → "lambs a year old"** (v38) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־שָׁנָה, literally "sons of a year". **"day by day continually"** — Hebrew לַיּוֹם תָּמִיד.
- **"at even" → "between the two evenings"** (v39, 41) — Hebrew בֵּין הָעַרְבָּיִם, a dual. Which hours are meant (dusk, or from the sun's decline to sunset) is disputed.
- **"a tenth deal of flour" → "a tenth of fine flour"** (v40) — Hebrew עִשָּׂרֹן סֹלֶת; the measure (an ephah) is not named. The hin is a liquid measure. "Beaten oil": see 27:20.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v41) — Hebrew מִנְחָה. "You shall do for it" is feminine in the Hebrew (לָּהּ), which fits "the evening" or the offering rather than the lamb; the reference is not clear.
- **v42–46, word for word.** **"where I will meet you, to speak to you there"** (v42) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר אִוָּעֵד לָכֶם שָׁמָּה לְדַבֵּר אֵלֶיךָ שָׁם: "you" is plural the first time (Israel) and singular the second (Moses); "there" stands twice. נוֹעַד is "meet by appointment", the root of מוֹעֵד in "tent of meeting".
- **"the tabernacle shall be sanctified by my glory" → "it shall be sanctified by my glory"** (v43) — Hebrew וְנִקְדַּשׁ בִּכְבֹדִי, with no subject named: the place, or "he" (Israel). KJV supplies "the tabernacle".
- **"dwell" → "abide"** (v45, 46) — Hebrew שָׁכַן, the root of מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling". **"will be their God" → "will be Elohim to them"** (v45) — Hebrew וְהָיִיתִי לָהֶם לֵאלֹהִים, "I will be to them for Elohim". **"that I may dwell among them" → "that I might abide in their midst"** (v46) — Hebrew לְשָׁכְנִי בְתוֹכָם, "for my abiding in their midst".

### Notes

- **Seven-day installation** — the installing of the priests takes a full week, with daily sacrifices.
- **Blood on ear / thumb / toe (v20)** — hearing, acting and walking. The same rite is used for a cleansed leper (Lev 14:14).
- **Daily continual burnt offering (v38–42)** — the *tamid* sacrifice: two lambs each day, morning and evening.
- **"I will abide in their midst" (v45–46)** — the goal of the whole dwelling: Yahweh abiding among his people. Echoes 25:8 and is taken up in John 1:14 (*eskēnōsen*, "pitched his tent" among us).
