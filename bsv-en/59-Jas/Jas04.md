# James 4

[1] From where are wars and from where are fights among you? Is it not from here, out of your pleasures that wage war in your members?

[2] You desire, and do not have; you murder and are jealous, and cannot obtain; you fight and war. You do not have, because you do not ask;

[3] you ask and do not receive, because you ask wrongly, that you may spend it in your pleasures.

[4] Adulteresses, do you not know that the friendship of the world is enmity with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? Whoever therefore wills to be a friend of the world is made an enemy of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] Or do you think that the Scripture says emptily, "Unto envy yearns the spirit that he made to dwell in us"?

[6] But he gives greater grace; therefore it says, "**⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ opposes the proud, but to the humble he gives grace**."

[7] **Be subject therefore to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; but resist the devil, and he will flee from you;**

[8] **draw near to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse hands, sinners, and purify hearts, you double-souled.**

[9] Be miserable and mourn and weep; let your laughter be turned into mourning and your joy into dejection;

[10] humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will lift you up.

[11] Do not speak against one another, brothers. He who speaks against a brother or judges his brother speaks against the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and judges the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧; but if you judge the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, you are not a doer of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ but a judge.

[12] One is the lawgiver and judge, he who is able to save and to destroy; but you, who are you, who judge the neighbor?

[13] Come now, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go into this city and spend a year there and trade and gain,"

[14] you who do not know the matter of tomorrow, of what sort your life is; for you are a vapor that appears for a little while and then also vanishes;

[15] instead of your saying, "If the Lord wills, we will both live and do this or that."

[16] But now you boast in your pretensions; all such boasting is evil.

[17] Therefore to him who knows to do good and does not do it, to him it is sin.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"wars and fightings" → "wars … fights"** (v1) — Greek πόλεμοι … μάχαι; the verbs of v2 ("fight and war") are the same two roots. NA28 has πόθεν, "from where", twice.
- **"your lusts that war in your members" → "your pleasures that wage war in your members"** (v1) — Greek ἐκ τῶν ἡδονῶν ὑμῶν τῶν στρατευομένων ἐν τοῖς μέλεσιν ὑμῶν *ek tōn hēdonōn hymōn tōn strateuomenōn en tois melesin hymōn*; ἡδονή is "pleasure" (also v3); "members" as in 3:5–6.
- **"ye lust … ye kill, and desire to have" → "You desire … you murder and are jealous"** (v2) — Greek ἐπιθυμεῖτε (the verb of "desire", 1:14), φονεύετε (as 2:11), ζηλοῦτε (the verb of "jealousy", 3:14). The Greek has no "so"; the verse is divided as SBLGNT punctuates it. Another division: "You desire and do not have: you murder. And you are jealous and cannot obtain: you fight and war."
- **"yet ye have not, because ye ask not" → "You do not have, because you do not ask"** (v2) — NA28 has no "yet".
- **"ask amiss" → "ask wrongly"** (v3) — Greek κακῶς, "badly". "Ask" changes from active (αἰτεῖτε) to middle (αἰτεῖσθε); English shows no difference.
- **"adulterers and adulteresses" → "Adulteresses"** (v4) — NA28 μοιχαλίδες *moichalides*, feminine plural only: the prophets' picture of the people as an unfaithful wife (Hos 3:1; Ezek 16).
- **"the friendship of the world is enmity with God" kept** (v4) — Greek ἡ φιλία τοῦ κόσμου ἔχθρα τοῦ θεοῦ, two genitives: "friendship of the world", "enmity of (toward) *Elohim*".
- **"will be a friend … is the enemy" → "wills to be a friend … is made an enemy"** (v4) — Greek βουληθῇ, "wills, resolves" (as 1:18); καθίσταται, "is made" or "makes himself" (as 3:6).
- **"in vain" → "emptily"** (v5) — Greek κενῶς, the word of "empty" in 2:20.
- **"The spirit that dwelleth in us lusteth to envy" → "Unto envy yearns the spirit that he made to dwell in us"** (v5) — NA28 Πρὸς φθόνον ἐπιποθεῖ τὸ πνεῦμα ὃ κατῴκισεν ἐν ἡμῖν; KJV follows the reading κατῴκησεν, "dwelt". The Greek can be read two ways: "the spirit that he made to dwell in us yearns unto envy", or "he yearns unto envy (jealously) for the spirit that he made to dwell in us". The verse keeps the Greek word order. φθόνος is "envy". No such sentence stands in the Hebrew Scriptures as we have them.
- **"more grace" → "greater grace"** (v6) — Greek μείζονα χάριν.
- **"God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble" → "*Elohim* opposes the proud, but to the humble he gives grace"** (v6) — OT quotation (Prov 3:34 LXX); ἀντιτάσσεται "sets himself against" (also 5:6); ταπεινοῖς "humble" as 1:9; the Greek order is kept.
- **"Submit yourselves" → "Be subject"** (v7) — Greek ὑποτάγητε; "resist" is ἀντίστητε, "stand against". NA28 has δέ, "but".
- **"Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you" → "draw near to *Elohim*, and he will draw near to you"** (v8) — "nigh" → "near".
- **"Cleanse your hands, ye sinners; and purify your hearts, ye double minded" → "Cleanse hands, sinners, and purify hearts, you double-souled"** (v8) — the Greek has no "your"; δίψυχοι as 1:8. "Purify" is ἁγνίσατε, the root of "pure" (ἁγνή) in 3:17.
- **"Be afflicted" → "Be miserable"** (v9) — Greek ταλαιπωρήσατε *talaipōrēsate*; the noun in 5:1 ("miseries").
- **"heaviness" → "dejection"** (v9) — Greek κατήφεια, "a downcast look".
- **"Humble yourselves … and he shall lift you up" kept** (v10) — Greek ταπεινώθητε, passive in form ("be humbled, be made low"), also used for "humble yourselves"; the root of "humble" (v6; 1:9–10), and ὑψώσει is the root of "height" (1:9).
- **"Speak not evil" → "Do not speak against"** (v11) — Greek καταλαλεῖτε, three times in the verse. "A brother or judges" is NA28 ἤ; KJV "and".
- **"There is one lawgiver" → "One is the lawgiver and judge"** (v12) — Greek εἷς ἐστιν ὁ νομοθέτης καὶ κριτής *nomothetēs kai kritēs*: ἐστιν WH ] + ὁ Treg NA28 RP, and the verse follows "+ ὁ" ("the lawgiver"); καὶ κριτής WH Treg NA28 ] – RP, so "and judge" is absent from KJV's text. The Greek has no "only".
- **"who art thou that judgest another?" → "but you, who are you, who judge the neighbor?"** (v12) — NA28 σὺ δὲ τίς εἶ, ὁ κρίνων τὸν πλησίον.
- **"into such a city" → "into this city"** (v13) — Greek εἰς τήνδε τὴν πόλιν, "this city here": the speaker names a particular one.
- **"continue there a year, and buy and sell, and get gain" → "spend a year there and trade and gain"** (v13) — Greek ποιήσομεν ἐκεῖ ἐνιαυτόν, literally "do a year there"; ἐμπορευσόμεθα, κερδήσομεν.
- **"Whereas ye know not what shall be on the morrow. For what is your life?" → "you who do not know the matter of tomorrow, of what sort your life is"** (v14) — Greek οἵτινες οὐκ ἐπίστασθε τὸ τῆς αὔριον ποία ἡ ζωὴ ὑμῶν, as SBLGNT prints it, without a break; it can also be divided "…the matter of tomorrow. Of what sort is your life?" τὸ Treg NA28 RP ] – WH; without τό: "you do not know of what sort your life will be tomorrow".
- **"It is even a vapour, that appeareth for a little time, and then vanisheth away" → "you are a vapor that appears for a little while and then also vanishes"** (v14) — NA28 ἀτμὶς γάρ ἐστε, "you are"; ἀτμίς is "vapor, steam, smoke".
- **"For that ye ought to say" → "instead of your saying"** (v15) — Greek ἀντὶ τοῦ λέγειν ὑμᾶς; no "ought". The sentence runs on from v13.
- **"If the Lord will, we shall live, and do" → "If the Lord wills, we will both live and do"** (v15) — Greek καὶ ζήσομεν καὶ ποιήσομεν; "wills" here is θελήσῃ (θέλω, as 2:20), not the verb of v4.
- **"ye rejoice in your boastings: all such rejoicing" → "you boast in your pretensions; all such boasting"** (v16) — Greek καυχᾶσθε … καύχησις, "boast, boasting" (as 1:9); ἀλαζονείαις, plural, "empty pretensions, braggings".
- **"to him that knoweth to do good, and doeth it not" kept** (v17) — Greek εἰδότι οὖν καλὸν ποιεῖν καὶ μὴ ποιοῦντι, "knowing (how) to do good and not doing".

### Notes
- **v6** quotes Prov 3:34.
