# James 1

[1] ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧, a slave of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

To the twelve tribes that are in the Dispersion:

Greeting.

[2] Count it all joy, my brothers, when you fall into various trials,

[3] knowing that the testing of your faith works out endurance;

[4] and let endurance have a perfect work, that you may be perfect and whole, lacking in nothing.

[5] But if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who gives to all simply and does not reproach, and it will be given to him.

[6] But let him ask in faith, doubting nothing, for the one who doubts is like a wave of the sea, driven by the wind and tossed.

[7] For let that person not suppose that he will receive anything from the Lord,

[8] a double-souled man, restless in all his ways.

[9] But let the humble brother boast in his height,

[10] and the rich in his humbling, because like a flower of grass he will pass away.

[11] For the sun rose with the scorching heat and dried up the grass, and its flower fell off, and the beauty of its face perished; so also the rich man will fade away in his journeys.

[12] Blessed is the man who endures trial, because, having become approved, he will receive the crown of life, which he promised to those who love him.

[13] Let no one, being tempted, say, "I am tempted from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧"; for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ cannot be tempted by evil things, and he himself tempts no one.

[14] But each one is tempted by his own desire, being drawn out and enticed;

[15] then the desire, having conceived, bears sin, and the sin, having been brought to completion, brings forth death.

[16] Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers.

[17] **Every good giving and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of the lights, with whom there is no variation or shadow of turning.**

[18] Having so willed, he brought us forth by the word of truth, for us to be a kind of firstfruits of his creatures.

[19] Know, my beloved brothers. But **let every person be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger**,

[20] for a man's anger does not work the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[21] Therefore, putting away all filthiness and abundance of wickedness, in meekness accept the implanted word, which is able to save your souls.

[22] But **be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deluding yourselves**.

[23] Because if anyone is a hearer of the word and not a doer, this one is like a man observing the face of his birth in a mirror;

[24] for he observed himself and has gone away, and at once forgot of what sort he was.

[25] But he who has looked into the perfect ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, that of liberty, and has remained, having become not a hearer of forgetfulness but a doer of work, this one will be blessed in his doing.

[26] If anyone thinks he is religious, not bridling his tongue but deceiving his heart, this one's religion is vain.

[27] Religion pure and undefiled before the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father is this: to visit orphans and widows in their affliction, to keep oneself unspotted from the world.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v1) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1) — systematic dual substitution.
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v1) — Greek δοῦλος *doulos*.
- **"scattered abroad" → "in the Dispersion"** (v1) — Greek ἐν τῇ διασπορᾷ *en tē diaspora*, a noun with the article: "the Dispersion".
- **"greeting" kept** (v1) — Greek χαίρειν, literally "to rejoice", the usual letter greeting; "joy" (χαράν, v2) takes up the same root.
- **"divers temptations" → "various trials"** (v2) — Greek πειρασμοῖς ποικίλοις; "fall into" (περιπέσητε) kept. The noun πειρασμός (v2, 12) and the verb πειράζω "tempt" (v13–14) are one root; English needs two words.
- **"the trying of your faith worketh patience" → "the testing of your faith works out endurance"** (v3) — Greek τὸ δοκίμιον ὑμῶν τῆς πίστεως, "the testing" or "the tested, genuine part" of your faith; κατεργάζεται "works out"; ὑπομονή "endurance" (v3, 4), the verb ὑπομένει "endures" in v12. "Knowing" (γινώσκοντες) is a participle.
- **"her perfect work" → "a perfect work"** (v4) — Greek ἔργον τέλειον, without article or pronoun; τέλειος "perfect" twice in the verse, again in v17, 25.
- **"entire, wanting nothing" → "whole, lacking in nothing"** (v4) — Greek ὁλόκληροι; λειπόμενοι is the same verb as "lacks" (λείπεται) in v5.
- **"liberally" → "simply"** (v5) — Greek ἁπλῶς, "simply, singly, without a second thought"; it can also be understood as "generously". Opposite of the "double-souled" man of v8.
- **"upbraideth not" → "does not reproach"** (v5) — Greek μὴ ὀνειδίζοντος, a second participle beside "gives".
- **"nothing wavering" → "doubting nothing"** (v6) — Greek μηδὲν διακρινόμενος, "being divided in oneself"; the same verb in 2:4.
- **"that man" → "that person"** (v7) — Greek ἄνθρωπος; "man" in v8, 12, 20, 23 is ἀνήρ.
- **"A double minded man is unstable" → "a double-souled man, restless"** (v8) — Greek ἀνὴρ δίψυχος, "of two souls" (ψυχή, as in v21); also 4:8. ἀκατάστατος is "unsettled, never at rest" (also 3:8; the noun in 3:16); "unstable" is kept for ἀστήρικτος. The Greek has no verb: the words stand in apposition to "that person" (v7).
- **"of low degree … in that he is exalted … in that he is made low" → "humble … in his height … in his humbling"** (v9–10) — Greek ὁ ταπεινός, ἐν τῷ ὕψει, ἐν τῇ ταπεινώσει: nouns, and one root for "humble" (low) and "humbling" (being made low); the verb in 4:10.
- **"is no sooner risen … withereth … falleth … perisheth" → "rose … dried up … fell off … perished"** (v11) — Greek aorists (ἀνέτειλεν, ἐξήρανεν, ἐξέπεσεν, ἀπώλετο), as in Isa 40:7 LXX; they state a general truth as a thing that happened. καύσων is "scorching heat" or the scorching east wind.
- **"the grace of the fashion of it" → "the beauty of its face"** (v11) — Greek ἡ εὐπρέπεια τοῦ προσώπου αὐτοῦ; πρόσωπον "face" as in v23.
- **"in his ways" → "in his journeys"** (v11) — Greek ἐν ταῖς πορείαις, "goings, journeys"; a different word from "ways" (ὁδοῖς) in v8. "In the midst of" is not in the Greek.
- **"temptation" → "trial"** (v12) — Greek πειρασμόν, as v2.
- **"when he is tried" → "having become approved"** (v12) — Greek δόκιμος γενόμενος; δόκιμος is "tested and found genuine", the root of δοκίμιον (v3).
- **"which the Lord hath promised" → "which he promised"** (v12) — NA28 ἐπηγγείλατο has no expressed subject; "the Lord" (KJV) and "God" are later additions to the text.
- **"tempted of God" → "tempted from *Elohim*"** (v13) — Greek ἀπὸ θεοῦ, "from"; v14 has ὑπό, "by".
- **"cannot be tempted with evil" → "cannot be tempted by evil things"** (v13) — Greek ἀπείραστός ἐστιν κακῶν: κακῶν is plural; the adjective can also mean "untried in, without experience of, evil things".
- **"when he is drawn away of his own lust, and enticed" → "by his own desire, being drawn out and enticed"** (v14) — Greek order; "by his own desire" can go with "is tempted" or with the two participles. ἐπιθυμία is "desire" (v14, 15); δελεαζόμενος is "lured with bait".
- **"when it is finished, bringeth forth" → "having been brought to completion, brings forth"** (v15) — Greek ἀποτελεσθεῖσα; ἀποκύει is the same verb as "brought us forth" in v18; "bears" is τίκτει.
- **"Do not err" → "Do not be deceived"** (v16) — Greek μὴ πλανᾶσθε, "do not be deceived / do not go astray"; the same root in 5:19–20 ("is led astray", "error").
- **"Every good gift and every perfect gift" → "Every good giving and every perfect gift"** (v17) — Greek has two words, δόσις (the act of giving, or the gift) and δώρημα.
- **"Father of lights" → "Father of the lights"** (v17) — Greek τῶν φώτων, with the article: the lights of the sky.
- **"variableness, neither shadow of turning" → "no variation or shadow of turning"** (v17) — Greek παραλλαγὴ ἢ τροπῆς ἀποσκίασμα *parallagē ē tropēs aposkiasma*, "a shadow cast by turning"; τροπή is also the word for the sun's turning (solstice). The heavenly lights shift and are shadowed; their Father is not.
- **"Of his own will begat he us" → "Having so willed, he brought us forth"** (v18) — Greek βουληθείς, a participle, "having willed"; ἀπεκύησεν *apekyēsen* is "gave birth to", the verb of v15.
- **"Wherefore" → "Know"** (v19) — NA28 Ἴστε; KJV follows the later reading ὥστε. Ἴστε can be a command ("know") or a statement ("you know"). The Greek has no object and then begins a new sentence with δέ, "but".
- **"swift" → "quick"; "wrath" → "anger"** (v19, 20) — Greek ταχύς, ὀργή.
- **"the wrath of man worketh not" → "a man's anger does not work"** (v20) — Greek ἀνδρός, of a man (ἀνήρ), not ἄνθρωπος as in v19; οὐκ ἐργάζεται, "does not work".
- **οὐκ ἐργάζεται WH Treg NA27 ] οὐ κατεργάζεται NA28 RP** (v20) — "does not work" against "does not work out" (the verb of v3); the verse follows the printed οὐκ ἐργάζεται.
- **"lay apart … and receive" → "putting away …, in meekness accept"** (v21) — Greek ἀποθέμενοι is a participle; "in meekness" stands between the two clauses; δέξασθε is "accept" ("receive" in v7, 12 is λαμβάνω).
- **"superfluity of naughtiness" → "abundance of wickedness"** (v21) — Greek περισσείαν κακίας *perisseian kakias*, "abundance, overflow of wickedness (or malice)".
- **"the engrafted word" → "the implanted word"** (v21) — Greek ἔμφυτον λόγον *emphyton logon* = "innate / implanted / engrafted word".
- **"deceiving your own selves" → "deluding yourselves"** (v22) — Greek παραλογιζόμενοι, "reasoning oneself astray"; a different word from "deceiving" (ἀπατῶν) in v26.
- **"beholding his natural face in a glass" → "observing the face of his birth in a mirror"** (v23) — Greek τὸ πρόσωπον τῆς γενέσεως αὐτοῦ, literally "the face of his birth (origin)", the face he was born with; γένεσις again in 3:6. κατανοέω is "observe, consider" (also v24).
- **"beholdeth … goeth his way … forgetteth" → "observed … has gone away … forgot"** (v24) — Greek aorist, perfect, aorist (κατενόησεν, ἀπελήλυθεν, ἐπελάθετο).
- **"the perfect law of liberty" → "the perfect law, that of liberty"** (v25) — Greek νόμον τέλειον τὸν τῆς ἐλευθερίας *nomon teleion ton tēs eleutherias*; "law" stands once. παρακύψας is "having stooped to look"; παραμείνας "having remained (beside it)".
- **"a forgetful hearer, but a doer of the work" → "a hearer of forgetfulness but a doer of work"** (v25) — Greek ἀκροατὴς ἐπιλησμονῆς … ποιητὴς ἔργου, two genitives; ποίησις "doing" is the noun of ποιητής "doer".
- **"seem to be religious" → "thinks he is religious"** (v26) — Greek δοκεῖ … εἶναι, "thinks himself to be" or "seems to be". The two participles ("not bridling … but deceiving") are kept.
- **"vain" kept** (v26) — Greek μάταιος, "empty, futile".
- **"Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father" → "Religion pure and undefiled before the *Elohim* and Father"** (v27) — Greek παρὰ τῷ θεῷ καὶ πατρί, one article for both nouns.
- **"and to keep himself" → "to keep oneself"** (v27) — the Greek has no "and" between the two infinitives.
