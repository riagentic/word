# James 5

[1] Come now, you rich, weep, howling over your miseries that are coming on.

[2] Your riches have rotted, and your garments have become moth-eaten,

[3] your gold and silver have rusted, and their rust will be for a testimony against you and will eat your flesh; like fire you stored up treasure in the last days.

[4] Behold, the wage of the workers who mowed your fields, which has been kept back by you, cries out, and the cries of the reapers have entered into the ears of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ of ⟦tzevaot|Sabaoth|Hosts⟧.

[5] You lived in luxury on the earth and in indulgence; you fed your hearts in a day of slaughter.

[6] You condemned, you murdered the righteous one. Does he not oppose you?

[7] Be patient therefore, brothers, until the coming of the Lord. Behold, the farmer waits for the precious fruit of the earth, being patient over it until it receives the early and the late.

[8] Be patient, you also; make your hearts stable, because the coming of the Lord has drawn near.

[9] Do not groan, brothers, against one another, that you may not be judged; behold, the judge stands before the doors.

[10] Take as an example, brothers, of suffering hardship and of patience the prophets, who spoke in the name of the Lord.

[11] Behold, we call blessed those who endured. You heard of the endurance of ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧, and you saw the end of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ is very compassionate and merciful.

[12] But before all things, my brothers, do not swear, neither by heaven nor by the earth nor any other oath; but let your "Yes" be yes and the "No" no, that you may not fall under judgment.

[13] Is anyone among you suffering hardship? Let him pray. Is anyone cheerful? Let him sing psalms.

[14] Is anyone among you sick? Let him call to himself the elders of the church, and let them pray over him, having anointed him with oil in the name of the Lord;

[15] and the prayer of faith will save the ailing one, and the Lord will raise him up; and if he has committed sins, it will be forgiven him.

[16] **Confess therefore your sins to one another and pray for one another, that you may be healed. The supplication of a righteous one has much strength as it works.**

[17] ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ was a person of like nature with us, and he prayed with prayer that it might not rain, and it did not rain on the earth for three years and six months;

[18] and he prayed again, and the heaven gave rain, and the earth put forth its fruit.

[19] My brothers, if anyone among you is led astray from the truth and someone turns him back,

[20] let him know that **he who turns a sinner back from the error of his way will save his soul from death and will cover a multitude of sins**.

## Changes

- **"weep and howl for your miseries that shall come upon you" → "weep, howling over your miseries that are coming on"** (v1) — Greek κλαύσατε ὀλολύζοντες (a participle) ἐπὶ ταῖς ταλαιπωρίαις ὑμῶν ταῖς ἐπερχομέναις; "miseries" as 4:9.
- **"are corrupted" → "have rotted"** (v2) — Greek σέσηπεν; three perfects in v2–3.
- **"is cankered; and the rust of them" → "have rusted, and their rust"** (v3) — Greek κατίωται … ὁ ἰός; ἰός is the word for "poison" in 3:8.
- **"a witness against you" → "for a testimony against you"** (v3) — Greek εἰς μαρτύριον ὑμῖν, a dative: "to you" or "against you". "Flesh" is plural (τὰς σάρκας).
- **"as it were fire. Ye have heaped treasure together for the last days" → "like fire you stored up treasure in the last days"** (v3) — Greek ἐν ἐσχάταις ἡμέραις, "in", not "for". SBLGNT puts the stop before ὡς πῦρ: "like fire" belongs to "you stored up" (what they stored up is as fire).
- **NA28: "…will eat your flesh like fire. You stored up treasure …"** (v3) — NA28 puts the stop after ὡς πῦρ (from memory).
- **"the hire of the labourers who have reaped down" → "the wage of the workers who mowed"** (v4) — Greek ὁ μισθὸς τῶν ἐργατῶν τῶν ἀμησάντων; "reapers" is a second word (θερισάντων).
- **"which is of you kept back by fraud, crieth" → "which has been kept back by you, cries out"** (v4) — ἀφυστερημένος WH Treg ] ἀπεστερημένος NA28 RP: "withheld, kept back" against "robbed, kept back by fraud" (so KJV); the verse follows the printed ἀφυστερημένος. ἀφʼ ὑμῶν ("from you") can go with "kept back" or with "cries out". The Greek has no "against you".
- **"Lord of Sabaoth" → "Yahweh of Sabaoth" / "the Lord of Hosts"** (v4) — Greek Κυρίου Σαβαώθ transliterates Hebrew יהוה צְבָאוֹת *YHWH Tzeva'ot*, "of armies / hosts" (as in LXX Isa 5:9).
- **"are entered into the ears" → "have entered into the ears"** (v4) — Greek εἰς τὰ ὦτα … εἰσεληλύθασιν.
- **"Ye have lived in pleasure on the earth, and been wanton; ye have nourished" → "You lived in luxury on the earth and in indulgence; you fed"** (v5) — Greek ἐτρυφήσατε … ἐσπαταλήσατε, ἐθρέψατε *etryphēsate … espatalēsate, ethrepsate*, aorists; τρέφω is "feed, nourish". "As" is not in the critical text.
- **"condemned and killed the just; and he doth not resist you" → "You condemned, you murdered the righteous one. Does he not oppose you?"** (v6) — Greek κατεδικάσατε, ἐφονεύσατε τὸν δίκαιον, without "and"; φονεύω as 2:11; 4:2. ἀντιτάσσεται is the verb of 4:6; its subject is not stated (the righteous one, or *Elohim* as in 4:6). SBLGNT prints the clause as a question, which expects the answer "yes".
- **NA28: a statement** (v6) — NA28 punctuates "he does not oppose you" (from memory).
- **"Be patient therefore, brethren, unto the coming of the Lord" kept** (v7) — Greek μακροθυμήσατε, "be long-tempered"; παρουσία "coming, presence".
- **"and hath long patience for it, until he receive the early and latter rain" → "being patient over it until it receives the early and the late"** (v7) — Greek ἕως λάβῃ πρόϊμον καὶ ὄψιμον *proïmon kai opsimon*, "early and late", without a noun: the early (autumn) and late (spring) rain is meant, and KJV's text adds ὑετόν, "rain"; nothing is supplied; the subject of "receives" can be the fruit or the farmer.
- **"stablish your hearts" → "make your hearts stable"** (v8) — Greek στηρίξατε.
- **"draweth nigh" → "has drawn near"** (v8) — Greek ἤγγικεν, perfect; the verb of 4:8.
- **"Grudge not … lest ye be condemned" → "Do not groan … that you may not be judged"** (v9) — Greek μὴ στενάζετε, "groan, sigh"; NA28 κριθῆτε, "be judged". "Doors" is plural (θυρῶν).
- **"suffering affliction" → "suffering hardship"** (v10, 13) — Greek κακοπαθίας, κακοπαθεῖ.
- **"we count them happy which endure" → "we call blessed those who endured"** (v11) — Greek μακαρίζομεν, the verb of "blessed" (1:12, 25); NA28 ὑπομείναντας, aorist; ὑπομονή "endurance" as 1:3–4.
- **"the end of the Lord"** (v11) — τὸ τέλος κυρίου, kept literal: τέλος = end/outcome (not "purpose"); the outcome the Lord gave Job's trial (Job 42:10–17). ὅτι is "that" or "because".
- **"very pitiful, and of tender mercy" → "very compassionate and merciful"** (v11) — Greek πολύσπλαγχνος, "of much σπλάγχνα (inward parts)"; οἰκτίρμων, "pitying", a different word from "mercy" (ἔλεος) in 2:13; 3:17.
- **"above all things" → "before all things"** (v12) — Greek πρὸ πάντων.
- **"let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay; lest ye fall into condemnation" → "let your "Yes" be yes and the "No" no, that you may not fall under judgment"** (v12) — Greek ὑπὸ κρίσιν, "under judgment" (κρίσις as 2:13).
- **"merry" → "cheerful"; "sing psalms" kept** (v13) — Greek εὐθυμεῖ; ψαλλέτω, "play a stringed instrument, sing (praise)".
- **"anointing him" → "having anointed him"** (v14) — Greek ἀλείψαντες, an aorist participle: "having anointed" or "anointing". "Call to himself" is προσκαλεσάσθω.
- **"shall save the sick" → "will save the ailing one"** (v15) — Greek τὸν κάμνοντα, "the weary, the ill", a different word from "sick" (ἀσθενεῖ) in v14. σώσει is "save" or "make well". "Prayer" here is εὐχή (also "vow"); "pray" in v13–14, 17–18 is προσεύχομαι, in v16 εὔχομαι.
- **"they shall be forgiven him" → "it will be forgiven him"** (v15) — Greek ἀφεθήσεται αὐτῷ, singular and impersonal.
- **"Confess your faults" → "Confess therefore your sins"** (v16) — NA28 οὖν … τὰς ἁμαρτίας; KJV follows τὰ παραπτώματα.
- **"the effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much" → "The supplication of a righteous one has much strength as it works"** (v16) — Greek πολὺ ἰσχύει δέησις δικαίου ἐνεργουμένη *poly ischyei deēsis dikaiou energoumenē*; δέησις is "request, supplication"; ἐνεργουμένη is a participle, "working" or "being made to work".
- **"a man subject to like passions as we are" → "a person of like nature with us"** (v17) — Greek ἄνθρωπος … ὁμοιοπαθὴς ἡμῖν *homoiopathēs hēmin*, "experiencing (suffering) the same things as we".
- **"prayed earnestly" → "prayed with prayer"** (v17) — Greek προσευχῇ προσηύξατο, a Hebrew way of stressing the verb. "The earth" (τῆς γῆς) can also be "the land".
- **"brought forth her fruit" → "put forth its fruit"** (v18) — Greek ἐβλάστησεν, "made sprout".
- **"do err from the truth, and one convert him" → "is led astray from the truth and someone turns him back"** (v19) — Greek πλανηθῇ, the verb of 1:16 ("be deceived"); with "from the truth" English cannot say "is deceived from", so "is led astray"; ἐπιστρέψῃ, "turns back" (also v20).
- **"shall save a soul from death, and shall hide" → "will save his soul from death and will cover"** (v20) — NA28 ψυχὴν αὐτοῦ, "his soul"; "his" can be the sinner or the one who turns him back. "From the error of his way" is ἐκ πλάνης ὁδοῦ αὐτοῦ, "out of the wandering of his way".
