# James 3

[1] Do not many of you become teachers, my brothers, knowing that we will receive a greater sentence;

[2] for in many things we all stumble. If anyone does not stumble in word, this one is a perfect man, able to bridle also the whole body.

[3] Now if we put the bits into the mouths of the horses for them to obey us, we guide also their whole body.

[4] Behold, the ships also, being so great and driven by harsh winds, are guided by a very small rudder wherever the impulse of the one steering wills;

[5] so also the tongue is a small member and boasts great things.

Behold, how small a fire kindles how great a forest!

[6] And **the tongue is a fire**; the tongue is made the world of unrighteousness among our members, staining the whole body and setting on fire the wheel of birth and being set on fire by ⟦gehenna|Gehenna|hell⟧.

[7] For every nature of beasts and of birds, of creeping things and of sea creatures, is tamed and has been tamed by the human nature;

[8] but the tongue no one of men is able to tame: a restless evil, full of death-bringing poison.

[9] With it we bless the Lord and Father, and with it we curse the people who have been made according to the likeness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[10] out of the same mouth comes forth blessing and curse. These things, my brothers, ought not to be so.

[11] Does the spring gush out of the same opening the sweet and the bitter?

[12] Can a fig tree, my brothers, produce olives, or a vine figs? Neither can what is salt produce sweet water.

[13] Who is wise and understanding among you? Let him show by the good conduct his works in meekness of wisdom.

[14] But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your heart, do not boast and lie against the truth.

[15] This is not the wisdom coming down from above, but earthly, soulish, demonic;

[16] for where jealousy and selfish ambition are, there is unrest and every worthless deed.

[17] But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, compliant, full of mercy and good fruits, undivided, without hypocrisy;

[18] and the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace for those who make peace.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — systematic.
- **"be not many masters" → "Do not many of you become teachers"** (v1) — Greek Μὴ πολλοὶ διδάσκαλοι γίνεσθε, a command; διδάσκαλοι *didaskaloi* = "teachers" (KJV "masters" meant "schoolmasters"). "Knowing" (εἰδότες) is a participle.
- **"the greater condemnation" → "a greater sentence"** (v1) — Greek μεῖζον κρίμα λημψόμεθα; κρίμα is the sentence passed ("judgment" in 2:13; 5:12 is κρίσις); the Greek has neither "we who teach" nor "strictness".
- **"offend" → "stumble"** (v2) — Greek πταίομεν, as 2:10; πολλά is "in many things" or "often". "In word" is ἐν λόγῳ.
- **"Behold" → "Now if"** (v3) — NA28 εἰ δέ; KJV follows the reading ἴδε / ἰδού.
- **"turn about" → "guide"** (v3, 4) — Greek μετάγομεν, μετάγεται, "lead to another place".
- **"fierce winds" → "harsh winds"** (v4) — Greek ἀνέμων σκληρῶν, "hard, harsh".
- **"whithersoever the governor listeth" → "wherever the impulse of the one steering wills"** (v4) — Greek ὅπου ἡ ὁρμὴ τοῦ εὐθύνοντος βούλεται; ὁρμή is "impulse, push"; the verb is that of 1:18, "willed".
- **"boasteth great things" kept** (v5) — Greek μεγάλα αὐχεῖ; a different verb from "boast" (καυχάομαι) in 1:9; 3:14; 4:16.
- **"how great a matter a little fire kindleth" → "how small a fire kindles how great a forest"** (v5) — NA28 ἡλίκον πῦρ ἡλίκην ὕλην ἀνάπτει; ἡλίκος, "of what size", stands twice (the first time for smallness); ὕλη is "wood, forest" and also "material".
- **"a world of iniquity: so is the tongue among our members" → "the tongue is made the world of unrighteousness among our members"** (v6) — Greek ὁ κόσμος τῆς ἀδικίας ἡ γλῶσσα καθίσταται ἐν τοῖς μέλεσιν ἡμῶν; καθίσταται "is set, is made, makes itself", as in 4:4; "so" (οὕτως) is not in the critical text. With other punctuation: "the tongue is a fire, the world of unrighteousness; the tongue is set among our members".
- **"defileth" → "staining"** (v6) — Greek ἡ σπιλοῦσα, the root of "unspotted" (ἄσπιλον) in 1:27; three participles in a row.
- **"the course of nature" → "the wheel of birth"** (v6) — Greek τὸν τροχὸν τῆς γενέσεως *ton trochon tēs geneseōs*, literally "the wheel of birth (origin, existence)": the whole round of life. γένεσις as in 1:23.
- **"hell" → "Gehenna"** (v6) — Greek γεέννης *geennēs*.
- **"every kind … of mankind" → "every nature … by the human nature"** (v7) — Greek φύσις twice: πᾶσα φύσις … τῇ φύσει τῇ ἀνθρωπίνῃ. "Is tamed" (δαμάζεται) is a plain present; the Greek has no "can be".
- **"serpents" → "creeping things"** (v7) — Greek ἑρπετῶν.
- **"an unruly evil" → "a restless evil"** (v8) — Greek ἀκατάστατον κακόν *akatastaton kakon*, the word of 1:8 ("restless"); KJV follows the reading ἀκατάσχετον, "that cannot be restrained". The Greek has no "it is".
- **"deadly" → "death-bringing"** (v8) — Greek θανατηφόρου.
- **"God, even the Father" → "the Lord and Father"** (v9) — NA28 τὸν κύριον καὶ πατέρα; KJV follows the reading θεόν. The Greek has no "our".
- **"the similitude of God" → "the likeness of *Elohim*"** (v9) — Greek καθ᾽ ὁμοίωσιν θεοῦ *kath' homoiōsin theou*; echoes Gen 1:26. γεγονότας is "have come to be, have been made".
- **"sweet water and bitter" → "the sweet and the bitter"** (v11) — Greek τὸ γλυκὺ καὶ τὸ πικρόν, without "water"; πικρός "bitter" returns in v14. μήτι expects the answer "no".
- **"bear" → "produce"** (v12) — Greek ποιῆσαι, twice.
- **"so can no fountain both yield salt water and fresh" → "Neither can what is salt produce sweet water"** (v12) — NA28 οὔτε ἁλυκὸν γλυκὺ ποιῆσαι ὕδωρ; the Greek names no "pond" or "spring" here.
- **"endued with knowledge" → "understanding"** (v13) — Greek ἐπιστήμων, "knowing, skilled".
- **"out of a good conversation" → "by the good conduct"** (v13) — Greek ἐκ τῆς καλῆς ἀναστροφῆς, "out of the good way of life".
- **"envying and strife" → "jealousy and selfish ambition"** (v14, 16) — Greek ζῆλον … ἐριθείαν *zēlon … eritheian*; *zēlos* = "jealousy / zeal"; *eritheia* = "self-seeking, a hireling's pursuit of his own gain", one Greek word; by others taken as "strife, rivalry".
- **"in your hearts" → "in your heart"** (v14) — Greek ἐν τῇ καρδίᾳ ὑμῶν, singular.
- **"glory not, and lie not against the truth" → "do not boast and lie against the truth"** (v14) — Greek μὴ κατακαυχᾶσθε καὶ ψεύδεσθε κατὰ τῆς ἀληθείας; "against the truth" can go with both verbs. κατακαυχάομαι as in 2:13.
- **"sensual, devilish" → "soulish, demonic"** (v15) — Greek ψυχική, "of the soul (ψυχή)", as Jude 19; δαιμονιώδης, "demon-like".
- **"confusion and every evil work" → "unrest and every worthless deed"** (v16) — Greek ἀκαταστασία *akatastasia*, "unrest, disorder, instability", the noun of "restless" (1:8; 3:8); φαῦλον πρᾶγμα, "worthless, base thing or deed". The Greek has no verb.
- **"easy to be intreated" → "compliant"** (v17) — Greek εὐπειθής, "easily persuaded, ready to obey".
- **"without partiality" → "undivided"** (v17) — Greek ἀδιάκριτος, from the verb of 1:6 and 2:4 (διακρίνομαι): "not divided", that is, "not doubting" or "not making distinctions (impartial)".
- **"pure"** (v17) — Greek ἁγνή; "pure" in 1:27 is καθαρά.
- **"the fruit of righteousness" kept** (v18) — Greek καρπός, "fruit", not "harvest".
- **"of them that make peace" → "for those who make peace"** (v18) — Greek τοῖς ποιοῦσιν εἰρήνην, a plain dative: "for those" or "by those".
