# Luke 10

[1] Now after these things the Lord designated seventy-two others and sent them two by two before his face into every city and place where he himself was about to come.

[2] And he was saying to them, "The harvest indeed is great, but the workers are few; beseech therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he may thrust out workers into his harvest.

[3] Go; behold, I send you out as lambs in the midst of wolves.

[4] Bear no purse, no bag, no sandals, and greet no one along the road.

[5] And into whatever house you enter, first say, 'Peace to this house.'

[6] And if a son of peace is there, your peace will rest upon him; but if not, it will turn back again upon you.

[7] And remain in that very house, eating and drinking the things from them, for the worker is worthy of his wage. Do not pass on from house to house.

[8] And into whatever city you enter and they accept you, eat the things set before you,

[9] and heal the sick in it, and say to them, 'The kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has drawn near upon you.'

[10] But into whatever city you enter and they do not accept you, going out into its broad streets say,

[11] 'Even the dust that clung to us out of your city, to the feet, we wipe off against you; nevertheless know this, that the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has drawn near.'

[12] I say to you that for ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ in that day it will be more bearable than for that city.

[13] "Woe to you, ⟦chorazin|Korazin|Chorazin⟧! Woe to you, ⟦bethsaida|Beit Tzaidah|Bethsaida⟧! For if the powers that took place in you had taken place in ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, long ago they would have repented, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.

[14] Nevertheless for ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧ it will be more bearable in the judgment than for you.

[15] And you, ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧, will you be lifted up as far as heaven? As far as Hades you will go down.

[16] "The one who hears you hears me, and the one who sets you aside sets me aside; and the one who sets me aside sets aside the one who sent me."

[17] And the seventy-two returned with joy, saying, "Lord, even the demons are subject to us in your name!"

[18] And he said to them, "I was beholding Satan fall as lightning out of heaven.

[19] Behold, I have given you the authority to tread upon serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy, and nothing will by any means harm you.

[20] Nevertheless do not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subject to you, but rejoice that your names have been inscribed in the heavens."

[21] In that very hour he exulted in the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ and said, "I confess to you, Father, Lord of heaven and of earth, that you hid these things from the wise and understanding and revealed them to infants; yes, Father, because thus it became good pleasure before you.

[22] All things were delivered to me by my Father, and no one knows who the Son is except the Father, and who the Father is except the Son and he to whom the Son wishes to reveal him."

[23] And turning to the disciples privately he said, "Blessed are the eyes that see what you see.

[24] For I say to you that many prophets and kings wanted to see what you see and did not see, and to hear what you hear and did not hear."

[25] And behold, a certain lawyer rose up, tempting him, saying, "Teacher, by doing what shall I inherit eternal life?"

[26] And he said to him, "In the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ what is written? How do you read?"

[27] And he answering said, "'**You shall love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from your whole heart and with your whole soul and with your whole strength and with your whole mind**, and **your neighbor as yourself**.'"

[28] And he said to him, "You answered rightly; do this, and you will live."

[29] But he, wanting to justify himself, said to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, "And who is my neighbor?"

[30] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, taking it up, said, "A certain man was going down from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, and he fell in with robbers, who both stripped him and laid blows on him and went away, leaving him half dead.

[31] Now by coincidence a certain priest was going down on that road, and seeing him he passed by on the opposite side;

[32] and likewise also a ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, coming to the place and seeing, passed by on the opposite side.

[33] But a certain ⟦samaritans|Shomroni|Samaritan⟧, journeying, came to him, and seeing he was moved with compassion,

[34] and approaching he bound up his wounds, pouring on oil and wine; and having set him on his own beast, he brought him to an inn and took care of him.

[35] And on the morrow, putting forth two denarii, he gave them to the innkeeper and said, 'Take care of him, and whatever you spend besides, I, when I come back, will repay you.'

[36] Which of these three seems to you to have become a neighbor of the one who fell among the robbers?"

[37] And he said, "The one who did the mercy with him." And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "**Go, and you also do likewise.**"

[38] Now as they were going, he himself entered into a certain village; and a certain woman, ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ by name, received him.

[39] And she had a sister called ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, who also, having sat down beside the feet of the Lord, was hearing his word.

[40] But ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ was distracted about much service; and standing by she said, "Lord, is it no concern to you that my sister was leaving me behind alone to serve? Tell her therefore that she help me."

[41] But the Lord answering said to her, "⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧, ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧, you are anxious and in a commotion about many things,

[42] but **there is need of one thing; for ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ chose the good part, which will not be taken away from her**."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv29, 30, 37) — Hebrew name preserved. In v38 the Greek has only αὐτός, "he himself"; in v39 the critical text has "of the Lord" (τοῦ κυρίου WH Treg NA28; RP "of Jesus"), and in v41 "the Lord" (WH NA28; Treg RP "Jesus").
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv9, 11, 27) — divine name retained.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v21) — Hebrew divine Spirit. The printed text has the plain dative τῷ πνεύματι τῷ ἁγίῳ (WH Treg; NA28 ἐν τῷ …): "in" or "by" the Spirit. RP: "Jesus exulted in the spirit."
- **"appointed other seventy also" → "designated seventy-two others"** (v1) — Greek ἀνέδειξεν, literally "showed forth" (Acts 1:24 "show"), here of naming men to a task; a different verb from "appoint" (καθίστημι). ἑβδομήκοντα δύο, "seventy-two" (WH NA28, so also v17; Treg RP "seventy"). "Also" (καί, Treg RP) is not in the text (WH NA28). ἀνὰ δύο δύο, "two by two"; πρὸ προσώπου αὐτοῦ, "before his face".
- **"Therefore said he" → "And he was saying"** (v2) — Greek ἔλεγεν δέ, imperfect.
- **"pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he would send forth labourers" → "beseech therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he may thrust out workers"** (v2) — the same Greek as Matt 9:37–38 (only the order ἐργάτας ἐκβάλῃ differs) and the same wording; ἐκβάλλω is the verb "cast out", here a strong word for sending out: "thrust out", since "cast out workers" would be heard as driving them away.
- **"Carry neither purse, nor scrip, nor shoes: and salute no man by the way" → "Bear no purse, no bag, no sandals, and greet no one along the road"** (v4) — Greek βαστάζετε, βαλλάντιον, πήραν, ὑποδήματα, ἀσπάσησθε.
- **"Peace be to this house" → "Peace to this house"** (v5) — no verb in the Greek. "House" is οἰκία the first time and οἶκος the second; vv7 οἰκία.
- **"it shall rest upon it … it shall turn to you again" → "will rest upon him … it will turn back again upon you"** (v6) — Greek ἐπ' αὐτόν, masculine: "upon him" (the son of peace) or "upon it" (the house, οἶκος, also masculine); ἀνακάμψει, "turn back again" (as Acts 18:21). "A son of peace" is a Hebrew way of speaking: one who belongs to peace.
- **"in the same house remain … such things as they give: for the labourer is worthy of his hire" → "remain in that very house … the things from them, for the worker is worthy of his wage"** (v7) — Greek ἐν αὐτῇ τῇ οἰκίᾳ; μένετε, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a stay in a place: "remain"; τὰ παρ' αὐτῶν; the saying as in 1 Tim 5:18. μεταβαίνετε, "pass on".
- **"receive" → "accept"** (vv8, 10) — Greek δέχομαι, as Matt 10:14, 40; Mark 6:11. In v38 "received" is ὑποδέχομαι, to receive as a guest.
- **"is come nigh unto you" → "has drawn near upon you"** (v9) — Greek ἤγγικεν ἐφ' ὑμᾶς; in v11 "upon you" is not in the text (WH Treg NA28; RP has it).
- **"streets" → "broad streets"** (v10) — Greek πλατείας.
- **"the very dust of your city, which cleaveth on us, we do wipe off against you: notwithstanding be ye sure of this" → "Even the dust that clung to us out of your city, to the feet, we wipe off against you; nevertheless know this"** (v11) — Greek τὸν κολληθέντα ἡμῖν … εἰς τοὺς πόδας (WH Treg NA28; RP lacks "to the feet"); κολλάομαι is "be joined to" elsewhere, said of dust "clung". "Against you" is the plain dative ὑμῖν, "to you / for you".
- **"more tolerable" → "more bearable"; "But" → "Nevertheless"** (vv11, 12, 14, 20) — Greek ἀνεκτότερον; πλήν.
- **"mighty works" → "powers"** (v13) — Greek δυνάμεις, as Matt 11:21. Luke alone has "sitting" (καθήμενοι).
- **"which art exalted to heaven, shalt be thrust down to hell" → "will you be lifted up as far as heaven? As far as Hades you will go down"** (v15) — Greek μὴ ἕως οὐρανοῦ ὑψωθήσῃ; a question expecting "no" (WH Treg NA28; RP "who have been lifted up to heaven"). καταβήσῃ, "you will go down" (WH NA28); Treg RP καταβιβασθήσῃ, "you will be brought down". ᾅδης *Hadēs*, the realm of the dead; the line echoes Isa 14:13–15, where the Septuagint's *Hadēs* renders Hebrew שְׁאוֹל *Sheol*. The verse gives the Greek word.
- **"despiseth" → "sets aside"** (v16) — Greek ἀθετέω, four times; as John 12:48.
- **"I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven" → "I was beholding Satan fall as lightning out of heaven"** (v18) — Greek ἐθεώρουν, imperfect; πεσόντα, an aorist participle: "fall" or "having fallen"; ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ.
- **"I give unto you power … nothing shall by any means hurt you" → "I have given you the authority … nothing will by any means harm you"** (v19) — Greek δέδωκα, perfect (WH Treg NA28; RP δίδωμι "I give"); ἐξουσία "authority", δύναμις "power"; οὐ μή, a strong negation. οὐδέν can also be read "in nothing will he (the enemy) harm you".
- **"are written in heaven" → "have been inscribed in the heavens"** (v20) — Greek ἐγγέγραπται, perfect (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐγράφη "were written"); ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς, plural.
- **"rejoiced in spirit" → "exulted in the *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v21) — Greek ἠγαλλιάσατο; a different verb from "rejoice" (χαίρω) in v20.
- **"I thank thee … hast hid … unto babes: even so, Father; for so it seemed good in thy sight" → "I confess to you … you hid … to infants; yes, Father, because thus it became good pleasure before you"** (v21) — the same Greek as Matt 11:25–26 and the same wording; ἐξομολογοῦμαι with a dative: to acknowledge openly, to give praise. Luke has the compound ἀπέκρυψας where Matthew has ἔκρυψας; English "hid" for both.
- **"no man knoweth who the Son is, but the Father" → "no one knows who the Son is except the Father"** (v22) — Greek γινώσκει τίς ἐστιν; Matt 11:27 has "fully knows the Son" (ἐπιγινώσκει). "Him" after "reveal" is supplied. RP begins the verse "And turning to the disciples he said".
- **"he turned him unto his disciples, and said privately" → "turning to the disciples privately he said"** (v23) — Greek κατ' ἰδίαν stands between "turning to the disciples" and "he said" and can go with either.
- **"have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them" → "wanted to see what you see and did not see"** (v24) — Greek ἠθέλησαν (Matt 13:17 ἐπεθύμησαν, "desired"); "you" is stressed the first time (ὑμεῖς); no "them" in the Greek.
- **"stood up, and tempted him, saying, Master, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?" → "rose up, tempting him, saying, 'Teacher, by doing what shall I inherit eternal life?'"** (v25) — Greek ἐκπειράζων; διδάσκαλε; τί ποιήσας … κληρονομήσω, a participle and a future (Mark 10:17 has "what shall I do that I may inherit").
- **"What is written in the law? how readest thou?" → "In the *Torah* what is written? How do you read?"** (v26) — Greek order; no "it".
- **"with all thy heart, and with all thy soul" → "from your whole heart and with your whole soul"** (v27) — Greek ἐξ ὅλης τῆς καρδίας, then three times ἐν ὅλῃ (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐξ four times). Mark 12:30 has ἐξ throughout, Matt 22:37 ἐν. "The Lord" is κύριος without the article, standing for the name YHWH (Deut 6:5).
- **"Thou hast answered right" → "You answered rightly"** (v28) — Greek ὀρθῶς ἀπεκρίθης, aorist.
- **"willing to justify himself" → "wanting to justify himself"** (v29) — Greek θέλων.
- **"And Jesus answering said … fell among thieves, which stripped him of his raiment, and wounded him, and departed" → "*Yeshua*, taking it up, said … fell in with robbers, who both stripped him and laid blows on him and went away"** (v30) — Greek ὑπολαβών, "taking up" what was said ("it" supplied); no "and" (WH Treg NA28; RP δέ). λῃσταῖς περιέπεσεν; λῃστής is a robber, not a thief; πληγὰς ἐπιθέντες. In v36 "fell among" is a different verb, ἐμπεσόντος εἰς.
- **"by chance … he passed by on the other side" → "by coincidence … he passed by on the opposite side"** (vv31, 32) — Greek κατὰ συγκυρίαν; ἀντιπαρῆλθεν.
- **"when he was at the place, came and looked on him" → "coming to the place and seeing"** (v32) — the text has only ἐλθών (WH Treg); NA28 RP add γενόμενος, "being at the place, having come". No "him" in the Greek, nor in v33 (WH NA28; Treg RP have it there).
- **"came where he was … had compassion on him" → "came to him … was moved with compassion"** (v33) — Greek ἦλθεν κατ' αὐτόν; ἐσπλαγχνίσθη, from σπλάγχνα, the inward parts.
- **"took out two pence … whatsoever thou spendest more, when I come again, I will repay thee" → "putting forth two denarii … whatever you spend besides, I, when I come back, will repay you"** (v35) — Greek ἐκβαλών, the verb "cast out" (v2), here of bringing out; "I" (ἐγώ) is stressed. RP adds "when he departed" and "to him".
- **"thinkest thou, was neighbour unto him" → "seems to you to have become a neighbor of the one"** (v36) — Greek δοκεῖ σοι γεγονέναι, "to have become".
- **"He that shewed mercy on him" → "The one who did the mercy with him"** (v37) — Greek ὁ ποιήσας τὸ ἔλεος μετ' αὐτοῦ, a Hebrew way of speaking (as Jas 2:13). καὶ σύ, "you also".
- **"received him into her house" → "received him"** (v38) — Greek ὑπεδέξατο αὐτόν (NA28). The other editions add the house, each in its own words: WH εἰς τὴν οἰκίαν, "into the house"; Treg RP εἰς τὸν οἶκον αὐτῆς, "into her house". No one of these readings has most editions, so the printed text stands.
- **"sat at Jesus' feet, and heard his word" → "having sat down beside the feet of the Lord, was hearing his word"** (v39) — Greek παρακαθεσθεῖσα πρός; ἤκουεν, imperfect; λόγος, "word".
- **"was cumbered about much serving … dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me" → "was distracted about much service … is it no concern to you that my sister was leaving me behind alone to serve? Tell her therefore that she help me"** (v40) — Greek περιεσπᾶτο, literally "was being drawn about"; διακονία, "service"; οὐ μέλει σοι (as Mark 4:38); κατέλειπεν, imperfect (WH Treg RP; NA28 κατέλιπεν, "left"); ἐπιστᾶσα, "standing by".
- **"thou art careful and troubled" → "you are anxious and in a commotion"** (v41) — Greek μεριμνᾷς, as Matt 6:25–34; θορυβάζῃ (WH Treg NA28; RP τυρβάζῃ), of the root of θόρυβος, "commotion".
- **"one thing is needful" → "there is need of one thing"** (v42) — Greek ἑνὸς δέ ἐστιν χρεία (Treg NA28 RP); WH reads ὀλίγων δέ ἐστιν χρεία ἢ ἑνός, "but there is need of few things, or of one". ἑνός can be "one thing" or "one person". "For *Miryam*" is Μαριὰμ γάρ (WH NA28; Treg RP "but Mary"); ἐξελέξατο, aorist, "chose"; μερίς, "part".

### Notes
- **v1** — the editions differ: "seventy-two" WH NA28 (the text); "seventy" Treg RP.
- **v27** quotes Deut 6:5 + Lev 19:18.
- **v2–16** — much of this speech has the same Greek as Matt 9:37–38; 10:7–16, 40; 11:21–23, and vv21–24 as Matt 11:25–27; 13:16–17; where the Greek is the same, the wording is the same.
