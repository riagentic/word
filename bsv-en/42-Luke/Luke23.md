## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v8, 20, 25, 26, 28, 34, 42, 46, 52) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v35, 40, 47, 51) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v2, 35, 39) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"v17 absent"** — Luke 23:17 ("For of necessity he must release one unto them at the feast") is not in WH, Treg or NA28; only RP has it. The verse stays out.
- **"Arimathaea" → "Arimathea, a city of the Jews"** (v51) — Greek ἀπὸ Ἁριμαθαίας πόλεως τῶν Ἰουδαίων; often identified with Ramathaim(-zophim), Samuel's town (1 Sam 1:1; LXX Αρμαθαιμ), but the identification is not certain.
- **"And the whole multitude of them arose, and led him unto Pilate" → "And the whole multitude of them, rising, led him before Pilate"** (v1) — Greek ἀναστάν; πλῆθος, "multitude"; ἤγαγον … ἐπὶ τὸν Πιλᾶτον.
- **"We found this fellow perverting the nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, saying that he himself is Christ a King" → "This one we found perverting our nation and hindering the giving of tribute to Caesar and saying that he himself is Mashiach, a king"** (v2) — Greek τοῦτον stands first; διαστρέφοντα (Acts 13:10); "our" (WH Treg NA28); κωλύοντα φόρους Καίσαρι διδόναι, literally "hindering to give tributes to Caesar"; χριστὸν βασιλέα without articles: "Mashiach, a king", or "an anointed king". ἑαυτόν (Treg NA28 RP) and αὑτόν (WH) mean the same.
- **"And he answered him and said, Thou sayest it" → "And he, answering him, said, 'You say it'"** (v3) — Greek σὺ λέγεις, as Mark 15:2.
- **"I find no fault in this man" → "I find no cause in this man"** (v4) — Greek αἴτιον, "cause, ground" for a charge (so v14, 22; Acts 19:40); ἀρχιερεῖς, "high priests".
- **"And they were the more fierce, saying, He stirreth up the people, teaching throughout all Jewry, beginning from Galilee to this place" → "But they were growing more forceful, saying, 'He stirs up the people, teaching throughout the whole of Judea, even beginning from Galilee as far as here'"** (v5) — Greek ἐπίσχυον, an imperfect, "were growing stronger"; ἀνασείει (Mark 15:11); καὶ ἀρξάμενος (WH Treg NA28); ἕως ὧδε.
- **"When Pilate heard of Galilee, he asked whether the man were a Galilaean" → "And Pilate, hearing it, asked whether the man is a Galilean"** (v6) — "Galilee" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28; ἀκούσας has no object, and "it" is supplied.
- **"And as soon as he knew that he belonged unto Herod's jurisdiction, he sent him to Herod, who himself also was at Jerusalem at that time" → "and coming to know that he is from the authority of Herod, he sent him up to Herod, who was himself also in Jerusalem in these days"** (v7) — Greek ἐπιγνούς; ἐκ τῆς ἐξουσίας Ἡρῴδου, "from (under) the authority of Herod"; ἀνέπεμψεν, "sent up" to the one who has the say (as Acts 25:21); in v11 and v15 the same verb is "sent back" (as Phlm 12).
- **"he was exceeding glad: for he was desirous to see him of a long season, because he had heard many things of him; and he hoped to have seen some miracle done by him" → "rejoiced greatly, for he had been wanting for a considerable time to see him, because of hearing about him, and he was hoping to see some sign done by him"** (v8) — Greek ἐχάρη λίαν; ἐξ ἱκανῶν χρόνων, literally "from considerable times"; "many things" (RP) is not in the critical text; σημεῖον, "sign"; γινόμενον, "coming to pass".
- **"Then he questioned with him in many words; but he answered him nothing" → "And he was asking him in considerable words; but he himself answered him nothing"** (v9) — Greek ἐπηρώτα, an imperfect; ἐν λόγοις ἱκανοῖς, the ἱκανός of v8: at length; αὐτὸς δέ.
- **"stood and vehemently accused him" → "stood there, vigorously accusing him"** (v10) — Greek εἱστήκεισαν; εὐτόνως (Acts 18:28).
- **"And Herod with his men of war set him at nought, and mocked him, and arrayed him in a gorgeous robe, and sent him again to Pilate" → "And Herod also with his troops, having despised him and mocked him, clothing him in bright clothing, sent him back to Pilate"** (v11) — Greek καὶ ὁ Ἡρῴδης, "Herod also" (NA28 and the printed SBLGNT; WH Treg without καί — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides); ἐξουθενήσας, "having despised, made nothing of"; στρατεύμασιν, "troops, soldiery"; ἐσθῆτα λαμπράν, "bright clothing" (Jas 2:2): white or shining.
- **"And the same day Pilate and Herod were made friends together: for before they were at enmity between themselves" → "And both Herod and Pilate became friends with one another on that very day; for before they had been at enmity toward each other"** (v12) — Greek order "Herod and Pilate" (WH Treg NA28); προϋπῆρχον ἐν ἔχθρᾳ ὄντες. Editions: πρὸς αὐτούς Treg NA28, "toward them" ] αὑτούς WH (and the printed SBLGNT), "toward themselves"; ἑαυτούς RP: the difference is one of breathing only, and the sense of the passage is "each other".
- **"Ye have brought this man unto me, as one that perverteth the people: and, behold, I, having examined him before you, have found no fault in this man touching those things whereof ye accuse him" → "You brought to me this man as one turning the people away, and behold, I, having examined him before you, found in this man no cause of the things of which you accuse him"** (v14) — Greek ἀποστρέφοντα, "turning away" (a different word from διαστρέφοντα in v2); ἀνακρίνας; οὐθὲν … αἴτιον.
- **"No, nor yet Herod: for I sent you to him; and, lo, nothing worthy of death is done unto him" → "But neither did Herod, for he sent him back to us; and behold, nothing worthy of death has been practiced by him"** (v15) — Greek ἀνέπεμψεν γὰρ αὐτὸν πρὸς ἡμᾶς (WH NA28); Treg RP have ἀνέπεμψα γὰρ ὑμᾶς πρὸς αὐτόν, "for I sent you to him", which the KJV follows. πεπραγμένον αὐτῷ, "practiced by him" (πράσσω as Acts 26:31).
- **"I will therefore chastise him, and release him" → "Having chastised him, therefore, I will release him"** (v16, 22) — Greek παιδεύσας, a participle. παιδεύω is elsewhere "train" (Heb 12:6); here it is the governor's word for a beating given as a lesson, and "train" would be wrong: "chastise" is kept. ἀπολύσω, "release" (the verb "send away").
- **"And they cried out all at once, saying, Away with this man, and release unto us Barabbas" → "But they cried out all together, saying, 'Take this one away, and release to us Barabbas!'"** (v18) — Greek ἀνέκραγον παμπληθεί, "with the whole multitude"; αἶρε τοῦτον (as Acts 21:36).
- **"(Who for a certain sedition made in the city, and for murder, was cast into prison.)" → "one who had been cast into the prison because of a certain insurrection that came to pass in the city, and murder"** (v19) — Greek ὅστις ἦν … βληθεὶς ἐν τῇ φυλακῇ (WH Treg NA28); στάσιν, "insurrection" (Mark 15:7); γενομένην.
- **"Pilate therefore, willing to release Jesus, spake again to them" → "And again Pilate addressed them, wanting to release Yeshua"** (v20) — Greek δέ (WH Treg NA28). Editions: προσεφώνησεν αὐτοῖς WH NA28, "addressed them" ] without αὐτοῖς Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides. θέλων, "wanting".
- **"But they cried, saying, Crucify him, crucify him" → "But they were calling out, saying, 'Crucify, crucify him!'"** (v21) — Greek ἐπεφώνουν, an imperfect (Acts 21:34); σταύρου σταύρου αὐτόν, "him" once.
- **"Why, what evil hath he done? I have found no cause of death in him" → "For what evil did this one do? I found no cause of death in him"** (v22) — Greek τί γὰρ κακὸν ἐποίησεν οὗτος (Mark 15:14); οὐδὲν αἴτιον θανάτου.
- **"And they were instant with loud voices, requiring that he might be crucified. And the voices of them and of the chief priests prevailed" → "But they were pressing upon him with loud voices, asking that he be crucified, and their voices were prevailing"** (v23) — Greek ἐπέκειντο, "lay upon, pressed"; "him" is supplied; αἰτούμενοι, "asking"; κατίσχυον, an imperfect (the verb of 21:36); "and of the chief priests" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28.
- **"And Pilate gave sentence that it should be as they required" → "And Pilate gave sentence that their request be done"** (v24) — Greek ἐπέκρινεν, "adjudged"; γενέσθαι τὸ αἴτημα αὐτῶν, "that what they asked should come to pass".
- **"And he released unto them him that for sedition and murder was cast into prison, whom they had desired; but he delivered Jesus to their will" → "and he released the one who had been cast into prison because of insurrection and murder, whom they were asking for, but Yeshua he delivered up to their will"** (v25) — "unto them" is not in the Greek; ᾐτοῦντο, an imperfect; παρέδωκεν, "delivered up".
- **"they laid hold upon one Simon, a Cyrenian, coming out of the country, and on him they laid the cross, that he might bear it after Jesus" → "laying hold of a certain Simon of Cyrene, coming from the field, they laid upon him the cross, to bear it behind Yeshua"** (v26) — Greek ἐπιλαβόμενοι; ἐρχόμενον ἀπ' ἀγροῦ, word for word Mark 15:21; ὄπισθεν, "behind".
- **"a great company of people, and of women, which also bewailed and lamented him" → "a great multitude of the people, and of women who were wailing and lamenting him"** (v27) — Greek πλῆθος; ἐκόπτοντο, "were beating themselves" in mourning, "wailing" (Matt 24:30); ἐθρήνουν, "were lamenting" (John 16:20).
- **"weep not for me, but weep for yourselves, and for your children" → "do not weep over me; nevertheless weep over yourselves and over your children"** (v28) — Greek ἐπ' ἐμέ, ἐφ' ἑαυτάς, ἐπὶ τὰ τέκνα; πλήν.
- **"the days are coming, in the which they shall say, Blessed are the barren, and the wombs that never bare, and the paps which never gave suck" → "days are coming in which they will say, 'Blessed are the barren, and the wombs which did not give birth, and breasts which did not nourish'"** (v29) — Greek ἔρχονται ἡμέραι, without the article; ἐγέννησαν; ἔθρεψαν (WH Treg NA28), "nourished, fed"; RP has ἐθήλασαν, "gave suck".
- **"For if they do these things in a green tree, what shall be done in the dry?" → "because if they do these things in the moist wood, what will come to pass in the dry?"** (v31) — Greek ἐν τῷ ὑγρῷ ξύλῳ, "in the wet (sappy) wood", wood that is still green and hard to burn; ξύλον is "wood" and also "tree"; γένηται.
- **"And there were also two other, malefactors, led with him to be put to death" → "And there were also being led others, two evildoers, with him to be done away with"** (v32) — Greek ἕτεροι κακοῦργοι δύο (WH NA28); κακοῦργος, "evildoer" (2 Tim 2:9); ἀναιρεθῆναι (as 22:2).
- **"the place, which is called Calvary … one on the right hand, and the other on the left" → "the place called Skull … one at the right hand and one at the left"** (v33) — Greek Κρανίον, "Skull" ("Calvary" is the Latin word for it); ὃν μὲν ἐκ δεξιῶν ὃν δὲ ἐξ ἀριστερῶν.
- **"Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots" → "And Yeshua was saying, 'Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they do.' And dividing his garments among themselves, they cast lots"** (v34) — Greek ἔλεγεν, an imperfect; διαμεριζόμενοι (Mark 15:24); κλήρους, "lots" (NA28 and the printed SBLGNT; WH Treg RP κλῆρον, "a lot" — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides).
- **"v34a textual note"** — the sentence "And Yeshua was saying, 'Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they do'" is in the text of Treg and NA28 (and RP); WH prints it in double brackets as a very early addition. Some early manuscripts do not have it. The verse follows the majority of the four editions.
- **"And the people stood beholding. And the rulers also with them derided him, saying, He saved others; let him save himself, if he be Christ, the chosen of God" → "And the people stood beholding. And the rulers also were turning up their noses, saying, 'Others he saved; let him save himself, if this one is the Mashiach of Elohim, the Elect'"** (v35) — Greek ἐξεμυκτήριζον, "turned up the nose, sneered" (16:14); "with them" (RP) is not in the critical text; ὁ χριστὸς τοῦ θεοῦ, ὁ ἐκλεκτός (WH NA28): "of God" goes with "the Mashiach"; Treg RP have ὁ τοῦ θεοῦ ἐκλεκτός, "the Elect of God".
- **"And the soldiers also mocked him, coming to him, and offering him vinegar" → "And the soldiers also mocked him, approaching, offering him vinegar"** (v36) — Greek ἐνέπαιξαν (WH NA28), a simple past; ὄξος, "vinegar", the soldiers' sour wine (Mark 15:36).
- **"And a superscription also was written over him in letters of Greek, and Latin, and Hebrew, THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS" → "And there was also an inscription over him: 'The King of the Jews, this one'"** (v38) — "written … in letters of Greek, and Latin, and Hebrew" (RP) is not in the critical text; Greek ὁ βασιλεὺς τῶν Ἰουδαίων οὗτος, without a verb.
- **"And one of the malefactors which were hanged railed on him, saying, If thou be Christ, save thyself and us" → "And one of the evildoers who were hanged was blaspheming him, saying, 'Are you not the Mashiach? Save yourself and us'"** (v39) — Greek ἐβλασφήμει, an imperfect (as Mark 15:29); οὐχὶ σὺ εἶ ὁ χριστός (WH Treg NA28), a question.
- **"But the other answering rebuked him, saying, Dost not thou fear God, seeing thou art in the same condemnation?" → "But the other, answering, rebuking him, said, 'Do you not even fear Elohim, because you are in the same sentence?'"** (v40) — Greek οὐδὲ φοβῇ σὺ τὸν θεόν, "not even you" or "do you not even fear"; κρίματι, "sentence".
- **"for we receive the due reward of our deeds: but this man hath done nothing amiss" → "for we are receiving back things worthy of what we practiced; but this one practiced nothing amiss"** (v41) — Greek ἄξια ὧν ἐπράξαμεν ἀπολαμβάνομεν; ἔπραξεν; ἄτοπον, "out of place, amiss".
- **"And he said unto Jesus, Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom" → "And he was saying, 'Yeshua, remember me when you come into your kingdom'"** (v42) — Greek ἔλεγεν· Ἰησοῦ, an address (WH Treg NA28); "Lord" (RP) is not in the critical text. Editions: εἰς τὴν βασιλείαν WH NA28, "into your kingdom" ] ἐν τῇ βασιλείᾳ Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT), "in your kingdom", that is, in your royal rule — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides.
- **"Verily I say unto thee, To day shalt thou be with me in paradise" → "Amen, I say to you today you will be with me in the paradise"** (v43) — Greek ἀμήν σοι λέγω σήμερον μετ' ἐμοῦ ἔσῃ ἐν τῷ παραδείσῳ. The old manuscripts have no punctuation, and the Greek printed here has no comma either, so the verse has none after "Amen": σήμερον "today" can go with what follows ("I say to you, today you will be with me") or with what precedes ("I say to you today, you will be with me"). Most editions and versions put the comma before "today". ἐν τῷ παραδείσῳ, with the article (2 Cor 12:4).
- **"And it was about the sixth hour, and there was a darkness over all the earth until the ninth hour. And the sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was rent in the midst" → "And it was already about the sixth hour, and darkness came upon the whole earth until the ninth hour, the sun having failed; and the veil of the sanctuary was torn in the middle"** (v44–45) — Greek ἤδη, "already" (WH Treg NA28); σκότος ἐγένετο ἐφ' ὅλην τὴν γῆν ἕως ὥρας ἐνάτης, word for word Mark 15:33 (γῆ is "earth" or "land"); τοῦ ἡλίου ἐκλιπόντος (WH NA28), "the sun failing, giving out" (ἐκλείπω as 22:32); Treg RP have καὶ ἐσκοτίσθη ὁ ἥλιος, "and the sun was darkened". ναοῦ, the sanctuary itself; μέσον.
- **"And when Jesus had cried with a loud voice, he said, Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit: and having said thus, he gave up the ghost" → "And calling with a loud voice, Yeshua said, 'Father, into your hands I entrust my spirit.' And having said this, he breathed his last"** (v46) — Greek φωνήσας φωνῇ μεγάλῃ; παρατίθεμαι (WH Treg NA28), a present, "I entrust, deposit"; ἐξέπνευσεν (Mark 15:37).
- **"Now when the centurion saw what was done, he glorified God, saying, Certainly this was a righteous man" → "And the centurion, seeing what had come to pass, was glorifying Elohim, saying, 'Really this man was righteous'"** (v47) — Greek ἑκατοντάρχης (Mark has the Latin κεντυρίων); ἐδόξαζεν, an imperfect (WH Treg NA28); ὄντως, "really"; δίκαιος, "righteous", which in a court is also "innocent".
- **"And all the people that came together to that sight, beholding the things which were done, smote their breasts, and returned" → "And all the crowds that had come together for this sight, having beheld the things that had come to pass, were returning, striking their breasts"** (v48) — Greek ὄχλοι; θεωρίαν, "sight, spectacle", and θεωρήσαντες, of one root; τύπτοντες τὰ στήθη; ὑπέστρεφον, an imperfect.
- **"And all his acquaintance, and the women that followed him from Galilee, stood afar off, beholding these things" → "And all those known to him stood from afar, and women, those following along with him from Galilee, seeing these things"** (v49) — Greek οἱ γνωστοὶ αὐτῷ (John 18:15). Editions: ἀπὸ μακρόθεν WH NA28 ] μακρόθεν Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) — 2–2, NA28 decides; the sense is the same. γυναῖκες without the article; ὁρῶσαι, "seeing", feminine: said of the women.
- **"a man named Joseph, a counsellor; and he was a good man, and a just: (the same had not consented to the counsel and deed of them;) he was of Arimathaea, a city of the Jews: who also himself waited for the kingdom of God" → "a man, Joseph by name, being a councillor, a good and righteous man — this one had not consented to their counsel and practice — from Arimathea, a city of the Jews, who was awaiting the kingdom of Elohim"** (v50–51) — the Greek sentence is kept in its own order, with the inserted clause between dashes as printed. βουλευτής, "councillor", a member of a council (βουλή): the word "Sanhedrin" is not here (the earlier token is removed). βουλῇ, "counsel"; πράξει, "practice, deed"; προσεδέχετο, "was awaiting" (Mark 15:43); "also himself" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"This man went unto Pilate, and begged the body of Jesus" → "this one, approaching Pilate, asked for the body of Yeshua"** (v52) — word for word Matt 27:58.
- **"And he took it down, and wrapped it in linen, and laid it in a sepulchre that was hewn in stone, wherein never man before was laid" → "and taking it down, he wrapped it in a linen cloth and laid him in a grave hewn in rock, where no one was yet lying"** (v53) — Greek ἐνετύλιξεν … σινδόνι (Matt 27:59); ἔθηκεν αὐτόν, "him" (WH Treg NA28); μνήματι, "grave" (in v55 μνημεῖον, "tomb"); λαξευτῷ, "cut in stone"; οὐκ ἦν οὐδεὶς οὔπω κείμενος.
- **"And that day was the preparation, and the sabbath drew on" → "And it was a day of Preparation, and a Shabbat was dawning"** (v54) — Greek ἡμέρα ἦν παρασκευῆς; σάββατον ἐπέφωσκεν, "was dawning" (Matt 28:1): said of the day coming on, though the Shabbat begins at evening.
- **"And the women also, which came with him from Galilee, followed after, and beheld the sepulchre, and how his body was laid" → "And the women who had come together with him out of Galilee, having followed after, beheld the tomb and how his body was laid"** (v55) — Greek κατακολουθήσασαι (Acts 16:17); συνεληλυθυῖαι; ἐθεάσαντο; μνημεῖον.
- **"And they returned, and prepared spices and ointments; and rested the sabbath day according to the commandment" → "and returning, they made ready spices and ointments. And on the Shabbat indeed they were quiet according to the commandment,"** (v56) — Greek ἡτοίμασαν; ἀρώματα καὶ μύρα; ἡσύχασαν, "were quiet, kept still" (Acts 11:18), that is, rested. τὸ μὲν σάββατον … is the first half of a sentence that goes on in 24:1 (τῇ δὲ μιᾷ …, "but on the first day …"), so the chapter ends with a comma as the Greek is printed.
