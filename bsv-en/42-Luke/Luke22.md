# Luke 22

[1] Now the feast of the ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ was drawing near, the one called ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[2] And the high priests and the scribes were seeking how they might do away with him, for they were fearing the people.

[3] And Satan entered into ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, the one called Iscariot, who was of the number of the twelve;

[4] and going away, he talked with the high priests and captains about how he might deliver him up to them.

[5] And they rejoiced and agreed to give him silver.

[6] And he consented, and he was seeking an opportunity to deliver him up to them apart from a crowd.

[7] And the day of the ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ came, on which the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ had to be sacrificed;

[8] and he sent ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, saying, "Go and make ready for us the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧, that we may eat."

[9] And they said to him, "Where do you want us to make ready?"

[10] And he said to them, "Behold, when you have entered into the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you; follow him into the house into which he goes.

[11] And you shall say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says to you, "Where is the guest room, where I may eat the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ with my disciples?"'

[12] And that one will show you a large upper room, spread; there make ready."

[13] And going away, they found even as he had said to them, and they made ready the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[14] And when the hour came, he reclined, and the apostles with him.

[15] And he said to them, "With desire I desired to eat this ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ with you before I suffer;

[16] for I say to you that I shall by no means eat it until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[17] And having accepted a cup, having given thanks, he said, "Take this and divide it among yourselves;

[18] for I say to you, I shall by no means drink from now on from the produce of the vine until the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ comes."

[19] And taking bread, having given thanks, he broke it and gave to them, saying, "This is my body, which is being given for you; do this unto my remembrance."

[20] And the cup in the same way, after the supper, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is being poured out for you.

[21] Nevertheless, behold, the hand of the one delivering me up is with me on the table;

[22] because the Son of Man indeed goes according to what has been determined, nevertheless woe to that man through whom he is delivered up!"

[23] And they began to dispute among themselves which of them it might be who was about to do this.

[24] And there came to be also a contention among them, which of them is reputed to be greater.

[25] But he said to them, "The kings of the nations are lords over them, and those having authority over them are called benefactors.

[26] But you, not so; but let the greater among you become as the younger, and the one leading as the one serving.

[27] For who is greater, the one reclining or the one serving? Is it not the one reclining? But I am in the midst of you as the one serving.

[28] "But you are those who have continued with me in my trials;

[29] and I covenant to you, even as my Father covenanted to me, a kingdom,

[30] that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and you will sit upon thrones judging the twelve tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[31] "⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, behold, Satan asked for you, to sift you as the wheat;

[32] but I besought concerning you that your faith may not fail; and you, when once you have turned back, make your brothers stable."

[33] But he said to him, "Lord, with you I am ready to go both to prison and to death."

[34] But he said, "I say to you, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, a rooster will not crow today until you have three times denied knowing me."

[35] And he said to them, "When I sent you without purse and bag and sandals, were you in lack of anything?" And they said, "Of nothing."

[36] And he said to them, "But now, let the one who has a purse take it, likewise also a bag; and let the one who has none sell his garment and buy a sword.

[37] For I say to you that this which is written must be finished in me: '**And with lawless ones he was reckoned**.' For indeed that which concerns me has an end."

[38] And they said, "Lord, behold, here are two swords." And he said to them, "It is enough."

[39] And going out, he went according to the custom to ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧; and the disciples also followed him.

[40] And coming to the place, he said to them, "Pray not to enter into trial."

[41] And he himself was drawn away from them about a stone's throw, and kneeling down he was praying,

[42] saying, "Father, if you wish, take this cup away from me; nevertheless, not my will but yours be done."

[43] And there appeared to him an angel from heaven, strengthening him.

[44] And being in agony, he was praying more earnestly; and his sweat became as clots of blood going down upon the ground.

[45] And rising from the prayer, coming to the disciples, he found them asleep from the sorrow,

[46] and he said to them, "Why do you sleep? Rise and pray, that you may not enter into trial."

[47] While he was still speaking, behold, a crowd, and the one called ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, one of the twelve, was going before them, and he drew near to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ to kiss him.

[48] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, with a kiss do you deliver up the Son of Man?"

[49] And those around him, seeing what was going to be, said, "Lord, shall we strike with a sword?"

[50] And a certain one of them struck the slave of the high priest and took off his right ear.

[51] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, answering, said, "Let be, as far as this." And touching the ear, he healed him.

[52] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to those who had arrived against him, high priests and captains of the temple and elders, "As against a robber you came out with swords and clubs?

[53] Daily, while I was with you in the temple, you did not stretch out your hands against me; but this is your hour, and the authority of the darkness."

[54] And apprehending him, they led him and brought him into the house of the high priest; and ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was following from afar.

[55] And when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the court and had sat down together, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was sitting in the midst of them.

[56] And a certain slave woman, seeing him sitting at the light and gazing at him, said, "This one also was with him."

[57] But he denied, saying, "I do not know him, woman."

[58] And after a short while another, seeing him, said, "You also are of them." But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said, "Man, I am not."

[59] And when about one hour had passed, a certain other kept insisting, saying, "Of a truth this one also was with him, for he is also a Galilean."

[60] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said, "Man, I do not know what you are saying." And instantly, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.

[61] And the Lord, turning, looked at ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧; and ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was reminded of the word of the Lord, how he said to him, "Before a rooster crows today, you will deny me three times."

[62] And going out outside, he wept bitterly.

[63] And the men who were holding him were mocking him, beating him,

[64] and having covered him, they were asking him, saying, "Prophesy: who is the one who struck you?"

[65] And many other things they were saying against him, blaspheming.

[66] And when it became day, the eldership of the people was gathered together, both high priests and scribes, and they led him away into their ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧,

[67] saying, "If you are the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, tell us." But he said to them, "If I tell you, you will by no means believe;

[68] and if I ask, you will by no means answer.

[69] But from now on **the Son of Man will be sitting at the right hand of the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**"

[70] And they all said, "You, then, are the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?" And he said to them, "You say that I am."

[71] And they said, "What need do we still have of testimony? For we ourselves heard from his mouth."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v47, 48, 51, 52) — Hebrew name preserved. The name stands in the Greek only in these verses; where the earlier text supplied it or "Peter" (v8, 33, 34, 63), the pronoun of the Greek is restored.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v16, 18, 69, 70) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v67) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v8, 34, 54, 55, 58, 60, 61) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"the feast of unleavened bread drew nigh, which is called the Passover" → "the feast of the Matzot was drawing near, the one called Pesach"** (v1) — Greek ἤγγιζεν, an imperfect; ἡ ἑορτὴ τῶν ἀζύμων, "the feast of the unleavened (loaves)"; πάσχα.
- **"the chief priests and scribes sought how they might kill him; for they feared the people" → "the high priests and the scribes were seeking how they might do away with him, for they were fearing the people"** (v2) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς; ἐζήτουν, ἐφοβοῦντο, imperfects; ἀνέλωσιν, from ἀναιρέω, "do away with", Luke's usual word for killing (Acts 2:23), not ἀποκτείνω "kill" (Mark 14:1).
- **"Then entered Satan into Judas surnamed Iscariot, being of the number of the twelve" → "And Satan entered into Judas, the one called Iscariot, who was of the number of the twelve"** (v3) — Greek δέ; Σατανᾶς without the article (with it in v31); καλούμενον (WH Treg NA28).
- **"communed with the chief priests and captains, how he might betray him unto them" → "talked with the high priests and captains about how he might deliver him up to them"** (v4) — Greek συνελάλησεν (as Mark 9:4); στρατηγοῖς, the captains of the temple guard (v52; Acts 4:1); παραδῷ, "deliver up", so also v6, 21, 22, 48 ("betray" is another word, and Luke does not use it of the act).
- **"And they were glad, and covenanted to give him money" → "And they rejoiced and agreed to give him silver"** (v5) — Greek ἐχάρησαν (Mark 14:11); συνέθεντο, "agreed together" (Acts 23:20); ἀργύριον, "silver".
- **"And he promised, and sought opportunity to betray him unto them in the absence of the multitude" → "And he consented, and he was seeking an opportunity to deliver him up to them apart from a crowd"** (v6) — Greek ἐξωμολόγησεν, "agreed fully, gave his word"; ἐζήτει, an imperfect; εὐκαιρίαν (Matt 26:16); ἄτερ ὄχλου, "without a crowd": no article.
- **"Then came the day of unleavened bread, when the passover must be killed" → "And the day of the Matzot came, on which the Pesach had to be sacrificed"** (v7) — Greek ἔδει θύεσθαι, "it was necessary that … be sacrificed" (θύω as Mark 14:12); τὸ πάσχα is here the lamb: no "lamb" is added.
- **"Go and prepare us the passover, that we may eat" → "Go and make ready for us the Pesach, that we may eat"** (v8) — Greek ἑτοιμάσατε, "make ready", four times (v8, 9, 12, 13), as Mark 14:12–16; φάγωμεν without an object.
- **"Where wilt thou that we prepare?" → "Where do you want us to make ready?"** (v9) — Greek ποῦ θέλεις ἑτοιμάσωμεν, as Mark 14:12.
- **"when ye are entered into the city, there shall a man meet you, bearing a pitcher of water; follow him into the house where he entereth in" → "when you have entered into the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you; follow him into the house into which he goes"** (v10) — Greek ἄνθρωπος κεράμιον ὕδατος βαστάζων, word for word Mark 14:13; εἰς ἣν εἰσπορεύεται (WH Treg NA28).
- **"the goodman of the house, The Master saith unto thee, Where is the guestchamber" → "the master of the house, 'The Teacher says to you, "Where is the guest room"'"** (v11) — Greek τῷ οἰκοδεσπότῃ τῆς οἰκίας, literally "the house-master of the house"; ὁ διδάσκαλος; κατάλυμα; the question is word for word Mark 14:14 without "my".
- **"And he shall shew you a large upper room furnished: there make ready" → "And that one will show you a large upper room, spread; there make ready"** (v12) — Greek κἀκεῖνος, "and that one"; ἐστρωμένον, "spread" with couches or coverings (Mark 14:15).
- **"And they went, and found as he had said unto them" → "And going away, they found even as he had said to them"** (v13) — Greek ἀπελθόντες; καθὼς εἰρήκει, a pluperfect (WH Treg NA28).
- **"he sat down, and the twelve apostles with him" → "he reclined, and the apostles with him"** (v14) — Greek ἀνέπεσεν, "lay back" at table; "twelve" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"With desire I have desired to eat this passover with you" → "With desire I desired to eat this Pesach with you"** (v15) — Greek ἐπιθυμίᾳ ἐπεθύμησα, the Hebrew way of strengthening a verb, kept word for word ("I greatly desired"); ἐπεθύμησα is a past tense.
- **"I will not any more eat thereof" → "I shall by no means eat it"** (v16) — Greek οὐ μὴ φάγω αὐτό (WH Treg NA28), the strong negation, also v18, 67, 68; "any more" (Treg RP οὐκέτι) is not in WH NA28.
- **"And he took the cup, and gave thanks, and said, Take this, and divide it among yourselves" → "And having accepted a cup, having given thanks, he said, 'Take this and divide it among yourselves'"** (v17) — Greek δεξάμενος, "accepting, receiving" what is handed to him (not λαβών "taking", v19); ποτήριον without the article.
- **"I will not drink of the fruit of the vine, until the kingdom of God shall come" → "I shall by no means drink from now on from the produce of the vine until the kingdom of Elohim comes"** (v18) — Greek οὐ μὴ πίω; ἀπὸ τοῦ νῦν, "from now on" (WH Treg NA28; not in RP), as v69; γένημα τῆς ἀμπέλου (Mark 14:25).
- **"And he took bread, and gave thanks, and brake it, and gave unto them, saying, This is my body which is given for you: this do in remembrance of me" → "And taking bread, having given thanks, he broke it and gave to them, saying, 'This is my body, which is being given for you; do this unto my remembrance'"** (v19) — Greek λαβὼν ἄρτον εὐχαριστήσας ἔκλασεν; διδόμενον, a present participle; εἰς τὴν ἐμὴν ἀνάμνησιν, as 1 Cor 11:24.
- **"Likewise also the cup after supper, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood, which is shed for you" → "And the cup in the same way, after the supper, saying, 'This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is being poured out for you'"** (v20) — Greek καὶ τὸ ποτήριον ὡσαύτως μετὰ τὸ δειπνῆσαι (Treg NA28; compare 1 Cor 11:25); the saying has no verb, and "is" is supplied; διαθήκη, "covenant". τὸ ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν ἐκχυννόμενον, a present participle, stands in the nominative, the case of "cup", not in the dative of "blood": by grammar it is the cup that is poured out, which English "which" cannot show.
- **"vv19b–20 textual note"** — the words from "which is being given for you" (v19) to the end of v20 are in the text of Treg and NA28 (and RP); WH prints them in double brackets as a very early addition, and the SBLGNT in single brackets. The verse follows the majority of the four editions, so "the new covenant" stands here; in Mark 14:24 and Matt 26:28 the critical text has "covenant" without "new".
- **"But, behold, the hand of him that betrayeth me is with me on the table" → "Nevertheless, behold, the hand of the one delivering me up is with me on the table"** (v21) — Greek πλήν, "nevertheless", three times in the chapter (v21, 22, 42); τοῦ παραδιδόντος, a present participle.
- **"And truly the Son of man goeth, as it was determined: but woe unto that man by whom he is betrayed" → "because the Son of Man indeed goes according to what has been determined, nevertheless woe to that man through whom he is delivered up"** (v22) — Greek ὅτι … μέν (WH Treg NA28), "because … indeed", as Mark 14:21; κατὰ τὸ ὡρισμένον, a perfect participle of ὁρίζω (Acts 2:23); πλὴν οὐαί; δι' οὗ, "through whom".
- **"And they began to enquire among themselves, which of them it was that should do this thing" → "And they began to dispute among themselves which of them it might be who was about to do this"** (v23) — Greek συζητεῖν, "dispute, argue the question" (Mark 9:10); εἴη, an optative; μέλλων πράσσειν: πράσσω is elsewhere "practice", which English cannot say of a single act.
- **"And there was also a strife among them, which of them should be accounted the greatest" → "And there came to be also a contention among them, which of them is reputed to be greater"** (v24) — Greek ἐγένετο; φιλονεικία, "love of strife, contention"; δοκεῖ εἶναι, "seems, is held to be" (as Mark 10:42); μείζων, a comparative, "greater", so also v26, 27.
- **"The kings of the Gentiles exercise lordship over them; and they that exercise authority upon them are called benefactors" → "The kings of the nations are lords over them, and those having authority over them are called benefactors"** (v25) — Greek ἐθνῶν, "nations"; κυριεύουσιν, from κύριος "lord" (Mark 10:42 has the stronger κατακυριεύουσιν "lord it over"); οἱ ἐξουσιάζοντες; εὐεργέται, "Benefactor", a title that kings bore.
- **"But ye shall not be so: but he that is greatest among you, let him be as the younger; and he that is chief, as he that doth serve" → "But you, not so; but let the greater among you become as the younger, and the one leading as the one serving"** (v26) — Greek ὑμεῖς δὲ οὐχ οὕτως, without a verb; γινέσθω, "let him become"; ὁ ἡγούμενος (Heb 13:7); ὁ διακονῶν.
- **"he that sitteth at meat, or he that serveth? … I am among you as he that serveth" → "the one reclining or the one serving? … I am in the midst of you as the one serving"** (v27) — Greek ὁ ἀνακείμενος; ἐν μέσῳ ὑμῶν.
- **"Ye are they which have continued with me in my temptations" → "But you are those who have continued with me in my trials"** (v28) — Greek δέ; διαμεμενηκότες, a perfect, "have remained throughout" (Gal 2:5); πειρασμοῖς, "trials" (as v40, 46).
- **"And I appoint unto you a kingdom, as my Father hath appointed unto me" → "and I covenant to you, even as my Father covenanted to me, a kingdom"** (v29) — Greek διατίθεμαι … διέθετο, the verb from which διαθήκη "covenant" (v20) comes: to dispose of by a binding settlement, to assign, also to bequeath; "covenant" is kept as in Acts 3:25 so that the tie with v20 is seen. βασιλείαν stands last and may be the object of both verbs, or only of the second: "I covenant to you, even as my Father covenanted to me a kingdom, that you may eat …".
- **"That ye may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones" → "that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and you will sit upon thrones"** (v30) — Greek καθήσεσθε (Treg NA28), a future, "you will sit"; WH has καθῆσθε, "and may sit".
- **"And the Lord said, Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat" → "Simon, Simon, behold, Satan asked for you, to sift you as the wheat"** (v31) — "And the Lord said" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28. Greek ὁ Σατανᾶς, with the article; ἐξῃτήσατο, "asked to have for himself, demanded", a past tense; ὑμᾶς is plural, all the disciples; in v32 "you" is singular, Simon: English has one word for both. τὸν σῖτον, "the wheat".
- **"But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren" → "but I besought concerning you that your faith may not fail; and you, when once you have turned back, make your brothers stable"** (v32) — Greek ἐδεήθην, from δέομαι "beseech"; περὶ σοῦ; ποτε ἐπιστρέψας, "having once turned back"; στήρισον, "make stable, fix firmly" (as Acts 14:22).
- **"And he said unto him, Lord, I am ready to go with thee, both into prison, and to death" → "But he said to him, 'Lord, with you I am ready to go both to prison and to death'"** (v33) — Greek ὁ δὲ εἶπεν; μετὰ σοῦ stands first.
- **"the cock shall not crow this day, before that thou shalt thrice deny that thou knowest me" → "a rooster will not crow today until you have three times denied knowing me"** (v34) — Greek οὐ φωνήσει σήμερον ἀλέκτωρ ἕως τρίς με ἀπαρνήσῃ εἰδέναι (WH Treg NA28): no article with "rooster"; φωνέω is elsewhere "call".
- **"When I sent you without purse, and scrip, and shoes, lacked ye any thing? And they said, Nothing" → "When I sent you without purse and bag and sandals, were you in lack of anything? And they said, 'Of nothing'"** (v35) — Greek βαλλαντίου, πήρας, ὑποδημάτων (10:4); ὑστερήσατε, the verb of ὑστέρημα "lack" (21:4); οὐθενός, a genitive.
- **"he that hath a purse, let him take it, and likewise his scrip: and he that hath no sword, let him sell his garment, and buy one" → "let the one who has a purse take it, likewise also a bag; and let the one who has none sell his garment and buy a sword"** (v36) — Greek ὁ μὴ ἔχων has no object: "the one who has none", either no purse or no sword; μάχαιραν stands only at the end. ἱμάτιον, "garment".
- **"this that is written must yet be accomplished in me, And he was reckoned among the transgressors: for the things concerning me have an end" → "this which is written must be finished in me: 'And with lawless ones he was reckoned.' For indeed that which concerns me has an end"** (v37) — "yet" (RP ἔτι) is not in the critical text. Greek τελεσθῆναι, "be finished, brought to its end" (as John 19:28, 30), and τέλος ἔχει, "has an end": one root. μετὰ ἀνόμων ἐλογίσθη, "with lawless ones he was reckoned" (Isa 53:12, translated from the Greek as printed); τὸ περὶ ἐμοῦ, singular (WH Treg NA28).
- **"It is enough"** (v38) — Greek ἱκανόν ἐστιν (ἱκανός "sufficient"; elsewhere "fit", "considerable"): whether of the two swords or as a word that ends the talk, the Greek does not say.
- **"And he came out, and went, as he was wont, to the mount of Olives; and his disciples also followed him" → "And going out, he went according to the custom to the Mount of Olives; and the disciples also followed him"** (v39) — Greek κατὰ τὸ ἔθος; "his" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"And when he was at the place, he said unto them, Pray that ye enter not into temptation" → "And coming to the place, he said to them, 'Pray not to enter into trial'"** (v40) — Greek προσεύχεσθε μὴ εἰσελθεῖν, an infinitive (in v46 ἵνα μὴ εἰσέλθητε, "that you may not enter", as Matt 26:41); πειρασμόν, "trial, testing"; "temptation" is the same word.
- **"And he was withdrawn from them about a stone's cast, and kneeled down, and prayed" → "And he himself was drawn away from them about a stone's throw, and kneeling down he was praying"** (v41) — Greek καὶ αὐτός, "and he himself"; ἀπεσπάσθη, a passive, "was drawn (torn) away" (Acts 21:1); θεὶς τὰ γόνατα, literally "placing the knees" (Acts 7:60); προσηύχετο, an imperfect.
- **"Father, if thou be willing, remove this cup from me: nevertheless not my will, but thine, be done" → "Father, if you wish, take this cup away from me; nevertheless, not my will but yours be done"** (v42) — Greek εἰ βούλει, from βούλομαι "wish" (θέλημα "will" follows); παρένεγκε (WH Treg NA28), an imperative, "carry past, take away" (Mark 14:36); γινέσθω.
- **"And there appeared an angel unto him from heaven, strengthening him. And being in an agony he prayed more earnestly: and his sweat was as it were great drops of blood falling down to the ground" → "And there appeared to him an angel from heaven, strengthening him. And being in agony, he was praying more earnestly; and his sweat became as clots of blood going down upon the ground"** (v43–44) — Greek ὤφθη; ἐνισχύων; γενόμενος ἐν ἀγωνίᾳ, "having come to be in agony", the strain of a contest; ἐκτενέστερον; ἐγένετο, "became"; θρόμβοι αἵματος, "clots, thick drops of blood"; ὡσεί, "as, like": a comparison; καταβαίνοντες, "going down".
- **"vv43–44 textual note"** — the two verses are in the text of Treg and NA28 (and RP); WH prints them in double brackets as a very early addition. Some early manuscripts do not have them. The verse follows the majority of the four editions.
- **"And when he rose up from prayer, and was come to his disciples, he found them sleeping for sorrow" → "And rising from the prayer, coming to the disciples, he found them asleep from the sorrow"** (v45) — Greek κοιμωμένους, "asleep" (in v46 καθεύδετε, "sleep"); ἀπὸ τῆς λύπης.
- **"Why sleep ye? rise and pray, lest ye enter into temptation" → "Why do you sleep? Rise and pray, that you may not enter into trial"** (v46) — Greek ἀναστάντες; ἵνα μὴ εἰσέλθητε εἰς πειρασμόν.
- **"And while he yet spake, behold a multitude, and he that was called Judas, one of the twelve, went before them, and drew near unto Jesus to kiss him" → "While he was still speaking, behold, a crowd, and the one called Judas, one of the twelve, was going before them, and he drew near to Yeshua to kiss him"** (v47) — "And" (RP δέ) is not in the critical text; ἰδοὺ ὄχλος has no verb; προήρχετο, an imperfect; φιλῆσαι, "kiss", the verb "have affection for".
- **"Judas, betrayest thou the Son of man with a kiss?" → "Judas, with a kiss do you deliver up the Son of Man?"** (v48) — Greek φιλήματι stands first; παραδίδως.
- **"When they which were about him saw what would follow, they said unto him, Lord, shall we smite with the sword?" → "And those around him, seeing what was going to be, said, 'Lord, shall we strike with a sword?'"** (v49) — Greek τὸ ἐσόμενον, "what was going to be"; "unto him" (RP) is not in the critical text; εἰ πατάξομεν ἐν μαχαίρῃ, without the article.
- **"And one of them smote the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear" → "And a certain one of them struck the slave of the high priest and took off his right ear"** (v50) — Greek εἷς τις; δοῦλον, "slave"; ἀφεῖλεν, "took off" (Mark 14:47).
- **"And Jesus answered and said, Suffer ye thus far. And he touched his ear, and healed him" → "But Yeshua, answering, said, 'Let be, as far as this.' And touching the ear, he healed him"** (v51) — Greek ἐᾶτε ἕως τούτου, literally "let, as far as this": a short saying whose reach is not certain ("allow even this", said to the disciples about the seizure, or "stop, no further"). ὠτίου, "ear", a different word from οὖς in v50; "his" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"Then Jesus said unto the chief priests, and captains of the temple, and the elders, which were come to him, Be ye come out, as against a thief, with swords and staves?" → "And Yeshua said to those who had arrived against him, high priests and captains of the temple and elders, 'As against a robber you came out with swords and clubs?'"** (v52) — Greek παραγενομένους ἐπ' αὐτόν, "arrived against him"; the question is word for word Mark 14:48 without "to apprehend me"; λῃστήν, "robber".
- **"When I was daily with you in the temple, ye stretched forth no hands against me: but this is your hour, and the power of darkness" → "Daily, while I was with you in the temple, you did not stretch out your hands against me; but this is your hour, and the authority of the darkness"** (v53) — Greek καθ' ἡμέραν stands first; ἐξουσία, "authority"; τοῦ σκότους, with the article.
- **"Then took they him, and led him, and brought him into the high priest's house. And Peter followed afar off" → "And apprehending him, they led him and brought him into the house of the high priest; and Kefa was following from afar"** (v54) — Greek συλλαβόντες, "apprehending" (Mark 14:48); ἠκολούθει, an imperfect; μακρόθεν.
- **"And when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the hall, and were set down together, Peter sat down among them" → "And when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the court and had sat down together, Kefa was sitting in the midst of them"** (v55) — Greek περιαψάντων (WH Treg NA28), "kindled all round"; αὐλῆς, "court"; ἐκάθητο, an imperfect; μέσος αὐτῶν.
- **"But a certain maid beheld him as he sat by the fire, and earnestly looked upon him, and said, This man was also with him" → "And a certain slave woman, seeing him sitting at the light and gazing at him, said, 'This one also was with him'"** (v56) — Greek παιδίσκη, "slave woman" (Mark 14:66); πρὸς τὸ φῶς, "at the light" of the fire (Mark 14:54); ἀτενίσασα, "gazing" (Acts 1:10).
- **"And he denied him, saying, Woman, I know him not" → "But he denied, saying, 'I do not know him, woman'"** (v57) — Greek ἠρνήσατο without an object ("him", RP, is not in the critical text); γύναι stands last (WH Treg NA28).
- **"And after a little while another saw him, and said, Thou art also of them" → "And after a short while another, seeing him, said, 'You also are of them'"** (v58) — Greek μετὰ βραχύ; ἕτερος, a man; ἐξ αὐτῶν.
- **"And about the space of one hour after another confidently affirmed, saying, Of a truth this fellow also was with him: for he is a Galilaean" → "And when about one hour had passed, a certain other kept insisting, saying, 'Of a truth this one also was with him, for he is also a Galilean'"** (v59) — Greek διαστάσης, "having stood between, intervened"; διϊσχυρίζετο, an imperfect, "was affirming strongly" (as Acts 12:15); ἐπ' ἀληθείας (Acts 4:27); καὶ γάρ.
- **"Man, I know not what thou sayest. And immediately, while he yet spake, the cock crew" → "Man, I do not know what you are saying. And instantly, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed"** (v60) — Greek παραχρῆμα, "instantly", Luke's word (Mark has εὐθύς "immediately"); ἀλέκτωρ without the article.
- **"And the Lord turned, and looked upon Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said unto him, Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice" → "And the Lord, turning, looked at Kefa; and Kefa was reminded of the word of the Lord, how he said to him, 'Before a rooster crows today, you will deny me three times'"** (v61) — Greek ἐνέβλεψεν, "looked at" (Mark 14:67); ὑπεμνήσθη, a passive, "was reminded"; σήμερον, "today" (WH Treg NA28; not in RP). Editions: ῥήματος WH NA28 ] λόγου Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides; both are "word" (ῥῆμα as Acts 11:16, the spoken word).
- **"And Peter went out, and wept bitterly" → "And going out outside, he wept bitterly"** (v62) — Greek ἐξελθὼν ἔξω ἔκλαυσεν πικρῶς, word for word Matt 26:75; "Peter" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"And the men that held Jesus mocked him, and smote him" → "And the men who were holding him were mocking him, beating him"** (v63) — Greek αὐτόν (WH Treg NA28; RP "Jesus"); συνέχοντες, "holding fast"; ἐνέπαιζον, an imperfect; δέροντες, "beating".
- **"And when they had blindfolded him, they struck him on the face, and asked him, saying, Prophesy, who is it that smote thee?" → "and having covered him, they were asking him, saying, 'Prophesy: who is the one who struck you?'"** (v64) — Greek περικαλύψαντες, "covering" (Mark 14:65); "they struck him on the face" (RP) is not in the critical text; ἐπηρώτων, an imperfect; τίς ἐστιν ὁ παίσας σε, word for word Matt 26:68.
- **"And many other things blasphemously spake they against him" → "And many other things they were saying against him, blaspheming"** (v65) — Greek βλασφημοῦντες ἔλεγον εἰς αὐτόν; βλασφημέω is "blaspheme, revile".
- **"And as soon as it was day, the elders of the people and the chief priests and the scribes came together, and led him into their council" → "And when it became day, the eldership of the people was gathered together, both high priests and scribes, and they led him away into their Sanhedrin"** (v66) — Greek τὸ πρεσβυτέριον τοῦ λαοῦ, "the eldership, the body of elders" (Acts 22:5), with "both high priests and scribes" set beside it; συνήχθη, a passive; ἀπήγαγον (WH Treg NA28); συνέδριον.
- **"Art thou the Christ? tell us. And he said unto them, If I tell you, ye will not believe" → "If you are the Mashiach, tell us. But he said to them, 'If I tell you, you will by no means believe'"** (v67) — Greek εἰ σὺ εἶ ὁ χριστός, εἰπὸν ἡμῖν, "if"; οὐ μὴ πιστεύσητε.
- **"And if I also ask you, ye will not answer me, nor let me go" → "and if I ask, you will by no means answer"** (v68) — Greek ἐὰν δὲ ἐρωτήσω, οὐ μὴ ἀποκριθῆτε (WH NA28); Treg RP add μοι ἢ ἀπολύσητε, "me, nor release me".
- **"Hereafter shall the Son of man sit on the right hand of the power of God" → "But from now on the Son of Man will be sitting at the right hand of the power of Elohim"** (v69) — Greek ἀπὸ τοῦ νῦν δέ (as v18); ἔσται καθήμενος, "will be sitting"; ἐκ δεξιῶν τῆς δυνάμεως τοῦ θεοῦ: Mark 14:62 and Matt 26:64 have "the Power" alone as a name for God, Luke adds "of God" and so makes it "the power of God".
- **"Then said they all, Art thou then the Son of God? And he said unto them, Ye say that I am" → "And they all said, 'You, then, are the Son of Elohim?' And he said to them, 'You say that I am'"** (v70) — Greek σὺ οὖν εἶ; ὑμεῖς λέγετε ὅτι ἐγώ εἰμι: ὅτι is "that" or "because" ("You say it, because I am"); the Greek leaves both open.
- **"What need we any further witness? for we ourselves have heard of his own mouth" → "What need do we still have of testimony? For we ourselves heard from his mouth"** (v71) — Greek μαρτυρίας, "testimony" (Mark 14:63 has μαρτύρων "witnesses"); ἠκούσαμεν, a past tense, without an object; ἀπὸ τοῦ στόματος αὐτοῦ.
