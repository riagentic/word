# Luke 8

[1] And it came to pass in the time next in order that he was journeying through city by city and village by village, proclaiming and preaching as gospel the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the twelve with him,

[2] and certain women who had been healed from evil spirits and weaknesses: ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, the one called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out,

[3] and ⟦joanna|Yochanah|Joanna⟧, wife of ⟦chuza|Kuza|Chuza⟧, ⟦herod|Herodes's|Herod's⟧ manager, and ⟦susanna|Shoshanah|Susanna⟧, and many others, who were serving them out of what belonged to them.

[4] And as a great crowd was coming together, and those from city after city were journeying to him, he said through a parable:

[5] "The sower went out to sow his seed. And in his sowing, some fell beside the way, and it was trampled down, and the birds of the heaven devoured it.

[6] And other fell down upon the rock, and having grown, it withered, because of not having moisture.

[7] And other fell in the midst of the thorns, and the thorns, growing up with it, choked it.

[8] And other fell into the good earth, and having grown, it made fruit a hundredfold." Saying these things, he was calling, "**He who has ears to hear, let him hear.**"

[9] And his disciples were asking him what this parable might be.

[10] And he said, "To you it has been given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but to the rest in parables, so that '**seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand**.'

[11] Now the parable is this: The seed is the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[12] And those beside the way are the ones who heard; then the devil comes and takes away the word from their heart, that they may not, having believed, be saved.

[13] And those upon the rock are they who, whenever they hear, accept the word with joy; and these have no root, who believe for a time, and in a time of trial depart.

[14] And that which fell into the thorns, these are the ones who heard, and, going on, they are choked by anxieties and riches and pleasures of livelihood, and do not bring fruit to maturity.

[15] And that in the good earth, these are they who, having heard the word in a heart fine and good, hold it fast and bear fruit in endurance.

[16] "And no one, having lit a lamp, covers it with a vessel or puts it underneath a bed, but puts it upon a lampstand, that those entering in may see the light.

[17] For there is nothing hidden that will not become manifest, nor concealed that will by no means be known and come into manifest view.

[18] Look therefore how you hear; for whoever has, to him it will be given; and whoever does not have, even what he seems to have will be taken away from him."

[19] And there arrived to him his mother and brothers, and they were not able to reach him because of the crowd.

[20] And it was reported to him, "Your mother and your brothers are standing outside, wanting to see you."

[21] But he, answering, said to them, "**My mother and my brothers are these, who hear and do the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**"

[22] Now it came to pass on one of the days that he stepped into a boat, and his disciples, and he said to them, "Let us go through to the other side of the lake." And they put out.

[23] And as they were sailing, he fell asleep. And a storm of wind came down upon the lake, and they were being filled and were in danger.

[24] And approaching, they roused him, saying, "Chief, Chief, we are perishing!" And he, being roused, rebuked the wind and the surge of the water; and they ceased, and there came a calm.

[25] And he said to them, "Where is your faith?" And being afraid, they marveled, saying to one another, "Who then is this, that he orders even the winds and the water, and they obey him?"

[26] And they sailed down to the country of the Gerasenes, which is opposite ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧.

[27] And as he came out upon the land, there met him a certain man out of the city, having demons; and for a considerable time he had put on no garment, and he was not remaining in a house but in the graves.

[28] And seeing ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, crying out he fell down before him and with a loud voice said, "What have I to do with you, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, Son of the Most High ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? I beseech you, do not torment me."

[29] For he was charging the unclean spirit to come out from the man. For many times it had seized him, and he was bound with chains and shackles, being guarded, and tearing the bonds apart he was driven by the demon into the wildernesses.

[30] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ asked him, "What is your name?" And he said, "Legion," for many demons had entered into him.

[31] And they were appealing to him that he would not order them to go away into the abyss.

[32] Now there was there a herd of pigs, a considerable number, feeding on the mountain; and they appealed to him that he would permit them to enter into those; and he permitted them.

[33] And the demons, coming out from the man, entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and was drowned.

[34] And those feeding them, seeing what had happened, fled and reported it to the city and to the farms.

[35] And they came out to see what had happened, and they came to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and found the man from whom the demons had gone out sitting, clothed and of sound mind, at the feet of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧; and they were afraid.

[36] And those who had seen it reported to them how the man who had been demonized was saved.

[37] And all the multitude of the surrounding region of the Gerasenes requested him to go away from them, because they were held by a great fear; and he, stepping into a boat, returned.

[38] But the man from whom the demons had gone out was beseeching him that he might be with him; but he sent him away, saying,

[39] "Return to your house, and relate how much ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has done for you." And he went away, proclaiming through the whole city how much ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had done for him.

[40] Now as ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was returning, the crowd accepted him, for they were all expecting him.

[41] And behold, there came a man whose name was ⟦jairus|Yair|Jairus⟧, and this one was a ruler of the synagogue; and falling at ⟦yeshua|Yeshua's|Jesus'⟧ feet, he was appealing to him to enter into his house,

[42] because he had an only daughter, about twelve years old, and she was dying.

And as he was going, the crowds were choking him.

[43] And a woman who had been in a flow of blood for twelve years, who, having spent all her livelihood on physicians, had not strength to be healed by anyone,

[44] approaching from behind, touched the fringe of his garment, and instantly the flow of her blood stood still.

[45] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Who is the one who touched me?" And as all were denying it, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said, "Chief, the crowds hold you in and press against you."

[46] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Someone touched me, for I knew power to have gone out from me."

[47] And the woman, seeing that she had not escaped notice, came trembling, and falling down before him she reported before all the people for what cause she touched him, and how she was healed instantly.

[48] And he said to her, "Daughter, your faith has saved you; go in peace."

[49] While he is still speaking, there comes someone from the synagogue ruler's house, saying, "Your daughter has died; bother the Teacher no longer."

[50] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, hearing it, answered him, "Do not fear; only believe, and she will be saved."

[51] And coming into the house, he did not let anyone enter with him except ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧, and the father of the girl and the mother.

[52] And all were weeping and wailing for her. But he said, "Do not weep, for she has not died but sleeps."

[53] And they were laughing at him, knowing that she had died.

[54] But he, taking hold of her hand, called, saying, "Girl, arise."

[55] And her spirit turned back, and she rose instantly; and he gave orders that something be given her to eat.

[56] And her parents were amazed; but he charged them to tell no one what had happened.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v51) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved. Where the Greek has only "he" (v27, 38, 42) no name is supplied.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 11, 21, 28, 39) — divine name retained.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v45) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"most high God" → "the Most High *Elohim*"** (v28) — Greek τοῦ Θεοῦ τοῦ ὑψίστου *tou Theou tou hypsistou* = Hebrew אֵל עֶלְיוֹן *El Elyon*.
- **"devils" → "demons"** (vv2, 27, 29, 30, 33, 35, 38) — Greek *daimonia*.
- **"afterward" → "in the time next in order"** (v1) — Greek ἐν τῷ καθεξῆς; καθεξῆς is "in order, one after another".
- **"preaching and shewing the glad tidings of the kingdom" → "proclaiming and preaching as gospel the kingdom"** (v1) — Greek κηρύσσων καὶ εὐαγγελιζόμενος τὴν βασιλείαν: two verbs, and the kingdom is the object of both.
- **"infirmities" → "weaknesses"** (v2) — Greek ἀσθένειαι. Of *Miryam* the Greek says only "the one called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out"; nothing else is said of her.
- **"steward" → "manager"; "ministered unto him of their substance" → "were serving them out of what belonged to them"** (v3) — Greek ἐπίτροπος (Matt 20:8); διηκόνουν αὐτοῖς ἐκ τῶν ὑπαρχόντων αὐταῖς. The printed text has "them" (αὐτοῖς), not "him"; "out of" is ἐκ (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀπό).
- **"by the way side" → "beside the way"; "fowls of the air" → "birds of the heaven"** (v5, 12) — Greek παρὰ τὴν ὁδόν; τὰ πετεινὰ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ, as Mark 4:4, 32.
- **"choked"** (v7, 14, 33, 42) — one family of verbs: ἀπέπνιξαν (v7), συμπνίγονται (v14), ἀπεπνίγη "was drowned" (v33), συνέπνιγον of the crowds (v42).
- **"bare fruit" → "made fruit"** (v8) — Greek ἐποίησεν καρπόν; "bear fruit" (v15) is καρποφορέω.
- **"he cried" → "he was calling"** (v8) — Greek ἐφώνει, imperfect.
- **"What might this parable be?"** (v9) — reported speech in the text (τίς αὕτη εἴη ἡ παραβολή); "saying" (KJV) is RP only.
- **"to others" → "to the rest"; "that seeing they might not see"** (v10) — Greek τοῖς λοιποῖς; ἵνα with the subjunctive, "so that". Mark 4:12 is longer.
- **"they that hear" → "the ones who heard"** (v12, 14) — Greek οἱ ἀκούσαντες, aorist (WH Treg NA28; RP present in v12).
- **"lest they should believe and be saved" → "that they may not, having believed, be saved"** (v12) — Greek ἵνα μὴ πιστεύσαντες σωθῶσιν. "heart" is singular.
- **"receive" → "accept"; "for a while" → "for a time"; "temptation" → "trial"; "fall away" → "depart"** (v13) — Greek δέχονται (Mark 4:16 has λαμβάνουσιν "receive"); πρὸς καιρόν; πειρασμός; ἀφίστανται, "stand off, withdraw".
- **"cares … of this life" → "anxieties … of livelihood"** (v14) — Greek μέριμναι; τοῦ βίου: βίος is life as it is lived and what one lives on (v43; 1 John 2:16), not ζωή. "this" is not in the Greek.
- **"bring no fruit to perfection" → "do not bring fruit to maturity"** (v14) — Greek οὐ τελεσφοροῦσιν.
- **"in an honest and good heart" → "in a heart fine and good"** (v15) — Greek ἐν καρδίᾳ καλῇ καὶ ἀγαθῇ: two words for "good". The earth is ἀγαθή in v8 and καλή in v15.
- **"keep it" → "hold it fast"; "with patience" → "in endurance"** (v15) — Greek κατέχουσιν; ἐν ὑπομονῇ.
- **"candle … candlestick" → "lamp … lampstand"** (v16) — Greek λύχνος, λυχνία.
- **"nothing is secret … neither any thing hid" → "nothing hidden … nor concealed"** (v17) — Greek κρυπτόν; ἀπόκρυφον, as Mark 4:22. "will by no means be known" is οὐ μὴ γνωσθῇ (WH Treg NA28; RP future).
- **"that which he seemeth to have" kept** (v18) — Greek ὃ δοκεῖ ἔχειν: "what he seems to have" or "what he thinks he has"; the Greek leaves it open.
- **"could not come at him" → "were not able to reach him"** (v19) — Greek συντυχεῖν αὐτῷ, "to meet with him".
- **"which hear the word of God, and do it" → "who hear and do the word of *Elohim*"** (v21) — Greek οἱ τὸν λόγον τοῦ θεοῦ ἀκούοντες καὶ ποιοῦντες; "it" is RP only.
- **"they launched forth" → "they put out"** (v22) — Greek ἀνήχθησαν.
- **"Master, master" → "Chief, Chief"** (v24, 45) — Greek ἐπιστάτα, Luke's own word (5:5); Mark 4:38 has "Teacher".
- **"awoke him … he arose" → "roused him … being roused"** (v24) — Greek διήγειραν; διεγερθείς (WH Treg NA28), as Mark 4:38–39. "the raging of the water" → "the surge of the water" (κλύδων).
- **"commandeth" → "orders"** (v25, 31) — Greek ἐπιτάσσω.
- **"Gadarenes" → "Gerasenes"** (v26, 37) — Γερασηνῶν WH Treg NA28; Γαδαρηνῶν RP.
- **"a certain man, which had devils long time, and ware no clothes" → "having demons; and for a considerable time he had put on no garment"** (v27) — the text is ἔχων δαιμόνια· καὶ χρόνῳ ἱκανῷ οὐκ ἐνεδύσατο ἱμάτιον (WH NA28; Treg RP ὃς εἶχεν … ἐκ χρόνων ἱκανῶν): the time belongs to the clothing. "Was not remaining" is οὐκ ἔμενεν, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a stay in a place: "remain".
- **"tombs" → "graves"** (v27) — Greek μνήμασιν, as Mark 5:3, 5.
- **"What have I to do with thee"** (v28) — Greek Τί ἐμοὶ καὶ σοί, as Mark 5:7; "beseech" is δέομαι (Mark: "I adjure you by *Elohim*").
- **"he had commanded" → "he was charging"** (v29) — Greek παρήγγελλεν, imperfect (WH Treg; NA28 RP aorist παρήγγειλεν "he charged").
- **"oftentimes it had caught him" → "many times it had seized him"** (v29) — Greek πολλοῖς χρόνοις συνηρπάκει αὐτόν: "on many occasions" or "for a long time"; both are possible.
- **"bands" → "bonds"; "wilderness" → "wildernesses"** (v29) — Greek δεσμά (elsewhere "chains"; here "chains" is ἁλύσεσιν, "shackles" πέδαις); εἰς τὰς ἐρήμους, plural.
- **"the deep" → "the abyss"** (v31) — Greek ἄβυσσος.
- **"an herd of many swine" → "a herd of pigs, a considerable number"** (v32) — Greek ἀγέλη χοίρων ἱκανῶν. "suffer" → "permit" (ἐπιτρέπω).
- **"told it in the city and in the country" → "reported it to the city and to the farms"** (v34) — Greek ἀπήγγειλαν εἰς τὴν πόλιν καὶ εἰς τοὺς ἀγρούς, as Mark 5:14.
- **"in his right mind" → "of sound mind"** (v35) — Greek σωφρονοῦντα, as Mark 5:15.
- **"he that was possessed of the devils was healed" → "the man who had been demonized was saved"** (v36) — Greek ἐσώθη ὁ δαιμονισθείς: σῴζω "save", here of deliverance from the demons.
- **"taken with great fear" → "held by a great fear"** (v37) — Greek φόβῳ μεγάλῳ συνείχοντο.
- **"shew how great things" → "relate how much"; "published" → "proclaiming"** (v39) — Greek διηγοῦ ὅσα; κηρύσσων.
- **"gladly received him" → "accepted him"; "waiting for" → "expecting"** (v40) — Greek ἀπεδέξατο; προσδοκῶντες.
- **"a ruler of the synagogue"** (v41) — Greek ἄρχων τῆς συναγωγῆς; in v49 the same man is ἀρχισυνάγωγος "synagogue ruler".
- **"one only daughter"** (v42) — Greek θυγάτηρ μονογενής. "the people thronged him" → "the crowds were choking him" (συνέπνιγον, the verb of v14).
- **"an issue of blood" → "a flow of blood"; "all her living" → "all her livelihood"; "neither could be healed of any" → "had not strength to be healed by anyone"** (v43) — Greek ῥύσις αἵματος (Mark 5:25); ὅλον τὸν βίον; οὐκ ἴσχυσεν. The words "having spent all her livelihood on physicians" are read by Treg NA28 RP and omitted by WH (2–2, NA28 decides).
- **"border" → "fringe"; "immediately" → "instantly"; "stanched" → "stood still"** (v44, 47, 55) — Greek κράσπεδον; παραχρῆμα; ἔστη.
- **"Peter and they that were with him said" → "*Kefa* said"; "and sayest thou, Who touched me?" not in the text** (v45) — WH NA28; Treg RP add both.
- **"throng thee and press thee" → "hold you in and press against you"** (v45) — Greek συνέχουσιν (the verb of v37) καὶ ἀποθλίβουσιν.
- **"I perceive that virtue is gone out of me" → "I knew power to have gone out from me"** (v46) — Greek ἔγνων δύναμιν ἐξεληλυθυῖαν ἀπʼ ἐμοῦ.
- **"that she was not hid" → "that she had not escaped notice"; "declared unto him" → "reported"** (v47) — Greek οὐκ ἔλαθεν; ἀπήγγειλεν ("to him" is RP only).
- **"Daughter, be of good comfort: thy faith hath made thee whole" → "Daughter, your faith has saved you"** (v48) — "be of good comfort" is RP only. Greek σέσωκεν, "has saved", here of healing, the same words as 7:50 and Mark 5:34. So also "she will be saved" in v50 (σωθήσεται), of the girl who has died.
- **"Thy daughter is dead; trouble not the Master" → "Your daughter has died; bother the Teacher no longer"** (v49) — Greek τέθνηκεν; μηκέτι σκύλλε τὸν διδάσκαλον (WH Treg NA28). "comes" is a present in Greek; "house" is supplied, as Mark 5:35.
- **"believe only" → "only believe"** (v50) — Greek πίστευσον, aorist (WH Treg NA28); Mark 5:36 has the present.
- **"the maiden" / "Maid" → "the girl" / "Girl"** (v51, 54) — Greek ἡ παῖς, the feminine of παῖς "boy" (7:7); Mark has παιδίον and κοράσιον.
- **"bewailed her" → "were wailing for her"** (v52) — Greek ἐκόπτοντο αὐτήν, literally "were beating themselves for her" (Matt 11:17).
- **"she is not dead, but sleepeth" → "she has not died but sleeps"** (v52) — Greek οὐ γὰρ ἀπέθανεν ἀλλὰ καθεύδει, as Mark 5:39. "laughed him to scorn" → "were laughing at him" (κατεγέλων).
- **"And he put them all out" not in the text** (v54) — RP only. "took her by the hand" → "taking hold of her hand" (κρατήσας τῆς χειρός).
- **"her spirit came again" → "her spirit turned back"; "arose straightway" → "rose instantly"; "commanded" → "gave orders"** (v55) — Greek ἐπέστρεψεν ("returned" in v37, 39, 40 is ὑποστρέφω); ἀνέστη παραχρῆμα; διέταξεν, the verb "ordain" of Gal 3:19, here of an order about food ("give orders", as Matt 11:1); "commanded" in v56 is παρήγγειλεν.
- **"astonished" → "amazed"** (v56) — Greek ἐξέστησαν.
