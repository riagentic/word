# Proverbs 24

[1] Do not envy men of evil,
and do not crave to be with them,

[2] for havoc their heart utters,
and toil their lips speak.

[3] By wisdom a house is built,
and by understanding it is established,

[4] and by knowledge rooms are filled
with all wealth, precious and pleasant.

[5] A wise mighty man is in strength,
and a man of knowledge strengthens power,

[6] for by steering you will make war for yourself,
and salvation is in a multitude of counselors.

[7] Wisdoms are heights to the senseless one;
in the gate he does not open his mouth.

[8] One who devises to do evil,
him they will call a master of devisings.

[9] The depravity of folly is sin,
and an abomination to mankind is the scoffer.

[10] You have shown yourself slack in the day of distress —
narrow is your power.

[11] Deliver those taken to death,
and those tottering to the slaughter — if only you would withhold them!

[12] When you say, "Behold, we did not know this,"
does not the weigher of hearts, he, understand,
and the one who guards your soul, he, know,
and return to man according to his work?

[13] Eat, my son, honey, for it is good,
and flowing honey is sweet upon your palate.

[14] So know wisdom for your soul:
if you have found it, then there is a latter end,
and your hope will not be cut off.

[15] Do not lie in ambush, wicked one, against the habitation of the righteous;
do not lay waste his resting place.

[16] For seven times a righteous one falls and rises,
but the wicked stumble in evil.

[17] When your enemy falls, do not rejoice,
and when he stumbles, let your heart not be glad,

[18] lest ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ see, and it be evil in his eyes,
and he turn back his anger from upon him.

[19] Do not burn with anger at evildoers;
do not envy the wicked,

[20] for there will be no latter end for the evil man;
the lamp of the wicked will go out.

[21] Fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my son, and the king;
with those who change do not mix,

[22] for suddenly their calamity will arise,
and the disaster of the two of them — who knows?

[23] These also are of the wise.
To recognize faces in judgment is not good.

[24] Whoever says to the wicked, "You are righteous," peoples will lay a curse on him;
folks will denounce him.

[25] But for those who reprove, it will be pleasant,
and upon them will come a blessing of good.

[26] Lips he kisses,
who brings back straightforward words.

[27] Prepare your work outside,
and make it ready for yourself in the field;
afterward, then you shall build your house.

[28] Do not be a witness without cause against your neighbor;
and would you entice with your lips?

[29] Do not say, "As he did to me, so I will do to him;
I will return to the man according to his work."

[30] By the field of a sluggard man I passed,
and by the vineyard of a man lacking heart,

[31] and behold, it had all come up in thistles;
nettles covered its face,
and its fence of stones was torn down.

[32] And I beheld, I myself, I set my heart;
I saw, I took discipline.

[33] A little sleep, a little slumber,
a little folding of the hands to lie down,

[34] and your poverty will come, one walking about,
and your lacks like a man of shield.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v18, 21) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Layout** (v24) — the first line now runs to "peoples will lay a curse on him", where the accents divide the verse.
- **"Be not thou envious against evil men, neither desire to be with them" → "Do not envy men of evil, and do not crave to be with them"** (v1) — Hebrew קִנֵּא בְּ "envy"; אַנְשֵׁי רָעָה "men of evil"; תִּתְאָיו "crave" (הִתְאַוָּה) is the qere, the written form תתאו a spelling of the same word.
- **"For their heart studieth destruction, and their lips talk of mischief" → "for havoc their heart utters, and toil their lips speak"** (v2) — Hebrew שֹׁד "havoc" (as 21:7); יֶהְגֶּה "utters" (הָגָה of plotting: reviewer row); עָמָל "toil" (WORDS-HE: the word is "toil"; here the harm worked on others); the objects stand first.
- **"And by knowledge shall the chambers be filled with all precious and pleasant riches" → "and by knowledge rooms are filled with all wealth, precious and pleasant"** (v4) — Hebrew חֲדָרִים "rooms" (no article); הוֹן "wealth"; יָקָר "precious", נָעִים "pleasant".
- **"A wise man is strong; yea, a man of knowledge increaseth strength" → "A wise mighty man is in strength, and a man of knowledge strengthens power"** (v5) — Hebrew גֶּבֶר "mighty man" beside אִישׁ "man" (standing decision: kept apart); בַּעוֹז "in strength" (עֹז); מְאַמֶּץ־כֹּחַ "strengthens power" (אִמֵּץ; כֹּחַ "power"). The Septuagint is cited for a comparison, "a wise man is better than a strong one" (from memory; not followed).
- **"For by wise counsel thou shalt make thy war: and in multitude of counsellors there is safety" → "for by steering you will make war for yourself, and salvation is in a multitude of counselors"** (v6) — Hebrew תַּחְבֻּלוֹת "steering" (WISDOM.md; "guidance" is the sense); תַּעֲשֶׂה־לְּךָ מִלְחָמָה "you will make war for yourself"; תְשׁוּעָה "salvation"; יוֹעֵץ "counselor" is singular (a collective: "counselors").
- **"Wisdom is too high for a fool" → "Wisdoms are heights to the senseless one"** (v7) — Hebrew רָאמוֹת "heights" (also read as "corals", the word of Job 28:18, of a thing out of reach of price: uncertain); חָכְמוֹת "wisdoms", plural as 1:20; אֱוִיל "senseless one" (WISDOM.md).
- **"He that deviseth to do evil shall be called a mischievous person" → "One who devises to do evil, him they will call a master of devisings"** (v8) — Hebrew מְחַשֵּׁב "devising" (חָשַׁב); לְהָרֵעַ "to do evil"; בַּעַל־מְזִמּוֹת "master of devisings" (מְזִמָּה, WISDOM.md; בַּעַל as 22:24); יִקְרָאוּ "they will call" (no passive).
- **"The thought of foolishness is sin" → "The depravity of folly is sin"** (v9) — Hebrew זִמָּה "depravity" (WORDS-HE: a shameful design; one root with מְזִמּוֹת "devisings", v8); אִוֶּלֶת "folly"; לְאָדָם "to mankind"; the second line ends with "the scoffer", as kept.
- **"If thou faint in the day of adversity, thy strength is small" → "You have shown yourself slack in the day of distress — narrow is your power"** (v10) — Hebrew הִתְרַפִּיתָ, a perfect with no "if" (hitpael of רָפָה "be slack"): a statement, though the sense is that of a condition; צָרָה "distress" and צַר "narrow" are one root (a play on words); כֹּחַ "power".
- **"If thou forbear to deliver them that are drawn unto death, and those that are ready to be slain" → "Deliver those taken to death, and those tottering to the slaughter — if only you would withhold them!"** (v11) — Hebrew הַצֵּל "deliver" is an imperative (not "if thou forbear to deliver"); לְקֻחִים "taken"; מָטִים "tottering" (מוֹט); הֶרֶג "slaughter"; אִם־תַּחְשׂוֹךְ "if you withhold" (חָשַׂךְ): a wish ("if only you would hold them back") or a question ("will you hold back?"); "them" is supplied.
- **"If thou sayest, Behold, we knew it not; doth not he that pondereth the heart consider it? and he that keepeth thy soul, doth not he know it? and shall not he render to every man according to his works?" → "When you say, "Behold, we did not know this," does not the weigher of hearts, he, understand, and the one who guards your soul, he, know, and return to man according to his work?"** (v12) — Hebrew הֵן "behold"; זֶה "this"; תֹכֵן לִבּוֹת "the weigher of hearts" (as 21:2); הוּא twice, "he" (emphatic); נֹצֵר "guarding" (נָצַר); וְהֵשִׁיב "and he will return" (הֵשִׁיב); לְאָדָם "to man" ("every" not written); כְּפָעֳלוֹ "according to his work" (singular).
- **"My son, eat thou honey, because it is good; and the honeycomb, which is sweet to thy taste" → "Eat, my son, honey, for it is good, and flowing honey is sweet upon your palate"** (v13) — Hebrew נֹפֶת "flowing honey" (honey that drips from the comb); מָתוֹק "sweet" (a predicate: no "which"); עַל־חִכֶּךָ "upon your palate".
- **"So shall the knowledge of wisdom be unto thy soul: when thou hast found it, then there shall be a reward" → "So know wisdom for your soul: if you have found it, then there is a latter end"** (v14) — Hebrew דְּעֶה, an imperative of יָדַע ("know"); כֵּן "so" (as the honey); וְיֵשׁ אַחֲרִית "and there is a latter end" (the וְ opens the result: "then"; אַחֲרִית as 23:18, not "reward"); the last two lines are 23:18 again.
- **"Lay not wait, O wicked man, against the dwelling of the righteous; spoil not his resting place" → "Do not lie in ambush, wicked one, against the habitation of the righteous; do not lay waste his resting place"** (v15) — No "O" (the Hebrew has none). אָרַב "lie in ambush"; נְוֵה "habitation" (as 21:20); תְּשַׁדֵּד "lay waste" (שָׁדַד, the root of שֹׁד "havoc", v2); רִבְצוֹ "his resting place" (where a flock lies down: רָבַץ).
- **"For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief" → "For seven times a righteous one falls and rises, but the wicked stumble in evil"** (v16) — Hebrew וָקָם "and rises" ("again" not written); יִכָּשְׁלוּ "stumble" (another verb than נָפַל "fall"); בְרָעָה "in evil" (by calamity: the word is "evil").
- **"Rejoice not when thine enemy falleth" → "When your enemy falls, do not rejoice"** (v17) — Hebrew אוֹיִבְךָ "your enemy" is the qere (the written form אויביך is plural, "your enemies"); שָׂמַח "rejoice", גִּיל "be glad" (as 23:24, 25).
- **"Lest the LORD see it, and it displease him, and he turn away his wrath from him" → "lest Yahweh see, and it be evil in his eyes, and he turn back his anger from upon him"** (v18) — Hebrew וְרַע בְּעֵינָיו "and it be evil in his eyes" ("it" not written after "see"); וְהֵשִׁיב "and he turn back"; מֵעָלָיו "from upon him"; אַפּוֹ "his anger" (literally "his nose").
- **"Fret not thyself because of evil men" → "Do not burn with anger at evildoers"** (v19) — Hebrew תִּתְחַר, hitpael of חָרָה "burn": "do not heat yourself" (WORDS-HE, as Ps 37:1); מְרֵעִים "evildoers".
- **"For there shall be no reward to the evil man; the candle of the wicked shall be put out" → "for there will be no latter end for the evil man; the lamp of the wicked will go out"** (v20) — Hebrew אַחֲרִית "latter end" (as v14; 23:18); לָרָע "for the evil (one)"; נֵר "lamp"; יִדְעָךְ "will go out".
- **"meddle not with them that are given to change" → "with those who change do not mix"** (v21) — Hebrew שׁוֹנִים "those who change" (or "those who differ, who think otherwise": uncertain); תִּתְעָרָב "mix" (hitpael of עָרַב; the qal is "be surety", 22:26); the object stands first.
- **"For their calamity shall rise suddenly; and who knoweth the ruin of them both?" → "for suddenly their calamity will arise, and the disaster of the two of them — who knows?"** (v22) — Hebrew אֵיד "calamity", פִּיד "disaster" (WORDS-HE); שְׁנֵיהֶם "the two of them" (Yahweh and the king, or the two sorts of men: not said).
- **"These things also belong to the wise. It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment" → "These also are of the wise. To recognize faces in judgment is not good"** (v23) — Hebrew גַּם־אֵלֶּה לַחֲכָמִים: a new short collection begins (24:23–34); לְ "of" (by the wise, or belonging to them: as the Psalm headings, not decided); "things" / "sayings" not written. הַכֵּר־פָּנִים "recognize faces" (= be partial: Deut 1:17); בַּל־טוֹב "not good".
- **"He that saith unto the wicked, Thou art righteous; him shall the people curse, nations shall abhor him" → "Whoever says to the wicked, "You are righteous," peoples will lay a curse on him; folks will denounce him"** (v24) — Hebrew צַדִּיק אָתָּה "You are righteous"; יִקְּבֻהוּ "lay a curse on him" (from קָבַב, or from נָקַב with the same sense, as Num 23:8); עַמִּים "peoples" (plural); יִזְעָמוּהוּ "denounce him" (זָעַם); לְאֻמִּים "folks".
- **"But to them that rebuke him shall be delight, and a good blessing shall come upon them" → "But for those who reprove, it will be pleasant, and upon them will come a blessing of good"** (v25) — Hebrew הַמּוֹכִיחִים "those who reprove" (no "him"); יִנְעָם "it will be pleasant" (verb of נָעִים); בִרְכַּת־טוֹב "a blessing of good".
- **"Every man shall kiss his lips that giveth a right answer" → "Lips he kisses, who brings back straightforward words"** (v26) — Hebrew שְׂפָתַיִם יִשָּׁק "lips he kisses" ("every man" and "his" are not written; whose lips is not said); מֵשִׁיב דְּבָרִים "bringing back words" (= answering); נְכֹחִים "straightforward" (from נֹכַח "in front").
- **"Prepare thy work without, and make it fit for thyself in the field; and afterwards build thine house" → "Prepare your work outside, and make it ready for yourself in the field; afterward, then you shall build your house"** (v27) — Hebrew בַּחוּץ "outside" (in the open; elsewhere "in the street", 22:13); מְלָאכָה "work"; עַתְּדָהּ "make it ready"; אַחַר "afterward", then וּבָנִיתָ, a perfect with "and": "then you shall build".
- **"Be not a witness against thy neighbour without cause; and deceive not with thy lips" → "Do not be a witness without cause against your neighbor; and would you entice with your lips?"** (v28) — Hebrew עֵד־חִנָּם "a witness without cause"; בְּרֵעֶךָ "against your neighbor"; וַהֲפִתִּיתָ, with the question הֲ: "and would you entice?" (פִּתָּה, as 1:10); not a command.
- **"Say not, I will do so to him as he hath done to me: I will render to the man according to his work" → "Do not say, "As he did to me, so I will do to him; I will return to the man according to his work""** (v29) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר … כֵּן "as … so", in the Hebrew order; אָשִׁיב "I will return" (הֵשִׁיב, as v12); כְּפָעֳלוֹ "according to his work".
- **"I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding" → "By the field of a sluggard man I passed, and by the vineyard of a man lacking heart"** (v30) — Hebrew אִישׁ־עָצֵל "a sluggard man" (אִישׁ) and אָדָם חֲסַר־לֵב "a man lacking heart" (אָדָם: English has "man" for both); חֲסַר־לֵב "lacking heart" (WISDOM.md; the heart as the mind); עָבַרְתִּי "I passed".
- **"And, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down" → "and behold, it had all come up in thistles; nettles covered its face, and its fence of stones was torn down"** (v31) — Hebrew וְהִנֵּה "and behold"; עָלָה כֻלּוֹ קִמְּשֹׂנִים "it had all come up (in) thistles"; קִמְּשֹׂנִים "thistles" and חֲרֻלִּים "nettles": kinds of plant uncertain; גֶדֶר אֲבָנָיו "its fence of stones"; נֶהֱרָסָה "was torn down" (הָרַס).
- **"Then I saw, and considered it well: I looked upon it, and received instruction" → "And I beheld, I myself, I set my heart; I saw, I took discipline"** (v32) — Hebrew וָאֶחֱזֶה "and I beheld" (חָזָה); אָנֹכִי "I" (emphatic); אָשִׁית לִבִּי "I set my heart" (= paid attention: "set the heart to", as 22:17); רָאִיתִי "I saw"; לָקַחְתִּי מוּסָר "I took discipline" (as 1:3).
- **"Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep" → "A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to lie down"** (v33) — Hebrew מְעַט "a little" three times ("yet" not written); שֵׁנוֹת and תְּנוּמוֹת are plural ("sleeps", "slumbers": English uses the singular); לִשְׁכָּב "to lie down". v33–34 are 6:10–11 again, with מִתְהַלֵּךְ (v34) where 6:11 has כִמְהַלֵּךְ "like a walker".
- **"So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth; and thy want as an armed man" → "and your poverty will come, one walking about, and your lacks like a man of shield"** (v34) — Hebrew וּבָא "and will come"; מִתְהַלֵּךְ "one walking about" (with no "as"; a vagrant or a prowler); רֵישׁ "poverty" (root of רָשׁ "destitute", 22:2); מַחְסֹרֶיךָ "your lacks" (plural of מַחְסוֹר, 21:5); אִישׁ מָגֵן "a man of shield" (an armed man).
