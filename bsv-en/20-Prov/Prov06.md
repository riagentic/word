# Proverbs 6

[1] My son, if you have been surety for your neighbor,
have struck your palms for a stranger,

[2] you are snared by the sayings of your mouth,
you are captured by the sayings of your mouth.

[3] Do this then, my son, and deliver yourself,
for you have come into the palm of your neighbor:
go, humble yourself, and press your neighbors.

[4] Do not give sleep to your eyes,
nor slumber to your eyelids;

[5] deliver yourself like a gazelle from the hand,
and like a bird from the hand of the fowler.

[6] Go to the ant, sluggard;
see her ways, and be wise,

[7] which has no captain,
officer or ruler;

[8] she prepares her bread in the summer,
she has gathered in her food at the harvest.

[9] How long, sluggard, will you lie down?
When will you rise from your sleep?

[10] A little sleep, a little slumber,
a little folding of the hands to lie down,

[11] and your poverty shall come like one who roams,
and your want like a man with a shield.

[12] A man ⟦belial|of Beliyyaal|of worthlessness⟧, a man of wickedness,
walking with crookedness of mouth,

[13] winking with his eyes, speaking with his feet,
pointing with his fingers;

[14] perversities are in his heart, he devises evil at every time;
he sends out strifes.

[15] Therefore suddenly his calamity shall come;
in an instant he shall be broken, and there is no healing.

[16] Six are these that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ hates,
and seven are an abomination of his soul:

[17] eyes that are high, a tongue of falsehood,
and hands that shed innocent blood,

[18] a heart that devises plans of wickedness,
feet that hasten to run to evil,

[19] one who breathes out lies, a false witness,
and one who sends out strifes between brothers.

[20] Guard, my son, the commandment of your father,
and do not abandon the ⟦torah|torah|teaching⟧ of your mother.

[21] Bind them on your heart continually;
tie them on your neck.

[22] When you walk about, it shall lead you;
when you lie down, it shall keep watch over you;
and when you awake, it shall talk with you.

[23] For a lamp is the commandment, and the ⟦torah|torah|teaching⟧ a light,
and the way of life are reproofs of discipline,

[24] to keep you from the woman of evil,
from the smoothness of the tongue of a foreign woman.

[25] Do not covet her beauty in your heart,
and do not let her take you with her eyelids;

[26] for on account of a woman who is a prostitute, down to a loaf of bread, and the wife of a man —
a precious soul she hunts.

[27] Can a man scoop fire into his bosom,
and his garments not be burned?

[28] Or can a man walk on the coals,
and his feet not be scorched?

[29] So is the one who goes in to the wife of his neighbor;
no one who touches her shall be clear.

[30] They do not despise a thief when he steals
to fill his soul when he is hungry;

[31] and if he is found, he shall repay sevenfold;
all the wealth of his house he shall give.

[32] He who commits adultery with a woman is lacking heart;
he who would destroy his soul, he does it.

[33] A stroke and disgrace he shall find,
and his reproach shall not be wiped out.

[34] For jealousy is the fury of a man,
and he will not spare in the day of vengeance.

[35] He will not lift up the face of any ransom,
and he will not be willing, though you multiply the bribe.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v3 and v22 have three cola. v14 and v26 are now divided where the accents divide them (v26: the main pause falls after "the wife of a man").
- **"if thou be surety for thy friend, if thou hast stricken thy hand with a stranger" → "if you have been surety for your neighbor, have struck your palms for a stranger"** (v1) — Hebrew עָרַבְתָּ לְרֵעֶךָ "you have been surety for" (perfect); תָּקַעְתָּ … כַּפֶּיךָ "struck your palms" (the handclasp of a pledge), plural "palms"; the second line has no "if". רֵעַ "neighbor" through the chapter (v1, 3, 29), as the law of Exod 20:17 (WORDS-HE "friend" of a companion); זָר "stranger".
- **"Thou art snared … thou art taken" → "you are snared … you are captured"** (v2) — Hebrew perfects with no "if" (the current "if" is removed): נוֹקַשְׁתָּ, nifal of יָקַשׁ (the root of מוֹקֵשׁ "snare"); נִלְכַּדְתָּ, לָכַד "capture". אִמְרֵי־פִיךָ "the sayings of your mouth", both times.
- **"deliver thyself, when thou art come into the hand of thy friend; go, humble thyself, and make sure thy friend" → "deliver yourself, for you have come into the palm of your neighbor: go, humble yourself, and press your neighbors"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵפוֹא "then"; הִנָּצֵל "deliver yourself" (also v5); כִּי "for"; כַף "palm" (as v1). הִתְרַפֵּס, literally "let yourself be trampled" (as Ps 68:30 "humbling himself"); רְהַב "press, storm at" (urge without letting up). רֵעֶיךָ is pointed as a plural, "your neighbors"; many take it as a singular in sense.
- **"Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids"** (v4) — kept. עַפְעַפַּיִם "eyelids" (also v25).
- **"from the hand of the hunter" → "from the hand"** (v5) — Hebrew מִיָּד, "from the hand", with no "of the hunter": the second line names the fowler (יָקוּשׁ).
- **"thou sluggard; consider her ways" → "sluggard; see her ways"** (v6, 9) — Hebrew עָצֵל, no "O"; רְאֵה "see". The ant (נְמָלָה) is feminine: "her".
- **"Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler" → "which has no captain, officer or ruler"** (v7) — Hebrew קָצִין "captain" (one who decides; Josh 10:24), שֹׁטֵר "officer" (Exod 5), מֹשֵׁל "ruler"; וּ "and", "or" after the negative.
- **"and gathereth her food in the harvest" → "she has gathered in her food at the harvest"** (v8) — Hebrew אָגְרָה, a perfect of אָגַר "gather in" (beside the yiqtol תָּכִין "she prepares"), with no "and". מַאֲכָל "food".
- **"How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard?" → "How long, sluggard, will you lie down?"** (v9) — Hebrew תִּשְׁכָּב "lie down" (שָׁכַב), in the Hebrew order.
- **"a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep" → "… to lie down"** (v10) — Hebrew שֵׁנוֹת and תְּנוּמוֹת are plurals ("sleeps, slumbers"); לִשְׁכָּב "to lie down" (as v9).
- **"So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as an armed man" → "and your poverty shall come like one who roams, and your want like a man with a shield"** (v11) — Hebrew וּבָא, "and … shall come"; כִמְהַלֵּךְ, the piel participle of הָלַךְ, one who goes about (a vagrant or a prowler; uncertain); כְּאִישׁ מָגֵן, literally "like a man of a shield".
- **"A naughty person, a wicked man, walketh with a froward mouth" → "A man of Beliyyaal (of worthlessness), a man of wickedness, walking with crookedness of mouth"** (v12) — Hebrew אָדָם בְּלִיַּעַל אִישׁ אָוֶן: בְּלִיַּעַל keeps its token ("worthlessness": a common noun, WORDS-HE); אָדָם and אִישׁ both "man" (אָדָם "a human", אִישׁ "a man"); אָוֶן "wickedness". הוֹלֵךְ is a participle, as the participles of v13–14; עִקְּשׁוּת "crookedness" (noun of עִקֵּשׁ "crooked").
- **"He winketh with his eyes, he speaketh with his feet, he teacheth with his fingers" → "winking with his eyes, speaking with his feet, pointing with his fingers"** (v13) — Hebrew participles; מֹלֵל from מָלַל "speak" (KJV), also explained as "scrape, shuffle" (uncertain); מֹרֶה "pointing", the participle of הוֹרָה "point, teach" (the word of "my teachers", 5:13); אֶצְבְּעֹתָיו plural "fingers".
- **"Frowardness is in his heart, he deviseth mischief continually; he soweth discord" → "perversities are in his heart, he devises evil at every time; he sends out strifes"** (v14) — Hebrew תַּהְפֻּכוֹת "perversities" (plural; also 8:13); חֹרֵשׁ, literally "plows" (= devises; also v18); רָע "evil"; בְּכָל־עֵת "at every time". The written text has מדנים, the qere מִדְיָנִים: both are plurals of "strife" (מָדוֹן); יְשַׁלֵּחַ "sends out" (also v19).
- **"his calamity … suddenly shall he be broken without remedy" → "suddenly his calamity shall come; in an instant he shall be broken, and there is no healing"** (v15) — Hebrew פִּתְאֹם "suddenly", פֶּתַע "in an instant" (two words); אֵיד "calamity"; וְאֵין מַרְפֵּא "and there is no healing".
- **"These six things doth the LORD hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him" → "Six are these that Yahweh hates, and seven are an abomination of his soul"** (v16) — Hebrew שֶׁשׁ־הֵנָּה, "six they are"; "things" is not written. The numerical saying (six … seven) is printed as it stands; the list of v17–19 has seven members. The written text has תועבות "abominations", the qere תּוֹעֲבַת "an abomination of": the qere is translated. נַפְשׁוֹ "his soul" (not "unto him").
- **"A proud look, a lying tongue" → "eyes that are high, a tongue of falsehood"** (v17) — Hebrew עֵינַיִם רָמוֹת, literally "high eyes" (as 2 Sam 22:28 "the high"); לְשׁוֹן שָׁקֶר "a tongue of falsehood". דָּם־נָקִי "innocent blood".
- **"wicked imaginations" → "plans of wickedness"** (v18) — Hebrew מַחְשְׁבוֹת אָוֶן; חֹרֵשׁ "devises" (v14). **"be swift in running to mischief" → "hasten to run to evil"** — מְמַהֲרוֹת (מִהַר "hasten"); רָעָה "evil".
- **"A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren" → "one who breathes out lies, a false witness, and one who sends out strifes between brothers"** (v19) — Hebrew יָפִיחַ כְּזָבִים עֵד שָׁקֶר, in this order: "breathes out lies" (הֵפִיחַ), then "a false witness" (literally "a witness of falsehood"); together the sixth member. מְדָנִים "strifes" (as v14); בֵּין אַחִים "between brothers".
- **"keep thy father's commandment, and forsake not the law of thy mother" → "Guard, my son, the commandment of your father, and do not abandon the torah of your mother"** (v20) — Hebrew נְצֹר "guard" (נָצַר), in the Hebrew order; תּוֹרַת אִמֶּךָ keeps the `torah` token (WISDOM.md; a parent's teaching, "teaching" in traditional mode); אַל־תִּטֹּשׁ "do not abandon" (as 1:8).
- **"Bind them continually upon thine heart, and tie them about thy neck" → "Bind them on your heart continually; tie them on your neck"** (v21) — Hebrew תָמִיד "continually"; גַּרְגְּרֹת "neck" is plural (as 1:9); no "and".
- **"they shall lead thee … it shall keep thee … it shall talk with thee" → "it shall lead you … it shall keep watch over you … it shall talk with you"** (v22) — Hebrew תַּנְחֶה, תִּשְׁמֹר, הִיא תְשִׂיחֶךָ: all singular feminine ("it", "she"), the torah of v20 (or the commandment and torah as one); "they" is not written. בְּהִתְהַלֶּכְךָ "when you walk about" (hitpael); תִּשְׁמֹר עָלֶיךָ "keep watch over you"; וַהֲקִיצוֹתָ "and when you awake" (weqatal); תְשִׂיחֶךָ, שִׂיחַ "talk, muse" with a suffix: "talk with you".
- **"For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light" → "For a lamp is the commandment, and the torah a light"** (v23) — Hebrew order kept; תּוֹרָה with the `torah` token (as v20). **"and reproofs of instruction are the way of life" → "and the way of life are reproofs of discipline"** — תּוֹכְחוֹת מוּסָר; the Hebrew order kept.
- **"To keep thee from the evil woman, from the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman" → "to keep you from the woman of evil, from the smoothness of the tongue of a foreign woman"** (v24) — Hebrew מֵאֵשֶׁת רָע "from a woman of evil" (the same consonants can be read רֵעַ, "the wife of a neighbor", as v29; the pointing is followed); חֶלְקַת לָשׁוֹן "smoothness of tongue" (חֶלְקָה, from חָלָק "smooth", 5:3; not the חֶלְקָה "portion" of land); נָכְרִיָּה "a foreign woman" (WISDOM.md).
- **"Lust not after her beauty … neither let her take thee with her eyelids" → "Do not covet her beauty … do not let her take you with her eyelids"** (v25) — Hebrew תַּחְמֹד "covet" (חָמַד, Exod 20:17); לָקַח "take".
- **"For by means of a whorish woman a man is brought to a piece of bread: and the adulteress will hunt for the precious life" → "for on account of a woman who is a prostitute, down to a loaf of bread, and the wife of a man — a precious soul she hunts"** (v26) — Hebrew כִּי בְעַד־אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה עַד־כִּכַּר לָחֶם וְאֵשֶׁת אִישׁ נֶפֶשׁ יְקָרָה תָצוּד: the first line has no verb ("a man is brought" is supplied by KJV; others: "the price of a prostitute is a loaf of bread"); בְעַד "on account of, for the price of"; עַד "as far as, down to"; כִּכָּר "loaf" (literally "round"). אֵשֶׁת אִישׁ "the wife of a man" ("adulteress", "married woman" interpret); נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; צוּד "hunt".
- **"Can a man take fire in his bosom" → "Can a man scoop fire into his bosom"** (v27) — Hebrew יַחְתֶּה, חָתָה "scoop up, take up (coals)"; חֵיק "bosom"; בְּגָדָיו "his garments".
- **"Can one go upon hot coals" → "Or can a man walk on the coals"** (v28) — Hebrew אִם "or", אִישׁ "a man" (not "one"); גֶּחָלִים "coals" (live coals).
- **"whosoever toucheth her shall not be innocent" → "no one who touches her shall be clear"** (v29) — Hebrew לֹא יִנָּקֶה כָּל־הַנֹּגֵעַ בָּהּ, נָקָה nifal "be clear" (of guilt and its punishment). הַבָּא אֶל "the one who goes in to".
- **"Men do not despise a thief" → "They do not despise a thief"** (v30) — Hebrew לֹא־יָבוּזוּ, "they do not despise", with no subject named ("men" is supplied by KJV). Some read the line as a question ("Do they not despise …?"). **"to satisfy his soul" → "to fill his soul"** — לְמַלֵּא נַפְשׁוֹ.
- **"But if he be found, he shall restore sevenfold; he shall give all the substance of his house" → "and if he is found, he shall repay sevenfold; all the wealth of his house he shall give"** (v31) — Hebrew וְנִמְצָא, a weqatal "and (if) he is found": "if" is supplied for the condition; שִׁלֵּם "repay"; שִׁבְעָתַיִם "sevenfold"; הוֹן "wealth".
- **"But whoso committeth adultery with a woman lacketh understanding: he that doeth it destroyeth his own soul" → "He who commits adultery with a woman is lacking heart; he who would destroy his soul, he does it"** (v32) — Hebrew נֹאֵף אִשָּׁה חֲסַר־לֵב: חֲסַר־לֵב "lacking heart" (WISDOM.md; the heart as the seat of the mind); מַשְׁחִית נַפְשׁוֹ הוּא יַעֲשֶׂנָּה, word for word "a destroyer of his soul, he will do it": the one who does it is the one who destroys himself.
- **"A wound and dishonour shall he get" → "A stroke and disgrace he shall find"** (v33) — Hebrew נֶגַע "stroke" (a blow dealt; Deut 17:8), קָלוֹן "disgrace", יִמְצָא "he shall find"; חֶרְפָּתוֹ "his reproach"; מָחָה "wipe out".
- **"For jealousy is the rage of a man" → "For jealousy is the fury of a man"** (v34) — Hebrew חֲמַת־גָּבֶר: חֵמָה "fury"; גֶּבֶר "man" (the husband; "mighty man" of the standing decision would say a warrior here). חָמַל "spare"; נָקָם "vengeance".
- **"He will not regard any ransom; neither will he rest content, though thou givest many gifts" → "He will not lift up the face of any ransom, and he will not be willing, though you multiply the bribe"** (v35) — Hebrew לֹא־יִשָּׂא פְּנֵי כָל־כֹּפֶר, "lift up the face of" (= show regard to; kept, WORDS-HE); כֹּפֶר "ransom"; אָבָה "be willing"; שֹׁחַד "bribe" (singular).

### Notes
- **Hebrew verse numbers** — the same as ours in this chapter.
- **Numerical saying** (v16–19) — the form "x … x+1" (as 30:15–31; Amos 1–2); the second number gives the count of the list.
