# Proverbs 2

[1] My son, if you take my sayings
and my commandments hide away with you,

[2] so that your ear gives heed to wisdom,
you turn your heart to understanding;

[3] yes, if you call to understanding,
to understanding you give your voice,

[4] if you seek it like silver
and like hidden treasures search for it,

[5] then you will understand the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and the knowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you will find.

[6] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gives wisdom;
from his mouth are knowledge and understanding.

[7] He hides away sound wisdom for the upright,
a shield for those who walk in integrity,

[8] to guard the paths of judgment,
and the way of his faithful ones he keeps.

[9] Then you will understand righteousness and judgment
and uprightness, every good course.

[10] For wisdom will come into your heart,
and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul;

[11] devising will keep watch over you,
understanding will guard you,

[12] to deliver you from the way of evil,
from the man who speaks perversities,

[13] those who forsake the paths of uprightness
to walk in the ways of darkness,

[14] who rejoice to do evil,
are glad in the perversities of evil,

[15] whose paths are crooked,
and devious in their courses;

[16] to deliver you from a strange woman,
from a foreign woman who has made her sayings smooth,

[17] who forsakes the companion of her youth
and the covenant of her ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has forgotten;

[18] for her house sinks down to death,
and to the shades her courses;

[19] none who go in to her return,
and they do not attain the paths of life.

[20] So that you may walk in the way of the good,
and the paths of the righteous you shall keep.

[21] For the upright shall abide in the land,
and the blameless shall be left in it,

[22] and the wicked shall be cut off from the land,
and the treacherous shall be torn away from it.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים; in v17 אֱלֹהֶיהָ "her Elohim" (the God of the covenant she has forgotten; "her god" is also read).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order. v1–4 are one condition ("if … if"), v5–8 and v9–11 its two "then" halves, v12–15 and v16–19 two "to deliver you"; v20–22 close.
- **"if thou wilt receive my words, and hide my commandments with thee" → "if you take my sayings and my commandments hide away with you"** (v1) — Hebrew לָקַח "take" (as 1:3; 4:10), אֲמָרַי "my sayings" (אֵמֶר, as 1:2, 21); צָפַן "hide away" (as 1:11, 18, there "lie hidden"), object first, as kept.
- **"So that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom, and apply thine heart to understanding" → "so that your ear gives heed to wisdom, you turn your heart to understanding"** (v2) — Hebrew לְהַקְשִׁיב … אָזְנֶךָ, an infinitive with the ear as subject (הִקְשִׁיב "give heed", as 1:24); תַּטֶּה לִבְּךָ, an imperfect, still under the "if" of v1 (הִטָּה לֵב "turn the heart", "incline" in Changes as Josh 24:23); תְּבוּנָה "understanding".
- **"Yea, if thou criest after knowledge, and liftest up thy voice for understanding" → "yes, if you call to understanding, to understanding you give your voice"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי אִם "yes, if"; לַבִּינָה … לַתְּבוּנָה: first בִּינָה, then תְּבוּנָה, two words of the root בִּין that English and WISDOM.md render "understanding" (KJV "knowledge" for the first is not the Hebrew word דַּעַת); קָרָא לְ "call to"; נָתַן קוֹל "give the voice" (as 1:20).
- **"seekest her … searchest for her as for hid treasures" → "seek it … like hidden treasures search for it"** (v4) — Hebrew תְּבַקְשֶׁנָּה, תַּחְפְּשֶׂנָּה with a feminine suffix: understanding (v3), or Wisdom herself; "her" is also possible. בִּקֵּשׁ "seek", חָפַשׂ "search"; מַטְמוֹנִים "hidden treasures"; כְּ "like" (no second "as for").
- **"Then shalt thou understand the fear of the LORD, and find the knowledge of God"** (v5) — Hebrew תָּבִין "you will understand" (as v9); the second line puts the object first, as kept.
- **"out of his mouth cometh knowledge and understanding" → "from his mouth are knowledge and understanding"** (v6) — the Hebrew has no verb; "are" is supplied. תְּבוּנָה.
- **"He layeth up sound wisdom for the righteous: he is a buckler to them that walk uprightly" → "He hides away sound wisdom for the upright, a shield for those who walk in integrity"** (v7) — Hebrew יִצְפֹּן qere ("he hides away", the verb of v1); the written form (ketiv) וצפן, "and he has hidden away", differs only in tense. תּוּשִׁיָּה "sound wisdom" (uncertain: also "success, lasting help"); יְשָׁרִים "the upright" (not "the righteous": that is צַדִּיקִים, v20); מָגֵן "shield", with no "he is"; הֹלְכֵי תֹם "those who walk in integrity" (תֹּם "integrity").
- **"He keepeth the paths of judgment, and preserveth the way of his saints" → "to guard the paths of judgment, and the way of his faithful ones he keeps"** (v8) — Hebrew לִנְצֹר "to guard" (an infinitive: the purpose of the shield); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; חֲסִידָיו qere "his faithful ones" (the ketiv חסידו "his faithful one" is singular); שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"righteousness, and judgment, and equity; yea, every good path" → "righteousness and judgment and uprightness, every good course"** (v9) — Hebrew מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness" (as 1:3); מַעְגָּל "course", the track of a wheel (from עֲגָלָה "wagon"; 1 Sam 17:20 the camp's ring), as v15, 18; 4:11, 26; there is no "yea". WORDS-HE (Ps 139–141 row) gives מַעְגָּל "path"; here "course" keeps it apart from אֹרַח "path", which stands beside it in v15 and through the chapter (v8, 13, 19, 20).
- **"When wisdom entereth into thine heart" → "For wisdom will come into your heart"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי "for" (or "when"); בּוֹא "come"; נָעֵם "be pleasant"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"Discretion shall preserve thee, understanding shall keep thee" → "devising will keep watch over you, understanding will guard you"** (v11) — Hebrew מְזִמָּה "devising" (as 1:4); שָׁמַר עַל "keep watch over", נָצַר "guard"; תְּבוּנָה.
- **"from the way of the evil man, from the man that speaketh froward things" → "from the way of evil, from the man who speaks perversities"** (v12) — Hebrew מִדֶּרֶךְ רָע "from the way of evil" (no "man"); אִישׁ singular, then the plurals of v13–15 ("those who forsake"): the one man stands for a kind; תַּהְפֻּכוֹת "perversities" (as v14), from הָפַךְ "turn over".
- **"Who leave the paths of uprightness" → "those who forsake the paths of uprightness"** (v13) — Hebrew הָעֹזְבִים, with the article; עָזַב "forsake" (as v17); יֹשֶׁר "uprightness" (another noun than מֵישָׁרִים of v9; English has one word); חֹשֶׁךְ "darkness".
- **"and delight in the frowardness of the wicked" → "are glad in the perversities of evil"** (v14) — Hebrew יָגִילוּ "they are glad" (גִּיל, beside שָׂמַח "rejoice"); תַּהְפֻּכוֹת רָע "perversities of evil" (רָע "evil", not "the wicked": that is רְשָׁעִים, v22).
- **"Whose ways are crooked, and they froward in their paths" → "whose paths are crooked, and devious in their courses"** (v15) — Hebrew אָרְחֹתֵיהֶם "their paths", עִקְּשִׁים "crooked"; נְלוֹזִים "devious", nifal of לוּז (the qal "depart", 3:21; 4:21); מַעְגְּלוֹת "courses" (as v9). No "men" or "they" is written.
- **"from the strange woman, even from the stranger which flattereth with her words" → "from a strange woman, from a foreign woman who has made her sayings smooth"** (v16) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה זָרָה "a strange woman" (WISDOM.md: זָר "stranger", one outside the man's own house and marriage; not "forbidden woman", which interprets) and נָכְרִיָּה "a foreign woman" (from נָכְרִי "foreigner"); both are without the article. הֶחֱלִיקָה "has made smooth" (= flatters), אֲמָרֶיהָ "her sayings".
- **"Which forsaketh the guide of her youth" → "who forsakes the companion of her youth"** (v17) — Hebrew אַלּוּף, here "close companion, intimate" (Ps 55:13; Jer 3:4), not the "chief" of Edom (the same word in another sense); נְעוּרִים "youth"; the second line puts the object first, as kept. "The covenant of her Elohim" may be the marriage made before God (Mal 2:14) or the covenant with Israel; the verse does not say.
- **"For her house inclineth unto death, and her paths unto the dead" → "for her house sinks down to death, and to the shades her courses"** (v18) — Hebrew שָׁחָה "sink down" (bow down) is feminine and בֵּיתָהּ "her house" is masculine: "she sinks down to death, (to) her house" is also read; the MT is kept. רְפָאִים "the shades", the dead in Sheol (*rephaim*, the same word as the ancient people of that name; no token: the people are not meant; as Job 26:5; Ps 88:10); מַעְגְּלֹתֶיהָ "her courses" (as v9, 15).
- **"None that go unto her return again, neither take they hold of the paths of life" → "none who go in to her return, and they do not attain the paths of life"** (v19) — Hebrew כָּל־בָּאֶיהָ לֹא יְשׁוּבוּן, literally "all who go in to her shall not return" (בּוֹא with a woman as object, "go in to", as of marriage); הִשִּׂיג "attain, reach" (as Lev 5:7); אָרְחוֹת חַיִּים "the paths of life".
- **"That thou mayest walk in the way of good men" → "So that you may walk in the way of the good"** (v20) — Hebrew לְמַעַן "so that"; טוֹבִים "the good" ("men" is not written); the second line puts the object first; שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"For the upright shall dwell in the land, and the perfect shall remain in it" → "For the upright shall abide in the land, and the blameless shall be left in it"** (v21) — Hebrew שָׁכַן "abide" (as 1:33; not יָשַׁב "dwell"); אֶרֶץ "land" (the land given to Israel, as v22); תְמִימִים "blameless" (whole); יִוָּתְרוּ "be left (over)", nifal of יָתַר.
- **"But the wicked shall be cut off from the earth, and the transgressors shall be rooted out of it" → "and the wicked shall be cut off from the land, and the treacherous shall be torn away from it"** (v22) — Hebrew וּרְשָׁעִים "and the wicked"; מֵאֶרֶץ "from the land" (as v21; KJV "earth"); נִכְרַת "be cut off"; בּוֹגְדִים "the treacherous" (בָּגַד "deal treacherously"; not "transgressors"); יִסְּחוּ, as pointed an active form with no subject ("they shall tear away"), = "shall be torn away" (נָסַח, as Deut 28:63).

### Notes
- **"My son"** (v1) — the father's address of Prov 1–9.
- **Structure** — one sentence in Hebrew: if (v1–4) … then (v5–11) … to deliver you (v12–19) … so that (v20–22).
