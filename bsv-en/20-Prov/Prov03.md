# Proverbs 3

[1] My son, ⟦torah|my torah|my teaching⟧ do not forget,
and my commandments let your heart guard,

[2] for length of days and years of life
and ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ they will add to you.

[3] Let not ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth forsake you;
bind them on your neck,
write them on the tablet of your heart,

[4] and find favor and good insight
in the eyes of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and man.

[5] Trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with all your heart,
and on your understanding do not lean.

[6] In all your ways know him,
and he will make your paths straight.

[7] Do not be wise in your eyes;
fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and turn aside from evil.

[8] It will be healing for your navel
and drink for your bones.

[9] Honor ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from your wealth
and from the first of all your yield,

[10] and your storehouses will be filled with plenty,
and with new wine your winepresses will burst.

[11] The discipline of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my son, do not reject,
and do not loathe his reproof,

[12] for whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loves he reproves,
and like a father the son he is pleased with.

[13] Happy is the man who has found wisdom,
and the man who obtains understanding,

[14] for better is her trade than the trade of silver,
and than fine gold her yield.

[15] More precious is she than rubies,
and all your delights do not equal her.

[16] Length of days is in her right hand,
in her left riches and glory.

[17] Her ways are ways of pleasantness,
and all her tracks are ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[18] A tree of life is she to those who hold fast to her,
and those who grasp her are called happy.

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ by wisdom founded the earth,
established the heavens by understanding;

[20] by his knowledge the deeps were split,
and the skies drip dew.

[21] My son, let them not depart from your eyes;
guard sound wisdom and devising,

[22] and they will be life for your soul
and favor for your neck.

[23] Then you will walk your way in security,
and your foot will not strike.

[24] If you lie down, you will not be afraid;
and you will lie down, and your sleep will be sweet.

[25] Do not be afraid of sudden dread,
or of the devastation of the wicked, when it comes,

[26] for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will be your confidence,
and he will keep your foot from capture.

[27] Do not hold back good from its owners,
when it is in the power of your hand to do it.

[28] Do not say to your neighbor, "Go, and return, and tomorrow I will give,"
when it is with you.

[29] Do not devise evil against your neighbor,
when he dwells in security with you.

[30] Do not contend with a man without cause,
if he has not dealt you evil.

[31] Do not envy a man of violence,
and do not choose any of his ways,

[32] for an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the devious,
and with the upright is his secret counsel.

[33] The curse of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is in the house of the wicked,
and the habitation of the righteous he blesses.

[34] Surely he mocks the scoffers,
and to the humble he gives favor.

[35] Glory the wise shall inherit,
and fools lift up disgrace.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 19, 26, 32, 33) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*. v6, 25, 34 have no divine name: "him", "he" as the Hebrew.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v3 has three cola; v28 is divided where the accents divide it (after "I will give").
- **"forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments" → "my torah do not forget, and my commandments let your heart guard"** (v1) — Hebrew תּוֹרָתִי "my torah", the father's teaching (token `torah`, WISDOM.md; "teaching" in traditional mode); objects first, as kept; וְ "and" (not "but"); יִצֹּר, from נָצַר "guard" (beside שָׁמַר "keep"), a wish: "let … guard".
- **"and long life, and peace" → "and years of life and shalom"** (v2) — Hebrew וּשְׁנוֹת חַיִּים "years of life"; שָׁלוֹם (token `shalom`).
- **"mercy and truth" → "chesed and truth"** (v3) — Hebrew חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת (token `steadfast-love`); אֱמֶת "truth" (אֱמוּנָה is "faithfulness"). "Them" in "bind them" are these two. **"the table of thine heart" → "the tablet of your heart"** — לוּחַ "tablet"; גַּרְגְּרוֹת "neck" (plural "throat", as 1:9).
- **"So shalt thou find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man" → "and find favor and good insight in the eyes of Elohim and man"** (v4) — Hebrew וּמְצָא, an imperative ("and find"), the outcome of the imperatives of v3 ("so you will find" is the sense); חֵן "favor"; שֵׂכֶל "insight" (WISDOM.md; not "understanding"); בְּעֵינֵי "in the eyes of"; אָדָם "man" (mankind).
- **"lean not unto thine own understanding" → "on your understanding do not lean"** (v5) — Hebrew בָּטַח אֶל "trust in"; וְאֶל־בִּינָתְךָ אַל־תִּשָּׁעֵן, object first; נִשְׁעַן "lean on"; בִּינָה "understanding"; "own" is not written.
- **"acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths" → "know him, and he will make your paths straight"** (v6) — Hebrew דָעֵהוּ "know him" (יָדַע; "acknowledge" is a reading of it); יְיַשֵּׁר "make straight" (piel of יָשַׁר, the root of יָשָׁר "upright"); אֹרְחֹתֶיךָ "your paths".
- **"Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the LORD, and depart from evil" → "Do not be wise in your eyes; fear Yahweh and turn aside from evil"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּעֵינֶיךָ "in your eyes" ("own" not written); סוּר מֵרָע "turn aside from evil" (as Job 1:1).
- **"health to thy navel, and marrow to thy bones" → "healing for your navel and drink for your bones"** (v8) — Hebrew רִפְאוּת "healing" (only here; from רָפָא "heal"); לְשָׁרֶּךָ "for your navel" (שֹׁר, Ezek 16:4; reading לִשְׁאֵרֶךָ "for your flesh" is a correction, not kept); שִׁקּוּי "drink, watering" (Hos 2:5 "my drinks"; "refreshment" is the sense; "marrow" is not the word).
- **"Honour the LORD with thy substance, and with the firstfruits of all thine increase" → "Honor Yahweh from your wealth and from the first of all your yield"** (v9) — Hebrew כַּבֵּד "honor" (lit. "give weight to"); מֵהוֹנֶךָ "from your wealth" (מִן, a part of it; הוֹן as 1:13); רֵאשִׁית "first" (the first part; "beginning" in 1:7), not בִּכּוּרִים "firstfruits"; תְּבוּאָה "yield".
- **"So shall thy barns be filled … and thy presses shall burst out with new wine" → "and your storehouses will be filled with plenty, and with new wine your winepresses will burst"** (v10) — Hebrew וְ "and" (the outcome of v9); אֲסָמִים "storehouses"; שָׂבָע "plenty"; תִּירוֹשׁ "new wine"; יְקָבִים "winepresses" (the vats); פָּרַץ "break out, burst".
- **"despise not the chastening of the LORD; neither be weary of his correction" → "The discipline of Yahweh, my son, do not reject, and do not loathe his reproof"** (v11) — Hebrew מוּסַר יְהוָה first, as kept; מָאַס "reject" (not בָּזָה "despise", 1:7); קוּץ בְּ "loathe" (feel disgust at; "be weary of" is a weaker reading); תּוֹכַחַת "reproof". Heb 12:5 cites v11–12 in the Greek form; the verses here follow the Hebrew.
- **"For whom the LORD loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth" → "for whom Yahweh loves he reproves, and like a father the son he is pleased with"** (v12) — Hebrew יוֹכִיחַ "he reproves" (הוֹכִיחַ, as תּוֹכַחַת v11); וּכְאָב "and like a father", as the MT points it; יִרְצֶה "he is pleased with" (רָצָה). Heb 12:6 reads "and he scourges every son whom he receives", the Greek translation, which read the same letters as a verb (וְיַכְאִב "and he causes pain"; from memory); not taken into the verse.
- **"Happy is the man that findeth wisdom, and the man that getteth understanding" → "Happy is the man who has found wisdom, and the man who obtains understanding"** (v13) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "Happy" (not "Blessed"); מָצָא, a perfect, "has found"; יָפִיק, hifil of פּוּק, "brings out, obtains" (not קָנָה "get", 4:5, 7); אָדָם "man" twice; תְּבוּנָה.
- **"For the merchandise of it is better than the merchandise of silver, and the gain thereof than fine gold" → "for better is her trade than the trade of silver, and than fine gold her yield"** (v14) — Hebrew סַחְרָהּ … מִסְּחַר־כָּסֶף "her trade … than the trade of silver" (what dealing in her brings; סֹחֵר "trader"); חָרוּץ "fine gold" (a poetic word for gold); תְּבוּאָתָהּ "her yield" (as v9). "Her" is Wisdom (feminine), as v15–18.
- **"She is more precious than rubies: and all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her" → "More precious is she than rubies, and all your delights do not equal her"** (v15) — Hebrew מִפְּנִינִים qere ("than rubies"); the written form (ketiv) מפניים is another spelling; פְּנִינִים is uncertain ("pearls", "corals" are also read; red in Lam 4:7). חֲפָצֶיךָ "your delights" (things you take pleasure in); שָׁוָה בְּ "equal".
- **"and in her left hand riches and honour" → "in her left riches and glory"** (v16) — Hebrew בִּשְׂמֹאולָהּ "in her left" ("hand" not written; "her right hand" is יְמִינָהּ); עֹשֶׁר "riches"; כָּבוֹד "glory" (as v35). No "and".
- **"and all her paths are peace" → "and all her tracks are shalom"** (v17) — Hebrew נְתִיבוֹתֶיהָ "her tracks" (as 1:15); שָׁלוֹם (token); נֹעַם "pleasantness".
- **"to them that lay hold upon her: and happy is every one that retaineth her" → "to those who hold fast to her, and those who grasp her are called happy"** (v18) — Hebrew הַמַּחֲזִיקִים בָּהּ "those who hold fast to her" (הֶחֱזִיק בְּ); תֹמְכֶיהָ "those who grasp her" (תָּמַךְ "take hold, support"); מְאֻשָּׁר, pual of אִשֵּׁר "call happy", singular after a plural subject: each one of them is called happy.
- **"The LORD by wisdom hath founded the earth; by understanding hath he established the heavens" → "Yahweh by wisdom founded the earth, established the heavens by understanding"** (v19) — Hebrew יָסַד "found"; כּוֹנֵן "establish" (polel of כּוּן); the second line has no "he" and puts the verb first, as kept; תְּבוּנָה; שָׁמַיִם "heavens" (plural).
- **"the depths are broken up, and the clouds drop down the dew" → "the deeps were split, and the skies drip dew"** (v20) — Hebrew תְּהוֹמוֹת "deeps" (as Gen 1:2; Gen 7:11 "split" of the springs of the deep); נִבְקָעוּ "were split" (nifal of בָּקַע); שְׁחָקִים "skies" (beside עָנָן "cloud"); רָעַף "drip"; טַל "dew" (no article).
- **"let not them depart from thine eyes: keep sound wisdom and discretion" → "let them not depart from your eyes; guard sound wisdom and devising"** (v21) — Hebrew אַל־יָלֻזוּ, from לוּז "depart" (the root of נָלוֹז "devious", v32; 2:15); what "they" are is not named (the father's words, or sound wisdom and devising named after it); נָצַר "guard"; תֻּשִׁיָּה "sound wisdom" (as 2:7); מְזִמָּה "devising".
- **"So shall they be life unto thy soul, and grace to thy neck" → "and they will be life for your soul and favor for your neck"** (v22) — Hebrew וְיִהְיוּ "and they will be"; חֵן "favor" (as v4, 34; 1:9).
- **"Then shalt thou walk in thy way safely, and thy foot shall not stumble" → "Then you will walk your way in security, and your foot will not strike"** (v23) — Hebrew לָבֶטַח "in security" (as v29; 1:33); לֹא תִגּוֹף, from נָגַף "strike" (against a stone, Ps 91:12); "stumble" is כָּשַׁל (4:12, 19).
- **"When thou liest down … yea, thou shalt lie down" → "If you lie down … and you will lie down"** (v24) — Hebrew אִם "if"; וְשָׁכַבְתָּ "and you will lie down"; פָּחַד "be afraid"; עָרֵב "be sweet".
- **"Be not afraid of sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked" → "Do not be afraid of sudden dread, or of the devastation of the wicked"** (v25) — Hebrew פַּחַד פִּתְאֹם "sudden dread" (פַּחַד as 1:26, 27, 33); שֹׁאָה "devastation" (as 1:27); "of the wicked": the devastation that comes on the wicked, or the one they bring; not decided.
- **"For the LORD shall be thy confidence, and shall keep thy foot from being taken" → "for Yahweh will be your confidence, and he will keep your foot from capture"** (v26) — Hebrew בְכִסְלֶךָ, literally "in your confidence" (= as your confidence); כֶּסֶל is also "flank, loins" ("at your side" is also read); שָׁמַר "keep"; לֶכֶד "capture" (only here; from לָכַד).
- **"Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, when it is in the power of thine hand to do it" → "Do not hold back good from its owners, when it is in the power of your hand to do it"** (v27) — Hebrew מָנַע "hold back" (as 1:15); מִבְּעָלָיו "from its owners" (בַּעַל of a thing: those to whom the good belongs, or those who need it); לְאֵל יָדְךָ qere "in the power of your hand" (אֵל "power" in this idiom, Gen 31:29; not the divine name); the written form (ketiv) ידיך "your hands" is plural.
- **"Say not unto thy neighbour, Go, and come again, and to morrow I will give; when thou hast it by thee" → "Do not say to your neighbor, 'Go, and return, and tomorrow I will give,' when it is with you"** (v28) — Hebrew לְרֵעֲךָ qere "to your neighbor" (the written form לרעיך "to your neighbors" is plural); לֵךְ וָשׁוּב "go and return" (שׁוּב); אֶתֵּן "I will give", with no "it"; וְיֵשׁ אִתָּךְ, literally "and there is with you": "it" is supplied.
- **"Devise not evil against thy neighbour, seeing he dwelleth securely by thee" → "Do not devise evil against your neighbor, when he dwells in security with you"** (v29) — Hebrew תַּחֲרֹשׁ, from חָרַשׁ "plow, engrave", of evil "devise" (the image of plowing; note the word); וְהוּא יוֹשֵׁב "while he dwells"; לָבֶטַח "in security" (as v23).
- **"Strive not with a man without cause, if he have done thee no harm" → "Do not contend with a man without cause, if he has not dealt you evil"** (v30) — Hebrew תָּרִיב qere ("contend"; the written form תרוב is another spelling of the same verb); אָדָם "a man"; חִנָּם "without cause" (as 1:11); גְמָלְךָ רָעָה "he has dealt you evil" (גָּמַל; not "harm").
- **"Envy thou not the oppressor" → "Do not envy a man of violence"** (v31) — Hebrew קִנֵּא בְּ "envy" (the root of "jealous"); אִישׁ חָמָס "a man of violence"; בָּחַר בְּ "choose".
- **"For the froward is abomination to the LORD: but his secret is with the righteous" → "for an abomination to Yahweh is the devious, and with the upright is his secret counsel"** (v32) — Hebrew תּוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה "the abomination of Yahweh" (= to him), first, as kept; נָלוֹז "the devious one" (as 2:15); יְשָׁרִים "the upright" (not "the righteous"); סוֹד "secret counsel" (the close circle and what is said in it; "friendship" is also read).
- **"The curse of the LORD is in the house of the wicked: but he blesseth the habitation of the just" → "… and the habitation of the righteous he blesses"** (v33) — Hebrew מְאֵרָה "curse" (from אָרַר; beside קְלָלָה, which does not stand here); רָשָׁע "the wicked" (singular); נָוֶה "habitation" (lit. "pasture, abode"); צַדִּיקִים "the righteous"; וְ "and", the object first, as kept.
- **"Surely he scorneth the scorners: but he giveth grace unto the lowly" → "Surely he mocks the scoffers, and to the humble he gives favor"** (v34) — Hebrew אִם־לַלֵּצִים הוּא־יָלִיץ: אִם is "if", here taken as "surely"; "though he mocks the scoffers, yet to the humble he gives favor" is the other reading. הוּא "he" (emphatic: he himself, Yahweh of v33); הֵלִיץ "mock" (the verb of לֵץ "scoffer"); וְלַעֲנָוִים qere "and to the humble" (the written form ולעניים "the afflicted"); חֵן "favor" (not "grace"). Jas 4:6 and 1 Pet 5:5 cite the verse in the Greek form ("God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble"); the verse here follows the Hebrew.
- **"The wise shall inherit glory: but shame shall be the promotion of fools" → "Glory the wise shall inherit, and fools lift up disgrace"** (v35) — Hebrew כָּבוֹד first, as kept; נָחַל "inherit"; וּכְסִילִים מֵרִים קָלוֹן: מֵרִים is a singular participle of הֵרִים "lift up, raise" after the plural "fools": "fools carry off disgrace" (each takes it up as his portion), or "disgrace is what raises fools" (the KJV reading); not decided. קָלוֹן "disgrace".

### Notes
- **v5–6** — the call to trust is set against one's own understanding (v5b, 7a).
- **v11–12** — cited in Heb 12:5–6 from the Greek translation; **v34** — cited in Jas 4:6; 1 Pet 5:5 from the Greek translation.
