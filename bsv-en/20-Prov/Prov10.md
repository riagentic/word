# Proverbs 10

[1] The proverbs of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.
A wise son makes a father glad,
but a foolish son is the grief of his mother.

[2] Treasures of wickedness do not profit,
but righteousness delivers from death.

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ does not make the soul of the righteous hungry,
but the desire of the wicked he pushes away.

[4] Poor is he who works with a palm of slackness,
but the hand of the diligent makes rich.

[5] He who gathers in summer is a son who acts with insight;
he who sleeps deeply in harvest is a son who causes shame.

[6] Blessings are for the head of the righteous,
but the mouth of the wicked covers violence.

[7] The remembrance of the righteous is for a blessing,
but the name of the wicked will rot.

[8] The wise of heart will take commandments,
but a senseless one of lips will be thrown down.

[9] He who walks in integrity walks in security,
but he who makes his ways crooked will be known.

[10] He who winks the eye gives pain,
and a senseless one of lips will be thrown down.

[11] A fountain of life is the mouth of the righteous,
but the mouth of the wicked covers violence.

[12] Hatred stirs up strifes,
but over all transgressions love covers.

[13] On the lips of one who understands, wisdom is found,
but a rod is for the back of one lacking heart.

[14] The wise hide away knowledge,
but the mouth of a senseless one is ruin close at hand.

[15] The wealth of the rich is the town of his strength;
the ruin of the poor is their poverty.

[16] The wage of the righteous is for life,
the yield of the wicked for sin.

[17] A path to life is he who keeps discipline,
but he who forsakes reproof leads astray.

[18] He who covers hatred has lips of falsehood,
and he who brings out a report, he is a fool.

[19] In a multitude of words transgression does not cease,
but he who withholds his lips acts with insight.

[20] Chosen silver is the tongue of the righteous;
the heart of the wicked is as a little.

[21] The lips of the righteous shepherd many,
but senseless ones die through lack of heart.

[22] The blessing of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, it makes rich,
and he does not add pain with it.

[23] Like laughter to a fool is doing depravity,
and wisdom to a man of understanding.

[24] The terror of the wicked, it will come to him,
but the desire of the righteous he will give.

[25] When the tempest passes over, then the wicked is no more,
but the righteous is an everlasting foundation.

[26] Like vinegar to the teeth and like smoke to the eyes,
so is the sluggard to those who send him.

[27] The fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ adds days,
but the years of the wicked will be short.

[28] The expectation of the righteous is gladness,
but the hope of the wicked will perish.

[29] A strong place for integrity is the way of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but ruin for the workers of wickedness.

[30] The righteous will never be shaken,
but the wicked will not abide in the land.

[31] The mouth of the righteous puts forth wisdom,
but the tongue of perversities will be cut off.

[32] The lips of the righteous know what is pleasing,
but the mouth of the wicked, perversities.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 22, 27, 29) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Layout and joins** (throughout) — from here each verse is two lines (cola), kept as two printed lines in the Hebrew order. The second line has "but" or "and" only where the Hebrew has וְ ("but" where the two lines are set against each other, "and" where they run on); where the Hebrew has no וְ (v5, 15, 16, 20) no joining word is added.
- **"The proverbs of Solomon"** (v1) — Hebrew מִשְׁלֵי שְׁלֹמֹה, the heading of the collection (10:1–22:16), printed as its own line within v1 as in the Hebrew (a section break פ follows it). מָשָׁל is a saying built on a likeness; of these short sayings "proverb".
- **"maketh a glad father" → "makes a father glad"** (v1) — Hebrew יְשַׂמַּח־אָב, "father" without the article. **"the heaviness of his mother" → "the grief of his mother"** — תּוּגָה "grief". כְּסִיל "fool", here as an adjective, "foolish".
- **"Treasures of wickedness profit nothing" → "Treasures of wickedness do not profit"** (v2; also 11:4) — Hebrew לֹא־יוֹעִילוּ. אוֹצָר is the store and what is stored ("treasury", Deut 28:12); here the stored wealth.
- **"will not suffer the soul of the righteous to famish" → "does not make the soul of the righteous hungry"** (v3) — Hebrew לֹא־יַרְעִיב, hifil "make hungry"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"he casteth away the substance of the wicked" → "the desire of the wicked he pushes away"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהַוַּת רְשָׁעִים יֶהְדֹּף, in this order. הַוָּה has two senses: "desire, craving" (as here, 11:6; Mic 7:3) and "destruction" (the Psalms row). Here it stands against the hunger of the righteous: "desire". הָדַף "push (away)".
- **"He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand" → "Poor is he who works with a palm of slackness"** (v4) — Hebrew רָאשׁ עֹשֶׂה כַף־רְמִיָּה, literally "poor is one making a palm of slackness": כַּף "palm" (יָד "hand" in the second line); "with" is supplied. רְמִיָּה here is "slackness" (as 12:24, 27; 19:15), set against "the diligent"; the same letters elsewhere mean "treachery" (the Psalms row), another sense. חָרוּצִים "the diligent" (not the חָרוּץ "maimed" of Lev 22:22).
- **"a wise son" → "a son who acts with insight"** (v5) — Hebrew בֵּן מַשְׂכִּיל, the הִשְׂכִּיל "act with insight". **"he that sleepeth" → "he who sleeps deeply"** — נִרְדָּם, the deep sleep of תַּרְדֵּמָה. אָגַר "gather in". No "but": the second line has no וְ.
- **"Blessings are upon the head" → "Blessings are for the head"** (v6) — Hebrew לְרֹאשׁ, "for, to the head" (also 11:26).
- **"violence covereth the mouth of the wicked" → "the mouth of the wicked covers violence"** (v6, 11) — Hebrew וּפִי רְשָׁעִים יְכַסֶּה חָמָס. Either noun can be the subject: "the mouth covers (hides) violence" or "violence covers the mouth". The Hebrew order is kept, with the mouth first; the other reading is the KJV's.
- **"The memory of the just is blessed" → "The remembrance of the righteous is for a blessing"** (v7) — Hebrew זֵכֶר … לִבְרָכָה, "remembrance … for a blessing". רָקַב "rot".
- **"will receive commandments" → "will take commandments"** (v8) — Hebrew יִקַּח, the verb לָקַח "take".
- **"a prating fool shall fall" → "a senseless one of lips will be thrown down"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew אֱוִיל שְׂפָתַיִם, "a senseless one of lips" (one whose lips are senseless); אֱוִיל "senseless one", kept apart from כְּסִיל "fool". יִלָּבֵט, nifal of לָבַט (also Hos 4:14), "be thrown down, come to ruin": the sense is not certain.
- **"walketh uprightly walketh surely" → "walks in integrity walks in security"** (v9) — Hebrew בַּתֹּם … בֶּטַח: תֹּם "integrity", בֶּטַח "in security". **"he that perverteth his ways shall be known" → "he who makes his ways crooked will be known"** — מְעַקֵּשׁ, piel of the root of עִקֵּשׁ "crooked"; יִוָּדֵעַ "will be known" (= found out).
- **"causeth sorrow" → "gives pain"** (v10) — Hebrew יִתֵּן עַצָּבֶת, "gives pain" (עַצֶּבֶת, of the root of עֶצֶב "pain"). **"but a prating fool" → "and a senseless one of lips"** — the second line repeats v8b with וְ; it does not stand against the first line: "and". The Septuagint has another second line here (from memory): the MT is kept.
- **"a well of life" → "A fountain of life"** (v11) — Hebrew מְקוֹר חַיִּים, מָקוֹר "fountain", first in the line as in the Hebrew.
- **"Hatred stirreth up strifes: but love covereth all sins" → "Hatred stirs up strifes, but over all transgressions love covers"** (v12) — Hebrew מְדָנִים, plural of מָדוֹן "strife"; וְעַל כָּל־פְּשָׁעִים תְּכַסֶּה אַהֲבָה, in this order: כִּסָּה עַל "cover over"; פֶּשַׁע "transgression" (not "sin").
- **"of him that hath understanding" → "of one who understands"** (v13) — Hebrew נָבוֹן. **"void of understanding" → "lacking heart"** (v13; also v21; 11:12; 12:11) — חֲסַר־לֵב, as 9:4. גֵּו "back".
- **"Wise men lay up knowledge" → "The wise hide away knowledge"** (v14) — Hebrew יִצְפְּנוּ, צָפַן "hide (away), store up": what is hidden is kept, not lost. **"the mouth of the foolish is near destruction" → "the mouth of a senseless one is ruin close at hand"** — אֱוִיל; מְחִתָּה "ruin" (also v15, 29), קְרֹבָה "near".
- **"his strong city" → "the town of his strength"** (v15) — Hebrew קִרְיַת עֻזּוֹ, קִרְיָה "town", עֹז "strength". **"the destruction of the poor" → "the ruin of the poor"** — מְחִתַּת דַּלִּים; רֵישׁ "poverty". No "but" (no וְ).
- **"The labour of the righteous tendeth to life: the fruit of the wicked to sin" → "The wage of the righteous is for life, the yield of the wicked for sin"** (v16) — Hebrew פְּעֻלָּה "wage" (literally "work", what work earns), תְּבוּאָה "yield"; לְ "for". "tendeth" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"He is in the way of life that keepeth instruction" → "A path to life is he who keeps discipline"** (v17) — Hebrew אֹרַח לְחַיִּים שׁוֹמֵר מוּסָר, in this order: אֹרַח "path", מוּסָר "discipline". **"he that refuseth reproof erreth" → "he who forsakes reproof leads astray"** — עֹזֵב "forsakes"; מַתְעֶה is hifil, "causes to wander, leads astray", without an object (himself or others: not said).
- **"He that hideth hatred with lying lips, and he that uttereth a slander, is a fool" → "He who covers hatred has lips of falsehood, and he who brings out a report, he is a fool"** (v18) — two lines, two sayings. Hebrew מְכַסֶּה שִׂנְאָה שִׂפְתֵי־שָׁקֶר, literally "one covering hatred: lips of falsehood" ("has" supplied). מוֹצִא דִבָּה "brings out a report" (a report that defames); הוּא "he" kept.
- **"In the multitude of words there wanteth not sin" → "In a multitude of words transgression does not cease"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא יֶחְדַּל־פָּשַׁע, חָדַל "cease", פֶּשַׁע "transgression". **"he that refraineth his lips is wise" → "he who withholds his lips acts with insight"** — חֹשֵׂךְ "withholds"; מַשְׂכִּיל as v5.
- **"The tongue of the just is as choice silver" → "Chosen silver is the tongue of the righteous"** (v20) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף נִבְחָר, "chosen silver", first in the line, no "as". **"is little worth" → "is as a little"** — כִּמְעָט, "like a little". No "but" (no וְ).
- **"feed many" → "shepherd many"** (v21) — Hebrew יִרְעוּ, the verb רָעָה "shepherd". **"fools die for want of wisdom" → "senseless ones die through lack of heart"** — אֱוִילִים; בַּחֲסַר־לֵב, literally "in lacking heart".
- **"and he addeth no sorrow with it" → "and he does not add pain with it"** (v22) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יוֹסִף עֶצֶב עִמָּהּ; עֶצֶב "pain" (painful toil). The subject may also be עֶצֶב: "and toil adds nothing to it"; the line is kept as it stands.
- **"It is as sport to a fool to do mischief: but a man of understanding hath wisdom" → "Like laughter to a fool is doing depravity, and wisdom to a man of understanding"** (v23) — Hebrew כִּשְׂחוֹק "like laughter" (sport); זִמָּה "depravity"; the second line has no verb and leans on the first ("and wisdom [is like laughter, a delight,] to a man of understanding"); תְּבוּנָה "understanding".
- **"The fear of the wicked" → "The terror of the wicked"** (v24) — Hebrew מְגוֹרַת, "terror" (root גּוּר "be afraid"); יִרְאָה "fear" does not stand here. **"shall be granted" → "he will give"** — יִתֵּן, active, the subject not named; תַּאֲוָה "craving", of a good wish "desire".
- **"As the whirlwind passeth, so is the wicked no more" → "When the tempest passes over, then the wicked is no more"** (v25) — Hebrew כַּעֲבוֹר סוּפָה וְאֵין רָשָׁע; סוּפָה "tempest"; the וְ that opens the main clause is "then". **"an everlasting foundation"** — יְסוֹד עוֹלָם, literally "a foundation of the age".
- **"prolongeth days" → "adds days"** (v27) — Hebrew תּוֹסִיף, the verb יָסַף "add". **"shall be shortened" → "will be short"** — תִּקְצֹרְנָה, qal.
- **"The hope of the righteous shall be gladness: but the expectation of the wicked" → "The expectation of the righteous is gladness, but the hope of the wicked"** (v28) — Hebrew תּוֹחֶלֶת "expectation" (root יחל) in the first line, תִּקְוָה "hope" in the second; KJV has them the other way. No verb in the first line.
- **"The way of the LORD is strength to the upright: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity" → "A strong place for integrity is the way of Yahweh, but ruin for the workers of wickedness"** (v29) — Hebrew מָעוֹז לַתֹּם, in this order: מָעוֹז "strong place"; תֹּם is "integrity" as pointed (v9), not תָּם "the blameless one", which many read here. פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness". "shall be" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"shall never be removed" → "will never be shaken"** (v30) — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם בַּל־יִמּוֹט; מוֹט nifal "be shaken". **"shall not inhabit the earth" → "will not abide in the land"** — שָׁכַן "abide"; אֶרֶץ "land".
- **"bringeth forth wisdom" → "puts forth wisdom"** (v31) — Hebrew יָנוּב, of a plant "bear fruit, sprout" (Ps 62:10; 92:14). **"the froward tongue shall be cut out" → "the tongue of perversities will be cut off"** — לְשׁוֹן תַּהְפֻּכוֹת; נִכְרַת "be cut off".
- **"know what is acceptable" → "know what is pleasing"** (v32) — Hebrew רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (as 11:1, 20, 27; 12:2, 22); with "know", "what is pleasing". **"speaketh frowardness" → "perversities"** — the line has no verb; "speaketh" is supplied in KJV.

### Notes
- **Begins the main Solomonic collection** (10:1–22:16) — short sayings of two lines, most of them set one line against the other.
