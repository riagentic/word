# Proverbs 17

[1] Better is a dry morsel, and ease with it,
than a house full of sacrifices of contention.

[2] A slave who acts with insight will rule over a son who brings shame,
and in the midst of brothers he will share an inheritance.

[3] A refining pot is for silver, and a furnace for gold,
and he who tries hearts is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] An evildoer gives heed to a lip of wickedness;
falsehood gives ear to a tongue of destructions.

[5] He who derides the destitute reproaches his Maker;
he who is glad at calamity will not be clear.

[6] The crown of old men is sons of sons,
and the beauty of sons is their fathers.

[7] Not fitting for a base fool is a lip of excess;
how much less for a noble a lip of falsehood.

[8] A stone of favor is the bribe in the eyes of its owner;
to all that he turns, he acts with insight.

[9] He who covers a transgression seeks love,
but he who repeats a matter separates a close friend.

[10] A rebuke goes down into one who has understanding
more than striking a fool a hundred times.

[11] Only rebellion does the evil one seek,
and a cruel messenger will be sent against him.

[12] Let a bereaved bear meet a man,
and not a fool in his folly.

[13] He who returns evil for good —
evil will not depart from his house.

[14] One who lets out water is the beginning of strife;
and before the contention breaks out, leave it.

[15] He who justifies the wicked and he who condemns the righteous —
an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, even both of them.

[16] Why is there this price in the hand of a fool,
to buy wisdom, when there is no heart?

[17] At every time the friend loves,
and a brother is born for distress.

[18] A man lacking heart strikes the palm,
becoming surety with a pledge before his neighbor.

[19] He who loves transgression loves struggle;
he who makes his entrance high seeks breaking.

[20] The crooked of heart will not find good,
and he who is turned about in his tongue will fall into evil.

[21] He who begets a fool, it is to grief for him,
and the father of a base fool will not rejoice.

[22] A glad heart makes healing good,
but a stricken spirit dries up the bone.

[23] A bribe from the bosom the wicked takes,
to turn aside the paths of judgment.

[24] Before the face of one who has understanding is wisdom,
but the eyes of a fool are on the end of the earth.

[25] A vexation to his father is a foolish son,
and bitterness to her who bore him.

[26] Also, to fine the righteous is not good,
to strike nobles for uprightness.

[27] He who withholds his sayings knows knowledge,
and precious of spirit is a man of understanding.

[28] Even a senseless one who keeps silent is reckoned wise;
he who shuts his lips, understanding.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 15) — Hebrew יהוה.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; each line keeps its וְ "and" (or "but" where the second line sets itself against the first) as the Hebrew has it, and no line is turned into a subordinate clause.
- **"quietness therewith … sacrifices with strife" → "and ease with it … sacrifices of contention"** (v1) — Hebrew וְשַׁלְוָה־בָהּ "and ease in it"; זִבְחֵי־רִיב "sacrifices of contention": the meat of sacrifices eaten at a feast; רִיב "contention".
- **"A wise servant … a son that causeth shame" → "A slave who acts with insight … a son who brings shame"** (v2) — Hebrew עֶבֶד "slave"; מַשְׂכִּיל "acting with insight" (הִשְׂכִּיל); מֵבִישׁ "bringing shame". **"shall have part of the inheritance among the brethren" → "in the midst of brothers he will share an inheritance"** — יַחֲלֹק "divide", here of taking a share; נַחֲלָה without the article.
- **"but the LORD trieth the hearts" → "and he who tries hearts is Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew וּבֹחֵן לִבּוֹת יְהוָה, a participle, in the Hebrew order; בָּחַן "try"; מַצְרֵף "refining pot" (from צָרַף "refine"); כּוּר "furnace" for smelting.
- **"A wicked doer … false lips … a liar … a naughty tongue" → "An evildoer … a lip of wickedness; falsehood … a tongue of destructions"** (v4) — Hebrew שְׂפַת־אָוֶן "a lip of wickedness"; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" stands as the subject (= the liar); לְשׁוֹן הַוֺּת "a tongue of destructions", plural as printed. הִקְשִׁיב "give heed", הֶאֱזִין "give ear".
- **"Whoso mocketh the poor" → "He who derides the destitute"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹעֵג לָרָשׁ; רָשׁ "destitute" (as Ps 82:3; דַּל is "poor"). עֹשֵׂהוּ "his Maker", lit. "the one who made him". **"shall not be unpunished" → "will not be clear"** — לֹא יִנָּקֶה, nifal of נָקָה, "be clear" (acquitted). אֵיד "calamity", singular.
- **"Children's children … the glory of children" → "sons of sons … the beauty of sons"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּנֵי בָנִים, בָּנִים "sons"; תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (also 19:11; 20:29); זְקֵנִים "old men" (elsewhere "elders": the aged are meant here).
- **"Excellent speech becometh not a fool: much less do lying lips a prince" → "Not fitting for a base fool is a lip of excess; how much less for a noble a lip of falsehood"** (v7) — Hebrew נָבָל "base fool" (not כְּסִיל); שְׂפַת־יֶתֶר "a lip of excess": יֶתֶר "what is over, excess" (elsewhere "the rest"); whether fine or arrogant speech is meant is not said. נָדִיב "noble"; אַף כִּי "how much less".
- **"A gift is as a precious stone in the eyes of him that hath it: whithersoever it turneth, it prospereth" → "A stone of favor is the bribe in the eyes of its owner; to all that he turns, he acts with insight"** (v8) — Hebrew אֶבֶן־חֵן "a stone of favor" (a charm that wins favor); שֹׁחַד "bribe"; בְּעָלָיו "its owner" (plural form). יִפְנֶה "he / it turns": the owner or the bribe; יַשְׂכִּיל "act with insight", also "succeed" (Josh 1:8): the sense here may be "he succeeds".
- **"separateth very friends" → "separates a close friend"** (v9) — Hebrew מַפְרִיד אַלּוּף, singular; אַלּוּף "close friend" (as Prov 16:28; the "chief" of Edom is another sense of the word). שֹׁנֶה בְדָבָר "repeats a matter".
- **"A reproof entereth more into a wise man than an hundred stripes into a fool" → "A rebuke goes down into one who has understanding more than striking a fool a hundred times"** (v10) — Hebrew תֵּחַת "goes down"; גְּעָרָה "rebuke" (as 2 Sam 22:16); מֵבִין "one who has understanding" (not "wise"); מֵהַכּוֹת כְּסִיל מֵאָה "than striking a fool a hundred": "times" supplied.
- **"An evil man seeketh only rebellion: therefore a cruel messenger" → "Only rebellion does the evil one seek, and a cruel messenger"** (v11) — Hebrew אַךְ־מְרִי יְבַקֶּשׁ־רָע, Hebrew order; the line can also be read "a rebel seeks only evil". "therefore" is not in the Hebrew (וּ "and"). מַלְאָךְ "messenger".
- **"Let a bear robbed of her whelps meet a man, rather than a fool" → "Let a bereaved bear meet a man, and not a fool"** (v12) — Hebrew פָּגוֹשׁ, infinitive absolute as a wish; דֹּב שַׁכּוּל "a bereaved bear" (masculine forms); וְאַל "and not".
- **"Whoso rewardeth evil for good" → "He who returns evil for good"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵשִׁיב "returns"; תַּחַת "for" (lit. "in place of"). Qere תָמוּשׁ "will depart" (ketiv תמיש, the same verb in another form).
- **"The beginning of strife is as when one letteth out water: therefore leave off contention, before it be meddled with" → "One who lets out water is the beginning of strife; and before the contention breaks out, leave it"** (v14) — Hebrew פּוֹטֵר מַיִם רֵאשִׁית מָדוֹן, no "as"; מָדוֹן "strife", רִיב "contention"; הִתְגַּלַּע "break out" (also 18:1; 20:3; meaning not certain: "burst forth, show the teeth"); נְטוֹשׁ "leave" (it).
- **"even they both are abomination to the LORD" → "an abomination to Yahweh, even both of them"** (v15) — Hebrew תּוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה גַּם־שְׁנֵיהֶם, lit. "an abomination of Yahweh"; Hebrew order (as 20:10, 12). הִצְדִּיק "justify", הִרְשִׁיעַ "condemn".
- **"Wherefore is there a price … to get wisdom, seeing he hath no heart to it?" → "Why is there this price … to buy wisdom, when there is no heart?"** (v16) — Hebrew לָמָּה־זֶּה "why this"; מְחִיר "price"; קָנָה "buy"; וְלֶב־אָיִן "and heart there is none" (heart = mind, as v18).
- **"A friend loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity" → "At every time the friend loves, and a brother is born for distress"** (v17) — Hebrew order; הָרֵעַ "the friend", with the article; צָרָה "distress".
- **"A man void of understanding striketh hands, and becometh surety in the presence of his friend" → "A man lacking heart strikes the palm, becoming surety with a pledge before his neighbor"** (v18) — Hebrew אָדָם חֲסַר־לֵב "a man lacking heart" (heart = mind); תּוֹקֵעַ כָּף "strikes the palm" (singular); עֹרֵב עֲרֻבָּה "going surety (with) a pledge"; רֵעֵהוּ "his neighbor".
- **"He loveth transgression that loveth strife: and he that exalteth his gate seeketh destruction" → "He who loves transgression loves struggle; he who makes his entrance high seeks breaking"** (v19) — Hebrew order (the lover of transgression is the subject); מַצָּה "struggle" (from נָצָה); פִּתְחוֹ "his entrance" (פֶּתַח, not "gate"); שֶׁבֶר "breaking".
- **"He that hath a froward heart findeth no good: and he that hath a perverse tongue falleth into mischief" → "The crooked of heart will not find good, and he who is turned about in his tongue will fall into evil"** (v20) — Hebrew עִקֶּשׁ־לֵב "crooked of heart"; נֶהְפָּךְ בִּלְשׁוֹנוֹ "turned about in his tongue" (nifal of הָפַךְ); רָעָה "evil".
- **"doeth it to his sorrow … the father of a fool hath no joy" → "it is to grief for him … the father of a base fool will not rejoice"** (v21) — Hebrew לְתוּגָה לוֹ "to grief for him"; the second line has נָבָל "base fool" (the first כְּסִיל "fool"); לֹא־יִשְׂמַח "will not rejoice". יֹלֵד "begets".
- **"A merry heart doeth good like a medicine: but a broken spirit drieth the bones" → "A glad heart makes healing good, but a stricken spirit dries up the bone"** (v22) — Hebrew יֵיטִב גֵּהָה "makes good a healing" (גֵּהָה only here; "medicine" is a guess at the noun, and "like" is not in the Hebrew); רוּחַ נְכֵאָה "a stricken spirit"; גָּרֶם "bone", singular.
- **"A wicked man taketh a gift out of the bosom to pervert the ways of judgment" → "A bribe from the bosom the wicked takes, to turn aside the paths of judgment"** (v23) — Hebrew order; שֹׁחַד "bribe"; לְהַטּוֹת "to turn aside" ("pervert" is the sense); אָרְחוֹת "paths".
- **"Wisdom is before him that hath understanding; but the eyes of a fool are in the ends of the earth" → "Before the face of one who has understanding is wisdom … on the end of the earth"** (v24) — Hebrew אֶת־פְּנֵי מֵבִין "with / before the face of one who has understanding"; בִּקְצֵה־אָרֶץ, singular "end".
- **"A foolish son is a grief to his father" → "A vexation to his father is a foolish son"** (v25) — Hebrew כַּעַס "vexation" (not תּוּגָה "grief" of v21); Hebrew order; מֶמֶר "bitterness".
- **"to punish the just is not good, nor to strike princes for equity" → "to fine the righteous is not good, to strike nobles for uprightness"** (v26) — Hebrew עֲנוֹשׁ "to fine"; נְדִיבִים "nobles"; עַל־יֹשֶׁר "for uprightness" (יֹשֶׁר, root of יָשָׁר); no "nor" in the Hebrew.
- **"He that hath knowledge spareth his words: and a man of understanding is of an excellent spirit" → "He who withholds his sayings knows knowledge, and precious of spirit is a man of understanding"** (v27) — Hebrew חוֹשֵׂךְ אֲמָרָיו יוֹדֵעַ דָּעַת, in this order; qere יְקַר־רוּחַ "precious of spirit" (rare, sparing: as 1 Sam 3:1 "rare"); the ketiv וְקַר־רוּחַ "and cool of spirit". אִישׁ תְּבוּנָה "a man of understanding".
- **"Even a fool, when he holdeth his peace, is counted wise: and he that shutteth his lips is esteemed a man of understanding" → "Even a senseless one who keeps silent is reckoned wise; he who shuts his lips, understanding"** (v28) — Hebrew אֱוִיל "senseless one" (not כְּסִיל); מַחֲרִישׁ "keeping silent"; יֵחָשֵׁב "is reckoned"; the second line has no verb ("is reckoned" carries over) and no "a man": נָבוֹן "understanding".

### Notes
- **Words of the book** — כְּסִיל "fool", אֱוִיל "senseless one", נָבָל "base fool", מֵבִין "one who has understanding", נָבוֹן "understanding", רָשׁ "destitute", דַּל "poor": one rendering each in Proverbs.
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew numbering is the same.
