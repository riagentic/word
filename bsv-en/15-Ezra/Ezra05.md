# Ezra 5

[1] And ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ the son of Iddo, the prophets, prophesied to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were in ⟦judah|Yehud|Judah⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, in the name of the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ that was over them.

[2] Then ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ the son of Shealtiel and ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ the son of ⟦jozadak|Yotzadaq|Jozadak⟧ rose up and began to build the house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and with them were the prophets of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, helping them.

[3] At that time Tattenai the governor across the River and ⟦shethar-bozenai|Shetar-Bozenai|Shethar-bozenai⟧ and their associates came to them, and thus they said to them: "Who made a decree for you to build this house and to finish this structure?"

[4] Then thus we said to them: "What are the names of the men who are building this building?"

[5] But the eye of their ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ was on the elders of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and they did not make them cease until a report should go to ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧, and then they would return a document about this.

[6] The copy of the letter that Tattenai the governor across the River and ⟦shethar-bozenai|Shetar-Bozenai|Shethar-bozenai⟧ and his associates, the investigators who were across the River, sent to ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king.

[7] They sent word to him, and thus was written in it:

"To ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king, all peace.

[8] Be it known to the king that we went to the province of ⟦judah|Yehud|Judah⟧, to the house of the great ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧; and it is being built of great stones, and timber is being set in the walls; and this work is being done with diligence and prospers in their hand.

[9] Then we asked these elders; thus we said to them: 'Who made a decree for you to build this house and to finish this structure?'

[10] And also their names we asked them, to make known to you, that we might write the names of the men who are at their head.

[11] And thus they returned us word, saying: 'We are the servants of the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens and the earth, and we are building the house that was built many years before this, which a great king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ built and finished.

[12] But because our fathers angered the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens, he gave them into the hand of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdi|Chaldean⟧; and this house he tore down, and the people he carried into exile to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[13] However, in the first year of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ the king made a decree to build this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧.

[14] And also the vessels of the house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, of gold and silver, which ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ took out of the temple that was in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and brought them to the temple of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ the king took them out of the temple of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and they were given to one whose name was ⟦sheshbazzar|Sheshbatzar|Sheshbazzar⟧, whom he had made governor.

[15] And he said to him, "Take these vessels; go, put them down in the temple that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and let the house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ be built on its place."

[16] Then this ⟦sheshbazzar|Sheshbatzar|Sheshbazzar⟧ came, he laid the foundations of the house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and from then until now it is being built, and it is not finished.'

[17] And now, if it is good to the king, let search be made in the house of the treasures of the king, there in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, whether it is so that a decree was made by ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ the king to build this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and the will of the king about this let him send to us."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elah*"** (throughout) — Aramaic אֱלָהּ *Elah* (v1, 11, 12; with the emphatic ending, אֱלָהָא *Elaha*, v2, 8, 13–17; אֱלָהֲהֹם "their Elah", v5), the Aramaic word for "God"; the whole chapter is Aramaic.
- **"Then the prophets, Haggai the prophet, and Zechariah the son of Iddo" → "And Haggai the prophet and Zechariah the son of Iddo, the prophets"** (v1) — the Aramaic order: וְהִתְנַבִּי חַגַּי נְבִיָּא וּזְכַרְיָה בַר־עִדּוֹא נְבִיַּיָּא (qere נְבִיָּא "the prophet", נְבִיַּיָּא "the prophets"; the ketiv spells them otherwise). **"Judah"** (v1, 8) — Aramaic יְהוּד *Yehud*, the name of the Persian province. **"even unto them" → "that was over them"** — Aramaic עֲלֵיהוֹן "over / upon them": the name, or the Elah of Israel, was over them; the Aramaic does not decide.
- **"helping them"** (v2) — Aramaic מְסָעֲדִין. **"to build"** (v2, 3, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17) — Aramaic בְּנָה "build", not "rebuild".
- **"At the same time" → "At that time"** (v3) — Aramaic בֵּהּ־זִמְנָא. **"governor on this side the river" → "the governor across the River"** (v3, 6) — Aramaic פַּחַת עֲבַר־נַהֲרָה. **"Who hath commanded you" → "Who made a decree for you"** (v3, 9) — Aramaic מַן־שָׂם לְכֹם טְעֵם, literally "Who set for you a decree". **"to make up this wall" → "to finish this structure"** (v3, 9) — Aramaic אֻשַּׁרְנָא, a Persian loanword whose sense is uncertain ("structure", "furnishing", "woodwork" are given); שַׁכְלֵל "finish".
- **"Then said we" → "Then thus we said"** (v4) — the Aramaic has "we" (אֲמַרְנָא); the Septuagint has "they said" (cited from memory). The MT is kept.
- **"that they could not cause them to cease" → "and they did not make them cease"** (v5) — Aramaic וְלָא־בַטִּלוּ הִמּוֹ. **"till the matter came to Darius" → "until a report should go to Darius"** — Aramaic טַעְמָא, the word that is "decree" elsewhere; here what is reported. **"returned answer by letter" → "would return a document"** — Aramaic נִשְׁתְּוָנָא, as 4:7.
- **"the Apharsachites" → "the investigators"** (v6) — Aramaic אֲפַרְסְכָיֵא, taken as a Persian title, "investigators, inspectors" (uncertain); KJV takes it as a people. It is another word than אֲפָרְסָיֵא "Persians" in 4:9.
- **"They sent a letter unto him" → "They sent word to him"** (v7) — Aramaic פִּתְגָמָא, as 4:17. **"wherein was written thus" → "and thus was written in it"** — Aramaic בְּגַוֵּהּ "in its midst". **"all peace"** — Aramaic שְׁלָמָא כֹלָּא.
- **"the province of Judea" → "the province of Judah"** (v8) — Aramaic יְהוּד מְדִינְתָּא. **"the great God" → "the great Elah"** — Aramaic אֱלָהָא רַבָּא. **"great stones"** (v8) — Aramaic אֶבֶן גְּלָל, literally "stone of rolling": stones so great that they are rolled (others take it as "dressed stone"; uncertain). **"walls"** — Aramaic כֻּתְלַיָּא, the walls of a building (שׁוּר in 4:12 is the wall of a city; English has one word). **"goeth fast on" → "is being done with diligence"** — Aramaic אָסְפַּרְנָא, a Persian loanword, "diligently, exactly" (also 6:8, 12, 13; 7:17, 21, 26).
- **"to certify thee" → "to make known to you"** (v10) — as 4:14. **"the names of the men"** — Aramaic שֻׁם־גֻּבְרַיָּא, "name" in the singular, of each. **"that were the chief of them" → "who are at their head"** — Aramaic דִּי בְרָאשֵׁיהֹם.
- **"returned us answer" → "returned us word"** (v11) — Aramaic פִּתְגָמָא. **"the God of heaven and earth" → "the Elah of the heavens and the earth"** — Aramaic שְׁמַיָּא, plural in form as Hebrew שָׁמַיִם (so v12; 6:9, 10; 7:12, 21, 23). **"these many years ago" → "many years before this"** — Aramaic מִקַּדְמַת דְּנָה שְׁנִין שַׂגִּיאָן. **"set up" → "finished"** — שַׁכְלֵל, as v3.
- **"provoked … unto wrath" → "angered"** (v12) — Aramaic הַרְגִּזוּ. **"the Chaldean"** — qere כַּסְדָּאָה, singular, of Nebuchadnezzar. **"destroyed" → "tore down"** — Aramaic סַתְרֵהּ. **"carried … away" → "carried into exile"** — Aramaic הַגְלִי, as 4:10.
- **"those did Cyrus … take out" → "Cyrus the king took them out"** (v14) — the Aramaic repeats the object (הִמּוֹ). **"one, whose name was"** — Aramaic שְׁמֵהּ "his name".
- **"carry them into" → "put them down in"** (v15) — Aramaic אֲחֵת "put down, deposit"; qere אֵל "these" (ketiv אלה, the Hebrew spelling). **"in his place" → "on its place"** — Aramaic עַל־אַתְרֵהּ.
- **"laid the foundation" → "laid the foundations"** (v16) — Aramaic אֻשַּׁיָּא, plural. **"hath it been in building, and yet it is not finished" → "it is being built, and it is not finished"** — Aramaic מִתְבְּנֵא וְלָא שְׁלִם.
- **"if it seem good to the king" → "if it is good to the king"** (v17) — Aramaic הֵן עַל־מַלְכָּא טָב, literally "if upon the king it is good". **"the king's treasure house" → "the house of the treasures of the king"** — Aramaic בְּבֵית גִּנְזַיָּא. **"his pleasure" → "the will of the king"** — Aramaic רְעוּת מַלְכָּא.

### Notes

- **Language** — the whole chapter is Aramaic (4:8–6:18).
- **Haggai and Zechariah** — prophets of the second year of Darius I (520 BC; Hag 1:1; Zech 1:1). Zechariah is "son of Berechiah, son of Iddo" in Zech 1:1.
- **"We said"** (v4) — the narrator speaks in the first person here, or the words belong to the officials' report that follows (v9–10); the verse does not say.
- **Tattenai** — governor of the province across the River under Darius; the name is also known from a Babylonian document (from memory).
- **Sheshbazzar and Zerubbabel** — the reply names Sheshbazzar as the one who laid the foundations (v16); 3:8–10 tells it of Zerubbabel and Jeshua. They may be one man, or two leaders one after the other.
- **"A great king of Israel"** (v11) — Solomon (1 Kgs 6).
