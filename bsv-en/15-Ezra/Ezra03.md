# Ezra 3

[1] And the seventh month arrived, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were in the cities; and the people gathered as one man to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] And ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ the son of ⟦jozadak|Yotzadaq|Jozadak⟧ arose, and his brothers the priests, and ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ the son of Shealtiel and his brothers, and they built the altar of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to offer up burnt offerings on it, as it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] And they established the altar on its bases, for dread was upon them from the peoples of the lands; and they offered up burnt offerings on it to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, burnt offerings for the morning and for the evening.

[4] And they made ⟦sukkot|the Feast of Sukkot|the Feast of Booths⟧, as it is written, and the burnt offering of day by day by number, according to the ordinance, the matter of a day in its day;

[5] and after that the continual burnt offering, and for the months, and for all the appointed times of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that were sanctified, and for everyone who offered willingly a freewill offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer up burnt offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had not been founded.

[7] And they gave silver to the hewers and to the craftsmen, and food and drink and oil to the ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧ and to the ⟦tyrians|Tzorim|Tyrians⟧, to bring cedar wood from the ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ to the sea of ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧, according to the permission of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧ for them.

[8] And in the second year of their coming to the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, in the second month, ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ the son of Shealtiel and ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ the son of ⟦jozadak|Yotzadaq|Jozadak⟧ began, and the rest of their brothers, the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and all who had come from the captivity to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and they made the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ stand, from twenty years old and upward, to oversee the work of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] And ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ stood, his sons and his brothers, ⟦kadmiel|Qadmiel|Kadmiel⟧ and his sons, the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, as one, to oversee those doing the work in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: the sons of ⟦henadad|Chenadad|Henadad⟧, their sons and their brothers, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧.

[10] And the builders founded the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they made the priests stand, clothed, with trumpets, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ the sons of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧ with cymbals, to praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at the hands of David king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[11] And they answered in praising and in giving thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For he is good,
for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever upon ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

And all the people shouted a great shout in praising ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, because the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was founded.

[12] And many of the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and the heads of the fathers, the old men who had seen the first house at its founding — this house in their eyes — were weeping with a loud voice; and many, with shouting in gladness, raised the voice.

[13] And the people were not discerning the sound of the shout of gladness from the sound of the weeping of the people, for the people were shouting a great shout, and the sound was heard as far as far off.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v2 ("the man of Elohim"), v8, v9 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when the seventh month was come … in the cities" → "And the seventh month arrived … in the cities"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּגַּע, of נָגַע "touch, reach": the month reached them; בֶּעָרִים "in the cities", with the article, not "their". וַיֵּאָסְפוּ, nifal: "gathered" (or "were gathered").
- **"to offer burnt offerings" → "to offer up burnt offerings"** (v2, 3, 6) — Hebrew הֶעֱלָה עֹלוֹת, literally "make go up goings-up"; one root. **"the law of Moses"** — token *torah*.
- **"they set the altar upon his bases; for fear was upon them because of the people of those countries" → "they established the altar on its bases, for dread was upon them from the peoples of the lands"** (v3) — Hebrew הֵכִין "establish"; מְכוֹנֹתָיו "its bases", plural: the place where it had stood; כִּי בְּאֵימָה עֲלֵיהֶם, literally "for in dread upon them": the clause is short and hard; "dread was upon them" is its sense. עַמֵּי הָאֲרָצוֹת "the peoples of the lands". **"they offered"** — the qere וַיַּעֲלוּ "and they offered up"; the ketiv ויעל is singular, "and he offered up". **"morning and evening" → "for the morning and for the evening"** — Hebrew לַבֹּקֶר וְלָעָרֶב.
- **"kept also the feast of tabernacles … offered the daily burnt offerings by number, according to the custom, as the duty of every day required" → "made the Feast of Sukkot … and the burnt offering of day by day by number, according to the ordinance, the matter of a day in its day"** (v4) — Hebrew עָשָׂה חַג "make a feast"; עֹלַת יוֹם בְּיוֹם, a singular; "offered" is not in the Hebrew: "made" covers it; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", of a ruling "ordinance"; דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ, as 2 Chr 8:13.
- **"both of the new moons, and of all the set feasts of the LORD that were consecrated, and of every one that willingly offered a freewill offering" → "and for the months, and for all the appointed times of Yahweh that were sanctified, and for everyone who offered willingly a freewill offering"** (v5) — Hebrew חֳדָשִׁים "months" (the new moons are meant); מוֹעֲדֵי יְהוָה "the appointed times of Yahweh", not "feasts"; מְקֻדָּשִׁים "sanctified"; מִתְנַדֵּב נְדָבָה, verb and noun of one root.
- **"But the foundation of the temple of the LORD was not yet laid" → "and the temple of Yahweh had not been founded"** (v6) — Hebrew לֹא יֻסָּד, a pual of יָסַד "found"; no "foundation" noun and no "yet". הֵיכָל "temple".
- **"They gave money also unto the masons, and to the carpenters; and meat, and drink, and oil, unto them of Zidon, and to them of Tyre … to the sea of Joppa, according to the grant that they had of Cyrus" → "they gave silver to the hewers and to the craftsmen, and food and drink and oil … to bring cedar wood from the Lebanon to the sea of Joppa, according to the permission of Cyrus … for them"** (v7) — no "also" in the Hebrew; כֶּסֶף "silver"; חֹצְבִים "hewers"; חָרָשִׁים "craftsmen"; מַאֲכָל "food"; מִשְׁתֶּה here "drink" (elsewhere "feast", a drinking); עֲצֵי אֲרָזִים "cedar wood" (literally "wood of cedars"); הַלְּבָנוֹן has the article; רִשְׁיוֹן, found only here, "permission"; עֲלֵיהֶם, literally "upon them".
- **"to the house of God at Jerusalem … appointed the Levites … to set forward the work" → "to the house of Elohim, to Jerusalem … made the Levites stand … to oversee the work"** (v8) — Hebrew לִירוּשָׁלִַם "to Jerusalem", as a second goal; הֶעֱמִיד "make stand"; נִצֵּחַ עַל "oversee". "From twenty years old and upward": literally "from a son of twenty years and upward". The Hebrew puts "began" before the names.
- **"Then stood Jeshua with his sons and his brethren, Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah, together, to set forward the workmen … with their sons and their brethren the Levites" → "And Jeshua stood, his sons and his brothers … as one, to oversee those doing the work … the sons of Henadad, their sons and their brothers, the Levites"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֹד, singular, with the others named after it; כְּאֶחָד "as one"; עֹשֵׂה הַמְּלָאכָה "the doers of the work"; no "with" and no "also" before "the sons of Henadad". "The sons of Judah" is MT (see Notes).
- **"when the builders laid the foundation … they set the priests in their apparel with trumpets … after the ordinance of David" → "And the builders founded … and they made the priests stand, clothed, with trumpets … at the hands of David"** (v10) — Hebrew וְיִסְּדוּ "and they founded", a main clause; הֶעֱמִיד "make stand"; מְלֻבָּשִׁים "clothed" ("in their apparel" is not in the Hebrew); חֲצֹצְרוֹת "trumpets", plain; עַל־יְדֵי, literally "upon the hands of": by David's direction.
- **"they sang together by course in praising and giving thanks unto the LORD; because he is good, for his mercy endureth for ever toward Israel" → "And they answered in praising and in giving thanks to Yahweh: 'For he is good, for his chesed is forever upon Israel'"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲנוּ "and they answered": one group answered another, or they answered in song; "sang by course" is the sense, not the word. כִּי … כִּי "for … for"; the refrain has no verb ("is", not "endures"); עַל "upon". **"shouted with a great shout … because the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid" → "shouted a great shout … because the house of Yahweh was founded"** — Hebrew הֵרִיעוּ תְרוּעָה, verb and noun of one root, of voices; הוּסַד, hofal of יָסַד "found".
- **"ancient men, that had seen the first house, when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes, wept with a loud voice; and many shouted aloud for joy" → "the old men who had seen the first house at its founding — this house in their eyes — were weeping with a loud voice; and many, with shouting in gladness, raised the voice"** (v12) — Hebrew הַזְּקֵנִים, the word for "elders" (literally "old men"): here those old enough to remember; בְּיָסְדוֹ "at its founding" may belong to "the first house" (as the accents divide) or to "this house"; זֶה הַבַּיִת בְּעֵינֵיהֶם, literally "this house in their eyes": before their eyes, or as it seemed to them (Hag 2:3); both are possible. בֹּכִים "weeping", a participle; בִּתְרוּעָה בְשִׂמְחָה לְהָרִים קוֹל, literally "with shouting, in gladness, to raise the voice": an infinitive, not a finite verb. "Gladness": שִׂמְחָה.
- **"could not discern the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping … the noise was heard afar off" → "were not discerning the sound of the shout of gladness from the sound of the weeping … the sound was heard as far as far off"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵין … מַכִּירִים, a participle: "were not recognizing" (הִכִּיר "recognize"); קוֹל "voice", of a noise "sound" (one word); עַד־לְמֵרָחוֹק, literally "as far as to from afar".

### Notes
- **Seventh month** — Tishri. Begins with Feast of Trumpets, Yom Kippur, and Sukkot. Appropriate month for restoration.
- **Altar first** — even before temple foundation, daily sacrifice restored. Worship prioritized over building.
- **"Dread from the peoples of the lands"** (v3) — hostile neighbors (Samarians, Arabs). Worship a defiant act.
- **Second year, second month** (v8) — builds begin roughly 536 BC. Parallels Solomon's temple-start.
- **"The sons of Judah"** (v9) — MT *bnei Yehudah*; in this Levite list likely = "the sons of Hodaviah" (2:40; Neh 7:43 Hodevah), the two names being close in Hebrew. MT kept.
- **Phoenician cedar again** — repeats Solomon's pattern with Hiram. Cyrus's grant provides the Persian state-level funding.
- **Mixed tears and shouts** (v12-13) — older generation remembers First Temple's glory; younger rejoices at any restoration. Hag 2:3 echoes this ambivalence.
- **"His chesed is forever"** — standard liturgical refrain (Ps 136). Continuity with pre-exilic worship.
