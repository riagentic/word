## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v36 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And" · lists** (throughout) — each "and" stands where the Hebrew has וְ (v6–8, 10–14 begin with it; v2–5, 9 do not); the list has no verb, and "were" is supplied only in v3 and v33. Numbers are given in words in English order (v9 has "two hundred and eighteen", the Hebrew order "two hundred and eight-ten").
- **"These are now the chief of their fathers, and this is the genealogy of them that went up with me from Babylon, in the reign of Artaxerxes the king" → "And these are the heads of their fathers, and their enrollment by genealogy, who went up with me in the reign of Artaxerxes the king from Babylon"** (v1) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי אֲבֹתֵיהֶם; וְהִתְיַחְשָׂם, the infinitive of הִתְיַחֵשׂ with a suffix, "their enrollment by genealogy" (as v3 and 1 Chr 7:5); the Hebrew order of "in the reign" and "from Babylon" is kept.
- **"Of the sons of Shechaniah, of the sons of Pharosh; Zechariah" → "of the sons of Shecaniah; of the sons of Parosh, Zechariah"** (v2–3) — the Hebrew has a section break (ס) after "of the sons of Shecaniah": the words may close the line of Hattush (1 Chr 3:22 has Hattush among the sons of Shemaiah, son of Shecaniah) or stand alone. **"were reckoned by genealogy of the males an hundred and fifty"** — Hebrew הִתְיַחֵשׂ לִזְכָרִים, literally "were enrolled by genealogy, as to males".
- **"Of the sons of Shechaniah; the son of Jahaziel" → "of the sons of Shecaniah son of Jahaziel"** (v5) — so the Hebrew, מִבְּנֵי שְׁכַנְיָה בֶּן־יַחֲזִיאֵל: the head of the house is not named, or Shecaniah is the man and the clan name is lacking. The verse had "(Zattu)" after Shecaniah: the name is not in the Hebrew; it is the reading of the Greek (1 Esd 8:32, from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept. The same in v10: **"of the sons of Shelomith son of Josiphiah"** — the verse had "(Bani)": not in the Hebrew (the Greek reading, from memory).
- **"the last sons of Adonikam" → "of the sons of Adonikam, the last ones"** (v13) — Hebrew אַחֲרֹנִים "last (ones)": those who came last, or the youngest line of the house.
- **"Uthai, and Zabbud, and with them seventy" → "Uthai and Zaccur, and with him seventy"** (v14) — the written text (ketiv) is זבוד "Zabbud", the reading tradition (qere) וְזַכּוּר "and Zaccur"; the qere is translated. וְעִמּוֹ "and with him", singular, as in the verses before, though two men are named.
- **"abode we in tents three days: and I viewed the people" → "we camped there three days. And I gave heed among the people"** (v15) — Hebrew חָנָה "camp"; וָאָבִינָה בָעָם, of בִּין "discern, give heed", with בְּ "among": he looked them over. **"found there none of the sons of Levi" → "of the sons of Levi I did not find any there"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"chief men … men of understanding" → "heads … teachers"** (v16) — Hebrew רָאשִׁים "heads"; מְבִינִים, the hifil participle of בִּין "understand": "those who make (others) understand" (as Neh 8:7, 9) or "men of discernment" (1 Chr 15:22); "teachers" takes the first. Each name has its own לְ "for".
- **"I sent them with commandment unto Iddo the chief at the place Casiphia" → "I commanded them to Iddo the head at Casiphia the place"** (v17) — the written text (ketiv) is ואוצאה "and I brought them out (to)", the reading tradition (qere) וָאֲצַוֶּה "and I commanded them"; the qere is translated. **"I told them what they should say" → "I put in their mouth words to speak"** — Hebrew וָאָשִׂימָה בְּפִיהֶם דְּבָרִים. **"unto Iddo, and to his brethren the Nethinims" → "to Iddo, his brother, the Netinim"** — so the Hebrew, אִדּוֹ אָחִיו הַנְּתִינִים: "his brother" singular and no "and"; the sense may be "Iddo and his brothers, the Netinim". The ketiv has הנתונים "the given ones", the qere הַנְּתִינִים; the qere is translated. מְשָׁרְתִים "ministers".
- **"by the good hand of our God upon us they brought us a man of understanding" → "And they brought to us, according to the good hand of our Elohim upon us, a man of insight"** (v18) — Hebrew כְּיַד "according to the hand"; אִישׁ שֶׂכֶל "a man of insight". **"and Sherebiah"** — the Hebrew has וְ "and": "the man of insight" may be Sherebiah himself ("namely") or another man; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"with his sons and his brethren, eighteen" · "his brethren and their sons, twenty"** (v18, 19) — the Hebrew has no "with" in v19: "his brothers and their sons".
- **"appointed for the service of the Levites" → "had given for the service of the Levites"** (v20) — Hebrew נָתַן "give": a play on נְתִינִים, "given ones". **"all of them were expressed by name" → "all of them were designated by names"** — Hebrew נִקְּבוּ בְשֵׁמוֹת, as 1 Chr 16:41.
- **"that we might afflict ourselves … a right way … all our substance" → "to humble ourselves … a straight way … all our goods"** (v21) — Hebrew לְהִתְעַנּוֹת, hitpael of עָנָה, literally "to let ourselves be afflicted" (as Lev 16:29, 31: by fasting); דֶּרֶךְ יְשָׁרָה "a straight way" (level, without obstacle); רְכוּשׁ "goods".
- **"to require of the king a band of soldiers" → "to ask of the king a force"** (v22) — Hebrew שָׁאַל "ask"; חַיִל, one word, "force" (of an army). **"is upon all them for good that seek him; but his power and his wrath" → "is upon all who seek him, for good, and his strength and his anger"** — Hebrew לְטוֹבָה stands after "who seek him"; וְעֻזּוֹ וְאַפּוֹ, with "and", not "but": עֹז "strength", אַף "anger" (literally "nose").
- **"besought our God for this: and he was intreated of us" → "sought from our Elohim concerning this, and he let himself be pleaded with by us"** (v23) — Hebrew בִּקֵּשׁ "seek" (as v21, 22); וַיֵּעָתֵר, nifal of עָתַר "plead".
- **"I separated twelve of the chief of the priests, Sherebiah, Hashabiah" → "I set apart of the chiefs of the priests twelve: Sherebiah, Hashabiah"** (v24) — Hebrew הִבְדִּיל "separate, set apart"; שָׂרֵי הַכֹּהֲנִים "chiefs of the priests", as 2 Chr 36:14. Before Sherebiah stands לְ: it may mark the object (the two are named among the twelve), or mean "besides" — then the twelve priests are joined by the two Levites of v18–19 and their ten brothers. The Hebrew does not decide.
- **"the vessels, even the offering of the house of our God, which … his lords … had offered" → "the utensils, the contribution of the house of our Elohim, which … his princes … had lifted up"** (v25) — Hebrew כֵּלִים "utensils" (any made article); תְּרוּמָה "contribution", literally "what is lifted off", and its verb הֵרִים "lift up": the one root kept. שָׂרָיו "his princes". The written text has ואשקולה, the qere וָאֶשְׁקֳלָה "and I weighed out": the same meaning.
- **"silver vessels an hundred talents" → "utensils of silver, a hundred, by talents"** (v26) — Hebrew כְּלֵי־כֶסֶף מֵאָה לְכִכָּרִים, word for word; "worth a hundred talents", "a hundred utensils weighing … talents" (a number lost) and other readings have been proposed. Uncertain.
- **"twenty basons of gold, of a thousand drams; and two vessels of fine copper, precious as gold" → "twenty basins of gold, of a thousand darics; and two utensils of bronze, gleaming, good, desirable as gold"** (v27) — Hebrew כְּפֹרֵי "basins" (as 1 Chr 28:17; the kind of vessel is uncertain); אֲדַרְכֹנִים "darics", a Persian gold coin (as 1 Chr 29:7); נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze"; מֻצְהָב, found only here, "gleaming" (from a root "yellow, shining"; uncertain); טוֹבָה "good"; חֲמוּדֹת "desirable".
- **"Ye are holy unto the LORD; the vessels are holy also" → "You are holy to Yahweh, and the utensils are holy"** (v28) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ, the noun "holiness", twice as a predicate: "you are holiness to Yahweh". נְדָבָה "freewill offering".
- **"Watch ye, and keep them … the chief of the priests … and chief of the fathers of Israel" → "Be watchful and keep them … the chiefs of the priests … and the princes of the fathers of Israel"** (v29) — Hebrew שָׁקַד "be watchful", שָׁמַר "keep"; שָׂרֵי, the same word twice: "chiefs" of the priests, "princes" of the fathers (as 1 Chr 29:6); "in" before "the chambers" is supplied (the Hebrew sets the noun without a preposition).
- **"took" → "accepted"** (v30) — Hebrew קִבֵּל, as 1 Chr 21:11. מִשְׁקַל "weight". "Them" is supplied.
- **"Then we departed from the river of Ahava" → "And we set out from the river Ahava"** (v31) — Hebrew נָסַע. **"from the hand of the enemy, and of such as lay in wait by the way" → "from the palm of the enemy and of the one lying in ambush on the way"** — Hebrew מִכַּף "from the palm"; אוֹרֵב, a singular participle.
- **"abode" → "dwelt"** (v32) — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"by the hand of Meremoth" → "into the hand of Meremoth"** (v33) — Hebrew עַל יַד, literally "upon the hand of": into his keeping. "Was" and "were" are supplied.
- **"By number and by weight of every one: and all the weight was written" → "by number, by weight, for the whole; and all the weight was written"** (v34) — Hebrew בְּמִסְפָּר בְּמִשְׁקָל לַכֹּל.
- **"the children of those that had been carried away, which were come out of the captivity, offered" → "Those who came from the captivity, the sons of the exile, brought near"** (v35) — the Hebrew order: הַבָּאִים מֵהַשְּׁבִי בְּנֵי־הַגּוֹלָה; שְׁבִי "captivity"; גּוֹלָה "exile" (the exiles as a body); הִקְרִיב "bring near". **"twelve he goats for a sin offering: all this was a burnt offering" → "twelve he-goats of sin offering; the whole a burnt offering"** — Hebrew צְפִירֵי חַטָּאת; הַכֹּל, no verb. "Ninety-six", "seventy-seven": the Hebrew says "ninety and six", "seventy and seven".
- **"the king's commissions unto the king's lieutenants, and to the governors on this side the river: and they furthered the people" → "the laws of the king to the satraps of the king and the governors across the River, and they supported the people"** (v36) — Hebrew דָּתֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ, דָּת "law, decree" (a Persian word, as Ezra 7:12–26; Esth); אֲחַשְׁדַּרְפְּנִים "satraps"; פַּחוֹת "governors"; עֵבֶר הַנָּהָר "across the River" (the Euphrates, named from the east); נִשְּׂאוּ, piel of נָשָׂא, literally "lift up" (as Ezra 1:4).

### Notes
- **Ezra's caravan** — second major return, ca. 458 BC. About 1,500 men + families.
- **"Hattush of the sons of David"** (v2) — Davidic descendant; further exiles include Davidic line.
- **No Levites initially** (v15) — Levites reluctant to return (temple-task less lucrative?). Special recruitment at Casiphia (a Jewish teaching center in Babylonia? Proto-academy?).
- **Faith vs military escort** — Ezra refuses armed guard; trusts divine protection based on prior boast to king.
- **Massive treasure** — 650 silver talents + 100 gold talents + utensils. Real Persian-era figures suggesting the imperial favor.
- **Three days at Jerusalem** (v32) — as Nehemiah on his arrival (Neh 2:11).
- **Sacrificial numbers** — 12 bulls, 12 goats (tribal symbolism); 96 rams (8 × 12); 77 lambs. Pan-Israelite dedication.
- **"Satraps"** — authentic Persian provincial title (Hebrew *achashdarpan*, from Old Persian *xshaçapāvan*).
