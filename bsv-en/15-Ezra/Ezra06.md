# Ezra 6

[1] Then ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king made a decree, and they searched in the house of the books, where the treasures were laid up, there in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[2] And there was found in ⟦ecbatana|Achmeta|Ecbatana⟧, in the citadel that is in the province of ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧, a scroll, and thus was written in it: "A record.

[3] In the first year of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ the king, ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ the king made a decree: 'The house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: let the house be built, the place where they sacrifice sacrifices, and let its foundations be laid; its height sixty cubits, its breadth sixty cubits,

[4] three courses of great stone and one course of new timber; and let the expense be given from the house of the king.

[5] And also the vessels of the house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, of gold and silver, which ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ took out of the temple that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and brought to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, let them be returned, and let each go to the temple that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to its place; and you shall put it down in the house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧.'

[6] Now, Tattenai, governor across the River, ⟦shethar-bozenai|Shetar-Bozenai|Shethar-bozenai⟧, and their associates the investigators who are across the River, be far from there.

[7] Let alone the work of this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧; the governor of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and the elders of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ shall build this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ on its place.

[8] And by me a decree is made as to what you shall do with these elders of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ for the building of this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧: from the goods of the king, from the tribute across the River, with diligence the expense shall be given to these men, so that they are not made to cease.

[9] And whatever is needed, young bulls and rams and lambs for burnt offerings to the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens, wheat, salt, wine and oil, according to the word of the priests who are in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, let it be given to them day by day without negligence,

[10] that they may be bringing near soothing offerings to the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens, and praying for the life of the king and his sons.

[11] And by me a decree is made that any man who alters this word, a beam shall be pulled out of his house, and lifted up he shall be struck upon it; and his house shall be made a dunghill for this.

[12] And may the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ who has made his name abide there overthrow every king and people that stretches out its hand to alter, to destroy this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. I, ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧, have made a decree; let it be done with diligence."

[13] Then Tattenai, governor across the River, ⟦shethar-bozenai|Shetar-Bozenai|Shethar-bozenai⟧, and their associates, according to what ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king had sent, so they did with diligence.

[14] And the elders of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were building and prospering through the prophesying of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ the son of Iddo; and they built and finished by the decree of the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and by the decree of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ and ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ and ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧.

[15] And this house was completed by the third day of the month Adar, which was the sixth year of the reign of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king.

[16] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and the rest of the sons of the exile, made the dedication of this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ with joy.

[17] And they brought near for the dedication of this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ a hundred bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs; and as a sin offering for all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, twelve he-goats of the goats, according to the number of the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[18] And they set the priests in their sections and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ in their divisions, over the service of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, according to the writing of the book of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[19] And the sons of the exile made the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ on the fourteenth of the first month.

[20] For the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ had cleansed themselves as one; all of them were clean. And they slaughtered the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ for all the sons of the exile and for their brothers the priests and for themselves.

[21] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who had returned from the exile ate, and everyone who had separated himself to them from the uncleanness of the nations of the land, to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[22] And they made the Feast of ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ seven days with gladness, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had made them glad and had turned the heart of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ to them, to make their hands strong in the work of the house of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elah*"** (v3–18) — Aramaic אֱלָהּ *Elah* (v9, 10, 14) and its emphatic form אֱלָהָא *Elaha* (v3, 5, 7, 8, 12, 16–18), the Aramaic word for "God". **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v22 the first is with the article, בֵּית־הָאֱלֹהִים. **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v21, 22) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"made a decree"** (v1, 3, 8, 11, 12) — Aramaic שִׂים טְעֵם, literally "set a decree", as 4:19.
- **"the house of the rolls" → "the house of the books"** (v1) — Aramaic בֵּית סִפְרַיָּא. **"were laid up in Babylon" → "were laid up, there in Babylon"** — Aramaic תַּמָּה "there".
- **"Achmetha" → "Ecbatana"** (v2) — Aramaic אַחְמְתָא, the capital of Media (token). **"the palace" → "the citadel"** — Aramaic בִּירְתָא (as Hebrew בִּירָה, 1 Chr 29:1). **"the province of the Medes" → "the province of Media"** — Aramaic מָדַי. **"a roll" → "a scroll"** — Aramaic מְגִלָּה חֲדָה, literally "one scroll". **"therein was a record thus written" → "thus was written in it: A record"** — Aramaic דִּכְרוֹנָה, the heading of the text that follows (cf. דָּכְרָנַיָּא "records", 4:15).
- **"concerning the house of God at Jerusalem, Let the house be builded" → "The house of Elah in Jerusalem: let the house be built"** (v3) — the Aramaic names the house first, then the order. **"the place where they offered sacrifices" → "the place where they sacrifice sacrifices"** — Aramaic דָּבְחִין דִּבְחִין, participle (present) and its noun. **"let the foundations thereof be strongly laid" → "let its foundations be laid"** — Aramaic וְאֻשּׁוֹהִי מְסוֹבְלִין, literally "and its foundations being borne": uncertain ("kept", "raised", "laid firm" are given). **"threescore cubits" → "sixty cubits"**.
- **"With three rows of great stones, and a row of new timber" → "three courses of great stone and one course of new timber"** (v4) — Aramaic נִדְבָּכִין "courses"; אֶבֶן גְּלָל as 5:8; תְּלָתָא "three", חֲדַת "new"; the Aramaic has no "with". **"the expenses" → "the expense"** — Aramaic נִפְקְתָא (also v8).
- **"be restored, and brought again … every one to his place, and place them" → "let them be returned, and let each go … to its place; and you shall put it down"** (v5) — Aramaic יַהֲתִיבוּן "let them return", וִיהָךְ "and let it go" (singular: each vessel), וְתַחֵת "and you shall put down" (the verb of 5:15; the form may also be "and it shall be put down").
- **"your companions" → "their associates"** (v6, 13) — Aramaic כְּנָוָתְהוֹן "their associates". **"the Apharsachites" → "the investigators"** (v6) — as 5:6. **"be ye far from thence"** — Aramaic רַחִיקִין הֱווֹ מִן־תַּמָּה.
- **"let the governor of the Jews … build" → "the governor of the Jews … shall build"** (v7) — Aramaic יִבְנוֹן, imperfect.
- **"what ye shall do to the elders of these Jews" → "as to what you shall do with these elders of the Jews"** (v8) — Aramaic לְמָא דִּי־תַעַבְדוּן עִם־שָׂבֵי יְהוּדָיֵא אִלֵּךְ. **"of the king's goods, even of the tribute beyond the river" → "from the goods of the king, from the tribute across the River"** — Aramaic מִנִּכְסֵי מַלְכָּא דִּי מִדַּת עֲבַר נַהֲרָה. **"forthwith" → "with diligence"** — אָסְפַּרְנָא, as 5:8. **"that they be not hindered" → "so that they are not made to cease"** — Aramaic דִּי־לָא לְבַטָּלָא, the verb of 4:21; 5:5.
- **"that which they have need of" → "whatever is needed"** (v9) — Aramaic חַשְׁחָן. **"young bullocks"** — Aramaic בְנֵי תוֹרִין, literally "sons of bulls". **"for the burnt offerings of the God of heaven" → "for burnt offerings to the Elah of the heavens"** — Aramaic לַעֲלָוָן לֶאֱלָהּ שְׁמַיָּא. **"according to the appointment of" → "according to the word of"** — Aramaic כְּמֵאמַר. **"without fail" → "without negligence"** — Aramaic דִּי־לָא שָׁלוּ, the noun of 4:22.
- **"offer sacrifices of sweet savours" → "be bringing near soothing offerings"** (v10) — Aramaic מְהַקְרְבִין נִיחוֹחִין: the causative of קְרֵב "come near" (as Hebrew הִקְרִיב, also v17; 7:17), and נִיחוֹחִין "soothing (things)", the word of Hebrew רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ "soothing smell"; "offerings" is supplied.
- **"this word"** (v11) — Aramaic פִּתְגָמָא, as 4:17. **"let timber be pulled down from his house, and being set up, let him be hanged thereon" → "a beam shall be pulled out of his house, and lifted up he shall be struck upon it"** — Aramaic יִתְנְסַח אָע מִן־בַּיְתֵהּ וּזְקִיף יִתְמְחֵא עֲלֹהִי: מְחָא is "strike"; impaled, or fastened, on the beam. **"a dunghill"** — Aramaic נְוָלוּ, uncertain ("refuse heap", "ruin").
- **"hath caused his name to dwell" → "has made his name abide"** (v12) — Aramaic שַׁכִּן, the verb of Hebrew שָׁכַן "abide" (Deut 12:11). **"destroy" → "overthrow"** — Aramaic יְמַגַּר, a wish. **"put to their hand" → "stretches out its hand"** — Aramaic יִשְׁלַח יְדֵהּ. **"to alter and to destroy" → "to alter, to destroy"** — no "and"; לְחַבָּלָה "to destroy" is of the root of חֲבָל "harm", 4:22. **"with speed" → "with diligence"** (v12, 13) — אָסְפַּרְנָא.
- **"according to that which … so they did speedily" → "according to what … so they did with diligence"** (v13) — Aramaic לָקֳבֵל דִּי … כְּנֵמָא אָסְפַּרְנָא עֲבַדוּ.
- **"builded, and they prospered" → "were building and prospering"** (v14) — Aramaic participles. **"Haggai the prophet"** — qere נְבִיָּא. **"finished it"** — Aramaic שַׁכְלִלוּ, no object. **"according to the commandment of … according to the commandment of" → "by the decree of … by the decree of"** — Aramaic טַעַם / טְעֵם, the one word of God and of the kings.
- **"was finished on the third day" → "was completed by the third day"** (v15) — Aramaic שֵׁיצִיא "was completed" (another verb than שַׁכְלֵל "finish") and עַד "until". **"the month Adar"** — Aramaic יְרַח, the word for "month". **"which was in the sixth year" → "which was the sixth year"** — Aramaic דִּי־הִיא שְׁנַת־שֵׁת.
- **"the children of Israel … the children of the captivity" → "the sons of Israel … the sons of the exile"** (v16, 19, 20, 21) — Aramaic בְּנֵי־גָלוּתָא, Hebrew בְּנֵי הַגּוֹלָה. **"kept the dedication" → "made the dedication"** (v16) — Aramaic עֲבַדוּ חֲנֻכַּת. **"with joy"** — Aramaic בְּחֶדְוָה.
- **"offered" → "brought near"** (v17) — Aramaic הַקְרִבוּ, as v10. **"bullocks" → "bulls"** — Aramaic תּוֹרִין. **"twelve he goats" → "twelve he-goats of the goats"** — Aramaic צְפִירֵי עִזִּין, as Hebrew צְפִיר עִזִּים (2 Chr 29:21). **"for a sin offering" → "as a sin offering"** — qere לְחַטָּאָה (ketiv לחטיא).
- **"in their divisions … in their courses" → "in their sections … in their divisions"** (v18) — Aramaic פְּלֻגָּה and מַחְלְקָה, as Hebrew פְּלֻגּוֹת and מַחְלְקוֹת (2 Chr 35:4–5). **"for the service of God, which is at Jerusalem" → "over the service of Elah that is in Jerusalem"** — Aramaic עַל־עֲבִידַת אֱלָהָא; עֲבִידָה is "work" in 4:24; 5:8; 6:7, here the service of the house. **"as it is written in the book of Moses" → "according to the writing of the book of Moses"** — Aramaic כִּכְתָב סְפַר מֹשֶׁה.
- **"kept the passover" → "made the Pesach"** (v19) — Hebrew עָשָׂה פֶּסַח. **"upon the fourteenth day" → "on the fourteenth"** — no "day" in the Hebrew.
- **"were purified together" → "had cleansed themselves as one"** (v20) — Hebrew הִטַּהֲרוּ כְּאֶחָד. **"pure" → "clean"** — Hebrew טְהוֹרִים. **"and killed the passover" → "And they slaughtered the Pesach"** — Hebrew שָׁחַט; no "lamb" added.
- **"which were come again out of captivity" → "who had returned from the exile"** (v21) — Hebrew הַשָּׁבִים מֵהַגּוֹלָה. **"such as had separated themselves unto them from the filthiness of the heathen of the land" → "everyone who had separated himself to them from the uncleanness of the nations of the land"** — Hebrew וְכֹל הַנִּבְדָּל … אֲלֵהֶם, singular; טֻמְאָה "uncleanness", גּוֹיִם "nations". **"did eat" → "ate"** — the verb stands at the head of the verse.
- **"kept the feast" → "made the Feast"** (v22) — Hebrew עָשָׂה חַג. **"with joy … joyful" → "with gladness … made them glad"** — Hebrew שִׂמְחָה and שִׂמַּח, one root. **"unto them" → "to them"** — Hebrew עֲלֵיהֶם. **"to strengthen their hands" → "to make their hands strong"** — Hebrew חִזֵּק. **"the king of Assyria"** — Hebrew מֶלֶךְ־אַשּׁוּר; the Persian king, ruler of the lands that Assyria once held.

### Notes

- **Language** — v1–18 are Aramaic: the Aramaic that began at 4:8 ends with v18. At v19 the text changes back to Hebrew.
- **The record and the letter** (v2–12) — v3–5 give Cyrus's decree as the record has it; v6–12 are Darius's own order to Tattenai, which follows without a mark in the text.
- **Temple completed** — Adar of Darius's sixth year (516/515 BC).
- **Twelve he-goats** (v17) — one for each tribe of Israel.
- **The separated** (v21) — those of the land who separated themselves from its uncleanness and joined the returned exiles.
