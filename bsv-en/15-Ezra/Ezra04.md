# Ezra 4

[1] And the adversaries of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ heard that the sons of the exile were building a temple for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And they drew near to ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ and to the heads of the fathers, and said to them, "Let us build with you, for like you we seek your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and to him we have been sacrificing since the days of ⟦esarhaddon|Esar-Chaddon|Esarhaddon⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, who brought us up here."

[3] And ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ and ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ and the rest of the heads of the fathers of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to them, "It is not for you and for us to build a house for our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; for we together will build for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, as King ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧, the king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧, has commanded us."

[4] And the people of the land were weakening the hands of the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and terrifying them from building,

[5] and hiring counselors against them to frustrate their counsel, all the days of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧ and until the reign of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧.

[6] And in the reign of ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, in the beginning of his reign, they wrote an accusation against those dwelling in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[7] And in the days of ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧, Bishlam, ⟦mithredath|Mitredat|Mithredath⟧, ⟦tabeel|Taveel|Tabeel⟧ and the rest of his associates wrote to ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧; and the writing of the document was written in ⟦aramaic|Aramit|Aramaic⟧ and translated: ⟦aramaic|Aramit|Aramaic⟧.

[8] ⟦rehum|Rechum|Rehum⟧ the master of decree and Shimshai the scribe wrote a letter against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ the king, as follows:

[9] Then ⟦rehum|Rechum|Rehum⟧ the master of decree and Shimshai the scribe and the rest of their associates, the judges and the envoys, the officials, the ⟦persians|Parsi|Persians⟧, the men of Erech, the ⟦babylonians|Bavlaye|Babylonians⟧, the men of ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧, the Dehaites, the ⟦elamites|Elmaye|Elamites⟧,

[10] and the rest of the nations whom the great and honored Osnappar carried into exile and made to dwell in the city of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and the rest across the River, and now.

[11] This is the copy of the letter that they sent to him:

"To ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ the king. Your servants, the men across the River, and now.

[12] Be it known to the king that the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who came up from you to us have come to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; the rebellious and evil city they are building, and the walls they have finished, and the foundations they are repairing.

[13] Now be it known to the king that if this city is built and the walls are finished, tribute, tax and toll they will not give, and in the end it will do damage to the kings.

[14] Now, because we have eaten the salt of the palace, and it is not fitting for us to see the dishonor of the king, for this we have sent and made it known to the king,

[15] that search may be made in the book of the records of your fathers; and you will find in the book of the records and know that this city is a rebellious city, and doing damage to kings and provinces, and that they have made sedition in its midst from the days of old; for this this city was laid waste.

[16] We make known to the king that if this city is built and its walls are finished, because of this you will have no share across the River."

[17] The king sent word:

"To ⟦rehum|Rechum|Rehum⟧ the master of decree and Shimshai the scribe and the rest of their associates who dwell in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and the rest across the River: Peace, and now.

[18] The document that you sent to us has been read distinctly before me.

[19] And by me a decree was made, and they searched and found that this city from the days of old has lifted itself up against kings, and that rebellion and sedition have been made in it.

[20] And mighty kings have been over ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and ruling in all that is across the River; and tribute, tax and toll were given to them.

[21] Now make a decree to make these men cease, and this city shall not be built until a decree is made by me.

[22] And be careful of committing negligence about this. Why should the harm grow to the damage of kings?"

[23] Then, as soon as the copy of the document of ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ the king was read before ⟦rehum|Rechum|Rehum⟧ and Shimshai the scribe and their associates, they went in haste to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and made them cease by arm and force.

[24] Then the work of the house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ ceased; and it was at a standstill until the second year of the reign of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God" → "*Elah*"** (v24) — Aramaic אֱלָהָא *Elaha*, the emphatic ("the") form of אֱלָהּ *Elah*, the Aramaic word for "God"; it gets its own name, not *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (v1–7) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the children of the captivity" → "the sons of the exile"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הַגּוֹלָה. **"builded the temple" → "were building a temple"** — participle, no article.
- **"Then they came" → "And they drew near"** (v2) — Hebrew נָגַשׁ. **"the chief of the fathers" → "the heads of the fathers"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת.
- **"and we do sacrifice unto him" → "and to him we have been sacrificing"** (v2) — the qere וְלוֹ "and to him"; the written text (ketiv) has וְלֹא "and not": "and we have not been sacrificing since the days of Esarhaddon". The qere is translated; the ketiv gives the opposite claim.
- **"Assur" → "Assyria"** (v2) — Hebrew אַשּׁוּר.
- **"Ye have nothing to do with us to build" → "It is not for you and for us to build"** (v3) — Hebrew לֹא־לָכֶם וָלָנוּ לִבְנוֹת, word for word. **"but we ourselves together" → "for we together"** — Hebrew כִּי אֲנַחְנוּ יַחַד.
- **"weakened … and troubled them in building" → "were weakening … and terrifying them from building"** (v4) — וַיְהִי with participles: an ongoing action. The qere וּמְבַהֲלִים "terrifying" (ketiv ומבלהים, the same letters in another order); לִבְנוֹת is literally "to build".
- **"hired" → "hiring"** (v5) — participle. **"to frustrate their purpose" → "to frustrate their counsel"** — Hebrew לְהָפֵר עֲצָתָם: הֵפֵר is "break, make void"; עֵצָה "counsel", the noun of יוֹעֲצִים "counselors". **"even until" → "and until"** — Hebrew וְעַד.
- **"wrote they unto him an accusation" → "they wrote an accusation"** (v6) — no "unto him" in the Hebrew; שִׂטְנָה "accusation" is from the root of שָׂטָן "adversary". **"the inhabitants of" → "those dwelling in"** — Hebrew יֹשְׁבֵי, participle of יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"Bishlam" kept** (v7) — Hebrew בִּשְׁלָם; the accents take it as a name. It can also be read "in peace" / "with the consent of" (so the Septuagint, cited from memory). The verb כָּתַב "wrote" is singular. **"their companions" → "his associates"** — qere כְּנָוֺתָיו "his associates" (ketiv כנותו); כְּנָוָת is the Aramaic word for colleagues, also v9, 17, 23.
- **"the writing of the letter was written in the Syrian tongue, and interpreted in the Syrian tongue" → "the writing of the document was written in Aramaic and translated: Aramaic"** (v7) — Hebrew וּכְתָב הַנִּשְׁתְּוָן כָּתוּב אֲרָמִית וּמְתֻרְגָּם אֲרָמִית. נִשְׁתְּוָן is a Persian loanword, "document, written order" (also v18, 23; 5:5; 7:11), kept apart from אִגְּרָה "letter" (v8, 11). The second "Aramaic" may also be a note to the reader that the text now goes on in Aramaic, as it does from v8.
- **"chancellor" → "master of decree"** (v8, 9, 17) — Aramaic בְּעֵל־טְעֵם, literally "owner of decree": an official title; what office it named is not certain. טְעֵם is "decree" throughout these chapters.
- **"in this sort" → "as follows"** (v8) — Aramaic כְּנֵמָא "thus". **"a letter"** — Aramaic אִגְּרָה חֲדָה, literally "one letter".
- **"Then wrote Rehum" → "Then Rehum …"** (v9–10) — Aramaic אֱדַיִן "then", with no verb: v9–10 name the senders and stand as the heading of the letter.
- **"the Dinaites, the Apharsathchites, the Tarpelites, the Apharsites, the Archevites, the Babylonians, the Susanchites, the Dehavites, and the Elamites" → "the judges and the envoys, the officials, the Persians, the men of Erech, the Babylonians, the men of Susa, the Dehaites, the Elamites"** (v9) — Aramaic nouns in ‑āyē, which name either peoples or office holders. דִּינָיֵא is taken as "judges" (from דִּין "judge"), אֲפַרְסַתְכָיֵא as "envoys" and טַרְפְּלָיֵא as "officials" (Persian loanwords; both uncertain), אֲפָרְסָיֵא as "Persians" (uncertain: perhaps also a title). אַרְכְּוָיֵא (qere) "the men of Erech", שׁוּשַׁנְכָיֵא "the men of Susa". **"the Dehavites" → "the Dehaites"** — the qere דֶּהָיֵא, a people nowhere else known; the written text (ketiv) דהוא reads "that is" (then "that is, the Elamites"), which many follow.
- **"Asnappar brought over, and set in the cities of Samaria" → "Osnappar carried into exile and made to dwell in the city of Samaria"** (v10) — Aramaic הַגְלִי "carried into exile" (as Hebrew הִגְלָה) and הוֹתֵב "made dwell" (causative of יְתִב "dwell"); בְּקִרְיָה is singular, "city". Osnappar is commonly taken as Ashurbanipal. **"great and noble" → "great and honored"** — Aramaic יַקִּיר "honored, precious". **"nations"** — Aramaic אֻמַּיָּא.
- **"on this side the river" → "across the River"** (v10, 11, 16, 17, 20) — Aramaic עֲבַר־נַהֲרָה, the land west of the Euphrates as named from the east; no word "province" in the text.
- **"and at such a time" → "and now"** (v10, 11, 17) — Aramaic וּכְעֶנֶת / וּכְעֶת, the letter formula "and now" that leads into the matter.
- **"that they sent unto him, even unto Artaxerxes the king; Thy servants" → "that they sent to him: To Artaxerxes the king. Your servants"** (v11) — the address of the letter: Aramaic עַל־אַרְתַּחְשַׁשְׂתְּא מַלְכָּא "to Artaxerxes the king", then עַבְדָיךְ אֱנָשׁ עֲבַר־נַהֲרָה "your servants, the men of across the River". The Aramaic does not mark where the copy begins.
- **"are come unto Jerusalem, building the rebellious and the bad city, and have set up the walls thereof, and joined the foundations" → "have come to Jerusalem; the rebellious and evil city they are building, and the walls they have finished, and the foundations they are repairing"** (v12) — the Aramaic order; qere וּבִישְׁתָּא "and evil", וְשׁוּרַיָּא שַׁכְלִילוּ "and the walls they have finished" (the ketiv spells the same words otherwise). יַחִיטוּ "repair" is uncertain ("join", "dig out" are also given). שׁוּר is the wall of a city.
- **"set up again" → "are finished"** (v13, 16) — Aramaic שַׁכְלֵל "finish, complete". **"toll, tribute, and custom" → "tribute, tax and toll"** (v13, 20) — Aramaic מִנְדָּה בְלוֹ וַהֲלָךְ, three kinds of payment; how they differ is not certain. **"and so thou shalt endamage the revenue of the kings" → "and in the end it will do damage to the kings"** (v13) — Aramaic וְאַפְּתֹם מַלְכִים תְּהַנְזִק: אַפְּתֹם is a Persian loanword, "in the end" or "revenue" ("the revenue of the kings will suffer damage"); the sense is uncertain.
- **"we have maintenance from the king's palace" → "we have eaten the salt of the palace"** (v14) — Aramaic מְלַח הֵיכְלָא מְלַחְנָא, literally "the salt of the palace we have salted": they are bound to the king by his salt. **"the king's dishonour" → "the dishonor of the king"** — Aramaic עַרְוַת מַלְכָּא, literally "the nakedness of the king" (as Hebrew עֶרְוָה). **"therefore" → "for this"** (v14, 15) — Aramaic עַל־דְּנָה. **"certified" → "made it known"** (v14, 16) — Aramaic הוֹדַע "make known".
- **"hurtful unto kings" → "doing damage to kings"** (v15) — Aramaic מְהַנְזְקַת, the verb of v13. **"moved sedition within the same of old time" → "made sedition in its midst from the days of old"** (v15, 19) — Aramaic מִן־יוֹמָת עָלְמָא, literally "from the days of the age". **"destroyed" → "laid waste"** — Aramaic הָחָרְבַת.
- **"by this means" → "because of this"** (v16) — Aramaic לָקֳבֵל דְּנָה. **"portion" → "share"** — Aramaic חֲלָק (Hebrew חֵלֶק).
- **"sent the king an answer" → "The king sent word"** (v17) — Aramaic פִּתְגָמָא, a Persian loanword, "word, message" (also 5:7, 11; 6:11). **"Peace"** — Aramaic שְׁלָם, the Aramaic form of Hebrew *shalom*, here the greeting of a letter; plain "peace".
- **"plainly read" → "read distinctly"** (v18) — Aramaic מְפָרַשׁ, "made distinct": read clearly, or translated (as 4:7).
- **"I commanded" → "by me a decree was made"** (v19) — Aramaic וּמִנִּי שִׂים טְעֵם, literally "and from me a decree was set" (so v21; 6:1, 3, 8, 11, 12). **"hath made insurrection" → "has lifted itself up"** — Aramaic מִתְנַשְּׂאָה.
- **"which have ruled over all countries beyond the river" → "and ruling in all that is across the River"** (v20) — Aramaic וְשַׁלִּיטִין בְּכֹל עֲבַר נַהֲרָה; no "countries".
- **"Give ye now commandment" → "Now make a decree"** (v21) — Aramaic שִׂימוּ טְעֵם. **"to cause these men to cease" → "to make these men cease"** — Aramaic לְבַטָּלָא, causative of בְּטֵל "cease" (v23, 24; 5:5; 6:8). **"until another commandment shall be given from me" → "until a decree is made by me"** — no "another".
- **"Take heed now that ye fail not to do this" → "And be careful of committing negligence about this"** (v22) — Aramaic וּזְהִירִין הֱווֹ שָׁלוּ לְמֶעְבַּד עַל־דְּנָה. **"damage … the hurt" → "the harm … the damage"** — Aramaic חֲבָלָא "harm" and הַנְזָקָה "damage" (the verb of v13, 15).
- **"Now when" → "Then, as soon as"** (v23) — Aramaic אֱדַיִן מִן־דִּי. **"the copy of king Artaxerxes' letter" → "the copy of the document of Artaxerxes the king"** — נִשְׁתְּוָן, as v7, 18. **"went up in haste"** — Aramaic אֲזַלוּ "went". **"by force and power" → "by arm and force"** — Aramaic בְּאֶדְרָע וְחָיִל.
- **"So it ceased" → "and it was at a standstill"** (v24) — Aramaic וַהֲוָת בָּטְלָא, literally "and it was ceasing": the participle of the verb of the first clause.

### Notes

- **Language** — v1–7 are Hebrew. At v8 the text changes to Aramaic, and the Aramaic runs on to 6:18 (and again 7:12–26). v7 says that the document was written in Aramaic; from v8 the book quotes the letters, and goes on telling in the same language.
- **Chronological jump** (v6–23) — Ahasuerus (Xerxes I, 486–465) and Artaxerxes I (465–424) are later than Darius I (522–486). These letters oppose a later rebuilding of the city and its walls, and are placed here to show the opposition as a whole. v24 returns to the reign of Darius and to the house of Elah.
- **"Adversaries"** (v1) — the people settled in the land by the Assyrian kings (2 Kgs 17:24–41); they claim to have sacrificed to Israel's Elohim since Esarhaddon.
- **"People of the land"** (v4) — here those living in the land who were not of the exile.
- **Salt** (v14) — eating a ruler's salt binds the eater to him (cf. "a covenant of salt", Num 18:19; 2 Chr 13:5).
