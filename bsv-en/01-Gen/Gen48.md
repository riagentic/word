# Genesis 48

[1] And it came to pass after these things that one said to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, "Behold, your father is sick." And he took his two sons with him, ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[2] And one told ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ and said, "Behold, your son ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ has come to you." And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ strengthened himself and sat upon the bed.

[3] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ said to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, "⟦el-shaddai|El Shaddai|God Almighty⟧ appeared to me at Luz in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, and blessed me,

[4] and said to me, 'Behold, I am making you fruitful, and I will multiply you, and I will make you an assembly of peoples; and I will give this land to your seed after you, an everlasting possession.'

[5] And now, your two sons who were born to you in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ before I came to you to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, they are mine; ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ shall be mine, as ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧.

[7] And as for me, when I came from Paddan, Rachel died beside me in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, on the way, while there was still a stretch of land to come to ⟦ephrath|Efrat|Ephrath⟧; and I buried her there on the way to ⟦ephrath|Efrat|Ephrath⟧, that is, ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧."

[10] And the eyes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were heavy from old age; he could not see. And he brought them near to him, and he kissed them and embraced them.

[11] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, "To see your face I had not thought; and behold, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has made me see your seed also."

[12] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ brought them out from beside his knees, and bowed down with his face to the earth.

[13] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ took the two of them, ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ in his right hand on the left of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ in his left hand on the right of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and brought them near to him.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"one told Joseph" → "one said to Joseph"** (v1) and **"one told Jacob"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר, וַיַּגֵּד, "and he said", "and he told", with no subject named: someone. "Sick": חֹלֶה.
- **"sat upon the bed" kept** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֵּשֶׁב עַל־הַמִּטָּה; הַמִּטָּה "the bed", as in 47:31.
- **"God Almighty" → "El Shaddai"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵל שַׁדַּי; the meaning of שַׁדַּי is not certain. "Appeared": נִרְאָה, as 12:7.
- **"I will make thee fruitful" → "I am making you fruitful"** (v4) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מַפְרְךָ, "behold me making you fruitful", a participle, followed by "and I will multiply you".
- **"a multitude of people" → "an assembly of peoples"** (v4) — Hebrew קְהַל עַמִּים; קָהָל "assembly", עַמִּים plural.
- **"an everlasting possession" kept** (v4) — Hebrew אֲחֻזַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a possession of the age".
- **"before I came" kept** (v5) — Hebrew עַד־בֹּאִי, literally "until my coming".
- **"thy issue, which thou begettest" → "your offspring that you have begotten"** (v6) — Hebrew וּמוֹלַדְתְּךָ אֲשֶׁר־הוֹלַדְתָּ, a noun and a verb of the one root "beget"; the verb is a perfect. מוֹלֶדֶת is elsewhere "kindred"; here it is those begotten.
- **"Rachel died by me" → "Rachel died beside me"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵתָה עָלַי רָחֵל, literally "died upon me": beside him, or to his sorrow.
- **"a little way" → "a stretch of land"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּבְרַת־אֶרֶץ, a measure of distance (also 35:16; 2 Kgs 5:19); how far is not known.
- **"Bring them, I pray thee, unto me" → "Take them to me, please"** (v9) — Hebrew קָחֶם־נָא אֵלַי, לָקַח "take"; "and let me bless them": וַאֲבָרֲכֵם.
- **"dim" → "heavy"** (v10) — Hebrew כָּבְדוּ, "were heavy".
- **"shewed me" → "has made me see"** (v11) — Hebrew הֶרְאָה אֹתִי, the causative of "see", the verb of the first half of the verse.
- **"bowed himself with his face to the earth" → "bowed down with his face to the earth"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁתַּחוּ לְאַפָּיו אָרְצָה, literally "to his nostrils, to the earth". "From beside his knees": מֵעִם בִּרְכָּיו, the knees of Israel.
- **"guiding his hands wittingly" → "he crossed his hands"** (v14) — Hebrew שִׂכֵּל אֶת־יָדָיו. The meaning is uncertain: the verb in this form stands only here; it is taken either as "laid crosswise" or, from שָׂכַל "be prudent", as "he laid his hands with understanding". "For Manasseh was the firstborn": כִּי, "for", can also be taken "although".
- **"The God which fed me all my life long" → "the *Elohim* who has shepherded me ever since I was"** (v15) — Hebrew הָרֹעֶה אֹתִי מֵעוֹדִי, the participle of רָעָה "shepherd", and literally "from my still-being".
- **"The Angel which redeemed me" → "the angel who has redeemed me"** (v16) — Hebrew הַמַּלְאָךְ הַגֹּאֵל אֹתִי. מַלְאָךְ is "messenger"; גֹּאֵל is a participle of גָּאַל "redeem" (the one redeeming me), parallel to "the *Elohim* shepherding me".
- **"bless the lads" → "may he bless the boys"** (v15–16) — Hebrew יְבָרֵךְ, a wish in the singular: "the *Elohim* … the *Elohim* … the angel …" are followed by one verb, "may he bless".
- **"let my name be named on them" → "let my name be called in them"** (v16) — Hebrew וְיִקָּרֵא בָהֶם שְׁמִי.
- **"let them grow into a multitude" → "let them teem into a multitude"** (v16) — Hebrew וְיִדְגּוּ לָרֹב, a verb found only here, of one root with דָּג "fish": multiply as fish do. "In the midst of the land": הָאָרֶץ, "the land" or "the earth".
- **"it displeased him" → "it was bad in his eyes"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיֵּרַע בְּעֵינָיו.
- **"a multitude of nations" → "the fullness of the nations"** (v19) — Hebrew מְלֹא־הַגּוֹיִם; מְלֹא is "fullness, what fills"; another word than "multitude" in v16.
- **"In thee shall Israel bless" → "By you Israel shall bless"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּךָ יְבָרֵךְ יִשְׂרָאֵל; "you" is singular here and in "make you".
- **"Behold, I die" → "Behold, I am dying"** (v21) — Hebrew הִנֵּה אָנֹכִי מֵת, a participle. "With you … bring you back … your fathers" are plural.
- **"one portion above thy brethren" → "one shoulder above your brothers"** (v22) — Hebrew שְׁכֶם אַחַד עַל־אַחֶיךָ. שְׁכֶם is "shoulder", and the name of the city Shechem; what is meant (a ridge of land, a share, Shechem itself) is uncertain. "I have given": נָתַתִּי, a perfect.

### Notes

- **v5–6** — Ephraim and Manasseh are counted as sons of Jacob, as Reuben and Simeon are.
- **v15** — "he blessed Joseph", and the words of the blessing are about the boys.
- **v22** — Genesis does not tell elsewhere of Jacob taking land from the Amorite with sword and bow; Joseph's bones are buried at Shechem (Josh 24:32).
