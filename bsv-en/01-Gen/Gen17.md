# Genesis 17

[1] And ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ was ninety-nine years old, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ and said to him, "I am ⟦el-shaddai|El Shaddai|God Almighty⟧. Walk before me and be blameless,

[2] and I will give my covenant between me and you, and I will multiply you very exceedingly."

[3] And ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ fell on his face, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke with him, saying,

[4] "As for me, behold, my covenant is with you, and you shall become a father of a multitude of nations.

[5] And your name shall no longer be called ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧, but your name shall be ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, for a father of a multitude of nations I have made you.

[6] And I will make you very exceedingly fruitful, and I will make you into nations, and kings shall come out from you.

[7] And I will establish my covenant between me and you and your seed after you, for their generations, for an everlasting covenant, to be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you and to your seed after you.

[8] And I will give to you and to your seed after you the land of your sojournings, all the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, for an everlasting possession; and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them."

[9] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, "And you, my covenant you shall keep, you and your seed after you, for their generations.

[10] This is my covenant, which you shall keep, between me and you and your seed after you: every male among you shall be circumcised.

[11] And you shall be circumcised in the flesh of your foreskin, and it shall be for a sign of a covenant between me and you.

[12] And at eight days old every male among you shall be circumcised, for your generations — he who is born in the house, and he who is bought with money from any foreigner, who is not of your seed.

[13] He who is born in your house and he who is bought with your money shall surely be circumcised; and my covenant shall be in your flesh for an everlasting covenant.

[14] And an uncircumcised male, who is not circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin — that soul shall be cut off from its peoples; my covenant he has broken."

[15] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, "Sarai your wife — you shall not call her name Sarai, but Sarah is her name.

[16] And I will bless her, and also I have given you a son from her; and I will bless her, and she shall become nations; kings of peoples shall be from her."

[17] And ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ fell on his face and laughed, and said in his heart, "Shall a child be born to one who is a hundred years old? Or shall Sarah, who is ninety years old, bear?"

[18] And ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ said to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "Oh that ⟦ishmael|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ might live before you!"

[19] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Truly, Sarah your wife is to bear you a son, and you shall call his name ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧; and I will establish my covenant with him for an everlasting covenant, for his seed after him.

[20] And as for ⟦ishmael|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧, I have heard you: behold, I have blessed him, and I will make him fruitful, and I will multiply him very exceedingly. Twelve chieftains he shall beget, and I will make him a great nation.

[21] But my covenant I will establish with ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧, whom Sarah shall bear to you at this appointed time in the next year."

[22] And he finished speaking with him, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ went up from ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[23] And ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ took ⟦ishmael|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ his son and all those born in his house and all those bought with his money, every male among the men of ⟦abraham|Avraham's|Abraham's⟧ house, and he circumcised the flesh of their foreskin on that very day, as ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had spoken with him.

[24] And ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ was ninety-nine years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

[25] And ⟦ishmael|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ his son was thirteen years old when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

[26] On that very day ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ was circumcised, and ⟦ishmael|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ his son;

[27] and all the men of his house, he who was born in the house and he who was bought with money from a foreigner, were circumcised with him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v18 the Hebrew has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים "the Elohim".
- **"And" kept** (throughout) — Hebrew וַ / וְ at the head of each clause.
- **"ninety years old and nine" → "ninety-nine years old"** (v1, 24, 25) — Hebrew בֶּן־תִּשְׁעִים שָׁנָה וְתֵשַׁע שָׁנִים, literally "a son of ninety years and nine years", the Hebrew way of giving an age; v24 has the shorter form "ninety and nine years". The number is given in ordinary English form.
- **"Almighty God" → "El Shaddai"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל שַׁדַּי *El Shaddai*, the name kept. The meaning of *Shaddai* is not certain: possibly from *shadad* ("overpower") → "Almighty"; or from *shad* ("breast"; see 49:25's *shadayim* "breasts" parallel); or from Akkadian *shadû* ("mountain") → "Mountain One." Traditional English "Almighty" via LXX παντοκράτωρ / Vulgate *omnipotens* (both from memory).
- **"perfect" → "blameless"** (v1) — Hebrew תָּמִים *tamim* = whole / complete / of integrity. As in 6:9. "Walk" is the hitpael הִתְהַלֵּךְ, to walk about, of a way of life (as 5:22).
- **"I will make my covenant" → "I will give my covenant"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאֶתְּנָה בְרִיתִי, the verb נָתַן "give / set", in the cohortative after the commands of v1: "that I may give". The same verb is "made you" in v5, 6, 20 (literally "I have given you as a father …", "I will give you to nations", "I will give him to a great nation").
- **"exceedingly" → "very exceedingly"** (v2, 6, 20) — Hebrew בִּמְאֹד מְאֹד, the word "very" twice.
- **"father of many nations" → "a father of a multitude of nations"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew אַב־הֲמוֹן גּוֹיִם *av-hamon goyim*. *Hamon* = multitude / tumultuous throng — not simply "many." The new name Abraham (*Avraham*) is explained as *av-hamon* (father of a multitude). The explanation is a wordplay on the sound; the text does not give a derivation.
- **"I have made thee" kept** (v5) — Hebrew נְתַתִּיךָ, a perfect: the thing is spoken of as already done. So also "I have given you a son from her" (v16, נָתַתִּי) and "I have blessed him" (v20).
- **"I will make nations of thee" → "I will make you into nations"** (v6) — Hebrew וּנְתַתִּיךָ לְגוֹיִם. "Come out from you": יֵצֵאוּ.
- **"seed" kept** (v7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 19) — Hebrew זֶרַע *zera*, a singular collective.
- **"establish"** (v7, 19, 21) — Hebrew הֵקִים, literally "make stand". "Everlasting" (v7, 8, 13, 19) is the noun עוֹלָם as a genitive, literally "a covenant of the age", "a possession of the age": time that reaches out of sight.
- **"to be a God unto thee" → "to be Elohim to you"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew לִהְיוֹת לְךָ לֵאלֹהִים, literally "to be to you for Elohim"; v8 "and I will be to them for Elohim".
- **"the land wherein thou art a stranger" → "the land of your sojournings"** (v8) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ מְגֻרֶיךָ *eretz megurekha*, a plural noun from the root of *ger* (sojourner).
- **"you" singular and plural** (v9–13) — v9 speaks to Abraham alone; from "which you shall keep" (v10) to v13 "you" and "your" are plural (Abraham and his seed), except "your seed" (v10, 12), "your house" and "your money" (v13), which are singular.
- **"man child" → "male"** (v10, 12, 14) — Hebrew זָכָר *zakhar*. Male, not "man-child." Includes infants.
- **"ye shall circumcise the flesh" → "you shall be circumcised in the flesh"** (v11) — Hebrew וּנְמַלְתֶּם, a passive (niphal): the Hebrew does not say who does it. So also v10, 12, 13, 14, 24–27. The active form stands only in v23.
- **"token of the covenant" → "a sign of a covenant"** (v11) — Hebrew אוֹת בְּרִית *ot berit*, without the article.
- **"bought with money of any stranger" → "bought with money from any foreigner"** (v12, 27) — Hebrew מִקְנַת־כֶּסֶף "a purchase of silver"; בֶּן־נֵכָר *ben-nekhar*, literally "a son of foreignness" = a foreigner. Different word from *ger* (sojourner). "At eight days old": literally "a son of eight days".
- **"must needs be circumcised" → "shall surely be circumcised"** (v13) — Hebrew הִמּוֹל יִמּוֹל, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "being circumcised he shall be circumcised".
- **"that soul shall be cut off from his people" → "that soul shall be cut off from its peoples"** (v14) — Hebrew וְנִכְרְתָה הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַהִוא מֵעַמֶּיהָ. נֶפֶשׁ *nephesh* "soul" is the living person. עַמֶּיהָ is plural, "its peoples" = its kinsfolk. The verb is from כָּרַת "cut", the verb of "cutting" a covenant (15:18). What the cutting off is (death, exclusion, or an act of God) the text does not say.
- **"Sarah shall her name be" → "Sarah is her name"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי שָׂרָה שְׁמָהּ, no verb. כִּי after a negative: "but" (or "for"). שָׂרַי → שָׂרָה *Sarai* → *Sarah*; *sarah* is "princess" (the feminine of שַׂר "prince"). The text gives no explanation of the change.
- **"she shall be a mother of nations" → "she shall become nations"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהָיְתָה לְגוֹיִם *we-hayetah le-goyim*. "Mother" is not in the Hebrew. "Give thee a son also of her" → "from her" (מִמֶּנָּה).
- **"Shall a child be born unto him that is an hundred years old?"** (v17) — Hebrew הַלְּבֶן מֵאָה־שָׁנָה יִוָּלֵד, literally "To a son of a hundred years shall there be born?" The Hebrew names no subject; "a child" is supplied because English needs one. "Bear" has no object.
- **"And God said, Sarah thy wife shall bear thee a son indeed" → "Truly, Sarah your wife is to bear you a son"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲבָל *aval*, "truly / in truth" (as 42:21), here correcting what Abraham said: "no, but". יֹלֶדֶת is a participle, "is bearing", of what is about to be.
- **"Isaac" — etymology preserved** (v19) — Hebrew יִצְחָק *Yitschaq* from root צחק *tsachaq* ("to laugh"): "he laughs". Wordplay with v17 where Abraham *laughs*. The root returns in 18:12–15; 19:14; 21:6, 9; 26:8.
- **"as for Ishmael, I have heard thee"** (v20) — Hebrew וּלְיִשְׁמָעֵאל שְׁמַעְתִּיךָ, a wordplay on the name *Yishma'el* ("El hears").
- **"twelve princes shall he beget" → "twelve chieftains he shall beget"** (v20) — Hebrew שְׁנֵים־עָשָׂר נְשִׂיאִם יוֹלִיד *sheneym-asar nesi'im yolid*. *Nasi* = "lifted-up one" = tribal chief / leader; a different word from שַׂר "prince". The twelve are named in 25:13–16.
- **"at this set time in the next year" → "at this appointed time in the next year"** (v21) — Hebrew לַמּוֹעֵד הַזֶּה בַּשָּׁנָה הָאַחֶרֶת, literally "in the other year". מוֹעֵד *mo'ed* — the same word as "appointed times" in 1:14.
- **"he left off talking with him" → "he finished speaking with him"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיְכַל לְדַבֵּר אִתּוֹ. "As God had said unto him" → "had spoken with him" (v23, דִּבֶּר אִתּוֹ).
- **"in the selfsame day" → "on that very day"** (v23, 26) — Hebrew בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "in the bone of this day".

### Notes

- **Name changes** — divinely-given name changes mark covenantal milestones (Abram→Abraham, Sarai→Sarah, later Jacob→Israel).
- **Circumcision as covenantal sign** — bodily and visible. Already practiced in Egypt (though differently) and among some Semitic peoples; Abraham's instance makes the practice a covenant marker on the organ of generation — fitting for a covenant about seed.
- **Ishmael not excluded from blessing** — v20 keeps the blessing on Ishmael and his twelve sons. The covenant is established with Isaac (v21), but Ishmael is not cursed or cut off.
