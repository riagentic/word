# Genesis 34

[1] And Dinah the daughter of Leah, whom she had borne to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, went out to see the daughters of the land.

[2] And Shechem the son of ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ the ⟦hivite|Chivvi|Hivite⟧, the chieftain of the land, saw her; and he took her and lay with her and humbled her.

[3] And his soul clung to Dinah the daughter of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and he loved the girl and spoke to the heart of the girl.

[4] And Shechem said to ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ his father, saying, "Take for me this young girl for a wife."

[5] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ heard that he had defiled Dinah his daughter; and his sons were with his livestock in the field, and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ kept silent until they came.

[6] And ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ the father of Shechem went out to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ to speak with him.

[7] And the sons of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ came from the field when they heard; and the men were grieved, and they burned with anger greatly, because he had done an outrage in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to lie with the daughter of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧; and so it is not done.

[8] And ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ spoke with them, saying, "Shechem my son — his soul longs for your daughter; give her, please, to him for a wife.

[9] And intermarry with us: your daughters you shall give to us, and our daughters you shall take for yourselves.

[10] And with us you shall dwell, and the land shall be before you; dwell and trade in it, and get possessions in it."

[11] And Shechem said to her father and to her brothers, "Let me find favor in your eyes, and what you say to me I will give.

[12] Multiply upon me very much bride-price and gift, and I will give as you say to me; and give me the girl for a wife."

[13] And the sons of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ answered Shechem and ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ his father with deceit, and they spoke, because he had defiled Dinah their sister,

[14] and they said to them, "We cannot do this thing, to give our sister to a man who has a foreskin; for that is a reproach to us.

[15] Only on this will we consent to you: if you will be like us, that every male of you be circumcised.

[16] Then we will give our daughters to you, and your daughters we will take for ourselves, and we will dwell with you, and we will become one people.

[17] And if you will not listen to us, to be circumcised, then we will take our daughter and go."

[18] And their words were good in the eyes of ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ and in the eyes of Shechem the son of ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧.

[19] And the young man did not delay to do the thing, because he delighted in the daughter of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧; and he was honored above all the house of his father.

[20] And ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ and Shechem his son came to the gate of their city and spoke to the men of their city, saying,

[21] "These men are peaceable with us; and let them dwell in the land and trade in it; and the land, behold, is wide on both hands before them. Their daughters let us take for ourselves for wives, and our daughters let us give to them.

[22] Only on this will the men consent to us, to dwell with us, to become one people: when every male of us is circumcised, as they are circumcised.

[23] Their livestock and their property and all their beasts — will they not be ours? Only let us consent to them, and they will dwell with us."

[24] And all who went out of the gate of his city listened to ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ and to Shechem his son; and every male was circumcised, all who went out of the gate of his city.

[25] And it came to pass on the third day, when they were in pain, that two of the sons of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧ and Levi, the brothers of Dinah, took each man his sword and came upon the city in security and killed every male.

[26] And ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧ and Shechem his son they killed with the edge of the sword; and they took Dinah from the house of Shechem and went out.

[27] The sons of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ came upon the slain and plundered the city, because they had defiled their sister.

[28] Their flock and their herd and their donkeys, and what was in the city and what was in the field, they took.

[29] And all their wealth and all their little ones and their wives they took captive and plundered, and all that was in the house.

[30] And ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ said to ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧ and to Levi, "You have troubled me, to make me stink with the inhabitant of the land, with the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and with the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧; and I am few in number, and they will gather against me and strike me, and I shall be destroyed, I and my house."

[31] And they said, "Shall he deal with our sister as with a prostitute?"

## Changes

- **"And" kept at the head of the clauses** (throughout) — Hebrew וַ / וְ; "when", "but", "so", "now" are not put in its place. v27 begins without "and" in the Hebrew.
- **"prince of the country" → "the chieftain of the land"** (v2) — Hebrew נְשִׂיא הָאָרֶץ; נָשִׂיא, "one lifted up", is kept apart from שַׂר "prince".
- **"he took her, and lay with her, and defiled her" → "he took her and lay with her and humbled her"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיִּקַּח אֹתָהּ וַיִּשְׁכַּב אֹתָהּ וַיְעַנֶּהָ, three verbs. לָקַח is "take" (not "seize"). וַיִּשְׁכַּב אֹתָהּ is pointed with the object marker, literally "he lay her", not "with her" (אִתָּהּ). וַיְעַנֶּהָ is the verb עִנָּה "afflict, bring low, humble" (31:50 "if you afflict my daughters"; 16:6), used of a man forcing or dishonoring a woman (Deut 22:24, 29; 2 Sam 13:12, 14). KJV's "defiled" is the word of v5; here it is another verb.
- **"his soul clave unto"** (v3) — Hebrew וַתִּדְבַּק נַפְשׁוֹ: דָּבַק "cling" (as 2:24), נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"spake kindly unto the damsel" → "spoke to the heart of the girl"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיְדַבֵּר עַל־לֵב הַנַּעֲרָ, literally "upon the heart".
- **"damsel" → "girl" / "young girl"** (v3, 4, 12) — Hebrew נַעֲרָה "girl" (v3, 12); in v4 Shechem's word is another, יַלְדָּה, the feminine of יֶלֶד "child".
- **"defiled"** (v5, 13, 27) — Hebrew טִמֵּא, "make unclean". In v27 the verb is plural, "they had defiled".
- **"held his peace" → "kept silent"; "until they were come" → "until they came"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהֶחֱרִשׁ; עַד־בֹּאָם, without "home".
- **"when they heard it"** (v7) — Hebrew כְּשָׁמְעָם, "when they heard", with no object. The accents join it to what goes before ("came from the field when they heard"); it can also be taken with what follows.
- **"were grieved, and they were very wroth" → "were grieved, and they burned with anger greatly"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּתְעַצְּבוּ; וַיִּחַר לָהֶם מְאֹד, literally "it burned to them greatly".
- **"wrought folly in Israel" → "done an outrage in Israel"** (v7) — Hebrew נְבָלָה עָשָׂה בְיִשְׂרָאֵל. נְבָלָה is the deed of the נָבָל, the base and godless fool: a shameful crime, not a foolish mistake (Deut 22:21; Judg 20:6; 2 Sam 13:12). בְיִשְׂרָאֵל is "in Israel" or "against Israel"; the name stands here for Jacob's house.
- **"which thing ought not to be done" → "and so it is not done"** (v7) — Hebrew וְכֵן לֹא יֵעָשֶׂה, "and thus it is not (shall not be) done".
- **"The soul of my son Shechem longeth for" → "Shechem my son — his soul longs for"** (v8) — the Hebrew order; חָשְׁקָה is "be bound to, long for". "Your" is plural (בְּבִתְּכֶם): the father and the brothers.
- **"make ye marriages with us" → "intermarry with us"** (v9) — Hebrew וְהִתְחַתְּנוּ אֹתָנוּ, from חָתָן "son-in-law".
- **"trade ye therein, and get you possessions therein"** (v10) — Hebrew וּסְחָרוּהָ, "go about in it" as traders; וְהֵאָחֲזוּ בָּהּ, "take hold in it" (אֲחֻזָּה "possession").
- **"Ask me never so much dowry and gift" → "Multiply upon me very much bride-price and gift"** (v12) — Hebrew הַרְבּוּ עָלַי מְאֹד מֹהַר וּמַתָּן. מֹהַר is what the bridegroom pays to the bride's family, not a dowry. "Only" (v12) is not in the Hebrew.
- **"answered … deceitfully, and said" → "answered … with deceit, and they spoke"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּמִרְמָה וַיְדַבֵּרוּ; the second verb is kept.
- **"to one that is uncircumcised" → "to a man who has a foreskin"** (v14) — Hebrew לְאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ עָרְלָה.
- **"Only on this"; "consent"** (v15, 22, 23) — Hebrew אַךְ־בְּזֹאת; נֵאוֹת, "agree". "Condition" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"pleased" → "were good in the eyes of"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיִּיטְבוּ … בְּעֵינֵי.
- **"more honourable than all" → "honored above all"** (v19) — Hebrew נִכְבָּד מִכֹּל. הַנַּעַר is "the boy / young man".
- **"peaceable"** (v21) — Hebrew שְׁלֵמִים, the adjective of 33:18 ("safe"): whole, at one with us. It is not the noun *shalom*.
- **"large enough for them" → "wide on both hands before them"** (v21) — Hebrew רַחֲבַת־יָדַיִם לִפְנֵיהֶם, literally "wide of two hands": broad on either side.
- **"their substance … every beast of theirs" → "their property and all their beasts"** (v23) — Hebrew מִקְנֵהֶם וְקִנְיָנָם וְכָל־בְּהֶמְתָּם.
- **"hearkened unto" → "listened to"** (v17, 24) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל.
- **"came upon the city boldly" → "came upon the city in security"** (v25) — Hebrew בֶּטַח, "security, confidence", used as an adverb. It can belong to the brothers (they came confidently, unopposed) or to the city (it was unsuspecting); the Hebrew does not decide, and the English leaves it as open.
- **"slew" → "killed"** (v25, 26) — Hebrew הָרַג. "The edge of the sword" is literally "the mouth of the sword" (לְפִי־חָרֶב).
- **"spoiled" → "plundered"; "that which was in the house"** (v27, 29) — Hebrew בָּזַז; בַּבָּיִת is singular, "in the house".
- **"sheep … oxen … asses" → "flock … herd … donkeys"** (v28) — Hebrew צֹאן, בָּקָר, חֲמֹרִים.
- **"Ye have troubled me to make me to stink"** (v30) — Hebrew עֲכַרְתֶּם אֹתִי לְהַבְאִישֵׁנִי: עָכַר "trouble, bring ruin on"; הִבְאִישׁ "make stink" = make hateful. "The inhabitant", "the Canaanite", "the Perizzite" are singular in Hebrew, each for the whole people.
- **"I being few in number"** (v30) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי מְתֵי מִסְפָּר, literally "and I am men of number", men who can be counted.
- **"slay me" → "strike me"** (v30) — Hebrew וְהִכּוּנִי.
- **"Should he deal with our sister as with an harlot?"** (v31) — Hebrew הַכְזוֹנָה יַעֲשֶׂה אֶת־אֲחוֹתֵנוּ; זוֹנָה "prostitute".

### Notes

- **Dinah** — she does not speak in the chapter; her father, her brothers, Shechem and Hamor speak about her. The verbs of v2 ("took … lay … humbled") are followed by those of v3 ("clung … loved … spoke to the heart"); the narrator gives both and calls the deed נְבָלָה, an outrage (v7), and "defiling" (v5, 13, 27).
- **Simeon and Levi's deceit** — they use circumcision, the sign of the covenant (ch 17), to disable the men of the city and kill them. Jacob's rebuke here speaks of the danger to his house (v30); his judgment on their anger comes in 49:5–7.
- **"Shall he deal with our sister as with a prostitute?" (v31)** — the chapter ends with the brothers' question; the narrator adds no answer.
