# Genesis 13

[1] And ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ went up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, he and his wife and all that he had, and Lot with him, to the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧.

[2] And ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ was very rich in livestock, in silver, and in gold.

[3] And he went by his journeys from the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ as far as ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, as far as the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and Ai,

[4] to the place of the altar which he had made there at the first; and there ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ called on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] And Lot also, who went with ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧, had flocks and herds and tents.

[6] And the land did not bear them, to dwell together, for their goods were many, and they were not able to dwell together.

[7] And there was contention between the shepherds of ⟦abram|Avram's|Abram's⟧ livestock and the shepherds of Lot's livestock. And the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ were then dwelling in the land.

[8] And ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ said to Lot, "Please let there be no strife between me and you, and between my shepherds and your shepherds, for we are men, brothers.

[9] Is not all the land before you? Part, please, from me: if to the left, then I will go to the right; and if to the right, then I will go to the left."

[10] And Lot lifted up his eyes and saw all the circle of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, that all of it was watered — before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ruined ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧ — like the garden of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, like the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, as you come to ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧.

[11] And Lot chose for himself all the circle of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and Lot journeyed east; and they parted, each man from his brother.

[12] ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ dwelt in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, and Lot dwelt in the cities of the circle and moved his tent as far as ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧.

[13] And the men of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ were evil, and sinners against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ exceedingly.

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧, after Lot had parted from him, "Lift up your eyes, please, and see, from the place where you are, northward and southward and eastward and westward;

[15] for all the land which you see, to you I will give it, and to your seed forever.

[16] And I will make your seed as the dust of the earth, so that if a man is able to count the dust of the earth, your seed also will be counted.

[17] Arise, walk about in the land, through its length and through its breadth, for to you I will give it."

[18] And ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧ moved his tent, and came and dwelt by the oaks of Mamre, which are in ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧; and he built there an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And" kept at the head of the clauses** (throughout) — Hebrew וַ / וְ.
- **"south" → "Negev"** (v1, 3) — as in 12:9.
- **"very rich" kept** (v2) — Hebrew כָּבֵד מְאֹד, literally "very heavy"; the same word as the "heavy" famine of 12:10, here in the sense "weighty in goods".
- **"cattle" → "livestock"** (v2, 7) — Hebrew מִקְנֶה *miqneh*, animals owned.
- **"went on his journeys" → "went by his journeys"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ לְמַסָּעָיו; מַסָּע is from נָסַע "journey": by the stages of his march.
- **"his tent"** (v3) — written אהלה, read (qere) אָהֳלוֹ; both mean "his tent".
- **"Hai" → "Ai"** (v3) — as in 12:8.
- **"at the beginning" / "at the first"** (v3, 4) — two Hebrew words, בַּתְּחִלָּה and בָּרִאשֹׁנָה.
- **"the land was not able to bear them" → "the land did not bear them"** (v6) — Hebrew וְלֹא־נָשָׂא אֹתָם הָאָרֶץ, "the land did not carry them"; "able" stands only in the second clause (וְלֹא יָכְלוּ).
- **"their substance was great" → "their goods were many"** (v6) — Hebrew רְכוּשָׁם רָב; רְכוּשׁ as 12:5.
- **"strife" → "contention"** (v7), **"strife" kept** (v8) — two Hebrew nouns of one root: רִיב (v7) and מְרִיבָה (v8).
- **"herdmen" → "shepherds"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew רֹעֵי, participle of רָעָה "to shepherd".
- **"the Canaanite and the Perizzite dwelled" kept** (v7) — Hebrew singular for the peoples, with a singular participle יֹשֵׁב.
- **"we be brethren" → "we are men, brothers"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲנָשִׁים אַחִים אֲנָחְנוּ, literally "men, brothers, are we". Lot is the son of Abram's brother; אָח "brother" is used of a kinsman.
- **"separate thyself" → "part"** (v9, 11, 14) — Hebrew פָּרַד niphal, as 10:5, 32.
- **"if thou wilt take the left hand" → "if to the left"** (v9) — Hebrew אִם־הַשְּׂמֹאל וְאֵימִנָה, literally "if the left, then I will go right"; the Hebrew has no verb "take".
- **"plain of Jordan" → "circle of the Jordan"** (v10, 11, 12) — Hebrew כִּכַּר הַיַּרְדֵּן *kikkar ha-Yarden*. כִּכָּר is "something round" (also a round loaf, a talent of metal): the round district of the lower Jordan valley. "Plain" is a different Hebrew word (בִּקְעָה, 11:2).
- **"well watered every where" → "all of it was watered"** (v10) — Hebrew כֻלָּהּ מַשְׁקֶה, "all of it a watered place"; "well" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"destroyed" → "ruined"** (v10) — Hebrew שַׁחֵת, piel of שָׁחַת, the verb of 6:11–13, 17 and 19:13.
- **"as thou comest unto Zoar" → "as you come to Zoar"** (v10) — Hebrew בֹּאֲכָה צֹעַר, literally "your coming to Zoar": in the direction of Zoar.
- **"journeyed east" kept** (v11) — Hebrew מִקֶּדֶם, literally "from the east", the same word as in 11:2; here the sense is "eastward" (or "on the east side").
- **"one from the other" → "each man from his brother"** (v11) — Hebrew אִישׁ מֵעַל אָחִיו.
- **"pitched his tent toward Sodom" → "moved his tent as far as Sodom"** (v12), **"removed his tent" → "moved his tent"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיֶּאֱהַל, a verb made from אֹהֶל "tent", literally "he tented"; עַד־סְדֹם is "as far as Sodom". "His" is supplied.
- **"wicked and sinners before the LORD exceedingly" → "evil, and sinners against Yahweh exceedingly"** (v13) — Hebrew רָעִים וְחַטָּאִים לַיהוָה מְאֹד; רַע is "evil", לַיהוָה is literally "to Yahweh" (against him, or: in his sight).
- **"Lift up now thine eyes" → "Lift up your eyes, please"** (v14) — Hebrew שָׂא נָא. "Southward" is literally "toward the Negev" (נֶגְבָּה), "westward" is "toward the sea" (יָמָּה).
- **"seed" kept** (v15, 16) — Hebrew זֶרַע, singular, as 12:7.
- **"for ever" → "forever"** (v15) — Hebrew עַד־עוֹלָם, literally "until the age".
- **"number… be numbered" → "count… be counted"** (v16) — Hebrew מָנָה twice; "a man" is אִישׁ.
- **"walk through the land" → "walk about in the land"** (v17) — Hebrew הִתְהַלֵּךְ, hitpael: walk to and fro.
- **"plain of Mamre" → "oaks of Mamre"** (v18) — Hebrew אֵלֹנֵי מַמְרֵא, plural of אֵלוֹן, as 12:6 (a great tree; the kind is not certain).

### Notes

- **Lot and Abram** — Lot "lifted up his eyes and saw" (v10); Yahweh then tells Abram, "Lift up your eyes and see" (v14): the same two verbs.
- **Sodom and Gomorrah foreshadowed** — the clause "before Yahweh ruined Sodom and Gomorrah" (v10) looks ahead to ch 19.
- **"Dust of the earth" (v16)** — the first of the comparisons in the promises to Abram (dust here; stars in 15:5; sand in 22:17).
