## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins clause to clause, and name to name in the lists, with וְ "and"; the verses keep each one, and leave it out only where the Hebrew does (v11, 16, 21: "Gershon, Kohath and Merari"; "Ziphion and Haggi, Shuni and Ezbon …").
- **"took his journey" → "set out"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּסַּע, נָסַע "journey", here of the start; "and all that was his" is the Hebrew order (וְכָל־אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ).
- **"offered sacrifices" → "sacrificed sacrifices"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּזְבַּח זְבָחִים, the verb and its own noun.
- **"spake … and said" → "said … and said"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר twice, the same verb. "Visions of the night": בְּמַרְאֹת הַלַּיְלָה, plural.
- **"Here am I" → "Here I am"** (v2) — Hebrew הִנֵּנִי, literally "behold me".
- **"I am God" → "I am the El"** (v3) — Hebrew הָאֵל *ha-El*, with the article, followed by אֱלֹהֵי אָבִיךָ "the *Elohim* of your father."
- **"I will also surely bring thee up again" → "I myself will also surely bring you up"** (v4) — Hebrew אַעַלְךָ גַם־עָלֹה, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "I will bring you up, also bringing up"; "again" is not in the Hebrew. "I myself" twice: the pronoun אָנֹכִי is written out.
- **"Joseph shall put his hand upon thine eyes" → "Joseph shall put his hand on your eyes"** (v4) — Hebrew וְיוֹסֵף יָשִׁית יָדוֹ עַל־עֵינֶיךָ, word for word. It is commonly understood of closing the eyes of the dying; the verse does not say "close".
- **"seed" kept** (v6, 7) — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **"souls" kept** (v15, 18, 22, 25, 26, 27) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ *nephesh*, here the word for a person counted (as in 12:5). The Hebrew is singular each time ("every soul … thirty-three").
- **"Phallu" → "Pallu"** (v9) — Hebrew פַּלּוּא *Pallu*.
- **"Phuvah" / "Job" → "Puvah" / "Iob"** (v13) — Hebrew פֻוָּה, יוֹב *Yov*; not the name of the Job of the book (אִיּוֹב *Iyyov*).
- **"of a Canaanitish woman" → "of the Canaanite woman"** (v10) — Hebrew בֶּן־הַכְּנַעֲנִית, with the article.
- **"the sons of Dan: Hushim"** (v23) — the Hebrew has the plural בְּנֵי "sons" with one name; kept.
- **"which came out of his loins" → "who came out of his thigh"** (v26) — Hebrew יֹצְאֵי יְרֵכוֹ; יָרֵךְ is "thigh", the same word as in 24:2; 32:25; 47:29. It means his own offspring. "Belonging to Jacob": Hebrew הַבָּאָה לְיַעֲקֹב, literally "that came, of Jacob".
- **"threescore and six" / "threescore and ten" → "sixty-six" / "seventy"** (v26, 27) — the Hebrew numbers, שִׁשִּׁים וָשֵׁשׁ and שִׁבְעִים. The lists give 33 + 16 + 14 + 7 = 70 names; sixty-six are those who came with Jacob; seventy is the whole house, with Joseph and his two sons already in Egypt. Acts 7:14 says seventy-five, the number of the Greek translation of this verse (from memory); the verse keeps the Hebrew number.
- **"to direct his face unto Goshen" → "to point the way before him to Goshen"** (v28) — Hebrew לְהוֹרֹת לְפָנָיו גֹּשְׁנָה, literally "to point out before him, to Goshen"; "the way" is supplied, and what Judah was to point out is not said.
- **"presented himself unto him" → "appeared to him"** (v29) — Hebrew וַיֵּרָא אֵלָיו, niphal of רָאָה, "he was seen by him, appeared to him", the same verb form as "Yahweh appeared to Abram" (12:7). The Hebrew does not name the subject.
- **"a good while" → "a long while"** (v29) — Hebrew עוֹד, literally "still, yet more".
- **"since I have seen thy face, because thou art yet alive" → "after I have seen your face, that you are still alive"** (v30) — Hebrew אַחֲרֵי רְאוֹתִי אֶת־פָּנֶיךָ כִּי עוֹדְךָ חָי; כִּי can be "that" or "for". "Now": הַפָּעַם, literally "this time".
- **"house" kept** (v31) — Hebrew בֵּית אָבִיו, "his father's house".
- **"shepherds" → "shepherds of the flock"** (v32, 34) — Hebrew רֹעֵי צֹאן; צֹאן is the flock of sheep and goats. "Their trade hath been to feed cattle" → "they have been men of livestock": Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי מִקְנֶה הָיוּ.
- **"What is your occupation?" → "What is your work?"** (v33) — Hebrew מַה־מַּעֲשֵׂיכֶם, literally "what are your works?"
- **"servants" kept** (v34) — Hebrew עֲבָדֶיךָ, עֶבֶד "slave", here the polite word of men speaking to a king.
- **"an abomination unto the Egyptians" kept** (v34) — Hebrew תוֹעֲבַת מִצְרַיִם, literally "the abomination of Mitzrayim (Egypt)"; תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination".

### Notes

- **"Abomination to the Egyptians" (v34)** — the text does not say why; the same word is used in 43:32 of Egyptians eating with Hebrews.
- **The descent into Egypt** — compare Gen 15:13–16 (the sojourn in a land not theirs).
- **v8–27** — the names are given as the Hebrew has them here; Num 26 and 1 Chr 4–8 give some of them in other forms.
