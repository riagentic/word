# Genesis 45

[1] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ was not able to restrain himself before all those standing by him, and he called out, "Make every man go out from me." And no man stood with him when ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ made himself known to his brothers.

[2] And he gave forth his voice in weeping, and ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ heard, and the house of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ heard.

[3] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ said to his brothers, "I am ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧. Is my father yet alive?" And his brothers were not able to answer him, for they were dismayed before his face.

[4] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ said to his brothers, "Come near to me, please." And they came near. And he said, "I am ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ your brother, whom you sold into ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[5] And now, do not be grieved, and let there be no anger in your eyes that you sold me here, for it was for the preservation of life that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent me before you.

[6] For these two years the famine has been in the midst of the land, and there are yet five years in which there is no plowing and no harvest.

[7] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent me before you to set for you a remnant in the land, and to preserve life for you, for a great escape.

[8] And now, it was not you who sent me here, but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and he has set me as a father to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and as lord to all his house and ruler in all the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[9] Hasten and go up to my father, and say to him, 'Thus says your son ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧: ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has set me as lord to all ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧. Come down to me; do not stand still.

[10] And you shall dwell in the land of Goshen, and you shall be near to me, you and your sons and the sons of your sons, and your flock and your herd and all that is yours.

[11] And I will sustain you there, for there are yet five years of famine, lest you be dispossessed, you and your house and all that is yours.'

[12] And behold, your eyes see, and the eyes of my brother ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, that it is my mouth that is speaking to you.

[13] And you shall tell my father all my glory in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and all that you have seen, and you shall hasten and bring my father down here."

[14] And he fell on the neck of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ his brother and wept, and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ wept on his neck.

[15] And he kissed all his brothers and wept upon them, and after this his brothers spoke with him.

[16] And the voice was heard in the house of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, saying, "The brothers of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ have come." And it was good in the eyes of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and in the eyes of his servants.

[17] And ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ said to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, "Say to your brothers, 'Do this: load your animals and go, enter the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧.

[18] And take your father and your houses and come to me, and let me give you the good of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and eat the fat of the land.'

[19] And you, you are commanded: 'Do this: take for yourselves from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ wagons for your little ones and for your wives, and carry your father and come.

[20] And let not your eye pity your vessels, for the good of all the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ is yours.'"

[21] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did so, and ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ gave them wagons at the command of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, and he gave them provisions for the way.

[22] To all of them he gave, to each man, changes of cloaks; and to ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ he gave three hundred pieces of silver and five changes of cloaks.

[23] And to his father he sent as follows: ten donkeys carrying of the good of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and ten she-donkeys carrying grain and bread and nourishment for his father for the way.

[24] And he sent his brothers away, and they went. And he said to them, "Do not be agitated on the way."

[25] And they went up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and came to the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ their father.

[26] And they told him, saying, "⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ is yet alive, and he is ruler in all the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧." And his heart grew numb, for he did not believe them.

[27] And they spoke to him all the words of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ that he had spoken to them, and he saw the wagons that ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ had sent to carry him, and the spirit of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ their father came alive.

[28] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said, "Enough! ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ my son is yet alive. Let me go and see him before I die."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v8 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"refrain himself" → "restrain himself"** (v1) — Hebrew לְהִתְאַפֵּק, as 43:31. "Every man … no man" is אִישׁ both times.
- **"he wept aloud" → "he gave forth his voice in weeping"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן אֶת־קֹלוֹ בִּבְכִי, literally "he gave his voice in weeping".
- **"the Egyptians" → "Egypt"** (v2) — Hebrew מִצְרַיִם, with a plural verb.
- **"troubled at his presence" → "dismayed before his face"** (v3) — Hebrew נִבְהֲלוּ מִפָּנָיו, literally "from his face".
- **"nor angry with yourselves" → "let there be no anger in your eyes"** (v5) — Hebrew וְאַל־יִחַר בְּעֵינֵיכֶם, literally "and let it not burn in your eyes". "Be grieved" is תֵּעָצְבוּ.
- **"to preserve life" → "for the preservation of life"** (v5) — Hebrew לְמִחְיָה, a noun, placed first in its clause; "it was … that" carries that order.
- **"earing nor harvest" → "plowing and no harvest"** (v6) — Hebrew חָרִישׁ וְקָצִיר.
- **"to preserve you a posterity in the earth" → "to set for you a remnant in the land"** (v7) — Hebrew לָשׂוּם לָכֶם שְׁאֵרִית בָּאָרֶץ; שְׁאֵרִית is "remnant", what is left. The verb "set" (שִׂים) returns in v8, 9.
- **"to save your lives by a great deliverance" → "to preserve life for you, for a great escape"** (v7) — Hebrew וּלְהַחֲיוֹת לָכֶם לִפְלֵיטָה גְּדֹלָה, literally "and to make live for you, for a great escape". פְּלֵיטָה is an escape, or those who have escaped (a body of survivors); it is not the word "deliverance" or "salvation". "Preserve life" is the verb of "preservation of life" in v5.
- **"it was not you that sent me hither, but God" kept** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא־אַתֶּם שְׁלַחְתֶּם אֹתִי הֵנָּה כִּי הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"made me" → "set me"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew שִׂים. "Ruler" is מֹשֵׁל.
- **"tarry not" → "do not stand still"** (v9) — Hebrew אַל־תַּעֲמֹד, "do not stand".
- **"thy children, and thy children's children" → "your sons and the sons of your sons"** (v10) — Hebrew בָּנֶיךָ וּבְנֵי בָנֶיךָ. "Flock" and "herd" are צֹאן and בָּקָר.
- **"nourish" → "sustain"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּלְכֵּל.
- **"come to poverty" → "be dispossessed"** (v11) — Hebrew תִּוָּרֵשׁ, the passive of "possess" (יָרַשׁ): to lose what one has.
- **"the fame thereof" → "the voice"** (v16) — Hebrew הַקֹּל: the news. "It was good in the eyes of" is literal.
- **"lade your beasts" → "load your animals"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּעִירְכֶם, beasts of burden; a different word from בְּהֵמָה "beast".
- **"the good of the land" kept** (v18, 20, 23) — Hebrew טוּב, "the good"; "the fat" (v18) is חֵלֶב.
- **"regard not your stuff" → "let not your eye pity your vessels"** (v20) — Hebrew וְעֵינְכֶם אַל־תָּחֹס עַל־כְּלֵיכֶם; כְּלִי is any vessel or piece of household goods.
- **"according to the commandment of Pharaoh" → "at the command of Pharaoh"** (v21) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי פַרְעֹה, literally "upon the mouth of Pharaoh", as 41:40.
- **"changes of raiment" → "changes of cloaks"** (v22) — Hebrew חֲלִפוֹת שְׂמָלֹת, as 41:14.
- **"three hundred pieces of silver" kept** (v22) — Hebrew שְׁלֹשׁ מֵאוֹת כֶּסֶף, "three hundred of silver"; "pieces" is supplied (the unit, the shekel weight, is not named).
- **"corn and bread and meat" → "grain and bread and nourishment"** (v23) — Hebrew בָּר וָלֶחֶם וּמָזוֹן.
- **"See that ye fall not out" → "Do not be agitated"** (v24) — Hebrew אַל־תִּרְגְּזוּ; רָגַז is "tremble, be stirred up", in anger (a quarrel) or in fear. The Hebrew leaves it open, and so does the verse.
- **"Jacob's heart fainted" → "his heart grew numb"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיָּפָג לִבּוֹ; "Jacob" is not in the Hebrew of this clause.
- **"revived" → "came alive"** (v27) — Hebrew וַתְּחִי, "lived".
- **"It is enough" → "Enough!"** (v28) — Hebrew רַב, one word, "much". "Let me go" is a cohortative (אֵלְכָה).

### Notes

- **Joseph's reading of what was done (v5–8)** — "you sold me here" (v5) and "*Elohim* sent me" (v5, 7, 8) stand side by side; the thought returns in 50:20. Joseph says "*Elohim*", not the name Yahweh.
- **The land of Goshen (v10)** — in the eastern Nile delta; the text does not describe it further here (see 46:34; 47:6).
- **Three hundred pieces of silver (v22)** — Joseph was sold for twenty (37:28).
