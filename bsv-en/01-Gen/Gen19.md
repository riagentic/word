# Genesis 19

[1] And the two angels came to ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ in the evening, and Lot was sitting in the gate of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧; and Lot saw, and rose to meet them, and bowed down with his face to the earth,

[2] and said, "Behold now, my lords, turn aside, please, to the house of your servant, and lodge, and wash your feet; and you shall rise early and go on your way." And they said, "No, for in the square we will lodge."

[3] And he pressed them greatly, and they turned aside to him and came into his house; and he made them a feast, and baked unleavened bread, and they ate.

[4] Before they lay down, the men of the city, the men of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧, surrounded the house, from boy to old man, all the people, from the end.

[5] And they called to Lot and said to him, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us, and let us know them."

[6] And Lot went out to them, to the entrance, and the door he shut behind him.

[7] And he said, "Please, my brothers, do not do evil.

[8] Behold now, I have two daughters who have not known a man; let me, please, bring them out to you, and do to them as is good in your eyes; only to these men do not do a thing, for therefore they have come into the shadow of my roof."

[9] And they said, "Stand back!" And they said, "This one came to sojourn, and he surely judges! Now we will do more evil to you than to them." And they pressed upon the man, upon Lot, greatly, and drew near to break the door.

[10] And the men stretched out their hand and brought Lot to them into the house, and the door they shut.

[11] And the men who were at the entrance of the house they struck with blindness, from small to great, and they wearied themselves to find the entrance.

[12] And the men said to Lot, "Whom else have you here? A son-in-law, and your sons and your daughters, and all whom you have in the city, bring out of the place.

[13] For we are ruining this place, for their outcry has become great before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has sent us to ruin it."

[14] And Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law, the takers of his daughters, and said, "Rise, go out from this place, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is ruining the city." And he was as one jesting in the eyes of his sons-in-law.

[15] And as the dawn came up, the angels urged Lot, saying, "Rise, take your wife and your two daughters who are found here, lest you be swept away in the iniquity of the city."

[16] And he lingered; and the men took hold of his hand and of the hand of his wife and of the hand of his two daughters, in the pity of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ upon him; and they brought him out and set him outside the city.

[17] And it came to pass, when they had brought them outside, that he said, "Escape for your soul! Do not look behind you, and do not stand in all the circle; escape to the mountain, lest you be swept away."

[18] And Lot said to them, "No, please, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧!

[19] Behold now, your servant has found favor in your eyes, and you have made great your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ which you have done with me, to keep my soul alive; and I am not able to escape to the mountain, lest the evil cling to me and I die.

[20] Behold now, this city is near to flee there, and it is a little thing; let me escape there, please — is it not a little thing? — and my soul shall live."

[21] And he said to him, "Behold, I have lifted up your face as to this thing also, that I will not overthrow the city of which you have spoken.

[22] Hasten, escape there, for I am not able to do a thing until you come there." Therefore the name of the city was called ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧.

[23] The sun had gone out over the earth, and Lot came to ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧.

[24] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ caused to rain on ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and on ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧ sulfur and fire, from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, out of the heavens;

[25] and he overthrew those cities, and all the circle, and all who dwelt in the cities, and the growth of the ground.

[26] And his wife looked from behind him, and she became a pillar of salt.

[27] And ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ rose early in the morning to the place where he had stood before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[28] And he looked down over the face of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧, and over all the face of the land of the circle; and he saw, and behold, the smoke of the land went up like the smoke of a kiln.

[29] And it came to pass, when ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ ruined the cities of the circle, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ remembered ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, and sent Lot out of the midst of the overthrow, when he overthrew the cities in which Lot had dwelt.

[30] And Lot went up from ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧ and dwelt in the mountain, and his two daughters with him, for he feared to dwell in ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧; and he dwelt in the cave, he and his two daughters.

[31] And the firstborn said to the younger, "Our father is old, and there is no man in the land to come in to us, as is the way of all the earth.

[32] Come, let us make our father drink wine, and let us lie with him, and let us keep seed alive from our father."

[33] And they made their father drink wine that night; and the firstborn came and lay with her father, and he did not know of her lying down or of her rising.

[34] And it came to pass on the next day that the firstborn said to the younger, "Behold, I lay last night with my father; let us make him drink wine tonight also, and come, lie with him, and let us keep seed alive from our father."

[35] And they made their father drink wine that night also; and the younger rose and lay with him, and he did not know of her lying down or of her rising.

[36] And the two daughters of Lot conceived by their father.

[37] And the firstborn bore a son and called his name ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧; he is the father of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ to this day.

[38] And the younger, she also bore a son and called his name ⟦ben-ammi|Ben-Ammi|Ben-ammi⟧; he is the father of the sons of Ammon to this day.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v29) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And" kept** (throughout) — Hebrew וַ / וְ at the head of each clause.
- **"two angels" → "the two angels"** (v1, 15) — Hebrew שְׁנֵי הַמַּלְאָכִים *sheney ha-mal'akhim*, with the article. The word *mal'akh* means "messenger" (one sent), as in 16:7; here and in the phrase "the angel of Yahweh" it is rendered "angel". The same two are called "the men" (הָאֲנָשִׁים) in v10, 12, 16, as in 18:22.
- **"sat in the gate" (v1)** — Hebrew יֹשֵׁב בְּשַׁעַר־סְדֹם. The open place at the city gate was where the elders sat and where business and judgment were done.
- **"with his face toward the ground" → "with his face to the earth"** (v1) — Hebrew אַפַּיִם אָרְצָה, literally "nostrils to the earth".
- **"my lords"** (v2) — Hebrew אֲדֹנַי *adonay*, pointed with the short vowel (patach): the plain plural "my lords", said to the two; the verbs and "your" in the verse are plural. Compare 18:3 and v18.
- **"tarry all night" / "abide" → "lodge"** (v2) — Hebrew לוּן, twice. "In the street" → "in the square": בָרְחוֹב *ba-rechov* = the broad open place of a town.
- **"he pressed upon them greatly" → "he pressed them greatly"** (v3, 9) — Hebrew פָּצַר בְּ. "Feast": מִשְׁתֶּה, literally "a drinking".
- **"compassed the house round" → "surrounded the house"** (v4) — Hebrew נָסַבּוּ *nasabbu*. "Both old and young" → "from boy to old man": מִנַּעַר וְעַד־זָקֵן. "From every quarter" → "from the end": מִקָּצֶה, that is, from the farthest end of the city: all of them.
- **"that we may know them" → "let us know them"** (v5) — Hebrew וְנֵדְעָה אֹתָם *we-ned'ah otam*, a cohortative of יָדַע "know". The same verb stands in v8, "who have not known a man", where it is the knowing of a man and a woman (as 4:1). The word "know" is kept in both verses; v8 shows in what sense the men of the city use it.
- **"do not so wickedly" → "do not do evil"** (v7) — Hebrew אַל־נָא … תָּרֵעוּ *al-na … tare'u*. Root *ra'a* (to do evil); the same verb in v9, "we will do more evil to you".
- **"let me, I pray you, bring them out"** (v8) — Hebrew אוֹצִיאָה־נָּא, a cohortative with נָא. "For therefore": כִּי־עַל־כֵּן, as in 18:5. "The shadow of my roof": קֹרָתִי, literally "my beam". The text records Lot's offer without comment.
- **"Stand back" kept** (v9) — Hebrew גֶּשׁ־הָלְאָה *gesh-hal'ah*, literally "draw near, farther off": "get out of the way".
- **"he will needs be a judge" → "he surely judges"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁפֹּט שָׁפוֹט *wa-yishpot shafot*, the verb with its infinitive absolute, literally "and he judged, judging": he goes on playing the judge. "Came to sojourn": לָגוּר, the verb of the sojourner (*ger*).
- **"put forth their hand, and pulled Lot" → "stretched out their hand and brought Lot"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁלְחוּ … אֶת־יָדָם וַיָּבִיאוּ.
- **"blindness" kept** (v11) — Hebrew סַנְוֵרִים *sanverim*, found only here and in 2 Kgs 6:18 — not the ordinary word for blindness. Its exact sense is uncertain: a sudden blinding or dazzling. "Both small and great" → "from small to great". "Struck": הִכּוּ.
- **"Hast thou here any besides?" → "Whom else have you here?"** (v12) — Hebrew עֹד מִי־לְךָ פֹה. "A son-in-law": חָתָן, singular, without "your".
- **"we will destroy this place" → "we are ruining this place"** (v13, 14, 29) — Hebrew מַשְׁחִתִים אֲנַחְנוּ, a participle (of what is about to be done); the verb שָׁחַת, as in 6:13 and 18:28.
- **"the cry of them is waxen great" → "their outcry has become great"** (v13) — Hebrew צַעֲקָתָם, the word of 18:20–21: the outcry about them, or the outcry that rises from them.
- **"which married his daughters" → "the takers of his daughters"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹקְחֵי בְנֹתָיו, a participle: it can mean those who had taken his daughters (other, married daughters) or those who were to take them (the two of v8). The Hebrew does not decide.
- **"he seemed as one that mocked" → "he was as one jesting"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּמְצַחֵק *kimtsacheq*, the piel of צחק "laugh" (the root of Isaac's name): to laugh, jest, play.
- **"which are here" → "who are found here"** (v15) — Hebrew הַנִּמְצָאֹת "who are found"; "here" is supplied.
- **"lest thou be consumed" → "lest you be swept away"** (v15, 17) — Hebrew פֶּן־תִּסָּפֶה *pen-tissapheh*, the verb of 18:23–24.
- **"the LORD being merciful unto him" → "in the pity of Yahweh upon him"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּחֶמְלַת יְהוָה עָלָיו, a noun: pity that spares.
- **"Escape for thy life" → "Escape for your soul"** (v17, 19, 20) — Hebrew עַל־נַפְשֶׁךָ; נֶפֶשׁ *nephesh* "soul" is the life of the living person. "He said": the Hebrew has the singular, without naming the speaker.
- **"neither stay thou in all the plain" → "do not stand in all the circle"** (v17, 25, 28, 29) — Hebrew כִּכָּר *kikkar*, literally "a round": the round district of the lower Jordan (13:10). "Mountain": הָהָרָה, "to the mountain" (or "to the hill country").
- **"Oh, not so, my Lord" → "No, please, Adonai"** (v18) — Hebrew אַל־נָא אֲדֹנָי. The word is pointed with the long vowel (qamets), as in 18:3, the form used when God is named. But it stands at the end of the verse, where the plain plural "my lords" (v2) takes the same long vowel; so the pointing can be read either way. Lot speaks "to them", and then to one: "your servant", "your eyes", "you have done" (v19) are singular, and the answer is "he said" (v21).
- **"mercy" → "*chesed*"** (v19) — Hebrew חֶסֶד *chesed*: loyal love, kindness shown in faithfulness. "Which you have done with me": עָשִׂיתָ עִמָּדִי. "Lest some evil take me" → "lest the evil cling to me": הָרָעָה with the article, תִּדְבָּקַנִי.
- **"is it not a little one?"** (v20) — Hebrew מִצְעָר *mits'ar* "a little thing", a wordplay with the name Zoar (צוֹעַר *Tso'ar*) in v22. "Flee" (לָנוּס) and "escape" (נִמְלַט) are two verbs.
- **"I have accepted thee concerning this thing" → "I have lifted up your face as to this thing also"** (v21) — Hebrew נָשָׂאתִי פָנֶיךָ *nasa'ti fanekha*, literally so: to lift up the face of one who asks = to grant him his request.
- **"the name of the city was called Zoar"** (v22) — Hebrew קָרָא "one called", without a named subject.
- **"The sun was risen upon the earth when Lot entered" → "The sun had gone out over the earth, and Lot came"** (v23) — Hebrew הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ יָצָא עַל־הָאָרֶץ וְלוֹט בָּא; "and", not "when".
- **"the LORD rained … from the LORD out of heaven"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיהוָה הִמְטִיר … מֵאֵת יְהוָה מִן־הַשָּׁמָיִם. Yahweh is named twice in the one sentence, as its subject and again in "from Yahweh"; both are kept. הִמְטִיר is "cause to rain". "Heavens": plural in Hebrew.
- **"brimstone and fire" → "sulfur and fire"** (v24) — Hebrew גָּפְרִית וָאֵשׁ *gophrit wa-esh*.
- **"overthrew"** (v25, 29; v21) — Hebrew הָפַךְ, literally "turned over"; הֲפֵכָה "the overthrow". "That which grew upon the ground" → "the growth of the ground": צֶמַח הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"his wife looked back from behind him" → "his wife looked from behind him"** (v26) — Hebrew וַתַּבֵּט אִשְׁתּוֹ מֵאַחֲרָיו; "back" is not in the Hebrew; Lot is not named. "Pillar": נְצִיב *netsiv*, something set up.
- **"gat up early in the morning to the place"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁכֵּם … אֶל־הַמָּקוֹם; the Hebrew has no verb of going.
- **"furnace" → "kiln"** (v28) — Hebrew כִּבְשָׁן *kivshan* = smelting furnace / kiln. "Smoke": קִיטֹר, a thick smoke; not the common word עָשָׁן.
- **"God remembered Abraham" (v29)** — as in 8:1. The verse names *Elohim*, where the rest of the chapter has Yahweh.
- **"dwelt in the mountain"** (v30) — Hebrew בָּהָר. "In a cave" → "in the cave": בַּמְּעָרָה, with the article.
- **"to come in unto us after the manner of all the earth" → "to come in to us, as is the way of all the earth"** (v31) — Hebrew לָבוֹא עָלֵינוּ כְּדֶרֶךְ כָּל־הָאָרֶץ; the preposition is עַל "upon". אֶרֶץ is "land" in the first half and "earth" in the second: one word.
- **"preserve seed of our father" → "keep seed alive from our father"** (v32, 34) — Hebrew וּנְחַיֶּה מֵאָבִינוּ זָרַע *u-nechayyeh me-avinu zara*. *Chayah* (piel) = keep alive. "Let us lie", "let us keep alive": cohortatives.
- **"he perceived not when she lay down, nor when she arose" → "he did not know of her lying down or of her rising"** (v33, 35) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָדַע בְּשִׁכְבָהּ וּבְקוּמָהּ, the verb "know".
- **"were with child by their father" → "conceived by their father"** (v36) — Hebrew וַתַּהֲרֶיןָ … מֵאֲבִיהֶן, literally "from their father".
- **"the father of the Moabites" → "the father of Moab"** (v37) — Hebrew אֲבִי־מוֹאָב. The name מוֹאָב *Mo'av* sounds like מֵאָב *me-av* "from a father" (cf. "from our father", v32, 34); the Hebrew text does not explain the name.
- **"the father of the children of Ammon" → "the father of the sons of Ammon"** (v38) — Hebrew אֲבִי בְנֵי־עַמּוֹן. בֶּן־עַמִּי *Ben-Ammi* = "son of my people / of my kinsman".

### Notes

- **Sodom's sin** — the chapter shows the violence of the whole city against strangers; 18:20 and 19:13 speak of the "outcry". Ezek 16:49–50 names pride, fullness of bread and careless ease, not strengthening the hand of the poor and needy, and abominations; Jude 7 names sexual immorality and going after other flesh (both from memory).
- **Lot's daughters (v30–38)** — the origin of Moab and Ammon, Israel's neighbors east of the Jordan. Ruth the Moabitess is the great-grandmother of David (Ruth 4).
- **Lot's wife (v26)** — why she looked is not said.
