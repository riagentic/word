## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"an officer of Pharaoh" → "a eunuch of Pharaoh"** (v1) — Hebrew סְרִיס *saris*: "eunuch", also the title of a court officer whether or not he is castrated (eunuch / officer); Potiphar has a wife (v7). One rendering is kept for the word (Gen 37:36; 40:2, 7).
- **"captain of the guard" → "chief of the guards"** (v1) — Hebrew שַׂר הַטַּבָּחִים, literally "chief of the slaughterers": butchers or cooks, and hence executioners or the royal bodyguard; which is meant is not certain. The same word שַׂר is "chief" in "the chief of the prison house" (v21–23; KJV "keeper").
- **"an Egyptian" → "an Egyptian man"** (v1) — Hebrew אִישׁ מִצְרִי; so also אִישׁ עִבְרִי "a Hebrew man" (v14).
- **"of the hands of the Ishmeelites"** (v1) — the Hebrew has "the Ishmaelites" here; Gen 37:36 has "the Medanites". Not harmonised.
- **"a prosperous man" → "a prospering man"** (v2, 3, 23) — Hebrew אִישׁ מַצְלִיחַ *ish matsliach*, the participle of הִצְלִיחַ "prosper / make prosper", the same verb as "Yahweh made prosper" in v3, 23: one who succeeds in what he does, not one who is rich.
- **"master" → "lord"** (v2, 3, 7, 8, 16, 19, 20) — Hebrew אָדוֹן *adon*, one rendering throughout.
- **"served him" → "ministered to him"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְשָׁרֶת אֹתוֹ: שֵׁרֵת, to attend on a person, not the word "serve" (עָבַד).
- **"made him overseer over his house" → "appointed him over his house"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew וַיַּפְקִדֵהוּ עַל־בֵּיתוֹ; "overseer" is not in the Hebrew. "Gave into his hand" is נָתַן בְּיָדוֹ (v4, 8, 22).
- **"for Joseph's sake" → "because of Joseph"** (v5) — Hebrew בִּגְלַל יוֹסֵף.
- **"he knew not ought he had" → "with him he did not know anything"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָדַע אִתּוֹ מְאוּמָה, literally "and he did not know with him anything": having Joseph, he took knowledge of nothing. The verb is יָדַע "know"; "had no concern" interprets it. "Bread" is לֶחֶם.
- **"a goodly person, and well favoured" → "beautiful in form and beautiful in appearance"** (v6) — Hebrew יְפֵה־תֹאַר וִיפֵה מַרְאֶה, the words used of Rachel (Gen 29:17).
- **"cast her eyes upon" → "lifted up her eyes to"** (v7) — Hebrew וַתִּשָּׂא … אֶת־עֵינֶיהָ אֶל.
- **"There is none greater" → "He is not greater"** (v9) — Hebrew אֵינֶנּוּ גָדוֹל … מִמֶּנִּי, "he is not great in this house more than I".
- **"this great wickedness" → "this great evil"** (v9) — Hebrew הָרָעָה הַגְּדֹלָה הַזֹּאת. "Sin against Elohim" is וְחָטָאתִי לֵאלֹהִים.
- **"to lie by her, or to be with her" → "to lie beside her, to be with her"** (v10) — the Hebrew has no "or".
- **"about this time" → "on one such day"** (v11) — Hebrew כְּהַיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "as this day"; the exact force is not certain.
- **"fled, and got him out" → "fled and went outside"** (v12, 15) — two Hebrew verbs, וַיָּנָס וַיֵּצֵא; in v13, 18 only "fled outside".
- **"to mock us" → "to make sport of us"** (v14, 17) — Hebrew לְצַחֶק בָּנוּ / בִּי, the piel of צָחַק "laugh" (the root of the name Isaac) with בְּ: to make sport of, to toy with; the word can carry a sexual sense (Gen 26:8).
- **"I cried with a loud voice" → "I called out with a loud voice"** (v14, 15, 18) — Hebrew וָאֶקְרָא, the verb "call" of v14 ("she called"); "loud voice" is literally "great voice".
- **"laid up his garment by her"** (v16) — Hebrew וַתַּנַּח, "she laid (let rest)".
- **"The Hebrew servant" → "the Hebrew slave"** (v17, 19) — Hebrew עֶבֶד. The Hebrew order of v17 is kept: "to make sport of me" can go with "came in to me" or with "whom you brought to us".
- **"After this manner did thy servant to me" → "Such things as these your slave did to me"** (v19) — Hebrew כַּדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה, the same phrase as "according to these words" in v17; דָּבָר is "word" and "thing".
- **"his wrath was kindled" → "his anger burned"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר אַפּוֹ, literally "his nose burned". The Hebrew does not say against whom.
- **"prison" → "prison house"** (v20–23) — Hebrew בֵּית הַסֹּהַר *beit ha-sohar*, "the house of the *sohar*". The word סֹהַר is found only in Gen 39–40; its sense is uncertain (perhaps "round house" or "enclosure").
- **"the king's prisoners were bound"** (v20) — the reading tradition (qere) has אֲסִירֵי "prisoners of"; the written text (ketiv) has אסורי "bound ones of". Both are of the root אָסַר "bind", as is "were bound".
- **"shewed him mercy" → "stretched out *chesed* to him"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיֵּט אֵלָיו חָסֶד: נָטָה "stretch out", חֶסֶד *chesed*.
- **"gave him favour" → "gave his favor"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן חִנּוֹ, "his favor": the favor shown to him.
- **"he was the doer of it" → "he was the one doing it"** (v22) — Hebrew הוּא הָיָה עֹשֶׂה; "all that they did there" is כָּל־אֲשֶׁר עֹשִׂים שָׁם.
- **"looked not to any thing that was under his hand" → "did not look to anything at all in his hand"** (v23) — Hebrew אֵין … רֹאֶה אֶת־כָּל־מְאוּמָה בְּיָדוֹ; "his hand" is Joseph's, whom the Hebrew does not name here.

### Notes

- **"Yahweh was with Joseph"** — said in v2, 3, 21, 23, in the house of his lord and in the prison house.
- **Garment** — Joseph's tunic is stripped from him by his brothers (37:23) and shown as evidence (37:32); here his garment (בֶּגֶד, another word) is left in the woman's hand and shown as evidence.
