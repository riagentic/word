## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v25, 28, 32 (twice) Joseph says הָאֱלֹהִים with the article, "the *Elohim*"; in v16, 38, 39, 51, 52 the word has no article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of clauses with וַ / וְ is kept; וַיְהִי is "And it came to pass" (v1, 8, 13).
- **"river" → "Nile"** (v1, 2, 3, 17, 18) — Hebrew יְאֹר *Ye'or*. Egyptian-loan word specifically for the Nile (from Egyptian *iteru*). Unlike the generic *nahar* (river).
- **"two full years" kept** (v1) — Hebrew שְׁנָתַיִם יָמִים, literally "two years of days". "Was dreaming" is a participle (חֹלֵם), as are "standing", "coming up", "springing up" in the dreams.
- **"kine" → "cows"** (v2, 3, 4, 18, 19, 20, 26, 27) — "Kine" is archaic plural of "cow."
- **The dreams and their retelling** (v2–7, 17–24) — each Hebrew word has one rendering in both: מַרְאֶה "appearance" (v2, 3, 4, 21), תֹּאַר "form" (v18, 19); יָפֶה "beautiful"; רַע "bad" (v3, 4, 19, 20, 21, 27; לָרֹעַ "for badness" v19); בָּרִיא "fat" (of cows v2, 4, 18, 20 and of ears v5, 7); דַּק "thin" (of cows v3, 4 and of ears v6, 7, 23, 24); רַק "lean" (v19, 20, 27); דַּל "poor" (v19); מָלֵא "full" (v7, 22); טוֹב "good" (v5, 22, 24, 26); רֵק "empty" (v27). Where Pharaoh's retelling differs from the narrator's telling (form for appearance, lean for thin, "poor", "I have not seen such", v21, "withered"), the difference is the Hebrew's.
- **"the other kine" → "the cows"** (v3), **"the seven thin ears" → "the thin ears"** (v7), **"again"** (v22 dropped) — "other", "seven" and "again" are not in the Hebrew of these places.
- **"well favoured / ill favoured" → "beautiful / bad of appearance"** (v2, 3, 4) — Hebrew יְפוֹת מַרְאֶה / רָעוֹת מַרְאֶה.
- **"fatfleshed / leanfleshed" → "fat / thin of flesh"** (v2, 3, 4, 18, 19) — Hebrew בְּרִיאֹת בָּשָׂר / דַּקּוֹת בָּשָׂר (v19 רַקּוֹת בָּשָׂר "lean of flesh").
- **"a meadow" → "the reed grass"** (v2, 18) — Hebrew בָּאָחוּ *ba-achu*. *Achu* = riverside reeds / marsh grass, an Egyptian-loan word. Not any meadow.
- **"ears of corn" → "ears"** (v5, 6, 7, 22, 23, 24, 26, 27) — Hebrew שִׁבֳּלִים, ears (of grain); "of corn" is supplied in the KJV.
- **"corn" → "grain"** (v35, 49, 56, 57) — Hebrew בָּר *bar* (v35, 49); in v56, 57 the verb שָׁבַר, "buy / sell grain", from שֶׁבֶר "grain". "Corn" in 1611 English = grain.
- **"rank" → "fat / full"** (v5, 7, 22) — Hebrew בְּרִיאוֹת / מְלֵאוֹת *beri'ot / mele'ot*; the first is the same word as "fat" of the cows.
- **"blasted" → "scorched"** (v6, 23, 27) — Hebrew שְׁדוּפוֹת *shedufot* = scorched (by the hot east wind).
- **"they had eaten them up" → "they went into their inward parts"** (v21) — Hebrew וַתָּבֹאנָה אֶל־קִרְבֶּנָה, literally "they came into their inward part".
- **"withered" kept** (v23) — Hebrew צְנֻמוֹת, found only here; the meaning ("dried up, hardened") is uncertain.
- **"magicians" kept** (v8, 24) — Hebrew חַרְטֻמִּים *chartummim*, an Egyptian-loan word (commonly derived from *ḥry-tp*, the title of the chief lector priest), in the Hebrew Bible only of the learned men of the Egyptian and Babylonian courts who interpret dreams and work secret arts (Exod 7–9; Dan 1–2). The exact sense is uncertain; one rendering, "magicians", is kept wherever the word stands.
- **"spirit was troubled" kept** (v8) — Hebrew וַתִּפָּעֶם רוּחוֹ = "his spirit was struck / agitated."
- **"interpret them"** (v8) — the Hebrew has "his dream" (singular) and then "them" (אוֹתָם, plural).
- **"I do remember my faults" → "My sins I bring to remembrance"** (v9) — Hebrew חֲטָאַי "my sins"; מַזְכִּיר (hiphil) "cause to be remembered, mention".
- **"wroth" → "wrathful"** (v10) — Hebrew קָצַף; a different word from "anger" (אַף).
- **"chief butler / chief baker / captain of the guard" → "chief of the cupbearers / of the bakers / of the guards"** (v9, 10, 12) — Hebrew שַׂר each time (elsewhere "prince"); הַטַּבָּחִים is literally "the slaughterers", the royal bodyguard.
- **"told" → "recounted"** (v8, 12) — Hebrew סִפֵּר; "tell" is kept for הִגִּיד (v24, 25).
- **"changed his raiment" → "changed his cloaks"** (v14) — Hebrew שִׂמְלֹתָיו, plural of שִׂמְלָה, the outer wrap; "garments" (v42) is בֶּגֶד.
- **"I have heard say of thee" → "I have heard it said about you"** (v15) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי עָלֶיךָ לֵאמֹר, literally "I have heard about you, saying".
- **"bank"** (v3, 17) — Hebrew שְׂפַת, literally "lip".
- **"young man" kept; "servant" → "slave"** (v12) — Hebrew נַעַר (elsewhere "boy"), of a grown man; עֶבֶד, of the bought slave Joseph (39:1). Pharaoh's עֲבָדִים, his court, are "servants" (v10, 37, 38).
- **"me he restored … him he hanged"** (v13) — the Hebrew does not name the subject ("he"): Pharaoh, or Joseph by his interpretation.
- **"dungeon" → "pit"** (v14) — Hebrew הַבּוֹר, the same word as the pit of 37:24 and 40:15.
- **"It is not in me" → "It is apart from me"** (v16) — Hebrew בִּלְעָדָי "apart from me"; the same word in v44, "apart from you".
- **"an answer of peace" → "will answer the peace of Pharaoh"** (v16) — Hebrew יַעֲנֶה אֶת־שְׁלוֹם פַּרְעֹה, literally so: *Elohim* will give the answer that is Pharaoh's peace / welfare.
- **"is about to do" → "is doing"** (v25, 28) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה, a participle; of what is at hand.
- **"shall be seven years of famine"** (v27) — the Hebrew changes from "are" (הֵנָּה) to "will be" (יִהְיוּ).
- **"thing" → "word"** (v28) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר; the same word is "thing" in v32, 37, where no speech is meant.
- **"shall not be known … for it shall be very grievous" → "will not be known … very heavy"** (v31) — Hebrew לֹא־יִוָּדַע; כָבֵד "heavy". "Plenty" is שָׂבָע, "satiety".
- **"will shortly bring it to pass" → "is hastening to do it"** (v32) — Hebrew מְמַהֵר … לַעֲשֹׂתוֹ. "From *Elohim*" is מֵעִם, "from with".
- **"look out a man discreet" → "look for a man understanding"** (v33, 39) — Hebrew יֵרֶא "let him see" (as 22:8); נָבוֹן from the root of בִּינָה "understanding".
- **"take up the fifth part of the land" → "take a fifth of the land"** (v34) — Hebrew וְחִמֵּשׁ אֶת־אֶרֶץ, literally "and let him fifth the land"; "of the produce" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"under the hand of Pharaoh" kept** (v35) — Hebrew תַּחַת יַד־פַּרְעֹה. "Heap up" is צָבַר (also v49).
- **"that the land perish not" → "the land will not be cut off"** (v36) — Hebrew וְלֹא־תִכָּרֵת.
- **"Can we find" → "Shall we find"** (v38) — Hebrew הֲנִמְצָא: "shall we find?" or (as a passive) "is there found?"
- **"Spirit of God" → "Spirit of *Elohim*"** (v38) — Hebrew רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים *ruach Elohim*, the same two words as 1:2 and Exod 31:3, without the article; Hebrew has no capital letters. In the mouth of the Egyptian king the words can also be heard as "a spirit of gods" (compare Dan 4:8).
- **"according unto thy word shall all my people be ruled" → "at your command all my people shall submit"** (v40) — Hebrew וְעַל־פִּיךָ יִשַּׁק כָּל־עַמִּי, literally "and upon your mouth all my people shall kiss"; the meaning of the verb here is uncertain. "Only in the throne" is literally "only the throne".
- **"ring" kept; "a gold chain" → "the chain of gold"** (v42) — Hebrew טַבַּעַת "ring"; רְבִד הַזָּהָב has the article. שֵׁשׁ *shesh* = fine linen.
- **"Bow the knee" → "Avrech!"** (v43) — Hebrew אַבְרֵךְ *avrekh*. The meaning is unknown. Proposals:
  1. an Egyptian word ("attention!", "make way!");
  2. Hebrew "bow the knee" (from *barakh*), the traditional rendering;
  3. a title of a high official.
  The file's choice is kept: the word is transliterated, and the traditional rendering stands as the other form. The last clause, "and he set him", is an infinitive absolute (וְנָתוֹן).
- **"Zaphnath-paaneah" → "Tzofnat Paneach"** (v45) — Hebrew צָפְנַת פַּעְנֵחַ. Egyptian name, meaning uncertain.
- **"priest of On"** (v45, 50) — Hebrew כֹּהֵן אֹן *kohen On*. On = Heliopolis (Egyptian *Iunu*).
- **"thirty years old" kept** (v46) — Hebrew בֶּן־שְׁלֹשִׁים שָׁנָה, literally "a son of thirty years".
- **"brought forth" → "produced"** (v47) — Hebrew וַתַּעַשׂ, literally "made".
- **"numbering … without number" → "to number … no number"** (v49) — Hebrew לִסְפֹּר … אֵין מִסְפָּר.
- **"the years of famine" → "the year of famine"** (v50) — Hebrew שְׁנַת, singular.
- **Manasseh / Ephraim** (v51, 52) — the names play on the reasons given:
  - *Menashe* ← נַשַּׁנִי *nashshani* "has made me forget";
  - *Efrayim* ← הִפְרַנִי *hifrani* "has made me fruitful".
  "Toil" is עָמָל; "affliction" is עֳנִי.
- **"all the Egyptians" → "all Egypt"** (v55, 56) — Hebrew מִצְרַיִם, the same word as the land.
- **"all the storehouses" → "all that was in them"** (v56) — Hebrew אֶת־כָּל־אֲשֶׁר בָּהֶם, literally so; the Masoretic Text does not name what "them" refers to (the cities of v48). Translations commonly supply "storehouses" (so the Septuagint, from memory). "Sold grain" is וַיִּשְׁבֹּר, a form that elsewhere means "bought grain" (v57; 42:3).
- **"sore" → "strong"** (v56, 57) — Hebrew חָזַק. הָאָרֶץ is "the earth" in v56a, 57 (all lands, v54) and "the land" where Egypt is meant.

### Notes

- **Joseph's political ascent** — from prison to second-in-Egypt in one day. The narrative compresses vast political change: Joseph is made vizier, granted royal seal, chariot, new name, and aristocratic marriage. Historical setting plausibly Second Intermediate Period (Hyksos rule, c. 1650–1550 BCE), during which a Semitic foreigner could plausibly rise to such power; this is disputed dating.
- **Joseph's Egyptianization** — name, wife, dress, office: Joseph becomes fully Egyptian on the surface. The narrative holds this tension throughout.
- **Manasseh / Ephraim's names** — "*Elohim* has made me forget all my toil and all the house of my father" — including his father. The text reports this without comment.
