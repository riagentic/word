# Genesis 1

[1] In the beginning ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ created the heavens and the earth.

[2] And the earth was waste and void, and darkness was over the face of the deep, and the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was hovering over the face of the waters.

[3] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let there be light," and there was light.

[4] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ saw the light, that it was good, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ separated the light from the darkness.

[5] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ called the light "Day," and the darkness he called "Night." And there was evening and there was morning — one day.

[6] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let there be an expanse in the midst of the waters, and let it separate waters from waters."

[7] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ made the expanse and separated the waters that were under the expanse from the waters that were above the expanse. And it was so.

[8] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ called the expanse "Heavens." And there was evening and there was morning — a second day.

[9] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let the waters under the heavens be gathered to one place, and let the dry land appear." And it was so.

[10] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ called the dry land "Earth," and the gathering of the waters he called "Seas." And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ saw that it was good.

[11] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let the earth sprout vegetation, plants yielding seed, fruit trees making fruit after their kind, whose seed is in them, on the earth." And it was so.

[12] And the earth brought forth vegetation, plants yielding seed after their kind, and trees making fruit, whose seed is in them, after their kind. And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ saw that it was good.

[13] And there was evening and there was morning — a third day.

[14] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let there be lights in the expanse of the heavens to separate the day from the night, and let them be for signs and for appointed times and for days and years,

[15] and let them be for lights in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth." And it was so.

[16] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ made the two great lights, the greater light for the rule of the day and the lesser light for the rule of the night, and the stars.

[17] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ placed them in the expanse of the heavens to give light on the earth,

[18] and to rule over the day and over the night, and to separate the light from the darkness. And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ saw that it was good.

[19] And there was evening and there was morning — a fourth day.

[20] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let the waters swarm with swarming things, living souls, and let birds fly above the earth, over the face of the expanse of the heavens."

[21] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ created the great sea monsters and every living soul that creeps, with which the waters swarmed, after their kind, and every winged bird after its kind. And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ saw that it was good.

[22] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ blessed them, saying, "Be fruitful and multiply and fill the waters in the seas, and let the birds multiply on the earth."

[23] And there was evening and there was morning — a fifth day.

[24] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let the earth bring forth living souls after their kind: beasts and creeping things and living things of the earth after their kind." And it was so.

[25] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ made the living things of the earth after their kind, and the beasts after their kind, and every creeping thing of the ground after its kind. And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ saw that it was good.

[26] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let us make man in our image, according to our likeness, and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over the beasts and over all the earth and over every creeping thing that creeps on the earth."

[27] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ created man in his image; in the image of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ he created him; male and female he created them.

[28] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ blessed them, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth and subdue it, and have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living thing that creeps on the earth."

[29] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Behold, I have given you every plant sowing seed that is on the face of all the earth, and every tree in which is the fruit of a tree sowing seed; to you it shall be for food.

[30] And to every living thing of the earth and to every bird of the heavens and to everything that creeps on the earth, in which there is a living soul, every green plant for food." And it was so.

[31] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ saw all that he had made, and behold, it was very good. And there was evening and there was morning — the sixth day.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*. The form is plural, but here it takes singular verbs (בָּרָא "he created"): the one God. The name is kept in its original form.
- **"In the beginning God created" kept** (v1) — Hebrew בְּרֵאשִׁית בָּרָא אֱלֹהִים. בְּרֵאשִׁית has no article ("in beginning"). Two readings of the same letters: (1) an independent sentence, "In the beginning *Elohim* created the heavens and the earth" (the Masoretic accents separate בְּרֵאשִׁית from what follows, and the verb is pointed as a finite perfect, בָּרָא); (2) a clause of time, "In the beginning of *Elohim*'s creating the heavens and the earth — the earth was waste and void … — *Elohim* said" (רֵאשִׁית is elsewhere mostly bound to a following noun, as Jer 26:1). The verse follows the pointed text, reading 1; reading 2 is possible Hebrew.
- **"created" kept** (v1, 21, 27) — Hebrew בָּרָא: in the Hebrew Bible only *Elohim* is its subject. The verb itself does not say "out of nothing". It is kept apart from עָשָׂה "make" (v7, 16, 25, 26, 31).
- **"the heaven" → "the heavens"** (v1, 8, 9, 14, 15, 17, 20, 26, 28, 30) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם is always plural.
- **"And the earth" kept** (v2 and the beginning of almost every verse) — Hebrew וְ / וַ "and" stands at the head of the clauses and is not dropped.
- **"without form, and void" → "waste and void"** (v2) — Hebrew תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ, two nouns joined by "and": תֹּהוּ is a waste, an empty place, nothingness (Deut 32:10 of the desert); בֹּהוּ is found only beside תֹּהוּ (Jer 4:23, where the same pair describes a land laid waste; Isa 34:11) and means emptiness. Literally "the earth was a waste and an emptiness". Also possible: "formless and empty"; "form" is not in either word.
- **"the deep" kept** (v2) — Hebrew תְהוֹם, without the article: the deep waters. "The face of the deep" and "the face of the waters" are literal (פְּנֵי).
- **"the Spirit of God" → "the Spirit of *Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים. רוּחַ is one word for "spirit", "wind" and "breath". Options: "the Spirit of *Elohim*"; "a wind of *Elohim*" / "a wind from *Elohim*"; "the breath of *Elohim*"; and, taking אֱלֹהִים as a superlative, "a mighty wind". The verse keeps "Spirit" because the participle that follows is not said of wind elsewhere; the capital letter is English only.
- **"moved upon" → "was hovering over"** (v2) — Hebrew מְרַחֶפֶת, a participle (ongoing action) of רִחֵף, found again only in Deut 32:11, of an eagle hovering over its young. "Was moving" is too general; "brooding" is an older guess from Syriac. The participle is feminine, as רוּחַ is.
- **"God saw the light, that it was good" kept** (v4) — the Hebrew has the object first and then the clause; in v10, 12, 18, 21, 25 only "that it was good".
- **"divided the light from the darkness" → "separated the light from the darkness"** (v4, also v6, 7, 14, 18) — Hebrew הִבְדִּיל בֵּין … וּבֵין, literally "made a separation between the light and between the darkness"; one verb, one rendering. In v6 "let it separate" is a participle, literally "let it be separating between waters to waters".
- **"the first day" → "one day"** (v5) — Hebrew יוֹם אֶחָד, the cardinal number "one", not "first". The other days have ordinals without the article, "a second day" (v8, 13, 19, 23); only the sixth has it, on the number: יוֹם הַשִּׁשִּׁי "the sixth day" (v31).
- **"firmament" → "expanse"** (v6, 7, 8, 14, 15, 17, 20) — Hebrew רָקִיעַ, from רָקַע "beat out, spread out" (of metal beaten thin, and of the earth spread out): something spread out, here what holds the waters above. KJV "firmament" comes from the Latin *firmamentum*. Whether a solid vault is meant the word alone does not say.
- **"Heaven" → "Heavens"** (v8) — the same word as in v1.
- **"the dry land" kept; "Earth" kept** (v9, 10) — Hebrew יַבָּשָׁה "the dry", and אֶרֶץ, the same word as "the earth" in v1 and 2; so the name in v10 is "Earth", not "Land".
- **"the gathering together of the waters" → "the gathering of the waters"** (v10) — Hebrew מִקְוֵה הַמַּיִם, a noun from the verb "be gathered" in v9.
- **"Let the earth bring forth grass" → "Let the earth sprout vegetation"** (v11) — Hebrew תַּדְשֵׁא … דֶּשֶׁא, verb and noun of one root: "let the earth green with green growth". דֶּשֶׁא is fresh green growth, grass. It may be the general word that the two following kinds explain (so the verse), or the first of three kinds: "grass, plants, trees". The Hebrew has no "and" between them in v11.
- **"the herb yielding seed" → "plants yielding seed"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew עֵשֶׂב מַזְרִיעַ זֶרַע, "plant causing seed to be"; in v29 the verb is the simple form, זֹרֵעַ זֶרַע "sowing seed". The Hebrew nouns in v11–12, 20–25 are collective singulars ("plant", "tree", "bird", "beast"); English uses the plural, and "after their kind" stands for לְמִינוֹ / לְמִינֵהוּ / לְמִינָהּ "after its kind".
- **"yielding fruit" → "making fruit"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה פְּרִי, the verb עָשָׂה "make".
- **"whose seed is in itself" → "whose seed is in them"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר זַרְעוֹ־בוֹ "which its seed is in it": in the tree, or in the fruit.
- **"lights" kept** (v14, 15, 16) — Hebrew מְאֹרֹת, מָאוֹר: a thing that gives light, a different word from אוֹר "light" in v3–5, 18. English has no second plain word.
- **"for seasons" → "for appointed times"** (v14) — Hebrew מוֹעֲדִים, set times (the word used of the appointed feasts). The Hebrew has "for signs and for appointed times and for days and years", with "for" three times.
- **"the greater light to rule the day" → "the greater light for the rule of the day"** (v16) — Hebrew לְמֶמְשֶׁלֶת, a noun; the adjectives are literally "the great light … the small light". In v18 the verb is מָשַׁל "rule", with בְּ "over".
- **"set them" → "placed them"** (v17) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "gave".
- **"the moving creature that hath life" → "swarming things, living souls"** (v20) — Hebrew יִשְׁרְצוּ … שֶׁרֶץ נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה: "swarm" and "swarming things" are one root, and נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה "living soul" stands beside it as an explanation. נֶפֶשׁ is "soul" here as everywhere; the same two words are said of the land animals (v24), of all animals (v30) and of the man (2:7). KJV has "creature" for the animals and "soul" for the man; the Hebrew makes no such difference.
- **"fowl that may fly" → "let birds fly"** (v20) — Hebrew וְעוֹף יְעוֹפֵף, again noun and verb of one root ("flying things … fly about"); עוֹף is all that flies. "In the open firmament of heaven" is literally "over the face of the expanse of the heavens".
- **"great whales" → "the great sea monsters"** (v21) — Hebrew הַתַּנִּינִם הַגְּדֹלִים. תַּנִּין is a great serpent-like creature (the staff that becomes a serpent in Exod 7:9; the dragon in the sea in Isa 27:1). What animal is meant here is not certain; "sea" is from the context (the waters), not in the word.
- **"every living creature that moveth" → "every living soul that creeps"** (v21) — Hebrew כָּל־נֶפֶשׁ הַחַיָּה הָרֹמֶשֶׂת. רָמַשׂ is one verb in v21, 26, 28, 30 (KJV "move" and "creep"): to move along low, to glide or creep; its noun is רֶמֶשׂ "creeping thing" (v24, 25, 26). "Swarmed" is a Hebrew perfect.
- **"cattle" → "beasts"; "beast of the earth" → "living things of the earth"** (v24, 25, 26, 28, 30) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה, the four-footed animals, chiefly the tame ones, and חַיָּה "living thing" (חַיְתוֹ־אֶרֶץ, חַיַּת הָאָרֶץ); "wild" is not in the word. In v28 חַיָּה is "living thing" too.
- **"Let us make man in our image, after our likeness" → "Let us make man in our image, according to our likeness"** (v26) — Hebrew נַעֲשֶׂה, a first person plural ("let us make"), with plural suffixes ("our"); in v27 the verbs and suffixes are singular ("he created", "his image"). Who is addressed the verse does not say. Readings: *Elohim* speaks with those around him (as 1 Kgs 22:19–22; Job 1:6; Isa 6:8); a plural of deliberation with himself (as 11:7); a plural of fullness within *Elohim*. צֶלֶם "image" (a figure that represents: also of statues) and דְּמוּת "likeness" (from דָּמָה "be like") are two words with two prepositions, בְּ "in" and כְּ "according to, like"; in 5:3 the prepositions change places.
- **"man" kept** (v26, 27) — Hebrew אָדָם, mankind, not the name of one man: "let *them* have dominion", "he created *them*". In v26 it has no article; in v27 it has (הָאָדָם), which English does not use for mankind. The word belongs with אֲדָמָה "ground" (v25; 2:7).
- **"have dominion" kept** (v26, 28) — Hebrew רָדָה, a different verb from מָשַׁל "rule" (v18).
- **"over all the earth" kept** (v26) — so the Hebrew, וּבְכָל־הָאָרֶץ, in the middle of a list of living things. The Syriac version has "over all the living things of the earth" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"in his own image" → "in his image"** (v27) — Hebrew בְּצַלְמוֹ; "own" is not there. The verse has three clauses, each with "created".
- **"male and female"** (v27) — Hebrew זָכָר וּנְקֵבָה, the words used of animals also (6:19), not "man and woman" (אִישׁ, אִשָּׁה: 2:23).
- **"replenish the earth" → "fill the earth"** (v28) — Hebrew מִלְאוּ "fill"; "replenish" meant "fill" in 1611. In v22 the fish are to "fill" the waters, but the birds only to "multiply": so the Hebrew.
- **"every herb bearing seed" → "every plant sowing seed"; "meat" → "food"** (v29, 30) — Hebrew זֹרֵעַ זֶרַע; אָכְלָה is food of any kind (KJV "meat" meant food). "To you it shall be" is singular in Hebrew.
- **"wherein there is life" → "in which there is a living soul"** (v30) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־בּוֹ נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה, the same words as v20, 24. The verse has no verb of its own: "I have given" in v29 carries on.

### Notes (context, not changes)

- **v5, 8, 13 …** — "And there was evening and there was morning" is the literal form; the day is named after the two clauses.
- **v7, 16, 25** — "made" (עָשָׂה) and "created" (בָּרָא, v1, 21, 27) stand side by side in the chapter; 2:3 joins the two.
- **v27** — three short parallel clauses; many print them as verse. The layout here is kept as prose.
