# Genesis 11

[1] And all the earth was of one lip and of the same words.

[2] And it came to pass, as they journeyed from the east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar, and they dwelt there.

[3] And they said, each man to his neighbor, "Come, let us make bricks and burn them thoroughly." And they had the brick for stone, and the bitumen they had for mortar.

[4] And they said, "Come, let us build ourselves a city and a tower with its top in the heavens, and let us make ourselves a name, lest we be scattered over the face of all the earth."

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came down to see the city and the tower which the sons of man had built.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ scattered them from there over the face of all the earth, and they ceased to build the city.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And" kept at the head of the clauses** (throughout) — Hebrew וַ / וְ; the chain of clauses is not turned into subordinate English.
- **"of one language, and of one speech" → "of one lip and of the same words"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׂפָה אֶחָת וּדְבָרִים אֲחָדִים *sapha echat u-devarim achadim*, literally "was one lip and one words": שָׂפָה is "lip" (the organ, here for speech; also v6, 7, 9), דְּבָרִים is "words", and אֲחָדִים is the plural of "one" ("the same", or "few"). "Language" would be an interpretation of "lip".
- **"people" not supplied** (v2) — the Hebrew has only "as they journeyed"; no subject is named.
- **"from the east" kept** (v2) — Hebrew מִקֶּדֶם *miqqedem*; the same word means "eastward" in 13:11, so "as they journeyed east" is also possible.
- **"it came to pass" kept** (v2) — Hebrew וַיְהִי.
- **"one to another" → "each man to his neighbor"** (v3, 7) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ, literally "a man to his neighbor".
- **"Go to" → "Come"** (v3, 4, 7) — Hebrew הָבָה *havah*, literally "give!", an exhortation.
- **"burn them throughly" → "burn them thoroughly"** (v3) — Hebrew וְנִשְׂרְפָה לִשְׂרֵפָה, literally "let us burn to a burning"; "them" is supplied, the Hebrew has no object. "Let us make bricks" is likewise one root twice: נִלְבְּנָה לְבֵנִים "let us brick bricks".
- **"slime… morter" → "bitumen… mortar"** (v3) — Hebrew חֵמָר *chemar* = bitumen / asphalt; חֹמֶר *chomer* = clay, mortar. The two words sound alike.
- **"whose top may reach unto heaven" → "with its top in the heavens"** (v4) — Hebrew וְרֹאשׁוֹ בַשָּׁמַיִם, literally "and its head in the heavens"; there is no verb "reach".
- **"let us make us a name" → "let us make ourselves a name"** (v4) — Hebrew וְנַעֲשֶׂה־לָּנוּ שֵׁם.
- **"children of men" → "sons of man"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הָאָדָם *bene ha-adam*, "the sons of the man / of mankind".
- **"Behold, the people is one" → "Behold, one people, and one lip for them all"** (v6) — Hebrew הֵן עַם אֶחָד וְשָׂפָה אַחַת לְכֻלָּם, a clause without a verb.
- **"this they begin to do" → "this is what they begin to do"** (v6) — Hebrew וְזֶה הַחִלָּם לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "and this is their beginning to do". The Hebrew has no "only".
- **"nothing will be restrained from them" → "nothing … will be withheld from them"** (v6) — Hebrew לֹא־יִבָּצֵר מֵהֶם, niphal of בָּצַר "cut off, make inaccessible" (as Job 42:2); literally "all that they purpose to do will not be withheld from them".
- **"imagined" → "purpose"** (v6) — Hebrew זָמַם *zamam* = purpose, devise. Not the *yetser* of 6:5 / 8:21.
- **"confound their language" → "confuse their lip"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew בָּלַל *balal*, literally "mix".
- **"understand" → "hear"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׁמַע *shama*, "hear"; here in the sense "understand".
- **"left off to build" → "ceased to build"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיַּחְדְּלוּ.
- **"is the name of it called" → "its name was called"** (v9) — Hebrew קָרָא שְׁמָהּ, literally "he (one) called its name".
- **"Babel" — the wordplay** (v9) — Hebrew בָּבֶל *Bavel* is explained by בָּלַל *balal* ("mix, confuse"): "its name was called *Bavel*, because there Yahweh *balal* the lip of all the earth". The Akkadian name of the city is *Babili* = "gate of the god" (*bab-ili*).
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v10–27) — Hebrew וַיּוֹלֶד / הוֹלִיד, hiphil of יָלַד, "beget"; תּוֹלְדֹת "generations" (v10, 27) is from the same root.
- **"an hundred years old" → "a hundred years old, and he begot"** (v10) — Hebrew בֶּן־מְאַת שָׁנָה וַיּוֹלֶד, literally "a son of a hundred years, and he begot"; not "when he begot".
- **Numbers in ordinary English order** (v12–32) — the Hebrew puts the smaller number first and repeats "years": v13 is literally "three years and four hundred years" (403), v12 "five and thirty years" (35). The order carries no meaning.
- **"before his father Terah" → "in the presence of Terah his father"** (v28) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי תֶּרַח אָבִיו, literally "upon the face of Terah his father": while his father was still living (or: before him in time).
- **"land of his nativity" → "land of his kindred"** (v28) — Hebrew מוֹלֶדֶת *moledet*, the same word as "your kindred" in 12:1.
- **"the father of Milcah, and the father of Iscah" kept** (v29) — Hebrew repeats אֲבִי.
- **"his son's son" → "the son of his son"** (v31) — Hebrew בֶּן־בְּנוֹ.
- **"they went forth with them" → "they went out with them"** (v31) — Hebrew וַיֵּצְאוּ אִתָּם, so the Masoretic Text; who "they" and "them" are is not said. The Samaritan text and the Septuagint are commonly cited for "he brought them out" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"they came unto Haran" → "they came as far as Haran"** (v31) — Hebrew עַד־חָרָן. The Hebrew is a plain "and they came … and dwelt", not "but when they came".
- **Name forms modernized**: "Arphaxad" → "Arpachshad," "Salah" → "Shelah." See Gen 10 notes.

### Notes

- **Babel structure** — tight chiastic composition (A. one lip / A'. scattered / B. build a city / B'. ceased to build / C. Yahweh comes down / C'. Yahweh confuses). The architecture mirrors what it narrates: a monument built up, Yahweh's intervention, dispersion out.
- **"Let us go down" (v7)** — plural, paralleling "let us make" (1:26) and "like one of us" (3:22). Same translational options apply.
- **Lifespans continue declining** — from ch 5 (900s) to ch 11 (200s / 400s) — a narrative pattern of shortening human life-spans until Abraham (175) and on to Moses (120).
- **Numbers** — the figures are those of the Masoretic Text.
- **"Ur of the Chaldeans" (v28, 31)** — the "Chaldeans" (*Kasdim*) as a people are attested in Mesopotamia only from the 9th c. BCE onward, centuries after Abraham would have lived. Often treated as an anachronistic updating for a later audience. Location of Ur debated: traditional identification with Sumerian Ur in southern Mesopotamia; alternative (Gordon, Cyrus) with an Urfa / Haran region Ur in northern Mesopotamia, closer to Haran.
- **Haran** (v26–31) — the man is הָרָן *Haran*, the city (v31, 32) is חָרָן *Charan*: two different names in Hebrew.
- **Abram / Sarai** — spellings kept as in MT; the name changes to Abraham / Sarah come in ch 17.
