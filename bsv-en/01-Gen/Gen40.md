# Genesis 40

[1] And it came to pass after these things that the cupbearer of the king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and the baker sinned against their lord, the king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[2] And ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ was wrathful against his two eunuchs, against the chief of the cupbearers and against the chief of the bakers.

[3] And he put them in custody, in the house of the chief of the guards, into the prison house, the place where ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ was bound.

[4] And the chief of the guards appointed ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ with them, and he ministered to them; and they were in custody for days.

[5] And they dreamed a dream, both of them, each man his dream, in one night, each man according to the interpretation of his dream — the cupbearer and the baker who belonged to the king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, who were bound in the prison house.

[6] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ came to them in the morning and saw them, and behold, they were dejected.

[7] And he asked the eunuchs of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ who were with him in the custody of the house of his lord, saying, "Why are your faces bad today?"

[8] And they said to him, "We have dreamed a dream, and there is no interpreter of it." And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ said to them, "Do not interpretations belong to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? Recount it to me, please."

[9] And the chief of the cupbearers recounted his dream to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ and said to him, "In my dream, behold, a vine before me,

[10] and on the vine three branches; and it was as though budding: its blossom came up, its clusters ripened grapes.

[11] And the cup of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ was in my hand, and I took the grapes and pressed them into the cup of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, and I gave the cup upon the palm of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧."

[12] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ said to him, "This is its interpretation: the three branches are three days.

[13] In yet three days ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ will lift up your head and return you to your station, and you shall give the cup of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ into his hand, according to the former manner, when you were his cupbearer.

[14] Only remember me with yourself when it is well with you, and do, please, ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with me, and mention me to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, and bring me out of this house.

[15] For I was surely stolen from the land of the ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧, and here also I have not done anything, that they should have put me in the pit."

[16] And the chief of the bakers saw that he had interpreted well, and he said to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, "I also, in my dream, and behold, three baskets of white bread on my head,

[17] and in the uppermost basket, of all the food of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, the work of a baker; and the birds were eating them out of the basket from upon my head."

[18] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ answered and said, "This is its interpretation: the three baskets are three days.

[19] In yet three days ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ will lift up your head from upon you and hang you on a tree, and the birds will eat your flesh from upon you."

[20] And it came to pass on the third day, the day of the birth of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, that he made a feast for all his servants, and he lifted up the head of the chief of the cupbearers and the head of the chief of the bakers in the midst of his servants.

[21] And he returned the chief of the cupbearers to his cupbearing, and he gave the cup upon the palm of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧.

[22] And the chief of the bakers he hanged, as ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ had interpreted to them.

[23] And the chief of the cupbearers did not remember ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and he forgot him.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"butler" → "cupbearer"** (v1, 2, 5, 9, 13, 20, 21, 23) — Hebrew מַשְׁקֶה *mashqeh*, one who gives drink. In v1, 5 the Hebrew has "the cupbearer" and "the baker"; from v2 on "the chief of the cupbearers" and "the chief of the bakers" (שַׂר הַמַּשְׁקִים, שַׂר הָאוֹפִים). The two forms are kept apart.
- **"had offended" → "sinned against"** (v1) — Hebrew חָטְאוּ לַ, the verb of Gen 39:9.
- **"was wroth against two of his officers" → "was wrathful against his two eunuchs"** (v2, 7) — Hebrew וַיִּקְצֹף; סָרִיס *saris*, "eunuch", also the title of a court officer (eunuch / officer), as Gen 37:36; 39:1.
- **"chief of the butlers … captain of the guard … prison"** (v2–4) — one Hebrew word שַׂר, "chief", in all of them. "The chief of the guards" is literally "of the slaughterers" (Gen 37:36); he is Joseph's lord (v7, Gen 39:1). "The prison house" is בֵּית הַסֹּהַר, a word of uncertain sense found only in Gen 39–40.
- **"in ward" → "in custody"** (v3, 4, 7) — Hebrew מִשְׁמָר, a keeping. "Was bound" (v3, 5) is אָסוּר, the root of "prisoner".
- **"charged Joseph with them" → "appointed Joseph with them"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיִּפְקֹד … אֶת־יוֹסֵף אִתָּם. "He ministered to them" is שֵׁרֵת, as Gen 39:4.
- **"they continued a season in ward" → "they were in custody for days"** (v4) — Hebrew יָמִים, "days": some time.
- **"each man according to the interpretation of his dream"** (v5) — Hebrew אִישׁ כְּפִתְרוֹן חֲלֹמוֹ: each dream with a meaning of its own. "A dream" is singular in v5 and v8.
- **"they were sad" → "they were dejected"** (v6) — Hebrew זֹעֲפִים.
- **"Wherefore look ye so sadly" → "Why are your faces bad"** (v7) — Hebrew מַדּוּעַ פְּנֵיכֶם רָעִים, literally "why are your faces bad / evil": רַע, of looks.
- **"tell me them" → "Recount it to me"** (v8) — Hebrew סַפְּרוּ־נָא לִי, without an object; "it" is supplied. "Interpretations" is plural; the Hebrew is literally "are not interpretations to Elohim?"
- **"it was as though it budded" → "it was as though budding"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהִיא כְפֹרַחַת עָלְתָה נִצָּהּ, hard to construe: "and it, as budding, its blossom came up". "Ripened grapes" is literally "its clusters made grapes ripe".
- **"into Pharaoh's hand" → "upon the palm of Pharaoh"** (v11, 21) — Hebrew עַל־כַּף פַּרְעֹה; "hand" in v11a, 13 is יָד.
- **"Yet within three days" → "In yet three days"** (v13, 19) — Hebrew בְּעוֹד שְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים, the same words in both verses.
- **"lift up your head"** (v13, 19, 20) — Hebrew יִשָּׂא פַרְעֹה אֶת־רֹאשֶׁךָ, the same words three times. In v13 they are followed by "and return you to your station"; in v19 by מֵעָלֶיךָ "from upon you"; in v20 Pharaoh "lifted up the head" of both men. The Hebrew says no more than that; the phrase is rendered the same each time.
- **"restore thee unto thy place" → "return you to your station"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־כַּנֶּךָ, literally "upon your base / stand": his post.
- **"after the former manner"** (v13) — Hebrew כַּמִּשְׁפָּט הָרִאשׁוֹן; מִשְׁפָּט, elsewhere "judgment", here the settled practice.
- **"But think on me" → "Only remember me with yourself"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־זְכַרְתַּנִי אִתְּךָ, literally "only, if you have remembered me with you"; the construction is hard and the sense is a request. "Remember" (v14, 23) and "mention" (v14) are one root, זָכַר.
- **"shew kindness unto me" → "do *chesed* with me"** (v14) — Hebrew וְעָשִׂיתָ־נָּא עִמָּדִי חָסֶד.
- **"bring me out of this house"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהוֹצֵאתַנִי, "and you shall bring me out": the cupbearer is the subject, not Pharaoh.
- **"indeed I was stolen away" → "I was surely stolen"** (v15) — the infinitive absolute with its verb, גֻנֹּב גֻּנַּבְתִּי "stolen, I was stolen".
- **"into the dungeon" → "in the pit"** (v15) — Hebrew בַּבּוֹר, the word used of the pit into which his brothers threw him (Gen 37:24).
- **"that the interpretation was good" → "that he had interpreted well"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי טוֹב פָּתָר, "that he interpreted good": a good meaning.
- **"three white baskets" → "three baskets of white bread"** (v16) — Hebrew סַלֵּי חֹרִי. חֹרִי is found only here and its meaning is uncertain: "white bread" or "wicker" (baskets with holes). One rendering is kept, without certainty.
- **"all manner of bakemeats for Pharaoh" → "of all the food of Pharaoh, the work of a baker"** (v17) — Hebrew מִכֹּל מַאֲכַל פַּרְעֹה מַעֲשֵׂה אֹפֶה. The Hebrew of v16–17 has no verb "was"; "the birds" is the collective עוֹף.
- **"hang thee on a tree"** (v19, 22) — Hebrew וְתָלָה אוֹתְךָ עַל־עֵץ. עֵץ is "tree" and "wood"; a stake may be meant, and the hanging may be the exposure of the body. The Hebrew does not say which.
- **"Pharaoh's birthday" → "the day of the birth of Pharaoh"** (v20) — Hebrew יוֹם הֻלֶּדֶת אֶת־פַּרְעֹה, literally "the day of Pharaoh's being born".
- **"servants"** (v20) — Hebrew עֲבָדָיו, the word "slave", here of a king's men.
- **"restored the chief butler unto his butlership" → "returned the chief of the cupbearers to his cupbearing"** (v21) — Hebrew עַל־מַשְׁקֵהוּ.

### Notes

- **Dreams** — Joseph's own dreams stand in ch 37, these two here, Pharaoh's in ch 41. Joseph says: "Do not interpretations belong to *Elohim*?" (v8).
- **v23** — "did not remember … and he forgot him": two verbs, both kept. Joseph's request of v14 ("remember me … mention me") is left unanswered until ch 41.
