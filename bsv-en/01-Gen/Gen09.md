# Genesis 9

[1] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ blessed ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ and his sons, and said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth.

[2] And the fear of you and the dread of you shall be on every living thing of the earth and on all the birds of the heavens, with all that creeps on the ground and with all the fish of the sea; into your hand they are given.

[3] Every creeping thing that lives shall be food for you; as the green plant I have given you all.

[4] Only flesh with its soul, its blood, you shall not eat.

[5] And surely your blood, of your souls, I will require; from the hand of every living thing I will require it, and from the hand of man, from the hand of each man, his brother, I will require the soul of man.

[6] He who sheds the blood of man, by man his blood shall be shed, for in the image of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ he made man.

[7] And you, be fruitful and multiply; swarm on the earth and multiply in it."

[8] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ and to his sons with him, saying,

[9] "And I, behold, I am establishing my covenant with you and with your seed after you,

[10] and with every living soul that is with you, of birds, of beasts and of every living thing of the earth with you, from all that went out of the ark to every living thing of the earth.

[11] And I will establish my covenant with you, and all flesh shall not be cut off any more by the waters of the flood, and there shall not be any more a flood to ruin the earth."

[12] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "This is the sign of the covenant which I am giving between me and you and every living soul that is with you, for everlasting generations:

[13] My bow I have set in the cloud, and it shall be for a sign of a covenant between me and the earth.

[14] And it shall be, when I bring a cloud over the earth, that the bow shall be seen in the cloud,

[15] and I will remember my covenant which is between me and you and every living soul in all flesh; and the waters shall not any more become a flood to ruin all flesh.

[16] And the bow shall be in the cloud, and I will see it, to remember the everlasting covenant between ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and every living soul in all flesh that is on the earth."

[17] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, "This is the sign of the covenant which I have established between me and all flesh that is on the earth."

[18] And the sons of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ who went out from the ark were Shem and ⟦ham|Cham|Ham⟧ and ⟦japheth|Yefet|Japheth⟧; and ⟦ham|Cham|Ham⟧, he is the father of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧.

[19] These three were the sons of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, and from these all the earth was scattered.

[20] And ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, the man of the ground, began and planted a vineyard.

[21] And he drank of the wine and was drunk, and he uncovered himself in the midst of his tent.

[22] And ⟦ham|Cham|Ham⟧, the father of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brothers outside.

[23] And Shem and ⟦japheth|Yefet|Japheth⟧ took the cloak and put it on the shoulder of them both, and went backward and covered the nakedness of their father; and their faces were backward, and the nakedness of their father they did not see.

[24] And ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ awoke from his wine, and knew what his youngest son had done to him.

[25] And he said,

"Cursed be ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧;
a slave of slaves he shall be to his brothers."

[26] And he said,

"Blessed be ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Shem;
and let ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧ be a slave to them.

[27] May ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ enlarge ⟦japheth|Yefet|Japheth⟧,
and let him abide in the tents of Shem;
and let ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧ be a slave to them."

[28] And ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years.

[29] And all the days of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ were nine hundred and fifty years; and he died.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"replenish" → "fill"** (v1) — Hebrew מָלֵא *male* = fill. "Replenish" in modern English = refill.
- **"every beast of the earth" → "every living thing of the earth"** (v2, 5, 10) — Hebrew חַיָּה (7:14); "beasts" in v10 is בְּהֵמָה.
- **"fowl of the air" → "birds of the heavens"** (v2) — as in Gen 1.
- **"upon all that moveth upon the earth" → "with all that creeps on the ground"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר תִּרְמֹשׂ הָאֲדָמָה, literally "in all with which the ground creeps": the ground is the subject of the verb. בְּ here and before "the fish" is "in / with", not the "on" (עַל) of the first half.
- **"Every moving thing" → "Every creeping thing"** (v3) — Hebrew רֶמֶשׂ, as in 6:7; here of every animal.
- **"meat" → "food"** (v3) — Hebrew אָכְלָה.
- **"even as the green herb have I given you all things" → "as the green plant I have given you all"** (v3) — Hebrew כְּיֶרֶק עֵשֶׂב נָתַתִּי לָכֶם אֶת־כֹּל (1:30).
- **"But flesh with the life thereof, which is the blood thereof" → "Only flesh with its soul, its blood"** (v4) — Hebrew אַךְ־בָּשָׂר בְּנַפְשׁוֹ דָמוֹ. "Its blood" stands beside "its soul" with no joining word; "which is" / "that is" is not in the Hebrew. נֶפֶשׁ *nephesh* is "soul": the life of a living creature (Lev 17:11, 14).
- **"your blood of your lives will I require" → "your blood, of your souls, I will require"** (v5) — Hebrew אֶת־דִּמְכֶם לְנַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶם אֶדְרֹשׁ, literally "your blood for / belonging to your souls". דָּרַשׁ is "seek": seek out, demand back. "Surely" is אַךְ, the "only" of v4.
- **"at the hand of every man's brother" → "from the hand of each man, his brother"** (v5) — Hebrew מִיַּד אִישׁ אָחִיו, literally "from the hand of a man, his brother". Two words for "man" stand in the verse: הָאָדָם (man, mankind) and אִישׁ (a man, each one).
- **"the life of man" → "the soul of man"** (v5) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ הָאָדָם.
- **"Whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed" kept** (v6) — Hebrew שֹׁפֵךְ דַּם הָאָדָם בָּאָדָם דָּמוֹ יִשָּׁפֵךְ; the words mirror each other (shed – blood – man / man – blood – shed). בָּאָדָם is "by man" (the one who does it); it can also be read "for the man" (in exchange for him).
- **"in the image of God made he man"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּצֶלֶם אֱלֹהִים עָשָׂה אֶת־הָאָדָם, as 1:27. The Hebrew does not name the subject of "he made".
- **"bring forth abundantly" → "swarm"** (v7) — Hebrew שִׁרְצוּ, as 1:20.
- **"saying" kept** (v8) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר.
- **"seed" kept** (v9) — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **"creature" → "soul"** (v10, 12, 15, 16) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה, "living soul", the same of animals here as of man in 2:7.
- **"from all that go out of the ark, to every beast of the earth" kept** (v10) — Hebrew מִכֹּל יֹצְאֵי הַתֵּבָה לְכֹל חַיַּת הָאָרֶץ.
- **"neither shall all flesh be cut off any more"** (v11) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִכָּרֵת כָּל־בָּשָׂר עוֹד, "all flesh shall not be cut off": not ever again the whole.
- **"destroy" → "ruin"** (v11, 15) — Hebrew שָׁחַת, the verb of 6:11–13, 17.
- **"token" → "sign"** (v12, 13, 17) — Hebrew אוֹת *ot*, the word of 4:15.
- **"which I make" → "which I am giving"** (v12) — Hebrew נֹתֵן, "give / set". The same verb is "I have set" in v13 (נָתַתִּי). "Which" may be the sign or the covenant.
- **"for perpetual generations" → "for everlasting generations"** (v12) — Hebrew לְדֹרֹת עוֹלָם, literally "for generations of the age"; so "the everlasting covenant" (v16), בְּרִית עוֹלָם.
- **"my bow"** (v13) — Hebrew קֶשֶׁת *qeshet* = bow, the weapon; Hebrew has no separate word for the rainbow.
- **"a token of a covenant" → "a sign of a covenant"** (v13) — Hebrew לְאוֹת בְּרִית, without the article.
- **"when I bring a cloud"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּעַנְנִי עָנָן, verb and noun of one root, literally "when I cloud a cloud".
- **"I will look upon it" → "I will see it"** (v16) — Hebrew וּרְאִיתִיהָ.
- **"of all flesh" → "in all flesh"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew בְּכָל־בָּשָׂר.
- **"overspread" → "was scattered"** (v19) — Hebrew נָפְצָה, "scattered": the people of the whole earth spread out from these. The verb of 11:4, 8, 9.
- **"began to be an husbandman, and he planted" → "the man of the ground, began and planted"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיָּחֶל נֹחַ אִישׁ הָאֲדָמָה וַיִּטַּע כָּרֶם. Two readings: "Noah, the man of the ground, began and planted a vineyard" (he was the first to plant one), or "Noah began to be a man of the ground, and he planted a vineyard". The verse keeps the Hebrew order, which allows both.
- **"he was uncovered" → "he uncovered himself"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיִּתְגַּל, a reflexive form.
- **"a garment" → "the cloak"** (v23) — Hebrew הַשִּׂמְלָה, with the article. "The shoulder of them both": שְׁכֶם שְׁנֵיהֶם, singular.
- **"their faces were backward" kept** (v23) — Hebrew וּפְנֵיהֶם אֲחֹרַנִּית, the same word as "went backward".
- **"his younger son" → "his youngest son"** (v24) — Hebrew בְּנוֹ הַקָּטָן, literally "his son, the small / young one". With the article and among three it is commonly "the youngest"; it can also be "the younger". The sons are elsewhere listed Shem, Ham, Japheth.
- **"a servant of servants" → "a slave of slaves"** (v25) — Hebrew עֶבֶד עֲבָדִים; so "slave" in v26, 27.
- **v25 "Cursed be Canaan"** — the narrative says Ham saw, and Noah curses Canaan, Ham's son. The text does not explain; the translation is literal.
- **v26 "Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Shem"** — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי שֵׁם.
- **"his servant" → "a slave to them"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew עֶבֶד לָמוֹ. לָמוֹ is a poetic form that is ordinarily "to them"; it is also taken as "to him" (Shem in v26; Japheth or Shem in v27). The verse keeps the ordinary sense of the form.
- **v27 "enlarge Japheth"** — Hebrew יַפְתְּ אֱלֹהִים לְיֶפֶת *yaft Elohim le-Yefet*, literally "may *Elohim* make wide for Japheth": the verb (פָּתָה, in this sense only here) sounds like the name. "Let him abide": the Hebrew does not say whether "he" is Japheth or *Elohim*. שָׁכַן "abide" is another word than יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"three hundred and fifty years" kept** (v28, 29) — Hebrew שְׁלֹשׁ מֵאוֹת שָׁנָה וַחֲמִשִּׁים שָׁנָה, literally "three hundred years and fifty years"; the Hebrew says "years" twice.

### Notes

- **Noahic covenant (v8–17)** — the first covenant named in the Bible, made with all flesh, not only with man; no obligation of the other side is stated in v8–17. Contrast the later covenants with Abraham and at Sinai.
- **"saw the nakedness of his father" (v22–25)** — two readings: (a) he looked, did not cover, and told; (b) a stronger sense of the phrase (in Lev 18:7, 8; 20:11 "to uncover the nakedness of one's father" is said of lying with the father's wife). The text says no more than that he saw and told.
- **Curse of Canaan — historical misuse** — this passage was used for centuries in defense of slavery ("curse of Ham" applied to Africans). The text names *Canaan*, not Ham generally, and says nothing about skin color.
