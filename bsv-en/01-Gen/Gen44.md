# Genesis 44

[1] And he commanded the one who was over his house, saying, "Fill the bags of the men with food, as much as they are able to carry, and put each man's silver in the mouth of his bag.

[2] And my goblet, the goblet of silver, you shall put in the mouth of the bag of the youngest, and the silver for his grain." And he did according to the word of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ that he had spoken.

[3] The morning was light, and the men were sent away, they and their donkeys.

[4] They had gone out of the city; they had not gone far, and ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ said to the one who was over his house, "Arise, pursue after the men, and when you overtake them, say to them, 'Why have you repaid evil for good?

[5] Is not this the one from which my lord drinks? And he, he surely divines by it. You have done evil in what you have done.'"

[6] And he overtook them and spoke to them these words.

[7] And they said to him, "Why does my lord speak such words as these? Far be it from your servants to do such a thing as this.

[8] Behold, the silver that we found in the mouth of our bags we returned to you from the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧; and how should we steal from the house of your lord silver or gold?

[9] With whomever of your servants it is found, he shall die, and we also will be slaves to my lord."

[10] And he said, "Now also, according to your words, so it is: the one with whom it is found shall be a slave to me, and you shall be innocent."

[11] And they hastened and brought down each man his bag to the earth, and they opened each man his bag.

[12] And he searched; with the eldest he began and with the youngest he finished. And the goblet was found in the bag of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧.

[13] And they tore their cloaks, and each man loaded his donkey, and they returned to the city.

[14] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and his brothers came to the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and he was yet there, and they fell before him to the earth.

[15] And ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ said to them, "What is this deed that you have done? Do you not know that a man who is like me surely divines?"

[16] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ said, "What shall we say to my lord? What shall we speak? And how shall we justify ourselves? ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has found the iniquity of your servants. Behold, we are slaves to my lord, both we and the one in whose hand the goblet was found."

[17] And he said, "Far be it from me to do this. The man in whose hand the goblet was found, he shall be a slave to me; and you, go up in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to your father."

[18] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ drew near to him and said, "Oh, my lord, please let your servant speak a word in the ears of my lord, and let not your anger burn against your servant, for you are like ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧.

[19] My lord asked his servants, saying, 'Have you a father or a brother?'

[20] And we said to my lord, 'We have a father, an old man, and a child of his old age, a little one; and his brother is dead, and he alone remains of his mother, and his father loves him.'

[21] And you said to your servants, 'Bring him down to me, and let me set my eye upon him.'

[22] And we said to my lord, 'The boy is not able to leave his father; and should he leave his father, he would die.'

[23] And you said to your servants, 'If your youngest brother does not come down with you, you shall not see my face again.'

[24] And it came to pass, when we went up to your servant my father, that we told him the words of my lord.

[25] And our father said, 'Return, buy us a little food.'

[26] And we said, 'We are not able to go down. If our youngest brother is with us, then we will go down; for we are not able to see the face of the man when our youngest brother is not with us.'

[27] And your servant my father said to us, 'You know that my wife bore me two.

[28] And the one went out from me, and I said, "Surely he has been torn to pieces," and I have not seen him until now.

[29] And should you take this one also from before my face, and harm befall him, you will bring down my gray hair in evil to Sheol.'

[30] And now, when I come to your servant my father, and the boy is not with us — and his soul is bound up with his soul —

[31] it will come to pass, when he sees that the boy is not, that he will die; and your servants will bring down the gray hair of your servant our father in sorrow to Sheol.

[32] For your servant became surety for the boy with my father, saying, 'If I do not bring him to you, I shall have sinned against my father all the days.'

[33] And now, please let your servant remain in place of the boy, a slave to my lord, and let the boy go up with his brothers.

[34] For how shall I go up to my father when the boy is not with me? Lest I look on the evil that will find my father."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v16) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article.
- **"the steward of his house" → "the one who was over his house"** (v1, 4) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עַל־בֵּיתוֹ, as 43:16.
- **"sacks" → "bags"** (v1, 2, 8, 11, 12) — Hebrew אַמְתַּחַת throughout, as in ch 43.
- **"cup" → "goblet"** (v2, 12, 16, 17) — Hebrew גָּבִיעַ, a different word from "cup" (כּוֹס, 40:11).
- **"his corn money" → "the silver for his grain"** (v2) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף שִׁבְרוֹ, literally "the silver of his grain"; כֶּסֶף is silver by weight, here the price (also v1, 8).
- **"As soon as the morning was light"** (v3) — Hebrew הַבֹּקֶר אוֹר, two words: "the morning was light". In v4 the Hebrew sets two short clauses side by side: "they had gone out of the city; they had not gone far".
- **"Up, follow" → "Arise, pursue"** (v4) — Hebrew קוּם רְדֹף.
- **"whereby indeed he divineth" → "he surely divines by it"** (v5), **"can certainly divine" → "surely divines"** (v15) — Hebrew נַחֵשׁ יְנַחֵשׁ, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "divining he divines"; נִחֵשׁ is to practise divination, to seek omens. The verb is left as it stands; "can" is not in the Hebrew. The Septuagint has a longer text at the start of v5 (from memory: a question about the stolen cup); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"God forbid" → "Far be it"** (v7, 17) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לְ; "God" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"thy servants" / "bondmen" → "your servants" / "slaves"** (throughout) — one Hebrew word, עֶבֶד: "your servant(s)" where the brothers speak of themselves or of their father before the ruler, "slave(s)" where bondage is meant (v9, 10, 16, 17, 33). In v16 both stand together: "the iniquity of your servants … we are slaves to my lord".
- **"let him die" → "he shall die"** (v9) — Hebrew וָמֵת.
- **"blameless" → "innocent"** (v10) — Hebrew נְקִיִּם.
- **"began at the eldest, and left at the youngest"** (v12) — Hebrew בַּגָּדוֹל … וּבַקָּטֹן, literally "with the great one … with the small one".
- **"clothes" → "cloaks"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׂמְלֹתָם, as 41:14.
- **"wot ye not" → "Do you not know"** (v15) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא יְדַעְתֶּם.
- **"clear ourselves" → "justify ourselves"** (v16) — Hebrew נִצְטַדָּק, from the root "be righteous".
- **"found out" → "found"** (v16) — Hebrew מָצָא, the same verb as "found" of the silver and the goblet (v8, 9, 10, 12, 16, 17) and as "the evil that will find my father" (v34).
- **"in peace" → "in *shalom*"** (v17) — Hebrew לְשָׁלוֹם.
- **"Oh my lord" kept** (v18) — Hebrew בִּי אֲדֹנִי, literally "upon me, my lord". "Please" is נָא (also v33). "Let not your anger burn" is literally "let not your nose burn" (אַף). "You are like Pharaoh" is literally "like you is like Pharaoh".
- **"a child of his old age, a little one" kept** (v20) — Hebrew יֶלֶד זְקֻנִים קָטָן; יֶלֶד "child", beside נַעַר "boy" (v22, 30–34; KJV "lad").
- **"is left" → "remains"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּוָּתֵר, a different word from "is left" in 42:38.
- **"that I may set mine eyes upon him" → "let me set my eye upon him"** (v21) — Hebrew עֵינִי, singular.
- **"his father would die" → "he would die"** (v22) — Hebrew וְעָזַב אֶת־אָבִיו וָמֵת, "and he leaves his father and he dies": the Hebrew does not say whether the father or the boy would die.
- **"ye shall see my face no more" → "you shall not see my face again"** (v23) — Hebrew לֹא תֹסִפוּן לִרְאוֹת, literally "you shall not add to see".
- **"Go again" → "Return"** (v25) — Hebrew שֻׁבוּ, as 43:2.
- **"my wife bare me two sons" → "my wife bore me two"** (v27) — "sons" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Surely he is torn in pieces" → "Surely he has been torn to pieces"** (v28) — Hebrew אַךְ טָרֹף טֹרָף, the infinitive absolute with its verb, as 37:33.
- **"if ye take … and mischief befall him" → "should you take … and harm befall him"** (v29) — the Hebrew has no "if"; אָסוֹן "harm", as 42:4, 38. "From before my face" is מֵעִם פָּנַי.
- **"with sorrow to the grave" → "in evil to Sheol" / "in sorrow to Sheol"** (v29, 31) — Hebrew בְּרָעָה "in evil" (v29) and בְּיָגוֹן "in sorrow" (v31, as 42:38); שְׁאֹלָה "to Sheol"; שֵׂיבָה "gray hair", singular.
- **"his life is bound up in the lad's life" → "his soul is bound up with his soul"** (v30) — Hebrew וְנַפְשׁוֹ קְשׁוּרָה בְנַפְשׁוֹ, literally so; the Hebrew does not name whose soul is whose (the father's with the boy's).
- **"that the lad is not with us" → "that the boy is not"** (v31) — Hebrew כִּי־אֵין הַנַּעַר; "with us" is not in the Masoretic Text.
- **"then I shall bear the blame to my father for ever" → "I shall have sinned against my father all the days"** (v32) — Hebrew וְחָטָאתִי לְאָבִי כָּל־הַיָּמִים, as 43:9. "With my father" is מֵעִם אָבִי, "from with my father".
- **"abide" → "remain"** (v33) — Hebrew יֵשֶׁב, elsewhere "dwell" or "sit"; here of the one who stays behind when the others go up.
- **"lest peradventure I see the evil that shall come on my father" → "Lest I look on the evil that will find my father"** (v34) — Hebrew פֶּן אֶרְאֶה בָרָע אֲשֶׁר יִמְצָא אֶת־אָבִי; the sentence breaks off with "lest".

### Notes

- **Judah's speech (v18–34)** — the longest speech in Genesis. Judah, who once proposed selling Joseph (37:26–27), now offers himself as a slave in place of Benjamin.
- **"*Elohim* has found the iniquity of your servants" (v16)** — Judah does not say which iniquity; of the goblet the brothers are innocent.
- **"You are like Pharaoh" (v18)** — Judah's way of approaching the highest authority.
