# Esther 4

[1] And ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ knew all that had been done, and ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ tore his garments and put on sackcloth and ashes, and went out into the midst of the city and cried out with a great and bitter outcry.

[2] And he came as far as before the king's gate, for none might come into the king's gate in clothing of sackcloth.

[3] And in each province, the place where the word of the king and his law arrived, there was great mourning for the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and fasting and weeping and lamenting; sackcloth and ashes were spread out as a bed for many.

[4] And the girls of ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ and her eunuchs came and told her, and the queen was in great anguish; and she sent garments to clothe ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ and to remove his sackcloth from upon him, and he did not accept.

[5] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ called for ⟦hathach|Hatach|Hathach⟧, of the king's eunuchs, whom he had made to stand before her, and she commanded him concerning ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, to know what this was and why this was.

[6] And ⟦hathach|Hatach|Hathach⟧ went out to ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, to the square of the city that was before the king's gate.

[7] And ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ told him all that had befallen him, and the exact account of the silver that Haman had said he would weigh into the king's treasuries for the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, to do away with them.

[8] And the copy of the writing of the law that had been given in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ to destroy them he gave him, to show it to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ and to tell her, and to command her to go in to the king, to plead with him and to seek from before him for her people.

[9] And ⟦hathach|Hatach|Hathach⟧ came and told ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the words of ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧.

[10] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ spoke to ⟦hathach|Hatach|Hathach⟧ and commanded him for ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧:

[11] "All the king's servants and the people of the king's provinces know that any man or woman who comes to the king into the inner court, who is not called — one is his law: to put to death, except the one to whom the king holds out the golden scepter, and he shall live. And I have not been called to come to the king these thirty days."

[12] And they told ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the words of ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧.

[13] And ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ said to answer ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧, "Do not imagine in your soul that you will escape in the king's house, out of all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[14] For if you surely keep silent at this time, relief and deliverance will arise for the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ from another place, and you and the house of your father will perish. And who knows whether for a time like this you have arrived at the kingdom?"

[15] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ said to answer ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧,

[16] "Go, gather all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who are found in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧, and fast for me, and do not eat and do not drink three days, night and day; I also and my girls will fast so. And so I will go in to the king, which is not according to the law; and as I perish, I perish."

[17] And ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ passed on and did according to all that ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ had commanded him.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. The chapter names no god, and none is supplied (v14).
- **"When Mordecai perceived" → "And Mordecai knew"** (v1) — Hebrew יָדַע, a finite verb, not a time clause. **"rent his clothes" → "tore his garments"** — בְּגָדִים "garments" (v1, 4). **"cried with a loud and a bitter cry" → "cried out with a great and bitter outcry"** — Hebrew זָעַק זְעָקָה גְדֹלָה וּמָרָה, verb and noun of one root.
- **"came even before … for none might enter into the king's gate clothed with sackcloth" → "came as far as before … for none might come into the king's gate in clothing of sackcloth"** (v2) — Hebrew עַד לִפְנֵי; אֵין לָבוֹא, literally "there is no coming"; בִּלְבוּשׁ שָׂק "in clothing of sackcloth".
- **"whithersoever the king's commandment and his decree came, there was great mourning among the Jews … and wailing; and many lay in sackcloth and ashes" → "the place where the word of the king and his law arrived, there was great mourning for the Jews … and lamenting; sackcloth and ashes were spread out as a bed for many"** (v3) — Hebrew מַגִּיעַ "arrive, reach" (as 2:12; v14); לַיְּהוּדִים "for / to the Jews"; מִסְפֵּד "lamenting" (the loud wailing, of סָפַד); יֻצַּע "was spread out as a bed" (hofal of יָצַע); "lay" is not written. מְדִינָה וּמְדִינָה "each province", as 3:12.
- **"Esther's maids and her chamberlains … Then was the queen exceedingly grieved … to take away his sackcloth … but he received it not" → "the girls of Esther and her eunuchs … the queen was in great anguish … to remove his sackcloth … and he did not accept"** (v4) — Hebrew נַעֲרוֹת "girls" (attendants); סָרִיס "eunuch" (v4, 5); הִתְחַלְחֵל, literally "writhe" (as in pangs); הֵסִיר "remove"; קִבֵּל "accept", no object.
- **"whom he had appointed to attend upon her, and gave him a commandment to Mordecai, to know what it was, and why it was" → "whom he had made to stand before her, and she commanded him concerning Mordecai, to know what this was and why this was"** (v5) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד לְפָנֶיהָ "made to stand before her" (= attend on her); עַל־מָרְדֳּכָי; עַל־מַה־זֶּה, literally "on account of what this is".
- **"the street of the city" → "the square of the city"** (v6) — רְחוֹב, the open place before a gate.
- **"all that had happened unto him, and of the sum of the money that Haman had promised to pay to the king's treasuries for the Jews, to destroy them" → "all that had befallen him, and the exact account of the silver that Haman had said he would weigh … for the Jews, to do away with them"** (v7) — Hebrew קָרָה "befall"; פָּרָשַׁת הַכֶּסֶף "the exact account of the silver" (פָּרָשָׁה, of פָּרַשׁ "declare distinctly"); אָמַר "said"; שָׁקַל "weigh", as 3:9; גִּנְזֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ "the king's treasuries" (a Persian loan word); לְאַבְּדָם as 3:9. Ketiv ביהודיים, qere בַּיְּהוּדִים: the same word, "for the Jews".
- **"the copy of the writing of the decree … to declare it unto her, and to charge her that she should go in unto the king, to make supplication unto him, and to make request before him for her people" → "the copy of the writing of the law … to tell her, and to command her to go in to the king, to plead with him and to seek from before him for her people"** (v8) — Hebrew פַּתְשֶׁגֶן כְּתָב־הַדָּת, as 3:14; the Hebrew order puts the object first ("And the copy … he gave him"); הִתְחַנֵּן "plead with" (literally "seek favor for oneself"); בִּקֵּשׁ "seek".
- **"Esther spake unto Hatach, and gave him commandment unto Mordecai" → "Esther spoke to Hatach and commanded him for Mordecai"** (v10) — Hebrew וַתֹּאמֶר … וַתְּצַוֵּהוּ אֶל־מָרְדֳּכָי.
- **"whosoever, whether man or woman, shall come … who is not called, there is one law of his to put him to death … that he may live: but I have not been called" → "any man or woman who comes … who is not called — one is his law: to put to death … and he shall live. And I have not been called"** (v11) — Hebrew אַחַת דָּתוֹ לְהָמִית, literally "one is his law, to put to death" (no object; "his" may be the man's or the king's); הוֹשִׁיט "hold out"; שַׁרְבִיט "scepter"; וְחָיָה "and he shall live". זֶה שְׁלוֹשִׁים יוֹם "these thirty days".
- **"And they told to Mordecai Esther's words"** (v12) — וַיַּגִּידוּ, plural, kept; who "they" are is not said.
- **"Then Mordecai commanded to answer Esther, Think not with thyself that thou shalt escape in the king's house, more than all the Jews" → "And Mordecai said to answer Esther, Do not imagine in your soul that you will escape in the king's house, out of all the Jews"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר "said"; אַל־תְּדַמִּי בְנַפְשֵׁךְ "do not imagine in your soul"; לְהִמָּלֵט "to escape" (נִמְלַט); מִכָּל־הַיְּהוּדִים "out of all the Jews" = alone of them all.
- **"For if thou altogether holdest thy peace at this time, then shall there enlargement and deliverance arise to the Jews from another place; but thou and thy father's house shall be destroyed: and who knoweth whether thou art come to the kingdom for such a time as this?" → "For if you surely keep silent at this time, relief and deliverance will arise for the Jews from another place, and you and the house of your father will perish. And who knows whether for a time like this you have arrived at the kingdom?"** (v14) — Hebrew הַחֲרֵשׁ תַּחֲרִישִׁי, literally "keeping silent you keep silent" (infinitive absolute: "surely"). רֶוַח, literally "space, room": relief. הַצָּלָה "deliverance", of נָצַל. יַעֲמוֹד, literally "will stand": "arise". מִמָּקוֹם אַחֵר "from another place", word for word: the Hebrew names no one, and nothing is supplied. תֹּאבֵדוּ "you will perish" (אָבַד, as v16); "and", not "but". הִגַּעַתְּ "you have arrived, reached" (as v3). The Hebrew order puts "for a time like this" first.
- **"Then Esther bade them return Mordecai this answer" → "And Esther said to answer Mordecai"** (v15) — as v13.
- **"gather together all the Jews that are present in Shushan … neither eat nor drink three days, night or day: I also and my maidens will fast likewise; and so will I go in … and if I perish, I perish" → "gather all the Jews who are found in Shushan … and do not eat and do not drink three days, night and day; I also and my girls will fast so. And so I will go in … and as I perish, I perish"** (v16) — Hebrew כְּנוֹס "gather"; הַנִּמְצְאִים "who are found"; צוּמוּ עָלַי "fast for me" (literally "upon me"); לַיְלָה וָיוֹם "night and day"; נַעֲרֹתַי "my girls"; כֵּן … וּבְכֵן "so … and so"; כַּדָּת "according to the law" (דָּת as 3:8); וְכַאֲשֶׁר אָבַדְתִּי אָבָדְתִּי, literally "and as I have perished, I have perished": the perfect of resolve; English "if" is not written.
- **"So Mordecai went his way" → "And Mordecai passed on"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבֹר, literally "passed over"; where he passed (the river? the square?) is not said. "According to all that" — כְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר.

### Notes
- **Sackcloth and ashes** — public mourning; Persian court protocol excludes such near the king.
- **Approaching the king unsummoned** — capital offense; Greek authors report the Persian practice (Herodotus III.118, Xenophon Cyropaedia).
- **"Thirty days"** — Esther not recently called; her position uncertain.
- **"Another place"** (v14) — the Hebrew says only מָקוֹם אַחֵר; whether it points to divine providence or to another human helper is not said. The book names no god anywhere.
- **"For a time like this"** — signature verse; providence-and-agency tension. The narrator offers the suggestion without theological certainty.
- **Three-day fast** — solidarity, resolve. Communal pre-action preparation.
- **"As I perish, I perish"** — Esther's resolve. Role-reversal from cousin and ward (2:7) to decisive queen.
