# Esther 6

[1] On that night the sleep of the king fled, and he said to bring the book of the memorials, the chronicles; and they were being read before the king.

[2] And it was found written that ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ had told about ⟦bigthan|Bigtana|Bigthana⟧ and Teresh, two of the king's eunuchs, of the keepers of the threshold, who had sought to stretch out a hand against King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧.

[3] And the king said, "What honor and greatness has been done to ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ for this?" And the king's young men, who ministered to him, said, "Nothing has been done with him."

[4] And the king said, "Who is in the court?" And Haman had come into the outer court of the king's house to say to the king to hang ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ on the tree that he had prepared for him.

[5] And the king's young men said to him, "Behold, Haman is standing in the court." And the king said, "Let him come in."

[6] And Haman came in, and the king said to him, "What is to be done for the man whose honor the king delights in?" And Haman said in his heart, "To whom would the king delight to do honor more than to me?"

[7] And Haman said to the king, "The man whose honor the king delights in —

[8] let them bring royal clothing that the king has worn, and a horse that the king has ridden on, and on whose head a royal crown has been set;

[9] and let the clothing and the horse be given into the hand of a man of the king's princes, the nobles, and let them clothe the man whose honor the king delights in, and make him ride on the horse in the square of the city, and call out before him, 'Thus shall it be done to the man whose honor the king delights in!'"

[10] And the king said to Haman, "Hasten, take the clothing and the horse, as you have spoken, and do so to ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, who sits in the king's gate. Do not let a word fall from all that you have spoken."

[11] And Haman took the clothing and the horse, and clothed ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, and made him ride in the square of the city, and called out before him, "Thus shall it be done to the man whose honor the king delights in!"

[12] And ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ returned to the king's gate; and Haman hastened to his house, mourning and with his head covered.

[13] And Haman recounted to Zeresh his wife and to all those who love him all that had befallen him. And his wise men and Zeresh his wife said to him, "If ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, before whom you have begun to fall, is of the seed of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, you will not overcome him, but you will surely fall before him."

[14] While they were still speaking with him, the king's eunuchs arrived, and they hurried to bring Haman to the feast that ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ had made.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. The book of Esther names no divine name; none is added.
- **"On that night could not the king sleep" → "On that night the sleep of the king fled"** (v1) — Hebrew נָדְדָה שְׁנַת הַמֶּלֶךְ, lit. "the sleep of the king wandered off".
- **"he commanded to bring" → "he said to bring"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר לְהָבִיא; אָמַר is "say".
- **"the book of records of the chronicles" → "the book of the memorials, the chronicles"** (v1) — Hebrew סֵפֶר הַזִּכְרֹנוֹת דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים: זִכָּרוֹן "memorial"; דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, lit. "the words / matters of the days", as in Kings ("the book of the chronicles of the kings"). **"they were read" → "they were being read"** — וַיִּהְיוּ נִקְרָאִים, a participle: the reading went on.
- **"chamberlains" → "eunuchs"** (v2, 14) — Hebrew סָרִיס. The word is also used of a court officer; whether these men were castrated, the word alone does not say ("eunuch / officer").
- **"Bigthana" (v2)** — Hebrew בִּגְתָנָא here, בִּגְתָן in 2:21; the token is kept.
- **"the keepers of the door" → "of the keepers of the threshold"** (v2) — Hebrew מִשֹּׁמְרֵי הַסַּף; the מִן is "of (among)". **"to lay hand on" → "to stretch out a hand against"** — שָׁלַח יָד בְּ.
- **"What honour and dignity hath been done" → "What honor and greatness has been done"** (v3) — Hebrew יְקָר וּגְדוּלָּה. **"There is nothing done for him" → "Nothing has been done with him"** — לֹא־נַעֲשָׂה עִמּוֹ דָּבָר, lit. "not a thing has been done with him".
- **"the king's servants that ministered unto him" → "the king's young men, who ministered to him"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew נַעֲרֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ; נַעַר is "boy", of attendants "young man".
- **"gallows" → "tree"** (v4) — Hebrew עֵץ, "tree" and "wood": a stake or pole of wood is meant (fifty cubits high, 5:14). Whether a man was hanged on it or impaled, the word does not say. **"to speak unto the king to hang" → "to say to the king to hang"** — לֵאמֹר.
- **"whom the king delighteth to honour" → "whose honor the king delights in"** (v6, 7, 9, 11) — Hebrew חָפֵץ בִּיקָרוֹ, lit. "delights in his honor"; in v6b לַעֲשׂוֹת יְקָר "to do honor".
- **"Haman thought in his heart" → "Haman said in his heart"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … בְּלִבּוֹ.
- **"For the man" → "The man … —"** (v7) — Hebrew אִישׁ with no preposition: Haman begins with the man and breaks off; the dash keeps the broken sentence.
- **"Let the royal apparel be brought which the king useth to wear" → "let them bring royal clothing that the king has worn"** (v8) — Hebrew יָבִיאוּ "let them bring"; לְבוּשׁ מַלְכוּת, lit. "clothing of kingship". **"the crown royal which is set upon his head" → "and on whose head a royal crown has been set"** — וַאֲשֶׁר נִתַּן כֶּתֶר מַלְכוּת בְּרֹאשׁוֹ. The clause follows "a horse"; whether the head is the horse's or the king's, the Hebrew does not say. כֶּתֶר is Esther's word for the royal crown (1:11; 2:17).
- **"delivered to the hand of one of the king's most noble princes" → "given into the hand of a man of the king's princes, the nobles"** (v9) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ הַפַּרְתְּמִים; פַּרְתְּמִים is a Persian loan word, "the foremost ones" (1:3). וְנָתוֹן, an infinitive used as a command. **"bring him on horseback through the street" → "make him ride on the horse in the square"** (v9, 11) — הִרְכִּיב "make ride"; רְחוֹב is the open square. **"proclaim" → "call out"** — קָרָא.
- **"Make haste" → "Hasten"** (v10) — Hebrew מַהֵר. **"do even so" → "do so"**. **"that sitteth at the king's gate" → "who sits in the king's gate"** — בְּשַׁעַר. **"let nothing fail" → "Do not let a word fall"** — אַל־תַּפֵּל דָּבָר.
- **"came again" → "returned"** (v12) — Hebrew שׁוּב. **"hasted" → "hastened"** — נִדְחַף, lit. "was pushed". **"having his head covered" → "with his head covered"** — חֲפוּי רֹאשׁ.
- **"told" → "recounted"** (v13) — Hebrew סִפֵּר. **"all his friends" → "all those who love him"** — אֹהֲבָיו, the participle of אָהַב "love" (= his friends).
- **"thou shalt not prevail against him, but shalt surely fall" → "you will not overcome him, but you will surely fall"** (v13) — Hebrew לֹא־תוּכַל לוֹ, lit. "you will not be able for him"; נָפוֹל תִּפּוֹל, lit. "falling you will fall".
- **"hasted to bring" → "hurried to bring"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיַּבְהִלוּ (hifil of בָּהַל). **"banquet" → "feast"** — מִשְׁתֶּה, "a drinking". **"prepared" → "made"** — עָשְׂתָה.

### Notes
- **The king's sleepless night** (v1) — the turning point of the narrative.
- **Mordecai's service unrewarded** — the deed of 2:21–23 was written down but not repaid.
- **Haman's assumption** — dramatic irony: the honor he plans for himself he must carry out for his enemy.
- **Public proclamation** — not death, but for Haman a visible abasement.
- **"If he is of the seed of the Jews..."** (v13) — Haman's own wife and wise men foresee his fall.
- **Head covered** — a sign of mourning (2 Sam 15:30).
- **The summons to the feast** interrupts him. Next chapter: Esther names Haman.
