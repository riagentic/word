# 1 Peter 4

[1] ⟦mashiach|The Mashiach|Christ⟧ therefore having suffered in flesh, you also arm yourselves with the same thought, because he who suffered in flesh has ceased from sin,

[2] so as no longer to live the remaining time in flesh for desires of men but for the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] For sufficient is the time gone by to have worked out the intention of the nations, having gone on in acts of licentiousness, desires, bouts of drunkenness, revelries, drinking parties, and unlawful idolatries.

[4] In this they think it strange that you do not run together with them into the same outpouring of dissoluteness, blaspheming;

[5] who will render an account to him who is ready to judge the living and the dead;

[6] for unto this the good news was brought also to the dead, that they might be judged indeed according to men in flesh, but live according to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in spirit.

[7] But the end of all things has drawn near. Be sober-minded therefore and be sober unto prayers;

[8] before all things **having earnest love among yourselves, because love covers a multitude of sins**;

[9] hospitable to one another without grumbling;

[10] each one, as he received a gift, serving one another with it, as good stewards of the varied grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧:

[11] if anyone speaks, as oracles of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; if anyone serves, as from strength which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ provides; that in all things ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may be glorified through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, to whom is the glory and the might unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

[12] Beloved, do not think strange the burning among you, which comes to you for trial, as though a strange thing were happening to you,

[13] but insofar as you share in the sufferings of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, rejoice, that also in the revelation of his glory you may rejoice, exulting.

[14] If you are reproached in the name of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, blessed are you, because the Spirit of glory and of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rests upon you.

[15] For let none of you suffer as a murderer or a thief or an evildoer, or as a meddler in what belongs to others;

[16] but if as a ⟦christian|Christianos|Christian⟧, let him not be ashamed, but let him glorify ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in this name.

[17] Because it is the time for the judgment to begin from the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and if first from us, what is the end of those who disobey the gospel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[18] And

"**If the righteous is scarcely saved,
where will the ungodly and sinner appear?**"

[19] So then let those also who suffer according to the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ entrust their souls to a faithful Creator in doing good.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v1, 13, 14) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v11) — systematic.
- **"Forasmuch then as Christ hath suffered for us in the flesh" → "The *Mashiach* therefore having suffered in flesh"** (v1) — printed text Χριστοῦ οὖν παθόντος σαρκί, without "for us"; σαρκί is a dative without article or preposition (also v2, 6; 3:18).
- **"the same mind" → "the same thought"** (v1) — Greek ἔννοια, "thought, intent". ὅτι is "because", or "that" (giving the content of the thought).
- **"hath ceased from sin" kept** (v1) — Greek πέπαυται ἁμαρτίας (printed text, singular), middle or passive: "has ceased from" or "has been made to cease from".
- **"that he no longer should live the rest of his time in the flesh to the lusts of men" → "so as no longer to live the remaining time in flesh for desires of men"** (v2) — Greek εἰς τὸ … βιῶσαι, an infinitive without a subject: "he" (the one who suffered) or "you".
- **"the time past of our life may suffice us to have wrought the will of the Gentiles" → "sufficient is the time gone by to have worked out the intention of the nations"** (v3) — the printed text has no "us" and no "of our life"; βούλημα ("intention, purpose") is a different word from θέλημα "will" (v2); κατειργάσθαι "worked out" (Jas 1:3); ἔθνη "nations".
- **"when we walked in lasciviousness, lusts, excess of wine, revellings, banquetings, and abominable idolatries" → "having gone on in acts of licentiousness, desires, bouts of drunkenness, revelries, drinking parties, and unlawful idolatries"** (v3) — Greek πεπορευμένους ("having gone", not περιπατέω "walk"); all six nouns are plural: ἀσελγείαις (2 Pet 2:2), ἐπιθυμίαις, οἰνοφλυγίαις, κώμοις, πότοις; ἀθεμίτοις is "against what is laid down as right, unlawful" (Acts 10:28).
- **"Wherein they think it strange that ye run not with them to the same excess of riot, speaking evil of you" → "In this they think it strange that you do not run together with them into the same outpouring of dissoluteness, blaspheming"** (v4) — Greek ξενίζονται (the verb of v12, from ξένος "strange"); ἀνάχυσις "a pouring out"; ἀσωτία as Titus 1:6; βλασφημοῦντες has no object.
- **"shall give account" → "will render an account"** (v5) — Greek ἀποδώσουσιν λόγον; "living and dead" have no article.
- **"was the gospel preached also to them that are dead" → "the good news was brought also to the dead"** (v6) — Greek νεκροῖς εὐηγγελίσθη: "to dead ones", without article, the word of v5; the verb as 1:12, 25.
- **"according to men in the flesh … according to God in the spirit" → "according to men in flesh … according to *Elohim* in spirit"** (v6) — Greek κατὰ ἀνθρώπους σαρκί … κατὰ θεὸν πνεύματι; the same pair of datives as 3:18.
- **"is at hand" → "has drawn near"** (v7) — Greek ἤγγικεν, as Jas 5:8.
- **"be ye therefore sober, and watch unto prayer" → "be sober-minded therefore and be sober unto prayers"** (v7) — Greek σωφρονήσατε, νήψατε (1:13; 5:8); προσευχάς is plural.
- **"have fervent charity among yourselves: for charity shall cover the multitude of sins" → "having earnest love among yourselves, because love covers a multitude of sins"** (v8) — Greek ἐκτενῆ, as ἐκτενῶς in 1:22; ἔχοντες is a participle; printed text καλύπτει, present. Echoes Prov 10:12.
- **"Use hospitality one to another without grudging" → "hospitable to one another without grumbling"** (v9) — Greek φιλόξενοι, an adjective without a verb; printed text γογγυσμοῦ, singular.
- **"minister the same one to another" → "serving one another with it"** (v10) — Greek εἰς ἑαυτοὺς αὐτὸ διακονοῦντες, a participle; χάρισμα is a gift of grace (χάρις). ποικίλης is the word "various" of 1:6.
- **"let him speak as the oracles of God … let him do it as of the ability which God giveth" → "as oracles of *Elohim* … as from strength which *Elohim* provides"** (v11) — the Greek has no second verb in either clause; ἰσχύος "strength"; χορηγεῖ "provides" (2 Cor 9:10; the compound ἐπιχορηγέω is "supply", 2 Pet 1:5).
- **"to whom be praise and dominion for ever and ever" → "to whom is the glory and the might unto the ages of the ages"** (v11) — Greek ᾧ ἐστιν, a statement; δόξα "glory"; κράτος "might", as Jude 25. "To whom" can be *Elohim* or *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.
- **"think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you" → "do not think strange the burning among you, which comes to you for trial"** (v12) — Greek τῇ ἐν ὑμῖν πυρώσει πρὸς πειρασμὸν ὑμῖν γινομένῃ: πύρωσις is "burning" (also of the refiner's fire, Prov 27:21 LXX); πειρασμός "trial" (1:6).
- **"rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ's sufferings … ye may be glad also with exceeding joy" → "insofar as you share in the sufferings of the *Mashiach*, rejoice … you may rejoice, exulting"** (v13) — Greek κοινωνεῖτε; χαίρετε … χαρῆτε ἀγαλλιώμενοι, the same verb twice, then the "exult" of 1:6, 8; ἐν τῇ ἀποκαλύψει "in the revelation".
- **"reproached for the name of Christ, happy are ye" → "reproached in the name of the *Mashiach*, blessed are you"** (v14) — Greek ἐν ὀνόματι Χριστοῦ; μακάριοι, as 3:14.
- **"the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you" kept** (v14) — Greek τὸ τῆς δόξης καὶ τὸ τοῦ θεοῦ πνεῦμα, with the article twice: "the (Spirit) of the glory and the Spirit of *Elohim*". The printed text ends the verse here; KJV's "on their part he is evil spoken of, but on your part he is glorified" is not in it.
- **"But let none of you suffer" → "For let none of you suffer"** (v15) — Greek γάρ.
- **"a busybody in other men's matters" → "a meddler in what belongs to others"** (v15) — Greek ἀλλοτριεπίσκοπος, a word found only here: one who "oversees" (ἐπίσκοπος) what is another's (ἀλλότριος); the exact sense is uncertain.
- **"Yet if any man suffer as a Christian" → "but if as a *Christianos*"** (v16) — the Greek has no verb; "suffer" is carried over from v15.
- **"on this behalf" → "in this name"** (v16) — Greek ἐν τῷ ὀνόματι τούτῳ. Editions: ὀνόματι WH Treg NA27 ] μέρει NA28 RP ("in this respect", KJV's "on this behalf").
- **"judgment must begin at the house of God" → "the judgment to begin from the house of *Elohim*"** (v17) — Greek τὸ κρίμα, elsewhere "sentence" (2 Pet 2:3; Jas 3:1); here it is something that "begins", the act of judging, so "judgment". ἀπό "from"; οἶκος "house". The second clause has no verb.
- **"them that obey not" → "those who disobey"** (v17) — Greek ἀπειθούντων (2:8; 3:1, 20); τέλος "end" (1:9).
- **"where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?" → "where will the ungodly and sinner appear?"** (v18) — quotation of Prov 11:31 (LXX); printed text ὁ ἀσεβὴς καὶ ἁμαρτωλός, one article for both; φανεῖται "will appear"; μόλις "scarcely, with difficulty".
- **"commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator" → "entrust their souls to a faithful Creator in doing good"** (v19) — Greek παρατιθέσθωσαν "entrust"; the printed text has no "as"; καί "also"; ἀγαθοποιΐα is the noun of "doing good" (2:15, 20; 3:6, 17).

### Notes
- **v18** quotes Prov 11:31 (LXX).
- **v7–11** — one sentence in Greek: after the two commands of v7 come participles and adjectives ("having", "hospitable", "serving").
