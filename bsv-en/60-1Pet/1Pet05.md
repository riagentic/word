# 1 Peter 5

[1] Elders among you therefore I exhort, I the fellow elder and witness of the sufferings of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, the sharer also of the glory about to be revealed:

[2] **shepherd the flock of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that is among you, overseeing not by compulsion but willingly, according to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; nor for shameful gain but readily;**

**[3] nor as lording it over the allotted portions, but becoming patterns of the flock**;

[4] and when the Chief Shepherd is manifested, you will carry off the unfading crown of glory.

[5] Likewise, younger ones, be subject to elders. And all of you, tie on humility toward one another, because "**⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ opposes the proud, but to the humble he gives grace**."

[6] Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that he may lift you up in due time,

[7] **casting all your anxiety upon him, because he cares about you**.

[8] **Be sober, be awake. Your adversary the devil walks about as a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour;**

[9] resist him, firm in the faith, knowing that the same sufferings are being completed in your brotherhood in the world.

[10] And the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of all grace, who called you unto his eternal glory in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, after you have suffered a little while, will himself restore, make stable, strengthen, found you.

[11] To him the might unto the ages. Amen.

[12] Through Silvanus, the faithful brother, as I consider, I have written to you in few words, exhorting and testifying that this is the true grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; stand in it.

[13] She who is in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, elect together with you, greets you, and ⟦mark|Marcus|Mark⟧ my son.

[14] Greet one another with a kiss of love.

Peace to you all who are in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v1, 10, 14) — systematic.
- **"The elders which are among you I exhort, who am also an elder" → "Elders among you therefore I exhort, I the fellow elder"** (v1) — Greek Πρεσβυτέρους οὖν ἐν ὑμῖν, with "therefore" and without the article (οὖν WH Treg NA27 ] τοὺς NA28 RP, "the elders among you", without "therefore"); ὁ συμπρεσβύτερος "the fellow elder". παρακαλῶ is "exhort", as 2:11.
- **"a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed" → "the sharer also of the glory about to be revealed"** (v1) — Greek κοινωνός (2 Pet 1:4; the verb in 4:13); μελλούσης ἀποκαλύπτεσθαι "about to be revealed".
- **"Feed the flock of God" → "shepherd the flock of *Elohim*"** (v2) — Greek ποιμάνατε, the verb of ποιμήν "shepherd" (2:25) and ἀρχιποίμην (v4).
- **"taking the oversight thereof" → "overseeing"** (v2) — Greek ἐπισκοποῦντες, the verb of ἐπίσκοπος "overseer" (2:25). Editions: ἐπισκοποῦντες Treg NA28 RP ] – WH.
- **"not by constraint, but willingly" → "not by compulsion but willingly, according to *Elohim*"** (v2) — the verse has κατὰ θεόν, "according to *Elohim*" (as 4:6): κατὰ θεόν Treg NA28 ] – WH RP (two against two of the four; NA28 decides).
- **"not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind" → "nor for shameful gain but readily"** (v2) — Greek μηδὲ αἰσχροκερδῶς ἀλλὰ προθύμως (προθυμία "readiness", 2 Cor 8:11).
- **"neither as being lords over God's heritage, but being ensamples to the flock" → "nor as lording it over the allotted portions, but becoming patterns of the flock"** (v3) — Greek κατακυριεύοντες τῶν κλήρων: κλῆρος is a "lot", a share assigned by lot; here the parts of the flock assigned to each elder; the Greek has no "God's". τύποι "patterns"; γινόμενοι "becoming"; τοῦ ποιμνίου is a genitive.
- **"when the chief Shepherd shall appear, ye shall receive a crown of glory that fadeth not away" → "when the Chief Shepherd is manifested, you will carry off the unfading crown of glory"** (v4) — Greek φανερωθέντος (1:20); κομιεῖσθε (1:9); ἀμαράντινον, "of amaranth", the flower that does not fade (ἀμάραντος, 1:4).
- **"ye younger, submit yourselves unto the elder" → "younger ones, be subject to elders"** (v5) — Greek ὑποτάγητε πρεσβυτέροις, without article.
- **"Yea, all of you be subject one to another, and be clothed with humility" → "And all of you, tie on humility toward one another"** (v5) — the printed text has no "be subject" here; ἐγκομβώσασθε is "tie on (a garment, as a slave's apron)"; ταπεινοφροσύνη "humility".
- **"God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble" → "*Elohim* opposes the proud, but to the humble he gives grace"** (v5) — quotation of Prov 3:34 (LXX), in the same words as Jas 4:6.
- **"that he may exalt you in due time" → "that he may lift you up in due time"** (v6) — Greek ὑψώσῃ, as Jas 4:10; ἐν καιρῷ, "in season, at the right time" ("due" is in the idiom, not a separate word).
- **"Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you" → "casting all your anxiety upon him, because he cares about you"** (v7) — Greek μέριμναν (singular) and μέλει are different words; ἐπιρίψαντες is an aorist participle depending on "humble yourselves" (Ps 55:22, LXX 54:23).
- **"Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour" → "Be sober, be awake. Your adversary the devil walks about as a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour"** (v8) — Greek νήψατε, γρηγορήσατε; the Greek has no "because" and reads τινα "someone" (τινα NA28 ] τίνα "whom" RP; – WH Treg); περιπατεῖ "walks"; καταπιεῖν is literally "to swallow down".
- **"Whom resist stedfast in the faith" → "resist him, firm in the faith"** (v9) — Greek ᾧ ἀντίστητε (a relative, "whom"), as Jas 4:7; στερεοί "solid, firm".
- **"the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world" → "the same sufferings are being completed in your brotherhood in the world"** (v9) — Greek τὰ αὐτὰ τῶν παθημάτων, "the same kinds of sufferings"; ἀδελφότης "brotherhood" (2:17), a dative: "in" or "for"; ἐπιτελεῖσθαι "to be completed" (2 Cor 8:6).
- **"who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus" → "who called you unto his eternal glory in the *Mashiach*"** (v10) — Greek ὑμᾶς "you" and ἐν Χριστῷ, without "Jesus": Χριστῷ WH NA28 ] + Ἰησοῦ Treg NA27 RP.
- **"make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you" → "will himself restore, make stable, strengthen, found you"** (v10) — Greek καταρτίσει, στηρίξει, σθενώσει, θεμελιώσει, four futures (statements, not wishes; καταρτίσει WH Treg NA28 ] καταρτίσαι ὑμᾶς RP, a wish); "you" stands once in Greek, with "having suffered a little". καταρτίζω is "put in order, restore, make complete"; στηρίζω "make stable" (2 Pet 1:12); θεμελιόω "set on a foundation" (θεμελιώσει NA28 RP ] – WH Treg; two against two of the four, NA28 decides).
- **"To him be glory and dominion for ever and ever" → "To him the might unto the ages"** (v11) — the Greek has only τὸ κράτος, without "glory" (αὐτῷ WH Treg NA28 ] + ἡ δόξα καὶ RP) and without a verb; κράτος as 4:11. The verse reads εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας, "unto the ages", without "of the ages", against the printed SBLGNT: τῶν αἰώνων Treg NIV RP ] – WH NA28 (two against two of the four; NA28 decides).
- **"a faithful brother unto you, as I suppose" → "the faithful brother, as I consider"** (v12) — Greek ὑμῖν τοῦ πιστοῦ ἀδελφοῦ, ὡς λογίζομαι: "to you" can go with "I have written" or with "the faithful brother"; λογίζομαι "consider, reckon".
- **"I have written briefly" → "I have written to you in few words"** (v12) — Greek διʼ ὀλίγων ἔγραψα, "through few".
- **"wherein ye stand" → "stand in it"** (v12) — printed text εἰς ἣν στῆτε, an imperative, literally "into which stand".
- **"The church that is at Babylon, elected together with you" → "She who is in Babylon, elect together with you"** (v13) — Greek ἡ ἐν Βαβυλῶνι συνεκλεκτή, a feminine without a noun: a congregation or a woman; the Greek has no "church".
- **"a kiss of charity" → "a kiss of love"** (v14) — Greek ἐν φιλήματι ἀγάπης.
- **"Peace be with you all that are in Christ Jesus. Amen." → "Peace to you all who are in the *Mashiach*."** (v14) — printed text ἐν Χριστῷ, without "Jesus" and without "Amen".

### Notes
- **v5** quotes Prov 3:34.
- **v13** — "Babylon" is widely understood as a code-name for Rome.
