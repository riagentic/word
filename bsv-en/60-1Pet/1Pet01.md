# 1 Peter 1

[1] ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, an apostle of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, to the elect sojourners of the Dispersion of ⟦pontus|Pontos|Pontus⟧, Galatia, ⟦cappadocia|Kappadokia|Cappadocia⟧, Asia, and Bithynia,

[2] according to the foreknowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father, in sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧: Grace to you and peace be multiplied.

[3] Blessed be the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, who according to his great mercy begot us again unto a living hope through the resurrection of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ from the dead,

[4] unto an inheritance incorruptible and undefiled and unfading, kept in the heavens for you,

[5] who in the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ are being guarded through faith unto a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.

[6] In this you exult, having now for a little while, if it is necessary, been grieved in various trials,

[7] so that the testing of your faith, more precious than gold that perishes yet is tested through fire, may be found unto praise and glory and honor in the revelation of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[8] whom, not having seen, you love; in whom, now not seeing but believing, you exult with joy inexpressible and glorified,

[9] carrying off the end of your faith, the salvation of souls.

[10] Concerning this salvation prophets sought out and searched out, those who prophesied concerning the grace that is for you,

[11] searching into what or what kind of time the Spirit of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ in them was making clear, testifying beforehand to the sufferings unto ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and the glories after these;

[12] to whom it was revealed that not to themselves but to you they were serving these things, which have now been announced to you through those who brought you the good news by the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ sent from heaven — things into which angels desire to look.

[13] Therefore, having girded up the loins of your mind, being perfectly sober, hope on the grace that is being brought to you in the revelation of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[14] As children of obedience, not conforming yourselves to the former desires in your ignorance,

[15] but according to the Holy One who called you, you yourselves also **become holy in all conduct**,

[16] because it is written, "**You shall be holy, because I am holy**."

[17] And if you call upon as Father the one who judges without respect of persons according to each one's work, conduct yourselves in fear during the time of your dwelling as aliens,

[18] knowing that not with corruptible things, silver or gold, were you ransomed from your vain conduct handed down from your fathers,

[19] but with the precious blood of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, as of a lamb unblemished and spotless,

[20] foreknown indeed before the foundation of the world, but manifested at the last of the times because of you,

[21] who through him are believers in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope are in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[22] Having purified your souls in the obedience of the truth unto unfeigned brotherly affection, love one another from the heart earnestly,

[23] having been begotten again, not of corruptible seed but of incorruptible, through the living and abiding word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[24] because

"**All flesh is as grass,
and all its glory as the flower of grass.
The grass withered,
and the flower fell off,
[25] but the word of the Lord abides forever**."

And this is the word that was brought as good news to you.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 2, 3, 7, 13) — systematic.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v1) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kefa* = "rock/stone"; the name Jesus gave Simon (John 1:42); Greek Πέτρος *Petros* is a translation of the Aramaic; the original Hebrew/Aramaic name restored.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v12) — systematic. Greek πνεύματι ἁγίῳ, a plain dative without article or preposition: "by" the Holy Spirit (ὑμᾶς WH Treg ] + ἐν NA28 RP, "in the Holy Spirit").
- **"strangers scattered throughout" → "sojourners of the Dispersion of"** (v1) — Greek παρεπιδήμοις διασπορᾶς Πόντου …: παρεπίδημος is one who stays for a time in a place that is not his own (also 2:11); "exile" would add banishment. "Of the Dispersion" and the five place names are plain genitives.
- **"sanctification of the Spirit" kept** (v2) — Greek ἐν ἁγιασμῷ πνεύματος, no article: most naturally the Spirit who sanctifies; the genitive is kept as it stands.
- **"unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ" kept** (v2) — Greek εἰς ὑπακοὴν καὶ ῥαντισμὸν αἵματος Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ: one preposition for both nouns; the Greek does not say "obedience to *Yeshua*" nor "his blood".
- **"Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied" kept** (v2) — Greek πληθυνθείη, a wish; the same words as 2 Pet 1:2.
- **"hath begotten us again" → "begot us again"** (v3, 23) — Greek ἀναγεννάω, γεννάω ("beget") with ἀνα- "again"; v3 is an aorist participle with the article ("who … begot"), not a new sentence.
- **"incorruptible" / "corruptible" kept** (v4, 18, 23) — Greek ἄφθαρτος, φθαρτός, from the root of φθορά "corruption" (2 Pet 1:4).
- **"reserved in heaven" → "kept in the heavens"** (v4) — Greek τετηρημένην ἐν οὐρανοῖς, plural; τηρέω "keep".
- **"wherein ye greatly rejoice" → "in this you exult"** (v6, 8) — Greek ἀγαλλιᾶσθε; "rejoice" is χαίρω (4:13, where both stand together). ἐν ᾧ can be "in which" (the last time, or all of v3–5) or "in whom"; the form can also be an imperative. There is no "though" in the Greek.
- **"the trial of your faith" → "the testing of your faith"** (v7) — Greek τὸ δοκίμιον ὑμῶν τῆς πίστεως, the same words as Jas 1:3; δοκίμιον can also mean "what is tested and genuine" in your faith. δοκιμαζομένου ("tested") is the same root.
- **"at the appearing" → "in the revelation"** (v7, 13) — Greek ἐν ἀποκαλύψει.
- **"joy unspeakable and full of glory" → "joy inexpressible and glorified"** (v8) — Greek χαρᾷ ἀνεκλαλήτῳ καὶ δεδοξασμένῃ, a perfect passive participle, "having been glorified".
- **"the salvation of your souls" → "the salvation of souls"** (v9) — Greek σωτηρίαν ψυχῶν; "your" stands only with "faith". κομιζόμενοι is "carrying off (as one's own)", as Heb 10:36; also 5:4.
- **"enquired and searched diligently" → "sought out and searched out"** (v10–11) — Greek ἐξεζήτησαν καὶ ἐξηραύνησαν … ἐραυνῶντες; "prophets" has no article.
- **"what, or what manner of time" → "what or what kind of time"** (v11) — Greek εἰς τίνα ἢ ποῖον καιρόν: τίνα can be "what (time)" or "whom" ("what person or what kind of time"). ἐδήλου "was making clear", as 2 Pet 1:14.
- **"the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow" → "the sufferings unto the *Mashiach* and the glories after these"** (v11) — Greek τὰ εἰς Χριστὸν παθήματα, not a plain genitive: the sufferings "unto", destined for, the *Mashiach*; δόξας is plural. The participle προμαρτυρόμενον is neuter with πνεῦμα; the verse has no "he".
- **"they did minister" → "they were serving"** (v12) — Greek διηκόνουν.
- **"them that have preached the gospel unto you" → "those who brought you the good news"** (v12, 25) — Greek εὐαγγελίζομαι.
- **"desire to look into" kept** (v12) — Greek ἐπιθυμοῦσιν … παρακύψαι, "to stoop to look".
- **"gird up the loins of your mind" kept** (v13) — Greek ἀναζωσάμενοι τὰς ὀσφύας τῆς διανοίας ὑμῶν, the image of tucking up the robe for work or travel.
- **"be sober, and hope to the end" → "being perfectly sober, hope"** (v13) — Greek νήφοντες τελείως, ἐλπίσατε: the printed text puts the comma after τελείως ("perfectly, completely"), joining it to "being sober"; it can also be read with "hope". The grace is "being brought" (φερομένην, present).
- **"obedient children" → "children of obedience"** (v14) — Greek τέκνα ὑπακοῆς; "lusts" → "desires" (ἐπιθυμίαις).
- **"as he which hath called you is holy" → "according to the Holy One who called you"** (v15) — Greek κατὰ τὸν καλέσαντα ὑμᾶς ἅγιον. γενήθητε is "become".
- **"conversation" → "conduct"** (v15, 18) — Greek ἀναστροφή, as 2 Pet 2:7; 3:11; the verb ἀναστράφητε (v17) is "conduct yourselves".
- **"Be ye holy; for I am holy" → "You shall be holy, because I am holy"** (v16) — Greek Ἅγιοι ἔσεσθε, ὅτι ἐγὼ ἅγιος, a future (ἔσεσθε WH Treg NA28 ] γίνεσθε RP); the Greek has no "am" (ἅγιος WH Treg NA28 ] + εἰμι NA27 RP). The ὅτι that introduces the quotation is left out (ὅτι WH NA27 ] – Treg NA28 RP); it is not translated either way. Quotation of Lev 11:44; 19:2.
- **"the time of your sojourning" → "the time of your dwelling as aliens"** (v17) — Greek παροικία, the stay of a πάροικος, a resident without citizen's rights (2:11); "work" (ἔργον) is singular.
- **"redeemed … from your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers" → "ransomed from your vain conduct handed down from your fathers"** (v18) — Greek ἐλυτρώθητε (λύτρον "ransom", as Titus 2:14); ματαίας "vain"; πατροπαραδότου "handed down from fathers".
- **"foreordained" → "foreknown"** (v20) — Greek προεγνωσμένου, the verb of πρόγνωσις "foreknowledge" (v2).
- **"in these last times" → "at the last of the times"** (v20) — printed text ἐπʼ ἐσχάτου τῶν χρόνων; φανερωθέντος "manifested".
- **"who by him do believe in God" → "who through him are believers in *Elohim*"** (v21) — printed text πιστοὺς εἰς θεόν, the adjective πιστός (elsewhere "faithful"); with εἰς it means "trusting in". Editions: πιστοὺς WH Treg NA28 ] πιστεύοντας RP (KJV's "do believe").
- **"in obeying the truth through the Spirit" → "in the obedience of the truth"** (v22) — Greek ἐν τῇ ὑπακοῇ τῆς ἀληθείας; "through the Spirit" is not in the printed text.
- **"unfeigned love of the brethren" → "unfeigned brotherly affection"** (v22) — Greek φιλαδελφίαν ἀνυπόκριτον; φιλαδελφία as 2 Pet 1:7, beside ἀγαπήσατε "love".
- **"with a pure heart fervently" → "from the heart earnestly"** (v22) — the verse reads ἐκ καρδίας, without "pure": ἐκ WH Treg NIV ] + καθαρᾶς RP NA28 ("from a pure heart"). ἐκτενῶς is "at full stretch, earnestly".
- **"by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever" → "through the living and abiding word of *Elohim*"** (v23) — Greek διὰ λόγου ζῶντος θεοῦ καὶ μένοντος; "for ever" is not in the printed text. The two participles can go with "word" or with "*Elohim*" ("the word of the living and abiding *Elohim*").
- **"The grass withereth, and the flower thereof falleth away" → "The grass withered, and the flower fell off"** (v24) — Greek ἐξηράνθη, ἐξέπεσεν, aorists, as Jas 1:11; the printed text has "its glory" (αὐτῆς) and no "thereof". The Greek has no "is".
- **"the word of the Lord endureth for ever" → "the word of the Lord abides forever"** (v25) — Greek μένει, the verb of v23. "Word" here is ῥῆμα, the spoken word (twice in v25); in v23 it is λόγος.

### Notes
- **v16** quotes Lev 11:44; 19:2.
- **v24-25** quotes Isa 40:6-8.
- **v3–12** — one sentence in Greek, joined by relatives ("who", "in which", "whom", "concerning which", "to whom").
