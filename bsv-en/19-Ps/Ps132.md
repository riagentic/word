# Psalm 132

A song of the ascents.

[1] Remember, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for David
all his being afflicted,

[2] how he swore to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
vowed to the Mighty One of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧:

[3] "I will not come into the tent of my house;
I will not go up upon the bed of my couch;

[4] I will not give sleep to my eyes,
to my eyelids slumber,

[5] until I find a place for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
dwellings for the Mighty One of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧."

[6] Behold, we heard of it in ⟦ephrathah|Efratah|Ephrathah⟧;
we found it in the fields of ⟦jaar|Yaar|Jaar⟧.

[7] Let us come into his dwellings;
let us bow down at the footstool of his feet.

[8] Arise, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, to your resting place,
you and the ark of your strength.

[9] Let your priests be clothed with righteousness,
and let your faithful ones shout for joy.

[10] For the sake of David your servant,
do not turn back the face of your ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧.

[11] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has sworn to David truth;
he will not turn back from it:
"Of the fruit of your belly
I will set upon the throne for you.

[12] If your sons keep my covenant
and my testimony, which I will teach them,
also their sons perpetually
shall sit upon the throne for you."

[13] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has chosen ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
he has desired it for a dwelling place for himself:

[14] "This is my resting place perpetually;
here I will dwell, for I have desired it.

[15] Her provision I will surely bless;
her needy I will sate with bread.

[16] And her priests I will clothe with salvation,
and her faithful ones shall surely shout for joy.

[17] There I will make a horn spring up for David;
I have arranged a lamp for my ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧.

[18] His enemies I will clothe with shame,
and upon him his crown shall blossom."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 5, 8, 11, 13) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees" → "A song of the ascents."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת, with the article (the heading row).
- **"LORD, remember David, and all his afflictions" → "Remember, Yahweh, for David all his being afflicted"** (v1) — Hebrew זְכוֹר־יְהוָה לְדָוִד: an imperative with לְ, "remember for David" (to his good, or "remember of David"; the לְ does not decide). No "O": the Hebrew has no word for it. עֻנּוֹתוֹ is a pual infinitive of עָנָה "afflict" (the עִנָּה row): "his being afflicted"; "the hardships he endured" said more than the word. Some derive it from another עָנָה, "be busy, take pains" (his toil for the ark): uncertain.
- **"How he sware unto the LORD, and vowed unto the mighty God of Jacob" → "how he swore to Yahweh, vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob"** (v2) — Hebrew נִשְׁבַּע · נָדַר; no "and" between the two lines. אֲבִיר יַעֲקֹב "the Mighty One of Jacob" (its row; no word "God"; also v5; Gen 49:24).
- **"Surely I will not come into the tabernacle of my house, nor go up into my bed" → "I will not come into the tent of my house; I will not go up upon the bed of my couch"** (v3) — Hebrew אִם־אָבֹא … אִם־אֶעֱלֶה, the oath form, literally "if I come … if I go up" (= "I will not"; the oath row; also v4 אִם־אֶתֵּן). בְּאֹהֶל בֵּיתִי "into the tent of my house": "tent" was dropped. עַל־עֶרֶשׂ יְצוּעָי "upon the bed of my couch": two words for a bed; יָצוּעַ "couch" (its row), so עֶרֶשׂ "bed" here (Ps 6:6 has "couch" for עֶרֶשׂ beside מִטָּה "bed").
- **"I will not give sleep to mine eyes, or slumber to mine eyelids"** (v4) — Hebrew שְׁנָת לְעֵינָי לְעַפְעַפַּי תְּנוּמָה: the second line in the Hebrew order, "to my eyelids slumber"; the verb carries over.
- **"a habitation for the mighty God of Jacob" → "dwellings for the Mighty One of Jacob"** (v5) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּנוֹת, plural (as v7; Ps 84:1): the plural kept; whether it means one sanctuary is not said.
- **"Lo, we heard of it at Ephratah: we found it in the fields of the wood"** (v6) — Hebrew הִנֵּה "behold"; שְׁמַעֲנוּהָ · מְצָאנוּהָ "we heard (of) her · we found her": the feminine "it" has no named noun before it (the ark is commonly meant; אֲרוֹן is sometimes feminine; or the "place" of v5: uncertain). שְׂדֵי־יָעַר "the fields of Yaar": יַעַר is "forest"; taken as the place name (Kiriath-jearim, where the ark stayed, 1 Sam 7:1–2; from memory), the token kept; "fields of the forest" in Changes.
- **"We will go into his tabernacles: we will worship at his footstool" → "Let us come into his dwellings; let us bow down at the footstool of his feet"** (v7) — Hebrew נָבוֹאָה · נִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה, cohortatives, "let us". הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" (its row; not "worship"). לַהֲדֹם רַגְלָיו "at the footstool of his feet": "of his feet" was dropped. The quotation marks are removed: the Hebrew does not mark speech, and the "we" of v6 goes on.
- **"Arise, O LORD, into thy rest; thou, and the ark of thy strength" → "Arise, Yahweh, to your resting place, you and the ark of your strength"** (v8) — Hebrew קוּמָה יְהוָה לִמְנוּחָתֶךָ: no "O", no "and go" (the 2 Chr 6:41–42 = Ps 132:8–10 row); מְנוּחָה "resting place" (also v14). עֹז "strength" (not "might"). 2 Chr 6:41 has "And now", "Yahweh Elohim" and לְנוּחֶךָ "to your rest".
- **"Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy"** (v9) — kept: יִלְבְּשׁוּ־צֶדֶק "be clothed with righteousness"; חֲסִידֶיךָ "your faithful ones" (those who keep *chesed*); יְרַנֵּנוּ "shout for joy" (רִנֵּן). 2 Chr 6:41 has תְשׁוּעָה "salvation" (as our v16) and יִשְׂמְחוּ בַטּוֹב "rejoice in the good".
- **"For thy servant David's sake turn not away the face of thine anointed" → "For the sake of David your servant, do not turn back the face of your anointed"** (v10) — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר "for the sake of" (its row), in the Hebrew order "David your servant". אַל־תָּשֵׁב פְּנֵי "do not turn back the face" (to refuse one who asks, 1 Kgs 2:16). מְשִׁיחֶךָ singular, "your anointed", with its token (also v17); 2 Chr 6:42 prints the plural and has "remember the chesed of David your servant" in place of this line's first half.
- **"The LORD hath sworn in truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne" → "Yahweh has sworn to David truth; he will not turn back from it: 'Of the fruit of your belly I will set upon the throne for you'"** (v11) — Hebrew אֱמֶת stands without a preposition: "(a) truth" as the thing sworn, or "in truth" (an adverb); "a sure oath" interpreted it. לֹא־יָשׁוּב מִמֶּנָּה "he will not turn back from it" (שׁוּב; the feminine "it" is the truth). מִפְּרִי בִטְנְךָ "of the fruit of your belly" (בֶּטֶן "belly", its row; "body" softened it; "One" is not written). אָשִׁית "I will set" (שִׁית). לְכִסֵּא־לָךְ, literally "to a throne to you" (= your throne; kept "the throne for you", also v12). 2 Sam 7:12–16 gives the promise in other words ("your seed … who shall come out of your inward parts … I will establish the throne of his kingdom"); nothing is levelled.
- **"If thy children will keep my covenant and my testimony that I shall teach them, their children shall also sit upon thy throne for evermore" → "If your sons keep my covenant and my testimony, which I will teach them, also their sons perpetually shall sit upon the throne for you"** (v12) — Hebrew בָּנֶיךָ · בְּנֵיהֶם "your sons · their sons" (the בֵּן row). וְעֵדֹתִי זוֹ: עֵדֹתִי is pointed as a singular, "my testimony" (the plural would be עֵדוֹתַי), and זוֹ is the poetic relative "which" (as זוּ). אֲלַמְּדֵם imperfect, "I will teach them" (לִמֵּד). עֲדֵי־עַד "perpetually" (as Ps 83:17; 92:7; also v14). גַּם "also" at the head of the line. יֵשְׁבוּ "shall sit" (יָשַׁב of the body).
- **"he hath desired it for his habitation" → "he has desired it for a dwelling place for himself"** (v13) — Hebrew אִוָּהּ "he has desired it" (אִוָּה; also v14 אִוִּתִיהָ); לְמוֹשָׁב לוֹ "for a dwelling place for himself" (מוֹשָׁב, its row). בָּחַר בְּ "choose" (its row).
- **"This is my rest for ever: here will I dwell" → "This is my resting place perpetually; here I will dwell"** (v14) — Hebrew זֹאת־מְנוּחָתִי עֲדֵי־עַד (as v8, 12); אֵשֵׁב "I will dwell" (יָשַׁב).
- **"I will abundantly bless her provision: I will satisfy her poor with bread" → "Her provision I will surely bless; her needy I will sate with bread"** (v15) — Hebrew צֵידָהּ בָּרֵךְ אֲבָרֵךְ: the infinitive absolute with its verb, "surely bless" (the standing rule; literally "blessing I will bless"; also v16); the Hebrew order kept. צֵידָה "provision" (food laid in; as Josh 9). אֶבְיוֹנֶיהָ "her needy" (אֶבְיוֹן row; not "poor"). אַשְׂבִּיעַ "I will sate" (hifil of שָׂבַע). The suffixes "her" are Zion (feminine).
- **"I will also clothe her priests with salvation: and her saints shall shout aloud for joy" → "And her priests I will clothe with salvation, and her faithful ones shall surely shout for joy"** (v16) — Hebrew וְכֹהֲנֶיהָ אַלְבִּישׁ יֶשַׁע, וְ "And", Hebrew order; יֶשַׁע "salvation" (the answer to v9 "righteousness"). רַנֵּן יְרַנֵּנוּ, the infinitive absolute: "shall surely shout for joy" (literally "shouting for joy they shall shout for joy").
- **"There will I make the horn of David to bud: I have ordained a lamp for mine anointed" → "There I will make a horn spring up for David; I have arranged a lamp for my anointed"** (v17) — Hebrew אַצְמִיחַ קֶרֶן לְדָוִד: הִצְמִיחַ "cause to spring up" (its row); קֶרֶן without the article, "a horn", לְ "for David". עָרַכְתִּי "I have arranged" (עָרַךְ, its row; the word for setting the lamps in order, Lev 24:3–4, from memory), a perfect.
- **"His enemies will I clothe with shame: but upon himself shall his crown flourish" → "His enemies I will clothe with shame, and upon him his crown shall blossom"** (v18) — Hebrew וְעָלָיו "and upon him" (וְ, not "but"). יָצִיץ from צוּץ "blossom" (a play on צִיץ "blossom", the plate of the high priest's crown, Exod 28:36; "shine, sparkle" is also proposed: uncertain). נֵזֶר "crown" (its row: the mark of one set apart).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading stands in Hebrew v1 with the first words of the psalm: the numbers do not shift.
- **2 Chr 6:41–42** — v8–10 are the end of Solomon's prayer in Chronicles; the shared words have one wording, the differences are listed at v8–10.
- **Davidic covenant psalm** — David's oath for a place for Yahweh (v1–10), Yahweh's oath for David's house and for Zion (v11–18): "swore" (v2, 11) and "turn back" (v10, 11) answer each other.
