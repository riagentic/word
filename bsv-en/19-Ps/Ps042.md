# Psalm 42

To the overseer. A Maskil. Of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧.

[1] As a deer pants for channels of water,
so my soul pants for you, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] My soul thirsts for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for the living ⟦el|El|God⟧;
when shall I come and appear before the face of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[3] My tears have been bread to me day and night,
while it is said to me all the day,
"Where is your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[4] These let me remember,
and let me pour out my soul within me:
how I would pass along with the throng,
I would go slowly with them to the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
with the voice of a ringing cry and thanksgiving,
a multitude keeping festival.

[5] Why are you bowed down, my soul,
and in turmoil within me?
Hope for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for I shall yet give thanks to him,
the salvations of his face.

[6] My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, within me my soul is bowed down;
therefore I remember you
from the land of ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧
and the ⟦hermon|Chermons|Hermons⟧, from Mount ⟦mizar|Mitzar|Mizar⟧.

[7] Deep calls to deep
at the sound of your water channels;
all your breakers and your waves
have passed over me.

[8] By day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will command his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and in the night his song is with me,
a prayer to the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of my life.

[9] Let me say to ⟦el|El|God⟧, my crag,
"Why have you forgotten me?
Why do I go darkened
in the oppression of the enemy?"

[10] With murder in my bones
my adversaries have reproached me,
while they say to me all the day,
"Where is your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[11] Why are you bowed down, my soul,
and why are you in turmoil within me?
Hope for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for I shall yet give thanks to him,
the salvations of my face and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*. Book II (Ps 42–72) is the first part of the Elohistic psalms (42–83), which use אֱלֹהִים where other psalms have יהוה; each name stands as printed (v8 has יְהוָה).
- **"God" → "El"** (v2, 8, 9) — Hebrew *El*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"To the chief Musician, Maschil, for the sons of Korah" → "To the overseer. A Maskil. Of the sons of Korah."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ מַשְׂכִּיל לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). מַשְׂכִּיל is kept as a word (from הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight": a skilful or instructive song; the exact sense is uncertain). לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" the sons of Korah; "of" leaves it open.
- **"As the hart panteth after the water brooks" → "As a deer pants for channels of water"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּאַיָּל תַּעֲרֹג עַל־אֲפִיקֵי־מָיִם. אַיָּל "deer" is masculine, the verb תַּעֲרֹג feminine (so "hind, doe" is often read; the Hebrew has the masculine noun); עָרַג "pant, long" (only here and Joel 1:20); אֲפִיקִים "channels" (the 2 Sam 22:16 row; Ps 1:3 has another word, פַּלְגֵי מַיִם, with the same English "channels of water").
- **"when shall I come and appear before God" → "when shall I come and appear before the face of Elohim"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאֵרָאֶה פְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים, nifal "be seen, appear", with פְּנֵי "the face of" ("before" is supplied). The same letters read as qal give "and see the face of Elohim"; the verse follows the pointing.
- **"My tears have been my meat … while they continually say unto me" → "My tears have been bread to me … while it is said to me all the day"** (v3) — Hebrew הָיְתָה־לִּי דִמְעָתִי לֶחֶם … בֶּאֱמֹר אֵלַי כָּל־הַיּוֹם: לֶחֶם "bread"; בֶּאֱמֹר, an infinitive without a subject ("while saying to me"); v10 has בְּאָמְרָם "while they say".
- **"When I remember these things, I pour out my soul in me: for I had gone with the multitude, I went with them to the house of God" → "These let me remember, and let me pour out my soul within me: how I would pass along with the throng, I would go slowly with them to the house of Elohim"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵלֶּה אֶזְכְּרָה וְאֶשְׁפְּכָה עָלַי נַפְשִׁי כִּי אֶעֱבֹר בַּסָּךְ אֶדַּדֵּם: two cohortatives; עָלַי "upon me" (= within me; also v5, 6, 11); כִּי "how, that"; the imperfects אֶעֱבֹר … אֶדַּדֵּם of what used to be. סָךְ only here, "throng" (uncertain; also "covert, booth"); אֶדַּדֵּם from דָּדָה "go slowly" (Isa 38:15) with "them" (uncertain: "lead them in procession").
- **"with the voice of joy and praise, with a multitude that kept holyday" → "with the voice of a ringing cry and thanksgiving, a multitude keeping festival"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּקוֹל־רִנָּה וְתוֹדָה הָמוֹן חוֹגֵג; רִנָּה "ringing cry" (of joy here, as Ps 30:5; the cry of prayer elsewhere); תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving"; הָמוֹן "multitude"; חָגַג "keep festival".
- **"Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted in me?" → "Why are you bowed down, my soul, and in turmoil within me?"** (v5) — Hebrew מַה־תִּשְׁתּוֹחֲחִי נַפְשִׁי וַתֶּהֱמִי עָלָי; שָׁחַח "be bowed down" (as Ps 38:6; not הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" in worship); הָמָה "be in turmoil" (as Ps 39:6). In v5 the second verb has no מַה (v11; 43:5 have וּמַה־תֶּהֱמִי "and why … in turmoil").
- **"hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him for the help of his countenance" → "Hope for Elohim, for I shall yet give thanks to him, the salvations of his face"** (v5) — Hebrew הוֹחִילִי לֵאלֹהִים כִּי־עוֹד אוֹדֶנּוּ יְשׁוּעוֹת פָּנָיו: יִחֵל hifil "hope"; הוֹדָה "give thanks to"; יְשׁוּעוֹת plural "salvations"; פָּנָיו "his face". Verse 5 ends here, as the Masoretic Text divides it; v11 and 43:5 read פָּנַי וֵאלֹהָי "my face and my Elohim", and many read v5–6 the same way by moving אֱלֹהַי back into v5 (Septuagint and Syriac, from memory). The verse keeps the MT: "his face", and "My Elohim" opens v6. The former text had moved "and my Elohim" into v5.
- **"O my God, my soul is cast down within me: therefore will I remember thee from the land of Jordan, and of the Hermonites, from the hill Mizar" → "My Elohim, within me my soul is bowed down; therefore I remember you from the land of Jordan and the Hermons, from Mount Mitzar"** (v6) — Hebrew אֱלֹהַי עָלַי נַפְשִׁי תִשְׁתּוֹחָח עַל־כֵּן אֶזְכָּרְךָ מֵאֶרֶץ יַרְדֵּן וְחֶרְמוֹנִים מֵהַר מִצְעָר: יַרְדֵּן without the article; חֶרְמוֹנִים plural, "the Hermons" (the peaks of the range); מִצְעָר as a name (the word means "smallness"; "from a little mountain" is also possible).
- **"at the noise of thy waterspouts" → "at the sound of your water channels"** (v7) — Hebrew לְקוֹל צִנּוֹרֶיךָ; קוֹל "sound" of a thing; צִנּוֹר "water channel" (only here and 2 Sam 5:8; uncertain: "cataracts, shafts"); מִשְׁבָּר "breaker"; עָבַר "pass over".
- **"Yet the LORD will command his lovingkindness in the day time, and in the night his song shall be with me" → "By day Yahweh will command his chesed, and in the night his song is with me"** (v8) — Hebrew יוֹמָם יְצַוֶּה יְהוָה חַסְדּוֹ וּבַלַּיְלָה שִׁירוֹ עִמִּי: no "yet"; צִוָּה "command"; חֶסֶד carries its token. שִׁירוֹ "his song" is the qere; the ketiv שירה is "a song".
- **"I will say unto God my rock" → "Let me say to El, my crag"** (v9) — Hebrew אוֹמְרָה לְאֵל סַלְעִי, cohortative; סֶלַע "crag" (צוּר is "Rock").
- **"why go I mourning because of the oppression of the enemy?" → "Why do I go darkened in the oppression of the enemy?"** (v9) — Hebrew לָמָּה־קֹדֵר אֵלֵךְ בְּלַחַץ אוֹיֵב; קֹדֵר "darkened" (in the dark clothes and face of mourning; as Ps 35:14; 38:6); הָלַךְ "go" (43:2 has אֶתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about"); בְּ "in"; לַחַץ "oppression".
- **"As with a sword in my bones, mine enemies reproach me" → "With murder in my bones my adversaries have reproached me"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּרֶצַח בְּעַצְמוֹתַי חֵרְפוּנִי צוֹרְרָי; רֶצַח "murder" (noun of רָצַח; here a deadly crushing: uncertain; no "sword"); חֵרֵף "reproach"; צֹרֵר "adversary".
- **Refrain (v5, 11; 43:5)** — one wording where the Hebrew is the same: v11 and 43:5 are word for word alike; v5 differs only where the Hebrew differs (no second "why", "his face" without "and my Elohim").

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–11 = Heb v2–12.
- **Opens Book II** of the Psalter (Ps 42–72) and the Elohistic psalms (42–83).
- **Sons of Korah** — Levite singers (1 Chr 6:22, 31–37; 2 Chr 20:19).
- **Ps 42 and 43** — one refrain (42:5, 11; 43:5) joins them; Ps 43 has no heading.
