# Psalm 73

A psalm. Of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] Surely good to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
to the pure of heart.

[2] And I — my feet had almost turned aside;
my steps had all but been poured out.

[3] For I envied the boastful;
the ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ of the wicked I saw.

[4] For there are no bonds at their death,
and fat is their body.

[5] In the toil of mortal man they are not,
and with mankind they are not plagued.

[6] Therefore pride is their necklace;
violence wraps them as a garment.

[7] Their eye has gone out from fat;
the imaginings of the heart have passed over.

[8] They jeer and speak in evil — extortion;
from on high they speak.

[9] They have set their mouth in the heavens,
and their tongue walks on the earth.

[10] Therefore his people return here,
and waters of fullness are drained out to them.

[11] And they say, "How does ⟦el|El|God⟧ know?
And is there knowledge in ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧?"

[12] Behold, these are the wicked;
and at ease forever, they have increased wealth.

[13] Surely in vain I have made my heart pure
and washed my palms in innocence.

[14] And I was plagued all the day,
and my reproof was every morning.

[15] If I had said, "Let me recount thus,"
behold, with the generation of your sons I would have dealt treacherously.

[16] And I thought, to know this;
it was toil in my eyes,

[17] until I came into the sanctuaries of ⟦el|El|God⟧;
I understood their end.

[18] Surely in smooth places you set them;
you have made them fall to ruins.

[19] How they have become a thing that appalls, as in a moment!
They have ceased, they have come to an end from terrors.

[20] Like a dream on waking,
⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, when you rouse yourself, you will despise their image.

[21] When my heart grew sour,
and I was pierced in my kidneys,

[22] and I was brutish and did not know;
a beast I was with you.

[23] And I am continually with you;
you have taken hold of my right hand.

[24] With your counsel you will lead me,
and afterward you will take me to glory.

[25] Who is there for me in the heavens?
And with you I have no delight on the earth.

[26] My flesh and my heart have failed;
the Rock of my heart and my share is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ forever.

[27] For behold, those far from you shall perish;
you have annihilated everyone who plays the prostitute away from you.

[28] And I — the nearness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is good for me;
I have set in ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ my refuge,
to recount all your works.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 26, 28) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v28) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token).
- **El** (v11, 17) — Hebrew *El*, retained; **Adonai** (v20) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"A Psalm of Asaph" → "A psalm. Of Asaph."** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְאָסָף, the first words of v1: "A psalm", and לְאָסָף "by", "for" or "belonging to Asaph" ("of" leaves it open), as Ps 74–83.
- **"Truly God is good to Israel, even to such as are of a clean heart" → "Surely good to Israel is Elohim, to the pure of heart"** (v1) — Hebrew אַךְ טוֹב לְיִשְׂרָאֵל אֱלֹהִים לְבָרֵי לֵבָב, in the Hebrew order; אַךְ "surely" (also v13, 18: the three turns of the psalm); בַּר "pure"; "even" is not in the Hebrew. A redivision לַיָּשָׁר אֵל "to the upright is El" is proposed (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"But as for me, my feet were almost gone; my steps had well nigh slipped" → "And I — my feet had almost turned aside; my steps had all but been poured out"** (v2) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי כִּמְעַט נָטָיוּ רַגְלָי כְּאַיִן שֻׁפְּכוּ אֲשֻׁרָי; qere נָטָיוּ, שֻׁפְּכוּ (ketiv נטוי, שפכה: the same verbs, other forms, no change of sense); נָטָה "turn"; כְּאַיִן, lit. "as nothing" = all but; שָׁפַךְ "pour out" (= slip, as water spilled: kept).
- **"For I was envious at the foolish, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked" → "For I envied the boastful; the shalom of the wicked I saw"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־קִנֵּאתִי בַּהוֹלְלִים שְׁלוֹם רְשָׁעִים אֶרְאֶה; קִנֵּא בְּ "envy" (Ps 37:1); הוֹלְלִים "the boastful" (Ps 5:5); שָׁלוֹם (token; not "prosperity"); no "when": two clauses side by side; אֶרְאֶה imperfect (what I kept seeing).
- **"For there are no bands in their death: but their strength is firm" → "For there are no bonds at their death, and fat is their body"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי אֵין חַרְצֻבּוֹת לְמוֹתָם וּבָרִיא אוּלָם; חַרְצֻבּוֹת "bonds" (Isa 58:6), here the pains that bind; לְמוֹתָם lit. "to their death"; בָּרִיא "fat"; אוּל "body" (uncertain: "strength", "belly"). Many redivide לָמוֹ תָּם "for them; whole and fat is their body" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"They are not in trouble as other men; neither are they plagued like other men" → "In the toil of mortal man they are not, and with mankind they are not plagued"** (v5) — Hebrew בַּעֲמַל אֱנוֹשׁ אֵינֵמוֹ וְעִם־אָדָם לֹא יְנֻגָּעוּ; עָמָל "toil"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man" and אָדָם "mankind" kept apart; נֻגַּע "be plagued" (the root of נֶגַע "plague"; also v14).
- **"Therefore pride compasseth them about as a chain; violence covereth them as a garment" → "Therefore pride is their necklace; violence wraps them as a garment"** (v6) — Hebrew לָכֵן עֲנָקַתְמוֹ גַאֲוָה יַעֲטָף־שִׁית חָמָס לָמוֹ; עָנַק, a verb from עֲנָק "necklace", lit. "pride has necklaced them"; גַּאֲוָה "pride"; עָטַף "wrap"; שִׁית "garment" ("as" supplied: lit. "a garment of violence wraps them", or "violence wraps itself as a garment for them").
- **"Their eyes stand out with fatness: they have more than heart could wish" → "Their eye has gone out from fat; the imaginings of the heart have passed over"** (v7) — Hebrew יָצָא מֵחֵלֶב עֵינֵמוֹ עָבְרוּ מַשְׂכִּיּוֹת לֵבָב; עֵינֵמוֹ singular "their eye" (the Septuagint read עֲוֹנָמוֹ "their iniquity", from memory; the MT is kept); חֵלֶב "fat"; מַשְׂכִּיּוֹת "imaginings" (the word of carved figures, Lev 26:1: what the heart pictures); עָבַר "pass over" (= overflow, go beyond bounds).
- **"They are corrupt, and speak wickedly concerning oppression: they speak loftily" → "They jeer and speak in evil — extortion; from on high they speak"** (v8) — Hebrew יָמִיקוּ וִידַבְּרוּ בְרָע עֹשֶׁק מִמָּרוֹם יְדַבֵּרוּ; מוּק, only here, "jeer" (an Aramaic word); עֹשֶׁק "extortion" (as עוֹשֵׁק Ps 72:4); the accents join עֹשֶׁק to the first line, so it is "evil … extortion" that they speak (or "speak in evil of extortion"); מִמָּרוֹם "from on high".
- **"They set their mouth against the heavens" → "They have set their mouth in the heavens"** (v9) — Hebrew שַׁתּוּ בַשָּׁמַיִם פִּיהֶם; בְּ "in" (not "against"); פִּיהֶם singular "mouth"; תִּהֲלַךְ "walks".
- **"Therefore his people return hither: and waters of a full cup are wrung out to them" → "Therefore his people return here, and waters of fullness are drained out to them"** (v10) — Hebrew לָכֵן יָשׁוּב עַמּוֹ הֲלֹם וּמֵי מָלֵא יִמָּצוּ לָמוֹ; qere יָשׁוּב "return", ketiv ישיב "he brings back (his people)"; מֵי מָלֵא "waters of fullness" ("cup" is not in the Hebrew); מָצָה "drain". Whose people, and what the verse means (the people drawn to the wicked and drinking in their words?), is uncertain.
- **"How doth God know? and is there knowledge in the most High?" → "How does El know? And is there knowledge in Elyon?"** (v11) — Hebrew אֵיכָה יָדַע־אֵל וְיֵשׁ דֵּעָה בְעֶלְיוֹן; "And" kept; וְאָמְרוּ "And they say".
- **"these are the ungodly, who prosper in the world; they increase in riches" → "these are the wicked; and at ease forever, they have increased wealth"** (v12) — Hebrew וְשַׁלְוֵי עוֹלָם הִשְׂגּוּ־חָיִל; שָׁלֵו "at ease"; עוֹלָם "forever" (lit. "at ease of the age"; not "the world"); חַיִל "wealth"; רָשָׁע "wicked".
- **"Verily I have cleansed my heart in vain, and washed my hands in innocency" → "Surely in vain I have made my heart pure and washed my palms in innocence"** (v13) — Hebrew אַךְ־רִיק זִכִּיתִי לְבָבִי וָאֶרְחַץ בְּנִקָּיוֹן כַּפָּי; רִיק, lit. "emptiness", here as an adverb "in vain" (the Ps 1–4 row "an empty thing" is for the noun as object); זִכָּה "make pure"; כַּף "palm"; נִקָּיוֹן "innocence".
- **"For all the day long have I been plagued, and chastened every morning" → "And I was plagued all the day, and my reproof was every morning"** (v14) — Hebrew וָאֱהִי נָגוּעַ כָּל־הַיּוֹם וְתוֹכַחְתִּי לַבְּקָרִים; וָאֱהִי "And I was"; נָגוּעַ "plagued" (v5); תּוֹכַחַת "reproof" (a noun, Ps 38:14); לַבְּקָרִים lit. "to the mornings" = every morning.
- **"If I say, I will speak thus; behold, I should offend against the generation of thy children" → "If I had said, 'Let me recount thus,' behold, with the generation of your sons I would have dealt treacherously"** (v15) — Hebrew אִם־אָמַרְתִּי אֲסַפְּרָה כְמוֹ הִנֵּה דוֹר בָּנֶיךָ בָגָדְתִּי; אֲסַפְּרָה cohortative of סִפֵּר "recount"; כְמוֹ "thus" (or "like them"); הִנֵּה "behold" kept; בָּגַד "deal treacherously"; בָּנִים "sons".
- **"When I thought to know this, it was too painful for me" → "And I thought, to know this; it was toil in my eyes"** (v16) — Hebrew וָאֲחַשְּׁבָה לָדַעַת זֹאת עָמָל הוּא בְעֵינָי; qere הוּא, ketiv היא (no change of sense); עָמָל "toil" (v5); בְעֵינָי "in my eyes".
- **"the sanctuary of God; then understood I their end" → "the sanctuaries of El; I understood their end"** (v17) — Hebrew מִקְדְּשֵׁי־אֵל plural (the holy precincts, or a plural of majesty); "then" is not in the Hebrew; אָבִינָה, a cohortative form, in a past frame; אַחֲרִית "end" (lit. "after-part").
- **"Surely thou didst set them in slippery places: thou castedst them down into destruction" → "Surely in smooth places you set them; you have made them fall to ruins"** (v18) — Hebrew אַךְ בַּחֲלָקוֹת תָּשִׁית לָמוֹ הִפַּלְתָּם לְמַשּׁוּאוֹת; חֲלָקוֹת "smooth things" (Ps 12:2), here of the ground; מַשּׁוּאוֹת "ruins" (also Ps 74:3).
- **"How are they brought into desolation, as in a moment! they are utterly consumed with terrors" → "How they have become a thing that appalls, as in a moment! They have ceased, they have come to an end from terrors"** (v19) — Hebrew אֵיךְ הָיוּ לְשַׁמָּה כְרָגַע סָפוּ תַמּוּ מִן־בַּלָּהוֹת; שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls"; two verbs, סוּף "cease" and תַּם "come to an end"; בַּלָּהוֹת "terrors"; מִן "from".
- **"As a dream when one awaketh; so, O Lord, when thou awakest, thou shalt despise their image" → "Like a dream on waking, Adonai, when you rouse yourself, you will despise their image"** (v20) — Hebrew כַּחֲלוֹם מֵהָקִיץ אֲדֹנָי בָּעִיר צַלְמָם תִּבְזֶה; no "O" and no "so"; בָּעִיר is read as בְּהָעִיר "in rousing (yourself)", the hifil of עוּר; as pointed it can also be "in the city" (uncertain; the verse follows the common reading); צֶלֶם "image".
- **"Thus my heart was grieved, and I was pricked in my reins" → "When my heart grew sour, and I was pierced in my kidneys"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּי יִתְחַמֵּץ לְבָבִי וְכִלְיוֹתַי אֶשְׁתּוֹנָן; כִּי "when" (or "for"); הִתְחַמֵּץ, from חָמֵץ "be leavened": "grow sour"; שָׁנַן "sharpen": "be pierced"; כְּלָיוֹת "kidneys".
- **"So foolish was I, and ignorant: I was as a beast before thee" → "and I was brutish and did not know; a beast I was with you"** (v22) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי־בַעַר וְלֹא אֵדָע בְּהֵמוֹת הָיִיתִי עִמָּךְ; בַּעַר "brutish"; בְּהֵמוֹת is plural ("beasts": an intensive "a very beast", the word of Job 40:15); no "as"; עִמָּךְ "with you".
- **"Nevertheless I am continually with thee: thou hast holden me by my right hand" → "And I am continually with you; you have taken hold of my right hand"** (v23) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי "And I" (not "Nevertheless"); אָחַז בְּ "take hold of".
- **"Thou shalt guide me with thy counsel, and afterward receive me to glory" → "With your counsel you will lead me, and afterward you will take me to glory"** (v24) — Hebrew בַּעֲצָתְךָ תַנְחֵנִי וְאַחַר כָּבוֹד תִּקָּחֵנִי; נָחָה "lead"; לָקַח "take"; כָּבוֹד has no preposition: "to glory", "in glory", or "after glory" (as Zech 2:8): the sense is uncertain.
- **"Whom have I in heaven but thee? and there is none upon earth that I desire beside thee" → "Who is there for me in the heavens? And with you I have no delight on the earth"** (v25) — Hebrew מִי־לִי בַשָּׁמָיִם וְעִמְּךָ לֹא־חָפַצְתִּי בָאָרֶץ; "but thee" is not in the Hebrew; עִמְּךָ "with you" (= having you); חָפֵץ "delight".
- **"My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever" → "My flesh and my heart have failed; the Rock of my heart and my share is Elohim forever"** (v26) — Hebrew כָּלָה שְׁאֵרִי וּלְבָבִי צוּר־לְבָבִי וְחֶלְקִי אֱלֹהִים לְעוֹלָם; כָּלָה perfect "have failed" (not "may fail"); שְׁאֵר "flesh" (of the body here; the "near kin" of its row is the same word); no "but"; צוּר "Rock" as a name of God (not "strength"); חֵלֶק "share".
- **"thou hast destroyed all them that go a whoring from thee" → "you have annihilated everyone who plays the prostitute away from you"** (v27) — Hebrew הִצְמַתָּה כָּל־זוֹנֶה מִמֶּךָּ; הִצְמִית "annihilate" (Ps 18:40); זָנָה "play the prostitute"; מִן "away from".
- **"But it is good for me to draw near to God: I have put my trust in the Lord GOD, that I may declare all thy works" → "And I — the nearness of Elohim is good for me; I have set in Adonai Yahweh my refuge, to recount all your works"** (v28) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי קִרֲבַת אֱלֹהִים לִי־טוֹב שַׁתִּי בַּאדֹנָי יְהוִה מַחְסִי לְסַפֵּר כָּל־מַלְאֲכוֹתֶיךָ; קִרְבָה "nearness"; שִׁית "set"; מַחְסֶה "refuge" (not "trust"); סִפֵּר "recount"; מְלָאכָה "work". Lines by the accents (three).
- **Poetic structure** — crisis of envy (v1-14), resolution in sanctuary (v15-20), renewed intimacy (v21-28).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **Opens Book III** (73-89).
- **Wisdom struggle** — why wicked prosper. Resolution in v17.
- **"Who is there for me in the heavens?"** (v25) — pinnacle verse of piety.
