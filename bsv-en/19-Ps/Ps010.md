# Psalm 10

[1] Why, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do you stand far off,
hide for times in distress?

[2] In the pride of the wicked he hotly pursues the afflicted;
they are seized in the schemes that they have devised.

[3] For the wicked boasts of the craving of his soul,
and he who gets unjust gain blesses — he spurns ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] The wicked, according to the height of his nose, does not seek;
"There is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧" — all his schemes.

[5] His ways are firm at every time;
on high are your judgments, away from before him;
all his adversaries — he puffs at them.

[6] He says in his heart, "I shall not be shaken;
to generation and generation, one who is not in evil."

[7] His mouth is full of a curse and of deceits and oppression;
under his tongue are toil and wickedness.

[8] He sits in the place of ambush of the villages;
in the secret places he kills the innocent;
his eyes lie hidden for the helpless.

[9] He lies in ambush in the secret place, like a lion in his thicket;
he lies in ambush to snatch the afflicted;
he snatches the afflicted when he draws in his net.

[10] He is crushed, he bows down,
and a force of the dejected falls by his mighty ones.

[11] He says in his heart, "⟦el|El|God⟧ has forgotten;
he has hidden his face; he never sees."

[12] Arise, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧; ⟦el|El|God⟧, lift up your hand;
do not forget the humble.

[13] Why does the wicked spurn ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?
He says in his heart, "You will not require."

[14] You have seen, for you look upon toil and vexation,
to give it into your hand;
upon you the helpless leaves it;
of the fatherless you have been the helper.

[15] Break the arm of the wicked;
and the evil one — you will require his wickedness; you will not find it.

[16] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is king forever and ever;
nations have perished from his land.

[17] The desire of the humble you have heard, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
you establish their heart;
you make your ear give heed,

[18] to judge the fatherless and the crushed;
mortal man from the earth shall no more again strike terror.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 12, 16, 17) — Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים. **"God" → "El"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew אֵל.
- **Tenses** (v3–11, 13) — the wicked man is drawn with perfects and imperfects of what he always does; both are given as English present ("he says", Hebrew אָמַר "he has said"), the perfect of v14, 16, 17 as perfect.
- **"why hidest thou thyself in times of trouble?" → "hide for times in distress?"** (v1) — Hebrew לָמָה stands once and rules both lines; תַּעְלִים "hide" has no object ("yourself" or "your eyes" is supplied in other versions); לְעִתּוֹת בַּצָּרָה "for times in distress" (as 9:9). בְּרָחוֹק "far off".
- **"The wicked in his pride doth persecute the poor: let them be taken in the devices that they have imagined" → "In the pride of the wicked he hotly pursues the afflicted; they are seized in the schemes that they have devised"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּגַאֲוַת רָשָׁע "in the pride of the wicked"; יִדְלַק עָנִי, דָּלַק "hotly pursue" (Gen 31:36); it may also be read "the afflicted burns". יִתָּפְשׂוּ "they are seized" (or the wish "let them be seized"); who is seized, the afflicted or the wicked, is not said. מְזִמּוֹת "schemes" (also v4); חָשַׁב "devise".
- **"boasteth of his heart's desire, and blesseth the covetous, whom the LORD abhorreth" → "boasts of the craving of his soul, and he who gets unjust gain blesses — he spurns Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew הִלֵּל עַל "praise over" = boast of; תַּאֲוַת נַפְשׁוֹ "the craving of his soul"; וּבֹצֵעַ בֵּרֵךְ נִאֵץ יְהוָה: בֹּצֵעַ "one who gets unjust gain" (בֶּצַע) is the subject of "blesses", and נִאֵץ "spurn" (also v13) follows without "and". "Blesses" may stand for "curses", as the text does elsewhere (1 Kgs 21:10; Job 1:5); or he blesses himself. "whom" is not written.
- **"through the pride of his countenance, will not seek after God: God is not in all his thoughts" → "according to the height of his nose, does not seek; 'There is no Elohim' — all his schemes"** (v4) — Hebrew כְּגֹבַהּ אַפּוֹ "according to the height of his nose" (his nose held high = haughtiness); בַּל־יִדְרֹשׁ "does not seek", without an object; it may also be his words, "He will not require" (as v13). אֵין אֱלֹהִים כָּל־מְזִמּוֹתָיו "'There is no Elohim' — all his schemes" (= that is all his scheming).
- **"His ways are always grievous; thy judgments are far above out of his sight" → "His ways are firm at every time; on high are your judgments, away from before him"** (v5) — Hebrew יָחִילוּ, from חִיל "be firm, endure" (uncertain; also "prosper"; KJV "grievous" takes the other חִיל, "writhe"). Ways: the qere דְרָכָיו "his ways"; the ketiv has דרכו "his way" (same sense). מָרוֹם "on high"; מִנֶּגְדּוֹ "away from before him". צוֹרְרָיו "his adversaries"; יָפִיחַ בָּהֶם "he puffs at them" (= scorns; as 12:5).
- **"I shall not be moved: for I shall never be in adversity" → "I shall not be shaken; to generation and generation, one who is not in evil"** (v6) — Hebrew בַּל־אֶמּוֹט, מוֹט nifal "be shaken"; לְדֹר וָדֹר "to generation and generation"; אֲשֶׁר לֹא־בְרָע "who (is) not in evil" (= I am one whom no evil reaches); "for" and "never" are not written; the line is terse and uncertain.
- **"full of cursing and deceit and fraud: under his tongue is mischief and vanity" → "full of a curse and of deceits and oppression: under his tongue are toil and wickedness"** (v7) — Hebrew אָלָה "a curse" (in Hebrew order it comes first: "a curse his mouth is full of"); מִרְמוֹת "deceits", plural; תֹּךְ "oppression"; עָמָל "toil" (the trouble he makes for others; also v14); אָוֶן "wickedness". Rom 3:14 cites the verse in its Greek form.
- **"He sitteth in the lurking places of the villages: in the secret places doth he murder the innocent: his eyes are privily set against the poor" → "He sits in the place of ambush of the villages; in the secret places he kills the innocent; his eyes lie hidden for the helpless"** (v8) — Hebrew מַאְרַב "place of ambush"; חֲצֵרִים "villages" (unwalled settlements); מִסְתָּרִים "secret places"; הָרַג "kill" (not רָצַח "murder"); נָקִי "innocent"; צָפַן "hide": "his eyes lie hidden" (= watch from hiding). חֵלְכָה (v8, 14; ketiv v10) "the helpless": the word is found only in this psalm; meaning uncertain ("hapless, wretched").
- **"He lieth in wait secretly as a lion in his den … to catch the poor … when he draweth him into his net" → "He lies in ambush in the secret place, like a lion in his thicket … to snatch the afflicted … when he draws in his net"** (v9) — Hebrew אָרַב "lie in ambush" (twice); בַּמִּסְתָּר "in the secret place" (as v8); סֻכֹּה "his thicket" (a covert, a booth of branches); חָטַף "snatch" (twice); בְּמָשְׁכוֹ בְרִשְׁתּוֹ "in his drawing with his net": "him" is not written.
- **"He croucheth, and humbleth himself, that the poor may fall by his strong ones" → "He is crushed, he bows down, and a force of the dejected falls by his mighty ones"** (v10) — the qere is translated: יִדְכֶּה "he is crushed" (KJV "he croucheth", of the wicked like the lion; who is meant is not said); the ketiv ודכה has "and" and may be read "and he crushes". יָשֹׁחַ "he bows down". The qere חֵיל כָּאִים "a force of the dejected" (חַיִל "force", as of an army; כָּאִים from כָּאָה "be dejected", Ezek 13:22); the ketiv חלכאים "the helpless ones" (plural of חֵלְכָה, v8). The verb "falls" is singular. עֲצוּמָיו "his mighty ones" (his strong limbs or his strong men). "that" is not written. Meaning uncertain.
- **"he hideth his face; he will never see it" → "he has hidden his face; he never sees"** (v11) — Hebrew הִסְתִּיר פָּנָיו "he has hidden his face"; בַּל־רָאָה לָנֶצַח, lit. "he has not seen, forever"; "it" is not written.
- **"O God, lift up thine hand: forget not the humble" → "El, lift up your hand; do not forget the humble"** (v12) — the qere עֲנָוִים "humble"; the ketiv has עניים "afflicted".
- **"Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God? he hath said in his heart, Thou wilt not require it" → "Why does the wicked spurn Elohim? He says in his heart, 'You will not require.'"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־מֶה "on what account"; נִאֵץ "spurn" (as v3); לֹא תִדְרֹשׁ "you will not require" (דָּרַשׁ of a reckoning, as 9:12; also v15); "it" is not written.
- **"Thou hast seen it; for thou beholdest mischief and spite, to requite it with thy hand: the poor committeth himself unto thee; thou art the helper of the fatherless" → "You have seen, for you look upon toil and vexation, to give it into your hand; upon you the helpless leaves it; of the fatherless you have been the helper"** (v14) — Hebrew רָאִתָה "you have seen" (no object); עָמָל "toil" (as v7) and כַּעַס "vexation"; הִבִּיט "look upon"; לָתֵת בְּיָדֶךָ "to give into your hand" ("it" supplied; "to requite" is a reading of the sense); עָלֶיךָ יַעֲזֹב חֵלֶכָה "upon you the helpless leaves" ("it" supplied: his cause, or himself); הָיִיתָ "you have been".
- **"Break thou the arm of the wicked and the evil man: seek out his wickedness till thou find none" → "Break the arm of the wicked; and the evil one — you will require his wickedness; you will not find it"** (v15) — Hebrew וָרָע "and the evil one" opens the second half (the accents divide after רָשָׁע); תִּדְרוֹשׁ "you will require" (or "may you require"; as v13); בַל־תִּמְצָא "you will not find", without "till": when it is required, it is no longer found. "it" is supplied.
- **"The LORD is King for ever and ever: the heathen are perished out of his land" → "Yahweh is king forever and ever; nations have perished from his land"** (v16) — Hebrew מֶלֶךְ עוֹלָם וָעֶד, lit. "king of the age and ever" (no לְ); אָבְדוּ "have perished"; גּוֹיִם without the article.
- **"thou hast heard the desire of the humble: thou wilt prepare their heart, thou wilt cause thine ear to hear" → "The desire of the humble you have heard … you establish their heart; you make your ear give heed"** (v17) — Hebrew תַּאֲוָה, here of a good wish: "desire" (the "craving" of v3: one word); עֲנָוִים "humble"; הֵכִין "establish" (or "prepare"); תַּקְשִׁיב אָזְנֶךָ "you make your ear give heed" (הִקְשִׁיב "give heed").
- **"To judge the fatherless and the oppressed, that the man of the earth may no more oppress" → "to judge the fatherless and the crushed; mortal man from the earth shall no more again strike terror"** (v18) — Hebrew יָתוֹם "fatherless"; דַּךְ "crushed" (as 9:9); בַּל־יוֹסִיף עוֹד "shall not add again" = no more again; לַעֲרֹץ "to strike terror" (עָרַץ, no object); אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; מִן־הָאָרֶץ "from the earth": man who is of the earth, or "strike terror (and drive) from the land": not decided.

### Notes
- **No heading** — Ps 10 continues the acrostic of Ps 9 (see the Notes there): ל (10:1), ק (10:12), ר (10:14), ש (10:15), ת (10:17); the letters between do not appear in 10:2–11. Many qere in v5, 10 and 12.
- **"There is no Elohim"** (v4) — the wicked man's thought: he reckons with no one who will require it (v11, 13).
