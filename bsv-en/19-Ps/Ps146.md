let me make music to my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ while I still am.

[3] Do not trust in nobles,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 5, 10) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"El"** (v5) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v1, 10) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ, "praise Yah" (יָהּ, the short form of the name), with its token. **"Praise the LORD, O my soul" → "Praise Yahweh, my soul"** — הַלְלִי נַפְשִׁי, a feminine singular command to the soul; "O" is not written.
- **"While I live will I praise the LORD: I will sing praises unto my God while I have any being" → "Let me praise Yahweh in my life; let me make music to my Elohim while I still am"** (v2) — Hebrew אֲהַלְלָה … אֲזַמְּרָה, cohortatives ("let me"); בְּחַיָּי "in my life"; זִמֵּר "make music"; בְּעוֹדִי "while I still am" (lit. "in my yet"). As Ps 104:33, where the first verb is אָשִׁירָה "let me sing".
- **"Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help" → "Do not trust in nobles, in a son of man, who has no salvation"** (v3) — Hebrew אַל־תִּבְטְחוּ "do not trust"; נְדִיבִים "nobles" (lit. "willing ones", as 1 Sam 2:8); בְּבֶן־אָדָם "in a son of man" (no article, no "nor"); שֶׁאֵין לוֹ תְשׁוּעָה "who has no salvation" (lit. "to whom there is no salvation": whether he has none to give or none for himself is left open).
- **"His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish" → "His spirit goes out, he returns to his ground; on that day his thoughts perish"** (v4) — Hebrew תֵּצֵא רוּחוֹ: רוּחַ "spirit" (also "breath, wind": one word; as Ps 104:29); לְאַדְמָתוֹ "to his ground" (אֲדָמָה, which sounds with אָדָם "man" of v3); אָבְדוּ, a perfect, "perish"; עֶשְׁתֹּנֹת is found only here: "thoughts" (plans), from עָשַׁת "think"; the sense is not certain. No "and" joins the clauses.
- **"Happy is he that hath the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in the LORD his God" → "Happy is he who has the El of Jacob as his help, whose hope is on Yahweh his Elohim"** (v5) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "happy" (not "blessed": that is בָּרוּךְ); שֶׁאֵל יַעֲקֹב בְּעֶזְרוֹ, lit. "that the El of Jacob is in (= as) his help"; שִׂבְרוֹ עַל "his hope is on".
- **"Which made heaven, and earth … which keepeth truth for ever" → "the maker of heavens and earth … who keeps truth forever"** (v6) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה שָׁמַיִם וָאָרֶץ, a participle, "the maker of heavens and earth" (not a past tense; no articles; as Ps 115:15; 121:2; 124:8; 134:3); the participles run on through v7; הַשֹּׁמֵר אֱמֶת "who keeps truth".
- **"Which executeth judgment for the oppressed: which giveth food to the hungry. The LORD looseth the prisoners" → "who does judgment for those who are extorted from, who gives bread to the hungry. Yahweh looses those who are bound"** (v7) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה מִשְׁפָּט "does judgment"; עֲשׁוּקִים, passive of עָשַׁק "extort" (as Ps 103:6); לֶחֶם "bread"; מַתִּיר (hifil of נָתַר) "looses"; אֲסוּרִים "those who are bound" (passive of אָסַר "bind"; "prisoner" is אָסִיר).
- **"The LORD openeth the eyes of the blind: the LORD raiseth them that are bowed down" → "Yahweh opens the eyes of the blind; Yahweh straightens those who are bent down"** (v8) — Hebrew פֹּקֵחַ עִוְרִים, lit. "opens the blind": "the eyes of" is supplied because פָּקַח is said only of opening eyes (and ears); זֹקֵף כְּפוּפִים "straightens the bent down" (זָקַף "raise upright", only here and Ps 145:14).
- **"The LORD preserveth the strangers; he relieveth the fatherless and widow: but the way of the wicked he turneth upside down" → "Yahweh keeps sojourners; the fatherless and the widow he upholds, and the way of the wicked he makes crooked"** (v9) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר אֶת־גֵּרִים "keeps sojourners"; יָתוֹם וְאַלְמָנָה "fatherless and widow" first, as the Hebrew order; יְעוֹדֵד (polel of עוּד) "upholds" (relieve, restore; the sense is not certain; as Ps 147:6); וְ "and", not "but"; יְעַוֵּת (piel of עָוַת) "makes crooked" (bends, subverts).
- **"The LORD shall reign for ever, even thy God, O Zion, unto all generations" → "Yahweh shall reign forever, your Elohim, Zion, to generation and generation"** (v10) — Hebrew יִמְלֹךְ, imperfect (also a wish, "may Yahweh reign"; as Exod 15:18); לְדֹר וָדֹר "to generation and generation" (as Ps 49:11); "even" and "O" are not written; "your" is feminine singular (Zion).

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew; our verse numbers are the Hebrew ones.
- **First of the closing Hallel** (Pss 146–150) — five psalms that open and close with Halleluyah.
- **Structure** — the singer's vow (v1–2), not princes but the El of Jacob (v3–5), Yahweh's acts in a chain of participles (v6–9), Yahweh reigns (v10).
