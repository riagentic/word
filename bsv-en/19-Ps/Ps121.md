# Psalm 121

A song for the ascents.

[1] I lift up my eyes to the mountains:
from where will my help come?

[2] My help is from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the maker of heavens and earth.

[3] May he not let your foot slip;
may he who keeps you not slumber.

[4] Behold, he will not slumber and will not sleep,
he who keeps ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is your keeper;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is your shade by your right hand.

[6] By day the sun shall not strike you,
nor the moon by night.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will keep you from all evil;
he will keep your soul.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will keep your going out and your coming in
from now and forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 5, 7, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees" → "A song for the ascents"** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר לַמַּעֲלוֹת, the first words of v1: here לְ "for, of" stands where the other songs (Ps 120; 122–134) have the article, הַמַּעֲלוֹת "of the ascents".
- **"I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help" → "I lift up my eyes to the mountains: from where will my help come?"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶשָּׂא עֵינַי אֶל־הֶהָרִים: הַר "mountain" (not "hill"); אֶשָּׂא is an imperfect ("I lift up" or "I will lift up"). מֵאַיִן יָבֹא עֶזְרִי, a question (KJV took it as "from whence"); יָבֹא "will come".
- **"My help cometh from the LORD, which made heaven and earth" → "My help is from Yahweh, the maker of heavens and earth"** (v2) — Hebrew עֶזְרִי מֵעִם יְהוָה, no verb ("is" supplied), מֵעִם "from with"; עֹשֵׂה שָׁמַיִם וָאָרֶץ, a participle, "the maker of heavens and earth" (שָׁמַיִם is plural; no articles are written). So Ps 124:8.
- **"He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber" → "May he not let your foot slip; may he who keeps you not slumber"** (v3) — Hebrew אַל־יִתֵּן … אַל־יָנוּם: אַל with the jussive is a wish or a plea ("may he not"), not the plain "he will not" of v4 (לֹא); the speaker changes to "you" here, and v3 may be the pilgrim's companion speaking. לַמּוֹט רַגְלֶךָ, literally "give your foot to slipping" (מוֹט). שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep" → "Behold, he will not slumber and will not sleep, he who keeps Israel"** (v4) — Hebrew הִנֵּה לֹא־יָנוּם וְלֹא יִישָׁן שׁוֹמֵר יִשְׂרָאֵל: לֹא twice (a statement), and the subject stands last, in the second line.
- **"the LORD is thy shade upon thy right hand" → "Yahweh is your shade by your right hand"** (v5) — Hebrew צִלְּךָ עַל־יַד יְמִינֶךָ: צֵל "shade" (of protection, as Num 14:9); עַל־יַד יְמִינֶךָ, literally "upon the hand of your right" = beside your right hand. שֹׁמְרֶךָ "your keeper" (participle of שָׁמַר).
- **"The sun shall not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night" → "By day the sun shall not strike you, nor the moon by night"** (v6) — Hebrew יוֹמָם "by day" stands first; הִכָּה "strike"; the second line has no verb of its own.
- **"The LORD shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul" → "Yahweh will keep you from all evil; he will keep your soul"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew יִשְׁמָרְךָ … יִשְׁמֹר: שָׁמַר "keep", the same verb as v3–5 and v8 (KJV "preserve" in v7, 8); the forms may also be read as wishes ("may Yahweh keep you"). רָע "evil"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"from this time forth, and even for evermore" → "from now and forever"** (v8) — Hebrew מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם, "from now and until forever" (עוֹלָם). צֵאתְךָ וּבוֹאֶךָ "your going out and your coming in".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **"Keep"** — שָׁמַר stands six times (v3, 4, 5, 7 twice, 8).
