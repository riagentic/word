# Psalm 48

A song, a psalm. Of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧.

[1] Great is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and greatly praised
in the city of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
his holy mountain.

[2] Beautiful in elevation,
the joy of all the earth,
Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, the remote parts of the north,
the town of the great King.

[3] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in her citadels
has made himself known as a secure height.

[4] For behold, the kings met;
they passed on together.

[5] They saw; so they were astounded;
they were dismayed, they were alarmed.

[6] Trembling took hold of them there,
anguish like one giving birth.

[7] By an east wind you break in pieces
the ships of Tarshish.

[8] As we have heard, so we have seen
in the city of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
in the city of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will establish it forever. Selah.

[9] We have thought, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, of your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧
in the midst of your temple.

[10] Like your name, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
so is your praise upon the ends of the earth;
your right hand is full of righteousness.

[11] Let Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ rejoice;
let the daughters of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ be glad
on account of your judgments.

[12] Go around ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, and circle her;
number her towers.

[13] Set your heart to her rampart;
pass through her citadels,
so that you may recount to a later generation

[14] that this is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ forever and ever;
he will lead us upon death.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 3, 8, 9, 10, 14) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of hosts"** (v8) — compound divine name.
- **"A Song and Psalm for the sons of Korah" → "A song, a psalm. Of the sons of Korah."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר מִזְמוֹר לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח, in this order, with no "and". לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" the sons of Korah; "of" leaves it open.
- **"greatly to be praised … in the mountain of his holiness" → "greatly praised … his holy mountain"** (v1) — Hebrew וּמְהֻלָּל מְאֹד, a participle, "praised" (as 1 Chr 16:25); הַר־קָדְשׁוֹ stands beside "the city" with no "in", literally "the mountain of his holiness".
- **"Beautiful for situation … is mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King" → "Beautiful in elevation … Mount Zion, the remote parts of the north, the town of the great King"** (v2) — Hebrew יְפֵה נוֹף: נוֹף only here, taken as "height, elevation" (uncertain); no verb in the verse. יַרְכְּתֵי צָפוֹן, literally "the flanks of the north" (as 2 Kgs 19:23 "the remote parts of Lebanon"); צָפוֹן "north" is also the name of a mountain (Zaphon) of Canaanite myth, and Isa 14:13 has the same phrase of the mount of assembly. קִרְיַת "town" (beside עִיר "city" in v1, 8); מֶלֶךְ רָב, רָב "great, many" (not the גָּדוֹל of v1).
- **"God is known in her palaces for a refuge" → "Elohim in her citadels has made himself known as a secure height"** (v3) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים בְּאַרְמְנוֹתֶיהָ נוֹדַע לְמִשְׂגָּב, in this order; נוֹדַע nifal, "has made himself known" or "is known"; אַרְמוֹן "citadel"; מִשְׂגָּב "secure height" (a high place out of reach, as 2 Sam 22:3).
- **"the kings were assembled, they passed by together" → "the kings met; they passed on together"** (v4) — Hebrew נוֹעֲדוּ, nifal of יעד, "met (by appointment)"; עָבְרוּ יַחְדָּו "passed on together": whether they advanced or passed away is open.
- **"They saw it, and so they marvelled; they were troubled, and hasted away" → "They saw; so they were astounded; they were dismayed, they were alarmed"** (v5) — Hebrew הֵמָּה רָאוּ כֵּן תָּמָהוּ נִבְהֲלוּ נֶחְפָּזוּ: no object "it", כֵּן "so"; נִבְהַל "be dismayed"; נֶחְפַּז, literally "hurry away in alarm" (as Deut 20:3 "be alarmed"), no "and".
- **"Fear took hold upon them there, and pain, as of a woman in travail" → "Trembling took hold of them there, anguish like one giving birth"** (v6) — Hebrew רְעָדָה "trembling"; חִיל "anguish" (the writhing of labor), no "and"; כַּיּוֹלֵדָה, a feminine participle, "like one giving birth".
- **"Thou breakest the ships of Tarshish with an east wind" → "By an east wind you break in pieces the ships of Tarshish"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּרוּחַ קָדִים תְּשַׁבֵּר, "east wind" first; שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces"; the imperfect may be past ("you broke") or what Elohim does. קָדִים literally "front" (east for one facing the sunrise).
- **"God will establish it for ever" → "Elohim will establish it forever"** (v8) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים יְכוֹנְנֶהָ עַד־עוֹלָם, a clause of its own (no "which").
- **"We have thought of thy lovingkindness, O God" → "We have thought, Elohim, of your chesed"** (v9) — Hebrew דִּמִּינוּ אֱלֹהִים חַסְדֶּךָ, the address between verb and object; דִּמָּה piel "think, picture" (also "compare": uncertain; 50:21 "you thought"); *chesed* token.
- **"so is thy praise unto the ends of the earth" → "so is your praise upon the ends of the earth"** (v10) — Hebrew עַל־קַצְוֵי־אֶרֶץ, "upon the ends"; no verb (the copula only). צֶדֶק מָלְאָה יְמִינֶךָ, "with righteousness your right hand is full".
- **"Let mount Zion rejoice, let the daughters of Judah be glad, because of thy judgments" → "Let Mount Zion rejoice; let the daughters of Judah be glad on account of your judgments"** (v11) — Hebrew יִשְׂמַח "rejoice" and תָּגֵלְנָה "be glad" (as Ps 96:11); לְמַעַן before a noun "on account of".
- **"Walk about Zion, and go round about her: tell the towers thereof" → "Go around Zion, and circle her; number her towers"** (v12) — Hebrew סֹבּוּ … וְהַקִּיפוּהָ (סָבַב "go around", הִקִּיף "circle"); סִפְרוּ, qal of סָפַר "number", against the piel תְּסַפְּרוּ "recount" of v13 (one root).
- **"Mark ye well her bulwarks, consider her palaces; that ye may tell it to the generation following" → "Set your heart to her rampart; pass through her citadels, so that you may recount to a later generation"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׁיתוּ לִבְּכֶם לְחֵילָה, "set your heart to her rampart" (חֵיל singular); פַּסְּגוּ only here, meaning uncertain ("pass through", "consider", "go between"); לְמַעַן תְּסַפְּרוּ "so that you may recount", no "it"; לְדוֹר אַחֲרוֹן "to a later (last) generation".
- **"For this God is our God for ever and ever: he will be our guide even unto death" → "that this is Elohim, our Elohim forever and ever; he will lead us upon death"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי זֶה אֱלֹהִים אֱלֹהֵינוּ עוֹלָם וָעֶד: כִּי may be "that" (what is recounted, v13) or "for"; זֶה may also go with the noun, "this Elohim is our Elohim" (KJV). הוּא יְנַהֲגֵנוּ, "he, he will lead us" (נִהַג piel, of a shepherd leading; "drive" in Exod 10:13). **עַל־מוּת is translated as printed, "upon death"** (מוּת, the infinitive "dying"), and its sense is in doubt: KJV "unto death"; read as one word עַלְמוּת "forever" by the Septuagint (from memory) and by the earlier form of this verse; or a tune heading like 9:1 עַל־מוּת לַבֵּן and 46:1 עַל־עֲלָמוֹת, standing at the end of this psalm or belonging to the next.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–14 = Heb v2–15.
- **Zion hymn** — praise of Jerusalem.
- **"The remote parts of the north"** (v2) — *yarketei tzafon*; mythological Mount-of-assembly (cf. Isa 14:13).
