# Psalm 63

A psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my ⟦el|El|God⟧ are you; earnestly I seek you;
my soul thirsts for you;
my flesh longs for you,
in a land of dryness, and weary, without water.

[2] So in the Holy I have beheld you,
to see your strength and your glory.

[3] For better is your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ than life;
my lips shall praise you.

[4] So I will bless you in my life;
in your name I will lift up my palms.

[5] As with fat and fatness my soul shall be sated,
and with lips of ringing cries my mouth shall praise,

[6] when I remember you upon my couch,
in the watches I softly recite of you;

[7] for you have been a help to me,
and in the shade of your wings I shout for joy.

[8] My soul has clung after you;
your right hand has held me fast.

[9] And they — for ruin they seek my soul;
they shall go into the lower parts of the earth.

[10] They shall pour him out upon the hands of the sword;
a portion of foxes they shall be.

[11] And the king shall be glad in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
everyone who swears by him shall glory,
for the mouth of those who speak falsehood shall be stopped.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v1) — Hebrew *El*.
- **"A Psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah" → "A psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah."** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד בִּהְיוֹתוֹ בְּמִדְבַּר יְהוּדָה. לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open. מִדְבָּר "wilderness".
- **"O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee" → "Elohim, my El are you; earnestly I seek you"** (v1) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים אֵלִי אַתָּה, in this order, without "O". אֲשַׁחֲרֶךָּ, from שִׁחֵר "seek earnestly"; the root is that of שַׁחַר "dawn", so "seek early" (KJV) is also read.
- **"my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is" → "my flesh longs for you, in a land of dryness, and weary, without water"** (v1) — Hebrew כָּמַהּ, found only here: "long, yearn" (also taken as "faint"; uncertain). בְּאֶרֶץ־צִיָּה וְעָיֵף בְּלִי־מָיִם: "in", not "as in" (the Hebrew has בְּ, not כְּ); צִיָּה "dryness, drought"; עָיֵף "weary" is masculine and "land" is feminine, so "weary" may describe the speaker ("and I am weary") as much as the land. בְּלִי־מָיִם "without water".
- **"To see thy power and thy glory, so as I have seen thee in the sanctuary" → "So in the Holy I have beheld you, to see your strength and your glory"** (v2) — Hebrew כֵּן בַּקֹּדֶשׁ חֲזִיתִיךָ לִרְאוֹת עֻזְּךָ וּכְבוֹדֶךָ, in this order. בַּקֹּדֶשׁ "in the Holy" (the holy place; also read "in holiness"); חָזָה "behold"; עֹז "strength" (כֹּחַ is "power").
- **"Because thy lovingkindness is better than life, my lips shall praise thee" → "For better is your steadfast love than life; my lips shall praise you"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־טוֹב חַסְדְּךָ מֵחַיִּים, "better" first; שִׁבַּח "praise" (another verb than הִלֵּל of v5).
- **"Thus will I bless thee while I live: I will lift up my hands in thy name" → "So I will bless you in my life; in your name I will lift up my palms"** (v4) — Hebrew בְחַיָּי "in my life"; כַפָּי, כַּף "palm" (not יָד "hand").
- **"My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness; and my mouth shall praise thee with joyful lips" → "As with fat and fatness my soul shall be sated, and with lips of ringing cries my mouth shall praise"** (v5) — Hebrew חֵלֶב וָדֶשֶׁן, "fat and fatness" (דֶּשֶׁן, the "fat ashes" of the altar; here richness); שָׂבַע "be sated". וְשִׂפְתֵי רְנָנוֹת "and lips of ringing cries" (רְנָנָה, one root with רִנָּה); יְהַלֶּל־פִּי "my mouth shall praise": "you" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches" → "when I remember you upon my couch, in the watches I softly recite of you"** (v6) — Hebrew אִם־זְכַרְתִּיךָ "when (if) I remember you", a perfect. יָצוּעַ "couch". בְּאַשְׁמֻרוֹת "in the watches" ("night" is not in the Hebrew). אֶהְגֶּה־בָּךְ, הָגָה "softly recite" (as Ps 1:2; Josh 1:8; "meditate" names only thinking); literally "recite in you": "of" is supplied. No "and" between the lines.
- **"Because thou hast been my help, therefore in the shadow of thy wings will I rejoice" → "for you have been a help to me, and in the shade of your wings I shout for joy"** (v7) — Hebrew עֶזְרָתָה לִּי "a help to me"; צֵל "shade"; אֲרַנֵּן, רָנַן piel "shout for joy" (as Ps 33:1), an imperfect.
- **"My soul followeth hard after thee: thy right hand upholdeth me" → "My soul has clung after you; your right hand has held me fast"** (v8) — Hebrew דָּבְקָה נַפְשִׁי אַחֲרֶיךָ, דָּבַק "cling", a perfect; בִּי תָּמְכָה יְמִינֶךָ "on me your right hand has taken hold" (תָּמַךְ "hold fast"), a perfect; "me" stands first.
- **"But those that seek my soul, to destroy it, shall go into the lower parts of the earth" → "And they — for ruin they seek my soul; they shall go into the lower parts of the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew וְהֵמָּה "and they", set first; לְשׁוֹאָה "for ruin"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul". תַּחְתִּיּוֹת הָאָרֶץ "the lower parts of the earth".
- **"They shall fall by the sword: they shall be a portion for foxes" → "They shall pour him out upon the hands of the sword; a portion of foxes they shall be"** (v10) — Hebrew יַגִּירֻהוּ עַל־יְדֵי־חָרֶב, hifil of נָגַר "pour out": "they (unnamed) shall pour him out", "him" singular (each of the enemies, or their leader); עַל־יְדֵי "upon the hands of" (= into the power of). מְנָת שֻׁעָלִים "a portion of foxes" (שׁוּעָל "fox"; the word may also cover the jackal).
- **"But the king shall rejoice in God; every one that sweareth by him shall glory: but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped" → "And the king shall be glad in Elohim; everyone who swears by him shall glory, for the mouth of those who speak falsehood shall be stopped"** (v11) — Hebrew וְהַמֶּלֶךְ "and the king"; יִתְהַלֵּל, הִתְהַלֵּל "glory" (as Ps 34:2); בּוֹ "by him" (Elohim, or the king: open). כִּי "for"; פִּי "the mouth", singular; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v11 = Heb v12.
- **Poetic structure** — thirst for *Elohim* (v1–2), satisfaction in the vision in the Holy (v3–5), the night (v6–8), the end of the enemies and the king's gladness (v9–11).
- **Wilderness psalm** — thirst imagery for *Elohim*.
- **"Chesed better than life"** — beloved line.
