# Psalm 68

To the overseer. Of David, a psalm, a song.

[1] Let ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ arise,
let his enemies be scattered,
and let those who hate him flee from his face.

[2] As smoke is driven away, you drive them away;
as wax melts from the face of fire,
let the wicked perish from the face of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] But let the righteous rejoice,
let them be jubilant before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and let them exult with gladness.

[4] Sing to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, make music to his name;
build up the road for him who rides through the steppes;
in ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧ is his name, and be jubilant before him.

[5] Father of the fatherless and judge of widows
is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in his holy abode.

[6] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ makes the solitary dwell in a house;
he brings out prisoners into prosperity;
but the stubborn abide in a parched land.

[7] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, when you went out before your people,
when you marched through the desert, Selah,

[8] the earth quaked, the heavens also dropped from the face of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
this Sinai,
from the face of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[9] A rain of free gifts you shed, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
your inheritance, and it was weary — you yourself established it.

[10] Your living things dwelt in it;
you prepared in your goodness for the afflicted, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[11] ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ gives speech;
the women who bear the news are a great host.

[12] Kings of hosts flee, they flee,
and she who stays at home divides the spoil.

[13] If you lie down between the sheepfolds —
the wings of a dove covered with silver,
and her pinions with greenish gold.

[14] When ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ spreads out kings in it,
it snows on ⟦zalmon|Tzalmon|Zalmon⟧.

[15] Mountain of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, mountain of Bashan;
mountain of peaks, mountain of Bashan.

[16] Why do you look with envy, mountains of peaks,
at the mountain ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has desired for his dwelling?
Yes, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will abide forever.

[17] The chariots of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ are twice ten thousand, thousands upon thousands;
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is among them, Sinai in the holy place.

[18] You have gone up on high, you have taken captives captive;
you have taken gifts among man,
and even the stubborn, that ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may abide.

[19] Blessed is ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧; day by day he carries the load for us,
the ⟦el|El|God⟧, our salvation. Selah.

[20] The ⟦el|El|God⟧ is to us ⟦el|an El|a God⟧ for ⟦moshaot|moshaot — saving acts|salvation⟧,
and to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|GOD⟧ ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧
belong the ways out from death.

[21] But ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will strike through the head of his enemies,
the hairy crown
of him who walks about in his guilt.

[22] ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ said,
"From Bashan I will bring back,
I will bring back from the depths of the sea,

[23] so that your foot may strike through in blood,
the tongue of your dogs,
from the enemies, its portion."

[24] They have seen your processions, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the processions of my ⟦el|El|God⟧, my King, in the holy place.

[25] Singers went in front, players of strings behind,
in the midst of young women beating timbrels.

[26] In the assemblies bless ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, from the fountain of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[27] There is ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, the youngest, having dominion over them,
the princes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, their throng,
the princes of ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧, the princes of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧.

[28] Your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has commanded your strength;
be strong, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
in that which you have worked for us.

[29] From your temple over ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
to you kings shall bring a present.

[30] Rebuke the living thing of the reed,
the congregation of mighty ones with the calves of the peoples,
humbling himself with pieces of silver;
he has scattered peoples who delight in battles.

[31] Nobles shall come out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ shall make his hands run to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[32] Kingdoms of the earth, sing to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
make music to ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, Selah,

[33] to him who rides in the heavens of the heavens of old;
behold, he gives with his voice a voice of strength.

[34] Give strength to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is his exaltation,
and his strength is in the skies.

[35] Fearsome is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from your sanctuaries;
the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — he gives strength and might to the people.
Blessed is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1–10, 15–18, 21, 24, 26, 28, 31, 32, 34, 35) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*. An Elohistic psalm: אֱלֹהִים as printed; יהוה stands in v16, 26 (and יְהוִה v20), יָהּ in v4, 18: they are kept as they stand. **"God" → "El"** (v19, 20, 24, 35) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim* (v19, 20 הָאֵל, with the article: "the El"). **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v11, 17, 19, 20, 22, 32) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v14) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי; the meaning of the name is not certain.
- **No "O"** (v7, 9, 10, 16, 24, 28, 32) — the Hebrew vocatives have none.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm or Song of David" → "To the overseer. Of David, a psalm, a song."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר שִׁיר, in this order: לַמְנַצֵּחַ, participle of נִצַּח "oversee" (1 Chr 15:21), traditional "To the chief musician"; לְדָוִד may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to David"; there is no "or".
- **"let them also that hate him flee before him" → "and let those who hate him flee from his face"** (v1) — Hebrew וְיָנוּסוּ מְשַׂנְאָיו מִפָּנָיו, "and"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (also v2, 8). The verse takes up Num 10:35, where Moses speaks to Yahweh ("Arise, Yahweh"); here יָקוּם, the third person, "Let Elohim arise".
- **"As smoke is driven away, so drive them away" → "As smoke is driven away, you drive them away"** (v2) — Hebrew כְּהִנְדֹּף עָשָׁן תִּנְדֹּף, an imperfect, "you drive away" (no "so"); "them" is supplied: the Hebrew has no object. **"as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God" → "as wax melts from the face of fire, let the wicked perish from the face of Elohim"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי־אֵשׁ … מִפְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים, the one phrase twice; no "so"; רְשָׁעִים "the wicked". The lines follow the Hebrew accents (three lines).
- **"But let the righteous be glad; let them rejoice before God: yea, let them exceedingly rejoice" → "But let the righteous rejoice, let them be jubilant before Elohim, and let them exult with gladness"** (v3) — Hebrew וְצַדִּיקִים יִשְׂמְחוּ יַעַלְצוּ … וְיָשִׂישׂוּ בְשִׂמְחָה: three verbs, שָׂמַח "rejoice", עָלַץ "be jubilant", שׂוּשׂ "exult", and the noun שִׂמְחָה "gladness". The disjunctive וְ before the noun sets the righteous against the wicked of v2: "But".
- **"Sing unto God, sing praises to his name" → "Sing to Elohim, make music to his name"** (v4) — Hebrew זַמְּרוּ שְׁמוֹ, זִמֵּר "make music", his name as direct object ("to" supplied). **"extol him that rideth upon the heavens" → "build up the road for him who rides through the steppes"** — Hebrew סֹלּוּ לָרֹכֵב בָּעֲרָבוֹת: סָלַל "heap up, build up (a road)", the verb of מְסִלָּה "highway" ("the road" is supplied); "extol", "lift up a song" are other readings of the verb. עֲרָבוֹת "steppes" (dry plains, עֲרָבָה); the reading "him who rides on the clouds" (another word for clouds, cf. v33 "rides in the heavens"; from memory) changes the word: the verse keeps the MT. **"by his name JAH" → "in Yah is his name"** — Hebrew בְּיָהּ שְׁמוֹ, literally "in Yah his name": the בְּ most take as "as" (his name is Yah), as Exod 6:3 בְּאֵל שַׁדַּי "as El Shaddai"; the Hebrew form is kept. יָהּ is the short form of the name Yahweh. **"and rejoice before him" → "and be jubilant before him"** — Hebrew וְעִלְזוּ, from עָלַז, a near twin of עָלַץ in v3; English has one word for both.
- **"A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows" → "Father of the fatherless and judge of widows"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲבִי יְתוֹמִים וְדַיַּן אַלְמָנוֹת, no articles; יָתוֹם "fatherless"; דַּיָּן "judge", from דִּין, one who judges a cause (the word is not שֹׁפֵט). **"in his holy habitation" → "in his holy abode"** — Hebrew בִּמְעוֹן קָדְשׁוֹ, מָעוֹן "abode" (as Deut 26:15), literally "the abode of his holiness".
- **"God setteth the solitary in families" → "Elohim makes the solitary dwell in a house"** (v6) — Hebrew מוֹשִׁיב יְחִידִים בַּיְתָה: הוֹשִׁיב "make dwell"; יָחִיד "only one, alone" (Gen 22:2), here plural: those who are alone; בַּיְתָה "into a house, homeward". **"he bringeth out those which are bound with chains" → "he brings out prisoners into prosperity"** — Hebrew מוֹצִיא אֲסִירִים בַּכּוֹשָׁרוֹת: כּוֹשָׁרוֹת is found only here, taken from כָּשֵׁר "succeed": "into prosperity"; uncertain ("with singing" is also read). **"but the rebellious dwell in a dry land" → "but the stubborn abide in a parched land"** — Hebrew אַךְ סוֹרֲרִים שָׁכְנוּ צְחִיחָה: אַךְ "only, surely" (here setting the stubborn apart: "but"); סוֹרֵר "stubborn" (as Deut 21:18); שָׁכַן "abide"; צְחִיחָה "parched (land)", "land" supplied; שָׁכְנוּ is a perfect ("have abode").
- **"when thou didst march through the wilderness" → "when you marched through the desert"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּצַעְדְּךָ בִישִׁימוֹן: צָעַד "march" (Judg 5:4); יְשִׁימוֹן "desert" (as Deut 32:10; not מִדְבָּר "wilderness"). Selah stands in the middle of the sentence, which runs on into v8.
- **"The earth shook, the heavens also dropped at the presence of God: even Sinai itself was moved at the presence of God" → "the earth quaked, the heavens also dropped from the face of Elohim, this Sinai, from the face of Elohim"** (v8) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ רָעָשָׁה אַף־שָׁמַיִם נָטְפוּ מִפְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים זֶה סִינַי, as Judg 5:4–5 (רָעַשׁ "quake", נָטַף "drop", זֶה סִינַי "this Sinai"); "was moved" is not in the Hebrew here (Judg 5:5 has "mountains flowed"). זֶה סִינַי may also be read "the One of Sinai" (= Elohim). The lines follow the accents: "this Sinai" is a line of its own, as in Judg 5:5.
- **"Thou, O God, didst send a plentiful rain" → "A rain of free gifts you shed, Elohim"** (v9) — Hebrew גֶּשֶׁם נְדָבוֹת תָּנִיף אֱלֹהִים: נְדָבָה "free gift, freewill offering" ("plentiful" is the sense most give); הֵנִיף, elsewhere "wave" (the wave offering), here of rain: "shed, sprinkle" (the one verb: note). An imperfect, taken as past with the march of v7–8. **"whereby thou didst confirm thine inheritance, when it was weary" → "your inheritance, and it was weary — you yourself established it"** — Hebrew נַחֲלָתְךָ וְנִלְאָה אַתָּה כּוֹנַנְתָּהּ: וְנִלְאָה "and it was weary" (נִלְאָה "be weary"); אַתָּה "you" stressed; כּוֹנֵן "establish".
- **"Thy congregation hath dwelt therein" → "Your living things dwelt in it"** (v10) — Hebrew חַיָּתְךָ יָשְׁבוּ־בָהּ: חַיָּה "living thing" (as v30 and Gen 1), here collective with a plural verb; "your company" (as 2 Sam 23:11, 13 "a troop") and "your flock" are other readings. **"thou, O God, hast prepared of thy goodness for the poor" → "you prepared in your goodness for the afflicted, Elohim"** — Hebrew תָּכִין בְּטוֹבָתְךָ לֶעָנִי: תָּכִין, an imperfect (the root of "established", v9); no object; עָנִי "afflicted" (singular, collective).
- **"The Lord gave the word" → "Adonai gives speech"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יִתֶּן־אֹמֶר, an imperfect; אֹמֶר "speech" (a form of אֵמֶר "saying", as Ps 19:2, 3), without the article. **"great was the company of those that published it" → "the women who bear the news are a great host"** — Hebrew הַמְבַשְּׂרוֹת צָבָא רָב: מְבַשְּׂרוֹת, a feminine participle (of בִּשֵּׂר "bring news"): "women" is in the form; צָבָא "host".
- **"Kings of armies did flee apace" → "Kings of hosts flee, they flee"** (v12) — Hebrew מַלְכֵי צְבָאוֹת יִדֹּדוּן יִדֹּדוּן, the verb twice; צְבָאוֹת "hosts", the word of v11. The earlier text put the words in quotation marks as the women's news; the Hebrew does not mark it. **"and she that tarried at home divided the spoil" → "and she who stays at home divides the spoil"** — Hebrew וּנְוַת בַּיִת: uncertain; נְוַת is taken from נָוָה "dwell" ("she who dwells / stays at home"); "the beauty of the house" (from נָאוָה "lovely") and "the pasture of the house" are also read. תְּחַלֵּק, an imperfect, "divides".
- **"Though ye have lien among the pots, yet shall ye be as the wings of a dove" → "If you lie down between the sheepfolds — the wings of a dove"** (v13) — Hebrew אִם־תִּשְׁכְּבוּן בֵּין שְׁפַתָּיִם כַּנְפֵי יוֹנָה: אִם "if, when, though"; "you" is plural; "men" is not in the Hebrew. שְׁפַתַּיִם is taken as "sheepfolds" (as Judg 5:16 מִשְׁפְּתַיִם); "hearthstones" ("pots", KJV) and "saddlebags" are also read: uncertain. "yet shall ye be as" is not in the Hebrew: the wings stand without a verb, and how they join the first line is not said. **"covered with silver, and her feathers with yellow gold" → "covered with silver, and her pinions with greenish gold"** — Hebrew נֶחְפָּה בַכֶּסֶף וְאֶבְרוֹתֶיהָ בִּירַקְרַק חָרוּץ: אֶבְרָה "pinion"; יְרַקְרַק "greenish" (as Lev 13:49; 14:37: a yellow-green sheen); חָרוּץ, a poetic word for "gold".
- **"When the Almighty scattered kings in it, it was white as snow in Salmon" → "When Shaddai spreads out kings in it, it snows on Tzalmon"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּפָרֵשׂ שַׁדַּי מְלָכִים בָּהּ תַּשְׁלֵג בְּצַלְמוֹן: פֵּרֵשׂ "spread out" (= scatter; פּוּץ of v1 is another verb); בָּהּ "in it" (the land); תַּשְׁלֵג "it snows" (also "you make it snow"); "white as" is not in the Hebrew. Tzalmon, a mountain near Shechem (Judg 9:48).
- **"The hill of God is as the hill of Bashan; an high hill as the hill of Bashan" → "Mountain of Elohim, mountain of Bashan; mountain of peaks, mountain of Bashan"** (v15) — Hebrew הַר־אֱלֹהִים הַר־בָּשָׁן הַר גַּבְנֻנִּים הַר־בָּשָׁן, four names with no verb and no "as": either "Bashan is a mountain of Elohim" (or "a mighty mountain": אֱלֹהִים as a superlative) or a call to the mountain; the verse leaves the words as they stand. גַּבְנֻנִּים "peaks, humps" (from גִּבֵּן "hunchbacked", Lev 21:20).
- **"Why leap ye, ye high hills? this is the hill which God desireth to dwell in" → "Why do you look with envy, mountains of peaks, at the mountain Elohim has desired for his dwelling?"** (v16) — Hebrew לָמָּה תְּרַצְּדוּן הָרִים גַּבְנֻנִּים הָהָר חָמַד אֱלֹהִים לְשִׁבְתּוֹ: רָצַד is found only here, "look with envy, watch jealously": uncertain ("leap", KJV); "at" is supplied; "this is" is not in the Hebrew; חָמַד "desire" (elsewhere "covet"; of a good thing "desire"); לְשִׁבְתּוֹ "for his dwelling", יָשַׁב. **"yea, the LORD will dwell in it for ever" → "Yes, Yahweh will abide forever"** — Hebrew אַף־יְהוָה יִשְׁכֹּן לָנֶצַח: שָׁכַן "abide"; "in it" is not in the Hebrew; לָנֶצַח "forever".
- **"The chariots of God are twenty thousand, even thousands of angels" → "The chariots of Elohim are twice ten thousand, thousands upon thousands"** (v17) — Hebrew רֶכֶב אֱלֹהִים רִבֹּתַיִם אַלְפֵי שִׁנְאָן: רֶכֶב "chariots" (collective); רִבֹּתַיִם "two ten-thousands"; שִׁנְאָן is found only here, taken as "repetition" (from שָׁנָה "repeat": thousands repeated); "angels" is not in the word: uncertain. **"the Lord is among them, as in Sinai, in the holy place" → "Adonai is among them, Sinai in the holy place"** — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי בָם סִינַי בַּקֹּדֶשׁ, with no "as" (the earlier "is now in the sanctuary" added "now"); "in the holy place" or "in holiness" (קֹדֶשׁ, as v24). Many read בָּא מִסִּינַי "he has come from Sinai" (a conjecture, from memory): the verse keeps the MT.
- **"Thou hast ascended on high, thou hast led captivity captive" → "You have gone up on high, you have taken captives captive"** (v18) — Hebrew עָלִיתָ לַמָּרוֹם שָׁבִיתָ שֶּׁבִי: עָלָה "go up"; שָׁבָה "take captive" with its noun שְׁבִי "captives" (collective). **"thou hast received gifts for men" → "you have taken gifts among man"** — Hebrew לָקַחְתָּ מַתָּנוֹת בָּאָדָם: לָקַח "take" (not "give"); בָּאָדָם "among man / in man" (אָדָם "man, mankind"; not "for men"). Eph 4:8 quotes the verse with "he gave gifts to men" (Greek ἔδωκεν δόματα τοῖς ἀνθρώποις); the Hebrew here has "you have taken", and the verse is not levelled to the quotation. The Greek of the Psalm has "you took gifts among man" (from memory). **"yea, for the rebellious also, that the LORD God might dwell among them" → "and even the stubborn, that Yah Elohim may abide"** — Hebrew וְאַף סוֹרְרִים לִשְׁכֹּן יָהּ אֱלֹהִים: "and even the stubborn" stands without a verb or a preposition ("among" and "for" are readings); סוֹרֵר "stubborn" as v6; לִשְׁכֹּן "to abide", שָׁכַן; "among them" is not in the Hebrew. יָהּ אֱלֹהִים: "Yah" and "Elohim" side by side.
- **"Blessed be the Lord, who daily loadeth us with benefits, even the God of our salvation" → "Blessed is Adonai; day by day he carries the load for us, the El, our salvation"** (v19) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ אֲדֹנָי יוֹם יוֹם יַעֲמָס־לָנוּ הָאֵל יְשׁוּעָתֵנוּ: בָּרוּךְ with no verb ("is"); יוֹם יוֹם "day by day"; עָמַס "carry a load" ("the load" is supplied; "he lays a load on us" is also read; "with benefits" is not in the Hebrew); הָאֵל יְשׁוּעָתֵנוּ "the El, our salvation", in apposition; יְשׁוּעָה "salvation".
- **"He that is our God is the God of salvation; and unto GOD the Lord belong the issues from death" → "The El is to us an El for saving acts, and to Yahweh Adonai belong the ways out from death"** (v20) — Hebrew הָאֵל לָנוּ אֵל לְמוֹשָׁעוֹת: "the El (is) to us an El for saving acts" (מוֹשָׁעוֹת plural, found only here). וְלֵיהוִה אֲדֹנָי, the name יהוה pointed to be read *Elohim* beside אֲדֹנָי, in this order (not אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה). לַמָּוֶת תּוֹצָאוֹת, literally "for death the goings out": "the ways out from death" is the sense most take; "the ways out to death" (death too is in his hand) is also read. The lines follow the accents (three lines).
- **"But God shall wound the head of his enemies, and the hairy scalp of such an one as goeth on still in his trespasses" → "But Elohim will strike through the head of his enemies, the hairy crown of him who walks about in his guilt"** (v21) — Hebrew אַךְ־אֱלֹהִים יִמְחַץ רֹאשׁ אֹיְבָיו קָדְקֹד שֵׂעָר מִתְהַלֵּךְ בַּאֲשָׁמָיו: אַךְ as v6 ("but", also "surely"); מָחַץ "strike through"; רֹאשׁ singular; קָדְקֹד שֵׂעָר "the crown of hair"; הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about"; אֲשָׁמָיו "his guilt" (plural in the Hebrew: "his guilts"; not "trespasses"). Three lines, as the accents.
- **"I will bring again from Bashan, I will bring my people again from the depths of the sea" → "From Bashan I will bring back, I will bring back from the depths of the sea"** (v22) — Hebrew מִבָּשָׁן אָשִׁיב אָשִׁיב מִמְּצֻלוֹת יָם, "from Bashan" first; הֵשִׁיב "bring back" with no object ("my people", KJV, and "them", the earlier text, are supplied: whom he brings back, his people or his enemies, is not said); מְצוֹלָה "depth".
- **"That thy foot may be dipped in the blood of thine enemies, and the tongue of thy dogs in the same" → "so that your foot may strike through in blood, the tongue of your dogs, from the enemies, its portion"** (v23) — Hebrew לְמַעַן תִּמְחַץ רַגְלְךָ בְּדָם לְשׁוֹן כְּלָבֶיךָ מֵאֹיְבִים מִנֵּהוּ: לְמַעַן "so that"; תִּמְחַץ is the verb of v21, "strike through" (the foot as subject; "that you may strike through your foot" is also possible); many read תִּרְחַץ "bathe" (as Ps 58:10) — a change of one letter, not in the MT. מִנֵּהוּ is found only here, taken as "its portion" (from מָנָה "portion"); "from him" is also read: uncertain. Three lines, as the accents.
- **"They have seen thy goings, O God; even the goings of my God, my King, in the sanctuary" → "They have seen your processions, Elohim, the processions of my El, my King, in the holy place"** (v24) — Hebrew רָאוּ הֲלִיכוֹתֶיךָ, active "they have seen" (the earlier "is seen" was passive); הֲלִיכָה "going, procession" (literally "goings"); בַּקֹּדֶשׁ "in the holy place" or "in holiness", as v17 (not מִקְדָּשׁ "sanctuary").
- **"The singers went before, the players on instruments followed after; among them were the damsels playing with timbrels" → "Singers went in front, players of strings behind, in the midst of young women beating timbrels"** (v25) — Hebrew קִדְּמוּ שָׁרִים אַחַר נֹגְנִים בְּתוֹךְ עֲלָמוֹת תּוֹפֵפוֹת: קִדֵּם "go in front" (elsewhere "come to meet"); נֹגְנִים "players of strings" (נִגֵּן, as בִּנְגִינוֹת in the headings); עַלְמָה "young woman"; תּוֹפֵף "beat the timbrel" (תֹּף "timbrel"); "were" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Bless ye God in the congregations, even the Lord, from the fountain of Israel" → "In the assemblies bless Elohim, Yahweh, from the fountain of Israel"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּמַקְהֵלוֹת בָּרְכוּ אֱלֹהִים יְהוָה מִמְּקוֹר יִשְׂרָאֵל, "in the assemblies" first; the name is יהוה; מָקוֹר "fountain". "You who are" (the earlier text) is supplied: the Hebrew has "from the fountain of Israel" alone (= those who spring from Israel). The earlier text set the verse in quotation marks as the song of v25: the Hebrew does not mark it.
- **"There is little Benjamin with their ruler" → "There is Benjamin, the youngest, having dominion over them"** (v27) — Hebrew שָׁם בִּנְיָמִן צָעִיר רֹדֵם: צָעִיר "young, youngest" (Benjamin was the youngest son); רֹדֵם, a participle with "them": "having dominion over them" (רָדָה); "in the lead" is a reading. **"the princes of Judah and their council" → "the princes of Judah, their throng"** — Hebrew רִגְמָתָם is found only here: "their throng" (or "heap", from רָגַם "stone"): uncertain; no "and". שַׂר "prince".
- **"Thy God hath commanded thy strength: strengthen, O God, that which thou hast wrought for us" → "Your Elohim has commanded your strength; be strong, Elohim, in that which you have worked for us"** (v28) — Hebrew צִוָּה אֱלֹהֶיךָ עֻזֶּךָ, a perfect, spoken to Israel ("your" singular); the Greek and the Syriac read "Command, Elohim, your strength" (from memory); the verse keeps the MT. עוּזָּה "be strong" (imperative of עָזַז, the root of עֹז "strength"); זוּ "that which" ("in" is supplied); פָּעַל "work". Three lines, as the accents (the earlier "Summon your power" read the imperative).
- **"Because of thy temple at Jerusalem shall kings bring presents unto thee" → "From your temple over Jerusalem to you kings shall bring a present"** (v29) — Hebrew מֵהֵיכָלֶךָ עַל־יְרוּשָׁלִַם, "from your temple" (מִן; "because of" is a reading); עַל "over, above"; לְךָ "to you" first; שַׁי "present" (a gift of homage; singular; another word than מַתָּנוֹת "gifts", v18).
- **"Rebuke the company of spearmen, the multitude of the bulls, with the calves of the people" → "Rebuke the living thing of the reed, the congregation of mighty ones with the calves of the peoples"** (v30) — Hebrew גְּעַר חַיַּת קָנֶה עֲדַת אַבִּירִים בְּעֶגְלֵי עַמִּים: חַיַּת קָנֶה "the living thing of the reed" (an animal of the reeds; often taken as Egypt's crocodile or hippopotamus: not in the words); עֵדָה "congregation"; אַבִּירִים "mighty ones" (bulls are meant, as Ps 22:12; 50:13); עֵגֶל "calf". **"till every one submit himself with pieces of silver" → "humbling himself with pieces of silver"** — Hebrew מִתְרַפֵּס בְּרַצֵּי־כָסֶף, a participle: הִתְרַפֵּס "humble oneself" (Prov 6:3; also "trample"); רַצֵּי is found only here, "pieces" (or "bars"): uncertain; who humbles himself is not named; "till every one" is not in the Hebrew. **"scatter thou the people that delight in war" → "he has scattered peoples who delight in battles"** — Hebrew בִּזַּר עַמִּים קְרָבוֹת יֶחְפָּצוּ: בִּזַּר a perfect, "he has scattered" (= פִּזַּר); the imperative "scatter" (the versions, from memory) is another pointing; קְרָבוֹת "battles" (not מִלְחָמָה "war"); חָפֵץ "delight in". The lines follow the earlier layout: the Hebrew halves divide after "silver". גָּעַר "rebuke".
- **"Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall soon stretch out her hands unto God" → "Nobles shall come out of Egypt; Cush shall make his hands run to Elohim"** (v31) — Hebrew יֶאֱתָיוּ חַשְׁמַנִּים מִנִּי מִצְרָיִם: חַשְׁמַנִּים is found only here: "nobles" or "envoys" (also read "bronze", as gifts): uncertain; אָתָה "come" (a poetic word). כּוּשׁ תָּרִיץ יָדָיו "Cush makes his hands run" (= hastens to stretch them out); יָדָיו "his hands"; Cush is the land south of Egypt.
- **"Sing unto God, ye kingdoms of the earth; O sing praises unto the Lord" → "Kingdoms of the earth, sing to Elohim; make music to Adonai"** (v32) — Hebrew מַמְלְכוֹת הָאָרֶץ שִׁירוּ לֵאלֹהִים זַמְּרוּ אֲדֹנָי, in this order; זִמֵּר as v4. The sentence runs on into v33.
- **"To him that rideth upon the heavens of heavens, which were of old" → "to him who rides in the heavens of the heavens of old"** (v33) — Hebrew לָרֹכֵב בִּשְׁמֵי שְׁמֵי־קֶדֶם, "in"; קֶדֶם "of old". **"lo, he doth send out his voice, and that a mighty voice" → "behold, he gives with his voice a voice of strength"** — Hebrew הֵן יִתֵּן בְּקוֹלוֹ קוֹל עֹז: הֵן "behold"; נָתַן בְּקוֹל "give with the voice" (= utter, thunder); קוֹל עֹז "a voice of strength".
- **"Ascribe ye strength unto God: his excellency is over Israel, and his strength is in the clouds" → "Give strength to Elohim; over Israel is his exaltation, and his strength is in the skies"** (v34) — Hebrew תְּנוּ עֹז, נָתַן "give" (= ascribe); עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל גַּאֲוָתוֹ, "over Israel" first; גַּאֲוָה "exaltation" (as Deut 33:26); שְׁחָקִים "skies" (as Deut 33:26; not עָנָן "cloud").
- **"O God, thou art terrible out of thy holy places" → "Fearsome is Elohim from your sanctuaries"** (v35) — Hebrew נוֹרָא אֱלֹהִים מִמִּקְדָּשֶׁיךָ: נוֹרָא "fearsome"; "your sanctuaries" (plural, מִקְדָּשׁ) speaks to Elohim while the line names him: Elohim may be a call ("Fearsome, Elohim, …", KJV "thou art"); the verse keeps the words as they stand. **"the God of Israel is he that giveth strength and power unto his people" → "the El of Israel — he gives strength and might to the people"** — Hebrew אֵל יִשְׂרָאֵל הוּא נֹתֵן עֹז וְתַעֲצֻמוֹת לָעָם: עֹז "strength"; תַּעֲצֻמוֹת "might" (only here, from עָצַם "be mighty"); לָעָם "to the people" (not "his"). **"Blessed be God" → "Blessed is Elohim"** — no verb ("is").

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v35 = Heb v36.
- **A hard text** — many words of this psalm are found nowhere else (v6, 13, 16, 17, 23, 27, 30, 31): each is translated as the Masoretic Text stands, with its doubt in Changes.
- **Parallels** — v1 = Num 10:35 (in the third person); v7–8 = Judg 5:4–5; v18 is quoted in Eph 4:8 in another form.
