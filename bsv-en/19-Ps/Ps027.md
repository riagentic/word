# Psalm 27

Of David.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is my light and my salvation;
whom shall I fear?
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is the strong place of my life;
of whom shall I be in dread?

[2] When evildoers came near against me
to eat my flesh,
my adversaries and my enemies, mine,
they themselves stumbled and fell.

[3] If a camp should camp against me,
my heart shall not fear;
if war should rise against me,
in this I am trusting.

[4] One thing I have asked from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
that I will seek:
my dwelling in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
all the days of my life,
to behold the pleasantness of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and to inquire in his temple.

[5] For he will conceal me in his booth
in the day of evil;
he will hide me in the hiding place of his tent;
upon a rock he will raise me high.

[6] And now my head shall be high
above my enemies around me,
and I will sacrifice in his tent
sacrifices of shouting;
I will sing and make music to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] Hear, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, my voice — I call;
and show me favor and answer me.

[8] To you my heart has said,
"Seek my face."
Your face, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I will seek.

[9] Do not hide your face from me;
do not turn your servant aside in anger;
you have been my help;
do not abandon me and do not forsake me,
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation.

[10] For my father and my mother have forsaken me,
but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will gather me in.

[11] Teach me your way, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and lead me in a level path
on account of those who watch me.

[12] Do not give me into the soul of my adversaries;
for witnesses of falsehood have risen against me,
and one who breathes out violence.

[13] If I had not believed that I would look on the goodness of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
in the land of the living —

[14] Wait for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
be strong, and let your heart be firm;
and wait for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13, 14) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "Of David"** (heading) — Hebrew לְדָוִד, the first word of v1: "by", "for" or "belonging to David".
- **"the strength of my life" → "the strong place of my life"** (v1) — Hebrew מָעוֹז "strong place, refuge". **"of whom shall I be afraid?" → "of whom shall I be in dread?"** — Hebrew אֶפְחָד, from פָּחַד "dread" (יָרֵא "fear" is the first line).
- **"When the wicked, even mine enemies and my foes, came upon me to eat up my flesh, they stumbled and fell" → "When evildoers came near against me to eat my flesh, my adversaries and my enemies, mine, they themselves stumbled and fell"** (v2) — Hebrew בִּקְרֹב עָלַי מְרֵעִים, "in the coming near of evildoers against me"; צָרַי וְאֹיְבַי לִי, with לִי "to me, mine" (as 2 Sam 22:2); הֵמָּה כָשְׁלוּ, with the stressed "they".
- **"Though an host should encamp against me" → "If a camp should camp against me"** (v3) — Hebrew אִם־תַּחֲנֶה עָלַי מַחֲנֶה, the verb and its noun of one root (חָנָה "camp", מַחֲנֶה "camp", also of the host in it). **"in this will I be confident" → "in this I am trusting"** — Hebrew בְּזֹאת אֲנִי בוֹטֵחַ, a participle; "this" may point back to v1 or on to v4, or mean "even so".
- **"One thing have I desired of the LORD" → "One thing I have asked from Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew שָׁאַלְתִּי מֵאֵת. **"that I may dwell" → "my dwelling"** — Hebrew שִׁבְתִּי, an infinitive with "my". **"to behold the beauty of the LORD" → "to behold the pleasantness of Yahweh"** — Hebrew לַחֲזוֹת בְּנֹעַם; נֹעַם "pleasantness, delight". **"to enquire in his temple" kept** — Hebrew וּלְבַקֵּר, a verb that elsewhere is "search, examine" (Lev 13:36); here seeking an answer or looking with care: uncertain.
- **"in the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion: in the secret of his tabernacle shall he hide me; he shall set me up upon a rock" → "he will conceal me in his booth in the day of evil; he will hide me in the hiding place of his tent; upon a rock he will raise me high"** (v5) — Hebrew יִצְפְּנֵנִי (צָפַן "conceal, store away") and יַסְתִּרֵנִי בְּסֵתֶר (סָתַר "hide", its noun "hiding place"): English "hide" is kept for the one root. בְּסֻכֹּה "in his booth", written with the old suffix ה- (סֻכָּה "booth"); אֹהֶל "tent"; בְּיוֹם רָעָה "in the day of evil"; יְרוֹמְמֵנִי "he will raise me high".
- **"shall mine head be lifted up" → "my head shall be high"** (v6) — Hebrew יָרוּם, qal "be high". **"therefore will I offer in his tabernacle sacrifices of joy" → "and I will sacrifice in his tent sacrifices of shouting"** — Hebrew וְאֶזְבְּחָה … זִבְחֵי תְרוּעָה, the verb and its noun; תְּרוּעָה, here with no instrument named, "shouting" (a shout of joy or the blast of a horn). **"I will sing, yea, I will sing praises" → "I will sing and make music"** — Hebrew אָשִׁירָה וַאֲזַמְּרָה; זִמֵּר "make music".
- **"when I cry with my voice: have mercy also upon me" → "my voice — I call; and show me favor"** (v7) — Hebrew שְׁמַע־יְהוָה קוֹלִי אֶקְרָא וְחָנֵּנִי, "my voice" the object of "hear", then "I call" with no joining word.
- **"When thou saidst, Seek ye my face; my heart said unto thee" → "To you my heart has said, 'Seek my face'"** (v8) — Hebrew לְךָ אָמַר לִבִּי בַּקְּשׁוּ פָנָי: there is no "you said" in the Hebrew. The heart speaks "to you" (or "for you", on Yahweh's behalf) the command "Seek (plural) my face"; the line is hard, and the earlier text had supplied a speaker. **"Thy face, LORD, will I seek" kept.**
- **"Hide not thy face far from me" → "Do not hide your face from me"** (v9) — Hebrew מִמֶּנִּי "from me". **"put not thy servant away in anger" → "do not turn your servant aside in anger"** — Hebrew אַל־תַּט־בְּאַף, from נָטָה "turn aside"; אַף is "nose", here "anger". **"thou hast been my help" kept** — the earlier "O you who" was not in the Hebrew. **"leave me not" → "do not abandon me"** — Hebrew תִּטְּשֵׁנִי, from נָטַשׁ "abandon" (עָזַב "forsake" follows).
- **"When my father and my mother forsake me, then the LORD will take me up" → "For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but Yahweh will gather me in"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי … עֲזָבוּנִי, a perfect; כִּי may also be "though, when". וַיהוָה יַאַסְפֵנִי: אָסַף "gather", of taking a man into one's house (Josh 20:4).
- **"lead me in a plain path, because of mine enemies" → "lead me in a level path on account of those who watch me"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּאֹרַח מִישׁוֹר; לְמַעַן שׁוֹרְרָי, from שׁוּר "watch" (of foes who watch for a fall).
- **"Deliver me not over unto the will of mine enemies" → "Do not give me into the soul of my adversaries"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּנֶפֶשׁ צָרָי; נֶפֶשׁ "soul", here the craving of the soul. **"false witnesses" → "witnesses of falsehood"** — Hebrew עֵדֵי־שֶׁקֶר. **"and such as breathe out cruelty" → "and one who breathes out violence"** — Hebrew וִיפֵחַ חָמָס, a singular noun or adjective "breathing out"; a word of the same letters in Ugaritic means "witness" (from memory), which would give "and a witness of violence".
- **"I had fainted, unless I had believed to see the goodness of the LORD" → "If I had not believed that I would look on the goodness of Yahweh in the land of the living —"** (v13) — Hebrew לוּלֵא הֶאֱמַנְתִּי לִרְאוֹת בְּטוּב־יְהוָה: לוּלֵא "if not, unless", and the sentence breaks off; "I had fainted" was supplied by the KJV. The Masoretes set dots over and under לוּלֵא (one of the "extraordinary points"), marking the word as doubtful; without it the verse reads "I believe that I shall look on the goodness of Yahweh". The printed text with its word is kept, and the break is left open.
- **"be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart: wait, I say, on the LORD" → "be strong, and let your heart be firm; and wait for Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew חֲזַק וְיַאֲמֵץ לִבֶּךָ: the hifil of אָמֵץ "be firm", either "let him make your heart firm" or "let your heart be firm"; "he" (Yahweh) is not written. The last line begins with וְ "and"; "I say" is not in the Hebrew.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first word of v1.
- **Two parts** — trust (v1–6) and plea (v7–14), ending with a call to wait for Yahweh.
