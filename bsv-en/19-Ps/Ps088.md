# Psalm 88

A song, a psalm. Of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧. To the overseer, upon ⟦mahalath-tune|machalat|Mahalath⟧ ⟦leannoth|Leannot|Leannoth⟧. A Maskil. Of Heman the ⟦ezrahite|Ezrachi|Ezrahite⟧.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation,
by day I have cried out, in the night, before you.

[2] Let my prayer come before you;
incline your ear to my cry.

[3] For my soul is sated with evils,
and my life has reached to Sheol.

[4] I am reckoned with those who go down to the pit;
I have become like a man without strength,

[5] among the dead, free,
like the slain who lie in the grave,
whom you remember no more,
and they are cut off from your hand.

[6] You have set me in the pit of the lowest parts,
in dark places, in the depths.

[7] Upon me your fury has leaned,
and with all your breakers you have afflicted me. Selah.

[8] You have put my acquaintances far from me;
you have set me as abominations to them;
I am shut up, and I do not go out.

[9] My eye languishes because of affliction;
I have called on you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, every day;
I have spread out my palms to you.

[10] Will you do a wonder for the dead?
Or shall the shades rise, give thanks to you? Selah.

[11] Shall your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ be recounted in the grave,
your faithfulness in ⟦abaddon|Avaddon|Abaddon⟧?

[12] Shall your wonder be known in the darkness,
and your righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

[13] But I, to you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, have cried for help,
and in the morning my prayer comes to meet you.

[14] Why, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do you cast off my soul,
do you hide your face from me?

[15] Afflicted am I, and expiring from youth;
I have borne your dreads; I am helpless.

[16] Over me your burning angers have passed;
your terrors have put an end to me.

[17] They have surrounded me like the waters all the day;
they have closed around me together.

[18] You have put lover and friend far from me;
my acquaintances — a dark place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 9, 13, 14) — Tetragrammaton. No "O" before the name (v1, 9, 13, 14): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"A Song or Psalm for the sons of Korah, to the chief Musician upon Mahalath Leannoth, Maschil of Heman the Ezrahite" → "A song, a psalm. Of the sons of Korah. To the overseer, upon Mahalath Leannoth. A Maskil. Of Heman the Ezrahite."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר מִזְמוֹר לִבְנֵי קֹרַח לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל־מָחֲלַת לְעַנּוֹת מַשְׂכִּיל לְהֵימָן הָאֶזְרָחִי, in this order ("a song, a psalm": here שִׁיר stands first). לְ (twice) may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to"; "of" leaves it open. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditionally "to the chief musician"). מָחֲלַת: meaning unknown (a tune, an instrument, or "sickness", מַחֲלָה; as Ps 53); the `mahalath-tune` token, which the lexicon gives for Ps 53 and 88 (the file had the key of Esau's wife). לְעַנּוֹת: meaning unknown; "for singing" (as עַנּוֹת "singing", Exod 32:18) or "to afflict" (עִנָּה) are both defended. מַשְׂכִּיל: a hifil participle of שָׂכַל ("give insight"); as a heading term its sense is uncertain; the word is kept. הָאֶזְרָחִי "the Ezrahite" (Heman and Ethan are named so in 1 Kgs 4:31).
- **"I have cried day and night before thee" → "by day I have cried out, in the night, before you"** (v1) — Hebrew יוֹם־צָעַקְתִּי בַלַּיְלָה נֶגְדֶּךָ, a perfect ("I have cried out"); the words stand in this order, "day" before the verb and "in the night" after it.
- **"incline thine ear unto my cry"** — kept (v2): רִנָּה "cry" (in prayer, as Ps 17:1; 1 Kgs 8:28).
- **"my soul is full of troubles: and my life draweth nigh unto the grave" → "my soul is sated with evils, and my life has reached to Sheol"** (v3) — Hebrew שָׂבְעָה "is sated" (שָׂבַע); רָעוֹת "evils" (plural of רָעָה); הִגִּיעוּ לִשְׁאוֹל, a perfect, "have reached to" (not "draws near"); חַיַּי "my life" (a plural noun). שְׁאוֹל "Sheol".
- **"I am counted with them … I am as a man that hath no strength" → "I am reckoned with those … I have become like a man without strength"** (v4) — Hebrew נֶחְשַׁבְתִּי (nifal of חָשַׁב "reckon"); הָיִיתִי "I have become"; גֶּבֶר "man" (alone here); אֵין־אֱיָל "there is no strength" (אֱיָל only here).
- **"Free among the dead, like the slain that lie in the grave, whom thou rememberest no more: and they are cut off from thy hand" → "among the dead, free, like the slain who lie in the grave, whom you remember no more, and they are cut off from your hand"** (v5) — Hebrew בַּמֵּתִים חָפְשִׁי, "among the dead, free" (חָפְשִׁי "free", the word of the released slave, Exod 21:2): set loose from life, or left to himself (uncertain). The earlier "like those whom" repeated "like"; the Hebrew has "like" once (כְּמוֹ חֲלָלִים). חָלָל "slain" (one pierced); קֶבֶר "grave". וְהֵמָּה "and they" (not "for"); נִגְזָרוּ "are cut off" (גָּזַר "cut").
- **"Thou hast laid me in the lowest pit, in darkness, in the deeps" → "You have set me in the pit of the lowest parts, in dark places, in the depths"** (v6) — Hebrew שַׁתַּנִי (שִׁית "set", also v8); בְּבוֹר תַּחְתִּיּוֹת "in a pit of the lowest parts"; מַחֲשַׁכִּים "dark places" (plural; the singular in v18); מְצֹלוֹת "depths".
- **"Thy wrath lieth hard upon me, and thou hast afflicted me with all thy waves" → "Upon me your fury has leaned, and with all your breakers you have afflicted me"** (v7) — Hebrew עָלַי סָמְכָה חֲמָתֶךָ: "upon me" first; סָמַךְ "lean, press" (the verb of laying the hand on an offering); חֵמָה "fury". וְכָל־מִשְׁבָּרֶיךָ עִנִּיתָ, literally "and all your breakers you have afflicted": "with" and "me" are supplied (English cannot stand without them). מִשְׁבָּרִים "breakers" (as 2 Sam 22:5).
- **"Thou hast put away mine acquaintance far from me; thou hast made me an abomination unto them: I am shut up, and I cannot come forth" → "You have put my acquaintances far from me; you have set me as abominations to them; I am shut up, and I do not go out"** (v8) — Hebrew הִרְחַקְתָּ "you have put far" (also v18; the earlier "caused … to shun me" interprets); מְיֻדָּעַי "my acquaintances" (those known to me; also v18); תּוֹעֵבוֹת "abominations", plural; כָּלֻא "shut up", a passive participle of כָּלָא (elsewhere "keep back"); וְלֹא אֵצֵא "and I do not / shall not go out". Three lines by the accents; v9 begins a new sentence.
- **"Mine eye mourneth by reason of affliction: LORD, I have called daily upon thee, I have stretched out my hands unto thee" → "My eye languishes because of affliction; I have called on you, Yahweh, every day; I have spread out my palms to you"** (v9) — Hebrew דָּאֲבָה "languishes, pines" (rare); מִנִּי עֹנִי "from affliction" (עֳנִי "affliction"; "sorrow" was not the word); שִׁטַּחְתִּי "I have spread out"; כַּפַּי "my palms".
- **"Wilt thou shew wonders to the dead? shall the dead arise and praise thee?" → "Will you do a wonder for the dead? Or shall the shades rise, give thanks to you?"** (v10) — Hebrew תַּעֲשֶׂה־פֶּלֶא "do a wonder" (singular, also v12); אִם "or"; רְפָאִים "the shades" of the dead (the same word as the ancient people Rephaim; no token: the people are not meant, as Job 26:5); יָקוּמוּ יוֹדוּךָ "shall rise, shall give thanks to you", two verbs side by side (הוֹדָה "give thanks").
- **"Shall thy lovingkindness be declared in the grave? or thy faithfulness in destruction?" → "Shall your steadfast love be recounted in the grave, your faithfulness in Abaddon?"** (v11) — Hebrew הַיְסֻפַּר (pual of סִפֵּר "recount"); חֶסֶד carries the `steadfast-love` token (the earlier verse had plain words); there is no "or". אֲבַדּוֹן *Abaddon*, "destruction" (from אָבַד "perish"), a name for the place of the dead.
- **"Shall thy wonders be known in the dark? and thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?" → "Shall your wonder be known in the darkness, and your righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?"** (v12) — Hebrew פִּלְאֶךָ "your wonder", singular; וְ "and"; נְשִׁיָּה "forgetfulness" (only here).
- **"But unto thee have I cried, O LORD; and in the morning shall my prayer prevent thee" → "But I, to you, Yahweh, have cried for help, and in the morning my prayer comes to meet you"** (v13) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי, the pronoun set first (contrast: "But I"); שִׁוַּעְתִּי "I have cried for help" (שִׁוַּע); תְקַדְּמֶךָּ "comes to meet you" (קִדֵּם; KJV "prevent" in its old sense).
- **"LORD, why castest thou off my soul? why hidest thou thy face from me?" → "Why, Yahweh, do you cast off my soul, do you hide your face from me?"** (v14) — Hebrew לָמָה stands once, before both verbs; זָנַח "cast off" (also 89:38).
- **"I am afflicted and ready to die from my youth up: while I suffer thy terrors I am distracted" → "Afflicted am I, and expiring from youth; I have borne your dreads; I am helpless"** (v15) — Hebrew עָנִי אֲנִי, "afflicted" first (עָנִי, the poor man bowed down); גֹוֵעַ "expiring" (גָּוַע); מִנֹּעַר "from youth"; נָשָׂאתִי "I have borne"; אֵמֶיךָ "your dreads" (אֵימָה; בִּעוּתִים in v16 is "terrors"). אָפוּנָה is found only here; its meaning is uncertain ("I am helpless", "I am distraught", "I am benumbed"; some read it with פָּנָה "turn").
- **"Thy fierce wrath goeth over me; thy terrors have cut me off" → "Over me your burning angers have passed; your terrors have put an end to me"** (v16) — Hebrew עָלַי "over me" first; חֲרוֹנֶיךָ "your burning angers" (plural of חָרוֹן); עָבְרוּ "have passed over"; בִּעוּתִים "terrors" (from בִּעֵת "terrify"); צִמְּתוּתֻנִי, an unusual form of צָמַת "put an end to, silence, destroy".
- **"They came round about me daily like water; they compassed me about together" → "They have surrounded me like the waters all the day; they have closed around me together"** (v17) — Hebrew סַבּוּנִי (סָבַב "surround"); כָּל־הַיּוֹם "all the day"; הִקִּיפוּ עָלַי "they have closed around me" (הִקִּיף עַל).
- **"Lover and friend hast thou put far from me, and mine acquaintance into darkness" → "You have put lover and friend far from me; my acquaintances — a dark place"** (v18) — Hebrew הִרְחַקְתָּ as v8; אֹהֵב "one who loves" ("lover"), רֵעַ "friend". The last line has no verb: מְיֻדָּעַי מַחְשָׁךְ, "my acquaintances: a dark place" (מַחְשָׁךְ "dark place", the singular of v6). It is read "my acquaintances are darkness", "darkness is my acquaintance", or (KJV) "my acquaintance into darkness"; the Hebrew does not decide.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–18 = Heb v2–19.
- **Darkest psalm** — no turn to hope; it ends with "a dark place".
- **Heman the Ezrahite** — a sage (1 Kgs 4:31); a Heman is also one of David's singers (1 Chr 15:17; 25:5).
