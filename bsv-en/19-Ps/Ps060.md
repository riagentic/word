# Psalm 60

To the overseer, upon ⟦shushan-eduth|Shushan Edut|Shushan Eduth⟧. A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧ of David, to teach, when he struggled against ⟦aram-naharaim|Aram Naharayim|Aram-naharaim⟧ and against ⟦aram-zobah|Aram Tzovah|Aram-zobah⟧, and ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ returned and struck down Edom in the Valley of Salt, twelve thousand.

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you have cast us off, you have breached us;
you have been angry; you will restore us.

[2] You have made the land quake; you have split it open;
heal its breaks, for it has tottered.

[3] You have made your people see a hard thing;
you have made us drink wine of reeling.

[4] You have given to those who fear you a banner,
to lift itself up, because of the truth. Selah.

[5] So that your beloved ones may be freed,
save with your right hand, and answer me.

[6] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has spoken in his holiness:
"Let me be jubilant; let me divide Shechem,
and the valley of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ I will measure out.

[7] ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ is mine, and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ is mine;
and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is the strong place of my head;
⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ is my ruler's staff.

[8] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is the pot of my washing;
upon Edom I will throw my sandal;
over me, ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧, raise a shout."

[9] Who will bring me to a city of defense?
Who has led me as far as Edom?

[10] Is it not you, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who have cast us off,
and who do not go out, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, with our hosts?

[11] Give us help from the adversary,
and empty is the salvation of man.

[12] In ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we shall do valiantly,
and it is he who will trample our adversaries.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 6, 10, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"To the chief Musician upon Shushaneduth, Michtam of David, to teach; when he strove with Aramnaharaim and with Aramzobah, when Joab returned, and smote of Edom in the valley of salt twelve thousand" → "To the overseer, upon Shushan Edut. A Miktam of David, to teach, when he struggled against Aram Naharayim and against Aram Tzovah, and Joab returned and struck down Edom in the Valley of Salt, twelve thousand."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל־שׁוּשַׁן עֵדוּת מִכְתָּם לְדָוִד לְלַמֵּד, then בְּהַצּוֹתוֹ אֶת אֲרַם נַהֲרַיִם וְאֶת־אֲרַם צוֹבָה וַיָּשָׁב יוֹאָב וַיַּךְ אֶת־אֱדוֹם בְּגֵיא־מֶלַח שְׁנֵים עָשָׂר אָלֶף: the first two verses of the Hebrew. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21; traditional "to the chief musician"). עַל + the name of a tune, kept as a name: word for word "lily of testimony"; the meaning is unknown. *Miktam* is retained (meaning not known); the לְ of לְדָוִד may be "by", "for" or "belonging to"; לְלַמֵּד "to teach". בְּהַצּוֹתוֹ, hifil of נָצָה "struggle against". וַיָּשָׁב … וַיַּךְ "and Joab returned and struck down" (no "when", no "of"; הִכָּה, of killing "strike down"). 2 Sam 8:13 has 18,000 and "Aram", 1 Chr 18:12 18,000 by Abishai: the heading is kept as printed.
- **"O God, thou hast cast us off, thou hast scattered us, thou hast been displeased; O turn thyself to us again" → "Elohim, you have cast us off, you have breached us; you have been angry; you will restore us"** (v1) — Hebrew זְנַחְתָּנוּ (זָנַח "cast off"); פְרַצְתָּנוּ, פָּרַץ "break through, breach" (not "scatter"); אָנַפְתָּ "you have been angry"; תְּשׁוֹבֵב לָנוּ, polel imperfect of שׁוּב "bring back, restore", with לָנוּ "for us": a plea ("restore us!") or a statement; the form is kept.
- **"Thou hast made the earth to tremble; thou hast broken it: heal the breaches thereof; for it shaketh" → "You have made the land quake; you have split it open; heal its breaks, for it has tottered"** (v2) — Hebrew הִרְעַשְׁתָּה אֶרֶץ, hifil of רָעַשׁ "quake"; אֶרֶץ "land" (or "earth"; the lament is for the people's land); פְּצַמְתָּהּ "you have split it open" (only here); רְפָה "heal" (a spelling of רְפָא); שְׁבָרֶיהָ "its breaks"; מָטָה, perfect of מוֹט "totter".
- **"Thou hast shewed thy people hard things: thou hast made us to drink the wine of astonishment" → "You have made your people see a hard thing; you have made us drink wine of reeling"** (v3) — Hebrew הִרְאִיתָה … קָשָׁה, "made see a hard thing" (singular); יַיִן תַּרְעֵלָה "wine of reeling" (תַּרְעֵלָה, staggering, as Isa 51:17, 22).
- **"Thou hast given a banner to them that fear thee, that it may be displayed because of the truth" → "You have given to those who fear you a banner, to lift itself up, because of the truth"** (v4) — Hebrew נֵס "banner"; לְהִתְנוֹסֵס, hitpolel from נֵס, "to lift itself up as a banner" (or from נוּס "to flee"); מִפְּנֵי קֹשֶׁט "because of the truth" (קֹשֶׁט "truth", as Prov 22:21). Many read קֶשֶׁת "bow", "to flee from the face of the bow" (from memory); the verse follows the printed text.
- **"That thy beloved may be delivered; save with thy right hand, and hear me" → "So that your beloved ones may be freed, save with your right hand, and answer me"** (v5) — Hebrew לְמַעַן יֵחָלְצוּן יְדִידֶיךָ, nifal of חָלַץ "be drawn out, be freed" (the "armed" of Numbers is another use); יְמִינְךָ "your right hand" (an accusative of means; "with" is supplied); וַעֲנֵנִי, the qere, "and answer me" (עָנָה "answer"; not "hear"); the written form ועננו is "and answer us". Ps 108:6 writes וַעֲנֵנִי.
- **"God hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth" → "Elohim has spoken in his holiness: 'Let me be jubilant; let me divide Shechem, and the valley of Sukkot I will measure out'"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּקָדְשׁוֹ "in his holiness" (or "in his holy place"); אֶעְלֹזָה אֲחַלְּקָה, cohortatives of עָלַז "be jubilant" and חִלֵּק "divide"; אֲמַדֵּד, piel of מָדַד "measure out"; Hebrew order. The speech runs to the end of v8.
- **"Ephraim also is the strength of mine head; Judah is my lawgiver" → "and Ephraim is the strong place of my head; Judah is my ruler's staff"** (v7) — Hebrew מָעוֹז רֹאשִׁי "the strong place of my head" (a helmet is meant); מְחֹקְקִי "my ruler's staff" (מְחֹקֵק, as Gen 49:10; "lawgiver" or "commander" are also possible). Ps 108:8 lacks the וְ "and" before the second לִי.
- **"Moab is my washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe: Philistia, triumph thou because of me" → "Moab is the pot of my washing; upon Edom I will throw my sandal; over me, Philistia, raise a shout"** (v8) — Hebrew סִיר רַחְצִי "the pot of my washing"; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; נַעַל "sandal"; עָלַי פְּלֶשֶׁת הִתְרֹעָעִי, "over me, Philistia, shout!", an imperative (feminine), hitpolel of רוּעַ "shout": a shout of homage or of alarm; not certain. Ps 108:9 has עֲלֵי־פְלֶשֶׁת אֶתְרוֹעָע "over Philistia I will shout".
- **"Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?" → "Who will bring me to a city of defense? Who has led me as far as Edom?"** (v9) — Hebrew עִיר מָצוֹר "a city of defense" (מָצוֹר of a city, as 2 Chr 11:5; no article; Ps 108:10 has עִיר מִבְצָר "a fortified city"); מִי נָחַנִי, perfect of נָחָה "lead": "who has led me", perhaps "who will lead me" (KJV); עַד־אֱדוֹם "as far as Edom".
- **"Wilt not thou, O God, which hadst cast us off? and thou, O God, which didst not go out with our armies?" → "Is it not you, Elohim, who have cast us off, and who do not go out, Elohim, with our hosts?"** (v10) — Hebrew הֲלֹא־אַתָּה אֱלֹהִים זְנַחְתָּנוּ וְלֹא־תֵצֵא אֱלֹהִים בְּצִבְאוֹתֵינוּ: "who" is supplied twice; צָבָא "host". Ps 108:11 lacks אַתָּה "you".
- **"Give us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man" → "Give us help from the adversary, and empty is the salvation of man"** (v11) — Hebrew הָבָה־לָּנוּ, הָבָה here with its own sense "give"; מִצָּר "from the adversary" (צַר; also "from distress"); וְשָׁוְא "and empty" (וְ, not "for"); תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation".
- **"Through God we shall do valiantly: for he it is that shall tread down our enemies" → "In Elohim we shall do valiantly, and it is he who will trample our adversaries"** (v12) — Hebrew בֵּאלֹהִים "in Elohim" (as 56:4); נַעֲשֶׂה־חָיִל "we shall do valiantly" (lit. "make force", חַיִל); וְהוּא "and he" (the pronoun stands out); בּוּס "trample"; צָרֵינוּ "our adversaries".
- **Ps 108** — v5–12 = Ps 108:6–13; the differences are named at each verse; nothing is levelled.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1–2: our v1–12 = Heb v3–14.
- **2 Sam 8; 1 Chr 18** — David's wars with Aram and Edom.
- **Divine oracle** (v6–8) — *Elohim* claims the tribes and the lands round about.
