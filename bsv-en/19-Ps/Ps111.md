# Psalm 111

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
I will give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with a whole heart,
in the council of the upright, and the congregation.

[2] Great are the works of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
sought out for all their delights.

[3] Majesty and splendor is his work,
and his righteousness stands perpetually.

[4] A remembrance he has made for his wonderful deeds;
gracious and compassionate is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] Food he has given to those who fear him;
he will remember his covenant forever.

[6] The power of his works he has told to his people,
to give them the inheritance of nations.

[7] The works of his hands are truth and judgment;
sure are all his precepts,

[8] upheld perpetually, forever,
made in truth and uprightness.

[9] Ransoming he has sent to his people;
he has commanded his covenant forever;
holy and fearsome is his name.

[10] The beginning of wisdom is the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
good insight have all who do them;
his praise stands perpetually.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 10) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v1) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ יָהּ (printed as two words), literally "Praise Yah": a plural imperative with the short form of the name. It stands before the first line of the acrostic.
- **"I will praise the LORD with my whole heart, in the assembly of the upright, and in the congregation" → "I will give thanks to Yahweh with a whole heart, in the council of the upright, and the congregation"** (v1) — Hebrew אוֹדֶה, הוֹדָה "give thanks to" (not הִלֵּל "praise"); בְּכָל־לֵבָב "with all (a) heart", without "my"; בְּסוֹד יְשָׁרִים, סוֹד "council" (the close circle that takes counsel together); וְעֵדָה "and the congregation" (no second "in", no article).
- **"sought out of all them that have pleasure therein" → "sought out for all their delights"** (v2) — Hebrew דְּרוּשִׁים לְכָל־חֶפְצֵיהֶם: דְּרוּשִׁים, passive participle of דָּרַשׁ "seek"; חֶפְצֵיהֶם is pointed as the noun חֵפֶץ "delight", plural, "their delights" (the works', or the seekers'); "by all who delight in them" reads the same letters as the participle (חֲפֵצֵיהֶם); the sense is open.
- **"His work is honourable and glorious: and his righteousness endureth for ever" → "Majesty and splendor is his work, and his righteousness stands perpetually"** (v3) — Hebrew הוֹד־וְהָדָר פָּעֳלוֹ, nouns: הוֹד "majesty", הָדָר "splendor"; פֹּעַל "work". עֹמֶדֶת "stands" (עָמַד); לָעַד "perpetually" (עַד "perpetuity", as Ps 9:18; beside לְעוֹלָם "forever", v5, 8, 9). So also v10; 112:3, 9.
- **"He hath made his wonderful works to be remembered: the LORD is gracious and full of compassion" → "A remembrance he has made for his wonderful deeds; gracious and compassionate is Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew זֵכֶר עָשָׂה לְנִפְלְאֹתָיו, "a remembrance he has made" (זֵכֶר "remembrance"; עָשָׂה "make"); נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds". חַנּוּן "gracious" (of חָנַן "show favor"), רַחוּם "compassionate" (as Exod 34:6).
- **"He hath given meat unto them that fear him: he will ever be mindful of his covenant" → "Food he has given to those who fear him; he will remember his covenant forever"** (v5) — Hebrew טֶרֶף, literally "prey" (what is torn); of what is given to eat, "food" (as Prov 31:15; Mal 3:10): the literal word would say a hunted animal in English. יִזְכֹּר "he will remember" (זָכַר); לְעוֹלָם "forever".
- **"He hath shewed his people the power of his works, that he may give them the heritage of the heathen" → "The power of his works he has told to his people, to give them the inheritance of nations"** (v6) — Hebrew כֹּחַ מַעֲשָׂיו "the power of his works" first; הִגִּיד "tell" (not "show"); לָתֵת לָהֶם "to give them"; נַחֲלַת גּוֹיִם "the inheritance of nations" (נַחֲלָה; גּוֹיִם without the article).
- **"The works of his hands are verity and judgment; all his commandments are sure" → "The works of his hands are truth and judgment; sure are all his precepts"** (v7) — Hebrew אֱמֶת וּמִשְׁפָּט "truth and judgment"; נֶאֱמָנִים "sure" (נֶאֱמָן, "faithful" of persons); פִּקּוּדִים "precepts" (as Ps 19:8; מִצְוָה is "commandment", 112:1).
- **"They stand fast for ever and ever, and are done in truth and uprightness" → "upheld perpetually, forever, made in truth and uprightness"** (v8) — Hebrew סְמוּכִים לָעַד לְעוֹלָם, סָמוּךְ "upheld" (passive of סָמַךְ "lean, support"), and two words for time side by side: לָעַד "perpetually", לְעוֹלָם "forever". עֲשׂוּיִם "made, done"; בֶּאֱמֶת וְיָשָׁר "in truth and uprightness" (יָשָׁר "upright", here as a noun). The verse goes on from v7 (the precepts), without a verb of its own.
- **"He sent redemption unto his people: he hath commanded his covenant for ever: holy and reverend is his name" → "Ransoming he has sent to his people; he has commanded his covenant forever; holy and fearsome is his name"** (v9) — Hebrew פְּדוּת "ransoming" (of פָּדָה "ransom"; גְּאֻלָּה "redemption" is another root); צִוָּה "command"; נוֹרָא "fearsome" (not "reverend", "awesome").
- **"The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments" → "The beginning of wisdom is the fear of Yahweh; good insight have all who do them"** (v10) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית חָכְמָה יִרְאַת יְהוָה, in this order (רֵאשִׁית "beginning", or "first part"); שֵׂכֶל "insight" (בִּינָה is "understanding"); לְכָל־עֹשֵׂיהֶם "to all who do them": "them" (plural) looks back to the precepts of v7; "his commandments" (KJV) is not in the Hebrew. תְּהִלָּתוֹ "his praise".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours.
- **Alphabetic acrostic** — after "Halleluyah", each line (half-verse; three lines in v9 and v10) opens with the next letter of the alphabet, all twenty-two in order: א אוֹדֶה (v1a), ב בְּסוֹד (v1b), ג (v2a), ד (v2b), ה (v3a), ו (v3b), ז (v4a), ח (v4b), ט (v5a), י (v5b), כ (v6a), ל (v6b), מ (v7a), נ (v7b), ס (v8a), ע (v8b), פ (v9a), צ (v9b), ק (v9c), ר (v10a), ש (v10b), ת (v10c). The English does not imitate the scheme; the printed lines are the Hebrew lines. Ps 112 has the same scheme and many of the same words.
- **"Fear of Yahweh" = beginning of wisdom** — shared with Prov 1:7; 9:10.
