# Psalm 108

A song, a psalm. Of David.

[1] My heart is established, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
let me sing and let me make music, also my glory.

[2] Awake, harp and lyre!
Let me awaken the dawn.

[3] I will give thanks to you among the peoples, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and I will make music to you among the folks.

[4] For great above the heavens is your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and to the skies your truth.

[5] Be exalted above the heavens, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
and over all the earth be your glory.

[6] So that your beloved ones may be freed,
save with your right hand, and answer me.

[7] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has spoken in his holiness:
"Let me be jubilant; let me divide Shechem,
and the valley of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ I will measure out.

[8] ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ is mine, ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ is mine;
and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is the strong place of my head;
⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ is my ruler's staff.

[9] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is the pot of my washing;
upon Edom I will throw my sandal;
over ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧ I will raise a shout."

[10] Who will bring me to a fortified city?
Who has led me as far as Edom?

[11] Have you not, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, cast us off,
and you do not go out, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, with our hosts?

[12] Give us help from the adversary,
and empty is the salvation of man.

[13] In ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we shall do valiantly,
and it is he who will trample our adversaries.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 7, 11, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3) — Tetragrammaton, where Ps 57:9 has אֲדֹנָי.
- **"A Song or Psalm of David" → "A song, a psalm. Of David."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד, the first verse of the Hebrew, in this order (as Ps 83); the לְ of לְדָוִד may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" David.
- **Ps 57:7–11 and Ps 60:5–12** — v1–5 and v6–13 have the wording of those verses where the Hebrew is the same; every difference is named at its verse below, as Ps 108 has it; nothing is levelled.
- **"O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise, even with my glory" → "My heart is established, Elohim; let me sing and let me make music, also my glory"** (v1) — Hebrew נָכוֹן לִבִּי אֱלֹהִים, once (Ps 57:7 has it twice); אָשִׁירָה וַאֲזַמְּרָה, cohortatives; אַף־כְּבוֹדִי "also my glory": no "with"; "my glory" (the inner self, or "my liver", כְּבֵדִי, by other vowels, from memory) sings too; it stands where Ps 57:8 has עוּרָה כְבוֹדִי "Awake, my glory".
- **"Awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake early" → "Awake, harp and lyre! Let me awaken the dawn"** (v2) — Hebrew עוּרָה הַנֵּבֶל וְכִנּוֹר (נֵבֶל "harp", כִּנּוֹר "lyre"; the kinds are uncertain); אָעִירָה שָּׁחַר, cohortative hifil "let me awaken the dawn" (שַׁחַר is the object), as Ps 57:8.
- **"I will praise thee, O LORD, among the people: and I will sing praises unto thee among the nations" → "I will give thanks to you among the peoples, Yahweh, and I will make music to you among the folks"** (v3) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ בָעַמִּים יְהוָה וַאֲזַמֶּרְךָ בַּל־אֻמִּים: יְהוָה where Ps 57:9 has אֲדֹנָי, and וַ "and" before the second verb (Ps 57:9 has none); בַּל־אֻמִּים is written as two words and read as בַּלְאֻמִּים "among the folks" (לְאֹם), as Ps 57:9; uncertain. No "O".
- **"For thy mercy is great above the heavens: and thy truth reacheth unto the clouds" → "For great above the heavens is your chesed, and to the skies your truth"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי־גָדוֹל מֵעַל־שָׁמַיִם חַסְדֶּךָ, Hebrew order: מֵעַל "above" where Ps 57:10 has עַד "to"; no verb in the second line ("reacheth" is supplied); שְׁחָקִים "skies".
- **"Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens: and thy glory above all the earth" → "Be exalted above the heavens, Elohim; and over all the earth be your glory"** (v5) — Hebrew רוּמָה עַל־שָׁמַיִם … וְעַל כָּל־הָאָרֶץ כְּבוֹדֶךָ: וְ "and" where Ps 57:11 has none; the second line has no verb ("be" is supplied).
- **"That thy beloved may be delivered: save with thy right hand, and answer me" → "So that your beloved ones may be freed, save with your right hand, and answer me"** (v6) — Hebrew לְמַעַן יֵחָלְצוּן יְדִידֶיךָ, nifal of חָלַץ "be freed"; יְמִינְךָ "your right hand" ("with" is supplied); וַעֲנֵנִי "and answer me" is written so (in Ps 60:5 it is the qere of ועננו "and answer us").
- **"God hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth" → "Elohim has spoken in his holiness: 'Let me be jubilant; let me divide Shechem, and the valley of Sukkot I will measure out'"** (v7) — the same Hebrew as Ps 60:6: בְּקָדְשׁוֹ "in his holiness" (or "in his holy place"); אֶעְלֹזָה אֲחַלְּקָה, cohortatives; אֲמַדֵּד "I will measure out"; Hebrew order. The speech runs to the end of v9.
- **"Gilead is mine; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the strength of mine head; Judah is my lawgiver" → "Gilead is mine, Manasseh is mine; and Ephraim is the strong place of my head; Judah is my ruler's staff"** (v8) — Hebrew לִי גִלְעָד לִי מְנַשֶּׁה, without the וְ "and" that Ps 60:7 has before the second לִי; מָעוֹז רֹאשִׁי "the strong place of my head" (a helmet is meant); מְחֹקְקִי "my ruler's staff" (מְחֹקֵק, as Gen 49:10; "lawgiver" or "commander" are also possible).
- **"Moab is my washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe; over Philistia will I triumph" → "Moab is the pot of my washing; upon Edom I will throw my sandal; over Philistia I will raise a shout"** (v9) — Hebrew סִיר רַחְצִי "the pot of my washing"; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; נַעַל "sandal"; עֲלֵי־פְלֶשֶׁת אֶתְרוֹעָע "over Philistia I will raise a shout" (hitpolel of רוּעַ "shout"; not "triumph"), where Ps 60:8 has עָלַי פְּלֶשֶׁת הִתְרֹעָעִי "over me, Philistia, raise a shout".
- **"Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?" → "Who will bring me to a fortified city? Who has led me as far as Edom?"** (v10) — Hebrew עִיר מִבְצָר "a fortified city" (lit. "a city of fortress", מִבְצָר; no article), where Ps 60:9 has עִיר מָצוֹר "a city of defense"; מִי נָחַנִי, perfect of נָחָה "lead": "who has led me", perhaps "who will lead me" (KJV), as Ps 60:9; עַד־אֱדוֹם "as far as Edom".
- **"Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our hosts?" → "Have you not, Elohim, cast us off, and you do not go out, Elohim, with our hosts?"** (v11) — Hebrew הֲלֹא־אֱלֹהִים זְנַחְתָּנוּ וְלֹא־תֵצֵא אֱלֹהִים בְּצִבְאֹתֵינוּ: without the אַתָּה "you" of Ps 60:10 ("Is it not you, Elohim, who …"); זָנַח "cast off"; the second clause is a statement inside the question ("and you do not go out"), not a second question; צָבָא "host".
- **"Give us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man" → "Give us help from the adversary, and empty is the salvation of man"** (v12) — the same Hebrew as Ps 60:11: הָבָה־לָּנוּ "give us"; מִצָּר "from the adversary" (צַר; also "from distress"); וְשָׁוְא "and empty" (וְ, not "for"); תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation".
- **"Through God we shall do valiantly: for he it is that shall tread down our enemies" → "In Elohim we shall do valiantly, and it is he who will trample our adversaries"** (v13) — the same Hebrew as Ps 60:12: בֵּאלֹהִים "in Elohim"; נַעֲשֶׂה־חָיִל "we shall do valiantly" (lit. "make force"); וְהוּא "and he"; בּוּס "trample"; צָרֵינוּ "our adversaries".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–13 = Heb v2–14.
- **Composite** — v1–5 = Ps 57:7–11; v6–13 = Ps 60:5–12. Ps 57 and 60 stand in the Elohistic psalms (42–83); here v3 has יְהוָה, the other divine names are as in the two psalms.
