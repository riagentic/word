# Psalm 89

A Maskil. Of ⟦ethan|Etan|Ethan⟧ the ⟦ezrahite|Ezrachi|Ezrahite⟧.

[1] The ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|acts of steadfast love⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ forever I will sing;
to generation and generation I will make known your faithfulness with my mouth.

[2] For I have said, "Forever ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ shall be built;
the heavens, you establish your faithfulness in them."

[3] I have cut a covenant with my chosen one;
I have sworn to David my servant:

[4] Forever I will establish your seed,
and build your throne to generation and generation. Selah.

[5] And the heavens shall give thanks for your wonder, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
your faithfulness also in the assembly of the holy ones.

[6] For who in the skies can be set beside ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
be like ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ among the sons of ⟦el|elim|God⟧?

[7] ⟦el|El|God⟧ is dreaded greatly in the council of the holy ones,
and fearsome above all who are around him.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts,
who is like you, mighty one, ⟦yah|Yah|LORD⟧?
And your faithfulness is around you.

[9] You rule over the swelling of the sea;
when its waves lift up, you still them.

[10] You crushed ⟦rahab-monster|Rahav|Rahab⟧ like one slain;
with the arm of your strength you scattered your enemies.

[11] Yours are the heavens; yours also is the earth;
the world and its fullness, you founded them.

[12] North and south, you created them;
⟦tabor|Tavor|Tabor⟧ and ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧ shout for joy in your name.

[13] Yours is an arm with might;
your hand is strong, your right hand is high.

[14] Righteousness and judgment are the established place of your throne;
⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth come to meet your face.

[15] Happy is the people who know the shouting;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, in the light of your face they walk.

[16] In your name they are glad all the day,
and in your righteousness they are high.

[17] For you are the beauty of their strength,
and in your good pleasure our horn is high.

[18] For to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ belongs our shield,
and to the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ our king.

[19] Then you spoke in a vision to your faithful ones, and said,
"I have set help upon a mighty one;
I have raised a chosen one from the people.

[20] I have found David my servant;
with my holy oil I have anointed him,

[21] with whom my hand shall be established;
my arm also shall strengthen him.

[22] An enemy shall not deceive him,
and a son of injustice shall not afflict him.

[23] And I will beat down his adversaries from before him,
and those who hate him I will strike.

[24] And my faithfulness and my ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ are with him,
and in my name his horn shall be high.

[25] And I will set his hand on the sea,
and his right hand on the rivers.

[26] He shall call to me, 'You are my Father,
my ⟦el|El|God⟧, and the Rock of my salvation.'

[27] I also will make him firstborn,
highest of the kings of the earth.

[28] Forever I will keep for him my ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and my covenant shall be sure for him.

[29] And I will set his seed perpetually,
and his throne as the days of the heavens.

[30] If his sons forsake my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧
and do not walk in my judgments,

[31] if they profane my statutes
and do not keep my commandments,

[32] then I will visit their transgression with the rod,
and their iniquity with strokes.

[33] But my ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ I will not break off from him,
and I will not be false in my faithfulness.

[34] I will not profane my covenant,
and what has gone out of my lips I will not change.

[35] Once I have sworn by my holiness:
I will not lie to David.

[36] His seed shall be forever,
and his throne like the sun before me.

[37] Like the moon it shall be established forever,
and a witness in the skies is faithful." Selah.

[38] But you have cast off and rejected;
you have been enraged with your ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧.

[39] You have spurned the covenant of your servant;
you have profaned his crown to the ground.

[40] You have breached all his walls;
you have set his fortresses as a ruin.

[41] All who pass by on the way have pillaged him;
he has become a reproach to his neighbors.

[42] You have raised the right hand of his adversaries;
you have made all his enemies rejoice.

[43] You also turn back the edge of his sword,
and you have not made him stand in the battle.

[44] You have made him cease from his brightness,
and his throne you have hurled to the ground.

[45] You have cut short the days of his youth;
you have covered him over with shame. Selah.

[46] How long, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧? Will you hide yourself forever?
Shall your fury burn like fire?

[47] Remember, I — what a lifespan!

[48] Who is the man who shall live and not see death,

[49] Where are your former ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|acts of steadfast love⟧, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧,

[50] Remember, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, the reproach of your servants,
my bearing in my bosom all the many peoples,

[51] with which your enemies have reproached, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,

[52] Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 6, 8, 15, 18, 46, 51, 52) — Tetragrammaton. No "O" before a name or title (v5, 8, 15, 46, 49, 50, 51): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"God" → "El"** (v7, 26) and **"the mighty" → "the sons of elim"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵל and בְּנֵי אֵלִים, as Ps 29:1: אֵלִים is the plural of אֵל, heavenly beings (whether "sons of gods" or "sons of El" is debated, from memory).
- **"A Maschil of Ethan the Ezrahite" → "A Maskil. Of Ethan the Ezrahite."** (heading) — Hebrew מַשְׂכִּיל לְאֵיתָן הָאֶזְרָחִי, in this order. מַשְׂכִּיל: a heading term of uncertain sense (from שָׂכַל "give insight"); kept. לְ: "by", "for" or "belonging to"; "of" leaves it open. Ethan the Ezrahite: 1 Kgs 4:31.
- **"I will sing of the mercies of the LORD for ever: with my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all generations" → "The acts of steadfast love of Yahweh forever I will sing; to generation and generation I will make known your faithfulness with my mouth"** (v1) — Hebrew חַסְדֵי יְהוָה, the plural of *chesed* (*chasadim*, as Ps 17:7; Isa 55:3), set first; עוֹלָם "forever"; אָשִׁירָה, a cohortative ("I will sing"); לְדֹר וָדֹר "to generation and generation".
- **"Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy faithfulness shalt thou establish in the very heavens" → "Forever chesed shall be built; the heavens, you establish your faithfulness in them"** (v2) — Hebrew עוֹלָם חֶסֶד יִבָּנֶה "forever chesed shall be built"; שָׁמַיִם תָּכִן אֱמוּנָתְךָ בָהֶם, "the heavens" set first and taken up by "in them"; תָּכִן is from כּוּן "establish" (the root of v4, 14, 21, 37). How far the words of "I have said" reach is not marked.
- **"I have made a covenant with my chosen" → "I have cut a covenant with my chosen one"** (v3) — Hebrew כָּרַתִּי בְרִית "I have cut a covenant"; לִבְחִירִי, literally "for / to my chosen one". The speaker changes without a word: Yahweh speaks in v3–4 (the earlier "You have said" was supplied; no quotation marks in the Hebrew).
- **"Thy seed will I establish for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations" → "Forever I will establish your seed, and build your throne to generation and generation"** (v4) — Hebrew עַד־עוֹלָם אָכִין זַרְעֶךָ, the words of 2 Sam 7:12–13, 16: זֶרַע "seed", הֵכִין "establish", כִּסֵּא "throne", עַד־עוֹלָם "forever", the same English as there; וּבָנִיתִי "and I will build".
- **"And the heavens shall praise thy wonders, O LORD: thy faithfulness also in the congregation of the saints" → "And the heavens shall give thanks for your wonder, Yahweh, your faithfulness also in the assembly of the holy ones"** (v5) — Hebrew וְיוֹדוּ (הוֹדָה "give thanks"; "for" supplied before the object), an imperfect with "and"; פִּלְאֲךָ "your wonder", singular; קָהָל "assembly"; קְדֹשִׁים "holy ones" (also v7).
- **"For who in the heaven can be compared unto the LORD? who among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto the LORD?" → "For who in the skies can be set beside Yahweh, be like Yahweh among the sons of elim?"** (v6) — Hebrew שַׁחַק "skies" (also v37); יַעֲרֹךְ לְ, from עָרַךְ "arrange, set in order": "set in a row beside" = compare; יִדְמֶה "be like". The second line has no second "who": one question.
- **"God is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints, and to be had in reverence of all them that are about him" → "El is dreaded greatly in the council of the holy ones, and fearsome above all who are around him"** (v7) — Hebrew אֵל נַעֲרָץ (nifal of עָרַץ "be terrified": "dreaded"); סוֹד "council"; רַבָּה "greatly" stands at the end of the first line by the accents (it is also read "great", of the council); נוֹרָא "fearsome"; עַל "above".
- **"O LORD God of hosts, who is a strong LORD like unto thee? or to thy faithfulness round about thee?" → "Yahweh Elohim of Hosts, who is like you, mighty one, Yah? And your faithfulness is around you"** (v8) — Hebrew מִי־כָמוֹךָ "who is like you"; חֲסִין "mighty" (only here); יָהּ *Yah*; וֶאֱמוּנָתְךָ סְבִיבוֹתֶיךָ, literally "and your faithfulness, your surroundings", a clause of its own after the main break.
- **"Thou rulest the raging of the sea: when the waves thereof arise, thou stillest them" → "You rule over the swelling of the sea; when its waves lift up, you still them"** (v9) — Hebrew מוֹשֵׁל בְּ "rule over"; גֵּאוּת "swelling" (literally "rising up, majesty"); בְּשׂוֹא, the infinitive of נָשָׂא "lift up"; אַתָּה "you" stands in both lines.
- **"Thou hast broken Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain; thou hast scattered thine enemies with thy strong arm" → "You crushed Rahab like one slain; with the arm of your strength you scattered your enemies"** (v10) — Hebrew דִכִּאתָ "you crushed"; כֶחָלָל "like one slain" (חָלָל, one pierced; "carcass" was not the word); רַהַב, the sea monster of the old poetry (Job 26:12), elsewhere a name for Egypt (87:4): the `rahab-monster` token; בִּזְרוֹעַ עֻזְּךָ "with the arm of your strength" (עֹז).
- **"The heavens are thine, the earth also is thine: as for the world and the fulness thereof, thou hast founded them"** — kept in sense (v11): תֵּבֵל "world"; מְלֹא "fullness".
- **"The north and the south thou hast created them: Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name" → "North and south, you created them; Tabor and Hermon shout for joy in your name"** (v12) — Hebrew צָפוֹן וְיָמִין, literally "north and right" (the right hand of one facing east is the south); בָּרָא "create"; יְרַנֵּנוּ "shout for joy" (רִנֵּן), an imperfect.
- **"Thou hast a mighty arm: strong is thy hand, and high is thy right hand" → "Yours is an arm with might; your hand is strong, your right hand is high"** (v13) — Hebrew לְךָ זְרוֹעַ עִם־גְּבוּרָה "to you (is) an arm with might" (גְּבוּרָה "might"); תָּעֹז "is strong" (עָזַז); תָּרוּם "is high": רוּם qal is "be high" through the psalm (v13, 16, 17, 24; as Ps 18:46), the hifil הֵרִים "raise" (v19, 42).
- **"Justice and judgment are the habitation of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face" → "Righteousness and judgment are the established place of your throne; chesed and truth come to meet your face"** (v14) — Hebrew צֶדֶק "righteousness"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; מְכוֹן "established place" (from כּוּן, as Exod 15:17); אֱמֶת "truth"; יְקַדְּמוּ פָנֶיךָ "come to meet your face" (קִדֵּם; "go before" is another reading of the verb).
- **"Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound: they shall walk, O LORD, in the light of thy countenance" → "Happy is the people who know the shouting; Yahweh, in the light of your face they walk"** (v15) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "happy" ("blessed" is בָּרוּךְ); תְּרוּעָה "shouting" (neither voice nor instrument is named; a blast of the shofar may be meant); "Yahweh" begins the second line by the accents; יְהַלֵּכוּן "they walk" (hitpael: walk about).
- **"In thy name shall they rejoice all the day" → "In your name they are glad all the day"** (v16) — Hebrew יְגִילוּן (גִּיל "be glad"); יָרוּמוּ "they are high".
- **"For thou art the glory of their strength: and in thy favour our horn shall be exalted" → "For you are the beauty of their strength, and in your good pleasure our horn is high"** (v17) — Hebrew תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty"; רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (as Ps 30:5). The qere תָּרוּם "is high" is translated; the ketiv תָּרִים is "you exalt (our horn)".
- **"For the LORD is our defence; and the Holy One of Israel is our king" → "For to Yahweh belongs our shield, and to the Holy One of Israel our king"** (v18) — Hebrew לַיהוָה מָגִנֵּנוּ "to Yahweh (is) our shield"; מָגֵן "shield"; וְ "and".
- **"Then thou spakest in vision to thy holy one, and saidst, I have laid help upon one that is mighty; I have exalted one chosen out of the people" → "Then you spoke in a vision to your faithful ones, and said, 'I have set help upon a mighty one; I have raised a chosen one from the people'"** (v19) — Hebrew אָז "then" (not "of old"); לַחֲסִידֶיךָ "to your faithful ones", plural (many Hebrew manuscripts have the singular, from memory); חָסִיד "faithful one" (one who keeps *chesed*); שִׁוִּיתִי "I have set"; עֵזֶר "help"; גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; בָחוּר is "a young man" or "a chosen one" (passive of בָּחַר): the second is taken, beside בְּחִירִי "my chosen one" of v3; uncertain. The words of Yahweh run to v37.
- **"With whom my hand shall be established: mine arm also shall strengthen him"** — kept (v21): Hebrew אֲשֶׁר … עִמּוֹ "with whom" (the earlier "so that" was not the word); תִּכּוֹן "shall be established" (כּוּן); תְאַמְּצֶנּוּ "shall strengthen him" (אִמֵּץ).
- **"The enemy shall not exact upon him; nor the son of wickedness afflict him" → "An enemy shall not deceive him, and a son of injustice shall not afflict him"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹא־יַשִּׁא אוֹיֵב בּוֹ: יַשִּׁא is the hifil of נָשָׁא "deceive" (as Gen 3:13), or of נָשָׁה "lend on interest" with בְּ ("exact from him", KJV); uncertain. וּבֶן־עַוְלָה לֹא יְעַנֶּנּוּ, the words of 2 Sam 7:10 in the singular: "son of injustice … afflict" as there.
- **"And I will beat down his foes before his face, and plague them that hate him" → "And I will beat down his adversaries from before him, and those who hate him I will strike"** (v23) — Hebrew כַּתּוֹתִי (כָּתַת "beat down, crush in pieces"); מִפָּנָיו "from before him"; צַר "adversary" (also v42); אֶגּוֹף "I will strike" (נָגַף).
- **"But my faithfulness and my mercy shall be with him" → "And my faithfulness and my chesed are with him"** (v24) — Hebrew וְ "and"; no verb ("are" supplied).
- **"I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers" → "And I will set his hand on the sea, and his right hand on the rivers"** (v25) — Hebrew שַׂמְתִּי (שִׂים "set", also v29); בְּ "in / on".
- **"Thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation"** (v26) — Hebrew אָבִי אָתָּה, literally "my father are you" (2 Sam 7:14 "I will be to him for a father"); אֵלִי "my El"; צוּר "Rock", a name of God (capital).
- **"Also I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth" → "I also will make him firstborn, highest of the kings of the earth"** (v27) — Hebrew אַף־אָנִי בְּכוֹר אֶתְּנֵהוּ, literally "I also, firstborn I will give him" (no "my"); עֶלְיוֹן "highest" (the word of the title "Most High"; not the title here, so no token) לְמַלְכֵי־אָרֶץ, literally "to the kings of the earth".
- **"My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my covenant shall stand fast with him" → "Forever I will keep for him my chesed, and my covenant shall be sure for him"** (v28) — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם first; אֶשְׁמָר (qere; the ketiv אשמור is the same verb); נֶאֱמֶנֶת "sure" (נֶאְמַן, as 2 Sam 7:16 "your house … shall be sure").
- **"His seed also will I make to endure for ever" → "And I will set his seed perpetually"** (v29) — Hebrew וְשַׂמְתִּי לָעַד זַרְעוֹ, literally "and I will set his seed to perpetuity" (לָעַד "perpetually"); כִּימֵי שָׁמָיִם "as the days of the heavens".
- **"If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments" → "If his sons forsake my torah and do not walk in my judgments"** (v30) — Hebrew בָנָיו "his sons"; תּוֹרָה *torah*; מִשְׁפָּטַי "my judgments" (rulings; "rules" was not the word).
- **"If they break my statutes" → "if they profane my statutes"** (v31) — Hebrew יְחַלֵּלוּ, חִלֵּל "profane": the one verb of v31, 34 ("I will not profane my covenant") and v39 ("you have profaned his crown"). חֻקָּה "statute"; מִצְוָה "commandment".
- **"Then will I visit their transgression with the rod, and their iniquity with stripes"** (v32) — Hebrew פָקַדְתִּי "I will visit" (פָּקַד: attend to, here for punishment); שֵׁבֶט "rod" and נְגָעִים "strokes", the two words of 2 Sam 7:14 ("the rod of men … the strokes of the sons of man").
- **"Nevertheless my lovingkindness will I not utterly take from him, nor suffer my faithfulness to fail" → "But my chesed I will not break off from him, and I will not be false in my faithfulness"** (v33) — Hebrew לֹא־אָפִיר, hifil of פָּרַר "break, annul"; 2 Sam 7:15 has another verb, סוּר "depart / remove" (some Hebrew manuscripts and versions read אָסִיר "I will remove" here, from memory: the MT is kept). אֲשַׁקֵּר בְּ "be false in" (שֶׁקֶר "falsehood").
- **"My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips" → "I will not profane my covenant, and what has gone out of my lips I will not change"** (v34) — Hebrew אֲחַלֵּל as v31; מוֹצָא שְׂפָתַי, literally "the going out of my lips"; שִׁנָּה "change".
- **"Once have I sworn by my holiness that I will not lie unto David" → "Once I have sworn by my holiness: I will not lie to David"** (v35) — Hebrew אַחַת "once" (or "one thing"); the oath formula אִם־לְדָוִד אֲכַזֵּב, literally "if I lie to David", is a sworn "I will not lie" (the word-for-word "if" would say the opposite).
- **"His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me" → "His seed shall be forever, and his throne like the sun before me"** (v36) — Hebrew יִהְיֶה "shall be"; נֶגְדִּי "before me" (in front of me).
- **"It shall be established for ever as the moon, and as a faithful witness in heaven" → "Like the moon it shall be established forever, and a witness in the skies is faithful"** (v37) — Hebrew יִכּוֹן (כּוּן); וְעֵד בַּשַּׁחַק נֶאֱמָן "and a witness in the skies (is) faithful": there is no "as"; whether the witness is the moon or another is not said; נֶאֱמָן of a person "faithful" (of a house "sure", v28).
- **"But thou hast cast off and abhorred, thou hast been wroth with thine anointed" → "But you have cast off and rejected; you have been enraged with your anointed"** (v38) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה "But you"; זָנַח "cast off" (as 88:14); מָאַס "reject" (no object in the Hebrew); הִתְעַבַּרְתָּ עִם "you have been enraged with"; מָשִׁיחַ, the *mashiach* token. "Now" was not in the Hebrew.
- **"Thou hast made void the covenant of thy servant: thou hast profaned his crown by casting it to the ground" → "You have spurned the covenant of your servant; you have profaned his crown to the ground"** (v39) — Hebrew נֵאַרְתָּה (נֵאֵר, rare: "spurn, renounce"); בְּרִית עַבְדֶּךָ "the covenant of your servant"; נֵזֶר "crown" (the mark of one set apart); לָאָרֶץ "to the ground".
- **"Thou hast broken down all his hedges; thou hast brought his strong holds to ruin" → "You have breached all his walls; you have set his fortresses as a ruin"** (v40) — Hebrew פָּרַצְתָּ "you have breached" (פָּרַץ); גְּדֵרֹת "walls" (stone walls, of a vineyard or a town; "folds" where flocks are meant); מִבְצָר "fortress"; שַׂמְתָּ … מְחִתָּה "you have set … as a ruin" (מְחִתָּה "ruin", from חָתַת "be shattered").
- **"All that pass by the way spoil him: he is a reproach to his neighbours" → "All who pass by on the way have pillaged him; he has become a reproach to his neighbors"** (v41) — Hebrew שַׁסֻּהוּ (שָׁסָה "pillage"); עֹבְרֵי דָרֶךְ "passers of the way"; הָיָה "has become"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (also v50; the verb חֵרֵף "reproach" v51).
- **"Thou hast set up the right hand of his adversaries" → "You have raised the right hand of his adversaries"** (v42) — Hebrew הֲרִימוֹתָ (הֵרִים "raise", as v19).
- **"Thou hast also turned the edge of his sword, and hast not made him to stand in the battle" → "You also turn back the edge of his sword, and you have not made him stand in the battle"** (v43) — Hebrew תָּשִׁיב, an imperfect ("you turn back"); צוּר חַרְבּוֹ, literally "the rock / flint of his sword" (= its edge; the word "rock" of v26); הֲקֵימֹתוֹ "made him stand"; מִלְחָמָה "battle" (a single fight).
- **"Thou hast made his glory to cease, and cast his throne down to the ground" → "You have made him cease from his brightness, and his throne you have hurled to the ground"** (v44) — Hebrew הִשְׁבַּתָּ מִטְּהָרוֹ, literally "you have made cease from his brightness" (טְהָר "brightness, cleanness"; "him" is supplied); some repoint it "you have made cease his scepter" (מַטֵּה), from memory: the MT is kept. מִגַּרְתָּה "you have hurled" (מִגֵּר, rare).
- **"thou hast covered him with shame" → "you have covered him over with shame"** (v45) — Hebrew הֶעֱטִיתָ עָלָיו בּוּשָׁה, literally "you have wrapped shame over him" (עָטָה עַל "cover over"); עֲלוּמִים "youth".
- **"How long, LORD? wilt thou hide thyself for ever? shall thy wrath burn like fire?" → "How long, Yahweh? Will you hide yourself forever? Shall your fury burn like fire?"** (v46) — Hebrew עַד־מָה stands once (the earlier second "How long" was supplied); לָנֶצַח "forever"; חֵמָה "fury".
- **"Remember how short my time is: wherefore hast thou made all men in vain?" → "Remember, I — what a lifespan! For what emptiness have you created all the sons of man?"** (v47) — Hebrew זְכָר־אֲנִי מֶה־חָלֶד, word for word "remember — I — what a lifespan" (חֶלֶד "duration of life, the world"); "how short my time is" interprets. The word list has "world" for חֶלֶד (Ps 47–50 row); here the line is about a man's span beside "all the sons of man", so the sense "duration" is kept ("what a world" would say something else). Some read זְכֹר אֲדֹנָי "remember, Adonai" (as v50), from memory: the MT is kept. עַל־מַה־שָּׁוְא "for what emptiness" (שָׁוְא); בְּנֵי־אָדָם "sons of man".
- **"What man is he that liveth, and shall not see death? shall he deliver his soul from the hand of the grave?" → "Who is the man who shall live and not see death, who shall rescue his soul from the hand of Sheol?"** (v48) — Hebrew מִי גֶבֶר "who (is) the man" (גֶּבֶר, alone in the verse); יְמַלֵּט "rescue" (מִלֵּט); מִיַּד־שְׁאוֹל "from the hand of Sheol".
- **"Lord, where are thy former lovingkindnesses, which thou swarest unto David in thy truth?" → "Where are your former acts of steadfast love, Adonai, which you swore to David in your faithfulness?"** (v49) — Hebrew אַיֵּה חֲסָדֶיךָ הָרִאשֹׁנִים, the plural of *chesed* (as v1); אֲדֹנָי after the question, as in the Hebrew; "which" is supplied (the Hebrew joins the clause without a word); בֶּאֱמוּנָתֶךָ "in your faithfulness" (אֱמוּנָה; "truth" is אֱמֶת).
- **"how I do bear in my bosom the reproach of all the mighty people" → "my bearing in my bosom all the many peoples"** (v50) — Hebrew שְׂאֵתִי בְחֵיקִי כָּל־רַבִּים עַמִּים, word for word "my bearing in my bosom all many peoples": the reproach of v50a is understood ("the reproach", "the insults" are supplied in other versions; an emendation reads "the strivings of peoples", from memory). עֲבָדֶיךָ "your servants", plural (some manuscripts have the singular, from memory). חֵיק "bosom".
- **"Wherewith thine enemies have reproached, O LORD; wherewith they have reproached the footsteps of thine anointed" → "with which your enemies have reproached, Yahweh, with which they have reproached the footsteps of your anointed"** (v51) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר twice, "with which" (KJV "wherewith"; "with" supplied: the reproach of v50); חֵרֵף "reproach"; עִקְּבוֹת "footsteps" (literally "heels, tracks").
- **"Blessed be the LORD for evermore. Amen, and Amen" → "Blessed is Yahweh forever. Amen and Amen."** (v52) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה לְעוֹלָם, no verb: "is" (as Ps 41:13); אָמֵן וְאָמֵן "Amen and Amen", as Ps 41:13.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–52 = Heb v2–53.
- **The promise to David** (v3–4, 19–37) — the words of 2 Sam 7:8–16 are taken up; the same English stands where the Hebrew is the same (seed, establish, throne, forever, rod, strokes, son of injustice, afflict, sure); where the psalm has its own word, it has its own English (v33 "break off", 2 Sam 7:15 "depart / remove").
- **Massive tension** — the covenant sworn (v19–37) and its apparent collapse (v38–51).
- **v52** — doxology closing Book III (as 41:13 closes Book I, 72:18–19 Book II).
