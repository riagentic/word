# Psalm 21

To the overseer. A psalm of David.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, in your strength the king rejoices,
and in your salvation how greatly he is glad!

[2] The desire of his heart you have given him,
and the request of his lips you have not held back. Selah.

[3] For you come to meet him with blessings of good;
you set on his head a crown of fine gold.

[4] Life he asked of you; you have given to him
length of days, forever and ever.

[5] Great is his glory in your salvation;
majesty and splendor you lay upon him.

[6] For you set him as blessings forever;
you make him joyful with gladness with your face.

[7] For the king trusts in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and in the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ of ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ he shall not be shaken.

[8] Your hand will find all your enemies;
your right hand will find those who hate you.

[9] You will set them like an oven of fire
at the time of your face;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in his anger will swallow them,
and fire will consume them.

[10] Their fruit you will do away with from the earth,
and their seed from the sons of man.

[11] For they stretched out evil against you;
they devised a scheme — they shall not be able.

[12] For you will set them as a shoulder;
with your bowstrings you will aim at their faces.

[13] Be high, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, in your strength!
Let us sing and make music to your might.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 7, 9, 13) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד, as Ps 19; לְ "of" (by, for or belonging to: not decided).
- **"The king shall joy in thy strength … how greatly shall he rejoice" → "in your strength the king rejoices … how greatly he is glad"** (v1) — Hebrew יִשְׂמַח (שָׂמַח "rejoice"); יָגֶל (גִּיל "be glad"), the qere; the written form יגיל is the same verb. עֹז "strength" (v13). מֶלֶךְ stands without the article (as often in poetry); v7 has הַמֶּלֶךְ.
- **"his heart's desire … hast not withholden" → "The desire of his heart … you have not held back"** (v2) — Hebrew תַּאֲוַת לִבּוֹ (תַּאֲוָה, elsewhere "craving"; here a good wish, said without blame); מָנַע "hold back". אֲרֶשֶׁת "request" is found only here.
- **"thou preventest him with the blessings of goodness" → "you come to meet him with blessings of good"** (v3) — Hebrew תְקַדְּמֶנּוּ (קִדֵּם "come to meet"); בִּרְכוֹת טוֹב. **"settest a crown of pure gold on his head" → "you set on his head a crown of fine gold"** — Hebrew שִׁית "set" (also v6, 9, 12); פָּז "fine gold".
- **"thou gavest it him, even length of days" → "you have given to him length of days"** (v4) — Hebrew נָתַתָּה לּוֹ: no "it"; "length of days" stands beside "life" as what was given. **"for ever and ever"** — Hebrew עוֹלָם וָעֶד, "age and perpetuity".
- **"His glory is great in thy salvation: honour and majesty hast thou laid upon him" → "majesty and splendor you lay upon him"** (v5) — Hebrew הוֹד וְהָדָר; תְּשַׁוֶּה "lay, place".
- **"thou hast made him most blessed for ever" → "you set him as blessings forever"** (v6) — Hebrew תְשִׁיתֵהוּ בְרָכוֹת, literally "you set him blessings": he is made a blessing; "as" is supplied. **"thou hast made him exceeding glad with thy countenance" → "you make him joyful with gladness with your face"** — Hebrew תְּחַדֵּהוּ (חָדָה "be joyful"; שִׂמַּח is "gladden"); שִׂמְחָה "gladness"; אֶת־פָּנֶיךָ "with your face", that is, in your presence.
- **"through the mercy of the most High he shall not be moved" → "in the chesed (steadfast love) of Elyon he shall not be shaken"** (v7) — Hebrew וּבְחֶסֶד עֶלְיוֹן; בַּל־יִמּוֹט (מוֹט nifal "be shaken"; בַּל is a poetic "not").
- **"shall find out" → "will find"** (v8) — Hebrew תִּמְצָא, the plain verb, the first time with לְ ("find to", reach).
- **"Thou shalt make them as a fiery oven in the time of thine anger" → "You will set them like an oven of fire at the time of your face"** (v9) — Hebrew כְּתַנּוּר אֵשׁ לְעֵת פָּנֶיךָ: "anger" is not written here; פָּנִים "face". **"in his wrath" → "in his anger"** — Hebrew בְּאַפּוֹ (אַף "nose, anger"). **"devour" → "consume"** — Hebrew תֹאכְלֵם, literally "eat". Whom v8–12 address (the king, or Yahweh) is not said; the verse moves from "you" to "Yahweh … his".
- **"Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from among the children of men" → "do away with … from the sons of man"** (v10) — Hebrew תְּאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד "perish"; זֶרַע "seed"; בְּנֵי אָדָם "the sons of man".
- **"they intended evil against thee: they imagined a mischievous device, which they are not able to perform" → "they stretched out evil against you; they devised a scheme — they shall not be able"** (v11) — Hebrew נָטוּ עָלֶיךָ רָעָה "they stretched out evil against you"; חָשְׁבוּ מְזִמָּה; בַּל־יוּכָלוּ, "which … to perform" is not written.
- **"thou shalt make them turn their back" → "you will set them as a shoulder"** (v12) — Hebrew תְּשִׁיתֵמוֹ שֶׁכֶם, literally "you set them a shoulder": they show their shoulder, turning to flee. **"when thou shalt make ready thine arrows upon thy strings against the face of them" → "with your bowstrings you will aim at their faces"** — Hebrew בְּמֵיתָרֶיךָ (מֵיתָר "cord"); תְּכוֹנֵן "make ready, aim" (the root of "establish"); "arrows" is not written.
- **"Be thou exalted … so will we sing and praise thy power" → "Be high … Let us sing and make music to your might"** (v13) — Hebrew רוּמָה "be high"; נָשִׁירָה וּנְזַמְּרָה, cohortatives; זִמֵּר "make music"; גְּבוּרָתֶךָ "your might".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v13 = Heb v14.
- **Royal thanksgiving** — after Ps 20's prayer, Ps 21's answer.
- **Companion pair** — before and after battle.
