# Psalm 138

Of David.

[1] I will give you thanks with all my heart;
before gods I will make music to you.

[2] I will bow down toward your holy temple,
and give thanks to your name for your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and for your truth;
for you have made great your saying above all your name.

[3] On the day I called, then you answered me;
you made me bold in my soul with strength.

[4] All the kings of the earth shall give you thanks, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
for they have heard the sayings of your mouth.

[5] And they shall sing in the ways of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for great is the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is high, and the low he sees;
and the high one he knows from afar.

[7] If I walk in the midst of distress, you let me live;
against the anger of my enemies you stretch out your hand,
and your right hand saves me.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will complete for me;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever;
do not let drop the works of your hands.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 5, 6, 8) — Tetragrammaton; the Hebrew vocatives have no "O" (v4, 8).
- **"A Psalm of David" → "Of David"** (heading) — Hebrew לְדָוִד only, the first word of v1 (the Hebrew counts it in v1; our numbering is the same); מִזְמוֹר "a psalm" is not written. The לְ may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and is not decided here.
- **"I will praise thee with my whole heart: before the gods will I sing praise unto thee" → "I will give you thanks with all my heart; before gods I will make music to you"** (v1) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ "I will give you thanks" (הוֹדָה, also v2, 4); בְכָל־לִבִּי "with all my heart"; אֲזַמְּרֶךָּ "I will make music to you" (זִמֵּר). The Hebrew of v1 has no divine name: "O Yahweh" (the earlier text) is not written (the Greek has "Lord", from memory). נֶגֶד אֱלֹהִים "before gods", without the article, as printed: the sense is not decided in the verse — the gods of the nations, set against v4 "all the kings of the earth"; heavenly beings (the Greek "angels", from memory; as Ps 82:1, 6); or "before Elohim", God (the noun is the same). The plural "gods" is given as in Ps 82:1; 86:8.
- **"I will worship toward thy holy temple, and praise thy name for thy lovingkindness and for thy truth: for thou hast magnified thy word above all thy name" → "I will bow down toward your holy temple, and give thanks to your name for your chesed and for your truth; for you have made great your saying above all your name"** (v2) — Hebrew אֶשְׁתַּחֲוֶה "bow down" (הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה); הֵיכַל קָדְשְׁךָ "your holy temple" (lit. "the temple of your holiness"); חֶסֶד; אֱמֶת "truth"; הִגְדַּלְתָּ "you have made great"; אִמְרָתְךָ "your saying" (אִמְרָה; v4 אֵמֶר). The last line stands as the Hebrew has it, though the sense is hard: "your saying" is made great above "all your name" (above all that your name has made known?); the earlier text ("you have exalted above all things your name and your word") follows an emended text (from memory), not the printed one.
- **"In the day when I cried thou answeredst me, and strengthenedst me with strength in my soul" → "On the day I called, then you answered me; you made me bold in my soul with strength"** (v3) — Hebrew קָרָאתִי "I called" (קָרָא); וַתַּעֲנֵנִי, the וַ after the time clause, "then you answered me"; תַּרְהִבֵנִי, hifil of רָהַב: the meaning is uncertain ("make bold", "embolden"; Song 6:5 "overwhelm"); בְנַפְשִׁי עֹז "in my soul, strength" (עֹז "strength").
- **"shall praise thee, O LORD, when they hear the words of thy mouth" → "shall give you thanks, Yahweh, for they have heard the sayings of your mouth"** (v4) — Hebrew יוֹדוּךָ "shall give you thanks" (or the wish "let … give you thanks"); כִּי שָׁמְעוּ "for they have heard", a perfect (not "when they hear"); אִמְרֵי־פִיךָ "the sayings of your mouth" (אֵמֶר "saying").
- **"Yea, they shall sing in the ways of the LORD" → "And they shall sing in the ways of Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew וְיָשִׁירוּ "and they shall sing"; בְּדַרְכֵי "in the ways" (or "of the ways": בְּ with שִׁיר can mark the theme).
- **"Though the LORD be high, yet hath he respect unto the lowly: but the proud he knoweth afar off" → "For Yahweh is high, and the low he sees; and the high one he knows from afar"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי־רָם יְהוָה "for Yahweh is high" ("though" is not written); וְשָׁפָל יִרְאֶה "and the low he sees" (שָׁפָל "low"; רָאָה "see"); וְגָבֹהַּ "and the high one" (גָּבֹהַּ "high", the word set against שָׁפָל; "proud" is the sense, as 1 Sam 2:3); מִמֶּרְחָק "from afar"; יְיֵדָע, an unusual form of יָדַע "know".
- **"Though I walk in the midst of trouble, thou wilt revive me: thou shalt stretch forth thine hand against the wrath of mine enemies, and thy right hand shall save me" → "If I walk in the midst of distress, you let me live; against the anger of my enemies you stretch out your hand, and your right hand saves me"** (v7) — Hebrew אִם "if"; צָרָה "distress"; תְּחַיֵּנִי "you let me live" (חִיָּה, as Ps 41:2); עַל אַף אֹיְבַי "against the anger of my enemies" (אַף, lit. "nose"); שָׁלַח יָד "stretch out the hand"; וְתוֹשִׁיעֵנִי "and … saves me" (הוֹשִׁיעַ).
- **"The LORD will perfect that which concerneth me: thy mercy, O LORD, endureth for ever: forsake not the works of thine own hands" → "Yahweh will complete for me; Yahweh, your chesed is forever; do not let drop the works of your hands"** (v8) — Hebrew יִגְמֹר בַּעֲדִי "will complete for me" (גָּמַר, as Ps 57:2; no object is written: "that which concerneth me", "his purpose" are supplied); חַסְדְּךָ לְעוֹלָם "your chesed is forever" (no verb, as the refrain of Ps 136); מַעֲשֵׂי יָדֶיךָ "the works of your hands", plural; אַל־תֶּרֶף, hifil of רָפָה, "do not let drop" (as Deut 31:6; not עָזַב "forsake").
