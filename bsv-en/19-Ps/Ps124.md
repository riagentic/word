# Psalm 124

A song of the ascents. Of David.

[1] If it had not been ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who was for us —
let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ now say —

[2] if it had not been ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who was for us
when man rose up against us,

[3] then they would have swallowed us alive,
when their anger burned against us;

[4] then the waters would have overflowed us,
the torrent would have passed over our soul;

[5] then there would have passed over our soul
the presumptuous waters.

[6] Blessed be ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who has not given us
as prey to their teeth.

[7] Our soul, like a bird, has escaped
from the trap of the fowlers;
the trap is broken,
and we have escaped.

[8] Our help is in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the maker of heavens and earth.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 6, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees of David" → "A song of the ascents. Of David."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת לְדָוִד, the first words of v1 (see Ps 120). לְדָוִד "Of David": לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"If it had not been the LORD who was on our side" → "If it had not been Yahweh who was for us"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew לוּלֵי יְהוָה שֶׁהָיָה לָנוּ, "were it not Yahweh who was for us" (לָנוּ, as Ps 118:6 לִי "for me"). יֹאמַר־נָא יִשְׂרָאֵל "let Israel now say" (jussive with נָא, as Ps 118:2).
- **"when men rose up against us" → "when man rose up against us"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּקוּם עָלֵינוּ אָדָם: אָדָם "man" (singular, mankind; "people" in the former wording).
- **"Then they had swallowed us up quick, when their wrath was kindled against us" → "then they would have swallowed us alive, when their anger burned against us"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲזַי "then" (an old form of אָז, v3, 4, 5); חַיִּים בְּלָעוּנוּ "alive they would have swallowed us" (בָּלַע "swallow"; the perfect after לוּלֵי is what would have happened). בַּחֲרוֹת אַפָּם בָּנוּ "when their anger burned against us" (אַף "anger", literally "nose").
- **"Then the waters had overwhelmed us, the stream had gone over our soul" → "then the waters would have overflowed us, the torrent would have passed over our soul"** (v4) — Hebrew הַמַּיִם "the waters" (the former "the flood" is not the word); שָׁטַף "overflow" (as Ps 78:20); נַחְלָה, נַחַל with an ending of direction or a poetic ending: "the torrent" (נַחַל of the water itself). עָבַר "pass over"; עַל־נַפְשֵׁנוּ "over our soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; the former "over us" dropped it, v4, 5).
- **"Then the proud waters had gone over our soul" → "then there would have passed over our soul the presumptuous waters"** (v5) — Hebrew עָבַר עַל־נַפְשֵׁנוּ הַמַּיִם הַזֵּידוֹנִים: the waters stand last, as in the Hebrew. זֵידוֹן, from the root of זִיד / הֵזִיד "act presumptuously": "presumptuous" (KJV "proud"; "raging" in the former wording gives the sense of the water, not the word).
- **"Blessed be the LORD, who hath not given us as a prey to their teeth" → "Blessed be Yahweh, who has not given us as prey to their teeth"** (v6) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה, no verb ("be" / "is" supplied); טֶרֶף "prey".
- **"Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers" → "Our soul, like a bird, has escaped from the trap of the fowlers"** (v7) — Hebrew נַפְשֵׁנוּ … נִמְלְטָה "our soul has escaped" (dropped in the former "We have escaped"); נִמְלַט "escape", twice (the second time "we have escaped", אֲנַחְנוּ stressed). פַּח "trap" (the fowler's, twice); יוֹקְשִׁים "fowlers" (those who lay snares).
- **"Our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth" → "the maker of heavens and earth"** (v8) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה שָׁמַיִם וָאָרֶץ, a participle, as Ps 121:2.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
