# Psalm 39

To the overseer, to ⟦jeduthun|Yedutun|Jeduthun⟧. A psalm of David.

[1] I said, "Let me keep my ways
from sinning with my tongue;
let me keep a muzzle for my mouth,
while a wicked one is before me."

[2] I was mute with silence; I kept still from good,
and my pain was troubled.

[3] My heart was hot within me;
in my murmuring a fire burned;
I spoke with my tongue:

[4] "Make me know, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, my end,
and the measure of my days, what it is;
let me know how transient I am.

[5] Behold, handbreadths you have made my days,
and my lifetime is as nothing before you;
surely every man standing is all vapor. Selah.

[6] Surely as an image a man walks about;
surely for vapor they are in turmoil;
he heaps up, and does not know who gathers them.

[7] And now, what do I wait for, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧?
My hope, it is for you.

[8] From all my transgressions deliver me;
do not make me the reproach of a fool.

[9] I was mute; I do not open my mouth,
for it is you who have done it.

[10] Remove your stroke from upon me;
by the hostility of your hand I am spent.

[11] With reproofs for iniquity
you discipline a man,
and you melt like a moth what is desirable to him;
surely every man is vapor. Selah.

[12] Hear my prayer, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and to my cry for help give ear;
at my tears do not be deaf.
For a sojourner am I with you,
a settler, like all my fathers.

[13] Look away from me, that I may brighten up,
before I go and am no more."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 12) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v7) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"To the chief Musician, even to Jeduthun, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer, to Yedutun. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לִידוּתוּן מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). לִידוּתוּן is the qere; the ketiv ידיתון reads the name *Yedithun*. "Even" is not in the Hebrew: the same לְ "to" as in לַמְנַצֵּחַ. לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"I will take heed to my ways … I will keep my mouth with a bridle" → "Let me keep my ways … let me keep a muzzle for my mouth"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶשְׁמְרָה twice, cohortative ("let me keep", or "I will keep"); שָׁמַר "keep" both times. מַחְסוֹם "muzzle" (from חָסַם "muzzle", Deut 25:4); לְפִי "for my mouth".
- **"while the wicked is before me" → "while a wicked one is before me"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּעֹד רָשָׁע לְנֶגְדִּי, singular and without the article; לְנֶגְדִּי "before me" (the word of נֶגְדֶּךָ "before you", v5).
- **"I was dumb with silence, I held my peace, even from good" → "I was mute with silence; I kept still from good"** (v2) — Hebrew נֶאֱלַמְתִּי דוּמִיָּה הֶחֱשֵׁיתִי מִטּוֹב. מִטּוֹב "from good" is open: "away from (speaking) good", "even from good", or "without good coming of it" ("to no avail" of the former text was one guess).
- **"my sorrow was stirred" → "my pain was troubled"** (v2) — Hebrew וּכְאֵבִי נֶעְכָּר; כְּאֵב "pain"; נֶעְכָּר nifal of עָכַר "trouble" (also of water "be stirred up, muddied").
- **"while I was musing the fire burned: then spake I" → "in my murmuring a fire burned; I spoke"** (v3) — Hebrew בַּהֲגִיגִי תִבְעַר־אֵשׁ דִּבַּרְתִּי; הֲגִיג "murmuring" (as Ps 5:1; root of הָגָה "softly recite"; uncertain: "musing"); אֵשׁ has no article ("a fire"); "then" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"that I may know how frail I am" → "let me know how transient I am"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵדְעָה מֶה־חָדֵל אָנִי, cohortative "let me know"; חָדֵל, from חָדַל "cease", "ceasing, passing" (lit. "what a ceasing one I am"; "frail", "fleeting" are guesses at the image).
- **"thou hast made my days as an handbreadth" → "handbreadths you have made my days"** (v5) — Hebrew טְפָחוֹת נָתַתָּה יָמַי, plural "handbreadths", no "as", no "few"; נָתַן lit. "give, set". חֶלְדִּי "my lifetime" (חֶלֶד lit. "duration"; the Ps 47–50 row has "world", but with "my" and beside "my days" the length of life is meant).
- **"verily every man at his best state is altogether vanity" → "surely every man standing is all vapor"** (v5) — Hebrew אַךְ כָּל־הֶבֶל כָּל־אָדָם נִצָּב, lit. "surely all vapor, every man stationed": נִצָּב "standing, set firm" (the נִצָּב row: "stand"); הֶבֶל "vapor" (lit. "breath"; "vanity" in KJV), also v6, 11.
- **"Surely every man walketh in a vain shew" → "Surely as an image a man walks about"** (v6) — Hebrew אַךְ־בְּצֶלֶם יִתְהַלֶּךְ־אִישׁ; צֶלֶם "image" (here an image without substance, a likeness only; cf. Ps 73:20; "shadow" and "vain show" are guesses at it); בְּ "in, as"; הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about".
- **"surely they are disquieted in vain: he heapeth up riches" → "surely for vapor they are in turmoil; he heaps up"** (v6) — Hebrew אַךְ־הֶבֶל יֶהֱמָיוּן יִצְבֹּר; הָמָה "be in turmoil" (lit. "roar, murmur"); יִצְבֹּר "he heaps up" has no object ("riches", "wealth" were added); אֹסְפָם "gathers them".
- **"what wait I for? my hope is in thee" → "what do I wait for, Adonai? My hope, it is for you"** (v7) — Hebrew מַה־קִּוִּיתִי אֲדֹנָי תּוֹחַלְתִּי לְךָ הִיא. קִוָּה "wait for", perfect (of a lasting state; "what have I waited for" is also possible); תּוֹחֶלֶת "hope"; לְךָ "for you, to you" ("in you" was an interpretation).
- **"make me not the reproach of the foolish" → "do not make me the reproach of a fool"** (v8) — Hebrew חֶרְפַּת נָבָל, singular, no article.
- **"I was dumb, I opened not my mouth; because thou didst it" → "I was mute; I do not open my mouth, for it is you who have done it"** (v9) — Hebrew נֶאֱלַמְתִּי לֹא אֶפְתַּח־פִּי כִּי אַתָּה עָשִׂיתָ: perfect "I was mute" (as v2), imperfect "I do not open"; אַתָּה stands with the verb ("it is you who"); the object "it" is supplied.
- **"I am consumed by the blow of thine hand" → "by the hostility of your hand I am spent"** (v10) — Hebrew מִתִּגְרַת יָדְךָ אֲנִי כָלִיתִי. תִּגְרָה only here, from גָּרָה "stir up strife": "hostility" (uncertain; "blow", "attack" are also given). נֶגַע "stroke" (a blow dealt; lit. "touch"); כָּלָה "be spent, come to an end".
- **"When thou with rebukes dost correct man for iniquity, thou makest his beauty to consume away like a moth" → "With reproofs for iniquity you discipline a man, and you melt like a moth what is desirable to him"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּתוֹכָחוֹת עַל־עָוֹן יִסַּרְתָּ אִישׁ וַתֶּמֶס כָּעָשׁ חֲמוּדוֹ. עָוֹן "iniquity" (the former text had "sin"); יִסֵּר "discipline"; וַתֶּמֶס hifil of מָסָה "melt, dissolve" (not "consume"); חֲמוּדוֹ "his desirable thing" (from חָמַד "desire"). The perfect and the wayyiqtol state what is always so: present tense.
- **"give ear unto my cry; hold not thy peace at my tears" → "to my cry for help give ear; at my tears do not be deaf"** (v12) — Hebrew וְשַׁוְעָתִי הַאֲזִינָה אֶל־דִּמְעָתִי אַל־תֶּחֱרַשׁ, Hebrew order; שַׁוְעָה "cry for help"; חָרַשׁ "be deaf" of God not answering (as Ps 28:1; the word also means "be silent").
- **"I am a stranger with thee, and a sojourner" → "a sojourner am I with you, a settler"** (v12) — Hebrew גֵר אָנֹכִי עִמָּךְ תּוֹשָׁב: גֵּר "sojourner", תּוֹשָׁב "settler" (one dwelling on land not his own), as Lev 25:23; "with you" without the dash.
- **"O spare me, that I may recover strength" → "Look away from me, that I may brighten up"** (v13) — Hebrew הָשַׁע מִמֶּנִּי וְאַבְלִיגָה. הָשַׁע read from שָׁעָה "look" ("look away from me"; or from שָׁעַע "smear shut": "shut your eyes from me", cf. Isa 6:10); אַבְלִיגָה "let me brighten up" (as Job 9:27; 10:20). "Spare" and "recover strength" are not the words.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–13 = Heb v2–14.
- **Meditation on mortality**; *hevel* "vapor" three times (v5, 6, 11).
- **"A sojourner with you"** — compare Lev 25:23; 1 Chr 29:15; Heb 11:13.
