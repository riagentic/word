# Psalm 82

A psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ stands in the congregation of ⟦el|El|God⟧;
in the midst of gods he judges:

[2] "How long will you judge unjustly
and lift up the faces of the wicked? Selah.

[3] Judge the poor and the fatherless;
justify the afflicted and the destitute.

[4] Rescue the poor and the needy;
from the hand of the wicked deliver them.

[5] They do not know, and they do not understand;
in darkness they walk about;
all the foundations of the earth are shaken.

[6] I said, 'You are gods,
and sons of ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧, all of you.'

[7] Surely like man you shall die,
and like one of the princes you shall fall."

[8] Arise, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, judge the earth,
for you shall inherit in all the nations!

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 8) — Hebrew *Elohim*. Ps 82 is one of the Elohistic psalms (42–83): the divine names stand as printed.
- **"O God" → no "O"** (v8) — the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"A Psalm of Asaph" → "A psalm of Asaph"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְאָסָף, the first words of v1; לְ "of" (by, for or belonging to: not decided).
- **"God standeth in the congregation of the mighty; he judgeth among the gods" → "Elohim stands in the congregation of El; in the midst of gods he judges"** (v1) — Hebrew נִצָּב "stands" (is stationed, has taken his stand); בַּעֲדַת־אֵל "in the congregation of El" (עֵדָה, the word for the gathered congregation of Israel; token `el`; "of the mighty" and "the divine council" interpret); בְּקֶרֶב אֱלֹהִים "in the midst of gods": the same word אֱלֹהִים as the subject of the line, here of many (בְּקֶרֶב "in the midst of" needs more than one), so plain "gods", not the token for the one God. Who they are (heavenly beings, or men who judge) the psalm does not say. יִשְׁפֹּט "he judges" (imperfect).
- **"How long will ye judge unjustly, and accept the persons of the wicked?" → "How long will you judge unjustly and lift up the faces of the wicked?"** (v2) — Hebrew תִּשְׁפְּטוּ־עָוֶל, literally "judge injustice" ("judge unjustly" is the sense: the literal words would say the reverse in English); פְּנֵי רְשָׁעִים תִּשְׂאוּ "lift up the faces of the wicked" (= show them favor in judgment; נָשָׂא פָּנִים, kept).
- **"Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy" → "Judge the poor and the fatherless; justify the afflicted and the destitute"** (v3) — Hebrew שִׁפְטוּ "judge" (= give a just judgment for); הַצְדִּיקוּ "justify" (declare righteous); דַּל "poor", יָתוֹם "fatherless", עָנִי "afflicted", רָשׁ "destitute" (another word than אֶבְיוֹן "needy", v4).
- **"Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked" → "Rescue the poor and the needy; from the hand of the wicked deliver them"** (v4) — Hebrew פַּלְּטוּ "rescue" (פִּלֵּט), הַצִּילוּ "deliver" (הִצִּיל); "them" is supplied: English needs an object.
- **"They know not, neither will they understand; they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth are out of course" → "They do not know, and they do not understand; in darkness they walk about; all the foundations of the earth are shaken"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא יָדְעוּ וְלֹא יָבִינוּ, a perfect and an imperfect; יִתְהַלָּכוּ "walk about" (hitpael); יִמּוֹטוּ "are shaken" (מוֹט nifal). Who "they" are is not said (the judges of v2, or the poor).
- **"I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the most High" → "I said, 'You are gods, and sons of Elyon, all of you.'"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲנִי־אָמַרְתִּי, with the stressed "I"; אֱלֹהִים אַתֶּם "gods are you", the word of v1; וּבְנֵי עֶלְיוֹן כֻּלְּכֶם "and sons of Elyon, all of you" (not "children"). John 10:34 quotes the first line; the verse is translated from the Hebrew, nothing levelled. The Hebrew has no quotation marks: the quotation is closed at the end of v6, and v7 is read as the answer to it.
- **"But ye shall die like men, and fall like one of the princes" → "Surely like man you shall die, and like one of the princes you shall fall"** (v7) — Hebrew אָכֵן "surely" (also "yet in truth": the word sets v7 against v6); כְּאָדָם "like man" (or "like Adam": no article); כְּאַחַד הַשָּׂרִים "like one of the princes" (not "any prince").
- **"Arise, O God, judge the earth: for thou shalt inherit all nations" → "Arise, Elohim, judge the earth, for you shall inherit in all the nations"** (v8) — Hebrew קוּמָה; אַתָּה "you" stressed; תִנְחַל בְּכָל־הַגּוֹיִם, literally "you shall inherit in all the nations" (= have your inheritance in them, as Num 18:20 נָחַל בְּ).
- **Poetic structure** — divine council scene (v1), the accusation (v2-5), judgment oracle (v6-7), closing plea (v8).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **Who are the *elohim*?** — heavenly beings judged by *Elohim*, or human judges (so John 10:34-35 uses v6); the psalm does not decide.
