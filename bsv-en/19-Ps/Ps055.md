# Psalm 55

To the overseer. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.

[1] Give ear, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to my prayer,
and do not hide yourself from my plea for favor.

[2] Attend to me, and answer me;
I am restless in my complaint, and I am in turmoil,

[3] at the voice of the enemy,
because of the pressure of the wicked;
for they bring down wickedness upon me,
and in anger they bear a grudge against me.

[4] My heart writhes within me,
and the terrors of death have fallen upon me.

[5] Fear and trembling come into me,
and shuddering has covered me.

[6] And I said, "Would that I had a pinion like the dove!
I would fly away and abide.

[7] Behold, I would flee far away;
I would lodge in the wilderness. Selah.

[8] I would hasten to my place of escape
from the rushing wind, from the tempest."

[9] Swallow up, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, divide their tongue;
for I have seen violence and contention in the city.

[10] Day and night they go around it upon its walls,
and wickedness and toil are in its midst;

[11] destruction is in its midst,
and oppression and deceit do not depart from its square.

[12] For it is not an enemy who reproaches me —
then I would bear it;
it is not one who hates me who has made himself great against me —
then I would hide myself from him.

[13] But you — a mortal man of my rank,
my companion and my acquaintance;

[14] we who together made sweet secret counsel;
in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we walked in the throng.

[15] Let death come deceitfully upon them;
let them go down to Sheol alive;
for evils are in their dwelling, within them.

[16] I — to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ I will call,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will save me.

[17] Evening and morning and noon
I will complain and moan,
and he has heard my voice.

[18] He has ransomed my soul in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧
from the battle against me,
for among many they were with me.

[19] ⟦el|El|God⟧ will hear and answer them,
he who sits from of old, Selah,
those who have no changes,
and have not feared ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] He has stretched out his hands against those at peace with him;
he has profaned his covenant.

[21] Smooth were the butterings of his mouth,
and his heart was battle;
his words were softer than oil,
and they were drawn swords.

[22] Cast upon ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your burden, and he will sustain you;
he will never let the righteous be shaken.

[23] And you, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, will bring them down to the well of the pit;
men of bloodguilt and deceit shall not halve their days;
but I will trust in you.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 14, 16, 19, 23) — Hebrew *Elohim*, as printed (the Elohistic psalms).
- **"God" → "El"** (v19) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v16, 22) — Hebrew יהוה, beside אֱלֹהִים in v16: both as printed.
- **"grave/hell" → "Sheol"** (v15) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל, the plain word.
- **"To the chief Musician on Neginoth, Maschil, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ בִּנְגִינֹת מַשְׂכִּיל לְדָוִד, as Ps 54: no word "psalm"; *Maskil* kept (meaning unknown; from הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight"); לְ of לְדָוִד "by / for / belonging to": "of" leaves it open.
- **"Give ear to my prayer, O God; and hide not thyself from my supplication" → "Give ear, Elohim, to my prayer, and do not hide yourself from my plea for favor"** (v1) — Hebrew הַאֲזִינָה אֱלֹהִים תְּפִלָּתִי וְאַל־תִּתְעַלַּם מִתְּחִנָּתִי, in this order; תְּחִנָּה "plea for favor" (noun of חָנַן; the Solomon's prayer row).
- **"I mourn in my complaint, and make a noise" → "I am restless in my complaint, and I am in turmoil"** (v2) — Hebrew אָרִיד בְּשִׂיחִי וְאָהִימָה: אָרִיד from רוּד "roam, be restless"; שִׂיחַ "complaint" (musing poured out; the verb of v17); וְאָהִימָה, cohortative of הוּם "be in tumult" (the root of the הוּם "resound" row: "I must be in turmoil"), kept apart from הָמָה "moan" in v17.
- **"Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked" → "at the voice of the enemy, because of the pressure of the wicked"** (v3) — Hebrew מִקּוֹל אוֹיֵב מִפְּנֵי עָקַת רָשָׁע: two prepositions, מִן "at, from" and מִפְּנֵי "because of" (its row); עָקָה "pressure" (only here).
- **"for they cast iniquity upon me" → "for they bring down wickedness upon me"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־יָמִיטוּ עָלַי אָוֶן: hifil of מוֹט "make totter, let fall" (the "be shaken" of v22); אָוֶן "wickedness" (its row; also v10).
- **"in wrath they hate me" → "in anger they bear a grudge against me"** (v3) — Hebrew וּבְאַף יִשְׂטְמוּנִי: אַף "anger" (lit. "nose"); שָׂטַם "bear a grudge against" (its row).
- **"My heart is sore pained within me" → "My heart writhes within me"** (v4) — Hebrew לִבִּי יָחִיל בְּקִרְבִּי, חִיל "writhe" (as of a woman in labor).
- **"are fallen upon me" → "have fallen upon me"** (v4) — Hebrew נָפְלוּ, perfect.
- **"Fearfulness and trembling are come upon me, and horror hath overwhelmed me" → "Fear and trembling come into me, and shuddering has covered me"** (v5) — Hebrew יִרְאָה וָרַעַד יָבֹא בִי וַתְּכַסֵּנִי פַּלָּצוּת: בִּי "into me"; כִּסָּה "cover" (its row); פַּלָּצוּת "shuddering".
- **"And I said, Oh that I had wings like a dove! for then would I fly away, and be at rest" → "And I said, 'Would that I had a pinion like the dove! I would fly away and abide'"** (v6) — Hebrew וָאֹמַר מִי־יִתֶּן־לִּי אֵבֶר כַּיּוֹנָה אָעוּפָה וְאֶשְׁכֹּנָה: וָאֹמַר narrative "and I said"; מִי־יִתֵּן "who will give" = "would that"; אֵבֶר "pinion" (the wing's flight feathers, singular); כַּיּוֹנָה "like the dove"; שָׁכַן "abide" (its row; not "be at rest"); the cohortatives "I would fly … abide", no "for then".
- **"Lo, then would I wander far off" → "Behold, I would flee far away"** (v7) — Hebrew הִנֵּה אַרְחִיק נְדֹד, "behold, I would make far to flee" (נָדַד "flee, wander").
- **"and remain in the wilderness" → "I would lodge in the wilderness"** (v7) — Hebrew אָלִין בַּמִּדְבָּר; לִין "lodge" (spend the night), no "and".
- **"I would hasten my escape from the windy storm and tempest" → "I would hasten to my place of escape from the rushing wind, from the tempest"** (v8) — Hebrew אָחִישָׁה מִפְלָט לִי מֵרוּחַ סֹעָה מִסָּעַר: "I would hasten a place of escape for myself" ("to" supplied); מִפְלָט "place of escape" (of the פִּלֵּט "rescue" row); סֹעָה "rushing" (only here); "from" twice.
- **"Destroy, O Lord, and divide their tongues" → "Swallow up, Adonai, divide their tongue"** (v9) — Hebrew בַּלַּע אֲדֹנָי פַּלַּג לְשׁוֹנָם: בִּלַּע "swallow up" (the root of בֶּלַע, 52:4); no "and"; פִּלַּג "divide" (the word of Gen 10:25); לְשׁוֹנָם singular "their tongue". אֲדֹנָי "Adonai".
- **"for I have seen violence and strife in the city" → "for I have seen violence and contention in the city"** (v9) — Hebrew חָמָס וְרִיב; רִיב "contention" (its row).
- **"mischief also and sorrow are in the midst of it" → "and wickedness and toil are in its midst"** (v10) — Hebrew וְאָוֶן וְעָמָל בְּקִרְבָּהּ; אָוֶן "wickedness", עָמָל "toil" (their row).
- **"Wickedness is in the midst thereof: deceit and guile depart not from her streets" → "destruction is in its midst, and oppression and deceit do not depart from its square"** (v11) — Hebrew הַוּוֹת בְּקִרְבָּהּ וְלֹא־יָמִישׁ מֵרְחֹבָהּ תֹּךְ וּמִרְמָה: הַוּוֹת "destruction" (as 52:2, 7); תֹּךְ "oppression"; מִרְמָה "deceit"; רְחוֹב "open square" (singular).
- **"For it was not an enemy that reproached me; then I could have borne it: neither was it he that hated me that did magnify himself against me; then I would have hid myself from him" → "For it is not an enemy who reproaches me — then I would bear it; it is not one who hates me who has made himself great against me — then I would hide myself from him"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי לֹא־אוֹיֵב יְחָרְפֵנִי וְאֶשָּׂא לֹא־מְשַׂנְאִי עָלַי הִגְדִּיל וְאֶסָּתֵר מִמֶּנּוּ: חֵרֵף "reproach" (its row); מְשַׂנֵּא "one who hates" (the שָׂנֵא row; not "adversary"); הִגְדִּיל עַל "make oneself great against"; וְ + imperfect "then I would …" ("it" supplied after "bear").
- **"But it was thou, a man mine equal, my guide, and mine acquaintance" → "But you — a mortal man of my rank, my companion and my acquaintance"** (v13) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה אֱנוֹשׁ כְּעֶרְכִּי אַלּוּפִי וּמְיֻדָּעִי: no verb ("it was" supplied in KJV); אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man" (standing decision); כְּעֶרְכִּי "according to my valuation" (עֵרֶךְ, the Lev "valuation" row: "of my rank" = my equal); אַלּוּף here "companion" (as Prov 2:17; 16:28; Mic 7:5), not the "chief" of its row (another sense of the word); מְיֻדָּע "one known, acquaintance".
- **"We took sweet counsel together, and walked unto the house of God in company" → "we who together made sweet secret counsel; in the house of Elohim we walked in the throng"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יַחְדָּו נַמְתִּיק סוֹד בְּבֵית אֱלֹהִים נְהַלֵּךְ בְּרָגֶשׁ: אֲשֶׁר "who" with first-person verbs ("we who"); the imperfects of what was done again and again; סוֹד "secret counsel" (its row); בְּבֵית "in the house" (not "unto"); רֶגֶשׁ "throng" (uncertain; only here).
- **"Let death seize upon them" → "Let death come deceitfully upon them"** (v15) — qere יַשִּׁי מָוֶת עָלֵימוֹ, "let death deceive (come by surprise) upon them", from הִשִּׁיא "deceive"; the written form ישימות reads "desolations (be) upon them".
- **"let them go down quick into hell" → "let them go down to Sheol alive"** (v15) — Hebrew יֵרְדוּ שְׁאוֹל חַיִּים, as Num 16:30, 33.
- **"for wickedness is in their dwellings, and among them" → "for evils are in their dwelling, within them"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי־רָעוֹת בִּמְגוּרָם בְּקִרְבָּם: רָעוֹת plural "evils"; מָגוֹר "dwelling" (the place of sojourning, from גּוּר); בְּקִרְבָּם "within them", no "and".
- **"As for me, I will call upon God" → "I — to Elohim I will call"** (v16) — Hebrew אֲנִי אֶל־אֱלֹהִים אֶקְרָא, the pronoun first, then the name.
- **"Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he shall hear my voice" → "Evening and morning and noon I will complain and moan, and he has heard my voice"** (v17) — Hebrew עֶרֶב וָבֹקֶר וְצָהֳרַיִם אָשִׂיחָה וְאֶהֱמֶה וַיִּשְׁמַע קוֹלִי: שִׂיחַ "complain" (the noun of v2); הָמָה "moan"; וַיִּשְׁמַע, the narrative form, "and he has heard" (the hearing told as done); KJV "shall hear" levels it.
- **"He hath delivered my soul in peace from the battle that was against me: for there were many with me" → "He has ransomed my soul in shalom from the battle against me, for among many they were with me"** (v18) — Hebrew פָּדָה בְשָׁלוֹם נַפְשִׁי מִקֲּרָב־לִי כִּי־בְרַבִּים הָיוּ עִמָּדִי: פָּדָה "ransom" (its row); token `shalom`; קְרָב "battle" (also v21); the last line word for word: "with me" (עִמָּדִי) may mean "beside me" (many were on my side) or "against me" (many fought with me); בְּרַבִּים "among many" or "as many". Left open.
- **"God shall hear, and afflict them, even he that abideth of old" → "El will hear and answer them, he who sits from of old"** (v19) — Hebrew יִשְׁמַע אֵל וְיַעֲנֵם וְיֹשֵׁב קֶדֶם: וְיַעֲנֵם is pointed as עָנָה "answer" (the KJV "afflict" is another verb of the same letters); יָשַׁב "sit" (of one enthroned; no "throne" in the Hebrew); קֶדֶם "of old" (lit. "before, east").
- **"Because they have no changes, therefore they fear not God" → "those who have no changes, and have not feared Elohim"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר אֵין חֲלִיפוֹת לָמוֹ וְלֹא יָרְאוּ אֱלֹהִים: אֲשֶׁר "who" (not "because"); חֲלִיפוֹת "changes" (its row; whether a change of mind or of fortune, the word does not say); וְ "and", no "therefore"; perfect "have not feared".
- **"He hath put forth his hands against such as be at peace with him" → "He has stretched out his hands against those at peace with him"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁלַח יָדָיו בִּשְׁלֹמָיו: שָׁלַח יָד "stretch out the hand" (its row); שְׁלֹמִים "those at peace (with him)", an adjective of the root of שָׁלוֹם (no token). The subject is not named ("My companion" is added).
- **"he hath broken his covenant" → "he has profaned his covenant"** (v20) — Hebrew חִלֵּל בְּרִיתוֹ; חִלֵּל "profane" (its row).
- **"The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart" → "Smooth were the butterings of his mouth, and his heart was battle"** (v21) — Hebrew חָלְקוּ מַחְמָאֹת פִּיו וּקֲרָב־לִבּוֹ: מַחְמָאֹת as pointed is one noun, "butterings" (from חֶמְאָה "butter"); read מֵחֶמְאֹת it is "than butter" (KJV), one vowel apart; "words" is not written. קְרָב "battle", as v18; "his heart (was) battle", no "in".
- **"yet were they drawn swords" → "and they were drawn swords"** (v21) — Hebrew וְהֵמָּה פְתִחוֹת, "and they were drawn ones" (פְּתִיחָה, only here, from פָּתַח "open"): "swords" is not written but is the sense.
- **"Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and he shall sustain thee" → "Cast upon Yahweh your burden, and he will sustain you"** (v22) — Hebrew הַשְׁלֵךְ עַל־יְהוָה יְהָבְךָ וְהוּא יְכַלְכְּלֶךָ: יְהָב "burden" (only here; uncertain: "what is given, one's lot"); כִּלְכֵּל "sustain" (its row). 1 Pet 5:7 has its own Greek wording.
- **"he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved" → "he will never let the righteous be shaken"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹא־יִתֵּן לְעוֹלָם מוֹט לַצַּדִּיק, "he will not give forever a shaking to the righteous"; מוֹט "be shaken" (the Ps 13–16 row).
- **"But thou, O God, shalt bring them down into the pit of destruction" → "And you, Elohim, will bring them down to the well of the pit"** (v23) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה אֱלֹהִים תּוֹרִדֵם לִבְאֵר שַׁחַת: בְּאֵר "well" (its row); שַׁחַת "pit" (also read "corruption", from שָׁחַת).
- **"bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days" → "men of bloodguilt and deceit shall not halve their days"** (v23) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי דָמִים וּמִרְמָה לֹא־יֶחֱצוּ יְמֵיהֶם: דָּמִים "bloodguilt" (lit. "bloods", its row); חָצָה "halve" (they will not reach half).
- **"but I will trust in thee" → "but I will trust in you"** (v23) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי אֶבְטַח־בָּךְ.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is Heb v1: our v1 = Heb v2, v23 = Heb v24.
- **The friend's betrayal** (v12–14, 20–21) — often linked with Ahithophel (2 Sam 15–17); the psalm names no one.
