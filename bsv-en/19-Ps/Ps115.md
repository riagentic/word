# Psalm 115

[1] Not to us, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, not to us,
but to your name give glory,
on account of your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, on account of your truth!

[2] Why should the nations say,
"Where, now, is their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[3] And our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in the heavens;
all that he delights in he has done.

[4] Their idols are silver and gold,
the work of the hands of man.

[5] A mouth they have, and they do not speak;
eyes they have, and they do not see.

[6] Ears they have, and they do not hear;
a nose they have, and they do not smell.

[7] Their hands, and they do not feel;
their feet, and they do not walk;
they do not make a sound in their throat.

[8] Like them shall be those who make them,
everyone who trusts in them.

[9] ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
Their helper and their shield is he.

[10] House of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
Their helper and their shield is he.

[11] You who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
Their helper and their shield is he.

[12] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has remembered us; he will bless;
he will bless the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
he will bless the house of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧;

[13] he will bless those who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the small with the great.

[14] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ add to you,
to you and to your sons!

[15] Blessed are you of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the maker of heavens and earth.

[16] The heavens are ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧ heavens,
and the earth he has given to the sons of man.

[17] The dead do not praise ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧,
nor any who go down into silence.

[18] But we will bless ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧
from now and forever.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yah"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew יָהּ, the short form of the name; kept. In v17 יְהַלְלוּ־יָהּ is the verb "they praise" with Yah, not the cry Halleluyah of v18.
- **"Not unto us, O LORD … for thy mercy, and for thy truth's sake" → "Not to us, Yahweh … on account of your chesed, on account of your truth"** (v1) — no "O"; עַל־חַסְדְּךָ עַל־אֲמִתֶּךָ, two phrases with עַל "on account of", no "and"; חֶסֶד *chesed*; אֱמֶת "truth" (not "faithfulness": that is אֱמוּנָה).
- **"Where is now their God?" → "Where, now, is their Elohim?"** (v2) — Hebrew אַיֵּה־נָא, the particle נָא kept ("now").
- **"But our God is in the heavens: he hath done whatsoever he hath pleased" → "And our Elohim is in the heavens; all that he delights in he has done"** (v3) — Hebrew וֵאלֹהֵינוּ "and our Elohim"; כֹּל אֲשֶׁר־חָפֵץ עָשָׂה, in the Hebrew order; חָפֵץ "delight in" (row), עָשָׂה a perfect, "has done".
- **"the work of men's hands" → "the work of the hands of man"** (v4) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה יְדֵי אָדָם. Ps 135:15 has the same line after עֲצַבֵּי הַגּוֹיִם "the idols of the nations".
- **"They have mouths, but they speak not" → "A mouth they have, and they do not speak"** (v5–6) — Hebrew פֶּה־לָהֶם וְלֹא יְדַבֵּרוּ, "a mouth (is) to them, and they do not speak": פֶּה and אַף (v6) are singular, the organ stands first, וְ "and". הֵרִיחַ "smell" (row).
- **"They have hands, but they handle not" → "Their hands, and they do not feel"** (v7) — Hebrew יְדֵיהֶם וְלֹא יְמִישׁוּן רַגְלֵיהֶם וְלֹא יְהַלֵּכוּ: here no לָהֶם "to them", only "their hands … their feet", each set before the verb ("as for their hands, they do not feel"). לֹא־יֶהְגּוּ בִּגְרוֹנָם: הָגָה, the verb of Ps 1:2 ("softly recite"), here of a low sound ("make a sound": no words, not even a murmur).
- **"They that make them are like unto them; so is every one that trusteth in them" → "Like them shall be those who make them, everyone who trusts in them"** (v8) — Hebrew כְּמוֹהֶם יִהְיוּ עֹשֵׂיהֶם, an imperfect, "shall be" (it may also be a wish: "may … be"); כֹּל אֲשֶׁר־בֹּטֵחַ בָּהֶם, singular, "everyone who trusts"; "so is" is not written. Ps 135:18 has the same words.
- **"O Israel, trust thou in the LORD: he is their help and their shield" → "Israel, trust in Yahweh! Their helper and their shield is he"** (v9–11) — no "O"; MT בְּטַח, an imperative (the Greek reads a past "hoped", from memory; not followed). עֶזְרָם וּמָגִנָּם הוּא in the Hebrew order (as Ps 33:20); עֵזֶר "helper" (row). "their" stands in the Hebrew though the call is "you". יִרְאֵי יְהוָה (v11, 13) "those who fear Yahweh": "You" is needed for the English call.
- **"he will bless us" → "he will bless"** (v12) — Hebrew יְבָרֵךְ, no object (the "us" of זְכָרָנוּ is not repeated). The three יְבָרֵךְ are imperfects, "will bless" (also v13; a wish "may he bless" is possible).
- **"both small and great" → "the small with the great"** (v13) — Hebrew הַקְּטַנִּים עִם־הַגְּדֹלִים, "with".
- **"The LORD shall increase you more and more, you and your children" → "May Yahweh add to you, to you and to your sons"** (v14) — Hebrew יֹסֵף יְהוָה עֲלֵיכֶם, "add upon you"; יֹסֵף may be a wish or a statement ("Yahweh will add"): the wish is given. בָּנִים "sons" (not "children").
- **"Ye are blessed of the LORD which made heaven and earth" → "Blessed are you of Yahweh, the maker of heavens and earth"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּרוּכִים אַתֶּם לַיהוָה: no verb, "are" (as the בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה row), not "be"; לְ "of" (the one who blesses, as Gen 14:19). עֹשֵׂה, a participle, "the maker of"; שָׁמַיִם is plural, "heavens".
- **"The heaven, even the heavens, are the LORD's: but the earth hath he given to the children of men" → "The heavens are Yahweh's heavens, and the earth he has given to the sons of man"** (v16) — Hebrew הַשָּׁמַיִם שָׁמַיִם לַיהוָה, "the heavens are heavens to Yahweh" (they belong to him); וְ "and"; בְּנֵי־אָדָם "sons of man" (row).
- **"neither any that go down into silence" → "nor any who go down into silence"** (v17) — Hebrew דּוּמָה "silence (of the dead)" (row; as Ps 94:17).
- **"from this time forth and for evermore" → "from now and forever"** (v18) — Hebrew מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם, as Ps 113:2. וַאֲנַחְנוּ "But we": the pronoun set against "the dead".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours. The Greek joins Ps 115 to Ps 114 (from memory).
- **Idol polemic** (v4–8) — the lifeless images set against "our Elohim in the heavens" (v3); v4–8 = Ps 135:15–18, which has: 135:15 עֲצַבֵּי הַגּוֹיִם "the idols of the nations"; 135:16 = 115:5; 135:17 יַאֲזִינוּ "give ear" and, for the nose, אַף אֵין־יֶשׁ־רוּחַ בְּפִיהֶם "also there is no breath in their mouth"; 115:7 has no parallel there; 135:18 = 115:8.
