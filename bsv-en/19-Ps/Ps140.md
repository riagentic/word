# Psalm 140

To the overseer. A psalm of David.

[1] Free me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, from evil man;
from the man of violence guard me,

[2] who have devised evils in the heart;
every day they stir up wars.

[3] They have sharpened their tongue like a serpent;
the venom of an asp is under their lips. Selah.

[4] Keep me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, from the hands of the wicked;
from the man of violence guard me,
who have devised to thrust down my steps.

[5] The proud have hidden a trap for me, and cords;
they have spread a net beside the course;
snares they have set for me. Selah.

[6] I have said to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "My ⟦el|El|God⟧ are you."
Give ear, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, to the voice of my pleas for favor.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|GOD⟧, ⟦adonai|Adonai|my Lord⟧, the strength of my salvation,
you have screened my head in the day of weapons.

[8] Do not give, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, the desires of the wicked;
his plot do not further — they would be exalted. Selah.

[9] The head of those who surround me —
let the toil of their lips cover them.

[10] Let coals be shaken down upon them;
into the fire let him make them fall,
into deep pits; they shall not rise.

[11] A man of tongue shall not be established in the land;
a man of violence, evil — it will hunt him to downfall.

[12] I know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will execute the cause of the afflicted,
the judgment of the needy.

[13] Surely the righteous will give thanks to your name;
the upright will dwell before your face.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 6, 8, 12) — Tetragrammaton. **"GOD the Lord" → "Yahweh, Adonai"** (v7) — Hebrew יְהֹוִה אֲדֹנָי, in this order (the name pointed to be read *Elohim*), both words. **"God" → "El"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵלִי "my El". No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד, verse 1 of the Hebrew. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Deliver me, O LORD, from the evil man: preserve me from the violent man" → "Free me, Yahweh, from evil man; from the man of violence guard me"** (v1) — Hebrew חַלְּצֵנִי, piel of חָלַץ "free"; אָדָם רָע "evil man" (אָדָם, man as mankind, singular, without the article); אִישׁ חֲמָסִים, literally "a man of violences" (plural, also v4; חָמָס "violence"); תִּנְצְרֵנִי, נָצַר "guard", a future form beside the imperative, taken as a plea (also v4).
- **"Which imagine mischiefs in their heart; continually are they gathered together for war" → "who have devised evils in the heart; every day they stir up wars"** (v2) — Hebrew חָשְׁבוּ "they have devised"; רָעוֹת "evils"; בְּלֵב "in the heart", no "their"; כָּל־יוֹם "every day"; יָגוּרוּ מִלְחָמוֹת "they stir up wars" (גּוּר, as Ps 56:6; 59:3; the sense "gather" is also given, KJV "are gathered together").
- **"They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent; adders' poison is under their lips" → "They have sharpened their tongue like a serpent; the venom of an asp is under their lips"** (v3) — Hebrew לְשׁוֹנָם "their tongue", singular; כְּמוֹ־נָחָשׁ "like a serpent"; חֲמַת "venom" (as Ps 58:4); עַכְשׁוּב, found only here, "asp" (the kind of snake is not known); singular. Rom 3:13 quotes this line in the Greek form ἰὸς ἀσπίδων ὑπὸ τὰ χείλη αὐτῶν, "venom of asps", plural: the Hebrew is kept as printed.
- **"Keep me, O LORD, from the hands of the wicked … who have purposed to overthrow my goings" → "Keep me, Yahweh, from the hands of the wicked … who have devised to thrust down my steps"** (v4) — Hebrew שָׁמְרֵנִי, שָׁמַר "keep", beside נָצַר "guard" in the same verse; רָשָׁע singular, "the wicked"; חָשְׁבוּ "have devised" (as v2); לִדְחוֹת, דָּחָה "thrust, push down"; פְּעָמָי, פַּעַם "foot", here of walking: "my steps".
- **"The proud have hid a snare for me, and cords; they have spread a net by the wayside; they have set gins for me" → "The proud have hidden a trap for me, and cords; they have spread a net beside the course; snares they have set for me"** (v5) — Hebrew טָמְנוּ, טָמַן "hide"; גֵּאִים "the proud"; פַּח "trap", מוֹקְשִׁים "snares" (two words, as Josh 23:13); וַחֲבָלִים "and cords", joined by the accents either to "hidden" (as here) or to "spread" ("and with cords they have spread a net"); לְיַד־מַעְגָּל, literally "at the hand of the path": beside it (מַעְגָּל "path", as Ps 23:3). The line breaks follow the Hebrew accents: "beside the path" ends the second line.
- **"I said unto the LORD, Thou art my God: hear the voice of my supplications, O LORD" → "I have said to Yahweh, "My El are you." Give ear, Yahweh, to the voice of my pleas for favor"** (v6) — Hebrew אָמַרְתִּי "I have said"; אֵלִי אָתָּה, "my El are you", in the Hebrew order; הַאֲזִינָה "give ear"; תַּחֲנוּנָי "my pleas for favor" (from חָנַן, as Ps 86:6).
- **"thou hast covered my head in the day of battle" → "you have screened my head in the day of weapons"** (v7) — Hebrew עֹז יְשׁוּעָתִי "the strength of my salvation"; סַכֹּתָה לְרֹאשִׁי, סָכַךְ "screen" (as Exod 40:3), literally "screened for my head"; בְּיוֹם נָשֶׁק "in the day of weapons", not "battle".
- **"Grant not, O LORD, the desires of the wicked: further not his wicked device; lest they exalt themselves" → "Do not give, Yahweh, the desires of the wicked; his plot do not further — they would be exalted"** (v8) — Hebrew אַל־תִּתֵּן "do not give"; מַאֲוַיֵּי, found only here, "desires" (root of אִוָּה "desire"); רָשָׁע singular; זְמָמוֹ "his plot" (from זָמַם "devise"), no "wicked"; אַל־תָּפֵק, hifil of פּוּק "bring out, further"; יָרוּמוּ "they would be exalted" (or "will be"): no "lest" in the Hebrew. Some join יָרוּמוּ to v9 ("those who surround me raise the head"), against the verse division.
- **"As for the head of those that compass me about, let the mischief of their own lips cover them" → "The head of those who surround me — let the toil of their lips cover them"** (v9) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ מְסִבָּי, no "as for"; עֲמַל "toil" (עָמָל; KJV "mischief"); no "own"; qere יְכַסֵּמוֹ "let it cover them" (ketiv יכסומו, a plural form: "let them cover them"; the same sense).
- **"Let burning coals fall upon them: let them be cast into the fire; into deep pits, that they rise not up again" → "Let coals be shaken down upon them; into the fire let him make them fall, into deep pits; they shall not rise"** (v10) — qere יִמּוֹטוּ, nifal of מוֹט "be shaken, totter": the coals are shaken loose ("down" supplied for the English); ketiv ימיטו, "let them make (coals) totter down". גֶּחָלִים "coals", no "burning"; יַפִּלֵם, "let him make them fall", active, the subject not named (not "let them be cast"); מַהֲמֹרוֹת, found only here, "deep pits" (uncertain: also "floods"); בַּל־יָקוּמוּ "they shall not rise", no "again".
- **"Let not an evil speaker be established in the earth: evil shall hunt the violent man to overthrow him" → "A man of tongue shall not be established in the land; a man of violence, evil — it will hunt him to downfall"** (v11) — Hebrew אִישׁ לָשׁוֹן, literally "a man of tongue" (one who slanders); בַּל־יִכּוֹן "shall not be established"; אֶרֶץ "land". אִישׁ־חָמָס רָע: the accents set רָע "evil" with the first half (the main pause falls on it), so "an evil man of violence"; then יְצוּדֶנּוּ "it (or one) will hunt him", the subject not named; most join רָע to the verb: "evil will hunt the man of violence". לְמַדְחֵפֹת, found only here, plural, from דָּחַף "thrust, drive": "to downfall" (literally "to thrusts").
- **"I know that the LORD will maintain the cause of the afflicted, and the right of the poor" → "I know that Yahweh will execute the cause of the afflicted, the judgment of the needy"** (v12) — qere יָדַעְתִּי "I know" (ketiv ידעת "you know"); יַעֲשֶׂה "will do", "execute" (as "execute judgment"); דִּין "cause"; עָנִי "afflicted"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; אֶבְיוֹנִים "needy"; no "and".
- **"the upright shall dwell in thy presence" → "the upright will dwell before your face"** (v13) — Hebrew אַךְ "surely"; יוֹדוּ "will give thanks"; יֵשְׁבוּ "will dwell"; אֶת־פָּנֶיךָ, literally "with your face": before it.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–13 = Heb v2–14.
- **v3 and the New Testament** — Rom 3:13 cites the line among the verses on all who are under sin.
