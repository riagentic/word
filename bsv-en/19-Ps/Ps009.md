# Psalm 9

To the overseer, upon ⟦muth-labben|Mut-labben|Muth-labben⟧. A psalm of David.

[1] I will give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with all my heart;
I will recount all your wonderful deeds.

[2] I will rejoice and be jubilant in you;
I will make music to your name, ⟦elyon|Elyon|Most High⟧.

[3] When my enemies turn back,
they stumble and perish from your face.

[4] For you have executed my judgment and my cause;
you have sat on the throne, a judge of righteousness.

[5] You have rebuked nations, you have done away with the wicked;
their name you have blotted out forever and ever.

[6] The enemy — they have come to an end, ruins forever;
and cities you have plucked up;
their remembrance has perished, theirs.

[7] But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sits forever;
he has established his throne for judgment.

[8] And he judges the world in righteousness;
he judges folks in uprightness.

[9] And let ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be a secure height for the crushed,
a secure height for times in distress.

[10] And those who know your name will trust in you,
for you have not forsaken those who seek you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[11] Make music to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who dwells in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
tell among the peoples his deeds.

[12] For he who requires blood has remembered them;
he has not forgotten the cry of the humble.

[13] Show me favor, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
see my affliction from those who hate me,
you who raise me high from the gates of death,

[14] so that I may recount all your praises;
in the gates of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
I will be glad in your salvation.

[15] Nations have sunk in the pit that they made;
in the net that they hid their foot is captured.

[16] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is known: he has executed judgment;
in the work of his palms he snares the wicked. ⟦higgaion|Higgayon|Higgaion⟧. Selah.

[17] The wicked shall return to Sheol,
all nations who forget ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[18] For not forever shall the needy be forgotten,
nor the hope of the afflicted perish perpetually.

[19] Arise, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧; let not mortal man be strong;
let nations be judged before your face.

[20] Set a fear for them, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
let nations know they are mortal man. Selah.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים. **"O thou most High" → "Elyon"** (v2) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן.
- **"To the chief Musician upon Muthlabben, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer, upon Mut-labben. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ, participle of נִצַּח "oversee" (1 Chr 15:21; traditionally "the chief musician"). עַלְמוּת לַבֵּן: the printed text writes עַלְמוּת as one word; "upon Mut-labben" reads it as עַל־מוּת "upon the death of the son"; others read עֲלָמוֹת "Alamoth" (Ps 46 heading) — from memory. The meaning is unknown, perhaps a tune. לְדָוִד "of David": לְ can say "by", "for" or "belonging to"; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"I will praise thee … with my whole heart; I will shew forth all thy marvellous works" → "I will give thanks to Yahweh with all my heart; I will recount all your wonderful deeds"** (v1) — Hebrew אוֹדֶה "give thanks"; בְּכָל־לִבִּי "with all my heart"; אֲסַפְּרָה "recount" (also v14); נִפְלְאוֹת "wonderful deeds". אֲסַפְּרָה, אֶשְׂמְחָה, אֶעֶלְצָה, אֲזַמְּרָה, אָגִילָה (v1–2, 14) are cohortatives: "I will" of resolve ("let me").
- **"I will be glad and rejoice in thee: I will sing praise to thy name" → "I will rejoice and be jubilant in you; I will make music to your name"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׂמַח "rejoice", עָלַץ "be jubilant", זִמֵּר "make music" (also v11).
- **"they shall fall and perish at thy presence" → "they stumble and perish from your face"** (v3) — Hebrew יִכָּשְׁלוּ "stumble"; מִפָּנֶיךָ "from your face" (before you, at the sight of you).
- **"thou hast maintained my right and my cause; thou satest in the throne judging right" → "you have executed my judgment and my cause; you have sat on the throne, a judge of righteousness"** (v4) — Hebrew עָשִׂיתָ מִשְׁפָּטִי וְדִינִי, lit. "you have done my judgment and my cause" (as מִשְׁפָּט עָשָׂה v16); שׁוֹפֵט צֶדֶק "a judge of righteousness" (= a righteous judge, or "judging righteously").
- **"the heathen … destroyed … put out their name" → "nations … done away with … blotted out"** (v5) — Hebrew גּוֹיִם without the article (also v15, 19, 20); אִבַּדְתָּ, piel of אָבַד "perish": "do away with"; רָשָׁע singular "the wicked (one)"; מָחָה "blot out".
- **"O thou enemy, destructions are come to a perpetual end: and thou hast destroyed cities; their memorial is perished with them" → "The enemy — they have come to an end, ruins forever; and cities you have plucked up; their remembrance has perished, theirs"** (v6) — Hebrew הָאוֹיֵב תַּמּוּ חֳרָבוֹת לָנֶצַח: "the enemy" with a plural verb ("they have come to an end"), then "ruins forever"; the line is hard and the link of its words uncertain (KJV takes "the enemy" as an address and "ruins" as the subject). נָתַשׁ "pluck up"; זִכְרָם הֵמָּה "their remembrance, they": the pronoun stresses "their" ("the very remembrance of them"); "with them" is not written.
- **"But the LORD shall endure for ever: he hath prepared his throne" → "But Yahweh sits forever; he has established his throne"** (v7) — Hebrew יֵשֵׁב "sits" (on the throne, as v4 יָשַׁבְתָּ "you have sat"; v11 יֹשֵׁב צִיּוֹן "who dwells in Zion": one verb); כּוֹנֵן "establish".
- **"he shall minister judgment to the people in uprightness" → "he judges folks in uprightness"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁפַט and then דִּין, two verbs with one English word "judge"; לְאֻמִּים "folks" (עַם is "people"); מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness" (root of יָשָׁר "upright"); תֵּבֵל "the world".
- **"The LORD also will be a refuge for the oppressed, a refuge in times of trouble" → "And let Yahweh be a secure height for the crushed, a secure height for times in distress"** (v9) — Hebrew וִיהִי is the short form (jussive), a wish; the plain future "will be" in Changes. מִשְׂגָּב "secure height" (a high place out of reach); דַּךְ "crushed"; לְעִתּוֹת בַּצָּרָה "for times in distress" (as 10:1).
- **"will put their trust in thee" → "will trust in you"** (v10) — Hebrew וְיִבְטְחוּ, after the wish of v9 (or "and let … trust"). דָּרַשׁ "seek".
- **"declare among the people his doings" → "tell among the peoples his deeds"** (v11) — Hebrew הִגִּיד "tell"; עַמִּים plural; עֲלִילוֹת "deeds" (as Ps 105:1).
- **"When he maketh inquisition for blood, he remembereth them: he forgetteth not the cry of the humble" → "For he who requires blood has remembered them; he has not forgotten the cry of the humble"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; דֹרֵשׁ דָּמִים "he who requires bloods" (דָּרַשׁ "require" of blood, Gen 9:5; plural "bloods" = blood shed); perfects. Humble: the qere עֲנָוִים "humble"; the written text (ketiv) has עניים "afflicted" (the reverse in v18).
- **"Have mercy upon me … consider my trouble which I suffer of them that hate me" → "Show me favor … see my affliction from those who hate me"** (v13) — Hebrew חָנְנֵנִי, of חָנַן "show favor"; רְאֵה "see"; עָנְיִי "my affliction"; מְרוֹמְמִי "you who raise me high"; "death" without a capital (no name is meant).
- **"That I may shew forth all thy praise in the gates of the daughter of Zion: I will rejoice in thy salvation" → "so that I may recount all your praises; in the gates … I will be glad in your salvation"** (v14) — Hebrew לְמַעַן "so that"; תְּהִלֹּתֶיךָ plural "praises"; the accents set "in the gates of the daughter of Zion" with the second half; אָגִילָה, of גִּיל "be glad".
- **"The heathen are sunk down … is their own foot taken" → "Nations have sunk … their foot is captured"** (v15) — Hebrew טָבַע "sink"; טָמַן "hide"; נִלְכְּדָה, of לָכַד "capture"; "own" is not written.
- **"The LORD is known by the judgment which he executeth: the wicked is snared in the work of his own hands" → "Yahweh is known: he has executed judgment; in the work of his palms he snares the wicked"** (v16) — Hebrew נוֹדַע יְהוָה מִשְׁפָּט עָשָׂה, two clauses ("by" is not written). נוֹקֵשׁ is pointed as the active participle "snaring": Yahweh snares the wicked in the work of the wicked's own palms (כַּפָּיו "his palms"). Many read נוֹקַשׁ "is snared" (KJV) — that is a change of the pointing; the printed pointing is kept. הִגָּיוֹן "Higgaion" (perhaps a soft sound, of the root of הָגָה; meaning unknown).
- **"shall be turned into hell" → "shall return to Sheol"** (v17) — Hebrew יָשׁוּבוּ "return"; לִשְׁאוֹלָה "to Sheol"; רְשָׁעִים without the article; "and" before "all nations" is not written.
- **"For the needy shall not alway be forgotten: the expectation of the poor shall not perish for ever" → "For not forever shall the needy be forgotten, nor the hope of the afflicted perish perpetually"** (v18) — Hebrew לֹא stands once, at the head of the verse, and rules both lines; "nor" carries it into the second. לָנֶצַח "forever"; לָעַד "perpetually" (another word, עַד "perpetuity"). אֶבְיוֹן "needy"; תִּקְוָה "hope"; afflicted: the qere עֲנִיִּים "afflicted"; the ketiv has ענוים "humble".
- **"let not man prevail: let the heathen be judged in thy sight" → "let not mortal man be strong; let nations be judged before your face"** (v19) — Hebrew עָזַז "be strong"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man" (also v20); עַל־פָּנֶיךָ "before your face".
- **"Put them in fear … that the nations may know themselves to be but men" → "Set a fear for them … let nations know they are mortal man"** (v20) — Hebrew שִׁיתָה … מוֹרָה לָהֶם: מוֹרָה is spelled with ה; it is taken as מוֹרָא "fear" (Gen 9:2; Deut 4:34). Spelled so, the word is elsewhere "razor" (Judg 13:5); "a teacher" (מוֹרֶה) is also proposed. Meaning not certain. "but" is not written: אֱנוֹשׁ הֵמָּה "mortal man they are".

### Notes
- **The Hebrew numbering** — the Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–20 = Heb v2–21. Our verse numbers are kept.
- **One acrostic in two psalms** — Ps 9 and 10 run through the Hebrew alphabet, mostly one letter to every second verse: א (9:1), ב (9:3), ג (9:5), ה (9:6), ו (9:7, 9), ז (9:11), ח (9:13), ט (9:15), י (9:17), כ (9:18), ל (10:1); ד is missing, מ to צ do not appear in 10:2–11, and ק (10:12), ר (10:14), ש (10:15), ת (10:17) close it. Ps 10 has no heading. The Greek version counts 9 and 10 as one psalm (from memory). The letters are not imitated in the translation.
