# Psalm 117

[1] Praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all nations;
laud him, all the tribes!

[2] For his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ has prevailed over us,
and the truth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is forever.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"praise him, all ye people" → "laud him, all the tribes"** (v1) — Hebrew שַׁבְּחוּהוּ כָּל־הָאֻמִּים: שִׁבַּח "laud", another verb than הִלֵּל "praise" in the first line (the Ps 61–65 row gives "praise" to both; here they stand together and are kept apart). אֻמָּה "tribe" (row, which names this verse); with the article, "all the tribes"; גּוֹיִם "nations" without it. Rom 15:11 quotes the Greek of the verse (from memory).
- **"For his merciful kindness is great toward us" → "For his chesed has prevailed over us"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי גָבַר עָלֵינוּ חַסְדּוֹ: גָּבַר "prevail" (be strong; row, as Ps 65:3) with עַל "over": his chesed is stronger than all that is against us, or greater than we are ("is great toward us" gives the sense, not the verb). חֶסֶד *chesed*.
- **"and the truth of the LORD endureth for ever" → "and the truth of Yahweh is forever"** (v2) — Hebrew וֶאֱמֶת־יְהוָה לְעוֹלָם, no verb: "endures" is not written. אֱמֶת "truth".

### Notes
- **Shortest psalm** (and the shortest chapter in the Bible).
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours.
- **Cited in Rom 15:11** on the nations' praise.
