# Psalm 130

A song of the ascents.

[1] Out of the deep places I have called you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[2] ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, listen to my voice!
Let your ears be attentive
to the voice of my pleas for favor.

[3] If iniquities you should keep, ⟦yah|Yah|LORD⟧,
⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, who could stand?

[4] For with you is the forgiveness,
so that you may be feared.

[5] I have waited for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my soul has waited,
and for his word I have hoped.

[6] My soul is for ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧
more than watchmen for the morning,
watchmen for the morning.

[7] Hope, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, toward ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and much with him is ransoming.

[8] And he will ransom ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧
from all his iniquities.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 7) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Yah"** (v3) — Hebrew יָהּ, the short form of the name; retained. אֲדֹנָי "Adonai" (v2, 3, 6) is another word.
- **"A Song of degrees" → "A song of the ascents"** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת, the first words of v1; מַעֲלָה "a going up, a step", with the article.
- **"Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O LORD" → "Out of the deep places I have called you, Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew מִמַּעֲמַקִּים "out of deep places" (as Ps 69:2, 14); קְרָאתִיךָ, a perfect with "you" as its object. No "O" (no such word in the Hebrew; also v2, 3, 7).
- **"Lord, hear my voice: let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications" → "Adonai, listen to my voice! Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my pleas for favor"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׁמְעָה בְקוֹלִי, שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of"; קַשֻּׁבוֹת "attentive" (as 2 Chr 6:40); תַּחֲנוּנַי "my pleas for favor" (from חָנַן "show favor").
- **"If thou, LORD, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand?" → "If iniquities you should keep, Yah, Adonai, who could stand?"** (v3) — Hebrew אִם־עֲוֹנוֹת תִּשְׁמָר־יָהּ, "iniquities" first, as in the Hebrew; שָׁמַר "keep" (keep watch over, keep in store), not "mark"; "you" is in the verb.
- **"But there is forgiveness with thee" → "For with you is the forgiveness"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי "for", not "but"; הַסְּלִיחָה with the article (as Neh 9:17 סְלִיחוֹת "forgiveness"). **"that thou mayest be feared" → "so that you may be feared"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן "so that".
- **"I wait for the LORD, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope" → "I have waited for Yahweh, my soul has waited, and for his word I have hoped"** (v5) — Hebrew קִוִּיתִי … קִוְּתָה … הוֹחָלְתִּי, three perfects: קִוָּה "wait for", הוֹחִיל "hope"; וְלִדְבָרוֹ "and for his word", first in its line as in the Hebrew.
- **"My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning" → "My soul is for Adonai more than watchmen for the morning, watchmen for the morning"** (v6) — Hebrew נַפְשִׁי לַאדֹנָי, no verb: "waits" is not written ("is" supplied). מִשֹּׁמְרִים "more than watchmen" (שֹׁמְרִים, the participle of שָׁמַר "keep", as v3), then only שֹׁמְרִים לַבֹּקֶר again: the second "more than" and "I say" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"Let Israel hope in the LORD" → "Hope, Israel, toward Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew יַחֵל יִשְׂרָאֵל, יַחֵל the imperative of יִחֵל "hope", Israel addressed; אֶל "toward" (not בְּ "in"). Also Ps 131:3. **"for with the LORD there is mercy" → "for with Yahweh is the steadfast love"** — Hebrew הַחֶסֶד, *chesed* with the article, with its token. **"and with him is plenteous redemption" → "and much with him is ransoming"** — Hebrew וְהַרְבֵּה עִמּוֹ פְדוּת, the Hebrew order; הַרְבֵּה "much"; פְּדוּת "ransoming", from פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem").
- **"And he shall redeem Israel" → "And he will ransom Israel"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהוּא יִפְדֶּה, with the pronoun הוּא "he" standing first (he and no other); פָּדָה "ransom".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew too; the numbers agree.
- **Poetic structure** — the call out of the deep places (v1–2), iniquities and forgiveness (v3–4), waiting (v5–6), Israel called to hope (v7–8).
- **Sixth penitential psalm** — *De Profundis* (Christian reckoning).
