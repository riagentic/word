# Psalm 104

[1] Bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my soul.
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you are very great;
majesty and splendor you have put on,

[2] wrapping yourself in light as in a garment,
stretching out the heavens like a curtain,

[3] who lays the beams of his upper rooms in the waters,
who sets thick clouds as his chariot,
who goes on the wings of the wind,

[4] making his messengers winds,
his ministers a blazing fire.

[5] He founded the earth on its established places;
it shall not be shaken forever and ever.

[6] With the deep, as with a garment, you covered it;
above the mountains waters were standing.

[7] From your rebuke they fled;
from the voice of your thunder they hurried away in alarm.

[8] They went up the mountains, they went down the valleys,
to the place that you founded for them.

[9] A border you set; they shall not pass over,
they shall not return to cover the earth.

[10] He sends out springs into the wadis;
between the mountains they go.

[11] They give drink to all the living things of the field;
wild donkeys quench their thirst.

[12] By them the birds of the heavens abide;
from among the branches they give voice.

[13] He gives drink to the mountains from his upper rooms;
from the fruit of your works the earth is sated.

[14] He makes grass sprout for the beasts,
and plants for the labor of man,
to bring forth bread from the earth,

[15] and wine makes glad the heart of mortal man,
to make the face shine from oil,
and bread sustains the heart of mortal man.

[16] The trees of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are sated,
the cedars of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ that he planted,

[17] where birds make their nests;
the stork — the cypresses are her house.

[18] The high mountains are for the wild goats;
the crags are a refuge for the hyraxes.

[19] He made the moon for appointed times;
the sun knows its setting.

[20] You set darkness, and it is night;
in it all the living things of the forest creep.

[21] The young lions are roaring for prey,
and to seek their food from ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[22] The sun rises; they are gathered in,
and in their dens they lie down.

[23] ⟦adam|Adam|Man⟧ goes out to his work
and to his labor until the evening.

[24] How many are your works, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧!
All of them in wisdom you have made;
the earth is full of your property.

[25] This is the sea, great and wide on both sides;
there are creeping things, and there is no number,
living things small with great.

[26] There ships go;
⟦leviathan|Livyatan|Leviathan⟧, which you formed to play in it.

[27] All of them hope in you,
to give their food in its time.

[28] You give to them, they gather;
you open your hand, they are sated with good.

[29] You hide your face, they are dismayed;
you gather in their spirit, they expire,
and to their dust they return.

[30] You send out your spirit, they are created,
and you renew the face of the ground.

[31] Let the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be forever;
let ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be glad in his works,

[32] who looks at the earth, and it trembles;
he touches the mountains, and they smoke.

[33] Let me sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in my life;
let me make music to my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ while I still am.

[34] May my musing be sweet to him;
I, I will be glad in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[35] Let sinners come to an end from the earth,
and the wicked be no more.
Bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my soul.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 16, 24, 31, 33, 34, 35) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 33) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v21) — Hebrew מֵאֵל "from *El*".
- **"O my soul. O LORD my God, thou art very great; thou art clothed with honour and majesty" → "my soul. Yahweh my Elohim, you are very great; majesty and splendor you have put on"** (v1) — no "O" (also v24, 35). גָּדַלְתָּ "you are great"; הוֹד "majesty", הָדָר "splendor", first in the Hebrew; לָבַשׁ "put on, clothe oneself".
- **"Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment: who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain" → "wrapping yourself in light as in a garment, stretching out the heavens like a curtain"** (v2) — Hebrew עֹטֶה, נוֹטֶה, participles without the article: they mark no person, so "yourself" follows v1 and v3 turns to "his". עָטָה "wrap oneself"; יְרִיעָה "curtain", one length of tent cloth (not "tent").
- **"Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind" → "who lays the beams of his upper rooms in the waters, who sets thick clouds as his chariot, who goes on the wings of the wind"** (v3) — Hebrew הַמְקָרֶה, piel of קָרָה "lay beams"; עֲלִיָּה "upper room" (also v13); שָׂם "set"; עָבִים "thick clouds" (עַב, beside עָנָן "cloud"); רְכוּב, only here, "chariot" (lit. "what is ridden"); הַמְהַלֵּךְ "who goes (about)", not "rides".
- **"Who maketh his angels spirits; his ministers a flaming fire" → "making his messengers winds, his ministers a blazing fire"** (v4) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה מַלְאָכָיו רוּחוֹת מְשָׁרְתָיו אֵשׁ לֹהֵט, in this order: "his messengers" is the first object, "winds" the second. רוּחַ is "wind" and "spirit"; after v3 (clouds, wind) "winds" is the sense; מַלְאָךְ is "messenger", the word for "angel". Heb 1:7 quotes the Greek, "who makes his angels spirits (winds), and his ministers a flame of fire" (from memory); many translations reverse the objects, "he makes winds his messengers, flames of fire his ministers" (from memory) — the Hebrew word order is kept, not levelled to either. אֵשׁ לֹהֵט "blazing fire" (לָהַט "blaze").
- **"Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever" → "He founded the earth on its established places; it shall not be shaken forever and ever"** (v5) — Hebrew יָסַד־אֶרֶץ עַל־מְכוֹנֶיהָ: יָסַד "found" (also v8), מָכוֹן, of כּוּן "establish", "established place", plural; no "that": בַּל־תִּמּוֹט "it shall not be shaken" (מוֹט); עוֹלָם וָעֶד "forever and ever".
- **"Thou coveredst it with the deep as with a garment: the waters stood above the mountains" → "With the deep, as with a garment, you covered it; above the mountains waters were standing"** (v6) — Hebrew תְּהוֹם כַּלְּבוּשׁ כִּסִּיתוֹ, "the deep" first; the suffix of כִּסִּיתוֹ is masculine, while אֶרֶץ is feminine: "it" is the earth only by sense (others take the deep itself as the object, from memory). יַעַמְדוּ, an imperfect, "were standing".
- **"At thy rebuke they fled; at the voice of thy thunder they hasted away" → "From your rebuke they fled; from the voice of your thunder they hurried away in alarm"** (v7) — Hebrew מִן … מִן "from"; חָפַז nifal "hurry away in alarm".
- **"They go up by the mountains; they go down by the valleys" → "They went up the mountains, they went down the valleys"** (v8) — Hebrew יַעֲלוּ הָרִים יֵרְדוּ בְקָעוֹת, no preposition. The subject is the waters of v6–7 (יֵרְדוּ is masculine; בְּקָעוֹת is feminine and cannot be its subject); "The mountains rose, the valleys sank" (from memory, many modern versions) reads the nouns as subjects. זֶה "that" (as v26).
- **"Thou hast set a bound that they may not pass over; that they turn not again to cover the earth" → "A border you set; they shall not pass over, they shall not return to cover the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew גְּבוּל "border", first; בַּל … בַּל "not … not", no "that"; עָבַר "pass over"; שׁוּב + infinitive, "return to cover" = cover again.
- **"He sendeth the springs into the valleys, which run among the hills" → "He sends out springs into the wadis; between the mountains they go"** (v10) — Hebrew שִׁלַּח "send out" (also v30); נַחַל "wadi"; הָרִים "mountains" (as v6, 8, 13, 18, 32); יְהַלֵּכוּן "they go" (as v3, 26).
- **"They give drink to every beast of the field: the wild asses quench their thirst" → "They give drink to all the living things of the field; wild donkeys quench their thirst"** (v11) — Hebrew חַיְתוֹ שָׂדָי, חַיָּה "living thing" (also v20; not "beast": that is בְּהֵמָה, v14); פֶּרֶא "wild donkey"; יִשְׁבְּרוּ צְמָאָם, lit. "break their thirst".
- **"By them shall the fowls of the heaven have their habitation, which sing among the branches" → "By them the birds of the heavens abide; from among the branches they give voice"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁכַן "abide"; עֳפָאיִם "branches", only here; נָתַן קוֹל "give voice".
- **"He watereth the hills from his chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works" → "He gives drink to the mountains from his upper rooms; from the fruit of your works the earth is sated"** (v13) — Hebrew מַשְׁקֶה, the verb of v11 ("give drink"); "his upper rooms" (as v3) and "your works" stand side by side as printed: the person changes within the verse. שָׂבַע "be sated" (also v16, 28).
- **"He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man: that he may bring forth food out of the earth" → "He makes grass sprout for the beasts, and plants for the labor of man, to bring forth bread from the earth"** (v14) — Hebrew הִצְמִיחַ "make sprout"; בְּהֵמָה "beast"; עֵשֶׂב "plant"; לַעֲבֹדַת הָאָדָם "for the labor of man" (עֲבֹדָה "service, labor", also v23): either the work man does on the plants or the use he has of them; לְהוֹצִיא "to bring forth", without a subject; לֶחֶם "bread" (also v15).
- **"And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oil to make his face to shine, and bread which strengtheneth man's heart" → "and wine makes glad the heart of mortal man, to make the face shine from oil, and bread sustains the heart of mortal man"** (v15) — Hebrew יְשַׂמַּח and יִסְעָד are finite verbs, not relative clauses; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man", twice; לְהַצְהִיל פָּנִים מִשָּׁמֶן "to make the face shine from oil" (the face without "his"; מִן may also be "more than oil"); סָעַד "sustain, support".
- **"The trees of the LORD are full of sap" → "The trees of Yahweh are sated"** (v16) — Hebrew יִשְׂבְּעוּ "are sated" (as v13).
- **"as for the stork, the fir trees are her house" → "the stork — the cypresses are her house"** (v17) — Hebrew חֲסִידָה בְּרוֹשִׁים בֵּיתָהּ, no verb; בְּרוֹשׁ is a conifer, the kind uncertain (cypress, juniper or fir). צִפֳּרִים "birds" (צִפּוֹר; v12 has עוֹף, another word).
- **"The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats; and the rocks for the conies" → "The high mountains are for the wild goats; the crags are a refuge for the hyraxes"** (v18) — Hebrew הָרִים הַגְּבֹהִים לַיְּעֵלִים, no "refuge" in the first line; סְלָעִים "crags" (סֶלַע); מַחְסֶה "refuge"; שָׁפָן "hyrax" (fairly sure, as Lev 11:5).
- **"He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down" → "He made the moon for appointed times; the sun knows its setting"** (v19) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "made"; מוֹעֲדִים "appointed times"; מְבוֹאוֹ, lit. "its going in".
- **"Thou makest darkness, and it is night: wherein all the beasts of the forest do creep forth" → "You set darkness, and it is night; in it all the living things of the forest creep"** (v20) — Hebrew תָּשֶׁת, of שִׁית "set"; בּוֹ "in it"; רָמַשׂ "creep".
- **"roar after their prey, and seek their meat from God" → "are roaring for prey, and to seek their food from El"** (v21) — Hebrew שֹׁאֲגִים, a participle; וּלְבַקֵּשׁ, an infinitive, "and to seek"; אֹכֶל "food" (also v27).
- **"they gather themselves together, and lay them down in their dens" → "they are gathered in, and in their dens they lie down"** (v22) — Hebrew יֵאָסֵפוּן, nifal of אָסַף; רָבַץ "lie (down)", of animals. No "when" in the Hebrew.
- **"unto his work and to his labour" → "to his work and to his labor"** (v23) — Hebrew פֹּעַל "work", עֲבֹדָה "labor" (as v14). The `adam` token stands for אָדָם, here without the article: mankind.
- **"O LORD, how manifold are thy works! … the earth is full of thy riches" → "How many are your works, Yahweh! … the earth is full of your property"** (v24) — Hebrew מָה־רַבּוּ "how many are"; קִנְיָן "property", what one has got (of קָנָה "get"; some take it as "your creatures", from קָנָה in the sense "create", as Gen 14:19).
- **"So is this great and wide sea, wherein are things creeping innumerable, both small and great beasts" → "This is the sea, great and wide on both sides; there are creeping things, and there is no number, living things small with great"** (v25) — Hebrew זֶה הַיָּם; רְחַב יָדַיִם, lit. "wide of hands", on both sides; שָׁם "there"; רֶמֶשׂ "creeping things"; וְאֵין מִסְפָּר "and there is no number"; חַיּוֹת "living things" (חַיָּה); עִם "with".
- **"There go the ships: there is that leviathan, whom thou hast made to play therein" → "There ships go; Leviathan, which you formed to play in it"** (v26) — Hebrew שָׁם אֳנִיּוֹת יְהַלֵּכוּן; לִוְיָתָן *Livyatan*, with its token; "there is" is not written. יָצַר "form"; לְשַׂחֶק־בּוֹ "to play in it" (the sea) or "to play with him" (as Job 40:29): בּוֹ allows both.
- **"These wait all upon thee; that thou mayest give them their meat in due season" → "All of them hope in you, to give their food in its time"** (v27) — Hebrew שִׂבֵּר "hope" (as Esth 9:1), with אֵלֶיךָ "toward you"; לָתֵת "to give", an infinitive without a subject; בְּעִתּוֹ "in its time".
- **"That thou givest them they gather: thou openest thine hand, they are filled with good" → "You give to them, they gather; you open your hand, they are sated with good"** (v28) — Hebrew תִּתֵּן לָהֶם יִלְקֹטוּן, clauses side by side, no "when" (also v29–30); לָקַט "gather (pick up)"; טוֹב "good", singular.
- **"thou takest away their breath, they die" → "you gather in their spirit, they expire"** (v29) — Hebrew תֹּסֵף, of אָסַף "gather" (v22): God draws it back to himself; רוּחָם "their spirit" (רוּחַ, also "breath"), the word of v30 "your spirit"; גָּוַע "expire"; נִבְהַל "be dismayed".
- **"thou renewest the face of the earth" → "you renew the face of the ground"** (v30) — Hebrew אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"The glory of the LORD shall endure for ever: the LORD shall rejoice in his works" → "Let the glory of Yahweh be forever; let Yahweh be glad in his works"** (v31) — Hebrew יְהִי, a jussive, "let it be"; יִשְׂמַח, jussive in sense, "let … be glad" (שָׂמַח, also v34).
- **"He looketh on the earth" → "who looks at the earth"** (v32) — Hebrew הַמַּבִּיט, a participle with the article; וַתִּרְעָד "and it trembles"; נָגַע בְּ "touch".
- **"I will sing … as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have my being" → "Let me sing … in my life; let me make music to my Elohim while I still am"** (v33) — Hebrew אָשִׁירָה, אֲזַמְּרָה, cohortatives; בְּחַיָּי "in my life"; זִמֵּר "make music"; בְּעוֹדִי, lit. "in my still".
- **"My meditation of him shall be sweet: I will be glad in the LORD" → "May my musing be sweet to him; I, I will be glad in Yahweh"** (v34) — Hebrew יֶעֱרַב, jussive, "may it be sweet" (עָרַב "be pleasant"); עָלָיו "to him, upon him"; שִׂיחַ "musing" (the noun is "complaint" where a lament is poured out, Ps 64:1; 142:2; here it is the song of v33, so the sense of the verb "muse"); אָנֹכִי, the pronoun stressed.
- **"Let the sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more" → "Let sinners come to an end from the earth, and the wicked be no more"** (v35) — Hebrew יִתַּמּוּ, of תָּמַם "come to an end"; וּרְשָׁעִים עוֹד אֵינָם "and the wicked, no more are they", a clause without a verb, carried by the wish of the first line.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Hallelujah"** (v35) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ "praise Yah" (יָהּ, the short form of the name), with its token; the first time in the Psalter. The Greek puts it at the head of Ps 105 (from memory); the Hebrew has it here.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Ps 104 has no heading in the Hebrew; the verse numbers are the same.
- **Creation** — the psalm walks through the works of Gen 1 (light, the heavens, the waters bounded, plants, moon and sun, the sea and its creatures) and adds man at his labor (v23); it ends with the breath that Yahweh gives and takes (v29–30).
- **Person** — the psalm moves between "you" and "he" (v1–2, 3–4, 5, 6–9, 10–12, 13); each verse keeps the person the Hebrew has.
