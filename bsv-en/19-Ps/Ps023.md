# Psalm 23

A psalm of David.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is my shepherd;
I shall not lack.

[2] In pastures of grass he makes me lie down;
beside waters of rest he leads me.

[3] He restores my soul.
He leads me in courses of righteousness
on account of his name.

[4] Even though I walk in the valley of the shadow of death,
I will not fear evil,
for you are with me;
your rod and your staff, they comfort me.

[5] You arrange before me a table
in front of those who harass me;
you have made my head fat with oil;
my cup is brimful.

[6] Surely good and ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ shall pursue me
all the days of my life,
and I shall return to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for length of days.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "A psalm of David"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד, the first words of v1. לְדָוִד may mean "by David", "for David" or "belonging to David"; "of David" leaves it open.
- **"I shall not want" → "I shall not lack"** (v1) — Hebrew לֹא אֶחְסָר, from חָסֵר "lack, decrease".
- **"in green pastures … beside the still waters" → "In pastures of grass … beside waters of rest"** (v2) — Hebrew בִּנְאוֹת דֶּשֶׁא "in pastures of grass"; עַל־מֵי מְנֻחוֹת, literally "beside waters of rests" (מְנוּחָה "rest, resting place", plural). The Hebrew order (the place first) is kept.
- **"He restoreth my soul" kept** (v3) — Hebrew נַפְשִׁי יְשׁוֹבֵב, literally "my soul he brings back" (שׁוֹבֵב, from שׁוּב "return"). **"for his name's sake" → "on account of his name"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן שְׁמוֹ.
- **"through the valley of the shadow of death" → "in the valley of the shadow of death"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּגֵיא צַלְמָוֶת. צַלְמָוֶת is translated as the Masoretes pointed it: צֵל "shadow" + מָוֶת "death" (as Job 3:5). Many read the word as צַלְמוּת "deep darkness", one word from a root "be dark"; the meaning is not certain. **"I will fear no evil" → "I will not fear evil"** — Hebrew לֹא־אִירָא רָע.
- **"Thou preparest a table" → "You arrange … a table"** (v5) — Hebrew תַּעֲרֹךְ, from עָרַךְ "arrange, set in order". **"in the presence of mine enemies" → "in front of those who harass me"** — Hebrew נֶגֶד צֹרְרָי, from צָרַר "harass, show hostility" (אֹיֵב "enemy" is another word). **"thou anointest my head with oil" → "you have made my head fat with oil"** — Hebrew דִּשַּׁנְתָּ, a perfect of דָּשֵׁן "be fat" (piel "make fat"); מָשַׁח "anoint" is not the word. **"my cup runneth over" → "my cup is brimful"** — Hebrew כּוֹסִי רְוָיָה, a noun: "my cup is saturation" (from רָוָה "drink one's fill").
- **"goodness and mercy shall follow me" → "good and steadfast love shall pursue me"** (v6) — Hebrew טוֹב וָחֶסֶד יִרְדְּפוּנִי; רָדַף is "pursue". אַךְ "surely" may also be "only". **"and I will dwell in the house of the LORD" → "and I shall return to the house of Yahweh"** — the Hebrew is pointed וְשַׁבְתִּי, from שׁוּב "return" (literally "return in the house"). The usual "dwell" reads וְיָשַׁבְתִּי, from יָשַׁב, as the Greek and other versions have it (from memory); the written letters allow either. **"for ever" → "for length of days"** — Hebrew לְאֹרֶךְ יָמִים; לְעוֹלָם "forever" is not the word.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **Shepherd** — Yahweh as shepherd (Gen 48:15; 49:24; Ezek 34; John 10).
- **Rod and staff** — שֵׁבֶט and מִשְׁעֶנֶת, the shepherd's club and his staff.
- **The table** — the host who receives a guest while those who harass him look on.
