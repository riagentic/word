# Psalm 15

A psalm of David.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, who shall sojourn in your tent?
Who shall abide on your holy mountain?

[2] He who walks blameless and works righteousness
and speaks truth in his heart;

[3] he has not slandered with his tongue,
he has not done evil to his neighbor,
and a reproach he has not taken up against the one near to him;

[4] despised in his eyes is one rejected,
but those who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he honors;
he has sworn to do harm and does not exchange it;

[5] his money he has not given at interest,
and a bribe against the innocent he has not taken.
He who does these things shall not be shaken forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"A Psalm of David"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד; the לְ may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" David.
- **"who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?" → "who shall sojourn in your tent? Who shall abide on your holy mountain?"** (v1) — Hebrew גּוּר "sojourn" (live as a guest, not as a native); אֹהֶל "tent"; שָׁכַן "abide"; הַר קָדְשֶׁךָ "the mountain of your holiness".
- **"He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness" → "He who walks blameless and works righteousness"** (v2) — Hebrew הוֹלֵךְ תָּמִים, "walking whole, blameless"; פֹּעֵל צֶדֶק "working righteousness".
- **"He that backbiteth not with his tongue" → "he has not slandered with his tongue"** (v3) — Hebrew לֹא־רָגַל עַל־לְשֹׁנוֹ, literally "he has not footed it upon his tongue": רָגַל, from רֶגֶל "foot", is to go about as a talebearer. v3–5 are perfects ("he has not …"), set out as what the man has done.
- **"his neighbour" … "his neighbour" → "his neighbor" … "the one near to him"** (v3) — Hebrew רֵעֵהוּ "his neighbor", then קְרֹבוֹ "the one near to him" (by kin or by dwelling: not said); two words. "and a reproach" keeps וְ and the Hebrew order (חֶרְפָּה first); נָשָׂא "take up, lift up".
- **"In whose eyes a vile person is contemned" → "despised in his eyes is one rejected"** (v4) — Hebrew נִבְזֶה בְּעֵינָיו נִמְאָס, two passive participles: בָּזָה "despise", מָאַס "reject". The words may also be read of the man himself: "he is despised in his own eyes, rejected" (lowly in his own sight). The Hebrew order is kept, so both readings stay open.
- **"He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not" → "he has sworn to do harm and does not exchange it"** (v4) — Hebrew נִשְׁבַּע לְהָרַע וְלֹא יָמִר, "he has sworn to do evil / to bring harm, and does not exchange" (הֵרַע is "do evil" elsewhere; "harm" here, so that the verse does not say an oath to an evil deed). "His own" is not in the Hebrew: the usual sense is an oath that turns out to his own harm and is kept all the same. The Septuagint reads "to his neighbor" (לְרֵעַ for לְהָרַע; from memory). יָמִר, hifil of מוּר "exchange" (as Lev 27:10): he does not put something else in its place; "it" is supplied.
- **"He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent" → "his money he has not given at interest, and a bribe against the innocent he has not taken"** (v5) — Hebrew כַּסְפּוֹ לֹא־נָתַן בְּנֶשֶׁךְ, "his silver he has not given at interest" (כֶּסֶף "money" where a payment is meant; נֶשֶׁךְ "interest", literally "a bite"); שֹׁחַד "bribe"; the objects first, as in the Hebrew.
- **"shall never be moved" → "shall not be shaken forever"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא יִמּוֹט לְעוֹלָם, "shall not be shaken, forever" (= never); מוֹט nifal "be shaken" (as Ps 13:4; 16:8).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew too; the numbers agree.
- **Entrance liturgy** — temple-gate question-and-answer. Ten ethical qualifications for worshiper.
- **Sworn to do harm** — integrity even at personal cost (as the verse is usually read).
