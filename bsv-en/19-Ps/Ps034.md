# Psalm 34

Of David, when he changed his sense before ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧, and he drove him out, and he went.

[1] Let me bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at every time;
continually his praise is in my mouth.

[2] In ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my soul glories;
let the humble hear and rejoice.

[3] Magnify ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with me,
and let us exalt his name together.

[4] I sought ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he answered me,
and from all my terrors he delivered me.

[5] They looked to him and were radiant,
and their faces, let them not be abashed.

[6] This afflicted one called, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ heard,
and from all his distresses he saved him.

[7] The angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ camps
around those who fear him, and he pulls them out.

[8] Taste and see that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is good;
happy is the man who takes refuge in him.

[9] Fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, his holy ones,
for there is no lack to those who fear him.

[10] Young lions have become poor and hungry,
but those who seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall not lack any good.

[11] Come, sons, listen to me;
the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will teach you.

[12] Who is the man who delights in life,
who loves days, to see good?

[13] Guard your tongue from evil,
and your lips from speaking deceit.

[14] Depart from evil and do good;
seek ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and pursue it.

[15] The eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are toward the righteous,
and his ears toward their cry for help.

[16] The face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is against those who do evil,
to cut off from the earth their remembrance.

[17] They cried out, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ heard,
and from all their distresses he delivered them.

[18] Near is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to the broken of heart,
and the crushed of spirit he saves.

[19] Many are the evils of the righteous one,
but from them all ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ delivers him.

[20] He keeps all his bones;
not one of them has been broken.

[21] Evil shall put the wicked to death,
and those who hate the righteous shall be guilty.

[22] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ ransoms the soul of his servants,
and none of those who take refuge in him shall be guilty.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 22) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Psalm of David, when he changed his behaviour before Abimelech; who drove him away, and he departed" → "Of David, when he changed his sense before Abimelech, and he drove him out, and he went"** (heading) — Hebrew לְדָוִד בְּשַׁנּוֹתוֹ אֶת־טַעְמוֹ לִפְנֵי אֲבִימֶלֶךְ וַיְגָרֲשֵׁהוּ וַיֵּלַךְ. There is no word "psalm". לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open. טַעַם "sense" (literally "taste"), as 1 Sam 21:13 וַיְשַׁנּוֹ אֶת־טַעְמוֹ "he changed his sense", where he feigns madness. The heading names Abimelech; 1 Sam 21:10–15 names Achish king of Gath. The name stands as printed. Who drove whom out is not said ("he … him"). גֵּרֵשׁ "drive out"; הָלַךְ "go".
- **"I will bless the LORD at all times: his praise shall continually be in my mouth" → "Let me bless Yahweh at every time; continually his praise is in my mouth"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲבָרֲכָה, a cohortative ("let me bless", "I will bless"); בְּכָל־עֵת "at every time"; the second line has no verb.
- **"My soul shall make her boast in the LORD" → "In Yahweh my soul glories"** (v2) — Hebrew בַּיהוָה תִּתְהַלֵּל נַפְשִׁי, "in Yahweh" first; הִתְהַלֵּל "glory" (boast). עָנָו "humble"; יִשְׁמְעוּ … וְיִשְׂמָחוּ jussive "let … hear and rejoice"; שָׂמַח "rejoice".
- **"O magnify the LORD with me" → "Magnify Yahweh with me"** (v3) — Hebrew גַּדְּלוּ לַיהוָה, "make great to Yahweh" (= declare him great); there is no "O". רוֹמֵם "exalt".
- **"delivered me from all my fears" → "from all my terrors he delivered me"** (v4) — Hebrew מְגוּרוֹתַי, from גּוּר "be afraid"; the Hebrew order.
- **"They looked unto him, and were lightened: and their faces were not ashamed" → "They looked to him and were radiant, and their faces, let them not be abashed"** (v5) — Hebrew הִבִּיטוּ אֵלָיו וְנָהָרוּ וּפְנֵיהֶם אַל־יֶחְפָּרוּ. "They" has no named antecedent (the humble of v2 may be meant); the Greek and Syriac read commands, "Look to him and be radiant" (from memory); the Hebrew is kept. אַל with the verb is a wish ("let them not"); חָפֵר "be abashed" (another root than חָפַר "dig").
- **"This poor man cried … and saved him out of all his troubles" → "This afflicted one called … and from all his distresses he saved him"** (v6) — Hebrew עָנִי "afflicted"; קָרָא "call"; שָׁמֵעַ "heard" without an object ("him" is not in the Hebrew); צָרָה "distress" (also v17).
- **"The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them" → "The angel of Yahweh camps around those who fear him, and he pulls them out"** (v7) — Hebrew חֹנֶה מַלְאַךְ־יְהוָה סָבִיב לִירֵאָיו וַיְחַלְּצֵם. מַלְאָךְ is "messenger"; מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה "the angel of Yahweh", one rendering through the Old Testament. חָנָה "camp"; חִלֵּץ "pull out" (also "rescue"); וַיְחַלְּצֵם is a wayyiqtol after the participle, in a general statement: present.
- **"O taste and see … blessed is the man that trusteth in him" → "Taste and see … happy is the man who takes refuge in him"** (v8) — Hebrew טַעֲמוּ, the root of טַעַם "sense" in the heading; no "O". אַשְׁרֵי "happy" ("blessed" is בָּרוּךְ). הַגֶּבֶר "the man" (גֶּבֶר, a strong man; no other word for "man" stands beside it here). חָסָה בְּ "take refuge in" (also v22).
- **"O fear the LORD, ye his saints: for there is no want to them that fear him" → "Fear Yahweh, his holy ones, for there is no lack to those who fear him"** (v9) — Hebrew יְראוּ, written without the second yod; קְדֹשָׁיו "his holy ones"; מַחְסוֹר "lack".
- **"The young lions do lack, and suffer hunger: but they that seek the LORD shall not want any good thing" → "Young lions have become poor and hungry, but those who seek Yahweh shall not lack any good"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּפִירִים רָשׁוּ וְרָעֵבוּ, two perfects; רוּשׁ "be poor". The Greek reads "rich men" for כְּפִירִים (from memory); the Hebrew is kept. דָּרַשׁ "seek" (also v4); חָסֵר "lack".
- **"Come, ye children" → "Come, sons"** (v11) — Hebrew בָּנִים "sons"; שָׁמַע לְ "listen to"; "the fear of Yahweh" stands first.
- **"What man is he that desireth life, and loveth many days" → "Who is the man who delights in life, who loves days"** (v12) — Hebrew הֶחָפֵץ חַיִּים אֹהֵב יָמִים; חָפֵץ "delight in"; "many" is not in the Hebrew. אִישׁ "man".
- **"Keep thy tongue … from speaking guile" → "Guard your tongue … from speaking deceit"** (v13) — Hebrew נְצֹר "guard"; מִרְמָה "deceit". The address is singular.
- **"Depart from evil … seek peace" → "Depart from evil … seek shalom"** (v14) — Hebrew סוּר מֵרָע "depart from evil"; שָׁלוֹם *shalom*; רָדַף "pursue".
- **"his ears are open unto their cry" → "his ears toward their cry for help"** (v15) — Hebrew וְאָזְנָיו אֶל־שַׁוְעָתָם, no verb ("are open" is not in the Hebrew); שַׁוְעָה "cry for help".
- **"to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth" → "to cut off from the earth their remembrance"** (v16) — Hebrew לְהַכְרִית מֵאֶרֶץ זִכְרָם; זֵכֶר "remembrance"; פְּנֵי … בְּ "the face … against".
- **"The righteous cry, and the LORD heareth" → "They cried out, and Yahweh heard"** (v17) — Hebrew צָעֲקוּ וַיהוָה שָׁמֵעַ, perfects, with no subject: "the righteous" is not in the Hebrew (the righteous of v15 may be meant; the Greek adds "the righteous", from memory). צָעַק "cry out"; הִצִּיל "deliver".
- **"The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit" → "Near is Yahweh to the broken of heart, and the crushed of spirit he saves"** (v18) — Hebrew קָרוֹב יְהוָה לְנִשְׁבְּרֵי־לֵב וְאֶת־דַּכְּאֵי־רוּחַ יוֹשִׁיעַ, in the Hebrew order; דַּכָּא "crushed"; הוֹשִׁיעַ "save".
- **"Many are the afflictions of the righteous" → "Many are the evils of the righteous one"** (v19) — Hebrew רַבּוֹת רָעוֹת צַדִּיק; רָעָה "evil" (the hurt that befalls him); צַדִּיק is singular, without the article.
- **"not one of them is broken" → "not one of them has been broken"** (v20) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר, a participle without a subject ("He" supplied); אַחַת מֵהֵנָּה לֹא נִשְׁבָּרָה, literally "one of them has not been broken". John 19:36 cites it.
- **"Evil shall slay the wicked: and they that hate the righteous shall be desolate" → "Evil shall put the wicked to death, and those who hate the righteous shall be guilty"** (v21) — Hebrew תְּמוֹתֵת, polel of מוּת, "put to death"; רָעָה "evil". אָשֵׁם "be guilty" (also v22; it may also be taken as "bear guilt", "be held guilty").
- **"The LORD redeemeth the soul of his servants: and none of them that trust in him shall be desolate" → "Yahweh ransoms the soul of his servants, and none of those who take refuge in him shall be guilty"** (v22) — Hebrew פּוֹדֶה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem"); עֶבֶד is "slave", of Yahweh's men "servant"; וְלֹא יֶאְשְׁמוּ כָּל־ "and not … all" = "none".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–22 = Heb v2–23.
- **Acrostic** — the verses begin with the letters of the Hebrew alphabet in order, א (v1) to ת (v21). No verse begins with ו: the second line of v5 does (וּפְנֵיהֶם). v22 begins with פ and stands after ת, outside the series (as Ps 25:22).
- **Heading** — David at the court of Achish of Gath, 1 Sam 21:10–15; "Abimelech" (Hebrew *Avimelech*, "my father is king") is the name the heading gives.
- **v20 cited John 19:36** — bone not broken of the Messiah.
- **"Taste and see"** (v8) — taken up in 1 Pet 2:3.
