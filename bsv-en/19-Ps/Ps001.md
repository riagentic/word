# Psalm 1

[1] Happy is the man
who has not walked in the counsel of the wicked,
and in the way of sinners has not stood,
and in the seat of scoffers has not sat.

[2] But in the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his delight,
and his ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ he softly recites day and night.

[3] And he shall be like a tree planted by streams of water,
which gives its fruit in its season,
and its leaf does not wither;
and all that he does prospers.

[4] Not so the wicked,
but like the chaff that wind drives away.

[5] Therefore the wicked shall not stand in the judgment,
nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.

[6] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ knows the way of the righteous,
but the way of the wicked shall perish.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v1 and v3 keep the existing break inside the first or second line where the Hebrew accents mark a pause.
- **"Blessed is the man" → "Happy is the man"** (v1) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי, "happy, how happy" (as Deut 33:29; 1 Kgs 10:8); "blessed" is the word of בָּרוּךְ, which does not stand here.
- **"that walketh not … nor standeth … nor sitteth" → "who has not walked … has not stood … has not sat"** (v1) — Hebrew perfects (הָלַךְ, עָמָד, יָשָׁב), said of what the man has never done; English "does not walk" gives the same general truth. The second and third lines keep the Hebrew order ("and in the way of sinners has not stood"), each with its וְ "and".
- **"the ungodly" → "the wicked"** (v1, 4, 5, 6) — Hebrew רְשָׁעִים.
- **"the seat of the scornful" → "the seat of scoffers"** (v1) — Hebrew מוֹשַׁב לֵצִים. מוֹשָׁב is elsewhere "dwelling place" (noun of יָשַׁב); here, after "walked … stood", it is the place where one sits, so "seat"; "sat" is יָשַׁב of the body. לֵצִים "scoffers, mockers".
- **"But his delight is in the law of the LORD" → "But in the torah of Yahweh is his delight"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי אִם, "but rather" after the negatives of v1 (not "except" here); the Hebrew order is kept. חֵפֶץ "delight".
- **"in his law doth he meditate" → "his torah he softly recites"** (v2) — Hebrew וּבְתוֹרָתוֹ יֶהְגֶּה. הָגָה is to give a low sound; of a man with a text, to say it over half aloud (as Josh 1:8, where the same בְּ is "it"); "meditate" names only thinking. Literally "and in his torah he recites". "His" is Yahweh's torah.
- **"rivers of water" → "streams of water"** (v3) — Hebrew פַּלְגֵי מָיִם; פֶּלֶג is a dug channel or watercourse, not a river (נָהָר); "channels" is kept for אָפִיק (Ps 18:15; 42:1). עַל "by", literally "upon".
- **"And he shall be"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהָיָה, kept.
- **"that bringeth forth his fruit" → "which gives its fruit"** (v3) — Hebrew יִתֵּן, "gives". The suffixes "his / its" refer to the tree.
- **"whatsoever he doeth shall prosper" → "all that he does prospers"** (v3) — Hebrew יַצְלִיחַ, hifil of צָלַח: "prospers", or, with the man as subject, "he makes prosper" ("all that he does he makes prosper"); the Hebrew allows both.
- **"The ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff" → "Not so the wicked, but like the chaff"** (v4) — the Hebrew has no verb in either line. "The chaff" has the article; רוּחַ "wind" has none (רוּחַ is also "spirit, breath": here wind).
- **"in the congregation of the righteous"** (v5) — Hebrew עֲדַת, the gathered body (as the congregation of Israel). **"stand"** — Hebrew יָקֻמוּ, literally "rise".
- **"but the way of the ungodly shall perish"** (v6) — Hebrew תֹּאבֵד, "perish".

### Notes
- **Introductory psalm** — wisdom theme; the two ways.
- **No heading** — Psalm 1 has no heading in the Hebrew; our verse numbers are the Hebrew ones.
- **Tree by waters** — Jer 17:8 parallel.
