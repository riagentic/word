# Psalm 16

A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧ of David.

[1] Keep me, ⟦el|El|God⟧, for I have taken refuge in you.

[2] You have said to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "You are ⟦adonai|Adonai|my Lord⟧;
my good is not beyond you."

[3] As for the holy ones who are in the land, they —
and the nobles: all my delight is in them.

[4] Their sorrows shall multiply; they have hastened to another;
I will not pour out their drink offerings of blood,
and I will not take up their names upon my lips.

[5] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is the portion of my share and my cup;
you hold fast my lot.

[6] Cords have fallen to me in the pleasant places;
yes, an inheritance is fair to me.

[7] I will bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who has counseled me;
also in the nights my kidneys discipline me.

[8] I have set ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ before me continually;
for at my right hand — I shall not be shaken.

[9] Therefore my heart rejoices, and my glory is glad;
also my flesh shall abide in security.

[10] For you will not leave my soul to Sheol;
you will not give your faithful one to see the pit.

[11] You will make known to me the path of life;
fullness of gladness is with your face;
pleasant things are in your right hand forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 5, 7, 8) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "El"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"Michtam of David" → "A Miktam of David"** (heading) — Hebrew מִכְתָּם לְדָוִד, the first words of v1. *Miktam* is retained: its meaning is not known (sometimes "inscription" or "golden psalm"). The לְ may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" David.
- **"Preserve me, O God: for in thee do I put my trust" → "Keep me, El, for I have taken refuge in you"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׁמְרֵנִי "keep me"; חָסִיתִי בָךְ, perfect of חָסָה "take refuge".
- **"O my soul, thou hast said unto the LORD" → "You have said to Yahweh"** (v2) — the printed Hebrew אָמַרְתְּ is pointed as "you (feminine) have said"; "O my soul" is KJV's supply for the one addressed and is not in the Hebrew. Many Hebrew manuscripts and the versions read "I have said" (אָמַרְתִּי), and the written form may be an old spelling of it; the verse follows the Masoretic pointing.
- **"my goodness extendeth not to thee" → "my good is not beyond you"** (v2) — Hebrew טוֹבָתִי בַּל־עָלֶיךָ, "my good — not upon you": the line has no verb, and עַל can be "beyond, besides" ("I have no good beyond you"), "above" or "upon" ("my good is no duty laid on you", KJV). Meaning uncertain.
- **"But to the saints that are in the earth, and to the excellent, in whom is all my delight" → "As for the holy ones who are in the land, they — and the nobles: all my delight is in them"** (v3) — Hebrew לִקְדוֹשִׁים אֲשֶׁר־בָּאָרֶץ הֵמָּה וְאַדִּירֵי כָּל־חֶפְצִי־בָם, word for word "to / as for the holy ones who are in the land, they, and the nobles of — all my delight in them". אַדִּירֵי is a construct form with no noun after it; the sense of the verse is uncertain and is left as the words stand. "Holy ones" (קְדוֹשִׁים) may be persons of Israel or, as elsewhere, beings around Yahweh; אֶרֶץ "land" or "earth".
- **"Their sorrows shall be multiplied that hasten after another god" → "Their sorrows shall multiply; they have hastened to another"** (v4) — Hebrew יִרְבּוּ עַצְּבוֹתָם אַחֵר מָהָרוּ, "their sorrows shall multiply — another they have hastened". "God" is not in the Hebrew; "to" is supplied. מָהַר may also be "pay the bride-price for" (as Exod 22:16): "they have bought another". עַצְּבוֹת "sorrows" sounds like עֲצַבִּים "idols".
- **"their drink offerings of blood will I not offer" → "I will not pour out their drink offerings of blood"** (v4) — Hebrew בַּל־אַסִּיךְ נִסְכֵּיהֶם מִדָּם, הִסִּיךְ "pour out (a drink offering)"; מִדָּם "of / from blood".
- **"The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot" → "Yahweh is the portion of my share and my cup; you hold fast my lot"** (v5) — Hebrew מְנָת־חֶלְקִי וְכוֹסִי, "the portion of my share, and my cup" (חֵלֶק "share", not "inheritance": that is נַחֲלָה, v6); תּוֹמִיךְ גּוֹרָלִי, taken from תָּמַךְ "hold fast, support"; the form is unusual and its sense uncertain.
- **"The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant places; yea, I have a goodly heritage" → "Cords have fallen to me in the pleasant places; yes, an inheritance is fair to me"** (v6) — Hebrew חֲבָלִים, "cords", the measuring cords that mark out a portion of land (as Deut 32:9 "cord of his inheritance"); נְעִמִים "pleasant (places)", with the article; נַחֲלָת שָׁפְרָה עָלָי "an inheritance is fair upon me".
- **"who hath given me counsel: my reins also instruct me in the night seasons" → "who has counseled me; also in the nights my kidneys discipline me"** (v7) — Hebrew יְעָצָנִי, perfect of יָעַץ "counsel"; כִּלְיוֹת "kidneys", the seat of the inner man (KJV "reins"); יִסֵּר "discipline"; לֵילוֹת plural "nights".
- **"because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved" → "for at my right hand — I shall not be shaken"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי מִימִינִי בַּל־אֶמּוֹט, "for from my right — I shall not be shaken": "he is" is not written. Acts 2:25 has ὅτι ἐκ δεξιῶν μού ἐστιν "for he is at my right hand". תָּמִיד "continually". מוֹט nifal "be shaken" (as Ps 13:4; 15:5).
- **"my glory rejoiceth: my flesh also shall rest in hope" → "my glory is glad; also my flesh shall abide in security"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיָּגֶל כְּבוֹדִי, גִּיל "be glad"; כָּבוֹד "glory" (some read כְּבֵדִי "my liver"; Acts 2:26 has ἡ γλῶσσά μου "my tongue"). יִשְׁכֹּן לָבֶטַח, שָׁכַן "abide", בֶּטַח "in security"; Acts 2:26 has ἐπʼ ἐλπίδι "in hope". The perfect שָׂמַח and the form with וַ are rendered as present ("rejoices … is glad"), literally "has rejoiced … and was glad".
- **"thou wilt not leave my soul in hell; neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption" → "you will not leave my soul to Sheol; you will not give your faithful one to see the pit"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא־תַעֲזֹב נַפְשִׁי לִשְׁאוֹל, "leave … to Sheol" (direction, not "in"); שְׁאוֹל "Sheol", plain word. לֹא־תִתֵּן "you will not give (= let)". חֲסִידְךָ, the qere, singular: "your faithful one" (חָסִיד, from חֶסֶד); the written form חסידיך can be read as plural, "your faithful ones" (from memory: the tool prints the qere only). שַׁחַת "pit", without the article; Acts 2:27; 13:35 have διαφθοράν "corruption", the word of the Septuagint (from memory for the Septuagint).
- **"Thou wilt shew me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore" → "You will make known to me the path of life; fullness of gladness is with your face; pleasant things are in your right hand forever"** (v11) — Hebrew תּוֹדִיעֵנִי, imperfect "you will make known"; אֹרַח "path"; שֹׂבַע שְׂמָחוֹת אֶת־פָּנֶיךָ, "satiety of gladnesses with your face" (שִׂמְחָה plural; פָּנִים "face"); נְעִמוֹת "pleasant things" (as v6 "pleasant"); בִּימִינְךָ "in (or at) your right hand"; נֶצַח "forever" (to the end). Acts 2:28 has ἐγνώρισάς μοι ὁδοὺς ζωῆς "you made known to me ways of life" and πληρώσεις με εὐφροσύνης μετὰ τοῦ προσώπου σου "you will fill me with gladness with your face".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew too; the numbers agree.
- **Classic Davidic confidence** psalm.
- **v8–11 cited in Acts 2:25–28; v10 in Acts 13:35** — Peter and Paul apply them to the resurrection of the Messiah; the Greek there has the wording named above, the verses here keep the Hebrew.
