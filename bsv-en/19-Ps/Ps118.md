# Psalm 118

[1] Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he is good,
for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever.

[2] Let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ now say,
"For his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever."

[3] Let the house of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ now say,
"For his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever."

[4] Let those who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ now say,
"For his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever."

[5] Out of the narrow place I called on ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧;
⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧ answered me in the broad place.

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is for me; I will not fear.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is for me among my helpers,
and I will look on those who hate me.

[8] It is better to take refuge in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧

[9] It is better to take refuge in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧

[10] All nations surrounded me;

[11] They surrounded me, yes, they surrounded me;
in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ indeed I will cut them off.

[12] They surrounded me like bees;
they were quenched like a fire of thorns;
in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ indeed I will cut them off.

[13] You surely pushed me, that I should fall,
but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ helped me.

[14] My strength and song is ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧,
and he has become my salvation.

[15] The voice of a ringing cry and of salvation is in the tents of the righteous:
"The right hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ does valiantly;

[16] the right hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is exalted;
the right hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ does valiantly."

[17] I shall not die, but I shall live,
and recount the deeds of ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧.

[18] ⟦yah|Yah|The LORD⟧ has surely disciplined me,

[19] Open to me the gates of righteousness;
I will enter through them, I will give thanks to ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧.

[20] This is the gate of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
the righteous shall enter through it.

[21] I will give thanks to you, for you have answered me
and have become my salvation.

[22] A stone that the builders rejected
has become the ⟦rosh-pinnah|rosh pinnah|cornerstone⟧.

[23] From ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ this has come to be;

[24] This is the day that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made;

[25] Ah, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, save, please!

[26] Blessed is he who comes in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;

[27] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is ⟦el|El|God⟧, and he has given us light.

[29] Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he is good,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16, 20, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 29) — Tetragrammaton. **"LORD" → "Yah"** (v5, 14, 17, 18, 19) — Hebrew יָהּ, the short form of the name. **"God" → "El"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew אֵל. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v28) — Hebrew אֱלֹהַי "my Elohim".
- **"O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good: because his mercy endureth for ever" → "Give thanks to Yahweh, for he is good, for his chesed is forever"** (v1, 29) — Hebrew הוֹדוּ לַיהוָה כִּי־טוֹב כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ, as 1 Chr 16:34: no "Oh", and no verb in the second line ("endureth" is supplied). הוֹדָה "give thanks to"; חֶסֶד *chesed*.
- **"Let Israel now say, that his mercy endureth for ever" → "Let Israel now say, 'For his chesed is forever'"** (v2, 3, 4) — Hebrew יֹאמַר־נָא / יֹאמְרוּ־נָא: the jussive with נָא "now" (dropped in the former wording). The refrain keeps its כִּי "for" as in v1; after "say" it may also be "that" (KJV), introducing what is said.
- **"I called upon the LORD in distress: the LORD answered me, and set me in a large place" → "Out of the narrow place I called on Yah; Yah answered me in the broad place"** (v5) — Hebrew מִן־הַמֵּצַר "out of the narrow place" (מֵצַר, a strait, set against מֶרְחָב "the broad place", as 2 Sam 22:20; Ps 18:19). עָנָנִי בַמֶּרְחָב "he answered me in the broad place": "set me" is not written; the answer is given by bringing him into the open (said here, not added to the verse). The name stands last in the Hebrew line.
- **"The LORD is on my side" → "Yahweh is for me"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew יְהוָה לִי, "Yahweh is to / for me". מַה־יַּעֲשֶׂה לִי אָדָם, an imperfect, "what can (will) man do to me?" (Heb 13:6 quotes it).
- **"The LORD taketh my part with them that help me: therefore shall I see my desire upon them that hate me" → "Yahweh is for me among my helpers, and I will look on those who hate me"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּעֹזְרָי "among my helpers" (or "as my helper", בְּ of identity); וַאֲנִי אֶרְאֶה בְשֹׂנְאָי "and I, I will look on those who hate me": רָאָה בְּ is looking on someone's fall; "my desire" and "in triumph" are not written.
- **"put confidence in princes" → "trust in nobles"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew לַחֲסוֹת "to take refuge" (חָסָה) set against בְּטֹחַ "to trust" (בָּטַח): two verbs, kept apart. נְדִיבִים "nobles" (lit. "willing ones", as 1 Sam 2:8).
- **"but in the name of the LORD will I destroy them" → "in the name of Yahweh indeed I will cut them off"** (v10, 11, 12) — Hebrew כִּי אֲמִילַם: כִּי here stresses the clause ("indeed"; or "that"). אֲמִילַם is the hifil imperfect of מוּל, the verb that elsewhere means "circumcise"; the sense "cut off" (or "ward off") is the best supported, but uncertain. The imperfect may also describe what he did again and again ("I cut them off").
- **"They compassed me about; yea, they compassed me about" → "They surrounded me, yes, they surrounded me"** (v11) — Hebrew סַבּוּנִי גַם־סְבָבוּנִי, the one verb twice (two forms), with גַם "yes, also"; "on every side" is not written.
- **"they are quenched as the fire of thorns" → "they were quenched like a fire of thorns"** (v12) — Hebrew דֹּעֲכוּ, a passive perfect of דָּעַךְ "be extinguished": the attackers die down like a thorn fire, which flares and goes out at once. The Septuagint has "they blazed up" (from memory); the Masoretic text is kept. אֵשׁ קוֹצִים "a fire of thorns".
- **"Thou hast thrust sore at me that I might fall" → "You surely pushed me, that I should fall"** (v13) — Hebrew דַּחֹה דְחִיתַנִי לִנְפֹּל, literally "pushing you pushed me, to fall": the infinitive absolute with its verb, rendered by "surely". The verb is second person ("you"), addressed to the enemy; the former "I was pushed" made it passive. וַיהוָה עֲזָרָנִי "but Yahweh helped me".
- **"The LORD is my strength and song, and is become my salvation" → "My strength and song is Yah, and he has become my salvation"** (v14) — Hebrew עָזִּי וְזִמְרָת יָהּ וַיְהִי־לִי לִישׁוּעָה, word for word as Exod 15:2 and in its finished wording (also Isa 12:2). וַיְהִי־לִי לִישׁוּעָה is literally "and he has become to me for salvation"; יְשׁוּעָה "salvation".
- **"The voice of rejoicing and salvation is in the tabernacles of the righteous" → "The voice of a ringing cry and of salvation is in the tents of the righteous"** (v15) — Hebrew קוֹל רִנָּה וִישׁוּעָה: קוֹל "voice", רִנָּה "a ringing cry" (of joy, Ps 30:5), יְשׁוּעָה "salvation"; "glad songs" was a paraphrase. אֹהֶל "tent". The words of v15b–16 are taken as what that voice says (the quotation marks are ours).
- **"the right hand of the LORD doeth valiantly" → "does valiantly"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew עֹשָׂה חָיִל, literally "makes force" (חַיִל), as Ps 60:12; Num 24:18.
- **"The right hand of the LORD is exalted" → "is exalted"** (v16) — Hebrew רוֹמֵמָה, from רוּם "be high": read as "is lifted high" (KJV) or as a perfect with no object, "has raised high" (the former wording "exalts"); the object is not written. Uncertain.
- **"I shall not die, but live" → "I shall not die, but I shall live"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי־אֶחְיֶה, כִּי after a negative = "but". וַאֲסַפֵּר "and recount" (סִפֵּר); מַעֲשֵׂי יָהּ "the deeds of Yah".
- **"The LORD hath chastened me sore: but he hath not given me over unto death" → "Yah has surely disciplined me, but to death he has not given me"** (v18) — Hebrew יַסֹּר יִסְּרַנִּי יָּהּ, literally "disciplining Yah has disciplined me" (infinitive absolute: "surely"); יִסֵּר "discipline". וְלַמָּוֶת לֹא נְתָנָנִי "and to death he has not given me": "over" is not written; "to death" stands first.
- **"I will go into them, and I will praise the LORD" → "I will enter through them, I will give thanks to Yah"** (v19) — Hebrew אָבֹא־בָם אוֹדֶה יָהּ, two imperfects without "and" (they may also be read as wishes, "let me enter … let me give thanks"); הוֹדָה "give thanks to" as v1. Layout: the Hebrew verse has two lines, not three.
- **"This gate of the LORD" → "This is the gate of Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew זֶה־הַשַּׁעַר לַיהוָה, "this is the gate belonging to / for Yahweh"; צַדִּיקִים "righteous ones" (no article) יָבֹאוּ בוֹ "shall enter through it".
- **"I will praise thee: for thou hast heard me" → "I will give thanks to you, for you have answered me"** (v21) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ "I will give thanks to you" (הוֹדָה, as v1, 19, 28); עֲנִיתָנִי "you have answered me" (עָנָה "answer", as v5). וַתְּהִי־לִי לִישׁוּעָה as v14, here of "you".
- **"The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner" → "A stone that the builders rejected has become the rosh pinnah"** (v22) — Hebrew אֶבֶן מָאֲסוּ הַבּוֹנִים הָיְתָה לְרֹאשׁ פִּנָּה: אֶבֶן has no article, and the relative "that" is not written (an English necessity); מָאַס "reject"; רֹאשׁ פִּנָּה, literally "head of a corner" (a cornerstone, or the top stone of the corner: not certain). Quoted in Matt 21:42; Mark 12:10; Luke 20:17; Acts 4:11; 1 Pet 2:7 from the Greek, "the head of the corner"; the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"This is the LORD's doing; it is marvellous in our eyes" → "From Yahweh this has come to be; it is wonderful in our eyes"** (v23) — Hebrew מֵאֵת יְהוָה הָיְתָה זֹּאת, "from (with) Yahweh this has been"; "doing" is not written. הִיא נִפְלָאת "it is wonderful" (פָּלָא, the root of פֶּלֶא "wonder"). Quoted with v22 in Matt 21:42; Mark 12:11.
- **"we will rejoice and be glad in it" → "let us be glad and rejoice in it"** (v24) — Hebrew נָגִילָה וְנִשְׂמְחָה, two cohortatives: גִּיל "be glad" stands first, שָׂמַח "rejoice" second (as the rows for Ps 96). בוֹ "in it" (the day), or "in him" (Yahweh): the form is the same.
- **"Save now, I beseech thee, O LORD: O LORD, I beseech thee, send now prosperity" → "Ah, Yahweh, save, please! Ah, Yahweh, make prosper, please!"** (v25) — Hebrew אָנָּא יְהוָה הוֹשִׁיעָה נָּא אָנָּא יְהוָה הַצְלִיחָה נָּא: אָנָּא "ah", a cry of entreaty (as Neh 1:5, 11); הוֹשִׁיעָה "save" (imperative) with נָא "please"; no object ("us") is written. הִצְלִיחַ "make prosper" (not "give success"; no object is written). הוֹשִׁיעָה נָּא is the source of "hosanna" (Greek ὡσαννά, Matt 21:9; Mark 11:9; John 12:13); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the LORD: we have blessed you out of the house of the LORD" → "Blessed is he who comes in the name of Yahweh; we have blessed you from the house of Yahweh"** (v26) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ הַבָּא בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה, "blessed is the one coming in the name of Yahweh" (the Gospels quote it, Matt 21:9; 23:39). בֵּרַכְנוּכֶם is a perfect, "we have blessed you" (plural "you"); it may be said as the blessing itself ("we bless you").
- **"God is the LORD, which hath shewed us light: bind the sacrifice with cords" → "Yahweh is El, and he has given us light. Bind the feast with ropes"** (v27) — Hebrew אֵל יְהוָה, "El is Yahweh" in the Hebrew order (the predicate stands first: Yahweh is El). וַיָּאֶר לָנוּ, hifil of אוֹר, "and he has given light to us"; "his light" is not written. אִסְרוּ־חַג "bind the feast": חַג is "feast", here of what belongs to it, the feast's victim (KJV "sacrifice"). עֲבֹתִים "ropes" (the word of Exod 28:14; others read "leafy boughs", from memory). How the victim is bound "up to the horns of the altar" is not explained in the text.
- **"Thou art my God, and I will praise thee: thou art my God, I will exalt thee" → "You are my El, and I will give thanks to you; my Elohim, I will extol you"** (v28) — Hebrew אֵלִי אַתָּה וְאוֹדֶךָּ אֱלֹהַי אֲרוֹמְמֶךָּ: two names, אֵלִי "my El" and אֱלֹהַי "my Elohim"; "you are" stands only in the first line. הוֹדָה "give thanks to" as v1; רוֹמֵם "extol" as Exod 15:2.
- **No "O"** (v25) — the Hebrew vocatives have none.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours; the psalm has no heading.
- **Closes the Egyptian Hallel** (Ps 113–118), sung at Passover.
- **v22–23** — quoted in Matt 21:42; Mark 12:10–11; Luke 20:17; Acts 4:11; 1 Pet 2:7.
- **v25–26** — the crowd's cry at the entry into Jerusalem (Matt 21:9; Mark 11:9–10; John 12:13).
