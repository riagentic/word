# Psalm 41

To the overseer. A psalm of David.

[1] Happy is he who acts with insight toward the poor;
in the day of evil ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will rescue him.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will keep him and let him live,
and he shall be called happy in the land;
and do not give him up to the soul of his enemies.

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will sustain him on the couch of languishing;
all his bed you have turned in his sickness.

[4] As for me, I said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, show me favor;
heal my soul, for I have sinned against you."

[5] My enemies say evil of me:
"When will he die, and his name perish?"

[6] And if he comes to see, he speaks emptiness;
his heart gathers wickedness for itself;
he goes out to the street, he speaks.

[7] Together against me they whisper, all who hate me;
against me they devise evil for me:

[8] "A thing ⟦belial|of Beliyyaal|of worthlessness⟧ is poured out on him,
and he who has lain down will not rise again."

[9] Even the man of my ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, in whom I trusted,
who eats my bread,
has made the heel great against me.

[10] And you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, show me favor and raise me up,
and let me repay them.

[11] By this I know that you delight in me:
that my enemy does not shout over me.

[12] And I — in my integrity you have supported me,
and you have set me before you forever.

[13] Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
from the age and to the age.
Amen and amen.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 10, 13) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Blessed is he that considereth the poor" → "Happy is he who acts with insight toward the poor"** (v1) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי מַשְׂכִּיל אֶל־דָּל; אַשְׁרֵי "happy" (never "blessed": that is בָּרוּךְ); הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight, act with insight" (with אֶל: give heed to); דַּל "poor".
- **"the LORD will deliver him in time of trouble" → "in the day of evil Yahweh will rescue him"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם רָעָה יְמַלְּטֵהוּ יְהוָה; רָעָה "evil"; מִלֵּט "rescue" (הִצִּיל is "deliver").
- **"preserve him, and keep him alive; and he shall be blessed upon the earth" → "keep him and let him live, and he shall be called happy in the land"** (v2) — Hebrew יִשְׁמְרֵהוּ וִיחַיֵּהוּ וְאֻשַּׁר בָּאָרֶץ. שָׁמַר "keep"; חִיָּה piel "let live". וְאֻשַּׁר is the qere, pual of אִשֵּׁר "call happy" (the root of אַשְׁרֵי, v1); the ketiv יאשר is "he shall be called happy" without "and". אֶרֶץ "land" (or "earth").
- **"thou wilt not deliver him unto the will of his enemies" → "and do not give him up to the soul of his enemies"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאַל־תִּתְּנֵהוּ בְּנֶפֶשׁ אֹיְבָיו: אַל with the short form is a request (not "you will not"); the Psalm turns to speak to Yahweh. נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (here its craving: "will", "desire" in the sense).
- **"strengthen him upon the bed of languishing: thou wilt make all his bed in his sickness" → "sustain him on the couch of languishing; all his bed you have turned in his sickness"** (v3) — Hebrew יִסְעָדֶנּוּ עַל־עֶרֶשׂ דְּוָי כָּל־מִשְׁכָּבוֹ הָפַכְתָּ בְחָלְיוֹ. עֶרֶשׂ "couch"; דְּוַי "languishing" (root of דָּוָה "be unwell"); מִשְׁכָּב "bed"; הָפַכְתָּ perfect "you have turned" (lit. "turned over": changed his lying from sickness, or overturned it; "make his bed" in KJV); חֳלִי "sickness".
- **"be merciful unto me" → "show me favor"** (v4, 10) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי, from חָנַן "show favor".
- **"Mine enemies speak evil of me, When shall he die" → "My enemies say evil of me: 'When will he die'"** (v5) — Hebrew אוֹיְבַי יֹאמְרוּ רַע לִי; אָמַר "say"; רַע "evil" ("in malice" was an interpretation); וְאָבַד "and perish".
- **"if he come to see me, he speaketh vanity: his heart gathereth iniquity to itself; when he goeth abroad, he telleth it" → "if he comes to see, he speaks emptiness; his heart gathers wickedness for itself; he goes out to the street, he speaks"** (v6) — Hebrew וְאִם־בָּא לִרְאוֹת שָׁוְא יְדַבֵּר לִבּוֹ יִקְבָּץ־אָוֶן לוֹ יֵצֵא לַחוּץ יְדַבֵּר: "me" and "it" are not written; שָׁוְא "emptiness"; אָוֶן "wickedness"; חוּץ "the street, outside"; יְדַבֵּר "he speaks" both times.
- **"against me do they devise my hurt" → "against me they devise evil for me"** (v7) — Hebrew עָלַי יַחְשְׁבוּ רָעָה לִי; חָשַׁב "devise"; רָעָה "evil" ("the worst" was added).
- **"An evil disease, say they, cleaveth fast unto him" → "A thing of Beliyyaal is poured out on him"** (v8) — Hebrew דְּבַר־בְּלִיַּעַל יָצוּק בּוֹ. דָּבָר "thing" (or "word": an evil word spoken over him; the Hebrew does not say which); בְּלִיַּעַל "worthlessness" (token, as 2 Sam 22:5); יָצוּק "poured out" (participle of יָצַק "pour"), בּוֹ "on / in him". "say they" is not in the Hebrew: the quotation marks show the words of v7's whisperers.
- **"he shall rise up no more" → "will not rise again"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא־יוֹסִיף לָקוּם, lit. "will not add to rise".
- **"mine own familiar friend … which did eat of my bread, hath lifted up his heel against me" → "the man of my shalom … who eats my bread, has made the heel great against me"** (v9) — Hebrew גַּם־אִישׁ שְׁלוֹמִי … אוֹכֵל לַחְמִי הִגְדִּיל עָלַי עָקֵב: אִישׁ שְׁלוֹמִי "the man of my shalom" (the token; a friend at peace with me); אוֹכֵל participle "who eats"; הִגְדִּיל עָקֵב lit. "made great the heel" (raised the heel against me), no "his". John 13:18 quotes the verse in a Greek form ("lifted up his heel"; from memory).
- **"that I may requite them" → "and let me repay them"** (v10) — Hebrew וַאֲשַׁלְּמָה לָהֶם, cohortative with "and"; שִׁלֵּם "repay".
- **"I know that thou favourest me, because mine enemy doth not triumph over me" → "I know that you delight in me: that my enemy does not shout over me"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּי־חָפַצְתָּ בִּי כִּי לֹא־יָרִיעַ אֹיְבִי עָלָי; חָפֵץ בְּ "delight in"; the second כִּי "that" (or "because"); הֵרִיעַ "shout" ("in triumph" is the sense, not a word).
- **"And as for me, thou upholdest me in mine integrity, and settest me before thy face for ever" → "And I — in my integrity you have supported me, and you have set me before you forever"** (v12) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי בְּתֻמִּי תָּמַכְתָּ בִּי וַתַּצִּיבֵנִי לְפָנֶיךָ לְעוֹלָם; תֹּם "integrity" (בְּ "in" or "because of"); תָּמַךְ "support"; הִצִּיב "set up".
- **"Blessed be the LORD God of Israel from everlasting, and to everlasting" → "Blessed is Yahweh the Elohim of Israel, from the age and to the age"** (v13) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל מֵהָעוֹלָם וְעַד הָעוֹלָם: no verb, "is" (as 1 Chr 16:36); עוֹלָם with the article both times and וְ before עַד: "from the age and to the age" (the reviewer's row).
- **"Amen, and Amen" → "Amen and Amen"** (v13) — Hebrew אָמֵן וְאָמֵן.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–13 = Heb v2–14.
- **Close of Book I of the Psalter** (Ps 1–41); v13 is the doxology that ends the book.
- **v9 in John 13:18** — applied to the betrayer.
