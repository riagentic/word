# Psalm 123

A song of the ascents.

[1] To you I have lifted up my eyes,
you who dwell in the heavens.

[2] Behold, as the eyes of slaves
to the hand of their lords,
as the eyes of a maidservant
to the hand of her mistress,
so our eyes are to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
until he shows us favor.

[3] Show us favor, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, show us favor,
for greatly we are sated with contempt.

[4] Greatly our soul is sated
with the derision of those at ease,
the contempt of the proud.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 3) — Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"A Song of degrees" → "A song of the ascents"** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת, the first words of v1 (see Ps 120).
- **"Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens" → "To you I have lifted up my eyes, you who dwell in the heavens"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵלֶיךָ נָשָׂאתִי אֶת־עֵינַי, "to you" first, a perfect ("I have lifted up"). הַיֹּשְׁבִי בַּשָּׁמָיִם: יָשַׁב "dwell" (also "sit", of one enthroned: the former "enthroned" chose that sense), with an old ending ־ִי; שָׁמַיִם "heavens". No "O".
- **"as the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters" → "as the eyes of slaves to the hand of their lords"** (v2) — Hebrew עֲבָדִים "slaves" (עֶבֶד) set beside שִׁפְחָה "maidservant"; אֲדוֹנֵיהֶם "their lords" (אָדוֹן; the plural may be the plural of one owner); גְּבִרְתָּהּ "her mistress" (גְּבֶרֶת, as Gen 16:4). No verb is written: "look" is not in the Hebrew; כֵּן עֵינֵינוּ "so our eyes are" ("are" supplied).
- **"until that he have mercy upon us. Have mercy upon us, O LORD, have mercy upon us" → "until he shows us favor. Show us favor, Yahweh, show us favor"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew חָנַן "show favor" (the verb of חֵן "favor"), three times; not "have mercy" (that is רִחַם).
- **"for we are exceedingly filled with contempt" → "for greatly we are sated with contempt"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־רַב שָׂבַעְנוּ בוּז: רַב "greatly, much" stands first; שָׂבַע "be sated" (a perfect, "we have been / are sated"); בּוּז "contempt".
- **"Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease, and with the contempt of the proud" → "Greatly our soul is sated with the derision of those at ease, the contempt of the proud"** (v4) — Hebrew רַבַּת שָׂבְעָה־לָּהּ נַפְשֵׁנוּ: רַבַּת "greatly"; לָּהּ "for itself" (not carried into English, as Ps 120:6); נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (singular: "our soul"). הַלַּעַג הַשַּׁאֲנַנִּים "the derision of those at ease" (לַעַג "derision", as Ps 44:13; שַׁאֲנָן "at ease", as Job 12:5); הַבּוּז לִגְאֵיוֹנִים "the contempt of (belonging to) the proud": לְ, not a construct; no "and" is written.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
