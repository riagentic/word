# Psalm 24

Of David, a psalm.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|The LORD's⟧ is the earth and its fullness,
the world and those who dwell in it;

[2] for he has founded it upon the seas,
and upon the rivers he establishes it.

[3] Who shall go up into the mountain of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?
And who shall stand in his holy place?

[4] One innocent of palms and pure of heart,
who has not lifted up my soul for emptiness
and has not sworn for deceit.

[5] He shall bear a blessing from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and righteousness from the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of his salvation.

[6] This is the generation of those who seek him,
who seek your face — ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧. Selah.

[7] Lift up, gates, your heads,
and be lifted up, everlasting entrances,
and the King of glory shall come in.

[8] Who is this, the King of glory?
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, strong and mighty,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, mighty in war.

[9] Lift up, gates, your heads,
and lift up, everlasting entrances,
and the King of glory shall come in.

[10] Who is he, this King of glory?
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts — he is the King of glory. Selah.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 5, 8, 10) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v10) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "Of David, a psalm"** (heading) — Hebrew לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר, in this order, the first words of v1. לְדָוִד may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to David".
- **"The earth is the LORD's, and the fulness thereof" → "Yahweh's is the earth and its fullness"** (v1) — Hebrew לַיהוָה הָאָרֶץ וּמְלוֹאָהּ, "to Yahweh" first; מְלוֹא "fullness, what fills it" (1 Cor 10:26 quotes the verse).
- **"established it upon the floods" → "upon the rivers he establishes it"** (v2) — Hebrew וְעַל־נְהָרוֹת יְכוֹנְנֶהָ, an imperfect beside the perfect יְסָדָהּ "he has founded it"; it may also be read as past ("established"). נָהָר is "river".
- **"Who shall ascend into the hill of the LORD? or who shall stand" → "Who shall go up into the mountain of Yahweh? And who shall stand"** (v3) — Hebrew יַעֲלֶה (עָלָה "go up"), הַר "mountain", וּמִי "and who".
- **"He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart" → "One innocent of palms and pure of heart"** (v4) — Hebrew נְקִי כַפַּיִם וּבַר־לֵבָב, two adjectives with their nouns; "One" is supplied for English. נָקִי "innocent, clear (of guilt)"; כַּף "palm". **"who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity" → "who has not lifted up my soul for emptiness"** — the printed Hebrew has נַפְשִׁי "my soul": Yahweh speaks of his own soul, as one lifts up the name of Yahweh "for emptiness" in a false oath (Exod 20:7, the same words נָשָׂא … לַשָּׁוְא). Many Hebrew manuscripts and the versions have נַפְשׁוֹ "his soul" (from memory). **"nor sworn deceitfully" → "and has not sworn for deceit"** — Hebrew וְלֹא נִשְׁבַּע לְמִרְמָה, "and not", the same לְ as "for emptiness".
- **"He shall receive the blessing" → "He shall bear a blessing"** (v5) — Hebrew יִשָּׂא בְרָכָה; נָשָׂא is the verb of v4 ("lifted up") and of v7, 9 ("lift up"): "bear, carry, lift". "Receive" is the sense; the verb is kept. בְּרָכָה has no article.
- **"This is the generation of them that seek him" kept** (v6) — qere דֹּרְשָׁיו "those who seek him"; the ketiv דרשו is the singular "who seeks him". דָּרַשׁ and בִּקֵּשׁ ("who seek your face") are two Hebrew verbs; English has one word, "seek". **"that seek thy face, O Jacob" → "who seek your face — Jacob"** — Hebrew מְבַקְשֵׁי פָנֶיךָ יַעֲקֹב. "Jacob" may be the generation itself ("they are Jacob") or a call ("O Jacob"); the dash keeps both. The Greek and the Syriac have "the face of the Elohim of Jacob" (from memory); the earlier text had followed them.
- **"Lift up your heads, O ye gates; and be ye lift up, ye everlasting doors" → "Lift up, gates, your heads, and be lifted up, everlasting entrances"** (v7) — Hebrew שְׂאוּ שְׁעָרִים רָאשֵׁיכֶם וְהִנָּשְׂאוּ פִּתְחֵי עוֹלָם, in this order. פֶּתַח is "entrance" (דֶּלֶת is "door"); עוֹלָם as a genitive is "everlasting", literally "of the age", time out of sight backward or forward ("ancient" names only the past). **"and the King of glory shall come in" kept** — Hebrew וְיָבוֹא; after the commands it may also be read as purpose, "so that … may come in". מֶלֶךְ הַכָּבוֹד is literally "the King of the glory".
- **"Who is this King of glory?" → "Who is this, the King of glory?"** (v8) — Hebrew מִי זֶה מֶלֶךְ הַכָּבוֹד. **"mighty in battle" → "mighty in war"** — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר מִלְחָמָה, literally "a mighty one of war"; עִזּוּז "strong" is found only here and Isa 43:17.
- **"even lift them up, ye everlasting doors" → "and lift up, everlasting entrances"** (v9) — Hebrew וּשְׂאוּ, active, with no object (v7 has the passive "be lifted up"); "them" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Who is this King of glory?" → "Who is he, this King of glory?"** (v10) — Hebrew מִי הוּא זֶה, with "he" (v8 has only זֶה).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **Three parts** — the earth is Yahweh's (v1–2); who may go up (v3–6); the gates and the King (v7–10).
- **v1 quoted by Paul** (1 Cor 10:26).
