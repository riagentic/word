# Psalm 57

To the overseer. ⟦al-tashcheth|Al-tashchet|Do Not Destroy⟧. Of David. A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧, when he fled from the face of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, in the cave.

[1] Show me favor, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, show me favor,
for in you my soul has taken refuge;
and in the shade of your wings I will take refuge,
until destructions pass by.

[2] I will call to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ ⟦elyon|Elyon|Most High⟧,
to ⟦el|El|God⟧ who completes for me.

[3] He will send from the heavens and save me;
he has reproached the one who pants after me. Selah.
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will send his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and his truth.

[4] My soul is in the midst of lionesses;
I will lie down among those who blaze, the sons of man;
their teeth are spear and arrows,
and their tongue a sharp sword.

[5] Be exalted above the heavens, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
over all the earth be your glory.

[6] A net they have prepared for my feet;
he has bowed down my soul;
they have dug a pit before me;
they have fallen into the midst of it. Selah.

[7] My heart is established, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my heart is established;
let me sing and let me make music.

[8] Awake, my glory!
Awake, harp and lyre!
Let me awaken the dawn.

[9] I will give thanks to you among the peoples, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧;
I will make music to you among the folks.

[10] For great to the heavens is your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and to the skies your truth.

[11] Be exalted above the heavens, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
over all the earth be your glory.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"God most high" → "*Elohim* Elyon"** (v2) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים עֶלְיוֹן, *Elyon* "Most High" retained.
- **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי (Ps 108:3 has יהוה here).
- **"To the chief Musician, Altaschith, Michtam of David, when he fled from Saul in the cave" → "To the overseer. Al-tashchet. Of David. A Miktam, when he fled from the face of Saul, in the cave."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת לְדָוִד מִכְתָּם בְּבָרְחוֹ מִפְּנֵי־שָׁאוּל בַּמְּעָרָה, the first verse of the Hebrew. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21; traditional "to the chief musician"). אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת "do not ruin / destroy" (hifil of שָׁחַת), kept as a name: probably a tune; its use here is not known (no עַל "upon" stands before it). לְדָוִד "of David": the לְ may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" David. *Miktam* is retained: its meaning is not known. בָּרַח מִפְּנֵי "flee from the face of".
- **"Be merciful unto me … for my soul trusteth in thee: yea, in the shadow of thy wings will I make my refuge, until these calamities be overpast" → "Show me favor … for in you my soul has taken refuge; and in the shade of your wings I will take refuge, until destructions pass by"** (v1) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי, from חָנַן "show favor"; חָסָיָה, perfect of חָסָה "take refuge", then the imperfect אֶחְסֶה of the same verb; צֵל "shade"; הַוּוֹת plural "destructions" (no "these"), with a singular verb יַעֲבֹר.
- **"unto God that performeth all things for me" → "to El who completes for me"** (v2) — Hebrew לָאֵל גֹּמֵר עָלָי, "to El completing upon me": גָּמַר "complete, bring to an end" has no object ("all things", "his purpose" are supplied in versions). אֶקְרָא "I will call".
- **"He shall send from heaven, and save me from the reproach of him that would swallow me up" → "He will send from the heavens and save me; he has reproached the one who pants after me"** (v3) — Hebrew חֵרֵף שֹׁאֲפִי, perfect of חֵרֵף "reproach" (no "from"): with Elohim as subject, "he has reproached the one who pants after me"; it may also be read "the one who pants after me has reproached" (subject after the verb). Not certain. שֹׁאֲפִי from שָׁאַף "pant after" (as 56:1–2). **"his mercy and his truth" → "his chesed and his truth"** — Hebrew חַסְדּוֹ וַאֲמִתּוֹ; אֱמֶת "truth" (also v10).
- **"My soul is among lions: and I lie even among them that are set on fire, even the sons of men" → "My soul is in the midst of lionesses; I will lie down among those who blaze, the sons of man"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ לְבָאִם "in the midst of lionesses" (לָבִיא, rendered "lioness" as Num 23:24; its gender is not certain); אֶשְׁכְּבָה cohortative "I will lie down"; לֹהֲטִים "blazing ones" (לָהַט "blaze, devour"), with no preposition: "among" is supplied; בְּנֵי־אָדָם "sons of man". The lines follow the Hebrew accents. **"spears" → "spear"** — Hebrew חֲנִית singular; לְשׁוֹנָם "their tongue", singular.
- **"Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens; let thy glory be above all the earth" → "Be exalted above the heavens, Elohim; over all the earth be your glory"** (v5, 11) — Hebrew רוּמָה … עַל כָּל־הָאָרֶץ כְּבוֹדֶךָ: the second line has no verb ("be" is supplied). v5 has עַל־הַשָּׁמַיִם with the article, v11 עַל־שָׁמַיִם without it; English cannot show the difference.
- **"They have prepared a net for my steps; my soul is bowed down: they have digged a pit before me, into the midst whereof they are fallen themselves" → "A net they have prepared for my feet; he has bowed down my soul; they have dug a pit before me; they have fallen into the midst of it"** (v6) — Hebrew רֶשֶׁת הֵכִינוּ לִפְעָמַי (פַּעַם "foot, step"; as 58:10); כָּפַף נַפְשִׁי, active, masculine singular: "he has bowed down my soul", no subject named (the soul is feminine and is the object); כָּרָה "dig"; שִׁיחָה "pit"; נָפְלוּ בְתוֹכָהּ, no "themselves".
- **"My heart is fixed, O God, my heart is fixed: I will sing and give praise" → "My heart is established, Elohim, my heart is established; let me sing and let me make music"** (v7) — Hebrew נָכוֹן לִבִּי "my heart is established" (nifal of כּוּן); אָשִׁירָה וַאֲזַמֵּרָה, cohortatives; זִמֵּר "make music". Ps 108:1 has נָכוֹן לִבִּי once and adds אַף־כְּבוֹדִי "also my glory".
- **"Awake up, my glory; awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake early" → "Awake, my glory! Awake, harp and lyre! Let me awaken the dawn"** (v8) — Hebrew עוּרָה הַנֵּבֶל וְכִנּוֹר (נֵבֶל "harp", כִּנּוֹר "lyre"; the kinds are uncertain; the harp has the article); אָעִירָה שָּׁחַר, cohortative hifil "let me awaken the dawn" (שַׁחַר "dawn" is the object). Ps 108:2 lacks "Awake, my glory".
- **"among the people … among the nations" → "among the peoples … among the folks"** (v9) — Hebrew בָעַמִּים "among the peoples"; בַּל־אֻמִּים is written as two words (בַּל "not"); it is read as בַּלְאֻמִּים, "among the folks" (לְאֹם), as also Ps 108:3; uncertain. אוֹדְךָ "I will give thanks to you"; אֲזַמֶּרְךָ "I will make music to you".
- **"For thy mercy is great unto the heavens, and thy truth unto the clouds" → "For great to the heavens is your chesed, and to the skies your truth"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי־גָדֹל עַד־שָׁמַיִם חַסְדֶּךָ, Hebrew order; שְׁחָקִים "skies" (as 2 Sam 22:12). Ps 108:4 has מֵעַל־שָׁמַיִם "above the heavens".
- **Ps 108** — v7–11 = Ps 108:1–5; the differences are named at each verse; nothing is levelled.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–11 = Heb v2–12.
- **1 Sam 22; 24** — David in the cave.
- **Poetic structure** — lament from the cave (v1–6) turning to praise; refrain v5 = v11.
