# Psalm 105

[1] Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, call on his name;
make known among the peoples his deeds.

[2] Sing to him, make music to him;
speak of all his wonderful deeds.

[3] Glory in his holy name;
let the heart of those who seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ rejoice.

[4] Seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and his strength;
seek his face continually.

[5] Remember his wonderful deeds that he has done,
his wonders and the judgments of his mouth,

[6] seed of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ his servant,
sons of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, his chosen ones.

[7] He is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
in all the earth are his judgments.

[8] He has remembered forever his covenant,
the word he commanded for a thousand generations,

[9] which he cut with ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧,
and his oath to ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧;

[10] and he made it stand for ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ as a statute,
for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ an everlasting covenant,

[11] saying, "To you I will give the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧,
the portion of your inheritance,"

[12] when they were few men,
very few, and sojourners in it.

[13] And they walked about from nation to nation,
from a kingdom to another people.

[14] He did not let a man extort from them,
and he reproved kings on account of them:

[15] "Do not touch my ⟦anointed|meshichim|anointed ones⟧,
and to my prophets do no evil."

[16] And he called a famine upon the land;
every staff of bread he broke.

[17] He sent before them a man;
as a slave ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ was sold.

[18] They afflicted his foot with the fetter;
into iron came his soul,

[19] until the time that his word came;
the saying of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ refined him.

[20] A king sent and loosed him,
a ruler of peoples, and set him free.

[21] He set him lord to his house
and ruler in all his property,

[22] to bind his princes by his soul,
and his elders he would make wise.

[23] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ sojourned in the land of ⟦ham|Cham|Ham⟧.

[24] And he made his people very fruitful,
and made them mightier than their adversaries.

[25] He turned their heart to hate his people,
to plot against his servants.

[26] He sent ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ his servant,
⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, whom he had chosen.

[27] They set among them the words of his signs,
and wonders in the land of ⟦ham|Cham|Ham⟧.

[28] He sent darkness, and made it dark;
and they did not rebel against his word.

[29] He turned their waters to blood,
and put their fish to death.

[30] Their land swarmed with frogs,
in the chambers of their kings.

[31] He said, and a swarm came,
gnats in all their territory.

[32] He gave hail for their rains,
fire of flames in their land.

[33] And he struck their vine and their fig tree,
and broke in pieces the trees of their territory.

[34] He said, and locusts came,
and hopping locusts, and without number;

[35] and they ate every plant in their land,
and they ate the fruit of their ground.

[36] And he struck every firstborn in their land,
the beginning of all their vigor.

[37] And he brought them out with silver and gold,
and there was none in his tribes who stumbled.

[38] ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ rejoiced when they went out,
for the dread of them had fallen upon them.

[39] He spread a cloud for a screen,
and fire to give light by night.

[40] They asked, and he brought quail,
and with bread of the heavens he sated them.

[41] He opened a rock, and waters flowed;
they went in the dry places, a river.

[42] For he remembered his holy word,
⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ his servant.

[43] And he brought out his people with joy,
with a ringing cry his chosen ones.

[44] And he gave them the lands of nations,
and the toil of folks they possessed,

[45] so that they might keep his statutes
and guard his ⟦torah|torot|laws⟧.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 4, 7, 19) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **v1–15 = 1 Chr 16:8–22** — the same Hebrew has the same wording as there (the fixed song row). The Psalm differs from Chronicles in v5 (פִיו for פִיהוּ, "his mouth", the same sense), v6, 8, 9, 12, 13, 14 and 15; each is noted below. Nothing is levelled.
- **"O give thanks unto the LORD; call upon his name: make known his deeds among the people" → "Give thanks to Yahweh, call on his name; make known among the peoples his deeds"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹדוּ … קִרְאוּ בִּשְׁמוֹ הוֹדִיעוּ בָעַמִּים עֲלִילוֹתָיו, in the Hebrew order; עַמִּים "peoples" (plural). No "O" is written.
- **"sing psalms unto him: talk ye of all his wondrous works" → "make music to him; speak of all his wonderful deeds"** (v2) — Hebrew זַמְּרוּ־לוֹ; שִׂיחוּ covers speaking and musing ("speak of" as 1 Chr 16:9; elsewhere in the Psalms the verb is "muse"); נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds" (also v5). **"wonders"** (v5) is מֹפְתִים, as v27.
- **"Glory ye"** (v3) — Hebrew הִתְהַלְלוּ, the reflexive of "praise". **"let the heart of them rejoice that seek"** — לֵב "heart" is singular.
- **"Seek … seek"** (v4) — two Hebrew verbs, דָּרַשׁ and בִּקֵּשׁ; English has one word. **"his face evermore" → "his face continually"** — Hebrew פָּנָיו תָּמִיד.
- **"O ye seed of Abraham his servant, ye children of Jacob his chosen" → "seed of Abraham his servant, sons of Jacob, his chosen ones"** (v6) — Hebrew זֶרַע אַבְרָהָם עַבְדּוֹ בְּנֵי יַעֲקֹב בְּחִירָיו; 1 Chr 16:13 has "Israel" for "Abraham". "his servant" is singular; "his chosen ones" plural.
- **"He hath remembered his covenant for ever" → "He has remembered forever his covenant"** (v8) — Hebrew זָכַר, a perfect, in the Hebrew order; 1 Chr 16:15 has the command זִכְרוּ "Remember". **"the word which he commanded to a thousand generations"** — Hebrew דָּבָר צִוָּה לְאֶלֶף דּוֹר.
- **"which covenant he made" → "which he cut"** (v9) — Hebrew כָּרַת, the usual verb for making a covenant; "covenant" is not repeated. The Psalm spells Isaac יִשְׂחָק (as Jer 33:26; Amos 7:9, 16); 1 Chr 16:16 has יִצְחָק. The name is the same.
- **"confirmed the same unto Jacob for a law" → "he made it stand for Jacob as a statute"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמִידֶהָ לְיַעֲקֹב לְחֹק; חֹק "statute". **"everlasting"** — lit. "a covenant of the age".
- **"the lot of your inheritance" → "the portion of your inheritance"** (v11) — Hebrew חֶבֶל, lit. "cord" (the measuring line). "To you" is singular, "your" plural.
- **"When they were but a few men in number; yea, very few, and strangers in it" → "when they were few men, very few, and sojourners in it"** (v12) — Hebrew בִּהְיוֹתָם מְתֵי מִסְפָּר, lit. "men of number"; כִּמְעַט, lit. "as a little"; גֵּרִים "sojourners". 1 Chr 16:19 has "you" (בִּהְיוֹתְכֶם), the Psalm "they".
- **"When they went from one nation to another" → "And they walked about from nation to nation"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּתְהַלְּכוּ, a new clause with "and", the hitpael "walk about". **"from a kingdom"** — the Psalm has no "and" here (מִמַּמְלָכָה); 1 Chr 16:20 has וּמִמַּמְלָכָה.
- **"He suffered no man to do them wrong: yea, he reproved kings for their sakes" → "He did not let a man extort from them, and he reproved kings on account of them"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹא־הִנִּיחַ אָדָם לְעָשְׁקָם; the Psalm has אָדָם "a man, a human", 1 Chr 16:21 לְאִישׁ. עָשַׁק "extort, oppress". וַיּוֹכַח "and he reproved".
- **"Saying, Touch not mine anointed, and do my prophets no harm" → ""Do not touch my *meshichim*, and to my prophets do no evil""** (v15) — Hebrew אַל־תִּגְּעוּ בִמְשִׁיחָי וְלִנְבִיאַי אַל־תָּרֵעוּ; מְשִׁיחִים "anointed ones" (plural); אַל־תָּרֵעוּ "do no evil". "Saying" is not written. The Psalm has לִנְבִיאַי "to my prophets", 1 Chr 16:22 בִּנְבִיאַי; the sense is the same.
- **"Moreover he called for a famine upon the land: he brake the whole staff of bread" → "And he called a famine upon the land; every staff of bread he broke"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא רָעָב עַל־הָאָרֶץ, "and he called"; כָּל־מַטֵּה־לֶחֶם שָׁבָר, "every staff of bread" first, as in the Hebrew; "staff of bread" as Lev 26:26 (the bread a life leans on).
- **"He sent a man before them, even Joseph, who was sold for a servant" → "He sent before them a man; as a slave Joseph was sold"** (v17) — Hebrew שָׁלַח לִפְנֵיהֶם אִישׁ לְעֶבֶד נִמְכַּר יוֹסֵף, in the Hebrew order; "sent … before" as Gen 45:5. עֶבֶד "slave" (as Joseph's sale, Gen 37:28).
- **"Whose feet they hurt with fetters: he was laid in iron" → "They afflicted his foot with the fetter; into iron came his soul"** (v18) — Hebrew עִנּוּ בַכֶּבֶל רַגְלוֹ בַּרְזֶל בָּאָה נַפְשׁוֹ. The qere רַגְלוֹ "his foot" is followed; the written form (ketiv) רגליו is "his feet". עִנָּה "afflict"; כֶּבֶל "fetter" (only here and Ps 149:8). נֶפֶשׁ "soul": many take it here as "neck" or "his person" (an iron collar); the Hebrew word is "soul". "into" is not written: "iron his soul came".
- **"Until the time that his word came: the word of the LORD tried him" → "until the time that his word came; the saying of Yahweh refined him"** (v19) — Hebrew עַד־עֵת בֹּא־דְבָרוֹ אִמְרַת יְהוָה צְרָפָתְהוּ. "his word" does not say whose: Joseph's word (his dreams and their reading) or Yahweh's. אִמְרָה "saying" (another word than דָּבָר); צָרַף "refine" (of metal; as 2 Sam 22:31 "the saying of Yahweh is refined").
- **"The king sent and loosed him; even the ruler of the people, and let him go free" → "A king sent and loosed him, a ruler of peoples, and set him free"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁלַח מֶלֶךְ וַיַּתִּירֵהוּ מֹשֵׁל עַמִּים וַיְפַתְּחֵהוּ: "king" and "ruler" have no article; עַמִּים "peoples". הִתִּיר "loose" (as of bonds); פִּתַּח, lit. "open" (unbind).
- **"He made him lord of his house, and ruler of all his substance" → "He set him lord to his house and ruler in all his property"** (v21) — Hebrew שָׂמוֹ אָדוֹן לְבֵיתוֹ וּמֹשֵׁל בְּכָל־קִנְיָנוֹ; "lord to … ruler in" as Gen 45:8. קִנְיָן "property" (what one has got).
- **"To bind his princes at his pleasure; and teach his senators wisdom" → "to bind his princes by his soul, and his elders he would make wise"** (v22) — Hebrew לֶאְסֹר שָׂרָיו בְּנַפְשׁוֹ: בְּנַפְשׁוֹ, lit. "by / in his soul", means "at his will" (as "according to your soul", Deut 23:24). The verb is אָסַר "bind" (the Greek "to instruct" reads another verb, from memory; not followed). וּזְקֵנָיו יְחַכֵּם, "and his elders he would make wise" (חִכֵּם "make wise", an imperfect); זָקֵן "elder".
- **"Israel also came into Egypt" → "And Israel came to Egypt"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיָּבֹא יִשְׂרָאֵל מִצְרָיִם. **"sojourned"** — Hebrew גָּר (גּוּר "sojourn", as Gen 47:4). "the land of Ham" (also v27): Egypt, named after Ham (Gen 10:6).
- **"And he increased his people greatly; and made them stronger than their enemies" → "And he made his people very fruitful, and made them mightier than their adversaries"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיֶּפֶר אֶת־עַמּוֹ מְאֹד וַיַּעֲצִמֵהוּ מִצָּרָיו: no name is written (the former verse had "Yahweh", which is not in the Hebrew); הִפְרָה "make fruitful", הֶעֱצִים "make mighty" (as Exod 1:7 "were fruitful … became mighty"); צַר "adversary". "him … his" are singular in the Hebrew, of the people.
- **"He turned their heart to hate his people, to deal subtilly with his servants" → "to plot against his servants"** (v25) — Hebrew לְהִתְנַכֵּל בַּעֲבָדָיו, as Gen 37:18 "plotted against him". לִבָּם "their heart" is singular.
- **"and Aaron whom he had chosen" → "Aaron, whom he had chosen"** (v26) — Hebrew אַהֲרֹן אֲשֶׁר בָּחַר־בּוֹ, with no "and".
- **"They shewed his signs among them" → "They set among them the words of his signs"** (v27) — Hebrew שָׂמוּ־בָם דִּבְרֵי אֹתוֹתָיו: שָׂם "set, put"; דִּבְרֵי "words of" (or "matters of", the things of his signs; the word is kept). The verb is plural, of Moses and Aaron (the Greek and Syriac have "he set", from memory; not followed). מוֹפְתִים "wonders".
- **"and they rebelled not against his word"** (v28) — so the Masoretic Text, וְלֹא־מָרוּ אֶת־דְּבָרוֹ: "they" are most likely Moses and Aaron, who did as they were told; the Greek has "they rebelled against his words", without "not" (from memory). The MT is translated. The qere דְּבָרוֹ "his word" is followed; the ketiv דברוו is "his words". מָרָה "rebel against". **"made it dark"** — Hebrew וַיַּחְשִׁךְ, with no object.
- **"slew their fish" → "put their fish to death"** (v29) — Hebrew וַיָּמֶת, the causative of "die". **"into blood" → "to blood"** — הָפַךְ … לְדָם, as Exod 7:20.
- **"Their land brought forth frogs in abundance" → "Their land swarmed with frogs"** (v30) — Hebrew שָׁרַץ אַרְצָם צְפַרְדְּעִים, the verb of Exod 8:3. No "even" is written before "in the chambers".
- **"He spake, and there came divers sorts of flies, and lice in all their coasts" → "He said, and a swarm came, gnats in all their territory"** (v31) — Hebrew אָמַר "he said" (also v34); עָרֹב "swarm", without the article here (the plague of Exod 8:21–31; meaning uncertain: a mixture of insects, or of beasts; "of flies" is not in the word); כִּנִּים "gnats" (uncertain: gnats, mosquitoes or lice, as Exod 8:16–18); גְּבוּל "border", here the land within it, "territory" (also v33).
- **"He gave them hail for rain, and flaming fire in their land" → "He gave hail for their rains, fire of flames in their land"** (v32) — Hebrew נָתַן גִּשְׁמֵיהֶם בָּרָד, lit. "he gave their rains hail" (made their rains hail; "for" is supplied; "them" is not written); אֵשׁ לֶהָבוֹת "fire of flames" (as the fire in the hail, Exod 9:23–24).
- **"He smote their vines also and their fig trees; and brake the trees of their coasts" → "And he struck their vine and their fig tree, and broke in pieces the trees of their territory"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ גַּפְנָם וּתְאֵנָתָם וַיְשַׁבֵּר עֵץ גְּבוּלָם: "vine", "fig tree" and "tree" are singular (collectives); English keeps the first two. שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces", as Exod 9:25 "every tree of the field it broke in pieces".
- **"the locusts came, and caterpillers, and that without number" → "locusts came, and hopping locusts, and without number"** (v34) — Hebrew אַרְבֶּה "locusts" (a collective, as Exod 10); יֶלֶק, a kind or stage of locust, "hopping locust" (uncertain; also Joel 1:4; 2:25; Nah 3:15, 16); וְאֵין מִסְפָּר, lit. "and there was no number".
- **"And did eat up all the herbs in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground" → "and they ate every plant in their land, and they ate the fruit of their ground"** (v35) — Hebrew וַיֹּאכַל … וַיֹּאכַל, one verb twice (singular, of the locusts as one); עֵשֶׂב "plant", as Exod 10:12, 15; אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"He smote also all the firstborn in their land, the chief of all their strength" → "And he struck every firstborn in their land, the beginning of all their vigor"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ כָּל־בְּכוֹר (as Exod 12:29 "struck every firstborn"); רֵאשִׁית לְכָל־אוֹנָם, "the beginning of my vigor" as Gen 49:3 (the first sons of their manhood).
- **"He brought them forth also with silver and gold: and there was not one feeble person among their tribes" → "And he brought them out with silver and gold, and there was none in his tribes who stumbled"** (v37) — Hebrew וַיּוֹצִיאֵם: "them", no name (the former verse had "Israel", which is not written); כּוֹשֵׁל "one who stumbles"; בִּשְׁבָטָיו "in his tribes" (singular "his", of the people).
- **"Egypt was glad when they departed: for the fear of them fell upon them" → "Egypt rejoiced when they went out, for the dread of them had fallen upon them"** (v38) — Hebrew שָׂמַח מִצְרַיִם, a singular verb: "Egypt" (token `egypt`); then עֲלֵיהֶם "upon them", plural, of the Egyptians: the one Hebrew word is treated first as one, then as many. בְּצֵאתָם "in their going out"; פַּחַד "dread".
- **"He spread a cloud for a covering; and fire to give light in the night" → "He spread a cloud for a screen, and fire to give light by night"** (v39) — Hebrew פָּרַשׂ עָנָן לְמָסָךְ; מָסָךְ "screen" (the word of the screen of the tent); לְהָאִיר "to give light", as Exod 13:21.
- **"The people asked" → "They asked"** (v40) — Hebrew שָׁאַל, a singular verb with no subject (the people as one, or "one asked"); "the people" is not written (KJV marks it as supplied), and English "they" is used for the singular, as in v35. Many read the plural שָׁאֲלוּ (from memory). **"and satisfied them with the bread of heaven" → "and with bread of the heavens he sated them"** — Hebrew וְלֶחֶם שָׁמַיִם יַשְׂבִּיעֵם, "bread" first; שָׁמַיִם "heavens"; הִשְׂבִּיעַ "sate" (as Exod 16:12 "be sated with bread").
- **"He opened the rock, and the waters gushed out; they ran in the dry places like a river" → "He opened a rock, and waters flowed; they went in the dry places, a river"** (v41) — Hebrew פָּתַח צוּר וַיָּזוּבוּ מָיִם הָלְכוּ בַּצִּיּוֹת נָהָר: "rock" without the article; זוּב "flow"; הָלְכוּ "they went"; צִיּוֹת, plural of צִיָּה "dryness": "dry places"; נָהָר "a river" stands with no "like".
- **"his holy promise, and Abraham his servant" → "his holy word, Abraham his servant"** (v42) — Hebrew אֶת־דְּבַר קָדְשׁוֹ אֶת־אַבְרָהָם עַבְדּוֹ: דָּבָר "word" (lit. "the word of his holiness"); no "and" is written. The second אֶת can mark a second object ("remembered … Abraham") or mean "with" ("his holy word with Abraham").
- **"with joy, and his chosen with gladness" → "with joy, with a ringing cry his chosen ones"** (v43) — Hebrew בְשָׂשׂוֹן בְּרִנָּה אֶת־בְּחִירָיו, no "and"; שָׂשׂוֹן "joy", רִנָּה "a ringing cry"; "his chosen ones" plural, as v6.
- **"the lands of the heathen: and they inherited the labour of the people" → "the lands of nations, and the toil of folks they possessed"** (v44) — Hebrew אַרְצוֹת גּוֹיִם וַעֲמַל לְאֻמִּים יִירָשׁוּ: גּוֹיִם "nations", לְאֻמִּים "folks", both without the article; עָמָל "toil"; יָרַשׁ "possess". "the fruit of" (former wording) is not written.
- **"That they might observe his statutes, and keep his laws" → "so that they might keep his statutes and guard his *torot*"** (v45) — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר, here a conjunction, "so that"; שָׁמַר "keep", נָצַר "guard"; תּוֹרֹת, plural of *torah*.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v45) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ, "praise Yah", kept as the one word (token `praise-the-lord`).

### Notes
- **No superscription** in the Masoretic Text; the Hebrew verse numbers are ours. The Psalm ends with Hallelujah, and Ps 106 begins with it.
- **Historical psalm** — the covenant with the fathers (v8–15), Joseph (v16–22), Israel in Egypt and the plagues (v23–36), the going out and the wilderness (v37–41), the land (v42–45). The plagues are told in another order than Exodus, and not all are named.
