# Psalm 19

To the overseer. A psalm of David.

[1] The heavens recount the glory of ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and the work of his hands the expanse tells.

[2] Day to day pours out speech,
and night to night declares knowledge.

[3] There is no speech, and there are no words;
their voice is not heard.

[4] Through all the earth their line has gone out,
and to the end of the world their words.
For the sun he has put a tent in them.

[5] And it is like a bridegroom coming out from his chamber;
it exults like a mighty one to run the path.

[6] From the end of the heavens is its going out,
and its circuit is to their ends,
and there is nothing hidden from its heat.

[7] The ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is blameless, bringing back the soul;
the testimony of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is sure, making wise the simple.

[8] The precepts of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are right, gladdening the heart;
the commandment of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is pure, enlightening the eyes.

[9] The fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is clean, standing forever;
the judgments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are truth, they are righteous together.

[10] More desirable are they than gold, and than much fine gold,
and sweeter than honey and the drippings of the honeycombs.

[11] Also your servant is warned by them;
in keeping them is great reward.

[12] Errors — who can discern?
From what is hidden clear me.

[13] Also from the presumptuous keep back your servant;
let them not rule over me; then I shall be blameless,
and I shall be clear of great transgression.

[14] Let the sayings of my mouth be for acceptance,
and the meditation of my heart before you,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, my Rock and my redeemer.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ, the participle of נִצַּח "oversee" (1 Chr 15:21); "chief musician" is the traditional rendering. לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open. The heading is verse 1 of the Hebrew.
- **"God" → "El"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"declare … sheweth his handywork" → "recount … the work of his hands the expanse tells"** (v1) — Hebrew מְסַפְּרִים (סִפֵּר "recount"), מַגִּיד (הִגִּיד "tell"); מַעֲשֵׂה יָדָיו "the work of his hands". **"firmament" → "expanse"** — Hebrew רָקִיעַ, as Gen 1:6. The second line ends with its subject, as in the Hebrew.
- **"uttereth … sheweth" → "pours out … declares"** (v2) — Hebrew יַבִּיעַ "make bubble out, pour out"; יְחַוֶּה "declare, show".
- **"There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard" → "There is no speech, and there are no words; their voice is not heard"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵין־אֹמֶר וְאֵין דְּבָרִים בְּלִי נִשְׁמָע קוֹלָם, literally "without being heard is their voice": the heavens speak without speech. "Where" is not in the Hebrew. אֹמֶר is a form of the noun "saying" (אֵמֶר, v14); "speech" here, because "no saying" would mean "no proverb".
- **"Their line is gone out"** kept (v4) — Hebrew קַוָּם "their (measuring) line". The Septuagint has "their sound" (from memory), which Rom 10:18 quotes; the MT is kept. **"In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun" → "For the sun he has put a tent in them"** — Hebrew לַשֶּׁמֶשׁ שָׂם־אֹהֶל בָּהֶם, in this order; אֹהֶל "tent".
- **"Which is as a bridegroom" → "And it is like a bridegroom"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהוּא, "and he": the sun is masculine in Hebrew. **"rejoiceth as a strong man to run a race" → "exults like a mighty one to run the path"** — Hebrew יָשִׂישׂ (שׂוּשׂ "exult"); גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; אֹרַח "path".
- **"His going forth is from the end of the heaven, and his circuit unto the ends of it" → "From the end of the heavens is its going out, and its circuit is to their ends"** (v6) — Hebrew מוֹצָאוֹ "its going out"; עַל־קְצוֹתָם, plural, "their" = the heavens'.
- **"perfect, converting the soul" → "blameless, bringing back the soul"** (v7) — Hebrew תְּמִימָה, literally "whole" (the root of אֵיתָם, v13); מְשִׁיבַת נֶפֶשׁ, hifil of שׁוּב "bring back, restore".
- **"rejoicing the heart" → "gladdening the heart"** (v8) — Hebrew מְשַׂמְּחֵי־לֵב, piel of שָׂמַח. **"right"** kept — Hebrew יְשָׁרִים, of things ("upright" is said of persons).
- **"enduring for ever" → "standing forever"** (v9) — Hebrew עוֹמֶדֶת לָעַד. **"the judgments of the LORD are true and righteous altogether" → "are truth, they are righteous together"** — Hebrew אֱמֶת, a noun; צָדְקוּ יַחְדָּו, a verb, "they are righteous", and "together".
- **"More to be desired are they"** (v10) — Hebrew הַנֶּחֱמָדִים, with the article, "the desirable ones"; "they are" is supplied. **"sweeter also than honey and the honeycomb" → "and sweeter than honey and the drippings of the honeycombs"** — Hebrew וּמְתוּקִים "and sweet(er)": no "also"; נֹפֶת צוּפִים, honey dripping from the combs, plural.
- **"Moreover by them is thy servant warned" → "Also your servant is warned by them"** (v11, 13) — Hebrew גַּם; עַבְדְּךָ, the word is "slave". **"great reward"** kept — Hebrew עֵקֶב רָב; עֵקֶב is literally "heel": what follows on a thing, its outcome.
- **"Who can understand his errors?" → "Errors — who can discern?"** (v12) — Hebrew שְׁגִיאוֹת מִי־יָבִין: the noun stands first; "his" is not in the Hebrew. **"cleanse thou me from secret faults" → "From what is hidden clear me"** — Hebrew מִנִּסְתָּרוֹת "from hidden (things)": "faults" is not written; נַקֵּנִי, piel of נָקָה, "hold guiltless, clear".
- **"Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins" → "Also from the presumptuous keep back your servant"** (v13) — Hebrew זֵדִים, a plural adjective, "presumptuous ones": proud men or presumptuous sins, both possible; "sins" is not written. חֲשֹׂךְ "withhold, keep back". **"have dominion" → "rule"** — Hebrew מָשַׁל. **"upright … innocent from the great transgression" → "blameless … clear of great transgression"** — Hebrew אֵיתָם (תָּמַם "be whole"); וְנִקֵּיתִי (נָקָה "be clear"); פֶּשַׁע רָב has no article.
- **"Let the words of my mouth … be acceptable in thy sight" → "Let the sayings of my mouth be for acceptance … before you"** (v14) — Hebrew אִמְרֵי־פִי (אֵמֶר "saying"); לְרָצוֹן "for acceptance"; לְפָנֶיךָ "before you". **"meditation"** kept — Hebrew הֶגְיוֹן, a noun of הָגָה "softly recite" (Ps 1:2), a low sounding; "of my heart" makes it inward. **"my strength" → "my Rock"** — Hebrew צוּרִי; גֹּאֲלִי "my redeemer".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v14 = Heb v15.
- **Two revelation themes** — general (creation, v1-6) and special (Torah, v7-11).
- **Famous opening** — "The heavens recount the glory of El."
- **Benediction** — v14 used widely in worship.
