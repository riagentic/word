# Psalm 107

[1] Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he is good,
for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever.

[2] Let the redeemed of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ say,
whom he has redeemed from the hand of the adversary,

[3] and from the lands he has gathered them,
from the east and from the west,
from the north and from the sea.

[4] They wandered in the wilderness, in a desert way;
a city of dwelling they did not find.

[5] Hungry, thirsty as well,
their soul within them was fainting.

[6] And they cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in their distress;
from their straits he delivered them.

[7] And he made them tread in a straight way,
to go to a city of dwelling.

[8] Let them give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧
and his wonderful deeds to the sons of man.

[9] For he has satisfied the longing soul,
and the hungry soul he has filled with good.

[10] Those who dwelt in darkness and the shadow of death,
prisoners of affliction and iron,

[11] for they had rebelled against the sayings of ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and the counsel of ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ they had spurned.

[12] And he brought low their heart with toil;
they stumbled, and there was no helper.

[13] And they cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in their distress;
from their straits he saved them.

[14] He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death,
and their bonds he snapped.

[15] Let them give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧
and his wonderful deeds to the sons of man.

[16] For he has broken the doors of bronze,
and the bars of iron he has hewn through.

[17] Fools, because of the way of their transgression,
and because of their iniquities, were afflicted.

[18] All food their soul abominated,
and they reached as far as the gates of death.

[19] And they cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in their distress;
from their straits he saved them.

[20] He sent his word and healed them,
and rescued them from their pits.

[21] Let them give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧
and his wonderful deeds to the sons of man.

[22] And let them sacrifice sacrifices of thanksgiving,
and recount his works with a ringing cry.

[23] Those who go down to the sea in ships,
doing work on many waters,

[24] they have seen the works of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and his wonderful deeds in the depth.

[25] And he said, and set up a storm wind,
and it raised high its waves.

[26] They went up to the heavens; they went down to the deeps;
their soul melted away in evil.

[27] They reeled and swayed like the drunkard,
and all their wisdom was swallowed up.

[28] And they cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in their distress,
and from their straits he brought them out.

[29] He set the storm to a stillness,
and their waves were silent.

[30] And they were glad because they grew quiet,
and he led them to the haven of their delight.

[31] Let them give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧
and his wonderful deeds to the sons of man.

[32] And let them exalt him in the assembly of the people,
and in the seat of the elders let them praise him.

[33] He makes rivers into a wilderness,
and outlets of water into thirsty ground,

[34] a land of fruit into a salt land,
because of the evil of those who dwell in it.

[35] He makes a wilderness into a pool of water,
and a land of dryness into outlets of water.

[36] And he makes the hungry dwell there,
and they establish a city of dwelling.

[37] And they sow fields and plant vineyards,
and they make fruit of yield.

[38] And he blesses them, and they multiply greatly,
and their beasts he does not make few.

[39] And they become few and are bowed down
through restraint, evil and sorrow.

[40] He pours contempt on nobles,
and makes them wander in a waste without a way.

[41] And he sets the needy on high from affliction,
and makes clans like the flock.

[42] The upright see and are glad,
and all injustice shuts its mouth.

[43] Who is wise? Then let him keep these things,
and let them consider the ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|steadfast love⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 6, 8, 13, 15, 19, 21, 24, 28, 31, 43) — Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "El"** (v11) — Hebrew אֵל. **"the most High" → "Elyon"** (v11) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן.
- **"O give thanks unto the LORD, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever" → "Give thanks to Yahweh, for he is good, for his chesed is forever"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹדוּ לַיהוָה כִּי־טוֹב כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ, the refrain of 1 Chr 16:34; Ps 106:1; 118:1; 136, in the same words: no verb in the second line ("is", not "endures"); no "O".
- **"Let the redeemed of the LORD say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy" → "Let the redeemed of Yahweh say, whom he has redeemed from the hand of the adversary"** (v2) — Hebrew יֹאמְרוּ, jussive, with no object ("so" is supplied in KJV; what they say is v1); גָּאַל "redeem"; מִיַּד־צָר "from the hand of the adversary" (צַר; also "of distress", as בַּצַּר v6).
- **"And gathered them out of the lands … from the north, and from the south" → "and from the lands he has gathered them … from the north and from the sea"** (v3) — Hebrew וּמֵאֲרָצוֹת קִבְּצָם, Hebrew order; וּמִיָּם "and from the sea": the word list renders יָם "west" as a quarter of the sky, but here מַעֲרָב "west" stands already, so the word is kept literal ("south", KJV, is taken from the sea to the south; uncertain). Three lines by the accents (ole veyored and atnach).
- **"They wandered in the wilderness in a solitary way; they found no city to dwell in" → "They wandered in the wilderness, in a desert way; a city of dwelling they did not find"** (v4) — Hebrew תָּעוּ בַמִּדְבָּר בִּישִׁימוֹן דָּרֶךְ, "in a desert, a way" (יְשִׁימוֹן "desert", as Deut 32:10; the two words stand side by side: uncertain); "Some" (older modern versions) is not in the Hebrew. עִיר מוֹשָׁב "a city of dwelling" (מוֹשָׁב "dwelling place", noun of יָשַׁב; also v7, 36), Hebrew order.
- **"Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them" → "Hungry, thirsty as well, their soul within them was fainting"** (v5) — Hebrew רְעֵבִים גַּם־צְמֵאִים, גַּם "also"; תִּתְעַטָּף, hitpael imperfect of עָטַף "faint": continuing in the past.
- **"Then they cried unto the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them out of their distresses" → "And they cried out to Yahweh in their distress; from their straits he delivered them"** (v6, 13, 19, 28) — the refrain, one wording where the Hebrew is the same: וַיִּצְעֲקוּ (v6, 28) and וַיִּזְעֲקוּ (v13, 19) are the two spellings of one verb "cry out" (one English); בַּצַּר לָהֶם "in their distress" (lit. "in the distress to them"); מְצוּקוֹת "straits" (as מָצוֹק Deut 28:53; plural). The last verb differs as in the Hebrew: v6 יַצִּילֵם "he delivered them" (הִצִּיל), v13, 19 יוֹשִׁיעֵם "he saved them" (יָשַׁע), v28 יוֹצִיאֵם "he brought them out"; v28 alone has וּ "and" before "from their straits". The imperfects follow wayyiqtol and are given as past; "Then" (KJV) is וַ "And".
- **"And he led them forth by the right way, that they might go to a city of habitation" → "And he made them tread in a straight way, to go to a city of dwelling"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיַּדְרִיכֵם, hifil of דָּרַךְ "tread" (as Ps 25:5); דֶּרֶךְ יְשָׁרָה, the "upright" word, of a way "straight"; לָלֶכֶת "to go".
- **"Oh that men would praise the LORD for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!" → "Let them give thanks to Yahweh for his chesed and his wonderful deeds to the sons of man"** (v8, 15, 21, 31) — the second refrain, the same Hebrew each time: יוֹדוּ, jussive of הוֹדָה "give thanks" (not "praise"; no "men" as subject); חַסְדּוֹ and נִפְלְאוֹתָיו stand as objects without a preposition ("for" is supplied once); נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds"; לִבְנֵי אָדָם "to the sons of man" (not "children").
- **"For he satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with goodness" → "For he has satisfied the longing soul, and the hungry soul he has filled with good"** (v9) — Hebrew הִשְׂבִּיעַ, מִלֵּא, perfects; שֹׁקֵקָה "longing" (שָׁקַק "run to and fro", of a thirsting soul: uncertain); טוֹב "good".
- **"Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron" → "Those who dwelt in darkness and the shadow of death, prisoners of affliction and iron"** (v10) — Hebrew יֹשְׁבֵי, participle of יָשַׁב "dwell" (the past from the context); צַלְמָוֶת "the shadow of death" as pointed (also v14; the reading צַלְמוּת "deep darkness" is possible); אֲסִירֵי עֳנִי וּבַרְזֶל "prisoners of affliction and iron", a noun ("being bound" turns it into a verb).
- **"Because they rebelled against the words of God, and contemned the counsel of the most High" → "for they had rebelled against the sayings of El, and the counsel of Elyon they had spurned"** (v11) — Hebrew הִמְרוּ, hifil of מָרָה "rebel against"; אִמְרֵי־אֵל "the sayings of El" (אֵמֶר "saying", as Num 24:4, beside דָּבָר "word"); נָאָצוּ, נִאֵץ "spurn"; Hebrew order.
- **"Therefore he brought down their heart with labour; they fell down, and there was none to help" → "And he brought low their heart with toil; they stumbled, and there was no helper"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיַּכְנַע, hifil of כָּנַע "bring low" (וַ "And", not "Therefore"); עָמָל "toil"; כָּשְׁלוּ "they stumbled"; וְאֵין עֹזֵר "and there was no helper" (participle of עָזַר).
- **"and brake their bands in sunder" → "and their bonds he snapped"** (v14) — Hebrew וּמוֹסְרוֹתֵיהֶם יְנַתֵּק, Hebrew order; נִתֵּק "snap" (as Judg 16:9, 12). יוֹצִיאֵם, imperfect, given as past from the context.
- **"For he hath broken the gates of brass, and cut the bars of iron in sunder" → "For he has broken the doors of bronze, and the bars of iron he has hewn through"** (v16) — Hebrew שִׁבַּר, piel "break in pieces"; דַּלְתוֹת "doors" (not "gates": that is שַׁעַר); נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze"; בְּרִיחֵי "bars"; גִּדֵּעַ "hew" (the word list has "hew down", which is said of trees; of bars "hew through").
- **"Fools because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted" → "Fools, because of the way of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, were afflicted"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱוִלִים "fools" as printed (many read חֹלִים "sick", from memory; the MT is kept); מִדֶּרֶךְ פִּשְׁעָם "because of the way of their transgression" (מִן, lit. "from"); יִתְעַנּוּ, hitpael of עָנָה (the word list has "humble oneself", lit. "let oneself be afflicted"): of men brought low by their own sins "were afflicted"; the imperfect is given as past from the context.
- **"Their soul abhorreth all manner of meat; and they draw near unto the gates of death" → "All food their soul abominated, and they reached as far as the gates of death"** (v18) — Hebrew כָּל־אֹכֶל "all food", Hebrew order; תְּתַעֵב, תִּעֵב "abominate"; וַיַּגִּיעוּ עַד, hifil of נָגַע "reach … as far as".
- **"He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions" → "He sent his word and healed them, and rescued them from their pits"** (v20) — Hebrew יִשְׁלַח … וְיִרְפָּאֵם וִימַלֵּט: the imperfects are given as past from the context; מִלֵּט "rescue" (not הִצִּיל "deliver", v6); מִשְּׁחִיתוֹתָם "from their pits" (שְׁחִית, as Lam 4:20; also taken as "destructions" from שָׁחַת: uncertain).
- **"And let them sacrifice the sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare his works with rejoicing" → "And let them sacrifice sacrifices of thanksgiving, and recount his works with a ringing cry"** (v22) — Hebrew וְיִזְבְּחוּ זִבְחֵי תוֹדָה, jussive, no article; סִפֵּר "recount"; רִנָּה "a ringing cry" (of joy).
- **"They that go down to the sea in ships, that do business in great waters; These see the works of the LORD" → "Those who go down to the sea in ships, doing work on many waters, they have seen the works of Yahweh"** (v23–24) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂי מְלָאכָה "doing work" (מְלָאכָה "work"; "business" in KJV); בְּמַיִם רַבִּים "on many waters"; הֵמָּה רָאוּ "they have seen", the pronoun set first, perfect; מַעֲשֵׂי "works"; נִפְלְאוֹתָיו "his wonderful deeds" (as the refrain); מְצוּלָה "depth".
- **"For he commandeth, and raiseth the stormy wind, which lifteth up the waves thereof" → "And he said, and set up a storm wind, and it raised high its waves"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר "And he said" (not "commanded"); וַיַּעֲמֵד, hifil of עָמַד "set up, make stand"; רוּחַ סְעָרָה "a wind of storm" (סְעָרָה "storm", also v29); וַתְּרוֹמֵם, feminine: the wind is the subject; רוֹמֵם "raise high" (the same verb as "exalt", v32); גַּלָּיו "its waves", masculine: the sea's (v23). Wayyiqtol as past.
- **"They mount up to the heaven, they go down again to the depths: their soul is melted because of trouble" → "They went up to the heavens; they went down to the deeps; their soul melted away in evil"** (v26) — Hebrew יַעֲלוּ … יֵרְדוּ, imperfects, given as past with the narrative (the subject may be the waves of v25 or the sailors); שָׁמַיִם "heavens"; תְּהוֹמוֹת "deeps" (תְּהוֹם); בְּרָעָה "in evil"; תִתְמוֹגָג, hitpolel of מוּג "melt away".
- **"They reel to and fro, and stagger like a drunken man, and are at their wit's end" → "They reeled and swayed like the drunkard, and all their wisdom was swallowed up"** (v27) — Hebrew יָחוֹגּוּ "reel" (חָגַג, here of staggering; the same letters are "hold a feast": uncertain); וְיָנוּעוּ, נוּעַ "sway"; כַּשִּׁכּוֹר "like the drunkard" (article); וְכָל־חָכְמָתָם תִּתְבַּלָּע, word for word ("at their wit's end" is the sense).
- **"He maketh the storm a calm, so that the waves thereof are still" → "He set the storm to a stillness, and their waves were silent"** (v29) — Hebrew יָקֵם סְעָרָה לִדְמָמָה, hifil of קוּם "make stand, set", לְ "to, into"; דְּמָמָה "stillness" (as 1 Kgs 19:12); וַיֶּחֱשׁוּ, חָשָׁה "be silent"; גַּלֵּיהֶם "their waves" (no "so that"); past with the narrative.
- **"Then are they glad because they be quiet; so he bringeth them unto their desired haven" → "And they were glad because they grew quiet, and he led them to the haven of their delight"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂמְחוּ כִּי־יִשְׁתֹּקוּ: the subject of "grew quiet" may be the men or the waves; וַיַּנְחֵם, נָחָה "lead"; מְחוֹז, only here, "haven" (uncertain: perhaps "city, market"); חֶפְצָם "their delight" (חֵפֶץ).
- **"Let them exalt him also in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the assembly of the elders" → "And let them exalt him in the assembly of the people, and in the seat of the elders let them praise him"** (v32) — Hebrew וִירֹמְמוּהוּ "And let them exalt him" (no "also"); בִּקְהַל־עָם "in the assembly of the people" (קָהָל; no article in the Hebrew); בְּמוֹשַׁב זְקֵנִים "in the seat of the elders" (מוֹשָׁב, as Ps 1:1); Hebrew order.
- **v33–43** — the hymn: participles and imperfects (v33, 35, 38, 40, 42), and wayyiqtol forms (v36–41) that carry them on; all are given in the present, as the same Hebrew in Job 12:21, 24; a past ("he made …") is possible for the wayyiqtol forms.
- **"He turneth rivers into a wilderness, and the watersprings into dry ground" → "He makes rivers into a wilderness, and outlets of water into thirsty ground"** (v33, 35) — Hebrew יָשֵׂם "he puts, makes" … לְ "into"; מוֹצָאֵי מַיִם "outlets of water" (lit. "goings out of water": springs); צִמָּאוֹן "thirsty ground" (as Deut 8:15); v35 אֲגַם־מַיִם "a pool of water", אֶרֶץ צִיָּה "a land of dryness".
- **"A fruitful land into barrenness, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein" → "a land of fruit into a salt land, because of the evil of those who dwell in it"** (v34) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ פְּרִי "a land of fruit"; מְלֵחָה "a salt land" (as Job 39:6); מֵרָעַת "because of the evil of" (רָעָה).
- **"And there he maketh the hungry to dwell, that they may prepare a city for habitation" → "And he makes the hungry dwell there, and they establish a city of dwelling"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיּוֹשֶׁב, hifil of יָשַׁב; וַיְכוֹנְנוּ "and they establish" (כּוֹנֵן; not "that they may").
- **"And sow the fields, and plant vineyards, which may yield fruits of increase" → "And they sow fields and plant vineyards, and they make fruit of yield"** (v37) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲשׂוּ פְּרִי תְבוּאָה "and they make fruit of yield" (עָשָׂה פְּרִי "make fruit"; תְּבוּאָה "yield"); no "which may".
- **"He blesseth them also, so that they are multiplied greatly; and suffereth not their cattle to decrease" → "And he blesses them, and they multiply greatly, and their beasts he does not make few"** (v38) — Hebrew וַיְבָרֲכֵם וַיִּרְבּוּ מְאֹד, two clauses joined by וַ; בְּהֶמְתָּם "their beasts" (בְּהֵמָה); לֹא יַמְעִיט, hifil of מָעַט "make few".
- **"Again, they are minished and brought low through oppression, affliction, and sorrow" → "And they become few and are bowed down through restraint, evil and sorrow"** (v39) — Hebrew וַיִּמְעֲטוּ וַיָּשֹׁחוּ, מָעַט "become few", שָׁחַח "be bowed down"; מֵעֹצֶר רָעָה וְיָגוֹן: עֹצֶר "restraint" (from עָצַר; "oppression" in KJV), רָעָה "evil", יָגוֹן "sorrow". Who "they" are (the people of v36–38 or others) is not said.
- **"He poureth contempt upon princes, and causeth them to wander in the wilderness, where there is no way" → "He pours contempt on nobles, and makes them wander in a waste without a way"** (v40) — Hebrew שֹׁפֵךְ בּוּז עַל־נְדִיבִים … וַיַּתְעֵם בְּתֹהוּ לֹא־דָרֶךְ, the same words as Job 12:21a, 24b, and the same wording: נְדִיבִים "nobles"; תֹּהוּ "waste" (not מִדְבָּר "wilderness").
- **"Yet setteth he the poor on high from affliction, and maketh him families like a flock" → "And he sets the needy on high from affliction, and makes clans like the flock"** (v41) — Hebrew וַיְשַׂגֵּב "and he sets on high" (שִׂגֵּב); אֶבְיוֹן "needy"; עוֹנִי "affliction" (עֳנִי); וַיָּשֶׂם כַּצֹּאן מִשְׁפָּחוֹת "and he makes like the flock clans" (מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan"; no "him"; צֹאן "flock", article).
- **"The righteous shall see it, and rejoice: and all iniquity shall stop her mouth" → "The upright see and are glad, and all injustice shuts its mouth"** (v42) — Hebrew יְשָׁרִים "the upright" (not צַדִּיקִים "righteous"); no "it"; עַוְלָה "injustice"; קָפְצָה פִּיהָ, perfect, as Job 5:16 ("shuts its mouth").
- **"Whoso is wise, and will observe these things, even they shall understand the lovingkindness of the LORD" → "Who is wise? Then let him keep these things, and let them consider the chasadim of Yahweh"** (v43) — Hebrew מִי־חָכָם וְיִשְׁמָר־אֵלֶּה, a question followed by a jussive with וְ (as Hos 14:9; "Whoever is wise, let him keep" is also possible); שָׁמַר "keep"; וְיִתְבּוֹנְנוּ, plural, hitpolel of בִּין "consider"; חַסְדֵי, the plural of *chesed* (as Ps 25:6).

### Notes
- **Opens Book V** (107–150). No heading; the Hebrew numbering agrees with ours.
- **Two refrains** — v6, 13, 19, 28 (the cry and the rescue) and v8, 15, 21, 31 (the thanks), each after one of four scenes: those who wandered (v4–9), the prisoners (v10–16), the fools (v17–22), the seafarers (v23–32); hymn (v33–43).
- **Inverted nuns** (׆) — the Masoretic text marks the ends of v20–25 and v39 with a reversed letter nun; what the marks mean is not known.
