# Psalm 76

To the overseer, with stringed instruments. A psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧, a song.

[1] Known in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ great is his name.

[2] And his booth was in ⟦salem|Shalem|Salem⟧,
and his abode in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[3] There he broke in pieces the flames of the bow,
shield and sword and war. Selah.

[4] Shining are you, mightier than the mountains of prey.

[5] The mighty ones of heart were plundered;
they slumbered their sleep,
and all the men of ability did not find their hands.

[6] From your rebuke, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
both chariots and horse fell into deep sleep.

[7] You, fearsome are you,
and who can stand before you from the time of your anger?

[8] From the heavens you made a sentence heard;
the earth feared and was quiet,

[9] when ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rose up for judgment,
to save all the humble of the earth. Selah.

[10] For the fury of man shall give thanks to you;
the remnant of furies you will gird on.

[11] Vow and repay to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
all who are around him;
let them bring a gift to the Fear.

[12] He cuts off the spirit of leaders;
fearsome to the kings of the earth.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 6, 9, 11) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים, as printed. **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v11) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"To the chief Musician on Neginoth, A Psalm or Song of Asaph" → "To the overseer, with stringed instruments. A psalm of Asaph, a song"** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ בִּנְגִינֹת מִזְמוֹר לְאָסָף שִׁיר, in this order. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (the verb of 1 Chr 15:21; traditional "to the chief musician"); בִּנְגִינֹת "with stringed instruments" (נְגִינָה, from נִגֵּן "play a stringed instrument", as Ps 4). There is no "or". לְאָסָף: by, for or belonging to Asaph; "of" leaves it open.
- **"In Judah is God known: his name is great in Israel" → "Known in Judah is Elohim; in Israel great is his name"** (v1) — Hebrew נוֹדָע בִּיהוּדָה אֱלֹהִים בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל גָּדוֹל שְׁמוֹ, the Hebrew order kept in both lines.
- **"In Salem also is his tabernacle, and his dwelling place in Zion" → "And his booth was in Salem, and his abode in Zion"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיְהִי, narrative form, "and was" (KJV "also is"); סֻכּוֹ "his booth" (סֹךְ, as סֻכָּה; Ps 27:5); מְעוֹנָתוֹ "his abode" (the מָעוֹן row; the feminine form is also the lair of lions, Job 37:8; Ps 104:22; Nah 2:12, and Jer 25:38 has סֻכּוֹ of a lion: a lion's covert may be pictured, with v4 "prey"). "Was" carries over to the second line.
- **"There brake he the arrows of the bow, the shield, and the sword, and the battle" → "There he broke in pieces the flames of the bow, shield and sword and war"** (v3) — Hebrew שִׁבַּר, piel "break in pieces"; רִשְׁפֵי־קָשֶׁת "the flames of the bow" (רֶשֶׁף "flame, flash", Song 8:6: the arrows, flying or burning; "arrows" interprets, "flashing arrows" adds); מָגֵן וְחֶרֶב וּמִלְחָמָה, without articles, "shield and sword and war" (מִלְחָמָה "war", its row; "weapons of" is not written).
- **"Selah"** (v3, 9) — Hebrew סֶלָה, a term of the psalm's performance; its meaning is not known.
- **"Thou art more glorious and excellent than the mountains of prey" → "Shining are you, mightier than the mountains of prey"** (v4) — Hebrew נָאוֹר אַתָּה אַדִּיר מֵהַרְרֵי־טָרֶף: נָאוֹר, nifal participle of אוֹר "be light", "shining, lit up" (only here in this form); אַדִּיר "mighty" (as Ps 8:1; KJV "excellent"); מִן "than" (or "from": "mighty from the mountains of prey", coming from them); טֶרֶף "prey". The Greek has "everlasting mountains" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"The stouthearted are spoiled, they have slept their sleep: and none of the men of might have found their hands" → "The mighty ones of heart were plundered; they slumbered their sleep, and all the men of ability did not find their hands"** (v5) — Hebrew אֶשְׁתּוֹלְלוּ, hitpolel of שָׁלַל "plunder" with an Aramaic-like prefix, "were plundered" (the earlier "stripped of their spoil" adds); אַבִּירֵי לֵב "the mighty ones of heart" (אַבִּיר, as Ps 22:12); נָמוּ שְׁנָתָם "they slumbered their sleep" (נוּם; the sleep of death); אַנְשֵׁי־חַיִל "men of ability" (its row: able men of war); "did not find their hands", word for word: they could not lift them (the earlier "were unable to use their hands" gives the sense in place of the words).
- **"At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, both the chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep" → "From your rebuke, Elohim of Jacob, both chariots and horse fell into deep sleep"** (v6) — Hebrew מִגַּעֲרָתְךָ "from your rebuke" (as Ps 18:15); נִרְדָּם, nifal of רָדַם "fall into deep sleep" (Judg 4:21; Jonah 1:5), singular, with both nouns as subject; רֶכֶב "chariots", a collective (its row; the earlier "rider" is רֹכֵב, another word). No "O".
- **"Thou, even thou, art to be feared: and who may stand in thy sight when once thou art angry?" → "You, fearsome are you, and who can stand before you from the time of your anger?"** (v7) — Hebrew אַתָּה נוֹרָא אַתָּה, the pronoun twice; נוֹרָא "fearsome" (its row; also v12); וּמִי "and who"; לְפָנֶיךָ "before you"; מֵאָז אַפֶּךָ, word for word "from then of your anger": as soon as it burns. אַף "anger" (also "nose").
- **"Thou didst cause judgment to be heard from heaven" → "From the heavens you made a sentence heard"** (v8) — Hebrew מִשָּׁמַיִם (plural, first, as in the Hebrew); הִשְׁמַעְתָּ, hifil "made heard"; דִּין "sentence", the ruling given (the דִּין row has "cause", of a case brought to court; here it is the verdict spoken, kept apart from מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" in v9); יָרְאָה "feared"; שָׁקְטָה "was quiet" (its row).
- **"When God arose to judgment, to save all the meek of the earth" → "when Elohim rose up for judgment, to save all the humble of the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּקוּם־לַמִּשְׁפָּט "at rising for judgment" (the earlier "to establish judgment" is not written); עַנְוֵי "the humble" (its row).
- **"Surely the wrath of man shall praise thee: the remainder of wrath shalt thou restrain" → "For the fury of man shall give thanks to you; the remnant of furies you will gird on"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי "For" (or "Surely"); חֲמַת אָדָם "the fury of man" (חֵמָה "fury", its row); תּוֹדֶךָּ "shall give thanks to you" (הוֹדָה); שְׁאֵרִית חֵמֹת "the remnant of furies" (plural); תַּחְגֹּר "you will gird on" (חָגַר "gird"; KJV "restrain" and the earlier "like a belt" interpret). The sense of the verse is not certain: the fury of men turned to his praise, and what is left of it worn by him as his own; the Greek has "shall keep festival to you" (reading תָּחֹג, from memory). The MT is kept.
- **"Vow, and pay unto the LORD your God: let all that be round about him bring presents unto him that ought to be feared" → "Vow and repay to Yahweh your Elohim, all who are around him; let them bring a gift to the Fear"** (v11) — Hebrew נִדְרוּ וְשַׁלְּמוּ, two imperatives (שִׁלֵּם "repay", its row); אֱלֹהֵיכֶם "your" (plural). By the accents כָּל־סְבִיבָיו "all who are around him" closes the first half: those around him are called to vow (it may also be the subject of the next line). יוֹבִילוּ שַׁי "let them bring a gift" (imperfect; or "they shall bring"; שַׁי "gift, tribute", singular); לַמּוֹרָא "to the Fear": מוֹרָא "fear" (its row), here the one who is feared, as "the Fear of Isaac" (Gen 31:42, פַּחַד, another word) and Isa 8:13 ("let him be your fear").
- **"He shall cut off the spirit of princes: he is terrible to the kings of the earth" → "He cuts off the spirit of leaders; fearsome to the kings of the earth"** (v12) — Hebrew יִבְצֹר, from בָּצַר, the verb of cutting off grapes at the vintage (its row: "pick", Lev 25:5, 11): the leaders are cut off like clusters (another root בצר "restrain, make inaccessible" is also proposed); נְגִידִים "leaders" (its row; not "princes"); נוֹרָא "fearsome" as v7, with no "he is" written.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v12 = Heb v13.
- **Structure** — Elohim known in Judah and Zion (v1–3), his victory over the warriors (v4–6), his judgment from the heavens (v7–10), the call to vow and bring gifts (v11–12).
- **"Salem"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁלֵם, set beside Zion: Jerusalem (as Gen 14:18 is commonly read).
