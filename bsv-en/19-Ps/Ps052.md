# Psalm 52

To the overseer. A Maskil of David, when Doeg the ⟦edomites|Edomi|Edomite⟧ came and told ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and said to him, "David has come to the house of ⟦ahimelech|Achimelech|Ahimelech⟧."

[1] Why do you boast in evil, mighty one?
The ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ of ⟦el|El|God⟧ is all the day.

[2] Destruction your tongue devises,
like a sharpened razor, you worker of treachery.

[3] You love evil more than good,
falsehood more than speaking righteousness. Selah.

[4] You love all words that swallow up,
you tongue of deceit.

[5] Also ⟦el|El|God⟧ will break you down forever;
he will snatch you up and tear you away from the tent,
and root you out from the land of the living. Selah.

[6] And the righteous shall see and fear,
and over him they shall laugh:

[7] Behold, the mighty man who does not make ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ his strong place,
and trusted in the abundance of his riches;
he is strong in his destruction.

[8] But I am like a luxuriant olive tree
in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
I have trusted in the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
forever and ever.

[9] I will give thanks to you forever,
for you have done it;
and I will wait for your name, for it is good,
before your faithful ones.

## Changes

- **"God" → "El"** (v1, 5) — Hebrew אֵל *El*.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew *Elohim*, as printed (the Elohistic psalms).
- **"To the chief Musician, Maschil, A Psalm of David, when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul, and said unto him" → "To the overseer. A Maskil of David, when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul and said to him"** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ מַשְׂכִּיל לְדָוִד בְּבוֹא דּוֹאֵג הָאֲדֹמִי וַיַּגֵּד לְשָׁאוּל וַיֹּאמֶר לוֹ: there is no word "psalm" (מִזְמוֹר) here. מַשְׂכִּיל is a participle of הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight"; as the name of a kind of psalm its meaning is not known ("a song of insight", "a skilful song"): kept as *Maskil*. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (traditional "to the chief musician"); לְ of לְדָוִד "by / for / belonging to": "of" leaves it open. The event: 1 Sam 21:7; 22:9–10.
- **"Why boastest thou thyself in mischief, O mighty man?" → "Why do you boast in evil, mighty one?"** (v1) — Hebrew מַה־תִּתְהַלֵּל בְּרָעָה הַגִּבּוֹר: רָעָה "evil" (the רַע row); הַגִּבּוֹר "mighty one" (the גִּבּוֹר row), kept apart from הַגֶּבֶר "the mighty man" in v7.
- **"the goodness of God endureth continually" → "The *chesed* of El is all the day"** (v1) — Hebrew חֶסֶד אֵל כָּל־הַיּוֹם, no verb: "endures" is added; token `steadfast-love`.
- **"Thy tongue deviseth mischiefs; like a sharp razor, working deceitfully" → "Destruction your tongue devises, like a sharpened razor, you worker of treachery"** (v2) — Hebrew הַוּוֹת תַּחְשֹׁב לְשׁוֹנֶךָ כְּתַעַר מְלֻטָּשׁ עֹשֵׂה רְמִיָּה: הַוּוֹת "destruction" (plural, as v7 and Ps 55:11), the object first; חָשַׁב "devise"; מְלֻטָּשׁ "sharpened"; עֹשֵׂה רְמִיָּה is masculine, so it names the man, not the tongue (feminine): "you worker of treachery". רְמִיָּה "treachery" is kept apart from מִרְמָה "deceit" (v4).
- **"lying rather than to speak righteousness" → "falsehood more than speaking righteousness"** (v3) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶר מִדַּבֵּר צֶדֶק; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (its row); צֶדֶק "righteousness". אָהַבְתָּ (v3, 4) is a perfect of state, "you love".
- **"all devouring words, O thou deceitful tongue" → "all words that swallow up, you tongue of deceit"** (v4) — Hebrew כָּל־דִּבְרֵי־בָלַע לְשׁוֹן מִרְמָה, "words of swallowing" (בָּלַע "swallow"); מִרְמָה "deceit" (its row).
- **"God shall likewise destroy thee for ever, he shall take thee away, and pluck thee out of thy dwelling place" → "Also El will break you down forever; he will snatch you up and tear you away from the tent"** (v5) — Hebrew גַּם־אֵל יִתָּצְךָ לָנֶצַח יַחְתְּךָ וְיִסָּחֲךָ מֵאֹהֶל: גַּם "also" (not "but"); נָתַץ "break down" (the reviewer row; said of altars and houses); לָנֶצַח "forever" (the Ps 13–16 row); חָתָה "snatch up" (as coals from a fire); מֵאֹהֶל "from the tent", no "your".
- **"and root thee out" → "and root you out"** (v5) — Hebrew וְשֵׁרֶשְׁךָ, with "and".
- **"The righteous also shall see, and fear, and shall laugh at him" → "And the righteous shall see and fear, and over him they shall laugh"** (v6) — Hebrew וְיִרְאוּ צַדִּיקִים וְיִירָאוּ וְעָלָיו יִשְׂחָקוּ: "see" and "fear" are a play of sound (יִרְאוּ / יִירָאוּ); "over him" stands first. No "saying" in the Hebrew: v7 is what they say, but the Hebrew does not mark it, so no quotation marks.
- **"Lo, this is the man that made not God his strength" → "Behold, the mighty man who does not make Elohim his strong place"** (v7) — Hebrew הִנֵּה הַגֶּבֶר לֹא יָשִׂים אֱלֹהִים מָעוּזּוֹ: הִנֵּה "behold"; הַגֶּבֶר "the mighty man" (standing decision; v1 has גִּבּוֹר); יָשִׂים imperfect "does not make"; מָעוֹז "strong place" (its row).
- **"and strengthened himself in his wickedness" → "he is strong in his destruction"** (v7) — Hebrew יָעֹז בְּהַוָּתוֹ: יָעֹז from עָזַז "be strong", no "and"; הַוָּה "destruction" as v2. Some derive the verb from עוּז "take refuge" ("sought refuge in his destruction"); the pointing is that of עָזַז.
- **"But I am like a green olive tree" → "But I am like a luxuriant olive tree"** (v8) — Hebrew כְּזַיִת רַעֲנָן; רַעֲנָן "luxuriant" (its row; not the colour).
- **"I trust in the mercy of God for ever and ever" → "I have trusted in the *chesed* of Elohim forever and ever"** (v8) — Hebrew בָּטַחְתִּי בְחֶסֶד־אֱלֹהִים עוֹלָם וָעֶד: perfect "I have trusted"; token `steadfast-love`; עוֹלָם וָעֶד "forever and ever".
- **"I will praise thee for ever, because thou hast done it" → "I will give thanks to you forever, for you have done it"** (v9) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ לְעוֹלָם כִּי עָשִׂיתָ: הוֹדָה "give thanks to" (its row); "it" is supplied for English (the verb has no object).
- **"and I will wait on thy name; for it is good before thy saints" → "and I will wait for your name, for it is good, before your faithful ones"** (v9) — Hebrew וַאֲקַוֶּה שִׁמְךָ כִי־טוֹב נֶגֶד חֲסִידֶיךָ: וְ "and"; קִוָּה "wait for" (its row); "it" supplied for the bare כִי־טוֹב; חָסִיד "faithful one" (no token; its row). Whether "before your faithful ones" goes with "wait" or with "good" the Hebrew leaves open.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is Heb v1–2: our v1 = Heb v3, v9 = Heb v11.
- **Doeg** — 1 Sam 21:7; 22:9–22.
- **The luxuriant olive tree** (v8) is set against the one torn from the tent and rooted out (v5).
