# Psalm 91

[1] One who dwells in the hiding place of ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧
in the shade of ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ will lodge.

[2] I will say to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "My refuge and my stronghold,
my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, I will trust in him."

[3] For he, he will deliver you from the trap of the fowler,
from the pestilence of destructions.

[4] With his pinion he will screen you,
and under his wings you will take refuge;
a large shield and a buckler is his truth.

[5] You will not be afraid of the dread of night,
of the arrow that flies by day,

[6] of the pestilence that walks in gloom,
of the destruction that devastates at noon.

[7] A thousand will fall at your side,
and ten thousand at your right hand;
to you it will not draw near.

[8] Only with your eyes will you look,
and the repayment of the wicked you will see.

[9] For you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, are my refuge!
⟦elyon|Elyon|The Most High⟧ you have made your abode.

[10] Evil will not be made to befall you,
and a plague will not come near your tent.

[11] For his angels he will command concerning you,
to keep you in all your ways.

[12] On palms they will lift you up,
lest you strike your foot against the stone.

[13] On fierce lion and cobra you will tread;
you will trample young lion and dragon.

[14] "Because on me he has set his longing, I will rescue him;
I will set him on high, because he has known my name.

[15] He will call me, and I will answer him;
with him am I in distress;
I will pull him out and honor him.

[16] With length of days I will sate him,
and I will make him look on my salvation."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 9) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"the most High" → "Elyon"** (v1, 9) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v1) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי; the meaning of the name is not certain.
- **"He that dwelleth in the secret place … shall abide under the shadow" → "One who dwells in the hiding place … in the shade … will lodge"** (v1) — Hebrew יֹשֵׁב, participle without article, "one who dwells"; סֵתֶר "hiding place" (as Ps 27:5; 31:20); צֵל "shade" (as Num 14:9: protection); יִתְלוֹנָן, hitpolel of לוּן "lodge, spend the night". The two lines are in the Hebrew order.
- **"I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust" → "I will say to Yahweh, 'My refuge and my stronghold, my Elohim, I will trust in him'"** (v2) — Hebrew לַיהוָה "to Yahweh"; מַחְסִי "my refuge"; מְצוּדָתִי "my stronghold" (as Ps 18:2); "He is" is not written; אֶבְטַח־בּוֹ "I will trust in him", its own clause. The Greek reads "he will say" (from memory); the MT has "I will say".
- **"Surely he shall deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, and from the noisome pestilence" → "For he, he will deliver you from the trap of the fowler, from the pestilence of destructions"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי הוּא "for he" (the pronoun stressed); פַּח "trap" (as Ps 11:6); יָקוּשׁ "fowler" (one who lays snares); דֶּבֶר הַוּוֹת "the pestilence of destructions" (הַוּוֹת plural, as Ps 5:9; the plural keeps it apart from קֶטֶב "destruction" in v6); no "and".
- **"He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler" → "With his pinion he will screen you, and under his wings you will take refuge; a large shield and a buckler is his truth"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּאֶבְרָתוֹ "with his pinion" (singular); יָסֶךְ לָךְ, from סָכַךְ "screen, cover over"; תֶּחְסֶה, from חָסָה "take refuge" (not בָּטַח "trust"); צִנָּה "large shield" (as Ps 35:2); סֹחֵרָה found only here: "buckler" or "wall around", uncertain; אֲמִתּוֹ "his truth" (אֱמֶת), with no "your".
- **"Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow … Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday" → "You will not be afraid of the dread of night, of the arrow … of the pestilence that walks in gloom, of the destruction that devastates at noon"** (v5–6) — Hebrew לֹא־תִירָא מִן "not be afraid of"; the מִן is repeated four times, "nor" is not written; פַּחַד "dread"; אֹפֶל "gloom"; קֶטֶב "destruction" (meaning uncertain, as Deut 32:24); יָשׁוּד "devastate" (שָׁדַד); צָהֳרָיִם "noon".
- **"but it shall not come nigh thee" → "to you it will not draw near"** (v7) — Hebrew מִצִּדְּךָ, lit. "from your side" (= at your side); אֵלֶיךָ "to you" first; נָגַשׁ "draw near"; "but" is not written.
- **"Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked" → "Only with your eyes will you look, and the repayment of the wicked you will see"** (v8) — Hebrew תַבִּיט "look" and וְ … תִּרְאֶה "and … see", two clauses; שִׁלֻּמָה, found only here, from שִׁלֵּם "repay": "repayment".
- **"Because thou hast made the LORD, which is my refuge, even the most High, thy habitation" → "For you, Yahweh, are my refuge! Elyon you have made your abode"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי־אַתָּה יְהוָה מַחְסִי עֶלְיוֹן שַׂמְתָּ מְעוֹנֶךָ: the first line speaks to Yahweh ("you … my refuge"), the second to the one addressed in v3–8 ("you have made your abode"); the Hebrew sets the two side by side without a link. KJV joins them into one clause ("Because thou hast made the LORD, which is my refuge … thy habitation"), and the first line can also be read "For you — 'Yahweh is my refuge'": uncertain. מָעוֹן "abode".
- **"There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling" → "Evil will not be made to befall you, and a plague will not come near your tent"** (v10) — Hebrew תְאֻנֶּה, pual of אָנָה "be made to meet"; רָעָה "evil"; נֶגַע "plague"; בְּאָהֳלֶךָ "your tent" (lit. "in your tent").
- **"For he shall give his angels charge over thee" → "For his angels he will command concerning you"** (v11) — Hebrew מַלְאָכָיו "his angels" (the word is "messengers") first; יְצַוֶּה־לָּךְ "he will command for you"; לִשְׁמָרְךָ "to keep you" (שָׁמַר). Matt 4:6 quotes v11–12 in Greek and leaves out "to keep you in all your ways" (SBLGNT: Τοῖς ἀγγέλοις αὐτοῦ ἐντελεῖται περὶ σοῦ καὶ ἐπὶ χειρῶν ἀροῦσίν σε, μήποτε προσκόψῃς πρὸς λίθον τὸν πόδα σου); the Hebrew stands here as it is.
- **"They shall bear thee up in their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone" → "On palms they will lift you up, lest you strike your foot against the stone"** (v12) — Hebrew עַל־כַּפַּיִם "on palms" ("their" is not written); יִשָּׂאוּנְךָ, from נָשָׂא "lift up"; תִּגֹּף, from נָגַף, of a foot against a stone: "strike" (the "smite" of the word list is for a blow from God); בָּאֶבֶן "against the stone", with the article. Matt 4:6 has "on hands" and "a stone" (see v11).
- **"Thou shalt tread upon the lion and adder: the young lion and the dragon shalt thou trample under feet" → "On fierce lion and cobra you will tread; you will trample young lion and dragon"** (v13) — Hebrew שַׁחַל "fierce lion" (a poetic word, kept apart from אַרְיֵה "lion"); פֶּתֶן "cobra" (kind uncertain, as Deut 32:33); כְּפִיר "young lion"; תַּנִּין "dragon" (a great serpent-like creature; uncertain); no articles.
- **"Because he hath set his love upon me, therefore will I deliver him: I will set him on high, because he hath known my name" → "Because on me he has set his longing, I will rescue him; I will set him on high, because he has known my name"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי בִי חָשַׁק, "on me" first; חָשַׁק "long for" (lit. "be bound to"; as Gen 34:8); וַאֲפַלְּטֵהוּ "I will rescue him" (פִּלֵּט; the וְ opens the main clause after "because" and has no English word: "because … and" would not stand); אֲשַׂגְּבֵהוּ, from שִׂגֵּב "set on high". The speaker from v14 to v16 is Yahweh (the quotation marks are not in the Hebrew).
- **"He shall call upon me … I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him, and honour him" → "He will call me … with him am I in distress; I will pull him out and honor him"** (v15) — Hebrew יִקְרָאֵנִי "he will call me"; עִמּוֹ־אָנֹכִי, no verb: "with him am I"; צָרָה "distress"; אֲחַלְּצֵהוּ, from חִלֵּץ "pull out" (as Ps 6:4); כִּבֵּד "honor".
- **"With long life will I satisfy him, and shew him my salvation" → "With length of days I will sate him, and I will make him look on my salvation"** (v16) — Hebrew אֹרֶךְ יָמִים "length of days"; אַשְׂבִּיעֵהוּ, hifil of שָׂבַע "be sated"; וְאַרְאֵהוּ בִּישׁוּעָתִי, hifil of רָאָה with בְּ, "make him look on" (see with his own eyes).

### Notes
- **No heading** — the psalm has none in the Hebrew.
- **v11–12** — quoted in Matt 4:6; Luke 4:10–11.
