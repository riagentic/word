# Psalm 94

[1] ⟦el|El|God⟧ of vengeances, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
⟦el|El|God⟧ of vengeances, shine forth!

[2] Lift yourself up, Judge of the earth;
bring back on the proud their dealing!

[3] How long shall the wicked, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
how long shall the wicked be jubilant?

[4] They pour out, they speak arrogance;
all the workers of wickedness boast themselves.

[5] Your people, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, they crush,
and your inheritance they afflict.

[6] The widow and the sojourner they kill,
and the fatherless they murder.

[7] And they say, "⟦yah|Yah|The LORD⟧ does not see,
and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ does not understand."

[8] Understand, brutish ones among the people;
and fools, when will you have insight?

[9] He who planted the ear, does he not hear?
Or he who formed the eye, does he not look?

[10] He who disciplines nations, does he not reprove,
he who teaches man knowledge?

[11] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ knows the plans of man,
that they are a vapor.

[12] Happy is the man whom you discipline, ⟦yah|Yah|LORD⟧,
and out of your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ you teach him,

[13] to give him quiet from days of evil,
until a pit is dug for the wicked.

[14] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will not abandon his people,
and his inheritance he will not forsake.

[15] For judgment shall return to righteousness,
and after it all the upright of heart.

[16] Who will rise up for me against evildoers?
Who will take his stand for me against the workers of wickedness?

[17] If ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had not been a help to me,
in a little my soul would have abode in silence.

[18] When I said, "My foot has slipped,"
your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, holds me up.

[19] In the abundance of my disquieting thoughts within me,
your comforts delight my soul.

[20] Shall a throne of destructions be joined to you,
one that forms toil by statute?

[21] They band together against the soul of the righteous,
and innocent blood they condemn.

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has become a secure height for me,
and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Rock of my refuge.

[23] And he has brought back on them their wickedness,
and in their evil he will annihilate them;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will annihilate them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 5, 11, 14, 17, 18, 22, 23) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 22, 23) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Yah"** (v7, 12) — Hebrew יָהּ, the short form of the name; retained.
- **Vocative** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 12, 18) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"O LORD God, to whom vengeance belongeth" → "El of vengeances, Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל־נְקָמוֹת, "El" and the plural "vengeances" (as Ps 18:47); "to whom … belongeth" is not written. **"shew thyself" → "shine forth"** — Hebrew הוֹפִיעַ, as Deut 33:2. The pointing is that of a perfect ("has shone forth"); the imperatives of v2 make it a command, as most take it: both in the note.
- **"Lift up thyself" → "Lift yourself up"** (v2) — Hebrew הִנָּשֵׂא, nifal imperative of נָשָׂא (not קוּם "rise up"). **"render a reward to the proud" → "bring back on the proud their dealing"** — Hebrew הָשֵׁב גְּמוּל עַל־גֵּאִים, lit. "bring back dealing upon proud ones" (גְּמוּל, as Ps 28:4 "return to them their dealing": what one has done to others); "what they deserve" is not written.
- **"triumph" → "be jubilant"** (v3) — Hebrew יַעֲלֹזוּ, עָלַז as Ps 96:12; the Hebrew order is "until when the wicked, Yahweh, until when shall the wicked be jubilant".
- **"How long shall they utter and speak hard things? and all the workers of iniquity boast themselves?" → "They pour out, they speak arrogance; all the workers of wickedness boast themselves"** (v4) — Hebrew יַבִּיעוּ יְדַבְּרוּ עָתָק, two verbs without "and" (הִבִּיעַ "pour out", as Ps 19:2); עָתָק "arrogance" (as 1 Sam 2:3). יִתְאַמְּרוּ, hitpael of אָמַר, found only here in this sense: "boast themselves" (or "speak proudly") is uncertain. פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness" (as Ps 5:5; also v16). The KJV carries the "How long" of v3 on; the Hebrew does not repeat it.
- **"break in pieces … afflict thine heritage" → "they crush … your inheritance they afflict"** (v5) — Hebrew יְדַכְּאוּ "crush"; נַחֲלָה "inheritance" (also v14); עִנָּה "afflict". The objects stand first, as in the Hebrew (also v6).
- **"stranger" → "sojourner"; "slay … murder" → "kill … murder"** (v6) — Hebrew גֵּר; הָרַג "kill" and רִצַּח "murder", two verbs. יְתוֹמִים "the fatherless" (plural).
- **"Yet they say … neither shall the God of Jacob regard it" → "And they say … and the Elohim of Jacob does not understand"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמְרוּ, "and"; לֹא־יָבִין, the verb הֵבִין "understand" (as the imperative בִּינוּ, v8).
- **"ye brutish among the people: and ye fools, when will ye be wise?" → "brutish ones among the people; and fools, when will you have insight?"** (v8) — Hebrew בֹּעֲרִים "brutish ones" (as cattle are, without understanding); כְּסִילִים "fools"; הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight".
- **"He that planted the ear … he that formed the eye, shall he not see?" → "He who planted the ear, does he not hear? Or he who formed the eye, does he not look?"** (v9) — Hebrew הֲנֹטַע … אִם־יֹצֵר, two participles ("the planter", "the former"); אִם "or" of a double question; הִבִּיט "look" (not רָאָה "see"). יָצַר "form" (also v20).
- **"He that chastiseth the heathen, shall not he correct? he that teacheth man knowledge, shall not he know?" → "He who disciplines nations, does he not reprove, he who teaches man knowledge?"** (v10) — Hebrew הֲיֹסֵר גּוֹיִם הֲלֹא יוֹכִיחַ הַמְלַמֵּד אָדָם דָּעַת: יִסֵּר "discipline" (also v12), הוֹכִיחַ "reprove". The second line has no verb: "shall not he know?" is not written. הַמְלַמֵּד may be read as a second question or as the subject of v11 ("he who teaches man knowledge — Yahweh knows …"): the question mark of our line is an editor's choice.
- **"the thoughts of man, that they are vanity" → "the plans of man, that they are a vapor"** (v11) — Hebrew מַחְשְׁבוֹת "plans" (as Ps 33:10); הֶבֶל "vapor" (breath; "vanity" in the KJV); "but" is not written. כִּי may also be "for" ("for they are a vapor").
- **"Blessed" → "Happy"** (v12) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי (as Deut 33:29; 1 Kgs 10:8); "blessed" is בָּרוּךְ. הַגֶּבֶר "the man" (גֶּבֶר, alone in the verse, as Ps 34:8). **"teachest him out of thy law" → "out of your Torah you teach him"** — Hebrew וּמִתּוֹרָתְךָ תְלַמְּדֶנּוּ, "and", in the Hebrew order; the second "whom" is not written.
- **"give him rest from the days of adversity" → "give him quiet from days of evil"** (v13) — Hebrew לְהַשְׁקִיט לוֹ מִימֵי רָע: הִשְׁקִיט, hifil of שָׁקַט "be quiet" (not נוּחַ "rest"); רָע "evil". שַׁחַת "pit".
- **"For the LORD will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance" → "For Yahweh will not abandon his people, and his inheritance he will not forsake"** (v14) — Hebrew נָטַשׁ "abandon" (leave to itself), עָזַב "forsake"; "and" and the Hebrew order kept.
- **"But judgment shall return unto righteousness: and all the upright in heart shall follow it" → "For judgment shall return to righteousness, and after it all the upright of heart"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי־עַד־צֶדֶק יָשׁוּב מִשְׁפָּט: כִּי "for"; צֶדֶק "righteousness" (not "the righteous", as the earlier wording had). The second line has no verb: "shall follow" is not written. כָּל־יִשְׁרֵי־לֵב "all the upright of heart" (as Ps 32:11).
- **"Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? or who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?" → "Who will rise up for me against evildoers? Who will take his stand for me against the workers of wickedness?"** (v16) — Hebrew מְרֵעִים "evildoers" (the earlier wording had the two words exchanged); הִתְיַצֵּב "take one's stand"; עִם "with", here "against". No "or".
- **"Unless the LORD had been my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence" → "If Yahweh had not been a help to me, in a little my soul would have abode in silence"** (v17) — Hebrew לוּלֵי יְהוָה עֶזְרָתָה לִּי, lit. "if not Yahweh a help to me"; "had been" is supplied. כִּמְעַט "in a little" (almost); שָׁכַן "abide"; דּוּמָה "silence" (the silence of the dead, Ps 115:17): "the land of" is not written.
- **"When I said, My foot slippeth; thy mercy, O LORD, held me up" → "When I said, 'My foot has slipped,' your steadfast love, Yahweh, holds me up"** (v18) — Hebrew אִם "if, when"; מָטָה, a perfect of מוֹט "slip" (of the foot, as Deut 32:35); חֶסֶד *chesed*; יִסְעָדֵנִי, an imperfect of סָעַד "support": "holds me up" (also read as past after the perfect, "held me up").
- **"In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul" → "In the abundance of my disquieting thoughts within me, your comforts delight my soul"** (v19) — Hebrew שַׂרְעַפַּי "my disquieting thoughts" (as Ps 139:23); בְּקִרְבִּי "within me" (the earlier wording had "of my heart"); תַּנְחוּמֶיךָ "your comforts" (plural, root נִחַם); שִׁעֲשַׁע "delight".
- **"Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frameth mischief by a law?" → "Shall a throne of destructions be joined to you, one that forms toil by statute?"** (v20) — Hebrew הַיְחָבְרְךָ כִּסֵּא הַוּוֹת: חָבַר "join" (the form is unusual: "be joined to you", or "have you as a companion"); כִּסֵּא "throne" (no "rulers"); הַוּוֹת "destruction" (the word is plural, "destructions", as Ps 5:9). יֹצֵר עָמָל עֲלֵי־חֹק "forming toil upon statute": יָצַר "form" as v9, עָמָל "toil", עֲלֵי "upon" (by, under cover of); חֹק "statute".
- **"They gather themselves together against the soul of the righteous, and condemn the innocent blood" → "They band together against the soul of the righteous, and innocent blood they condemn"** (v21) — Hebrew יָגוֹדּוּ, uncertain ("band together", from גְּדוּד "band"; or "attack"); נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (not "life"); דָּם נָקִי "innocent blood"; הִרְשִׁיעַ "condemn". "to death" is not written.
- **"But the LORD is my defence; and my God is the rock of my refuge" → "And Yahweh has become a secure height for me, and my Elohim the Rock of my refuge"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיְהִי, "and he became"; מִשְׂגָּב "secure height" (as Ps 18:2); צוּר of Yahweh "Rock" (capital); מַחְסִי "my refuge".
- **"And he shall bring upon them their own iniquity, and shall cut them off in their own wickedness; yea, the LORD our God shall cut them off" → "And he has brought back on them their wickedness, and in their evil he will annihilate them; Yahweh our Elohim will annihilate them"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׁב, a narrative form ("and he brought back"; many read it as a future with the following imperfects); אוֹנָם "their wickedness" (אָוֶן, as v4, 16); בְּרָעָתָם "in their evil" (or "for their evil"); הִצְמִית "annihilate" (as Ps 18:40), twice.

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew.
- **Structure** — appeal to the El of vengeances (v1–7), the brutish taught (v8–11), the happy man and the help of Yahweh (v12–23).
