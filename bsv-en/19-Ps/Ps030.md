# Psalm 30

A psalm, a song of the dedication of the house. Of David.

[1] I will raise you high, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for you have drawn me up,
and have not made my enemies glad over me.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, I cried to you for help,
and you healed me.

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you have brought up my soul from Sheol;
you have kept me alive from my going down to the pit.

[4] Make music to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, his faithful ones,
and give thanks to the remembrance of his holiness.

[5] For a moment in his anger,
life in his good pleasure;
in the evening weeping lodges,
and at the morning a ringing cry.

[6] And I, I said in my ease,
"I shall never be shaken."

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, in your good pleasure
you made strength stand for my mountain;
you hid your face; I was dismayed.

[8] To you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I called,
and to ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ I pleaded for favor:

[9] "What gain is in my blood,
in my going down to the pit?
Will dust give thanks to you?
Will it declare your truth?

[10] Hear, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and show me favor;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, be a helper to me."

[11] You have turned my lamenting into dancing for me;
you have loosed my sackcloth,
and girded me with gladness,

[12] so that glory may make music to you and not be silent;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, forever I will give thanks to you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 12) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"LORD" → "Adonai"** (v8, second line) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי *Adonai*, not the Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Heading "A Psalm and Song at the dedication of the house of David" → "A psalm, a song of the dedication of the house. Of David"** — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר שִׁיר־חֲנֻכַּת הַבַּיִת לְדָוִד, in this order; חֲנֻכָּה "dedication" (verb חָנַךְ, 1 Kgs 8:63). לְדָוִד goes with the whole heading, not with "the house"; לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and "Of David" leaves it open.
- **"extol … lifted me up … made my foes to rejoice over me" → "raise you high … drawn me up … made my enemies glad over me"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲרוֹמִמְךָ, polel of רוּם "raise high"; דִלִּיתָנִי, from דָּלָה "draw (water)", as from a well; שִׂמַּחְתָּ "made glad" (the earlier "let … rejoice" softens the causative); אֹיְבַי "my enemies".
- **"cried … hast healed me" → "cried … for help … healed me"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׁוַּעְתִּי "I cried for help"; וַתִּרְפָּאֵנִי, narrative form, "and you healed me".
- **"the grave" → "Sheol"** (v3) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל. **"thou hast kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit" → "you have kept me alive from my going down to the pit"** — Hebrew חִיִּיתַנִי, piel of חָיָה; the qere מִיָּרְדִי "from my going down" is translated. The ketiv מיורדי reads "from those who go down" (the earlier "restored me to life from among those who go down" follows it). בּוֹר "pit".
- **"Sing … O ye saints of his, and give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness" → "Make music … his faithful ones, and give thanks to the remembrance of his holiness"** (v4) — Hebrew זַמְּרוּ, from זִמֵּר "make music" (with voice and instrument); חֲסִידָיו "his faithful ones", those who keep *chesed* (no token: the token is for the noun); לְזֵכֶר "to the remembrance", זֵכֶר the word of Exod 3:15, how he is remembered and named.
- **"For his anger endureth but a moment; in his favour is life: weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning" → "For a moment in his anger, life in his good pleasure; in the evening weeping lodges, and at the morning a ringing cry"** (v5) — the Hebrew has no verbs in the first two lines: רֶגַע בְּאַפּוֹ חַיִּים בִּרְצוֹנוֹ, "a moment — in his anger; life — in his good pleasure" (חַיִּים "life", also "a lifetime"); אַף "anger" (also "nose"); רָצוֹן "good pleasure". יָלִין "lodges, spends the night"; רִנָּה "a ringing cry", here of joy (in Solomon's prayer "cry", 1 Kgs 8:28); "comes" is not written.
- **"And in my prosperity I said, I shall never be moved" → "And I, I said in my ease, 'I shall never be shaken'"** (v6) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי אָמַרְתִּי, the pronoun stressed; שַׁלְוָה "ease, quiet"; בַּל־אֶמּוֹט לְעוֹלָם, lit. "I shall not be shaken forever", that is "never".
- **"thou hast made my mountain to stand strong: thou didst hide thy face, and I was troubled" → "you made strength stand for my mountain; you hid your face; I was dismayed"** (v7) — Hebrew הֶעֱמַדְתָּה לְהַרְרִי עֹז, word for word "you made stand, for my mountain, strength"; the sense is not certain (the earlier "made my mountain stand strong" takes לְ as the object marker and עֹז as an adverb; others read "made me stand on strong mountains", from memory). הָיִיתִי נִבְהָל "I was dismayed".
- **"I cried to thee, O LORD; and unto the LORD I made supplication" → "To you, Yahweh, I called, and to Adonai I pleaded for favor"** (v8) — Hebrew imperfects אֶקְרָא, אֶתְחַנָּן, given as past: v8–10 tell what he said in his trouble (as v6). הִתְחַנֵּן, of the root חָנַן "favor".
- **"What profit is there in my blood, when I go down to the pit? Shall the dust praise thee? shall it declare thy truth?" → "What gain is in my blood, in my going down to the pit? Will dust give thanks to you? Will it declare your truth?"** (v9) — Hebrew בֶּצַע "gain" (elsewhere "unjust gain": here no wrong is meant, so "gain" alone); בְּדָמִי "in my blood" (the earlier "death" interprets); בְּרִדְתִּי "in my going down"; שַׁחַת "pit", another word than בּוֹר of v3; יוֹדְךָ "give thanks to you" (הוֹדָה, as v4, 12; not "praise"); אֲמִתֶּךָ "your truth" (אֱמֶת).
- **"have mercy upon me … be thou my helper" → "show me favor … be a helper to me"** (v10) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי, from חָנַן "show favor"; הֱיֵה־עֹזֵר לִי.
- **"my mourning … thou hast put off my sackcloth" → "my lamenting … you have loosed my sackcloth"** (v11) — Hebrew מִסְפֵּד, noun of סָפַד "lament"; פִּתַּחְתָּ "loosed"; וַתְּאַזְּרֵנִי "and girded me" (אָזַר; the earlier "clothed" is another verb).
- **"To the end that my glory may sing praise to thee" → "so that glory may make music to you"** (v12) — Hebrew לְמַעַן יְזַמֶּרְךָ כָבוֹד: "glory" without "my" (commonly taken as "my glory", that is "my soul", as Gen 49:6; the Greek has "my glory", from memory). יִדֹּם "be silent". "forever I will give thanks" keeps the Hebrew order.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v12 = Heb v13.
- **Structure** — thanks (v1–5) gives way to a look back on the trouble (v6–10) and the turn (v11–12); v9–10 are the words of his plea, introduced by v8.
- **"The house"** — the heading does not say which house was dedicated.
