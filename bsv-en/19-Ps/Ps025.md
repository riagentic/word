# Psalm 25

Of David.

[1] To you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I lift up my soul.

[2] My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in you I have trusted; let me not be ashamed;
let not my enemies be jubilant over me.

[3] Indeed, all who wait for you shall not be ashamed;
ashamed shall be those who deal treacherously without cause.

[4] Make me know your ways, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
teach me your paths.

[5] Make me tread in your truth and teach me,
for you are the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation;
for you I have waited all the day.

[6] Remember your compassion, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and your ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|steadfast love⟧,
for they are from of old.

[7] Do not remember the sins of my youth and my transgressions;
according to your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ remember me, you,
on account of your goodness, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[8] Good and upright is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
therefore he teaches sinners in the way.

[9] He makes the humble tread in judgment,
and he teaches the humble his way.

[10] All the paths of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth
to those who guard his covenant and his testimonies.

[11] On account of your name, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and you shall pardon my iniquity, for it is great.

[12] Who is this, the man who fears ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?
He will teach him in the way he will choose.

[13] His soul shall lodge in good,
and his seed shall possess the land.

[14] The secret counsel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is for those who fear him,
and his covenant, to make it known to them.

[15] My eyes are continually toward ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for he will bring my feet out of the net.

[16] Turn to me and show me favor,
for I am alone and afflicted.

[17] The distresses of my heart have enlarged;
bring me out of my straits.

[18] See my affliction and my toil,
and forgive all my sins.

[19] See my enemies, for they are many,
and with a hatred of violence they hate me.

[20] Keep my soul and deliver me;
let me not be ashamed, for I have taken refuge in you.

[21] May integrity and uprightness guard me,
for I have waited for you.

[22] Ransom ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, from all his distresses.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 5, 22) — Hebrew *Elohim*; v22 has אֱלֹהִים where the rest of the psalm has יְהוָה.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "Of David"** (heading) — Hebrew לְדָוִד, the first word of v1: "by", "for" or "belonging to David".
- **"O my God, I trust in thee" → "My Elohim, in you I have trusted"** (v2) — Hebrew אֱלֹהַי בְּךָ בָטַחְתִּי, a perfect. **"let me not be ashamed" kept** — Hebrew אַל־אֵבוֹשָׁה (בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", so v3, 20). **"triumph over me" → "be jubilant over me"** — Hebrew עָלַץ, literally "be jubilant to me".
- **"let none that wait on thee be ashamed" → "all who wait for you shall not be ashamed"** (v3) — Hebrew גַּם כָּל־קֹוֶיךָ לֹא יֵבֹשׁוּ: לֹא with the imperfect states (a wish would have אַל); "all" is כָּל. **"let them be ashamed which transgress" → "ashamed shall be those who deal treacherously"** — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ הַבּוֹגְדִים, the verb first, as the Hebrew orders it; בָּגַד "deal treacherously". **"without cause"** — Hebrew רֵיקָם, literally "emptily".
- **"Shew me thy ways" → "Make me know your ways"** (v4) — Hebrew הוֹדִיעֵנִי.
- **"Lead me in thy truth" → "Make me tread in your truth"** (v5) — Hebrew הַדְרִיכֵנִי, the hifil of דָּרַךְ "tread" (the root of דֶּרֶךְ "way"); so v9. **"on thee do I wait" → "for you I have waited"** — Hebrew קִוִּיתִי, a perfect (so v21); קִוָּה "wait for".
- **"thy tender mercies and thy lovingkindnesses" → "your compassion … and your steadfast love"** (v6) — Hebrew רַחֲמֶיךָ (always plural, "compassion") and חֲסָדֶיךָ, the plural of *chesed*. **"for they have been ever of old" → "for they are from of old"** — Hebrew כִּי מֵעוֹלָם הֵמָּה, no verb.
- **"nor my transgressions" → "and my transgressions"** (v7) — Hebrew וּפְשָׁעַי. **"according to thy mercy remember thou me" → "according to your steadfast love remember me, you"** — Hebrew זְכָר־לִי־אַתָּה, with the stressed "you". **"for thy goodness' sake" → "on account of your goodness"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן טוּבְךָ (so v11).
- **"will he teach sinners" → "he teaches sinners"** (v8) — Hebrew יוֹרֶה, from הוֹרָה "teach".
- **"The meek will he guide in judgment: and the meek will he teach his way" → "He makes the humble tread in judgment, and he teaches the humble his way"** (v9) — Hebrew יַדְרֵךְ עֲנָוִים בַּמִּשְׁפָּט וִילַמֵּד: יַדְרֵךְ has the short form that can also be a wish ("let him make … tread"); here taken as a statement, beside v8. עָנָו "humble" (not "meek"); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment".
- **"mercy and truth unto such as keep" → "steadfast love and truth to those who guard"** (v10) — Hebrew לְנֹצְרֵי, from נָצַר "guard" (שָׁמַר is "keep", v20).
- **"pardon mine iniquity" → "and you shall pardon my iniquity"** (v11) — Hebrew וְסָלַחְתָּ, "and" with the perfect: a request in the form of a statement ("and you will pardon"); the "and" is kept.
- **"What man is he that feareth the LORD?" → "Who is this, the man who fears Yahweh?"** (v12) — Hebrew מִי־זֶה הָאִישׁ יְרֵא יְהוָה. **"in the way that he shall choose" → "in the way he will choose"** — Hebrew בְּדֶרֶךְ יִבְחָר; "he" may be the man or Yahweh.
- **"His soul shall dwell at ease" → "His soul shall lodge in good"** (v13) — Hebrew נַפְשׁוֹ בְּטוֹב תָּלִין, from לוּן "lodge, spend the night"; טוֹב "good". **"inherit the earth" → "possess the land"** — Hebrew יִירַשׁ אָרֶץ; יָרַשׁ "possess" (of an heir "inherit"); אֶרֶץ is "land" or "earth".
- **"The secret of the LORD is with them that fear him" → "The secret counsel of Yahweh is for those who fear him"** (v14) — Hebrew סוֹד יְהוָה לִירֵאָיו: סוֹד is the close counsel of those who sit together; לְ "for, to". **"and he will shew them his covenant" → "and his covenant, to make it known to them"** — Hebrew וּבְרִיתוֹ לְהוֹדִיעָם, a noun and an infinitive, no finite verb; "it" is supplied.
- **"ever" → "continually"** (v15) — Hebrew תָּמִיד. **"he shall pluck my feet out of the net" → "he will bring my feet out of the net"** — Hebrew יוֹצִיא, from הוֹצִיא "bring out" (so v17).
- **"have mercy upon me" → "show me favor"** (v16) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי, from חָנַן "show favor". **"desolate" → "alone"** — Hebrew יָחִיד "only one, solitary" (Gen 22:2 "your only one"); עָנִי "afflicted".
- **"The troubles of my heart are enlarged" → "The distresses of my heart have enlarged"** (v17) — Hebrew צָרוֹת לְבָבִי הִרְחִיבוּ, the hifil "enlarge, make wide", here without an object (the distresses are the subject). A common emendation divides the letters as הַרְחֵב וּ… "relieve the distresses of my heart, and bring me out"; the Masoretic Text is kept. **"distresses" → "straits"** — Hebrew מְצוּקוֹתַי (צָרָה is "distress").
- **"Look upon mine affliction and my pain" → "See my affliction and my toil"** (v18) — Hebrew רְאֵה עָנְיִי וַעֲמָלִי; עָמָל "toil". **"forgive"** — Hebrew וְשָׂא לְ, literally "lift up for".
- **"Consider mine enemies" → "See my enemies"** (v19) — Hebrew רְאֵה, the verb of v18. **"they hate me with cruel hatred" → "with a hatred of violence they hate me"** — Hebrew שִׂנְאַת חָמָס.
- **"O keep my soul" → "Keep my soul"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁמְרָה. **"for I put my trust in thee" → "for I have taken refuge in you"** — Hebrew חָסִיתִי בָךְ, from חָסָה "take refuge" (בָּטַח "trust" is v2).
- **"preserve me" → "guard me"** (v21) — Hebrew יִצְּרוּנִי, from נָצַר, as v10.
- **"Redeem Israel … out of all his troubles" → "Ransom Israel … from all his distresses"** (v22) — Hebrew פְּדֵה, from פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem"); צָרוֹת, as v17.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first word of v1.
- **Acrostic** — the verses begin with the letters of the Hebrew alphabet in order: א v1 (after the heading), ב v2 (the second word, בְּךָ), ג v3, ד v4, ה v5, ז v6, ח v7, ט v8, י v9, כ v10, ל v11, מ v12, נ v13, ס v14, ע v15, פ v16, צ v17, ר v18, ר v19, ש v20, ת v21. ו has no verse of its own (v5b begins with וְ); ק is missing and ר stands twice (v18, 19). v22, a plea for Israel, begins with פ and stands outside the scheme (so Ps 34:22). The translation does not imitate the letters.
- ***Chesed*** — v6, 7, 10.
