# Psalm 83

A song, a psalm. Of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, let there be no silence for you;
do not be deaf, and do not be quiet, ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[2] For behold, your enemies are in uproar,
and those who hate you have lifted up the head.

[3] Against your people they make secret counsel crafty,
and they take counsel together against your ⟦tzefunim|tzefunim — hidden ones|treasured ones⟧.

[4] They have said, "Come, and let us efface them from being a nation,
and let the name of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ be remembered no more."

[5] For they have taken counsel together in heart;
against you they cut a covenant —

[6] the tents of Edom and the ⟦ishmaelites|Yishmeelim|Ishmaelites⟧,
⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and the ⟦hagrites|Hagrim|Hagrites⟧,

[7] ⟦gebal|Geval|Gebal⟧ and Ammon and ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧,
⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧ with those who dwell in ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧;

[8] also ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ has been joined with them;
they have become an arm to the sons of Lot. Selah.

[9] Do to them as to ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧,
as to Sisera, as to ⟦jabin|Yavin|Jabin⟧, at the wadi ⟦kishon|Qishon|Kishon⟧.

[10] They were destroyed at ⟦en-dor|Ein Dor|En-dor⟧;
they became dung for the ground.

[11] Set them, their nobles, like ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ and like ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧,
and like ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and like ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧ all their princes,

[12] who have said, "Let us take possession for ourselves
of the pastures of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[13] My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, set them like the whirl,
like stubble before the wind.

[14] As fire burns a forest,
and as a flame sets mountains ablaze,

[15] so may you pursue them with your storm,
and with your tempest dismay them.

[16] Fill their faces with disgrace,
and let them seek your name, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[17] Let them be ashamed and be dismayed perpetually,
and let them be abashed and perish.

[18] And let them know that you — your name is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — alone
are ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ over all the earth.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 12, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim*; the last of the Elohistic psalms (42–83): the divine names as printed. **"El"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל *El*. **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v16, 18) — Tetragrammaton, which stands here also in this Elohistic psalm.
- **"A Song or Psalm of Asaph" → "A song, a psalm. Of Asaph."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר מִזְמוֹר לְאָסָף, in this order (as Ps 48); the לְ of לְאָסָף may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" Asaph, and is not decided here.
- **"Keep not thou silence, O God: hold not thy peace, and be not still, O God" → "let there be no silence for you; do not be deaf, and do not be quiet"** (v1) — Hebrew אַל־דֳּמִי־לָךְ, a noun clause, literally "no silence to you"; חָרַשׁ "be deaf" (of Elohim not answering, as Ps 28:1); שָׁקַט "be quiet". No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"make a tumult … have lifted up the head" → "are in uproar … have lifted up the head"** (v2) — Hebrew יֶהֱמָיוּן, from הָמָה "roar, be in uproar"; נָשְׂאוּ רֹאשׁ, "head" singular, without "their".
- **"They have taken crafty counsel against thy people, and consulted against thy hidden ones" → "they make secret counsel crafty, and they take counsel together against your tzefunim"** (v3) — Hebrew יַעֲרִימוּ סוֹד, hifil of עָרַם "make crafty"; סוֹד "secret counsel" (the close circle and what is said in it); וְיִתְיָעֲצוּ, hitpael of יָעַץ, "take counsel with one another"; צְפוּנֶיךָ, "your hidden (treasured) ones", passive of צָפַן "hide away".
- **"They have said … cut them off from being a nation" → "They have said … let us efface them from being a nation, and let the name of Israel be remembered no more"** (v4) — Hebrew אָמְרוּ, perfect; נַכְחִידֵם, hifil of כָּחַד "efface, make vanish"; מִגּוֹי "from (being) a nation"; וְלֹא־יִזָּכֵר "and let it not be remembered".
- **"they have consulted together with one consent: they are confederate against thee" → "they have taken counsel together in heart; against you they cut a covenant"** (v5) — Hebrew נוֹעֲצוּ לֵב יַחְדָּו, literally "they have taken counsel, heart together" (= with one heart; "one" is not written); בְּרִית יִכְרֹתוּ, "they cut a covenant" (imperfect).
- **"the inhabitants of Tyre" → "those who dwell in Tyre"** (v7) — Hebrew יֹשְׁבֵי צוֹר, participle of יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"Assur also is joined with them: they have holpen the children of Lot" → "also Ashur has been joined with them; they have become an arm to the sons of Lot"** (v8) — Hebrew נִלְוָה עִמָּם "has been joined with them"; הָיוּ זְרוֹעַ, "they have become an arm" (= a help, a strength), no "strong"; בְּנֵי־לוֹט "sons of Lot" (Moab and Ammon, Gen 19:36–38).
- **"Do unto them as unto the Midianites; as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the brook of Kison" → "Do to them as to Midian, as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the wadi Kishon"** (v9) — Hebrew כְּמִדְיָן "like Midian", the name (not "the Midianites"); "to" is supplied after "as"; כְּסִיסְרָא כְיָבִין, no "and"; נַחַל "wadi" (as Judg 4:7; 5:21). Judg 4–5 (Sisera and Jabin); Judg 7–8 (Midian, Oreb, Zeeb, Zebah, Zalmunna).
- **"Which perished at Endor" → "They were destroyed at En-dor"** (v10) — Hebrew נִשְׁמְדוּ, nifal of שָׁמַד "be destroyed"; a new sentence, no "who". דֹּמֶן "dung".
- **"Make their nobles like Oreb … all their princes as Zebah" → "Set them, their nobles, like Oreb and like Zeeb, and like Zebah and like Zalmunna all their princes"** (v11) — Hebrew שִׁיתֵמוֹ נְדִיבֵמוֹ, "set them — their nobles" (שִׁית "set", as Ps 21:9 "set them like an oven"); every name has its own כְּ "like"; נְסִיכֵמוֹ "their princes" (נָסִיךְ, perhaps "one installed").
- **"Who said, Let us take to ourselves the houses of God in possession" → "who have said, Let us take possession for ourselves of the pastures of Elohim"** (v12) — Hebrew אָמְרוּ, perfect; נִירֲשָׁה לָּנוּ, cohortative of יָרַשׁ "take possession"; נְאוֹת "pastures" (as Ps 23:2), not "houses".
- **"make them like a wheel; as the stubble before the wind" → "set them like the whirl, like stubble before the wind"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׁיתֵמוֹ "set them" (as v11); כַגַּלְגַּל, with the article, "like the whirl": גַּלְגַּל is "wheel, whirl" (also Isa 17:13 "the whirl before the storm"); whether whirling dust or a wind-blown dry plant is meant is not certain. קַשׁ "stubble"; רוּחַ "wind".
- **"As the fire burneth a wood, and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire" → "As fire burns a forest, and as a flame sets mountains ablaze"** (v14) — Hebrew תִּבְעַר־יָעַר, "burns a forest" (no article); וּכְלֶהָבָה "and as a flame"; תְּלַהֵט הָרִים, "sets mountains ablaze".
- **"So persecute them with thy tempest, and make them afraid with thy storm" → "so may you pursue them with your storm, and with your tempest dismay them"** (v15) — Hebrew תִּרְדְּפֵם … תְבַהֲלֵם, imperfects in a prayer, taken as wishes (or "so you will pursue"); רָדַף "pursue"; סַעַר "storm" (beside סְעָרָה), סוּפָה "tempest"; בִּהַל "dismay", the Hebrew order of the second line kept.
- **"Fill their faces with shame; that they may seek thy name" → "Fill their faces with disgrace, and let them seek your name"** (v16) — Hebrew קָלוֹן "disgrace" (from קָלַל "be light"; beside כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" and בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed" of v17); וִיבַקְשׁוּ, jussive with "and" ("so that they seek" is the other reading).
- **"Let them be confounded and troubled for ever; yea, let them be put to shame, and perish" → "Let them be ashamed and be dismayed perpetually, and let them be abashed and perish"** (v17) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ "be ashamed"; וְיִבָּהֲלוּ "be dismayed" (the verb of v15); עֲדֵי־עַד "perpetually" (עַד, not עוֹלָם); וְיַחְפְּרוּ, from חָפֵר "be abashed" (another root than חָפַר "dig"); אָבַד "perish".
- **"That men may know that thou, whose name alone is JEHOVAH, art the most high over all the earth" → "And let them know that you — your name is Yahweh — alone are Elyon over all the earth"** (v18) — Hebrew וְיֵדְעוּ "and let them know" (no "men"); כִּי־אַתָּה שִׁמְךָ יְהוָה לְבַדֶּךָ, literally "that you, your name Yahweh, alone": "alone" may go with "you" or with "your name Yahweh"; the English keeps the Hebrew order. Two lines by the Hebrew accents (the verse was in three).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v18 = Heb v19.
- **Last Asaph psalm** (Ps 50; 73–83).
- **Ten names in the alliance** (v6–8): peoples around Israel; the time is not named.
