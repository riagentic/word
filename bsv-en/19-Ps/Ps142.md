# Psalm 142

A Maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer.

[1] With my voice to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I cry out;
with my voice to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I plead for favor.

[2] I pour out before him my complaint;
my distress before him I tell.

[3] When my spirit grows faint within me,
then you know my track.
In the path where I walk
they have hidden a trap for me.

[4] Look to the right and see:
and there is none who recognizes me;
a place of flight has perished from me;
there is none who seeks for my soul.

[5] I have cried out to you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
I have said, "You are my refuge,
my share in the land of the living."

[6] Give heed to my cry,
for I have been brought very low.
Deliver me from my pursuers,
for they are stronger than I.

[7] Bring my soul out of the prison,
to give thanks to your name.
The righteous will encircle me,
for you will deal with me.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5) — Tetragrammaton. No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word (v5).
- **"Maschil of David; A Prayer when he was in the cave" → "A Maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer."** (heading) — Hebrew מַשְׂכִּיל לְדָוִד בִּהְיוֹתוֹ בַמְּעָרָה תְפִלָּה, in this order: תְּפִלָּה "a prayer" stands last. מַשְׂכִּיל, a kind of psalm of unknown meaning (from הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight"): kept as *Maskil*. לְ of לְדָוִד "by / for / belonging to": "of" leaves it open. The cave: 1 Sam 22:1; 24:3.
- **"I cried unto the LORD with my voice; with my voice unto the LORD did I make my supplication" → "With my voice to Yahweh I cry out; with my voice to Yahweh I plead for favor"** (v1) — Hebrew קוֹלִי אֶל־יְהוָה אֶזְעָק קוֹלִי אֶל־יְהוָה אֶתְחַנָּן, literally "my voice — to Yahweh I cry out" (as Ps 3:4), twice in the same order; imperfects, present. זָעַק "cry out"; אֶתְחַנָּן, hitpael of חָנַן "show favor": "plead for favor" (as Ps 30:8); "for mercy" is not written.
- **"I poured out my complaint before him; I shewed before him my trouble" → "I pour out before him my complaint; my distress before him I tell"** (v2) — Hebrew אֶשְׁפֹּךְ לְפָנָיו שִׂיחִי צָרָתִי לְפָנָיו אַגִּיד, imperfects, in this order; שִׂיחַ "complaint" (its row; "musing" is also possible); צָרָה "distress"; הִגִּיד "tell".
- **"When my spirit was overwhelmed within me, then thou knewest my path. In the way wherein I walked have they privily laid a snare for me" → "When my spirit grows faint within me, then you know my track. In the path where I walk they have hidden a trap for me"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּהִתְעַטֵּף עָלַי רוּחִי, infinitive: "in the growing faint of my spirit" (עָטַף hitpael, as Ps 77:3; 143:4); עָלַי, literally "upon me" (as Ps 42:6). וְאַתָּה יָדַעְתָּ, "and you, you know" (the וְ opens the main clause: "then"); a perfect, of knowing that holds ("you knew" is also possible). נְתִיבָה "track", beside אֹרַח "path" (two words). טָמְנוּ "they have hidden" (in the ground, as Ps 31:4); פַּח "trap" (the fowler's; מוֹקֵשׁ is "snare"); "privily" is not a second word.
- **"I looked on my right hand, and beheld, but there was no man that would know me: refuge failed me; no man cared for my soul" → "Look to the right and see: and there is none who recognizes me; a place of flight has perished from me; there is none who seeks for my soul"** (v4) — Hebrew הַבֵּיט יָמִין וּרְאֵה, pointed as an infinitive absolute and an imperative ("look … and see"); "I looked … and saw" (KJV, the versions) reads other vowels (from memory). "hand" is not written: יָמִין "the right". וְאֵין־לִי מַכִּיר, literally "and there is not for me one who recognizes" (הִכִּיר "recognize", as Ruth 2:19). אָבַד מָנוֹס מִמֶּנִּי: אָבַד "perish"; מָנוֹס "place of flight" (from נוּס "flee"; the 2 Sam 22 row), not "refuge". אֵין דּוֹרֵשׁ לְנַפְשִׁי: דָּרַשׁ "seek", with לְ "for": one who looks out for my soul; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"I cried unto thee, O LORD: I said, Thou art my refuge and my portion" → "I have cried out to you, Yahweh; I have said, 'You are my refuge, my share in the land of the living'"** (v5) — Hebrew זָעַקְתִּי … אָמַרְתִּי, perfects; מַחְסֶה "refuge" (noun of חָסָה); חֵלֶק "share" (as Ps 73:26); "and" is not written before "my share".
- **"Attend unto my cry; for I am brought very low: deliver me from my persecutors; for they are stronger than I" → "Give heed to my cry, for I have been brought very low. Deliver me from my pursuers, for they are stronger than I"** (v6) — Hebrew הַקְשִׁיבָה "give heed" (as Ps 5:2; 17:1); רִנָּה "cry" (as Ps 61:1); דַּלּוֹתִי מְאֹד, perfect of דָּלַל "be low" (as Ps 79:8); רֹדְפַי, participle of רָדַף "pursue"; אָמְצוּ מִמֶּנִּי "they are stronger than I" (as Ps 18:17).
- **"Bring my soul out of prison, that I may praise thy name: the righteous shall compass me about; for thou shalt deal bountifully with me" → "Bring my soul out of the prison, to give thanks to your name. The righteous will encircle me, for you will deal with me"** (v7) — Hebrew הוֹצִיאָה מִמַּסְגֵּר נַפְשִׁי: הוֹצִיא "bring out"; מַסְגֵּר "prison" (from סָגַר "shut": a place shut in); לְהוֹדוֹת אֶת־שְׁמֶךָ "to give thanks to your name" (infinitive; הוֹדָה "give thanks to"). בִּי יַכְתִּרוּ צַדִּיקִים: כִּתֵּר "encircle" (its row; Ps 22:12), with בִּי "about me"; from the same root as כֶּתֶר "crown", some read "the righteous will crown themselves because of me" (from memory). כִּי תִגְמֹל עָלָי "for you will deal with me" (as Ps 13:6); "bountifully" is not written.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to our v7 = Heb v8.
- **The cave** — 1 Sam 22:1 (Adullam) or 1 Sam 24:3 (En-gedi); the heading does not say which.
