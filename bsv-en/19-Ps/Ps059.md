# Psalm 59

To the overseer. ⟦al-tashcheth|Al-tashchet|Do Not Destroy⟧. Of David. A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧, when ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ sent, and they watched the house to put him to death.

[1] Deliver me from my enemies, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
from those who rise up against me set me on high.

[2] Deliver me from workers of wickedness,
and from men of blood save me.

[3] For behold, they have lain in ambush for my soul;
strong ones stir up strife against me —
not my transgression and not my sin, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;

[4] without iniquity they run and make themselves ready.
Awake to meet me, and see!

[5] And you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
wake up to visit all the nations;
do not show favor to any who deal treacherously in wickedness. Selah.

[6] They return at evening;
they growl like the dog,
and go around the city.

[7] Behold, they pour forth with their mouth;
swords are in their lips,
for who hears?

[8] But you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, laugh at them;
you deride all nations.

[9] His strength — for you I will keep watch,
for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my secure height.

[10] The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ will come to meet me;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will make me look upon those who watch for me.

[11] Do not kill them, lest my people forget;
make them sway by your force, and bring them down,
our shield, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧.

[12] The sin of their mouth, the word of their lips:
let them be captured in their pride,
and for the curse and for the lie that they recount.

[13] Make an end in fury,
make an end, and they are not;
and let them know that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rules in ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
to the ends of the earth. Selah.

[14] And they return at evening;
they growl like the dog,
and go around the city.

[15] They roam about to eat;
if they are not sated, then they spend the night.

[16] But I — I will sing your strength,
and sing aloud in the morning your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧;
for you have been a secure height for me
and a place of flight in the day of distress for me.

[17] My strength, to you I will make music;
for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my secure height,
the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 9, 10, 13, 17) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 5, 8) — Tetragrammaton (Elohistic psalm: it stands here).
- **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"To the chief Musician, Altaschith, Michtam of David; when Saul sent, and they watched the house to kill him" → "To the overseer. Al-tashchet. Of David. A Miktam, when Saul sent, and they watched the house to put him to death."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת לְדָוִד מִכְתָּם בִּשְׁלֹחַ שָׁאוּל וַיִּשְׁמְרוּ אֶת־הַבַּיִת לַהֲמִיתוֹ, the first verse of the Hebrew; the formulas as Ps 57. בִּשְׁלֹחַ שָׁאוּל "when Saul sent": no object ("men" is not written); אֶת־הַבַּיִת "the house" (no "his"); לַהֲמִיתוֹ, hifil of מוּת "put to death".
- **"defend me from them that rise up against me" → "from those who rise up against me set me on high"** (v1) — Hebrew תְּשַׂגְּבֵנִי, piel of שָׂגַב "be high" (the verb of מִשְׂגָּב "secure height", v9, 16, 17); the form is imperfect, here a plea after the imperative ("you will set me on high"). אֱלֹהָי "my Elohim".
- **"the workers of iniquity … bloody men" → "workers of wickedness … men of blood"** (v2) — Hebrew פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן (no article, as Ps 5:5); אַנְשֵׁי דָמִים, "men of bloods".
- **"For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul: the mighty are gathered against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin" → "For behold, they have lain in ambush for my soul; strong ones stir up strife against me — not my transgression and not my sin"** (v3) — Hebrew אָרְבוּ, perfect of אָרַב "lie in ambush"; יָגוּרוּ עָלַי "stir up strife against me" (as 56:6; "gather" KJV; uncertain); עַזִּים "strong ones"; לֹא־פִשְׁעִי וְלֹא־חַטָּאתִי, "not my transgression and not my sin" (no "for").
- **"They run and prepare themselves without my fault" → "without iniquity they run and make themselves ready"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּלִי־עָוֹן "without iniquity" first (no "my"); יִכּוֹנָנוּ, hitpolel of כּוּן "make oneself ready". **"awake to help me" → "Awake to meet me"** — Hebrew עוּרָה לִקְרָאתִי "to meet me".
- **"O LORD God of hosts" → "Yahweh Elohim of Hosts"** (v5) — Hebrew יְהוָה־אֱלֹהִים צְבָאוֹת, the three words joined as printed (the same in Ps 80:4, 19). **"awake to visit all the heathen: be not merciful to any wicked transgressors" → "wake up to visit all the nations; do not show favor to any who deal treacherously in wickedness"** — Hebrew הָקִיצָה (קִיץ "wake up"; v4 has עוּר "awake"); פָּקַד "visit"; הַגּוֹיִם "the nations"; אַל־תָּחֹן, from חָנַן "show favor"; כָּל־בֹּגְדֵי אָוֶן "all who deal treacherously in wickedness" (בָּגַד).
- **"They return at evening: they make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city" → "They return at evening; they growl like the dog, and go around the city"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew יָשׁוּבוּ לָעֶרֶב; יֶהֱמוּ, הָמָה "roar, murmur", of a dog "growl"; כַכָּלֶב "like the dog" (the article of kind); יְסוֹבְבוּ עִיר, "go around a city" (no article). v14 is the same with וְ "And". The bare imperfects may also be wishes ("let them return", KJV v14).
- **"Behold, they belch out with their mouth: swords are in their lips: for who, say they, doth hear?" → "Behold, they pour forth with their mouth; swords are in their lips, for who hears?"** (v7) — Hebrew יַבִּיעוּן, hifil of נָבַע "pour forth, gush"; כִּי־מִי שֹׁמֵעַ "for who hears?": "say they" is not written.
- **"But thou, O LORD, shalt laugh at them" → "But you, Yahweh, laugh at them"** (v8) — Hebrew תִּשְׂחַק, imperfect; תִּלְעַג לְ "deride".
- **"Because of his strength will I wait upon thee: for God is my defence" → "His strength — for you I will keep watch, for Elohim is my secure height"** (v9) — Hebrew עֻזּוֹ "his strength", as printed; many Hebrew manuscripts and the versions have עֻזִּי "my strength", as v17 (from memory). אֵלֶיךָ אֶשְׁמֹרָה, cohortative of שָׁמַר: "for you I will keep watch". מִשְׂגָּב "secure height" (as 2 Sam 22:3).
- **"The God of my mercy shall prevent me: God shall let me see my desire upon mine enemies" → "The Elohim of my chesed will come to meet me; Elohim will make me look upon those who watch for me"** (v10) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי חַסְדִּי, the qere, "the Elohim of my chesed" (as v17); the written form חסדו reads "my Elohim — his chesed will come to meet me". קִדֵּם "come to meet". יַרְאֵנִי בְשֹׁרְרָי, "will make me look upon" (hifil of רָאָה with בְּ: look on in triumph; "my desire" is not written); שׁוֹרְרַי "those who watch for me" (as 56:2).
- **"Slay them not … scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O Lord our shield" → "Do not kill them … make them sway by your force, and bring them down, our shield, Adonai"** (v11) — Hebrew אַל־תַּהַרְגֵם (הָרַג "kill"); הֲנִיעֵמוֹ, hifil of נוּעַ "sway, stagger, wander"; בְחֵילְךָ, חַיִל "force" (strength, or an army); מָגִנֵּנוּ אֲדֹנָי, Hebrew order.
- **"For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips let them even be taken in their pride: and for cursing and lying which they speak" → "The sin of their mouth, the word of their lips: let them be captured in their pride, and for the curse and for the lie that they recount"** (v12) — Hebrew חַטַּאת־פִּימוֹ דְּבַר־שְׂפָתֵימוֹ, two phrases with no verb and no "for"; דְּבַר singular; וְיִלָּכְדוּ "and let them be captured" (לָכַד); גָּאוֹן "pride" (of God it is "exaltation", Exod 15:7: one word, two senses); וּמֵאָלָה וּמִכַּחַשׁ יְסַפֵּרוּ, "and from curse and from lie they recount" (אָלָה "curse", כַּחַשׁ "lie", סִפֵּר "recount").
- **"Consume them in wrath, consume them, that they may not be: and let them know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth" → "Make an end in fury, make an end, and they are not; and let them know that Elohim rules in Jacob, to the ends of the earth"** (v13) — Hebrew כַּלֵּה, piel of כָּלָה "make an end" (no object written, twice); בְחֵמָה "in fury"; וְאֵינֵמוֹ "and they are not"; מֹשֵׁל בְּ "rules in". The atnach falls after "Jacob": "to the ends of the earth" may belong to "let them know" or to "rules".
- **"Let them wander up and down for meat, and grudge if they be not satisfied" → "They roam about to eat; if they are not sated, then they spend the night"** (v15) — Hebrew הֵמָּה יְנִיעוּן, the qere (hifil of נוּעַ, "they roam"); the written form ינועון (qal) "they wander": the sense is the same. לֶאֱכֹל "to eat". וַיָּלִינוּ may be from לוּן "lodge, spend the night" or from לוּן "grumble" (the forms are alike here; "grudge" KJV, "growl" others); the verse takes "spend the night"; uncertain.
- **"But I will sing of thy power; yea, I will sing aloud of thy mercy in the morning: for thou hast been my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble" → "But I — I will sing your strength, and sing aloud in the morning your chesed; for you have been a secure height for me and a place of flight in the day of distress for me"** (v16) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי, the pronoun stands out; עֻזְּךָ "your strength" and חַסְדֶּךָ objects of the verbs; רִנֵּן "sing aloud"; מִשְׂגָּב "secure height"; מָנוֹס "place of flight" (from נוּס "flee"); בְּיוֹם צַר־לִי, "in the day (it is) narrow for me".
- **"Unto thee, O my strength, will I sing" → "My strength, to you I will make music"** (v17) — Hebrew עֻזִּי אֵלֶיךָ אֲזַמֵּרָה, cohortative of זִמֵּר "make music"; v9 has אֶשְׁמֹרָה "I will keep watch" and עֻזּוֹ "his strength".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–17 = Heb v2–18.
- **1 Sam 19:11** — Saul's men watch David's house.
- **Refrains** — v6 = v14 (the dogs at evening); v9 ≈ v17 (watch / make music to "my strength").
