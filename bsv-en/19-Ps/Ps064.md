# Psalm 64

To the overseer. A psalm of David.

[1] Hear, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my voice in my complaint;
from the dread of the enemy guard my life.

[2] Hide me from the secret counsel of evildoers,
from the throng of the workers of wickedness,

[3] who have sharpened their tongue like the sword;
they have aimed their arrow, a bitter word,

[4] to shoot in the hiding places at the blameless;
suddenly they shoot him, and they do not fear.

[5] They make strong for themselves an evil matter;
they recount how to hide snares;
they have said, "Who will see them?"

[6] They search out injustices:
"We have completed a search well searched out!"
And the inward part of a man and the heart are deep.

[7] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shot them, an arrow, suddenly;
their blows came to be.

[8] And they made him stumble; their tongue is upon them;
all who see them shake themselves.

[9] And all mankind feared,
and they told the work of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and they had insight into his deed.

[10] Let the righteous one be glad in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and take refuge in him;
and let all the upright in heart glory.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 7, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v10) — Tetragrammaton: the Hebrew has יהוה here, in a psalm that otherwise has *Elohim*; kept as printed.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy" → "Hear, Elohim, my voice in my complaint; from the dread of the enemy guard my life"** (v1) — Hebrew שְׁמַע־אֱלֹהִים קוֹלִי בְשִׂיחִי, in this order, without "O"; שִׂיחַ "complaint" (what one pours out, as 1 Sam 1:16; also "musing"). מִפַּחַד אוֹיֵב תִּצֹּר חַיָּי: פַּחַד "dread"; נָצַר "guard". תִּצֹּר has the form of the imperfect ("you will guard"); the same form serves as a request (so here), as תַּסְתִּירֵנִי in v2.
- **"Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity" → "Hide me from the secret counsel of evildoers, from the throng of the workers of wickedness"** (v2) — Hebrew סוֹד "secret counsel" (the close circle and what is said in it; "council" also read); מְרֵעִים "evildoers" (those who do evil; not רְשָׁעִים "the wicked"). רִגְשָׁה "throng" (a noisy crowd; one root with רָגַשׁ, Ps 2:1); פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness".
- **"Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words" → "who have sharpened their tongue like the sword; they have aimed their arrow, a bitter word"** (v3) — Hebrew perfects; לְשׁוֹנָם "their tongue", חִצָּם "their arrow", דָּבָר מָר "a bitter word": all singular; כַחֶרֶב "like the sword". דָּרְכוּ חִצָּם, literally "they have trodden their arrow" (the verb of treading the bow to string it; "bows" is not in the Hebrew).
- **"That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not" → "to shoot in the hiding places at the blameless; suddenly they shoot him, and they do not fear"** (v4) — Hebrew בַּמִּסְתָּרִים "in the hiding places" (מִסְתָּר "secret place", plural); תָּם "blameless"; יֹרֻהוּ "they shoot him"; וְלֹא יִירָאוּ "and they do not fear".
- **"They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them?" → "They make strong for themselves an evil matter; they recount how to hide snares; they have said, 'Who will see them?'"** (v5) — Hebrew יְחַזְּקוּ־לָמוֹ דָּבָר רָע, חִזֵּק "make strong"; דָּבָר "matter" (no speech meant here; the word of v3). יְסַפְּרוּ, סִפֵּר "recount"; לִטְמוֹן מוֹקְשִׁים "to hide snares". אָמְרוּ "they have said", a perfect. מִי יִרְאֶה־לָּמוֹ, literally "who will see to them": "them" may be the snares or the plotters.
- **"They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep" → "They search out injustices: 'We have completed a search well searched out!' And the inward part of a man and the heart are deep"** (v6) — Hebrew עוֹלֹת "injustices" (עַוְלָה). תַּמְנוּ, as printed "we have completed" (תַּם "come to an end"): the words of the plotters, with no "saying" in the Hebrew; some manuscripts and many scholars read טָמְנוּ "they have hidden" (as v5), from memory: the verse keeps the printed text. חֵפֶשׂ מְחֻפָּשׂ "a search searched out". קֶרֶב אִישׁ "the inward part of a man" (each one); וְלֵב "and the heart", no "his"; עָמֹק "deep".
- **"But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded" → "And Elohim shot them, an arrow, suddenly; their blows came to be"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיֹּרֵם אֱלֹהִים חֵץ פִּתְאוֹם, a narrative form (wayyiqtol), given as a past, as are the verbs of v8–9; many take the whole as what will happen (KJV future, the earlier text present). חֵץ "an arrow", object beside "them"; the accents join פִּתְאוֹם "suddenly" to this line. הָיוּ מַכּוֹתָם "their blows were (came to be)": מַכָּה "blow".
- **"So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away" → "And they made him stumble; their tongue is upon them; all who see them shake themselves"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיַּכְשִׁילוּהוּ עָלֵימוֹ לְשׁוֹנָם, obscure: "and they made him stumble" (הִכְשִׁיל "make stumble"; "him" singular: each of them, or "it", the tongue: "they made their tongue stumble against themselves"); עָלֵימוֹ לְשׁוֹנָם "upon them their tongue" (it turns against them). יִתְנֹדֲדוּ, hitpolel of נוּד: "shake themselves" (the head, in mockery, as Jer 48:27) or "flee" (KJV); uncertain.
- **"And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing" → "And all mankind feared, and they told the work of Elohim, and they had insight into his deed"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּירְאוּ כָּל־אָדָם "and all mankind feared" (אָדָם); וַיַּגִּידוּ "and they told"; פֹּעַל "work", מַעֲשֶׂה "deed" (two words). וּמַעֲשֵׂהוּ הִשְׂכִּילוּ "and his deed they had insight into": הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight", a perfect; "for" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"The righteous shall be glad in the LORD, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory" → "Let the righteous one be glad in Yahweh and take refuge in him; and let all the upright in heart glory"** (v10) — Hebrew יִשְׂמַח צַדִּיק, singular; the form may be a wish (so here) or a future (KJV); וְחָסָה בוֹ, חָסָה "take refuge" (not "trust": that is בָּטַח); וְיִתְהַלְלוּ "and let … glory" (הִתְהַלֵּל, as Ps 34:2; 63:11); יִשְׁרֵי־לֵב "the upright in heart".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v10 = Heb v11.
- **Poetic structure** — the enemies' plotting (v1–6), Elohim's arrow (v7–9), the call to gladness (v10).
