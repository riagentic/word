# Judges 6

[1] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave them into the hand of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ seven years.

[2] And the hand of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ was strong over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. Because of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ made for themselves the dens that are in the mountains, and the caves, and the strongholds.

[3] And it would be, when ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had sown, that ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ would come up, and ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧ and the sons of the east, and they would come up against him.

[4] And they camped against them and ruined the produce of the land until you come to ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧, and they would leave no sustenance in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, nor sheep nor ox nor donkey.

[5] For they and their livestock would come up, and their tents, and they would come like locusts for multitude, and for them and for their camels there was no number; and they came into the land to ruin it.

[6] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ became very poor because of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And it came to pass, when the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on account of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧,

[8] that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sent a man, a prophet, to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he said to them, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'I myself brought you up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and brought you out from the house of slaves.

[9] And I delivered you from the hand of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and from the hand of all your oppressors, and I drove them out from before you and gave you their land.

[10] And I said to you, "I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; you shall not fear the gods of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, in whose land you are dwelling." But you have not listened to my voice.'"

[11] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came and sat under the terebinth that was in ⟦ophrah|Ofrah|Ophrah⟧, which belonged to ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ the ⟦abiezrites|Aviezri|Abiezrite⟧; and ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ his son was beating out wheat in the winepress, to make it flee from the face of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧.

[12] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to him and said to him, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is with you, mighty one of ability."

[13] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ said to him, "Oh, my lord, and if ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is with us, why then has all this found us? And where are all his wonderful deeds that our fathers recounted to us, saying, 'Did not ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ bring us up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧?' But now ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has abandoned us and given us into the palm of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧."

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ turned to him and said, "Go in this power of yours, and you shall save ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the palm of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧. Have I not sent you?"

[15] And he said to him, "Oh, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, with what shall I save ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧? Behold, my thousand is the poorest in ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, and I am the youngest in the house of my father."

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "For I will be with you, and you shall strike down ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ as one man."

[17] And he said to him, "If, please, I have found favor in your eyes, then make for me a sign that it is you who are speaking with me.

[18] Please do not depart from here until I come to you and bring out my offering and set it down before you." And he said, "I will sit until you return."

[19] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ went in and prepared a kid of the goats, and an ephah of meal as unleavened bread; the flesh he put in the basket, and the broth he put in the pot, and he brought it out to him under the terebinth and brought it near.

[20] And the angel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to him, "Take the flesh and the unleavened bread and set them down on this crag, and the broth pour out." And he did so.

[21] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ stretched out the end of the staff that was in his hand and touched the flesh and the unleavened bread; and the fire went up from the rock and consumed the flesh and the unleavened bread. And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went from his eyes.

[22] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ saw that he was the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ said, "Alas, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! For therefore I have seen the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ face to face."

[23] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧ to you; do not fear; you shall not die."

[24] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ built there an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and called it ⟦yhwh-shalom|Yahweh Shalom|The LORD Is Peace⟧. To this day it is still in ⟦ophrah|Ofrah|Ophrah⟧ of the ⟦abiezrites|Aviezri|Abiezrite⟧.

[25] And it came to pass that night that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Take the bull of the ox that is your father's, and the second bull, seven years old, and tear down the altar of the Baal that is your father's, and the Asherah that is by it you shall cut down.

[26] And you shall build an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ on the top of this strong place, in the arrangement; and you shall take the second bull and offer up a burnt offering with the wood of the Asherah that you shall cut down."

[27] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ took ten men of his slaves and did as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken to him. And it came to pass, because he feared the house of his father and the men of the city too much to do it by day, that he did it by night.

[28] And the men of the city rose early in the morning, and behold, the altar of the Baal was broken down, and the Asherah that was by it was cut down, and the second bull had been offered up on the altar that was built.

[29] And they said, each man to his neighbor, "Who has done this thing?" And they inquired and sought, and they said, "⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ the son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ has done this thing."

[30] And the men of the city said to ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧, "Bring out your son, and let him die, for he has broken down the altar of the Baal, and for he has cut down the Asherah that was by it."

[31] And ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ said to all who stood against him, "Is it you who will contend for the Baal? Or is it you who will save him? Whoever contends for him shall be put to death by the morning. If he is a god, let him contend for himself, for he has broken down his altar."

[32] And he called him on that day ⟦jerubbaal|Yerubbaal|Jerubbaal⟧, saying, "Let the Baal contend against him, for he has broken down his altar."

[33] And all ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧ and the sons of the east were gathered together, and they passed over and camped in the valley of ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧.

[34] And the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ clothed ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, and he blew on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, and ⟦abiezer|Aviezer|Abiezer⟧ was called out after him.

[35] And he sent messengers through all ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, and it also was called out after him; and he sent messengers through Asher and through ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧ and through ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, and they came up to meet them.

[36] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ said to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "If you are saving ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ by my hand, as you have spoken,

[37] behold, I am setting the fleece of wool on the threshing floor. If there is dew on the fleece alone, and on all the ground dryness, then I shall know that you will save ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ by my hand, as you have spoken."

[38] And it was so. And he rose early on the morrow and pressed the fleece and drained dew from the fleece, the fullness of the bowl of water.

[39] And ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧ said to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "Let not your anger burn against me, and let me speak only this once. Let me test, please, only this once with the fleece: let there be, please, dryness on the fleece alone, and on all the ground let there be dew."

[40] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did so that night; and there was dryness on the fleece alone, and on all the ground there was dew.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v20, 36, 39 the Hebrew has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים "the Elohim"; in v40 it has none.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the LORD" → "the sons of Israel did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲשׂוּ … הָרַע בְּעֵינֵי יְהוָה, with the article: "the evil". The frame formula of the book (2:11; 3:7, 12; 4:1; 10:6; 13:1), here without "again". בְּנֵי is "sons of".
- **"the Midianites" → "Midian"; "the Amalekites" → "Amalek"** (v2, 3, 7, 11, 33) — the Hebrew has the names מִדְיָן, עֲמָלֵק in every place, with a singular verb in v3 ("Midian would come up") and plural verbs after it. **"against them" → "against him"** (v3) — Hebrew עָלָיו, singular: Israel.
- **"prevailed against" → "was strong over"** (v2) — Hebrew וַתָּעָז … עַל, from עָזַז "be strong", as 3:10.
- **"because of the Midianites" → "Because of Midian"** (v2, 6) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי, literally "from the face of"; the same word in v11, where a flight is meant ("from the face of Midian"), and in v9 ("from before you").
- **"dens" (v2)** — Hebrew מִנְהָרוֹת, found only here; the meaning is uncertain (hollows or clefts in the mountains). **"strong holds" → "strongholds"** — Hebrew מְצָדוֹת, mountain fastnesses; another word than the "strong place" of v26.
- **"And so it was, when Israel had sown, that the Midianites came up" → "And it would be, when Israel had sown, that Midian would come up"** (v3–5) — the Hebrew forms (וְהָיָה, וְעָלָה, יַשְׁאִירוּ, יַעֲלוּ) tell what happened again and again; the narrative past stands between them (v4 "camped", "ruined", v5 "came").
- **"destroyed the increase of the earth" → "ruined the produce of the land"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew הִשְׁחִית "ruin" (v5 "to ruin it"), יְבוּל "produce". **"till thou come unto Gaza" → "until you come to Gaza"** — Hebrew עַד־בּוֹאֲךָ עַזָּה, literally "until your coming to Gaza".
- **"sustenance" kept** (v4) — Hebrew מִחְיָה, literally "preservation of life", from "live": what keeps one alive. **"neither sheep" → "nor sheep nor ox nor donkey"** — the Hebrew has "and sheep and ox and donkey" under the one "not"; שֶׂה is one animal of the flock, sheep or goat.
- **"cattle" → "livestock"** (v5) — Hebrew מִקְנֶה. **"they came as grasshoppers for multitude" → "they would come like locusts for multitude"** — Hebrew אַרְבֶּה "locust"; כְדֵי, literally "as the sufficiency of". The written text has יבאו "they would come"; the reading tradition has וּבָאוּ "and they would come": no difference in meaning. **"without number" → "there was no number"** — Hebrew אֵין מִסְפָּר.
- **"was greatly impoverished" → "became very poor"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּדַּל, from דָּלַל "be low, become small, languish"; the adjective of this root is "poor" in v15. "Brought low" is kept for another verb (8:28).
- **"cried unto the LORD" → "cried out to Yahweh"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew זָעַק; the frame formula of the book (3:9, 15; 4:3; 10:10). **"because of" → "on account of"** (v7) — Hebrew עַל אֹדוֹת, another expression than מִפְּנֵי in v6.
- **"a prophet" → "a man, a prophet"** (v8) — Hebrew אִישׁ נָבִיא, two nouns. **"I brought you up" → "I myself brought you up"** — the Hebrew has the pronoun אָנֹכִי "I" before the verb. **"the house of bondage" → "the house of slaves"** — Hebrew בֵּית עֲבָדִים.
- **"out of the hand of the Egyptians" → "from the hand of Egypt"** (v9) — Hebrew מִצְרַיִם, the name of the land and people. **"all that oppressed you" → "all your oppressors"** — Hebrew לֹחֲצֵיכֶם, a participle; לָחַץ is literally "press, squeeze".
- **"fear not" → "you shall not fear"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא תִירְאוּ, the lasting prohibition (לֹא with the imperfect); in v23 אַל־תִּירָא "do not fear". **"the Amorites" → "the Amorite"** — Hebrew הָאֱמֹרִי, singular with the article. **"ye have not obeyed my voice" → "you have not listened to my voice"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל.
- **"an angel of the LORD" → "the angel of Yahweh"** (v11, 12, 21, 22); **"the angel of God" → "the angel of *Elohim*"** (v20) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה, in v20 מַלְאַךְ הָאֱלֹהִים; the word מַלְאָךְ means "messenger", the same word as the "messengers" Gideon sends in v35. In v14, 16, 23 the speaker is named "Yahweh".
- **"an oak" → "the terebinth"** (v11, 19) — Hebrew הָאֵלָה, with the article; a great tree, the kind is not certain. **"that pertained unto Joash the Abiezrite"** — Hebrew אֲבִי הָעֶזְרִי, "the Abiezrite" (so also v24); whether "which belonged to Joash" speaks of the tree or of Ophrah is not said.
- **"threshed wheat by the winepress" → "was beating out wheat in the winepress"** (v11) — Hebrew חֹבֵט, to beat out with a stick (the verb of beating an olive tree, Deut 24:20), not the threshing of the threshing floor; בַּגַּת "in the winepress", the place where grapes are trodden (גַּת; the vat is יֶקֶב, 7:25). **"to hide it from the Midianites" → "to make it flee from the face of Midian"** — Hebrew לְהָנִיס, the causative of נוּס "flee": to get it quickly out of reach. "Hide" is not the word.
- **"thou mighty man of valour" → "mighty one of ability"** (v12) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר הֶחָיִל, with the article: "the mighty one of ability". גִּבּוֹר is "mighty one"; חַיִל is ability, strength, also wealth and an armed force; the phrase names a man of standing and strength fit for war (so Josh 1:14; 6:2; 8:3).
- **"Oh my Lord" → "Oh, my lord"** (v13); **"Oh my Lord" → "Oh, *Adonai*"** (v15) — Hebrew בִּי אֲדֹנִי in v13, literally "upon me, my lord", the words of one who asks leave to speak; in v15 the Masoretic pointing is אֲדֹנָי *Adonai*, the form used of God. The consonants are the same in both verses.
- **"if the LORD be with us, why then is all this befallen us" → "and if Yahweh is with us, why then has all this found us"** (v13) — Hebrew וְיֵשׁ יְהוָה עִמָּנוּ, literally "and there is Yahweh with us": Gideon takes up the angel's "with you" (singular) as "with us". מָצָא is "find". **"miracles" → "wonderful deeds"** — Hebrew נִפְלָאוֹת. **"told us of" → "recounted to us"** — Hebrew סִפֵּר.
- **"hath forsaken us" → "has abandoned us"** (v13) — Hebrew נָטַשׁ "leave to itself, cast off"; "forsake" is עָזַב. **"into the hands of the Midianites" → "into the palm of Midian"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew כַּף "palm", the hollow of the hand; in v1, 9 the word is יָד "hand".
- **"the LORD looked upon him" → "Yahweh turned to him"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיִּפֶן אֵלָיו. **"in this thy might" → "in this power of yours"** — Hebrew בְּכֹחֲךָ זֶה; כֹּחַ is "power". **"and thou shalt save"** — Hebrew וְהוֹשַׁעְתָּ; after the command "Go" the form may also be heard as a command, "and save". הוֹשִׁיעַ "save" is the verb of the book's "savior" (3:9, 15) and stands in v14, 15, 31, 36, 37.
- **"my family is poor in Manasseh, and I am the least" → "my thousand is the poorest in Manasseh, and I am the youngest"** (v15) — Hebrew אַלְפִּי "my thousand": the אֶלֶף is a division of a tribe, a clan (Num 1:16; 1 Sam 10:19). הַדַּל "the poor one": low, weak, of little account. הַצָּעִיר "the young one": the youngest, or the least.
- **"Surely I will be with thee" → "For I will be with you"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי אֶהְיֶה עִמָּךְ, the very words spoken to Moses in Exod 3:12. כִּי may be "for", "because", "surely", or only introduce the speech. אֶהְיֶה "I will be" is the word of Exod 3:14. **"smite" → "strike down"** — Hebrew הִכָּה.
- **"If now I have found grace in thy sight, then shew me a sign that thou talkest with me" → "If, please, I have found favor in your eyes, then make for me a sign that it is you who are speaking with me"** (v17) — Hebrew אִם־נָא; חֵן "favor"; וְעָשִׂיתָ לִּי אוֹת "and you shall make for me a sign"; שָׁאַתָּה מְדַבֵּר עִמִּי "that you are speaking with me", with the short form שֶׁ of the relative, rare in early prose.
- **"my present" → "my offering"** (v18) — Hebrew מִנְחָה: a gift brought to a superior, and the word for an offering to God; the verse does not decide which Gideon means. **"I will tarry until thou come again" → "I will sit until you return"** — Hebrew אֵשֵׁב, the verb of v11 ("sat").
- **"made ready a kid, and unleavened cakes of an ephah of flour" → "prepared a kid of the goats, and an ephah of meal as unleavened bread"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ "made"; גְּדִי־עִזִּים; אֵיפַת־קֶמַח מַצּוֹת, literally "an ephah of meal, unleavened bread". An ephah is a large measure (some twenty litres or more). **"presented it" → "brought it near"** — Hebrew וַיַּגַּשׁ. **"flesh" kept** (v19–21) — Hebrew בָּשָׂר.
- **"upon this rock" → "on this crag"** (v20); **"out of the rock"** (v21) — two Hebrew words: סֶלַע "crag" in v20, צוּר "rock" in v21.
- **"put forth the end of the staff" → "stretched out the end of the staff"** (v21) — Hebrew שָׁלַח; מִשְׁעֶנֶת is a staff to lean on. **"there rose up fire" → "the fire went up"** — Hebrew וַתַּעַל הָאֵשׁ, with the article, the verb of the "burnt offering" (v26). **"departed out of his sight" → "went from his eyes"** — Hebrew הָלַךְ מֵעֵינָיו.
- **"perceived" → "saw"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיַּרְא, the verb of "I have seen" in the same verse. **"Alas, O Lord GOD! for because I have seen" → "Alas, Lord Yahweh! For therefore I have seen"** — Hebrew אֲהָהּ אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה; "O" is not in the Hebrew. כִּי־עַל־כֵּן is literally "for on account of thus" (Gen 18:5; 19:8; 33:10); it gives the ground of the cry: "for it has come to this, that I have seen". Gideon's fear is that of Exod 33:20.
- **"Peace be unto thee" → "*Shalom* to you"** (v23) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם לְךָ, without a verb. **"fear not: thou shalt not die"** — Hebrew אַל־תִּירָא לֹא תָּמוּת.
- **"Jehovah-shalom" → "Yahweh Shalom"** (v24) — Hebrew יְהוָה שָׁלוֹם, the two words of v23: "Yahweh (is) *shalom*", "Yahweh is peace". "Jehovah" is a later mixed form of the Tetragrammaton with the vowels of *Adonai*. **"it is yet in Ophrah of the Abiezrites" → "it is still in Ophrah of the Abiezrite"** — Hebrew singular, as v11.
- **"Take thy father's young bullock, even the second bullock of seven years old" → "Take the bull of the ox that is your father's, and the second bull, seven years old"** (v25) — Hebrew קַח אֶת־פַּר־הַשּׁוֹר אֲשֶׁר לְאָבִיךָ וּפַר הַשֵּׁנִי שֶׁבַע שָׁנִים, word for word. פַּר is "bull", שׁוֹר "ox" (one head of cattle). Whether one animal is meant ("the bull, namely the second bull") or two is not clear from the Hebrew; only "the second bull" is spoken of afterwards (v26, 28). What "second" refers to is not said. The text is difficult and is left as it stands.
- **"throw down" → "tear down"** (v25); **"cast down" / "thrown down" → "broken down"** (v28, 30, 31, 32) — two Hebrew verbs: הָרַס in the command, נָתַץ in what follows.
- **"the altar of Baal" → "the altar of the Baal"** (v25, 28, 30, 31, 32) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל, always with the article: the word means "lord, owner" and is used as the name of the god.
- **"the grove" → "the Asherah"** (v25, 26, 28, 30) — Hebrew הָאֲשֵׁרָה: an object of wood (v26) that bears the name of the goddess Asherah. "Grove" follows the Greek and Latin versions (from memory). **"that is by it"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־עָלָיו, literally "that is upon it": beside it or on it.
- **"upon the top of this rock, in the ordered place" → "on the top of this strong place, in the arrangement"** (v26) — Hebrew הַמָּעוֹז "place of strength, refuge". בַּמַּעֲרָכָה, from עָרַךְ "arrange": the word for a row, a layer, a battle line. What is meant is uncertain: with the stones or the wood laid in order, or on the built-up row of stones. **"offer a burnt sacrifice" → "offer up a burnt offering"** — Hebrew וְהַעֲלִיתָ עוֹלָה, literally "make go up a going-up": verb and noun are one root (so v28).
- **"servants" → "slaves"** (v27) — Hebrew עֲבָדָיו. **"as the LORD had said" → "had spoken"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר. **"because he feared … that he could not do it by day"** — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר יָרֵא … מֵעֲשׂוֹת יוֹמָם, literally "as he feared … from doing by day".
- **"they said one to another" → "each man to his neighbor"** (v29) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ. **"when they enquired and asked" → "they inquired and sought"** — Hebrew וַיִּדְרְשׁוּ וַיְבַקְשׁוּ, two verbs that both mean "seek"; English has no second plain word, so the first is "inquired".
- **"that he may die" → "and let him die"** (v30) — Hebrew וְיָמֹת, a wish.
- **"Will ye plead for Baal? will ye save him?" → "Is it you who will contend for the Baal? Or is it you who will save him?"** (v31) — the Hebrew sets the pronoun "you" before each verb. רִיב "contend" is the verb in the name Jerubbaal (v32). **"he that will plead for him, let him be put to death whilst it is yet morning" → "Whoever contends for him shall be put to death by the morning"** — Hebrew עַד־הַבֹּקֶר, literally "until the morning". **"because one hath cast down his altar" → "for he has broken down his altar"** — Hebrew כִּי נָתַץ, active, with no subject named (so also v32): "he", the man who did it. **"a god"** — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים, here of Baal.
- **"Therefore on that day he called him Jerubbaal" → "And he called him on that day Jerubbaal"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא־לוֹ, "he called him" or "one called him"; no "therefore". The name יְרֻבַּעַל is explained by the words that follow, יָרֶב בּוֹ הַבַּעַל "let the Baal contend against him" (בּוֹ, "against him" or "with him"). The name itself can be heard as "let Baal contend" or "Baal contends".
- **"went over, and pitched" → "passed over and camped"** (v33) — Hebrew עָבַר (over the Jordan; the river is not named), חָנָה.
- **"came upon Gideon" → "clothed Gideon"** (v34) — Hebrew לָבְשָׁה *lavshah*, the verb of putting on a garment: the spirit "clothed Gideon", or "clothed itself with Gideon" (also 1 Chr 12:18; 2 Chr 24:20).
- **"he blew a trumpet" → "he blew on the shofar"** (v34) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָר *shofar*, a ram's horn, not a metal trumpet. **"Abiezer was gathered after him" → "Abiezer was called out after him"** (v34, 35) — Hebrew נִזְעַק, the passive of the verb "cry out" (v6): summoned by a cry.
- **"throughout all Manasseh; who also was gathered" → "through all Manasseh, and it also was called out"** (v35) — Hebrew גַּם־הוּא "it also", singular. **"to meet them"** — Hebrew לִקְרָאתָם; who "them" are is not said.
- **"If thou wilt save Israel by mine hand, as thou hast said" → "If you are saving Israel by my hand, as you have spoken"** (v36, 37) — Hebrew אִם־יֶשְׁךָ מוֹשִׁיעַ, literally "if there is you saving": if you are indeed the one who saves. דִּבֶּר "speak".
- **"I will put a fleece of wool in the floor" → "I am setting the fleece of wool on the threshing floor"** (v37) — Hebrew מַצִּיג, a participle; the fleece has the article. **"dry upon all the earth beside" → "on all the ground dryness"** (v37, 39, 40) — Hebrew חֹרֶב, a noun, "dryness"; הָאָרֶץ is the word for "land, earth", here of the ground around.
- **"thrust the fleece together, and wringed the dew out of the fleece, a bowl full of water" → "pressed the fleece and drained dew from the fleece, the fullness of the bowl of water"** (v38) — Hebrew וַיָּזַר "pressed", וַיִּמֶץ "drained out"; מְלוֹא הַסֵּפֶל מָיִם, literally "the fullness of the bowl, water". סֵפֶל is found only here and in 5:25.
- **"Let not thine anger be hot against me, and I will speak but this once: let me prove, I pray thee, but this once" → "Let not your anger burn against me, and let me speak only this once. Let me test, please, only this once"** (v39) — Hebrew אַף "anger", literally "nose"; וַאֲדַבְּרָה, a form of asking leave; אַךְ הַפַּעַם and רַק־הַפַּעַם, two words for "only"; נָא "please" twice. נִסָּה "test" is the verb of Exod 17:2; Deut 6:16.

### Notes
- **Midianite oppression** — seven years of locust-like raiding. Israel becomes poor and is driven to dens and caves.
- **Gideon's call** (v11–24) — the angel of Yahweh comes to a man beating out wheat in a winepress, out of sight of Midian, and greets him as "mighty one of ability".
- **Gideon's doubt** — repeated throughout: his low standing (v15), the request for a sign (v17–22), the two tests with the fleece (v36–40).
- **Altar of the Baal torn down** (v25–32) — Gideon's first command is domestic, not military: to tear down the altar on his own father's ground. Joash answers the men of the city: if the Baal is a god, let him contend for himself.
- **Jerubbaal** — becomes Gideon's second name (7:1; 8:29, 35; 9; 1 Sam 12:11; in 2 Sam 11:21 "Jerubbesheth", with *bosheth* "shame" in place of Baal).
- **Fleece test** (v36–40) — reversed the second time: first dew on the fleece and dryness on the ground, then dryness on the fleece and dew on the ground.
