# Judges 11

[1] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ the ⟦gileadite|Giladi|Gileadite⟧ was a mighty one of ability, and he was the son of a woman, a prostitute; and ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ begot ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧.

[2] And the wife of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ bore him sons; and the sons of the wife grew up and drove out ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧, and they said to him, "You shall not inherit in the house of our father, for you are the son of another woman."

[3] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ fled from the face of his brothers and dwelt in the land of ⟦tob|Tov|Tob⟧; and empty men gathered themselves to ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧, and they went out with him.

[4] And it came to pass after some days that the sons of Ammon fought with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] And it came to pass, when the sons of Ammon fought with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that the elders of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ went to take ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ from the land of ⟦tob|Tov|Tob⟧.

[6] And they said to ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧, "Come and be our captain, and let us fight against the sons of Ammon."

[7] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ said to the elders of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, "Was it not you who hated me and drove me out of the house of my father? And why have you come to me now, when you are in distress?"

[8] And the elders of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ said to ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧, "Therefore now we have returned to you; and you shall go with us and fight against the sons of Ammon, and you shall be for us head of all the inhabitants of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧."

[9] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ said to the elders of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, "If you are bringing me back to fight against the sons of Ammon, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sets them before me, I, I will be your head."

[10] And the elders of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ said to ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ shall be hearing between us: surely according to your word, so we will do."

[11] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ went with the elders of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, and the people put him over them as head and as captain; and ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ spoke all his words before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧.

[12] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ sent messengers to the king of the sons of Ammon, saying, "What have I to do with you, that you have come to me to fight in my land?"

[13] And the king of the sons of Ammon said to the messengers of ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧, "Because ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ took my land when he came up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the Arnon and as far as the ⟦jabbok|Yabboq|Jabbok⟧ and as far as the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧; and now, return them in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧."

[14] And yet again ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ sent messengers to the king of the sons of Ammon,

[15] and he said to him, "Thus says ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧: ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did not take the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ or the land of the sons of Ammon.

[16] For when they came up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went in the wilderness as far as the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧ and came to ⟦kadesh|Qadesh|Kadesh⟧.

[17] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, 'Let me pass, please, through your land'; and the king of Edom did not listen. And also to the king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ he sent, and he was not willing. And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ dwelt in ⟦kadesh|Qadesh|Kadesh⟧.

[18] And he went in the wilderness and went around the land of Edom and the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and he came from the rising of the sun to the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and they camped across the Arnon; and they did not come into the border of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, for the Arnon is the border of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

[19] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sent messengers to ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, king of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to him, 'Let us pass, please, through your land as far as my place.'

[20] And ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ did not trust ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to pass through his border; and ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ gathered all his people, and they camped in ⟦jahaz|Yahtzah|Jahaz⟧, and he fought with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, gave ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ and all his people into the hand of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they struck them down; and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ took possession of all the land of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, the inhabitant of that land.

[22] And they took possession of all the border of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, from the Arnon and as far as the ⟦jabbok|Yabboq|Jabbok⟧, and from the wilderness and as far as the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[23] And now ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, has dispossessed the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ from before his people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and you, will you take possession of him?

[24] Is it not that which ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧ your god gives you to possess — of that you will take possession? And everyone whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has dispossessed from before us, of him we will take possession.

[25] And now, are you surely better than ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ the son of ⟦zippor|Tzippor|Zippor⟧, king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧? Did he surely contend with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, or did he surely fight against them?

[26] While ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ dwelt in ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ and in its daughters, and in Aroer and in its daughters, and in all the cities that are alongside the Arnon, three hundred years — and why did you not deliver them in that time?

[27] And I, I have not sinned against you, and you are doing evil with me, to fight against me. Let ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the Judge, judge today between the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the sons of Ammon."

[28] And the king of the sons of Ammon did not listen to the words of ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ that he had sent to him.

[29] And the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧, and he passed through ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, and he passed through ⟦mizpeh|Mitzpeh|Mizpeh⟧ of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, and from ⟦mizpeh|Mitzpeh|Mizpeh⟧ of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ he passed over to the sons of Ammon.

[30] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ vowed a vow to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "If you will surely give the sons of Ammon into my hand,

[31] then it shall be that the one coming out, that comes out from the doors of my house to meet me when I return in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ from the sons of Ammon, shall be ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧, and I will offer it up as a burnt offering."

[32] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ passed over to the sons of Ammon to fight against them, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave them into his hand.

[33] And he struck them from Aroer and as far as your coming to ⟦minnith|Minnit|Minnith⟧, twenty cities, and as far as ⟦abel-keramim|Avel Keramim|Abel-keramim⟧, a very great blow; and the sons of Ammon were brought low before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[34] And ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ came to the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧, to his house, and behold, his daughter was coming out to meet him with timbrels and with dances. And there was only she, an only one; he had from himself no son or daughter.

[35] And it came to pass, when he saw her, that he tore his garments and said, "Alas, my daughter! You have surely made me bow down, and you, you have become one of those who trouble me. And I, I have opened my mouth to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I cannot turn back."

[36] And she said to him, "My father, you have opened your mouth to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; do to me according to what has gone out of your mouth, after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done vengeance for you on your enemies, on the sons of Ammon."

[37] And she said to her father, "Let this thing be done for me: let go of me two months, and let me go, and I will go down upon the mountains and weep over my virginity, I and my friends."

[38] And he said, "Go." And he sent her away two months; and she went, she and her friends, and she wept over her virginity upon the mountains.

[39] And it came to pass at the end of two months that she returned to her father, and he did to her his vow that he had vowed; and she had not known a man. And it became a statute in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[40] from year to year the daughters of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ go to recount the daughter of ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧ the ⟦gileadite|Giladi|Gileadite⟧, four days in the year.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v21, 23, 24) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v24 the same word is used of Chemosh, "your god": כְּמוֹשׁ אֱלֹהֶיךָ beside יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵינוּ.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Ammon / of Israel" → "sons of Ammon / of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי "sons of".
- **"a mighty man of valour" → "a mighty one of ability"** (v1) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר חַיִל; חַיִל is ability, strength, also wealth and an armed force. "Warrior" is not in the words.
- **"the son of an harlot" → "the son of a woman, a prostitute"** (v1) — Hebrew בֶּן־אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה, two nouns side by side (as Josh 2:1). **"begat" → "begot"** — Hebrew וַיּוֹלֶד. Gilead is here the name of a man; elsewhere in the chapter it is the land.
- **"thrust out" → "drove out"** (v2, 7) — Hebrew גֵּרֵשׁ. **"a strange woman" → "another woman"** (v2) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה אַחֶרֶת.
- **"fled from his brethren" → "fled from the face of his brothers"** (v3) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי.
- **"vain men" → "empty men"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲנָשִׁים רֵיקִים, literally "empty men": men with nothing (cf. 9:4; 1 Sam 22:2). Whether "without property" or "without worth" is meant, the word does not say. **"were gathered" → "gathered themselves"** — Hebrew וַיִּתְלַקְּטוּ, the reflexive stem of לָקַט "gather, pick up".
- **"in process of time" → "after some days"** (v4) — Hebrew מִיָּמִים, literally "from days".
- **"made war against" → "fought with"** (v4, 5, 20) — Hebrew נִלְחַם עִם "fight with"; נִלְחַם בְּ is "fight against" (v6, 8, 9, 12, 25, 27, 32).
- **"went to fetch" → "went to take"** (v5) — Hebrew לָקַחַת.
- **"captain" kept** (v6, 11) — Hebrew קָצִין, one who decides: a leader in war (Josh 10:24). In v8, 9, 11 the word is רֹאשׁ "head"; v11 has both.
- **"Did not ye hate me" → "Was it not you who hated me"** (v7) — the Hebrew sets the pronoun first: הֲלֹא אַתֶּם שְׂנֵאתֶם אוֹתִי.
- **"Therefore we turn again to thee now" → "Therefore now we have returned to you"** (v8) — Hebrew לָכֵן עַתָּה שַׁבְנוּ אֵלֶיךָ; לָכֵן is "therefore", not "nevertheless".
- **"If ye bring me home again … and the LORD deliver them before me, shall I be your head?" → "If you are bringing me back … and Yahweh sets them before me, I, I will be your head."** (v9) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי אֶהְיֶה לָכֶם לְרֹאשׁ: there is no mark of a question, and the pronoun stands first for weight. It can also be spoken as a question. נָתַן לִפְנֵי is "set before" (the verb is "give"), as Deut 2:31, 33. "Home" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"The LORD be witness between us, if we do not so according to thy words" → "Yahweh shall be hearing between us: surely according to your word, so we will do."** (v10) — Hebrew יְהוָה יִהְיֶה שֹׁמֵעַ בֵּינוֹתֵינוּ, literally "Yahweh shall be one who hears between us"; "witness" is another word. The second half is the oath form אִם־לֹא "if not …", which means a sworn "surely"; "word" is singular.
- **"made him head and captain over them" → "put him over them as head and as captain"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיָּשִׂימוּ … אוֹתוֹ עֲלֵיהֶם לְרֹאשׁ וּלְקָצִין. **"uttered all his words" → "spoke all his words"** — Hebrew דִּבֵּר. **"in Mizpeh" → "at the Mizpah"** (v11, 34) — Hebrew הַמִּצְפָּה with the article; v29 has another form, מִצְפֵּה גִלְעָד "Mizpeh of Gilead".
- **"What hast thou to do with me" → "What have I to do with you"** (v12) — Hebrew מַה־לִּי וָלָךְ, literally "What to me and to you?"
- **"when they came up" → "when he came up"** (v13) — Hebrew בַּעֲלוֹתוֹ, singular: Israel as one. In v16 the form is plural (בַּעֲלוֹתָם), and v18 goes from "he went … he came" to "they camped … they did not come" as the Hebrew does.
- **"restore those lands again peaceably" → "return them in *shalom*"** (v13) — Hebrew הָשִׁיבָה אֶתְהֶן בְּשָׁלוֹם: "them" is feminine plural (the lands or cities; no noun is given); בְּשָׁלוֹם "in peace".
- **"sent messengers again" → "yet again … sent messengers"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיּוֹסֶף עוֹד … וַיִּשְׁלַח, literally "and he added yet … and sent".
- **v15–22 and Num 20–21; Deut 2** — Jephthah's account uses the words of Num 20:14–21; 21:4, 13, 21–26 and Deut 2:26–37, and where the Hebrew is the same the wording is the same: "Let me pass, please, through your land", "went around the land of Edom", "for the Arnon is the border of Moab", "Sihon king of the Amorite", "to pass through his border; and Sihon gathered all his people", "took possession of". The sending to the king of Moab (v17) is told only here.
- **"walked through the wilderness unto the Red sea" → "went in the wilderness as far as the Sea of Reeds"** (v16) — Hebrew יַם־סוּף *yam suf*, "sea of reeds".
- **"would not hearken … would not consent" → "did not listen … was not willing"** (v17) — Hebrew וְלֹא שָׁמַע … וְלֹא אָבָה. **"abode" → "dwelt"** — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"compassed" → "went around"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיָּסָב, as Num 21:4. **"came by the east side of the land of Moab" → "came from the rising of the sun to the land of Moab"** — Hebrew מִמִּזְרַח־שֶׁמֶשׁ לְאֶרֶץ מוֹאָב: on the side of sunrise with regard to the land of Moab, that is, east of it. **"pitched on the other side of Arnon" → "camped across the Arnon"** — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר אַרְנוֹן. **"within the border" → "into the border"** — Hebrew גְּבוּל, the border and the land inside it.
- **"into my place" → "as far as my place"** (v19) — Hebrew עַד־מְקוֹמִי; the request is in the plural ("let us pass"), the place is "my".
- **"Sihon trusted not Israel to pass through his coast" → "Sihon did not trust Israel to pass through his border"** (v20) — Hebrew וְלֹא־הֶאֱמִין סִיחוֹן אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל עֲבֹר בִּגְבֻלוֹ. הֶאֱמִין is elsewhere "believe"; with a person and an act it is "trust … to". Num 21:23 has "did not allow" (לֹא־נָתַן) in the same sentence. **"pitched in Jahaz" → "camped in Jahaz"** — Hebrew בְּיָהְצָה.
- **"smote them" → "struck them down"** (v21) — Hebrew הִכָּה. **"possessed" → "took possession of"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew יָרַשׁ. **"the inhabitants of that country" → "the inhabitant of that land"** — Hebrew יוֹשֵׁב הָאָרֶץ, singular. **"all the coasts" → "all the border"** (v22) — Hebrew גְּבוּל, singular.
- **"shouldest thou possess it?" → "will you take possession of him?"** (v23) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה תִּירָשֶׁנּוּ. Two forms of one verb stand against each other in v23–24: the hifil הוֹרִישׁ "dispossess" (Yahweh drives the Amorite out) and the qal יָרַשׁ "take possession of". The object of the qal here is "him", the Amorite just named: to take over what was his. With a person as object the qal is elsewhere "dispossess" (Deut 2:12, 21); here it is kept one with v21, 22 and v24, where the object is what a god gives.
- **"Wilt not thou possess that which Chemosh thy god giveth thee to possess?" → "Is it not that which Chemosh your god gives you to possess — of that you will take possession?"** (v24) — Hebrew הֲלֹא אֵת אֲשֶׁר יוֹרִישְׁךָ כְּמוֹשׁ אֱלֹהֶיךָ אוֹתוֹ תִירָשׁ: the object stands first and is taken up again by אוֹתוֹ. יוֹרִישְׁךָ is the hifil with two objects, "makes you possess". **"whomsoever the LORD our God shall drive out … them will we possess" → "everyone whom Yahweh our Elohim has dispossessed … of him we will take possession"** — Hebrew הוֹרִישׁ, a perfect; אוֹתוֹ "him / it", singular.
- **"art thou any thing better … did he ever strive … or did he ever fight" → "are you surely better … Did he surely contend … or did he surely fight"** (v25) — three times the infinitive absolute with its verb: הֲטוֹב טוֹב … הֲרוֹב רָב … אִם־נִלְחֹם נִלְחַם, literally "being better are you better … contending did he contend … fighting did he fight". רִיב is "contend".
- **"and her towns" → "and in its daughters"** (v26) — Hebrew וּבִבְנוֹתֶיהָ: the settlements that belong to a city, as Num 21:25. **"by the coasts of Arnon" → "alongside the Arnon"** — Hebrew עַל־יְדֵי אַרְנוֹן, literally "on the hands of". **"why therefore did ye not recover them" → "and why did you not deliver them"** — Hebrew הִצַּלְתֶּם, "deliver, snatch away"; the "you" is plural. **"within that time" → "in that time"** — Hebrew בָּעֵת הַהִיא.
- **"thou doest me wrong to war against me" → "you are doing evil with me, to fight against me"** (v27) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה אִתִּי רָעָה. **"the LORD the Judge be judge this day" → "Let Yahweh, the Judge, judge today"** — Hebrew יִשְׁפֹּט יְהוָה הַשֹּׁפֵט הַיּוֹם: the verb and the title are one root.
- **"hearkened not unto the words" → "did not listen to the words"** (v28) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל.
- **"Spirit of the LORD came upon" → "the spirit of Yahweh was upon"** (v29) — Hebrew וַתְּהִי עַל־יִפְתָּח רוּחַ יְהוָה, the verb "be": "was upon", or "came to be upon", as 3:10; רוּחַ is "spirit", "wind" and "breath". The book has other verbs of the spirit in 13:25 ("began to stir") and 14:6, 19 ("rushed upon").
- **"passed over Gilead … passed over unto the children of Ammon" → "passed through Gilead … passed over to the sons of Ammon"** (v29) — one verb, עָבַר, three times: twice with אֶת and a land ("pass through"), the third time with "the sons of Ammon" and no preposition; v32 has it with אֶל "to".
- **"If thou shalt without fail deliver" → "If you will surely give"** (v30) — Hebrew אִם־נָתוֹן תִּתֵּן, the infinitive absolute with its verb: "giving you give".
- **"whatsoever cometh forth of the doors of my house … shall surely be the LORD's, and I will offer it up for a burnt offering" → "the one coming out, that comes out from the doors of my house … shall be Yahweh's, and I will offer it up as a burnt offering"** (v31) — Hebrew וְהָיָה הַיּוֹצֵא אֲשֶׁר יֵצֵא מִדַּלְתֵי בֵיתִי לִקְרָאתִי … וְהָיָה לַיהוָה וְהַעֲלִיתִהוּ עוֹלָה, word for word: "and it shall be, the one coming out, who / that comes out from the doors of my house to meet me …, and he / it shall be Yahweh's, and I will make him / it go up, a burnt offering." הַיּוֹצֵא is a masculine singular participle with the article, and the suffix ־הוּ is masculine singular; Hebrew uses the masculine for a person and for an animal alike, and also where the kind is not known. English has to choose a pronoun; "it" is KJV's and is kept, and it must not be heard as deciding for an animal. "Surely" is not in the Hebrew. The last clause is joined with וְ "and"; the reading "or I will offer it up" is not what is written. הֶעֱלָה עוֹלָה is "make go up a going-up", as Gen 22:2.
- **"smote them … with a very great slaughter" → "struck them … a very great blow"** (v33) — Hebrew מַכָּה גְּדוֹלָה מְאֹד; מַכָּה is the noun of הִכָּה "strike". **"even till thou come to Minnith" → "and as far as your coming to Minnith"** — Hebrew וְעַד־בּוֹאֲךָ מִנִּית. **"the plain of the vineyards" → "Abel-keramim"** — Hebrew אָבֵל כְּרָמִים, a place name ("meadow of vineyards").
- **"were subdued before" → "were brought low before"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיִּכָּנְעוּ מִפְּנֵי, literally "were brought low from the face of", as 3:30; 8:28. "Subdue" is another verb (כָּבַשׁ).
- **"his daughter came out" → "his daughter was coming out"** (v34) — Hebrew participle יֹצֵאת, the same verb as in the vow (v31). **"timbrels and dances" kept** — Hebrew תֻּפִּים, hand drums, and מְחֹלוֹת, as Exod 15:20.
- **"she was his only child; beside her he had neither son nor daughter" → "there was only she, an only one; he had from himself no son or daughter"** (v34) — Hebrew וְרַק הִיא יְחִידָה אֵין־לוֹ מִמֶּנּוּ בֵּן אוֹ־בַת. יְחִידָה is "an only one" (the word of Gen 22:2); "child" and "his" are not written. מִמֶּנּוּ is "from him", masculine: no son or daughter of his own. The old versions have "besides her" (cited from memory), which needs מִמֶּנָּה; the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"rent his clothes" → "tore his garments"** (v35) — Hebrew בְּגָדָיו.
- **"thou hast brought me very low, and thou art one of them that trouble me" → "You have surely made me bow down, and you, you have become one of those who trouble me"** (v35) — Hebrew הַכְרֵעַ הִכְרַעְתִּנִי וְאַתְּ הָיִיתְ בְּעֹכְרָי. הִכְרִיעַ is "make bow down, bring to the knees" (infinitive absolute with its verb); עָכַר is "trouble", the verb of Josh 7:25. The two words play on each other: *hikhra'tini* … *be'okhrai*, the same three consonants in another order.
- **"I have opened my mouth unto the LORD, and I cannot go back" → "… and I cannot turn back"** (v35, 36) — Hebrew פָּצִיתִי־פִי "I have opened my mouth wide" (פָּצָה, as Num 16:30 of the ground); לָשׁוּב "return, turn back".
- **"forasmuch as the LORD hath taken vengeance for thee of thine enemies" → "after Yahweh has done vengeance for you on your enemies"** (v36) — Hebrew אַחֲרֵי אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה לְךָ יְהוָה נְקָמוֹת מֵאֹיְבֶיךָ; נְקָמוֹת is plural, "vengeances".
- **"let me alone two months, that I may go up and down upon the mountains, and bewail my virginity, I and my fellows" → "let go of me two months, and let me go, and I will go down upon the mountains and weep over my virginity, I and my friends"** (v37) — Hebrew הַרְפֵּה מִמֶּנִּי "let go of me" (as Deut 9:14); וְאֵלְכָה וְיָרַדְתִּי עַל־הֶהָרִים "and let me go, and I will go down upon the mountains": the Hebrew says "go down", though one goes up to mountains; it is kept as written. בְּתוּלַי "my virginity". "Friends" is the qere וְרֵעוֹתָי; the written form (ורעיתי) is another form of the same word.
- **"she went with her companions, and bewailed" → "she went, she and her friends, and she wept"** (v38) — the verbs are singular. **"sent her away for two months"** — Hebrew שִׁלַּח.
- **"who did with her according to his vow which he had vowed: and she knew no man" → "he did to her his vow that he had vowed; and she had not known a man"** (v39) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ לָהּ אֶת־נִדְרוֹ אֲשֶׁר נָדָר וְהִיא לֹא־יָדְעָה אִישׁ, word for word. "According to" is not in the Hebrew. What he did is told only in these words; the verse adds nothing to them. The last clause is a perfect with the subject first: "and she — she had not known a man".
- **"And it was a custom in Israel" → "And it became a statute in Israel"** (v39) — Hebrew וַתְּהִי־חֹק בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל. חֹק is "statute", a fixed rule. The verb is feminine, the noun masculine: "it became" (the feminine used for a matter), or "she became a statute in Israel".
- **"went yearly to lament" → "from year to year … go to recount"** (v40) — Hebrew מִיָּמִים יָמִימָה, literally "from days to days", as Exod 13:10. The verb תֵּלַכְנָה tells of a lasting practice: "go" or "used to go". לְתַנּוֹת לְ is found only here and in 5:11 (יְתַנּוּ); the meaning is not certain. The sense best supported from 5:11 is "recount, tell again"; the old versions have "lament" (Septuagint, Vulgate: cited from memory), KJV's margin "talk with". Whether the daughters of Israel mourned her, praised her or told her story, the word does not settle.

### Notes
- **Jephthah's background** (v1–3) — the son of a prostitute, driven out by his brothers, leader of "empty men".
- **The message to the king** (v12–27):
  - Israel did not take the land of Moab or of Ammon; the land in dispute was taken from Sihon the Amorite (Num 21:21–26; Deut 2:26–37).
  - "Three hundred years" (v26) — a number of years counted from the taking of Heshbon.
  - "Chemosh your god" (v24) — Chemosh is elsewhere the god of Moab (Num 21:29; 1 Kgs 11:7, 33); the god of the sons of Ammon is elsewhere Milcom (1 Kgs 11:5, 33). The speech is addressed to the king of the sons of Ammon and speaks of Moab's land, king and god (v15, 17, 18, 25).
- **The vow** (v30–31) — the words do not say who or what Jephthah expected to come out. "The one coming out … to meet me" is masculine singular in Hebrew, which fits a person or an animal.
- **What was done** (v39) — the text says: "he did to her his vow that he had vowed; and she had not known a man." Two readings are old: that he offered her as a burnt offering, as the vow says; and that she was given to Yahweh for life and remained a virgin (so some medieval Jewish interpreters; cited from memory). The verse does not say more than its words.
- **The yearly custom** (v39–40) — four days in the year, kept by the daughters of Israel.
- **Heb 11:32** names Jephthah among those who acted by faith.
