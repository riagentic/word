# Judges 18

[1] In those days there was no king in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. And in those days the tribe of the ⟦danites|Dani|Danites⟧ was seeking for itself an inheritance to dwell in, for to that day it had not fallen to it as an inheritance in the midst of the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And the sons of Dan sent from their clan five men from their whole number, men who were sons of ability, from ⟦zorah|Tzorah|Zorah⟧ and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land and to examine it, and they said to them, "Go, examine the land." And they came to the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, as far as the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧, and they lodged there.

[3] They were by the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧, and they recognized the voice of the young man, the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, and they turned aside there and said to him, "Who brought you here? And what are you doing in this place? And what have you here?"

[4] And he said to them, "Like this and like this ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ has done to me, and he hired me, and I became priest to him."

[5] And they said to him, "Ask, please, of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and let us know whether our way on which we are going will prosper."

[6] And the priest said to them, "Go in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧. In front of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is your way on which you go."

[7] And the five men went and came to ⟦laish|Layish|Laish⟧, and they saw the people who were in its midst, dwelling in security, after the manner of the ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧, quiet and secure, and there was no one in the land putting anything to shame, possessing restraint; and they were far from the ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧, and they had no matter with any man.

[8] And they came to their brothers at ⟦zorah|Tzorah|Zorah⟧ and Eshtaol, and their brothers said to them, "What are you?"

[9] And they said, "Arise, and let us go up against them, for we have seen the land, and behold, it is very good. And you are keeping still! Do not be sluggish to go, to come in, to take possession of the land.

[10] When you come, you will come to a secure people, and the land is wide on both hands, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given it into your hand: a place where there is no lack of any thing that is in the earth."

[11] And there set out from there, from the clan of the ⟦danites|Dani|Danites⟧, from ⟦zorah|Tzorah|Zorah⟧ and from Eshtaol, six hundred men girded with weapons of war.

[12] And they went up and camped at ⟦kiriath-jearim|Qiryat Yearim|Kiriath-jearim⟧ in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. Therefore they have called that place ⟦mahaneh-dan|Machaneh Dan|Mahaneh-dan⟧ to this day; behold, it is behind ⟦kiriath-jearim|Qiryat Yearim|Kiriath-jearim⟧.

[13] And they passed on from there to the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and came as far as the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧.

[14] And the five men who had gone to spy out the land of ⟦laish|Layish|Laish⟧ answered and said to their brothers, "Do you know that there are in these houses an ephod and ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧ and a carved image and a molten image? And now, know what you will do."

[15] And they turned aside there and came to the house of the young man, the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧, the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧, and they asked him about ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[16] And six hundred men girded with their weapons of war were standing at the entrance of the gate, who were of the sons of Dan.

[17] And the five men who had gone to spy out the land went up; they came in there; they took the carved image and the ephod and the ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧ and the molten image. And the priest was standing at the entrance of the gate, and the six hundred men who were girded with the weapons of war.

[18] And these came into the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ and took the carved image of the ephod and the ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧ and the molten image. And the priest said to them, "What are you doing?"

[19] And they said to him, "Be silent! Put your hand on your mouth, and go with us, and be to us a father and a priest. Is it better that you be priest to the house of one man, or that you be priest to a tribe and to a clan in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"

[20] And the heart of the priest was good, and he took the ephod and the ⟦teraphim|teraphim|household gods⟧ and the carved image and came into the midst of the people.

[21] And they turned and went, and they put the little ones and the livestock and the valuables before them.

[22] They had gone far from the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧, and the men who were in the houses that were by the house of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ were called out, and they caught up with the sons of Dan.

[23] And they called to the sons of Dan, and they turned their faces and said to ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧, "What ails you, that you have been called out?"

[24] And he said, "My gods that I made you have taken, and the priest, and you have gone, and what have I more? And what is this that you say to me, 'What ails you?'"

[25] And the sons of Dan said to him, "Do not let your voice be heard with us, lest men bitter of soul fall upon you, and you gather in your soul and the soul of your house."

[26] And the sons of Dan went on their way, and ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ saw that they were stronger than he, and he turned and returned to his house.

[27] And they, they took what ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ had made and the priest who had been his, and they came upon ⟦laish|Layish|Laish⟧, upon a people quiet and secure, and they struck them with the edge of the sword, and the city they burned with fire.

[28] And there was no deliverer, for it was far from ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, and they had no matter with any man; and it was in the valley that belongs to ⟦beth-rehob|Beit Rechov|Beth-rehob⟧. And they built the city and dwelt in it.

[29] And they called the name of the city Dan, by the name of Dan their father, who was born to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; but ⟦laish|Layish|Laish⟧ was the name of the city at the first.

[30] And the sons of Dan raised up for themselves the carved image, and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ the son of Gershom, the son of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of the ⟦danites|Dani|Danites⟧ until the day of the exile of the land.

[31] And they set for themselves the carved image of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ that he had made, all the days that the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was in Shiloh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v6) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 10, 31) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v31 with the article, "the house of the Elohim". In v5 the men say only "ask of Elohim"; the priest answers with the name Yahweh (v6). In v24 the same word is Micah's "gods that I made".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"In those days there was no king in Israel"** (v1) — the first half of the sentence of 17:6 (19:1; 21:25), in the same words.
- **"the tribe of the Danites sought them an inheritance to dwell in; for unto that day all their inheritance had not fallen unto them" → "the tribe of the Danite was seeking for itself an inheritance to dwell in, for to that day it had not fallen to it as an inheritance"** (v1) — Hebrew שֵׁבֶט הַדָּנִי, a singular, "the Danite"; מְבַקֶּשׁ is a participle. כִּי לֹא־נָפְלָה לּוֹ … בְּנַחֲלָה: the verb is feminine and has no named subject (land is meant); "all" is not written. "Tribe" is שֵׁבֶט here and in v19, 30.
- **"children of Dan" → "sons of Dan"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־דָן.
- **"five men from their coasts, men of valour" → "five men from their whole number, men who were sons of ability"** (v2) — Hebrew מִקְצוֹתָם, literally "from their ends" (out of all of them, from one end to the other); בְּנֵי־חַיִל "sons of ability": חַיִל is ability, strength, also an armed force.
- **"to spy out the land, and to search it" → "to spy out the land and to examine it"** (v2) — Hebrew רִגֵּל "spy out" and חָקַר "examine". **"mount Ephraim" → "the hill country of Ephraim"** (v2, 13).
- **"When they were by the house of Micah, they knew the voice" → "They were by the house of Micah, and they recognized the voice"** (v3) — two clauses joined by "and"; הִכִּיר is "recognize" (his voice, or his way of speaking). **"what makest thou in this place? and what hast thou here?"** — Hebrew וּמָה־אַתָּה עֹשֶׂה בָּזֶה וּמַה־לְּךָ פֹה.
- **"Thus and thus dealeth Micah with me, and hath hired me, and I am his priest" → "Like this and like this Micah has done to me, and he hired me, and I became priest to him"** (v4) — Hebrew כָּזֹה וְכָזֶה עָשָׂה לִי.
- **"Ask counsel, we pray thee, of God, that we may know whether our way which we go shall be prosperous" → "Ask, please, of Elohim, and let us know whether our way on which we are going will prosper"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁאַל־נָא בֵאלֹהִים: "ask of / by Elohim" (as by the ephod of 17:5); "counsel" is not written. תַצְלִיחַ is "prosper" (it may also be read "whether you / it will make our way prosper").
- **"Go in peace: before the LORD is your way wherein ye go" → "Go in shalom. In front of Yahweh is your way on which you go"** (v6) — Hebrew לְכוּ לְשָׁלוֹם, literally "go to *shalom*"; נֹכַח יְהוָה "opposite, straight in front of Yahweh": under his eye. The saying does not state whether for good or for harm.
- **"how they dwelt careless, after the manner of the Zidonians, quiet and secure" → "dwelling in security, after the manner of the Sidonians, quiet and secure"** (v7) — Hebrew יוֹשֶׁבֶת־לָבֶטַח, a feminine participle (as of a city) beside "the people"; כְּמִשְׁפַּט is "after the custom of" (the word "judgment"); שֹׁקֵט וּבֹטֵחַ "quiet and trusting": בֹּטֵחַ is of one root with בֶּטַח "security".
- **"there was no magistrate in the land, that might put them to shame in any thing" → "there was no one in the land putting anything to shame, possessing restraint"** (v7) — Hebrew וְאֵין־מַכְלִים דָּבָר בָּאָרֶץ יוֹרֵשׁ עֶצֶר, given word for word; the meaning is uncertain. מַכְלִים is "one who puts to shame, humiliates"; עֶצֶר is found only here (root "restrain, hold back": perhaps "rule" or "pressure"; others take it as "wealth"); יוֹרֵשׁ is "one who possesses". It is commonly understood: no one in the land held power over them to do them any hurt.
- **"had no business with any man" → "had no matter with any man"** (v7, 28) — Hebrew וְדָבָר אֵין־לָהֶם עִם־אָדָם: דָּבָר "word, matter"; אָדָם "man, mankind". Some Greek witnesses read "with Aram" (אֲרָם for אָדָם, one letter: cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"What say ye?" → "What are you?"** (v8) — Hebrew מָה אַתֶּם, two words; no verb. It asks how they have fared.
- **"and are ye still? be not slothful" → "And you are keeping still! Do not be sluggish"** (v9) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם מַחְשִׁים, a statement in form ("and you are being silent / inactive"); it may be heard as a question. תֵּעָצְלוּ is from the root of עָצֵל "sluggard" (the verb only here).
- **"ye shall come unto a people secure, and to a large land" → "you will come to a secure people, and the land is wide on both hands"** (v10) — Hebrew רַחֲבַת יָדַיִם, as in Gen 34:21. **"into your hands" → "into your hand"** — singular. **"in the earth"** — Hebrew בָּאָרֶץ, "in the earth" or "in the land".
- **"there went from thence of the family of the Danites … six hundred men appointed with weapons of war" → "there set out from there, from the clan of the Danite … six hundred men girded with weapons of war"** (v11, 16, 17) — Hebrew נָסַע "set out"; חָגוּר "girded"; כְּלֵי מִלְחָמָה "implements of war".
- **"pitched in Kirjath-jearim" → "camped at Kiriath-jearim"** (v12) — Hebrew חָנָה. **"Mahaneh-dan"** — Hebrew מַחֲנֵה־דָן, "the camp of Dan" (13:25); the name is kept. **"behind"** — Hebrew אַחֲרֵי, that is, to the west of it (one faces east).
- **"answered"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲנוּ: they begin to speak. **"the country of Laish" → "the land of Laish"** — Hebrew הָאָרֶץ לַיִשׁ, "the land, Laish". **"now therefore consider what ye have to do" → "And now, know what you will do"** — Hebrew דְּעוּ "know".
- **"ephod … teraphim … carved image … molten image"** (v14, 17, 18, 20) — the four words of 17:3–5, each kept by its own word. In v17 the order is "the carved image and the ephod and the teraphim and the molten image"; in v20 only three are named.
- **"saluted him" → "asked him about shalom"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאֲלוּ־לוֹ לְשָׁלוֹם: they greeted him by asking after his *shalom* (Exod 18:7).
- **"went up, and came in thither, and took" → "went up; they came in there; they took"** (v17) — Hebrew בָּאוּ שָׁמָּה לָקְחוּ, two verbs set side by side without "and".
- **"fetched the carved image, the ephod" → "took the carved image of the ephod"** (v18) — the Masoretic Text has אֶת־פֶּסֶל הָאֵפוֹד, "the carved image of the ephod", the two joined (v17 has "the carved image and the ephod"). The Greek has "the carved image and the ephod" here too (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is translated as it stands.
- **"Hold thy peace, lay thine hand upon thy mouth" → "Be silent! Put your hand on your mouth"** (v19) — Hebrew הַחֲרֵשׁ שִׂים־יָדְךָ עַל־פִּיךָ. **"a tribe and a family" → "a tribe and … a clan"** — Hebrew לְשֵׁבֶט וּלְמִשְׁפָּחָה.
- **"the priest's heart was glad" → "the heart of the priest was good"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּיטַב לֵב, as 16:25.
- **"the little ones and the cattle and the carriage" → "the little ones and the livestock and the valuables"** (v21) — Hebrew הַכְּבוּדָּה, "what is weighty": goods of value; a rare word.
- **"when they were a good way from the house of Micah, the men … were gathered together, and overtook" → "They had gone far from the house of Micah, and the men … were called out, and they caught up with"** (v22) — Hebrew הֵמָּה הִרְחִיקוּ; נִזְעֲקוּ "were called together by a cry" (v23); וַיַּדְבִּיקוּ, literally "clung to": came close up to.
- **"What aileth thee, that thou comest with such a company?" → "What ails you, that you have been called out?"** (v23) — Hebrew כִּי נִזְעָקְתָּ, the verb of v22.
- **"Ye have taken away my gods which I made" → "My gods that I made you have taken"** (v24) — Hebrew אֶת־אֱלֹהַי אֲשֶׁר־עָשִׂיתִי stands first. The form may be singular or plural: "my god that I made" or "my gods". Of made images the plain word "gods" is used.
- **"lest angry fellows run upon thee, and thou lose thy life, with the lives of thy household" → "lest men bitter of soul fall upon you, and you gather in your soul and the soul of your house"** (v25) — Hebrew אֲנָשִׁים מָרֵי נֶפֶשׁ "men bitter of soul"; "you" in "fall upon you" is plural. וְאָסַפְתָּה נַפְשְׁךָ is literally "and you gather your soul": bring it to its end, lose it. נֶפֶשׁ is "soul".
- **"too strong for him" → "stronger than he"** (v26) — Hebrew חֲזָקִים הֵמָּה מִמֶּנּוּ.
- **"the priest which he had" → "the priest who had been his"** (v27) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הָיָה־לוֹ. **"came unto Laish" → "came upon Laish"** — Hebrew עַל "upon, against".
- **"there was no deliverer"** (v28) — Hebrew וְאֵין מַצִּיל. **"the valley that lieth by Beth-rehob" → "the valley that belongs to Beth-rehob"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לְבֵית־רְחוֹב. **"built a city" → "built the city"** — with the article: they built it again.
- **"after the name of Dan their father" → "by the name of Dan their father"** (v29) — Hebrew בְּשֵׁם. **"howbeit the name of the city was Laish at the first"** — Hebrew וְאוּלָם לַיִשׁ שֵׁם־הָעִיר לָרִאשֹׁנָה.
- **"set up the graven image" → "raised up … the carved image"** (v30) and **"set them up Micah's graven image" → "set for themselves the carved image of Micah"** (v31) — two Hebrew verbs, הֵקִים "raise up, make stand" and שִׂים "put, set".
- **"the son of Manasseh"** (v30) — the Westminster Leningrad Codex prints בֶּן־מְנַשֶּׁה, "the son of Manasseh", and so it is translated. In the manuscripts the letter נ of this name is written raised above the line (מְנַשֶּׁה): without it the consonants are משה, "Moses" (*Mosheh*), and Gershom is the name of the son of Moses (Exod 2:22; 18:3). "Moses" is the other reading, found in some Greek witnesses and in the Latin (cited from memory), and Jewish tradition itself says that the נ was added to spare the name of Moses.
- **"until the day of the captivity of the land" → "until the day of the exile of the land"** (v30) — Hebrew עַד־יוֹם גְּלוֹת הָאָרֶץ: גָּלָה is "go into exile" (literally "uncover, remove"). Which exile is meant is not said.
- **"all the time that the house of God was in Shiloh" → "all the days that the house of Elohim was in Shiloh"** (v31) — Hebrew כָּל־יְמֵי הֱיוֹת בֵּית־הָאֱלֹהִים בְּשִׁלֹה.

### Notes
- **Danite migration** — the tribe that could not hold its inheritance toward the coast (1:34; Josh 19:47) moves north and takes a quiet, unsuspecting city.
- **Laish / Dan** — the city in the far north, struck and burned and built again; from it comes the saying "from Dan to Beersheba" (20:1).
- **Taking of the images** (v14–20) — the Danites take all that Micah had made, and his priest with it; the priest's heart is good at the offer.
- **Jonathan the son of Gershom** (v30) — see Changes on "the son of Manasseh" and the raised letter.
- **The exile of the land** (v30) — often taken of the Assyrian deportations of the north (2 Kgs 15:29; 17:6); the verse does not name it.
- **1 Kgs 12:29** — Jeroboam later sets one of his golden calves at Dan.
- **Shiloh and Dan** (v31) — the house of Elohim stands in Shiloh all the days that Micah's carved image stands in Dan.
