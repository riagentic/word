# Judges 13

[1] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ again did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave them into the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ forty years.

[2] And there was one man from ⟦zorah|Tzorah|Zorah⟧, of the clan of the ⟦danites|Dani|Danite⟧, and his name was ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧; and his wife was barren and had not borne.

[3] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to the woman and said to her, "Behold now, you are barren and have not borne; and you shall conceive and bear a son.

[4] And now, take heed, please, and do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat anything unclean.

[5] For behold, you are with child and shall bear a son, and a razor shall not go up on his head, for a ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the boy shall be from the belly; and he, he shall begin to save ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧."

[6] And the woman came and said to her husband, saying, "A man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to me, and his appearance was like the appearance of the angel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, very fearsome; and I did not ask him where he was from, and his name he did not tell me.

[7] And he said to me, 'Behold, you are with child and shall bear a son; and now, do not drink wine or strong drink, and do not eat any uncleanness, for a ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the boy shall be from the belly until the day of his death.'"

[8] And ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ pleaded with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "Oh, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ whom you sent, let him come, please, again to us and teach us what we shall do for the boy who is to be born."

[9] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ listened to the voice of ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧, and the angel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came again to the woman, and she was sitting in the field, and ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ her husband was not with her.

[10] And the woman hastened and ran and told her husband, and she said to him, "Behold, the man who came to me that day has appeared to me."

[11] And ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ arose and went after his wife, and he came to the man and said to him, "Are you the man who spoke to the woman?" And he said, "I am."

[12] And ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ said, "Now let your words come. What shall be the ordinance of the boy, and his work?"

[13] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧, "Of all that I said to the woman let her take heed.

[15] And ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ said to the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "Let us keep you back, please, and let us make ready before you a kid of the goats."

[16] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧, "If you keep me back, I will not eat of your bread; and if you make ready a burnt offering, to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ you shall offer it up." For ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ did not know that he was the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] And ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ said to the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "What is your name? When your word comes, we will honor you."

[18] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Why is this, that you ask for my name? And it is wonderful."

[19] And ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ took the kid of the goats and the grain offering and offered it up on the rock to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and he was doing wonderfully, and ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ and his wife were looking on.

[20] And it came to pass, when the flame went up from upon the altar toward the heavens, that the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went up in the flame of the altar; and ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ and his wife were looking on, and they fell on their faces to the earth.

[21] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did not appear again to ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ and to his wife. Then ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ knew that he was the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] And ⟦manoah|Manoach|Manoah⟧ said to his wife, "We shall surely die, for we have seen ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[23] And his wife said to him, "If ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had delighted to put us to death, he would not have taken from our hand a burnt offering and a grain offering, and he would not have shown us all these things, and at this time he would not have let us hear such a thing as this."

[24] And the woman bore a son and called his name ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧; and the boy grew, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ blessed him.

[25] And the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ began to stir him in ⟦mahaneh-dan|Machaneh Dan|Mahaneh-dan⟧, between ⟦zorah|Tzorah|Zorah⟧ and Eshtaol.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. The chapter changes its words, and the verse follows each change: מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה "the angel of Yahweh" (v3, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21), מַלְאַךְ הָאֱלֹהִים "the angel of Elohim" (v6, 9), אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים "the man of Elohim" (v6, 8), הָאֱלֹהִים "Elohim" (v9), אֱלֹהִים without the article (v5, 7, 22). The narrator says "the angel of Yahweh"; the woman and Manoah say "man of Elohim" and "the man" (v10, 11) until v21.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"did evil again" → "again did the evil"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּסִפוּ … לַעֲשׂוֹת הָרַע, literally "added to do the evil". **"delivered them" → "gave them"** — Hebrew נָתַן.
- **"a certain man … of the family of the Danites" → "one man … of the clan of the Danite"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶחָד "one man"; מִשְׁפַּחַת הַדָּנִי, a singular with the article. **"bare not" → "had not borne"** (v2, 3) — no object is written.
- **"the angel of the LORD" → "the angel of Yahweh"** (v3 and throughout) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה; the word מַלְאָךְ is "messenger".
- **"Behold now" kept** (v3) — Hebrew הִנֵּה־נָא. **"but thou shalt conceive" → "and you shall conceive"** — Hebrew וְהָרִית.
- **"beware, I pray thee" → "take heed, please"** (v4) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמְרִי נָא, literally "keep yourself"; the same verb in v13 ("let her take heed") and, in the qal, v14 ("she shall keep"). **"any unclean thing" → "anything unclean"** (v4) / **"any uncleanness"** (v7, 14) — Hebrew כָּל־טָמֵא (the adjective) in v4, כָּל־טֻמְאָה (the noun) in v7, 14.
- **"thou shalt conceive, and bear a son" → "you are with child and shall bear a son"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew הִנָּךְ הָרָה וְיֹלַדְתְּ בֵּן, the words of Gen 16:11: הָרָה is the adjective "with child", not the verb of v3. It is said of what is now so or is at hand.
- **"no razor shall come on his head" → "a razor shall not go up on his head"** (v5) — Hebrew וּמוֹרָה לֹא־יַעֲלֶה עַל־רֹאשׁוֹ. מוֹרָה "razor" is found only here, in 16:17 and in 1 Sam 1:11; Num 6:5 has another word (תַּעַר).
- **"a Nazarite unto God from the womb" → "a *nazir* of Elohim … from the belly"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew נְזִיר אֱלֹהִים, a construct: "one kept apart of / for Elohim" (נָזִיר, from the root "keep apart", Num 6:2). בֶּטֶן is "belly"; "womb" is another word (רֶחֶם). The word order sets "a *nazir* of Elohim" first.
- **"the child" → "the boy"** (v5, 7, 8, 12, 24) — Hebrew הַנַּעַר.
- **"he shall begin to deliver" → "he, he shall begin to save"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהוּא יָחֵל לְהוֹשִׁיעַ, with the pronoun for weight; הוֹשִׁיעַ "save".
- **"told her husband, saying" → "said to her husband, saying"** (v6) — Hebrew וַתֹּאמֶר … לֵאמֹר.
- **"A man of God" → "A man of Elohim"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים: the phrase is a fixed title (Deut 33:1; Josh 14:6), and in form it is definite, "the man of the Elohim". In the woman's first report English needs "a"; in v8 it is "the man of Elohim whom you sent".
- **"his countenance was like the countenance of an angel of God, very terrible" → "his appearance was like the appearance of the angel of Elohim, very fearsome"** (v6) — Hebrew מַרְאֶה "appearance"; מַלְאַךְ הָאֱלֹהִים, definite; נוֹרָא, from יָרֵא "fear".
- **"whence he was" → "where he was from"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵי־מִזֶּה הוּא. **"neither told he me his name" → "his name he did not tell me"** — the object stands first.
- **"intreated the LORD" → "pleaded with Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיֶּעְתַּר אֶל, as Gen 25:21.
- **"O my Lord" → "Oh, Adonai"** (v8) — Hebrew בִּי אֲדוֹנָי: בִּי is a word of entreaty ("oh, please"); אֲדוֹנָי is pointed as the divine title *Adonai*, as Exod 4:10; Josh 7:8.
- **"teach us what we shall do unto the child that shall be born" → "teach us what we shall do for the boy who is to be born"** (v8) — Hebrew וְיוֹרֵנוּ, the hifil of ירה, the root of *torah*; הַיּוּלָּד, a participle, "the one born / being born".
- **"God hearkened to the voice of" → "Elohim listened to the voice of"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמַע … בְּקוֹל. **"as she sat" → "and she was sitting"** — Hebrew וְהִיא יוֹשֶׁבֶת.
- **"made haste, and ran, and shewed her husband" → "hastened and ran and told her husband"** (v10) — Hebrew הִגִּיד "tell". **"that came unto me the other day" → "who came to me that day"** — Hebrew בַיּוֹם, literally "in the day": the day of the first coming.
- **"spakest unto the woman" → "spoke to the woman"** (v11) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר אֶל; Manoah says "the woman", not "my wife".
- **"Now let thy words come to pass. How shall we order the child, and how shall we do unto him?" → "Now let your words come. What shall be the ordinance of the boy, and his work?"** (v12) — Hebrew עַתָּה יָבֹא דְבָרֶיךָ מַה־יִּהְיֶה מִשְׁפַּט־הַנַּעַר וּמַעֲשֵׂהוּ. The first clause is "now your words shall come" or "let … come" (also taken as "now, when your words come"); the verb is singular, the noun plural. מִשְׁפָּט is "judgment", here the rule laid down for the boy; מַעֲשֵׂהוּ is "his work / his doing": what he is to do, or what is to be done with him.
- **"let her beware" → "let her take heed"** (v13) — Hebrew תִּשָּׁמֵר; the form is also "you shall take heed" (second person masculine), but "the woman" and v14 ("I commanded her") show the third person.
- **"any thing that cometh of the vine" → "all that comes out of the vine of wine"** (v14) — Hebrew גֶּפֶן הַיַּיִן, as Num 6:4. The angel's words to Manoah are about the woman; a rule for the boy beyond v5 is not given.
- **"let us detain thee, until we shall have made ready a kid for thee" → "Let us keep you back, please, and let us make ready before you a kid of the goats"** (v15) — Hebrew נַעְצְרָה־נָּא אוֹתָךְ: עָצַר "hold back, restrain"; וְנַעֲשֶׂה לְפָנֶיךָ "and let us make / do before you"; גְּדִי עִזִּים. The verb עָשָׂה is used of preparing food and of offering a sacrifice; the angel takes it up in v16 ("if you make ready a burnt offering"), and one English word is kept for both.
- **"Though thou detain me" → "If you keep me back"** (v16) — Hebrew אִם. **"thy bread"** — Hebrew לֶחֶם. **"thou must offer it unto the LORD" → "to Yahweh you shall offer it up"** — "to Yahweh" stands first; הֶעֱלָה "make go up", the verb of עֹלָה "burnt offering".
- **"What is thy name" kept** (v17) — Hebrew מִי שְׁמֶךָ, literally "Who is your name?" **"that when thy sayings come to pass we may do thee honour" → "When your word comes, we will honor you"** — Hebrew כִּי־יָבֹא דְבָרְךָ וְכִבַּדְנוּךָ; כִּי is "when" or "for". "Word" is the qere (singular); the written text has the plural, "your words".
- **"Why askest thou thus after my name, seeing it is secret?" → "Why is this, that you ask for my name? And it is wonderful."** (v18) — Hebrew לָמָּה זֶּה תִּשְׁאַל לִשְׁמִי, the very words of Gen 32:29, and וְהוּא־פֶלִאי "and it is wonderful": the adjective of פֶּלֶא "a wonder", a thing beyond what one can grasp (the root of Gen 18:14 "too wonderful", of Isa 9:6 "Wonderful", and of Ps 139:6, where the same adjective stands). "Secret" is not what the word says. Whether "it" is the name ("my name is Wonderful") or the asking is beyond him ("it is too wonderful") is left open, as in the Hebrew.
- **"a kid with a meat offering" → "the kid of the goats and the grain offering"** (v19) — Hebrew הַמִּנְחָה; both nouns have the article. **"offered it upon a rock" → "offered it up on the rock"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַל עַל־הַצּוּר; the verb has no object written, "it" is supplied; הַצּוּר has the article.
- **"and the angel did wonderously" → "and he was doing wonderfully"** (v19) — Hebrew וּמַפְלִא לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "and making wonderful to do": a participle with no subject written, from the same root as "wonderful" in v18. "The angel" is supplied by KJV. English needs a subject; "he" is supplied and may be Yahweh, named just before, or the angel. Some join it to "Yahweh": "to Yahweh, the one who does wonderfully" (so the Septuagint in one form; cited from memory).
- **"looked on" → "were looking on"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew participle רֹאִים.
- **"when the flame went up toward heaven from off the altar" → "when the flame went up from upon the altar toward the heavens"** (v20) — Hebrew מֵעַל הַמִּזְבֵּחַ הַשָּׁמַיְמָה. **"ascended" → "went up"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַל, the same verb. "The altar" is the rock of v19.
- **"did no more appear" → "did not appear again"** (v21) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָסַף עוֹד … לְהֵרָאֹה, literally "did not add any more to be seen".
- **"We shall surely die, because we have seen God" → "… for we have seen *Elohim*"** (v22) — Hebrew מוֹת נָמוּת כִּי אֱלֹהִים רָאִינוּ: the infinitive absolute with its verb ("dying we shall die"), and "Elohim" set before the verb. The word has no article here. Manoah has seen "the angel of Yahweh" (v21) and says "we have seen Elohim" (cf. Gen 32:30; Exod 33:20); the word can also be heard as "a divine being".
- **"If the LORD were pleased to kill us, he would not have received" → "If Yahweh had delighted to put us to death, he would not have taken"** (v23) — Hebrew חָפֵץ "delight", הֵמִית "put to death", לָקַח "take"; "from our hand" is singular. **"nor would as at this time have told us such things as these" → "and at this time he would not have let us hear such a thing as this"** — Hebrew וְכָעֵת לֹא הִשְׁמִיעָנוּ כָּזֹאת; כָּזֹאת is singular, "the like of this".
- **"Samson"** (v24) — Hebrew שִׁמְשׁוֹן *Shimshon*; the name is like שֶׁמֶשׁ "sun". The verse gives no meaning for it.
- **"began to move him at times in the camp of Dan" → "began to stir him in Mahaneh-dan"** (v25) — Hebrew וַתָּחֶל רוּחַ יְהוָה לְפַעֲמוֹ. The verb פָּעַם "thrust, impel, stir" is found in this form only here (its other forms are "be troubled", Gen 41:8; Ps 77:4); it is the root of פַּעַם "a beat, a time", which KJV put in as "at times". מַחֲנֵה־דָן is "the camp of Dan" (18:12). רוּחַ is "spirit", "wind" and "breath".

### Notes
- **The announcement of a birth** — a barren woman is told she will bear a son: so also Sarah (Gen 18), Hannah (1 Sam 1), and in the New Testament Elizabeth (Luke 1).
- **A *nazir* from the belly** — in Num 6 a man or a woman takes the vow of a *nazir* for a time; here the boy is one from before his birth "until the day of his death" (v7), and it is his mother who is told to keep from wine and from what is unclean. Of the three rules of Num 6 only the razor is named for the boy (v5).
- **"And it is wonderful"** (v18) — the same root comes back in v19, "doing wonderfully".
- **Fire and the angel** (v20) — the angel of Yahweh goes up in the flame of the altar; cf. the angel of Yahweh in the flame of fire in the bush (Exod 3:2).
- **Manoah's wife** — she is not named. The angel comes to her both times; she answers her husband's fear (v23).
- **Mahaneh-dan** (v25) — "the camp of Dan"; 18:12 gives the name to a place "behind Kiriath-jearim".
