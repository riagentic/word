# Judges 10

[1] And after ⟦abimelech|Avimelech|Abimelech⟧ there arose to save ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ Tola the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man of ⟦issachar|Yissachar|Issachar⟧; and he was dwelling in Shamir in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[2] And he judged ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ twenty-three years, and he died and was buried in Shamir.

[3] And after him arose ⟦jair|Yair|Jair⟧ the ⟦gileadite|Giladi|Gileadite⟧, and he judged ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ twenty-two years.

[4] And he had thirty sons riding on thirty donkey colts, and they had thirty cities; them they call ⟦havvoth-jair|Chavvot Yair|Havvoth-jair⟧ to this day, which are in the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[5] And ⟦jair|Yair|Jair⟧ died and was buried in ⟦kamon|Qamon|Kamon⟧.

[6] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ again did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they served the Baals and the ⟦ashtaroth-goddesses|Ashtarot|Ashtaroth⟧, and the gods of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and the gods of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧ and the gods of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and the gods of the sons of Ammon and the gods of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; and they forsook ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and did not serve him.

[7] And the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he sold them into the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and into the hand of the sons of Ammon.

[8] And they shattered and crushed the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in that year — eighteen years — all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who were across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, in the land of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, which is in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[9] And the sons of Ammon passed over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to fight also against ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and against ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and against the house of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧; and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was greatly distressed.

[10] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "We have sinned against you, in that we have forsaken our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and have served the Baals."

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "Was it not from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and from the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ and from the sons of Ammon and from the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ —

[15] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "We have sinned. Do you to us according to all that is good in your eyes; only deliver us, please, this day."

[18] And the people, the princes of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, said each man to his neighbor, "Who is the man who will begin to fight against the sons of Ammon? He shall be head of all the inhabitants of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton kept.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10) — Hebrew *Elohim*. The same word with the article and plural pronouns in v14 (הָאֱלֹהִים אֲשֶׁר בְּחַרְתֶּם בָּם … הֵמָּה) is "the gods".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel / of Ammon" → "sons of Israel / of Ammon"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי "sons of".
- **"to defend Israel" → "to save Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew לְהוֹשִׁיעַ, the hifil of יָשַׁע "save"; the same verb in v12, 13, 14. "Deliver" (v15) is another verb, הִצִּיל.
- **"the son of Dodo" kept** (v1) — Hebrew בֶּן־דּוֹדוֹ. The word can also be read "the son of his uncle" (דּוֹד "uncle" with a suffix), and so the Septuagint takes it (cited from memory); the Masoretic pointing is kept as a name.
- **"he dwelt" → "he was dwelling"** (v1) — Hebrew participle יֹשֵׁב. **"mount Ephraim" → "the hill country of Ephraim"** — Hebrew הַר אֶפְרַיִם; the word is "mountain".
- **"that rode on thirty ass colts, and they had thirty cities" → "riding on thirty donkey colts, and they had thirty cities"** (v4) — Hebrew עֲיָרִים twice: "donkey colts" (עַיִר, a young male donkey, Gen 32:15) and "cities", which is elsewhere written עָרִים. The second word is pointed like the first for the play of sound.
- **"which are called Havoth-jair" → "them they call Havvoth-jair"** (v4) — Hebrew לָהֶם יִקְרְאוּ, "them they call", with the pronoun first. חַוֺּת is "tent villages" (Num 32:41; Deut 3:14); the name is "the tent villages of Jair".
- **"did evil again" → "again did the evil"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֹּסִפוּ … לַעֲשׂוֹת הָרַע, literally "added to do the evil", with the article.
- **"Baalim, and Ashtaroth" → "the Baals and the Ashtaroth"** (v6, 10) — both are plurals with the article: the local forms of Baal and of the goddess Ashtoreth (Astarte). **"Syria" → "Aram"** — Hebrew אֲרָם.
- **"the anger of the LORD was hot" → "the anger of Yahweh burned"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף; אַף is "nose", and so "anger".
- **"vexed and oppressed" → "shattered and crushed"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיִּרְעֲצוּ וַיְרֹצְצוּ *wa-yir'atzu wa-yerotzetzu*: two verbs of almost one sound, רָעַץ "shatter" (Exod 15:6) and רָצַץ "crush". "Oppress" is another verb (לָחַץ, v12).
- **"that year … eighteen years" → "in that year — eighteen years —"** (v8) — the Hebrew sets the two times side by side without a link: בַּשָּׁנָה הַהִיא שְׁמֹנֶה עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה. KJV's "that year they vexed … : eighteen years, all the children" is the same order; nothing is supplied.
- **"on the other side Jordan" → "across the Jordan"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן; here the east side. **"the Amorites" → "the Amorite"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew הָאֱמֹרִי, a singular with the article.
- **"passed over Jordan" → "passed over the Jordan"** (v9) — Hebrew עָבַר.
- **"Israel was sore distressed" → "Israel was greatly distressed"** (v9) — Hebrew וַתֵּצֶר לְיִשְׂרָאֵל מְאֹד, literally "and it was very narrow for Israel"; the root of צָרָה "distress" (v14).
- **"cried unto" → "cried out to"** (v10, 12, 14) — Hebrew זָעַק (v10, 14) and צָעַק (v12): one verb in two spellings.
- **"both because we have forsaken" → "in that we have forsaken"** (v10) — Hebrew וְכִי עָזַבְנוּ, literally "and that / and because we have forsaken".
- **"Did not I deliver you from the Egyptians" → "Was it not from Egypt …"** (v11) — the Hebrew of v11 has no verb: הֲלֹא מִמִּצְרַיִם וּמִן־הָאֱמֹרִי …, "Was it not from Egypt and from the Amorite …". The sentence breaks off and v12 goes on with "and the Sidonians … oppressed you … and I saved you from their hand". "Did I not deliver you" is supplied by KJV; the broken sentence is kept. מִצְרַיִם is "Egypt", not "the Egyptians".
- **"the Zidonians also, and the Amalekites, and the Maonites" → "and the Sidonians and Amalek and Maon"** (v12) — Hebrew צִידוֹנִים וַעֲמָלֵק וּמָעוֹן: the last two are the names of the peoples in the singular. Maon is not named elsewhere as an oppressor of Israel; the Septuagint has "Midian" (cited from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"I will deliver you no more" → "I will not save you again"** (v13) — Hebrew לֹא־אוֹסִיף לְהוֹשִׁיעַ, literally "I will not add to save".
- **"let them deliver you" → "they, let them save you"** (v14) — Hebrew הֵמָּה יוֹשִׁיעוּ לָכֶם, with the pronoun set first for weight. The verb form is also a plain future: "they will save you".
- **"whatsoever seemeth good unto thee" → "according to all that is good in your eyes"** (v15) — Hebrew כְּכָל־הַטּוֹב בְּעֵינֶיךָ. **"deliver us only, we pray thee" → "only deliver us, please"** — Hebrew נָא.
- **"put away the strange gods from among them" → "removed the foreign gods from their midst"** (v16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי הַנֵּכָר, literally "the gods of the foreign land / of what is foreign"; הֵסִיר "remove".
- **"his soul was grieved for the misery of Israel" → "his soul was short at the toil of Israel"** (v16) — Hebrew וַתִּקְצַר נַפְשׁוֹ בַּעֲמַל יִשְׂרָאֵל, word for word; the verb is "be / become short". "The soul is short" is said of one who can bear a thing no longer (Num 21:4, of the people on the way; Judg 16:16, of Samson; Zech 11:8). עָמָל is "toil", also the hardship one suffers. Whether Israel's suffering or Israel's effort is meant, the Hebrew does not say; the verse keeps the words.
- **"were gathered together" → "were called out"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיִּצָּעֲקוּ, the nifal of צָעַק "cry out": summoned by a cry. "Gathered", of Israel, is another verb (אָסַף). **"encamped" → "camped"** — Hebrew חָנָה. **"in Mizpeh" → "at the Mizpah"** — Hebrew בַּמִּצְפָּה, with the article: "the watchtower".
- **"the people and princes of Gilead" → "the people, the princes of Gilead"** (v18) — the Hebrew has no "and": הָעָם שָׂרֵי גִלְעָד; the second phrase stands beside the first. שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander".
- **"said one to another" → "said each man to his neighbor"** (v18) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ.
- **"What man is he that will begin" → "Who is the man who will begin"** (v18) — Hebrew מִי הָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר יָחֵל. **"head over all" → "head of all"** — Hebrew לְרֹאשׁ לְכֹל, literally "for a head to all".

### Notes
- **Two minor judges** (v1–5):
  - **Tola** — Issachar. 23 years.
  - **Jair** — Gilead. 22 years. Thirty sons on thirty donkey colts with thirty cities.
- **The list of gods** (v6) — the Baals, the Ashtaroth and the gods of five peoples: the longest list of this kind in the book.
- **Yahweh's answer** (v11–14) — "I will not save you again. Go and cry out to the gods whom you have chosen." The answer of v16 is given without a new word from Yahweh.
- **Israel's turning** (v15–16) — they remove the foreign gods and serve Yahweh; then "his soul was short at the toil of Israel".
- **The Ammonite war sets up Jephthah** — the people need a leader; the princes of Gilead make a public offer (v18). Jephthah will answer.
