# 2 John 1

[1] The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in truth, and not I alone, but also all who have come to know the truth,

[2] because of the truth that abides in us, and it will be with us forever:

[3] Grace, mercy, peace will be with us from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father and from ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

[4] I rejoiced greatly that I have found some of your children walking in truth, just as we received a commandment from the Father.

[5] And now I request you, lady — not as writing to you a new commandment, but the one which we had from the beginning — that we love one another.

[6] And this is love, that we walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, just as you heard from the beginning, that you walk in it.

[7] For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ coming in flesh. This is the deceiver and the antichrist.

[8] Watch yourselves, that you do not lose what we wrought, but receive a full reward.

[9] Everyone who goes forward and does not abide in the teaching of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ does not have ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. The one who abides in the teaching, this one has both the Father and the Son.

[10] If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into the house, and do not say a greeting to him,

[11] for the one who says a greeting to him shares in his evil works.

[12] Having many things to write to you, I did not wish to do so with paper and ink, but I hope to come to you and to speak mouth to mouth, so that your joy may be made full.

[13] The children of your elect sister greet you.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 9) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v3, 7) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v9) — systematic.
- **"church" → omitted here** — this letter has no explicit church reference; the "elect lady" of v1 may refer to a local congregation personified, but the word does not appear.
- **"the elect lady" kept** (v1) — Greek ἐκλεκτῇ κυρίᾳ has no article: "to an elect lady" or "to the elect lady"; some take either word as a proper name (Eklekte, Kyria).
- **"they that have known the truth" → "all who have come to know the truth"** (v1) — Greek οἱ ἐγνωκότες, a perfect participle.
- **"Grace be with you, mercy, and peace" → "Grace, mercy, peace will be with us"** (v3) — Greek ἔσται μεθ' ἡμῶν: a future statement, not a wish, and "us", not "you"; the three nouns stand without "and".
- **"walking in truth" kept** (v4) — Greek περιπατοῦντας ἐν ἀληθείᾳ, without the article; ἐκ τῶν τέκνων σου is "of your children" ("some" is supplied for English).
- **"lady" kept** (v5) — Greek κυρία alone; no "dear".
- **"that … ye should walk in it" → "that you walk in it"** (v6) — Greek ἐν αὐτῇ, feminine: "it" may be the commandment (ἐντολή) or love (ἀγάπη); left open.
- **"who confess not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh" → "those who do not confess *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* coming in flesh"** (v7) — Greek ἐρχόμενον ἐν σαρκί, a present participle, "coming" (not the perfect "has come" of 1 John 4:2); no article with "flesh".
- **"Look to yourselves" → "Watch yourselves"** (v8) — Greek βλέπετε ἑαυτούς *blepete heautous* = "watch yourselves / look to yourselves".
- **"that we lose not those things which we have wrought, but that we receive" → "that you do not lose what we wrought, but receive"** (v8) — Greek ἀπολέσητε … εἰργασάμεθα … ἀπολάβητε: "you" lose and receive, "we" wrought: εἰργασάμεθα (SBLGNT WH NA28 RP); εἰργάσασθε "you wrought" (Treg). ἀπολαμβάνω is "receive", not "win".
- **"Whosoever transgresseth, and abideth not in the doctrine of Christ" → "Everyone who goes forward and does not abide in the teaching of the *Mashiach*"** (v9) — Greek πᾶς ὁ προάγων καὶ μὴ μένων ἐν τῇ διδαχῇ τοῦ Χριστοῦ *pas ho proagōn kai mē menōn en tē didachē tou Christou*; KJV follows the later reading παραβαίνων "transgresseth"; *proagōn* = "going forward / going ahead"; "doctrine" → "teaching". The genitive "of the *Mashiach*" may be the teaching he gave or the teaching about him.
- **"receive him not into your house" → "do not receive him into the house"** (v10) — Greek εἰς οἰκίαν; no "your".
- **"neither bid him God speed" → "do not say a greeting to him"** (v10, 11) — Greek χαίρειν αὐτῷ μὴ λέγετε, literally "do not say to him 'rejoice'"; χαίρειν is the ordinary Greek word of greeting.
- **"is partaker of his evil deeds" → "shares in his evil works"** (v11) — Greek κοινωνεῖ τοῖς ἔργοις αὐτοῦ τοῖς πονηροῖς; ἔργα "works", the same root as "wrought" in v8.
- **"I would not write with paper and ink" → "I did not wish to do so with paper and ink"** (v12) — Greek οὐκ ἐβουλήθην διὰ χάρτου καὶ μέλανος; the verb "write" is not repeated ("to do so" is supplied for English).
- **"speak face to face" → "speak mouth to mouth"** (v12) — Greek στόμα πρὸς στόμα *stoma pros stoma*.
- **"that our joy may be full" → "so that your joy may be made full"** (v12) — Greek πεπληρωμένη ᾖ, a perfect passive; the verse reads ὑμῶν "your" (SBLGNT WH Treg); ἡμῶν "our joy" (NA28 RP).
- **"for ever" kept as "forever"** (v2) — Greek εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, literally "unto the age"; the fixed idiom for "forever", so αἰών is not rendered "age" here.
