## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"And it came to pass after the plague, that the LORD spake" → "And it came to pass after the plague, that Yahweh said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי אַחֲרֵי הַמַּגֵּפָה … וַיֹּאמֶר. In the Hebrew the first four words are a verse of their own (25:19), and a paragraph break stands after them, in the middle of the sentence.
- **"Take the sum of" → "Lift up the head of"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׂאוּ אֶת־רֹאשׁ, the idiom for counting people, as 1:2; Exod 30:12. The command is plural: Moses and Eleazar.
- **"throughout their fathers' house, all that are able to go to war in Israel" → "by their fathers' houses, everyone who goes out to the host in Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew לְבֵית אֲבֹתָם and כָּל־יֹצֵא צָבָא, the formulas of 1:2–3: צָבָא is an army, an ordered multitude; "able" and "war" are not in the Hebrew. **"from twenty years old and upward"** (v2, 4) — literally "from a son of twenty years and upward".
- **"spake with them in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho" → "spoke with them in the plains of Moab by the Jordan of Jericho"** (v3, 63) — Hebrew עַל־יַרְדֵּן יְרֵחוֹ, literally so: the stretch of the Jordan over against Jericho. אֹתָם may be "with them" or "them" (the object).
- **"Take the sum of the people, from twenty years old and upward; as the LORD commanded Moses and the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt" → "From twenty years old and upward, as Yahweh commanded Moses and the sons of Israel who came out from the land of Egypt"** (v4) — the Hebrew has no "Take the sum of the people": the verse begins with "from twenty years old and upward" and has no verb of its own. The accents join "and the sons of Israel who came out from the land of Egypt" to "Moses" (those to whom the command of 1:1–3 was given). The words can also be taken as the heading of the list: "And the sons of Israel who came out from the land of Egypt were:".
- **"families" → "clans"** (throughout) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה, the kin group between the tribe and the father's house, as in ch 1–4.
- **"of Hanoch, of whom cometh the family of the Hanochites: of Pallu, the family of the Palluites" → "Hanoch, the clan of the Hanochites; of Pallu, the clan of the Palluites"** (v5 and throughout) — Hebrew לְפַלּוּא מִשְׁפַּחַת הַפַּלֻּאִי, literally "to Pallu (belongs) the clan of the Palluite". The clan names are singular forms with the article ("the Hanochite"), used for the whole clan. Where the Hebrew has the name without לְ, the verse has no "of": Hanoch (v5), Tola (v23), Iezer (v30), Asriel, Shechem, Shemida, Hepher (v31, 32, each with "and"), and the Ardites (v40).
- **"they that were numbered of them" → "those of them who were numbered"** (v7, 18, 22, 25, 27, 34, 37, 41, 43, 47, 50, 62) — Hebrew פְּקֻדֵיהֶם, the passive participle of פָּקַד "muster, number", as in ch 1. לִפְקֻדֵיהֶם is "by those of them who were numbered", with the לְ of "by their clans". In v14 the Simeonites have the figure without this word.
- **"forty and three thousand and seven hundred and thirty" → "forty-three thousand seven hundred and thirty"** (v7 and throughout) — numbers in words, in ordinary English order; the Hebrew has "three and forty thousand and seven hundred and thirty", and so on. The figures as the Hebrew gives them: Reuben 43,730; Simeon 22,200; Gad 40,500; Judah 76,500; Issachar 64,300; Zebulun 60,500; Manasseh 52,700; Ephraim 32,500; Benjamin 45,600; Dan 64,400; Asher 53,400; Naphtali 45,400; together 601,730 (v51: "six hundred thousand and a thousand, seven hundred and thirty"), which is their sum.
- **"the sons of Pallu; Eliab"** (v8) — the Hebrew has the plural "sons" though one is named; so v36.
- **"which were famous in the congregation, who strove against Moses and against Aaron in the company of Korah, when they strove against the LORD" → "the called ones of the congregation, who struggled against Moses and against Aaron in the congregation of Korah, when they struggled against Yahweh"** (v9) — Hebrew qere קְרִיאֵי, written קרואי: both forms are from קָרָא "call": those called, summoned (1:16; 16:2). הִצּוּ … בְּהַצֹּתָם is the causative of נָצָה "struggle, fight" (Exod 2:13). עֲדַת־קֹרַח: the word is עֵדָה, the same "congregation" as just before and in v10.
- **"swallowed them up together with Korah, when that company died, what time the fire devoured two hundred and fifty men: and they became a sign" → "swallowed them and Korah, when the congregation died, when the fire consumed two hundred and fifty men; and they became a signal"** (v10) — Hebrew וַתִּבְלַע אֹתָם וְאֶת־קֹרַח: Korah is named with those whom the earth swallowed (16:32 has "them and their houses and all the men who belonged to Korah"). אָכַל is "eat, consume". נֵס is a banner, a pole set up to be seen from far (21:8; Exod 17:15; Isa 11:10): here a thing held up to be looked at, a warning. It is not the word "sign" (אוֹת).
- **"Notwithstanding the children of Korah died not" → "And the sons of Korah did not die"** (v11) — Hebrew וּבְנֵי־קֹרַח לֹא־מֵתוּ.
- **Names beside Gen 46:8–24** — where the Hebrew spelling is that of Genesis the name is the same (Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, Carmi; Jamin, Jachin, Shaul; Haggi, Shuni, Eri, Areli; Shelah, Perez, Zerah, Hezron, Hamul; Tola, Puvah, Shimron; Sered, Elon, Jahleel; Bela, Ashbel, Naaman, Ard; Imnah, Ishvi, Beriah, Heber, Malchiel, Serah; Jahzeel, Guni, Jezer, Shillem). Where the Hebrew differs, the name differs: Nemuel (Gen: Jemuel), Zerah (Zohar), Zephon (Ziphion), Ozni (Ezbon), Arod (Arodi), Jashub (Iob), Ahiram (Ehi), Shephupham (Muppim), Hupham (Huppim), Shuham (Hushim). Ohad (Simeon), Ishvah (Asher) and Becher, Gera, Rosh (Benjamin) of Genesis are not in this list; Ard and Naaman are sons of Benjamin there and sons of Bela here.
- **"of Pua, the family of the Punites"** (v23) — Hebrew לְפֻוָה … הַפּוּנִי: the clan name has נ where the man's name has ו; both are kept as written. **"of Shupham, the family of the Shuphamites" → "of Shephupham, the clan of the Shuphamites"** (v39) — Hebrew לִשְׁפוּפָם … הַשּׁוּפָמִי: the man's name is written Shephupham in this text; kept. **"of Jimna, the family of the Jimnites"** (v44) — in the Hebrew the clan name is the same form as the man's (הַיִּמְנָה).
- **"And the sons of Judah after their families were" → "And the sons of Judah by their clans were"** (v20, 21, 40) — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ "and they were".
- **"Machir begat Gilead"** kept as **"begot"** (v29, 58) — Hebrew הוֹלִיד.
- **"had no sons, but daughters" → "had no sons, except daughters"** (v33) — Hebrew כִּי אִם. "The names" is a singular in the Hebrew (וְשֵׁם), as in v46, 59.
- **"of Ard, the family of the Ardites: and of Naaman" → "the clan of the Ardites; of Naaman"** (v40) — the Hebrew has no "of Ard" before "the clan of the Ardites".
- **"Unto these the land shall be divided for an inheritance according to the number of names"** (v53) — Hebrew תֵּחָלֵק "be divided" (the verb of חֵלֶק "share"); נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; בְּמִסְפַּר שֵׁמוֹת as 1:2.
- **"To many thou shalt give the more inheritance, and to few thou shalt give the less inheritance: to every one shall his inheritance be given according to those that were numbered of him" → "For the many you shall make his inheritance large, and for the few you shall make his inheritance small; each man, according to those of his who were numbered, shall be given his inheritance"** (v54) — Hebrew לָרַב תַּרְבֶּה … וְלַמְעַט תַּמְעִיט, literally "for the many you shall make many … for the few you shall make few": each verb is of one root with its noun (so 33:54). "Tribe" is not in the Hebrew; the singular "his" is kept (so v56). אִישׁ לְפִי פְקֻדָיו is literally "a man according to the mouth of his numbered ones".
- **"Notwithstanding the land shall be divided by lot: according to the names of the tribes of their fathers" → "Only by lot shall the land be divided; by the names of the tribes of their fathers"** (v55) — Hebrew אַךְ "only"; מַטּוֹת "tribes" (literally "staffs").
- **"According to the lot shall the possession thereof be divided between many and few" → "At the mouth of the lot shall his inheritance be divided, between the many and the few"** (v56) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי הַגּוֹרָל, as "at the mouth of Yahweh" (3:16); נַחֲלָתוֹ is "his inheritance", the word of v53–54.
- **"they that were numbered of the Levites"** (v57) — Hebrew הַלֵּוִי, a singular for the whole tribe.
- **"Jochebed, the daughter of Levi, whom her mother bare to Levi in Egypt" → "Jochebed the daughter of Levi, whom she bore to Levi in Egypt"** (v59) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יָלְדָה אֹתָהּ לְלֵוִי: the verb is feminine and its subject is not named ("her mother" is not in the Hebrew). The sense is "who was born to Levi in Egypt".
- **"when they offered strange fire" → "when they brought near strange fire"** (v61) — Hebrew בְּהַקְרִיבָם אֵשׁ־זָרָה, as Lev 10:1.
- **"all males" → "every male"** (v62) — Hebrew כָּל־זָכָר. **"they were not numbered among the children of Israel, because there was no inheritance given them among" → "they were not numbered in the midst of the sons of Israel, for no inheritance was given to them in the midst of"** — Hebrew הָתְפָּקְדוּ, the rare form of 1:47; כִּי twice; בְּתוֹךְ.
- **"they that were numbered by Moses"** (v63, 64) — Hebrew פְּקוּדֵי מֹשֶׁה, literally "the numbered ones of Moses".
- **"They shall surely die in the wilderness"** kept (v65) — Hebrew מוֹת יָמֻתוּ, literally "dying they shall die". לָהֶם is "of them" or "to them". **"there was not left a man of them, save" → "there did not remain of them a man, except"** — Hebrew נוֹתַר; כִּי אִם.

### Notes
- **Verse numbering** — in the Hebrew, "And it came to pass after the plague" is 25:19, and 26:1 begins with "And Yahweh said".
- **Second numbering** — after the forty years (cf. 1:1; 33:38). The first numbering gave 603,550 (1:46); this one 601,730. Of those numbered at Sinai only Caleb and Joshua remain (v64–65; 14:29–30).
- **Dathan, Abiram, Korah** (v9–11) — see ch 16. "The sons of Korah did not die"; "sons of Korah" are named in the headings of Ps 42–49; 84–85; 87–88.
- **Zelophehad's daughters** (v33) — named here; their case follows in ch 27 and ch 36.
- **Division of the land** (v52–56) — by the numbers of each tribe (v54) and by lot (v55–56).
- **Levi** (v57–62) — numbered apart, from a month old, as in 3:15, 39 (there twenty-two thousand). The line Levi – Kohath – Amram – Aaron, Moses, Miriam is that of Exod 6:16–23. The five clans of v58 are named without their fathers: Libni is a son of Gershon, Hebron and Korah are of Kohath, Mahli and Mushi are sons of Merari (3:18–20; Exod 6:21).
