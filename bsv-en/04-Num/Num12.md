# Numbers 12

[1] And ⟦miriam|Miryam|Miriam⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ spoke against ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ on account of the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ woman whom he had taken, for he had taken a ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ woman.

[2] And they said, "Is it indeed only with ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken? Has he not spoken with us also?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ heard.

[3] And the man ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ was very humble, more than all mankind that was on the face of the ground.

[4] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said suddenly to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to ⟦miriam|Miryam|Miriam⟧, "Come out, you three, to the tent of meeting." And the three of them came out.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came down in a pillar of cloud and stood at the entrance of the tent, and he called ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and ⟦miriam|Miryam|Miriam⟧, and the two of them came out.

[6] And he said, "Hear, please, my words: If there is a prophet of yours, I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, make myself known to him in the vision; in the dream I speak with him.

[7] Not so my servant ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧; in all my house he is faithful.

[8] Mouth to mouth I speak with him, and by appearance, and not in riddles, and the form of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he looks upon. And why did you not fear to speak against my servant, against ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧?"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Miriam and Aaron spake against Moses" kept** (v1) — Hebrew וַתְּדַבֵּר, a feminine singular: "and she spoke, Miriam, and Aaron". דִּבֶּר בְּ is "speak against" here and in v8; the same words with Yahweh as the speaker (v2, 6, 8) are "speak with" (see v2).
- **"because of the Ethiopian woman whom he had married" → "on account of the Cushite woman whom he had taken"** (v1) — Hebrew הָאִשָּׁה הַכֻּשִׁית; לָקַח "take" (a wife). Cush is the land south of Egypt; who the woman is the text does not say.
- **"Hath the LORD indeed spoken only by Moses? hath he not spoken also by us?" → "Is it indeed only with Moses that Yahweh has spoken? Has he not spoken with us also?"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲרַק אַךְ־בְּמֹשֶׁה דִּבֶּר יְהוָה, two words for "only". בְּ with "speak" is "with" (v6, 8, where the same form אֲדַבֶּר־בּוֹ stands) and also "in, through, by": one rendering is kept through the passage.
- **"heard it" → "heard"** (v2) — the Hebrew has no object.
- **"very meek, above all the men which were upon the face of the earth" → "very humble, more than all mankind that was on the face of the ground"** (v3) — Hebrew עָנָו (written ענו, read עָנָיו; the sense is the same): bowed low, humble, of the root of "afflict / humble oneself"; said of a single man only here. הָאָדָם is "man, mankind"; אֲדָמָה "ground". The brackets round the verse are not in the Hebrew.
- **"spake suddenly" → "said suddenly"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … פִּתְאֹם. **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"in the pillar of the cloud" → "in a pillar of cloud"** (v5) — no article in the Hebrew. **"they both came forth" → "the two of them came out"** — Hebrew וַיֵּצְאוּ, the verb of v4.
- **"If there be a prophet among you, I the LORD will make myself known unto him in a vision" → "If there is a prophet of yours, I, Yahweh, make myself known to him in the vision"** (v6) — Hebrew אִם־יִהְיֶה נְבִיאֲכֶם יְהוָה בַּמַּרְאָה אֵלָיו אֶתְוַדָּע, literally "if there is your prophet, Yahweh, in the vision to him I make myself known". The sentence is hard. The accents join "Yahweh" to what follows, and so it is translated. The words can also be taken "if your prophet is Yahweh's" ("a prophet of Yahweh among you"), as some versions have it (from memory). נָא "please" is kept. מַרְאָה "vision" and חֲלוֹם "dream" have the article; מַרְאָה is of one root with מַרְאֶה "appearance" in v8.
- **"My servant Moses is not so, who is faithful in all mine house" → "Not so my servant Moses; in all my house he is faithful"** (v7) — the Hebrew order. נֶאֱמָן is "firm, trusted, faithful" (cited in Heb 3:2, 5). עֶבֶד is "slave"; of one who serves Yahweh, "servant".
- **"With him will I speak mouth to mouth, even apparently, and not in dark speeches" → "Mouth to mouth I speak with him, and by appearance, and not in riddles"** (v8) — Hebrew פֶּה אֶל־פֶּה. וּמַרְאֶה is literally "and appearance", a noun standing alone ("by" is supplied): in what is seen, plainly. חִידֹת "riddles".
- **"the similitude of the LORD shall he behold" → "the form of Yahweh he looks upon"** (v8) — Hebrew תְּמוּנַת יְהוָה, the word of Exod 20:4 and Deut 4:12, 15 ("you saw no form"): a visible shape. הִבִּיט "look at". Yahweh speaks of himself by name.
- **"wherefore then were ye not afraid to speak against my servant Moses?" → "And why did you not fear to speak against my servant, against Moses?"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּעַבְדִּי בְמֹשֶׁה, "against" twice.
- **"was kindled against them; and he departed" → "burned against them, and he went"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר אַף; אַף is "nose" and "anger". וַיֵּלַךְ "and he went".
- **"became leprous, white as snow" → "was leprous, like snow"** (v10) — Hebrew מְצֹרַעַת כַּשָּׁלֶג (as Exod 4:6); "white" is not in the Hebrew. צָרַעַת is a scaly outbreak of the skin that makes unclean (Lev 13–14); it is not the leprosy of modern medicine. **"looked upon" → "turned toward"** — Hebrew וַיִּפֶן.
- **"Alas, my lord, I beseech thee, lay not the sin upon us, wherein we have done foolishly" → "Oh, my lord, do not, please, set sin upon us, in that we have been foolish"** (v11) — Hebrew בִּי אֲדֹנִי; חַטָּאת without the article; נוֹאַלְנוּ.
- **"Let her not be as one dead, of whom the flesh is half consumed when he cometh out of his mother's womb" → "Let her not, please, be like the dead one, of whom, at his coming out from the womb of his mother, half of his flesh is eaten"** (v12) — the Hebrew order; נָא "please". An old scribal tradition (the *tiqqune sopherim*) says the text once read "our mother" and "our flesh" (from memory).
- **"Heal her now, O God, I beseech thee" → "El, please, heal her, please"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵל נָא רְפָא נָא לָהּ, five words: אֵל *El*, and נָא "please" twice. "O" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"If her father had but spit in her face, should she not be ashamed" → "And had her father surely spit in her face, would she not be put to shame"** (v14) — Hebrew וְאָבִיהָ יָרֹק יָרַק, the verb with its infinitive absolute, literally "and her father, spitting he spat in her face"; the Hebrew has no "if". תִכָּלֵם "be humiliated".
- **"let her be shut out from the camp … received in again" → "Let her be shut up … outside the camp, and afterward she shall be gathered in"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew תִּסָּגֵר, the verb of Lev 13:4; תֵּאָסֵף, from אָסַף "gather" (as 11:30).
- **"journeyed not" → "did not set out"; "removed … and pitched" → "set out … and they camped"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew נָסַע; חָנָה.

### Notes
- **Shortest crisis in Numbers** — briefer than ch 11, more intense. Authority challenge from Moses' own siblings.
- **Miriam named first, stricken alone** — the verb in v1 is feminine singular though the subject is "Miriam and Aaron". Only she becomes leprous.
- **Cushite wife mystery** — no follow-up in the text. Possibilities: (a) Zipporah being of Midianite-Cushite ancestry; (b) a second wife; (c) the real issue is the claim of v2 rather than the wife itself.
- **Moses' prophetic uniqueness** (v6–8) — Yahweh distinguishes Moses from prophets generally: Moses receives direct speech, not vision/dream; he looks upon Yahweh's "form." Deut 34:10 echoes: "no prophet has arisen since like Moses."
- **Moses' short prayer for Miriam** (v13) — five Hebrew words. Intercession despite the attack against him. Sets the pattern of his repeated intercessions in Numbers.
- **Seven days outside** (v14–15) — compare the seven days of shutting up in Lev 13–14. The whole people waits for her.
