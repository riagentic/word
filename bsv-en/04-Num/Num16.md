## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9, 22) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים. **"O God, the God of the spirits of all flesh" → "El, Elohim of the spirits of all flesh"** (v22) — Hebrew אֵל אֱלֹהֵי הָרוּחֹת לְכָל־בָּשָׂר: two words, אֵל and אֱלֹהִים; there is no "O". Literally "the spirits for all flesh" (so 27:16).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 38, 40, 41) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"Now Korah … took men" → "And Korah … took —"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּקַּח קֹרַח: the verb "took" has no object in the Masoretic Text, and the sentence runs on into v2 "and they rose up". KJV supplies "men"; others supply "himself" or read another verb ("rose up", "was insolent"). Nothing is supplied here, and the break is marked by a dash. Dathan, Abiram and On are joined to Korah by "and"; whether they are also subjects of "took" is not said.
- **"certain of the children of Israel, two hundred and fifty princes of the assembly, famous in the congregation, men of renown" → "men of the sons of Israel, two hundred and fifty, chieftains of the congregation, called ones of the meeting, men of name"** (v2) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאֵי עֵדָה, literally "lifted-up ones of the congregation"; קְרִאֵי מוֹעֵד, "called ones of the meeting" (מוֹעֵד as in "tent of meeting"; 1:16 has "called ones of the congregation"); אַנְשֵׁי־שֵׁם, as Gen 6:4.
- **"gathered themselves together against" → "assembled against"** (v3, 19, 42) — Hebrew נִקְהַל / הִקְהִיל עַל, the verb of קָהָל "assembly" (v3, 33, 47). עַל may also be "to, around". **"congregation" / "assembly"** — two Hebrew words, עֵדָה and קָהָל, kept apart (KJV exchanges them).
- **"Ye take too much upon you" → "Enough for you!"** (v3, 7) — Hebrew רַב־לָכֶם, literally "much for you". Moses gives the words back in v7, and answers "much" with "little": הַמְעַט מִכֶּם "is it too little for you" (v9), which Dathan and Abiram turn on him in v13.
- **"seeing all the congregation are holy, every one of them" → "For all the congregation, all of them, are holy"** (v3) — Hebrew כָל־הָעֵדָה כֻּלָּם קְדֹשִׁים. **"lift ye up yourselves above" → "lift yourselves up over"** — Hebrew תִּתְנַשְּׂאוּ, of the same root as נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (v2).
- **"when Moses heard it" → "And Moses heard, and he fell"** (v4) — two main clauses; no object.
- **"all his company" → "all his congregation"** (v5, 6, 11, 16, 40) — Hebrew עֲדָתוֹ, the same word עֵדָה as "the congregation" of Israel (v3, 9, 19): Korah has a congregation of his own.
- **"Even to morrow the LORD will shew who are his, and who is holy; and will cause him to come near unto him" → "In the morning Yahweh will make known him who is his, and the holy one, and will bring him near to himself"** (v5) — Hebrew בֹּקֶר וְיֹדַע יְהוָה, literally "morning, and Yahweh will make known"; "who is his" and "the holy one" are singular. The first "bring near" has no object in the Hebrew; "him" is supplied. הִקְרִיב "bring near" (v5, 9, 10) is the verb used of the men who "bring near" their firepans and incense (v17, 35, 38, 39) and of the one who must not "come near" (v40, קָרַב): one root through the chapter.
- **"censers" → "firepans"** (v6, 17, 18, 37–39, 46) — Hebrew מַחְתָּה, a pan for carrying coals (Lev 10:1; 16:12).
- **"put fire therein, and put incense in them" → "place fire in them and put incense on them"** (v7, 18, 46) — two Hebrew verbs, נָתַן and שִׂים.
- **"Hear, I pray you" → "Hear, please"** (v8) — Hebrew נָא.
- **"Seemeth it but a small thing unto you" → "Is it too little for you"** (v9); **"Is it a small thing"** (v13) — Hebrew הַמְעַט, literally "is it little".
- **"to do the service of the tabernacle" → "to serve the service of the dwelling"** (v9) — Hebrew לַעֲבֹד אֶת־עֲבֹדַת מִשְׁכַּן, verb and noun of one root. **"to minister unto them"** — Hebrew שֵׁרֵת, another verb.
- **"and seek ye the priesthood also?"** (v10) — the Hebrew has no question word; it may also be a statement, "and you seek the priesthood also".
- **"For which cause both thou and all thy company are gathered together against the LORD" → "Therefore you and all your congregation are those who have met together against Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew הַנֹּעָדִים עַל־יְהוָה, nifal of יָעַד "meet by appointment", the root of מוֹעֵד (v2). **"murmur" → "grumble"** (v11, 41) — Hebrew לוּן. In v11 the written form is the nifal and the reading tradition the hifil; the sense is the same.
- **"to kill us" → "to put us to death"** (v13); **"Ye have killed" → "You, you have put to death"** (v41) — Hebrew הֵמִית; in v41 the pronoun אַתֶּם is expressed.
- **"except thou make thyself altogether a prince over us" → "that you also surely make yourself a prince over us"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי־תִשְׂתָּרֵר עָלֵינוּ גַּם־הִשְׂתָּרֵר, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "that you make yourself prince over us, also making yourself prince"; the verb is from the root of שַׂר "prince".
- **"an inheritance of fields and vineyards" → "of field and vineyard"** (v14) — singular in the Hebrew. **"wilt thou put out the eyes of these men?" → "Will you gouge out the eyes of those men?"** — Hebrew תְּנַקֵּר "bore out"; הָהֵם "those". Whether this is meant of blinding in fact or of deceiving is not said.
- **"Moses was very wroth" → "Moses burned with anger exceedingly"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר לְמֹשֶׁה מְאֹד, literally "it burned to Moses greatly". **"Respect not thou their offering" → "Do not turn to their offering"** — Hebrew אַל־תֵּפֶן אֶל; מִנְחָה is here "offering" in the wide sense (elsewhere the grain offering). **"neither have I hurt one of them" → "I have not done evil to one of them"** — Hebrew הֲרֵעֹתִי. "Taken" is נָשָׂאתִי, "lifted, carried off".
- **"stood in the door of the tabernacle of the congregation with Moses and Aaron" → "they stood at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and Moses and Aaron"** (v18) — Hebrew וּמֹשֶׁה וְאַהֲרֹן, "and Moses and Aaron", added after the verb.
- **"that I may consume them" → "that I may make an end of them"** (v21, 45) — Hebrew כִּלָּה; "consume" (v35) is אָכַל "eat". **"wilt thou be wroth" → "will you be wrathful"** (v22) — Hebrew קָצַף, the verb of קֶצֶף "wrath" (v46).
- **"Get you up from about the tabernacle of Korah" → "Go up from around the dwelling of Korah"** (v24, 27) — Hebrew הֵעָלוּ, nifal of עָלָה "go up"; in v45 another verb, הֵרֹמּוּ "get up" (of רוּם "be high"), and in v21 הִבָּדְלוּ "separate yourselves". מִשְׁכַּן is singular, "the dwelling of Korah, Dathan and Abiram": the word used of the dwelling of Yahweh (v9).
- **"Depart, I pray you" → "Depart, please"** (v26) — Hebrew סוּרוּ נָא מֵעַל. **"lest ye be consumed" → "lest you be swept away"** — Hebrew סָפָה, as Gen 18:23.
- **"stood in the door of their tents" → "came out, standing at the entrance of their tents"** (v27) — Hebrew יָצְאוּ נִצָּבִים. **"little children" → "little ones"** — Hebrew טַף.
- **"for I have not done them of mine own mind" → "for it is not from my heart"** (v28) — Hebrew כִּי־לֹא מִלִּבִּי.
- **"If these men die the common death of all men, or if they be visited after the visitation of all men" → "if these die as all mankind dies, and the visitation of all mankind is visited upon them"** (v29) — Hebrew כְּמוֹת כָּל־הָאָדָם, literally "like the death of all mankind"; פְקֻדַּת … יִפָּקֵד, noun and verb of פָּקַד "visit". The Hebrew has "and", not "or".
- **"if the LORD make a new thing" → "if Yahweh creates a created thing"** (v30) — Hebrew בְּרִיאָה יִבְרָא, verb and noun of בָּרָא "create", the verb of Gen 1:1; the noun is found only here. "New" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the earth open her mouth" → "the ground opens its mouth"** (v30); **"the earth opened her mouth"** (v32) — in v30–31 the word is אֲדָמָה "ground", in v32–34 אֶרֶץ "earth"; "open" is פָּצָה in v30 (as Gen 4:11) and פָּתַח in v32.
- **"they go down quick into the pit" → "they go down alive to Sheol"** (v30, 33) — Hebrew חַיִּים שְׁאֹלָה. שְׁאוֹל is kept as the Hebrew word: the place of the dead. **"have provoked the LORD" → "have spurned Yahweh"** (v30) — Hebrew נִאֵץ, "treat with contempt".
- **"the ground clave asunder" → "the ground … was split open"** (v31) — Hebrew וַתִּבָּקַע, a passive.
- **"all the men that appertained unto Korah, and all their goods" → "all the men who were Korah's, and all the goods"** (v32) — Hebrew כָּל־הָאָדָם אֲשֶׁר לְקֹרַח: אָדָם, human beings (the word of v29 "mankind"). Korah himself is not named among those swallowed; see 26:10.
- **"the earth closed upon them" → "the earth covered over them"** (v33) — Hebrew וַתְּכַס עֲלֵיהֶם. **"from among the congregation" → "from the midst of the assembly"** — Hebrew הַקָּהָל.
- **"fled at the cry of them" → "fled at their voice"** (v34) — Hebrew לְקֹלָם.
- **"that offered incense" → "who brought near the incense"** (v35) — Hebrew מַקְרִיבֵי הַקְּטֹרֶת.
- **Verse numbers** (v36–50) — in the Hebrew Bible these verses are 17:1–15.
- **"that he take up the censers out of the burning, and scatter thou the fire yonder; for they are hallowed" → "and let him lift up the firepans from the midst of the burning, and strew the fire far off; for they have become holy"** (v37) — Hebrew וְיָרֵם, of הֵרִים "lift up"; מִבֵּין הַשְּׂרֵפָה, literally "from between the burning"; זְרֵה is an imperative, "strew"; קָדֵשׁוּ is a verb, "they have become holy".
- **"The censers of these sinners against their own souls, let them make them broad plates for a covering of the altar" → "the firepans of these who sinned against their souls. And they shall make them into beaten-out plates, an overlay for the altar"** (v38) — Hebrew הַחַטָּאִים הָאֵלֶּה בְּנַפְשֹׁתָם, "these sinners at (the cost of) their souls"; רִקֻּעֵי פַחִים, from רָקַע "beat out" (Exod 39:3); צִפּוּי, the noun of צִפָּה "overlay". **"they offered them" → "they brought them near"** — Hebrew הִקְרִיבֻם.
- **"the brasen censers, wherewith they that were burnt had offered" → "the bronze firepans that the burned ones had brought near"** (v39) — Hebrew הַשְּׂרֻפִים.
- **"that no stranger, which is not of the seed of Aaron, come near to offer incense" → "so that no strange man, who is not of the seed of Aaron, should come near to burn incense"** (v40) — Hebrew אִישׁ זָר, "a strange man": one who does not belong there, here anyone not of Aaron's seed. הַקְטִיר קְטֹרֶת, verb and noun of one root ("turn into smoke"). **"that he be not as Korah" → "and he shall not be like Korah"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִהְיֶה. **"as the LORD said to him by the hand of Moses" → "as Yahweh spoke by the hand of Moses to him"** — Hebrew לוֹ "to him" stands last; it is taken of Eleazar, or of Korah ("concerning him").
- **"on the morrow"** (v41) — Hebrew מִמָּחֳרָת.
- **"before the tabernacle of the congregation" → "to the front of the tent of meeting"** (v43) — Hebrew אֶל־פְּנֵי, literally "to the face of".
- **"Get you up from among this congregation" → "Get up from the midst of this congregation"** (v45) — Hebrew הֵרֹמּוּ.
- **"go quickly unto the congregation" → "carry it quickly to the congregation"** (v46) — Hebrew וְהוֹלֵךְ, "make go"; "it" is supplied. **"there is wrath gone out from the LORD" → "the wrath has gone out from before Yahweh"** — Hebrew הַקֶּצֶף מִלִּפְנֵי יְהוָה. **"the plague"** (v46, 47) is נֶגֶף, and in v48–50 מַגֵּפָה: two nouns of one root, literally "a blow".
- **"Aaron took as Moses commanded" → "Aaron took, as Moses had spoken"** (v47) — the verb has no object in the Hebrew. **"put on incense" → "placed the incense"** — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן. **"make atonement"** (v46, 47) — Hebrew כִּפֶּר, literally "cover" (or "wipe off").
- **"the plague was stayed" → "the plague was restrained"** (v48, 50) — Hebrew נֶעֱצַר, "be held back, shut in".
- **"about the matter of Korah" → "over the matter of Korah"** (v49) — Hebrew עַל־דְּבַר־קֹרַח.

### Notes

- **Who rises** — Korah, a Levite of the sons of Kohath (v1, 8–10), Dathan, Abiram and On of Reuben, and two hundred and fifty chieftains (v2). On is not named again.
- **Two ends** — the ground swallows Dathan, Abiram and the men who were Korah's (v31–33; Deut 11:6; Ps 106:17); fire consumes the two hundred and fifty who brought near the incense (v35). The sons of Korah did not die (26:11); psalms bear their name (Ps 42–49; 84; 85; 87; 88).
- **"Elohim of the spirits of all flesh"** (v22) — again in 27:16.
- **The plates on the altar** (v37–40) — the firepans became holy by being brought near, and are made a sign and a memorial.
- **Aaron between the dead and the living** (v46–48) — the incense that brought death on the two hundred and fifty makes atonement in the hand of the one who was chosen.
- **Jude 11** — "the rebellion of Korah".
