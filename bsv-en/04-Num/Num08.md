# Numbers 8

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and say to him: 'When you set up the lamps, over against the face of the lampstand the seven lamps shall give light.'"

[3] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ did so: over against the face of the lampstand he set up its lamps, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[4] And this was the work of the lampstand: hammered work of gold; to its base, to its flower, it was hammered work. According to the appearance that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had shown ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so he made the lampstand.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[6] "Take the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ from the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and cleanse them.

[7] And thus you shall do to them to cleanse them: sprinkle on them water of purging from sin, and they shall make a razor pass over all their flesh, and they shall wash their garments and cleanse themselves.

[8] And they shall take a bull of the herd, and its grain offering, fine flour mixed with oil; and a second bull of the herd you shall take for a sin offering.

[9] And you shall bring the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ near before the tent of meeting, and you shall assemble all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[10] And you shall bring the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall lay their hands on the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧.

[11] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall wave the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they shall be for serving the service of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ shall lay their hands on the head of the bulls; and offer the one as a sin offering and the one as a burnt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to make atonement for the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧.

[13] And you shall make the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ stand before ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and before his sons, and you shall wave them as a wave offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] And you shall separate the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ from the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ shall be mine.

[15] And after that the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ shall come to serve the tent of meeting; and you shall cleanse them and wave them as a wave offering.

[16] For given, given are they to me from the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; in place of the firstling of every womb, the firstborn of all, from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, I have taken them to myself.

[17] For mine is every firstborn among the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, among man and among beast; on the day when I struck down every firstborn in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ I sanctified them to myself.

[18] And I took the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ in place of every firstborn among the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[19] And I gave the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ as given ones to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons from the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to serve the service of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the tent of meeting and to make atonement for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and there shall be no plague among the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ when the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ draw near to the Holy."

[20] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧; according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ for the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, so the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did to them.

[22] And after that the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ came to serve their service in the tent of meeting before ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and before his sons; as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ concerning the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, so they did to them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. In v19 the phrase stands five times; none is dropped.
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"** (v2, 3, 4) — Hebrew מְנוֹרָה.
- **"When thou lightest the lamps" → "When you set up the lamps"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew הֶעֱלָה, literally "make go up": to set the lamps up on the lampstand, or to make their flame go up (light them); as Exod 25:37; 40:4, 25.
- **"over against the candlestick" → "over against the face of the lampstand"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew אֶל־מוּל פְּנֵי הַמְּנוֹרָה, literally "toward the front of the face of the lampstand" (מוּל "front, opposite", פָּנִים "face"), as Exod 26:9; 28:25, 37: the lamps give their light toward the space before the lampstand. Exod 25:37 says it with other words ("toward the side of its face"). In v3 the Hebrew has only "he set up its lamps"; "to give light" is not repeated.
- **"this work of the candlestick was of beaten gold" → "this was the work of the lampstand: hammered work of gold"** (v4) — Hebrew מַעֲשֶׂה "work, what is made"; מִקְשָׁה "hammered work" (Exod 25:31, 36), twice in the verse.
- **"unto the shaft thereof, unto the flowers thereof" → "to its base, to its flower"** (v4) — Hebrew יָרֵךְ, literally "thigh": the base of the lampstand (Exod 25:31); פֶּרַח "flower" is singular. The Hebrew has "to … to", without "from".
- **"according unto the pattern" → "According to the appearance"** (v4) — Hebrew מַרְאֶה "appearance", what is seen, from the same root as "had shown" (הֶרְאָה, "made see"); "pattern" is another word (תַּבְנִית, Exod 25:9, 40). **"so he made"** — the Hebrew does not name who.
- **"from among" → "from the midst of"** (v6, 14, 16, 19) — Hebrew מִתּוֹךְ.
- **"Sprinkle water of purifying upon them" → "sprinkle on them water of purging from sin"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵי חַטָּאת, literally "water of sin": חַטָּאת is the word for "sin" and for the "sin offering" (v8, 12); here it names what takes sin away, as the verb חִטֵּא "purge of sin" (v21). What water this was the verse does not say; in Num 19:9 the ashes of the heifer, kept for the "water of impurity", are called by the same word (חַטָּאת הִוא).
- **"let them shave all their flesh" → "they shall make a razor pass over all their flesh"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהֶעֱבִירוּ תַעַר עַל־כָּל־בְּשָׂרָם, word for word; the verb "shave" (גִּלַּח) is not used.
- **"wash their clothes, and so make themselves clean" → "wash their garments and cleanse themselves"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּבֵּס is the washing of cloth; הִטֶּהָרוּ is the reflexive of "cleanse" (v6, 15, 21); "and so" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"a young bullock" → "a bull of the herd"** (v8) — Hebrew פַּר בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "a bull, a son of the herd"; the phrase says nothing of age. **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה.
- **"bring" → "bring near"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב, the causative of "come near"; the same verb is used of bringing an offering.
- **"gather the whole assembly … together" → "assemble all the congregation"** (v9) — Hebrew הִקְהִיל "assemble" and עֵדָה "congregation".
- **"Aaron shall offer the Levites before the LORD for an offering" → "Aaron shall wave the Levites as a wave offering"** (v11, 13, 15, 21) — Hebrew הֵנִיף … תְּנוּפָה, verb and noun of one root, "wave a waving": the word used of the breast and the other parts moved to and fro before Yahweh (Exod 29:24; Lev 8:27). How men were "waved" is not said. In v15 the Hebrew has no "to Yahweh" or "before Yahweh".
- **"of the children of Israel" → "from the sons of Israel"** (v11) — Hebrew מֵאֵת, "from with".
- **"that they may execute the service of the LORD" → "and they shall be for serving the service of Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ לַעֲבֹד אֶת־עֲבֹדַת יְהוָה; verb and noun of one root (עָבַד, עֲבֹדָה), so also in v19, 22, 26. In v15 the object of "serve" is the tent of meeting itself.
- **"the heads of the bullocks" → "the head of the bulls"** (v12) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ is singular. **"thou shalt offer the one … and the other" → "offer the one … and the one"** — Hebrew וַעֲשֵׂה, an imperative; the verb is "do, make", used of preparing a sacrifice; the Hebrew says "the one" both times.
- **"set the Levites" → "make the Levites stand"** (v13) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד, the causative of "stand".
- **"go in to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "come to serve the tent of meeting"** (v15) — Hebrew לַעֲבֹד אֶת־אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"and thou shalt cleanse them"** — the Hebrew goes on with "and"; it does not say "when you have cleansed them".
- **"wholly given" → "given, given"** (v16) — Hebrew נְתֻנִים נְתֻנִים, the word said twice, as in 3:9; in v19 once, "as given ones". The name of the later temple servants, the Nethinim (נְתִינִים, Ezra 2:43), is a kindred form of the same root.
- **"instead of such as open every womb, even instead of the firstborn of all the children of Israel" → "in place of the firstling of every womb, the firstborn of all, from the sons of Israel"** (v16) — Hebrew פִּטְרַת כָּל־רֶחֶם, literally "what first opens every womb" (the form פִּטְרָה only here; elsewhere פֶּטֶר, Exod 13:2); בְּכוֹר כֹּל מִבְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל; "instead" stands once.
- **"both man and beast" → "among man and among beast"** (v17) — Hebrew בָּאָדָם וּבַבְּהֵמָה, as Exod 13:2. **"smote" → "struck down"** — Hebrew הִכָּה. **"sanctified them for myself" → "sanctified them to myself"** — Hebrew הִקְדַּשְׁתִּי אֹתָם לִי.
- **"I have taken … I have given" → "I took … I gave"** (v18, 19) — the Hebrew forms (וָאֶקַּח, וָאֶתְּנָה) tell a past act.
- **"as a gift" → "as given ones"** (v19) — Hebrew נְתֻנִים, the word of v16.
- **"that there be no plague" → "and there shall be no plague"** (v19) — Hebrew וְלֹא יִהְיֶה … נֶגֶף, literally "a blow", from נָגַף "smite" (Exod 12:13; 30:12). **"come nigh unto the sanctuary" → "draw near to the Holy"** — Hebrew נָגַשׁ "draw near"; הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "the holiness": the Holy, the holy room, or the holy things; "sanctuary" is another word (מִקְדָּשׁ).
- **"concerning the Levites" → "for the Levites" / "concerning the Levites"** (v20, 22) — Hebrew לַלְוִיִּם in v20, עַל־הַלְוִיִּם in v22.
- **"the Levites were purified" → "the Levites purged themselves of sin"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיִּתְחַטְּאוּ, the reflexive of חִטֵּא "purge of sin" (Exod 29:36; Lev 8:15), from the word "sin"; not the word "cleanse" at the end of the verse.
- **"This is it that belongeth unto the Levites" → "This is what is for the Levites"** (v24) — Hebrew זֹאת אֲשֶׁר לַלְוִיִּם; no word for "rule".
- **"from twenty and five years old" → "from twenty-five years old"** (v24) — literally "from a son of five and twenty years". Num 4:3, 23, 30 has thirty years; the two numbers stand in the Hebrew text side by side and are not reconciled there.
- **"they shall go in to wait upon the service" → "he shall come to muster in the host, in the service"** (v24) — Hebrew לִצְבֹא צָבָא, verb and noun of one root, literally "to host a host": to come in as an ordered body, the word of an army (as Exod 38:8; Num 4:23). The Hebrew is singular: "he".
- **"they shall cease waiting upon the service thereof" → "he shall return from the host of the service"** (v25) — Hebrew יָשׁוּב מִצְּבָא הָעֲבֹדָה.
- **"But shall minister with their brethren" → "And he shall minister to his brothers"** (v26) — Hebrew וְשֵׁרֵת אֶת־אֶחָיו; אֶת may mark the object ("minister to his brothers", attend on them) or mean "with" ("minister with his brothers"). **"to keep the charge, and shall do no service" → "to keep a charge, but service he shall not serve"** — Hebrew לִשְׁמֹר מִשְׁמֶרֶת, "keep a keeping", without the article; וַעֲבֹדָה לֹא יַעֲבֹד.
- **"touching their charge" → "in their charges"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּמִשְׁמְרֹתָם, plural.

### Notes
- **The seven lamps** (v2) — the lamps are set so that they give light over against the face of the lampstand (Exod 25:37); the table stands opposite it (Exod 40:22–24).
- **The Levites set apart** (v5–22) — distinct from the installation of the priests (Lev 8): the Levites are cleansed and "waved" before Yahweh, themselves given as a wave offering from the sons of Israel.
- **In place of the firstborn** (v16–18) — as 3:12–13, 40–51: the Levites stand for every firstborn of Israel, whom Yahweh sanctified to himself on the day he struck down the firstborn of Egypt.
- **From fifty years old** (v25–26) — the service (עֲבֹדָה) ends; ministering to his brothers and keeping a charge go on. The priests are given no such limit.
- **"No plague"** (v19) — the Levites are placed between the sons of Israel and the Holy; compare 1:53.
