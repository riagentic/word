# Numbers 6

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'A man or a woman, when one makes a special vow, the vow of a ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧, to keep apart for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[3] from wine and strong drink he shall keep apart; vinegar of wine and vinegar of strong drink he shall not drink, and any juice of grapes he shall not drink, and grapes, fresh or dried, he shall not eat.

[4] All the days of his keeping apart, of all that is made from the vine of wine, from the seeds to the skin, he shall not eat.

[5] All the days of the vow of his keeping apart a razor shall not pass over his head; until the days are full that he keeps apart for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he shall be holy, letting the loose hair of his head grow long.

[6] All the days of his keeping apart for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he shall not come to a soul of a dead one.

[7] For his father and for his mother, for his brother and for his sister — he shall not defile himself for them when they die, for the keeping apart of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is on his head.

[8] All the days of his keeping apart he is holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] And when someone dies beside him suddenly, in an instant, and defiles the head of his keeping apart, then he shall shave his head on the day of his cleansing; on the seventh day he shall shave it.

[10] And on the eighth day he shall bring two turtledoves or two sons of a dove to the priest, to the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[11] And the priest shall offer one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering, and he shall make atonement for him, because he sinned concerning the soul; and he shall sanctify his head on that day.

[12] And he shall keep apart for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the days of his keeping apart, and he shall bring a lamb a year old for a guilt offering; and the former days shall fall, for his keeping apart was unclean.

[13] And this is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧: on the day when the days of his keeping apart are full, one shall bring him to the entrance of the tent of meeting,

[14] and he shall bring near his offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: one lamb a year old without blemish for a burnt offering, and one ewe lamb a year old without blemish for a sin offering, and one ram without blemish for peace offerings,

[15] and a basket of unleavened bread of fine flour, cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and their grain offering and their drink offerings.

[16] And the priest shall bring them near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he shall offer his sin offering and his burnt offering.

[17] And the ram he shall offer as a sacrifice of peace offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, together with the basket of unleavened bread; and the priest shall offer its grain offering and its drink offering.

[18] And the ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧ shall shave at the entrance of the tent of meeting the head of his keeping apart, and he shall take the hair of the head of his keeping apart and put it on the fire that is under the sacrifice of the peace offerings.

[19] And the priest shall take the foreleg, boiled, from the ram, and one unleavened cake from the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and he shall put them on the palms of the ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧ after he has shaved his keeping apart.

[20] And the priest shall wave them as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it is holy for the priest, together with the breast of the wave offering and together with the leg of the contribution; and afterward the ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧ may drink wine.

[21] This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of the ⟦nazirite|nazir|Nazirite⟧ who vows, his offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for his keeping apart, besides what his hand attains to; according to his vow that he vows, so he shall do, according to ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of his keeping apart.'"

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[23] "Speak to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons, saying: 'Thus you shall bless the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying to them:

[24] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ bless you
and keep you.

[25] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ make his face shine toward you
and show favor to you.

[26] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lift up his face toward you
and put ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ for you.'

[27] And they shall put my name on the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I, I will bless them."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v7) — Hebrew אֱלֹהָיו.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 23, 27) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"shall separate themselves to vow a vow of a Nazarite" → "makes a special vow, the vow of a nazir"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי יַפְלִא לִנְדֹּר נֶדֶר נָזִיר, literally "does a special thing, to vow a vow of a nazir". הִפְלִיא is of the root "be wonderful" (Lev 27:2); its exact sense with a vow is uncertain. The verbs are masculine singular after "a man or a woman".
- **"Nazarite" → "nazir"; "to separate themselves" → "to keep apart"; "separation" → "keeping apart"** (throughout) — one Hebrew root: נָזִיר "one kept apart", הִזִּיר "keep apart" (Lev 15:31), נֵזֶר / נִזְרוֹ "his keeping apart". The verb has no "himself" in the Hebrew. נֵזֶר is also the "crown" of the priest (Exod 29:6; Lev 21:12); in v7 "the keeping apart of his Elohim is on his head" may be read so, and in v19 the word stands for the hair itself.
- **"liquor of grapes" → "juice of grapes"** (v3) — Hebrew מִשְׁרַת עֲנָבִים; מִשְׁרָה is found only here: what grapes have been steeped in, or their juice; uncertain. **"moist grapes" → "fresh"** — Hebrew לַחִים.
- **"made of the vine tree, from the kernels even to the husk" → "made from the vine of wine, from the seeds to the skin"** (v4) — Hebrew גֶּפֶן הַיַּיִן; חַרְצַנִּים and זָג are both found only here, and which parts of the grape they name is uncertain.
- **"there shall no razor come upon his head" → "a razor shall not pass over his head"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־יַעֲבֹר עַל. **"shall let the locks of the hair of his head grow" → "letting the loose hair of his head grow long"** — Hebrew גַּדֵּל פֶּרַע שְׂעַר רֹאשׁוֹ, literally "making great the loose growth of the hair of his head"; פֶּרַע is of the root "let loose" (5:18).
- **"he shall come at no dead body" → "he shall not come to a soul of a dead one"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־נֶפֶשׁ מֵת, as Lev 21:11: נֶפֶשׁ is the word "soul". So "concerning the soul" (v11), Hebrew עַל־הַנָּפֶשׁ: the dead person.
- **"He shall not make himself unclean … when they die"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא־יִטַּמָּא, reflexive; בְּמֹתָם, "at their death".
- **"if any man die very suddenly by him" → "when someone dies beside him suddenly, in an instant"** (v9) — Hebrew וְכִי־יָמוּת מֵת עָלָיו, literally "when a dead one dies upon him"; בְּפֶתַע פִּתְאֹם, two words of one sense. **"and he hath defiled the head of his consecration" → "and defiles the head of his keeping apart"** — Hebrew וְטִמֵּא; the subject is not named again.
- **"two young pigeons" → "two sons of a dove"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יוֹנָה, as Lev 5:7; whether בֶּן says "young" is not certain.
- **"shall offer"** (v11, 16, 17) — Hebrew עָשָׂה, "do, make"; "bring near" (v14, 16) is הִקְרִיב. **"for that he sinned by the dead" → "because he sinned concerning the soul"** (v11) — Hebrew מֵאֲשֶׁר חָטָא עַל־הַנָּפֶשׁ, literally "from that which he sinned". **"hallow" → "sanctify"** — Hebrew קִדֵּשׁ.
- **"he shall consecrate unto the LORD the days of his separation" → "he shall keep apart for Yahweh the days of his keeping apart"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהִזִּיר … אֶת־יְמֵי נִזְרוֹ, here with an object. **"a lamb of the first year" → "a lamb a year old"** (v12, 14) — Hebrew בֶּן־שְׁנָתוֹ, literally "a son of its year". **"trespass offering" → "guilt offering"** — Hebrew אָשָׁם. **"the days that were before shall be lost" → "the former days shall fall"** — Hebrew יִפְּלוּ, the plain verb "fall": they do not count. **"because his separation was defiled" → "for his keeping apart was unclean"** — Hebrew טָמֵא.
- **"the law of the Nazarite" → "the torah of the nazir"** (v13, 21) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה, the instruction for one matter. **"he shall be brought" → "one shall bring him"** (v13) — Hebrew יָבִיא אֹתוֹ, active, "he shall bring him"; who brings whom is not said (also read "he shall bring himself" or "it", the offering).
- **"shall offer his offering" → "shall bring near his offering"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהִקְרִיב אֶת־קָרְבָּנוֹ, verb and noun of one root. **"without blemish"** — Hebrew תָּמִים, "whole". **"peace offerings"** — Hebrew שְׁלָמִים, a plural.
- **"a basket of unleavened bread, cakes of fine flour mingled with oil" → "a basket of unleavened bread of fine flour, cakes mixed with oil"** (v15) — Hebrew סַל מַצּוֹת סֹלֶת חַלֹּת בְּלוּלֹת בַּשֶּׁמֶן. **"their meat offering" → "their grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָתָם, singular; "their drink offerings" is plural.
- **"shall bring them"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהִקְרִיב without an object; "them" is supplied.
- **"with the basket" → "together with the basket"** (v17, 20) — Hebrew עַל, literally "upon". **"his meat offering, and his drink offering" → "its grain offering and its drink offering"** (v17) — "his" or "its": the Hebrew is one word.
- **"shall shave the head of his separation at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "at the entrance of the tent of meeting"** (v18) — Hebrew פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"the sodden shoulder of the ram" → "the foreleg, boiled, from the ram"** (v19) — Hebrew הַזְּרֹעַ בְּשֵׁלָה מִן־הָאַיִל; זְרוֹעַ is "arm", of an animal the foreleg. **"upon the hands" → "on the palms"** — Hebrew כַּפֵּי. **"after the hair of his separation is shaven" → "after he has shaved his keeping apart"** — Hebrew אַחַר הִתְגַּלְּחוֹ אֶת־נִזְרוֹ; "hair" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"wave offering … heave shoulder" → "the breast of the wave offering … the leg of the contribution"** (v20) — Hebrew חֲזֵה הַתְּנוּפָה, שׁוֹק הַתְּרוּמָה (Lev 7:34). **"this is holy for the priest"** — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ, the noun "holiness, a holy thing".
- **"and of his offering unto the LORD for his separation, beside that that his hand shall get" → "his offering to Yahweh for his keeping apart, besides what his hand attains to"** (v21) — Hebrew קָרְבָּנוֹ … עַל־נִזְרוֹ מִלְּבַד אֲשֶׁר־תַּשִּׂיג יָדוֹ: what he can afford beyond it. **"according to the vow which he vowed" → "according to his vow that he vows"** — Hebrew כְּפִי, literally "according to the mouth of".
- **"saying unto them" kept** (v23) — Hebrew אָמוֹר לָהֶם, an infinitive: "saying to them".
- **"The LORD bless thee, and keep thee" → "May Yahweh bless you / and keep you"** (v24–26) — the six verbs are wishes (jussives); "you" is singular throughout. Each verse is printed as two lines.
- **"make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee" → "make his face shine toward you / and show favor to you"** (v25) — Hebrew אֵלֶיךָ, "to, toward"; וִיחֻנֶּךָּ, the verb of חֵן "favor".
- **"lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace" → "lift up his face toward you / and put shalom for you"** (v26) — Hebrew פָּנָיו, the same word "face" as in v25; וְיָשֵׂם לְךָ שָׁלוֹם, the verb "put, set"; שָׁלוֹם is wholeness, well-being, peace.
- **"and I will bless them" → "and I, I will bless them"** (v27) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי אֲבָרֲכֵם, with the pronoun written out. "Put my name on" is the same verb "put" as in v26.

### Notes

- **The nazir** — three things mark him: no produce of the vine, no razor, no coming near a dead person. The vow here is for a set time; Samson (Judg 13:5) and Samuel (1 Sam 1:11) are given for life.
- **A new beginning** (v9–12) — a sudden death beside him makes the head of his keeping apart unclean; the days already kept do not count.
- **The hair** (v18) — at the end it is shaved and put on the fire under the sacrifice of the peace offerings.
- **Acts 21:23–26** — Paul joins four men who have a vow on them and pays for their shaving.
- **The blessing** (v24–26) — three sentences, each with the name Yahweh, of three, five and seven Hebrew words. A form of it stands on two small silver scrolls found at Ketef Hinnom in Jerusalem.
- **"Put my name on"** (v27) — the name is spoken three times over Israel, and Yahweh himself blesses.
