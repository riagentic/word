# Numbers 27

[1] And the daughters of ⟦zelophehad|Tzelofchad|Zelophehad⟧ the son of ⟦hepher|Chefer|Hepher⟧, the son of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, the son of Machir, the son of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, of the clans of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ the son of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, came near; and these are the names of his daughters: ⟦mahlah|Machlah|Mahlah⟧, Noah and ⟦hoglah|Choglah|Hoglah⟧ and ⟦milcah|Milkah|Milcah⟧ and ⟦tirzah|Tirtzah|Tirzah⟧.

[2] And they stood before ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and before ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest and before the chieftains and all the congregation, at the entrance of the tent of meeting, saying,

[3] "Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the midst of the congregation of those who met together against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the congregation of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧, but in his sin he died; and he had no sons.

[5] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ brought their judgment near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[7] "Rightly do the daughters of ⟦zelophehad|Tzelofchad|Zelophehad⟧ speak. You shall surely give them a possession of an inheritance in the midst of the brothers of their father, and you shall make the inheritance of their father pass over to them.

[8] And to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall speak, saying: 'When a man dies and has no son, you shall make his inheritance pass over to his daughter.

[9] And if he has no daughter, you shall give his inheritance to his brothers.

[10] And if he has no brothers, you shall give his inheritance to the brothers of his father.

[11] And if his father has no brothers, you shall give his inheritance to his near kin who is near to him, of his clan, and he shall possess it.' And it shall be to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a statute of judgment, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧."

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Go up to this mountain of ⟦abarim|Avarim|Abarim⟧ and see the land that I have given to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[13] And you shall see it, and you shall be gathered to your peoples, you also, as ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ your brother was gathered,

[14] as you rebelled against my mouth in the wilderness of ⟦zin|Tzin|Zin⟧, in the strife of the congregation, to sanctify me by the waters before their eyes." Those are the waters of ⟦meribah|Merivah|Meribah⟧ of ⟦kadesh|Qadesh|Kadesh⟧, in the wilderness of ⟦zin|Tzin|Zin⟧.

[15] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying,

[16] "Let ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the spirits of all flesh, appoint a man over the congregation,

[17] who shall go out before them and who shall come in before them, and who shall bring them out and who shall bring them in, and let the congregation of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ not be like a flock that has no shepherd."

[18] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Take for yourself ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun, a man in whom is spirit, and you shall lay your hand on him.

[19] And you shall make him stand before ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest and before all the congregation, and you shall command him before their eyes.

[20] And you shall place some of your majesty on him, so that all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ may hear.

[21] And before ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest he shall stand, and he shall ask for him by the judgment of the Urim before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. [22] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ did as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded him, and he took ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and made him stand before ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest and before all the congregation.

[23] And he laid his hands on him and commanded him, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v16) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"Then came the daughters of Zelophehad … of the families of Manasseh" → "And the daughters of Zelophehad … of the clans of Manasseh … came near"** (v1) — Hebrew וַתִּקְרַבְנָה, "came near" (קָרַב); מִשְׁפְּחֹת "clans", as ch 26. The order of the names is the Hebrew's: "Mahlah, Noah and Hoglah and Milcah and Tirzah" (in 26:33 "Mahlah and Noah, Hoglah, Milcah and Tirzah").
- **"the princes" → "the chieftains"** (v2) — Hebrew הַנְּשִׂיאִם. **"by the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "at the entrance of the tent of meeting"** — Hebrew פֶּתַח אֹהֶל־מוֹעֵד.
- **"he was not in the company of them that gathered themselves together against the LORD in the company of Korah; but died in his own sin" → "he was not in the midst of the congregation of those who met together against Yahweh in the congregation of Korah, but in his sin he died"** (v3) — Hebrew הָעֵדָה … עֲדַת־קֹרַח, the word "congregation" twice (as 26:9); הַנּוֹעָדִים, "those who met by appointment" (the verb of "tent of meeting"); בְחֶטְאוֹ, "in his sin": "own" is not in the Hebrew. כִּי after the negative is "but".
- **"Why should the name of our father be done away from among his family" → "Why should the name of our father be diminished from the midst of his clan"** (v4) — Hebrew יִגָּרַע, "be diminished, taken away" (the verb of Exod 5:8, 11). **"Give unto us therefore a possession among" → "Give us a possession in the midst of"** — Hebrew תְּנָה, a singular: said to Moses; אֲחֻזָּה, "possession", land held (Gen 23:4; Lev 25); בְּתוֹךְ.
- **"Moses brought their cause before the LORD" → "Moses brought their judgment near before Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיַּקְרֵב … אֶת־מִשְׁפָּטָן: מִשְׁפָּט is "judgment": here the matter to be judged, their case. In the Hebrew text the last letter of this word is written large.
- **"spake" → "said"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר.
- **"The daughters of Zelophehad speak right" → "Rightly do the daughters of Zelophehad speak"** (v7) — Hebrew כֵּן stands first. **"thou shalt surely give them"** — Hebrew נָתֹן תִּתֵּן, literally "giving you shall give". **"a possession of an inheritance among their father's brethren; and thou shalt cause the inheritance of their father to pass unto them" → "a possession of an inheritance in the midst of the brothers of their father, and you shall make the inheritance of their father pass over to them"** — Hebrew אֲחֻזַּת נַחֲלָה: אֲחֻזָּה "possession" and נַחֲלָה "inheritance" are two words and are kept apart; הַעֲבַרְתָּ, the causative of עָבַר "pass over". "Them" and "their father" (the first time) are masculine forms in the Hebrew.
- **"If a man die" → "When a man dies"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי; the cases that follow (v9–11) have אִם "if".
- **"unto his kinsman that is next to him of his family" → "to his near kin who is near to him, of his clan"** (v11) — Hebrew לִשְׁאֵרוֹ הַקָּרֹב אֵלָיו מִמִּשְׁפַּחְתּוֹ; שְׁאֵר is literally "flesh" (Lev 18:6; 21:2). **"he shall possess it"** — Hebrew יָרַשׁ, "take possession of" (also "be heir to"); it is not the verb of "inheritance" (נָחַל). **"a statute of judgment"** kept — Hebrew חֻקַּת מִשְׁפָּט (again 35:29): a statute by which judgment is given.
- **"Get thee up into this mount Abarim" → "Go up to this mountain of Abarim"** (v12) — Hebrew עֲלֵה אֶל־הַר הָעֲבָרִים הַזֶּה; the name has the article, "the Abarim" (33:47–48).
- **"And when thou hast seen it, thou also shalt be gathered unto thy people" → "And you shall see it, and you shall be gathered to your peoples, you also"** (v13) — Hebrew וְרָאִיתָה … וְנֶאֱסַפְתָּ, two clauses joined by "and"; עַמֶּיךָ is a plural: his kinsfolk (Gen 25:8; Num 20:24).
- **"For ye rebelled against my commandment in the desert of Zin, in the strife of the congregation, to sanctify me at the water before their eyes" → "as you rebelled against my mouth in the wilderness of Zin, in the strife of the congregation, to sanctify me by the waters before their eyes"** (v14) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר, the "as" of v13, here giving the ground; מְרִיתֶם פִּי, literally so ("you" is plural: Moses and Aaron; 20:24); מְרִיבַת הָעֵדָה, "the strife of the congregation"; לְהַקְדִּישֵׁנִי, "to sanctify me": the sentence is short for "when you were to sanctify me" (20:12). **"that is the water of Meribah in Kadesh" → "Those are the waters of Meribah of Kadesh"** — Hebrew מֵי־מְרִיבַת קָדֵשׁ, literally "the waters of the strife of Kadesh": *Merivah* is the word "strife" of the line before. Whether this sentence is part of what Yahweh says the Hebrew does not mark.
- **"the God of the spirits of all flesh"** (v16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי הָרוּחֹת לְכָל־בָּשָׂר, word for word (16:22); רוּחַ is "spirit, breath, wind". **"set a man over the congregation" → "appoint a man over the congregation"** — Hebrew יִפְקֹד, the verb "number" of ch 26 and "visit" of Gen 21:1.
- **"which may go out before them, and which may go in before them, and which may lead them out, and which may bring them in; that the congregation of the LORD be not as sheep which have no shepherd" → "who shall go out before them and who shall come in before them, and who shall bring them out and who shall bring them in, and let the congregation of Yahweh not be like a flock that has no shepherd"** (v17) — Hebrew יוֹצִיאֵם … יְבִיאֵם, the causatives of "go out" and "come in"; וְלֹא תִהְיֶה, joined by "and"; צֹאן is "flock" (sheep and goats). The Hebrew has the article ("like the flock"), as it does in comparisons.
- **"Take thee Joshua" → "Take for yourself Joshua"** (v18) — Hebrew קַח־לְךָ. **"a man in whom is the spirit" → "a man in whom is spirit"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־רוּחַ בּוֹ, without the article.
- **"set him before" → "make him stand before"** (v19, 22) — Hebrew הַעֲמַדְתָּ, the causative of "stand" (v21). **"give him a charge in their sight" → "command him before their eyes"** (v19, 23) — Hebrew צִוָּה "command"; לְעֵינֵיהֶם.
- **"thou shalt put some of thine honour upon him, that all the congregation of the children of Israel may be obedient" → "you shall place some of your majesty on him, so that all the congregation of the sons of Israel may hear"** (v20) — Hebrew וְנָתַתָּה מֵהוֹדְךָ, literally "you shall give from your majesty": הוֹד is majesty, splendor (Ps 21:5; 96:6); it is not the word "honor" or "authority". יִשְׁמְעוּ is "may hear" (listen); the Hebrew has no object.
- **"who shall ask counsel for him after the judgment of Urim" → "and he shall ask for him by the judgment of the Urim"** (v21) — Hebrew וְשָׁאַל לוֹ: the subject is not named again (Eleazar asks for Joshua). הָאוּרִים as Exod 28:30: the word is kept; what the Urim were is not known. **"at his word" → "at his mouth"** — Hebrew עַל־פִּיו; whose mouth (Eleazar's, by the Urim, or Yahweh's) the Hebrew does not say. **"both he, and all the children of Israel with him, even all the congregation" → "he and all the sons of Israel with him, and all the congregation"** — Hebrew וְכָל־הָעֵדָה, "and".
- **"gave him a charge, as the LORD commanded by the hand of Moses" → "commanded him, as Yahweh had spoken by the hand of Moses"** (v23) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר "spoke"; בְּיַד־מֹשֶׁה.

### Notes
- **Daughters of Zelophehad** (v1–11) — five named women bring their case before Moses, Eleazar, the chieftains and the congregation; the answer becomes a statute for Israel. The matter is taken up again in ch 36 and carried out in Josh 17:3–6.
- **"In his sin he died"** (v3) — they set their father apart from the congregation of Korah (ch 16); he died in the wilderness with his generation (26:64–65).
- **Order of inheritance** (v8–11) — son, daughter, brothers, father's brothers, the near kin of the clan.
- **Moses and the land** (v12–14) — the reason is that of 20:12, 24; the account of his death is Deut 32:48–52; 34.
- **"Like a flock that has no shepherd"** (v17) — the words are taken up in 1 Kgs 22:17 and in Matt 9:36; Mark 6:34.
- **Joshua** (v18–23) — the laying on of the hand and the command before the priest and the congregation; so Deut 34:9. He is to stand before Eleazar, who asks by the Urim.
- **"*Elohim* of the spirits of all flesh"** (v16) — as 16:22.
