## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"When ye come into the land of Canaan" → "When you are coming into the land, Canaan"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי־אַתֶּם בָּאִים אֶל־הָאָרֶץ כְּנָעַן: a participle, and "the land" with the article, so that "Canaan" stands beside it as a name; the second time the Hebrew has the usual "the land of Canaan". **"with the coasts thereof" → "by its borders"** (v2, 12) — Hebrew לִגְבֻלֹתֶיהָ. גְּבוּל *gevul* is "border" in every place in this chapter (KJV "coast", "border", "quarter" is old English for a boundary); the word also means the land inside a border.
- **"your south quarter" → "the south side"** (v3) — Hebrew פְּאַת־נֶגֶב; פֵּאָה is an edge, a side. **"along by the coast of Edom" → "alongside Edom"** — Hebrew עַל־יְדֵי אֱדוֹם, literally "at the hands of Edom". **"the outmost coast of the salt sea eastward" → "from the end of the Salt Sea eastward"** — Hebrew מִקְצֵה … קֵדְמָה. The quarters of the sky are named in Hebrew by "Negev" (south), "sea" (west), "front" (east), "hidden / dark side" (north, צָפוֹן).
- **"ye shall have" for הָיָה לָכֶם** (v3, 6) — literally "there shall be for you".
- **"shall turn … and pass on … the going forth thereof … shall go on" → "shall turn … pass on … its outgoings … go out"** (v4, 5, 8, 9, 12) — the border is described as walking: נָסַב "turn", עָבַר "pass on" (the "pass over" of 32:5), יָצָא "go out", יָרַד "go down" (v11, 12). תּוֹצָאוֹת, a plural noun from יָצָא, is the place where the border comes out, its end; one rendering, "outgoings". In v4 the written text has the verb in the singular (והיה), the reading tradition in the plural (וְהָיוּ); the meaning is the same.
- **"from the south to the ascent of Akrabbim" → "from the south of the ascent of Akrabbim"** (v4) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶב לְ, "on the south of"; so also for Kadesh-barnea. "For you" (לָכֶם) is kept. "Akrabbim" means "scorpions".
- **"the river of Egypt" → "the wadi of Egypt"** (v5) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed; not the Nile. **"at the sea"** — Hebrew הַיָּמָּה, "to the sea, seaward".
- **"ye shall even have the great sea for a border" → "you shall have the Great Sea and the border"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהָיָה לָכֶם הַיָּם הַגָּדוֹל וּגְבוּל, literally "and there shall be for you the Great Sea and border". What "and border" adds is not certain: the sea and its shore land, or "the sea, as a border" (so also Deut 3:16; Josh 13:23; 15:12). "West" is the word "sea"; the Great Sea is the Mediterranean.
- **"ye shall point out for you" → "you shall mark out for yourselves"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew תְּתָאוּ, a verb found only in these two verses; "mark out, draw a line" is the sense the context gives; uncertain. In v10 the Hebrew has another form, וְהִתְאַוִּיתֶם, which elsewhere means "desire, crave" (11:4); here it is commonly taken as the same "mark out" (or from the root of אוֹת "sign"). One rendering is kept for both; the meaning of v10 is not certain.
- **"unto the entrance of Hamath" → "to Lebo-hamath"** (v8) — Hebrew לְבֹא חֲמָת, literally "to the coming to Hamath", "as far as the entering of Hamath"; commonly taken as a place name (Lebweh in Lebanon). Either is possible.
- **"Mount Hor"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew הֹר הָהָר, the same name as the mountain where Aaron died (33:38); here a mountain in the north.
- **"shall go on to Ziphron" → "shall go out to Ziphron"** (v9) — Hebrew יָצָא.
- **"the coast shall go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain" → "the border shall go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east of Ain"** (v11) — Hebrew הָרִבְלָה has the article ("the Riblah"), and so has לָעָיִן, "the Ain", that is, "the spring". **"shall reach unto the side of the sea of Chinnereth eastward" → "shall strike upon the shoulder of the Sea of Chinnereth eastward"** — Hebrew וּמָחָה עַל־כֶּתֶף: מָחָה in this sense ("strike against, reach") stands only here; כֶּתֶף is "shoulder", the slope beside the lake. The Sea of Chinnereth is the Sea of Galilee.
- **"salt sea" → "Salt Sea"** (v3, 12) — the Dead Sea.
- **"to give unto the nine tribes, and to the half tribe" → "to give to the nine tribes and the half of the tribe"** (v13) — Hebrew מַטֶּה, literally "staff", in every place in this chapter (v13–28); the other word for "tribe", שֵׁבֶט, does not stand in it (see 32:28). חֲצִי הַמַּטֶּה is "the half of the tribe".
- **"the tribe of the children of Reuben according to the house of their fathers … have received their inheritance" → "the tribe of the sons of the Reubenite by their fathers' house … have taken"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הָראוּבֵנִי, בְּנֵי־הַגָּדִי, "the sons of the Reubenite … of the Gadite"; לְבֵית אֲבֹתָם, "house" in the singular; לָקְחוּ "have taken", twice: the first time without an object, which comes at the end of the verse.
- **"on this side Jordan near Jericho eastward, toward the sunrising" → "across the Jordan of Jericho eastward, toward the sunrise"** (v15) — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לְיַרְדֵּן יְרֵחוֹ קֵדְמָה מִזְרָחָה: "across", as 32:19; "the Jordan of Jericho", as 33:48; two words for the east, "frontward" and "toward the rising".
- **"which shall divide the land unto you" → "who shall inherit the land for you"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew יִנְחֲלוּ לָכֶם, the simple stem of נָחַל "inherit": they take the land as inheritance on behalf of the people. "Divide, apportion" is not the Hebrew verb. In v29 the form is the causative-intensive לְנַחֵל, "make inherit".
- **"ye shall take one prince of every tribe" → "one chieftain, one chieftain from a tribe, you shall take"** (v18) — Hebrew נָשִׂיא אֶחָד נָשִׂיא אֶחָד מִמַּטֶּה: the repetition means "one each". **"prince" → "chieftain"** (v18, 22–28) — Hebrew נָשִׂיא *nasi*. In v19–21 the word is not used.
- **"Of the tribe of" → "for the tribe of"** (v19–28) — Hebrew לְמַטֵּה, "for"; "and" stands where the Hebrew has it.
- **"to divide the inheritance unto the children of Israel" → "to make the sons of Israel inherit"** (v29) — Hebrew לְנַחֵל אֶת־בְּנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל.

### Notes
- **Idealized borders** — this is the largest "promised land" extent: from the Wadi of Egypt in the south to Lebo-hamath in the north; from the Mediterranean to the Jordan. Actually approximated only under David and Solomon (1 Kgs 4:21, 8:65).
- **Nine-and-a-half tribes** — Reuben, Gad, and half-Manasseh are already settled east of the Jordan (ch 32); the remaining 9.5 tribes will receive land west of the Jordan.
- **Distribution commissioners** (v17–28) — Eleazar + Joshua + one chief from each of the 10 relevant tribes (no commissioner needed for Transjordan tribes). Names otherwise unknown, preserving tribal records.
- **Caleb representing Judah** (v19) — the faithful scout (Num 13–14) stands in place of the deceased Nahshon (the earlier Judahite chief).
- **Execution in Joshua** — Joshua 13–21 carries out this allotment; the lot falls, each tribe receives its inheritance.
