# Numbers 30

[1] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to the heads of the tribes of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "This is the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded:

[2] When a man vows a vow to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, or swears an oath to bind a bond on his soul, he shall not profane his word; according to all that goes out of his mouth he shall do.

[3] And when a woman vows a vow to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and binds a bond, in the house of her father, in her youth,

[4] and her father hears her vow and her bond that she has bound on her soul, and her father is silent to her, then all her vows shall stand, and every bond that she has bound on her soul shall stand.

[5] But if her father restrains her on the day that he hears, none of her vows and her bonds that she has bound on her soul shall stand; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will pardon her, because her father restrained her.

[6] And if she surely becomes a man's, and her vows are on her, or the rash utterance of her lips that she has bound on her soul,

[7] and her husband hears, on the day that he hears, and is silent to her, then her vows shall stand, and her bonds that she has bound on her soul shall stand.

[8] But if on the day that her husband hears he restrains her, then he makes void her vow that is on her, and the rash utterance of her lips that she has bound on her soul; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will pardon her.

[9] And the vow of a widow and of a divorced woman: all that she has bound on her soul shall stand on her.

[10] And if in the house of her husband she has vowed, or has bound a bond on her soul with an oath,

[11] and her husband hears and is silent to her — he has not restrained her — then all her vows shall stand, and every bond that she has bound on her soul shall stand.

[12] But if her husband surely makes them void on the day that he hears, nothing that went out of her lips, for her vows and for the bond of her soul, shall stand; her husband has made them void, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will pardon her.

[13] Every vow and every oath of a bond to afflict the soul: her husband may make it stand, and her husband may make it void.

[14] But if her husband is surely silent to her from day to day, then he makes all her vows stand, or all her bonds that are on her; he has made them stand, because he was silent to her on the day that he heard.

[15] But if he surely makes them void after he has heard, then he shall bear her iniquity."

[16] These are the statutes that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, between a man and his wife, between a father and his daughter, in her youth, in the house of her father.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept ("But" before "if" in v5, 8, 12, 14, 15, where the case turns). **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"unto the heads of the tribes concerning the children of Israel" → "to the heads of the tribes of the sons of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הַמַּטּוֹת לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, literally "the heads of the tribes belonging to the sons of Israel"; מַטֶּה "tribe" is the word for "staff". **"This is the thing" → "This is the word"** — Hebrew הַדָּבָר (Exod 16:16; 35:4; Lev 17:2).
- **"If a man vow a vow"** kept (v2, 3) — Hebrew יִדֹּר נֶדֶר, verb and noun of one root. **"swear an oath"** — Hebrew הִשָּׁבַע שְׁבֻעָה, again one root; the verb is an infinitive standing for the finite form.
- **"to bind his soul with a bond" → "to bind a bond on his soul"** (v2; so v4–11) — Hebrew לֶאְסֹר אִסָּר עַל־נַפְשׁוֹ, word for word: the bond is bound "on" the soul, as a thing tied on. אִסָּר (only in this chapter) is the noun of אָסַר "bind": an obligation taken on, commonly understood as a pledge to abstain (v13), beside נֶדֶר, the vow to give or do. נֶפֶשׁ is "soul": the person himself; "bind himself" gives the sense.
- **"he shall not break his word" → "he shall not profane his word"** (v2) — Hebrew לֹא יַחֵל, from חלל "profane": treat as common what is holy. **"all that proceedeth out of his mouth" → "all that goes out of his mouth"** — Hebrew הַיֹּצֵא.
- **"being in her father's house in her youth" → "in the house of her father, in her youth"** (v3; so v16) — the Hebrew has no "being".
- **"her father shall hold his peace at her" → "her father is silent to her"** (v4, 7, 11, 14) — Hebrew הֶחֱרִישׁ לָהּ, "be silent to / toward her" (Exod 14:14).
- **"shall stand"** kept (v4–12) — Hebrew קוּם "rise, stand": be valid. In v13, 14 the causative of the same verb, הֵקִים, is **"make stand"** (KJV "establish", "confirm": two words for one).
- **"if her father disallow her" → "if her father restrains her"** (v5, 8, 11) — Hebrew הֵנִיא, "hinder, restrain" (32:7, 9; Ps 33:10): he holds her back from it. **"in the day that he heareth"** — Hebrew בְּיוֹם שָׁמְעוֹ, literally "on the day of his hearing"; the Hebrew gives "hear" no object in v5, 7, 8, 11, 12, 14, 15.
- **"not any of her vows, or of her bonds … shall stand" → "none of her vows and her bonds … shall stand"** (v5) — literally "all her vows and her bonds … shall not stand"; the verb is singular. So in v12: literally "all that went out of her lips … shall not stand".
- **"the LORD shall forgive her" → "Yahweh will pardon her"** (v5, 8, 12) — Hebrew סָלַח, the verb said only of God (Lev 4:20; Num 14:19).
- **"if she had at all an husband, when she vowed" → "if she surely becomes a man's, and her vows are on her"** (v6) — Hebrew וְאִם־הָיוֹ תִהְיֶה לְאִישׁ וּנְדָרֶיהָ עָלֶיהָ, literally "if being she is / becomes a man's, and her vows upon her": the infinitive absolute with its verb ("surely"); "become a man's" is "be married" (Lev 22:12). אִישׁ is "man" and "husband": one word (v6–16). Whether she marries with the vows already on her, or vows as a wife, the words do not say; v10 names the vow made in the husband's house apart.
- **"or uttered ought out of her lips" → "or the rash utterance of her lips"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew מִבְטָא שְׂפָתֶיהָ, a noun, from בִּטֵּא "utter rashly, speak without thought" (Lev 5:4; Ps 106:33); how much "rashly" is in the word is not certain.
- **"then he shall make her vow … of none effect" → "then he makes void her vow"** (v8; so v12, 13, 15) — Hebrew הֵפֵר, "make ineffectual, bring to nothing"; it is the verb of "break a covenant" (Gen 17:14), but here the husband has the right to do it, and "break her vow" would say that he trespasses. KJV has "make of none effect" and "make void" for the one verb. The clause may also be read as part of the condition ("and makes void … , then Yahweh will pardon her").
- **"every vow of a widow, and of her that is divorced, wherewith they have bound their souls, shall stand against her" → "the vow of a widow and of a divorced woman: all that she has bound on her soul shall stand on her"** (v9) — the Hebrew is singular throughout; גְּרוּשָׁה is literally "one driven out"; עָלֶיהָ "on her" as in v6, 8, 14: it rests on her, binding her.
- **"if she vowed in her husband's house"** (v10) — the Hebrew has בֵּית אִישָׁהּ without "in". **"with an oath"** — Hebrew בִּשְׁבֻעָה.
- **"if her husband hath utterly made them void"** → **"if her husband surely makes them void"** (v12; so v15), **"if her husband altogether hold his peace" → "if her husband is surely silent"** (v14) — the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "making void he makes void", "being silent he is silent".
- **"whatsoever proceeded out of her lips concerning her vows, or concerning the bond of her soul" → "nothing that went out of her lips, for her vows and for the bond of her soul"** (v12) — Hebrew מוֹצָא שְׂפָתֶיהָ, "what goes out of her lips"; לְ "for, as to"; אִסַּר נַפְשָׁהּ "the bond of her soul".
- **"every binding oath to afflict the soul" → "every oath of a bond to afflict the soul"** (v13) — Hebrew שְׁבֻעַת אִסָּר לְעַנֹּת נָפֶשׁ: עִנָּה נֶפֶשׁ as in 29:7, self-denial (fasting or other abstaining). **"her husband may establish it, or her husband may make it void" → "her husband may make it stand, and her husband may make it void"** — Hebrew "and", not "or"; the verbs are plain imperfects ("makes / shall make"); "may" gives the right that the verse states.
- **"he confirmeth them" → "he has made them stand"** (v14) — Hebrew הֵקִים, a perfect.
- **"after that he hath heard them"** (v15) — Hebrew אַחֲרֵי שָׁמְעוֹ, literally "after his hearing": after the day of v14. **"he shall bear her iniquity"** kept — Hebrew וְנָשָׂא אֶת־עֲוֺנָהּ: the guilt of the vow not kept falls on him.
- **"between a man and his wife"** kept (v16) — Hebrew בֵּין אִישׁ לְאִשְׁתּוֹ, literally "between a man to his wife".

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew Bible counts 29:40 as 30:1, so our 30:1–16 is 30:2–17 there; our numbers are kept.
- **Four words** — נֶדֶר "vow", אִסָּר "bond", שְׁבוּעָה "oath", מִבְטָא "rash utterance" are kept apart through the chapter, and so the verbs הֶחֱרִישׁ "be silent", הֵנִיא "restrain", הֵפֵר "make void", קוּם / הֵקִים "stand / make stand".
- **Vow validity framework** — ancient Israelite vows are legally binding speech-acts. Ch 30 addresses the social complications: can a woman's vow bind the household?
- **Men and women** — a man's vow always stands (v2). A woman's vow in her father's or her husband's house can be made void by him on the day he hears of it. The vows of a widow and of a divorced woman (v9) stand.
- **Day-of-hearing rule** — the father or husband must speak on the day he hears. Silence makes the vow stand. A later voiding puts her iniquity on the husband (v15).
- **Pardon clause** (v5, 8, 12) — Yahweh will pardon the woman whose vow was made void.
- **Later NT context** — Jesus' discussion of *Corban* (Mk 7:9–13) addresses misuse of vow-obligations. The Sermon on the Mount (Mt 5:33–37) moves toward simple truthfulness over elaborate oath-systems.
- **Cultural framing** — the chapter encodes patriarchal household structure but within it creates meaningful protections: a woman is not abandoned to destructive self-binding; the authority who should protect her bears the guilt if he misuses the right to make her vow void.
