# Numbers 17

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and take from them a staff for each father's house, from all their chieftains by their fathers' houses, twelve staffs; each man's name you shall write on his staff.

[3] And the name of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ you shall write on the staff of Levi; for there is one staff for the head of their fathers' house.

[4] And you shall lay them up in the tent of meeting before the testimony, where I meet you.

[5] And it shall be that the man whom I choose, his staff shall bud; and I will make subside from upon me the grumblings of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which they grumble against you."

[6] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and all their chieftains gave him a staff for each chieftain, by their fathers' houses, twelve staffs; and the staff of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ was in the midst of their staffs.

[7] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ laid up the staffs before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the tent of the testimony.

[8] And it came to pass on the morrow that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ came into the tent of the testimony, and behold, the staff of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, for the house of Levi, had budded; and it brought out a bud and blossomed a blossom and ripened almonds.

[9] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ brought out all the staffs from before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and they saw, and they took each man his staff.

[10] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Return the staff of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ before the testimony, for keeping, for a sign to the sons of rebellion; and you shall make an end of their grumblings from upon me, and they shall not die."

[11] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ did; as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded him, so he did.

[12] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying, "Behold, we expire, we perish, all of us perish.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 5, 6, 9, 12) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew Bible this chapter is 17:16–28; Hebrew 17:1–15 is our 16:36–50.
- **"rod" → "staff"** (throughout) — Hebrew מַטֶּה. The same word means "tribe" (1:4, 16; 18:2): each staff stands for its tribe.
- **"take of every one of them a rod according to the house of their fathers, of all their princes according to the house of their fathers" → "take from them a staff for each father's house, from all their chieftains by their fathers' houses"** (v2) — Hebrew מַטֶּה מַטֶּה לְבֵית אָב, literally "a staff, a staff for a father's house"; "from them" is מֵאִתָּם, with no "every one". נָשִׂיא is literally "one lifted up". So v6, literally "a staff for one chieftain, a staff for one chieftain".
- **"for one rod shall be for the head of the house of their fathers" → "for there is one staff for the head of their fathers' house"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי מַטֶּה אֶחָד לְרֹאשׁ בֵּית אֲבוֹתָם, a clause without a verb.
- **"in the tabernacle of the congregation" → "in the tent of meeting"** (v4) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"where I will meet with you" → "where I meet you"** — Hebrew אִוָּעֵד, of the root of מוֹעֵד (Exod 25:22); "you" is plural. **"lay them up"** (v4, 7) — Hebrew הִנִּיחַ, as Exod 16:33.
- **"the man's rod, whom I shall choose, shall blossom" → "the man whom I choose, his staff shall bud"** (v5, 8) — Hebrew יִפְרָח; in v8 the same root follows as a noun, פֶּרַח "bud" (the "flower" of the lampstand, Exod 25:33), before צִיץ "blossom".
- **"I will make to cease from me the murmurings" → "I will make subside from upon me the grumblings"** (v5) — Hebrew וַהֲשִׁכֹּתִי מֵעָלַי, hifil of שָׁכַךְ, used of the waters in Gen 8:1. **"whereby they murmur against you" → "which they grumble against you"** — Hebrew תְּלֻנּוֹת … מַלִּינִם, noun and verb of לוּן.
- **"the tabernacle of witness" → "the tent of the testimony"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew אֹהֶל הָעֵדֻת; "the testimony" (v4, 10) is the tablets in the ark (Exod 25:16, 21).
- **"was budded, and brought forth buds, and bloomed blossoms, and yielded almonds" → "had budded; and it brought out a bud and blossomed a blossom and ripened almonds"** (v8) — Hebrew פָּרַח … וַיֹּצֵא פֶרַח וַיָּצֵץ צִיץ וַיִּגְמֹל שְׁקֵדִים: "bud" and "blossom" are singular, each verb with a noun of its own root in the second pair. **"for the house of Levi"** — Hebrew לְבֵית לֵוִי.
- **"they looked" → "they saw"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּרְאוּ.
- **"Bring Aaron's rod again before the testimony, to be kept for a token against the rebels" → "Return the staff of Aaron before the testimony, for keeping, for a sign to the sons of rebellion"** (v10) — Hebrew לְמִשְׁמֶרֶת, the noun of שָׁמַר "keep", as Exod 16:33; לְאוֹת לִבְנֵי־מֶרִי, literally "for a sign to the sons of rebellion". **"thou shalt quite take away their murmurings from me, that they die not" → "you shall make an end of their grumblings from upon me, and they shall not die"** — Hebrew וּתְכַל, of כִּלָּה "finish, make an end of" (16:21); the form may also be read "that their grumblings may come to an end".
- **"Behold, we die, we perish, we all perish" → "Behold, we expire, we perish, all of us perish"** (v12) — Hebrew גָּוַעְנוּ אָבַדְנוּ כֻּלָּנוּ אָבָדְנוּ: three perfects, literally "we have expired, we have perished, all of us have perished", said of what the speakers hold to be certain. גָּוַע is "expire", not the common word "die" (v13 יָמוּת).
- **"Whosoever cometh any thing near" → "Everyone who comes near, who comes near"** (v13) — Hebrew כֹּל הַקָּרֵב הַקָּרֵב: the participle stands twice. **"shall we be consumed with dying?" → "Have we come to an end in expiring?"** — Hebrew הַאִם תַּמְנוּ לִגְוֺעַ, literally "have we come to an end, to expire?": are we to expire to the last one?

### Notes

- **The staff of Aaron** — the staff of the man whom Yahweh chooses buds (v5); 16:5 said that Yahweh would make known whom he chooses. The staff is laid up before the testimony (v10); Heb 9:4 names it with the jar of manna and the tablets.
- **Almonds** — Hebrew שָׁקֵד; Jer 1:11–12 plays on the word and the verb שָׁקַד "watch".
- **The fear of the people** (v12–13) — answered in ch. 18: the priests and the Levites keep the charge of the sanctuary, "and there shall be no more wrath on the sons of Israel" (18:5).
