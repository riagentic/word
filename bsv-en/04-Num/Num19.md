# Numbers 19

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, saying,

[2] "This is the statute of ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded, saying: Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and let them take to you a red cow, whole, in which there is no blemish, on which no yoke has gone up.

[3] And you shall give it to ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest, and he shall bring it out to the outside of the camp, and one shall slaughter it before him.

[4] And ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest shall take of its blood with his finger and sprinkle of its blood toward the front of the tent of meeting seven times.

[5] And one shall burn the cow before his eyes; its skin and its flesh and its blood, with its dung, he shall burn.

[6] And the priest shall take cedar wood and hyssop and scarlet, and throw them into the midst of the burning of the cow.

[7] And the priest shall wash his garments and wash his flesh in water, and afterward he shall come into the camp; and the priest shall be unclean until the evening.

[8] And he who burns it shall wash his garments in water and wash his flesh in water, and he shall be unclean until the evening.

[9] And a clean man shall gather the ashes of the cow and lay them up outside the camp in a clean place; and it shall be for the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ for keeping, for water of impurity; it is a sin offering.

[10] And he who gathers the ashes of the cow shall wash his garments and be unclean until the evening; and it shall be for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and for the sojourner who sojourns in their midst an everlasting statute.

[11] He who touches the dead, any soul of man, shall be unclean seven days.

[12] He shall purge himself of sin with it on the third day, and on the seventh day he shall be clean; and if he does not purge himself of sin on the third day, then on the seventh day he shall not be clean.

[13] Everyone who touches the dead, the soul of the man who has died, and does not purge himself of sin, has defiled the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and that soul shall be cut off from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. Because water of impurity was not dashed on him, he shall be unclean; his uncleanness is still in him.

[14] This is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧: when a man dies in a tent, everyone who comes into the tent and everything that is in the tent shall be unclean seven days.

[15] And every open vessel on which there is no cover with a cord is unclean.

[16] And everyone who touches, on the face of the field, one slain by the sword, or one dead, or a bone of a man, or a grave, shall be unclean seven days.

[17] And they shall take for the unclean one of the dust of the burning of the sin offering, and one shall place on it living water, into a vessel.

[18] And a clean man shall take hyssop and dip it in the water, and sprinkle on the tent and on all the vessels and on the souls who were there, and on him who touched the bone or the slain one or the dead one or the grave.

[19] And the clean one shall sprinkle on the unclean one on the third day and on the seventh day, and he shall purge him of sin on the seventh day; and he shall wash his garments and wash in water, and he shall be clean in the evening.

[20] And a man who is unclean and does not purge himself of sin, that soul shall be cut off from the midst of the assembly, for he has defiled the sanctuary of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; water of impurity was not dashed on him; he is unclean.

[21] And it shall be to them an everlasting statute. And he who sprinkles the water of impurity shall wash his garments, and he who touches the water of impurity shall be unclean until the evening.

[22] And everything that the unclean one touches shall be unclean, and the soul that touches shall be unclean until the evening."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 9, 10) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"the ordinance of the law" → "the statute of the torah"** (v2); **"This is the law" → "This is the torah"** (v14) — Hebrew חֻקַּת הַתּוֹרָה, הַתּוֹרָה: the instruction for this one matter.
- **"that they bring thee a red heifer without spot, wherein is no blemish, and upon which never came yoke" → "let them take to you a red cow, whole, in which there is no blemish, on which no yoke has gone up"** (v2) — Hebrew וְיִקְחוּ אֵלֶיךָ, "and let them take to you". פָּרָה is "cow" (Gen 41:2), a grown female; "heifer" is another word, עֶגְלָה. תְּמִימָה is "whole, complete", elsewhere rendered "without blemish"; here "in which there is no blemish" (מוּם) follows as words of its own, so the two are kept apart. Jewish tradition joins the word to "red": "wholly red". "Red" is אֲדֻמָּה, the word of Gen 25:30. עָלָה is "go up".
- **"that he may bring her forth without the camp, and one shall slay her before his face" → "he shall bring it out to the outside of the camp, and one shall slaughter it before him"** (v3) — Hebrew וְשָׁחַט אֹתָהּ לְפָנָיו: the verb is singular with no subject named, another than Eleazar; so "one shall burn" (v5), "one shall place" (v17). The cow is feminine in the Hebrew.
- **"sprinkle of her blood directly before the tabernacle of the congregation" → "sprinkle of its blood toward the front of the tent of meeting"** (v4) — Hebrew אֶל־נֹכַח פְּנֵי, literally "toward opposite the face of".
- **"in his sight" → "before his eyes"** (v5) — Hebrew לְעֵינָיו. **"her skin … with her dung"** — Hebrew עַל־פִּרְשָׁהּ, literally "upon its dung": the contents of the bowels.
- **"cedar wood, and hyssop, and scarlet"** (v6) — as Lev 14:4; שְׁנִי תוֹלָעַת is literally "scarlet of the worm", the dye and what is dyed with it; "yarn" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"wash his clothes, and he shall bathe his flesh in water" → "wash his garments and wash his flesh in water"** (v7, 8, 10, 19, 21) — two Hebrew verbs, כִּבֵּס of garments and רָחַץ of the body; English has one word for both.
- **"until the even" → "until the evening"** (v7, 8, 10, 21, 22).
- **"a man that is clean shall gather up the ashes of the heifer, and lay them up" → "a clean man shall gather the ashes of the cow and lay them up"** (v9) — Hebrew אֵפֶר "ashes"; in v17 the word is עָפָר "dust". "Them" is supplied after "lay up".
- **"it shall be kept for the congregation … for a water of separation: it is a purification for sin" → "it shall be for the congregation … for keeping, for water of impurity; it is a sin offering"** (v9) — Hebrew לְמִשְׁמֶרֶת, the noun of שָׁמַר "keep" (17:10). מֵי נִדָּה is literally "water of impurity": נִדָּה is the word used of a woman's monthly impurity (Lev 15:19) and of what is cast off as unclean; its root sense ("separation" or "expulsion") is not certain. Here it is water for the removing of impurity (so Zech 13:1); the verse keeps the two words as they stand. חַטָּאת הִוא is "it is a sin offering", literally "it is a sin": the one word for the sin and for what takes it away; so v17 "the burning of the sin offering". In 8:7 the water is called "water of purging from sin".
- **"the stranger that sojourneth among them, for a statute for ever" → "the sojourner who sojourns in their midst an everlasting statute"** (v10, 21) — Hebrew גֵּר; חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age".
- **"He that toucheth the dead body of any man" → "He who touches the dead, any soul of man"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּמֵת לְכָל־נֶפֶשׁ אָדָם, literally "a dead one, for any soul of man". נֶפֶשׁ "soul" is said here of the dead person (so v13; Lev 21:11); "body" is not in the Hebrew. "Man" is אָדָם, a human being (v11, 13, 14, 16).
- **"He shall purify himself with it" → "He shall purge himself of sin with it"** (v12, 13, 20); **"shall purify him" → "shall purge him of sin"** (v19) — Hebrew הִתְחַטֵּא, חִטֵּא, verbs made from חַטָּאת "sin": literally "de-sin". "Be clean" is another root, טָהֵר.
- **"on the third day, and on the seventh day he shall be clean"** (v12) — the Hebrew word order is kept. The words may also be divided "on the third day and on the seventh day, and he shall be clean" (so in the second half), as v19 has the sprinkling on both days.
- **"Whosoever toucheth the dead body of any man that is dead" → "Everyone who touches the dead, the soul of the man who has died"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּמֵת בְּנֶפֶשׁ הָאָדָם אֲשֶׁר־יָמוּת. **"defileth the tabernacle" → "has defiled the dwelling"** — Hebrew טִמֵּא, a perfect; מִשְׁכָּן. In v20 it is "the sanctuary" (מִקְדָּשׁ).
- **"the water of separation was not sprinkled upon him" → "water of impurity was not dashed on him"** (v13, 20) — Hebrew זֹרַק, the passive of זָרַק "dash, throw"; "sprinkle" (v4, 18, 19, 21) is another verb, הִזָּה. **"his uncleanness is yet upon him" → "is still in him"** — Hebrew בוֹ, "in him" or "on him".
- **"all that come into the tent, and all that is in the tent"** (v14) — Hebrew כָּל … וְכָל; the second may be "everyone who" or "everything that".
- **"every open vessel, which hath no covering bound upon it" → "every open vessel on which there is no cover with a cord"** (v15) — Hebrew צָמִיד פָּתִיל עָלָיו. The meaning is uncertain: צָמִיד (elsewhere "bracelet", from a root "bind, join") is taken as a lid that is fastened on, and פָּתִיל is "cord"; also read "no cover fastened upon it".
- **"one that is slain with a sword in the open fields" → "on the face of the field, one slain by the sword"** (v16) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה; חֲלַל־חֶרֶב, literally "one pierced by the sword".
- **"of the ashes of the burnt heifer of purification for sin, and running water shall be put thereto in a vessel" → "of the dust of the burning of the sin offering, and one shall place on it living water, into a vessel"** (v17) — Hebrew מֵעֲפַר שְׂרֵפַת הַחַטָּאת; "heifer" is not in the Hebrew. מַיִם חַיִּים is "living water", running water from a spring (Lev 14:5).
- **"upon the persons that were there" → "on the souls who were there"** (v18) — Hebrew הַנְּפָשׁוֹת, here the living.
- **"the soul that toucheth it" → "the soul that touches"** (v22) — the Hebrew has no object.

### Notes

- **The red cow** — slaughtered and burned outside the camp, with its blood; its ashes are kept for the water of impurity. Heb 9:13 refers to these ashes.
- **Those who prepare it are unclean** (v7, 8, 10, 21) — the priest, the one who burns, the one who gathers the ashes and the one who sprinkles are unclean until the evening, though the water makes the unclean clean.
- **Uncleanness from the dead** (v11–16) — seven days, with the water on the third and on the seventh day; without it the dwelling of Yahweh is defiled (v13, 20).
- **Cedar wood, hyssop, scarlet** (v6) — the three of the cleansing of the leper (Lev 14:4, 6, 49).
