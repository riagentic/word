## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Send thou men, that they may search" → "Send for yourself men, and let them explore"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׁלַח־לְךָ, with לְךָ "for yourself" (as Exod 34:1 "Hew for yourself"); וְיָתֻרוּ "and let them explore".
- **"search" / "spy out" → "explore"** (v2, 16, 17, 21, 25, 32) — Hebrew תּוּר, "go about, seek out, explore" (10:33 of the ark seeking out a resting place). It is one verb throughout; מְרַגֵּל "spy" (Gen 42:9) is another word, not used in this chapter.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 3, 24, 26, 32) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"of every tribe of their fathers shall ye send a man, every one a ruler among them" → "one man for each tribe of his fathers you shall send, every one a chieftain among them"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶחָד אִישׁ אֶחָד לְמַטֵּה אֲבֹתָיו, literally "one man, one man for the tribe of his fathers"; the verb is plural. נָשִׂיא "chieftain", literally "one lifted up". מַטֶּה "tribe" is the word for "staff".
- **"by the commandment of the LORD" → "at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי יְהוָה, at his word. **"all those men were heads" → "all of them were men, heads … were they"** — Hebrew כֻּלָּם אֲנָשִׁים, רָאשֵׁי … הֵמָּה.
- **"Of the tribe" → "for the tribe"** (v4–15) — Hebrew לְמַטֵּה. In v11 the Hebrew has "for the tribe of Joseph, for the tribe of Manasseh" with no word between; "namely" was taken out.
- **"Oshea" → "Hoshea"; "Jehoshua" → "Joshua"** (v8, 16) — Hebrew הוֹשֵׁעַ "he saved / salvation", יְהוֹשֻׁעַ "Yahweh is salvation"; בִּן־נוּן "the son of Nun".
- **"Get you up this way southward, and go up into the mountain" → "Go up here in the Negev, and you shall go up into the hill country"** (v17) — Hebrew עֲלוּ זֶה בַּנֶּגֶב, literally "go up this in the Negev" (this way, or here). נֶגֶב is the dry southland, a name; it is also the word for "south" (so v22, 29). הָהָר is "the mountain", here the hill country (v29).
- **"see the land, what it is; and the people that dwelleth therein, whether they be strong or weak, few or many" → "you shall see the land, what it is, and the people that dwells upon it, whether it is strong or weak, whether it is few or many"** (v18) — the people is singular in the Hebrew ("it") through v18–19, 28, 31. רָפֶה "weak" is literally "slack".
- **"good or bad" kept** (v19) — Hebrew רָעָה, the word "evil", here of the quality of a land. **"in tents, or in strong holds" → "in camps or in fortresses"** — Hebrew בְּמַחֲנִים "in camps" (open settlements); מִבְצָרִים "fortified places".
- **"whether there be wood therein, or not" → "whether there are trees in it or not"** (v20) — Hebrew עֵץ, a collective singular, "tree, wood". **"be ye of good courage, and bring of the fruit" → "you shall strengthen yourselves and take of the fruit"** — Hebrew וְהִתְחַזַּקְתֶּם, from חָזַק "be strong"; וּלְקַחְתֶּם "and take". **"Now the time was the time of the firstripe grapes" → "And the days were the days of the firstfruits of grapes"** — Hebrew וְהַיָּמִים יְמֵי בִּכּוּרֵי עֲנָבִים.
- **"unto Rehob, as men come to Hamath" → "as far as Rehob, toward Lebo-hamath"** (v21) — Hebrew לְבֹא חֲמָת, literally "to the coming in of Hamath": either a place name, Lebo of Hamath, or "at the entrance to Hamath".
- **"they ascended by the south, and came unto Hebron" → "they went up in the Negev, and he came as far as Hebron"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיָּבֹא is singular, "and he came", after the plural "they went up"; who is meant is not said (Josh 14:9–14 speaks of Caleb and Hebron). Some manuscripts and versions have the plural (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"the children of Anak" → "those born of the Anak"** (v22, 28) — Hebrew יְלִידֵי הָעֲנָק, "the born ones of the Anak", with the article (in v33 בְּנֵי עֲנָק "sons of Anak" without it). עֲנָק is also a word for "neck, necklace"; the name may mean "the long-necked".
- **"(Now Hebron was built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.)" → "And Hebron was built seven years before Zoan of Egypt"** (v22) — Hebrew צֹעַן מִצְרָיִם; the brackets are not in the Hebrew.
- **"the brook of Eshcol" → "the wadi of Eshcol"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed. אֶשְׁכֹּל *eshkol* is the word "cluster": the name and the cluster are one word in v24.
- **"cut down from thence a branch with one cluster of grapes, and they bare it between two upon a staff; and they brought of the pomegranates, and of the figs" → "cut from there a vine shoot and one cluster of grapes, and they carried it on the pole by two, and some of the pomegranates and some of the figs"** (v23) — Hebrew זְמוֹרָה, a shoot of the vine; "and … one cluster"; בַּמּוֹט "on the pole", with the article; בִּשְׁנָיִם "by two". The last words have no verb of their own in the Hebrew ("they brought" is not there).
- **"The place was called" → "That place he called"** (v24) — Hebrew קָרָא, "he called" (one called); the namer is not said. **"because of" → "on account of"** — Hebrew עַל אֹדוֹת.
- **"after forty days" → "at the end of forty days"** (v25) — Hebrew מִקֵּץ.
- **"unto the wilderness of Paran, to Kadesh" kept** (v26) — Hebrew קָדֵשָׁה "to Kadesh".
- **"they told him … surely it floweth with milk and honey" → "they recounted to him … it is also flowing with milk and honey"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיְסַפְּרוּ; וְגַם "and also".
- **"Nevertheless the people be strong … and the cities are walled, and very great" → "Only that the people that dwells in the land is strong, and the cities are fortified, very great"** (v28) — Hebrew אֶפֶס כִּי "except that". עַז "strong" here is another word than חָזָק "strong" in v18, 31 (English has one word for both). בְּצֻרוֹת "fortified", literally "cut off, made inaccessible".
- **"The Amalekites dwell … the Hittites, and the Jebusites, and the Amorites, dwell in the mountains: and the Canaanites dwell by the sea, and by the coast of Jordan" → "Amalek dwells … the Hittite and the Jebusite and the Amorite dwells in the hill country, and the Canaanite dwells by the sea and at the side of the Jordan"** (v29) — the Hebrew has the name Amalek and singulars with the article for the peoples, with a singular verb. עַל יַד is literally "at the hand of".
- **"Caleb stilled the people before Moses" → "Caleb hushed the people toward Moses"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיַּהַס, from the call הַס "hush!"; אֶל־מֹשֶׁה "toward Moses".
- **"Let us go up at once, and possess it; for we are well able to overcome it" → "Let us surely go up, and we shall possess it, for we shall surely overcome it"** (v30) — Hebrew עָלֹה נַעֲלֶה … יָכוֹל נוּכַל, two verbs each with its infinitive absolute, literally "going up let us go up … being able we shall be able for it". יָרַשׁ is "possess, take possession of".
- **"against the people; for they are stronger than we" → "to the people, for it is stronger than we"** (v31) — Hebrew אֶל־הָעָם; מִמֶּנּוּ "than we" (the same form is also "than he").
- **"they brought up an evil report of the land" → "they brought out a report of the land"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיּוֹצִיאוּ דִּבַּת הָאָרֶץ. דִּבָּה is a report that defames (Gen 37:2); "evil" is not in this verse (it is in 14:37).
- **"The land, through which we have gone to search it, is a land that eateth up the inhabitants thereof" → "The land that we passed through to explore it, a land that eats its inhabitants is it"** (v32) — the Hebrew order; אֹכֶלֶת "eating".
- **"men of a great stature" → "men of stature"** (v32) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי מִדּוֹת, literally "men of measures"; "great" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants" → "the Nephilim, the sons of Anak, from the Nephilim"** (v33) — Hebrew הַנְּפִילִים … מִן־הַנְּפִלִים, the word of Gen 6:4, kept as a name: its meaning is uncertain (perhaps "fallen ones", from נָפַל "fall"). "Giants" is the Septuagint's rendering (from memory). "Who come" is not in the Hebrew: "the sons of Anak, from the Nephilim".
- **"we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight" → "we were in our eyes like grasshoppers, and so we were in their eyes"** (v33) — Hebrew בְעֵינֵינוּ … בְּעֵינֵיהֶם; חָגָב, a kind of locust (Lev 11:22).

### Notes
- **Twelve men** — one per tribe (Levi excluded). Notable figures: Caleb (Judah), Hoshea/Joshua (Ephraim).
- **Forty days** (v25) — compare Moses' forty days on Sinai (Ex 24:18, 34:28) and the forty years of 14:34.
- **Milk and honey confirmed** (v27) — the land is what Yahweh promised. The fruit is real.
- **Anak / Nephilim** — linked back to Gen 6:4. Josh 11:21–22 tells of Joshua later cutting off the Anakim; Josh 15:14 of Caleb and the three of v22.
- **"Grasshoppers"** (v33) — the men speak of how they looked in their own eyes and in the eyes of the others.
- **Two reports, one land** — Caleb (and Joshua, ch 14) say "we shall surely overcome it"; the others, "we are not able". This chapter is the hinge of the wilderness generation's fate.
