# Numbers 22

[1] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ set out and camped in the plains of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧.

[2] And ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ son of ⟦zippor|Tzippor|Zippor⟧ saw all that ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had done to the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧.

[3] And ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ was greatly afraid because of the people, for it was many; and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ felt loathing because of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] And ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ said to the elders of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, "Now the assembly will lick up all that is around us, as the ox licks up the green of the field." And ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ son of ⟦zippor|Tzippor|Zippor⟧ was king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ at that time.

[5] And he sent messengers to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ son of Beor, to ⟦pethor|Petor|Pethor⟧, which is by the River, the land of the sons of his people, to call him, saying, "Behold, a people has come out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; behold, it has covered the eye of the land, and it is dwelling opposite me.

[6] And now, come, please, curse this people for me, for it is mightier than I; perhaps I shall be able — we shall strike it — and I shall drive it out from the land; for I know that he whom you bless is blessed, and he whom you curse will be cursed."

[7] And the elders of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and the elders of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ went, with divinations in their hand; and they came to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ and spoke to him the words of ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧.

[8] And he said to them, "Lodge here tonight, and I will return you word, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ speaks to me." And the princes of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ remained with ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧.

[9] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ and said, "Who are these men with you?"

[10] And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ said to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ son of ⟦zippor|Tzippor|Zippor⟧, king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, has sent to me:

[11] 'Behold, the people that comes out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and it has covered the eye of the land. Now come, lay a curse on it for me; perhaps I shall be able to fight against it, and I shall drive it out.'"

[12] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, "You shall not go with them; you shall not curse the people, for it is blessed."

[13] And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ rose in the morning and said to the princes of ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧, "Go to your land, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has refused to allow me to go with you."

[14] And the princes of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ rose and came to ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ and said, "⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ has refused to go with us."

[15] And ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ again sent princes, more numerous and more honored than these.

[16] And they came to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ and said to him, "Thus says ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ son of ⟦zippor|Tzippor|Zippor⟧: 'Please do not be held back from coming to me;

[17] for I will surely honor you greatly, and all that you say to me I will do; and come, please, lay a curse on this people for me.'"

[18] And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ answered and said to the servants of ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧, "If ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ should give me his house full of silver and gold, I could not pass over the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to do a small thing or a great.

[19] And now, remain here, please, you also, tonight, that I may know what more ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will speak with me."

[20] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ by night and said to him, "If it is to call you that the men have come, rise, go with them; but only the word that I speak to you, that you shall do."

[21] And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ rose in the morning and saddled his she-donkey and went with the princes of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

[22] And the anger of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ burned because he was going; and the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ took his stand in the way as an adversary to him. And he was riding on his she-donkey, and his two young men were with him.

[23] And the she-donkey saw the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ standing in the way, and his sword drawn in his hand; and the she-donkey turned from the way and went into the field; and ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ struck the she-donkey, to turn her to the way.

[24] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ stood in a hollow way of the vineyards, a fence on this side and a fence on that side.

[25] And the she-donkey saw the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and she pressed herself to the wall and pressed the foot of ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ to the wall; and he struck her again.

[26] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ again passed on and stood in a narrow place, where there was no way to turn to the right or to the left.

[27] And the she-donkey saw the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and she lay down under ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧; and the anger of ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ burned, and he struck the she-donkey with the rod.

[28] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ opened the mouth of the she-donkey, and she said to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, "What have I done to you, that you have struck me these three times?"

[29] And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ said to the she-donkey, "Because you have dealt ruthlessly with me. Would that there were a sword in my hand, for now I would have killed you."

[30] And the she-donkey said to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, "Am I not your she-donkey, on whom you have ridden ever since you were until this day? Have I indeed been accustomed to do thus to you?" And he said, "No."

[31] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ uncovered the eyes of ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, and he saw the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ standing in the way, and his sword drawn in his hand; and he bowed his head and bowed down on his face.

[32] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Why have you struck your she-donkey these three times? Behold, I myself have come out as an adversary, for the way is headlong before me.

[33] And the she-donkey saw me and turned before me these three times. Perhaps she turned from my face; for now I would have killed even you, and her I would have kept alive."

[34] And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ said to the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "I have sinned, for I did not know that you were standing to meet me in the way. And now, if it is evil in your eyes, let me get myself back."

[35] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, "Go with the men; but nothing except the word that I speak to you, that you shall speak." And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ went with the princes of ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧.

[36] And ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ heard that ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ had come, and he went out to meet him to ⟦ir-moab|Ir Moav|the city of Moab⟧, which is on the border of the Arnon, which is at the end of the border.

[37] And ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ said to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, "Did I not surely send to you to call you? Why did you not come to me? Am I truly not able to honor you?"

[38] And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ said to ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧, "Behold, I have come to you. Now, am I indeed able to speak anything? The word that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ puts in my mouth, that I shall speak."

[39] And ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ went with ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧, and they came to ⟦kiriath-huzoth|Qiryat Chutzot|Kiriath-huzoth⟧.

[40] And ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ sacrificed herd and flock, and sent to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ and to the princes who were with him.

[41] And it came to pass in the morning that ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ took ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ and brought him up to ⟦bamoth-baal|Bamot Baal|Bamoth-baal⟧, and from there he saw the end of the people.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9, 10, 12, 18, 20, 22, 38) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים; in v10 הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article. The narrative has "Elohim" in v9–12, 20, 22, 38 and "Yahweh" in v8, 13, 18, 19, 28, 31 and "the angel of Yahweh"; each stands as the Hebrew has it.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"set forward, and pitched in the plains of Moab on this side Jordan by Jericho" → "set out and camped in the plains of Moab, across the Jordan of Jericho"** (v1) — Hebrew עַרְבוֹת מוֹאָב, the dry lowland of Moab by the Jordan (עֲרָבָה is dry steppe; בִּקְעָה "plain" is another word). מֵעֵבֶר לְיַרְדֵּן יְרֵחוֹ is literally "on the other side of the Jordan of Jericho": the stretch of the Jordan at Jericho, a genitive, not "at" or "near"; the same words are used for either bank, as the speaker stands.
- **"Amorites" → "the Amorite"** (v2) — Hebrew לָאֱמֹרִי, a singular for the people.
- **"Moab was sore afraid of the people … distressed because of the children of Israel" → "Moab was greatly afraid because of the people … felt loathing because of the sons of Israel"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיָּגָר … מִפְּנֵי "was afraid from the face of" (גּוּר, another verb than יָרֵא "fear"); וַיָּקָץ … מִפְּנֵי "felt a sick dread because of", the verb of Exod 1:12. "For it was many": the people is spoken of in the singular through v3–12.
- **"this company" → "the assembly"** (v4) — Hebrew הַקָּהָל. **"the grass of the field" → "the green of the field"** — Hebrew יֶרֶק.
- **"by the river of the land of the children of his people" → "by the River, the land of the sons of his people"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עַל־הַנָּהָר אֶרֶץ בְּנֵי־עַמּוֹ: "the River" is the Euphrates. "The land of the sons of his people" is the Masoretic Text; the Samaritan text and some versions read "the sons of Ammon" (cited from memory), and others take עַמּוֹ as a name ("Amaw"). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"they cover the face of the earth, and they abide over against me" → "it has covered the eye of the land, and it is dwelling opposite me"** (v5, 11) — Hebrew כִסָּה אֶת־עֵין הָאָרֶץ, literally "the eye of the land": its visible surface (Exod 10:5, 15). יֹשֵׁב "dwelling".
- **"curse"** (v6, 12) and **"lay a curse on"** (v11, 17) — two Hebrew verbs: אָרַר (v6, 12; 23:7; 24:9) is "curse"; קָבַב (v11, 17; 23:8–27; 24:10) is another verb of the same sense, rendered "lay a curse on" so that the two can be told apart. Balaam, retelling Balak's message (v11), does not use Balak's word (v6).
- **"they are too mighty for me" → "it is mightier than I"** (v6) — Hebrew עָצוּם הוּא מִמֶּנִּי. **"peradventure I shall prevail, that we may smite them" → "perhaps I shall be able — we shall strike it"** — Hebrew אוּלַי אוּכַל נַכֶּה־בּוֹ, word for word "perhaps I shall be able, we shall strike it": the verb changes from "I" to "we" without a link. **"is cursed" → "will be cursed"** — Hebrew יוּאָר, an imperfect, beside the participle מְבֹרָךְ "is blessed".
- **"with the rewards of divination in their hand" → "with divinations in their hand"** (v7) — Hebrew וּקְסָמִים בְּיָדָם, literally "and divinations in their hand". Whether the fee for divination or the means of divination is meant, the word does not say.
- **"I will bring you word again" → "I will return you word"** (v8) — Hebrew וַהֲשִׁבֹתִי אֶתְכֶם דָּבָר. **"abode" → "remained"** (v8), **"tarry" → "remain"** (v19) — Hebrew יָשַׁב "dwell, sit"; "dwelt" would say more than a night's stay.
- **"princes"** (v8–40) — Hebrew שָׂרִים; in v7 the same men are "elders" (זְקֵנִים) and in v18 "servants" (עֲבָדִים) of Balak.
- **"What men are these with thee?" → "Who are these men with you?"** (v9).
- **"hath sent unto me, saying" → "has sent to me:"** (v10) — the Hebrew has no "saying".
- **"there is a people come out of Egypt, which covereth" → "the people that comes out from Egypt, and it has covered"** (v11) — Hebrew הָעָם הַיֹּצֵא, a participle; וַיְכַס "and it covered". **"overcome them" → "fight against it"** — Hebrew לְהִלָּחֶם בּוֹ.
- **"they are blessed" → "it is blessed"** (v12) — Hebrew בָרוּךְ הוּא, singular.
- **"the LORD refuseth to give me leave" → "Yahweh has refused to allow me"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵאֵן … לְתִתִּי, literally "has refused to give me to go".
- **"sent yet again princes, more, and more honourable than they" → "again sent princes, more numerous and more honored than these"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף עוֹד … שְׁלֹחַ, literally "added yet to send"; רַבִּים "many"; נִכְבָּדִים "honored, weighty", of the root of "honor" in v17, 37.
- **"Let nothing, I pray thee, hinder thee from coming" → "Please do not be held back from coming"** (v16) — Hebrew אַל־נָא תִמָּנַע מֵהֲלֹךְ, a passive: "do not be held back from going"; the verb of 24:11.
- **"I will promote thee unto very great honour" → "I will surely honor you greatly"** (v17) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb: כַבֵּד אֲכַבֶּדְךָ, literally "honoring I will honor you".
- **"I cannot go beyond the word of the LORD my God, to do less or more" → "I could not pass over the mouth of Yahweh my *Elohim*, to do a small thing or a great"** (v18) — Hebrew לַעֲבֹר אֶת־פִּי יְהוָה: "the mouth of Yahweh" is what he says, his command; "pass over" it is to transgress it. קְטַנָּה אוֹ גְדוֹלָה "small or great". "His house full" is literally "the fullness of his house".
- **"what the LORD will say unto me more" → "what more Yahweh will speak with me"** (v19) — Hebrew מַה־יֹּסֵף יְהוָה דַּבֵּר עִמִּי, literally "what Yahweh will add to speak with me".
- **"If the men come to call thee" → "If it is to call you that the men have come"** (v20) — Hebrew אִם־לִקְרֹא לְךָ בָּאוּ הָאֲנָשִׁים: "to call you" stands first. **"but yet" → "but only"** — Hebrew וְאַךְ; in v35 another word, וְאֶפֶס "but nothing except".
- **"ass" → "she-donkey"** (v21–33) — Hebrew אָתוֹן, the female animal.
- **"God's anger was kindled because he went" → "the anger of *Elohim* burned because he was going"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף "the nose burned" (also v27); כִּי־הוֹלֵךְ הוּא, a participle: "because he was going", or "as he was going". The verse does not say why the going that v20 allowed is met with anger.
- **"the angel of the LORD" kept** (v22–35) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה; the word is "messenger", the word used of Balak's messengers in v5. One rendering through the Old Testament (Gen 16:7; Exod 3:2).
- **"stood in the way for an adversary against him" → "took his stand in the way as an adversary to him"** (v22, 32) — Hebrew לְשָׂטָן לוֹ: שָׂטָן *satan* is here a common noun, "adversary, opposer"; the angel of Yahweh is the one called so. **"his two servants" → "his two young men"** — Hebrew נְעָרָיו.
- **"turned aside out of the way … to turn her into the way" → "turned from the way … to turn her to the way"** (v23) — Hebrew נָטָה, the verb of v26, 33.
- **"in a path of the vineyards, a wall being on this side" → "in a hollow way of the vineyards, a fence on this side"** (v24) — Hebrew מִשְׁעוֹל is found only here: a narrow or hollow way; uncertain. גָּדֵר "fence" (a field wall of stones) is another word than קִיר "wall" (v25).
- **"thrust herself unto the wall, and crushed Balaam's foot" → "pressed herself to the wall and pressed the foot of Balaam"** (v25) — Hebrew וַתִּלָּחֵץ … וַתִּלְחַץ, one verb twice. **"he smote her again"** — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף לְהַכֹּתָהּ, literally "he added to strike her"; so "again passed on" (v26).
- **"she fell down under Balaam" → "she lay down under Balaam"** (v27) — Hebrew וַתִּרְבַּץ, of an animal lying down. **"with a staff" → "with the rod"** — Hebrew בַּמַּקֵּל.
- **"these three times"** (v28, 32, 33) — Hebrew שָׁלֹשׁ רְגָלִים, literally "three feet"; in 24:10 the usual word פְּעָמִים.
- **"Because thou hast mocked me" → "Because you have dealt ruthlessly with me"** (v29) — Hebrew הִתְעַלַּלְתְּ בִּי, to deal wantonly with, make sport of (Exod 10:2). **"I would there were a sword … for now would I kill thee" → "Would that there were a sword … for now I would have killed you"** — Hebrew לוּ יֶשׁ … כִּי עַתָּה הֲרַגְתִּיךְ.
- **"ever since I was thine unto this day" → "ever since you were until this day"** (v30) — Hebrew מֵעוֹדְךָ, literally "from your still-being": all your life. **"was I ever wont to do so" → "Have I indeed been accustomed to do thus"** — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb, in a question: הַהַסְכֵּן הִסְכַּנְתִּי.
- **"opened the eyes" → "uncovered the eyes"** (v31) — Hebrew וַיְגַל, another verb than "opened" (וַיִּפְתַּח) in v28; the word of 24:4, 16. **"bowed down his head, and fell flat on his face" → "bowed his head and bowed down on his face"** — Hebrew וַיִּקֹּד וַיִּשְׁתַּחוּ לְאַפָּיו.
- **"I went out to withstand thee, because thy way is perverse before me" → "I myself have come out as an adversary, for the way is headlong before me"** (v32) — Hebrew לְשָׂטָן as v22. כִּי־יָרַט הַדֶּרֶךְ לְנֶגְדִּי: the verb יָרַט is found only here and in Job 16:11; its meaning is uncertain ("be headlong, reckless"; KJV "perverse"). The Hebrew has "the way", not "your way".
- **"unless she had turned from me, surely now also I had slain thee" → "Perhaps she turned from my face; for now I would have killed even you"** (v33) — Hebrew אוּלַי נָטְתָה מִפָּנַי כִּי עַתָּה …: the Masoretic Text has אוּלַי "perhaps", translated as it stands. The sense required by what follows is "if she had not turned" (so KJV, with the Septuagint and other versions, cited from memory; many read לוּלֵי "unless"). **"saved her alive" → "kept alive"** — Hebrew הֶחֱיֵיתִי.
- **"thou stoodest in the way against me" → "you were standing to meet me in the way"** (v34) — Hebrew נִצָּב לִקְרָאתִי. **"if it displease thee, I will get me back again" → "if it is evil in your eyes, let me get myself back"** — Hebrew רַע בְּעֵינֶיךָ; אָשׁוּבָה לִּי, "let me return" with the reflexive לִי.
- **"unto a city of Moab … in the utmost coast" → "to Ir Moav … at the end of the border"** (v36) — Hebrew עִיר מוֹאָב, "the city of Moab" or a name, *Ir-Moab* (Ar of Moab, 21:28?); בִּקְצֵה הַגְּבוּל.
- **"Did I not earnestly send" → "Did I not surely send"** (v37) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb: שָׁלֹחַ שָׁלַחְתִּי. **"am I not able indeed to promote thee to honour?" → "Am I truly not able to honor you?"** — Hebrew הַאֻמְנָם לֹא אוּכַל כַּבְּדֶךָ.
- **"have I now any power at all to say any thing?" → "Now, am I indeed able to speak anything?"** (v38) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb, in a question: הֲיָכוֹל אוּכַל. **"that shall I speak"** — a plain imperfect: "shall / must / will".
- **"offered oxen and sheep" → "sacrificed herd and flock"** (v40) — Hebrew וַיִּזְבַּח … בָּקָר וָצֹאן, the two collective words for cattle and for sheep and goats. **"sent to Balaam"** — no object is named; what he sent (portions of the sacrifice) is not said.
- **"it came to pass on the morrow … brought him up into the high places of Baal, that thence he might see the utmost part of the people" → "it came to pass in the morning … brought him up to Bamot Baal, and from there he saw the end of the people"** (v41) — Hebrew בָּמוֹת בָּעַל *Bamot Baal*, "the high places of Baal", a place name (Josh 13:17). וַיַּרְא "and he saw", a statement, not a purpose. קְצֵה הָעָם "the end of the people": its edge; whether only the nearest part or the people to its farthest end is meant is not said (23:13).

### Notes
- **Balaam introduced** — a non-Israelite from the Euphrates region, to whom Yahweh speaks. The chapter does not call him a prophet; Josh 13:22 calls him "the diviner".
- **Speaking she-donkey** (v28–30) — the animal sees the angel whom the seer does not see until Yahweh uncovers his eyes (v31).
- **"The angel of Yahweh"** (v22) — the figure speaks as Yahweh's messenger and with Yahweh's authority (v35 "the word that I speak to you"; cf. v20). How he is related to Yahweh the text does not explain; Jewish and Christian readings differ.
- **Threes** — the she-donkey turns three times and is struck three times (v23, 25, 27); Balaam will be brought to three places (22:41; 23:14, 28).
- **2 Pet 2:15–16; Jude 11; Rev 2:14** — the NT uses Balaam as the figure of one who loved the wages of unrighteousness. Num 31:8, 16 and Deut 23:4–5 are the Old Testament basis.
