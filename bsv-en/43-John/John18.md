# John 18

[1] Having said these things, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ went out with his disciples across the torrent of the ⟦kidron|Qidron|Kidron⟧, where there was a garden, into which he entered, he and his disciples.

[2] Now ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ also, the one delivering him up, knew the place, because ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was often gathered together there with his disciples.

[3] So ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, having taken the cohort, and officers from the high priests and from the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, comes there with lanterns and torches and weapons.

[4] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing all the things coming upon him, went out and says to them, "Whom do you seek?"

[5] They answered him, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧." He says to them, "**I am**." Now ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ also, the one delivering him up, was standing with them.

[6] So when he said to them, "I am," they went away backward and fell to the ground.

[7] So he asked them again, "Whom do you seek?" And they said, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧."

[8] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "I told you that I am. If therefore you seek me, let these go away,"

[9] that the word might be fulfilled which he said: "Those whom you have given me, I lost not one of them."

[10] So ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, having a sword, drew it and struck the slave of the high priest and cut off his right ear. Now the name of the slave was ⟦malchus|Malchos|Malchus⟧.

[11] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "Put the sword into the sheath; the cup which the Father has given me, shall I not drink it?"

[12] So the cohort and the chiliarch and the officers of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ apprehended ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and bound him

[13] and led him to ⟦annas|Chanan|Annas⟧ first; for he was father-in-law of ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧, who was high priest of that year.

[14] Now ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧ was the one who had counseled the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ that it is profitable for one man to die for the people.

[15] Now ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was following ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and another disciple. And that disciple was known to the high priest, and he entered together with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ into the court of the high priest,

[16] but ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was standing at the door outside. So the other disciple, the one known to the high priest, went out and spoke to the doorkeeper and brought ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ in.

[17] So the slave woman, the doorkeeper, says to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "You also are not of the disciples of this man, are you?" That one says, "I am not."

[18] Now the slaves and the officers were standing there, having made a charcoal fire, because it was cold, and they were warming themselves; and ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ also was with them, standing and warming himself.

[19] So the high priest asked ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ about his disciples and about his teaching.

[20] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered him, "I have spoken openly to the world; I always taught in synagogue and in the temple, where all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ come together, and in secret I spoke nothing.

[21] Why do you ask me? Ask those who have heard what I spoke to them; see, these know the things which I said."

[22] And when he had said these things, one of the officers standing by gave ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ a slap, saying, "Is that how you answer the high priest?"

[23] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered him, "If I spoke evilly, testify about the evil; but if well, why do you beat me?"

[24] So ⟦annas|Chanan|Annas⟧ sent him bound to ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧ the high priest.

[25] Now ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was standing and warming himself. So they said to him, "You also are not of his disciples, are you?" That one denied and said, "I am not."

[26] One of the slaves of the high priest, being a kinsman of him whose ear ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ cut off, says, "Did I not see you in the garden with him?"

[27] So ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ denied again; and immediately a rooster crowed.

[28] So they lead ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ from ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧ into the praetorium; and it was early; and they themselves did not enter into the praetorium, that they might not be defiled, but might eat the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[29] So ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ went out to them outside and says, "What accusation do you bring against this man?"

[30] They answered and said to him, "If this one were not doing evil, we would not have delivered him up to you."

[31] So ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to them, "Take him yourselves, and judge him according to your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧." The ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ said to him, "It is not permitted to us to kill anyone,"

[32] that the word of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ might be fulfilled which he said, signifying by what kind of death he was about to die.

[33] So ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ entered again into the praetorium and called ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and said to him, "Are you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?"

[34] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "Do you say this from yourself, or did others tell you about me?"

[35] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ answered, "Am I a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧? Your own nation and the high priests delivered you up to me. What did you do?"

[36] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "**My kingdom is not of this world. If my kingdom were of this world, my officers would be struggling, that I might not be delivered up to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧. But now my kingdom is not from here.**"

[37] So ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to him, "So then, you are a king?" ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "You say that I am a king. For this I have been born, and for this I have come into the world: that I might testify to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth hears my voice."

[38] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ says to him, "What is truth?"

And having said this, he went out again to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and says to them, "I find in him no cause.

[39] But you have a custom that I should release one to you at the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧. Do you wish, then, that I release to you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?"

[40] So they shouted again, saying, "Not this one, but ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧!" Now ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧ was a robber.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** — no direct occurrence; "Father" used in *Yeshua's* speech.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v10, 11, 15–27) — Aramaic name used consistently.
- **"Caiaphas"** (v13, 24, 28) — retained as familiar form; Aramaic קַיָּפָא *Qayyapha*.
- **"the brook Cedron" → "the torrent of the Qidron"** (v1) — Greek χείμαρρος, a stream that flows in winter. The verse follows τοῦ Κεδρών, "of the Qidron" (Tregelles, NA28, SBLGNT); Westcott-Hort and the Byzantine text read τῶν Κέδρων, "of the cedars".
- **"which betrayed him" → "the one delivering him up"** (v2, 5) — Greek ὁ παραδιδοὺς αὐτόν, a present participle of παραδίδωμι "deliver up", the same verb as in v30, 35, 36; "betray" is a different word.
- **"resorted" → "was gathered together"** (v2) — Greek συνήχθη, a passive.
- **"a band of men and officers" → "the cohort, and officers"** (v3, 12) — Greek σπεῖρα, the Roman cohort (a tenth of a legion; the word also stands for a smaller detachment); ὑπηρέται "officers" are the men under the orders of the high priests, the same word as "my officers" in v36 (1 Cor 4:1 "assistants").
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v3, 35) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of the word for "high priest" (v10, 13, 19).
- **"cometh" → "comes"** (v3; also "says", v4, 5, 17, 26, 29, 38; "lead", v28) — the Greek present tense in narration is kept.
- **"all things that should come upon him" → "all the things coming upon him"** (v4) — Greek πάντα τὰ ἐρχόμενα ἐπ' αὐτόν.
- **"went forth, and said" → "went out and says"** (v4) — ἐξῆλθεν καὶ λέγει (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the Byzantine text has ἐξελθὼν εἶπεν.
- **"Jesus of Nazareth" → "Yeshua the Nazorean"** (v5, 7) — Greek Ἰησοῦν τὸν Ναζωραῖον (as Matt 2:23; 26:71); the place name is not in the verse.
- **"Jesus saith unto them, I am he" → "He says to them, 'I am'"** (v5, 6, 8) — Greek ἐγώ εἰμι *egō eimi*, "I am", with no "he" in the Greek. It answers "Whom do you seek?" and so can mean "I am he"; it is also the absolute "I am" of 8:24, 28, 58 (cf. Ex 3:14; Isa 43:10), and those who hear it fall to the ground (v6). The name "Jesus" in v5 (Byzantine text) is not in the critical text.
- **"went backward" → "went away backward"** (v6) — Greek ἀπῆλθον εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω.
- **"let these go their way" → "let these go away,"** (v8–9) — the Greek sentence runs on into v9 (ἵνα "that"); the same in v31–32.
- **"Of them which thou gavest me have I lost none" → "Those whom you have given me, I lost not one of them"** (v9) — Greek Οὓς δέδωκάς μοι οὐκ ἀπώλεσα ἐξ αὐτῶν οὐδένα, with the perfect "have given" and the aorist "lost"; the word order of the Greek is kept (cf. 17:12).
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v10, 18, 26) — Greek δοῦλος *doulos*. The brackets around the last sentence of v10 are dropped: the Greek has none.
- **"ear"** (v10, 26) — v10 has ὠτάριον, v26 ὠτίον, both diminutive forms of οὖς "ear" (the Byzantine text has ὠτίον in both).
- **"Put up thy sword into the sheath" → "Put the sword into the sheath"** (v11) — "your" (Byzantine σου) is not in the critical text. The word order of the second clause is the Greek's; οὐ μὴ πίω is the strongest form of the question.
- **"the captain" → "the chiliarch"** (v12) — Greek χιλίαρχος, "commander of a thousand", the Greek name for the Roman military tribune who commanded a cohort.
- **"took Jesus" → "apprehended"** (v12) — Greek συνέλαβον.
- **"that same year" → "of that year"** (v13) — Greek τοῦ ἐνιαυτοῦ ἐκείνου, a genitive.
- **"gave counsel … that it was expedient" → "had counseled … that it is profitable"** (v14) — Greek συμφέρει, present tense: the words of 11:50 are quoted.
- **"palace" → "court"** (v15) — Greek αὐλή, an open court; the word for the "fold" of the sheep in 10:1, 16.
- **"that kept the door" → "the doorkeeper"; "the damsel" → "the slave woman"** (v16, 17) — Greek θυρωρός (here feminine) and παιδίσκη, a female slave (as Gal 4:22).
- **"Art not thou also one of this man's disciples?" → "You also are not of the disciples of this man, are you?"** (v17, 25) — the Greek question with μή expects the answer "no"; "one" is not in the Greek (ἐκ τῶν μαθητῶν).
- **"He saith" → "That one says"** (v17, 25) — Greek ἐκεῖνος.
- **"a fire of coals" → "a charcoal fire"** (v18) — Greek ἀνθρακιά, as in 21:9.
- **"doctrine" → "teaching"** (v19) — Greek διδαχή.
- **"I spake openly" → "I have spoken openly"** (v20) — Greek παρρησίᾳ, the dative of the noun "boldness": here "in public", against "in secret". λελάληκα is a perfect (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28; Byzantine ἐλάλησα). "in synagogue" has no article in the Greek. "where all the Jews come together": πάντες (critical text); the Byzantine text has πάντοτε, "where the Jews always come together".
- **"temple"** (v20) — Greek ἱερόν, the whole temple precinct.
- **"behold, they know what I said" → "see, these know the things which I said"** (v21) — Greek ἴδε οὗτοι οἴδασιν ἃ εἶπον ἐγώ, with an emphatic "I"; "spoke" (λαλέω) and "said" (λέγω) are kept apart in v20–21.
- **"struck Jesus with the palm of his hand" → "gave Yeshua a slap"** (v22) — Greek ἔδωκεν ῥάπισμα; ῥάπισμα is a blow with the open hand (or with a rod).
- **"If I have spoken evil, bear witness of the evil … why smitest thou me" → "If I spoke evilly, testify about the evil; but if well, why do you beat me"** (v23) — Greek κακῶς … περὶ τοῦ κακοῦ … καλῶς; κακός is "bad, evil" (as in v30 κακὸν ποιῶν "doing evil"; Byzantine κακοποιός "an evildoer"). δέρω is "beat".
- **"Now Annas had sent him" → "So Annas sent him"** (v24) — Greek ἀπέστειλεν οὖν, a plain past with "so" (the Byzantine text has no οὖν); the pluperfect "had sent" is an attempt to harmonise.
- **"He denied it" → "That one denied"** (v25, 27) — the Greek has no object.
- **"the cock crew" → "a rooster crowed"** (v27) — Greek ἀλέκτωρ ἐφώνησεν, without article; φωνέω is the verb "call" of v33.
- **"hall of judgment" → "praetorium"** (v28, 33) — Greek πραιτώριον, the Latin word *praetorium*, the governor's official residence; the Greek keeps the Latin word.
- **"lest they should be defiled; but that they might eat the passover"** (v28) — "the Pesach" is here the Passover meal (or the sacrifices of the feast week).
- **"malefactor" → "doing evil"** (v30) — see v23.
- **"delivered him up"** (v30, 35, 36) — Greek παραδίδωμι, the verb used of Yehudah in v2, 5.
- **"It is not lawful for us to put any man to death" → "It is not permitted to us to kill anyone"** (v31) — Greek οὐκ ἔξεστιν … ἀποκτεῖναι.
- **"Sayest thou this thing of thyself" → "from yourself"** (v34) — Greek ἀπὸ σεαυτοῦ.
- **"my servants would fight" → "my officers would be struggling"** (v36) — Greek οἱ ὑπηρέται οἱ ἐμοὶ ἠγωνίζοντο ἄν: the word of v3, 12, 18, 22, and the imperfect of ἀγωνίζομαι "struggle". "of this world" is ἐκ "out of, from"; "not from here" is ἐντεῦθεν.
- **"Art thou a king then?" → "So then, you are a king?"** (v37) — Greek Οὐκοῦν βασιλεὺς εἶ σύ;
- **"To this end was I born" → "For this I have been born"** (v37) — Greek γεγέννημαι, the perfect passive of γεννάω ("beget"; in the passive also "be born").
- **"bear witness unto the truth" → "testify to the truth"** (v37) — Greek μαρτυρήσω.
- **"I find in him no fault" → "I find in him no cause"** (v38) — Greek αἰτία, a cause, a ground for a charge; also 19:4, 6.
- **"Will ye therefore" → "Do you wish, then"** (v39) — Greek βούλεσθε.
- **"Then cried they all again" → "So they shouted again"** (v40) — Greek ἐκραύγασαν; "all" (Tregelles, Byzantine πάντες) is not in Westcott-Hort, NA28 and SBLGNT.
- **"robber"** (v40) — Greek λῃστής, a robber or bandit; the word is also used of insurrectionists.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** (thee, thou, art, etc.) modernized throughout.
