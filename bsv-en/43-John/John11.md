# John 11

[1] Now a certain man was sick, ⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧ from ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧, of the village of ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ and ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ her sister.

[2] Now it was ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ who anointed the Lord with ointment and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother ⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧ was sick.

[3] So the sisters sent to him, saying, "Lord, see, the one for whom you have affection is sick."

[4] But having heard, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "This sickness is not unto death, but for the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may be glorified through it."

[5] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ loved ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ and her sister and ⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧.

[6] So when he heard that he was sick, then indeed he remained two days in the place where he was;

[7] then after this he says to the disciples, "Let us go into ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ again."

[8] The disciples say to him, "Rabbi, just now the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were seeking to stone you, and you go there again?"

[9] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "Are there not twelve hours of the day? If anyone walks in the day, he does not stumble, because he sees the light of this world;

[10] but if anyone walks in the night, he stumbles, because the light is not in him."

[11] These things he said, and after this he says to them, "⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧ our friend has fallen asleep, but I go that I may wake him out of sleep."

[12] So the disciples said to him, "Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will be saved."

[13] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had spoken concerning his death, but those supposed that he speaks concerning the repose of sleep.

[14] So then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them plainly, "⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧ died,

[15] and I rejoice on your account — that you may believe — that I was not there; but let us go to him."

[16] So ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧, the one called the Twin, said to the fellow disciples, "Let us also go, that we may die with him."

[17] So having come, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ found him already four days in the tomb.

[18] Now ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧ was near ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, about fifteen stadia off,

[19] and many of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ had come to ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ and ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, that they might comfort them concerning the brother.

[20] So ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧, when she heard that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is coming, met him; but ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ was sitting in the house.

[21] So ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ said to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, "Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died;

[22] even now I know that whatever you ask of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will give you."

[23] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to her, "Your brother will rise."

[24] ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ says to him, "I know that he will rise in the resurrection in the last day."

[25] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to her, "**I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me, even if he dies, will live,**

**[26] and everyone who lives and believes in me will by no means die forever.** Do you believe this?"

[27] She says to him, "Yes, Lord; I have believed that you are the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the one coming into the world."

[28] And having said this, she went away and called ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ her sister, secretly saying, "The Teacher is present and calls you."

[29] And that one, when she heard, arose quickly and was coming to him;

[30] now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had not yet come into the village, but was still in the place where ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧ met him.

[31] So the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were with her in the house and comforting her, seeing ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, that she rose quickly and went out, followed her, supposing that she goes to the tomb that she may weep there.

[32] So ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, when she came where ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was, seeing him, fell at his feet, saying to him, "Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died."

[33] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, when he saw her weeping and the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who had come with her weeping, was moved with indignation in the spirit and troubled himself,

[34] and said, "Where have you laid him?" They say to him, "Lord, come and see."

[35] **⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ shed tears.**

[36] So the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were saying, "See how he had affection for him!"

[37] But some of them said, "Could not this one, who opened the eyes of the blind man, have made that this one also should not die?"

[38] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, again moved with indignation in himself, comes to the tomb; now it was a cave, and a stone was lying upon it.

[39] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says, "Take away the stone." The sister of the deceased, ⟦martha|Marta|Martha⟧, says to him, "Lord, he already stinks, for he is of the fourth day."

[40] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to her, "Did I not say to you that if you believe, you will see the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[41] So they took away the stone. And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ lifted his eyes upward and said, "Father, I give thanks to you that you heard me;

[42] and I knew that you always hear me; but because of the crowd standing around I said it, that they may believe that you sent me."

[43] And having said these things, he shouted with a loud voice, "⟦lazarus|Elazar|Lazarus⟧, come out!"

[44] The one who had died came out, bound feet and hands with grave bands, and his face was bound about with a cloth. ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "Loose him and let him go."

[45] So many of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, those who had come to ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ and beheld the things he did, believed in him;

[46] but some of them went away to the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and told them the things ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ did.

[47] So the high priests and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ gathered a ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧ and were saying, "What are we doing? For this man does many signs.

[48] If we leave him thus, all will believe in him, and the Romans will come and take away both our place and our nation."

[49] But a certain one of them, ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧, being high priest of that year, said to them, "You know nothing,

[50] nor do you consider that it is profitable for you that one man die for the people and not the whole nation perish."

[51] Now this he did not say from himself, but being high priest of that year he prophesied that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was about to die for the nation,

[52] and not for the nation only, but that he might also gather into one the children of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who have been scattered.

[53] So from that day they took counsel that they might kill him.

[54] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ no longer walked openly among the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, but went away from there into the country near the wilderness, into a city called ⟦ephraim-town|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and there he abided with the disciples.

[55] Now the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ was near, and many went up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ out of the country before the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧, that they might purify themselves.

[56] So they were seeking ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and were saying with one another, standing in the temple, "What does it seem to you? That he will by no means come to the feast?"

[57] Now the high priests and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ had given commandments that if anyone came to know where he is, he should disclose it, so that they might seize him.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration. The Greek does not name Lazarus in v6 and v17 ("he", "him"); the verses follow it.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v27) — Hebrew messianic title.
- **"Mary" → "*Miryam*"** (throughout) — the Greek has Μαρίας in v1 and the Hebrew form Μαριάμ elsewhere (v2, 19, 28, 31, 32, 45: WH Treg NA28; RP has the Greek form). In v20 the verse follows Μαριάμ (WH NA28; Treg RP and the SBLGNT Μαρία).
- **"Thomas" / "*Didymos*"** (v16) — Greek Δίδυμος *Didymos* ("Twin") is introduced as a name-gloss; Aramaic *Toma* = "twin"; text preserves "Thomas" as the recognized form with gloss.
- **"Caiaphas"** (v49) — retained as familiar English form; Hebrew קַיָּפָא *Qayyapha*.
- **"was sick" kept; "the town of Mary" → "of the village of *Miryam*"** (v1) — Greek ἀσθενῶν; ἀπὸ Βηθανίας ἐκ τῆς κώμης, "from Bethany, out of the village".
- **"he whom thou lovest" → "the one for whom you have affection"** (v3, 36) — Greek φιλέω (v3 φιλεῖς, v36 ἐφίλει), the verb of friendship (φίλος, "friend", v11); "loved" in v5 is ἀγαπάω. "See" (ἴδε) is kept in v3.
- **"This sickness is not unto death" kept** (v4) — Greek πρὸς θάνατον. "For the glory": ὑπὲρ τῆς δόξης.
- **"he abode two days still" → "then indeed he remained two days"** (v6) — Greek τότε μὲν ἔμεινεν … ἔπειτα, "then on the one hand he remained … after that"; the Greek has no "still, longer". ἔμεινεν is the verb μένω ("abide" of persons, v54), here of a stay in a place: "remain".
- **"saith" → "says"** (v7, 8, 11, 23, 24, 27, 34, 38, 39, 40, 44) — the Greek presents are kept.
- **"the Jews of late sought" → "just now the *Yehudim* were seeking"** (v8) — Greek νῦν ἐζήτουν.
- **"awake him out of sleep" kept** (v11) — Greek ἐξυπνίσω, from ὕπνος, "sleep". "Our friend": ὁ φίλος ἡμῶν. "Has fallen asleep": κεκοίμηται.
- **"he shall do well" → "he will be saved"** (v12) — Greek σωθήσεται, the verb "save": here of coming safely through an illness.
- **"taking of rest in sleep" → "the repose of sleep"** (v13) — Greek τῆς κοιμήσεως τοῦ ὕπνου; κοίμησις is the noun of "fall asleep" (v11), ὕπνος the ordinary word for sleep. "Those": ἐκεῖνοι. "Speaks": λέγει, a present.
- **"Lazarus is dead" → "Lazarus died"** (v14) — Greek ἀπέθανεν, an aorist. "Plainly": παρρησίᾳ, "openly" in v54.
- **"I am glad for your sakes that I was not there, to the intent ye may believe" → "I rejoice on your account — that you may believe — that I was not there"** (v15) — Greek χαίρω δι' ὑμᾶς, ἵνα πιστεύσητε, ὅτι οὐκ ἤμην ἐκεῖ, in the Greek order.
- **"he found that he had lain in the grave four days already" → "found him already four days in the tomb"** (v17) — Greek εὗρεν αὐτὸν τέσσαρας ἤδη ἡμέρας ἔχοντα ἐν τῷ μνημείῳ, literally "having four days already in the tomb".
- **"about fifteen furlongs off" → "about fifteen stadia off"** (v18) — Greek ὡς ἀπὸ σταδίων δεκαπέντε; a stadion is about 185 m, so nearly 3 km.
- **"concerning their brother" → "concerning the brother"** (v19) — Greek περὶ τοῦ ἀδελφοῦ (WH Treg NA28; RP adds αὐτῶν, "their").
- **"sat still in the house" → "was sitting in the house"** (v20) — Greek ἐκαθέζετο, an imperfect. "Is coming" (ἔρχεται) keeps the present of what she heard; so "goes" in v31 and "is" in v57.
- **"But I know, that even now" → "even now I know"** (v22) — Greek καὶ νῦν οἶδα (WH Treg and the SBLGNT); NA28 RP read ἀλλὰ καὶ νῦν, "but even now".
- **"shall rise again" → "will rise"** (v23, 24) — Greek ἀναστήσεται; "again" is not in the Greek. "In the last day": ἐν τῇ ἐσχάτῃ ἡμέρᾳ.
- **"though he were dead, yet shall he live" → "even if he dies, will live"** (v25) — Greek κἂν ἀποθάνῃ ζήσεται.
- **"shall never die" → "will by no means die forever"** (v26) — Greek οὐ μὴ ἀποθάνῃ εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, the strong denial with "unto the age": never.
- **"I believe" → "I have believed"; "which should come" → "the one coming"** (v27) — Greek πεπίστευκα, a perfect; ὁ … ἐρχόμενος.
- **"called Mary her sister secretly, saying, The Master is come" → "called *Miryam* her sister, secretly saying, 'The Teacher is present'"** (v28) — Greek λάθρᾳ stands between "called … her sister" and "saying" and can go with either; ὁ διδάσκαλος πάρεστιν.
- **"she arose quickly, and came unto him" → "that one … arose quickly and was coming to him"** (v29) — Greek ἐκείνη; ἠγέρθη, ἤρχετο (WH Treg NA28; RP has presents). "Rose" in v31 is another verb, ἀνέστη.
- **"saying, She goeth unto the grave" → "supposing that she goes to the tomb"** (v31) — Greek δόξαντες (WH Treg NA28; RP λέγοντες, "saying").
- **"he groaned in the spirit, and was troubled" → "was moved with indignation in the spirit and troubled himself"** (v33, 38) — Greek ἐνεβριμήσατο τῷ πνεύματι, ἐμβριμώμενος ἐν ἑαυτῷ: ἐμβριμάομαι is literally "snort", a word of strong and stern feeling, of anger or rebuke (as in Mark 1:43); it is not a word for sorrow. ἐτάραξεν ἑαυτόν is active with "himself", not a passive. "The spirit" is his own spirit.
- **"Jesus wept" → "*Yeshua* shed tears"** (v35) — Greek ἐδάκρυσεν ὁ Ἰησοῦς, from δάκρυ, "a tear"; a different verb from "weep" (κλαίω) in v31 and v33. Two words in the Greek besides the article.
- **"have caused that even this man should not have died" → "have made that this one also should not die"** (v37) — Greek ποιῆσαι ἵνα καὶ οὗτος μὴ ἀποθάνῃ.
- **"a stone lay upon it" → "a stone was lying upon it"** (v38) — Greek ἐπέκειτο ἐπ' αὐτῷ, "upon" or "against" it.
- **"the sister of him that was dead" → "The sister of the deceased"** (v39) — Greek τοῦ τετελευτηκότος, "of the one who has come to his end" (WH NA28; RP τεθνηκότος, as in v44).
- **"by this time he stinketh: for he hath been dead four days" → "he already stinks, for he is of the fourth day"** (v39) — Greek ἤδη ὄζει, τεταρταῖος γάρ ἐστιν, "for he is a fourth-day man"; the Greek has no word "dead".
- **"from the place where the dead was laid" dropped** (v41) — WH Treg NA28 have only "the stone"; RP adds οὗ ἦν ὁ τεθνηκὼς κείμενος. "Took away" and "lifted" are one verb (ἦραν, ἦρεν).
- **"I thank thee that thou hast heard me" → "I give thanks to you that you heard me"** (v41) — Greek εὐχαριστῶ; ἤκουσας, an aorist.
- **"the people which stand by" → "the crowd standing around"** (v42) — Greek τὸν ὄχλον τὸν περιεστῶτα. "Sent": ἀπέστειλας.
- **"cried" → "shouted"** (v43) — Greek ἐκραύγασεν (κραυγάζω, as in 12:13; 18:40; 19:6), a different verb from κράζω, "cry out". "Come out": δεῦρο ἔξω, literally "here, outside!"
- **"bound hand and foot with graveclothes … a napkin" → "bound feet and hands with grave bands … a cloth"** (v44) — Greek κειρίαις, strips for binding a corpse; σουδαρίῳ, the Latin *sudarium*, a face cloth. "Let him go": ὑπάγειν.
- **"had seen the things which Jesus did" → "beheld the things he did"** (v45) — Greek θεασάμενοι ἃ ἐποίησεν (ἅ NA28 RP and the SBLGNT; WH Treg ὅ, "what he did", singular; "*Yeshua*" is the RP reading).
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"; "a council" → "a *Sanhedrin*"** (v47, 57) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of "high priest" (v49); συνέδριον without the article, a sitting of the council.
- **"What do we?" → "What are we doing?"** (v47) — Greek Τί ποιοῦμεν ὅτι …, a present: "What are we doing, seeing that this man does many signs?"
- **"If we let him thus alone" → "If we leave him thus"** (v48) — Greek ἐὰν ἀφῶμεν αὐτὸν οὕτως. "Take away": ἀροῦσιν, the verb of v39, 41 and 10:18.
- **"it is expedient for us" → "it is profitable for you"** (v50) — Greek συμφέρει ὑμῖν (WH Treg NA28; RP ἡμῖν, "for us"); λογίζεσθε, "consider".
- **"being high priest that year" → "being high priest of that year"** (v49, 51) — Greek τοῦ ἐνιαυτοῦ ἐκείνου, a genitive. "Was about to die": ἔμελλεν ἀποθνῄσκειν. "Nation" is ἔθνος, "people" (v50) λαός.
- **"they took counsel together for to put him to death" → "they took counsel that they might kill him"** (v53) — Greek ἐβουλεύσαντο (WH Treg NA28; RP συνεβουλεύσαντο), ἀποκτείνωσιν.
- **"continued" → "abided"** (v54) — Greek ἔμεινεν (WH Treg NA28; RP διέτριβεν). "Country": χώρα; "city": πόλις.
- **"that he will not come to the feast" → "That he will by no means come to the feast?"** (v56) — Greek οὐ μὴ ἔλθῃ, the strong denial. "What does it seem to you?": Τί δοκεῖ ὑμῖν;
- **"had given a commandment … shew it, that they might take him" → "had given commandments … disclose it, so that they might seize him"** (v57) — Greek ἐντολάς, plural (WH Treg NA28; RP singular); μηνύσῃ; πιάσωσιν.
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** (thee, thou, thy, art, hath, etc.) modernized throughout.
