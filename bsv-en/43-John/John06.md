# John 6

[1] After these things ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ went away to the other side of the Sea of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, of ⟦tiberias|Tveryah|Tiberias⟧.

[2] Now a great crowd was following him, because they were beholding the signs which he was doing upon those who were sick.

[3] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ went up into the mountain, and there he was sitting with his disciples.

[4] Now the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ was near, the feast of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[5] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, lifting up his eyes and beholding that a great crowd is coming to him, says to ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧, "From where are we to buy loaves, that these may eat?"

[6] Now this he was saying, testing him, for he himself knew what he was about to do.

[7] ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ answered him, "Loaves worth two hundred denarii are not enough for them, that each may receive a little."

[8] One of his disciples, ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧, the brother of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, says to him,

[9] "There is a lad here who has five barley loaves and two fish; but what are these for so many?"

[10] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Make the people recline." Now there was much grass in the place. So the men reclined, in number about five thousand.

[11] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ took the loaves, and having given thanks he distributed to those who were reclining; likewise also of the fish, as much as they wanted.

[12] And when they were filled up, he says to his disciples, "Gather the fragments that are left over, that nothing may perish."

[13] So they gathered, and filled twelve baskets with fragments from the five barley loaves, which were left over to those who had eaten.

[14] So the people, seeing the sign which he did, were saying, "This is truly the Prophet who is coming into the world."

[15] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing that they are about to come and snatch him that they may make him king, withdrew again into the mountain, himself alone.

[16] Now when evening came, his disciples went down to the sea,

[17] and having got into a boat, they were going to the other side of the sea, to ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧. And darkness had already come, and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had not yet come to them,

[18] and the sea was being roused, a great wind blowing.

[19] So, having rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they behold ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ walking upon the sea and coming near the boat, and they were afraid.

[20] But he says to them, "**It is I; do not be afraid.**"

[21] So they wanted to take him into the boat, and immediately the boat came to be at the land to which they were going.

[22] On the next day the crowd that was standing on the other side of the sea saw that there was no other little boat there except one, and that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ did not enter the boat together with his disciples, but his disciples went away alone;

[23] but little boats came from ⟦tiberias|Tveryah|Tiberias⟧ near the place where they ate the bread, the Lord having given thanks.

[24] So when the crowd saw that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is not there, nor his disciples, they themselves got into the little boats and came to ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧, seeking ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[25] And having found him on the other side of the sea, they said to him, "Rabbi, when have you come to be here?"

[26] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered them and said, "Amen, amen, I say to you, you seek me, not because you saw signs, but because you ate of the loaves and were filled.

[27] Work not for the food that perishes, but for the food that remains unto eternal life, which the Son of Man will give you; for this one the Father sealed, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[28] So they said to him, "What are we to do, that we may work the works of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[29] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to them, "**This is the work of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that you believe in the one whom that one sent.**"

[30] So they said to him, "What sign then do you do, that we may see and believe you? What do you work?

[31] Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, just as it is written, '**Bread out of heaven he gave them to eat**.'"

[32] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Amen, amen, I say to you, it is not ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ who has given you the bread out of heaven, but my Father gives you the bread out of heaven, the true one;

[33] for the bread of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is the one that comes down out of heaven and gives life to the world."

[34] So they said to him, "Lord, always give us this bread."

[35] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "**I am the bread of life; the one who comes to me shall by no means hunger, and the one who believes in me shall by no means thirst, ever.**

[36] But I said to you that you have both seen me and do not believe.

[37] All that the Father gives me will come to me, and the one who comes to me I will by no means cast out,

[38] because I have come down from heaven, not that I may do my own will, but the will of the one who sent me.

[39] And this is the will of the one who sent me, that I should not lose any of all that he has given me, but should raise it up on the last day.

[40] For this is the will of my Father, that everyone who beholds the Son and believes in him should have eternal life, and I will raise him up on the last day."

[41] So the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were murmuring concerning him, because he said, "I am the bread that came down out of heaven,"

[42] and they were saying, "Is this not ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the son of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, whose father and mother we know? How does he now say, 'I have come down out of heaven'?"

[43] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to them, "Do not murmur with one another.

[44] No one is able to come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him, and I will raise him up in the last day.

[45] It is written in the Prophets, '**And they shall all be taught of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧**.' Everyone who has heard from the Father and has learned comes to me.

[46] Not that anyone has seen the Father, except the one who is from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; this one has seen the Father.

[47] Amen, amen, I say to you, the one who believes has eternal life.

[48] I am the bread of life.

[49] Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died.

[50] This is the bread that comes down out of heaven, that one may eat of it and not die.

[51] I am the living bread that came down out of heaven; if anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever; and the bread also which I will give is my flesh, for the life of the world."

[52] So the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ were fighting with one another, saying, "How is this one able to give us his flesh to eat?"

[53] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Amen, amen, I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you do not have life in yourselves.

[54] The one who eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up on the last day.

[55] For my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink.

[56] The one who eats my flesh and drinks my blood abides in me, and I in him.

[57] Just as the living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father, so the one who eats me, that one also will live because of me.

[58] This is the bread that came down out of heaven, not as the fathers ate and died; the one who eats this bread will live forever."

[59] These things he said in a synagogue, teaching in ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧.

[60] So many of his disciples, having heard, said, "This word is hard; who is able to hear it?"

[61] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing in himself that his disciples are murmuring concerning this, said to them, "Does this make you stumble?

[62] If then you behold the Son of Man going up where he was before —?

[63] The Spirit is that which makes alive; the flesh profits nothing; the words which I have spoken to you are spirit and are life.

[64] But there are some of you who do not believe." For ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ knew from the beginning who those are who do not believe, and who it is who will deliver him up.

[65] And he was saying, "Because of this I have said to you that no one is able to come to me unless it has been given to him from the Father."

[66] From this, many of his disciples went away back and no longer walked with him.

[67] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to the twelve, "Do you also want to go?"

[68] ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ answered him, "Lord, to whom shall we go away? You have words of eternal life,

[69] and we have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[70] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered them, "Did I not choose you, the twelve? And one of you is a devil."

[71] Now he was speaking of ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, son of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ Iscariot; for this one was about to deliver him up, one of the twelve.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v8, 68) — the Greek has Πέτρος; the Aramaic name is used consistently; cf. ch. 1 note.
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v26, 32, 47, 53) — Greek ἀμὴν ἀμήν, the Hebrew אָמֵן left untranslated, as the Greek leaves it.
- **"over the sea of Galilee, which is the sea of Tiberias" → "to the other side of the Sea of Galilee, of Tiberias"** (v1) — Greek πέραν τῆς θαλάσσης τῆς Γαλιλαίας τῆς Τιβεριάδος: two genitives side by side; "which is the sea" is not in the Greek. μετὰ ταῦτα is "after these things".
- **"because they saw his miracles which he did on them that were diseased" → "because they were beholding the signs which he was doing upon those who were sick"** (v2) — Greek ἐθεώρουν (WH Treg NA28; RP ἑώρων "saw"), imperfect; σημεῖα "signs"; ἐπὶ τῶν ἀσθενούντων.
- **"went up into a mountain, and there he sat" → "went up into the mountain, and there he was sitting"** (v3, 15) — Greek εἰς τὸ ὄρος, with the article; ἐκάθητο, imperfect.
- **"the passover, a feast of the Jews, was nigh" → "the Passover was near, the feast of the Jews"** (v4) — Greek ἡ ἑορτή, with the article.
- **"saw a great company come unto him, he saith … Whence shall we buy bread" → "beholding that a great crowd is coming to him, says … From where are we to buy loaves"** (v5) — Greek θεασάμενος "having beheld"; ἔρχεται, λέγει, presents; ἄρτους, plural, "loaves" (v5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 26); ἀγοράσωμεν, a subjunctive (RP has the future). "People" is not in the Greek: οὗτοι, "these".
- **"this he said to prove him" → "this he was saying, testing him"** (v6) — Greek πειράζων, the verb "tempt" of the other gospels (root of πειρασμός "trial"): here "put to the test", not "entice to sin"; ἔλεγεν, imperfect; ἔμελλεν ποιεῖν, "was about to do".
- **"Two hundred pennyworth of bread is not sufficient for them, that every one of them may take a little" → "Loaves worth two hundred denarii are not enough for them, that each may receive a little"** (v7) — Greek διακοσίων δηναρίων ἄρτοι οὐκ ἀρκοῦσιν, a present indicative, not "would not be"; λάβῃ "receive". βραχύ τι NA28 RP; WH Treg βραχύ, the same sense.
- **"There is a lad here, which hath five barley loaves, and two small fishes: but what are they among so many?" → "There is a lad here who has five barley loaves and two fish; but what are these for so many?"** (v9, 11) — Greek παιδάριον, a diminutive of παῖς: a boy or a young slave; ὀψάρια, fish as the food eaten with bread (the word of 21:9–13), not "small"; εἰς τοσούτους.
- **"Make the men sit down … So the men sat down" → "Make the people recline … So the men reclined"** (v10) — two Greek words: τοὺς ἀνθρώπους, "the people" (so v14), then οἱ ἄνδρες, "the men" (males), who are counted; ἀναπεσεῖν, "recline" (literally "fall back").
- **"he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to them that were set down" → "he distributed to those who were reclining"** (v11) — "to the disciples, and the disciples" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28. There is no object "them" in the Greek.
- **"When they were filled … Gather up the fragments that remain, that nothing be lost" → "when they were filled up … Gather the fragments that are left over, that nothing may perish"** (v12, 13) — Greek ἐνεπλήσθησαν, a different verb from "were filled" (ἐχορτάσθητε) in v26; λέγει, a present; τὰ περισσεύσαντα, the verb περισσεύω "abound, be over and above"; ἀπόληται, the verb of v27 ("perishes") and v39 ("lose").
- **"Then those men, when they had seen the miracle that Jesus did, said, This is of a truth that prophet that should come into the world" → "So the people, seeing the sign which he did, were saying, This is truly the Prophet who is coming into the world"** (v14) — Greek οἱ ἄνθρωποι; ὃ ἐποίησεν σημεῖον, singular (Treg NA28 RP; WH plural, "the signs which he did"); "Jesus" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28; ἔλεγον, imperfect; ὁ ἐρχόμενος, a present participle.
- **"When Jesus therefore perceived that they would come and take him by force, to make him a king, he departed again into a mountain himself alone" → "So *Yeshua*, knowing that they are about to come and snatch him that they may make him king, withdrew again into the mountain, himself alone"** (v15) — Greek γνούς; ἁρπάζειν "snatch, seize"; ἵνα ποιήσωσιν βασιλέα, "that they may make (him) king" ("him" is supplied; RP has it); ἀνεχώρησεν "withdrew".
- **"entered into a ship, and went over the sea toward Capernaum. And it was now dark, and Jesus was not come to them" → "having got into a boat, they were going to the other side of the sea, to Capernaum. And darkness had already come, and *Yeshua* had not yet come to them"** (v17) — Greek ἤρχοντο, imperfect; σκοτία ἤδη ἐγεγόνει, "darkness had already come to be"; οὔπω "not yet" (WH Treg NA28; RP οὐκ "not"). The sentence runs on into v18.
- **"the sea arose by reason of a great wind that blew" → "the sea was being roused, a great wind blowing"** (v18) — Greek διεγείρετο, imperfect passive of "rouse, awaken" (the verb of Mark 4:39).
- **"about five and twenty or thirty furlongs, they see Jesus walking on the sea" → "about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they behold *Yeshua* walking upon the sea"** (v19) — Greek σταδίους, the stadion, about 185 m: some 4.5–5.5 km; θεωροῦσιν, a present.
- **"It is I; be not afraid" kept** (v20) — Greek Ἐγώ εἰμι, literally "I am": here it plainly identifies the one whom they see on the sea (as Mark 6:50), so "It is I". The same two words stand absolutely in 8:24, 28, 58, where they are rendered "I am".
- **"Then they willingly received him into the ship" → "So they wanted to take him into the boat"** (v21) — Greek ἤθελον οὖν λαβεῖν, "they wanted to take": it is not said that they did. "Were glad" is not in the Greek. ἐγένετο, "came to be".
- **"the people which stood on the other side of the sea saw that there was none other boat there, save that one whereinto his disciples were entered" → "the crowd that was standing on the other side of the sea saw that there was no other little boat there except one"** (v22) — "that one whereinto his disciples were entered" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28. πλοιάριον is a diminutive, "little boat" (v22, 23, 24), beside πλοῖον "boat". The Greek verbs are simple pasts: "was", "did not enter", "went away".
- **"(Howbeit there came other boats from Tiberias …)" → "but little boats came from Tiberias …"** (v23) — the editions differ only in the accent: ἀλλὰ "but" (WH Treg, printed) or ἄλλα "other" (NA28; RP ἄλλα δέ); the old manuscripts have no accents. With ἄλλα: "other little boats came from Tiberias". πλοιάρια Treg NA28 RP; WH πλοῖα "boats". εὐχαριστήσαντος τοῦ κυρίου, "the Lord having given thanks".
- **"that Jesus was not there … they also took shipping" → "that *Yeshua* is not there … they themselves got into the little boats"** (v24) — Greek ἔστιν, present; αὐτοί.
- **"Rabbi, when camest thou hither?" → "Rabbi, when have you come to be here?"** (v25) — Greek πότε ὧδε γέγονας, a perfect of γίνομαι.
- **"Jesus answered them and said" kept** (v26, 29, 43) — Greek ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν, both verbs.
- **"because ye did eat of the loaves, and were filled" kept** (v26) — Greek ἐχορτάσθητε; "your fill of" is not a separate phrase.
- **"Labour not for the meat which perisheth, but for that meat which endureth unto everlasting life … for him hath God the Father sealed" → "Work not for the food that perishes, but for the food that remains unto eternal life … for this one the Father sealed, *Elohim*"** (v27) — Greek ἐργάζεσθε, the verb "work" of v28, 30 (ἔργον "work", v28, 29), with a plain accusative ("for" is supplied); βρῶσις, "eating", here the thing eaten; μένουσαν, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons, v56), here of a thing: "remain"; ἐσφράγισεν, aorist; ὁ θεός stands last in the sentence. "Set his seal on" is "sealed".
- **"What shall we do, that we might work the works of God?" → "What are we to do, that we may work the works of *Elohim*?"** (v28) — Greek ἐργαζώμεθα τὰ ἔργα.
- **"that ye believe on him whom he hath sent" → "that you believe in the one whom that one sent"** (v29) — Greek πιστεύητε εἰς, present subjunctive (RP aorist); ἀπέστειλεν ἐκεῖνος.
- **"What sign shewest thou then … what dost thou work?" → "What sign then do you do … What do you work?"** (v30) — Greek ποιεῖς; τί ἐργάζῃ, the same verb again.
- **"He gave them bread from heaven to eat" → "Bread out of heaven he gave them to eat"** (v31) — Greek Ἄρτον ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ ἔδωκεν αὐτοῖς φαγεῖν (Ps 78:24; Exod 16:4, 15), in this order. ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ is "out of heaven" throughout (v31–58); v38 has ἀπό, "from heaven" (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐκ).
- **"Moses gave you not that bread from heaven; but my Father giveth you the true bread from heaven" → "it is not Moses who has given you the bread out of heaven, but my Father gives you the bread out of heaven, the true one"** (v32) — Greek οὐ Μωϋσῆς, the negative on the name; δέδωκεν, a perfect (NA28 RP) ] ἔδωκεν, "gave" (WH Treg) — 2–2, NA28 decides; τὸν ἀληθινόν stands last, "the true one" (ἀληθινός; v55 has ἀληθής).
- **"the bread of God is he which cometh down from heaven" → "the bread of *Elohim* is the one that comes down out of heaven"** (v33) — Greek ὁ καταβαίνων, masculine like ἄρτος "bread": "that which comes down" (the bread) or "he who comes down"; the hearers take it of bread (v34).
- **"Lord, evermore give us this bread" → "Lord, always give us this bread"** (v34) — Greek Κύριε; πάντοτε.
- **"he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst" → "the one who comes to me shall by no means hunger, and the one who believes in me shall by no means thirst, ever"** (v35, 37) — Greek οὐ μή, the strong negation, three times; πώποτε "ever" stands only with "thirst".
- **"ye also have seen me, and believe not" → "you have both seen me and do not believe"** (v36) — Greek καὶ ἑωράκατέ με καὶ οὐ πιστεύετε, "both … and"; "yet" is not in the Greek.
- **"shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out" → "will come to me, and the one who comes to me I will by no means cast out"** (v37) — Greek πᾶν ὅ, neuter singular, "all that" (so v39); ἥξει; ἐκβάλω ἔξω, literally "cast out outside". The sentence runs on into v38 ("because").
- **"For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will" → "because I have come down from heaven, not that I may do my own will"** (v38) — Greek ὅτι καταβέβηκα, a perfect; ἵνα ποιῶ.
- **"this is the Father's will which hath sent me, that of all which he hath given me I should lose nothing, but should raise it up again at the last day" → "this is the will of the one who sent me, that I should not lose any of all that he has given me, but should raise it up on the last day"** (v39) — "Father" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28. Greek πᾶν ὃ δέδωκέν μοι μὴ ἀπολέσω ἐξ αὐτοῦ, literally "all that he has given me — that I should not lose (any) out of it"; αὐτό "it", neuter. τῇ ἐσχάτῃ ἡμέρᾳ WH Treg RP; NA28 adds ἐν (so too in v40).
- **"this is the will of him that sent me, that every one which seeth the Son" → "this is the will of my Father, that everyone who beholds the Son"** (v40) — Greek τοῦ πατρός μου (WH Treg NA28; RP "of him who sent me"); γάρ "for" (RP δέ); θεωρῶν "beholds".
- **"The Jews then murmured at him" → "So the Jews were murmuring concerning him"** (v41, 43, 61) — Greek ἐγόγγυζον περὶ αὐτοῦ, imperfect; γογγύζω is "murmur", as of Israel in the wilderness (Exod 16:2; 1 Cor 10:10). The sentence runs on into v42.
- **"how is it then that he saith, I came down from heaven?" → "How does he now say, I have come down out of heaven?"** (v42) — Greek νῦν "now" (WH Treg NA28; RP οὖν); καταβέβηκα, a perfect.
- **"Murmur not among yourselves" → "Do not murmur with one another"** (v43) — Greek μετ' ἀλλήλων.
- **"No man can come to me, except the Father which hath sent me draw him … at the last day" → "No one is able to come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him … in the last day"** (v44, 65) — Greek οὐδεὶς δύναται; ἑλκύσῃ "draw, pull" (12:32; 21:6, 11); ἐν τῇ ἐσχάτῃ ἡμέρᾳ, here with ἐν.
- **"And they shall be all taught of God. Every man therefore that hath heard, and hath learned of the Father" → "And they shall all be taught of *Elohim*. Everyone who has heard from the Father and has learned"** (v45) — Greek διδακτοὶ θεοῦ, "taught ones of God", a genitive (Isa 54:13); no "therefore" in WH Treg NA28; ἀκούσας παρὰ τοῦ πατρὸς καὶ μαθών, in this order. The sentence ends here; v46 begins a new one.
- **"save he which is of God, he hath seen the Father" → "except the one who is from *Elohim*; this one has seen the Father"** (v46) — Greek ὁ ὢν παρὰ τοῦ θεοῦ, "the one who is from beside God"; οὗτος.
- **"He that believeth on me hath everlasting life" → "the one who believes has eternal life"** (v47) — ὁ πιστεύων without an object (WH NA28); Treg RP add εἰς ἐμέ, "in me".
- **"I am that bread of life" → "I am the bread of life"** (v35, 48) — Greek ὁ ἄρτος τῆς ζωῆς. In v35, 41, 48, 51 ἐγώ εἰμι has a predicate ("the bread").
- **"that a man may eat thereof, and not die" → "that one may eat of it and not die"** (v50) — Greek ἵνα τις ἐξ αὐτοῦ φάγῃ καὶ μὴ ἀποθάνῃ.
- **"he shall live for ever: and the bread that I will give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world" → "he will live forever; and the bread also which I will give is my flesh, for the life of the world"** (v51, 58) — Greek εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, "unto the age"; καὶ ὁ ἄρτος δέ, "and the bread, moreover"; ἡ σάρξ μού ἐστιν ὑπὲρ τῆς τοῦ κόσμου ζωῆς, in this order: "which I will give" a second time (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28.
- **"The Jews therefore strove among themselves" → "So the Jews were fighting with one another"** (v52) — Greek ἐμάχοντο πρὸς ἀλλήλους, the verb "fight" (2 Tim 2:24; Jas 4:2), here with words. "His flesh": αὐτοῦ WH NA28; Treg RP "the flesh".
- **"ye have no life in you" → "you do not have life in yourselves"** (v53) — Greek οὐκ ἔχετε ζωὴν ἐν ἑαυτοῖς.
- **"Whoso eateth my flesh" → "The one who eats my flesh"** (v54, 56, 57, 58) — Greek ὁ τρώγων, a second verb for "eat" (literally "chew, munch"; also 13:18), which John uses for the present tense; v53 and the earlier verses have φάγω. No "feeds on".
- **"my flesh is meat indeed, and my blood is drink indeed" → "my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink"** (v55) — Greek ἀληθής, the adjective (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀληθῶς "truly"); βρῶσις, πόσις, literally "eating", "drinking".
- **"dwelleth in me" → "abides in me"** (v56) — Greek μένει.
- **"As the living Father hath sent me, and I live by the Father: so he that eateth me, even he shall live by me" → "Just as the living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father, so the one who eats me, that one also will live because of me"** (v57) — Greek ἀπέστειλεν, aorist; διὰ τὸν πατέρα … δι' ἐμέ, διά with the accusative, "because of"; κἀκεῖνος.
- **"not as your fathers did eat manna, and are dead" → "not as the fathers ate and died"** (v58) — "your" and "manna" (RP) are not in WH Treg NA28; ἐξ οὐρανοῦ here without the article. "The bread" after "like" is not in the Greek.
- **"These things said he in the synagogue, as he taught in Capernaum" → "These things he said in a synagogue, teaching in Capernaum"** (v59) — Greek ἐν συναγωγῇ, without the article ("in a synagogue", "in synagogue"); the name "Jesus" is not in the verse.
- **"Many therefore of his disciples, when they had heard this, said, This is an hard saying; who can hear it?" → "So many of his disciples, having heard, said, This word is hard; who is able to hear it?"** (v60) — Greek ἀκούσαντες without an object; ὁ λόγος οὗτος, "this word"; αὐτοῦ ἀκούειν, "hear it" (the word) or "hear him".
- **"When Jesus knew in himself that his disciples murmured at it, he said unto them, Doth this offend you?" → "But *Yeshua*, knowing in himself that his disciples are murmuring concerning this, said to them, Does this make you stumble?"** (v61) — Greek γογγύζουσιν, present; σκανδαλίζει, "cause to stumble".
- **"What and if ye shall see the Son of man ascend up where he was before?" → "If then you behold the Son of Man going up where he was before —?"** (v62) — Greek ἐὰν οὖν θεωρῆτε …: a condition without its conclusion; the sentence is left unfinished. "What" is not in the Greek. ἀναβαίνοντα, "going up".
- **"It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life" → "The Spirit is that which makes alive; the flesh profits nothing; the words which I have spoken to you are spirit and are life"** (v63) — Greek τὸ πνεῦμά ἐστιν τὸ ζῳοποιοῦν: "that which" follows the neuter participle, and the gender is grammatical; ζῳοποιέω "make alive" (5:21); ῥήματα, "spoken words"; λελάληκα, a perfect (WH Treg NA28; RP λαλῶ "speak"); πνεῦμά ἐστιν καὶ ζωή ἐστιν, without articles, "is" twice.
- **"who they were that believed not, and who should betray him" → "who those are who do not believe, and who it is who will deliver him up"** (v64, 71) — Greek present tenses; παραδίδωμι is "deliver up, hand over" (the verb of Mark 9:31; Rom 8:32), not a special word for betrayal. The sentence is the evangelist's; the Greek has no brackets.
- **"Therefore said I unto you, that no man can come unto me, except it were given unto him of my Father" → "Because of this I have said to you that no one is able to come to me unless it has been given to him from the Father"** (v65) — Greek ἔλεγεν, imperfect; εἴρηκα, perfect; ᾖ δεδομένον; ἐκ τοῦ πατρός, "out of the Father" ("my", RP, is not in WH Treg NA28).
- **"From that time many of his disciples went back" → "From this, many of his disciples went away back"** (v66) — Greek ἐκ τούτου, "from this": from this time, or because of this; ἀπῆλθον εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω, "went away to the things behind".
- **"Will ye also go away?" → "Do you also want to go?"** (v67) — Greek Μὴ καὶ ὑμεῖς θέλετε ὑπάγειν; the question with μή expects the answer "no". ὑπάγω is "go"; "go away" is ἀπέρχομαι (v66, 68).
- **"thou hast the words of eternal life" → "You have words of eternal life"** (v68) — Greek ῥήματα without the article.
- **"we believe and are sure that thou art that Christ, the Son of the living God" → "we have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of *Elohim*"** (v69) — Greek πεπιστεύκαμεν καὶ ἐγνώκαμεν, perfects; ὁ ἅγιος τοῦ θεοῦ (WH Treg NA28); RP "the Christ, the Son of the living God".
- **"Have not I chosen you twelve, and one of you is a devil?" → "Did I not choose you, the twelve? And one of you is a devil"** (v70) — Greek ἐξελεξάμην, aorist; καί is "and", not "and yet". διάβολος, "slanderer, accuser", the word for the devil, here without the article.
- **"He spake of Judas Iscariot the son of Simon: for he it was that should betray him, being one of the twelve" → "Now he was speaking of Judas, son of Simon Iscariot; for this one was about to deliver him up, one of the twelve"** (v71) — Greek Ἰούδαν Σίμωνος Ἰσκαριώτου (WH Treg NA28): "Iscariot" is in the genitive and goes with Simon; RP has the accusative, with Judas. ἔμελλεν παραδιδόναι.
