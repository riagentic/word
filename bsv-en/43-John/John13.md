# John 13

[1] Now before the feast of the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing that his hour had come that he should pass over out of this world to the Father, having loved his own who were in the world, loved them to the end.

[2] And supper taking place, the devil having already put it into the heart that ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, son of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ Iscariot, should deliver him up,

[3] knowing that the Father gave all things into his hands, and that he came out from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and is going to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[4] he arises from the supper and lays down his garments, and taking a towel he girded himself.

[5] Then he puts water into the basin, and he began to wash the feet of the disciples and to wipe them with the towel with which he was girded.

[6] So he comes to ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧. He says to him, "Lord, do you wash my feet?"

[7] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to him, "What I am doing you do not know now, but you will come to know after these things."

[8] ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ says to him, "You shall by no means wash my feet, forever." ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered him, "If I do not wash you, you have no part with me."

[9] ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ says to him, "Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head."

[10] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "The one who has been bathed has no need except to wash his feet, but is wholly clean; and you are clean, but not all."

[11] For he knew the one who was delivering him up; because of this he said, "You are not all clean."

[12] So when he had washed their feet and taken his garments and reclined again, he said to them, "Do you know what I have done to you?

[13] You call me 'The Teacher' and 'The Lord', and you say well, for I am.

[14] If I then, the Lord and the Teacher, washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet;

[15] for I gave you an example, that just as I did to you, you also should do.

[16] Amen, amen, I say to you, a slave is not greater than his lord, nor an apostle greater than the one who sent him.

[17] If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them.

[18] I do not speak concerning all of you; I know whom I chose; but — that the Scripture may be fulfilled: '**The one who eats my bread lifted up his heel against me**.'

[19] From now on I tell you before it comes to pass, so that when it comes to pass you may believe that I am.

[20] Amen, amen, I say to you, the one who receives whomever I send receives me, and the one who receives me receives the one who sent me."

[21] Having said these things, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was troubled in spirit, and testified and said, "Amen, amen, I say to you that one of you will deliver me up."

[22] The disciples were looking at one another, being at a loss about whom he speaks.

[23] There was reclining in the bosom of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ one of his disciples, whom ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ loved;

[24] so ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ nods to this one to inquire who it might be about whom he speaks.

[25] That one, leaning back thus upon the breast of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, says to him, "Lord, who is it?"

[26] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answers, "It is that one for whom I shall dip the morsel and give it to him." So, having dipped the morsel, he takes it and gives it to ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, son of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ Iscariot.

[27] And after the morsel, then Satan entered into that one. So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "What you do, do quickly."

[28] But none of those reclining knew for what purpose he said this to him;

[29] for some were supposing, since ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ had the money box, that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "Buy what we have need of for the feast," or that he should give something to the poor.

[30] So, having received the morsel, that one went out immediately. **And it was night.**

[31] So when he had gone out, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says, "Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has been glorified in him;

[32] if ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has been glorified in him, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will also glorify him in himself, and will glorify him immediately.

[33] Little children, yet a little while I am with you. You will seek me, and just as I said to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, 'Where I go, you cannot come,' I say to you also now.

[34] **A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another; just as I loved you, that you also love one another.**

**[35] By this all will know that you are my disciples, if you have love among one another.**"

[36] ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ says to him, "Lord, where are you going?" ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "Where I go, you cannot follow me now, but you will follow later."

[37] ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ says to him, "Lord, why can I not follow you now? I will lay down my soul for you."

[38] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answers, "Will you lay down your soul for me? Amen, amen, I say to you, a rooster shall by no means crow until you deny me three times."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"Peter" / "*Kefa*"** (v6, 8, 9, 24, 36, 37) — Aramaic name used consistently.
- **"Satan"** (v27) — Hebrew שָׂטָן *Satan* ("adversary"); retained in Hebrew form as it is a name/title, not a common noun.
- **"Verily, verily" → "Amen, amen"** (v16, 20, 21, 38) — Greek ἀμὴν ἀμήν, the Hebrew word left untranslated, as the Greek leaves it.
- **Present tenses kept** (v4–6, 8–10, 24–27, 31, 36–38) — "arises", "lays down", "puts", "comes", "says", "nods", "answers", "takes", "gives": the Greek tells these in the present, between past tenses (v4 "girded", v5 "began").
- **"depart" → "pass over"** (v1) — Greek μεταβῇ, as in 5:24 and 1 John 3:14.
- **"he loved them unto the end" kept** (v1) — Greek εἰς τέλος, "to the end" or "to the uttermost". The verse is one sentence: "knowing … having loved … loved".
- **"supper being ended" → "supper taking place"** (v2) — Greek δείπνου γινομένου, a present participle (WH, Treg, NA28; KJV follows γενομένου, RP).
- **"put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, Simon's son, to betray him" → "put it into the heart that *Yehudah*, son of *Shimon* Iscariot, should deliver him up"** (v2) — Greek βεβληκότος εἰς τὴν καρδίαν ἵνα παραδοῖ αὐτὸν Ἰούδας Σίμωνος Ἰσκαριώτου: "*Yehudah*" is the subject of "deliver up", and the Greek does not say whose heart (most take it as the heart of *Yehudah*; the words also allow the devil's own). "Iscariot" stands in the genitive with "*Shimon*" (NA28, RP; WH and Treg have Ἰσκαριώτης, with "*Yehudah*"); "son" is supplied for the bare genitive, also in v26. KJV follows the RP wording Ἰούδα … ἵνα αὐτὸν παραδῷ.
- **"betray" → "deliver up"** (v2, 11, 21) — Greek παραδίδωμι, "hand over"; v11 τὸν παραδιδόντα is a present participle, "the one delivering him up".
- **"Jesus knowing" → "knowing"** (v3) — "*Yeshua*" is not in the critical text here (WH, Treg, NA28; RP adds ὁ Ἰησοῦς). ἔδωκεν "gave" (RP δέδωκεν "has given"); ὑπάγει "is going" has no word for "back".
- **"laid aside his garments" → "lays down his garments"** (v4) — Greek τίθησιν τὰ ἱμάτια; τίθημι is the verb of "lay down my soul" (v37–38; 10:17–18), and "taken" (v12, ἔλαβεν) the verb of "take it again" there. ἱμάτια is the plain plural "garments"; "outer" is not in the Greek.
- **"poureth water into a bason" → "puts water into the basin"** (v5) — Greek βάλλει, the verb of v2 ("put into the heart"); τὸν νιπτῆρα has the article.
- **"Peter saith unto him" → "He says to him"** (v6) — Greek λέγει αὐτῷ, without a named subject (Treg and RP add ἐκεῖνος, "that one"). In "do you wash my feet?" the Greek sets "you" and "my" side by side (σύ μου).
- **"thou knowest not now; but thou shalt know hereafter" → "you do not know now, but you will come to know after these things"** (v7) — two Greek verbs, οἶδας and γνώσῃ; μετὰ ταῦτα. Also v12 Γινώσκετε "Do you know" (it can also be a command, "Know") and v17 οἴδατε.
- **"Thou shalt never wash my feet" → "You shall by no means wash my feet, forever"** (v8) — Greek οὐ μὴ … εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, the strong negation and "unto the age" (the fixed phrase "forever").
- **"He that is washed needeth not save to wash his feet" → "The one who has been bathed has no need except to wash his feet"** (v10) — two Greek verbs: λελουμένος (λούω, bathe the whole body; the perfect can be "has bathed") and νίψασθαι (νίπτω, wash a part). "You are clean" is plural (ὑμεῖς).
- **"was set down again, he said" → "reclined again, he said"** (v12) — Greek ἀνέπεσεν, "fell back", reclined at table. πάλιν "again" can go with "reclined" or with "he said to them" (so the SBLGNT comma).
- **"Master and Lord" → "'The Teacher' and 'The Lord'"** (v13, 14) — Greek ὁ διδάσκαλος καὶ ὁ κύριος, with the article, as titles; v14 has the reverse order and no "your".
- **"I have washed" / "I have given" / "I have done" → "washed" / "gave" / "did"** (v14, 15) — Greek aorists; v12 πεποίηκα "I have done" is a perfect.
- **"The servant is not greater than his lord; neither he that is sent" → "a slave is not greater than his lord, nor an apostle"** (v16) — Greek δοῦλος "slave"; κύριος "lord", the word of v13–14; ἀπόστολος "apostle", literally "one sent" — the only place the word stands in this gospel.
- **"I know whom I have chosen" → "I know whom I chose"** (v18) — Greek ἐξελεξάμην, an aorist; τίνας "which ones" (RP οὕς).
- **"but that the scripture may be fulfilled" → "but — that the Scripture may be fulfilled"** (v18) — Greek ἀλλ' ἵνα … πληρωθῇ, a purpose clause without a main verb; the dash keeps it unfinished.
- **"He that eateth bread with me hath lifted up" → "The one who eats my bread lifted up"** (v18) — Greek ὁ τρώγων μου τὸν ἄρτον (WH, Treg, NA28; RP μετ' ἐμοῦ "with me"), a present participle, the verb of 6:54–58; ἐπῆρεν, an aorist. The words of Ps 41:9 as printed in the Greek here.
- **"Now I tell you before it come" → "From now on I tell you before it comes to pass"** (v19) — Greek ἀπ' ἄρτι, "from now".
- **"that I am he" → "that I am"** (v19) — Greek ἐγώ εἰμι, standing alone as in 8:24, 28, 58; "he" is not in the Greek.
- **"troubled in spirit" kept** (v21) — Greek ἐταράχθη τῷ πνεύματι, the verb of 11:33; 12:27; 14:1, 27. "testified and said": both verbs kept.
- **"doubting of whom he spake" → "being at a loss about whom he speaks"** (v22) — Greek ἀπορούμενοι; λέγει, a present. ἔβλεπον, an imperfect.
- **"leaning on Jesus' bosom" → "reclining in the bosom of *Yeshua*"** (v23) — Greek ἀνακείμενος … ἐν τῷ κόλπῳ τοῦ Ἰησοῦ; κόλπος is the word of 1:18 ("in the bosom of the Father"). "At table" and "side" are not in the Greek.
- **"beckoned to him, that he should ask who it should be" → "nods to this one to inquire who it might be"** (v24) — Greek νεύει … πυθέσθαι τίς ἂν εἴη (NA28, RP; 2–2, NA28 decides). WH and Treg read καὶ λέγει αὐτῷ Εἰπὲ τίς ἐστιν, "and says to him, 'Say who it is'". "*Yeshua*" is not in the Greek of this verse.
- **"He then lying on Jesus' breast" → "That one, leaning back thus upon the breast of *Yeshua*"** (v25) — Greek ἀναπεσὼν ἐκεῖνος οὕτως ἐπὶ τὸ στῆθος: the verb of v12 ("reclined", literally "fell back"); οὕτως "thus"; στῆθος "breast", a different word from κόλπος (v23). NA28 adds οὖν, "So"; KJV follows ἐπιπεσών (RP).
- **"He it is, to whom I shall give a sop, when I have dipped it" → "It is that one for whom I shall dip the morsel and give it to him"** (v26) — Greek ᾧ ἐγὼ βάψω τὸ ψωμίον καὶ δώσω αὐτῷ (WH, Treg, NA28; KJV follows RP βάψας … ἐπιδώσω). ψωμίον is a small piece, a morsel; "of bread" is not in the word.
- **"he gave it to Judas" → "he takes it and gives it to *Yehudah*"** (v26) — the verse follows λαμβάνει καὶ δίδωσιν, "takes and gives" (WH, Treg, NA28); SBLGNT and RP have only δίδωσιν, "gives".
- **"after the sop Satan entered into him" → "after the morsel, then Satan entered into that one"** (v27) — Greek μετὰ τὸ ψωμίον τότε … εἰς ἐκεῖνον; "he had taken" is not in the Greek. ἐκεῖνος "that one" also v25, 26, 30.
- **"That thou doest, do quickly" kept** (v27) — Greek Ὃ ποιεῖς ποίησον τάχιον; ποιεῖς is a plain present ("what you do / are doing"), τάχιον a comparative in form ("more quickly").
- **"no man at the table knew for what intent" → "none of those reclining knew for what purpose"** (v28) — Greek τῶν ἀνακειμένων; πρὸς τί.
- **"the bag" → "the money box"** (v29) — Greek γλωσσόκομον, a case or box, as in 12:6. λέγει "says" is a present.
- **"Now is the Son of man glorified" → "Now the Son of Man has been glorified"** (v31, 32) — Greek ἐδοξάσθη, an aorist passive, three times.
- **"If God be glorified in him" kept** (v32) — these words stand in Treg, NA28 and RP; WH omits them.
- **"shall also glorify him in himself" kept** (v32) — the verse follows ἐν αὐτῷ (Treg, NA28; WH and SBLGNT αὑτῷ, RP ἑαυτῷ). The difference is only the breathing mark: "in himself" (in *Elohim*) by the sense; unmarked, the word can also be read "in him".
- **"Whither I go" kept** (v33, 36) — Greek ὑπάγω "go, go away" (also v3); "now" (ἄρτι) stands last in v33.
- **"as I have loved you, that ye also love" → "just as I loved you, that you also love"** (v34) — Greek ἠγάπησα, an aorist; the second ἵνα "that" is kept. The clause can also be read "that, just as I loved you, you also love one another".
- **"love one to another" → "love among one another"** (v35) — Greek ἐν ἀλλήλοις; "all" (πάντες) has no noun.
- **"Jesus answered him" → "*Yeshua* answered"** (v36) — ἀπεκρίθη Ἰησοῦς (WH, Treg, SBLGNT; NA28 adds αὐτῷ "him").
- **"I will lay down my life" → "I will lay down my soul"** (v37, 38) — Greek τὴν ψυχήν μου θήσω, as in 10:11–18 and 1 John 3:16.
- **"The cock shall not crow, till thou hast denied me thrice" → "a rooster shall by no means crow until you deny me three times"** (v38) — Greek οὐ μὴ ἀλέκτωρ φωνήσῃ, the strong negation and no article; ἀρνήσῃ (WH, Treg, NA28; RP ἀπαρνήσῃ).
- **"linen towel" → "towel"** (v4, 5) — Greek λέντιον *lention* (Latin *linteum*, a linen cloth).
- **Archaic pronouns and verb forms** (thee, thou, thy, ye, hath, art, etc.) modernized throughout.

### Notes
- **v18** quotes Ps 41:9.
