[4] And he had to pass through ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[5] So he comes to a city of ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧ called Sychar, near the plot of land which ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ gave to ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ his son;

[6] and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov's|Jacob's⟧ spring was there. So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, wearied from the journey, was sitting thus at the spring; the hour was about the sixth.

[7] A woman of ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧ comes to draw water. ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to her, "Give me to drink."

[8] For his disciples had gone away into the city, that they might buy food.

[9] So the ⟦samaritans|Shomroni|Samaritan⟧ woman says to him, "How do you, being a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, ask to drink from me, being a ⟦samaritans|Shomroni|Samaritan⟧ woman?" For ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ have no dealings with ⟦samaritans|Shomronim|Samaritans⟧.

[10] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to her, "If you knew the gift of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and who it is who says to you, 'Give me to drink,' you would have asked him, and he would have given you **living water**."

[11] The woman says to him, "Lord, you have nothing to draw with, and the well is deep; from where then do you have the living water?

[12] Are you greater than our father ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, who gave us the well, and himself drank from it, and his sons and his livestock?"

[13] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered and said to her, "Everyone who drinks of this water will thirst again;

[14] but **whoever drinks of the water which I will give him will by no means thirst forever; but the water which I will give him will become in him a spring of water leaping up unto eternal life.**"

[15] The woman says to him, "Lord, give me this water, that I may not thirst nor come through here to draw."

[16] He says to her, "Go, call your husband and come here."

[17] The woman answered and said to him, "I have no husband." ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to her, "You said well, 'A husband I do not have';

[18] for you have had five husbands, and he whom you now have is not your husband; this you have said truly."

[19] The woman says to him, "Lord, I behold that you are a prophet.

[20] Our fathers bowed down on this mountain, and you say that in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ is the place where one must bow down."

[21] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to her, "Believe me, woman, that an hour is coming when neither on this mountain nor in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ will you bow down to the Father.

[23] but an hour is coming, and now is, when the true ones who bow down will bow down to the Father in spirit and truth; for the Father also seeks such to bow down to him.

[24] **⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is spirit, and those who bow down to him must bow down in spirit and truth.**"

[25] The woman says to him, "I know that ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Messiah⟧ is coming, the one called Christ; when that one comes, he will announce to us all things."

[26] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to her, "**I am, the one speaking to you.**"

[27] And upon this his disciples came, and they were marveling that he was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, "What do you seek?" or, "Why do you speak with her?"

[28] So the woman left her water jar and went away into the city, and she says to the men,

[29] "Come, see a man who told me all things that I did. Can this be the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧?"

[30] They went out of the city and were coming to him.

[31] In the meantime the disciples were requesting him, saying, "Rabbi, eat."

[32] But he said to them, "I have food to eat which you do not know."

[33] So the disciples were saying to one another, "Has anyone brought him anything to eat?"

[34] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "**My food is that I do the will of the one who sent me and perfect his work.**

[35] Do you not say, 'There are yet four months, and the harvest comes'? Behold, I say to you, lift up your eyes and behold the fields, that they are white for harvest. Already

[36] the one who reaps receives a wage and gathers fruit unto eternal life, that the one who sows and the one who reaps may rejoice together.

[37] For in this the word is true, 'One is the one who sows, and another the one who reaps.'

[38] I sent you to reap that on which you have not labored; others have labored, and you have entered into their labor."

[39] Now from that city many of the ⟦samaritans|Shomronim|Samaritans⟧ believed in him because of the word of the woman testifying, "He told me all things that I did."

[40] So when the ⟦samaritans|Shomronim|Samaritans⟧ came to him, they were requesting him to abide with them; and he abode there two days.

[41] And many more believed because of his word,

[42] and they were saying to the woman, "No longer because of your talk do we believe; for we ourselves have heard, and we know that this is truly the Savior of the world."

[43] Now after the two days he went out from there into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧;

[44] for ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ himself testified that a prophet has no honor in his own fatherland.

[45] So when he came into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, the Galileans accepted him, having seen all things that he did in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ at the feast; for they also went to the feast.

[46] So he came again to ⟦cana|Qanah|Cana⟧ of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain royal official whose son was sick in ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧.

[48] So ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Unless you see signs and wonders, you will by no means believe."

[49] The royal official says to him, "Lord, come down before my young child dies."

[50] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "Go; your son lives." The man believed the word which ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, and he was going.

[51] And as he was already going down, his slaves met him, saying that his boy lives.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic name transliteration.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 24) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"baptize" → "immerse"** (v1, 2) — Greek βαπτίζω, "to immerse".
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v29) — Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ ("anointed"); Greek ὁ χριστός.
- **"When therefore the Lord knew" → "So when *Yeshua* knew"** (v1) — Ἰησοῦς SBLGNT NA28 ] κύριος "the Lord" WH Treg NIV RP: a 2–2 tie among the four editions, which NA28 decides. The verse says "*Yeshua*" twice, as the Greek does. "Was making and immersing" is a Greek present (ποιεῖ καὶ βαπτίζει), kept from the direct speech.
- **"(Though Jesus himself baptized not, but his disciples,)" → "although *Yeshua* himself was not immersing, but his disciples"** (v2) — Greek ἐβάπτιζεν, an imperfect; the Greek has no "only". The printed text sets the verse between dashes.
- **"departed again into Galilee" → "went away again into *Galil*"** (v3) — Greek ἀπῆλθεν. πάλιν WH Treg NA28 ] – RP.
- **"he must needs go through" → "he had to pass through"** (v4) — Greek ἔδει, "it was necessary".
- **"Then cometh he" → "So he comes"** (v5, 7, 9, 11, 15–17, 19, 21, 25, 26, 28, 34, 49, 50) — the Greek present in narrative is kept as a present; οὖν is "so".
- **"parcel of ground" → "plot of land"** (v5) — Greek χωρίον.
- **"Jacob's well" → "*Yaaqov's* spring" (v6) but "well" (v11, 12)** — two Greek words: πηγή, a spring (v6 twice, v14), and φρέαρ, a dug well.
- **"being wearied with his journey, sat thus on the well" → "wearied from the journey, was sitting thus at the spring"** (v6) — Greek κεκοπιακὼς ἐκ τῆς ὁδοιπορίας: κοπιάω, the verb rendered "labor" in v38; ἐκαθέζετο is an imperfect; οὕτως "thus", just as he was; ἐπὶ τῇ πηγῇ "at, upon the spring". ὡς WH Treg NA28 ] ὡσεί RP.
- **"Give me to drink" kept** (v7, 10) — Greek Δός μοι πεῖν; no noun "a drink".
- **"(For his disciples were gone away unto the city to buy meat.)" → "For his disciples had gone away into the city, that they might buy food"** (v8) — Greek τροφάς, "foods, provisions". The parentheses here and in v9, 44 are not in the printed Greek.
- **"How is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me, which am a woman of Samaria?" → "How do you, being a *Yehudi*, ask to drink from me, being a *Shomroni* woman?"** (v9) — Greek γυναικὸς Σαμαρίτιδος οὔσης, "being a Samaritan woman": the adjective, as at the beginning of the verse.
- **"have no dealings with" kept** (v9) — Greek οὐ συγχρῶνται, "do not use together with" (χράομαι "use"): no dealings in general, or no sharing of vessels. Neither noun has the article.
- **"Jesus answered and said" kept** (v10, 13, 17) — both Greek verbs.
- **"Sir" → "Lord"** (v11, 15, 19, 49) — Greek Κύριε, the one word of address in the gospels; it is also the ordinary polite address to a man.
- **"thou hast nothing to draw with" kept** (v11) — Greek οὔτε ἄντλημα ἔχεις, "you have not even a drawing vessel". ἡ γυνή Treg NA28 RP ] – WH.
- **"Art thou greater"** (v12) — the question begins with μή and expects the answer "no". "Livestock" is θρέμματα.
- **"shall never thirst" → "will by no means thirst forever"** (v14) — Greek οὐ μὴ διψήσει εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα: the strong negative οὐ μή with "unto the age", together "never". Both parts are kept, as in 8:51–52; 10:28; 11:26.
- **"a well of water springing up into everlasting life" → "a spring of water leaping up unto eternal life"** (v14) — Greek πηγὴ ὕδατος ἁλλομένου εἰς ζωὴν αἰώνιον: πηγή as v6; ἅλλομαι "leap"; αἰώνιος "eternal".
- **"neither come hither to draw" → "nor come through here to draw"** (v15) — διέρχωμαι WH NA28 ] ἔρχωμαι Treg; ἔρχομαι RP ("come").
- **"Jesus saith unto her" → "He says to her"** (v16) — αὐτῇ WH NA28 ] + ὁ Ἰησοῦς Treg RP.
- **"husband" kept** (v16–18) — Greek ἀνήρ, "man, husband"; "he whom you now have is not your man / husband".
- **"Thou hast well said, I have no husband" → "You said well, 'A husband I do not have'"** (v17) — he repeats her words with "husband" put first (Ἄνδρα οὐκ ἔχω).
- **"in that saidst thou truly" → "this you have said truly"** (v18) — Greek τοῦτο ἀληθὲς εἴρηκας, "this, a true thing, you have said".
- **"I perceive" → "I behold"** (v19) — Greek θεωρῶ.
- **"worship" → "bow down (to)"** (v20–24, ten times) — Greek προσκυνέω, "bow down, fall down before, do obeisance", the usual word for homage to God or to a man; "worship" is kept for λατρεύω, as in Hebrews and Mark 5:6. "Worshippers" (v23) is προσκυνηταί, "ones who bow down", only here in the New Testament.
- **"ye say"** (v20–22) — "you" is plural throughout: "you [*Yehudim*] say", "you [*Shomronim*] will bow down", against "we". "One must" is δεῖ; the Greek has no "men".
- **"Woman, believe me, the hour cometh" → "Believe me, woman, that an hour is coming"** (v21, 23) — Πίστευέ μοι, γύναι WH Treg NA28 ] Γύναι, πίστευέ μοι RP. ὥρα has no article.
- **"salvation is of the Jews" → "salvation is from the *Yehudim*"** (v22) — Greek ἡ σωτηρία ἐκ τῶν Ἰουδαίων ἐστίν; "salvation" has the article.
- **"for the Father seeketh such to worship him" → "for the Father also seeks such to bow down to him"** (v23) — Greek καὶ γὰρ ὁ πατὴρ τοιούτους ζητεῖ τοὺς προσκυνοῦντας αὐτόν, "seeks such as the ones bowing down to him". "In spirit and truth": one preposition, no articles.
- **"God is a Spirit" → "*Elohim* is spirit"** (v24) — Greek πνεῦμα ὁ θεός: "spirit" stands first, without the article and without a verb, as what is said of *Elohim* (the form of 1:1, "the Word was *Elohim*"). English must choose between "spirit", "a spirit" and "Spirit"; the Greek does not.
- **"Messias cometh, which is called Christ" → "*Mashiach* is coming, the one called Christ"** (v25) — Greek Μεσσίας (no article), the Hebrew / Aramaic word in Greek letters, as 1:41, and ὁ λεγόμενος χριστός, its Greek equivalent. "That one" is ἐκεῖνος; "announce" ἀναγγελεῖ.
- **"I that speak unto thee am he" → "I am, the one speaking to you"** (v26) — Greek Ἐγώ εἰμι, ὁ λαλῶν σοι. The Greek has no "he"; the same two words stand alone in 8:24, 28, 58. Here they answer v25 and can be taken as "I am he".
- **"And upon this came his disciples" kept; "marvelled" → "were marveling"** (v27) — ἐθαύμαζον WH Treg NA28 ] ἐθαύμασαν RP. "With a woman" has no article. τί λαλεῖς is "why do you speak" or "what do you speak".
- **"saith to the men" → "says to the men"** (v28) — Greek τοῖς ἀνθρώποις, "the people".
- **"all things that ever I did" → "all things that I did"** (v29, 39) — the Greek has no "ever". ὅσα Treg NA28 RP ] ἅ WH (v29); ἅ WH Treg NA28 ] ὅσα RP (v39).
- **"is not this the Christ?" → "Can this be the *Mashiach*?"** (v29) — Greek μήτι, which marks a hesitant question, leaning to "no".
- **"prayed him" → "were requesting him"** (v31, 40, 47) — Greek ἐρωτάω, imperfects; "request" of asking for something ("ask" is αἰτέω, v9–10).
- **"meat" → "food"** (v32, 34) — two Greek words: βρῶσις (v32), "eating, food", and βρῶμα (v34), "food". "Which you do not know" has no "of".
- **"Hath any man brought him ought to eat?"** (v33) — the question begins with μή and expects "no"; "anything" is supplied.
- **"to do the will … and to finish his work" → "that I do the will … and perfect his work"** (v34) — Greek ἵνα ποιήσω (WH Treg NA28; ποιῶ RP) … καὶ τελειώσω: τελειόω "perfect, bring to its end", as 1 John.
- **"look on the fields; for they are white already to harvest. And he that reapeth" → "behold the fields, that they are white for harvest. Already the one who reaps"** (v35–36) — Greek θεάσασθε "behold" (ἰδού "Behold" before it is another word); χώρας, "lands, fields". ἤδη "already" stands at the end of v35; the printed text (SBLGNT) puts the stop before it and joins it to v36. The other punctuation joins it to "white for harvest" (so the KJV). ὁ WH Treg NA28 ] Καὶ ὁ RP.
- **"receiveth wages, and gathereth fruit unto life eternal" → "receives a wage and gathers fruit unto eternal life"** (v36) — Greek μισθόν, καρπόν. ἵνα WH Treg NA28 ] + καί RP.
- **"herein is that saying true" → "in this the word is true"** (v37) — Greek ὁ λόγος … ἀληθινός.
- **"whereon ye bestowed no labour" → "that on which you have not labored"** (v38) — Greek κεκοπιάκατε, κεκοπιάκασιν, κόπον: one root, "labor".
- **"for the saying of the woman, which testified" → "because of the word of the woman testifying"** (v39) — Greek διὰ τὸν λόγον τῆς γυναικὸς μαρτυρούσης.
- **"that he would tarry with them: and he abode there" → "to abide with them; and he abode there"** (v40) — Greek μεῖναι, ἔμεινεν, one verb.
- **"not because of thy saying" → "No longer because of your talk"** (v42) — Greek λαλιάν, "talk, speech", not λόγος (v39, 41); οὐκέτι "no longer".
- **"the Christ, the Saviour of the world" → "the Savior of the world"** (v42) — κόσμου WH Treg NA28 ] + ὁ χριστός RP. ἀληθῶς "truly".
- **"departed thence, and went into Galilee" → "went out from there into *Galil*"** (v43) — ἐκεῖθεν WH Treg NA28 ] + καὶ ἀπῆλθεν RP.
- **"in his own country" → "in his own fatherland"** (v44) — Greek πατρίδι, as Heb 11:14. "Testified" is ἐμαρτύρησεν, an aorist.
- **"received him" → "accepted him"** (v45) — Greek ἐδέξαντο, δέχομαι.
- **"nobleman" → "royal official"** (v46, 49) — Greek βασιλικός, "royal": a man in the king's service, or of the king's family.
- **"where he made the water wine" kept** (v46) — Greek ἐποίησεν τὸ ὕδωρ οἶνον. "In *Kfar Nachum*" stands last in the Greek and goes with "was sick" or with "there was". "Sick" is ἠσθένει.
- **"besought him that he would come down" → "was requesting that he come down"** (v47) — ἠρώτα WH Treg NA28 ] + αὐτόν RP. "At the point of death" is ἤμελλεν ἀποθνῄσκειν, "was about to die"; "heal" is ἰάομαι.
- **"ye will not believe" → "you will by no means believe"** (v48) — Greek οὐ μὴ πιστεύσητε; "you" is plural, though he speaks to the one man.
- **"my child" → "my young child"** (v49) — Greek τὸ παιδίον; "son" (υἱός) in v46, 47, 50, 53, and "boy" (παῖς) in v51.
- **"thy son liveth" → "your son lives"** (v50, 51, 53) — Greek ζῇ, a present; not "will live". "Go" is Πορεύου; "he was going" ἐπορεύετο, an imperfect.
- **"his servants met him, and told him, saying, Thy son liveth" → "his slaves met him, saying that his boy lives"** (v51) — Greek οἱ δοῦλοι; αὐτῷ WH NA28 ] + καὶ ἀπήγγειλαν "and reported" Treg RP; ὁ παῖς αὐτοῦ WH Treg NA28 ] σου "your" RP.
- **"when he began to amend" → "in which he got better"** (v52) — Greek κομψότερον ἔσχεν, "had it better". "The fever left him" is ἀφῆκεν.
- **"at the same hour" → "in that hour"** (v53) — ὅτι WH ] + ἐν Treg NA28 RP: the verse follows "in".
- **"This is again the second miracle that Jesus did" → "Now this again, a second sign, *Yeshua* did"** (v54) — Greek τοῦτο δὲ πάλιν δεύτερον σημεῖον ἐποίησεν; σημεῖον is "sign" (2:11).

### Notes
- **v5** — the plot of land: Gen 33:19; 48:22; Josh 24:32.
- **v6, 52** — the sixth hour counted from sunrise is about noon, the seventh about one in the afternoon.
- **v20** — "this mountain" is Mount Gerizim.
