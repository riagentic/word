# Revelation 6

[1] And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying, as with a voice of thunder, "Come!"

[2] And I saw, and behold, a white horse, and the one sitting on it having a bow; and a crown was given to him, and he went out overcoming and that he might overcome.

[3] And when he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, "Come!"

[4] And another horse went out, fiery red; and to the one sitting on it, to him it was given to take peace from the earth, and that they shall slaughter one another; and a great sword was given to him.

[5] And when he opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, "Come!" And I saw, and behold, a black horse, and the one sitting on it having a balance in his hand.

[6] And I heard as it were a voice in the midst of the four living creatures saying, "A ⟦choinix|choinix|quart⟧ of wheat for a denarius, and three ⟦choinix|choinikes|quarts⟧ of barley for a denarius; and the oil and the wine do not harm."

[7] And when he opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature saying, "Come!"

[8] And I saw, and behold, a pale horse, and the one sitting upon it, his name was Death, and Hades was following with him; and authority was given to them over the fourth part of the earth, to kill with sword and with famine and with death, and by the beasts of the earth.

[9] And when he opened the fifth seal, I saw underneath the altar the souls of those slaughtered because of the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and because of the testimony which they had.

[10] And they cried out with a loud voice, saying, "How long, Master, holy and true, do you not judge and avenge our blood on those who dwell on the earth?"

[11] And a white robe was given to them, to each one, and it was said to them that they shall rest yet a little time, until their fellow slaves also and their brothers should be fulfilled, those who are about to be killed as they also were.

[12] And I saw when he opened the sixth seal, and a great earthquake took place, and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the whole moon became as blood,

[13] and the stars of heaven fell to the earth, as a fig tree casts its unripe figs when shaken by a great wind,

[14] and the heaven was parted as a book being rolled up, and every mountain and island were moved out of their places.

[15] And the kings of the earth and the great men and the chiliarchs and the rich and the strong and every slave and free man hid themselves in the caves and in the rocks of the mountains;

[16] and they say to the mountains and to the rocks, "**Fall on us and hide us from the face of the one sitting on the throne and from the wrath of the Lamb**,

[17] because the great day of their wrath has come, and who is able to stand?"

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — systematic.
- **"And" kept at the head of the sentences** (throughout) — Greek καί *kai* opens almost every sentence of the book; it is not turned into "now", "then", "when" or dropped.
- **"as it were the noise of thunder" → "as with a voice of thunder"** (v1) — Greek ὡς φωνῇ βροντῆς as printed (dative); other editions accent φωνή (nominative), which stands outside the grammar of the sentence: "saying — as a voice of thunder". φωνή is "voice" throughout.
- **"Come and see" → "Come!"** (v1, 3, 5, 7) — Greek Ἔρχου alone (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); "+ and see" is the Byzantine text (RP). The same word means "go"; to whom it is said (the horseman or John) the Greek does not say.
- **"he that sat on him had a bow" → "the one sitting on it having a bow"** (v2, 5) — Greek ὁ καθήμενος ἐπʼ αὐτὸν ἔχων: a participle, no finite verb; "rider" is not in the Greek.
- **"crown" kept** (v2) — Greek στέφανος *stephanos*, the victor's wreath, not the royal diadem (διάδημα, 12:3; 19:12).
- **"conquering, and to conquer" → "overcoming and that he might overcome"** (v2) — Greek νικῶν καὶ ἵνα νικήσῃ; νικάω is "overcome" throughout (2:7; 5:5; 1 John 5:4).
- **"red" → "fiery red"** (v4) — Greek πυρρός *pyrros*, from πῦρ "fire"; as 12:3.
- **"power was given to him that sat thereon to take peace" → "to the one sitting on it, to him it was given to take peace"** (v4) — Greek τῷ καθημένῳ … ἐδόθη αὐτῷ: the pronoun repeats the dative, a Semitic turn kept as it stands; no "power" and no "permitted" in the Greek.
- **"that they should kill one another" → "that they shall slaughter one another"** (v4) — Greek ἵνα … σφάξουσιν, a future indicative after ἵνα (against normal grammar; RP has the subjunctive σφάξωσιν). σφάζω *sphazō* "slaughter", the verb of the Lamb in 5:6 and of the souls in v9; "kill" (v8, 11) is ἀποκτείνω. No subject is expressed ("people").
- **"sword"** (v4, 8) — two Greek words: μάχαιρα *machaira* in v4, ῥομφαία *rhomphaia* (the large broad sword) in v8.
- **"a pair of balances" → "a balance"** (v5) — Greek ζυγός *zygos*, the beam of a scale (elsewhere "yoke").
- **"A measure of wheat for a penny, and three measures of barley for a penny" → "A choinix of wheat for a denarius, and three choinikes of barley for a denarius"** (v6) — Greek χοῖνιξ … τρεῖς χοίνικες *choinix … treis choinikes*: the same dry measure (≈ 1.1 L, a day's grain ration) for both grains; δηνάριον *dēnarion*, the Roman silver coin, a labourer's wage for a day (Matt 20:2); original units kept.
- **"see thou hurt not the oil and the wine" → "the oil and the wine do not harm"** (v6) — Greek word order kept; ἀδικέω *adikeō*, elsewhere "wrong", is "harm" in this book (7:2–3; 9:4, 10, 19; 11:5).
- **"pale" kept** (v8) — Greek χλωρός *chlōros*, the word for "green" in 8:7; 9:4; of a horse the yellowish-green pallor of sickness or of a corpse.
- **"his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him" → "the one sitting upon it, his name was Death, and Hades was following with him"** (v8) — Greek ὁ καθήμενος ἐπάνω αὐτοῦ ὄνομα αὐτῷ ὁ Θάνατος: the nominative is left hanging and taken up by "his" (the grammar is broken in the Greek); ἠκολούθει μετʼ αὐτοῦ, imperfect, "with him" (RP: αὐτῷ "followed him"). ᾅδης is Hades, not "hell".
- **"power was given unto them" → "authority was given to them"** (v8) — Greek ἐξουσία; αὐτοῖς "to them" (RP: αὐτῷ "to him").
- **"with hunger, and with death, and with the beasts of the earth" → "with famine and with death, and by the beasts of the earth"** (v8) — Greek ἐν θανάτῳ, "with death", the same word as the horseman's name; the Greek Old Testament uses θάνατος for the Hebrew דֶּבֶר "pestilence" (Ezek 14:21, which this verse echoes), so "pestilence" may be meant, but the word is "death". θηρία *thēria* "beasts"; "wild" is not a separate word. The last member changes to ὑπό "by".
- **"slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held" → "slaughtered because of the word of *Elohim* and because of the testimony which they had"** (v9) — Greek ἐσφαγμένων (σφάζω, as v4); διά with the accusative, "because of"; ἣν εἶχον "which they had". RP adds "of the Lamb" after "testimony".
- **"How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge" → "How long, Master, holy and true, do you not judge"** (v10) — Greek ὁ δεσπότης *ho despotēs* "the Master" (a nominative used as an address), the owner's title over slaves (v11 "fellow slaves"), not κύριος "Lord"; οὐ κρίνεις καὶ ἐκδικεῖς are present tenses with "not".
- **"white robes were given unto every one of them" → "a white robe was given to them, to each one"** (v11) — Greek ἐδόθη αὐτοῖς ἑκάστῳ στολὴ λευκή, singular.
- **"rest yet for a little season, until their fellowservants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they were, should be fulfilled" → "rest yet a little time, until their fellow slaves also and their brothers should be fulfilled, those who are about to be killed as they also were"** (v11) — Greek χρόνον μικρόν "a little time"; σύνδουλοι "fellow slaves"; ἕως πληρωθῶσιν "until they should be fulfilled / made complete" (SBLGNT, WH, NA28; Treg and RP read πληρώσωσιν "should fulfil", that is, complete their course). "The number of" is not in the Greek. ἵνα ἀναπαύσονται is again a future indicative after ἵνα.
- **"and, lo, there was a great earthquake" → "and a great earthquake took place"** (v12) — no "behold" in the Greek here; ἐγένετο.
- **"the moon became as blood" → "the whole moon became as blood"** (v12) — Greek ἡ σελήνη ὅλη.
- **"untimely figs" → "unripe figs"** (v13) — Greek ὄλυνθοι *olynthoi*, the late figs that do not ripen and fall in winter; "a mighty wind" → "a great wind" (μέγας).
- **"the heaven departed as a scroll when it is rolled together" → "the heaven was parted as a book being rolled up"** (v14) — Greek ἀπεχωρίσθη, passive of ἀποχωρίζω "separate" (Acts 15:39); βιβλίον *biblion* is "book" throughout (5:1; 10:8), here plainly a scroll.
- **"the great men … the chief captains, and the mighty men" → "the great men and the chiliarchs … and the strong"** (v15) — Greek μεγιστᾶνες; χιλίαρχοι *chiliarchoi*, commanders of a thousand (as John 18:12; Acts 21:31); ἰσχυροί.
- **"every bondman, and every free man" → "every slave and free man"** (v15) — Greek πᾶς δοῦλος καὶ ἐλεύθερος.
- **"in the dens and in the rocks" → "in the caves and in the rocks"** (v15) — Greek εἰς τὰ σπήλαια καὶ εἰς τὰς πέτρας, literally "into".
- **"and said" → "and they say"** (v16) — Greek λέγουσιν, present tense.
- **"the great day of his wrath" → "the great day of their wrath"** (v17) — Greek αὐτῶν "their" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); RP reads αὐτοῦ "his".

### Notes
- **v10** — ἐκδικεῖς … ἐκ τῶν κατοικούντων, literally "avenge our blood from those who dwell on the earth".
