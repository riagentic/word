# Revelation 1

[1] The revelation of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave him to show to his slaves the things that must come to pass shortly, and he signified it, sending through his angel to his slave ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧,

[2] who testified to the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to the testimony of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, all that he saw.

[3] Blessed is the one who reads and those who hear the words of the prophecy and keep the things written in it, for the time is near.

[4] ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ to the seven churches that are in Asia: Grace to you and peace from **the one who is and the one who was and the one who is coming**, and from the seven spirits which are before his throne,

[5] and from ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To the one who loves us and loosed us from our sins in his blood —

[6] and he made us a kingdom, priests to his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father — to him be the glory and the might unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

[7] Behold, he is coming with the clouds,
and every eye will see him,
and those who pierced him,
and all the tribes of the earth will wail over him.
Yes, amen.

[8] "**I am the Alpha and the Omega,**" says the Lord ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "the one who is and the one who was and the one who is coming, the Almighty."

[9] I, ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, your brother and fellow sharer in the affliction and kingdom and endurance in ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, came to be on the island called Patmos because of the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the testimony of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[10] I came to be in the Spirit on the Lord's day, and I heard behind me a loud voice as of a trumpet

[11] saying, "What you see, write in a book and send to the seven churches: to ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧ and to Smyrna and to ⟦pergamum|Pergamon|Pergamum⟧ and to ⟦thyatira|Thyateira|Thyatira⟧ and to ⟦sardis|Sardeis|Sardis⟧ and to ⟦philadelphia|Philadelpheia|Philadelphia⟧ and to ⟦laodicea|Laodikeia|Laodicea⟧."

[12] And I turned to see the voice that was speaking with me; and having turned I saw seven golden lampstands,

[13] and in the midst of the lampstands one like a son of man, clothed in a robe reaching to the feet and girded at the breasts with a golden belt;

[14] and his head and his hair were white as white wool, as snow, and his eyes as a flame of fire,

[15] and his feet like burnished bronze, as when refined in a furnace, and his voice as the voice of many waters,

[16] and having in his right hand seven stars, and out of his mouth a sharp two-edged sword going forth, and his face as the sun shines in its power.

[17] And when I saw him, I fell at his feet as dead; and he placed his right hand on me, saying, "**Do not fear; I am the first and the last,**

**[18] and the living one — and I became dead, and behold, I am living unto the ages of the ages — and I have the keys of death and of Hades.**

[19] Write therefore the things that you saw, and the things that are, and the things that are about to come to pass after these things.

[20] The mystery of the seven stars that you saw upon my right hand, and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are seven churches."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 2, 5) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v9) — systematic.
- **"the Revelation of Jesus Christ" → "The revelation of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1) — Greek ἀποκάλυψις *apokalypsis* = "unveiling / disclosure / revelation". The genitive is plain: it can be the revelation he gives or the revelation about him.
- **"servants" / "servant" → "slaves" / "slave"** (v1) — Greek δοῦλος *doulos*.
- **"shortly come to pass" → "come to pass shortly"** (v1) — Greek ἐν τάχει, "in quickness": shortly, or speedily once it begins.
- **"he sent and signified it by his angel" → "he signified it, sending through his angel"** (v1) — Greek ἐσήμανεν ἀποστείλας διὰ τοῦ ἀγγέλου; σημαίνω is "to make known by a sign"; "it" is supplied for English.
- **"bare record of" → "testified to"** (v2) — Greek ἐμαρτύρησεν with a plain accusative; "testimony" (μαρτυρία) is the same root. "The testimony of *Yeshua*" (v2, 9) is a plain genitive: the testimony he gave, or the testimony about him.
- **"he that readeth" → "the one who reads"** (v3) — Greek ὁ ἀναγινώσκων, one reader and many hearers: the reading aloud in the assembly. The verse has one "blessed" and says "the prophecy", not "this prophecy".
- **"from him which is, and which was, and which is to come" → "from the one who is and the one who was and the one who is coming"** (v4, 8) — Greek ἀπὸ ὁ ὢν καὶ ὁ ἦν καὶ ὁ ἐρχόμενος. The Greek breaks its own grammar: ἀπό takes the genitive, but the title stands in the nominative, unchanged, as a name (cf. Exod 3:14 LXX ὁ ὤν); ὁ ἦν puts the article before a finite verb, "the 'he was'". English "the one" has no case, so the title reads the same in v4 and v8. The last member is "the one coming" (ἐρχόμενος), not "the one who will be".
- **"the seven Spirits which are before his throne" → "the seven spirits which are before his throne"** (v4) — Greek πνευμάτων ἅ, a neuter relative (the gender is grammatical); the Greek has no verb here.
- **"the faithful witness, and the first begotten of the dead, and the prince of the kings of the earth" → "the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead and the ruler of the kings of the earth"** (v5) — in Greek the three titles stand in the nominative after the genitive "from Jesus Christ": again the grammar is broken and the titles are left unchanged. English does not show case.
- **"unto him that loved us, and washed us from our sins in his own blood" → "To the one who loves us and loosed us from our sins in his blood"** (v5) — ἀγαπῶντι is present, "loves"; λύσαντι "loosed" WH Treg NA28, λούσαντι "washed" RP (one letter apart); ἐκ "out of" WH Treg NA28, ἀπό RP; ἐν τῷ αἵματι "in his blood" (ἐν may be "by").
- **"and hath made us kings and priests" → "and he made us a kingdom, priests"** (v6) — Greek βασιλείαν, ἱερεῖς (Exod 19:6). The sentence changes construction: after two participles ("who loves … and loosed") comes a finite verb, then "to him"; the dashes keep that. "Be" is supplied; "of the ages" (τῶν αἰώνων) Treg NA28 RP, lacking in WH.
- **"dominion" → "might"** (v6) — Greek κράτος.
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v6, 18) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων.
- **"and they also which pierced him" → "and those who pierced him"** (v7) — Greek καὶ οἵτινες; the καί may be "and" or "even".
- **"all kindreds of the earth shall wail because of him" → "all the tribes of the earth will wail over him"** (v7) — Greek κόψονται ἐπʼ αὐτόν, to beat the breast in lament; φυλαί "tribes". "Even so" → "Yes" (ναί).
- **"Alpha and Omega" → "the Alpha and the Omega"** (v8) — Greek τὸ Ἄλφα καὶ τὸ Ὦ: the second letter is written as the letter itself (Ō), not by its later name. KJV "the beginning and the ending" here and "I am Alpha and Omega, the first and the last: and" in v11 are not in the text of the editions.
- **"companion in tribulation, and in the kingdom and patience of Jesus Christ" → "fellow sharer in the affliction and kingdom and endurance in *Yeshua*"** (v9) — Greek συγκοινωνός; one article joins the three nouns θλῖψις, βασιλεία, ὑπομονή; ἐν Ἰησοῦ "in Jesus" WH Treg NA28 (RP "in Christ Jesus").
- **"was in the isle" → "came to be on the island"**, **"I was in the Spirit" → "I came to be in the Spirit"** (v9, 10) — Greek ἐγενόμην, "I came to be", not ἤμην "I was"; so also v18 "I became dead". ἐν πνεύματι has no article: "in spirit" or "in the Spirit".
- **"a great voice, as of a trumpet, saying" → "a loud voice as of a trumpet saying"** (v10–11) — Greek φωνὴν μεγάλην ὡς σάλπιγγος λεγούσης: "saying" agrees with "trumpet", not with "voice".
- **"What thou seest, write in a book, and send it unto the seven churches" → "What you see, write in a book and send to the seven churches"** (v11) — Greek βιβλίον, a book in roll form; "it" is not in the Greek. Every city has its own "and to".
- **"like unto the Son of man" → "like a son of man"** (v13) — Greek ὅμοιον υἱὸν ἀνθρώπου, without the article (Dan 7:13); "of the lampstands" WH NA28, "of the seven lampstands" Treg RP; υἱόν is accusative where ὅμοιος takes the dative (WH NA28; Treg RP υἱῷ).
- **"a garment down to the foot, and girt about the paps with a golden girdle" → "a robe reaching to the feet and girded at the breasts with a golden belt"** (v13) — Greek ποδήρη; πρὸς τοῖς μαστοῖς.
- **"His head and his hairs" kept** (v14) — Greek ἡ κεφαλὴ αὐτοῦ καὶ αἱ τρίχες, "his head and the hairs", not "the hairs of his head". ὡς is rendered "as" and ὅμοιος "like" throughout.
- **"like unto fine brass, as if they burned in a furnace" → "like burnished bronze, as when refined in a furnace"** (v15) — Greek χαλκολιβάνῳ, a word found only here and in 2:18; its exact meaning is not known (a bronze or brass alloy). πεπυρωμένης "made to glow, refined by fire" (WH Treg NA28) is a feminine genitive that agrees with no noun in the sentence; RP πεπυρωμένοι agrees with "feet".
- **"the sound of many waters" → "the voice of many waters"** (v15) — Greek φωνή, the same word as "his voice".
- **"And he had in his right hand" → "and having in his right hand"** (v16) — Greek ἔχων, a participle; the sentence of v12–16 has no finite verb after "I saw". "Countenance" → "face" (ὄψις, also "appearance"); "in his strength" → "in its power" (δύναμις).
- **"I am he that liveth, and was dead" → "and the living one — and I became dead"** (v18) — Greek καὶ ὁ ζῶν, καὶ ἐγενόμην νεκρός; ζῶν εἰμι "I am living". "Amen" after "ages" is RP only. "Hell" → "Hades" (ᾅδης); "death" and "Hades" each have the article.
- **"the things which shall be hereafter" → "the things that are about to come to pass after these things"** (v19) — Greek ἃ μέλλει γίνεσθαι μετὰ ταῦτα (γίνεσθαι WH Treg RP, γενέσθαι NA28; no difference in English).
- **"in my right hand" → "upon my right hand"** (v20) — Greek ἐπὶ τῆς δεξιᾶς μου (v16 ἐν "in"). "The seven golden lampstands" is accusative, loosely joined to "the mystery of the seven stars". "Angels" and the second "seven churches" have no article. ἄγγελος is "angel" or "messenger".

### Notes
- **v7** echoes Dan 7:13 + Zech 12:10.
- **v13-16** echoes Dan 7:13; 10:5-6.
