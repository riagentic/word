# Revelation 13

[1] And I saw a beast coming up out of the sea, having ten horns and seven heads, and on its horns ten diadems, and on its heads names of blasphemy.

[2] And the beast which I saw was like a leopard, and its feet as a bear's, and its mouth as a lion's mouth. And the dragon gave to it his power and his throne and great authority.

[3] And one of its heads as slaughtered unto death, and the stroke of its death was healed. And the whole earth marveled after the beast,

[4] and they bowed down to the dragon, because he gave the authority to the beast, and they bowed down to the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast, and who is able to war with it?"

[5] And there was given to it a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies, and there was given to it authority to act forty and two months.

[6] And it opened its mouth unto blasphemies against ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to blaspheme his name and his tent, those tenting in heaven.

[7] And it was given to it to make war with the saints and to overcome them, and there was given to it authority over every tribe and people and tongue and nation.

[8] And all those who dwell on the earth will bow down to him, whose name is not written in the book of life of the Lamb who has been slaughtered, from the foundation of the world.

[9] If anyone has an ear, let him hear.

[10] If anyone is for captivity,
into captivity he goes;
if anyone is to be killed with the sword,
he is with the sword to be killed.

Here is the endurance and the faith of the saints.

[11] And I saw another beast coming up out of the earth, and it had two horns like a lamb, and it was speaking as a dragon.

[12] And all the authority of the first beast it exercises before it. And it makes the earth and those who dwell in it that they shall bow down to the first beast, whose stroke of death was healed.

[13] And it does great signs, that it should even make fire come down out of heaven to the earth before men.

[14] And it deceives those who dwell on the earth because of the signs which it was given to it to do before the beast, saying to those who dwell on the earth to make an image to the beast, who has the stroke of the sword and lived.

[15] And it was given to it to give spirit to the image of the beast, that the image of the beast should even speak, and should make that as many as do not bow down to the image of the beast be killed.

[16] And it makes all, the small and the great, and the rich and the poor, and the free and the slaves, that they should give them a mark on their right hand or on their forehead,

[17] and that no one should be able to buy or to sell except the one having the mark, the name of the beast or the number of its name.

[18] Here is wisdom: let the one having a mind count the number of the beast, for it is a number of a man; and its number is **six hundred sixty-six**.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6) — systematic.
- **"And" kept at the head of the sentences** (throughout) — Greek καί; not turned into "then" or "also".
- **"rise up" → "coming up"** (v1, 11) — Greek ἀναβαῖνον.
- **"the name of blasphemy" → "names of blasphemy"** (v1) — Greek ὀνόματα βλασφημίας, a plural noun with a genitive (ὀνόματα WH Treg NA28 RP); not the adjective "blasphemous".
- **"him" / "his" → "it" / "its"** (throughout) — Greek θηρίον "beast" is a neuter noun. Twice the Greek itself goes over to the masculine: αὐτόν "him" (v8) and ὃς ἔχει "who has" (v14). Both are kept as "him" and "who".
- **"like unto a leopard … as the feet of a bear … as the mouth of a lion" → "like a leopard … as a bear's … as a lion's mouth"** (v2) — Greek ὅμοιον "like" once, then ὡς "as" twice.
- **"his seat" → "his throne"** (v2) — Greek θρόνος.
- **"And I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed" → "And one of its heads as slaughtered unto death, and the stroke of its death was healed"** (v3) — Greek καὶ μίαν ἐκ τῶν κεφαλῶν αὐτοῦ ὡς ἐσφαγμένην εἰς θάνατον: an accusative without a verb ("I saw" of v1 is not repeated); the sentence is left as it stands. ἐσφαγμένην "slaughtered" is the word used of the Lamb (v8; 5:6). ἡ πληγὴ τοῦ θανάτου αὐτοῦ "the stroke of its death": πληγή is the word rendered "plague" in chapters 15–16; here and in v12, 14 it is a blow, so "stroke".
- **"all the world wondered after the beast" → "the whole earth marveled after the beast"** (v3) — Greek ὅλη ἡ γῆ "the whole earth"; ἐθαυμάσθη ὀπίσω "marveled after", a passive form.
- **"worshipped" → "bowed down to"** (v4, 8, 12, 15) — Greek προσκυνέω.
- **"which gave power unto the beast … who is able to make war with him?" → "because he gave the authority to the beast … who is able to war with it?"** (v4) — Greek ὅτι ἔδωκεν "because he gave" (WH Treg NA28; τῷ δεδωκότι "who had given" RP); ἐξουσία "authority"; πολεμῆσαι "to war".
- **"power was given unto him to continue forty and two months" → "there was given to it authority to act forty and two months"** (v5) — Greek ἐξουσία ποιῆσαι: the verb is "do, make"; it can mean "to act" or "to spend" the months. "Forty and two": τεσσεράκοντα καὶ δύο WH NA28; without καί ("forty-two") Treg RP.
- **"he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God" → "it opened its mouth unto blasphemies against *Elohim*"** (v6) — Greek εἰς βλασφημίας, plural (WH Treg NA28; singular RP).
- **"his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven" → "his tent, those tenting in heaven"** (v6) — Greek τὴν σκηνὴν αὐτοῦ, τοὺς ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ σκηνοῦντας: noun and verb of one root, and no "and" between them, so that "those tenting in heaven" stands in apposition to "his tent".
- **"overcome" kept; "power … over all kindreds, and tongues, and nations" → "authority over every tribe and people and tongue and nation"** (v7) — Greek νικῆσαι; ἐξουσία; four nouns, each singular.
- **"all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world" → "all those who dwell on the earth will bow down to him, whose name is not written in the book of life of the Lamb who has been slaughtered, from the foundation of the world"** (v8) — the Greek word order is kept: ἐν τῷ βιβλίῳ τῆς ζωῆς τοῦ ἀρνίου τοῦ ἐσφαγμένου ἀπὸ καταβολῆς κόσμου. "From the foundation of the world" can attach either way: (a) to "slaughtered", the Lamb slaughtered from the foundation of the world; (b) to "written", the name not written from the foundation of the world (so 17:8, where the Lamb is not named). The verse does not decide. οὗ … τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ "whose name", singular, after the plural "all" (WH Treg NA28; ὧν "whose", plural, RP): the break in number is kept.
- **"If any man have an ear" → "If anyone has an ear"** (v9) — Greek εἴ τις ἔχει οὖς.
- **"He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword" → "If anyone is for captivity, into captivity he goes; if anyone is to be killed with the sword, he is with the sword to be killed"** (v10) — Greek εἴ τις εἰς αἰχμαλωσίαν, εἰς αἰχμαλωσίαν ὑπάγει: no verb in the first clause ("is" supplied). Second half: εἴ τις ἐν μαχαίρῃ ἀποκτανθῆναι αὐτὸν ἐν μαχαίρῃ ἀποκτανθῆναι, two passive infinitives without a finite verb (NA28); ἀποκτενεῖ δεῖ, "if anyone will kill with the sword, he must be killed with the sword" (WH Treg RP). First half: εἰς αἰχμαλωσίαν, εἰς αἰχμαλωσίαν WH NA28; εἰς αἰχμαλωσίαν once Treg; ἔχει αἰχμαλωσίαν RP.
- **"Here is the patience and the faith of the saints" → "Here is the endurance and the faith of the saints"** (v10) — Greek ὑπομονή "endurance"; no "call for" in the Greek.
- **"he spake as a dragon" → "it was speaking as a dragon"** (v11) — Greek ἐλάλει, an imperfect; ὅμοια ἀρνίῳ "like a lamb", ὡς δράκων "as a dragon".
- **"he exerciseth all the power of the first beast before him, and causeth the earth and them which dwell therein to worship" → "all the authority of the first beast it exercises before it. And it makes the earth and those who dwell in it that they shall bow down"** (v12) — Greek ποιεῖ "does, makes", the verb that runs through v12–16 (exercises authority, makes the earth, does signs, makes fire come down, make an image, makes all); "exercises" only here, since English does not "do" authority. ἵνα προσκυνήσουσιν: ἵνα with a future indicative, against the usual grammar (WH Treg NA28; subjunctive RP).
- **"so that he maketh fire come down from heaven" → "that it should even make fire come down out of heaven"** (v13) — Greek ἵνα καὶ πῦρ ποιῇ … καταβαίνειν.
- **"by the means of those miracles which he had power to do in the sight of the beast" → "because of the signs which it was given to it to do before the beast"** (v14) — Greek διὰ τὰ σημεῖα, διά with the accusative; σημεῖα "signs"; ἐδόθη "was given"; ἐνώπιον "before".
- **"the beast, which had the wound by a sword, and did live" → "the beast, who has the stroke of the sword and lived"** (v14) — Greek ὃς ἔχει, a masculine relative and a present tense (WH Treg NA28; ὃ εἶχεν "which had" RP); ἔζησεν "lived" or "came to life". λέγων "saying" is also masculine, though "beast" is neuter.
- **"he had power to give life unto the image" → "it was given to it to give spirit to the image"** (v15) — Greek δοῦναι πνεῦμα, "spirit" or "breath"; not ζωή "life". αὐτῷ Treg NA28 RP ] αὐτῇ WH.
- **"cause that as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed" → "should make that as many as do not bow down to the image of the beast be killed"** (v15) — Greek καὶ ποιήσῃ ἵνα …: the subject of "make" can be the image or the second beast.
- **"he causeth all … to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads" → "it makes all … that they should give them a mark on their right hand or on their forehead"** (v16) — Greek ἵνα δῶσιν αὐτοῖς "that they should give them", with no named subject; χάραγμα "mark", something stamped or engraved; μέτωπον singular; δούλους "slaves".
- **"or the name of the beast" → "the name of the beast"** (v17) — Greek τὸ χάραγμα, τὸ ὄνομα τοῦ θηρίου: the name stands in apposition to the mark, without "or".
- **"Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man" → "let the one having a mind count the number of the beast, for it is a number of a man"** (v18) — Greek νοῦν "mind"; ψηφισάτω "count, reckon" (from ψῆφος, a pebble used in counting); ἀριθμὸς ἀνθρώπου without articles. αὐτοῦ can be "its" (the beast) or "his" (the man).
- **"Six hundred threescore and six" → "six hundred sixty-six"** (v18) — written out in words, as the Greek: ἑξακόσιοι ἑξήκοντα ἕξ WH NA28; ἑξακόσια ἑξήκοντα ἕξ RP; χξϛʹ (in numeral letters) Treg.
