# Revelation 10

[1] And I saw another strong angel coming down out of heaven, clothed with a cloud, and the rainbow upon his head, and his face as the sun, and his feet as pillars of fire,

[2] and having in his hand a little book opened. And he set his right foot upon the sea, and the left upon the earth,

[3] and he cried out with a loud voice, just as a lion roars. And when he cried out, the seven thunders spoke their own voices.

[4] And when the seven thunders spoke, I was about to write; and I heard a voice out of heaven saying, "Seal the things which the seven thunders spoke, and do not write them."

[5] And the angel whom I saw standing upon the sea and upon the earth lifted his right hand to heaven,

[6] and swore by the one who lives unto the ages of the ages, who created the heaven and the things in it, and the earth and the things in it, and the sea and the things in it, that there will be time no longer;

[7] but in the days of the voice of the seventh angel, when he is about to sound the trumpet, then the mystery of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was finished, as he preached the gospel to his own slaves the prophets.

[8] And the voice which I heard out of heaven, again speaking with me and saying, "Go, take the book that is opened in the hand of the angel who stands upon the sea and upon the earth."

[9] And I went away to the angel, telling him to give me the little book. And he says to me, "Take and devour it; and it will make your belly bitter, but in your mouth it will be sweet as honey."

[10] And I took the little book out of the hand of the angel and devoured it, and it was in my mouth as honey, sweet; and when I ate it, my belly was made bitter.

[11] And they say to me, "You must prophesy again about peoples and nations and tongues and many kings."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7) — systematic.
- **"another mighty angel" → "another strong angel"** (v1) — Greek ἰσχυρόν (as 5:2; 18:21); "another" is absent in RP.
- **"a rainbow was upon his head" → "the rainbow upon his head"** (v1) — Greek ἡ ἶρις, with the article; no verb in this clause or the next two. "Upon his head" is ἐπὶ τὴν κεφαλήν (SBLGNT, WH, Treg; NA28 and RP have the genitive, same sense).
- **"feet" kept** (v1) — Greek πόδες; "legs" is not the word.
- **"he had in his hand a little book open" → "having in his hand a little book opened"** (v2) — Greek ἔχων, a participle; βιβλαρίδιον *biblaridion* (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28; RP βιβλίον), a diminutive of βιβλίον "book"; ἠνεῳγμένον, perfect passive participle, "having been opened". The book is a small scroll.
- **"on the earth" kept** (v2, 5, 8) — Greek ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς; γῆ is "earth" throughout, here the dry land beside the sea.
- **"as when a lion roareth" → "just as a lion roars"** (v3) — Greek ὥσπερ λέων μυκᾶται.
- **"seven thunders uttered their voices" → "the seven thunders spoke their own voices"** (v3) — Greek ἐλάλησαν … τὰς ἑαυτῶν φωνάς: λαλέω "speak" (three times in v3–4); the thunders have the article.
- **"Seal up those things which the seven thunders uttered, and write them not"** (v4) — Greek σφράγισον, the verb of the seals (7:3); here "seal" = keep closed.
- **"lifted up his hand to heaven" → "lifted his right hand to heaven"** (v5) — Greek τὴν χεῖρα αὐτοῦ τὴν δεξιάν; "right" is not in the KJV.
- **"sware by him that liveth for ever and ever" → "swore by the one who lives unto the ages of the ages"** (v6) — the verse follows ὤμοσεν ἐν τῷ ζῶντι (WH, Treg, NA28: "+ ἐν"); the printed SBLGNT text and RP have ὤμοσεν τῷ ζῶντι without ἐν. Both mean "swore by".
- **"that there should be time no longer" → "that there will be time no longer"** (v6) — Greek ὅτι χρόνος οὐκέτι ἔσται, future indicative: literally "time will be no longer". χρόνος is "time"; the sense usually taken is "no more time will pass", that is, no more delay (v7 "but in the days …"); the word "delay" is not in the Greek.
- **"when he shall begin to sound, the mystery of God should be finished" → "when he is about to sound the trumpet, then the mystery of *Elohim* was finished"** (v7) — Greek ὅταν μέλλῃ σαλπίζειν, καὶ ἐτελέσθη: the main clause begins with καί ("and", here "then"), and its verb is an aorist, "was finished", though the time is future: the Greek speaks of it as already done. τελέω "finish" (as 11:7; John 19:30). "In the days of the voice" is literal (τῆς φωνῆς).
- **"as he hath declared to his servants the prophets" → "as he preached the gospel to his own slaves the prophets"** (v7) — Greek εὐηγγέλισεν, the verb "bring good news, preach the gospel" (active here, with the persons in the accusative); τοὺς ἑαυτοῦ δούλους "his own slaves".
- **"And the voice which I heard from heaven spake unto me again, and said" → "And the voice which I heard out of heaven, again speaking with me and saying"** (v8) — the Greek sentence has no main verb: ἡ φωνή (nominative) is followed by λαλοῦσαν … λέγουσαν (accusative participles; RP has nominatives). The broken grammar is kept.
- **"the little book which is open" → "the book that is opened"** (v8) — Greek τὸ βιβλίον here (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28; RP βιβλιδάριον "little book"); v2, 9, 10 have the diminutive.
- **"I went unto the angel, and said unto him, Give me" → "I went away to the angel, telling him to give me"** (v9) — Greek ἀπῆλθα … λέγων αὐτῷ δοῦναί μοι, an infinitive, not direct speech.
- **"he said" → "he says"** (v9), **"he said unto me" → "they say to me"** (v11) — Greek λέγει, present; λέγουσιν, present plural with no subject named ("I was told" hides the plural).
- **"eat it up" → "devour it"** (v9, 10) — Greek κατάφαγε, κατέφαγον (κατεσθίω, as 11:5; 12:4), a stronger word than ἔφαγον "I ate" at the end of v10.
- **"it was in my mouth sweet as honey" → "it was in my mouth as honey, sweet"** (v10) — Greek word order ὡς μέλι γλυκύ (v9 has γλυκὺ ὡς μέλι).
- **"my belly was bitter" → "my belly was made bitter"** (v10) — Greek ἐπικράνθη, passive of πικραίνω (v9 πικρανεῖ; 8:11); κοιλία "belly".
- **"Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings" → "You must prophesy again about peoples and nations and tongues and many kings"** (v11) — Greek ἐπὶ λαοῖς καὶ ἔθνεσιν καὶ γλώσσαις καὶ βασιλεῦσιν πολλοῖς: ἐπί with the dative, "about, with regard to" (also possible: "against", "before"); πολλοῖς "many" stands last, beside "kings", and may be meant for all four nouns.

### Notes
- **v9-10** echoes Ezek 3:1-3.
- **v6** — the oath echoes Dan 12:7.
