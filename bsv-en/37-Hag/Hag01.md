# Haggai 1

[1] In the second year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king, in the sixth month, on day one of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came by the hand of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet to ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, governor of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and to ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the great priest, saying:

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, saying: This people have said, It is not the time to come, the time for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to be built.

[3] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came by the hand of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet, saying:

[4] Is it a time for you, you yourselves, to dwell in your paneled houses, while this house lies waste?

[5] And now, thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Set your heart on your ways.

[6] You have sown much and brought in little; you eat, and there is not enough to be satisfied; you drink, and there is not enough to be drunk; you clothe yourselves, and there is no warmth for one; and he who earns wages earns wages into a bag pierced through.

[7] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Set your heart on your ways.

[8] Go up the mountain, and bring wood, and build the house, and I will be pleased with it, and I will be glorified, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] You looked for much, and behold, it came to little; and you brought it to the house, and I blew on it. Because of what? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts. Because of my house, which lies waste, while you run each to his own house.

[10] Therefore, over you, the heavens have kept back their dew, and the earth has kept back its produce.

[11] And I called for dryness on the land, and on the mountains, and on the grain, and on the new wine, and on the fresh oil, and on what the ground brings forth, and on man, and on beast, and on all the labor of hands.

[12] And ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, and ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the great priest, and all the remnant of the people listened to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and to the words of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had sent him; and the people feared before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧, the messenger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, said in the message of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to the people, saying: **I am with you**, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ stirred up the spirit of ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, governor of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and the spirit of ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the great priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people; and they came and did work in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[15] on day twenty-four of the month, in the sixth, in the second year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv12, 14) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵיהֶם "their Elohim".
- **Layout** (throughout) — prose, as the Hebrew; the quotation marks are dropped (the Hebrew has none). v13 keeps its bold.
- **"in the first day of the month, came the word of the LORD by Haggai the prophet … the high priest, saying" → "on day one of the month, the word of Yahweh came by the hand of Haggai the prophet … the great priest, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם אֶחָד לַחֹדֶשׁ "on day one of the month" (dates as printed); בְּיַד "by the hand of" (also vv3; 2:1); הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל "the great priest" (as Num 35:25; "high priest", also vv12, 14); לֵאמֹר "saying".
- **"Thus speaketh the LORD of hosts, saying, This people say, The time is not come, the time that the LORD'S house should be built" → "Thus says Yahweh of Hosts, saying: This people have said, It is not the time to come, the time for the house of Yahweh to be built"** (v2) — Hebrew כֹּה אָמַר "Thus says" with לֵאמֹר "saying"; אָמְרוּ "have said" (perfect); לֹא עֶת־בֹּא "it is not the time to come" (literally "not a time of coming"); a reading עַתָּה בָא "now has come" is a conjecture (from memory) and is not followed.
- **"Is it time for you, O ye, to dwell in your cieled houses, and this house lie waste?" → "Is it a time for you, you yourselves, to dwell in your paneled houses, while this house lies waste?"** (v4) — Hebrew לָכֶם אַתֶּם "for you, you yourselves" (no "O"); סְפוּנִים "paneled" (the verb of 1 Kgs 6:9; 7:3, of covering with boards); חָרֵב "lies waste" (also v9), a play on חֹרֶב "dryness" in v11.
- **"Consider your ways" → "Set your heart on your ways"** (vv5, 7) — Hebrew שִׂימוּ לְבַבְכֶם עַל־דַּרְכֵיכֶם (also 2:15, 18).
- **"Ye have sown much, and bring in little; ye eat, but ye have not enough; ye drink, but ye are not filled with drink; ye clothe you, but there is none warm; and he that earneth wages earneth wages to put it into a bag with holes" → "You have sown much and brought in little; you eat, and there is not enough to be satisfied; you drink, and there is not enough to be drunk; you clothe yourselves, and there is no warmth for one; and he who earns wages earns wages into a bag pierced through"** (v6) — Hebrew infinitives after a perfect (וְהָבֵא, אָכוֹל, שָׁתוֹ, לָבוֹשׁ); וְאֵין־לְשָׂבְעָה "there is not enough to be satisfied" (literally "there is none for satisfying"); לְשָׁכְרָה "to be drunk"; וְאֵין־לְחֹם לוֹ "there is no warmth for one" (literally "for him"); הַמִּשְׂתַּכֵּר מִשְׂתַּכֵּר "he who earns wages earns wages"; צְרוֹר נָקוּב "a bag pierced through".
- **"Go up to the mountain … and I will take pleasure in it, and I will be glorified, saith the LORD" → "Go up the mountain … and I will be pleased with it, and I will be glorified, says Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew עֲלוּ הָהָר "go up the mountain"; רָצָה "be pleased with"; the qere וְאֶכָּבְדָה (a cohortative, "and let me be glorified") is in the verse, the ketiv ואכבד "and I will be glorified" has the same sense; אָמַר יְהוָה "says Yahweh". The rabbis read the missing ה of the ketiv as the five things the second house lacked (Yoma 21b, from memory).
- **"Ye looked for much, and, lo, it came to little; and when ye brought it home, I did blow upon it. Why? saith the LORD of hosts. Because of mine house that is waste, and ye run every man unto his own house" → "You looked for much, and behold, it came to little; and you brought it to the house, and I blew on it. Because of what? declares Yahweh of Hosts. Because of my house, which lies waste, while you run each to his own house"** (v9) — Hebrew פָּנֹה "looking (turning) toward"; וְהִנֵּה "and behold"; הַבַּיִת "to the house"; נָפַחְתִּי בוֹ "I blew on it"; יַעַן מֶה "because of what?"; נְאֻם "declares".
- **"Therefore the heaven over you is stayed from dew, and the earth is stayed from her fruit" → "Therefore, over you, the heavens have kept back their dew, and the earth has kept back its produce"** (v10) — Hebrew כָּלְאוּ "kept back" (twice); מִטָּל literally "from dew"; יְבוּל "produce".
- **"And I called for a drought … upon the corn, and upon the new wine, and upon the oil … and upon men, and upon cattle, and upon all the labour of the hands" → "And I called for dryness … on the grain, and on the new wine, and on the fresh oil … and on man, and on beast, and on all the labor of hands"** (v11) — Hebrew חֹרֶב "dryness"; דָּגָן "grain"; תִּירוֹשׁ "new wine"; יִצְהָר "fresh oil"; אָדָם "man"; בְּהֵמָה "beast"; יְגִיעַ כַּפָּיִם "labor of hands" (literally "of palms").
- **"with all the remnant of the people, obeyed the voice of the LORD their God … and the people did fear before the LORD" → "and all the remnant of the people listened to the voice of Yahweh their Elohim … and the people feared before Yahweh"** (v12) — Hebrew וְכֹל שְׁאֵרִית הָעָם "and all the remnant of the people" (also v14; 2:2); שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of" ("obey" is not a Hebrew word); מִפְּנֵי "before" (literally "from the face of").
- **"Then spake Haggai the LORD'S messenger in the LORD'S message unto the people, saying, I am with you, saith the LORD" → "And Haggai, the messenger of Yahweh, said in the message of Yahweh to the people, saying: I am with you, declares Yahweh"** (v13) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה "the messenger of Yahweh" (the word for "angel" too), of a prophet; בְּמַלְאֲכוּת "in the message" (only here); נְאֻם "declares".
- **"and they came and did work in the house of the LORD of hosts, their God" → "and they came and did work in the house of Yahweh of Hosts, their Elohim"** (v14) — Hebrew הֵעִיר אֶת־רוּחַ "stirred up the spirit of" (three times); מְלָאכָה "work".
- **"In the four and twentieth day of the month, in the sixth month" → "on day twenty-four of the month, in the sixth"** (v15) — Hebrew בַּשִּׁשִּׁי "in the sixth", without "month" (as printed). The Hebrew has a paragraph break after v14; the date is read with v14 (as KJV), or in part with 2:1 (from memory).

### Notes
- **Shealtiel** (vv12, 14) — here the Hebrew spells the name שַׁלְתִּיאֵל *Shaltiel*; in v1 (and 2:23) שְׁאַלְתִּיאֵל *Shealtiel*.
- **Dates** — the second year of Darius I, 520 BC; the sixth month, day one, and day twenty-four (v15).
