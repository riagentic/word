# Daniel 11

[1] "And as for me, in the first year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the ⟦medes|Madi|Mede⟧, I stood as one making strong and as a stronghold for him.

[2] "And now I will tell you truth. Behold, yet three kings are standing up for ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧, and the fourth shall grow rich with riches greater than all; and when he is strong through his riches, he shall stir up all, the kingdom of ⟦greece|Yavan|Greece⟧.

[3] And a mighty king shall stand up, and he shall rule with great rule and do according to his will.

[4] And when he has stood up, his kingdom shall be broken and divided toward the four winds of the heavens, and not to his posterity, and not according to his rule with which he ruled; for his kingdom shall be plucked up, and be for others apart from these.

[5] "And the king of the south shall be strong, and one of his princes; and he shall be strong over him and shall rule; his rule is a great rule.

[6] And at the end of years they shall join themselves together; and the daughter of the king of the south shall come to the king of the north to make uprightness. And she shall not retain the power of the arm, and he shall not stand, nor his arm; and she shall be given up, and those who brought her, and the one who begot her, and the one who made her strong in the times.

[7] "And from a branch of her roots one shall stand up in his station, and he shall come to the force and come into the stronghold of the king of the north, and act against them and show strength.

[8] And also their gods, with their molten images, with their desirable vessels of silver and gold, he shall bring into captivity to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and he shall stand off for years from the king of the north.

[9] And he shall come into the kingdom of the king of the south and return to his own ground.

[10] "And his sons shall provoke and gather a multitude of many forces; and he shall surely come and overflow and pass over, and he shall return and provoke as far as his stronghold.

[11] And the king of the south shall be embittered, and go out and fight with him, with the king of the north; and he shall make a great multitude stand, and the multitude shall be given into his hand.

[12] And the multitude shall be carried off, and his heart shall be high, and he shall make ten thousands fall; but he shall not be strong.

[13] And the king of the north shall return and make a multitude stand greater than the first; and at the end of the times, of years, he shall surely come with a great force and with many goods.

[14] "And in those times many shall stand up against the king of the south; and the sons of the violent ones of your people shall lift themselves up to make a vision stand, and they shall stumble.

[15] And the king of the north shall come and pour out a siege mound and capture a city of fortifications; and the arms of the south shall not stand, nor the people of his chosen ones, and there shall be no power to stand.

[16] And the one who comes against him shall do according to his will, and there shall be none standing before him; and he shall stand in the land of the beauty, and a full end shall be in his hand.

[17] And he shall set his face to come with the power of all his kingdom, and upright terms with him, and he shall act; and the daughter of women he shall give him, to ruin it; and she shall not stand, and shall not be his.

[18] And he shall set his face to the coastlands and capture many; and a commander shall make his reproach cease for him; only, his reproach he shall turn back upon him.

[19] And he shall turn back his face to the strongholds of his own land, and he shall stumble and fall and not be found.

[20] "And there shall stand up in his station one who makes a taskmaster pass through the splendor of the kingdom; and in a few days he shall be broken, and not in anger and not in war.

[21] "And there shall stand up in his station a despised one, on whom they have not given royal majesty; and he shall come in ease and take hold of the kingdom by slipperiness.

[22] And the arms of the flood shall be flooded away before him and be broken, and also a leader of a covenant.

[23] And from the joining with him he shall work deceit; and he shall go up and become mighty with a few of a nation.

[24] In ease, and into the fat places of a province he shall come, and he shall do what his fathers and the fathers of his fathers did not do: plunder and spoil and goods he shall scatter for them; and against fortifications he shall devise his devices, and until a time.

[25] And he shall stir up his power and his heart against the king of the south with a great force; and the king of the south shall provoke himself to the war with a great and exceedingly mighty force; and he shall not stand, for they shall devise devices against him.

[26] And those who eat his portion of food shall break him, and his force shall overflow, and many shall fall slain.

[27] And the two of them, the kings, their heart is for doing evil; and at one table they shall speak a lie, and it shall not prosper; for yet an end is for the appointed time.

[28] And he shall return to his land with great goods, and his heart against a holy covenant; and he shall act and return to his land.

[29] At the appointed time he shall return and come into the south; and it shall not be as at the first, so at the last.

[30] And ships of Kittim shall come against him, and he shall be disheartened and return and be indignant against a holy covenant, and act; and he shall return and give heed to those who forsake a holy covenant.

[31] And arms from him shall stand up, and they shall profane the sanctuary, the stronghold, and remove the continual, and set up **the detestable thing making desolate**.

[32] And those who do wickedly against a covenant he shall make godless with smoothness; but the people who know its ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall show strength and act.

[33] "And those of the people who have insight shall give understanding to the many; and they shall stumble by sword and by flame, by captivity and by plunder, for days.

[34] And when they stumble, they shall be helped with a little help; and many shall join themselves to them in slipperiness.

[35] And some of those with insight shall stumble, to refine among them and to purify and to make white, until a time of the end; for it is yet for the appointed time.

[36] "And the king shall do according to his will, and he shall exalt himself and magnify himself over every ⟦el|el|god⟧, and against the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of ⟦el|elim|gods⟧ he shall speak wonderful things; and he shall prosper until the indignation is finished, for what is decided shall be done.

[37] And to the gods of his fathers he shall not give heed, nor to the desire of women; and to any ⟦eloah|eloah|god⟧ he shall not give heed, for over all he shall magnify himself.

[38] And the ⟦eloah|eloah|god⟧ of strongholds in his station he shall honor; and an ⟦eloah|eloah|god⟧ whom his fathers did not know he shall honor with gold and with silver and with precious stone and with desirable things.

[39] And he shall act for the fortifications of strongholds with a foreign ⟦eloah|eloah|god⟧; whoever he recognizes, he shall increase with glory, and he shall make them rule over the many, and ground he shall divide for a price.

[40] "And at a time of the end the king of the south shall gore with him, and the king of the north shall storm against him with chariots and with horsemen and with many ships; and he shall come into lands and overflow and pass over.

[41] And he shall come into the land of the beauty, and many shall stumble; and these shall escape from his hand: Edom and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and the first of the sons of Ammon.

[42] And he shall stretch out his hand against lands, and the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall not be for an escape.

[43] And he shall rule over the hidden treasures of gold and of silver and over all the desirable things of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and ⟦libyans|Luvim|Libyans⟧ and ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ shall be at his steps.

[44] And reports shall alarm him from the east and from the north, and he shall go out in great fury to destroy and to devote many.

[45] And he shall plant the tents of his palace between the seas and the mountain of the beauty of holiness; and he shall come to his end, and there is none helping him.

## Changes

- **"I stood to confirm and to strengthen him" → "I stood as one making strong and as a stronghold for him"** (v1) — Hebrew עָמְדִי לְמַחֲזִיק וּלְמָעוֹז לוֹ, literally "my standing was for one making strong and for a stronghold to him"; מָעוֹז "stronghold" (the row; v7, 10, 19, 31, 38, 39). **"Darius the Mede"** — הַמָּדִי, the gentilic.
- **"will I shew thee the truth" → "I will tell you truth"** (v2) — Hebrew אֱמֶת, without the article. **"there shall stand up yet three kings" → "yet three kings are standing up"** — a participle. **"far richer than they all" → "shall grow rich with riches greater than all"** — Hebrew יַעֲשִׁיר עֹשֶׁר־גָּדוֹל (עֹשֶׁר "riches", the row). **"by his strength through his riches" → "when he is strong through his riches"**. **"he shall stir up all against the realm of Grecia" → "he shall stir up all, the kingdom of Greece"** — Hebrew יָעִיר הַכֹּל אֵת מַלְכוּת יָוָן: אֵת marks "the kingdom of Greece" as the object beside "all", or means "with"; "against" is not written.
- **"a mighty king shall stand up"** (v3) — עָמַד "stand (up)" through the chapter (KJV varies "stand up", "arise"). **"shall rule with great dominion" → "he shall rule with great rule"** — Hebrew מָשַׁל מִמְשָׁל רַב (the מָשַׁל row; v4, 5). **"according to his will"** (v3, 16, 36) — Hebrew כִּרְצוֹנוֹ.
- **"when he shall stand up" → "when he has stood up"** (v4). **"to his posterity"** — Hebrew לְאַחֲרִיתוֹ, literally "to his after-part" (the אַחֲרִית of 8:19, 23). **"his dominion which he ruled" → "his rule with which he ruled"**. **"shall be plucked up, even for others beside those" → "shall be plucked up, and be for others apart from these"** — Hebrew תִנָּתֵשׁ (the נָתַשׁ row) · מִלְּבַד־אֵלֶּה.
- **"the king of the south" · "the king of the north"** (v5–40) — Hebrew מֶלֶךְ הַנֶּגֶב · מֶלֶךְ הַצָּפוֹן; נֶגֶב is literally "the Negev, the dry south". No king is named in the verses. **"and one of his princes; and he shall be strong above him" → "and one of his princes; and he shall be strong over him"** (v5) — Hebrew וְיֶחֱזַק עָלָיו (the חָזַק עַל row). **"his dominion shall be a great dominion" → "his rule is a great rule"** — Hebrew מִמְשָׁל רַב מֶמְשַׁלְתּוֹ, no verb.
- **"in the end of years they shall join themselves together"** (v6) — Hebrew יִתְחַבָּרוּ. **"to make an agreement" → "to make uprightness"** — Hebrew מֵישָׁרִים (the Prov 1–4 row "uprightness"): an equitable settlement. **"she shall not retain the power of the arm"** — Hebrew עָצַר כֹּחַ (the row). **"neither shall he stand, nor his arm" → "and he shall not stand, nor his arm"**. **"he that begat her" → "the one who begot her"** — Hebrew וְהַיֹּלְדָהּ, a participle with a suffix ("her child", הַיַּלְדָּה, is a common repointing, from memory; the MT is kept). **"he that strengthened her" → "the one who made her strong"** — Hebrew וּמַחֲזִקָהּ.
- **"out of a branch of her roots shall one stand up in his estate" → "from a branch of her roots one shall stand up in his station"** (v7, 20, 21, 38) — Hebrew נֵצֶר (the Isa 11:1 row "branch"); כַּנּוֹ "his station" (the כֵּן row, literally "base, stand"). **"shall come with an army" → "shall come to the force"** — Hebrew אֶל־הַחַיִל (the חַיִל row; v13, 25, 26 "force"). **"shall enter into the fortress" → "come into the stronghold"** — מָעוֹז. **"shall deal against them, and shall prevail" → "act against them and show strength"** — Hebrew וְעָשָׂה בָהֶם וְהֶחֱזִיק, the hifil of חָזַק without an object (so v32).
- **"with their princes" → "with their molten images"** (v8) — Hebrew נְסִכֵיהֶם, of נָסַךְ "pour, cast": images cast in metal, beside the gods (the נָסִיךְ "prince" of Josh 13:21 is the same spelling: KJV's reading; uncertain). **"their precious vessels" → "their desirable vessels"** — Hebrew כְּלֵי חֶמְדָּתָם, of the root חָמַד (v37, 38, 43; 9:23). **"shall carry captives into Egypt" → "he shall bring into captivity to Egypt"** — Hebrew בַּשְּׁבִי. **"he shall continue more years than the king of the north" → "he shall stand off for years from the king of the north"** — Hebrew שָׁנִים יַעֲמֹד מִמֶּלֶךְ הַצָּפוֹן.
- **"So the king of the south shall come into his kingdom" → "And he shall come into the kingdom of the king of the south"** (v9) — Hebrew וּבָא בְּמַלְכוּת מֶלֶךְ הַנֶּגֶב: the one who comes is not named. **"his own land" → "his own ground"** — Hebrew אַדְמָתוֹ (the אֲדָמָה row).
- **"his sons shall be stirred up" → "his sons shall provoke"** (v10, 25) — qere וּבָנָיו; Hebrew יִתְגָּרוּ (the הִתְגָּרָה row). **"one shall certainly come" → "he shall surely come"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew וּבָא בוֹא (the infinitive with its verb; literally "and coming he shall come"). **"overflow" … "pass through" → "overflow and pass over"** (v10, 22, 26, 40) — Hebrew שָׁטַף · עָבַר (the עָבַר row). **"then shall he return, and be stirred up, even to his fortress" → "and he shall return and provoke as far as his stronghold"** — qere וְיִתְגָּרֶה (singular) and מָעֻזּוֹ "his stronghold".
- **"shall be moved with choler" → "shall be embittered"** (v11) — Hebrew וְיִתְמַרְמַר, as 8:7. **"he shall set forth a great multitude" → "he shall make a great multitude stand"** (v11, 13) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד (the row).
- **"when he hath taken away the multitude" → "the multitude shall be carried off"** (v12) — Hebrew וְנִשָּׂא הֶהָמוֹן, nifal ("be lifted up / be carried away"). **"his heart shall be lifted up" → "his heart shall be high"** — qere וְרָם (ketiv יָרוּם, the same verb). **"he shall cast down many ten thousands" → "he shall make ten thousands fall"** — Hebrew וְהִפִּיל רִבֹּאוֹת. **"he shall not be strengthened by it" → "he shall not be strong"** — Hebrew לֹא יָעוֹז (of עָזַז, another root than חָזַק).
- **"after certain years" → "at the end of the times, of years"** (v13) — Hebrew וּלְקֵץ הָעִתִּים שָׁנִים. **"with much riches" → "with many goods"** (v13, 24, 28) — Hebrew רְכוּשׁ (the row).
- **"the robbers of thy people shall exalt themselves to establish the vision" → "the sons of the violent ones of your people shall lift themselves up to make a vision stand"** (v14) — Hebrew וּבְנֵי פָּרִיצֵי עַמְּךָ יִנַּשְּׂאוּ לְהַעֲמִיד חָזוֹן. **"they shall fall" → "they shall stumble"** (v14, 19, 33, 34, 35, 41) — Hebrew כָּשַׁל.
- **"cast up a mount" → "pour out a siege mound"** (v15) — Hebrew וְיִשְׁפֹּךְ סוֹלְלָה (שָׁפַךְ "pour out"). **"take the most fenced cities" → "capture a city of fortifications"** — Hebrew וְלָכַד עִיר מִבְצָרוֹת (the לָכַד and מִבְצָר rows). **"neither his chosen people" → "nor the people of his chosen ones"** — Hebrew וְעַם מִבְחָרָיו. **"neither shall there be any strength to withstand" → "there shall be no power to stand"** — כֹּחַ.
- **"he that cometh against him" → "the one who comes against him"** (v16). **"the glorious land" → "the land of the beauty"** (v16, 41) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ־הַצְּבִי (as 8:9 הַצְּבִי). **"which by his hand shall be consumed" → "and a full end shall be in his hand"** — Hebrew וְכָלָה בְיָדוֹ: כָּלָה as the noun of 9:27 ("a full end"); a verb "and it shall be finished by his hand" is also possible.
- **"with the strength of his whole kingdom" → "with the power of all his kingdom"** (v17) — Hebrew תֹּקֶף (the Esther row). **"and upright ones with him; thus shall he do" → "and upright terms with him, and he shall act"** — Hebrew וִישָׁרִים עִמּוֹ וְעָשָׂה; "terms" is supplied for יְשָׁרִים "upright (things)". **"the daughter of women"** — Hebrew בַּת הַנָּשִׁים. **"corrupting her" → "to ruin it"** — Hebrew לְהַשְׁחִיתָהּ (the הִשְׁחִית row), the suffix feminine: "her" or the kingdom. **"neither be for him" → "and shall not be his"**.
- **"After this shall he turn his face" → "And he shall set his face"** (v18) — qere וְיָשֵׂם "set" (ketiv וַיָּשֶׁב "turn back"). **"the isles" → "the coastlands"** — Hebrew אִיִּים (the אִי row). **"take many" → "capture many"**. **"a prince for his own behalf shall cause the reproach offered by him to cease" → "a commander shall make his reproach cease for him"** — Hebrew וְהִשְׁבִּית קָצִין חֶרְפָּתוֹ לוֹ (קָצִין "commander"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach"). **"without his own reproach he shall cause it to turn upon him" → "only, his reproach he shall turn back upon him"** — Hebrew בִּלְתִּי חֶרְפָּתוֹ יָשִׁיב לוֹ; בִּלְתִּי "except, only" (uncertain here).
- **"toward the fort of his own land" → "to the strongholds of his own land"** (v19) — Hebrew לְמָעוּזֵּי, plural.
- **"a raiser of taxes in the glory of the kingdom" → "one who makes a taskmaster pass through the splendor of the kingdom"** (v20) — Hebrew מַעֲבִיר נוֹגֵשׂ הֶדֶר מַלְכוּת: נֹגֵשׂ "taskmaster" (the row), הֶדֶר "splendor". **"within few days" → "in a few days"**. **"neither in anger, nor in battle" → "and not in anger and not in war"** — Hebrew בְאַפַּיִם, literally "in nostrils" (the אַף decision); מִלְחָמָה "war".
- **"a vile person" → "a despised one"** (v21) — Hebrew נִבְזֶה (the בָּזָה row). **"to whom they shall not give the honour of the kingdom" → "on whom they have not given royal majesty"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־נָתְנוּ עָלָיו הוֹד מַלְכוּת, a perfect (the הוֹד מַלְכוּת row). **"he shall come in peaceably" → "he shall come in ease"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew בְשַׁלְוָה, as 8:25. **"obtain the kingdom by flatteries" → "take hold of the kingdom by slipperiness"** (v21, 34) — Hebrew הֶחֱזִיק … בַּחֲלַקְלַקּוֹת, literally "slipperinesses", a doubled form of חָלָק "smooth" (v32 חֲלַקּוֹת "smoothness").
- **"with the arms of a flood shall they be overflown" → "the arms of the flood shall be flooded away"** (v22) — Hebrew וּזְרֹעוֹת הַשֶּׁטֶף יִשָּׁטְפוּ (שֶׁטֶף "flood", 9:26). **"yea, also the prince of the covenant" → "and also a leader of a covenant"** — Hebrew נְגִיד בְּרִית, without articles (the נָגִיד row, as 9:25, 26); no capital.
- **"after the league made with him" → "from the joining with him"** (v23) — Hebrew הִתְחַבְּרוּת, only here. **"he shall work deceitfully" → "he shall work deceit"** — מִרְמָה. **"for he shall come up, and shall become strong with a small people" → "and he shall go up and become mighty with a few of a nation"** — Hebrew וְעָלָה וְעָצַם בִּמְעַט־גּוֹי (עָצַם "be mighty").
- **"He shall enter peaceably even upon the fattest places of the province" → "In ease, and into the fat places of a province he shall come"** (v24) — Hebrew בְּשַׁלְוָה וּבְמִשְׁמַנֵּי מְדִינָה. **"he shall scatter among them the prey, and spoil, and riches" → "plunder and spoil and goods he shall scatter for them"** — Hebrew בִּזָּה וְשָׁלָל וּרְכוּשׁ (the שָׁלָל · בָּז row). **"he shall forecast his devices against the strong holds" → "against fortifications he shall devise his devices"** (v24, 25) — Hebrew יְחַשֵּׁב מַחְשְׁבֹתָיו, literally "think his thoughts" (the חָשַׁב מַחֲשָׁבוֹת row). **"even for a time" → "and until a time"**.
- **"with a great army" → "with a great force"** (v25). **"shall be stirred up to battle" → "shall provoke himself to the war"** — Hebrew יִתְגָּרֶה לַמִּלְחָמָה (the הִתְגָּרָה row). **"for they shall forecast devices against him" → "for they shall devise devices against him"**.
- **"that feed of the portion of his meat" → "who eat his portion of food"** (v26) — Hebrew פַת־בָּגוֹ, the Persian loan word of 1:5, 8 (the king's allotted food). **"his army shall overflow" → "his force shall overflow"**. **"many shall fall down slain" → "many shall fall slain"** — חֲלָלִים.
- **"both these kings' hearts shall be to do mischief" → "the two of them, the kings, their heart is for doing evil"** (v27) — Hebrew לְבָבָם לְמֵרָע, singular "heart". **"they shall speak lies at one table" → "at one table they shall speak a lie"** — כָּזָב. **"but it shall not prosper"** — Hebrew וְלֹא תִצְלָח. **"for yet the end shall be at the time appointed" → "for yet an end is for the appointed time"** (v27, 29, 35) — Hebrew כִּי־עוֹד קֵץ לַמּוֹעֵד (the מוֹעֵד row).
- **"against the holy covenant" → "against a holy covenant"** (v28, 30) — Hebrew בְּרִית קֹדֶשׁ, without the article: literally "a covenant of holiness". **"he shall do exploits" → "he shall act"** (v28, 30) — Hebrew וְעָשָׂה.
- **"but it shall not be as the former, or as the latter" → "and it shall not be as at the first, so at the last"** (v29) — Hebrew וְלֹא־תִהְיֶה כָרִאשֹׁנָה וְכָאַחֲרֹנָה.
- **"the ships of Chittim" → "ships of Kittim"** (v30) — Hebrew צִיִּים כִּתִּים, without the article; צִי "ship" (as Num 24:24; Isa 33:21), not the צִיִּים "desert dwellers" of the Ps 72 row. **"he shall be grieved" → "he shall be disheartened"** — Hebrew וְנִכְאָה. **"have indignation against" → "be indignant against"** — Hebrew וְזָעַם עַל. **"have intelligence with them that forsake" → "give heed to those who forsake"** — Hebrew וְיָבֵן עַל (בִּין "understand", with עַל "give heed to", as v37).
- **"arms shall stand on his part" → "arms from him shall stand up"** (v31) — Hebrew וּזְרֹעִים מִמֶּנּוּ יַעֲמֹדוּ. **"they shall pollute the sanctuary of strength" → "they shall profane the sanctuary, the stronghold"** — Hebrew וְחִלְּלוּ הַמִּקְדָּשׁ הַמָּעוֹז (the חִלֵּל "profane" row), two nouns side by side. **"shall take away the daily sacrifice" → "remove the continual"** — Hebrew וְהֵסִירוּ הַתָּמִיד (the הֵסִיר row; הַתָּמִיד as 8:11). **"they shall place the abomination that maketh desolate" → "set up the detestable thing making desolate"** — Hebrew וְנָתְנוּ הַשִּׁקּוּץ מְשׁוֹמֵם, as printed: שִׁקּוּץ "detestable thing" with the article, singular (9:27 plural); מְשׁוֹמֵם, the polel participle, without the article.
- **"such as do wickedly against the covenant shall he corrupt by flatteries" → "those who do wickedly against a covenant he shall make godless with smoothness"** (v32) — Hebrew וּמַרְשִׁיעֵי בְרִית יַחֲנִיף בַּחֲלַקּוֹת: הֶחֱנִיף "make godless, pollute"; חֲלַקּוֹת "smoothness". **"the people that do know their God" → "the people who know its Elohim"** — Hebrew עַם יֹדְעֵי אֱלֹהָיו, "his" in the singular of the people. **"shall be strong, and do exploits" → "shall show strength and act"** — Hebrew יַחֲזִקוּ וְעָשׂוּ.
- **"they that understand among the people" → "those of the people who have insight"** (v33, 35) — Hebrew מַשְׂכִּילֵי עָם, the participle of הִשְׂכִּיל (the row; 12:3, 10). **"shall instruct many" → "shall give understanding to the many"** — Hebrew יָבִינוּ לָרַבִּים, with the article. **"yet they shall fall by the sword, and by flame, by captivity, and by spoil, many days" → "and they shall stumble by sword and by flame, by captivity and by plunder, for days"** — Hebrew בִּזָּה "plunder"; יָמִים "days" ("many" is not written).
- **"Now when they shall fall" → "And when they stumble"** (v34). **"cleave to them with flatteries" → "join themselves to them in slipperiness"** — Hebrew וְנִלְווּ עֲלֵיהֶם (the נִלְוָה row).
- **"some of them of understanding" → "some of those with insight"** (v35) — Hebrew וּמִן־הַמַּשְׂכִּילִים. **"to try them, and to purge, and to make them white" → "to refine among them and to purify and to make white"** — Hebrew לִצְרוֹף בָּהֶם וּלְבָרֵר וְלַלְבֵּן (צָרַף "refine", the row). **"even to the time of the end: because it is yet for a time appointed" → "until a time of the end; for it is yet for the appointed time"**.
- **"above every god" → "over every el"** (v36) — Hebrew עַל־כָּל־אֵל, the word אֵל (the token, small letters, as the אֵל אַחֵר row). **"the God of gods" → "the El of elim"** — Hebrew אֵל אֵלִים. **"marvellous things" → "wonderful things"** — Hebrew נִפְלָאוֹת (the row; 8:24). **"till the indignation be accomplished" → "until the indignation is finished"** — Hebrew עַד־כָּלָה זַעַם. **"for that that is determined shall be done" → "for what is decided shall be done"** — Hebrew כִּי נֶחֱרָצָה נֶעֱשָׂתָה, a perfect ("has been done"), the decision seen as done (9:26, 27).
- **"Neither shall he regard the God of his fathers" → "And to the gods of his fathers he shall not give heed"** (v37) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי אֲבֹתָיו: the plural form may mean "gods" or "the Elohim of his fathers"; of a foreign king the plain plural is kept. **"nor the desire of women"** — Hebrew חֶמְדַּת נָשִׁים: what women desire, or one whom women desire (not said). **"nor regard any god" → "and to any eloah he shall not give heed"** (v37, 38, 39) — Hebrew כָּל־אֱלוֹהַּ, the word אֱלוֹהַּ (token, small letters).
- **"in his estate shall he honour the God of forces" → "the eloah of strongholds in his station he shall honor"** (v38) — Hebrew לֶאֱלֹהַּ מָעֻזִּים עַל־כַּנּוֹ יְכַבֵּד (כִּבֵּד "honor", the row). **"pleasant things" → "desirable things"** — Hebrew חֲמֻדוֹת.
- **"Thus shall he do in the most strong holds" → "And he shall act for the fortifications of strongholds"** (v39) — Hebrew וְעָשָׂה לְמִבְצְרֵי מָעֻזִּים. **"whom he shall acknowledge and increase with glory" → "whoever he recognizes, he shall increase with glory"** — qere יַכִּיר (ketiv הִכִּיר "he has recognized"; the הִכִּיר row); כָּבוֹד "glory". **"he shall cause them to rule over many" → "he shall make them rule over the many"**. **"divide the land for gain" → "ground he shall divide for a price"** — Hebrew וַאֲדָמָה יְחַלֵּק בִּמְחִיר (אֲדָמָה "ground"; מְחִיר "price", the row).
- **"at the time of the end" → "at a time of the end"** (v40; v35 "until a time of the end") — Hebrew בְעֵת קֵץ, without the article (so 12:4, 9; 8:17 עֶת־קֵץ). **"shall the king of the south push at him" → "the king of the south shall gore with him"** — Hebrew יִתְנַגַּח עִמּוֹ (the נָגַח row; 8:4). **"shall come against him like a whirlwind" → "shall storm against him"** — Hebrew וְיִשְׂתָּעֵר עָלָיו, one verb. **"he shall enter into the countries" → "he shall come into lands"** — without the article.
- **"many countries shall be overthrown" → "many shall stumble"** (v41) — Hebrew וְרַבּוֹת יִכָּשֵׁלוּ: רַבּוֹת feminine ("many", of lands?); pointed רִבּוֹת it would be "ten thousands" (from memory). **"these shall escape out of his hand"** — Hebrew יִמָּלְטוּ (the נִמְלַט row). **"the chief of the children of Ammon" → "the first of the sons of Ammon"** — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן: the people's name with "sons of" (the standing decision), as Jeremiah has it.
- **"upon the countries" → "against lands"** (v42) — Hebrew בַּאֲרָצוֹת. **"shall not escape" → "shall not be for an escape"** — Hebrew לֹא תִהְיֶה לִפְלֵיטָה (the פְּלֵיטָה row).
- **"he shall have power over the treasures" → "he shall rule over the hidden treasures"** (v43) — Hebrew וּמָשַׁל בְּמִכְמַנֵּי. **"the precious things" → "the desirable things"** — Hebrew חֲמֻדוֹת. **"the Libyans and the Ethiopians shall be at his steps" → "Libyans and Cushites shall be at his steps"** — Hebrew וְלֻבִים וְכֻשִׁים בְּמִצְעָדָיו, without articles.
- **"tidings out of the east and out of the north shall trouble him" → "reports shall alarm him from the east and from the north"** (v44) — Hebrew שְׁמֻעוֹת · בִּהֵל. **"with great fury"** — Hebrew בְּחֵמָא, the Aramaic spelling of חֵמָה. **"to destroy, and utterly to make away many" → "to destroy and to devote many"** — Hebrew לְהַשְׁמִיד וּלְהַחֲרִים (the הֶחֱרִים "devote" row; here beside "destroy").
- **"the tabernacles of his palace" → "the tents of his palace"** (v45) — Hebrew אָהֳלֵי אַפַּדְנוֹ (אַפֶּדֶן, a Persian loan word, only here). **"between the seas in the glorious holy mountain" → "between the seas and the mountain of the beauty of holiness"** — Hebrew בֵּין יַמִּים לְהַר־צְבִי־קֹדֶשׁ: בֵּין … לְ "between … and". **"none shall help him" → "there is none helping him"** — a participle.

### Notes
- **The kings of the south and of the north** (v5–45) — commonly taken as the Ptolemies of Egypt and the Seleucids of Syria, from Alexander (v3) to Antiochus IV (v21–45); the daughter of v6 as Berenice, the daughter of women of v17 as Cleopatra I, the ships of Kittim (v30) as Rome. The verses themselves name only Persia, Greece, Egypt, Edom, Moab, Ammon, Libyans, Cushites and Kittim. Many read v36–45 of a king still to come.
- **"The detestable thing making desolate"** (v31) — as 9:27 and 12:11; commonly taken of the altar set up in the temple in 167 BC (1 Macc 1:54).
