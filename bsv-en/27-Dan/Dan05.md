# Daniel 5

[1] ⟦belshazzar|Belshatzar|Belshazzar⟧ the king made a great feast for a thousand of his lords, and before the thousand he was drinking wine.

[2] ⟦belshazzar|Belshatzar|Belshazzar⟧ said, under the taste of the wine, to bring the vessels of gold and of silver that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ his father had taken out from the temple that was in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, that the king and his lords, his consorts and his concubines might drink from them.

[3] Then the vessels of gold were brought that had been taken out from the temple of the house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that was in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and the king and his lords, his consorts and his concubines drank from them.

[4] They drank wine, and praised the gods of gold and silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone.

[5] In that same hour the fingers of a man's hand came out and were writing opposite the lampstand, upon the plaster of the wall of the palace of the king; and the king was seeing the palm of the hand that was writing.

[6] Then the king, his brightness was changed on him, and his thoughts alarmed him, and the knots of his loins were loosened, and his knees knocked one against the other.

[7] The king called out with strength to bring in the enchanters, the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, and the determiners. The king answered and said to the wise men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, "Any man who reads this writing and shows me its interpretation shall be clothed in purple, and a chain of gold shall be on his neck, and as third in the kingdom he shall rule."

[8] Then all the wise men of the king came in, but they were not able to read the writing or to make known its interpretation to the king.

[9] Then King ⟦belshazzar|Belshatzar|Belshazzar⟧ was greatly alarmed, and his brightness was changed on him, and his lords were perplexed.

[10] The queen, because of the words of the king and his lords, came into the house of the banquet. The queen answered and said, "King, live forever! Let not your thoughts alarm you, and let not your brightness be changed.

[11] There is a man in your kingdom in whom is a spirit of holy gods; and in the days of your father light and insight and wisdom like the wisdom of gods were found in him; and King ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ your father made him chief of the magicians, enchanters, ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, determiners — your father the king —

[12] because an excellent spirit, and knowledge and insight, interpreting of dreams and showing of riddles and loosing of knots, were found in him, in ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, to whom the king set the name ⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧. Now let ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ be called, and he will show the interpretation."

[13] Then ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ was brought in before the king. The king answered and said to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, "You are that ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ who is of the sons of the exile of ⟦judah|Yehud|Judah⟧, whom the king my father brought from ⟦judah|Yehud|Judah⟧.

[14] And I have heard of you that a spirit of gods is in you, and light and insight and excellent wisdom are found in you.

[15] And now there have been brought in before me the wise men, the enchanters, that they should read this writing and make known to me its interpretation; but they are not able to show the interpretation of the matter.

[16] And I have heard of you that you are able to interpret interpretations and to loose knots. Now if you are able to read the writing and to make known to me its interpretation, in purple you shall be clothed, and a chain of gold shall be on your neck, and as third in the kingdom you shall rule."

[17] Then ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ answered and said before the king, "Let your gifts be for yourself, and give your rewards to another; however, the writing I will read to the king, and the interpretation I will make known to him.

[18] You, King: ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ gave the kingdom and the greatness and the honor and the splendor to ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ your father.

[19] And because of the greatness that he gave him, all the peoples, nations, and tongues were trembling and fearing before him; whom he willed he killed, and whom he willed he kept alive, and whom he willed he raised up, and whom he willed he brought low.

[20] And when his heart was lifted up and his spirit grew strong to act presumptuously, he was brought down from the throne of his kingdom, and the honor was made to pass from him.

[21] And from the sons of man he was driven, and his heart was made like the beasts', and with the wild donkeys was his dwelling; plants like oxen he was made to eat, and with the dew of the heavens his body was wet, until he knew that ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ rules in the kingdom of man, and whom he wills he sets up over it.

[22] And you, his son, ⟦belshazzar|Belshatzar|Belshazzar⟧, have not brought low your heart, although you knew all this,

[23] and against the Lord of the heavens you have lifted yourself up; and the vessels of his house have been brought before you, and you and your lords, your consorts and your concubines, have been drinking wine from them; and the gods of silver and gold, bronze, iron, wood, and stone, which do not see and do not hear and do not know, you have praised; and the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ in whose hand is your breath, and whose are all your ways, you have not glorified.

[24] Then from before him the palm of the hand was sent, and this writing was inscribed.

[25] And this is the writing that was inscribed: **MENE, MENE, TEQEL, UFARSIN**.

[26] This is the interpretation of the matter:
**MENE** — ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ has numbered your kingdom and finished it.

[27] **TEQEL** — you have been weighed in the balances and found lacking.

[28] **PERES** — your kingdom has been divided and given to the ⟦medes|Madai|Medes⟧ and ⟦persians|Paras|Persians⟧."

[29] Then ⟦belshazzar|Belshatzar|Belshazzar⟧ said, and ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ was clothed in purple, and a chain of gold was put on his neck, and it was proclaimed concerning him that he should rule as third in the kingdom.

[30] In that night ⟦belshazzar|Belshatzar|Belshazzar⟧ the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdi|Chaldean⟧ king was killed.

[31] And ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the ⟦medes|Madi|Mede⟧ received the kingdom, being about sixty-two years old.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elah*"** (v3, 18, 21, 23, 26) — Aramaic אֱלָהָא, the God of Israel, with its token (as ch. 2–4); v18, 21 אֱלָהָא עִלָּאָה (qere; ketiv עליא) "Elah the Most High", tokens `elah-aram` and `elyon`. **"the gods of gold"** (v4, 23) and "a spirit of (holy) gods" (v11, 14), "the wisdom of gods" (v11) — plural אֱלָהִין / אֱלָהֵי: plain.
- **"Belshazzar the king"** (v1) — בֵּלְשַׁאצַּר מַלְכָּא, the Aramaic order; v9 מַלְכָּא בֵלְשַׁאצַּר "King Belshazzar". v30 spells בֵּלְאשַׁצַּר; one token.
- **"drank wine before the thousand" → "before the thousand he was drinking wine"** (v1) — participle.
- **"whiles he tasted the wine" → "under the taste of the wine"** (v2) — בִּטְעֵם חַמְרָא, the word טְעֵם ("decree", "discretion" elsewhere): under the effect of the wine. **"commanded to bring" → "said to bring"** (v2; v29 "said") — אֲמַר. **"had taken out of the temple" → "had taken out from the temple"** — הֵיכְלָא, "temple" of a god (v5 "palace" of the king). **"his wives, and his concubines" → "his consorts and his concubines"** (v2, 3, 23) — שֵׁגְלָתֵהּ וּלְחֵנָתֵהּ: two ranks of royal women (שֵׁגַל also of a queen, Neh 2:6; Ps 45:9); "wives" is not the word.
- **"they brought" → "were brought"** (v3, 23) — a plural verb with no subject (הַיְתִיו), as 4:25. **"the temple of the house of God" → "the temple of the house of Elah"** — מִן־הֵיכְלָא דִּי־בֵית אֱלָהָא.
- **"In the same hour came forth fingers of a man's hand" → "In that same hour the fingers of a man's hand came out"** (v5) — qere נְפַקָה (ketiv נפקו). **"wrote over against the candlestick" → "were writing opposite the lampstand"** — participle. **"the part of the hand" → "the palm of the hand"** (v5, 24) — פַּס יְדָה, the flat of the hand (palm or back; uncertain).
- **"the king's countenance was changed" → "the king, his brightness was changed on him"** (v6, 9; v10 "your brightness") — זִיוֹהִי, the word "brightness" (2:31; 4:36): the light of his face. **"troubled" → "alarmed"** (v6, 9, 10) — בְּהַל, as 4:5. **"the joints of his loins were loosed" → "the knots of his loins were loosened"** — קִטְרֵי חַרְצֵהּ; קִטְרִין is the word of v12, 16 "knots" (KJV "doubts"): the play is kept. **"one against another" → "one against the other"** — דָּא לְדָא.
- **"cried aloud" → "called out with strength"** (v7) — as 3:4. **"the astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers" → "the enchanters, the Chaldeans, and the determiners"** — qere כַּשְׂדָּאֵי; as 4:7. **"shall be clothed with scarlet" → "shall be clothed in purple"** (v7, 16, 29) — אַרְגְּוָנָא, the red-purple dye. **"a chain of gold about his neck" → "a chain of gold shall be on his neck"** — qere הַמְנִיכָא (ketiv המונכא; v16, 29 the same), a Persian loanword, "necklace". **"shall be the third ruler in the kingdom" → "as third in the kingdom he shall rule"** (v7, 16, 29) — תַלְתִּי (v16, 29 תַלְתָּא), "third", or a title "triumvir, one of three" (uncertain).
- **"came in" … "could not"** (v8) — qere עָלִּין; qere וּפִשְׁרֵהּ "its interpretation" (ketiv ופשרא, "the interpretation"). **"they could not" → "they were not able"** (v8, 15) — כָהֲלִין.
- **"were astonied" → "were perplexed"** (v9) — מִשְׁתַּבְּשִׁין.
- **"the queen"** (v10) — מַלְכְּתָא, not the king's consorts of v2–3 (שֵׁגְלָתֵהּ): the queen mother is the common reading. **"came into the banquet house" → "came into the house of the banquet"** — qere עַלַּת; לְבֵית מִשְׁתְּיָא. **"O king, live for ever" → "King, live forever!"** — no "O". **"nor let thy countenance be changed" → "and let not your brightness be changed"**.
- **"the spirit of the holy gods" → "a spirit of holy gods"** (v11; v14 "a spirit of gods") — no article, plural, as 4:8. **"light and understanding" → "light and insight"** (v11, 12, 14) — נַהִירוּ וְשָׂכְלְתָנוּ (שָׂכְלְתָנוּ the root of Hebrew הַשְׂכֵּל "insight", 1:17). **"master of the magicians, astrologers, Chaldeans, and soothsayers" → "chief of the magicians, enchanters, Chaldeans, determiners"** — no "and"; רַב "chief". **"I say, thy father" → "your father the king"** — the Aramaic repeats אֲבוּךְ מַלְכָּא at the end; KJV supplies "I say".
- **"an excellent spirit, and knowledge, and understanding, interpreting of dreams, and shewing of hard sentences, and dissolving of doubts" → "an excellent spirit, and knowledge and insight, interpreting of dreams and showing of riddles and loosing of knots"** (v12) — מְפַשַּׁר חֶלְמִין וַאַחֲוָיַת אֲחִידָן וּמְשָׁרֵא קִטְרִין. **"in the same Daniel" → "in him, in Daniel"** — בֵּהּ בְּדָנִיֵּאל. **"whom the king named" → "to whom the king set the name"** — שָׂם־שְׁמֵהּ (as 1:7 "set names").
- **"Art thou that Daniel" → "You are that Daniel"** (v13) — qere אַנְתְּ־הוּא; a statement in form ("Are you …?" is possible). **"of the children of the captivity of Judah" → "who is of the sons of the exile of Judah"** — as 2:25; יְהוּד, the province name (twice). **"out of Jewry" → "from Judah"**.
- **"of thee"** (v14, 16) — qere עֲלָךְ. **"excellent wisdom"** — חָכְמָה יַתִּירָה.
- **"have been brought in before me" → "there have been brought in before me"** (v15) — הֻעַלּוּ.
- **"thou canst make interpretations, and dissolve doubts" → "you are able to interpret interpretations and to loose knots"** (v16) — qere תִיכּוּל / תִּכוּל (ketiv תוכל); פִּשְׁרִין לְמִפְשַׁר.
- **"Let thy gifts be to thyself, and give thy rewards to another" → "Let your gifts be for yourself, and give your rewards to another"** (v17) — נְבָזְבְּיָתָךְ (2:6 "reward"). **"yet" → "however"** — בְּרַם.
- **"O thou king, the most high God gave Nebuchadnezzar thy father a kingdom, and majesty, and glory, and honour" → "You, King: Elah the Most High gave the kingdom and the greatness and the honor and the splendor to Nebuchadnezzar your father"** (v18) — qere אַנְתְּ; מַלְכוּתָא וּרְבוּתָא וִיקָרָא וְהַדְרָה (רְבוּ "greatness" as 4:22, 36; יְקָר "honor"; הֲדַר "splendor", 4:30).
- **"for the majesty that he gave him" → "because of the greatness"** (v19). **"languages" → "tongues"**. **"trembled and feared" → "were trembling and fearing"** — qere זָיְעִין (ketiv זאעין). **"whom he would he slew; and whom he would he kept alive" → "whom he willed he killed, and whom he willed he kept alive"** — מַחֵא, haphel of חֲיָא "make live" (as pointed; the letters are those of מְחָא "strike", 4:35). **"set up … put down" → "raised up … brought low"** — מָרִים … מַשְׁפִּיל.
- **"his mind hardened in pride" → "his spirit grew strong to act presumptuously"** (v20) — רוּחֵהּ תִּקְפַת לַהֲזָדָה (the root of Hebrew זִיד "presume"). **"he was deposed from his kingly throne" → "he was brought down from the throne of his kingdom"** — הָנְחַת מִן־כָּרְסֵא מַלְכוּתֵהּ. **"they took his glory from him" → "the honor was made to pass from him"** — הֶעְדִּיוּ, a plural verb with no subject, the haphel of עֲדָה "pass" (3:27; 4:31).
- **"he was driven from the sons of men" → "from the sons of man he was driven"** (v21) — בְּנֵי אֲנָשָׁא. **"his heart was made like the beasts"** — qere שַׁוִּיו (ketiv שוי), literally "they made his heart equal with the beasts". **"they fed him with grass like oxen" → "plants like oxen he was made to eat"** — as 4:25. **"appointeth over it whomsoever he will" → "whom he wills he sets up over it"** — qere עֲלַהּ.
- **"And thou his son, O Belshazzar" → "And you, his son, Belshazzar"** (v22) — qere וְאַנְתְּ. **"hast not humbled thine heart" → "have not brought low your heart"** — הַשְׁפֵּלְתְּ (as v19). **"though thou knewest" → "although you knew"** — כָּל־קֳבֵל דִּי, elsewhere "because" (2:8 etc.); here the sense is "although" or "inasmuch as" (the Aramaic allows both).
- **"hast lifted up thyself against the Lord of heaven" → "against the Lord of the heavens you have lifted yourself up"** (v23) — מָרֵא־שְׁמַיָּא. qere קָדָמָךְ, וְאַנְתְּ, וְרַבְרְבָנָךְ. **"the gods of silver, and gold"** — here silver first (v4 gold first), as the Aramaic. **"in whose hand thy breath is"** — נִשְׁמְתָךְ. **"hast thou not glorified" → "you have not glorified"** — הַדַּרְתָּ (4:34, 37).
- **"Then was the part of the hand sent from him" → "Then from before him the palm of the hand was sent"** (v24) — מִן־קֳדָמוֹהִי. **"written" → "inscribed"** (v24, 25) — רְשִׁים.
- **"MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN" → "MENE, MENE, TEQEL, UFARSIN"** (v25) — מְנֵא מְנֵא תְּקֵל וּפַרְסִין, transliterated as printed (וּ "and" belongs to the last word; ק as *q*, the soft פ as *f*). The words are weights or coins (*mina*, *mina*, *shekel*, and *half-pieces*); v26–28 read each as a verb.
- **"This is the interpretation of the thing" → "This is the interpretation of the matter"** (v26). **"God hath numbered thy kingdom, and finished it" → "Elah has numbered your kingdom and finished it"** — מְנָה … וְהַשְׁלְמַהּ: "the days" is not in the Aramaic.
- **"Thou art weighed in the balances, and art found wanting" → "you have been weighed in the balances and found lacking"** (v27) — תְּקִילְתָּה … חַסִּיר.
- **"PERES; Thy kingdom is divided, and given to the Medes and Persians" → "PERES — your kingdom has been divided and given to the Medes and Persians"** (v28) — פְּרֵס פְּרִיסַת … לְמָדַי וּפָרָס: פְּרֵס "divided" and פָרָס "Persia" are one set of letters.
- **"Then commanded Belshazzar, and they clothed Daniel with scarlet, and put a chain of gold about his neck" → "Then Belshazzar said, and Daniel was clothed in purple, and a chain of gold was put on his neck"** (v29) — וְהַלְבִּישׁוּ, plural with no subject; qere וְהַמְנִיכָא. **"made a proclamation concerning him" → "it was proclaimed concerning him"** — וְהַכְרִזוּ, plural with no subject, as וְהַלְבִּישׁוּ.
- **"In that night was Belshazzar the king of the Chaldeans slain" → "In that night Belshazzar the Chaldean king was killed"** (v30) — qere כַשְׂדָּאָה (ketiv כשדיא), "the Chaldean".
- **"being about threescore and two years old" → "being about sixty-two years old"** (v31) — כְּבַר שְׁנִין שִׁתִּין וְתַרְתֵּין, literally "as a son of sixty and two years". **"Darius the Median" → "Darius the Mede"** — מָדָיָא.

### Notes
- **Language** — Aramaic (2:4b–7:28).
- **Numbering** — our 5:31 = Aramaic 6:1.
- **Belshazzar** — called the son of Nebuchadnezzar (v2, 11, 13, 18, 22); in the Babylonian records he is the son of Nabonidus, ruling in Babylon for his father. "Father" may be used of a predecessor.
- **539 BC** — fall of Babylon to the Persians.
