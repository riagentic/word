# Daniel 1

[1] In the third year of the reign of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and besieged it.

[2] And ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ gave into his hand ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and part of the vessels of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and he brought them to the land of Shinar, to the house of his god, and the vessels he brought into the treasure house of his god.

[3] And the king said to Ashpenaz, the chief of his eunuchs, to bring some of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, both of the seed of the kingship and of the nobles,

[4] youths in whom there was no blemish at all, and good in appearance, and having insight in all wisdom, and knowing knowledge, and understanding learning, and in whom there was power to stand in the palace of the king; and to teach them the writing and the tongue of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧.

[5] And the king appointed for them a day's portion on its day from the delicacies of the king and from the wine of his drinking, and to bring them up three years, and at the end of them they should stand before the king.

[6] And among them were, of the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧, Mishael, and ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧.

[7] And the chief of the eunuchs set names for them: and he set for ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ ⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧, and for ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ Shadrach, and for Mishael Meshach, and for ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧.

[8] And ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ set it upon his heart that he would not defile himself with the delicacies of the king or with the wine of his drinking; and he sought from the chief of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself.

[9] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ to ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and to compassion before the chief of the eunuchs.

[10] And the chief of the eunuchs said to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, "I fear my lord the king, who appointed your food and your drink; for why should he see your faces dejected, more than the youths who are of your age? And you would make my head guilty to the king."

[11] And ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ said to the steward whom the chief of the eunuchs had appointed over ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧, Mishael, and ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧,

[12] "Please test your servants ten days, and let them give us some of the vegetables, that we may eat, and water, that we may drink.

[13] And let our appearance be seen before you, and the appearance of the youths who eat the delicacies of the king; and as you see, do with your servants."

[14] And he listened to them in this matter, and tested them ten days.

[15] And at the end of ten days their appearance was seen to be better, and they were fatter in flesh, than all the youths who ate the delicacies of the king.

[16] And the steward was taking away their delicacies and the wine of their drinking, and giving them vegetables.

[17] And these youths, the four of them, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave them learning and insight in all writing and wisdom; and ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ had understanding in every vision and dreams.

[18] And at the end of the days that the king had said to bring them in, the chief of the eunuchs brought them in before ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧.

[19] And the king spoke with them, and among them all none was found like ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧, Mishael, and ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧; and they stood before the king.

[20] And in every matter of wisdom of understanding that the king sought from them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians, the enchanters, who were in all his kingdom.

[21] And ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ was until the first year of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ the king.

## Changes

- **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v2) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 9, 17) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article (v2 בֵית־הָאֱלֹהִים, v9, 17): "the house of Elohim", the article not rendered (as the finished books). **"with part of the vessels" → "and part of the vessels"** — וּמִקְצָת, a second object of "gave". **"the house of his god … the treasure house of his god"** — אֱלֹהָיו, plural in form ("his gods" is possible); the singular is kept as the usual sense with a king's own god. **"the land of Shinar"** — Hebrew אֶרֶץ־שִׁנְעָר, the old name of Babylonia (Gen 10:10; 11:2).
- **"spake unto Ashpenaz the master of his eunuchs, that he should bring" → "said to Ashpenaz, the chief of his eunuchs, to bring"** (v3) — וַיֹּאמֶר … לְהָבִיא; רַב סָרִיסָיו "the chief of his eunuchs" (v7–11, 18 שַׂר הַסָּרִיסִים, the same English). סָרִיס "eunuch" (one rendering): the word is also used of a court officer, whether or not castrated. **"certain of the children of Israel" → "some of the sons of Israel"** — מִבְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"of the king's seed" → "of the seed of the kingship"** — זֶרַע הַמְּלוּכָה. **"of the princes" → "of the nobles"** — פַּרְתְּמִים, a Persian loanword, literally "foremost ones" (as Esth 1:3; 6:9).
- **"Children" → "youths"** (v4, 10, 13, 15, 17) — יְלָדִים, literally "children, ones born"; here of young men old enough to serve at court, so "children" would mislead. **"in whom was no blemish" → "in whom there was no blemish at all"** (v4) — qere מוּם (ketiv מאום, the same word spelled with א), with כָּל under the negative. **"well favoured" → "good in appearance"** — טוֹבֵי מַרְאֶה. **"skilful in all wisdom" → "having insight in all wisdom"** — מַשְׂכִּילִים (v17 הַשְׂכֵּל "insight"). **"cunning in knowledge" → "knowing knowledge"** — יֹדְעֵי דַעַת. **"understanding science" → "understanding learning"** — מְבִינֵי מַדָּע (מַדָּע "learning", v17). **"such as had ability in them" → "in whom there was power"** — וַאֲשֶׁר כֹּחַ בָּהֶם; כֹּחַ "power". **"the king's palace" → "the palace of the king"** — הֵיכַל, "temple" of a god, "palace" of a king. **"the learning and the tongue" → "the writing and the tongue"** — סֵפֶר, the word for "book", here what is written (script and texts); לָשׁוֹן "tongue".
- **"a daily provision of the king's meat" → "a day's portion on its day from the delicacies of the king"** (v5) — דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ, literally "the matter of a day in its day"; פַּת־בַּג (v8, 13, 15, 16 פַּתְבַּג), a Persian loanword for the king's portion of fine food (sense uncertain: "delicacies", "rations"). **"the wine which he drank" → "the wine of his drinking"** (v5, 8; v16 "of their drinking") — יֵין מִשְׁתָּיו. **"so nourishing them" → "and to bring them up"** — וּלְגַדְּלָם, literally "to make them great": rearing and training both. **"at the end thereof" → "at the end of them"** — וּמִקְצָתָם, of the years.
- **"of the children of Judah" → "of the sons of Judah"** (v6) — מִבְּנֵי יְהוּדָה.
- **"gave names: for he gave unto Daniel the name of" → "set names for them: and he set for Daniel"** (v7) — וַיָּשֶׂם … שֵׁמוֹת וַיָּשֶׂם, the one verb twice.
- **"purposed in his heart" → "set it upon his heart"** (v8) — וַיָּשֶׂם עַל־לִבּוֹ, the verb of v7 again. **"requested of" → "sought from"** — וַיְבַקֵּשׁ (בִּקֵּשׁ "seek"; also v20). **"with the portion of the king's meat" → "with the delicacies of the king"** — פַּתְבַּג.
- **"had brought Daniel into favour and tender love with" → "gave Daniel to *chesed* and to compassion before"** (v9) — וַיִּתֵּן … אֶת־דָּנִיֵּאל לְחֶסֶד וּלְרַחֲמִים לִפְנֵי: Daniel is "given" to meet *chesed* and compassion from the chief (as 1 Kgs 8:50; Ps 106:46). חֶסֶד carries its token; רַחֲמִים "compassion".
- **"your meat and your drink" → "your food and your drink"** (v10) — מַאֲכַלְכֶם, מִשְׁתֵּיכֶם (plural "your": all four). **"for why should he see your faces worse liking than the children which are of your sort" → "for why should he see your faces dejected, more than the youths who are of your age"** — אֲשֶׁר לָמָּה; זֹעֲפִים (as Gen 40:6, "dejected"); כְּגִילְכֶם, literally "according to your circle", of age. **"then shall ye make me endanger my head to the king" → "And you would make my head guilty to the king"** — וְחִיַּבְתֶּם אֶת־רֹאשִׁי, "make guilty, forfeit" (only here in the Hebrew Bible; Aramaic sense).
- **"Melzar" → "the steward"** (v11, 16) — הַמֶּלְצַר, with the article: a title, not a name (an Akkadian loanword for a guardian or overseer; sense uncertain). **"had set over" → "had appointed over"** — מִנָּה, as v10.
- **"Prove thy servants, I beseech thee" → "Please test your servants"** (v12) — נַס־נָא (נִסָּה "test"; נָא "please"). **"pulse" → "vegetables"** — הַזֵּרֹעִים (v16 זֵרְעֹנִים), literally "sown things": food from seed plants. **"to eat … to drink" → "that we may eat … that we may drink"** — וְנֹאכְלָה, וְנִשְׁתֶּה, cohortative.
- **"Then let our countenances be looked upon" → "And let our appearance be seen"** (v13) — וְיֵרָאוּ … מַרְאֵינוּ, מַרְאֶה "appearance" (also v15). **"as thou seest, deal with" → "as you see, do with"** — עֲשֵׂה עִם.
- **"So he consented to them" → "And he listened to them"** (v14) — וַיִּשְׁמַע לָהֶם. **"matter"** — לַדָּבָר, the word that is "word": here their request.
- **"their countenances appeared fairer" → "their appearance was seen to be better"** (v15) — נִרְאָה מַרְאֵיהֶם טוֹב … מִן. **"fatter in flesh"** — בְּרִיאֵי בָּשָׂר; "they were" supplied for the plural adjective.
- **"took away … and gave" → "was taking away … and giving"** (v16) — participles (וַיְהִי … נֹשֵׂא … וְנֹתֵן): what he kept doing.
- **"As for these four children" → "And these youths, the four of them"** (v17) — הַיְלָדִים הָאֵלֶּה אַרְבַּעְתָּם. **"knowledge and skill in all learning" → "learning and insight in all writing"** — מַדָּע וְהַשְׂכֵּל בְּכָל־סֵפֶר. **"in all visions" → "in every vision"** — בְּכָל־חָזוֹן, singular; וַחֲלֹמוֹת "and dreams", plural.
- **"Now at the end of the days that the king had said he should bring them in" → "the king had said to bring them in"** (v18) — לַהֲבִיאָם, no subject. **"Nebuchadnezzar"** — here נְבֻכַדְנֶצַּר, without א (v1 נְבוּכַדְנֶאצַּר): the two spellings stand through Daniel; one token.
- **"therefore stood they" → "and they stood"** (v19) — וַיַּעַמְדוּ.
- **"in all matters of wisdom and understanding" → "in every matter of wisdom of understanding"** (v20) — וְכֹל דְּבַר חָכְמַת בִּינָה, construct. **"that the king enquired of them" → "that the king sought from them"** — בִּקֵּשׁ. **"ten times"** — עֶשֶׂר יָדוֹת, literally "ten hands". **"the magicians and astrologers" → "the magicians, the enchanters"** — הַחַרְטֻמִּים הָאַשָּׁפִים, no "and". חַרְטֹם "magician" (an Egyptian loanword for the learned men of the courts; exact sense uncertain); אַשָּׁף "enchanter" (an Akkadian loanword for a conjuring priest; not "astrologer").
- **"And Daniel continued even unto" → "And Daniel was until"** (v21) — וַיְהִי דָּנִיֵּאל עַד־, literally "and Daniel was until": that he continued (at the court) is the sense; "there", "continued" are not in the Hebrew. **"King Cyrus" → "Cyrus the king"** — לְכוֹרֶשׁ הַמֶּלֶךְ.

### Notes
- **Language** — 1:1–2:4a is Hebrew; at 2:4 the Chaldeans speak "in Aramaic", and the book goes on in Aramaic to 7:28.
- **Four Hebrew names → Babylonian names**: *Daniyel* ("El is my judge") → *Belteshatzar*; *Chananyah* → *Shadrach*; *Mishael* → *Meshach*; *Azaryah* → *Abednego*. The meanings of the Babylonian names are not certain.
- **v1 third year of Jehoiakim** — about 605 BC by the usual reckoning.
- **v21 Cyrus** — 539 BC; 10:1 dates a vision to "the third year of Cyrus".
