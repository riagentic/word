# Daniel 6

[1] It seemed good before ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧, and he set up over the kingdom a hundred and twenty satraps, who should be in all the kingdom;

[2] and over them three overseers, of whom ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ was one, that these satraps should give account to them, and the king should not suffer damage.

[3] Then this ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ was distinguishing himself above the overseers and the satraps, because an excellent spirit was in him; and the king planned to set him up over all the kingdom.

[4] Then the overseers and the satraps were seeking to find a pretext against ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ from the side of the kingdom; but any pretext or corruption they were not able to find, because he was trustworthy, and no negligence or corruption was found in him.

[5] Then these men said, "We shall not find against this ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ any pretext, except we find it against him in the law of his ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧."

[6] Then these overseers and satraps came thronging to the king, and thus they said to him: "⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king, live forever!

[7] All the overseers of the kingdom, the prefects and the satraps, the counselors and the governors, have taken counsel together that the king should establish a statute and make strong a ban, that whoever asks a petition of any god or man for thirty days, except of you, King, shall be cast into the pit of the lions.

[8] Now, King, establish the ban and inscribe the writing, that it is not to be changed, according to the law of the ⟦medes|Madai|Medes⟧ and ⟦persians|Paras|Persians⟧, which does not pass away."

[9] Because of this King ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ inscribed the writing and the ban.

[10] And ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, when he knew that the writing was inscribed, went into his house — and he had windows open in his upper room toward ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ — and three times in the day he was kneeling on his knees and praying and giving thanks before his ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, just as he had been doing before this.

[11] Then these men came thronging, and found ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ asking and pleading for favor before his ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧.

[12] Then they came near and said before the king concerning the ban of the king, "Have you not inscribed a ban, that any man who asks of any god or man for thirty days, except of you, King, shall be cast into the pit of the lions?" The king answered and said, "The word is certain, according to the law of the ⟦medes|Madai|Medes⟧ and ⟦persians|Paras|Persians⟧, which does not pass away."

[13] Then they answered and said before the king, "⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, who is of the sons of the exile of ⟦judah|Yehud|Judah⟧, has set no regard upon you, King, nor upon the ban that you have inscribed; but three times in the day he asks his petition."

[14] Then the king, when he heard the word, was much displeased, and on ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ he set his mind to rescue him; and until the going in of the sun he was striving to deliver him.

[15] Then these men came thronging to the king, and said to the king, "Know, King, that it is a law of the ⟦medes|Madai|Medes⟧ and ⟦persians|Paras|Persians⟧ that any ban or statute that the king establishes is not to be changed."

[16] Then the king said, and ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ was brought and cast into the pit of the lions. The king answered and said to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, "Your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, whom you serve continually, he will rescue you."

[17] And a stone was brought and set upon the mouth of the pit, and the king sealed it with his signet and with the signet of his lords, that the purpose concerning ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ might not be changed.

[18] Then the king went to his palace and passed the night fasting, and diversions were not brought in before him, and his sleep fled from him.

[19] Then the king rose at dawn, at first light, and in haste went to the pit of the lions.

[20] And as he came near to the pit, to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ he cried out with a pained voice. The king answered and said to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, "⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, servant of the living ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, whom you serve continually, has he been able to rescue you from the lions?"

[21] Then ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ spoke with the king: "King, live forever!

[22] My ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ sent his angel and shut the mouth of the lions, and they have not harmed me, because before him innocence was found in me; and also before you, King, I have done no harm."

[23] Then the king was very glad, and ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ he said to take up from the pit. And ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ was taken up from the pit, and no harm was found in him, because he trusted in his ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧.

[24] And the king said, and those men who had accused ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ were brought, and into the pit of the lions they were cast, they, their sons, and their wives; and they had not reached the bottom of the pit before the lions had power over them and crushed all their bones.

[25] Then ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king wrote to all the peoples, nations, and tongues who dwell in all the earth: "May your peace be multiplied!

[26] From before me a decree is made, that in all the dominion of my kingdom they shall tremble and fear before the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧,
for he is the living ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧,
and enduring forever;
and his kingdom is one that shall not be destroyed,
and his dominion is to the end.

[27] He rescues and delivers,
and he works signs and wonders
in the heavens and on the earth,
who has rescued ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧
from the hand of the lions."

[28] And this ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ prospered in the reign of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ and in the reign of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ the ⟦persians|Parsi|Persian⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elah*"** (v5, 10, 11, 16, 20, 22, 23, 26) — Aramaic אֱלָהּ / אֱלָהָא, the God of Israel, with its token (as ch. 2–5). **"any God or man" → "any god or man"** (v7, 12) — כָּל־אֱלָהּ, any god: plain.
- **"It pleased Darius" → "It seemed good before Darius, and he set up"** (v1) — שְׁפַר קֳדָם דָּרְיָוֶשׁ וַהֲקִים (as 4:2). **"an hundred and twenty princes" → "a hundred and twenty satraps"** (v1–4, 6, 7) — אֲחַשְׁדַּרְפְּנַיָּא (Persian; as 3:2). **"which should be over the whole kingdom" → "who should be in all the kingdom"** — בְּכָל־מַלְכוּתָא.
- **"three presidents" → "three overseers"** (v2–4, 6, 7) — סָרְכִין, a Persian loanword (uncertain; "chief officials"). **"might give accounts unto them" → "should give account to them"** — טַעְמָא, the word "decree" (3:10), here a report. **"have no damage" → "should not suffer damage"** — נָזִק (as Ezra 4:13–22).
- **"was preferred above" → "was distinguishing himself above"** (v3) — הֲוָא מִתְנַצַּח, participle. **"an excellent spirit"** — רוּחַ יַתִּירָא (5:12). **"thought to set him" → "planned to set him up"** — עֲשִׁית לַהֲקָמוּתֵהּ.
- **"sought to find occasion against Daniel concerning the kingdom" → "were seeking to find a pretext against Daniel from the side of the kingdom"** (v4, 5) — עִלָּה "pretext"; מִצַּד מַלְכוּתָא, literally "from the side of the kingdom": in matters of state. **"occasion nor fault" → "pretext or corruption"** — וּשְׁחִיתָה (2:9 "corrupt"). **"faithful" → "trustworthy"** — מְהֵימַן (2:45). **"error" → "negligence"** — שָׁלוּ (as 3:29).
- **"concerning the law of his God" → "in the law of his Elah"** (v5) — בְּדָת אֱלָהֵהּ; דָּת "law" (Persian loan; not *torah*, no token; also v8, 12, 15).
- **"assembled together to the king" → "came thronging to the king"** (v6, 11, 15) — הַרְגִּשׁוּ, "came in a throng, in commotion" (uncertain: "by agreement" is also given). **"King Darius, live for ever" → "Darius the king, live forever!"** (v6; v25 "Darius the king") — דָּרְיָוֶשׁ מַלְכָּא, the Aramaic order; no "O". v9 מַלְכָּא דָּרְיָוֶשׁ "King Darius".
- **"the presidents of the kingdom, the governors, and the princes, the counsellors, and the captains" → "the overseers of the kingdom, the prefects and the satraps, the counselors and the governors"** (v7) — סִגְנַיָּא "prefects", הַדָּבְרַיָּא "counselors" (3:24), פַחֲוָתָא "governors". **"have consulted together" → "have taken counsel together"** — אִתְיָעַטוּ. **"to establish a royal statute, and to make a firm decree" → "that the king should establish a statute and make strong a ban"** — לְקַיָּמָה קְיָם מַלְכָּא וּלְתַקָּפָה אֱסָר: "the king" may be the subject ("that the king should establish") or go with "statute" ("a royal statute"); אֱסָר, literally "a binding", a prohibition (also v8, 9, 12, 13, 15). **"ask a petition" → "asks a petition"** — יִבְעֵה בָעוּ. **"save of thee, O king" → "except of you, King"** — לָהֵן מִנָּךְ מַלְכָּא. **"into the den of lions" → "into the pit of the lions"** (v7, 12, 16, 17, 19, 20, 23, 24) — גֹּב / גֻּבָּא "pit".
- **"establish the decree, and sign the writing" → "establish the ban and inscribe the writing"** (v8, 9, 10, 12, 13) — רְשַׁם "inscribe" (5:24, 25). **"that it be not changed" → "that it is not to be changed"** (v8, 15) — דִּי לָא לְהַשְׁנָיָה. **"which altereth not" → "which does not pass away"** (v8, 12) — דִּי־לָא תֶעְדֵּא (עֲדָה "pass", 3:27; 4:31).
- **"he went into his house; and his windows being open in his chamber toward Jerusalem" → "went into his house — and he had windows open in his upper room toward Jerusalem —"** (v10) — וְכַוִּין פְּתִיחָן לֵהּ בְּעִלִּיתֵהּ. **"he kneeled upon his knees three times a day" → "three times in the day he was kneeling on his knees"** — participles (בָּרֵךְ, מְצַלֵּא, מוֹדֵא). **"as he did aforetime" → "just as he had been doing before this"** — כָּל־קֳבֵל דִּי־הֲוָא עָבֵד מִן־קַדְמַת דְּנָה; כָּל־קֳבֵל דִּי here "just as" (elsewhere "because").
- **"praying and making supplication" → "asking and pleading for favor"** (v11) — בָּעֵא וּמִתְחַנַּן (the root of Hebrew חָנַן "show favor").
- **"the king's decree" → "the ban of the king"** (v12). **"every man that shall ask a petition" → "any man who asks"** — יִבְעֵה (no "petition" here). **"The thing is true" → "The word is certain"** — יַצִּיבָא מִלְּתָא (2:45; 3:24).
- **"which is of the children of the captivity of Judah" → "who is of the sons of the exile of Judah"** (v13) — as 2:25; 5:13. **"regardeth not thee, O king" → "has set no regard upon you, King"** — qere עֲלָךְ; as 3:12. **"maketh his petition" → "he asks his petition"** — בָּעֵא בָּעוּתֵהּ.
- **"these words" → "the word"** (v14) — מִלְּתָא. **"was sore displeased with himself" → "was much displeased"** — שַׂגִּיא בְּאֵשׁ עֲלוֹהִי, literally "it was very evil upon him" (v23 the opposite: "it was very good upon him"). **"set his heart on Daniel to deliver him" → "on Daniel he set his mind to rescue him"** — שָׂם בָּל, "set (his) mind" (only here); שֵׁיזִב "rescue", הַצֵּל "deliver" (as 3:15, 29). **"till the going down of the sun" → "until the going in of the sun"** — מֶעָלֵי שִׁמְשָׁא, "the entering of the sun": sunset. **"laboured" → "was striving"** — מִשְׁתַּדַּר.
- **"no decree nor statute" → "any ban or statute … is not to be changed"** (v15).
- **"commanded, and they brought Daniel, and cast him" → "said, and Daniel was brought and cast"** (v16; v24 the same) — plural verbs with no subject, as 4:25. **"Thy God … he will deliver thee" → "Your Elah, … he will rescue you"** — qere אַנְתְּ; יְשֵׁיזְבִנָּךְ, imperfect: a statement ("he will rescue you") or a wish ("may he rescue you"); the Aramaic does not mark which.
- **"a stone was brought, and laid" → "a stone was brought and set"** (v17) — שֻׂמַת. **"his own signet"** — בְּעִזְקְתֵהּ. **"that the purpose might not be changed concerning Daniel"** — צְבוּ "purpose, matter".
- **"neither were instruments of musick brought before him" → "and diversions were not brought in before him"** (v18) — דַחֲוָן, only here; meaning unknown ("music", "food", "women" are given). **"his sleep went from him" → "his sleep fled from him"** — נַדַּת עֲלוֹהִי, literally "fled upon him" (as Hebrew 2:1 עָלָיו).
- **"very early in the morning" → "at dawn, at first light"** (v19) — בִּשְׁפַּרְפָּרָא … בְּנָגְהָא.
- **"he cried with a lamentable voice unto Daniel" → "to Daniel he cried out with a pained voice"** (v20) — בְּקָל עֲצִיב. **"O Daniel" → "Daniel"** — no "O". qere אַנְתְּ. **"the living God"** (v20, 26) — אֱלָהָא חַיָּא.
- **"Then said Daniel unto the king" → "Then Daniel spoke with the king"** (v21) — עִם־מַלְכָּא מַלִּל (spelled דָּנִיֶּאל).
- **"hath sent his angel"** (v22) — מַלְאֲכֵהּ, "messenger" (as 3:28). **"the lions' mouths" → "the mouth of the lions"** — פֻּם אַרְיָוָתָא, singular. **"have not hurt me" → "have not harmed me"** — חַבְּלוּנִי. **"innocency was found in me" → "innocence was found in me"** — זָכוּ. **"before thee"** — qere קָדָמָךְ. **"done no hurt" → "done no harm"** — חֲבוּלָה.
- **"exceeding glad for him" → "very glad"** (v23) — שַׂגִּיא טְאֵב עֲלוֹהִי, literally "it was very good upon him" (v14). **"commanded that they should take Daniel up" → "Daniel he said to take up"** — לְהַנְסָקָה. **"manner of hurt" → "harm"** — חֲבָל (3:25). **"believed in his God" → "trusted in his Elah"** — הֵימִן (root of מְהֵימַן "trustworthy", v4).
- **"which had accused Daniel"** (v24) — אֲכַלוּ קַרְצוֹהִי, "ate his pieces", as 3:8. **"their children" → "their sons"** — בְּנֵיהוֹן. **"ere ever they came at the bottom of the den" → "they had not reached the bottom of the pit before"** — וְלָא־מְטוֹ … עַד דִּי. **"had the mastery of them" → "had power over them"** — שְׁלִטוּ (3:27). **"brake all their bones in pieces" → "crushed all their bones"** — הַדִּקוּ (2:34).
- **"that dwell in all the earth"** (v25) — qere דָיְרִין; **"Peace be multiplied unto you" → "May your peace be multiplied!"** — as 4:1; שְׁלָם plain "peace".
- **"I make a decree" → "From before me a decree is made"** (v26) — מִן־קֳדָמַי שִׂים טְעֵם. **"men tremble and fear" → "they shall tremble and fear"** — qere זָיְעִין; לֶהֱוֺן with no subject: "men" is not in the Aramaic. **"stedfast for ever" → "enduring forever"** — קַיָּם לְעָלְמִין. **"his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed" → "his kingdom is one that shall not be destroyed"** — as 2:44.
- **"He delivereth and rescueth" → "He rescues and delivers"** (v27) — מְשֵׁיזִב וּמַצִּל, the two verbs of v14. **"from the power of the lions" → "from the hand of the lions"** — מִן־יַד.
- **"the Persian"** (v28) — qere פָּרְסָאָה (ketiv פרסיא).

### Notes
- **Language** — Aramaic (2:4b–7:28).
- **Numbering** — our 6:1–28 = Aramaic 6:2–29 (our 5:31 = Aramaic 6:1).
- **Darius the Mede** — not known by this name from other sources; his identity is debated.
