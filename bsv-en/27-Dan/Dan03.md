# Daniel 3

[1] ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king made an image of gold; its height was sixty cubits, its breadth six cubits. He set it up in the plain of Dura, in the province of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[2] And ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king sent to gather the satraps, the prefects and the governors, the counselors, the treasurers, the law officers, the magistrates, and all the rulers of the provinces, to come to the dedication of the image that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king had set up.

[3] Then the satraps, the prefects and the governors, the counselors, the treasurers, the law officers, the magistrates, and all the rulers of the provinces were gathered to the dedication of the image that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king had set up; and they were standing before the image that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ had set up.

[4] And the herald called out with strength: "To you it is said, peoples, nations, and tongues:

[5] at the time that you hear the sound of the horn, the pipe, the lyre, the trigon, the harp, the bagpipe, and all kinds of music, you shall fall and do homage to the image of gold that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king has set up.

[6] And whoever does not fall and do homage shall in that same hour be cast into the midst of the furnace of burning fire."

[7] Because of this, at that time, as all the peoples heard the sound of the horn, the pipe, the lyre, the trigon, the harp, and all kinds of music, all the peoples, nations, and tongues fell, doing homage to the image of gold that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king had set up.

[8] Because of this, at that time, ⟦chaldeans|Kasdi|Chaldean⟧ men came near and accused the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[9] They answered and said to ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king, "King, live forever!

[10] You, King, have made a decree, that every man who hears the sound of the horn, the pipe, the lyre, the trigon, the harp, and the bagpipe, and all kinds of music, shall fall and do homage to the image of gold;

[11] and whoever does not fall and do homage shall be cast into the midst of the furnace of burning fire.

[12] There are men, ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, whom you have appointed over the work of the province of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧: Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧. These men, King, have set no regard upon you; your gods they do not serve, and to the image of gold that you have set up they do not do homage."

[13] Then ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ in rage and fury said to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧. Then these men were brought before the king.

[14] ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ answered and said to them, "Is it true, Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧, that my gods you do not serve, and to the image of gold that I have set up you do not do homage?

[15] Now if you are ready, that at the time that you hear the sound of the horn, the pipe, the lyre, the trigon, the harp, and the bagpipe, and all kinds of music, you fall and do homage to the image that I have made —; but if you do not do homage, in that same hour you shall be cast into the midst of the furnace of burning fire; and who is the god who will rescue you out of my hands?"

[16] Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧ answered and said to the king, "⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, we have no need to return you a word concerning this.

[17] If it is so, our ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ whom we serve is able to rescue us; from the furnace of burning fire and from your hand, King, he will rescue.

[18] And if not, be it known to you, King, that your gods we do not serve, and to the image of gold that you have set up we will not do homage."

[19] Then ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ was filled with fury, and the image of his face was changed against Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧. He answered and said to heat the furnace seven times more than it was seen fit to heat it.

[20] And to men, mighty ones of strength who were in his army, he said to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧, to cast them into the furnace of burning fire.

[21] Then these men were bound in their cloaks, their tunics, and their caps, and their garments, and were cast into the midst of the furnace of burning fire.

[22] Because of this, since the word of the king was harsh and the furnace heated exceedingly, those men who took up Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧ — the flame of the fire killed them.

[23] And these men, the three of them, Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧, fell into the midst of the furnace of burning fire, bound.

[24] Then ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king was astonished and rose up in haste. He answered and said to his counselors, "Did we not cast three men into the midst of the fire, bound?" They answered and said to the king, "Certainly, King."

[25] He answered and said, "Behold, I see four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, and there is no harm in them; and the appearance of the fourth is like **a son of gods**."

[26] Then ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ came near to the door of the furnace of burning fire; he answered and said, "Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧, servants of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧, come out, and come!" Then Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧ came out from the midst of the fire.

[27] And the satraps, the prefects and the governors and the counselors of the king, being gathered, were seeing these men, that the fire had no power over their bodies, and the hair of their head was not singed, and their cloaks were not changed, and the smell of fire had not passed upon them.

[28] ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ answered and said, "Blessed be the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧, who sent his angel and rescued his servants, who trusted upon him; and they set aside the word of the king, and gave their bodies, that they might not serve and not do homage to any god except their own ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧.

[29] And by me a decree is made, that any people, nation, or tongue that speaks negligence against the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧ shall be made into limbs, and his house shall be made a dunghill; because there is no other god who is able to deliver like this."

[30] Then the king made Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧ prosper in the province of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elah*"** (v17, 26, 28, 29) — Aramaic אֱלָהּ / אֱלָהָא, the God of Israel, with its token (as ch. 2). **"gods" / "god"** (v12, 14, 15, 18, 28, 29) — the king's gods and "any god", "another god": plain. **"who is that God" → "who is the god"** (v15) — מַן־הוּא אֱלָהּ, the king's challenge about any god: plain, no token.
- **"Nebuchadnezzar the king"** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 24) — נְבוּכַדְנֶצַּר מַלְכָּא, the Aramaic order (KJV has "Nebuchadnezzar the king" in v1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9; v24 "the king"). v3 ends without "the king", as the Aramaic. v14 spells נְבֻכַדְנֶצַּר.
- **"an image of gold, whose height was threescore cubits" → "an image of gold; its height was sixty cubits"** (v1) — צְלֵם דִּי־דְהַב רוּמֵהּ אַמִּין שִׁתִּין. **"set it up in the plain of Dura"** — אֲקִימֵהּ בְּבִקְעַת דּוּרָא.
- **"the princes, the governors, and the captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers" → "the satraps, the prefects and the governors, the counselors, the treasurers, the law officers, the magistrates, and all the rulers"** (v2, 3; v27 the first three and "the counselors of the king") — אֲחַשְׁדַּרְפְּנַיָּא "satraps" (Persian), סִגְנַיָּא "prefects" (2:48), פַחֲוָתָא "governors", אֲדַרְגָּזְרַיָּא "counselors" (Persian, uncertain), גְדָבְרַיָּא "treasurers" (Persian, uncertain; another word than Ezra 1:8 גִּזְבָּר), דְּתָבְרַיָּא "law officers" (Persian, literally "law-bearers"; uncertain), תִּפְתָּיֵא "magistrates" (uncertain), שִׁלְטֹנֵי מְדִינָתָא "the rulers of the provinces" (root שׁלט). The "and" stands where the Aramaic has it; the list is the same in v2 and v3.
- **"were gathered together … and they stood" → "were gathered … and they were standing"** (v3) — participles; qere וְקָיְמִין (ketiv וקאמין, another spelling).
- **"cried aloud" → "called out with strength"** (v4) — קָרֵא בְחָיִל. **"To you it is commanded, O people" → "To you it is said, peoples"** — לְכוֹן אָמְרִין, "to you they say"; no "O". **"languages" → "tongues"** (v4, 7, 29) — לִשָּׁנַיָּא, the word "tongue".
- **The instruments** (v5, 7, 10, 15) — קַרְנָא "the horn"; מַשְׁרוֹקִיתָא "the pipe" (root שְׁרַק "whistle"); qere קַתְרוֹס "the lyre", from Greek κίθαρις (ketiv קיתרוס / קיתרס); סַבְּכָא (v7, 10, 15 שַׂבְּכָא) "the trigon", from Greek σαμβύκη, a small harp; פְּסַנְתֵּרִין (v7 פְּסַנְטֵרִין) "the harp", from Greek ψαλτήριον; סוּמְפֹּנְיָה (v10 qere סוּפֹּנְיָה, ketiv סיפניה) "the bagpipe", from Greek συμφωνία, "sounding together" (an instrument; which one is uncertain: "bagpipe", "drum", or "music together" are given). KJV "cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, dulcimer". The list is kept as each verse has it: v7 has no "bagpipe"; v10 and v15 have "and the bagpipe", v5 has no "and" before it. **"all kinds of musick" → "all kinds of music"** — כֹל זְנֵי זְמָרָא.
- **"fall down and worship" → "fall and do homage"** (v5–7, 10–12, 14, 15, 18, 28) — נְפַל "fall" and סְגִד, bowing to the ground (as 2:46: "fall down", the Isa 44–47 row for סָגַד, is not used beside נְפַל). **"golden image" → "image of gold"** — צֶלֶם דַּהֲבָא.
- **"the same hour" → "in that same hour"** (v6, 15) — בַּהּ־שַׁעֲתָא. **"into the midst of a burning fiery furnace" → "into the midst of the furnace of burning fire"** (v6, 11, 15, 21, 23; v17, 20, 26 without "the midst") — אַתּוּן נוּרָא יָקִדְתָּא, "the furnace of the fire, the burning (one)": "burning" goes with "fire".
- **"when all the people heard … fell down and worshipped" → "as all the peoples heard … fell, doing homage"** (v7) — נָפְלִין … סָגְדִין, two participles without "and".
- **"certain Chaldeans" → "Chaldean men"** (v8) — גֻּבְרִין כַּשְׂדָּאִין. **"accused the Jews"** — וַאֲכַלוּ קַרְצֵיהוֹן דִּי יְהוּדָיֵא, literally "ate the pieces of the Jews": an idiom for slander.
- **"They spake and said" → "They answered and said"** (v9) — עֲנוֹ וְאָמְרִין.
- **"Thou, O king" → "You, King"** (v10) — qere אַנְתְּ (ketiv אנתה, another spelling). **"hast made a decree"** — שָׂמְתָּ טְּעֵם, literally "set a decree" (v29 "by me a decree is made").
- **"certain Jews" → "men, Jews,"** (v12) — גֻּבְרִין יְהוּדָאיִן, "Jewish men". **"over the affairs" → "over the work"** — עֲבִידַת (as 2:49). **"have not regarded thee" → "have set no regard upon you"** — לָא־שָׂמוּ עֲלָךְ טְעֵם (qere עֲלָךְ), טְעֵם here "regard, heed" (the word that is "decree" in v10). **"they serve not thy gods" → "your gods they do not serve"** — qere לֵאלָהָךְ, the Aramaic plural with "your" (ketiv לאלהיך, the Hebrew-like spelling; also v18), as 2:4 qere לְעַבְדָךְ "your servants". פְּלַח "serve".
- **"in his rage and fury commanded to bring" → "in rage and fury said to bring"** (v13) — בִּרְגַז וַחֲמָה אֲמַר לְהַיְתָיָה; חֱמָה "fury" (also v19). **"they brought these men" → "these men were brought"** — הֵיתָיוּ, passive.
- **"Is it true"** (v14) — הַצְדָּא, a word found only here (KJV margin "of purpose"; "is it on purpose" is also defended).
- **"well" → "—"** (v15) — the Aramaic leaves the sentence unfinished after "the image that I have made"; KJV supplies "well". **"who is that God that shall deliver you" → "who is the god who will rescue you"** — שֵׁיזִב "rescue" (v15, 17, 28); הַצֵּל "deliver" (v29), as Hebrew הִצִּיל.
- **"O Nebuchadnezzar" → "Nebuchadnezzar"** (v16) — the accent sets the name at the head of the answer (a bare name, no "O"); "said to King Nebuchadnezzar" is the other reading. **"we are not careful to answer thee in this matter" → "we have no need to return you a word concerning this"** — לָא־חַשְׁחִין … פִּתְגָם לַהֲתָבוּתָךְ.
- **"If it be so, our God whom we serve is able" → "If it is so, our Elah whom we serve is able to rescue us; from the furnace … he will rescue"** (v17) — הֵן אִיתַי … יָכִל לְשֵׁיזָבוּתַנָא, the accent divides after "to rescue us". הֵן אִיתַי may also go with what follows: "If our Elah whom we serve is able …" (or "If he exists, our Elah …"); the Aramaic is open.
- **"we will not serve thy gods" → "your gods we do not serve"** (v18) — qere אִיתַנָא פָלְחִין, participle.
- **"the form of his visage was changed" → "the image of his face was changed"** (v19) — וּצְלֵם אַנְפּוֹהִי אֶשְׁתַּנִּי (qere), צְלֵם the word for "image". **"one seven times more than it was wont to be heated" → "seven times more than it was seen fit to heat it"** — חַד־שִׁבְעָה עַל דִּי חֲזֵה לְמֵזְיֵהּ, literally "one seven above what was seen".
- **"the most mighty men that were in his army" → "men, mighty ones of strength who were in his army"** (v20) — גֻבְרִין גִּבָּרֵי־חַיִל דִּי בְחַיְלֵהּ: חַיִל is "strength" and "army" (one word). **"commanded" → "said"** (v13, 19, 20) — אֲמַר.
- **"in their coats, their hosen, and their hats" → "in their cloaks, their tunics, and their caps"** (v21) — סַרְבָּלֵיהוֹן, qere פַּטְּשֵׁיהוֹן, כַרְבְּלָתְהוֹן: loanwords for garments, each uncertain ("trousers", "leggings", "helmets" are given). **"were cast"** — רְמִיו.
- **"because the king's commandment was urgent" → "since the word of the king was harsh"** (v22) — מִלַּת מַלְכָּא מַחְצְפָה (as 2:15). **"slew those men" → "those men … the flame of the fire killed them"** — the object first, then taken up by הִמּוֹן.
- **"these three men" → "these men, the three of them"** (v23) — גֻבְרַיָּא אִלֵּךְ תְּלָתֵּהוֹן.
- **"rose up in haste, and spake" → "rose up in haste. He answered and said"** (v24) — עָנֵה וְאָמַר. **"True, O king" → "Certainly, King"** — יַצִּיבָא (2:8, 45 "certain").
- **"Lo, I see four men loose" → "Behold, I see four men loose"** (v25) — הָא. **"they have no hurt" → "there is no harm in them"** — חֲבָל. **"the form of the fourth is like the Son of God" → "the appearance of the fourth is like a son of gods"** — qere רְבִיעָאָה; בַר־אֱלָהִין, "son" without article and אֱלָהִין plural: "a son of gods", in the king's mouth a divine being (v28 he calls him "his angel"). KJV "the Son of God"; Christian reading sees the Son of God here.
- **"the mouth of the burning fiery furnace" → "the door of the furnace of burning fire"** (v26) — תְרַע, "gate, door" (2:49). **"ye servants of the most high God" → "servants of Elah the Most High"** — qere עִלָּאָה (ketiv עליא); token `elyon`. **"come forth, and come hither" → "come out, and come!"** — פֻּקוּ וֶאֱתוֹ. **"came forth of the midst of the fire" → "came out from the midst of the fire"**.
- **"being gathered together, saw these men, upon whose bodies the fire had no power" → "being gathered, were seeing these men, that the fire had no power over their bodies"** (v27) — participles. **"neither were their coats changed" → "and their cloaks were not changed"** — שְׁנוֹ, the verb of v19, 28. **"nor the smell of fire had passed on them" → "the smell of fire had not passed upon them"** — עֲדָת.
- **"Blessed be the God of Shadrach" → "Blessed be the Elah of Shadrach"** (v28) — אֱלָהֲהוֹן דִּי־שַׁדְרַךְ, literally "their Elah, of Shadrach" (so v29). **"his angel"** — מַלְאֲכֵהּ, the word is "messenger". **"have changed the king's word" → "they set aside the word of the king"** — וּמִלַּת מַלְכָּא שַׁנִּיו, literally "changed the word of the king": they made it of no effect. **"yielded their bodies" → "gave their bodies"** — qere גֶשְׁמְהוֹן.
- **"Therefore I make a decree" → "And by me a decree is made"** (v29) — וּמִנִּי שִׂים טְעֵם. **"speak any thing amiss" → "speaks negligence"** — qere שָׁלוּ "negligence" (as Ezra 4:22; ketiv שלה, "error"). **"shall be cut in pieces, and their houses shall be made a dunghill" → "shall be made into limbs, and his house shall be made a dunghill"** — as 2:5; בַיְתֵהּ singular. **"that can deliver after this sort" → "who is able to deliver like this"** — לְהַצָּלָה כִּדְנָה.
- **"promoted" → "made … prosper"** (v30) — הַצְלַח.

### Notes
- **Language** — the whole chapter is Aramaic (2:4b–7:28).
- **Numbering** — our 3:1–30 = Aramaic 3:1–30; the Aramaic 3:31–33 are our 4:1–3.
- **v25 "a son of gods"** (*bar elahin*) — read in Christian tradition as the Son of God; in the Aramaic, a divine being (v28 "his angel").
