# Daniel 4

[1] ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king, to all the peoples, nations, and tongues who dwell in all the earth: May your peace be multiplied!

[2] The signs and the wonders that ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ has done with me, it has seemed good before me to show.

[3] His signs, how great!
and his wonders, how mighty!
His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom,
and his dominion is with generation and generation.

[4] I, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, was at ease in my house and flourishing in my palace.

[5] A dream I saw, and it made me afraid; and the imaginings upon my bed and the visions of my head alarmed me.

[6] And by me a decree was made to bring in before me all the wise men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, that they should make known to me the interpretation of the dream.

[7] Then came in the magicians, the enchanters, the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, and the determiners; and the dream I told before them, but its interpretation they did not make known to me.

[8] And at last there came in before me ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, whose name is ⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧ according to the name of my god, and in whom is a spirit of holy gods; and the dream I told before him:

[9] "⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧, chief of the magicians, because I know that a spirit of holy gods is in you, and no mystery is too hard for you, the visions of my dream that I have seen, and its interpretation, tell.

[10] And the visions of my head upon my bed: I was seeing, and behold, a tree in the midst of the earth, and its height was great.

[11] The tree grew and became strong, and its height reached to the heavens, and its sight to the end of all the earth.

[12] Its foliage was fair and its fruit much, and in it was food for all; under it the beasts of the field found shade, and in its boughs dwelt the birds of the heavens, and from it all flesh was fed.

[13] I was seeing in the visions of my head upon my bed, and behold, a watcher and a holy one came down from the heavens.

[14] He called out with strength, and thus he said: 'Hew down the tree and cut off its boughs, strip off its foliage and scatter its fruit; let the beasts flee from under it, and the birds from its boughs.

[15] But leave the stump of its roots in the earth, and in a band of iron and bronze, in the vegetation of the field; and with the dew of the heavens let him be wet, and with the beasts be his portion in the plants of the earth.

[16] Let his heart be changed from man's, and let a beast's heart be given to him; and let seven times pass over him.

[17] By the decision of the watchers is the word, and the saying of the holy ones is the matter, to the end that the living may know that ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ rules in the kingdom of man, and to whom he wills he gives it, and the lowest of men he sets up over it.'

[18] This dream I saw, I, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king. And you, ⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧, tell its interpretation, because all the wise men of my kingdom are not able to make known to me the interpretation; but you are able, for a spirit of holy gods is in you."

[19] Then ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, whose name was ⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧, was appalled for about one hour, and his thoughts alarmed him. The king answered and said, "⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧, let not the dream and the interpretation alarm you." ⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧ answered and said, "My lord, the dream be for those who hate you, and its interpretation for your adversaries!

[20] The tree that you saw, which grew and became strong, and its height reached to the heavens, and its sight to all the earth,

[21] and its foliage fair and its fruit much, and in it food for all; under it the beasts of the field dwelt, and in its boughs the birds of the heavens abode —

[22] it is you, King, who have grown and become strong; and your greatness has grown and reached to the heavens, and your dominion to the end of the earth.

[23] And that the king saw a watcher and a holy one coming down from the heavens and saying, 'Hew down the tree and destroy it; but leave the stump of its roots in the earth, and in a band of iron and bronze, in the vegetation of the field; and with the dew of the heavens let him be wet, and with the beasts of the field be his portion, until seven times pass over him' —

[24] this is the interpretation, King, and it is the decision of ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ that has come upon my lord the king:

[25] and you shall be driven from men, and with the beasts of the field shall be your dwelling, and plants like oxen you shall be made to eat, and with the dew of the heavens you shall be wet, and seven times shall pass over you, until you know that ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ rules in the kingdom of man, and to whom he wills he gives it.

[26] And that they said to leave the stump of the roots of the tree: your kingdom shall be sure to you from when you know that **the heavens rule**.

[27] Therefore, King, let my counsel be pleasing to you: and break off your sins by righteousness, and your iniquities by showing favor to the afflicted; if there may be a lengthening of your ease."

[28] All this came upon ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king.

[29] At the end of twelve months he was walking upon the palace of the kingdom of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[30] The king answered and said, "Is not this great ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, which I have built for a house of the kingdom by the strength of my might and for the honor of my splendor?"

[31] While the word was in the mouth of the king, a voice fell from the heavens: "To you it is said, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ the king: the kingdom has passed from you,

[32] and from men you shall be driven, and with the beasts of the field shall be your dwelling; plants like oxen you shall be made to eat, and seven times shall pass over you, until you know that ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ rules in the kingdom of man, and to whom he wills he gives it."

[33] In that same hour the word was fulfilled upon ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧; and from men he was driven, and plants like oxen he ate, and with the dew of the heavens his body was wet, until his hair grew like eagles', and his nails like birds'.

[34] And at the end of the days I, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, lifted up my eyes to the heavens, and my knowledge returned to me; and ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ I blessed, and him who lives forever I praised and glorified, for his dominion is an everlasting dominion, and his kingdom is with generation and generation;

[35] and all the inhabitants of the earth are accounted as nothing, and according to his will he does in the host of the heavens and among the inhabitants of the earth; and there is none who can stay his hand or say to him, "What have you done?"

[36] At the same time my knowledge returned to me, and for the honor of my kingdom my splendor and my brightness returned to me; and my counselors and my lords sought me; and over my kingdom I was established, and excellent greatness was added to me.

[37] Now I, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, praise and exalt and glorify the King of the heavens, for all his works are truth and his ways justice; and those who walk in pride he is able to bring low.

## Changes

- **"the most high God" → "Elah the Most High"** (v2) — Aramaic אֱלָהָא עִלָּאָה (qere; ketiv עליא), tokens `elah-aram` and `elyon`; "the Most High" alone (v17, 24, 25, 32, 34) is qere עִלָּאָה, in v25, 32 written עִלָּיָא. **"my god"** (v8) — אֱלָהִי, the king's god: plain. **"the spirit of the holy gods" → "a spirit of holy gods"** (v8, 9, 18) — רוּחַ־אֱלָהִין קַדִּישִׁין, no article, plural noun and plural adjective: the king's words; "the spirit of the holy God" is the other reading (as the Hebrew plural אֱלֹהִים).
- **"Nebuchadnezzar the king"** (v1, 28, 31) — the Aramaic order; v18 "I, Nebuchadnezzar the king". **"languages" → "tongues"** (v1) — as 3:4. **"that dwell"** — qere דָיְרִין (ketiv דארין; so v35 qere דָּיְרֵי). **"Peace be multiplied unto you" → "May your peace be multiplied!"** — שְׁלָמְכוֹן יִשְׂגֵּא; שְׁלָם, the Aramaic form of *shalom*, plain "peace" (reviewer's row).
- **"I thought it good to shew" → "it has seemed good before me to show"** (v2) — שְׁפַר קָדָמַי. **"wrought toward me" → "has done with me"** — עֲבַד עִמִּי.
- **"How great are his signs" → "His signs, how great!"** (v3) — אָתוֹהִי כְּמָה רַבְרְבִין, Aramaic order. **"an everlasting kingdom"** (v3; v34 "an everlasting dominion") — מַלְכוּת עָלַם, literally "a kingdom of the age". **"from generation to generation" → "with generation and generation"** (v3, 34) — עִם־דָּר וְדָר: it lasts along with each generation.
- **"flourishing"** (v4) — רַעְנַן, the word for a green, leafy tree (Hebrew רַעֲנָן "luxuriant"), here of the king, before the dream of the tree.
- **"I saw a dream which made me afraid" → "A dream I saw, and it made me afraid"** (v5). **"the thoughts" → "the imaginings"** — הַרְהֹרִין (only here). **"troubled" → "alarmed"** (v5, 19) — בְּהַל, as 2:25 "in haste" (בְּהִתְבְּהָלָה).
- **"Therefore made I a decree" → "And by me a decree was made"** (v6) — וּמִנִּי שִׂים טְעֵם (as 3:29).
- **"the astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers" → "the enchanters, the Chaldeans, and the determiners"** (v7) — אָשְׁפַיָּא, qere כַּשְׂדָּאֵי, גָזְרַיָּא (as 2:27); qere עָלִּין "came in" (ketiv עללין).- **"But at the last" → "And at last"** (v8) — וְעַד אָחֳרֵין. **"whose name was … according to the name of my god" → "whose name is"** — the king speaks of the present.
- **"O Belteshazzar, master of the magicians" → "Belteshazzar, chief of the magicians"** (v9) — no "O"; רַב "chief" (as 2:48). **"no secret troubleth thee" → "no mystery is too hard for you"** — לָא־אָנֵס לָךְ, literally "does not press you". **"tell me the visions of my dream" → "the visions of my dream that I have seen, and its interpretation, tell"** — the Aramaic order; the king then tells the dream himself (v10–17), so "tell (me) the interpretation of the visions" is meant.
- **"Thus were the visions" → "And the visions of my head upon my bed"** (v10) — a heading without a verb. **"I saw" → "I was seeing"** (v10, 13) — חָזֵה הֲוֵית. **"a tree"** — אִילָן.
- **"the height thereof reached unto heaven" → "its height reached to the heavens"** (v11, 20, 22). **"and the sight thereof to the end of all the earth" → "and its sight to the end of all the earth"** — וַחֲזוֹתֵהּ: it was seen from everywhere (v20 "to all the earth").
- **"The leaves thereof were fair" → "Its foliage was fair"** (v12, 21) — עָפְיֵהּ שַׁפִּיר; v14 "strip off its foliage". **"had shadow" → "found shade"** — תַּטְלֵל (v12); v21 has תְּדוּר "dwelt". **"the fowls of the heaven dwelt in the boughs" → "in its boughs dwelt the birds of the heavens"** — qere יְדוּרָן (ketiv ידרון); v21 has יִשְׁכְּנָן "abode" (as Hebrew שָׁכַן). צִפַּר "bird"; עֲנַף "bough".
- **"a watcher and an holy one" → "a watcher and a holy one"** (v13, 23; v17 "the watchers … the holy ones") — עִיר, literally "one who is awake", a heavenly being; וְקַדִּישׁ may name the same being ("a watcher, a holy one"). **"came down"** — נָחִת.
- **"He cried aloud" → "He called out with strength"** (v14) — קָרֵא בְחַיִל (as 3:4). **"Hew down"** (v14, 23) — גֹּדּוּ. **"shake off his leaves" → "strip off its foliage"** — אַתַּרוּ.
- **"leave the stump of his roots in the earth, even with a band" → "leave the stump of its roots in the earth, and in a band"** (v15, 23) — וּבֶאֱסוּר, "and in a bond". **"in the tender grass of the field" → "in the vegetation of the field"** — בְּדִתְאָא (as Hebrew דֶּשֶׁא). **"in the grass of the earth" → "in the plants of the earth"** (v15; v25, 32, 33 "plants like oxen") — עֲשַׂב (as Hebrew עֵשֶׂב "plant", not "grass"). **"let it be wet" → "let him be wet"** — יִצְטַבַּע: from here the tree is spoken of as a man.
- **"his heart be changed from man's"** (v16) — qere אֲנָשָׁא (ketiv אנושא; also v17); לְבַב "heart" (not "mind"). **"let seven times pass over him"** (v16, 23, 25, 32) — שִׁבְעָה עִדָּנִין, "seven times (periods)": how long a "time" is, is not said.
- **"This matter is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones" → "By the decision of the watchers is the word, and the saying of the holy ones is the matter"** (v17) — בִּגְזֵרַת עִירִין פִּתְגָמָא וּמֵאמַר קַדִּישִׁין שְׁאֵלְתָא; גְּזֵרָה "decision" (root "cut"; also v24), kept apart from טְעֵם "decree" (v6); שְׁאֵלְתָא, literally "the asking": the matter laid down (uncertain). **"ruleth in the kingdom of men" → "rules in the kingdom of man"** (v17, 25, 32) — שַׁלִּיט … בְּמַלְכוּת אֲנָשָׁא. **"setteth up over it the basest of men" → "the lowest of men he sets up over it"** — qere עֲלַהּ.
- **"Now thou, O Belteshazzar" → "And you, Belteshazzar"** (v18) — qere וְאַנְתְּ (twice). **"declare the interpretation thereof" → "tell its interpretation"** — אֱמַר.
- **"was astonied for one hour" → "was appalled for about one hour"** (v19) — אֶשְׁתּוֹמַם כְּשָׁעָה חֲדָה, "as one hour": a short while. **"or the interpretation" → "and the interpretation"**. **"My lord"** — qere מָרִי (ketiv מראי). **"to them that hate thee … to thine enemies" → "for those who hate you … for your adversaries"** — qere לְשָׂנְאָךְ, לְעָרָךְ, עָר "adversary".
- **"It is thou, O king" → "it is you, King"** (v22) — qere אַנְתְּ.
- **"and destroy it"** (v23) — חַבְּלוּהִי (verb of חֲבָל "harm", 3:25). **"with the beasts of the field"** — v23 has חֵיוַת בָּרָא, v15 only חֵיוְתָא.
- **"this is the decree of the most High" → "and it is the decision of the Most High"** (v24) — וּגְזֵרַת עִלָּאָה הִיא; qere מָרִי.
- **"they shall drive thee … they shall make thee to eat grass as oxen, and they shall wet thee" → "you shall be driven … you shall be made to eat … you shall be wet"** (v25, 32) — plural verbs with no subject (טָרְדִין, יְטַעֲמוּן, מְצַבְּעִין): the Aramaic way of saying the passive; "they" is not a named agent. **"grass as oxen" → "plants like oxen"** — עִשְׂבָּא כְתוֹרִין.
- **"whereas they commanded to leave" → "And that they said to leave"** (v26) — וְדִי אֲמַרוּ. **"shall be sure unto thee" → "shall be sure to you"** — קַיָּמָה. **"after that thou shalt have known that the heavens do rule" → "from when you know that the heavens rule"** — מִן־דִּי; שַׁלִּטִן שְׁמַיָּא, "the heavens" for God (the only place in the Hebrew Bible).
- **"let my counsel be acceptable unto thee" → "let my counsel be pleasing to you"** (v27) — qere עֲלָךְ. **"break off thy sins by righteousness" → "and break off your sins by righteousness"** — qere וַחֲטָאָךְ (ketiv וחטיך); פְרֻק "break off" (also "redeem"); צִדְקָה "righteousness" (later Aramaic also "almsgiving"). **"by shewing mercy to the poor" → "by showing favor to the afflicted"** — בְּמִחַן עֲנָיִן (מִחַן root of Hebrew חָנַן "show favor"; עֲנָיִן as Hebrew עָנִי "afflicted"). **"if it may be a lengthening of thy tranquillity" → "if there may be a lengthening of your ease"** — הֵן … שְׁלֵוְתָךְ.
- **"he walked in the palace of the kingdom" → "he was walking upon the palace of the kingdom"** (v29) — עַל־הֵיכַל, "upon": on its roof (the word "roof" is not in the Aramaic).
- **"that I have built for the house of the kingdom by the might of my power, and for the honour of my majesty" → "for a house of the kingdom by the strength of my might and for the honor of my splendor"** (v30) — בִּתְקַף חִסְנִי וְלִיקָר הַדְרִי (as 2:37 חִסְנָא, תְּקָף, יְקָר); הֲדַר "splendor" (also v36), verb הַדַּר "glorify" (v34, 37).
- **"While the word was in the king's mouth"** (v31). **"O king Nebuchadnezzar, to thee it is spoken" → "To you it is said, Nebuchadnezzar the king"** — לָךְ אָמְרִין (as 3:4). **"The kingdom is departed from thee" → "the kingdom has passed from you"** — עֲדָת (as 3:27).
- **"over thee"** (v32) — qere עֲלָךְ. v32 has no clause about the dew.
- **"The same hour" → "In that same hour"** (v33) — בַּהּ־שַׁעֲתָא. **"was the thing fulfilled" → "the word was fulfilled"** — מִלְּתָא סָפַת. **"his hairs were grown like eagles' feathers, and his nails like birds' claws" → "his hair grew like eagles', and his nails like birds'"** — כְּנִשְׁרִין … כְצִפְּרִין: "feathers" and "claws" are not in the Aramaic.
- **"mine understanding" → "my knowledge"** (v34, 36) — מַנְדְּעִי (as 2:21 "knowledge"). **"I blessed the most High"** — qere וּלְעִלָּאָה (ketiv ולעליא). **"honoured" → "glorified"** — הַדְּרֵת. **"him that liveth for ever"** — לְחַי עָלְמָא, literally "the living one of the age". **"whose dominion" → "for his dominion"** — דִּי.
- **"are reputed as nothing" → "are accounted as nothing"** (v35) — כְּלָה חֲשִׁיבִין. **"in the army of heaven" → "in the host of the heavens"** — בְּחֵיל שְׁמַיָּא, the word of 3:20 "army". **"none can stay his hand"** — יְמַחֵא בִידֵהּ, literally "strike on his hand": restrain him.
- **"mine honour and brightness" → "my splendor and my brightness"** (v36) — הַדְרִי וְזִוִי (2:31 "brightness"). **"sought unto me" → "sought me"** — יְבַעוֹן. **"excellent majesty" → "excellent greatness"** — וּרְבוּ יַתִּירָה (רְבוּ as v22 "greatness").
- **"extol and honour" → "exalt and glorify"** (v37) — מְרוֹמֵם וּמְהַדַּר. **"truth … judgment" → "truth … justice"** — קְשֹׁט … דִּין. **"to abase" → "to bring low"** — לְהַשְׁפָּלָה (root of שְׁפַל "lowest", v17).

### Notes
- **Language** — Aramaic (2:4b–7:28).
- **Numbering** — our 4:1–3 = Aramaic 3:31–33; our 4:4–37 = Aramaic 4:1–34.
- **Voice** — the chapter is told by the king in the first person (v1–18, 34–37); v19–33 speak of him in the third person.
