# Amos 2

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
and for four, I will not turn it back;
because he burned the bones of the king of Edom
to lime.

[2] And I will send fire on ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
and it shall devour the citadels of ⟦kerioth|Qeriyot|Kerioth⟧;
and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall die in tumult,
with shouting, with the sound of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧;

[3] and I will cut off the judge from its midst,
and all its princes I will kill with him,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
and for four, I will not turn it back;
because they rejected the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and his statutes they did not keep,
and their lies caused them to wander,
after which their fathers walked.

[5] And I will send fire on ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
and it shall devour the citadels of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

[6] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and for four, I will not turn it back;
because they sold the righteous for silver,
and the needy for the sake of a pair of sandals —

[7] who pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor,
and the way of the humble they turn aside;
and a man and his father go to the girl,
in order to profane my holy name;

[8] and on garments taken in pledge they stretch out
beside every altar,
and the wine of those who were fined they drink
in the house of their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] And I myself destroyed the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧ from before them,
whose height was like the height of cedars,
and he was strong as the oaks;
and I destroyed his fruit from above
and his roots from beneath.

[10] And I myself brought you up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and made you walk in the wilderness forty years,
to take possession of the land of the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧.

[11] And I raised up some of your sons as prophets,
and some of your young men as ⟦nazirites|Nezirim|Nazirites⟧.
Is it not even so, sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] "But you made the ⟦nazirites|Nezirim|Nazirites⟧ drink wine,
and on the prophets you laid a command, saying,
'You shall not prophesy.'

[13] Behold, I am pressing down beneath you,
as the cart presses that is full of sheaves.

[14] And a place of flight shall perish from the swift,
and the strong shall not make firm his power,
and the mighty one shall not rescue his soul;

[15] and the one who handles the bow shall not stand,
and the swift of foot shall not rescue,
and the rider of the horse shall not rescue his soul;

[16] and the stout of heart among the mighty ones
shall flee naked in that day,"
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים. **"Thus saith the LORD" → "Thus says Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 6); **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v11, 16) — נְאֻם יְהוָה; "says Yahweh" (v3) for אָמַר יְהוָה.
- **"I will not turn away the punishment thereof" → "I will not turn it back"** (v1, 4, 6) — the refrain of 1:3–2:6 in one wording: לֹא אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּ (see 1:3); פֶּשַׁע "transgression".
- **"because he burned the bones of the king of Edom into lime" → "because he burned the bones of the king of Edom to lime"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׂרַף "burn"; לַשִּׂיד "to lime" (שִׂיד "plaster, lime").
- **"But I will send a fire upon Moab, and it shall devour the palaces of Kirioth: and Moab shall die with tumult, with shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet" → "And I will send fire on Moab, and it shall devour the citadels of Kerioth; and Moab shall die in tumult, with shouting, with the sound of the shofar"** (v2, 5) — as 1:4; הַקְּרִיּוֹת has the article ("the towns"), the name kept; שָׁאוֹן "tumult"; תְּרוּעָה "shouting" (no blowing is named: the war cry; with the shofar beside it, "a blast" is possible); בְּקוֹל "with the sound of".
- **"and will slay all the princes thereof with him" → "and all its princes I will kill with him"** (v3) — Hebrew order, the object first; הָרַג "kill"; שֹׁפֵט "judge" (the ruler).
- **"because they have despised the law of the LORD, and have not kept his commandments, and their lies caused them to err, after the which their fathers have walked" → "because they rejected the torah of Yahweh, and his statutes they did not keep, and their lies caused them to wander, after which their fathers walked"** (v4) — Hebrew מָאַס "reject"; תּוֹרַת יְהוָה, token *torah*; חֻקָּיו "his statutes" (not "commandments": that is מִצְוָה), the object first; הִתְעָה "cause to wander"; כְּזָבִים "lies" (no "but").
- **"because they sold the righteous for silver, and the poor for a pair of shoes" → "because they sold the righteous for silver, and the needy for the sake of a pair of sandals"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־מִכְרָם, literally "on account of their selling"; בַּכֶּסֶף "for silver" (a price: the word is "silver"); אֶבְיוֹן "needy"; בַּעֲבוּר "for the sake of" (or "for the price of"); נַעֲלַיִם "a pair of sandals" (the dual).
- **"That pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor, and turn aside the way of the meek: and a man and his father will go in unto the same maid, to profane my holy name" → "who pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor, and the way of the humble they turn aside; and a man and his father go to the girl, in order to profane my holy name"** (v7) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁאֲפִים "who pant after" (שָׁאַף; a second root "trample", taken by many here, as שׁוּף); דַּלִּים "the poor"; עֲנָוִים "the humble"; נָטָה "turn aside"; הָלַךְ אֶל "go to" (not the בּוֹא אֶל "go in to" of the marriage rows); הַנַּעֲרָה "the girl" (no "same"; what she is — a servant, a cult woman — is not said); לְמַעַן + infinitive "in order to"; שֵׁם קָדְשִׁי, literally "the name of my holiness".
- **"And they lay themselves down upon clothes laid to pledge by every altar, and they drink the wine of the condemned in the house of their god" → "and on garments taken in pledge they stretch out beside every altar, and the wine of those who were fined they drink in the house of their Elohim"** (v8) — Hebrew order; חֲבֻלִים "taken in pledge" (חָבַל); יַטּוּ "stretch out" (the verb of v7 "turn aside", נָטָה); אֵצֶל "beside"; עֲנוּשִׁים "those who were fined" (עָנַשׁ "fine"); אֱלֹהֵיהֶם "their Elohim" (or "their gods"; the word does not decide).
- **"Yet destroyed I the Amorite before them … yet I destroyed his fruit from above, and his roots from beneath" → "And I myself destroyed the Amorite from before them … and I destroyed his fruit from above and his roots from beneath"** (v9) — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי, the pronoun set first for weight ("I myself", also v10); הִשְׁמִיד "destroy"; מִפְּנֵיהֶם "from before them"; חָסֹן "strong"; אַלּוֹן "oak".
- **"Also I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and led you forty years through the wilderness, to possess the land of the Amorite" → "And I myself brought you up from the land of Egypt, and made you walk in the wilderness forty years, to take possession of the land of the Amorite"** (v10) — Hebrew הֶעֱלָה "bring up"; הוֹלִיךְ "make walk"; לָרֶשֶׁת "to take possession of".
- **"And I raised up of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for Nazarites. Is it not even thus, O ye children of Israel?" → "And I raised up some of your sons as prophets, and some of your young men as Nazirites. Is it not even so, sons of Israel?"** (v11) — Hebrew מִן "some of"; לְ "as" (literally "for"); בַּחוּרִים "young men"; נְזִירִים, token *nazirites*; הַאַף אֵין־זֹאת "is it not even so"; no "O"; בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "sons of Israel".
- **"But ye gave the Nazarites wine to drink; and commanded the prophets, saying, Prophesy not" → "But you made the Nazirites drink wine, and on the prophets you laid a command, saying, You shall not prophesy"** (v12) — Hebrew הִשְׁקָה "make drink"; צִוָּה עַל "lay a command on"; לֵאמֹר "saying"; לֹא תִּנָּבְאוּ "you shall not prophesy" (לֹא, not אַל).
- **"Behold, I am pressed under you, as a cart is pressed that is full of sheaves" → "Behold, I am pressing down beneath you, as the cart presses that is full of sheaves"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵעִיק, hifil participle of עוּק (only here): "press down" (or "make totter", "split open"; uncertain); תַּחְתֵּיכֶם "beneath you" (or "in your place"); הָעֲגָלָה "the cart" (עֲגָלָה "wagon, cart"); הַמְלֵאָה לָהּ, literally "that is full for itself"; עָמִיר "sheaves".
- **"Therefore the flight shall perish from the swift, and the strong shall not strengthen his force, neither shall the mighty deliver himself" → "And a place of flight shall perish from the swift, and the strong shall not make firm his power, and the mighty one shall not rescue his soul"** (v14) — Hebrew וְ "and" (no "therefore"); מָנוֹס "place of flight" (from נוּס "flee", v16); אִמֵּץ "make firm"; כֹּחַ "power"; גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; מִלֵּט נַפְשׁוֹ "rescue his soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; also v15).
- **"Neither shall he stand that handleth the bow; and he that is swift of foot shall not deliver himself" → "and the one who handles the bow shall not stand, and the swift of foot shall not rescue"** (v15) — Hebrew תֹּפֵשׂ הַקֶּשֶׁת "the one who handles the bow" (תָּפַשׂ "seize, hold"); קַל בְּרַגְלָיו "the swift of foot" (literally "swift in his feet"); לֹא יְמַלֵּט "shall not rescue" with no object (the Hebrew has none: "himself" is understood); רֹכֵב הַסּוּס "the rider of the horse".
- **"And he that is courageous among the mighty shall flee away naked in that day" → "and the stout of heart among the mighty ones shall flee naked in that day"** (v16) — Hebrew אַמִּיץ לִבּוֹ "stout of his heart"; גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones"; עָרוֹם "naked"; בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "in that day".

### Notes
- **v4–5** — Judah too is under the refrain: the Torah of Yahweh is the measure, where the nations were judged for their deeds of war.
- **v6** — "sell the righteous for silver" returns in 8:6.
