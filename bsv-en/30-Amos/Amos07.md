# Amos 7

[1] Thus ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ showed me: and behold, he was forming locusts at the beginning of the coming up of the late growth; and behold, it was late growth after the mowings of the king.

[2] And it was, when it had finished eating the plants of the land, that I said,

"⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, pardon, please!
How shall ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ stand?
For he is small."

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ relented concerning this: "It shall not be," said ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] Thus ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ showed me: and behold, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ was calling to contend by fire; and it devoured the great deep, and it was devouring the portion.

[5] And I said,

"⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, cease, please!
How shall ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ stand?
For he is small."

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ relented concerning this: "This also shall not be," said ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[7] Thus he showed me: and behold, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ was standing on a plumb-line wall, and in his hand a plumb line.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A plumb line." And ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ said,

"Behold, I am setting a plumb line
in the midst of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
I will not again pass by him any more;

[9] and the high places of ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ shall be desolate,
and the sanctuaries of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be laid waste,
and I will rise against the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ with the sword."

[10] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ the priest of ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ sent to ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. The land is not able to contain all his words.

[11] For thus Amos has said, 'By the sword ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ shall die, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall surely go into exile from upon his ground.'"

[12] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ said to Amos, "Seer, go, flee away to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and eat bread there, and there you shall prophesy;

[13] but at ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ you shall not again prophesy any more, for it is a king's sanctuary, and it is a kingdom's house."

[14] And Amos answered and said to ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧, "I am not a prophet, nor a son of a prophet; but I am a herdsman and a dresser of sycamores.

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ took me from behind the flock, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Go, prophesy to my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'

[16] And now hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. You are saying, 'You shall not prophesy against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and you shall not drip against the house of ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧.'

[17] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

'Your wife shall play the prostitute in the city,
and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword,
and your ground shall be divided by the line;
and you, on an unclean ground you shall die,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall surely go into exile from upon his ground.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv1, 2, 4, 5, 6) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words; vv7, 8 אֲדֹנָי alone: token `adonai`.
- **Vocatives without "O"** (vv2, 5, 12) — the Hebrew has no word for it (reviewer row).
- **"Thus hath the Lord GOD shewed unto me; and, behold, he formed grasshoppers" → "Thus the Lord Yahweh showed me: and behold, he was forming locusts"** (vv1, 4, 7) — Hebrew כֹּה הִרְאַנִי "thus he made me see"; וְהִנֵּה "and behold" (the וְ kept); יוֹצֵר, a participle (יָצַר "form", row); גֹּבַי "locusts" (a kind of locust; which is not known).
- **"in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth; and, lo, it was the latter growth after the king's mowings" → "at the beginning of the coming up of the late growth; and behold, it was late growth after the mowings of the king"** (v1) — Hebrew עֲלוֹת "the coming up" (עָלָה); לֶקֶשׁ "late growth" (the second crop after the spring rain); גִּזֵּי הַמֶּלֶךְ "the mowings of the king" (the king's share cut first).
- **"And it came to pass, that when they had made an end of eating the grass of the land, then I said" → "And it was, when it had finished eating the plants of the land, that I said"** (v2) — Hebrew וְהָיָה אִם־כִּלָּה, a weqatal where a wayyiqtol is expected (as printed; "and it was"); כִּלָּה "finish" (row), singular ("it": the swarm); עֵשֶׂב "plant" (row; not "grass").
- **"O Lord GOD, forgive, I beseech thee: by whom shall Jacob arise? for he is small" → "Lord Yahweh, pardon, please! How shall Jacob stand? For he is small"** (vv2, 5) — Hebrew סְלַח־נָא (סָלַח "pardon", row; נָא "please"); מִי יָקוּם יַעֲקֹב, literally "who shall Jacob stand?" (= "how", or "as who"): "how" is the sense, the word "who" in Changes; קוּם "stand" (row); כִּי קָטֹן הוּא "for he is small" ("so" is not written).
- **"The LORD repented for this" → "Yahweh relented concerning this"** (vv3, 6) — Hebrew נִחַם יְהוָה עַל־זֹאת (the נִחַם "relent concerning" row); אָמַר "said" (not נְאֻם).
- **"the Lord GOD called to contend by fire, and it devoured the great deep, and did eat up a part" → "the Lord Yahweh was calling to contend by fire; and it devoured the great deep, and it was devouring the portion"** (v4) — Hebrew קֹרֵא, a participle; לָרִב "to contend" (רִיב, row; "judgment" was a gloss; reading לִרְבִיב אֵשׁ "a shower of fire" is a conjecture, not followed); וַתֹּאכַל … וְאָכְלָה: the fire (feminine) devoured the deep, and was devouring (weqatal: what was going on or about to be) הַחֵלֶק "the portion" (חֵלֶק of land, row; Israel's allotted land is meant: the old "the land" gave the sense for the word).
- **"Then said I, O Lord GOD, cease, I beseech thee" → "And I said, 'Lord Yahweh, cease, please!'"** (v5) — Hebrew חֲדַל־נָא (חָדַל "cease", row).
- **"behold, the Lord stood upon a wall made by a plumbline, with a plumbline in his hand" → "behold, the Lord was standing on a plumb-line wall, and in his hand a plumb line"** (v7) — Hebrew נִצָּב עַל־חוֹמַת אֲנָךְ (נִצָּב "stand, be stationed", row); אֲנָךְ is found only in vv7–8: "plumb line" is the old sense, "tin" (as Akkadian *annaku*) is also proposed: uncertain; חוֹמַת אֲנָךְ "a wall of plumb line"; וּבְיָדוֹ אֲנָךְ "and in his hand a plumb line" (no verb).
- **"Amos, what seest thou?" → "What do you see, Amos?"** (v8) — Hebrew מָה־אַתָּה רֹאֶה עָמוֹס, the name last.
- **"I will not again pass by them any more" → "I will not again pass by him any more"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא־אוֹסִיף עוֹד עֲבוֹר לוֹ, literally "I will not add again to pass by him" (the יָסַף row; לוֹ singular: the people); עָבַר לְ "pass by" (= let go unpunished).
- **"the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste; and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam" → "the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste, and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam"** (v9) — Hebrew וְנָשַׁמּוּ "and shall be desolate" (שָׁמֵם, row; the וְ joins it to v8); יֶחֱרָבוּ "shall be laid waste" (חָרֵב); מִקְדְּשֵׁי "sanctuaries" (row); קַמְתִּי עַל "I will rise against".
- **"Then Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent" → "And Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁלַח. **"Amos hath conspired against thee" → "Amos has conspired against you"** — קָשַׁר עַל (row). **"the land is not able to bear all his words" → "The land is not able to contain all his words"** — לְהָכִיל "to contain, hold" ("bear" is the sense).
- **"Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel shall surely be led away captive out of their own land" → "By the sword Jeroboam shall die, and Israel shall surely go into exile from upon his ground"** (vv11, 17) — Hebrew בַּחֶרֶב first; גָּלֹה יִגְלֶה, literally "going into exile shall go into exile" (infinitive absolute: "surely", as 5:5); מֵעַל אַדְמָתוֹ "from upon his ground" (אֲדָמָה "ground", row; singular "his").
- **"O thou seer, go, flee thee away into the land of Judah, and there eat bread, and prophesy there" → "Seer, go, flee away to the land of Judah, and eat bread there, and there you shall prophesy"** (v12) — Hebrew חֹזֶה "seer (gazer)" (row); בְּרַח־לְךָ "flee for yourself" (the לְךָ of the "Get yourself" reviewer row: English has no reflexive for "flee"; "away" carries it); לֶחֶם "bread" (row); וְשָׁם תִּנָּבֵא, a yiqtol "and there you shall prophesy".
- **"But prophesy not again any more at Bethel: for it is the king's chapel, and it is the king's court" → "but at Bethel you shall not again prophesy any more, for it is a king's sanctuary, and it is a kingdom's house"** (v13) — Hebrew וּבֵית־אֵל first; לֹא־תוֹסִיף עוֹד "you shall not add again" (the יָסַף row); מִקְדַּשׁ־מֶלֶךְ "a sanctuary of a king" (מִקְדָּשׁ, row), בֵּית מַמְלָכָה "a house of a kingdom" (מַמְלָכָה, row; "temple" was a gloss for בַּיִת), both without the article.
- **"I was no prophet, neither was I a prophet's son; but I was an herdman, and a gatherer of sycomore fruit" → "I am not a prophet, nor a son of a prophet; but I am a herdsman and a dresser of sycamores"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹא־נָבִיא אָנֹכִי וְלֹא בֶן־נָבִיא אָנֹכִי כִּי־בוֹקֵר אָנֹכִי, verbless clauses: the Hebrew names no tense. "I am" is the plain English copula; "I was (no prophet when I was called)" is the other reading, and "I am not a prophet" is read by some as a denial that he belongs to the prophets' guilds (בֶּן־נָבִיא "son of a prophet" = a member of a band of prophets, as 2 Kgs 2:3): the verse does not decide. כִּי after the negative "but"; בּוֹקֵר "herdsman" (from בָּקָר "herd"); בּוֹלֵס "dresser" (found only here: one who nips the fruit to ripen it, uncertain); שִׁקְמִים "sycamores" (the sycamore fig).
- **"And the LORD took me as I followed the flock" → "And Yahweh took me from behind the flock"** (v15) — Hebrew מֵאַחֲרֵי הַצֹּאן "from behind the flock" (צֹאן, row); לָקַח "take" (row); לֵךְ הִנָּבֵא "Go, prophesy", two imperatives; אֶל "to".
- **"Now therefore hear thou the word of the LORD: Thou sayest, Prophesy not against Israel, and drop not thy word against the house of Isaac" → "And now hear the word of Yahweh. You are saying, 'You shall not prophesy against Israel, and you shall not drip against the house of Isaac'"** (v16) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה "and now"; אַתָּה אֹמֵר, a participle; לֹא תִנָּבֵא · וְלֹא תַטִּיף, לֹא with the yiqtol (a lasting prohibition, not אַל); הִטִּיף "drip" (row: of words let fall like rain; no object supplied; "preach" was a gloss; "thy word" is not written).
- **"Thy wife shall be an harlot in the city … and thy land shall be divided by line; and thou shalt die in a polluted land" → "Your wife shall play the prostitute in the city … and your ground shall be divided by the line; and you, on an unclean ground you shall die"** (v17) — Hebrew תִּזְנֶה "she shall play the prostitute" (זָנָה, row; a verb); אַדְמָתְךָ "your ground" and אֲדָמָה טְמֵאָה "an unclean ground" (אֲדָמָה, row; טָמֵא "unclean", row: a land outside Yahweh's, where the food is unclean); בַּחֶבֶל "by the line" (the measuring cord, with the article: חֶבֶל row); תְּחֻלָּק "shall be divided" (חָלַק); וְאַתָּה "and you", the pronoun first.
