# Amos 8

[1] Thus ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ showed me: and behold, a basket of summer fruit.

[2] And he said, "What do you see, Amos?" And I said, "A basket of summer fruit." And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me,

"The end has come to my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
I will not again pass by him any more.

[3] And the songs of the temple shall wail in that day,"
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧;
"many are the carcasses; in every place he has thrown them out.
Hush!"

[4] Hear this, you who pant after the needy,
and to make the afflicted of the land cease,

[5] saying, "When will the new moon pass,
so that we may sell grain?
And the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧,
so that we may open up grain?
To make the ephah small and to make the shekel great,
and to make crooked the balances of deceit,

[6] to buy the poor for money,
and the needy for a pair of sandals;
and the refuse of the grain we will sell."

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has sworn by the pride of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧:
"I will never forget any of their works."

[8] Shall not the land tremble on account of this,
and everyone who dwells in it mourn?
And it shall rise like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧, all of it,
and be tossed and sink like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[9] "And it shall be in that day," declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧,
"that I will make the sun set at noon,
and I will darken the earth in a day of light.

[10] And I will turn your feasts into mourning,
and all your songs into a dirge;
and I will bring up sackcloth on all loins,
and on every head baldness;
and I will make it like the mourning for an only one,
and its end like a bitter day.

[11] "Behold, days are coming," declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧,
"and I will send a famine on the land;
not a famine for bread, and not a thirst for water,
but for hearing the words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] And they shall stagger from sea to sea,
and from north even to east;
they shall roam to seek the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and they shall not find it.

[13] "In that day the beautiful virgins and the young men
shall faint for thirst;

[14] those who swear by the guilt of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧,
and say, 'As your god lives, Dan,'
and, 'As the way of ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧ lives,'
and they shall fall, and not rise again."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv1, 3, 9, 11) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **Poetic structure** — vv2b–14 set as verse; v1 and v2a stay prose.
- **Vocatives without "O"** (v14) — the Hebrew has no word for it (reviewer row).
- **"Thus hath the Lord GOD shewed unto me: and behold a basket of summer fruit" → "Thus the Lord Yahweh showed me: and behold, a basket of summer fruit"** (v1) — Hebrew כֹּה הִרְאַנִי (as 7:1); כְּלוּב "basket"; קַיִץ "summer (fruit)".
- **"The end is come upon my people of Israel; I will not again pass by them any more" → "The end has come to my people Israel; I will not again pass by him any more"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּא הַקֵּץ אֶל; קֵץ "end" (row). The vision turns on the sound: קַיִץ *qayitz* "summer fruit" — קֵץ *qetz* "end"; English cannot keep it. לֹא־אוֹסִיף עוֹד עֲבוֹר לוֹ as 7:8 (לוֹ singular).
- **"And the songs of the temple shall be howlings in that day" → "And the songs of the temple shall wail in that day"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהֵילִילוּ שִׁירוֹת הֵיכָל, the songs are the subject of the verb (הֵילִיל "wail", row); repointed שָׁרוֹת "singing women" is a conjecture, not followed; הֵיכָל "temple" (row; "palace" is also possible).
- **"there shall be many dead bodies in every place; they shall cast them forth with silence" → "many are the carcasses; in every place he has thrown them out. Hush!"** (v3) — Hebrew רַב הַפֶּגֶר "many is the carcass" (collective singular; פֶּגֶר, row); בְּכָל־מָקוֹם הִשְׁלִיךְ, a perfect with no subject named and no object ("them" supplied; also read as an infinitive "throwing out"); הָס "Hush!" (as 6:10). The line is terse and its sense uncertain.
- **"O ye that swallow up the needy, even to make the poor of the land to fail" → "you who pant after the needy, and to make the afflicted of the land cease"** (v4) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁאֲפִים "who pant after" (שָׁאַף, row; as 2:7; "trample" from another root, in Changes only); וְלַשְׁבִּית, an infinitive (הִשְׁבִּית "make cease", row); qere עֲנִיֵּי "the afflicted of" (עָנִי, row), ketiv ענוי "the humble of" (עָנָו): the sense is close.
- **"When will the new moon be gone, that we may sell corn? and the sabbath, that we may set forth wheat" → "When will the new moon pass, so that we may sell grain? And the Shabbat, so that we may open up grain?"** (v5) — Hebrew הַחֹדֶשׁ, here the day of the new moon beside the Shabbat (the "month" row is for the offering of the month; this sense needs "new moon"); וְנַשְׁבִּירָה שֶּׁבֶר "that we may sell grain" (הִשְׁבִּיר with its own noun שֶׁבֶר, row); וְנִפְתְּחָה־בָּר "that we may open up grain" (the stores; בָּר, another word for grain, row); עָבַר "pass".
- **"making the ephah small, and the shekel great, and falsifying the balances by deceit" → "To make the ephah small and to make the shekel great, and to make crooked the balances of deceit"** (v5) — Hebrew three infinitives with לְ; מֹאזְנֵי מִרְמָה "balances of deceit" (מִרְמָה, row); שֶׁקֶל "shekel", a weight (row).
- **"That we may buy the poor for silver, and the needy for a pair of shoes; yea, and sell the refuse of the wheat" → "to buy the poor for money, and the needy for a pair of sandals; and the refuse of the grain we will sell"** (v6) — Hebrew בַּכֶּסֶף (כֶּסֶף "money" of a price, row; the word is "silver"); בַּעֲבוּר נַעֲלָיִם "for the sake of sandals" (dual: a pair); מַפַּל בַּר "what falls of the grain" (the refuse; "chaff" is מֹץ); נַשְׁבִּיר "we will sell (grain)".
- **"The LORD hath sworn by the excellency of Jacob, Surely I will never forget any of their works" → "Yahweh has sworn by the pride of Jacob: 'I will never forget any of their works'"** (v7) — Hebrew גְּאוֹן יַעֲקֹב "the pride of Jacob" (as 6:8, where Yahweh abominates it; whether it is a name of God here, as "the Glory of Israel", is debated); the oath אִם־אֶשְׁכַּח לָנֶצַח "if I forget forever" = "I will never forget" (Standing decisions); כָּל־מַעֲשֵׂיהֶם "all their works".
- **"Shall not the land tremble for this, and every one mourn that dwelleth therein? and it shall rise up wholly as a flood; and it shall be cast out and drowned, as by the flood of Egypt" → "Shall not the land tremble on account of this, and everyone who dwells in it mourn? And it shall rise like the Nile, all of it, and be tossed and sink like the Nile of Egypt"** (v8) — Hebrew תִרְגַּז (רָגַז "be agitated, tremble", row); כָאֹר is written without the yod of יְאֹר (9:5 has כַּיְאֹר in the same line): read as "like the Nile" (token; the letters could be read "like the light", which gives no sense; no qere is printed); וְנִגְרְשָׁה "and be tossed"; qere וְנִשְׁקְעָה "and sink" (ketiv ונשקה "and be watered"); כֻּלָּהּ "all of it" stands last, as printed.
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day … that I will cause the sun to go down at noon, and I will darken the earth in the clear day" → "And it shall be in that day … that I will make the sun set at noon, and I will darken the earth in a day of light"** (v9) — Hebrew וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא (the prophets' formula row); וְהֵבֵאתִי הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ, literally "I will bring in the sun" (the sun "comes in" when it sets); בְּיוֹם אוֹר "in a day of light".
- **"I will bring up sackcloth upon all loins, and baldness upon every head; and I will make it as the mourning of an only son" → "I will bring up sackcloth on all loins, and on every head baldness; and I will make it like the mourning for an only one"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהַעֲלֵיתִי "and I will bring up"; מָתְנַיִם "loins" (not "waist"); שַׂק "sackcloth" (row); קָרְחָה "baldness" (row); אֵבֶל יָחִיד "the mourning of an only one" (יָחִיד, row: "son" is not in the word); קִינָה "dirge" (row); אַחֲרִיתָהּ "its end".
- **"Behold, the days come … that I will send a famine in the land … but of hearing the words of the LORD" → "Behold, days are coming … and I will send a famine on the land; not a famine for bread, and not a thirst for water, but for hearing the words of Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew הִנֵּה יָמִים בָּאִים, no article (as 4:2); וְהִשְׁלַחְתִּי, a weqatal "and I will send"; לַלֶּחֶם · לַמַּיִם "for bread, for water"; כִּי אִם after the negative, one thing put in place of another: "but" (reviewer row); לִשְׁמֹעַ "for hearing" (שָׁמַע, row).
- **"And they shall wander from sea to sea, and from the north even to the east, they shall run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it" → "And they shall stagger from sea to sea, and from north even to east; they shall roam to seek the word of Yahweh, and they shall not find it"** (v12) — Hebrew וְנָעוּ (נוּעַ "stagger", row; as 4:8); מִיָּם עַד־יָם "from sea to sea" (the seas themselves, not "west"); יְשׁוֹטְטוּ "they shall roam" (שׁוּט); בִּקֵּשׁ "seek" (row); "it" supplied for English.
- **"In that day shall the fair virgins and young men faint for thirst" → "In that day the beautiful virgins and the young men shall faint for thirst"** (v13) — Hebrew הַבְּתוּלֹת הַיָּפוֹת "the beautiful virgins" (בְּתוּלָה "virgin", row); תִּתְעַלַּפְנָה "shall faint" (feminine, but said of both); בַּצָּמָא "for (with) the thirst" (צָמָא, row).
- **"They that swear by the sin of Samaria, and say, Thy god, O Dan, liveth; and, The manner of Beer-sheba liveth; even they shall fall, and never rise up again" → "those who swear by the guilt of Samaria, and say, 'As your god lives, Dan,' and, 'As the way of Beersheba lives,' and they shall fall, and not rise again"** (v14) — Hebrew אַשְׁמַת "guilt" (אַשְׁמָה, row; "Ashima", a god of Samaria's settlers, 2 Kgs 17:30, is a repointing, not followed); חֵי "as … lives" (the oath form); אֱלֹהֶיךָ of Dan's idol: plain "god"; דֶּרֶךְ "way" (row; the pilgrim road, or a name; דֹּדְךָ "your beloved" is a conjecture); וְנָפְלוּ "and they shall fall" (the וְ kept); לֹא־יָקוּמוּ עוֹד "not rise again" ("never" says more than עוֹד).
