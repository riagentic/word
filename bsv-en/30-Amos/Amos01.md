# Amos 1

[1] The words of Amos, who was among the sheep breeders from ⟦tekoa|Teqoa|Tekoa⟧, which he beheld concerning ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the days of ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the days of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, two years before the earthquake.

[2] And he said:
"⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ roars from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ he utters his voice;
and the pastures of the shepherds mourn,
and the top of ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ withers."

[3] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧,
and for four, I will not turn it back;
because they threshed ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧
with the threshing sledges of iron.

[4] And I will send fire on the house of ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧,
and it shall devour the citadels of ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧.

[5] And I will break the bar of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧,
and cut off the inhabitant from the plain of Aven,
and the one who holds the scepter from ⟦beth-eden|Beit Eden|Beth-eden⟧;
and the people of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ shall go into exile to ⟦kir|Qir|Kir⟧,
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[6] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧,
and for four, I will not turn it back;
because they carried into exile a whole exile,
to hand them over to Edom.

[7] And I will send fire on the wall of ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧,
and it shall devour its citadels.

[8] And I will cut off the inhabitant from Ashdod,
and the one who holds the scepter from ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧;
and I will turn my hand against ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧,
and the remnant of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ shall perish,"
says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[9] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧,
and for four, I will not turn it back;
because they handed over a whole exile to Edom,
and did not remember the covenant of brothers.

[10] And I will send fire on the wall of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧,
and it shall devour its citadels."

[11] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of Edom,
and for four, I will not turn it back;
because he pursued his brother with the sword,
and ruined his compassion,
and his anger tore perpetually,
and his raging he kept forever.

[12] And I will send fire on Teman,
and it shall devour the citadels of ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧."

[13] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of the sons of Ammon,
and for four, I will not turn it back;
because they split open the women with child of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧,
in order to widen their border.

[14] And I will kindle fire on the wall of Rabbah,
and it shall devour its citadels,
with shouting on the day of battle,
with a storm on the day of tempest;

[15] and their king shall go into exile,
he and his princes together,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words. **"Thus saith the LORD" → "Thus says Yahweh"** (v3, 6, 9, 11, 13) — כֹּה אָמַר יְהוָה, the one wording of the prophets; אָמַר יְהוָה at the end of an oracle "says Yahweh" (v5, 8, 15).
- **"who was among the herdmen of Tekoa, which he saw" → "who was among the sheep breeders from Tekoa, which he beheld"** (v1) — Hebrew נֹקְדִים "sheep breeders" (as Mesha, 2 Kgs 3:4); מִתְּקוֹעַ "from Tekoa"; חָזָה "behold" (as Isa 1:1; 2:1). הָרַעַשׁ "the earthquake" (literally "the quaking").
- **"The LORD will roar from Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the habitations of the shepherds shall mourn, and the top of Carmel shall wither" → "Yahweh roars from Zion, and from Jerusalem he utters his voice; and the pastures of the shepherds mourn, and the top of Carmel withers"** (v2) — the first two lines are the Hebrew of Joel 3:16, in the same wording: שָׁאַג "roar"; נָתַן קוֹלוֹ "utter his voice" (literally "give"); "from Jerusalem" first in its line. The verbs are yiqtol and weqatal: present (as Joel 3:16), the future equally possible. נְאוֹת "pastures"; אָבַל "mourn"; יָבֵשׁ "wither" (be dried out).
- **"For three transgressions of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof" → "For three transgressions of Damascus, and for four, I will not turn it back"** (v3, 6, 9, 11, 13; 2:1, 4, 6) — one wording for the refrain in all eight oracles. Hebrew פֶּשַׁע "transgression" (a breach, a revolt); לֹא אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּ "I will not turn it back" (הֵשִׁיב with "it"): what "it" is the text does not say (the punishment, the decree, the word of v2); "the punishment" is not in the Hebrew. "Three … and four": the number named, and one more.
- **"because they have threshed Gilead with threshing instruments of iron" → "because they threshed Gilead with the threshing sledges of iron"** (v3) — Hebrew עַל־דּוּשָׁם, literally "on account of their threshing"; חֲרֻצוֹת הַבַּרְזֶל "the threshing sledges of iron" (sledges set with iron teeth; the article kept).
- **"But I will send a fire into the house of Hazael, which shall devour the palaces of Benhadad" → "And I will send fire on the house of Hazael, and it shall devour the citadels of Ben-hadad"** (v4, 7, 10, 12, 14; 2:2, 5) — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּחְתִּי "and I will send" (no "but", no "so"); אֵשׁ "fire" without the article; בְּ "on" each time; וְאָכְלָה "and it shall devour"; אַרְמוֹן "citadel" (the fortified great house of a city).
- **"I will break also the bar of Damascus, and cut off the inhabitant from the plain of Aven, and him that holdeth the sceptre from the house of Eden: and the people of Syria shall go into captivity unto Kir" → "And I will break the bar of Damascus, and cut off the inhabitant from the plain of Aven, and the one who holds the scepter from Beth-eden; and the people of Aram shall go into exile to Kir"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּרִיחַ "bar" (of the city gate); יוֹשֵׁב "inhabitant" (or "the one who sits", enthroned, beside "the one who holds the scepter"; both in v8); בִּקְעָה "plain"; אָוֶן "wickedness", here a name; תָּמַךְ "hold"; שֵׁבֶט "scepter"; גָּלָה "go into exile"; קִירָה "to Kir".
- **"because they carried away captive the whole captivity, to deliver them up to Edom" → "because they carried into exile a whole exile, to hand them over to Edom"** (v6) — Hebrew הַגְלוֹתָם גָּלוּת שְׁלֵמָה, verb and noun of one root, literally "their exiling of a whole exile" (an entire population, none left); הִסְגִּיר "hand over". v9 has the same words.
- **"and I will turn mine hand against Ekron … saith the Lord GOD" → "and I will turn my hand against Ekron … says Lord Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew הֲשִׁיבוֹתִי יָדִי עַל, הֵשִׁיב, the verb of the refrain ("turn back"); שְׁאֵרִית "remnant".
- **"and remembered not the brotherly covenant" → "and did not remember the covenant of brothers"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּרִית אַחִים "covenant of brothers"; זָכַר "remember".
- **"because he did pursue his brother with the sword, and did cast off all pity, and his anger did tear perpetually, and he kept his wrath for ever" → "because he pursued his brother with the sword, and ruined his compassion, and his anger tore perpetually, and his raging he kept forever"** (v11) — Hebrew שִׁחֵת רַחֲמָיו "ruined his compassion" (שִׁחֵת "ruin"; רַחֲמִים "compassion", from רֶחֶם "womb": some take it as "his kin" or "his allies"; uncertain); טָרַף "tear" (as a beast); לָעַד "perpetually"; עֶבְרָה "raging"; נֶצַח "forever". שְׁמָרָה is pointed without the mark of "it" (שְׁמָרָהּ "he kept it"); read so, with the object first; "it kept watch" (the raging as subject) is the other reading.
- **"because they have ripped up the women with child of Gilead, that they might enlarge their border" → "because they split open the women with child of Gilead, in order to widen their border"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן "the sons of Ammon"; בָּקַע "split"; הָרוֹת "women with child"; לְמַעַן + infinitive "in order to"; הִרְחִיב "widen"; גְּבוּל "border".
- **"But I will kindle a fire in the wall of Rabbah … with shouting in the day of battle, with a tempest in the day of the whirlwind" → "And I will kindle fire on the wall of Rabbah … with shouting on the day of battle, with a storm on the day of tempest"** (v14) — Hebrew הִצַּתִּי "kindle"; תְּרוּעָה "shouting" (no instrument named); סַעַר "storm", סוּפָה "tempest".
- **"And their king shall go into captivity" → "and their king shall go into exile"** (v15) — Hebrew מַלְכָּם "their king" (the Greek and some read "Milcom", the god, as in Jer 49:1, 3 — from memory; the Masoretic Text is kept); גּוֹלָה "exile"; שָׂרִים "princes".

### Notes
- **"Three transgressions and four"** — the refrain of eight oracles (1:3–2:6), moving from the nations round about to Judah and Israel.
- **The earthquake** (v1) — remembered still in Zech 14:5.
