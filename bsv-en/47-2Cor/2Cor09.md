# 2 Corinthians 9

[1] For concerning the service to the saints it is superfluous for me to write to you,

[2] for I know your readiness, of which I boast on your behalf to the ⟦macedonians|Makedones|Macedonians⟧, that Achaia has been prepared since last year; and your zeal stirred up the greater number.

[3] But I sent the brothers, so that our boast on your behalf may not be emptied in this respect, so that, just as I was saying, you may be prepared,

[4] lest perhaps, if ⟦macedonians|Makedones|Macedonians⟧ come with me and find you unprepared, we — that we may not say you — should be put to shame in this assurance.

[5] Therefore I counted it necessary to exhort the brothers to go on ahead to you and put in order beforehand your blessing promised beforehand, that this be ready thus as a blessing and not as covetousness.

[6] But this: **he who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and he who sows upon blessings will also reap upon blessings**.

[7] Each one as he has purposed in his heart, not out of sorrow or out of necessity; for **⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ loves a cheerful giver**.

[8] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is powerful to make all grace abound unto you, so that in everything, always, having all sufficiency, you may abound unto every good work;

[9] (as it is written,

"**He scattered, he gave to the needy;
his righteousness abides forever**";

[10] and he who supplies seed to the sower and bread for eating will provide and multiply your seed and make the produce of your righteousness grow;)

[11] in everything being made rich unto all simplicity, which works out through us thanksgiving to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ —

[12] because the service of this ministry is not only filling up the lacks of the saints, but also abounding through many thanksgivings to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ —

[13] through the proof of this service glorifying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for the subjection of your confession unto the gospel of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and for the simplicity of the fellowship unto them and unto all,

[14] and they, with supplication on your behalf, longing for you because of the exceeding grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ upon you.

[15] Thanks be to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for his indescribable gift.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v13) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"ministering" → "service"** (v1, 12, 13) — Greek διακονία; "ministry" (v12) is λειτουργία, a public or sacred service.
- **"forwardness of your mind" → "readiness"** (v2) — Greek προθυμίαν, as 8:11, 12, 19. "On your behalf" is ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν (also v3, 14).
- **"them of Macedonia" → "the Macedonians"** (v2) — Greek Μακεδόσιν, the people; v4 Μακεδόνες has no "some".
- **"was ready a year ago" → "has been prepared since last year"** (v2) — Greek παρεσκεύασται ἀπὸ πέρυσι, perfect (as 8:10); the same verb in v3 ("prepared") and, negated, v4 ("unprepared").
- **"hath provoked very many" → "stirred up the greater number"** (v2) — Greek ἠρέθισε τοὺς πλείονας, aorist; "the more", the majority.
- **"have I sent" → "I sent"** (v3) — Greek ἔπεμψα, aorist (see 8:17).
- **"lest our boasting of you should be in vain in this behalf" → "so that our boast on your behalf may not be emptied in this respect"** (v3) — Greek τὸ καύχημα … κενωθῇ ἐν τῷ μέρει τούτῳ; κενόω is "empty" (Phil 2:7).
- **"we (that we say not, ye)" → "we — that we may not say you —"** (v4) — Greek ἵνα μὴ λέγωμεν ὑμεῖς (SBLGNT, WH, Treg). NA28: λέγω, "that I may not say".
- **"in this same confident boasting" → "in this assurance"** (v4) — Greek ἐν τῇ ὑποστάσει ταύτῃ; the printed text has no "of boasting". ὑπόστασις is what stands under: a ground, a firm confidence; some take it here as "undertaking".
- **"thought" → "counted"** (v5) — Greek ἡγησάμην. "Exhort" is παρακαλέσαι.
- **"make up beforehand your bounty, whereof ye had notice before" → "put in order beforehand your blessing promised beforehand"** (v5) — Greek προκαταρτίσωσι τὴν προεπηγγελμένην εὐλογίαν ὑμῶν: three verbs with προ- ("ahead", "beforehand", "beforehand"). προκαταρτίζω is a compound of καταρτίζω "mend, fit together".
- **"bounty" → "blessing"** (v5, 6) — Greek εὐλογία, "blessing", here of a gift given freely and fully; "bountifully" (v6) is ἐπʼ εὐλογίαις, "upon blessings", twice.
- **"covetousness" kept** (v5) — Greek πλεονεξίαν, the wish to have more: a gift given as by people who would rather keep it, or one wrung out of them.
- **"But this I say" → "But this:"** (v6) — Greek Τοῦτο δέ, without a verb. "Sparingly" is φειδομένως, from φείδομαι "spare".
- **"so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity" → "Each one as he has purposed in his heart, not out of sorrow or out of necessity"** (v7) — the Greek has no verb "give"; ἐκ λύπης is "out of sorrow" (λύπη, as 7:10), ἐξ ἀνάγκης "out of necessity".
- **"God loveth a cheerful giver" → "*Elohim* loves a cheerful giver"** (v7) — Prov 22:8 LXX citation.
- **"able" → "powerful"** (v8) — Greek δυνατεῖ. "Sufficiency" is αὐτάρκειαν, having enough of one's own. "Unto" is εἰς twice.
- **"He hath dispersed abroad; he hath given to the poor" → "He scattered, he gave to the needy"** (v9) — Greek Ἐσκόρπισεν, ἔδωκεν τοῖς πένησιν, two aorists; πένης is the man who must work for his bread, a different word from "poor" (πτωχός, 6:10). "Abides" is μένει.
- **"(…)"** (v9–10) — the printed text sets v9–10 in brackets; v11 goes on from v8.
- **"minister" → "provide"** (v10) — Greek χορηγήσει, the simple verb beside ἐπιχορηγῶν "supplies". The three verbs are future in the printed text ("will provide … multiply … make grow"); other manuscripts have wishes. "Produce" is γενήματα.
- **"Being enriched in every thing to all bountifulness" → "in everything being made rich unto all simplicity"** (v11) — Greek πλουτιζόμενοι, a participle with no main verb; ἁπλότητα as 8:2. "Works out" is κατεργάζεται.
- **"the administration of this service not only supplieth the want" → "the service of this ministry is not only filling up the lacks"** (v12) — Greek ἡ διακονία τῆς λειτουργίας ταύτης … προσαναπληροῦσα τὰ ὑστερήματα. "Abounding" is περισσεύουσα.
- **"by the experiment of this ministration they glorify God for your professed subjection" → "through the proof of this service glorifying *Elohim* for the subjection of your confession"** (v13) — Greek διὰ τῆς δοκιμῆς … δοξάζοντες … ἐπὶ τῇ ὑποταγῇ τῆς ὁμολογίας ὑμῶν: the participle has no stated subject (the saints who receive, or the givers).
- **"for your liberal distribution" → "for the simplicity of the fellowship"** (v13) — Greek ἁπλότητι τῆς κοινωνίας; "others" is not in the Greek.
- **"by their prayer for you, which long after you" → "and they, with supplication on your behalf, longing for you"** (v14) — Greek καὶ αὐτῶν δεήσει ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν ἐπιποθούντων ὑμᾶς, a genitive absolute without a main verb. "Exceeding" is ὑπερβάλλουσαν.
- **"Thanks be unto God" kept** (v15) — Greek χάρις τῷ θεῷ, the word "grace" (v8, 14). "Indescribable" is ἀνεκδιηγήτῳ, what cannot be told out; "gift" is δωρεᾷ.

### Notes
- **v9** quotes Ps 112:9.
