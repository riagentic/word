# 2 Corinthians 6

[1] And working together, we also appeal that you not accept the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in vain;

[2] for he says,

"**At an acceptable time I heard you,
and in a day of salvation I helped you**";

behold, now is the well-acceptable time; behold, now is the day of salvation;

[3] giving no cause of stumbling in anything, so that the service may not be blamed,

[4] but in everything commending ourselves as servants of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: in much endurance, in afflictions, in necessities, in straits,

[5] in blows, in prisons, in disorders, in labors, in watchings, in fastings,

[6] in purity, in knowledge, in patience, in kindness, in the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, in love without hypocrisy,

[7] in the word of truth, in the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; through the weapons of righteousness of the right hand and of the left,

[8] through glory and dishonor, through ill report and good report; as deceivers and true,

[9] as unknown and fully known, as dying and behold, we live, as disciplined and not put to death,

[10] as grieved but always rejoicing, as poor but making many rich, as having nothing and possessing all things.

[11] Our mouth is open toward you, ⟦corinthians|Korinthioi|Corinthians⟧; our heart is widened;

[12] you are not straitened in us, but you are straitened in your inward parts;

[13] now as the same recompense — I speak as to children — be widened, you also.

[14] Do not become unequally yoked with unbelievers; for what partaking have righteousness and lawlessness, or what fellowship has light with darkness?

[15] And what harmony has ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ with ⟦beliar|Beliar|Belial⟧, or what portion has a believer with an unbeliever?

[16] And what agreement has the sanctuary of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with idols? For we are the sanctuary of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; as ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said,

"**I will dwell in them and walk among them,
and I will be their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and they shall be my people**.
[17] Therefore come out from the midst of them,
and be separated, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧,
and do not touch an unclean thing;
and I will welcome you,
[18] and I will be to you a father,
and you shall be to me sons and daughters,
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ Almighty."

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v15) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 4, 7, 16) — systematic.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v6) — systematic. Greek ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ has no article: "in the Holy Spirit" or "in a holy spirit", in a list of qualities.
- **"We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also" → "And working together, we also appeal"** (v1) — Greek Συνεργοῦντες δὲ καὶ παρακαλοῦμεν; "with him" is not in the Greek (working together with *Elohim*, 5:20, or with the Corinthians). παρακαλέω is "appeal" for a request in this letter (5:20). "Accept" is δέξασθαι.
- **"he saith"** (v2) — Greek λέγει has no subject: "he says" or "it says".
- **"a time accepted" / "the accepted time" → "an acceptable time" / "the well-acceptable time"** (v2) — Greek δεκτῷ in the quotation, the stronger εὐπρόσδεκτος in Paul's own words. "Heard" (ἐπήκουσα) and "helped" (ἐβοήθησα) are both aorist.
- **"Giving no offence" kept as a participle** (v3–10) — the Greek sentence runs from v1 ("we appeal") through v10 on participles; v2 is an aside. "The service" (ἡ διακονία) has no "our".
- **"distresses" → "straits"** (v4) — Greek στενοχωρίαις, narrow places; the same root as "straitened" in v12.
- **"tumults" → "disorders"** (v5) — Greek ἀκαταστασίαις. "Watchings" is ἀγρυπνίαις, nights without sleep; "fastings" is νηστείαις, going without food, by choice or not.
- **"by" → "in"** (v4–7) — Greek ἐν eighteen times, then διά ("through") three times, then ὡς ("as") seven times; the three series are kept apart.
- **"honour" → "glory"** (v8) — Greek δόξης.
- **"well known" → "fully known"** (v9) — Greek ἐπιγινωσκόμενοι.
- **"chastened" → "disciplined"** (v9) — Greek παιδευόμενοι, elsewhere "train"; here the correcting blow is meant (Ps 118:18).
- **"sorrowful" → "grieved"** (v10) — Greek λυπούμενοι, passive.
- **"possessing"** (v10) — Greek κατέχοντες, a compound of the verb just used (ἔχοντες, "having"): having nothing, having everything in hand. Elsewhere "hold fast".
- **"O ye Corinthians, our mouth is open unto you, our heart is enlarged" → "Our mouth is open toward you, Corinthians; our heart is widened"** (v11) — Greek ἀνέῳγεν, πεπλάτυνται, both perfect; the same verb returns in v13 ("be widened").
- **"bowels" → "inward parts"** (v12) — Greek σπλάγχνοις.
- **"be ye also enlarged" → "be widened, you also"** (v13) — Greek πλατύνθητε καὶ ὑμεῖς; "your hearts" is not in the Greek. "As the same recompense" is τὴν αὐτὴν ἀντιμισθίαν, an accusative without a verb.
- **"unequally yoked" kept** (v14) — Greek ἑτεροζυγοῦντες, yoked with an animal of another kind (Lev 19:19 LXX ἑτερόζυγος).
- **"fellowship" → "partaking"** (v14) — Greek μετοχή; "fellowship" is κοινωνία in the next clause.
- **"he that believeth" → "a believer"** (v15) — Greek πιστῷ, elsewhere "faithful"; here it stands against ἀπίστου, "unbeliever" (v14).
- **"I will dwell in them, and walk in them"** (v16) — Greek Ἐνοικήσω ἐν αὐτοῖς καὶ ἐμπεριπατήσω: ἐν αὐτοῖς stands once, "in them" or "among them".
- **"be ye separate" → "be separated"** (v17) — Greek ἀφορίσθητε, passive; "from them" is not repeated. "And I" is κἀγώ.
- **OT quotations** (v2 from Isa 49:8; v16 echoing Lev 26:12 / Ezek 37:27; v17 from Isa 52:11; v18 echoing 2 Sam 7:14) — all noted; formatted as poetry.

### Notes
- **v2** quotes Isa 49:8.
- **v16** echoes Lev 26:12; Ezek 37:27.
- **v17** quotes Isa 52:11.
- **v18** echoes 2 Sam 7:14.
