# 2 Corinthians 13

[1] This third time I am coming to you. "**At the mouth of two witnesses and of three shall every word be established**."

[2] I have said before and I say beforehand, as when present the second time, so now being absent, to those who have sinned before and to all the rest, that if I come again I will not spare,

[3] since you seek a proof of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ speaking in me, who toward you is not weak but is powerful among you;

[4] for indeed he was crucified out of weakness, but he lives out of the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. For we also are weak in him, but we will live with him out of the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ toward you.

[5] **Put yourselves to the test, whether you are in the faith; test yourselves.** Or do you not fully know yourselves, that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is in you? — unless indeed you are unapproved.

[6] But I hope that you will know that we are not unapproved.

[7] But we pray to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that you do nothing evil — not that we may appear approved, but that you may do the good, and we be as unapproved.

[8] For we can do nothing against the truth, but on behalf of the truth.

[9] For we rejoice when we are weak and you are powerful; this also we pray for, your restoration.

[10] Because of this I write these things being absent, that being present I may not deal sharply, according to the authority which the Lord gave me for building up and not for pulling down.

[11] Finally, brothers, rejoice, be restored, be encouraged, think the same thing, be at peace; and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of love and peace will be with you.

[12] Greet one another with a holy kiss.

[13] All the saints greet you.

[14] **The grace of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the fellowship of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ be with you all.**

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v5, 14) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v14) — Greek τοῦ ἁγίου πνεύματος; Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness"; preserved in the famous Trinitarian benediction.
- **"This is the third time I am coming to you" → "This third time I am coming to you"** (v1) — Greek τρίτον τοῦτο ἔρχομαι, as 12:14.
- **"In the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established" → "At the mouth of two witnesses and of three shall every word be established"** (v1) — Greek ἐπὶ στόματος δύο μαρτύρων καὶ τριῶν σταθήσεται πᾶν ῥῆμα: καί, "and", not "or"; ῥῆμα, "word, matter"; the Greek has neither "charge" nor "evidence".
- **"I told you before, and foretell you, as if I were present, the second time; and being absent now I write to them which heretofore have sinned" → "I have said before and I say beforehand, as when present the second time, so now being absent, to those who have sinned before"** (v2) — Greek προείρηκα καὶ προλέγω ὡς παρὼν τὸ δεύτερον καὶ ἀπὼν νῦν: literally "as being present the second time and being absent now". The printed text has no "I write". "I will not spare" (φείσομαι, 1:23; 12:6) has no object. The sentence runs on to v4.
- **"a proof of Christ speaking in me, which to you-ward is not weak, but is mighty in you" → "a proof of the *Mashiach* speaking in me, who toward you is not weak but is powerful among you"** (v3) — Greek δοκιμήν (2:9; 8:2; 9:13), the root of "approved, unapproved, test" in v5–7; a plain genitive, not "proof that"; δυνατεῖ, the verb of "power" (v4); ἐν ὑμῖν, "among you" or "in you". The Greek has no "in dealing with".
- **"For though he was crucified through weakness, yet he liveth by the power of God" → "for indeed he was crucified out of weakness, but he lives out of the power of *Elohim*"** (v4) — the printed text has καὶ γάρ, without "though" (εἰ); ἐξ ἀσθενείας, ἐκ δυνάμεως, "out of, from". εἰς ὑμᾶς, "toward you", stands last in the Greek.
- **"Examine yourselves … prove your own selves. Know ye not your own selves … except ye be reprobates?" → "Put yourselves to the test … test yourselves. Or do you not fully know yourselves … unless indeed you are unapproved"** (v5) — Greek πειράζετε, the verb of "trial" and "tempt" (here "put to the test", as 1 Cor 10:9); δοκιμάζετε "test", a different verb; ἐπιγινώσκετε; ἀδόκιμοι, "not standing the test, unapproved" (v6, 7), the opposite of δόκιμοι "approved" (v7; 10:18). ἐν ὑμῖν is "in you" or "among you".
- **"I trust" → "I hope"** (v6) — Greek ἐλπίζω.
- **"Now I pray to God that ye do no evil; not that we should appear approved, but that ye should do that which is honest, though we be as reprobates" → "But we pray to *Elohim* that you do nothing evil — not that we may appear approved, but that you may do the good, and we be as unapproved"** (v7) — the printed text has εὐχόμεθα, "we pray" (εὔχομαι, also "wish"; so too v9); τὸ καλόν; ἡμεῖς δὲ ὡς ἀδόκιμοι ὦμεν, with no "though" and no "seem".
- **"but for the truth" → "but on behalf of the truth"** (v8) — Greek ὑπὲρ τῆς ἀληθείας; there is no "only".
- **"For we are glad, when we are weak, and ye are strong: and this also we wish, even your perfection" → "For we rejoice when we are weak and you are powerful; this also we pray for, your restoration"** (v9) — Greek χαίρομεν; δυνατοί (12:10); κατάρτισιν, from καταρτίζω "mend, fit together, restore" (v11), not the word "perfect" (τέλειος).
- **"lest being present I should use sharpness, according to the power which the Lord hath given me to edification, and not to destruction" → "that being present I may not deal sharply, according to the authority which the Lord gave me for building up and not for pulling down"** (v10) — Greek ἀποτόμως χρήσωμαι (Titus 1:13); ἐξουσία "authority"; οἰκοδομή, καθαίρεσις as 10:8.
- **"farewell. Be perfect, be of good comfort, be of one mind, live in peace" → "rejoice, be restored, be encouraged, think the same thing, be at peace"** (v11) — Greek χαίρετε, "rejoice" (also the word of greeting and farewell); καταρτίζεσθε; παρακαλεῖσθε, middle or passive ("be encouraged" or "encourage one another"); τὸ αὐτὸ φρονεῖτε (Phil 2:2); εἰρηνεύετε (1 Thess 5:13).
- **"be with you all. Amen." → "be with you all."** (v14) — the printed text has no "Amen". The sentence has no verb; "be" is supplied.
- **Verse numbers** (v12–14) — the printed text counts "Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the saints greet you" as v12 and the blessing as v13; the verse numbers here are those of the KJV.

### Notes
- **v1** quotes Deut 19:15.
