# 2 Corinthians 11

[1] Would that you bore with me in some little senselessness; but indeed you do bear with me.

[2] For I am jealous over you with a jealousy of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for I betrothed you to one husband, to present a pure virgin to ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧;

[3] but I fear lest somehow, as the serpent deceived ⟦eve|Chavvah|Eve⟧ in his craftiness, your thoughts should be corrupted from the simplicity and the purity that is unto ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[4] For if indeed the one who comes proclaims another ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ whom we did not proclaim, or you receive a different spirit which you did not receive, or a different gospel which you did not accept, you bear with it well.

[5] For I consider that I have come short in nothing of the superlative apostles;

[6] but even if I am unskilled in word, yet not in knowledge; but in everything having manifested it in all things unto you.

[7] Or did I commit a sin, humbling myself that you might be exalted, because I brought the gospel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you as good news for nothing?

[8] Other churches I robbed, taking wages for your service,

[9] and being present with you and being in lack, I was a burden to no one; for my lack the brothers who came from ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ filled up besides; and in everything I kept myself not burdensome to you, and will keep.

[10] The truth of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is in me, that this boasting will not be stopped as to me in the regions of Achaia.

[11] Why? Because I do not love you? ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ knows.

[12] But what I do, I also will do, that I may cut off the occasion of those who want an occasion, that in what they boast they may be found even as we also.

[13] For such ones are false apostles, guileful workers, refashioning themselves into apostles of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧;

[14] and no wonder, for Satan himself refashions himself into an angel of light;

[15] it is no great thing, then, if his servants also refashion themselves as servants of righteousness; whose end will be according to their works.

[16] Again I say, let no one think me to be senseless; but if otherwise, even as senseless accept me, that I also may boast some little.

[17] What I speak, I do not speak according to the Lord, but as in senselessness, in this assurance of boasting.

[18] Since many boast according to the flesh, I also will boast.

[19] For gladly you bear with the senseless, being sensible;

[20] for you bear with it if anyone enslaves you, if anyone devours, if anyone takes, if anyone lifts himself up, if anyone beats you in the face.

[21] By way of dishonor I speak, as though we have been weak.

But in whatever anyone dares — I speak in senselessness — I also dare.

[22] Are they ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧? I also. Are they ⟦israelite|Yisraelim|Israelites⟧? I also. Are they seed of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧? I also.

[23] Are they servants of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧? — I speak being out of my senses — I more: in labors more abundantly, in prisons more abundantly, in blows exceedingly, in deaths often;

[24] by ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ five times I received forty less one,

[25] three times I was beaten with rods, once I was stoned, three times I was shipwrecked, a night and a day I have spent in the deep;

[26] in journeys often, in dangers of rivers, in dangers of robbers, in dangers from the race, in dangers from nations, in dangers in the city, in dangers in the wilderness, in dangers in the sea, in dangers among false brothers,

[27] in labor and toil, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness;

[28] apart from the things besides, the pressure upon me daily, the anxiety for all the churches.

[29] Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is made to stumble, and I do not burn?

[30] If it is necessary to boast, I will boast of the things of my weakness.

[31] The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ knows, he who is blessed forever, that I do not lie.

[32] In ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ the ethnarch of Aretas the king was guarding the city of ⟦damascus|the Dammeseqites|the Damascenes⟧ to seize me,

[33] and through a window in a basket I was let down through the wall and escaped his hands.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 7, 11, 31) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v4, 31) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"folly" / "fool" → "senselessness" / "senseless"** (v1, 16, 17, 19, 21; 12:6, 11) — Greek ἀφροσύνη, ἄφρων: ἀ- "without" + φρήν "sense, mind"; the opposite, φρόνιμοι "sensible", stands beside it in v19, and παραφρονῶν "being out of my senses" in v23. "Fool" is kept for another word (μωρός).
- **"Would to God ye could bear with me a little in my folly: and indeed bear with me" → "Would that you bore with me in some little senselessness; but indeed you do bear with me"** (v1) — Greek ὄφελον, a wish with no "God"; μικρόν τι ἀφροσύνης, with no "my". The second ἀνέχεσθε can be a statement ("you do bear") or a command ("do bear"). ἀνέχομαι "bear with" also in v4, 19, 20.
- **"I am jealous over you with godly jealousy" → "I am jealous over you with a jealousy of *Elohim*"** (v2) — Greek ζηλῶ … θεοῦ ζήλῳ: a noun, "of God", not an adjective. ζῆλος is "zeal" elsewhere in the letter (7:7, 11; 9:2); here and in 12:20 "jealousy".
- **"I have espoused you … that I may present you as a chaste virgin" → "I betrothed you … to present a pure virgin"** (v2) — Greek ἡρμοσάμην, "joined, betrothed"; παρθένον ἁγνὴν παραστῆσαι, with no second "you" and no "as".
- **"beguiled Eve through his subtilty, so your minds should be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ" → "deceived *Chavvah* in his craftiness, your thoughts should be corrupted from the simplicity and the purity that is unto the *Mashiach*"** (v3) — Greek ἐξηπάτησεν; πανουργία (4:2; 12:16); νοήματα "thoughts" (10:5); φθαρῇ "be corrupted" (7:2); ἁπλότης "simplicity, singleness" (8:2; 9:11, 13); εἰς τὸν Χριστόν, "unto, toward". "And the purity" (καὶ τῆς ἁγνότητος) is in the printed text; the KJV's text lacks it. The Greek has no "devotion".
- **"he that cometh preacheth another Jesus … another spirit … another gospel … ye might well bear with him" → "the one who comes proclaims another *Yeshua* … a different spirit … a different gospel … you bear with it well"** (v4) — Greek ἄλλον, then ἕτερον twice; ἐλάβετε "received", ἐδέξασθε "accepted"; ἀνέχεσθε, present, "you bear with". The Greek names no object: "it" or "him". "Spirit" has no article.
- **"I suppose I was not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles" → "I consider that I have come short in nothing of the superlative apostles"** (v5) — Greek λογίζομαι; ὑστερηκέναι, the verb of "lack" (v9); τῶν ὑπερλίαν ἀποστόλων, an adverb "beyond exceedingly" used as an adjective (also 12:11).
- **"rude in speech … we have been throughly made manifest among you in all things" → "unskilled in word … in everything having manifested it in all things unto you"** (v6) — Greek ἰδιώτης τῷ λόγῳ, an untrained man, a layman; φανερώσαντες, an active participle in the plural with no object ("it" is supplied) and no main verb. ἐν πᾶσιν is "in all things" or "among all". The KJV follows the passive reading φανερωθέντες.
- **"abasing myself … I have preached to you the gospel of God freely" → "humbling myself … I brought the gospel of *Elohim* to you as good news for nothing"** (v7) — Greek ταπεινῶν; τὸ τοῦ θεοῦ εὐαγγέλιον εὐηγγελισάμην, the noun "gospel" with its own verb; δωρεάν, "as a gift, for nothing" (2 Thess 3:8).
- **"to do you service" → "for your service"** (v8) — Greek πρὸς τὴν ὑμῶν διακονίαν, "for the service of you", that is, the service done to them. ὀψώνιον is a soldier's pay, "wages". The sentence runs on into v9.
- **"when I was present with you, and wanted, I was chargeable to no man: for that which was lacking to me the brethren … supplied" → "being present with you and being in lack, I was a burden to no one; for my lack the brothers … filled up besides"** (v9) — Greek ὑστερηθείς, ὑστέρημα: one root (v5); κατενάρκησα, "weighed on as a dead weight" (12:13, 14); προσανεπλήρωσαν, "filled up in addition" (9:12).
- **"I have kept myself from being burdensome unto you, and so will I keep myself" → "I kept myself not burdensome to you, and will keep"** (v9) — Greek ἀβαρῆ … ἐτήρησα καὶ τηρήσω.
- **"As the truth of Christ is in me, no man shall stop me of this boasting" → "The truth of the *Mashiach* is in me, that this boasting will not be stopped as to me"** (v10) — Greek ἔστιν ἀλήθεια Χριστοῦ ἐν ἐμοὶ ὅτι: a statement, not an oath formula with "as". φραγήσεται, "be fenced in, blocked"; εἰς ἐμέ, "unto me".
- **"God knoweth" kept** (v11) — Greek ὁ θεὸς οἶδεν; no "I do".
- **"that I may cut off occasion from them which desire occasion; that wherein they glory, they may be found even as we" → "that I may cut off the occasion of those who want an occasion, that in what they boast they may be found even as we also"** (v12) — Greek ἀφορμή twice (5:12); there is no "mission", "claim" or "terms" in the Greek.
- **"deceitful workers, transforming themselves into" → "guileful workers, refashioning themselves into"** (v13, 14, 15) — Greek δόλιοι, from δόλος "guile" (12:16); μετασχηματίζομαι three times, as Phil 3:21. In v15 the Greek has ὡς, "as", not "into".
- **"no marvel" → "no wonder"; "angel of light" kept** (v14) — Greek θαῦμα; ἄγγελος is "messenger" or "angel", the same word in 12:7.
- **"Therefore it is no great thing" kept; "ministers" → "servants"** (v15) — Greek οὐ μέγα; διάκονοι, as v23.
- **"if otherwise, yet as a fool receive me, that I may boast myself a little" → "if otherwise, even as senseless accept me, that I also may boast some little"** (v16) — Greek εἰ δὲ μή γε; δέξασθε; μικρόν τι as v1.
- **"I speak it not after the Lord, but as it were foolishly, in this confidence of boasting" → "I do not speak according to the Lord, but as in senselessness, in this assurance of boasting"** (v17) — Greek κατὰ κύριον; ἐν ἀφροσύνῃ; ὑπόστασις as 9:4 ("substance, ground", so "assurance"). The Greek does not say "as the Lord would".
- **"seeing ye yourselves are wise" → "being sensible"** (v19) — Greek φρόνιμοι ὄντες.
- **"bring you into bondage … take of you … exalt himself … smite you on the face" → "enslaves you … takes … lifts himself up … beats you in the face"** (v20) — Greek καταδουλοῖ; λαμβάνει with no object (the verb of 12:16, "I took you with guile"); ἐπαίρεται as 10:5; εἰς πρόσωπον … δέρει.
- **"I speak as concerning reproach, as though we had been weak" → "By way of dishonor I speak, as though we have been weak"** (v21) — Greek κατὰ ἀτιμίαν λέγω, ὡς ὅτι ἡμεῖς ἠσθενήκαμεν (perfect). The Greek does not say whose dishonor, nor "too weak for that".
- **"whereinsoever any is bold, (I speak foolishly,) I am bold also" → "in whatever anyone dares — I speak in senselessness — I also dare"** (v21) — Greek τολμᾷ, τολμῶ (10:2, 12); there is no "boast" here.
- **"So am I" → "I also"; "the seed of Abraham" → "seed of *Avraham*"** (v22) — Greek κἀγώ; σπέρμα Ἀβραάμ.
- **"ministers of Christ? (I speak as a fool) I am more; in labours more abundant, in stripes above measure, in prisons more frequent, in deaths oft" → "servants of the *Mashiach*? — I speak being out of my senses — I more: in labors more abundantly, in prisons more abundantly, in blows exceedingly, in deaths often"** (v23) — Greek παραφρονῶν, stronger than ἄφρων; ὑπὲρ ἐγώ, "I beyond", with no verb and no "better"; περισσοτέρως twice, ὑπερβαλλόντως, πολλάκις; θανάτοις, plural, "deaths". The order (prisons before blows) is that of the printed text.
- **"Of the Jews five times received I forty stripes save one" → "by *Yehudim* five times I received forty less one"** (v24) — Greek ὑπὸ Ἰουδαίων, without the article; τεσσεράκοντα παρὰ μίαν, with no noun ("stripes, lashes"); see Deut 25:3.
- **"a night and a day I have been in the deep" → "a night and a day I have spent in the deep"** (v25) — Greek ἐν τῷ βυθῷ πεποίηκα; βυθός is the deep sea.
- **"in perils of waters … by mine own countrymen … by the heathen" → "in dangers of rivers … from the race … from nations"** (v26) — Greek κινδύνοις eight times; ποταμῶν "of rivers"; ἐκ γένους, "from race" (Phil 3:5), with no "my"; ἐξ ἐθνῶν. "In the city", "in the wilderness", "in the sea" have no article in Greek.
- **"In weariness and painfulness, in watchings often … in fastings often" → "in labor and toil, in watchings often … in fastings often"** (v27) — Greek κόπῳ καὶ μόχθῳ (1 Thess 2:9); ἀγρυπνίαις, "wakeful times" (6:5); νηστείαις, "fastings", not "without food".
- **"Beside those things that are without, that which cometh upon me daily, the care of all the churches" → "apart from the things besides, the pressure upon me daily, the anxiety for all the churches"** (v28) — Greek χωρὶς τῶν παρεκτός, "apart from the things outside" (outward things, or things left unmentioned); ἡ ἐπίστασίς μοι, "the pressing upon me" (also "attention, oversight"); μέριμνα "anxiety" (Phil 2:20; 4:6). The verse has no verb.
- **"who is offended, and I burn not?" → "Who is made to stumble, and I do not burn?"** (v29) — Greek σκανδαλίζεται; πυροῦμαι, "am set on fire".
- **"I will glory of the things which concern mine infirmities" → "I will boast of the things of my weakness"** (v30) — Greek τὰ τῆς ἀσθενείας μου, singular.
- **"The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which is blessed for evermore" → "The *Elohim* and Father of the Lord *Yeshua* … he who is blessed forever"** (v31) — the printed text has neither "our" nor "Christ". εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας, "unto the ages", is the idiom "forever".
- **"the governor under Aretas the king kept the city of the Damascenes with a garrison, desirous to apprehend me" → "the ethnarch of Aretas the king was guarding the city of the Damascenes to seize me"** (v32) — Greek ὁ ἐθνάρχης Ἁρέτα τοῦ βασιλέως, a plain genitive; ἐφρούρει; πόλιν Δαμασκηνῶν; πιάσαι, with no "desirous".
- **"by the wall" → "through the wall"** (v33) — Greek διὰ τοῦ τείχους.

### Notes
- **v21** — the paragraph break in the middle of the verse follows the sense; the Greek verse is one.
- **v32–33** — see Acts 9:23–25.
