# 2 Corinthians 2

[1] For I judged this for myself, not to come to you again in sorrow.

[2] For if I grieve you, who then is the one who gladdens me, except the one who is grieved by me?

[3] And I wrote this very thing, so that, having come, I should not have sorrow from those of whom I ought to rejoice, being confident in you all that my joy is that of you all.

[4] For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you through many tears, not that you should be grieved, but that you might know the love which I have more abundantly toward you.

[5] But if anyone has grieved, he has not grieved me, but in part, that I may not burden, all of you.

[6] Sufficient for such a one is this punishment by the greater number,

[7] so that on the contrary you should rather forgive and encourage him, lest somehow such a one be swallowed up by the more abundant sorrow.

[8] Therefore I appeal to you to ratify love toward him.

[9] For to this end also I wrote, that I might know the proof of you, whether you are obedient in all things.

[10] And to whom you forgive anything, I also; for indeed what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, is for your sake in the face of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[11] so that we may not be defrauded by Satan; for we are not ignorant of his thoughts.

[12] Now having come to Troas for the gospel of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and a door having been opened to me in the Lord,

[13] I had no relief for my spirit, because I did not find Titus my brother; but taking leave of them I went out into ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧.

[14] But thanks be to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who always leads us in triumph in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and manifests the fragrance of the knowledge of him through us in every place;

[15] for we are a sweet smell of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing,

[16] to the ones a fragrance from death unto death, to the others a fragrance from life unto life. And for these things who is sufficient?

[17] For we are not as the many, peddling the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but as from sincerity, but as from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ we speak.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v10, 12, 14, 15, 17) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v14, 15, 17) — systematic.
- **"I determined this with myself, that I would not come again to you in heaviness" → "I judged this for myself, not to come to you again in sorrow"** (v1) — Greek ἔκρινα … ἐμαυτῷ; ἐν λύπῃ, "in sorrow". The Greek has no "visit". λύπη is "sorrow" and λυπέω "grieve" throughout v1–7 (eight times); the KJV changes between "heaviness", "sorry", "sorrow" and "grief".
- **"who is he then that maketh me glad, but the same which is made sorry by me" → "who then is the one who gladdens me, except the one who is grieved by me"** (v2) — Greek present participles ὁ εὐφραίνων, ὁ λυπούμενος ἐξ ἐμοῦ ("from me").
- **"I wrote this same unto you" → "I wrote this very thing"** (v3) — Greek ἔγραψα τοῦτο αὐτό; "to you" is not in the printed text.
- **"having confidence in you all, that my joy is the joy of you all" → "being confident in you all that my joy is that of you all"** (v3) — Greek πεποιθὼς ἐπὶ πάντας ὑμᾶς; "is" is present tense (ἐστιν), and "joy" stands once.
- **"with many tears" → "through many tears"; "the love which I have more abundantly unto you" → "the love which I have more abundantly toward you"** (v4) — Greek διὰ πολλῶν δακρύων; περισσοτέρως, as 1:12. In the Greek "the love" stands first in its clause, with emphasis.
- **"if any have caused grief, he hath not grieved me, but in part: that I may not overcharge you all" → "if anyone has grieved, he has not grieved me, but in part, that I may not burden, all of you"** (v5) — Greek ἀλλὰ ἀπὸ μέρους ἵνα μὴ ἐπιβαρῶ πάντας ὑμᾶς. The words can be divided two ways: "but in part (that I may not burden) all of you", or "but in part, that I may not burden all of you". The Greek has no "to put it too severely".
- **"which was inflicted of many" → "by the greater number"** (v6) — Greek ὑπὸ τῶν πλειόνων, "by the more", the majority (the same phrase in 4:15). ἐπιτιμία, "punishment", occurs only here in the New Testament; it is of the root of ἐπιτιμάω, "rebuke".
- **"ye ought rather to forgive him, and comfort him" → "you should rather forgive and encourage him"** (v7) — Greek χαρίσασθαι καὶ παρακαλέσαι; χαρίζομαι is of the root of χάρις "grace" (also v10), παρακαλέω as 1:4. The Greek has no object; "him" is supplied.
- **"swallowed up with overmuch sorrow" → "swallowed up by the more abundant sorrow"** (v7) — Greek τῇ περισσοτέρᾳ λύπῃ, the word of v4.
- **"I beseech you that ye would confirm your love toward him" → "I appeal to you to ratify love toward him"** (v8) — Greek παρακαλῶ, here a request; κυρῶσαι, "ratify" formally (Gal 3:15), a different word from "confirm" (βεβαιόω, 1:21). The Greek has no "your".
- **"that I might know the proof of you" kept** (v9) — Greek τὴν δοκιμὴν ὑμῶν, as Phil 2:22; the Greek has no "test you and".
- **"for your sakes forgave I it in the person of Christ" → "is for your sake in the face of the *Mashiach*"** (v10) — Greek διʼ ὑμᾶς ἐν προσώπῳ Χριστοῦ, without a verb ("is" is supplied). πρόσωπον is "face" (as 4:6): before the face of the *Mashiach*, in his presence. The printed text reads "what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything"; the KJV follows another order ("if I forgave any thing, to whom I forgave it").
- **"Lest Satan should get an advantage of us" → "so that we may not be defrauded by Satan"** (v11) — Greek πλεονεκτηθῶμεν, passive; πλεονεκτέω is "take more than one's share from, defraud" (1 Thess 4:6; also 7:2; 12:17–18).
- **"devices" → "thoughts"** (v11) — Greek νοήματα, as 3:14; 4:4.
- **"when I came to Troas to preach Christ's gospel" → "having come to Troas for the gospel of the *Mashiach*"** (v12) — Greek εἰς τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τοῦ Χριστοῦ; the Greek has no "to preach" and no "even though".
- **"I had no rest in my spirit" → "I had no relief for my spirit"** (v13) — Greek οὐκ ἔσχηκα ἄνεσιν τῷ πνεύματί μου; ἄνεσις is "relief" (2 Thess 1:7). The Greek has no "there".
- **"which always causeth us to triumph in Christ" → "who always leads us in triumph in the *Mashiach*"** (v14) — Greek τῷ … θριαμβεύοντι ἡμᾶς. With an object the verb means to lead someone in a triumphal procession (Col 2:15, "triumphed over them"); the KJV's "causeth us to triumph" is not a known meaning of the word.
- **"maketh manifest the savour of his knowledge by us" → "manifests the fragrance of the knowledge of him through us"** (v14) — Greek φανεροῦντι; ὀσμή "fragrance" (v16); αὐτοῦ, "of him", can be *Elohim* or the *Mashiach*.
- **"a sweet savour of Christ" → "a sweet smell of the *Mashiach*"** (v15) — Greek εὐωδία, a different word from ὀσμή; together they are the Septuagint's phrase for the smell of an offering (Phil 4:18).
- **"in them that are saved, and in them that perish" → "among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing"** (v15) — Greek present participles.
- **"the savour of death unto death … the savour of life unto life" → "a fragrance from death unto death … a fragrance from life unto life"** (v16) — the printed text has ἐκ, "from", both times.
- **"which corrupt the word of God" → "peddling the word of *Elohim*"** (v17) — Greek καπηλεύοντες, from κάπηλος, a retail dealer: to trade in something for gain, and so also to adulterate it.
- **"but as of sincerity, but as of God, in the sight of God speak we in Christ" → "but as from sincerity, but as from *Elohim*, before *Elohim*, in the *Mashiach* we speak"** (v17) — Greek ἀλλʼ ὡς ἐξ εἰλικρινείας, ἀλλʼ ὡς ἐκ θεοῦ κατέναντι θεοῦ ἐν Χριστῷ λαλοῦμεν. The Greek has no "men" and no "commissioned".
- **"Satan" (v11)** — Σατανᾶς retained as proper name.

### Notes
- **v12–13** — the account breaks off here and is taken up again in 7:5.
