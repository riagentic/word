# Habakkuk 3

[1] A prayer of ⟦habakkuk|Chavaqquq|Habakkuk⟧ the prophet, upon ⟦shigyonot|Shigyonot|Shigionoth⟧.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I have heard the report of you; I have feared.
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your work — in the midst of years give it life;
in the midst of years make it known;
in agitation remember compassion.

[3] ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ comes from Teman,
and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah.
His majesty has covered the heavens,
and the earth is full of his praise.

[4] And the brightness is like the light;
horns from his hand are his,
and there is the hiding of his strength.

[5] Before him walks pestilence,
and burning plague goes out at his feet.

[6] He stood and measured the earth;
he looked and made the nations start up;
and the perpetual mountains were shattered,
the everlasting hills bowed down;
the everlasting processions are his.

[7] Under trouble I saw the tents of ⟦cushan|Kushan|Cushan⟧;
the curtains of the land of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ tremble.

[8] Was it against the rivers that it burned, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧?
Or against the rivers your anger,
or against the sea your raging,
that you ride upon your horses,
your chariots of salvation?

[9] Bare, laid bare is your bow;
the oaths of the staffs — a speech. Selah.
With rivers you split the earth.

[10] The mountains saw you; they writhe;
a downpour of waters passed over;
the deep gave its voice;
on high it lifted up its hands.

[11] Sun, moon stood in the lofty dwelling;
at the light of your arrows they go,
at the brightness of the lightning of your spear.

[12] In indignation you march through the earth;
in anger you thresh nations.

[13] You went out for the salvation of your people,
for salvation with your ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧;
you struck through the head from the house of the wicked,
laying bare the foundation up to the neck. Selah.

[14] You pierced with his own staffs the head of his warriors;
they storm to scatter me;
their jubilation is as if to devour the afflicted in the secret place.

[15] You trod the sea with your horses,
the heap of many waters.

[16] I heard, and my belly trembled;
at the sound my lips quivered;
rottenness comes into my bones,
and in my place I tremble,
that I must rest for the day of distress,
for the people that will raid us to come up.

[17] **Though the fig tree shall not bud,
and there is no produce on the vines;
the work of the olive has lied,
and the fields have made no food;
the flock is cut off from the fold,
and there is no herd in the stalls —**

**[18] yet I — in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will be jubilant;
I will be glad in the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation.**

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|GOD⟧ ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is my force,
and he has set my feet like the does,
and upon my high places he makes me tread.

To the overseer, with my stringed instruments.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v3) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ (not אֱלֹהִים): token `eloah` in place of the former `elohim`. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v18) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Yahweh Adonai"** (v19) — Hebrew יְהוִה אֲדֹנָי, both words, in the MT order.
- **Layout** (throughout) — a psalm: one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order. The heading line of v19 (לַמְנַצֵּחַ בִּנְגִינוֹתָי) stands apart, as the Psalm headings; v17–18 keep their bold.
- **"A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet upon Shigionoth" → "A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, upon Shigyonot"** (v1) — Hebrew תְּפִלָּה "A prayer" (the Psalm heading row); עַל שִׁגְיֹנוֹת "upon *shigyonot*" (the plural of the שִׁגָּיוֹן of Ps 7:1; a musical or literary term whose meaning is not known: token).
- **"O LORD, I have heard thy speech, and was afraid: O LORD, revive thy work in the midst of the years, in the midst of the years make known; in wrath remember mercy" → "Yahweh, I have heard the report of you; I have feared. Yahweh, your work — in the midst of years give it life; in the midst of years make it known; in agitation remember compassion"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי שִׁמְעֲךָ "I have heard the report of you"; יָרֵאתִי, a perfect, "I have feared"; no "O"; פָּעָלְךָ "your work" first; חַיֵּיהוּ "give it life"; בְּרֹגֶז "in agitation" (רֹגֶז, noun of רָגַז "be agitated", the word of v16; KJV "wrath"); רַחֵם "compassion" (to have compassion; KJV "mercy").
- **"God came from Teman, and the Holy One from mount Paran. Selah. His glory covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise" → "Eloah comes from Teman, and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah. His majesty has covered the heavens, and the earth is full of his praise"** (v3) — Hebrew יָבוֹא, an imperfect ("comes"); כִּסָּה, מָלְאָה perfects; הוֹד "majesty"; סֶלָה plain "Selah", as the Psalms.
- **"And his brightness was as the light; he had horns coming out of his hand: and there was the hiding of his power" → "And the brightness is like the light; horns from his hand are his, and there is the hiding of his strength"** (v4) — Hebrew וְנֹגַהּ "and the brightness" (no "his"); תִּהְיֶה, an imperfect; קַרְנַיִם "horns" (rays of light; the word kept); חֶבְיוֹן "hiding"; עֻזֹּה "his strength".
- **"Before him went the pestilence, and burning coals went forth at his feet" → "Before him walks pestilence, and burning plague goes out at his feet"** (v5) — Hebrew imperfects; דֶּבֶר "pestilence"; רֶשֶׁף "burning plague" (literally "flame"; uncertain).
- **"He stood, and measured the earth: he beheld, and drove asunder the nations; and the everlasting mountains were scattered, the perpetual hills did bow: his ways are everlasting" → "He stood and measured the earth; he looked and made the nations start up; and the perpetual mountains were shattered, the everlasting hills bowed down; the everlasting processions are his"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיְמֹדֶד "and measured" (the Septuagint, from memory, "shook", from מוּד; the MT is kept); וַיַּתֵּר, hifil of נָתַר "leap, loose": "made start up" (uncertain); הַרְרֵי־עַד "mountains of perpetuity", גִּבְעוֹת עוֹלָם "hills of everlasting" (two words, as KJV in the other order); וַיִּתְפֹּצְצוּ "were shattered"; שַׁחוּ "bowed down"; הֲלִיכוֹת "processions" (literally "goings", as Ps 68:24).
- **"I saw the tents of Cushan in affliction: and the curtains of the land of Midian did tremble" → "Under trouble I saw the tents of Cushan; the curtains of the land of Midian tremble"** (v7) — Hebrew תַּחַת אָוֶן "under trouble" (first in the line); יִרְגְּזוּן, an imperfect, רָגַז "tremble"; יְרִיעוֹת "curtains".
- **"Was the LORD displeased against the rivers? was thine anger against the rivers? was thy wrath against the sea, that thou didst ride upon thine horses and thy chariots of salvation?" → "Was it against the rivers that it burned, Yahweh? Or against the rivers your anger, or against the sea your raging, that you ride upon your horses, your chariots of salvation?"** (v8) — Hebrew הֲבִנְהָרִים חָרָה יְהוָה "was it against the rivers (that) it burned, Yahweh" (חָרָה with no subject: anger burned); אַף "anger"; עֶבְרָה "raging"; תִרְכַּב, an imperfect; מַרְכְּבֹתֶיךָ יְשׁוּעָה "your chariots — salvation".
- **"Thy bow was made quite naked, according to the oaths of the tribes, even thy word. Selah. Thou didst cleave the earth with rivers" → "Bare, laid bare is your bow; the oaths of the staffs — a speech. Selah. With rivers you split the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew עֶרְיָה תֵעוֹר "bareness, it is laid bare"; שְׁבֻעוֹת מַטּוֹת אֹמֶר, three nouns, "oaths, staffs (or "tribes"), a speech": one of the hardest lines of the Bible; many repoint שְׁבֻעוֹת as "sated" ("the arrows are sated"); the words are kept and the sense is left open. בָּקַע "split".
- **"The mountains saw thee, and they trembled: the overflowing of the water passed by: the deep uttered his voice, and lifted up his hands on high" → "The mountains saw you; they writhe; a downpour of waters passed over; the deep gave its voice; on high it lifted up its hands"** (v10) — Hebrew יָחִילוּ, חִיל "writhe" (the anguish of labor); זֶרֶם "downpour"; נָתַן … קוֹלוֹ "gave its voice"; רוֹם "on high", first.
- **"The sun and moon stood still in their habitation: at the light of thine arrows they went, and at the shining of thy glittering spear" → "Sun, moon stood in the lofty dwelling; at the light of your arrows they go, at the brightness of the lightning of your spear"** (v11) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶשׁ יָרֵחַ, no "and"; עָמַד singular; זְבֻלָה "to the lofty dwelling" (זְבוּל, 1 Kgs 8:13); יְהַלֵּכוּ "they go" (the arrows); לְנֹגַהּ בְּרַק "at the brightness of the lightning".
- **"Thou didst march through the land in indignation, thou didst thresh the heathen in anger" → "In indignation you march through the earth; in anger you thresh nations"** (v12) — Hebrew order; imperfects; זַעַם "indignation"; צָעַד "march"; דּוּשׁ "thresh".
- **"Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, even for salvation with thine anointed; thou woundedst the head out of the house of the wicked, by discovering the foundation unto the neck. Selah" → "You went out for the salvation of your people, for salvation with your anointed; you struck through the head from the house of the wicked, laying bare the foundation up to the neck. Selah"** (v13) — Hebrew לְיֵשַׁע אֶת־מְשִׁיחֶךָ: אֶת "with" (as KJV; or the sign of the object, "to save your anointed"); מָשִׁיחַ token, no capital; מָחַצְתָּ "struck through"; עָרוֹת "laying bare"; יְסוֹד "foundation"; צַוָּאר "neck".
- **"Thou didst strike through with his staves the head of his villages: they came out as a whirlwind to scatter me: their rejoicing was as to devour the poor secretly" → "You pierced with his own staffs the head of his warriors; they storm to scatter me; their jubilation is as if to devour the afflicted in the secret place"** (v14) — Hebrew נָקַבְתָּ "you pierced"; בְמַטָּיו "with his staffs" (the word of v9); the qere פְּרָזָיו (written פרזו), a word of the root of פְּרָזוֹן (Judg 5:7, "villagers"): here "his warriors" (KJV "villages"; uncertain); יִסְעֲרוּ "they storm"; עֲלִיצֻתָם "their jubilation" (עָלַץ "be jubilant"); עָנִי "afflicted"; מִסְתָּר "secret place".
- **"Thou didst walk through the sea with thine horses, through the heap of great waters" → "You trod the sea with your horses, the heap of many waters"** (v15) — Hebrew דָּרַכְתָּ "trod"; חֹמֶר "heap" (of foaming water; or "mortar, mud", as Nah 3:14); רַבִּים "many".
- **"When I heard, my belly trembled; my lips quivered at the voice: rottenness entered into my bones, and I trembled in myself, that I might rest in the day of trouble: when he cometh up unto the people, he will invade them with his troops" → "I heard, and my belly trembled; at the sound my lips quivered; rottenness comes into my bones, and in my place I tremble, that I must rest for the day of distress, for the people that will raid us to come up"** (v16) — Hebrew וַתִּרְגַּז, אֶרְגָּז, רָגַז "tremble" twice; בִּטְנִי "my belly"; צָלְלוּ "quivered" (of ears "tingle"); יָבוֹא, an imperfect; וְתַחְתַּי "and in my place" (literally "under me"); אֲשֶׁר אָנוּחַ "that I must rest" (wait in quiet; uncertain); לַעֲלוֹת לְעַם יְגוּדֶנּוּ "for the coming up of the people (that) will raid us" (גּוּד "raid"; "us" or "it": the suffix allows both).
- **"Although the fig tree shall not blossom, neither shall fruit be in the vines; the labour of the olive shall fail, and the fields shall yield no meat; the flock shall be cut off from the fold, and there shall be no herd in the stalls" → "Though the fig tree shall not bud, and there is no produce on the vines; the work of the olive has lied, and the fields have made no food; the flock is cut off from the fold, and there is no herd in the stalls"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי "though"; תִפְרָח, פָּרַח "bud"; יְבוּל "produce"; כִּחֵשׁ "has lied" (= failed; the word kept); מַעֲשֵׂה־זַיִת "the work of the olive"; שְׁדֵמוֹת "fields" (terraces: uncertain); עָשָׂה "made"; אֹכֶל "food"; גָּזַר "is cut off"; מִכְלָה "fold"; רְפָתִים "stalls".
- **"Yet I will rejoice in the LORD, I will joy in the God of my salvation" → "yet I — in Yahweh I will be jubilant; I will be glad in the Elohim of my salvation"** (v18) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי "and I" (emphatic); אֶעְלוֹזָה, אָגִילָה cohortatives; עָלַז "be jubilant", גִּיל "be glad" (two verbs); יִשְׁעִי "my salvation".
- **"The LORD God is my strength, and he will make my feet like hinds' feet, and he will make me to walk upon mine high places" → "Yahweh Adonai is my force, and he has set my feet like the does, and upon my high places he makes me tread"** (v19) — Hebrew חֵילִי "my force" (חַיִל "ability, force"; כֹּחַ is "power", עֹז "strength"); וַיָּשֶׂם "and he has set"; כָּאַיָּלוֹת "like the does" (as Ps 18:33); יַדְרִכֵנִי, הִדְרִיךְ "make tread". **"To the chief singer on my stringed instruments" → "To the overseer, with my stringed instruments"** — לַמְנַצֵּחַ "To the overseer" (the Psalm heading row); בִּנְגִינוֹתָי "with my stringed instruments" (the "my" kept).

### Notes

- **v1, 19** — a heading and a closing line as in the Psalms: the chapter is a psalm.
- **v19** — compare 2 Sam 22:34 / Ps 18:33.
