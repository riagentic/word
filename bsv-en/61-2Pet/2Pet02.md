# 2 Peter 2

[1] But there arose also false prophets among the people, as among you also there will be false teachers, who will secretly bring in heresies of destruction, even denying the Master who bought them, bringing upon themselves swift destruction.

[2] And many will follow their acts of licentiousness, because of whom the way of the truth will be blasphemed.

[3] And in greed, with fabricated words, they will make merchandise of you; for whom the sentence from of old is not idle, and their destruction does not slumber.

[4] For if ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not spare angels who sinned, but with chains of gloom cast them into ⟦tartarus|Tartaros|hell⟧ and delivered them up, being kept for judgment,

[5] and did not spare the ancient world, but guarded ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, the eighth, a herald of righteousness, bringing a flood upon the world of the ungodly,

[6] and, reducing the cities of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧ to ashes, condemned them with an overthrow, having set them as an example of those who are going to act ungodly,

[7] and rescued righteous Lot, oppressed by the conduct of the unprincipled in licentiousness —

[8] for by sight and hearing that righteous one, dwelling among them, day after day was tormenting a righteous soul with lawless deeds —

[9] the Lord knows how to rescue the godly out of trial, and to keep the unrighteous, being punished, for the day of judgment,

[10] and especially those who go after flesh in desire of defilement and despise lordship.

Daring, self-willed, they do not tremble, blaspheming glories,

[11] whereas angels, being greater in strength and power, do not bring against them before the Lord a blasphemous judgment.

[12] But these, like unreasoning animals, born as creatures of nature for capture and corruption, blaspheming in things of which they are ignorant, in their corruption will also be corrupted,

[13] suffering wrong as the wage of unrighteousness; counting indulgence in the daytime a pleasure; spots and blemishes, indulging in their deceits while they feast together with you;

[14] having eyes full of an adulteress and unceasing from sin; enticing unstable souls; having a heart trained in greed; children of a curse.

[15] Having left the straight way, they were led astray, having followed the way of ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ the son of Bosor, who loved the wage of unrighteousness,

[16] but had a reproof of his own lawbreaking: a voiceless beast of burden, uttering in a man's voice, hindered the madness of the prophet.

[17] These are waterless springs and mists driven by a squall, for whom the gloom of darkness has been kept.

[18] For, uttering swollen things of vanity, they entice in desires of flesh, by acts of licentiousness, those who are barely escaping from those who conduct themselves in error,

[19] promising them freedom, while they themselves are slaves of corruption; for by what anyone has been defeated, to that he has been enslaved.

[20] For if, having escaped the defilements of the world in the full knowledge of the Lord and Savior ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, but being again entangled in these, they are defeated, the last things have become worse for them than the first.

[21] For it would have been better for them not to have fully known the way of righteousness than, having fully known it, to turn back from the holy commandment delivered to them.

[22] There has happened to them the thing of the true proverb: "**A dog returned to its own vomit**," and, "A sow, having washed, to wallowing in mire."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v20) — systematic.
- **"damnable heresies" → "heresies of destruction"** (v1) — Greek αἱρέσεις ἀπωλείας *haireseis apōleias*; αἵρεσις is a chosen opinion or party ("sect, faction"); ἀπώλεια "destruction" comes twice in the verse and again in v3; 3:7, 16. παρεισάξουσιν is "bring in alongside", hence "secretly bring in".
- **"the Lord that bought them" → "the Master who bought them"** (v1) — Greek δεσπότην *despotēn*, the master of slaves, not κύριος; ἀγοράσαντα "bought" (at the market).
- **"pernicious ways" → "their acts of licentiousness"** (v2) — Greek ἀσελγείαις *aselgeiais*, plural; the same word in v7, 18 (and Jude 4).
- **"by reason of whom" → "because of whom"** (v2) — Greek διʼ οὕς.
- **"through covetousness shall they with feigned words make merchandise of you" → "in greed, with fabricated words, they will make merchandise of you"** (v3) — Greek πλαστοῖς "moulded, made up"; ἐμπορεύσονται "trade in" kept.
- **"whose judgment now of a long time lingereth not" → "for whom the sentence from of old is not idle"** (v3) — Greek κρίμα (the sentence passed; κρίσις "judgment" in v4, 9, 11); ἀργεῖ "is idle", the root of "idle" in 1:8.
- **"their damnation slumbereth not" → "their destruction does not slumber"** (v3) — Greek ἡ ἀπώλεια αὐτῶν οὐ νυστάζει, "does not nod off"; ἀπώλεια = "destruction," as in v1.
- **"cast them down to hell, and delivered them into chains of darkness" → "with chains of gloom cast them into Tartaros and delivered them up"** (v4) — Greek σειραῖς ζόφου ταρταρώσας παρέδωκεν: "with chains of gloom" stands before both verbs and can go with either. ταρταρώσας *tartarōsas* = "casting into Tartarus" (the Greek underworld prison of the Titans, used here for the holding place of the angels who sinned). The only NT use of this word; kept distinct from *Hades* (realm of the dead) and *Gehenna* (place of judgment), which KJV also flattens into "hell." ζόφος "gloom" as in v17 and Jude 6, 13. σειραῖς "chains" is the reading of SBLGNT NA28 RP; WH and Treg read σιροῖς, "pits".
- **"to be reserved unto judgment" → "being kept for judgment"** (v4) — Greek τηρουμένους, a present participle; τηρέω "keep" also in v9, 17; 3:7.
- **"saved Noah the eighth person" → "guarded *Noach*, the eighth"** (v5) — Greek ὄγδοον Νῶε … ἐφύλαξεν: "the eighth" means with seven others (Gen 7:13); the Greek does not name them. φυλάσσω "guard" (also 3:17) is a different verb from "keep".
- **"if" not repeated** (v5–7) — the Greek has "if" once (v4) and goes on with "and … and … and"; the conclusion comes in v9 without "then".
- **"condemned them with an overthrow" kept** (v6) — Greek καταστροφῇ κατέκρινεν.
- **"an ensample unto those that after should live ungodly" → "an example of those who are going to act ungodly"** (v6) — Greek ὑπόδειγμα μελλόντων ἀσεβεῖν (Treg NA28 RP). The other reading, ἀσεβέσιν (SBLGNT WH NA27): "an example of the things to come for the ungodly".
- **"Lot, vexed with the filthy conversation of the wicked" → "righteous Lot, oppressed by the conduct of the unprincipled in licentiousness"** (v7) — "conversation" in 1611 = "way of life / conduct" (ἀναστροφή); καταπονούμενον "worn down, oppressed"; ἄθεσμος "without ordinance, unprincipled" (also 3:17), a different word from "lawless" (ἄνομος, v8).
- **"in seeing and hearing, vexed his righteous soul … with their unlawful deeds" → "by sight and hearing … was tormenting a righteous soul with lawless deeds"** (v8) — Greek ψυχὴν δικαίαν ἀνόμοις ἔργοις ἐβασάνιζεν: no "his" and no "their"; the soul is his own, the deeds are theirs.
- **"out of temptations" → "out of trial"** (v9) — Greek ἐκ πειρασμοῦ, singular; "trial" or "temptation".
- **"unto the day of judgment to be punished" → "being punished, for the day of judgment"** (v9) — Greek κολαζομένους, a present passive participle: "while being punished" or "to be punished"; the verse leaves it open.
- **"walk after the flesh in the lust of uncleanness, and despise government" → "go after flesh in desire of defilement and despise lordship"** (v10) — Greek ὀπίσω σαρκὸς ἐν ἐπιθυμίᾳ μιασμοῦ πορευομένους; κυριότητος *kyriotētos* "lordship", as Jude 8.
- **"presumptuous are they, selfwilled, they are not afraid to speak evil of dignities" → "Daring, self-willed, they do not tremble, blaspheming glories"** (v10) — Greek τολμηταί, αὐθάδεις; δόξας *doxas* = "glories" (as Jude 8); the Greek does not say who the glories are.
- **"railing accusation against them before the Lord" → "against them before the Lord a blasphemous judgment"** (v11) — Greek κατʼ αὐτῶν παρὰ κυρίῳ βλάσφημον κρίσιν: "before the Lord" (παρὰ κυρίῳ) is read by WH Treg NA28 RP; NA27 reads παρὰ κυρίου, "from the Lord"; SBLGNT (and WH margin) does not have the words.
- **"natural brute beasts, made to be taken and destroyed" → "unreasoning animals, born as creatures of nature for capture and corruption"** (v12) — Greek ἄλογα ζῷα γεγεννημένα φυσικὰ εἰς ἅλωσιν καὶ φθοράν.
- **"shall utterly perish in their own corruption" → "in their corruption will also be corrupted"** (v12) — Greek ἐν τῇ φθορᾷ αὐτῶν καὶ φθαρήσονται: noun and verb of one root; the sense here is perishing, not moral corruption (the animals are born "for capture and corruption", that is, to be caught and killed). φθορά (decay, perishing, destruction; as 1:4; 2:19) is a different word from "destruction" (ἀπώλεια, v1, 3). "Their" can be the animals' or their own.
- **"shall receive the reward of unrighteousness" → "suffering wrong as the wage of unrighteousness"** (v13) — Greek ἀδικούμενοι μισθὸν ἀδικίας, a wordplay on one root ("being wronged" / "unrighteousness") that English cannot keep; KJV follows κομιούμενοι "receiving". μισθὸν ἀδικίας is the same phrase in v15.
- **"to riot in the day time" / "sporting themselves" → "indulgence in the daytime" / "indulging"** (v13) — Greek τρυφήν … ἐντρυφῶντες, one root: luxury, soft living.
- **"spots they are and blemishes … with their own deceivings" → "spots and blemishes … in their deceits"** (v13) — Greek σπίλοι καὶ μῶμοι; the opposite pair, "spotless and unblemished", in 3:14. ἀπάταις "deceits"; some manuscripts read ἀγάπαις "love feasts" (as Jude 12).
- **"eyes full of adultery, and that cannot cease from sin" → "eyes full of an adulteress and unceasing from sin"** (v14) — Greek μεστοὺς μοιχαλίδος, "full of an adulteress" (a woman, not the abstract noun); ἀκαταπαύστους ἁμαρτίας "not ceasing from sin".
- **"beguiling unstable souls" → "enticing unstable souls"** (v14, 18) — Greek δελεάζοντες, "to catch with bait"; ἀστηρίκτους "unstable", see 1:12.
- **"An heart they have exercised with covetous practices; cursed children" → "having a heart trained in greed; children of a curse"** (v14) — Greek καρδίαν γεγυμνασμένην πλεονεξίας (singular "heart"; πλεονεξία "greed" as v3); κατάρας τέκνα. v13–14 are one chain of participles in Greek, without "they are".
- **"the son of Bosor" kept** (v15) — Greek τοῦ Βοσόρ, "of Bosor" (Treg NA28 RP; WH reads Βεώρ, "Beor"); in Num 22:5 the father of Balaam is Beor. The verse keeps the form the Greek has. The Greek has no word "son".
- **"the right way" → "the straight way"** (v15) — Greek εὐθεῖαν ὁδόν; ἐπλανήθησαν "were led astray" (passive of πλανάω; "deceived" does not fit a road; πλάνη "error" in v18).
- **"the wages of unrighteousness" → "the wage of unrighteousness"** (v15) — Greek μισθὸν ἀδικίας, singular; as v13.
- **"was rebuked for his iniquity: the dumb ass speaking with man's voice forbad" → "had a reproof of his own lawbreaking: a voiceless beast of burden, uttering in a man's voice, hindered"** (v16) — Greek ἔλεγξιν … ἔσχεν ἰδίας παρανομίας (ἔλεγξις from ἐλέγχω "reprove"); ὑποζύγιον "one under the yoke", a beast of burden (the donkey of Num 22); ἄφωνον … φωνῇ "voiceless … voice"; παρανομίαν / παραφρονίαν ("lawbreaking" / "madness") is a wordplay.
- **"wells without water, clouds that are carried with a tempest" → "waterless springs and mists driven by a squall"** (v17) — NA28 πηγαὶ ἄνυδροι καὶ ὁμίχλαι; KJV follows νεφέλαι "clouds".
- **"the mist of darkness is reserved for ever" → "the gloom of darkness has been kept"** (v17) — Greek ὁ ζόφος τοῦ σκότους τετήρηται; "for ever" is not in the critical text (it stands in Jude 13). The Greek has no "utter".
- **"great swelling words of vanity" → "swollen things of vanity"** (v18) — Greek ὑπέρογκα … ματαιότητος; as Jude 16.
- **"through the lusts of the flesh, through much wantonness" → "in desires of flesh, by acts of licentiousness"** (v18) — Greek ἐν ἐπιθυμίαις σαρκὸς ἀσελγείαις.
- **"those that were clean escaped" → "those who are barely escaping"** (v18) — Greek τοὺς ὀλίγως ἀποφεύγοντας, "barely / only just escaping"; the participle is present (SBLGNT WH Treg NA27). The other reading, ὄντως (NA28 RP): "those who are truly escaping" (so KJV's text, "clean").
- **"them who live in error" → "those who conduct themselves in error"** (v18) — Greek ἀναστρεφομένους, the verb of "conduct" (v7).
- **"servants of corruption" → "slaves of corruption"** (v19) — Greek δοῦλοι; δεδούλωται "has been enslaved".
- **"of whom a man is overcome" → "by what anyone has been defeated"** (v19) — Greek ᾧ … τούτῳ can be "by what … to that" or "by whom … to him". ἥττηται, ἡττῶνται (v19, 20) "be defeated, be worsted"; a different verb from νικάω "overcome". Before "has been enslaved" Treg and RP (and SBLGNT) read καί, "also"; WH and NA28 do not, and the verse follows them.
- **"chains" kept** (v4) — Greek σειραῖς (σειρά "cord, chain"), a different word from δεσμοί "chains" in Jude 6.
- **"pollutions" → "defilements"** (v20) — Greek μιάσματα, the root of "defilement" (v10).
- **"through the knowledge of the Lord" → "in the full knowledge of the Lord"** (v20) — Greek ἐν ἐπιγνώσει τοῦ κυρίου; the critical text has no "our". ἐπίγνωσις "full knowledge" as 1:2; the verb ἐπιγινώσκω twice in v21, "fully known".
- **"made" → "born"** (v12) — Greek γεγεννημένα, of animals: "born".
- **"the latter end is worse with them than the beginning" → "the last things have become worse for them than the first"** (v20) — Greek γέγονεν αὐτοῖς τὰ ἔσχατα χείρονα τῶν πρώτων (as Matt 12:45).
- **"The dog is turned to his own vomit again" → "A dog returned to its own vomit"** (v22) — OT quotation (Prov 26:11). Both sayings have only a participle in Greek and no main verb: Κύων ἐπιστρέψας …, Ὗς λουσαμένη εἰς κυλισμὸν βορβόρου.

### Notes
- **v22** quotes Prov 26:11.
- **v4–10** — one long sentence in Greek: "if" (v4) … "the Lord knows" (v9).
