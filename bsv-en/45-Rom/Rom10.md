# Romans 10

[1] Brothers, the good pleasure of my heart and the supplication to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for them is unto salvation.

[2] For I testify of them that they have a zeal of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but not according to full knowledge;

[3] for, being ignorant of the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and seeking to establish their own, they were not subject to the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] For ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is the end of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ unto righteousness to everyone who believes.

[5] For ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ writes of the righteousness that is from the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, that "**the man who has done them will live in it**."

[6] But the righteousness from faith says thus: "**Do not say in your heart, 'Who will ascend into heaven?'**" (that is, to bring ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ down)

[7] "**or 'Who will descend into the abyss?'**" (that is, to bring ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ up from the dead).

[8] But what does it say? "**The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart**" (that is, the word of the faith which we proclaim);

[9] because, **if you confess in your mouth ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ as Lord, and believe in your heart that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised him from the dead, you will be saved**;

[10] for with the heart it is believed unto righteousness, and with the mouth it is confessed unto salvation.

[11] For the Scripture says, "**Everyone who believes on him will not be put to shame**."

[12] For there is no distinction of ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ and ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧; for the same is Lord of all, being rich unto all who call upon him;

[13] for "**everyone who calls upon the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ will be saved**."

[14] How then are they to call upon him in whom they did not believe? And how are they to believe him whom they did not hear? And how are they to hear apart from one proclaiming?

[15] And how are they to proclaim unless they are sent? Just as it is written, "**How beautiful are the feet of those who preach the good things!**"

[16] But not all obeyed the gospel. For ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ says, "**Lord, who believed our report?**"

[17] So faith is from hearing, and hearing through the word of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[18] But I say, did they not hear? Rather,

"**Into all the earth their sound went out,
and unto the ends of the inhabited earth their words**."

[19] But I say, did ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ not know? First ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ says,

"**I will provoke you to jealousy over what is not a nation;
over a nation without understanding I will provoke you to wrath**."

[20] But ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ is very daring and says,

"**I was found among those not seeking me;
I became manifest to those not inquiring of me**."

[21] But to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he says, "**The whole day I stretched out my hands to a people disobeying and contradicting**."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" / "Christ" → "*Yeshua*" / "*Mashiach*"** (v4, 6, 7, 9, 17) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"my heart's desire and prayer to God for Israel is, that they might be saved" → "the good pleasure of my heart and the supplication to *Elohim* for them is unto salvation"** (v1) — Greek ἡ μὲν εὐδοκία τῆς ἐμῆς καρδίας καὶ ἡ δέησις πρὸς τὸν θεὸν ὑπὲρ αὐτῶν εἰς σωτηρίαν, without a verb ("is" is supplied). εὐδοκία is "good pleasure", what the heart delights in and wants; δέησις "supplication". "For them" (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the Byzantine text has "for Israel".
- **"I bear them record" → "I testify of them"** (v2) — Greek μαρτυρῶ αὐτοῖς, as Col 4:13.
- **"a zeal of God" kept** (v2) — Greek ζῆλον θεοῦ, a plain genitive; the sense is "zeal for *Elohim*".
- **"knowledge" → "full knowledge"** (v2) — Greek ἐπίγνωσιν.
- **"going about to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves" → "seeking to establish their own, they were not subject"** (v3) — Greek τὴν ἰδίαν ζητοῦντες στῆσαι … οὐχ ὑπετάγησαν. "Their own" without the noun follows Westcott-Hort and Tregelles; NA28 and the Byzantine text add "righteousness".
- **"Christ is the end of the law for righteousness" → "the *Mashiach* is the end of *Torah* unto righteousness"** (v4) — Greek τέλος γὰρ νόμου Χριστὸς εἰς δικαιοσύνην; neither τέλος nor νόμου has the article. τέλος is "end" and also "goal, completion"; the one English word cannot hold both.
- **"Moses describeth the righteousness which is of the law, That the man which doeth those things shall live by them" → "Moses writes of the righteousness that is from the *Torah*, that 'the man who has done them will live in it'"** (v5) — the verse follows τὴν δικαιοσύνην τὴν ἐκ τοῦ νόμου ὅτι and ποιήσας + αὐτά (Tregelles, NA28, Byzantine); the printed SBLGNT with Westcott-Hort has ὅτι τὴν δικαιοσύνην τὴν ἐκ τοῦ νόμου ὁ ποιήσας: "writes that the man who has done the righteousness that is from the law will live in it". "In it" (αὐτῇ, the righteousness: Westcott-Hort, Tregelles); NA28 and the Byzantine text have αὐτοῖς, "in them", as Gal 3:12.
- **"which is of faith speaketh on this wise" → "from faith says thus"** (v6) — Greek ἡ δὲ ἐκ πίστεως δικαιοσύνη οὕτως λέγει.
- **"the deep" → "the abyss"** (v7) — Greek ἄβυσσον.
- **"The word is nigh thee" → "The word is near you"** (v8, 17, 18) — Greek ῥῆμα, the spoken word, not λόγος; so also "the word of the *Mashiach*" (v17) and "their words" (v18).
- **"the word of faith, which we preach" → "the word of the faith which we proclaim"** (v8) — Greek τὸ ῥῆμα τῆς πίστεως ὃ κηρύσσομεν.
- **"That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus" → "because, if you confess in your mouth *Yeshua* as Lord"** (v9) — Greek ὅτι ἐὰν ὁμολογήσῃς ἐν τῷ στόματί σου κύριον Ἰησοῦν. ὅτι is "because" or "that" (giving the content of the word of v8). κύριον Ἰησοῦν is "*Yeshua* as Lord" or "the Lord *Yeshua*". "In your mouth … in your heart" as in v8.
- **"with the heart man believeth unto righteousness" → "with the heart it is believed unto righteousness"** (v10) — Greek πιστεύεται, ὁμολογεῖται, passives without a subject.
- **"Whosoever believeth on him shall not be ashamed" → "Everyone who believes on him will not be put to shame"** (v11) — Greek πᾶς ὁ πιστεύων ἐπ' αὐτῷ οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται, as 9:33 with "everyone" added.
- **"no difference between the Jew and the Greek" → "no distinction of *Yehudi* and *Yevani*"** (v12) — Greek διαστολὴ Ἰουδαίου τε καὶ Ἕλληνος, as 3:22.
- **"the same Lord over all is rich unto all" → "the same is Lord of all, being rich unto all"** (v12) — Greek ὁ γὰρ αὐτὸς κύριος πάντων, πλουτῶν εἰς πάντας, without a verb; also "the same Lord of all".
- **"How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed?" → "How then are they to call upon him in whom they did not believe?"** (v14, 15) — Greek ἐπικαλέσωνται, πιστεύσωσιν, ἀκούσωσιν, κηρύξωσιν, subjunctives (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the Byzantine text has futures.
- **"of whom they have not heard" → "whom they did not hear"** (v14) — Greek οὗ οὐκ ἤκουσαν, a genitive with "hear": the one heard speaking, or "of whom".
- **"without a preacher" → "apart from one proclaiming"** (v14) — Greek χωρὶς κηρύσσοντος.
- **"the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things" → "the feet of those who preach the good things"** (v15) — Greek τῶν εὐαγγελιζομένων τὰ ἀγαθά (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28), "bringing the good things as good news"; the verb of "gospel" (εὐαγγέλιον, v16). "Of peace" is in the Byzantine text only. Paul's wording, not the Hebrew of Isa 52:7.
- **"who hath believed our report?" → "who believed our report?"** (v16, 17) — Greek τῇ ἀκοῇ ἡμῶν, "our hearing": what was heard from us. The same word is "hearing" in v17 and Gal 3:2, 5.
- **"faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God" → "faith is from hearing, and hearing through the word of the *Mashiach*"** (v17) — Greek ἡ πίστις ἐξ ἀκοῆς, ἡ δὲ ἀκοὴ διὰ ῥήματος Χριστοῦ, without a verb; Χριστοῦ (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28), θεοῦ in the Byzantine text. ῥήματος has no article.
- **"Yes verily" → "Rather"** (v18) — Greek μενοῦνγε, as 9:20.
- **"unto the ends of the world" → "unto the ends of the inhabited earth"** (v18) — Greek οἰκουμένης.
- **"by them that are no people, and by a foolish nation I will anger you" → "over what is not a nation; over a nation without understanding I will provoke you to wrath"** (v19) — Greek ἐπ' οὐκ ἔθνει, ἐπ' ἔθνει ἀσυνέτῳ παροργιῶ ὑμᾶς; παραζηλώσω and παροργιῶ are both "provoke to".
- **"Esaias is very bold" → "Isaiah is very daring"** (v20) — Greek ἀποτολμᾷ, a stronger form of τολμάω "dare".
- **"I was found of them that sought me not; I was made manifest unto them that asked not after me" → "I was found among those not seeking me; I became manifest to those not inquiring of me"** (v20) — Greek εὑρέθην ἐν τοῖς ἐμὲ μὴ ζητοῦσιν (ἐν in Tregelles and NA28; without it, "by those"), ἐμφανὴς ἐγενόμην τοῖς ἐμὲ μὴ ἐπερωτῶσιν.
- **"All day long I have stretched forth my hands unto a disobedient and gainsaying people" → "The whole day I stretched out my hands to a people disobeying and contradicting"** (v21) — Greek ἐξεπέτασα, an aorist; ἀπειθοῦντα καὶ ἀντιλέγοντα, participles. πρός stands twice: "to Israel" (also "with regard to Israel") and "to a people".
- **Multiple OT quotations** (v5, 6-8, 11, 13, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20-21) — in bold; translated from Paul's Greek, not from the Hebrew of the passages quoted. Sources in Notes.
