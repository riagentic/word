# Romans 16

[1] Now I commend to you ⟦phoebe|Phoibe|Phoebe⟧ our sister, who is also a servant of the church that is in ⟦cenchreae|Kenchreai|Cenchreae⟧,

[2] that you may receive her in the Lord worthily of the saints, and stand by her in whatever matter she may need you; for she herself also has become a patroness of many, and of me myself.

[3] Greet Prisca and Aquila, my fellow workers in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[4] who for my soul laid down their own neck, to whom not I alone give thanks but also all the churches of the nations,

[5] and the church at their house. Greet ⟦epaenetus|Epainetos|Epaenetus⟧ my beloved, who is the firstfruits of Asia unto ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[6] Greet ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, who labored much for you.

[7] Greet ⟦andronicus|Andronikos|Andronicus⟧ and Junia, my kinsmen and my fellow captives, who are notable among the apostles, who also have been in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ before me.

[8] Greet ⟦ampliatus|Ampliatos|Ampliatus⟧, my beloved in the Lord.

[9] Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and Stachys my beloved.

[10] Greet Apelles, the approved in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧. Greet those from among the people of ⟦aristobulus|Aristoboulos|Aristobulus⟧.

[11] Greet Herodion my kinsman. Greet those from among the people of ⟦narcissus|Narkissos|Narcissus⟧ who are in the Lord.

[12] Greet ⟦tryphaena|Tryphaina|Tryphaena⟧ and Tryphosa, who labor in the Lord. Greet Persis the beloved, who labored much in the Lord.

[13] Greet Rufus, the elect in the Lord, and his mother and mine.

[14] Greet ⟦asyncritus|Asynkritos|Asyncritus⟧, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers with them.

[15] Greet ⟦philologus|Philologos|Philologus⟧ and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints with them.

[16] Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ greet you.

[17] Now I exhort you, brothers, to look at those who make the dissensions and the stumbling blocks contrary to the teaching which you learned, and turn away from them;

[18] for such ones are not slaves to our Lord ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ but to their own belly, and through smooth speech and fair words they utterly beguile the hearts of the guileless.

[19] For your obedience has reached unto all; over you therefore I rejoice, but I want you to be wise unto the good, and innocent unto the evil.

[20] **And the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of peace will crush Satan under your feet shortly.** The grace of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ be with you.

[21] ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ my fellow worker greets you, and Lucius and ⟦jason|Iason|Jason⟧ and ⟦sosipater|Sosipatros|Sosipater⟧, my kinsmen.

[22] I, Tertius, the one who wrote the letter, greet you in the Lord.

[23] Gaius, my host and the host of the whole church, greets you. ⟦erastus|Erastos|Erastus⟧ the steward of the city greets you, and Quartus the brother.

[25] Now to him who is able to make you stable according to my gospel and the proclamation of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, according to the revelation of a mystery kept in silence through times eternal

[26] but now manifested, and through prophetic Scriptures, according to the command of the eternal ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, made known unto all the nations unto obedience of faith —

[27] to the only wise ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, to whom be the glory unto the ages. Amen.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v3, 18, 20, 25, 27) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v20, 26, 27) — systematic.
- **"servant" kept** (v1) — Greek διάκονον, the word of 13:4 and 15:8; "also" (καί: SBLGNT, WH, NA28) is absent in Treg and RP.
- **"assist her in whatsoever business she hath need of you: for she hath been a succourer" → "stand by her in whatever matter she may need you; for she herself also has become a patroness"** (v2) — Greek παραστῆτε, "stand beside"; προστάτις *prostatis*, the feminine of the word for a patron or protector, literally "one who stands before" (the root of προΐστημι, "lead"). "Receive" is προσδέξησθε, as Phil 2:29.
- **"Priscilla" → "Prisca"** (v3) — Πρίσκαν *Priskan*, the short form of her name (so also 1 Cor 16:19; 2 Tim 4:19); the diminutive "Priscilla" stands in Acts 18.
- **"for my life laid down their own necks" → "for my soul laid down their own neck"** (v4) — Greek ὑπὲρ τῆς ψυχῆς μου τὸν ἑαυτῶν τράχηλον ὑπέθηκαν, literally "put their own neck under"; "neck" is singular. "Give thanks" stands once in the Greek.
- **"Gentiles" → "nations"** (v4, 26) — Greek ἔθνη.
- **"church"** (v1, 4, 5, 16, 23) — ἐκκλησία *ekklesia* = assembly/congregation; KJV "church" retained; the word here means a local gathering of believers, not the later ecclesiastical institution. v4, 16 "churches" (Greek plural). v5 κατ' οἶκον, "at their house", as Phlm 2.
- **"the firstfruits of Achaia" → "the firstfruits of Asia"** (v5) — Greek Ἀσίας (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); KJV follows Ἀχαΐας (RP). "Unto the *Mashiach*" is εἰς Χριστόν.
- **"Mary … bestowed much labour on us" → "*Miryam* … labored much for you"** (v6) — the verse follows Μαρίαν (WH, Treg, NA28), the Greek form *Maria*; the printed SBLGNT and RP read Μαριάμ, the Hebrew form *Miryam*. "For you" is εἰς ὑμᾶς (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); RP ἡμᾶς, "us". "Labored" is κοπιάω (also v12).
- **"Junia" (v7)** — Ἰουνίαν *Iounian*. The accent is the editors' work: Ἰουνίαν (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, RP) is the woman's name Junia; Ἰουνιᾶν (NA28) would be a man's name, Junias. The feminine is kept. "Notable among the apostles" (ἐπίσημοι ἐν τοῖς ἀποστόλοις) can mean notable as apostles or notable in the eyes of the apostles; the wording leaves it open.
- **"fellowprisoners" → "fellow captives"** (v7) — Greek συναιχμαλώτους, captives of war, as Phlm 23. "Have been" is γέγοναν, perfect.
- **"them which are of Aristobulus' household" → "those from among the people of *Aristoboulos*"** (v10, 11) — Greek τοὺς ἐκ τῶν Ἀριστοβούλου, "those from among those of Aristobulus"; the Greek has no noun ("household", "people").
- **"chosen" → "the elect"** (v13) — Greek τὸν ἐκλεκτόν. "His mother and mine": τὴν μητέρα αὐτοῦ καὶ ἐμοῦ.
- **"All the churches"** (v16) — πᾶσαι (SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28); absent in RP and KJV.
- **"I beseech you … mark them which cause divisions and offences … avoid them" → "I exhort you … look at those who make the dissensions and the stumbling blocks … turn away from them"** (v17) — Greek παρακαλῶ (here urging; "appeal" in 15:30 of a request), σκοπεῖν (as Phil 3:17), διχοστασίας, σκάνδαλα (as 14:13), ἐκκλίνετε. "Teaching" is διδαχή; "which you learned", ἐμάθετε.
- **"serve not our Lord Jesus Christ" → "are not slaves to our Lord the *Mashiach*"** (v18) — Greek δουλεύουσιν; "Jesus" (RP) is not in SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28.
- **"by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the simple" → "through smooth speech and fair words they utterly beguile the hearts of the guileless"** (v18) — Greek χρηστολογία ("kind-sounding speech") and εὐλογία, elsewhere "blessing" (15:29), here of fine speech; ἐξαπατῶσι; ἀκάκων, as Heb 7:26.
- **"is come abroad unto all men" → "has reached unto all"** (v19) — Greek εἰς πάντας ἀφίκετο. "Innocent" is ἀκεραίους, literally "unmixed", as Phil 2:15.
- **"shall bruise Satan … shortly" → "will crush Satan … shortly"** (v20) — Greek συντρίψει … ἐν τάχει ("in quickness": soon, or swiftly). Σατανᾶς *Satanas* is retained as a proper name; the passage echoes Gen 3:15.
- **"our Lord Jesus Christ" → "our Lord *Yeshua*"** (v20) — the verse follows the text without Χριστοῦ (WH, NA28); the printed SBLGNT, Treg and RP have it. The Greek has no verb ("be").
- **"who wrote this epistle, salute you in the Lord"** (v22) — in the Greek "in the Lord" stands last, after "who wrote the letter"; it can go with "greet" or with "wrote".
- **"mine host … the chamberlain of the city" → "my host … the steward of the city"** (v23) — Greek ξένος (elsewhere "stranger"; here the one who lodges a guest), οἰκονόμος.
- **v24 absent** — "The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen." (v24 KJV, RP) is not in WH, Treg, NA28; omitted here.
- **"stablish … the preaching" → "make stable … the proclamation"** (v25) — Greek στηρίξαι, κήρυγμα; "of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" is a plain genitive.
- **"kept secret since the world began" → "kept in silence through times eternal"** (v25) — Greek χρόνοις αἰωνίοις σεσιγημένου; "revelation of a mystery" has no article.
- **"made manifest, and by the scriptures of the prophets" → "manifested, and through prophetic Scriptures"** (v26) — Greek φανερωθέντος, διά τε γραφῶν προφητικῶν. "Unto obedience of faith" (εἰς ὑπακοὴν πίστεως), as 1:5.
- **"be glory through Jesus Christ for ever" → "through *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, to whom be the glory unto the ages"** (v27) — the Greek has the relative ᾧ, "to whom", so the sentence begun in v25 is never finished. "Unto the ages" is εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας.

### Notes
- **v20** echoes Gen 3:15.
- **v24** is absent from WH, Treg, NA28.
- **v25–27** — the doxology stands here in SBLGNT, WH, Treg, NA28; RP places it after 14:23.
