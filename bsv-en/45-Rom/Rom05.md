# Romans 5

[1] Having therefore been justified from faith, we have peace with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[2] through whom also we have obtained the access by the faith into this grace in which we stand, and we boast upon hope of the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[3] and not only so, but we also boast in the afflictions, knowing that the affliction works out endurance,

[4] and the endurance proof, and the proof hope;

[5] and the hope does not put to shame, because the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has been poured out in our hearts through the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ that was given to us.

[6] For ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, while we were yet weak, yet in due time died for the ungodly.

[7] For scarcely will anyone die for a righteous one; for perhaps for the good one someone even dares to die;

[8] but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shows his own love unto us, in that, while we were yet sinners, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ died for us.

[9] Much more then, having now been justified in his blood, we shall be saved through him from the wrath.

[10] For if, being enemies, we were reconciled to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through the death of his Son, much more, having been reconciled, we shall be saved in his life;

[11] and not only so, but also boasting in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, through whom we have now received the reconciliation.

[12] Therefore, just as through one man sin entered into the world, and through sin death, and so death passed through unto all men, because all sinned —

[13] for until ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ sin was in the world, but sin is not put to account when there is no law,

[14] but death reigned from Adam until ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ even over those who did not sin in the likeness of the transgression of Adam, who is a pattern of the one to come.

[15] But not as the trespass, so also the gift of grace. For if by the trespass of the one the many died, much more did the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the gift in grace, that of the one man ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, abound unto the many.

[16] And the gift is not as through one who sinned; for the sentence was from one unto condemnation, but the gift of grace from many trespasses unto acquittal.

[17] For if by the trespass of the one death reigned through the one, much more will those who receive the abundance of the grace and of the gift of righteousness reign in life through the one, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[18] So then, as through one trespass unto all men unto condemnation, so also through one righteous deed unto all men unto justification of life;

[19] for just as through the disobedience of the one man the many were made sinners, so also through the obedience of the one the many will be made righteous.

[20] But ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ came in beside, that the trespass might increase; but **where sin increased, grace abounded beyond measure**,

[21] that, just as sin reigned in death, so also grace might reign through righteousness unto eternal life through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ our Lord.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 11, 15, 17, 21) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v5) — KJV's "Holy Ghost" = πνεῦμα ἅγιον *pneuma hagion* = רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ *Ruach HaQodesh*; here without the article in Greek.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v6, 8) — systematic.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v13, 20) — Greek νόμος, both times without the article: "until law", "law came in beside". The second νόμος in v13 ("when there is no law") is left as "law".
- **"being justified by faith" → "Having therefore been justified from faith"** (v1) — Greek δικαιωθέντες ἐκ πίστεως, a participle; ἐκ is "from", as in Gal 2:16; 3:24.
- **"we have peace with God" kept** (v1) — Greek εἰρήνην ἔχομεν πρὸς τὸν θεόν; πρός is "toward, with". ἔχομεν "we have" NA28 RP ] ἔχωμεν "let us have" WH Treg; SBLGNT prints ἔχομεν.
- **"By whom also we have access by faith" → "through whom also we have obtained the access by the faith"** (v2) — Greek τὴν προσαγωγὴν ἐσχήκαμεν τῇ πίστει, a perfect ("we have had and have"), with articles; προσαγωγή as Eph 2:18; 3:12. v1–2 are one sentence.
- **"rejoice in hope" → "boast upon hope"; "we glory in tribulations" → "we boast in the afflictions"; "we also joy in God" → "also boasting in *Elohim*"** (v2, 3, 11) — Greek καυχάομαι, "boast", three times: ἐπʼ ἐλπίδι "upon hope", ἐν ταῖς θλίψεσιν, ἐν τῷ θεῷ. In v11 it is a participle (καυχώμενοι) and the sentence has no finite verb. v3 καυχώμεθα WH NA28 RP ] καυχώμενοι Treg.
- **"tribulation worketh patience" → "the affliction works out endurance"** (v3) — Greek θλῖψις, ὑπομονή, κατεργάζεται (as Jas 1:3; 2 Cor 4:17).
- **"experience" → "proof"** (v4) — Greek δοκιμή, the quality of having been tested and approved (as Phil 2:22; 2 Cor 2:9). The verse has no verb in Greek.
- **"maketh not ashamed" → "does not put to shame"; "is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost which is given unto us" → "has been poured out in our hearts through the *Ruach HaQodesh* that was given to us"** (v5) — Greek οὐ καταισχύνει, without an object; ἐκκέχυται ἐν ταῖς καρδίαις, perfect, "in"; διὰ … τοῦ δοθέντος, "through", aorist. "The love of *Elohim*" is a plain genitive (his love or love for him).
- **"when we were yet without strength, in due time Christ died" → "the *Mashiach*, while we were yet weak, yet in due time died"** (v6) — Greek ἔτι γὰρ Χριστὸς ὄντων ἡμῶν ἀσθενῶν ἔτι κατὰ καιρόν: ἔτι "yet, still" stands twice. Ἔτι γάρ Treg NA28 RP ] Εἴ γε "if indeed" WH; the second ἔτι WH Treg NA28 ] omitted RP. κατὰ καιρόν is "according to the time". ἀσθενής is "weak" throughout.
- **"for a righteous man … for a good man some would even dare to die" → "for a righteous one … for the good one someone even dares to die"** (v7) — Greek ὑπὲρ δικαίου, ὑπὲρ τοῦ ἀγαθοῦ: no word "man"; τοῦ ἀγαθοῦ has the article and can also be neuter, "for the good". τολμᾷ is a present, "dares"; both clauses begin with γάρ, "for".
- **"commendeth his love toward us" → "shows his own love unto us"** (v8) — Greek συνίστησιν τὴν ἑαυτοῦ ἀγάπην εἰς ἡμᾶς; συνίστημι is "commend" (2 Cor 3:1) and "show, prove"; "shows" as in 3:5.
- **"being now justified by his blood … saved from wrath through him" → "having now been justified in his blood, we shall be saved through him from the wrath"** (v9) — Greek ἐν τῷ αἵματι, "in"; ἀπὸ τῆς ὀργῆς, "from the wrath": the Greek does not say whose.
- **"when we were enemies" → "being enemies"; "by the death … by his life" → "through the death … in his life"** (v10) — Greek ἐχθροὶ ὄντες; διὰ τοῦ θανάτου, ἐν τῇ ζωῇ. ἐχθροί is "enemies" or "hated ones".
- **"atonement" → "reconciliation"** (v11) — Greek καταλλαγή, the noun of "reconciled" in v10 (as 2 Cor 5:18–19).
- **"death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned" → "death passed through unto all men, because all sinned"** (v12) — Greek εἰς πάντας ἀνθρώπους … διῆλθεν, "went through"; ἥμαρτον, aorist. ἐφʼ ᾧ is taken as "because" (as 2 Cor 5:4); literally "upon which" or "upon whom". The sentence that begins with "just as" is not finished (v12 ends with a dash in the printed text).
- **"until the law sin was in the world: but sin is not imputed" → "until law sin was in the world, but sin is not put to account"** (v13) — Greek ἄχρι νόμου, no "given"; ἐλλογεῖται, "enter in an account" (as Phlm 18), a different word from "reckon" (λογίζομαι).
- **"Nevertheless death reigned from Adam to Moses … after the similitude of Adam's transgression, who is the figure of him that was to come" → "but death reigned from Adam until *Moshe* … in the likeness of the transgression of Adam, who is a pattern of the one to come"** (v14) — Greek ἐπὶ τῷ ὁμοιώματι, "upon the likeness" (ὁμοίωμα as 6:5; 8:3; Phil 2:7); ἐστιν, present; τύπος, "pattern" (as Phil 3:17), from which "type" comes; τοῦ μέλλοντος, "of the coming one".
- **"offence" → "trespass"** (v15, 16, 17, 18, 20) — Greek παράπτωμα, "a fall beside"; kept apart from "transgression" (παράβασις, v14) and "sin" (ἁμαρτία).
- **"not as the offence, so also is the free gift" → "not as the trespass, so also the gift of grace"** (v15) — the Greek has no verb. χάρισμα is a gift of grace (χάρις); "free" is not in the Greek. Three words are kept apart in v15–17: χάρισμα "gift of grace" (v15, 16), δωρεά "gift" (v15, 17), δώρημα "gift", the thing given (v16; as Jas 1:17).
- **"through the offence of one many be dead … the gift by grace, which is by one man" → "by the trespass of the one the many died … the gift in grace, that of the one man"** (v15) — Greek τοῦ ἑνός, οἱ πολλοί, τοὺς πολλούς, all with the article; ἀπέθανον, aorist; ἐν χάριτι τῇ τοῦ ἑνὸς ἀνθρώπου, "in grace, the grace of the one man".
- **"not as it was by one that sinned, so is the gift" → "the gift is not as through one who sinned"** (v16) — Greek οὐχ ὡς διʼ ἑνὸς ἁμαρτήσαντος τὸ δώρημα, without a verb; no "result" in the Greek.
- **"the judgment was by one to condemnation, but the free gift is of many offences unto justification" → "the sentence was from one unto condemnation, but the gift of grace from many trespasses unto acquittal"** (v16) — Greek κρίμα, "sentence"; ἐξ ἑνός, "from one" (one man or one trespass); κατάκριμα, the sentence against. δικαίωμα is the declaration that one is righteous, the opposite of κατάκριμα; it is not the word "justification" (δικαίωσις, v18). The same word δικαίωμα is "righteous deed" in v18 and "righteous ordinance" in 1:32; 2:26; 8:4. The Greek has no verbs here ("was" is supplied).
- **"by one man's offence death reigned by one … shall reign in life by one, Jesus Christ" → "by the trespass of the one death reigned through the one … will … reign in life through the one, *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v17) — Greek τοῦ ἑνός three times; the Greek has no "man" here. "The abundance of the grace and of the gift of righteousness": both genitives depend on "abundance".
- **"as by the offence of one judgment came upon all men to condemnation; even so by the righteousness of one the free gift came upon all men unto justification of life" → "as through one trespass unto all men unto condemnation, so also through one righteous deed unto all men unto justification of life"** (v18) — the Greek has no verb and no subject in either half: "judgment came" and "the free gift came" are KJV supplies. διʼ ἑνὸς παραπτώματος, διʼ ἑνὸς δικαιώματος can also be "through the trespass of one", "through the righteous deed of one". δικαίωσιν ζωῆς is a plain genitive, "justification of life".
- **"were made sinners … shall be made righteous" kept** (v19) — Greek κατεστάθησαν, κατασταθήσονται, from καθίστημι, "set down as, appoint" (Titus 1:5; "is made" in Jas 4:4). παρακοή "disobedience", ὑπακοή "obedience": one root.
- **"the law entered, that the offence might abound. But where sin abounded, grace did much more abound" → "*Torah* came in beside, that the trespass might increase; but where sin increased, grace abounded beyond measure"** (v20) — Greek παρεισῆλθεν, "came in beside" (the verb of Gal 2:4, "slipped in"); πλεονάζω "increase" twice, ὑπερεπερίσσευσεν "abounded beyond" (as 2 Cor 7:4).
- **"as sin hath reigned unto death" → "just as sin reigned in death"; "by Jesus Christ" → "through *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v21) — Greek ἐν τῷ θανάτῳ; διά twice, "through".
