# Romans 13

[1] Let every soul be subject to superior authorities. For there is no authority except by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and those that exist have been set in place by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] So then the one who opposes the authority has resisted the ordinance of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and those who have resisted will receive a sentence for themselves.

[3] For the rulers are not a fear to the good work but to the evil. And do you want not to fear the authority? Do the good, and you will have praise from it;

[4] for it is ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ servant to you for the good. But if you do the evil, fear; for it does not bear the sword in vain; for it is ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ servant, an avenger for wrath to the one who practices the evil.

[5] Therefore it is a necessity to be subject, not only because of the wrath but also because of the conscience,

[6] for because of this you also pay tributes, for they are ministers of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, persevering in this very thing.

[7] Render to all what is owed: the tribute to whom the tribute, the toll to whom the toll, the fear to whom the fear, the honor to whom the honor.

[8] Owe no one anything, except to love one another; for the one who loves the other has fulfilled ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[9] For this, "**You shall not commit adultery, You shall not murder, You shall not steal, You shall not desire**," and if there is any other commandment, is summed up in this word, in this: "**You shall love your neighbor as yourself**."

[10] Love works no evil to the neighbor; therefore **love is the fullness of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧**.

[11] And this, knowing the time, that it is already the hour for you to be roused out of sleep; for now salvation is nearer to us than when we believed.

[12] The night has advanced, and the day has drawn near. Let us therefore put off the works of the darkness, and let us put on the weapons of the light.

[13] As in the day, let us walk becomingly, not in revelries and drunkennesses, not in beds and acts of licentiousness, not in strife and jealousy,

[14] but put on the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and make no provision for the flesh unto desires.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" / "Lord Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v14) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 4, 6) — systematic.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v8, 10) — *nomos* here refers to the Mosaic law which Paul says love fulfills (cf. Gal 5:14). In both verses νόμος has no article, so the verse has none.
- **"soul" kept** (v1) — Greek πᾶσα ψυχή, "every soul"; not "person".
- **"the higher powers" → "superior authorities"** (v1) — ἐξουσίαις ὑπερεχούσαις, without an article: authorities that are above (the same verb as "supreme" in 1 Pet 2:13).
- **"of God" → "by *Elohim*"** (v1) — Greek ὑπὸ θεοῦ, twice: "by", not "from".
- **"ordained" → "set in place"; "ordinance" kept** (v1, 2) — τεταγμέναι (τάσσω "set, arrange") and διαταγή; "be subject" (ὑποτάσσομαι) and "oppose" (ἀντιτάσσομαι) are from the same root.
- **"resisteth … resisteth" → "opposes … has resisted"** (v2) — two Greek verbs, ἀντιτασσόμενος and ἀνθέστηκεν (perfect); "the authority" is singular.
- **"damnation" → "a sentence"** (v2) — Greek κρίμα.
- **"a terror" → "a fear"** (v3) — Greek φόβος, the same word as "fear" in v7; "to the good work", singular (τῷ ἀγαθῷ ἔργῳ).
- **"the power … he" → "the authority … it"** (v3, 4) — the subject of v4 is still ἡ ἐξουσία, "the authority" (v3 ἐξ αὐτῆς, "from it"); the Greek names no person. "Servant" is διάκονος.
- **"a revenger to execute wrath upon him" → "an avenger for wrath to the one"** (v4) — Greek ἔκδικος εἰς ὀργὴν τῷ … πράσσοντι; no verb and no "God's" in the Greek.
- **"for wrath" → "because of the wrath"** (v5) — Greek διὰ τὴν ὀργήν; "God's" is not in the Greek. The printed text has a comma after v5, and v6 goes on with "for".
- **"tribute" kept; "custom" → "toll"** (v6, 7) — φόρος *phoros*, the tribute paid by a subject people (plural in v6); τέλος *telos*, a toll or customs duty.
- **"God's ministers, attending continually" → "ministers of *Elohim*, persevering"** (v6) — Greek λειτουργοί and προσκαρτεροῦντες; the Greek does not name the subject ("they").
- **"their dues" → "what is owed"** (v7) — Greek τὰς ὀφειλάς, "the debts", the root of "owe" in v8. The four pairs have no verb: "to the one [owed] the tribute, the tribute".
- **"another" → "the other"** (v8) — Greek τὸν ἕτερον. The words τὸν ἕτερον νόμον could also be read together, "has fulfilled the other law"; the usual reading is kept.
- **"it is briefly comprehended in this saying, namely" → "is summed up in this word, in this"** (v9) — Greek ἐν τῷ λόγῳ τούτῳ ἀνακεφαλαιοῦται, ἐν τῷ; "and if there is any other commandment" (εἴ τις ἑτέρα ἐντολή).
- **"Thou shalt not kill" → "You shall not murder"** (v9) — OT quotation from Ex 20:13; the Hebrew/Greek (φονεύσεις *phoneuseis*) means murder specifically (not all killing); corrected from KJV's misleadingly broad "kill."
- **"Thou shalt not covet" → "You shall not desire"** (v9) — Greek οὐκ ἐπιθυμήσεις, the verb of "desires" (ἐπιθυμίας) in v14; "covet" is the traditional English.
- **"Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself" → "You shall love your neighbor as yourself"** (v9) — OT quotation from Lev 19:18; archaic forms modernized.
- **"worketh no ill" → "works no evil"; "the fulfilling" → "the fullness"** (v10) — Greek ἐργάζεται; πλήρωμα *plērōma*, the noun "fullness", not an action.
- **"nearer than when we believed"** (v11) — Greek ἐγγύτερον ἡμῶν ἡ σωτηρία: "salvation is nearer to us" or "our salvation is nearer"; the Greek allows both. "Be roused" is ἐγερθῆναι, the passive of the verb "raise". "For you" follows ὑμᾶς (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); Tregelles and RP read ἡμᾶς, "for us".
- **"far spent … at hand" → "has advanced … has drawn near"** (v12) — Greek προέκοψεν, ἤγγικεν.
- **"cast off" → "put off"** (v12) — the verse follows ἀποθώμεθα (WH, Treg, NA28, RP); the printed SBLGNT reads ἀποβαλώμεθα, "let us cast away".
- **"armour" → "weapons"** (v12) — Greek ὅπλα *hopla*, as in 2 Cor 6:7; 10:4.
- **"rioting … chambering and wantonness" → "revelries … beds and acts of licentiousness"** (v13) — all plural in Greek: κώμοις, μέθαις, κοίταις, ἀσελγείαις. κοίτη is "bed" (Heb 13:4), here of sexual intercourse. "Strife and jealousy" are singular; ζῆλος is also "zeal".
- **"to fulfil the lusts thereof" → "unto desires"** (v14) — Greek εἰς ἐπιθυμίας; no verb and no "its" in the Greek.
- **OT quotations** (v9 from Ex 20/Deut 5 + Lev 19:18) — noted.

### Notes
- **v9** quotes Ex 20:13-17; Deut 5:17-21 + Lev 19:18.
