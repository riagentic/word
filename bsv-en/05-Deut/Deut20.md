# Deuteronomy 20

[1] "When you go out to battle against your enemies and see horse and chariot, a people more numerous than you, you shall not fear them, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is with you, who brought you up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[2] And it shall be, when you come near to the battle, that the priest shall draw near and speak to the people,

[3] and he shall say to them, 'Hear, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: you are coming near today to the battle against your enemies. Let not your heart be soft; do not fear, and do not be alarmed, and do not be terrified because of them,

[4] for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is the one who goes with you, to fight for you with your enemies, to save you.'

[5] And the officers shall speak to the people, saying, 'Who is the man who has built a new house and has not dedicated it? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle and another man dedicate it.

[6] And who is the man who has planted a vineyard and has not put it to common use? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle and another man put it to common use.

[7] And who is the man who has betrothed a woman and has not taken her? Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle and another man take her.'

[8] And the officers shall speak yet more to the people and say, 'Who is the man who is fearful and soft of heart? Let him go and return to his house, and the heart of his brothers shall not melt as his heart.'

[9] And it shall be, when the officers have finished speaking to the people, that they shall appoint commanders of hosts at the head of the people.

[10] When you come near to a city to fight against it, you shall call to it for ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[11] And it shall be, if it answers you ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and opens to you, then it shall be that all the people who are found in it shall be yours for forced labor, and they shall serve you.

[12] And if it does not make peace with you and makes war with you, then you shall besiege it.

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall give it into your hand, and you shall strike down all its males with the edge of the sword.

[14] Only the women and the little ones and the beasts and all that is in the city, all its spoil, you shall plunder for yourself; and you shall eat the spoil of your enemies, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given to you.

[15] So you shall do to all the cities that are very far from you, which are not of the cities of these nations.

[16] Only of the cities of these peoples, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you as an inheritance, you shall not let any breath live;

[17] for you shall surely devote them to destruction: the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧, the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦perizzite|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧, the ⟦hivite|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has commanded you,

[18] so that they do not teach you to do according to all their abominations, which they have done for their gods, and you sin against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[19] When you besiege a city many days, fighting against it to seize it, you shall not ruin its trees by swinging an axe against them, for from them you may eat, and them you shall not cut down; for man is the tree of the field, to come into the siege from before you.

[20] Only a tree of which you know that it is not a tree for food, that you may ruin and cut down; and you shall build siege works against the city that makes war with you, until it comes down.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. "You" is one person (singular) in v1, 10–17, 19, 20 and many (plural) in v2–4, 18, as the Hebrew has it.
- **"battle" / "war"** (v1–7; v12, 20) — one Hebrew word, מִלְחָמָה: "battle" where the fight at hand is meant, "war" in "make war" (עָשָׂה מִלְחָמָה).
- **"horses, and chariots, and a people more than thou" → "horse and chariot, a people more numerous than you"** (v1) — Hebrew סוּס וָרֶכֶב עַם רַב מִמְּךָ: two collective singulars, and no "and" before "a people". **"be not afraid" → "you shall not fear"** — Hebrew לֹא תִירָא. **"brought thee up"** — Hebrew הַמַּעַלְךָ, "the one bringing you up".
- **"And it shall be"** (v2, 9, 11) — Hebrew וְהָיָה, kept. **"are come nigh … shall approach" → "come near … shall draw near"** (v2) — two verbs, קָרַב and נָגַשׁ.
- **"let not your hearts faint" → "Let not your heart be soft"** (v3), **"fainthearted" → "soft of heart"** (v8) — Hebrew אַל־יֵרַךְ לְבַבְכֶם and רַךְ הַלֵּבָב: the word is רַךְ "tender, soft" (Gen 18:7; 33:13). "Tender of heart" would say "kind" in English, so "soft" stands here. "Heart" is singular.
- **"do not tremble, neither be ye terrified because of them" → "do not be alarmed, and do not be terrified because of them"** (v3) — Hebrew חָפַז is to hurry away in alarm (the noun חִפָּזוֹן "haste", Exod 12:11); עָרַץ is "be terrified, dread". מִפְּנֵיהֶם is literally "from their face".
- **"against your enemies" → "with your enemies"** (v4) — Hebrew עִם, "with"; "against" in v1, 3 is עַל.
- **"What man is there that …?" → "Who is the man who …?"** (v5–8) — Hebrew מִי־הָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר, the same words each time; so are "Let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle and another man …" (יֵלֵךְ וְיָשֹׁב לְבֵיתוֹ פֶּן־יָמוּת בַּמִּלְחָמָה וְאִישׁ אַחֵר). **"officers"** — Hebrew שֹׁטְרִים, the lower officials who keep order and lists (Exod 5:6; Num 11:16).
- **"dedicated"** (v5) — Hebrew חָנַךְ, "begin the use of, dedicate" (the noun חֲנֻכָּה, Num 7:10).
- **"hath not yet eaten of it … another man eat of it" → "has not put it to common use … another man put it to common use"** (v6) — Hebrew חִלְּלוֹ, literally "has not profaned it": חִלֵּל is "profane, treat as common". The fruit of the first years is kept from use and that of the fourth year is holy (Lev 19:23–25); to "profane" the vineyard is to begin the ordinary use of its fruit (Jer 31:5). Plain "profane" would say a wrong done, which is not meant. Neither "yet" nor "eat" is in the Hebrew.
- **"betrothed a wife" → "betrothed a woman"** (v7) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה, "woman" and "wife"; "taken her" is the word for taking a wife.
- **"shall speak further" → "shall speak yet more"** (v8) — Hebrew וְיָסְפוּ … לְדַבֵּר, literally "shall add to speak". **"lest his brethren's heart faint as well as his heart" → "and the heart of his brothers shall not melt as his heart"** — Hebrew וְלֹא יִמַּס אֶת־לְבַב אֶחָיו כִּלְבָבוֹ: "and … not", not the "lest" of v5–7. The verb is pointed as a passive ("be melted") and yet has the object mark אֶת before "the heart"; some read it as "and let him not melt the heart of his brothers". The sense is the same.
- **"they shall make captains of the armies to lead the people" → "they shall appoint commanders of hosts at the head of the people"** (v9) — Hebrew וּפָקְדוּ שָׂרֵי צְבָאוֹת בְּרֹאשׁ הָעָם. The words can also be read with the commanders as the subject: "the commanders of hosts shall muster at the head of the people". שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander"; צְבָאוֹת "hosts".
- **"proclaim peace unto it" → "call to it for shalom"** (v10) — Hebrew וְקָרָאתָ אֵלֶיהָ לְשָׁלוֹם, literally so. **"if it make thee answer of peace" → "if it answers you shalom"** (v11) — Hebrew אִם־שָׁלוֹם תַּעַנְךָ. **"make peace"** (v12) — Hebrew תַשְׁלִים, the verb of שָׁלוֹם.
- **"shall be tributaries unto thee" → "shall be yours for forced labor"** (v11) — Hebrew יִהְיוּ לְךָ לָמַס; מַס is forced labor, not tribute (Gen 49:15; Exod 1:11).
- **"And when the LORD thy God hath delivered it into thine hands" → "And Yahweh your Elohim shall give it into your hand"** (v13) — Hebrew וּנְתָנָהּ … בְּיָדֶךָ, a plain statement; "hand" is singular. **"smite every male thereof" → "strike down all its males"** — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike"; זְכוּרָהּ "its males", a collective. "The edge of the sword" is literally "the mouth of the sword" (לְפִי־חָרֶב).
- **"But" → "Only"** (v14, 16, 20) — Hebrew רַק. **"the cattle" → "the beasts"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה. **"shalt thou take unto thyself" → "you shall plunder for yourself"** — Hebrew תָּבֹז לָךְ, the verb בָּזַז "plunder". "Eat the spoil" is literal (אָכַל).
- **"people" → "peoples"** (v16) — Hebrew הָעַמִּים, beside הַגּוֹיִם "nations" in v15. **"thou shalt save alive nothing that breatheth" → "you shall not let any breath live"** — Hebrew לֹא תְחַיֶּה כָּל־נְשָׁמָה: נְשָׁמָה is "breath" (Gen 2:7; 7:22).
- **"But thou shalt utterly destroy them" → "for you shall surely devote them to destruction"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי־הַחֲרֵם תַּחֲרִימֵם, literally "devoting you shall devote them": הֶחֱרִים is to give over to Yahweh beyond recall, by destroying. כִּי is "for". The six names are singular with the article and stand in three pairs, as in the Hebrew.
- **"That they teach you not" → "so that they do not teach you"** (v18) — Hebrew לְמַעַן אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יְלַמְּדוּ. **"so should ye sin" → "and you sin"** — Hebrew וַחֲטָאתֶם. "For their gods" is לֵאלֹהֵיהֶם.
- **"in making war against it to take it" → "fighting against it to seize it"** (v19) — Hebrew לְהִלָּחֵם עָלֶיהָ לְתָפְשָׂהּ; תָּפַשׂ is "lay hold of, seize" (21:19; 22:28). **"destroy" → "ruin"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew הִשְׁחִית, the "ruin" of Gen 6:13. **"its trees … them"** — the Hebrew has the singular "its tree … it" for the whole class. **"by forcing an axe against them" → "by swinging an axe against them"** — Hebrew לִנְדֹּחַ עָלָיו גַּרְזֶן, "to drive, swing an axe upon it".
- **"(for the tree of the field is man's life) to employ them in the siege" → "for man is the tree of the field, to come into the siege from before you"** (v19) — Hebrew כִּי הָאָדָם עֵץ הַשָּׂדֶה לָבֹא מִפָּנֶיךָ בַּמָּצוֹר, word for word; the sentence is hard and its meaning is not certain. As the Masoretic Text is pointed, הָאָדָם is "the man" with the article, and the clause is a statement. It is taken (1) as it stands: "man is the tree of the field" — man lives by it (so the KJV, "is man's life"); or (2) as a question, "is the tree of the field a man, that it should come into the siege before you?" — so the old versions (the Septuagint, cited from memory), which needs the other pointing הֶאָדָם; some keep the pointing and take כִּי itself as "is it that …?". The verse keeps the printed text. "To come into the siege" (בּוֹא בַמָּצוֹר) is to come under siege, as in 2 Kgs 24:10; מִפָּנֶיךָ is literally "from your face".
- **"bulwarks" → "siege works"** (v20) — Hebrew מָצוֹר, the word for "siege" in v19. **"until it be subdued" → "until it comes down"** — Hebrew עַד רִדְתָּהּ, literally "until its going down": until it falls.

### Notes
- **Three exemptions from military draft** (v5–8) — (1) new-house owner who hasn't dedicated it; (2) vineyard-planter who hasn't begun to use it; (3) betrothed man who hasn't married. Plus: (4) the fearful, dismissed so that the heart of the others does not melt. Unique in ancient war-law.
- **Distant cities vs Canaanite cities** (v10–18) — two categories:
  - *Far cities*: call for peace; if accepted, forced labor; if rejected, strike down the males; women, little ones, beasts and goods are spoil.
  - *Cities of the peoples of the land* (six named here; seven in 7:1): *cherem*, devoted to destruction.
- **The limits of warfare** — this is one of the ancient world's earliest attempts at circumscribing war. Even distant cities must be offered peace first; the devoting to destruction is limited to the peoples named.
- **Tree preservation** (v19–20) — trees that give food are spared during a siege; other trees may be cut for siege works. Jewish tradition (*bal tashchit*, "do not destroy") derives its rule against wasteful destruction from this verse.
- **The priest before battle** (v2–4) — the priest speaks to the people before the fight: Yahweh himself goes with them.
