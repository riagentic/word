# Deuteronomy 3

[1] "And we turned and went up by the way of the Bashan; and Og king of the Bashan came out to meet us, he and all his people, to battle at Edrei.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Do not fear him, for into your hand I have given him and all his people and his land; and you shall do to him as you did to ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, who dwelt in ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧.'

[3] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave into our hand also Og king of the Bashan and all his people, and we struck him down until no survivor was left to him.

[4] And we captured all his cities at that time; there was not a town that we did not take from them: sixty cities, all the region of ⟦argob|Argov|Argob⟧, the kingdom of Og in the Bashan.

[5] All these were fortified cities: a high wall, doors and a bar; apart from the cities of the open country, very many.

[6] And we devoted them to destruction, as we did to ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, devoting to destruction every city of men, the women and the little ones.

[7] And all the beasts and the spoil of the cities we plundered for ourselves.

[8] And we took at that time the land from the hand of the two kings of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ who were across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, from the wadi Arnon as far as Mount ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧

[9] — ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧ call ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧ ⟦sirion|Siryon|Sirion⟧, and the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, they call it Senir —

[10] all the cities of the tableland and all ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and all the Bashan, as far as ⟦salecah|Salchah|Salecah⟧ and Edrei, cities of the kingdom of Og in the Bashan.

[11] For only Og king of the Bashan was left of the rest of the ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧. Behold, his bed is a bed of iron; is it not in Rabbah of the sons of Ammon? Nine cubits is its length and four cubits its breadth, by the cubit of a man.

[12] And this land we took possession of at that time. From Aroer, which is on the wadi Arnon, and half the hill country of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and its cities, I gave to the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenite⟧ and to the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadite⟧;

[13] and the rest of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and all the Bashan, the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧: all the region of the ⟦argob|Argov|Argob⟧, with all the Bashan; that is called a land of ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧.

[14] ⟦jair|Yair|Jair⟧ the son of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ took all the region of ⟦argob|Argov|Argob⟧ as far as the border of the ⟦geshurites|Geshuri|Geshurite⟧ and the ⟦maacathites|Maachati|Maacathite⟧, and he called them by his name, the Bashan, ⟦havvoth-jair|Chavvot Yair|Havvoth-jair⟧, to this day.

[15] And to Machir I gave ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[16] And to the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenite⟧ and to the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadite⟧ I gave from ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and as far as the wadi Arnon, the middle of the wadi and the border, and as far as the ⟦jabbok|Yabboq|Jabbok⟧, the wadi, the border of the sons of Ammon;

[17] and the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, and the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and the border, from ⟦chinnereth|Kinneret|Chinnereth⟧ and as far as the sea of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, the Salt Sea, under the slopes of the Pisgah eastward.

[18] And I commanded you at that time, saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given you this land to possess it. Armed you shall pass over before your brothers the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, all the sons of ability.

[19] Only your wives and your little ones and your livestock — I know that you have much livestock — shall dwell in your cities that I have given you,

[20] until ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gives rest to your brothers as to you, and they also take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to them across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧; and you shall return, each man to his possession that I have given you.'

[21] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ I commanded at that time, saying, 'Your eyes are the ones that saw all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did to these two kings; so will ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ do to all the kingdoms to which you are passing over.

[22] You shall not fear them, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is the one who fights for you.'

[23] And I pleaded with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at that time, saying,

[24] '⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, you yourself have begun to show your servant your greatness and your strong hand; for what ⟦el|el|god⟧ is there in the heavens and on the earth who does according to your works and according to your mighty deeds?

[25] Let me pass over, please, and see the good land that is across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, this good hill country, and ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧.'

[26] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was enraged with me on your account and did not listen to me. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Enough for you! Do not speak to me any more of this matter.

[27] Go up to the top of the Pisgah and lift up your eyes westward and northward and southward and eastward, and see with your eyes; for you shall not pass over this ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[28] And command ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, and make him strong and strengthen him; for he shall pass over before this people, and he shall make them inherit the land that you will see.'

[29] And we dwelt in the valley opposite ⟦beth-peor|Beit Peor|Beth-peor⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Then we turned, and went up the way to Bashan: and Og the king of Bashan came out against us" → "And we turned and went up by the way of the Bashan; and Og king of the Bashan came out to meet us"** (v1) — the words of Num 21:33 in the same English, with "we" for "they". הַבָּשָׁן has the article (so through the chapter); לִקְרָאתֵנוּ "to meet us".
- **"Fear him not: for I will deliver him, and all his people, and his land, into thy hand … which dwelt at Heshbon" → "Do not fear him, for into your hand I have given him and all his people and his land … who dwelt in Heshbon"** (v2) — the words of Num 21:34. Hebrew נָתַתִּי, a perfect, "I have given"; הָאֱמֹרִי "the Amorite", a singular with the article for the people (so v8, 9).
- **"delivered into our hands Og also … and we smote him until none was left to him remaining" → "gave into our hand also Og … and we struck him down until no survivor was left to him"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן "gave"; בְּיָדֵנוּ "into our hand"; שָׂרִיד "survivor" (Num 21:35).
- **"we took all his cities … there was not a city which we took not from them, threescore cities" → "we captured all his cities … there was not a town that we did not take from them: sixty cities"** (v4) — Hebrew לָכַד "capture" and לָקַח "take"; קִרְיָה "town" beside עִיר "city". **"all the region of Argob"** — Hebrew חֶבֶל, literally "cord, measuring line": a measured stretch of land.
- **"All these cities were fenced with high walls, gates, and bars; beside unwalled towns a great many" → "All these were fortified cities: a high wall, doors and a bar; apart from the cities of the open country, very many"** (v5) — the Hebrew sets the words side by side with no "with"; חוֹמָה גְבֹהָה is singular; דְּלָתַיִם, a pair of doors; בְּרִיחַ "bar", singular. עָרֵי הַפְּרָזִי, literally "the cities of the open-country dweller": towns without a wall.
- **"we utterly destroyed them … utterly destroying the men, women, and children, of every city" → "we devoted them to destruction … devoting to destruction every city of men, the women and the little ones"** (v6) — Hebrew הֶחֱרִים (*cherem*, 2:34); עִיר מְתִם "city of men", the men of each city; טַף "little ones".
- **"all the cattle … we took for a prey to ourselves" → "all the beasts … we plundered for ourselves"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה; בָּזַז.
- **"out of the hand of the two kings of the Amorites the land that was on this side Jordan, from the river of Arnon" → "the land from the hand of the two kings of the Amorite who were across the Jordan, from the wadi Arnon"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן "across the Jordan": here the east side, though Moses speaks on that side; in v20, 25 the same words are the west side. אֲשֶׁר may go with the kings or with the land. נַחַל "wadi".
- **"(Which Hermon the Sidonians call Sirion; and the Amorites call it Shenir;)" → "— Sidonians call Hermon Sirion, and the Amorite, they call it Senir —"** (v9) — no brackets in the Hebrew; "Sidonians" has no article; שְׂנִיר *Senir*.
- **"All the cities of the plain" → "all the cities of the tableland"** (v10) — Hebrew הַמִּישֹׁר, "the level land": the high plain east of the Dead Sea; another word than the עֲרָבָה of v17.
- **"remained of the remnant of giants; behold, his bedstead was a bedstead of iron … after the cubit of a man" → "was left of the rest of the Rephaim. Behold, his bed is a bed of iron … by the cubit of a man"** (v11) — Hebrew הָרְפָאִים, the name of a people (2:11); יֶתֶר "rest", the word of v13. עֶרֶשׂ is a couch or bed; that a coffin of stone is meant is a guess. The Hebrew has no verb: the bed is spoken of as still to be seen. בְּאַמַּת־אִישׁ, "by the cubit of a man": the ordinary cubit, a man's forearm.
- **"this land, which we possessed at that time … and half mount Gilead, and the cities thereof, gave I unto the Reubenites and to the Gadites" → "this land we took possession of at that time. From Aroer … and half the hill country of Gilead and its cities, I gave to the Reubenite and to the Gadite"** (v12, 16) — Hebrew יָרַשְׁנוּ, a main clause; הַר־הַגִּלְעָד (הַר of a region, 1:2); the tribes are named in the singular with the article.
- **"being the kingdom of Og, gave I unto the half tribe of Manasseh; all the region of Argob, with all Bashan, which was called the land of giants" → "the kingdom of Og, I gave to the half of the tribe of Manasseh: all the region of the Argob, with all the Bashan; that is called a land of Rephaim"** (v13) — Hebrew לְכָל־הַבָּשָׁן, literally "to / as to all the Bashan". The Hebrew accents make a break after "the Bashan", so that הַהוּא "that" begins the last clause; without the break the words read "all that Bashan is called a land of Rephaim". יִקָּרֵא "is called", an imperfect.
- **"all the country of Argob unto the coasts of Geshuri and Maachathi; and called them after his own name, Bashan-havoth-jair" → "all the region of Argob as far as the border of the Geshurite and the Maacathite, and he called them by his name, the Bashan, Havvoth-jair"** (v14) — Hebrew חֶבֶל as v4; גְּבוּל "border". "Them" has no plural noun before it in this verse (Num 32:41 has "their tent villages"); "the Bashan" stands beside it as the Hebrew has it. חַוֺּת יָאִיר *Chavvot Yair* is "the tent villages of Jair".
- **"half the valley, and the border even unto the river Jabbok" → "the middle of the wadi and the border, and as far as the Jabbok, the wadi"** (v16) — Hebrew תּוֹךְ הַנַּחַל וּגְבֻל, literally "the midst of the wadi, and border": the middle of the stream bed being the border (so "and the Jordan and the border", v17; Num 34:6).
- **"The plain also, and Jordan, and the coast thereof, from Chinnereth even unto the sea of the plain, even the salt sea, under Ashdoth-pisgah eastward" → "and the Arabah, and the Jordan and the border, from Chinnereth and as far as the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, under the slopes of the Pisgah eastward"** (v17) — Hebrew הָעֲרָבָה, a name (1:1); אַשְׁדֹּת הַפִּסְגָּה: אַשְׁדֹּת is "slopes" (Num 21:15; the sense is not certain), and "the Pisgah" has the article.
- **"ye shall pass over armed before your brethren the children of Israel, all that are meet for the war" → "Armed you shall pass over before your brothers the sons of Israel, all the sons of ability"** (v18) — Hebrew חֲלוּצִים "armed" (Num 32:21), first in the clause; כָּל־בְּנֵי־חָיִל, literally "all sons of ability": חַיִל is ability, strength, a force of men; here the men fit to fight. "War" is not in the Hebrew. The "you" of v18–20 are the Reubenite, the Gadite and the half of Manasseh.
- **"(for I know that ye have much cattle,) shall abide in your cities" → "— I know that you have much livestock — shall dwell in your cities"** (v19) — Hebrew מִקְנֶה "livestock"; יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"Until the LORD have given rest unto your brethren, as well as unto you, and until they also possess the land which the LORD your God hath given them beyond Jordan" → "until Yahweh gives rest to your brothers as to you, and they also take possession of the land that Yahweh your Elohim is giving to them across the Jordan"** (v20) — Hebrew נֹתֵן, a participle, "is giving"; בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן, here the west side (v8). יְרֻשָּׁה "possession" (2:5).
- **"Thine eyes have seen" → "Your eyes are the ones that saw"** (v21) — Hebrew עֵינֶיךָ הָרֹאֹת, literally "your eyes are the seeing ones" (so 4:3; 11:7). "Your eyes", "you are passing over" are singular, to Joshua; "your Elohim" in this verse and all of v22 are plural.
- **"for the LORD your God he shall fight for you" → "for Yahweh your Elohim, he is the one who fights for you"** (v22) — Hebrew הוּא הַנִּלְחָם, a participle with the article.
- **"I besought the LORD" → "I pleaded with Yahweh"** (v23) — Hebrew וָאֶתְחַנַּן, from חָנַן "show favor": to seek favor for oneself (Gen 42:21).
- **"O Lord God, thou hast begun to shew thy servant thy greatness, and thy mighty hand: for what God is there in heaven or in earth, that can do according to thy works, and according to thy might?" → "Lord Yahweh, you yourself have begun to show your servant your greatness and your strong hand; for what *el* is there in the heavens and on the earth who does according to your works and according to your mighty deeds?"** (v24) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai YHWH*, both words; אַתָּה "you" is written out; חֲזָקָה "strong"; אֵל *el*, "a god"; יַעֲשֶׂה "does" ("can" is not a word of the Hebrew); גְּבוּרֹת, a plural, "mighty deeds". עֶבֶד is "slave"; of one who serves Yahweh, "servant".
- **"I pray thee, let me go over, and see the good land that is beyond Jordan, that goodly mountain, and Lebanon" → "Let me pass over, please, and see the good land that is across the Jordan, this good hill country, and Lebanon"** (v25) — Hebrew נָא "please"; הָהָר הַטּוֹב הַזֶּה, "this good mountain", the hill country west of the Jordan; "Lebanon" has the article.
- **"the LORD was wroth with me for your sakes, and would not hear me … Let it suffice thee; speak no more unto me of this matter" → "Yahweh was enraged with me on your account and did not listen to me … Enough for you! Do not speak to me any more of this matter"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיִּתְעַבֵּר, the verb of עֶבְרָה "overflowing rage"; it sounds like עָבַר "pass over", the word of Moses' request (v25) and of the refusal (v27). לְמַעַנְכֶם "on your account" (1:37 has "because of you"). רַב־לָךְ "much for you" (1:6); אַל־תּוֹסֶף דַּבֵּר … עוֹד, literally "do not add to speak … again".
- **"Get thee up into the top of Pisgah, and lift up thine eyes westward … and behold it with thine eyes" → "Go up to the top of the Pisgah and lift up your eyes westward … and see with your eyes"** (v27) — "it" is not in the Hebrew. The four directions are literally "toward the sea, toward the north, toward Teman, toward the sunrise".
- **"But charge Joshua, and encourage him, and strengthen him … and he shall cause them to inherit" → "And command Joshua, and make him strong and strengthen him … and he shall make them inherit"** (v28) — Hebrew צִוָּה "command" (Num 27:19); חִזֵּק "make strong" and אִמֵּץ "make firm", two verbs for strength; הִנְחִיל (1:38).
- **"So we abode in the valley over against Beth-peor" → "And we dwelt in the valley opposite Beth-peor"** (v29) — Hebrew יָשַׁב; גַּיְא, a narrow valley; מוּל "opposite".

### Notes
- **Og's iron bed** (v11) — cited as a thing that can be seen in Rabbah of the sons of Ammon. Nine cubits by four is about 4 × 1.8 m (13 × 6 feet).
- **Transjordan allotment** — Reuben and Gad receive the south (from the Arnon to the Jabbok and half the hill country of Gilead); the half of the tribe of Manasseh receives the rest of Gilead and the Bashan (Num 32).
- **Moses' plea** (v23–25) — he asks once, is refused, and is told to speak of it no more.
- **View from the Pisgah** (v27) — Moses will see but not pass over (ch 34). The four directions are those of Gen 13:14.
- **"Yahweh … fights for you"** (v22) — compare 1:30; 20:4; Exod 14:14.
- **"On your account"** (v26) — as 1:37 and 4:21; Num 20:12 gives the unbelief of Moses and Aaron as the reason.
