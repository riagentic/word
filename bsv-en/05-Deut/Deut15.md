# Deuteronomy 15

[1] "At the end of seven years you shall make a release.

[2] And this is the matter of the release: every owner of a loan of his hand shall let drop what he lends to his neighbor; he shall not press his neighbor and his brother, for a release to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has been proclaimed.

[3] The foreigner you may press, but what is yours with your brother your hand shall let drop.

[4] Only that there shall be no needy one among you, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will surely bless you in the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you as an inheritance to possess it,

[5] only if you will surely listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to keep to do all this commandment that I am commanding you today.

[6] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has blessed you, as he spoke to you; and you shall lend on pledge to many nations, but you shall not borrow on pledge; and you shall rule over many nations, but over you they shall not rule.

[7] When there is among you a needy one, one of your brothers, in one of your gates, in your land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, you shall not strengthen your heart, and you shall not shut your hand from your needy brother.

[8] For you shall surely open your hand to him, and you shall surely lend to him on pledge enough for his lack, which he lacks.

[9] Take heed to yourself, lest there be a word with your heart, ⟦belial|Beliyyaal|worthlessness⟧, saying, 'The seventh year, the year of the release, has come near,' and your eye be evil against your needy brother, and you do not give to him, and he call against you to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and there be sin in you.

[10] You shall surely give to him, and your heart shall not be evil when you give to him, for because of this thing ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will bless you in all your work and in all that you stretch out your hand to.

[11] For the needy one will not cease from the midst of the land; therefore I am commanding you, saying, 'You shall surely open your hand to your brother, to your afflicted one and to your needy one, in your land.'

[12] When your brother, the ⟦hebrew|Ivri|Hebrew⟧ man or the ⟦hebrew|Ivri|Hebrew⟧ woman, is sold to you, he shall serve you six years, and in the seventh year you shall let him go free from you.

[13] And when you let him go free from you, you shall not let him go empty.

[14] You shall surely furnish him from your flock and from your threshing floor and from your winepress; with what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has blessed you, you shall give to him.

[15] And you shall remember that you were a slave in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ ransomed you; therefore I am commanding you this word today.

[16] And it shall be, when he says to you, 'I will not go out from you,' because he loves you and your house, because it is good for him with you,

[17] then you shall take the awl and put it in his ear and in the door, and he shall be your slave forever. And also to your slave woman you shall do so.

[18] It shall not be hard in your eyes when you let him go free from you, for double the wages of a hired man he has served you six years; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will bless you in all that you do.

[19] Every firstborn that is born in your herd and in your flock, the male, you shall sanctify to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; you shall not make the firstborn of your ox serve, and you shall not shear the firstborn of your flock.

[20] Before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you shall eat it, year by year, in the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will choose, you and your house.

[21] And when there is a blemish in it, lame or blind, any evil blemish, you shall not sacrifice it to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[22] In your gates you shall eat it, the unclean and the clean together, as the gazelle and as the deer.

[23] Only its blood you shall not eat; on the earth you shall pour it out like water.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"every seven years" → "seven years"; "release" kept** (v1, 2, 9) — Hebrew מִקֵּץ שֶׁבַע־שָׁנִים "at the end of seven years"; "every" is not written. שְׁמִטָּה *shemittah* is one word, "a letting drop, a release", from the verb שָׁמַט "let drop" (v2, 3; Exod 23:11 of the land in the seventh year). "Of debts" is not in the Hebrew. Whether the debt is dropped for good or only not pressed during that year is debated; the verse says only "let drop". **"make"** — Hebrew עָשָׂה.
- **"the manner of the release" → "the matter of the release"** (v2) — Hebrew דְּבַר הַשְּׁמִטָּה, literally "the word of the release" (so 19:4).
- **"Every creditor that lendeth ought unto his neighbour shall release it" → "every owner of a loan of his hand shall let drop what he lends to his neighbor"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁמוֹט כָּל־בַּעַל מַשֵּׁה יָדוֹ אֲשֶׁר יַשֶּׁה בְּרֵעֵהוּ. שָׁמוֹט is an infinitive used as a command. The grouping of the words is not certain: (1) "every owner of a loan of his hand" (a creditor) lets drop "what he lends"; (2) "every owner of a loan shall let his hand drop [from] what he lends" (as v3 "your hand shall let drop"). בַּעַל is "owner"; מַשֶּׁה "loan" is found only here.
- **"exact" → "press"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew נָגַשׂ "drive, press" (the verb of the "taskmasters", Exod 3:7). **"of his neighbour, or of his brother" → "his neighbor and his brother"** — the Hebrew has וְ "and". **"because it is called the LORD's release" → "for a release to Yahweh has been proclaimed"** — Hebrew כִּי־קָרָא שְׁמִטָּה לַיהוָה, literally "for one has proclaimed a release to Yahweh" (קָרָא "proclaim", as Lev 25:10).
- **"Of a foreigner thou mayest exact it again" → "The foreigner you may press"** (v3) — Hebrew הַנָּכְרִי; the verb form is "you shall / may press". **"thine hand shall release" → "your hand shall let drop"** — Hebrew תַּשְׁמֵט יָדֶךָ; the form may also be read "you shall make your hand let drop".
- **"Save when there shall be no poor among you" → "Only that there shall be no needy one among you"** (v4) — Hebrew אֶפֶס כִּי, a restricting particle (Num 13:28). It may be taken as a limit on what goes before ("save that there shall be no needy one": then the law would not be needed) or as a command ("only, let there be no needy one among you"). The verse keeps the particle and decides nothing. **"poor" → "needy"** (v4, 7, 9, 11) — Hebrew אֶבְיוֹן; in v11 it stands beside עָנִי "afflicted" (the poor man bowed down).
- **"shall greatly bless thee" → "will surely bless you"** (v4) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb, בָרֵךְ יְבָרֶכְךָ, literally "blessing he will bless you". The same form stands in v5 (שָׁמוֹעַ תִּשְׁמַע), v8 (פָתֹחַ תִּפְתַּח, הַעֲבֵט תַּעֲבִיטֶנּוּ), v10 (נָתוֹן תִּתֵּן), v11 and v14 (הַעֲנֵיק תַּעֲנִיק); each is "surely" with the verb.
- **"carefully hearken … to observe to do" → "surely listen to the voice … to keep to do"** (v5) — Hebrew לִשְׁמֹר לַעֲשׂוֹת, two infinitives side by side ("be careful to do"); שָׁמַר stays "keep". **"all these commandments" → "all this commandment"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַמִּצְוָה הַזֹּאת, singular: the whole charge as one.
- **"blesseth thee, as he promised thee" → "has blessed you, as he spoke to you"** (v6) — Hebrew בֵּרַכְךָ is a perfect; דִּבֶּר is "spoke". **"lend" / "borrow" → "lend on pledge" / "borrow on pledge"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew הֶעֱבִיט and עָבַט, from עֲבוֹט "pledge" (24:10–13): another verb than the "lend" of v2 (נָשָׁה) and the usual הִלְוָה.
- **"a poor man of one of thy brethren within any of thy gates" → "a needy one, one of your brothers, in one of your gates"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵאַחַד אַחֶיךָ בְּאַחַד שְׁעָרֶיךָ, literally "from one of your brothers, in one of your gates". "Gates" stands for the towns.
- **"harden thine heart" → "strengthen your heart"** (v7) — Hebrew תְאַמֵּץ, piel of אָמֵץ "be strong" (as 2:30): the heart made firm against him. "Harden" is kept for another verb (הִקְשָׁה). **"shut thine hand from" kept** — Hebrew קָפַץ "draw shut".
- **"sufficient for his need, in that which he wanteth" → "enough for his lack, which he lacks"** (v8) — Hebrew דֵּי מַחְסֹרוֹ אֲשֶׁר יֶחְסַר לוֹ: noun and verb of one root.
- **"Beware that there be not a thought in thy wicked heart" → "Take heed to yourself, lest there be a word with your heart, *Beliyyaal*"** (v9) — Hebrew דָבָר עִם־לְבָבְךָ בְלִיַּעַל, literally "a word with your heart, worthlessness". בְּלִיַּעַל is a common noun, "worthlessness" ("without worth", as usually explained), not a name; it is the word of "sons of *Beliyyaal*" in 13:13. It describes the word, not the heart. **"is at hand" → "has come near"** — Hebrew קָרְבָה. **"thou givest him nought" → "you do not give to him"** — no object in the Hebrew. **"cry" → "call"** — Hebrew קָרָא.
- **"thine heart shall not be grieved" → "your heart shall not be evil"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא־יֵרַע לְבָבְךָ, the root of "your eye be evil" in v9. **"for this thing" → "because of this thing"** — Hebrew בִּגְלַל הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה. **"in all that thou puttest thine hand unto" → "in all that you stretch out your hand to"** — Hebrew מִשְׁלַח יָדֶךָ, literally "the stretching out of your hand", the noun of שָׁלַח יָד "stretch out the hand" (12:7, 18).
- **"the poor shall never cease out of the land" → "the needy one will not cease from the midst of the land"** (v11) — Hebrew לֹא־יֶחְדַּל … מִקֶּרֶב הָאָרֶץ; "never" is not written. **"to thy poor, and to thy needy" → "to your afflicted one and to your needy one"** — Hebrew לַעֲנִיֶּךָ וּלְאֶבְיֹנְךָ.
- **"an Hebrew man, or an Hebrew woman" → "the Hebrew man or the Hebrew woman"** (v12) — Hebrew הָעִבְרִי אוֹ הָעִבְרִיָּה, the masculine and the feminine form of one word, each with the article; "man" and "woman" stand for the two forms. **"be sold unto thee"** — Hebrew יִמָּכֵר, a nifal: "is sold" or "sells himself" (as Lev 25:39). Exod 21:2 has "when you buy a Hebrew slave"; here he is "your brother", and the word "slave" is not used of him until v17. **"let him go free"** (v12, 13, 18) — Hebrew שִׁלַּח חָפְשִׁי "send away free" (Exod 21:26); Exod 21:2 has "he shall go out free".
- **"furnish him liberally" → "surely furnish him"** (v14) — Hebrew הַעֲנֵיק תַּעֲנִיק, a verb found in this sense only here; it is from עֲנָק "necklace": literally perhaps "make a necklace for him", that is, load him richly. The exact sense is not certain. **"of that wherewith the LORD thy God hath blessed thee" → "with what Yahweh your Elohim has blessed you"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר בֵּרַכְךָ; it may also be read "as he has blessed you".
- **"bondman" → "slave"** (v15, 17) — Hebrew עֶבֶד. **"redeemed" → "ransomed"** (v15) — Hebrew פָּדָה "ransom"; גָּאַל is "redeem". **"this thing" → "this word"** — Hebrew הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה, here of what is commanded; in v10 the same words are "this thing" (the giving).
- **"go away" → "go out"; "thine house" kept; "because he is well with thee" → "because it is good for him with you"** (v16) — Hebrew יָצָא (as Exod 21:2, 5); בֵּיתֶךָ; כִּי־טוֹב לוֹ עִמָּךְ.
- **"thrust it through his ear unto the door" → "put it in his ear and in the door"** (v17) — Hebrew וְנָתַתָּה בְאָזְנוֹ וּבַדֶּלֶת, literally "give [it] in his ear and in the door". Exod 21:6 has "his lord shall pierce his ear with the awl"; there he is brought "to the Elohim", which is not said here. **"thy servant for ever" → "your slave forever"** — Hebrew עֶבֶד עוֹלָם, literally "a slave of the age": for his lifetime. **"maidservant" → "slave woman"** — Hebrew אָמָה.
- **"It shall not seem hard unto thee" → "It shall not be hard in your eyes"** (v18) — Hebrew לֹא־יִקְשֶׁה בְעֵינֶךָ. **"he hath been worth a double hired servant to thee, in serving thee six years" → "double the wages of a hired man he has served you six years"** — Hebrew מִשְׁנֶה שְׂכַר שָׂכִיר עֲבָדְךָ. מִשְׁנֶה is "double" (or "the equivalent": so some, from texts outside the Bible); the sense is that his service was worth twice a hired man's wages, or equal to them.
- **"All the firstling males" → "Every firstborn … the male"** (v19) — Hebrew כָּל־הַבְּכוֹר … הַזָּכָר. **"thou shalt do no work with the firstling of thy bullock" → "you shall not make the firstborn of your ox serve"** — Hebrew לֹא תַעֲבֹד בְּ, literally "you shall not serve by means of"; שׁוֹר is "ox", one head of cattle. **"sanctify"** — Hebrew הִקְדִּישׁ.
- **"in the place which the LORD shall choose" kept** (v20) — Hebrew בַּמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר־יִבְחַר יְהוָה, as 12:5–26. **"year by year"** — Hebrew שָׁנָה בְשָׁנָה, literally "year in year". **"thy household" → "your house"** — Hebrew בֵּיתֶךָ.
- **"if there be any blemish therein, as if it be lame, or blind, or have any ill blemish" → "when there is a blemish in it, lame or blind, any evil blemish"** (v21) — Hebrew מוּם "blemish" (Lev 22:20–25); מוּם רָע, literally "an evil blemish": רַע here of quality, "bad".
- **"the unclean and the clean person shall eat it alike, as the roebuck, and as the hart" → "the unclean and the clean together, as the gazelle and as the deer"** (v22) — Hebrew יַחְדָּו "together"; "person" and "shall eat it" are not written. צְבִי and אַיָּל are game, eaten without sacrifice (12:15, 22).
- **"upon the ground" → "on the earth"** (v23) — Hebrew עַל־הָאָרֶץ (as 12:16, 24).

### Notes
- **The release** (v1–11) — in the seventh year what was lent to a brother is let drop. The foreigner (נָכְרִי, the outsider, not the sojourner who lives among Israel) may be pressed (v3).
- **"No needy one among you" and "the needy one will not cease"** (v4, 11) — the two sentences stand side by side in the chapter. v11 is taken up in Matt 26:11; Mark 14:7; John 12:8 ("the poor you always have with you").
- **Six years of service** (v12–18) — parallel to Exod 21:2–6 (and Jer 34:14). Deuteronomy names the woman beside the man (v12, 17), and adds that he is not to be let go empty (v13–14).
- **The ear and the door** (v17) — a visible sign of lifelong service, made at the door; which door is not said.
- **"You were a slave in the land of Egypt"** (v15) — the ground given for the command.
- **The firstborn of herd and flock** (v19–23) — sanctified to Yahweh and eaten before him in the place he will choose; if blemished, eaten in the gates like game. Compare Exod 13:2, 12; 22:30; Num 18:17–18.
