# Deuteronomy 4

[1] "And now, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, listen to the statutes and to the ordinances that I am teaching you to do, so that you may live and go in and take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers, is giving to you.

[2] You shall not add to the word that I am commanding you, and you shall not diminish from it, to keep the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that I am commanding you.

[3] Your eyes are the ones that saw what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did at ⟦baal-peor|Baal Peor|Baal-peor⟧; for every man who went after ⟦baal-peor|Baal Peor|Baal of Peor⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ destroyed him from your midst.

[4] And you, who cling to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, are alive, all of you, today.

[5] See, I have taught you statutes and ordinances, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded me, to do so in the midst of the land to which you are going in to possess it.

[6] And you shall keep and do; for that is your wisdom and your understanding before the eyes of the peoples, who will hear all these statutes and say, 'Only a wise and understanding people is this great nation.'

[7] For what great nation is there that has gods near to it, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in all our calling to him?

[8] And what great nation is there that has statutes and ordinances righteous as all this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ that I am setting before you today?

[9] Only take heed to yourself and keep your soul greatly, lest you forget the things that your eyes have seen, and lest they depart from your heart, all the days of your life; and you shall make them known to your sons and to your sons' sons:

[10] the day that you stood before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ at ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Assemble the people for me, and I will make them hear my words, that they may learn to fear me all the days that they are alive on the ground, and their sons they shall teach.'

[11] And you came near and stood under the mountain; and the mountain was burning with fire to the heart of the heavens: darkness, cloud and dark cloud.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to you from the midst of the fire; a voice of words you were hearing, and a form you were not seeing, only a voice.

[13] And he told you his covenant, which he commanded you to do, the ten words; and he wrote them on two tablets of stone.

[14] And me ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded at that time to teach you statutes and ordinances, for you to do them in the land to which you are passing over to possess it.

[15] And you shall take great heed for your souls, for you did not see any form on the day that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to you at ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧ from the midst of the fire,

[16] lest you work ruin and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of any figure, the pattern of male or female,

[17] the pattern of any beast that is on the earth, the pattern of any winged bird that flies in the heavens,

[18] the pattern of anything that creeps on the ground, the pattern of any fish that is in the waters under the earth;

[19] and lest you lift up your eyes to the heavens and see the sun and the moon and the stars, all the host of the heavens, and be drawn away and bow down to them and serve them, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has apportioned to all the peoples under all the heavens.

[20] And you ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ took, and he brought you out from the furnace of iron, from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, to be to him a people of inheritance, as at this day.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was angry with me because of your words, and he swore that I should not pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and that I should not go into the good land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you as an inheritance.

[22] For I am dying in this land; I am not passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧; and you are passing over, and you shall take possession of this good land.

[23] Take heed to yourselves, lest you forget the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which he cut with you, and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of anything, concerning which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has commanded you.

[24] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, a consuming fire is he, a jealous ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[25] When you beget sons and sons' sons and have grown old in the land, and you work ruin and make a carved image, the form of anything, and do the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to vex him,

[26] I call the heavens and the earth to witness against you today, that you shall surely perish quickly from upon the land to which you are passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to possess it; you shall not prolong days upon it, for you shall surely be destroyed.

[27] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will scatter you among the peoples, and you shall be left few men among the nations to which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will drive you.

[28] And there you shall serve gods, the work of the hands of man, wood and stone, which do not see and do not hear and do not eat and do not smell.

[29] And you shall seek from there ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall find, when you seek him with all your heart and with all your soul.

[30] When you are in distress and all these things have found you, in the latter days you shall return to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and listen to his voice.

[31] For a compassionate ⟦el|El|God⟧ is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; he will not let go of you and he will not ruin you, and he will not forget the covenant of your fathers, which he swore to them.

[32] For ask, please, of the former days that were before you, from the day that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ created man on the earth, and from the end of the heavens and as far as the end of the heavens: has there been anything like this great thing, or has anything like it been heard?

[33] Has a people heard the voice of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ speaking from the midst of the fire, as you have heard, you, and lived?

[34] Or has ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ tried to come to take for himself a nation from the midst of a nation, with testings, with signs and with wonders and with war and with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with great fearsome deeds, according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did for you in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ before your eyes?

[35] You, you were shown, to know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; there is none else besides him.

[36] From the heavens he made you hear his voice, to discipline you; and on the earth he showed you his great fire, and his words you heard from the midst of the fire.

[37] And because he loved your fathers and chose his seed after him, and brought you out with his face, with his great power, from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[38] to dispossess nations greater and mightier than you from before you, to bring you in, to give to you their land as an inheritance, as at this day —

[39] and you shall know today and bring back to your heart that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the heavens above and on the earth beneath; there is none else.

[40] And you shall keep his statutes and his commandments, which I am commanding you today, that it may go well with you and with your sons after you, and so that you may prolong days upon the ground that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, all the days."

[41] Then ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ separated three cities across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, toward the rising of the sun,

[42] for a murderer to flee there, who slays his neighbor without knowledge, and he was not hating him yesterday and the day before; and he shall flee to one of these cities and live:

[43] ⟦bezer|Betzer|Bezer⟧ in the wilderness, in the land of the tableland, for the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenite⟧; and ⟦ramoth|Ramot|Ramoth⟧ in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, for the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadite⟧; and Golan in the Bashan, for the ⟦manassites|Menashi|Manassite⟧.

[44] And this is the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ put before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[45] These are the testimonies and the statutes and the ordinances that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ when they came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[46] across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, in the valley opposite ⟦beth-peor|Beit Peor|Beth-peor⟧, in the land of ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, who dwelt in ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, whom ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ struck down when they came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[47] And they took possession of his land and the land of Og king of the Bashan, the two kings of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ who were across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, toward the rising of the sun,

[48] from Aroer, which is on the edge of the wadi Arnon, and as far as Mount ⟦sion|Siyon|Sirion⟧, that is, ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧,

[49] and all the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧ across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ eastward, and as far as the sea of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, under the slopes of the Pisgah.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Now therefore hearken, O Israel, unto the statutes and unto the judgments, which I teach you, for to do them, that ye may live, and go in and possess" → "And now, Israel, listen to the statutes and to the ordinances that I am teaching you to do, so that you may live and go in and take possession of"** (v1) — Hebrew שְׁמַע אֶל "listen to" (singular, to Israel as one; the rest of the verse is plural); חֻקִּים וּמִשְׁפָּטִים "statutes and ordinances" (מִשְׁפָּט is "judgment"; of a rule laid down, "ordinance", as Lev 18:4; so v5, 8, 14, 45); מְלַמֵּד, a participle; "them" is not in the Hebrew; לְמַעַן "so that" (so v40 and through the book); יָרַשׁ "take possession" (1:8).
- **"Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish ought from it, that ye may keep" → "You shall not add to the word that I am commanding you, and you shall not diminish from it, to keep"** (v2) — word for word: מְצַוֶּה, a participle, both times; גָּרַע "diminish, take away"; לִשְׁמֹר, an infinitive, "to keep": the not adding and not diminishing is itself the keeping, or serves it. "Ought" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Your eyes have seen what the LORD did because of Baal-peor: for all the men that followed Baal-peor" → "Your eyes are the ones that saw what Yahweh did at Baal-peor; for every man who went after Baal of Peor"** (v3) — Hebrew עֵינֵיכֶם הָרֹאֹת (3:21); בְּבַעַל פְּעוֹר, "in / at Baal Peor": the place (Hos 9:10), or "in the matter of Baal Peor", the god; the second time it is the god (Num 25:3). הָלַךְ אַחֲרֵי "go after". "Destroyed him from your midst" is singular in the Hebrew.
- **"ye that did cleave unto the LORD your God are alive every one of you this day" → "you, who cling to Yahweh your Elohim, are alive, all of you, today"** (v4) — Hebrew הַדְּבֵקִים, "the clinging ones", the word of Gen 2:24.
- **"that ye should do so in the land whither ye go to possess it" → "to do so in the midst of the land to which you are going in to possess it"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּקֶרֶב "in the midst of"; בָּאִים, a participle. רְאֵה "see" is singular.
- **"Keep therefore and do them; for this is your wisdom and your understanding in the sight of the nations … Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people" → "And you shall keep and do; for that is your wisdom and your understanding before the eyes of the peoples … Only a wise and understanding people is this great nation"** (v6) — the two verbs have no object in the Hebrew; לְעֵינֵי הָעַמִּים; רַק "only": nothing but (it is also taken as "surely").
- **"For what nation is there so great, who hath God so nigh unto them, as the LORD our God is in all things that we call upon him for?" → "For what great nation is there that has gods near to it, as Yahweh our Elohim is in all our calling to him?"** (v7) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים קְרֹבִים, with a plural adjective: "gods near"; the words may also be read of one, "a god near to it" or "Elohim near to it". בְּכָל־קָרְאֵנוּ אֵלָיו, literally "in all our calling to him": whenever we call.
- **"statutes and judgments so righteous as all this law, which I set before you this day" → "statutes and ordinances righteous as all this Torah that I am setting before you today"** (v8) — Hebrew צַדִּיקִם "righteous"; נֹתֵן לִפְנֵיכֶם "setting before you", literally "giving before you" (the phrase of 1:8; 11:26, 32; v44 has another verb, שִׂים "put").
- **"Only take heed to thyself, and keep thy soul diligently … but teach them thy sons, and thy sons' sons" → "Only take heed to yourself and keep your soul greatly … and you shall make them known to your sons and to your sons' sons"** (v9) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֶר לְךָ וּשְׁמֹר נַפְשְׁךָ מְאֹד, the one verb "keep" twice; הוֹדַעְתָּם "make them known" (לִמֵּד "teach" is the word of v1, 10). The verse and v10 speak to "you" in the singular.
- **"Specially the day that thou stoodest … Gather me the people together … all the days that they shall live upon the earth, and that they may teach their children" → "the day that you stood … Assemble the people for me … all the days that they are alive on the ground, and their sons they shall teach"** (v10) — "specially" is not in the Hebrew; הַקְהֶל, the verb of קָהָל "assembly"; אֲשֶׁר יִלְמְדוּן "that they may learn" (or "which they shall learn"); לָמַד "learn" and לִמֵּד "teach" are one root; אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"stood under the mountain; and the mountain burned with fire unto the midst of heaven, with darkness, clouds, and thick darkness" → "stood under the mountain; and the mountain was burning with fire to the heart of the heavens: darkness, cloud and dark cloud"** (v11) — Hebrew בֹּעֵר, a participle; לֵב "heart"; עָנָן "cloud" and עֲרָפֶל "dark cloud" (Exod 20:21); no "with".
- **"ye heard the voice of the words, but saw no similitude; only ye heard a voice" → "a voice of words you were hearing, and a form you were not seeing, only a voice"** (v12) — Hebrew קוֹל דְּבָרִים, "a voice (sound) of words", and again קוֹל at the end; participles; תְּמוּנָה "form", a visible shape (Exod 20:4; so v15, 16, 23, 25). The second "ye heard" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"he declared unto you his covenant, which he commanded you to perform, even ten commandments; and he wrote them upon two tables of stone" → "he told you his covenant, which he commanded you to do, the ten words; and he wrote them on two tablets of stone"** (v13) — Hebrew הִגִּיד "tell"; עֲשֶׂרֶת הַדְּבָרִים *aseret ha-devarim*, "the ten words" (Exod 34:28): "words", not "commandments". לֻחוֹת אֲבָנִים, literally "tablets of stones".
- **"that ye might do them in the land whither ye go over to possess it" → "for you to do them in the land to which you are passing over to possess it"** (v14) — Hebrew לַעֲשֹׂתְכֶם אֹתָם; עֹבְרִים "passing over" (v5 has "going in").
- **"Take ye therefore good heed unto yourselves; for ye saw no manner of similitude" → "And you shall take great heed for your souls, for you did not see any form"** (v15) — Hebrew וְנִשְׁמַרְתֶּם מְאֹד לְנַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶם; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"Lest ye corrupt yourselves, and make you a graven image, the similitude of any figure, the likeness of male or female" → "lest you work ruin and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of any figure, the pattern of male or female"** (v16) — Hebrew תַּשְׁחִתוּן, the "ruin" verb of Gen 6:12, with no object (so v25; Exod 32:7); פֶּסֶל "carved image" (Exod 20:4), תְּמוּנָה "form", סֶמֶל "figure, statue" (exact sense uncertain), תַּבְנִית "pattern", a thing built after the shape of another (Exod 25:9): four words, kept apart. דְּמוּת "likeness" is not in these verses.
- **"any winged fowl that flieth in the air" → "any winged bird that flies in the heavens"** (v17) — Hebrew צִפּוֹר כָּנָף, "bird of wing"; בַּשָּׁמָיִם.
- **"lest thou lift up thine eyes unto heaven … shouldest be driven to worship them, and serve them, which the LORD thy God hath divided unto all nations under the whole heaven" → "lest you lift up your eyes to the heavens … and be drawn away and bow down to them and serve them, which Yahweh your Elohim has apportioned to all the peoples under all the heavens"** (v19) — Hebrew נִדַּחְתָּ "be thrust, drawn away"; הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down"; חָלַק, the verb of חֵלֶק "share": to deal out as a share; עַמִּים "peoples". The verse is singular.
- **"brought you forth out of the iron furnace, even out of Egypt, to be unto him a people of inheritance, as ye are this day" → "brought you out from the furnace of iron, from Egypt, to be to him a people of inheritance, as at this day"** (v20) — Hebrew כּוּר הַבַּרְזֶל, a furnace for smelting iron; כַּיּוֹם הַזֶּה "as this day".
- **"the LORD was angry with me for your sakes" → "Yahweh was angry with me because of your words"** (v21) — Hebrew עַל־דִּבְרֵיכֶם, literally "over your words" (or "over your matters": on your account); 1:37 has בִּגְלַלְכֶם, 3:26 לְמַעַנְכֶם. **"giveth thee for an inheritance" → "is giving to you as an inheritance"** — a participle; "you" is singular.
- **"But I must die in this land, I must not go over Jordan: but ye shall go over" → "For I am dying in this land; I am not passing over the Jordan; and you are passing over"** (v22) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; participles; "must" is not a word of the Hebrew.
- **"the covenant … which he made with you, and make you a graven image, or the likeness of any thing, which the LORD thy God hath forbidden thee" → "the covenant … which he cut with you, and make for yourselves a carved image, the form of anything, concerning which Yahweh your Elohim has commanded you"** (v23) — Hebrew כָּרַת "cut" a covenant; תְּמוּנַת כֹּל "the form of anything"; אֲשֶׁר צִוְּךָ, literally "which Yahweh your Elohim commanded you": the Hebrew has no word "forbid"; he gave command about it (not to make it). "You" in "commanded you" is singular.
- **"For the LORD thy God is a consuming fire, even a jealous God" → "For Yahweh your Elohim, a consuming fire is he, a jealous El"** (v24) — Hebrew אֵשׁ אֹכְלָה הוּא אֵל קַנָּא; אֹכְלָה is "eating" (Exod 24:17); אֵל קַנָּא *El qanna*, as Exod 20:5; 34:14 (קַנָּא is "jealous" or "zealous").
- **"When thou shalt beget children, and children's children, and ye shall have remained long in the land, and shall corrupt yourselves … and shall do evil in the sight of the LORD thy God, to provoke him to anger" → "When you beget sons and sons' sons and have grown old in the land, and you work ruin … and do the evil in the eyes of Yahweh your Elohim, to vex him"** (v25) — Hebrew נוֹשַׁנְתֶּם, from יָשָׁן "old"; הָרַע "the evil", with the article; הִכְעִיס "vex" ("anger" is not a second word in the Hebrew). "You beget" and "your Elohim" are singular, the other verbs plural.
- **"ye shall soon utterly perish from off the land … ye shall not prolong your days upon it, but shall utterly be destroyed" → "you shall surely perish quickly from upon the land … you shall not prolong days upon it, for you shall surely be destroyed"** (v26) — Hebrew אָבֹד תֹּאבֵדוּן and הִשָּׁמֵד תִּשָּׁמֵדוּן, each verb with its infinitive absolute, literally "perishing you shall perish", "being destroyed you shall be destroyed"; כִּי "for". הַעִידֹתִי בָכֶם, literally "I have made (them) witness against you".
- **"ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the LORD shall lead you" → "you shall be left few men among the nations to which Yahweh will drive you"** (v27) — Hebrew מְתֵי מִסְפָּר, literally "men of number" (men who can be counted); גּוֹיִם "nations"; נִהַג "drive".
- **"the work of men's hands" → "the work of the hands of man"** (v28) — Hebrew אָדָם.
- **"But if from thence thou shalt seek the LORD thy God, thou shalt find him, if thou seek him" → "And you shall seek from there Yahweh your Elohim, and you shall find, when you seek him"** (v29) — no "if" at the head of the Hebrew verse; "seek" is plural, "your Elohim" and the rest singular; "him" after "find" is not in the Hebrew; כִּי "when" (or "for", "if"). Two verbs for "seek": בִּקֵּשׁ and דָּרַשׁ.
- **"When thou art in tribulation, and all these things are come upon thee, even in the latter days, if thou turn to the LORD thy God, and shalt be obedient unto his voice" → "When you are in distress and all these things have found you, in the latter days you shall return to Yahweh your Elohim and listen to his voice"** (v30) — Hebrew בַּצַּר לְךָ, literally "in the distress to you"; מְצָאוּךָ "have found you"; בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים, "in the after-part of the days", set by the Hebrew accents with "you shall return"; וְשַׁבְתָּ "and you shall return" (it may also be read as the condition, "and you return …", with v31 as the answer); שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice".
- **"(For the LORD thy God is a merciful God;) he will not forsake thee, neither destroy thee, nor forget the covenant of thy fathers" → "For a compassionate El is Yahweh your Elohim; he will not let go of you and he will not ruin you, and he will not forget the covenant of your fathers"** (v31) — word for word: אֵל רַחוּם *El rachum* (Exod 34:6); יַרְפְּךָ, from רָפָה "sink, let drop"; יַשְׁחִיתֶךָ, the "ruin" verb (v16), not the "destroy" of v26. No brackets in the Hebrew.
- **"ask now of the days that are past … since the day that God created man upon the earth, and ask from the one side of heaven unto the other" → "ask, please, of the former days … from the day that Elohim created man on the earth, and from the end of the heavens and as far as the end of the heavens"** (v32) — Hebrew נָא "please"; the second "ask" is not in the Hebrew; אָדָם "man, mankind" (Gen 1:27).
- **"Did ever people hear the voice of God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as thou hast heard, and live?" → "Has a people heard the voice of Elohim speaking from the midst of the fire, as you have heard, you, and lived?"** (v33) — אֱלֹהִים has no article and a singular participle; "a god" is also a possible reading here and in v34.
- **"Or hath God assayed to go and take him a nation … by temptations … and by a mighty hand, and by a stretched out arm, and by great terrors" → "Or has Elohim tried to come to take for himself a nation … with testings … and with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with great fearsome deeds"** (v34) — the one preposition בְּ seven times, "with" each time (בְּיָד חֲזָקָה "with a strong hand", as Exod 13:9; 32:11); Hebrew נִסָּה, the verb "test", here "make trial, try"; מַסֹּת "testings" is its noun; חֲזָקָה "strong" (Exod 6:1); מוֹרָאִים, literally "fears", things that make men fear ("deeds" is supplied). "Did for you" is plural, "before your eyes" singular.
- **"Unto thee it was shewed, that thou mightest know that the LORD he is God; there is none else beside him" → "You, you were shown, to know that Yahweh, he is Elohim; there is none else besides him"** (v35) — word for word: אַתָּה הָרְאֵתָ לָדַעַת, "you were made to see, to know"; הָאֱלֹהִים has the article, "the Elohim": the one who is God (so v39); אֵין עוֹד מִלְבַדּוֹ.
- **"that he might instruct thee" → "to discipline you"** (v36) — Hebrew יִסֵּר "discipline, correct" (8:5).
- **"therefore he chose their seed after them, and brought thee out in his sight with his mighty power" → "and chose his seed after him, and brought you out with his face, with his great power"** (v37) — the Masoretic Text has the singular, "his seed after him" (of one father); the ancient versions have the plural (from memory). בְּפָנָיו "with his face": he himself (Exod 33:14). The Hebrew has no "therefore": the sentence of v37–38 runs on into v39.
- **"To drive out nations from before thee" → "to dispossess nations … from before you"** (v38) — Hebrew הוֹרִישׁ.
- **"Know therefore this day, and consider it in thine heart, that the LORD he is God in heaven above, and upon the earth beneath: there is none else" → "and you shall know today and bring back to your heart that Yahweh, he is Elohim in the heavens above and on the earth beneath; there is none else"** (v39) — word for word: וַהֲשֵׁבֹתָ אֶל־לְבָבֶךָ, "and make (it) return to your heart"; הָאֱלֹהִים as v35; אֵין עוֹד.
- **"that it may go well with thee … and that thou mayest prolong thy days upon the earth, which the LORD thy God giveth thee, for ever" → "that it may go well with you … and so that you may prolong days upon the ground that Yahweh your Elohim is giving to you, all the days"** (v40) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יִיטַב לְךָ, literally "that it may be good for you" (אֲשֶׁר "that"; the second clause has לְמַעַן "so that"); אֲדָמָה "ground"; כָּל־הַיָּמִים "all the days" ("forever" is another word).
- **"Then Moses severed three cities on this side Jordan toward the sunrising" → "Then Moses separated three cities across the Jordan, toward the rising of the sun"** (v41) — Hebrew אָז יַבְדִּיל, "then" with an imperfect; הִבְדִּיל "separate" (Gen 1:4); בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן, here the east side.
- **"That the slayer might flee thither, which should kill his neighbour unawares, and hated him not in times past" → "for a murderer to flee there, who slays his neighbor without knowledge, and he was not hating him yesterday and the day before"** (v42) — Hebrew רוֹצֵחַ … יִרְצַח, the noun and the verb of Exod 20:13 "murder". The noun is "murderer" as in Num 35, where it names the man who killed, whatever his intent; the verb is given as "slays" (as Num 35:27), because "murders … without knowledge" would say an intent that the clause denies. בִּבְלִי־דַעַת "without knowledge" is another expression than the בִּשְׁגָגָה "in error" of Num 35:11. מִתְּמוֹל שִׁלְשׁוֹם, "from yesterday, the day before": before.
- **"Bezer in the wilderness, in the plain country, of the Reubenites" → "Bezer in the wilderness, in the land of the tableland, for the Reubenite"** (v43) — Hebrew הַמִּישֹׁר (3:10); the tribes are named in the singular with the article.
- **"this is the law which Moses set before the children of Israel" → "this is the Torah that Moses put before the sons of Israel"** (v44) — Hebrew שָׂם "put"; בְּנֵי "sons".
- **"the testimonies, and the statutes, and the judgments … after they came forth out of Egypt" → "the testimonies and the statutes and the ordinances … when they came out from Egypt"** (v45, 46) — Hebrew עֵדֹת "testimonies"; בְּצֵאתָם, "in their coming out".
- **"On this side Jordan … king of the Amorites, who dwelt at Heshbon, whom Moses and the children of Israel smote" → "across the Jordan … king of the Amorite, who dwelt in Heshbon, whom Moses and the sons of Israel struck down"** (v46) — as 1:4.
- **"they possessed his land … toward the sunrising" → "they took possession of his land … toward the rising of the sun"** (v47) — the words of Num 21:35.
- **"by the bank of the river Arnon, even unto mount Sion, which is Hermon" → "on the edge of the wadi Arnon, and as far as Mount Sion, that is, Hermon"** (v48) — Hebrew שְׂפַת "lip, edge"; שִׂיאֹן *Si'on*, another name than Zion (צִיּוֹן); 3:9 has Sirion.
- **"all the plain on this side Jordan eastward, even unto the sea of the plain, under the springs of Pisgah" → "all the Arabah across the Jordan eastward, and as far as the sea of the Arabah, under the slopes of the Pisgah"** (v49) — as 3:17.

### Notes
- **Transition chapter** — ends the historical prologue (chs 1–3) and begins the exhortation. Key themes: keeping, remembering, no image.
- **"No form"** (v12, 15) — a voice was heard and no form was seen; from this the chapter argues against every carved image.
- **Sun, moon and stars** (v19) — "apportioned to all the peoples": the words say only that Yahweh dealt them out to all the peoples; in what sense (as lights for all, or as what the peoples serve) is not said. Compare 29:26; 32:8.
- **"Furnace of iron"** (v20) — repeated in 1 Kgs 8:51; Jer 11:4.
- **Scattering and return** (v27–31) — unfaithfulness, scattering, seeking, return.
- **"Yahweh, he is *Elohim*; there is none else"** (v35, 39) — echoed in 1 Kgs 18:39.
- **Three cities east of the Jordan** (v41–43) — Num 35:14; the three in the land of Canaan are named in Josh 20.
- **"Torah"** (v44) — the heading of what follows.
- **"You" singular and plural** — the Hebrew moves between the plural and the singular "you", sometimes within one verse (v3, 19–21, 23, 25, 29, 34); English shows no difference.
