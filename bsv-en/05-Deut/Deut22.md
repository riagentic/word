# Deuteronomy 22

[1] "You shall not see your brother's ox or his sheep driven astray and hide yourself from them; you shall surely return them to your brother.

[2] And if your brother is not near to you, and you do not know him, then you shall gather it into the midst of your house, and it shall be with you until your brother seeks it, and you shall return it to him.

[3] And so you shall do with his donkey, and so you shall do with his cloak, and so you shall do with every lost thing of your brother's, which is lost from him and you find it; you cannot hide yourself.

[4] You shall not see your brother's donkey or his ox fallen on the way and hide yourself from them; you shall surely raise them up with him.

[5] A man's article shall not be on a woman, and a man shall not put on a woman's cloak, for an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is everyone who does these things.

[6] When a bird's nest happens to be before you on the way, in any tree or on the ground, fledglings or eggs, and the mother is lying on the fledglings or on the eggs, you shall not take the mother together with the young.

[7] You shall surely send the mother away, and the young you may take for yourself, so that it may go well with you and you may prolong days.

[8] When you build a new house, you shall make a parapet for your roof, and you shall not put bloodguilt on your house when the one who falls falls from it.

[9] You shall not sow your vineyard with two kinds, lest the fullness become holy: the seed that you sow and the yield of the vineyard.

[10] You shall not plow with an ox and with a donkey together.

[11] You shall not put on mixed stuff, wool and linen together.

[12] Twisted cords you shall make for yourself on the four corners of your covering, with which you cover yourself.

[13] When a man takes a wife and goes in to her and hates her,

[14] and lays against her deeds of words and brings out an evil name upon her and says, 'This woman I took, and I came near to her, and I did not find in her virginity,'

[15] then the father of the girl and her mother shall take and bring out the virginity of the girl to the elders of the city, to the gate.

[16] And the father of the girl shall say to the elders, 'My daughter I gave to this man as a wife, and he hated her,

[17] and behold, he has laid deeds of words, saying, "I did not find in your daughter virginity"; and these are the virginity of my daughter.' And they shall spread out the cloak before the elders of the city.

[18] And the elders of that city shall take the man and discipline him,

[19] and they shall fine him a hundred of silver and give them to the father of the girl, for he has brought out an evil name upon a virgin of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and she shall be his wife; he cannot send her away all his days.

[20] But if this word was truth, virginity was not found in the girl,

[21] then they shall bring the girl out to the entrance of her father's house, and the men of her city shall stone her with stones, and she shall die, for she has done an outrage in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to play the prostitute in her father's house; and you shall purge the evil from your midst.

[22] When a man is found lying with a woman married to a husband, then they shall die, both of them also, the man who lies with the woman, and the woman; and you shall purge the evil from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[23] When there is a girl, a virgin, betrothed to a man, and a man finds her in the city and lies with her,

[24] then you shall bring both of them out to the gate of that city and stone them with stones, and they shall die: the girl, in the matter that she did not cry out in the city, and the man, in the matter that he humbled his neighbor's wife; and you shall purge the evil from your midst.

[25] But if in the field the man finds the betrothed girl, and the man holds her fast and lies with her, then the man who lay with her shall die, he alone.

[26] And to the girl you shall do nothing; the girl has no sin of death; for as when a man rises against his neighbor and murders him, a soul, so is this matter.

[27] For in the field he found her; the betrothed girl cried out, and there was none to save her.

[28] When a man finds a girl, a virgin, who is not betrothed, and seizes her and lies with her, and they are found,

[29] then the man who lies with her shall give to the father of the girl fifty of silver, and she shall be his wife, because he humbled her; he cannot send her away all his days.

[30] A man shall not take his father's wife, and he shall not uncover his father's corner.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. "You" is one person (singular) throughout, except "you shall bring … and stone" in v24, which is plural.
- **"go astray" → "driven astray"** (v1) — Hebrew נִדָּחִים, "driven away". **"thou shalt in any case bring them again" → "you shall surely return them"** — Hebrew הָשֵׁב תְּשִׁיבֵם, literally "returning you shall return them". "Sheep" is שֶׂה, one animal of the flock, sheep or goat.
- **"or if thou know him not" → "and you do not know him"** (v2) — Hebrew וְלֹא. **"bring it unto thine own house" → "gather it into the midst of your house"** — Hebrew וַאֲסַפְתּוֹ אֶל־תּוֹךְ בֵּיתֶךָ. **"restore" → "return"** — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב, as v1.
- **"his raiment" → "his cloak"** (v3) — Hebrew שִׂמְלָה, as v5, 17. **"which he hath lost" → "which is lost from him"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־תֹּאבַד מִמֶּנּוּ. **"thou mayest not hide thyself" → "you cannot hide yourself"** — Hebrew לֹא תוּכַל, "you are not able": you are not free to.
- **"thou shalt surely help him to lift them up again" → "you shall surely raise them up with him"** (v4) — Hebrew הָקֵם תָּקִים עִמּוֹ, literally "raising you shall raise with him"; the Hebrew has no object.
- **"The woman shall not wear that which pertaineth unto a man" → "A man's article shall not be on a woman"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־יִהְיֶה כְלִי־גֶבֶר עַל־אִשָּׁה, word for word. כְּלִי is any made article: a vessel, a tool, an ornament, a piece of clothing, a weapon. The word for "man" here is גֶּבֶר both times, the word for a man in his strength (elsewhere "mighty man"), not אִישׁ; set against "woman" it is "man", and "mighty man" would narrow the law to warriors. Some do take כְּלִי־גֶבֶר as a warrior's gear, his weapons. **"a woman's garment" → "a woman's cloak"** — Hebrew שִׂמְלָה. **"all that do so are abomination unto the LORD" → "an abomination to Yahweh … is everyone who does these things"** — Hebrew תוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה, literally "an abomination of Yahweh".
- **"chance to be" → "happens to be"** (v6) — Hebrew יִקָּרֵא. **"young ones" → "fledglings"; "the young"** (v6, 7) — two words: אֶפְרֹחִים "fledglings" and בָּנִים, literally "sons". **"the dam sitting" → "the mother is lying"** — Hebrew רֹבֶצֶת (Gen 49:9). **"with the young" → "together with the young"** — Hebrew עַל־הַבָּנִים, literally "upon the sons" (Gen 32:11).
- **"let the dam go" → "send the mother away"** (v7) — Hebrew שַׁלֵּחַ תְּשַׁלַּח, literally "sending you shall send". **"that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy days" → "so that it may go well with you and you may prolong days"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן יִיטַב לָךְ וְהַאֲרַכְתָּ יָמִים (worded as 5:16; 6:18; 12:25), literally "that it may be good for you"; "your" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"battlement" → "parapet"** (v8) — Hebrew מַעֲקֶה, found only here: a low wall round the flat roof. **"that thou bring not blood upon thine house, if any man fall from thence" → "and you shall not put bloodguilt on your house when the one who falls falls from it"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־תָשִׂים דָּמִים בְּבֵיתֶךָ כִּי־יִפֹּל הַנֹּפֵל מִמֶּנּוּ: דָּמִים, "bloods" in the plural, is guilt for a death; בְּבֵיתֶךָ is literally "in your house"; הַנֹּפֵל "the faller".
- **"with divers seeds" → "with two kinds"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּלְאָיִם, a dual (Lev 19:19); "seeds" is not in the Hebrew. **"lest the fruit of thy seed which thou hast sown, and the fruit of thy vineyard, be defiled" → "lest the fullness become holy: the seed that you sow and the yield of the vineyard"** — Hebrew פֶּן־תִּקְדַּשׁ הַמְלֵאָה, word for word: מְלֵאָה is "fullness", the full produce (Exod 22:29); תִּקְדַּשׁ is "become holy", that is, fall to the sanctuary and be lost to the owner. "Defiled" is not what the word says.
- **"with an ox and an ass" → "with an ox and with a donkey"** (v10) — the Hebrew repeats the preposition.
- **"Thou shalt not wear a garment of divers sorts, as of woollen and linen together" → "You shall not put on mixed stuff, wool and linen together"** (v11) — Hebrew שַׁעַטְנֵז, a word of unknown origin (Lev 19:19), explained here by "wool and linen together"; "garment" is not in the Hebrew. "Put on" is לָבַשׁ, as in v5. "Linen" is פִּשְׁתִּים, "flax".
- **"fringes upon the four quarters of thy vesture" → "Twisted cords … on the four corners of your covering"** (v12) — Hebrew גְּדִלִים, "twisted threads" (1 Kgs 7:17), another word than the "tassel" (צִיצִת) of Num 15:38; כַּנְפוֹת is literally "wings"; כְּסוּת "covering", of one root with "cover yourself".
- **"If any man take a wife … and hate her" → "When a man takes a wife … and hates her"** (v13, 16) — Hebrew שָׂנֵא, the plain word "hate".
- **"give occasions of speech against her" → "lays against her deeds of words"** (v14, 17) — Hebrew וְשָׂם לָהּ עֲלִילֹת דְּבָרִים, word for word; the phrase is found only here and its exact sense is not certain. עֲלִילָה is "a deed, a doing", often a wanton one; דְּבָרִים is "words" or "things": deeds that are laid on her in words, that is, charges of shameful conduct, or baseless charges. **"bring up an evil name upon her" → "brings out an evil name upon her"** (v14, 19) — Hebrew הוֹצִיא … שֵׁם רָע.
- **"when I came to her, I found her not a maid" → "I came near to her, and I did not find in her virginity"** (v14, 17, 20) — Hebrew וְלֹא־מָצָאתִי לָהּ בְּתוּלִים: בְּתוּלִים is "virginity", a plural noun; here the signs of it are meant. **"the tokens of the damsel's virginity" → "the virginity of the girl"** (v15, 17) — Hebrew בְּתוּלֵי הַנַּעֲרָה; "tokens" and "evidence" are not in the Hebrew. What is brought out is the cloak of v17.
- **"damsel" → "girl"** (v15–29) — Hebrew נַעֲרָה, "girl"; "virgin" (v19, 23, 28) is בְּתוּלָה; the two stand side by side in v23, 28. In this chapter the word is written נער, without its last letter, everywhere but v19; the reading tradition (qere) reads נַעֲרָה each time.
- **"in the gate" → "to the gate"** (v15) — Hebrew הַשָּׁעְרָה. **"the cloth" → "the cloak"** (v17) — Hebrew הַשִּׂמְלָה, as v3, 5.
- **"chastise" → "discipline"** (v18) — Hebrew יִסֵּר, as 21:18.
- **"amerce him in an hundred shekels of silver" → "fine him a hundred of silver"** (v19), **"fifty shekels of silver" → "fifty of silver"** (v29) — Hebrew מֵאָה כֶסֶף, חֲמִשִּׁים כָּסֶף; "shekels" is not written (as Gen 20:16), though the shekel is the weight meant. **"he may not put her away" → "he cannot send her away"** (v19, 29) — Hebrew לֹא־יוּכַל לְשַׁלְּחָהּ: שִׁלַּח is "send away", the word for dismissing a wife (24:1).
- **"if this thing be true" → "if this word was truth"** (v20) — Hebrew אֱמֶת הָיָה הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה; דָּבָר is "word" and "thing".
- **"door" → "entrance"** (v21) — Hebrew פֶּתַח. **"stone her with stones that she die" → "stone her with stones, and she shall die"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew וָמֵתָה; the verb is סָקַל (in 21:21 רָגַם). **"she hath wrought folly in Israel" → "she has done an outrage in Israel"** — Hebrew נְבָלָה, as Gen 34:7. **"to play the whore in her father's house" → "to play the prostitute in her father's house"** — Hebrew לִזְנוֹת בֵּית אָבִיהָ; the Hebrew has no "in". **"so shalt thou put evil away from among you" → "and you shall purge the evil from your midst"** (v21, 22, 24) — Hebrew וּבִעַרְתָּ הָרָע מִקִּרְבֶּךָ, as 21:21; in v22 "from Israel".
- **"a woman married to an husband"** (v22) — Hebrew בְעֻלַת־בַּעַל, literally "owned by an owner" (Gen 20:3). **"then they shall both of them die" → "then they shall die, both of them also"** — Hebrew וּמֵתוּ גַּם־שְׁנֵיהֶם.
- **"betrothed unto an husband" → "betrothed to a man"** (v23) — Hebrew לְאִישׁ.
- **"because she cried not … because he hath humbled his neighbour's wife" → "in the matter that she did not cry out … in the matter that he humbled his neighbor's wife"** (v24) — Hebrew עַל־דְּבַר אֲשֶׁר both times. **"humbled"** (v24, 29) — kept: Hebrew עִנָּה, literally "afflict, humble": the word for a man's taking a woman who is not his (Gen 34:2; Deut 21:14). In v24 the girl did not cry out, so the word alone does not say that force was used.
- **"and the man force her" → "and the man holds her fast"** (v25) — Hebrew וְהֶחֱזִיק־בָּהּ, "take a strong hold of", from חָזַק "be strong"; another verb than "seizes" in v28 (תָּפַשׂ). **"the man only" → "the man …, he alone"** — Hebrew לְבַדּוֹ.
- **"there is in the damsel no sin worthy of death" → "the girl has no sin of death"** (v26) — Hebrew אֵין לַנַּעֲרָה חֵטְא מָוֶת. **"slayeth him" → "murders him, a soul"** — Hebrew וּרְצָחוֹ נֶפֶשׁ, literally so: murders him as to the soul, takes his life; רָצַח is the verb of Exod 20:13.
- **"and there was none to save her"** (v27) — Hebrew וְאֵין מוֹשִׁיעַ לָהּ, "there was no savior for her".
- **"lay hold on her" → "seizes her"** (v28) — Hebrew וּתְפָשָׂהּ (20:19; 21:19). Whether the verb here says force, as "holds her fast" in v25 does, is disputed; "they are found" speaks of both. Exod 22:16 has "entices" (פִּתָּה) for a like case.
- **"nor discover his father's skirt" → "and he shall not uncover his father's corner"** (v30) — Hebrew כְּנַף אָבִיו, literally "the wing of his father": the corner of his garment (v12), which a man spreads over the woman he takes (Ruth 3:9; Ezek 16:8). "Uncover" is גִּלָּה, as in "uncover the nakedness" (Lev 18:8).

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — v30 is 23:1 in the Hebrew.
- **Mixed civic, agricultural, family laws** — the chapter is a patchwork of practical legal provisions.
- **Duty to a brother** (v1–4) — an active duty: strayed animals and lost things must be returned; fallen animals raised up. One "cannot hide" oneself.
- **A man's article, a woman's cloak** (v5) — the verse gives no reason beyond "an abomination to Yahweh". Proposed backgrounds (rites of other cults; the mixing of what is to be kept apart, as in v9–11) are guesses.
- **Mother bird** (v6–7) — the young may be taken, the mother must be sent away. The same promise, "that it may go well with you", stands with the commandment about father and mother (5:16).
- **Parapet on roof** (v8) — flat roofs were used as living space; the builder answers for a fall.
- **Virginity** (v13–21) — the parents bring out the cloak; if the charge is false, the man is disciplined and fined and can never send her away; if it is true, the girl is stoned.
- **City and field** (v23–27) — the betrothed girl counts as a neighbor's wife. In the city she could have cried out; in the field she is held fast, she cried out, and no one could save her: the man alone dies, and the deed is set beside murder.
- **The girl not betrothed** (v28–29) — the man pays her father fifty of silver, she becomes his wife, and he can never send her away (compare Exod 22:16–17, where the father may refuse).
