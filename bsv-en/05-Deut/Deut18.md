# Deuteronomy 18

[1] "The priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, all the tribe of Levi, shall have no share and inheritance with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and his inheritance they shall eat.

[2] And an inheritance he shall not have in the midst of his brothers; ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he is his inheritance, as he spoke to him.

[3] And this shall be the ordinance of the priests from the people, from those who sacrifice the sacrifice, whether ox or sheep: he shall give to the priest the foreleg and the two jaws and the stomach.

[4] The first of your grain, your new wine and your fresh oil, and the first of the shearing of your flock, you shall give to him.

[5] For him ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has chosen out of all your tribes, to stand to minister in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he and his sons, all the days.

[6] And when the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ comes from one of your gates, out of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, where he is sojourning, and comes with all the desire of his soul to the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will choose,

[7] and ministers in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, like all his brothers the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ who stand there before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[8] share like share they shall eat, apart from his sales of what belongs to the fathers.

[9] When you are coming to the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, you shall not learn to do according to the abominations of those nations.

[10] There shall not be found among you one who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, one who practices divinations, one who practices soothsaying, and one who divines, and a sorcerer,

[11] and one who binds spells, and one who asks a ghost or a knowing spirit, and one who seeks to the dead.

[12] For everyone who does these is an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and because of these abominations ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is dispossessing them from before you.

[13] Blameless you shall be with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[14] For these nations, whom you are dispossessing, listen to those who practice soothsaying and to those who practice divination; but you, not so has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ given to you.

[15] A prophet from your midst, from your brothers, like me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will raise up for you; to him you shall listen,

[16] according to all that you asked from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ at ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧ on the day of the assembly, saying, 'I will not again hear the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and this great fire I will not see anymore, and I will not die.'

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'They have done well in what they have spoken.

[18] A prophet I will raise up for them from the midst of their brothers, like you, and I will put my words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all that I command him.

[19] And it shall be, the man who does not listen to my words that he speaks in my name, I myself will require it from him.

[20] But the prophet who acts presumptuously, to speak a word in my name that I have not commanded him to speak, and who speaks in the name of other gods, that prophet shall die.'

[21] And when you say in your heart, 'How shall we know the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not spoken?'

[22] What the prophet speaks in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the word does not happen and does not come: that is the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not spoken; with presumption the prophet has spoken it; you shall not be afraid of him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"The priests the Levites, and all the tribe of Levi, shall have no part nor inheritance" → "The priests, the Levites, all the tribe of Levi, shall have no share and inheritance"** (v1) — Hebrew לַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם כָּל־שֵׁבֶט לֵוִי: three terms side by side, with no "and" before "all the tribe". Whether "all the tribe of Levi" says the same as "the priests, the Levites" or widens it is debated; the verse keeps the Hebrew as it stands. חֵלֶק וְנַחֲלָה "a share and an inheritance" (Num 18:20). **"the offerings of the LORD made by fire, and his inheritance"** — Hebrew אִשֵּׁי יְהוָה וְנַחֲלָתוֹ; the derivation of אִשֶּׁה from "fire" is not certain. "His inheritance" may be Yahweh's (what falls to him) or the tribe's.
- **"Therefore shall they have no inheritance among their brethren" → "And an inheritance he shall not have in the midst of his brothers"** (v2) — the Hebrew is singular (the tribe): לוֹ "to him", אֶחָיו "his brothers", נַחֲלָתוֹ "his inheritance". **"as he hath said unto them" → "as he spoke to him"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר־לוֹ (Num 18:20).
- **"the priest's due" → "the ordinance of the priests"** (v3) — Hebrew מִשְׁפַּט הַכֹּהֲנִים, literally "the judgment of the priests": what is theirs by rule (as Exod 21:9; 1 Sam 2:13). **"them that offer a sacrifice" → "those who sacrifice the sacrifice"** — Hebrew זֹבְחֵי הַזֶּבַח. **"ox or sheep"** — Hebrew שׁוֹר, שֶׂה (17:1). **"they shall give" → "he shall give"** — Hebrew וְנָתַן, singular. **"the shoulder, and the two cheeks, and the maw" → "the foreleg and the two jaws and the stomach"** — Hebrew זְרֹעַ, literally "arm"; לְחָיַיִם "jaws, cheeks"; קֵבָה (Num 25:8). Lev 7:31–34 names the breast and the right leg (שׁוֹק) as the priests' part of the peace offerings; the parts named here are others.
- **"The firstfruit also of thy corn, of thy wine, and of thine oil, and the first of the fleece of thy sheep" → "The first of your grain, your new wine and your fresh oil, and the first of the shearing of your flock"** (v4) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית "the first", both times; דָּגָן, תִּירֹשׁ, יִצְהָר (Num 18:12); גֵּז "shearing, what is shorn"; צֹאן "flock".
- **"to stand to minister" kept; "for ever" → "all the days"** (v5) — Hebrew לַעֲמֹד לְשָׁרֵת; כָּל־הַיָּמִים. "Him" is singular: the tribe, or the priest of v3.
- **"if a Levite come from any of thy gates" → "when the Levite comes from one of your gates"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי; הַלֵּוִי with the article; מֵאַחַד שְׁעָרֶיךָ. **"where he sojourned" → "where he is sojourning"** — Hebrew גָּר, a participle: he lives there as a sojourner (גֵּר), without land of his own. **"desire of his mind" → "desire of his soul"** — Hebrew אַוַּת נַפְשׁוֹ.
- **"Then he shall minister" → "and ministers"** (v7) — Hebrew וְשֵׁרֵת. The sentence can be divided in two ways: (1) v6–7 give the case, and v8 the ruling ("share like share they shall eat"); (2) v6 gives the case and v7 the ruling ("then he shall minister"). The verse follows the first; the Hebrew form allows both.
- **"They shall have like portions to eat" → "share like share they shall eat"** (v8) — Hebrew חֵלֶק כְּחֵלֶק יֹאכֵלוּ. **"beside that which cometh of the sale of his patrimony" → "apart from his sales of what belongs to the fathers"** — Hebrew לְבַד מִמְכָּרָיו עַל־הָאָבוֹת, literally "apart from his sales upon the fathers". The meaning is not certain: usually taken as what he has from selling what came to him from his fathers.
- **"When thou art come into the land" → "When you are coming to the land"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי אַתָּה בָּא, a participle.
- **"that maketh his son or his daughter to pass through the fire" kept** (v10) — Hebrew מַעֲבִיר בְּנוֹ־וּבִתּוֹ בָּאֵשׁ, literally "makes his son and his daughter pass over in the fire" (הֶעֱבִיר, as Lev 18:21 "make pass over to Molech"; here Molech is not named and "in the fire" is written). What was done is not described (compare 12:31 "they burn with fire").
- **The practices of v10–11**, each by its own word:
  - **"that useth divination" → "one who practices divinations"** — קֹסֵם קְסָמִים, verb and noun of one root, literally "divining divinations" (Num 22:7; 23:23); so v14 "those who practice divination".
  - **"an observer of times" → "one who practices soothsaying"** — מְעוֹנֵן (Lev 19:26); the exact sense is unknown. So v14.
  - **"an enchanter" → "one who divines"** — מְנַחֵשׁ (Gen 44:5; Lev 19:26): one who seeks omens. English has no second plain word beside קֹסֵם.
  - **"a witch" → "a sorcerer"** — מְכַשֵּׁף (Exod 7:11; 22:18); the form is masculine.
  - **"a charmer" → "one who binds spells"** — חֹבֵר חָבֶר, literally "one who joins a joining" (חָבַר "join"): one who ties by spells or knots; the exact sense is uncertain (Ps 58:5).
  - **"a consulter with familiar spirits, or a wizard" → "one who asks a ghost or a knowing spirit"** — שֹׁאֵל אוֹב וְיִדְּעֹנִי, literally "asks a ghost and a knowing spirit" (Lev 19:31; 20:6, 27). The words name the spirits, not the persons; the exact sense of both is uncertain. "Medium", "wizard", "familiar spirit" are other renderings.
  - **"a necromancer" → "one who seeks to the dead"** — דֹרֵשׁ אֶל־הַמֵּתִים (דָּרַשׁ "seek", as 17:4, 9): one who turns to the dead to inquire of them.
- **"all that do these things are an abomination" → "everyone who does these is an abomination"** (v12) — Hebrew כָּל־עֹשֵׂה אֵלֶּה, singular. **"doth drive them out" → "is dispossessing them"** — Hebrew מוֹרִישׁ, hifil of יָרַשׁ. In v14 "whom you are dispossessing" is the qal of the same verb with the nations as object (יוֹרֵשׁ אוֹתָם), literally "whom you are possessing": taking their place.
- **"Thou shalt be perfect with" → "Blameless you shall be with"** (v13) — Hebrew תָּמִים "whole, complete"; עִם "with".
- **"hearkened unto observers of times, and unto diviners" → "listen to those who practice soothsaying and to those who practice divination"** (v14) — Hebrew מְעֹנְנִים, קֹסְמִים; יִשְׁמָעוּ. **"hath not suffered thee so to do" → "not so has Yahweh your Elohim given to you"** — Hebrew לֹא כֵן נָתַן לְךָ: נָתַן is "give"; "to do" is not written. It may be heard as "has not permitted you so" or "has not given you such".
- **"will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken" → "A prophet from your midst, from your brothers, like me, Yahweh your Elohim will raise up for you; to him you shall listen"** (v15) — the Hebrew order is kept: "a prophet" stands first. "You" is singular until "you shall listen" (תִּשְׁמָעוּן), which is plural. שָׁמַע אֶל is "listen to". Cited in Acts 3:22; 7:37, where the Greek has "from your brothers" without "from your midst" and "him you shall hear".
- **"desiredst" → "asked"; "Let me not hear again … neither let me see … that I die not" → "I will not again hear … I will not see … and I will not die"** (v16) — Hebrew שָׁאַל "ask"; לֹא אֹסֵף לִשְׁמֹעַ, literally "I will not add to hear"; לֹא־אֶרְאֶה עוֹד; וְלֹא אָמוּת. The forms are statements with לֹא "not"; they are commonly taken as a request ("let me not hear … lest I die"; compare 5:25–27; Exod 20:19). **"on the day of the assembly"** — Hebrew בְּיוֹם הַקָּהָל (9:10; 10:4).
- **"They have well spoken that which they have spoken" → "They have done well in what they have spoken"** (v17) — Hebrew הֵיטִיבוּ אֲשֶׁר דִּבֵּרוּ (5:28).
- **"I will raise them up a Prophet from among their brethren, like unto thee" → "A prophet I will raise up for them from the midst of their brothers, like you"** (v18) — Hebrew מִקֶּרֶב אֲחֵיהֶם. **"put"** — Hebrew נָתַן "give".
- **"whosoever will not hearken" → "the man who does not listen"** (v19) — Hebrew הָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יִשְׁמַע אֶל. **"I will require it of him"** — Hebrew אָנֹכִי אֶדְרֹשׁ מֵעִמּוֹ, with the stressed "I"; "it" is supplied. Acts 3:23 gives the sentence in other words ("every soul that does not hear that prophet shall be utterly destroyed from the people"), which are not the Hebrew of this verse (compare Lev 23:29).
- **"which shall presume to speak" → "who acts presumptuously, to speak"** (v20) — Hebrew יָזִיד, as 17:13. **"or that shall speak" → "and who speaks"** — Hebrew וַאֲשֶׁר.
- **"if thou say" → "when you say"** (v21) — Hebrew וְכִי.
- **"When a prophet speaketh … if the thing follow not, nor come to pass" → "What the prophet speaks … and the word does not happen and does not come"** (v22) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יְדַבֵּר "what he speaks"; וְלֹא־יִהְיֶה הַדָּבָר וְלֹא יָבוֹא, literally "and the word is not and does not come". **"presumptuously" → "with presumption"** — Hebrew בְּזָדוֹן (17:12). **"thou shalt not be afraid of him"** — Hebrew גּוּר (Num 22:3), another verb than יָרֵא "fear".

### Notes
- **The priests' part** (v1–8) — the tribe of Levi has no share of land; Yahweh is its inheritance (Num 18:20–24). From a sacrifice the priest receives the foreleg, the jaws and the stomach; and the first of grain, new wine, fresh oil and wool.
- **The Levite who comes** (v6–8) — a Levite from any gate may come to the place that Yahweh will choose and minister there with a share like the others. Compare 2 Kgs 23:9.
- **The practices forbidden** (v10–11) — each is named by its own word. 1 Sam 28 tells of Saul and the woman at En-dor who was "mistress of a ghost".
- **"A prophet like me"** (v15–19) — the passage speaks of a prophet whom Yahweh will raise up from among the brothers, in whose mouth he puts his words. It is applied to Jesus in Acts 3:22–23; 7:37 (compare John 1:21; 6:14).
- **The word that does not come** (v21–22) — the mark given here for a word Yahweh has not spoken. 13:1–5 gives another: a sign that comes to pass, with the call to go after other gods.
