# Deuteronomy 27

[1] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ commanded the people, saying, "Keep all the commandment that I am commanding you today.

[2] And it shall be, on the day that you pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ to the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, that you shall raise up for yourself great stones and plaster them with plaster.

[3] And you shall write on them all the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ when you pass over, so that you may come into the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, a land flowing with milk and honey, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers, spoke to you.

[4] And it shall be, when you pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, that you shall raise up these stones, which I am commanding you today, on Mount ⟦ebal|Eival|Ebal⟧, and you shall plaster them with plaster.

[5] And you shall build there an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, an altar of stones; you shall not wave iron over them.

[6] Of whole stones you shall build the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall offer up on it burnt offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[7] And you shall sacrifice peace offerings and eat there, and you shall rejoice before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] And you shall write on the stones all the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ very plainly."

[9] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, spoke to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "Be silent and hear, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: this day you have become a people to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] And you shall listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and do his commandments and his statutes, which I am commanding you today."

[11] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded the people on that day, saying,

[12] "These shall stand to bless the people on Mount Gerizim when you pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧: ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧ and Levi and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦issachar|Yissachar|Issachar⟧ and ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧.

[13] And these shall stand for the curse on Mount ⟦ebal|Eival|Ebal⟧: ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧, Gad and Asher and ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧, Dan and ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧.

[14] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ shall answer and say to every man of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ with a raised voice:

[15] 'Cursed is the man who makes a carved image or a molten image, an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the work of the hands of a craftsman, and sets it up in secret.' And all the people shall answer and say, 'Amen.'

[16] 'Cursed is he who makes light of his father and his mother.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[17] 'Cursed is he who moves back the border of his neighbor.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[18] 'Cursed is he who makes a blind man err on the way.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[19] 'Cursed is he who turns aside the judgment of a sojourner, a fatherless one and a widow.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[20] 'Cursed is he who lies with the wife of his father, for he has uncovered the corner of his father.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[21] 'Cursed is he who lies with any beast.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[22] 'Cursed is he who lies with his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[23] 'Cursed is he who lies with his mother-in-law.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[24] 'Cursed is he who strikes his neighbor in secret.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[25] 'Cursed is he who takes a bribe to strike down a soul, innocent blood.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'

[26] 'Cursed is he who does not make the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ stand, to do them.' And all the people shall say, 'Amen.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept.
- **"Keep all the commandments" → "Keep all the commandment"** (v1) — Hebrew הַמִּצְוָה, singular. "Keep" is an infinitive used as a command; "you" is plural.
- **"when ye shall pass over Jordan … thou shalt set thee up great stones, and plaister them with plaister" → "on the day that you pass over the Jordan … you shall raise up for yourself great stones and plaster them with plaster"** (v2, 4) — Hebrew הֵקִים "make stand". וְשַׂדְתָּ … בַּשִּׂיד is verb and noun of one root; שִׂיד is lime. "You pass over" is plural in v2 and v4, the rest singular (v4 "you shall raise up" is plural, "you shall plaster" singular); so also v12.
- **"this law" → "this Torah"** (v3, 8, 26) — Hebrew הַתּוֹרָה הַזֹּאת.
- **"as the LORD God of thy fathers hath promised thee" → "as Yahweh, the Elohim of your fathers, spoke to you"** (v3) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר "spoke".
- **"in mount Ebal"** (v4) — so the Masoretic Text; the Samaritan text reads "Gerizim" here (from memory).
- **"thou shalt not lift up any iron tool upon them" → "you shall not wave iron over them"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־תָנִיף עֲלֵיהֶם בַּרְזֶל; הֵנִיף is "wave", the verb of Exod 20:25; "tool" is not written.
- **"offer burnt offerings" → "offer up … burnt offerings"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהַעֲלִיתָ … עוֹלֹת, verb and noun of one root, "make go up a going-up". "Whole" is שְׁלֵמוֹת, as 25:15.
- **"offer peace offerings" → "sacrifice peace offerings"** (v7) — Hebrew זָבַח.
- **"very plainly"** (v8) — Hebrew בַּאֵר הֵיטֵב, two infinitives: "making plain, doing well".
- **"the priests the Levites … Take heed, and hearken, O Israel; this day thou art become the people of the LORD thy God" → "the priests, the Levites … Be silent and hear, Israel: this day you have become a people to Yahweh your Elohim"** (v9) — Hebrew הַסְכֵּת, found only here; the meaning is uncertain ("be silent" or "pay attention"). שְׁמַע is "hear", as 6:4. לְעָם לַיהוָה "a people to Yahweh".
- **"obey the voice" → "listen to the voice"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל.
- **"charged" → "commanded"** (v11) — Hebrew צִוָּה, as v1.
- **"upon mount Ebal to curse" → "for the curse on Mount Ebal"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־הַקְּלָלָה "over, for the curse": a noun, קְלָלָה, from another root than the אָרוּר "cursed" of v15–26. The names stand with and without "and" as the Hebrew has them.
- **"say unto all the men of Israel with a loud voice" → "say to every man of Israel with a raised voice"** (v14) — Hebrew כָּל־אִישׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל, singular; קוֹל רָם "a high voice".
- **"Cursed be" → "Cursed is"** (v15–26) — Hebrew אָרוּר, a participle with no verb: twelve times in the same form. In v16–25 it is followed by a participle ("he who makes light", "he who lies"); in v15 by "the man who", in v26 by "who".
- **"any graven or molten image … and putteth it in a secret place" → "a carved image or a molten image … and sets it up in secret"** (v15) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל וּמַסֵּכָה, literally "and"; בַּסָּתֶר "in the hiding place" (so v24). "An abomination to Yahweh" is literally "the abomination of Yahweh".
- **"setteth light by" → "makes light of"** (v16) — Hebrew מַקְלֶה, of the root "be light" (25:3); the opposite of "honor" (Exod 20:12), which is "make heavy". The Hebrew has "his father and his mother".
- **"removeth his neighbour's landmark" → "moves back the border of his neighbor"** (v17) — Hebrew מַסִּיג גְּבוּל, as 19:14.
- **"maketh the blind to wander out of the way" → "makes a blind man err on the way"** (v18) — Hebrew מַשְׁגֶּה, the causative of שָׁגָה "err"; בַּדָּרֶךְ "on the way".
- **"perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow" → "turns aside the judgment of a sojourner, a fatherless one and a widow"** (v19) — Hebrew מַטֶּה מִשְׁפַּט, as Exod 23:6; גֵּר.
- **"because he uncovereth his father's skirt" → "for he has uncovered the corner of his father"** (v20) — Hebrew כְּנַף "wing", the corner of a garment, which a man spreads over his wife (Ruth 3:9); so 22:30. "Garment" is not written. A perfect.
- **"any manner of beast" → "any beast"** (v21) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה.
- **"mother in law"** (v23) — Hebrew חֹתֶנֶת, the wife's mother; found only here.
- **"smiteth his neighbour secretly" → "strikes his neighbor in secret"** (v24) — Hebrew מַכֵּה, the verb "strike", which is also used of striking dead (so v25).
- **"taketh reward to slay an innocent person" → "takes a bribe to strike down a soul, innocent blood"** (v25) — Hebrew לְהַכּוֹת נֶפֶשׁ דָּם נָקִי, word for word: "to strike a soul, innocent blood".
- **"Cursed be he that confirmeth not all the words of this law to do them" → "Cursed is he who does not make the words of this Torah stand, to do them"** (v26) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יָקִים אֶת־דִּבְרֵי הַתּוֹרָה־הַזֹּאת; הֵקִים is "make stand" (the verb of v2, 4). The Hebrew has no "all" and no "everyone". Gal 3:10 cites the verse in a Greek form: "Cursed is everyone who does not abide in all the things written in the book of the Torah, to do them."

### Notes

- **Gerizim and Ebal** (v4, 12–13) — the two mountains at Shechem (11:29–30). Josh 8:30–35 tells of the altar on Mount Ebal and the reading.
- **Iron** (v5) — so Exod 20:25.
- **Twelve times "cursed"** (v15–26) — v15 and v24 name what is done "in secret".
- **Singular and plural "you"** — both stand side by side in v1–4 and v12.
