# Deuteronomy 14

[1] "Sons you are to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. You shall not gash yourselves, and you shall not put a baldness between your eyes for the dead.

[2] For a holy people you are to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and it is you that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has chosen to be to him a people of treasured possession, out of all the peoples that are on the face of the ground.

[3] You shall not eat any abomination.

[4] These are the beasts that you may eat: the ox, the sheep of the lambs and the sheep of the goats,

[5] the deer and the gazelle and the roebuck, and the wild goat and the addax and the antelope and the mountain sheep.

[6] And every beast that has a hoof and cleaves a cleft of two hoofs, that brings up the cud, among the beasts — that you may eat.

[7] Yet this you shall not eat, of those that bring up the cud and of those that have a cleft hoof: the camel and the hare and the hyrax, for they bring up the cud, but a hoof they do not have; they are unclean to you.

[8] And the pig, for it has a hoof, but no cud; it is unclean to you. Of their flesh you shall not eat, and their carcass you shall not touch.

[9] This you may eat, of all that is in the waters: everything that has fins and scales you may eat.

[10] And everything that has no fins and scales you shall not eat; it is unclean to you.

[11] Every clean bird you may eat.

[12] And this is what you shall not eat of them: the eagle and the bearded vulture and the black vulture,

[13] and the red kite and the falcon, and the kite after its kind,

[14] and every raven after its kind,

[15] and the ostrich and the nighthawk and the sea gull, and the hawk after its kind,

[16] the little owl and the great owl and the white owl,

[17] and the desert owl and the Egyptian vulture and the cormorant,

[18] and the stork, and the heron after its kind, and the hoopoe and the bat.

[19] And every swarming thing that flies — it is unclean to you; they shall not be eaten.

[20] Every clean flying thing you may eat.

[21] You shall not eat any carcass; to the sojourner who is in your gates you may give it, and he may eat it, or sell it to a foreigner; for you are a holy people to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. You shall not boil a kid in its mother's milk.

[22] You shall surely give a tenth of all the yield of your seed that comes out of the field, year by year.

[23] And you shall eat before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in the place that he will choose to make his name abide there, the tithe of your grain, your new wine and your fresh oil, and the firstborn of your herd and of your flock, so that you may learn to fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ all the days.

[24] And if the way is too great for you, for you are not able to carry it, for the place is far from you that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose to put his name there, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will bless you,

[25] then you shall give it for money, and you shall bind up the money in your hand and go to the place that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will choose.

[26] And you shall give the money for all that your soul desires: for herd and for flock and for wine and for strong drink, and for all that your soul asks of you; and you shall eat there before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you shall rejoice, you and your house.

[27] And the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ who is in your gates — you shall not forsake him, for he has no share and inheritance with you.

[28] At the end of three years you shall bring out all the tithe of your yield in that year, and you shall lay it up in your gates.

[29] And the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ shall come, for he has no share and inheritance with you, and the sojourner and the fatherless and the widow who are in your gates, and they shall eat and be sated, so that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may bless you in all the work of your hand that you do.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"you"** (throughout) — the Hebrew speaks to Israel in the plural in v1 and v4–21a, and in the singular in v2–3 and v21b–29; English "you" cannot show it.
- **"Ye are the children of the LORD your God" → "Sons you are to Yahweh your Elohim"** (v1) — Hebrew בָּנִים אַתֶּם לַיהוָה אֱלֹהֵיכֶם; בָּנִים is "sons", and it stands first.
- **"Ye shall not cut yourselves" → "You shall not gash yourselves"** (v1) — Hebrew תִתְגֹּדְדוּ, the reflexive of גָּדַד "cut into" (1 Kgs 18:28). It is another word than the "cut" of Lev 19:28; 21:5 (root שׂרט).
- **"nor make any baldness" → "you shall not put a baldness"** (v1) — Hebrew תָשִׂימוּ קָרְחָה; קָרְחָה is a place shaved bare (Lev 21:5), "between your eyes" is the front of the head. "Any" is not in the Hebrew. **"for the dead"** — Hebrew לָמֵת, singular: "for a dead one".
- **"the LORD hath chosen thee to be a peculiar people unto himself, above all the nations" → "it is you that Yahweh has chosen to be to him a people of treasured possession, out of all the peoples"** (v2) — Hebrew וּבְךָ בָּחַר יְהוָה לִהְיוֹת לוֹ לְעַם סְגֻלָּה מִכֹּל הָעַמִּים. "You" stands first. סְגֻלָּה is a king's own private treasure (Exod 19:5); עַם סְגֻלָּה is "a people of" it. מִכֹּל is "out of all" or "more than all"; עַמִּים is "peoples". The sentence is that of Deut 7:6 and is worded as there: **"For thou art an holy people unto the LORD thy God" → "For a holy people you are to Yahweh your Elohim"**, Hebrew כִּי עַם קָדוֹשׁ אַתָּה, "a holy people" first (7:6 has no "and" and adds "your Elohim" in the second clause).
- **"upon the earth" → "on the face of the ground"** (v2) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"any abominable thing" → "any abomination"** (v3) — Hebrew תּוֹעֵבָה. Lev 11 calls the forbidden animals שֶׁקֶץ "a detestable thing", another word; this chapter does not use it and says "unclean" (v7, 8, 10, 19).
- **"These are the beasts which ye shall eat"** (v4) — the Hebrew is a collective singular, "This is the beast", as Lev 11:2; בְּהֵמָה is the four-footed animal.
- **"the ox, the sheep, and the goat" → "the ox, the sheep of the lambs and the sheep of the goats"** (v4) — Hebrew שׁוֹר שֵׂה כְשָׂבִים וְשֵׂה עִזִּים. שֶׂה is one animal of the flock, whether of the sheep or of the goats (Num 15:11); English has no word for it, and "sheep" stands for it as in Exod 12:5. כְּשָׂבִים "lambs" is the sheep kind. The Hebrew has no articles.
- **The beasts of v5** — seven names. Fairly sure are אַיָּל "deer" (KJV "hart") and צְבִי "gazelle" (KJV "roebuck"); יַחְמוּר "roebuck" (KJV "fallow deer") is some kind of deer (1 Kgs 4:23). The last four are found only here (תְּאוֹ also Isa 51:20) and their meaning is uncertain: אַקּוֹ "wild goat", דִּישֹׁן "addax" (KJV "pygarg"; a kind of antelope), תְּאוֹ "antelope" (KJV "wild ox"), זֶמֶר "mountain sheep" (KJV "chamois"). Lev 11 has no such list.
- **"parteth the hoof, and cleaveth the cleft into two claws" → "has a hoof and cleaves a cleft of two hoofs"** (v6) — Hebrew מַפְרֶסֶת פַּרְסָה וְשֹׁסַעַת שֶׁסַע שְׁתֵּי פְרָסוֹת, the words of Lev 11:3 with "And every beast" before them and "two" added: the cleft makes two hoofs of the one. "Has a hoof" is a verb made from the noun "hoof", literally "hoofs a hoof"; the traditional "divides the hoof" takes it from another verb (see Lev 11:3, 26). In Lev 11:3 the same words without "two" are rendered "cleaves a cleft in the hoofs"; "in two hoofs" would say that two of the hoofs are cleft, so "of" is kept here.
- **"cheweth the cud" → "brings up the cud"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew מַעֲלַת גֵּרָה, "making the cud come up", as Lev 11:3–6.
- **"them that divide the cloven hoof" → "those that have a cleft hoof"** (v7) — Hebrew מַפְרִיסֵי הַפַּרְסָה הַשְּׁסוּעָה; "cleft" is not in Lev 11:4. **"the camel, and the hare, and the coney" → "the camel and the hare and the hyrax"** — the three of Lev 11:4–6, here in one sentence and with the hare before the hyrax (שָׁפָן, Ps 104:18; Prov 30:26). **"divide not the hoof" → "a hoof they do not have"** — Hebrew וּפַרְסָה לֹא הִפְרִיסוּ.
- **"swine" → "pig"** (v8) — Hebrew חֲזִיר. **"yet cheweth not the cud" → "but no cud"** — the Hebrew has only וְלֹא גֵרָה, "and not cud", without a verb; Lev 11:7 has the full sentence ("and cleaves a cleft in the hoof, but it does not chew the cud"). **"their dead carcase" → "their carcass"** — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, singular, as Lev 11:8.
- **"These ye shall eat" → "This you may eat"** (v9) — Hebrew אֶת־זֶה, singular. Verses 9–10 are Lev 11:9–10 in short; where Lev 11:10–12 says "a detestable thing", v10 says "unclean".
- **"Of all clean birds ye shall eat" → "Every clean bird you may eat"** (v11) — Hebrew כָּל־צִפּוֹר טְהֹרָה; "of" is not in the Hebrew. **"all clean fowls" → "Every clean flying thing"** (v20) — Hebrew כָּל־עוֹף טָהוֹר: עוֹף is all that flies (Lev 11:13 "birds"), and here it follows the winged swarming things of v19; where it stands beside צִפּוֹר "bird" it is "flying thing", as Gen 7:14.
- **The birds of v12–18** — twenty-one names, rendered as in Lev 11:13–19; the identification of most of them is uncertain (see there; fairly sure are "eagle", "raven", "hawk", "stork", "bat"). Deuteronomy differs from Leviticus in these points, and is followed as it stands: in v13 it has three names where Lev 11:14 has two. רָאָה "red kite" (KJV "glede") is found only here; it stands where Lev 11:14 has דָּאָה "kite" and differs from it by one letter (ר for ד, which are much alike), and it may be the same bird: uncertain. דַּיָּה "kite" (KJV "vulture"; Isa 34:15) is taken as another form of דָּאָה. "After its kind" belongs to the last of the three, not to the falcon as in Lev 11:14. In v16–17 the order is another one (the cormorant, שָׁלָךְ, comes last), and the Egyptian vulture is written רָחָמָה (Lev 11:18 רָחָם). "And" stands before "every raven" (v14) and before "the heron" (v18), not before "the little owl" (v16).
- **"these are they of which ye shall not eat" → "this is what you shall not eat of them"** (v12) — Hebrew וְזֶה אֲשֶׁר לֹא־תֹאכְלוּ מֵהֶם.
- **"every creeping thing that flieth" → "every swarming thing that flies"** (v19) — Hebrew שֶׁרֶץ הָעוֹף, literally "swarming thing of the flying things": the winged insects, as Lev 11:20. "It is unclean" is singular and "they shall not be eaten" plural in the Hebrew. Lev 11:20–23 says "a detestable thing", adds "that goes on four", and allows the locusts; this chapter does not name them.
- **"any thing that dieth of itself" → "any carcass"** (v21) — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, the body of an animal that has died (v8). **"the stranger that is in thy gates" → "the sojourner who is in your gates"** — Hebrew גֵּר, the one from outside who lives among Israel; **"an alien" → "a foreigner"** — Hebrew נָכְרִי. They are two words. **"that he may eat it" → "and he may eat it"** — Hebrew וַאֲכָלָהּ. **"or thou mayest sell it" → "or sell it"** — Hebrew אוֹ מָכֹר, the bare verb form ("or a selling to a foreigner"); "it" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"seethe a kid in his mother's milk" → "boil a kid in its mother's milk"** (v21) — Hebrew לֹא־תְבַשֵּׁל גְּדִי בַּחֲלֵב אִמּוֹ, word for word as Exod 23:19; 34:26.
- **"Thou shalt truly tithe all the increase of thy seed" → "You shall surely give a tenth of all the yield of your seed"** (v22) — Hebrew עַשֵּׂר תְּעַשֵּׂר, literally "giving a tenth you shall give a tenth"; the verb of מַעֲשֵׂר "tithe" (v23, 28), which is literally "tenth".
- **"that the field bringeth forth" → "that comes out of the field"** (v22) — Hebrew הַיֹּצֵא הַשָּׂדֶה, literally "that goes out, the field", with no preposition. The form agrees with "seed", so "your seed that goes out to the field" (the seed that is sown) is also possible. **"year by year"** — Hebrew שָׁנָה שָׁנָה, "year, year".
- **"to place his name there" → "to make his name abide there"** (v23) — Hebrew לְשַׁכֵּן שְׁמוֹ שָׁם, the causative of שָׁכַן "abide"; in v24 it is לָשׂוּם "to put". **"of thy wine, and of thine oil" → "your new wine and your fresh oil"** — Hebrew תִּירֹשׁ and יִצְהָר, not the common words for wine (v26) and oil. **"always" → "all the days"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים.
- **"if the way be too long for thee" → "if the way is too great for you"** (v24) — Hebrew יִרְבֶּה מִמְּךָ, literally "is more than you". The verse has כִּי four times: the first is "if / when"; the others are rendered "for", and may each also be "when" ("when the place is far from you … when Yahweh your Elohim blesses you"). **"be too far from thee" → "is far from you"** — Hebrew יִרְחַק מִמְּךָ, as Deut 12:21.
- **"turn it into money" → "give it for money"** (v25) — Hebrew וְנָתַתָּה בַּכָּסֶף, "you shall give for the silver"; "it" is not in the Hebrew. כֶּסֶף is "silver": weighed silver, which is bound up in the hand. **"bestow that money" → "give the money"** (v26) — the same verb.
- **"whatsoever thy soul lusteth after" → "all that your soul desires"** (v26) — Hebrew תְּאַוֶּה נַפְשְׁךָ; the verb belongs to תַּאֲוָה "craving" (Num 11:4) and is here said without blame. **"for oxen, or for sheep" → "for herd and for flock"** — Hebrew בָּקָר and צֹאן. **"strong drink"** — Hebrew שֵׁכָר, a drink that makes drunk, other than wine. **"whatsoever thy soul desireth" → "all that your soul asks of you"** — Hebrew תִּשְׁאָלְךָ נַפְשֶׁךָ, another verb. **"thine household" → "your house"** — Hebrew בֵּיתֶךָ.
- **"no part nor inheritance" → "no share and inheritance"** (v27, 29) — Hebrew חֵלֶק וְנַחֲלָה, as Deut 12:12.
- **"At the end of three years"** (v28) — Hebrew מִקְצֵה שָׁלֹשׁ שָׁנִים; "every" is not in the Hebrew. **"shalt lay it up"** — Hebrew וְהִנַּחְתָּ; "it" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the stranger" → "the sojourner"** (v29) — Hebrew גֵּר, as v21. **"be satisfied" → "be sated"** — Hebrew שָׂבֵעוּ. **"the work of thine hand"** — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה יָדְךָ, singular.

### Notes
- **Parallel to Lev 11** — Deut 14:3–21 and Lev 11 largely overlap on the dietary laws, with differences: Deut names the clean beasts (v4–5); Lev has more detail on swarming things and on the uncleanness of a carcass. Lev speaks of "a detestable thing", Deut of "abomination" (v3) and "unclean".
- **"Sons you are to Yahweh"** (v1) — familial-election framing. The refusal of these mourning practices is about identity: Israel belongs to Yahweh.
- **Kid in mother's milk** (v21) — three times in the Torah (Exod 23:19; 34:26; here). Basis for the Jewish *kashrut* separation of meat and dairy; perhaps originally aimed against a specific Canaanite ritual.
- **Sojourner and foreigner** (v21) — the sojourner (*ger*) lives in Israel's towns and may be given the carcass; the foreigner (*nokhri*) is the outsider, to whom it may be sold.
- **Tithe for the Levite, the sojourner, the fatherless and the widow** (v28–29) — the tithe laid up in the towns at the end of three years is for those without land of their own (Deut 26:12 calls the third year "the year of the tithe").
- **Distance provision** (v24–26) — if the place is too far to carry the tithe in kind, it is given for silver, and the silver is spent on the meal at the place Yahweh will choose.
- **Strong drink at the meal** (v26) — wine and *shekhar*. The meal before Yahweh is one of rejoicing.
