# Deuteronomy 19

[1] "When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ cuts off the nations whose land ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you, and you dispossess them and dwell in their cities and in their houses,

[2] three cities you shall separate for yourself in the midst of your land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you to possess it.

[3] You shall prepare the way for yourself, and you shall divide in three the territory of your land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ makes you inherit; and it shall be for every murderer to flee there.

[4] And this is the matter of the murderer who flees there and lives: he who strikes his neighbor without knowledge, and he was not hating him yesterday and the day before;

[5] and he who goes with his neighbor into the forest to hew wood, and his hand is thrust with the axe to cut the tree, and the iron slips from the wood and finds his neighbor, and he dies: he shall flee to one of these cities and live,

[6] lest the redeemer of blood pursue after the murderer, because his heart is hot, and overtake him, because the way is long, and strike down his soul, and for him there is no judgment of death, for he was not hating him yesterday and the day before.

[7] Therefore I am commanding you, saying, 'Three cities you shall separate for yourself.'

[8] And if ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ enlarges your border, as he swore to your fathers, and gives to you all the land that he spoke to give to your fathers,

[9] when you keep all this commandment, to do it, which I am commanding you today, to love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to walk in his ways all the days, then you shall add for yourself three cities more to these three,

[10] that innocent blood be not shed in the midst of your land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you as an inheritance, and bloodguilt be upon you.

[11] And when there is a man hating his neighbor, and he lies in ambush for him and rises against him and strikes down his soul, and he dies, and he flees to one of these cities,

[12] then the elders of his city shall send and take him from there and give him into the hand of the redeemer of blood, and he shall die.

[13] Your eye shall not pity him, and you shall purge the blood of the innocent from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and it shall be good for you.

[14] You shall not move back the border of your neighbor, which the first ones set as a border, in your inheritance that you shall inherit in the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you to possess it.

[15] One witness shall not rise against a man for any iniquity and for any sin, in any sin that he sins; at the mouth of two witnesses or at the mouth of three witnesses a word shall stand.

[16] When a witness of violence rises against a man, to answer defection against him,

[17] then the two men who have the contention shall stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, before the priests and the judges who shall be in those days,

[18] and the judges shall seek well; and behold, the witness is a false witness, falsehood he has answered against his brother,

[19] then you shall do to him as he devised to do to his brother; and you shall purge the evil from your midst.

[20] And those who are left shall hear and fear, and they shall no more again do anything like this evil thing in your midst.

[21] And your eye shall not pity: soul for soul, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"hath cut off" → "cuts off"; "thou succeedest them" → "you dispossess them"** (v1) — Hebrew יַכְרִית; וִירִשְׁתָּם, the qal of יָרַשׁ with persons as object: take their place as owner. **"is giving to you"** (v1, 2, 10, 14) — Hebrew נֹתֵן לְךָ, a participle.
- **"separate" kept** (v2, 7) — Hebrew הִבְדִּיל (Gen 1:4), as 4:41. The word "refuge" (מִקְלָט, Num 35:6–32) is not used in this chapter. **"to possess it"** — Hebrew לְרִשְׁתָּהּ.
- **"divide the coasts of thy land … into three parts" → "divide in three the territory of your land"** (v3) — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּשְׁתָּ, a verb made from "three"; גְּבוּל is "border" (v8, 14), here the land within it. **"giveth thee to inherit" → "makes you inherit"** — Hebrew יַנְחִילְךָ. **"that every slayer may flee thither" → "it shall be for every murderer to flee there"** — Hebrew וְהָיָה לָנוּס שָׁמָּה כָּל־רֹצֵחַ.
- **"slayer" → "murderer"** (v3, 4, 6) — Hebrew רֹצֵחַ, the word of Exod 20:13 and of Num 35: the one word is used also of the man who killed without knowledge; the name goes with the deed, not with the guilt. **"the case of the slayer" → "the matter of the murderer"** (v4) — Hebrew דְּבַר הָרֹצֵחַ, literally "the word of the murderer" (15:2). **"that he may live" → "and lives"** — Hebrew וָחָי.
- **"Whoso killeth his neighbour ignorantly" → "he who strikes his neighbor without knowledge"** (v4) — Hebrew יַכֶּה "strikes"; בִּבְלִי־דַעַת, literally "in the lack of knowledge" (4:42; Josh 20:3). Num 35:11, 15 has another expression, בִּשְׁגָגָה "in error"; the two are kept apart. **"whom he hated not in time past" → "he was not hating him yesterday and the day before"** (v4, 6) — Hebrew מִתְּמֹל שִׁלְשֹׁם, literally "from yesterday, the third day" = before now.
- **"his hand fetcheth a stroke with the axe" → "his hand is thrust with the axe"** (v5) — Hebrew וְנִדְּחָה יָדוֹ, nifal of נדח "thrust, drive": the hand swings out. **"to hew wood" / "to cut down the tree"** — Hebrew חָטַב עֵצִים, כָּרַת הָעֵץ; עֵץ is "tree" and "wood". **"the head slippeth from the helve" → "the iron slips from the wood"** — Hebrew וְנָשַׁל הַבַּרְזֶל מִן־הָעֵץ: "the wood" is either the handle (the iron flies off it) or the tree (the iron glances off it); the Hebrew leaves it open. **"lighteth upon" → "finds"** — Hebrew מָצָא.
- **"the avenger of the blood" → "the redeemer of blood"** (v6, 12) — Hebrew גֹּאֵל הַדָּם, as Num 35:19–27: גֹּאֵל is "redeemer", the kinsman on whom the duty falls (Lev 25:25); "avenger" names what he does here. **"while his heart is hot" → "because his heart is hot"** — Hebrew כִּי־יֵחַם לְבָבוֹ; כִּי is "for, because" or "when", both times in the verse. **"slay him" → "strike down his soul"** (v6, 11) — Hebrew וְהִכָּהוּ נָפֶשׁ, literally "strike him, soul" (Gen 37:21; Num 35:11 "one who strikes down a soul"). **"whereas he was not worthy of death" → "and for him there is no judgment of death"** — Hebrew וְלוֹ אֵין מִשְׁפַּט־מָוֶת.
- **"coast" → "border"; "which he promised to give" → "that he spoke to give"** (v8) — Hebrew גְּבוּל (Exod 34:24); דִּבֶּר.
- **"If thou shalt keep all these commandments to do them" → "when you keep all this commandment, to do it"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי־תִשְׁמֹר אֶת־כָּל־הַמִּצְוָה הַזֹּאת לַעֲשֹׂתָהּ: "commandment" is singular, the whole charge as one. **"to walk ever in his ways" → "to walk in his ways all the days"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים. **"three cities more for thee, beside these three" → "three cities more to these three"** — Hebrew עַל "upon, in addition to". These would be three beyond the three of v2 and the three across the Jordan (4:41–43).
- **"That innocent blood be not shed" kept; "and so blood be upon thee" → "and bloodguilt be upon you"** (v10) — Hebrew וְלֹא יִשָּׁפֵךְ, literally "and innocent blood shall not be shed"; דָּמִים, literally "bloods" (plural): guilt for a death. Both clauses stand under the one "not".
- **"lie in wait" → "lies in ambush"** (v11) — Hebrew אָרַב; "lie in wait" is kept for צָדָה (Exod 21:13; Num 35:20). **"smite him mortally that he die" → "strikes down his soul, and he dies"** — as v6.
- **"deliver him" → "give him"; "that he may die" → "and he shall die"** (v12) — Hebrew וְנָתְנוּ; וָמֵת.
- **"thou shalt put away the guilt of innocent blood" → "you shall purge the blood of the innocent"** (v13) — Hebrew וּבִעַרְתָּ דַם־הַנָּקִי: בִּעֵר is literally "burn away, clear away" (v19; 13:5); "the guilt of" is not written; הַנָּקִי has the article, "the innocent one" (or "the innocent blood", taken as an adjective). **"that it may go well with thee" → "and it shall be good for you"** — Hebrew וְטוֹב לָךְ.
- **"Thou shalt not remove thy neighbour's landmark, which they of old time have set" → "You shall not move back the border of your neighbor, which the first ones set as a border"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹא תַסִּיג גְּבוּל, literally "you shall not make the border go back"; גָּבְלוּ is the verb of גְּבוּל; רִאשֹׁנִים "first ones, former ones". "Landmark" (a stone) is not a separate word.
- **"One witness shall not rise up against a man" kept; "that he sinneth" → "that he sins"; "shall the matter be established" → "a word shall stand"** (v15) — Hebrew לֹא־יָקוּם עֵד אֶחָד … יָקוּם דָּבָר: the same verb קוּם opens and closes the verse ("rise" / "stand"); דָּבָר is "word" and "matter". עַל־פִּי "at the mouth of" is written twice. Cited in Matt 18:16; 2 Cor 13:1, where the Greek has "every word" (πᾶν ῥῆμα); "every" is not in the Hebrew. The three nouns are עָוֺן "iniquity", חַטָּאת "sin" and חֵטְא "sin" (two forms of one root).
- **"a false witness" → "a witness of violence"** (v16) — Hebrew עֵד־חָמָס (Exod 23:1): a witness who does violence, a malicious one; "false witness" (עֵד־שֶׁקֶר) comes in v18. **"to testify against him that which is wrong" → "to answer defection against him"** — Hebrew לַעֲנוֹת בּוֹ סָרָה: עָנָה בְּ "answer against" is the phrase of Exod 20:16; סָרָה is "a turning aside, defection" (13:5), from סוּר. It may be what he charges the man with (defection from Yahweh, or from the right), or what his own testimony is (a turning from the truth).
- **"between whom the controversy is" → "who have the contention"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־לָהֶם הָרִיב: the case at law. **"before the LORD, before the priests and the judges"** — as 17:9.
- **"make diligent inquisition" → "seek well"** (v18) — Hebrew וְדָרְשׁוּ … הֵיטֵב (13:14; 17:4). **"if the witness be a false witness, and hath testified falsely" → "the witness is a false witness, falsehood he has answered"** — Hebrew עֵד־שֶׁקֶר הָעֵד שֶׁקֶר עָנָה בְאָחִיו, literally "a witness of falsehood is the witness; falsehood he has answered against his brother"; "if" is not written.
- **"as he had thought to have done" → "as he devised to do"** (v19) — Hebrew זָמַם "devise, purpose". "You shall do" is plural (וַעֲשִׂיתֶם); "you shall purge" is singular. **"put the evil away" → "purge the evil"** — as 13:5; 17:7.
- **"those which remain" → "those who are left"; "shall henceforth commit no more any such evil" → "shall no more again do anything like this evil thing"** (v20) — Hebrew הַנִּשְׁאָרִים; וְלֹא־יֹסִפוּ לַעֲשׂוֹת עוֹד כַּדָּבָר הָרָע הַזֶּה, literally "they shall not add to do again according to this evil word / thing" (13:11).
- **"life shall go for life" → "soul for soul"** (v21) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ בְּנֶפֶשׁ, and so each pair with בְּ "for, at the price of". Exod 21:23–25 and Lev 24:18, 20 have the same pairs with תַּחַת "in the place of"; here the list stops at "foot for foot". **"thine eye shall not pity"** — Hebrew חוּס (v13; 7:16). Cited in Matt 5:38.

### Notes
- **Three cities** (v1–10) — in the land west of the Jordan; Josh 20:7 names Kedesh, Shechem and Hebron. With the three of 4:41–43 they are the six of Num 35:13–14. The chapter does not use the name "cities of refuge".
- **Three more** (v8–9) — to be added if the border is enlarged; no account of this being done is given in the Bible.
- **The redeemer of blood** (v6, 12) — the kinsman who pursues the one who killed; the cities stand between him and the man who killed without knowledge, while the man who killed in hatred is taken from the city and given into his hand.
- **The border** (v14) — so also 27:17; Prov 22:28; 23:10.
- **Two or three witnesses** (v15) — so also 17:6; Num 35:30. Taken up in Matt 18:16; 2 Cor 13:1; 1 Tim 5:19.
- **The false witness** (v16–19) — what he devised to do to his brother is done to him.
- **"Soul for soul"** (v21) — the measure of Exod 21:23–25; Lev 24:17–21, here applied to the false witness. In Matt 5:38–39 Jesus cites "eye for eye and tooth for tooth".
