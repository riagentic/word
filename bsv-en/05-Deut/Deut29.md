# Deuteronomy 29

[1] These are the words of the covenant that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ to cut with the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, besides the covenant that he cut with them at ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧.

[2] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ called to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said to them, "You, you have seen all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did before your eyes in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and to all his servants and to all his land,

[3] the great testings that your eyes saw, the signs and those great wonders.

[4] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not given you a heart to know and eyes to see and ears to hear, to this day.

[5] And I made you walk forty years in the wilderness; your cloaks did not wear out from upon you, and your sandal did not wear out from upon your foot.

[6] Bread you did not eat, and wine and strong drink you did not drink, so that you might know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[7] And you came to this place, and ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ and Og king of Bashan came out to meet us for war, and we struck them down.

[8] And we took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenite⟧ and to the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadite⟧ and to the half tribe of the ⟦manassites|Menashi|Manassite⟧.

[9] And you shall keep the words of this covenant and do them, so that you may act with insight in all that you do.

[10] You are standing today, all of you, before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: your heads, your tribes, your elders and your officers, every man of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[11] your little ones, your wives, and your sojourner who is in the midst of your camp, from the hewer of your wood to the drawer of your water,

[12] for you to pass over into the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and into his curse, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is cutting with you today,

[13] in order to establish you today for himself as a people, and he, he will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you, as he spoke to you and as he swore to your fathers, to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, to ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[14] And not with you alone am I cutting this covenant and this curse,

[15] but with him who is here with us, standing today before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and with him who is not here with us today.

[16] For you, you know how we dwelt in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and how we passed through the midst of the nations that you passed through;

[17] and you saw their detestable things and their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, that were with them.

[18] Lest there be among you a man or a woman or a clan or a tribe whose heart is turning today from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to go to serve the gods of those nations; lest there be among you a root bearing poison and wormwood;

[19] and it shall be, when he hears the words of this curse, that he blesses himself in his heart, saying, '⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧ shall be mine, though in the stubbornness of my heart I walk', so as to sweep away the watered with the thirsty.

[20] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will not be willing to pardon him, for then the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and his jealousy will smoke against that man, and all the curse that is written in this book shall lie upon him, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will blot out his name from under the heavens.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will separate him for evil from all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, according to all the curses of the covenant that is written in this book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧.

[22] And the later generation shall say, your sons who rise up after you, and the foreigner who comes from a far land, and they shall see the blows of that land and its sicknesses with which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made it sick:

[23] sulfur and salt, a burning, all its land; it shall not be sown, and it shall make nothing spring up, and no plant shall come up in it; like the overthrow of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧, Admah and ⟦zeboiim|Tzevoyim|Zeboiim⟧, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ overthrew in his anger and in his fury —

[24] and all the nations shall say, 'For what has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ done thus to this land? What is the burning of this great anger?'

[25] And they shall say, 'Because they forsook the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers, which he cut with them when he brought them out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[26] and they went and served other gods and bowed down to them, gods that they had not known and that he had not apportioned to them.

[27] And the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned against that land, to bring upon it all the curse that is written in this book.

[28] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ plucked them up from upon their ground in anger and in fury and in great wrath, and he threw them into another land, as at this day.'

[29] The concealed things belong to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the revealed things belong to us and to our sons forever, to do all the words of this ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept.
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew, v1 is 28:69 and v2–29 are 29:1–28. Whether v1 closes the words of chapters 5–28 or opens what follows is not said by the text.
- **"to make with the children of Israel … which he made with them" → "to cut with the sons of Israel … that he cut with them"** (v1, 12, 14, 25) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant"; בְּנֵי "sons of".
- **"Ye have seen" → "You, you have seen"** (v2, 16) — the pronoun is written and stressed. **"called unto"** — Hebrew קָרָא אֶל.
- **"The great temptations which thine eyes have seen, the signs, and those great miracles" → "the great testings that your eyes saw, the signs and those great wonders"** (v3) — Hebrew מַסּוֹת, as 7:19; מֹפְתִים. "Your eyes" is singular; so "your sandal … your foot" (v5) and v11–13; the rest of v2–18 is plural.
- **"Yet the LORD hath not given you an heart to perceive" → "And Yahweh has not given you a heart to know"** (v4) — Hebrew וְלֹא "and not"; לָדַעַת "to know". Rom 11:8 joins words of this verse with Isa 29:10.
- **"I have led you … your clothes are not waxen old upon you, and thy shoe is not waxen old upon thy foot" → "And I made you walk … your cloaks did not wear out from upon you, and your sandal did not wear out from upon your foot"** (v5) — Hebrew וָאוֹלֵךְ, the causative of "walk", as 8:2; שַׂלְמָה (= שִׂמְלָה "cloak"); מֵעַל "from upon". The speaker passes into the first person of Yahweh (v5–6).
- **"came out against us unto battle, and we smote them" → "came out to meet us for war, and we struck them down"** (v7) — Hebrew לִקְרָאתֵנוּ לַמִּלְחָמָה; הִכָּה "strike". "Bashan" has the article in the Hebrew.
- **"unto the Reubenites, and to the Gadites, and to the half tribe of Manasseh" → "to the Reubenite and to the Gadite and to the half tribe of the Manassite"** (v8) — Hebrew singulars with the article, as 3:12.
- **"that ye may prosper in all that ye do" → "so that you may act with insight in all that you do"** (v9) — Hebrew הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight, act with insight"; "prosper" is what follows from it (Josh 1:7, 8).
- **"your captains of your tribes, your elders, and your officers, with all the men of Israel" → "your heads, your tribes, your elders and your officers, every man of Israel"** (v10) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁיכֶם שִׁבְטֵיכֶם, two nouns side by side; "of" is not written (the Septuagint has "the heads of your tribes", from memory).
- **"thy stranger that is in thy camp" → "your sojourner who is in the midst of your camp"** (v11) — Hebrew גֵּר; בְּקֶרֶב. מַחֲנֶיךָ may also be read as a plural, "your camps".
- **"That thou shouldest enter into covenant with the LORD thy God, and into his oath, which the LORD thy God maketh with thee" → "for you to pass over into the covenant of Yahweh your Elohim and into his curse, which Yahweh your Elohim is cutting with you"** (v12) — Hebrew עָבַר בְּ "pass over into", found with "covenant" only here. **"oath" / "curse" → "curse"** (v12, 14, 19, 20, 21) — Hebrew אָלָה in all five verses: an oath that calls down a curse on the one who breaks it, and the curse itself (Num 5:21). KJV changes from "oath" (v12, 14) to "curse" (v19–21); one word is kept, because v19 "the words of this curse" looks back to v12 and v14. "The curse" of v27 is another word, קְלָלָה.
- **"That he may establish thee to day for a people unto himself, and that he may be unto thee a God" → "in order to establish you today for himself as a people, and he, he will be Elohim to you"** (v13) — Hebrew הָקִים, as 28:9; וְהוּא יִהְיֶה, a new clause with the pronoun.
- **"with him that standeth here with us this day … with him that is not here" → "with him who is here with us, standing today … with him who is not here"** (v15) — singular in the Hebrew; יֶשְׁנוֹ "he is", אֵינֶנּוּ "he is not".
- **"how we came through the nations which ye passed by" → "how we passed through the midst of the nations that you passed through"** (v16) — one verb, עָבַר.
- **"their abominations, and their idols … which were among them" → "their detestable things and their idols … that were with them"** (v17) — Hebrew שִׁקּוּצִים; גִּלּוּלִים, a word of contempt (Lev 26:30); עִמָּהֶם.
- **"Lest there should be among you man, or woman, or family, or tribe … a root that beareth gall and wormwood" → "Lest there be among you a man or a woman or a clan or a tribe … a root bearing poison and wormwood"** (v18) — Hebrew פֶּן "lest", twice, with no main clause before it; "beware" is not written. מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan". רֹאשׁ is a poisonous plant, which one is not known; לַעֲנָה is wormwood. Heb 12:15 takes up the words in their Greek form.
- **"that he bless himself in his heart, saying, I shall have peace, though I walk in the imagination of mine heart, to add drunkenness to thirst" → "that he blesses himself in his heart, saying, 'Shalom shall be mine, though in the stubbornness of my heart I walk', so as to sweep away the watered with the thirsty"** (v19) — Hebrew הִתְבָּרֵךְ "bless oneself"; שָׁלוֹם יִהְיֶה־לִּי; כִּי, rendered "though", may also be "for" or "when"; שְׁרִרוּת "firmness, stubbornness". The last clause, לְמַעַן סְפוֹת הָרָוָה אֶת־הַצְּמֵאָה, is word for word "so as to sweep away the watered [and / with] the thirsty": two feminine adjectives with no noun (land, plant or soul). Its meaning is uncertain: the watered and the dry swept away together; or, with סְפוֹת taken from "add", "to add the watered to the thirsty" (KJV "drunkenness to thirst"). Who says it, the man or the speaker, is also open.
- **"The LORD will not spare him, but then … all the curses that are written in this book shall lie upon him" → "Yahweh will not be willing to pardon him, for then … all the curse that is written in this book shall lie upon him"** (v20) — Hebrew לֹא־יֹאבֶה … סְלֹחַ "will not be willing to pardon"; כִּי "for"; הָאָלָה singular; רָבַץ "lie", of an animal lying down (Gen 4:7). "Anger" is אַף, literally "nose".
- **"shall separate him unto evil … according to all the curses of the covenant that are written in this book of the law" → "will separate him for evil … according to all the curses of the covenant that is written in this book of the Torah"** (v21) — "written" is singular and belongs to "the covenant"; "this" belongs to "book" (28:61).
- **"the generation to come of your children that shall rise up after you, and the stranger … the plagues of that land, and the sicknesses which the LORD hath laid upon it" → "the later generation … your sons who rise up after you, and the foreigner … the blows of that land and its sicknesses with which Yahweh has made it sick"** (v22) — Hebrew הַדּוֹר הָאַחֲרוֹן; נָכְרִי; מַכּוֹת, as 28:59; תַּחֲלֻאִים and חִלָּה are one root. What they say comes in v24, after "and all the nations shall say"; the long sentence is left as the Hebrew has it.
- **"brimstone, and salt, and burning … nor beareth, nor any grass groweth therein … in his anger, and in his wrath" → "sulfur and salt, a burning, all its land … it shall make nothing spring up, and no plant shall come up in it … in his anger and in his fury"** (v23) — Hebrew גָּפְרִית (Gen 19:24); עֵשֶׂב; מַהְפֵּכָה and הָפַךְ "overthrow" (Gen 19:25); חֵמָה. "Zeboiim" follows the reading tradition (qere); the written form differs only in spelling.
- **"Wherefore hath the LORD done thus … what meaneth the heat of this great anger?" → "For what has Yahweh done thus … What is the burning of this great anger?"** (v24) — Hebrew עַל־מֶה; מֶה חֳרִי הָאַף; "meaneth" is not written.
- **"Because they have forsaken"** (v25) — Hebrew עָזַב. "They shall say" has no subject in the Hebrew.
- **"whom he had not given unto them" → "that he had not apportioned to them"** (v26) — Hebrew חָלַק, as 4:19.
- **"was kindled against this land, to bring upon it all the curses" → "burned against that land, to bring upon it all the curse"** (v27) — Hebrew הַהִוא "that"; הַקְּלָלָה singular.
- **"rooted them out of their land in anger, and in wrath, and in great indignation, and cast them into another land, as it is this day" → "plucked them up from upon their ground in anger and in fury and in great wrath, and he threw them into another land, as at this day"** (v28) — Hebrew נָתַשׁ; אַדְמָתָם; אַף, חֵמָה, קֶצֶף, three words; הִשְׁלִיךְ.
- **"The secret things belong unto the LORD our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do" → "The concealed things belong to Yahweh our Elohim, and the revealed things belong to us and to our sons forever, to do"** (v29) — Hebrew הַנִּסְתָּרֹת לַיהוָה אֱלֹהֵינוּ וְהַנִּגְלֹת לָנוּ וּלְבָנֵינוּ עַד־עוֹלָם: two participles, "the concealed" and "the revealed", each with לְ "to, for"; "belong" renders that לְ, and there is no verb; "and", not "but". "To do" is an infinitive without a subject. In the manuscripts the words "to us and to our sons" carry dots above the letters; what the dots mean is not known.

### Notes

- **The covenant in the land of Moab** (v1) — beside the covenant at Horeb (5:2).
- **All who stand** (v10–15) — heads, tribes, elders, officers, every man, little ones, wives, the sojourner; and "him who is not here with us today".
- **Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim** (v23) — Gen 10:19; 14:2; 19:24–25; Hos 11:8.
- **"As at this day"** (v28) — the words of the nations' answer, spoken from the time when the land lies waste.
