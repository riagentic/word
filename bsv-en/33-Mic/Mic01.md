# Micah 1

[1] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that came to ⟦micah-prophet|Michah|Micah⟧ of ⟦moresheth|Moreshet|Moresheth⟧ in the days of ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧, ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧, ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, which he saw concerning ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] Hear, peoples, all of them;
give heed, earth, and its fullness;
and let ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ be against you for a witness,
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ from his holy temple.

[3] For behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is coming out from his place;
and he will come down and tread on the high places of the earth.

[4] And the mountains shall melt under him,
and the valleys shall split themselves,
like wax before the fire,
like waters poured down a slope.

[5] For the transgression of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ is all this,
and for the sins of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.
Who is the transgression of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧? Is it not ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧?
And who are the high places of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧? Is it not ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?

[6] And I will make ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ a heap of the field,
a planting place of a vineyard;
and I will pour down her stones into the valley,
and her foundations I will uncover.

[7] And all her carved images shall be beaten to pieces,
and all her hires shall be burned with fire,
and all her idols I will make a desolation;
for from the hire of a prostitute she gathered them,
and to the hire of a prostitute they shall return.

[8] For this let me lament and wail;
let me go stripped and naked;
let me make a lamenting like the jackals,
and mourning like the daughters of the ostrich.

[9] For her wounds are incurable;
for it has come to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧;
it has reached to the gate of my people,
to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] In ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ do not tell it;
do not weep at all;
in ⟦beth-le-aphrah|Beit LeAfrah|Beth-le-aphrah⟧
roll yourself in the dust.

[11] Pass on your way, dweller of ⟦shaphir|Shafir|Shaphir⟧, in nakedness, shame;
the dweller of ⟦zaanan|Tzaanan|Zaanan⟧ has not come out;
the lamenting of ⟦beth-ezel|Beit HaEtzel|Beth-ezel⟧ —
it shall take from you its standing place.

[12] For the dweller of ⟦maroth|Marot|Maroth⟧ is in anguish for good,
for evil has come down from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
to the gate of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] Bind the chariot to the steeds,
dweller of Lachish;
she was the beginning of sin
to the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
for in you were found
the transgressions of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[14] Therefore you shall give parting gifts
to ⟦moresheth-gath|Moreshet Gat|Moresheth-gath⟧;
the houses of ⟦achzib|Achziv|Achzib⟧
shall be a deceitful stream
to the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] Yet the possessor I will bring to you,
dweller of Mareshah;
to Adullam shall come
the glory of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[16] Make yourself bald and shave yourself
for the sons of your delights;
make your baldness wide like the eagle,
for they have gone into exile from you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton; אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה "Lord Yahweh" and אֲדֹנָי "Adonai" (v2), each as printed.
- **Layout** — v2–16 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents (v11 relaid: four cola); v1 the prose heading. No "O" before "earth" (v2): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"Micah the Morasthite … Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah" → "Micah of Moresheth … Jotham, Ahaz, Hezekiah"** (v1) — הַמֹּרַשְׁתִּי "the man of Moresheth" (the gentilic); the three names stand without "and".
- **"Hear, all ye people; hearken, O earth, and all that therein is: and let the Lord GOD be witness against you" → "Hear, peoples, all of them; give heed, earth, and its fullness; and let Lord Yahweh be against you for a witness"** (v2) — עַמִּים כֻּלָּם "peoples, all of them" (third person, as 1 Kgs 22:28); הַקְשִׁיבִי "give heed"; מְלֹאָהּ "its fullness"; וִיהִי jussive "let … be"; בָּכֶם לְעֵד "against you for a witness".
- **"For, behold, the LORD cometh forth out of his place, and will come down, and tread upon the high places of the earth" → "For behold, Yahweh is coming out from his place; and he will come down and tread on the high places of the earth"** (v3) — יֹצֵא participle "is coming out"; the qere בָּמֳתֵי "high places of" is read (the written form the same word).
- **"And the mountains shall be molten under him, and the valleys shall be cleft, as wax before the fire, and as the waters that are poured down a steep place" → "And the mountains shall melt under him, and the valleys shall split themselves, like wax before the fire, like waters poured down a slope"** (v4) — יִתְבַּקָּעוּ hitpael "split themselves"; מוֹרָד "slope" (lit. "descent").
- **"For the transgression of Jacob is all this … What is the transgression of Jacob? is it not Samaria? and what are the high places of Judah? are they not Jerusalem?" → "For the transgression of Jacob is all this … Who is the transgression of Jacob? Is it not Samaria? And who are the high places of Judah? Is it not Jerusalem?"** (v5) — מִי "who" (the city is named as the guilty one); בָּמוֹת "high places", as printed (the Greek reads "the sin of the house of Judah", from memory; the MT kept); חַטֹּאות "sins"; פֶּשַׁע "transgression".
- **"Therefore I will make Samaria as an heap of the field, and as plantings of a vineyard … and I will discover the foundations thereof" → "And I will make Samaria a heap of the field, a planting place of a vineyard … and her foundations I will uncover"** (v6) — וְ "and" (not "therefore"); עִי "heap" (of ruins); מַטָּעֵי כָרֶם "plantings of a vineyard" (the ground made fit for vines); גִּלָּה "uncover"; the object first, as the Hebrew.
- **"And all the graven images thereof shall be beaten to pieces, and all the hires thereof shall be burned with the fire, and all the idols thereof will I lay desolate: for she gathered it of the hire of an harlot, and they shall return to the hire of an harlot" → "And all her carved images shall be beaten to pieces, and all her hires shall be burned with fire, and all her idols I will make a desolation; for from the hire of a prostitute she gathered them …"** (v7) — פְּסִילִים "carved images"; אֶתְנַן "hire" (what a prostitute is given, its row; "wages", "fee" replaced); עֲצַבִּים "idols"; שְׁמָמָה "desolation"; זוֹנָה "prostitute". "Them" for the unexpressed object of קִבָּצָה.
- **"Therefore I will wail and howl, I will go stripped and naked: I will make a wailing like the dragons, and mourning as the owls" → "For this let me lament and wail; let me go stripped and naked; let me make a lamenting like the jackals, and mourning like the daughters of the ostrich"** (v8) — עַל־זֹאת "for this"; the four verbs are cohortatives ("let me"); סָפַד "lament", מִסְפֵּד "lamenting" (its row, as v11); the qere שׁוֹלָל "stripped" is read (the written שילל, the same word); תַּנִּים "jackals" (not תַּנִּין "dragon"); בְּנוֹת יַעֲנָה "daughters of the ostrich" (the Isa 33–35 row).
- **"For her wound is incurable; for it is come unto Judah; he is come unto the gate of my people" → "For her wounds are incurable; for it has come to Judah; it has reached to the gate of my people"** (v9) — מַכּוֹתֶיהָ plural "her wounds" (with a singular adjective: the Hebrew joins them); נָגַע "reached".
- **"Declare ye it not at Gath, weep ye not at all: in the house of Aphrah roll thyself in the dust" → "In Gath do not tell it; do not weep at all; in Beth-le-aphrah roll yourself in the dust"** (v10) — בָּכוֹ אַל־תִּבְכּוּ lit. "weeping do not weep"; the qere הִתְפַּלָּשִׁי "roll yourself" is read (the written התפלשתי "I have rolled myself"). V10–15 play on the names, the plays not imitated: בְּגַת … תַּגִּידוּ (Gath / "tell"; the line is David's, 2 Sam 1:20), לְעַפְרָה … עָפָר (Aphrah / "dust").
- **"Pass ye away, thou inhabitant of Saphir, having thy shame naked: the inhabitant of Zaanan came not forth in the mourning of Bethezel; he shall receive of you his standing" → "Pass on your way, dweller of Shaphir, in nakedness, shame; the dweller of Zaanan has not come out; the lamenting of Beth-ezel — it shall take from you its standing place"** (v11) — עִבְרִי לָכֶם "pass on for yourselves" (the plural לָכֶם beside a feminine singular verb); יוֹשֶׁבֶת "dweller" (feminine, the town as a woman, as Jer 48:19); עֶרְיָה־בֹשֶׁת "nakedness, shame" (two nouns); the line breaks by the accents (the lamenting belongs to Beth-ezel, not to Zaanan). Plays: Shaphir "beautiful" / shame; Zaanan / יָצְאָה "come out"; Beth-ezel "house of nearness" / its standing taken away. עֶמְדָּה only here: "standing place" (uncertain: support, or place to stand).
- **"For the inhabitant of Maroth waited carefully for good: but evil came down from the LORD unto the gate of Jerusalem" → "For the dweller of Maroth is in anguish for good, for evil has come down from Yahweh to the gate of Jerusalem"** (v12) — חָלָה "be sick, in anguish" (or from חִיל "writhe"; KJV "wait" from יָחַל: uncertain); כִּי twice "for"; רָע "evil" (not "disaster"); play: Maroth "bitterness" / good.
- **"O thou inhabitant of Lachish, bind the chariot to the swift beast: she is the beginning of the sin to the daughter of Zion" → "Bind the chariot to the steeds, dweller of Lachish; she was the beginning of sin to the daughter of Zion"** (v13) — רְתֹם infinitive as a command "bind"; הַמֶּרְכָּבָה singular; רֶכֶשׁ "steeds" (its row); הִיא "she". Play: Lachish / לָרֶכֶשׁ.
- **"Therefore shalt thou give presents to Moreshethgath: the houses of Achzib shall be a lie to the kings of Israel" → "Therefore you shall give parting gifts to Moresheth-gath; the houses of Achzib shall be a deceitful stream to the kings of Israel"** (v14) — שִׁלּוּחִים "parting gifts" (what is given when one is sent away: a dowry, 1 Kgs 9:16); עַל "to" (lit. "upon, concerning"); אַכְזָב "a deceitful stream" (a brook that fails, Jer 15:18). Plays: Moresheth / מְאֹרָשָׂה "betrothed" (perhaps, with the parting gifts); Achzib / אַכְזָב.
- **"Yet will I bring an heir unto thee, O inhabitant of Mareshah: he shall come unto Adullam the glory of Israel" → "Yet the possessor I will bring to you, dweller of Mareshah; to Adullam shall come the glory of Israel"** (v15) — עֹד "yet"; הַיֹּרֵשׁ "the possessor" (the one who takes possession: heir or conqueror); אָבִי "I will bring" (אָבִיא written short); כְּבוֹד יִשְׂרָאֵל "the glory of Israel" (its nobles, or its honor: open) comes to Adullam (David's hiding place, 1 Sam 22:1). Play: Mareshah / הַיֹּרֵשׁ.
- **"Make thee bald, and poll thee for thy delicate children; enlarge thy baldness as the eagle; for they are gone into captivity from thee" → "Make yourself bald and shave yourself for the sons of your delights; make your baldness wide like the eagle, for they have gone into exile from you"** (v16) — גֹּזִּי "shave yourself" (lit. "shear"); בְּנֵי תַּעֲנוּגָיִךְ "the sons of your delights"; הַרְחִבִי קָרְחָתֵךְ "widen your baldness"; נֶשֶׁר "eagle" (the bald-headed griffon vulture is meant: the one word); גָּלוּ perfect "have gone into exile".

### Notes
- **v10–15** — a dirge over the towns of the Shephelah around Micah's own Moresheth, each name answered by a word that sounds like it (named under each verse). The names are kept as printed; the plays are not imitated.
