# Micah 3

[1] And I said:
Hear, please, heads of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
and captains of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:
Is it not for you to know judgment? —

[2] haters of good and lovers of evil,
who tear their skin from off them
and their meat from off their bones;

[3] and who have eaten the meat of my people,
and their skin from off them they have stripped,
and their bones they have broken,
and they have spread them out as in the pot,
and like flesh in the midst of the cauldron.

[4] Then they will cry out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and he will not answer them;
and he will hide his face from them at that time,
as they have made their doings evil.

[5] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the prophets who cause my people to wander, who bite with their teeth and call out "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧"; and whoever does not put something on their mouths, against him they sanctify war.

[6] Therefore it shall be night to you, without vision,
and it shall be dark to you, without divination;
and the sun shall go down on the prophets,
and the day shall be black over them.

[7] And the seers shall be ashamed,
and the diviners abashed;
and they shall cover the lip, all of them,
for there is no answer of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] But I, I am full of power,
with the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and of judgment and might,
to declare to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ his transgression
and to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his sin.

[9] Hear this, please, heads of the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
and captains of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
who abominate judgment
and twist all that is straight,

[10] building ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ with blood
and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with injustice.

[11] Her heads judge for a bribe,
and her priests teach for a price,
and her prophets divine for money;
and on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they lean, saying,
Is not ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in our midst?
Evil shall not come upon us.

[12] Therefore because of you
**⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ shall be plowed as a field,
and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall become heaps,
and the mountain of the house high places of a forest.**

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **Layout and speech marks** — poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents; v5 prose as before. The quotation marks of v11 are dropped (the Hebrew has לֵאמֹר "saying"); "Shalom" in v5 is the one word they call out.
- **"Hear, I pray you, O heads of Jacob, and ye princes of the house of Israel; Is it not for you to know judgment?" → "Hear, please, heads of Jacob and captains of the house of Israel: Is it not for you to know judgment?"** (v1, as v9) — וָאֹמַר "and I said"; נָא "please"; no "O"; קָצִין "captain" (its row; beside רֹאשׁ "head"); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (not "justice").
- **"Who hate the good, and love the evil; who pluck off their skin from off them, and their flesh from off their bones" → "haters of good and lovers of evil, who tear their skin from off them and their meat from off their bones"** (v2) — the qere רָע "evil" is read (the written רעה, the same sense); participles "haters … lovers"; עוֹרָם "their skin" (no "my people" in v2); שְׁאֵר "meat" (v2, 3), beside בָּשָׂר "flesh" (v3): two words.
- **"Who also eat the flesh of my people … and they break their bones, and chop them in pieces, as for the pot, and as flesh within the caldron" → "and who have eaten the meat of my people, and their skin from off them they have stripped, and their bones they have broken, and they have spread them out as in the pot, and like flesh in the midst of the cauldron"** (v3) — perfects ("have eaten … stripped … broken"); the objects first, as the Hebrew; פָּרְשׂוּ "spread out" (as printed; "chop", "break in pieces" read פָּרְסוּ: uncertain); כַּאֲשֶׁר בַּסִּיר "as in the pot".
- **"Then shall they cry unto the LORD, but he will not hear them: he will even hide his face from them at that time, as they have behaved themselves ill in their doings" → "Then they will cry out to Yahweh, and he will not answer them; and he will hide his face from them at that time, as they have made their doings evil"** (v4) — עָנָה "answer"; וְ "and" (twice); הֵרֵעוּ מַעַלְלֵיהֶם "they have made their doings evil".
- **"the prophets that make my people err, that bite with their teeth, and cry, Peace; and he that putteth not into their mouths, they even prepare war against him" → "the prophets who cause my people to wander, who bite with their teeth and call out Shalom; and whoever does not put something on their mouths, against him they sanctify war"** (v5) — הִתְעָה "cause to wander" (its row); נֹשְׁכִים בְּשִׁנֵּיהֶם "bite with their teeth" (= have food to chew; the words kept); שָׁלוֹם the token; לֹא־יִתֵּן עַל־פִּיהֶם "does not give on their mouth" ("something" supplied); קִדְּשׁוּ מִלְחָמָה "sanctify war" (proclaim a war as holy; "prepare" is the sense, not the word).
- **"Therefore night shall be unto you, that ye shall not have a vision; and it shall be dark unto you, that ye shall not divine" → "Therefore it shall be night to you, without vision, and it shall be dark to you, without divination"** (v6) — מֵחָזוֹן, מִקְּסֹם "from vision, from divination" (= without); חָשְׁכָה a verb, "it shall be dark"; קָדַר "be black".
- **"Then shall the seers be ashamed, and the diviners confounded: yea, they shall all cover their lips; for there is no answer of God" → "And the seers shall be ashamed, and the diviners abashed; and they shall cover the lip, all of them, for there is no answer of Elohim"** (v7) — חֹזִים "seers" (the חֹזֶה row); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", חָפֵר "be abashed"; עָטָה עַל־שָׂפָם "cover the lip" (the upper lip and its beard, the sign of a mourner or a leper, Lev 13:45; singular); מַעֲנֵה אֱלֹהִים "answer of Elohim".
- **"But truly I am full of power by the spirit of the LORD, and of judgment, and of might" → "But I, I am full of power, with the spirit of Yahweh, and of judgment and might"** (v8) — וְאוּלָם אָנֹכִי "but I"; אֶת־רוּחַ יְהוָה "with the spirit of Yahweh" (אֵת here "with"; it may also mark "the spirit" as a second thing filled with: open); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; גְּבוּרָה "might".
- **"that abhor judgment, and pervert all equity" → "who abominate judgment and twist all that is straight"** (v9) — תִּעֵב "abominate" (its row); עִקֵּשׁ "twist"; הַיְשָׁרָה "what is straight".
- **"They build up Zion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity" → "building Zion with blood and Jerusalem with injustice"** (v10) — בֹּנֶה singular participle, carrying on v9 ("building"); עַוְלָה "injustice" (not עָוֹן "iniquity").
- **"The heads thereof judge for reward … and the prophets thereof divine for money: yet will they lean upon the LORD, and say, Is not the LORD among us? none evil can come upon us" → "Her heads judge for a bribe … and her prophets divine for money; and on Yahweh they lean, saying, Is not Yahweh in our midst? Evil shall not come upon us"** (v11) — "her" (Zion / Jerusalem, feminine); שֹׁחַד "bribe"; מְחִיר "price"; כֶּסֶף "money" (lit. "silver", of payment: its row); וְעַל־יְהוָה יִשָּׁעֵנוּ "and on Yahweh they lean" (Hebrew order); לֵאמֹר "saying"; בְּקִרְבֵּנוּ "in our midst"; רָעָה "evil" (not "disaster").
- **"Therefore shall Zion for your sake be plowed as a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of the forest" → "Therefore because of you Zion shall be plowed as a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house high places of a forest"** (v12) — the wording of Jer 26:18, where the elders quote these words: the same Hebrew, the same English; Micah adds לָכֵן בִּגְלַלְכֶם "therefore because of you" and spells עִיִּין (the Aramaic plural ending) for Jeremiah's עִיִּים "heaps" (of ruins); שָׂדֶה "as a field" ("as" supplied); לְבָמוֹת יָעַר "high places of a forest" (the verb carries over).

### Notes
- **v12** — quoted by the elders defending Jeremiah in Jer 26:18; Hezekiah's answer there (Jer 26:19) is that Yahweh relented.
