# Matthew 9

[1] And getting into a boat, he crossed over and came into his own city.

[2] And behold, they were bringing to him a paralytic laid upon a bed. And seeing their faith, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to the paralytic, "Be of good courage, child; your sins are forgiven."

[3] And behold, some of the scribes said within themselves, "This one blasphemes."

[4] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing their reflections, said, "Why do you reflect on evil things in your hearts?

[5] For which is easier, to say, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Arise and walk'?

[6] But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority upon the earth to forgive sins" — then he says to the paralytic — "Arise, take up your bed and go to your house."

[7] And arising, he went away to his house.

[8] And seeing it, the crowds were afraid and glorified ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who had given such authority to men.

[9] And passing along from there, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ saw a man sitting at the toll booth, called ⟦matthew|Mattai|Matthew⟧, and he says to him, "Follow me." And rising, he followed him.

[10] And it came to pass, as he was reclining in the house, and behold, many toll collectors and sinners came and were reclining with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and his disciples.

[11] And seeing it, the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ were saying to his disciples, "Why does your Teacher eat with the toll collectors and sinners?"

[12] But he, hearing it, said, "Those who are strong have no need of a physician, but those who are ill.

[13] But go and learn what this is: '**Mercy I want, and not sacrifice.**' For I did not come to call righteous ones, but sinners."

[14] Then the disciples of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ approach him, saying, "Why do we and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ fast much, but your disciples do not fast?"

[15] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Are the sons of the bridal chamber able to mourn as long as the bridegroom is with them? But days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will fast.

[16] And no one puts a patch of unshrunk cloth upon an old garment; for that which fills it takes away from the garment, and a worse tear comes to be.

[17] Nor do they put young wine into old wineskins; otherwise the wineskins are burst, and the wine is poured out, and the wineskins perish. But they put young wine into new wineskins, and both are kept safe."

[18] While he was speaking these things to them, behold, one ruler came and was bowing down to him, saying, "My daughter has just now died; but come, lay your hand upon her, and she will live."

[19] And arising, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was following him, and his disciples.

[20] And behold, a woman who had had a flow of blood twelve years, approaching from behind, touched the fringe of his garment;

[21] for she was saying within herself, "If only I touch his garment, I will be saved."

[22] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, turning and seeing her, said, "Be of good courage, daughter; your faith has saved you." And the woman was saved from that hour.

[23] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, coming into the house of the ruler and seeing the flute players and the crowd making a commotion,

[24] was saying, "Withdraw, for the girl has not died but sleeps." And they were laughing at him.

[25] But when the crowd had been cast out, he entered and took hold of her hand, and the girl arose.

[26] And this report went out into all that land.

[27] And as ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was passing along from there, two blind men followed him, crying out and saying, "Have mercy on us, Son of David!"

[28] And when he had come into the house, the blind men approached him, and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "Do you believe that I am able to do this?" They say to him, "Yes, Lord."

[29] Then he touched their eyes, saying, "According to your faith let it come to pass for you."

[30] And their eyes were opened. And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ spoke sternly to them, saying, "See, let no one know it."

[31] But they, going out, spread the report of him abroad in all that land.

[32] And as they were going out, behold, they brought to him a mute man, demonized.

[33] And when the demon had been cast out, the mute man spoke. And the crowds marveled, saying, "Never did it appear thus in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[34] But the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ were saying, "By the ruler of the demons he casts out the demons."

[35] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was going around all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues and proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom and healing every disease and every sickness.

[36] And seeing the crowds, he was moved with compassion concerning them, because they were harassed and cast down, like sheep not having a shepherd.

[37] Then he says to his disciples, "The harvest indeed is great, but the workers are few;

[38] beseech therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he may thrust out workers into his harvest."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved. In v10 the Greek has the name once (συνανέκειντο τῷ Ἰησοῦ); the first, supplied one was taken out.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8) — Greek θεός *theos*; divine name preserved.
- **"a ship" → "a boat"** (v1) — Greek πλοῖον, without the article (WH Treg NA28; RP "the boat").
- **"they brought to him a man sick of the palsy, lying on a bed" → "they were bringing to him a paralytic laid upon a bed"** (v2) — Greek προσέφερον, imperfect; βεβλημένον, "laid" (8:6, 14); κλίνη "bed" (Mark 2:4 has κράβαττος, "pallet").
- **"Son, be of good cheer; thy sins be forgiven thee" → "Be of good courage, child; your sins are forgiven"** (v2, 5) — Greek θάρσει; τέκνον "child"; ἀφίενται, a present, "are (being) forgiven" (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀφέωνται, a perfect, "have been forgiven").
- **"This man blasphemeth" → "This one blasphemes"** (v3) — Greek οὗτος; ἐν ἑαυτοῖς, "within themselves".
- **"knowing their thoughts said, Wherefore think ye evil" → "knowing their reflections, said, 'Why do you reflect on evil things'"** (v4) — Greek ἐνθυμήσεις … ἐνθυμεῖσθε, noun and verb of one root (the noun as Heb 4:12). εἰδώς "knowing" (WH Treg SBLGNT); NA28 and RP ἰδών "seeing".
- **"Arise, take up thy bed, and go unto thine house" → "Arise, take up your bed and go to your house"** (vv5–7) — Greek ἔγειρε (v5); ἐγερθείς (vv6, 7), a participle: "having arisen" (NA28 RP SBLGNT; WH Treg ἔγειρε in v6). "then he says": τότε λέγει, a present; the sentence before it breaks off, as in Mark 2:10.
- **"they marvelled" → "were afraid"** (v8) — ἐφοβήθησαν (WH Treg NA28); RP ἐθαύμασαν "marveled".
- **"sitting at the receipt of custom" → "sitting at the toll booth"; "publicans" → "toll collectors"** (vv9–11) — Greek τελώνιον, τελῶναι: collectors of tolls and duties.
- **"named Matthew" → "called *Mattai*"** (v9) — Greek Μαθθαῖον λεγόμενον.
- **"as Jesus sat at meat in the house" → "as he was reclining in the house"** (v10) — Greek αὐτοῦ ἀνακειμένου, "he reclining" (at a meal); καὶ ἰδού "and behold" follows as in Hebrew narrative.
- **"Why eateth your Master" → "Why does your Teacher eat"** (v11) — Greek ὁ διδάσκαλος ὑμῶν. ἔλεγον, imperfect (WH Treg NA28; RP εἶπον).
- **"They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick" → "Those who are strong have no need of a physician, but those who are ill"** (v12) — the Greek of Mark 2:17 word for word. "he" (WH NA28; Treg RP "Jesus").
- **"what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice" → "what this is: 'Mercy I want, and not sacrifice'"** (v13) — Greek τί ἐστιν, "what it is"; Hos 6:6 as Matthew gives it in Greek: ἔλεος "mercy" (the Hebrew has *chesed*), θέλω "want".
- **"the righteous, but sinners to repentance" → "righteous ones, but sinners"** (v13) — no articles in the Greek; "to repentance" (εἰς μετάνοιαν) stands in RP only.
- **"fast oft" → "fast much"** (v14) — πολλά (Treg NA28 RP; not in WH). προσέρχονται, a present: "approach".
- **"the children of the bridechamber" → "the sons of the bridal chamber"** (v15) — Greek οἱ υἱοὶ τοῦ νυμφῶνος, as Mark 2:19; Matthew has πενθεῖν "mourn" where Mark has "fast".
- **"No man putteth a piece of new cloth unto an old garment, for that which is put in to fill it up taketh from the garment, and the rent is made worse" → "no one puts a patch of unshrunk cloth upon an old garment; for that which fills it takes away from the garment, and a worse tear comes to be"** (v16) — Greek ἐπιβάλλει ἐπίβλημα (Mark 2:21 "sews"); ῥάκους ἀγνάφου; τὸ πλήρωμα αὐτοῦ, "its filling".
- **"new wine into old bottles: else the bottles break, and the wine runneth out, and the bottles perish: but they put new wine into new bottles, and both are preserved" → "young wine into old wineskins; otherwise the wineskins are burst, and the wine is poured out, and the wineskins perish. But they put young wine into new wineskins, and both are kept safe"** (v17) — Greek ἀσκοί, skins; νέος "young" of the wine, καινός "new" of the skins, two words; ῥήγνυνται, ἐκχεῖται, ἀπόλλυνται, presents; συντηροῦνται, "are kept safe together" (the verb of Mark 6:20).
- **"there came a certain ruler, and worshipped him" → "one ruler came and was bowing down to him"** (v18) — Greek ἄρχων εἷς ἐλθών (Treg NA28 RP; WH προσελθών); προσεκύνει, imperfect, "bow down to" (8:2). Mark 5:22 names him and calls him a synagogue ruler.
- **"is even now dead" → "has just now died"** (v18) — Greek ἄρτι ἐτελεύτησεν, "came to her end".
- **"Jesus arose, and followed him" → "arising, *Yeshua* was following him"** (v19) — ἠκολούθει, imperfect (WH Treg SBLGNT); NA28 RP ἠκολούθησεν "followed".
- **"diseased with an issue of blood twelve years, came behind him, and touched the hem of his garment" → "who had had a flow of blood twelve years, approaching from behind, touched the fringe of his garment"** (v20) — Greek αἱμορροοῦσα, one word: "bleeding"; κράσπεδον, the fringe or tassel on the corner of a garment (Num 15:38–39).
- **"I shall be whole … thy faith hath made thee whole. And the woman was made whole" → "I will be saved … your faith has saved you. And the woman was saved"** (vv21–22) — Greek σῴζω, "save", three times; here of healing from the illness. As Mark 5:28, 34.
- **"from that hour"** (v22) — Greek ἀπὸ τῆς ὥρας ἐκείνης; not "instantly".
- **"the minstrels and the people making a noise" → "the flute players and the crowd making a commotion"** (v23) — Greek αὐλητάς; θορυβούμενον.
- **"Give place: for the maid is not dead, but sleepeth. And they laughed him to scorn" → "Withdraw, for the girl has not died but sleeps. And they were laughing at him"** (v24) — Greek ἀναχωρεῖτε; κοράσιον; ἀπέθανεν, aorist; κατεγέλων, imperfect. As Mark 5:39–40.
- **"when the people were put forth, he went in, and took her by the hand, and the maid arose" → "when the crowd had been cast out, he entered and took hold of her hand, and the girl arose"** (v25) — Greek ἐξεβλήθη, "was cast out"; ἐκράτησεν τῆς χειρός; ἠγέρθη, passive in form ("was raised" or "arose").
- **"the fame hereof went abroad into all that land" → "this report went out into all that land"** (v26) — Greek φήμη; γῆ "land" (also v31).
- **"Thou Son of David, have mercy on us" → "Have mercy on us, Son of David"** (v27) — the Greek order. υἱός, a nominative used in address (Treg NA28); WH RP and the printed SBLGNT υἱέ, the vocative; no difference in English.
- **"straitly charged them, saying, See that no man know it" → "spoke sternly to them, saying, 'See, let no one know it'"** (v30) — Greek ἐνεβριμήθη, as Mark 1:43; γινωσκέτω, a third-person imperative.
- **"spread abroad his fame in all that country" → "spread the report of him abroad in all that land"** (v31) — Greek διεφήμισαν αὐτόν, the verb of φήμη "report" (v26).
- **"a dumb man possessed with a devil" → "a mute man, demonized"** (vv32–33) — Greek κωφός, "dull": deaf or mute; here of one who then speaks. δαιμονιζόμενον, one word. "man" (Treg NA28 RP; not in WH).
- **"It was never so seen in Israel" → "Never did it appear thus in *Yisrael*"** (v33) — Greek ἐφάνη, "appeared".
- **"He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils" → "By the ruler of the demons he casts out the demons"** (v34) — the Greek of Mark 3:22 word for word; ἄρχων is the word for the "ruler" of v18, 23.
- **"went about" → "was going around"** (v35) — Greek περιῆγεν, imperfect.
- **"preaching … healing every sickness and every disease among the people" → "proclaiming … healing every disease and every sickness"** (v35) — Greek κηρύσσων; νόσον, μαλακίαν; "among the people" stands in RP only.
- **"they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd" → "they were harassed and cast down, like sheep not having a shepherd"** (v36) — Greek ἐσκυλμένοι, from σκύλλω "flay, harass" ("bother" in Mark 5:35); ἐρριμμένοι, "thrown down"; ὡσεί "like" (WH NA28 RP; Treg ὡς "as", the word of Mark 6:34).
- **"The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few" → "The harvest indeed is great, but the workers are few"** (v37) — Greek πολύς; ἐργάται, "workers" (1 Tim 5:18).
- **"Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers" → "beseech therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he may thrust out workers"** (v38) — Greek δεήθητε, "beseech"; ἐκβάλῃ, the verb "cast out" used of the demons (vv33–34): here a strong word for sending out, "thrust out, put forth" (as 12:20, 35; 13:52). "Cast out workers" would be heard as driving them away.

### Notes
- **v13** quotes Hos 6:6.
