# Matthew 10

[1] And calling his twelve disciples to himself, he gave them authority over unclean spirits, so as to cast them out and to heal every disease and every sickness.

[2] Now the names of the twelve apostles are these: first, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, the one called ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, and ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧ his brother, and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the son of ⟦zebedee|Zebedaios|Zebedee⟧, and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ his brother,

[3] ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ and ⟦bartholomew|Bar-Talmai|Bartholomew⟧, ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧ and ⟦matthew|Mattai|Matthew⟧ the toll collector, ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the son of ⟦alphaeus|Alphaios|Alphaeus⟧, and ⟦thaddaeus|Thaddaios|Thaddaeus⟧,

[4] ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ the Cananaean, and ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ the Iscariot, the one who also delivered him up.

[5] These twelve ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ sent out, charging them, saying, "Do not go away into a way of nations, and do not enter into a city of ⟦samaritans|Shomronim|Samaritans⟧;

[6] but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] And as you go, proclaim, saying, 'The kingdom of the heavens has drawn near.'

[8] Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons; as a gift you received, as a gift give.

[9] Do not acquire gold nor silver nor copper for your belts,

[10] nor a bag for the road, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the worker is worthy of his food.

[11] And into whatever city or village you enter, search out who in it is worthy, and remain there until you go out.

[12] And as you enter into the house, greet it;

[13] and if indeed the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it; but if it is not worthy, let your peace turn back to you.

[14] And whoever does not accept you nor hear your words, going outside that house or city, shake off the dust of your feet.

[15] Amen, I say to you, it will be more bearable for the land of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧ in the day of judgment than for that city.

[16] "Behold, I send you out as sheep in the midst of wolves; become therefore sensible as the serpents and innocent as the doves.

[17] But take heed of men; for they will deliver you up to ⟦sanhedrin|sanhedrins|councils⟧, and in their synagogues they will scourge you;

[18] and before governors also and kings you will be led for my sake, for a testimony to them and to the nations.

[19] But when they deliver you up, do not be anxious about how or what you should speak; for it will be given to you in that hour what you should speak;

[20] for it is not you who are speaking, but the Spirit of your Father which is speaking in you.

[21] "And brother will deliver up brother to death, and a father a child, and children will rise up against parents and put them to death;

[22] and you will be hated by all because of my name. But the one who has endured to the end, this one will be saved.

[23] But when they persecute you in this city, flee to the other; for amen, I say to you, you will by no means finish the cities of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ until the Son of Man comes.

[24] "A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a slave above his lord.

[25] It is sufficient for the disciple that he become as his teacher, and the slave as his lord. If they called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more those of his household.

[26] "Therefore do not fear them; for nothing is covered that will not be revealed, and hidden that will not be known.

[27] What I say to you in the darkness, say in the light; and what you hear into the ear, proclaim upon the housetops.

[28] And do not be afraid of those who kill the body but are not able to kill the soul; but fear rather the one who is able to destroy both soul and body in ⟦gehenna|Gehenna|hell⟧.

[29] Are not two sparrows sold for an assarion? And one of them will not fall upon the earth without your Father.

[30] But of you even the hairs of the head are all numbered.

[31] Therefore do not fear; you are worth more than many sparrows.

[32] "Everyone therefore who will confess me before men, I also will confess him before my Father who is in the heavens;

[33] but whoever denies me before men, I also will deny him before my Father who is in the heavens.

[34] "Do not suppose that I came to cast peace upon the earth; I did not come to cast peace, but a sword.

[35] For I came to divide a man against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;

[36] and a man's enemies are those of his household.

[37] "The one who has affection for father or mother above me is not worthy of me; and the one who has affection for son or daughter above me is not worthy of me;

[38] and whoever does not take his cross and follow after me is not worthy of me.

[39] The one who has found his soul will lose it, and the one who has lost his soul for my sake will find it.

[40] "The one who accepts you accepts me, and the one who accepts me accepts the one who sent me.

[41] The one who accepts a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet's wage, and the one who accepts a righteous one in the name of a righteous one will receive a righteous one's wage.

[42] And whoever gives one of these little ones only a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple — Amen, I say to you, he will by no means lose his wage."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v5) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v2) — Greek Πέτρος; the Aramaic name behind it.
- **"when he had called unto him his twelve disciples, he gave them power against unclean spirits" → "calling his twelve disciples to himself, he gave them authority over unclean spirits"** (v1) — Greek προσκαλεσάμενος; ἐξουσίαν πνευμάτων ἀκαθάρτων, a plain genitive ("authority of unclean spirits"), as Mark 6:7; ὥστε "so as to". νόσον, μαλακίαν as 9:35.
- **"James the son of Zebedee … James the son of Alphaeus"** (vv2–3) — Greek ὁ τοῦ Ζεβεδαίου, ὁ τοῦ Ἁλφαίου, "the one of Zebedee / of Alphaeus"; "son" is supplied (4:21 names Zebedee as the father; so also Mark 3:17–18). "and *Yaaqov*": καί (WH NA28; not in Treg, RP and the printed SBLGNT; on a 2–2 split NA28 decides).
- **"the publican" → "the toll collector"** (v3) — Greek ὁ τελώνης (9:9).
- **"Lebbaeus, whose surname was Thaddaeus" → "*Thaddaios*"** (v3) — Θαδδαῖος (WH Treg NA28); RP "Lebbaeus who was surnamed Thaddaeus".
- **"Simon the Canaanite" → "*Shimon* the Cananaean"** (v4) — Greek ὁ Καναναῖος (WH Treg NA28; RP Κανανίτης), from Aramaic *qan'ana*, "zealous one" (Luke 6:15 translates it: "the Zealot"); not "Canaanite". As Mark 3:18.
- **"Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him" → "*Yehudah* the Iscariot, the one who also delivered him up"** (v4) — Greek ὁ Ἰσκαριώτης, with the article (WH NA28; Treg RP without); παραδούς, "deliver up, hand over": the verb of vv17, 19, 21.
- **"commanded them, saying, Go not into the way of the Gentiles, and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not" → "charging them, saying, 'Do not go away into a way of nations, and do not enter into a city of *Shomronim*'"** (v5) — Greek παραγγείλας; εἰς ὁδὸν ἐθνῶν, "into a road of nations" (a road leading to the nations), no articles; ἔθνη, "nations" (also v18).
- **"The kingdom of heaven is at hand" → "The kingdom of the heavens has drawn near"** (v7) — Greek ἤγγικεν, a perfect, as Mark 1:15; τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural.
- **"Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils: freely ye have received, freely give" → "Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons; as a gift you received, as a gift give"** (v8) — the order of WH Treg NA28 (RP has no "raise the dead"); no articles; δωρεάν, "as a gift, for nothing".
- **"Provide neither gold, nor silver, nor brass in your purses, nor scrip for your journey, neither two coats, neither shoes, nor yet staves: for the workman is worthy of his meat" → "Do not acquire gold nor silver nor copper for your belts, nor a bag for the road, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the worker is worthy of his food"** (vv9–10) — Greek κτήσησθε, "acquire"; χαλκόν, copper coin; ζώνας, belts (in which money was carried); πήραν; χιτῶνας; ὑποδήματα; ῥάβδον, singular (WH Treg NA28; RP plural); τροφῆς. In Mark 6:8–9 a staff and sandals are allowed; the difference is left as it stands.
- **"enquire who in it is worthy; and there abide till ye go thence" → "search out who in it is worthy, and remain there until you go out"** (v11) — Greek ἐξετάσατε (2:8); μείνατε, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a stay in a place: "remain".
- **"when ye come into an house, salute it" → "as you enter into the house, greet it"** (v12) — Greek εἰς τὴν οἰκίαν, with the article; ἀσπάσασθε.
- **"let your peace return to you" → "let your peace turn back to you"** (v13) — Greek ἐπιστραφήτω.
- **"whosoever shall not receive you … when ye depart out of that house or city" → "whoever does not accept you … going outside that house or city"** (v14) — Greek δέξηται, "accept" (also vv40–41); ἐξερχόμενοι ἔξω (WH Treg NA28; RP without ἔξω); κονιορτόν, "dust" (Mark 6:11 χοῦν).
- **"Verily I say unto you, It shall be more tolerable" → "Amen, I say to you, it will be more bearable"** (v15; "Amen" also vv23, 42) — Greek ἀμήν; ἀνεκτότερον. ἐν ἡμέρᾳ κρίσεως has no article ("in a day of judgment").
- **"be ye therefore wise as serpents, and harmless as doves" → "become therefore sensible as the serpents and innocent as the doves"** (v16) — Greek γίνεσθε; φρόνιμοι (7:24); ἀκέραιοι, "unmixed, innocent" (Phil 2:15); the articles are in the Greek.
- **"beware of men: for they will deliver you up to the councils, and they will scourge you in their synagogues" → "take heed of men; for they will deliver you up to *sanhedrins*, and in their synagogues they will scourge you"** (v17) — Greek προσέχετε ἀπό (7:15); συνέδρια, the plural of the word for the Sanhedrin: local councils, as Mark 13:9; no article.
- **"ye shall be brought before governors and kings for my sake, for a testimony against them and the Gentiles" → "before governors also and kings you will be led for my sake, for a testimony to them and to the nations"** (v18) — Greek ἀχθήσεσθε, "be led" (not "dragged"); αὐτοῖς, a dative: "to them".
- **"take no thought how or what ye shall speak" → "do not be anxious about how or what you should speak"** (v19) — Greek μεριμνήσητε (6:25); λαλήσητε twice, one verb.
- **"the Spirit of your Father which speaketh in you" → "the Spirit of your Father which is speaking in you"** (v20) — Greek τὸ λαλοῦν, a neuter participle agreeing with πνεῦμα; the gender is grammatical. ἐν ὑμῖν, "in you" (not "through").
- **"the brother shall deliver up the brother to death, and the father the child: and the children shall rise up against their parents, and cause them to be put to death" → "brother will deliver up brother to death, and a father a child, and children will rise up against parents and put them to death"** (v21) — the Greek of Mark 13:12 word for word; no articles; θανατώσουσιν, "put to death".
- **"hated of all men for my name's sake: but he that endureth to the end shall be saved" → "hated by all because of my name. But the one who has endured to the end, this one will be saved"** (v22) — the Greek of Mark 13:13 word for word: διὰ τὸ ὄνομά μου; ὑπομείνας, an aorist participle. σῴζω, "save", here of the final rescue.
- **"flee ye into another: for verily I say unto you, Ye shall not have gone over the cities of Israel, till the Son of man be come" → "flee to the other; for amen, I say to you, you will by no means finish the cities of *Yisrael* until the Son of Man comes"** (v23) — Greek εἰς τὴν ἑτέραν, "to the other" (WH Treg NA28; RP ἄλλην); οὐ μή, the strong negation; τελέσητε, "finish, complete" (7:28): either finish going through them or finish the work in them.
- **"The disciple is not above his master, nor the servant above his lord" → "A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a slave above his lord"** (vv24–25) — Greek διδάσκαλος, "teacher"; δοῦλος, "slave"; κύριος, "lord".
- **"It is enough" → "It is sufficient"** (v25) — Greek ἀρκετόν, as 6:34.
- **"If they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, how much more shall they call them of his household?" → "If they called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more those of his household"** (v25) — Greek ἐπεκάλεσαν, "called by a name, surnamed" (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐκάλεσαν); the second half has no verb; Βεελζεβούλ.
- **"Fear them not therefore" → "Therefore do not fear them"** (v26) — Greek κεκαλυμμένον "covered", ἀποκαλυφθήσεται "revealed" (uncovered), one root; κρυπτόν "hidden" (as Mark 4:22).
- **"what ye hear in the ear, that preach ye upon the housetops" → "what you hear into the ear, proclaim upon the housetops"** (v27) — Greek εἰς τὸ οὖς; κηρύξατε. "in the darkness": σκοτία.
- **"fear not them which kill the body … but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell" → "do not be afraid of those who kill the body … but fear rather the one who is able to destroy both soul and body in *Gehenna*"** (v28) — Greek φοβεῖσθε ἀπό, "be afraid of" (literally "fear from"), then φοβεῖσθε with a plain object, "fear"; ψυχή "soul"; ἀπολέσαι "destroy" (the verb "lose" of v39); γέεννα.
- **"sold for a farthing? and one of them shall not fall on the ground without your Father" → "sold for an assarion? And one of them will not fall upon the earth without your Father"** (v29) — Greek ἀσσάριον, a small Roman copper coin (the *as*); ἄνευ τοῦ πατρὸς ὑμῶν, "without your Father" (no word for "will" or "knowledge").
- **"ye are of more value than many sparrows" → "you are worth more than many sparrows"** (v31) — Greek διαφέρετε, as 6:26.
- **"Whosoever therefore shall confess me before men, him will I confess also" → "Everyone therefore who will confess me before men, I also will confess him"** (v32) — Greek ὁμολογήσει ἐν ἐμοί … ἐν αὐτῷ, literally "confess in me … in him", a Semitic turn of phrase; in v33 "deny" has a plain object. ὁμολογέω, "confess" (7:23).
- **"my Father which is in heaven" → "my Father who is in the heavens"** (vv32–33) — ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς, with the article (WH Treg NA28); RP and the printed SBLGNT ἐν οὐρανοῖς, without it; the sense is the same.
- **"Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword" → "Do not suppose that I came to cast peace upon the earth; I did not come to cast peace, but a sword"** (v34) — Greek νομίσητε; ἦλθον, an aorist; βαλεῖν, "cast, throw".
- **"to set a man at variance against his father" → "to divide a man against his father"** (v35) — Greek διχάσαι, "divide in two"; νύμφη, "bride": a son's young wife, a daughter-in-law. The words are those of Mic 7:6.
- **"a man's foes shall be they of his own household" → "a man's enemies are those of his household"** (v36) — Greek ἐχθροὶ τοῦ ἀνθρώπου οἱ οἰκιακοὶ αὐτοῦ, without a verb; "are" is supplied.
- **"He that loveth father or mother more than me" → "The one who has affection for father or mother above me"** (v37) — Greek ὁ φιλῶν, the verb φιλέω "have affection for" (not ἀγαπάω "love"); ὑπὲρ ἐμέ, "above me", as ὑπέρ in v24.
- **"he that taketh not his cross, and followeth after me" → "whoever does not take his cross and follow after me"** (v38) — Greek λαμβάνει "take" (Mark 8:34 has ἀράτω "take up").
- **"He that findeth his life shall lose it: and he that loseth his life for my sake shall find it" → "The one who has found his soul will lose it, and the one who has lost his soul for my sake will find it"** (v39) — Greek ψυχή, "soul" (as v28; Mark 8:35); εὑρών, ἀπολέσας, aorist participles.
- **"He that receiveth you receiveth me" → "The one who accepts you accepts me"** (vv40–41) — Greek δέχομαι, "accept", as Mark 9:37; λήμψεται (λαμβάνω), "will receive", is another verb.
- **"in the name of a prophet … a prophet's reward" → "in the name of a prophet … a prophet's wage"** (vv41–42) — Greek εἰς ὄνομα, literally "into the name of": because he is one; μισθός, "wage" (6:1). δίκαιον, "a righteous one".
- **"a cup of cold water only" → "only a cup of cold water"** (v42) — Greek ποτήριον ψυχροῦ μόνον, "a cup of cold"; "water" is supplied. οὐ μή, "by no means".

### Notes
- **v25 "Beelzebul"** — Greek Βεελζεβούλ, probably from *Ba'al-Zevul* ("lord of the lofty abode"); in 12:24 the name of the ruler of the demons.
- **v35–36** — the words follow Mic 7:6.
