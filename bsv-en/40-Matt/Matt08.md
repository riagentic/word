# Matthew 8

[1] And when he had come down from the mountain, many crowds followed him.

[2] And behold, a leper, approaching, was bowing down to him, saying, "Lord, if you are willing, you are able to cleanse me."

[3] And stretching out his hand, he touched him, saying, "I am willing; be cleansed." And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.

[4] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "See that you tell no one, but go, show yourself to the priest and offer the gift which ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ prescribed, for a testimony to them."

[5] And when he had entered into ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧, a centurion approached him, appealing to him

[6] and saying, "Lord, my boy has been laid in the house, a paralytic, terribly tormented."

[7] And he says to him, "I will come and heal him."

[8] And answering, the centurion said, "Lord, I am not fit that you should enter under my roof; but only say it with a word, and my boy will be healed.

[9] For I also am a man under authority, having soldiers under myself; and I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes, and to another, 'Come,' and he comes, and to my slave, 'Do this,' and he does it."

[10] And hearing it, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ marveled and said to those following, "Amen, I say to you, with no one have I found so great faith in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[11] And I say to you that many will come from east and west and will recline with ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ in the kingdom of the heavens,

[12] but the sons of the kingdom will be cast out into the outer darkness. There will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth."

[13] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to the centurion, "Go; as you believed, let it come to pass for you." And the boy was healed in that hour.

[14] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, coming into ⟦peter|Kefa's|Peter's⟧ house, saw his mother-in-law laid down and in a fever;

[15] and he touched her hand, and the fever left her, and she arose and was serving him.

[16] And when evening had come, they brought to him many who were demonized; and he cast out the spirits with a word, and all who were ill he healed,

[17] that what was spoken through ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, "He himself took our weaknesses and bore the diseases."

[18] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, seeing a crowd around him, commanded to go away to the other side.

[19] And one scribe, approaching, said to him, "Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go."

[20] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "The foxes have dens, and the birds of the heaven dwelling places, but the Son of Man has nowhere he may lay his head."

[21] And another of the disciples said to him, "Lord, permit me first to go away and bury my father."

[22] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "Follow me, and leave the dead to bury their own dead."

[23] And when he got into a boat, his disciples followed him.

[24] And behold, there came a great shaking in the sea, so that the boat was being covered by the waves; but he himself was sleeping.

[25] And approaching, they roused him, saying, "Lord, save! We are perishing."

[26] And he says to them, "Why are you cowardly, you of little faith?" Then, arising, he rebuked the winds and the sea, and there came a great calm.

[27] And the men marveled, saying, "Of what sort is this one, that even the winds and the sea obey him?"

[28] And when he had come to the other side, into the country of the Gadarenes, there met him two demonized men coming out of the tombs, exceedingly fierce, so that no one had strength to pass by through that way.

[29] And behold, they cried out, saying, "What have we to do with you, Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? Did you come here before the time to torment us?"

[30] Now there was far from them a herd of many pigs feeding.

[31] And the demons were appealing to him, saying, "If you cast us out, send us into the herd of the pigs."

[32] And he said to them, "Go." And coming out, they went away into the pigs; and behold, the whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and they died in the waters.

[33] And those feeding them fled, and going away into the city they reported everything, and the things of the demonized men.

[34] And behold, the whole city came out to meet ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and seeing him, they appealed to him that he would pass on from their borders.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved. The name is not in the Greek of v3 (RP adds it) and was taken out there.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v29) — Greek θεός *theos*; divine name preserved.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v14) — Greek Πέτρου; the Aramaic name behind it.
- **"great multitudes" → "many crowds"** (v1) — Greek ὄχλοι πολλοί.
- **"came and worshipped him" → "approaching, was bowing down to him"** (v2) — Greek προσελθών, "coming up to"; προσεκύνει, an imperfect of προσκυνέω, "bow down before" (not "worship", and not Mark's γονυπετῶν "kneeling", Mark 1:40).
- **"if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean … I will; be thou clean" → "if you are willing, you are able to cleanse me … I am willing; be cleansed"** (vv2–3) — the Greek of Mark 1:40–41 word for word: θέλω "be willing", καθαρίζω "cleanse" three times (v3 also "his leprosy was cleansed").
- **"saith" → "says"** (vv4, 7, 20, 22, 26) — the Greek present tense in narrative is kept.
- **"See thou tell no man" → "See that you tell no one"** (v4) — Greek μηδενὶ εἴπῃς; Mark 1:44 has μηδενὶ μηδέν, "nothing to anyone".
- **"commanded" → "prescribed"** (v4) — Greek προσέταξεν, as Mark 1:44.
- **"my servant lieth at home sick of the palsy, grievously tormented" → "my boy has been laid in the house, a paralytic, terribly tormented"** (v6; also vv8, 13) — Greek παῖς, "boy": a child or a young servant; the word does not decide (Luke 7:2 has δοῦλος "slave", and v9 here uses δοῦλος of another). βέβληται, the perfect passive of βάλλω "cast, lay": laid on a bed (also v14; 9:2).
- **"I will come and heal him"** (v7) — Greek ἐγὼ ἐλθὼν θεραπεύσω αὐτόν, with an emphatic "I"; it can also be read as a question: "Shall I come and heal him?" "And he says to him" (WH NA28; Treg RP add "Jesus"); "And" (Treg NA28 RP; not in WH).
- **"I am not worthy that thou shouldest come under my roof: but speak the word only" → "I am not fit that you should enter under my roof; but only say it with a word"** (v8) — Greek ἱκανός, "fit, sufficient" (ἄξιος is "worthy"); εἰπὲ λόγῳ, a dative: "say by a word".
- **"healed"** (vv7, 8, 13, 16) — two Greek verbs: θεραπεύω (vv7, 16) and ἰάομαι (vv8, 13).
- **"under me … to this man … to my servant" → "under myself … to this one … to my slave"** (v9) — Greek δοῦλος, "slave". "under authority" (Treg NA28 RP; WH adds τασσόμενος, "set under authority").
- **"Verily I say unto you, I have not found so great faith, no, not in Israel" → "Amen, I say to you, with no one have I found so great faith in *Yisrael*"** (v10) — Greek παρ᾽ οὐδενὶ τοσαύτην πίστιν ἐν τῷ Ἰσραὴλ εὗρον (WH Treg NA28); RP "not even in Israel".
- **"shall sit down" → "will recline"** (v11) — Greek ἀνακλιθήσονται, recline at a meal. "from east and west": ἀπὸ ἀνατολῶν καὶ δυσμῶν, literally "from risings and settings" (of the sun).
- **"the kingdom of heaven" → "the kingdom of the heavens"** (v11) — Greek τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural.
- **"the children of the kingdom … there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth" → "the sons of the kingdom … There will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth"** (v12) — Greek υἱοί; ἐκεῖ "there", in that place; both nouns have the article.
- **"as thou hast believed, so be it done unto thee" → "as you believed, let it come to pass for you"** (v13) — Greek ἐπίστευσας, an aorist; γενηθήτω, as 6:10. "the boy" (WH Treg SBLGNT; NA28 RP "his boy"); "in that hour", ἐν τῇ ὥρᾳ ἐκείνῃ.
- **"laid, and sick of a fever" → "laid down and in a fever"** (v14) — Greek βεβλημένην καὶ πυρέσσουσαν; Mark 1:30 has κατέκειτο πυρέσσουσα.
- **"she arose, and ministered unto them" → "she arose and was serving him"** (v15) — Greek ἠγέρθη (passive in form: "was raised" or "arose"); διηκόνει, imperfect; αὐτῷ "him" (Mark 1:31 "them").
- **"possessed with devils" → "demonized"** (vv16, 28, 33) — Greek δαιμονιζόμενος, one word; "possessed" is not in it. In v31 the Greek has δαίμονες (δαίμων), the only place in the New Testament; elsewhere δαιμόνιον.
- **"all that were sick" → "all who were ill"** (v16) — Greek τοὺς κακῶς ἔχοντας, "those having it badly", as Mark 1:32.
- **"Himself took our infirmities, and bare our sicknesses" → "He himself took our weaknesses and bore the diseases"** (v17) — Isa 53:4, translated from the Greek as Matthew gives it: ἀσθενείας "weaknesses", τὰς νόσους "the diseases" (no second "our"). Matthew's wording is close to the Hebrew, not to the Septuagint ("he bears our sins").
- **"great multitudes about him" → "a crowd around him"** (v18) — ὄχλον (WH NA28); Treg, RP and the printed SBLGNT πολλοὺς ὄχλους "many crowds". On a 2–2 split NA28 decides.
- **"gave commandment to depart" → "commanded to go away"** (v18) — Greek ἐκέλευσεν ἀπελθεῖν.
- **"a certain scribe … Master" → "one scribe … Teacher"** (v19) — Greek εἷς γραμματεύς; διδάσκαλε.
- **"holes … the birds of the air have nests" → "dens … the birds of the heaven dwelling places"** (v20) — Greek φωλεούς, lairs; κατασκηνώσεις, "places to camp, to dwell" (the verb in Mark 4:32); "nest" is another word (νοσσιά).
- **"hath not where to lay his head" → "has nowhere he may lay his head"** (v20) — Greek οὐκ ἔχει ποῦ … κλίνῃ.
- **"another of his disciples … suffer me" → "another of the disciples … permit me"** (v21) — τῶν μαθητῶν (WH Treg SBLGNT; NA28 RP add "his"); ἐπίτρεψον.
- **"let the dead bury their dead" → "leave the dead to bury their own dead"** (v22) — Greek ἄφες, "leave, let"; τοὺς ἑαυτῶν νεκρούς.
- **"a ship" → "a boat"** (vv23–24) — Greek πλοῖον; "into a boat" without the article (WH Treg SBLGNT; NA28 RP "the boat").
- **"there arose a great tempest in the sea" → "there came a great shaking in the sea"** (v24) — Greek σεισμὸς μέγας, the word for an earthquake (24:7; 27:54; 28:2); Mark 4:37 has λαῖλαψ, "storm".
- **"was covered with the waves: but he was asleep" → "was being covered by the waves; but he himself was sleeping"** (v24) — Greek καλύπτεσθαι; αὐτὸς δὲ ἐκάθευδεν, imperfect.
- **"his disciples came to him, and awoke him, saying, Lord, save us: we perish" → "approaching, they roused him, saying, 'Lord, save! We are perishing.'"** (v25) — "his disciples" and "us" stand in RP only; σῶσον has no object. σῴζω, "save", here from death in the sea.
- **"Why are ye fearful, O ye of little faith?" → "Why are you cowardly, you of little faith?"** (v26) — Greek δειλοί, as Mark 4:40; ὀλιγόπιστοι, one word (6:30).
- **"What manner of man is this" → "Of what sort is this one"** (v27) — Greek ποταπός ἐστιν οὗτος; no word for "man".
- **"the country of the Gergesenes" → "the country of the Gadarenes"** (v28) — Γαδαρηνῶν (WH Treg NA28); RP Γεργεσηνῶν. Mark 5:1 has "Gerasenes".
- **"exceeding fierce, so that no man might pass by that way" → "exceedingly fierce, so that no one had strength to pass by through that way"** (v28) — Greek χαλεποὶ λίαν, "hard, dangerous" (2 Tim 3:1 "hard times"); ἰσχύειν, "have strength".
- **"What have we to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of God?" → "What have we to do with you, Son of *Elohim*?"** (v29) — Greek τί ἡμῖν καὶ σοί, literally "what to us and to you?"; "Jesus" stands in RP only.
- **"a good way off" → "far"** (v30) — Greek μακράν.
- **"the devils besought him … suffer us to go away into the herd of swine" → "the demons were appealing to him … send us into the herd of the pigs"** (v31) — Greek παρεκάλουν, imperfect ("appeal" for παρακαλέω in this book, vv5, 34); ἀπόστειλον ἡμᾶς (WH Treg NA28); RP "permit us to go away".
- **"ran violently down a steep place into the sea, and perished in the waters" → "rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and they died in the waters"** (v32) — Greek ὥρμησεν … κατὰ τοῦ κρημνοῦ, as Mark 5:13; ἀπέθανον, "died", plural.
- **"they that kept them fled … and told every thing, and what was befallen to the possessed of the devils" → "those feeding them fled … they reported everything, and the things of the demonized men"** (v33) — Greek οἱ βόσκοντες; ἀπήγγειλαν; καὶ τὰ τῶν δαιμονιζομένων, "also the matters of the demonized"; no "especially".
- **"besought him that he would depart out of their coasts" → "appealed to him that he would pass on from their borders"** (v34) — Greek μεταβῇ, "move on, pass over to another place"; ὁρίων, "borders".

### Notes
- **v17** quotes Isa 53:4.
- **v22** "leave the dead to bury their own dead": the saying is given as it stands; who "the dead" who bury are is not said.
