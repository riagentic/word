# Matthew 28

[1] Now late on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, as it was dawning toward the first day of the week, ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the Magdalene and the other ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ came to behold the sepulchre.

[2] And behold, a great earthquake came; for an angel of the Lord, coming down out of heaven and approaching, rolled away the stone and was sitting upon it.

[3] Now his appearance was as lightning, and his clothing white as snow.

[4] And from the fear of him those keeping guard quaked and became as dead men.

[5] But the angel, answering, said to the women, "**Do not be afraid**, you; for I know that you seek ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, the crucified one.

[6] He is not here, for he was raised, even as he said. Come, see the place where he was lying.

[7] And going quickly, say to his disciples, 'He was raised from the dead, and behold, he goes before you into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧; there you will see him.' Behold, I told you."

[8] And going away quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy, they ran to report to his disciples.

[9] And behold, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ met them, saying, "Hail!" And they, approaching, took hold of his feet and bowed down to him.

[10] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "Do not be afraid; go, report to my brothers that they should go away into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, and there they will see me."

[11] Now as they were going, behold, some of the guard, coming into the city, reported to the high priests all the things that had come to pass.

[12] And being gathered together with the elders and taking counsel, they gave a considerable number of silver pieces to the soldiers,

[13] saying, "Say, 'His disciples, coming by night, stole him while we were asleep.'

[14] And if this is heard before the governor, we will persuade him and make you free of anxiety."

[15] And they, taking the silver pieces, did as they were taught. And this word was spread abroad among ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ until this very day.

[16] Now the eleven disciples went into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, to the mountain where ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ appointed for them.

[17] And seeing him, they bowed down; but some doubted.

[18] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, approaching, spoke to them, saying, "**All authority in heaven and upon the earth was given to me.**

[19] Going therefore, make disciples of all the nations, ⟦immerse|immersing|baptizing⟧ them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧,

**[20] teaching them to keep all things, whatever I gave you as commandment. And behold, I am with you all the days, until the consummation of the age.**"

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"**, **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v19), **"baptizing" → "immersing"** (v19) — systematic; Greek βαπτίζω is "dip, immerse".
- **"In the end of the sabbath, as it began to dawn toward" → "late on the Shabbat, as it was dawning toward"** (v1) — Greek Ὀψὲ δὲ σαββάτων, τῇ ἐπιφωσκούσῃ εἰς μίαν σαββάτων. ὀψέ with a genitive is "late on" or "after": "late on the Sabbath" or "after the Sabbath" (Mark 16:1: "when the Sabbath had passed"). ἐπιφώσκω is "grow light"; in Luke 23:54 it is used of the Sabbath "drawing on" at evening. "The first day of the week" is literally "one of the sabbaths". **"Mary Magdalene" → "Miryam the Magdalene"** — Greek ἡ Μαγδαληνή. **"to see" → "to behold"** — Greek θεωρῆσαι. **"sepulchre"** — Greek τάφος, as 27:61; "tomb" (v8) is μνημεῖον.
- **"there was a great earthquake" → "a great earthquake came"** (v2) — Greek σεισμὸς ἐγένετο μέγας. **"descended from heaven, and came and rolled back" → "coming down out of heaven and approaching, rolled away"** — Greek καταβὰς ἐξ οὐρανοῦ καὶ προσελθὼν ἀπεκύλισε. **"sat" → "was sitting"** — Greek ἐκάθητο, imperfect. "From the door" is only in the Byzantine text.
- **"countenance" → "appearance"** (v3) — Greek εἰδέα. **"raiment" → "clothing"** — Greek ἔνδυμα.
- **"for fear of him the keepers did shake" → "from the fear of him those keeping guard quaked"** (v4) — Greek ἐσείσθησαν οἱ τηροῦντες: the verb of the earthquake (σεισμός, v2; 27:51) and the "keeping" of 27:36, 54; "guard" is supplied.
- **"Fear not ye" → "Do not be afraid, you"** (v5) — Greek Μὴ φοβεῖσθε ὑμεῖς, with the emphatic "you" (the women, not the guards). **"which was crucified" → "the crucified one"** — Greek τὸν ἐσταυρωμένον, a perfect participle, as Mark 16:6.
- **"he is risen" → "he was raised"** (v6, 7) — Greek ἠγέρθη, aorist passive, as Mark 16:6. **"where the Lord lay" → "where he was lying"** — Greek ἔκειτο, imperfect; "the Lord" is read by Tregelles and the Byzantine text, not by WH and NA28.
- **"tell his disciples that he is risen" → "say to his disciples, 'He was raised …'"** (v7) — Greek εἴπατε … ὅτι Ἠγέρθη: the words are given as direct speech, as in Mark 16:7; the Greek does not mark where the message ends. **"goeth before you"** — Greek προάγει, present. **"lo, I have told you" → "Behold, I told you"** — Greek εἶπον, aorist (Mark: "even as he said to you").
- **"departed quickly from the sepulchre" → "going away quickly from the tomb"** (v8) — Greek ἀπὸ τοῦ μνημείου. **"to bring his disciples word" → "to report to his disciples"** (v8, 10, 11) — Greek ἀπαγγέλλω.
- **"All hail" → "Hail!"** (v9) — Greek Χαίρετε, "rejoice", the common greeting, plural. **"held him by the feet" → "took hold of his feet"** — Greek ἐκράτησαν. **"worshipped him" → "bowed down to him"** (v9), **"bowed down"** (v17) — Greek προσκυνέω, to bow down, prostrate oneself; "worship" as service to God is λατρεύω. In v17 "him" is only in the Byzantine text. The KJV's "as they went to tell his disciples" (v9) is only in the Byzantine text.
- **"some of the watch" → "some of the guard"** (v11) — Greek τῆς κουστωδίας, as 27:65. **"chief priests" → "high priests"** — Greek ἀρχιερεῦσιν.
- **"large money" → "a considerable number of silver pieces"** (v12, 15) — Greek ἀργύρια ἱκανά, the plural of 26:15; 27:3–9; "number" is supplied.
- **"while we slept" → "while we were asleep"** (v13) — Greek ἡμῶν κοιμωμένων.
- **"if this come to the governor's ears" → "if this is heard before the governor"** (v14) — Greek ἐὰν ἀκουσθῇ τοῦτο ἐπὶ τοῦ ἡγεμόνος (WH, NA28, Byzantine), "before the governor", as at a hearing; Tregelles has ὑπό, "by the governor". **"secure you" → "make you free of anxiety"** — Greek ὑμᾶς ἀμερίμνους ποιήσομεν.
- **"this saying is commonly reported among the Jews" → "this word was spread abroad among Jews"** (v15) — Greek διεφημίσθη ὁ λόγος οὗτος παρὰ Ἰουδαίοις, aorist, and "Jews" without the article. **"until this day" → "until this very day"** — Greek μέχρι τῆς σήμερον ἡμέρας, "until today's day".
- **"had appointed them" → "appointed for them"** (v16) — Greek οὗ ἐτάξατο αὐτοῖς: τάσσω is "set in place, arrange" (so Acts 13:48; 22:10); here of a place arranged for meeting, so "appoint".
- **"but some doubted"** (v17) — Greek οἱ δὲ ἐδίστασαν, "be of two minds" (14:31). The words can mean "but some doubted" or "but they doubted".
- **"All power is given unto me" → "All authority … was given to me"** (v18) — Greek Ἐδόθη μοι πᾶσα ἐξουσία, aorist passive; ἐξουσία is "authority" (δύναμις is "power"). "Upon the earth" has the article in WH, Tregelles and NA28.
- **"Go ye therefore, and teach all nations" → "Going therefore, make disciples of all the nations"** (v19) — Greek πορευθέντες οὖν μαθητεύσατε πάντα τὰ ἔθνη: the one command is "make disciples"; "going", "immersing" and "teaching" are participles. "All the nations" is the direct object of the verb ("disciple all the nations"). "Them" (αὐτούς) is masculine, though "nations" is neuter: the persons are meant.
- **"in the name" → "into the name"** (v19) — Greek εἰς τὸ ὄνομα, "into", as Acts 8:16; 19:5; 1 Cor 1:13; "name" is singular, followed by three genitives each with its article: τοῦ πατρὸς καὶ τοῦ υἱοῦ καὶ τοῦ ἁγίου πνεύματος. "Immersing" is the present participle βαπτίζοντες (WH, NA28, Byzantine); Tregelles has the aorist βαπτίσαντες, "having immersed".
- **"to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you" → "to keep all things, whatever I gave you as commandment"** (v20) — Greek τηρεῖν πάντα ὅσα ἐνετειλάμην ὑμῖν: τηρέω "keep"; ἐντέλλομαι is the verb of ἐντολή "commandment", aorist.
- **"alway" → "all the days"** (v20) — Greek πάσας τὰς ἡμέρας. **"unto the end of the world" → "until the consummation of the age"** — Greek ἕως τῆς συντελείας τοῦ αἰῶνος, as 13:39, 40, 49; 24:3: συντέλεια is "completion", not τέλος "end", and αἰών is "age", not κόσμος "world". "Amen" is only in the Byzantine text.

### Notes
- **v19** — the only place in the New Testament where immersion is "into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"; in Acts it is "into" or "in the name of the Lord Yeshua" (Acts 2:38; 8:16; 10:48; 19:5).
- **v18** — compare Dan 7:14.
