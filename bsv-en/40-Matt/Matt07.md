# Matthew 7

[1] "Do not judge, that you may not be judged;

[2] for in what judgment you judge you will be judged, and in what measure you measure, it will be measured to you.

[3] And why do you see the speck that is in your brother's eye, but the beam in your own eye you do not observe?

[4] Or how will you say to your brother, 'Let me cast the speck out of your eye,' and behold, the beam is in your eye?

[5] Hypocrite, first cast the beam out of your eye, and then you will see clearly to cast the speck out of your brother's eye.

[6] "Do not give what is holy to the dogs, nor cast your pearls before the pigs, lest they trample them with their feet and, turning, rend you.

[7] "Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.

[8] For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.

[9] Or what man is there of you, whom his son will ask for bread — will he hand him a stone?

[10] Or he will also ask for a fish — will he hand him a serpent?

[11] If therefore you, being evil, know to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father who is in the heavens give good things to those who ask him.

[12] "**All things therefore, as many as you want that men should do to you, so also you do to them; for this is the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ and the Prophets.**

[13] "Enter through the narrow gate; because broad is the gate and spacious the way that leads away to destruction, and many are those who enter through it.

[14] How narrow the gate and constricted the way that leads away to life, and few are those who find it!

[15] "Take heed of the false prophets, who come to you in clothing of sheep, but inwardly are ravening wolves.

[16] From their fruits you will recognize them. Do they gather grapes from thorns, or figs from thistles?

[17] So every good tree makes good fruits, but the rotten tree makes evil fruits;

[18] a good tree cannot make evil fruits, nor a rotten tree make good fruits.

[19] Every tree not making good fruit is cut down and cast into fire.

[20] So then, from their fruits you will recognize them.

[21] "Not everyone who says to me, 'Lord, Lord,' will enter into the kingdom of the heavens, but the one who does the will of my Father who is in the heavens.

[22] Many will say to me in that day, 'Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy by your name, and by your name cast out demons, and by your name do many powers?'

[23] And then I will confess to them, 'I never knew you; withdraw from me, you who work lawlessness.'

[24] "Everyone therefore who hears these words of mine and does them will be likened to a sensible man, who built his house upon the rock.

[25] And the rain came down, and the rivers came, and the winds blew and fell upon that house, and it did not fall, for it had been founded upon the rock.

[26] And everyone who hears these words of mine and does not do them will be likened to a foolish man, who built his house upon the sand.

[27] And the rain came down, and the rivers came, and the winds blew and struck against that house, and it fell, and its fall was great."

[28] And it came to pass, when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ finished these words, the crowds were astonished at his teaching,

[29] for he was teaching them as one having authority, and not as their scribes.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v28) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"with what judgment ye judge" → "in what judgment you judge"** (v2) — Greek ἐν ᾧ κρίματι κρίνετε, noun and verb of one root; κρίμα is "sentence" in Mark 12:40, here "judgment" so that the root is heard. The second half is word for word Mark 4:24.
- **"mote" → "speck"; "beam" kept** (vv3–5) — Greek κάρφος, a dry splinter or bit of straw; δοκός, a beam of a house.
- **"considerest not" → "you do not observe"** (v3) — Greek κατανοεῖς, "observe, take note of".
- **"Let me pull out" → "Let me cast … out"** (vv4–5) — Greek ἐκβάλλω, "cast out", the verb used of the demons in v22.
- **"Thou hypocrite" → "Hypocrite"** (v5) — Greek ὑποκριτά, a vocative alone.
- **"swine" → "pigs"** (v6) — Greek χοίρων, as in Mark 5:11–13.
- **"turn again and rend you" → "turning, rend you"** (v6) — Greek στραφέντες ῥήξωσιν, "having turned, tear"; "attack" was too weak.
- **"what man is there of you, whom if his son ask bread, will he give him a stone?" → "what man is there of you, whom his son will ask for bread — will he hand him a stone?"** (vv9–10) — Greek αἰτήσει, a future (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐὰν αἰτήσῃ "if he asks"); the sentence breaks off and starts again with μή, a question that expects "no". ἐπιδώσει, "hand over to". ἐστιν "is there" (NA28 RP; not in WH Treg; on a 2–2 split NA28 decides).
- **"know how to give" → "know to give"** (v11) — Greek οἴδατε … διδόναι. "being evil": πονηροὶ ὄντες, a participle.
- **"your Father which is in heaven" → "your Father who is in the heavens"** (vv11, 21) — Greek ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς, plural; "the kingdom of the heavens" (v21), τῶν οὐρανῶν.
- **"all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them" → "All things therefore, as many as you want that men should do to you, so also you do to them"** (v12) — Greek πάντα οὖν ὅσα ἐὰν θέλητε … οὕτως καὶ ὑμεῖς ποιεῖτε; οἱ ἄνθρωποι, "men, people" (not "others").
- **"the law and the prophets" → "the *Torah* and the Prophets"** (v12) — Greek ὁ νόμος καὶ οἱ προφῆται, the two parts of the Scriptures; νόμος carries the *Torah* token as elsewhere.
- **"strait" → "narrow"; "wide" → "broad"; "broad" → "spacious"** (vv13–14) — Greek στενή; πλατεῖα "broad" (the word for the broad streets, 6:5); εὐρύχωρος, "roomy, spacious". "the gate" in v13 (Treg NA28 RP; not in WH).
- **"that leadeth" → "that leads away"** (vv13–14) — Greek ἀπάγουσα, "lead away".
- **"Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way" → "How narrow the gate and constricted the way"** (v14) — Greek τί, an exclamation "how!" (Treg NA28 RP); WH and the printed SBLGNT read ὅτι "because". τεθλιμμένη, the perfect participle of θλίβω "press, afflict": pressed in, constricted.
- **"Beware of false prophets" → "Take heed of the false prophets"** (v15) — Greek προσέχετε ἀπό (in Mark the verb is βλέπετε ἀπό, "beware of"); the article is in the Greek.
- **"in sheep's clothing" → "in clothing of sheep"; "ravening" kept** (v15) — Greek ἐν ἐνδύμασι προβάτων (ἔνδυμα, as 6:25, 28); ἅρπαγες, "snatching, rapacious".
- **"Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather" → "From their fruits you will recognize them. Do they gather"** (vv16, 20) — Greek ἀπό "from"; ἐπιγνώσεσθε, "recognize"; μήτι expects "no"; no word for "men".
- **"bringeth forth good fruit" → "makes good fruits"** (vv17–19) — Greek ποιεῖ, "makes"; καρπούς plural in vv17–18, singular in v19. "Good" stands for two words: ἀγαθόν of the tree, καλούς of the fruits.
- **"a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit" → "the rotten tree makes evil fruits"** (vv17–18) — Greek σαπρόν, "rotten, decayed" (not "diseased"); πονηρούς, "evil". ποιεῖν in v18 (Treg NA28 RP; WH ἐνεγκεῖν "bear").
- **"is hewn down, and cast into the fire" → "is cut down and cast into fire"** (v19) — Greek εἰς πῦρ, without the article; present tenses.
- **"Wherefore" → "So then"** (v20) — Greek ἄρα γε.
- **"in thy name" → "by your name"** (v22) — Greek τῷ σῷ ὀνόματι, a dative without a preposition, three times and each time before its verb; Mark 9:38 has ἐν τῷ ὀνόματί σου, "in your name".
- **"many wonderful works" → "many powers"** (v22) — Greek δυνάμεις, "powers": deeds of power.
- **"profess" → "confess"** (v23) — Greek ὁμολογήσω, "confess, declare openly".
- **"depart from me, ye that work iniquity" → "withdraw from me, you who work lawlessness"** (v23) — Greek ἀποχωρεῖτε; ἀνομία, "lawlessness". The words are those of Ps 6:8 (6:9 in the Greek Psalter).
- **"I will liken him unto a wise man" → "will be likened to a sensible man"** (vv24, 26) — Greek ὁμοιωθήσεται, a passive (WH Treg NA28; RP ὁμοιώσω αὐτόν "I will liken him"); φρόνιμος, "sensible, prudent" (σοφός is "wise").
- **"sayings" → "words"** (vv24, 26, 28) — Greek λόγους, the same word each time.
- **"the rain descended, and the floods came … and beat upon that house" → "the rain came down, and the rivers came … and fell upon that house"** (vv25, 27) — Greek ποταμοί, "rivers"; two verbs for the winds: προσέπεσαν "fell upon" (v25), προσέκοψαν "struck against" (v27; elsewhere "stumble").
- **"And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended these sayings, the people were astonished at his doctrine" → "And it came to pass, when *Yeshua* finished these words, the crowds were astonished at his teaching"** (v28) — Greek ἐτέλεσεν "finished"; οἱ ὄχλοι "the crowds"; διδαχή "teaching".
- **"as one having authority, and not as the scribes" → "as one having authority, and not as their scribes"** (v29) — as Mark 1:22; Matthew adds αὐτῶν "their" (WH Treg NA28; not in RP).
