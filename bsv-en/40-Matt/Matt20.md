# Matthew 20

[1] "For the kingdom of the heavens is like a man, a master of a house, who went out early in the morning to hire workers into his vineyard.

[2] And agreeing with the workers for a denarius the day, he sent them into his vineyard.

[3] And going out about the third hour, he saw others standing in the marketplace idle;

[4] and to those he said, 'Go you also into the vineyard, and whatever is just I will give you.'

[5] And they went away. And again going out about the sixth and the ninth hour, he did likewise.

[6] And about the eleventh, going out, he found others standing, and he says to them, 'Why do you stand here the whole day idle?'

[7] They say to him, 'Because no one hired us.' He says to them, 'Go you also into the vineyard.'

[8] And when evening had come, the lord of the vineyard says to his manager, 'Call the workers and render to them the wage, beginning from the last up to the first.'

[9] And those of about the eleventh hour, coming, received a denarius each.

[10] And the first, coming, supposed that they will receive more; and they also received the denarius each.

[11] And receiving it, they were murmuring against the master of the house,

[12] saying, 'These last did one hour, and you made them equal to us who bore the burden of the day and the scorching heat.'

[13] But he, answering, said to one of them, 'Companion, I do you no wrong; did you not agree with me for a denarius?

[14] Take what is yours and go; but I want to give to this last one as also to you.

[15] Is it not permitted for me to do what I want with what is mine? Or is your eye evil because I am good?'

[16] So the last will be first, and the first last."

[17] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, going up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, took along the twelve disciples privately, and on the way he said to them,

[18] "Behold, we are going up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and the Son of Man will be delivered up to the high priests and scribes, and they will condemn him to death,

[19] and they will deliver him up to the nations to mock and to scourge and to crucify, and on the third day he will be raised."

[20] Then the mother of the sons of ⟦zebedee|Zebedaios|Zebedee⟧ approached him with her sons, bowing down and asking something from him.

[21] And he said to her, "What do you want?" She says to him, "Say that these two sons of mine may sit, one at your right hand and one at your left, in your kingdom."

[22] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, answering, said, "You do not know what you are asking. Are you able to drink the cup which I am about to drink?" They say to him, "We are able."

[23] He says to them, "My cup indeed you will drink, but to sit at my right hand and at the left is not mine to give, but to those for whom it has been prepared by my Father."

[24] And hearing it, the ten were indignant about the two brothers.

[25] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, calling them to himself, said, "You know that the rulers of the nations lord it over them, and the great ones exercise authority over them.

[26] It will not be so among you; but whoever wants to become great among you shall be your servant,

[27] and whoever wants to be first among you shall be your slave;

[28] just as the Son of Man did not come to be served but to serve, and to give his soul as a ransom for many."

[29] And as they were going out from ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, a great crowd followed him.

[30] And behold, two blind men sitting beside the way, hearing that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is passing by, cried out, saying, "Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!"

[31] But the crowd rebuked them, that they should be silent; but they cried out the more, saying, "Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!"

[32] And standing still, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ called them and said, "What do you want me to do for you?"

[33] They say to him, "Lord, that our eyes may be opened."

[34] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, moved with compassion, touched their eyes, and immediately they received sight and followed him.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv17, 22, 25, 30, 32, 34) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"kingdom of heaven" → "kingdom of the heavens"** (v1) — Greek τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural.
- **"a man that is an householder" → "a man, a master of a house"** (vv1, 11) — Greek ἀνθρώπῳ οἰκοδεσπότῃ.
- **"early in the morning" kept** (v1) — Greek ἅμα πρωΐ, "together with the early hour", at daybreak.
- **"labourers" → "workers"** (vv1, 2, 8) — Greek ἐργάτας, from ἐργάζομαι, "work".
- **"into his vineyard" kept** (vv1, 2, 4, 7) — Greek εἰς τὸν ἀμπελῶνα.
- **"for a penny a day" → "for a denarius the day"** (v2) — Greek ἐκ δηναρίου τὴν ἡμέραν; the denarius is a Roman silver coin.
- **"whatsoever is right" → "whatever is just"** (v4) — Greek δίκαιον, the word "righteous", as Col 4:1.
- **"went their way" → "went away"** (v5) — Greek ἀπῆλθον. "And again" follows πάλιν δέ (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the printed SBLGNT has no δέ.
- **"the eleventh hour … standing idle" → "the eleventh … standing"** (v6) — "hour" and "idle" are in the Byzantine text only.
- **"saith … say" → "says … say"** (vv6, 7, 8, 21, 22, 23, 33) — Greek presents; kept.
- **"Why stand ye here" → "Why do you stand here"** (v6) — Greek ἑστήκατε, a perfect with present meaning.
- **"hath hired" → "hired"** (v7) — Greek ἐμισθώσατο, aorist. "And whatsoever is right, that shall ye receive" is in the Byzantine text only.
- **"when even was come" → "when evening had come"** (v8) — Greek ὀψίας δὲ γενομένης.
- **"steward" → "manager"** (v8) — Greek ἐπιτρόπῳ, the one to whom a man's affairs are turned over; in Gal 4:2, of a child's guardian, the same word is "guardian". "Steward" is οἰκονόμος.
- **"give them their hire" → "render to them the wage"** (v8) — Greek ἀπόδος … τὸν μισθόν, to give what is owed. "Them" (αὐτοῖς) is not in Westcott-Hort.
- **"every man a penny" → "a denarius each"** (vv9–10) — Greek ἀνὰ δηνάριον; in v10 with the article, "the denarius each".
- **"they supposed that they should have received more" → "supposed that they will receive more"** (v10) — Greek ἐνόμισαν ὅτι πλεῖον λήμψονται, the future of the thought itself.
- **"murmured" → "were murmuring"** (v11) — Greek ἐγόγγυζον, imperfect.
- **"have wrought but one hour" → "did one hour"** (v12) — Greek μίαν ὥραν ἐποίησαν: ποιέω, "do, make", here of time spent; the same verb follows in "you made them equal". The Greek has no "only".
- **"the burden and heat of the day" → "the burden of the day and the scorching heat"** (v12) — Greek τὸ βάρος τῆς ἡμέρας καὶ τὸν καύσωνα, as Jas 1:11.
- **"Friend" → "Companion"** (v13) — Greek Ἑταῖρε, "companion" (22:12; 26:50); "friend" is φίλος.
- **"Take that thine is, and go thy way: I will give" → "Take what is yours and go; but I want to give"** (v14) — Greek θέλω, "I want".
- **"Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own?" → "Is it not permitted for me to do what I want with what is mine?"** (v15) — Greek οὐκ ἔξεστίν μοι; ἐν τοῖς ἐμοῖς, "in the things that are mine". NA28 and the Byzantine text begin with "Or" (ἤ); Westcott-Hort, Tregelles and the printed text do not.
- **"Is thine eye evil" → "Or is your eye evil"** (v15) — Greek ὁ ὀφθαλμός σου πονηρός, the "evil eye": envy, grudging (Deut 15:9; Prov 22:9; Matt 6:23). The verse keeps the words.
- **"for many be called, but few chosen" omitted** (v16) — read by Tregelles and the Byzantine text; not in Westcott-Hort and NA28 (the words stand in 22:14).
- **"took the twelve disciples apart in the way, and said" → "took along the twelve disciples privately, and on the way he said"** (v17) — Greek παρέλαβεν … κατ' ἰδίαν, καὶ ἐν τῇ ὁδῷ εἶπεν. Westcott-Hort begin "And being about to go up" (Μέλλων δὲ ἀναβαίνειν); Tregelles has no "disciples".
- **"betrayed" → "delivered up"; "deliver" → "deliver up"** (vv18–19) — Greek παραδοθήσεται, παραδώσουσιν, one verb, "hand over".
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v18) — Greek ἀρχιερεῦσιν.
- **"Gentiles" → "nations"** (vv19, 25) — Greek ἔθνεσιν, ἐθνῶν.
- **"the third day he shall rise again" → "on the third day he will be raised"** (v19) — Greek ἐγερθήσεται, passive (the Byzantine text has ἀναστήσεται, "rise", as Mark 10:34). Mark has "after three days" and adds "spit on him"; only Matthew names crucifixion here.
- **"the mother of Zebedee's children" → "the mother of the sons of *Zebedaios*"** (v20) — Greek τῶν υἱῶν Ζεβεδαίου. In Mark 10:35 the two sons themselves ask.
- **"worshipping him, and desiring a certain thing of him" → "bowing down and asking something from him"** (v20) — Greek προσκυνοῦσα, the act of bowing to the ground, without an object; αἰτοῦσα. "From" is ἀπ' (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the printed SBLGNT has παρ' with the Byzantine text.
- **"What wilt thou?" → "What do you want?"** (v21) — Greek Τί θέλεις;
- **"Grant that these my two sons may sit" → "Say that these two sons of mine may sit"** (v21) — Greek Εἰπὲ ἵνα καθίσωσιν.
- **"on thy right hand … on the left" → "at your right hand … at your left"** (vv21, 23) — Greek ἐκ δεξιῶν, ἐξ εὐωνύμων; the Greek has no word "hand". In v23 "left" has no "my".
- **"the cup that I shall drink of" → "the cup which I am about to drink"** (v22) — Greek ὃ ἐγὼ μέλλω πίνειν; Mark 10:38 has "which I drink".
- **"and to be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with" omitted** (vv22–23) — in the Byzantine text only, from Mark 10:38–39.
- **"Ye shall drink indeed of my cup" → "My cup indeed you will drink"** (v23) — Greek Τὸ μὲν ποτήριόν μου πίεσθε.
- **"but it shall be given to them for whom it is prepared of my Father" → "but to those for whom it has been prepared by my Father"** (v23) — Greek ἀλλ' οἷς ἡτοίμασται ὑπὸ τοῦ πατρός μου; the Greek has no "it shall be given". NA28 reads "this is not mine to give" (+ τοῦτο). "By my Father" is not in Mark 10:40.
- **"moved with indignation against" → "indignant about"** (v24) — Greek ἠγανάκτησαν περί.
- **"the princes of the Gentiles exercise dominion over them, and they that are great exercise authority upon them" → "the rulers of the nations lord it over them, and the great ones exercise authority over them"** (v25) — Greek ἄρχοντες; κατακυριεύουσιν, κατεξουσιάζουσιν, as Mark 10:42. Mark has "those who are reputed to rule".
- **"it shall not be so" → "It will not be so"** (v26) — Greek ἔσται (NA28; the Byzantine text with δέ); Westcott-Hort and Tregelles read ἐστίν, "it is not so", as Mark 10:43.
- **"whosoever will be great … let him be your minister" → "whoever wants to become great … shall be your servant"** (v26) — Greek θέλῃ … γενέσθαι; ἔσται ὑμῶν διάκονος, a future.
- **"chief … let him be your servant" → "first … shall be your slave"** (v27) — Greek πρῶτος; ἔσται ὑμῶν δοῦλος (the Byzantine text has the imperative). Mark 10:44 has "slave of all".
- **"Even as" → "just as"** (v28) — Greek ὥσπερ; Mark 10:45 has "for even" (καὶ γάρ).
- **"to be ministered unto, but to minister" → "to be served but to serve"** (v28) — Greek διακονηθῆναι, διακονῆσαι, the verb of διάκονος (v26).
- **"his life" → "his soul"** (v28) — Greek τὴν ψυχὴν αὐτοῦ.
- **"a ransom for many" kept** (v28) — Greek λύτρον ἀντὶ πολλῶν; λύτρον is the price paid to set a captive or slave free; ἀντί is "in exchange for, in place of".
- **"as they departed from Jericho, a great multitude" → "as they were going out from *Yericho*, a great crowd"** (v29) — Greek ἐκπορευομένων; ὄχλος πολύς.
- **"by the way side" → "beside the way"** (v30) — Greek παρὰ τὴν ὁδόν.
- **"passed by" → "is passing by"** (v30) — Greek παράγει, a present.
- **"Have mercy on us, O Lord, thou Son of David" → "Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David"** (vv30–31) — the order of Westcott-Hort, Tregelles and the printed text; NA28 and the Byzantine text have "Have mercy on us, Lord". The Greek has no "O"; υἱός is a nominative used in address. Mark 10:46 has one blind man, *Bartimaios*.
- **"because they should hold their peace" → "that they should be silent"** (v31) — Greek ἵνα σιωπήσωσιν.
- **"stood still" → "standing still"** (v32) — Greek στάς.
- **"What will ye that I shall do unto you?" → "What do you want me to do for you?"** (v32) — Greek Τί θέλετε ποιήσω ὑμῖν;
- **"had compassion on them" → "moved with compassion"** (v34) — Greek σπλαγχνισθείς, from σπλάγχνα, "inward parts". "On them" is not in the Greek.
- **"their eyes" (twice)** (vv33–34) — two Greek words: ὀφθαλμοί in v33, ὄμματα in v34.
- **"their eyes received sight" → "they received sight"** (v34) — Greek ἀνέβλεψαν, "looked up, saw again"; "their eyes" is in the Byzantine text only.

### Notes
- **vv1–16** — only in Matthew. The hours are counted from sunrise: the third is mid-morning, the eleventh one hour before sunset.
- **v28** — "soul" (ψυχή) is here his life, given up in death; the same word as 16:25–26.
