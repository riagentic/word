# Matthew 5

[1] And seeing the crowds, he went up into the mountain; and when he had sat down, his disciples approached him;

[2] and opening his mouth he was teaching them, saying,

[3] "Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens.

[4] Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted.

[5] Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.

[6] Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be filled.

[7] Blessed are the merciful, for they shall be shown mercy.

[8] Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] Blessed are those who have been persecuted on account of righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens.

[11] Blessed are you when they reproach you and persecute you and say every evil thing against you, lying, on account of me.

[12] Rejoice and exult, for your wage is great in the heavens; for thus they persecuted the prophets who were before you.

[13] "You are the salt of the earth; but if the salt becomes tasteless, with what shall it be salted? It has strength for nothing any longer, except, being cast out, to be trampled down by men.

[14] "You are the light of the world. A city lying upon a mountain cannot be hidden;

[15] nor do they light a lamp and put it under the bushel measure, but upon the lampstand, and it shines for all those in the house.

[16] Thus let your light shine before men, so that they may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in the heavens.

[17] "Do not suppose that I came to tear down the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ or the Prophets; I did not come to tear down but to fulfill.

[18] For amen, I say to you, until heaven and earth pass away, one iota or one stroke shall by no means pass away from the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, until all things come to pass.

[19] Whoever therefore looses one of these least commandments and teaches men so shall be called least in the kingdom of the heavens; but whoever does and teaches, this one shall be called great in the kingdom of the heavens.

[20] For I say to you that unless your righteousness abounds more than that of the scribes and ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, you shall by no means enter into the kingdom of the heavens.

[21] "You heard that it was said to the ancients, 'You shall not murder; and whoever murders shall be liable to the judgment.'

[22] But I say to you that everyone who is angry with his brother shall be liable to the judgment; and whoever says to his brother, '⟦raqa|Raqa|Raca⟧,' shall be liable to the ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧; and whoever says, 'Fool,' shall be liable unto the ⟦gehenna|Gehenna|hell⟧ of fire.

[23] "If therefore you are offering your gift upon the altar and there remember that your brother has something against you,

[24] leave your gift there before the altar and go; first be reconciled to your brother, and then come and offer your gift.

[25] "Be well-disposed toward your adversary quickly, while you are with him on the way, lest the adversary deliver you up to the judge, and the judge to the officer, and you be cast into prison.

[26] Amen, I say to you, you shall by no means come out from there until you have rendered the last quadrans.

[27] "You heard that it was said, 'You shall not commit adultery.'

[28] But I say to you that everyone who looks at a woman in order to desire her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

[29] But if your right eye makes you stumble, take it out and cast it from you; for it is profitable for you that one of your members perish and not your whole body be cast into ⟦gehenna|Gehenna|hell⟧.

[30] And if your right hand makes you stumble, cut it off and cast it from you; for it is profitable for you that one of your members perish and not your whole body go away into ⟦gehenna|Gehenna|hell⟧.

[31] "And it was said, 'Whoever sends away his wife, let him give her a divorce.'

[32] But I say to you that everyone who sends away his wife, apart from a matter of sexual immorality, makes her commit adultery, and whoever marries a woman who has been sent away commits adultery.

[33] "Again, you heard that it was said to the ancients, 'You shall not swear falsely, but shall render to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ your oaths.'

[34] But I say to you not to swear at all: neither by the heaven, for it is the throne of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[35] nor by the earth, for it is the footstool of his feet, nor by ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for it is the city of the great King;

[36] nor shall you swear by your head, for you are not able to make one hair white or black.

[37] But let your word be 'Yes, yes,' 'No, no'; and what is more than these is of the evil one.

[38] "You heard that it was said, 'An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.'

[39] But I say to you not to resist the one who is evil; but whoever slaps you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also;

[40] and to the one who wants to go to law with you and take your tunic, leave him the garment also;

[41] and whoever shall press you into service for one mile, go with him two.

[42] To the one who asks you, give; and from the one who wants to borrow from you, do not turn away.

[43] "You heard that it was said, 'You shall love your neighbor and hate your enemy.'

[44] But I say to you, love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you,

[45] so that you may become sons of your Father who is in the heavens, for he makes his sun rise on evil and good and rains on righteous and unrighteous.

[46] For if you love those who love you, what wage do you have? Do not even the toll collectors do the same?

[47] And if you greet your brothers only, what more are you doing? Do not even the Gentiles do the same?

[48] You therefore shall be perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv8–9, 34) — Greek θεός *theos*; underlying Hebrew divine name preserved.
- **"he went up into a mountain: and when he was set, his disciples came unto him" → "he went up into the mountain; and when he had sat down, his disciples approached him"** (v1) — Greek εἰς τὸ ὄρος, with the article; προσῆλθαν, "approached" (as 4:3, 11).
- **"taught" → "was teaching"** (v2) — Greek ἐδίδασκεν, an imperfect.
- **"Blessed" kept** (v3–11) — Greek μακάριοι, without a verb ("are" is supplied); "happy, fortunate", of those whom God favours.
- **"the poor in spirit" kept** (v3) — Greek οἱ πτωχοὶ τῷ πνεύματι, "the poor in the spirit"; πτωχός is one who has nothing and must beg.
- **"the kingdom of heaven" → "the kingdom of the heavens"** (v3, 10, 19, 20) — Greek ἡ βασιλεία τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural, as 3:2; "in the heavens" (v12, 16, 45) is plural in Greek as well.
- **"comforted" kept** (v4) — Greek παρακληθήσονται, as 2:18.
- **"the earth" kept** (v5) — Greek τὴν γῆν, "the earth" or "the land" (Ps 37:11).
- **"which do hunger and thirst after righteousness" → "who hunger and thirst for righteousness"** (v6) — Greek τὴν δικαιοσύνην, a direct object with the article: "hunger and thirst the righteousness".
- **"they shall be filled" kept** (v6) — Greek χορτασθήσονται (as 14:20; Mark 6:42).
- **"they shall obtain mercy" → "they shall be shown mercy"** (v7) — Greek ἐλεηθήσονται, a passive.
- **"the children of God" → "sons of *Elohim*"** (v9) — Greek υἱοὶ θεοῦ, without articles; so also "sons of your Father" (v45).
- **"they which are persecuted for righteousness' sake" → "those who have been persecuted on account of righteousness"** (v10) — Greek οἱ δεδιωγμένοι, a perfect participle; ἕνεκεν, "on account of" (v11 also).
- **"when men shall revile you … and shall say all manner of evil against you falsely, for my sake" → "when they reproach you … and say every evil thing against you, lying, on account of me"** (v11) — Greek ὀνειδίσωσιν, "reproach"; no subject is named; πᾶν πονηρόν (RP adds ῥῆμα, "word"); ψευδόμενοι, "lying", a participle.
- **"be exceeding glad: for great is your reward in heaven" → "exult, for your wage is great in the heavens"** (v12, 46) — Greek ἀγαλλιᾶσθε; μισθός is "wage, pay" (as 20:8).
- **"if the salt have lost his savour, wherewith shall it be salted? it is thenceforth good for nothing, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men" → "if the salt becomes tasteless, with what shall it be salted? It has strength for nothing any longer, except, being cast out, to be trampled down by men"** (v13) — Greek μωρανθῇ, literally "is made foolish" (μωρός, "fool", v22): of salt, to become insipid; Mark 9:50 says "becomes saltless". ἰσχύει, "has strength"; βληθὲν ἔξω, a participle (WH Treg NA28).
- **"A city that is set on an hill" → "A city lying upon a mountain"** (v14) — Greek ἐπάνω ὄρους κειμένη; ὄρος is "mountain", as v1; κεῖμαι, "lie", as 3:10.
- **"Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all" → "nor do they light a lamp and put it under the bushel measure, but upon the lampstand, and it shines for all those"** (v15) — Greek καίουσιν λύχνον; ὑπὸ τὸν μόδιον, ἐπὶ τὴν λυχνίαν, with the articles (as Mark 4:21); λάμπει, "shines", the verb of v16.
- **"Let your light so shine before men" → "Thus let your light shine before men"** (v16) — Greek οὕτως, "thus", looks back to the lamp; τῶν ἀνθρώπων, "men, people". δοξάσωσιν, "glorify".
- **"Think not that I am come to destroy the law" → "Do not suppose that I came to tear down the *Torah*"** (v17) — Greek μὴ νομίσητε; ἦλθον, a simple past; καταλῦσαι, "loosen down, demolish" (of the temple, 26:61), and of a law "annul". The second καταλῦσαι and πληρῶσαι have no object in Greek ("them" is not supplied).
- **"verily" → "amen"** (v18, 26) — Greek ἀμήν, the Hebrew word, left as the Greek leaves it.
- **"one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled" → "one iota or one stroke shall by no means pass away from the *Torah*, until all things come to pass"** (v18) — Greek ἰῶτα ἓν ἢ μία κεραία: the smallest letter and a "little horn", a stroke of a letter; οὐ μή, the strongest "not"; γένηται, "happen, come to pass" (not πληρόω, "fulfill", v17).
- **"shall break one of these least commandments, and shall teach men so" → "looses one of these least commandments and teaches men so"** (v19) — Greek λύσῃ, "loose" (as 16:19), here to make a commandment not binding; the verb inside καταλῦσαι (v17).
- **"shall do and teach them" → "does and teaches"** (v19) — the Greek has no object.
- **"except your righteousness shall exceed the righteousness of the scribes" → "unless your righteousness abounds more than that of the scribes"** (v20) — Greek περισσεύσῃ … πλεῖον τῶν γραμματέων, "abounds more than the scribes"; "that of" is supplied. οὐ μὴ εἰσέλθητε, "by no means".
- **"Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time" → "You heard that it was said to the ancients"** (v21, 27, 33, 38, 43) — Greek ἠκούσατε, a simple past; τοῖς ἀρχαίοις, a dative: "to the ancients" (it can also be read "by").
- **"kill" → "murder"** (v21) — Greek φονεύσεις *phoneuseis* = commit murder (not kill generally). The sixth commandment (Exod 20:13) prohibits unlawful killing; "murder" is the accurate translation.
- **"in danger of the judgment" → "liable to the judgment"** (v21, 22) — Greek ἔνοχος τῇ κρίσει, τῷ συνεδρίῳ, datives: answerable to a court; with a genitive the word is "guilty of" (26:66). The third time it is εἰς τὴν γέενναν τοῦ πυρός, "unto the Gehenna of fire".
- **"whosoever is angry with his brother without a cause" → "everyone who is angry with his brother"** (v22) — editions: αὐτοῦ WH NA28 ] + εἰκῆ, "without cause", Treg RP — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides.
- **"Thou fool" → "Fool"** (v22) — Greek Μωρέ, one word; the Greek has no "You" and no exclamation mark.
- **"bring thy gift to the altar … rememberest" → "are offering your gift upon the altar … remember"** (v23, 24) — Greek προσφέρῃς … ἐπὶ τὸ θυσιαστήριον; προσφέρω is "offer" in both verses.
- **"go thy way; first be reconciled" → "go; first be reconciled"** (v24) — Greek ὕπαγε πρῶτον διαλλάγηθι: "first" can go with "go" or with "be reconciled".
- **"Agree with thine adversary quickly, whiles thou art in the way with him" → "Be well-disposed toward your adversary quickly, while you are with him on the way"** (v25) — Greek ἴσθι εὐνοῶν, "be well-minded"; ἀντίδικος, the opponent in a lawsuit; the Greek has no "to court".
- **"deliver thee to the judge, and the judge deliver thee to the officer" → "deliver you up to the judge, and the judge to the officer"** (v25) — Greek παραδῷ once (Treg RP repeat it); ὑπηρέτης, "officer".
- **"till thou hast paid the uttermost farthing" → "until you have rendered the last quadrans"** (v26) — Greek ἀποδῷς, "give back what is owed" (the verb of v33); κοδράντην, the Roman *quadrans*, the smallest copper coin (Mark 12:42).
- **"whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her" → "everyone who looks at a woman in order to desire her"** (v28) — Greek πρὸς τὸ ἐπιθυμῆσαι αὐτήν, a purpose; ἐπιθυμέω is "desire" (the verb of "You shall not covet", Rom 7:7). γυναῖκα is "woman" or "wife".
- **"offend thee" → "makes you stumble"; "pluck it out" → "take it out"; "cast it from thee" kept** (v29, 30) — Greek σκανδαλίζει, as Mark 9:43–47; ἔξελε (Mark 9:47: ἔκβαλε, "cast it out"); ἔκκοψον (Mark 9:43: ἀπόκοψον), both "cut it off".
- **"it is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish, and not that thy whole body should be cast into hell" kept** (v29, 30) — Greek συμφέρει γάρ σοι ἵνα ἀπόληται ἓν τῶν μελῶν σου; v30 ends εἰς γέενναν ἀπέλθῃ, "go away into Gehenna" (WH Treg NA28; RP repeats "be cast").
- **"put away" → "send away"; "a writing of divorcement" → "a divorce"** (v31, 32) — Greek ἀπολύω, "send away, release" (as 1:19; Mark 10:2–12): to divorce. ἀποστάσιον is the deed of divorce itself, one word; Mark 10:4 and Matt 19:7 have βιβλίον ἀποστασίου, "a document of divorce" (Deut 24:1).
- **"saving for the cause of fornication" → "apart from a matter of sexual immorality"** (v32) — Greek παρεκτὸς λόγου πορνείας: λόγος, elsewhere "word", is here "matter, ground" (compare Deut 24:1, "a matter of nakedness"); πορνεία is unlawful sexual intercourse of any kind, a wider word than "adultery" (μοιχεία).
- **"causeth her to commit adultery" → "makes her commit adultery"** (v32) — Greek ποιεῖ αὐτὴν μοιχευθῆναι, a passive infinitive (the form used of the woman): literally "makes her to have adultery committed with her". The last word, μοιχᾶται, is another verb of the same root. Treg NA28 WH read "everyone who sends away" (πᾶς ὁ ἀπολύων); RP "whoever sends away".
- **"her that is divorced" → "a woman who has been sent away"** (v32) — Greek ἀπολελυμένην, a perfect participle of ἀπολύω.
- **"Thou shalt not forswear thyself, but shalt perform unto the Lord thine oaths" → "You shall not swear falsely, but shall render to *Yahweh* your oaths"** (v33) — Greek ἐπιορκήσεις, "swear falsely" or "break an oath"; ἀποδώσεις, "give back what is owed"; τοὺς ὅρκους σου, "your oaths". τῷ κυρίῳ stands for the Name (Num 30:2; Deut 23:21).
- **"Swear not at all" → "not to swear at all"** (v34, 39) — Greek μὴ ὀμόσαι, μὴ ἀντιστῆναι: infinitives after "I say to you". "by" is ἐν three times and εἰς, "toward", with Jerusalem.
- **"his footstool" → "the footstool of his feet"** (v35) — Greek ὑποπόδιον τῶν ποδῶν αὐτοῦ (Isa 66:1).
- **"let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil" → "let your word be 'Yes, yes,' 'No, no'; and what is more than these is of the evil one"** (v37) — Greek ὁ λόγος ὑμῶν; ναὶ ναί, οὒ οὔ, each word twice; ἐκ τοῦ πονηροῦ can be masculine, "of the evil one", or neuter, "of evil" (as 6:13).
- **"resist not evil" → "not to resist the one who is evil"** (v39) — Greek τῷ πονηρῷ, masculine or neuter: "the evil person" or "evil"; what follows speaks of persons.
- **"smite thee on thy right cheek" → "slaps you on your right cheek"** (v39) — Greek ῥαπίζει, a present tense. Editions: σιαγόνα RP (and the printed SBLGNT) ] + σου, "your", WH Treg NA28 — the verse follows the majority.
- **"if any man will sue thee at the law, and take away thy coat, let him have thy cloke also" → "to the one who wants to go to law with you and take your tunic, leave him the garment also"** (v40) — Greek τῷ θέλοντί σοι κριθῆναι, "wanting to be judged with you"; χιτών (the garment worn next to the body) and ἱμάτιον (the outer garment); ἄφες, "leave, let go" (the verb of v24).
- **"shall compel thee to go a mile" → "shall press you into service for one mile"** (v41) — Greek ἀγγαρεύσει, a word for forced service to the state (as 27:32; Mark 15:21); μίλιον, the Roman mile.
- **"from him that would borrow of thee turn not thou away" kept** (v42) — Greek αἰτοῦντι, "asks"; μὴ ἀποστραφῇς.
- **"that ye may be the children of your Father which is in heaven" → "so that you may become sons of your Father who is in the heavens"** (v45) — Greek γένησθε, "become"; ἐν οὐρανοῖς.
- **"on the evil and on the good … on the just and on the unjust" → "on evil and good … on righteous and unrighteous"** (v45) — the Greek has no articles and one "on" for each pair; δίκαιος is "righteous" (as 1:19). βρέχει, "rains".
- **"publican" → "toll collector"** (v46) — Greek τελῶναι *telōnai*, those who collected tolls and customs (as Mark 2:15); in v47 KJV repeats "publicans", but the critical text reads ἐθνικοί "Gentiles", and "your brothers" where the KJV has "your friends" (RP).
- **"what do ye more than others?" → "what more are you doing?"** (v47) — Greek τί περισσὸν ποιεῖτε, "what that is more"; περισσόν as in v37. The Greek has no "than others".
- **"Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven" → "You therefore shall be perfect, as your heavenly Father"** (v48) — Greek ἔσεσθε, a future; ὁ πατὴρ ὑμῶν ὁ οὐράνιος (WH Treg NA28).
- **The longer v44 of the KJV** ("bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you … which despitefully use you") is the Byzantine reading (RP); WH Treg NA28 do not have it.

### Notes
- **v22 Gehenna** — the burning Valley of Hinnom outside Jerusalem; image of final judgment (not "hell" as cosmological place).
- **v22 *Raqa*** (Greek ῥακά, retained untranslated): Aramaic insult "empty-head" (→ Sanhedrin); distinct from μωρέ "Fool" (→ Gehenna).
