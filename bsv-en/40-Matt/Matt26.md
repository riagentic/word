# Matthew 26

[1] And it came to pass, when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ finished all these words, he said to his disciples,

[2] "You know that after two days the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ comes, and the Son of Man is delivered up to be crucified."

[3] Then the high priests and the elders of the people were gathered together into the court of the high priest, the one called ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧,

[4] and they counseled together that they might take hold of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ by guile and kill him.

[5] But they were saying, "Not in the feast, that a commotion may not arise among the people."

[6] Now when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had come to be in ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧, in the house of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ the leper,

[7] there approached him a woman having an alabaster flask of ointment of great price, and she poured it down upon his head as he was reclining.

[8] But seeing it, the disciples were indignant, saying, "For what is this waste?

[9] For this could have been sold for much and given to poor people."

[10] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, knowing it, said to them, "Why do you give troubles to the woman? For she has worked a good work toward me;

[11] for the poor you always have with yourselves, but me you do not always have.

[12] For she, putting this ointment upon my body, did it to prepare me for burial.

[13] Amen, I say to you, wherever this gospel is proclaimed in the whole world, also what this woman did will be spoken of for a memorial of her."

[14] Then one of the twelve, the one called ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ Iscariot, going to the high priests,

[15] said, "What do you want to give me, and I will deliver him up to you?" And they set for him thirty silver pieces.

[16] And from then he was seeking an opportunity that he might deliver him up.

[17] Now on the first day of the ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ the disciples approached ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, saying, "Where do you want us to make ready for you to eat the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧?"

[18] And he said, "Go into the city to such a one and say to him, 'The Teacher says, "My time is near; with you I keep the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ with my disciples."'"

[19] And the disciples did as ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ directed them, and they made ready the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[20] And when evening had come, he was reclining with the twelve.

[21] And as they were eating, he said, "Amen, I say to you that one of you will deliver me up."

[22] And being exceedingly grieved, they began to say to him, each one, "Surely it is not I, Lord?"

[23] But he, answering, said, "The one who dipped the hand with me in the bowl, this one will deliver me up.

[24] The Son of Man indeed goes, even as it is written about him, but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is delivered up! It would have been good for him if that man had not been born."

[25] And ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, the one delivering him up, answering, said, "Surely it is not I, Rabbi?" He says to him, "You have said it."

[26] Now as they were eating, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, taking bread and having blessed, broke it and, giving to the disciples, said, "Take, eat; this is my body."

[27] And taking a cup and having given thanks, he gave to them, saying, "Drink from it, all of you,

[28] for this is my blood of the covenant, which is being poured out for many unto forgiveness of sins.

[29] But I say to you, I shall by no means drink from now on from this produce of the vine until that day when I drink it new with you in the kingdom of my Father."

[30] And having sung a hymn, they went out to ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧.

[31] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "You will all be made to stumble in me in this night, for it is written, '**I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered**.'

[32] But after I have been raised, I will go before you into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧."

[33] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, answering, said to him, "If all will be made to stumble in you, I will never be made to stumble."

[34] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Amen, I say to you that in this night, before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times."

[35] ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ says to him, "Even if I must die with you, I will by no means deny you." Likewise also all the disciples said.

[36] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ comes with them to a plot of land called ⟦gethsemane|Gethsemani|Gethsemane⟧, and he says to the disciples, "Sit here while I go away and pray there."

[37] And taking along ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and the two sons of ⟦zebedee|Zebedaios|Zebedee⟧, he began to be grieved and distressed.

[38] Then he says to them, "My soul is deeply grieved, unto death; remain here and be awake with me."

[39] And going forward a little, he fell upon his face, praying and saying, "My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass away from me; yet not as I want, but as you."

[40] And he comes to the disciples and finds them sleeping, and he says to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "So, did you not have strength to be awake one hour with me?

[41] Be awake and pray, that you may not enter into trial; the spirit indeed is ready, but the flesh is weak."

[42] Again, a second time, going away he prayed, saying, "My Father, if this cannot pass away unless I drink it, let your will be done."

[43] And coming again he found them sleeping, for their eyes were weighed down.

[44] And leaving them, again going away he prayed a third time, saying the same word again.

[45] Then he comes to the disciples and says to them, "Sleep on now and rest. Behold, the hour has drawn near, and the Son of Man is delivered up into the hands of sinners.

[46] Arise, let us go; behold, the one delivering me up has drawn near."

[47] And while he was still speaking, behold, ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great crowd with swords and clubs, from the high priests and elders of the people.

[48] Now the one delivering him up gave them a sign, saying, "Whomever I kiss, he it is; take hold of him."

[49] And immediately approaching ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ he said, "Hail, Rabbi," and he kissed him.

[50] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Companion, that for which you are here —" Then, approaching, they laid hands on ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and took hold of him.

[51] And behold, one of those with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, stretching out the hand, drew out his sword and, striking the slave of the high priest, took off his ear.

[52] Then ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "**Turn your sword back into its place, for all who take a sword will perish by a sword.**

[53] Or do you think that I am not able to appeal to my Father, and he will present to me now more than twelve legions of angels?

[54] How then should the Scriptures be fulfilled, that thus it must come to pass?"

[55] In that hour ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to the crowds, "As against a robber you came out with swords and clubs to apprehend me? Daily in the temple I was sitting teaching, and you did not take hold of me.

[56] But this whole thing has come to pass that the Scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled." Then the disciples all, leaving him, fled.

[57] Now those who had taken hold of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ led him away to ⟦caiaphas|Qayafa|Caiaphas⟧ the high priest, where the scribes and the elders were gathered together.

[58] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was following him from afar, as far as the court of the high priest, and entering inside he was sitting with the officers to see the end.

[59] Now the high priests and the whole ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧ were seeking false testimony against ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, so that they might put him to death,

[60] and they did not find any, though many false witnesses approached. But afterward two, approaching,

[61] said, "This one said, 'I am able to tear down the sanctuary of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and in three days to build it.'"

[62] And the high priest, rising, said to him, "Do you answer nothing? What is it that these testify against you?"

[63] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was silent. And the high priest said to him, "I adjure you by the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that you tell us if you are the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[64] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "**You have said it. Yet I say to you, from now on you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of the Power and coming upon the clouds of heaven.**"

[65] Then the high priest tore his garments, saying, "He blasphemed! What need do we still have of witnesses? See, now you heard the blasphemy.

[66] What do you think?" And they, answering, said, "He is guilty of death."

[67] Then they spat into his face and buffeted him, and some slapped him,

[68] saying, "Prophesy to us, ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧: who is the one who struck you?"

[69] Now ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was sitting outside in the court, and one slave woman approached him, saying, "You also were with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the Galilean."

[70] But he denied before all, saying, "I do not know what you are saying."

[71] And when he had gone out into the gateway, another woman saw him, and she says to those there, "This one was with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazorean⟧."

[72] And again he denied with an oath, "I do not know the man."

[73] And after a little, those standing there, approaching, said to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "Truly you also are of them, for your speech also makes you evident."

[74] Then he began to curse and to swear, "I do not know the man." And immediately a rooster crowed.

[75] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ remembered the word of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, who had said, "Before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times." And going out outside, he wept bitterly.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v61, 63), **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v63, 68) — systematic. In v68 χριστέ is a bare vocative: "*Mashiach*", with no "you" or "thou".
- **"sayings" → "words"** (v1) — Greek λόγους. The formula καὶ ἐγένετο ὅτε ἐτέλεσεν closes each of the five discourses (7:28; 11:1; 13:53; 19:1; 26:1); only here "all".
- **"is the feast of the passover" → "the Pesach comes"** (v2) — Greek τὸ πάσχα γίνεται; "feast" is not in the Greek. **"is betrayed" → "is delivered up"** (v2, 15, 16, 21, 23, 24, 25, 45, 46, 48) — Greek παραδίδωμι, "hand over, deliver up", the one verb of the whole passion account (also of the high priests, 27:2, and of Pilatus, 27:26); "betray" would be προδίδωμι.
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v3, 14, 47, 59) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of the word for "the high priest" in the same verse. The Byzantine text adds "and the scribes" in v3.
- **"palace" → "court"** (v3, 58, 69) — Greek αὐλή, the open court of the house. **"assembled together" → "were gathered together"** (v3, 57) — Greek συνήχθησαν, passive.
- **"consulted" → "counseled together"** (v4) — Greek συνεβουλεύσαντο. **"subtilty" → "guile"** — Greek δόλῳ. **"take" → "take hold of"** (v4, 48, 50, 55, 57) — Greek κρατέω; "arrest" is not in the word.
- **"on the feast day" → "in the feast"** (v5) — Greek ἐν τῇ ἑορτῇ. **"uproar" → "commotion"** — Greek θόρυβος.
- **"was" → "had come to be"** (v6) — Greek γενομένου (Mark 14:3 has ὄντος, "while he was").
- **"very precious" → "of great price"** (v7) — Greek βαρυτίμου, "heavy in price" (Mark 14:3 has πολυτελοῦς, "very costly"). **"as he sat at meat" → "as he was reclining"** (v7, 20) — Greek ἀνάκειμαι.
- **"To what purpose is this waste?" → "For what is this waste?"** (v8) — Greek Εἰς τί ἡ ἀπώλεια αὕτη; the noun is elsewhere "destruction". Here "the disciples" are indignant; in Mark 14:4 "some".
- **"to the poor" → "to poor people"** (v9) — Greek πτωχοῖς without the article (Mark 14:5 has it); in v11 τοὺς πτωχούς, "the poor".
- **"When Jesus understood it" → "knowing it"** (v10) — Greek γνούς. **"trouble ye" → "give troubles to"** — Greek κόπους παρέχετε. **"a good work upon me" → "a good work toward me"** — Greek εἰς ἐμέ (Mark 14:6 has ἐν ἐμοί, "in me"); "good" is καλόν.
- **"for my burial" → "to prepare me for burial"** (v12) — Greek πρὸς τὸ ἐνταφιάσαι με; ἐνταφιάζω is to prepare a body for the tomb (John 19:40). "Putting" is βαλοῦσα, "casting".
- **"Verily" → "Amen"** (v13, 21, 34) — Greek ἀμήν, left untranslated. **"be told" → "be spoken of"** (v13) — Greek λαληθήσεται.
- **"What will ye give me" → "What do you want to give me"** (v15) — Greek Τί θέλετέ μοι δοῦναι. **"they covenanted with him for" → "they set for him"** — Greek ἔστησαν αὐτῷ, "set, placed": either they weighed out the silver on the scale (the wording of Zech 11:12 in the Greek Old Testament) or they fixed that sum for him. **"pieces of silver" → "silver pieces"** — Greek ἀργύρια (Mark 14:11 has the singular ἀργύριον, "silver").
- **"sought opportunity" → "was seeking an opportunity"** (v16) — Greek ἐζήτει, imperfect.
- **"the first day of the feast of unleavened bread" → "the first day of the Matzot"** (v17) — Greek τῇ πρώτῃ τῶν ἀζύμων; "feast" is not in the Greek, and "day" is supplied here (Mark 14:12 has ἡμέρᾳ). **"prepare" → "make ready"** (v17, 19) — Greek ἑτοιμάζω.
- **"to such a man" → "to such a one"** (v18) — Greek πρὸς τὸν δεῖνα, "to so-and-so": the name is withheld. **"at hand" → "near"** — Greek ἐγγύς. **"I will keep" → "I keep"** — Greek ποιῶ, present; πρὸς σέ is "with you, at your house".
- **"had appointed" → "directed"** (v19) — Greek συνέταξεν.
- **"with the twelve"** (v20) — "disciples" (μαθητῶν, printed in SBLGNT with WH) is not in Tregelles, NA28 or the Byzantine text, and the verse follows the majority.
- **"exceeding sorrowful" → "exceedingly grieved"** (v22), **"sorrowful" → "grieved"** (v37), **"exceeding sorrowful" → "deeply grieved"** (v38) — Greek λυπούμενοι σφόδρα, λυπεῖσθαι, περίλυπος: one root. **"every one of them" → "each one"** — Greek εἷς ἕκαστος. **"Lord, is it I?" → "Surely it is not I, Lord?"** (v22, 25) — Greek Μήτι ἐγώ εἰμι, a question that expects "no". The eleven say "Lord", Judas "Rabbi".
- **"He that dippeth" → "The one who dipped"** (v23) — Greek ὁ ἐμβάψας, aorist (Mark 14:20: present, "the one dipping"). **"dish" → "bowl"** — Greek τρύβλιον.
- **"goeth as it is written of him" → "goes, even as it is written about him"** (v24) — as Mark 14:21. **"it had been good" → "It would have been good"** — Greek καλὸν ἦν; Mark has no verb.
- **"which betrayed him" → "the one delivering him up"** (v25, 46, 48) — Greek ὁ παραδιδούς, present participle. **"Thou hast said" → "You have said it"** (v25, 64) — Greek Σὺ εἶπας, with the emphatic "you" and no object ("it" is supplied, as in "You say it", 27:11; Mark 15:2): the answer hands the words back to the one who asked; it is an assent in form, not a plain "yes" (Mark 14:62 has "I am").
- **"blessed it … gave it" → "having blessed … giving"** (v26) — Greek εὐλογήσας … δούς: no object; "it" after "broke" is supplied. The Byzantine text has "having given thanks".
- **"the cup" → "a cup"** (v27) — Greek ποτήριον without the article. **"Drink ye all of it" → "Drink from it, all of you"** — Greek πίετε ἐξ αὐτοῦ πάντες: "all" is the persons.
- **"my blood of the new testament" → "my blood of the covenant"** (v28) — Greek τὸ αἷμά μου τῆς διαθήκης (WH, NA28); "new" (καινῆς) is read by Tregelles and the Byzantine text and is not in the verse. **"is shed" → "is being poured out"** — Greek ἐκχυννόμενον, present participle. **"for many"** — Greek περὶ πολλῶν, "concerning, for many" (Mark 14:24 has ὑπὲρ πολλῶν). **"for the remission" → "unto forgiveness"** — Greek εἰς ἄφεσιν ἁμαρτιῶν, as Mark 1:4; only Matthew has these words here.
- **"I will not drink henceforth of this fruit" → "I shall by no means drink from now on from this produce"** (v29) — Greek οὐ μὴ πίω ἀπ᾽ ἄρτι ἐκ τούτου τοῦ γενήματος; γένημα is "produce", not καρπός "fruit".
- **"shall be offended because of me" → "will be made to stumble in me"** (v31, 33) — Greek σκανδαλισθήσεσθε ἐν ἐμοί, as Mark 6:3. **"smite" → "strike"** — Greek πατάξω; the quotation is Zech 13:7 as Matthew's Greek gives it ("of the flock" is not in Mark 14:27).
- **"after I am risen again" → "after I have been raised"** (v32) — Greek μετὰ τὸ ἐγερθῆναί με, passive.
- **"Though I should die with thee" → "Even if I must die with you"** (v35) — Greek Κἂν δέῃ. **"yet will I not" → "I will by no means"** — Greek οὐ μή. **"Likewise"** — Greek ὁμοίως, as 27:41; Mark 15:31 (Mark 14:31 has ὡσαύτως here, also "likewise"). "Said" is aorist (Mark: "were saying").
- **"a place" → "a plot of land"** (v36) — Greek χωρίον, as Mark 14:32. **"while I go and pray yonder" → "while I go away and pray there"** — Greek ἕως οὗ ἀπελθὼν ἐκεῖ προσεύξωμαι; "there" can go with either verb.
- **"took with him" → "taking along"** (v37) — Greek παραλαβών. **"very heavy" → "distressed"** — Greek ἀδημονεῖν.
- **"tarry ye here, and watch" → "remain here and be awake"** (v38, 40, 41) — Greek μείνατε, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a stay in a place: "remain"; γρηγορεῖτε.
- **"went a little farther" → "going forward a little"** (v39) — Greek προελθών (WH, NA28; Tregelles and the Byzantine text: προσελθών, "approaching"). **"pass" → "pass away"** (v39, 42) — Greek παρέρχομαι. **"nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt" → "yet not as I want, but as you"** — Greek πλὴν οὐχ ὡς ἐγὼ θέλω ἀλλ᾽ ὡς σύ: no second verb.
- **"What, could ye not" → "So, did you not have strength"** (v40) — Greek Οὕτως οὐκ ἰσχύσατε; the verb is plural, though the words are spoken to Kefa.
- **"enter not into temptation" → "may not enter into trial"** (v41) — Greek πειρασμός (Mark 14:38 has "come into"). **"willing" → "ready"** — Greek πρόθυμον.
- **"thy will be done" kept** (v42) — Greek γενηθήτω τὸ θέλημά σου, the words of 6:10. The Byzantine text adds "cup" and "from me".
- **"heavy" → "weighed down"** (v43) — Greek βεβαρημένοι, perfect passive participle.
- **"the same words" → "the same word"** (v44) — Greek τὸν αὐτὸν λόγον, singular.
- **"Sleep on now, and take your rest"** (v45) — Greek Καθεύδετε τὸ λοιπὸν καὶ ἀναπαύεσθε, as Mark 14:41; it can also be read as a question ("Are you still sleeping and resting?"). **"is at hand" → "has drawn near"** (v45, 46) — Greek ἤγγικεν, perfect.
- **"Rise" → "Arise"** (v46) — Greek ἐγείρεσθε.
- **"staves" → "clubs"** (v47, 55) — Greek ξύλων, "pieces of wood".
- **"Whomsoever I shall kiss, that same is he: hold him fast" → "Whomever I kiss, he it is; take hold of him"** (v48) — as Mark 14:44; here σημεῖον "sign" (Mark: σύσσημον "signal") and the aorist "gave" (Mark: "had given"). "Kiss" is φιλήσω; in v49 the compound κατεφίλησεν.
- **"Hail, master" → "Hail, Rabbi"** (v49), **"Master" → "Rabbi"** (v25) — Greek ῥαββί. "Hail" is Χαῖρε, "rejoice", the common greeting.
- **"Friend, wherefore art thou come?" → "Companion, that for which you are here —"** (v50) — Greek Ἑταῖρε, ἐφ᾽ ὃ πάρει: a relative clause with no main verb, left as it stands. It is read either as a command with the verb unsaid ("do that for which you are here"), or as a question (so the KJV), or as an exclamation ("for this you are here!"). ἑταῖρος is "companion" (20:13; 22:12); "friend" is φίλος. The Byzantine text has ἐφ᾽ ᾧ.
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v51) — Greek δοῦλον. **"smote off" → "took off"** — Greek ἀφεῖλεν, as Mark 14:47. "Drew out" is ἀπέσπασεν.
- **"Put up again thy sword" → "Turn your sword back"** (v52) — Greek Ἀπόστρεψον. **"with the sword" → "by a sword"** — Greek ἐν μαχαίρῃ, without the article.
- **"pray to" → "appeal to"** (v53) — Greek παρακαλέσαι. **"give me" → "present to me"** — Greek παραστήσει. **"presently" → "now"** — Greek ἄρτι.
- **"that thus it must be" → "that thus it must come to pass"** (v54) — Greek ὅτι οὕτως δεῖ γενέσθαι.
- **"a thief" → "a robber"** (v55) — Greek λῃστής, also "insurrectionist". **"to take me" → "to apprehend me"** — Greek συλλαβεῖν. "With you" (πρὸς ὑμᾶς) after "daily" is read by Tregelles and the Byzantine text, not by WH and NA28.
- **"all this was done" → "this whole thing has come to pass"** (v56) — Greek τοῦτο δὲ ὅλον γέγονεν, perfect. The sentence may be the words of Yeshua (so here, as in Mark 14:49) or of the evangelist (as 1:22; 21:4). **"forsook" → "leaving"** — Greek ἀφέντες.
- **"servants" → "officers"** (v58) — Greek ὑπηρέται.
- **"council" → "Sanhedrin"** (v59) — Greek συνέδριον. **"false witness" → "false testimony"** — Greek ψευδομαρτυρίαν (Mark 14:55 has "testimony"). The Byzantine text adds "and the elders".
- **"At the last" → "afterward"** (v60) — Greek ὕστερον. The Byzantine text reads "two false witnesses".
- **"destroy" → "tear down"** (v61) — Greek καταλῦσαι. **"to build it in three days" → "in three days to build it"** — Greek διὰ τριῶν ἡμερῶν οἰκοδομῆσαι; "it" is supplied (the Byzantine text has it). "Sanctuary" is ναός, the temple building itself; "temple" in v55 is ἱερόν, the whole precinct.
- **"held his peace" → "was silent"** (v63) — Greek ἐσιώπα, imperfect. **"that thou tell us whether" → "that you tell us if"** — Greek ἵνα ἡμῖν εἴπῃς εἰ.
- **"Hereafter" → "from now on"** (v64) — Greek ἀπ᾽ ἄρτι, as v29. **"nevertheless" → "yet"** — Greek πλήν, as v39. **"of power" → "of the Power"** — Greek τῆς δυνάμεως, a name used in place of the name of God. **"in the clouds" → "upon the clouds"** — Greek ἐπὶ τῶν νεφελῶν (Mark 14:62: μετά, "with").
- **"rent his clothes" → "tore his garments"** (v65) — Greek τὰ ἱμάτια (Mark 14:63: τοὺς χιτῶνας, "tunics"). **"He hath spoken blasphemy" → "He blasphemed"** — Greek Ἐβλασφήμησεν, one word, aorist. "See" is ἴδε.
- **"What think ye?"** (v66) — Greek τί ὑμῖν δοκεῖ, "what seems to you?" (Mark 14:64: φαίνεται). **"guilty of death"** — Greek ἔνοχος θανάτου.
- **"spit in his face" → "spat into his face"** (v67) — Greek εἰς τὸ πρόσωπον. **"buffeted"** — Greek ἐκολάφισαν, struck with the fist. **"smote him with the palms of their hands" → "slapped"** — Greek ἐράπισαν, to slap, or to strike with a rod.
- **"smote" → "struck"** (v68) — Greek ὁ παίσας (παίω); in v31, 51 πατάσσω.
- **"a damsel" → "one slave woman"** (v69) — Greek μία παιδίσκη. In v71 "another" is feminine (ἄλλη): "woman" is supplied.
- **"I know not what thou sayest" → "I do not know what you are saying"** (v70) — as Mark 14:68. "It" after "denied" is not in the Greek.
- **"porch" → "gateway"** (v71) — Greek πυλών (Mark 14:68: προαύλιον, "forecourt"). **"Jesus of Nazareth" → "Yeshua the Nazorean"** — Greek Ἰησοῦ τοῦ Ναζωραίου, an adjective (as 2:23; John 18:5); the place name itself is not in the verse.
- **"Surely thou also art one of them; for thy speech bewrayeth thee" → "Truly you also are of them, for your speech also makes you evident"** (v73) — Greek Ἀληθῶς καὶ σὺ ἐξ αὐτῶν εἶ, καὶ γὰρ ἡ λαλιά σου δῆλόν σε ποιεῖ; "one" is not in the Greek.
- **"to curse and to swear"** (v74) — Greek καταθεματίζειν, "to call down curses" (Mark 14:71: ἀναθεματίζειν), with no object. **"the cock" → "a rooster"** (v34, 74, 75) — no article. Matthew has one crowing; Mark 14:30, 72 "twice".
- **"the word of Jesus, which said unto him" → "the word of Yeshua, who had said"** (v75) — Greek τοῦ ῥήματος Ἰησοῦ εἰρηκότος; "to him" is only in the Byzantine text. **"went out" → "going out outside"** — Greek ἐξελθὼν ἔξω.

### Notes
- **v31** quotes Zech 13:7.
- **v64** echoes Dan 7:13 and Ps 110:1.
- **v15** — thirty silver pieces: compare Zech 11:12 and Exod 21:32.
