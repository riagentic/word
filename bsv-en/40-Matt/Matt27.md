# Matthew 27

[1] Now when morning had come, all the high priests and the elders of the people took counsel against ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, so as to put him to death;

[2] and having bound him, they led him away and delivered him up to ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ the governor.

[3] Then ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, the one delivering him up, seeing that he was condemned, regretting it, returned the thirty silver pieces to the high priests and elders,

[4] saying, "I sinned, delivering up innocent blood." But they said, "What is it to us? You will see to it."

[5] And throwing the silver pieces into the sanctuary, he withdrew, and going away he hanged himself.

[6] But the high priests, taking the silver pieces, said, "It is not permitted to put them into the treasury, since it is the price of blood."

[7] And taking counsel, they bought with them the Field of the Potter, for a burial place for the strangers.

[8] Therefore that field was called Field of Blood, until today.

[9] Then was fulfilled what was spoken through ⟦jeremiah-nt|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet, saying, "And they took the thirty silver pieces, the price of the one priced, whom they priced from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[10] and they gave them for the field of the potter, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ directed me."

[11] Now ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, saying, "Are you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?" And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "You say it."

[12] And while he was being accused by the high priests and elders, he answered nothing.

[13] Then ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ says to him, "Do you not hear how many things they testify against you?"

[14] And he did not answer him, not even to one word, so that the governor marveled greatly.

[15] Now at each feast the governor was accustomed to release to the crowd one prisoner, whom they wanted.

[16] And they had then a notable prisoner called ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧.

[17] So when they were gathered together, ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to them, "Whom do you want me to release to you: ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧, or ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the one called ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧?"

[18] For he knew that because of envy they delivered him up.

[19] Now while he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying, "Have nothing to do with that righteous one, for I suffered many things today in a dream because of him."

[20] But the high priests and the elders persuaded the crowds that they should ask for ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧ and destroy ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[21] And the governor, answering, said to them, "Which of the two do you want me to release to you?" And they said, "⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧."

[22] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ says to them, "What then shall I do with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the one called ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧?" They all say, "Let him be crucified!"

[23] But he said, "For what evil did he do?" But they were crying out exceedingly, saying, "Let him be crucified!"

[24] And ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧, seeing that he profits nothing, but rather a commotion is arising, taking water, washed off his hands before the crowd, saying, "I am innocent of the blood of this one; you will see to it."

[25] And answering, all the people said, "His blood upon us and upon our children!"

[26] Then he released ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧ to them, but ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, having flogged him, he delivered up that he might be crucified.

[27] Then the soldiers of the governor, taking ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ along into the praetorium, gathered together upon him the whole cohort.

[28] And stripping him, they put a scarlet cloak around him,

[29] and having woven a crown out of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand, and kneeling before him they mocked him, saying, "Hail, King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧!"

[30] And spitting upon him, they took the reed and were beating him on his head.

[31] And when they had mocked him, they took the cloak off him and put his garments on him, and led him away to crucify him.

[32] And going out, they found a man of ⟦cyrene|Kyrene|Cyrene⟧, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ by name; this one they pressed into service, that he might take up his cross.

[33] And coming to a place called **⟦golgotha|Gulgolta|Golgotha⟧**, which is called Place of a Skull,

[34] they gave him wine to drink, mixed with gall; and tasting it, he did not want to drink.

[35] And having crucified him, they divided his garments among themselves, casting a lot,

[36] and sitting, they were keeping him there.

[37] And they put above his head his charge, written, "**This is ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧**."

[38] Then two robbers are crucified with him, one at the right hand and one at the left.

[39] And those passing by were blaspheming him, shaking their heads

[40] and saying, "You who tear down the sanctuary and in three days build it, save yourself! If you are Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, come down from the cross."

[41] Likewise also the high priests, mocking with the scribes and elders, were saying,

[42] "Others he saved; himself he is not able to save. He is King of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; let him come down now from the cross, and we will believe on him.

[43] He has confidence in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; let him rescue him now, if he wants him; for he said, 'I am ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ Son.'"

[44] And with the same thing the robbers also, who were crucified together with him, were reproaching him.

[45] Now from the sixth hour darkness came upon all the earth until the ninth hour.

[46] And about the ninth hour ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ cried aloud with a loud voice, saying, "***Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani?***" that is, "**My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, why have you forsaken me?**"

[47] And some of those standing there, hearing, were saying, "This one calls ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧."

[48] And immediately one of them, running and taking a sponge, and filling it with vinegar and putting it around a reed, was giving him to drink.

[49] But the rest were saying, "Let be; let us see whether ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ comes to save him."

[50] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, again crying out with a loud voice, let go the spirit.

[51] And behold, the veil of the sanctuary was torn from above to below into two, and the earth quaked, and the rocks were split,

[52] and the tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep were raised,

[53] and coming out of the tombs after his raising they entered into the holy city and appeared to many.

[54] Now the centurion and those with him keeping ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, seeing the earthquake and the things that came to pass, feared exceedingly, saying, "**Truly this was ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ Son!**"

[55] Now there were there many women beholding from afar, who followed ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, serving him,

[56] among whom was ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the Magdalene, and ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the mother of ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and the mother of the sons of ⟦zebedee|Zebedaios|Zebedee⟧.

[57] Now when evening had come, there came a rich man from ⟦arimathea|Arimathaia|Arimathea⟧, ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ by name, who also himself was made a disciple to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧;

[58] this one, approaching ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧, asked for the body of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧. Then ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ commanded it to be rendered up.

[59] And taking the body, ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ wrapped it in a clean linen cloth

[60] and laid it in his new tomb, which he hewed in the rock; and having rolled a great stone against the door of the tomb, he went away.

[61] And ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the Magdalene was there, and the other ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧, sitting opposite the sepulchre.

[62] Now on the morrow, which is after the Preparation, the high priests and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ were gathered together to ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧,

[63] saying, "Lord, we remembered that that deceiver said, while still living, 'After three days I am raised.'

[64] Command therefore that the sepulchre be made secure until the third day, lest his disciples, coming, steal him and say to the people, 'He was raised from the dead,' and the last deception will be worse than the first."

[65] ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ said to them, "You have a guard; go, make it secure as you know."

[66] And they, going, made the sepulchre secure, having sealed the stone, with the guard.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v40, 43, 46, 54), **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v17, 22), **"James" → "*Yaaqov*"** (v56) — systematic.
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v1, 3, 6, 12, 20, 41, 62) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς. **"took counsel"** (v1, 7) — Greek συμβούλιον ἔλαβον (Mark 15:1: "having made counsel").
- **"delivered him to Pontius Pilate" → "delivered him up to Pilatus"** (v2) — Greek παρέδωκαν, the verb used of Judas (v3, 4), of the high priests (v2, 18) and of Pilatus (v26). "Pontius" is only in the Byzantine text.
- **"which had betrayed him" → "the one delivering him up"** (v3) — Greek ὁ παραδιδούς, present participle (Tregelles, NA28, Byzantine; WH has the aorist παραδούς). **"repented himself" → "regretting it"** — Greek μεταμεληθείς (μεταμέλομαι), not the word for repentance (μετανοέω). **"brought again" → "returned"** — Greek ἔστρεψεν.
- **"I have sinned in that I have betrayed" → "I sinned, delivering up"** (v4) — Greek Ἥμαρτον παραδούς. "Innocent" is ἀθῷον (WH: δίκαιον, "righteous"). **"see thou to that" → "You will see to it"** (v4, 24) — Greek σὺ ὄψῃ, ὑμεῖς ὄψεσθε, futures: "you will see".
- **"cast down the pieces of silver in the temple" → "throwing the silver pieces into the sanctuary"** (v5) — Greek εἰς τὸν ναόν, the temple building itself (so also v40, 51); "temple" is ἱερόν. **"departed" → "withdrew"** — Greek ἀνεχώρησεν.
- **"not lawful" → "not permitted"** (v6) — Greek οὐκ ἔξεστιν. **"treasury"** — Greek τὸν κορβανᾶν, the Aramaic name of the temple treasury (from *qorban*, "offering", Mark 7:11). **"price of blood"** — Greek τιμὴ αἵματος; the word "price" comes three more times in v9.
- **"the potter's field, to bury strangers in" → "the Field of the Potter, for a burial place for the strangers"** (v7) — Greek τὸν Ἀγρὸν τοῦ Κεραμέως εἰς ταφὴν τοῖς ξένοις. "With them" is ἐξ αὐτῶν, "out of them".
- **"The field of blood"** (v8) — Greek Ἀγρὸς Αἵματος, with ἀγρός "field". Acts 1:19 has χωρίον, "Plot of Blood", and gives the Aramaic name; the two accounts use different words. **"unto this day" → "until today"** — Greek ἕως τῆς σήμερον.
- **"by Jeremy the prophet" → "through Yirmeyahu the prophet"** (v9) — Greek διὰ Ἰερεμίου τοῦ προφήτου: the Greek names Jeremiah, and so does the verse, although the words are nearest to Zech 11:12–13 (compare Jer 19:1–13; 32:6–9). **"the price of him that was valued, whom they of the children of Israel did value" → "the price of the one priced, whom they priced from the sons of Israel"** — Greek τὴν τιμὴν τοῦ τετιμημένου ὃν ἐτιμήσαντο ἀπὸ υἱῶν Ἰσραήλ: one root three times; ἀπὸ υἱῶν Ἰσραήλ is either the subject ("whom some of the sons of Israel priced") or the side from which the price came. ἔλαβον can be "I took" or "they took"; "they gave" (v10) decides for the plural.
- **"appointed" → "directed"** (v10) — Greek συνέταξεν, as 26:19. "The Lord" is κύριος without the article, standing for the divine name.
- **"Thou sayest" → "You say it"** (v11) — Greek Σὺ λέγεις, as Mark 15:2; "it" is supplied. "Stood" is ἐστάθη, a passive form ("was set").
- **"how many things they witness against thee" → "how many things they testify against you"** (v13) — Greek καταμαρτυροῦσιν (Mark 15:4: κατηγοροῦσιν, "accuse").
- **"to never a word" → "not even to one word"** (v14) — Greek πρὸς οὐδὲ ἓν ῥῆμα. **"marvelled greatly"** — Greek θαυμάζειν … λίαν.
- **"at that feast" → "at each feast"** (v15) — Greek κατὰ δὲ ἑορτήν, as Mark 15:6. **"was wont" → "was accustomed"** — Greek εἰώθει. **"release"** (v15, 17, 21, 26) — Greek ἀπολύω, the verb "send away".
- **"a notable prisoner, called Barabbas" → "a notable prisoner called Yeshua Bar-Abba"** (v16), **"Barabbas, or Jesus" → "Yeshua Bar-Abba, or Yeshua the one called Mashiach"** (v17) — the name Ἰησοῦν before Βαραββᾶν is printed by SBLGNT and NA28; WH, Tregelles and the Byzantine text do not have it. The editions stand two against two and NA28 decides. Few manuscripts have the name; with it, Pilatus' question sets two men named Yeshua side by side. ἐπίσημος is "notable", in a good or a bad sense.
- **"for envy" → "because of envy"** (v18) — Greek διὰ φθόνον. "They delivered" is aorist (Mark 15:10: pluperfect).
- **"Have thou nothing to do with that just man" → "Have nothing to do with that righteous one"** (v19) — Greek Μηδὲν σοὶ καὶ τῷ δικαίῳ ἐκείνῳ, "nothing to you and to that righteous one", with no verb. **"I have suffered many things this day" → "I suffered many things today"** — Greek ἔπαθον, aorist.
- **"multitude" → "crowds"** (v20) — Greek τοὺς ὄχλους, plural; in v25 "all the people" is πᾶς ὁ λαός.
- **"Why, what evil hath he done?" → "For what evil did he do?"** (v23) — Greek Τί γὰρ κακὸν ἐποίησεν, as Mark 15:14. **"cried out the more" → "were crying out exceedingly"** — Greek περισσῶς ἔκραζον, imperfect.
- **"that he could prevail nothing, but that rather a tumult was made" → "that he profits nothing, but rather a commotion is arising"** (v24) — Greek οὐδὲν ὠφελεῖ ("he profits nothing" or "it profits nothing") ἀλλὰ μᾶλλον θόρυβος γίνεται, presents. **"washed" → "washed off"** — Greek ἀπενίψατο. **"of the blood of this just person" → "of the blood of this one"** — Greek ἀπὸ τοῦ αἵματος τούτου (WH, NA28), which can also be read "of this blood"; "righteous" is added by Tregelles and the Byzantine text.
- **"His blood be on us" → "His blood upon us"** (v25) — the Greek has no verb.
- **"when he had scourged Jesus" → "having flogged him"** (v26) — Greek φραγελλώσας, the Latin *flagello*, as Mark 15:15.
- **"common hall" → "praetorium"** (v27) — Greek πραιτώριον, the Latin word kept. **"band" → "cohort"** — Greek σπεῖρα. "Upon him" is ἐπ᾽ αὐτόν, "against" or "around" him.
- **"robe" → "cloak"** (v28, 31) — Greek χλαμύς, the short military cloak; "scarlet" is κοκκίνην (Mark 15:17 and John 19:2 have "purple").
- **"platted" → "woven"** (v29) — Greek πλέξαντες, as Mark 15:17. "Crown" is στέφανος, a wreath. **"bowed the knee" → "kneeling"** — Greek γονυπετήσαντες. "Mocked" is aorist (WH, NA28; Tregelles and the Byzantine text: imperfect).
- **"spit upon him … smote him" → "spitting upon him … were beating him"** (v30) — Greek ἐμπτύσαντες εἰς αὐτόν … ἔτυπτον εἰς τὴν κεφαλὴν αὐτοῦ, imperfect.
- **"his own raiment" → "his garments"** (v31) — Greek τὰ ἱμάτια αὐτοῦ.
- **"compelled" → "pressed into service"** (v32) — Greek ἠγγάρευσαν, a Persian loanword for forced service, as 5:41; Mark 15:21. **"to bear" → "that he might take up"** — Greek ἵνα ἄρῃ. "A man of Cyrene" is ἄνθρωπον Κυρηναῖον, "a Cyrenian man".
- **"that is to say, a place of a skull" → "which is called Place of a Skull"** (v33) — Greek ὅ ἐστιν Κρανίου Τόπος λεγόμενος. Γολγοθᾶ is the Aramaic *gulgulta*, "skull".
- **"vinegar … mingled with gall" → "wine … mixed with gall"** (v34) — Greek οἶνον (WH, Tregelles, NA28; the Byzantine text has ὄξος, "vinegar", as Ps 69:21). **"he would not drink" → "he did not want to drink"** — Greek οὐκ ἠθέλησεν.
- **"parted his garments, casting lots" → "divided his garments among themselves, casting a lot"** (v35) — Greek διεμερίσαντο … βάλλοντες κλῆρον, singular; the words of Ps 22:18. The KJV's "that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet …" is not in the printed text.
- **"watched him" → "were keeping him"** (v36), **"watching Jesus" → "keeping Yeshua"** (v54) — Greek τηρέω, "keep", here of keeping guard; the same verb in 28:4, 20.
- **"his accusation" → "his charge"** (v37) — Greek τὴν αἰτίαν αὐτοῦ. Mark 15:26 has only "The King of the Jews".
- **"thieves" → "robbers"** (v38, 44) — Greek λῃσταί, also "insurrectionists". **"were crucified" → "are crucified"** — Greek σταυροῦνται, present.
- **"reviled" → "were blaspheming"** (v39) — Greek ἐβλασφήμουν. **"wagging" → "shaking"** — Greek κινοῦντες.
- **"Thou that destroyest … and buildest" → "You who tear down … and build"** (v40) — Greek ὁ καταλύων … οἰκοδομῶν, present participles; "it" is supplied. **"the Son of God" → "Son of Elohim"** — Greek υἱὸς … τοῦ θεοῦ, "son" without the article, as 4:3, 6. NA28 alone adds "and" before "come down".
- **"Likewise"** (v41) — Greek ὁμοίως, as Mark 15:31.
- **"If he be the King of Israel" → "He is King of Israel"** (v42) — Greek βασιλεὺς Ἰσραήλ ἐστιν (WH, Tregelles, NA28); "if" is only in the Byzantine text. **"believe him" → "believe on him"** — Greek ἐπ᾽ αὐτόν.
- **"He trusted in God; let him deliver him" → "He has confidence in Elohim; let him rescue him"** (v43) — Greek πέποιθεν, perfect; ῥυσάσθω, "let him rescue", has no named subject. **"if he will have him" → "if he wants him"** — Greek εἰ θέλει αὐτόν, the words of Ps 22:8 in the Greek Old Testament, where θέλω means "delight in". **"I am the Son of God" → "I am Elohim's Son"** — Greek Θεοῦ εἰμι υἱός, without articles and with "God's" first.
- **"cast the same in his teeth" → "with the same thing … were reproaching him"** (v44) — Greek τὸ δ᾽ αὐτὸ … ὠνείδιζον αὐτόν.
- **"over all the land" → "upon all the earth"** (v45) — Greek ἐπὶ πᾶσαν τὴν γῆν; γῆ is "earth" or "land" (Mark 15:33: "the whole earth").
- **"Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani" → "Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani"** (v46) — as the Greek prints it: Ἠλὶ ἠλὶ λεμὰ σαβαχθάνι (SBLGNT, Tregelles, NA28, Byzantine; WH has Ἐλωῒ ἐλωΐ, as Mark 15:34). *Eli* is the Hebrew form of "my God", *lema sabachthani* is Aramaic. **"cried" → "cried aloud"** — Greek ἀνεβόησεν (NA28, Byzantine; WH and Tregelles ἐβόησεν, "cried"). **"why"** — Greek ἱνατί (Mark: εἰς τί, "for what"); "My God" is the vocative Θεέ μου (Mark: the nominative ὁ θεός μου). Ps 22:1.
- **"This man calleth for Elias" → "This one calls Eliyahu"** (v47) — Greek Ἠλίαν φωνεῖ οὗτος.
- **"vinegar"** (v48) — Greek ὄξος, sour wine. **"gave him to drink" → "was giving him to drink"** — Greek ἐπότιζεν, imperfect.
- **"Let be"** (v49) — Greek Ἄφες, singular (Mark 15:36: plural, and said by the one with the sponge). **"will come to save" → "comes to save"** — Greek ἔρχεται … σώσων. WH adds here in double brackets, from John 19:34, "and another, taking a spear, pierced his side, and there came out water and blood"; Tregelles, NA28 and the Byzantine text do not have it.
- **"yielded up the ghost" → "let go the spirit"** (v50) — Greek ἀφῆκεν τὸ πνεῦμα: ἀφίημι is "send away, let go, leave"; τὸ πνεῦμα is "the spirit" (or "the breath"), with no "his". Mark 15:37 has ἐξέπνευσεν, "breathed his last"; John 19:30 "delivered up the spirit".
- **"rent in twain from the top to the bottom" → "torn from above to below into two"** (v51) — the Greek order. **"did quake" → "quaked"** — Greek ἐσείσθη. **"rent" → "split"** — Greek ἐσχίσθησαν, the same verb as "was torn".
- **"graves" → "tombs"** (v52, 53, 60) — Greek μνημεῖα. **"which slept arose" → "who had fallen asleep were raised"** — Greek τῶν κεκοιμημένων … ἠγέρθησαν, passive.
- **"after his resurrection" → "after his raising"** (v53) — Greek μετὰ τὴν ἔγερσιν αὐτοῦ, the noun of ἐγείρω "raise", only here in the New Testament; not ἀνάστασις. **"appeared"** — Greek ἐνεφανίσθησαν, "were made visible".
- **"those things that were done" → "the things that came to pass"** (v54) — Greek τὰ γενόμενα (NA28, Byzantine; WH and Tregelles: τὰ γινόμενα, "that were coming to pass"). **"feared greatly" → "feared exceedingly"** — Greek ἐφοβήθησαν σφόδρα. **"the Son of God" → "Elohim's Son"** — Greek θεοῦ υἱός, without articles, as Mark 15:39: "God's Son", "a son of God" or "a son of a god" are all possible in the mouth of a Roman soldier. "Centurion" is ἑκατόνταρχος (Mark: the Latin κεντυρίων).
- **"beholding afar off" → "beholding from afar"** (v55) — Greek ἀπὸ μακρόθεν θεωροῦσαι. **"ministering" → "serving"** — Greek διακονοῦσαι.
- **"Mary Magdalene" → "Miryam the Magdalene"** (v56, 61) — Greek ἡ Μαγδαληνή, with the article. **"Joses" → "Yosef"** — Greek Ἰωσήφ (WH, NA28; Tregelles and the Byzantine text: Ἰωσῆ, "Joses", as Mark 15:40).
- **"who also himself was Jesus' disciple" → "who also himself was made a disciple to Yeshua"** (v57) — Greek ἐμαθητεύθη τῷ Ἰησοῦ, passive (WH, Tregelles, NA28; the Byzantine text has the active ἐμαθήτευσεν, "was a disciple"); the verb of 13:52 and 28:19.
- **"begged" → "asked for"** (v58) — Greek ᾐτήσατο. **"commanded the body to be delivered" → "commanded it to be rendered up"** — Greek ἐκέλευσεν ἀποδοθῆναι, "to be given back, rendered"; "the body" is repeated only in Tregelles and the Byzantine text.
- **"wrapped it in a clean linen cloth"** (v59) — Greek ἐνετύλιξεν αὐτὸ ἐν σινδόνι καθαρᾷ: "in" (ἐν) is read by WH, Tregelles and NA28 and not printed in SBLGNT; the verse follows the three. Mark 15:46 has ἐνείλησεν.
- **"had hewn out" → "hewed"** (v60) — Greek ἐλατόμησεν, aorist. **"rolled a great stone to" → "rolled a great stone against"** — Greek προσκυλίσας, as Mark 15:46.
- **"sepulchre"** (v61, 64, 66) — Greek τάφος, a different word from "tomb" (μνημεῖον, v52, 53, 60).
- **"the next day, that followed the day of the preparation" → "on the morrow, which is after the Preparation"** (v62) — Greek Τῇ δὲ ἐπαύριον, ἥτις ἐστὶν μετὰ τὴν παρασκευήν: the Sabbath is not named. **"came together unto" → "were gathered together to"** — Greek συνήχθησαν πρός.
- **"Sir" → "Lord"** (v63) — Greek Κύριε. **"I will rise again" → "I am raised"** — Greek ἐγείρομαι, present, passive or middle ("I rise"). "We remembered" is aorist.
- **"He is risen" → "He was raised"** (v64) — Greek Ἠγέρθη, as 28:6, 7. **"error" → "deception"** — Greek πλάνη, elsewhere "error" (Rom 1:27; 1 John 4:6); here it answers to ὁ πλάνος, "the deceiver" (v63), and means the act of leading astray. "By night" is only in the Byzantine text.
- **"Ye have a watch" → "You have a guard"** (v65, 66) — Greek κουστωδίαν, the Latin *custodia*; ἔχετε can be a statement ("you have") or a command ("have, take a guard"). **"as sure as ye can" → "as you know"** — Greek ὡς οἴδατε.
- **"sealing the stone, and setting a watch" → "having sealed the stone, with the guard"** (v66) — Greek μετὰ τῆς κουστωδίας; no verb of setting.

### Notes
- **v46** quotes Ps 22:1.
- **v35** echoes Ps 22:18; **v43** Ps 22:8; **v34, 48** Ps 69:21.
- **v9–10** — ascribed to Jeremiah; the wording is nearest to Zech 11:12–13, with Jer 19 and 32 in view.
