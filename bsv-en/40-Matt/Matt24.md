# Matthew 24

[1] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, going out from the temple, was going on his way, and his disciples approached to show him the buildings of the temple.

[2] But he, answering, said to them, "Do you not see all these things? Amen, I say to you, there shall by no means be left here stone upon stone which will not be torn down."

[3] And as he was sitting on ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧, the disciples approached him privately, saying, "Tell us, when will these things be, and what is the sign of your coming and of the consummation of the age?"

[4] And answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Look, lest anyone deceive you.

[5] For many will come upon my name, saying, 'I am the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧,' and they will deceive many.

[6] And you will be about to hear of wars and reports of wars; see, do not be alarmed; for it must come to pass, but the end is not yet.

[7] For nation will arise against nation and kingdom against kingdom, and there will be famines and earthquakes in places;

[8] but all these things are a beginning of birth pains.

[9] "Then they will deliver you up to affliction and will kill you, and you will be hated by all the nations because of my name.

[10] And then many will be made to stumble and will deliver up one another and will hate one another;

[11] and many false prophets will arise and will deceive many;

[12] and because lawlessness is multiplied, the love of the many will grow cold.

[13] But the one who has endured to the end, this one will be saved.

[14] And this gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed in the whole inhabited earth for a testimony to all the nations, and then the end will come.

[15] "When therefore you see the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of through ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ the prophet, standing in a holy place (let the one reading perceive),

[16] then let those in ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ flee into the mountains,

[17] let the one on the housetop not go down to take the things out of his house,

[18] and let the one in the field not turn back to take his garment.

[19] But woe to those who are with child and to those who are nursing in those days!

[20] And pray that your flight may not come to pass in winter nor on a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧;

[21] for then there will be great affliction, such as there has not been from the beginning of the world until now, nor by any means shall be.

[22] And if those days had not been cut short, no flesh would have been saved; but for the sake of the elect those days will be cut short.

[23] "Then if anyone says to you, 'Behold, here is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧!' or, 'Here!' — do not believe;

[24] for ⟦mashiach|false mashiachs|false christs⟧ and false prophets will arise and will give great signs and wonders, so as to deceive, if possible, even the elect;

[25] behold, I have told you beforehand.

[26] If therefore they say to you, 'Behold, he is in the wilderness,' do not go out; 'Behold, in the inner rooms,' do not believe;

[27] for just as the lightning comes out from the east and appears as far as the west, so will be the coming of the Son of Man;

[28] wherever the corpse is, there the eagles will be gathered together.

[29] "But immediately after the affliction of those days the sun will be darkened,
and the moon will not give its light,
and the stars will fall from heaven,
and the powers of the heavens will be shaken.

[30] And then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven, and then all the tribes of the earth will wail, and **they will see the Son of Man coming upon the clouds of heaven with power and much glory**;

[31] and he will send his angels with a great trumpet, and they will gather together his elect out of the four winds, from the ends of the heavens to their ends.

[32] "But from the fig tree learn the parable: when its branch has already become tender and puts forth the leaves, you know that the summer is near;

[33] so also you, when you see all these things, know that it is near, at the doors.

[34] Amen, I say to you that this generation shall by no means pass away until all these things come to pass.

[35] The heaven and the earth will pass away, but my words shall by no means pass away.

[36] "But about that day and hour **no one knows, not even the angels of the heavens, nor the Son, except the Father alone**.

[37] For just as the days of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, so will be the coming of the Son of Man;

[38] for as in those days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day on which ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ entered into the ark,

[39] and they did not know until the flood came and took them all away, so will be also the coming of the Son of Man.

[40] Then two will be in the field: one is taken along and one is left;

[41] two women grinding at the mill: one is taken along and one is left.

[42] "Be awake therefore, because you do not know on what day your Lord comes.

[43] But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what watch the thief comes, he would have been awake and would not have let his house be dug through.

[44] Because of this you also, be ready, because the Son of Man comes at an hour when you do not think.

[45] "Who then is the faithful and sensible slave, whom the lord appointed over his household slaves, to give them the food in season?

[46] Blessed is that slave whom his lord, coming, will find doing so;

[47] Amen, I say to you that he will appoint him over all that belongs to him.

[48] But if that bad slave says in his heart, 'My lord delays,'

[49] and begins to strike his fellow slaves, and eats and drinks with those who are drunk,

[50] the lord of that slave will come on a day when he is not waiting and at an hour which he does not know,

[51] and will cut him in two and will set his part with the hypocrites; there will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v1, 4) — Hebrew name preserved. In v2 the critical text has "he" (RP adds the name).
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (vv5, 23–24) — Greek ὁ χριστός. In v24 ψευδόχριστοι *pseudochristoi*, "false anointed ones", is the same word with ψευδο- before it: "false mashiachs".
- **"And" / "But" kept** (throughout) — the καί and δέ at the start of sentences are kept as they are.
- **"Jesus went out, and departed from the temple … to shew him the buildings" → "Yeshua, going out from the temple, was going on his way … to show him the buildings"** (v1) — Greek ἐξελθὼν … ἀπὸ τοῦ ἱεροῦ ἐπορεύετο (WH Treg NA28), an imperfect; "temple" is ἱερόν, the whole precinct.
- **"See ye not all these things? verily … There shall not be left here one stone upon another, that shall not be thrown down" → "Do you not see all these things? Amen … there shall by no means be left here stone upon stone which will not be torn down"** (v2) — Greek ἀμήν; οὐ μὴ ἀφεθῇ, the strong negation, as Mark 13:2; ὃς οὐ καταλυθήσεται, a plain future (Mark has a second οὐ μή); καταλύω as Gal 2:18.
- **"what shall be the sign of thy coming, and of the end of the world?" → "what is the sign of your coming and of the consummation of the age?"** (v3) — Greek τί τὸ σημεῖον, without a verb; τῆς σῆς παρουσίας καὶ συντελείας τοῦ αἰῶνος: one article for "coming" and "consummation" (WH Treg NA28); παρουσία is "presence, arrival" (also v27, 37, 39); συντέλεια is a bringing to completion (Heb 9:26), not τέλος "end" (v6, 13, 14); αἰών is "age", not "world". Mark 13:4 has neither "coming" nor "age".
- **"Take heed that no man deceive you" → "Look, lest anyone deceive you"** (v4) — Greek Βλέπετε μή, word for word as Mark 13:5.
- **"in my name" → "upon my name"** (v5) — Greek ἐπὶ τῷ ὀνόματί μου, as Mark 13:6: on the ground of my name. Mark has "I am" only; Matthew adds "the *Mashiach*".
- **"ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass" → "you will be about to hear of wars and reports of wars; see, do not be alarmed; for it must come to pass"** (v6) — Greek μελλήσετε ἀκούειν, a future of μέλλω "be about to"; ἀκοὰς πολέμων; ὁρᾶτε, μὴ θροεῖσθε, two commands; δεῖ γὰρ γενέσθαι: "all these things" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places" → "famines and earthquakes in places"** (v7) — "pestilences" (RP) is not in WH Treg NA28; κατὰ τόπους, "place by place". "Arise" is ἐγερθήσεται, as Mark 13:8; also v11, 24.
- **"All these are the beginning of sorrows" → "but all these things are a beginning of birth pains"** (v8) — Greek ἀρχὴ ὠδίνων, without articles; ὠδῖνες are the pains of a woman in labor (1 Thess 5:3).
- **"deliver you up to be afflicted" → "deliver you up to affliction"** (v9) — Greek εἰς θλῖψιν, a noun (v9, 21, 29). "All the nations": πάντων τῶν ἐθνῶν.
- **"shall many be offended, and shall betray one another" → "many will be made to stumble and will deliver up one another"** (v10) — Greek σκανδαλισθήσονται; παραδώσουσιν, the verb of v9.
- **"because iniquity shall abound, the love of many shall wax cold" → "because lawlessness is multiplied, the love of the many will grow cold"** (v12) — Greek διὰ τὸ πληθυνθῆναι τὴν ἀνομίαν; τῶν πολλῶν with the article: "of the many", the majority.
- **"he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved" → "the one who has endured to the end, this one will be saved"** (v13) — Greek word for word as Mark 13:13.
- **"shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations" → "will be proclaimed in the whole inhabited earth for a testimony to all the nations"** (v14) — Greek κηρυχθήσεται; ἐν ὅλῃ τῇ οἰκουμένῃ, the inhabited earth (not κόσμος "world", v21); εἰς μαρτύριον.
- **"the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, stand in the holy place, (whoso readeth, let him understand)" → "the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in a holy place (let the one reading perceive)"** (v15) — Greek τὸ ῥηθὲν διὰ Δανιήλ, "through"; ἑστός, a neuter (WH Treg NA28; Mark 13:14 has the masculine "standing where he must not"); ἐν τόπῳ ἁγίῳ, without the article; νοείτω, as Mark.
- **"flee into the mountains"** (v16) — kept: the verse follows εἰς τὰ ὄρη (WH Treg NA28); the SBLGNT prints ἐπί, "to / upon the mountains" (with RP).
- **"to take any thing out of his house" → "to take the things out of his house"** (v17) — Greek ἆραι τὰ ἐκ τῆς οἰκίας αὐτοῦ, "the things from his house".
- **"return back to take his clothes" → "turn back to take his garment"** (v18) — Greek τὸ ἱμάτιον, singular (WH Treg NA28).
- **"woe unto them that are with child, and to them that give suck"** (v19) — Greek word for word as Mark 13:17; ἐν γαστρὶ ἐχούσαις, literally "having in the belly".
- **"that your flight be not in the winter, neither on the sabbath day" → "that your flight may not come to pass in winter nor on a Shabbat"** (v20) — Greek γένηται; σαββάτῳ, without the article. Mark 13:18 has neither "your flight" nor the *Shabbat*.
- **"great tribulation, such as was not since the beginning of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be" → "great affliction, such as there has not been from the beginning of the world until now, nor by any means shall be"** (v21) — Greek θλῖψις μεγάλη; γέγονεν, a perfect; ἀπ' ἀρχῆς κόσμου (Mark "of the creation"); οὐδ' οὐ μὴ γένηται.
- **"except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved … shall be shortened" → "if those days had not been cut short, no flesh would have been saved … will be cut short"** (v22) — Greek ἐκολοβώθησαν, a passive (in Mark 13:20 "the Lord cut short"); οὐκ ἂν ἐσώθη πᾶσα σάρξ; κολοβωθήσονται, a future.
- **"Lo, here is Christ, or there; believe it not" → "Behold, here is the Mashiach! or, Here! — do not believe"** (v23) — Greek Ἰδοὺ ὧδε ὁ χριστός, ἤ· Ὧδε: "here" twice; μὴ πιστεύσητε, without an object (also v26).
- **"shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect" → "will give great signs and wonders, so as to deceive, if possible, even the elect"** (v24) — Greek δώσουσιν; ὥστε πλανῆσαι (NA28 RP; WH Treg πλανᾶσθαι, "so that even the elect are deceived"); εἰ δυνατόν; καὶ τοὺς ἐκλεκτούς. Mark 13:22 has ἀποπλανᾶν, "lead astray".
- **"in the desert … in the secret chambers" → "in the wilderness … in the inner rooms"** (v26) — Greek ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ; ἐν τοῖς ταμείοις, the inner store-rooms of a house (as 6:6). The second saying has no verb.
- **"as the lightning cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" → "just as the lightning comes out from the east and appears as far as the west, so will be the coming of the Son of Man"** (v27) — Greek φαίνεται, "appears, is seen"; ἕως δυσμῶν; "also" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"wheresoever the carcase is, there will the eagles be gathered together" → "wherever the corpse is, there the eagles will be gathered together"** (v28) — Greek πτῶμα, a fallen body (as Mark 6:29); ἀετοί, "eagles"; the word is also used of vultures, which gather at a corpse. "For" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"the stars shall fall from heaven"** (v29) — Greek πεσοῦνται ἀπὸ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ (Mark 13:25 "will be falling out of heaven"); αἱ δυνάμεις τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural "heavens". "Immediately": εὐθέως.
- **"then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn … in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory" → "then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven, and then all the tribes of the earth will wail … upon the clouds of heaven with power and much glory"** (v30) — Greek ἐν οὐρανῷ (WH Treg NA28, without the article; the SBLGNT and RP have τῷ); κόψονται, "beat the breast, wail" (Zech 12:10–14; as Rev 1:7); πᾶσαι αἱ φυλαὶ τῆς γῆς, "of the earth" or "of the land"; ἐπὶ τῶν νεφελῶν, "upon" (Mark 13:26 "in clouds"); δόξης πολλῆς. "The sign of the Son of Man": a plain genitive.
- **"with a great sound of a trumpet … from one end of heaven to the other" → "with a great trumpet … from the ends of the heavens to their ends"** (v31) — Greek μετὰ σάλπιγγος μεγάλης (WH NA28; Treg RP add φωνῆς, "sound"); ἀπ' ἄκρων οὐρανῶν ἕως τῶν ἄκρων αὐτῶν, plurals. "Gather together": ἐπισυνάξουσιν, plural: the angels gather (in Mark 13:27 "he will gather").
- **"Now learn a parable of the fig tree; When his branch is yet tender, and putteth forth leaves, ye know that summer is nigh" → "But from the fig tree learn the parable: when its branch has already become tender and puts forth the leaves, you know that the summer is near"** (v32) — Greek as Mark 13:28, in another word order and without "is".
- **"know that it is near, even at the doors"** (v33) — Greek ἐγγύς ἐστιν ἐπὶ θύραις: "it is near" or "he is near"; γινώσκετε can be a statement ("you know") or a command ("know"), in v32 as in v33.
- **"Verily … This generation shall not pass, till all these things be fulfilled" → "Amen … this generation shall by no means pass away until all these things come to pass"** (v34) — Greek οὐ μὴ παρέλθῃ; γένηται.
- **"Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away" → "The heaven and the earth will pass away, but my words shall by no means pass away"** (v35) — Greek ὁ οὐρανὸς καὶ ἡ γῆ, with the articles; οὐ μὴ παρέλθωσιν.
- **"of that day and hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels of heaven, but my Father only" → "about that day and hour no one knows, not even the angels of the heavens, nor the Son, except the Father alone"** (v36) — Greek οὐδὲ ὁ υἱός is in WH NA28 and the SBLGNT; Treg and RP do not have it. The editions stand two against two and NA28 decides: the words stay. "My" Father is RP only. τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural.
- **"But as the days of Noe were" → "For just as the days of Noach"** (v37) — Greek ὥσπερ γάρ (WH Treg NA28), without a verb.
- **"as in the days that were before the flood" → "as in those days that were before the flood"** (v38) — the verse follows ἐν ταῖς ἡμέραις ἐκείναις (WH Treg NA28); the SBLGNT and RP have no "those". τρώγοντες, "eating" (a verb for chewing, as John 6:54); γαμίζοντες (WH NA28).
- **"knew not until the flood came, and took them all away"** (v39) — Greek οὐκ ἔγνωσαν; ἦρεν ἅπαντας. "Also": καί (NA28 RP and the SBLGNT; WH Treg do not have it).
- **"the one shall be taken, and the other left" → "one is taken along and one is left"** (v40, 41) — Greek παραλαμβάνεται … ἀφίεται, present tenses; παραλαμβάνω is "take to oneself, take along" (as 17:1; 26:37), ἀφίημι "leave, let go". The Greek does not say who takes or to what end. "Two" in v40 is masculine; in v41 the participle is feminine ("women" is supplied).
- **"Watch therefore: for ye know not what hour your Lord doth come" → "Be awake therefore, because you do not know on what day your Lord comes"** (v42) — Greek γρηγορεῖτε, as Mark 13:35; ποίᾳ ἡμέρᾳ (WH Treg NA28; RP "hour"); ἔρχεται, a present (also v43, 44).
- **"if the goodman of the house had known in what watch the thief would come, he would have watched, and would not have suffered his house to be broken up" → "if the master of the house had known in what watch the thief comes, he would have been awake and would not have let his house be dug through"** (v43) — Greek οἰκοδεσπότης; φυλακῇ, a watch of the night (as 14:25); ἐγρηγόρησεν; διορυχθῆναι, "dug through" (as 6:19): through the wall of clay.
- **"in such an hour as ye think not" → "at an hour when you do not think"** (v44) — Greek ᾗ οὐ δοκεῖτε ὥρᾳ; γίνεσθε ἕτοιμοι, literally "become ready".
- **"a faithful and wise servant, whom his lord hath made ruler over his household, to give them meat in due season" → "the faithful and sensible slave, whom the lord appointed over his household slaves, to give them the food in season"** (v45) — Greek ὁ πιστὸς δοῦλος καὶ φρόνιμος; κατέστησεν (also v47); οἰκετείας (WH Treg NA28), the body of household slaves; ἐν καιρῷ, "at the (right) time".
- **"servant" → "slave"; "fellowservants" → "fellow slaves"** (v45–50) — Greek δοῦλος, σύνδουλος.
- **"he shall make him ruler over all his goods" → "he will appoint him over all that belongs to him"** (v47) — Greek ἐπὶ πᾶσιν τοῖς ὑπάρχουσιν αὐτοῦ.
- **"that evil servant … My lord delayeth his coming" → "that bad slave … My lord delays"** (v48) — Greek κακός (not πονηρός "evil"); Χρονίζει μου ὁ κύριος (WH Treg NA28); "to come" is RP.
- **"to smite his fellowservants, and to eat and drink with the drunken" → "to strike his fellow slaves, and eats and drinks with those who are drunk"** (v49) — Greek τύπτειν; ἐσθίῃ δὲ καὶ πίνῃ (WH Treg NA28), finite verbs; μεθυόντων, a participle.
- **"in a day when he looketh not for him, and in an hour that he is not aware of" → "on a day when he is not waiting and at an hour which he does not know"** (v50) — Greek οὐ προσδοκᾷ, "wait for, expect" (as 2 Pet 3:12), without an object; οὐ γινώσκει.
- **"shall cut him asunder, and appoint him his portion with the hypocrites: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth" → "will cut him in two and will set his part with the hypocrites; there will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth"** (v51) — Greek διχοτομήσει, literally "cut in two"; τὸ μέρος αὐτοῦ … θήσει; ὁ κλαυθμὸς καὶ ὁ βρυγμὸς τῶν ὀδόντων, with the articles.
- **"elect" kept** (v22, 24, 31) — Greek ἐκλεκτούς *eklektous*.
- **"Son of man"** (v27, 30, 37, 39, 44) — Greek ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου.

### Notes
- **v15** refers to Dan 9:27; 11:31; 12:11.
- **v29** echoes Isa 13:10; 34:4; Joel 2:10.
- **v30** echoes Dan 7:13; Zech 12:10–14.
