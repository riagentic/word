# Jude 1

[1] ⟦jude|Yehudah|Jude⟧, a slave of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and brother of ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧,

To the called, beloved in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father and kept for ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧:

[2] Mercy and peace and love be multiplied to you.

[3] Beloved, giving all diligence to write to you about our common salvation, I had need to write to you, exhorting you to contend for the faith once for all delivered to the saints.

[4] For certain people have crept in, those who long ago were written beforehand for this sentence, ungodly ones, changing the grace of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ into licentiousness and denying our only master and Lord, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[5] Now I wish to remind you, though you know all things once for all, that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, having saved a people out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, the second time destroyed those who did not believe.

[6] And angels who did not keep their own rule, but left their own dwelling, he has kept for the judgment of the great day in everlasting chains under gloom,

[7] as ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧ and the cities around them, which in the same manner as these committed sexual immorality and went after other flesh, are set forth as an example, undergoing the penalty of eternal fire.

[8] Yet in the same way these also, dreaming, defile flesh, reject lordship, and blaspheme glories.

[9] But Michael the archangel, when, contending with the devil, he disputed about the body of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, did not dare to bring a judgment of blasphemy, but said, "**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ rebuke you**."

[10] But these blaspheme whatever they do not know; and whatever they understand by nature, like the unreasoning animals, in these things they are corrupted.

[11] Woe to them! For they went in the way of ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧, and for a wage were poured out into ⟦balaam|Bilam's|Balaam's⟧ error, and perished in ⟦korah|Qorach's|Korah's⟧ contradiction.

[12] These are the reefs at your love feasts, feasting with you, shepherding themselves without fear; waterless clouds, carried along by winds; late-autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, uprooted;

[13] wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shames; wandering stars, for whom the gloom of darkness has been kept forever.

[14] And to these also ⟦enoch|Chanoch|Enoch⟧, the seventh from Adam, prophesied, saying, "**Behold, the Lord came with his holy myriads,**

**[15] to execute judgment against all and to convict all the ungodly concerning all their works of ungodliness in which they acted ungodly, and concerning all the hard things which ungodly sinners spoke against him**."

[16] These are grumblers, complaining of their lot, going according to their desires; and their mouth speaks swollen things, admiring faces for the sake of advantage.

[17] But you, beloved, remember the words spoken beforehand by the apostles of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[18] that they were saying to you, "At the last time there will be mockers, going according to their own desires of ungodliness."

[19] These are the ones who cause divisions, soulish, not having the Spirit.

[20] But you, beloved, building yourselves up on your most holy faith, praying in the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧,

[21] keep yourselves in the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, awaiting the mercy of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ unto eternal life.

[22] And on some have mercy, who are doubting;

[23] and others save, snatching them out of the fire; and on others have mercy in fear, hating even the tunic stained by the flesh.

[24] Now to him who is able to guard you from stumbling and to set you before his glory blameless in exultation,

[25] to the only ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, our Savior, through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ our Lord, glory, majesty, might, and authority, before all the age and now and unto all the ages. Amen.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v1) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 4, 17, 21, 25) — systematic.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v20) — systematic; the Greek ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ has no article.
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v1) — Greek δοῦλος *doulos*.
- **"sanctified by God the Father, and preserved in Jesus Christ, and called" → "the called, beloved in *Elohim* the Father and kept for *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1) — Greek ἠγαπημένοις "beloved" (KJV follows the later ἡγιασμένοις "sanctified"); the dative Ἰησοῦ Χριστῷ may be "for" or "by" *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*. "Kept" is τηρέω, the key verb of the letter (v6 twice, v13, v21).
- **"Mercy unto you, and peace, and love, be multiplied" kept** (v2) — Greek πληθυνθείη, an optative of wish; "and … and" as in the Greek.
- **"when I gave all diligence to write unto you … it was needful for me to write" → "giving all diligence to write to you … I had need to write to you"** (v3) — Greek πᾶσαν σπουδὴν ποιούμενος … ἀνάγκην ἔσχον; a participle, with no "although" in the Greek. παρακαλῶν is rendered "exhorting" here (its sense in this letter).
- **"before of old ordained to this condemnation" → "who long ago were written beforehand for this sentence"** (v4) — Greek προγεγραμμένοι *progegrammenoi*, "written beforehand"; κρίμα "sentence", a different word from κρίσις "judgment" (v6, 9, 15).
- **"turning the grace of our God into lasciviousness" → "changing the grace of our *Elohim* into licentiousness"** (v4) — Greek μετατιθέντες "transferring, changing"; ἀσέλγεια *aselgeia* "licentiousness, unrestrained conduct".
- **"denying the only Lord God, and our Lord Jesus Christ" → "denying our only master and Lord, *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v4) — Greek τὸν μόνον δεσπότην καὶ κύριον ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦν Χριστόν, without "God"; δεσπότης "master" (the owner of slaves, cf. v1 "slave"); one article joins "master and Lord". It can also be read "the only master, and our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*".
- **"the Lord, having saved the people" → "*Yeshua*, having saved a people"** (v5) — the verse reads Ἰησοῦς "Jesus" (SBLGNT NA28); κύριος "Lord" (WH Treg), ὁ κύριος "the Lord" (NA27 RP, so KJV). "the second time" is τὸ δεύτερον; "though you know all things once for all" is εἰδότας ὑμᾶς ἅπαξ πάντα.
- **"kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation" → "did not keep their own rule, but left their own dwelling"** (v6) — Greek ἀρχή *archē* = "rule, domain" or "beginning" (hence KJV "first estate"). The same verb "keep" (τηρέω) stands twice: they did not keep, he has kept.
- **"everlasting chains under darkness" → "everlasting chains under gloom"** (v6) — Greek ἀϊδίοις "everlasting", a different word from αἰωνίου "eternal" in v7; ζόφος "gloom", as in v13.
- **"giving themselves over to fornication, and going after strange flesh" → "in the same manner as these committed sexual immorality and went after other flesh"** (v7) — Greek τὸν ὅμοιον τρόπον τούτοις ἐκπορνεύσασαι (the verb of πορνεία, strengthened by ἐκ-) … ἀπελθοῦσαι ὀπίσω σαρκὸς ἑτέρας, literally "other flesh". "These" (τούτοις, masculine) are most likely the angels of v6, or the people of v4.
- **"filthy dreamers" → "dreaming"** (v8) — Greek ἐνυπνιαζόμενοι; "filthy" is not in the Greek.
- **"despise dominion, and speak evil of dignities" → "reject lordship, and blaspheme glories"** (v8) — Greek κυριότητα "lordship"; δόξας *doxas*, literally "glories", commonly understood as glorious (angelic) beings.
- **"durst not bring against him a railing accusation" → "did not dare to bring a judgment of blasphemy"** (v9) — Greek κρίσιν ἐπενεγκεῖν βλασφημίας; the genitive can mean a slanderous judgment or a judgment for blasphemy. "against him" is not in the Greek.
- **"The Lord rebuke thee" kept** (v9) — Greek ἐπιτιμήσαι σοι κύριος, an optative of wish; the words of Zech 3:2, where the Hebrew has Yahweh.
- **"in those things they corrupt themselves" → "in these things they are corrupted"** (v10) — Greek φθείρονται, "are corrupted" (also "are ruined, destroyed"). Two verbs of knowing: οἴδασιν "know", ἐπίστανται "understand".
- **"ran greedily after the error of Balaam for reward, and perished in the gainsaying of Core" → "for a wage were poured out into Bilam's error, and perished in Qorach's contradiction"** (v11) — Greek μισθοῦ "for a wage"; ἐξεχύθησαν, literally "were poured out"; ἀντιλογία "contradiction" (the noun of ἀντιλέγω "contradict"), here the speaking against Moses that was Korah's rebellion (Num 16).
- **"spots in your feasts of charity" → "the reefs at your love feasts"** (v12) — Greek σπιλάδες *spilades* = "rocks in the sea, reefs" (some take it as "spots, stains", cf. v23); "love feasts" = Greek ἀγάπαις *agapais* (the communal *agape* meals).
- **"feeding themselves without fear" → "feasting with you, shepherding themselves without fear"** (v12) — Greek ἑαυτοὺς ποιμαίνοντες, "shepherding themselves"; the printed text (SBLGNT) sets the comma before ἀφόβως, so "without fear" goes with the shepherding. NA28: comma after ἀφόβως, "feasting with you without fear" (quoted from memory); the manuscripts have no punctuation.
- **"clouds without water" → "waterless clouds"** (v12) — Greek νεφέλαι ἄνυδροι *nephelai anydroi*.
- **"trees whose fruit withereth, without fruit" → "late-autumn trees without fruit"** (v12) — Greek δένδρα φθινοπωρινὰ ἄκαρπα.
- **"raging waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame" → "wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shames"** (v13) — Greek ἐπαφρίζοντα τὰς ἑαυτῶν αἰσχύνας, plural "shames".
- **"the blackness of darkness" → "the gloom of darkness"** (v13) — Greek ὁ ζόφος τοῦ σκότους; "has been kept" is τετήρηται, the verb of v6. "forever" is εἰς αἰῶνα, literally "unto an age": the fixed idiom, so αἰών is not rendered "age" here (it is in v25).
- **"prophesied of these" → "to these also … prophesied"** (v14) — Greek τούτοις, a dative: "to these", "for these" or "with reference to these".
- **"the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints" → "the Lord came with his holy myriads"** (v14) — Greek ἦλθεν, an aorist, "came"; ἐν ἁγίαις μυριάσιν αὐτοῦ, "with (among) his holy myriads".
- **"to convince all that are ungodly among them" → "to convict all the ungodly"** (v15) — the verse reads πάντας τοὺς ἀσεβεῖς "all the ungodly" (SBLGNT WH Treg); πᾶσαν ψυχήν "every soul" (NA28). ἐλέγξαι is "convict" here (a judge's act, with "judgment"), not the usual "reprove". "in which they acted ungodly" is the verb ἠσέβησαν; "hard things" is τῶν σκληρῶν.
- **"murmurers, complainers" → "grumblers, complaining of their lot"** (v16) — Greek γογγυσταί, μεμψίμοιροι *gongystai, mempsimoiroi*; the second word is literally "blaming one's lot".
- **"walking after their own lusts" → "going according to their desires"** (v16, 18) — Greek κατὰ τὰς ἐπιθυμίας αὐτῶν πορευόμενοι (v16, "their": αὐτῶν SBLGNT WH Treg RP; ἑαυτῶν "their own" NA28), κατὰ τὰς ἑαυτῶν ἐπιθυμίας (v18, "their own"); ἐπιθυμία is "desire"; "sinful" is not in the Greek. In v18 τῶν ἀσεβειῶν is a plural genitive, "of ungodlinesses": ungodly desires, or desires for ungodly things.
- **"great swelling words, having men's persons in admiration because of advantage" → "swollen things, admiring faces for the sake of advantage"** (v16) — Greek ὑπέρογκα, a neuter plural; θαυμάζοντες πρόσωπα, literally "admiring faces", the Hebrew idiom for showing partiality.
- **"sensual, having not the Spirit" → "soulish, not having the Spirit"** (v19) — Greek ψυχικοί *psychikoi*, from ψυχή "soul": people of the soul only. πνεῦμα has no article and may also be "spirit".
- **"building up yourselves on your most holy faith" kept** (v20) — Greek ἐποικοδομοῦντες … τῇ … πίστει; this and "praying", "awaiting" (v21) are participles around the one command "keep yourselves".
- **"looking for the mercy … unto eternal life" → "awaiting the mercy … unto eternal life"** (v21) — Greek εἰς ζωὴν αἰώνιον; no "that leads".
- **"And of some have compassion, making a difference: and others save with fear, pulling them out of the fire" → "And on some have mercy, who are doubting; and others save, snatching them out of the fire; and on others have mercy in fear"** (v22, 23) — the verse has three groups (οὓς μὲν … οὓς δὲ … οὓς δέ: SBLGNT Treg NA28); two groups, "on some have mercy, who are doubting — save them, snatching them out of the fire" (WH); Treg reads ἐλέγχετε "reprove" for the first "have mercy". διακρινομένους is "doubting" or "disputing"; it is the verb rendered "contending" in v9. "Tunic" is χιτών, the garment worn next to the skin.
- **"Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling" → "Now to him who is able to guard you from stumbling"** (v24) — Greek φυλάξαι "guard", a different verb from "keep" (τηρέω); ἀπταίστους *aptaistous* = "without stumbling".
- **"before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy" → "before his glory blameless in exultation"** (v24) — Greek κατενώπιον τῆς δόξης αὐτοῦ … ἐν ἀγαλλιάσει *en agalliasei* = "in exultation".
- **"To the only wise God our Saviour, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever" → "to the only *Elohim*, our Savior, through *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* our Lord, glory, majesty, might, and authority, before all the age and now and unto all the ages"** (v25) — the printed text has no σοφῷ "wise" and has "through Jesus Christ our Lord" and "before all the age" (πρὸ παντὸς τοῦ αἰῶνος). The Greek has no verb ("be" or "is"); κράτος "might", ἐξουσία "authority".

### Notes
- **v9** draws on the lost *Assumption of Moses*.
- **v14-15** quotes *1 Enoch* 1:9.
