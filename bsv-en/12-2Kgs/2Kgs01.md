# 2 Kings 1

[1] And ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ revolted against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ after the death of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧.

[2] And ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ fell through the lattice in his upper room that was in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and he became sick. And he sent messengers and said to them, "Go, seek by ⟦baal-zebub|Baal Zevuv|Baal-zebub⟧ the god of ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ whether I shall recover from this sickness."

[3] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ the ⟦tishbite|Tishbi|Tishbite⟧, "Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and speak to them, 'Is it because there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ that you are going to seek by ⟦baal-zebub|Baal Zevuv|Baal-zebub⟧ the god of ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧?

[4] And therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: The bed to which you have gone up, you shall not come down from it, for you shall surely die.'" And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ went.

[5] And the messengers returned to him, and he said to them, "Why is this, that you have returned?"

[6] And they said to him, "A man came up to meet us and said to us, 'Go, return to the king who sent you and speak to him: Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Is it because there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ that you are sending to seek by ⟦baal-zebub|Baal Zevuv|Baal-zebub⟧ the god of ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧? Therefore the bed to which you have gone up, you shall not come down from it, for you shall surely die.'"

[7] And he spoke to them, "What was the manner of the man who came up to meet you and spoke to you these words?"

[8] And they said to him, "A hairy man, and a belt of skin girded on his loins." And he said, "It is ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ the ⟦tishbite|Tishbi|Tishbite⟧."

[9] And he sent to him a commander of fifty and his fifty. And he went up to him, and behold, he was sitting on the top of the mountain. And he spoke to him, "Man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the king has spoken: Come down."

[10] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ answered and spoke to the commander of the fifty, "And if I am a man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, let fire come down from the heavens and consume you and your fifty." And fire came down from the heavens and consumed him and his fifty.

[11] And he again sent to him another commander of fifty and his fifty. And he answered and spoke to him, "Man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, thus says the king: Quickly, come down."

[12] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ answered and spoke to them, "If I am a man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, let fire come down from the heavens and consume you and your fifty." And the fire of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came down from the heavens and consumed him and his fifty.

[13] And he again sent a commander of a third fifty and his fifty. And the third commander of fifty went up and came and bent down on his knees in front of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ and pleaded with him and spoke to him, "Man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, let my soul, please, and the soul of these your servants, fifty, be precious in your eyes.

[14] Behold, fire came down from the heavens and consumed the two first commanders of the fifties and their fifties; and now let my soul be precious in your eyes."

[15] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, "Go down with him; do not be afraid of him." And he arose and went down with him to the king.

[16] And he spoke to him, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Because you sent messengers to seek by ⟦baal-zebub|Baal Zevuv|Baal-zebub⟧ the god of ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ — is it because there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to seek by his word? — therefore the bed to which you have gone up, you shall not come down from it, for you shall surely die."

[17] And he died according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ had spoken. And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ began to reign in his place, in the second year of ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ the son of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, for he had no son.

[18] And the rest of the acts of ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. "Man of *Elohim*" has the article in v9, 11, 12, 13 (אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים) and none in v10 (אִישׁ אֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, and no subject is supplied where the Hebrew names none ("And he sent", v9, 11, 13: the king).
- **"Then Moab rebelled against Israel" → "And Moab revolted against Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew פָּשַׁע, the verb of פֶּשַׁע "transgression": to break faith with one to whom allegiance is owed, to break away. "Revolt" says that; "transgress" would speak of a law, not of a lord. "Rebel" is kept for מָרַד / מָרָה (reviewer). One rendering with 1 Kgs 12:19.
- **"through a lattice in his upper chamber that was in Samaria, and was sick" → "through the lattice in his upper room that was in Samaria, and he became sick"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׂבָכָה, a net-like grating (1 Kgs 7:17), with the article; עֲלִיָּה, a room on the roof; וַיָּחַל "and he became sick".
- **"enquire of Baalzebub … whether I shall recover of this disease" → "seek by Baal Zevuv … whether I shall recover from this sickness"** (v2, 3, 6, 16) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ בְּ "seek by / in": to seek an answer through a god or a medium (1 Sam 28:7). אִם־אֶחְיֶה מֵחֳלִי זֶה is literally "whether I shall live from this sickness"; חֳלִי "sickness".
- **"the god of Ekron"** (v2, 3, 6, 16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי עֶקְרוֹן: the same word *elohim*, here of a god of the Philistines, so plain text.
- **"Baal-zebub" — retained** (v2, 3, 6, 16) — Hebrew בַּעַל זְבוּב "Baal (lord) of flies". Possibly an intentional deformation of "Baal-zebul" ("Prince Baal"); NT "Beelzebul" (Matt 10:25, 12:24) preserves the other form.
- **"the angel of the LORD said" → "the angel of Yahweh spoke"** (v3, 15) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה; the word is "messenger", the same word as the "messengers" of the king in v2, 3, 5, 16. דִּבֶּר "speak" (v3, 7, 9–13, 15–17) is kept apart from אָמַר "say".
- **"Is it not because there is not a God in Israel, that ye go" → "Is it because there is no *Elohim* in Israel that you are going"** (v3, 6, 16) — Hebrew הַמִבְּלִי אֵין, literally "is it from the lack of there being no": a doubled negative. In v16 the Hebrew ends לִדְרֹשׁ בִּדְבָרוֹ "to seek by his word".
- **"Thou shalt not come down from that bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt surely die" → "The bed to which you have gone up, you shall not come down from it, for you shall surely die"** (v4, 6, 16) — the bed stands first in the Hebrew; כִּי "for"; מוֹת תָּמוּת, literally "dying you shall die". **"departed" → "went"** — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ.
- **"Why are ye now turned back?" → "Why is this, that you have returned?"** (v5) — Hebrew מַה־זֶּה שַׁבְתֶּם; שׁוּב "return".
- **"What manner of man was he" → "What was the manner of the man"** (v7) — Hebrew מִשְׁפַּט הָאִישׁ, literally "the judgment of the man": here what marks him, his way and look. "Judgment", the usual rendering of מִשְׁפָּט, would say something else in English here.
- **"He was an hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins" → "A hairy man, and a belt of skin girded on his loins"** (v8) — Hebrew אִישׁ בַּעַל שֵׂעָר, literally "a man, a possessor of hair". בַּעַל with a noun names one marked by the thing (בַּעַל כָּנָף "winged", Prov 1:17; בַּעַל הַחֲלֹמוֹת "the dreamer", Gen 37:19), so "a hairy man". Some take it of a garment of hair (Zech 13:4 אַדֶּרֶת שֵׂעָר), but no garment is named here. The sentence has no verb. אֵזוֹר a waist-belt; עוֹר "skin"; מָתְנַיִם "loins".
- **"Then the king sent unto him a captain of fifty with his fifty" → "And he sent to him a commander of fifty and his fifty"** (v9–14) — Hebrew שַׂר־חֲמִשִּׁים; שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander". "The king" is not written. **"on the top of an hill" → "on the top of the mountain"** — Hebrew הָהָר, with the article.
- **"Thou man of God, the king hath said, Come down" → "Man of *Elohim*, the king has spoken: Come down"** (v9, 11) — no "O" or "thou" in the Hebrew; דִּבֶּר "spoke". In v11 מְהֵרָה רֵדָה "quickly, come down".
- **"If I be a man of God, then let fire come down from heaven, and consume thee" → "And if I am a man of *Elohim*, let fire come down from the heavens and consume you"** (v10, 12, 14) — v10 begins with וְ "and"; v12 does not. שָׁמַיִם "heavens" is plural; אָכַל, literally "eat".
- **"And he answered and said unto him" → "And he answered and spoke to him"** (v11) — the Masoretic Text has וַיַּעַן "and he answered", though nothing has been said to him; "and he went up" (וַיַּעַל, as v9, 13) is a common correction (from memory). The text is translated as it stands.
- **"the fire of God"** (v12) — Hebrew אֵשׁ־אֱלֹהִים; v10 has only "fire". The phrase may also be heard as "a mighty fire".
- **"a captain of the third fifty … and fell on his knees before Elijah, and besought him" → "a commander of a third fifty … and bent down on his knees in front of Elijah and pleaded with him"** (v13) — the Hebrew has שְׁלִשִׁים, a plural form, "a commander of a third fifty", and then הַשְּׁלִישִׁי "the third commander of fifty". כָּרַע עַל־בִּרְכָּיו as 1 Kgs 8:54; לְנֶגֶד "in front of"; הִתְחַנֵּן "plead for favor" (root חֵן).
- **"let my life, and the life of these fifty thy servants, be precious in thy sight" → "let my soul, please, and the soul of these your servants, fifty, be precious in your eyes"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; נָא "please"; "servants" is עֲבָדִים, the word "slaves"; בְּעֵינֶיךָ "in your eyes".
- **"the two captains of the former fifties with their fifties: therefore" → "the two first commanders of the fifties and their fifties; and now"** (v14) — Hebrew הָרִאשֹׁנִים "the first" goes with the commanders; וְעַתָּה "and now".
- **"be not afraid of him"** (v15) — Hebrew אַל־תִּירָא מִפָּנָיו, literally "do not fear from his face". "With him" is אוֹתוֹ, written like the object "him".
- **"Forasmuch as thou hast sent" → "Because you sent"** (v16) — Hebrew יַעַן אֲשֶׁר.
- **"And Jehoram reigned in his stead in the second year of Jehoram" → "And Jehoram began to reign in his place, in the second year of Jehoram"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיִּמְלֹךְ "and he reigned", here of the beginning, as in the regnal formulas of 1 Kings; תַּחְתָּיו "in his place"; כִּי "for". The Masoretic Text has a paragraph break in the middle of the verse, after "in his place".
- **"Now the rest of the acts of Ahaziah which he did"** (v18) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי, literally "the words / matters of Ahaziah … upon the book of the matters of the days of the kings of Israel".

### Notes
- **Ahaziah's fall** — mundane accident leads to fatal oracular misstep. He consults Philistine oracle rather than Yahweh.
- **Moab rebels** — Mesha Stela (ca. 840 BC) records this from Moabite side: Mesha throws off Israelite yoke. 2 Kgs 3 expands.
- **Elijah's garb** — hair and belt become the prophetic uniform. John the Baptist wears the same (Matt 3:4, Mark 1:6), explicitly marking him as Elijah redivivus.
- **Fire from heaven** — echoes Carmel. Judgment for impertinent summoning of Yahweh's prophet.
- **Third commander spared** — contrast: humility vs command. Elijah's fire is not automatic; it responds to posture.
- **Jesus and this text** (Luke 9:54–55) — disciples invoke this precedent for calling down fire on Samaritan village; Jesus rebukes them. Contrast between prophetic era and gospel.
- **Jehoram/Joram of Israel** — Ahaziah's brother (no son to succeed). Last Omride; will fall to Jehu (2 Kgs 9).
- **Elijah's name** — the Hebrew has the short form אֵלִיָּה in v3, 4, 8, 12 and the long form אֵלִיָּהוּ elsewhere; Ahaziah is אֲחַזְיָה in v2 and אֲחַזְיָהוּ in v18.
