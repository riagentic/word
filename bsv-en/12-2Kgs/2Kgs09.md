# 2 Kings 9

[1] And Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Gird your loins, and take this flask of oil in your hand, and go to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧.

[2] And you shall come there, and see there ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ the son of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ the son of Nimshi; and you shall come and make him arise from the midst of his brothers and bring him into a chamber within a chamber.

[3] And you shall take the flask of oil and pour it on his head and say, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: I have anointed you as king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.' And you shall open the door and flee, and you shall not wait."

[4] And the young man, the young man, the prophet, went to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧.

[5] And he came, and behold, the commanders of the force were sitting. And he said, "I have a word for you, commander." And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ said, "For which of all of us?" And he said, "For you, commander."

[6] And he arose and came into the house, and he poured the oil on his head and said to him, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: I have anointed you as king over the people of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] And you shall strike down the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ your lord, and I will avenge the blood of my servants the prophets and the blood of all the servants of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the hand of ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧.

[8] And all the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ shall perish, and I will cut off from ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ one who urinates against a wall, shut in and left, in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[9] And I will make the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ like the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧ and like the house of ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ the son of ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧.

[10] And ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ the dogs shall eat in the portion of ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, and there shall be none to bury." And he opened the door and fled.

[11] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ went out to the servants of his lord, and one said to him, "Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧? Why did this madman come to you?" And he said to them, "You know the man and his talk."

[12] And they said, "Falsehood! Tell us, please." And he said, "Thus and thus he said to me, saying, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: I have anointed you as king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'"

[13] And they hastened and took, each man his garment, and put it under him on the bare steps, and they blew on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ and said, "⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ reigns!"

[14] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ the son of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ the son of Nimshi conspired against ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧. And ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ had been keeping guard at ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧, he and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, because of ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧.

[15] And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ the king had returned to be healed in ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧ from the wounds with which the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ had struck him, when he fought with ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧. And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ said, "If it is your soul, let no fugitive go out from the city to go to tell it in ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧."

[16] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ rode and went to ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, for ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ was lying there. And ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ had come down to see ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧.

[17] And the watchman was standing on the tower in ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, and he saw the throng of ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ as he came, and he said, "I see a throng." And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ said, "Take a rider and send to meet them, and let him say, 'Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧?'"

[18] And the rider of the horse went to meet him and said, "Thus says the king: 'Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧?'" And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ said, "What have you to do with ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧? Turn around behind me." And the watchman told, saying, "The messenger came as far as them, and he has not returned."

[19] And he sent a second rider of a horse, and he came to them and said, "Thus says the king: '⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧?'" And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ said, "What have you to do with ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧? Turn around behind me."

[20] And the watchman told, saying, "He came as far as to them, and he has not returned. And the driving is like the driving of ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ the son of Nimshi, for he drives with madness."

[21] And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ said, "Harness!" And one harnessed his chariot. And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went out, and ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, each man in his chariot, and they went out to meet ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, and they found him in the portion of ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreeli|Jezreelite⟧.

[22] And it came to pass, when ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ saw ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, that he said, "Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧?" And he said, "What ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, as long as the prostitutions of ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ your mother and her many sorceries last?"

[23] And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ turned his hands and fled, and he said to ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧, "Deceit, ⟦ahaziah|Achazyah|Ahaziah⟧!"

[24] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ filled his hand with the bow and struck ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ between his arms, and the arrow went out from his heart, and he stooped in his chariot.

[25] And he said to ⟦bidkar|Bidqar|Bidkar⟧ his officer, "Lift him up, throw him into the portion of the field of ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreeli|Jezreelite⟧; for remember, I and you were riding in pairs behind ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ his father, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lifted up this burden upon him:

[26] 'Surely the blood of ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ and the blood of his sons I saw yesterday, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will repay you in this portion, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.' And now, lift him up, throw him into the portion, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[27] And ⟦ahaziah|Achazyah|Ahaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ saw, and he fled by the way of ⟦beth-haggan|Beit HaGan|Beth-haggan⟧. And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ pursued after him and said, "Him also! Strike him down in the chariot!" — at the ascent of Gur, which is by ⟦ibleam|Yivleam|Ibleam⟧. And he fled to Megiddo and died there.

[28] And his servants made him ride to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they buried him in his burial place with his fathers in the city of David.

[29] And in the eleventh year of ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, ⟦ahaziah|Achazyah|Ahaziah⟧ began to reign over ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[30] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ came to ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, and ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ heard, and she put her eyes in paint and made her head fair, and she looked down through the window.

[31] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ came into the gate, and she said, "Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, Zimri, killer of his lord?"

[32] And he lifted up his face to the window and said, "Who is with me? Who?" And two, three eunuchs looked down at him.

[33] And he said, "Let her drop!" And they let her drop, and some of her blood spattered on the wall and on the horses, and he trampled her.

[34] And he came in and ate and drank, and he said, "Attend, please, to this cursed woman and bury her, for she is a king's daughter."

[35] And they went to bury her, and they did not find of her anything except the skull and the feet and the palms of the hands.

[36] And they returned and told him, and he said, "It is the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which he spoke by the hand of his servant ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ the ⟦tishbite|Tishbi|Tishbite⟧, saying, 'In the portion of ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧ the dogs shall eat the flesh of ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧,

[37] and the carcass of ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ shall be like dung on the face of the field in the portion of ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, so that they shall not say, This is ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"box of oil" → "flask of oil"** (v1, 3) — Hebrew פַּךְ (1 Sam 10:1).
- **"make him arise up from among his brethren, and carry him to an inner chamber" → "make him arise from the midst of his brothers and bring him into a chamber within a chamber"** (v2) — Hebrew חֶדֶר בְּחָדֶר, as 1 Kgs 20:30; 22:25.
- **"I have anointed thee king over Israel"** (v3, 6, 12) — the Hebrew has אֶל, "to", for "over": "as king to Israel". **"tarry not" → "you shall not wait"**.
- **"So the young man, even the young man the prophet, went" → "And the young man, the young man, the prophet, went"** (v4) — Hebrew הַנַּעַר הַנַּעַר הַנָּבִיא, with "the young man" twice; it can also be read "the young man, the prophet's young man". נַעַר is "boy", here of an attendant.
- **"the captains of the host were sitting … O captain" → "the commanders of the force … commander"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַחַיִל; הַשָּׂר, "the prince", of an army "commander". The Hebrew has no "O".
- **"thou shalt smite the house of Ahab thy master, that I may avenge" → "you shall strike down the house of Ahab your lord, and I will avenge"** (v7) — Hebrew הִכָּה, "strike", here of killing; אֲדֹנֶיךָ. "Blood" is both times the plural דְּמֵי, blood that has been shed.
- **"For the whole house of Ahab shall perish: and I will cut off from Ahab him that pisseth against the wall, and him that is shut up and left in Israel" → "And all the house of Ahab shall perish, and I will cut off from Ahab one who urinates against a wall, shut in and left, in Israel"** (v8) — the words of 1 Kgs 21:21 (and 14:10). מַשְׁתִּין בְּקִיר is translated as it stands: every male is meant. עָצוּר וְעָזוּב, "shut in and left", is a fixed pair whose sense is uncertain ("bond and free", "under age and of age", "helpless and abandoned").
- **"the dogs shall eat Jezebel in the portion of Jezreel"** (v10, 36, 37) — Hebrew בְּחֵלֶק יִזְרְעֶאל, "in the portion (share of land) of Jezreel". In 1 Kgs 21:23, which is cited here, the Hebrew has בְּחֵל, "in the rampart", one letter less; each place is translated as it is written. In v21, 25, 26 the "portion" of Naboth is the related word חֶלְקָה, a plot of land.
- **"Is all well? wherefore came this mad fellow to thee? … Ye know the man, and his communication" → "Is it shalom? Why did this madman come to you? … You know the man and his talk"** (v11) — Hebrew הֲשָׁלוֹם, "is it peace?" (v11, 17, 18, 22, 31); הַמְשֻׁגָּע, "the madman", said of the prophet; שִׂיחוֹ, "his talk, his musing". The Hebrew has "and he said to him", a singular: "one said".
- **"It is false; tell us now" → "Falsehood! Tell us, please"** (v12) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶר.
- **"put it under him on the top of the stairs, and blew with trumpets, saying, Jehu is king" → "put it under him on the bare steps, and they blew on the shofar and said, Jehu reigns!"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶל־גֶּרֶם הַמַּעֲלוֹת, literally "on the bone of the steps": the expression stands only here and its meaning is uncertain ("the steps themselves, the bare steps", or a part of the stair). מָלַךְ יֵהוּא, "Jehu reigns / has become king", as 2 Sam 15:10.
- **"Joram had kept Ramoth-gilead" → "Joram had been keeping guard at Ramoth-gilead"** (v14) — Hebrew הָיָה שֹׁמֵר בְּ. **"because of Hazael"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי, "from the face of". In this chapter the Hebrew writes the king's name both יוֹרָם (Joram) and יְהוֹרָם (Jehoram); each is given as written. So also Ahaziah: אֲחַזְיָהוּ and אֲחַזְיָה.
- **"the wounds which the Syrians had given him" → "the wounds with which the Arameans had struck him"** (v15) — Hebrew אֲרַמִּים, the plural; מִן־הַמַּכִּים אֲשֶׁר יַכֻּהוּ, "from the strokes with which they struck him". **"If it be your minds, then let none go forth nor escape out of the city" → "If it is your soul, let no fugitive go out from the city"** — Hebrew אִם־יֵשׁ נַפְשְׁכֶם, "if your soul is": if you are willing (Gen 23:8); פָּלִיט, one who escapes. "To tell" follows the reading tradition (qere).
- **"So Jehu rode in a chariot" → "And Jehu rode"** (v16) — the Hebrew has only וַיִּרְכַּב. **"Joram lay there" → "was lying there"**.
- **"he spied the company of Jehu … I see a company … Take an horseman" → "he saw the throng of Jehu … I see a throng … Take a rider"** (v17) — Hebrew שִׁפְעָה, "abundance": a mass of men; רַכָּב, elsewhere a chariot driver (1 Kgs 22:34), here a mounted man (v18 "the rider of the horse").
- **"What hast thou to do with peace? turn thee behind me"** (v18, 19) — Hebrew מַה־לְּךָ וּלְשָׁלוֹם, "what to you and to peace?"; סֹב אֶל־אַחֲרָי, "turn around to behind me". In v19 the Hebrew has only שָׁלוֹם, "peace", without the question particle.
- **"the driving is like the driving of Jehu the son of Nimshi; for he driveth furiously" → "he drives with madness"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּשִׁגָּעוֹן, the noun of "madman" in v11. Jehu is called "son of Nimshi" here; in v2, 14 Nimshi is his father's father.
- **"Make ready. And his chariot was made ready" → "Harness! And one harnessed his chariot"** (v21) — Hebrew אֱסֹר … וַיֶּאְסֹר, "bind … and he bound". **"met him in the portion of Naboth" → "found him"** — Hebrew וַיִּמְצָאֻהוּ.
- **"What peace, so long as the whoredoms of thy mother Jezebel and her witchcrafts are so many?" → "What shalom, as long as the prostitutions of Jezebel your mother and her many sorceries last?"** (v22) — Hebrew עַד, "until, as long as", with no verb: "last" is supplied; זְנוּנִים, "prostitution" in the plural, the word also used of unfaithfulness to Yahweh; כְּשָׁפִים, "sorceries"; הָרַבִּים, "the many".
- **"turned his hands, and fled … There is treachery, O Ahaziah" → "turned his hands and fled … Deceit, Ahaziah!"** (v23) — he turns the chariot (1 Kgs 22:34); Hebrew מִרְמָה.
- **"Jehu drew a bow with his full strength, and smote Jehoram between his arms, and the arrow went out at his heart, and he sunk down in his chariot" → "Jehu filled his hand with the bow and struck Jehoram between his arms, and the arrow went out from his heart, and he stooped in his chariot"** (v24) — Hebrew מִלֵּא יָדוֹ בַקֶּשֶׁת; "between his arms" is between the shoulders, from behind; כָּרַע, "bend the knees, sink down" (Judg 5:27).
- **"Bidkar his captain, Take up, and cast him in the portion of the field of Naboth" → "Bidkar his officer, Lift him up, throw him into the portion of the field"** (v25, 26) — "his officer" follows the reading tradition (qere) שָׁלִשׁוֹ, literally "his third man"; the written text has שלשה. **"when I and thou rode together after Ahab his father, the LORD laid this burden upon him" → "I and you were riding in pairs behind Ahab his father, and Yahweh lifted up this burden upon him"** — Hebrew רֹכְבִים צְמָדִים, "riding as pairs"; נָשָׂא עָלָיו אֶת־הַמַּשָּׂא, "lifted up upon him the burden": מַשָּׂא is a prophet's utterance; it is one root with "lift him up".
- **"Surely I have seen yesterday the blood of Naboth, and the blood of his sons, saith the LORD; and I will requite thee in this plat" → "Surely the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons I saw yesterday, declares Yahweh, and I will repay you in this portion"** (v26) — the oath form אִם־לֹא, "if I have not seen", which means "surely I have seen"; אֶמֶשׁ, "yesterday, last night"; נְאֻם־יְהוָה, "the declaration of Yahweh", twice; שִׁלֵּם, "repay". The sons of Naboth are not named in 1 Kgs 21.
- **"he fled by the way of the garden house … Smite him also in the chariot. And they did so at the going up to Gur" → "he fled by the way of Beth-haggan … Him also! Strike him down in the chariot! — at the ascent of Gur"** (v27) — Hebrew בֵּית הַגָּן, "the house of the garden", probably a place name. The Hebrew has no "and they did so" / "and they struck him": the command is followed at once by "at the ascent of Gur". The ancient versions supply the missing words ("from memory"); the verse keeps the Hebrew.
- **"his servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem, and buried him in his sepulchre" → "made him ride to Jerusalem, and they buried him in his burial place"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיַּרְכִּבוּ; קְבֻרָה.
- **"in the eleventh year of Joram"** (v29) — 8:25 has the twelfth year; each verse gives its own number.
- **"she painted her face, and tired her head, and looked out at a window" → "she put her eyes in paint and made her head fair, and she looked down through the window"** (v30) — Hebrew וַתָּשֶׂם בַּפּוּךְ עֵינֶיהָ, "she set her eyes in *pukh*", the black eye paint (antimony); "face" is not written. וַתֵּיטֶב אֶת־רֹאשָׁהּ, "she made her head good": dressed her hair or adorned her head.
- **"Had Zimri peace, who slew his master?" → "Is it shalom, Zimri, killer of his lord?"** (v31) — Hebrew הֲשָׁלוֹם זִמְרִי הֹרֵג אֲדֹנָיו: she calls Jehu "Zimri" (1 Kgs 16:9–18). The words can also be read "Did Zimri, the killer of his lord, have peace?".
- **"two or three eunuchs"** (v32) — Hebrew "two, three"; סָרִיסִים, "eunuchs / court officers".
- **"Throw her down. So they threw her down: and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses: and he trode her under foot" → "Let her drop! And they let her drop, and some of her blood spattered on the wall and on the horses, and he trampled her"** (v33) — Hebrew שָׁמַט, "let drop, let go"; וַיִּרְמְסֶנָּה is singular, "he trampled her". "Let her drop" follows the reading tradition (qere); the written text has "let him drop".
- **"Go, see now this cursed woman" → "Attend, please, to this cursed woman"** (v34) — Hebrew פִּקְדוּ־נָא, the verb "visit, attend to".
- **"they found no more of her than the skull, and the feet, and the palms of her hands"** (v35) — Hebrew וְכַפּוֹת הַיָּדָיִם, "the palms of the hands".
- **"This is the word of the LORD, which he spake by his servant Elijah" → "by the hand of his servant Elijah"** (v36) — Hebrew בְּיַד.
- **"the carcase of Jezebel shall be as dung upon the face of the field … so that they shall not say, This is Jezebel"** (v37) — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, "carcass", the word for a dead body of beast or man; דֹּמֶן, "dung". These words are not in 1 Kgs 21:23. "Shall be" follows the reading tradition (qere).

### Notes
- **Jehu's anointing** — 1 Kgs 19:16; done by a young man sent by Elisha.
- **The oracle** — v7–10 take up 1 Kgs 21:21–24 almost word for word.
- **v13** — garments under him, the shofar, the cry "Jehu reigns": the commanders make him king.
- **Naboth's portion** — Joram's body is thrown where Naboth's blood was shed (1 Kgs 21:19, 29).
- **Ahaziah** — the king of Judah, of the house of Ahab by marriage (8:27), dies with it.
- **Jezebel** — the eunuchs of her own house let her drop.
- **Jehu** — Hos 1:4 later speaks of "the blood of Jezreel" against his house.
