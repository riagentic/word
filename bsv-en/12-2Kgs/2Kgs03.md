# 2 Kings 3

[1] And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ began to reign over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ in the eighteenth year of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he reigned twelve years.

[2] And he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, only not like his father and like his mother, and he removed the pillar of the Baal that his father had made.

[3] Only to the sins of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧, who caused ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to sin, he clung; he did not depart from it.

[4] And Mesha king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ was a sheep breeder, and he would return to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a hundred thousand lambs and a hundred thousand rams, wool.

[5] And it came to pass, when ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ died, that the king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ revolted against the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[6] And King ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ went out on that day from ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and numbered all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] And he went and sent to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying, "The king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has revolted against me. Will you go with me to ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ for the battle?" And he said, "I will go up. As I am, so are you; as my people, so your people; as my horses, so your horses."

[8] And he said, "Which way shall we go up?" And he said, "The way of the wilderness of Edom."

[9] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went, and the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the king of Edom, and they went around a way of seven days, and there was no water for the camp and for the beasts that were at their feet.

[10] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said, "Alas! For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has called these three kings to give them into the hand of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧."

[11] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said, "Is there not here a prophet of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that we may seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from him?" And one of the servants of the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ answered and said, "Here is Elisha the son of ⟦shaphat|Shafat|Shaphat⟧, who poured water on the hands of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧."

[12] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said, "The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is with him." And they went down to him, the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ and the king of Edom.

[13] And Elisha said to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "What have I to do with you? Go to the prophets of your father and to the prophets of your mother." And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to him, "No, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has called these three kings to give them into the hand of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧."

[14] And Elisha said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts lives, before whom I stand, surely, were it not that I lift up the face of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, I would not look at you nor see you.

[15] And now, take for me a player." And it was, when the player played, that the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon him.

[16] And he said, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Make this wadi pits, pits.'

[17] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'You shall not see wind and you shall not see rain, and that wadi shall be filled with water, and you shall drink, you and your livestock and your beasts.'

[18] And this is a light thing in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he will give ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ into your hand.

[19] And you shall strike every fortified city and every choice city, and every good tree you shall fell, and all springs of water you shall stop up, and every good portion you shall mar with stones."

[20] And it came to pass in the morning, at the offering up of the grain offering, that behold, water was coming from the way of Edom, and the land was filled with the water.

[21] And all ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ heard that the kings had come up to fight against them, and they were called out, from everyone girding on a girdle and upward, and they stood on the border.

[22] And they rose early in the morning, and the sun had dawned upon the water, and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ saw the water opposite, red as blood.

[23] And they said, "This is blood! The kings have surely fought one another, and they have struck down each man his neighbor. And now, to the spoil, ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧!"

[24] And they came to the camp of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ arose and struck ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and they fled from their face; and they struck in it, striking ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

[25] And the cities they would throw down, and on every good portion they would throw each man his stone and fill it, and every spring of water they would stop up, and every good tree they would fell, until its stones were left in ⟦kir-hareseth|Qir Chareset|Kir-hareseth⟧; and the slingers went around and struck it.

[26] And the king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ saw that the battle was too strong for him, and he took with him seven hundred men drawing the sword, to break through to the king of Edom, and they were not able.

[27] And he took his firstborn son, who was to reign in his place, and offered him up as a burnt offering on the wall. And there was great wrath upon ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they set out from him and returned to the land.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"began to reign … and reigned twelve years"** (v1) — Hebrew מָלַךְ "reigned", here of the beginning, as in the regnal formulas of 1 Kings.
- **"he wrought evil in the sight of the LORD; but not like his father … for he put away the image of Baal" → "he did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh, only not like his father … and he removed the pillar of the Baal"** (v2) — Hebrew הָרַע with the article; רַק "only"; וַיָּסַר "and he removed" ("for" is not written); מַצֵּבָה, a standing stone; הַבַּעַל with the article.
- **"Nevertheless he cleaved unto the sins of Jeroboam … he departed not therefrom" → "Only to the sins of Jeroboam … he clung; he did not depart from it"** (v3) — Hebrew רַק again; דָּבַק "cling"; מִמֶּנָּה "from it", singular, though "sins" is plural.
- **"was a sheepmaster, and rendered unto the king of Israel an hundred thousand lambs, and an hundred thousand rams, with the wool" → "was a sheep breeder, and he would return to the king of Israel a hundred thousand lambs and a hundred thousand rams, wool"** (v4) — Hebrew נֹקֵד, an owner and breeder of sheep (Amos 1:1). הֵשִׁיב "return, bring back": what a subject king pays, tribute; the form tells what he did regularly. אֵילִים צָמֶר is "rams, wool": either the wool of so many rams, or rams with their wool; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"rebelled against" → "revolted against"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew פָּשַׁע, the verb of פֶּשַׁע "transgression": to break faith with one to whom allegiance is owed, to break away. "Revolt" says that; "transgress" would speak of a law, not of a lord. "Rebel" is kept for מָרַד / מָרָה (reviewer). One rendering with 1 Kgs 12:19 and 2 Kgs 1:1.
- **"the same time, and numbered all Israel" → "on that day … and numbered all Israel"** (v6) — Hebrew בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא; פָּקַד "number, muster".
- **"wilt thou go with me against Moab to battle? … I am as thou art" → "Will you go with me to Moab for the battle? … As I am, so are you"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶל־מוֹאָב "to Moab"; כָּמוֹנִי כָמוֹךָ as 1 Kgs 22:4.
- **"they fetched a compass of seven days' journey: and there was no water for the host, and for the cattle that followed them" → "they went around a way of seven days, and there was no water for the camp and for the beasts that were at their feet"** (v9) — Hebrew סָבַב "go around"; מַחֲנֶה "camp"; בְּרַגְלֵיהֶם "at their feet".
- **"that the LORD hath called these three kings together, to deliver them" → "For Yahweh has called these three kings to give them"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; "together" is not written; נָתַן "give".
- **"that we may enquire of the LORD by him" → "that we may seek Yahweh from him"** (v11) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ "seek", as 1 Kgs 22:7, 8.
- **"What have I to do with thee?"** (v13) — Hebrew מַה־לִּי וָלָךְ, literally "What to me and to you". **"Nay"** — Hebrew אַל.
- **"were it not that I regard the presence of Jehoshaphat" → "were it not that I lift up the face of Jehoshaphat"** (v14) — Hebrew פְּנֵי … נֹשֵׂא "lift up the face of": show regard to. The oath continues אִם־אַבִּיט, literally "if I would look"; the "if" of an oath means a sworn "not". "Before whom I stand" is a perfect, as 1 Kgs 17:1.
- **"bring me a minstrel. And it came to pass, when the minstrel played, that the hand of the LORD came upon him" → "take for me a player. And it was, when the player played, that the hand of Yahweh was upon him"** (v15) — Hebrew לָקַח "take"; מְנַגֵּן, one who plays a stringed instrument; וַתְּהִי "was", not "came". The clause opens with וְהָיָה, the form that usually tells what would happen each time.
- **"Make this valley full of ditches" → "Make this wadi pits, pits"** (v16) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed that runs only after rain; גֵּבִים גֵּבִים "pits, pits": pit upon pit. עָשֹׂה is an infinitive: a command "make", or Yahweh's own "I will make"; the form does not decide.
- **"ye, and your cattle, and your beasts" → "you and your livestock and your beasts"** (v17) — Hebrew מִקְנֶה and בְּהֵמָה.
- **"he will deliver the Moabites also into your hand" → "and he will give Moab into your hand"** (v18) — Hebrew מוֹאָב "Moab", the people named as one, in the whole chapter (v18, 21–24); "also" is not written.
- **"ye shall smite every fenced city … and mar every good piece of land with stones" → "you shall strike every fortified city … and every good portion you shall mar with stones"** (v19, 25) — Hebrew חֶלְקָה "portion" of a field; הִכְאִיב is literally "cause pain to".
- **"when the meat offering was offered … there came water by the way of Edom, and the country was filled with water" → "at the offering up of the grain offering … water was coming from the way of Edom, and the land was filled with the water"** (v20) — as 1 Kgs 18:36; מִנְחָה "grain offering"; אֶרֶץ "land".
- **"they gathered all that were able to put on armour, and upward" → "they were called out, from everyone girding on a girdle and upward"** (v21) — Hebrew נִצְעַק "be called out" by a cry; חֹגֵר חֲגֹרָה, one who girds on a belt: every man of an age to bear arms. "Armour" is not written.
- **"the sun shone upon the water" → "the sun had dawned upon the water"** (v22) — Hebrew זָרַח, of the sun rising. מִנֶּגֶד "opposite".
- **"the kings are surely slain, and they have smitten one another" → "The kings have surely fought one another, and they have struck down each man his neighbor"** (v23) — Hebrew הָחֳרֵב נֶחֶרְבוּ, the infinitive with its verb ("surely"). The verb is found in this sense only here; it is taken from חֶרֶב "sword": "they have put one another to the sword". It can also be read as "have been laid waste". The meaning is uncertain.
- **"the Israelites rose up and smote the Moabites, so that they fled before them: but they went forward smiting the Moabites, even in their country" → "Israel arose and struck Moab, and they fled from their face; and they struck in it, striking Moab"** (v24) — the reading tradition has וַיַּכּוּ־בָהּ "and they struck in it" (in the land); the written text has letters that may be read "and they came into it". "The Israelites", "the Moabites" and "even in their country" are not written.
- **"they beat down the cities … only in Kirharaseth left they the stones thereof; howbeit the slingers went about it, and smote it" → "the cities they would throw down … until its stones were left in Kir-hareseth; and the slingers went around and struck it"** (v25) — the verbs tell what was done again and again. עַד־הִשְׁאִיר אֲבָנֶיהָ בַּקִּיר חֲרָשֶׂת is difficult: literally "until one left its stones in the Kir Hareseth" (קִיר is "wall"; "the wall of potsherds" is the name of the city, Isa 16:7). "Only" is not written.
- **"the battle was too sore for him … seven hundred men that drew swords, to break through even unto the king of Edom: but they could not" → "the battle was too strong for him … seven hundred men drawing the sword, to break through to the king of Edom, and they were not able"** (v26) — Hebrew חָזַק מִמֶּנּוּ "was strong beyond him"; הַבְקִיעַ, literally "to split".
- **"that should have reigned in his stead, and offered him for a burnt offering upon the wall. And there was great indignation against Israel: and they departed from him, and returned to their own land" → "who was to reign in his place, and offered him up as a burnt offering on the wall. And there was great wrath upon Israel, and they set out from him and returned to the land"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיְהִי קֶצֶף־גָּדוֹל עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל, word for word. The Hebrew does not say whose wrath it was; קֶצֶף is used mostly of the wrath of God, also of men. נָסַע "set out"; לָאָרֶץ "to the land"; "their own" is not written.

### Notes
- **Mesha of Moab** — known from the Mesha Stela (Louvre), which records the rebellion from Moabite perspective. Stela mentions "Omri king of Israel"; confirms historicity.
- **Three-king coalition** — Israel + Judah + Edom (Edom subordinate to Judah at this point, v9).
- **Elisha's rebuke of Jehoram** — prophet has no respect for the Omride; stays only for Jehoshaphat's sake.
- **Player as prophetic catalyst** — music aids prophetic reception; cf. Saul's prophesying with musicians (1 Sam 10), David's lyre.
- **Blood-red water** — refracted sunrise on reddish Edomite soil/sandstone. Moab takes it for blood and misreads the situation.
- **Mesha's child sacrifice** — "there was great wrath upon Israel" is left open by the text: divine wrath? The fury of Moab rallying its defenders? The dismay of Israel? The verse does not say.
- **Fulfilled (partially)** — Moab is devastated, but not taken. Stele claims Mesha regained independence; biblical account admits Israel withdrew. Honest report of a partial victory.
