## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Jehoiakim became his servant three years: then he turned and rebelled" → "Jehoiakim became his servant three years; and he turned and rebelled"** (v1) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word for "slave", here of a king made subject ("vassal"); וַיָּשָׁב וַיִּמְרָד, literally "and he turned back and rebelled": he changed his course.
- **"bands of the Chaldees, and bands of the Syrians, and bands of the Moabites, and bands of the children of Ammon" → "the raiding bands of the Chaldeans … of Aram … of Moab … of the sons of Ammon"** (v2) — Hebrew גְּדוּדִים "raiding bands"; כַּשְׂדִּים; אֲרָם and מוֹאָב, the names of the peoples themselves, not "Syrians", "Moabites"; בְּנֵי "sons of". **"to destroy it" → "to make it perish"** — Hebrew הֶאֱבִיד, the causative of "perish". **"by his servants" → "by the hand of his servants"** — Hebrew בְּיַד.
- **"Surely at the commandment of the LORD came this upon Judah, to remove them out of his sight" → "Only, at the mouth of Yahweh it came upon Judah, to remove it from before his face"** (v3) — Hebrew אַךְ "only, surely"; עַל־פִּי "at the mouth of"; הָיְתָה בִּיהוּדָה, literally "it was in / against Judah", with no subject named; לְהָסִיר "to remove", with no object ("it" is supplied); מֵעַל פָּנָיו, literally "from upon his face".
- **"for the innocent blood that he shed … which the LORD would not pardon" → "the blood of the innocent that he shed … and Yahweh was not willing to pardon"** (v4) — Hebrew דַּם־הַנָּקִי "the blood of the innocent", then דָּם נָקִי "innocent blood"; וְלֹא־אָבָה "and he was not willing"; סָלַח "pardon", said only of God.
- **"the rest of the acts … in the book of the chronicles"** (v5) — Hebrew וְיֶתֶר דִּבְרֵי … עַל־סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, literally "the rest of the words / matters of … upon the book of the matters of the days".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v6) — Hebrew שָׁכַב. Nothing is said here of a burial.
- **"came not again any more out of his land" → "did not come out from his land any more again"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא־הֹסִיף עוֹד … לָצֵאת, literally "did not add any more to come out". **"river of Egypt" → "wadi of Egypt"** — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed; נְהַר "river" of the Euphrates.
- **"Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he began to reign"** (v8, 18) — Hebrew בֶּן־… שָׁנָה … בְּמָלְכוֹ, literally "a son of … years … at his reigning". 2 Chr 36:9 has "eight years"; the figure of Kings is kept.
- **"came up against Jerusalem, and the city was besieged" → "came up to Jerusalem, and the city came into the siege"** (v10) — the Hebrew has no preposition before "Jerusalem"; וַתָּבֹא הָעִיר בַּמָּצוֹר. The reading tradition (qere) has the plural עָלוּ "came up"; the written text (ketiv) has the singular עלה.
- **"came against the city, and his servants did besiege it" → "came against the city, while his servants were besieging it"** (v11) — Hebrew צָרִים, a participle.
- **"went out to the king of Babylon … his princes, and his officers" → "went out to the king of Babylon … his princes and his eunuchs"** (v12) — Hebrew עַל, here for "to" (as 22:8); שָׂרִים "princes"; סָרִיסִים "eunuchs", also used of court officers. **"in the eighth year of his reign"** — the reign of the king of Babylon; Hebrew לְמָלְכוֹ "of his reigning".
- **"vessels of gold" → "utensils of gold"** (v13) — Hebrew כֵּלִים, as 1 Kgs 7:48. **"as the LORD had said" → "as Yahweh had spoken"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר.
- **"he carried away all Jerusalem … all the mighty men of valour, even ten thousand captives, and all the craftsmen and smiths … save the poorest sort of the people of the land" → "he carried into exile all Jerusalem … all the mighty ones of ability, ten thousand exiles, and all the craftsmen and the smiths … except the poor of the people of the land"** (v14) — Hebrew הִגְלָה "carry into exile"; גִּבּוֹרֵי הַחַיִל; גּוֹלֶה "going into exile"; הֶחָרָשׁ וְהַמַּסְגֵּר, two collective singulars, "the craftsman and the smith": מַסְגֵּר is from the root "shut" and its exact sense is uncertain (a smith, or a maker of locks); דַּלַּת "the poor of". For "ten" the qere has עֲשֶׂרֶת, the ketiv עשרה; the number is the same.
- **"the mighty of the land" → "the leading men of the land"** (v15) — the qere has אֵילֵי "the rams of", a word for leading men; the ketiv has אולי. **"those carried he into captivity" → "he led into exile"** — Hebrew הוֹלִיךְ גּוֹלָה, literally "made go as an exile-band".
- **"all the men of might … all that were strong and apt for war" → "all the men of ability … all of them mighty ones, makers of war"** (v16) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי הַחַיִל; גִּבּוֹרִים עֹשֵׂי מִלְחָמָה. **"brought captive" → "brought into exile"** — Hebrew גּוֹלָה.
- **"his father's brother" → "his uncle"** (v17) — Hebrew דֹּד, the father's brother; "his" is Jehoiachin's. **"changed his name"** — Hebrew וַיַּסֵּב, literally "turned about" (23:34). "Made … king": Hebrew הִמְלִיךְ, literally "made reign".
- **"Hamutal"** (v18) — so the qere, חֲמוּטַל (23:31); the ketiv has חמיטל "Hamital".
- **"through the anger of the LORD it came to pass in Jerusalem and Judah, until he had cast them out from his presence" → "on account of the anger of Yahweh it came to be in Jerusalem and in Judah, until he had thrown them from before his face"** (v20) — Hebrew עַל־אַף, literally "upon the nose"; הָיְתָה, with no subject named; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; מֵעַל פָּנָיו. The verses 24:18–25:30 stand almost word for word in Jer 52.

### Notes
- **Nebuchadnezzar II** — Chaldean/Neo-Babylonian king (605-562 BC). After Carchemish (605 BC) rout of Egypt, dominates the Levant.
- **Jehoiakim's death** — terse here; Jer 22:19 specifies dishonorable burial ("buried with the burial of a donkey"). May have died mid-siege or been executed.
- **First deportation (597 BC)** — Jehoiachin + elite; the verses give ten thousand (v14) and seven thousand with a thousand craftsmen and smiths (v16). Babylonian Chronicle confirms date and event. Ezekiel among the exiles.
- **Jehoiachin (=Jeconiah, Coniah)** — held in Babylon; Babylonian ration-tablets attest his presence and royal provisions. Released under Evil-merodach (2 Kgs 25:27-30). Line of Jesus (Matt 1:11-12).
- **Zedekiah's name change** — Mattaniah ("gift of Yahweh") → Zedekiah ("Yahweh is my righteousness"). Both Yahwistic; throne-naming asserts Babylonian authority.
- **Final collapse mechanism** — Zedekiah's rebellion (under Egyptian encouragement, cf. Jer 37) triggers destruction in ch 25.
- **Wadi of Egypt to Euphrates** — Babylonian dominance of traditional Egyptian sphere.
