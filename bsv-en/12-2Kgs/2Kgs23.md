## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"all the men of Judah … both small and great" → "every man of Judah … from small to great"** (v2) — Hebrew כָּל־אִישׁ יְהוּדָה; לְמִקָּטֹן וְעַד־גָּדוֹל.
- **"the book of the covenant"** (v2, 21) — Hebrew סֵפֶר הַבְּרִית, as Exod 24:7; סֵפֶר is any written document, "book" (its form was a scroll). "Written on this book" (v3, 21, 24): Hebrew עַל "upon".
- **"made a covenant" → "cut the covenant"** (v3) — Hebrew כָּרַת "cut", with the article. **"by a pillar" → "by the pillar"** — Hebrew עַל־הָעַמּוּד, literally "upon the pillar"; also understood as a raised stand. **"with all their heart and all their soul" → "with all the heart and with all the soul"** — the Hebrew has no "their" and repeats "with all". **"to perform the words" → "to establish the words"** (v3, 24) — Hebrew הֵקִים, literally "make stand". **"stood to the covenant" → "stood in the covenant"** — Hebrew בַּבְּרִית.
- **"the high priest, and the priests of the second order" → "the great priest and the priests of the second rank"** (v4) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל; כֹּהֲנֵי הַמִּשְׁנֶה "the priests of the second": "rank" is supplied.
- **"vessels" → "utensils"** (v4) — Hebrew כֵּלִים, any made article, as 1 Kgs 7:45–51. **"for Baal, and for the grove" → "for the Baal and for the Asherah"** (v4, 6, 7) — Hebrew אֲשֵׁרָה with the article: the goddess, or the wooden object that bears her name. "Pole" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"in the fields of Kidron, and carried the ashes of them" → "in the fields of Kidron, and he carried their dust"** (v4) — Hebrew שַׁדְמוֹת "fields" (Deut 32:32); עָפָר "dust" (also v6, 12, 15).
- **"he put down the idolatrous priests, whom the kings of Judah had ordained to burn incense" → "he made cease the idol-priests whom the kings of Judah had put in place, and one made smoke"** (v5) — Hebrew הִשְׁבִּית, the causative of "cease" (also v11). כְּמָרִים is another word for "priests", used in the Hebrew Bible only of priests of other cults (Hos 10:5; Zeph 1:4); "idol-" marks the word, the Hebrew has one word. נָתְנוּ, literally "had given". וַיְקַטֵּר is a singular, "and he made smoke", with no subject named; "to burn incense" renders a plural, as some versions have it (from memory). The verb has no object: "make smoke" (also v8).
- **"in the places round about Jerusalem" → "the surroundings of Jerusalem"** (v5) — Hebrew מְסִבֵּי.
- **"planets" → "constellations"** (v5) — Hebrew מַזָּלוֹת, found only here; the meaning is uncertain (constellations, the signs of the zodiac, or planets).
- **"brook" → "wadi"** (v6, 12) — Hebrew נַחַל. **"stamped it small to powder, and cast the powder thereof upon the graves of the children of the people" → "crushed it to dust, and he threw its dust on the grave of the sons of the people"** (v6) — Hebrew הֵדֵק "crush fine"; קֶבֶר "grave", singular; בְּנֵי הָעָם "the sons of the people", the common people.
- **"sodomites" → "male cult prostitutes" (*qedeshim*)** (v7) — Hebrew קְדֵשִׁים, the plural of קָדֵשׁ "consecrated man" (Deut 23:17; 1 Kgs 14:24). **"wove hangings for the grove" → "were weaving houses for the Asherah"** — Hebrew בָּתִּים "houses": what was woven is not known (coverings, tents or shrines of cloth); the word is kept.
- **"had burned incense"** (v8) — Hebrew קִטְּרוּ without an object: "had made smoke".
- **"the governor of the city, which were on a man's left hand at the gate of the city" → "the prince of the city, which were on a man's left in the gate of the city"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׂר "prince, chief"; בְּשַׁעַר "in the gate". "The high places of the gates": so the Hebrew, בָּמוֹת הַשְּׁעָרִים; a reading "of the goat-demons" (שְׂעִירִים) is a conjecture and is not followed.
- **"Nevertheless the priests of the high places came not up" → "Only, the priests of the high places would not go up"** (v9) — Hebrew אַךְ; יַעֲלוּ, an imperfect of what was usual.
- **"Topheth" → "the Topheth"** (v10) — Hebrew הַתֹּפֶת, with the article. **"the valley of the children of Hinnom" → "the valley of the son of Hinnom"** — the reading tradition (qere) has בֶן "son"; the written text (ketiv) has בני "sons". **"to Molech"** — Hebrew לַמֹּלֶךְ, with the article. "So that no man might": Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי לְהַעֲבִיר אִישׁ; "his son or his daughter" is literally "his son and his daughter".
- **"he took away the horses … at the entering in of the house of the LORD, by the chamber of Nathan-melech the chamberlain, which was in the suburbs" → "he made cease the horses … at the entering of the house of Yahweh, toward the chamber of Nathan-melech the eunuch, which was in the precincts"** (v11) — Hebrew מִבֹּא, literally "from the entering"; אֶל "to, toward"; סָרִיס "eunuch", also used of a court officer. פַּרְוָרִים is found only here (compare 1 Chr 26:18): the meaning is not known; a part of the temple grounds.
- **"on the top of the upper chamber of Ahaz" → "on the roof, the upper room of Ahaz"** (v12) — Hebrew עַל־הַגָּג עֲלִיַּת אָחָז, the two nouns side by side.
- **"did the king beat down, and brake them down from thence" → "the king broke down; and he ran from there"** (v12) — Hebrew נָתַץ "break down"; וַיָּרָץ מִשָּׁם is pointed as a form of רוּץ "run". Many read a form of רצץ "crush" ("he crushed them there"); the Masoretic pointing is kept.
- **"before Jerusalem, which were on the right hand of the mount of corruption" → "that faced Jerusalem, which were to the south of the mountain of the destroyer"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי "upon the face of"; מִימִין "on the right", that is, the south for one who faces east; הַר־הַמַּשְׁחִית "the mountain of the destroyer" or "of destruction" (the word of Exod 12:23). It is commonly taken as a mocking turn of הַר הַמִּשְׁחָה "the mountain of anointing", the Mount of Olives.
- **"the abomination of the Zidonians … the abomination of the Moabites … the abomination of the children of Ammon" → "the detestable thing of the Sidonians … the detestable thing of Moab … the abomination of the sons of Ammon"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׁקֻּץ twice, then תּוֹעֵבָה: two words, kept apart (1 Kgs 11:5, 7). "Ashtoreth", "Chemosh", "Milcom" each as the Hebrew prints them.
- **"images … groves … the bones of men" → "the pillars … the Asherim … bones of man"** (v14) — Hebrew מַצֵּבוֹת "pillars", standing stones; אֲשֵׁרִים; עַצְמוֹת אָדָם (also v20).
- **"and the high place which Jeroboam … had made" → "the high place that Jeroboam … had made"** (v15) — the Hebrew has no "and": the altar is itself called "the high place". **"stamped it small to powder, and burned the grove" → "he crushed it to dust, and he burned an Asherah"** — Hebrew אֲשֵׁרָה, without the article.
- **"the sepulchres that were there in the mount" → "the graves that were there in the mountain"** (v16) — Hebrew קְבָרִים; בָּהָר. **"which the man of God proclaimed, who proclaimed these words" → "that the man of Elohim had called, who called these words"** (v16, 17) — Hebrew קָרָא "call", as 1 Kgs 13:2, 4, 32. Some versions have a longer text here (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"What title is that" → "What is this marker"** (v17) — Hebrew צִיּוּן, a sign set up to mark a place. "The grave of the man of Elohim": Hebrew הַקֶּבֶר אִישׁ־הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article on "grave". "The altar of Bethel": Hebrew הַמִּזְבַּח בֵּית־אֵל.
- **"Let him alone; let no man move his bones. So they let his bones alone" → "Leave him; let no man move his bones. And they rescued his bones"** (v18) — Hebrew הַנִּיחוּ לוֹ; וַיְמַלְּטוּ, from מִלֵּט "rescue, let escape". "With the bones": Hebrew אֵת.
- **"to provoke the LORD to anger" → "to give vexation"** (v19) — Hebrew לְהַכְעִיס, "to vex", with no object: the Masoretic Text does not name Yahweh here (some versions add the name: from memory).
- **"he slew all the priests of the high places" → "he sacrificed all the priests of the high places"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּזְבַּח, the verb of sacrifice, as 1 Kgs 13:2 foretold.
- **"Keep the passover" → "Make a Pesach"** (v21, 22, 23) — Hebrew עָשָׂה פֶסַח "make a Pesach"; in v21 without the article. **"Surely there was not holden such a passover" → "For there had not been made the like of this Pesach"** (v22) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; כַּפֶּסַח הַזֶּה "like this Pesach".
- **"the workers with familiar spirits, and the wizards, and the images, and the idols, and all the abominations that were spied … did Josiah put away" → "the ghosts and the knowing spirits and the teraphim and the idols and all the detestable things that were seen … Josiah cleared away"** (v24) — Hebrew אֹבוֹת and יִדְּעֹנִים name the spirits (21:6; Lev 20:27; exact sense uncertain); תְּרָפִים is kept as a word (household images; meaning uncertain); גִּלֻּלִים "idols"; שִׁקֻּצִים "detestable things"; בִּעֵר "clear away, purge" (1 Kgs 22:46).
- **"with all his heart, and with all his soul, and with all his might"** (v25) — Hebrew בְּכָל־לְבָבוֹ וּבְכָל־נַפְשׁוֹ וּבְכָל־מְאֹדוֹ, the words of Deut 6:5. מְאֹד is the adverb "very, much" used as a noun only in these two places: literally "with all his muchness".
- **"Notwithstanding the LORD turned not from the fierceness of his great wrath, wherewith his anger was kindled against Judah, because of all the provocations that Manasseh had provoked him withal" → "Only, Yahweh did not turn back from the burning of his great anger, with which his anger burned against Judah, on account of all the vexations with which Manasseh had vexed him"** (v26) — Hebrew חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ, literally "the burning of his nose"; חָרָה אַפּוֹ; כְּעָסִים … הִכְעִיסוֹ, noun and verb of one root.
- **"out of my sight … and will cast off this city Jerusalem which I have chosen" → "from before my face … and I will reject this city that I chose, Jerusalem"** (v27) — Hebrew מֵעַל פָּנַי, literally "from upon my face"; מָאַס "reject".
- **"the rest of the acts … in the book of the chronicles"** (v28) — Hebrew וְיֶתֶר דִּבְרֵי … עַל־סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, literally "the rest of the words / matters of … upon the book of the matters of the days".
- **"went up against the king of Assyria to the river Euphrates" → "went up against the king of Assyria, by the river Euphrates"** (v29) — Hebrew עָלָה … עַל־מֶלֶךְ אַשּׁוּר עַל־נְהַר־פְּרָת. With a verb of marching, עָלָה עַל is ordinarily "go up against" (1 Kgs 14:25), and that sense is kept. But עַל also stands where אֶל "to" is expected, in this very passage (22:8 "said to Shaphan"), so that "went up to the king of Assyria" is a possible reading of the same words. The verse does not say which; the question of what happened in that year is not used to decide the rendering. The second עַל is "upon, by" the river. **"slew him" → "put him to death"** — Hebrew הֵמִית.
- **"his servants carried him in a chariot dead" → "his servants made him ride, dead"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיַּרְכִּבֻהוּ, the causative of "ride"; "chariot" is not written. **"in his own sepulchre" → "in his burial place"** — Hebrew קְבֻרָה. "Made him king": Hebrew הִמְלִיךְ, literally "made reign" (also v34).
- **"Jehoahaz was twenty and three years old when he began to reign"** (v31, 36) — Hebrew בֶּן־… שָׁנָה … בְּמָלְכוֹ, literally "a son of … years … at his reigning".
- **"put him in bands at Riblah … that he might not reign in Jerusalem; and put the land to a tribute" → "bound him at Riblah … so that he should not reign in Jerusalem; and he put a fine on the land"** (v33) — Hebrew אָסַר "bind". The reading tradition (qere) has מִמְּלֹךְ, literally "from reigning"; the written text (ketiv) has במלך "when he reigned". עֹנֶשׁ is "a fine".
- **"turned his name to Jehoiakim" → "changed his name to Jehoiakim"** (v34) — Hebrew וַיַּסֵּב, literally "turned about".
- **"he taxed the land to give the money according to the commandment of Pharaoh: he exacted the silver and the gold of the people of the land, of every one according to his taxation" → "he valued the land, to give the silver at the mouth of Pharaoh: from each according to his valuation he pressed the silver and the gold, from the people of the land"** (v35) — Hebrew הֶעֱרִיךְ "value, assess" and עֵרֶךְ "valuation"; עַל־פִּי "at the mouth of"; נָגַשׂ "press, drive"; כֶּסֶף "silver".
- **"Zebudah"** (v36) — the reading tradition (qere) has זְבוּדָּה; the written text (ketiv) has זבידה "Zebidah".

### Notes
- **Josiah's reform** — most thorough cultic purge in Judean history. Centralizes worship in Jerusalem (Deut 12 program).
- **Dust carried to Bethel** (v4) — ritual defilement of the northern rival shrine; Josiah extends reform into former-Israel territory (Assyria's weakening allows).
- **Horses and chariots of the sun** — Assyrian/Babylonian astral cult imports. Archaeology confirms solar-worship elements in Judah.
- **Topheth/Gehenna** — Valley of Hinnom becomes Jerusalem's perpetual trash-fire; etymological root of NT "Gehenna" (hell).
- **Man of *Elohim*'s bones preserved** (v17-18) — fulfills 1 Kgs 13:31-32. Long-range prophecy: the man of God from Judah prophesied Josiah's name three centuries earlier (1 Kgs 13:2).
- **Sacrificing the priests of the high places** (v20) — Josiah enforces Deut 13's penalty for apostasy with extreme rigor; done in Israelite (not Judean) territory.
- **Passover** (v21-23) — first centralized national Passover in centuries, keeping Deut 16.
- **Post-Josianic decline** — Assyria falling (Nineveh 612 BC, Haran 610). Josiah goes to meet Neco at Megiddo and dies; why Neco marched, and on whose side, the verse leaves open (see v29 above). Neco installs Jehoiakim and takes Jehoahaz away.
- **Jehoiakim's tax** — oppressive exactions; Jer 22 condemns him.
