# 2 Kings 18

[1] And it came to pass in the third year of Hoshea the son of Elah, king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ the son of ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, began to reign.

[2] He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and twenty-nine years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦abi|Avi|Abi⟧ the daughter of ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧.

[3] And he did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to all that David his father had done.

[4] He removed the high places and broke in pieces the pillars and cut down the Asherah, and he beat in pieces the serpent of bronze that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ had made, for until those days the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were making smoke to it; and he called it ⟦nehushtan|Nechushtan|Nehushtan⟧.

[5] In ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he trusted, and after him there was none like him among all the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and those who were before him.

[6] And he clung to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; he did not turn aside from after him, and he kept his commandments that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[7] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was with him; wherever he went out he acted with insight. And he rebelled against the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and did not serve him.

[8] He struck the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ as far as ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧ and its borders, from a tower of guards to a fortified city.

[9] And it came to pass in the fourth year of King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ — it was the seventh year of Hoshea the son of Elah, king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — that Shalmaneser king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ went up against ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and besieged it.

[10] And they captured it at the end of three years; in the sixth year of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ — it was the ninth year of Hoshea king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ was captured.

[11] And the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ carried ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ into exile to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, and he led them to ⟦halah|Chalach|Halah⟧ and to the ⟦habor|Chavor|Habor⟧, the river of Gozan, and the cities of ⟦medes|Madai|the Medes⟧,

[12] because they did not listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and overstepped his covenant, all that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded; and they did not hear and did not do.

[13] And in the fourteenth year of King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ went up against all the fortified cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and seized them.

[14] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ sent to the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, to Lachish, saying, "I have sinned. Turn back from me; what you place upon me I will bear." And the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ put upon ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold.

[15] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ gave all the silver that was found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and in the treasuries of the house of the king.

[16] At that time ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ cut off the doors of the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the supports that ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ had overlaid, and he gave them to the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.

[17] And the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ sent Tartan and ⟦rab-saris|Rav-Saris|Rab-saris⟧ and ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧ from Lachish to King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ with a heavy force, to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And they went up and came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and they went up and came and stood by the conduit of the upper pool, which is on the highway of the field of the washer.

[18] And they called to the king, and there came out to them ⟦eliakim|Elyaqim|Eliakim⟧ the son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧, who was over the house, and ⟦shebna|Shevna|Shebna⟧ the scribe, and ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ the son of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧, the recorder.

[19] And ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧ said to them, "Say, please, to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧: Thus says the great king, the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧: What is this trust with which you have trusted?

[20] You have said — only a word of the lips — counsel and might for the war. Now, in whom have you trusted, that you have rebelled against me?

[21] Now, behold, you have trusted for yourself in the staff of this crushed reed, in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, which, when a man leans on it, goes into his palm and pierces it. So is ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to all who trust in him.

[22] And if you say to me, 'In ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we have trusted,' is it not he whose high places and whose altars ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ has removed, and he said to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, 'Before this altar you shall bow down in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧'?

[23] And now, exchange pledges, please, with my lord, the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, and let me give you two thousand horses, if you are able to set for yourself riders upon them.

[24] And how will you turn back the face of one governor of the least of the servants of my lord? And you have trusted for yourself in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ for chariots and for horsemen.

[25] Now, is it apart from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that I have come up against this place to ruin it? ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said to me, 'Go up against this land and ruin it.'"

[26] And ⟦eliakim|Elyaqim|Eliakim⟧ the son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ and ⟦shebna|Shevna|Shebna⟧ and ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ said to ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧, "Speak, please, to your servants in ⟦aramaic|Aramit|Aramaic⟧, for we understand, and do not speak with us in ⟦judahite|Yehudit|the language of Judah⟧ in the ears of the people who are on the wall."

[27] And ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧ said to them, "Is it to your lord and to you that my lord has sent me to speak these words? Is it not to the men who sit on the wall, to eat their excrement and to drink the water of their feet with you?"

[28] And ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧ stood and called with a loud voice in ⟦judahite|Yehudit|the language of Judah⟧, and he spoke and said, "Hear the word of the great king, the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧!

[29] Thus says the king: Let not ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ deceive you, for he will not be able to deliver you from his hand.

[30] And let not ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ make you trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will surely deliver us, and this city will not be given into the hand of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.'

[31] Do not listen to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, for thus says the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧: Make a blessing with me and come out to me, and eat each man his vine and each man his fig tree, and drink each man the water of his pit,

[32] until I come and take you to a land like your land, a land of grain and new wine, a land of bread and vineyards, a land of the olive of fresh oil and of honey; and live, and do not die. And do not listen to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, for he incites you, saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will deliver us.'

[33] Have the gods of the nations surely delivered each his land from the hand of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧?

[34] Where are the gods of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ and Arpad? Where are the gods of ⟦sepharvaim|Sefarvayim|Sepharvaim⟧, Hena and Ivvah, that they have delivered ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ from my hand?

[35] Who among all the gods of the lands are they who have delivered their land from my hand, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ should deliver ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from my hand?"

[36] And the people were silent and did not answer him a word, for it was the commandment of the king, saying, "You shall not answer him."

[37] And ⟦eliakim|Elyaqim|Eliakim⟧ the son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧, who was over the house, and ⟦shebna|Shevna|Shebna⟧ the scribe, and ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ the son of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧, the recorder, came to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ with garments torn, and they told him the words of ⟦rabshakeh|Rav-Shaqeh|Rabshakeh⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 12, 22) — Hebrew *Elohim*. "The gods of the nations / of the lands" (v33–35) is the same word, of the gods of other peoples.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Hezekiah"** — the Hebrew writes the name in its short form חִזְקִיָּה in v1, 10, 13–16 and in its long form חִזְקִיָּהוּ elsewhere in the chapter. So also Hilkiah: חִלְקִיָּהוּ in v18, 26, חִלְקִיָּה in v37; and Shebna: שֶׁבְנָה in v18, 26, שֶׁבְנָא in v37.
- **"began to reign"** (v1) — the verb is מָלַךְ, "reign", here of the beginning. **"Twenty and five years old"** (v2) — literally "a son of twenty-five years".
- **"did that which was right in the sight of the LORD" → "did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינֵי.
- **"brake the images, and cut down the groves, and brake in pieces the brasen serpent … did burn incense to it" → "broke in pieces the pillars and cut down the Asherah, and he beat in pieces the serpent of bronze … were making smoke to it"** (v4) — Hebrew שִׁבַּר; מַצֵּבֹת; הָאֲשֵׁרָה, singular; כִּתַּת; נְחַשׁ הַנְּחֹשֶׁת, as Num 21:9; מְקַטְּרִים without an object. **"he called it Nehushtan"** — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא־לוֹ, "and he called it" or "and one called it": who gave the name, Hezekiah or the people, is not said. נְחֻשְׁתָּן sounds like both נָחָשׁ "serpent" and נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze".
- **"He trusted in the LORD God of Israel"** (v5) — Hebrew בָּטַח, the word of v19–24, 30; 19:10; the Hebrew sets "In Yahweh the Elohim of Israel" first. **"nor any that were before him" → "and those who were before him"** — Hebrew וַאֲשֶׁר הָיוּ לְפָנָיו, "and who were before him"; "those" is supplied.
- **"he clave to the LORD, and departed not from following him" → "he clung to Yahweh; he did not turn aside from after him"** (v6) — Hebrew דָּבַק; סוּר מֵאַחֲרָיו.
- **"he prospered whithersoever he went forth" → "wherever he went out he acted with insight"** (v7) — Hebrew יַשְׂכִּיל, "act with insight" and so succeed (Josh 1:7; 1 Sam 18:5).
- **"the coasts thereof, from the tower of the watchmen to the fenced city" → "its borders, from a tower of guards to a fortified city"** (v8) — Hebrew גְּבוּלֶיהָ; the words of 17:9.
- **"they took it"** (v10) — "they captured it" is plural in the Hebrew. **"at the end of three years"** — Hebrew מִקְצֵה.
- **"did carry away Israel unto Assyria, and put them in Halah" → "carried Israel into exile to Assyria, and he led them to Halah"** (v11) — Hebrew הִגְלָה; וַיַּנְחֵם, as pointed from נָחָה "lead": "he led them in Halah". The letters can also be read "he set them" (from הִנִּיחַ). "To" stands for the Hebrew "in"; 17:6 has "made them dwell".
- **"obeyed not the voice … but transgressed his covenant … and would not hear them, nor do them" → "did not listen to the voice … and overstepped his covenant … and they did not hear and did not do"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל; עָבַר בְּרִית, "pass over"; the last two verbs have no object.
- **"the fenced cities of Judah, and took them" → "the fortified cities of Judah and seized them"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּצֻרוֹת; תָּפַשׂ.
- **"I have offended; return from me: that which thou puttest on me will I bear. And the king of Assyria appointed" → "I have sinned. Turn back from me; what you place upon me I will bear. And the king of Assyria put upon"** (v14) — Hebrew חָטָאתִי; שׁוּב מֵעָלַי, "turn back from upon me"; נָתַן עַל and שִׂים עַל, two verbs.
- **"cut off the gold from the doors of the temple … and from the pillars" → "cut off the doors of the temple … and the supports"** (v16) — Hebrew קִצַּץ אֶת־דַּלְתוֹת: "the gold from" is not written; what he cut away was what had been overlaid. אֹמְנוֹת is found only here: "supports" (from אמן "be firm"); doorposts or pillars are meant, and the exact sense is uncertain. הֵיכָל is "temple, palace".
- **"Tartan and Rabsaris and Rab-shakeh"** (v17) — three Assyrian titles (the army commander, the chief of the eunuchs or officers, the chief cupbearer), written in the Hebrew without the article, like names; so through the chapter. **"with a great host" → "with a heavy force"** — Hebrew חֵיל כָּבֵד. **"the conduit of the upper pool, which is in the highway of the fuller's field" → "… on the highway of the field of the washer"** — Hebrew כּוֹבֵס, one who washes cloth (כִּבֵּס); "fuller" is the traditional word. "They went up and came" stands twice in the Hebrew.
- **"which was over the household … the recorder"** (v18, 37) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עַל־הַבַּיִת, "who was over the house"; הַמַּזְכִּיר, "the one who brings to remembrance".
- **"What confidence is this wherein thou trustest?" → "What is this trust with which you have trusted?"** (v19) — Hebrew הַבִּטָּחוֹן … בָּטָחְתָּ, noun and verb of one root. The verb בָּטַח stands with three prepositions, עַל (v20, 21, 24), אֶל (v22, 30) and בְּ (v5; 19:10); English has "trust in" for all three.
- **"Thou sayest, (but they are but vain words,) I have counsel and strength for the war" → "You have said — only a word of the lips — counsel and might for the war"** (v20) — the Hebrew is as short as this: אָמַרְתָּ אַךְ־דְּבַר־שְׂפָתַיִם עֵצָה וּגְבוּרָה לַמִּלְחָמָה. Either "you have said that a mere word of the lips is counsel and might for the war" or "you have said (it is only talk): there is counsel and might for the war".
- **"thou trustest upon the staff of this bruised reed … on which if a man lean, it will go into his hand" → "you have trusted for yourself in the staff of this crushed reed … which, when a man leans on it, goes into his palm"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew בָטַחְתָּ לְּךָ, "trusted for yourself"; רָצוּץ, "crushed"; כַּף, "palm".
- **"ye say"** (v22) — plural, to the three men; the rest of the speech is singular, to Hezekiah. **"Ye shall worship before this altar" → "Before this altar you shall bow down"** — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה.
- **"give pledges to my lord" → "exchange pledges, please, with my lord"** (v23) — Hebrew הִתְעָרֶב נָא אֶת, from עָרַב "be surety, give in pledge": make a wager or a bargain. **"I will deliver thee" → "let me give you"** — Hebrew וְאֶתְּנָה.
- **"turn away the face of one captain of the least of my master's servants" → "turn back the face of one governor of the least of the servants of my lord"** (v24) — Hebrew פַּחַת, "governor" (1 Kgs 10:15; 20:24); אֲדֹנִי, "my lord", the word of v23, 27.
- **"Am I now come up without the LORD against this place to destroy it?" → "is it apart from Yahweh that I have come up against this place to ruin it?"** (v25) — Hebrew מִבַּלְעֲדֵי; הִשְׁחִית.
- **"in the Syrian language; for we understand it … in the Jews' language" → "in Aramaic, for we understand … in Judahite"** (v26, 28) — Hebrew אֲרָמִית; יְהוּדִית, the speech of Judah. שֹׁמְעִים is "hear": "understand" is the sense of hearing a language. **"in the ears of the people"** — kept.
- **"that they may eat their own dung, and drink their own piss with you" → "to eat their excrement and to drink the water of their feet with you"** (v27) — the verse follows the reading tradition (qere): צוֹאָתָם, "their excrement, filth", and מֵימֵי רַגְלֵיהֶם, "the water of their feet". The written text (ketiv) has the coarse words, חריהם, "their dung", and שיניהם, "their urine". Both say the same thing; nothing is softened in the sense. "Sit on the wall" is יֹשְׁבִים, "sit" or "dwell".
- **"he shall not be able to deliver you out of his hand"** (v29) — the Hebrew has מִיָּדוֹ, "from his hand", where "my hand" is expected (the versions have "my hand", from memory); the Hebrew is kept.
- **"Neither let Hezekiah make you trust in the LORD"** (v30) — the causative of בָּטַח. **"will surely deliver us"** — Hebrew הַצֵּל יַצִּילֵנוּ, literally "delivering he will deliver us"; so also v33.
- **"Make an agreement with me by a present" → "Make a blessing with me"** (v31) — Hebrew עֲשׂוּ־אִתִּי בְרָכָה: the word is "blessing"; a peace made with greetings and gifts is meant. **"eat ye every man of his own vine … the waters of his cistern" → "eat each man his vine and each man his fig tree, and drink each man the water of his pit"** — Hebrew בּוֹר, "pit": a cistern cut in the rock.
- **"a land of corn and wine, a land of bread and vineyards, a land of oil olive and of honey, that ye may live" → "a land of grain and new wine … a land of the olive of fresh oil and of honey; and live"** (v32) — Hebrew דָּגָן, תִּירוֹשׁ, זֵית יִצְהָר (Deut 8:8 has זֵית שֶׁמֶן); וִחְיוּ is a command. **"when he persuadeth you" → "for he incites you"** — Hebrew הֵסִית.
- **"have they delivered Samaria out of mine hand?" → "that they have delivered Samaria from my hand?"** (v34) — Hebrew כִּי־הִצִּילוּ, "that they delivered": the question of the first half runs on. Who "they" are, the gods just named or the gods of Samaria, is not said.
- **"the people held their peace" → "the people were silent"** (v36) — Hebrew הֶחֱרִישׁ. **"with their clothes rent" → "with garments torn"** (v37) — Hebrew קְרוּעֵי בְגָדִים.

### Notes
- **Hezekiah** — compare Isa 36–39; 2 Chr 29–32. From v13 to 20:19 the text stands almost word for word in Isaiah 36–39; v14–16 are only here.
- **Nehushtan** — the serpent of bronze that Moses made (Num 21:8–9) had become a thing to which smoke was made.
- **The capture of Samaria** (v9–12) — told again from 17:5–6 and dated by the years of Hezekiah.
- **Sennacherib** (v13) — his own annals tell of this campaign (commonly dated 701 BC) and of the payment of Hezekiah.
- **Lachish** — a fortified city of Judah, south-west of Jerusalem; the camp of the king of Assyria (v14, 17).
- **Tartan, Rab-saris, Rabshakeh** — titles of Assyrian officers, not personal names.
- **The conduit of the upper pool** (v17) — the place of Isa 7:3.
- **"Each man his vine and each man his fig tree"** (v31) — compare 1 Kgs 4:25; Mic 4:4.
- **Hamath, Arpad, Sepharvaim, Hena, Ivvah** (v34) — cities taken by Assyria; compare 17:24, 30–31; 19:13.
