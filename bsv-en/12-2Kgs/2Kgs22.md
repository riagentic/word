# 2 Kings 22

[1] ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ was eight years old when he began to reign, and thirty-one years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦jedidah|Yedidah|Jedidah⟧ the daughter of ⟦adaiah|Adayah|Adaiah⟧, from ⟦bozkath|Botzqat|Bozkath⟧.

[2] And he did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and walked in all the way of David his father, and he did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

[3] And it came to pass in the eighteenth year of King ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ that the king sent ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the son of ⟦azaliah|Atzalyahu|Azaliah⟧, the son of Meshullam, the scribe, to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying,

[4] "Go up to ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the great priest, and let him sum up the silver that has been brought into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which the keepers of the threshold have gathered from the people.

[5] And let them give it into the hand of the doers of the work who are appointed over the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and let them give it to the doers of the work who are in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to repair the breach of the house:

[6] to the craftsmen and to the builders and to the wall-builders, and to buy wood and hewn stones to repair the house.

[7] Only, the silver that is given into their hand shall not be reckoned with them, for they are acting in faithfulness."

[8] And ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the great priest said to ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe, "The book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ I have found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ gave the book to ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧, and he read it.

[9] And ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe came to the king and brought back word to the king and said, "Your servants have poured out the silver that was found in the house and have given it into the hand of the doers of the work who are appointed over the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[10] And ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe told the king, saying, "A book ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the priest has given me." And ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ read it before the king.

[11] And it came to pass, when the king heard the words of the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, that he tore his garments.

[12] And the king commanded ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the priest and ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧ the son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ and Achbor the son of ⟦micaiah|Michayah|Micaiah⟧ and ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe and ⟦asaiah|Asayah|Asaiah⟧ the servant of the king, saying,

[13] "Go, seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for me and for the people and for all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, concerning the words of this book that has been found; for great is the fury of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that is kindled against us, because our fathers did not listen to the words of this book, to do according to all that is written concerning us."

[14] And ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the priest went, and ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧ and Achbor and ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ and ⟦asaiah|Asayah|Asaiah⟧, to ⟦huldah|Chuldah|Huldah⟧ the prophetess, the wife of Shallum the son of ⟦tikvah|Tiqvah|Tikvah⟧, the son of ⟦harhas|Charchas|Harhas⟧, the keeper of the garments; and she was dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, in the Second quarter; and they spoke to her.

[15] And she said to them, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'Say to the man who sent you to me,

[16] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am bringing evil to this place and upon its inhabitants, all the words of the book that the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has read,

[17] because they have forsaken me and have made smoke to other gods, in order to vex me with all the work of their hands; and my fury shall be kindled against this place and shall not be quenched.

[18] And to the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, who is sending you to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, thus you shall say to him: Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: The words that you have heard —

[19] because your heart was soft and you brought yourself low before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ when you heard what I spoke against this place and against its inhabitants, that they should become a thing that appalls and a curse, and you tore your garments and wept before me, I also have heard, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[20] Therefore, behold, I am gathering you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered to your graves in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and your eyes shall not look on all the evil that I am bringing upon this place.'" And they brought back word to the king.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15, 18) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign"** (v1) — Hebrew בֶּן־שְׁמֹנֶה שָׁנָה … בְמָלְכוֹ, literally "a son of eight years was Josiah at his reigning"; the verb is "reign", here of the beginning.
- **"did that which was right in the sight of the LORD … turned not aside to the right hand or to the left" → "did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh … did not turn aside to the right or to the left"** (v2) — Hebrew הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינֵי; יָמִין וּשְׂמֹאול, literally "right and left", with no preposition.
- **"the high priest" → "the great priest"** (v4, 8) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל, as Num 35:25; in v10, 12, 14 he is "the priest".
- **"that he may sum the silver" → "let him sum up the silver"** (v4) — Hebrew וְיַתֵּם, from תמם "be complete": literally "let him make complete", that is, make up the full amount. The sense is not certain; some read a form of נתך "pour out", the verb of v9 (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept. **"money" → "silver"** (v4, 7, 9) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף.
- **"keepers of the door" → "keepers of the threshold"** (v4) — Hebrew שֹׁמְרֵי הַסַּף.
- **"let them deliver it into the hand of the doers of the work, that have the oversight of the house" → "let them give it into the hand of the doers of the work who are appointed over the house"** (v5, 9) — Hebrew נָתַן עַל־יַד "give upon the hand of"; הַמֻּפְקָדִים "the appointed ones". In v5 the reading tradition (qere) has בֵּית "[over] the house" and וְיִתְּנֻהוּ "let them give it"; the written text (ketiv) has בבית "in the house" and a singular "let him give it".
- **"to repair the breaches of the house" → "to repair the breach of the house"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew לְחַזֵּק "to make strong"; בֶּדֶק "breach", a gap or leak, singular.
- **"carpenters, and builders, and masons … timber and hewn stone" → "the craftsmen … the builders … the wall-builders … wood and hewn stones"** (v6) — Hebrew חָרָשִׁים "craftsmen", in wood, stone or metal; גֹּדְרִים, those who build walls; עֵצִים "wood"; אַבְנֵי מַחְצֵב, literally "stones of hewing".
- **"Howbeit there was no reckoning made with them of the money that was delivered into their hand, because they dealt faithfully" → "Only, the silver that is given into their hand shall not be reckoned with them, for they are acting in faithfulness"** (v7) — Hebrew יֵחָשֵׁב, an imperfect, and עֹשִׂים, a participle: the verse is still the king's order, not a report of what was done (12:15 has the report). בֶאֱמוּנָה "in faithfulness".
- **"book of the law" → "the book of the Torah"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew סֵפֶר הַתּוֹרָה: סֵפֶר is any written document, "book" (its form was a scroll); תּוֹרָה "instruction, law". With the article, "the book", and placed first in the sentence. **"said unto Shaphan"** — Hebrew עַל, where אֶל "to" is expected.
- **"have gathered the money" → "have poured out the silver"** (v9) — Hebrew הִתִּיכוּ, the causative of נתך "pour out": emptied it out (of the chest), or melted it down.
- **"shewed the king" → "told the king"** (v10) — Hebrew הִגִּיד.
- **"rent his clothes" → "tore his garments"** (v11, 19) — Hebrew בְּגָדִים.
- **"Michaiah"** (v12) — Hebrew מִיכָיָה.
- **"enquire of the LORD" → "seek Yahweh"** (v13, 18) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ אֶת־יְהוָה, as 1 Kgs 22:8. **"wrath" → "fury"** (v13, 17) — Hebrew חֵמָה, literally "heat". **"hearkened unto" → "listen to"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע עַל. "Written concerning us": Hebrew עָלֵינוּ, literally "upon us"; it can also mean "laid upon us".
- **"keeper of the wardrobe" → "the keeper of the garments"** (v14) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר הַבְּגָדִים.
- **"she dwelt in Jerusalem in the college" → "she was dwelling in Jerusalem, in the Second quarter"** (v14) — Hebrew בַּמִּשְׁנֶה "in the Second": a part of the city (Zeph 1:10); "quarter" is supplied. **"communed with her" → "spoke to her"** — Hebrew דִּבֵּר אֶל.
- **"I will bring evil upon this place" → "I am bringing evil to this place"** (v16, 20) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, a participle; אֶל "to" in v16, עַל "upon" in v20.
- **"have burned incense unto other gods, that they might provoke me to anger" → "have made smoke to other gods, in order to vex me"** (v17) — Hebrew קִטֵּר without an object: "make smoke", of any offering burned; לְמַעַן הַכְעִיסֵנִי "in order to vex me" ("anger" is not a second word). "Because": Hebrew תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר, literally "in the place of this, that".
- **"therefore my wrath shall be kindled" → "and my fury shall be kindled"** (v17) — Hebrew וְנִצְּתָה, with "and"; the form is a perfect and can also be heard as "has been kindled".
- **"which sent you" → "who is sending you"** (v18) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁלֵחַ, a participle. **"As touching the words which thou hast heard" → "The words that you have heard —"** — Hebrew הַדְּבָרִים אֲשֶׁר שָׁמָעְתָּ, with nothing before it; the sentence is left open as in the Hebrew.
- **"thine heart was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself" → "your heart was soft and you brought yourself low"** (v19) — Hebrew רַךְ "soft"; נִכְנַע "be brought low", here of his own doing; מִפְּנֵי "from before".
- **"a desolation and a curse" → "a thing that appalls and a curse"** (v19) — Hebrew שַׁמָּה, from the root "be desolate, be appalled"; קְלָלָה.
- **"I also have heard thee, saith the LORD" → "I also have heard, declares Yahweh"** (v19) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי without an object; נְאֻם־יְהוָה, literally "the declaration of Yahweh".
- **"I will gather thee unto thy fathers, and thou shalt be gathered into thy grave in peace" → "I am gathering you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered to your graves in shalom"** (v20) — Hebrew אֹסִפְךָ עַל "gather you upon"; קִבְרֹתֶיךָ "your graves", plural; שָׁלוֹם. **"shall not see" → "shall not look on"** — Hebrew רָאָה בְּ.

### Notes
- **Josiah** — great reformer king (640-609 BC). Ascended at eight following Amon's murder.
- **Book found in the temple** — nearly universally identified with all or part of Deuteronomy (or proto-Deuteronomy). Internal fit: Josianic reforms match Deut 12 centralization, Deut 17 kingship, Deut 28 curses that terrify the king.
- **Historical-critical note** — the "finding" has been theorized as either authentic rediscovery (book hidden/lost during Manasseh's reign) or pious retrieval of previously suppressed text. Either way, the narrative presents it as discovery.
- **Huldah the prophetess** — one of the few named female prophets (with Miriam, Deborah, Noadiah, Isaiah's wife). Consulted in preference to Jeremiah (who was also active). No sense of unusualness.
- **Josiah's soft heart** — immediate repentance. Contrast with Ahab's partial repentance (1 Kgs 21). Yahweh postpones judgment beyond Josiah's lifetime.
- **"In shalom"** — Josiah actually dies at Megiddo (2 Kgs 23:29); "peace" here likely = before the national catastrophe, without seeing Jerusalem's destruction.
- **Temple-repair reform precedes Torah-discovery** — Yahweh rewards structural care of the house with covenantal renewal.
