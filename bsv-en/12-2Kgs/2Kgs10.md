# 2 Kings 10

[1] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ had seventy sons in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧. And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ wrote letters and sent to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, to the princes of ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, the elders, and to the foster fathers of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, saying,

[2] "And now, when this letter comes to you — and with you are the sons of your lord, and with you are the chariots and the horses and a fortified city and the weapons —

[3] then you shall see the best and the most upright of the sons of your lord, and you shall put him on the throne of his father, and fight for the house of your lord."

[4] And they feared very, very greatly, and they said, "Behold, the two kings did not stand before him, and how shall we stand, we?"

[5] And he who was over the house, and he who was over the city, and the elders and the foster fathers sent to ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, saying, "We are your servants, and all that you say to us we will do. We will not make any man king. Do what is good in your eyes."

[6] And he wrote to them a letter a second time, saying, "If you are mine, and to my voice you are listening, take the heads of the men, the sons of your lord, and come to me about this time tomorrow at ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧." And the sons of the king, seventy men, were with the great ones of the city, who were bringing them up.

[7] And it came to pass, when the letter came to them, that they took the sons of the king and slaughtered them, seventy men, and they put their heads in the baskets and sent to him at ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧.

[8] And the messenger came and told him, saying, "They have brought the heads of the sons of the king." And he said, "Put them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate until the morning."

[9] And it came to pass in the morning that he went out and stood and said to all the people, "You are righteous. Behold, I conspired against my lord and killed him; and who struck down all these?

[10] Know, then, that nothing shall fall to the earth of the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke concerning the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done what he spoke by the hand of his servant ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧."

[11] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ struck down all who were left of the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ in ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, and all his great ones and his acquaintances and his priests, until he left him no survivor.

[12] And he arose and came and went to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧. He was at ⟦beth-eked|Beit Eqed|Beth-eked⟧ of the shepherds, on the way,

[13] and ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ found the brothers of ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he said, "Who are you?" And they said, "We are the brothers of ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧, and we have come down for the ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ of the sons of the king and the sons of the great lady."

[14] And he said, "Seize them alive." And they seized them alive and slaughtered them at the pit of ⟦beth-eked|Beit Eqed|Beth-eked⟧, forty-two men, and he left not a man of them.

[15] And he went from there and found ⟦jehonadab|Yehonadav|Jehonadab⟧ the son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧ coming to meet him, and he blessed him and said to him, "Is your heart upright, as my heart is with your heart?" And ⟦jehonadab|Yehonadav|Jehonadab⟧ said, "It is, and it is! Give your hand." And he gave his hand, and he made him come up to him into the chariot.

[16] And he said, "Come with me, and see my zeal for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And they made him ride in his chariot.

[17] And he came to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and struck down all who were left to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, until he had destroyed him, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he spoke to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧.

[18] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ gathered all the people and said to them, "⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ served the Baal a little; ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ will serve him much.

[19] And now, all the prophets of the Baal, all those who serve him and all his priests, call to me; let not a man be missing, for I have a great sacrifice for the Baal. Everyone who is missing shall not live." And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ acted with cunning, in order to make those who served the Baal perish.

[20] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ said, "Sanctify a solemn assembly for the Baal." And they proclaimed it.

[21] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ sent through all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and all those who served the Baal came, and not a man was left who did not come. And they came into the house of the Baal, and the house of the Baal was filled from end to end.

[22] And he said to him who was over the wardrobe, "Bring out clothing for all those who serve the Baal." And he brought out the clothing for them.

[23] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ came, and ⟦jehonadab|Yehonadav|Jehonadab⟧ the son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧, into the house of the Baal, and he said to those who served the Baal, "Search and see, lest there be here with you any of the servants of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, but only those who serve the Baal alone."

[24] And they came in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ had put for himself outside eighty men, and he said, "The man who escapes, of the men whom I am bringing upon your hands — his soul shall be in place of his soul."

[25] And it came to pass, when he had finished offering the burnt offering, that ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ said to the runners and to the officers, "Come in, strike them down; let not a man come out." And they struck them down with the edge of the sword, and the runners and the officers threw them out, and they went as far as the city of the house of the Baal.

[26] And they brought out the pillars of the house of the Baal and burned it.

[27] And they broke down the pillar of the Baal, and they broke down the house of the Baal and made it latrines, to this day.

[28] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ destroyed the Baal out of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[29] Only, the sins of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧, with which he caused ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to sin — ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ did not turn aside from after them: the calves of gold that were at ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and that were in Dan.

[30] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, "Because you have done well in doing what is right in my eyes — according to all that was in my heart you have done to the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ — sons of the fourth generation shall sit for you on the throne of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[31] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ did not keep to walk in the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ with all his heart; he did not turn aside from the sins of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, with which he caused ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to sin.

[32] In those days ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ began to cut off parts in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ struck them in all the border of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[33] from the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ toward the rising of the sun, all the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadite⟧ and the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenite⟧ and the ⟦manassites|Menashi|Manassite⟧, from Aroer, which is on the wadi Arnon, and ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and the Bashan.

[34] And the rest of the acts of ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, and all that he did, and all his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[35] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ lay with his fathers, and they buried him in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and ⟦jehoahaz|Yehoachaz|Jehoahaz⟧ his son reigned in his place.

[36] And the days that ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ reigned over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were twenty-eight years, in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v31) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Baal" → "the Baal"** (throughout) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל, with the article.
- **"unto the rulers of Jezreel, to the elders, and to them that brought up Ahab's children" → "to the princes of Jezreel, the elders, and to the foster fathers of Ahab"** (v1, 5) — the verse gives the Hebrew as it stands: אֶל־שָׂרֵי יִזְרְעֶאל הַזְּקֵנִים וְאֶל־הָאֹמְנִים אַחְאָב, "to the princes of Jezreel, the elders, and to the foster fathers — Ahab". "Jezreel" is hard in a letter sent to Samaria, and "the foster fathers Ahab" lacks "of the sons of"; the Greek reads otherwise in both places ("from memory"). אֹמֵן is one who brings up a child (Num 11:12). "Sons" (בָּנִים) may include sons' sons. **"letters"** — Hebrew סְפָרִים, the word "book", of any written document; "sent" has no object in the Hebrew.
- **"your master's sons … a fenced city also, and armour" → "the sons of your lord … a fortified city and the weapons"** (v2, 3, 6) — Hebrew אֲדֹנֵיכֶם; עִיר מִבְצָר; נֶשֶׁק.
- **"Look even out the best and meetest of your master's sons, and set him on his father's throne" → "you shall see the best and the most upright of the sons of your lord, and you shall put him on the throne of his father"** (v3) — Hebrew הַטּוֹב וְהַיָּשָׁר, literally "the good and the upright".
- **"they were exceedingly afraid" → "they feared very, very greatly"** (v4) — Hebrew מְאֹד מְאֹד. **"how then shall we stand?" → "and how shall we stand, we?"** — the Hebrew adds the pronoun.
- **"we will not make any king" → "We will not make any man king"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־נַמְלִיךְ אִישׁ.
- **"If ye be mine, and if ye will hearken unto my voice, take ye the heads of the men your master's sons" → "If you are mine, and to my voice you are listening, take the heads of the men, the sons of your lord"** (v6) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי אַנְשֵׁי בְנֵי־אֲדֹנֵיכֶם, "the heads of the men of the sons of your lord". "Heads" can also be taken as "chiefs"; v7 shows how it was understood. **"with the great men of the city, which brought them up" → "with the great ones of the city, who were bringing them up"** — Hebrew מְגַדְּלִים, "making great".
- **"slew seventy persons" → "slaughtered them, seventy men"** (v7, 14) — Hebrew שָׁחַט, the word for cutting an animal's throat; אִישׁ, "man". **"baskets"** — Hebrew דּוּדִים, baskets or pots. "Sent" has no object in the Hebrew.
- **"Lay ye them in two heaps at the entering in of the gate" → "Put them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate"** (v8).
- **"Ye be righteous: behold, I conspired against my master, and slew him: but who slew all these?" → "You are righteous. Behold, I conspired against my lord and killed him; and who struck down all these?"** (v9) — Hebrew הָרַג, then הִכָּה: two verbs. "Righteous": in the right, without guilt in this.
- **"there shall fall unto the earth nothing of the word of the LORD"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא יִפֹּל מִדְּבַר יְהוָה אַרְצָה. **"by his servant Elijah" → "by the hand of his servant Elijah"** — Hebrew בְּיַד; the word is 1 Kgs 21:19–24, 29.
- **"slew all that remained of the house of Ahab in Jezreel, and all his great men, and his kinsfolks, and his priests, until he left him none remaining" → "struck down all who were left … and all his great ones and his acquaintances and his priests, until he left him no survivor"** (v11, 17) — Hebrew מְיֻדָּעָיו, "those known to him": close friends, not relatives; שָׂרִיד.
- **"And as he was at the shearing house in the way" → "He was at Beth-eked of the shepherds, on the way"** (v12, 14) — Hebrew בֵּית־עֵקֶד הָרֹעִים, "the house of binding of the shepherds": probably a place name; the meaning of עֵקֶד is not certain.
- **"the brethren of Ahaziah … we go down to salute the children of the king and the children of the queen" → "the brothers of Ahaziah … we have come down for the shalom of the sons of the king and the sons of the great lady"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲחֵי, "brothers", also of kinsmen (2 Chr 22:8 has "the sons of the brothers of Ahaziah"); לִשְׁלוֹם, "for the peace of": to ask after them; הַגְּבִירָה, a rank at court, the "great lady" (1 Kgs 15:13): the word says neither "queen" nor "mother".
- **"Take them alive … and slew them at the pit of the shearing house, even two and forty men; neither left he any of them" → "Seize them alive … and slaughtered them at the pit of Beth-eked, forty-two men, and he left not a man of them"** (v14) — Hebrew תָּפַשׂ; בּוֹר, a cistern.
- **"he lighted on Jehonadab … and he saluted him" → "found Jehonadab … and he blessed him"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיְבָרְכֵהוּ, "blessed", the word of greeting.
- **"Is thine heart right, as my heart is with thy heart? And Jehonadab answered, It is. If it be, give me thine hand" → "Is your heart upright, as my heart is with your heart? And Jehonadab said, It is, and it is! Give your hand"** (v15) — Hebrew הֲיֵשׁ אֶת־לְבָבְךָ יָשָׁר, "is there uprightness with your heart"; then יֵשׁ וָיֵשׁ תְּנָה אֶת־יָדֶךָ, "there is, and there is, give your hand". The Hebrew does not mark a change of speaker and has no "if"; it is commonly divided "It is." — "And if it is, give your hand", with the second part given to Jehu. **"he took him up to him into the chariot" → "he made him come up to him"** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲלֵהוּ.
- **"see my zeal for the LORD. So they made him ride in his chariot"** (v16) — Hebrew קִנְאָה, "zeal, jealousy"; וַיַּרְכִּבוּ is plural, "they made him ride".
- **"till he had destroyed him, according to the saying of the LORD, which he spake to Elijah"** (v17) — Hebrew עַד־הִשְׁמִידוֹ; דְּבַר, "word".
- **"all his servants … the worshippers of Baal" → "all those who serve him … those who served the Baal"** (v19–23) — Hebrew עֹבְדָיו, עֹבְדֵי הַבַּעַל, "those serving": the verb of "Ahab served the Baal" in v18. In v23 "the servants of Yahweh" is the noun עַבְדֵי. **"let none be wanting … whosoever shall be wanting" → "let not a man be missing … Everyone who is missing"** — Hebrew נִפְקַד. **"I have a great sacrifice to do to Baal" → "I have a great sacrifice for the Baal"**.
- **"Jehu did it in subtilty, to the intent that he might destroy" → "Jehu acted with cunning, in order to make … perish"** (v19) — Hebrew בְעָקְבָּה, found only here, from the root of "heel" and of the name Jacob; הַאֲבִיד.
- **"Proclaim a solemn assembly for Baal" → "Sanctify a solemn assembly for the Baal"** (v20) — Hebrew קַדְּשׁוּ, "make holy"; עֲצָרָה.
- **"the house of Baal was full from one end to another" → "was filled from end to end"** (v21) — literally "mouth to mouth" (פֶּה לָפֶה).
- **"him that was over the vestry, Bring forth vestments" → "him who was over the wardrobe, Bring out clothing"** (v22) — Hebrew מֶלְתָּחָה, found only here; "wardrobe" is taken from the context. לְבוּשׁ, מַלְבּוּשׁ are the common words for clothing.
- **"when they went in to offer sacrifices … If any of the men whom I have brought into your hands escape, he that letteth him go, his life shall be for the life of him" → "they came in to offer sacrifices … The man who escapes, of the men whom I am bringing upon your hands — his soul shall be in place of his soul"** (v24) — the verb "offer" is עָשָׂה, "do, make". The Hebrew as pointed is "the man who escapes" (יִמָּלֵט); "he that letteth him go" is not written, and the sense "whoever lets one escape, his soul for that man's soul" is drawn from the end of the sentence.
- **"as soon as he had made an end of offering the burnt offering" → "when he had finished offering the burnt offering"** (v25) — "he" is not named.
- **"the guard and the captains" → "the runners and the officers"** (v25) — Hebrew רָצִים, the king's guard, who run before his chariot; שָׁלִשִׁים, as 7:2. **"cast them out, and went to the city of the house of Baal" → "threw them out, and they went as far as the city of the house of the Baal"** — "them" is supplied; עִיר בֵּית־הַבַּעַל is "the city of the house of the Baal": what part of the building or precinct is meant is not known.
- **"they brought forth the images out of the house of Baal, and burned them" → "they brought out the pillars of the house of the Baal and burned it"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew מַצְּבוֹת, standing stones, in the plural, and then "burned it" in the singular; v27 has the singular "the pillar of the Baal". The verse gives each as written.
- **"brake down the image of Baal, and brake down the house of Baal, and made it a draught house" → "broke down the pillar of the Baal, and they broke down the house of the Baal and made it latrines"** (v27) — Hebrew נָתַץ. "Latrines" follows the reading tradition (qere) מוֹצָאוֹת, "places of going out"; the written text (ketiv) has the coarse word מחראות, "shit-houses" (from חֲרָאִים "dung", 6:25; 18:27).
- **"Howbeit from the sins of Jeroboam … Jehu departed not from after them, to wit, the golden calves" → "Only, the sins of Jeroboam …, with which he caused Israel to sin — Jehu did not turn aside from after them: the calves of gold"** (v29, 31) — Hebrew רַק; לֹא־סָר מֵאַחֲרֵיהֶם; in v31 מֵעַל, "from upon".
- **"Because thou hast done well in executing that which is right in mine eyes … thy children of the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel" → "Because you have done well in doing what is right in my eyes … sons of the fourth generation shall sit for you on the throne of Israel"** (v30) — Hebrew בְּנֵי רְבִעִים, "sons of fourths"; לְךָ, "for you".
- **"Jehu took no heed to walk in the law of the LORD" → "Jehu did not keep to walk in the Torah of Yahweh"** (v31) — Hebrew לֹא שָׁמַר לָלֶכֶת.
- **"the LORD began to cut Israel short: and Hazael smote them in all the coasts of Israel" → "Yahweh began to cut off parts in Israel, and Hazael struck them in all the border of Israel"** (v32) — Hebrew לְקַצּוֹת בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל, "to cut off in Israel": to cut pieces from it; גְּבוּל, "border".
- **"From Jordan eastward, all the land of Gilead, the Gadites, and the Reubenites, and the Manassites, from Aroer, which is by the river Arnon, even Gilead and Bashan" → "from the Jordan toward the rising of the sun … the Gadite and the Reubenite and the Manassite, from Aroer, which is on the wadi Arnon, and Gilead and the Bashan"** (v33) — the peoples are named in the singular with the article; "Gilead" has the article both times; נַחַל is a wadi.
- **"the rest of the acts of Jehu … are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?"** (v34) — literally "the words / matters of Jehu … upon the book of the matters of the days".
- **"Jehu slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v35) — Hebrew שָׁכַב.
- **"the time that Jehu reigned" → "the days that Jehu reigned"** (v36) — Hebrew הַיָּמִים.

### Notes
- **Seventy sons** — the house of Ahab in Samaria; the number as written.
- **v9** — Jehu owns the killing of the king and asks who killed the seventy; the heads were cut off at his letter (v6).
- **Forty-two** — also the number in 2:24.
- **Jehonadab the son of Rechab** — Jer 35 tells of his house and his commands.
- **The house of the Baal** — built by Ahab in Samaria (1 Kgs 16:32).
- **The calves of gold** — 1 Kgs 12:28–30; Jehu keeps them.
- **The fourth generation** — Jehoahaz, Joash, Jeroboam, Zechariah (15:12).
- **Hazael** — 8:12; 1 Kgs 19:17. Israel loses the land east of the Jordan.
