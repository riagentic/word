And a living thing of the field that is in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ passed by and trampled the thistle.

[11] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ did not listen, and ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went up, and they looked one another in the face, he and ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, at ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧, which belongs to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v25) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"reigned Amaziah" → "Amaziah … began to reign"** (v1, 23) — Hebrew מָלַךְ "reigned", here of the beginning; with a number of years "reigned" (v2). **"He was twenty and five years old"** (v2) — Hebrew בֶּן־עֶשְׂרִים וְחָמֵשׁ שָׁנָה, literally "a son of twenty and five years".
- **"Jehoaddan"** (v2) — the qere יְהוֹעַדָּן is translated (so also 2 Chr 25:1); the ketiv is יהועדין "Jehoaddin".
- **"that which was right in the sight of the LORD, yet not like David" → "what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, only not like David"** (v3) — Hebrew רַק "only".
- **"Howbeit the high places were not taken away: as yet the people did sacrifice and burnt incense" → "Only, the high places did not depart; the people were still sacrificing and making smoke"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא־סָרוּ "did not depart"; מְקַטְּרִים, with no object: "making smoke". Whether incense or the smoke of sacrifices is meant is not said.
- **"as soon as the kingdom was confirmed in his hand, that he slew" → "when the kingdom was strong in his hand, that he struck down"** (v5) — Hebrew חָזְקָה "was strong"; הִכָּה "strike", here of killing, the same verb for what the servants did and what was done to them.
- **"the children of the murderers he slew not" → "the sons of the strikers he did not put to death"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הַמַּכִּים, the participle of the verb of v5; הֵמִית "put to death".
- **"The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, nor the children be put to death for the fathers; but every man shall be put to death for his own sin" → "Fathers shall not be put to death on account of sons, and sons shall not be put to death on account of fathers; but each man in his sin shall be put to death"** (v6) — the words of Deut 24:16, in the same wording as there. Two things differ in the Hebrew: Kings has כִּי אִם "but" before the last clause (Deuteronomy has none), and the last verb is singular here, plural in Deuteronomy ("they shall be put to death"). The qere יוּמָת "shall be put to death" is translated; the ketiv ימות is "shall die". אֲשֶׁר־צִוָּה יְהוָה "which Yahweh commanded" may also be taken "as Yahweh commanded".
- **"He slew of Edom in the valley of salt ten thousand, and took Selah by war" → "He struck down Edom in the Valley of Salt, ten thousand, and he seized Sela in the battle"** (v7) — the qere גֵּיא־מֶלַח is translated (the ketiv has the article). הַסֶּלַע is "the crag", with the article: a rock stronghold of Edom, taken as a name. תָּפַשׂ "seize". **"unto this day" → "to this day"** — "Joktheel" is its name "to this day".
- **"let us look one another in the face"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew נִתְרָאֶה פָנִים, literally "let us see one another, faces": to meet face to face, here in battle.
- **"The thistle"** (v9) — Hebrew הַחוֹחַ, a thorny plant; "thistle" or "bramble", the kind is not certain. **"to wife" → "as a wife"**. **"there passed by a wild beast" → "a living thing of the field … passed by"** — Hebrew חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה; the word says "living", not "wild". **"trode down" → "trampled"** — Hebrew רָמַס.
- **"Thou hast indeed smitten Edom" → "You have surely struck down Edom"** (v10) — Hebrew הַכֵּה הִכִּיתָ, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "striking you have struck". **"glory of this, and tarry at home" → "Be glorified, and sit in your house"** — Hebrew הִכָּבֵד (nifal of כָּבֵד "be heavy, honored"); וְשֵׁב בְּבֵיתֶךָ. **"why shouldest thou meddle to thy hurt, that thou shouldest fall" → "why should you provoke evil and fall"** — Hebrew תִתְגָּרֶה בְּרָעָה, literally "stir yourself up against evil".
- **"would not hear" → "did not listen"** (v11) — Hebrew וְלֹא־שָׁמַע.
- **"was put to the worse before Israel; and they fled every man to their tents" → "was smitten before Israel, and they fled each man to his tents"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּגֶף (nifal of נָגַף "smite"); the qere לְאֹהָלָיו "to his tents" is translated (the ketiv is singular, "to his tent").
- **"took Amaziah … and came to Jerusalem, and brake down the wall of Jerusalem from the gate of Ephraim unto the corner gate" → "seized … and he came to Jerusalem and made a breach in the wall of Jerusalem, at the Gate of Ephraim as far as the Gate of the Corner"** (v13) — the object stands first in the Hebrew; תָּפַשׂ "seize". The qere וַיָּבֹא "and he came" is translated (the ketiv is "and they came"). וַיִּפְרֹץ בְּחוֹמַת "broke through in the wall"; בְּשַׁעַר אֶפְרַיִם is "at / in the gate of Ephraim" (2 Chr 25:23 has מִשַּׁעַר "from the gate").
- **"and hostages" → "and the hostages"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הַתַּעֲרֻבוֹת, literally "the sons of the pledges" (from עָרַב "be surety"); found only here and in 2 Chr 25:24.
- **"the rest of the acts … the book of the chronicles"** (v15, 18, 28) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי twice: "the words / matters of Jehoash … upon the book of the matters of the days". v18 has only "the rest of the acts of Amaziah". גְּבוּרָתוֹ "his might".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v16, 22, 29) — Hebrew שָׁכַב.
- **"they made a conspiracy" → "they conspired a conspiracy"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיִּקְשְׁרוּ … קֶשֶׁר, verb and noun of one root. **"slew him" → "put him to death"** — Hebrew הֵמִית.
- **"they brought him on horses" → "they carried him upon the horses"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂאוּ אֹתוֹ עַל־הַסּוּסִים, with the article.
- **"which was sixteen years old, and made him king instead of" → "and he was sixteen years old, and they made him king in place of"** (v21) — Hebrew וְהוּא בֶּן־שֵׁשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה; וַיַּמְלִכוּ "made him reign"; תַּחַת "in place of".
- **"restored it to Judah" → "returned it to Judah"** (v22), **"He restored the coast of Israel" → "He returned the border of Israel"** (v25), **"how he recovered Damascus" → "how he returned Damascus"** (v28) — one verb, הֵשִׁיב (hifil of שׁוּב "return"): brought back.
- **"from the entering of Hamath unto the sea of the plain" → "from Lebo-hamath to the sea of the Arabah"** (v25) — Hebrew מִלְּבוֹא חֲמָת, literally "from the entering of Hamath" (or a town, Lebo of Hamath); יָם הָעֲרָבָה is the Salt Sea (Deut 3:17). **"by the hand of his servant Jonah, the son of Amittai, the prophet, which was of Gath-hepher"** — בְּיַד "by the hand of"; עַבְדּוֹ is "his slave", of Yahweh's men "servant".
- **"the affliction of Israel, that it was very bitter" → "the affliction of Israel, very bitter"** (v26) — Hebrew מֹרֶה מְאֹד. As pointed the word is a participle of מָרָה, which elsewhere is "rebel" ("very rebellious"); it is generally taken as "bitter" (root מרר), and so the old versions (from memory). Uncertain.
- **"there was not any shut up, nor any left" → "there was none shut in or left"** (v26) — Hebrew וְאֶפֶס עָצוּר וְאֶפֶס עָזוּב, literally "and none shut in and none left": the fixed pair of Deut 32:36; 1 Kgs 14:10; 21:21; 2 Kgs 9:8. Its sense is not certain (those under restraint and those at large; bond and free; ruler and helper are all proposed); the words are kept. **"nor any helper" → "there was no helper"** — Hebrew וְאֵין עֹזֵר.
- **"the LORD said not that he would blot out" → "Yahweh had not spoken to blot out"** (v27) — Hebrew וְלֹא־דִבֶּר … לִמְחוֹת; דִּבֶּר "speak". **"from under heaven" → "from under the heavens"** — the Hebrew is plural.
- **"how he warred, and how he recovered Damascus, and Hamath, which belonged to Judah, for Israel" → "how he fought, and how he returned Damascus and Hamath to Judah in Israel"** (v28) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הֵשִׁיב אֶת־דַּמֶּשֶׂק וְאֶת־חֲמָת לִיהוּדָה בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל, word for word. What "to Judah in Israel" says is not clear (cities once Judah's, in David's day, brought back by Israel is the usual understanding); the Masoretic Text is kept as it stands.

### Notes
- **Names** — the Hebrew writes the king of Israel as יוֹאָשׁ (v1, 23, 27) and יְהוֹאָשׁ (v8–17), his father as יוֹאָחָז (v1) and יְהוֹאָחָז (v8, 17), and Amaziah as אֲמַצְיָהוּ and אֲמַצְיָה (v8); the tokens give one form for each name, except Joahaz / Jehoahaz and Joash / Jehoash, which follow the Hebrew.
- **Chronology** — the figures are as the Masoretic Text prints them (v1, 2, 17, 23); 15:1 puts the accession of Azariah in the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam, which does not agree with v17 and v23.
- **Deut 24:16 cited directly** — rare explicit quotation of the Torah's individual-responsibility principle. Amaziah restrains the Near Eastern norm of exterminating traitors' families.
- **Sela = Petra?** — later identified as Petra by many; Hebrew *sela* = crag. Renamed Joktheel.
- **Fable of thistle and cedar** — Jehoash's put-down. Amaziah overreaches after Edom victory.
- **Beth-shemesh battle** — Judah's defeat. Jerusalem wall breached; the house of Yahweh plundered again.
- **Elath** — Red-Sea port rebuilt under Azariah. Strategic for Red Sea trade.
- **Jeroboam II** — longest Northern reign (41 years). Territorial expansion. Amos and Hosea prophesy in his days (Amos 1:1; Hos 1:1); Jonah the son of Amittai is named here.
- **"Lebo-hamath to the sea of the Arabah"** — compare the bounds named in 1 Kgs 8:65.
- **Yahweh's forbearance** — even under a king who did the evil, because Yahweh "had not spoken to blot out the name of Israel".
