# 2 Kings 2

[1] And it came to pass, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was bringing ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ up in the storm to the heavens, that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ and Elisha went from Gilgal.

[2] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to Elisha, "Sit here, please, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has sent me as far as ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧." And Elisha said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives and by the life of your soul, I will not forsake you." And they went down to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧.

[3] And the sons of the prophets who were at ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is taking your lord from over your head?" And he said, "I also know; keep silent."

[4] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to him, "Elisha, sit here, please, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has sent me to ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧." And he said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives and by the life of your soul, I will not forsake you." And they came to ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧.

[5] And the sons of the prophets who were at ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ drew near to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that today ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is taking your lord from over your head?" And he said, "I also know; keep silent."

[6] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to him, "Sit here, please, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has sent me to the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧." And he said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives and by the life of your soul, I will not forsake you." And the two of them went.

[7] And fifty men of the sons of the prophets went and stood opposite, at a distance, and the two of them stood by the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[8] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ took his mantle and rolled it up and struck the waters, and they were divided this way and that, and the two of them passed over on the dry ground.

[9] And it came to pass, when they had passed over, that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to Elisha, "Ask what I shall do for you before I am taken from you." And Elisha said, "And let there be, please, a double portion of your spirit to me."

[10] And he said, "You have made your asking hard. If you see me taken from you, it shall be so for you; and if not, it shall not be."

[11] And it came to pass, as they were going, going and speaking, that behold, chariots of fire and horses of fire, and they parted the two of them; and ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ went up in the storm to the heavens.

[12] And Elisha was seeing, and he was crying out, "My father, my father! The chariots of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and its horsemen!" And he saw him no more. And he took hold of his garments and tore them into two torn pieces.

[13] And he lifted up the mantle of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ that had fallen from upon him, and he returned and stood on the shore of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[14] And he took the mantle of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ that had fallen from upon him and struck the waters and said, "Where is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧?" And he also struck the waters, and they were divided this way and that, and Elisha passed over.

[15] And the sons of the prophets who were at ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, opposite, saw him and said, "The spirit of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ has rested on Elisha." And they came to meet him and bowed down to him to the earth.

[16] And they said to him, "Behold, please, there are with your servants fifty men, sons of ability; let them go, please, and seek your lord, lest the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has carried him and thrown him on one of the mountains or in one of the valleys." [17] And they pressed him until he was ashamed, and he said, "Send." And they sent fifty men, and they sought three days and did not find him.

[18] And they returned to him, and he was dwelling in ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, and he said to them, "Did I not say to you, 'Do not go'?"

[19] And the men of the city said to Elisha, "Behold, please, the site of the city is good, as my lord sees, and the waters are bad, and the land bereaves."

[20] And he said, "Take for me a new bowl and put salt there." And they took it to him.

[21] And he went out to the going out of the waters and threw salt there and said, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'I have healed these waters; there shall not be from there any more death and bereaving.'"

[22] And the waters were healed, to this day, according to the word of Elisha that he spoke.

[23] And he went up from there to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧. And as he was going up on the way, small boys came out from the city and scoffed at him and said to him, "Go up, bald one! Go up, bald one!"

[24] And he turned behind him and saw them and cursed them in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And two she-bears came out from the forest and split open forty-two children of them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when the LORD would take up Elijah into heaven by a whirlwind, that Elijah went with Elisha" → "when Yahweh was bringing Elijah up in the storm to the heavens, that Elijah and Elisha went"** (v1, 11) — Hebrew בְּהַעֲלוֹת "in the bringing up" (the causative of "go up"); בַּסְעָרָה "in the storm", with the article: סְעָרָה is a storm wind; "whirl" is not in the word. שָׁמַיִם "heavens" is plural. "Gilgal" has the article in the Hebrew.
- **"Tarry here, I pray thee" → "Sit here, please"** (v2, 4, 6) — Hebrew שֵׁב, the verb "sit, dwell": remain here. נָא "please". **"to Bethel" → "as far as Bethel"** (v2) — Hebrew עַד.
- **"As the LORD liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee" → "As Yahweh lives and by the life of your soul, I will not forsake you"** (v2, 4, 6) — Hebrew חַי־יְהוָה וְחֵי־נַפְשְׁךָ: two forms, two wordings, as 1 Sam 20:3; 25:26. The oath continues אִם־אֶעֶזְבֶךָּ, literally "if I forsake you"; the "if" of an oath means a sworn "not". עָזַב "forsake".
- **"the LORD will take away thy master from thy head to day … Yea, I know it; hold ye your peace" → "today Yahweh is taking your lord from over your head … I also know; keep silent"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew לֹקֵחַ, a participle; אָדוֹן "lord"; מֵעַל רֹאשֶׁךָ "from upon your head"; גַּם־אֲנִי יָדַעְתִּי "I also know"; הֶחֱשׁוּ "keep silent".
- **"stood to view afar off" → "stood opposite, at a distance"** (v7, 15) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶד "opposite", מֵרָחוֹק "from afar"; "to view" is not written.
- **"wrapped it together, and smote the waters, and they were divided hither and thither, so that they two went over on dry ground" → "rolled it up and struck the waters, and they were divided this way and that, and the two of them passed over on the dry ground"** (v8, 14) — Hebrew גָּלַם is found only here as a verb: "wrap, roll together". חָצָה "divide in half"; עָבַר "pass over"; חָרָבָה "dry ground".
- **"let a double portion of thy spirit be upon me" → "And let there be, please, a double portion of your spirit to me"** (v9) — Hebrew פִּי שְׁנַיִם, literally "a mouth of two": two parts, the share of the firstborn (Deut 21:17), or two thirds (Zech 13:8); not "twice as much as you have". בְּרוּחֲךָ is literally "in your spirit"; אֵלָי "to me". The sentence begins with "and".
- **"Thou hast asked a hard thing" → "You have made your asking hard"** (v10) — Hebrew הִקְשִׁיתָ לִשְׁאוֹל, literally "you have made hard to ask". **"when I am taken"** — Hebrew לֻקָּח "taken", without "when".
- **"as they still went on, and talked, that, behold, there appeared a chariot of fire, and horses of fire, and parted them both asunder" → "as they were going, going and speaking, that behold, chariots of fire and horses of fire, and they parted the two of them"** (v11) — Hebrew הֹלְכִים הָלוֹךְ וְדַבֵּר; "there appeared" is not written. רֶכֶב is a collective singular: "chariots", or one chariot; so also in v12.
- **"And Elisha saw it, and he cried" → "And Elisha was seeing, and he was crying out"** (v12) — two participles; no "it". **"his own clothes, and rent them in two pieces" → "his garments and tore them into two torn pieces"** — Hebrew לִשְׁנַיִם קְרָעִים, with the noun of the verb "tear"; "own" is not written.
- **"the bank of Jordan" → "the shore of the Jordan"** (v13) — Hebrew שְׂפַת, literally "the lip of".
- **"Where is the LORD God of Elijah? and when he also had smitten the waters" → "Where is Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Elijah?" And he also struck the waters"** (v14) — Hebrew אַף־הוּא "even he / he also". The Masoretic accents set it with what follows ("he also, and he struck"); it can also be read with the question: "Where is Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Elijah, even he?"
- **"The spirit of Elijah doth rest on Elisha" → "has rested"** (v15) — Hebrew נָחָה, a perfect. **"bowed themselves to the ground before him" → "bowed down to him to the earth"**.
- **"fifty strong men … peradventure the Spirit of the LORD hath taken him up, and cast him upon some mountain, or into some valley" → "fifty men, sons of ability … lest the spirit of Yahweh has carried him and thrown him on one of the mountains or in one of the valleys"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־חַיִל; פֶּן "lest"; נָשָׂא as 1 Kgs 18:12; רוּחַ is "spirit" or "wind". "Valleys": the reading tradition and the written text have two spellings of the same word.
- **"when they urged him till he was ashamed" → "they pressed him until he was ashamed"** (v17) — Hebrew פָּצַר בְּ; עַד־בֹּשׁ as Judg 3:25.
- **"for he tarried at Jericho" → "and he was dwelling in Jericho"** (v18) — Hebrew יֹשֵׁב.
- **"the situation of this city is pleasant … but the water is naught, and the ground barren" → "the site of the city is good … and the waters are bad, and the land bereaves"** (v19, 21) — Hebrew מוֹשַׁב הָעִיר, literally "the dwelling / seat of the city"; טוֹב "good"; רָעִים "evil, bad"; מְשַׁכָּלֶת, the participle of שִׁכֵּל "bereave of children": it makes childless, of miscarriage or of the death of the young. In v21 the same word is "bereaving".
- **"Bring me a new cruse, and put salt therein" → "Take for me a new bowl and put salt there"** (v20) — Hebrew לָקַח "take"; צְלֹחִית is found only here: a bowl or dish, the kind uncertain.
- **"the spring of the waters, and cast the salt in there" → "the going out of the waters and threw salt there"** (v21) — Hebrew מוֹצָא הַמַּיִם, the place where the water comes out: the spring. **"there shall not be from thence any more death or barren land" → "there shall not be from there any more death and bereaving"**.
- **"there came forth little children out of the city, and mocked him … Go up, thou bald head" → "small boys came out from the city and scoffed at him … Go up, bald one"** (v23) — Hebrew נְעָרִים קְטַנִּים: נַעַר "boy", a word also used of attendants and young men; קָטֹן "small, young" (Solomon calls himself נַעַר קָטֹן, 1 Kgs 3:7). No age is stated. הִתְקַלֵּס "scoff, make sport of"; קֵרֵחַ "bald".
- **"And he turned back, and looked on them … and tare forty and two children of them" → "And he turned behind him and saw them … and split open forty-two children of them"** (v24) — Hebrew יְלָדִים "children", another word than in v23, also used of young men (1 Kgs 12:8); again no age is stated. קִלֵּל "curse", literally "make light of". בָּקַע piel "split, tear open"; the Hebrew does not say whether they died. "She-bears": the number and the verbs are feminine, the noun has the masculine plural form.

### Notes
- **Elijah's translation** — second person in Scripture taken without death (Enoch, Gen 5:24). Becomes basis for expectation of Elijah's return (Mal 4:5, NT).
- **Gilgal → Bethel → Jericho → Jordan** — reverse-conquest itinerary; Elijah retraces Joshua's entry in reverse, exiting through the Jordan.
- **Dividing Jordan** — Elijah crosses like Moses at the sea, Joshua at the Jordan. Succession of covenant-mediators.
- **Double portion** — inheritance law (Deut 21:17): firstborn's share. Elisha asks to inherit as Elijah's firstborn prophet-son.
- **Chariots of Israel** (v12) — repeated at Elisha's death (2 Kgs 13:14) by Joash. The prophet as true military power of Israel.
- **Healing of Jericho's waters** — reverses Jericho's curse (Josh 6:26, 1 Kgs 16:34). Elisha inaugurates ministry with grace.
- **Bears at Bethel** — at the site of Jeroboam's golden calf, the young of the city scoff at the prophet. The curse / judgment frames Bethel's rebellion against Yahweh's representatives.
- **"Go up"** (v23) — likely mocking Elijah's ascension: "go up [like your master did]." Not mere bald-joke.
