## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"began … to reign"** (v1, 8, 13, 17, 23, 27, 32) — Hebrew מָלַךְ "reigned", here of the beginning; with a length of time "reigned". Where the length follows the same verb, it is set after a comma ("began to reign … six months"). The Hebrew repeats "year" with the numbers. **"Sixteen years old was he"** (v2, 33) — Hebrew בֶּן־… שָׁנָה, literally "a son of sixteen years".
- **"that which was right in the sight of the LORD" → "what was right in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v3, 34); **"that which was evil in the sight of the LORD" → "the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v9, 18, 24, 28) — Hebrew הַיָּשָׁר · הָרַע בְּעֵינֵי יְהוָה.
- **"Save that the high places were not removed: the people sacrificed and burnt incense still" → "Only, the high places did not depart; the people were still sacrificing and making smoke"** (v4, 35) — Hebrew לֹא־סָרוּ "did not depart"; מְקַטְּרִים, with no object: "making smoke". Whether incense or the smoke of sacrifices is meant is not said.
- **"the LORD smote the king, so that he was a leper" → "Yahweh plagued the king, and he was a leper"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיְנַגַּע, the piel of נָגַע "touch": strike with a plague (נֶגַע), as Gen 12:17. מְצֹרָע is one struck with צָרַעַת, a scaly outbreak that makes unclean; it is not the leprosy of modern medicine, and no diagnosis is put in the verse.
- **"dwelt in a several house" → "dwelt in the house of freedom"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּבֵית הַחָפְשִׁית, word for word; חָפְשִׁי is "free" (Exod 21:2). What the name says is not known: a house where he was "free" of the duties of a king, a house apart, or a softened name for a place of shutting away are all proposed (and a like expression in Ugaritic is said to name the underworld: from memory). 2 Chr 26:21 has the same words and adds "for he was cut off from the house of Yahweh". Uncertain.
- **"over the house, judging the people of the land"** (v5) — Hebrew עַל־הַבַּיִת, the title of the one set over the king's house (1 Kgs 4:6).
- **"the rest of the acts … the book of the chronicles"** (v6 and often) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי twice: "the words / matters of Azariah … upon the book of the matters of the days". Where the Hebrew has הִנָּם it is "behold, they are written" (v11, 15, 26, 31); where it has הֲלֹא־הֵם it is "are they not written" (v6, 21, 36). v36 has "that he did", with no "all".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v7, 22, 38) — Hebrew שָׁכַב.
- **"departed not from the sins of Jeroboam … who made Israel to sin" → "did not turn aside from the sins of Jeroboam …, with which he caused Israel to sin"** (v9, 18, 24, 28) — Hebrew לֹא סָר מִן; הֶחֱטִיא hifil. In v18 the Hebrew has מֵעַל "from upon" in place of מִן "from", and adds "all his days" at the end of the verse.
- **"smote him before the people, and slew him" → "struck him before the people and put him to death"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיַּכֵּהוּ קָבָלְ־עָם וַיְמִיתֵהוּ. קָבָלְ־עָם is taken as "before (the) people", with a word known from Aramaic and found nowhere else in Hebrew; the form is strange, and many read a place name, "in Ibleam" (so, it is said, one form of the Greek: from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept. Uncertain. **"struck … put to death"** (v10, 14, 25, 30) — two verbs each time, הִכָּה and הֵמִית.
- **"Thy sons shall sit on the throne of Israel unto the fourth generation" → "Sons of the fourth generation shall sit for you on the throne of Israel"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּנֵי רְבִיעִים יֵשְׁבוּ לְךָ, the words of 10:30; רְבִיעִים is "those of the fourth (generation)". **"And so it came to pass" → "And it was so"** — Hebrew וַיְהִי־כֵן.
- **"a full month" → "a month of days"** (v13) — Hebrew יֶרַח־יָמִים.
- **"the conspiracy which he made" → "his conspiracy that he conspired"** (v15) — Hebrew וְקִשְׁרוֹ אֲשֶׁר קָשָׁר; so also "conspired a conspiracy" (v30).
- **"smote Tiphsah, and all that were therein, and the coasts thereof from Tirzah: because they opened not to him, therefore he smote it; and all the women therein that were with child he ripped up" → "struck Tiphsah and all who were in it and its borders, from Tirzah, for it did not open, and he struck; all its women with child he split open"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי לֹא פָתַח וַיַּךְ: "for he / it did not open, and he struck"; no "to him" and no object after "struck". גְּבוּלֶיהָ "its borders" (its territory). בִּקֵּעַ, piel of בָּקַע "split". Tiphsah is elsewhere the far bound of Solomon's rule "across the River" (1 Kgs 4:24); which town is meant here is not certain (some read "Tappuah" with a form of the Greek: from memory).
- **"that his hand might be with him to confirm the kingdom in his hand" → "for his hands to be with him, to hold fast the kingdom in his hand"** (v19) — Hebrew לִהְיוֹת יָדָיו אִתּוֹ "his hands" (plural); לְהַחֲזִיק, hifil of חָזַק. The verse begins without "and". **"talents"** — Hebrew כִּכָּר, literally "a round": a weight.
- **"Menahem exacted the money of Israel, even of all the mighty men of wealth" → "Menahem brought out the silver upon Israel, upon all the mighty ones of ability"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיֹּצֵא … אֶת־הַכֶּסֶף עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל, hifil of יָצָא "go out" with עַל "upon": he laid the silver on them as a charge and drew it from them ("exacted"). גִּבּוֹרֵי הַחַיִל "the mighty ones of ability"; חַיִל is "ability", of goods "wealth", of an army "force": men of standing, who can bear arms or pay. **"of each man fifty shekels of silver" → "fifty shekels of silver for each man"** — literally "for one man"; the shekel is a weight, not a coin. **"stayed not there" → "did not stand there"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־עָמַד.
- **"a captain of his" → "his officer"** (v25) — Hebrew שָׁלִישׁוֹ, literally "his third man": the third man in a chariot, or a rank; not certain. **"in the palace of the king's house" → "in the citadel of the house of the king"** — Hebrew אַרְמוֹן (as 1 Kgs 16:18); the qere הַמֶּלֶךְ has the article. **"with Argob and Arieh"** — Hebrew אֶת־אַרְגֹּב וְאֶת־הָאַרְיֵה. אֶת may be "with" (Argob and Arieh as men who fell with the king, or who struck with Pekah), or the mark of the object. הָאַרְיֵה has the article and is the common word "the lion"; Argob is elsewhere a region of Bashan (Deut 3:4). The words are obscure; some take them as place names that belong in v29. The Masoretic Text is kept. **"of the Gileadites" → "of the sons of the Gileadites"** — Hebrew מִבְּנֵי גִלְעָדִים.
- **"took Ijon … and Gilead, and Galilee" → "took Ijon and … Gilead and Galilee"** (v29) — every name has "and" in the Hebrew; "Gilead" and "Galilee" have the article (הַגִּלְעָד, הַגָּלִילָה). **"carried them captive to Assyria" → "carried them into exile to Assyria"** — Hebrew וַיַּגְלֵם, hifil of גָּלָה "go into exile" (the verb of 17:6; 24:14).
- **"the higher gate" → "the upper gate"** (v35) — Hebrew שַׁעַר … הָעֶלְיוֹן.
- **"began to send against Judah"** (v37) — Hebrew לְהַשְׁלִיחַ בִּיהוּדָה, the hifil of שָׁלַח: "let loose upon Judah".

### Notes
- **Azariah = Uzziah** — one king under two names in this chapter: עֲזַרְיָה / עֲזַרְיָהוּ (v1, 6, 7, 8, 17, 23, 27) and עֻזִיָּה / עֻזִיָּהוּ (v13, 30, 32, 34). 2 Chr 26:16–21 tells how he became a leper.
- **Chronology** — the figures are as the Masoretic Text prints them, and they do not all agree: v1 (the twenty-seventh year of Jeroboam) beside 14:17, 23; v30 (the twentieth year of Jotham) beside v33 (Jotham reigned sixteen years); v27 (Pekah twenty years) beside 17:1. Nothing is adjusted.
- **Fourth-generation fulfillment** — Jehu's house ends with Zechariah (Jehu → Jehoahaz → Joash → Jeroboam → Zechariah). The word of 10:30 is fulfilled (v12).
- **Rapid regicides** — Zechariah (six months), Shallum (a month), Menahem, Pekahiah (two years), Pekah. The northern kingdom is breaking up.
- **Menahem's brutality** (v16) — the splitting open of women with child is named also in 8:12; Amos 1:13; Hos 13:16.
- **Pul and Tiglath-pileser** — commonly held to be one king, Tiglath-pileser III of Assyria (745–727 BC), "Pul" being his name in Babylon; 1 Chr 5:26 names both. His own records are said to list the tribute of Menahem of Samaria (from memory).
- **Galilee and Gilead lost** (v29) — first carrying away of the north into exile; compare Isa 9:1.
- **Rezin and Pekah against Judah** (v37) — the war of Aram and Israel against Judah; backdrop for Isa 7 and 2 Kgs 16.
