# 2 Samuel 21

[1] And there was a famine in the days of David, three years, year after year; and David sought the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "It is for ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and for the house of bloodguilt, because he put the ⟦gibeonite|Givonim|Gibeonites⟧ to death."

[2] And the king called the ⟦gibeonite|Givonim|Gibeonites⟧ and said to them — and the ⟦gibeonite|Givonim|Gibeonites⟧, they were not of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, but of the rest of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧; and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had sworn to them, and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ sought to strike them down in his being zealous for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ —

[3] and David said to the ⟦gibeonite|Givonim|Gibeonites⟧, "What shall I do for you? And with what shall I make atonement? And bless the inheritance of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[4] And the ⟦gibeonite|Givonim|Gibeonites⟧ said to him, "We have no silver and gold with ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and with his house, and we have no man to put to death in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧." And he said, "What you are saying, I will do for you."

[5] And they said to the king, "The man who made an end of us and who intended against us — we were destroyed from taking our stand in all the territory of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ —

[6] let seven men of his sons be given to us, and we will expose them to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, the chosen of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And the king said, "I will give."

[7] And the king had pity on ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧ the son of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, because of the oath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that was between them, between David and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[8] And the king took the two sons of ⟦rizpah|Ritzpah|Rizpah⟧ the daughter of ⟦aiah|Ayyah|Aiah⟧, whom she had borne to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, Armoni and ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧, and the five sons of Michal the daughter of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, whom she had borne to Adriel the son of Barzillai the ⟦meholathite|Mecholati|Meholathite⟧.

[9] And he gave them into the hand of the ⟦gibeonite|Givonim|Gibeonites⟧, and they exposed them on the mountain before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the seven of them fell together. And they were put to death in the days of harvest, in the first days, at the beginning of the harvest of barley.

[10] And ⟦rizpah|Ritzpah|Rizpah⟧ the daughter of ⟦aiah|Ayyah|Aiah⟧ took the sackcloth and stretched it out for herself toward the rock, from the beginning of harvest until water was poured on them from the heavens; and she did not let the birds of the heavens rest on them by day, nor the living things of the field by night.

[11] And it was told to David what ⟦rizpah|Ritzpah|Rizpah⟧ the daughter of ⟦aiah|Ayyah|Aiah⟧, the concubine of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, had done.

[12] And David went and took the bones of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and the bones of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ his son from the lords of ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧, who had stolen them from the square of ⟦beth-shan|Beit Shan|Beth-shan⟧, where the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ had hung them on the day that the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ struck down ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ in Gilboa.

[13] And he brought up from there the bones of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and the bones of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ his son, and they gathered the bones of those who had been exposed.

[14] And they buried the bones of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ his son in the land of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, in ⟦zela|Tzela|Zela⟧, in the grave of ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧ his father, and they did all that the king commanded. And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ let himself be pleaded with for the land after that.

[15] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ again had war with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and David went down, and his servants with him, and they fought with the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; and David was weary.

[16] And ⟦ishbi-benob|Yishbi-Benov|Ishbi-benob⟧, who was among those born of the ⟦rapha|Rafa|Rapha⟧ — and the weight of his lance was three hundred, the weight of bronze, and he was girded with a new one — said to strike down David.

[17] And ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ helped him, and he struck the ⟦philistines|Pelishti|Philistine⟧ and put him to death. Then the men of David swore to him, saying, "You shall not go out with us to the battle any more, and you shall not quench the lamp of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[18] And it came to pass after this that there was again the war in ⟦gob|Gov|Gob⟧ with the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧. Then ⟦sibbecai|Sibbechai|Sibbecai⟧ the ⟦hushathite|Chushati|Hushathite⟧ struck down ⟦saph|Saf|Saph⟧, who was among those born of the ⟦rapha|Rafa|Rapha⟧.

[19] And there was again the war in ⟦gob|Gov|Gob⟧ with the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and ⟦elhanan|Elchanan|Elhanan⟧ the son of ⟦jaare-oregim|Yaarei-Orgim|Jaare-oregim⟧ the ⟦bethlehemite|Beit-HaLachmi|Bethlehemite⟧ struck down ⟦goliath|Golyat|Goliath⟧ the ⟦gittite|Gitti|Gittite⟧, and the shaft of his spear was like a weavers' beam.

[20] And there was again war in ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧, and there was a man of stature, and the fingers of his hands and the fingers of his feet were six and six, twenty-four in number; and he also was born to the ⟦rapha|Rafa|Rapha⟧.

[21] And he reproached ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ the son of ⟦shimeah|Shimah|Shimeah⟧, the brother of David, struck him down.

[22] These four were born to the ⟦rapha|Rafa|Rapha⟧ in ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧, and they fell by the hand of David and by the hand of his servants.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v14) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"enquired of the LORD" → "sought the face of Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְבַקֵּשׁ … אֶת־פְּנֵי יְהוָה.
- **"It is for Saul, and for his bloody house" → "It is for Saul and for the house of bloodguilt"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶל־שָׁאוּל וְאֶל־בֵּית הַדָּמִים, literally "to Saul and to the house of the bloods"; "it is" is supplied. The Hebrew has "the house", not "his house". דָּמִים, the plural "bloods", is bloodguilt.
- **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"remnant" → "rest"** — Hebrew יֶתֶר. "The Amorite" is singular in the Hebrew. **"in his zeal to" → "in his being zealous for"** — Hebrew בְּקַנֹּאתוֹ לְ, the verb "be jealous / zealous for". The words about the Gibeonites break into the sentence; the Hebrew does not mark them off, and v3 takes up "said" again.
- **"that ye may bless" → "And bless"** (v3) — Hebrew וּבָרְכוּ, an imperative: "and bless (you) the inheritance of Yahweh". The sense is "so that you may bless".
- **"We will have no silver nor gold of Saul, nor of his house; neither for us shalt thou kill any man in Israel" → "We have no silver and gold with Saul and with his house, and we have no man to put to death in Israel"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵין־לָנוּ (qere; the ketiv has לִי "I have") כֶּסֶף וְזָהָב עִם־שָׁאוּל וְעִם־בֵּיתוֹ וְאֵין־לָנוּ אִישׁ לְהָמִית בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל, word for word. The sense is commonly given as "it is not a matter of silver or gold between us and Saul, and it is not for us to put any man to death in Israel". **"What ye shall say, that will I do for you"** — Hebrew מָה־אַתֶּם אֹמְרִים אֶעֱשֶׂה לָכֶם; it can also be read as a question, "What do you say that I should do for you?"
- **"The man that consumed us, and that devised against us that we should be destroyed from remaining in any of the coasts of Israel" → "The man who made an end of us and who intended against us — we were destroyed from taking our stand in all the territory of Israel"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּלָּנוּ "made an end of us"; דִּמָּה־לָנוּ "intended for / against us"; נִשְׁמַדְנוּ "we were destroyed" (a perfect, with no "that"); מֵהִתְיַצֵּב "from taking a stand". The sentence is broken in the Hebrew and is left so. גְּבוּל is "border, territory".
- **"hang them up unto the LORD" → "expose them to Yahweh"** (v6, 9, 13) — Hebrew הוֹקַע, found only here and in Num 25:4. The meaning is not certain: a form of execution or of public display of the body; "hang" and "impale" are other renderings. **"Let seven men of his sons be delivered" → "be given"** — qere יֻתַּן. **"whom the LORD did choose" → "the chosen of Yahweh"** — Hebrew בְּחִיר יְהוָה. Some versions have "in Gibeon, on the mountain of Yahweh" here (from memory); the verse keeps the Hebrew. **"I will give them" → "I will give"** — the Hebrew has no object.
- **"spared" → "had pity on"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיַּחְמֹל עַל.
- **"Michal" kept** (v8) — the Hebrew text has מִיכַל "Michal". 1 Sam 18:19 says that Merab, the daughter of Saul, was given to Adriel the Meholathite, and 2 Sam 6:23 that Michal had no child to the day of her death. Two Hebrew manuscripts and some of the versions have "Merab" here, and many translations follow them (from memory); the KJV keeps "Michal" and turns the verb ("whom she brought up for Adriel"). The verse keeps the text as printed: "Michal … whom she had borne to Adriel" (יָלְדָה, "bore").
- **"delivered them into the hands" → "gave them into the hand"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנֵם בְּיַד. **"they fell all seven together" → "the seven of them fell together"** — qere שְׁבַעְתָּם; the ketiv has שבעתים, "sevenfold". **"in the first days"** — Hebrew בָּרִאשֹׁנִים, "in the first ones".
- **"spread it for her upon the rock" → "stretched it out for herself toward the rock"** (v10) — Hebrew וַתַּטֵּהוּ לָהּ אֶל־הַצּוּר; נָטָה is the verb for pitching a tent; אֶל is "to, toward". **"until water dropped upon them out of heaven" → "until water was poured on them from the heavens"** — Hebrew נִתַּךְ. **"the beasts of the field" → "the living things of the field"** — Hebrew חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה.
- **"the men of Jabesh-gilead" → "the lords of Jabesh-gilead"** (v12) — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי יָבֵישׁ גִּלְעָד: the men of the town are meant. **"street" → "square"** — Hebrew רְחֹב, the broad open place of a town (1 Sam 31:10, 12 has "the wall of Beth-shan"). **"had hanged them"** — qere תְּלָאוּם שָׁמָּה פְּלִשְׁתִּים; "hung" is תָּלָה, another word than "expose". **"had slain Saul" → "struck down Saul"** — Hebrew הַכּוֹת.
- **"in the sepulchre of Kish" → "in the grave of Kish"** (v14) — Hebrew קֶבֶר. **"God was intreated for the land" → "Elohim let himself be pleaded with for the land"** — Hebrew וַיֵּעָתֵר, nifal of עָתַר "plead" (so also 24:25).
- **"the Philistines had yet war again with Israel"** (v15) — Hebrew וַתְּהִי־עוֹד מִלְחָמָה לַפְּלִשְׁתִּים אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל, "there was again war for the Philistines with Israel". **"waxed faint" → "was weary"** — Hebrew וַיָּעַף.
- **"Ishbi-benob"** (v16) — qere וְיִשְׁבִּי בְּנֹב; the ketiv has וישבו בנב, "and they dwelt in Nob". The name is found only here.
- **"of the sons of the giant" → "among those born of the Rapha"** (v16, 18) — Hebrew בִּילִידֵי הָרָפָה, "among the ones born of the Raphah"; in v20, 22 יֻלַּד לְהָרָפָה, "was born to the Raphah". The word has the article and is singular; whether it is a name, a title or the name of a kind ("the giant"; compare the Rephaim) is not known. It is kept as a name with the article, as in 1 Chr 20:6, 8 (there spelled הָרָפָא). The earlier text had "the Rephaim" here: that plural is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the weight of whose spear weighed three hundred shekels of brass in weight" → "the weight of his lance was three hundred, the weight of bronze"** (v16) — Hebrew וּמִשְׁקַל קֵינוֹ שְׁלֹשׁ מֵאוֹת מִשְׁקַל נְחֹשֶׁת. קַיִן stands only here in this sense and is uncertain ("spear" is חֲנִית, v19). "Shekels" is not in the Hebrew. **"girded with a new sword" → "girded with a new one"** — Hebrew חָגוּר חֲדָשָׁה, "girded with a new (thing)", a feminine adjective with no noun; "sword" is commonly understood. **"thought to have slain David" → "said to strike down David"** — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר לְהַכּוֹת.
- **"succoured him, and smote the Philistine, and killed him" → "helped him, and he struck the Philistine and put him to death"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲזָר, וַיַּךְ, וַיְמִיתֵהוּ. **"that thou quench not the light of Israel" → "and you shall not quench the lamp of Israel"** — Hebrew וְלֹא תְכַבֶּה אֶת־נֵר יִשְׂרָאֵל, a plain "and … not"; נֵר is "lamp".
- **"Elhanan … slew the brother of Goliath the Gittite" → "Elhanan … struck down Goliath the Gittite"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ אֶלְחָנָן בֶּן־יַעְרֵי אֹרְגִים בֵּית הַלַּחְמִי אֵת גָּלְיָת הַגִּתִּי. The Hebrew text has no "the brother of"; the KJV supplies it from 1 Chr 20:5, which reads "Elhanan the son of Jair struck down Lahmi the brother of Goliath the Gittite". 1 Sam 17 tells that David struck down the Philistine Goliath of Gath. The verse gives the text of Samuel as it is printed and supplies nothing. "Oregim" ("weavers") in the father's name is the same word as the last word of the verse. **"the staff of whose spear" → "and the shaft of his spear"** — Hebrew וְעֵץ חֲנִיתוֹ, literally "the wood of his spear" (as 1 Sam 17:7).
- **"a man of great stature" → "a man of stature"** (v20) — qere מָדוֹן, ketiv מדין. The word is uncertain: as pointed it is elsewhere "strife" ("a man of strife"); "stature" takes it from מָדַד "measure", as 1 Chr 20:6 has אִישׁ מִדָּה, "a man of measure". "Great" is not in the Hebrew. **"that had on every hand six fingers, and on every foot six toes" → "the fingers of his hands and the fingers of his feet were six and six"** — Hebrew שֵׁשׁ וָשֵׁשׁ; one word, אֶצְבַּע, for fingers and toes.
- **"defied" → "reproached"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיְחָרֵף, as 1 Sam 17. **"Shimeah"** — qere שִׁמְעָה; the ketiv has שמעי, "Shimei". He is Shammah in 1 Sam 16:9 and Shimeah in 2 Sam 13:3.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v16).

### Notes
- **Saul and the Gibeonites** — the putting to death of the Gibeonites is not told in 1 Samuel. The oath of the sons of Israel is in Josh 9.
- **The seven** (v8–9) — two sons of Rizpah and five sons of the daughter of Saul.
- **Rizpah** (v10–11) — her watch over the bodies is told to David, and he brings up the bones of Saul and Jonathan (1 Sam 31:11–13).
- **Four born to the Rapha** (v15–22) — the parallel is 1 Chr 20:4–8, which has three of the four and differs in v19.
- **"The lamp of Israel"** (v17) — Hebrew נֵר *ner*, "lamp". Compare 1 Kgs 11:36; 15:4.
