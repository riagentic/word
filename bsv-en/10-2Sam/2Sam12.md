# 2 Samuel 12

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sent ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ to David, and he came to him and said to him, "Two men were in one city, one rich and one poor.

[2] The rich one had flock and herd, very much.

[3] And the poor one had nothing at all except one small ewe lamb that he had bought; and he kept it alive, and it grew up with him and with his sons together. Of his bit it would eat, and from his cup it would drink, and in his bosom it would lie, and it was to him like a daughter.

[4] And a traveler came to the rich man, and he spared to take from his flock and from his herd to make ready for the wayfarer who had come to him, and he took the ewe lamb of the poor man and made it ready for the man who had come to him."

[5] And the anger of David burned greatly against the man, and he said to ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, the man who does this is a son of death.

[6] And the ewe lamb he shall repay fourfold, because he did this thing and because he did not spare."

[7] And ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ said to David, "You are the man. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'I anointed you as king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I delivered you from the hand of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[8] And I gave to you the house of your lord, and the wives of your lord into your bosom, and I gave to you the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; and if that were little, I would add to you like these and like these.

[9] Why have you despised the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to do what is evil in my eyes? ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ you have struck down with the sword, and his wife you have taken to yourself as a wife, and him you have killed with the sword of the sons of Ammon.

[10] And now, the sword shall not depart from your house forever, because you have despised me and have taken the wife of ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ to be your wife.'

[11] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Behold, I am raising up against you evil from your house, and I will take your wives before your eyes and give them to your friend, and he shall lie with your wives before the eyes of this sun.

[12] For you did it in secret, and I will do this thing in front of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and in front of the sun.'"

[13] And David said to ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧, "I have sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ said to David, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ also has made your sin pass over; you shall not die.

[14] Only, because you have surely spurned the enemies of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ by this thing, the son also who is born to you shall surely die."

[15] And ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ went to his house. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ smote the child that the wife of ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ had borne to David, and he became gravely sick.

[16] And David sought ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for the boy, and David fasted a fast, and he would go in and lodge and lie on the earth.

[17] And the elders of his house arose over him to raise him up from the earth, and he was not willing, and he did not eat bread with them.

[18] And it came to pass on the seventh day that the child died. And the servants of David feared to tell him that the child was dead, for they said, "Behold, while the child was alive, we spoke to him, and he did not listen to our voice; and how shall we say to him, 'The child is dead'? And he will do evil."

[19] And David saw that his servants were whispering together, and David understood that the child was dead. And David said to his servants, "Is the child dead?" And they said, "He is dead."

[20] And David arose from the earth and washed and anointed himself and changed his cloaks, and he came into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and bowed down. And he came to his house and asked, and they set bread for him, and he ate.

[21] And his servants said to him, "What is this thing that you have done? For the sake of the child, alive, you fasted and wept; and when the child died, you arose and ate bread."

[22] And he said, "While the child was still alive, I fasted and wept, for I said, 'Who knows? ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ may show favor to me, and the child may live.'

[23] And now he is dead. Why is it that I should fast? Am I able to bring him back again? I am going to him, and he will not return to me."

[24] And David comforted ⟦bathsheba|Bat-Sheva|Bathsheba⟧ his wife, and he went in to her and lay with her, and she bore a son, and she called his name ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loved him.

[25] And he sent by the hand of ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ the prophet, and he called his name ⟦jedidiah|Yedidyah|Jedidiah⟧, for the sake of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[26] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ fought against Rabbah of the sons of Ammon and captured the city of the kingship.

[27] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ sent messengers to David and said, "I have fought against Rabbah; I have also captured the city of the waters.

[28] And now, gather the rest of the people and camp against the city and capture it, lest I myself capture the city and my name be called over it."

[29] And David gathered all the people and went to Rabbah and fought against it and captured it.

[30] And he took the crown of their king from upon his head, and its weight was a talent of gold, and a precious stone, and it was upon the head of David. And the spoil of the city he brought out, very much.

[31] And the people who were in it he brought out, and he put them to the saw and to the cutting tools of iron and to the axes of iron, and he made them pass through the brick mold; and thus he would do to all the cities of the sons of Ammon. And David and all the people returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v16 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"in one city"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּעִיר אֶחָת. **"exceeding many flocks and herds" → "flock and herd, very much"** (v2) — Hebrew צֹאן וּבָקָר הַרְבֵּה מְאֹד; both nouns are collective singulars.
- **"one little ewe lamb, which he had bought and nourished up" → "one small ewe lamb that he had bought; and he kept it alive"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּבְשָׂה, a female lamb; וַיְחַיֶּהָ, "he kept it alive, let it live". **"with his children" → "with his sons"** — Hebrew בָּנָיו. **"of his own meat" → "of his bit"** — Hebrew פִּתּוֹ, "his bit (of bread)". The three verbs "would eat … would drink … would lie" are Hebrew imperfects of what happened again and again.
- **"a traveller … the wayfaring man … the man that was come to him"** (v4) — three Hebrew words: הֵלֶךְ (literally "a going": one on a journey; the word is rare), אֹרֵחַ "wayfarer", אִישׁ "man". **"he spared to take"** — Hebrew וַיַּחְמֹל, the verb "have pity, spare" of v6 ("because he did not spare", לֹא־חָמָל): he had pity on what was his and none on the poor man. **"to dress" → "to make ready"** — Hebrew עָשָׂה "do, make".
- **"David's anger was greatly kindled" → "the anger of David burned greatly"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף. **"the man that hath done this thing shall surely die" → "the man who does this is a son of death"** — Hebrew בֶן־מָוֶת, "a son of death": one who belongs to death, who must die (1 Sam 20:31; 26:16); the participle הָעֹשֶׂה is "who does".
- **"he shall restore the lamb fourfold" → "the ewe lamb he shall repay fourfold"** (v6) — Hebrew שִׁלֵּם "repay"; אַרְבַּעְתָּיִם "fourfold" (Exod 22:1). The Septuagint has "sevenfold" (from memory).
- **"I anointed thee king … and I delivered thee"** (v7) — the Hebrew has the pronoun אָנֹכִי "I" written out both times.
- **"thy master's house, and thy master's wives" → "the house of your lord, and the wives of your lord"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנֶיךָ. **"and if that had been too little, I would moreover have given unto thee such and such things" → "and if that were little, I would add to you like these and like these"** — Hebrew וְאִם־מְעָט וְאֹסִפָה לְּךָ כָּהֵנָּה וְכָהֵנָּה, word for word.
- **"to do evil in his sight" → "to do what is evil in my eyes"** (v9) — the reading tradition (qere) בְּעֵינַי "in my eyes"; the written text (ketiv) בעינו "in his eyes". **"hast slain him" → "have killed"** — Hebrew הָרַג; "struck down" is הִכָּה.
- **"shall never depart" → "shall not depart … forever"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא־תָסוּר … עַד־עוֹלָם, "to the age".
- **"give them unto thy neighbour" → "give them to your friend"** (v11) — Hebrew לְרֵעֶיךָ: רֵעַ, "fellow, friend" (the "neighbor" of "each man his neighbor"); the form is written like a plural, the verb that follows ("and he shall lie") is singular. **"in the sight of this sun" → "before the eyes of this sun"** — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי. **"I will raise up" → "I am raising up"** — a participle.
- **"before all Israel, and before the sun" → "in front of all Israel and in front of the sun"** (v12) — Hebrew נֶגֶד.
- **"The LORD also hath put away thy sin" → "Yahweh also has made your sin pass over"** (v13) — Hebrew הֶעֱבִיר, the causative of עָבַר "pass over, pass by": he has let it pass (so also 24:10).
- **"because by this deed thou hast given great occasion to the enemies of the LORD to blaspheme" → "because you have surely spurned the enemies of Yahweh by this thing"** (v14) — Hebrew נִאֵץ נִאַצְתָּ אֶת־אֹיְבֵי יְהוָה, literally "spurning you have spurned the enemies of Yahweh". That is what the Masoretic Text says, and it is difficult: the verb נִאֵץ means "spurn, treat with contempt" and nowhere "cause to spurn". It is usually understood in one of two ways: (1) "you have made the enemies of Yahweh spurn" (so the KJV), which gives the verb a sense it has nowhere else; (2) "you have surely spurned Yahweh", on the view that "the enemies of" was written in by scribes so that the words "spurned Yahweh" would not be said. A Samuel scroll from Qumran is reported to read "the word of Yahweh" (from memory). None of these is in the verse. The earlier note here, which gave a Dead Sea Scrolls reading "caused the enemies of Yahweh to scorn", was wrong and is removed. **"the child also that is born unto thee" → "the son also who is born to you"** — Hebrew הַבֵּן. **"shall surely die"** — literally "dying he shall die"; so also "surely spurned".
- **"the LORD struck the child … and it was very sick" → "Yahweh smote the child … and he became gravely sick"** (v15) — Hebrew נָגַף, a blow from God; וַיֵּאָנַשׁ, a verb found only here, of one root with אָנוּשׁ "incurable".
- **"besought God for the child" → "sought Elohim for the boy"** (v16) — Hebrew בִּקֵּשׁ "seek"; הַנַּעַר "the boy" here, הַיֶּלֶד "the child" in the other verses. **"David fasted, and went in, and lay all night upon the earth" → "David fasted a fast, and he would go in and lodge and lie on the earth"** — the three last verbs are in the Hebrew form for what is done again and again; לוּן is "lodge, spend the night".
- **"went to him" → "arose over him"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיָּקֻמוּ … עָלָיו. **"neither did he eat bread"** — Hebrew בָּרָא, a rare verb "eat" (written here with א).
- **"how will he then vex himself" → "And he will do evil"** (v18) — Hebrew וְעָשָׂה רָעָה, "and he will do evil": harm; to whom is not said.
- **"perceived" → "understood"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיָּבֶן.
- **"anointed himself, and changed his apparel … and worshipped" → "anointed himself and changed his cloaks … and bowed down"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיָּסֶךְ, of rubbing the body with oil (not the verb of anointing a king, v7); שִׂמְלֹתָיו "his cloaks" (qere, plural; the ketiv is singular); וַיִּשְׁתָּחוּ "bowed down". **"when he required, they set bread before him" → "asked, and they set bread for him"**.
- **"thou didst fast and weep for the child, while it was alive" → "For the sake of the child, alive, you fasted and wept"** (v21) — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר הַיֶּלֶד חַי, word for word "for the sake of the child alive": difficult. It is commonly read as "while the child was alive" (as v22 has, בְּעוֹד; so the Septuagint, from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Who can tell whether GOD will be gracious to me" → "Who knows? Yahweh may show favor to me"** (v22) — Hebrew מִי יוֹדֵעַ; the qere is וְחַנַּנִי, the ketiv יְחָנֵּנִי, both of חָנַן "show favor"; the name is Yahweh.
- **"wherefore should I fast?" → "Why is it that I should fast?"** (v23) — Hebrew לָמָּה זֶּה אֲנִי צָם, "why this, I am fasting". **"I shall go to him" → "I am going to him"** — a participle.
- **"he called his name Solomon" → "she called his name Solomon"** (v24) — the qere וַתִּקְרָא "she called"; the ketiv ויקרא "he called". The name שְׁלֹמֹה is of one root with שָׁלוֹם "peace".
- **"he called his name Jedidiah, because of the LORD" → "he called his name Jedidiah, for the sake of Yahweh"** (v25) — יְדִידְיָהּ means "beloved of Yah"; the meaning is not written in the Hebrew verse, and the gloss that stood in the verse is removed. Who "sent" and who "called" (Yahweh, David, Nathan) is not said. בַּעֲבוּר is "for the sake of".
- **"took the royal city" → "captured the city of the kingship"** (v26) — Hebrew עִיר הַמְּלוּכָה; לָכַד "capture" (v26–29). **"the city of waters" → "the city of the waters"** (v27).
- **"lest I take the city, and it be called after my name" → "lest I myself capture the city and my name be called over it"** (v28) — Hebrew וְנִקְרָא שְׁמִי עָלֶיהָ.
- **"their king's crown" → "the crown of their king"** (v30) — Hebrew מַלְכָּם as pointed, "their king". The same letters can be read "Milcom", the god of the sons of Ammon (1 Kgs 11:5, 33; so the Septuagint in some copies, from memory); the earlier text had that reading in the verse, and it is removed. **"with the precious stones" → "and a precious stone"** — Hebrew וְאֶבֶן יְקָרָה, singular, joined loosely: the weight is said of the gold "and a precious stone". **"and it was set on David's head" → "and it was upon the head of David"**. A talent (כִּכָּר) is a weight.
- **"put them under saws, and under harrows of iron, and under axes of iron, and made them pass through the brickkiln" → "put them to the saw and to the cutting tools of iron and to the axes of iron, and he made them pass through the brick mold"** (v31) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׂם בַּמְּגֵרָה וּבַחֲרִצֵי הַבַּרְזֶל וּבְמַגְזְרֹת הַבַּרְזֶל וְהֶעֱבִיר אוֹתָם בַּמַּלְבֵּן, word for word "and he put in / with the saw and in / with the sharp things of iron and in / with the axes of iron, and he made them pass over in / through the brick mold". The verse is difficult. The preposition בְּ does not say whether they were put under these tools (killed with them) or put at them (set to work with them); "put … to" leaves it as open as the Hebrew. חָרִיץ is "a thing cut or sharp" (a pick or a threshing tool; uncertain); מַגְזֵרָה is found only here. The qere is בַּמַּלְבֵּן, "the brick mold" (Nah 3:14) or a place of bricks (Jer 43:9); the ketiv במלכן is not a known word (it has been read "in Malken" or with the name of Molech). 1 Chr 20:3 has "and he sawed" (וַיָּשַׂר) for "and he put". The earlier text had "set to work … made them labor at the brick-kilns"; the Hebrew has no word for "work" or "labor". **"thus did he" → "thus he would do"** — an imperfect.
- **"servants"** (v18, 19, 21) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, "slave"; of a king's men "servant".

### Notes
- **The parable** (v1–4) — David gives judgment on the man (v5–6); "You are the man" (v7).
- **Fourfold** (v6) — the law of the stolen sheep, Exod 22:1.
- **The sword and the wives** (v10–12) — see chapters 13; 16:21–22; 18.
- **"I have sinned against Yahweh"** (v13) — two words in Hebrew, חָטָאתִי לַיהוָה. The heading of Ps 51 names this hour.
- **Solomon / Jedidiah** (v24–25) — two names for the one son.
- **Rabbah** (v26–31) — the war of 11:1 is ended.
