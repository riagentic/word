# 2 Samuel 2

[1] And it came to pass after this that David asked of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "Shall I go up into one of the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Go up." And David said, "Where shall I go up?" And he said, "To ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧."

[2] And David went up there, and also his two wives, ⟦ahinoam|Achinoam|Ahinoam⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreelit|Jezreelitess⟧ and ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ the wife of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ the ⟦carmel|Karmeli|Carmelite⟧.

[3] And his men who were with him David brought up, each man and his house, and they dwelt in the cities of ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧.

[4] And the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ came, and there they anointed David as king over the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. And they told David, saying, "It is the men of ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧ who buried ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧."

[5] And David sent messengers to the men of ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧ and said to them, "Blessed are you to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who have done this ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with your lord, with ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and have buried him.

[6] And now, may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ do with you ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth; and I also will do with you this good, because you have done this thing.

[7] And now, let your hands be strong, and be sons of ability, for your lord ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ is dead, and also me the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has anointed as king over them."

[8] And ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ the son of Ner, the commander of the host that was ⟦saul|Shaul's|Saul's⟧, took ⟦ish-bosheth|Ish-Boshet|Ish-bosheth⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and made him pass over to ⟦mahanaim|Machanayim|Mahanaim⟧.

[9] And he made him king over ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and over the ⟦ashurites|Ashuri|Ashurites⟧ and over ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, and over ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and over ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, all of it.

[10] ⟦ish-bosheth|Ish-Boshet|Ish-bosheth⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was forty years old when he began to reign over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and two years he reigned. Only the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ were after David.

[11] And the number of the days that David was king in ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧ over the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ was seven years and six months.

[12] And ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ the son of Ner went out, and the servants of ⟦ish-bosheth|Ish-Boshet|Ish-bosheth⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, from ⟦mahanaim|Machanayim|Mahanaim⟧ to ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧.

[13] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ and the servants of David went out, and they met them by the pool of ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ together. And they sat, these by the pool on this side and these by the pool on that side.

[14] And ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ said to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, "Let the young men arise, please, and play before us." And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said, "Let them arise."

[15] And they arose and passed over by number: twelve for ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and for ⟦ish-bosheth|Ish-Boshet|Ish-bosheth⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and twelve of the servants of David.

[16] And they took fast hold, each man of the head of his neighbor, and his sword was in the side of his neighbor, and they fell together. And that place was called ⟦helkath-hazzurim|Chelqat-HaTzurim|Helkath-hazzurim⟧, which is in ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧.

[17] And the battle was exceedingly hard on that day, and ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ and the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were smitten before the servants of David.

[18] And the three sons of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ were there, ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ and Asahel. And Asahel was swift on his feet, like one of the gazelles that are in the field.

[19] And Asahel pursued after ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧, and he did not turn to go to the right or to the left from after ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧.

[20] And ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ turned behind him and said, "Is this you, Asahel?" And he said, "It is I."

[21] And ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ said to him, "Turn yourself to your right or to your left, and take hold for yourself of one of the young men, and take for yourself what is stripped off him." And Asahel was not willing to turn aside from after him.

[22] And ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ said again to Asahel, "Turn yourself aside from after me. Why should I strike you to the earth? And how should I lift up my face to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ your brother?"

[23] And he refused to turn aside, and ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ struck him with the hinder end of the spear in the belly, and the spear came out behind him, and he fell there and died in his place. And it came to pass that everyone who came to the place where Asahel had fallen and died stood still.

[24] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ pursued after ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧. And the sun had gone in, and they had come to the hill of Ammah, which is facing ⟦giah|Giach|Giah⟧, on the way of the wilderness of ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧.

[25] And the sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ gathered themselves after ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ and became one band, and they stood on the top of one hill.

[26] And ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ called to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and said, "Shall the sword devour forever? Do you not know that it will be bitter in the end? And how long will you not say to the people to turn back from after their brothers?"

[27] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said, "As ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ lives, if you had not spoken, surely then from the morning the people would have gone up, each man from after his brother."

[28] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ blew on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, and all the people stood still, and they pursued no more after ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they fought no more again.

[29] And ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ and his men went in the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧ all that night, and they passed over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and went through all the ⟦bithron|Bitron|Bithron⟧ and came to ⟦mahanaim|Machanayim|Mahanaim⟧.

[30] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ returned from after ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ and gathered all the people, and there were missed of the servants of David nineteen men and Asahel.

[31] And the servants of David had struck down of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and among the men of ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧: three hundred and sixty men died.

[32] And they took up Asahel and buried him in the grave of his father, which was in ⟦bethlehem|Beit Lechem|Bethlehem⟧. And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and his men went all the night, and it grew light for them in ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v27) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"David enquired of the LORD" → "David asked of Yahweh, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאַל … בַּיהוָה לֵאמֹר; the verb is "ask" (as 1 Sam 23:2; 30:8). **"into any of the cities" → "into one of the cities"** — Hebrew בְּאַחַת.
- **"his men that were with him did David bring up, every man with his household" → "his men who were with him David brought up, each man and his house"** (v3) — Hebrew אִישׁ וּבֵיתוֹ; the Hebrew order. **"dwelt in the cities of Hebron"** — Hebrew וַיֵּשְׁבוּ בְּעָרֵי חֶבְרוֹן.
- **"anointed David king" → "anointed David as king"** (v4, 7) — Hebrew לְמֶלֶךְ. **"That the men of Jabesh-gilead were they that buried Saul" → "It is the men of Jabesh-gilead who buried Saul"** (v4) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי יָבֵישׁ גִּלְעָד אֲשֶׁר קָבְרוּ אֶת־שָׁאוּל, literally "the men of Jabesh-gilead, who buried Saul": the sentence has no verb of its own; "It is" is supplied.
- **"Blessed be ye of the LORD, that ye have shewed this kindness unto your lord" → "Blessed are you to Yahweh, who have done this *chesed* (steadfast love) with your lord"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּרֻכִים אַתֶּם לַיהוָה (as 1 Sam 15:13); עֲשִׂיתֶם הַחֶסֶד הַזֶּה עִם: חֶסֶד with עָשָׂה "do" and עִם "with". "Your lord, Saul" is literally "with your lord, with Saul".
- **"the LORD shew kindness and truth unto you: and I also will requite you this kindness" → "may Yahweh do with you *chesed* and truth; and I also will do with you this good"** (v6) — Hebrew חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת; אֶעֱשֶׂה אִתְּכֶם הַטּוֹבָה הַזֹּאת: the same verb "do" as in v5 and in the first half of the verse; הַטּוֹבָה is "the good", not "kindness". "Requite", "reward" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"let your hands be strengthened, and be ye valiant" → "let your hands be strong, and be sons of ability"** (v7) — Hebrew תֶּחֱזַקְנָה; וִהְיוּ לִבְנֵי־חַיִל (as 1 Sam 18:17). **"also me …"** — the Hebrew puts "me" first.
- **"captain of Saul's host" → "the commander of the host that was Saul's"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׂר־צָבָא אֲשֶׁר לְשָׁאוּל; שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander". **"brought him over" → "made him pass over"** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבִרֵהוּ.
- **"Ish-bosheth"** (v8, 10, 12, 15) — Hebrew אִישׁ־בֹּשֶׁת, "man of shame", as printed. 1 Chr 8:33; 9:39 name a son of Saul אֶשְׁבָּעַל "Eshbaal"; the Masoretic Text of Samuel has only the form given here, and it is kept.
- **"made him king over Gilead … and over all Israel" → "… and over Israel, all of it"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיַּמְלִכֵהוּ, "made him reign". The first three names have the preposition אֶל ("to, toward"), the last three עַל ("over"); English "over" stands for both. הָאֲשׁוּרִי is "the Ashurite", singular; who is meant is uncertain (the versions are reported to read "the Geshurite" or "the Asherite": from memory). כֻּלֹּה "all of it".
- **"Ish-bosheth Saul's son was forty years old when he began to reign"** (v10) — Hebrew בֶּן־אַרְבָּעִים שָׁנָה … בְּמָלְכוֹ, literally "a son of forty years … at his reigning". **"But the house of Judah followed David" → "Only the house of Judah were after David"** — Hebrew אַךְ … הָיוּ אַחֲרֵי.
- **"the time that David was king" → "the number of the days that David was king"** (v11) — Hebrew מִסְפַּר הַיָּמִים.
- **"met together by the pool of Gibeon: and they sat down, the one on the one side of the pool, and the other on the other side" → "met them by the pool of Gibeon together. And they sat, these by the pool on this side and these by the pool on that side"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּפְגְּשׁוּם, "met them"; אֵלֶּה עַל־הַבְּרֵכָה מִזֶּה וְאֵלֶּה עַל־הַבְּרֵכָה מִזֶּה.
- **"Let the young men now arise, and play before us"** (v14) — Hebrew נָא "please"; וִישַׂחֲקוּ, the plain verb "play, make sport"; what kind of contest is meant the word does not say (the earlier text had "hold a contest").
- **"they caught every one his fellow by the head, and thrust his sword in his fellow's side" → "they took fast hold, each man of the head of his neighbor, and his sword was in the side of his neighbor"** (v16) — Hebrew הֶחֱזִיק בְּ "hold fast" (1:11); וַיַּחֲזִקוּ אִישׁ בְּרֹאשׁ רֵעֵהוּ וְחַרְבּוֹ בְּצַד רֵעֵהוּ: the second clause has no verb ("thrust" was supplied); "was" is the copula.
- **"Helkath-hazzurim"** (v16) — Hebrew חֶלְקַת הַצֻּרִים, "the portion (field) of the flints" or "of the edges" (צֹר is "flint" and the edge of a blade, Ps 89:43); the meaning of the name is not certain. The earlier text had a gloss in the verse; the Hebrew has only the name.
- **"there was a very sore battle" → "the battle was exceedingly hard"** (v17) — Hebrew קָשָׁה עַד־מְאֹד. **"was beaten" → "were smitten before"** — Hebrew וַיִּנָּגֶף … לִפְנֵי (as 1 Sam 4:2).
- **"as light of foot as a wild roe" → "swift on his feet, like one of the gazelles that are in the field"** (v18) — Hebrew קַל בְּרַגְלָיו כְּאַחַד הַצְּבָיִם אֲשֶׁר בַּשָּׂדֶה; קַל is the word of 1:23 ("swifter"). The names are joined by "and".
- **"turned not to the right hand nor to the left from following Abner" → "did not turn to go to the right or to the left from after Abner"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא־נָטָה לָלֶכֶת; מֵאַחֲרֵי "from after" (also v21, 22, 26, 27, 30).
- **"Art thou Asahel?" → "Is this you, Asahel?"** (v20) — Hebrew הַאַתָּה זֶה. The answer is one word, אָנֹכִי "I".
- **"Turn thee aside … lay thee hold … take thee his armour" → "Turn yourself … take hold for yourself … take for yourself what is stripped off him"** (v21) — the Hebrew has לְךָ three times; נָטָה "turn" stands beside סוּר "turn aside" (v21b, 22, 23). חֲלִצָה is what is stripped off a fallen man (Judg 14:19): his gear and garments.
- **"wherefore should I smite thee to the ground?" → "Why should I strike you to the earth?"** (v22) — Hebrew אַכֶּכָּה אַרְצָה. "Said again" is literally "added yet to say".
- **"with the hinder end of the spear smote him under the fifth rib" → "struck him with the hinder end of the spear in the belly"** (v23) — Hebrew אֶל־הַחֹמֶשׁ. חֹמֶשׁ stands only in 2 Samuel (2:23; 3:27; 4:6; 20:10) and its meaning is uncertain: "belly, abdomen" is the best-supported sense; "the fifth (rib)" of the KJV takes it from חָמֵשׁ "five". **"died in the same place" → "died in his place"** — Hebrew תַּחְתָּיו (qere), "under him": where he stood. **"stood still"** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֹדוּ.
- **"the sun went down when they were come" → "And the sun had gone in, and they had come"** (v24) — Hebrew וְהַשֶּׁמֶשׁ בָּאָה וְהֵמָּה בָּאוּ. **"that lieth before Giah" → "which is facing Giah"** — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי. "On the way of the wilderness of Gibeon" is דֶּרֶךְ מִדְבַּר גִּבְעוֹן. The places Ammah and Giah are not known.
- **"the children of Benjamin" → "the sons of Benjamin"** (v25) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"became one troop" → "became one band"** — Hebrew אֲגֻדָּה, "a bundle, a band".
- **"Shall the sword devour for ever?"** (v26) — Hebrew תֹּאכַל, the verb "eat". **"it will be bitterness in the latter end" → "it will be bitter in the end"** — Hebrew מָרָה … בָּאַחֲרוֹנָה. **"how long shall it be then, ere thou bid the people return" → "how long will you not say to the people to turn back"** — Hebrew וְעַד־מָתַי לֹא־תֹאמַר לָעָם לָשׁוּב.
- **"As God liveth, unless thou hadst spoken, surely then in the morning the people had gone up every one from following his brother" → "As Elohim lives, if you had not spoken, surely then from the morning the people would have gone up, each man from after his brother"** (v27) — Hebrew כִּי לוּלֵא דִּבַּרְתָּ כִּי אָז מֵהַבֹּקֶר נַעֲלָה הָעָם. The sentence can be taken two ways: had Abner not spoken now, the people would not have left off until the morning; or had Abner not spoken (v14), the people would have left off already in the morning. מֵהַבֹּקֶר is "from the morning"; נַעֲלָה is "be taken up, get oneself up (from)". The verse keeps the Hebrew words and does not decide.
- **"blew a trumpet" → "blew on the *shofar*"** (v28) — Hebrew בַּשּׁוֹפָר, the ram's-horn. **"neither fought they any more" → "they fought no more again"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָסְפוּ עוֹד לְהִלָּחֵם, literally "did not add again to fight".
- **"walked all that night through the plain" → "went in the Arabah all that night"** (v29) — Hebrew בָּעֲרָבָה, the dry valley of the Jordan. **"went through all Bithron" → "went through all the Bithron"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַבִּתְרוֹן, with the article; the word stands only here and its meaning is uncertain ("the ravine", from בָּתַר "cut in two", or "the forenoon"): the name is kept.
- **"there lacked of David's servants" → "there were missed of the servants of David"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיִּפָּקְדוּ (as 1 Sam 20:18).
- **"had smitten of Benjamin, and of Abner's men, so that three hundred and threescore men died" → "had struck down of Benjamin and among the men of Abner: three hundred and sixty men died"** (v31) — Hebrew הִכּוּ מִבִּנְיָמִן וּבְאַנְשֵׁי אַבְנֵר … מֵתוּ: two prepositions (מִן "of", בְּ "among"); "so that" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"sepulchre" → "grave"** (v32) — Hebrew קֶבֶר. **"they came to Hebron at break of day" → "it grew light for them in Hebron"** — Hebrew וַיֵּאֹר לָהֶם בְּחֶבְרוֹן.

### Notes
- **Two houses** — Abner makes Ish-bosheth king at Mahanaim; the house of Judah anoints David at Hebron (v4, 8–11).
- **The men of Jabesh-gilead** (v4–7) — see 1 Sam 31:11–13; Saul had saved their city (1 Sam 11).
- **Twelve and twelve** (v14–16) — the verb is "play"; all fall together, and the battle follows.
- **The death of Asahel** (v18–23) — Abner warns him twice; this is the blood that Joab later avenges (3:27, 30).
- **"Shall the sword devour forever?"** (v26) — Abner's words end the pursuit.
