# 2 Samuel 19

[1] And it was told to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, "Behold, the king is weeping and mourning over ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧."

[2] And the salvation on that day became mourning for all the people, for the people heard on that day, saying, "The king is grieved over his son."

[3] And the people stole away on that day to come into the city, as people steal away who are put to shame when they flee in the battle.

[4] And the king covered his face, and the king cried out with a loud voice, "My son ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧, ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧, my son, my son!"

[5] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ came to the king, into the house, and said, "You have shamed today the faces of all your servants, who have rescued your soul today, and the soul of your sons and your daughters, and the soul of your wives and the soul of your concubines,

[6] to love those who hate you and to hate those who love you. For you have told today that you have no commanders and servants; for I know today that if ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ were alive and all of us today dead, that then it would be right in your eyes.

[7] And now, arise, go out and speak to the heart of your servants, for by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I swear that, if you do not go out, not a man will lodge with you tonight; and this will be worse for you than all the evil that has come upon you from your youth until now."

[8] And the king arose and sat in the gate. And they told all the people, saying, "Behold, the king is sitting in the gate." And all the people came before the king. And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had fled, each man to his tents.

[9] And all the people were at strife through all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "The king delivered us from the palm of our enemies, and he rescued us from the palm of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and now he has fled from the land, away from ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧.

[10] And ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧, whom we anointed over us, has died in the battle. And now, why are you silent about bringing back the king?"

[11] And King David sent to ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ and to ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ the priests, saying, "Speak to the elders of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying, 'Why should you be the last to bring back the king to his house? And the word of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has come to the king, to his house.

[12] My brothers are you; my bone and my flesh are you. And why should you be the last to bring back the king?'

[13] And to Amasa you shall say, 'Are you not my bone and my flesh? Thus may ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ do to me, and thus may he add: you shall surely be commander of the host before me all the days, in place of ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧.'"

[14] And he turned the heart of all the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ as one man, and they sent to the king: "Return, you and all your servants."

[15] And the king returned and came as far as the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧. And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ came to Gilgal, to go to meet the king, to make the king pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[16] And ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ the son of Gera, the ⟦benjaminite|Yemini|Benjaminite⟧, who was from ⟦bahurim|Bachurim|Bahurim⟧, hastened and went down with the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to meet King David.

[17] And a thousand men were with him from ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, and ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧, the young man of the house of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and his fifteen sons and his twenty servants with him; and they rushed through the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ before the king.

[18] And the ferry passed over, to make the house of the king pass over and to do what was good in his eyes. And ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ the son of Gera fell before the king as he was passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[19] And he said to the king, "Let not my lord reckon iniquity to me, and do not remember how your servant did iniquity on the day that my lord the king went out from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for the king to set it to his heart.

[20] For your servant knows that I have sinned; and behold, I have come today, first of all the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, to go down to meet my lord the king."

[21] And ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ answered and said, "Shall ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ not be put to death for this, that he cursed the ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"

[22] And David said, "What have I to do with you, sons of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧, that you should be to me today as an adversary? Today shall a man be put to death in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧? For do I not know that today I am king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"

[23] And the king said to ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧, "You shall not die." And the king swore to him.

[24] And ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ came down to meet the king. And he had not done his feet, and he had not done his moustache, and his garments he had not washed, from the day the king went until the day that he came in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[25] And it came to pass, when he came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to meet the king, that the king said to him, "Why did you not go with me, ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧?"

[26] And he said, "My lord the king, my servant deceived me. For your servant said, 'Let me saddle the donkey for myself, and I will ride on it and go with the king,' for your servant is lame.

[27] And he has slandered your servant to my lord the king. And my lord the king is as an angel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and do what is good in your eyes.

[28] For all the house of my father were nothing but men of death to my lord the king, and you set your servant among those who eat at your table. And what righteousness have I yet, and to cry out yet to the king?"

[29] And the king said to him, "Why do you yet speak your words? I have said: you and ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ shall divide the field."

[30] And ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧ said to the king, "Let him take even the whole, after my lord the king has come in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to his house."

[31] And Barzillai the ⟦gileadite|Giladi|Gileadite⟧ came down from Rogelim, and he passed over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ with the king, to send him away at the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[32] And Barzillai was very old, eighty years old. And he had sustained the king during his stay in ⟦mahanaim|Machanayim|Mahanaim⟧, for he was a very great man.

[33] And the king said to Barzillai, "You, pass over with me, and I will sustain you with me in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

[34] And Barzillai said to the king, "How many are the days of the years of my life, that I should go up with the king to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?

[35] Eighty years old am I today. Do I know between good and evil? Or does your servant taste what I eat and what I drink? Or do I still listen to the voice of singing men and singing women? And why should your servant be yet a burden to my lord the king?

[36] For a little your servant will pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ with the king. And why should the king deal to me this dealing?

[37] Let your servant return, please, and let me die in my city, by the grave of my father and my mother. And behold, your servant ⟦chimham|Kimham|Chimham⟧: let him pass over with my lord the king, and do to him what is good in your eyes."

[38] And the king said, "With me ⟦chimham|Kimham|Chimham⟧ shall pass over, and I will do to him what is good in your eyes, and all that you choose upon me I will do for you."

[39] And all the people passed over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and the king passed over. And the king kissed Barzillai and blessed him, and he returned to his place.

[40] And the king passed over to Gilgal, and ⟦chimham|Kimham|Chimham⟧ passed over with him. And all the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ made the king pass over, and also half the people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[41] And behold, all the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were coming to the king, and they said to the king, "Why have our brothers, the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, stolen you and made the king and his house pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and all the men of David with him?"

[42] And all the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ answered the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "Because the king is near to me. And why is it that you burn with anger over this thing? Have we indeed eaten from the king? Or has anything been carried away for us?"

[43] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ answered the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and said, "Ten hands have I in the king, and also in David I am more than you. And why did you make light of me? And was not my word the first, for me, to bring back my king?" And the word of the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ was harder than the word of the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13, 27) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v27 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew counts 18:33 as 19:1, so that v1–43 of this chapter are 19:2–44 in the Hebrew numbering.
- **"weepeth and mourneth for Absalom" → "is weeping and mourning over Absalom"** (v1) — Hebrew בֹּכֶה וַיִּתְאַבֵּל עַל.
- **"the victory that day was turned into mourning" → "the salvation on that day became mourning"** (v2) — Hebrew הַתְּשֻׁעָה, "the salvation"; וַתְּהִי … לְאֵבֶל, "became mourning". **"how the king was grieved for his son" → "saying, 'The king is grieved over his son'"** — direct speech; נֶעֱצַב.
- **"gat them by stealth that day into the city, as people being ashamed steal away when they flee in battle" → "stole away on that day to come into the city, as people steal away who are put to shame when they flee in the battle"** (v3) — Hebrew הִתְגַּנֵּב, "steal oneself away"; הַנִּכְלָמִים.
- **"O my son Absalom, O Absalom, my son, my son!" → "My son Absalom, Absalom, my son, my son!"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּנִי אַבְשָׁלוֹם אַבְשָׁלוֹם בְּנִי בְנִי; no "O". **"cried with a loud voice" → "cried out"** — Hebrew זָעַק; literally "a great voice".
- **"Thou hast shamed this day the faces of all thy servants, which this day have saved thy life" → "You have shamed today the faces of all your servants, who have rescued your soul today"** (v5) — Hebrew הֹבַשְׁתָּ; הַמְמַלְּטִים, "who made escape" (מִלֵּט; "delivered" in v9 is הִצִּיל); נֶפֶשׁ "soul", four times. "Servants" is the word "slaves" (עֲבָדִים), as everywhere of the king's men.
- **"In that thou lovest thine enemies, and hatest thy friends" → "to love those who hate you and to hate those who love you"** (v6) — Hebrew לְאַהֲבָה אֶת־שֹׂנְאֶיךָ וְלִשְׂנֹא אֶת־אֹהֲבֶיךָ, two infinitives and two participles of "love" and "hate". **"thou hast declared this day, that thou regardest neither princes nor servants" → "you have told today that you have no commanders and servants"** — Hebrew הִגַּדְתָּ; כִּי אֵין לְךָ שָׂרִים וַעֲבָדִים, "that there are to you no commanders and servants". **"if Absalom had lived … then it had pleased thee well" → "if Absalom were alive … then it would be right in your eyes"** — "if" is the reading tradition (qere לוּ; the written text has לא "not"); יָשָׁר בְּעֵינֶיךָ.
- **"speak comfortably unto thy servants" → "speak to the heart of your servants"** (v7) — Hebrew דַבֵּר עַל־לֵב. **"I swear by the LORD, if thou go not forth, there will not tarry one with thee this night" → "by Yahweh I swear that, if you do not go out, not a man will lodge with you tonight"** — the oath is literally "that you are not going out — if a man lodges with you tonight": the "if" of an oath means "no man will". **"worse unto thee than all the evil that befell thee" → "worse for you than all the evil that has come upon you"** — Hebrew וְרָעָה לְךָ זֹאת מִכָּל־הָרָעָה, "and evil for you is this, more than all the evil".
- **"every man to his tent" → "each man to his tents"** (v8) — Hebrew לְאֹהָלָיו, plural. "Israel" here is those who had followed Absalom (18:17).
- **"all the people were at strife"** (v9) — Hebrew נָדוֹן, from דִּין "judge": arguing the matter with one another. **"saved us out of the hand of our enemies, and he delivered us out of the hand of the Philistines" → "delivered us from the palm of our enemies, and he rescued us from the palm of the Philistines"** — Hebrew הִצִּילָנוּ, מִלְּטָנוּ; כַּף "palm", twice. **"fled out of the land for Absalom" → "fled from the land, away from Absalom"** — Hebrew מֵעַל אַבְשָׁלוֹם, "from upon Absalom".
- **"why speak ye not a word of bringing the king back?" → "why are you silent about bringing back the king?"** (v10) — Hebrew מַחֲרִשִׁים.
- **"seeing the speech of all Israel is come to the king, even to his house" → "And the word of all Israel has come to the king, to his house"** (v11) — Hebrew וּדְבַר כָּל־יִשְׂרָאֵל. Whether these words are still part of the message or the narrator's remark is not marked in the Hebrew.
- **"Ye are my brethren, ye are my bones and my flesh" → "My brothers are you; my bone and my flesh are you"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew עַצְמִי וּבְשָׂרִי, singular "my bone".
- **"God do so to me, and more also, if thou be not captain of the host before me continually in the room of Joab" → "Thus may Elohim do to me, and thus may he add: you shall surely be commander of the host before me all the days, in place of Joab"** (v13) — the oath formula, כֹּה יַעֲשֶׂה־לִּי אֱלֹהִים וְכֹה יוֹסִיף אִם־לֹא, literally "thus may Elohim do to me, and thus may he add, if you shall not be …": the "if not" of an oath means "you surely shall". שַׂר־צָבָא "commander of the host"; כָּל־הַיָּמִים "all the days".
- **"he bowed the heart of all the men of Judah, even as the heart of one man" → "he turned the heart of all the men of Judah as one man"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיַּט אֶת־לְבַב; who "he" is (David, or Amasa) is not said. "The heart of" is not repeated. "The men of Judah" is the singular אִישׁ יְהוּדָה, "the man of Judah", as a body (so through v43, also of Israel).
- **"to conduct the king over Jordan" → "to make the king pass over the Jordan"** (v15, 18, 40, 41) — Hebrew הֶעֱבִיר, the hifil of עָבַר "pass over", the verb that runs through v15–41.
- **"hasted and came down" → "hastened and went down"** (v16) — Hebrew יָרַד. **"a Benjamite" → "the Benjaminite"** — Hebrew בֶּן־הַיְמִינִי.
- **"Ziba the servant of the house of Saul" → "the young man of the house of Saul"** (v17) — Hebrew נַעַר. **"they went over Jordan before the king" → "they rushed through the Jordan before the king"** — Hebrew וְצָלְחוּ הַיַּרְדֵּן: צָלַח is "rush" (as of the spirit, 1 Sam 10:6); with "the Jordan" as its object it is found only here, and "through" is supplied.
- **"there went over a ferry boat to carry over the king's household" → "the ferry passed over, to make the house of the king pass over"** (v18) — Hebrew וְעָבְרָה הָעֲבָרָה, verb and noun of one root, "and the crossing crossed": עֲבָרָה is a ford or a means of crossing; in this sense it stands only here and is uncertain. The Greek has "and they did the service" (from memory). "In his eyes" is the reading tradition (qere). **"as he was come over Jordan" → "as he was passing over the Jordan"** — Hebrew בְּעָבְרוֹ בַּיַּרְדֵּן, "at his passing over in the Jordan": "he" can be Shimei or the king.
- **"Let not my lord impute iniquity unto me, neither do thou remember that which thy servant did perversely … that the king should take it to his heart" → "Let not my lord reckon iniquity to me, and do not remember how your servant did iniquity … for the king to set it to his heart"** (v19) — Hebrew חָשַׁב "reckon"; הֶעֱוָה, the verb of עָוֹן "iniquity" (7:14); לָשׂוּם הַמֶּלֶךְ אֶל־לִבּוֹ. In the Masoretic Text the word יָצָא "went out" is marked with dots over its letters.
- **"therefore, behold, I am come the first this day of all the house of Joseph" → "and behold, I have come today, first of all the house of Joseph"** (v20) — "therefore" is not in the Hebrew. A Benjaminite counts himself with "the house of Joseph", the northern tribes.
- **"the LORD's anointed" → "the anointed of Yahweh"** (v21) — Hebrew מְשִׁיחַ יְהוָה. **"cursed"** — Hebrew קִלֵּל, the verb of 16:5–13.
- **"that ye should this day be adversaries unto me" → "that you should be to me today as an adversary"** (v22) — Hebrew לְשָׂטָן, the common noun *satan*, "adversary, one who opposes" (Num 22:22; 1 Sam 29:4), singular. **"shall there any man be put to death this day in Israel?" → "Today shall a man be put to death in Israel?"** — "today" stands first; it stands three times in the verse.
- **"Therefore the king said"** (v23) — "therefore" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Mephibosheth the son of Saul"** (v24) — so the Hebrew, בֶּן־שָׁאוּל; he is the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul (9:6). **"had neither dressed his feet, nor trimmed his beard, nor washed his clothes" → "had not done his feet, and he had not done his moustache, and his garments he had not washed"** — Hebrew עָשָׂה "do, make", twice (to tend, to trim); שָׂפָם, the hair of the upper lip; בְּגָדָיו; כִּבֵּס. **"until the day he came again in peace" → "until the day that he came in shalom"** — Hebrew בְשָׁלוֹם; so also v30.
- **"when he was come to Jerusalem to meet the king"** (v25) — Hebrew כִּי־בָא יְרוּשָׁלִַם, translated as it stands; it is difficult, since Mephibosheth was in Jerusalem (16:3) and "came down" to the Jordan in v24. Some manuscripts of the Greek have "from Jerusalem" (from memory); the words can also be read "when Jerusalem came".
- **"I will saddle me an ass, that I may ride thereon" → "Let me saddle the donkey for myself, and I will ride on it"** (v26) — Hebrew אֶחְבְּשָׁה־לִּי הַחֲמוֹר. The Greek has "Saddle for me" (a command to the servant; from memory). **"go to the king" → "go with the king"** — Hebrew אֶת־הַמֶּלֶךְ.
- **"he hath slandered thy servant" → "he has slandered your servant"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיְרַגֵּל בְּ, the verb of "spy" (מְרַגֵּל, 15:10): to go about against someone with talk. **"as an angel of God" → "as an angel of Elohim"** — Hebrew כְּמַלְאַךְ הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article on Elohim (so that "as the angel of Elohim" is also possible), as 14:17, 20; מַלְאָךְ is "messenger" (the earlier form of this chapter had "the Messenger"); "angel of Elohim" is the one rendering of this phrase.
- **"were but dead men before my lord the king" → "were nothing but men of death to my lord the king"** (v28) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי־מָוֶת, as "son of death" (12:5). **"What right therefore have I yet to cry any more unto the king?" → "And what righteousness have I yet, and to cry out yet to the king?"** — Hebrew וּמַה־יֶּשׁ־לִי עוֹד צְדָקָה וְלִזְעֹק עוֹד, literally "and what is there for me yet of righteousness, and to cry out yet": צְדָקָה is here a rightful claim.
- **"Why speakest thou any more of thy matters? I have said, Thou and Ziba divide the land" → "Why do you yet speak your words? I have said: you and Ziba shall divide the field"** (v29) — Hebrew דְּבָרֶיךָ "your words"; הַשָּׂדֶה "the field".
- **"Yea, let him take all, forasmuch as" → "Let him take even the whole, after"** (v30) — Hebrew גַּם אֶת־הַכֹּל; אַחֲרֵי אֲשֶׁר.
- **"went over Jordan with the king, to conduct him over Jordan" → "passed over the Jordan with the king, to send him away at the Jordan"** (v31) — Hebrew לְשַׁלְּחוֹ, "to send him off"; the reading tradition (qere) is אֶת־הַיַּרְדֵּן, the written text (ketiv) בירדן "in the Jordan"; the clause is difficult.
- **"a very aged man, even fourscore years old: and he had provided the king of sustenance while he lay at Mahanaim" → "very old, eighty years old. And he had sustained the king during his stay in Mahanaim"** (v32, 33) — literally "a son of eighty years"; כִּלְכֵּל "sustain", in both verses; בְשִׁיבָתוֹ, "in his dwelling" (an unusual form).
- **"How long have I to live" → "How many are the days of the years of my life"** (v34) — Hebrew כַּמָּה יְמֵי שְׁנֵי חַיַּי.
- **"can I discern between good and evil? can thy servant taste what I eat or what I drink? can I hear any more the voice of singing men and singing women?" → "Do I know between good and evil? Or does your servant taste what I eat and what I drink? Or do I still listen to the voice of singing men and singing women?"** (v35) — word for word: הַאֵדַע בֵּין־טוֹב לְרָע אִם־יִטְעַם … אִם־אֶשְׁמַע עוֹד בְּקוֹל: "know", not "discern"; "can" is not written; three questions (הֲ … אִם … אִם). **"wherefore then should thy servant be yet a burden unto my lord the king?"** — Hebrew לְמַשָּׂא אֶל, the word of 15:33.
- **"Thy servant will go a little way over Jordan with the king: and why should the king recompense it me with such a reward?" → "For a little your servant will pass over the Jordan with the king. And why should the king deal to me this dealing?"** (v36) — Hebrew כִּמְעַט; יִגְמְלֵנִי … הַגְּמוּלָה הַזֹּאת, verb and noun of one root: "deal", here of a good return.
- **"be buried by the grave" → "by the grave"** (v37) — "be buried" is not in the Hebrew; קֶבֶר "grave". **"what shall seem good unto thee" → "what is good in your eyes"** (v37, 38).
- **"whatsoever thou shalt require of me" → "all that you choose upon me"** (v38) — Hebrew תִּבְחַר עָלַי, "choose (to lay) upon me".
- **"he returned unto his own place"** (v39) — "he" is Barzillai; the name is not repeated in the Hebrew.
- **"Chimham went on with him: and all the people of Judah conducted the king" → "Chimham passed over with him. And all the people of Judah made the king pass over"** (v40) — the name is written כִמְהָן, "Chimhan", in this verse; "made … pass over" is the reading tradition (qere הֶעֱבִירוּ); the written text (ketiv) is "passed over with the king".
- **"Why have our brethren the men of Judah stolen thee away" → "stolen you"** (v41) — Hebrew גְּנָבוּךָ.
- **"Because the king is near of kin to us: wherefore then be ye angry for this matter? have we eaten at all of the king's cost? or hath he given us any gift?" → "Because the king is near to me. And why is it that you burn with anger over this thing? Have we indeed eaten from the king? Or has anything been carried away for us?"** (v42) — the quarrel as the Hebrew has it: each side speaks as one man ("to me", "you" singular; so "I", "me", "my" in v43). קָרוֹב "near"; חָרָה לְךָ, literally "it burns to you"; הֶאָכוֹל אָכַלְנוּ מִן־הַמֶּלֶךְ, literally "eating have we eaten from the king". The last clause, אִם־נִשֵּׂאת נִשָּׂא לָנוּ, is difficult: נִשֵּׂאת is an unusual form of נָשָׂא "lift, carry", "has a carrying-off been carried off for us" or "has a gift been given us". "Answered" is here עָנָה עַל.
- **"We have ten parts in the king, and we have also more right in David than ye: why then did ye despise us, that our advice should not be first had in bringing back our king?" → "Ten hands have I in the king, and also in David I am more than you. And why did you make light of me? And was not my word the first, for me, to bring back my king?"** (v43) — Hebrew עֶשֶׂר־יָדוֹת, "ten hands": ten shares; וְגַם־בְּדָוִד אֲנִי מִמְּךָ, "and also in David I (am) more than you" (the Greek has "and I am firstborn rather than you", from memory); הֱקִלֹּתַנִי, the hifil of קלל "be light", the root of "curse" in v21; וְלֹא־הָיָה דְבָרִי רִאשׁוֹן לִי. **"the words of the men of Judah were fiercer" → "the word … was harder"** — Hebrew וַיִּקֶשׁ דְּבַר, singular.

### Notes
- **Joab's speech** (v5–7) — Joab tells the king to his face what his mourning says to the men who fought for him, and swears that they will leave him that night.
- **Amasa in place of Joab** (v13) — David swears to give Absalom's commander of the host Joab's place.
- **Shimei** (v16–23) — the king swears that he shall not die; see 1 Kgs 2:8–9, 36–46.
- **Mephibosheth** (v24–30) — his account differs from Ziba's (16:3); the king has the field divided. The narrator does not say who spoke the truth.
- **Barzillai** (v31–39) — declines the king's offer and sends Chimham with him (1 Kgs 2:7).
- **Israel and Judah** (v41–43) — the quarrel over who brings the king back leads into Sheba's revolt in ch 20.
