# 2 Samuel 16

[1] And David had passed on a little from the top, and behold, ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧, the young man of ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧, to meet him, and a pair of saddled donkeys, and on them two hundred loaves of bread and a hundred raisin cakes and a hundred of summer fruit and a jar of wine.

[2] And the king said to ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧, "What are these to you?" And ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ said, "The donkeys are for the house of the king to ride, and the bread and the summer fruit for the young men to eat, and the wine for the weary in the wilderness to drink."

[3] And the king said, "And where is the son of your lord?" And ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ said to the king, "Behold, he is dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for he said, 'Today the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ will return to me the kingdom of my father.'"

[4] And the king said to ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧, "Behold, yours is all that is ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧'s." And ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ said, "I bow down. Let me find favor in your eyes, my lord the king."

[5] And King David came as far as ⟦bahurim|Bachurim|Bahurim⟧, and behold, from there a man was coming out, of the clan of the house of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and his name was ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ the son of Gera; he came out, coming out and cursing.

[6] And he stoned David with stones, and all the servants of King David; and all the people and all the mighty ones were on his right and on his left.

[7] And thus ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ said in his cursing: "Go out, go out, man of blood and ⟦belial|man of Beliyyaal|man of worthlessness⟧!

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has returned upon you all the blood of the house of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, in whose place you have reigned, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given the kingship into the hand of ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ your son; and behold, you are in your evil, for a man of blood are you."

[9] And ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ said to the king, "Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? Let me pass over, please, and remove his head."

[10] And the king said, "What have I to do with you, sons of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧? Thus he curses, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has said to him, 'Curse David,' and who shall say, 'Why have you done so?'"

[11] And David said to ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ and to all his servants, "Behold, my son, who came out of my inward parts, is seeking my soul; and how much more now the ⟦benjaminite|Yemini|Benjaminite⟧! Leave him, and let him curse, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has said it to him.

[12] Perhaps ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will look on my eye, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will return to me good for his curse this day."

[13] And David went, and his men, on the way; and ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ was going on the side of the mountain alongside him, going and cursing, and he stoned with stones alongside him and dusted with dust.

[14] And the king came, and all the people who were with him, weary, and he was refreshed there.

[15] And ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ and all the people, the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and ⟦ahithophel|Achitofel|Ahithophel⟧ with him.

[16] And it came to pass, when ⟦hushai|Chushai|Hushai⟧ the ⟦archite|Arki|Archite⟧, the friend of David, came to ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧, that ⟦hushai|Chushai|Hushai⟧ said to ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧, "May the king live! May the king live!"

[17] And ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ said to ⟦hushai|Chushai|Hushai⟧, "This is your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with your friend? Why did you not go with your friend?"

[18] And ⟦hushai|Chushai|Hushai⟧ said to ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧, "No, for whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has chosen, and this people and all the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, his I will be, and with him I will dwell.

[22] And they pitched for ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ the tent on the roof, and ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ went in to the concubines of his father before the eyes of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[23] And the counsel of ⟦ahithophel|Achitofel|Ahithophel⟧ that he counseled in those days was as when a man asks of the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; so was all the counsel of ⟦ahithophel|Achitofel|Ahithophel⟧, both to David and to ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v23) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"a little past the top of the hill" → "a little from the top"** (v1) — Hebrew מֵהָרֹאשׁ, "from the head", the top of 15:32; "passed on" is עָבַר as in chapter 15. **"the servant of Mephibosheth" → "the young man of Mephibosheth"** — Hebrew נַעַר, "boy", of an attendant. **"met him" → "to meet him"** — the Hebrew has no verb. **"a couple of asses saddled" → "a pair of saddled donkeys"** — Hebrew חֲבֻשִׁים, "bound". **"two hundred loaves of bread, and an hundred bunches of raisins, and an hundred of summer fruits, and a bottle of wine" → "two hundred loaves of bread and a hundred raisin cakes and a hundred of summer fruit and a jar of wine"** — literally "two hundred of bread and a hundred of raisins and a hundred of summer fruit" ("loaves" and "cakes" are supplied with the numbers, as 1 Sam 25:18); נֵבֶל is a jar or a skin for wine.
- **"What meanest thou by these?" → "What are these to you?"** (v2) — Hebrew מָה־אֵלֶּה לָּךְ. **"the bread"** — the reading tradition (qere) וְהַלֶּחֶם; the written text (ketiv) is ולהלחם, which as it stands would be "and to fight". **"such as be faint" → "the weary"** — Hebrew הַיָּעֵף, singular.
- **"thy master's son" → "the son of your lord"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲדֹנֶיךָ, as in "my lord the king". **"abideth" → "is dwelling"** — Hebrew יוֹשֵׁב. **"restore me" → "return to me"** — Hebrew יָשִׁיבוּ, "bring back". **"the kingdom"** — Hebrew מַמְלְכוּת; in v8 "the kingship" is הַמְּלוּכָה.
- **"I humbly beseech thee that I may find grace" → "I bow down. Let me find favor"** (v4) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוֵיתִי, "I have bowed down", said as he does it; "humbly" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"of the family of the house of Saul" → "of the clan of the house of Saul"** (v5) — Hebrew מִשְׁפַּחַת. **"he came forth, and cursed still as he came" → "he came out, coming out and cursing"** — Hebrew יֹצֵא יָצוֹא וּמְקַלֵּל.
- **"cursed", "curse"** (v5, 7, 9–13) — one Hebrew verb, קִלֵּל (piel of קלל "be light": to treat as worthless, to call down evil on), as Exod 21:17; in v12 the noun קְלָלָה. The verb אָרַר "curse" does not stand in this chapter.
- **"cast stones at David" → "stoned David with stones"** (v6, 13) — Hebrew וַיְסַקֵּל בָּאֲבָנִים. **"mighty men" → "mighty ones"** — Hebrew גִּבֹּרִים.
- **"Come out, come out, thou bloody man, and thou man of Belial" → "Go out, go out, man of blood and man of Beliyyaal"** (v7) — Hebrew צֵא צֵא; אִישׁ הַדָּמִים, "the man of the bloods" (the plural of blood that has been shed; so also "all the blood", דְּמֵי, and "a man of blood" in v8); אִישׁ הַבְּלִיָּעַל, with the article. בְּלִיַּעַל is a common noun, "worthlessness", not a name.
- **"in whose stead"** (v8) — the reading tradition (qere) תַּחְתָּיו. **"behold, thou art taken in thy mischief" → "behold, you are in your evil"** — Hebrew וְהִנְּךָ בְּרָעָתֶךָ; "taken" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"let me go over, I pray thee, and take off his head" → "Let me pass over, please, and remove his head"** (v9) — Hebrew עָבַר; הֵסִיר "remove".
- **"What have I to do with you"** (v10) — literally "What to me and to you". **"so let him curse, because the LORD hath said unto him" → "Thus he curses, for Yahweh has said to him"** — the reading tradition (qere) כֹּה יְקַלֵּל כִּי; the verb can be "he curses" or "let him curse". The written text (ketiv) is כי יקלל וכי, "when he curses, and when Yahweh has said to him".
- **"which came forth of my bowels, seeketh my life" → "who came out of my inward parts, is seeking my soul"** (v11) — Hebrew מִמֵּעַי; נַפְשִׁי "my soul". **"this Benjamite" → "the Benjaminite"** — Hebrew בֶּן־הַיְמִינִי, "the son of the Yeminite"; "this" is not in the Hebrew. **"let him alone" → "Leave him"** — Hebrew הַנִּחוּ לוֹ. **"for the LORD hath bidden him" → "for Yahweh has said it to him"** — Hebrew אָמַר־לוֹ, the verb of v10.
- **"It may be that the LORD will look on mine affliction" → "Perhaps Yahweh will look on my eye"** (v12) — the reading tradition (qere) is בְּעֵינִי, "on my eye" (taken as his tears); the written text (ketiv) בעוני is "on my iniquity" (the wrong laid on me, or my guilt). The versions have "on my affliction" (בְּעָנְיִי; from memory), which the KJV follows. The qere is translated. **"requite me good for his cursing" → "return to me good for his curse"** — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב, the verb of v8; קִלְלָתוֹ, "his curse": the curse laid on David; תַּחַת is "in the place of".
- **"on the hill's side over against him" → "on the side of the mountain alongside him"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּצֵלַע הָהָר לְעֻמָּתוֹ. **"cast dust" → "dusted with dust"** — Hebrew וְעִפַּר בֶּעָפָר, verb and noun of one root.
- **"came weary, and refreshed themselves" → "came … weary, and he was refreshed"** (v14) — Hebrew עֲיֵפִים "weary" (some take it as a place name, since no place is named for "there"); וַיִּנָּפֵשׁ, singular, the verb of Exod 31:17, "took breath".
- **"God save the king" → "May the king live!"** (v16) — Hebrew יְחִי הַמֶּלֶךְ; "God" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Is this thy kindness to thy friend?" → "This is your chesed with your friend?"** (v17) — Hebrew זֶה חַסְדְּךָ אֶת־רֵעֶךָ: no question word; חֶסֶד is *chesed*.
- **"Nay; but whom the LORD … choose, his will I be, and with him will I abide" → "No, for whom Yahweh has chosen …, his I will be, and with him I will dwell"** (v18) — "his" is the reading tradition (qere) לוֹ; the written text (ketiv) לא is "not". The verb "has chosen" is singular, with Yahweh.
- **"And again, whom should I serve? should I not serve in the presence of his son?" → "And the second thing: whom shall I serve? Is it not before his son?"** (v19) — Hebrew וְהַשֵּׁנִית; the second "serve" is not in the Hebrew. **"so will I be in thy presence" → "so I will be before you"**.
- **"Give counsel among you" → "Give counsel, you"** (v20) — Hebrew הָבוּ לָכֶם עֵצָה, a plural: "give for yourselves counsel".
- **"that thou art abhorred of thy father" → "that you have made yourself stink with your father"** (v21) — Hebrew נִבְאַשְׁתָּ אֶת־אָבִיךָ, as 1 Sam 13:4; 27:12. **"shall be strong"** — Hebrew וְחָזְקוּ.
- **"a tent upon the top of the house" → "the tent on the roof"** (v22) — Hebrew הָאֹהֶל, with the article; גָּג "roof" (the roof of 11:2). **"in the sight of" → "before the eyes of"**.
- **"as if a man had enquired at the oracle of God" → "as when a man asks of the word of Elohim"** (v23) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר יִשְׁאַל … בִּדְבַר הָאֱלֹהִים, "asks of the word of Elohim" (שָׁאַל בְּ as "ask of Yahweh"); "a man" is the reading tradition (qere אִישׁ), which the written text lacks. **"which he counselled"** — Hebrew עֲצַת … אֲשֶׁר יָעַץ, noun and verb of one root.

### Notes
- **Ziba's report** — Ziba speaks against Mephibosheth and is given his estate. David grants it without investigation; see 19:24–30.
- **Shimei's curse** — Benjaminite (Saul's tribe) grievance emerges publicly. Which blood of the house of Saul he means is not said (compare 3:27; 4:5–12; 21:1–9).
- **David's response to Shimei** (v10–12) — remarkable restraint. He takes it as possibly said to Shimei by Yahweh.
- **Ahithophel's advice** (v21–22) — to go in to David's concubines publicly. Compare Nathan's word (12:11–12). Symbolic seizure of kingship; also burns bridges with David.
- **Ahithophel's counsel "as the word of Elohim"** (v23) — almost infallible reputation. Makes his defection extraordinarily dangerous.
