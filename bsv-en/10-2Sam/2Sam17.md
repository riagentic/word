## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"counsel", "counseled"** (v7, 11, 14, 15, 21, 23) — עֵצָה and יָעַץ, one root, one English word; "the counsel of Ahithophel" as in 15:31, 34; 16:23.
- **"Let me now choose out"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶבְחֲרָה נָּא, "let me choose, please"; "let me arise and pursue" are the same form. **"this night" → "tonight"**.
- **"weary and weak handed, and will make him afraid" → "wearied and slack of hands, and I will make him tremble"** (v2) — Hebrew יָגֵעַ, "worn with labor" (another word than עָיֵף "weary", v29); רְפֵה יָדַיִם; הֶחֱרִיד, the hifil of חָרַד "tremble". **"I will smite the king only" → "strike down the king alone"**.
- **"And I will bring back all the people unto thee: the man whom thou seekest is as if all returned: so all the people shall be in peace" → "And let me bring back all the people to you. As the returning of the whole is the man whom you are seeking; all the people will be shalom"** (v3) — the Hebrew is difficult: כְּשׁוּב הַכֹּל הָאִישׁ אֲשֶׁר אַתָּה מְבַקֵּשׁ, word for word "as the returning of the all, the man whom you are seeking". It is translated as it stands; it is commonly taken to mean that the death of the one man is as good as the return of all. The Greek has a longer text here, "as a bride returns to her husband; only the soul of one man you seek" (from memory), which many modern translations follow. The last clause has no preposition: "all the people will be peace" (שָׁלוֹם).
- **"the saying pleased Absalom well" → "the word was right in the eyes of Absalom"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיִּישַׁר הַדָּבָר בְּעֵינֵי.
- **"let us hear likewise what he saith" → "let us hear what is in his mouth, he also"** (v5) — Hebrew מַה־בְּפִיו גַּם־הוּא. "Call" is singular.
- **"Ahithophel hath spoken after this manner: shall we do after his saying?" → "According to this word Ahithophel has spoken. Shall we do his word?"** (v6) — Hebrew דָּבָר, twice.
- **"is not good at this time" → "this time"** (v7) — Hebrew בַּפַּעַם הַזֹּאת, "at this stroke": this once. "Not good" stands first.
- **"mighty men, and they be chafed in their minds, as a bear robbed of her whelps" → "mighty ones, and they are bitter of soul, like a bereaved bear"** (v8) — Hebrew גִּבֹּרִים; מָרֵי נֶפֶשׁ; דֹב שַׁכּוּל, "a bear bereaved": "of her whelps" is not in the Hebrew (the verb itself says the loss of young). **"will not lodge with the people"** — Hebrew לוּן.
- **"he is hid now in some pit, or in some other place" → "he has hidden himself in one of the pits or in one of the places"** (v9) — Hebrew נֶחְבָּא; "other" is not in the Hebrew. **"when some of them be overthrown at the first" → "at the falling among them at the first"** — Hebrew כִּנְפֹל בָּהֶם, "at (the) falling among them": who falls, or who falls upon whom, is not said. **"a slaughter" → "a smiting"** — Hebrew מַגֵּפָה, a blow, a defeat.
- **"he also that is valiant … shall utterly melt" → "he, even a son of ability … will surely melt"** (v10) — Hebrew בֶּן־חַיִל; הִמֵּס יִמָּס, literally "melting he will melt". **"valiant men" → "sons of ability"**.
- **"Therefore I counsel that all Israel be generally gathered" → "But I counsel: let all Israel surely be gathered"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּי "but, rather" after the "not" of v7; הֵאָסֹף יֵאָסֵף, literally "being gathered let it be gathered". **"and that thou go to battle in thine own person" → "and your face going in the battle"** — Hebrew וּפָנֶיךָ הֹלְכִים בַּקְרָב: "your face" is you in person; בַּקְרָב is pointed as "in the battle" (the consonants can also be read "in the midst").
- **"we will light upon him as the dew falleth" → "we will rest upon him"** (v12) — Hebrew וְנַחְנוּ עָלָיו, taken from נוּחַ "rest, settle"; the form is also the pronoun "and we" ("and we upon him"). "In one of the places" follows the reading tradition (qere בְּאַחַד). **"there shall not be left so much as one" → "there will not remain … even one"** — Hebrew נוֹתַר.
- **"if he be gotten into a city, then shall all Israel bring ropes" → "if he is gathered into a city, then all Israel will carry cords"** (v13) — Hebrew יֵאָסֵף, the verb of v11; הִשִּׂיאוּ, "make carry, bring"; חֲבָלִים. **"into the river" → "to the wadi"** — Hebrew הַנַּחַל. **"one small stone" → "a pebble"** — Hebrew צְרוֹר.
- **"the LORD had appointed to defeat the good counsel of Ahithophel, to the intent that the LORD might bring evil upon Absalom" → "Yahweh had commanded to make void the good counsel of Ahithophel, in order that Yahweh might bring the evil upon Absalom"** (v14) — Hebrew צִוָּה "commanded"; לְהָפֵר, as 15:34; הָרָעָה, "the evil", with the article.
- **"Thus and thus"** (v15) — Hebrew כָּזֹאת וְכָזֹאת, "like this and like this".
- **"Lodge not this night in the plains of the wilderness, but speedily pass over; lest the king be swallowed up" → "Do not lodge tonight in the plains of the wilderness, and also surely pass over, lest the king be swallowed up"** (v16) — Hebrew עָבוֹר תַּעֲבוֹר, literally "passing over you shall pass over"; פֶּן יְבֻלַּע לַמֶּלֶךְ, literally "lest it be swallowed up for the king and for all the people".
- **"stayed by En-rogel; for they might not be seen to come into the city: and a wench went and told them" → "were standing at En-rogel, and the maidservant would go and tell them … for they could not be seen to come into the city"** (v17) — Hebrew עֹמְדִים; הַשִּׁפְחָה, "the maidservant", with the article; the verbs tell what was done each time; לֹא יוּכְלוּ, "they were not able".
- **"a lad" → "a boy"** (v18) — Hebrew נַעַר. **"which had a well in his court; whither they went down" → "and he had a well in his court, and they went down there"** — Hebrew בְּאֵר; חָצֵר.
- **"spread a covering over the well's mouth, and spread ground corn thereon" → "spread the screen over the face of the well and spread out the groats on it"** (v19) — Hebrew הַמָּסָךְ "the screen", with the article; עַל־פְּנֵי "over the face of"; two verbs, פָּרַשׂ and שָׁטַח; הָרִפוֹת is found only here and in Prov 27:22 and its meaning is not certain (crushed or hulled grain).
- **"They be gone over the brook of water"** (v20) — Hebrew מִיכַל הַמָּיִם; מִיכַל is found only here and its meaning is not known ("brook" is the traditional rendering). **"when they had sought and could not find them" → "they sought and did not find"**.
- **"pass quickly over the water"** (v21) — "Arise and pass over" and "against you" are plural.
- **"by the morning light there lacked not one of them that was not gone over Jordan" → "until the light of the morning, until not one was missing who had not passed over the Jordan"** (v22) — Hebrew עַד־אַחַד לֹא נֶעְדָּר, "to one, none was missing".
- **"his counsel was not followed" → "was not done"** (v23) — Hebrew נֶעֶשְׂתָה. **"saddled his ass … and gat him home to his house, to his city, and put his household in order, and hanged himself" → "saddled the donkey and arose and went to his house, to his city, and he gave command to his house and strangled himself"** — Hebrew וַיְצַו אֶל־בֵּיתוֹ, "commanded to his house": his last orders; וַיֵּחָנַק, "strangled himself" (the verb of hanging by the neck). **"sepulchre" → "grave"** — Hebrew קֶבֶר.
- **"captain of the host instead of Joab" → "in place of Joab over the host"** (v25) — Hebrew תַּחַת יוֹאָב עַל־הַצָּבָא. **"Amasa was a man's son, whose name was Ithra an Israelite" → "Amasa was the son of a man, and his name was Ithra the Israelite"** — the Masoretic Text has הַיִּשְׂרְאֵלִי, "the Israelite", and is translated as it stands; 1 Chr 2:17 has "Jether the Ishmaelite" (יֶתֶר הַיִּשְׁמְעֵאלִי). **"Abigail the daughter of Nahash, sister to Zeruiah"** — Hebrew אֲבִיגַל; in 1 Chr 2:16 Zeruiah and Abigail are the sisters of the sons of Jesse. Who this Nahash is, is not said.
- **"pitched" → "camped"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיִּחַן.
- **"of Rabbah of the children of Ammon" → "from Rabbah of the sons of Ammon"** (v27) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־עַמּוֹן. The names are as the Hebrew has them: Shobi the son of Nahash, Machir the son of Ammiel from Lo-debar (9:4), Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim. The sentence has its verb only in v29.
- **"beds, and basons, and earthen vessels, and wheat, and barley, and flour, and parched corn, and beans, and lentiles, and parched pulse" → "bedding and basins and vessels of the potter, and wheat and barley and meal and parched grain, and beans and lentils and parched grain"** (v28) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּב, a singular, "what one lies on"; סַפּוֹת "basins"; כְּלִי יוֹצֵר, "vessel(s) of a potter"; קֶמַח "meal"; פוֹל "beans" (only here and Ezek 4:9). קָלִי "parched grain" stands twice in the Masoretic Text and is translated twice; the KJV made the second "parched pulse", and the earlier form of this chapter left it out.
- **"honey, and butter, and sheep, and cheese of kine" → "honey and curds and sheep and cheese of the herd"** (v29) — Hebrew חֶמְאָה "curds"; צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats); שְׁפוֹת בָּקָר is found only here and its meaning is not known ("cheese of the herd" is the traditional rendering). **"brought" → "brought near"** — Hebrew הִגִּישׁוּ.

### Notes
- **Council of war** — two counselors offer strategies: Ahithophel a strike that night at the wearied David, Hushai a muster of all Israel with Absalom himself going. The narrator calls Ahithophel's counsel "good" (v14).
- **"Yahweh had commanded"** (v14) — the narrator's own word on why Hushai's counsel was taken.
- **Underground network** — priest-sons as messengers; the maidservant at En-rogel as courier; Bahurim well as hiding place.
- **Ahithophel's death** (v23) — he strangles himself after setting his house in order. Compare Judas (Matt 27:5).
- **Amasa** — Absalom's commander of the host, kinsman of Joab. Joab kills him in ch 20.
- **Transjordan support** — Shobi, Machir, Barzillai bring bedding and food. Shobi is from Rabbah of Ammon (ch 10–12), Machir is the man in whose house Mephibosheth had been (9:4), Barzillai returns in ch 19.
