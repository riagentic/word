## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 23, 24) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"And again the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel, and he moved David against them" → "And the anger of Yahweh again burned against Israel, and he incited David against them"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף אַף־יְהוָה לַחֲרוֹת בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל וַיָּסֶת אֶת־דָּוִד בָּהֶם, literally "the anger (nose) of Yahweh added to burn against Israel, and he incited David against them". The second verb has no other subject than the first clause gives: Yahweh, or his anger. The verse is given word for word. The parallel 1 Chr 21:1 reads differently: "And *satan* (an adversary) stood up against Israel and incited David to count Israel"; nothing of that is brought into this verse. **"number" → "count"** — Hebrew מְנֵה.
- **"the captain of the host" → "the commander of the force"** (v2, 4) — Hebrew שַׂר־הַחַיִל; חַיִל of an army is "force" (צָבָא is "host"). **"Go now through" → "Roam, please, through"** (v2, 8) — Hebrew שׁוּט, "go about, rove"; נָא "please". **"number ye the people"** — Hebrew פִּקְדוּ, "number, muster" (also v4; the noun in v9); "count" in v1 is מָנָה and "numbered" in v10 is סָפַר: three Hebrew words.
- **"how many soever they be, an hundredfold, and that the eyes of my lord the king may see it" → "as they are and as they are, a hundred times, and the eyes of my lord the king seeing it"** (v3) — Hebrew כָּהֵם וְכָהֵם מֵאָה פְעָמִים, literally "like them and like them, a hundred times"; רֹאוֹת "seeing" ("it" is supplied). The speech begins with "And".
- **"the king's word prevailed against Joab" → "the word of the king was strong toward Joab and over the commanders"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיֶּחֱזַק … אֶל־יוֹאָב וְעַל. **"the people of Israel" → "the people, Israel"** — Hebrew אֶת־הָעָם אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"pitched in Aroer, on the right side of the city that lieth in the midst of the river of Gad" → "camped in Aroer, south of the city that is in the midst of the wadi of Gad"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיַּחֲנוּ בַעֲרוֹעֵר יְמִין הָעִיר אֲשֶׁר בְּתוֹךְ־הַנַּחַל הַגָּד וְאֶל־יַעְזֵר. יְמִין is "the right hand", that is, the south. הַנַּחַל הַגָּד is literally "the wadi, the Gad" (both with the article): difficult. The earlier text had "began from Aroer", which follows another reading (the Greek: from memory); the Hebrew has "camped in".
- **"Tahtim-hodshi … Dan-jaan"** (v6) — both names are found only here and are uncertain. **"and about to Zidon" → "and around to Sidon"** — Hebrew וְסָבִיב אֶל־צִידוֹן, with no verb.
- **"the strong hold of Tyre" → "the fortress of Tyre"** (v7) — Hebrew מִבְצַר־צֹר. "The Hivite", "the Canaanite" are singular. **"to the south of Judah, even to Beer-sheba" → "to the Negev of Judah, to Beersheba"**.
- **"the sum of the number of the people" → "the number of the numbering of the people"** (v9) — Hebrew מִסְפַּר מִפְקַד־הָעָם. **"eight hundred thousand valiant men that drew the sword … five hundred thousand men"** — the figures are those of the Hebrew text. 1 Chr 21:5 has one million one hundred thousand for all Israel and four hundred and seventy thousand for Judah. אִישׁ־חַיִל is "men of ability".
- **"David's heart smote him" → "the heart of David struck him"** (v10). **"take away the iniquity of thy servant" → "make the iniquity of your servant pass over"** — Hebrew הַעֲבֶר־נָא, as 12:13. "Servant" is עֶבֶד, "slave".
- **"For when David was up in the morning, the word of the LORD came" → "And David arose in the morning; and the word of Yahweh had come"** (v11) — Hebrew וּדְבַר־יְהוָה הָיָה, a perfect. **"David's seer"** — Hebrew חֹזֵה, "gazer".
- **"I offer thee three things" → "Three things I am lifting over you"** (v12) — Hebrew נוֹטֵל עָלֶיךָ, "lifting, holding over you"; "things" is supplied.
- **"seven years of famine" kept** (v13) — the Hebrew text has שֶׁבַע שָׁנִים "seven years". 1 Chr 21:12 has "three years of famine", and the Greek of Samuel has "three" (from memory); many translations follow them. The verse keeps the number as printed. **"while they pursue thee" → "while he pursues you"** — Hebrew וְהוּא רֹדְפֶךָ, singular, after the plural "your adversaries". **"advise, and see what answer I shall return" → "know and see what word I shall return"** — Hebrew דַּע וּרְאֵה מָה־אָשִׁיב … דָּבָר.
- **"I am in a great strait" → "I am in great distress"** (v14) — Hebrew צַר־לִי מְאֹד. **"his mercies are great" → "his compassions are many"** — Hebrew רַבִּים רַחֲמָיו (qere).
- **"sent a pestilence upon Israel … to the time appointed" → "gave a pestilence in Israel … until the appointed time"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן; עֵת מוֹעֵד, "the time of the appointment": which time is meant is not said.
- **"the angel" kept** (v16, 17) — Hebrew הַמַּלְאָךְ, "the messenger"; "the angel of Yahweh" at the end of v16 is מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה. The earlier text had "the Messenger" with a capital letter. **"to destroy it" → "to ruin it"** — Hebrew לְשַׁחֲתָהּ; "who was destroying among the people" is the same verb (הַמַּשְׁחִית בָּעָם), the word of "the destroyer" in Exod 12:23.
- **"the LORD repented him of the evil" → "Yahweh relented concerning the evil"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּחֶם יְהוָה אֶל־הָרָעָה: נִחַם "be sorry, relent"; רָעָה "evil" is the harm that was being done. **"It is enough: stay now thine hand" → "Enough! Now let your hand drop"** — Hebrew רַב עַתָּה הֶרֶף יָדֶךָ. **"Araunah"** — qere הָאֲרַוְנָה, with the article; the name is written three ways in the chapter (v16 ketiv האורנה, v18 ketiv ארניה); 1 Chr 21 has "Ornan".
- **"I have done wickedly: but these sheep" → "I have done iniquity; and these, the flock"** (v17) — Hebrew הֶעֱוֵיתִי; הַצֹּאן. The Hebrew has "said" twice.
- **"rear an altar" → "raise up an altar"** (v18) — Hebrew הָקֵם.
- **"coming on toward him" → "passing over to him"** (v20) — Hebrew עֹבְרִים עָלָיו. **"looked" → "looked down"** — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁקֵף. **"on his face upon the ground" → "his face to the earth"** — Hebrew אַפָּיו אָרְצָה.
- **"that the plague may be stayed from the people" → "and let the plague be restrained from upon the people"** (v21, 25) — Hebrew וְתֵעָצַר הַמַּגֵּפָה מֵעַל הָעָם; מַגֵּפָה is literally "a blow".
- **"oxen … threshing instruments and other instruments of the oxen" → "the herd … the threshing sledges and the gear of the herd"** (v22) — Hebrew הַבָּקָר; הַמֹּרִגִּים; כְּלֵי הַבָּקָר (yokes and harness). "What is good in his eyes" is the qere בְּעֵינָיו.
- **"All these things did Araunah, as a king, give unto the king" → "All of it Araunah the king gives to the king"** (v23) — Hebrew הַכֹּל נָתַן אֲרַוְנָה הַמֶּלֶךְ לַמֶּלֶךְ, word for word "the whole has given Araunah the king to the king". The verse is difficult. It is read as words of Araunah with an address, "All this, O king, Araunah gives to the king" (so many translations), or as "Araunah the king", a Jebusite king. The verse keeps the words in their order. **"accept thee" → "be pleased with you"** — Hebrew יִרְצֶךָ.
- **"Nay; but I will surely buy it of thee at a price: neither will I offer burnt offerings … of that which doth cost me nothing" → "No, for I will surely buy from you for a price, and I will not offer up … burnt offerings for nothing"** (v24) — Hebrew קָנוֹ אֶקְנֶה, "buying I will buy"; חִנָּם "for nothing". **"for fifty shekels of silver" → "for silver, fifty shekels"** — Hebrew בְּכֶסֶף שְׁקָלִים חֲמִשִּׁים; the figure is that of the Hebrew text. 1 Chr 21:25 has "shekels of gold, six hundred by weight", given "for the place".
- **"offered burnt offerings" → "offered up burnt offerings"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיַּעַל עֹלוֹת, verb and noun of one root. **"the LORD was intreated for the land" → "Yahweh let himself be pleaded with for the land"** — Hebrew וַיֵּעָתֵר, as 21:14.

### Notes
- **The numbering** — why it was sin the chapter does not say. Joab's question is in v3; compare Exod 30:12.
- **Three things** (v12–13) — famine, flight, pestilence. David's answer is v14.
- **Seventy thousand** (v15).
- **The angel** (v16–17) — the word is "messenger"; he is "the angel of Yahweh" at the threshing floor.
- **The threshing floor** (v18–25) — David will not take it for nothing. 2 Chr 3:1 names the threshing floor of Ornan as the place of Solomon's house of Yahweh.
- **The parallel** — 1 Chr 21, which differs in v1, in the figures of v9, 13, 24 and in other places.
