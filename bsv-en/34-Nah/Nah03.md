# Nahum 3

[1] Woe, city of bloodguilt!
All of her is full of lying, of plunder;
the prey does not depart.

[2] The sound of the whip, and the sound of the quaking of the wheel,
and horse galloping, and chariot skipping;

[3] horseman mounting, and the blade of the sword, and the lightning of the spear,
and a multitude of slain, and a weight of carcasses;
and there is no end to the bodies;
and they stumble on their bodies —

[4] because of the multitude of the prostitutions of the prostitute,
good of favor, a mistress of sorceries,
who sells nations by her prostitutions,
and clans by her sorceries.

[5] Behold, I am against you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
and I will uncover your skirts over your face,
and I will make nations see your nakedness,
and kingdoms your disgrace.

[6] And I will throw detestable things upon you, and treat you with contempt,
and set you as a spectacle.

[7] And it shall be: all who see you shall flee from you
and say, ⟦nineveh|Ninveh|Nineveh⟧ is laid waste; who will show grief for her?
From where shall I seek comforters for you?

[8] Are you better than ⟦thebes|No-Amon|Thebes⟧,
who dwelt among the ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧,
waters all around her,
whose rampart was the sea, from the sea her wall?

[9] ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ was her might, and ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and there was no end;
Put and the ⟦libyans|Luvim|Libyans⟧ were your help.

[10] She too went to exile, into captivity;
her infants too were dashed in pieces at the head of all the streets;
and for her honored ones they cast a lot,
and all her great ones were bound in fetters.

[11] You too shall be drunk; you shall be hidden;
you too shall seek a strong place from the enemy.

[12] All your fortresses are fig trees with firstfruits:
if they are shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater.

[13] Behold, your people are women in your midst;
to your enemies the gates of your land have surely been opened;
fire has consumed your bars.

[14] Draw yourself waters for the siege;
make your fortresses strong;
go into the mud and tread in the mortar;
take hold of the brick mold.

[15] There fire shall consume you;
the sword shall cut you off;
it shall consume you like the hopping locust.
Make yourself heavy like the hopping locust;
make yourself heavy like the locusts.

[16] You have multiplied your merchants more than the stars of the heavens;
the hopping locust strips and flies away.

[17] Your guardsmen are like the locusts,
and your marshals like a swarm of locusts
that camp in the walls on a day of cold;
the sun has dawned, and it flees,
and its place is not known — where are they?

[18] Your shepherds slumber, king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧;
your nobles abide;
your people are spread about on the mountains,
and there is none who gathers.

[19] There is no abating of your breaking;
your blow is grievous.
All who hear the report of you clap the palm over you;
for upon whom has your evil not passed continually?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v5) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, the prophets' formula; נְאֻם "declares".
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v2–4 are one sentence in the Hebrew (v4 opens with מֵרֹב "because of the multitude"), so v3 ends with a dash. The quotation marks of v7 are dropped (the Hebrew has none).
- **"Woe to the bloody city! it is all full of lies and robbery; the prey departeth not" → "Woe, city of bloodguilt! All of her is full of lying, of plunder; the prey does not depart"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹי עִיר דָּמִים, "Woe" with the bare noun (no "to"); דָּמִים, literally "bloods" (as Ezek 22:2; 24:6); כֻּלָּהּ "all of her"; כַּחַשׁ "lying"; פֶּרֶק "plunder" (literally "a tearing off"); לֹא יָמִישׁ "does not depart". The former "no end to the prey" was not the Hebrew.
- **"The noise of a whip, and the noise of the rattling of the wheels, and of the pransing horses, and of the jumping chariots" → "The sound of the whip, and the sound of the quaking of the wheel, and horse galloping, and chariot skipping"** (v2) — Hebrew קוֹל "sound" twice; רַעַשׁ "quaking"; אוֹפָן, סוּס, מֶרְכָּבָה singular; דֹּהֵר "galloping"; מְרַקֵּדָה, רִקֵּד "skip".
- **"The horseman lifteth up both the bright sword and the glittering spear" → "horseman mounting, and the blade of the sword, and the lightning of the spear"** (v3) — Hebrew פָּרָשׁ מַעֲלֶה "horseman making go up" (mounting, or charging; uncertain); לַהַב "blade" (literally "flame"); בְּרַק "lightning" (the flash of the spear). **"and there is a multitude of slain, and a great number of carcases; and there is none end of their corpses; they stumble upon their corpses" → "and a multitude of slain, and a weight of carcasses; and there is no end to the bodies; and they stumble on their bodies"** — כֹּבֶד "weight" (a heavy mass); פֶּגֶר "carcass", גְּוִיָּה "body" (two words); the qere וְכָשְׁלוּ "and they stumble" (written יכשלו "they shall stumble").
- **"Because of the multitude of the whoredoms of the wellfavoured harlot, the mistress of witchcrafts, that selleth nations through her whoredoms, and families through her witchcrafts" → "because of the multitude of the prostitutions of the prostitute, good of favor, a mistress of sorceries, who sells nations by her prostitutions, and clans by her sorceries"** (v4) — Hebrew זְנוּנֵי זוֹנָה "the prostitutions of a prostitute"; טוֹבַת חֵן "good of favor" (חֵן "favor"); בַּעֲלַת כְּשָׁפִים, literally "a lady of sorceries" (one marked by them); מֹכֶרֶת "selling" (KJV "selleth"; the former "betrays" was a guess); מִשְׁפָּחוֹת "clans".
- **"and I will discover thy skirts upon thy face, and I will shew the nations thy nakedness, and the kingdoms thy shame" → "and I will uncover your skirts over your face, and I will make nations see your nakedness, and kingdoms your disgrace"** (v5) — Hebrew גִּלֵּיתִי "I will uncover"; שׁוּלַיִךְ "your skirts"; הַרְאֵיתִי "I will make see"; מַעַר "nakedness" (a bare place); קָלוֹן "disgrace".
- **"And I will cast abominable filth upon thee, and make thee vile, and will set thee as a gazingstock" → "And I will throw detestable things upon you, and treat you with contempt, and set you as a spectacle"** (v6) — Hebrew שִׁקֻּצִים "detestable things" (the word of idols and unclean things); נִבַּלְתִּיךְ, נִבֵּל, of a person elsewhere "treat as a fool" (Deut 32:15): here, of the city put to shame, "treat with contempt" (as Jer 14:21); כְּרֹאִי "as a sight".
- **"And it shall come to pass, that all they that look upon thee shall flee from thee, and say, Nineveh is laid waste: who will bemoan her? whence shall I seek comforters for thee?" → "And it shall be: all who see you shall flee from you and say, Nineveh is laid waste; who will show grief for her? From where shall I seek comforters for you?"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; יִדּוֹד, נָדַד "flee"; שָׁדְּדָה, pual of שָׁדַד "lay waste"; יָנוּד לָהּ, literally "sway for her" (= show grief, as Job 2:11); מְנַחֲמִים "comforters".
- **"Art thou better than populous No, that was situate among the rivers, that had the waters round about it, whose rampart was the sea, and her wall was from the sea?" → "Are you better than No-Amon, who dwelt among the Niles, waters all around her, whose rampart was the sea, from the sea her wall?"** (v8) — Hebrew נֹא אָמוֹן *No-Amon* (Thebes; KJV "populous No" reads אָמוֹן as "multitude"); הַיֹּשְׁבָה "who dwelt"; בַּיְאֹרִים "among the Niles" (plural: the arms and canals of the river); חֵיל "rampart"; מִיָּם "from the sea" is the MT (the Septuagint, from memory, reads "water her wall": מַיִם); the MT is kept.
- **"Ethiopia and Egypt were her strength, and it was infinite; Put and Lubim were thy helpers" → "Cush was her might, and Egypt, and there was no end; Put and the Libyans were your help"** (v9) — Hebrew כּוּשׁ (token); עָצְמָה "her might"; וְאֵין קֵצֶה "and there was no end"; בְּעֶזְרָתֵךְ "your help" (בְּ "as", literally "in your help"; "your" feminine, the address to Nineveh breaking into the comparison, as printed).
- **"Yet was she carried away, she went into captivity" → "She too went to exile, into captivity"** (v10) — Hebrew גַּם־הִיא "she too" (גַּם four times in v10–11: "too"); לַגֹּלָה "to exile"; בַשֶּׁבִי "into captivity". **"her young children also were dashed in pieces at the top of all the streets" → "her infants too were dashed in pieces at the head of all the streets"** — עֹלָלֶיהָ "her infants"; בְּרֹאשׁ "at the head". **"and they cast lots for her honourable men, and all her great men were bound in chains" → "and for her honored ones they cast a lot, and all her great ones were bound in fetters"** — גּוֹרָל singular; יַדּוּ "they cast"; זִקִּים "fetters".
- **"Thou also shalt be drunken: thou shalt be hid, thou also shalt seek strength because of the enemy" → "You too shall be drunk; you shall be hidden; you too shall seek a strong place from the enemy"** (v11) — Hebrew תְּהִי נַעֲלָמָה "you shall be hidden"; מָעוֹז "strong place" (as 1:7).
- **"All thy strong holds shall be like fig trees with the firstripe figs: if they be shaken, they shall even fall into the mouth of the eater" → "All your fortresses are fig trees with firstfruits: if they are shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater"** (v12) — Hebrew has no "like": the fortresses *are* fig trees; בִּכּוּרִים "firstfruits" (the first-ripe figs); יִנּוֹעוּ, נוּעַ "sway, shake".
- **"Behold, thy people in the midst of thee are women: the gates of thy land shall be set wide open unto thine enemies: the fire shall devour thy bars" → "Behold, your people are women in your midst; to your enemies the gates of your land have surely been opened; fire has consumed your bars"** (v13) — Hebrew עַמֵּךְ "your people" (the former "troops" was interpretation); פָּתוֹחַ נִפְתְּחוּ, literally "opening they have been opened" (the infinitive absolute: "surely"); אָכְלָה, a perfect, אָכַל "consume"; בְּרִיחַ "bar".
- **"Draw thee waters for the siege, fortify thy strong holds: go into clay, and tread the morter, make strong the brickkiln" → "Draw yourself waters for the siege; make your fortresses strong; go into the mud and tread in the mortar; take hold of the brick mold"** (v14) — Hebrew מֵי מָצוֹר "waters of siege"; שַׁאֲבִי־לָךְ "draw for yourself"; חַזְּקִי "make strong"; טִיט "mud"; רִמְסִי "tread"; חֹמֶר "mortar"; הַחֲזִיקִי "take hold of"; מַלְבֵּן "brick mold".
- **"it shall eat thee up like the cankerworm: make thyself many as the cankerworm, make thyself many as the locusts" → "it shall consume you like the hopping locust. Make yourself heavy like the hopping locust; make yourself heavy like the locusts"** (v15) — Hebrew תֹּאכְלֵךְ, אָכַל "consume"; יֶלֶק "hopping locust" (kind or stage uncertain), אַרְבֶּה "locusts"; הִתְכַּבֵּד … הִתְכַּבְּדִי, hitpael of כָּבֵד "be heavy": "make yourself heavy" (= numerous, KJV "many"); the first form is masculine (or an infinitive), the second feminine, as printed.
- **"Thou hast multiplied thy merchants above the stars of heaven: the cankerworm spoileth, and flieth away" → "You have multiplied your merchants more than the stars of the heavens; the hopping locust strips and flies away"** (v16) — Hebrew רֹכְלַיִךְ "your merchants"; הַשָּׁמָיִם "the heavens"; פָּשַׁט "strip" (raid, or shed its skin; uncertain). The former note on יָנֵץ "spreads its wings" named a word that is not in the text.
- **"Thy crowned are as the locusts, and thy captains as the great grasshoppers, which camp in the hedges in the cold day, but when the sun ariseth they flee away, and their place is not known where they are" → "Your guardsmen are like the locusts, and your marshals like a swarm of locusts that camp in the walls on a day of cold; the sun has dawned, and it flees, and its place is not known — where are they?"** (v17) — Hebrew מִנְּזָרַיִךְ, found only here: "guardsmen" (KJV "crowned", from נֵזֶר; uncertain); טַפְסְרַיִךְ, a loan word, "marshals" (as Jer 51:27; uncertain); גּוֹב גֹּבָי "a swarm of locusts" (the word doubled); הַחוֹנִים "that camp"; גְּדֵרוֹת "walls" (stone walls of folds); זָרְחָה "has dawned"; וְנוֹדַד "and it flees" (singular, the swarm); אַיָּם "where are they?".
- **"Thy shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria: thy nobles shall dwell in the dust: thy people is scattered upon the mountains, and no man gathereth them" → "Your shepherds slumber, king of Assyria; your nobles abide; your people are spread about on the mountains, and there is none who gathers"** (v18) — Hebrew נָמוּ "slumber" (a perfect); no "O": the Hebrew has none; יִשְׁכְּנוּ, שָׁכַן "abide" (here lying still in death; "in the dust" is not in the Hebrew); נָפֹשׁוּ, from פּוּשׁ "spread about" (many read נָפֹצוּ "are scattered"; the MT is kept); מְקַבֵּץ "one who gathers".
- **"There is no healing of thy bruise; thy wound is grievous" → "There is no abating of your breaking; your blow is grievous"** (v19) — Hebrew אֵין־כֵּהָה, literally "no dimming" (of a pain: no abating); שִׁבְרֶךָ "your breaking"; נַחְלָה, nifal of חָלָה, "made sick", grievous; מַכָּתֶךָ "your blow". **"all that hear the bruit of thee shall clap the hands over thee" → "All who hear the report of you clap the palm over you"** — שֹׁמְעֵי שִׁמְעֲךָ "hearers of the report of you"; תָּקְעוּ כַף "clap the palm" (as Ps 47:1). **"for upon whom hath not thy wickedness passed continually?" → "for upon whom has your evil not passed continually?"** — רָעָתְךָ "your evil"; תָּמִיד "continually".

### Notes

- **v8** — נֹא אָמוֹן, "No (the city) of Amon": Thebes in Upper Egypt, taken by Assyria in 663 BC.
