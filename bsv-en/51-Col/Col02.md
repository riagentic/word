# Colossians 2

[1] For I want you to know how great a struggle I have for you and those in ⟦laodicea|Laodikeia|Laodicea⟧ and as many as have not seen my face in the flesh,

[2] that their hearts may be encouraged, being knit together in love and unto all riches of the full assurance of understanding, unto full knowledge of the mystery of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[3] in whom are all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge, hidden.

[4] This I say that no one may mislead you with persuasive speech.

[5] For even if I am absent in the flesh, yet in the spirit I am with you, rejoicing and seeing your order and the firmness of your faith toward ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[6] As therefore you received ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the Lord, walk in him,

[7] having been rooted and being built up in him and being confirmed in the faith, just as you were taught, abounding in thanksgiving.

[8] See that there will be no one who carries you off as plunder through the philosophy and empty deceit, according to the tradition of men, according to the elements of the world and not according to ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧;

[9] **for in him dwells all the fullness of the deity bodily**,

[10] and you are in him filled, who is the head of all principality and authority,

[11] in whom also you were circumcised with a circumcision not made with hands, in the putting off of the body of the flesh, in the circumcision of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[12] having been buried with him in the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧, in which also you were raised with him through the faith of the working of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who raised him from the dead.

[13] And you, being dead in the trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, he made you alive together with him, having forgiven us all the trespasses,

[14] **having wiped out the handwriting against us, with the decrees, which was opposed to us; and he has taken it out of the midst, having nailed it to the cross**;

[15] having put off the principalities and the authorities, he made a show of them openly, having triumphed over them in him.

[16] Therefore let no one judge you in eating and in drinking, or in the matter of a feast or of a new moon or of ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧,

[17] which are a shadow of the things to come, but the body is of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[18] Let no one arbitrate against you, delighting in humility and worship of the angels, entering into things which he has seen, vainly puffed up by the mind of his flesh,

[19] and not holding the head, from whom all the body, being supplied and knit together through the joints and bonds, grows the growth of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] If you died with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ from the elements of the world, why, as living in the world, do you submit to decrees —

[21] "Do not handle, nor taste, nor touch"

[22] (which are all unto corruption in the using up) — according to the precepts and doctrines of men?

[23] Which things have indeed a repute of wisdom in self-willed worship and humility and unsparing of the body, not in any honor, unto the satisfying of the flesh.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 12, 19) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"baptism" → "immersion"** (v12) — Greek ἐν τῷ βαπτισμῷ *baptismō* (Treg NA28), "the dipping, immersing"; βαπτίσματι (WH RP). "In which" (ἐν ᾧ) can also be read "in whom".
- **"what great conflict" → "how great a struggle"** (v1) — Greek ἀγῶνα, the root of "struggling" in 1:29; 4:12.
- **"my face in the flesh" kept** (v1) — Greek τὸ πρόσωπόν μου ἐν σαρκί.
- **"comforted" → "encouraged"** (v2) — Greek παρακληθῶσιν; also 4:8.
- **"acknowledgement" → "full knowledge"** (v2) — Greek ἐπίγνωσιν, as in 1:9–10; "knowledge" in v3 is γνῶσις.
- **"of the mystery of God, and of the Father, and of Christ" → "of the mystery of *Elohim*, of the *Mashiach*"** (v2) — NA28 τοῦ μυστηρίου τοῦ θεοῦ, Χριστοῦ. The genitive "of *Mashiach*" stands beside "of *Elohim*" without a link: it can explain "the mystery" (the mystery, that is, *Mashiach*) or belong to "*Elohim*". The verse adds no "which is".
- **"in whom are hid" → "in whom are …, hidden"** (v3) — Greek ἀπόκρυφοι stands last. "In whom" (ἐν ᾧ) can also be "in which" (the mystery).
- **"beguile you with enticing words" → "mislead you with persuasive speech"** (v4) — Greek παραλογίζηται ἐν πιθανολογίᾳ.
- **"joying and beholding" → "rejoicing and seeing"** (v5) — Greek χαίρων καὶ βλέπων, two participles.
- **"your faith in Christ" → "your faith toward the *Mashiach*"** (v5) — Greek εἰς Χριστόν.
- **"rooted and built up" → "having been rooted and being built up"** (v7) — Greek perfect ἐρριζωμένοι, then present ἐποικοδομούμενοι, βεβαιούμενοι.
- **"beware lest any man spoil you" → "see that there will be no one who carries you off as plunder"** (v8) — Greek Βλέπετε μή τις ὑμᾶς ἔσται ὁ συλαγωγῶν.
- **"rudiments" → "elements"** (v8, 20) — Greek στοιχεῖα, the basic components or first principles; whether things, teachings or powers are meant is open, and the verse does not add "spirits".
- **"Godhead" → "deity"** (v9) — Greek θεότης, the being-God.
- **"ye are complete in him" → "you are in him filled"** (v10) — Greek πεπληρωμένοι, the root of "fullness" (πλήρωμα) in v9.
- **"principality and power" → "principality and authority"** (v10, 15) — Greek ἀρχή, ἐξουσία, as in 1:16.
- **"the body of the sins of the flesh" → "the body of the flesh"** (v11) — "of the sins" is not in the critical text.
- **"through the faith of the operation of God" → "through the faith of the working of *Elohim*"** (v12) — Greek διὰ τῆς πίστεως τῆς ἐνεργείας τοῦ θεοῦ, a genitive: faith in that working, or faith that the working produces. ἐνέργεια as in 1:29; the Greek has no "powerful".
- **"hath he quickened" → "he made you alive"** (v13) — Greek συνεζωοποίησεν ὑμᾶς; the subject is not named (no "*Elohim*" in the Greek). "Forgiven us" is NA28 ἡμῖν; KJV "you".
- **"blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us" → "having wiped out the handwriting against us, with the decrees"** (v14) — Greek ἐξαλείψας τὸ καθʼ ἡμῶν χειρόγραφον τοῖς δόγμασιν. χειρόγραφον is a hand-written document (a note of debt); the dative τοῖς δόγμασιν ("with / in / by the decrees") is loosely attached. The Greek has no "debt" and no "legal demands".
- **"took it out of the way" → "has taken it out of the midst"** (v14) — Greek ἦρκεν ἐκ τοῦ μέσου, perfect.
- **"having spoiled" → "having put off"** (v15) — Greek ἀπεκδυσάμενος, middle voice, the verb of 3:9 and the noun of v11 ("putting off"): he stripped them off himself, or he stripped (disarmed) them.
- **"openly" kept** (v15) — Greek ἐν παρρησίᾳ; elsewhere "boldness", here the public, unconcealed manner of the display.
- **"in it" → "in him"** (v15) — Greek ἐν αὐτῷ: "in him" or "in it" (the cross).
- **"in meat, or in drink" → "in eating and in drinking"** (v16) — Greek βρῶσις, πόσις, the acts. "And" is καί (WH NA28); the printed SBLGNT has ἤ, "or" (Treg RP).
- **"of the sabbath days" → "of *Shabbatot*"** (v16) — Greek σαββάτων, plural; the plural form is also used for a single Sabbath.
- **"the body is of Christ" kept** (v17) — Greek τὸ δὲ σῶμα τοῦ Χριστοῦ, without a verb; σῶμα is "body" as everywhere in the letter.
- **"beguile you of your reward" → "arbitrate against you"** (v18) — Greek καταβραβευέτω, to decide against someone as umpire; the simple verb is in 3:15.
- **"in a voluntary humility" → "delighting in humility"** (v18) — Greek θέλων ἐν ταπεινοφροσύνῃ; θέλων ἐν is "delighting in" or "wishing (to do so) by". ταπεινοφροσύνη is "humility" as in 3:12.
- **"worshipping of angels" → "worship of the angels"** (v18) — Greek θρησκείᾳ τῶν ἀγγέλων: worship given to the angels, or the angels' worship.
- **"intruding into those things which he hath not seen" → "entering into things which he has seen"** (v18) — NA28 ἃ ἑόρακεν ἐμβατεύων, without "not". The meaning of ἐμβατεύων ("stepping in, entering upon") is disputed here.
- **"his fleshly mind" → "the mind of his flesh"** (v18) — Greek τοῦ νοὸς τῆς σαρκὸς αὐτοῦ.
- **"joints and bands" → "joints and bonds"** (v19) — Greek ἁφῶν καὶ συνδέσμων; σύνδεσμος is "bond" in 3:14.
- **"increaseth with the increase of God" → "grows the growth of *Elohim*"** (v19) — Greek αὔξει τὴν αὔξησιν τοῦ θεοῦ, a plain genitive.
- **"are ye subject to ordinances" → "do you submit to decrees"** (v20) — Greek δογματίζεσθε, the root of "decrees" (δόγμασιν) in v14. The critical text has no "wherefore".
- **"which all are to perish with the using" → "which are all unto corruption in the using up"** (v22) — Greek ἅ ἐστιν πάντα εἰς φθορὰν τῇ ἀποχρήσει; φθορά is decay, perishing.
- **"commandments and doctrines of men" → "precepts and doctrines of men"** (v22) — Greek ἐντάλματα καὶ διδασκαλίας; a different word from "commandments" (ἐντολάς) in 4:10.
- **"a shew of wisdom" → "a repute of wisdom"** (v23) — Greek λόγον … σοφίας, literally "a word of wisdom". λόγος is "word" everywhere else in the letter; here λόγον ἔχειν means "to have the name, the reputation of", and "a word of wisdom" would be read as a wise saying.
- **"will worship" → "self-willed worship"** (v23) — Greek ἐθελοθρησκία; θρησκεία "worship" as in v18.
- **"neglecting of the body" → "unsparing of the body"** (v23) — Greek ἀφειδίᾳ σώματος.
- **"not in any honour to the satisfying of the flesh" kept** (v23) — Greek οὐκ ἐν τιμῇ τινι πρὸς πλησμονὴν τῆς σαρκός. The clause is obscure: "of no value against the indulgence of the flesh", or "not of any honor, [serving only] to gratify the flesh". The verse keeps the Greek words and order.

### Notes
- **v13–15** — the subject of "made alive", "has taken", "made a show" is not named in the Greek.
