# Colossians 4

[1] Lords, grant to the slaves what is just and equal, knowing that you also have a Lord in heaven.

[2] Persevere in prayer, being awake in it in thanksgiving,

[3] praying at the same time also for us, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may open to us a door of the word, to speak the mystery of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, because of which also I have been bound,

[4] that I may manifest it, as I ought to speak.

[5] Walk in wisdom toward those outside, redeeming the time.

[6] Let your word be always in grace, **seasoned with salt**, to know how you ought to answer each one.

[7] All the things concerning me ⟦tychicus|Tychikos|Tychicus⟧ will make known to you, the beloved brother and faithful servant and fellow slave in the Lord,

[8] whom I have sent to you for this very thing, that you may know the things concerning us and that he may encourage your hearts,

[9] with ⟦onesimus|Onesimos|Onesimus⟧, the faithful and beloved brother, who is of you. They will make known to you all the things here.

[10] ⟦aristarchus|Aristarchos|Aristarchus⟧ my fellow captive greets you, and ⟦mark|Marcus|Mark⟧ the cousin of ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ (concerning whom you received commandments — if he comes to you, accept him),

[11] and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ who is called Justus, who are of the circumcision; these alone are fellow workers unto the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who became a solace to me.

[12] Epaphras greets you, who is of you, a slave of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, always struggling for you in the prayers, that you may stand perfect and fully assured in all the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[13] For I testify of him that he has much toil for you and those in ⟦laodicea|Laodikeia|Laodicea⟧ and those in Hierapolis.

[14] ⟦luke|Loukas|Luke⟧ the beloved physician greets you, and Demas.

[15] Greet the brothers in ⟦laodicea|Laodikeia|Laodicea⟧, and Nympha and the church at her house.

[16] And when the letter has been read among you, cause that it be read also in the church of the Laodiceans, and that you also read the one from ⟦laodicea|Laodikeia|Laodicea⟧.

[17] And say to ⟦archippus|Archippos|Archippus⟧, "See to the service which you received in the Lord, that you fulfill it."

[18] The greeting with my own hand, ⟦paul|of Paulus|of Paul⟧. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (vv3, 12) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3, 11, 12) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v11) — "Jesus who is called Justus" = a Jewish believer named *Yeshua*; the name is preserved in Hebrew/Aramaic form.
- **"Masters … servants … a Master" → "Lords … slaves … a Lord"** (v1) — Greek κύριοι, δούλοις, κύριον: one word for the human lords and for the Lord, as in 3:22–24.
- **"give … that which is just and equal" → "grant … what is just and equal"** (v1) — Greek τὸ δίκαιον καὶ τὴν ἰσότητα παρέχεσθε; ἰσότης is a noun, "equality".
- **"continue in prayer, and watch in the same with thanksgiving" → "persevere in prayer, being awake in it in thanksgiving"** (v2) — Greek προσκαρτερεῖτε, γρηγοροῦντες ἐν αὐτῇ ἐν εὐχαριστίᾳ.
- **"a door of utterance" → "a door of the word"** (v3) — Greek θύραν τοῦ λόγου, a genitive; λόγος is "word" throughout the letter.
- **"for which I am also in bonds" → "because of which also I have been bound"** (v3) — Greek διʼ ὃ καὶ δέδεμαι, perfect passive of "bind"; the Greek has no "prison".
- **"make it manifest" → "manifest it"** (v4) — Greek φανερώσω, as in 1:26; 3:4.
- **"them that are without" → "those outside"** (v5) — Greek τοὺς ἔξω.
- **"redeeming the time" kept** (v5) — Greek τὸν καιρὸν ἐξαγοραζόμενοι, "buying up the (right) time".
- **"let your speech be alway with grace" → "let your word be always in grace"** (v6) — Greek ὁ λόγος ὑμῶν πάντοτε ἐν χάριτι, without a verb; "be" is supplied.
- **"that ye may know" → "to know"** (v6) — Greek infinitive εἰδέναι.
- **"all my state" → "all the things concerning me"** (v7) — Greek τὰ κατʼ ἐμὲ πάντα.
- **"minister and fellowservant" → "servant and fellow slave"** (v7) — Greek διάκονος, σύνδουλος, as in 1:7.
- **"that he might know your estate" → "that you may know the things concerning us"** (v8) — NA28 ἵνα γνῶτε τὰ περὶ ἡμῶν.
- **"I have sent" kept** (v8) — Greek ἔπεμψα, the aorist of a letter writer: at the time of reading, the sending is past.
- **"who is one of you" → "who is of you"** (v9, 12) — Greek ἐξ ὑμῶν.
- **"all things which are done here" → "all the things here"** (v9) — Greek πάντα … τὰ ὧδε.
- **"fellowprisoner" → "fellow captive"** (v10) — Greek συναιχμάλωτος, a captive of war, as in Phlm 23.
- **"sister's son" → "cousin"** (v10) — Greek ἀνεψιός.
- **"commandments" kept; "receive him" → "accept him"** (v10) — Greek ἐντολάς; the Greek does not say from whom. "Accept" is δέξασθε; "received" in this verse (ἐλάβετε) and in v17 (παρέλαβες) are other verbs.
- **"These only are my fellowworkers" → "these alone are fellow workers"** (v11) — Greek οἱ ὄντες ἐκ περιτομῆς, οὗτοι μόνοι συνεργοί; the Greek has no "my". It can also be divided: "of those who are of the circumcision, these alone are fellow workers".
- **"a comfort" → "a solace"** (v11) — Greek παρηγορία, only here in the New Testament; not the word rendered "comfort" (παραμυθέομαι).
- **"a servant of Christ" → "a slave of *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v12) — Greek δοῦλος Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ (WH Treg NA28); the printed SBLGNT has Χριστοῦ only (RP).
- **"labouring fervently" → "struggling"** (v12) — Greek ἀγωνιζόμενος, as in 1:29.
- **"perfect and complete" → "perfect and fully assured"** (v12) — Greek τέλειοι (as 1:28) καὶ πεπληροφορημένοι (NA28), the root of "full assurance" in 2:2; it can also mean "brought to fullness". KJV follows the reading πεπληρωμένοι.
- **"I bear him record" → "I testify of him"; "a great zeal" → "much toil"** (v13) — Greek μαρτυρῶ … αὐτῷ; πολὺν πόνον, hard and painful work (not κόπος, "labor", 1:29).
- **"Nymphas, and the church which is in his house" → "Nympha and the church at her house"** (v15) — NA28 Νύμφαν … αὐτῆς, a woman; other manuscripts read "his" or "their".
- **"cause that it be read" kept** (v16) — Greek ποιήσατε ἵνα … ἀναγνωσθῇ.
- **"take heed to the ministry" → "see to the service"** (v17) — Greek Βλέπε τὴν διακονίαν, the root of διάκονος "servant".
- **"The salutation by the hand of me Paul" → "The greeting with my own hand, of *Paulus*"** (v18) — Greek Ὁ ἀσπασμὸς τῇ ἐμῇ χειρὶ Παύλου, without a verb.
- **"bonds" → "chains"** (v18) — Greek δεσμῶν, as in Phlm 10, 13; the root of "bound" in v3.
- **"Amen" omitted** (v18) — not in the critical text.
