## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins clauses and names with וַ / וְ "and"; it is kept, also in the lists.
- **"while he yet kept himself close because of Saul" → "while he was still kept back from the face of Saul"** (v1) — Hebrew עוֹד עָצוּר מִפְּנֵי שָׁאוּל: עָצוּר is passive, "restrained, shut in"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (also "because of"). **"the mighty men, helpers of the war" → "the mighty ones, helpers of the war"** — Hebrew בַּגִּבּוֹרִים עֹזְרֵי הַמִּלְחָמָה; "who helped him" (the earlier text) supplied "him".
- **"could use both the right hand and the left in hurling stones and shooting arrows out of a bow" → "using the right hand and using the left hand with stones and with arrows with the bow"** (v2) — Hebrew מַיְמִינִים וּמַשְׂמִאלִים בָּאֲבָנִים וּבַחִצִּים בַּקָּשֶׁת: two participles made from "right" and "left"; "sling" and "shoot" are not written. **"Saul's brethren" → "the brothers of Saul"** (v2, 29) — Hebrew אֲחֵי, kinsmen; the word "brother" is kept.
- **"The chief" → "The head"** (v3, 9, 14, 18, 20, 23, 32) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ, one word throughout. **"Jeziel"** (v3) — the qere; the written text has "Jezuel". **"a mighty man among the thirty" → "a mighty one among the thirty"** (v4) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר.
- **"the Haruphite"** (v5) — the qere; the written text has "the Hariphite". **"the Gedor"** (v7) — the Hebrew has the article, מִן־הַגְּדוֹר.
- **"into the hold to the wilderness men of might, and men of war fit for the battle, that could handle shield and buckler" → "to the stronghold in the wilderness, mighty ones of ability, men of the host for the war, arraying shield and spear"** (v8) — Hebrew לַמְצַד מִדְבָּרָה גִּבֹּרֵי הַחַיִל אַנְשֵׁי צָבָא לַמִּלְחָמָה עֹרְכֵי צִנָּה וָרֹמַח: חַיִל "ability, force"; צָבָא "host"; עָרַךְ "arrange, set in array"; צִנָּה is the large shield, רֹמַח a spear. "Trained" (the earlier text) is not written.
- **"whose faces were like the faces of lions, and were as swift as the roes upon the mountains" → "their faces were the faces of a lion, and they were like gazelles upon the mountains for haste"** (v8) — Hebrew וּפְנֵי אַרְיֵה פְּנֵיהֶם, no "like", "lion" singular; לְמַהֵר "to hasten".
- **"Ezer the first" → "Ezer the head"** (v9) — Hebrew הָרֹאשׁ.
- **"These were of the sons of Gad, captains of the host: one of the least was over an hundred, and the greatest over a thousand" → "These were from the sons of Gad, heads of the host; the least was one for a hundred, and the greatest for a thousand"** (v14) — Hebrew אֶחָד לְמֵאָה הַקָּטָן וְהַגָּדוֹל לְאָלֶף: either "a match for a hundred … for a thousand" or "over a hundred … over a thousand"; the Hebrew לְ is kept open. "These Gadites" (the earlier text) is "from the sons of Gad".
- **"went over Jordan … when it had overflown all his banks" → "passed over the Jordan … and it was filling over all its banks"** (v15) — Hebrew עָבְרוּ; וְהוּא מְמַלֵּא עַל־כָּל־גְּדוֹתָיו, a participle (the qere; the written form is another spelling). **"they put to flight all them of the valleys" → "they put to flight all the valleys"** — Hebrew וַיַּבְרִיחוּ אֶת־כָּל־הָעֲמָקִים: the valleys stand for those who dwelt in them.
- **"to the hold" → "as far as the stronghold"** (v16) — Hebrew עַד־לַמְצָד.
- **"David went out to meet them" → "David went out before them"** (v17) — Hebrew לִפְנֵיהֶם. **"answered and said"** — both verbs are in the Hebrew. **"If ye be come peaceably unto me to help me, mine heart shall be knit unto you" → "If you have come to me for shalom, to help me, I will have a heart toward you for union"** — Hebrew אִם־לְשָׁלוֹם; יִהְיֶה־לִּי עֲלֵיכֶם לֵבָב לְיָחַד, literally "there shall be to me upon you a heart for togetherness".
- **"to betray me to mine enemies, seeing there is no wrong in mine hands" → "to deceive me to my adversaries, when there is no violence in my palms"** (v17) — Hebrew לְרַמּוֹתַנִי לְצָרַי: רִמָּה "deceive" (to trick him for the gain of his adversaries); צַר "adversary"; חָמָס "violence"; כַּף "palm". **"look thereon, and rebuke it" → "see and reprove"** — Hebrew יֵרֶא … וְיוֹכַח, a wish; no object is written.
- **"Then the spirit came upon Amasai" → "And a spirit clothed Amasai"** (v18) — Hebrew וְרוּחַ לָבְשָׁה אֶת־עֲמָשַׂי: the verb is "put on, clothe" (Judg 6:34); רוּחַ has no article and no "of Yahweh" or "of Elohim": whose spirit is not said. **"chief of the captains" → "head of the officers"** — the qere הַשָּׁלִישִׁים; the written text has "the thirty".
- **"Thine are we, David, and on thy side, thou son of Jesse: peace, peace be unto thee, and peace be to thine helpers; for thy God helpeth thee" → "Yours, David, and with you, son of Jesse! Shalom, shalom to you, and shalom to your helper, for your Elohim has helped you"** (v18) — Hebrew לְךָ דָוִיד וְעִמְּךָ בֶן־יִשַׁי, no "are we"; לְעֹזְרֶךָ is singular as pointed; עֲזָרְךָ is a perfect.
- **"made them captains of the band" → "put them among the heads of the raiding band"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנֵם בְּרָאשֵׁי הַגְּדוּד; גְּדוּד "raiding band" (v21; 1 Sam 30:8).
- **"there fell some of Manasseh to David" → "some from Manasseh fell away to David"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew נָפְלוּ עַל, of going over to another side (1 Sam 29:3). **"but they helped them not"** kept — "they" are David and his men, "them" the Philistines. **"upon advisement" → "by counsel"** — Hebrew בְּעֵצָה. **"He will fall to his master Saul to the jeopardy of our heads" → "With our heads he will fall away to his lord Saul"** — Hebrew בְּרָאשֵׁינוּ, "with / at the price of our heads" (1 Sam 29:4); "jeopardy" is not written. סַרְנֵי "lords" is the Philistines' own word for their five rulers; אֲדֹנָיו "his lord" is another word.
- **"captains of the thousands" → "heads of the thousands"** (v20) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאֲלָפִים.
- **"they helped David against the band of the rovers" → "they helped with David against the raiding band"** (v21) — Hebrew עָזְרוּ עִם־דָּוִיד עַל־הַגְּדוּד: עַל may be "against" (the raiders of 1 Sam 30) or "over" (David's own band). **"mighty men of valour" → "mighty ones of ability"** (v21, 25, 28, 30) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרֵי חַיִל. **"captains in the host" → "commanders in the host"** — Hebrew שָׂרִים (also v28, 34), another word than "heads".
- **"a great host, like the host of God" → "a great camp, like a camp of Elohim"** (v22) — Hebrew מַחֲנֶה "camp"; the phrase may also say "a very great camp" (Elohim as a superlative).
- **"the numbers of the bands that were ready armed to the war" → "the numbers of the heads of those armed for the host"** (v23) — Hebrew מִסְפְּרֵי רָאשֵׁי הֶחָלוּץ לַצָּבָא: רָאשֵׁי is "heads"; whether chiefs, companies or totals are meant is not certain. **"to turn the kingdom of Saul to him, according to the word of the LORD" → "to turn the kingdom of Saul about to him, according to the mouth of Yahweh"** — Hebrew לְהָסֵב (as 10:14); כְּפִי יְהוָה.
- **"The children of" → "the sons of"** (v16, 24–32) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"ready armed to the war" → "armed for the host"** (v24) — Hebrew חֲלוּצֵי צָבָא.
- **"the children of Levi"** → "the sons of Levi" (v26) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הַלֵּוִי, with the article: "the sons of the Levite".
- **"Jehoiada was the leader of the Aaronites" → "Jehoiada, the leader for Aaron"** (v27) — Hebrew הַנָּגִיד לְאַהֲרֹן; the Masoretic Text has the name, not a gentilic. The verse has no verb.
- **"twenty and two captains"** (v28) — Hebrew וּבֵית־אָבִיו שָׂרִים עֶשְׂרִים וּשְׁנָיִם, "and his father's house, commanders twenty-two"; "with" (the earlier text) is not written.
- **"hitherto the greatest part of them had kept the ward of the house of Saul" → "until then the greater part of them were keeping the charge of the house of Saul"** (v29) — Hebrew וְעַד־הֵנָּה מַרְבִּיתָם שֹׁמְרִים מִשְׁמֶרֶת, literally "and as far as here"; "allegiance" (the earlier text) is an interpretation.
- **"famous throughout the house of their fathers" → "men of names, by their fathers' houses"** (v30) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי שֵׁמוֹת לְבֵית אֲבוֹתָם.
- **"which were expressed by name" → "who were designated by names"** (v31) — Hebrew נִקְּבוּ בְּשֵׁמוֹת (Num 1:17). "Tribe" is here מַטֶּה, in v37 שֵׁבֶט: two Hebrew words, one English.
- **"men that had understanding of the times" → "who knew understanding for the times"** (v32) — Hebrew יוֹדְעֵי בִינָה לַעִתִּים. **"all their brethren were at their commandment" → "all their brothers were at their mouth"** — Hebrew עַל־פִּיהֶם, at their word.
- **"such as went forth to battle, expert in war, with all instruments of war" → "those who go out to the host, drawing up for battle with all weapons of war"** (v33, 35, 36) — Hebrew יוֹצְאֵי צָבָא עֹרְכֵי מִלְחָמָה. **"which could keep rank: they were not of double heart" → "and to keep rank without a heart and a heart"** — Hebrew וְלַעֲדֹר בְּלֹא־לֵב וָלֵב: the verb עָדַר stands in this sense only here and in v38 and is uncertain ("keep rank", "set in order"; some read "to help", as the old versions are reported to do, from memory); "a heart and a heart" is a double heart (Ps 12:2).
- **"on the other side of Jordan" → "from across the Jordan"** (v37) — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן. **"with all manner of instruments of war for the battle" → "with all the weapons of the host of war"** — Hebrew בְּכֹל כְּלֵי צְבָא מִלְחָמָה.
- **"that could keep rank" → "keeping rank in the battle line"** (v38) — Hebrew עֹדְרֵי מַעֲרָכָה (the verb of v33; uncertain). **"perfect heart" → "whole heart"** — Hebrew לֵבָב שָׁלֵם.
- **"their brethren had prepared for them"** (v39) — Hebrew אֲחֵיהֶם "their brothers" (the earlier text: "kinsmen").
- **"brought bread on asses … meat, meal, cakes of figs, and bunches of raisins" → "were bringing bread on donkeys … food: meal, fig cakes and raisin cakes"** (v40) — Hebrew מְבִיאִים לֶחֶם, a participle; לֶחֶם "bread"; מַאֲכָל "food"; קֶמַח "meal" (kept); "abundant provisions" (the earlier text) is not written here, "in abundance" (לָרֹב) stands at the end of the list. **"oxen, and sheep" → "cattle and flock"** — Hebrew בָּקָר "herd, cattle", the same word as the pack animals before; צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats). **"joy" → "gladness"** — Hebrew שִׂמְחָה.
- **"Antothite" → "Anathothite"** (v3). **"roes" → "gazelles"** (v8) — Hebrew צְבָאיִם.
- **Numbers** — in words, in the ordinary English order.

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew divides our v4 into two verses (12:4–5), so that our v5–40 are 12:6–41 in the Hebrew.
- **Chronicler's emphasis** — progressive all-Israel support for David, from fugitive days (Ziklag) through stronghold to Hebron.
- **Benjaminite support** — unusual: Saul's own tribe falls away to David. Chronicler signals theological legitimation.
- **Gadites' heroism** — passing over the flooded Jordan in first month = high-water season. Feats of reconnaissance and valor.
- **Amasai's oracle** (v18) — poetic; "your *Elohim* has helped you." Spirit-clothed loyalty pledge.
- **Issachar "knew understanding for the times"** (v32) — classic phrase; contextual political wisdom. 200 tribal heads as counselors.
- **"Without a heart and a heart"** (v33) — Hebrew *be-lo-lev-va-lev*; unwavering commitment.
- **Total count** — ~340,000 men. Symbolic magnitude, showing comprehensive all-Israel backing.
- **Post-coronation feast** — three-day celebration. Unity theme.
