## Changes

- **"And" · lists** (throughout) — the lists have no verb; each "and" stands where the Hebrew has וְ. "Was" / "were" is supplied with the numbers and in v4, 16, 19, 24.
- **"Now the sons of Issachar were" → "And for the sons of Issachar"** (v1) — Hebrew וְלִבְנֵי, with לְ. **"Jashub"** — the written text (ketiv) is ישיב "Jashib"; the reading tradition (qere) יָשׁוּב is translated.
- **"heads of their father's house … valiant men of might in their generations" → "heads of their fathers' houses … mighty ones of ability, by their generations"** (v2) — Hebrew רָאשִׁים לְבֵית־אֲבוֹתָם; גִּבּוֹרֵי חַיִל: גִּבּוֹר "mighty one", חַיִל "ability" (also "force, wealth"); not "warriors". In v5, 7, 11, 40 the second word is plural, חֲיָלִים. תּוֹלְדוֹת "generations" (v2, 4, 9). The numbers are written in ordinary English order.
- **"five: all of them chief men" → "five; all of them heads"** (v3) — Hebrew רָאשִׁים. Four names follow "the sons of Izrahiah"; the five counts Izrahiah with them. "The sons of Uzzi" is plural before one name; so also v10, 17.
- **"with them … were bands of soldiers for war, six and thirty thousand men: for they had many wives and sons" → "with them … were bands of the host of war, thirty-six thousand; for they multiplied wives and sons"** (v4) — Hebrew וַעֲלֵיהֶם, literally "and upon them"; גְּדוּדֵי צְבָא מִלְחָמָה: גְּדוּד is the "raiding band" of 1 Sam 30:8, here of the troops of a host; "men" is not written; הִרְבּוּ "they made many".
- **"reckoned in all by their genealogies" → "was their enrollment by genealogy, of all"** (v5, 7, 9, 40) — Hebrew הִתְיַחְשָׂם, an infinitive used as a noun: their being entered in the register of descent. לַכֹּל "for all".
- **"The sons of Benjamin" → "Benjamin"** (v6) — the Hebrew has only the name; "the sons of" is not written.
- **"Jeush"** (v10) — ketiv יעיש "Jeish"; the qere is translated.
- **"by the heads of their fathers … fit to go out for war and battle" → "by the heads of the fathers … who go out to the host for the war"** (v11) — Hebrew לְרָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת; יֹצְאֵי צָבָא לַמִּלְחָמָה, as Num 1:3; "fit" is not written.
- **"Shuppim also, and Huppim, the children of Ir, and Hushim, the sons of Aher" → "And Shuppim and Huppim, the sons of Ir; Hushim, the sons of Aher"** (v12) — no verb in the Hebrew. אַחֵר is also the common word "another" ("the sons of another").
- **"Jahziel"** (v13) — written here יַחֲצִיאֵל. "The sons of Bilhah": Hebrew בְּנֵי, not "descendants".
- **"Ashriel, whom she bare: (but his concubine the Aramitess bare Machir the father of Gilead" → "Asriel, whom she bore; his Aramean concubine bore Machir the father of Gilead"** (v14) — the Hebrew is אַשְׂרִיאֵל אֲשֶׁר יָלָדָה פִּילַגְשׁוֹ הָאֲרַמִּיָּה יָלְדָה אֶת־מָכִיר; the Masoretic accents end the first half after "whom she bore", and who "she" is, is not said. Without the accents the words can be read "Asriel, whom his Aramean concubine bore; she bore Machir" (so the verse read before).
- **"took to wife the sister of Huppim and Shuppim, whose sister's name was Maachah" → "took a wife for Huppim and for Shuppim, and the name of his sister was Maacah"** (v15) — Hebrew לָקַח אִשָּׁה לְחֻפִּים וּלְשֻׁפִּים וְשֵׁם אֲחֹתוֹ: "his sister", singular suffix (the verse had "their sister"). "The second" (הַשֵּׁנִי) has no first named before it. "Zelophehad had daughters": "only" is not written. The verse is hard as it stands and is translated word for word.
- **"she called his name"** (v16), **"he called his name"** (v23) — Hebrew קָרָא שֵׁם.
- **"Hammoleketh"** (v18) — Hebrew הַמֹּלֶכֶת, with the article: "the Molecheth", or "she who reigns".
- **"whom the men of Gath that were born in that land slew, because they came down to take away their cattle" → "And the men of Gath who were born in the land killed them, for they came down to take their livestock"** (v21) — Hebrew וַהֲרָגוּם, a new clause; מִקְנֶה "livestock". Who came down, and whose livestock, is not said.
- **"because it went evil with his house" → "for in evil it was in his house"** (v23) — Hebrew כִּי בְרָעָה הָיְתָה בְּבֵיתוֹ; the name בְּרִיעָה *Beri'ah* sounds like בְּרָעָה *be-ra'ah* "in evil". The Hebrew words were written inside the verse and are taken out. "Went in to his wife" is the Hebrew בּוֹא אֶל.
- **"who built Beth-horon the nether, and the upper" → "and she built Beth-horon, the lower and the upper"** (v24) — Hebrew וַתִּבֶן.
- **"And Rephah was his son, also Resheph" → "And Rephah his son, and Resheph"** (v25) — no verb; Resheph has no "his son".
- **"Non"** (v27) — written here נוֹן; elsewhere Nun.
- **"their possessions and habitations were, Beth-el and the towns thereof … Gaza and the towns thereof" → "their possession and their dwelling places: Bethel and its daughters … as far as Ayyah and its daughters"** (v28) — Hebrew אֲחֻזָּה, singular; בְּנֹתֶיהָ "its daughters", the settlements that belong to a city (Num 21:25). עַיָּה is Ayyah, not Gaza (עַזָּה). "East" and "west" are מִזְרָח and מַעֲרָב.
- **"by the borders of the children of Manasseh" → "alongside the sons of Manasseh"** (v29) — Hebrew עַל־יְדֵי, literally "on the hands of"; no word "borders".
- **"Malchiel, who is the father of Birzavith" → "Malchiel; he is the father of Birzayit"** (v31) — ketiv ברזות "Birzoth"; qere בִּרְזָיִת, translated.
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v32) — Hebrew הוֹלִיד.
- **"Ahi, and Rohgah, Jehubbah, and Aram"** (v34) — ketiv ורוהגה and יחבה ("Jehubbah"); the qere reads וְרָהְגָּה "and Rohgah" and וְחֻבָּה "and Hubbah". The name stands in its written form, because the name token has no form for the qere; the reading tradition is "and Hubbah". אֲחִי "Ahi" is also the word "the brother of". "Shemer" is the Shomer of v32, pointed otherwise.
- **"the sons of his brother Helem" → "the son of Helem his brother"** (v35) — Hebrew וּבֶן, singular, before four names.
- **"choice and mighty men of valour, chief of the princes. And the number throughout the genealogy of them that were apt to the war and to battle" → "chosen ones, mighty ones of ability, heads of the chieftains; and their enrollment by genealogy, in the host, in the war: their number was"** (v40) — Hebrew בְּרוּרִים; רָאשֵׁי הַנְּשִׂיאִים: נָשִׂיא "chieftain"; בַּצָּבָא בַּמִּלְחָמָה, two nouns side by side; "apt" is not written.

### Notes
- **Missing tribes** — Dan and Zebulun are absent from Chronicler's tribal catalog. Dan's apostasy (Judg 18, Jeroboam's cult) may explain exclusion; Zebulun's omission unclear.
- **Issachar's 87,000** — largest tribal count in this chapter.
- **Benjamin partial** — fuller genealogy in ch 8. Chronicler gives preview here.
- **Ephraim's tragic incident** (v21-24) — a clash with the men of Gath over livestock; sons of Ephraim killed. Sheerah builds the two Beth-horons — rare mention of a woman building cities.
- **Joshua's lineage** — through Ephraim. Chronicler traces from Ephraim to Joshua son of Non (v20-27). Note the two Tahaths and two Shuthelahs.
- **Manasseh with Huppim/Shuppim** (v15) — textually complex; some emend to "Machir took a wife from Huppim and Shuppim's house."
- **Zelophehad's daughters** — well-known inheritance case (Num 27, 36).
