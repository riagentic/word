## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; with the article in v1, 6, 42 (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"they offered" → "they brought near"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּקְרִיבוּ, the verb "bring near"; 2 Sam 6:17 has another verb ("David offered up") and "in its place", which Chronicles does not have. In v2, 40 the verb is הֶעֱלָה, "offer up" (lit. "make go up", one root with עֹלָה "burnt offering").
- **"to every one of Israel, both man and woman" → "to every man of Israel, from man to woman"** (v3) — Hebrew לְכָל־אִישׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל מֵאִישׁ וְעַד־אִשָּׁה. 2 Sam 6:19 has "to all the people, to all the multitude of Israel" and "one" with each of the three gifts.
- **"a loaf of bread" → "a round of bread"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּכַּר־לֶחֶם; 2 Sam 6:19 has חַלַּת לֶחֶם "cake of bread".
- **"a good piece of flesh" → "a portion"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶשְׁפָּר, found only here and in 2 Sam 6:19; its meaning is not known. "of meat" is not written.
- **"flagon of wine" → "raisin cake"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲשִׁישָׁה; a cake of pressed raisins, not a vessel of wine.
- **"he appointed … to minister" → "he placed … as ministers"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן … מְשָׁרְתִים: the verb is "give", the second word a participle, "ones ministering".
- **"to record" → "to bring to remembrance"** (v4) — Hebrew לְהַזְכִּיר, lit. "to cause to be remembered" (elsewhere "mention"); no object is named. The same word stands in the headings of Ps 38 and 70.
- **"the chief, and next to him" → "the head, and his second"** (v5) — Hebrew הָרֹאשׁ וּמִשְׁנֵהוּ.
- **"with psalteries and with harps" → "with instruments of harps and with lyres"** (v5) — Hebrew בִּכְלֵי נְבָלִים וּבְכִנֹּרוֹת; the kinds of instrument are not certain. **"made a sound" → "sounding"** — Hebrew מַשְׁמִיעַ, lit. "making heard"; so also v42.
- **"David delivered first this psalm to thank" → "David then first appointed to give thanks"** (v7) — Hebrew אָז נָתַן דָּוִיד בָּרֹאשׁ לְהֹדוֹת לַיהוָה בְּיַד־אָסָף וְאֶחָיו, lit. "then David gave at the head to give thanks to Yahweh by the hand of Asaph and his brothers". No "psalm" or "song" is written and the verb has no object. בָּרֹאשׁ is "at the head": "first" in time, or "as the chief thing"; בְּיַד is "by the hand of" or, with "give", "into the hand of". The verse does not decide.
- **"talk ye" → "speak of"** (v9) — Hebrew שִׂיחוּ; the verb covers speaking and musing ("meditate").
- **"wondrous works", "marvellous works" → "wonderful deeds"** (v9, 12, 24) — one Hebrew word, נִפְלָאוֹת. **"wonders"** (v12) is מֹפְתִים.
- **"Glory ye"** (v10) — Hebrew הִתְהַלְלוּ, the reflexive of "praise". In v35 **"to boast"** is another verb, הִשְׁתַּבֵּחַ, the reflexive of "laud".
- **"Seek … seek"** (v11) — two Hebrew verbs, דָּרַשׁ and בִּקֵּשׁ; English has one word.
- **"O ye seed of Israel"** (v13) — so the Hebrew of Chronicles; Ps 105:6 has "Abraham". "his servant" is singular.
- **"Be ye mindful always" → "Remember forever"** (v15) — Hebrew זִכְרוּ, a command to many; Ps 105:8 has "he has remembered".
- **"which he made" → "which he cut"** (v16) — Hebrew כָּרַת, the usual verb for making a covenant.
- **"hath confirmed" → "made it stand"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמִידֶהָ. **"everlasting"** — lit. "a covenant of the age".
- **"the lot of your inheritance" → "the portion of your inheritance"** (v18) — Hebrew חֶבֶל, lit. "cord" (the measuring line). "To you" is singular, "your" plural.
- **"When ye were but few" → "when you were few men"** (v19) — Hebrew מְתֵי מִסְפָּר, lit. "men of number"; Chronicles has "you", Ps 105:12 "they". **"very few"** — Hebrew כִּמְעַט, lit. "as a little".
- **"he suffered no man to do them wrong" → "He did not let a man extort from them"** (v21) — Hebrew לְעָשְׁקָם; Chronicles has אִישׁ "man", Ps 105:14 אָדָם.
- **"do my prophets no harm" → "to my prophets do no evil"** (v22) — Hebrew אַל־תָּרֵעוּ.
- **"shew forth" → "bring the news of"** (v23) — Hebrew בַּשְּׂרוּ.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v24, 35).
- **"greatly to be praised" → "greatly praised"** (v25) — Hebrew מְהֻלָּל, a passive participle. **"to be feared" → "fearsome"** — Hebrew נוֹרָא.
- **"idols" → "worthless idols"** (v26) — Hebrew אֱלִילִים; the word says "worthless" and sounds like אֵל.
- **"Glory and honour" → "Majesty and splendor"** (v27) — Hebrew הוֹד וְהָדָר. **"gladness" → "joy"** — Hebrew חֶדְוָה; Ps 96:6 has "beauty" and "in his sanctuary".
- **"Give unto the LORD, ye kindreds" → "Give to Yahweh, clans"** (v28) — Hebrew מִשְׁפְּחוֹת.
- **"the glory due unto his name" → "the glory of his name"** (v29) — Hebrew כְּבוֹד שְׁמוֹ; "due" is not written. **"bring an offering" → "lift up an offering"** — Hebrew שְׂאוּ. **"worship" → "bow down to"** — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲווּ.
- **"beauty of holiness" → "splendor of holiness"** (v29) — Hebrew הַדְרַת־קֹדֶשׁ; the sense is not certain ("in holy adornment / attire" is the other way of taking it).
- **"Fear before him" → "Be in anguish before him"** (v30) — Hebrew חִילוּ, lit. "writhe" (as one in labor); commonly "tremble". "before" is מִלְּפָנָיו, lit. "from before him"; so also v33.
- **"it be not moved" → "it shall not be shaken"** (v30) — Hebrew בַּל־תִּמּוֹט.
- **"The LORD reigneth" → "Yahweh reigns"** (v31) — Hebrew מָלָךְ, a perfect: also "has become king".
- **"Let the sea roar" → "Let the sea thunder"** (v32) — Hebrew יִרְעַם. **"rejoice" → "be jubilant"** — Hebrew יַעֲלֹץ; "rejoice" (v10, 31) is שָׂמַח, "be glad" (v31) גִּיל.
- **"sing out" → "shout for joy"** (v33) — Hebrew יְרַנְּנוּ. **"he cometh"** — Hebrew בָא, "he comes" or "he has come".
- **"his mercy endureth for ever" → "his *chesed* is forever"** (v34, 41) — Hebrew כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ; no verb is written.
- **"Blessed be" → "Blessed is"** (v36) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה, with no verb. **"for ever and ever" → "from the age and to the age"** — Hebrew מִן־הָעוֹלָם וְעַד הָעֹלָם, with the article both times; commonly "from everlasting to everlasting".
- **"and praised the LORD"** (v36) — Hebrew וְהַלֵּל לַיהוָה; the form may also be read as a command, "and: Praise Yahweh" (Ps 106:48 has "Hallelujah").
- **"as every day's work required" → "for the matter of a day in its day"** (v37) — Hebrew לִדְבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ, as Exod 5:13.
- **"with their brethren" → "and their brothers"** (v38) — Hebrew וַאֲחֵיהֶם, "their", though one man is named. The second Obed-edom is "the son of Jedithun" (so spelled here; "Jeduthun" in v41, 42).
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v39) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן. No verb stands in v39: it depends on "he left" in v37.
- **"altar of the burnt offering"** (v40) — Hebrew מִזְבַּח הָעֹלָה. **"according to all" → "for all"** — Hebrew וּלְכָל. **"commanded Israel" → "commanded upon Israel"** — Hebrew צִוָּה עַל.
- **"the rest that were chosen, who were expressed by name" → "the rest of the select ones, who were designated by names"** (v41) — Hebrew הַבְּרוּרִים (another word than "chosen ones" in v13) and נִקְּבוּ בְּשֵׁמוֹת, as Num 1:17.
- **"musical instruments of God" → "the instruments of the song of Elohim"** (v42) — Hebrew כְּלֵי שִׁיר הָאֱלֹהִים. **"porters" → "for the gate"** — Hebrew לַשָּׁעַר.
- **"returned" → "turned"** (v43) — Hebrew וַיִּסֹּב; 2 Sam 6:20 has וַיָּשָׁב "returned".

### Notes
- **The song and the Psalms** — v8–22 stand also in Ps 105:1–15, v23–33 in Ps 96, v34–36 in Ps 106:1, 47–48. The verses here follow the Hebrew of Chronicles where the two differ (v13, 15, 19, 27, 29–33, 35–36).
- **Asaph the head** — many psalms (50, 73–83) bear his name.
- **Gibeon and Jerusalem** (v37–42) — the ark is in David's tent at Jerusalem; the dwelling of Yahweh and the altar of the burnt offering are at Gibeon.
- **The *Torah* of Yahweh** is named in v40.
