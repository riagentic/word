## Changes

- **"Now these were the sons of David" → "And these were the sons of David"** (v1) — וְאֵלֶּה. "And" at the head of a verse or list and "and" between names stand where the Hebrew has וְ and nowhere else (v6–8 every name with "and"; v18 "Jekamiah, Hoshama and Nedabiah"; v20 "Jushab-hesed" without "and"). Supplied: the colon after "the sons of X" and the copula.
- **"of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess … of Abigail the Carmelitess"** (v1) — לַאֲחִינֹעַם הַיִּזְרְעֵאלִית … לַאֲבִיגַיִל הַכַּרְמְלִית: the feminine gentilics, as 2 Sam 3:2–3 (the earlier "of Jezreel", "of Carmel" turned them into place phrases). לְ "of" names the mother. "a second" (שֵׁנִי) has no article in the Hebrew; the others have it.
- **"Daniel"** (v1) — דָּנִיֵּאל; 2 Sam 3:3 names the second son Chileab. The name of Chronicles stands.
- **"the third, Absalom"** (v2) — the Hebrew has לְאַבְשָׁלוֹם, with a לְ before the name that cannot be carried into English. "Adonijah" is the short form אֲדֹנִיָּה.
- **"and there he reigned" → "and he reigned there … and thirty-three years he reigned in Jerusalem"** (v4) — the word order of the Hebrew; the numbers are written in ordinary English order.
- **"Shimea … of Bath-shua the daughter of Ammiel"** (v5) — שִׁמְעָא (2 Sam 5:14 Shammua); בַּת־שׁוּעַ בַּת־עַמִּיאֵל as written (2 Sam 11:3 Bathsheba the daughter of Eliam). Nothing is taken over from Samuel.
- **"Elishama, and Eliphelet … Elishama, and Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine"** (v6–8) — the names stand twice in the Hebrew of Chronicles (v6 אֱלִיפָלֶט, v8 אֱלִיפֶלֶט; 2 Sam 5:15 has Elishua and no Nogah), and the count "nine" needs both. Kept as written.
- **"These were all the sons of David, beside the sons of the concubines, and Tamar their sister" → "All were sons of David, besides the sons of concubines; and Tamar was their sister"** (v9) — כֹּל בְּנֵי דָוִיד: no "these"; פִילַגְשִׁים has no article.
- **"And Solomon's son was Rehoboam, Abia his son" → "And the son of Solomon: Rehoboam; Abijah his son"** (v10) — no verb in the Hebrew.
- **Royal names** (v10–16) — as the Hebrew of this chapter spells them: Joram (יוֹרָם), Ahaziah (אֲחַזְיָהוּ), Joash (יוֹאָשׁ), Amaziah (אֲמַצְיָהוּ), Azariah (עֲזַרְיָה; the king called Uzziah elsewhere), Hezekiah (חִזְקִיָּהוּ), Josiah (יֹאשִׁיָּהוּ), Jeconiah (יְכָנְיָה; elsewhere Jehoiachin); Zedekiah is צִדְקִיָּהוּ in v15 and צִדְקִיָּה in v16.
- **"Jeconiah; Assir, Salathiel his son" → "Jeconiah the prisoner: Shealtiel his son"** (v17) — יְכָנְיָה אַסִּר: the accents join אַסִּר to the name Jeconiah, and אַסִּיר is "prisoner"; the word has no article, so it can also be read as a name, "Assir" (so the KJV). Not certain.
- **"And the sons of Zerubbabel" → "And the son of Zerubbabel"** (v19); **"And the sons of Hananiah" → "And the son of Hananiah"** (v21); **"And the sons of Neariah" → "And the son of Neariah"** (v23) — the Hebrew has the singular בֶּן each time, though more than one name follows; the singular is kept.
- **"and Shelomith their sister"** (v19) — no verb; v20 goes on with "and" and counts "five", which are the names of v20 alone.
- **"the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shechaniah"** (v21) — four times בְּנֵי with no names after it; how these belong to Hananiah is not said, and nothing is supplied. Obadiah is the short form עֹבַדְיָה.
- **"six"** (v22) — the Hebrew gives five names and the number six; left as it stands.
- **"Hodaiah" → "Hodaviah"** (v24) — qere הוֹדַוְיָהוּ; written הדיוהו.

### Notes
- **The house of David** — v1–9 the sons of David (2 Sam 3:2–5; 5:14–16), v10–16 the kings of Judah, v17–24 the line after the exile.
- **Six in Hebron, seven years and six months** (v1–4) — 2 Sam 3:2–5; 5:5.
- **Shallum** (v15) — the fourth son of Josiah; Jer 22:11 names Shallum the son of Josiah as king after his father.
- **Zerubbabel** (v19) — here a son of Pedaiah; Ezra 3:2 and Hag 1:1 call him the son of Shealtiel. Chronicles is left as written.
