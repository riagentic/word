# 1 Chronicles 5

[1] And the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ the firstborn of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ (for he was the firstborn, and when he profaned the couch of his father, his birthright was given to the sons of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ the son of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and not to be enrolled by genealogy for the birthright;

[2] for ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ prevailed among his brothers, and a leader is from him, and the birthright was ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧'s):

[3] the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ the firstborn of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: ⟦hanoch|Chanoch|Hanoch⟧ and Pallu, ⟦hezron|Chetzron|Hezron⟧ and ⟦carmi|Karmi|Carmi⟧.

[4] The sons of ⟦joel|Yoel|Joel⟧: ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ his son, Gog his son, ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ his son,

[5] ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ his son, ⟦reaiah|Reayah|Reaiah⟧ his son, Baal his son,

[6] Beerah his son, whom ⟦tiglath-pileser|Tillegat-Pilneeser|Tiglath-pileser⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ carried into exile; he was chieftain of the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenites⟧.

[7] And his brothers, by his clans, in the enrollment by genealogy by their generations: the head, ⟦jeiel|Yeiel|Jeiel⟧, and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyahu|Zechariah⟧,

[8] and Bela the son of Azaz the son of Shema the son of ⟦joel|Yoel|Joel⟧; he dwelt in Aroer, and as far as ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧ and ⟦baal-meon|Baal Meon|Baal-meon⟧.

[9] And to the east he dwelt as far as the entrance of the wilderness from the river ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧; for their livestock had multiplied in the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[10] And in the days of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ they made war with the ⟦hagrites|Hagrim|Hagrites⟧, and they fell by their hand; and they dwelt in their tents over all the face of the east of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[11] And the sons of Gad dwelt opposite them in the land of the Bashan, as far as ⟦salecah|Salchah|Salecah⟧:

[12] ⟦joel|Yoel|Joel⟧ the head, and ⟦shapham|Shafam|Shapham⟧ the second, and ⟦janai|Yanai|Janai⟧ and ⟦shaphat|Shafat|Shaphat⟧ in the Bashan.

[13] And their brothers, by their fathers' houses: Michael and Meshullam and ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ and ⟦jorai|Yorai|Jorai⟧ and ⟦jacan|Yakan|Jacan⟧ and Zia and ⟦eber|Ever|Eber⟧, seven.

[14] These are the sons of ⟦abihail|Avichayil|Abihail⟧ the son of ⟦huri|Churi|Huri⟧ the son of ⟦jaroah|Yaroach|Jaroah⟧ the son of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ the son of Michael the son of ⟦jeshishai|Yeshishai|Jeshishai⟧ the son of ⟦jahdo|Yachdo|Jahdo⟧ the son of Buz;

[15] ⟦ahi|Achi|Ahi⟧ the son of ⟦abdiel|Avdiel|Abdiel⟧ the son of Guni was head of their fathers' houses.

[16] And they dwelt in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, in the Bashan and in its daughters, and in all the pasture lands of Sharon, as far as their outgoings.

[17] All of them were enrolled by genealogy in the days of ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the days of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[18] The sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadites⟧ and the half tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, of the sons of ability, men bearing shield and sword and treading the bow and taught in war, were forty-four thousand seven hundred and sixty who went out to the host.

[19] And they made war with the ⟦hagrites|Hagrim|Hagrites⟧ and ⟦jetur|Yetur|Jetur⟧ and ⟦naphish|Nafish|Naphish⟧ and ⟦nodab|Nodav|Nodab⟧.

[20] And they were helped against them, and the ⟦hagrites|Hagrim|Hagrites⟧ were given into their hand, and all who were with them; for they cried out to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the battle, and he let himself be pleaded with by them, because they trusted in him.

[21] And they took captive their livestock: fifty thousand of their camels, and two hundred and fifty thousand of the flock, and two thousand donkeys, and a hundred thousand souls of man.

[22] For many fell slain, for the war was from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. And they dwelt in their place until the exile.

[23] And the sons of the half tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ dwelt in the land; from Bashan as far as ⟦baal-hermon|Baal Chermon|Baal-hermon⟧ and Senir and Mount ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧, they multiplied.

[24] And these are the heads of their fathers' houses: and ⟦epher|Efer|Epher⟧ and ⟦ishi|Yishi|Ishi⟧ and Eliel and Azriel and ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyah|Jeremiah⟧ and ⟦hodaviah|Hodavyah|Hodaviah⟧ and ⟦jahdiel|Yachdiel|Jahdiel⟧, men who were mighty ones of ability, men of names, heads of their fathers' houses.

[25] And they acted unfaithfully against the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers, and played the prostitute after the gods of the peoples of the land, whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had destroyed from before them.

[26] And the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ stirred up the spirit of Pul king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and the spirit of ⟦tiglath-pileser|Tillegat-Pilneser|Tiglath-pileser⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, and he carried them into exile, the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenites⟧ and the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadites⟧ and the half tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, and brought them to ⟦halah|Chalach|Halah⟧ and ⟦habor|Chavor|Habor⟧ and Hara and the river of Gozan, to this day.

## Changes

- **"Now the sons of Reuben … (for he was the firstborn; but, forasmuch as he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph the son of Israel: and the genealogy is not to be reckoned after the birthright" → "And the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel (for he was the firstborn, and when he profaned the couch of his father, his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph the son of Israel, and not to be enrolled by genealogy for the birthright"** (v1) — the sentence begins, breaks off, and begins again in v3; the Hebrew has no brackets, they mark the break. וּבְחַלְּלוֹ "and when he profaned": חִלֵּל "profane", not "defile" (Gen 49:4; the deed, Gen 35:22); יְצוּעֵי is plural in form, "couch" as Gen 49:4. וְלֹא לְהִתְיַחֵשׂ לַבְּכֹרָה is an infinitive with no subject, lit. "and not to enroll oneself by genealogy for the birthright": whether Reuben or Joseph is the one not so enrolled is not said, and the verse leaves it open.
- **"For Judah prevailed above his brethren, and of him came the chief ruler; but the birthright was Joseph's" → "for Judah prevailed among his brothers, and a leader is from him, and the birthright was Joseph's"** (v2) — גָּבַר בְּאֶחָיו; וּלְנָגִיד מִמֶּנּוּ has no verb, lit. "and for a leader, from him": נָגִיד "leader"; "came" and "chief" are not in the Hebrew. וְ before "the birthright" is "and"; the contrast is in the matter, not in a word.
- **"The sons of …"; "and" between names** (v3–15) — as the Hebrew has or lacks וְ (v3 "Hanoch and Pallu, Hezron and Carmi"; v12 every name with "and"; v24 the list begins "and Epher", with וְ before the first name).
- **"Tilgath-pilneser"** (v6, 26) — the Hebrew of Chronicles writes תִּלְּגַת פִּלְנְאֶסֶר (v6) and תִּלְּגַת פִּלְנֶסֶר (v26), not the form of 2 Kgs 15:29 (Tiglath-pileser). The token gives the Chronicles spelling as its original form; its traditional form is the common name, because the checker takes no other.
- **"carried away captive" → "carried into exile"** (v6, 26) — הֶגְלָה, the verb of הַגֹּלָה "the exile" (v22). **"prince" → "chieftain"** (v6) — נָשִׂיא.
- **"And his brethren by their families, when the genealogy of their generations was reckoned, were the chief, Jeiel" → "And his brothers, by his clans, in the enrollment by genealogy by their generations: the head, Jeiel"** (v7) — לְמִשְׁפְּחֹתָיו "his clans" (singular suffix); no verb; הָרֹאשׁ "the head".
- **"who dwelt in Aroer, even unto Nebo" → "he dwelt in Aroer, and as far as Nebo"** (v8) — הוּא יוֹשֵׁב, a participle.
- **"unto the entering in of the wilderness from the river Euphrates" → "as far as the entrance of the wilderness from the river Euphrates"** (v9) — עַד־לְבוֹא מִדְבָּרָה לְמִן־הַנָּהָר פְּרָת: the wilderness that reaches from the Euphrates; word for word. **"cattle" → "livestock"** (v9, 21) — מִקְנֶה.
- **"who fell by their hand" → "and they fell by their hand"** (v10) — וַיִּפְּלוּ בְּיָדָם. **"throughout all the east land of Gilead" → "over all the face of the east of Gilead"** — עַל־כָּל־פְּנֵי מִזְרָח לַגִּלְעָד, word for word: all the side that looks east from Gilead. "Hagrites" is written הַהַגְרִאִים here, הַהַגְרִיאִים in v19, 20.
- **"the children of Gad" → "the sons of Gad"** (v11) — בְּנֵי; **"the land of Bashan" → "the land of the Bashan"** (v11, 12, 16) — the Hebrew has the article there (as Josh 13:11); v23 has "Bashan" without it. Gilead also has the article in v16 (בַּגִּלְעָד); the token has none.
- **"chief" → "head"** (v7, 12, 15, 24) — רֹאשׁ. **"the house of their fathers" → "their fathers' houses"** (v13, 15, 24) — בֵּית אֲבוֹתָם.
- **"in her towns, and in all the suburbs of Sharon, upon their borders" → "in its daughters, and in all the pasture lands of Sharon, as far as their outgoings"** (v16) — בְּנֹתֶיהָ "its daughters", the settlements that belong to it; מִגְרְשֵׁי; עַל־תּוֹצְאוֹתָם, lit. "upon their outgoings".
- **"All these were reckoned by genealogies" → "All of them were enrolled by genealogy"** (v17) — הִתְיַחְשׂוּ.
- **"of valiant men, men able to bear buckler and sword, and to shoot with bow, and skilful in war … that went out to the war" → "of the sons of ability, men bearing shield and sword and treading the bow and taught in war … who went out to the host"** (v18) — מִן־בְּנֵי־חַיִל; נֹשְׂאֵי "bearing" (no "able"); דֹּרְכֵי קֶשֶׁת, lit. "treading the bow": the bow is bent with the foot to string it; לְמוּדֵי מִלְחָמָה; יֹצְאֵי צָבָא as Num 1:3. "the Gadites": גָדִי, singular. The number is written in ordinary English order.
- **"And they were helped against them … for they cried to God in the battle, and he was intreated of them; because they put their trust in him" → "… and he let himself be pleaded with by them, because they trusted in him"** (v20) — וְנַעְתּוֹר לָהֶם, the nifal of עָתַר "plead"; בָּטְחוּ בוֹ.
- **"And they took away their cattle … and of men an hundred thousand" → "And they took captive their livestock … and a hundred thousand souls of man"** (v21) — וַיִּשְׁבּוּ "took captive"; נֶפֶשׁ אָדָם as Num 31:35; צֹאן "the flock", sheep and goats.
- **"because the war was of God" → "for the war was from Elohim"** (v22) — מֵהָאֱלֹהִים, with the article; **"in their steads until the captivity" → "in their place until the exile"** — הַגֹּלָה.
- **"they increased from Bashan unto Baal-hermon" → "dwelt in the land; from Bashan as far as Baal-hermon and Senir and Mount Hermon, they multiplied"** (v23) — the Hebrew pauses after "in the land"; הֵמָּה רָבוּ "they multiplied" stands last.
- **"mighty men of valour, famous men" → "men who were mighty ones of ability, men of names"** (v24) — אֲנָשִׁים גִּבּוֹרֵי חַיִל אַנְשֵׁי שֵׁמוֹת.
- **"And they transgressed against the God of their fathers, and went a whoring after the gods of the people of the land" → "And they acted unfaithfully against the Elohim of their fathers, and played the prostitute after the gods of the peoples of the land"** (v25) — וַיִּמְעֲלוּ בְּ; וַיִּזְנוּ אַחֲרֵי; עַמֵּי plural. The same word אֱלֹהִים stands three times: of the one God it carries the token, of the peoples' gods it is "gods". מִפְּנֵיהֶם "from before them".
- **"and he carried them away … and brought them unto Halah, and Habor, and Hara, and to the river Gozan, unto this day" → "and he carried them into exile … and brought them to Halah and Habor and Hara and the river of Gozan, to this day"** (v26) — the Hebrew does not say again who "he" is (the Elohim of Israel, or the king); "where they remain", which stood here before, is not in the Hebrew. The three groups stand with לְ after "them" ("them, namely the Reubenite …"); the gentilics are singular. נְהַר גּוֹזָן "the river of Gozan"; 2 Kgs 17:6 has "on the Habor, the river of Gozan" and no Hara: Chronicles is left as written.

### Notes
- **Reuben, Gad, half Manasseh** — the tribes across the Jordan; v18–22 their war with the Hagrites, v25–26 their exile.
- **Pul and Tilgath-pilneser** (v26) — 2 Kgs 15:19, 29; the Hebrew names both, each with "the spirit of", and the verb that follows is singular.
