## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*, with the article each time.
- **"the captains of the host separated to the service of the sons of Asaph" → "the commanders of the host separated for the service the sons of Asaph"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַצָּבָא, the word "prince", of an army "commander"; לַעֲבֹדָה לִבְנֵי אָסָף: the לְ before "the sons" marks those set apart; it may also be read "some of the sons". **"who should prophesy with harps, with psalteries, and with cymbals" → "who prophesied with lyres, with harps and with cymbals"** — the reading tradition (qere) הַנִּבְּאִים, "who prophesied"; the written text (ketiv) has הנביאים, "the prophets". Hebrew כִּנּוֹר "lyre", נֵבֶל "harp" (the kinds of instrument are not certain), מְצִלְתַּיִם "cymbals". The word "prophesy" stands as written (v1, 2, 3); the verse does not explain it. **"the number of the workmen according to their service" → "their number, of the men of work for their service"** — Hebrew מִסְפָּרָם אַנְשֵׁי מְלָאכָה לַעֲבֹדָתָם; "of" is supplied.
- **"under the hands of Asaph, which prophesied according to the order of the king" → "at the hand of Asaph, who prophesied at the hands of the king"** (v2, 3, 6) — Hebrew עַל יַד and עַל־יְדֵי, literally "upon the hand / hands of": beside and under the direction of. "Who prophesied" (הַנִּבָּא, singular) is Asaph in v2; in v3 it may be Jeduthun or belong to the lyre-playing.
- **"Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, six"** (v3) — the Hebrew names five sons and says "six". The earlier verse added "Shimei", who is the tenth lot in v17 and is read here by the Greek (from memory); the name is not in the Hebrew of this verse and is taken out. **"who prophesied with a harp, to give thanks and to praise the LORD" → "with the lyre, who prophesied in giving thanks and praise to Yahweh"** — Hebrew בַּכִּנּוֹר הַנִּבָּא עַל־הֹדוֹת וְהַלֵּל לַיהוָה, literally "upon giving thanks and praising".
- **Names as printed** (v2–4, 9–31) — the lists spell several names in two ways, and each stands as the Hebrew has it: Nethaniah נְתַנְיָה (v2) and נְתַנְיָהוּ (v12); Asharelah אֲשַׂרְאֵלָה (v2) and Jesharelah יְשַׂרְאֵלָה (v14); Zeri צְרִי (v3) and Izri יִצְרִי (v11); Hashabiah חֲשַׁבְיָהוּ (v3) and חֲשַׁבְיָה (v19); Uzziel עֻזִּיאֵל (v4) and Azarel עֲזַרְאֵל (v18); Shebuel שְׁבוּאֵל (v4) and Shubael שׁוּבָאֵל (v20); Jerimoth יְרִימוֹת (v4) and Jeremoth יְרֵמוֹת (v22); Hananiah חֲנַנְיָה (v4) and חֲנַנְיָהוּ (v23); Eliathah אֱלִיאָתָה (v4) and אֱלִיָּתָה (v27). "And" stands in v2–4 only where the Hebrew has it.
- **"the king's seer in the words of God, to lift up the horn" → "the seer of the king in the words of Elohim, to raise the horn"** (v5) — Hebrew חֹזֶה, "seer" (one who gazes; not רֹאֶה); בְּדִבְרֵי הָאֱלֹהִים "in the words (or matters) of Elohim"; "to raise the horn" may go with the words of Elohim (a promise to raise Heman's horn, his strength) or with the sons. The earlier verse had "by the words".
- **"for song in the house of the LORD" → "in the song in the house of Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew בַּשִּׁיר בֵּית יְהוָה, literally "in the song, the house of Yahweh"; the second "in" is supplied. **"according to the king's order to Asaph, Jeduthun, and Heman" → "at the hands of the king: Asaph and Jeduthun and Heman"** — the three names stand at the end without a link word; the earlier verse carried them into v7.
- **"instructed in the songs of the LORD, even all that were cunning" → "taught in song to Yahweh, everyone who had understanding"** (v7) — Hebrew מְלֻמְּדֵי־שִׁיר לַיהוָה, from לִמֵּד "teach"; כָּל־הַמֵּבִין, from the root of "understanding". **"two hundred fourscore and eight" → "two hundred and eighty-eight"**.
- **"ward against ward, as well the small as the great, the teacher as the scholar" → "for the charge, alongside one another, the small as the great, the one who had understanding with the learner"** (v8) — Hebrew גּוֹרָלוֹת מִשְׁמֶרֶת לְעֻמַּת כַּקָּטֹן כַּגָּדוֹל מֵבִין עִם־תַּלְמִיד, literally "lots, charge, alongside, as the small as the great": the word "charge" stands once and לְעֻמַּת "alongside" has no noun after it; "for" and "one another" are supplied. מֵבִין is the word of v7 (not "teacher"); תַּלְמִיד "learner", from the root of "taught" in v7, is found only here in the Old Testament.
- **"the first lot came forth for Asaph to Joseph"** (v9) — "came out" (וַיֵּצֵא) as 24:7; for the first lot the Hebrew does not give "his sons and his brothers, twelve". In v9 the order is "he and his brothers and his sons"; in v10–31 "his sons and his brothers".
- **"The third to Zaccur, he, his sons, and his brethren, were twelve" → "the third, Zaccur: his sons and his brothers, twelve"** (v10–31) — the Hebrew has no verb and changes its form as it goes: the bare name in v10, 12–18, 20, 21; לְ "for" before the name in v11, 19; "for" before the number from v20 and before both from v22. The verse follows each as written. **"Azareel" → "Azarel"** (v18).

### Notes
- **Prophetic music** — Chronicler's distinctive view: temple music is prophetic. Cf. 1 Sam 10:5.
- **Twenty-four musical courses** — parallel to priestly courses. Matching weekly rotations.
- **288 musicians** = 24 courses × 12. Symmetry.
- **Last nine sons of Heman** (v4) — Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, Mahazioth — arrange in sequence and form a cryptic psalm-prayer: "Be gracious to me, Yah, be gracious to me; you are my God; I have magnified, and raised up help; sitting in distress, I have spoken abundantly, visions." Many commentators see a prayer embedded in the names.
- **"The seer of the king"** (v5) — Heman as prophetic figure close to David. Titular status: royal prophet.
- **"The small as the great"** (v8) — no seniority privilege in the casting of lots.
