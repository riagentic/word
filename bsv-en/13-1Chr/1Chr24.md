## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v5 with the article.
- **"Now these are the divisions of the sons of Aaron" → "And for the sons of Aaron, their divisions"** (v1) — Hebrew וְלִבְנֵי אַהֲרֹן מַחְלְקוֹתָם, a heading without a verb; nothing is supplied.
- **"had no children: therefore Eleazar and Ithamar executed the priest's office" → "they had no sons; and Eleazar and Ithamar were priests"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּנִים "sons"; וַיְכַהֲנוּ, the verb of the noun "priest". "Before their father" (לִפְנֵי אֲבִיהֶם) is in his lifetime, or in his presence (Num 3:4).
- **"And David distributed them, both Zadok … and Ahimelech …, according to their offices in their service" → "And David divided them, and Zadok … and Ahimelech …, by their appointment in their service"** (v3) — the verb is singular (the unusual form of 23:6), and the two priests are joined on with "and"; פְּקֻדָּה "appointment" as 23:11.
- **"there were more chief men found of the sons of Eleazar than of the sons of Ithamar; and thus were they divided" → "the sons of Eleazar were found more numerous, by the heads of the men, than the sons of Ithamar; and they divided them"** (v4) — Hebrew רַבִּים לְרָאשֵׁי הַגְּבָרִים, literally "many as to the heads of the men"; גֶּבֶר is the word "mighty man"; וַיַּחְלְקוּם is active, "they divided them". "Heads of fathers' houses" is רָאשִׁים לְבֵית־אָבוֹת, "heads for a house of fathers".
- **"by lot, one sort with another" → "by lots, these with those"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּגוֹרָלוֹת, plural; אֵלֶּה עִם־אֵלֶּה. **"the governors of the sanctuary, and governors of the house of God" → "holy princes and princes of Elohim"** — Hebrew שָׂרֵי־קֹדֶשׁ וְשָׂרֵי הָאֱלֹהִים, literally "princes of holiness and princes of the Elohim"; the first may also be "princes of the Holy" (the sanctuary); "the house of" is not in the Hebrew. **"of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar" → "from the sons of Eleazar and among the sons of Ithamar"** — the Hebrew changes the preposition (מִן … בְּ).
- **"one of the Levites" → "from the Levites"** (v6) — Hebrew מִן־הַלֵּוִי, singular with the article, of the tribe. **"one principal household being taken for Eleazar, and one taken for Ithamar" → "one father's house taken for Eleazar, and taken, taken for Ithamar"** — Hebrew בֵּית־אָב אֶחָד אָחֻז לְאֶלְעָזָר וְאָחֻז אָחֻז לְאִיתָמָר; "taken" is the passive of אָחַז "take hold of", repeated for Ithamar and without "one" there. The sense of the repetition is not certain (one by one in turn, or a difference in the way the houses were drawn). "The heads of the fathers for the priests and for the Levites" has no "houses" in the Hebrew.
- **"Now the first lot came forth to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah" → "And the first lot came out for Jehoiarib, for Jedaiah the second"** (v7–18) — the Hebrew order, name before number, is kept; the numbers are in ordinary English form.
- **Names as printed** — Shecaniah is שְׁכַנְיָהוּ (v11), Delaiah דְּלָיָהוּ and Maaziah מַעַזְיָהוּ (v18), Amariah אֲמַרְיָהוּ (v23, אֲמַרְיָה in 23:19), Zechariah זְכַרְיָהוּ (v25), Rehabiah רְחַבְיָהוּ (v21), Jerimoth יְרִימוֹת (v30, יְרֵמוֹת in 23:23), Shelomoth שְׁלֹמוֹת (v22, שְׁלֹמִית in 23:18), Shubael שׁוּבָאֵל (v20, שְׁבוּאֵל in 23:16). **"Aphses" → "Happizzez"** (v15) — הַפִּצֵּץ, with the article. **"Hezekel" → "Jehezkel"** (v16) — יְחֶזְקֵאל, the name of the prophet Ezekiel.
- **"These were the orderings of them in their service … according to their manner, under Aaron their father" → "These were their appointment for their service … according to their ordinance by the hand of Aaron their father"** (v19) — Hebrew פְקֻדָּתָם, singular; כְּמִשְׁפָּטָם; בְּיַד "by the hand of".
- **"And the rest of the sons of Levi were these: Of the sons of Amram" → "And for the sons of Levi who remained: for the sons of Amram"** (v20) — Hebrew וְלִבְנֵי לֵוִי הַנּוֹתָרִים, with לְ "for" as in v1; no verb.
- **"the first was Isshiah" → "the head, Isshiah"** (v21) — Hebrew הָרֹאשׁ.
- **"Of the Izharites"** (v22) — Hebrew לַיִּצְהָרִי, singular with the article.
- **"And the sons of Hebron; Jeriah the first, Amariah the second" → "And the sons: Jeriah; Amariah the second"** (v23) — the Hebrew is וּבְנָי יְרִיָּהוּ אֲמַרְיָהוּ הַשֵּׁנִי: "of Hebron" and "the first" are not written (they stand in 23:19) and are not supplied. The first word is not pointed as "the sons of" (בְּנֵי) and has a dividing accent, so the verse reads "And the sons: Jeriah"; by the consonants alone it could be "And the sons of Jeriah: Amariah the second …".
- **"Shamir"** (v24) — the reading tradition (qere) שָׁמִיר; the written text (ketiv) has שמור, "Shamur".
- **"the sons of Jaaziah; Beno"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יַעֲזִיָּהוּ בְנוֹ; בְנוֹ is also the common word "his son" ("the sons of Jaaziah his son"). In v27 "and" stands before Shoham, which counts Beno as a name in the list; the verse keeps it as a name. The meaning is not certain.
- **"who had no sons" → "and he had no sons"** (v28) — Hebrew וְלֹא־הָיָה לוֹ בָּנִים.
- **"These likewise cast lots over against their brethren" → "And they also cast lots alongside their brothers"** (v31) — Hebrew לְעֻמַּת, "alongside, parallel to": in the same way as. **"the principal fathers over against their younger brethren" → "the fathers, the head alongside his younger brother"** — Hebrew אָבוֹת הָרֹאשׁ לְעֻמַּת אָחִיו הַקָּטָן, literally "fathers, the head, alongside his small brother": the head of a house and his youngest brother drew alike. "In the presence of" is לִפְנֵי, "before".

### Notes
- **Twenty-four priestly divisions** — weekly rotation; each division served roughly two weeks per year. System still extant in New Testament times: Zechariah served in the division of Abijah (Luke 1:5), the eighth course.
- **Disproportion** (v4) — Eleazar's line twice as large as Ithamar's; lot distribution accordingly.
- **Jehoiarib** (v7) — Hasmonean Maccabees traced lineage here (1 Macc 2:1). First course most prestigious.
- **Post-exilic continuation** — only four courses returned per Ezra 2:36-39; later reorganized to 24 again.
- **Chronicler's design** — establishes David as founder of the post-exilic temple order his readers inherit. Temple-worship institutions trace to Davidic authority, not just Mosaic.
- **Jaaziah** (v26-27) — unique here; possibly a Merarite figure not elsewhere attested, or a variant name.
