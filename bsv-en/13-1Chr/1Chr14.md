# 1 Chronicles 14

[1] And ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ king of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ sent messengers to David, and cedar wood and craftsmen for walls and craftsmen in wood, to build a house for him.

[2] And David knew that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had established him as king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for his kingdom was lifted up on high for the sake of his people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] And David took more wives in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and David begot more sons and daughters.

[4] And these are the names of those born, who were his in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: Shammua and ⟦shobab|Shovav|Shobab⟧, ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ and ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧,

[5] and ⟦ibhar|Yivchar|Ibhar⟧ and Elishua and ⟦elpelet|Elpalet|Elpelet⟧,

[6] and Nogah and ⟦nepheg|Nefeg|Nepheg⟧ and ⟦japhia|Yafia|Japhia⟧,

[7] and Elishama and ⟦beeliada|Beelyada|Beeliada⟧ and ⟦eliphelet|Elifelet|Eliphelet⟧.

[8] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ heard that David had been anointed as king over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and all the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ went up to seek David; and David heard and went out before them.

[9] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ came and made a raid in the valley of ⟦rephaim|Refaim|Rephaim⟧.

[10] And David asked of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, saying, "Shall I go up against the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧? And will you give them into my hand?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Go up, and I will give them into your hand."

[11] And they went up to ⟦baal-perazim|Baal-Peratzim|Baal-perazim⟧, and David struck them there, and David said, "⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has breached my enemies by my hand like a breach of waters." Therefore they called the name of that place ⟦baal-perazim|Baal-Peratzim|Baal-perazim⟧.

[12] And they left their gods there, and David said, and they were burned with fire.

[13] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ yet again made a raid in the valley.

[14] And David asked of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ again, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to him, "You shall not go up after them. Turn around from them, and come to them from over against the balsam trees.

[15] And let it be, when you hear the sound of the marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then you shall go out into the battle, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has gone out before you to strike the camp of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧."

[16] And David did as ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had commanded him, and they struck the camp of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ from ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ and as far as Gezer.

[17] And the name of David went out in all the lands, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ put the dread of him upon all the nations.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 11, 14-16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; with the article in v11, 14 (second), 15, 16.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel: 2 Sam 5:11–25** — where the Hebrew is the same, the wording is that of the finished Samuel verse; every difference named below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Nothing is taken over from Samuel. In v10–16 Chronicles has "Elohim" where Samuel has "Yahweh" (but "Yahweh said" in v10).
- **"Hiram" → "Huram"** (v1) — the written text has חירם "Hiram"; the reading tradition (qere) is חוּרָם "Huram", the form of the name in Chronicles (2 Chr 2:3), and it is translated. **"timber of cedars, with masons and carpenters, to build him an house" → "cedar wood and craftsmen for walls and craftsmen in wood, to build a house for him"** — Hebrew וַעֲצֵי אֲרָזִים וְחָרָשֵׁי קִיר וְחָרָשֵׁי עֵצִים לִבְנוֹת לוֹ בָּיִת: literally "craftsmen of wall and craftsmen of woods". Samuel has "craftsmen in wood and craftsmen in stone for walls, and they built a house for David".
- **"David perceived that the LORD had confirmed him king" → "David knew that Yahweh had established him as king"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֵּדַע; הֱכִינוֹ. **"for his kingdom was lifted up on high"** — Hebrew כִּי־נִשֵּׂאת לְמַעְלָה מַלְכוּתוֹ: a passive, with לְמַעְלָה "upward, on high"; כִּי may also be "that". Samuel has "and that he had lifted up his kingdom" (active, and another word for "kingdom", מַמְלָכָה).
- **"David begat more sons and daughters" kept** (v3) — Hebrew וַיּוֹלֶד, "beget"; the earlier text had "fathered". Samuel has "concubines and wives from Jerusalem after his coming from Hebron, and more sons and daughters were born to David".
- **"the names of his children which he had in Jerusalem" → "the names of those born, who were his in Jerusalem"** (v4) — Hebrew הַיְלוּדִים אֲשֶׁר הָיוּ־לוֹ. The names are joined as the Hebrew joins them: no "and" before Nathan.
- **Elpelet, Nogah, Beeliada** (v5–7) — Elpelet and Nogah are not in 2 Sam 5:15; Beeliada (בְּעֶלְיָדָע, with the element *baal*) is Eliada in 2 Sam 5:16.
- **"when the Philistines heard … David heard of it, and went out against them" → "the Philistines heard … and David heard and went out before them"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי־נִמְשַׁח דָּוִיד, a passive (Samuel: "they had anointed David"), "over all Israel"; וַיֵּצֵא לִפְנֵיהֶם, "before them", to face them (Samuel: "went down to the stronghold"). **"to seek David"** — Hebrew לְבַקֵּשׁ.
- **"spread themselves in the valley of Rephaim" → "made a raid in the valley of Rephaim"** (v9, 13) — Hebrew וַיִּפְשְׁטוּ, the verb of raiding (1 Sam 27:8; 30:1); Samuel has another verb, "spread themselves out".
- **"David enquired of God" → "David asked of Elohim"** (v10, 14) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאַל … בֵּאלֹהִים. **"wilt thou deliver them" → "And will you give them"**; **"for I will deliver them" → "and I will give them"** — Hebrew וּנְתַתָּם … וּנְתַתִּים, the verb "give", joined by "and"; Samuel has "for I will surely give". "Against" is עַל (Samuel: אֶל "to").
- **"So they came up to Baal-perazim" → "And they went up to Baal-perazim"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲלוּ, plural (Samuel: "David came"). **"God hath broken in upon mine enemies by mine hand like the breaking forth of waters" → "Elohim has breached my enemies by my hand like a breach of waters"** — Hebrew פָּרַץ … כְּפֶרֶץ מָיִם, verb and noun of one root; the place name is "owner (or lord) of breaches". בְּיָדִי "by my hand" (Samuel: "before me"). **"they called"** — plural (Samuel: "he called").
- **"when they had left their gods there, David gave a commandment, and they were burned with fire" → "they left their gods there, and David said, and they were burned with fire"** (v12) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵיהֶם "their gods" (Samuel: "their idols"); וַיֹּאמֶר דָּוִיד וַיִּשָּׂרְפוּ בָּאֵשׁ, word for word: "said" in the sense of giving the order; what he said is not written. Samuel has "and David and his men took them up".
- **"the Philistines yet again spread themselves abroad in the valley" → "the Philistines yet again made a raid in the valley"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיֹּסִיפוּ עוֹד … וַיִּפְשְׁטוּ, literally "added yet … and raided"; "the valley" without the name (Samuel: "went up yet again … in the valley of Rephaim").
- **"Go not up after them; turn away from them, and come upon them over against the mulberry trees" → "You shall not go up after them. Turn around from them, and come to them from over against the balsam trees"** (v14) — Hebrew הָסֵב מֵעֲלֵיהֶם, literally "turn around from upon them" (Samuel: "You shall not go up. Turn around to behind them"). בְּכָאִים: the kind of tree is uncertain ("balsam trees", "mulberry trees").
- **"when thou shalt hear a sound of going" → "And let it be, when you hear the sound of the marching"** (v15) — Hebrew וִיהִי כְּשָׁמְעֲךָ אֶת־קוֹל הַצְּעָדָה, with the article. **"then thou shalt go out to battle" → "then you shall go out into the battle"** — Hebrew אָז תֵּצֵא בַמִּלְחָמָה (Samuel: "then you shall act sharply"). **"to smite the host" → "to strike the camp"** — Hebrew לְהַכּוֹת אֶת־מַחֲנֵה (Samuel: "to strike in the camp").
- **"they smote the host of the Philistines from Gibeon even to Gazer" → "they struck the camp of the Philistines from Gibeon and as far as Gezer"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיַּכּוּ, plural; Samuel has "he struck the Philistines from Geba until you come to Gezer".
- **"the fame of David went out into all lands; and the LORD brought the fear of him" → "the name of David went out in all the lands, and Yahweh put the dread of him"** (v17) — Hebrew שֵׁם "name"; נָתַן "give, put"; פַּחְדּוֹ "his dread" (פַּחַד, not יִרְאָה "fear"). The verse is only in Chronicles.
- **"Gazer" → "Gezer"** (v16).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 5:11-25** — Chronicler reorganizes: places Huram-alliance + Philistine wars after ark episode. Chronicles order: coronation → Jerusalem → ark attempt → Huram → Philistine victories → successful ark transfer.
- **Huram (Hiram) of Tyre** — Phoenician ally, supplies cedar. Same partnership crucial for Solomon's temple.
- **Thirteen sons in Jerusalem** (v4-7) — more than 2 Sam 5:14-16 (11). Chronicler adds Elpelet and Nogah.
- **Two Philistine wars** — both in Valley of Rephaim (south-southwest of Jerusalem). First = frontal assault; second = flanking maneuver with divine auditory sign.
- **"Balsam trees" march sign** — wind in the treetops interpreted as divine army. Tactical adjustment at Elohim's word.
- **Gibeon to Gezer** — long pursuit, western descent from hill country.
- **Chronicler's theology** — Yahweh's dread on nations through David parallels the Conquest. Davidic kingdom = recapitulation of Joshua's conquest on national scale.
