# 1 Chronicles 29

[1] And David the king said to all the assembly, "⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ my son, the one whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has chosen, is a boy and tender, and the work is great, for the citadel is not for man but for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] And with all my power I have prepared for the house of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the gold for the gold, and the silver for the silver, and the bronze for the bronze, the iron for the iron, and the wood for the wood; onyx stones and stones for setting, stones of antimony and variegated work, and every costly stone, and stones of marble in abundance.

[3] And further, because I am pleased with the house of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, I have a treasure of gold and silver; I have given it to the house of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, over and above all that I have prepared for the holy house:

[4] three thousand talents of gold, of the gold of ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧, and seven thousand talents of purified silver, to coat the walls of the houses;

[5] gold for the gold and silver for the silver, and for all the work by the hand of craftsmen. And who offers willingly, to fill his hand today for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"

[6] And the princes of the fathers and the princes of the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the commanders of the thousands and the hundreds, and the chiefs of the work of the king, offered willingly.

[7] And they gave for the service of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ five thousand talents of gold and ten thousand darics, and ten thousand talents of silver, and eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and a hundred thousand talents of iron.

[8] And those with whom stones were found gave them to the treasury of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, at the hand of ⟦jehiel|Yechiel|Jehiel⟧ the ⟦gershonites|Gershunni|Gershonite⟧.

[9] And the people rejoiced over their offering willingly, for with a whole heart they had offered willingly to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and David the king also rejoiced with great gladness.

[10] And David blessed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ before the eyes of all the assembly, and David said:

"Blessed are you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ our father, from age and to age.

[11] Yours, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, is the greatness and the might and the beauty and the eminence and the majesty, for all that is in the heavens and on the earth is yours; yours, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, is the kingdom, and you are the one lifted up as head over all.

[12] And the riches and the glory are from before you, and you rule over all, and in your hand are power and might, and in your hand it is to make great and to make strong all.

[13] And now, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, we give thanks to you and praise the name of your beauty.

[14] And who indeed am I, and who is my people, that we should retain power to offer willingly like this? For from you is all, and from your hand we have given to you.

[15] For sojourners are we before you, and settlers, like all our fathers; like a shadow are our days on the earth, and there is no hope.

[16] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, all this multitude that we have prepared to build for you a house for your holy name, from your hand it is, and yours is all.

[17] And I know, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that you test the heart, and with uprightness you are pleased. I, in the uprightness of my heart, have offered willingly all these; and now your people who are found here I have seen with gladness offering willingly to you.

[18] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ our fathers, keep this forever as the inclination of the thoughts of the heart of your people, and establish their heart toward you.

[19] And to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ my son give a whole heart, to keep your commandments, your testimonies and your statutes, and to do all, and to build the citadel that I have prepared."

[20] And David said to all the assembly, "Bless, please, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧." And all the assembly blessed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers, and they bowed their heads and bowed down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and to the king.

[21] And they sacrificed sacrifices to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and offered up burnt offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on the morrow of that day: a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, a thousand lambs, and their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[22] And they ate and drank before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on that day with great gladness. And they made ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ the son of David king a second time, and they anointed him for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ as leader, and ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ as priest.

[23] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sat on the throne of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ as king in the place of David his father, and he prospered; and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ listened to him.

[24] And all the princes and the mighty ones, and also all the sons of King David, gave the hand under ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ the king.

[25] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ exceedingly great before the eyes of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he put upon him a royal majesty that had not been upon any king before him over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[26] And David the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧ reigned over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[27] And the days that he reigned over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were forty years: in ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧ he reigned seven years, and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ he reigned thirty-three.

[28] And he died in a good old age, sated with days, riches and glory; and ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his son reigned in his place.

[29] And the acts of David the king, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the words of ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ the seer, and in the words of ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ the prophet, and in the words of Gad the seer,

[30] with all his reign and his might, and the times that passed over him and over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and over all the kingdoms of the lands.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v7 with the article. In v1 the Hebrew has יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים, "Yahweh Elohim".
- **"Solomon my son, whom alone God hath chosen, is yet young and tender" → "Solomon my son, the one whom *Elohim* has chosen, is a boy and tender"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶחָד בָּחַר־בּוֹ אֱלֹהִים, literally "one — Elohim has chosen him": "the one" or "him alone". נַעַר וָרָךְ: נַעַר is "boy / young man"; רַךְ "tender". "Yet" is not written.
- **"the palace" → "the citadel"** (v1, 19) — Hebrew הַבִּירָה, a late word for a fortified place, a citadel (Neh 1:1; Esth 1:2 of Susa; Neh 2:8 of the citadel by the house); only here is it said of the house of Yahweh. One rendering in both verses.
- **"with all my might" → "with all my power"** (v2) — Hebrew כֹּחַ "power" (גְּבוּרָה is "might", v11, 12, 30). **"the gold for things to be made of gold" → "the gold for the gold"** — Hebrew הַזָּהָב לַזָּהָב, and so for each material; "things" is not written.
- **"onyx stones, and stones to be set, glistering stones, and of divers colours, and all manner of precious stones, and marble stones" → "onyx stones and stones for setting, stones of antimony and variegated work, and every costly stone, and stones of marble"** (v2) — Hebrew אַבְנֵי־שֹׁהַם וּמִלּוּאִים "stones of onyx and of settings" (as Exod 25:7); אַבְנֵי־פוּךְ, "stones of antimony" (פּוּךְ is the dark eye paint, 2 Kgs 9:30; which stone is meant is not known); וְרִקְמָה, the word for embroidery, many-coloured work (here commonly taken of coloured stones; uncertain); יְקָרָה "costly"; שַׁיִשׁ, found only here, "marble" or "alabaster" (uncertain).
- **"because I have set my affection to the house of my God" → "because I am pleased with the house of my *Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew בִּרְצוֹתִי בְּ, "in my being pleased with", the verb רָצָה. **"of mine own proper good" → "a treasure"** — Hebrew סְגֻלָּה, the word for a king's own private treasure ("treasured possession", Exod 19:5). **"which I have given" → "I have given it"** — Hebrew נָתַתִּי, without a relative.
- **"refined silver" → "purified silver"** (v4) — Hebrew מְזֻקָּק, as 28:18. **"to overlay the walls of the houses" → "to coat the walls of the houses"** — Hebrew לָטוּחַ, "to smear, coat": the verb of plastering a wall (Lev 14:42). With gold and silver "plaster" would name the wrong stuff, so "coat"; "overlay" is another verb (צִפָּה).
- **"The gold for things of gold … and for all manner of work to be made by the hands of artificers" → "gold for the gold and silver for the silver, and for all the work by the hand of craftsmen"** (v5) — Hebrew לַזָּהָב לַזָּהָב וְלַכֶּסֶף לַכֶּסֶף, literally "for the gold, for the gold, and for the silver, for the silver". **"who then is willing to consecrate his service this day" → "who offers willingly, to fill his hand today"** — Hebrew מִתְנַדֵּב, "offer (oneself) willingly"; לְמַלֹּאות יָדוֹ, "to fill his hand", the phrase for installing a priest (Exod 28:41), kept.
- **"the chief of the fathers and princes of the tribes … the captains … the rulers of the king's work" → "the princes of the fathers and the princes of the tribes … the commanders … the chiefs of the work of the king"** (v6) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי four times (here also "of the fathers", not רָאשֵׁי "heads"); the last has לְ before it, not rendered. **"offered willingly"** (v5, 6, 9, 14, 17) — one verb, הִתְנַדֵּב.
- **"of gold five thousand talents and ten thousand drams" → "five thousand talents of gold and ten thousand darics"** (v7) — Hebrew אֲדַרְכֹנִים, darics, a Persian gold coin: a unit of the writer's own time, later than David (so also Ezra 8:27). The numbers are written in the ordinary English order; the Hebrew has "ten thousand and eight thousand" for eighteen thousand.
- **"they with whom precious stones were found" → "those with whom stones were found"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהַנִּמְצָא אִתּוֹ אֲבָנִים, "and he with whom stones were found", singular, with a plural verb following; "precious" is not written. **"by the hand of" → "at the hand of"** — Hebrew עַל יַד, "upon the hand of": into his care, as 26:28.
- **"for that they offered willingly, because with perfect heart" → "over their offering willingly, for with a whole heart"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּלֵב שָׁלֵם, as 28:9. **"rejoiced with great joy" → "rejoiced with great gladness"** — Hebrew שָׂמַח שִׂמְחָה גְדוֹלָה, verb and noun of one root.
- **"before all the congregation" → "before the eyes of all the assembly"** (v10) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי כָּל־הַקָּהָל. **"Blessed be thou, LORD God of Israel our father, for ever and ever" → "Blessed are you, Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Israel our father, from age and to age"** — no verb in the Hebrew ("are", not "be"); "our father" may go with "Israel" or with Yahweh; מֵעוֹלָם וְעַד־עוֹלָם, "from age and to age": no article here; 16:36 has it ("from the age and to the age").
- **"the greatness, and the power, and the glory, and the victory, and the majesty" → "the greatness and the might and the beauty and the eminence and the majesty"** (v11) — five nouns, each kept apart: גְּדֻלָּה "greatness", גְּבוּרָה "might", תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty", נֵצַח "eminence" (uncertain: "eminence, glory" or "endurance"; "victory" is a later sense), הוֹד "majesty". **"for all that is in the heaven and in the earth is thine"** — "is yours" is supplied; the Hebrew has only כִּי־כֹל בַּשָּׁמַיִם וּבָאָרֶץ, "for all in the heavens and on the earth". **"and thou art exalted as head above all" → "and you are the one lifted up as head over all"** — Hebrew וְהַמִּתְנַשֵּׂא לְכֹל לְרֹאשׁ, "and the one lifting himself up, over all, as head"; "you are" is supplied.
- **"Both riches and honour come of thee" → "And the riches and the glory are from before you"** (v12) — Hebrew כָּבוֹד "glory"; מִלְּפָנֶיךָ "from before you". **"power and might"** — Hebrew כֹּחַ וּגְבוּרָה. **"to give strength unto all" → "to make strong all"** — Hebrew לְחַזֵּק לַכֹּל.
- **"thy glorious name" → "the name of your beauty"** (v13) — Hebrew לְשֵׁם תִּפְאַרְתֶּךָ, the noun of v11.
- **"But who am I, and what is my people, that we should be able" → "And who indeed am I, and who is my people, that we should retain power"** (v14) — Hebrew וְכִי מִי אֲנִי וּמִי עַמִּי, "who" both times; נַעְצֹר כֹּחַ, "retain / hold power". **"for all things come of thee, and of thine own have we given thee" → "For from you is all, and from your hand we have given to you"** — Hebrew וּמִיָּדְךָ, "from your hand".
- **"strangers … and sojourners" → "sojourners … and settlers"** (v15) — Hebrew גֵּרִים, תּוֹשָׁבִים. **"our days on the earth are as a shadow, and there is none abiding" → "like a shadow are our days on the earth, and there is no hope"** — Hebrew צֵל "shade, shadow"; מִקְוֶה, "hope", from the root "wait for" (Ezra 10:2; Jer 14:8).
- **"all this store" → "all this multitude"** (v16) — Hebrew הֶהָמוֹן, "multitude": here the mass of things gathered. **"for thine holy name" → "for your holy name"** — Hebrew לְשֵׁם קָדְשֶׁךָ, literally "the name of your holiness". The written text (ketiv) has הִיא "she / it", the reading tradition (qere) הוּא "he / it"; no difference of meaning.
- **"thou triest the heart, and hast pleasure in uprightness" → "you test the heart, and with uprightness you are pleased"** (v17) — Hebrew בֹּחֵן "test"; מֵישָׁרִים … תִּרְצֶה, the verb רָצָה of v3. "Uprightness" in the next clause is another noun of the same root, יֹשֶׁר. **"which are present here" → "who are found here"** — Hebrew הַנִּמְצְאוּ־פֹה. **"with joy" → "with gladness"** — Hebrew שִׂמְחָה.
- **"keep this for ever in the imagination of the thoughts of the heart of thy people, and prepare their heart unto thee" → "keep this forever as the inclination of the thoughts of the heart of your people, and establish their heart toward you"** (v18) — Hebrew לְיֵצֶר מַחְשְׁבוֹת לְבַב, as 28:9; the לְ may be "as" or "for". וְהָכֵן, the verb הֵכִין, "make firm".
- **"a perfect heart" → "a whole heart"** (v19) — Hebrew לֵבָב שָׁלֵם. **"and to do all these things" → "and to do all"** — Hebrew הַכֹּל. **"for the which I have made provision" → "that I have prepared"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־הֲכִינוֹתִי.
- **"Now bless the LORD" → "Bless, please, Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew בָּרְכוּ־נָא, with the particle נָא. **"bowed down their heads, and worshipped the LORD, and the king" → "bowed their heads and bowed down to Yahweh and to the king"** — two Hebrew verbs, קָדַד and הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה; the second is said of Yahweh and of the king alike.
- **"on the morrow after that day" → "on the morrow of that day"** (v21) — Hebrew לְמָחֳרַת הַיּוֹם הַהוּא. **"bullocks" → "bulls"** — Hebrew פָּרִים.
- **"they made Solomon the son of David king the second time, and anointed him unto the LORD to be the chief governor" → "they made Solomon the son of David king a second time, and they anointed him for Yahweh as leader"** (v22) — Hebrew שֵׁנִית, "a second time", as it stands (cf. 23:1); "him" is supplied; נָגִיד is "leader".
- **"obeyed him" → "listened to him"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמְעוּ אֵלָיו.
- **"submitted themselves unto Solomon the king" → "gave the hand under Solomon the king"** (v24) — Hebrew נָתְנוּ יָד תַּחַת, word for word: they put the hand under him, in pledge of submission.
- **"magnified Solomon exceedingly" → "made Solomon exceedingly great"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיְגַדֵּל … לְמַעְלָה, literally "upward". **"bestowed upon him such royal majesty as had not been on any king before him in Israel" → "put upon him a royal majesty that had not been upon any king before him over Israel"** — Hebrew הוֹד מַלְכוּת, "majesty of kingdom"; עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל "over Israel".
- **"And the time that he reigned" → "And the days that he reigned"** (v27) — Hebrew וְהַיָּמִים, as 1 Kgs 2:11; the second number stands without "years".
- **"full of days, riches, and honour" → "sated with days, riches and glory"** (v28) — Hebrew שְׂבַע יָמִים, as Gen 25:8; כָּבוֹד "glory". "A good old age" is literally "good gray hair".
- **"written in the book of Samuel the seer, and in the book of Nathan the prophet, and in the book of Gad the seer" → "written in the words of Samuel the seer, and in the words of Nathan the prophet, and in the words of Gad the seer"** (v29) — Hebrew עַל־דִּבְרֵי three times: no word "book" is written. The same word, דִּבְרֵי, stands at the head of the verse ("the acts of David"): "words" or "matters, acts". "Seer" stands for two Hebrew words: הָרֹאֶה of Samuel, הַחֹזֶה of Gad.
- **"the times that went over him" → "the times that passed over him"** (v30) — Hebrew עָבְרוּ עָלָיו. **"the kingdoms of the countries" → "the kingdoms of the lands"** — Hebrew מַמְלְכוֹת הָאֲרָצוֹת.

### Notes
- **David's last public act** — financial consecration for temple. Personal gift (3,000 talents gold, 7,000 silver) + people's gift (5,000 gold + 10,000 darics + 10,000 silver + 18,000 bronze + 100,000 iron). Enormous quantities; symbolic.
- **"To fill his hand" (v5)** — Hebrew *lemallot yado* — priestly installation idiom. David extends ordination-language to lay donors.
- **David's prayer** (v10-19) — classic piece of biblical theology. Divine sovereignty, human smallness, heart uprightness, covenant continuity.
- **"A second time"** (v22) — first in 1 Kgs 1 at Gihon during Adonijah crisis; Chronicles omits crisis, gives this calmer public ratification.
- **"Throne of Yahweh"** (v23) — distinctively Chronistic: Davidic kingship is Yahweh's kingship.
- **Closing record** (v29) — Chronicler cites three prophetic sources for David's acts. Acknowledges his account is selective.
- **1 Chronicles closes** with transfer to Solomon and peaceful death of David. Chronicler's David: ideal king, temple-founder, liturgist.
