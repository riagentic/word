## Changes

- **Text and names** — the verses follow the Hebrew of Chronicles as printed (Masoretic Text, qere). Where Chronicles spells a name differently from Genesis, the Chronicles form stands and nothing is taken over from Genesis: Diphath (v6; Gen 10:3 Riphath), Tarshishah (v7; Gen 10:4 Tarshish), Rodanim (v7; Gen 10:4 Dodanim), Sabta and Raama (v9; Gen 10:7 Sabtah, Raamah), Meshech (v17; Gen 10:23 Mash), Ebal (v22; Gen 10:28 Obal), Zephi (v36; Gen 36:11 Zepho), Homam (v39; Gen 36:22 Hemam), Alian and Shephi (v40; Gen 36:23 Alvan, Shepho), Hamran (v41; Gen 36:26 Hemdan), Jaakan (v42; Gen 36:27 Akan), Hadad and Pai (v50; Gen 36:39 Hadar, Pau).
- **"and Riphath" → "and Diphath"** (v6) — דִיפַת; the earlier "Riphath" was the Genesis name.
- **"Dodanim" → "Rodanim"** (v7) — רוֹדָנִים is the written text here; there is no qere.
- **"The sons of …" / "And the sons of …"** (throughout) — "And" stands exactly where the Hebrew has וּבְנֵי and is absent where it has בְּנֵי; "and" between names likewise follows the Hebrew וְ (so v8 "Cush and Egypt, Put and Canaan"; v30 "Mishma and Dumah, Massa, Hadad and Tema"; v42 "Bilhan and Zaavan, Jaakan"). Supplied: only the colon after "the sons of X" and the copula.
- **"begat" → "begot"** (v10, 11, 13, 18, 20, 34) — יָלַד / הוֹלִיד of the father, as Genesis.
- **"mighty upon the earth" → "a mighty one on the earth"** (v10) — גִּבּוֹר, as Gen 10:8.
- **"Ludim"** (v11) — qere לוּדִים; the written form is לודיים.
- **"of whom came the Philistines" → "from where the Philistines went out"** (v12) — אֲשֶׁר יָצְאוּ מִשָּׁם, as Gen 10:14; the clause stands after Casluhim in the Hebrew.
- **"the Jebusite … the Hamathite"** (v14–16) — the Hebrew has the singular with the article in each name; the same Hebrew is given with plural forms in Gen 10:16–18.
- **"Aram, and Uz, and Hul"** (v17) — the Hebrew of Chronicles sets Uz, Hul, Gether and Meshech in one row with the sons of Shem; "the sons of Aram" (Gen 10:23) is not in the text and is not supplied.
- **"the same is Abraham" → "he is Abraham"** (v27) — הוּא אַבְרָהָם.
- **"Hadad"** (v30) — חֲדַד, with ח; the king of v46–51 is הֲדַד, with ה.
- **"Now the sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine: she bare …"** (v32) — the Hebrew breaks its own sentence in the same way: "And the sons of Keturah, the concubine of Abraham: she bore …"; nothing is smoothed.
- **"and Timna, and Amalek"** (v36) — וְתִמְנָע וַעֲמָלֵק: the Hebrew of Chronicles lists Timna and Amalek among the sons of Eliphaz. Gen 36:12 says that Timna was the concubine of Eliphaz and bore him Amalek; that is not written here and the earlier "and of Timna, Amalek" was taken from Genesis.
- **"The sons of Dishan" → "The sons of Dishon"** (v42) — the Hebrew has דִישׁוֹן both in v41 and in v42 (Gen 36:28 Dishan).
- **"before any king reigned over the children of Israel" → "before there reigned a king of the sons of Israel"** (v43) — לִפְנֵי מְלָךְ־מֶלֶךְ לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, as Gen 36:31; בְּנֵי is "sons of". The Hebrew has no "And Bela … reigned in Edom" here (Gen 36:32): the name follows without a verb.
- **"when Bela was dead" → "And Bela died, and … reigned in his place"** (v44–50) — two wayyiqtol clauses, as Gen 36:33–39.
- **"smote" → "struck"** (v46) — הַמַּכֶּה, as Gen 36:35; "Avith" is the qere (עֲוִית; written עיות).
- **"Rehoboth by the river" → "Rehoboth of the River"** (v48) — רְחֹבוֹת הַנָּהָר, a genitive.
- **"Aliah" → "Alvah"** (v51) — qere עַלְוָה; the written form is עליה (Aliah).
- **"Hadad died also. And the dukes of Edom were; duke Timnah …" → "And Hadad died. And the chiefs of Edom were: chief Timna …"** (v51–54) — וַיָּמָת has no "also"; אַלּוּף "chief", as a title before the name, as Gen 36.

### Notes
- **The Chronicler's opening** — the history from Adam to Israel is given as lists of names. v1–4 and v24–27 are bare names with no verb and no link word in the Hebrew.
- **Parallel material** — Gen 5; 10; 11:10–26; 25:1–4, 12–16; 36. Where the Hebrew is the same, the wording of those chapters is used; where Chronicles differs, it is followed.
- **Edomite kings "before there reigned a king of the sons of Israel"** (v43) — the list is taken over from Gen 36:31–39.
- **Hadad's wife** (v50) — the only woman named in the king-list.
