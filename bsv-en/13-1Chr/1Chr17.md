# 1 Chronicles 17

[1] And it came to pass, when David dwelt in his house, that David said to ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ the prophet, "Behold, I am dwelling in the house of cedars, and the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is under curtains."

[2] And ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ said to David, "All that is in your heart, do, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is with you."

[3] And it came to pass in that night that the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧, saying,

[4] "Go and say to David my servant, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Not you shall build for me the house to dwell in.

[5] For I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought up ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to this day, and I have been from tent to tent and from dwelling.

[6] In all where I have walked about in all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, did I speak a word with one of the judges of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whom I commanded to shepherd my people, saying, "Why have you not built for me a house of cedars?"'

[7] And now, thus you shall say to my servant, to David, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: I took you from the pasture, from after the flock, to be leader over my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] And I was with you in all where you went, and I cut off all your enemies from before you; and I will make for you a name, like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.

[9] And I will set a place for my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I will plant it, and it shall abide in its place and shall be agitated no more; and sons of injustice shall not wear it out again, as at the first,

[10] and from the days that I commanded judges over my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and I will bring low all your enemies. And I tell you, and a house ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will build for you.

[11] And it shall be, when your days are full to go with your fathers, that I will raise up your seed after you, who shall be of your sons, and I will establish his kingdom.

[12] He shall build for me a house, and I will establish his throne forever.

[13] I will be to him for a father, and he shall be to me for a son; and my ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ I will not remove from him, as I removed it from him who was before you.

[14] And I will make him stand in my house and in my kingdom forever, and his throne shall be established forever.'"

[15] According to all these words and according to all this vision, so ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ spoke to David.

[16] And King David came and sat before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he said, "Who am I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and who is my house, that you have brought me as far as here?

[17] And this was small in your eyes, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you have spoken concerning the house of your servant of what is far off; and you have seen me as the turn of the man of the ascent, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[18] What more shall David yet add to you for glory to your servant? And you, your servant you know.

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for the sake of your servant and according to your heart you have done all this greatness, to make known all the great deeds.

[20] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, there is none like you, and there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ besides you, in all that we have heard with our ears.

[21] And who is like your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, one nation on the earth, whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ went to ransom for himself a people, to set for yourself a name of great and fearsome deeds, to drive out nations from before your people, whom you ransomed from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧?

[22] And you gave your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to yourself as a people forever; and you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, became ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them.

[23] And now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, the word that you have spoken concerning your servant and concerning his house, let it be sure forever, and do as you have spoken.

[24] And let your name be sure and be great forever, saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧'; and the house of David your servant is established before you.

[25] For you, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, have uncovered the ear of your servant, to build for him a house; therefore your servant has found to pray before you.

[26] And now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you are ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you have spoken concerning your servant this good.

[27] And now, you have been willing to bless the house of your servant, to be forever before you; for you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, have blessed, and it is blessed forever."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; with the article in v2, 21, 26 (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"Lord of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v7, 24).
- **The chapter beside 2 Sam 7** — where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the wording is that of 2 Sam 7; each difference below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Nothing is taken over from Samuel.
- **"as David sat in his house" → "when David dwelt in his house"** (v1) — Hebrew יָשַׁב "dwell"; Chronicles names David where 2 Sam 7:1–3 has "the king", and has no clause about rest from his enemies.
- **"Lo, I dwell in an house of cedars" → "Behold, I am dwelling in the house of cedars"** (v1) — Hebrew הִנֵּה and בְּבֵית הָאֲרָזִים, with the article. **"remaineth under curtains" → "is under curtains"** — Hebrew תַּחַת יְרִיעוֹת, with no verb; 2 Sam 7:2 has "the ark of Elohim is dwelling in the midst of the curtain".
- **"Thou shalt not build me an house to dwell in" → "Not you shall build for me the house to dwell in"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא אַתָּה תִּבְנֶה־לִּי הַבַּיִת לָשָׁבֶת: a statement, with the weight on "you" and with the article on "house". 2 Sam 7:5 has a question ("Will you build for me a house for my dwelling?").
- **"but have gone from tent to tent, and from one tabernacle to another" → "and I have been from tent to tent and from dwelling"** (v5) — Hebrew וָאֶהְיֶה מֵאֹהֶל אֶל־אֹהֶל וּמִמִּשְׁכָּן; the sentence breaks off after "and from dwelling", and "to another" is not written. "from Egypt" is not written in Chronicles.
- **"judges of Israel"** (v6) — Hebrew שֹׁפְטֵי; 2 Sam 7:7 has שִׁבְטֵי "tribes".
- **"feed" → "shepherd"** (v6) — Hebrew רָעָה.
- **"sheepcote" → "pasture"** (v7) — Hebrew נָוֶה. **"ruler" → "leader"** — Hebrew נָגִיד.
- **"have made thee a name" → "I will make for you a name"** (v8) — Hebrew וְעָשִׂיתִי; "great" (2 Sam 7:9) is not written here.
- **"neither shall the children of wickedness waste them any more" → "sons of injustice shall not wear it out again"** (v9) — Hebrew לְבַלֹּתוֹ, "wear out" (as a garment); 2 Sam 7:10 has "afflict". "again" renders יוֹסִיפוּ, lit. "shall not add to".
- **"since the time" → "from the days"** (v10) — Hebrew וּלְמִיָּמִים, plural. **"I will subdue" → "I will bring low"** — Hebrew וְהִכְנַעְתִּי; 2 Sam 7:11 has "I will give you rest".
- **"Furthermore I tell thee that the LORD will build thee an house" → "And I tell you, and a house Yahweh will build for you"** (v10) — Hebrew וָאַגִּד לָךְ וּבַיִת יִבְנֶה־לְּךָ יְהוָה. The verb is in the first person and in the narrative form ("and I told / tell you"); what follows begins with "and", not "that". 2 Sam 7:11 has "Yahweh tells you that a house Yahweh will make for you".
- **"when thy days be expired that thou must go to be with thy fathers" → "when your days are full to go with your fathers"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּי־מָלְאוּ יָמֶיךָ לָלֶכֶת עִם־אֲבֹתֶיךָ. **"which shall be of thy sons"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יִהְיֶה מִבָּנֶיךָ; 2 Sam 7:12 has "who shall come out of your inward parts".
- **"He shall build me an house"** (v12) — 2 Sam 7:13 has "a house for my name" and "the throne of his kingdom".
- **"I will be his father, and he shall be my son" → "I will be to him for a father, and he shall be to me for a son"** (v13) — the Hebrew לְ kept, as 2 Sam 7:14. The words about his iniquity and the rod of men (2 Sam 7:14) do not stand in Chronicles.
- **"I will not take my mercy away from him" → "my *chesed* I will not remove from him"** (v13) — Hebrew וְחַסְדִּי לֹא־אָסִיר מֵעִמּוֹ, "I" is the subject; in 2 Sam 7:15 the *chesed* is the subject ("shall not depart").
- **"from him that was before thee"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵאֲשֶׁר הָיָה לְפָנֶיךָ; the name Saul (2 Sam 7:15) is not written.
- **"I will settle him in mine house and in my kingdom" → "I will make him stand in my house and in my kingdom"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהַעֲמַדְתִּיהוּ בְּבֵיתִי וּבְמַלְכוּתִי. 2 Sam 7:16 has "your house and your kingdom shall be sure forever before you; your throne". The first "forever" has the article (עַד־הָעוֹלָם).
- **"O LORD God" → "Yahweh Elohim"** (v16, 17) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים; 2 Sam 7:18–29 has אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה in these places, and Chronicles has "Yahweh" (v19, 20, 26, 27) or "Elohim" (v17) or "my Elohim" (v25).
- **"hast regarded me according to the estate of a man of high degree" → "you have seen me as the turn of the man of the ascent"** (v17) — Hebrew וּרְאִיתַנִי כְּתוֹר הָאָדָם הַמַּעֲלָה, word for word; the meaning is not known. תּוֹר is "a turn" (Esth 2:12, 15) and has been taken as "rank" or "manner"; הַמַּעֲלָה is "the ascent, the step" and has been taken as "of high degree" or "upward". 2 Sam 7:19 has "and this is the torah of man". Nothing is supplied.
- **"for thy servant's house" → "concerning the house of your servant"** (v17) — Hebrew עַל; 2 Sam 7:19 has אֶל "to".
- **"What can David speak more to thee for the honour of thy servant?" → "What more shall David yet add to you for glory to your servant?"** (v18) — Hebrew מַה־יּוֹסִיף עוֹד דָּוִיד אֵלֶיךָ לְכָבוֹד אֶת־עַבְדֶּךָ; no "speak" is written (2 Sam 7:20 has it), and "for glory, your servant" is difficult: commonly "for the honor done to your servant".
- **"for thy servant's sake"** (v19) — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר עַבְדְּךָ; 2 Sam 7:21 has "for the sake of your word". **"in making known all these great things" → "to make known all the great deeds"** — Hebrew הַגְּדֻלּוֹת, the plural of "greatness"; so also in v21.
- **"whom God went to redeem to be his own people" → "whom Elohim went to ransom for himself a people"** (v21) — Hebrew פָּדָה "ransom"; עָם stands without לְ (2 Sam 7:23 "as a people"), and the verb "went" is singular (in 2 Sam 7:23 plural). **"to make thee a name of greatness and terribleness" → "to set for yourself a name of great and fearsome deeds"** — Hebrew לָשׂוּם לְךָ שֵׁם גְּדֻלּוֹת וְנֹרָאוֹת; the verse passes from "himself" to "yourself" as the Hebrew does.
- **"didst thou make thine own people" → "you gave your people Israel to yourself as a people"** (v22) — Hebrew וַתִּתֵּן, the verb "give"; 2 Sam 7:24 has "you established for yourself".
- **"let … be established" → "let it be sure"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew יֵאָמֵן, the root of "amen" and "faithful"; 2 Sam 7:25 has "make it stand". In v24 "is established" is נָכוֹן, with no verb.
- **"hast told thy servant that thou wilt build him an house" → "have uncovered the ear of your servant, to build for him a house"** (v25) — Hebrew גָּלִיתָ אֶת־אֹזֶן עַבְדְּךָ לִבְנוֹת לוֹ בָּיִת.
- **"hath found in his heart to pray" → "has found to pray"** (v25) — Hebrew מָצָא … לְהִתְפַּלֵּל; "his heart" (2 Sam 7:27) is not written in Chronicles.
- **"hast promised this goodness unto thy servant" → "you have spoken concerning your servant this good"** (v26) — Hebrew וַתְּדַבֵּר עַל־עַבְדְּךָ הַטּוֹבָה הַזֹּאת.
- **"let it please thee to bless" → "you have been willing to bless"** (v27) — Hebrew הוֹאַלְתָּ, a perfect; 2 Sam 7:29 has the commands "be willing and bless". **"thou blessest, O LORD, and it shall be blessed for ever" → "you, Yahweh, have blessed, and it is blessed forever"** — Hebrew בֵּרַכְתָּ וּמְבֹרָךְ לְעוֹלָם.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 7** — the promise to David and David's prayer.
- **"My house, my kingdom"** (v14) — in Chronicles the house and the kingdom are Yahweh's; in 2 Sam 7:16 they are David's.
- **"Him who was before you"** (v13) — Saul, by 2 Sam 7:15; Chronicles does not name him.
- **"House"** — one Hebrew word, בַּיִת, for David's house of cedars, the house for Yahweh, and the house Yahweh will build for David.
- **Heb 1:5** cites v13 (2 Sam 7:14) in Greek.
