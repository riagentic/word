# 1 Chronicles 19

[1] And it came to pass after this that ⟦nahash|Nachash|Nahash⟧ king of the sons of Ammon died, and his son reigned in his place.

[2] And David said, "I will do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ the son of ⟦nahash|Nachash|Nahash⟧, for his father did ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with me." And David sent messengers to comfort him concerning his father. And the servants of David came to the land of the sons of Ammon, to ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧, to comfort him.

[3] And the princes of the sons of Ammon said to ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧, "Is David honoring your father in your eyes, because he has sent comforters to you? Is it not in order to examine and to overthrow and to spy out the land that his servants have come to you?"

[4] And ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ took the servants of David and shaved them and cut off their garments at the half, as far as the hip, and he sent them away.

[5] And they went and told David concerning the men, and he sent to meet them, for the men were greatly put to shame. And the king said, "Dwell in ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ until your beard springs up, and then return."

[6] And the sons of Ammon saw that they had made themselves stink with David, and ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ and the sons of Ammon sent a thousand talents of silver to hire for themselves from ⟦aram-naharaim|Aram Naharayim|Mesopotamia⟧ and from ⟦aram-maacah|Aram-maachah|Aram-maacah⟧ and from ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧ chariots and horsemen.

[7] And they hired for themselves thirty-two thousand chariots, and the king of ⟦maacah|Maachah|Maacah⟧ and his people; and they came and camped before ⟦medeba|Meidva|Medeba⟧. And the sons of Ammon were gathered from their cities and came to the battle.

[8] And David heard, and he sent ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and all the host of the mighty ones.

[9] And the sons of Ammon came out and drew up for battle at the entrance of the city; and the kings who had come were by themselves in the field.

[10] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ saw that the face of the battle was toward him, front and behind, and he chose from every chosen one in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and drew up to meet ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧.

[11] And the rest of the people he gave into the hand of ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ his brother, and they drew up to meet the sons of Ammon.

[12] And he said, "If ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ is stronger than I, then you shall be salvation to me; and if the sons of Ammon are stronger than you, then I will save you.

[13] Be strong, and let us strengthen ourselves for our people and for the cities of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, what is good in his eyes he will do."

[14] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ drew near, and the people who were with him, before ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ to the battle, and they fled from his face.

[15] And the sons of Ammon saw that ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ had fled, and they also fled from the face of ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ his brother and came into the city. And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[16] And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ saw that they were smitten before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they sent messengers and brought out ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ that was across the River; and ⟦shophach|Shofach|Shophach⟧, the commander of the host of Hadadezer, was before them.

[17] And it was told to David, and he gathered all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and passed over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and came to them and drew up against them. And David drew up for battle to meet ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, and they fought with him.

[18] And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ fled from before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and David killed of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ seven thousand chariots and forty thousand men on foot; and ⟦shophach|Shofach|Shophach⟧, the commander of the host, he put to death.

[19] And the servants of Hadadezer saw that they were smitten before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they made peace with David and served him. And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ was not willing to save the sons of Ammon any more.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **The chapter beside 2 Sam 10** — where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the wording is that of 2 Sam 10; each difference below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. No name, number or word is taken over from Samuel.
- **"the Syrians" → "Aram"** (v10, 12, 14–19) — Hebrew אֲרָם, the name of the people, with singular and plural verbs. **"children of Ammon" → "sons of Ammon"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן.
- **"Nahash … and his son"** (v1) — Chronicles names the father here and not the son; 2 Sam 10:1 names Hanun and not Nahash.
- **"I will shew kindness … because his father shewed kindness" → "I will do *chesed* … for his father did *chesed*"** (v2) — Hebrew עָשָׂה חֶסֶד עִם; "for" is כִּי (2 Sam 10:2 "as").
- **"sent messengers"** (v2) — Hebrew מַלְאָכִים; 2 Sam 10:2 has "by the hand of his servants". The words "to Hanun, to comfort him" are in Chronicles only.
- **"to search, and to overthrow, and to spy out the land" → "to examine and to overthrow and to spy out the land"** (v3) — the three verbs in the order of Chronicles, with "the land" where 2 Sam 10:3 has "the city"; here the servants "have come", there David "has sent" them.
- **"shaved them"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְגַלְּחֵם; "half of their beard" (2 Sam 10:4) is not written here.
- **"in the midst hard by their buttocks" → "at the half, as far as the hip"** (v4) — Hebrew עַד־הַמִּפְשָׂעָה, a word found only here; "hip" (the place of the stride) is the best-supported sense and is not certain. 2 Sam 10:4 has another word ("their buttocks").
- **"Then there went certain, and told David how the men were served" → "And they went and told David concerning the men"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיֵּלְכוּ וַיַּגִּידוּ לְדָוִיד עַל־הָאֲנָשִׁים; who went is not said.
- **"Tarry at Jericho" → "Dwell in Jericho"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁבוּ.
- **"had made themselves odious to David" → "had made themselves stink with David"** (v6) — Hebrew הִתְבָּאֲשׁוּ עִם־דָּוִיד; 2 Sam 10:6 has another form of the same verb, with בְּ.
- **"out of Mesopotamia, and out of Syria-maachah, and out of Zobah"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲרַם נַהֲרַיִם "Aram of the two rivers", אֲרַם מַעֲכָה, צוֹבָה. The thousand talents of silver, the chariots and the horsemen are in Chronicles only; 2 Sam 10:6 has other names and figures.
- **"thirty and two thousand chariots"** (v7) — the figure as printed. **"pitched" → "camped"** — Hebrew וַיַּחֲנוּ.
- **"all the host of the mighty men" → "all the host of the mighty ones"** (v8) — Hebrew כָּל־צְבָא הַגִּבּוֹרִים, a genitive; 2 Sam 10:7 has "all the host, the mighty ones".
- **"put the battle in array" → "drew up for battle"** (v9, 10, 11, 17) — Hebrew עָרַךְ מִלְחָמָה. **"before the gate of the city" → "at the entrance of the city"** (v9) — Hebrew פֶּתַח הָעִיר.
- **"the battle was set against him before and behind" → "the face of the battle was toward him, front and behind"** (v10) — Hebrew פְנֵי־הַמִּלְחָמָה אֵלָיו פָּנִים וְאָחוֹר. **"out of all the choice of Israel" → "from every chosen one in Israel"** — Hebrew מִכָּל־בָּחוּר בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל, singular.
- **"they set themselves in array"** (v11) — the verb is plural in Chronicles ("they drew up"), singular in 2 Sam 10:10.
- **"then thou shalt help me … then I will help thee" → "then you shall be salvation to me … then I will save you"** (v12) — Hebrew לִתְשׁוּעָה and וְהוֹשַׁעְתִּיךָ, one root; 2 Sam 10:11 has "then I will go to save you".
- **"Be of good courage, and let us behave ourselves valiantly" → "Be strong, and let us strengthen ourselves"** (v13) — Hebrew חֲזַק וְנִתְחַזְּקָה, one root.
- **"let the LORD do that which is good in his sight" → "Yahweh, what is good in his eyes he will do"** (v13) — the order of the Hebrew of Chronicles, וַיהוָה הַטּוֹב בְּעֵינָיו יַעֲשֶׂה; 2 Sam 10:12 has the verb first.
- **"before the Syrians unto the battle" → "before Aram to the battle"** (v14) — Hebrew לִפְנֵי אֲרָם לַמִּלְחָמָה.
- **"they likewise fled before Abishai his brother"** (v15) — "also" and "his brother" are in Chronicles only. "Abishai" is written "Abshai" in this chapter.
- **"they were put to the worse" → "they were smitten"** (v16, 19) — Hebrew נִגְּפוּ. **"they sent messengers"** (v16) — in 2 Sam 10:16 Hadadezer sends. **"beyond the river" → "across the River"** — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר הַנָּהָר, the Euphrates.
- **"Shophach"** (v16, 18) — the name as Chronicles has it; 2 Sam 10:16 has "Shobach". **"captain" → "commander"** — Hebrew שַׂר.
- **"and came upon them, and set the battle in array against them" → "and came to them and drew up against them"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיָּבֹא אֲלֵהֶם וַיַּעֲרֹךְ אֲלֵהֶם; 2 Sam 10:17 has "and came to Helam". In Chronicles David draws up to meet Aram; in Samuel Aram draws up to meet David.
- **"seven thousand men which fought in chariots, and forty thousand footmen" → "seven thousand chariots and forty thousand men on foot"** (v18) — Hebrew שִׁבְעַת אֲלָפִים רֶכֶב וְאַרְבָּעִים אֶלֶף אִישׁ רַגְלִי, the figures as printed; "men which fought in" is not written. 2 Sam 10:18 has "seven hundred chariots and forty thousand horsemen".
- **"killed" → "put to death"** (v18) — Hebrew הֵמִית; "killed" earlier in the verse is הָרַג.
- **"neither would the Syrians help" → "Aram was not willing to save"** (v19) — Hebrew וְלֹא־אָבָה אֲרָם לְהוֹשִׁיעַ; 2 Sam 10:19 has "Aram feared to save".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Sam 10.** Chronicles does not have the account of Bathsheba and Uriah that follows in 2 Sam 11–12.
- **Nahash** — the king of the sons of Ammon in 1 Sam 11; what *chesed* he did with David is not told.
- **"his people"** (v7) — the people of the king of Maacah.
