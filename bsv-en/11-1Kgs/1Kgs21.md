# 1 Kings 21

[1] And it came to pass after these things: ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreeli|Jezreelite⟧ had a vineyard, which was in ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, beside the palace of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[2] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ spoke to ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧, saying, "Give me your vineyard, and let it be to me for a garden of greens, for it is near, beside my house; and I will give you in its place a vineyard better than it. If it is good in your eyes, I will give you money, the price of this."

[3] And ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ said to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, "Far be it from me, from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to give the inheritance of my fathers to you."

[4] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ came to his house, sullen and dejected, over the word that ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreeli|Jezreelite⟧ had spoken to him; and he had said, "I will not give to you the inheritance of my fathers." And he lay down on his bed and turned his face around and did not eat bread.

[5] And ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ his wife came to him and spoke to him, "What is this, that your spirit is sullen and you are not eating bread?"

[6] And he spoke to her, "Because I was speaking to ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreeli|Jezreelite⟧, and I said to him, 'Give me your vineyard for money; or, if you delight, I will give you a vineyard in its place.' And he said, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'"

[7] And ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ his wife said to him, "You, now you exercise kingship over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧! Arise, eat bread, and let your heart be good. I, I will give you the vineyard of ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreeli|Jezreelite⟧."

[8] And she wrote letters in the name of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ and sealed them with his seal, and she sent letters to the elders and to the nobles who were in his city, who dwelt with ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧.

[9] And she wrote in the letters, saying, "Proclaim a fast, and seat ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ at the head of the people.

[10] And seat two men, ⟦belial|sons of Beliyyaal|sons of worthlessness⟧, opposite him, and let them testify against him, saying, 'You have blessed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the king.' And bring him out and stone him, and let him die."

[11] And the men of his city, the elders and the nobles who dwelt in his city, did as ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ had sent to them, as was written in the letters that she had sent to them.

[12] They proclaimed a fast and seated ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ at the head of the people.

[13] And the two men, ⟦belial|sons of Beliyyaal|sons of worthlessness⟧, came and sat opposite him, and the ⟦belial|men of Beliyyaal|men of worthlessness⟧ testified against him, against ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧, before the people, saying, "⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ has blessed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the king." And they brought him outside the city and stoned him with stones, and he died.

[14] And they sent to ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧, saying, "⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ has been stoned and is dead."

[15] And it came to pass, when ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ heard that ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ had been stoned and was dead, that ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ said to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, "Arise, take possession of the vineyard of ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreeli|Jezreelite⟧, which he refused to give you for money; for ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ is not alive, for he is dead."

[16] And it came to pass, when ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ heard that ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ was dead, that ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ arose to go down to the vineyard of ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreeli|Jezreelite⟧, to take possession of it.

[17] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ the ⟦tishbite|Tishbi|Tishbite⟧, saying,

[18] "Arise, go down to meet ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who is in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧; behold, he is in the vineyard of ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧, where he has gone down to take possession of it.

[19] And you shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Have you murdered, and also taken possession?' And you shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: In the place where the dogs lapped the blood of ⟦naboth|Navot|Naboth⟧, the dogs shall lap your blood, yours also.'"

[20] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ said to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, "Have you found me, my enemy?" And he said, "I have found you, because you have sold yourself to do the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[21] Behold, I am bringing evil to you, and I will clear away after you, and I will cut off from ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ one who urinates against a wall, shut in and left, in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[22] And I will make your house like the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧ and like the house of ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ the son of ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧, for the vexation with which you have vexed, and you have caused ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to sin.

[23] And of ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ also ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken, saying, 'The dogs shall eat ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ in the rampart of ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧.'

[25] Only, there was none like ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, who sold himself to do the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, whom ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ his wife incited.

[26] And he acted very abominably, going after the idols, according to all that the ⟦amorites|Emorim|Amorites⟧ had done, whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dispossessed from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[28] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ the ⟦tishbite|Tishbi|Tishbite⟧, saying,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"hard by the palace" → "beside the palace"** (v1) — Hebrew הֵיכָל, the word "temple", here of a king's house.
- **"that I may have it for a garden of herbs" → "let it be to me for a garden of greens"** (v2) — Hebrew וִיהִי־לִי לְגַן־יָרָק. **"I will give thee the worth of it in money" → "I will give you money, the price of this"** (v2, 6, 15) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף, the word "silver", here of payment. "I will give" is each time a form of resolve (וְאֶתְּנָה). The Hebrew has no "or" in v2: "if it is good in your eyes, I will give …".
- **"The LORD forbid it me" → "Far be it from me, from Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לִּי מֵיהוָה: a thing profane for me, as Yahweh sees it.
- **"heavy and displeased" → "sullen and dejected"** (v4) — Hebrew סַר וְזָעֵף, as 20:43; סָרָה of his spirit in v5 is the same word. **"turned away his face" → "turned his face around"** — Hebrew וַיַּסֵּב.
- **"Why is thy spirit so sad" → "What is this, that your spirit is sullen"** (v5) — Hebrew מַה־זֶּה רוּחֲךָ סָרָה.
- **"if it please thee" → "if you delight"** (v6) — Hebrew אִם־חָפֵץ אַתָּה.
- **"Dost thou now govern the kingdom of Israel?" → "You, now you exercise kingship over Israel!"** (v7) — Hebrew אַתָּה עַתָּה תַּעֲשֶׂה מְלוּכָה עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל, "you, now, you do kingship over Israel". The Hebrew has no question word; it can be a taunting question, a statement, or a command ("now show that you are king"). **"let thine heart be merry" → "let your heart be good"**.
- **"letters"** (v8, 9, 11) — Hebrew סְפָרִים, the word "book" (any written document). In v8b the reading tradition (qere) has "letters", the written text (ketiv) "the letters". **"the nobles"** — Hebrew חֹרִים, the free-born men of rank. "Sealed them": "them" is supplied.
- **"set Naboth on high among the people" → "seat Naboth at the head of the people"** (v9, 12) — Hebrew הוֹשִׁיבוּ … בְּרֹאשׁ הָעָם.
- **"two men, sons of Belial" → "two men, sons of Beliyyaal"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־בְלִיַּעַל, "sons of worthlessness": a common noun, not a name; in v13b אַנְשֵׁי הַבְּלִיַּעַל, "the men of the worthlessness". **"bear witness against him"** — Hebrew וִיעִדֻהוּ.
- **"Thou didst blaspheme God and the king" → "You have blessed Elohim and the king"** (v10, 13) — the Hebrew word is בֵּרַכְתָּ, בֵּרַךְ, "bless". What is meant is the opposite, "curse" (the charge of Exod 22:28; Lev 24:15, 16): the text writes "bless" so as not to set a word of cursing next to "Elohim". The same is done in Job 1:5, 11; 2:5, 9. The verse translates the word that stands in the Hebrew. Whether Jezebel's letter itself said "bless" or a scribe put it there cannot be known. אֱלֹהִים here can also be "gods".
- **"carried him forth out of the city"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיֹּצִאֻהוּ מִחוּץ לָעִיר. **"stoned him with stones, that he died" → "and he died"**.
- **"Naboth is stoned, and is dead"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew סֻקַּל נָבוֹת וַיָּמֹת, "and he died".
- **"for Naboth is not alive, but dead" → "for he is dead"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי־מֵת.
- **"Hast thou killed" → "Have you murdered"** (v19) — Hebrew רָצַח, the verb of the commandment (Exod 20:13). **"licked" → "lapped"** — Hebrew לָקַק (Judg 7:5). **"thy blood, even thine" → "your blood, yours also"** — Hebrew גַּם־אָתָּה.
- **"O mine enemy"** (v20) — the Hebrew has no "O". **"to work evil" → "to do the evil"** (v20, 25) — Hebrew הָרַע, with the article.
- **"I will bring evil upon thee, and will take away thy posterity, and will cut off from Ahab him that pisseth against the wall, and him that is shut up and left in Israel" → "I am bringing evil to you, and I will clear away after you, and I will cut off from Ahab one who urinates against a wall, shut in and left, in Israel"** (v21) — the words of 14:10 against Jeroboam. הִנְנִי מֵבִיא (qere) is "behold, I am bringing"; וּבִעַרְתִּי אַחֲרֶיךָ is "I will clear away (burn away) after you": "posterity" is not written. מַשְׁתִּין בְּקִיר is translated as it stands (1 Sam 25:22): every male is meant; the old "every male" was a paraphrase. עָצוּר וְעָזוּב, "shut in and left", is a fixed pair whose sense is uncertain ("bond and free", "under age and of age", "helpless and abandoned"); the Hebrew here has "and" before each of the two words. The speech passes without a mark from Elijah's "I have found" to Yahweh's "I".
- **"for the provocation wherewith thou hast provoked me to anger, and made Israel to sin" → "for the vexation with which you have vexed, and you have caused Israel to sin"** (v22) — Hebrew אֶל־הַכַּעַס אֲשֶׁר הִכְעַסְתָּ; "me" is not written.
- **"The dogs shall eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel" → "in the rampart of Jezreel"** (v23) — Hebrew בְּחֵל, the outer wall or its ditch. When the word is recalled in 2 Kgs 9:10, 36, 37 the Hebrew has בְּחֵלֶק, "in the portion of Jezreel", one letter more.
- **"Him that dieth of Ahab in the city" → "The one of Ahab's who dies in the city"** (v24) — as 14:11. **"the fowls of the air" → "the birds of the heavens"**.
- **"But there was none like unto Ahab … whom Jezebel his wife stirred up" → "Only, there was none like Ahab … whom Jezebel his wife incited"** (v25) — Hebrew רַק; הֵסִית.
- **"he did very abominably in following idols" → "he acted very abominably, going after the idols"** (v26) — Hebrew גִּלּוּלִים, a word of contempt. **"the Amorites … cast out" → "dispossessed"** — Hebrew הָאֱמֹרִי, a singular for the people; הוֹרִישׁ.
- **"rent his clothes … and went softly"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיְהַלֵּךְ אַט, "and he walked gently": slowly, quietly.
- **"Seest thou how Ahab humbleth himself before me?" → "Have you seen that Ahab has brought himself low before me?"** (v29) — Hebrew נִכְנַע, "be brought low, bow"; מִלְּפָנַי, "from before me". The Hebrew has no "but" before "in the days of his son".

### Notes
- **Ancestral inheritance (*nachalah*)** — Naboth refuses sale on Torah principle (Lev 25:23, Num 36:7).
- **Judicial murder** — Jezebel uses the legal form (two witnesses, the charge of cursing God and the ruler, stoning outside the city: Exod 22:28; Lev 24; Deut 17:6) to commit injustice.
- **Ahab and Jezebel's roles** — Ahab sulks; Jezebel acts. Ahab is held responsible ("sold himself").
- **Naboth's blood** — the dogs lap Ahab's blood at the pool of Samaria (22:38); the word against his house falls at Jezreel, on Naboth's field (2 Kgs 9:21–26).
- **"In his son's days"** — Joram / Jehoram dies at Naboth's field (2 Kgs 9:21–26).
