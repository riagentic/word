# 1 Kings 5

[1] And ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ king of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ sent his servants to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, for he had heard that they had anointed him as king in place of his father; for ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ had been one who loved David all the days.

[2] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sent to ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧, saying,

[3] "You, you know David my father, that he was not able to build a house for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ because of the war with which they surrounded him, until ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ placed them under the soles of my feet.

[4] And now ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given me rest all around; there is no adversary and no evil occurrence.

[5] And behold, I mean to build a house for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to David my father, saying, 'Your son, whom I will place in your place on your throne, he shall build the house for my name.'

[6] And now, command, and let them cut for me cedars from ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧; and my servants shall be with your servants, and the wages of your servants I will give to you according to all that you say; for you, you know that there is not among us a man who knows how to cut trees like the ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧."

[7] And it came to pass, when ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ heard the words of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, that he rejoiced greatly, and he said, "Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ today, who has given to David a wise son over this numerous people."

[8] And ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ sent to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, saying, "I have heard what you sent to me. I, I will do all your delight in cedar wood and in cypress wood.

[9] My servants shall bring them down from ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ to the sea, and I, I will make them into rafts on the sea, as far as the place of which you shall send to me, and I will break them up there, and you, you shall take them up; and you, you shall do my delight, to give the bread of my house."

[10] And ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ was giving to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ cedar wood and cypress wood, all his delight.

[11] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ gave to ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ twenty thousand kors of wheat as food for his house, and twenty kors of beaten oil. Thus ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ would give to ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ year by year.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave wisdom to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, as he had spoken to him; and there was ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ between ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ and ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, and the two of them cut a covenant.

[13] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ brought up forced labor out of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and the forced labor was thirty thousand men.

[14] And he sent them to ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, ten thousand a month, in changes: a month they would be in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, two months in his house. And Adoniram was over the forced labor.

[15] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had seventy thousand bearers of burdens and eighty thousand hewers in the mountain,

[16] apart from the chiefs of the prefects that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had, who were over the work, three thousand three hundred, who had dominion over the people doing the work.

[17] And the king commanded, and they quarried great stones, costly stones, to found the house, hewn stones.

[18] And the builders of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and the builders of ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ and the ⟦gebalites|Givlim|Gebalites⟧ hewed, and they prepared the wood and the stones to build the house.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"servants"** (v1, 6, 9) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word for a slave; of a king's men "servant".
- **"was ever a lover of David" → "had been one who loved David all the days"** (v1) — Hebrew אֹהֵב הָיָה … לְדָוִד כָּל־הַיָּמִים: a participle, "a lover of / a friend to David"; "all the days", not "always".
- **"Thou knowest how that David my father could not" → "You, you know David my father, that he was not able"** (v3) — the Hebrew has the pronoun beside the verb and makes David the object: "you know David …, that he …".
- **"the wars which were about him on every side" → "the war with which they surrounded him"** (v3) — Hebrew הַמִּלְחָמָה, singular, and a plural verb with no subject named: "(the enemies) surrounded him". מִפְּנֵי "because of" is literally "from the face of".
- **"under the soles of his feet" → "under the soles of my feet"** (v3) — the written text (ketiv) has רגלו "his foot" (David's); the reading tradition (qere) has רַגְלָי "my feet" (Solomon's). The qere is translated; the ketiv gives the easier sense and is followed by most translations.
- **"put" → "placed"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew נָתַן "give", of putting a thing in its place; in v5 "whom I will place in your place" (תַּחְתֶּיךָ).
- **"neither adversary nor evil occurrent" → "no adversary and no evil occurrence"** (v4) — "adversary" is שָׂטָן *satan*, the common noun. פֶּגַע is "what meets one", a mishap (only here and Eccl 9:11).
- **"rest on every side" → "rest all around"** (v4) — Hebrew הֵנִיחַ … מִסָּבִיב, as Deut 12:10; Josh 21:44.
- **"I purpose to build" → "I mean to build"** (v5) — Hebrew אֹמֵר לִבְנוֹת, literally "I am saying to build" (as Exod 2:14).
- **"hew me cedar trees" → "cut for me cedars"; "hew timber" → "cut trees"** (v6) — Hebrew כָּרַת "cut" both times; עֵצִים is "trees" or "wood".
- **"unto thee will I give hire for thy servants" → "the wages of your servants I will give to you"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׂכַר עֲבָדֶיךָ, a genitive.
- **"Blessed be the LORD this day" → "Blessed is Yahweh today"** (v7) — the Hebrew has no verb; "today" is הַיּוֹם.
- **"this great people" → "this numerous people"** (v7) — Hebrew עַם רָב.
- **"I have considered the things which thou sentest to me for" → "I have heard what you sent to me"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי "I have heard".
- **"desire" → "delight"** (v8, 9, 10) — Hebrew חֵפֶץ, the noun of חָפֵץ "delight in": what one is pleased to have. "Do all your delight" = do all you wish.
- **"fir" → "cypress"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew בְּרוֹשׁ *berosh*: a conifer of Lebanon; the kind is not certain (cypress, juniper or fir).
- **"I will convey them by sea in floats" → "I will make them into rafts on the sea"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲשִׂימֵם דֹּבְרוֹת "I will make them rafts". דֹּבְרוֹת is found only here; "rafts" is the best-supported sense. "Them" after "bring down" and after "take up" is supplied: the Hebrew verbs have no object.
- **"the place that thou shalt appoint me" → "the place of which you shall send to me"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־תִּשְׁלַח אֵלַי, literally "which you shall send to me" (send word).
- **"cause them to be discharged" → "break them up"** (v9) — Hebrew נִפֵּץ "shatter, break apart" (the rafts).
- **"in giving food for my household" → "to give the bread of my house"** (v9) — Hebrew לָתֵת לֶחֶם בֵּיתִי; לֶחֶם is "bread".
- **"So Hiram gave" → "And Hiram was giving"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיְהִי … נֹתֵן, a lasting action. The name is spelled חִירוֹם "Hirom" here and in v18; elsewhere in the chapter חִירָם.
- **"measures" → "kors"** (v11) — Hebrew כֹּר *kor*, a large dry measure. מַכֹּלֶת "food" is found only here (from אָכַל "eat").
- **"twenty measures of pure oil" → "twenty kors of beaten oil"** (v11) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן כָּתִית, oil from beaten olives (Exod 27:20). The Masoretic Text has "twenty kors"; the Septuagint has "twenty thousand baths" (from memory), as 2 Chr 2:10.
- **"as he promised him" → "as he had spoken to him"** (v12) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר "speak".
- **"peace" → "shalom"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁלֹם.
- **"they two made a league together" → "the two of them cut a covenant"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּכְרְתוּ בְרִית.
- **"raised a levy" → "brought up forced labor"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew וַיַּעַל … מַס: מַס *mas* is labor exacted, not a tax.
- **"by courses" → "in changes"** (v14) — Hebrew חֲלִיפוֹת "changes" (as of garments): relays. "In his house" is singular: each man at his own home.
- **"that bare burdens" → "bearers of burdens"** (v15) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא סַבָּל, literally "carrier, burden-bearer".
- **"hewers in the mountains" → "hewers in the mountain"** (v15) — Hebrew בָּהָר, singular with the article: "the mountain" or "the hill country". "Hew" here is חָצַב; in v18 it is פָּסַל (as Exod 34:1).
- **"the chief of Solomon's officers" → "the chiefs of the prefects that Solomon had"** (v16) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַנִּצָּבִים, literally "the chiefs of the ones stationed": the overseers; "prefects" as 4:5, 7 and 9:23 (the same word).
- **"which ruled over the people" → "who had dominion over the people"** (v16) — Hebrew רָדָה, as Gen 1:26.
- **"they brought great stones, costly stones, and hewed stones, to lay the foundation of the house" → "they quarried great stones, costly stones, to found the house, hewn stones"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּסִּעוּ, literally "they pulled out" (or "moved"): to quarry, or to transport; the Hebrew order is kept. יְקָרוֹת is "precious", of high worth. "Hewn stones" (אַבְנֵי גָזִית) stands last, in apposition.
- **"stonesquarers" → "Gebalites"** (v18) — Hebrew הַגִּבְלִים *ha-Givlim*: the men of Gebal (Byblos).
- **"timber" → "wood"** (v18) — Hebrew הָעֵצִים.

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew Bible this chapter is 5:15–32; Hebrew 5:1–14 is 4:21–34 in our numbering.
- **Hiram of Tyre alliance** — continues since David's time (2 Sam 5:11). Phoenician technology and timber; Israelite grain and oil.
- **Cedar and cypress** — Lebanon's prized lumber for royal construction.
- **Forced labor** (v13) — 30,000 out of all Israel, in three relays; plus 70,000 bearers and 80,000 hewers (compare 9:20–22).
- **Fulfillment of 2 Sam 7** — Solomon undertakes what was promised his father (v5).
