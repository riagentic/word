# 1 Kings 7

[1] And his house ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built thirteen years, and he finished all his house.

[2] And he built the House of the Forest of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧: a hundred cubits its length, and fifty cubits its width, and thirty cubits its height, on four rows of pillars of cedar, and cut cedars upon the pillars.

[3] And it was covered with cedar above, over the side chambers that were on the pillars, forty-five, fifteen to the row.

[4] And frames, three rows; and window to window, three times.

[5] And all the entrances and the doorposts were square in frame; and over against, window to window, three times.

[6] And the porch of pillars he made: fifty cubits its length, and thirty cubits its width; and a porch facing them, and pillars and a canopy facing them.

[7] And the porch of the throne, where he would judge, the porch of judgment, he made; and it was covered with cedar from the floor to the floor.

[8] And his house where he would dwell, the other court, inward from the porch, was like this work. And a house he would make for the daughter of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, whom ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had taken, like this porch.

[9] All these were of costly stones, according to the measures of hewn stone, sawn with the saw, inside and outside, and from the foundation to the copings, and from outside to the great court.

[10] And it was founded with costly stones, great stones, stones of ten cubits and stones of eight cubits.

[11] And above were costly stones, according to the measures of hewn stone, and cedar.

[12] And the great court around: three rows of hewn stone and a row of cut cedars; and for the inner court of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and for the porch of the house.

[13] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sent and took ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ from ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧.

[14] He was the son of a widow woman, from the tribe of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, and his father was a man of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, a craftsman in bronze; and he was filled with the wisdom and the understanding and the knowledge to do all work in bronze. And he came to King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and did all his work.

[15] And he formed the two pillars of bronze: eighteen cubits the height of the one pillar, and a thread of twelve cubits would go around the second pillar.

[16] And two capitals he made, to place on the tops of the pillars, cast of bronze: five cubits the height of the one capital, and five cubits the height of the second capital.

[17] Lattices, the work of a lattice, twisted cords, the work of chains, for the capitals that were on the top of the pillars: seven for the one capital and seven for the second capital.

[18] And he made the pillars, and two rows around on the one lattice, to cover the capitals that were on the top of the pomegranates; and so he did for the second capital.

[19] And the capitals that were on the top of the pillars were the work of a lily, in the porch, four cubits.

[20] And capitals were on the two pillars, also above, alongside the belly that was beside the lattice; and the pomegranates were two hundred, in rows around, on the second capital.

[21] And he raised up the pillars at the porch of the temple; and he raised up the right pillar and called its name ⟦jachin|Yachin|Jachin⟧, and he raised up the left pillar and called its name Boaz.

[22] And on the top of the pillars was the work of a lily; and the work of the pillars came to an end.

[23] And he made the sea, cast: ten cubits from its lip to its lip, round all around, and five cubits its height; and a line of thirty cubits would go around it.

[24] And gourds were under its lip around, going around it, ten by the cubit, circling the sea around; two rows were the gourds, cast in its casting.

[25] It was standing on twelve of the herd: three facing north and three facing west and three facing south and three facing east; and the sea was upon them above, and all their hinder parts were inward.

[26] And its thickness was a handbreadth, and its lip was like the work of the lip of a cup, a lily flower; two thousand baths it would hold.

[27] And he made the ten bases of bronze: four cubits the length of the one base, and four cubits its width, and three cubits its height.

[28] And this was the work of the base: they had frames, and frames between the crosspieces.

[29] And on the frames that were between the crosspieces were lions, cattle and ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧; and on the crosspieces likewise above; and beneath the lions and the cattle were wreaths, hanging work.

[30] And four wheels of bronze for the one base, and axles of bronze; and its four feet had shoulders; under the basin were the shoulders, cast, each beside wreaths.

[31] And its mouth within the capital and upward was a cubit; and its mouth was round, the work of a stand, a cubit and a half; and also on its mouth were carvings; and their frames were square, not round.

[32] And the four wheels were underneath the frames, and the stays of the wheels were in the base; and the height of the one wheel was a cubit and a half.

[33] And the work of the wheels was like the work of the wheel of a chariot: their stays and their rims and their spokes and their hubs, all was cast.

[34] And four shoulders were at the four corners of the one base; from the base were its shoulders.

[35] And on the top of the base, half a cubit in height, round all around; and on the top of the base its stays and its frames were from it.

[36] And he engraved on the plates, its stays, and on its frames, ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, lions and palm trees, according to the bare space of each, and wreaths around.

[37] Like this he made the ten bases: one casting, one measure, one form for all of them.

[38] And he made ten basins of bronze: forty baths the one basin would hold; four cubits the one basin; one basin on the one base, for the ten bases.

[39] And he placed the bases, five on the shoulder of the house on the right and five on the shoulder of the house on its left; and the sea he placed at the right shoulder of the house, eastward, over against the south.

[40] And ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ made the basins and the shovels and the bowls. And ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ finished doing all the work that he did for King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

[41] two pillars, and the globes of the capitals that were on the top of the pillars, two; and the lattices, two, to cover the two globes of the capitals that were on the top of the pillars;

[42] and the pomegranates, four hundred, for the two lattices, two rows of pomegranates for the one lattice, to cover the two globes of the capitals that were on the face of the pillars;

[43] and the bases, ten, and the basins, ten, on the bases;

[44] and the one sea, and the twelve of the herd under the sea;

[45] and the pots and the shovels and the bowls. And all these utensils that ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ made for King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ were of polished bronze.

[46] In the circle of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ the king cast them, in the thickness of the ground, between ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ and ⟦zarethan|Tzartan|Zarethan⟧.

[47] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ left all the utensils, because of the very, very great number; the weight of the bronze was not examined.

[48] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made all the utensils that were in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: the altar of gold, and the table on which was the bread of the face, of gold;

[49] and the lampstands, five on the right and five on the left, before the inner room, of refined gold; and the flower and the lamps and the tongs, of gold;

[50] and the basins and the snuffers and the bowls and the pans and the firepans, of refined gold; and the hinges for the doors of the inner house, for the Holy of Holies, for the doors of the house, for the temple, of gold.

[51] And all the work that King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ did in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was completed. And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ brought in the holy things of David his father; the silver and the gold and the utensils he placed in the treasuries of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Building terms** (throughout) — many words of this chapter are found only here, and the shape of several parts is not known. Each Hebrew word has one rendering, the plainest that is supported; the uncertain ones are named below. Words shared with the chapters of the tent (Exod 25–40) have the wording used there.
- **Measures** (throughout) — cubits and baths as the Hebrew gives them; "by the cubit" (בָּאַמָּה) is rendered "cubits" (as 6:3).
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (throughout) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת.
- **"But Solomon was building his own house" → "And his house Solomon built"** (v1) — Hebrew וְאֶת־בֵּיתוֹ בָּנָה: the object stands first; "own" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"upon four rows of cedar pillars, with cedar beams" → "on four rows of pillars of cedar, and cut cedars"** (v2) — Hebrew כְּרֻתוֹת אֲרָזִים, "cut things of cedars": beams (as 6:36).
- **"upon the beams, that lay on forty five pillars" → "over the side chambers that were on the pillars, forty-five"** (v3) — Hebrew הַצְּלָעֹת, the word of 6:5 ("side chambers") and 6:15 ("planks"); which is meant here is not certain. The Hebrew does not say that the pillars were forty-five.
- **"windows in three rows, and light was against light in three ranks" → "frames, three rows; and window to window, three times"** (v4) — Hebrew שְׁקֻפִים "frames" (as 6:4; uncertain) and מֶחֱזָה, found only in v4, 5, from חָזָה "behold": a place to look out. The usual word for "window" is חַלּוֹן.
- **"all the doors and posts were square, with the windows" → "all the entrances and the doorposts were square in frame"** (v5) — Hebrew רְבֻעִים שָׁקֶף. "Over against" is מוּל.
- **"porch of pillars" kept; "the other pillars and the thick beam were before them" → "pillars and a canopy facing them"** (v6) — Hebrew עָב, as an architectural term only here and Ezek 41:25, 26; uncertain (a canopy, a threshold, or a thick beam).
- **"a porch for the throne where he might judge" → "the porch of the throne, where he would judge"** (v7) — Hebrew אוּלָם הַכִּסֵּא אֲשֶׁר יִשְׁפָּט־שָׁם.
- **"from one side of the floor to the other" → "from the floor to the floor"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵהַקַּרְקַע עַד־הַקַּרְקָע, as the Masoretic Text has it. "To the beams" is read by the Vulgate and the Syriac (from memory).
- **"had another court within the porch, which was of the like work" → "the other court, inward from the porch, was like this work"** (v8) — Hebrew חָצֵר הָאַחֶרֶת מִבֵּית לָאוּלָם.
- **"whom he had taken to wife" → "whom Solomon had taken"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לָקַח שְׁלֹמֹה; "to wife" is not in the Hebrew. "He would make" is an imperfect (יַעֲשֶׂה).
- **"costly stones" kept** (v9–11) — Hebrew אֲבָנִים יְקָרֹת, "precious", of high worth (as 5:17).
- **"unto the coping" → "to the copings"** (v9) — Hebrew הַטְּפָחוֹת, only here in this sense; uncertain (root of טֶפַח "handbreadth").
- **"both for the inner court … and for the porch" → "and for the inner court … and for the porch"** (v12) — Hebrew וְלַחֲצַר … וּלְאֻלָם: the inner court and the porch were built the same way (6:36).
- **"fetched Hiram out of Tyre" → "took Hiram from Tyre"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּקַּח "took".
- **"a widow's son" → "the son of a widow woman"** (v14) — Hebrew בֶּן־אִשָּׁה אַלְמָנָה.
- **"a worker in brass … wisdom, and understanding, and cunning" → "a craftsman in bronze … the wisdom and the understanding and the knowledge"** (v14) — Hebrew חֹרֵשׁ; חָכְמָה, תְּבוּנָה, דַּעַת, the three words said of Bezalel (Exod 31:3), each here with the article.
- **"he cast two pillars" → "he formed the two pillars"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיָּצַר "formed, fashioned".
- **"a line of twelve cubits did compass either of them about" → "a thread of twelve cubits would go around the second pillar"** (v15) — Hebrew חוּט "thread"; the Masoretic Text gives the height of "the one pillar" and the girth of "the second pillar".
- **"chapiters" → "capitals"** (v16–20, 31, 41, 42) — Hebrew כֹּתֶרֶת, the crown of a pillar (root כֶּתֶר "crown"). In v31 the same word names a part of the base.
- **"nets of checker work, and wreaths of chain work" → "Lattices, the work of a lattice, twisted cords, the work of chains"** (v17) — Hebrew שְׂבָכָה, a lattice or mesh (2 Kgs 1:2; another word than רֶשֶׁת "net"); גְּדִלִים "twisted cords" (Deut 22:12); שַׁרְשְׁרוֹת "chains" (Exod 28:14). The Masoretic Text has "seven … seven"; the Septuagint has "a net … a net" (from memory).
- **"to cover the chapiters that were upon the top, with pomegranates" → "to cover the capitals that were on the top of the pomegranates"** (v18) — the Masoretic Text as it stands: "he made the pillars … on the top of the pomegranates". Some Hebrew manuscripts have the two nouns the other way round ("he made the pomegranates … on the top of the pillars": from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"lily work" → "the work of a lily"** (v19, 22) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה שׁוּשַׁן. The flower is not certainly known (lily or lotus).
- **"over against the belly which was by the network" → "alongside the belly that was beside the lattice"** (v20) — Hebrew מִלְּעֻמַּת הַבֶּטֶן: "the belly" is the rounded part of the capital.
- **"set up" → "raised up"** (v21) — Hebrew הֵקִים, as of the dwelling and its pillars (Exod 40:18).
- **"in the porch of the temple" → "at the porch of the temple"** (v21) — Hebrew לְאֻלָם הַהֵיכָל. "Right" and "left" are the south and the north for one who faces east. The names: יָכִין *Yakhin* "he establishes", בֹּעַז *Boaz*, perhaps "in him is strength"; the verse gives the names only.
- **"so was the work of the pillars finished" → "the work of the pillars came to an end"** (v22) — Hebrew וַתִּתֹּם.
- **"a molten sea" → "the sea, cast"** (v23) — Hebrew אֶת־הַיָּם מוּצָק; מוּצָק "cast" (Exod 25:12).
- **"from the one brim to the other" → "from its lip to its lip"** (v23, 24, 26) — Hebrew שָׂפָה "lip".
- **"knops … ten in a cubit" → "gourds … ten by the cubit"** (v24) — Hebrew פְּקָעִים (as 6:18) and עֶשֶׂר בָּאַמָּה, the same words that mean "ten cubits" in v23. Here they are commonly taken as "ten to each cubit"; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"twelve oxen" → "twelve of the herd"** (v25, 44) — Hebrew בָּקָר, the collective word for cattle; in v29 "cattle".
- **"looking toward" → "facing"** (v25) — Hebrew פֹּנִים. "West" is literally "seaward" (יָמָּה).
- **"hinder parts" kept** (v25) — Hebrew אֲחֹרֵיהֶם.
- **"with flowers of lilies: it contained two thousand baths" → "a lily flower; two thousand baths it would hold"** (v26) — Hebrew פֶּרַח שׁוֹשָׁן, singular; יָכִיל "hold". The bath is a liquid measure.
- **"bases" kept** (v27–43) — Hebrew מְכוֹנָה, from כּוּן "set firm": the wheeled stand of a basin. "Stand" is kept for כֵּן (v31).
- **"borders … ledges" → "frames … crosspieces"** (v28, 29) — Hebrew מִסְגֶּרֶת "frame" (Exod 25:25; uncertain) and שְׁלַבִּים, found only here (root "join"; uncertain).
- **"oxen" → "cattle"; "upon the ledges there was a base above" → "on the crosspieces likewise above"** (v29) — Hebrew כֵּן, "so" or "a stand"; both readings are possible.
- **"certain additions made of thin work" → "wreaths, hanging work"** (v29, 30, 36) — Hebrew לֹיוֹת, found only here ("wreaths": uncertain), and מַעֲשֵׂה מוֹרָד, literally "the work of a going down".
- **"plates of brass: and the four corners thereof had undersetters" → "axles of bronze; and its four feet had shoulders"** (v30) — Hebrew סַרְנֵי, in this sense only here ("axles": uncertain); פַּעֲמֹתָיו "its feet" (Exod 25:12); כְּתֵפֹת "shoulders" (supports). The end of the verse is obscure: literally "from the side of each, wreaths".
- **"laver" → "basin"** (v30, 38, 40, 43) — Hebrew כִּיּוֹר, as Exod 30:18.
- **"the mouth of it within the chapiter" → "its mouth within the capital"** (v31) — Hebrew פִּיהוּ, then פִּיהָ: "its mouth" twice, with a masculine and a feminine suffix. "The work of a stand" is מַעֲשֵׂה־כֵן. "Carvings" is מִקְלָעוֹת (6:18); "their frames" is as v28.
- **"the axletrees of the wheels were joined to the base" → "the stays of the wheels were in the base"** (v32, 33, 35, 36) — Hebrew יָדוֹת, literally "hands": holders or supports ("tenons" of a board, Exod 26:17). What they were on the wheels and on the base is not certain.
- **"their axletrees, and their naves, and their felloes, and their spokes" → "their stays and their rims and their spokes and their hubs"** (v33) — Hebrew גַּבֵּיהֶם, חִשֻּׁקֵיהֶם, חִשֻּׁרֵיהֶם. The last two are found only here; חִשֻּׁק is of the root of the "bands" of the court pillars (Exod 27:10). The parts cannot be told apart with certainty.
- **"the undersetters were of the very base itself" → "from the base were its shoulders"** (v34, 35) — Hebrew מִן־הַמְּכֹנָה, מִמֶּנָּה "from it": of one piece with it (as Exod 25:31).
- **"a round compass of half a cubit high" → "half a cubit in height, round all around"** (v35) — Hebrew עָגֹל סָבִיב.
- **"on the plates of the ledges thereof … he graved" → "he engraved on the plates, its stays, and on its frames"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיְפַתַּח עַל־הַלֻּחֹת יְדֹתֶיהָ; לֻחֹת is the word for "tablets" and "planks". "According to the bare space of each" is כְּמַעַר־אִישׁ, uncertain.
- **"one casting, one measure, and one size" → "one casting, one measure, one form"** (v37) — Hebrew קֶצֶב (as 6:25).
- **"on the right side of the house … on the left side" → "on the shoulder of the house on the right … on its left"** (v39) — Hebrew כֶּתֶף "shoulder". The last words are literally "at the right shoulder of the house, eastward, from over against the south (נֶגֶב)".
- **"Hiram made the lavers" → "Hiram made the basins"** (v40) — the Masoretic Text has הַכִּיֹּרוֹת "the basins"; "the pots" (הַסִּירוֹת, as v45 and 2 Chr 4:11) is read by many Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint (from memory). The name is spelled חִירוֹם "Hirom" at its first place in the verse.
- **"basons" → "bowls"** (v40, 45, 50) — Hebrew מִזְרָק, as Exod 27:3.
- **"for the house of the LORD" → "in the house of Yahweh"** (v40, 45, 48, 51) — Hebrew בֵּית יְהוָה, without a preposition.
- **"the two bowls of the chapiters" → "the globes of the capitals"** (v41, 42) — Hebrew גֻּלֹּת, a rounded thing (a bowl or a ball); another word than מִזְרָק "bowl".
- **"upon the pillars" → "on the face of the pillars"** (v42) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי.
- **"all these vessels" → "all these utensils"** (v45, 47, 48, 51) — Hebrew כֵּלִים, as in Exodus. "These" is the qere (הָאֵלֶּה); the written form is האהל "the tent".
- **"bright brass" → "polished bronze"** (v45) — Hebrew מְמֹרָט.
- **"In the plain of Jordan … in the clay ground" → "In the circle of the Jordan … in the thickness of the ground"** (v46) — Hebrew כִּכַּר הַיַּרְדֵּן (as Gen 13:10) and בְּמַעֲבֵה הָאֲדָמָה, literally "in the thickness of the ground": taken as clay moulds, or a foundry in the earth.
- **"left all the vessels unweighed, because they were exceeding many: neither was the weight of the brass found out" → "left all the utensils, because of the very, very great number; the weight of the bronze was not examined"** (v47) — Hebrew וַיַּנַּח "left"; "unweighed" is not in the Hebrew. מְאֹד is said twice. לֹא נֶחְקַר is "was not examined / searched out".
- **"the altar of gold" kept; "shewbread" → "bread of the face"** (v48) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים, literally "bread of the face": set before the face of Yahweh. "Showbread" and "bread of the Presence" are interpretations.
- **"candlesticks" → "lampstands"; "oracle" → "inner room"** (v49) — Hebrew מְנֹרוֹת, דְּבִיר (6:5). "The flower" is singular in the Hebrew.
- **"the bowls, and the snuffers, and the basons, and the spoons, and the censers" → "the basins and the snuffers and the bowls and the pans and the firepans"** (v50) — Hebrew סִפּוֹת (as Exod 12:22; English has the same word as for כִּיּוֹר in v38), מְזַמְּרוֹת (from זָמַר "prune": for trimming the wicks), מִזְרָקוֹת, כַּפּוֹת (literally "palms", Exod 25:29), מַחְתּוֹת (Exod 25:38).
- **"hinges" kept** (v50) — Hebrew פֹּתוֹת, in this sense only here; uncertain (hinges, or the sockets in which the door pivots turn).
- **"the most holy place" → "the Holy of Holies"** (v50) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים; "place" is not in the Hebrew. "For the temple" is לַהֵיכָל, the front room (6:3).
- **"was ended" → "was completed"** (v51) — Hebrew וַתִּשְׁלַם, of the root "be whole"; another verb than in v22.
- **"the things which David his father had dedicated" → "the holy things of David his father"** (v51) — Hebrew קָדְשֵׁי דָּוִד.

### Notes
- **Solomon's house and the house of Yahweh** — thirteen years for his own house (v1), seven for the house of Yahweh (6:38).
- **Jachin and Boaz** (v21) — two bronze pillars at the porch of the temple.
- **The sea** (v23–26) — a great bronze basin on twelve cattle; its use is given in 2 Chr 4:6.
- **Hiram** (v13) — a craftsman, of the same name as the king of Tyre.
- **Bronze** — 2 Kgs 25:13–17 tells how the Chaldeans broke up and carried off the pillars, the bases and the sea.
