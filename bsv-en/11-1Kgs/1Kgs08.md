## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v23 the word is generic ("there is no elohim like you"); in v60 it has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים ("Yahweh, he is the Elohim"), as Deut 4:35, 39.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, also through the long sentences of the prayer.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v1, 9, 63) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"the chief of the fathers of the children of Israel" → "the chieftains of the fathers of the sons of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאֵי הָאָבוֹת.
- **"which is Zion" → "that is, Zion"** (v1) — Hebrew הִיא צִיּוֹן.
- **"in the month Ethanim" → "in the moon of the Ethanim"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּיֶרַח הָאֵתָנִים: יֶרַח "moon" beside חֹדֶשׁ "month" in the same verse (as 6:38); the old month name has the article.
- **"took up" → "lifted up"** (v3) — Hebrew נָשָׂא, as Josh 3:6.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"; "holy vessels" → "holy utensils"** (v4) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד; כְּלֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the utensils of holiness".
- **"that were assembled unto him" → "who had met together to him"** (v5) — Hebrew הַנּוֹעָדִים עָלָיו, of the root of מוֹעֵד "meeting" (Num 14:35); another verb than "assembled" (v1, 2).
- **"sheep and oxen, that could not be told nor numbered" → "flock and herd, which could not be numbered and could not be counted"** (v5) — Hebrew צֹאן וּבָקָר; סָפַר "number", מָנָה "count".
- **"oracle" → "inner room"; "the most holy place" → "the Holy of Holies"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew דְּבִיר (6:5); קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים. "Place" is not in the Hebrew; so also "the Holy" (v8, 10).
- **"covered the ark" → "screened the ark"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיָּסֹכּוּ … עַל, as Exod 25:20; 40:3.
- **"staves" → "poles"; "they drew out the staves" → "the poles were long"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew וַיַּאֲרִכוּ: "were long" or "they made long"; "heads" (רָאשֵׁי) are the ends.
- **"before the oracle" → "facing the inner room"** (v8) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי.
- **"which Moses put there" → "that Moses laid up there"** (v9) — Hebrew הִנִּחַ, as Exod 16:33, 34.
- **"when the LORD made a covenant" → "where Yahweh cut a covenant"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר כָּרַת יְהוָה עִם: the Hebrew has only "cut with"; "a covenant" is supplied, as the verb alone means this (1 Sam 22:8).
- **"thick darkness" → "dark cloud"** (v12) — Hebrew עֲרָפֶל, as Exod 20:21; Deut 4:11.
- **"The LORD said that he would dwell" → "Yahweh has said that he would abide"** (v12) — Hebrew אָמַר לִשְׁכֹּן, literally "said to abide" (or "meant to abide", as 5:5); שָׁכַן "abide".
- **"I have surely built thee an house to dwell in, a settled place for thee to abide in for ever" → "I have surely built a lofty house for you, an established place for your dwelling forever"** (v13) — Hebrew בָּנֹה בָנִיתִי, literally "building I have built". בֵּית זְבֻל is "a house of loftiness" (זְבֻל: a high dwelling; rare). מָכוֹן "established place" (Exod 15:17). "Forever" is the plural עוֹלָמִים, "ages".
- **"congregation" → "assembly"** (v14, 22, 55, 65) — Hebrew קָהָל; "congregation" is עֵדָה (v5).
- **"Blessed be" → "Blessed is"** (v15, 56) — the Hebrew has no verb.
- **"which spake with his mouth unto David my father, and hath with his hand fulfilled it" → "who spoke with his mouth with David my father, and with his hand has fulfilled"** (v15, 24) — Hebrew אֵת דָּוִד "with David"; מִלֵּא "fill, fulfil" has no object.
- **"I chose no city" → "I did not choose a city"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא־בָחַרְתִּי בְעִיר.
- **"it was in the heart of David" → "it was with the heart of David"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew עִם־לְבַב.
- **"that shall come forth out of thy loins" → "who comes out from your loins"** (v19) — Hebrew הַיֹּצֵא מֵחֲלָצֶיךָ.
- **"hath performed his word … I am risen up" → "has established his word … I have risen"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיָּקֶם … וָאָקֻם: one root, "he made his word stand … and I stood up". "As Yahweh spoke" is דִּבֶּר, not "promised".
- **"I have set there a place" → "I have put there a place"** (v21) — Hebrew שִׂים.
- **"in the presence of" → "in front of"; "spread forth his hands toward heaven" → "spread out his palms to the heavens"** (v22, 38, 54) — Hebrew נֶגֶד; כַּפָּיו "his palms" (Exod 9:29); הַשָּׁמַיִם is always plural.
- **"who keepest covenant and mercy with thy servants" → "keeping the covenant and the chesed for your servants"** (v23) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר הַבְּרִית וְהַחֶסֶד לַעֲבָדֶיךָ, the words of Deut 7:9. "Servants" is עֲבָדִים, the word for slaves; of Yahweh's men "servant".
- **"that thou promisedst him" → "what you spoke to him"** (v24, 25) — Hebrew דִּבַּרְתָּ.
- **"There shall not fail thee a man in my sight to sit on the throne" → "There shall not be cut off for you a man from before me, sitting on the throne"** (v25) — Hebrew לֹא־יִכָּרֵת לְךָ אִישׁ מִלְּפָנַי יֹשֵׁב. "Only if your sons keep their way" is רַק אִם־יִשְׁמְרוּ; "keep" is the verb of v23–25 throughout.
- **"let thy word, I pray thee, be verified" → "let your word, please, be confirmed"** (v26) — Hebrew יֵאָמֶן נָא. "Your word" is the qere; the written form is plural, "your words".
- **"the heaven and heaven of heavens" → "the heavens and the heavens of the heavens"** (v27) — as Deut 10:14. "Contain" is כִּלְכֵּל, of one root with "hold" in v64 and 7:26.
- **"have thou respect unto" → "turn to"** (v28) — Hebrew וּפָנִיתָ אֶל.
- **"supplication" → "plea for favor"** (v28, 30, 38, 45, 49, 52, 54) — Hebrew תְּחִנָּה, the noun of חָנַן "show favor"; the verb הִתְחַנֵּן is "plead" (v33, 47, 59).
- **"hearken" → "listen to" / "hear"** (v28–30, 52) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to"; שָׁמַע alone is "hear" (v30, 32 and on).
- **The requests of the prayer** (v28–53) — the Hebrew uses the forms "and you will turn", "and you, you will hear … and pardon" (perfect with וְ and imperfect), which in a prayer ask; they are rendered as requests, and the pronoun אַתָּה is kept ("you, hear").
- **"hear thou in heaven thy dwelling place" → "hear in the place of your dwelling, in the heavens"** (v30) — Hebrew אֶל־מְקוֹם שִׁבְתְּךָ אֶל־הַשָּׁמַיִם, literally "to the place … to the heavens". In v39, 43, 49 the word is מְכוֹן "established place" (v13).
- **"forgive" → "pardon"** (v30, 34, 36, 39, 50) — Hebrew סָלַח, said only of God.
- **"If any man trespass against his neighbour, and an oath be laid upon him to cause him to swear, and the oath come" → "When a man sins against his neighbor, and he lays on him a curse, to put him under a curse, and the curse comes"** (v31) — Hebrew חָטָא "sin"; אָלָה is a curse, of an oath that calls a curse on the one who breaks it; לְהַאֲלֹתוֹ is the verb of the same root. The Masoretic Text has "and the curse comes" (וּבָא אָלָה).
- **"to bring his way upon his head … to give him" → "to put his way on his head … to give to him"** (v32) — Hebrew לָתֵת "give" both times.
- **"smitten down before the enemy" → "smitten before an enemy"** (v33) — Hebrew בְּהִנָּגֵף … לִפְנֵי אוֹיֵב, as Deut 28:25.
- **"confess thy name" → "give thanks to your name"** (v33, 35) — Hebrew הוֹדָה, "give thanks to, praise, acknowledge"; of sin "confess".
- **"make supplication unto thee" → "plead with you"** (v33, 47) — Hebrew הִתְחַנֵּן.
- **"the land which thou gavest unto their fathers" → "the ground that you gave to their fathers"** (v34, 40) — Hebrew הָאֲדָמָה "ground"; "land" (אֶרֶץ) stands in v36, 48.
- **"When heaven is shut up" → "When the heavens are closed up"** (v35) — Hebrew בְּהֵעָצֵר, as Deut 11:17.
- **"when thou afflictest them" → "because you afflict them"** (v35) — Hebrew כִּי תַעֲנֵם, as pointed; the same letters could be read "when you answer them".
- **"that thou teach them" → "for you teach them"** (v36) — Hebrew כִּי תוֹרֵם.
- **"blasting, mildew, locust, or if there be caterpiller" → "blight, mildew, locusts, devourer"** (v37) — Hebrew שִׁדָּפוֹן יֵרָקוֹן (as Deut 28:22), אַרְבֶּה, חָסִיל. חָסִיל is a kind or a stage of locust, named from חָסַל "devour" (Deut 28:38); which is not known.
- **"besiege them in the land of their cities" → "distresses him in the land of his gates"** (v37) — Hebrew יָצַר־לוֹ … בְּאֶרֶץ שְׁעָרָיו, as Deut 28:52; the Hebrew is singular. The Septuagint has "in one of his cities" (from memory).
- **"the plague of his own heart" → "the plague of his heart"** (v38) — Hebrew נֶגַע לְבָבוֹ: נֶגַע "plague" (literally "a touch, a stroke"), as in v37.
- **"children of men" → "sons of man"** (v39) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הָאָדָם.
- **"in the land" → "on the face of the ground"** (v40) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה (Deut 4:10).
- **"Moreover concerning a stranger" → "And also to the foreigner"** (v41) — Hebrew וְגַם אֶל־הַנָּכְרִי: the sentence is taken up in v43, "you, hear". "On account of your name" is לְמַעַן שְׁמֶךָ.
- **"thy strong hand, and of thy stretched out arm" → "your strong hand and your outstretched arm"** (v42) — as Deut 4:34; 5:15.
- **"this house … is called by thy name" → "your name is called upon this house"** (v43) — Hebrew שִׁמְךָ נִקְרָא עַל.
- **"people of the earth" → "peoples of the earth"** (v43, 53, 60) — Hebrew עַמֵּי הָאָרֶץ, plural.
- **"toward the city" → "in the direction of the city"** (v44, 48) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ "the way of"; "toward" is אֶל (v29, 30, 35, 38, 42).
- **"maintain their cause" → "do their judgment"** (v45, 49, 59) — Hebrew עָשָׂה מִשְׁפָּט: give them their right.
- **"deliver them to the enemy" → "set them before an enemy"** (v46) — Hebrew וּנְתַתָּם לִפְנֵי אוֹיֵב, literally "give them before" (Deut 28:25).
- **"if they shall bethink themselves" → "they bring back to their heart"** (v47) — Hebrew וְהֵשִׁיבוּ אֶל־לִבָּם, as Deut 4:39; 30:1; the verb is of the root of "return" (שׁוּב) in the same verse and in v33, 34, 35, 48.
- **"repent" → "return"** (v47) — Hebrew וְשָׁבוּ.
- **"have done perversely, we have committed wickedness" → "we have done iniquity, we have done wickedly"** (v47) — Hebrew הֶעֱוִינוּ (root of עָוֹן "iniquity"), רָשָׁעְנוּ.
- **"with all their heart, and with all their soul" kept** (v48) — as Deut 4:29; 30:2, 10.
- **"which I have built" kept** (v48) — the qere בָּנִיתִי "I have built"; the written form is בנית "you have built".
- **"give them compassion before them who carried them captive" → "give them for compassion before their captors"** (v50) — Hebrew וּנְתַתָּם לְרַחֲמִים.
- **"the furnace of iron" kept; "thine inheritance" kept** (v51) — as Deut 4:20; 9:26, 29.
- **"O Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v53) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה; the Hebrew has no "O".
- **"from kneeling on his knees" → "from bending down on his knees"** (v54) — Hebrew מִכְּרֹעַ עַל־בִּרְכָּיו, as Judg 7:6.
- **"hath given rest" → "has given a resting place"** (v56) — Hebrew מְנוּחָה, the noun (Deut 12:9).
- **"there hath not failed one word of all his good promise, which he promised" → "not one word has fallen of all his good word that he spoke"** (v56) — Hebrew לֹא־נָפַל דָּבָר אֶחָד מִכֹּל דְּבָרוֹ הַטּוֹב, as Josh 21:45; 23:14.
- **"let him not leave us, nor forsake us" → "let him not forsake us and let him not abandon us"** (v57) — Hebrew עָזַב "forsake", נָטַשׁ "abandon".
- **"That he may incline our hearts unto him" → "to turn our heart to him"** (v58) — Hebrew לְהַטּוֹת לְבָבֵנוּ אֵלָיו; "heart" is singular.
- **"judgments" → "ordinances"** (v58) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים, of rulings; "judgment" in v45, 49, 59 is the same word.
- **"as the matter shall require" → "the matter of a day in its day"** (v59) — Hebrew דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ, as Exod 5:13; 16:4.
- **"that the LORD is God, and that there is none else" → "that Yahweh, he is Elohim; there is none else"** (v60) — Hebrew יְהוָה הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים אֵין עוֹד, as Deut 4:35, 39.
- **"Let your heart therefore be perfect with" → "And your heart shall be whole with"** (v61) — Hebrew וְהָיָה לְבַבְכֶם שָׁלֵם עִם: שָׁלֵם "whole, complete".
- **"offered sacrifice" → "were sacrificing a sacrifice"** (v62) — Hebrew זֹבְחִים זֶבַח, a participle.
- **"two and twenty thousand oxen, and an hundred and twenty thousand sheep" → "of the herd, twenty-two thousand, and of the flock, a hundred and twenty thousand"** (v63) — Hebrew בָּקָר, צֹאן, the collective words; צֹאן covers sheep and goats.
- **"hallow" → "sanctified"; "meat offerings" → "grain offering"; "the fat" → "the fat parts"** (v64) — Hebrew קִדַּשׁ; מִנְחָה; חֶלְבֵי, plural. "He offered" is עָשָׂה "did, made". "To hold" is מֵהָכִיל (7:26).
- **"held a feast" → "made the feast"** (v65) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ … אֶת־הֶחָג, as Deut 16:13.
- **"from the entering in of Hamath unto the river of Egypt" → "from Lebo-hamath to the wadi of Egypt"** (v65) — Hebrew מִלְּבוֹא חֲמָת עַד־נַחַל מִצְרַיִם, as Num 34:5, 8.
- **"seven days and seven days, even fourteen days" kept** (v65) — as the Masoretic Text has it; "on the eighth day" follows in v66.
- **"joyful and glad of heart for all the goodness" → "rejoicing and good of heart over all the good"** (v66) — Hebrew שְׂמֵחִים וְטוֹבֵי לֵב עַל כָּל־הַטּוֹבָה.

### Notes
- **The ark brought in** — the ark is set under the cherubim, and the glory of Yahweh fills the house.
- **"The cloud filled the house"** (v10–11) — as with the dwelling in Exod 40:34–35.
- **Solomon's prayer** (v22–53) — seven cases: the curse between neighbors, defeat, no rain, famine and plague, the foreigner, battle, captivity.
- **"The heavens cannot contain you"** (v27) — the house is the place toward which prayer is made; the hearing is "in the heavens".
- **Captivity** (v46–51) — the prayer looks ahead to a people taken captive who pray toward the land, the city and the house; compare Deut 30:1–10.
- **The sacrifice** (v63) — 22,000 of the herd and 120,000 of the flock.
- **The feast** (v2, 65) — the feast of the seventh month (Lev 23:34).
