# 1 Kings 9

[1] And it came to pass, when ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had finished building the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the house of the king and all the longing of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ that he delighted to do,

[2] that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ a second time, as he had appeared to him at ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧.

[3] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "I have heard your prayer and your plea for favor, with which you have pleaded before me. I have sanctified this house that you have built, to put my name there forever, and my eyes and my heart shall be there all the days.

[4] And you, if you walk before me as David your father walked, in integrity of heart and in uprightness, to do according to all that I have commanded you, and my statutes and my ordinances you keep,

[5] then I will raise up the throne of your kingdom over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ forever, as I spoke concerning David your father, saying, 'There shall not be cut off for you a man from upon the throne of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'

[6] If you surely turn back, you and your sons, from after me, and do not keep my commandments, my statutes, that I have set before you, and you go and serve other gods and bow down to them,

[7] then I will cut off ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from upon the face of the ground that I have given to them, and the house that I have sanctified for my name I will send away from before my face, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall become a proverb and a taunt among all the peoples.

[8] And this house shall be high; everyone who passes by it shall be appalled and shall hiss, and they shall say, 'For what has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ done thus to this land and to this house?'

[9] And they shall say, 'Because they forsook ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought their fathers out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and they held fast to other gods and bowed down to them and served them; therefore ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has brought upon them all this evil.'"

[10] And it came to pass at the end of twenty years, in which ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had built the two houses, the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the house of the king

[11] — ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ king of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ had supplied ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ with cedar wood and with cypress wood and with gold, according to all his delight — that then King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ gave to ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ twenty cities in the land of the ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧.

[12] And ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ went out from ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ to see the cities that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had given him, and they were not right in his eyes.

[13] And he said, "What are these cities that you have given me, my brother?" And he called them the land of ⟦cabul|Kavul|Cabul⟧, to this day.

[14] And ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ sent to the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold.

[15] And this is the matter of the forced labor that King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ brought up, to build the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and his house and the Millo and the wall of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧ and Megiddo and Gezer.

[16] ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ had gone up and captured Gezer and burned it with fire, and the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ who dwelt in the city he had killed, and he had given it as a parting gift to his daughter, the wife of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[17] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built Gezer and Lower ⟦beth-horon|Beit Choron|Beth-horon⟧

[18] and ⟦baalath|Baalat|Baalath⟧ and Tadmor in the wilderness, in the land,

[19] and all the store cities that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had, and the cities of the chariots and the cities of the horsemen, and the longing of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ that he longed to build in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ and in all the land of his rule.

[20] All the people who remained from the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧, the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧, the ⟦hivites|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧, who were not of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧

[21] — their sons who remained after them in the land, whom the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were not able to devote to destruction — ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ brought them up to be slaves at forced labor, to this day.

[22] And of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made no one a slave, for they were the men of war and his servants and his commanders and his officers and the commanders of his chariots and his horsemen.

[26] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made a fleet at ⟦ezion-geber|Etzyon Gever|Ezion-geber⟧, which is beside ⟦eloth|Eilot|Eloth⟧, on the shore of the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧, in the land of Edom.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"And it came to pass"** (v1, 10) — Hebrew וַיְהִי.
- **"all Solomon's desire which he was pleased to do" → "all the longing of Solomon that he delighted to do"** (v1, 19) — Hebrew חֵשֶׁק "longing" (root חָשַׁק "be bound to, long for") and חָפֵץ "delight". In v19 noun and verb are one root: "the longing … that he longed to build".
- **"appeared"** (v2) — Hebrew נִרְאָה, literally "was seen by"; the first time was at Gibeon (3:5).
- **"thy supplication, that thou hast made before me" → "your plea for favor, with which you have pleaded before me"** (v3) — Hebrew תְּחִנָּה and הִתְחַנֵּן, noun and verb of one root (חָנַן "show favor"): to seek favor for oneself.
- **"hallowed" → "sanctified"** (v3, 7) — Hebrew הִקְדִּישׁ, "make holy". **"to put my name there for ever"** — the infinitive is kept; **"perpetually" → "all the days"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים.
- **"and wilt keep my statutes and my judgments" → "and my statutes and my ordinances you keep"** (v4) — Hebrew חֻקַּי וּמִשְׁפָּטַי תִּשְׁמֹר, joined without "and" ("and" is supplied); it may still belong to the "if". מִשְׁפָּט is "judgment"; of a ruling given as law it is "ordinance", as in Deuteronomy.
- **"as I promised to David" → "as I spoke concerning David"** (v5) — Hebrew דִּבַּרְתִּי עַל־דָּוִד: "spoke"; עַל is "concerning" (or "to").
- **"There shall not fail thee a man upon the throne" → "There shall not be cut off for you a man from upon the throne"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־יִכָּרֵת לְךָ אִישׁ מֵעַל כִּסֵּא, word for word (as 2:4; 8:25). **"I will establish the throne" → "I will raise up the throne"** — Hebrew הֵקִים, literally "make stand"; "establish" is kept for the other verb (כּוֹנֵן) of the promise in 2 Sam 7:13.
- **"if ye shall at all turn from following me" → "If you surely turn back … from after me"** (v6) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb, שׁוֹב תְּשֻׁבוּן, literally "turning back you turn back". From here "you" is plural (Solomon and Israel); in v3–5 it is singular. **"my commandments, my statutes"** — no "and" in the Hebrew. **"set before you"** — Hebrew נָתַן לִפְנֵי; the verb is "give".
- **"worship" → "bow down"** (v6, 9) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה. In v9 the written text has the singular (וישתחו), the reading tradition the plural, "and they bowed down"; the sense is the same.
- **"out of the land" → "from upon the face of the ground"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵעַל פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה. **"will I cast out of my sight" → "I will send away from before my face"** — Hebrew אֲשַׁלַּח מֵעַל פָּנָי, the verb "send away".
- **"a proverb and a byword" → "a proverb and a taunt"** (v7) — Hebrew לְמָשָׁל וְלִשְׁנִינָה, as Deut 28:37; שְׁנִינָה is literally "a sharp word".
- **"And at this house, which is high" → "And this house shall be high"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהַבַּיִת הַזֶּה יִהְיֶה עֶלְיוֹן, literally "and this house shall be highest / uppermost". The sentence is hard, since what follows is a threat; the parallel 2 Chr 7:21 has "this house, which was high". Some old versions read "shall become ruins" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"shall be astonished" → "shall be appalled"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁמֵם. **"Why" → "For what"** — Hebrew עַל־מֶה, as Deut 29:24.
- **"have taken hold upon other gods" → "held fast to other gods"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶחֱזִיק בְּ.
- **"had furnished" → "had supplied"** (v11) — Hebrew נִשָּׂא, literally "lifted up, carried". **"cedar trees and fir trees" → "cedar wood and cypress wood"** — Hebrew עֲצֵי אֲרָזִים / עֲצֵי בְרוֹשִׁים, literally "woods of cedars / of cypresses"; the kind of tree meant by בְּרוֹשׁ is not certain (cypress or juniper). **"desire" → "delight"** — Hebrew חֵפֶץ. **"the land of the Galilee"** — the Hebrew has the article, הַגָּלִיל "the circle, the district".
- **"they pleased him not" → "they were not right in his eyes"** (v12) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָשְׁרוּ בְּעֵינָיו.
- **"the land of Cabul"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ כָּבוּל. The meaning of the name is not known ("as good as nothing" is an old guess); no explanation stands in the Hebrew. "He called" may also be "one called".
- **"sixscore" → "a hundred and twenty"** (v14). **"talents"** — Hebrew כִּכָּר, a weight (literally "a round").
- **"the reason of the levy which king Solomon raised" → "the matter of the forced labor that King Solomon brought up"** (v15, 21) — Hebrew דְּבַר־הַמַּס אֲשֶׁר־הֶעֱלָה: מַס is forced labor, not a tax; דָּבָר is "word / matter"; הֶעֱלָה is literally "made go up" (as 5:13): he called the men up. **"his own house" → "his house"** — Hebrew בֵּיתוֹ.
- **"the Canaanites" → "the Canaanite"** (v16); **"the Amorite, the Hittite …"** (v20) — the Hebrew names each people in the singular with the article.
- **"for a present" → "as a parting gift"** (v16) — Hebrew שִׁלֻּחִים, literally "sendings away": what a father gives his daughter when he sends her to her husband (cf. Mic 1:14).
- **"nether" → "Lower"** (v17).
- **"Tadmor"** (v18) — the reading tradition (qere) has תַּדְמֹר Tadmor, as 2 Chr 8:4; the written text (ketiv) has תמר Tamar, a place in the south of Judah (Ezek 47:19). The qere is translated. "In the wilderness, in the land" is word for word; which land is meant is not said.
- **"cities of store" → "store cities"** (v19) — Hebrew עָרֵי הַמִּסְכְּנוֹת, as Exod 1:11. **"cities for his chariots, and cities for his horsemen" → "the cities of the chariots and the cities of the horsemen"** — no "his" in the Hebrew. **"dominion" → "rule"** — Hebrew מֶמְשָׁלָה.
- **"children" → "sons"** (v20, 21, 22) — Hebrew בְּנֵי / בְּנֵיהֶם. **"were left" → "remained"** — Hebrew נוֹתַר.
- **"utterly to destroy" → "to devote to destruction"** (v21) — Hebrew הֶחֱרִים: give over to Yahweh beyond recall.
- **"a tribute of bondservice" → "to be slaves at forced labor"** (v21) — Hebrew לְמַס־עֹבֵד, literally "for forced labor of one who serves" (Gen 49:15; Josh 16:10).
- **"did Solomon make no bondmen" → "Solomon made no one a slave"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹא־נָתַן … עָבֶד, literally "gave no slave". **"his servants"** — the same word עֶבֶד, of the king's men. **"his princes, and his captains, and rulers of his chariots" → "his commanders and his officers and the commanders of his chariots"** — Hebrew שַׂר ("prince", of an army "commander") twice; שָׁלִישׁ "officer" is literally "third man" (the third in a chariot, or a rank; uncertain).
- **"the chief of the officers" → "the chiefs of the prefects"** (v23) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַנִּצָּבִים, literally "the chiefs of the ones set in place, stationed" (the word of 4:5, 7; the same phrase in 5:16). **"bare rule over" → "had dominion over"** — Hebrew רָדָה.
- **"But" → "Only"** (v24) — Hebrew אַךְ. **"which Solomon had built" → "that he had built"** — the Hebrew names no subject.
- **"did Solomon offer" → "would offer up"** (v25) — Hebrew וְהֶעֱלָה, the form of a repeated act; literally "make go up", one root with "burnt offering" (עֹלָה). **"and he burnt incense upon the altar that was before the LORD" → "and make smoke with it, which was before Yahweh"** — Hebrew וְהַקְטֵיר אִתּוֹ אֲשֶׁר לִפְנֵי יְהוָה, word for word; the clause is obscure. הִקְטִיר is "turn into smoke" (of sacrifices and of incense alike) and has no object here; אִתּוֹ is "with it / with him"; "which was before Yahweh" is commonly referred to an altar. **"So he finished the house" → "and he completed the house"** — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּם, "made whole" (root of *shalom*).
- **"a navy of ships" → "a fleet"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew אֳנִי, a collective, "ships". **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** — Hebrew יַם־סוּף. **"shore"** — Hebrew שְׂפַת, literally "lip". **"beside Eloth"** — Hebrew אֶת־אֵלוֹת, "with / by".
- **"shipmen that had knowledge of the sea" → "men of ships who knew the sea"** (v27) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי אֳנִיּוֹת יֹדְעֵי הַיָּם.
- **"fetched" → "took"** (v28) — Hebrew לָקַח.

### Notes
- **Second theophany** — like Gibeon (ch 3), Yahweh appears to Solomon. Sanction + warning.
- **Conditional covenant** (v6–9) — if Solomon or his sons turn away, Israel will be cut off and the house sent away from Yahweh's face. Exile anticipated from the temple's very dedication.
- **Cabul cities** — the cities are not right in Hiram's eyes. International relations stress point.
- **Hazor, Megiddo, Gezer** (v15) — archaeology has found massive gates of similar plan at these sites; their dating to Solomon is debated.
- **Ophir gold** — legendary source; location debated (Yemen, Somalia, India). Red Sea expedition.
- **Pharaoh's daughter's house** (v24) — she goes up from the city of David to her own house; 2 Chr 8:11 gives a reason (the holiness of the places the ark had come to).
