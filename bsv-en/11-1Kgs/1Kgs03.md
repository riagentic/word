# 1 Kings 3

[1] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ became son-in-law to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and he took the daughter of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and brought her to the city of David, until he had finished building his house and the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the wall of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ all around.

[2] Only, the people were sacrificing on the high places, for a house had not been built for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ until those days.

[3] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ loved ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to walk in the statutes of David his father; only, on the high places he was sacrificing and making smoke.

[4] And the king went to ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ to sacrifice there, for that was the great high place; a thousand burnt offerings ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ would offer up on that altar.

[5] In ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in a dream of the night, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Ask what I shall give you."

[6] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ said, "You have done with your servant David my father great ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, as he walked before you in truth and in righteousness and in uprightness of heart with you; and you have kept for him this great ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and have given him a son sitting on his throne, as at this day.

[7] And now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you have made your servant king in the place of David my father, and I am a small boy; I do not know going out and coming in.

[8] And your servant is in the midst of your people whom you have chosen, a numerous people, that cannot be counted and cannot be numbered for multitude.

[9] And you shall give to your servant a hearing heart, to judge your people, to understand between good and evil; for who is able to judge this heavy people of yours?"

[10] And the thing was good in the eyes of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had asked this thing.

[11] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to him, "Because you have asked this thing, and have not asked for yourself many days, and have not asked for yourself riches, and have not asked the soul of your enemies, and have asked for yourself understanding, to hear judgment,

[12] behold, I have done according to your words. Behold, I have given you a heart wise and understanding, so that like you there has been none before you, and after you there shall not arise one like you.

[13] And also what you have not asked I have given you, both riches and glory, so that there has not been a man like you among the kings, all your days.

[14] And if you walk in my ways, to keep my statutes and my commandments, as David your father walked, then I will prolong your days."

[15] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ awoke, and behold, a dream. And he came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and stood before the ark of the covenant of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, and he offered up burnt offerings and offered peace offerings and made a feast for all his servants.

[16] Then two women, prostitutes, came to the king and stood before him.

[17] And the one woman said, "Oh, my lord, I and this woman dwell in one house, and I gave birth with her in the house.

[18] And it came to pass on the third day after my giving birth that this woman also gave birth. And we were together; there was no stranger with us in the house, except the two of us in the house.

[19] And the son of this woman died at night, because she lay on him.

[20] And she arose in the midst of the night and took my son from beside me, and your slave woman was asleep, and she laid him in her bosom, and her dead son she laid in my bosom.

[21] And I arose in the morning to nurse my son, and behold, he was dead. And I looked closely at him in the morning, and behold, he was not my son whom I had borne."

[22] And the other woman said, "No, for my son is the living one, and your son is the dead one." And this one was saying, "No, for your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one." And they spoke before the king.

[23] And the king said, "This one is saying, 'This is my son, the living one, and your son is the dead one'; and this one is saying, 'No, for your son is the dead one, and my son is the living one.'"

[24] And the king said, "Get me a sword." And they brought the sword before the king.

[25] And the king said, "Cut the living child in two, and give the half to one and the half to the other."

[26] And the woman whose son was the living one said to the king, for her compassion was stirred over her son, and she said, "Oh, my lord, give her the living newborn, and surely do not put him to death!" And this one was saying, "Neither mine nor yours shall he be; cut!"

[27] And the king answered and said, "Give her the living newborn, and surely do not put him to death; she is his mother."

[28] And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ heard the judgment that the king had judged, and they feared because of the king, for they saw that the wisdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was within him to do judgment.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 7, 11, 28) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"made affinity with Pharaoh" → "became son-in-law to Pharaoh"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּתְחַתֵּן אֶת, from חָתָן "son-in-law" (as 1 Sam 18:21). **"until he had made an end of building his own house" → "until he had finished building his house"** — Hebrew כַּלֹּתוֹ; no "own". **"round about" → "all around"**.
- **"in high places" → "on the high places"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew בַּבָּמוֹת, with the article. **"there was no house built" → "a house had not been built"**.
- **"walking in the statutes" → "to walk in the statutes"** (v3) — Hebrew לָלֶכֶת, an infinitive. **"he sacrificed and burnt incense" → "he was sacrificing and making smoke"** — Hebrew מְזַבֵּחַ וּמַקְטִיר, two participles; הִקְטִיר is "make go up in smoke", said of what is laid on an altar and of incense alike, and no object is written here.
- **"did Solomon offer" → "Solomon would offer up"** (v4) — Hebrew יַעֲלֶה, an imperfect: what he used to do, or what he did then; הֶעֱלָה עֹלָה, "make a burnt offering go up", verb and noun of one root.
- **"in a dream by night" → "in a dream of the night"** (v5) — Hebrew בַּחֲלוֹם הַלָּיְלָה. The verse has both names: Yahweh appeared, and Elohim said.
- **"Thou hast shewed unto thy servant David my father great mercy … this great kindness" → "You have done with your servant David my father great chesed … this great chesed"** (v6) — Hebrew עָשִׂיתָ עִם … חֶסֶד גָּדוֹל, and הַחֶסֶד הַגָּדוֹל הַזֶּה: one word both times. **"that thou hast given him a son to sit on his throne" → "and have given him a son sitting on his throne"** — Hebrew וַתִּתֶּן־לוֹ, a plain "and"; יֹשֵׁב, a participle. **"as it is this day" → "as at this day"**.
- **"I am but a little child: I know not how to go out or come in" → "I am a small boy; I do not know going out and coming in"** (v7) — Hebrew נַעַר קָטֹן; נַעַר is a boy or a young man, and the words say how he stands before the task, not his age in years. צֵאת וָבֹא are two infinitives: the going out and coming in of one who leads (as 1 Sam 18:13, 16).
- **"a great people, that cannot be numbered nor counted" → "a numerous people, that cannot be counted and cannot be numbered"** (v8) — Hebrew עַם־רָב; יִמָּנֶה "be counted", יִסָּפֵר "be numbered".
- **"Give therefore thy servant an understanding heart" → "And you shall give to your servant a hearing heart"** (v9) — Hebrew וְנָתַתָּ, "and you shall give": the request is made in the form of a statement; לֵב שֹׁמֵעַ, "a heart that hears". **"that I may discern between good and bad" → "to understand between good and evil"** — Hebrew לְהָבִין, an infinitive without "I": the verb of "understanding" (v11, 12); רַע "evil". **"this thy so great a people" → "this heavy people of yours"** — Hebrew הַכָּבֵד, "heavy": weighty, hard to bear, or great in number.
- **"the speech pleased the Lord" → "the thing was good in the eyes of Adonai"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיִּיטַב הַדָּבָר בְּעֵינֵי אֲדֹנָי; הַדָּבָר is "the word" or "the thing", the same word as "this thing" at the end of the verse.
- **"long life … the life of thine enemies" → "many days … the soul of your enemies"** (v11) — Hebrew יָמִים רַבִּים; נֶפֶשׁ "soul". **"but hast asked for thyself understanding to discern judgment" → "and have asked for yourself understanding, to hear judgment"** — Hebrew הָבִין לִשְׁמֹעַ מִשְׁפָּט, literally "to understand, to hear judgment": to hear a case and judge it. The Hebrew has "and", not "but".
- **"lo, I have given thee a wise and an understanding heart" → "Behold, I have given you a heart wise and understanding"** (v12) — Hebrew הִנֵּה twice.
- **"both riches, and honour: so that there shall not be any among the kings like unto thee" → "both riches and glory, so that there has not been a man like you among the kings"** (v13) — Hebrew כָּבוֹד "glory"; לֹא־הָיָה, a perfect, "there has not been", though "all your days" looks forward. The form is kept as it stands; it is commonly translated "there shall not be".
- **"then I will lengthen thy days" → "then I will prolong your days"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהַאֲרַכְתִּי, as Deut 4:40.
- **"behold, it was a dream" → "behold, a dream"** (v15) — the Hebrew has no verb. **"the ark of the covenant of the LORD" → "the ark of the covenant of Adonai"** — the Masoretic Text has אֲדֹנָי here (and in v10), not the name Yahweh. **"offered peace offerings"** — the verb is עָשָׂה "do, make", the same as "made a feast"; "offered up" before it is הֶעֱלָה.
- **"two women, that were harlots" → "two women, prostitutes"** (v16) — Hebrew נָשִׁים זֹנוֹת.
- **"I was delivered of a child with her in the house" → "I gave birth with her in the house"** (v17) — Hebrew וָאֵלֵד עִמָּהּ; no "child" is written.
- **"save we two in the house" → "except the two of us in the house"** (v18) — Hebrew זוּלָתִי שְׁתַּיִם־אֲנַחְנוּ, "except two, we". **"no stranger"** — Hebrew זָר.
- **"this woman's child died in the night; because she overlaid it" → "the son of this woman died at night, because she lay on him"** (v19) — Hebrew בֶּן "son"; שָׁכְבָה עָלָיו.
- **"at midnight" → "in the midst of the night"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ הַלַּיְלָה. **"while thine handmaid slept" → "and your slave woman was asleep"** — Hebrew אָמָה; a plain "and". **"her dead child" → "her dead son"**.
- **"to give my child suck" → "to nurse my son"** (v21). **"when I had considered it" → "I looked closely at him"** — Hebrew וָאֶתְבּוֹנֵן אֵלָיו, from the root of "understand".
- **"Nay; but the living is my son" → "No, for my son is the living one"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew לֹא כִי בְּנִי הַחַי. **"And this said" → "And this one was saying"** (v22, 26) — Hebrew אֹמֶרֶת, a participle.
- **"The one saith … and the other saith" → "This one is saying … and this one is saying"** (v23) — Hebrew זֹאת … וְזֹאת, "this one" both times.
- **"Bring me a sword" → "Get me a sword"** (v24) — Hebrew קְחוּ לִי, literally "take for me".
- **"Divide the living child in two" → "Cut the living child in two"** (v25, 26) — Hebrew גִּזְרוּ, "cut". **"half to the one, and half to the other"** — Hebrew אֶת־הַחֲצִי לְאַחַת … לְאֶחָת, "the half to one and the half to one".
- **"her bowels yearned upon her son" → "her compassion was stirred over her son"** (v26) — Hebrew נִכְמְרוּ רַחֲמֶיהָ, as Gen 43:30; רַחֲמִים "compassion" is of one root with "womb". **"the living child" → "the living newborn"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew הַיָּלוּד, "the one born", another word than יֶלֶד "child" in v25. **"in no wise slay it" → "surely do not put him to death"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew הָמֵת אַל־תְּמִיתֻהוּ, the verb with its infinitive ("putting to death, do not put him to death"). **"Let it be neither mine nor thine, but divide it" → "Neither mine nor yours shall he be; cut!"** — Hebrew גְּזֹרוּ, one word, with no object.
- **"Give her the living child"** (v27) — the king takes up the words of the first woman (v26); "her" is the one who spoke them.
- **"they feared the king" → "they feared because of the king"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיִּרְאוּ מִפְּנֵי, literally "feared from the face of" (as 1:50). **"in him" → "within him"** — Hebrew בְּקִרְבּוֹ. **"heard of the judgment"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע with a plain object, "heard the judgment".

### Notes
- **Pharaoh's daughter** (v1) — Solomon's marriage with the house of the king of Egypt (cf. 11:1–2).
- **High places** (v2–4) — the people and the king sacrifice there, "for a house had not been built for the name of Yahweh".
- **Gibeon** — the great high place; 1 Chr 16:39; 21:29 place the dwelling of Yahweh there.
- **Dream at Gibeon** — Solomon asks for a hearing heart; he is given wisdom, and riches and glory that he did not ask.
- **"A hearing heart"** (v9) — Hebrew לֵב שֹׁמֵעַ: a heart that hears, to judge.
- **The judgment of the two women** (v16–28) — all Israel sees "that the wisdom of Elohim was within him to do judgment".
