# 1 Kings 11

[1] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ loved many foreign women, and the daughter of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧: ⟦moabites|Moavi|Moabite⟧, ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧, ⟦edomites|Edomi|Edomite⟧, ⟦sidonians|Tzidoni|Sidonian⟧, ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ women,

[2] from the nations of which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "You shall not go in among them, and they shall not go in among you; surely they will turn your heart after their gods." To them ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ clung in love.

[3] And he had seven hundred wives, princesses, and three hundred concubines; and his wives turned his heart.

[4] And it came to pass, at the time of the old age of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, that his wives turned his heart after other gods, and his heart was not whole with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, as the heart of David his father.

[5] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ went after ⟦ashtoreth|Ashtoret|Ashtoreth⟧, the god of the ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧, and after ⟦milcom|Milkom|Milcom⟧, the detestable thing of the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧.

[6] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and did not follow ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ fully, as David his father.

[7] Then ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built a high place for ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧, the detestable thing of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, on the mountain that faces ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and for Molech, the detestable thing of the sons of Ammon.

[8] And so he did for all his foreign wives, who were making smoke and sacrificing to their gods.

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was angry with ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, because his heart had turned from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who had appeared to him twice

[10] and had commanded him concerning this thing, not to go after other gods; and he did not keep what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded.

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, "Because this has been with you, and you have not kept my covenant and my statutes that I commanded you, I will surely tear the kingdom from upon you and give it to your servant.

[12] Only, in your days I will not do it, on account of David your father; from the hand of your son I will tear it.

[13] Only, all the kingdom I will not tear away; one tribe I will give to your son, on account of David my servant and on account of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, which I have chosen."

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ raised up an adversary to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, Hadad the ⟦edomites|Edomi|Edomite⟧; he was of the seed of the king in Edom.

[15] And it came to pass, when David was with Edom, when ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ the commander of the host went up to bury the slain, and he struck down every male in Edom

[16] — for ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ dwelt there six months, until he had cut off every male in Edom —

[17] that Hadad fled, he and ⟦edomites|Edomi|Edomite⟧ men of the servants of his father with him, to go to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and Hadad was a small boy.

[18] And they arose from ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ and came to Paran, and they took men with them from Paran and came to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and he gave him a house, and bread he assigned to him, and land he gave him.

[19] And Hadad found favor in the eyes of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ greatly, and he gave him as wife the sister of his wife, the sister of ⟦tahpenes|Tachpenes|Tahpenes⟧ the great lady.

[20] And the sister of ⟦tahpenes|Tachpenes|Tahpenes⟧ bore him ⟦genubath|Genuvat|Genubath⟧ his son, and ⟦tahpenes|Tachpenes|Tahpenes⟧ weaned him in the midst of the house of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧; and ⟦genubath|Genuvat|Genubath⟧ was in the house of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, in the midst of the sons of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧.

[21] And Hadad heard in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ that David lay with his fathers and that ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ the commander of the host was dead, and Hadad said to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, "Send me away, and let me go to my land."

[22] And ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ said to him, "But what are you lacking with me, that behold, you are seeking to go to your land?" And he said, "No; but you shall surely send me away."

[23] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ raised up an adversary to him, Rezon the son of ⟦eliada|Elyada|Eliada⟧, who had fled from Hadadezer king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧, his lord.

[24] And he gathered men to himself and became chief of a raiding band, when David killed them; and they went to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ and dwelt in it and reigned in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧.

[25] And he was an adversary to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ all the days of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, together with the evil that Hadad did; and he loathed ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and reigned over ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧.

[26] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧, an ⟦ephraimite|Efrati|Ephraimite⟧ from ⟦zeredah|Tzeredah|Zeredah⟧ — and the name of his mother was ⟦zeruah|Tzeruah|Zeruah⟧, a widow woman — a servant of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, he raised a hand against the king.

[27] And this is the matter in which he raised a hand against the king: ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built the Millo; he closed the breach of the city of David his father.

[28] And the man ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ was a mighty one of ability; and ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ saw the young man, that he was a doer of work, and he appointed him over all the burden of the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[29] And it came to pass at that time that ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ went out from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧ the ⟦shilonite|Shiloni|Shilonite⟧, the prophet, found him on the way; and he was covering himself with a new garment, and the two of them were alone in the field.

[30] And ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧ seized the new garment that was on him and tore it into twelve pieces.

[31] And he said to ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, "Take for yourself ten pieces, for thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'Behold, I am tearing the kingdom from the hand of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, and I will give to you the ten tribes,

[32] and the one tribe shall be his, on account of my servant David and on account of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the city that I have chosen out of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[33] because they have forsaken me and bowed down to ⟦ashtoreth|Ashtoret|Ashtoreth⟧ the god of the ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧, to ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧ the god of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and to ⟦milcom|Milkom|Milcom⟧ the god of the sons of Ammon, and they have not walked in my ways, to do what is right in my eyes and my statutes and my ordinances, as David his father.

[34] And I will not take all the kingdom from his hand, for I will set him as chieftain all the days of his life, on account of David my servant, whom I chose, who kept my commandments and my statutes.

[35] And I will take the kingship from the hand of his son and give it to you, the ten tribes.

[36] And to his son I will give one tribe, in order that there may be a lamp for David my servant all the days before me in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the city that I have chosen for myself, to put my name there.

[37] And you I will take, and you shall reign over all that your soul desires, and you shall be king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[38] And it shall be, if you hear all that I command you and walk in my ways and do what is right in my eyes, to keep my statutes and my commandments, as David my servant did, then I will be with you and build for you a sure house, as I built for David, and I will give ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to you.

[39] And I will afflict the seed of David on account of this; only, not all the days.'"

[40] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sought to put ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ to death; and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ arose and fled to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, to ⟦shishak|Shishaq|Shishak⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and he was in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ until the death of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[41] And the rest of the acts of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, and all that he did, and his wisdom, are they not written in the book of the acts of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧?

[42] And the days that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were forty years.

[43] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ lay with his fathers and was buried in the city of David his father, and ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"strange women" → "foreign women"** (v1, 8) — Hebrew נָכְרִיּוֹת. **"together with the daughter of Pharaoh" → "and the daughter of Pharaoh"** — Hebrew וְאֶת, "and". אִשָּׁה is "woman" and "wife": one word in v1, 3, 4, 8.
- **"Ye shall not go in to them, neither shall they come in unto you" → "You shall not go in among them, and they shall not go in among you"** (v2) — Hebrew לֹא־תָבֹאוּ בָהֶם … לֹא־יָבֹאוּ בָכֶם, the same verb twice (as Josh 23:7, 12): mixing with the nations; marriage is not named. **"turn away your heart" → "turn your heart"** (v2, 3, 4) — Hebrew הִטָּה "turn, incline"; "away" is not in the Hebrew. **"clave unto these in love" → "To them Solomon clung in love"** — Hebrew בָּהֶם דָּבַק … לְאַהֲבָה, "to them" first; literally "for loving".
- **"when Solomon was old" → "at the time of the old age of Solomon"** (v4) — Hebrew לְעֵת זִקְנַת שְׁלֹמֹה. **"perfect" → "whole"** — Hebrew שָׁלֵם (root of *shalom*): undivided.
- **"the goddess of the Zidonians" → "the god of the Sidonians"** (v5, 33) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי, the ordinary word "god(s) of", used here of a female deity; biblical Hebrew has no word "goddess". In v33 the same word stands for all three.
- **"abomination" → "detestable thing"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew שִׁקֻּץ, as Deut 29:17; "abomination" is תּוֹעֵבָה.
- **"Milcom" (v5, 33) / "Molech" (v7)** — the Hebrew has מִלְכֹּם in v5 and v33 and מֹלֶךְ in v7, both of the god of Ammon; each is kept as printed. Some old versions read "Milcom" in v7 also (from memory).
- **"did evil in the sight of the LORD" → "did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew הָרַע, with the article. **"went not fully after" → "did not follow … fully"** — Hebrew לֹא מִלֵּא אַחֲרֵי, literally "did not fill after" (as Num 14:24).
- **"in the hill that is before Jerusalem" → "on the mountain that faces Jerusalem"** (v7) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי, "on the face of, facing"; the east is not named. **"Then … built"** — Hebrew אָז יִבְנֶה.
- **"which burnt incense and sacrificed" → "who were making smoke and sacrificing"** (v8) — Hebrew מַקְטִירוֹת וּמְזַבְּחוֹת, two participles. הִקְטִיר is "turn into smoke" (of sacrifices and of incense alike) and has no object here.
- **"was turned from" → "had turned from"** (v9) — Hebrew נָטָה … מֵעִם, literally "from with".
- **"Forasmuch as this is done of thee" → "Because this has been with you"** (v11) — Hebrew הָיְתָה־זֹּאת עִמָּךְ, word for word: this was your mind. **"I will surely rend"** — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb, קָרֹעַ אֶקְרַע, literally "tearing I will tear". **"from upon you"** — Hebrew מֵעָלֶיךָ, as 1 Sam 15:28. **"commanded you"** — Hebrew צִוִּיתִי עָלֶיךָ, literally "commanded upon you".
- **"Notwithstanding" / "Howbeit" → "Only"** (v12, 13, 39) — Hebrew אַךְ (v12, 39) and רַק (v13); English has one word for the two.
- **"for David thy father's sake" → "on account of David your father"** (v12, 13, 32, 34) — Hebrew לְמַעַן.
- **"stirred up an adversary" → "raised up an adversary"** (v14, 23) — Hebrew וַיָּקֶם … שָׂטָן: הֵקִים "make stand, raise up"; שָׂטָן *satan* is a common noun here, "adversary, one who stands against" (as Num 22:22). In v14 the subject is Yahweh, in v23 *Elohim*. **"the king's seed"** — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **"when David was in Edom" → "when David was with Edom"** (v15) — Hebrew בִּהְיוֹת דָּוִד אֶת־אֱדוֹם, word for word. The Septuagint reads "when David destroyed Edom" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. **"captain of the host" → "commander of the host"** (v15, 21) — Hebrew שַׂר הַצָּבָא. **"smitten" → "struck down"** — Hebrew הִכָּה.
- **"did Joab remain there" → "dwelt there"** (v16) — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"Hadad"** (v17) — in this verse the Hebrew spells the name אֲדַד *Adad*; elsewhere הֲדַד. **"a little child" → "a small boy"** — Hebrew נַעַר קָטָן.
- **"appointed him victuals" → "bread he assigned to him"** (v18) — Hebrew וְלֶחֶם אָמַר לוֹ, literally "and bread he said for him".
- **"the sister of Tahpenes the queen" → "the sister of Tahpenes the great lady"** (v19) — Hebrew הַגְּבִירָה, the feminine of "lord, master": the chief woman of the court (of the king's wife here, of the king's mother in 15:13); "queen" is another word (מַלְכָּה, 10:1). **"found great favour" → "found favor … greatly"** — Hebrew מְאֹד.
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v21, 43) — Hebrew שָׁכַב "lie down".
- **"Let me depart" → "Send me away"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew שַׁלְּחֵנִי. **"Nothing: howbeit let me go in any wise" → "No; but you shall surely send me away"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹא כִּי שַׁלֵּחַ תְּשַׁלְּחֵנִי: "no", and the infinitive absolute with its verb.
- **"another adversary" → "an adversary"** (v23) — "another" is not in the Hebrew. **"to him"** — Hebrew לוֹ, Solomon.
- **"captain over a band, when David slew them of Zobah" → "chief of a raiding band, when David killed them"** (v24) — Hebrew שַׂר־גְּדוּד; "of Zobah" is not in the Hebrew, which says only "them".
- **"beside the mischief that Hadad did" → "together with the evil that Hadad did"** (v25) — Hebrew וְאֶת־הָרָעָה אֲשֶׁר הֲדָד, literally "and with the evil that Hadad": the sentence has no verb ("did" is supplied) and is obscure. **"abhorred" → "loathed"** — Hebrew וַיָּקָץ בְּ. **"Syria" → "Aram"** — Hebrew אֲרָם.
- **"an Ephrathite of Zereda"** (v26) — Hebrew אֶפְרָתִי, here a man of Ephraim; מִן־הַצְּרֵדָה has the article, "from the Zeredah". **"lifted up his hand" → "raised a hand"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew הֵרִים יָד, no "his". **"a widow woman"** — two words in the Hebrew.
- **"this was the cause that" → "this is the matter in which"** (v27) — Hebrew וְזֶה הַדָּבָר אֲשֶׁר. **"repaired the breaches" → "closed the breach"** — Hebrew סָגַר אֶת־פֶּרֶץ, singular.
- **"a mighty man of valour" → "a mighty one of ability"** (v28) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר חָיִל. **"that he was industrious" → "that he was a doer of work"** — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה מְלָאכָה. **"made him ruler over all the charge" → "appointed him over all the burden"** — Hebrew הִפְקִיד and סֵבֶל "burden": the labor laid on the house of Joseph (the root of "their burdens" in Exod 1:11; another word than מַס "forced labor").
- **"and he had clad himself with a new garment" → "and he was covering himself with a new garment"** (v29) — Hebrew וְהוּא מִתְכַּסֶּה; the Hebrew does not name which of the two is meant. **"garment"** (v29, 30) — Hebrew שַׂלְמָה, a large outer cloth.
- **"caught" → "seized"** (v30) — Hebrew תָּפַשׂ בְּ. **"pieces"** (v30, 31) — Hebrew קְרָעִים, "torn pieces", from the verb "tear" of the same verses.
- **"I will rend" → "I am tearing"** (v31) — Hebrew הִנְנִי קֹרֵעַ, a participle. **"ten tribes" → "the ten tribes"** (v31, 35) — the Hebrew has the article.
- **"they have forsaken me, and have worshipped"** (v33) — the Masoretic Text has plural verbs here ("they have forsaken … bowed down … have not walked") and then "as David *his* father". The Septuagint has the singular throughout (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. **"Zidonians"** — the Hebrew has the Aramaic plural ending here, צִדֹנִין. **"to do what is right in my eyes and my statutes and my ordinances"** — the Hebrew joins the two nouns directly to "to do"; "to keep" is not written.
- **"I will make him prince" → "I will set him as chieftain"** (v34) — Hebrew נָשִׂיא אֲשִׁתֶנּוּ: נָשִׂיא "one lifted up", a chieftain; not the word "king". **"because he kept" → "who kept"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר.
- **"the kingdom" → "the kingship"** (v35) — Hebrew הַמְּלוּכָה; in v11, 13, 31, 34 the word is מַמְלָכָה "kingdom".
- **"that David my servant may have a light" → "in order that there may be a lamp for David my servant"** (v36) — Hebrew נִיר, taken as "lamp" (as נֵר): a burning lamp is a living house (2 Sam 21:17). The word may also be "dominion" or "newly broken ground"; uncertain. **"alway" → "all the days"** (v36, 39) — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים.
- **"according to all that thy soul desireth" → "over all that your soul desires"** (v37) — Hebrew בְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר־תְּאַוֶּה נַפְשֶׁךָ.
- **"if thou wilt hearken unto" → "if you hear"** (v38) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶת. **"that is right in my sight" → "what is right in my eyes"** (v33, 38) — Hebrew הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינַי. **"a sure house"** — Hebrew בַּיִת נֶאֱמָן, as 1 Sam 2:35; 25:28.
- **"sought … to kill" → "sought to put … to death"** (v40) — Hebrew לְהָמִית.
- **"the rest of the acts of Solomon … the book of the acts of Solomon"** (v41) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי twice: literally "the rest of the words / matters of Solomon … upon the book of the words / matters of Solomon".
- **"the time that Solomon reigned" → "the days that Solomon reigned"** (v42) — Hebrew הַיָּמִים.
- **"reigned in his stead" → "reigned in his place"** (v43) — Hebrew תַּחְתָּיו.

### Notes
- **Solomon's fall** — from wisdom's peak to covenantal failure. His wives turn his heart after other gods.
- **Seven hundred wives and three hundred concubines** — cf. Deut 17:17 ("he shall not multiply wives for himself, and his heart shall not turn aside").
- **Foreign gods** — Ashtoreth (Sidon), Chemosh (Moab), Milcom / Molech (Ammon). High places on "the mountain that faces Jerusalem", commonly taken as the Mount of Olives (cf. 2 Kgs 23:13).
- **Kingdom divided** — oracle pronounced. Ten tribes to Jeroboam; one tribe remains to David's line. Solomon himself is spared for David's sake. Twelve pieces, ten and one: the count is left as the Hebrew has it.
- **Three adversaries** — Hadad (Edom), Rezon (Aram), Jeroboam (internal). Yahweh / *Elohim* raises up the first two.
- **Ahijah's torn garment** — prophetic sign-act. Twelve pieces, ten to Jeroboam. Cf. the torn robe of 1 Sam 15:27–28.
- **"A lamp before me in Jerusalem"** (v36) — the promise to David's house persists despite the division.
