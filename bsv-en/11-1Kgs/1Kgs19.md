# 1 Kings 19

[1] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ told ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ all that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ had done, and all about how he had killed all the prophets with the sword.

[2] And ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ sent a messenger to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, saying, "Thus may the gods do, and thus may they add: surely about this time tomorrow I will make your soul like the soul of one of them."

[3] And he saw, and he arose and went for his soul, and he came to ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧, which belongs to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he left his young man there.

[4] And he himself went into the wilderness a day's way, and he came and sat under one broom tree, and he asked for his soul to die, and he said, "Enough! Now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, take my soul, for I am not better than my fathers."

[5] And he lay down and slept under one broom tree. And behold, an angel was touching him, and he said to him, "Arise, eat."

[6] And he looked, and behold, at his head was a cake baked on hot stones and a jug of water. And he ate and drank, and he lay down again.

[7] And the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ returned a second time and touched him and said, "Arise, eat, for the way is too much for you."

[8] And he arose and ate and drank, and he went in the power of that food forty days and forty nights, as far as the mountain of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧.

[9] And he came there to the cave and lodged there. And behold, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to him, and he said to him, "What have you to do here, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧?"

[10] And he said, "I have surely been zealous for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ have forsaken your covenant; your altars they have thrown down, and your prophets they have killed with the sword. And I remain, I alone, and they have sought my soul, to take it."

[11] And he said, "Go out and stand on the mountain before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was passing by, and a great and strong wind, tearing mountains apart and breaking crags in pieces before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was not in the wind. And after the wind an earthquake — ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was not in the earthquake.

[12] And after the earthquake a fire — ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was not in the fire. And after the fire a sound of thin stillness.

[13] And it came to pass, when ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ heard, that he wrapped his face in his mantle and went out and stood at the entrance of the cave. And behold, a voice came to him and said, "What have you to do here, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧?"

[14] And he said, "I have surely been zealous for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ have forsaken your covenant; your altars they have thrown down, and your prophets they have killed with the sword. And I remain, I alone, and they have sought my soul, to take it."

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Go, return on your way to the wilderness of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧; and you shall come and anoint ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ as king over ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧.

[16] And ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ the son of Nimshi you shall anoint as king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and Elisha the son of ⟦shaphat|Shafat|Shaphat⟧, from ⟦abel-meholah|Avel Mecholah|Abel-meholah⟧, you shall anoint as prophet in your place.

[17] And it shall be: the one who escapes from the sword of ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧, ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧ shall put to death; and the one who escapes from the sword of ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, Elisha shall put to death.

[18] And I will leave in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ seven thousand: all the knees that have not bowed to the Baal, and every mouth that has not kissed him."

[19] And he went from there and found Elisha the son of ⟦shaphat|Shafat|Shaphat⟧, and he was plowing, twelve yoke before him, and he was with the twelfth. And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ passed over to him and threw his mantle to him.

[20] And he left the herd and ran after ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ and said, "Let me kiss, please, my father and my mother, and let me go after you." And he said to him, "Go, return, for what have I done to you?"

[21] And he returned from after him and took the yoke of the herd and slaughtered it, and with the gear of the herd he boiled their flesh and gave to the people, and they ate. And he arose and went after ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ and ministered to him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8, 10, 14) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v8 with the article, הַר הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"and withal how he had slain" → "and all about how he had killed"** (v1) — Hebrew וְאֵת כָּל־אֲשֶׁר הָרַג, literally "and all that he had killed": "all" stands twice.
- **"So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time" → "Thus may the gods do, and thus may they add: surely about this time tomorrow I will make your soul like the soul of one of them"** (v2) — the oath formula of 1 Sam 25:22, here with plural verbs (יַעֲשׂוּן … יוֹסִפוּן), so אֱלֹהִים is "gods" in Jezebel's mouth. The Hebrew has no "to me"; the Septuagint adds it (from memory). כִּי after the oath is "surely". נֶפֶשׁ is "soul" through the chapter (v2, 3, 4, 10, 14). "Messenger" is מַלְאָךְ, the word of v5.
- **"And when he saw that, he arose, and went for his life" → "And he saw, and he arose and went for his soul"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיַּרְא "and he saw", as the Masoretic Text is pointed; the same letters can be read "and he was afraid" (וַיִּרָא), which the Septuagint has (from memory). וַיֵּלֶךְ אֶל־נַפְשׁוֹ is literally "went to his soul". **"his servant" → "his young man"** — Hebrew נַעַר.
- **"a juniper tree" → "one broom tree"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew רֹתֶם אֶחָד, "one broom shrub" (the desert broom, *Retama*; not juniper). **"he requested for himself that he might die" → "he asked for his soul to die"** — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאַל אֶת־נַפְשׁוֹ לָמוּת, "he asked his soul to die" (as Jonah 4:8). **"It is enough"** — Hebrew רַב, one word, "much!"; the word returns in v7. The Hebrew has no "O".
- **"an angel touched him"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהִנֵּה־זֶה מַלְאָךְ נֹגֵעַ בּוֹ, "and behold, this: a messenger touching him". מַלְאָךְ is "messenger", the word used of Jezebel's messenger in v2; in v7 it is מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה, "the angel (messenger) of Yahweh".
- **"a cake baken on the coals, and a cruse of water" → "a cake baked on hot stones and a jug of water"** (v6) — Hebrew עֻגַת רְצָפִים, "a cake of glowing stones" (or "of coals"); "baked on" is supplied. צַפַּחַת is "jug" (1 Sam 26:11). **"laid him down again"** — literally "and he returned and lay down".
- **"the journey is too great for thee" → "the way is too much for you"** (v7) — Hebrew רַב מִמְּךָ הַדָּרֶךְ; רַב is the "Enough!" of v4.
- **"in the strength of that meat" → "in the power of that food"** (v8) — Hebrew כֹּחַ; אֲכִילָה, "eating", found only here.
- **"What doest thou here, Elijah?" → "What have you to do here, Elijah?"** (v9, 13) — Hebrew מַה־לְּךָ פֹה, "what is there to you here?" In v9 the Hebrew has no verb: "and behold, the word of Yahweh to him".
- **"I have been very jealous for the LORD God of hosts" → "I have surely been zealous for Yahweh, the Elohim of Hosts"** (v10, 14) — Hebrew קַנֹּא קִנֵּאתִי, the verb with its infinitive, "being jealous I have been jealous"; קִנֵּא is "be jealous", with לְ "be zealous for". **"I, even I only, am left" → "I remain, I alone"** — Hebrew וָאִוָּתֵר, the verb of 18:22. **"they seek my life, to take it away" → "they have sought my soul, to take it"**.
- **"the LORD passed by, and a great and strong wind rent the mountains, and brake in pieces the rocks … but the LORD was not in the wind" → "Yahweh was passing by, and a great and strong wind, tearing mountains apart and breaking crags in pieces … — Yahweh was not in the wind"** (v11) — the Hebrew has participles and no finite verb: עֹבֵר "passing by", מְפָרֵק "tearing off", מְשַׁבֵּר "breaking in pieces"; "And behold, Yahweh is passing by …" can also be read as the end of the speech. There is no "but": לֹא בָרוּחַ יְהוָה, "not in the wind, Yahweh". רוּחַ is "wind" and "spirit"; רַעַשׁ is "quaking".
- **"a still small voice" → "a sound of thin stillness"** (v12) — Hebrew קוֹל דְּמָמָה דַקָּה, word for word: קוֹל "voice" or "sound" (it is "a voice" in v13); דְּמָמָה "stillness, silence" (from דָּמַם "be still, be silent"; Ps 107:29 of a calmed storm; Job 4:16 "stillness, and I heard a voice"); דַקָּה "thin, fine" (of the thin ears of Gen 41, of fine dust in Isa 29:5). Other readings of the same three words: "a voice of gentle stillness", "a low murmuring sound", "a sound of sheer silence". Whether it is a voice, a faint sound or silence itself the Hebrew leaves open. The verse does not say that Yahweh was in it.
- **"when Elijah heard it"** (v13) — no object in the Hebrew. **"there came a voice unto him"** — Hebrew וְהִנֵּה אֵלָיו קוֹל, "and behold, to him a voice".
- **"to be king over Syria" → "as king over Aram"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew לְמֶלֶךְ עַל־אֲרָם. "And you shall come and anoint" is two verbs in the Hebrew.
- **"him that escapeth … shall Jehu slay" → "the one who escapes … Jehu shall put to death"** (v17) — Hebrew יָמִית.
- **"Yet I have left me seven thousand" → "And I will leave … seven thousand"** (v18) — Hebrew וְהִשְׁאַרְתִּי, the form that carries on the future of v15–17: "and I will leave". Rom 11:4 cites it as a past, "I have left for myself". "Me" is not in the Hebrew. **"unto Baal" → "to the Baal"** — with the article.
- **"plowing with twelve yoke of oxen before him" → "plowing, twelve yoke before him"** (v19) — Hebrew שְׁנֵים־עָשָׂר צְמָדִים, "twelve pairs"; "of oxen" is not written. **"passed by him, and cast his mantle upon him" → "passed over to him and threw his mantle to him"** — Hebrew אֵלָיו, "to him", both times.
- **"the oxen" → "the herd"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew הַבָּקָר, the collective word for cattle. **"and then I will follow thee" → "and let me go after you"** — Hebrew וְאֵלְכָה, a form of wish. **"Go back again: for what have I done to thee?" → "Go, return, for what have I done to you?"** — Hebrew לֵךְ שׁוּב כִּי מֶה־עָשִׂיתִי לָךְ; the sense of the answer is debated (leave to go, or a reminder of what the mantle meant).
- **"slew them, and boiled their flesh with the instruments of the oxen" → "slaughtered it, and with the gear of the herd he boiled their flesh"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיִּזְבָּחֵהוּ, the verb "sacrifice", here of killing for a meal (as Deut 12:15); "it" is the yoke. בִּשְּׁלָם הַבָּשָׂר is literally "he boiled them, the flesh". כְּלִי is "gear": the wood of yoke and plow.

### Notes
- **Collapse after triumph** — Elijah just saw fire fall and still despairs.
- **Forty days to Horeb** — Horeb = Sinai. Pilgrimage to the covenant mountain, recapitulating Moses (Ex 34). Forty days echoes Moses on Sinai.
- **Theophany and Sinai** — at Sinai Yahweh came in thunder, quaking and fire (Ex 19). Here the text says three times that Yahweh was not in them.
- **"What have you to do here?"** — asked twice (v9, 13); Elijah's answer is word for word the same (v10, 14).
- **Three anointings** — Hazael (external instrument of judgment), Jehu (internal purge of Omrides), Elisha (successor). In the narrative that follows, Hazael and Jehu are dealt with by Elisha (2 Kgs 8, 9).
- **Seven thousand** — corrective to Elijah's "I alone". Paul cites in Rom 11:4.
- **Mantle on Elisha** — prophetic call-sign. Will pass to Elisha when Elijah is taken up (2 Kgs 2).
- **Twelve yoke** — Elisha is wealthy; burning his plow-gear = radical renunciation, no turning back.
