## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"reigned Abijam over Judah" → "Abijam began to reign over Judah"** (v1, 9, 25, 33) — Hebrew מָלַךְ "reign"; with the year of accession it tells the beginning of the reign. In v33 the one verb also carries the length: literally "Baasha … reigned over all Israel in Tirzah twenty-four years". In v29 כְמָלְכוֹ is literally "at his reigning".
- **"his heart was not perfect with the LORD" → "his heart was not whole with Yahweh"** (v3, 14) — Hebrew שָׁלֵם "whole, complete".
- **"Nevertheless for David's sake … to set up his son after him, and to establish Jerusalem" → "For on account of David … to raise up his son after him and to make Jerusalem stand"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; לְמַעַן "on account of"; נִיר "lamp" (11:36); הֵקִים "raise up"; הֶעֱמִיד "make stand".
- **"turned not aside from any thing that he commanded him … save only in the matter of Uriah" → "did not turn aside from all that he commanded him … only in the matter of Uriah"** (v5) — Hebrew מִכֹּל "from all"; רַק "only"; דְּבַר "word, matter".
- **"between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days of his life"** (v6) — so the Masoretic Text, repeating 14:30 in the account of Abijam; some Hebrew manuscripts and versions read "Abijam" (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"the rest of the acts … in the book of the chronicles"** (v7, 23, 31) — Hebrew וְיֶתֶר דִּבְרֵי … עַל־סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, literally "the rest of the words / matters of … upon the book of the matters of the days".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v8, 24) — Hebrew שָׁכַב "lie, lie down".
- **"reigned Asa over Judah" → "Asa king of Judah began to reign"** (v9) — Hebrew מָלַךְ אָסָא מֶלֶךְ יְהוּדָה.
- **"his mother's name was Maachah"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew אִמּוֹ "his mother", the same words as in v2 of Abijam's mother. If Asa was Abijam's son (v8), Maacah was his father's mother; the Hebrew word can be used of a grandmother, but the verse says "mother" and it is kept.
- **"sodomites" → "male cult prostitutes"** (v12) — Hebrew הַקְּדֵשִׁים, the plural of קָדֵשׁ "consecrated man" (14:24; Deut 23:17). **"took away … out of the land" → "made … pass away from the land"** — Hebrew הֶעֱבִיר "make pass".
- **"idols"** (v12) — Hebrew גִּלֻּלִים, a word of contempt whose derivation is uncertain.
- **"her he removed from being queen, because she had made an idol in a grove" → "he removed her from being great lady, because she had made a horrid thing for the Asherah"** (v13) — Hebrew גְּבִירָה "lady, mistress" (the feminine of "lord"), here the rank of the king's mother at court; the word itself says neither "queen" nor "mother". מִפְלֶצֶת is found only here and in 2 Chr 15:16: from a root "shudder", "a thing to shudder at"; what the object was is not known. לָאֲשֵׁרָה "for the Asherah", with the article: the goddess or her wooden object.
- **"destroyed her idol, and burnt it by the brook Kidron" → "cut down her horrid thing and burned it in the wadi Kidron"** (v13) — Hebrew כָּרַת "cut"; בְּנַחַל "in the wadi"; "it" is supplied.
- **"the high places were not removed: nevertheless" → "the high places did not depart; only"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹא־סָרוּ, the simple stem of סוּר "turn aside, depart" (v12 has the causative, "removed"); רַק "only".
- **"he brought in the things which his father had dedicated, and the things which himself had dedicated, into the house of the LORD" → "he brought the holy things of his father and the holy things of the house of Yahweh"** (v15) — Hebrew קָדְשֵׁי "holy things of". The reading tradition (qere) has וְקָדְשֵׁי בֵּית יְהוָה "and the holy things of the house of Yahweh"; the written text (ketiv) has וקדשו "and his own holy things, [into] the house of Yahweh", as 2 Chr 15:18. The qere is translated.
- **"that he might not suffer any to go out or come in to Asa" → "so as not to let anyone go out or come in to Asa"** (v17) — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי תֵּת יֹצֵא וָבָא לְאָסָא, literally "so as not to give one going out and coming in to Asa". "Ramah" has the article in the Hebrew, "the Height"; so also "the Mizpah" (v22).
- **"that were left in the treasures of the house of the LORD, and the treasures of the king's house" → "that remained in the treasuries … and the treasures"** (v18) — one Hebrew word, אוֹצְרוֹת, both times: the storehouses and what is stored in them. For "the king" the written text has מלך without the article.
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v18) — Hebrew *Aram*; "Syria" is later Greco-Roman. **"that dwelt at Damascus"** — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"There is a league between me and thee … a present … come and break thy league" → "There is a covenant between me and you … a bribe … Go, break your covenant"** (v19) — Hebrew בְּרִית "covenant"; the first sentence has no verb ("is" is supplied; it can also be heard as a wish, "Let there be"). שֹׁחַד is "bribe", a gift to buy a decision. לֵךְ "go". **"that he may depart from me" → "that he may go up from me"** — Hebrew וְיַעֲלֶה מֵעָלָי, literally "go up from upon me".
- **"hearkened unto king Asa, and sent the captains of the hosts which he had" → "listened to King Asa, and he sent the commanders of the forces that were his"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל; שָׂרֵי הַחֲיָלִים. **"smote" → "struck"** — Hebrew הִכָּה. **"with all the land of Naphtali" → "together with"** — Hebrew עַל, literally "upon".
- **"he left off building of Ramah" → "he ceased from building Ramah"** (v21) — Hebrew חָדַל. "He dwelt in Tirzah": Hebrew וַיֵּשֶׁב.
- **"made a proclamation throughout all Judah; none was exempted" → "made all Judah hear, none was clear"** (v22) — Hebrew הִשְׁמִיעַ "made hear" (summoned); אֵין נָקִי "there was none clear", free of the duty. **"timber" → "wood"** — Hebrew עֵצִים.
- **"Nevertheless in the time of his old age he was diseased in his feet" → "Only, at the time of his old age he became sick in his feet"** (v23) — Hebrew רַק "only"; חָלָה אֶת־רַגְלָיו.
- **"did evil in the sight of the LORD, and walked in the way of his father, and in his sin wherewith he made Israel to sin" → "did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh and walked … with which he caused Israel to sin"** (v26, 34) — Hebrew הָרַע "the evil", with the article; הֶחֱטִיא is the causative of "sin".
- **"smote him" → "struck him down"** (v27, 29) — Hebrew הִכָּה, where killing is meant. **"laid siege to" → "were besieging"** — Hebrew צָרִים עַל.
- **"did Baasha slay him" → "Baasha put him to death"** (v28) — Hebrew הֵמִית.
- **"he left not to Jeroboam any that breathed" → "he did not leave to Jeroboam any breath"** (v29) — Hebrew כָּל־נְשָׁמָה "any breath" (Deut 20:16; Josh 10:40).
- **"which he spake by his servant" → "that he spoke by the hand of his servant"** (v29) — Hebrew בְּיַד.
- **"by his provocation wherewith he provoked the LORD God of Israel to anger" → "by his vexation with which he vexed Yahweh the Elohim of Israel"** (v30) — Hebrew בְּכַעְסוֹ אֲשֶׁר הִכְעִיס: noun and verb of one root; "anger" is not a second word in the Hebrew.

### Notes
- **Abijam / Abijah** — Kings calls him Abijam; Chronicles calls him Abijah. Kings' negative portrayal contrasts with Chronicles' more favorable one.
- **"Lamp in Jerusalem"** (v4) — Davidic-covenant language. Yahweh preserves the dynasty despite unworthy kings.
- **Uriah caveat** (v5) — honest admission of David's one great failure (the clause "only in the matter of Uriah the Hittite" is said to be absent from the Septuagint; the Masoretic Text has it: from memory).
- **Maacah's deposition** — rare royal purge; removing the *gebirah* was serious. The Asherah cult had taken root even in the royal court.
- **High places not removed** — recurring refrain. Even good Judahite kings fail to centralize worship at Jerusalem until Hezekiah/Josiah.
- **Asa's Aramean alliance** — pragmatically effective, theologically questionable. 2 Chr 16 records Hanani the seer rebuking him for it.
- **Baasha's coup** — fulfills Ahijah's oracle against Jeroboam's house. First northern dynastic purge; pattern will repeat.
- **Gibbethon** — Danite town, but held by Philistines. Israel's western frontier unstable.
