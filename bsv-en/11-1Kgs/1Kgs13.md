# 1 Kings 13

[1] And behold, a man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ by the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ was standing upon the altar to make smoke.

[2] And he called against the altar by the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "Altar, altar, thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Behold, a son shall be born to the house of David, ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ his name, and he shall sacrifice upon you the priests of the high places who make smoke upon you, and bones of man they shall burn upon you.'"

[3] And he gave on that day a wonder, saying, "This is the wonder that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken: Behold, the altar shall be torn apart, and the fat ashes that are upon it shall be poured out."

[4] And it came to pass, when the king heard the word of the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that he had called against the altar in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, that ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ stretched out his hand from upon the altar, saying, "Seize him!" And his hand that he had stretched out against him dried out, and he was not able to bring it back to himself.

[5] And the altar was torn apart, and the fat ashes were poured out from the altar, according to the wonder that the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had given by the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And the king answered and said to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "Beseech, please, the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and pray for me, that my hand may return to me." And the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ besought the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the hand of the king returned to him, and it became as at the first.

[7] And the king spoke to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "Come with me to the house and sustain yourself, and let me give you a gift."

[8] And the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to the king, "If you give me the half of your house, I will not come with you, and I will not eat bread, and I will not drink water in this place.

[9] For so he commanded me by the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, 'You shall not eat bread, and you shall not drink water, and you shall not return by the way that you went.'"

[10] And he went by another way, and he did not return by the way by which he had come to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧.

[11] And a certain old prophet was dwelling in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, and his son came and recounted to him all the deed that the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had done that day in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧; the words that he had spoken to the king, they recounted them to their father.

[12] And their father spoke to them, "Which way did he go?" And his sons saw the way that the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who came from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ had gone.

[13] And he said to his sons, "Saddle the donkey for me." And they saddled the donkey for him, and he rode upon it.

[14] And he went after the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and found him sitting under the terebinth, and he said to him, "Are you the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who came from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?" And he said, "I am."

[15] And he said to him, "Go with me to the house and eat bread."

[16] And he said, "I am not able to return with you and to come with you, and I will not eat bread, and I will not drink water with you in this place.

[17] For a word came to me by the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'You shall not eat bread, and you shall not drink water there; you shall not return to go by the way by which you went.'"

[18] And he said to him, "I also am a prophet like you, and an angel spoke to me by the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, 'Bring him back with you to your house, that he may eat bread and drink water.'" He lied to him.

[19] And he returned with him and ate bread in his house and drank water.

[20] And it came to pass, as they were sitting at the table, that the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to the prophet who had brought him back.

[21] And he called to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who came from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Because you have rebelled against the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and have not kept the commandment that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded you,

[22] and you returned and ate bread and drank water in the place of which he spoke to you, "Do not eat bread and do not drink water," your dead body shall not come to the grave of your fathers.'"

[23] And it came to pass, after he had eaten bread and after he had drunk, that he saddled for him the donkey of the prophet who had brought him back.

[24] And he went, and a lion found him on the way and put him to death; and his dead body was thrown on the way, and the donkey was standing beside it, and the lion was standing beside the dead body.

[25] And behold, men were passing by, and they saw the dead body thrown on the way and the lion standing beside the dead body; and they came and spoke in the city in which the old prophet was dwelling.

[26] And the prophet who had brought him back from the way heard, and he said, "It is the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who rebelled against the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave him to the lion, and it broke him and put him to death, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he spoke to him."

[27] And he spoke to his sons, saying, "Saddle the donkey for me." And they saddled it.

[28] And he went and found his dead body thrown on the way, and the donkey and the lion standing beside the dead body; the lion had not eaten the dead body and had not broken the donkey.

[29] And the prophet lifted up the dead body of the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and laid it on the donkey and brought it back; and he came to the city of the old prophet to lament and to bury him.

[30] And he laid his dead body in his grave, and they lamented over him, "Alas, my brother!"

[31] And it came to pass, after he had buried him, that he said to his sons, saying, "When I die, you shall bury me in the grave in which the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is buried; beside his bones lay my bones.

[32] For the word that he called by the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ against the altar that is in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and against all the houses of the high places that are in the cities of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ shall surely come to pass."

[33] After this thing ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ did not return from his evil way, and he returned and made priests of high places from the ends of the people; whoever delighted, he filled his hand, and he became one of the priests of high places.

[34] And in this thing it became the sin of the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, and to annihilate and to destroy it from upon the face of the ground.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. "A man of *Elohim*" in v1 has no article; from v4 on it is אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים "the man of the Elohim".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"by the word of the LORD" → "by the word of Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 5, 9, 17, 18, 32) — Hebrew בִּדְבַר יְהוָה, literally "in the word of Yahweh"; seven times in the chapter.
- **"stood by the altar to burn incense" → "was standing upon the altar to make smoke"** (v1, 2, 4) — Hebrew לְהַקְטִיר, literally "to make smoke", with no object: the verb is used of incense and of what is laid on the altar, and the verse does not say which. עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ "upon the altar", as 12:33 ("he went up upon the altar"); it can also be "by the altar". In v4 he stretches out his hand מֵעַל "from upon" it.
- **"he cried against the altar" → "he called against the altar"** (v2, 4, 21, 32) — Hebrew קָרָא "call".
- **"O altar, altar" → "Altar, altar"** (v2) — the Hebrew has no "O".
- **"a child shall be born … Josiah by name" → "a son shall be born … Josiah his name"** (v2) — Hebrew בֵּן "son"; נוֹלָד is a participle, "is being born / is to be born". יֹאשִׁיָּהוּ שְׁמוֹ "Josiah his name".
- **"upon thee shall he offer the priests" → "he shall sacrifice upon you the priests"** (v2) — Hebrew זָבַח "sacrifice".
- **"men's bones shall be burnt upon thee" → "bones of man they shall burn upon you"** (v2) — Hebrew עַצְמוֹת אָדָם "bones of man(kind)"; the verb is active and plural, "they shall burn", with no subject named.
- **"sign" → "wonder"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew מוֹפֵת "wonder, portent"; "sign" is אוֹת. In v3 the verb is וְנָתַן, a form that usually looks forward ("and he shall give"); it is translated as the narrative requires.
- **"the altar shall be rent, and the ashes … poured out" → "the altar shall be torn apart, and the fat ashes … poured out"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew נִקְרָע, a participle of קָרַע "tear" ("is about to be torn"); the same verb as "tore the kingdom" (11:11–13; 14:8). דֶּשֶׁן is the fat-soaked ashes of the altar.
- **"when king Jeroboam heard the saying" → "when the king heard the word … Jeroboam stretched out"** (v4) — the Hebrew has "the king" in the first clause and the name in the second; דְּבַר is "word".
- **"put forth his hand" → "stretched out his hand"** (v4) — Hebrew שָׁלַח יָד.
- **"Lay hold on him" → "Seize him"** (v4) — Hebrew תָּפַשׂ.
- **"dried up, so that he could not pull it in again to him" → "dried out, and he was not able to bring it back to himself"** (v4) — Hebrew יָבֵשׁ "be dried out, wither"; וְלֹא יָכֹל "and he was not able"; הֵשִׁיב "bring back", the verb of v6 ("return"), v18–26 ("bring back").
- **"Intreat now the face of the LORD … besought the LORD" → "Beseech, please, the face of Yahweh … besought the face of Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew חַל־נָא אֶת־פְּנֵי, literally "make soft / sweet the face of"; "the face" stands both times. נָא "please".
- **"that my hand may be restored me again … was restored him again, and became as it was before" → "that my hand may return to me … returned to him, and it became as at the first"** (v6) — Hebrew שׁוּב "return"; כְּבָרִאשֹׁנָה "as at the first".
- **"Come home with me, and refresh thyself, and I will give thee a reward" → "Come with me to the house and sustain yourself, and let me give you a gift"** (v7) — Hebrew הַבַּיְתָה "to the house"; סְעָדָה "sustain" (with food; "yourself" is supplied, the Hebrew has no object); מַתָּת "gift"; וְאֶתְּנָה is the form of a wish or resolve.
- **"If thou wilt give me half thine house, I will not go in with thee" → "If you give me the half of your house, I will not come with you"** (v8) — Hebrew בּוֹא "come, go in".
- **"so was it charged me" → "so he commanded me"** (v9) — Hebrew צִוָּה אֹתִי, an active verb with no subject named: "he (or: one) commanded me".
- **"nor turn again by the same way that thou camest" → "you shall not return by the way that you went"** (v9, 17) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הָלָכְתָּ "that you went". In v17 לֹא־תָשׁוּב לָלֶכֶת "you shall not return to go".
- **"there dwelt an old prophet … his sons came and told him" → "a certain old prophet was dwelling … his son came and recounted to him"** (v11) — Hebrew נָבִיא אֶחָד "one prophet". The Masoretic Text has the singular "his son came and recounted", then the plural "they recounted them to their father"; the ancient versions have the plural throughout (from memory). סִפֵּר "recount". "That day" is הַיּוֹם, literally "today".
- **"all the works" → "all the deed"** (v11) — Hebrew הַמַּעֲשֶׂה, singular.
- **"For his sons had seen what way" → "And his sons saw the way"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּרְאוּ "and they saw", the plain narrative form; the ancient versions read "and they showed" (the same letters with other vowels; from memory). The Masoretic pointing is translated.
- **"ass" → "donkey"** (throughout) — Hebrew חֲמוֹר. **"Saddle"** (v13, 23, 27) — Hebrew חָבַשׁ, literally "bind". In v27 "it" is supplied.
- **"under an oak" → "under the terebinth"** (v14) — Hebrew הָאֵלָה, with the article; the kind of tree is not certain.
- **"Come home with me" → "Go with me to the house"** (v15) — Hebrew לֵךְ "go"; v7 has בֹּאָה "come".
- **"I may not return with thee, nor go in with thee" → "I am not able to return with you and to come with you"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא אוּכַל "I am not able".
- **"For it was said to me by the word of the LORD" → "For a word came to me by the word of Yahweh"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי־דָבָר אֵלַי, literally "for a word to me": the Masoretic Text points a noun; with other vowels it is "he spoke to me".
- **"an angel spake unto me"** (v18) — Hebrew מַלְאָךְ, the word for "messenger"; kept "angel" as the one rendering of a messenger from heaven.
- **"But he lied unto him" → "He lied to him"** (v18) — Hebrew כִּחֵשׁ לוֹ, two words with no "but" or "and".
- **"thou hast disobeyed the mouth of the LORD" → "you have rebelled against the mouth of Yahweh"** (v21, 26) — Hebrew מָרָה פִּי "rebel against the mouth of".
- **"thy carcase shall not come unto the sepulchre of thy fathers" → "your dead body shall not come to the grave of your fathers"** (v22–30) — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, the word for a carcass, here of a man: "dead body"; ten times in v22–30. קֶבֶר "grave".
- **"he saddled for him the ass, to wit, for the prophet whom he had brought back" → "he saddled for him the donkey of the prophet who had brought him back"** (v23) — Hebrew לַנָּבִיא אֲשֶׁר הֱשִׁיבוֹ. The same words in v20 and v26 mean "the prophet who had brought him back", and the man from Judah is nowhere called "prophet" by the narrator; so the לְ is taken as "belonging to". The words can also be read "for the prophet whom he had brought back".
- **"a lion met him by the way, and slew him" → "a lion found him on the way and put him to death"** (v24) — Hebrew מָצָא "find"; הֵמִית "put to death". **"cast in the way" → "thrown on the way"** (v24, 25, 28) — Hebrew מֻשְׁלֶכֶת, of הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw".
- **"told it in the city" → "spoke in the city"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיְדַבְּרוּ "and they spoke"; no object.
- **"who was disobedient unto the word of the LORD: therefore the LORD hath delivered him unto the lion, which hath torn him" → "who rebelled against the mouth of Yahweh; and Yahweh gave him to the lion, and it broke him"** (v26, 28) — Hebrew פִּי "mouth"; וַיִּתְּנֵהוּ "and he gave him"; שָׁבַר "break" (of a beast: maul, break the bones). In v28 the Hebrew has "a donkey" without the article.
- **"took up the carcase … to mourn and to bury him" → "lifted up the dead body … to lament and to bury him"** (v29, 30) — Hebrew נָשָׂא "lift up"; סָפַד "lament", the loud wailing for the dead. "And he came to the city of the old prophet": so the Hebrew, of the old prophet himself.
- **"in his own grave" → "in his grave"** (v30) — Hebrew בְּקִבְרוֹ; "own" is not written.
- **"the saying which he cried … shall surely come to pass"** (v32) — Hebrew הָיֹה יִהְיֶה, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "being it shall be".
- **"returned not from his evil way, but made again of the lowest of the people priests" → "did not return from his evil way, and he returned and made priests of high places from the ends of the people"** (v33) — Hebrew לֹא־שָׁב … וַיָּשָׁב: the same verb twice; "he returned and made" is the Hebrew way of saying "he made again". מִקְצוֹת הָעָם "from the ends of the people": from every part of the people (as 12:31), not "the lowest".
- **"whosoever would, he consecrated him, and he became one of the priests" → "whoever delighted, he filled his hand, and he became one of the priests of high places"** (v33) — Hebrew הֶחָפֵץ "the one who delighted"; יְמַלֵּא אֶת־יָדוֹ "he would fill his hand", the expression for installing a priest. The last clause is literally "and he became priests of high places"; "one of" is supplied.
- **"this thing became sin unto the house of Jeroboam, even to cut it off, and to destroy it from off the face of the earth" → "in this thing it became the sin of the house of Jeroboam, and to annihilate and to destroy it from upon the face of the ground"** (v34) — Hebrew בַּדָּבָר הַזֶּה "in / by this thing" (the ancient versions read "this thing"; from memory); לְחַטַּאת "for the sin of"; הִכְחִיד "annihilate" (literally "make disappear"), הִשְׁמִיד "destroy"; "it" is supplied; אֲדָמָה "ground".

### Notes
- **Josiah prophesied** (v2) — by name, three centuries before fulfillment (2 Kgs 23:15–20). Josiah will destroy this altar and burn bones on it.
- **Jeroboam's dried-out hand** — immediate confirmation-miracle.
- **The old prophet's deception** — odd narrative. He lies, then oracles truthfully against the man he just deceived.
- **Man of *Elohim* killed by lion** — stern consequence for rebelling against the word he was given, even when deceived.
- **Lion and donkey standing** — unnatural scene confirms divine judgment, not mere accident.
- **Bones preserved** — by the old prophet's request, the Judahite's bones are preserved when Josiah later desecrates the altar (2 Kgs 23:17–18).
- **Jeroboam's continued sin** — no turning back despite the wonder. Seals his house's doom.
