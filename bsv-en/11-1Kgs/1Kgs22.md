# 1 Kings 22

[1] And they sat three years; there was no war between ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And it came to pass in the third year that ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ came down to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to his servants, "Do you know that ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧ is ours? And we are keeping silent from taking it out of the hand of the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧."

[4] And he said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "Will you go with me to the battle, to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧?" And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "As I am, so are you; as my people, so your people; as my horses, so your horses."

[5] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "Seek, please, today the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[6] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ gathered the prophets, about four hundred men, and said to them, "Shall I go against ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧ to battle, or shall I cease?" And they said, "Go up, and ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ will give it into the hand of the king."

[7] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said, "Is there not here a prophet of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ still, that we may seek from him?"

[8] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "There is still one man to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from him, and I hate him, for he does not prophesy good concerning me, but evil: ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ the son of ⟦imlah|Yimlah|Imlah⟧." And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said, "Let not the king say so."

[9] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ called to one eunuch and said, "Hasten ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ the son of ⟦imlah|Yimlah|Imlah⟧."

[10] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ were sitting, each man on his throne, clothed in garments, at a threshing floor at the entrance of the gate of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and all the prophets were prophesying before them.

[11] And ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyah|Zedekiah⟧ the son of ⟦chenaanah|Kenaanah|Chenaanah⟧ made for himself horns of iron and said, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'With these you shall gore ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ until you have made an end of them.'"

[12] And all the prophets were prophesying so, saying, "Go up to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧ and prosper, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give it into the hand of the king."

[13] And the messenger who went to call ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ spoke to him, saying, "Behold, please, the words of the prophets, with one mouth, are good toward the king. Let your word, please, be like the word of one of them, and speak good."

[14] And ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, surely what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ says to me, that I will speak."

[15] And he came to the king, and the king said to him, "⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧, shall we go to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧ to battle, or shall we cease?" And he said to him, "Go up and prosper, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give it into the hand of the king."

[16] And the king said to him, "How many times am I making you swear that you shall speak to me nothing but truth in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"

[17] And he said, "I saw all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ scattered to the mountains, like the flock that has no shepherd. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, 'These have no lords; let them return, each man to his house, in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.'"

[18] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "Did I not say to you, 'He will not prophesy good concerning me, but evil'?"

[19] And he said, "Therefore hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: I saw ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sitting on his throne, and all the host of the heavens standing by him, on his right and on his left.

[20] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, 'Who will entice ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, and let him go up and fall at ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧?' And this one said thus, and that one was saying thus.

[21] And the spirit came out and stood before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, 'I, I will entice him.'

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, 'By what?' And he said, 'I will go out and be a spirit of falsehood in the mouth of all his prophets.' And he said, 'You shall entice, and you shall also be able. Go out and do so.'

[23] And now, behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given a spirit of falsehood in the mouth of all these your prophets, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken evil concerning you."

[24] And ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ the son of ⟦chenaanah|Kenaanah|Chenaanah⟧ drew near and struck ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ on the cheek and said, "Which way did the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ pass over from me to speak with you?"

[25] And ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ said, "Behold, you shall see on that day when you go into a chamber within a chamber to hide yourself."

[26] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said, "Take ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ and return him to Amon the prince of the city and to ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ the son of the king,

[27] and you shall say, 'Thus says the king: Put this one in the house of confinement, and feed him bread of oppression and water of oppression until I come in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.'"

[28] And ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ said, "If you surely return in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not spoken by me." And he said, "Hear, peoples, all of them!"

[29] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went up to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧.

[30] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "I will disguise myself and go into the battle; and you, put on your garments." And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ disguised himself and went into the battle.

[31] And the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ had commanded the commanders of the chariots that were his, thirty-two, saying, "You shall not fight with small or with great, but with the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ alone."

[32] And it came to pass, when the commanders of the chariots saw ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, that they said, "Surely he is the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧," and they turned aside against him to fight; and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ cried out.

[33] And it came to pass, when the commanders of the chariots saw that he was not the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that they turned back from after him.

[34] And a man drew the bow in his innocence and struck the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ between the joints and the armor. And he said to his charioteer, "Turn your hand and bring me out of the camp, for I am wounded."

[35] And the battle went up on that day, and the king was kept standing in the chariot opposite ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, and he died in the evening; and the blood of the wound poured into the bosom of the chariot.

[36] And the cry passed through the camp at the going in of the sun, saying, "Each man to his city, and each man to his land!"

[37] And the king died and came to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and they buried the king in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[38] And one rinsed the chariot at the pool of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and the dogs lapped his blood, and the prostitutes washed, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he had spoken.

[39] And the rest of the acts of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, and all that he did, and the house of ivory that he built, and all the cities that he built, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[40] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ lay with his fathers, and ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ his son reigned in his place.

[41] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ the son of Asa began to reign over ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ in the fourth year of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[42] ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ was thirty-five years old when he began to reign, and twenty-five years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦azubah|Azuvah|Azubah⟧ the daughter of ⟦shilhi|Shilchi|Shilhi⟧.

[43] And he walked in all the way of Asa his father; he did not turn aside from it, doing what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. Only, the high places did not depart; the people were still sacrificing and making smoke on the high places.

[44] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ made peace with the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[45] And the rest of the acts of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, and his might that he did, and how he fought, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?

[46] And the rest of the male cult prostitutes, who were left in the days of Asa his father, he cleared away from the land.

[47] And there was no king in Edom; a prefect was king.

[48] ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ made ships of Tarshish to go to ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧ for the gold, and he did not go, for the ships were broken at ⟦ezion-geber|Etzyon Gever|Ezion-geber⟧.

[49] Then ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "Let my servants go with your servants in the ships." And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ was not willing.

[50] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ lay with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David his father, and ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ his son reigned in his place.

[51] ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ began to reign over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ in the seventeenth year of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he reigned over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ two years.

[52] And he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and walked in the way of his father and in the way of his mother and in the way of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧, who caused ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to sin.

[53] And he served the Baal and bowed down to him, and he vexed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, according to all that his father had done.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v53) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Syria / the Syrians" → "Aram"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲרָם, the name, also for the people (v11, 35).
- **"they continued three years without war" → "they sat three years; there was no war"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֵּשְׁבוּ, "they sat, dwelt".
- **"we be still, and take it not" → "we are keeping silent from taking it"** (v3) — Hebrew מַחְשִׁים מִקַּחַת.
- **"I am as thou art, my people as thy people" → "As I am, so are you; as my people, so your people"** (v4) — Hebrew כָּמוֹנִי כָמוֹךָ, "like me, like you". "To the battle, to Ramoth-gilead": the Hebrew sets the two side by side without "at".
- **"Enquire, I pray thee, at the word of the LORD to day" → "Seek, please, today the word of Yahweh"** (v5, 7, 8) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ, "seek"; כַיּוֹם, "today" or "first of all".
- **"shall I forbear?" → "shall I cease?"** (v6, 15) — Hebrew חָדַל. **"for the Lord shall deliver it" → "and Adonai will give it"** — Hebrew וְיִתֵּן אֲדֹנָי, "and may Adonai give" or "and Adonai will give"; "it" is supplied here and in v12, 15. The four hundred say אֲדֹנָי, "Lord", not the name Yahweh, which they use in v11, 12.
- **"Is there not here a prophet of the LORD besides" → "still"** (v7) — Hebrew עוֹד.
- **"There is yet one man, Micaiah the son of Imlah, by whom we may enquire of the LORD: but I hate him"** (v8) — the Hebrew keeps the name to the end: "… but evil: Micaiah the son of Imlah".
- **"an officer" → "one eunuch"** (v9) — Hebrew סָרִיס אֶחָד; the word is also used of a court officer. **"Hasten hither Micaiah" → "Hasten Micaiah"**.
- **"having put on their robes, in a void place" → "clothed in garments, at a threshing floor"** (v10) — Hebrew מְלֻבָּשִׁים בְּגָדִים; בְּגֹרֶן, the open floor by the gate.
- **"Zedekiah"** (v11, 24) — the Hebrew writes the name in its short form צִדְקִיָּה in v11 and in its long form צִדְקִיָּהוּ in v24. **"With these shalt thou push the Syrians, until thou have consumed them" → "With these you shall gore Aram until you have made an end of them"** — Hebrew נִגַּח (Exod 21:28); עַד־כַּלֹּתָם.
- **"prophesied"** (v10, 12) — two forms of one verb (מִתְנַבְּאִים, נִבְּאִים).
- **"the words of the prophets declare good unto the king with one mouth" → "the words of the prophets, with one mouth, are good toward the king"** (v13) — the Hebrew has no verb. "Your word" is the reading tradition (qere); the written text has "your words".
- **"what the LORD saith unto me, that will I speak" → "surely what Yahweh says"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי after the oath.
- **"How many times shall I adjure thee" → "How many times am I making you swear"** (v16) — Hebrew עַד־כַּמֶּה פְעָמִים אֲנִי מַשְׁבִּעֶךָ, "up to how many times".
- **"scattered upon the hills, as sheep that have not a shepherd … These have no master" → "scattered to the mountains, like the flock that has no shepherd … These have no lords"** (v17) — Hebrew אֶל־הֶהָרִים; צֹאן; אֲדֹנִים, a plural. **"in peace" → "in shalom"** (v17, 27, 28).
- **"all the host of heaven standing by him" → "all the host of the heavens"** (v19) — Hebrew צְבָא הַשָּׁמַיִם; עֹמֵד עָלָיו, "standing over him, by him".
- **"Who shall persuade Ahab, that he may go up and fall" → "Who will entice Ahab, and let him go up and fall"** (v20–22) — Hebrew פִּתָּה, "entice, persuade, deceive" (Exod 22:16; Jer 20:7); the verb is the same three times and Yahweh is the one who asks for it.
- **"there came forth a spirit" → "the spirit came out"** (v21) — Hebrew הָרוּחַ, with the article: "the spirit". Who is meant is not said (a certain spirit of the host, or the spirit as such); רוּחַ is also "wind, breath".
- **"a lying spirit" → "a spirit of falsehood"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew רוּחַ שֶׁקֶר, "a spirit of a lie, of falsehood": a lying spirit. Nothing is softened in the Hebrew: the spirit offers to be this in the mouth of the prophets, Yahweh says "You shall entice, and you shall also be able (prevail); go out and do so", and Micaiah says that Yahweh "has given" (נָתַן) the spirit of falsehood into their mouth.
- **"Which way went the Spirit of the LORD from me to speak unto thee?" → "Which way did the spirit of Yahweh pass over from me to speak with you?"** (v24) — Hebrew אֵי־זֶה עָבַר; לְדַבֵּר אוֹתָךְ.
- **"into an inner chamber" → "into a chamber within a chamber"** (v25) — as 20:30. "You shall see" is "behold, you are seeing".
- **"carry him back unto Amon the governor of the city" → "return him to Amon the prince of the city"** (v26) — Hebrew שַׂר; the commands "take" and "return" are singular.
- **"Put this fellow in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction" → "Put this one in the house of confinement, and feed him bread of oppression"** (v27) — Hebrew בֵּית הַכֶּלֶא; לַחַץ, "oppression, distress": scant bread and water.
- **"If thou return at all in peace … Hearken, O people, every one of you" → "If you surely return in shalom … Hear, peoples, all of them!"** (v28) — Hebrew שׁוֹב תָּשׁוּב, the verb with its infinitive; דִבֶּר בִּי, "spoken in me, by me"; שִׁמְעוּ עַמִּים כֻּלָּם, the first words of the book of Micah (Mic 1:2).
- **"I will disguise myself, and enter into the battle"** (v30) — the Hebrew has two bare infinitives, הִתְחַפֵּשׂ וָבֹא, "to disguise oneself and go"; the Septuagint has "I will disguise myself" (from memory). **"robes" → "garments"**.
- **"his thirty and two captains that had rule over his chariots" → "the commanders of the chariots that were his, thirty-two"** (v31–33) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הָרֶכֶב.
- **"they turned back from pursuing him" → "from after him"** (v33).
- **"a certain man drew a bow at a venture, and smote the king of Israel between the joints of the harness" → "a man drew the bow in his innocence and struck the king of Israel between the joints and the armor"** (v34) — Hebrew לְתֻמּוֹ, "in his integrity": without design. בֵּין הַדְּבָקִים וּבֵין הַשִּׁרְיָן: דְּבָקִים, "joinings", is found in this sense only here; what part of the armor is meant is uncertain. **"Turn thine hand"** — the hand on the reins. **"I am wounded"** — Hebrew הָחֳלֵיתִי, "I have been made sick".
- **"the battle increased" → "the battle went up"** (v35) — Hebrew וַתַּעֲלֶה. **"was stayed up in his chariot against the Syrians" → "was kept standing in the chariot opposite Aram"** — Hebrew מָעֳמָד, "made to stand". **"the blood ran out of the wound into the midst of the chariot" → "the blood of the wound poured into the bosom of the chariot"** — Hebrew מַכָּה, "blow"; חֵיק, "bosom": the hollow floor.
- **"there went a proclamation throughout the host" → "the cry passed through the camp"** (v36) — Hebrew הָרִנָּה, a ringing cry.
- **"So the king died, and was brought to Samaria" → "And the king died and came to Samaria"** (v37) — Hebrew וַיָּבוֹא, "and he came".
- **"one washed the chariot in the pool of Samaria; and the dogs licked up his blood; and they washed his armour" → "one rinsed the chariot at the pool of Samaria, and the dogs lapped his blood, and the prostitutes washed"** (v38) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁטֹף, singular; לָקַק, the verb of 21:19; וְהַזֹּנוֹת רָחָצוּ, "and the prostitutes washed (bathed)": "armour" was never in the Hebrew. The brackets of the older wording are removed.
- **"the ivory house" → "the house of ivory"** (v39) — literally "the house of the tooth". **"the rest of the acts … the book of the chronicles"** (v39, 45) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי, "the words, matters of Ahab … the book of the matters of the days".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v40, 50) — Hebrew שָׁכַב.
- **"began to reign"** (v41, 51) — the verb is מָלַךְ, "reign", here of the beginning. **"thirty and five years old"** (v42) — literally "a son of thirty-five years".
- **"he turned not aside from it … nevertheless the high places were not taken away; for the people offered and burnt incense yet in the high places" → "he did not turn aside from it … Only, the high places did not depart; the people were still sacrificing and making smoke on the high places"** (v43) — one verb, סוּר, of the king and of the high places. מְקַטְּרִים has no object: "making smoke" (of sacrifice or of incense).
- **"the remnant of the sodomites" → "the rest of the male cult prostitutes"** (v46) — Hebrew הַקָּדֵשׁ, a collective singular, "the consecrated one": a man attached to a sanctuary; what he did is inferred. **"he took out of the land" → "he cleared away from the land"** — Hebrew בִּעֵר, as 21:21.
- **"There was then no king in Edom: a deputy was king" → "a prefect was king"** (v47) — Hebrew נִצָּב מֶלֶךְ, "one set, stationed (was) king": the word of Solomon's prefects (4:7).
- **"Jehoshaphat made ships of Tharshish … but they went not; for the ships were broken" → "and he did not go"** (v48) — "made" is the reading tradition (qere); the written text has the letters of "ten" (עשר). וְלֹא הָלָךְ is singular. "Were broken" is the qere (plural); the written text is singular.
- **"But Jehoshaphat would not" → "was not willing"** (v49) — Hebrew אָבָה.
- **"he did evil in the sight of the LORD" → "he did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v52) — with the article. **"who made Israel to sin" → "who caused Israel to sin"**.
- **"he served Baal, and worshipped him, and provoked to anger the LORD" → "he served the Baal and bowed down to him, and he vexed Yahweh"** (v53) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל; הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה; הִכְעִיס.

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew counts "Only, the high places did not depart …" (our v43b) as verse 44, so that our v44–53 are verses 45–54 in the Hebrew. In the Hebrew, verse 21 also includes "And Yahweh said to him, 'By what?'", the opening of our v22.
- **Ramoth-gilead** — strategic Transjordanian city, contested with Aram.
- **Four hundred prophets** — court prophets who speak in the name of Yahweh (v11, 12).
- **Zedekiah's iron horns** — prophetic sign-act; compare Deut 33:17.
- **The vision** (v19–23) — the heavenly court, as Job 1–2; Isa 6.
- **The arrow** — the man does not know whom he strikes; the word of 20:42 and 21:19 comes to pass.
- **Ivory house** (v39) — ivories of this age have been excavated at Samaria. Compare Amos 3:15.
- **Jehoshaphat of Judah** — 2 Chr 17–20 expands this brief notice.
- **Ships broken at Ezion-geber** — a port on the Gulf of Aqaba; 2 Chr 20:35–37 tells the matter differently.
- **Ahaziah of Israel** (v51) — his reign continues in 2 Kgs 1.
