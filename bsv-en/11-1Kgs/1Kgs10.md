# 1 Kings 10

[1] And the queen of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ was hearing the report of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and she came to test him with riddles.

[2] And she came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with a very heavy force, camels carrying spices and very much gold and precious stone. And she came to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and spoke to him all that was with her heart.

[3] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ told her all her words; there was not a word hidden from the king that he did not tell her.

[4] And the queen of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ saw all the wisdom of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, and the house that he had built,

[5] and the food of his table, and the seating of his servants, and the standing of his ministers, and their clothing, and his cupbearers, and his burnt offering that he would offer up at the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and there was no more spirit in her.

[6] And she said to the king, "The word was truth that I heard in my land about your words and about your wisdom.

[7] And I did not believe the words until I came and my eyes saw; and behold, the half was not told me. You have added wisdom and good to the report that I heard.

[8] Happy are your men, happy are these your servants, who stand before you continually, who hear your wisdom.

[9] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be blessed, who delighted in you, to place you on the throne of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. Because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loves ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ forever, he has set you as king, to do judgment and righteousness."

[10] And she gave the king a hundred and twenty talents of gold, and spices, very many, and precious stone. There came no more such spice for abundance as that which the queen of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ gave to King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[11] And also the fleet of ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧, which carried gold from ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧, brought from ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧ almug wood, very much, and precious stone.

[12] And the king made of the almug wood a support for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and for the house of the king, and lyres and harps for the singers. Such almug wood has not come and has not been seen, to this day.

[13] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ gave to the queen of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ all her delight that she asked, besides what he gave her according to the hand of King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧. And she turned and went to her land, she and her servants.

[14] And the weight of the gold that came to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in one year was six hundred and sixty-six talents of gold,

[15] apart from what came from the men who went about and the trade of the merchants, and all the kings of the mixed people and the governors of the land.

[16] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made two hundred large shields of beaten gold; six hundred of gold went up on the one large shield.

[17] And three hundred shields of beaten gold; three minas of gold went up on the one shield. And the king put them in the House of the Forest of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧.

[18] And the king made a great throne of ivory and overlaid it with fine gold.

[19] The throne had six steps, and the throne had a round top behind it, and arms on this side and on that at the place of the seat, and two lions standing beside the arms.

[20] And twelve lions were standing there on the six steps, on this side and on that. The like was not made for any kingdom.

[21] And all the drinking vessels of King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ were gold, and all the vessels of the House of the Forest of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ were refined gold. There was no silver; it was not reckoned as anything in the days of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[22] For the king had a fleet of Tarshish on the sea with the fleet of ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧. Once in three years the fleet of Tarshish would come, carrying gold and silver, ivory and apes and baboons.

[23] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ became greater than all the kings of the earth in riches and in wisdom.

[24] And all the earth were seeking the face of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, to hear his wisdom, which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had put in his heart.

[25] And they were bringing, each man his offering, vessels of silver and vessels of gold and garments and weapons and spices, horses and mules, the matter of a year in a year.

[26] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ gathered chariots and horsemen, and he had a thousand four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen; and he led them into the cities of the chariots and with the king in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[27] And the king made the silver in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ like the stones, and the cedars he made like the sycamores that are in the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧, for abundance.

[28] And the going out of the horses that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had was from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and from ⟦kue|Qeveh|Kue⟧: the traders of the king would take them from ⟦kue|Qeveh|Kue⟧ at a price.

[29] And a chariot came up and went out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ for six hundred of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty; and so for all the kings of the ⟦hittites|Chittim|Hittites⟧ and for the kings of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, by their hand they would bring them out.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"heard of the fame of Solomon concerning the name of the LORD" → "was hearing the report of Solomon, for the name of Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew שֹׁמַעַת אֶת־שֵׁמַע שְׁלֹמֹה לְשֵׁם יְהוָה: a participle ("was hearing"), and שֵׁמַע "what is heard, report". לְשֵׁם יְהוָה is literally "to / for the name of Yahweh"; how it joins the sentence is not clear ("in regard to", "owing to", "to the honor of"). The parallel 2 Chr 9:1 does not have the words.
- **"to prove him with hard questions" → "to test him with riddles"** (v1) — Hebrew נִסָּה "test" and חִידוֹת "riddles" (as Judg 14:12).
- **"a very great train" → "a very heavy force"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּחַיִל כָּבֵד מְאֹד: חַיִל is "force" (also "wealth, ability"), כָּבֵד "heavy". **"precious stones" → "precious stone"** (v2, 10, 11) — singular in the Hebrew, of a kind. **"communed with him of all that was in her heart" → "spoke to him all that was with her heart"** — Hebrew עִם־לְבָבָהּ.
- **"told her all her questions: there was not any thing hid" → "told her all her words; there was not a word hidden"** (v3) — Hebrew דְּבָרֶיהָ / דָּבָר, "word" or "matter": he explained all she put to him.
- **"the sitting of his servants, and the attendance of his ministers" → "the seating of his servants, and the standing of his ministers"** (v5) — Hebrew מוֹשַׁב (from "sit, dwell") and מַעֲמַד (from "stand"). "His ministers" follows the reading tradition (qere, plural); the written text has the singular. **"his cupbearers"** — Hebrew מַשְׁקָיו; the word may also be "his drinks".
- **"his ascent by which he went up unto the house of the LORD" → "his burnt offering that he would offer up at the house of Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew וְעֹלָתוֹ אֲשֶׁר יַעֲלֶה בֵּית יְהוָה, as pointed: עֹלָה "burnt offering" (singular) with its verb "make go up". The consonants can also be read "his going up by which he went up to the house of Yahweh"; 2 Chr 9:4 has another word (עֲלִיָּתוֹ).
- **"It was a true report" → "The word was truth"** (v6) — Hebrew אֱמֶת הָיָה הַדָּבָר. **"of thy acts" → "about your words"** — Hebrew דְּבָרֶיךָ, "words" or "matters".
- **"thy wisdom and prosperity exceedeth the fame" → "You have added wisdom and good to the report"** (v7) — Hebrew הוֹסַפְתָּ חָכְמָה וָטוֹב אֶל־הַשְּׁמוּעָה, word for word: what she finds is more than what she heard. שְׁמוּעָה "report" is another noun of the same root as שֵׁמַע in v1.
- **"Happy are thy men"** (v8) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי אֲנָשֶׁיךָ. The Septuagint reads "your wives" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Blessed be the LORD" → "May Yahweh your Elohim be blessed"** (v9) — Hebrew יְהִי … בָּרוּךְ, a wish with its verb. **"to set thee" → "to place you"** — Hebrew לְתִתְּךָ, literally "to give you". **"because the LORD loved Israel for ever" → "Because Yahweh loves Israel forever"** — Hebrew בְּאַהֲבַת יְהוָה אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל לְעֹלָם, literally "in Yahweh's loving Israel forever". **"made he thee king" → "he has set you as king"** — Hebrew וַיְשִׂימְךָ לְמֶלֶךְ. **"judgment and justice" → "judgment and righteousness"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה.
- **"sixscore" → "a hundred and twenty"** (v10). **"there came no more such abundance of spices" → "There came no more such spice for abundance"** — Hebrew לֹא־בָא כַבֹּשֶׂם הַהוּא עוֹד לָרֹב.
- **"almug trees" → "almug wood"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew עֲצֵי אַלְמֻגִּים; the tree is not known (2 Chr 9:10 writes אַלְגּוּמִּים). **"brought" → "carried"** — Hebrew נָשָׂא.
- **"pillars" → "a support"** (v12) — Hebrew מִסְעָד, found only here: from סָעַד "support"; what was made is not known (2 Chr 9:11 has מְסִלּוֹת "raised ways"). **"harps also and psalteries" → "lyres and harps"** — Hebrew כִּנּוֹר and נֵבֶל; the kinds of instrument are not certain.
- **"all her desire, whatsoever she asked" → "all her delight that she asked"** (v13) — Hebrew חֶפְצָהּ אֲשֶׁר שָׁאָלָה. **"of his royal bounty" → "according to the hand of King Solomon"** — Hebrew כְּיַד הַמֶּלֶךְ שְׁלֹמֹה, word for word: as a king's hand gives. **"she and her servants"** — the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"six hundred threescore and six" → "six hundred and sixty-six"** (v14).
- **"Beside that he had of the merchantmen, and of the traffick of the spice merchants" → "apart from what came from the men who went about and the trade of the merchants"** (v15) — Hebrew לְבַד מֵאַנְשֵׁי הַתָּרִים וּמִסְחַר הָרֹכְלִים, literally "apart from the men of those who explore, and the trade of the merchants"; "what came" is supplied. תָּרִים is the participle of תּוּר "explore, go about" (travelling traders are commonly understood); מִסְחָר is found only here.
- **"all the kings of Arabia" → "all the kings of the mixed people"** (v15) — the Masoretic Text points הָעֶרֶב *ha-erev*, "the mixed company" (as Jer 25:20, 24; cf. Exod 12:38). The same consonants pointed עֲרָב are "Arabia", and so the parallel 2 Chr 9:14 has it. The pointing of Kings is translated.
- **"targets" / "shields" → "large shields" / "shields"** (v16, 17) — Hebrew צִנָּה (the large shield that covers the body) and מָגֵן. **"beaten gold"** — Hebrew זָהָב שָׁחוּט; the meaning of שָׁחוּט is not certain ("beaten", or "alloyed"). **"six hundred shekels of gold went to one target" → "six hundred of gold went up on the one large shield"** — the Hebrew names no weight ("shekels" is commonly understood); יַעֲלֶה עַל is literally "went up upon". **"pound" → "minas"** (v17) — Hebrew מָנֶה.
- **"the House of the Forest of Lebanon"** (v17, 21) — Hebrew בֵּית יַעַר הַלְּבָנוֹן, a building of Solomon in Jerusalem (7:2).
- **"overlaid it with the best gold" → "overlaid it with fine gold"** (v18) — Hebrew זָהָב מוּפָז, found only here; the word is commonly joined with פָּז "fine gold", and its exact meaning is not certain (2 Chr 9:17 has זָהָב טָהוֹר "pure gold"). **"ivory"** — Hebrew שֵׁן, literally "tooth".
- **"the top of the throne was round behind" → "the throne had a round top behind it"** (v19) — Hebrew וְרֹאשׁ־עָגֹל לַכִּסֵּה מֵאַחֲרָיו, literally "a round head". The Septuagint reads "calves' heads" (the same consonants; from memory). **"stays" → "arms"** — Hebrew יָדֹת, literally "hands".
- **"there was not the like made in any kingdom" → "The like was not made for any kingdom"** (v20) — Hebrew לְכָל־מַמְלָכוֹת, plural.
- **"pure gold" → "refined gold"** (v21) — Hebrew זָהָב סָגוּר, literally "shut-up gold", as 6:20, 21; the sense is uncertain (pure, solid or stored gold). **"none were of silver: it was nothing accounted of" → "There was no silver; it was not reckoned as anything"** — Hebrew אֵין כֶּסֶף לֹא נֶחְשָׁב … לִמְאוּמָה.
- **"a navy of Tharshish" → "a fleet of Tarshish"** (v22) — Hebrew אֳנִי תַרְשִׁישׁ: ships that sail to Tarshish, or great sea-going ships. **"bringing" → "carrying"** — Hebrew נֹשְׂאֵת.
- **"ivory, and apes, and peacocks" → "ivory and apes and baboons"** (v22) — Hebrew שֶׁנְהַבִּים וְקֹפִים וְתֻכִּיִּים. The first is "ivory" (a longer word than שֵׁן in v18); the last is found only here and in 2 Chr 9:21, and its meaning is not known: "baboons" (a kind of ape) or "peacocks".
- **"exceeded all the kings" → "became greater than all the kings"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּגְדַּל … מִכֹּל.
- **"sought to Solomon" → "were seeking the face of Solomon"** (v24) — Hebrew מְבַקְשִׁים אֶת־פְּנֵי. **"which God had put in his heart"** — Hebrew נָתַן, "given".
- **"his present" → "his offering"** (v25) — Hebrew מִנְחָה, a gift brought to a superior; the same word is the "grain offering" of the altar. **"armour" → "weapons"** — Hebrew נֵשֶׁק. **"a rate year by year" → "the matter of a year in a year"** — Hebrew דְּבַר־שָׁנָה בְּשָׁנָה, as "the matter of a day in its day" (Exod 5:13): what was due each year.
- **"a thousand and four hundred chariots"** (v26) — רֶכֶב is a collective. **"whom he bestowed in the cities" → "and he led them into the cities"** — Hebrew וַיַּנְחֵם, as pointed from נָחָה "lead". The parallel 2 Chr 1:14 (and 9:25) has וַיַּנִּיחֵם "and he placed them" (one letter more), and so this verse is commonly read. The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"made silver to be in Jerusalem as stones" → "made the silver in Jerusalem like the stones"** (v27) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "gave"; the nouns have the article. **"sycomore trees that are in the vale" → "sycamores that are in the Shephelah"** — Hebrew שְׁפֵלָה, the lowland; the tree is the sycamore fig.
- **"Solomon had horses brought out of Egypt, and linen yarn: the king's merchants received the linen yarn at a price" → "the going out of the horses that Solomon had was from Egypt, and from Kue: the traders of the king would take them from Kue at a price"** (v28) — Hebrew וּמוֹצָא הַסּוּסִים … מִמִּצְרָיִם וּמִקְוֵה סֹחֲרֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ יִקְחוּ מִקְוֵה בִּמְחִיר. מוֹצָא is "going out": where they came from. מִקְוֵה, written twice, is taken as "from Qeveh" (Kue, a land in the south-east of Asia Minor; the Vulgate has "from Coa", from memory). The same letters can be read as a noun, "a gathering": "and the gathering of the king's traders would take a gathering (a drove) at a price"; "linen yarn" is an old guess. "Them" is supplied.
- **"six hundred shekels of silver" → "six hundred of silver"** (v29) — no weight is named in the Hebrew. **"did they bring them out by their means" → "by their hand they would bring them out"** — Hebrew בְּיָדָם יֹצִאוּ.
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v29) — Hebrew אֲרָם.

### Notes
- **Queen of Sheba** — Saba in southern Arabia (modern Yemen). Caravan trade. Later traditions: Ethiopian tradition claims she was Ethiopian queen who bore Solomon a son (Menelik I).
- **Her testimony** (v9) — a foreign monarch speaks of Yahweh's love for Israel. Theological echo of the "house of prayer for all nations" ideal.
- **666 talents** (v14) — the famous number. Rev 13:18 ("number of the beast") may allude to Solomon's excessive gold.
- **Solomon and Deut 17:16–17** — the law of the king forbids him to multiply horses, to make the people return to Egypt for horses, and to multiply silver and gold; this chapter tells of horses (v26), horses from Egypt (v28–29), and gold and silver (v14–27).
- **Tarshish fleet** — sea-going trading vessels. Tarshish possibly Tartessus in Spain or Sardinia.
- **Peak of empire** — ch 10 = Solomonic zenith. Ch 11 = Solomonic collapse.
