# 1 Kings 18

[1] And it came to pass after many days that the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ in the third year, saying, "Go, show yourself to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, and I will give rain upon the face of the ground."

[2] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ went to show himself to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧. And the famine was strong in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[3] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ called to ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧, who was over the house. And ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧ feared ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ greatly.

[4] And it came to pass, when ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ cut off the prophets of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧ took a hundred prophets and hid them, fifty men in the cave, and sustained them with bread and water.

[5] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ said to ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧, "Go in the land to all the springs of water and to all the wadis; perhaps we shall find grass and keep horse and mule alive, and we shall not cut off any of the beasts."

[6] And they divided the land for themselves, to pass through it. ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ went by one way, by himself, and ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧ went by one way, by himself.

[7] And ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧ was on the way, and behold, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ was coming to meet him. And he recognized him and fell on his face and said, "Is this you, my lord ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧?"

[8] And he said to him, "I. Go, say to your lord, 'Behold, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧.'"

[9] And he said, "What have I sinned, that you are giving your servant into the hand of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, to put me to death?

[10] As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ lives, there is no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent to seek you; and they would say, 'He is not here,' and he would make the kingdom and the nation swear that it could not find you.

[11] And now you are saying, 'Go, say to your lord, "Behold, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧."'

[12] And it shall be, I shall go from you, and the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will carry you to where I do not know, and I shall come to tell ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, and he will not find you, and he will kill me. And your servant has feared ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from my youth.

[13] Has it not been told to my lord what I did when ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧ killed the prophets of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I hid of the prophets of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a hundred men, fifty men by fifty in the cave, and sustained them with bread and water?

[14] And now you are saying, 'Go, say to your lord, "Behold, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧"' — and he will kill me."

[15] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts lives, before whom I stand, surely today I will show myself to him."

[16] And ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧ went to meet ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ and told him, and ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ went to meet ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧.

[17] And it came to pass, when ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ saw ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, that ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ said to him, "Is this you, troubler of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"

[18] And he said, "I have not troubled ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, but you and the house of your father, in your forsaking the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and you have gone after the Baals.

[19] And now, send, gather to me all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to Mount ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧, and the prophets of the Baal, four hundred and fifty, and the prophets of the Asherah, four hundred, who eat at the table of ⟦jezebel|Izevel|Jezebel⟧."

[20] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ sent among all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and gathered the prophets to Mount ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧.

[21] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ drew near to all the people and said, "How long are you limping on the two branches? If ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, go after him; and if the Baal, go after him." And the people did not answer him a word.

[22] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to the people, "I remain a prophet of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, I alone; and the prophets of the Baal are four hundred and fifty men.

[23] And let them give us two bulls, and let them choose for themselves the one bull and cut it in pieces and put it on the wood, and fire they shall not put; and I will make ready the other bull and place it on the wood, and fire I will not put.

[24] And you shall call on the name of your god, and I will call on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and it shall be, the god who answers by fire, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧." And all the people answered and said, "The word is good."

[25] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to the prophets of the Baal, "Choose for yourselves the one bull and make it ready first, for you are the many; and call on the name of your god, and fire you shall not put."

[26] And they took the bull that he gave them and made it ready, and they called on the name of the Baal from the morning until the noon, saying, "Baal, answer us!" And there was no voice, and there was none answering. And they limped by the altar that he had made.

[27] And it came to pass at noon that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ mocked them and said, "Call with a loud voice, for he is a god; for he is musing, and he has gone aside, and he is on a journey; perhaps he is asleep and will awake."

[28] And they called with a loud voice and gashed themselves, according to their ordinance, with swords and with spears, until blood poured out upon them.

[29] And it came to pass, when the noon had passed, that they prophesied until the offering up of the grain offering; and there was no voice, and there was none answering, and there was no attention.

[30] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to all the people, "Draw near to me." And all the people drew near to him. And he healed the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that was thrown down.

[31] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, to whom the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had come, saying, "⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be your name."

[32] And he built the stones into an altar in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he made a trench, like a space for two seahs of seed, around the altar.

[33] And he arranged the wood and cut the bull in pieces and put it on the wood. And he said, "Fill four jars with water and pour it on the burnt offering and on the wood."

[34] And he said, "Do it a second time," and they did it a second time. And he said, "Do it a third time," and they did it a third time.

[35] And the water went around the altar, and the trench also he filled with water.

[36] And it came to pass, at the offering up of the grain offering, that ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ the prophet drew near and said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, today let it be known that you are ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I am your servant, and by your word I have done all these things.

[37] Answer me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, answer me, and let this people know that you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, are ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and you have turned their heart backward."

[38] And the fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ fell and consumed the burnt offering and the wood and the stones and the dust, and the water that was in the trench it licked up.

[39] And all the people saw, and they fell on their faces and said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧! ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!"

[40] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to them, "Seize the prophets of the Baal; let not a man of them escape." And they seized them, and ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ brought them down to the wadi ⟦kishon|Qishon|Kishon⟧ and slaughtered them there.

[41] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, "Go up, eat and drink, for there is the sound of the roar of the rain."

[42] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ went up to eat and to drink. And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ went up to the top of ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ and crouched to the earth and put his face between his knees.

[43] And he said to his young man, "Go up, please, look toward the sea." And he went up and looked and said, "There is nothing." And he said, "Go back," seven times.

[44] And it came to pass at the seventh time that he said, "Behold, a small thick cloud, like the palm of a man, is coming up from the sea." And he said, "Go up, say to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, 'Harness and go down, and the rain shall not keep you back.'"

[45] And it came to pass meanwhile that the heavens grew dark with thick clouds and wind, and there was a great rain. And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ rode and went to ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧.

[46] And the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, and he girded up his loins and ran before ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ as far as the coming into ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"after many days" · "I will send rain upon the earth" → "I will give rain upon the face of the ground"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי יָמִים רַבִּים, literally "and there were many days"; "after" is supplied. וְאֶתְּנָה is a form of resolve (cohortative), "and let me give"; הָאֲדָמָה is "the ground".
- **"there was a sore famine" → "the famine was strong"** (v2) — Hebrew חָזָק.
- **"the governor of his house" → "who was over the house"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עַל־הַבָּיִת. The brackets around v3b–4 were not in the Hebrew and are removed.
- **"hid them by fifty in a cave, and fed them" → "hid them, fifty men in the cave, and sustained them"** (v4, 13) — Hebrew חֲמִשִּׁים אִישׁ בַּמְּעָרָה; v13 repeats the number, "fifty, fifty men in the cave": fifty to each cave. כִּלְכֵּל is "sustain". "Cut off" (v4, הַכְרִית) is the verb of v5; v13 has הָרַג "kill".
- **"save the horses and mules alive, that we lose not all the beasts" → "keep horse and mule alive, and we shall not cut off any of the beasts"** (v5) — Hebrew וּנְחַיֶּה (elsewhere "let live") and וְלוֹא נַכְרִית מֵהַבְּהֵמָה, "and not cut off from the beasts": not have to destroy some of them. "Go in the land" is לֵךְ בָּאָרֶץ.
- **"another way" → "by one way"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּדֶרֶךְ אֶחָד both times, "by one way … by one way".
- **"Art thou that my lord Elijah?" → "Is this you"** (v7, 17) — Hebrew הַאַתָּה זֶה.
- **"I am: go, tell thy lord, Behold, Elijah is here" → "I. Go, say to your lord, 'Behold, Elijah.'"** (v8, 11, 14) — Hebrew אָנִי, one word; הִנֵּה אֵלִיָּהוּ, "behold, Elijah": "is here" is not written.
- **"thy servant"** (v9, 12, 36) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word "slave". **"to slay me" → "to put me to death"** — Hebrew הֵמִית; "kill" (v12–14) is הָרַג.
- **"there is no nation or kingdom"** (v10) — the oath form, "As Yahweh lives, if there is a nation or kingdom …": the "if" of an oath means "there is none". "They would say … he would make swear" are forms of repeated action. אַיִן is "there is not"; "he" and "here" are supplied.
- **"as soon as I am gone from thee" → "And it shall be, I shall go from you"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהָיָה אֲנִי אֵלֵךְ. **"carry"** is נָשָׂא "lift up".
- **"I will surely shew myself"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי after the oath, "surely"; עָמַדְתִּי is "I have stood".
- **"he that troubleth Israel" → "troubler of Israel"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew עֹכֵר, the verb of Josh 7:25.
- **"in that ye have forsaken … and thou hast followed Baalim" → "in your forsaking … and you have gone after the Baals"** (v18) — Hebrew הַבְּעָלִים, plural with the article. "Your forsaking" is plural (Ahab and his father's house), "you have gone" is singular (Ahab).
- **"the prophets of Baal … the prophets of the groves" → "the prophets of the Baal … the prophets of the Asherah"** (v19, 22, 25, 26, 40) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל, הָאֲשֵׁרָה, each with the article. The numbers follow their nouns in the Hebrew. "Who eat at the table of Jezebel" is literally "eaters of the table of Jezebel".
- **"sent unto all the children of Israel" → "sent among all the sons of Israel"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּכָל־בְּנֵי.
- **"How long halt ye between two opinions?" → "How long are you limping on the two branches?"** (v21) — Hebrew פֹּסְחִים עַל־שְׁתֵּי הַסְּעִפִּים. פָּסַח is "limp, be lame" (2 Sam 4:4); the same verb stands in v26 of the prophets of the Baal at the altar, and both places have "limp". (Another פָּסַח is the "pass over" of Exod 12.) סְעִפִּים is the plural of סָעִיף, "cleft" (of a rock, Judg 15:8; Isa 2:21) and "branch, bough" (Isa 17:6; 27:10): a thing that divides in two. What is meant here is uncertain: "two opinions" (divided thoughts, like the related words of Ps 119:113; Job 4:13), "two crutches" (cut from boughs), or the fork of a way. The verse keeps the plain word; literally "limping upon the two branches".
- **"if the LORD be God" → "If Yahweh is Elohim"** (v21, 24, 37, 39) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, "the Elohim", with the article each time: the one who really is God. The translation writes "Elohim" without "the" for God through the whole Old Testament (as Deut 4:35, 39); here the article carries the point of the contest and of the people's cry in v39, יְהוָה הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים, "Yahweh, he is the Elohim". In v24 the same word with the article stands twice: "the Elohim who answers by fire, he is the Elohim"; the first is rendered "the god".
- **"follow him" → "go after him"** (v21) — Hebrew לְכוּ אַחֲרָיו, as "gone after the Baals" (v18).
- **"I, even I only, remain a prophet of the LORD" → "I remain a prophet of Yahweh, I alone"** (v22) — Hebrew נוֹתַרְתִּי … לְבַדִּי.
- **"bullocks" → "bulls"; "dress" → "make ready"; "lay … put" → "put … place"** (v23, 25, 26, 33) — Hebrew פַּר; עָשָׂה, the verb "do, make", here of preparing the sacrifice; שִׂים "put" and נָתַן "place" (literally "give"). "The other bull" is "the one bull" a second time (הַפָּר הָאֶחָד). "Fire they shall not put" keeps the Hebrew order.
- **"the name of your gods" → "the name of your god"** (v24, 25) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵיכֶם, which can be "your god" or "your gods"; the Baal is meant. **"It is well spoken" → "The word is good"** — Hebrew טוֹב הַדָּבָר.
- **"for ye are many" → "for you are the many"** (v25) — Hebrew הָרַבִּים.
- **"which was given them" → "that he gave them"** (v26) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־נָתַן לָהֶם, singular with no subject named. **"O Baal, hear us" → "Baal, answer us!"** — Hebrew הַבַּעַל עֲנֵנוּ; the verb "answer" runs through v24–37. **"nor any that answered" → "there was none answering"**.
- **"they leaped upon the altar which was made" → "they limped by the altar that he had made"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיְפַסְּחוּ עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ, the verb of v21 in its intensive form, with the same preposition עַל "upon, by": a limping or hopping dance at the altar. אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה is singular, "that he (or: one) had made"; other texts read "they had made" (from memory).
- **"Elijah mocked them"** (v27) — Hebrew הֵתֵל בְּ, elsewhere "cheat, trifle with" (Gen 31:7; Judg 16:10); here mockery.
- **"Cry aloud: for he is a god; either he is talking, or he is pursuing, or he is in a journey, or peradventure he sleepeth, and must be awaked" → "Call with a loud voice, for he is a god; for he is musing, and he has gone aside, and he is on a journey; perhaps he is asleep and will awake"** (v27) — the Hebrew has three nouns, each with כִּי "for": "for a musing, and for a going aside is his, and for a way is his". (1) שִׂיחַ is "musing, talk, complaint": he is deep in thought or in talk. (2) שִׂיג is found only here; it is taken from the root "move away, withdraw": he has gone aside. It is commonly understood as going aside to relieve himself (so the Targum, from memory); the Hebrew word itself does not say it, and the verse keeps the word as it stands. Some take שִׂיג as a second writing of שִׂיחַ. (3) דֶּרֶךְ is "way": a journey. (4) יָשֵׁן is "asleep"; וְיִקָץ is "and he will awake" (or "and let him awake"). "Call with a loud voice" is literally "with a great voice"; it is the verb of "call on the name" (v24–26).
- **"cut themselves after their manner with knives and lancets, till the blood gushed out" → "gashed themselves, according to their ordinance, with swords and with spears, until blood poured out upon them"** (v28) — Hebrew הִתְגֹּדֵד (forbidden in Deut 14:1); כְּמִשְׁפָּטָם, "according to their judgment": their rule, their custom; עַד־שְׁפָךְ־דָּם עֲלֵיהֶם, "until the pouring out of blood upon them".
- **"when midday was past, and they prophesied until the time of the offering of the evening sacrifice" → "when the noon had passed, that they prophesied until the offering up of the grain offering"** (v29, 36) — Hebrew וַיִּתְנַבְּאוּ, the usual verb "prophesy", here of their frenzy; עַד לַעֲלוֹת הַמִּנְחָה, "until the going up of the *minchah*", the grain offering (of the afternoon): "time" and "evening" are not written. **"nor any that regarded" → "there was no attention"** — Hebrew קָשֶׁב.
- **"repaired the altar of the LORD that was broken down" → "healed the altar of Yahweh that was thrown down"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיְרַפֵּא, the verb "heal"; הָרוּס "torn down".
- **"with the stones he built an altar" → "he built the stones into an altar"** (v32) — the stones are the object. **"a trench … as great as would contain two measures of seed" → "a trench, like a space for two seahs of seed"** — Hebrew כְּבֵית סָאתַיִם זֶרַע, literally "like a house of two seahs of seed": either a trench that would hold two seahs, or one enclosing ground that is sown with two seahs. Not certain.
- **"put the wood in order" → "arranged the wood"** (v33) — Hebrew עָרַךְ. **"barrels" → "jars"** — Hebrew כַּד.
- **"Do it the second time"** (v34) — the Hebrew has one verb each time: שְׁנוּ וַיִּשְׁנוּ, "Do-twice, and they did-twice", שַׁלֵּשׁוּ וַיְשַׁלֵּשׁוּ, "Do-thrice, and they did-thrice".
- **"the water ran round about" → "the water went around"** (v35) — Hebrew וַיֵּלְכוּ.
- **"LORD God of Abraham"** (v36) — the Hebrew has no "O". **"at thy word" → "by your word"** — the reading tradition (qere) is singular; the written text (ketiv) is plural, "by your words".
- **"Hear me, O LORD, hear me, that this people may know" → "Answer me, Yahweh, answer me, and let this people know"** (v37) — Hebrew עֲנֵנִי, the verb of v24, 26, 29. **"thou hast turned their heart back again" → "you have turned their heart backward"** — Hebrew וְאַתָּה הֲסִבֹּתָ אֶת־לִבָּם אֲחֹרַנִּית. It can mean "you have turned their heart back (to yourself)" or "it is you who turned their heart backward (away)"; the Hebrew does not decide, and "backward" is kept.
- **"when all the people saw it" → "all the people saw"** (v39) — no object in the Hebrew.
- **"Take the prophets of Baal" → "Seize"** (v40) — Hebrew תָּפַשׂ. **"brook" → "wadi"** — Hebrew נַחַל. **"slew them" → "slaughtered them"** — Hebrew שָׁחַט, the word for cutting the throat of an animal.
- **"a sound of abundance of rain" → "the sound of the roar of the rain"** (v41) — Hebrew הֲמוֹן, "tumult, roar" and "multitude".
- **"cast himself down upon the earth" → "crouched to the earth"** (v42) — Hebrew גָּהַר, found only here and in 2 Kgs 4:34, 35: "bend, crouch". "His knees" is the reading tradition (qere); the written text has "his knee".
- **"his servant" → "his young man"** (v43) — Hebrew נַעַר. **"Go up now" → "Go up, please"** — Hebrew נָא. "Toward the sea" is literally "the way of the sea".
- **"a little cloud out of the sea, like a man's hand" → "a small thick cloud, like the palm of a man"** (v44) — Hebrew עָב, a thick cloud (v45 "thick clouds"); כַּף "palm". **"Prepare thy chariot, and get thee down, that the rain stop thee not" → "Harness and go down, and the rain shall not keep you back"** — Hebrew אֱסֹר "bind"; עָצַר.
- **"in the mean while" → "meanwhile"** (v45) — Hebrew עַד־כֹּה וְעַד־כֹּה, "until thus and until thus". **"the heaven was black" → "the heavens grew dark"**.
- **"the hand of the LORD was on Elijah"** (v46) — Hebrew אֶל, "to, toward". **"girded up"** is שִׁנֵּס, found only here. **"to the entrance of Jezreel" → "as far as the coming into Jezreel"** — Hebrew עַד־בֹּאֲכָה, "until your coming".

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew, verse 34 begins with "And he said, 'Fill four jars …'", the second half of our v33.
- **Obadiah** — not the prophet of the book of Obadiah. A Yahwist official hiding prophets during Jezebel's purge.
- **Mount Carmel** — Phoenician-Israelite border highland. Baal worship established there. Setting the contest on Baal territory maximizes the polemic.
- **450 prophets of Baal + 400 of Asherah** — Asherah prophets drop out of the narrative (v22ff).
- **"The god who answers by fire"** — Baal = storm-lightning god. Elijah challenges Baal on his strongest supposed attribute.
- **Self-laceration** (v28) — Canaanite mourning/ecstatic rite; Ugaritic texts attest cutting for Baal.
- **Twelve stones / trench** — symbolic reclamation of twelve-tribe unity in a divided kingdom. Excess water makes divine fire more impressive.
- **Slaughter at Kishon** — echoes Deborah's victory (Judg 5:21). Carrying out Deut 13's penalty for prophets of other gods.
- **Man's-palm cloud** — classic weather sign over the Mediterranean. End of drought.
- **Running before Ahab** — a prophetic honor to escort the king.
