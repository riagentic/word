## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 10, 16, 17, 20, 21) — Hebrew *Elohim*. "The gods of the nations", "their gods", "no gods" (v12, 19) and "his god" (v38) are the same word. In v10 the king of Assyria says "your Elohim" of the God of Hezekiah.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel** (whole chapter) — the chapter stands almost word for word in 2 Kgs 19; Isa 37:15–16 are 2 Kgs 19:15, and from there the verse numbers of Kings are one lower. The wording of 2 Kings is kept where the Hebrew is the same; every place where Isaiah's Hebrew differs is translated as Isaiah has it and listed below. Nothing is levelled either way.
- **"Rabshakeh"** (v4, 8) — a title, written without the article, as in chapter 36.
- **"when king Hezekiah heard it, that he rent his clothes … and went into" → "when King Hezekiah heard, that he tore his garments … and came into"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי "and it came to pass"; "it" after "heard" is not written; קָרַע; בְּגָדִים; וַיָּבֹא.
- **"the elders of the priests covered with sackcloth, unto Isaiah the prophet the son of Amoz" → "the elders of the priests, covering themselves with sackcloth, to Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet"** (v2) — a participle, "covering themselves with the sackcloths". Isaiah has the order "the son of Amoz, the prophet" (2 Kgs 19:2 "the prophet, the son of Amoz"), and אֵת before Shebna.
- **"a day of trouble, and of rebuke, and of blasphemy: for the children are come to the birth, and there is not strength to bring forth" → "A day of distress and of reproof and of spurning … sons have come to the place of breaking forth, and there is no power to bear"** (v3) — Hebrew צָרָה; תּוֹכֵחָה, from הוֹכִיחַ "reprove" (v4); נְאָצָה, from נִאֵץ "spurn": whether the day is one on which Judah is spurned or one on which Yahweh is spurned is not said. בָנִים, "sons"; מַשְׁבֵּר, "the place of breaking": the mouth of the womb.
- **"It may be the LORD thy God will hear the words of Rabshakeh … and will reprove the words" → "Perhaps Yahweh your Elohim will hear the words of Rabshakeh … and will reprove the words"** (v4) — Hebrew אוּלַי; וְהוֹכִיחַ בַּדְּבָרִים, "and reprove for (or with) the words". Isaiah has "the words" where 2 Kgs 19:4 has "all the words". "The living Elohim" is אֱלֹהִים חַי, without the article (v4, 17). **"lift up thy prayer for the remnant that is left" → "lift up a prayer for the remnant that is found"** — Hebrew הַנִּמְצָאָה.
- **"Be not afraid of the words … wherewith the servants of the king of Assyria have blasphemed me" → "Do not fear because of the words … with which the young men of the king of Assyria have reviled me"** (v6) — Hebrew נַעֲרֵי, "boys, attendants": a slighting word for the king's officers; גִּדֵּף, "revile". **"Thus shall ye say unto your master" → "Thus you shall say to your lord"** — Hebrew אֲדֹנֵיכֶם.
- **"I will send a blast upon him, and he shall hear a rumour" → "I am placing a spirit in him, and he shall hear a report"** (v7) — Hebrew נוֹתֵן בּוֹ רוּחַ, "giving a spirit in him"; רוּחַ is "spirit, wind, breath"; שְׁמוּעָה, "a thing heard".
- **"warring against Libnah: for he had heard that he was departed" → "fighting against Libnah, for he had heard that he had set out"** (v8) — Hebrew נִלְחָם; נָסַע.
- **"he heard say concerning Tirhakah king of Ethiopia, He is come forth to make war with thee. And when he heard it, he sent messengers" → "he heard concerning Tirhakah king of Cush, saying, 'He has come out to fight with you.' And he heard, and he sent messengers"** (v9) — Hebrew כּוּשׁ. Isaiah has וַיִּשְׁמַע "and he heard" where 2 Kgs 19:9 has וַיָּשָׁב "and he returned" (= sent again), and no הִנֵּה "behold"; עַל "concerning" (2 Kgs אֶל).
- **"in whom thou trustest"** (v10) — a participle, "in whom you are trusting"; the word of 36:4–15.
- **"by destroying them utterly" → "devoting them to destruction"** (v11) — Hebrew לְהַחֲרִימָם.
- **"which my fathers have destroyed" → "which my fathers ruined"** (v12) — Hebrew הִשְׁחִיתוּ, hifil (2 Kgs 19:12 the piel שִׁחֲתוּ); one English verb. **"the children of Eden which were in Telassar" → "the sons of Eden who were in Telassar"**.
- **"Where is the king of Hamath"** (v13) — Hebrew אַיֵּה (2 Kgs 19:13 אַיּוֹ, "where is he"). The names Hena and Ivvah stand without "of" in the Hebrew.
- **"Hezekiah received the letter … and read it … and spread it" → "took the letters … and read it … and spread it out"** (v14) — Hebrew הַסְּפָרִים, plural, "the books, written sheets", and then two singular suffixes, "read it", "spread it" (2 Kgs 19:14 "read them"); kept as written.
- **"prayed unto the LORD, saying" → "prayed to Yahweh, saying"** (v15) — Isaiah has אֶל … לֵאמֹר, and v16 opens the prayer (2 Kgs 19:15 "prayed before Yahweh and said").
- **"O LORD of hosts, God of Israel, that dwellest between the cherubims, thou art the God, even thou alone, of all the kingdoms of the earth" → "Yahweh of Hosts, Elohim of Israel, who sits upon the cherubim, you are Elohim, you alone, for all the kingdoms of the earth"** (v16) — the Hebrew has no "O"; Isaiah has יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת "Yahweh of Hosts" where 2 Kgs 19:15 has "Yahweh, Elohim of Israel" only. יֹשֵׁב הַכְּרֻבִים, "sitting (on) the cherubim" ("upon" supplied), as 1 Sam 4:4; אַתָּה־הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים, "you are he, the Elohim": the article is in the Hebrew. **"heaven and earth" → "the heavens and the earth"**.
- **"Incline thine ear … open thine eyes … hear all the words of Sennacherib, which hath sent to reproach" → "Turn your ear … open your eye … hear all the words of Sennacherib, which he has sent to reproach"** (v17) — Hebrew הַטֵּה "turn, incline"; עֵינֶךָ, singular "your eye", as printed (2 Kgs 19:16 עֵינֶיךָ "your eyes"); כָּל "all" (not in 2 Kgs); אֲשֶׁר שָׁלַח, "which he has sent", of the words (2 Kgs שְׁלָחוֹ "who has sent him").
- **"have laid waste all the nations, and their countries" → "have laid waste all the lands and their land"** (v18) — Hebrew אֶת־כָּל־הָאֲרָצוֹת וְאֶת־אַרְצָם: Isaiah has "all the lands" where 2 Kgs 19:17 has "the nations", and so "the lands and their land" stands side by side; "their land" is singular. Kept as printed; the sense is hard, and "the nations" of Kings is not brought in. הֶחֱרִיב "lay waste", the verb of "dry up" in v25.
- **"have cast their gods into the fire … therefore they have destroyed them" → "they have put their gods in the fire … and they did away with them"** (v19) — Hebrew וְנָתֹן, an infinitive absolute carrying on the verb of v18 ("and putting"; 2 Kgs 19:18 the finite וְנָתְנוּ); וַיְאַבְּדוּם.
- **"save us from his hand, that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that thou art the LORD, even thou only" → "save us from his hand, and let all the kingdoms of the earth know that you are Yahweh, you alone"** (v20) — Hebrew וְיֵדְעוּ. Isaiah has no נָא "please" and reads "that you are Yahweh, you alone" (2 Kgs 19:19 "that you, Yahweh, are Elohim, you alone").
- **"Whereas thou hast prayed to me against Sennacherib king of Assyria" → "Because you have prayed to me concerning Sennacherib king of Assyria,"** (v21) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הִתְפַּלַּלְתָּ אֵלַי אֶל; Isaiah lacks the "I have heard" (שָׁמַעְתִּי) that closes 2 Kgs 19:20, so the clause runs into v22: אֲשֶׁר is read "because" (or "as to what you have prayed"); אֶל "to, concerning".
- **"hath despised thee, and laughed thee to scorn … hath shaken her head at thee" → "has despised you, she has derided you … behind you she has shaken her head"** (v22) — Hebrew בָּזָה; לָעַג; אַחֲרֶיךָ, "behind you": as he goes away; הֵנִיעָה, "made sway".
- **"Whom hast thou reproached and blasphemed?" → "reproached and reviled"** (v23) — Hebrew חֵרֵף; גִּדֵּף. **"exalted thy voice" → "raised your voice"** — Hebrew הֲרִימוֹתָה. "Against the Holy One" is אֶל (2 Kgs 19:22 עַל); one English.
- **"By thy servants" → "By the hand of your servants"** (v24) — Hebrew בְּיַד עֲבָדֶיךָ, where 2 Kgs 19:23 has "your messengers". **"the Lord"** — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי, not the name Yahweh. **"By the multitude of my chariots am I come up to the height of the mountains, to the sides of Lebanon; and I will cut down the tall cedars thereof, and the choice fir trees thereof: and I will enter into the height of his border, and the forest of his Carmel" → "With the multitude of my chariots I, I have gone up the height of the mountains, the remote parts of Lebanon; and I will cut down the height of its cedars, the choice of its cypresses; and I will enter its farthest height, the forest of its garden land"** — Hebrew בְּרֹב רִכְבִּי, written so here (2 Kgs 19:23 has it only as the qere); אֲנִי, the pronoun set out; יַרְכְּתֵי, "the flanks": the far recesses; קוֹמַת אֲרָזָיו, "the stature of its cedars"; בְּרוֹשׁ, a conifer whose kind is not certain. Isaiah has מְרוֹם קִצּוֹ, "the height of its end", where 2 Kgs has מְלוֹן קִצֹּה "its farthest lodging place"; כַּרְמֶל, "garden land, orchard", the word that is also the name Carmel.
- **"I have digged, and drunk water; and with the sole of my feet have I dried up all the rivers of the besieged places" → "I, I have dug and drunk water, and I will dry up with the sole of my feet all the Niles of Matzor"** (v25) — Hebrew קַרְתִּי; מַיִם, "water", with no "strange" (2 Kgs 19:24 מַיִם זָרִים); וְאַחְרִב, a future beside the past; יְאֹרֵי מָצוֹר: יְאֹר is the Nile and its arms, and מָצוֹר is a name of Egypt (Isa 19:6; Mic 7:12) that sounds like "siege, fortress"; the name is kept as printed. The word מִצְרַיִם, "Egypt", is not written here (the earlier "all the streams of Egypt" put it in).
- **"Hast thou not heard long ago, how I have done it; and of ancient times, that I have formed it? now have I brought it to pass, that thou shouldest be to lay waste defenced cities into ruinous heaps" → "Have you not heard? From afar I made it, from days of old, and I formed it; now I have brought it, and you are to crash fortified cities into ruined heaps"** (v26) — Hebrew לְמֵרָחוֹק, "from far off" in time; עָשִׂיתִי; מִימֵי קֶדֶם (2 Kgs 19:25 לְמִימֵי); יְצַרְתִּיהָ; הֲבֵאתִיהָ, "I have made it come"; וּתְהִי לְהַשְׁאוֹת, "and you are (or: and it is) for crashing into ruin"; גַּלִּים נִצִּים.
- **"their inhabitants were of small power, they were dismayed and confounded: they were as the grass of the field, and as the green herb, as the grass on the housetops, and as corn blasted before it be grown up" → "those who dwell in them, short of hand, were dismayed and ashamed; they became plants of the field and green vegetation, grass of the roofs, and a field before the standing grain"** (v27) — Hebrew קִצְרֵי־יָד; חַתּוּ וָבֹשׁוּ; עֵשֶׂב שָׂדֶה וִירַק דֶּשֶׁא; חֲצִיר גַּגּוֹת. Isaiah has שְׁדֵמָה, "a field, terrace" (Deut 32:32; 2 Kgs 23:4), where 2 Kgs 19:26 has שְׁדֵפָה "a blight"; the words differ by one letter, and the MT of Isaiah is kept. What "a field before the standing grain" means is not clear; שָׂדֶה earlier in the verse is another word with the same English.
- **"I know thy abode, and thy going out, and thy coming in, and thy rage against me" → "your sitting and your going out and your coming in I know, and your raging against me"** (v28, 29) — Hebrew שִׁבְתְּךָ; הִתְרַגֶּזְךָ, from רָגַז "be agitated, tremble".
- **"thy tumult is come up into mine ears" → "your ease has come up into my ears"** (v29) — Hebrew שַׁאֲנַנְךָ, "your ease": the careless pride of one who feels secure. **"I will turn thee back" → "I will bring you back"** — Hebrew וַהֲשִׁיבֹתִיךָ.
- **"Ye shall eat this year such as groweth of itself; and the second year that which springeth of the same" → "eat this year the aftergrowth, and in the second year what springs of itself"** (v30) — "for you" is singular, to Hezekiah. אָכוֹל is a bare infinitive, "eating"; סָפִיחַ, as Lev 25:5; שָׁחִיס is found only here (2 Kgs 19:29 סָחִישׁ) and its exact sense is uncertain: what grows of itself in the second year. **"and eat the fruit thereof"** — the verse follows the reading tradition (qere) וְאִכְלוּ, "and eat"; the written text (ketiv) has ואכול, "and eating", the infinitive of the start of the verse.
- **"the remnant that is escaped of the house of Judah shall again take root downward, and bear fruit upward" → "the escape of the house of Judah that is left shall add root downward and make fruit upward"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew פְּלֵיטָה, "escape": those who have escaped; הַנִּשְׁאָרָה, "that is left"; וְיָסְפָה … שֹׁרֶשׁ, "shall add root": take root again; עָשָׂה פְרִי. In v32 שְׁאֵרִית, "remnant", stands beside פְּלֵיטָה.
- **"the zeal of the LORD of hosts"** (v32) — "of Hosts" is written in the Hebrew text here (in 2 Kgs 19:31 it is only read). קִנְאָה is "jealousy, zeal".
- **"He shall not come into this city … nor come before it with shields, nor cast a bank against it" → "He shall not come to this city … and he shall not come to meet it with a shield, and he shall not pour out a mound against it"** (v33) — Hebrew אֶל; קִדֵּם; מָגֵן, singular; שָׁפַךְ סֹלְלָה: the earth of a siege mound is poured out.
- **"By the way that he came" → "By the way by which he came"** (v34) — Hebrew בָּא, a perfect (2 Kgs 19:33 יָבֹא "he comes"). **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** — Hebrew נְאֻם, "the declaration of".
- **"I will defend this city to save it for mine own sake" → "I will shield this city, to save it, on account of myself"** (v35) — Hebrew גָּנַן, the root of מָגֵן "shield" (v33); עַל (2 Kgs 19:34 אֶל); לְמַעֲנִי.
- **"Then the angel of the LORD went forth, and smote … they were all dead corpses" → "And the angel of Yahweh went out and struck down … all of them were dead carcasses"** (v36) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה: the word is "messenger"; הִכָּה; פְּגָרִים מֵתִים. Isaiah has no "And it came to pass on that night" (2 Kgs 19:35). Who "rose early" is not said; KJV "when they arose" and the earlier "when people arose" supply it. "The camp of Assyria" (not "of the Assyrians").
- **"departed, and went and returned, and dwelt at Nineveh" → "set out and went and returned, and he dwelt in Nineveh"** (v37) — three verbs, as in the Hebrew.
- **"as he was worshipping in the house of Nisroch his god … his sons smote him … the land of Armenia" → "as he was bowing down … his sons struck him down … the land of Ararat"** (v38) — Hebrew מִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה; אֲרָרָט. "His sons" is written here (in 2 Kgs 19:37 it is only read). No god Nisroch is known from elsewhere.

### Notes
- **Parallel** — 2 Kgs 19; compare 2 Chr 32:20–21.
- **Tirhakah king of Cush** (v9) — Taharqa, of the Cushite line that ruled Egypt.
- **The prayer of Hezekiah** (v15–20) — he answers the words of 36:18–20 and 37:10–13: the gods of the nations were the work of the hands of man.
- **The word concerning Sennacherib** (v22–29) — poetry. In v24–25 the king of Assyria is quoted; in v26–29 Yahweh speaks to him.
- **The sign** (v30) — two years of what grows of itself, and sowing in the third.
- **The death of Sennacherib** (v38) — the verse does not say how long after his return it was.
