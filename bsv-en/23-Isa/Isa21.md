# Isaiah 21

[1] The burden of the wilderness of the sea.

Like tempests in the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧, sweeping through,
from the wilderness it comes,
from a fearsome land.

[2] A hard vision has been told to me:
the treacherous one deals treacherously,
and the waster lays waste.
Go up, Elam!
Besiege, ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧!
All her sighing I have made cease.

[3] Therefore my loins are filled with anguish;
pangs have taken hold of me,
like the pangs of a woman giving birth;
I am bent from hearing,
I am dismayed from seeing.

[4] My heart wanders, shuddering has terrified me;
the twilight of my longing
he has made for me into trembling.

[5] Arranging the table,
spreading the rug,
eating, drinking!
Arise, princes,
anoint the shield!

[6] For thus ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has said to me:

"Go, station the watchman;
what he sees, let him tell.

[7] And he shall see chariots, a pair of horsemen,
chariots of donkeys, chariots of camels,
and he shall listen closely,
very closely."

[8] And he called out, a lion:

"Upon a watchtower, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, I am standing
continually by day,
and at my post I am stationed
all the nights.

[9] And behold, here comes a chariot of men,
a pair of horsemen!"

And he answered and said:

"Fallen, fallen is ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and all the carved images of her gods
he has broken in pieces to the ground."

[10] My threshed one, and son of my threshing floor,
what I have heard from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, I have told to you.

[11] The burden of Dumah.

To me one is calling from Seir:
"Watchman, what of the night?
Watchman, what of the night?"

[12] The watchman has said:
"Morning has come, and also night.
If you will inquire, inquire;
turn back, come."

[13] The burden in ⟦arabia|Arav|Arabia⟧.

In the forest in ⟦arabia|Arav|Arabia⟧ you shall lodge,
caravans of ⟦dedanites|Dedanim|Dedanites⟧.

[14] To meet the thirsty bring water;
the inhabitants of the land of Tema
with his bread have come to meet the fugitive.

[15] For from the face of swords they have fled,
from the face of the drawn sword,
and from the face of the bent bow,
and from the face of the heaviness of war.

[16] For thus ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has said to me: "Within yet a year, like the years of a hired man, and all the glory of ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧ shall come to an end.

[17] And the remnant of the number of the bow, the mighty ones of the sons of ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧, shall be few, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, has spoken."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v10, 17) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v10) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 17). **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v6, 8, 16) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order. v2 "all her sighing I have made cease" is one colon and now one line; v10 is set as the two halves of the Hebrew verse; v14 follows the Hebrew order of its lines.
- **"The burden of the desert of the sea" → "The burden of the wilderness of the sea"** (v1), **"The burden of Dumah"** (v11), **"The burden upon Arabia" → "The burden in Arabia"** (v13) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא, "what is lifted up", a load (the same word as "the burden" on the peg, 22:25); usually rendered "oracle" (a lifting up of the voice). מִדְבַּר־יָם "the wilderness of the sea": what place is meant is not said (Babylon, v9). v13 בַּעְרָב: בְּ "in" (or "against"); the same letters can be read "in the steppe" or "in the evening": the pointing gives Arabia, as in the next line.
- **"As whirlwinds in the south pass through" → "Like tempests in the Negev, sweeping through"** (v1) — Hebrew סוּפוֹת "tempests"; לַחֲלֹף "to pass through, sweep on". **"it cometh"** — בָּא, a participle without a named subject: "it comes". **"a terrible land" → "a fearsome land"** — נוֹרָאָה, nifal participle of יָרֵא "fear".
- **"A grievous vision" → "A hard vision"** (v2) — Hebrew חָזוּת קָשָׁה; **"is declared unto me" → "has been told to me"** (הֻגַּד; also v6 "let him tell", v10 "I have told", one verb). **"the treacherous dealer dealeth treacherously, and the spoiler spoileth" → "the treacherous one deals treacherously, and the waster lays waste"** — Hebrew בּוֹגֵד … שׁוֹדֵד, each participle with its verb (שָׁדַד "lay waste", as Prov 24:15). **"O Elam … O Media" → "Elam! … Media!"** — the Hebrew has no "O". **"all the sighing thereof have I made to cease" → "All her sighing I have made cease"** — אַנְחָתָה "her sighing": the sighing she caused or her own (the suffix allows both); הִשְׁבַּתִּי, a perfect, hifil of שָׁבַת "cease".
- **"pangs as the pangs of a woman that travaileth" → "pangs … like the pangs of a woman giving birth"** (v3) — Hebrew צִירִים, יוֹלֵדָה. **"I was bowed down at the hearing of it; I was dismayed at the seeing of it" → "I am bent from hearing, I am dismayed from seeing"** — נַעֲוֵיתִי מִשְּׁמֹעַ נִבְהַלְתִּי מֵרְאוֹת: מִן may mean "because of" (what I hear and see) or "away from, so that I cannot" (hear and see); "from" keeps both.
- **"My heart panted, fearfulness affrighted me" → "My heart wanders, shuddering has terrified me"** (v4) — Hebrew תָּעָה "wander, go astray"; פַּלָּצוּת "shuddering"; בִּעֵת "terrify". **"the night of my pleasure hath he turned into fear unto me" → "the twilight of my longing he has made for me into trembling"** — נֶשֶׁף "twilight"; חֵשֶׁק "longing, desire"; שָׂם "he has set, made", with no subject named; חֲרָדָה "trembling" (verb חָרַד).
- **"Prepare the table, watch in the watchtower, eat, drink" → "Arranging the table, spreading the rug, eating, drinking!"** (v5) — Hebrew עָרֹךְ … צָפֹה … אָכוֹל שָׁתֹה, four infinitives absolute with no person or subject: they may be commands or a description of the feast; the English infinitive-like forms keep that open. צָפֹה הַצָּפִית is uncertain: צָפִית is found only here; "spread the rug" (צָפָה "overlay, spread") or "keep the watch" (צָפָה "look out"; KJV). **"anoint the shield"** — מִשְׁחוּ מָגֵן, kept.
- **"Go, set a watchman, let him declare what he seeth" → "Go, station the watchman; what he sees, let him tell"** (v6) — Hebrew הַעֲמֵד "make stand"; הַמְצַפֶּה "the watchman" (one who looks out, from צָפָה; in v11–12 "watchman" is שֹׁמֵר, "one who keeps": English has one word, the Hebrew two).
- **"And he saw a chariot with a couple of horsemen" → "And he shall see chariots, a pair of horsemen"** (v7) — Hebrew וְרָאָה, a perfect with וְ after the command: "and he shall see" (or "and when he sees"). רֶכֶב "chariots" (a collective; it can also mean a train of riders or mounts, as "chariots of donkeys, chariots of camels"); צֶמֶד "a pair, a yoke". **"and he hearkened diligently with much heed" → "and he shall listen closely, very closely"** — literally "and he shall give heed, heed, much of heed" (הִקְשִׁיב קֶשֶׁב רַב־קָשֶׁב).
- **"And he cried, A lion" → "And he called out, a lion"** (v8) — the Masoretic Text has אַרְיֵה "a lion" (Num 23:24 row), with the accent dividing the verse after it: the word may be the subject ("a lion called out"), the content of the cry, or "like a lion". The scroll 1QIsa and many translations read הָרֹאֶה "the one who saw" (= the watchman; from memory): named here, not followed (OT.md). **"My lord, I stand" → "Adonai, I am standing"** — אֲדֹנָי with the divine pointing; no "O". **"whole nights" → "all the nights"** — כָּל־הַלֵּילוֹת. נִצָּב "stationed".
- **"here cometh a chariot of men" → "here comes a chariot of men"** (v9) — רֶכֶב אִישׁ (אִישׁ "man", as in 22:6 אָדָם; both "men" in English). **"And he answered and said"** — וַיַּעַן וַיֹּאמֶר, both verbs kept. **"all the graven images of her gods he hath broken unto the ground" → "all the carved images of her gods he has broken in pieces to the ground"** — פְּסִילֵי; שִׁבַּר (piel of שָׁבַר "break"); אֱלֹהֶיהָ "her gods", plural beings; no subject named for "he has broken".
- **"O my threshing, and the corn of my floor" → "My threshed one, and son of my threshing floor"** (v10) — Hebrew מְדֻשָׁתִי "my threshed one", בֶּן־גָּרְנִי "son of my threshing floor" (בֵּן kept; = what comes from it). **"have I declared unto you" → "I have told to you"** — לָכֶם, plural.
- **"He calleth to me out of Seir" → "To me one is calling from Seir"** (v11) — קֹרֵא, a participle, no subject. **"Watchman, what of the night?"** — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר מַה־מִלַּיְלָה … מַה־מִלֵּיל, literally "what from the night" (= how much of it is past); two forms of the word for night.
- **"The watchman said, The morning cometh, and also the night" → "The watchman has said: Morning has come, and also night"** (v12) — Hebrew אָמַר, אָתָה, perfects; אָתָה, בְּעָה "inquire" and אֵתָיוּ "come" are Aramaic-like forms. **"return, come"** — שֻׁבוּ אֵתָיוּ, two imperatives ("come back again" merged them).
- **"In the forest in Arabia shall ye lodge, O ye travelling companies of Dedanim" → "In the forest in Arabia you shall lodge, caravans of Dedanites"** (v13) — יַעַר "forest"; אֹרְחוֹת "caravans"; no "O".
- **"The inhabitants of the land of Tema brought water to him that was thirsty, they prevented with their bread him that fled" → "To meet the thirsty bring water; the inhabitants of the land of Tema with his bread have come to meet the fugitive"** (v14) — Hebrew הֵתָיוּ, pointed as an imperative ("bring"), the first line ending at the main accent; קִדְּמוּ, a perfect ("have come to meet"); בְּלַחְמוֹ "with his bread" (the bread for him). נֹדֵד "fugitive", the root of נָדָדוּ "they have fled" (v15).
- **"For they fled from the swords" → "For from the face of swords they have fled"** (v15) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי four times, kept; **"the drawn sword"** — חֶרֶב נְטוּשָׁה, literally "a sword let loose" (uncertain); **"the bent bow"** — קֶשֶׁת דְּרוּכָה, literally "trodden" (the bow strung with the foot); **"the grievousness of war" → "the heaviness of war"** — כֹּבֶד, root of כָּבֵד "heavy".
- **"Within a year, according to the years of an hireling" → "Within yet a year, like the years of a hired man"** (v16) — בְּעוֹד שָׁנָה; שָׂכִיר "hired man" (his years counted exactly). **"and all the glory of Kedar shall fail" → "and all the glory of Kedar shall come to an end"** — the וְ before the clause is kept.
- **"And the residue of the number of archers, the mighty men of the children of Kedar" → "And the remnant of the number of the bow, the mighty ones of the sons of Kedar"** (v17) — Hebrew מִסְפַּר־קֶשֶׁת "number of the bow"; גִּבּוֹרֵי "mighty ones"; בְּנֵי "sons of". **"shall be diminished" → "shall be few"** — יִמְעָטוּ.

### Notes
- **v9** — "Fallen, fallen is Babylon": echoed in Rev 14:8; 18:2.
