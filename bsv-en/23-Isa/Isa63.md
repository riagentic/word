# Isaiah 63

[1] Who is this who comes from Edom,
crimsoned of garments, from ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧,
this one splendid in his clothing,
striding in the greatness of his power?
"I, who speak in righteousness,
great to save."

[2] Why is there red on your clothing,
and your garments like one who treads in a winepress?

[3] "The trough I have trodden alone,
and of the peoples there was no man with me;
and I trod them in my anger,
and trampled them in my fury;
and their juice spattered on my garments,
and all my clothing I have stained.

[4] For a day of vengeance was in my heart,
and the year of my redeemed has come.

[5] And I looked, and there was none helping,
and I was appalled, and there was none upholding;
and my arm saved for me,
and my fury, it upheld me.

[6] And I trampled down peoples in my anger,
and made them drunk in my fury,
and brought down their juice to the earth."

[7] The ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|acts of steadfast love⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will mention,
the praises of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has dealt to us,
and the great goodness to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
which he has dealt to them according to his compassion
and according to the abundance of his ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|acts of steadfast love⟧.

[8] And he said, "Surely they are my people,
sons who will not deal falsely";
and he became their savior.

[9] In all their distress he had distress,
and the ⟦malakh|mal'akh|angel⟧ of his face saved them;
in his love and in his pity he redeemed them,
and he lifted them up and carried them all the days of old.

[10] And they rebelled
and grieved his holy spirit;
and he was turned to be their enemy,
he himself fought against them.

[11] And he remembered the days of old,
⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, his people:
Where is he who brought them up from the sea
with the shepherds of his flock?
Where is he who set in its midst
his holy spirit,

[12] who made the arm of his beauty go
at the right hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧,
splitting the waters before them
to make for himself an everlasting name,

[13] who made them walk through the deeps?
Like the horse in the wilderness, they did not stumble.

[14] Like the beast that goes down into the plain,
the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave him rest.
So you led your people,
to make for yourself a name of beauty.

[15] Look from the heavens and see,
from the lofty dwelling of your holiness and your beauty.
Where are your zeal and your mighty deeds?
The stirring of your inward parts and your compassion
toward me have held themselves back.

[16] For you are our Father,
for ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ does not know us,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ does not recognize us;
you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, are our Father,
our redeemer from of old is your name.

[17] Why, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do you make us wander from your ways,
do you harden our heart from fearing you?
Return on account of your servants,
the tribes of your inheritance.

[18] For a little while the people of your holiness possessed;
our adversaries have trampled your sanctuary.

[19] We have become those whom from of old you have not ruled,
over whom your name has not been called.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Speakers** (v1–6) — a watcher asks (v1a, 2), the one who comes answers (v1b, 3–6); the Hebrew marks no speakers: the quotation marks follow the sense.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ / וַ "and"; kept (v3, 5, 6, 8–11), also where KJV has "for", "therefore", "but", "so", "then" (v3, 5, 8, 10, 11).
- **Tenses in v3–6** — the verbs after the first perfect (דָּרַכְתִּי "I have trodden") are mostly וְ with an imperfect (וְאֶדְרְכֵם, וְאֶרְמְסֵם, וְיֵז, וְאַבִּיט, וְאֶשְׁתּוֹמֵם, וְאָבוּס, וַאֲשַׁכְּרֵם, וְאוֹרִיד), which can tell what is to come (KJV "I will tread … I will stain … I will tread down"); beside them stand perfects (אֶגְאָלְתִּי, בָּאָה, סְמָכָתְנִי) and the past narrative form וַתּוֹשַׁע "and it saved". The verse renders the whole as one past account; the future reading is possible for v3b and v6.
- **"with dyed garments from Bozrah" → "crimsoned of garments, from Bozrah"** (v1) — Hebrew חֲמוּץ בְּגָדִים, "red-stained as to garments" (חָמַץ here "be red, bright"; not the חָמוֹץ "ruthless" of 1:17); Edom and Bozrah (its city), a play on אָדֹם "red" (v2) and בָּצִיר "vintage". **"this that is glorious in his apparel" → "this one splendid in his clothing"** — זֶה הָדוּר בִּלְבוּשׁוֹ, passive participle of הָדַר "adorn" (root of הָדָר "splendor"); לְבוּשׁ "clothing" (v1, 2), beside בְּגָדִים "garments" and מַלְבּוּשׁ "clothing" (v3). **"travelling" → "striding"** — צֹעֶה, from צָעָה "bend, stoop" (Isa 51:14 "the one bowed down"); here of a proud gait, or read צֹעֵד "marching" (a common change of one letter): uncertain; MT kept. **"strength" → "power"** — כֹּחַ. **"I that speak in righteousness, mighty to save" → "I, who speak in righteousness, great to save"** — אֲנִי מְדַבֵּר בִּצְדָקָה רַב לְהוֹשִׁיעַ; רַב "great, much".
- **"Wherefore art thou red in thine apparel" → "Why is there red on your clothing"** (v2) — Hebrew מַדּוּעַ אָדֹם לִלְבוּשֶׁךָ, literally "why red to your clothing". **"like him that treadeth in the winefat" → "like one who treads in a winepress"** — כְּדֹרֵךְ בְּגַת; גַּת "winepress" (the treading place).
- **"I have trodden the winepress alone" → "The trough I have trodden alone"** (v3) — Hebrew פּוּרָה (also Hag 2:16), a trough or vat for treading, beside גַּת of v2 (English has one word for both): uncertain. **"and of the people there was none with me" → "and of the peoples there was no man with me"** — מֵעַמִּים plural; אֵין־אִישׁ "no man". **"fury"** — חֵמָה (v3, 5, 6). **"their blood shall be sprinkled" → "their juice spattered"** — נִצְחָם, "their juice" (the juice of the grapes, the blood of the trodden; the word נֵצַח elsewhere "eminence, endurance": the sense here is uncertain; also v6, KJV "strength"); יֵז, from נָזָה "spatter, sprinkle". **"and I will stain all my raiment" → "and all my clothing I have stained"** — Hebrew order; אֶגְאָלְתִּי, a mixed form from גָּאַל II "defile, stain" (another root than גָּאַל "redeem" of v4, 9, 16).
- **"For the day of vengeance is in mine heart" → "For a day of vengeance was in my heart"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי יוֹם נָקָם בְּלִבִּי, no verb, no article (as 61:2). **"and the year of my redeemed is come" → "and the year of my redeemed has come"** — שְׁנַת גְּאוּלַי, "my redeemed ones" (passive participle, as 62:12; the former "my year of redemption" made it a noun of action).
- **"there was none to help … there was none to uphold" → "there was none helping … there was none upholding"** (v5) — participles עֹזֵר, סוֹמֵךְ. **"and I wondered" → "and I was appalled"** — אֶשְׁתּוֹמֵם, hitpolel of שָׁמֵם "be desolate, appalled" (Isa 59:16 has the same verse with other words). **"therefore mine own arm brought salvation unto me" → "and my arm saved for me"** — וַתּוֹשַׁע לִי זְרֹעִי; "own" is not in the Hebrew. **"my fury, it upheld me"** — הִיא kept.
- **"And I will tread down the people" → "And I trampled down peoples"** (v6) — וְאָבוּס עַמִּים, plural, no article; בּוּס "trample down" (as v18). **"make them drunk"** — וַאֲשַׁכְּרֵם (some Hebrew manuscripts have וַאֲשַׁבְּרֵם "and I shattered them", one letter different: from memory; MT kept). **"their strength" → "their juice"** — נִצְחָם as v3.
- **"I will mention the lovingkindnesses of the LORD" → "The acts of chesed of Yahweh I will mention"** (v7) — Hebrew חַסְדֵי יְהוָה אַזְכִּיר, the plural of חֶסֶד (token, as Ps 89:1), first in the verse; אַזְכִּיר, hifil of זָכַר "mention" (literally "cause to be remembered"). The former first line added a second rendering of the same word ("the mercies … the steadfast love deeds"). **"bestowed on us … bestowed on them" → "dealt to us … dealt to them"** — גָּמַל "deal" (twice). **"according to his mercies" → "according to his compassion"** — כְּרַחֲמָיו, רַחֲמִים "compassion" (plural in form). **"the multitude of his lovingkindnesses" → "the abundance of his acts of chesed"** — וּכְרֹב חֲסָדָיו, plural (token).
- **"children that will not lie" → "sons who will not deal falsely"** (v8) — בָּנִים "sons"; שִׁקֵּר "deal falsely". **"so he was their Saviour" → "and he became their savior"** — וַיְהִי לָהֶם לְמוֹשִׁיעַ, literally "and he became for them a savior"; מוֹשִׁיעַ "savior" (as 60:16).
- **"In all their affliction he was afflicted" → "In all their distress he had distress"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּכָל־צָרָתָם לוֹ צָר: the qere לוֹ "to him" is translated ("to him there was distress"); the written form (ketiv) is לֹא "not": "in all their distress he was not distressed", or, joined to what follows, "not an envoy (צִיר) nor an angel, his face saved them" (the Septuagint reads so, from memory). צָרָה "distress" (as Gen 42:21). **"the angel of his presence" → "the angel of his face"** — מַלְאַךְ פָּנָיו; מַלְאָךְ "messenger, angel" (one word); פָּנִים "face" (as Exod 33:14 "my face shall go"); the token form no longer carries a gloss. **"pity"** — חֶמְלָה. **"and he bare them, and carried them" → "and he lifted them up and carried them"** — וַיְנַטְּלֵם (נָטַל piel "lift up") וַיְנַשְּׂאֵם (נָשָׂא piel "carry"). **"all the days of old"** — כָּל־יְמֵי עוֹלָם, kept.
- **"But they rebelled, and vexed his holy Spirit" → "And they rebelled and grieved his holy spirit"** (v10) — וְהֵמָּה "and they" (pronoun stressed); מָרָה "rebel" (as Num 20:10); עִצֵּב "grieve" (as Eph 4:30 in Greek); רוּחַ קָדְשׁוֹ, literally "the spirit of his holiness" (as Ps 51:11): plain "spirit", as the Hebrew has no capital and no fixed name (not רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ, the later term). **"therefore he was turned to be their enemy, and he fought against them" → "and he was turned to be their enemy, he himself fought against them"** — וַיֵּהָפֵךְ לָהֶם לְאוֹיֵב, nifal of הָפַךְ "turn"; הוּא נִלְחַם־בָּם, no "and", הוּא "he himself".
- **"Then he remembered the days of old, Moses, and his people, saying" → "And he remembered the days of old, Moses, his people:"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיִּזְכֹּר יְמֵי־עוֹלָם מֹשֶׁה עַמּוֹ: no "and" between Moses and "his people", no "saying"; who remembers is not named (Yahweh, or "his people" as subject: "his people remembered the days of old, of Moses"); the questions that follow are what is remembered. **"with the shepherd of his flock" → "with the shepherds of his flock"** — רֹעֵי, plural in the printed text (Moses and Aaron; some manuscripts have the singular, from memory). **"within him" → "in its midst"** — בְּקִרְבּוֹ, a masculine singular suffix: the flock or the people.
- **"That led them by the right hand of Moses with his glorious arm" → "who made the arm of his beauty go at the right hand of Moses"** (v12) — Hebrew מוֹלִיךְ לִימִין מֹשֶׁה זְרוֹעַ תִּפְאַרְתּוֹ: the arm is the object of מוֹלִיךְ "who makes go"; תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (as 60:7). **"dividing the water" → "splitting the waters"** — בּוֹקֵעַ מַיִם (בָּקַע "split", as Exod 14:16). **"before them"** — מִפְּנֵיהֶם.
- **"That led them through the deep, as an horse in the wilderness, that they should not stumble?" → "who made them walk through the deeps? Like the horse in the wilderness, they did not stumble."** (v13) — מוֹלִיכָם (the verb of v12); תְּהוֹמוֹת "deeps", plural; כַּסּוּס "like the horse"; לֹא יִכָּשֵׁלוּ, an imperfect without "that": the accents end the question at "deeps".
- **"As a beast goeth down into the valley, the Spirit of the LORD caused him to rest" → "Like the beast that goes down into the plain, the spirit of Yahweh gave him rest"** (v14) — כַּבְּהֵמָה בַּבִּקְעָה תֵרֵד; בְּהֵמָה "beast", בִּקְעָה "plain" (a broad valley floor); תְּנִיחֶנּוּ "gave him / it rest" (singular, the people; the Septuagint has "guided them", as if from נָחָה, from memory). **"so didst thou lead thy people, to make thyself a glorious name" → "So you led your people, to make for yourself a name of beauty"** — נִהַגְתָּ "you led"; שֵׁם תִּפְאָרֶת "a name of beauty".
- **"Look down from heaven, and behold" → "Look from the heavens and see"** (v15) — הַבֵּט "look", "down" not in the word; שָׁמַיִם "heavens"; רְאֵה "see". **"from the habitation of thy holiness and of thy glory" → "from the lofty dwelling of your holiness and your beauty"** — מִזְּבֻל, זְבֻל "lofty dwelling" (as 1 Kgs 8:13); תִּפְאַרְתֶּךָ "your beauty". **"where is thy zeal and thy strength" → "Where are your zeal and your mighty deeds?"** — קִנְאָתְךָ (קִנְאָה "jealousy, zeal": here zeal for his people) וּגְבוּרֹתֶךָ, plural "mighty deeds". **"the sounding of thy bowels and of thy mercies toward me? are they restrained?" → "The stirring of your inward parts and your compassion toward me have held themselves back."** — הֲמוֹן מֵעֶיךָ, הָמוֹן "multitude, din": of the inward parts, their stirring (as Jer 31:20 הָמוּ מֵעַי); מֵעִים "inward parts"; הִתְאַפָּקוּ "restrained themselves" (as Gen 43:31), a statement (no question word).
- **"Doubtless thou art our father, though Abraham be ignorant of us" → "For you are our Father, for Abraham does not know us"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי־אַתָּה אָבִינוּ כִּי אַבְרָהָם לֹא יְדָעָנוּ: כִּי "for" twice (the second may be "though"); "Father" with a capital (God addressed). **"acknowledge us not" → "does not recognize us"** — הִכִּיר. **"thou, O LORD, art our father, our redeemer; thy name is from everlasting" → "you, Yahweh, are our Father, our redeemer from of old is your name"** — no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it); the accents join גֹּאֲלֵנוּ מֵעוֹלָם "our redeemer from of old", and שְׁמֶךָ "your name" ends the verse.
- **"O LORD, why hast thou made us to err from thy ways" → "Why, Yahweh, do you make us wander from your ways"** (v17) — Hebrew לָמָּה תַתְעֵנוּ יְהוָה, the name after the verb; no "O"; הִתְעָה "cause to wander" (as 3:12); imperfects. **"and hardened our heart from thy fear" → "do you harden our heart from fearing you"** — תַּקְשִׁיחַ (only here and Job 39:16: "make hard, harsh"), without "and"; מִיִּרְאָתֶךָ "from your fear" (the fear of you). **"for thy servants' sake" → "on account of your servants"** — לְמַעַן (as 62:1); עֲבָדֶיךָ, the word is "slaves" (of Yahweh "servants"). **"heritage" → "inheritance"** — נַחֲלָה.
- **"The people of thy holiness have possessed it but a little while" → "For a little while the people of your holiness possessed"** (v18) — Hebrew לַמִּצְעָר יָרְשׁוּ עַם־קָדְשֶׁךָ: "for a little while" first; יָרַשׁ "possess" without an object ("it", "the land" not in the Hebrew); the line is hard. **"have trodden down" → "have trampled"** — בּוֹסְסוּ, polel of בּוּס (as v6).
- **"We are thine: thou never barest rule over them; they were not called by thy name" → "We have become those whom from of old you have not ruled, over whom your name has not been called"** (v19) — Hebrew הָיִינוּ מֵעוֹלָם לֹא־מָשַׁלְתָּ בָּם לֹא־נִקְרָא שִׁמְךָ עֲלֵיהֶם: הָיִינוּ "we have become" ("we are thine" is not in the Hebrew); "those whom" is supplied (no "like"); מֵעוֹלָם "from of old" may also go with "we have become"; נִקְרָא שֵׁם עַל "the name is called over" = they belong to him (as Deut 28:10).
- **Verse division** (v19) — the Hebrew 63:19 goes on with לוּא־קָרַעְתָּ שָׁמַיִם יָרַדְתָּ … "Oh that you would tear the heavens, would come down …", which is our 64:1 (the Hebrew numbers 64:1–12 are our 64:2–12); it is translated there.

### Notes
- **v1–6** — Rev 19:13, 15: the garment dipped in blood, the winepress of the fury of the wrath of God.
- **v10** — Eph 4:30 "do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God".
- **v16** — God addressed as "our Father", also 64:8.
