# Isaiah 57

[1] The righteous one has perished,
and no man lays it to heart;
and men of ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ are gathered away,
with none understanding,
for from the face of the evil the righteous one is gathered away.

[2] He enters into ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧;
they rest on their beds,
he who walks in his uprightness.

[3] And you, draw near here,
sons of a soothsaying woman,
seed of an adulterer and of one who played the prostitute.

[4] Over whom do you delight yourselves?
Against whom do you make wide the mouth,
make long the tongue?
Are you not children of transgression,
seed of falsehood,

[5] who heat yourselves among the terebinths,
under every luxuriant tree,
slaughtering the children in the wadis,
under the clefts of the crags?

[6] Among the smooth ones of the wadi is your share;
they, they are your lot;
to them also you have poured out a drink offering,
you have brought up a grain offering.
Shall I be comforted over these?

[7] On a mountain high and lifted up
you have set your bed;
there also you went up to sacrifice a sacrifice.

[8] And behind the door and the doorpost
you have set your memorial;
for away from me you have uncovered,
and you have gone up,
you have made your bed wide;
and you have cut for yourself from them,
you have loved their bed,
you have beheld a hand.

[9] And you journeyed to the king with oil,
and multiplied your perfumes;
and you sent your envoys far off,
and abased yourself even to Sheol.

[10] In the abundance of your way you grew weary;
you did not say, "Hopeless!"
You found the life of your hand;
therefore you did not grow weak.

[11] And whom have you been anxious about and feared,
that you lie,
and me you have not remembered,
you have not set it on your heart?
Have I not been silent, and that from of old,
and me you do not fear?

[12] I will declare your righteousness,
and your works, and they shall not profit you.

[13] When you cry out, let your gatherings deliver you!
And all of them a wind shall lift up,
a vapor shall take away.
But he who takes refuge in me shall inherit the land
and shall possess my holy mountain.

[14] And he will say,
"Build up, build up, prepare a way;
lift up the stumbling block from the way of my people."

[15] For thus says One high and lifted up,
abiding forever, and Holy is his name:

"In the height, and holy, I abide,
and with the crushed and lowly of spirit,
to make alive the spirit of the lowly,
and to make alive the heart of the crushed.

[16] For not forever will I contend,
and not forever will I be wrathful;
for the spirit would grow faint before me,
and the breaths that I have made.

[17] In the iniquity of his unjust gain I was wrathful,
and I struck him; I hid, and I was wrathful;
and he went on backsliding in the way of his heart.

[18] His ways I have seen, and I will heal him;
and I will lead him, and repay comforts to him and to his mourners,

[19] creating the fruit of the lips.
⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧, ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to the far and to the near,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "and I will heal him.

[20] But the wicked are like the driven sea,
for it cannot be quiet,
and its waters drive up mire and mud.

[21] There is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧," says my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "for the wicked."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v21) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v19) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"peace" → "shalom"** (v2, 19, 21) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם, token `shalom` in every place.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v12 now breaks at the athnach (after "your righteousness").
- **"The righteous perisheth" → "The righteous one has perished"** (v1) — Hebrew הַצַּדִּיק אָבָד, perfect. **"no man layeth it to heart"** — אֵין אִישׁ שָׂם עַל־לֵב, literally "sets upon heart"; "it" is supplied. **"merciful men are taken away" → "men of chesed are gathered away"** — אַנְשֵׁי־חֶסֶד (token, as everywhere); נֶאֱסָפִים, nifal of אָסַף "gather" (of the dead, "gathered to his people"), twice in the verse. **"none considering that the righteous is taken away from the evil to come" → "with none understanding, for from the face of the evil the righteous one is gathered away"** — בְּאֵין מֵבִין; כִּי "for" (or "that"); מִפְּנֵי הָרָעָה "from the face of the evil" (also "because of the evil": the Hebrew allows both); "to come" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"He shall enter into peace" → "He enters into shalom"** (v2) — Hebrew יָבוֹא שָׁלוֹם has no preposition; it may also be read "shalom comes". **"they shall rest in their beds, each one walking in his uprightness" → "they rest on their beds, he who walks in his uprightness"** — יָנוּחוּ עַל־מִשְׁכְּבוֹתָם, plural; הֹלֵךְ נְכֹחוֹ singular, literally "walking his straightness" (root of נְכֹחָה "uprightness", 59:14); the change of number is the Hebrew's.
- **"But draw near hither, ye sons of the sorceress" → "And you, draw near here, sons of a soothsaying woman"** (v3) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם קִרְבוּ־הֵנָּה; עֹנְנָה, feminine participle of עוֹנֵן "practice soothsaying" (its exact sense is not known); no article. **"the seed of the adulterer and the whore" → "seed of an adulterer and of one who played the prostitute"** — זֶרַע מְנָאֵף וַתִּזְנֶה: the last word is a verb, literally "and she played the prostitute" (or "and you played the prostitute"); "of one who" is supplied for English. זָנָה "play the prostitute".
- **"Against whom do ye sport yourselves?" → "Over whom do you delight yourselves?"** (v4) — Hebrew עַל־מִי תִּתְעַנָּגוּ, the verb of 58:14 ("delight yourself in Yahweh"): here of taking pleasure over someone, making sport. **"make ye a wide mouth, and draw out the tongue" → "make wide the mouth, make long the tongue"** — תַּרְחִיבוּ פֶה תַּאֲרִיכוּ לָשׁוֹן. **"a seed of falsehood"** — זֶרַע שָׁקֶר; יַלְדֵי "children" (יֶלֶד).
- **"Enflaming yourselves with idols under every green tree" → "who heat yourselves among the terebinths, under every luxuriant tree"** (v5) — Hebrew הַנֵּחָמִים בָּאֵלִים: nifal of חָמַם "be hot" ("with lust" was supplied); אֵלִים may be "terebinths" (as 1:29) or "gods" (plural of אֵל): the Hebrew allows both. רַעֲנָן "luxuriant". **"slaying the children in the valleys under the clifts of the rocks" → "slaughtering the children in the wadis, under the clefts of the crags"** — שָׁחַט "slaughter"; נַחַל "wadi"; סְעִפֵי הַסְּלָעִים.
- **"Among the smooth stones of the stream is thy portion" → "Among the smooth ones of the wadi is your share"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּחַלְּקֵי־נַחַל חֶלְקֵךְ, a play on one root: חַלְּקֵי "smooth ones" (no "stones" in the Hebrew), חֵלֶק "share". From here to v13 the one addressed is feminine singular. **"thou hast offered a meat offering" → "you have brought up a grain offering"** — הֶעֱלִית מִנְחָה; שָׁפַכְתְּ נֶסֶךְ "poured out a drink offering"; גַּם "also". **"Should I receive comfort in these?" → "Shall I be comforted over these?"** — הַעַל אֵלֶּה אֶנָּחֵם, nifal of נָחַם with עַל: "be comforted over" (= be appeased by), or "relent concerning"; the Hebrew allows both.
- **"Upon a lofty and high mountain" → "On a mountain high and lifted up"** (v7) — Hebrew עַל הַר־גָּבֹהַּ וְנִשָּׂא; וְנִשָּׂא "lifted up", the word of v15. **"to offer sacrifice" → "to sacrifice a sacrifice"** — לִזְבֹּחַ זָבַח.
- **"Behind the doors also and the posts hast thou set up thy remembrance" → "And behind the door and the doorpost you have set your memorial"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאַחַר הַדֶּלֶת וְהַמְּזוּזָה, both singular; זִכָּרוֹן "memorial". **"for thou hast discovered thyself to another than me" → "for away from me you have uncovered"** — כִּי מֵאִתִּי גִּלִּית: גִּלָּה "uncover" has no object written ("yourself" or "your bed" is supplied by others). **"thou hast enlarged thy bed, and made thee a covenant with them" → "you have made your bed wide; and you have cut for yourself from them"** — הִרְחַבְתְּ מִשְׁכָּבֵךְ; וַתִּכְרָת־לָךְ מֵהֶם, כָּרַת "cut" without the word בְּרִית "covenant" (of a bargain cut with them). **"thou lovedst their bed where thou sawest it" → "you have beheld a hand"** — יָד חָזִית: יָד as 56:5 ("monument"), or a sexual euphemism; the meaning is uncertain. חָזָה "behold".
- **"thou wentest to the king with ointment" → "you journeyed to the king with oil"** (v9) — Hebrew וַתָּשֻׁרִי, of uncertain sense ("journey", or "gaze on"); לַמֶּלֶךְ "to the king" as pointed (read by some as "to Molech"); שֶׁמֶן "oil". רִקֻּחַיִךְ "your perfumes" (only here). **"messengers" → "envoys"** — צִירַיִךְ, צִיר "envoy" (not מַלְאָךְ). **"didst debase thyself even unto hell" → "abased yourself even to Sheol"** — וַתַּשְׁפִּילִי עַד־שְׁאוֹל, hifil of שָׁפֵל "make low" with no object written ("yourself" supplied); שְׁאוֹל "Sheol".
- **"Thou art wearied in the greatness of thy way" → "In the abundance of your way you grew weary"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּרֹב דַּרְכֵּךְ יָגַעַתְּ; no "but". **"There is no hope" → "Hopeless!"** — נוֹאָשׁ, one word. **"thou hast found the life of thine hand; therefore thou wast not grieved" → "You found the life of your hand; therefore you did not grow weak"** — חַיַּת יָדֵךְ מָצָאת, literally (= renewed strength; "new life for your strength" was a paraphrase); חָלָה "become weak".
- **"And of whom hast thou been afraid or feared" → "And whom have you been anxious about and feared"** (v11) — Hebrew דָּאַגְתְּ וַתִּירְאִי: דָּאַג "be anxious", joined by וְ. **"that thou hast lied" → "that you lie"** — כִּי תְכַזֵּבִי, imperfect. **"and hast not remembered me, nor laid it to thy heart" → "and me you have not remembered, you have not set it on your heart"** — וְאוֹתִי לֹא זָכַרְתְּ, the object first; שַׂמְתְּ עַל־לִבֵּךְ as v1. **"have not I held my peace even of old" → "Have I not been silent, and that from of old"** — הֲלֹא אֲנִי מַחְשֶׁה וּמֵעֹלָם; מֵעוֹלָם "from of old" (not "for a long time"). **"and thou fearest me not"** — וְאוֹתִי לֹא תִירָאִי, the object first again.
- **"I will declare thy righteousness, and thy works; for they shall not profit thee" → "I will declare your righteousness, and your works, and they shall not profit you"** (v12) — Hebrew וְלֹא יוֹעִילוּךְ "and they shall not": וְ, not "for" or "but". The athnach stands after צִדְקָתֵךְ.
- **"let thy companies deliver thee" → "let your gatherings deliver you"** (v13) — Hebrew קִבּוּצַיִךְ, from קָבַץ "gather" (as 56:8): what or whom she has gathered (idols or allies); "of idols" was supplied. **"but the wind shall carry them all away; vanity shall take them" → "And all of them a wind shall lift up, a vapor shall take away"** — יִשָּׂא־רוּחַ, נָשָׂא "lift up"; הֶבֶל "vapor" (literally "breath"). **"he that putteth his trust in me shall possess the land, and shall inherit my holy mountain" → "he who takes refuge in me shall inherit the land and shall possess my holy mountain"** — הַחוֹסֶה בִי, חָסָה "take refuge"; יִנְחַל "inherit", וְיִירַשׁ "possess": the two verbs were exchanged.
- **"And shall say" → "And he will say"** (v14) — Hebrew וְאָמַר, the subject not named (not the passive "it shall be said"). **"Cast ye up, cast ye up, prepare the way, take up the stumblingblock" → "Build up, build up, prepare a way; lift up the stumbling block"** — סֹלּוּ סֹלּוּ, סָלַל "build up" (a road); פַּנּוּ־דָרֶךְ as 40:3, דֶּרֶךְ without the article; הָרִימוּ מִכְשׁוֹל, "every" was supplied.
- **"the high and lofty One that inhabiteth eternity, whose name is Holy" → "One high and lifted up, abiding forever, and Holy is his name"** (v15) — Hebrew רָם וְנִשָּׂא שֹׁכֵן עַד וְקָדוֹשׁ שְׁמוֹ, word for word: רָם וְנִשָּׂא without the article ("lifted up" as v7; 6:1); שֹׁכֵן "abiding" (שָׁכַן "abide"), עַד "perpetuity"; וְקָדוֹשׁ שְׁמוֹ "and holy is his name". **"I dwell in the high and holy place" → "In the height, and holy, I abide"** — מָרוֹם וְקָדוֹשׁ אֶשְׁכּוֹן: "place" is not in the Hebrew; קָדוֹשׁ may describe the height or the one who abides. **"with him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit" → "and with the crushed and lowly of spirit"** — וְאֶת־דַּכָּא וּשְׁפַל־רוּחַ: דַּכָּא "crushed" (singular), שְׁפַל "low". **"to revive the spirit of the humble, and to revive the heart of the contrite ones" → "to make alive the spirit of the lowly, and to make alive the heart of the crushed"** — לְהַחֲיוֹת, hifil of חָיָה "live"; שְׁפָלִים "lowly", נִדְכָּאִים "crushed" (the roots of the line before).
- **"neither will I be always wroth" → "and not forever will I be wrathful"** (v16) — Hebrew לָנֶצַח beside לְעוֹלָם (English has one word "forever"); קָצַף "be wrathful". **"for the spirit should fail before me" → "for the spirit would grow faint before me"** — כִּי־רוּחַ מִלְּפָנַי יַעֲטוֹף, עָטַף "be faint". **"and the souls which I have made"** — וּנְשָׁמוֹת אֲנִי עָשִׂיתִי, נְשָׁמוֹת plural of נְשָׁמָה "breath", plain as Gen 2:7 (the old token form carried a gloss).
- **"For the iniquity of his covetousness was I wroth" → "In the iniquity of his unjust gain I was wrathful"** (v17) — Hebrew בַּעֲוֹן בִּצְעוֹ; בֶּצַע "unjust gain"; קָצַף "be wrathful" (not אַף "anger"). **"I hid me, and was wroth" → "I hid, and I was wrathful"** — הַסְתֵּר, infinitive absolute of הִסְתִּיר "hide", with no object written ("my face" was supplied). **"he went on frowardly" → "he went on backsliding"** — וַיֵּלֶךְ שׁוֹבָב, from שׁוּב "turn back"; "own" was supplied.
- **"and restore comforts unto him" → "and repay comforts to him"** (v18) — Hebrew וַאֲשַׁלֵּם, שִׁלֵּם "repay"; נִחֻמִים "comforts". The verse begins דְּרָכָיו רָאִיתִי "His ways I have seen", object first; וְ "and", not "but".
- **"I create the fruit of the lips" → "creating the fruit of the lips"** (v19) — Hebrew בּוֹרֵא, participle of בָּרָא "create"; the qere נִיב "fruit" is translated (the written נוב is another form of the same word). **"to him that is far off, and to him that is near" → "to the far and to the near"** — לָרָחוֹק וְלַקָּרוֹב.
- **"like the troubled sea, when it cannot rest" → "like the driven sea, for it cannot be quiet"** (v20) — Hebrew כַּיָּם נִגְרָשׁ, nifal of גָּרַשׁ "drive", the verb of the last line וַיִּגְרְשׁוּ "drive up"; כִּי "for"; הַשְׁקֵט "be quiet". **"mire and dirt" → "mire and mud"** — רֶפֶשׁ וָטִיט.
