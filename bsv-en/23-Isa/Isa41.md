# Isaiah 41

[1] Be silent to me, coastlands,
and let folks renew power;
let them draw near, then let them speak;
together to judgment let us come near.

[2] Who has stirred up from the east
him whom righteousness calls to his foot?
He gives nations before him,
and over kings he makes him have dominion;
he makes them like dust to his sword,
like driven stubble to his bow.

[3] He pursues them, he passes over in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧;
on a path with his feet he does not come.

[4] Who has wrought and done it,
calling the generations from the beginning?
I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, am first,
and with the last ones I am he.

[5] Coastlands have seen, and they fear;
the ends of the earth tremble;
they have come near, and they came.

[6] Each man helps his neighbor,
and to his brother he says, "Be strong!"

[7] And the craftsman makes the refiner strong,
he who smooths with the hammer him who strikes the anvil,
saying of the soldering, "It is good";
and he makes it strong with nails;
it shall not be shaken.

[8] And you, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, my servant,
⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, whom I have chosen,
the seed of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, who loves me;

[9] you whom I took hold of from the ends of the earth,
and from its corners I called you,
and said to you, "You are my servant,
I have chosen you and have not rejected you";

[10] do not fear, for I am with you;
do not be dismayed, for I am your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
I have strengthened you, yes, I have helped you,
yes, I have upheld you with the right hand of my righteousness.

[11] Behold, they shall be ashamed and put to shame,
all who are incensed against you;
they shall be as nothing and shall perish,
the men of your contention.

[12] You shall seek them and not find them,
the men of your strife;
they shall be as nothing and as naught,
the men of your war.

[13] For I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
am holding your right hand,
who says to you, "Do not fear,
I have helped you."

[14] Do not fear, worm ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
I have helped you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and your Redeemer is the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] Behold, I have set you as a threshing sledge,
sharp, new, having edges;
you shall thresh mountains and crush them,
and hills you shall make like chaff;

[16] you shall winnow them, and wind shall carry them away,
and the storm shall scatter them.
And you shall be glad in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
in the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall glory.

[17] The afflicted and the needy are seeking water,
and there is none;
their tongue is parched with thirst;
I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, will answer them;
the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, I will not forsake them.

[18] I will open rivers on the bare heights,
and springs in the midst of the plains;
I will make the wilderness into a pool of water,
and a dry land into outlets of water.

[19] I will give in the wilderness the cedar,
the acacia, and the myrtle, and the oil tree;
I will set in the steppe the cypress,
the elm and the box tree together,

[20] so that they may see and know,
and may take note and have insight together,
that the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done this,
and the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has created it.

[21] Bring near your case, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
bring forward your strong reasons, says the King of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[22] Let them bring them forward and tell us
what shall happen;
the former things, what they are, tell,
and let us set our heart on them,
and let us know their latter end;
or let us hear the things to come.

[23] Tell the things to come afterward,
and let us know that you are gods;
yes, do good or do evil,
and let us be dismayed and see together.

[24] Behold, you are less than nothing,
and your work less than naught;
an abomination is he who chooses you.

[25] I have stirred up one from the north, and he has come,
from the rising of the sun he shall call on my name;
and he shall come upon prefects as upon mortar,
and as a potter treads clay.

[26] Who has told it from the beginning, that we may know,
and from before, that we may say, "Righteous!"?
Yes, there is none who tells, yes, none who makes it heard,
yes, none who hears your sayings.

[27] First to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧: "Behold, behold them!"
and to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ I will give a bearer of news.

[28] And I look, and there is no man;
and of these, there is no counselor,
that I may ask them and they bring back word.

[29] Behold, all of them are wickedness;
naught are their works;
wind and emptiness are their molten images.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 13, 17) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v23 אֱלֹהִים of the idols is plain "gods".
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v11 and v12 are put back in the Hebrew order of their lines.
- **"Keep silence before me, O islands" → "Be silent to me, coastlands"** (v1) — Hebrew הַחֲרִישׁוּ אֵלַי אִיִּים; אִי "coastland" (land reached by sea); no "O". **"let the people renew their strength" → "and let folks renew power"** — וּלְאֻמִּים, לְאֹם "folk"; כֹּחַ "power" (the words of 40:31); "their" is not written. **"let us come near together to judgment"** — יַחְדָּו לַמִּשְׁפָּט נִקְרָבָה, Hebrew order; נָגַשׁ "draw near", קָרַב "come near": two verbs.
- **"Who raised up the righteous man from the east, called him to his foot" → "Who has stirred up from the east him whom righteousness calls to his foot?"** (v2) — Hebrew מִי הֵעִיר מִמִּזְרָח צֶדֶק יִקְרָאֵהוּ לְרַגְלוֹ: הֵעִיר "stir up" (as v25); צֶדֶק is "righteousness" (not "the righteous man", and not "victory"); "him whom" is supplied (the Hebrew has no object for "stirred up": the second clause names him). Also read "whom he calls in righteousness to his foot". The one stirred up is commonly taken as Cyrus (44:28; 45:1); the verse does not name him.
- **"made him rule over kings" → "and over kings he makes him have dominion"** (v2) — Hebrew וּמְלָכִים יַרְדְּ, taken as hifil of רָדָה "have dominion"; also read "bring down" (from יָרַד); the form is uncertain. **"he gave them as the dust to his sword, and as driven stubble to his bow" → "he makes them like dust to his sword, like driven stubble to his bow"** — יִתֵּן כֶּעָפָר חַרְבּוֹ כְּקַשׁ נִדָּף קַשְׁתּוֹ, literally "he gives like dust his sword, like driven stubble his bow"; "them" and "to" are supplied (the KJV's reading); others read "his sword makes them like dust".
- **"passed safely" → "he passes over in shalom"** (v3) — Hebrew יַעֲבוֹר שָׁלוֹם, the noun *shalom* (token as everywhere; "safely" is its sense here). **"by the way that he had not gone with his feet" → "on a path with his feet he does not come"** — אֹרַח בְּרַגְלָיו לֹא יָבוֹא, Hebrew order: either he is so swift that his feet do not touch the path, or he goes on a path he has never trodden; the Hebrew allows both.
- **"Who hath wrought and done it"** (v4) — Hebrew מִי־פָעַל וְעָשָׂה; "it" (not "this") as the KJV, the object not written. **"I the LORD, the first, and with the last; I am he" → "I, Yahweh, am first, and with the last ones I am he"** — אֲנִי יְהוָה רִאשׁוֹן וְאֶת־אַחֲרֹנִים אֲנִי־הוּא: "am" supplied; אַחֲרֹנִים plural, "the last ones"; אֲנִי־הוּא "I am he" (as 43:10, 13).
- **"The isles saw it, and feared" → "Coastlands have seen, and they fear"** (v5) — Hebrew רָאוּ אִיִּים וְיִירָאוּ, no object, no article. **"drew near, and came" → "they have come near, and they came"** — קָרְבוּ וַיֶּאֱתָיוּן, a perfect and a narrative form.
- **"They helped every one his neighbour; and every one said to his brother" → "Each man helps his neighbor, and to his brother he says"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶת־רֵעֵהוּ יַעְזֹרוּ וּלְאָחִיו יֹאמַר, "each man … his neighbor"; Hebrew order. **"Be of good courage" → "Be strong!"** — חֲזָק, the verb of v7.
- **"the carpenter encouraged the goldsmith" → "the craftsman makes the refiner strong"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיְחַזֵּק חָרָשׁ אֶת־צֹרֵף, וְ "and"; חִזֵּק "make strong" both times in the verse ("he makes it strong with nails"); צֹרֵף "refiner" (as 40:19). **"him that smote the anvil"** — הוֹלֶם פָּעַם; פַּעַם is elsewhere "foot, step, time"; here the anvil (the only place). **"that it should not be moved" → "it shall not be shaken"** — לֹא יִמּוֹט, מוֹט nifal (as 40:20).
- **"But thou, Israel" → "And you, Israel"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה. **"my servant"** — עַבְדִּי, of Israel as Yahweh's: "servant". **"the seed of Abraham my friend" → "the seed of Abraham, who loves me"** — זֶרַע אַבְרָהָם אֹהֲבִי: זֶרַע "seed"; אֹהֲבִי is the participle of אָהַב "love" with "me": "the one who loves me" ("my friend" in KJV, as 2 Chr 20:7; Jas 2:23 Greek φίλος).
- **"whom I have taken" → "whom I took hold of"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶחֱזַקְתִּיךָ, hifil of חָזַק "take hold of" (as v13 "holding"). **"called thee from the chief men thereof" → "from its corners I called you"** — וּמֵאֲצִילֶיהָ, "its sides, corners" (Exod 24:11 has the same word of "nobles": uncertain which here; the parallel "ends of the earth" points to "corners"); Hebrew order. **"and said unto thee"** — וָאֹמַר, narrative "and said". **"not cast thee away" → "have not rejected you"** — מָאַס "reject".
- **"be not dismayed" → "do not be dismayed"** (v10) — Hebrew אַל־תִּשְׁתָּע, שָׁתַע found only here and v23: "look about (in fear)". **"I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee" → "I have strengthened you, yes, I have helped you, yes, I have upheld you"** — אִמַּצְתִּיךָ אַף־עֲזַרְתִּיךָ אַף־תְּמַכְתִּיךָ are perfects (the prophetic perfect: done in Yahweh's word; English future is the other reading); אַף "yes". **"with the right hand of my righteousness"** — בִּימִין צִדְקִי, the genitive kept (KJV has it so).
- **"all they that were incensed against thee shall be ashamed and confounded" → "they shall be ashamed and put to shame, all who are incensed against you"** (v11) — Hebrew הֵן יֵבֹשׁוּ וְיִכָּלְמוּ כֹּל הַנֶּחֱרִים בָּךְ, Hebrew order; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed" beside נִכְלַם "be put to shame" (the verb of כְּלִמָּה "dishonor"), as the Psalms. **"they that strive with thee" → "the men of your contention"** — אַנְשֵׁי רִיבֶךָ (literal; the men who contend with you); likewise v12 "the men of your strife" (מַצּוּת) and "the men of your war".
- **"Thou shalt seek them, and shalt not find them, even them that contended with thee" → "You shall seek them and not find them, the men of your strife"** (v12) — Hebrew order. **"as nothing, and as a thing of nought" → "as nothing and as naught"** — כְאַיִן וּכְאֶפֶס, two words: אַיִן "nothing", אֶפֶס "naught" (as 40:17; 41:29).
- **"will hold thy right hand, saying unto thee" → "am holding your right hand, who says to you"** (v13) — Hebrew מַחֲזִיק … הָאֹמֵר, participles. **"I will help thee" → "I have helped you"** — עֲזַרְתִּיךָ, perfect (also v14).
- **"Fear not, thou worm Jacob, and ye men of Israel" → "Do not fear, worm Jacob, men of Israel"** (v14) — Hebrew אַל־תִּירְאִי תּוֹלַעַת יַעֲקֹב מְתֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל: תּוֹלַעַת "worm" (the verb and the noun are feminine); מְתֵי "men of" (as Deut 2:34; 4:27 "few men"); "maggot" (reading רִמַּת) is a conjecture and is not followed. No "O", no "you". **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** — נְאֻם־יְהוָה. **"thy redeemer, the Holy One of Israel" → "and your Redeemer is the Holy One of Israel"** — וְגֹאֲלֵךְ קְדוֹשׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל; גֹּאֵל "redeemer"; "is" supplied.
- **"I will make thee a new sharp threshing instrument having teeth" → "I have set you as a threshing sledge, sharp, new, having edges"** (v15) — Hebrew שַׂמְתִּיךְ לְמוֹרַג חָרוּץ חָדָשׁ בַּעַל פִּיפִיּוֹת: perfect "I have set" (שִׂים לְ); Hebrew order "sharp, new"; בַּעַל פִּיפִיּוֹת "having edges" (lit. "owner of mouths": double-edged blades set in the sledge). **"thou shalt thresh the mountains" → "you shall thresh mountains"** — no article; **"and shalt make the hills as chaff" → "and hills you shall make like chaff"** — Hebrew order.
- **"Thou shalt fan them" → "you shall winnow them"** (v16) — Hebrew תִּזְרֵם, זָרָה of grain. **"the wind" → "wind"** — רוּחַ without the article. **"the whirlwind" → "the storm"** — סְעָרָה (as 40:24); הֵפִיץ "scatter". **"thou shalt rejoice" → "you shall be glad"** — תָּגִיל, גִּיל "be glad" (beside שָׂמַח "rejoice"). **"shalt glory"** — תִּתְהַלָּל, hitpael of הִלֵּל "praise": "glory, boast".
- **"When the poor and needy seek water" → "The afflicted and the needy are seeking water"** (v17) — Hebrew הָעֲנִיִּים וְהָאֶבְיוֹנִים מְבַקְשִׁים: עָנִי "afflicted", אֶבְיוֹן "needy", a participle; there is no "when". **"faileth for thirst" → "is parched with thirst"** — נָשָׁתָּה. **"I the God of Israel" → "the Elohim of Israel"** — אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל לֹא אֶעֶזְבֵם, "I" is in the verb.
- **"in high places" → "on the bare heights"** (v18) — Hebrew שְׁפָיִים. **"fountains in the midst of the valleys" → "springs in the midst of the plains"** — מַעְיָנוֹת "springs"; בְּקָעוֹת "plains" (בִּקְעָה). **"a pool of water"** — אֲגַם. **"the dry land springs of water" → "a dry land into outlets of water"** — אֶרֶץ צִיָּה (no article) לְמוֹצָאֵי מָיִם, "goings out of water" (as Ps 107:35).
- **"I will plant in the wilderness" → "I will give in the wilderness"** (v19) — Hebrew אֶתֵּן, "give". **"the shittah tree" → "the acacia"** — שִׁטָּה; **"the oil tree"** — עֵץ שֶׁמֶן, literally "tree of oil" (not the olive זַיִת: Neh 8:15 names both; the kind is uncertain). **"in the desert" → "in the steppe"** — בָּעֲרָבָה (as 40:3). **"the fir tree, and the pine, and the box tree" → "the cypress, the elm and the box tree"** — בְּרוֹשׁ תִּדְהָר וּתְאַשּׁוּר: all three uncertain (בְּרוֹשׁ cypress or juniper; תִּדְהָר elm, plane or ash; תְּאַשּׁוּר box tree or cypress). "the" supplied with the tree names.
- **"That they may see" → "so that they may see"** (v20) — Hebrew לְמַעַן. **"and consider, and understand together" → "and may take note and have insight together"** — וְיָשִׂימוּ, literally "and set" (the heart: "set our heart" is written out in v22); הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight". **"and the Holy One of Israel hath created it"** — וּ "and"; בָּרָא "create".
- **"Produce your cause" → "Bring near your case"** (v21) — Hebrew קָרְבוּ רִיבְכֶם; רִיב in law "case". **"bring forth your strong reasons" → "bring forward your strong reasons"** — הַגִּישׁוּ עֲצֻמוֹתֵיכֶם, literally "your strong (things)"; "reasons" supplied as the KJV.
- **"Let them bring them forth, and shew us what shall happen" → "Let them bring them forward and tell us what shall happen"** (v22) — Hebrew יַגִּישׁוּ וְיַגִּידוּ לָנוּ אֵת אֲשֶׁר תִּקְרֶינָה; "them" supplied (as the KJV); הִגִּיד "tell" in v22, 23, 26 alike. **"that we may consider them" → "and let us set our heart on them"** — וְנָשִׂימָה לִבֵּנוּ, cohortative, literally "and let us set our heart"; "on them" supplied. **"the latter end of them" → "their latter end"** — אַחֲרִיתָן. **"or declare us things for to come" → "or let us hear the things to come"** — הַשְׁמִיעֻנוּ, "make us hear".
- **"that we may know that ye are gods" → "and let us know that you are gods"** (v23) — Hebrew וְנֵדְעָה, cohortative; אֱלֹהִים plain "gods". **"yea, do good, or do evil" → "yes, do good or do evil"** — אַף; הֵיטִיב · הֵרַע. **"that we may be dismayed, and behold it together" → "and let us be dismayed and see together"** — וְנִשְׁתָּעָה וְנִרְאֶה: the qere וְנִרְאֶה "and see" (the verse); the ketiv וְנִרְא may be read "and fear" (וְנִירָא).
- **"ye are of nothing, and your work of nought" → "you are less than nothing, and your work less than naught"** (v24) — Hebrew מֵאַיִן … מֵאָפַע; מִן as 40:17 ("less than"; also read "of"). אָפַע is found only here: taken as a form of אֶפֶס "naught" (uncertain; others read "of a viper", אֶפְעֶה, from memory). **"an abomination is he that chooseth you"** — תּוֹעֵבָה יִבְחַר בָּכֶם, literally "an abomination — he chooses you"; "he who" supplied.
- **"I have raised up one from the north" → "I have stirred up one from the north"** (v25) — Hebrew הַעִירוֹתִי, הֵעִיר as v2; "one" supplied. **"shall he call upon my name"** — יִקְרָא בִשְׁמִי, "call on my name". **"and he shall come upon princes as upon morter" → "and he shall come upon prefects as upon mortar"** — וְיָבֹא סְגָנִים כְּמוֹ־חֹמֶר: the MT has יָבֹא "come" ("upon" supplied); "trample" (יָבֻס) is a conjecture and is not followed; סְגָנִים "prefects". **"as the potter treadeth clay" → "and as a potter treads clay"** — וּ "and"; יוֹצֵר, participle of יָצַר "form"; רָמַס "tread, trample".
- **"Who hath declared from the beginning" → "Who has told it from the beginning"** (v26) — Hebrew מִי־הִגִּיד מֵרֹאשׁ; "it" supplied. **"and beforetime, that we may say, He is righteous?" → "and from before, that we may say, Righteous!"** — וּמִלְּפָנִים וְנֹאמַר צַדִּיק: the one word "righteous" (= he is in the right). **"yea, there is none that sheweth … that declareth … that heareth your words" → "Yes, there is none who tells, yes, none who makes it heard, yes, none who hears your sayings"** — אַף three times; מַשְׁמִיעַ "makes heard"; אֵמֶר "saying".
- **"The first shall say to Zion, Behold, behold them" → "First to Zion: Behold, behold them!"** (v27) — Hebrew רִאשׁוֹן לְצִיּוֹן הִנֵּה הִנָּם has no verb; "shall say" / "I said" are supplied by others. **"one that bringeth good tidings" → "a bearer of news"** — מְבַשֵּׂר (as 40:9).
- **"For I beheld, and there was no man" → "And I look, and there is no man"** (v28) — Hebrew וְאֵרֶא וְאֵין אִישׁ, a short form after וְ (not the narrative form): present or wish ("let me look"). **"even among them" → "and of these"** — וּמֵאֵלֶּה וְאֵין יוֹעֵץ, literally "and of these, and there is no counselor". **"that, when I asked of them, could answer a word" → "that I may ask them and they bring back word"** — וְאֶשְׁאָלֵם וְיָשִׁיבוּ דָבָר; הֵשִׁיב דָּבָר "bring back word".
- **"they are all vanity" → "all of them are wickedness"** (v29) — Hebrew הֵן כֻּלָּם אָוֶן; אָוֶן "wickedness" (also "trouble, nothingness"); 1QIsa reads אַיִן "nothing" (from memory): the MT is kept. **"their works are nothing" → "naught are their works"** — אֶפֶס, Hebrew order. **"their molten images are wind and confusion" → "wind and emptiness are their molten images"** — רוּחַ וָתֹהוּ נִסְכֵּיהֶם; תֹּהוּ "emptiness"; נְסָכִים is the word for drink offerings, here what is poured as metal (from נָסַךְ "cast", 40:19).

### Notes
- **v2, 25** — the one from the east / north is commonly taken as Cyrus the Persian (named in 44:28; 45:1).
- **v8–9** — Israel, Jacob, "my servant": the first of the servant passages of chs 40–55 (42:1; 43:10; 44:1–2, 21; 49:3).
