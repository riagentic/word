# Isaiah 16

[1] Send the lamb of the ruler of the land
from Sela toward the wilderness,
to the mount of the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[2] And it shall be: like fleeing birds,
a nest sent away,
the daughters of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ will be
at the fords of the Arnon.

[3] Bring counsel,
make a decision;
set your shade like the night
in the midst of noon;
hide the ones driven out;
do not uncover the fugitive.

[4] Let my ones driven out sojourn among you;
⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, be a hiding place to them
from the face of the one who lays waste.
For the oppressor is gone,
havoc is finished,
the trampler has come to an end from the land.

[5] And a throne will be established in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and there will sit upon it in truth,
in the tent of David,
one who judges and seeks judgment
and is swift in righteousness.

[6] We have heard of the pride of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
very proud,
his haughtiness and his pride and his raging;
not right is his empty talk.

[7] Therefore ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ will wail for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
all of it will wail;
for the raisin cakes of ⟦kir-hareseth|Qir Chareset|Kir-hareseth⟧ you will moan,
utterly stricken.

[8] For the fields of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ languish,
the vine of ⟦sibmah|Sivmah|Sibmah⟧;
the lords of the nations
have struck down its choice branches;
to ⟦jazer|Yazer|Jazer⟧ they reached,
they wandered into the wilderness;
its shoots spread out,
they passed over the sea.

[9] Therefore I weep with the weeping of ⟦jazer|Yazer|Jazer⟧,
vine of ⟦sibmah|Sivmah|Sibmah⟧;
I drench you with my tears,
⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ and ⟦elealeh|Elaleh|Elealeh⟧;
for upon your summer fruit and upon your harvest
the shout has fallen.

[10] And gladness and rejoicing are gathered away from the garden land,
and in the vineyards there is no ringing cry, no shouting;
wine in the presses the treader does not tread;
the shout I have made to cease.

[11] Therefore my inward parts for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧
murmur like a lyre,
and my inmost part for ⟦kir-heres|Qir Cheres|Kir-heres⟧.

[12] And it shall be, when he appears, when ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has wearied himself upon the high place,
and he comes to his sanctuary to pray,
that he will not be able.

[13] This is the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke concerning ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ from long ago.

[14] And now ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken, saying: In three years, like the years of a hired man, the glory of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ will be made light, with all the great multitude; and the remnant will be a little, a trifle, not mighty.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v13, 14) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **Layout** — v1–12 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents; v13–14 prose. In v4 "Moab" begins the second line, where the accents put it (it was joined to "my outcasts"); v11 now has the three lines of the Hebrew. The quotation marks of v3–4 are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark who speaks (Moab's envoys to Zion, or the prophet).
- **"Send ye the lamb to the ruler of the land from Sela to the wilderness, unto the mount of the daughter of Zion" → "Send the lamb of the ruler of the land from Sela toward the wilderness, to the mount of the daughter of Zion"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁלְחוּ־כַר מֹשֵׁל־אֶרֶץ, "send the lamb of the ruler of the land" (the lamb due to the ruler, as tribute; no "to"); סֶלַע "Sela" (the word is "crag": a place in Edom, or "from the crag"); מִדְבָּרָה "toward the wilderness".
- **"For it shall be, that, as a wandering bird cast out of the nest, so the daughters of Moab shall be at the fords of Arnon" → "And it shall be: like fleeing birds, a nest sent away, the daughters of Moab will be at the fords of the Arnon"** (v2) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; עוֹף "birds" (collective); נוֹדֵד "fleeing" (נָדַד; also v3 "the fugitive"); קֵן מְשֻׁלָּח "a nest sent away" (the brood driven from it; no "out of"); no "so". "The daughters of Moab" may be its women or its towns.
- **"Take counsel, execute judgment; make thy shadow as the night in the midst of the noonday; hide the outcasts; bewray not him that wandereth" → "Bring counsel, make a decision; set your shade like the night in the midst of noon; hide the ones driven out; do not uncover the fugitive"** (v3) — the qere הָבִיאִי "bring" (feminine singular, as the verbs after it); the written text has הביאו (plural); עֲשׂוּ "make" stands in the plural in both. פְּלִילָה, only here, "decision" (arbitration; from פָּלַל "arbitrate"); שִׁית "set"; צֵל "shade"; נִדָּחִים "ones driven out" (נָדַח, also v4); גִּלָּה "uncover".
- **"Let mine outcasts dwell with thee, Moab; be thou a covert to them from the face of the spoiler: for the extortioner is at an end, the spoiler ceaseth, the oppressors are consumed out of the land" → "Let my ones driven out sojourn among you; Moab, be a hiding place to them from the face of the one who lays waste. For the oppressor is gone, havoc is finished, the trampler has come to an end from the land"** (v4) — Hebrew נִדָּחַי "my ones driven out" (as pointed; "the outcasts of Moab", reading נִדְּחֵי, is the versions' and many modern translations', from memory); גּוּר "sojourn"; סֵתֶר "hiding place"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of"; שׁוֹדֵד "one who lays waste", שֹׁד "havoc" (the root of 13:6; 15:1); אָפֵס "be gone"; הַמֵּץ, only here, "the oppressor" (lit. "the presser", from מוּץ "press"); כָּלָה "be finished"; תַּמּוּ, plural verb with the singular רֹמֵס "trampler" (collective); תַּם "come to an end". "When … then" (ESV) is not in the Hebrew: כִּי "for".
- **"And in mercy shall the throne be established: and he shall sit upon it in truth in the tabernacle of David, judging, and seeking judgment, and hasting righteousness" → "And a throne will be established in chesed / steadfast love, and there will sit upon it in truth, in the tent of David, one who judges and seeks judgment and is swift in righteousness"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהוּכַן, hofal of כּוּן "be established"; חֶסֶד (token); כִּסֵּא "a throne" (no article); the subject of וְיָשַׁב is the participle at the end, "one who judges" (no "he" supplied); אֱמֶת "truth"; אֹהֶל "tent"; שֹׁפֵט "one who judges"; דֹּרֵשׁ מִשְׁפָּט "seeking judgment"; מְהִר צֶדֶק, "swift (skilled) in righteousness".
- **"We have heard of the pride of Moab; he is very proud: even of his haughtiness, and his pride, and his wrath: but his lies shall not be so" → "We have heard of the pride of Moab, very proud, his haughtiness and his pride and his raging; not right is his empty talk"** (v6) — Hebrew גְּאוֹן "pride", גֵּא "proud", גַּאֲוָה "haughtiness" (as 13:11), גָּאוֹן again, עֶבְרָה "raging": four words of one root and one more, heaped up; no verb "he is". לֹא־כֵן בַּדָּיו, "not right (not so) is his empty talk": בַּד "empty talk, boasting" (not בַּד "linen", "pole"). Jer 48:29–30 has nearly the same words; it is translated in its place, as printed.
- **"Therefore shall Moab howl for Moab, every one shall howl: for the foundations of Kirhareseth shall ye mourn; surely they are stricken" → "Therefore Moab will wail for Moab; all of it will wail; for the raisin cakes of Kir-hareseth you will moan, utterly stricken"** (v7) — Hebrew יְיֵלִיל imperfect, "will wail" (not "let"); כֻּלֹּה "all of it"; אֲשִׁישֵׁי "raisin cakes" (as 2 Sam 6:19; Hos 3:1; KJV "foundations" from another root; Jer 48:31 has "the men of Kir-heres"); תֶּהְגּוּ, plural "you will moan" (הָגָה, of a mourner's low sound); אַךְ־נְכָאִים, "only stricken ones": "utterly stricken".
- **"For the fields of Heshbon languish, and the vine of Sibmah: the lords of the heathen have broken down the principal plants thereof, they are come even unto Jazer, they wandered through the wilderness: her branches are stretched out, they are gone over the sea" → "For the fields of Heshbon languish, the vine of Sibmah; the lords of the nations have struck down its choice branches; to Jazer they reached, they wandered into the wilderness; its shoots spread out, they passed over the sea"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׁדְמוֹת "fields" (as 2 Kgs 23:4; another word than שָׂדֶה); אֻמְלָל "languish" (singular); בַּעֲלֵי גוֹיִם "the lords of the nations"; הָלַם "strike down"; שְׂרוּקִּים "choice branches" (the red vines; uncertain). The Hebrew allows also "the vine of Sibmah, whose choice branches struck down the lords of the nations" (its wine overcame them); the order is kept and the subject left as printed. נָגַע עַד "reach to" (lit. "touch"); תָּעָה "wander"; שְׁלֻחוֹת "shoots" (what is sent out); נִטַּשׁ "spread out" (lit. "be let loose"); עָבַר "pass over".
- **"I will bewail with the weeping of Jazer the vine of Sibmah: I will water thee with my tears, O Heshbon, and Elealeh: for the shouting for thy summer fruits and for thy harvest is fallen" → "Therefore I weep with the weeping of Jazer, vine of Sibmah; I drench you with my tears, Heshbon and Elealeh; for upon your summer fruit and upon your harvest the shout has fallen"** (v9) — Hebrew גֶּפֶן שִׂבְמָה stands without "for": taken as an address, with Heshbon and Elealeh (or the object "I weep … the vine"); אֲרַיָּוֶךְ, a rare form of רָוָה "drench"; דִּמְעָה "tears" (singular in Hebrew); vocatives without "O"; הֵידָד "shout" (the cry of the grape treaders, v10; here perhaps the enemy's shout falling on the crop: not decided); נָפַל "has fallen" (not "ceased"; קַיִץ "summer fruit", קָצִיר "harvest").
- **"And gladness is taken away, and joy out of the plentiful field; and in the vineyards there shall be no singing, neither shall there be shouting: the treaders shall tread out no wine in their presses; I have made their vintage shouting to cease" → "And gladness and rejoicing are gathered away from the garden land, and in the vineyards there is no ringing cry, no shouting; wine in the presses the treader does not tread; the shout I have made to cease"** (v10) — Hebrew נֶאֱסַף "be gathered away"; שִׂמְחָה "gladness", גִּיל "rejoicing"; כַּרְמֶל "garden land"; לֹא־יְרֻנָּן לֹא יְרֹעָע, "no ringing cry is raised, no shout is raised" (רָנַן, רוּעַ, passives); יֶקֶב "press"; דָּרַךְ … הַדֹּרֵךְ "tread … the treader" (singular); הֵידָד "shout"; הִשְׁבַּתִּי "I have made to cease".
- **"Wherefore my bowels shall sound like an harp for Moab, and mine inward parts for Kirharesh" → "Therefore my inward parts for Moab murmur like a lyre, and my inmost part for Kir-heres"** (v11) — Hebrew מֵעַי "my inward parts", קִרְבִּי "my inmost part" (two words); הָמָה "murmur" (lit. "roar, sound"); כִּנּוֹר "lyre"; קִיר חָרֶשׂ "Kir-heres" (the shorter form of v7's name; the token's traditional form corrected to it).
- **"And it shall come to pass, when it is seen that Moab is weary on the high place, that he shall come to his sanctuary to pray; but he shall not prevail" → "And it shall be, when he appears, when Moab has wearied himself upon the high place, and he comes to his sanctuary to pray, that he will not be able"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי־נִרְאָה כִּי־נִלְאָה מוֹאָב, "when he appears, when Moab has wearied himself" (נִרְאָה "appear, be seen"; a play of sound with נִלְאָה "weary oneself"); וּבָא "and he comes"; לֹא יוּכָל "he will not be able" (no object).
- **"This is the word that the LORD hath spoken concerning Moab since that time" → "This is the word that Yahweh spoke concerning Moab from long ago"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶל "to, concerning"; מֵאָז "from long ago" (lit. "from then").
- **"Within three years, as the years of an hireling, and the glory of Moab shall be contemned, with all that great multitude; and the remnant shall be very small and feeble" → "In three years, like the years of a hired man, the glory of Moab will be made light, with all the great multitude; and the remnant will be a little, a trifle, not mighty"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּשָׁלֹשׁ שָׁנִים "in three years"; שָׂכִיר "hired man" (whose years are counted exactly); נִקְלָה "be made light" (set against כָּבוֹד "glory", literally "weight"); הָמוֹן "multitude"; שְׁאָר "remnant" (as 14:22); מְעַט מִזְעָר "a little, a trifle"; לוֹא כַבִּיר "not mighty".

### Notes
- **v6–11** — beside Jer 48:29–36 (from memory); each is translated as printed.
