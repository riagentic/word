# Isaiah 54

[1] "Shout for joy, barren one, who did not bear;
break forth into a ringing cry and cry aloud,
you who have not writhed in labor!
For more are the sons of the desolate one
than the sons of the married one," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] "Enlarge the place of your tent,
and the curtains of your dwellings let them stretch out; do not withhold;
lengthen your cords,
and your pegs make strong.

[3] For right and left you shall spread abroad,
and your seed shall dispossess nations,
and desolate cities they shall make to be dwelt in.

[4] Do not fear, for you shall not be ashamed;
and do not be put to shame, for you shall not be abashed;
for the shame of your youth you shall forget,
and the reproach of your widowhood you shall remember no more.

[5] For your husband is your Maker,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name;
and your Redeemer is the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of all the earth he shall be called.

[6] For like a wife forsaken and grieved in spirit
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has called you,
and a wife of youth, when she is rejected,"
says your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[7] "In a small moment I forsook you,
but with great compassion I will gather you.

[8] In a flood of wrath
I hid my face for a moment from you,
but with everlasting ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ I have had compassion on you,"
says your Redeemer, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] "For the waters of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ this is to me:
as I swore that the waters of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧
should no more pass over the earth,
so I have sworn that I will not be wrathful with you
and will not rebuke you.

[10] For the mountains shall depart
and the hills shall totter,
but my ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ shall not depart from you,
and the covenant of my ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ shall not totter,"
says the one who has compassion on you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] "Afflicted one, storm-tossed, not comforted,
behold, I am laying your stones in antimony,
and I will found you with sapphires.

[12] And I will make your pinnacles of ruby,
and your gates of sparkling stones,
and all your border of stones of delight.

[13] And all your sons shall be taught of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and great shall be the ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ of your sons.

[14] In righteousness you shall be established;
be far from extortion,
for you shall not fear;
and from terror, for it shall not come near you.

[15] Behold, they shall surely stir up strife:
it is not from me;
whoever stirs up strife with you
shall fall because of you.

[16] Behold, I have created the craftsman
who blows on the fire of coals
and brings out a weapon for his work;
and I have created the destroyer to wreck.

[17] No weapon that is formed against you shall prosper,
and every tongue that rises against you in judgment you shall condemn.
This is the inheritance of the servants of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and their righteousness is from me," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v5) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order. v2 "do not withhold" ends the first half (the former text put it at the head of the second); v6 and v8 follow the Hebrew order of their lines.
- **"Sing, O barren, thou that didst not bear" → "Shout for joy, barren one, who did not bear"** (v1) — Hebrew רָנִּי, רָנַן "shout for joy"; no "O" (the vocative is bare). **"break forth into singing, and cry aloud" → "break forth into a ringing cry and cry aloud"** — פִּצְחִי רִנָּה (רִנָּה "a ringing cry") וְצַהֲלִי (צָהַל "cry shrilly"). **"thou that didst not travail with child" → "you who have not writhed in labor"** — לֹא־חָלָה, חִיל "writhe". **"for more are the children of the desolate than the children of the married wife" → "For more are the sons of the desolate one than the sons of the married one"** — בְּנֵי "sons"; שׁוֹמֵמָה "the desolate one" (שָׁמֵם); בְעוּלָה, passive of בָּעַל "become the husband / owner of": "the one who has a husband". Gal 4:27 quotes the verse in the Greek ("Rejoice, barren one who does not bear; break forth and cry out, you who are not in labor; for many are the children of the desolate one, more than of her who has the husband"); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"and let them stretch forth the curtains of thine habitations: spare not" → "and the curtains of your dwellings let them stretch out; do not withhold"** (v2) — Hebrew יַטּוּ, "let them stretch out", the subject not named; יְרִיעָה "curtain"; מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling"; חָשַׂךְ "withhold". **"strengthen thy stakes" → "your pegs make strong"** — יָתֵד "peg"; חִזֵּק "make strong".
- **"For thou shalt break forth on the right hand and on the left" → "For right and left you shall spread abroad"** (v3) — Hebrew יָמִין וּשְׂמֹאול, "right and left" (on both sides; also "south and north"); תִּפְרֹצִי, פָּרַץ "break out", of increase "spread abroad" (as Gen 28:14). **"and thy seed shall inherit the Gentiles" → "and your seed shall dispossess nations"** — זֶרַע "seed"; יָרַשׁ with persons as object, "dispossess" (literally "possess"); גּוֹיִם without article. **"and make the desolate cities to be inhabited" → "and desolate cities they shall make to be dwelt in"** — יוֹשִׁיבוּ, hifil of יָשַׁב, plural ("they": the seed).
- **"Fear not … neither be thou confounded … for thou shalt not be put to shame" → "Do not fear … and do not be put to shame, for you shall not be abashed"** (v4) — three verbs: בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", נִכְלַם "be put to shame", חָפֵר "be abashed". **"the shame of thy youth"** — בֹּשֶׁת "shame"; עֲלוּמִים "youth" (another word than נְעוּרִים, v6: English has one). **"the reproach of thy widowhood"** — חֶרְפָּה "reproach".
- **"For thy Maker is thine husband" → "For your husband is your Maker"** (v5) — Hebrew בֹעֲלַיִךְ עֹשַׂיִךְ, Hebrew order; both are plural participles in form (as said of Elohim), "the one who is husband to you" (בָּעַל) and "the one who makes you" (עָשָׂה). **"the Holy One of Israel; The God of the whole earth shall he be called" → "the Holy One of Israel, the Elohim of all the earth he shall be called"** — כָל־הָאָרֶץ "all the earth"; יִקָּרֵא, imperfect. גֹּאֵל "redeemer".
- **"For the LORD hath called thee as a woman forsaken and grieved in spirit" → "For like a wife forsaken and grieved in spirit Yahweh has called you"** (v6) — Hebrew order kept; אִשָּׁה "woman / wife" (one word: here a wife); עֲזוּבָה, עָזַב "forsake" (as v7). **"and a wife of youth, when thou wast refused" → "and a wife of youth, when she is rejected"** — תִּמָּאֵס, nifal of מָאַס "reject", third person ("she"); כִּי may also make a question ("can a wife of youth be rejected?").
- **"For a small moment have I forsaken thee" → "In a small moment I forsook you"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּרֶגַע קָטֹן; עָזַב "forsake". רַחֲמִים "compassion".
- **"In a little wrath" → "In a flood of wrath"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּשֶׁצֶף קֶצֶף: קֶצֶף "wrath" (not אַף "anger"); שֶׁצֶף is found only here, taken as "flood, outpouring" (uncertain; it sounds with קֶצֶף). **"I hid my face from thee for a moment" → "I hid my face for a moment from you"** — Hebrew order. **"but with everlasting kindness will I have mercy on thee" → "but with everlasting chesed I have had compassion on you"** — Hebrew חֶסֶד עוֹלָם (עוֹלָם, literally "of the age", time out of sight); רִחַמְתִּיךְ, a perfect ("I have had compassion": what is decided is said as done). **"saith the LORD thy Redeemer" → "says your Redeemer, Yahweh"** — Hebrew order.
- **"For this is as the waters of Noah unto me" → "For the waters of Noah this is to me"** (v9) — the MT reads כִּי־מֵי נֹחַ, "for the waters of Noah"; some Hebrew manuscripts and versions read כִּימֵי, "like the days of Noah" (from memory), which the former text followed; the MT is kept. **"for as I have sworn" → "as I swore"** — אֲשֶׁר נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי, answered by כֵּן "so". **"should no more go over the earth" → "should no more pass over the earth"** — מֵעֲבֹר … עוֹד, עָבַר "pass over". **"that I would not be wroth with thee, nor rebuke thee" → "that I will not be wrathful with you and will not rebuke you"** — קָצַף "be wrathful" (as קֶצֶף, v8); גָּעַר "rebuke".
- **"For the mountains shall depart, and the hills be removed" → "For the mountains shall depart and the hills shall totter"** (v10) — Hebrew יָמוּשׁוּ (מוּשׁ "depart") and תְּמוּטֶנָה (מוּט "totter"), each verb used twice in the verse and kept the same both times. **"my kindness shall not depart from thee" → "my chesed shall not depart from you"** — חַסְדִּי. **"neither shall the covenant of my peace be removed" → "and the covenant of my shalom shall not totter"** — בְּרִית שְׁלוֹמִי, "the covenant of my *shalom*" (the former "my covenant of peace" moved the "my"). **"saith the LORD that hath mercy on thee" → "says the one who has compassion on you, Yahweh"** — מְרַחֲמֵךְ, a participle of רִחַם, before the name.
- **"O thou afflicted, tossed with tempest, and not comforted" → "Afflicted one, storm-tossed, not comforted"** (v11) — עֲנִיָּה, עָנִי "afflicted"; no "O", no "and". **"I will lay thy stones with fair colours" → "I am laying your stones in antimony"** — מַרְבִּיץ, a participle, literally "making lie down"; פּוּךְ "antimony" (eye-paint; here a dark setting or mortar; uncertain). **"and lay thy foundations with sapphires" → "and I will found you with sapphires"** — וִיסַדְתִּיךְ, "I will found you"; סַפִּיר "sapphire" (probably lapis lazuli).
- **"And I will make thy windows of agates" → "And I will make your pinnacles of ruby"** (v12) — Hebrew שִׁמְשֹׁתַיִךְ, literally "your suns" (the parts that catch the sun: pinnacles or battlements; uncertain); כַּדְכֹּד, a red stone (ruby or agate; uncertain). **"and thy gates of carbuncles" → "and your gates of sparkling stones"** — אַבְנֵי אֶקְדָּח, from קָדַח "kindle": "stones of fire" (the kind uncertain). **"and all thy borders of pleasant stones" → "and all your border of stones of delight"** — גְּבוּל "border" (the boundary or the land within; not "wall"); אַבְנֵי־חֵפֶץ "stones of delight".
- **"And all thy children shall be taught of the LORD" → "And all your sons shall be taught of Yahweh"** (v13) — Hebrew בָּנַיִךְ "your sons"; לִמּוּדֵי יְהוָה, "taught ones of Yahweh", the copula supplied. **"and great shall be the peace of thy children" → "and great shall be the shalom of your sons"** — the second בָּנָיִךְ is read by some as בֹּנָיִךְ "your builders" (a scroll from Qumran, from memory); the MT is kept. John 6:45 quotes v13a in the Greek ("And they shall all be taught of God").
- **"thou shalt be far from oppression" → "be far from extortion"** (v14) — Hebrew רַחֲקִי, an imperative; עֹשֶׁק "extortion" (as Lev 6:4; the verb of 52:4; "oppression" in the wider sense). **"and from terror"** — מְחִתָּה "terror" (root of חַת "dread"). תִּכּוֹנָנִי "you shall be established" (כּוּן).
- **"Behold, they shall surely gather together, but not by me" → "Behold, they shall surely stir up strife: it is not from me"** (v15) — Hebrew גּוֹר יָגוּר, with the infinitive absolute, literally "stirring strife he stirs strife" (the subject not named: "they", or "one"); גּוּר here is the root of strife or attack, not "sojourn" (as Ps 56:6; uncertain); אֶפֶס מֵאוֹתִי "nothing from me". **"whosoever shall gather together against thee shall fall for thy sake" → "whoever stirs up strife with you shall fall because of you"** — אִתָּךְ "with you"; עָלַיִךְ "upon you": "because of you" (or "shall go over to you").
- **"Behold, I have created the smith" → "Behold, I have created the craftsman"** (v16) — the qere הִנֵּה is translated (written הן, also "behold"); אָנֹכִי, an emphatic "I"; חָרָשׁ "craftsman". **"that bloweth the coals in the fire" → "who blows on the fire of coals"** — נֹפֵחַ בְּאֵשׁ פֶּחָם. **"and that bringeth forth an instrument for his work" → "and brings out a weapon for his work"** — כְּלִי "article, vessel", here a weapon; לְמַעֲשֵׂהוּ "for his work" (the former "for its purpose" interpreted). **"and I have created the waster to destroy" → "and I have created the destroyer to wreck"** — וְאָנֹכִי "and I" (no "also"); מַשְׁחִית "destroyer" (from שָׁחַת "ruin"); לְחַבֵּל, חִבֵּל "wreck", another root.
- **"No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper" → "No weapon that is formed against you shall prosper"** (v17) — כָּל־כְּלִי … לֹא "every weapon … not"; יָצַר "form"; צָלַח "prosper". **"and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn"** — אִתָּךְ "with you" (= against); הִרְשִׁיעַ "condemn" (declare wicked). **"This is the heritage of the servants of the LORD" → "This is the inheritance of the servants of Yahweh"** — נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; עַבְדֵי יְהוָה, עֶבֶד "slave", of Yahweh's men "servant". **"and their righteousness is of me, saith the LORD" → "and their righteousness is from me, declares Yahweh"** — מֵאִתִּי "from me"; נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh".

### Notes
