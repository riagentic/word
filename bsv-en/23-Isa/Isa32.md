# Isaiah 32

[1] Behold, for righteousness a king shall reign,
and as for princes, for judgment they shall be princes.

[2] And a man shall be like a shelter from wind
and a hiding place from downpour,
like streams of water in a dry place,
like the shade of a heavy crag in a weary land.

[3] And the eyes of those who see shall not be blind,
and the ears of those who hear shall give heed.

[4] And the heart of the hasty shall understand to know,
and the tongue of stammerers shall hasten to speak clear things.

[5] No more shall the base fool be called noble,
and of the knave it shall not be said, "Rich."

[6] For the base fool speaks outrage,
and his heart works wickedness,
to work godlessness,
and to speak error against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
to leave empty the soul of the hungry,
and the drink of the thirsty he makes to fail.

[7] And the knave, his instruments are evil;
he counsels depravities,
to ravage the afflicted with sayings of falsehood,
even when the needy speaks judgment.

[8] But the noble counsels noble things,
and upon noble things he stands.

[9] Women at ease, rise, hear my voice;
trusting daughters, give ear to my saying.

[10] Days upon a year
you shall be agitated, trusting ones;
for the vintage is at an end,
the gathering shall not come.

[11] Tremble, you at ease;
be agitated, trusting ones;
strip, and make yourselves bare,
and gird upon the loins.

[12] Upon breasts they are lamenting,
for the desirable fields,
for the fruitful vine,

[13] for the ground of my people,
where thorn, brier comes up;
yes, for all the houses of exultation,
the jubilant town.

[14] For the citadel is abandoned,
the multitude of the city is forsaken;
⟦ophel|Ofel|Ophel⟧ and watchtower
have become caves forever,
the exultation of wild donkeys,
a pasture of droves;

[15] until the spirit is poured out upon us from on high,
and the wilderness becomes the garden land,
and the garden land is reckoned as the forest.

[16] And judgment shall abide in the wilderness,
and righteousness shall dwell in the garden land.

[17] And the work of righteousness shall be ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧,
and the service of righteousness,
quietness and security forever.

[18] And my people shall dwell in a habitation of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧,
and in dwellings of trust,
and in resting places at ease.

[19] And it shall hail when the forest comes down,
and in lowness the city shall be low.

[20] Happy are you who sow beside all waters,
who send out the foot of the ox and the donkey.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v6) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order (v18 now three lines, as its accents divide it).
- **"a king shall reign in righteousness, and princes shall rule in judgment" → "for righteousness a king shall reign, and as for princes, for judgment they shall be princes"** (v1) — Hebrew לְצֶדֶק יִמְלָךְ־מֶלֶךְ וּלְשָׂרִים לְמִשְׁפָּט יָשֹׂרוּ: לְ "for, according to" (not "in"); the second לְ before "princes" sets them first ("as for princes"); שָׂרַר "be prince", the verb of שַׂר "prince" (the play kept). מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (also v7, 16).
- **"And a man shall be" kept** (v2) — Hebrew וְהָיָה־אִישׁ: "a man" (or "each one"); the earlier "Each" chose one. **"an hiding place from the wind, and a covert from the tempest" → "a shelter from wind and a hiding place from downpour"** — מַחֲבֵא "shelter" (of חָבָא "hide"), סֵתֶר "hiding place": two words, kept apart; זֶרֶם "downpour" (as 30:30). **"rivers of water in a dry place" → "streams of water"** — פַּלְגֵי־מַיִם. **"a great rock" → "a heavy crag"** — סֶלַע כָּבֵד. **"a weary land"** — אֶרֶץ עֲיֵפָה.
- **"shall not be dim" → "shall not be blind"** (v3) — Hebrew תִשְׁעֶינָה, of שָׁעַע "be smeared shut", the verb of 29:9 "blind yourselves, and be blind": the curse of 29:9–10 undone. **"shall hearken" → "shall give heed"** — הִקְשִׁיב.
- **"The heart also of the rash shall understand knowledge" → "And the heart of the hasty shall understand to know"** (v4) — Hebrew נִמְהָרִים, of מָהַר "hasten", the verb that follows (תְּמַהֵר "shall hasten"); לָדָעַת "to know". **"shall be ready to speak plainly" → "shall hasten to speak clear things"** — צָחוֹת "clear (bright) things"; עִלְּגִים "stammerers".
- **"The vile person shall be no more called liberal, nor the churl said to be bountiful" → "No more shall the base fool be called noble, and of the knave it shall not be said, 'Rich'"** (v5; so v6, 8) — Hebrew נָבָל "base fool" (the godless fool who acts shamefully, as in Proverbs; not "the vile person"); נָדִיב "noble" ("generous" is also in the word); כִּילַי (v5, 7: כֵּלַי) "knave", only here (a cheat; uncertain); שׁוֹעַ "rich" (one of rank and means, Job 34:19; "generous" in the earlier text).
- **"For the vile person will speak villany" → "For the base fool speaks outrage"** (v6) — נָבָל נְבָלָה, the noun of the same root (the deed of the base fool, as Gen 34:7). **"his heart will work iniquity" → "his heart works wickedness"** — אָוֶן "wickedness". **"to practise hypocrisy" → "to work godlessness"** — חֹנֶף, of חָנֵף "godless" (literally "polluted"); the same verb עָשָׂה "work, do" in both lines. **"to utter error against the LORD"** — וּלְדַבֵּר אֶל־יְהוָה תּוֹעָה, אֶל "to, against"; תּוֹעָה "error" (of תָּעָה "wander"). **"to make empty the soul of the hungry" → "to leave empty the soul of the hungry"** — הָרִיק, נֶפֶשׁ "soul". **"he will cause the drink of the thirsty to fail" → "the drink of the thirsty he makes to fail"** — מַשְׁקֶה here "drink" (elsewhere "cupbearer", the one who gives drink); הֶחְסִיר "make lack".
- **"The instruments also of the churl are evil" → "And the knave, his instruments are evil"** (v7) — Hebrew וְכֵלַי כֵּלָיו רָעִים, the play כֵּלַי / כֵּלָיו kept; כְּלִי "instruments" (his means). **"he deviseth wicked devices" → "he counsels depravities"** — זִמּוֹת יָעָץ: זִמָּה "depravity", יָעַץ "counsel" (as v8). **"to destroy the poor with lying words" → "to ravage the afflicted with sayings of falsehood"** — חִבֵּל "ravage"; the qere עֲנִיִּים "afflicted"; the written form ענוים "humble"; אִמְרֵי־שֶׁקֶר. **"even when the needy speaketh right" → "even when the needy speaks judgment"** — וּבְדַבֵּר אֶבְיוֹן מִשְׁפָּט.
- **"But the liberal deviseth liberal things; and by liberal things shall he stand" → "But the noble counsels noble things, and upon noble things he stands"** (v8) — נָדִיב · נְדִיבוֹת; יָעַץ "counsel"; עַל "upon"; קוּם "stand, rise".
- **"Rise up, ye women that are at ease" → "Women at ease, rise"** (v9; so v11, 18) — Hebrew נָשִׁים שַׁאֲנַנּוֹת קֹמְנָה, in that order; שַׁאֲנָן "at ease". **"ye careless daughters" → "trusting daughters"** (v9, 10, 11) — בֹּטְחוֹת, participle of בָּטַח "trust" (said of a false security; "careless" is the sense, not the word). **"give ear unto my speech" → "give ear to my saying"** — הַאְזֵנָּה אִמְרָתִי.
- **"Many days and years shall ye be troubled" → "Days upon a year you shall be agitated"** (v10) — Hebrew יָמִים עַל־שָׁנָה, "days over a year", that is, in a little more than a year (as "year upon year", 29:1); רָגַז "be agitated" (v10, 11). **"the vintage shall fail" → "the vintage is at an end"** — כָּלָה בָצִיר, perfect. **"the gathering shall not come"** — אֹסֶף.
- **"Tremble, ye women that are at ease" → "Tremble, you at ease"** (v11) — חִרְדוּ שַׁאֲנַנּוֹת: the imperatives are masculine or unusual in form while the women are addressed; the Hebrew kept. **"strip you, and make you bare" → "strip, and make yourselves bare"** — פְּשֹׁטָה וְעֹרָה. **"gird sackcloth upon your loins" → "gird upon the loins"** — Hebrew וַחֲגוֹרָה עַל־חֲלָצָיִם: "sackcloth" and "your" are not written.
- **"They shall lament for the teats" → "Upon breasts they are lamenting"** (v12) — Hebrew עַל־שָׁדַיִם סֹפְדִים: סָפַד "lament" (beating the breast), participle, masculine; עַל "upon / for"; שָׁדַיִם "breasts" is set beside שְׂדֵי "fields" (the play of sound). **"the pleasant fields" → "the desirable fields"** — שְׂדֵי־חֶמֶד, of חָמַד "desire". **"the fruitful vine"** — גֶּפֶן פֹּרִיָּה.
- **"Upon the land of my people shall come up thorns and briers" → "for the ground of my people, where thorn, brier comes up"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל אַדְמַת עַמִּי קוֹץ שָׁמִיר תַּעֲלֶה, עַל continuing the "for" of v12; אֲדָמָה "ground"; two nouns, singular, without "and". **"yea, upon all the houses of joy in the joyous city" → "yes, for all the houses of exultation, the jubilant town"** — מָשׂוֹשׂ "exultation" (also v14); קִרְיָה "town"; עַלִּיזָה, of עָלַז "be jubilant"; "in" is not written.
- **"Because the palaces shall be forsaken" → "For the citadel is abandoned"** (v14) — אַרְמוֹן "citadel", singular; נֻטָּשׁ, of נָטַשׁ "abandon". **"the multitude of the city shall be left" → "the multitude of the city is forsaken"** — הֲמוֹן עִיר עֻזָּב, עָזַב "forsake". **"the forts and towers" → "Ophel and watchtower"** — עֹפֶל וָבַחַן: עֹפֶל "Ophel", the hill of Jerusalem south of the temple (token; without the article here, so it may also be the common noun "swelling, mound"); בַּחַן, only here, "watchtower" (uncertain). **"shall be for dens for ever" → "have become caves forever"** — Hebrew הָיָה בְעַד מְעָרוֹת, a perfect; בְעַד, literally "for, in place of" (uncertain here); מְעָרָה "cave". **"a joy of wild asses, a pasture of flocks" → "the exultation of wild donkeys, a pasture of droves"** — מְשׂוֹשׂ פְּרָאִים; עֵדֶר "drove".
- **"Until the spirit be poured upon us from on high" → "until the spirit is poured out upon us from on high"** (v15) — Hebrew עַד־יֵעָרֶה, of עֵרָה "pour out" (literally "lay bare"); רוּחַ has no article ("the" is supplied for English; "spirit" lower case, the Hebrew does not say more). **"and the wilderness be a fruitful field, and the fruitful field be counted for a forest" → "and the wilderness becomes the garden land, and the garden land is reckoned as the forest"** — כַּרְמֶל "garden land" (as 29:17); the qere וְהַכַּרְמֶל with the article, the written form without it: the same sense.
- **"Then judgment shall dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness remain in the fruitful field" → "And judgment shall abide in the wilderness, and righteousness shall dwell in the garden land"** (v16) — וְשָׁכַן "abide", תֵּשֵׁב "dwell" (two verbs kept apart); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (not "justice").
- **"the work of righteousness shall be peace" → "shall be shalom"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם, token `shalom`. **"the effect of righteousness quietness and assurance for ever" → "the service of righteousness, quietness and security forever"** — עֲבֹדַת הַצְּדָקָה "the service of righteousness" (עֲבֹדָה, not "effect"); הַשְׁקֵט "quietness" (as 30:15); בֶּטַח "security".
- **"in a peaceable habitation" → "in a habitation of shalom"** (v18) — נְוֵה שָׁלוֹם (נָוֶה "habitation", literally "pasture, abode"). **"in sure dwellings" → "in dwellings of trust"** — מִשְׁכְּנוֹת מִבְטַחִים. **"in quiet resting places" → "in resting places at ease"** — מְנוּחֹת שַׁאֲנַנּוֹת.
- **"When it shall hail, coming down on the forest" → "And it shall hail when the forest comes down"** (v19) — Hebrew וּבָרַד בְּרֶדֶת הַיַּעַר: בָּרַד "hail" and בְּרֶדֶת (of יָרַד "come down") sound alike. **"the city shall be low in a low place" → "in lowness the city shall be low"** — וּבַשִּׁפְלָה תִּשְׁפַּל הָעִיר, noun and verb of one root, in the Hebrew order.
- **"Blessed are ye that sow beside all waters" → "Happy are you who sow beside all waters"** (v20) — אַשְׁרֵיכֶם "happy"; עַל "beside" (literally "upon"). **"that send forth thither the feet of the ox and the ass" → "who send out the foot of the ox and the donkey"** — שִׁלַּח "send out" (to pasture); רֶגֶל "foot", singular; "thither" is not written.
