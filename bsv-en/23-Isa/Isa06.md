# Isaiah 6

[1] In the year of the death of King ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ I saw ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ sitting on a throne, high and lifted up, and his skirts were filling the temple.

[2] ⟦seraphim|Serafim|Seraphim⟧ were standing above him; six wings, six wings to each one: with two he covered his face, and with two he covered his feet, and with two he flew.

[3] And this one called to that one and said:

"Holy, holy, holy is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts;
the fullness of all the earth is his glory!"

[4] And the posts of the thresholds swayed at the voice of him who called, and the house was filling with smoke.

[5] And I said, "Woe to me! For I am cut off; for I am a man unclean of lips, and in the midst of a people unclean of lips I dwell; for the King, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, my eyes have seen!"

[6] And one of the ⟦seraphim|serafim|seraphim⟧ flew to me, and in his hand a glowing coal, which he had taken with tongs from upon the altar.

[7] And he made it touch my mouth and said, "Behold, this has touched your lips, and your iniquity has departed, and your sin is atoned for."

[8] And I heard the voice of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ saying, "Whom shall I send, and who will go for us?" And I said, "Here I am; send me."

[9] And he said, "Go, and say to this people:

'Surely hear, and do not understand;
and surely see, and do not know.'

[10] Make fat the heart of this people,
and its ears make heavy,
and its eyes smear shut;
lest it see with its eyes,
and with its ears hear,
and its heart understand,
and it turn back, and one heal it."

[11] And I said, "How long, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧?" And he said:

"Until cities lie in ruins without inhabitant,
and houses without man,
and the ground is laid in ruins, a desolation,

[12] and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ removes mankind far away,
and the forsaken place is great in the midst of the land.

[13] And there is yet a tenth in it,
and it shall again be for burning,
like the terebinth and like the oak,
of which, at the felling, there is a stump in them;
holy seed is its stump."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v1, 8, 11) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v4–8, 13). In v1 the וָ of וָאֶרְאֶה follows the phrase of time and is not rendered.
- **"In the year that king Uzziah died" → "In the year of the death of King Uzziah"** (v1) — Hebrew בִּשְׁנַת־מוֹת הַמֶּלֶךְ, a noun "death". **"and his train filled the temple" → "and his skirts were filling the temple"** — שׁוּלָיו "his skirts, hems"; מְלֵאִים a participle. "High and lifted up" (רָם וְנִשָּׂא) may describe the throne or Adonai.
- **"Above it stood the seraphims" → "Seraphim were standing above him"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׂרָפִים, no article; עֹמְדִים a participle; מִמַּעַל לוֹ "above him" (or "above it", the throne). The word is from שָׂרַף "burn"; "angels" is not said. **"each one had six wings" → "six wings, six wings to each one"** — the number is repeated (= six wings each). **"covered … flew"** — imperfects (יְכַסֶּה, יְעוֹפֵף): what each did again and again.
- **"And one cried unto another" → "And this one called to that one"** (v3) — Hebrew וְקָרָא זֶה אֶל־זֶה, literally "this to this"; וְקָרָא … וְאָמַר are forms of repeated action ("kept calling … saying"). **"the whole earth is full of his glory" → "the fullness of all the earth is his glory"** — Hebrew מְלֹא כָל־הָאָרֶץ כְּבוֹדוֹ, word for word: "the fullness of all the earth" is what fills the earth (as Ps 24:1); the sense may be "all that fills the earth is his glory" or "his glory is what fills all the earth".
- **"the posts of the door moved" → "the posts of the thresholds swayed"** (v4) — Hebrew אַמּוֹת הַסִּפִּים: אַמָּה is elsewhere "cubit, forearm"; here "posts" or "pivots" (uncertain); סִפִּים "thresholds". נוּעַ "sway". **"was filled" → "was filling"** — יִמָּלֵא, imperfect.
- **"Woe is me! for I am undone" → "Woe to me! For I am cut off"** (v5) — Hebrew אוֹי־לִי (אוֹי, a cry of distress, not הוֹי); נִדְמֵיתִי from דָּמָה "be cut off, destroyed" (or from דָּמַם "be silenced": both in the root sense; uncertain). **"a man of unclean lips" → "a man unclean of lips"** — טְמֵא־שְׂפָתַיִם; "and in the midst of a people unclean of lips I dwell" keeps the Hebrew order.
- **"a live coal in his hand, which he had taken with the tongs" → "in his hand a glowing coal, which he had taken with tongs from upon the altar"** (v6) — Hebrew רִצְפָּה, a glowing coal or a hot stone (uncertain); מֵעַל "from upon".
- **"he laid it upon my mouth" → "he made it touch my mouth"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיַּגַּע, hifil of נָגַע "touch". **"thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged" → "your iniquity has departed, and your sin is atoned for"** — Hebrew וְסָר עֲוֺנֶךָ (סוּר "depart"); תְּכֻפָּר, pual of כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (root sense "cover").
- **"Here am I" → "Here I am"** (v8) — Hebrew הִנְנִי, literally "behold me".
- **"Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not" → "Surely hear, and do not understand; and surely see, and do not know"** (v9) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ שָׁמוֹעַ … וּרְאוּ רָאוֹ, an imperative with its infinitive absolute, literally "hear, hearing … see, seeing" (= hear on and on); וְאַל "and do not"; יָדַע "know".
- **"Make the heart of this people fat, and make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes" → "Make fat the heart of this people, and its ears make heavy, and its eyes smear shut"** (v10) — Hebrew הַשְׁמֵן, הַכְבֵּד (כָּבֵד "heavy"), הָשַׁע (from שָׁעַע "smear over"): three imperatives to the prophet; "this people" is singular, so "its". **"and convert, and be healed" → "and it turn back, and one heal it"** — Hebrew וָשָׁב וְרָפָא לוֹ, "and turn back and heal for it": the one who heals is not named (impersonal). Matt 13:14–15; Mark 4:12; Luke 8:10; John 12:40; Acts 28:26–27 quote these verses; the Greek of Matt 13:15 has statements (ἐπαχύνθη … ἐκάμμυσαν, "has grown fat … they have closed") and ἰάσομαι αὐτούς "I will heal them" (the Septuagint, from memory, reads the same); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"Until the cities be wasted" → "Until cities lie in ruins"** (v11) — Hebrew שָׁאוּ, "crash into ruins"; עָרִים without article. **"without man"** — מֵאֵין אָדָם. **"the land be utterly desolate" → "the ground is laid in ruins, a desolation"** — הָאֲדָמָה "the ground"; תִּשָּׁאֶה, nifal of the same verb שָׁאָה; שְׁמָמָה "desolation".
- **"have removed men far away" → "removes mankind far away"** (v12) — Hebrew וְרִחַק … אֶת־הָאָדָם. **"there be a great forsaking" → "the forsaken place is great"** — הָעֲזוּבָה, "the forsaken (one)", feminine: "place" is supplied (or "the forsaken land").
- **"But yet in it shall be a tenth, and it shall return, and shall be eaten" → "And there is yet a tenth in it, and it shall again be for burning"** (v13) — Hebrew וְעוֹד בָּהּ עֲשִׂרִיָּה; וְשָׁבָה וְהָיְתָה, literally "and it shall return and be" (= be again); לְבָעֵר "for burning" (the verb is also "graze", as 5:5: of trees here "burning"; uncertain). **"as a teil tree, and as an oak, whose substance is in them, when they cast their leaves: so the holy seed shall be the substance thereof" → "like the terebinth and like the oak, of which, at the felling, there is a stump in them; holy seed is its stump"** — Hebrew אֵלָה "terebinth", אַלּוֹן "oak" (kinds not certain); בְּשַׁלֶּכֶת "at the felling" (literally "at the casting down"; also read as the name of a gate, 1 Chr 26:16); מַצֶּבֶת "stump" (what stands); זֶרַע קֹדֶשׁ, literally "seed of holiness", no article. The last line is part of the answer (the former text set it outside the quotation).

### Notes
- **v3** — the threefold "holy"; also Rev 4:8.
- **v9–10** — quoted in Matt 13:14–15; Mark 4:12; Luke 8:10; John 12:40; Acts 28:26–27.
- **Seraphim** — from *saraph*, "to burn"; the same word names the burning serpents of Num 21:6.
