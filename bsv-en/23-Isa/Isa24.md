# Isaiah 24

[1] Behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is emptying the earth and devastating it,
and he will twist its face and scatter its inhabitants.

[2] And it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest;
as with the slave, so with his lord;
as with the maidservant, so with her mistress;
as with the buyer, so with the seller;
as with the lender, so with the borrower;
as with the creditor, so with the one who is in his debt.

[3] The earth shall surely be emptied, and surely be plundered,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken this word.

[4] The earth has mourned, has withered;
the world has languished, has withered;
the height of the people of the earth have languished.

[5] And the earth has been polluted under its inhabitants,
for they have overstepped ⟦torah|torot|laws⟧,
changed statute,
broken an everlasting covenant.

[6] Therefore a curse has devoured the earth,
and those who dwell in it have become guilty;
therefore the inhabitants of the earth have burned,
and mortal men are left, few.

[7] The new wine has mourned, the vine has languished;
all the glad of heart have sighed.

[8] The exultation of the timbrels has ceased,
the tumult of the jubilant has ceased,
the exultation of the lyre has ceased.

[9] With song they do not drink wine;
strong drink is bitter to those who drink it.

[10] The town of waste has been broken;
every house has been shut from entering.

[11] A crying over the wine in the streets;
all gladness has grown dark;
the exultation of the earth has gone into exile.

[12] In the city is left desolation,
and into ruin the gate is beaten.

[13] For thus it shall be in the midst of the earth,
among the peoples,
like the beating of an olive tree,
like the gleanings when the vintage is ended.

[14] They lift up their voice, they shout for joy;
over the exaltation of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they have cried aloud from the west.

[15] Therefore in the lights honor ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
in the coastlands of the sea,
the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[16] From the wing of the earth we have heard songs:
"Beauty to the righteous one!"

And I said: "Leanness to me, leanness to me, woe to me!
The treacherous have dealt treacherously,
and with treachery the treacherous have dealt treacherously."

[17] Dread and pitfall and trap
are upon you, inhabitant of the earth!

[18] And it shall be, he who flees from the sound of the dread
shall fall into the pitfall,
and he who comes up from the midst of the pitfall
shall be captured in the trap.
For windows from on high have been opened,
and the foundations of the earth have quaked.

[19] The earth has surely been broken,
the earth has surely been split apart,
the earth has surely tottered.

[20] The earth shall surely sway like a drunkard,
and it shall rock to and fro like a hut;
and its transgression shall be heavy upon it,
and it shall fall and not rise again.

[21] And it shall be in that day, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will visit
upon the host of the height in the height,
and upon the kings of the ground on the ground.

[22] And they shall be gathered, a gathering of prisoners,
into a pit,
and they shall be shut up in a dungeon,
and after many days they shall be visited.

[23] And the moon shall be abashed,
and the sun ashamed,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has become king
on Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
and before his elders is glory.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 14, 15, 21, 23) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v23). **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15).
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order (v15: three lines, as the Hebrew accents divide it).
- **Tense** (v4–12, 14, 16, 18–19, 23) — the Hebrew has perfects (what is seen as done) between imperfects (what shall be): the perfects are kept as English perfects ("has mourned", "has ceased"); KJV puts them in the present.
- **"the LORD maketh the earth empty, and maketh it waste, and turneth it upside down, and scattereth abroad the inhabitants thereof" → "Yahweh is emptying the earth and devastating it, and he will twist its face and scatter its inhabitants"** (v1) — Hebrew הִנֵּה with participles (בּוֹקֵק, בּוֹלְקָהּ), then perfects with וְ: "he will"; עִוָּה פָנֶיהָ "twist its face" (= its surface).
- **"as with the servant, so with his master; as with the maid" → "as with the slave, so with his lord; as with the maidservant"** (v2) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, אֲדֹנָיו, שִׁפְחָה. Each line is כְּ … כְּ, "as … so"; "with" is supplied. **"as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the taker of usury, so with the giver of usury to him" → "as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the creditor, so with the one who is in his debt"** — מַלְוֶה, לֹוֶה (הִלְוָה "lend"); נֹשֶׁה "creditor" (as Exod 22:25), נֹשֵׁא בוֹ "the one who has a loan with him" (the same root, נָשָׁה בְּ "lend to"): no word for "usury".
- **"The land shall be utterly emptied, and utterly spoiled" → "The earth shall surely be emptied, and surely be plundered"** (v3) — Hebrew הִבּוֹק תִּבּוֹק … הִבּוֹז תִּבּוֹז, literally "being emptied it shall be emptied … being plundered it shall be plundered": "surely". הָאָרֶץ is "the earth" through the chapter (the whole world, v4 תֵּבֵל; one word, "land" in other places).
- **"The earth mourneth and fadeth away, the world languisheth and fadeth away, the haughty people of the earth do languish" → "The earth has mourned, has withered; the world has languished, has withered; the height of the people of the earth have languished"** (v4) — Hebrew אָבַל, נָבֵל "wither", אֻמְלַל "languish"; מְרוֹם עַם־הָאָרֶץ "the height of the people of the earth" (= those on high among them), with a plural verb.
- **"The earth also is defiled under the inhabitants thereof" → "And the earth has been polluted under its inhabitants"** (v5) — Hebrew חָנְפָה (as הֶחֱנִיף "pollute", Num 35:33). **"because they have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant" → "for they have overstepped torot, changed statute, broken an everlasting covenant"** — תוֹרֹת, plural of *torah*, without the article (token added); עָבַר "overstep" (literally "pass over"); חָלְפוּ "changed" (or "passed by"); חֹק "statute", no article; בְּרִית עוֹלָם "an everlasting covenant" (literally "of the age"), no article in the Hebrew (Gen 9:16 has the same phrase).
- **"and they that dwell therein are desolate" → "and those who dwell in it have become guilty"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֶּאְשְׁמוּ, of אָשֵׁם "be guilty" (or "bear guilt"). אָלָה "curse"; אָכְלָה "has devoured". **"the inhabitants of the earth are burned" → "have burned"** — חָרוּ, qal (of חָרַר "burn", or חָרָה "be hot"; uncertain). **"and few men left" → "and mortal men are left, few"** — אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; מִזְעָר "a few".
- **"The new wine mourneth, the vine languisheth, all the merryhearted do sigh" → "The new wine has mourned, the vine has languished; all the glad of heart have sighed"** (v7) — תִּירוֹשׁ "new wine"; שִׂמְחֵי־לֵב "the glad of heart".
- **"The mirth of tabrets ceaseth, the noise of them that rejoice endeth, the joy of the harp ceaseth" → "The exultation of the timbrels has ceased, the tumult of the jubilant has ceased, the exultation of the lyre has ceased"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁבַת … חָדַל … שָׁבַת: two verbs, both "cease" (English has one word); מְשׂוֹשׂ "exultation", twice (as 65:18); שְׁאוֹן "tumult" (a roaring noise, as Isa 17:12); עַלִּיזִים "the jubilant" (as 22:2; 23:7); כִּנּוֹר "lyre". The Hebrew puts each verb first.
- **"They shall not drink wine with a song" → "With song they do not drink wine"** (v9) — Hebrew order; שֵׁכָר "strong drink".
- **"The city of confusion is broken down" → "The town of waste has been broken"** (v10) — Hebrew קִרְיַת־תֹּהוּ, קִרְיָה "town", תֹּהוּ "waste" (as Gen 1:2); which town is meant is not said. **"every house is shut up, that no man may come in" → "every house has been shut from entering"** — מִבּוֹא, literally "from entering" (no "man").
- **"There is a crying for wine in the streets" → "A crying over the wine in the streets"** (v11) — Hebrew צְוָחָה עַל־הַיַּיִן, no verb; עַל "over, concerning". **"all joy is darkened" → "all gladness has grown dark"** — עָרְבָה "has become evening"; שִׂמְחָה "gladness". **"the mirth of the land is gone" → "the exultation of the earth has gone into exile"** — גָּלָה "go into exile" (as 1 Sam 4:21).
- **"In the city is left desolation, and the gate is smitten with destruction" → "In the city is left desolation, and into ruin the gate is beaten"** (v12) — Hebrew שַׁמָּה "desolation" (elsewhere "a thing that appalls", Deut 28:37: of a place, what is left waste); שְׁאִיָּה, only here, "ruin" (uncertain); יֻכַּת, hofal of כָּתַת "beat in pieces"; שַׁעַר singular.
- **"among the people" → "among the peoples"** (v13) — Hebrew הָעַמִּים, plural. **"as the shaking of an olive tree, and as the gleaning grapes when the vintage is done" → "like the beating of an olive tree, like the gleanings when the vintage is ended"** — נֹקֶף "beating (off)"; עוֹלֵלֹת "gleanings" (of grapes; the noun of עוֹלֵל "glean"; לֶקֶט "gleanings" is another word); בָּצִיר "vintage"; כָּלָה "is ended".
- **"They shall lift up their voice, they shall sing for the majesty of the LORD, they shall cry aloud from the sea" → "They lift up their voice, they shout for joy; over the exaltation of Yahweh they have cried aloud from the west"** (v14) — Hebrew הֵמָּה "they", written out; יָרֹנּוּ "shout for joy"; בִּגְאוֹן "over (in) the exaltation of" (as 23:9); צָהֲלוּ, a perfect; מִיָּם, literally "from the sea" (= from the west; or "more than the sea").
- **"Wherefore glorify ye the LORD in the fires, even the name of the LORD God of Israel in the isles of the sea" → "Therefore in the lights honor Yahweh; in the coastlands of the sea, the name of Yahweh, the Elohim of Israel"** (v15) — Hebrew בָּאֻרִים, literally "in the lights" (uncertain: the regions of light, the east, set against "the sea", the west; or "in the fires"; or a lost name); כַּבְּדוּ "honor" (piel of כָּבֵד); the verb is written once and governs both lines; אִיֵּי "coastlands".
- **"From the uttermost part of the earth have we heard songs, even glory to the righteous" → "From the wing of the earth we have heard songs: Beauty to the righteous one!"** (v16) — Hebrew מִכְּנַף הָאָרֶץ, literally "from the wing (edge) of the earth"; זְמִרֹת "songs"; צְבִי "beauty" (as 23:9; "glory" is כָּבוֹד); לַצַּדִּיק "to the righteous one" (a man, a people, or God: not said; no capital). **"But I said, My leanness, my leanness, woe unto me!" → "And I said: Leanness to me, leanness to me, woe to me!"** — וָאֹמַר, a past form; רָזִי־לִי, of רָזָה "be lean" (as רָזוֹן Ps 106:15; or Aramaic רָז "secret": "my secret is mine", from memory as an old reading); אוֹי לִי. **"the treacherous dealers have dealt treacherously; yea, the treacherous dealers have dealt very treacherously" → "The treacherous have dealt treacherously, and with treachery the treacherous have dealt treacherously"** — בֹּגְדִים בָּגָדוּ וּבֶגֶד בּוֹגְדִים בָּגָדוּ, word for word (as 21:2).
- **"Fear, and the pit, and the snare" → "Dread and pitfall and trap"** (v17–18) — Hebrew פַּחַד וָפַחַת וָפָח, three words that sound alike; פַּחַד "dread"; פַּחַת "pitfall" (kept apart from בּוֹר "pit", v22); פַּח "trap" (the fowler's). **"O inhabitant of the earth" → "inhabitant of the earth"** — no "O".
- **"And it shall come to pass, that he who fleeth from the noise of the fear" → "And it shall be, he who flees from the sound of the dread"** (v18) — וְהָיָה; קוֹל "sound". **"he that cometh up out of the midst of the pit shall be taken in the snare" → "he who comes up from the midst of the pitfall shall be captured in the trap"** — לָכַד "capture". **"for the windows from on high are open" → "For windows from on high have been opened"** — אֲרֻבּוֹת מִמָּרוֹם, no article, no "heaven" (as Gen 7:11 "the windows of the heavens" has its own words); רָעַשׁ "quake".
- **"The earth is utterly broken down, the earth is clean dissolved, the earth is moved exceedingly" → "The earth has surely been broken, the earth has surely been split apart, the earth has surely tottered"** (v19) — three infinitives absolute with their verbs (רֹעָה הִתְרֹעֲעָה, פּוֹר הִתְפּוֹרְרָה, מוֹט הִתְמוֹטְטָה): "surely". The first may also be read of רוּעַ "shout" (uncertain).
- **"The earth shall reel to and fro like a drunkard, and shall be removed like a cottage" → "The earth shall surely sway like a drunkard, and it shall rock to and fro like a hut"** (v20) — Hebrew נוֹעַ תָּנוּעַ "surely sway"; שִׁכּוֹר "drunkard"; הִתְנוֹדְדָה "rock to and fro"; מְלוּנָה "hut" (a shelter for the night, from לוּן "lodge"; as Isa 1:8). **"and the transgression thereof shall be heavy upon it" → "and its transgression shall be heavy upon it"** — פֶּשַׁע; כָּבַד "be heavy". **"and it shall fall, and not rise again"** — וְלֹא־תֹסִיף קוּם, literally "and it shall not add to rise".
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD shall punish the host of the high ones that are on high, and the kings of the earth upon the earth" → "And it shall be in that day, Yahweh will visit upon the host of the height in the height, and upon the kings of the ground on the ground"** (v21) — Hebrew יִפְקֹד עַל "visit upon" (= bring to account; פָּקַד "visit" for good or ill, also v22); צְבָא הַמָּרוֹם בַּמָּרוֹם "the host of the height in the height" (no "heaven"); הָאֲדָמָה "the ground".
- **"And they shall be gathered together, as prisoners are gathered in the pit" → "And they shall be gathered, a gathering of prisoners, into a pit"** (v22) — Hebrew אֲסֵפָה אַסִּיר, literally "a gathering of a prisoner" (singular, collective); עַל־בּוֹר "into (literally 'upon') a pit", no article. **"and shall be shut up in the prison" → "shut up in a dungeon"** — מַסְגֵּר (from סָגַר "shut"). **"and after many days shall they be visited" → "they shall be visited"** — יִפָּקֵדוּ, nifal of פָּקַד: the word does not say whether for punishment or for release; kept.
- **"Then the moon shall be confounded, and the sun ashamed" → "And the moon shall be abashed, and the sun ashamed"** (v23) — Hebrew וְחָפְרָה הַלְּבָנָה וּבוֹשָׁה הַחַמָּה: the poetic names, "the white one" (moon) and "the hot one" (sun); חָפֵר "be abashed" beside בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed". **"when the LORD of hosts shall reign" → "for Yahweh of Hosts has become king"** — כִּי־מָלַךְ, a perfect ("has become king", or "reigns"). **"and before his ancients gloriously" → "and before his elders is glory"** — וְנֶגֶד זְקֵנָיו כָּבוֹד, no verb ("is" supplied); זְקֵנִים "elders".

### Notes
- **Chapters 24–27** — often called the "Isaiah Apocalypse": judgment on the whole earth and the reign of Yahweh on Zion.
- **v5** — "an everlasting covenant": the same words as Gen 9:16.
