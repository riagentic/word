# Isaiah 34

[1] Come near, nations, to hear,
and folks, give attention!
Let the earth hear, and its fullness,
the world, and all that comes forth from it.

[2] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has wrath against all the nations,
and fury against all their host;
he has devoted them to destruction,
he has given them to the slaughter.

[3] And their slain shall be thrown out,
and the stench of their carcasses shall go up;
and mountains shall melt with their blood.

[4] And all the host of the heavens shall rot away,
and the heavens shall be rolled up like a scroll;
and all their host shall wither,
as the leaf withers from the vine,
and like what withers from the fig tree.

[5] For my sword has drunk its fill in the heavens;
behold, upon Edom it shall come down,
and upon the people devoted by me to destruction, for judgment.

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has a sword; it is full of blood,
it is made fat with fat,
with the blood of lambs and he-goats,
with the fat of the kidneys of rams.
For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has a sacrifice in ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧,
and a great slaughter in the land of Edom.

[7] And wild oxen shall come down with them,
and bulls with mighty ones;
and their land shall drink its fill of blood,
and their dust shall be made fat with fat.

[8] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has a day of vengeance,
a year of recompenses for the case of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[9] And her wadis shall be turned into pitch,
and her dust into sulfur;
and her land shall become burning pitch.

[10] Night and day it shall not be quenched;
forever its smoke shall go up.
From generation to generation it shall lie waste;
forever and ever none shall pass through it.

[11] And the desert owl and the hedgehog shall possess it,
and the great owl and the raven shall abide in it;
and he shall stretch over it the line of waste
and the stones of void.

[12] Her nobles — and no kingship is there — they shall call;
and all her princes shall be nothing.

[13] And thorns shall come up in her citadels,
nettles and thistles in her fortresses;
and she shall be a habitation of jackals,
an abode for the daughters of the ostrich.

[14] And desert dwellers shall meet hyenas,
and a ⟦sair|sa'ir|wild goat⟧ shall call to his fellow;
surely there ⟦lilith|Lilit|the night bird⟧ has come to rest
and has found for herself a resting place.

[15] There the arrow snake has nested and laid,
and has hatched and brooded in her shadow;
surely there the kites have gathered,
each with her mate.

[16] Seek out of the book of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and read:
not one of these is missing;
none has missed her mate.
For my mouth, it has commanded,
and his spirit, it has gathered them.

[17] And he has made the lot fall for them,
and his hand has divided it to them by the line;
forever they shall possess it;
for generation and generation they shall abide in it.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order.
- **"Come near, ye nations, to hear; and hearken, ye people" → "Come near, nations, to hear, and folks, give attention"** (v1) — no "O" (vocative); לְאֻמִּים "folks" (לְאֹם; beside גּוֹיִם "nations"); הַקְשִׁיבוּ "give attention". **"all that is therein … all things that come forth of it" → "its fullness … all that comes forth from it"** — מְלֹאָהּ "its fullness" (what fills it); צֶאֱצָאֶיהָ "what comes forth from it" (its offspring, its produce).
- **"For the indignation of the LORD is upon all nations, and his fury upon all their armies" → "For Yahweh has wrath against all the nations, and fury against all their host"** (v2) — Hebrew קֶצֶף לַיהוָה "wrath to Yahweh" ("has"), חֵמָה "fury" (no "his"); הַגּוֹיִם with the article; צְבָאָם "their host" (singular). **"he hath utterly destroyed them, he hath delivered them" → "he has devoted them to destruction, he has given them"** — הֶחֱרִים (the חָרַם row); נְתָנָם, נָתַן "give".
- **"their stink shall come up out of their carcases" → "the stench of their carcasses shall go up"** (v3) — Hebrew וּפִגְרֵיהֶם יַעֲלֶה בָאְשָׁם, word for word "and their carcasses, their stench shall go up". **"be cast out" → "be thrown out"** — יֻשְׁלָכוּ (הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"). **"the mountains shall be melted with their blood" → "mountains shall melt with their blood"** — נָמַס "melt" (of mountains as of hearts); הָרִים without article.
- **"And all the host of heaven shall be dissolved" → "And all the host of the heavens shall rot away"** (v4) — Hebrew וְנָמַקּוּ (the נָמַק "rot away" row); הַשָּׁמַיִם "the heavens" twice (not "the skies"); the verse as printed in the MT (the Isaiah scroll differs in the first line, from memory: not followed). **"rolled together as a scroll" → "rolled up like a scroll"** — כַסֵּפֶר, סֵפֶר "book" (v16), here the written roll that is rolled up; מְגִלָּה "scroll" does not stand. **"all their host shall fall down, as the leaf falleth off from the vine, and as a falling fig" → "all their host shall wither, as the leaf withers from the vine, and like what withers from the fig tree"** — יִבּוֹל, כִּנְבֹל, נֹבֶלֶת: one verb, נָבֵל "wither" (not "fall"); נֹבֶלֶת "a withered thing" ("fig" is not in the word). Rev 6:13–14 takes up the picture.
- **"my sword shall be bathed in heaven" → "my sword has drunk its fill in the heavens"** (v5) — Hebrew רִוְּתָה, perfect, "has drunk its fill" (as v7). **"Idumea" → "Edom"** (v5, 6) — אֱדוֹם. **"the people of my curse" → "the people devoted by me to destruction"** — עַם חֶרְמִי, lit. "the people of my devoting", the noun חֵרֶם of v2; not קְלָלָה "curse".
- **"The sword of the LORD is filled with blood" → "Yahweh has a sword; it is full of blood"** (v6) — Hebrew חֶרֶב לַיהוָה "a sword to Yahweh" (as v2, 8); מָלְאָה "is full". **"made fat with fatness" → "made fat with fat"** (v6, 7) — הֻדַּשְּׁנָה / יְדֻשָּׁן (the דָּשֵׁן "grow fat" row), חֵלֶב "fat". **"lambs and goats" → "lambs and he-goats"** — כָּרִים, עַתּוּדִים.
- **"And the unicorns shall come down with them, and the bullocks with the bulls" → "And wild oxen shall come down with them, and bulls with mighty ones"** (v7) — Hebrew רְאֵמִים "wild oxen" (not "unicorns": the KJV follows the Greek μονόκερως); פָּרִים "bulls"; אַבִּירִים "mighty ones" (bulls are meant: the row). **"soaked with blood" → "drink its fill of blood"** — רִוְּתָה, as v5. **"their dust"** — עֲפָרָם.
- **"For it is the day of the LORD's vengeance, and the year of recompences for the controversy of Zion" → "For Yahweh has a day of vengeance, a year of recompenses for the case of Zion"** (v8) — Hebrew יוֹם נָקָם לַיהוָה, without articles; שִׁלּוּמִים plural; רִיב "case" (in law).
- **"And the streams thereof … the dust thereof … the land thereof" → "And her wadis … her dust … her land"** (v9) — Hebrew feminine suffixes (the land of Edom); "of Edom" is not in the Hebrew. נַחַל "wadi"; גָּפְרִית "sulfur".
- **"It shall not be quenched night nor day" → "Night and day it shall not be quenched"** (v10) — Hebrew לַיְלָה וְיוֹמָם first. **"it shall lie waste"** — תֶּחֱרָב, חָרֵב "be dry, lie waste" (the row's word of water gone, here of the land). **"for ever and ever"** — לְנֵצַח נְצָחִים, lit. "to perpetuity of perpetuities" (נֶצַח, beside לְעוֹלָם "forever" in the second line).
- **"But the cormorant and the bittern shall possess it; the owl also and the raven shall dwell in it" → "And the desert owl and the hedgehog shall possess it, and the great owl and the raven shall abide in it"** (v11) — Hebrew וִ "And"; קָאַת "desert owl", יַנְשׁוֹף "great owl", עֹרֵב "raven" as the birds of Lev 11 (the identifications cannot be proved); קִפּוֹד "hedgehog" (or "porcupine"; a bird, "bittern", is also proposed: uncertain); יָרַשׁ "possess"; יִשְׁכְּנוּ, שָׁכַן "abide". **"the line of confusion, and the stones of emptiness" → "the line of waste and the stones of void"** — קַו־תֹהוּ וְאַבְנֵי־בֹהוּ: תֹּהוּ and בֹּהוּ are the two words of Gen 1:2 ("waste and void"), rendered as there so that the echo stands; קַו "line", אַבְנֵי "stones" (the weights of a plumb line).
- **"They shall call the nobles thereof to the kingdom, but none shall be there" → "Her nobles — and no kingship is there — they shall call"** (v12) — Hebrew חֹרֶיהָ וְאֵין־שָׁם מְלוּכָה יִקְרָאוּ, word for word; the sense is not certain: "her nobles they shall call (to kingship), and there is none", or "her nobles shall proclaim: No kingship there" (a name), or "her nobles" is the subject of "call". מְלוּכָה "kingship"; אֶפֶס "nothing".
- **"thorns shall come up in her palaces, nettles and brambles in the fortresses thereof" → "thorns shall come up in her citadels, nettles and thistles in her fortresses"** (v13) — Hebrew אַרְמְנֹתֶיהָ (אַרְמוֹן "citadel"), סִירִים "thorns", קִמּוֹשׂ "nettles", חוֹחַ "thistle", מִבְצָר "fortress". **"an habitation of dragons, and a court for owls" → "a habitation of jackals, an abode for the daughters of the ostrich"** — תַּנִּים "jackals" (not תַּנִּין "dragon"); חָצִיר here "abode" (as חָצֵר "court, settlement"; the word is elsewhere "grass", 35:7: uncertain); בְּנוֹת יַעֲנָה "daughters of the ostrich" (as Job 30:29; the bird is not certain).
- **"The wild beasts of the desert shall also meet with the wild beasts of the island" → "And desert dwellers shall meet hyenas"** (v14) — Hebrew צִיִּים "desert dwellers" (from צִיָּה "dry land": beasts here; the Ps 72–74 row), אִיִּים "hyenas" (taken as "howlers", from a root "howl"; the same letters are "coastlands": uncertain, as Isa 13:22). **"the satyr shall cry to his fellow" → "a sa'ir shall call to his fellow"** — שָׂעִיר, "hairy one, male goat"; Lev 17:7 has the same word of beings to whom sacrifice was brought, so "goat-demon" / "satyr" is also read: the plainest sense is the male goat; the token keeps the Hebrew word and the traditional "wild goat". רֵעַ "friend, fellow".
- **"the screech owl also shall rest there, and find for herself a place of rest" → "surely there Lilit has come to rest and has found for herself a resting place"** (v14) — Hebrew אַךְ "surely"; לִילִית, found only here, without article, feminine. The plainest sense: a creature of the night, named as a being; its kind is not known. The word is close to the Akkadian *lilitu*, a female demon of the storm or night (from memory), and later Jewish lore made Lilith a night demon; "screech owl" (KJV) and "night bird" (ESV) take it as a bird. Our token gives "Lilit" (the word) and "the night bird" (traditional); no explanation in the verse. הִרְגִּיעָה, מָצְאָה are perfects ("has come to rest … has found"), as v15, 17. מָנוֹחַ "resting place".
- **"There shall the great owl make her nest, and lay, and hatch, and gather under her shadow" → "There the arrow snake has nested and laid, and has hatched and brooded in her shadow"** (v15) — Hebrew קִפּוֹז, found only here (beside קִפּוֹד of v11): "arrow snake" (from a root "leap, dart"; uncertain: an owl is also read); קִנְּנָה "made a nest"; מִלֵּט "let slip" (= laid; the verb is "rescue" elsewhere); בָּקַע "split" (= hatched); דָּגַר "brood, gather under" (only here and Jer 17:11). No object ("eggs", "her young") is written. Perfects. **"the vultures" → "the kites"** — דַּיּוֹת (the Deut 14:13 row). **"every one with her mate" → "each with her mate"** — אִשָּׁה רְעוּתָהּ, lit. "a woman her fellow".
- **"Seek ye out of the book of the LORD … no one of these shall fail, none shall want her mate" → "Seek out of the book of Yahweh … not one of these is missing; none has missed her mate"** (v16) — Hebrew מֵעַל־סֵפֶר "from upon the book"; נֶעְדָּרָה "is missing" (perfect); אִשָּׁה רְעוּתָהּ לֹא פָקָדוּ, lit. "a woman her fellow they have not missed" (פָּקַד "visit, attend to", here "miss"). **"For the mouth of the LORD" → "For my mouth"** — כִּי־פִי "my mouth" (MT; the Isaiah scroll and others read "his mouth" or "the mouth of Yahweh", from memory): the change of person is kept; הוּא "it" (twice) kept. רוּחוֹ "his spirit" (also "breath").
- **"And he hath cast the lot for them" → "And he has made the lot fall for them"** (v17) — Hebrew הִפִּיל גּוֹרָל, hifil of נָפַל (the row: "cast" is not the Hebrew verb). חִלְּקַתָּה "has divided it" (the חִלֵּק row); בַּקָּו "by the line". **"from generation to generation" → "for generation and generation"** — לְדוֹר וָדוֹר (v10 has מִדּוֹר לָדוֹר "from generation to generation"). **"dwell" → "abide"** — יִשְׁכְּנוּ, as v11.

### Notes
- **v4** — taken up in Rev 6:13–14.
- **v11** — the "waste and void" of Gen 1:2 (Jer 4:23): the land is undone to the state before creation.
- **v14** — **Lilit**: Hebrew לִילִית is found only here; later Jewish folklore developed Lilith as a night-demon.
- **v11–15** — the animals and plants are not all certainly known; see Changes for each.
