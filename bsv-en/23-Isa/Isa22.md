# Isaiah 22

[1] The burden of the valley of vision.

What ails you, then,
that you have gone up, all of you, to the roofs?

[2] Full of shouts,
a city in uproar, a jubilant town!
Your slain are not slain of the sword,
and not dead of war.

[3] All your captains have fled together;
without the bow they have been bound.
All who were found of you have been bound together;
from afar they had fled.

[4] Therefore I said:
"Look away from me;
I will be bitter in weeping;
do not hasten to comfort me
over the havoc of the daughter of my people."

[5] For a day of panic and trampling and confusion
has ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts
in the valley of vision:
a battering down of wall,
and a crying for help to the mountain.

[6] And Elam has lifted up the quiver,
with chariots of men, horsemen,
and ⟦kir|Qir|Kir⟧ has laid bare the shield.

[7] And it came to pass: your choicest valleys were full of chariots,
and the horsemen have surely set themselves toward the gate.

[8] And he uncovered the screen of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and you looked in that day to the weapons of the House of the Forest.

[9] And the breaches of the city of David you saw, that they were many, and you gathered the waters of the lower pool.

[10] And the houses of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ you numbered, and you broke down the houses to fortify the wall.

[11] And a reservoir you made between the two walls for the waters of the old pool. But you did not look to its maker, and the one who formed it from afar you did not see.

[12] And ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts called in that day to weeping and to lamentation and to baldness and to girding with sackcloth.

[13] And behold, exultation and gladness, killing herd and slaughtering flock, eating flesh and drinking wine: "Eating and drinking, for tomorrow we die!"

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has revealed himself in my ears: "This iniquity shall not be atoned for you until you die," says ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts.

[15] Thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts: "Go, come to this steward, to ⟦shebna|Shevna|Shebna⟧, who is over the house:

[16] What have you here, and whom have you here, that you have hewn for yourself here a grave, hewing on the height his grave, carving in the crag a dwelling for himself?

[17] Behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is hurling you away with a hurling, mighty man, and will surely cover you over;

[18] he will surely wind you round with a winding, like a ball, into a land wide on both hands. There you shall die, and there the chariots of your glory, disgrace of the house of your lord!

[19] And I will push you from your standing place, and from your station he will throw you down."

[20] "And it shall be in that day that I will call my servant, ⟦eliakim|Elyaqim|Eliakim⟧ son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧,

[21] and I will clothe him with your tunic, and with your sash I will make him strong, and your dominion I will give into his hand; and he shall be a father to the inhabitant of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and to the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[22] And I will place **the key of the house of David** on his shoulder; and he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open.

[23] And I will thrust him in as a peg in a sure place, and he shall be a throne of glory to the house of his father.

[24] And they shall hang on him all the glory of the house of his father, the offspring and the offshoots, all the small vessels, from the vessels of bowls to all the vessels of jars.

[25] In that day," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, "the peg that was thrust in a sure place shall give way, and it shall be hewn down and fall, and the burden that is on it shall be cut off, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v14, 17, 25) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v14, 25). **"Lord GOD of hosts" → "the Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v5, 12, 14, 15) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת, all three words.
- **Layout** — v1–7 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order (v1 and v5 reordered to the Hebrew lines); v8–25 are prose, as printed.
- **"The burden of the valley of vision"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא, "what is lifted up", a load; usually rendered "oracle". The same word stands for the load on the peg in v25. גֵּיא חִזָּיוֹן "valley of vision" (also v5): the place is not named; Jerusalem is meant (v9–10). גַּיְא "valley" (a narrow one) and עֵמֶק "valley" (v7) are two Hebrew words.
- **"What aileth thee now, that thou art wholly gone up to the housetops?" → "What ails you, then, that you have gone up, all of you, to the roofs?"** (v1) — Hebrew מַה־לָּךְ אֵפוֹא; כֻּלָּךְ "all of you"; the "you" of v1–3 is feminine singular, the city.
- **"Thou that art full of stirs, a tumultuous city, a joyous city" → "Full of shouts, a city in uproar, a jubilant town!"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּשֻׁאוֹת "shouts" (the same word is "crashings" of thunder, Job 36:29); הוֹמִיָּה "in uproar"; קִרְיָה "town" beside עִיר "city"; עַלִּיזָה "jubilant". **"thy slain men are not slain with the sword, nor dead in battle" → "Your slain are not slain of the sword, and not dead of war"** — חַלְלֵי־חֶרֶב, מֵתֵי מִלְחָמָה, genitives.
- **"All thy rulers are fled together, they are bound by the archers" → "All your captains have fled together; without the bow they have been bound"** (v3) — Hebrew קְצִינַיִךְ "your captains"; מִקֶּשֶׁת "from the bow": "without (drawing) the bow" or "by the bowmen"; the Hebrew allows both. **"all that are found in thee are bound together, which have fled from far" → "All who were found of you have been bound together; from afar they had fled"** — מֵרָחוֹק "from afar" (also v11): "they had fled far off" or "they had fled from a distance". נָדַד "flee" and בָּרַח "flee" both stand in the verse; English has one word.
- **"I will weep bitterly, labour not to comfort me" → "I will be bitter in weeping; do not hasten to comfort me"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲמָרֵר בַּבֶּכִי; אַל־תָּאִיצוּ "do not press, do not hasten". **"because of the spoiling of the daughter of my people" → "over the havoc of the daughter of my people"** — שֹׁד "havoc" (as Prov 21:7); עַל "over".
- **"For it is a day of trouble, and of treading down, and of perplexity by the Lord GOD of hosts" → "For a day of panic and trampling and confusion has the Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v5) — Hebrew יוֹם … לַאדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת, literally "a day … is to Adonai Yahweh of Hosts": the day belongs to him; the Hebrew order kept. מְהוּמָה "panic" ("tumult" is kept for שָׁאוֹן, 24:8), מְבוּסָה "trampling", מְבוּכָה "confusion": three words that sound alike. **"breaking down the walls, and of crying to the mountains" → "a battering down of wall, and a crying for help to the mountain"** — מְקַרְקַר קִר, a word play (uncertain: "battering down of wall", or "shouting a shout"); קִר and הָהָר are singular; שׁוֹעַ "a crying for help".
- **"And Elam bare the quiver with chariots of men and horsemen" → "And Elam has lifted up the quiver, with chariots of men, horsemen"** (v6) — Hebrew נָשָׂא "lift up, carry"; רֶכֶב אָדָם "chariots of men" (אָדָם "man, mankind"; 21:9 has אִישׁ); "and" before "horsemen" is not written. **"and Kir uncovered the shield" → "and Kir has laid bare the shield"** — עֵרָה.
- **"And it shall come to pass, that thy choicest valleys shall be full of chariots" → "And it came to pass: your choicest valleys were full of chariots"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיְהִי, a past form; מִבְחַר־עֲמָקַיִךְ "the choicest of your valleys". **"the horsemen shall set themselves in array at the gate" → "the horsemen have surely set themselves toward the gate"** — שֹׁת שָׁתוּ, literally "setting they have set": "surely"; הַשָּׁעְרָה "toward the gate".
- **"And he discovered the covering of Judah" → "And he uncovered the screen of Judah"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיְגַל, a past form; מָסָךְ "screen" (as the screen of the tent); no subject named. **"and thou didst look in that day to the armour of the house of the forest" → "and you looked in that day to the weapons of the House of the Forest"** — וַתַּבֵּט, masculine singular "you" (or "she looked"); נֶשֶׁק "weapons"; the House of the Forest (of Lebanon), 1 Kgs 7:2; 10:17.
- **"Ye have seen also the breaches" → "And the breaches … you saw"** (v9–11) — each verse begins with וְ and puts its object first: kept. **"ye numbered" → "you numbered"** (v10) — סְפַרְתֶּם (סָפַר "number"); נָתַץ "break down". **"Ye made also a ditch" → "And a reservoir you made"** (v11) — מִקְוָה, a place where water is gathered. **"but ye have not looked unto the maker thereof, neither had respect unto him that fashioned it long ago" → "But you did not look to its maker, and the one who formed it from afar you did not see"** — עֹשֶׂיהָ, a plural form ("its makers") used of one; יֹצְרָהּ "the one who formed it" (יָצַר "form"); מֵרָחוֹק "from afar", of distance or of time; רָאָה "see".
- **"And in that day did the Lord GOD of hosts call to weeping, and to mourning, and to baldness, and to girding with sackcloth" → "And the Lord Yahweh of Hosts called in that day to weeping and to lamentation and to baldness and to girding with sackcloth"** (v12) — וַיִּקְרָא; מִסְפֵּד "lamentation" (סָפַד "lament"); קָרְחָה "baldness"; each לְ "to" kept.
- **"joy and gladness" → "exultation and gladness"** (v13) — Hebrew שָׂשׂוֹן וְשִׂמְחָה. **"slaying oxen, and killing sheep, eating flesh, and drinking wine" → "killing herd and slaughtering flock, eating flesh and drinking wine"** — four infinitives absolute; בָּקָר "herd", צֹאן "flock" (collectives), שָׁחַט "slaughter". **"let us eat and drink; for to morrow we shall die" → "Eating and drinking, for tomorrow we die!"** — אָכוֹל וְשָׁתוֹ, two more infinitives absolute: they name the acts without a person; "let us eat" is the sense of the Greek of 1 Cor 15:32 (φάγωμεν καὶ πίωμεν, from the Septuagint, from memory). The Hebrew is translated as it stands; the difference stays here.
- **"And it was revealed in mine ears by the LORD of hosts" → "And Yahweh of Hosts has revealed himself in my ears"** (v14) — Hebrew וְנִגְלָה בְאָזְנָי יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, nifal "be revealed, reveal oneself", Yahweh the subject (or "it was revealed in my ears: Yahweh of Hosts"). **"Surely this iniquity shall not be purged from you till ye die" → "This iniquity shall not be atoned for you until you die"** — the oath formula אִם־יְכֻפַּר, literally "if this iniquity is atoned for you": a sworn "it shall not"; כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (pual). **"saith the Lord GOD of hosts" → "says the Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** — אָמַר.
- **"Go, get thee unto this treasurer, even unto Shebna, which is over the house, and say" → "Go, come to this steward, to Shebna, who is over the house:"** (v15) — Hebrew לֶךְ־בֹּא, two imperatives; סֹכֵן "steward"; עַל־שֶׁבְנָא "to Shebna" (עַל also "against"). "and say" is not in the Hebrew (KJV italics); the words of v16–19 follow without it.
- **"What hast thou here? and whom hast thou here, that thou hast hewed thee out a sepulchre here, as he that heweth him out a sepulchre on high, and that graveth an habitation for himself in a rock?" → "What have you here, and whom have you here, that you have hewn for yourself here a grave, hewing on the height his grave, carving in the crag a dwelling for himself?"** (v16) — Hebrew פֹּה "here" three times; the second half turns to the third person, חֹצְבִי … קִבְרוֹ "hewing … his grave", לוֹ "for himself": kept. מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling"; סֶלַע "crag".
- **"Behold, the LORD will carry thee away with a mighty captivity" → "Behold, Yahweh is hurling you away with a hurling, mighty man"** (v17) — Hebrew מְטַלְטֶלְךָ טַלְטֵלָה גָּבֶר: a participle, "is hurling", with its noun; גֶּבֶר "mighty man", here as a call to Shebna (or "with the hurling of a mighty man"). **"and will surely cover thee" → "and will surely cover you over"** — וְעֹטְךָ עָטֹה, participle and infinitive absolute of עָטָה "cover, wrap" (or "grasp": uncertain).
- **"He will surely violently turn and toss thee like a ball into a large country" → "he will surely wind you round with a winding, like a ball, into a land wide on both hands"** (v18) — Hebrew צָנוֹף יִצְנָפְךָ צְנֵפָה, infinitive absolute, verb and noun of צָנַף "wrap, wind round"; רַחֲבַת יָדָיִם "wide of hands" (= spacious on both sides). **"and there the chariots of thy glory shall be the shame of thy lord's house" → "and there the chariots of your glory, disgrace of the house of your lord!"** — no verb in the Hebrew; קְלוֹן "disgrace", said to Shebna; אֲדֹנֶיךָ "your lord" (a plural form of one).
- **"And I will drive thee from thy station, and from thy state shall he pull thee down" → "And I will push you from your standing place, and from your station he will throw you down"** (v19) — הָדַף "push"; מַצָּב "standing place", מַעֲמָד "station"; הָרַס "throw down"; the change from "I" to "he" is the Hebrew's.
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day, that I will call my servant Eliakim" → "And it shall be in that day that I will call my servant, Eliakim"** (v20) — וְהָיָה … וְקָרָאתִי; עַבְדִּי "my servant" (of a king's man or of Yahweh's).
- **"thy robe … thy girdle" → "your tunic … your sash"** (v21) — Hebrew כֻּתָּנְתֶּךָ, אַבְנֵטְךָ. **"and strengthen him with thy girdle" → "and with your sash I will make him strong"** — אֲחַזְּקֶנּוּ (piel of חָזַק "make strong"; = bind it firmly on him). **"I will commit thy government into his hand" → "your dominion I will give into his hand"** — מֶמְשַׁלְתְּךָ. **"to the inhabitants" → "to the inhabitant"** — יוֹשֵׁב, singular (collective).
- **"And the key of the house of David will I lay upon his shoulder" → "And I will place the key of the house of David on his shoulder"** (v22) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי ("give, place"); שִׁכְמוֹ "his shoulder". **"so he shall open, and none shall shut"** — וְאֵין סֹגֵר, literally "and there is none shutting". The words are taken up in Rev 3:7 of the one "who has the key of David" (Greek, from memory: a shorter form); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"And I will fasten him as a nail in a sure place" → "And I will thrust him in as a peg in a sure place"** (v23, 25) — Hebrew תָּקַע "thrust, drive in"; יָתֵד "peg" (as of the tent); נֶאֱמָן "sure". **"a glorious throne" → "a throne of glory"** — כִּסֵּא כָבוֹד; כָּבוֹד "glory" also v18, 24.
- **"the offspring and the issue, all vessels of small quantity, from the vessels of cups, even to all the vessels of flagons" → "the offspring and the offshoots, all the small vessels, from the vessels of bowls to all the vessels of jars"** (v24) — Hebrew הַצֶּאֱצָאִים "what comes out, offspring"; הַצְּפִעוֹת only here, uncertain ("offshoots", "leaves"); כְּלֵי הַקָּטָן, literally "vessels of the small"; אַגָּנוֹת "bowls"; נְבָלִים "jars" (as 1 Sam 1:24).
- **"In that day, saith the LORD of hosts" → "In that day, declares Yahweh of Hosts"** (v25) — Hebrew נְאֻם יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"shall the nail … be removed" → "the peg … shall give way"** — תָּמוּשׁ "shall depart, give way". **"be cut down, and fall" → "be hewn down and fall"** — נִגְדְּעָה. **"the burden that was upon it" → "the burden that is on it"** — הַמַּשָּׂא, the word of the headings (v1).

### Notes
- **v22** — "the key of the house of David" quoted in Rev 3:7 (applied to Yeshua); bold kept.
- **v13** — quoted in 1 Cor 15:32 ("Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die").
