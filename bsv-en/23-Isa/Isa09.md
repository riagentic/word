# Isaiah 9

[1] For there is no gloom for her who was in straits. At the former time he made light of the land of ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧ and the land of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, and at the latter he made heavy the way of the sea, across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ of the nations.

[2] The people who walk in darkness
have seen a great light;
those who dwell in a land of the shadow of death,
light has shone on them.

[3] You have multiplied the nation,
for it you have made the gladness great;
they have rejoiced before you as the gladness at the harvest,
as they are glad when they divide spoil.

[4] For the yoke of his burden,
and the staff of his shoulder,
the rod of the taskmaster over him,
you have shattered as on the day of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧.

[5] For every boot of the one who tramps in quaking,
and a cloak rolled in blood,
and it shall be for burning, fuel for fire.

[6] For a child has been born to us,
a son has been given to us,
and the rule has been on his shoulder;
and he has called his name
Wonder, Counselor,
Mighty ⟦el|El|God⟧,
Father of Perpetuity,
Prince of ⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧.

[7] Of the increase of the rule and of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ there is no end,
on the throne of David and over his kingdom,
to establish it and to uphold it
with judgment and with righteousness,
from now and forever.
The zeal of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts will do this.

[8] A word ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ has sent against ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and it shall fall on ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[9] and the people shall know, all of it,
⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and the inhabitant of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧,
in pride and in greatness of heart, saying:

[10] "Bricks have fallen,
but with hewn stone we will build;
sycamores have been hewn down,
but cedars we will put in exchange."

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ set on high the adversaries of ⟦rezin|Retzin|Rezin⟧ against him,
and his enemies he spurs on;

[12] ⟦aram|Aram|The Syrians⟧ from the east and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ from the west,
and they devoured ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ with the whole mouth.
For all this his anger has not turned back,
and still his hand is stretched out.

[13] And the people have not turned back to him who struck it,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts they have not sought.

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut off from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ head and tail,
palm branch and rush, in one day —

[15] the elder and the one whose face is lifted up, he is the head,
and the prophet who teaches falsehood, he is the tail.

[16] And those who lead this people straight have become those who make it wander,
and those of it who are led straight are swallowed up.

[17] Therefore over its young men ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ does not rejoice,
and on its fatherless and its widows he has no compassion;
for all of it is godless and an evildoer,
and every mouth speaks outrage.
For all this his anger has not turned back,
and still his hand is stretched out.

[18] For wickedness has burned like fire;
briers and thorns it devours;
and it kindled in the thickets of the forest,
and they rolled upward, an exaltation of smoke.

[19] Through the raging of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts
the land is scorched,
and the people became like fuel for fire;
each man on his brother they have no pity.

[20] And he cut on the right and was hungry,
and he ate on the left and they were not sated;
each man the flesh of his arm they eat:

[21] ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧;
together they are against ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.
For all this his anger has not turned back,
and still his hand is stretched out.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v7, 13, 19) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **Layout** (v2–21) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v3 and v7 now follow the accents (in v3 "they have rejoiced … at the harvest" is one line, in v7 "Of the increase … no end" one line); v1 stays prose.
- **"Nevertheless the dimness shall not be such as was in her vexation" → "For there is no gloom for her who was in straits"** (v1) — Hebrew כִּי לֹא מוּעָף לַאֲשֶׁר מוּצָק לָהּ: מוּעָף "gloom" (only here; the מְעוּף of 8:22), מוּצָק "straits" (root צוּק, as מְצוּקָה "straits", Ps 107:6; צָרָה "distress" is another word). The verse joins 8:22.
- **"when at the first he lightly afflicted … and afterward did more grievously afflict" → "At the former time he made light of … and at the latter he made heavy"** (v1) — Hebrew כָּעֵת הָרִאשׁוֹן הֵקַל … וְהָאַחֲרוֹן הִכְבִּיד, literally "as the former time he made light … and the latter he made heavy". הֵקַל is "treat as light, bring into contempt"; הִכְבִּיד "make heavy" (root of כָּבוֹד "glory"). The pair is kept: "made heavy" can mean "honored, made glorious" (so the modern versions, and Matt 4:15–16 reads the verse of light) or "made the burden heavy" (so KJV); the Hebrew leaves it open. Both verbs are perfects. **"the way of the sea, beyond Jordan"** — עֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן "across the Jordan"; גְּלִיל הַגּוֹיִם "the district of the nations" (the `galilee` token).
- **"The people that walked in darkness"** (v2) — Hebrew הַהֹלְכִים, a participle: "who walk". **"in the land of the shadow of death"** kept — צַלְמָוֶת as pointed, צֵל "shadow" + מָוֶת "death" (as Job 3:5; Ps 23:4); the reading צַלְמוּת "deep darkness" is the other view. No article on "land". **"upon them hath the light shined" → "light has shone on them"** — no article on אוֹר.
- **"and not increased the joy" → "for it you have made the gladness great"** (v3) — the qere לוֹ "to it, for it"; the written text (ketiv) has לֹא "not" ("you have not made the gladness great"), which does not fit the rest of the verse. שִׂמְחָה "gladness" both times; שָׂמְחוּ "they have rejoiced" (perfect); יָגִילוּ "they are glad" (גִּיל). "Men" is not written: "as they are glad".
- **"the staff of his shoulder, the rod of his oppressor"** (v4) — Hebrew מַטֵּה שִׁכְמוֹ (genitive kept, "of his shoulder"), שֵׁבֶט הַנֹּגֵשׂ בּוֹ "the rod of the taskmaster over him" (נֹגֵשׂ "taskmaster" as Exod 5; בּוֹ "against / in him"). **"thou hast broken" → "you have shattered"** — הַחִתֹּתָ, hifil of חָתַת "be shattered".
- **"For every battle of the warrior is with confused noise, and garments rolled in blood; but this shall be with burning and fuel of fire" → "For every boot of the one who tramps in quaking, and a cloak rolled in blood, and it shall be for burning, fuel for fire"** (v5) — Hebrew סְאוֹן "boot" and סֹאֵן "one who tramps (in boots)" are found only here (the sense is taken from Aramaic and Akkadian; uncertain); רַעַשׁ "quaking" (of the earth or the din of battle). שִׂמְלָה "cloak", without article; דָּמִים "blood" is plural. וְהָיְתָה לִשְׂרֵפָה "and it shall be for burning"; מַאֲכֹלֶת אֵשׁ literally "food of fire" (also v19).
- **"For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called" → "For a child has been born to us, a son has been given to us, and the rule has been on his shoulder; and he has called his name"** (v6) — Hebrew יֻלַּד, נִתַּן (perfects), וַתְּהִי, וַיִּקְרָא (past narrative forms). A perfect in prophecy can speak of what is to come as done; the tenses are kept. וַיִּקְרָא שְׁמוֹ is active with no named subject: "he called his name" (or "one called"; the versions and KJV make it passive). מִשְׂרָה "rule" (only here and v7; from the root of שַׂר "prince").
- **"Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace" → "Wonder, Counselor, Mighty El, Father of Perpetuity, Prince of Shalom"** (v6) — Hebrew פֶּלֶא יוֹעֵץ אֵל גִּבּוֹר אֲבִי עַד שַׂר־שָׁלוֹם, word for word: פֶּלֶא "wonder" (a noun, not "wonderful"; as Exod 15:11); יוֹעֵץ "counselor" (participle of יָעַץ); the accents divide the two words, which can also be read as one title, "a wonder of a counselor"; אֵל גִּבּוֹר "Mighty El" (אֵל, not אֱלֹהִים; גִּבּוֹר "mighty", as 10:21); אֲבִי עַד "father of perpetuity" (עַד "perpetuity", as לָעַד "perpetually"; "everlasting" or "eternity" in the versions); שַׂר־שָׁלוֹם "prince of shalom". The Hebrew has no articles and no verb between the words. Whether all the words are the name given to the child, or the first words name the one who gives the name ("and Wonder-Counselor, Mighty El, Father of Perpetuity called his name Prince of Shalom", a Jewish reading), the Hebrew does not decide: the verse keeps the words in their order and adds nothing.
- **"Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end" → "Of the increase of the rule and of shalom there is no end"** (v7) — qere לְמַרְבֵּה; the written text has a closed (final) mem inside the word (לםרבה), a scribal oddity with no other meaning. הַמִּשְׂרָה "the rule" (no "his"); וּלְשָׁלוֹם "and of shalom"; no verb ("there is"). **"to order it, and to establish it with judgment and with justice" → "to establish it and to uphold it with judgment and with righteousness"** — לְהָכִין "establish" (כּוּן, as 2 Sam 7), לְסַעֲדָהּ "uphold"; "it" is the kingdom (feminine). **"from henceforth even for ever" → "from now and forever"** — מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם. **"The zeal of the LORD of hosts will perform this"** — קִנְאַת, as 2 Kgs 19:31.
- **"The Lord sent a word into Jacob, and it hath lighted upon Israel" → "A word Adonai has sent against Jacob, and it shall fall on Israel"** (v8) — Hebrew order kept (the word first); בְּ before both names, literally "in"; וְנָפַל and וְיָדְעוּ (v9) are forms of the future. אֲדֹנָי (v8, 17).
- **"And all the people shall know" → "and the people shall know, all of it"** (v9) — הָעָם כֻּלּוֹ; יוֹשֵׁב "the inhabitant" (singular); בְּגֹדֶל לֵבָב "in greatness of heart"; לֵאמֹר "saying".
- **"The bricks are fallen down, but we will build with hewn stones: the sycomores are cut down, but we will change them into cedars" → "Bricks have fallen, but with hewn stone we will build; sycamores have been hewn down, but cedars we will put in exchange"** (v10) — no articles; גָּזִית "hewn stone" (singular); גֻּדָּעוּ "hewn down" (as Deut 7:5); נַחֲלִיף "we will change, put in exchange" (hifil of חָלַף).
- **"Therefore the LORD shall set up the adversaries of Rezin against him, and join his enemies together" → "And Yahweh set on high the adversaries of Rezin against him, and his enemies he spurs on"** (v11) — וַיְשַׂגֵּב is a past narrative form (also v12, 14, 18, 19, 20); שִׂגֵּב "set on high" (here: raise up strong). "Therefore" is not in the Hebrew. יְסַכְסֵךְ "spur on, stir up" (only here and 19:2). Many read "his adversaries" (צָרָיו) for "the adversaries of Rezin" (a conjecture); the MT is kept.
- **"The Syrians before, and the Philistines behind; and they shall devour Israel with open mouth" → "Aram from the east and the Philistines from the west, and they devoured Israel with the whole mouth"** (v12) — מִקֶּדֶם "from the front / east", מֵאָחוֹר "from behind" = west (for one who faces the sunrise). בְּכָל־פֶּה literally "with all the mouth".
- **"For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still" → "For all this his anger has not turned back, and still his hand is stretched out"** (v12, 17, 21; 10:4) — Hebrew בְּכָל־זֹאת לֹא־שָׁב אַפּוֹ וְעוֹד יָדוֹ נְטוּיָה: בְּ "in all this" (= despite); שׁוּב "turn back" (also v13); אַף "anger" (literally "nose"); וְ "and", not "but".
- **"For the people turneth not unto him that smiteth them" → "And the people have not turned back to him who struck it"** (v13) — וְהָעָם לֹא־שָׁב עַד־הַמַּכֵּהוּ, perfect; הִכָּה "strike"; "it" is the people (singular suffix). **"neither do they seek"** — דָּרַשׁ "seek".
- **"branch and rush" → "palm branch and rush"** (v14) — כִּפָּה "palm branch" (as Job 15:32), אַגְמוֹן "rush" (as Job 41:2). **"the ancient and honourable" → "the elder and the one whose face is lifted up"** (v15) — זָקֵן "elder"; נְשׂוּא־פָנִים "lifted up of face" = one held in regard (the נָשָׂא פָּנִים row). **"lies" → "falsehood"** — שֶׁקֶר. The pronoun הוּא "he" is kept.
- **"For the leaders of this people cause them to err" → "And those who lead this people straight have become those who make it wander"** (v16) — וַיִּהְיוּ "and they have become"; מְאַשְּׁרִים "those who lead straight" (piel of אשר, as 3:12; the same letters as אִשֵּׁר "call happy", another sense); מַתְעִים "those who make (it) wander" (הִתְעָה, as 2 Kgs 21:9): "it" is supplied for the people. וּמְאֻשָּׁרָיו "and those of it who are led straight" (pual) — **"are destroyed" → "are swallowed up"** (מְבֻלָּעִים, בָּלַע).
- **"the Lord shall have no joy in their young men, neither shall have mercy on their fatherless and widows" → "over its young men Adonai does not rejoice, and on its fatherless and its widows he has no compassion"** (v17) — the suffixes are singular ("its": the people); each noun has its own "its". רִחַם "have compassion". **"every one is an hypocrite and an evildoer" → "all of it is godless and an evildoer"** — כֻּלּוֹ "all of it"; חָנֵף "godless" (literally "polluted, profane"), מֵרַע "an evildoer". **"folly" → "outrage"** — נְבָלָה, the deed of the נָבָל (as Gen 34:7).
- **"wickedness burneth as the fire: it shall devour the briers and thorns, and shall kindle in the thickets of the forest, and they shall mount up like the lifting up of smoke" → "wickedness has burned like fire; briers and thorns it devours; and it kindled in the thickets of the forest, and they rolled upward, an exaltation of smoke"** (v18) — רִשְׁעָה "wickedness"; שָׁמִיר וָשַׁיִת "briers and thorns" (the pair of 5:6; 7:23–25; 10:17; the kinds of plant are not certain). וַיִּתְאַבְּכוּ "they rolled upward" (only here; uncertain). גֵּאוּת "exaltation" (12:5): a towering of smoke; no "like".
- **"Through the wrath of the LORD of hosts is the land darkened" → "Through the raging of Yahweh of Hosts the land is scorched"** (v19) — עֶבְרָה "raging" (beside אַף "anger"); נֶעְתַּם only here, uncertain ("scorched" from the context and the Greek, cited from memory; KJV "darkened"). **"the people shall be as the fuel of the fire" → "the people became like fuel for fire"** — וַיְהִי. **"no man shall spare his brother" → "each man on his brother they have no pity"** — אִישׁ אֶל־אָחִיו לֹא יַחְמֹלוּ, Hebrew order; חָמַל "have pity".
- **"And he shall snatch on the right hand, and be hungry; and he shall eat on the left hand, and they shall not be satisfied" → "And he cut on the right and was hungry, and he ate on the left and they were not sated"** (v20) — וַיִּגְזֹר "and he cut" (גָּזַר, as 1 Kgs 3:25); the subject is not named, and the verbs pass from "he" to "they" as in the Hebrew. שָׂבַע "be sated". **"every man the flesh of his own arm"** — "own" is not written. Some read זַרְעוֹ "his seed" for זְרֹעוֹ "his arm" (a change of pointing); the MT is kept.
- **"Manasseh, Ephraim; and Ephraim, Manasseh" kept** (v21) — the Hebrew has no verb; "devours" of the earlier text is removed (the verb of v20 is understood).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — our 9:1 = Heb 8:23; our 9:2–21 = Heb 9:1–20.
- **vv1–2** — quoted in Matt 4:15–16 (Galilee, the light) from a Greek form of the text.
- **v6–7** — read of the coming king of David's line; in Christian tradition read of the Mashiach. The names are given above word for word.
- **"his hand is stretched out still"** — refrain (9:12, 17, 21; 10:4) binding four stanzas of judgment.
