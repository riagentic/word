# Isaiah 58

[1] "Call with the throat; do not withhold;
like the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ raise your voice;
and declare to my people their transgression,
and to the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ their sins.

[2] And me they seek day by day,
and the knowledge of my ways they desire,
like a nation that has done righteousness
and has not forsaken the judgment of its ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
they ask of me judgments of righteousness;
the nearness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ they desire.

[3] 'Why have we fasted, and you have not seen?
We have afflicted our soul, and you do not know?'
Behold, in the day of your fast you find pleasure,
and all your toilers you press.

[4] Behold, for contention and struggle you fast,
and to strike with a fist of wickedness.
You do not fast as today
to make your voice heard on high.

[5] Shall such be the fast that I choose,
a day for a man to afflict his soul?
Is it to bow down his head like a rush,
and to spread out sackcloth and ashes?
Will you call this a fast,
and a day of good pleasure to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?

[6] Is not this the fast that I choose:
to unbind the bonds of wickedness,
to loose the bands of the yoke-bar,
and to let the crushed go free,
and that you snap every yoke-bar?

[7] Is it not to break your bread for the hungry,
and that you bring the afflicted wanderers into a house;
when you see the naked, that you cover him,
and from your flesh you do not hide yourself?

[8] Then your light shall break open like the dawn,
and your healing shall spring up quickly;
and your righteousness shall go before you;
the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall gather you.

[9] Then you shall call, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will answer;
you shall cry for help, and he will say, 'Here I am.'
If you remove from your midst the yoke-bar,
the stretching out of the finger, and the speaking of wickedness,

[10] and you draw out your soul to the hungry
and satisfy the afflicted soul,
then your light shall rise in the darkness,
and your gloom shall be as the noonday.

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will lead you continually
and satisfy your soul in parched places
and make your bones strong;
and you shall be like a watered garden,
and like an outlet of water,
whose waters do not lie.

[12] And those from you shall build the ruins of old;
the foundations of generation and generation you shall raise up;
and you shall be called the one who walls up the breach,
the one who restores tracks for dwelling.

[13] If you turn back your foot from ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|the Sabbath⟧,
from doing your pleasures on my holy day,
and call ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|the Sabbath⟧ a delight,
the holy of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ honored,
and honor it, from doing your ways,
from finding your pleasure, and speaking a word,

[14] then you shall delight yourself in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and I will make you ride on the high places of the earth,
and I will make you eat the inheritance of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ your father,
for the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved; "the judgment of its *Elohim*", "the nearness of *Elohim*".
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the existing lines stand.
- **"Cry aloud, spare not" → "Call with the throat; do not withhold"** (v1) — Hebrew קְרָא בְגָרוֹן אַל־תַּחְשֹׂךְ: קָרָא "call", בְגָרוֹן "with the throat" (= with full voice); חָשַׂךְ "withhold". **"lift up thy voice like a trumpet" → "like the shofar raise your voice"** — כַּשּׁוֹפָר (token), with the article; הָרֵם, הֵרִים "raise"; Hebrew order. **"shew" → "declare"** — הַגֵּד; the verse has "and … and" (וְ … וּ).
- **"Yet they seek me daily, and delight to know my ways" → "And me they seek day by day, and the knowledge of my ways they desire"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאוֹתִי יוֹם יוֹם יִדְרֹשׁוּן וְדַעַת דְּרָכַי יֶחְפָּצוּן: the objects stand first; יוֹם יוֹם "day by day"; דַּעַת is a noun, "knowledge"; חָפֵץ with a direct object "desire" (as 1 Sam 2:25), kept apart from חֵפֶץ "pleasure" (v3, 13) and עֹנֶג "delight" (v13). **"as a nation that did righteousness, and forsook not the ordinance of their God" → "like a nation that has done righteousness and has not forsaken the judgment of its Elohim"** — the verbs are singular, of the nation (אֱלֹהָיו "its Elohim"); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment". **"the ordinances of justice" → "judgments of righteousness"** — מִשְׁפְּטֵי־צֶדֶק. **"they take delight in approaching to God" → "the nearness of Elohim they desire"** — קִרְבַת אֱלֹהִים, a noun, "nearness".
- **"Wherefore have we fasted, say they, and thou seest not?" → "'Why have we fasted, and you have not seen?"** (v3) — Hebrew לָמָּה צַּמְנוּ וְלֹא רָאִיתָ: "say they" is supplied by the KJV; רָאִיתָ perfect, no "it". **"wherefore have we afflicted our soul, and thou takest no knowledge?" → "We have afflicted our soul, and you do not know?"** — עִנִּינוּ נַפְשֵׁנוּ, the Lev 16:29 words: עִנָּה נֶפֶשׁ "afflict one's soul" (= fast); the Hebrew has no second "why": the one לָמָּה governs both lines, so the question is kept without the word. The fasting verb צוּם and noun צוֹם (v3–6) are "fast". **"ye find pleasure, and exact all your labours" → "you find pleasure, and all your toilers you press"** — תִּמְצְאוּ־חֵפֶץ; עַצְּבֵיכֶם, from עָצַב "toil, pain": "your toilers" (or "your toils"; uncertain); נָגַשׂ "press" (verb of נֹגֵשׂ "taskmaster").
- **"ye fast for strife and debate, and to smite with the fist of wickedness" → "for contention and struggle you fast, and to strike with a fist of wickedness"** (v4) — Hebrew לְרִיב וּמַצָּה תָּצוּמוּ: רִיב "contention", מַצָּה "struggle"; הִכָּה "strike"; Hebrew order; "only" was supplied. **"ye shall not fast as ye do this day, to make your voice to be heard on high" → "You do not fast as today to make your voice heard on high"** — לֹא־תָצוּמוּ כַיּוֹם לְהַשְׁמִיעַ בַּמָּרוֹם קוֹלְכֶם.
- **"Is it such a fast that I have chosen?" → "Shall such be the fast that I choose"** (v5) — Hebrew הֲכָזֶה יִהְיֶה צוֹם אֶבְחָרֵהוּ, imperfects. **"a day for a man to afflict his soul"** — יוֹם עַנּוֹת אָדָם נַפְשׁוֹ, as v3. **"to bow down his head as a bulrush, and to spread sackcloth and ashes under him" → "to bow down his head like a rush, and to spread out sackcloth and ashes"** — לָכֹף כְּאַגְמֹן רֹאשׁוֹ (אַגְמֹן "rush", from אֲגַם "reedy pool"); וְשַׂק וָאֵפֶר יַצִּיעַ, hifil of יָצַע "spread out (as a bed)": "under him" is not in the Hebrew. **"an acceptable day to the LORD" → "a day of good pleasure to Yahweh"** — יוֹם רָצוֹן, as Ps 69:13 "a time of good pleasure".
- **"to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens" → "to unbind the bonds of wickedness, to loose the bands of the yoke-bar"** (v6) — Hebrew פַּתֵּחַ חַרְצֻבּוֹת רֶשַׁע הַתֵּר אֲגֻדּוֹת מוֹטָה: פִּתַּח "unbind" (literally "open") and הִתִּיר "loose" kept apart (as Ps 105:20); חַרְצֻבּוֹת "bonds" (as Ps 73:4); אֲגֻדּוֹת "bands"; מוֹטָה is the bar of a yoke (Lev 26:13 "the bars of your yoke"): the one word "yoke-bar" in v6 twice and v9. **"to let the oppressed go free" → "to let the crushed go free"** — שַׁלַּח רְצוּצִים חָפְשִׁים: שִׁלַּח "let go" (of one who is held), רְצוּצִים "crushed". **"and that ye break every yoke" → "and that you snap every yoke-bar"** — וְכָל־מוֹטָה תְּנַתֵּקוּ, a finite verb (plural "you"), נִתֵּק "tear apart, snap".
- **"to deal thy bread to the hungry" → "to break your bread for the hungry"** (v7) — Hebrew פָרֹס, פָּרַס "break (bread)". **"and that thou bring the poor that are cast out to thy house" → "and that you bring the afflicted wanderers into a house"** — וַעֲנִיִּים מְרוּדִים תָּבִיא בָיִת: עָנִי "afflicted"; מְרוּדִים from רוּד "roam" ("homeless" in sense); בַיִת "a house", no "your". **"and that thou hide not thyself from thine own flesh" → "and from your flesh you do not hide yourself"** — וּמִבְּשָׂרְךָ לֹא תִתְעַלָּם; "own" is supplied in the KJV.
- **"Then shall thy light break forth as the morning" → "Then your light shall break open like the dawn"** (v8) — Hebrew יִבָּקַע, nifal of בָּקַע "split, break open"; שַׁחַר "dawn". **"thine health shall spring forth speedily" → "your healing shall spring up quickly"** — אֲרֻכָתְךָ, the new flesh that grows over a wound; צָמַח "spring up"; מְהֵרָה "quickly". **"the glory of the LORD shall be thy rereward" → "the glory of Yahweh shall gather you"** — יַאַסְפֶךָ, אָסַף "gather": of a rear guard that gathers up those who come behind (Num 10:25 מְאַסֵּף); "rear guard" is the sense, not the word.
- **"thou shalt cry" → "you shall cry for help"** (v9) — Hebrew תְּשַׁוַּע. **"Here I am"** — הִנֵּנִי, literally "behold me". **"the putting forth of the finger, and speaking vanity" → "the stretching out of the finger, and the speaking of wickedness"** — שְׁלַח אֶצְבַּע וְדַבֶּר־אָוֶן, two infinitives; אָוֶן "wickedness".
- **"And if thou draw out thy soul to the hungry, and satisfy the afflicted soul" → "and you draw out your soul to the hungry and satisfy the afflicted soul"** (v10) — Hebrew וְתָפֵק לָרָעֵב נַפְשֶׁךָ וְנֶפֶשׁ נַעֲנָה תַּשְׂבִּיעַ: הֵפִיק "draw out, furnish"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" twice (the earlier "pour yourself out … the desire of the afflicted" hid the word); נַעֲנָה nifal of עָנָה "afflict". The "if" of v9 runs on; "if" is not repeated. **"thy light rise in obscurity, and thy darkness be as the noonday" → "your light shall rise in the darkness, and your gloom shall be as the noonday"** — וְזָרַח בַּחֹשֶׁךְ … וַאֲפֵלָתְךָ: חֹשֶׁךְ "darkness", אֲפֵלָה "gloom".
- **"shall guide thee continually" → "will lead you continually"** (v11) — Hebrew וְנָחֲךָ, נָחָה "lead"; תָּמִיד "continually". **"satisfy thy soul in drought" → "satisfy your soul in parched places"** — בְּצַחְצָחוֹת, only here: "parched places" (uncertain). **"make fat thy bones" → "make your bones strong"** — עַצְמֹתֶיךָ יַחֲלִיץ, hifil of חָלַץ (of uncertain sense here: "make strong, equip", or "deliver"). **"like a spring of water, whose waters fail not" → "like an outlet of water, whose waters do not lie"** — וּכְמוֹצָא מַיִם, literally "a going out of water"; אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יְכַזְּבוּ מֵימָיו, כִּזֵּב "lie" (= fail; the verb of 57:11).
- **"And they that shall be of thee shall build the old waste places" → "And those from you shall build the ruins of old"** (v12) — Hebrew וּבָנוּ מִמְּךָ חָרְבוֹת עוֹלָם: the verb is active, "they shall build"; מִמְּךָ "from you"; חָרְבוֹת עוֹלָם literally "the ruins of the age" (of time long past). **"the foundations of many generations" → "the foundations of generation and generation"** — מוֹסְדֵי דוֹר־וָדוֹר, word for word; Hebrew order. **"The repairer of the breach, The restorer of paths to dwell in" → "the one who walls up the breach, the one who restores tracks for dwelling"** — גֹּדֵר פֶּרֶץ, גָּדַר "wall up, fence"; מְשֹׁבֵב, polel of שׁוּב "bring back"; נְתִיבוֹת "tracks"; לָשָׁבֶת "for dwelling".
- **"If thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath, from doing thy pleasure" → "If you turn back your foot from Shabbat, from doing your pleasures"** (v13) — Hebrew אִם־תָּשִׁיב מִשַּׁבָּת רַגְלֶךָ עֲשׂוֹת חֲפָצֶיךָ: הֵשִׁיב "turn back"; חֲפָצֶיךָ plural. **"call the sabbath a delight, the holy of the LORD, honourable" → "call Shabbat a delight, the holy of Yahweh honored"** — עֹנֶג "delight" (root of v14 תִּתְעַנַּג); לִקְדוֹשׁ יְהוָה מְכֻבָּד, "the holy (day) of Yahweh", מְכֻבָּד "honored" (pual of כִּבֵּד). **"and shalt honour him, not doing thine own ways, nor finding thine own pleasure, nor speaking thine own words" → "and honor it, from doing your ways, from finding your pleasure, and speaking a word"** — וְכִבַּדְתּוֹ מֵעֲשׂוֹת דְּרָכֶיךָ מִמְּצוֹא חֶפְצְךָ וְדַבֵּר דָּבָר: מִן "from" (= refraining from) twice; דַּבֵּר דָּבָר "speaking a word" (no "own", no "idly").
- **"Then shalt thou delight thyself in the LORD" → "then you shall delight yourself in Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew תִּתְעַנַּג עַל־יְהוָה, literally "upon Yahweh". **"to ride upon the high places of the earth"** — בָּמֳתֵי אָרֶץ, בָּמוֹת "high places". **"feed thee with the heritage of Jacob" → "make you eat the inheritance of Jacob"** — וְהַאֲכַלְתִּיךָ נַחֲלַת, hifil of אָכַל; נַחֲלָה "inheritance".

### Notes
- **True fast (v6-7)** — ethical, not ritual; foundational for Jewish and Christian social ethics.
