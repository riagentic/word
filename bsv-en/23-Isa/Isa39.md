# Isaiah 39

[1] At that time Merodach-baladan the son of Baladan, king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, sent letters and an offering to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧; and he heard that he had been sick and had become strong.

[2] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ was glad over them, and he showed them his treasure house, the silver and the gold and the spices and the good oil, and all the house of his gear, and all that was found in his treasuries. There was not a thing that ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ did not show them in his house and in all his rule.

[3] And ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet came to King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ and said to him, "What did these men say, and from where do they come to you?" And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said, "From a far land they have come to me, from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧."

[4] And he said, "What have they seen in your house?" And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said, "All that is in my house they have seen; there was not a thing that I did not show them in my treasuries."

[5] And ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ said to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, "Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

[6] Behold, days are coming, and all that is in your house, and what your fathers have treasured up to this day, shall be carried to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧; not a thing shall remain, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And of your sons who shall come out from you, whom you shall beget, they shall take, and they shall be eunuchs in the palace of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧."

[8] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said to ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧, "Good is the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that you have spoken." And he said, "For there shall be ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and truth in my days."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel** (whole chapter) — the chapter stands almost word for word in 2 Kgs 20:12–19; the wording of 2 Kings is kept where the Hebrew is the same, and every place where Isaiah's Hebrew differs is translated as Isaiah has it and listed below. Nothing is levelled either way.
- **"Merodach-baladan"** (v1) — Hebrew מְרֹדַךְ, as printed; 2 Kgs 20:12 has בְּרֹאדַךְ, "Berodach". **"sent letters and a present … for he had heard that he had been sick, and was recovered" → "sent letters and an offering … and he heard that he had been sick and had become strong"** (v1) — Hebrew מִנְחָה "offering"; וַיִּשְׁמַע, a narrative "and he heard" (2 Kgs כִּי שָׁמַע "for he had heard"; the narrative form may also stand for what happened before: "and he had heard"); וַיֶּחֱזָק, "and had become strong" (the חָזַק "be strong" of its row), which 2 Kgs lacks; Kings names Hezekiah a second time, Isaiah has "he".
- **"Hezekiah was glad of them, and shewed them the house of his precious things … the precious ointment, and all the house of his armour … in all his dominion" → "Hezekiah was glad over them, and he showed them his treasure house … the good oil, and all the house of his gear … in all his rule"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂמַח עֲלֵיהֶם, "was glad over them", where 2 Kgs 20:13 has וַיִּשְׁמַע "listened to them" (the two words differ in one letter). בֵּית נְכֹתוֹ, the reading tradition (qere; the written form נכתה is an old spelling of the same suffix), found only here and in Kings: "treasure house" is the best-supported sense and is uncertain. Isaiah has "his treasure house" without "all" and "all the house of his gear" with "all" (2 Kgs the other way round). הַשֶּׁמֶן הַטּוֹב; בֵּית כֵּלָיו, "the house of his vessels, gear, weapons": a storehouse or armory; מֶמְשַׁלְתּוֹ.
- **"whence came they unto thee? … They are come from a far country unto me" → "from where do they come to you? … From a far land they have come to me"** (v3) — Hebrew יָבֹאוּ; אֵלַי "to me", which 2 Kgs 20:14 lacks.
- **"there is nothing among my treasures that I have not shewed them" → "there was not a thing that I did not show them in my treasuries"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא־הָיָה דָבָר.
- **"Hear the word of the LORD of hosts"** (v5) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת; 2 Kgs 20:16 has "the word of Yahweh".
- **"Behold, the days come, that all that is in thine house … nothing shall be left, saith the LORD" → "Behold, days are coming, and all that is in your house … not a thing shall remain, says Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew אָצְרוּ, the root of "treasury" (v2, 4); יִוָּתֵר; אָמַר, "says", not נְאֻם. וְנִשָּׂא is "shall be lifted, carried".
- **"thy sons that shall issue from thee … shall they take away" → "your sons who shall come out from you … they shall take"** (v7) — Hebrew יִקָּחוּ, written so here (2 Kgs 20:18 has it only as the qere). **"eunuchs"** — Hebrew סָרִיסִים, also used of court officers.
- **"He said moreover, For there shall be peace and truth in my days" → "And he said, 'For there shall be shalom and truth in my days'"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר כִּי יִהְיֶה שָׁלוֹם וֶאֱמֶת בְּיָמָי: "and he said" is spoken, not "he thought" (the earlier "For he thought"). Where 2 Kgs 20:19 has a question ("Is it not so, if …"), Isaiah has כִּי, "for" or "that" ("that there shall be …") or a stressed "surely"; the Hebrew does not decide. Whether he speaks in thankfulness or in unconcern is not said. אֱמֶת "truth" (not "security").

### Notes
- **Parallel** — 2 Kgs 20:12–19; compare 2 Chr 32:31.
- **Merodach-baladan** (v1) — Marduk-apla-iddina, a king of Babylon known from Assyrian records.
- **The word of v6–7** — Babylon, not Assyria, will carry off the treasures; compare 2 Kgs 24:13; 25:13–17; Dan 1:3.
