# Isaiah 27

[1] In that day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will visit
with his hard and great and strong sword
upon ⟦leviathan|Livyatan|Leviathan⟧ the fleeing serpent,
and upon ⟦leviathan|Livyatan|Leviathan⟧ the twisting serpent,
and he will kill the sea monster that is in the sea.

[2] In that day,
"A vineyard of delight, sing of it!

[3] I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, am its guard;
every moment I water it.
Lest one visit upon it,
night and day I guard it.

[4] Fury I have none.
Would that I had briers, thorns in the battle!
I would step out against it,
I would set it on fire together.

[5] Or let him take hold of my strong place,
let him make ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with me;
⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ let him make with me."

[6] In days to come ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ will take root,
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ will blossom and bud,
and they will fill the face of the world with crops.

[7] Like the blow of him who struck him has he struck him?
Or like the killing of his slain has he been killed?

[8] By measure, by sending her away, you contend with her;
he has removed her with his hard wind
in the day of the east wind.

[9] Therefore by this the iniquity of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ will be atoned for,
and this is all the fruit of the removing of his sin:
when he makes all the stones of the altar
like stones of chalk shattered,
Asherim and incense altars shall not rise.

[10] For the fortified city is alone,
a habitation let go and forsaken like the wilderness;
there the calf feeds,
and there it lies down and makes an end of its branches.

[11] When its twigs are dried out, they are broken off;
women come, setting it alight.
For it is not a people of understanding;
therefore he who made him will not have compassion on him,
and he who formed him will not show him favor.

[12] And it shall be in that day, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will beat out the grain from the current of ⟦the-river|the River|the river Euphrates⟧ to the Wadi of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and you will be gathered up one by one, sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[13] And it shall be in that day, a blast will be blown on a great ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, and those who are perishing in the land of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ will come, and those driven away in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and they will bow down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on the holy mountain in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) or a part of it per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v1 now follows the Hebrew order; v12–13 stay prose. The quotation marks of the song (v2–5) are the editors'.
- **"shall punish" → "will visit … upon"** (v1) — Hebrew יִפְקֹד … עַל, פָּקַד "visit" (to punish; also v3). **"with his sore and great and strong sword"** — חַרְבּוֹ הַקָּשָׁה "hard" (as v8).
- **"leviathan the piercing serpent, even leviathan that crooked serpent" → "Leviathan the fleeing serpent, and upon Leviathan the twisting serpent"** (v1) — Hebrew לִוְיָתָן (the `leviathan` token); נָחָשׁ "serpent"; בָּרִחַ "fleeing" (from בָּרַח "flee"; KJV "piercing", from another root; also taken as "gliding"); עֲקַלָּתוֹן "twisting" (only here; from עָקַל "be crooked"); וְעַל "and upon": the Hebrew names it twice, with two words.
- **"he shall slay the dragon that is in the sea" → "he will kill the sea monster that is in the sea"** (v1) — Hebrew וְהָרַג אֶת־הַתַּנִּין אֲשֶׁר בַּיָּם: הָרַג "kill"; תַּנִּין "sea monster" where it is in the waters (as Gen 1:21; Ps 74:13; 148:7; "dragon" elsewhere; the exact creature is uncertain).
- **"sing ye unto her, A vineyard of red wine" → "A vineyard of delight, sing of it!"** (v2) — Hebrew כֶּרֶם חֶמֶד עַנּוּ־לָהּ, in this order. The MT has חֶמֶד "delight, desire"; KJV "red wine" follows חֶמֶר "foaming wine", the reading of many Hebrew manuscripts (not followed). עַנּוּ "sing" (piel of ענה; "answer" in the qal); לָהּ "of it / to it" (the vineyard).
- **"I the LORD do keep it … lest any hurt it, I will keep it night and day" → "I, Yahweh, am its guard … Lest one visit upon it, night and day I guard it"** (v3) — Hebrew נֹצְרָהּ (participle of נָצַר "guard") and אֶצֳּרֶנָּה (the same verb); לִרְגָעִים "every moment" (lit. "at moments"); פֶּן יִפְקֹד עָלֶיהָ "lest one visit upon it" (the subject is not named; "hurt" is the sense).
- **"Fury is not in me: who would set the briers and thorns against me in battle? I would go through them, I would burn them together" → "Fury I have none. Would that I had briers, thorns in the battle! I would step out against it, I would set it on fire together"** (v4) — Hebrew חֵמָה אֵין לִי "fury there is none to me"; מִי־יִתְּנֵנִי "would that I had" (lit. "who will give me"); שָׁמִיר שַׁיִת "briers, thorns" (no "and"; as 5:6; 7:23–25); אֶפְשְׂעָה "I would step" (only here; root of פֶּשַׂע "step"); בָהּ "against it", אֲצִיתֶנָּה "I would set it on fire" (feminine singular: the brier growth as one, or the vineyard); יָחַד "together".
- **"that he may make peace with me; and he shall make peace with me" → "let him make shalom with me; shalom let him make with me"** (v5) — Hebrew יַעֲשֶׂה שָׁלוֹם לִי שָׁלוֹם יַעֲשֶׂה־לִּי, the two halves in reverse order; the `shalom` token added for the noun; מָעוֹז "strong place" (as 25:4); לִי "with me" (lit. "to / for me").
- **"He shall cause them that come of Jacob to take root" → "In days to come Jacob will take root"** (v6) — Hebrew הַבָּאִים יַשְׁרֵשׁ יַעֲקֹב: הַבָּאִים is "the coming (ones)"; "days" is supplied, taking it as "(in days) to come"; KJV takes it as the object, "he shall cause those who come to take root" (יַשְׁרֵשׁ, hifil, may be "take root" or "make take root"). The sense is uncertain. **"blossom and bud"** — יָצִיץ וּפָרַח, "blossom" and "bud". **"fruit" → "crops"** — תְּנוּבָה "crops" (what the land yields; not פְּרִי "fruit", v9); "they will fill" (plural).
- **"Hath he smitten him, as he smote those that smote him? or is he slain according to the slaughter of them that are slain by him?" → "Like the blow of him who struck him has he struck him? Or like the killing of his slain has he been killed?"** (v7) — Hebrew הַכְּמַכַּת מַכֵּהוּ הִכָּהוּ אִם־כְּהֶרֶג הֲרֻגָיו הֹרָג: מַכָּה "blow", הִכָּה "strike", הֶרֶג "killing", הֲרֻגָיו "his slain" (those he killed), הֹרָג "he has been killed". Word for word, in the Hebrew order.
- **"In measure, when it shooteth forth, thou wilt debate with it: he stayeth his rough wind in the day of the east wind" → "By measure, by sending her away, you contend with her; he has removed her with his hard wind in the day of the east wind"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּסַאסְּאָה, only here: "by measure" (from סְאָה "seah"), or a sound of driving away; uncertain. בְּשַׁלְחָהּ "by sending her away" (שִׁלַּח); תְּרִיבֶנָּה "you contend with her" (רִיב); "her" is feminine (the vineyard, the people); הָגָה "remove" (as Prov 25:4, 5); רוּחַ "wind"; קָדִים "east wind".
- **"By this therefore shall the iniquity of Jacob be purged; and this is all the fruit to take away his sin" → "Therefore by this the iniquity of Jacob will be atoned for, and this is all the fruit of the removing of his sin"** (v9) — Hebrew יְכֻפַּר, pual of כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (lit. "be covered"); עָוֹן "iniquity"; הָסִר "removing"; חַטָּאת "sin".
- **"all the stones of the altar as chalkstones that are beaten in sunder, the groves and images shall not stand up" → "all the stones of the altar like stones of chalk shattered, Asherim and incense altars shall not rise"** (v9) — Hebrew מִזְבֵּחַ, singular "altar"; אַבְנֵי־גִר "stones of chalk"; מְנֻפָּצוֹת "shattered"; אֲשֵׁרִים "Asherim" (the wooden objects of worship bearing the goddess's name; not "groves"); חַמָּנִים "incense altars" (uncertain); יָקֻמוּ "rise".
- **"the defenced city shall be desolate, and the habitation forsaken, and left like a wilderness" → "the fortified city is alone, a habitation let go and forsaken like the wilderness"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; עִיר בְּצוּרָה "a fortified city" (no article); בָּדָד "alone" (no verb); נָוֶה "habitation"; מְשֻׁלָּח "let go" (pual of שִׁלַּח); עָזַב "forsake". **"there shall the calf feed … and consume the branches thereof" → "there the calf feeds … and makes an end of its branches"** — רָעָה "feed" (of the animal itself); רָבַץ "lie"; כִּלָּה "make an end of"; סְעִפִים "branches".
- **"When the boughs thereof are withered, they shall be broken off: the women come, and set them on fire" → "When its twigs are dried out, they are broken off; women come, setting it alight"** (v11) — Hebrew קָצִיר "twigs" (the same letters as "harvest"; here what is cut, as Job 14:9); יָבֵשׁ "be dried out"; נָשִׁים "women" (no article); מְאִירוֹת אוֹתָהּ "setting it alight" (hifil of אוֹר, "make light").
- **"a people of no understanding: therefore he that made them will not have mercy on them, and he that formed them will shew them no favour" → "not a people of understanding; therefore he who made him will not have compassion on him, and he who formed him will not show him favor"** (v11) — Hebrew בִּינוֹת "understanding" (a plural); the suffixes are masculine singular ("him": the people); עָשָׂה "make", יָצַר "form"; רִחַם "have compassion"; חָנַן "show favor".
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day, that the LORD shall beat off from the channel of the river unto the stream of Egypt" → "And it shall be in that day, Yahweh will beat out the grain from the current of the River to the Wadi of Egypt"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "And it shall be" (also v13); יַחְבֹּט "beat out" (as an olive tree or a crop: "the grain" is supplied, and שִׁבֹּלֶת "current" is also the word for an ear of grain: the image plays on both); הַנָּהָר "the River" (the Euphrates is meant but not named; the token keeps it); נַחַל "wadi".
- **"ye shall be gathered one by one, O ye children of Israel" → "you will be gathered up one by one, sons of Israel"** (v12) — Hebrew תְּלֻקְּטוּ, לָקַט "gather up" (pick up from the ground, as gleanings); לְאַחַד אֶחָד "one by one" (lit. "to one, one"); בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "sons of Israel"; no "O".
- **"the great trumpet shall be blown" → "a blast will be blown on a great shofar"** (v13) — Hebrew יִתָּקַע בְּשׁוֹפָר גָּדוֹל, an impersonal passive with בְּ "on"; no article.
- **"they shall come which were ready to perish in the land of Assyria, and the outcasts in the land of Egypt, and shall worship the LORD in the holy mount" → "those who are perishing in the land of Assyria will come, and those driven away in the land of Egypt, and they will bow down to Yahweh on the holy mountain"** (v13) — Hebrew הָאֹבְדִים "those perishing" (אָבַד "perish"); הַנִּדָּחִים "those driven away" (the נִדָּח row); בְּאֶרֶץ "in the land" both times (the old "to the land of Egypt" was not in the Hebrew); הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down"; הַר הַקֹּדֶשׁ "the holy mountain" (lit. "the mountain of holiness").

### Notes
- **v1** — Leviathan: a sea creature of the old Near Eastern stories (Ugaritic *Lotan*, "the fleeing serpent, the twisting serpent", from memory); cf. Job 3:8; 41; Ps 74:14; 104:26.
- **v13** — the great shofar: cf. Matt 24:31; 1 Thess 4:16.
