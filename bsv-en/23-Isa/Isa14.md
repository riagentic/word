# Isaiah 14

[1] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will have compassion on ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and will yet choose ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and will set them down on their ground; and the sojourner will be joined to them, and they will attach themselves to the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[2] And peoples will take them and bring them to their place, and the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ will inherit them on the ground of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for male slaves and for maidservants; and they will be captors of their captors, and they will have dominion over their taskmasters.

[3] And it shall be, in the day that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gives you rest from your pain and from your agitation and from the hard service with which you were made to serve,

[4] that you shall lift up this parable against the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and say:

How the taskmaster has ceased,
the exactress of gold has ceased!

[5] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has broken the staff of the wicked,
the rod of rulers,

[6] that struck peoples in raging
with a blow without turning aside,
that had dominion over nations in anger,
with a pursuit without withholding.

[7] All the earth is at rest, it is quiet;
they break forth in a ringing cry.

[8] Even the cypresses rejoice over you,
the cedars of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧:
"Since you lay down,
the cutter does not come up against us."

[9] Sheol from beneath is agitated for you
to meet your coming;
it rouses the shades for you,
all the he-goats of the earth;
it raises up from their thrones
all the kings of the nations.

[10] All of them will answer
and say to you:
"You also have been made weak as we are;
to us you have been made like."

[11] Brought down to Sheol is your pride,
the din of your harps;
beneath you maggots are spread out,
and those that cover you are worms.

[12] How you have fallen from the heavens,
Shining one, son of the dawn!
You are hewn down to the earth,
you who weakened nations!

[13] And you said in your heart:
"To the heavens I will go up;
above the stars of ⟦el|El|God⟧
I will raise my throne;
and I will sit on the mount of meeting
in the remote parts of the north;

[14] I will go up above the high places of the thick cloud;
I will make myself like ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧."

[15] Yet to Sheol you will be brought down,
to the remote parts of the pit.

[16] Those who see you will gaze at you,
they will consider you:
"Is this the man who made the earth tremble,
who made kingdoms shake,

[17] who set the world like the wilderness
and threw down its cities,
who did not loose his prisoners homeward?"

[18] All the kings of the nations, all of them,
lie in glory, each man in his house.

[19] But you are thrown out of your grave
like an abominated branch,
the clothing of the killed, pierced through by the sword,
who go down to the stones of the pit,
like a trampled carcass.

[20] You will not be united with them in burial,
for your land you have ruined,
your people you have killed.
The seed of evildoers will never be named.

[21] Prepare for his sons a place of slaughter
for the iniquity of their fathers,
lest they rise and possess the earth,
and fill the face of the world with cities.

[22] And I will rise up against them, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, and I will cut off from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ name and remnant, and offspring and posterity, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[23] And I will make it a possession of the hedgehog, and pools of water; and I will sweep it with the broom of destruction, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[24] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts has sworn, saying:

Surely as I have thought,
so it has come to be,
and as I have counseled,
it shall stand:

[25] to break ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ in my land,
and upon my mountains I will trample him;
and his yoke shall depart from upon them,
and his load shall depart from upon his shoulder.

[26] This is the counsel that is counseled concerning all the earth,
and this is the hand that is stretched out over all the nations.

[27] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has counseled,
and who will make it void?
And his hand is stretched out,
and who will turn it back?

[28] In the year that King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ died was this burden:

[29] Do not rejoice, ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧, all of you,
that the rod of him who struck you is broken;
for from the root of the serpent will come out an adder,
and its fruit will be a flying ⟦saraf|saraf|fiery serpent⟧.

[30] And the firstborn of the poor will pasture,
and the needy will lie down in security;
and I will put your root to death by famine,
and your remnant he will kill.

[31] Wail, gate! Cry out, city!
Melt away, ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧, all of you!
For from the north smoke comes,
and there is none alone in his ranks.

[32] And what will one answer the messengers of a nation?
That ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has founded ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and in her the afflicted of his people take refuge.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v22, 23, 24, 27) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew נְאֻם יְהוָה, "the declaration of Yahweh", one wording through the prophets.
- **Layout** — v1–4a and v22–23 are prose in the Hebrew, v4b–21 and v24–32 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents. The quotation marks round the whole parable (v4–21) and round Yahweh's words (v22–25) are dropped: the Hebrew has none and the speaker changes at v22; the speech of the trees (v8), the shades (v10), the king (v13–14) and the onlookers (v16–17) keeps them.
- **"and set them in their own land: and the strangers shall be joined with them, and they shall cleave to the house of Jacob" → "and will set them down on their ground; and the sojourner will be joined to them, and they will attach themselves to the house of Jacob"** (v1) — Hebrew וְהִנִּיחָם, hifil of נוּחַ with doubled נ, "set down, settle"; אֲדָמָה "ground"; הַגֵּר "the sojourner" (singular); נִלְוָה עַל "be joined to"; נִסְפַּח "attach oneself".
- **"and the house of Israel shall possess them in the land of the LORD for servants and handmaids: and they shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors" → "and the house of Israel will inherit them on the ground of Yahweh for male slaves and for maidservants; and they will be captors of their captors, and they will have dominion over their taskmasters"** (v2) — Hebrew הִתְנַחֵל "take as an inheritance"; אַדְמַת יְהוָה "the ground of Yahweh"; עֲבָדִים "male slaves", שְׁפָחוֹת "maidservants"; שֹׁבִים לְשֹׁבֵיהֶם, "captors of their captors"; רָדָה בְּ "have dominion over"; נֹגְשִׂים "taskmasters" (also v4).
- **"from thy sorrow, and from thy fear, and from the hard bondage wherein thou wast made to serve" → "from your pain and from your agitation and from the hard service with which you were made to serve"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it shall be"; הָנִיחַ "give rest" (נוּחַ); עֹצֶב "pain"; רֹגֶז "agitation" (noun of רָגַז, v9, 16); עֲבֹדָה "service"; עֻבַּד־בָּךְ, "which was served with you", the one root.
- **"take up this proverb against the king of Babylon" → "lift up this parable against the king of Babylon"** (v4) — Hebrew נָשָׂא מָשָׁל "lift up a parable" (a taunt song; as Num 23:7). **"the golden city ceased" → "the exactress of gold has ceased"** — Hebrew מַדְהֵבָה, only here: as printed, from Aramaic דְּהַב "gold" ("golden city" KJV, "exactress of gold"); the meaning is not certain. The Isaiah scroll from Qumran and many modern versions read מַרְהֵבָה "onslaught, insolent fury" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"the staff of the wicked, and the sceptre of the rulers" → "the staff of the wicked, the rod of rulers"** (v5) — Hebrew מַטֶּה "staff", שֵׁבֶט "rod" (no "and").
- **"He who smote the people in wrath with a continual stroke, he that ruled the nations in anger, is persecuted, and none hindereth" → "that struck peoples in raging with a blow without turning aside, that had dominion over nations in anger, with a pursuit without withholding"** (v6) — Hebrew מַכֶּה … מַכַּת, "striking … a blow" (one root); עֶבְרָה "raging"; בִּלְתִּי סָרָה "without turning aside"; רֹדֶה "having dominion"; מֻרְדָּף, as pointed a hofal participle "pursued": here taken as a noun "pursuit" (KJV "is persecuted"; meaning uncertain); בְּלִי חָשָׂךְ "without withholding".
- **"The whole earth is at rest, and is quiet: they break forth into singing" → "All the earth is at rest, it is quiet; they break forth in a ringing cry"** (v7) — Hebrew נוּחַ "rest", שָׁקַט "be quiet"; פָּצַח "break forth"; רִנָּה "a ringing cry".
- **"the fir trees rejoice at thee … Since thou art laid down, no feller is come up against us" → "Even the cypresses rejoice over you … Since you lay down, the cutter does not come up against us"** (v8) — Hebrew גַּם "even, also"; בְּרוֹשִׁים, a conifer of uncertain kind (as 2 Kgs 19:23); שָׁכַבְתָּ "you lay down" (of death); הַכֹּרֵת "the one who cuts".
- **"Hell from beneath is moved for thee to meet thee at thy coming: it stirreth up the dead for thee, even all the chief ones of the earth" → "Sheol from beneath is agitated for you to meet your coming; it rouses the shades for you, all the he-goats of the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (also v11, 15); רָגַז "be agitated"; עוֹרֵר "rouses"; רְפָאִים "the shades" (the dead; not the people Rephaim); עַתּוּדֵי אָרֶץ, "the he-goats of the earth" (the leaders of the flock: the chief men); הֵקִים "raise up".
- **"Art thou also become weak as we? art thou become like unto us?" → "You also have been made weak as we are; to us you have been made like"** (v10) — Hebrew חֻלֵּיתָ, pual "you have been made weak"; נִמְשַׁל אֶל "be made like to"; no question word (questions in KJV; a statement here, a question is possible).
- **"Thy pomp is brought down to the grave, and the noise of thy viols: the worm is spread under thee, and the worms cover thee" → "Brought down to Sheol is your pride, the din of your harps; beneath you maggots are spread out, and those that cover you are worms"** (v11) — Hebrew גָּאוֹן "pride" (as 13:11, 19); הֶמְיָה "din" (from הָמָה "roar"); נֵבֶל "harp"; רִמָּה "maggots" (collective) beside תּוֹלֵעָה "worm" (singular, here collective); מְכַסֶּיךָ "those covering you".
- **"How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer, son of the morning! how art thou cut down to the ground, which didst weaken the nations!" → "How you have fallen from the heavens, Shining one, son of the dawn! You are hewn down to the earth, you who weakened nations!"** (v12) — Hebrew הֵילֵל בֶּן־שָׁחַר: הֵילֵל "shining one" (from הָלַל "shine", 13:10; the bright morning star), שַׁחַר "dawn". "Lucifer" is the Latin of the Vulgate (*lucifer*, "light-bringer", the name of the morning star), not a Hebrew name; a vocative without "O". גִּדַּע nifal "be hewn down"; חוֹלֵשׁ עַל־גּוֹיִם, "weakening nations" (חָלַשׁ, Exod 17:13), no article.
- **"I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God: I will sit also upon the mount of the congregation, in the sides of the north" → "To the heavens I will go up; above the stars of El I will raise my throne; and I will sit on the mount of meeting in the remote parts of the north"** (v13) — Hebrew עָלָה "go up" (also v14); אֵל "El" (not אֱלֹהִים); אָרִים "I will raise"; הַר־מוֹעֵד, "the mount of meeting" (מוֹעֵד as in "tent of meeting"); יַרְכְּתֵי צָפוֹן "the remote parts of the north" (as Ps 48:2; צָפוֹן is also the name of a mountain, Zaphon, of Canaanite story).
- **"I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will be like the most High" → "I will go up above the high places of the thick cloud; I will make myself like Elyon"** (v14) — Hebrew בָּמֳתֵי עָב, "the high places of the thick cloud" (בָּמָה; עָב singular); אֶדַּמֶּה, hitpael, "I will make myself like".
- **"Yet thou shalt be brought down to hell, to the sides of the pit" → "Yet to Sheol you will be brought down, to the remote parts of the pit"** (v15) — Hebrew אַךְ "yet, only"; תּוּרָד imperfect; יַרְכְּתֵי־בוֹר, the same word as v13 (the heights and the depths set against each other); בּוֹר "pit".
- **"shall narrowly look upon thee, and consider thee … that made the earth to tremble, that did shake kingdoms" → "will gaze at you, they will consider you … who made the earth tremble, who made kingdoms shake"** (v16) — Hebrew הִשְׁגִּיחַ "gaze", הִתְבּוֹנֵן "consider"; מַרְגִּיז (רָגַז, v9), מַרְעִישׁ (רָעַשׁ, 13:13). הָאִישׁ "the man".
- **"That made the world as a wilderness, and destroyed the cities thereof; that opened not the house of his prisoners?" → "who set the world like the wilderness and threw down its cities, who did not loose his prisoners homeward?"** (v17) — Hebrew שָׂם "set"; מִדְבָּר "wilderness"; הָרַס "throw down"; עָרָיו, "its / his cities" (masculine suffix: the world or the king); אֲסִירָיו לֹא־פָתַח בָּיְתָה, "his prisoners he did not open homeward" (פָּתַח "open, loose"; בָּיְתָה "to the house, homeward").
- **"All the kings of the nations, even all of them, lie in glory, every one in his own house" → "All the kings of the nations, all of them, lie in glory, each man in his house"** (v18) — Hebrew שָׁכְבוּ perfect, "have lain down"; כָּבוֹד "glory"; אִישׁ בְּבֵיתוֹ "each man in his house" (the tomb is meant; no "own").
- **"But thou art cast out of thy grave like an abominable branch, and as the raiment of those that are slain, thrust through with a sword … as a carcase trodden under feet" → "But you are thrown out of your grave like an abominated branch, the clothing of the killed, pierced through by the sword … like a trampled carcass"** (v19) — Hebrew הָשְׁלַכְתָּ, hofal of הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; קֶבֶר "grave"; נֵצֶר "branch" (as 11:1); נִתְעָב "abominated" (תִּעֵב); לְבוּשׁ הֲרֻגִים, "the clothing of the killed" (noun, no "as"; הָרַג "kill", v20, 30): the king lies covered with the dead; מְטֹעֲנֵי חָרֶב "pierced through by the sword"; פֶּגֶר "carcass"; מוּבָס, hofal of בּוּס "trample" (v25).
- **"Thou shalt not be joined with them in burial, because thou hast destroyed thy land, and slain thy people: the seed of evildoers shall never be renowned" → "You will not be united with them in burial, for your land you have ruined, your people you have killed. The seed of evildoers will never be named"** (v20) — Hebrew יָחַד "be united"; שִׁחֵת "ruin"; הָרַג "kill"; לֹא־יִקָּרֵא לְעוֹלָם, "will not be called forever": a statement, not the wish "May … nevermore" (לֹא, not אַל); זֶרַע "seed"; מְרֵעִים "evildoers".
- **"Prepare slaughter for his children for the iniquity of their fathers; that they do not rise, nor possess the land, nor fill the face of the world with cities" → "Prepare for his sons a place of slaughter for the iniquity of their fathers, lest they rise and possess the earth, and fill the face of the world with cities"** (v21) — Hebrew הָכִינוּ "prepare"; מַטְבֵּחַ, only here, "a place of slaughter" (from טָבַח "slaughter"); בָּנִים "sons"; עָוֹן "iniquity"; בַּל "lest"; יָרַשׁ "possess"; עָרִים "cities" (some read עָרִים "enemies", from memory; the word as printed is "cities").
- **"and cut off from Babylon the name, and remnant, and son, and nephew" → "and I will cut off from Babylon name and remnant, and offspring and posterity"** (v22) — Hebrew שֵׁם וּשְׁאָר "name and remnant" (שְׁאָר; שְׁאֵרִית in v30 has the same English "remnant": English has one word); נִין וָנֶכֶד "offspring and posterity" (as Gen 21:23; Job 18:19).
- **"I will also make it a possession for the bittern, and pools of water: and I will sweep it with the besom of destruction" → "And I will make it a possession of the hedgehog, and pools of water; and I will sweep it with the broom of destruction"** (v23) — Hebrew קִפֹּד, the plainest sense "hedgehog" (or "porcupine"); KJV "bittern" and others "owl" take it as a bird (Isa 34:11; Zeph 2:14): uncertain. מוֹרָשׁ "possession"; אַגְמֵי־מַיִם "pools of water"; טֵאטֵא … מַטְאֲטֵא "sweep … broom" (one root); הַשְׁמֵד "destroying".
- **"Surely as I have thought, so shall it come to pass; and as I have purposed, so shall it stand" → "Surely as I have thought, so it has come to be, and as I have counseled, it shall stand"** (v24) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא, the oath form ("if not …"): "Surely"; דִּמִּיתִי "I have thought" (דִּמָּה); הָיָתָה perfect, "it has come to be"; יָעַצְתִּי "I have counseled" (יָעַץ, also v26–27).
- **"That I will break the Assyrian … and tread him under foot: then shall his yoke depart from off them, and his burden depart from off their shoulders" → "to break Ashur / Assyria in my land, and upon my mountains I will trample him; and his yoke shall depart from upon them, and his load shall depart from upon his shoulder"** (v25) — Hebrew לִשְׁבֹּר infinitive "to break"; אַשּׁוּר, the land and its king; בּוּס "trample"; סוּר "depart" (twice); עֹל "yoke"; סֻבֳּלוֹ "his load" (סֹבֶל, kept apart from מַשָּׂא "burden", v28); שִׁכְמוֹ "his shoulder" (singular, beside "them").
- **"This is the purpose that is purposed upon the whole earth" → "This is the counsel that is counseled concerning all the earth"** (v26) — Hebrew עֵצָה … הַיְּעוּצָה, noun and verb of one root; נְטוּיָה "stretched out".
- **"who shall disannul it?" → "who will make it void?"** (v27) — Hebrew יָפֵר (הֵפֵר "make void"); יְשִׁיבֶנָּה "turn it back".
- **"was this burden"** (v28) — Hebrew הַמַּשָּׂא "the burden" (as 13:1).
- **"Rejoice not thou, whole Palestina, because the rod of him that smote thee is broken: for out of the serpent's root shall come forth a cockatrice, and his fruit shall be a fiery flying serpent" → "Do not rejoice, Peleshet / Philistia, all of you, that the rod of him who struck you is broken; for from the root of the serpent will come out an adder, and its fruit will be a flying saraf"** (v29, 31) — Hebrew כֻּלֵּךְ "all of you" (feminine singular: the whole land); vocatives without "O"; שֵׁבֶט מַכֵּךְ "the rod of the one who struck you"; נָחָשׁ "serpent"; צֶפַע "adder" (kind uncertain); שָׂרָף מְעוֹפֵף "a flying saraf" (token, as Num 21:6; Isa 30:6; the word is "burning one").
- **"And the firstborn of the poor shall feed … and I will kill thy root with famine, and he shall slay thy remnant" → "And the firstborn of the poor will pasture … and I will put your root to death by famine, and your remnant he will kill"** (v30) — Hebrew רָעָה "pasture"; דַּלִּים "poor", אֶבְיוֹנִים "needy"; לָבֶטַח "in security"; רָבַץ "lie down"; הֵמִית "put to death"; יַהֲרֹג, third person, "he will kill": the subject changes from "I" (the famine, or the one from the north of v31; not named).
- **"Howl, O gate; cry, O city; thou, whole Palestina, art dissolved: for there shall come from the north a smoke, and none shall be alone in his appointed times" → "Wail, gate! Cry out, city! Melt away, Philistia, all of you! For from the north smoke comes, and there is none alone in his ranks"** (v31) — Hebrew הֵילִילִי, זַעֲקִי imperatives; נָמוֹג, infinitive absolute of מוּג "melt away" used for a command (or perfect "is melted"); עָשָׁן "smoke"; בּוֹדֵד "one alone" (a straggler); בְּמוֹעָדָיו, only here, "in his ranks" (uncertain; from יָעַד "appoint": his appointed places).
- **"What shall one then answer the messengers of the nation? That the LORD hath founded Zion, and the poor of his people shall trust in it" → "And what will one answer the messengers of a nation? That Yahweh has founded Zion, and in her the afflicted of his people take refuge"** (v32) — Hebrew יַעֲנֶה "he / one will answer" (no subject named); מַלְאֲכֵי־גוֹי "the messengers of a nation" (no article); יִסַּד "has founded"; עֲנִיִּים "the afflicted"; חָסָה בְּ "take refuge in".

### Notes
- **v4–21** — a מָשָׁל, a taunt in the lament (*qinah*) rhythm, on the fall of the king of Babylon; v12–15 have been read since the church fathers of the fall of Satan (Luke 10:18); the Hebrew speaks of the king (v4, 16).
