# Isaiah 46

[1] Bel has bowed, ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧ stoops;
their idols have become for the living thing and for the beast;
the things you bore are loaded,
a burden for the weary one.

[2] They stooped, they bowed together;
they could not rescue the burden,
and their soul has gone into captivity.

[3] "Listen to me, house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and all the remnant of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
who have been loaded from the belly,
who have been borne from the womb;

[4] and even to old age I am he,
and even to gray hair I will carry;
I have made, and I will bear,
and I will carry, and I will rescue.

[5] To whom will you liken me and make me equal,
and compare me, that we may be alike?

[6] Those who lavish gold from the bag,
and weigh silver with the reed,
hire a refiner, and he makes it ⟦el|an el|a god⟧;
they fall down, yes, they bow down.

[7] They bear it on the shoulder, they carry it,
and they set it down in its place, and it stands;
from its place it does not move.
Yes, he cries out to it, and it does not answer;
from his distress it does not save him.

[8] Remember this and be firm;
bring back to heart, transgressors.

[9] Remember the first things from of old,
for I am ⟦el|El|God⟧, and there is none else;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and there is none like me,

[10] telling from the beginning the end,
and from ancient times what has not been done,
saying, 'My counsel shall stand,
and all my delight I will do,'

[11] calling from the east a bird of prey,
from a far land the man of my counsel.
Yes, I have spoken; yes, I will bring it;
I have formed; yes, I will do it.

[12] Listen to me, mighty of heart,
who are far from righteousness:

[13] I have brought near my righteousness; it shall not be far,
and my salvation shall not delay;
and I will give salvation in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ my beauty."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew *Elohim*; the chapter's divine polemic uses *El*/*Elohim* primarily.
- **"Bel" / "Nebo" preserved** (v1) — Babylonian divine names, Hebrew בֵּל and נְבוֹ (token `nebo`; "Bel" has one form in English, the token shows in Czech); no gloss added since self-evident in context.
- **"God" → "El"** (v9) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*; v6 אֵל of a made god (token with a small letter, as 44:10).
- **Layout** — one Hebrew line per printed line, in the Hebrew order.
- **"Bel boweth down, Nebo stoopeth" → "Bel has bowed, Nebo stoops"** (v1) — Hebrew כָּרַע, perfect; קֹרֵס, participle (קָרַס, only here and v2, "stoop"). **"their idols were upon the beasts, and upon the cattle" → "their idols have become for the living thing and for the beast"** — הָיוּ … לַחַיָּה וְלַבְּהֵמָה: הָיָה לְ "become for" (= fallen to them as loads); חַיָּה "living thing" (not "wild beast"), בְּהֵמָה "beast". עֲצַבִּים "idols" (from עָצַב "shape"; another word than פֶּסֶל "carved image"). **"your carriages were heavy loaden; they are a burden to the weary beast" → "the things you bore are loaded, a burden for the weary one"** — נְשֻׂאֹתֵיכֶם (נָשָׂא "bear", as v3, 4, 7; סָבַל is "carry") עֲמוּסוֹת (עָמַס "load", as v3); מַשָּׂא "burden"; עֲיֵפָה "the weary (one)", feminine, "beast" is not written.
- **"They stoop, they bow down together" → "They stooped, they bowed together"** (v2) — Hebrew perfects קָרְסוּ כָרְעוּ. **"deliver" → "rescue"** — מַלֵּט (מִלֵּט "rescue", as v4). **"but themselves are gone into captivity" → "and their soul has gone into captivity"** — וְנַפְשָׁם … הָלָכָה: נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (= they themselves; the gods, or Bel and Nebo); וְ "and".
- **"Hearken unto me, O house of Jacob" → "Listen to me, house of Jacob"** (v3, 12) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ אֵלַי (שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to"); no "O". **"which are borne by me from the belly, which are carried from the womb" → "who have been loaded from the belly, who have been borne from the womb"** — הָעֲמֻסִים מִנִּי־בֶטֶן הַנְּשֻׂאִים מִנִּי־רָחַם: the two verbs of the idols in v1 (עָמַס "load", נָשָׂא "bear") are said of Israel, borne by Yahweh; "by me" is not written. בֶּטֶן "belly", רֶחֶם "womb".
- **"And even to your old age I am he; and even to hoar hairs will I carry you" → "and even to old age I am he, and even to gray hair I will carry"** (v4) — Hebrew זִקְנָה "old age", שֵׂיבָה "gray hair" (beside זִקְנָה, the literal word); "your" and "you" are not written. **"I have made, and I will bear; even I will carry, and will deliver you" → "I have made, and I will bear, and I will carry, and I will rescue"** — אֲנִי עָשִׂיתִי וַאֲנִי אֶשָּׂא וַאֲנִי אֶסְבֹּל וַאֲמַלֵּט: נָשָׂא "bear", סָבַל "carry" (a load; v4, 7; as Isa 53:4, 11), מִלֵּט "rescue"; "you" is not written; each "I" is the emphatic אֲנִי (four times in v4).
- **"and compare me, that we may be like" → "and compare me, that we may be alike"** (v5) — Hebrew תַמְשִׁלוּנִי (hifil of מָשַׁל, the root of מָשָׁל "likeness, parable"), וְנִדְמֶה.
- **"They lavish gold out of the bag, and weigh silver in the balance" → "Those who lavish gold from the bag, and weigh silver with the reed"** (v6) — Hebrew הַזָּלִים, participle with the article; קָנֶה "reed" (the beam of the balance: the word kept). **"a goldsmith" → "a refiner"** — צוֹרֵף (as Judg 17:4). **"he maketh it a god" → "he makes it an el"** — וְיַעֲשֵׂהוּ אֵל. **"they fall down, yea, they worship" → "they fall down, yes, they bow down"** — יִסְגְּדוּ (סָגַד "fall down", as 44:15–19) אַף־יִשְׁתַּחֲווּ (הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down").
- **"They bear him upon the shoulder, they carry him" → "They bear it on the shoulder, they carry it"** (v7) — Hebrew יִשָּׂאֻהוּ (נָשָׂא "bear") … יִסְבְּלֻהוּ (סָבַל "carry"); "it", the made image. **"and set him in his place" → "and they set it down in its place"** — וְיַנִּיחֻהוּ תַחְתָּיו (הִנִּיחַ "set down, let rest"; תַּחְתָּיו "in its place"). **"yea, one shall cry unto him, yet can he not answer, nor save him out of his trouble" → "Yes, he cries out to it, and it does not answer; from his distress it does not save him"** — אַף "yes, also"; יִצְעַק, the subject not named ("he", the worshipper); וְלֹא יַעֲנֶה "and it does not answer"; צָרָה "distress".
- **"and shew yourselves men" → "and be firm"** (v8) — Hebrew הִתְאֹשָׁשׁוּ, found only here: uncertain ("be firm", from אֹשׁ / אָשַׁשׁ "foundation"; or "act as men", from אִישׁ). **"bring it again to mind, O ye transgressors" → "bring back to heart, transgressors"** — הָשִׁיבוּ … עַל־לֵב (as 44:19 "bring back to his heart"); פּוֹשְׁעִים "transgressors" (פֶּשַׁע "transgression"); no "it", no "O".
- **"Remember the former things of old" → "Remember the first things from of old"** (v9) — Hebrew רִאשֹׁנוֹת, feminine plural of רִאשׁוֹן "first" (as 44:6 "I am first"); מֵעוֹלָם "from of old". **"I am God, and there is none like me" → "Elohim, and there is none like me"** — the Hebrew has אָנֹכִי once, for both lines; אֶפֶס כָּמוֹנִי, אֶפֶס "nothing, none" (as 45:6).
- **"Declaring the end from the beginning" → "telling from the beginning the end"** (v10) — Hebrew מַגִּיד (הִגִּיד "tell") מֵרֵאשִׁית אַחֲרִית, in the Hebrew order; מִקֶּדֶם "from ancient times"; אֲשֶׁר לֹא־נַעֲשׂוּ "what has not been done" ("yet" is not written). **"I will do all my pleasure" → "all my delight I will do"** — חֶפְצִי "my delight" (as 44:28).
- **"Calling a ravenous bird from the east, the man that executeth my counsel" → "calling from the east a bird of prey, from a far land the man of my counsel"** (v11) — Hebrew עַיִט "bird of prey"; qere עֲצָתִי "my counsel" is in the verse; the written text (ketiv) עצתו reads "his counsel". **"I have spoken it, I will also bring it to pass; I have purposed it, I will also do it" → "Yes, I have spoken; yes, I will bring it; I have formed; yes, I will do it"** — אַף "yes" (three times); אֲבִיאֶנָּה "I will bring it"; יָצַרְתִּי "I have formed" (יָצַר, of a plan: "purposed" is the sense, as 2 Kgs 19:25 = Isa 37:26); no "it" after "spoken" and "formed".
- **"ye stouthearted" → "mighty of heart"** (v12) — Hebrew אַבִּירֵי לֵב, literally (אַבִּיר "mighty one"; = the stubborn, or the strong-willed: the word does not say which).
- **"I bring near my righteousness" → "I have brought near my righteousness"** (v13) — Hebrew קֵרַבְתִּי, perfect; לֹא תִרְחָק "it shall not be far"; לֹא תְאַחֵר "shall not delay". **"I will place salvation in Zion for Israel my glory" → "I will give salvation in Zion, to Israel my beauty"** — וְנָתַתִּי "and I will give"; לְיִשְׂרָאֵל תִּפְאַרְתִּי, "to Israel, my beauty" (תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty"): either "my beauty I give to Israel" or "Israel, who is my beauty"; the Hebrew leaves it open.

### Notes
- **Bel** (Marduk) and **Nebo** (Nabu) — chief Babylonian gods, shown helpless.
- **v1–4** — one pair of verbs, "load" and "bear": the idols are loaded on beasts; Israel has been loaded and borne by Yahweh from the belly.
