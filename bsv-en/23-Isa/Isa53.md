# Isaiah 53

[1] Who has believed our report?
And the arm of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, upon whom has it been revealed?

[2] And he came up like a shoot before him,
and like a root from a dry land;
he had no form and no splendor;
that we should see him, and no appearance, that we should desire him.

[3] Despised, and ceasing among men,
a man of pains, and acquainted with sickness;
and like a hiding of the face from him,
despised, and we did not reckon him.

[4] Surely our sicknesses he bore,
and our pains, he carried them;
and we reckoned him stricken,
struck by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and afflicted.

[5] But he was pierced because of our transgressions,
crushed because of our iniquities;
the discipline of our ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ was upon him,
and by his stripe there was healing for us.

[6] All of us like the flock have wandered;
each to his way we have turned;
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made to come upon him
the iniquity of us all.

[7] He was pressed, and he was afflicted,
and he does not open his mouth;
like a sheep that is led to the slaughter,
and like a ewe that before her shearers is mute,
and he does not open his mouth.

[8] From restraint and from judgment he was taken;
and his generation, who shall muse?
For he was cut off from the land of the living;
because of the transgression of my people a stroke was to them.

[9] And he gave his grave with the wicked,
and with a rich man in his deaths,
although he had done no violence,
and no deceit was in his mouth.

[10] But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ desired to crush him;
he made him sick;
if his soul makes a guilt offering,
he shall see seed, he shall prolong days,
and the desire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall prosper in his hand.

[11] From the toil of his soul he shall see, he shall be sated;
by his knowledge shall the righteous one, my servant,
make righteous for the many,
and their iniquities he shall carry.

[12] Therefore I will divide for him among the many,
and with mighty ones he shall divide spoil,
because he poured out his soul to death,
and with transgressors he was counted;
and he bore the sin of many,
and for the transgressors he intercedes.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 6, 10) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Word for word** (52:13–53:12) — the Hebrew is translated as it stands, with no interpretation in the verse; where the New Testament quotes these verses in Greek, the difference is named here, and the verse is not levelled to it.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v2 follows the accent (the main break stands after "splendor": "that we should see him" belongs to the last line).
- **Tenses** (throughout) — the Hebrew perfects are kept past ("he bore", "he was pierced"); the imperfects of v7 ("he does not open") and the futures of v10–12 are kept as they stand.
- **"Who hath believed our report? and to whom is the arm of the LORD revealed?" → "Who has believed our report? And the arm of Yahweh, upon whom has it been revealed?"** (v1) — Hebrew לִשְׁמֻעָתֵנוּ, literally "our thing heard": what we heard, or what we made heard (the former "what they heard from us" chose one); הֶאֱמִין לְ "believe". עַל־מִי "upon whom"; נִגְלָתָה, nifal of גָּלָה "be uncovered, revealed". John 12:38 and Rom 10:16 quote the Greek, which begins with "Lord" (Κύριε), not in the Hebrew.
- **"For he shall grow up before him as a tender plant" → "And he came up like a shoot before him"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיַּעַל, wayyiqtol, "and he came up" (עָלָה, of a plant "come up", as 55:13); יוֹנֵק, literally "a suckling", of a plant a shoot that draws sap. **"as a root out of a dry ground" → "like a root from a dry land"** — אֶרֶץ צִיָּה. **"he hath no form nor comeliness" → "he had no form and no splendor"** — תֹּאַר "form", הָדָר "splendor". **"and when we shall see him, there is no beauty that we should desire him" → "that we should see him, and no appearance, that we should desire him"** — וְנִרְאֵהוּ וְלֹא־מַרְאֶה וְנֶחְמְדֵהוּ, two verbs with "and" (here of purpose: "that we should") around מַרְאֶה "appearance"; רָאָה "see".
- **"He is despised and rejected of men" → "Despised, and ceasing among men"** (v3) — Hebrew נִבְזֶה וַחֲדַל אִישִׁים, no "he is"; חֲדַל, from חָדַל "cease", is read two ways: "one whom men have ceased from" (forsaken by men) or "the most fleeting of men" (as חָדֵל "fleeting", Ps 39:4); "ceasing among men" keeps the word and leaves the two open. אִישִׁים is a rare plural of אִישׁ. **"a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief" → "a man of pains, and acquainted with sickness"** — מַכְאֹבוֹת "pains" (not "sorrows"); וִידוּעַ חֹלִי, literally "and known of sickness"; חֳלִי "sickness" (as v4, and the verb of v10). **"and we hid as it were our faces from him" → "and like a hiding of the face from him"** — וּכְמַסְתֵּר פָּנִים מִמֶּנּוּ: מַסְתֵּר "a hiding" (or "one who hides"); פָּנִים "face", no "our", no "we"; מִמֶּנּוּ is "from him" or "from us" (the same form): undecided. **"we esteemed him not" → "we did not reckon him"** — חֲשַׁבְנֻהוּ, חָשַׁב "reckon" (as v4): we counted him as nothing.
- **"Surely he hath borne our griefs, and carried our sorrows" → "Surely our sicknesses he bore, and our pains, he carried them"** (v4) — Hebrew אָכֵן חֳלָיֵנוּ הוּא נָשָׂא, with "our sicknesses" first and an emphatic הוּא "he"; וּמַכְאֹבֵינוּ סְבָלָם "and our pains — he carried them" (סָבַל "carry a load", as v11). "Griefs" and "sorrows" were renderings of mind; the words are "sicknesses" (חֳלִי) and "pains" (מַכְאֹב). Matt 8:17 renders the line in its own Greek: "He took our weaknesses and carried our diseases". **"yet we did esteem him stricken" → "and we reckoned him stricken"** — וַאֲנַחְנוּ "and we"; נָגוּעַ, from נָגַע "touch, strike" (the root of נֶגַע "stroke", v8). **"smitten of God" → "struck by Elohim"** — מֻכֵּה אֱלֹהִים, הִכָּה "strike". **"and afflicted"** — וּמְעֻנֶּה, עִנָּה "afflict".
- **"But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities" → "But he was pierced because of our transgressions, crushed because of our iniquities"** (v5) — Hebrew מְחֹלָל, from חָלַל "pierce" (another root than חִלֵּל "profane"); מְדֻכָּא, from דָּכָא "crush" (as v10); מִן "from", here the cause ("because of"); "he was" is not repeated in the second line. **"the chastisement of our peace was upon him" → "the discipline of our shalom was upon him"** — מוּסָר "discipline"; שְׁלוֹמֵנוּ "our *shalom*". **"and with his stripes we are healed" → "and by his stripe there was healing for us"** — וּבַחֲבֻרָתוֹ, singular, חַבּוּרָה "stripe, weal" (as Exod 21:25); נִרְפָּא־לָנוּ, an impersonal nifal perfect, literally "it was healed to us". 1 Pet 2:24 has "by whose stripe you were healed" (μώλωπι, singular).
- **"All we like sheep have gone astray" → "All of us like the flock have wandered"** (v6) — Hebrew כַּצֹּאן "like the flock" (צֹאן, the one word for sheep and goats); תָּעִינוּ, תָּעָה "wander". 1 Pet 2:25 "you were like sheep going astray". **"we have turned every one to his own way" → "each to his way we have turned"** — אִישׁ לְדַרְכּוֹ, Hebrew order; "own" is not in the Hebrew. **"and the LORD hath laid on him" → "and Yahweh has made to come upon him"** — הִפְגִּיעַ בּוֹ, hifil of פָּגַע "meet, come upon": "made (it) meet him"; the same verb is "intercede" in v12.
- **"He was oppressed, and he was afflicted, yet he opened not his mouth" → "He was pressed, and he was afflicted, and he does not open his mouth"** (v7) — Hebrew נִגַּשׂ, nifal of נָגַשׂ "press" (the verb of the taskmaster, Exod 3:7); וְהוּא נַעֲנֶה, nifal of עָנָה "be afflicted", also read reflexively "he let himself be afflicted, he humbled himself"; יִפְתַּח, imperfect. **"he is brought as a lamb to the slaughter, and as a sheep before her shearers is dumb" → "like a sheep that is led to the slaughter, and like a ewe that before her shearers is mute"** — שֶׂה "sheep" (one of the flock, not "lamb"), יוּבָל "is led"; רָחֵל "ewe"; נֶאֱלָמָה "is mute". The former text had "lamb … sheep" for "sheep … ewe". **"so he openeth not his mouth" → "and he does not open his mouth"** — וְלֹא "and not"; no "so" (the Greek of Acts 8:32 has οὕτως "so"). Acts 8:32–33 quotes v7–8 in the Greek ("Like a sheep he was led to slaughter, and like a lamb before the one who shears it silent, so he does not open his mouth. In the humiliation his judgment was taken away; his generation, who will describe? For his life is taken from the earth"); the Hebrew of v8 differs and is translated as it stands.
- **"He was taken from prison and from judgment" → "From restraint and from judgment he was taken"** (v8) — Hebrew מֵעֹצֶר "from restraint" (עֹצֶר, noun of עָצַר; "oppression" or "prison" in other renderings); לֻקָּח "he was taken". **"and who shall declare his generation?" → "and his generation, who shall muse?"** — וְאֶת־דּוֹרוֹ מִי יְשׂוֹחֵחַ: שִׂיחַ "muse, ponder"; דּוֹר "generation" (his contemporaries, or his posterity, or his time of life: undecided); אֶת may also be "with" ("with his generation who mused?"). **"for he was cut off out of the land of the living" → "For he was cut off from the land of the living"** — כִּי "for" (or "that", giving what no one mused on); נִגְזַר, גָּזַר "cut". **"for the transgression of my people was he stricken" → "because of the transgression of my people a stroke was to them"** — מִפֶּשַׁע עַמִּי נֶגַע לָמוֹ: נֶגַע "stroke, plague" (a noun, not a verb); לָמוֹ is the MT, a plural "to them" (the people); it is sometimes taken as a singular "to him" (as perhaps in 44:15); the Septuagint, from memory, read "he was led to death" (לַמָּוֶת). The MT is kept.
- **"And he made his grave with the wicked" → "And he gave his grave with the wicked"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן, "and he gave": the subject is not named (impersonal "one gave", or Yahweh); "they made" supplied a subject. אֶת "with". **"and with the rich in his death" → "and with a rich man in his deaths"** — עָשִׁיר singular, beside the plural "wicked"; בְּמֹתָיו is the MT plural, "in his deaths" (an intensive plural, or a reading; a scroll from Qumran, from memory, has another word); the MT is kept. **"because he had done no violence, neither was any deceit in his mouth" → "although he had done no violence, and no deceit was in his mouth"** — עַל לֹא־, literally "upon (that) not": "because" or "although" (as Job 16:17, "although no violence is in my hands"); חָמָס "violence"; מִרְמָה "deceit". 1 Pet 2:22 has "who did no sin" (ἁμαρτίαν) for "violence".
- **"Yet it pleased the LORD to bruise him; he hath put him to grief" → "But Yahweh desired to crush him; he made him sick"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיהוָה חָפֵץ דַּכְּאוֹ: חָפֵץ "desire" (as 1 Sam 2:25 "Yahweh desired to put them to death"); דָּכָא "crush" (as v5). הֶחֱלִי, hifil of חָלָה "be sick" (the root of חֳלִי, v3, 4), "he made sick"; "him" is supplied. **"when thou shalt make his soul an offering for sin" → "if his soul makes a guilt offering"** — אִם "if"; תָּשִׂים is third person feminine ("his soul makes", נֶפֶשׁ being feminine) or second person ("if you make his soul a guilt offering"): the verse takes the first, the second is KJV's; שִׂים "put, set"; אָשָׁם "guilt", the sacrifice "guilt offering" (Lev 5:14–6:7), not "sin offering" (חַטָּאת). **"he shall see his seed, he shall prolong his days" → "he shall see seed, he shall prolong days"** — זֶרַע "seed" and יָמִים "days" have no "his". **"and the pleasure of the LORD shall prosper in his hand" → "and the desire of Yahweh shall prosper in his hand"** — חֵפֶץ, noun of the verb at the head of the verse ("desired … desire"); צָלַח "prosper".
- **"He shall see of the travail of his soul, and shall be satisfied" → "From the toil of his soul he shall see, he shall be sated"** (v11) — Hebrew מֵעֲמַל נַפְשׁוֹ "from the toil of his soul" (עָמָל "toil"; מִן "from, after, because of"); יִרְאֶה has no object in the MT; the scrolls from Qumran and the Septuagint, from memory, read "he shall see light" (אוֹר); the MT is kept. יִשְׂבָּע "he shall be sated", without "and". **"by his knowledge shall my righteous servant justify many" → "by his knowledge shall the righteous one, my servant, make righteous for the many"** — בְּדַעְתּוֹ "by his knowledge" (of him, or his own: undecided); יַצְדִּיק צַדִּיק, a play on one root: hifil "make righteous / declare righteous"; צַדִּיק has no article ("a righteous one, my servant": "my righteous servant" is another reading); לָרַבִּים with לְ, "for the many" (with the article). **"for he shall bear their iniquities" → "and their iniquities he shall carry"** — וַעֲוֺנֹתָם הוּא יִסְבֹּל, "and", Hebrew order, the emphatic "he"; סָבַל "carry" (as v4).
- **"Therefore will I divide him a portion with the great" → "Therefore I will divide for him among the many"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲחַלֶּק־לוֹ בָרַבִּים: חִלֵּק "divide"; "a portion" is not in the Hebrew; בְּ "among" (or "I will give him the many as his share"); רַבִּים "the many", as v11. **"and he shall divide the spoil with the strong" → "and with mighty ones he shall divide spoil"** — עֲצוּמִים "mighty ones", no article; שָׁלָל "spoil". **"because he hath poured out his soul unto death" → "because he poured out his soul to death"** — תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר, literally "in place of (the fact) that"; הֶעֱרָה, literally "laid bare" (of the soul, "pour out", as Ps 141:8). **"and he was numbered with the transgressors" → "and with transgressors he was counted"** — אֶת־פֹּשְׁעִים, no article; נִמְנָה, מָנָה "count". Luke 22:37 quotes "And he was counted with the lawless" (ἀνόμων). **"and he bare the sin of many" → "and he bore the sin of many"** — וְהוּא "and he"; חֵטְא "sin"; רַבִּים without article. **"and made intercession for the transgressors" → "and for the transgressors he intercedes"** — יַפְגִּיעַ, imperfect, hifil of פָּגַע (as v6, "made to come upon"): "intervene, intercede"; לַפֹּשְׁעִים with the article.

### Notes
- **52:13–53:12** — the fourth servant passage; the New Testament quotes it or applies it to Yeshua: Matt 8:17 (v4); Luke 22:37 (v12); John 12:38 and Rom 10:16 (v1); Acts 8:32–35 (v7–8); 1 Pet 2:22–25 (v5, 6, 9, 12). The Greek differences are named under each verse.
- **v10 *asham*** — the guilt offering (Lev 5:14–6:7).
- **Jewish readings** — the servant read as Israel as a whole, as a righteous remnant or a prophet; rabbinic sources also give a messianic reading.
- **Dead Sea Scrolls (1QIsaᵃ)** — the chapter stands nearly as in the MT; the differences of v11 ("light") and v9 are named above (from memory).
