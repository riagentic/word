# Isaiah 19

[1] The burden of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

Behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is riding on a swift cloud
and shall come to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
and the worthless idols of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall quiver from his face,

[2] "And I will stir up ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ against ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

and its counsel I will swallow up;

[4] And I will hand over ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧
into the hand of a hard lord,
and a strong king shall rule over them,"
declares the Lord, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[5] And waters shall be drained from the sea,
and the river shall dry up and be dried out.

[6] And the rivers shall stink;
the ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧ of Matzor shall be brought low and dry up;

[8] And the fishermen shall lament,

[9] And those who work combed flax shall be ashamed,

[10] And her foundations shall be crushed;

[11] Surely senseless are the princes of ⟦zoan|Tzoan|Zoan⟧,

How do you say to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧,

a son of the kings of old"?

[12] Where are they, then, your wise men?
And let them tell you, please;
and let them know what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has counseled against ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[13] The princes of ⟦zoan|Tzoan|Zoan⟧ have been foolish,
the princes of ⟦memphis|Nof|Memphis⟧ are deceived;
they have made ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ wander,

[14] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has mixed within her a spirit of distortion,
and they have made ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ wander in all its work,
as a drunken man wanders in his vomit.

[15] And there shall not be for ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ a work
that head and tail, palm branch and bulrush, can do.

[16] In that day ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall be like women, and it shall tremble and be in dread because of the waving of the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, which he is waving over it.

[17] And the ground of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall be a staggering to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; everyone to whom one mentions it shall be in dread because of the counsel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, which he is counseling against it.

[18] In that day there shall be five cities in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ speaking the lip of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧ and swearing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts; "The city of destruction," it shall be said of one.

[19] In that day there shall be an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the midst of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and a pillar beside its border to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[20] And it shall be for a sign and for a witness to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; for they shall cry out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ because of oppressors, and he shall send them a savior and a great one, and he shall deliver them.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be made known to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ shall know ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in that day, and they shall serve with sacrifice and grain offering, and they shall vow a vow to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and repay it.

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall smite ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, smiting and healing; and they shall return to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he shall let himself be pleaded with by them and shall heal them.

[23] In that day there shall be a highway from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, and ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ shall come into ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ into ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧; and the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ shall serve with ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.

[24] In that day ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be a third to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, a blessing in the midst of the earth,

[25] whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts has blessed, saying, "Blessed is my people ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and the work of my hands ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, and my inheritance ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4, 12, 16, 17, 18, 20, 25) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"the Lord, the LORD of hosts" → "the Lord, Yahweh of Hosts"** (v4) — Hebrew הָאָדוֹן יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת: הָאָדוֹן "the Lord" with the article (not אֲדֹנָי), then the name and the title.
- **Layout** — v1–15 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents (v12 now three lines: the old layout ran over the main pause); v16–25 stay prose as before.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v1–10, 16, 17, 20–23).
- **"Egypt" / "the Egyptians"** (throughout) — the Hebrew word is one, מִצְרַיִם: "Egypt" where the Hebrew treats it as one (singular verbs and suffixes: v1, 3, 14, 16, 17), "the Egyptians" only where the verb is plural (v21, 23). v2, 4: KJV "the Egyptians" → "Egypt" (no plural verb with the noun; "they shall fight" and "over them" are plural, as written).
- **"saith the LORD" → "declares"** (v4) — Hebrew נְאֻם, "the declaration of".
- **"The burden of Egypt"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא "burden", literally "what is lifted up" ("oracle" in many translations; see 17:1). **"rideth … and shall come into Egypt" → "is riding … and shall come to Egypt"** — רֹכֵב participle, וּבָא weqatal. **"the idols of Egypt shall be moved at his presence" → "the worthless idols of Egypt shall quiver from his face"** — אֱלִילִים "worthless idols" (the word says "worthless" and sounds like אֵל); נוּעַ "quiver, sway"; מִפָּנָיו "from his face". **"in the midst of it" → "within it"** — בְּקִרְבּוֹ, singular.
- **"I will set the Egyptians against the Egyptians" → "I will stir up Egypt against Egypt"** (v2) — Hebrew סִכְסַכְתִּי "stir up, spur on". **"every one against his brother, and every one against his neighbour" → "each man against his brother and each man against his neighbor"** — אִישׁ … אָחִיו / רֵעֵהוּ. **"and kingdom" → "kingdom"** — no "and" in the Hebrew.
- **"the spirit of Egypt shall fail in the midst thereof; and I will destroy the counsel thereof" → "the spirit of Egypt shall be emptied out within it, and its counsel I will swallow up"** (v3) — Hebrew וְנָבְקָה "be emptied out"; בִּלַּע "swallow up"; object first. **"they shall seek to the idols, and to the charmers, and to them that have familiar spirits, and to the wizards" → "they shall seek to the worthless idols and to the mutterers, and to the ghosts and to the knowing spirits"** — דָּרַשׁ אֶל "seek to"; אִטִּים (only here; uncertain: "mutterers", from אַט "softly", or the spirits of the dead); אֹבוֹת "ghosts" and יִדְּעֹנִים "knowing spirits" (the spirits themselves; "mediums", "necromancers" name persons and are not the words).
- **"will I give over into the hand of a cruel lord" → "I will hand over … into the hand of a hard lord"** (v4) — Hebrew וְסִכַּרְתִּי, literally "I will shut up" (סָכַר = סָגַר, as הִסְגִּיר "hand over"); אֲדֹנִים קָשֶׁה, a plural of majesty with a singular adjective: one lord. **"a fierce king" → "a strong king"** — עַז "strong".
- **"the waters shall fail from the sea" → "waters shall be drained from the sea"** (v5) — Hebrew וְנִשְּׁתוּ (root uncertain: נָשַׁת "be dry", or שָׁתָה "be drunk up"); no article on "waters"; הַיָּם "the sea" (the Nile in its flood is called a sea, Nah 3:8). **"the river shall be wasted and dried up" → "the river shall dry up and be dried out"** — חָרֵב "dry up" (of water gone), יָבֵשׁ "be dried out".
- **"they shall turn the rivers far away" → "the rivers shall stink"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהֶאֶזְנִיחוּ, hifil of זָנַח "stink" (with an added א; "turn away" takes it from זָנַח "reject"). **"the brooks of defence shall be emptied and dried up" → "the Niles of Matzor shall be brought low and dry up"** — יְאֹרֵי מָצוֹר, as 2 Kgs 19:24 = Isa 37:25: יְאֹר is the Nile (here plural, its arms), מָצוֹר is a name for Egypt (the same letters spell "siege, defence"); דָּלַל "be brought low". **"the reeds and flags shall wither" → "reed and rush shall wilt"** — קָנֶה "reed" and סוּף (elsewhere "reeds", as Exod 2:3); English has no second plain word beside "reed", so סוּף is "rush" here; קָמֵל "wilt".
- **"The paper reeds by the brooks, by the mouth of the brooks" → "The bare places by the Nile, on the mouth of the Nile"** (v7) — Hebrew עָרוֹת (only here; from עָרָה "be bare": "bare places", meadows; "paper reeds" is a guess); עַל־פִּי "on the mouth of"; יְאוֹר "the Nile" (KJV "brooks"). **"and every thing sown by the brooks, shall wither, be driven away, and be no more" → "and all the sown land of the Nile shall be dried out, be driven away, and it is not"** — מִזְרַע "sown land"; יִיבַשׁ "be dried out"; נִדַּף "be driven away"; וְאֵינֶנּוּ "and it is not".
- **"The fishers also shall mourn, and all they that cast angle into the brooks shall lament" → "And the fishermen shall lament, and they shall mourn, all who throw a hook into the Nile"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאָנוּ "lament" (only here and Isa 3:26), וְאָבְלוּ "mourn" (אָבַל), the verbs before their subjects; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw". **"spread nets upon the waters" → "spread a dragnet on the face of the waters"** — מִכְמֹרֶת, singular (the net that is cast or dragged; רֶשֶׁת is another word); עַל־פְּנֵי־מַיִם. **"shall languish"** — אֻמְלָלוּ.
- **"Moreover they that work in fine flax, and they that weave networks, shall be confounded" → "And those who work combed flax shall be ashamed, and the weavers of white stuff"** (v9) — Hebrew order kept (the verb first); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"; פִּשְׁתִּים שְׂרִיקוֹת "combed flax" (שְׂרִיקוֹת only here); חוֹרָי "white stuff" (white cloth, as חוּר Esth 1:6; uncertain).
- **"And they shall be broken in the purposes thereof, all that make sluices and ponds for fish" → "And her foundations shall be crushed; all who make wages are grieved of soul"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׁתֹתֶיהָ "her foundations" (as Ps 11:3; uncertain: also taken from שְׁתִי "warp", "her weavers"; "the pillars of the land" is a gloss); מְדֻכָּאִים "crushed"; עֹשֵׂי שֶׂכֶר "those who make wages" (who work for hire); אַגְמֵי־נָפֶשׁ "grieved of soul" (אָגֵם only here; "ponds" reads it as אֲגַם "pool").
- **"Surely the princes of Zoan are fools, the counsel of the wise counsellors of Pharaoh is become brutish" → "Surely senseless are the princes of Zoan, the wise of the counselors of Pharaoh; counsel has become brutish"** (v11) — Hebrew אַךְ "surely"; אֱוִלִים "senseless", first; חַכְמֵי יֹעֲצֵי פַרְעֹה "the wise of the counselors of Pharaoh"; עֵצָה נִבְעָרָה "counsel has become brutish" (no "their": a clause of its own). **"I am the son of the wise, the son of ancient kings" → "A son of the wise am I, a son of the kings of old"** — בֶּן־חֲכָמִים אֲנִי, Hebrew order; מַלְכֵי־קֶדֶם "kings of old".
- **"Where are they? where are thy wise men? and let them tell thee now" → "Where are they, then, your wise men? And let them tell you, please"** (v12) — Hebrew אַיָּם אֵפוֹא חֲכָמֶיךָ, one question ("then" for אֵפוֹא); וְיַגִּידוּ נָא "and let them tell, please". **"hath purposed upon Egypt" → "has counseled against Egypt"** — יָעַץ "counsel" (one root with עֵצָה v3, 11, 17).
- **"The princes of Zoan are become fools" → "have been foolish"** (v13) — Hebrew נוֹאֲלוּ, nifal of יאל "be foolish" (as Num 12:11). **"Noph" → "Memphis"** (token) — נֹף. **"they have also seduced Egypt, even they that are the stay of the tribes thereof" → "they have made Egypt wander, the corner of her tribes"** — הִתְעוּ "make wander" (as v14), no "also"; פִּנַּת שְׁבָטֶיהָ "the corner of her tribes", singular, standing last as the subject (the leading men are meant).
- **"hath mingled a perverse spirit in the midst thereof" → "has mixed within her a spirit of distortion"** (v14) — Hebrew מָסַךְ "mix"; רוּחַ עִוְעִים "a spirit of distortion" (עִוְעִים only here, from עָוָה "twist"). **"caused Egypt to err in every work thereof, as a drunken man staggereth" → "made Egypt wander in all its work, as a drunken man wanders"** — הִתְעוּ and כְּהִתָּעוֹת, one root (תָּעָה "wander"), kept; קִיא "vomit".
- **"which the head or tail, branch or rush, may do" → "that head and tail, palm branch and bulrush, can do"** (v15) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ וְזָנָב כִּפָּה וְאַגְמוֹן, joined by "and" (the meaning "any of them" is the sense of the pairs); כִּפָּה "palm branch" (as 9:14); אַגְמוֹן "bulrush" (beside קָנֶה and סוּף, v6).
- **"it shall be afraid and fear because of the shaking of the hand" → "it shall tremble and be in dread because of the waving of the hand"** (v16) — Hebrew חָרַד "tremble", פָּחַד "be in dread"; תְּנוּפָה and מֵנִיף "waving … waving" (the verb of the wave offering, הֵנִיף); "Egypt … it" singular.
- **"the land of Judah shall be a terror unto Egypt" → "the ground of Judah shall be a staggering to Egypt"** (v17) — Hebrew אַדְמַת "ground" (אֲדָמָה, not אֶרֶץ); חָגָּא (only here; uncertain: from חָגַג "reel", Ps 107:27, "a staggering", hence "terror"). **"every one that maketh mention thereof shall be afraid in himself" → "everyone to whom one mentions it shall be in dread"** — כֹּל אֲשֶׁר יַזְכִּיר אֹתָהּ אֵלָיו, literally "all whom one makes it remembered to him"; "in himself" reads אֵלָיו with the next verb. **"which he hath determined against it" → "which he is counseling against it"** — יוֹעֵץ participle.
- **"shall five cities … speak the language of Canaan, and swear to the LORD of hosts" → "there shall be five cities … speaking the lip of Canaan and swearing to Yahweh of Hosts"** (v18) — Hebrew יִהְיוּ … מְדַבְּרוֹת … וְנִשְׁבָּעוֹת, participles; שְׂפַת "lip" (= speech, as Gen 11:1). **"one shall be called, The city of destruction" → "'The city of destruction,' it shall be said of one"** — MT עִיר הַהֶרֶס, "city of destruction" (הֶרֶס from הָרַס "tear down"), Hebrew order; יֵאָמֵר לְאֶחָת "it shall be said of one". The MT is kept. Other readings (from memory): some Hebrew manuscripts and the Isaiah scroll from Qumran have עִיר הַחֶרֶס "city of the sun" (Heliopolis; the reading of the old text of this chapter, "City of the Sun"), the Vulgate "city of the sun", the Septuagint "city of righteousness" (Asedek).
- **"a pillar at the border thereof" → "a pillar beside its border"** (v19) — Hebrew מַצֵּבָה "pillar" (a stone set up); אֵצֶל "beside".
- **"for they shall cry unto the LORD because of the oppressors" → "for they shall cry out to Yahweh because of oppressors"** (v20) — Hebrew כִּי "for" (also "when"); צָעַק "cry out"; לֹחֲצִים "oppressors", no article. **"a saviour, and a great one" → "a savior and a great one"** — מוֹשִׁיעַ וָרָב; וָרָב may also be "and he shall contend" (רִיב): uncertain. **"and he shall deliver them"** — הִצִּיל.
- **"the LORD shall be known to Egypt" → "Yahweh shall be made known to Egypt"** (v21) — Hebrew וְנוֹדַע, nifal. **"shall do sacrifice and oblation" → "they shall serve with sacrifice and grain offering"** — וְעָבְדוּ זֶבַח וּמִנְחָה, literally "serve sacrifice and grain offering" ("with" supplied); מִנְחָה "grain offering". **"they shall vow a vow unto the LORD, and perform it" → "they shall vow a vow to Yahweh and repay it"** — נָדַר נֶדֶר, שִׁלֵּם "repay" (a vow paid back; "it" supplied).
- **"he shall smite and heal it" → "smiting and healing"** (v22) — Hebrew נָגֹף וְרָפוֹא, two infinitives absolute after the verb נָגַף "smite". **"they shall return even to the LORD" → "they shall return to Yahweh"** — שׁוּב עַד. **"he shall be intreated of them" → "he shall let himself be pleaded with by them"** — נֶעְתַּר, nifal of עָתַר.
- **"the Assyrian shall come into Egypt, and the Egyptian into Assyria, and the Egyptians shall serve with the Assyrians" → "Assyria shall come into Egypt, and Egypt into Assyria; and the Egyptians shall serve with Assyria"** (v23) — Hebrew אַשּׁוּר and מִצְרַיִם, the names; וְעָבְדוּ מִצְרַיִם אֶת־אַשּׁוּר: אֵת may be "with" (worship together, as v21) or the sign of the object ("shall serve Assyria"); "with" is kept as KJV.
- **"shall Israel be the third with Egypt and with Assyria, even a blessing in the midst of the land" → "Israel shall be a third to Egypt and to Assyria, a blessing in the midst of the earth"** (v24) — Hebrew שְׁלִישִׁיָּה "a third" (no article), לְ … וּלְ "to … and to"; בְּקֶרֶב הָאָרֶץ "in the midst of the earth" (or "the land").
- **"Whom the LORD of hosts shall bless" → "whom Yahweh of Hosts has blessed"** (v25) — Hebrew בֵּרֲכוֹ, a perfect with a singular suffix ("blessed him / it"): the one blessed is not named (Israel, or the three as one). **"Blessed be Egypt my people" → "Blessed is my people Egypt"** — בָּרוּךְ, no verb ("is", not a wish); Hebrew order "my people Egypt … the work of my hands Assyria … my inheritance Israel".

### Notes
- **v24–25** — "my people", "the work of my hands" and "my inheritance" are words used elsewhere of Israel; here they are given to Egypt, Assyria and Israel side by side.
