# Isaiah 1

[1] The vision of ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧, which he beheld concerning ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ in the days of ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧, ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧, ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧, ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[2] Hear, heavens, and give ear, earth,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken:
"Sons I have reared and raised high,
but they have transgressed against me.

[3] The ox knows its possessor,
and the donkey the crib of its owner;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ does not know,
my people does not understand."

[4] Woe, sinning nation,
a people heavy with iniquity,
a seed of evildoers,
sons who work ruin!
They have forsaken ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
they have spurned the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
they are estranged backward.

[5] Why will you still be struck?
You will add defection.
The whole head is sick,
and the whole heart faint.

[6] From the sole of the foot and to the head
there is no soundness in it:
wound and stripe
and fresh blow;
they have not been pressed out and not bound up,
and it has not been softened with oil.

[7] Your land is a desolation;
your cities are burned with fire;
your ground — in front of you strangers are eating it;
and it is a desolation, like an overthrow by strangers.

[8] And the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ is left
like a booth in a vineyard,
like a lodge in a cucumber field,
like a city besieged.

[9] If ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts had not left over to us a survivor, just a little,
like ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ we would have been,
we would have been like ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧.

[10] Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
captains of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧!
Give ear to the ⟦torah|Torah|teaching⟧ of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
people of ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧!

[11] What to me is the multitude of your sacrifices? says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
I am sated with burnt offerings of rams
and the fat of fatlings;
and the blood of bulls and lambs and he-goats I do not delight in.

[12] When you come to appear before my face,
who has required this from your hand,
the trampling of my courts?

[13] Do not again bring an empty grain offering;
incense — it is an abomination to me.
New moon and ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, the calling of a convocation —
I cannot bear wickedness and solemn assembly.

[14] Your new moons and your appointed times my soul hates;
they have become a load upon me;
I am weary of bearing.

[15] And when you spread out your palms,
I will hide my eyes from you;
even when you multiply prayer,
I am not hearing;
your hands are full of blood.

[16] Wash yourselves; make yourselves clean;
remove the evil of your doings from in front of my eyes;
cease to do evil;

[17] learn to do good;
seek judgment,
lead the ruthless straight;
judge the fatherless,
contend for the widow.

[18] Come now, and let us reason together, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
if your sins are like scarlet,
as the snow they shall be white;
if they are red like crimson,
as wool they shall be.

[19] If you are willing and hear,
the good of the land you shall eat;

[20] and if you refuse and rebel,
by the sword you shall be eaten;
for the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[21] How she has become a prostitute,
the faithful town,
full of judgment!
Righteousness used to lodge in her,
and now murderers.

[22] Your silver has become dross,
your drink cut with water.

[23] Your princes are stubborn
and companions of thieves;
every one of them loves a bribe
and pursues rewards;
the fatherless they do not judge,
and the case of the widow does not come to them.

[24] Therefore declares the Lord, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the Mighty One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

"Woe! I will take comfort from my adversaries,
and avenge myself on my enemies.

[25] And I will turn my hand back upon you,
and refine as with lye your dross,
and remove all your tin.

[26] And I will restore your judges as at the first,
and your counselors as at the beginning.
Afterward you shall be called the city of righteousness,
the faithful town."

[27] ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ shall be ransomed with judgment,
and those of her who return, with righteousness.

[28] And the breaking of transgressors and sinners shall be together,
and those who forsake ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall come to an end.

[29] For they shall be ashamed because of the terebinths
that you desired,
and you shall be abashed because of the gardens
that you have chosen.

[30] For you shall be like a terebinth
whose leaf withers,
and like a garden that has no water.

[31] And the strong one shall become tow,
and his work a spark,
and both of them shall burn together,
and there is none who quenches.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים. **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v9, 24) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, as the earlier books (plain words; the `tzevaot` token belongs to the Greek σαβαώθ).
- **"saith the Lord, the LORD of hosts" → "declares the Lord, Yahweh of Hosts"** (v24) — Hebrew נְאֻם הָאָדוֹן יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת: נְאֻם "declaration" (the formula "declares Yahweh", one wording through the prophets); הָאָדוֹן is "the lord, the master" with the article, not אֲדֹנָי (*Adonai*), so plain "Lord". **"the mighty One of Israel" → "the Mighty One of Israel"** — Hebrew אֲבִיר יִשְׂרָאֵל; the phrase has no word "God".
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; v11–15 are poetry in the Hebrew and are now set in lines (they were prose paragraphs). The quotation marks of v11 are dropped: Yahweh speaks through v11–20, and "says Yahweh" stands inside the speech (v11, 18).
- **"The vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz, which he saw" → "The vision of Isaiah son of Amoz, which he beheld"** (v1) — Hebrew חֲזוֹן … אֲשֶׁר חָזָה: noun and verb of one root, "vision … beheld" (חָזָה beside רָאָה "see"). The four kings stand without "and".
- **"Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth" → "Hear, heavens, and give ear, earth"** (v2) — the Hebrew has no "O".
- **"I have nourished and brought up children, and they have rebelled against me" → "Sons I have reared and raised high, but they have transgressed against me"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּנִים "sons", first in the line; גִּדַּלְתִּי "made great" (of children: "reared") and רוֹמַמְתִּי "raised high"; וְהֵם "and they" stands against "I"; פָּשַׁע בְּ "transgress against" (פֶּשַׁע "transgression"), not the verb "rebel" (מָרָה, v20).
- **"his owner … his master's crib" → "its possessor … the crib of its owner"** (v3) — Hebrew קֹנֵהוּ "its possessor" (קָנָה "get, buy") and בְּעָלָיו "its owner" (בַּעַל): two words. **"doth not consider" → "does not understand"** — Hebrew הִתְבּוֹנָן, hitpolel of בִּין.
- **"Ah sinful nation … a seed of evildoers, children that are corrupters" → "Woe, sinning nation … a seed of evildoers, sons who work ruin"** (v4) — Hebrew הוֹי "Woe" (one wording through the prophets); חֹטֵא a participle, "sinning"; כֶּבֶד עָוֹן "heavy of iniquity"; זֶרַע "seed"; מַשְׁחִיתִים, hifil participle of שָׁחַת "ruin", without an object: "who work ruin". **"provoked the Holy One of Israel unto anger" → "spurned the Holy One of Israel"** — Hebrew נִאֲצוּ, "spurn" (Num 14:11); קְדוֹשׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל, Isaiah's title, one wording. **"they are gone away backward" → "they are estranged backward"** — Hebrew נָזֹרוּ אָחוֹר, nifal of זוּר "be a stranger".
- **"Why should ye be stricken any more? ye will revolt more and more" → "Why will you still be struck? You will add defection"** (v5) — Hebrew עַל מֶה, literally "upon what": "why", or "on what (part of the body)", which v6 would continue; both readings are open. תּוֹסִיפוּ סָרָה "you will add defection" (סָרָה from סוּר "turn aside"). **"the whole head is sick"** — Hebrew לָחֳלִי, literally "for sickness"; כָּל without the article: "every head" or "the whole head".
- **"wounds, and bruises, and putrifying sores" → "wound and stripe and fresh blow"** (v6) — Hebrew פֶּצַע · חַבּוּרָה · מַכָּה טְרִיָּה, singulars: "wound", "stripe" (Gen 4:23; Exod 21:25), "blow" (noun of הִכָּה), טְרִיָּה "fresh, raw". **"neither mollified with ointment" → "and it has not been softened with oil"** — רֻכְּכָה is feminine singular (the blow); שֶׁמֶן "oil".
- **"your land, strangers devour it in your presence, and it is desolate, as overthrown by strangers" → "your ground — in front of you strangers are eating it; and it is a desolation, like an overthrow by strangers"** (v7) — Hebrew אַדְמַתְכֶם "your ground" (beside אַרְצְכֶם "your land"), זָרִים "strangers", אֹכְלִים participle "eating"; שְׁמָמָה "desolation" both times; כְּמַהְפֵּכַת זָרִים literally "like an overthrow of strangers" (מַהְפֵּכָה as Deut 29:23). Many read "like the overthrow of Sodom" (סְדֹם for זָרִים): a conjecture, not in the verse.
- **"is left as a cottage … as a lodge … as a besieged city" → "is left like a booth … like a lodge in a cucumber field, like a city besieged"** (v8) — Hebrew וְנוֹתְרָה (weqatal; "is left" or "shall be left"), סֻכָּה "booth", מְלוּנָה "lodge" (from לוּן "lodge"), מִקְשָׁה "cucumber field". נְצוּרָה is "besieged" (from צוּר) or "watched, guarded" (from נָצַר); the form allows both.
- **"Except the LORD of hosts had left unto us a very small remnant" → "If Yahweh of Hosts had not left over to us a survivor, just a little"** (v9) — Hebrew לוּלֵי "if not"; הוֹתִיר "leave over"; שָׂרִיד "survivor" (one who is left after a slaughter), not שְׁאָר / שְׁאֵרִית "remnant"; כִּמְעָט, literally "as a little", goes with this line by the accents ("almost" if joined to the next line). Rom 9:29 quotes the Septuagint, which has "seed" (σπέρμα); the Hebrew is translated. **"we should have been as Sodom, and we should have been like unto Gomorrah"** — the Hebrew order: "like Sodom we would have been, to Gomorrah we would have been likened" (דָּמָה).
- **"ye rulers of Sodom … ye people of Gomorrah" → "captains of Sodom … people of Gomorrah"** (v10) — Hebrew קְצִינֵי "captains" (one who decides; Josh 10:24); no "you" in the Hebrew. **"the law of our God"** — Hebrew תּוֹרַת אֱלֹהֵינוּ: token *torah*, "instruction".
- **"To what purpose is the multitude of your sacrifices unto me?" → "What to me is the multitude of your sacrifices?"** (v11) — Hebrew לָמָּה־לִּי, "why to me". **"I am full of the burnt offerings of rams, and the fat of fed beasts" → "I am sated with burnt offerings of rams and the fat of fatlings"** — שָׂבַע "be sated"; מְרִיאִים "fatlings". **"of bullocks, or of lambs, or of he goats" → "of bulls and lambs and he-goats"** — פָּרִים · כְּבָשִׂים · עַתּוּדִים joined by "and"; לֹא חָפָצְתִּי "I do not delight in" (perfect).
- **"When ye come to appear before me" → "When you come to appear before my face"** (v12) — Hebrew לֵרָאוֹת פָּנָי, nifal "to be seen" with "my face" (the pointing; a qal "to see my face" is often assumed). **"who hath required this at your hand, to tread my courts?" → "who has required this from your hand, the trampling of my courts?"** — בִּקֵּשׁ "seek, require"; רְמֹס "trampling" (infinitive).
- **"Bring no more vain oblations; incense is an abomination unto me" → "Do not again bring an empty grain offering; incense — it is an abomination to me"** (v13) — Hebrew לֹא תוֹסִיפוּ הָבִיא "do not add to bring" (literal form of the "again" idiom); מִנְחַת־שָׁוְא "a grain offering of emptiness"; קְטֹרֶת תּוֹעֵבָה הִיא לִי can also be read "it is incense of abomination to me" (הִיא of the offering). **"the new moons and sabbaths, the calling of assemblies, I cannot away with; it is iniquity, even the solemn meeting" → "New moon and Shabbat, the calling of a convocation — I cannot bear wickedness and solemn assembly"** — singulars; קְרֹא מִקְרָא "the calling of a convocation" (מִקְרָא as מִקְרָא קֹדֶשׁ "holy convocation"); לֹא־אוּכַל "I am not able": "bear" is supplied; אָוֶן "wickedness"; עֲצָרָה "solemn assembly" (Lev 23:36: "solemn" is customary English, not a word of the Hebrew).
- **"your appointed feasts" → "your appointed times"** (v14) — Hebrew מוֹעֲדֵיכֶם (not חַג "feast"). **"they are a trouble unto me" → "they have become a load upon me"** — טֹרַח, "load" (Deut 1:12). **"I am weary to bear them" → "I am weary of bearing"** — נִלְאֵיתִי נְשֹׂא; no "them".
- **"when ye spread forth your hands" → "when you spread out your palms"** (v15) — Hebrew כַּפֵּיכֶם "your palms". **"when ye make many prayers, I will not hear" → "when you multiply prayer, I am not hearing"** — תְפִלָּה singular; אֵינֶנִּי שֹׁמֵעַ, a participle. **"full of blood"** — Hebrew דָּמִים, plural "bloods" (blood shed).
- **"make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes" → "make yourselves clean; remove the evil of your doings from in front of my eyes"** (v16) — הִזַּכּוּ, hitpael of זָכָה "be pure"; מַעַלְלִים "doings"; מִנֶּגֶד "from in front of".
- **"seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow" → "seek judgment, lead the ruthless straight; judge the fatherless, contend for the widow"** (v17) — מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; אַשְּׁרוּ חָמוֹץ: אִשֵּׁר piel "lead straight" (Prov 4:14; 23:19; one root with "go straight"), חָמוֹץ only here, "ruthless" (from "be sour, harsh"); "the oppressed" reads the passive form חָמוּץ. Uncertain. רִיבוּ "contend (in law)".
- **"let us reason together" → "Come now, and let us reason together"** (v18) — Hebrew לְכוּ־נָא וְנִוָּכְחָה: נָא "now", the "and" kept; nifal of יכח (the root of הוֹכִיחַ "reprove, decide"): to argue a case with one another. **"though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow; though they be red like crimson, they shall be as wool" → "if your sins are like scarlet, as the snow they shall be white; if they are red like crimson, as wool they shall be"** — the Hebrew as it stands: two conditions (אִם "if") with plain imperfects, in the Hebrew order; there is no question word. Some take the lines as questions ("shall they be white as snow?") or as irony; that is a reading, not the text. שָׁנִים "scarlet" (plural); תּוֹלָע "crimson" (literally "worm", the source of the dye).
- **"If ye be willing and obedient" → "If you are willing and hear"** (v19) — Hebrew אִם־תֹּאבוּ וּשְׁמַעְתֶּם; שָׁמַע "hear" ("obey" is not a Hebrew word here). Hebrew order: "the good of the land you shall eat".
- **"But if ye refuse and rebel, ye shall be devoured with the sword" → "and if you refuse and rebel, by the sword you shall be eaten"** (v20) — וְאִם "and if"; מְרִיתֶם, מָרָה "rebel"; חֶרֶב תְּאֻכְּלוּ, literally "sword you shall be eaten": the same verb as "eat" in v19, the preposition supplied.
- **"How is the faithful city become an harlot! it was full of judgment; righteousness lodged in it; but now murderers" → "How she has become a prostitute, the faithful town, full of judgment! Righteousness used to lodge in her, and now murderers"** (v21) — Hebrew זוֹנָה "prostitute"; קִרְיָה "town" (beside עִיר "city", v26); יָלִין, imperfect of what used to be; וְעַתָּה "and now".
- **"thy wine mixed with water" → "your drink cut with water"** (v22) — Hebrew סָבְאֵךְ "your drink" (strong drink; as סֹבֵא "drunkard"); מָהוּל only here, "cut, weakened" (uncertain).
- **"Thy princes are rebellious … every one loveth gifts, and followeth after rewards" → "Your princes are stubborn … every one of them loves a bribe and pursues rewards"** (v23) — סוֹרְרִים "stubborn" (Deut 21:18); שֹׁחַד "bribe"; רֹדֵף "pursues"; שַׁלְמֹנִים only here, "rewards" (payments; from שׁלם "repay"). **"neither doth the cause of the widow come unto them" → "and the case of the widow does not come to them"** — רִיב "case".
- **"Ah, I will ease me of mine adversaries" → "Woe! I will take comfort from my adversaries"** (v24) — Hebrew הוֹי, the same word as v4, kept as "Woe" (here it opens Yahweh's own resolve: the cry of grief and threat); אֶנָּחֵם, nifal of נחם "be comforted" (of a loss), with מִן "from": relief that comes from the adversaries' end. **"avenge me of mine enemies" → "avenge myself on my enemies"** — וְאִנָּקְמָה מֵאוֹיְבַי, literally "from my enemies"; צַר "adversary", אֹיֵב "enemy".
- **"purely purge away thy dross, and take away all thy tin" → "refine as with lye your dross, and remove all your tin"** (v25) — צָרַף "refine"; כַּבֹּר "as with lye" (בֹּר "potash"; the same letters are "cleanness" in 2 Sam 22:21: another sense); בְּדִילַיִךְ "your tin" (Num 31:22). **"turn my hand upon thee" → "turn my hand back upon you"** — וְאָשִׁיבָה, hifil of שׁוּב.
- **"thou shalt be called, The city of righteousness, the faithful city" → "you shall be called the city of righteousness, the faithful town"** (v26) — עִיר "city", קִרְיָה "town": two words, as v21.
- **"Zion shall be redeemed with judgment, and her converts with righteousness" → "Zion shall be ransomed with judgment, and those of her who return, with righteousness"** (v27) — Hebrew תִּפָּדֶה, פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem"); שָׁבֶיהָ "her returning ones" (from שׁוּב; "her captives" if read from שָׁבָה).
- **"And the destruction of the transgressors and of the sinners shall be together, and they that forsake the LORD shall be consumed" → "And the breaking of transgressors and sinners shall be together, and those who forsake Yahweh shall come to an end"** (v28) — Hebrew וְשֶׁבֶר, a noun ("breaking"), without a verb: "shall be" is supplied; פֹּשְׁעִים "transgressors"; יִכְלוּ, כָּלָה qal "come to an end".
- **"For they shall be ashamed of the oaks which ye have desired, and ye shall be confounded for the gardens" → "For they shall be ashamed because of the terebinths that you desired, and you shall be abashed because of the gardens"** (v29) — the Hebrew moves from "they" to "you", kept; מֵאֵילִים "because of the terebinths" (the tree of v30, אֵלָה); חָמַד "desire" (the "covet" verb, here of a thing desired in worship); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", חָפֵר "be abashed" (another root than "dig").
- **"as an oak whose leaf fadeth" → "like a terebinth whose leaf withers"** (v30) — Hebrew אֵלָה "terebinth" (אֵלוֹן is "oak"); נֹבֶלֶת "withers".
- **"the strong shall be as tow, and the maker of it as a spark" → "the strong one shall become tow, and his work a spark"** (v31) — Hebrew הָיָה לְ "become"; הֶחָסֹן "the strong one"; פֹּעֲלוֹ is pointed "his work" ("its maker" reads the letters as a participle). **"and none shall quench them" → "and there is none who quenches"** — וְאֵין מְכַבֶּה; no object.

### Notes
- **Superscription (v1)** — four kings = c. 740–686 BC.
- **v9** — שָׂרִיד "survivor"; Rom 9:29 quotes this verse in the Greek wording.
- **Qedosh Yisrael** — "the Holy One of Israel" (v4) is Isaiah's title for Yahweh through the book.
