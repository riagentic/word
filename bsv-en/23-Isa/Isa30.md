# Isaiah 30

[1] "Woe, stubborn sons," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"to make counsel, and not from me,
and to pour out a libation, and not of my spirit,
in order to add sin upon sin;

[2] who walk to go down to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and my mouth they have not asked,
to seek shelter in the strong place of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧,
and to take refuge in the shade of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧!

[3] And the strong place of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ shall be to you for shame,
and the taking refuge in the shade of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ for dishonor.

[4] For his princes are in ⟦zoan|Tzoan|Zoan⟧,
and his messengers reach ⟦hanes|Chanes|Hanes⟧.

[5] Everyone has been put to shame
over a people that do not profit them,
not for help and not for profit,
but for shame and also for reproach."

[6] The burden of the beasts of the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧.

In a land of distress and anguish,
from where come lioness and lion,
viper and flying ⟦saraf|saraf|fiery serpent⟧,
they carry their wealth on the shoulder of young donkeys,
and their treasures on the hump of camels,
to a people that do not profit.

[7] And ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|the Egyptians⟧ help with vapor and emptiness;
therefore I have called this one
"⟦rahab-monster|Rahav|Rahab⟧, they are a sitting."

[8] Now, come, write it on a tablet with them, and inscribe it on a book, and let it be for a latter day, perpetually, forever.

[9] For a people of rebellion it is, lying sons, sons who are not willing to hear the ⟦torah|Torah|instruction⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;

[10] who say to the seers, "You shall not see," and to the gazers, "You shall not behold right things for us; speak to us smooth things, behold deceptions;

[11] turn aside from the way, turn away from the path, make the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ cease from before us."

[12] Therefore thus says the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

"Because you reject this word,
and have trusted in extortion and in what is devious,
and have leaned on it,

[13] therefore this iniquity shall be to you
like a breach falling, bulging out in a lofty wall,
whose breaking comes in an instant, suddenly;

[14] and he shall break it like the breaking of a potters' jar,
beaten in pieces, he does not spare;
and there shall not be found among its pieces a shard
to take fire from the hearth,
or to scoop water from the pool."

[15] For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

"In returning and rest you shall be saved;
in quietness and in trust shall be your might."

And you were not willing,

[16] and you said,
"No, for on horses we will flee";
therefore you shall flee;
"and on the swift we will ride";
therefore your pursuers shall be swift.

[17] One thousand from before the rebuke of one,
from before the rebuke of five you shall flee,
until you are left like a pole on the top of the mountain,
and like a banner on the hill.

[18] And therefore ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ awaits, to show you favor,
and therefore he is exalted, to have compassion on you.
For an ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of judgment is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
happy are all who await him.

[19] For a people shall dwell in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; you shall surely not weep. He will surely show you favor at the voice of your cry; when he hears it, he answers you.

[20] And ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ will give you bread of distress and water of oppression; and your teacher shall no more be hidden in a corner, and your eyes shall be seeing your teacher.

[21] And your ears shall hear a word behind you, saying, "This is the way; walk in it," when you go to the right and when you go to the left.

[22] And you shall defile the overlay of your carved images of silver and the covering of your molten image of gold. You shall strew them like one unwell; "Go out!" you shall say to it.

[23] And he will give the rain of your seed, with which you sow the ground, and bread, the yield of the ground, and it shall be fat and rich. Your livestock shall feed in that day in a broad pasture,

[24] and the cattle and the young donkeys that work the ground shall eat salted fodder, which one winnows with the shovel and with the fork.

[25] And there shall be on every high mountain and on every lifted-up hill streams, courses of water, in the day of the great slaughter, when towers fall.

[26] And the light of the moon shall be like the light of the sun, and the light of the sun shall be sevenfold, like the light of seven days, in the day when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ binds up the breaking of his people and heals the wound of his blow.

[27] Behold, the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ comes from afar,
burning is his anger, and heavy the rising smoke;
his lips are full of indignation,
and his tongue is like a devouring fire;

[28] and his breath is like an overflowing torrent that reaches to the neck,
to sift nations with a sieve of emptiness,
and a halter that makes them wander on the jaws of peoples.

[29] The song shall be to you as in the night when a feast is sanctified,
and gladness of heart, as one who walks with the flute
to come into the mountain of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to the Rock of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[30] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will make heard the majesty of his voice,
and the coming down of his arm he will show,
in the rage of anger and the flame of a devouring fire,
shattering and downpour and hailstones.

[31] For at the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ shall be dismayed;
with the rod he will strike.

[32] And every passing of the appointed staff
that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lays upon him shall be with timbrels and with lyres;
and in battles of waving he fights against them.

[33] For ⟦topheth|Tofteh|Topheth⟧ has long been set in order;
it also is made ready for the king;
he has made it deep, he has made it wide;
its pyre is fire and wood in abundance;
the breath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, like a torrent of sulfur, burns in it.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v18) — "an *Elohim* of judgment is Yahweh."
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel" → "Lord Yahweh, the Holy One of Israel"** (v15) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words (token `adonai-yhwh`).
- **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v20) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **Layout** — v1–7, 12–18 and 27–33 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v8–11, 19–26 stay prose; v15 keeps "And you were not willing" before v16 as the earlier text has it.
- **"Woe to the rebellious children" → "Woe, stubborn sons"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹי בָּנִים סוֹרְרִים: הוֹי "Woe"; בָּנִים "sons" (v1, 9: not "children"); סוֹרֵר "stubborn" (as Deut 21:18). **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** — נְאֻם יְהוָה, the one wording. **"that take counsel, but not of me" → "to make counsel, and not from me"** — לַעֲשׂוֹת עֵצָה וְלֹא מִנִּי. **"that cover with a covering" → "to pour out a libation"** — Hebrew וְלִנְסֹךְ מַסֵּכָה, a verb and its own noun of נָסַךְ "pour": the pouring that seals a treaty is the common reading (מַסֵּכָה is "molten image" in v22; "libation" is used here to keep it from נֶסֶךְ "drink offering"). The other נָסַךְ "weave" gives "to weave a covering" (KJV "cover with a covering"; מַסֵּכָה "covering" in Isa 25:7; 28:20); the Hebrew allows both. **"that they may add" → "in order to add"** — לְמַעַן + infinitive.
- **"That walk to go down into Egypt" → "who walk to go down to Egypt"** (v2) — הַהֹלְכִים לָרֶדֶת. **"have not asked at my mouth" → "my mouth they have not asked"** — וּפִי לֹא שָׁאָלוּ, in that order. **"to strengthen themselves in the strength of Pharaoh" → "to seek shelter in the strong place of Pharaoh"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew לָעוֹז בְּמָעוֹז, the verb עוּז "take shelter" and its noun מָעוֹז "strong place". **"to trust in the shadow of Egypt" → "to take refuge in the shade of Egypt"** (v2, 3) — חָסָה בְּ "take refuge in"; צֵל "shade" (as of protection, Num 14:9).
- **"Therefore shall the strength of Pharaoh be your shame" → "And the strong place of Pharaoh shall be to you for shame"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהָיָה … לְבֹשֶׁת, וְ "And". **"the trust in the shadow of Egypt your confusion" → "the taking refuge in the shade of Egypt for dishonor"** — הֶחָסוּת, noun of חָסָה; כְּלִמָּה "dishonor".
- **"his ambassadors came to Hanes" → "his messengers reach Hanes"** (v4) — Hebrew מַלְאָכָיו … יַגִּיעוּ, an imperfect. "His" is not named (the king of Judah, or Pharaoh).
- **"They were all ashamed of a people that could not profit them" → "Everyone has been put to shame over a people that do not profit them"** (v5) — the qere הֹבִישׁ, hifil of בּוֹשׁ; the written form הבאיש would be "has made a stink" (as the הִבְאִישׁ of Gen 34:30). **"nor be an help nor profit, but a shame, and also a reproach" → "not for help and not for profit, but for shame and also for reproach"** — each with לְ "for"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach".
- **"The burden of the beasts of the south" → "The burden of the beasts of the Negev"** (v6) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא, literally "what is lifted up" ("oracle" in the earlier text); נֶגֶב as a region keeps its token. **"the land of trouble and anguish" → "a land of distress and anguish"** — צָרָה וְצוּקָה. **"the young and old lion" → "lioness and lion"** — Hebrew לָבִיא וָלַיִשׁ: לָבִיא "lioness" (gender not certain), לַיִשׁ another word for "lion" (kept apart from אֲרִי); מֵהֶם "from them", that is, from that land. **"the viper and fiery flying serpent" → "viper and flying saraf"** — אֶפְעֶה "viper"; שָׂרָף "saraf" (token: the burning serpent of Num 21:6, as Isa 14:29). **"they will carry their riches upon the shoulders of young asses" → "they carry their wealth on the shoulder of young donkeys"** — כֶּתֶף "shoulder" (singular), עַיִר "young donkey", חַיִל "wealth". **"bunches of camels" → "the hump of camels"** — דַּבֶּשֶׁת.
- **"For the Egyptians shall help in vain, and to no purpose" → "And the Egyptians help with vapor and emptiness"** (v7) — Hebrew וּמִצְרַיִם הֶבֶל וָרִיק יַעְזֹרוּ: the verb is plural, so the token `egyptians` (the Hebrew word is "Egypt"); הֶבֶל "vapor" (as "vanity", Eccl 1:2), רִיק "emptiness". **"therefore have I cried concerning this, Their strength is to sit still" → "therefore I have called this one 'Rahab, they are a sitting'"** — Hebrew לָכֵן קָרָאתִי לָזֹאת רַהַב הֵם שָׁבֶת: רַהַב, the sea monster, a name for Egypt (Ps 87:4; Isa 51:9; Job 26:12), token `rahab-monster`; הֵם שָׁבֶת "they are a sitting", שֶׁבֶת the infinitive of יָשַׁב "sit" (or a noun of שָׁבַת "cease"). The sense is "Rahab who sits still": the great monster that does nothing. The words are hard; some divide them otherwise ("Rahab the silenced", from memory); the MT kept.
- **"go, write it before them in a table, and note it in a book" → "come, write it on a tablet with them, and inscribe it on a book"** (v8) — Hebrew בּוֹא כָתְבָהּ עַל־לוּחַ אִתָּם וְעַל־סֵפֶר חֻקָּהּ: בּוֹא "come", אִתָּם "with them" (in their presence, or beside them), חָקַק "inscribe". **"that it may be for the time to come for ever and ever" → "and let it be for a latter day, perpetually, forever"** — Hebrew וּתְהִי לְיוֹם אַחֲרוֹן לָעַד עַד־עוֹלָם: לָעַד "perpetually", as pointed; read לְעֵד "for a witness" by the versions and the earlier text (from memory); the MT kept.
- **"a rebellious people, lying children, children that will not hear the law of the LORD" → "a people of rebellion it is, lying sons, sons who are not willing to hear the torah of Yahweh"** (v9) — Hebrew עַם מְרִי הוּא: מֶרִי "rebellion"; בָּנִים כֶּחָשִׁים "lying sons"; לֹא־אָבוּ "are not willing" (אָבָה).
- **"Which say to the seers, See not; and to the prophets, Prophesy not unto us right things" → "who say to the seers, 'You shall not see,' and to the gazers, 'You shall not behold right things for us'"** (v10) — Hebrew לָרֹאִים … וְלַחֹזִים: רֹאֶה "seer" and חֹזֶה "gazer" stand side by side (the חֹזֶה is the "seer" of 29:10; here the other word is kept apart), each with its own verb, רָאָה "see", חָזָה "behold". The earlier "prophets" is not in the Hebrew. לֹא + imperfect "You shall not". **"prophesy deceits" → "behold deceptions"** — חֲזוּ מַהֲתַלּוֹת (of הֵתֵל "cheat"). **"smooth things"** — חֲלָקוֹת.
- **"Get you out of the way, turn aside out of the path" → "turn aside from the way, turn away from the path"** (v11) — סוּרוּ … הַטּוּ. **"cause the Holy One of Israel to cease from before us" → "make the Holy One of Israel cease from before us"** — הַשְׁבִּיתוּ מִפָּנֵינוּ.
- **"Because ye despise this word, and trust in oppression and perverseness, and stay thereon" → "Because you reject this word, and have trusted in extortion and in what is devious, and have leaned on it"** (v12) — מָאַס "reject"; עֹשֶׁק "extortion" (לַחַץ is "oppression", v20); נָלוֹז "devious"; נִשְׁעַן עַל "lean on".
- **"as a breach ready to fall, swelling out in a high wall" → "like a breach falling, bulging out in a lofty wall"** (v13) — כְּפֶרֶץ נֹפֵל נִבְעֶה בְּחוֹמָה נִשְׂגָּבָה. **"suddenly at an instant" → "in an instant, suddenly"** — פִּתְאֹם לְפֶתַע, in that order (the reverse of 29:5). **"breaking"** — שֶׁבֶר.
- **"And he shall break it as the breaking of the potters' vessel that is broken in pieces; he shall not spare" → "and he shall break it like the breaking of a potters' jar, beaten in pieces, he does not spare"** (v14) — the MT points וּשְׁבָרָהּ as the verb "and he shall break it"; נֵבֶל "jar"; כָּתוּת "beaten in pieces"; חָמַל "spare". **"in the bursting of it" → "among its pieces"** — מְכִתָּתוֹ. **"a sherd to take fire from the hearth, or to take water withal out of the pit" → "a shard to take fire from the hearth, or to scoop water from the pool"** — חֶרֶשׂ "shard" (earthenware); חָשַׂף "scoop off"; גֶּבֶא "pool". The earlier text's "smashed so ruthlessly" was a paraphrase.
- **"For thus saith" → "For thus says"** (v15) — the one wording. **"In returning and rest shall ye be saved"** — בְּשׁוּבָה וָנַחַת; נַחַת "rest" (of נוּחַ). **"in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength" → "in quietness and in trust shall be your might"** — הַשְׁקֵט "being quiet"; בִּטְחָה "trust"; גְּבוּרָה "might". **"and ye would not" → "And you were not willing"** — אָבָה.
- **"No; for we will flee upon horses" → "No, for on horses we will flee"** (v16) — לֹא־כִי, then the Hebrew order; סוּס singular as a collective. **"We will ride upon the swift" → "on the swift we will ride"** — עַל־קַל; "steeds" is not written. The wordplay נָנוּס / תְּנוּסוּן ("flee") and קַל / יִקַּלּוּ ("swift") is kept.
- **"One thousand shall flee at the rebuke of one" → "One thousand from before the rebuke of one"** (v17) — Hebrew אֶלֶף אֶחָד מִפְּנֵי גַּעֲרַת אֶחָד: מִפְּנֵי "from before, from the face of"; the verb תָּנֻסוּ "you shall flee" stands once, in the second line. **"till ye be left as a beacon" → "until you are left like a pole"** — נוֹתַר "remain, be left"; תֹּרֶן "pole, mast". **"an ensign" → "a banner"** — נֵס.
- **"therefore will the LORD wait, that he may be gracious unto you" → "therefore Yahweh awaits, to show you favor"** (v18) — וְלָכֵן "And therefore" (twice); חִכָּה "await" (also חוֹכֵי "who await him"); חָנַן "show favor". **"therefore will he be exalted, that he may have mercy upon you" → "he is exalted, to have compassion on you"** — יָרוּם; רִחֵם "have compassion". **"a God of judgment"** — אֱלֹהֵי מִשְׁפָּט, in the Hebrew order. **"blessed are all they that wait for him" → "happy are all who await him"** — אַשְׁרֵי "happy".
- **"thou shalt weep no more" → "you shall surely not weep"** (v19) — Hebrew בָּכוֹ לֹא־תִבְכֶּה, literally "weeping you shall not weep". **"he will be very gracious unto thee" → "He will surely show you favor"** — חָנוֹן יָחְנְךָ, literally "showing favor he will show you favor". **"at the voice of thy cry"** — לְקוֹל זַעֲקֶךָ. **"when he shall hear it" → "when he hears it, he answers you"** — עָנָךְ, a perfect. The people (singular) is addressed from here as "you" singular.
- **"And though the Lord give you the bread of adversity" → "And Adonai will give you bread of distress"** (v20) — Hebrew וְנָתַן, "and will give" (not "though"); צָר "distress", לַחַץ "oppression". **"yet shall not thy teachers be removed into a corner any more" → "your teacher shall no more be hidden in a corner"** — יִכָּנֵף, only here, of כָּנָף "wing, corner": uncertain; the verb is singular; מוֹרֶיךָ can be read "your teachers" (plural form) or "your teacher" (singular with the verb). Whether Yahweh is meant is not said: no capital. **"thine eyes shall see thy teachers" → "your eyes shall be seeing your teacher"** — וְהָיוּ … רֹאוֹת, "shall be seeing".
- **"when ye turn to the right hand, and when ye turn to the left" → "when you go to the right and when you go to the left"** (v21) — כִּי תַאֲמִינוּ וְכִי תַשְׂמְאִילוּ, two verbs made from "right" and "left"; plural here.
- **"Ye shall defile also the covering of thy graven images of silver" → "And you shall defile the overlay of your carved images of silver"** (v22) — צִפּוּי "overlay"; פְּסִילִים "carved images". **"the ornament of thy molten images of gold" → "the covering of your molten image of gold"** — אֲפֻדָּה "covering" (of אֵפוֹד; Exod 28:8 the band of the ephod); מַסֵּכָה "molten image", singular. **"thou shalt cast them away as a menstruous cloth" → "You shall strew them like one unwell"** — זָרָה "strew"; דָוָה "unwell" (in her monthly impurity); "cloth" is not written. **"Get thee hence" → "'Go out!'"** — צֵא. The Hebrew passes from "you" plural to "you" singular, and from "them" to "it", as kept.
- **"Then shall he give the rain of thy seed" → "And he will give the rain of your seed"** (v23) — וְנָתַן. **"bread of the increase of the earth" → "bread, the yield of the ground"** — תְּבוּאַת הָאֲדָמָה. **"fat and plenteous" → "fat and rich"** — דָשֵׁן וְשָׁמֵן, two words for "fat" (as Deut 31:20; 32:15). **"thy cattle feed" → "your livestock shall feed"** — מִקְנֶה; רָעָה of animals "feed". **"in large pastures" → "in a broad pasture"** — כַּר נִרְחָב, singular; כַּר "pasture" (in Deut 32:14 the same letters are "lamb").
- **"The oxen likewise and the young asses that ear the ground" → "and the cattle and the young donkeys that work the ground"** (v24) — אֲלָפִים "cattle"; עֲיָרִים "young donkeys"; עָבַד of the ground "work". **"clean provender" → "salted fodder"** — בְּלִיל חָמִיץ (חָמִיץ, only here: "salted" or "soured", uncertain). **"which hath been winnowed with the shovel and with the fan" → "which one winnows with the shovel and with the fork"** — זֹרֶה, active participle (the earlier passive is not written); זָרָה here of grain "winnow".
- **"upon every high hill, rivers and streams of waters" → "on every lifted-up hill streams, courses of water"** (v25) — נִשָּׂאָה "lifted up"; פְּלָגִים "streams", יִבְלֵי־מָיִם "courses of water".
- **"in the day that the LORD bindeth up the breach of his people, and healeth the stroke of their wound" → "binds up the breaking of his people and heals the wound of his blow"** (v26) — שֶׁבֶר "breaking"; מַחַץ "wound" (of מָחַץ "strike through"); מַכָּה "blow", "his blow" as the Hebrew has it. לְבָנָה "the white one" and חַמָּה "the hot one" are the poetic words for moon and sun.
- **"burning with his anger, and the burden thereof is heavy" → "burning is his anger, and heavy the rising smoke"** (v27) — Hebrew בֹּעֵר אַפּוֹ וְכֹבֶד מַשָּׂאָה: אַף "anger"; מַשָּׂאָה, only here, "what rises up" (of smoke, or "uplifting": uncertain). **"indignation"** — זַעַם.
- **"his breath, as an overflowing stream, shall reach to the midst of the neck" → "his breath is like an overflowing torrent that reaches to the neck"** (v28) — רוּחַ "breath" (also "spirit, wind"); נַחַל of the water "torrent"; יֶחֱצֶה, literally "divides (in half)", that is, reaches the middle of a man. **"with the sieve of vanity" → "with a sieve of emptiness"** — נָפַת שָׁוְא. **"a bridle in the jaws of the people, causing them to err" → "a halter that makes them wander on the jaws of peoples"** — רֶסֶן "halter"; מַתְעֶה, hifil of תָּעָה "make wander"; עַמִּים "peoples". The verse has no verb "place".
- **"Ye shall have a song, as in the night when a holy solemnity is kept" → "The song shall be to you as in the night when a feast is sanctified"** (v29) — Hebrew הַשִּׁיר יִהְיֶה לָכֶם כְּלֵיל הִתְקַדֶּשׁ־חָג, the article on "song"; חַג "feast". **"goeth with a pipe" → "walks with the flute"** — חָלִיל. **"the mighty One of Israel" → "the Rock of Israel"** — צוּר, with a capital where it names Yahweh.
- **"his glorious voice" → "the majesty of his voice"** (v30) — הוֹד "majesty". **"the lighting down of his arm" → "the coming down of his arm"** — נַחַת, of נָחַת "come down" (not the "rest" of v15, which is of נוּחַ). **"with the indignation of his anger" → "in the rage of anger"** — בְּזַעַף אַף; "his" is not written. **"scattering, and tempest, and hailstones" → "shattering and downpour and hailstones"** — נֶפֶץ (of נָפַץ "shatter"), זֶרֶם "downpour", אֶבֶן בָּרָד.
- **"through the voice of the LORD shall the Assyrian be beaten down" → "at the voice of Yahweh Assyria shall be dismayed"** (v31) — יֵחַת, of חָתַת "be dismayed" (literally "shattered"). **"which smote with a rod" → "with the rod he will strike"** — בַּשֵּׁבֶט יַכֶּה; whether Yahweh or Assyria strikes is not said.
- **"in every place where the grounded staff shall pass" → "every passing of the appointed staff"** (v32) — Hebrew כֹּל מַעֲבַר מַטֵּה מוּסָדָה: מַעֲבַר "passing"; מוּסָדָה of יָסַד "found, appoint" (uncertain; some read מוּסָרָה "of discipline", from memory). **"with tabrets and harps" → "with timbrels and with lyres"** — תֹּף, כִּנּוֹר. **"in battles of shaking will he fight with it" → "in battles of waving he fights against them"** — תְּנוּפָה "waving" (the word of the wave offering); נִלְחַם בְּ "fight against"; the qere בָּם "them", the written form בה "her, it".
- **"For Tophet is ordained of old" → "For Topheth has long been set in order"** (v33) — Hebrew כִּי־עָרוּךְ מֵאֶתְמוּל תָּפְתֶּה: עָרַךְ "set in order"; מֵאֶתְמוּל, literally "from yesterday" (= for some time). תָּפְתֶּה, only here, a longer form of תֹּפֶת (the burning place in the valley of Ben Hinnom, 2 Kgs 23:10; Jer 7:31), token `topheth` with the form as printed. **"yea, for the king it is prepared" → "it also is made ready for the king"** — Hebrew גַּם־הִיא, the qere (the written form הוא is the masculine); לַמֶּלֶךְ "for the king", pointed so; the same letters spell Molech (Lev 18:21): the play is likely, the MT kept. **"he hath made it deep and large" → "he has made it deep, he has made it wide"** — two verbs. **"the pile thereof is fire and much wood" → "its pyre is fire and wood in abundance"** — מְדֻרָה "pyre". **"the breath of the LORD, like a stream of brimstone, doth kindle it" → "like a torrent of sulfur, burns in it"** — נְשָׁמָה "breath" (another word than רוּחַ in v28: the same English, note); גָּפְרִית "sulfur"; בֹּעֲרָה בָּהּ "burns in it".

### Notes
- **v7** — Rahab, the sea monster, as a name for Egypt (Ps 87:4; Isa 51:9).
- **v33** — Topheth: the burning place in the valley of Ben Hinnom (2 Kgs 23:10; Jer 7:31–32).
