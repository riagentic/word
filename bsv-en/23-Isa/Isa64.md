# Isaiah 64

[1] Oh that you had torn the heavens, had come down,
that from your face mountains quaked —

[2] as when fire kindles brushwood,
fire makes water boil —
to make your name known to your adversaries;
from your face nations would tremble.

[3] When you did fearsome things that we did not wait for,
you came down, from your face mountains quaked.

[4] And from of old they have not heard,
they have not given ear;
an eye has not seen ⟦elohim|an Elohim|a God⟧ besides you,
who acts for him who awaits him.

[5] You have met him who exults and does righteousness;
in your ways they remember you.
Behold, you were wrathful, and we sinned;
in them forever, and we shall be saved.

[6] And we became like the unclean, all of us,
and like a menstrual garment all our righteous acts;
and we withered like the leaf, all of us,
and our iniquity, like the wind, carries us away.

[7] And there is none who calls on your name,
who rouses himself to hold fast to you;
for you have hidden your face from us,
and you melted us by the hand of our iniquity.

[8] And now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you are our Father;
we are the clay, and you are our potter,
and the work of your hand are we all.

[9] Do not be wrathful, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, exceedingly,
and do not remember iniquity forever.
Behold, look, please: your people are we all.

[10] Your holy cities have become a wilderness;
⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ has become a wilderness,
⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ a desolation.

[11] Our holy and beautiful house,
where our fathers praised you,
has become a burning of fire,
and all our desirable things have become a ruin.

[12] At these will you restrain yourself, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧?
Will you be silent, and afflict us exceedingly?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved; no "O" before the name (v8, 9, 12): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order, as the existing layout has it.
- **"Oh that thou wouldest rend the heavens, that thou wouldest come down, that the mountains might flow down at thy presence" → "Oh that you had torn the heavens, had come down, that from your face mountains quaked"** (v1) — Hebrew לוּא קָרַעְתָּ … יָרַדְתָּ, לוּ with perfects: a wish ("if only you had torn"), commonly taken of the future ("would tear"); "that" is supplied. מִפָּנֶיךָ "from your face" (v1, 2, 3). הָרִים without article. נָזֹלּוּ is pointed as from זָלַל "quake" (nifal; also v3); the same consonants read from נָזַל are "flowed" (Judg 5:5 נָזְלוּ, pointed otherwise): the pointing is followed, "flow down" (KJV) in Changes only.
- **"As when the melting fire burneth, the fire causeth the waters to boil" → "as when fire kindles brushwood, fire makes water boil"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּקְדֹחַ אֵשׁ הֲמָסִים: קָדַח "kindle"; הֲמָסִים only here, "brushwood" (uncertain); תִּבְעֶה "makes boil". **"that the nations may tremble at thy presence" → "from your face nations would tremble"** — יִרְגָּזוּ, רָגַז "tremble" (as 5:25), no "that".
- **"terrible things which we looked not for: thou camest down" → "fearsome things that we did not wait for, you came down"** (v3) — Hebrew נוֹרָאוֹת "fearsome things"; לֹא נְקַוֶּה, קִוָּה "wait for". The last words are those of v1.
- **"For since the beginning of the world men have not heard, nor perceived by the ear, neither hath the eye seen, O God, beside thee, what he hath prepared for him that waiteth for him" → "And from of old they have not heard, they have not given ear; an eye has not seen an Elohim besides you, who acts for him who awaits him"** (v4) — Hebrew וּמֵעוֹלָם "and from of old"; לֹא־שָׁמְעוּ, a plural with no subject ("men" is not written); הֶאֱזִינוּ "given ear"; עַיִן without article. אֱלֹהִים זוּלָתְךָ may be the object ("an Elohim besides you") or an address ("Elohim, besides you"), with יַעֲשֶׂה "he acts / will do" read as "what he will do" (KJV): the words allow both; the object reading is kept. לִמְחַכֵּה־לוֹ singular, חִכָּה "await". 1 Cor 2:9 has in Greek "what eye has not seen and ear has not heard … what God has prepared for those who love him"; the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"Thou meetest him that rejoiceth and worketh righteousness, those that remember thee in thy ways" → "You have met him who exults and does righteousness; in your ways they remember you"** (v5) — Hebrew פָּגַעְתָּ, a perfect; שָׂשׂ "exults" (שׂוּשׂ); יִזְכְּרוּךָ "they remember you", its own clause. **"thou art wroth; for we have sinned: in those is continuance, and we shall be saved" → "you were wrathful, and we sinned; in them forever, and we shall be saved"** — Hebrew קָצַפְתָּ "be wrathful"; וַנֶּחֱטָא "and we sinned" (no "for"); בָּהֶם עוֹלָם וְנִוָּשֵׁעַ, four words without a verb: "in them (your ways) is continuance" (KJV), or, with בָּהֶם taken of the sins, "in them we have been long, and shall we be saved?" (a question with no question word); the text is often changed (from memory). The words are given as they stand.
- **"But we are all as an unclean thing, and all our righteousnesses are as filthy rags" → "And we became like the unclean, all of us, and like a menstrual garment all our righteous acts"** (v6) — Hebrew וַנְּהִי "and we became"; כַטָּמֵא "like the unclean (one)"; בֶּגֶד עִדִּים, literally "a garment of periods": a cloth of the monthly flow; צִדְקֹתֵינוּ "our righteous acts" (as Judg 5:11). **"we all do fade as a leaf" → "we withered like the leaf"** — וַנָּבֶל, נָבֵל "wither", wayyiqtol. **"our iniquities, like the wind, have taken us away" → "our iniquity, like the wind, carries us away"** — עֲוֺנֵנוּ is singular with a plural verb יִשָּׂאֻנוּ (נָשָׂא "lift up, carry"); also v7.
- **"none that calleth upon thy name, that stirreth up himself to take hold of thee" → "none who calls on your name, who rouses himself to hold fast to you"** (v7) — Hebrew קוֹרֵא בְשִׁמְךָ; מִתְעוֹרֵר; הֶחֱזִיק בְּ "hold fast". **"and hast consumed us, because of our iniquities" → "and you melted us by the hand of our iniquity"** — וַתְּמוּגֵנוּ, from מוּג "melt" (MT kept; "and you delivered us", תְּמַגְּנֵנוּ, as the Septuagint is read, from memory); בְּיַד "by the hand of".
- **"But now" → "And now"** (v8) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה. **"thou our potter"** — יֹצְרֵנוּ, participle of יָצַר "form" (as 29:16); חֹמֶר "clay". **"we all are the work of thy hand" → "the work of your hand are we all"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"Be not wroth very sore … neither remember iniquity for ever: behold, see, we beseech thee, we are all thy people" → "Do not be wrathful … exceedingly, and do not remember iniquity forever. Behold, look, please: your people are we all"** (v9) — Hebrew עַד־מְאֹד "exceedingly" (also v12); לָעַד "forever"; הֵן "behold"; הַבֶּט־נָא "look, please".
- **"Thy holy cities are a wilderness"** (v10) — Hebrew עָרֵי קָדְשְׁךָ, "the cities of your holiness"; perfects "have become"; שְׁמָמָה "desolation".
- **"Our holy and our beautiful house … is burned up with fire: and all our pleasant things are laid waste" → "Our holy and beautiful house … has become a burning of fire, and all our desirable things have become a ruin"** (v11) — Hebrew בֵּית קָדְשֵׁנוּ וְתִפְאַרְתֵּנוּ, "the house of our holiness and our beauty" (תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty"); הָיָה לִשְׂרֵפַת אֵשׁ "has become a burning of fire"; מַחֲמַדִּים "desirable things" (as 2 Chr 36:19); חָרְבָּה singular, "a ruin".
- **"Wilt thou refrain thyself for these things … wilt thou hold thy peace, and afflict us very sore?" → "At these will you restrain yourself … Will you be silent, and afflict us exceedingly?"** (v12) — Hebrew הַעַל־אֵלֶּה; הִתְאַפֵּק "restrain oneself"; חָשָׁה "be silent"; עִנָּה "afflict".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 64:1 is the second half of the Hebrew 63:19; our 64:2–12 are the Hebrew 64:1–11.
- **v4** — taken up in 1 Cor 2:9.
- The chapter continues the communal lament of 63:7–19.
