# Isaiah 66

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"The heavens are my throne,
and the earth is the footstool of my feet;
where is the house that you would build for me,
and where is the place of my rest?

[2] And all these my hand has made,
and all these came to be," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
"And to this one I will look:
to the afflicted and stricken of spirit,
and who trembles at my word.

[3] He who slaughters the ox — one who strikes down a man;
he who sacrifices the sheep — one who breaks the neck of a dog;
he who offers up a grain offering — the blood of a pig;
he who makes a memorial of frankincense —
one who blesses wickedness.
They too have chosen their ways,
and in their detestable things their soul delights;

[4] I too will choose their caprices,
and their terrors I will bring on them;
because I called and there was none answering,
I spoke and they did not hear,
and they did what was evil in my eyes,
and what I did not delight in, they chose."

[5] Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, you who tremble at his word:
"Your brothers have said, who hate you,
who cast you out on account of my name,
'Let ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be glorified, that we may see your gladness';
but they shall be ashamed.

[6] A voice of tumult from the city!
A voice from the temple!
The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
repaying dealing to his enemies!

[7] Before she was in anguish
she bore;
before a pang came to her
she delivered a male.

[8] Who has heard such as this?
Who has seen such as these?
Is a land brought forth in anguish in one day?
Or is a nation born at one time?
For ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ was in anguish,
also she bore her sons.

[9] Shall I bring to the breaking forth and not cause to bear?"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
"or shall I, who cause to bear, shut it up?"
says your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] "Rejoice with ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and be glad in her,
all who love her;
exult with her with exultation,
all who mourn over her;

[11] so that you may suck and be sated
from the breast of her comforts;
so that you may drain and delight yourselves
from the abundance of her glory."

[12] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Behold, I am stretching out to her ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ like a river,
and like an overflowing torrent the glory of the nations, and you shall suck;
on the side you shall be carried,
and on knees you shall be dandled.

[13] As a man whom his mother comforts,
so I will comfort you;
and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ you shall be comforted.

[14] And you shall see, and your heart shall exult,
and your bones like vegetation shall bud;
and the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be known with his servants,
and he shall be indignant with his enemies.

[15] For behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall come in fire,
and like the tempest his chariots,
to return his anger in fury,
and his rebuke in flames of fire.

[16] For by fire ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ enters into judgment,
and by his sword, with all flesh;
and many shall be the slain of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] "Those who sanctify themselves and those who purify themselves for the gardens, after one in the midst, eaters of the flesh of the pig and the detestable thing and the mouse, together shall come to an end," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] "And I — their works and their thoughts — it is coming, to gather all the nations and the tongues; and they shall come and see my glory.

[19] And I will set among them a sign, and I will send out from them fugitives to the nations, Tarshish, Pul and Lud, drawers of the bow, ⟦tubal|Tuval|Tubal⟧ and ⟦javan|Yavan|Javan⟧, the far coastlands that have not heard my report and have not seen my glory; and they shall tell my glory among the nations.

[20] And they shall bring all your brothers from all the nations, an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, on horses and in chariots and in covered wagons and on mules and on dromedaries, to my holy mountain ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "as the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ bring the grain offering in a clean vessel to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[21] And also from them I will take for the priests, for the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] "For as the new heavens and the new earth
that I am making stand before me," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"so shall your seed and your name stand.

[23] And it shall be, from new moon to new moon,
and from ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ to ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧,
all flesh shall come to bow down before me,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[24] "And they shall go out and look
on the carcasses of the men who transgressed against me;
for their worm shall not die,
and their fire shall not be quenched,
and they shall be an abhorrence to all flesh."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew אֱלֹהַיִךְ, "your (feminine) Elohim": Zion is addressed.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order, as the existing layout has it; v17–21 stay prose.
- **"declares Yahweh" / "says Yahweh"** (v2, 17, 22 / v9, 20, 21, 23) — Hebrew נְאֻם־יְהוָה "declaration of Yahweh" (v22 had "says"); אָמַר / יֹאמַר יְהוָה "says Yahweh" (v9 has both forms, imperfect and perfect, one English).
- **"The heaven is my throne, and the earth is my footstool: where is the house that ye build unto me? and where is the place of my rest?" → "The heavens are my throne, and the earth is the footstool of my feet; where is the house that you would build for me, and where is the place of my rest?"** (v1) — Hebrew הַשָּׁמַיִם plural; הֲדֹם רַגְלָי "the footstool of my feet"; אֵי־זֶה "where (then)"; מָקוֹם מְנוּחָתִי, "a place, my resting place". Acts 7:49–50 quotes v1–2 in Greek.
- **"For all those things hath mine hand made, and all those things have been" → "And all these my hand has made, and all these came to be"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאֶת־כָּל־אֵלֶּה, the object first; וַיִּהְיוּ "and they came to be" ("and they are mine", as the Septuagint is read, from memory: not followed). **"but to this man will I look, even to him that is poor and of a contrite spirit, and trembleth at my word" → "And to this one I will look: to the afflicted and stricken of spirit, and who trembles at my word"** — הִבִּיט "look"; עָנִי "afflicted"; נְכֵה־רוּחַ "stricken of spirit" (from נכה "strike"; "contrite" is the sense taken); חָרֵד עַל "trembling at" (also v5 חָרֵד אֶל).
- **"He that killeth an ox is as if he slew a man; he that sacrificeth a lamb, as if he cut off a dog's neck; he that offereth an oblation, as if he offered swine's blood; he that burneth incense, as if he blessed an idol" → "He who slaughters the ox — one who strikes down a man; he who sacrifices the sheep — one who breaks the neck of a dog; he who offers up a grain offering — the blood of a pig; he who makes a memorial of frankincense — one who blesses wickedness"** (v3) — the Hebrew sets two participles side by side with no word between: "as if" (KJV) or "and also" ("he who slaughters the ox also strikes down a man") are readings; the dashes keep the pairs unexplained. שָׁחַט "slaughter"; הַשּׁוֹר "the ox"; מַכֵּה־אִישׁ "who strikes down a man" (killing is meant); הַשֶּׂה "the sheep" (not "lamb"); עָרַף "break the neck of"; מַעֲלֵה מִנְחָה "who offers up a grain offering"; מַזְכִּיר לְבֹנָה "who makes a memorial of frankincense" (hifil of זָכַר, the word of the memorial portion); אָוֶן "wickedness" ("idol" is a sense taken from it, as Beth-aven, Hos 4:15). **"Yea, they have chosen their own ways, and their soul delighteth in their abominations" → "They too have chosen their ways, and in their detestable things their soul delights"** — גַּם־הֵמָּה "they too" (as v4 גַּם־אֲנִי "I too"); no "own"; שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things".
- **"I also will choose their delusions, and will bring their fears upon them" → "I too will choose their caprices, and their terrors I will bring on them"** (v4) — Hebrew תַּעֲלֻלִים "caprices" (as 3:4); מְגוּרֹת "terrors" (as Ps 34:4). **"when I called, none did answer"** — וְאֵין עוֹנֶה "and there was none answering". The rest as 65:12.
- **"Your brethren that hated you, that cast you out for my name's sake, said, Let the LORD be glorified: but he shall appear to your joy, and they shall be ashamed" → "Your brothers have said, who hate you, who cast you out on account of my name, 'Let Yahweh be glorified, that we may see your gladness'; but they shall be ashamed"** (v5) — Hebrew אָמְרוּ first; שֹׂנְאֵיכֶם · מְנַדֵּיכֶם participles; לְמַעַן שְׁמִי "on account of my name" (the accents may also join it to what follows: "on account of my name let Yahweh be glorified"); יִכְבַּד, כָּבֵד "be heavy, be honored"; וְנִרְאֶה "and we will see / that we may see", the words of the mockers (MT; KJV "he shall appear", reading a passive, is not followed); שִׂמְחָה "gladness"; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed".
- **"A voice of noise from the city, a voice from the temple, a voice of the LORD that rendereth recompence to his enemies" → "A voice of tumult from the city! A voice from the temple! The voice of Yahweh, repaying dealing to his enemies!"** (v6) — Hebrew קוֹל three times ("voice"; also "sound", or "Hark!"); שָׁאוֹן "tumult"; הֵיכָל "temple"; מְשַׁלֵּם גְּמוּל "repaying dealing" (what they have dealt).
- **"Before she travailed, she brought forth; before her pain came, she was delivered of a man child" → "Before she was in anguish she bore; before a pang came to her she delivered a male"** (v7) — Hebrew חִיל "be in anguish" (lit. "writhe", of labor; also v8 twice); חֵבֶל "pang"; וְהִמְלִיטָה "and she delivered" (the וְ of the apodosis left out in English); זָכָר "male".
- **"shall the earth be made to bring forth in one day? or shall a nation be born at once? for as soon as Zion travailed, she brought forth her children" → "Is a land brought forth in anguish in one day? Or is a nation born at one time? For Zion was in anguish, also she bore her sons"** (v8) — Hebrew הֲיוּחַל, hofal of חִיל; אֶרֶץ "land"; פַּעַם אֶחָת "at one time"; כִּי־חָלָה גַּם־יָלְדָה "for she was in anguish, also she bore" ("as soon as" is the sense taken); בָּנֶיהָ "her sons".
- **"Shall I bring to the birth, and not cause to bring forth? … shall I cause to bring forth, and shut the womb?" → "Shall I bring to the breaking forth and not cause to bear? … or shall I, who cause to bear, shut it up?"** (v9) — Hebrew אַשְׁבִּיר, hifil of שָׁבַר, "bring to the breaking forth" (as מַשְׁבֵּר 37:3); הוֹלִיד "cause to bear" (of the father it is "beget": here God brings the birth about); עָצַר "shut up" ("the womb" and "it" are not written; "it" supplied).
- **"Rejoice ye with Jerusalem, and be glad with her … rejoice for joy with her" → "Rejoice with Jerusalem, and be glad in her … exult with her with exultation"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׂמַח "rejoice"; גִּיל "be glad"; שִׂישׂוּ … מָשׂוֹשׂ "exult … exultation" (as 65:18); no "O", no "you" in the address; הַמִּתְאַבְּלִים "who mourn".
- **"That ye may suck, and be satisfied with the breasts of her consolations; that ye may milk out, and be delighted with the abundance of her glory" → "so that you may suck and be sated from the breast of her comforts; so that you may drain and delight yourselves from the abundance of her glory"** (v11) — Hebrew לְמַעַן "so that"; יָנַק "suck" (also v12); שָׂבַע "be sated"; שֹׁד singular "breast"; תַּנְחוּמִים "comforts"; תָּמֹצּוּ "drain" (מָצַץ only here); הִתְעַנֵּג "delight oneself" (as 58:14); זִיז "abundance" (uncertain; also taken as "teat"; another sense than the זִיז "what moves" of Ps 50:11).
- **"Behold, I will extend peace to her like a river, and the glory of the Gentiles like a flowing stream: then shall ye suck, ye shall be borne upon her sides, and be dandled upon her knees" → "Behold, I am stretching out to her shalom like a river, and like an overflowing torrent the glory of the nations, and you shall suck; on the side you shall be carried, and on knees you shall be dandled"** (v12) — Hebrew נֹטֶה, participle, נָטָה "stretch out"; שָׁלוֹם token; נַחַל שׁוֹטֵף "an overflowing torrent" (נַחַל of the water itself); עַל־צַד "on the side" ("her" is not written, also "on knees"); תְּשָׁעֳשָׁעוּ, pulpal of שָׁעַע, "be dandled" (one root with שִׁעֲשַׁע "delight", Ps 94:19).
- **"As one whom his mother comforteth" → "As a man whom his mother comforts"** (v13) — Hebrew כְּאִישׁ "as a man"; נִחַם piel "comfort", pual "be comforted".
- **"your heart shall rejoice, and your bones shall flourish like an herb: and the hand of the LORD shall be known toward his servants, and his indignation toward his enemies" → "your heart shall exult, and your bones like vegetation shall bud; and the hand of Yahweh shall be known with his servants, and he shall be indignant with his enemies"** (v14) — Hebrew שָׂשׂ "exult"; דֶּשֶׁא "vegetation"; פָּרַח "bud"; אֶת "with" (twice); וְזָעַם a verb, "and he shall be indignant" (not the Num 23 "denounce": no speech is meant).
- **"For, behold, the LORD will come with fire, and with his chariots like a whirlwind, to render his anger with fury" → "For behold, Yahweh shall come in fire, and like the tempest his chariots, to return his anger in fury"** (v15) — Hebrew בָּאֵשׁ "in fire"; סוּפָה "tempest"; לְהָשִׁיב "to return" (= pay back); אַף "anger", חֵמָה "fury"; גְּעָרָה "rebuke"; לַהֲבֵי־אֵשׁ "flames of fire".
- **"For by fire and by his sword will the LORD plead with all flesh" → "For by fire Yahweh enters into judgment, and by his sword, with all flesh"** (v16) — Hebrew נִשְׁפָּט, a participle, nifal of שָׁפַט, "enter into judgment" (with אֶת "with"); the Hebrew order. **"the slain of the LORD shall be many"** — וְרַבּוּ, חָלָל "slain".
- **"They that sanctify themselves, and purify themselves in the gardens behind one tree in the midst, eating swine's flesh, and the abomination, and the mouse" → "Those who sanctify themselves and those who purify themselves for the gardens, after one in the midst, eaters of the flesh of the pig and the detestable thing and the mouse"** (v17) — Hebrew אֶל־הַגַּנּוֹת "for (lit. to) the gardens"; qere אַחַת "one" (feminine; ketiv אחד masculine): "after one (woman) in the midst", perhaps one who leads the rite; "tree" is not written; שֶׁקֶץ "detestable thing"; עַכְבָּר "mouse"; יָסֻפוּ, סוּף "come to an end".
- **"For I know their works and their thoughts: it shall come, that I will gather all nations and tongues" → "And I — their works and their thoughts — it is coming, to gather all the nations and the tongues"** (v18) — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי מַעֲשֵׂיהֶם וּמַחְשְׁבֹתֵיהֶם, with no verb: "know" (KJV) and "the time" are not written; בָּאָה, feminine, "it is coming / it has come" (subject not named). The sentence stays broken, as the Hebrew.
- **"And I will set a sign among them, and I will send those that escape of them unto the nations" → "and I will send out from them fugitives to the nations"** (v19) — Hebrew שִׁלַּח piel "send out"; פְּלֵיטִים "fugitives" (those who have escaped). **"to Tarshish, Pul, and Lud" → "Tarshish, Pul and Lud"** — the names stand beside "the nations" without "to"; פּוּל MT (often read "Put", from memory). **"the isles afar off, that have not heard my fame" → "the far coastlands that have not heard my report"** — אִיִּים "coastlands"; שֵׁמַע "report"; הִגִּיד "tell".
- **"for an offering … and in litters … and upon swift beasts … as the children of Israel bring an offering in a clean vessel into the house of the LORD" → "an offering … and in covered wagons … and on dromedaries … as the sons of Israel bring the grain offering in a clean vessel to the house of Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew מִנְחָה twice: of the brothers brought, "an offering"; of what Israel brings, with the article, "the grain offering" (one word); צַבִּים "covered wagons" (as Num 7:3); כִּרְכָּרוֹת only here (dromedaries or swift beasts: uncertain); בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "the sons of Israel" (the token `israelite` replaced by `israel`); בֵּית יְהוָה without a preposition ("to" supplied).
- **"for priests and for Levites" → "for the priests, for the Levites"** (v21) — Hebrew לַכֹּהֲנִים לַלְוִיִּם, with the article and no "and" (many manuscripts have וְ, from memory).
- **"For as the new heavens and the new earth, which I will make, shall remain before me … so shall your seed and your name remain" → "For as the new heavens and the new earth that I am making stand before me … so shall your seed and your name stand"** (v22) — Hebrew הַשָּׁמַיִם הַחֳדָשִׁים וְהָאָרֶץ הַחֲדָשָׁה with articles (65:17 without); עֹשֶׂה participle "am making"; עָמַד "stand" (participle, then imperfect); זֶרַע "seed".
- **"from one new moon to another, and from one sabbath to another, shall all flesh come to worship before me" → "from new moon to new moon, and from Shabbat to Shabbat, all flesh shall come to bow down before me"** (v23) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "And it shall be"; מִדֵּי־חֹדֶשׁ בְּחָדְשׁוֹ, literally "as often as a new moon in its new moon" (and the same of the Shabbat); הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down".
- **"and look upon the carcases of the men that have transgressed against me" → "and look on the carcasses of the men who transgressed against me"** (v24) — Hebrew רָאָה בְּ "look on"; פֶּגֶר "carcass"; פָּשַׁע בְּ "transgress against" (as 1:2). **"and their fire"** — וְאִשָּׁם with "and". **"and they shall be an abhorring unto all flesh" → "an abhorrence"** — דֵּרָאוֹן, only here and Dan 12:2. Mark 9:48 takes up the words of the worm and the fire in Greek; the Hebrew is translated as it stands.

### Notes
- **v1–2** — quoted in Stephen's speech, Acts 7:49–50.
- **v24** — "their worm shall not die": taken up by Yeshua (Mark 9:48) of *Gehenna*.
- **In synagogue reading** — to avoid ending on judgment, v23 is repeated after v24.
- **Concludes Isaiah** — echoes Genesis: the heavens and the earth (new creation, 65:17; 66:22).
