# Mark 3

[1] And he entered again into the synagogue, and there was a man there having his hand withered.

[2] And they were watching him closely, whether he will heal him on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, that they might accuse him.

[3] And he says to the man having the withered hand, "Arise into the midst."

[4] And he says to them, "Is it permitted on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ to do good or to do evil, to save a soul or to kill?" But they were silent.

[5] And looking around at them with wrath, being grieved at the hardening of their heart, he says to the man, "Stretch out your hand." And he stretched it out, and his hand was restored.

[6] And going out, the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ immediately were giving counsel with the ⟦herodians|Herodianoi|Herodians⟧ against him, how they might destroy him.

[7] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ with his disciples withdrew to the sea; and a great multitude from ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ followed, and from ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧

[8] and from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and from ⟦idumea|Edom|Idumea⟧ and beyond the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and around ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, a great multitude, hearing how many things he was doing, came to him.

[9] And he told his disciples that a little boat should stay close by him because of the crowd, that they might not press upon him;

[10] for he healed many, so that as many as had scourges were falling upon him that they might touch him.

[11] And the unclean spirits, whenever they were beholding him, were falling down before him and crying out, saying, "You are the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[12] And he was rebuking them much, that they should not make him manifest.

[13] And he goes up into the mountain and calls to himself those whom he himself wanted, and they went away to him.

[14] And he made twelve, whom he also named apostles, that they might be with him and that he might send them to proclaim

[15] and to have authority to cast out the demons;

[16] and he made the twelve, and upon ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ he put the name ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧,

[17] and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the son of ⟦zebedee|Zebedaios|Zebedee⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the brother of ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ (and upon them he put the names Boanerges, which is, Sons of Thunder),

[18] and ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧ and ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ and ⟦bartholomew|Bar-Talmai|Bartholomew⟧ and ⟦matthew|Mattai|Matthew⟧ and ⟦thomas|Toma|Thomas⟧ and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the son of ⟦alphaeus|Alphaios|Alphaeus⟧ and ⟦thaddaeus|Thaddaios|Thaddaeus⟧ and ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ the Cananaean

[19] and ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ Iscariot, who also delivered him up.

[20] And he comes home; and the crowd comes together again, so that they were not able even to eat bread.

[21] And hearing it, his own people went out to take hold of him, for they were saying, "He is beside himself."

[22] And the scribes who came down from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ were saying, "He has Beelzebul," and, "By the ruler of the demons he casts out the demons."

[23] And calling them to himself, he was saying to them in parables, "How is Satan able to cast out Satan?

[24] And if a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom is not able to stand;

[25] and if a house is divided against itself, that house will not be able to stand;

[26] and if Satan has risen up against himself and has been divided, he is not able to stand, but has an end.

[27] But no one is able, entering into the house of the strong man, to plunder his goods, unless he first binds the strong man; and then he will plunder his house.

[28] Amen, I say to you that all things will be forgiven the sons of men, the sins and the blasphemies, as many as they may blaspheme;

[29] but whoever blasphemes against the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ has no forgiveness forever, but is guilty of an eternal sin" —

[30] because they were saying, "He has an unclean spirit."

[31] And his mother and his brothers come, and standing outside they sent to him, calling him.

[32] And a crowd was sitting around him, and they say to him, "Behold, your mother and your brothers are outside seeking you."

[33] And answering them he says, "Who is my mother and my brothers?"

[34] And looking around at those sitting in a circle around him, he says, "See, my mother and my brothers!

[35] **For whoever does the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, this one is my brother and sister and mother.**"

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v17, 18) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved. The name stands in the Greek only in v7.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv11, 35) — divine name retained.
- **"Simon he surnamed Peter" → "upon Simon he put the name *Kefa*"** (v16) — Greek ἐπέθηκεν ὄνομα τῷ Σίμωνι Πέτρον. Aramaic *Kefa* ("Rock") is Jesus's own name for Simon; Greek *Petros* is a translation of *Kefa*. The original Aramaic is preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v29) — Hebrew divine Spirit title preserved. Greek τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον, with the article.
- **"devils" → "demons"** (v15, 22) — Greek *daimonia*; see Mark 1.
- **"he saith … cometh … calleth" → presents kept** (v3, 4, 5, 13, 20, 31, 32, 33, 34) — Mark's present tense in narrative.
- **"which had a withered hand" → "having his hand withered"** (v1, 3) — Greek ἐξηραμμένην ἔχων τὴν χεῖρα, "having the hand dried up" (v1); ξηράν, "dry, withered" (v3). Editions (v1): εἰς WH (and the printed SBLGNT), "into a synagogue" ] + τὴν Treg NA28 RP, "into the synagogue" — 2–2, NA28 decides.
- **"they watched him, whether he would heal" → "they were watching him closely, whether he will heal"** (v2) — Greek παρετήρουν, imperfect, "watch from beside"; θεραπεύσει, a future.
- **"Stand forth" → "Arise into the midst"** (v3) — Greek ἔγειρε εἰς τὸ μέσον.
- **"Is it lawful to do good on the sabbath days, or to do evil? to save life" → "Is it permitted on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save a soul"** (v4) — Greek ἔξεστιν; ψυχὴν σῶσαι, ψυχή "soul" (a life). NA28 reads ἀγαθὸν ποιῆσαι for ἀγαθοποιῆσαι, with the same meaning.
- **"they held their peace" → "they were silent"** (v4) — Greek ἐσιώπων, imperfect.
- **"with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their hearts" → "with wrath, being grieved at the hardening of their heart"** (v5) — Greek μετ' ὀργῆς; συλλυπούμενος, "grieved (together) with"; πώρωσις, a growing hard, callous (not the word σκληρύνω "harden" of Heb 3:8); "heart" is singular.
- **"Stretch forth thine hand … was restored whole as the other" → "Stretch out your hand … was restored"** (v5) — Greek ἔκτεινον τὴν χεῖρα, "the hand" (NA28; WH Treg RP add σου, "your"). "whole as the other" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"took counsel" → "were giving counsel"** (v6) — Greek συμβούλιον ἐδίδουν, imperfect (RP ἐποίουν).
- **"a great multitude" kept** (v7, 8) — Greek πλῆθος, a different word from "crowd" (ὄχλος, v9, 20, 32).
- **"when they had heard what great things he did" → "hearing how many things he was doing"** (v8) — Greek ἀκούοντες ὅσα ἐποίει (NA28 RP; WH Treg ποιεῖ, "he is doing").
- **"a small ship should wait on him … lest they should throng him" → "a little boat should stay close by him … that they might not press upon him"** (v9) — Greek πλοιάριον προσκαρτερῇ αὐτῷ: προσκαρτερέω is "persevere" elsewhere, here of a boat kept constantly at hand. θλίβωσιν is the verb "afflict" (θλῖψις), here in its plain sense "press".
- **"he had healed many; insomuch that they pressed upon him … as many as had plagues" → "he healed many, so that as many as had scourges were falling upon him"** (v10) — Greek ἐθεράπευσεν; ἐπιπίπτειν, "fall upon"; μάστιγας, "whips, scourges": sufferings seen as blows.
- **"when they saw him, fell down before him" → "whenever they were beholding him, were falling down before him"** (v11) — Greek ὅταν αὐτὸν ἐθεώρουν, προσέπιπτον, repeated action.
- **"he straitly charged them that they should not make him known" → "he was rebuking them much, that they should not make him manifest"** (v12) — Greek πολλὰ ἐπετίμα, the verb of 1:25; φανερὸν ποιήσωσιν.
- **"calleth unto him whom he would: and they came unto him" → "calls to himself those whom he himself wanted, and they went away to him"** (v13) — Greek οὓς ἤθελεν αὐτός; ἀπῆλθον.
- **"he ordained twelve" → "he made twelve, whom he also named apostles"** (v14) — Greek ἐποίησεν, "made". Editions: δώδεκα alone Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) ] + οὓς καὶ ἀποστόλους ὠνόμασεν WH NA28 — 2–2, NA28 decides, so the clause stands in the verse.
- **"to have power to heal sicknesses, and to cast out devils" → "to have authority to cast out the demons"** (v15) — "to heal sicknesses" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"and he made the twelve"** (v16) — Greek καὶ ἐποίησεν τοὺς δώδεκα (WH NA28; not in Treg RP, nor in the KJV). In the Greek the names of v16–19 are objects of "he made"; "Simon" alone stands in the dative.
- **"he surnamed them Boanerges, which is, The sons of thunder" → "upon them he put the names Boanerges, which is, Sons of Thunder"** (v17) — Greek ὀνόματα, plural (Treg NA28 RP; WH ὄνομα, "the name").
- **"Simon the Canaanite" → "Simon the Cananaean"** (v18) — Greek Καναναῖον (WH Treg NA28), from Aramaic *qan'ana*, "zealous one" (Luke 6:15 "the Zealot"); not "Canaanite" (RP Κανανίτην).
- **"which also betrayed him" → "who also delivered him up"** (v19) — Greek παρέδωκεν, "handed over", the verb of 1:14; the word "betray" (προδίδωμι) is not used.
- **"they went into an house" → "he comes home"** (v19–20) — Greek ἔρχεται εἰς οἶκον, singular (WH NA28 RP; Treg ἔρχονται), without the article: "home" or "into a house", as 2:1.
- **"when his friends heard of it, they went out to lay hold on him" → "hearing it, his own people went out to take hold of him"** (v21) — Greek οἱ παρ' αὐτοῦ, "those from beside him": his family (v31) or his followers. κρατῆσαι as 1:31.
- **"He is beside himself" kept** (v21) — Greek ἐξέστη, "he has stood out (of himself)"; the verb "were amazed" of 2:12.
- **"by the prince of the devils" → "By the ruler of the demons"** (v22) — Greek ἐν τῷ ἄρχοντι, literally "in the ruler".
- **"How can Satan … cannot stand" → "How is Satan able … is not able to stand"** (v23–27) — Greek δύναμαι, six times.
- **"if Satan rise up against himself, and be divided … but hath an end" → "if Satan has risen up against himself and has been divided … but has an end"** (v26) — Greek ἀνέστη, ἐμερίσθη, past tenses (WH NA28); τέλος ἔχει.
- **"No man can enter" → "But no one is able, entering"** (v27) — Editions: ἀλλ' οὐ δύναται οὐδεὶς WH NA28 ] ἀλλ' οὐδεὶς δύναται Treg (and the printed SBLGNT); οὐδεὶς δύναται RP — 2–2, NA28 decides; the same meaning.
- **"Verily" → "Amen"** (v28) — Greek ἀμήν, the Hebrew אָמֵן left untranslated by Mark.
- **"All sins shall be forgiven unto the sons of men, and blasphemies wherewith soever they shall blaspheme" → "all things will be forgiven the sons of men, the sins and the blasphemies, as many as they may blaspheme"** (v28) — Greek πάντα ἀφεθήσεται τοῖς υἱοῖς τῶν ἀνθρώπων, τὰ ἁμαρτήματα καὶ αἱ βλασφημίαι. ἁμάρτημα is a sin as a deed done (so also v29).
- **"hath never forgiveness, but is in danger of eternal damnation" → "has no forgiveness forever, but is guilty of an eternal sin"** (v29) — Greek οὐκ ἔχει ἄφεσιν εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, literally "unto the age"; αἰωνίου ἁμαρτήματος (WH Treg NA28); the KJV follows κρίσεως, "judgment" (RP). αἰών and αἰώνιος are one root.
- **"There came then his brethren and his mother" → "And his mother and his brothers come"** (v31) — Greek ἔρχονται (WH Treg; NA28 ἔρχεται, singular); the order "mother … brothers" is that of WH Treg NA28. στήκοντες, καλοῦντες.
- **"Behold, thy mother and thy brethren"** (v32) — NA28 and RP add καὶ αἱ ἀδελφαί σου, "and your sisters"; WH, Treg and the printed SBLGNT do not (three of the four editions), so the verse does not have it.
- **"Who is my mother, or my brethren?" → "Who is my mother and my brothers?"** (v33) — Editions: καὶ WH Treg NA28 ] ἢ RP (and the printed SBLGNT), "or" — the verse follows the majority.
- **"he looked round about on them which sat about him" → "looking around at those sitting in a circle around him"** (v34) — Greek τοὺς περὶ αὐτὸν κύκλῳ καθημένους. Ἴδε "See" (v34) and Ἰδού "Behold" (v32) are two words.
- **"the same is my brother, and my sister" → "this one is my brother and sister"** (v35) — Greek οὗτος; "my" stands once (RP repeats it). WH has no "For".

### Notes
- **v28** — the quotation that begins in v23 runs on to v29.
