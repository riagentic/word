# Mark 16

[1] And when the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ had passed, ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the Magdalene and ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the mother of ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and Salome bought spices, that coming they might anoint him.

[2] And very early on the first day of the week they come to the tomb, when the sun had risen.

[3] And they were saying among themselves, "Who will roll away the stone for us out of the door of the tomb?"

[4] And looking up, they behold that the stone has been rolled away — for it was exceedingly great.

[5] And entering into the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right, clothed in a white robe, and they were utterly astounded.

[6] But he says to them, "Do not be utterly astounded. You seek ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazarene⟧, the crucified one. **He was raised; he is not here.** See, the place where they laid him.

[7] But go, say to his disciples and to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, 'He goes before you into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧; there you will see him, even as he said to you.'"

[8] And going out, they fled from the tomb, for trembling and amazement held them; and they said nothing to anyone, for they were afraid.

[9] [[Now having risen early on the first day of the week, he appeared first to ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the Magdalene, from whom he had cast out seven demons.

[10] That one, going, reported to those who had been with him, as they were mourning and weeping;

[11] and those, hearing that he lives and was beheld by her, disbelieved.

[12] And after these things he was manifested in a different form to two of them as they were walking, going into the field;

[13] and those, going away, reported to the rest; neither did they believe those.

[14] And afterward he was manifested to the eleven themselves as they were reclining, and he reproached their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they did not believe those who had beheld him raised.

[15] And he said to them, "**Going into all the world, proclaim the gospel to all the creation.**

**[16] The one who has believed and has been ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ will be saved, but the one who has disbelieved will be condemned.**

[17] And these signs will accompany those who have believed: in my name they will cast out demons, they will speak in new tongues,

[18] and in their hands they will take up serpents, and if they drink anything deadly it shall by no means harm them; upon the infirm they will lay hands, and they will be well."

[19] So then the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, after speaking to them, was taken up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] But those, going out, proclaimed everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word through the signs that followed.]]

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v1) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v6, 19) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v19) — divine name retained.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v7) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v16) — Greek βαπτισθείς; consistent rendering.
- **"vv9-20 longer ending"** — double-bracketed [[…]] as the double brackets of the printed Greek: WH and NA28 end the Gospel text at v8 (ἐφοβοῦντο γάρ); Treg and RP add v9–20. The verses are translated as printed, like any other verse; see ### Notes.
- **"devils" → "demons"** (v9, 17) — Greek *daimonia*.
- **"And" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — Mark's καί at the start of sentences and his present tenses in narrative ("come", v2; "behold", v4; "says", v6) are kept as they are.
- **"when the sabbath was past, Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Salome, had bought sweet spices, that they might come and anoint him" → "when the Sabbath had passed, Mary the Magdalene and Mary the mother of James and Salome bought spices, that coming they might anoint him"** (v1) — Greek διαγενομένου; Μαρία ἡ τοῦ Ἰακώβου, "Mary the one of James" ("mother" from 15:40); ἠγόρασαν, a simple past; ἀρώματα, aromatic spices or oils; ἀλείψωσιν, "anoint" (as 6:13; in 14:8 the verb is μυρίζω).
- **"very early in the morning the first day of the week, they came unto the sepulchre at the rising of the sun" → "very early on the first day of the week they come to the tomb, when the sun had risen"** (v2) — Greek λίαν πρωΐ; τῇ μιᾷ τῶν σαββάτων, literally "on the one of the sabbaths": σάββατα is here "week", and "day" is supplied by the feminine μιᾷ; ἔρχονται, a present; ἀνατείλαντος τοῦ ἡλίου, a past participle, "the sun having risen"; μνημεῖον, "tomb", as 15:46.
- **"they said among themselves, Who shall roll us away the stone from the door of the sepulchre?" → "they were saying among themselves, 'Who will roll away the stone for us out of the door of the tomb?'"** (v3) — Greek ἔλεγον, an imperfect; ἐκ τῆς θύρας, "out of the door" (WH NA28 RP; Treg ἀπό, "from").
- **"when they looked, they saw that the stone was rolled away: for it was very great" → "looking up, they behold that the stone has been rolled away — for it was exceedingly great"** (v4) — Greek ἀναβλέψασαι; θεωροῦσιν, a present; ἀποκεκύλισται, "has been rolled away", NA28 RP (printed) ] ἀνακεκύλισται, "has been rolled back", WH Treg — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides. "For it was exceedingly great" gives the reason for their question in v3.
- **"clothed in a long white garment; and they were affrighted" → "clothed in a white robe, and they were utterly astounded"** (v5, 6) — Greek στολὴν λευκήν; ἐξεθαμβήθησαν, μὴ ἐκθαμβεῖσθε, the verb of 14:33; ἐν τοῖς δεξιοῖς, "in the parts on the right".
- **"Ye seek Jesus of Nazareth, which was crucified: he is risen … behold the place" → "You seek Yeshua the Nazarene, the crucified one. He was raised … See, the place"** (v6) — Greek Ἰησοῦν … τὸν Ναζαρηνὸν τὸν ἐσταυρωμένον, "Yeshua the Nazarene, the one who has been crucified" (Ναζαρηνός as 1:24); ἠγέρθη, a passive and a simple past, "he was raised"; ἴδε. The opening words can also be read as a question: "Do you seek …?"
- **"tell his disciples and Peter that he goeth before you into Galilee" → "say to his disciples and to Kefa, 'He goes before you into Galilee …'"** (v7) — Greek εἴπατε … ὅτι Προάγει ὑμᾶς: ὅτι introduces the words to be said ("you" is the disciples); προάγει, the verb of 14:28.
- **"they went out quickly, and fled from the sepulchre; for they trembled and were amazed: neither said they any thing to any man" → "going out, they fled from the tomb, for trembling and amazement held them; and they said nothing to anyone"** (v8) — "quickly" (RP) is not in the critical text; Greek εἶχεν γὰρ αὐτὰς τρόμος καὶ ἔκστασις (ἔκστασις as 5:42); ἐφοβοῦντο γάρ, "for they were afraid", the last words of the Gospel text in WH and NA28.
- **"when Jesus was risen early the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, out of whom he had cast seven devils" → "having risen early on the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary the Magdalene, from whom he had cast out seven demons"** (v9) — Greek ἀναστάς, without a named subject; πρώτῃ σαββάτου, "on the first (day) of the week" (another expression than in v2); ἐφάνη, "appeared"; παρ' ἧς (Treg NA28).
- **"she went and told them that had been with him" → "That one, going, reported to those who had been with him"** (v10) — Greek ἐκείνη, "that one" (so also ἐκεῖνοι, "those", in v11, 13, 20); ἀπήγγειλεν, "report" (v13).
- **"when they had heard that he was alive, and had been seen of her, believed not" → "hearing that he lives and was beheld by her, disbelieved"** (v11) — Greek ζῇ, a present; ἐθεάθη, from θεάομαι "behold" (v14); ἠπίστησαν, the verb of ἀπιστία "unbelief" (v14, 16).
- **"he appeared in another form unto two of them, as they walked, and went into the country" → "he was manifested in a different form to two of them as they were walking, going into the field"** (v12) — Greek ἐφανερώθη, "was manifested" (also v14), a different verb from "appeared" in v9; ἐν ἑτέρᾳ μορφῇ; εἰς ἀγρόν, without the article, "to a field" or "into the country" (as 15:21).
- **"neither believed they them" → "neither did they believe those"** (v13) — Greek οὐδὲ ἐκείνοις ἐπίστευσαν.
- **"he appeared unto the eleven as they sat at meat, and upbraided them with their unbelief … them which had seen him after he was risen" → "he was manifested to the eleven themselves as they were reclining, and he reproached their unbelief … those who had beheld him raised"** (v14) — Greek ἀνακειμένοις; ὠνείδισεν, "reproach", as 15:32; σκληροκαρδία, "hardness of heart"; ἐγηγερμένον, a perfect passive, "having been raised" (WH adds "from the dead").
- **"Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature" → "Going into all the world, proclaim the gospel to all the creation"** (v15) — Greek πορευθέντες, a participle; κηρύξατε; πάσῃ τῇ κτίσει, with the article, "all the creation" (κτίσις also "creature").
- **"He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned" → "The one who has believed and has been immersed will be saved, but the one who has disbelieved will be condemned"** (v16) — Greek ὁ πιστεύσας καὶ βαπτισθείς … ὁ δὲ ἀπιστήσας, past participles; κατακριθήσεται, "condemn", as 14:64.
- **"these signs shall follow them that believe … they shall speak with new tongues" → "these signs will accompany those who have believed … they will speak in new tongues"** (v17) — Greek παρακολουθήσει, "follow beside, accompany" (NA28 RP; WH Treg ἀκολουθήσει, "follow"); τοῖς πιστεύσασιν, a past participle; γλώσσαις λαλήσουσιν, a dative, as 1 Cor 14; καιναῖς, "new", NA28 RP (printed) ] not in WH Treg, "they will speak in tongues" — 2–2, NA28 decides.
- **"They shall take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover" → "and in their hands they will take up serpents, and if they drink anything deadly it shall by no means harm them; upon the infirm they will lay hands, and they will be well"** (v18) — Editions: ὄφεις RP (and the printed SBLGNT) ] καὶ ἐν ταῖς χερσὶν ὄφεις, "and in their hands … serpents", WH Treg NA28 — the verse follows the three editions. οὐ μὴ αὐτοὺς βλάψῃ, the strong negation; ἀρρώστους, "infirm", as 6:5, 13; καλῶς ἕξουσιν, literally "they will have it well", the opposite of "those who were ill" (κακῶς ἔχοντες, 1:32).
- **"after the Lord had spoken unto them, he was received up into heaven, and sat on the right hand of God" → "the Lord Yeshua, after speaking to them, was taken up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of Elohim"** (v19) — Greek ὁ κύριος Ἰησοῦς (WH Treg NA28; RP has no "Jesus"); ἀνελήμφθη; ἐκάθισεν ἐκ δεξιῶν τοῦ θεοῦ (Ps 110:1).
- **"they went forth, and preached every where, the Lord working with them, and confirming the word with signs following. Amen" → "those, going out, proclaimed everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word through the signs that followed"** (v20) — Greek ἐκήρυξαν; συνεργοῦντος (as συνεργός "fellow worker"); βεβαιοῦντος, "confirm"; διὰ τῶν ἐπακολουθούντων σημείων, "through the signs following upon (it)". "Amen" (RP) is not in the critical text.

### Notes
- **v9-20** — the "longer ending," absent from the earliest and most reliable MSS (Vaticanus, Sinaiticus). Some MSS include a shorter ending instead. Most scholars regard Mark as originally ending at v8, with the longer ending added later.
- **v8** — the shorter ending, which the printed Greek gives in double brackets after v8 (Πάντα δὲ τὰ παρηγγελμένα …, "And all that had been commanded them they reported briefly to those around Peter. And after these things Yeshua himself also sent out through them, from the east and as far as the west, the sacred and incorruptible proclamation of eternal salvation. Amen."), is not part of the verse text here: of the editions compared, the apparatus lists it only for the Greek behind the NIV, beside v9–20 (WH NA28 end at v8; Treg RP add v9–20).
