# Mark 15

[1] And immediately, early, the high priests with the elders and scribes, and the whole ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧, having made counsel and having bound ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, took him away and delivered him up to ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧.

[2] And ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ asked him, "Are you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?" But he, answering him, says, "You say it."

[3] And the high priests were accusing him of many things.

[4] But ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ again was asking him, saying, "Do you answer nothing? See how many things they accuse you of."

[5] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ no longer answered anything, so that ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ marveled.

[7] Now there was the one called ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧, bound with the insurrectionists, who in the insurrection had committed murder.

[8] And the crowd, coming up, began to ask even as he used to do for them.

[9] But ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ answered them, saying, "Do you want me to release to you the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?"

[10] For he knew that because of envy the high priests had delivered him up.

[11] But the high priests stirred up the crowd, that he should rather release ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧ to them.

[12] But ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧, again answering, was saying to them, "What then do you want me to do with him whom you call the King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?"

[13] But they again cried out, "Crucify him!"

[14] But ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ was saying to them, "For what evil did he do?" But they cried out exceedingly, "Crucify him!"

[15] And ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧, wishing to satisfy the crowd, released ⟦barabbas|Bar-Abba|Barabbas⟧ to them, and delivered up ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, having flogged him, that he might be crucified.

[16] And the soldiers led him away inside the court, which is the praetorium, and they call together the whole cohort.

[17] And they clothe him in purple, and having woven a crown of thorns they put it around him;

[18] and they began to greet him, "Hail, King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧!"

[19] And they were beating his head with a reed and spitting on him, and bending the knees they were bowing down to him.

[20] And when they had mocked him, they took the purple off him and put his garments on him. And they lead him out that they might crucify him.

[21] And they press into service a certain one passing along, ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ of ⟦cyrene|Kyrene|Cyrene⟧, coming from the field, the father of ⟦alexander|Alexandros|Alexander⟧ and Rufus, that he might take up his cross.

[22] And they bring him to the place **⟦golgotha|Gulgolta|Golgotha⟧**, which is, being translated, Place of a Skull.

[23] And they were giving him wine mingled with myrrh, but he did not take it.

[24] And they crucify him and divide his garments among themselves, casting a lot upon them, who should take what.

[25] Now it was the third hour, and they crucified him.

[26] And the inscription of his charge was inscribed, "**The King of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧**."

[27] And with him they crucify two robbers, one at his right hand and one at his left.

[29] And those passing by were blaspheming him, shaking their heads and saying, "Ha! You who tear down the sanctuary and build it in three days,

[30] save yourself, coming down from the cross!"

[31] Likewise also the high priests, mocking among one another with the scribes, were saying, "Others he saved; himself he is not able to save.

[32] Let the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, the King of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe." And those crucified together with him were reproaching him.

[33] And when the sixth hour had come, darkness came upon the whole earth until the ninth hour.

[34] And at the ninth hour ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ cried with a loud voice, "***Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani?***" which is, being translated, "**My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for what have you forsaken me?**"

[35] And some of those standing by, hearing, were saying, "See, he calls ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧."

[36] And someone, running and filling a sponge with vinegar, putting it around a reed, was giving him to drink, saying, "Let be; let us see whether ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ comes to take him down."

[37] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, letting out a loud voice, breathed his last.

[38] And the veil of the sanctuary was torn into two, from above to below.

[39] And the centurion who stood by opposite him, seeing that he thus breathed his last, said, "**Truly this man was ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ Son!**"

[40] Now there were also women beholding from afar, among whom were both ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the Magdalene and ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the mother of ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the little and of ⟦joses|Yose|Joses⟧, and Salome,

[41] who, when he was in ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, were following him and serving him, and many others who came up together with him to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[42] And when evening had already come, since it was Preparation, which is the day before the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧,

[43] ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, the one from ⟦arimathea|Arimathaia|Arimathea⟧, an honorable councillor, who also himself was awaiting the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, coming, daring, went in to ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ and asked for the body of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[44] But ⟦pilate|Pilatus|Pilate⟧ marveled if he had already died, and calling the centurion to himself he asked him whether he had died long ago;

[45] and coming to know it from the centurion, he bestowed the corpse on ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[46] And having bought a linen cloth, taking him down, he wrapped him in the linen cloth and laid him in a tomb which had been hewn out of rock, and he rolled a stone against the door of the tomb.

[47] And ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the Magdalene and ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ the mother of ⟦joses|Yose|Joses⟧ were beholding where he has been laid.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v40) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv34, 39, 43) — divine name retained; in v34 the Aramaic cry "Eloi, Eloi" is preserved in text.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v32) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"Arimathaea" → "Arimathea"** (v43) — Greek Ἁριμαθαίας *Harimathaias*, the name the text gives; often identified with Ramathaim(-zophim), Samuel's town (1 Sam 1:1; LXX Αρμαθαιμ), but the identification is not certain.
- **"v28 absent"** — Mark 15:28 ("And the scripture was fulfilled, which saith, And he was numbered with the transgressors") stands in Treg RP, not in WH NA28 (nor in the printed SBLGNT); it is one of the 16 omitted verses.
- **"And" / "immediately" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — Mark's καί at the start of sentences, καὶ εὐθύς "and immediately" (v1) and his present tenses in narrative ("says", v2; "call together", v16; "clothe … put", v17; "lead out", v20; "press into service", v21; "bring", v22; "crucify … divide", v24; "crucify", v27) are kept as they are.
- **"straightway in the morning the chief priests held a consultation … and carried him away, and delivered him to Pilate" → "immediately, early, the high priests with the elders and scribes, and the whole Sanhedrin, having made counsel … took him away and delivered him up to Pilate"** (v1) — Greek πρωΐ, "early" (as 1:35); συμβούλιον ποιήσαντες (as 3:6); ὅλον τὸ συνέδριον is a second subject beside "the high priests", not governed by "with"; ἀρχιερεῖς, "high priests" (v1, 3, 10, 11, 31), as 8:31; ἀπήνεγκαν, "carried, took away"; παρέδωκαν, "handed over, delivered up", the verb of 14:10 (v10, 15).
- **"he answering said unto him, Thou sayest it" → "he, answering him, says, 'You say it'"** (v2) — Greek λέγει, a present (WH Treg NA28); Σὺ λέγεις, "you say" ("it" is supplied): an answer that neither denies nor plainly affirms; it can also be read as a question.
- **"accused him of many things: but he answered nothing" → "were accusing him of many things"** (v3) — Greek κατηγόρουν, an imperfect; "but he answered nothing" is not in the Greek.
- **"asked him again … behold how many things they witness against thee" → "again was asking him … See how many things they accuse you of"** (v4) — Greek ἐπηρώτα, an imperfect; ἴδε; κατηγοροῦσιν (WH Treg NA28; RP καταμαρτυροῦσιν, "witness against").
- **"Jesus yet answered nothing" → "Yeshua no longer answered anything"** (v5) — Greek οὐκέτι οὐδὲν ἀπεκρίθη.
- **"at that feast he released unto them one prisoner, whomsoever they desired" → "at each feast he used to release to them one prisoner, whom they asked for"** (v6) — Greek κατὰ ἑορτήν, "feast by feast"; ἀπέλυεν, an imperfect (a custom); ἀπολύω is the verb "send away" of 6:36, here of a prisoner "release" (v9, 11, 15); ὃν παρῃτοῦντο (WH NA28), "whom they were begging off".
- **"one named Barabbas, which lay bound with them that had made insurrection with him, who had committed murder in the insurrection" → "the one called Barabbas, bound with the insurrectionists, who in the insurrection had committed murder"** (v7) — Greek ὁ λεγόμενος Βαραββᾶς; μετὰ τῶν στασιαστῶν (WH Treg NA28); οἵτινες … πεποιήκεισαν, plural: the insurrectionists had committed the murder. Βαραββᾶς is Aramaic *Bar-Abba*, "son of the father".
- **"the multitude crying aloud began to desire him to do as he had ever done unto them" → "the crowd, coming up, began to ask even as he used to do for them"** (v8) — Greek ἀναβάς, "coming up" (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀναβοήσας, "crying aloud"); αἰτεῖσθαι καθὼς ἐποίει αὐτοῖς: the verb "ask" has no object, and Pilate is not named in this verse; "ever" (Treg RP ἀεί) is not in WH NA28.
- **"Will ye that I release unto you" → "Do you want me to release to you"** (v9) — Greek θέλετε ἀπολύσω.
- **"For he knew that the chief priests had delivered him for envy" → "For he knew that because of envy the high priests had delivered him up"** (v10) — Greek ἐγίνωσκεν, an imperfect, "he was coming to know, perceived"; διὰ φθόνον.
- **"moved the people" → "stirred up the crowd"** (v11) — Greek ἀνέσεισαν, "shook up"; τὸν ὄχλον.
- **"answered and said again unto them, What will ye then that I shall do unto him whom ye call" → "again answering, was saying to them, 'What then do you want me to do with him whom you call'"** (v12) — Greek ἔλεγεν, an imperfect (also v14). θέλετε, "do you want", Treg NA28 RP (printed) ] not in WH, "What then shall I do" — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides. ὃν λέγετε, "whom you call" (WH NA28 RP; not in Treg).
- **"Why, what evil hath he done? And they cried out the more exceedingly" → "For what evil did he do? But they cried out exceedingly"** (v14) — Greek τί γὰρ ἐποίησεν κακόν, literally "for what bad thing did he do?"; περισσῶς, "exceedingly" (WH Treg NA28; RP περισσοτέρως, "the more").
- **"willing to content the people … delivered Jesus, when he had scourged him, to be crucified" → "wishing to satisfy the crowd … delivered up Yeshua, having flogged him, that he might be crucified"** (v15) — Greek βουλόμενος; τὸ ἱκανὸν ποιῆσαι, literally "to do the sufficient thing" for the crowd, a Latin turn of phrase (*satisfacere*); φραγελλώσας, from Latin *flagellum*, the Roman flogging before crucifixion (a different word from "scourge", μάστιξ, 3:10).
- **"led him away into the hall, called Praetorium; and they call together the whole band" → "led him away inside the court, which is the praetorium, and they call together the whole cohort"** (v16) — Greek ἔσω τῆς αὐλῆς, the word "court" of 14:54, 66; ὅ ἐστιν πραιτώριον, the governor's residence; σπεῖρα, a cohort (a tenth of a legion, or a detachment of one).
- **"they clothed him with purple, and platted a crown of thorns, and put it about his head" → "they clothe him in purple, and having woven a crown of thorns they put it around him"** (v17) — Greek ἐνδιδύσκουσιν … περιτιθέασιν, presents; πορφύραν, "purple" (a purple garment); ἀκάνθινον στέφανον, "a thorny crown"; στέφανος is a wreath; the Greek has no word "head".
- **"began to salute him" → "began to greet him"** (v18) — Greek ἀσπάζεσθαι; Χαῖρε, the common greeting, literally "rejoice".
- **"they smote him on the head with a reed, and did spit upon him, and bowing their knees worshipped him" → "they were beating his head with a reed and spitting on him, and bending the knees they were bowing down to him"** (v19) — Greek imperfects; προσεκύνουν, "bow down to", as 5:6.
- **"took off the purple from him, and put his own clothes on him, and led him out to crucify him" → "took the purple off him and put his garments on him. And they lead him out that they might crucify him"** (v20) — Editions: τὰ ἱμάτια τὰ ἴδια, "his own garments", Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) ] τὰ ἱμάτια αὐτοῦ, "his garments", WH NA28 — 2–2, NA28 decides. ἐξάγουσιν, a present.
- **"they compel one Simon a Cyrenian, who passed by, coming out of the country … to bear his cross" → "they press into service a certain one passing along, Simon of Cyrene, coming from the field … that he might take up his cross"** (v21) — Greek ἀγγαρεύουσιν, a Persian loan-word for forced service to the state; παράγοντα, as 1:16; Κυρηναῖον, "a Cyrenian"; ἀπ' ἀγροῦ, without the article, "from a field" or "from the country" (ἀγρός as 13:16; 16:12); ἄρῃ, "take up", as 8:34.
- **"the place Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, The place of a skull" → "the place Golgotha, which is, being translated, Place of a Skull"** (v22) — Greek ἐπὶ τὸν Γολγοθᾶν τόπον (WH NA28): the Aramaic name גֻּלְגֻּלְתָּא, "skull", in the form the Greek gives it, with Mark's translation Κρανίου Τόπος; μεθερμηνευόμενον as 5:41.
- **"they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh: but he received it not" → "they were giving him wine mingled with myrrh, but he did not take it"** (v23) — Greek ἐδίδουν, an imperfect ("were offering"); "to drink" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"when they had crucified him, they parted his garments, casting lots upon them, what every man should take" → "they crucify him and divide his garments among themselves, casting a lot upon them, who should take what"** (v24) — Greek σταυροῦσιν … διαμερίζονται, presents (WH Treg NA28); κλῆρον, singular; τίς τί ἄρῃ (Ps 22:18).
- **"the superscription of his accusation was written over" → "the inscription of his charge was inscribed"** (v26) — Greek ἡ ἐπιγραφὴ … ἐπιγεγραμμένη, noun and verb of one root; αἰτία, "cause, charge".
- **"two thieves; the one on his right hand, and the other on his left" → "two robbers, one at his right hand and one at his left"** (v27) — Greek λῃστάς, "robbers" (as 14:48), not "thieves"; σταυροῦσιν, a present; ἐκ δεξιῶν … ἐξ εὐωνύμων, as 10:37, 40 (the Greek has no word "hand").
- **"they that passed by railed on him, wagging their heads … Ah, thou that destroyest the temple, and buildest it in three days" → "those passing by were blaspheming him, shaking their heads … Ha! You who tear down the sanctuary and build it in three days"** (v29) — Greek ἐβλασφήμουν αὐτόν; κινοῦντες, "moving" (Ps 22:7); Οὐά, a cry of scornful wonder; ὁ καταλύων τὸν ναόν, as 14:58 (ναός "sanctuary", the temple building itself; so also v38); "it" is supplied.
- **"Save thyself, and come down from the cross" → "save yourself, coming down from the cross"** (v30) — Greek καταβάς, a participle (WH Treg NA28).
- **"mocking said among themselves … himself he cannot save" → "mocking among one another … himself he is not able to save"** (v31) — Greek ἐμπαίζοντες πρὸς ἀλλήλους; οὐ δύναται. ἔσωσεν … σῶσαι, "save", as v30.
- **"Let Christ the King of Israel descend … and believe. And they that were crucified with him reviled him" → "Let the Mashiach, the King of Israel, come down … and believe. And those crucified together with him were reproaching him"** (v32) — Greek ὁ χριστὸς ὁ βασιλεὺς Ἰσραήλ; καταβάτω, as v30; πιστεύσωμεν, without an object (RP adds "him"); ὠνείδιζον, an imperfect, "reproach" (as 1 Pet 4:14).
- **"there was darkness over the whole land" → "darkness came upon the whole earth"** (v33) — Greek σκότος ἐγένετο ἐφ' ὅλην τὴν γῆν: γῆ is "earth" (as 13:27, 31) or "land"; the word does not decide how far the darkness reached.
- **"Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani? which is, being interpreted, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?" → "Eloi, Eloi, lema sabachthani? which is, being translated, 'My Elohim, my Elohim, for what have you forsaken me?'"** (v34) — Greek Ἐλωῒ ἐλωῒ λεμὰ σαβαχθάνι (NA28 and the printed SBLGNT; WH Treg λαμά): the Aramaic of Ps 22:1, אֱלָהִי אֱלָהִי לְמָא שְׁבַקְתַּנִי, in the form the Greek gives it. Mark's translation: ὁ θεός μου ὁ θεός μου, εἰς τί ἐγκατέλιπές με, literally "unto what did you forsake me?" (εἰς τί as 14:4; ἐγκαταλείπω, "forsake", a past tense). ἐβόησεν, "cried", as 1:3.
- **"Behold, he calleth Elias" → "See, he calls Elijah"** (v35) — Greek Ἴδε (WH Treg NA28); φωνεῖ, "calls".
- **"filled a spunge full of vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him to drink, saying, Let alone" → "filling a sponge with vinegar, putting it around a reed, was giving him to drink, saying, 'Let be'"** (v36) — Greek ὄξος, sour wine, wine vinegar, the drink of soldiers and laborers (Ps 69:21); ἐπότιζεν, an imperfect; Ἄφετε ἴδωμεν, "Let be; let us see", or simply "Let us see" (ἀφίημι as 14:6).
- **"cried with a loud voice, and gave up the ghost" → "letting out a loud voice, breathed his last"** (v37, 39) — Greek ἀφεὶς φωνὴν μεγάλην; ἐξέπνευσεν, one word, "breathed out" ("his last" is supplied), of the root of πνεῦμα "breath, spirit"; not ἐκψύχω "expire" (Acts 5:5).
- **"rent in twain from the top to the bottom" → "torn into two, from above to below"** (v38) — Greek ἐσχίσθη, the verb of 1:10; ἀπ' ἄνωθεν ἕως κάτω; καταπέτασμα, "veil", as Heb 6:19; 9:3.
- **"the centurion, which stood over against him, saw that he so cried out, and gave up the ghost … this man was the Son of God" → "the centurion who stood by opposite him, seeing that he thus breathed his last … this man was God's Son"** (v39) — "cried out" (Treg RP κράξας) is not in WH NA28. Greek υἱὸς θεοῦ, without articles: "God's Son", "the Son of God" (as 1:1) or "a son of a god"; the verse keeps the form without the article.
- **"looking on afar off: among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the less and of Joses" → "beholding from afar, among whom were both Mary the Magdalene and Mary the mother of James the little and of Joses"** (v40) — Greek θεωροῦσαι (v47); Μαρία ἡ Μαγδαληνή, "the Magdalene", the woman from Magdala; Ἰακώβου τοῦ μικροῦ, "the little" (in stature or in age); Ἰωσῆτος (WH Treg NA28).
- **"followed him, and ministered unto him" → "were following him and serving him"** (v41) — Greek ἠκολούθουν, διηκόνουν, imperfects; διακονέω as 1:13, 31; αἱ συναναβᾶσαι, "who came up together with".
- **"because it was the preparation, that is, the day before the sabbath" → "since it was Preparation, which is the day before the Sabbath"** (v42) — Greek παρασκευή, "preparation", the Jewish name for the day before the Sabbath; προσάββατον, one word, "fore-Sabbath".
- **"Joseph of Arimathaea, an honourable counsellor, which also waited for the kingdom of God, came, and went in boldly unto Pilate, and craved the body" → "Joseph, the one from Arimathea, an honorable councillor, who also himself was awaiting the kingdom of Elohim, coming, daring, went in to Pilate and asked for the body"** (v43) — Greek ὁ ἀπὸ Ἁριμαθαίας (Treg NA28 RP); εὐσχήμων, "of good standing" (the word "becoming" of 1 Cor 12:24); βουλευτής, a member of a council (βουλή): the word "Sanhedrin" is not used here; προσδεχόμενος, as Titus 2:13; τολμήσας, "having dared".
- **"marvelled if he were already dead … whether he had been any while dead" → "marveled if he had already died … whether he had died long ago"** (v44) — Greek ἐθαύμασεν εἰ ἤδη τέθνηκεν, literally "marveled if he is already dead"; πάλαι, "long ago, for some time", NA28 RP (printed) ] ἤδη, "already", WH Treg — 2–2, NA28 decides.
- **"when he knew it of the centurion, he gave the body to Joseph" → "coming to know it from the centurion, he bestowed the corpse on Joseph"** (v45) — Greek γνούς; ἐδωρήσατο, "gave as a gift" (as 2 Pet 1:3); πτῶμα, "corpse" (WH Treg NA28; 6:29), where v43 has σῶμα, "body".
- **"he bought fine linen, and took him down, and wrapped him in the linen, and laid him in a sepulchre which was hewn out of a rock, and rolled a stone unto the door of the sepulchre" → "having bought a linen cloth, taking him down, he wrapped him in the linen cloth and laid him in a tomb which had been hewn out of rock, and he rolled a stone against the door of the tomb"** (v46) — Greek σινδών, as 14:51; μνημεῖον, "tomb" (Treg NA28 RP; WH μνήματι), as 5:2; θύρα, "door". Joseph is not named in this verse.
- **"Mary the mother of Joses beheld where he was laid" → "Mary the mother of Joses were beholding where he has been laid"** (v47) — Greek Μαρία ἡ Ἰωσῆτος, "Mary the one of Joses" ("mother" from v40); ἐθεώρουν, an imperfect; τέθειται, a perfect (WH Treg NA28).

### Notes
- **v28** — one of the 16 omitted verses ("And the scripture was fulfilled...", Isa 53:12; compare Luke 22:37).
- **v34** — Aramaic cry of dereliction, Ps 22:1.
