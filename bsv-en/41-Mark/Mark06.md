# Mark 6

[1] And he went out from there, and he comes into his fatherland, and his disciples follow him.

[2] And when the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ came, he began to teach in the synagogue; and many, hearing, were astonished, saying, "From where does this one have these things, and what is the wisdom that has been given to this one, and such powers coming to pass through his hands?

[3] Is not this the carpenter, the son of ⟦mary|Miryam|Mary⟧ and brother of ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦joses|Yose|Joses⟧ and ⟦jude|Yehudah|Judas⟧ and ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧? And are not his sisters here with us?" And they were being made to stumble in him.

[4] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was saying to them, "**A prophet is not without honor except in his fatherland and among his kinsmen and in his house.**"

[5] And he was not able to do there any work of power, except that, laying his hands on a few infirm people, he healed them.

[6] And he was marveling because of their unbelief.

And he was going around the villages in a circuit, teaching.

[7] And he calls the twelve to himself, and he began to send them out two by two, and he was giving them authority over the unclean spirits,

[8] and he charged them that they should take nothing for the road except a staff only — no bread, no bag, no copper in the belt —

[9] but shod with sandals; and, "Do not put on two tunics."

[10] And he was saying to them, "Wherever you enter into a house, remain there until you go out from there.

[11] And whatever place does not accept you, and they do not hear you, going out from there shake off the dust that is under your feet for a testimony to them."

[12] And going out they proclaimed that they should repent,

[13] and they were casting out many demons, and were anointing with oil many infirm people and healing them.

[14] And King ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ heard, for his name had become manifest, and they were saying, "⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧ has been raised from the dead, and because of this the powers work in him."

[15] But others were saying, "It is ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧." And others were saying, "A prophet, like one of the prophets."

[16] But ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, hearing, was saying, "He whom I beheaded, ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ — this one was raised."

[17] For ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ himself, having sent, took hold of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ and bound him in prison because of Herodias, the wife of ⟦philip|Philippos|Philip⟧ his brother, because he had married her.

[18] For ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ was saying to ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧, "It is not lawful for you to have your brother's wife."

[19] And Herodias was holding it against him and wanted to kill him, and was not able,

[20] for ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ feared ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, knowing him to be a righteous and holy man, and he was keeping him safe; and hearing him he was much at a loss, and he was hearing him gladly.

[21] And when an opportune day came, when ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ on his birthday made a supper for his great men and the chiliarchs and the first men of ⟦galilee|the Galil|Galilee⟧,

[22] and when his daughter Herodias came in and danced, she pleased ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧ and those reclining with him. The king said to the girl, "Ask me whatever you want, and I will give it to you."

[23] And he swore to her, "Whatever you ask me I will give you, up to half of my kingdom."

[24] And going out she said to her mother, "What should I ask for?" And she said, "The head of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧."

[25] And coming in immediately with haste to the king she asked, saying, "I want that you give me at once on a platter the head of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧."

[26] And the king, though he became deeply grieved, because of the oaths and those reclining did not want to set her aside.

[27] And immediately the king, sending a guard, ordered his head to be brought. And going away he beheaded him in the prison

[28] and brought his head on a platter and gave it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother.

[29] And his disciples, hearing, came and took up his corpse and laid it in a tomb.

[30] And the apostles gather together to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and they reported to him all that they had done and that they had taught.

[31] And he says to them, "Come, you yourselves, privately into a desert place and rest a little." For those coming and those going were many, and they had no opportunity even to eat.

[32] And they went away in the boat to a desert place privately.

[33] And they saw them going, and many recognized them, and on foot from all the cities they ran together there and came before them.

[34] And coming out he saw a great crowd, and he was moved with compassion for them, because they were as sheep not having a shepherd, and he began to teach them many things.

[35] And when the hour was already late, his disciples, coming to him, were saying, "The place is desert, and the hour is already late;

[36] send them away, that going away into the farms and villages round about they may buy for themselves something to eat."

[37] But he, answering, said to them, "You give them to eat." And they say to him, "Shall we go away and buy loaves for two hundred denarii, and shall we give them to eat?"

[38] But he says to them, "How many loaves do you have? Go, see." And having found out they say, "Five, and two fish."

[39] And he ordered them to make all recline, company by company, on the green grass.

[40] And they reclined, plot by plot, by hundreds and by fifties.

[41] And taking the five loaves and the two fish, looking up into heaven he blessed and broke the loaves in pieces and was giving them to his disciples that they might set them before them, and the two fish he divided among all.

[42] And they all ate and were filled;

[43] and they took up fragments, the fullness of twelve baskets, and from the fish.

[44] And those who had eaten the loaves were five thousand men.

[45] And immediately he compelled his disciples to get into the boat and to go before to the other side, toward ⟦bethsaida|Beit Tzaidah|Bethsaida⟧, while he himself sends the crowd away.

[46] And having taken leave of them, he went away into the mountain to pray.

[47] And when evening came, the boat was in the middle of the sea, and he alone on the land.

[48] And seeing them tormented in the rowing, for the wind was against them, about the fourth watch of the night he comes to them walking on the sea; and he wanted to pass by them.

[49] But they, seeing him walking on the sea, thought that it was a phantom, and cried out,

[50] for they all saw him and were troubled. But he immediately spoke with them, and says to them, "Be of good courage; it is I; do not fear."

[51] And he went up to them into the boat, and the wind ceased. And they were exceedingly, beyond measure, amazed in themselves,

[52] for they had not understood about the loaves, but their heart was hardened.

[53] And having crossed over, they came to the land, to ⟦gennesaret|Ginosar|Gennesaret⟧, and moored.

[54] And when they had come out of the boat, immediately recognizing him,

[55] they ran about that whole country and began to carry about on pallets those who were ill, to where they were hearing that he was.

[56] And wherever he was entering, into villages or into cities or into farms, they were laying the sick in the marketplaces, and were appealing to him that they might touch even the fringe of his garment; and as many as touched him were being saved.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v3) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v4, 30) — Hebrew name preserved. In v14 and v27 the Greek has "his" (αὐτοῦ), and so does the verse.
- **"John the Baptist" → "John the Immerser"** (vv14, 24, 25) — "Immerser" renders *ho baptizōn* (v14, 24: a participle, "the one immersing") and *ho baptistēs* (v25: the noun); see Mark 1.
- **"devils" → "demons"** (v13) — Greek *daimonia*.
- **"straightway" → "immediately"** (v25, 27, 45, 50, 54) — Greek *euthys*. Mark's "And" at the start of sentences and his present tenses ("comes", "follow", v1; "calls", v7; "gather", v30; "says", v31, 38, 50; "say", v37, 38; "sends away", v45; "comes", v48) are kept as they are.
- **"his own country" → "his fatherland"** (v1, 4) — Greek πατρίς *patris*.
- **"many hearing" kept** (v2) — the verse follows the reading without the article (Treg NA28 RP); WH and the SBLGNT read οἱ πολλοί, "the many".
- **"mighty works" → "powers" / "work of power"** (v2, 14 / v5) — Greek δυνάμεις / δύναμιν, the plural and singular of δύναμις "power"; one word in all three verses.
- **"were offended at him" → "were being made to stumble in him"** (v3) — Greek ἐσκανδαλίζοντο ἐν αὐτῷ, an imperfect passive.
- **"sick folk" → "infirm people" / "those who were ill" / "the sick"** (v5, 13 / v55 / v56) — three Greek expressions: ἄρρωστος, οἱ κακῶς ἔχοντες (literally "those having it badly"), οἱ ἀσθενοῦντες.
- **"power over unclean spirits" → "authority over the unclean spirits"** (v7) — Greek ἐξουσία *exousia*, a genitive: "authority of the unclean spirits".
- **"commanded" → "charged"** (v8) — Greek παραγγέλλω.
- **"no money in their purse" → "no copper in the belt"** (v8) — Greek χαλκόν, copper, that is, copper coins; ζώνη is the belt.
- **"but be shod with sandals; and not put on two coats" → "but shod with sandals; and, 'Do not put on two tunics.'"** (v9) — the Greek sentence changes its construction twice: "shod" is an accusative participle, and "do not put on" (ἐνδύσησθε, Treg NA28 RP; WH ἐνδύσασθαι, "not to put on") is direct address.
- **"there abide" → "remain there"** (v10) — Greek μένετε, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a stay in a place: "remain".
- **"whosoever shall not receive you" → "whatever place does not accept you"** (v11) — Greek ὃς ἂν τόπος μὴ δέξηται (WH Treg NA28); δέχομαι "accept". "For a testimony to them": a plain dative (αὐτοῖς), which can also mean "against them".
- **"that men should repent" → "that they should repent"** (v12) — the Greek has no word "men".
- **"his name was spread abroad" → "his name had become manifest"** (v14) — Greek φανερὸν ἐγένετο.
- **"he said" → "they were saying"** (v14) — Greek ἔλεγον (WH NA28 SBLGNT; Treg RP ἔλεγεν, "he was saying").
- **"is risen from the dead" → "has been raised from the dead"** (v14, 16) — Greek ἐγήγερται, ἠγέρθη, passives of ἐγείρω "raise".
- **"mighty works do shew forth themselves in him" → "the powers work in him"** (v14) — Greek ἐνεργοῦσιν αἱ δυνάμεις ἐν αὐτῷ.
- **"laid hold upon" → "took hold of"** (v17) — Greek ἐκράτησεν.
- **"had a quarrel against him" → "was holding it against him"** (v19) — Greek ἐνεῖχεν αὐτῷ, literally "was holding in against him".
- **"he did many things" → "he was much at a loss"** (v20) — Greek ἠπόρει (WH NA28 and the SBLGNT); Treg and RP read ἐποίει, "he was doing many things".
- **"a convenient day" → "an opportune day"** (v21) — Greek εὔκαιρος; "they had no opportunity" in v31 is its verb (εὐκαίρουν).
- **"lords, high captains, and chief estates" → "great men … chiliarchs … first men"** (v21) — Greek μεγιστᾶνες, χιλίαρχοι (each the commander of a thousand; the Greek word is kept, as John 18:12), οἱ πρῶτοι.
- **"the daughter of the said Herodias" → "his daughter Herodias"** (v22) — the verse follows τῆς θυγατρὸς αὐτοῦ Ἡρῳδιάδος (WH NA28): the girl is called Herod's daughter and bears the name Herodias. Treg RP and the SBLGNT read τῆς θυγατρὸς αὐτῆς τῆς Ἡρῳδιάδος, "the daughter of Herodias herself".
- **"and pleased Herod" → "she pleased Herodes"** (v22) — the verse follows ἤρεσεν (WH Treg NA28), a main verb; RP and the SBLGNT read καὶ ἀρεσάσης, "and having pleased". v21–22 are one sentence in Greek.
- **"them that sat with him" → "those reclining with him"** (v22, 26) — Greek συνανακείμενοι, ἀνακείμενοι: guests recline at table.
- **"damsel" → "girl"** (v22, 28) — Greek κοράσιον, the same word as in 5:41–42.
- **"with haste" kept** (v25) — Greek μετὰ σπουδῆς; σπουδή is "diligence" in the letters, here plain haste.
- **"exceeding sorry" → "deeply grieved"** (v26) — Greek περίλυπος.
- **"he would not reject her" → "did not want to set her aside"** (v26) — Greek ἀθετῆσαι αὐτήν, "to set aside", used of annulling a word or a covenant: he did not want to break his word to her.
- **"an executioner" → "a guard"** (v27) — Greek σπεκουλάτωρ, the Latin word *speculator*: a soldier of the bodyguard, also used as a courier and executioner.
- **"his corpse" kept** (v29) — Greek πτῶμα.
- **"a desert place" kept** (v31, 32, 35) — Greek ἔρημος τόπος.
- **"when the day was now far spent" → "when the hour was already late"** (v35) — Greek ὥρας πολλῆς γενομένης, literally "much hour having come".
- **"into the country round about" → "into the farms … round about"** (v36, 56) — Greek ἀγροί, as in 5:14.
- **"by companies" → "company by company"** (v39) — Greek συμπόσια συμπόσια, the word doubled; a *symposion* is a party eating and drinking together.
- **"in ranks" → "plot by plot"** (v40) — Greek πρασιαὶ πρασιαί, the word doubled; a πρασιά is a garden bed.
- **"sit down" → "recline"** (v39, 40) — Greek ἀνακλῖναι, ἀνέπεσαν.
- **"twelve baskets full of the fragments" → "fragments, the fullness of twelve baskets"** (v43) — Greek κλάσματα δώδεκα κοφίνων πληρώματα (WH NA28), literally "fragments, fullnesses of twelve baskets".
- **"constrained" → "compelled"** (v45) — Greek ἠνάγκασεν.
- **"unto Bethsaida" → "toward Beit Tzaidah"** (v45) — Greek πρὸς Βηθσαϊδάν.
- **"toiling in rowing" → "tormented in the rowing"** (v48) — Greek βασανιζομένους, the verb of 5:7.
- **"a spirit" → "a phantom"** (v49) — Greek φάντασμα *phantasma*, an apparition; not πνεῦμα.
- **"Be of good cheer" → "Be of good courage"** (v50) — Greek θαρσεῖτε.
- **"it is I" kept** (v50) — Greek ἐγώ εἰμι, literally "I am".
- **"sore amazed in themselves beyond measure" → "exceedingly, beyond measure, amazed in themselves"** (v51) — Greek λίαν ἐκ περισσοῦ … ἐξίσταντο (Treg NA28 RP; WH omits ἐκ περισσοῦ, "beyond measure").
- **"they considered not the miracle of the loaves" → "they had not understood about the loaves"** (v52) — Greek οὐ συνῆκαν ἐπὶ τοῖς ἄρτοις, literally "upon the loaves"; the Greek has no word "miracle".
- **"drew to the shore" → "moored"** (v53) — Greek προσωρμίσθησαν.
- **"in beds" → "on pallets"** (v55) — Greek κράβαττος, a poor man's bed.
- **"were made whole" → "were being saved"** (v56) — Greek ἐσῴζοντο, an imperfect of σῴζω "save", as in 5:23, 28, 34. "Touched him": Greek αὐτοῦ, which can also be "it" (the fringe).

### Notes
- **v3** — "carpenter": Greek τέκτων *tektōn*, a builder or craftsman in wood or stone.
- **v8** — "staff": Greek ῥάβδος, "rod" in Heb 1:8; 9:4.
- **v22** — in v24 and v28 Herodias is the mother; with the reading followed in v22 the daughter bears the same name.
