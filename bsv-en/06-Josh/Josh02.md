# Joshua 2

[1] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun sent from Shittim two men, spies, secretly, saying, "Go, see the land, and ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧." And they went and came to the house of a woman, a prostitute, and her name was ⟦rahab|Rachav|Rahab⟧, and they lay down there.

[2] And it was said to the king of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, saying, "Behold, men have come here tonight from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to search out the land."

[3] And the king of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ sent to ⟦rahab|Rachav|Rahab⟧, saying, "Bring out the men who have come to you, who have come to your house, for to search out all the land they have come."

[4] And the woman took the two men and hid him. And she said, "Yes, the men came to me, and I did not know where they were from.

[5] And it came to pass, when the gate was to be shut, in the darkness, that the men went out. I do not know where the men went. Pursue quickly after them, for you will overtake them."

[6] And she, she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them in the stalks of flax that were arranged for her on the roof.

[7] And the men pursued after them by the way of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, to the fords; and the gate they shut, after the pursuers after them had gone out.

[8] And they, before they lay down, she, she came up to them on the roof,

[9] and she said to the men, "I know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given you the land, and that the dread of you has fallen on us, and that all those who dwell in the land have melted away because of you.

[10] For we have heard how ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dried up the waters of the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧ from before you when you came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and what you did to the two kings of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ who were across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, to ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ and to Og, whom you devoted to destruction.

[11] And we heard, and our heart melted, and spirit rose no more in any man because of you; for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the heavens above and on the earth beneath.

[12] And now, swear to me, please, by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for I have done ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with you, that you also will do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with the house of my father and will give me a sign of truth,

[13] and will keep alive my father and my mother and my brothers and my sisters and all that is theirs, and will deliver our souls from death."

[14] And the men said to her, "Our soul in place of you, to die! If you do not tell this matter of ours, then it shall be, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gives us the land, that we will do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth with you."

[15] And she let them down by the cord through the window, for her house was in the wall of the city wall, and in the city wall she was dwelling.

[16] And she said to them, "To the hill country go, lest the pursuers come upon you; and hide yourselves there three days, until the pursuers return, and afterward you shall go your way."

[17] And the men said to her, "We are clear from this oath of yours that you have made us swear.

[18] Behold, we are coming into the land. This line of scarlet thread you shall tie in the window through which you let us down, and your father and your mother and your brothers and all the house of your father you shall gather to yourself, to the house.

[19] And it shall be, everyone who goes out from the doors of your house to the outside, his blood is on his head, and we are clear; and everyone who is with you in the house, his blood is on our head, if a hand is on him.

[20] And if you tell this matter of ours, then we shall be clear from your oath that you have made us swear."

[21] And she said, "According to your words, so it is." And she sent them away, and they went; and she tied the line of scarlet in the window.

[22] And they went and came to the hill country and dwelt there three days, until the pursuers returned; and the pursuers sought in all the way and did not find.

[23] And the two men returned and came down from the hill country and passed over and came to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun, and they recounted to him all that had found them.

[24] And they said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has given all the land into our hand, and also all those who dwell in the land have melted away because of us."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"sent out of Shittim two men to spy secretly, saying" → "sent from Shittim two men, spies, secretly, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew שְׁנַיִם־אֲנָשִׁים מְרַגְּלִים חֶרֶשׁ לֵאמֹר: מְרַגְּלִים "spies" is a noun beside "men"; חֶרֶשׁ, found only here as an adverb, is "silently, secretly", and by its place it may go with "sent … spies" or with "saying". "Shittim" has the article in the Hebrew (הַשִּׁטִּים "the acacias").
- **"Go view the land, even Jericho" → "Go, see the land, and Jericho"** (v1) — Hebrew רְאוּ אֶת־הָאָרֶץ וְאֶת־יְרִיחוֹ, "the land and Jericho"; רָאָה is "see".
- **"into an harlot's house, named Rahab, and lodged there" → "to the house of a woman, a prostitute, and her name was Rahab, and they lay down there"** (v1) — Hebrew בֵּית־אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה, "a woman, a prostitute", both words; זוֹנָה is the plain word for a prostitute (Gen 38:15; Lev 21:7; Deut 23:18). וַיִּשְׁכְּבוּ is from שָׁכַב "lie down"; "lodge" is another verb (לוּן, 3:1).
- **"it was told the king" → "it was said to the king … saying"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֵּאָמַר … לֵאמֹר. **"to search out the country"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew לַחְפֹּר, literally "to dig" (Deut 1:22); הָאָרֶץ "the land".
- **"the men that are come to thee, which are entered into thine house" → "the men who have come to you, who have come to your house"** (v3) — Hebrew הַבָּאִים אֵלַיִךְ אֲשֶׁר־בָּאוּ לְבֵיתֵךְ, one verb (בּוֹא) twice, and a third time at the end of the verse. "Come to" a woman is also the Hebrew way of saying that a man goes in to her (Gen 16:2); the second clause says what kind of coming is meant. So also v4, "the men came to me".
- **"hid them" → "hid him"** (v4) — Hebrew וַתִּצְפְּנוֹ, with a singular suffix: "and she hid him", though two men are named. It is commonly read as "them" (so the old versions, cited from memory), or taken as "each one of them"; the printed text is kept. צָפַן "hide" (Exod 2:2); v6 has another verb, טָמַן "hide" (Gen 35:4). The verse may also be read "had taken … and hidden".
- **"There came men unto me, but I wist not whence they were" → "Yes, the men came to me, and I did not know where they were from"** (v4) — Hebrew כֵּן "so, right": she grants what was said.
- **"about the time of shutting of the gate, when it was dark" → "when the gate was to be shut, in the darkness"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הַשַּׁעַר לִסְגּוֹר בַּחֹשֶׁךְ, literally "and the gate was for shutting, in the darkness". **"whither the men went I wot not" → "I do not know where the men went"**. **"pursue after them quickly; for ye shall overtake them"** — Hebrew מַהֵר "hastening"; הִשִּׂיג "overtake".
- **"But she had brought them up to the roof of the house, and hid them with the stalks of flax, which she had laid in order upon the roof" → "And she, she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them in the stalks of flax that were arranged for her on the roof"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהִיא, the pronoun written out; פִּשְׁתֵּי הָעֵץ, literally "the flax of the wood", flax still on its stalk; הָעֲרֻכוֹת לָהּ, a passive, "arranged for her" (or "by her"). "Of the house" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the way to Jordan unto the fords: and as soon as they which pursued after them were gone out, they shut the gate" → "by the way of the Jordan, to the fords; and the gate they shut, after the pursuers after them had gone out"** (v7) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ הַיַּרְדֵּן עַל הַמַּעְבְּרוֹת; עַל is "upon, at": as far as the fords; מַעְבָּרוֹת "passing places", from עָבַר "pass over". אַחֲרֵי כַּאֲשֶׁר, literally "after when".
- **"And before they were laid down, she came up unto them" → "And they, before they lay down, she, she came up to them"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהֵמָּה … וְהִיא, both pronouns written out.
- **"your terror is fallen upon us, and that all the inhabitants of the land faint because of you" → "the dread of you has fallen on us, and that all those who dwell in the land have melted away because of you"** (v9, 24) — Hebrew אֵימָה "dread" and נָמֹגוּ "have melted away", the words of Exod 15:15, 16; יֹשְׁבֵי from יָשַׁב "dwell". **"because of you" / "from before you"** (v9, 10, 11, 24) — one Hebrew word, מִפְּנֵיכֶם, literally "from your face".
- **"how the LORD dried up the water of the Red sea for you" → "how Yahweh dried up the waters of the Sea of Reeds from before you"** (v10) — Hebrew אֵת אֲשֶׁר "that which"; יַם־סוּף *yam suf*, "sea of reeds". **"when ye came out of Egypt"** — Hebrew מִמִּצְרָיִם. **"the two kings of the Amorites, that were on the other side Jordan" → "the two kings of the Amorite who were across the Jordan"** — Hebrew הָאֱמֹרִי, a singular with the article; בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן, here the east side.
- **"whom ye utterly destroyed" → "whom you devoted to destruction"** (v10) — Hebrew הֶחֱרַמְתֶּם, from חֵרֶם: to give over to Yahweh beyond recall, as a rule by destroying (Deut 2:34; 3:6).
- **"as soon as we had heard these things, our hearts did melt, neither did there remain any more courage in any man" → "And we heard, and our heart melted, and spirit rose no more in any man"** (v11) — Hebrew וַנִּשְׁמַע וַיִּמַּס לְבָבֵנוּ, two plain clauses, "our heart" singular; נָמֵס "melt" is another verb than "melt away" in v9; וְלֹא־קָמָה עוֹד רוּחַ בְּאִישׁ, literally "and there rose no more spirit in a man": רוּחַ is "spirit, breath, wind".
- **"he is God in heaven above, and in earth beneath" → "he is *Elohim* in the heavens above and on the earth beneath"** (v11) — the words of Deut 4:39; שָׁמַיִם is plural.
- **"swear unto me by the LORD, since I have shewed you kindness, that ye will also shew kindness unto my father's house, and give me a true token" → "swear to me, please, by Yahweh, for I have done *chesed* with you, that you also will do *chesed* with the house of my father and will give me a sign of truth"** (v12) — Hebrew נָא "please"; כִּי־עָשִׂיתִי עִמָּכֶם חָסֶד וַעֲשִׂיתֶם גַּם־אַתֶּם, literally "that I have done *chesed* with you, and you also shall do": the כִּי may be "for" or "that". חֶסֶד *chesed* is faithful love shown in deeds, here to one who has no claim on it. אוֹת אֱמֶת "a sign of truth": a sign that can be trusted.
- **"that ye will save alive … and deliver our lives" → "and will keep alive … and will deliver our souls"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהַחֲיִתֶם, the causative of "live"; נַפְשֹׁתֵינוּ "our souls". **"my sisters"** — the reading tradition (qere) אַחְיוֹתַי; the written form (ketiv) is "my sister". **"all that they have" → "all that is theirs"** — Hebrew כָּל־אֲשֶׁר לָהֶם.
- **"Our life for yours, if ye utter not this our business. And it shall be" → "Our soul in place of you, to die! If you do not tell this matter of ours, then it shall be"** (v14) — Hebrew נַפְשֵׁנוּ תַחְתֵּיכֶם לָמוּת: נֶפֶשׁ "soul", singular; תַּחַת "in the place of"; לָמוּת "to die". "You" in "in place of you" and "you do not tell" is plural (Rahab and her house); in "with you" it is singular. דְּבָרֵנוּ זֶה "this word of ours": דָּבָר is "word" or "matter". **"we will deal kindly and truly with thee" → "we will do *chesed* and truth with you"** — Hebrew חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת (Gen 24:49).
- **"by a cord through the window: for her house was upon the town wall, and she dwelt upon the wall" → "by the cord through the window, for her house was in the wall of the city wall, and in the city wall she was dwelling"** (v15) — Hebrew בַּחֶבֶל, with the article; בְּקִיר הַחוֹמָה: two words for "wall", קִיר (the wall of a house, a side) and חוֹמָה (the wall round a city); "city" is added to keep them apart. יוֹשָׁבֶת is a participle.
- **"Get you to the mountain, lest the pursuers meet you" → "To the hill country go, lest the pursuers come upon you"** (v16, 22, 23) — Hebrew הָהָרָה, literally "to the mountain": the hills west of Jericho; פָּגַע בְּ "come upon". **"hide yourselves"** — Hebrew וְנַחְבֵּתֶם. **"afterward may ye go your way" → "afterward you shall go your way"** — Hebrew תֵּלְכוּ.
- **"We will be blameless of this thine oath" → "We are clear from this oath of yours"** (v17, 19, 20) — Hebrew נְקִיִּם אֲנַחְנוּ, with no verb; נָקִי "clear, free of guilt" (Gen 24:8, 41). "This" (הַזֶּה) is masculine though "oath" is feminine; so also "this line" in v18.
- **"Behold, when we come into the land, thou shalt bind this line of scarlet thread in the window" → "Behold, we are coming into the land. This line of scarlet thread you shall tie in the window"** (v18) — Hebrew הִנֵּה אֲנַחְנוּ בָאִים בָּאָרֶץ, a participle, with no "when"; the Hebrew has no "unless" between v17 and v18: the conditions are set side by side with the oath, and v20 names what frees the men. תִּקְוָה "line, cord" (found in this sense only here; the same letters are the word "hope"); חוּט "thread"; שָׁנִי "scarlet". **"thou shalt bring … home unto thee" → "you shall gather to yourself, to the house"** — Hebrew תַּאַסְפִי אֵלַיִךְ הַבָּיְתָה.
- **"into the street" → "to the outside"** (v19) — Hebrew הַחוּצָה. **"his blood shall be upon his head, and we will be guiltless" → "his blood is on his head, and we are clear"** — no verb in the Hebrew. **"if any hand be upon him" → "if a hand is on him"** — Hebrew אִם־יָד תִּהְיֶה־בּוֹ.
- **"so be it" → "so it is"** (v21) — Hebrew כֶּן־הוּא. **"sent them away"** — Hebrew שִׁלַּח. **"the scarlet line" → "the line of scarlet"** — Hebrew תִּקְוַת הַשָּׁנִי.
- **"abode there three days" → "dwelt there three days"** (v22) — Hebrew יָשַׁב. **"sought them throughout all the way, but found them not" → "sought in all the way and did not find"** — the Hebrew has no object.
- **"descended from the mountain, and passed over … and told him all things that befell them" → "came down from the hill country and passed over … and they recounted to him all that had found them"** (v23) — Hebrew סִפֵּר "recount"; הַמֹּצְאוֹת אוֹתָם, literally "the things finding them" (Deut 31:17).
- **"Truly the LORD hath delivered into our hands all the land; for even all the inhabitants of the country do faint because of us" → "Yahweh has given all the land into our hand, and also all those who dwell in the land have melted away because of us"** (v24) — Hebrew כִּי opens the speech and is not translated ("Truly" is KJV's rendering of it); נָתַן "give"; בְּיָדֵנוּ "into our hand", singular; וְגַם "and also".

### Notes
- **Rahab, the unlikely heroine** — a Canaanite prostitute becomes the saver of Israelite spies. Multiple NT resonances:
  - Heb 11:31 — listed among heroes of faith
  - James 2:25 — justified by works
  - Mt 1:5 — Rahab in Jesus' genealogy (mother of Boaz, ancestor of David)
- **"Melted away"** — threefold repetition (v9, 11, 24; two Hebrew verbs). The Canaanites' dread fulfills Yahweh's prediction (Ex 15:15–16, Deut 2:25).
- **Rahab's confession** (v11) — "Yahweh your *Elohim*, he is *Elohim* in the heavens above and on the earth beneath." The same formula used by Moses (Deut 4:39). Remarkable monotheistic profession from a Canaanite.
- **Scarlet line** (v18, 21) — sign of preservation; early Christian interpretation (1 Clement 12) compared to the Passover blood and to Christ's blood.
- **Rahab's deception** (v4–6) — she lies to protect the spies. Ancient and modern ethicists debate; her deception is not explicitly approved, but her faith-act is praised.
- **Parallel to Passover protection** — Rahab's household marked and kept safe amid general destruction, like Israel's houses at Egypt's tenth plague.
