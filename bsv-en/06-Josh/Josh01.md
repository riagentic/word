# Joshua 1

[1] And it came to pass after the death of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun, the minister of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ my servant is dead; and now arise, pass over this ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, you and all this people, to the land that I am giving to them, to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] Every place on which the sole of your foot treads, to you I have given it, as I spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[4] From the wilderness and this ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ and as far as the great river, the river ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧, all the land of the ⟦hittites|Chittim|Hittites⟧, and as far as the Great Sea, the going in of the sun, shall be your border.

[5] No man shall take his stand before you all the days of your life. As I was with ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, I will be with you; I will not let you drop and I will not forsake you.

[6] Be strong and be firm, for you, you shall make this people inherit the land that I swore to their fathers to give to them.

[7] Only be strong and be very firm, to keep to do according to all the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ my servant commanded you; do not turn aside from it to the right or to the left, so that you may act with insight wherever you go.

[8] This book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ shall not depart from your mouth, and you shall softly recite it day and night, so that you may keep to do according to all that is written in it; for then you shall make your ways prosper, and then you shall act with insight.

[9] Have I not commanded you? Be strong and be firm; do not be terrified and do not be dismayed, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is with you wherever you go."

[10] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ commanded the officers of the people, saying,

[11] "Pass through the midst of the camp and command the people, saying, 'Prepare provisions for yourselves, for in yet three days you are passing over this ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, to go in to take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to you to possess it.'"

[12] And to the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenite⟧ and to the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadite⟧ and to the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said, saying,

[13] "Remember the word that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded you, saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving rest to you, and he will give you this land.'

[14] Your wives, your little ones and your livestock shall dwell in the land that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ gave you across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧; and you, you shall pass over in battle array before your brothers, all the mighty ones of ability, and you shall help them,

[15] until ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gives rest to your brothers as to you, and they also take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving to them; and you shall return to the land of your possession and take possession of it, which ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave you across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, toward the rising of the sun."

[16] And they answered ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, saying, "All that you have commanded us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.

[17] According to all that we listened to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so we will listen to you; only may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be with you, as he was with ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[18] Every man who rebels against your mouth and does not hear your words, in all that you command him, shall be put to death. Only be strong and be firm."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Now after the death of Moses … it came to pass, that the LORD spake" → "And it came to pass after the death of Moses … that Yahweh said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי אַחֲרֵי מוֹת מֹשֶׁה … וַיֹּאמֶר; the verb is אָמַר "say". The book opens with "And", joined to the end of Deuteronomy. **"servant of the LORD"** (v1, 2, 7, 13, 15) — Hebrew עֶבֶד "slave, servant".
- **"Moses' minister" → "the minister of Moses"** (v1) — Hebrew מְשָׁרֵת, the participle of שֵׁרֵת "minister to, attend on a person" (Exod 24:13; 33:11; Num 11:28).
- **"go over" → "pass over"** (v2, 11, 14) — Hebrew עָבַר, one verb through the chapter and the book; in v11 עִבְרוּ בְּקֶרֶב "pass through the midst of" is the same verb. **"Now therefore" → "and now"** (v2) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה. **"unto the land which I do give to them, even to the children of Israel" → "to the land that I am giving to them, to the sons of Israel"** — Hebrew נֹתֵן, a participle; לָהֶם לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, "to them, to the sons of Israel", side by side; בְּנֵי is "sons of".
- **"Every place that the sole of your foot shall tread upon, that have I given unto you" → "Every place on which the sole of your foot treads, to you I have given it"** (v3) — Hebrew כָּל־מָקוֹם אֲשֶׁר תִּדְרֹךְ כַּף־רַגְלְכֶם בּוֹ לָכֶם נְתַתִּיו, the words of Deut 11:24 with "to you I have given it" (a perfect) for "shall be yours". "Your" and "you" are plural in v3, 4; from v5 on Joshua alone is addressed. **"as I said" → "as I spoke"** — Hebrew דִּבַּרְתִּי.
- **"even unto the great river … and unto the great sea toward the going down of the sun, shall be your coast" → "and as far as the great river … and as far as the Great Sea, the going in of the sun, shall be your border"** (v4) — Hebrew וְעַד "and as far as", twice; מְבוֹא הַשָּׁמֶשׁ, literally "the going in of the sun", the west (Deut 11:30), stands beside "the Great Sea" with no word "toward"; גְּבוּל "border", also the land within it. "This Lebanon": הַלְּבָנוֹן הַזֶּה, with the article and "this".
- **"There shall not any man be able to stand before thee" → "No man shall take his stand before you"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־יִתְיַצֵּב אִישׁ לְפָנֶיךָ, as Deut 7:24; 11:25; "be able" is not in the Hebrew. **"so I will be with thee" → "I will be with you"** — Hebrew אֶהְיֶה עִמָּךְ, with no "so"; אֶהְיֶה is the verb of Exod 3:12, 14.
- **"I will not fail thee, nor forsake thee" → "I will not let you drop and I will not forsake you"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא אַרְפְּךָ וְלֹא אֶעֶזְבֶךָּ: the causative of רָפָה "sink, be slack" (let go limp, let sink, let drop) and עָזַב "forsake"; the words of Deut 31:6, 8 in the first person. Heb 13:5 cites them.
- **"Be strong and of a good courage" → "Be strong and be firm"** (v6, 7, 9, 18) — Hebrew חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ, two verbs for being strong: חָזַק "be strong" and אָמֵץ "be strong, firm"; "courage" is not a word of the Hebrew. The words of Deut 31:6, 7, 23. In v7 "very" (מְאֹד) follows the second verb.
- **"for unto this people shalt thou divide for an inheritance the land" → "for you, you shall make this people inherit the land"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי אַתָּה תַּנְחִיל, the causative of נָחַל "inherit", with the pronoun "you" written out (Deut 1:38; 3:28; 31:7).
- **"that thou mayest observe to do according to all the law" → "to keep to do according to all the Torah"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew לִשְׁמֹר לַעֲשׂוֹת, the phrase of Deuteronomy ("keep to do": be careful to do); תּוֹרָה *torah*, "instruction, law". **"turn not from it" → "do not turn aside from it"** — Hebrew סוּר; מִמֶּנּוּ "from it" is masculine, though תּוֹרָה is feminine. **"to the right hand or to the left"** — Hebrew יָמִין וּשְׂמֹאול, literally "right and left".
- **"that thou mayest prosper whithersoever thou goest" → "so that you may act with insight wherever you go"** (v7); **"then thou shalt have good success" → "then you shall act with insight"** (v8) — Hebrew תַּשְׂכִּיל both times, from הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight, act with insight" (Deut 29:9), and so also "deal wisely with success". KJV has "prosper" for it in v7 and "have good success" in v8; "prosper" is another verb in v8. **"wherever you go"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew בְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר תֵּלֵךְ, literally "in all that you go".
- **"thou shalt meditate therein" → "you shall softly recite it"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהָגִיתָ בּוֹ. הָגָה is to give a low sound: of a lion growling (Isa 31:4), a dove moaning (Isa 38:14), of a man speaking low, reciting half aloud, and so "muse" (Ps 1:2 has the same words). It is said here of a book that is not to depart "from your mouth". "Meditate" names only the thinking; the Hebrew names the sound. "Softly recite" keeps the sound without the blame that "mutter" carries in English; literally "utter low in it". The earlier draft "mutter" was meant without blame: to say over in a low voice.
- **"then thou shalt make thy way prosperous" → "then you shall make your ways prosper"** (v8) — Hebrew תַּצְלִיחַ אֶת־דְּרָכֶךָ: הִצְלִיחַ "make prosper"; דְּרָכֶךָ is pointed as a plural, "your ways", though written without the letter that marks the plural (the consonants alone can be read "your way").
- **"Have not I commanded thee? Be strong"** (v9) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא צִוִּיתִיךָ חֲזַק וֶאֱמָץ; the words may also be read as one sentence, "Have I not commanded you, 'Be strong and be firm'?" (Deut 31:23). **"be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed" → "do not be terrified and do not be dismayed"** — Hebrew עָרַץ "be terrified" (Deut 1:29; 31:6) and חָתַת "be dismayed", literally "be shattered" (Deut 31:8). **"the LORD thy God is with thee"** — the Hebrew has no verb: "with you [is] Yahweh your Elohim".
- **"officers"** (v10) — Hebrew שֹׁטְרִים, lower officials who keep order and lists (Deut 1:15; 20:5).
- **"Pass through the host" → "Pass through the midst of the camp"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּקֶרֶב הַמַּחֲנֶה. **"victuals" → "provisions"** — Hebrew צֵידָה, food for the way. **"within three days ye shall pass over" → "in yet three days you are passing over"** — Hebrew בְּעוֹד שְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים (Gen 40:13), עֹבְרִים a participle.
- **"to go in to possess the land, which the LORD your God giveth you to possess it" → "to go in to take possession of the land that Yahweh your Elohim is giving to you to possess it"** (v11) — Hebrew לָבוֹא לָרֶשֶׁת … לְרִשְׁתָּהּ: the verb יָרַשׁ twice ("take possession of" and "possess" are one word), as in Deuteronomy; so also v15, where the noun יְרֻשָּׁה "possession" and the verb stand together.
- **"to the Reubenites, and to the Gadites, and to half the tribe of Manasseh, spake Joshua, saying" → "to the Reubenite and to the Gadite and to the half of the tribe of Manasseh Joshua said, saying"** (v12) — Hebrew singulars with the article, הָראוּבֵנִי, הַגָּדִי; "Manasseh" also has the article (הַמְנַשֶּׁה); שֵׁבֶט "tribe", literally "rod"; אָמַר … לֵאמֹר "said, saying".
- **"Remember"** (v13) — Hebrew זָכוֹר, the infinitive used as a command (Exod 20:8). **"hath given you rest, and hath given you this land" → "is giving rest to you, and he will give you this land"** — Hebrew מֵנִיחַ, a participle, and וְנָתַן, a perfect with "and", which looks forward. The words answer to Deut 3:18–20.
- **"shall remain in the land" → "shall dwell in the land"** (v14) — Hebrew יָשַׁב. **"on this side Jordan" → "across the Jordan"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן, here the east side, said by a speaker who stands on it; the same words name the west side elsewhere (Deut 3:20, 25).
- **"ye shall pass before your brethren armed, all the mighty men of valour" → "you, you shall pass over in battle array before your brothers, all the mighty ones of ability"** (v14) — Hebrew חֲמֻשִׁים, of uncertain meaning: "in battle array", "armed", or "in fifties" (Exod 13:18); Deut 3:18 has another word, "armed" (חֲלוּצִים). גִּבּוֹרֵי הַחַיִל: גִּבּוֹר "mighty one" and חַיִל "ability, strength, force" (Deut 3:18 "sons of ability").
- **"then ye shall return unto the land of your possession, and enjoy it" → "and you shall return to the land of your possession and take possession of it"** (v15) — Hebrew וִירִשְׁתֶּם אוֹתָהּ, the verb יָרַשׁ once more; "enjoy" is not in the word. The clause "which Moses … gave you" follows it in the Hebrew and is left in its place. **"toward the sunrising" → "toward the rising of the sun"** — Hebrew מִזְרַח הַשָּׁמֶשׁ.
- **"they answered Joshua, saying"** (v16) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר, kept. **"whithersoever thou sendest us" → "wherever you send us"** — Hebrew אֶל־כָּל־אֲשֶׁר תִּשְׁלָחֵנוּ, literally "to all where you send us".
- **"According as we hearkened unto Moses in all things" → "According to all that we listened to Moses"** (v17) — Hebrew כְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר־שָׁמַעְנוּ אֶל־מֹשֶׁה; שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to".
- **"Whosoever he be that doth rebel against thy commandment, and will not hearken unto thy words" → "Every man who rebels against your mouth and does not hear your words"** (v18) — Hebrew יַמְרֶה אֶת־פִּיךָ, literally "rebels against your mouth" (Deut 1:26, 43; מָרָה, another verb than מָרַד "rebel"); שָׁמַע אֶת "hear".

### Notes
- **Opening of the Former Prophets** — Joshua begins the "Former Prophets" (Joshua, Judges, Samuel, Kings) in Jewish canon; "Historical Books" in Christian Bibles.
- **"Servant of Yahweh"** (v1, 13, 15) — Moses' title. Joshua is not called that yet — he's "the minister of Moses." Only at book's end (Josh 24:29) does he earn the same title.
- **Be strong and be firm** — four-fold repetition (v6, 7, 9, 18). Joshua must internalize it before leading.
- **Softly recite day and night** (v8) — Hebrew הָגָה *hagah*. Low reciting of Torah — rumination, memorization-by-recitation. Ps 1:2 echoes.
- **Transjordan tribes' commitment** (v12–18) — they remember their Num 32 promise: fight across the Jordan first, then return home.
- **"Shall be put to death"** (v18) — the people affirm Joshua's authority even to capital sentence. Transition of leadership confirmed.
