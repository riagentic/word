## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"straitly shut up" → "shut and shut up"** (v1) — Hebrew סֹגֶרֶת וּמְסֻגֶּרֶת, two participles of one root: the active "shutting" (its gates) and the passive "shut up". מִפְּנֵי is literally "from the face of".
- **"the mighty men of valour" → "the mighty ones of ability"** (v2) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרֵי הֶחָיִל, set beside "its king" without "and". חַיִל is ability, strength, also an armed force.
- **"compass" → "go around" / "circling"** (v3, 4, 7, 11, 14, 15) — two Hebrew verbs: סָבַב "go around" and הַקֵּיף "circling" (v3, 11). In v3 "you shall go around" is plural and "you shall do" singular. In v11 the verb is causative: "he made the ark go around"; it can also be read "the ark went around".
- **"seven trumpets of rams' horns" → "seven shofars of the rams' horns"** (v4, 6, 8, 13) — Hebrew שׁוֹפְרוֹת הַיּוֹבְלִים. שׁוֹפָר *shofar* is the horn that is blown; יוֹבֵל *yovel* is the ram and its horn (Exod 19:13), the word that names the jubilee. v6 has no article. **"bear" / "take up" → "carry" / "lift up"** (v4, 6, 8, 12, 13) — one Hebrew verb, נָשָׂא "lift up, carry": "lift up" of the ark in v6, 12 (as 3:6), "carry" of the shofars.
- **"when they make a long blast with the ram's horn" → "at the drawing out on the horn of the ram"** (v5) — Hebrew בִּמְשֹׁךְ בְּקֶרֶן הַיּוֹבֵל, as Exod 19:13; here the "horn" (קֶרֶן) is named beside the *yovel*, so "ram" stands for it. **"when you hear"** — the written text has "in your hearing", the reading tradition "as you hear": no difference in sense.
- **"sound of the trumpet" → "voice of the shofar"** (v5, 20) — Hebrew קוֹל, the same word as "your voice" in v10.
- **"shout with a great shout" → "shout a great shout"** (v5, 10, 16, 20) — Hebrew הֵרִיעַ and תְּרוּעָה, here of the people's voices. On an instrument the same words are a "blast" (Num 10:5).
- **"fall down flat" → "fall in its place"** (v5, 20) — Hebrew תַּחְתֶּיהָ, literally "under itself". **"every man straight before him"** — literally "a man in front of him".
- **"And he said"** (v7) — the reading tradition; the written text has "And they said". **"pass on"** — Hebrew עָבַר "pass over". **"him that is armed" → "the armed men"** (v7, 9, 13) — Hebrew הֶחָלוּץ, a singular for the whole body.
- **"when Joshua had spoken" → "had said this"** (v8) — Hebrew כֶּאֱמֹר "as … said"; "this" is supplied.
- **"the priests that blew" (v9)** — the reading tradition, literally "the blowers of the shofars"; the written text has "they blew". **"the priests going on, and blowing" → "going on and blowing"** (v9, 13) — two Hebrew infinitives without a subject; "the priests" is not written. **"rereward" → "rear guard"** — Hebrew הַמְאַסֵּף, literally "the gatherer".
- **"after the same manner" → "according to this manner"** (v15) — Hebrew כַּמִּשְׁפָּט הַזֶּה; מִשְׁפָּט is "judgment", here the fixed way of doing a thing.
- **"accursed" → "a thing devoted to destruction" / "the devoted thing"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew חֵרֶם *cherem*: what is given over to Yahweh beyond recall, here by destroying it. **"utterly destroyed" → "devoted to destruction"** (v21) — the verb of the same root.
- **"harlot" → "prostitute"** (v17, 22, 25) — Hebrew זוֹנָה. **"messengers"** (v17, 25) — Hebrew מַלְאָכִים, the word also used for angels.
- **"keep yourselves from the accursed thing, lest ye make yourselves accursed, when ye take" → "keep from the devoted thing, lest you devote to destruction and take"** (v18) — Hebrew פֶּן־תַּחֲרִימוּ וּלְקַחְתֶּם. The Septuagint is commonly cited as "lest you covet" (from memory; compare 7:21); the Masoretic Text is kept. **"trouble it"** — Hebrew עָכַר, the word of 7:25, 26.
- **"consecrated unto the LORD" → "holy to Yahweh"** (v19) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ, the noun "holiness". **"brass" → "bronze"** (v19, 24).
- **"both man and woman, young and old" → "from man to woman, from boy to old man"** (v21) — Hebrew מִן … וְעַד "from … to". לְפִי־חָרֶב is literally "to the mouth of the sword".
- **"spied out" / "the young men, the spies"** (v22, 23) — the same two are "men" in v22 and "young men" (נְעָרִים) in v23. **"all her kindred" → "all her clans"** (v23) — Hebrew מִשְׁפְּחוֹתֶיהָ, plural. **"left them" → "set them"** — Hebrew הִנִּיחַ "set down, leave".
- **"saved alive" → "kept alive"** (v25) — Hebrew הֶחֱיָה.
- **"adjured them" → "made them swear"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁבַּע, causative of "swear"; the Hebrew names no object, "them" is supplied. **"Cursed be the man" → "Cursed is the man"** — the Hebrew has no verb. **"buildeth"** — Hebrew בָּנָה "build"; "rebuild" is not a separate word. **"in his firstborn … in his youngest" → "With his firstborn … with his youngest"** — Hebrew בְּ: at the price of (1 Kgs 16:34). "Youngest" is literally "young one" (צָעִיר). **"gates" → "doors"** — Hebrew דְּלָתֶיהָ.
- **"his fame was noised" → "the report of him was"** (v27) — Hebrew שָׁמְעוֹ, from "hear".

### Notes
- **Seven-day siege** — not a military assault but a liturgical procession. Seven priests + seven shofars + seven days + seven circuits on the seventh day.
- **The shout and the wall** — the wall falls not by battering ram but at the shout and the shofar.
- **Rahab kept alive** (v22–25) — the oath of chapter 2 is kept. She and her father's house are preserved; she "dwelt in the midst of Israel to this day" (cf. Matt 1:5).
- **Cherem violated in ch 7** — the silver and gold are "holy to Yahweh" (v19); Achan's taking from the devoted thing leads to the defeat at Ai.
- **Jericho curse** (v26) — 1 Kgs 16:34: Hiel of Bethel built Jericho in Ahab's time, "with Abiram his firstborn" and "with Segub his youngest".
- **Archaeology of Jericho** — much debated. Tell es-Sultan has Bronze Age fortifications that appear to have collapsed suddenly; the dating is contested.
- **"House of Yahweh"** (v24) — v19 has "the treasury of Yahweh"; the temple did not yet stand.
