## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. In the lists of towns "and" stands exactly where the Hebrew has it. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"families" → "clans"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחוֹת. **"tribe"** is מַטֶּה (literally "staff") in every place in this chapter.
- **"This then was the lot … even to the border of Edom the wilderness of Zin southward was the uttermost part of the south coast" → "And the lot … was to the border of Edom, the wilderness of Zin southward, at the end of the south"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶל־גְּבוּל אֱדוֹם מִדְבַּר־צִן נֶגְבָּה מִקְצֵה תֵימָן, literally "… from the end of the south"; two words for "south" stand here, נֶגֶב and תֵּימָן. גּוֹרָל is "lot"; גְּבוּל is "border" in every place (KJV "coast", "border").
- **The quarters of the sky** — in the verses "south", "north", "east", "west"; the Hebrew words are "Negev" (נֶגֶב), "hidden side" (צָפוֹן), "front" (קֶדֶם) and "sea" (יָם). Where הַנֶּגֶב is the region it keeps its name (v19, 21).
- **"went out … passed along … ascended up … fetched a compass" → "goes out … passes on … goes up … turns"** (v3–11) — the border is described as walking, one English verb for each Hebrew verb, as in Num 34: יָצָא "go out", עָבַר "pass on", עָלָה "go up", יָרַד "go down", נָסַב "turn", תָּאַר "bend" (v9, 11). The meaning of תָּאַר as a verb of the border (only in Josh 15; 18) is not certain: "bend, incline" or "be drawn, be marked out". The Hebrew forms are not the narrative past: after "And their south border was" (v2, וַיְהִי) the course is told in the descriptive form (וְיָצָא, וְעָבַר …); it is rendered by the English present. **"the going out"** — תּוֹצָאוֹת, "outgoings": the place where the border comes out, its end (v4, 7, 11). In v4 the written text has the verb in the singular (והיה), the reading tradition in the plural (וְהָיוּ); the meaning is the same.
- **"from the bay that looketh southward" → "from the tongue that faces southward"** (v2) — Hebrew הַלָּשֹׁן, "the tongue": a tongue of the sea (or of land). So also v5, "the tongue of the sea".
- **"to the south side to Maalehacrabbim" → "to the south of the ascent of Akrabbim"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶל־מִנֶּגֶב לְמַעֲלֵה עַקְרַבִּים; מִנֶּגֶב לְ is "from the south of", as Num 34:4. מַעֲלֶה is an ascent, a way up; "Akrabbim" means "scorpions". "Karka" has the article in Hebrew (הַקַּרְקָעָה, "the floor").
- **"the river of Egypt" → "the wadi of Egypt"** (v4, 47) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed; not the Nile. **"this shall be your south coast" → "This shall be your south border"** (v4) — "your" is plural in the Hebrew (לָכֶם): the sentence speaks to Israel, as Num 34:3–6 does.
- **"the end of Jordan"** (v5) — Hebrew קְצֵה הַיַּרְדֵּן: where the Jordan ends, at the Salt Sea. "Mouth" is not the Hebrew word. **"in the north quarter" → "for the north side"** — Hebrew לִפְאַת צָפוֹנָה; פֵּאָה is "side".
- **"toward Debir from the valley of Achor, and so northward, looking toward Gilgal" → "to Debir from the valley of Achor, and northward, facing toward Gilgal"** (v7) — Hebrew וְצָפוֹנָה פֹּנֶה אֶל־הַגִּלְגָּל: a participle, "facing", as in v2; what it is that faces Gilgal (the border, or Debir) is not said. "Gilgal" has the article. **"before the going up to Adummim" → "opposite the ascent of Adummim"** — Hebrew נֹכַח. **"on the south side of the river" → "from the south of the wadi"** — נַחַל. **"the going out thereof were at Enrogel"** — the Hebrew has אֶל, "to".
- **"valley" for two Hebrew words** (v7, 8) — עֵמֶק (the valley of Achor, of Rephaim) is a broad valley, גַּיְא (the valley of Ben-hinnom, of Hinnom) a narrow one. English has one word; both stand in v8.
- **"the south side of the Jebusite" → "the shoulder of the Jebusite from the south"** (v8) — Hebrew כֶּתֶף "shoulder": the slope or ridge of a hill; so also v10, 11. **"the valley of the giants" → "the valley of Rephaim"** — Hebrew עֵמֶק־רְפָאִים. **"that lieth before the valley of Hinnom" → "that is facing the valley of Hinnom"** — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי, literally "upon the face of".
- **"the border was drawn" → "the border bends"** (v9, 11) — Hebrew וְתָאַר; see above. **"the fountain of the water of Nephtoah" → "the spring of the waters of Nephtoah"** (v9) — מַעְיָן.
- **"mount Jearim, which is Chesalon, on the north side" → "the shoulder of Mount Jearim from the north — that is Chesalon"** (v10) — the Hebrew order: "from the north" belongs to the shoulder. הַר is "mountain / mount" here and "hill country" of a region (v48); one Hebrew word.
- **"the sea coast" → "the Great Sea and the border"** (v12, 47) — Hebrew הַיָּמָּה הַגָּדוֹל וּגְבוּל, literally "the Great Sea and border", as Num 34:6. What "and border" adds is not certain: the sea and its shore, or "the sea, as border". In v47 the written text has "the border sea" (הגבול); the reading tradition has "the Great Sea" (הַגָּדוֹל), which is translated.
- **"a part" → "a share"** (v13) — Hebrew חֵלֶק. **"according to the commandment of the LORD to Joshua" → "at the mouth of Yahweh to Joshua"** — Hebrew אֶל־פִּי יְהוָה, literally "to the mouth of"; "commandment" is not a word of the Hebrew. **"the city of Arba the father of Anak" → "Kiriath-arba, the father of the Anak"** — Hebrew קִרְיַת אַרְבַּע אֲבִי הָעֲנָק. "Kiriath-arba" is "the city of Arba"; "the father of the Anak" is said of Arba, whose name stands within the name of the city. No "Arba was" is supplied. "The Anak" has the article in v13, 14.
- **"drove thence" → "dispossessed from there"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיֹּרֶשׁ, the causative of יָרַשׁ "possess": to take another's place and holding; so also v63. **"the children of Anak" → "those born of the Anak"** — Hebrew יְלִידֵי הָעֲנָק, as Num 13:22.
- **"smiteth … and taketh it" → "strikes … and captures it"** (v16, 17) — Hebrew הִכָּה, לָכַד. **"the brother of Caleb"** (v17) — the Hebrew does not say whether Othniel or Kenaz is the brother of Caleb.
- **"as she came unto him, that she moved him to ask" → "when she came, that she incited him to ask"** (v18) — Hebrew בְּבוֹאָהּ, "at her coming"; "unto him" is not in the Hebrew. הֵסִית is "incite, urge on". **"she lighted off her ass" → "she alighted from the donkey"** — Hebrew וַתִּצְנַח, a verb found only here, in Judg 1:14 and in Judg 4:21 (of a tent peg going down into the ground); "came down" is the sense the context gives; uncertain. The Hebrew has "from upon the donkey". **"What wouldest thou?" → "What ails you?"** — Hebrew מַה־לָּךְ, literally "What to you?", as Gen 21:17.
- **"thou hast given me a south land; give me also springs of water" → "the land of the Negev you have given me; and you shall give me basins of water"** (v19) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ הַנֶּגֶב נְתַתָּנִי, with two objects: "you have given me the land of the Negev", or "you have given me away to the land of the Negev" (a dry land). וְנָתַתָּה לִי is "and you shall give me"; "also" is not in the Hebrew. גֻּלֹּת is the plural of גֻּלָּה "bowl, basin" (Zech 4:3; Eccl 12:6): basins or pools of water; "springs" is the old rendering. The word may also be a place name ("Gulloth-maim", "Upper Gulloth", "Lower Gulloth"). Uncertain.
- **"the uttermost cities … toward the coast of Edom southward" → "the cities at the end of the tribe … toward the border of Edom, in the Negev"** (v21) — Hebrew מִקְצֵה לְמַטֵּה, literally "from the end, of the tribe"; בַּנֶּגְבָּה has the article and names the first of the four regions of the list: the Negev (v21), the Shephelah (v33), the hill country (v48), the wilderness (v61).
- **Counts and names** (v32, 36) — the Hebrew numbers are kept as they stand. In v21–32 the list has thirty-six names (if "Hazor-hadattah" and "Kerioth-hezron" are each one name), the sum "twenty-nine"; in v33–36 there are fifteen names, the sum "fourteen". The other sums agree with their lists (v41, 44, 51, 54, 57, 59, 60, 62).
- **"in the valley" → "in the Shephelah"** (v33) — Hebrew הַשְּׁפֵלָה, "the lowland": the foothills between the hill country and the coast; the name is kept, as Deut 1:7.
- **"with her towns and her villages" → "and its daughters and its villages"** (v45, 47) — Hebrew בְּנֹתֶיהָ, "its daughters": the settlements that belong to a city, as Num 21:25. חָצֵר is a village, an unwalled settlement.
- **"from Ekron even unto the sea, all that lay near Ashdod" → "from Ekron and westward, all that is alongside Ashdod"** (v46) — Hebrew וָיָמָּה, "and seaward"; עַל־יַד, literally "at the hand of".
- **"Dumah" → "Rumah"** (v52) — the Leningrad Codex, which is translated here, has רוּמָה. Other Hebrew manuscripts and printed editions have דוּמָה "Dumah" (so the KJV); ר and ד are easily confused.
- **"Janum"** (v53) — the reading tradition (יָנוּם); the written text has ינים, "Janim".
- **A longer Greek text after v59** — the Greek translation has here a further group of eleven towns, among them Tekoa and Bethlehem (cited from memory, not checked here). The Hebrew text does not have it, and it is not in the verse.
- **"could not drive them out … dwell" → "could not dispossess them … has dwelt"** (v63) — Hebrew לְהוֹרִישָׁם; יָשַׁב "dwell". "The Jebusite" is a singular for the whole people. The written text has יוכלו, the reading tradition יָכְלוּ; the meaning is the same.
- **Names with the article** — the Hebrew has the article with Gilgal (v7), Karka (v3), Mount "the Baalah" (v11), Enam (v34), Gederah (v36), Mizpeh (v38), Kain (v57), Rabbah (v60), Nibshan (v62).

### Notes
- **Judah's territory** — the largest tribal inheritance. Reaches from the Dead Sea (east) to the Mediterranean (west), and from the Negev (south) to the Jerusalem area (north).
- **Achsah narrative** (v16–19) — anecdote about Caleb's daughter asking for water. The same account stands in Judg 1:10–15.
- **Othniel** — becomes the first judge (Judg 3:9–11), delivering Israel from Cushan-rishathaim.
- **"Could not dispossess"** (v63) — the failure to take Jerusalem. The Jebusites held out until David (2 Sam 5:6–9).
- **Jerusalem's frontier status** — sits on the Judah/Benjamin border. It is listed among the cities of Benjamin (18:28).
- **City-list format** — four regions: Negev (sum 29), Shephelah (in sections), hill country (in sections), wilderness (6 cities).
