# Joshua 24

[1] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ gathered all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to Shechem, and he called the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and its heads and its judges and its officers, and they took their stand before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to all the people, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'Across the River your fathers dwelt from of old, ⟦terah|Terach|Terah⟧ the father of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and the father of ⟦nahor|Nachor|Nahor⟧, and they served other gods.

[3] And I took your father ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ from across the River, and I made him walk in all the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, and I multiplied his seed and gave him ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧.

[4] And I gave to ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ and ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧; and I gave to ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ the hill country of Seir, to possess it; and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ and his sons went down to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[5] And I sent ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and I smote ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, as I did in its midst; and afterward I brought you out.

[6] And I brought your fathers out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and you came to the sea; and ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ pursued after your fathers with chariots and with horsemen to the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧.

[8] And I brought you to the land of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ who dwells across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and they fought with you; and I gave them into your hand, and you took possession of their land, and I destroyed them from before you.

[9] And ⟦balak|Balaq|Balak⟧ the son of ⟦zippor|Tzippor|Zippor⟧, king of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, arose and fought against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and he sent and called ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ the son of Beor to curse you.

[10] And I was not willing to listen to ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧, and he surely blessed you; and I delivered you from his hand.

[11] And you passed over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and came to ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧; and the lords of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ fought against you, the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦perizzite|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦girgashite|Girgashi|Girgashite⟧, the ⟦hivite|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧; and I gave them into your hand.

[12] And I sent the hornet before you, and it drove them out from before you, the two kings of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧; not with your sword and not with your bow.

[13] And I gave you a land in which you did not labor, and cities that you did not build, and you dwelt in them; vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant you are eating.'

[14] And now, fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and serve him in blamelessness and in truth; and put away the gods that your fathers served across the River and in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] And if it is evil in your eyes to serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, choose for yourselves today whom you will serve, whether the gods that your fathers served who were across the River, or the gods of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ in whose land you are dwelling; and I and my house, we will serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[16] And the people answered and said, "Far be it from us to forsake ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to serve other gods.

[17] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is the one who brought us and our fathers up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the house of slaves, and who did before our eyes these great signs, and kept us in all the way in which we went and among all the peoples through whose midst we passed.

[18] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ drove out all the peoples, and the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧, the dweller of the land, from before us. We also will serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he is our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[19] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to the people, "You will not be able to serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he is a holy ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; he is a jealous ⟦el|El|God⟧; he will not forgive your transgression and your sins.

[20] When you forsake ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and serve foreign gods, he will turn back and do evil to you and make an end of you, after he has done good to you."

[21] And the people said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "No, but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ we will serve."

[22] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to the people, "You are witnesses against yourselves that you, you have chosen ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for yourselves, to serve him." And they said, "Witnesses."

[23] "And now, put away the foreign gods that are in your midst, and turn your heart to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[24] And the people said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we will serve, and to his voice we will listen."

[25] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ cut a covenant for the people on that day, and he set for it a statute and an ordinance in Shechem.

[26] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ wrote these words in the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and he took a great stone and set it up there under the terebinth that is in the sanctuary of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[27] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to all the people, "Behold, this stone shall be a witness against us, for it has heard all the sayings of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he spoke with us; and it shall be a witness against you, lest you lie to your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[28] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ sent the people away, each man to his inheritance.

[29] And it came to pass after these things that ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun, the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, died, a hundred and ten years old.

[30] And they buried him in the border of his inheritance, in ⟦timnath-serah|Timnat Serach|Timnath-serah⟧, which is in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, north of Mount Gaash.

[31] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ served ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ all the days of ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and all the days of the elders who prolonged days after ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and who knew all the work of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he did for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[32] And the bones of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, which the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ brought up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, they buried in Shechem, in the portion of the field that ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ bought from the sons of ⟦hamor|Chamor|Hamor⟧, the father of Shechem, for a hundred ⟦qesitah|qesitahs|pieces of money⟧; and they became an inheritance for the sons of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[33] And ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the son of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ died, and they buried him in the ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ of ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧ his son, which was given to him in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v1 the word has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children" → "sons"** (v4, 32) — Hebrew בָּנִים, בְּנֵי.
- **"called for the elders of Israel, and for their heads" → "called the elders of Israel and its heads"** (v1) — the suffixes are singular, of Israel, as 23:2. **"they presented themselves" → "they took their stand"** — Hebrew וַיִּתְיַצְּבוּ.
- **"on the other side of the flood in old time" → "Across the River … from of old"** (v2, 3, 14, 15) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הַנָּהָר; הַנָּהָר, "the River", is the Euphrates (Gen 31:21; Exod 23:31); "flood" in the King James Version is an old word for a river. מֵעוֹלָם is "from of old". The place stands first in the Hebrew.
- **"led him throughout all the land of Canaan" → "made him walk in all the land of Canaan"** (v3) — Hebrew וָאוֹלֵךְ, the causative of "walk" (Deut 8:2). **"multiplied his seed"** — the written form (ketiv) is וארב, the reading tradition (qere) וָאַרְבֶּה; both are "and I multiplied". זֶרַע is "seed".
- **"mount Seir" → "the hill country of Seir"** (v4) — Hebrew הַר שֵׂעִיר, as Deut 2:5: הַר of a region. **"to possess it"** — Hebrew לָרֶשֶׁת אוֹתוֹ.
- **"I plagued Egypt, according to that which I did among them" → "I smote Egypt, as I did in its midst"** (v5) — Hebrew וָאֶגֹּף, נָגַף "smite", the verb of Exod 8:2; 12:23, 27; בְּקִרְבּוֹ, "in its midst", singular: Egypt as one.
- **"the Egyptians pursued after your fathers" → "Egypt pursued after your fathers"** (v6) — Hebrew מִצְרַיִם, the name of the land and the people, here with a plural verb (וַיִּרְדְּפוּ), as Exod 14:9; in v7 the Hebrew has the other word, הַמִּצְרִים, "the Egyptians". **"with chariots and horsemen unto the Red sea" → "with chariots and with horsemen to the Sea of Reeds"** — Hebrew רֶכֶב, a collective; יַם־סוּף. The speech passes between "your fathers" and "you" (v6, 7): those who hear are counted with those who came out.
- **"he put darkness between you and the Egyptians" → "he set a gloom between you and the Egyptians"** (v7) — Hebrew מַאֲפֵל, found only here, of the root of אֲפֵלָה "gloom" (Exod 10:22); Exod 14:20 has חֹשֶׁךְ "darkness". In this verse Yahweh is spoken of as "he" in the middle of his own speech; the Hebrew is kept. **"brought the sea upon them, and covered them"** — the Hebrew pronouns are singular, "upon him … covered him": Egypt as one, as Exod 14:25. "Them" is used because "him" after "the Egyptians" would not be understood. **"your eyes have seen" → "your eyes saw"**; **"a long season" → "many days"** — Hebrew יָמִים רַבִּים.
- **"the Amorites, which dwelt on the other side Jordan" → "the Amorite who dwells across the Jordan"** (v8, 12, 15, 18) — Hebrew הָאֱמֹרִי, a singular with the article, as Deut 7:1; so all seven names in v11. "I brought" follows the qere וָאָבִיא; the ketiv ואבאה is another form of the same verb. **"that ye might possess their land" → "and you took possession of their land"** — Hebrew וַתִּירְשׁוּ. **"destroyed them from before you"** — Hebrew הִשְׁמִיד … מִפְּנֵיכֶם.
- **"warred against Israel" → "fought against Israel"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּלָּחֶם בְּ, the verb of v8, 11. Num 22–24 tells of no battle. **"to curse you"** — Hebrew קִלֵּל, "make light of, curse" (Deut 23:4); Num 22–24 has other verbs (אָרַר, קָבַב).
- **"I would not hearken unto Balaam; therefore he blessed you still" → "I was not willing to listen to Balaam, and he surely blessed you"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא אָבִיתִי לִשְׁמֹעַ, the words of Deut 23:5; וַיְבָרֶךְ בָּרוֹךְ, the verb with its infinitive absolute after it, literally "and he blessed, blessing": he blessed indeed, or he went on blessing.
- **"ye went over Jordan" → "you passed over the Jordan"** (v11) — Hebrew עָבַר. **"the men of Jericho" → "the lords of Jericho"** — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי יְרִיחוֹ, the plural of בַּעַל "owner, master": the freemen or lords of the town (so Judg 9:2 of Shechem). The seven nations follow as a second naming of those who fought; the Hebrew has "and" before all but the Amorite and the Hivite.
- **"which drave them out from before you, even the two kings of the Amorites; but not with thy sword" → "and it drove them out from before you, the two kings of the Amorite; not with your sword and not with your bow"** (v12) — Hebrew הַצִּרְעָה, "the hornet", as Exod 23:28; Deut 7:20; the word is uncertain and is also taken as "dread, panic". גֵּרֵשׁ is "drive out". "Your sword … your bow" are singular (so also "you did not labor" in v13).
- **"a land for which ye did not labour" → "a land in which you did not labor"** (v13) — Hebrew יָגַעְתָּ בָּהּ, יָגַע "labor, grow weary"; עָמָל "toil" is another word. **"ye dwell in them; of the vineyards and oliveyards which ye planted not do ye eat" → "you dwelt in them; vineyards and olive trees that you did not plant you are eating"** — Hebrew וַתֵּשְׁבוּ; זֵיתִים "olive trees"; אֹכְלִים, a participle. The words are those of Deut 6:10–11.
- **"serve"** (v2, 14–16, 18–22, 24, 31) — Hebrew עָבַד, one word in all its places in the chapter (seven times in v14–15); **"the house of bondage" → "the house of slaves"** (v17) — Hebrew בֵּית עֲבָדִים, of the same root.
- **"in sincerity and in truth" → "in blamelessness and in truth"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּתָמִים וּבֶאֱמֶת: תָּמִים is "whole, blameless" (Gen 17:1; Deut 18:13), used here as a noun; אֱמֶת "truth, faithfulness". **"put away the gods"** (v14, 23) — Hebrew הָסִירוּ, as Gen 35:2. Gods served "in Egypt" are spoken of also in Ezek 20:7–8.
- **"if it seem evil unto you" → "if it is evil in your eyes"** (v15) — Hebrew רַע בְּעֵינֵיכֶם. **"choose you this day" → "choose for yourselves today"** — Hebrew בַּחֲרוּ לָכֶם הַיּוֹם. **"that were on the other side of the flood" → "who were across the River"** — the ketiv is בעבר "across", the qere מֵעֵבֶר "from across"; the sense is the same. **"but as for me and my house, we will serve" → "and I and my house, we will serve"** — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי וּבֵיתִי נַעֲבֹד.
- **"God forbid that we should forsake" → "Far be it from us to forsake"** (v16) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לָּנוּ, literally "a profane thing to us"; no divine name is in the phrase.
- **"he it is that brought us up … and which did those great signs in our sight, and preserved us" → "he is the one who brought us … up … and who did before our eyes these great signs, and kept us"** (v17) — Hebrew הַמַּעֲלֶה, a participle; לְעֵינֵינוּ "before our eyes"; שָׁמַר "keep". **"among all the people through whom we passed" → "among all the peoples through whose midst we passed"** — Hebrew הָעַמִּים, plural; בְּקִרְבָּם.
- **"all the people, even the Amorites which dwelt in the land" → "all the peoples, and the Amorite, the dweller of the land"** (v18) — Hebrew וְאֶת־הָאֱמֹרִי יֹשֵׁב הָאָרֶץ; "even" is one reading of the "and". **"therefore will we also serve" → "We also will serve"** — "therefore" is not written.
- **"Ye cannot serve the LORD: for he is an holy God; he is a jealous God" → "You will not be able to serve Yahweh, for he is a holy Elohim; he is a jealous El"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא תוּכְלוּ. אֱלֹהִים קְדֹשִׁים הוּא: the adjective "holy" is plural, agreeing in form with אֱלֹהִים, while "he" is singular; the words can be read "he is a holy Elohim" (so here, of the one God) or, by form alone, "holy gods". אֵל־קַנּוֹא is "a jealous El": the word is אֵל, not אֱלֹהִים; קַנּוֹא is another form of קַנָּא (Exod 20:5; 34:14), found also in Nah 1:2; "zealous" is the other sense of the word. **"he will not forgive your transgressions nor your sins" → "he will not forgive your transgression and your sins"** — Hebrew לֹא־יִשָּׂא לְ, literally "he will not lift up for": bear, take away (Exod 23:21); "transgression" is singular.
- **"If ye forsake the LORD, and serve strange gods, then he will turn and do you hurt, and consume you" → "When you forsake Yahweh and serve foreign gods, he will turn back and do evil to you and make an end of you"** (v20) — Hebrew כִּי, "when" or "if"; אֱלֹהֵי נֵכָר, "gods of foreignness" (Gen 35:2; Deut 32:12); וְשָׁב "turn back"; הֵרַע "do evil" and הֵיטִיב "do good" are set against each other; כִּלָּה "make an end of".
- **"Nay; but we will serve the LORD" → "No, but Yahweh we will serve"** (v21) — "Yahweh" stands first; so v24.
- **"Ye are witnesses against yourselves" kept; "We are witnesses" → "Witnesses"** (v22) — Hebrew עֵדִים אַתֶּם בָּכֶם; the answer is the one word עֵדִים. "You, you have chosen": the pronoun is written.
- **"put away … the strange gods which are among you, and incline your heart" → "put away the foreign gods that are in your midst, and turn your heart"** (v23) — the words of Gen 35:2, spoken at the same place; וְהַטּוּ, the causative of נָטָה "turn, stretch out": "incline" is the same sense. The verse has no "he said"; Joshua speaks on.
- **"his voice will we obey" → "to his voice we will listen"** (v24) — Hebrew וּבְקוֹלוֹ נִשְׁמָע.
- **"made a covenant with the people" → "cut a covenant for the people"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיִּכְרֹת … בְּרִית לָעָם: "cut", the usual verb, and לְ "for", not "with". **"set them a statute and an ordinance" → "set for it a statute and an ordinance"** — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׂם לוֹ חֹק וּמִשְׁפָּט, the words of Exod 15:25; "it" is the people.
- **"under an oak, that was by the sanctuary" → "under the terebinth that is in the sanctuary"** (v26) — Hebrew הָאַלָּה, pointed here as nowhere else; it is taken as a form of אֵלָה "terebinth" (Gen 35:4, "the terebinth that is by Shechem") or of אַלּוֹן "oak"; the kind of tree is not certain. בְּמִקְדַּשׁ יְהוָה is "in the sanctuary of Yahweh", the holy place. **"the book of the law of God" → "the book of the Torah of Elohim"** — Hebrew סֵפֶר תּוֹרַת אֱלֹהִים.
- **"this stone shall be a witness unto us; for it hath heard all the words" → "this stone shall be a witness against us, for it has heard all the sayings"** (v27) — Hebrew תִּהְיֶה־בָּנוּ לְעֵדָה, "shall be among us / against us for a witness"; אִמְרֵי, "sayings" (another word than דָּבָר). **"lest ye deny your God" → "lest you lie to your Elohim"** — Hebrew תְּכַחֲשׁוּן בֵּ, "lie to, be false to" (Lev 19:11).
- **"every man unto his inheritance" → "each man to his inheritance"** (v28).
- **"being an hundred and ten years old" → "a hundred and ten years old"** (v29) — Hebrew בֶּן־מֵאָה וָעֶשֶׂר שָׁנִים, literally "a son of a hundred and ten years"; so Joseph (Gen 50:26). **"servant"** — Hebrew עֶבֶד, "slave"; of Yahweh's man "servant", as Moses (Deut 34:5).
- **"in the border of his inheritance"** (v30) — Hebrew בִּגְבוּל, "in the border" or "in the territory"; the earlier text had dropped the word. **"on the north side of the hill of Gaash" → "north of Mount Gaash"** — Hebrew מִצְּפוֹן לְהַר־גָּעַשׁ.
- **"the elders that overlived Joshua" → "the elders who prolonged days after Joshua"** (v31) — Hebrew הֶאֱרִיכוּ יָמִים אַחֲרֵי, as Deut 4:40. **"which had known all the works" → "who knew all the work"** — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה, singular.
- **"in a parcel of ground which Jacob bought of the sons of Hamor … for an hundred pieces of silver" → "in the portion of the field that Jacob bought from the sons of Hamor … for a hundred qesitahs"** (v32) — the words of Gen 33:19. קְשִׂיטָה *qesitah* is a weight or piece of money of unknown value, found only in Gen 33:19; here; Job 42:11; "silver" is not written. **"it became the inheritance" → "they became an inheritance"** — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ, plural: the bones, or the field with them; the Hebrew is kept.
- **"in a hill that pertained to Phinehas his son" → "in the Gibeah of Phinehas his son"** (v33) — Hebrew בְּגִבְעַת פִּינְחָס: גִּבְעָה is "hill"; it can be a name ("Gibeah of Phinehas") or the plain word ("the hill of Phinehas"). **"which was given him"** — Hebrew נִתַּן־לוֹ; "him" is Phinehas, or Eleazar.

### Notes
- **Shechem** — where Abram first built an altar in the land (Gen 12:6–7), where Jacob bought the field and set up an altar (Gen 33:18–20) and where his house put away the foreign gods under the terebinth (Gen 35:2–4).
- **The retelling** (v2–13) — Yahweh speaks in the first person, from Terah to the land: Gen 11:26–12:7; 21; 25; 36:8; 46; Exod 3–14; Num 21–24; Josh 3; 6.
- **"Your fathers served other gods"** (v2, 14) — Terah's house across the River; Genesis does not say it.
- **"Choose for yourselves today"** (v15) — the people answer three times that they will serve Yahweh (v18, 21, 24).
- **"He will not forgive"** (v19) — as Exod 23:21; the warning stands beside Exod 34:6–7.
- **The stone** (v26–27) — set up under the terebinth in the sanctuary of Yahweh, as a witness.
- **The three burials** (v29–33) — Joshua, a hundred and ten years old; the bones of Joseph (Gen 50:25; Exod 13:19); Eleazar. Judg 2:6–9 repeats v28–31.

---

*End of Joshua. The conquest and settlement completed. Book of Judges follows — the fragile peace gives way to cycles of apostasy, oppression, and deliverance.*
