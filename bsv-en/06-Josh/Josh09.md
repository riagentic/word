# Joshua 9

[1] And it came to pass, when all the kings heard who were across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, in the hill country and in the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧ and on all the shore of the Great Sea toward the front of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧, the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧, the ⟦perizzite|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧, the ⟦hivite|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧,

[2] that they gathered themselves together to fight with ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, with one mouth.

[3] And the inhabitants of ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ heard what ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ had done to ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ and to Ai,

[4] and they also acted with cunning, and they went and made themselves envoys; and they took worn-out sacks for their donkeys, and skins of wine, worn out and split and bound up,

[5] and sandals worn out and patched on their feet, and worn-out cloaks on them; and all the bread of their provision was dry, it had become crumbs.

[6] And they went to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, to the camp at Gilgal, and said to him and to the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "From a far land we have come; and now, cut a covenant with us."

[7] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to the ⟦hivites|Chivvi|Hivite⟧, "Perhaps you are dwelling in my midst; and how shall I cut a covenant with you?"

[8] And they said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "We are your servants." And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to them, "Who are you, and from where do you come?"

[9] And they said to him, "From a very far land your servants have come, for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; for we have heard the report of him, and all that he did in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[10] and all that he did to the two kings of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ who were across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, to ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ and to Og king of the Bashan, who was in ⟦ashtaroth|Ashtarot|Ashtaroth⟧.

[11] And our elders and all the inhabitants of our land said to us, saying, 'Take in your hand provision for the way and go to meet them, and say to them, "We are your servants; and now, cut a covenant with us."'

[12] This our bread, warm we took it as provision from our houses on the day of our going out to go to you; and now, behold, it is dry and has become crumbs.

[13] And these skins of wine, which we filled new, behold, they have split; and these our cloaks and our sandals have worn out from the very long way."

[14] And the men took of their provision, and the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they did not ask.

[15] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ made ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with them and cut a covenant with them, to let them live; and the chieftains of the congregation swore to them.

[16] And it came to pass at the end of three days after they had cut a covenant with them, that they heard that they were near to him and that they were dwelling in his midst.

[17] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ set out and came to their cities on the third day; and their cities were ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ and ⟦chephirah|Kefirah|Chephirah⟧ and ⟦beeroth|Beerot|Beeroth⟧ and ⟦kiriath-jearim|Qiryat Yearim|Kiriath-jearim⟧.

[18] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did not strike them down, for the chieftains of the congregation had sworn to them by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and all the congregation grumbled against the chieftains.

[19] And all the chieftains said to all the congregation, "We, we have sworn to them by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and now we cannot touch them.

[20] This we will do to them, and keep them alive, and wrath will not be upon us because of the oath that we swore to them."

[21] And the chieftains said to them, "Let them live." And they became hewers of wood and drawers of water for all the congregation, as the chieftains had spoken to them.

[22] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ called to them and spoke to them, saying, "Why have you deceived us, saying, 'We are very far from you,' and you are dwelling in our midst?

[23] And now, cursed are you, and there shall not be cut off from you a slave, and hewers of wood and drawers of water, for the house of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[24] And they answered ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and said, "Because it was surely told to your servants what ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ his servant, to give you all the land and to destroy all the inhabitants of the land from before you; and we feared greatly for our souls because of you, and we did this thing.

[25] And now, behold, we are in your hand; as is good and as is right in your eyes to do to us, do."

[26] And he did to them so, and delivered them from the hand of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they did not kill them.

[27] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ made them on that day hewers of wood and drawers of water for the congregation and for the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to this day, to the place that he will choose.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept. **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of וַ / וְ is kept; v1–2 are one sentence, "when all the kings heard" without an object.
- **"on this side Jordan" → "across the Jordan"** (v1, 10) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן, the same words for the west side (v1) and the east side (v10). **"in the hills, and in the valleys" → "in the hill country and in the Shephelah"** — Hebrew הָהָר "the mountain" and הַשְּׁפֵלָה, the low hills toward the coast. **"over against Lebanon" → "toward the front of Lebanon"** — Hebrew אֶל־מוּל, as 8:33. The six peoples are singular with the article.
- **"with one accord" → "with one mouth"** (v2) — Hebrew פֶּה אֶחָד.
- **"They did work wilily" → "they also acted with cunning"** (v4) — Hebrew גַּם־הֵמָּה "they also": as Israel had done at Ai. **"made as if they had been ambassadors" → "made themselves envoys"** — Hebrew וַיִּצְטַיָּרוּ, found only here, from צִיר "envoy". A reading with one letter changed, "took provisions" (the verb of v12), is commonly preferred; the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"wine bottles, old, and rent, and bound up" → "skins of wine, worn out and split and bound up"** (v4, 13) — Hebrew נֹאדוֹת יַיִן; בָּלֶה "worn out" is said of the sacks, the skins, the sandals and the cloaks alike (Deut 29:5). **"clouted" → "patched"** (v5). **"garments" → "cloaks"** (v5, 13) — Hebrew שְׂלָמוֹת.
- **"mouldy" → "crumbs"** (v5, 12) — Hebrew נִקֻּדִים; the meaning is uncertain: crumbled, or spotted (with mould), from a root "to dot".
- **"the camp at Gilgal"** (v6) — Hebrew "the camp, the Gilgal". **"the men of Israel"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew אִישׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל, "the man of Israel", a singular for the whole; so in v7 he says "in my midst" and "shall I cut", and the Hivite is addressed as one. In v7 the reading tradition has "and he said" (the written text "and they said") and a shorter spelling of "I cut".
- **"make ye a league with us" → "cut a covenant with us"** (v6, 7, 11, 15, 16) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית לְ, as Deut 7:2.
- **"servants"** (v8, 9, 11, 24) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word for "slave" (v23), here the speech of one who submits.
- **"because of the name" → "for the name"** (v9) — Hebrew לְשֵׁם. **"the fame of him" → "the report of him"** — Hebrew שָׁמְעוֹ, as 6:27.
- **"victuals" → "provision"** (v5, 11, 14) — Hebrew צֵידָה / צַיִד. **"by reason of the very long journey" → "from the very long way"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵרֹב הַדֶּרֶךְ מְאֹד, literally "from the greatness of the way, very".
- **"asked not counsel at the mouth of the LORD" → "the mouth of Yahweh they did not ask"** (v14) — the Hebrew has no word for "counsel". "The men" who took of the provision are not named further.
- **"made peace" → "made *shalom*"** (v15) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם. **"to let them live"** (v15) and **"Let them live"** (v21) — the simple and the intensive stem; **"keep them alive"** (v20) is the causative. **"princes" → "chieftains"** (v15–21) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאִים.
- **"that they were their neighbours, and that they dwelt among them" → "that they were near to him and … dwelling in his midst"** (v16) — the Hebrew pronouns are singular: Israel as one.
- **"murmured" → "grumbled"** (v18) — Hebrew לוּן (nifal), as Exod 16:2.
- **"lest wrath be upon us" → "and wrath will not be upon us"** (v20) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִהְיֶה.
- **"beguiled" → "deceived"** (v22) — Hebrew רִמָּה. **"when ye dwell" → "and you are dwelling"** — Hebrew וְ.
- **"there shall none of you be freed from being bondmen" → "there shall not be cut off from you a slave"** (v23) — Hebrew לֹא־יִכָּרֵת מִכֶּם עֶבֶד, literally so: there will always be slaves from among you.
- **"it was certainly told" → "it was surely told"** (v24) — Hebrew הֻגֵּד הֻגַּד, the infinitive with its verb. **"from before you … because of you"** — one Hebrew word both times, מִפְּנֵיכֶם, literally "from your face". **"for our lives" → "for our souls"** — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ.
- **"as it seemeth good and right unto thee" → "as is good and as is right in your eyes"** (v25) — Hebrew כַּטּוֹב וְכַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינֶיךָ.
- **"made them"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנֵם, literally "gave them". **"in the place which he should choose" → "to the place that he will choose"** — Hebrew אֶל־הַמָּקוֹם אֲשֶׁר יִבְחָר, the wording of Deut 12:5; who chooses is not named here.

### Notes
- **Far and near** — Deut 20:10–18 sets the cities that are "very far" apart from the cities of the peoples of the land; the men of Gibeon speak as from a far land.
- **"The mouth of Yahweh they did not ask"** (v14) — compare Num 27:21.
- **The oath stands** (v18–20) — the chieftains had sworn by Yahweh. Compare 2 Sam 21:1–9.
- **"Hewers of wood and drawers of water"** (v21, 23, 27) — the words of Deut 29:11.
- **"The place that he will choose"** (v27) — Deut 12:5, 11, 14.
