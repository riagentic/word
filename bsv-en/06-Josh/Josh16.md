# Joshua 16

[1] And the lot came out for the sons of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ from the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, to the waters of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ eastward, the wilderness going up from ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ in the hill country to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧.

[2] And it goes out from ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ to Luz and passes on to the border of the ⟦archites|Arki|Archite⟧, to ⟦ataroth|Atarot|Ataroth⟧.

[3] And it goes down westward to the border of the ⟦japhletites|Yafleti|Japhletite⟧, as far as the border of Lower ⟦beth-horon|Beit Choron|Beth-horon⟧ and as far as Gezer, and its outgoings are at the sea.

[4] And the sons of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, inherited.

[5] And the border of the sons of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ was by their clans; and the border of their inheritance eastward was ⟦ataroth-addar|Atrot Addar|Ataroth-addar⟧, as far as Upper ⟦beth-horon|Beit Choron|Beth-horon⟧.

[6] And the border goes out westward, ⟦michmethath|Michmetat|Michmethath⟧ on the north; and the border turns eastward to ⟦taanath-shiloh|Taanat Shiloh|Taanath-shiloh⟧ and passes on by it from the east of ⟦janoah|Yanoach|Janoah⟧.

[7] And it goes down from ⟦janoah|Yanoach|Janoah⟧ to ⟦ataroth|Atarot|Ataroth⟧ and to Naarah and touches ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ and goes out to the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[8] From ⟦tappuah|Tappuach|Tappuah⟧ the border goes westward to the wadi ⟦kanah|Qanah|Kanah⟧, and its outgoings are at the sea. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the sons of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ by their clans,

[9] and the cities that were separated for the sons of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ in the midst of the inheritance of the sons of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, all the cities and their villages.

[10] And they did not dispossess the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ who dwells in Gezer; and the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ has dwelt in the midst of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ to this day, and he became a slave at forced labor.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"families" → "clans"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחוֹת.
- **"the lot … fell" → "the lot came out"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֵּצֵא, the verb "go out" (as Num 33:54). **"from Jordan by Jericho, unto the water of Jericho on the east, to the wilderness that goeth up from Jericho throughout mount Bethel" → "from the Jordan of Jericho, to the waters of Jericho eastward, the wilderness going up from Jericho in the hill country to Bethel"** — Hebrew מִיַּרְדֵּן יְרִיחוֹ, "the Jordan of Jericho" (as Num 34:15); הַמִּדְבָּר עֹלֶה, "the wilderness, going up": the words stand side by side without a link, and how "the wilderness" joins the sentence is not certain. בָּהָר is "in the hill country" (הַר with the article, so not "the hill country of Bethel").
- **"goeth out … passeth along … goeth down" → "goes out … passes on … goes down"** (v2, 3, 6–8) — the verbs of a border, one English verb for each Hebrew verb, as in Num 34 and Josh 15: יָצָא "go out", עָבַר "pass on", יָרַד "go down", נָסַב "turn", הָלַךְ "go" (v8), פָּגַע בְּ "touch" (v7; literally "meet, come upon"). The Hebrew uses its descriptive forms here, not the narrative past; they are rendered by the English present. תּוֹצָאוֹת is "outgoings": where the border comes out, its end (v3, 8). In v3 the written form (תצאתו) and the reading tradition (תֹצְאֹתָיו) mean the same.
- **"the borders of Archi" → "the border of the Archite"** (v2) — Hebrew הָאַרְכִּי, a singular with the article for the people; so also "the Japhletite" (v3) and "the Canaanite" (v10). גְּבוּל is "border" in every place. **"to Ataroth"** — the Hebrew has only the name, set beside "the border of the Archite".
- **"westward"** (v3, 6, 8) — Hebrew יָמָּה, "seaward". **"the goings out thereof are at the sea"** — the same word, "to the sea".
- **"took their inheritance" → "inherited"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיִּנְחֲלוּ, the verb alone.
- **"And the border of the children of Ephraim according to their families was thus: even the border of their inheritance on the east side was" → "And the border of the sons of Ephraim was by their clans; and the border of their inheritance eastward was"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיְהִי … וַיְהִי, "and … was" twice; "thus" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"toward the sea to Michmethah on the north side" → "westward, Michmethath on the north"** (v6) — Hebrew הַמִּכְמְתָת מִצָּפוֹן; the name has the article, and no "to" stands before it. **"and passed by it on the east to Janohah" → "and passes on by it from the east of Janoah"** — Hebrew וְעָבַר אוֹתוֹ מִמִּזְרַח יָנוֹחָה; the name ends in -ah also in v7 ("from Janoah"), so the ending is taken as part of the name; "on the east, to Janoah" is also possible.
- **"came to Jericho" → "touches Jericho"** (v7) — Hebrew וּפָגַע בִּירִיחוֹ.
- **"the river Kanah" → "the wadi Kanah"** (v8) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed.
- **"the separate cities" → "the cities that were separated"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶעָרִים הַמִּבְדָּלוֹת, from הִבְדִּיל "separate"; the form stands only here. **"among the inheritance" → "in the midst of the inheritance"** — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ.
- **"they drave not out" → "they did not dispossess"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא הוֹרִישׁוּ, the causative of יָרַשׁ "possess": to take another's place and holding. Here the text says only that they did not; in 15:63 and 17:12 it says "could not". **"dwell … unto this day"** — Hebrew וַיֵּשֶׁב, "has dwelt".
- **"serve under tribute" → "he became a slave at forced labor"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיְהִי לְמַס־עֹבֵד: מַס is forced labor, not tribute; עֹבֵד is "one who serves", the word of "slave".
- **"Beth-horon the nether / the upper" → "Lower / Upper Beth-horon"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew תַּחְתּוֹן, עֶלְיוֹן.

### Notes
- **Joseph's double portion** — Ephraim and Manasseh each receive a tribal territory, as Jacob promised (Gen 48).
- **Ephraim's territory** — central hill country north of Benjamin. Shechem and Shiloh (later the sanctuary) sit in this region.
- **Gezer not taken** — the Canaanites remain. Solomon later receives Gezer from Pharaoh as a gift to his daughter (1 Kgs 9:16).
- **"Cities separated for Ephraim within Manasseh"** (v9) — cities of Ephraim that lie in the inheritance of Manasseh.
