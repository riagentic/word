# Joshua 18

[3] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "How long are you slack to go in to take possession of the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers, has given you?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. In the lists of towns "and" stands exactly where the Hebrew has it. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"families" → "clans"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחוֹת.
- **"tribe" for two Hebrew words** — שֵׁבֶט (literally "rod") in v2, 4, 7; מַטֶּה (literally "staff") in v11, 21. The two name the same thing.
- **"set up the tabernacle of the congregation" → "made the tent of meeting abide"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁכִּינוּ, the causative of שָׁכַן "abide" (the root of מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling"); אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד is "the tent of meeting". **"assembled together at Shiloh"** — Hebrew שִׁלֹה, the name alone: "to Shiloh". עֵדָה is "congregation".
- **"which had not yet received their inheritance" → "who had not divided their inheritance"** (v2) — Hebrew לֹא־חָלְקוּ, the active "divide", the verb of חֵלֶק "share" (v5–7, 9, 10). "Yet" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"How long are ye slack to go to possess the land" → "How long are you slack to go in to take possession of the land"** (v3) — Hebrew מִתְרַפִּים, "letting yourselves go slack"; לָבוֹא לָרֶשֶׁת, "to go in, to take possession".
- **"Give out from among you three men for each tribe … and describe it" → "Provide for yourselves three men for a tribe … and write it"** (v4) — Hebrew הָבוּ לָכֶם, as Deut 1:13. כָּתַב is "write" in every place in this chapter (v4, 6, 8, 9): they are to write the land down; "describe" is not the Hebrew verb. **"according to the inheritance of them"** — Hebrew לְפִי נַחֲלָתָם, literally "at the mouth of their inheritance".
- **"they shall divide it into seven parts: Judah shall abide in their coast" → "they shall divide it among themselves into seven shares. Judah shall stand on his border"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהִתְחַלְּקוּ, the reflexive form; חֲלָקִים "shares"; יַעֲמֹד "shall stand"; גְּבוּל "border" (also the land within it). "On the south / on the north" is literally "from the south / from the north".
- **"that I may cast lots for you" → "and I will cast a lot for you"** (v6) — Hebrew וְיָרִיתִי גּוֹרָל, "cast" (the verb of shooting and throwing); in v8 and 10 the Hebrew has another verb, הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw". גּוֹרָל is singular. No object stands after "bring" in the Hebrew; "it" is supplied.
- **"the Levites have no part among you … their inheritance" → "there is no share for the Levites in your midst … his inheritance"** (v7) — Hebrew חֵלֶק; נַחֲלָתוֹ, "his": the Levite's, a singular for the whole tribe. **"have received" → "have taken"** — Hebrew לָקְחוּ, as Num 34:14. **"beyond Jordan on the east" → "across the Jordan eastward"** — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן מִזְרָחָה. "Manasseh" has the article here.
- **"charged them that went to describe the land" → "commanded those who went to write the land"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיְצַו. **"walk through the land" → "walk about in the land"** (v4, 8) — the reflexive form הִתְהַלֵּךְ.
- **"described it by cities into seven parts in a book" → "wrote it by cities, in seven shares, on a book"** (v9) — Hebrew עַל־סֵפֶר; סֵפֶר is any written document. **"to the host at Shiloh" → "to the camp at Shiloh"** — Hebrew הַמַּחֲנֶה שִׁלֹה, "the camp, Shiloh".
- **"according to their divisions"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּמַחְלְקֹתָם, from the same root as "divide" and "share".
- **"the coast of their lot came forth" → "the border of their lot went out"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיֵּצֵא גְּבוּל גּוֹרָלָם. "Between … and" is literally "between … and between".
- **"went up … passed over … descended … was drawn … compassed" → "goes up … passes on … goes down … bends … turns"** (v12–19) — the verbs of a border, one English verb for each Hebrew verb, as in Num 34 and Josh 15: עָלָה "go up", עָבַר "pass on", יָרַד "go down", יָצָא "go out", נָסַב "turn", תָּאַר "bend" (v14, 17; meaning not certain: "bend, incline" or "be drawn"). The Hebrew uses its descriptive forms, not the narrative past; they are rendered by the English present. תּוֹצָאוֹת is "outgoings": where the border comes out, its end (v12, 14, 19). In v12, 14, 19 the written text has the verb in the singular (והיה), the reading tradition in the plural (וְהָיוּ); in v19 the written תצאותיו ("its outgoings") is read תֹּצְאוֹת ("the outgoings of"). The meaning is the same.
- **"the side of Jericho" → "the shoulder of Jericho"** (v12, 13, 16, 18, 19) — Hebrew כֶּתֶף, "shoulder": the slope or ridge beside a place. **"through the mountains westward" → "in the hill country westward"** (v12) — Hebrew בָהָר; הַר is "hill country" of a region and "mountain" of a single height (v13, 14, 16). The quarters of the sky are "sea" (west), "Negev" (south), "hidden side" (north) and "rising" / "front" (east) in the Hebrew.
- **"near the hill that lieth on the south side of the nether Beth-horon" → "on the mountain that is from the south of Lower Beth-horon"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־הָהָר אֲשֶׁר מִנֶּגֶב לְ.
- **"compassed the corner of the sea southward" → "turns for the west side southward"** (v14) — Hebrew לִפְאַת־יָם; פֵּאָה is "side", and "sea" is the west. **"the hill that lieth before Beth-horon" → "the mountain that is facing Beth-horon"** — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי, literally "upon the face of". **"this was the west quarter" → "This is the west side"** — no verb in the Hebrew.
- **"the south quarter was from the end of Kirjath-jearim, and the border went out on the west" → "the south side is from the end of Kiriath-jearim; and the border goes out westward"** (v15) — Hebrew יָמָּה, "seaward, westward". The line as a whole runs east from Kiriath-jearim; what "westward" says here is not clear, and the Hebrew is translated as it stands. **"the well of waters of Nephtoah" → "the spring of the waters of Nephtoah"** — מַעְיָן.
- **"valley" for two Hebrew words** (v16) — גַּיְא (the valley of Ben-hinnom, of Hinnom), a narrow valley, and עֵמֶק (the valley of Rephaim), a broad one. **"the valley of the giants" → "the valley of Rephaim"**. **"the side of Jebusi" → "the shoulder of the Jebusite"** (v16, 28) — Hebrew הַיְבוּסִי, the name of the people with the article.
- **"was drawn from the north" → "bends from the north"** (v17) — Hebrew וְתָאַר מִצָּפוֹן; "on the north" is also possible. **"over against the going up of Adummim" → "opposite the ascent of Adummim"** — Hebrew נֹכַח. "Geliloth" is taken as a name; the word means "districts, circles", and 15:7 has "Gilgal" at this place.
- **"toward the side over against Arabah northward" → "to the shoulder over against the Arabah northward"** (v18) — Hebrew אֶל־כֶּתֶף מוּל־הָעֲרָבָה. The Hebrew text has "the Arabah", not "Beth-arabah" (so the old rendering, with the Greek, cited from memory, and 15:6). מוּל is "over against, in front of".
- **"the north bay of the salt sea at the south end of Jordan" → "the tongue of the Salt Sea northward, at the end of the Jordan southward"** (v19) — Hebrew לָשׁוֹן, "tongue", as 15:2, 5.
- **"Jordan was the border of it on the east side" → "the Jordan borders it for the east side"** (v20) — Hebrew יִגְבֹּל־אֹתוֹ, the verb of גְּבוּל "border". **"by the coasts thereof round about" → "by its borders all around"**.
- **"Chephar-haammonai"** (v24) — the written text has העמני, "(Chephar) of the Ammonite"; the reading tradition has הָעַמֹּנָה, "(Chephar) ha-Ammonah". The name keeps its usual form, which follows the written text. Avvim, Parah (v23), Ramah (v25), Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah (v26) and Eleph (v28) have the article in Hebrew.
- **"Zelah, Eleph, and Jebusi, which is Jerusalem, Gibeath, and Kirjath"** (v28) — the Hebrew has "and" only before "Zela" and "the Jebusite". The sum "fourteen" agrees with the list if Zela and Eleph, and Gibeath and Kiriath, are each two names; if they are one each ("Zela-haeleph", "Gibeath-kiriath"), the list has twelve. The sum of v21–24, "twelve", agrees with its list.

### Notes
- **Shiloh becomes the center** (v1) — the tent of meeting is at Shiloh, where it remains until the ark is captured by the Philistines (1 Sam 4).
- **Seven tribes** (v2) — Judah and Joseph (Ephraim and the half of Manasseh) have their lots. Seven remain: Benjamin, Simeon, Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, Dan.
- **Benjamin's inheritance** — between Judah and Joseph. Jerusalem is in its list (v28), though the Jebusite remains there until David.
- **Bethel on the border** — Bethel is listed with Benjamin (v22); later it belongs to the northern kingdom (1 Kgs 12:29).
