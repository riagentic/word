# Titus 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, a slave of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and an apostle of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, according to the faith of ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ elect and the full knowledge of the truth that is according to godliness,

[2] in hope of eternal life, which the unlying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ promised before times eternal,

[3] but in his own times manifested his word in a proclamation with which I was entrusted according to the command of our Savior ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[4] to Titus, a genuine child according to a common faith:

Grace and peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father and ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our Savior.

[5] For this reason I left you behind in ⟦crete|Krete|Crete⟧, that you might further set right the things lacking and appoint elders city by city, as I gave you orders:

[6] if anyone is blameless, a husband of one wife, having faithful children, not under accusation of dissoluteness nor insubordinate.

[7] For the overseer must be blameless, as ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ steward: not self-willed, not quick to anger, not given to wine, not a striker, not greedy for shameful gain,

[8] but hospitable, a lover of good, sober-minded, righteous, devout, self-controlled,

[9] holding fast to the faithful word that is according to the teaching, so that he may be able both to exhort in the healthy doctrine and to reprove those who contradict.

[10] For there are many, and insubordinate, vain talkers and deceivers of the mind, especially those of the circumcision,

[11] whose mouths must be stopped, who overturn whole houses, teaching what they must not, for the sake of shameful gain.

[12] One of them, a prophet of their own, said, "**⟦cretans|Kretes|Cretans⟧ are always liars, evil beasts, idle bellies**."

[13] This testimony is true. For which cause reprove them sharply, that they may be healthy in the faith,

[14] not giving heed to Jewish myths and commandments of people who turn away from the truth.

[15] All things are pure to the pure; but to the defiled and unbelieving nothing is pure, but both their mind and their conscience are defiled.

[16] They confess that they know ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but by their works they deny him, being abominable and disobedient and for every good work unapproved.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (vv1, 4) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 16) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v1) — in "an apostle of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*."
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v1) — Greek δοῦλος *doulos*.
- **"that cannot lie" → "the unlying"** (v2) — Greek ὁ ἀψευδὴς θεός, an adjective, "free from falsehood"; "cannot" is not in the Greek.
- **"before the world began" → "before times eternal"** (v2) — Greek πρὸ χρόνων αἰωνίων.
- **"in due times" → "in his own times"** (v3) — Greek καιροῖς ἰδίοις, "in own times": his own, or the times' own (the fitting times).
- **"manifested his word" kept** (v3) — Greek ἐφανέρωσεν … τὸν λόγον αὐτοῦ: "his word" is the object. The sentence leaves the relative clause of v2 ("eternal life, which …") and takes a new object. ἐν κηρύγματι, "in a proclamation".
- **"mine own son" → "a genuine child"** (v4) — Greek γνησίῳ τέκνῳ; there is no "my".
- **"Grace, mercy, and peace … the Lord Jesus Christ" → "Grace and peace … the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v4) — NA28 χάρις καὶ εἰρήνη … Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ; "mercy" and "the Lord" are not in the critical text.
- **"set in order the things that are wanting" → "further set right the things lacking"** (v5) — Greek ἐπιδιορθώσῃ, "set right in addition"; τὰ λείποντα, the same verb as in 3:13.
- **"faithful children" kept** (v6) — Greek τέκνα πιστά: πιστός is "faithful" or "believing"; the same word in v9 and 3:8.
- **"bishop" → "overseer"** (v7) — Greek ἐπίσκοπος *episkopos*.
- **"not given to filthy lucre" → "not greedy for shameful gain"** (v7) — Greek αἰσχροκερδῆ; the same words apart in v11, αἰσχροῦ κέρδους.
- **"sober" → "sober-minded"; "temperate" → "self-controlled"** (v8) — Greek σώφρων (also 2:2, 4, 5, 6, 12) and ἐγκρατής.
- **"holy" → "devout"** (v8) — Greek ὅσιος, devout in conduct; a different word from ἅγιος, "holy".
- **"as he hath been taught" → "that is according to the teaching"** (v9) — Greek τοῦ κατὰ τὴν διδαχὴν πιστοῦ λόγου.
- **"acknowledging" → "full knowledge"** (v1) — Greek ἐπίγνωσιν, as distinct from γνῶσις "knowledge".
- **"exhort" kept** (v9) — Greek παρακαλεῖν; "exhort" throughout the letter (also 2:6, 15), where it stands beside teaching and reproving.
- **"many unruly" → "many, and insubordinate"** (v10) — Greek πολλοὶ καὶ ἀνυπότακτοι, "many and insubordinate". NA28: καί stands in brackets (quoted from memory).
- **"sound" → "healthy"** (v9, 13) — Greek ὑγιαίνω, "to be in health"; also 2:1, 2, 8.
- **"One of themselves" → "One of them"** (v12) — Greek ἐξ αὐτῶν; the name stands only in the quotation.
- **"slow bellies" → "idle bellies"** (v12) — Greek γαστέρες ἀργαί.
- **"deny him" kept** (v16) — Greek ἀρνοῦνται has no object; "him" is supplied for English. It can also be read "deny it" (that they know him).
- **"reprobate" → "unapproved"** (v16) — Greek ἀδόκιμοι, "not standing the test".

### Notes
- **v12** — attributed to Epimenides of Crete.
- **v1–4** — one sentence in Greek.
