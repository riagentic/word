# Titus 3

[1] Remind them to be subject to principalities, to authorities, to obey, to be ready for every good work,

[2] to blaspheme no one, to be no fighters, gentle, showing all meekness toward all people.

[3] For we also were once foolish, disobedient, led astray, being slaves to various desires and pleasures, living in malice and envy, detestable, hating one another.

[4] But when the kindness and the love for mankind of our Savior ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ appeared,

[5] **not from works in righteousness that we ourselves did, but according to his mercy he saved us, through the washing of regeneration and renewal of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧**,

[6] which he poured out upon us richly through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ our Savior,

[7] so that, having been justified by that one's grace, we might become heirs according to hope of eternal life.

[8] Faithful is the word; and concerning these things I want you to affirm strongly, so that those who have believed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may take care to take the lead in good works. These things are good and profitable for people.

[9] But shun foolish questionings and genealogies and strifes and fights about the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, for they are unprofitable and vain.

[10] A factious person, after one and a second admonition, refuse,

[11] knowing that such a one has been perverted and sins, being self-condemned.

[12] When I send Artemas to you, or ⟦tychicus|Tychikos|Tychicus⟧, be diligent to come to me to ⟦nicopolis|Nikopolis|Nicopolis⟧, for I have decided to winter there.

[13] Send Zenas the lawyer and Apollos diligently on their way, so that nothing may be lacking to them.

[14] And let ours also learn to take the lead in good works for the necessary needs, that they may not be unfruitful.

[15] All who are with me greet you. Greet those who have affection for us in faith.

Grace be with you all.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v6) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 8) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v6) — in "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach* our Savior."
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v5) — Greek πνεύματος ἁγίου (no article); Hebrew *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness".
- **"principalities and powers, to obey magistrates" → "principalities, to authorities, to obey"** (v1) — NA28 ἀρχαῖς ἐξουσίαις … πειθαρχεῖν: no "and" between the two nouns, and "magistrates" is not a separate word.
- **"speak evil of" → "blaspheme"** (v2) — Greek βλασφημεῖν, the same verb as 2:5.
- **"no brawlers" → "no fighters"** (v2) — Greek ἀμάχους, "not fighting"; the root of "fights" (μάχας) in v9.
- **"deceived" → "led astray"** (v3) — Greek πλανώμενοι, passive or middle: "led astray" or "going astray".
- **"serving" → "being slaves to"** (v3) — Greek δουλεύοντες, "being slaves".
- **"hateful" → "detestable"** (v3) — Greek στυγητοί; a different word from "hating" (μισοῦντες).
- **"love … toward man" → "love for mankind"** (v4) — Greek φιλανθρωπία *philanthrōpia*; word order as in Greek, "of our Savior *Elohim*" last.
- **"Not by works … he saved us" kept in the Greek order** (v5) — Greek οὐκ ἐξ ἔργων … ἀλλὰ κατὰ τὸ αὐτοῦ ἔλεος ἔσωσεν ἡμᾶς; "we ourselves" renders the stressed ἡμεῖς.
- **"by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost" → "through the washing of regeneration and renewal of the *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v5) — Greek διὰ λουτροῦ παλιγγενεσίας καὶ ἀνακαινώσεως πνεύματος ἁγίου. "Renewal" can depend on "washing" (a washing of regeneration and of renewal) or on "through" (through a washing, and through a renewal); the verse leaves both open.
- **"Which he shed" → "which he poured out"** (v6) — Greek οὗ ἐξέχεεν; the relative agrees with πνεύματος, a neuter noun; the gender is grammatical.
- **"his grace" → "that one's grace"** (v7) — Greek τῇ ἐκείνου χάριτι, a demonstrative, not the plain "his".
- **"the hope" → "hope"** (v7) — Greek κατ᾽ ἐλπίδα ζωῆς αἰωνίου, no article. "Of eternal life" can go with "hope" or with "heirs".
- **"This is a faithful saying" → "Faithful is the word"** (v8) — Greek Πιστὸς ὁ λόγος.
- **"believed in God" → "believed *Elohim*"** (v8) — Greek πεπιστευκότες θεῷ, a plain dative, without "in".
- **"maintain good works" → "take the lead in good works"** (v8, 14) — Greek καλῶν ἔργων προΐστασθαι, literally "stand before good works": to be foremost in them, or to busy oneself with them.
- **"questions" → "questionings"; "contentions" → "strifes"; "strivings about the law" → "fights about the *Torah*"** (v9) — Greek ζητήσεις, ἔρεις (plural), μάχας νομικάς; νομικός is an adjective, "concerning the law".
- **"A man that is an heretick" → "A factious person"** (v10) — Greek αἱρετικὸν ἄνθρωπον, one who chooses a party (αἵρεσις); the later sense "heretic" is not yet in the word.
- **"the first and second" → "one and a second"** (v10) — Greek μίαν καὶ δευτέραν.
- **"reject" → "refuse"** (v10) — Greek παραιτοῦ, "decline, ask to be excused from".
- **"subverted" → "has been perverted"** (v11) — Greek ἐξέστραπται, perfect passive, "has been turned inside out".
- **"be diligent" kept; "diligently" kept** (v12, 13) — Greek σπούδασον, σπουδαίως, one root.
- **"that nothing be wanting" → "that nothing may be lacking"** (v13) — Greek λείπῃ, the same verb as 1:5.
- **"salute" / "Greet" → "greet" / "Greet"** (v15) — one Greek verb, ἀσπάζομαι.
- **"in the faith" → "in faith"** (v15) — Greek ἐν πίστει, no article.
- **"Amen" omitted** (v15) — not in NA28.
- **"you all"** (v15) — plural in Greek (πάντων ὑμῶν); elsewhere "you" is Titus alone.
