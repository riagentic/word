# Titus 2

[1] But you, speak the things that befit the healthy doctrine.

[2] Old men are to be temperate, dignified, sober-minded, healthy in faith, in love, in endurance.

[3] Old women likewise are to be in demeanor as befits what is sacred, not slanderers nor enslaved to much wine, teachers of what is good,

[4] that they may train the young women in sober-mindedness to love their husbands, to love their children,

[5] to be sober-minded, pure, workers at home, good, subject to their own husbands, that the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may not be blasphemed.

[6] The younger men likewise exhort to be sober-minded;

[7] in all things showing yourself a pattern of good works: in the doctrine incorruptness, dignity,

[8] a healthy word that cannot be condemned, so that he who is of the opposing side may be put to shame, having nothing bad to say about us.

[9] Slaves are to be subject to their own masters in all things, to be well-pleasing, not contradicting,

[10] not pilfering, but showing all good faith, so that they may adorn the doctrine of our Savior ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in all things.

[11] **For the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ appeared, bringing salvation to all people**,

[12] training us, so that, denying ungodliness and the worldly desires, we might live sober-mindedly and righteously and godly in the present age,

[13] awaiting the blessed hope and appearing of the glory of our great ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Savior ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[14] who gave himself for us, that he might ransom us from all lawlessness and purify for himself a people of his own possession, zealous for good works.

[15] These things speak and exhort and reprove with all command. Let no one look down on you.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v13) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv5, 10, 11, 13) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v13) — in "Savior *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*."
- **"sound" → "healthy"** (v1, 2, 8) — Greek ὑγιαίνω, ὑγιής, "in health"; as 1:9, 13.
- **"aged men" / "aged women" → "old men" / "old women"** (v2, 3) — Greek πρεσβύτας, πρεσβύτιδας; the same word as Phlm 9.
- **"sober" → "temperate"; "temperate" → "sober-minded"** (v2) — Greek νηφαλίους ("without wine, clear-headed") and σώφρονας; the σωφρ- words (v2, 4, 5, 6, 12; 1:8) are all rendered with "sober-mind-".
- **"as becometh holiness" → "as befits what is sacred"** (v3) — Greek ἱεροπρεπεῖς, "befitting the sacred"; πρέπει "befit" as in v1.
- **"not given to much wine" → "nor enslaved to much wine"** (v3) — Greek δεδουλωμένας, "having been made slaves".
- **"teach … to be sober" → "train … in sober-mindedness"** (v4) — Greek σωφρονίζωσιν, "bring to a sound mind"; one verb, no separate "teach".
- **"keepers at home" → "workers at home"** (v5) — NA28 οἰκουργούς, "working at home"; KJV follows the later reading οἰκουρούς, "keeping the house".
- **"good" kept** (v5) — Greek ἀγαθάς.
- **"obedient" → "subject"** (v5, 9) — Greek ὑποτάσσομαι, "to place oneself under"; also 3:1.
- **"blasphemed" kept** (v5) — Greek βλασφημῆται; the same verb in 3:2.
- **"in all things"** (v6–7) — Greek περὶ πάντα stands between "to be sober-minded" and "showing yourself"; it can belong to either. The printed text (SBLGNT) puts the stop before it and joins it to "showing yourself". NA28: the comma stands after περὶ πάντα, joining it to "to be sober-minded" (quoted from memory).
- **"gravity, sincerity" → "dignity"** (v7) — NA28 has ἀφθορίαν, σεμνότητα only; "sincerity" (ἀφθαρσίαν) is not in the critical text.
- **"sound speech" → "a healthy word"** (v8) — Greek λόγον ὑγιῆ; λόγος is "word" throughout the letter.
- **"of you" → "about us"** (v8) — NA28 περὶ ἡμῶν.
- **"servants" → "slaves"** (v9) — Greek δούλους.
- **"not answering again" → "not contradicting"** (v9) — Greek ἀντιλέγοντας, the same verb as 1:9.
- **"fidelity" → "faith"** (v10) — Greek πίστιν … ἀγαθήν; πίστις is "faith" or "faithfulness".
- **"hath appeared to all men" → "appeared, bringing salvation to all people"** (v11) — Greek Ἐπεφάνη … σωτήριος πᾶσιν ἀνθρώποις. "To all people" can go with "bringing salvation" (σωτήριος) or with "appeared".
- **"teaching" → "training"** (v12) — Greek παιδεύουσα, the bringing up of a child (παῖς), with discipline.
- **"lusts" → "desires"** (v12) — Greek ἐπιθυμίας; also 3:3.
- **"that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing" → "the blessed hope and appearing of the glory"** (v13) — Greek τὴν μακαρίαν ἐλπίδα καὶ ἐπιφάνειαν τῆς δόξης: one article for "hope and appearing"; "of the glory" is a noun, not an adjective.
- **"the great God and our Saviour" → "our great *Elohim* and Savior"** (v13) — Greek τοῦ μεγάλου θεοῦ καὶ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ: one article for "*Elohim* and Savior". The wording can be read of one person (*Yeshua*) or of two; the verse adds no comma and no second article.
- **"redeem" → "ransom"** (v14) — Greek λυτρώσηται, "release for a ransom (λύτρον)".
- **"iniquity" → "lawlessness"** (v14) — Greek ἀνομίας.
- **"a peculiar people" → "a people of his own possession"** (v14) — Greek λαὸν περιούσιον; the Septuagint's word for Hebrew *segullah* (Exod 19:5).
- **"authority" → "command"** (v15) — Greek ἐπιταγῆς, the same word as in 1:3.
- **"despise" → "look down on"** (v15) — Greek περιφρονείτω.

### Notes
- **v2–10** — the Greek has no "are to be": the infinitives depend on "speak" (v1) and "exhort" (v6).
