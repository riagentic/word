# 3 John 1

[1] The elder to Gaius the beloved, whom I love in truth.

[2] Beloved, concerning all things I pray that you prosper and be healthy, just as your soul prospers.

[3] For I rejoiced greatly when brothers were coming and testifying to your truth, just as you walk in truth.

[4] I have no greater joy than these things, that I hear of my own children walking in the truth.

[5] Beloved, you do a faithful thing in whatever you work for the brothers, and strangers at that,

[6] who testified to your love before the church; you will do well to send them forward worthily of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[7] For on behalf of the name they went out, taking nothing from the Gentiles.

[8] We therefore ought to take in such ones, that we may become fellow workers for the truth.

[9] I wrote something to the church; but Diotrephes, who loves to be first among them, does not receive us.

[10] Because of this, if I come, I will bring to remembrance his works which he does, babbling against us with evil words; and not content with these, neither does he himself receive the brothers, and those who wish to he hinders and casts out of the church.

[11] Beloved, do not imitate the evil but the good. The one who does good is from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; the one who does evil has not seen ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[12] ⟦demetrius|Demetrios|Demetrius⟧ has been testified to by all, and by the truth itself; and we also testify, and you know that our testimony is true.

[13] I had many things to write to you, but I do not wish to write to you with ink and pen.

[14] But I hope to see you immediately, and we will speak mouth to mouth. Peace to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6, 11) — systematic.
- **"church"** (v6, 9, 10) — Greek ἐκκλησία *ekklēsia* = "assembly/congregation"; KJV "church" retained.
- **"I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper" → "concerning all things I pray that you prosper"** (v2) — Greek περὶ πάντων εὔχομαι: "concerning all things", not "above all"; εὔχομαι is "pray" or "wish". εὐοδοῦσθαι "prosper" (literally "be led on a good road") is the same verb as in "your soul prospers"; ὑγιαίνειν "be healthy".
- **"when the brethren came and testified of the truth that is in thee" → "when brothers were coming and testifying to your truth"** (v3) — Greek ἐρχομένων ἀδελφῶν καὶ μαρτυρούντων σου τῇ ἀληθείᾳ: present participles, no article with "brothers", and "your truth".
- **"I have no greater joy than to hear that my children walk in truth" → "I have no greater joy than these things, that I hear of my own children walking in the truth"** (v4) — Greek μειζοτέραν τούτων, plural "than these things"; τὰ ἐμὰ τέκνα, the emphatic "my own". "in the truth": ἐν τῇ ἀληθείᾳ (SBLGNT WH Treg NA27); without the article, "in truth" (NA28 RP).
- **"thou doest faithfully whatsoever thou doest to the brethren, and to strangers" → "you do a faithful thing in whatever you work for the brothers, and strangers at that"** (v5) — Greek πιστὸν ποιεῖς ὃ ἐὰν ἐργάσῃ εἰς τοὺς ἀδελφοὺς καὶ τοῦτο ξένους: two different verbs ("do", "work"); καὶ τοῦτο = "and that": the brothers are themselves the strangers.
- **"bring forward on their journey after a godly sort" → "send them forward worthily of *Elohim*"** (v6) — Greek προπέμψας ἀξίως τοῦ θεοῦ, "worthily of God".
- **"for his name's sake" → "on behalf of the name"** (v7) — Greek ὑπὲρ τοῦ ὀνόματος; no "his". "Gentiles" is ἐθνικῶν *ethnikōn* ("people of the nations, pagans"), an adjective, not the usual ἔθνη.
- **"to receive such" → "to take in such ones"** (v8) — Greek ὑπολαμβάνειν, "take up, take under one's care"; a different verb from "receive" (ἐπιδέχεται) in v9, 10. "fellow workers for the truth": the dative τῇ ἀληθείᾳ may also be "with the truth".
- **"I wrote unto the church" → "I wrote something to the church"** (v9) — Greek ἔγραψά τι, "something".
- **"who loveth to have the preeminence among them" → "who loves to be first among them"** (v9) — Greek ὁ φιλοπρωτεύων αὐτῶν Διοτρέφης *ho philoprōteuōn autōn Diotrephes*.
- **"receiveth us not" → "does not receive us"** (v9) — Greek οὐκ ἐπιδέχεται ἡμᾶς; the same verb as "receive the brothers" in v10. The verse does not say "authority".
- **"I will remember his deeds which he doeth, prating against us with malicious words" → "I will bring to remembrance his works which he does, babbling against us with evil words"** (v10) — Greek ὑπομνήσω αὐτοῦ τὰ ἔργα ἃ ποιεῖ, λόγοις πονηροῖς φλυαρῶν ἡμᾶς *phlyarōn hēmas logois ponērois*.
- **"follow not that which is evil, but that which is good" → "do not imitate the evil but the good"** (v11) — Greek μὴ μιμοῦ τὸ κακὸν ἀλλὰ τὸ ἀγαθόν; the verb stands once.
- **"Demetrius hath good report of all men" → "Demetrios has been testified to by all"** (v12) — Greek Δημητρίῳ μεμαρτύρηται, a perfect passive of "testify"; no "good". "you know" is singular οἶδας.
- **"with ink and pen" kept** (v13) — Greek διὰ μέλανος καὶ καλάμου; κάλαμος is a reed pen.
- **"I trust I shall shortly see thee, and we shall speak face to face" → "I hope to see you immediately, and we will speak mouth to mouth"** (v14) — Greek εὐθέως "immediately, at once"; στόμα πρὸς στόμα *stoma pros stoma*.
- **"Greet the friends by name" kept** (v14) — Greek κατ' ὄνομα; no "each". The printed Greek (SBLGNT, as NA28) counts "Peace to you …" as v15; this text keeps the KJV numbering of 14 verses.
