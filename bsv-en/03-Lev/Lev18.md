# Leviticus 18

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] According to the deed of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, in which you dwelt, you shall not do; and according to the deed of the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, to which I am bringing you, you shall not do; and in their statutes you shall not walk.

[4] My ordinances you shall do, and my statutes you shall keep, to walk in them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] And you shall keep my statutes and my ordinances, which a man shall do and live by them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] Any man, to any near kin of his flesh — you shall not come near to uncover nakedness. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] The nakedness of your father and the nakedness of your mother you shall not uncover; she is your mother; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

[8] The nakedness of your father's wife you shall not uncover; it is your father's nakedness.

[9] The nakedness of your sister, your father's daughter or your mother's daughter, born in the house or born outside — you shall not uncover their nakedness.

[10] The nakedness of your son's daughter or of your daughter's daughter — you shall not uncover their nakedness, for they are your nakedness.

[11] The nakedness of the daughter of your father's wife, born of your father — she is your sister — you shall not uncover her nakedness.

[12] The nakedness of your father's sister you shall not uncover; she is your father's near kin.

[13] The nakedness of your mother's sister you shall not uncover, for she is your mother's near kin.

[14] The nakedness of your father's brother you shall not uncover; to his wife you shall not come near; she is your aunt.

[15] The nakedness of your daughter-in-law you shall not uncover; she is your son's wife; you shall not uncover her nakedness.

[16] The nakedness of your brother's wife you shall not uncover; it is your brother's nakedness.

[17] The nakedness of a woman and her daughter you shall not uncover; her son's daughter and her daughter's daughter you shall not take, to uncover her nakedness; they are near kin; it is depravity.

[18] And a woman to her sister you shall not take, to be a rival, to uncover her nakedness beside her, in her lifetime.

[19] And to a woman in the impurity of her uncleanness you shall not come near, to uncover her nakedness.

[20] And to the wife of your fellow you shall not give your lying for seed, to become unclean by her.

[21] And of your seed you shall not give, to make pass over to Molech; and you shall not profane the name of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] And with a male you shall not lie the lyings of a woman; it is an abomination.

[23] And with any beast you shall not give your lying, to become unclean by it; and a woman shall not stand before a beast to mate with it; it is confusion.

[24] Do not defile yourselves by any of these, for by all these the nations were defiled that I am sending away from before you.

[25] And the land became unclean, and I visited its iniquity upon it, and the land vomited out its inhabitants.

[26] And you, you shall keep my statutes and my ordinances, and you shall not do any of these abominations, the native and the sojourner who sojourns in your midst.

[27] For all these abominations the men of the land did, who were before you, and the land became unclean.

[28] And the land shall not vomit you out when you defile it, as it vomited out the nation that was before you.

[29] For everyone who does any of these abominations — the souls that do them shall be cut off from the midst of their people.

[30] And you shall keep my charge, not to do any of the statutes of abominations that were done before you, and you shall not defile yourselves by them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"After the doings of the land of Egypt … shall ye not do" → "According to the deed of the land of Egypt … you shall not do"** (v3) — Hebrew כְּמַעֲשֵׂה, a singular noun of the root of the verb "do" that follows: what is done there. **"wherein ye dwelt" → "in which you dwelt"** — Hebrew יָשַׁב. **"neither shall ye walk in their ordinances" → "in their statutes you shall not walk"** — Hebrew חֻקֹּת, the same word as "my statutes" in v4, 5, 26 and "the statutes of abominations" in v30.
- **"judgments" → "ordinances"** (v4, 5, 26) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים, "judgments": rulings laid down.
- **"which if a man do, he shall live in them" → "which a man shall do and live by them"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יַעֲשֶׂה אֹתָם הָאָדָם וָחַי בָּהֶם: there is no "if"; הָאָדָם is "the man" (mankind, anyone); בָּהֶם is "in them" or "by them".
- **"None of you shall approach to any that is near of kin to him" → "Any man, to any near kin of his flesh — you shall not come near"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ אִישׁ, literally "a man, a man", followed by a plural verb, "you shall not come near"; the break is the Hebrew's. שְׁאֵר בְּשָׂרוֹ is literally "flesh of his flesh", two words for "flesh": שְׁאֵר (v12, 13, 17) is used of blood relations and is "near kin"; בָּשָׂר is "flesh". **"approach" → "come near"** (v6, 14, 19) — Hebrew קָרַב.
- **"uncover the nakedness"** (v6–19) — Hebrew גִּלָּה עֶרְוָה, kept word for word and the same each time. It is the chapter's expression for sexual union; of whose nakedness it is, v7, 8, 10 and 16 speak themselves.
- **"or the nakedness of thy mother" → "and the nakedness of your mother"** (v7) — Hebrew וְ "and". Whether two persons are named or the second explains the first ("that is, the nakedness of your mother") the Hebrew does not say; the verse goes on with the mother alone.
- **"born at home, or born abroad" → "born in the house or born outside"** (v9) — Hebrew מוֹלֶדֶת בַּיִת אוֹ מוֹלֶדֶת חוּץ: מוֹלֶדֶת is a noun, "birth, offspring" (elsewhere "kindred"), so literally "a birth of the house or a birth of outside". What the two cases are (born in the father's house or elsewhere; in wedlock or not) is not said. **"begotten of thy father" → "born of your father"** (v11) — the same noun, מוֹלֶדֶת אָבִיךָ; "beget" is another word.
- **"for theirs is thine own nakedness" → "for they are your nakedness"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי עֶרְוָתְךָ הֵנָּה.
- **"she is thy father's near kinswoman" → "she is your father's near kin"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew שְׁאֵר, see v6. **"for they are her near kinswomen" → "they are near kin"** (v17) — Hebrew שַׁאֲרָה הֵנָּה: the form שַׁאֲרָה stands only here; "her" is not in the pointed text.
- **"thou shalt not approach to his wife" → "to his wife you shall not come near"** (v14) — the uncle's nakedness is uncovered in his wife (as v8, 16).
- **"it is wickedness" → "it is depravity"** (v17) — Hebrew זִמָּה, from a root "plan, devise": a shameful design; used chiefly of sexual wrong (19:29; 20:14).
- **"Neither shalt thou take a wife to her sister, to vex her … beside the other in her life time" → "a woman to her sister you shall not take, to be a rival … beside her, in her lifetime"** (v18) — Hebrew לִצְרֹר, an infinitive with no object: either "to be a rival wife" (צָרָה, 1 Sam 1:6) or "to distress"; the first is the better supported, the sense is not certain. אִשָּׁה is "woman" and "wife". עָלֶיהָ is "upon her, in addition to her".
- **"as long as she is put apart for her uncleanness" → "in the impurity of her uncleanness"** (v19) — Hebrew בְּנִדַּת טֻמְאָתָהּ: נִדָּה is the impurity of the monthly flow (15:19); "as long as" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"thou shalt not lie carnally with thy neighbour's wife" → "to the wife of your fellow you shall not give your lying for seed"** (v20) — Hebrew לֹא־תִתֵּן שְׁכָבְתְּךָ לְזָרַע, word for word; "lie carnally" is the meaning. עָמִית is "fellow, associate", another word than רֵעַ "neighbor". **"to defile thyself with her" → "to become unclean by her"** (v20, 23) — Hebrew לְטָמְאָה־בָהּ, the simple verb "be unclean".
- **"thou shalt not let any of thy seed pass through the fire to Molech" → "of your seed you shall not give, to make pass over to Molech"** (v21) — Hebrew וּמִזַּרְעֲךָ לֹא־תִתֵּן לְהַעֲבִיר לַמֹּלֶךְ. "The fire" is not in this verse (it stands in 2 Kgs 23:10). הֶעֱבִיר is "make pass over", the verb of Exod 13:12 for handing over the firstling. מֹלֶךְ is written with the article, "the Molech"; the consonants are those of מֶלֶךְ "king". The Septuagint, cited from memory, has "to serve a ruler" here.
- **"Thou shalt not lie with mankind, as with womankind" → "with a male you shall not lie the lyings of a woman"** (v22) — Hebrew וְאֶת־זָכָר לֹא תִשְׁכַּב מִשְׁכְּבֵי אִשָּׁה, word for word: זָכָר is "male"; מִשְׁכְּבֵי אִשָּׁה is "the lyings of a woman" (a plural noun of the verb "lie", the "bed" of 15:4), that is, the way one lies with a woman. "As with" is not in the Hebrew. **"abomination" kept** (v22, 26, 27, 29, 30) — Hebrew תּוֹעֵבָה.
- **"Neither shalt thou lie with any beast" → "with any beast you shall not give your lying"** (v23) — Hebrew וּבְכָל־בְּהֵמָה לֹא־תִתֵּן שְׁכָבְתְּךָ, as v20; בְּ is "in, with". **"to lie down thereto" → "to mate with it"** — Hebrew לְרִבְעָהּ, a verb used of animals coupling (19:19; 20:16), not the verb "lie". **"it is confusion" kept** — Hebrew תֶּבֶל, from בָּלַל "mix": a mixing of what is kept apart (also 20:12).
- **"Defile not ye yourselves … are defiled" → "Do not defile yourselves … were defiled"** (v24, 30) — Hebrew הִטַּמֵּא and נִטְמָא, of the root of טָמֵא "unclean", as "became unclean" (v25, 27), "defile" (v28) and "become unclean" (v20, 23). **"which I cast out" → "that I am sending away"** — Hebrew מְשַׁלֵּחַ.
- **"And the land is defiled: therefore I do visit … vomiteth out" → "And the land became unclean, and I visited … vomited out"** (v25) — Hebrew וַתִּטְמָא … וָאֶפְקֹד … וַתָּקִא, three narrative past forms: the speech looks back on it as done. "Therefore" and "itself" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"any stranger that sojourneth among you" → "the sojourner who sojourns in your midst"** (v26) — Hebrew הַגֵּר הַגָּר בְּתוֹכְכֶם. **"commit" → "do"** (v26, 27, 29, 30) — Hebrew עָשָׂה.
- **"That the land spue not you out also" → "And the land shall not vomit you out"** (v28) — Hebrew וְלֹא־תָקִיא; "also" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the souls that commit them" → "the souls that do them"** (v29) — Hebrew הַנְּפָשׁוֹת הָעֹשֹׂת; נֶפֶשׁ is "soul". "From the midst of their people": עַמָּם is singular here.
- **"keep mine ordinance, that ye commit not any one of these abominable customs" → "keep my charge, not to do any of the statutes of abominations"** (v30) — Hebrew מִשְׁמַרְתִּי, "what I have given to be kept"; חֻקּוֹת הַתּוֹעֵבֹת, "the statutes of the abominations", the word "statutes" of v3.

### Notes

- **Holiness Code** — in Lev 17–26 conduct in marriage and kinship, the worship of other gods and the land belong together: what makes the people unclean makes the land unclean, and the land "vomits out" its inhabitants.
- **"Uncover nakedness"** — the Hebrew expression (גִּלָּה עֶרְוָה) for sexual union, kept as it stands.
- **Forbidden degrees** (v6–18) — parallel list in ch 20 (with penalties). The Anglican "Table of Kindred and Affinity" is drawn from these verses.
- **Molech** (v21) — the giving of children to Molech is condemned also in 20:2–5, 2 Kgs 23:10 and Jer 32:35.
- **Land-vomit motif** (v25, 28) — the land itself is a party: it became unclean and vomited out those who were before Israel (20:22).
- **Paul's "live by them"** (v5) — Rom 10:5 and Gal 3:12 quote this verse.
