# Leviticus 8

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Take ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons with him, and the garments, and the oil of anointing, and the bull of the sin offering, and the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread;

[3] and assemble all the congregation at the entrance of the tent of meeting."

[4] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ did as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded him, and the congregation was assembled at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[5] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to the congregation, "This is the thing that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded to do."

[6] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ brought near ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons, and he washed them with water.

[7] And he placed the tunic on him, and girded him with the sash, and clothed him with the robe, and placed the ephod on him; and he girded him with the band of the ephod, and bound the ephod on him with it.

[8] And he put the breastpiece on him, and he placed into the breastpiece the Urim and the ⟦thummim|Tummim|Thummim⟧.

[9] And he put the turban on his head, and he put on the turban, over against its face, the plate of gold, the holy crown, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[10] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ took the oil of anointing and anointed the dwelling and all that was in it, and he sanctified them.

[11] And he sprinkled of it on the altar seven times, and he anointed the altar and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand, to sanctify them.

[12] And he poured of the oil of anointing on the head of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and he anointed him to sanctify him.

[13] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ brought near the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and clothed them with tunics, and girded them with a sash, and bound caps on them, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[14] And he brought forward the bull of the sin offering, and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons laid their hands on the head of the bull of the sin offering.

[15] And he slaughtered; and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ took the blood and placed it on the horns of the altar around with his finger, and he purged the altar of sin; and the blood he poured at the base of the altar, and he sanctified it, to make atonement for it.

[16] And he took all the fat that was on the entrails, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ turned them into smoke on the altar.

[17] And the bull, and its skin and its flesh and its dung, he burned with fire outside the camp, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[18] And he brought near the ram of the burnt offering, and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.

[19] And he slaughtered; and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ dashed the blood against the altar around.

[20] And the ram he cut into its pieces, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ turned into smoke the head and the pieces and the suet.

[21] And the entrails and the shanks he washed with water, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ turned the whole ram into smoke on the altar; it was a burnt offering for a soothing smell, an offering by fire it was to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[22] And he brought near the second ram, the ram of filling, and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons laid their hands on the head of the ram.

[23] And he slaughtered; and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ took of its blood and placed it on the lobe of the right ear of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and on the thumb of his right hand, and on the great toe of his right foot.

[24] And he brought near the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ placed of the blood on the lobe of their right ear, and on the thumb of their right hand, and on the great toe of their right foot; and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ dashed the blood against the altar around.

[25] And he took the fat and the fat tail, and all the fat that was on the entrails, and the lobe of the liver, and the two kidneys and their fat, and the right leg.

[26] And from the basket of unleavened bread that was before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ he took one unleavened cake, and one cake of bread with oil, and one wafer, and he put them on the fat parts and on the right leg.

[27] And he placed the whole on the palms of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and on the palms of his sons, and he waved them as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[28] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ took them from upon their palms and turned them into smoke on the altar, upon the burnt offering; they were a filling, for a soothing smell; it was an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[29] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; from the ram of filling it was the portion of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[30] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ took of the oil of anointing and of the blood that was on the altar, and he sprinkled it on ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, on his garments, and on his sons and on the garments of his sons with him; and he sanctified ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, his garments, and his sons and the garments of his sons with him.

[31] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons, "Boil the flesh at the entrance of the tent of meeting, and there you shall eat it, and the bread that is in the basket of filling, as I commanded, saying, '⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall eat it.'

[32] And what remains of the flesh and of the bread you shall burn with fire.

[33] And from the entrance of the tent of meeting you shall not go out seven days, until the day when the days of your filling are full; for seven days he will fill your hand.

[34] As he has done on this day, so ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded to do, to make atonement for you.

[35] And at the entrance of the tent of meeting you shall remain day and night, seven days, and you shall keep the charge of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and you shall not die; for so I have been commanded."

[36] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons did all the things that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ "and"; the chain is kept. The chapter carries out Exod 29, and the same Hebrew has the same English as there.
- **"the anointing oil" → "the oil of anointing"** (v2, 10, 12, 30) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה. **"a bullock for the sin offering" → "the bull of the sin offering"** (v2, 14) — Hebrew פַּר הַחַטָּאת; "sin offering" is the one word חַטָּאת "sin".
- **"gather thou all the congregation together" → "assemble all the congregation"; "the assembly was gathered together" → "the congregation was assembled"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew הַקְהֵל, וַתִּקָּהֵל, the verb of קָהָל "assembly"; the noun is עֵדָה "congregation" both times. **"the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "the entrance of the tent of meeting"** (v3, 4, 31, 33, 35) — Hebrew פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"brought" → "brought near"** (v6, 13, 18, 22, 24) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב, hifil of קָרַב "come near". In v14 the verb is another, הִגִּישׁ, **"brought forward"**.
- **"put" → "placed" / "put"** (v7–9, 15, 23, 24, 26, 27) — two Hebrew verbs alternate and are kept apart: נָתַן "place" (literally "give") and שִׂים "put".
- **"girded him with the girdle" → "girded him with the sash"** (v7); **"girded them with girdles" → "girded them with a sash"** (v13) — Hebrew אַבְנֵט, singular both times. **"coat" → "tunic"** (v7, 13) — Hebrew כֻּתֹּנֶת.
- **"he girded him with the curious girdle of the ephod, and bound it unto him therewith" → "he girded him with the band of the ephod, and bound the ephod on him with it"** (v7) — Hebrew חֵשֶׁב הָאֵפֹד, a band found only with the ephod; וַיֶּאְפֹּד לוֹ בּוֹ is a verb made from the noun "ephod": "he ephod-ed for him with it" (Exod 29:5).
- **"breastplate" → "breastpiece"** (v8) — Hebrew חֹשֶׁן; the sense of the word is not certain. **"he put in the breastplate" → "he placed into the breastpiece"** — Hebrew אֶל־הַחֹשֶׁן. What the Urim and Thummim were is not known.
- **"mitre" → "turban"** (v9) — Hebrew מִצְנֶפֶת. **"upon his forefront" → "over against its face"** — Hebrew אֶל־מוּל פָּנָיו: "its face" (the front of the turban, Exod 28:37) or "his face". **"the golden plate, the holy crown" → "the plate of gold, the holy crown"** — Hebrew צִיץ הַזָּהָב נֵזֶר הַקֹּדֶשׁ: צִיץ is literally "blossom", a shining ornament; נֵזֶר הַקֹּדֶשׁ is "the crown of holiness", the mark of one set apart.
- **"the tabernacle" → "the dwelling"** (v10) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן. **"sanctified"** (v10, 11, 12, 15, 30) — Hebrew קִדֵּשׁ, one verb for KJV "sanctify" and "consecrate".
- **"vessels" → "utensils"; "the laver and his foot" → "the basin and its stand"** (v11) — Hebrew כֵּלִים; הַכִּיֹּר וְכַנּוֹ.
- **"put bonnets upon them" → "bound caps on them"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיַּחֲבֹשׁ לָהֶם מִגְבָּעוֹת.
- **"And he slew it; and Moses took the blood" → "And he slaughtered; and Moses took the blood"** (v15, 19, 23) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁחָט, with no object and no subject named; the accents set it apart from what follows. The verse supplies neither. שָׁחַט is "slaughter".
- **"purified the altar" → "purged the altar of sin"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיְחַטֵּא אֶת־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ, the piel of חָטָא "sin": "de-sinned". **"poured the blood at the bottom of the altar" → "the blood he poured at the base of the altar"** — Hebrew יָצַק אֶל־יְסוֹד. **"to make reconciliation upon it" → "to make atonement for it"** — Hebrew לְכַפֵּר עָלָיו; כִּפֶּר is literally "cover", עָלָיו "upon it" or "for it".
- **"the inwards" → "the entrails"; "the caul above the liver" → "the lobe of the liver"** (v16, 25) — Hebrew קֶרֶב; יֹתֶרֶת הַכָּבֵד, an appendage of the liver, the exact part is uncertain.
- **"burned" → "turned into smoke"** (v16, 20, 21, 28); **"burnt with fire" kept** (v17, 32) — two Hebrew verbs: on the altar הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke"; outside the camp and of what remains שָׂרַף "burn".
- **"his hide" → "its skin"** (v17) — Hebrew עוֹר.
- **"sprinkled it upon the altar round about" → "dashed the blood against the altar around"** (v19, 24) — Hebrew זָרַק, to throw from a bowl; "sprinkle" (v11, 30) is הִזָּה.
- **"the fat" → "the suet"** (v20) — Hebrew פֶּדֶר, a word for fat found only here and in 1:8, 12; it is another word than חֵלֶב "fat".
- **"legs" → "shanks"** (v21) — Hebrew כְּרָעַיִם, the lower legs. **"a sweet savour" → "a soothing smell"** (v21, 28) — Hebrew רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ, from נוּחַ "rest". **"an offering made by fire" → "an offering by fire"** — Hebrew אִשֶּׁה, commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain.
- **"the ram of consecration" → "the ram of filling"** (v22, 29); **"consecrations" → "a filling"** (v28); **"the basket of consecrations" → "the basket of filling"** (v31) — Hebrew מִלֻּאִים, the noun of מִלֵּא יָד "fill the hand", the set expression for installing a priest ("ordain"); see v33.
- **"the tip of Aaron's right ear" → "the lobe of the right ear of Aaron"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew תְּנוּךְ אֹזֶן, the lobe (or the upper rim) of the ear. "Thumb" and "great toe" are one word, בֹּהֶן. In v24 "ear", "hand" and "foot" are singular in the Hebrew.
- **"the rump" → "the fat tail"; "the right shoulder" → "the right leg"** (v25, 26) — Hebrew הָאַלְיָה; שׁוֹק, the upper hind leg.
- **"a cake of oiled bread" → "one cake of bread with oil"** (v26) — Hebrew חַלַּת לֶחֶם שֶׁמֶן, "a cake of bread of oil". **"put them on the fat" → "on the fat parts"** — Hebrew הַחֲלָבִים, plural.
- **"upon Aaron's hands" → "on the palms of Aaron"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew כַּף. **"waved them for a wave offering"** (v27, 29) — Hebrew וַיָּנֶף … תְּנוּפָה, verb and noun of one root: to move to and fro (or to lift) before Yahweh.
- **"it was Moses' part" → "it was the portion of Moses"** (v29) — Hebrew לְמֹשֶׁה הָיָה לְמָנָה.
- **"upon Aaron, and upon his garments" → "on Aaron, on his garments"** (v30) — the Hebrew has no "and" before "his garments", either time.
- **"as I commanded"** (v31) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר צִוֵּיתִי, active, as it is pointed. (The old versions are said to read a passive, "as I was commanded", as in v35: from memory, not checked.)
- **"that which remaineth" → "what remains"** (v32) — Hebrew הַנּוֹתָר.
- **"until the days of your consecration be at an end: for seven days shall he consecrate you" → "until the day when the days of your filling are full; for seven days he will fill your hand"** (v33) — Hebrew עַד יוֹם מְלֹאת יְמֵי מִלֻּאֵיכֶם כִּי שִׁבְעַת יָמִים יְמַלֵּא אֶת־יֶדְכֶם: the root מלא "fill" three times. Who "he" is the Hebrew does not say.
- **"As he hath done this day"** (v34) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה בַּיּוֹם הַזֶּה, with the subject not named ("as has been done").
- **"abide" → "remain"** (v35) — Hebrew תֵּשְׁבוּ, "sit, dwell"; here of staying seven days, as 12:4. **"that ye die not" → "and you shall not die"** — Hebrew וְלֹא תָמוּתוּ.

### Notes

- **Exodus 29 carried out** — what was commanded there is done here, step by step; "as Yahweh commanded Moses" closes each part (v9, 13, 17, 21, 29).
- **Blood on ear, thumb and toe** (v23–24) — the same rite is used for a cleansed leper (14:14).
- **"Fill the hand"** (v33) — the parts of the ram of filling are laid on the palms of Aaron and his sons (v27).
- **Seven days at the entrance** (v33–35) — the filling lasts seven days; the eighth day follows in chapter 9.
