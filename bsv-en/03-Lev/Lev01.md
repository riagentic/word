# Leviticus 1

[1] And he called to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to him from the tent of meeting, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'When a man from among you brings near an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, from the beasts, from the herd and from the flock, you shall bring near your offering.

[3] If his offering is a burnt offering from the herd, a male without blemish he shall bring it near; to the entrance of the tent of meeting he shall bring it near, that he may be found pleasing before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] And he shall lay his hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it shall be found pleasing for him, to make atonement for him.

[5] And he shall slaughter the son of the herd before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, the priests, shall bring near the blood and dash the blood against the altar around, the altar that is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[6] And he shall flay the burnt offering and cut it into its pieces.

[7] And the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest shall place fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire.

[8] And the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, the priests, shall arrange the pieces, the head and the suet, on the wood that is on the fire that is on the altar.

[9] And its entrails and its shanks he shall wash with water; and the priest shall turn the whole into smoke on the altar: a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] And if his offering is from the flock, from the lambs or from the goats, for a burnt offering, a male without blemish he shall bring it near.

[11] And he shall slaughter it on the flank of the altar northward before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, the priests, shall dash its blood against the altar around.

[12] And he shall cut it into its pieces, and its head and its suet; and the priest shall arrange them on the wood that is on the fire that is on the altar.

[13] And the entrails and the shanks he shall wash with water; and the priest shall bring the whole near and turn it into smoke on the altar; it is a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] And if his offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a burnt offering from the birds, then he shall bring near his offering from the turtledoves or from the sons of the dove.

[15] And the priest shall bring it near to the altar and nip its head and turn it into smoke on the altar; and its blood shall be drained out against the wall of the altar.

[16] And he shall remove its crop with its feathers and throw it beside the altar eastward, to the place of the fat ashes.

[17] And he shall tear it open by its wings; he shall not separate it; and the priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar, on the wood that is on the fire; it is a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"And the LORD called unto Moses, and spake" → "And he called to Moses, and Yahweh spoke"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא אֶל־מֹשֶׁה וַיְדַבֵּר יְהוָה אֵלָיו: the first verb has no subject named; "Yahweh" stands with the second. **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (v1, 3, 5) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"If any man of you" → "When a man from among you"** — Hebrew אָדָם כִּי … מִכֶּם: אָדָם "a man, a human being".
- **"bring an offering" → "brings near an offering"** (v2, 3, 10, 14) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב קָרְבָּן, verb and noun of one root (קָרַב "come near"): the offering is "what is brought near". English cannot show the link in the noun; "offering" alone is always קָרְבָּן in this book. The same verb is "bring near" of the blood (v5) and of the parts (v13, 15).
- **"of the cattle, even of the herd, and of the flock" → "from the beasts, from the herd and from the flock"** (v2) — Hebrew מִן־הַבְּהֵמָה מִן־הַבָּקָר וּמִן־הַצֹּאן: בְּהֵמָה "beast", the four-footed animals, of which herd and flock are the two kinds; "and", not "or". The verb changes from "he brings near" to "you (plural) shall bring near".
- **"burnt sacrifice" → "burnt offering"** (v3, 4, 6, 9, 10, 13, 14, 17) — Hebrew עֹלָה, "what goes up".
- **"without blemish"** (v3, 10) — Hebrew תָּמִים, "whole, complete": the word that is "blameless" of a man.
- **"he shall offer it of his own voluntary will" → "he shall bring it near, that he may be found pleasing"** (v3) — Hebrew לִרְצֹנוֹ, literally "for his being found pleasing": the noun רָצוֹן of the verb in v4. KJV takes it of the man's own will; the next verse, with the same root, shows that it is the favor with which he (or it) is received before Yahweh.
- **"it shall be accepted for him" → "it shall be found pleasing for him"** (v4) — Hebrew וְנִרְצָה לוֹ, passive of רָצָה "be pleased with". **"to make atonement for him"** — Hebrew לְכַפֵּר עָלָיו; כִּפֶּר is literally "cover" (or "wipe off").
- **"put his hand upon" → "lay his hand on"** (v4) — Hebrew סָמַךְ "lean, press".
- **"kill the bullock" → "slaughter the son of the herd"** (v5, 11) — Hebrew שָׁחַט; and בֶּן הַבָּקָר, literally so: the animal "from the herd" of v3. The words say nothing of its age.
- **"the priests, Aaron's sons" → "the sons of Aaron, the priests"** (v5, 8, 11) — the Hebrew order. In v7 it is "the sons of Aaron the priest", singular (הַכֹּהֵן).
- **"sprinkle the blood round about upon the altar" → "dash the blood against the altar around"** (v5, 11) — Hebrew זָרַק, to throw from a bowl; as Exod 29:16. **"that is by the door" → "the altar that is at the entrance"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־פֶּתַח; "the altar" is repeated to carry the relative clause.
- **"flay"** (v6) — Hebrew הִפְשִׁיט, "strip". **"his pieces" → "its pieces"** — Hebrew נִתַּח … לִנְתָחֶיהָ, verb and noun of one root.
- **"put fire" → "place fire"** (v7) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "give". **"lay the wood in order" → "arrange wood"** (v7, 8, 12) — Hebrew עָרַךְ; עֵצִים "pieces of wood", without the article.
- **"the fat" → "the suet"** (v8, 12) — Hebrew פֶּדֶר, found only here and in 8:20: a word for fat other than חֵלֶב; its exact sense is uncertain.
- **"his inwards and his legs" → "its entrails and its shanks"** (v9, 13) — Hebrew קֶרֶב and כְּרָעַיִם, the lower legs.
- **"burn all on the altar" → "turn the whole into smoke on the altar"** (v9, 13, 15, 17) — Hebrew הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke" (the root of קְטֹרֶת "incense"); "burn" is another verb, שָׂרַף. הַמִּזְבֵּחָה is literally "to the altar".
- **"to be a burnt sacrifice, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour" → "a burnt offering, an offering by fire of a soothing smell"** (v9, 13, 17) — Hebrew אִשֵּׁה רֵיחַ־נִיחוֹחַ: אִשֵּׁה is the form that binds it to the next words. אִשֶּׁה is commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain. נִיחוֹחַ is from נוּחַ "rest"; as Gen 8:21. In v9 the Hebrew has no "it is"; v13 and v17 have it (הוּא).
- **"of the sheep, or of the goats" → "from the lambs or from the goats"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּשָׂבִים, the sheep of the flock beside the goats.
- **"on the side of the altar northward" → "on the flank of the altar northward"** (v11) — Hebrew יֶרֶךְ, literally "thigh"; as Exod 40:22.
- **"with his head and his fat" → "and its head and its suet"** (v12) — Hebrew וְאֶת־רֹאשׁוֹ וְאֶת־פִּדְרוֹ, joined loosely to "its pieces" by "and".
- **"of fowls" → "from the birds"** (v14) — Hebrew הָעוֹף, a collective. **"of turtledoves, or of young pigeons" → "from the turtledoves or from the sons of the dove"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי הַיּוֹנָה, literally so; יוֹנָה is the "dove" of Gen 8:8. Whether "son" here means a young bird ("young pigeons") or only one of the kind, as in "son of the herd" (v5), is not certain.
- **"wring off his head" → "nip its head"** (v15) — Hebrew מָלַק, found only here and in 5:8: to pinch through, as with the nail. In 5:8 the head is not separated. **"and burn it"** — the Hebrew has no object: "it" is supplied, and may be the head or the bird.
- **"the blood thereof shall be wrung out at the side of the altar" → "its blood shall be drained out against the wall of the altar"** (v15) — Hebrew נִמְצָה, "be pressed out, drained"; קִיר "wall".
- **"pluck away his crop with his feathers" → "remove its crop with its feathers"** (v16) — Hebrew הֵסִיר "remove"; מֻרְאָה is found only here. נֹצָה is "plumage" elsewhere (Ezek 17:3); here "its" is feminine and points to the crop, so that many read "its contents". The meaning is uncertain.
- **"cast it beside the altar on the east part, by the place of the ashes" → "throw it beside the altar eastward, to the place of the fat ashes"** (v16) — Hebrew קֵדְמָה, literally "to the front"; דֶּשֶׁן, the ashes soaked with fat.
- **"cleave it with the wings thereof, but shall not divide it asunder" → "tear it open by its wings; he shall not separate it"** (v17) — Hebrew שִׁסַּע; and לֹא יַבְדִּיל with no "but" and no object.

### Notes

- **Opening of Leviticus** — the book begins in the middle of the narrative: "And he called to Moses … from the tent of meeting". It continues the end of Exodus (40:35, Moses could not enter). The first word, וַיִּקְרָא "and he called", gives the book its Hebrew name.
- **Structure of Leviticus** — offerings (chs 1–7), the installing of the priests (8–10), clean and unclean (11–15), the Day of Atonement (16), the laws of holiness (17–26), vows (27).
- **Five offerings** — ch 1: burnt offering (עֹלָה); ch 2: grain offering (מִנְחָה); ch 3: sacrifice of peace offerings (זֶבַח שְׁלָמִים); ch 4–5: sin offering (חַטָּאת) and guilt offering (אָשָׁם).
- **Laying on of the hand (v4)** — the one who brings the animal presses his hand on its head; the verse says that it is then found pleasing for him, to make atonement for him.
