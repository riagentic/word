# Leviticus 27

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and say to them: 'A man, when he makes a special vow by your valuation of souls to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[3] then your valuation shall be: the male from twenty years old and up to sixty years old, your valuation shall be fifty shekels of silver, by the holy shekel.

[4] And if she is a female, then your valuation shall be thirty shekels.

[5] And if from five years old and up to twenty years old, then your valuation shall be: the male twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels.

[6] And if from a month old and up to five years old, then your valuation shall be: the male five shekels of silver, and for the female your valuation shall be three shekels of silver.

[7] And if from sixty years old and upward: if a male, then your valuation shall be fifteen shekels, and for the female ten shekels.

[8] And if he is too poor for your valuation, then he shall make him stand before the priest, and the priest shall value him; according to what the hand of him who vowed attains to, the priest shall value him.

[9] And if it is a beast of which they bring near an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all that he gives of it to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be holy.

[10] He shall not change it and he shall not exchange it, good for bad or bad for good; and if he surely exchanges beast for beast, then it and its exchange shall be holy.

[11] And if it is any unclean beast of which they do not bring near an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, then he shall make the beast stand before the priest,

[12] and the priest shall value it, between good and bad; according to your valuation, the priest's, so shall it be.

[13] And if he surely redeems it, then he shall add its fifth to your valuation.

[14] And a man, when he sanctifies his house as holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, then the priest shall value it, between good and bad; as the priest values it, so shall it stand.

[15] And if he who sanctifies redeems his house, then he shall add a fifth of the silver of your valuation to it, and it shall be his.

[16] And if a man sanctifies to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ some of the field of his possession, then your valuation shall be according to its seed: the seed of a homer of barley at fifty shekels of silver.

[17] If from the year of the jubilee he sanctifies his field, according to your valuation it shall stand.

[18] And if after the jubilee he sanctifies his field, then the priest shall reckon for him the silver according to the years that remain until the year of the jubilee, and it shall be diminished from your valuation.

[19] And if he who sanctifies it surely redeems the field, then he shall add a fifth of the silver of your valuation to it, and it shall be made sure to him.

[20] And if he does not redeem the field, and if he has sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed any more.

[21] And the field, when it goes out in the jubilee, shall be holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, like the field of the devoted thing; its possession shall be the priest's.

[22] And if he sanctifies to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a field of his purchase, which is not of the field of his possession,

[23] then the priest shall reckon for him the amount of your valuation until the year of the jubilee, and he shall give your valuation on that day, holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[24] In the year of the jubilee the field shall return to him from whom he bought it, to him whose is the possession of the land.

[25] And all your valuation shall be by the holy shekel; twenty gerahs shall the shekel be.

[26] Only a firstborn that is born first to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ among beasts, no man shall sanctify it; whether ox or sheep, it is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧.

[27] And if it is among the unclean beasts, then he shall ransom it by your valuation and add its fifth to it; and if it is not redeemed, then it shall be sold by your valuation.

[28] Only, every devoted thing that a man devotes to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from all that is his, from man and beast and from the field of his possession, shall not be sold and shall not be redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[29] Every devoted thing that is devoted from among men shall not be ransomed; he shall surely be put to death.

[30] And all the tithe of the land, from the seed of the land, from the fruit of the tree, is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧; it is holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] And if a man surely redeems some of his tithe, he shall add its fifth to it.

[32] And all the tithe of herd and flock, all that passes over under the rod: the tenth shall be holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[33] He shall not search between good and bad, and he shall not exchange it; and if he surely exchanges it, then it and its exchange shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed.'"

[34] These are the commandments that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on Mount Sinai.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2, 34) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"When a man shall make a singular vow, the persons shall be for the LORD by thy estimation" → "A man, when he makes a special vow by your valuation of souls to Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ כִּי יַפְלִא נֶדֶר בְּעֶרְכְּךָ נְפָשֹׁת לַיהוָה, a clause without a main verb: he vows to Yahweh the value of persons. יַפְלִא is from the root "be wonderful, beyond the ordinary" (Gen 18:14); the exact force with "vow" is not certain ("make a special vow", "set apart a vow", "fulfil a vow"). נְפָשֹׁת is "souls".
- **"thy estimation" → "your valuation"** (throughout) — Hebrew עֶרְכְּךָ, from עָרַךְ "set in order" (24:8 "arrange"), here "set a value". The suffix "your" has grown fixed to the word: it stands even with the article (v23) and where the priest is spoken of in the third person (v12). **"value"** (v8, 12, 14) is the verb of the same root.
- **"from twenty years old"** (v3, 5, 6, 7) — literally "from a son of twenty years".
- **"after the shekel of the sanctuary" → "by the holy shekel"** (v3, 25) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶל הַקֹּדֶשׁ "the shekel of holiness", or "of the Holy", as Exod 30:13. The shekel is a weight, not a coin.
- **"if he be poorer than thy estimation, then he shall present himself" → "if he is too poor for your valuation, then he shall make him stand"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew מָךְ, the verb of 25:25; וְהֶעֱמִידוֹ "he shall make him stand", with no subject named. **"according to his ability that vowed" → "according to what the hand of him who vowed attains to"** — Hebrew תַּשִּׂיג יַד, as 25:26.
- **"whereof men bring an offering" → "of which they bring near an offering"** (v9, 11) — Hebrew יַקְרִיבוּ … קָרְבָּן, verb and noun of one root, "bring near … what is brought near".
- **"He shall not alter it, nor change it" → "He shall not change it and he shall not exchange it"** (v10, 33) — Hebrew הֶחֱלִיף and הֵמִיר; תְּמוּרָה "its exchange" is the noun of the second: the beast put in its place. "Bad" is רַע, elsewhere "evil". "Surely exchanges" is literally "exchanging he exchanges"; so "surely redeems" (v13, 19, 31).
- **"whether it be good or bad" → "between good and bad"** (v12, 14) — Hebrew בֵּין טוֹב וּבֵין רָע. **"as thou valuest it, who art the priest" → "according to your valuation, the priest's"** (v12) — Hebrew כְּעֶרְכְּךָ הַכֹּהֵן, the fixed form with "the priest" beside it.
- **"redeem"** (v13, 15, 19, 20, 27, 28, 31, 33) — Hebrew גָּאַל, buying back what was one's own; **"ransom"** (v27, 29) is פָּדָה, another root (Exod 13:13). Both stand in v27.
- **"a fifth part thereof" → "its fifth"** (v13, 27, 31) — Hebrew חֲמִישִׁתוֹ.
- **"sanctify his house to be holy"** (v14–22, 26) — Hebrew הִקְדִּישׁ … קֹדֶשׁ, one root: "make holy … holiness". **"so shall it stand"** (v14, 17) — Hebrew יָקוּם.
- **"the money" → "the silver"** (v15, 18, 19) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף.
- **"an homer of barley seed shall be valued at fifty shekels" → "the seed of a homer of barley at fifty shekels"** (v16) — Hebrew זֶרַע חֹמֶר שְׂעֹרִים: the land that a homer of barley sows. "Shall be valued" is not in the Hebrew. The homer is a dry measure, an ass-load.
- **"it shall be abated" → "it shall be diminished"** (v18) — Hebrew נִגְרַע.
- **"it shall be assured to him" → "it shall be made sure to him"** (v19) — Hebrew וְקָם לוֹ, literally "it shall rise, stand for him" (Gen 23:17; 25:30).
- **"or if he have sold" → "and if he has sold"** (v20) — Hebrew וְאִם "and if"; who sells, and whether the second clause is a second case, is not said.
- **"as a field devoted; the possession thereof shall be the priest's" → "like the field of the devoted thing; its possession shall be the priest's"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּשְׂדֵה הַחֵרֶם; אֲחֻזָּתוֹ is "its possession" or "his possession".
- **"a field which he hath bought" → "a field of his purchase"** (v22) — Hebrew שְׂדֵה מִקְנָתוֹ.
- **"the worth of thy estimation" → "the amount of your valuation"** (v23) — Hebrew מִכְסַת, the "count" of Exod 12:4. "As a holy thing" is not in the Hebrew: קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה "holiness to Yahweh".
- **"twenty gerahs shall be the shekel"** (v25) — as Exod 30:13.
- **"the firstling of the beasts, which should be the LORD's firstling" → "a firstborn that is born first to Yahweh among beasts"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּכוֹר אֲשֶׁר־יְבֻכַּר לַיהוָה, noun and verb of one root: "a firstborn that is made firstborn to Yahweh". **"ox, or sheep"** — Hebrew שׁוֹר, שֶׂה, one head of cattle, one of the flock.
- **"redeem it according to thine estimation" → "ransom it by your valuation"** (v27) — Hebrew וּפָדָה; the next clause has יִגָּאֵל "is redeemed".
- **"no devoted thing, that a man shall devote" → "every devoted thing that a man devotes"** (v28, 29) — Hebrew חֵרֶם, הֶחֱרִים: what is given over to Yahweh beyond recall. Elsewhere the verb is "devote to destruction" (Exod 22:20); here a field and a beast are devoted as well, and "to destruction" is not put into the verse. In v29 the devoted one is a man, and the verse itself says what follows. "Most holy" is literally "holiness of holinesses".
- **"None devoted, which shall be devoted of men, shall be redeemed" → "Every devoted thing that is devoted from among men shall not be ransomed"** (v29) — Hebrew לֹא יִפָּדֶה, the verb "ransom", not "redeem". "Shall surely be put to death" is literally "dying he shall be made to die".
- **"tithe"** (v30, 31, 32) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂר "a tenth". **"whether of the seed of the land, or of the fruit of the tree"** — the Hebrew has "from … from" with no "whether … or".
- **"whatsoever passeth under the rod" → "all that passes over under the rod"** (v32) — Hebrew יַעֲבֹר תַּחַת הַשָּׁבֶט: the animals are counted as they pass under the shepherd's rod.
- **"He shall not search"** (v33) — Hebrew בִּקֵּר "look into, inspect", as 13:36.
- **"for the children of Israel" → "for the sons of Israel"** (v34) — Hebrew אֶל "to, for".

### Notes

- **Place of the chapter** — vows, sanctified things and tithes, after the blessing and curse of ch. 26; both end "on Mount Sinai" (26:46; 27:34).
- **The valuation of souls** (v2–8) — a man vows the value of a person to Yahweh; the sums are fixed by age and sex, and lowered for the poor.
- **Field and jubilee** (v16–24) — the valuation follows the years to the jubilee of ch. 25; a bought field returns to its seller.
- **The devoted thing** (v28–29) — חֵרֶם is given beyond recall: it is not sold, redeemed or ransomed (Num 18:14; Josh 6:17–19; 7).
- **The tithe** (v30–33) — of seed and fruit, redeemable with a fifth added; of herd and flock, every tenth animal, not to be exchanged.
