# Leviticus 5

[1] "And when a soul sins, and hears the voice of a curse, and he is a witness, whether he has seen or has known, if he does not tell, then he shall bear his iniquity.

[2] Or a soul that touches any unclean thing, or the carcass of an unclean living thing, or the carcass of an unclean beast, or the carcass of an unclean swarming thing, and it is hidden from him, and he is unclean, and is guilty;

[3] or when he touches the uncleanness of a man, as to any uncleanness of his by which he becomes unclean, and it is hidden from him, and he knows, and is guilty;

[4] or when a soul swears, uttering rashly with the lips, to do evil or to do good, as to anything that the man utters rashly with an oath, and it is hidden from him, and he knows, and is guilty in one of these:

[5] and it shall be, when he is guilty in one of these, that he shall confess that in which he has sinned.

[6] And he shall bring his guilt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for his sin that he has sinned: a female from the flock, a ewe lamb or a female goat, for a sin offering; and the priest shall make atonement for him from his sin.

[7] And if his hand does not reach enough for a sheep, then he shall bring as his guilt offering, for what he has sinned, two turtledoves or two sons of a dove to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, one for a sin offering and one for a burnt offering.

[8] And he shall bring them to the priest, and he shall bring near that which is for the sin offering first; and he shall nip its head opposite its neck, and shall not separate it.

[9] And he shall sprinkle of the blood of the sin offering on the wall of the altar, and what is left of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar; it is a sin offering.

[10] And the second he shall offer as a burnt offering according to the ordinance; and the priest shall make atonement for him from his sin that he has sinned, and it shall be pardoned him.

[11] And if his hand does not attain to two turtledoves or to two sons of a dove, then he shall bring as his offering, for what he has sinned, a tenth of the ephah of fine flour for a sin offering; he shall not put oil on it and shall not place frankincense on it, for it is a sin offering.

[12] And he shall bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take from it his full handful, its memorial portion, and turn it into smoke on the altar, upon the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it is a sin offering.

[13] And the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin that he has sinned in one of these, and it shall be pardoned him; and it shall be the priest's, like the grain offering."

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[15] "When a soul commits unfaithfulness and sins in error in the holy things of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, then he shall bring his guilt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: a ram without blemish from the flock, by your valuation in shekels of silver, by the holy shekel, for a guilt offering.

[16] And what he has sinned from the holy thing he shall repay, and its fifth he shall add to it and give it to the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him with the ram of the guilt offering, and it shall be pardoned him.

[17] And if a soul sins and does one of all the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that are not to be done, and he did not know, and is guilty, then he shall bear his iniquity.

[18] And he shall bring a ram without blemish from the flock, by your valuation, for a guilt offering, to the priest; and the priest shall make atonement for him for his error in which he erred, and he did not know, and it shall be pardoned him.

[19] It is a guilt offering; he has surely been guilty toward ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. Verses 1–4 are four cases joined by "or", and v5 takes them up.
- **"if a soul sin"** (v1, 2, 4, 15, 17) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ "soul", here a person; the verb is feminine with "soul", and the Hebrew goes on with "he", "him".
- **"hear the voice of swearing" → "hears the voice of a curse"** (v1) — Hebrew קוֹל אָלָה: אָלָה is a curse, and an oath that calls down a curse. What is meant is a curse spoken aloud on anyone who knows of a matter and does not come forward. **"whether he hath seen or known of it; if he do not utter it" → "whether he has seen or has known, if he does not tell"** — Hebrew הִגִּיד "tell"; the verbs have no object. **"his iniquity"** — Hebrew עָוֺן, the wrong and what it brings on him.
- **"a carcase of an unclean beast, or a carcase of unclean cattle, or the carcase of unclean creeping things" → "the carcass of an unclean living thing, or the carcass of an unclean beast, or the carcass of an unclean swarming thing"** (v2) — Hebrew חַיָּה "living thing", בְּהֵמָה "beast", שֶׁרֶץ "swarming thing"; נְבֵלָה is the dead body of an animal.
- **"if it be hidden from him; he also shall be unclean, and guilty" → "and it is hidden from him, and he is unclean, and is guilty"** (v2, 3, 4) — the Hebrew sets the clauses side by side with "and"; where the "if" ends is not marked. אָשֵׁם "be guilty" is the verb of אָשָׁם "guilt"; it is also read "incur guilt", "feel guilt" or "realize his guilt". **"when he knoweth of it" → "and he knows"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew וְהוּא יָדַע, literally "and he knew": commonly taken as "and he comes to know it".
- **"the uncleanness of man, whatsoever uncleanness it be that a man shall be defiled withal" → "the uncleanness of a man, as to any uncleanness of his by which he becomes unclean"** (v3) — Hebrew לְכֹל טֻמְאָתוֹ אֲשֶׁר יִטְמָא בָּהּ.
- **"pronouncing with his lips" → "uttering rashly with the lips"** (v4) — Hebrew בִּטֵּא, to speak out without thought (Ps 106:33); how much "rashly" lies in the word is not certain. **"whatsoever it be that a man shall pronounce" → "anything that the man utters rashly"** — Hebrew הָאָדָם, with the article.
- **"he shall confess that he hath sinned in that thing" → "he shall confess that in which he has sinned"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר חָטָא עָלֶיהָ.
- **"his trespass offering" → "his guilt offering"** (v6, 7, 15, 16, 18, 19) — Hebrew אָשָׁם, the one word "guilt". In v6 and v7 it is said of what is brought as a sin offering: what he brings for his guilt. Some render it there "his penalty".
- **"for his sin which he hath sinned … for a sin offering"** (v6, 10, 13) — "sin" and "sin offering" are one Hebrew word, חַטָּאת; "that he has sinned" is its verb. **"concerning his sin" → "from his sin"** (v6, 10) — Hebrew מֵחַטָּאתוֹ; in v13 it is עַל "for".
- **"a lamb or a kid of the goats" → "a ewe lamb or a female goat"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּשְׂבָּה and שְׂעִירַת עִזִּים, both feminine; the words do not say "kid".
- **"if he be not able to bring a lamb" → "if his hand does not reach enough for a sheep"** (v7); **"if he be not able to bring" → "if his hand does not attain to"** (v11) — Hebrew לֹא תַגִּיעַ יָדוֹ דֵּי שֶׂה and לֹא תַשִּׂיג יָדוֹ, two verbs: he cannot afford it. שֶׂה is one animal of the flock, sheep or goat.
- **"two young pigeons" → "two sons of a dove"** (v7, 11) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־יוֹנָה, literally so; see 1:14. Whether "son" means a young bird is not certain.
- **"offer" → "bring near"** (v8) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב; the subject is not named. **"wring off his head from his neck, but shall not divide it asunder" → "nip its head opposite its neck, and shall not separate it"** — Hebrew מָלַק, found only here and in 1:15; מִמּוּל עָרְפּוֹ, "over against the back of its neck".
- **"the rest of the blood shall be wrung out at the bottom of the altar" → "what is left of the blood shall be drained out at the base of the altar"** (v9) — Hebrew הַנִּשְׁאָר, יִמָּצֵה, אֶל־יְסוֹד.
- **"offer the second for a burnt offering, according to the manner" → "the second he shall offer as a burnt offering according to the ordinance"** (v10) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "do, make", the usual verb for preparing a sacrifice; כַּמִּשְׁפָּט, "according to the judgment": the rule of 1:14–17.
- **"it shall be forgiven him" → "it shall be pardoned him"** (v10, 13, 16, 18) — Hebrew וְנִסְלַח לוֹ; see 4:20.
- **"the tenth part of an ephah" → "a tenth of the ephah"** (v11) — Hebrew עֲשִׂירִת הָאֵפָה. **"put … put" → "put … place"** — Hebrew שִׂים and נָתַן.
- **"his handful of it, even a memorial thereof, and burn it" → "his full handful, its memorial portion, and turn it into smoke"** (v12) — see 2:2. **"according to the offerings made by fire" → "upon the offerings by fire"** — see 4:35.
- **"the remnant shall be the priest's, as a meat offering" → "it shall be the priest's, like the grain offering"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהָיְתָה לַכֹּהֵן כַּמִּנְחָה; "the remnant" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"commit a trespass, and sin through ignorance, in the holy things" → "commits unfaithfulness and sins in error in the holy things"** (v15) — Hebrew תִמְעֹל מַעַל, verb and noun of one root: to act unfaithfully, to break trust. בִּשְׁגָגָה "in error": see 4:2. מִקָּדְשֵׁי יְהוָה is literally "from the holy things of Yahweh": he has taken from what is Yahweh's.
- **"with thy estimation by shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary" → "by your valuation in shekels of silver, by the holy shekel"** (v15, 18) — Hebrew בְּעֶרְכְּךָ: "your" has grown fixed to the word and names no one. שֶׁקֶל הַקֹּדֶשׁ is literally "the shekel of holiness", or "of the Holy", the sanctuary's weight. The shekel is a weight, not a coin.
- **"make amends for the harm that he hath done in the holy thing" → "what he has sinned from the holy thing he shall repay"** (v16) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר חָטָא מִן־הַקֹּדֶשׁ יְשַׁלֵּם: שִׁלֵּם "repay", literally "make whole".
- **"though he wist it not, yet is he guilty, and shall bear his iniquity" → "and he did not know, and is guilty, then he shall bear his iniquity"** (v17) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָדַע וְאָשֵׁם וְנָשָׂא עֲוֺנוֹ, three clauses joined by "and". The conclusion may also begin one clause earlier: "he is guilty and shall bear his iniquity".
- **"concerning his ignorance wherein he erred" → "for his error in which he erred"** (v18) — Hebrew עַל שִׁגְגָתוֹ אֲשֶׁר־שָׁגָג, noun and verb of one root.
- **"he hath certainly trespassed against the LORD" → "he has surely been guilty toward Yahweh"** (v19) — Hebrew אָשֹׁם אָשַׁם לַיהוָה, literally "being guilty he has been guilty to Yahweh"; the verb of "guilt offering" in the same verse.

### Notes

- **Three steps (v6, 7, 11)** — a female of the flock; for one whose hand does not reach so far, two birds; for one whose hand does not attain to these, a tenth of the ephah of fine flour.
- **Guilt offering (v14–19)** — brought where the holy things of Yahweh have been wronged; what was taken is repaid with a fifth added (v16).
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew numbering this chapter runs on to 5:26; its 5:20–26 are 6:1–7 here.
