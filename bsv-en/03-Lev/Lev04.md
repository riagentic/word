# Leviticus 4

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'When a soul sins in error in any of the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that are not to be done, and does one of them:

[3] if the anointed priest sins, to the guilt of the people, then he shall bring near for his sin that he has sinned a bull of the herd without blemish to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for a sin offering.

[4] And he shall bring the bull to the entrance of the tent of meeting before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he shall lay his hand on the head of the bull and slaughter the bull before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] And the anointed priest shall take of the blood of the bull and bring it to the tent of meeting.

[6] And the priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle of the blood seven times before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in front of the holy veil.

[7] And the priest shall place of the blood on the horns of the altar of fragrant incense before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which is in the tent of meeting; and all the blood of the bull he shall pour out at the base of the altar of the burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[8] And all the fat of the bull of the sin offering he shall lift up from it: the fat that covers over the entrails and all the fat that is on the entrails,

[9] and the two kidneys and the fat that is on them, which is on the flanks, and the lobe on the liver; together with the kidneys he shall remove it,

[10] as it is lifted up from the ox of the sacrifice of the peace offerings; and the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar of the burnt offering.

[11] And the skin of the bull and all its flesh, with its head and with its shanks, and its entrails and its dung —

[12] and he shall bring out the whole bull to outside the camp, to a clean place, to the pouring place of the fat ashes, and he shall burn it on wood with fire; at the pouring place of the fat ashes it shall be burned.

[13] And if the whole congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ errs, and a thing is hidden from the eyes of the assembly, and they do one of all the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that are not to be done, and are guilty,

[14] and the sin by which they have sinned becomes known, then the assembly shall bring near a bull of the herd for a sin offering and bring it before the tent of meeting.

[15] And the elders of the congregation shall lay their hands on the head of the bull before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and one shall slaughter the bull before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] And the anointed priest shall bring of the blood of the bull to the tent of meeting.

[17] And the priest shall dip his finger from the blood and sprinkle seven times before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in front of the veil.

[18] And of the blood he shall place on the horns of the altar that is before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which is in the tent of meeting; and all the blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of the burnt offering, which is at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[19] And all its fat he shall lift up from it and turn into smoke on the altar.

[20] And he shall do to the bull as he did to the bull of the sin offering; so he shall do to it. And the priest shall make atonement for them, and it shall be pardoned them.

[21] And he shall bring out the bull to outside the camp and burn it as he burned the first bull; it is the sin offering of the assembly.

[22] When a chieftain sins and does one of all the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that are not to be done, in error, and is guilty,

[23] or his sin in which he has sinned is made known to him, then he shall bring his offering, a male goat, a male without blemish.

[24] And he shall lay his hand on the head of the male goat and slaughter it in the place where one slaughters the burnt offering before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it is a sin offering.

[25] And the priest shall take of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and place it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering; and its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar of the burnt offering.

[26] And all its fat he shall turn into smoke on the altar, like the fat of the sacrifice of the peace offerings; and the priest shall make atonement for him from his sin, and it shall be pardoned him.

[27] And if one soul from the people of the land sins in error, in doing one of the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that are not to be done, and is guilty,

[28] or his sin that he has sinned is made known to him, then he shall bring his offering, a female goat without blemish, a female, for his sin that he has sinned.

[29] And he shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter the sin offering in the place of the burnt offering.

[30] And the priest shall take of its blood with his finger and place it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering; and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.

[31] And all its fat he shall remove, as the fat was removed from upon the sacrifice of the peace offerings; and the priest shall turn it into smoke on the altar for a soothing smell to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and the priest shall make atonement for him, and it shall be pardoned him.

[32] And if he brings a lamb as his offering for a sin offering, a female without blemish he shall bring it.

[33] And he shall lay his hand on the head of the sin offering and slaughter it for a sin offering in the place where one slaughters the burnt offering.

[34] And the priest shall take of the blood of the sin offering with his finger and place it on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering; and all its blood he shall pour out at the base of the altar.

[35] And all its fat he shall remove, as the fat of the lamb is removed from the sacrifice of the peace offerings; and the priest shall turn them into smoke on the altar, upon the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and the priest shall make atonement for him for his sin that he has sinned, and it shall be pardoned him.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v22) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"If a soul shall sin through ignorance against any of the commandments" → "When a soul sins in error in any of the commandments"** (v2, 22, 27) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ "soul", here a person. בִּשְׁגָגָה is "in error": the noun of שָׁגָה / שָׁגַג "err, go astray" (v13), a wrong done by mistake; "through ignorance" and "unintentionally" say more than the word. מִכֹּל מִצְוֺת is literally "from all the commandments".
- **"concerning things which ought not to be done" → "that are not to be done"** (v2, 13, 22, 27) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא תֵעָשֶׂינָה, said of the commandments themselves: the things that Yahweh has commanded not to do.
- **"the priest that is anointed" → "the anointed priest"** (v3, 5, 16) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַמָּשִׁיחַ; מָשִׁיחַ is the adjective of מָשַׁח "anoint".
- **"according to the sin of the people" → "to the guilt of the people"** (v3) — Hebrew לְאַשְׁמַת הָעָם: so that guilt comes on the people.
- **"let him bring for his sin, which he hath sinned … for a sin offering"** (v3, 14, 23, 28, 35) — "sin" and "sin offering" are one Hebrew word, חַטָּאת; "that he has sinned" is its verb, חָטָא. English must use two renderings; the Hebrew says "for his sin … for a sin".
- **"bring" → "bring near"** (v3, 14) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב, the root of קָרְבָּן "offering" (v23, 28, 32); "bring" (v4, 5, 14, 16, 23, 28, 32) is הֵבִיא.
- **"a young bullock" → "a bull of the herd"** (v3, 14) — Hebrew פַּר בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "a bull, a son of the herd"; the words do not say "young". **"without blemish"** — Hebrew תָּמִים, "whole, complete".
- **"kill" → "slaughter"** (v4, 15, 24, 29, 33) — Hebrew שָׁחַט. In v15 the verb is singular with no subject named: "one shall slaughter". So "where one slaughters" (v24, 33).
- **"before the vail of the sanctuary" → "in front of the holy veil"** (v6) — Hebrew אֶת־פְּנֵי פָּרֹכֶת הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the face of the veil of holiness"; also "the veil of the Holy", the room before the veil (Exod 26:33).
- **"put some of the blood" → "place of the blood"** (v7, 18, 25, 30, 34) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "give"; מִן־הַדָּם "from the blood". **"the altar of sweet incense" → "the altar of fragrant incense"** — Hebrew קְטֹרֶת הַסַּמִּים.
- **"pour all the blood of the bullock at the bottom of the altar" → "all the blood of the bull he shall pour out at the base of the altar"** (v7, 18, 25, 30, 34) — Hebrew אֶל־יְסוֹד, "to the foundation". The Hebrew says "all the blood"; "the rest of" is not there.
- **"take off from it all the fat" → "all the fat … he shall lift up from it"** (v8, 10, 19) — Hebrew הֵרִים, the verb of תְּרוּמָה "contribution"; "remove" (v9, 31, 35) is הֵסִיר. **"covers over the entrails"** (v8) — Hebrew עַל־הַקֶּרֶב here, where 3:3 has אֶת.
- **"the caul above the liver, with the kidneys" → "the lobe on the liver; together with the kidneys"** (v9) — see 3:4.
- **"the bullock of the sacrifice of peace offerings" → "the ox of the sacrifice of the peace offerings"** (v10) — Hebrew שׁוֹר, one head of cattle; not the פַּר "bull" of the rest of the chapter.
- **"burn" → "turn into smoke"** (v10, 19, 26, 31, 35); **"burn" kept** (v12, 21) — two Hebrew verbs. On the altar it is הִקְטִיר, literally "make smoke"; outside the camp it is שָׂרַף "burn".
- **"his legs, and his inwards" → "its shanks, and its entrails"** (v11) — Hebrew כְּרָעַיִם, קֶרֶב. The verse has no verb; it names what v12 calls "the whole bull".
- **"where the ashes are poured out" → "to the pouring place of the fat ashes"** (v12) — Hebrew אֶל־שֶׁפֶךְ הַדֶּשֶׁן, literally "to the pouring-out of the fat ashes".
- **"if the whole congregation of Israel sin through ignorance" → "if the whole congregation of Israel errs"** (v13) — Hebrew יִשְׁגּוּ, the verb of שְׁגָגָה. **"congregation … assembly"** (v13, 14, 15, 21) — two Hebrew words, עֵדָה and קָהָל, kept apart; in v14 and v21 it is "the assembly" (KJV "congregation").
- **"and are guilty"** (v13, 22, 27) — Hebrew אָשֵׁם, the verb of אָשָׁם "guilt". It is also read "incur guilt", "feel guilt" or "realize their guilt".
- **"When the sin, which they have sinned against it, is known" → "and the sin by which they have sinned becomes known"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר חָטְאוּ עָלֶיהָ, literally "which they sinned upon it"; in v23 it is בָּהּ "in it".
- **"dip his finger in some of the blood" → "dip his finger from the blood"** (v17) — Hebrew מִן־הַדָּם, where v6 has בַּדָּם "in the blood".
- **"it shall be forgiven them" → "it shall be pardoned them"** (v20, 26, 31, 35) — Hebrew וְנִסְלַח, a passive with no subject named; סָלַח is said only of God. "Forgive" is kept for another verb, נָשָׂא.
- **"a sin offering for the congregation" → "the sin offering of the assembly"** (v21) — Hebrew חַטַּאת הַקָּהָל.
- **"When a ruler hath sinned" → "When a chieftain sins"** (v22) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר נָשִׂיא יֶחֱטָא: the verse begins with אֲשֶׁר, here "when"; נָשִׂיא is literally "one lifted up".
- **"Or if his sin … come to his knowledge" → "or his sin … is made known to him"** (v23, 28) — Hebrew אוֹ־הוֹדַע אֵלָיו: "or", set beside "and is guilty".
- **"a kid of the goats, a male" → "a male goat, a male"** (v23); **"a kid of the goats, a female" → "a female goat …, a female"** (v28) — Hebrew שְׂעִיר עִזִּים, שְׂעִירַת עִזִּים, literally "a hairy one of the goats"; the words do not say "kid". **"the common people" → "the people of the land"** (v27) — Hebrew עַם הָאָרֶץ.
- **"make an atonement for him as concerning his sin" → "make atonement for him from his sin"** (v26) — Hebrew מֵחַטָּאתוֹ; in v35 it is עַל "for". כִּפֶּר is literally "cover" (or "wipe off").
- **"for a sweet savour" → "for a soothing smell"** (v31) — Hebrew לְרֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ; only here of a sin offering.
- **"according to the offerings made by fire unto the LORD" → "upon the offerings by fire of Yahweh"** (v35) — Hebrew עַל אִשֵּׁי יְהוָה: upon them on the altar, or "in addition to", "after the manner of". The derivation of אִשֶּׁה from אֵשׁ "fire" is not certain.

### Notes

- **Four cases** — the anointed priest (v3–12, a bull), the whole congregation (v13–21, a bull), a chieftain (v22–26, a male goat), one of the people of the land (v27–35, a female goat or a female lamb).
- **Where the blood goes** — for the priest and for the congregation it is brought into the tent, sprinkled in front of the veil and placed on the horns of the altar of incense; for the chieftain and for one of the people it is placed on the horns of the altar of the burnt offering.
- **Outside the camp (v12, 21)** — the bull whose blood was brought into the tent is burned outside the camp. Heb 13:11–13 takes this up.
