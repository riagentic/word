# Leviticus 21

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Say to the priests, the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and you shall say to them: 'None shall defile himself for a soul among his peoples,

[2] except for his near kin that is near to him: for his mother and for his father and for his son and for his daughter and for his brother,

[3] and for his sister, a virgin, who is near to him, who has not been a man's: for her he may defile himself.

[4] He shall not defile himself as a husband among his peoples, to profane himself.

[5] They shall not make a baldness on their head, and the edge of their beard they shall not shave, and in their flesh they shall not cut a cut.

[6] Holy they shall be to their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they shall not profane the name of their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; for the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the bread of their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, they bring near, and they shall be holy.

[7] A woman who is a prostitute and profaned they shall not take, and a woman divorced from her husband they shall not take; for he is holy to his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] And you shall sanctify him, for the bread of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ he brings near; he shall be holy to you, for I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who sanctifies you, am holy.

[9] And the daughter of a man who is a priest, when she profanes herself by playing the prostitute: her father she profanes; with fire she shall be burned.

[10] And the priest who is great among his brothers, on whose head the oil of anointing is poured and whose hand is filled to put on the garments: his head he shall not let go loose, and his garments he shall not rip.

[11] And to any soul of a dead one he shall not come; for his father and for his mother he shall not defile himself.

[12] And from the sanctuary he shall not go out, and he shall not profane the sanctuary of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; for the crown of the oil of anointing of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is upon him. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And he, a woman in her virginity he shall take.

[14] A widow, and a divorced woman, and a profaned woman, a prostitute: these he shall not take; but a virgin from his peoples he shall take as wife.

[15] And he shall not profane his seed among his peoples, for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies him.'"

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[17] "Speak to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, saying: 'A man of your seed, for their generations, in whom there is a blemish, shall not come near to bring near the bread of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[18] For any man in whom there is a blemish shall not come near: a blind man, or a lame, or one with a mutilated face, or one with a limb too long,

[19] or a man in whom there is a fracture of the foot or a fracture of the hand,

[20] or a hunchback, or one who is thin, or one with a blur in his eye, or itch, or eruption, or a crushed testicle.

[21] Any man in whom there is a blemish, of the seed of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest, shall not draw near to bring near the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; a blemish is in him; the bread of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ he shall not draw near to bring near.

[22] The bread of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, of the most holy things and of the holy things, he may eat.

[23] Only to the veil he shall not come, and to the altar he shall not draw near, for a blemish is in him; and he shall not profane my sanctuaries, for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies them.'"

[24] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ spoke to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons and to all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v24) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"Speak unto the priests … and say unto them" → "Say to the priests … and you shall say to them"** (v1) — the Hebrew has the verb אָמַר "say" both times (אֱמֹר … וְאָמַרְתָּ), not דִּבֵּר "speak" (v17).
- **"There shall none be defiled for the dead among his people" → "None shall defile himself for a soul among his peoples"** (v1) — Hebrew לְנֶפֶשׁ לֹא־יִטַּמָּא בְּעַמָּיו, literally "for a soul he shall not make himself unclean among his peoples". נֶפֶשׁ "soul" here is a dead person (so v11; 19:28; 22:4; Num 5:2; 6:6); the verb is a reflexive, singular, with no subject named. עַמָּיו is plural: his kinsfolk (v4, 14, 15).
- **"his kin, that is near unto him" → "his near kin that is near to him"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׁאֵרוֹ הַקָּרֹב אֵלָיו; שְׁאֵר is "flesh", one's own flesh and blood (18:6); "near" is in the English of both words, but the Hebrew has two different words. The mother stands before the father. The wife is not named.
- **"which hath had no husband" → "who has not been a man's"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא־הָיְתָה לְאִישׁ, literally "who has not been to a man". **"for her may he be defiled" → "for her he may defile himself"** — the reflexive verb of v1.
- **"But he shall not defile himself, being a chief man among his people" → "He shall not defile himself as a husband among his peoples"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא יִטַּמָּא בַּעַל בְּעַמָּיו לְהֵחַלּוֹ, literally "he shall not make himself unclean, a *baal* among his peoples, to profane himself". בַּעַל is "owner, lord, husband". The verse is not understood with certainty: "as a husband" (for his wife, or for her kin), or "as a chief man among his peoples" (so the KJV). "As" is supplied; "but" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"They shall not make baldness upon their head" → "They shall not make a baldness on their head"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא־יִקְרְחוּ קָרְחָה, verb and noun of one root ("bald a baldness"): a place shaved bare in mourning. The written text has the verb in another form (יקרחה); the reading tradition, a plural, is followed; the sense is the same. **"the corner of their beard" → "the edge of their beard"** — Hebrew פֵּאָה, as 19:27. **"nor make any cuttings in their flesh" → "in their flesh they shall not cut a cut"** — Hebrew לֹא יִשְׂרְטוּ שָׂרָטֶת, verb and noun of one root (19:28).
- **"the offerings of the LORD made by fire, and the bread of their God, they do offer" → "the offerings by fire of Yahweh, the bread of their Elohim, they bring near"** (v6) — Hebrew אִשֵּׁי יְהוָה לֶחֶם אֱלֹהֵיהֶם, with no "and": the second names the first. אִשֶּׁה is commonly taken from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain. הִקְרִיב is "bring near" (v8, 17, 21), the causative of קָרַב "come near" (v17, 18). לֶחֶם is "bread", here for all that is laid on the altar. **"therefore they shall be holy" → "and they shall be holy"** — Hebrew וְהָיוּ קֹדֶשׁ, literally "and they shall be holiness".
- **"a wife that is a whore, or profane … put away from her husband" → "a woman who is a prostitute and profaned … divorced from her husband"** (v7) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה זֹנָה וַחֲלָלָה: אִשָּׁה is "woman" and "wife"; חֲלָלָה, from חִלֵּל "profane", is a woman who has been profaned (how is not said: by prostitution, or by rape, or by her birth). גְּרוּשָׁה is literally "driven out", the word for a divorced woman. "He is holy": the Hebrew passes from "they" to "he".
- **"Thou shalt sanctify him therefore" → "And you shall sanctify him"** (v8) — Hebrew וְקִדַּשְׁתּוֹ, singular: Israel is addressed; "therefore" is not in the Hebrew. "Who sanctifies you" is plural.
- **"the daughter of any priest, if she profane herself by playing the whore" → "the daughter of a man who is a priest, when she profanes herself by playing the prostitute"** (v9) — Hebrew בַת אִישׁ כֹּהֵן, literally "the daughter of a man, a priest"; כִּי תֵחֵל לִזְנוֹת, literally "when she is profaned to play the prostitute" (it can also be read "when she begins to play the prostitute").
- **"he that is the high priest among his brethren" → "the priest who is great among his brothers"** (v10) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל מֵאֶחָיו, literally "the priest, the great one from his brothers": "the great priest" is the Hebrew for the high priest; with "from his brothers" it is also "greater than his brothers". **"the anointing oil" → "the oil of anointing"** — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה. **"that is consecrated to put on the garments" → "whose hand is filled to put on the garments"** — Hebrew וּמִלֵּא אֶת־יָדוֹ, literally "and one has filled his hand": the Hebrew way of saying that a priest is installed (8:33; Exod 28:41).
- **"shall not uncover his head, nor rend his clothes" → "his head he shall not let go loose, and his garments he shall not rip"** (v10) — Hebrew פָּרַע, to let loose: the hair unbound and untended, or the head bared (10:6; 13:45); פָּרַם "rip" is another verb than the usual "tear" (קָרַע). Both are signs of mourning.
- **"Neither shall he go in to any dead body" → "And to any soul of a dead one he shall not come"** (v11) — Hebrew וְעַל כָּל־נַפְשֹׁת מֵת לֹא יָבֹא, literally "and upon all souls of a dead one he shall not come": נַפְשֹׁת is a plural.
- **"the crown of the anointing oil of his God" → "the crown of the oil of anointing of his Elohim"** (v12) — Hebrew נֵזֶר שֶׁמֶן מִשְׁחַת אֱלֹהָיו. נֵזֶר is the mark of one who is set apart (the root of "Nazirite"; 22:2): the "crown" of the high priest (8:9; Exod 29:6), or here his "consecration" by the oil.
- **"a wife in her virginity" → "a woman in her virginity"** (v13) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה. **"a virgin of his own people" → "a virgin from his peoples"** (v14) — Hebrew מֵעַמָּיו, plural. **"or an harlot" → "a profaned woman, a prostitute"** — Hebrew וַחֲלָלָה זֹנָה, with no "and" between the two: perhaps "one profaned by prostitution".
- **"Neither shall he profane his seed" → "And he shall not profane his seed"** (v15) — Hebrew זַרְעוֹ, his children.
- **"Whosoever he be of thy seed in their generations that hath any blemish, let him not approach to offer" → "A man of your seed, for their generations, in whom there is a blemish, shall not come near to bring near"** (v17) — Hebrew לֹא יִקְרַב לְהַקְרִיב, one verb in two forms: "come near" and "bring near". מוּם is a bodily defect.
- **"he that hath a flat nose, or any thing superfluous" → "one with a mutilated face, or one with a limb too long"** (v18) — Hebrew חָרֻם and שָׂרוּעַ. Both are uncertain. חָרֻם is found only here: "slit, mutilated" (in the nose or the face). שָׂרוּעַ (22:23) is "stretched out": with a limb too long, or with a limb too many.
- **"brokenfooted, or brokenhanded" → "in whom there is a fracture of the foot or a fracture of the hand"** (v19) — Hebrew שֶׁבֶר רָגֶל … שֶׁבֶר יָד, literally "a break of foot … a break of hand" (the word of 24:20).
- **"crookbackt, or a dwarf, or that hath a blemish in his eye, or be scurvy, or scabbed, or hath his stones broken" → "a hunchback, or one who is thin, or one with a blur in his eye, or itch, or eruption, or a crushed testicle"** (v20) — six words, most of them uncertain. גִּבֵּן is found only here ("hump-backed" is the usual sense). דַּק is "thin" (Gen 41:3): wasted, or small ("a dwarf"). תְּבַלֻּל is found only here, from בָּלַל "mix": a spot or blur in the eye. גָּרָב and יַלֶּפֶת (22:22) are two skin diseases whose kind is not known. מְרוֹחַ אָשֶׁךְ: both words are found only here; "crushed" or "swollen" in the testicle.
- **"come nigh to offer" → "draw near to bring near"** (v21) — Hebrew נָגַשׁ "draw near", another verb than קָרַב "come near" (v17, 18); so also v23.
- **"both of the most holy, and of the holy" → "of the most holy things and of the holy things"** (v22) — Hebrew מִקָּדְשֵׁי הַקֳּדָשִׁים וּמִן־הַקֳּדָשִׁים, literally "from the holinesses of the holinesses and from the holinesses". "He may eat" is the plain Hebrew future "he shall eat".
- **"Only he shall not go in unto the vail, nor come nigh unto the altar" → "Only to the veil he shall not come, and to the altar he shall not draw near"** (v23) — Hebrew אַךְ "only". **"that he profane not my sanctuaries" → "and he shall not profane my sanctuaries"** — Hebrew וְלֹא יְחַלֵּל; מִקְדָּשַׁי is plural: the holy places, or the holy things.
- **"Moses told it unto Aaron" → "Moshe spoke to Aharon"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיְדַבֵּר; "it" is not in the Hebrew.

### Notes
- **Priestly purity** — priests hold a higher standard: may defile themselves only for closest kin, not all relations. High priest cannot defile himself for the dead at all — even for parents (v11).
- **Why the blemish restriction?** (v17–23) — the priest's body is representative; physical integrity mirrors the integrity of the sacrificial animal (21:21 parallels 22:21 on blemished sacrifices). No exclusion from eating priestly food; only from officiating.
- **Moral inclusion, ritual exclusion** — blemished descendants of Aaron remain members of the priestly family, fully sustained; only the altar service is barred. The Hebrew vision is not disability-stigma but ritual representation.
- **Jesus as perfect high priest** (Heb 7:26) — "holy, innocent, unstained" — the NT reads Lev 21 as foreshadowing Christ's unblemished priesthood.
