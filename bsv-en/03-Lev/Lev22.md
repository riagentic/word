# Leviticus 22

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons, that they keep themselves apart from the holy things of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they shall not profane my holy name, which they sanctify to me. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] Say to them: 'For your generations, any man of all your seed who comes near to the holy things that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sanctify to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, with his uncleanness upon him: that soul shall be cut off from before me. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] Any man of the seed of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ who is leprous or who flows: of the holy things he shall not eat until he is clean. And he who touches anyone unclean by a soul, or a man from whom an emission of seed goes out,

[5] or a man who touches any swarming thing by which he becomes unclean, or a man by whom he becomes unclean, whatever his uncleanness:

[6] the soul that touches it shall be unclean until the evening, and he shall not eat of the holy things unless he has washed his flesh in water.

[7] And the sun shall set, and he shall be clean; and afterward he may eat of the holy things, for it is his bread.

[8] A carcass and a torn animal he shall not eat, to become unclean by it. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] And they shall keep my charge, and they shall not bear sin because of it and die by it when they profane it. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies them.

[10] And no stranger shall eat a holy thing; a settler of a priest and a hired man shall not eat a holy thing.

[11] And a priest, when he buys a soul, the property of his money: he may eat of it; and those born in his house, they may eat of his bread.

[12] And a priest's daughter, when she becomes a strange man's: she shall not eat of the contribution of the holy things.

[13] And a priest's daughter, when she is a widow or divorced, and she has no seed, and she returns to her father's house as in her youth: of her father's bread she may eat; and no stranger shall eat of it.

[14] And a man, when he eats a holy thing in error, shall add its fifth to it and give the holy thing to the priest.

[15] And they shall not profane the holy things of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which they lift up to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[16] and make them bear the iniquity of guilt when they eat their holy things; for I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies them.'"

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[18] "Speak to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and to his sons and to all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and say to them: 'Any man of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and of the sojourner in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who brings near his offering, for any of their vows and for any of their freewill offerings that they bring near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ as a burnt offering:

[19] that you may be found pleasing, it shall be without blemish, a male, of the herd, of the lambs or of the goats.

[20] Anything in which there is a blemish you shall not bring near, for it shall not be found pleasing for you.

[21] And a man, when he brings near a sacrifice of peace offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to make a special vow or as a freewill offering, of the herd or of the flock: it shall be without blemish, to be found pleasing; no blemish shall be in it.

[22] Blind, or broken, or maimed, or with a wart, or itch, or eruption: these you shall not bring near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and an offering by fire you shall not give of them on the altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[23] And an ox or a sheep with a limb too long or stunted: a freewill offering you may make it, but for a vow it shall not be found pleasing.

[24] And what is bruised, or crushed, or torn off, or cut you shall not bring near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and in your land you shall not do it.

[25] And from the hand of a foreigner you shall not bring near the bread of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from any of these, for their ruin is in them, a blemish is in them; they shall not be found pleasing for you.'"

[26] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[27] "An ox or a lamb or a goat, when it is born, shall be seven days under its mother; and from the eighth day and onward it shall be found pleasing as an offering of an offering by fire to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[28] And an ox or a sheep: it and its young you shall not slaughter on one day.

[29] And when you sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that you may be found pleasing you shall sacrifice it.

[30] On that day it shall be eaten; you shall not leave over any of it until morning. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] And you shall keep my commandments and do them. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[32] And you shall not profane my holy name, and I will be sanctified in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies you,

[33] who brought you out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ "and" at the head of a clause is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"that they separate themselves from the holy things" → "that they keep themselves apart from the holy things"** (v2) — Hebrew וְיִנָּזְרוּ, the verb of נֵזֶר (21:12) and of "Nazirite": to hold oneself back, to treat with reserve. **"and that they profane not my holy name in those things which they hallow unto me" → "and they shall not profane my holy name, which they sanctify to me"** — the Hebrew order is kept: the clause אֲשֶׁר הֵם מַקְדִּשִׁים לִי "which they sanctify to me" stands after "my holy name" and belongs in sense to "the holy things of the sons of Israel"; "in those things" is not in the Hebrew. הִקְדִּישׁ is "sanctify" (v3), of what is given over to Yahweh.
- **"that goeth unto the holy things" → "who comes near to the holy things"** (v3) — Hebrew יִקְרַב. **"having his uncleanness upon him" → "with his uncleanness upon him"** — literal. **"from my presence" → "from before me"** — Hebrew מִלְּפָנַי. "That soul" is the person.
- **"a leper, or hath a running issue" → "who is leprous or who flows"** (v4) — Hebrew צָרוּעַ, one struck with צָרַעַת (ch 13–14: a scaly outbreak that makes unclean, not the leprosy of modern medicine), and זָב, "one who flows" (ch 15).
- **"whoso toucheth any thing that is unclean by the dead" → "he who touches anyone unclean by a soul"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּכָל־טְמֵא־נֶפֶשׁ, literally "any unclean one of a soul": a person (or thing) made unclean by a dead body; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" stands for the dead person, as in 21:1, 11. **"whose seed goeth from him" → "from whom an emission of seed goes out"** — Hebrew שִׁכְבַת־זָרַע, literally "a lying of seed" (15:16).
- **"any creeping thing, whereby he may be made unclean" → "any swarming thing by which he becomes unclean"** (v5) — Hebrew שֶׁרֶץ (11:29–31). **"whatsoever uncleanness he hath" → "whatever his uncleanness"** — Hebrew לְכֹל טֻמְאָתוֹ, literally "as to all his uncleanness". "A man" in "a man by whom" is אָדָם.
- **"The soul which hath touched any such" → "the soul that touches it"** (v6) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ אֲשֶׁר תִּגַּע־בּוֹ. **"unless he wash his flesh with water" → "unless he has washed his flesh in water"** — literal.
- **"when the sun is down" → "And the sun shall set"** (v7) — Hebrew וּבָא הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ, literally "and the sun shall go in". **"because it is his food" → "for it is his bread"** — Hebrew לַחְמוֹ (so v11, 13, 25).
- **"That which dieth of itself, or is torn with beasts" → "A carcass and a torn animal"** (v8) — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, the body of an animal that has died of itself, and טְרֵפָה, "a torn thing" (17:15). **"to defile himself therewith" → "to become unclean by it"**.
- **"They shall therefore keep mine ordinance, lest they bear sin for it, and die therefore, if they profane it" → "And they shall keep my charge, and they shall not bear sin because of it and die by it when they profane it"** (v9) — Hebrew מִשְׁמֶרֶת, what is to be kept (from שָׁמַר "keep"); "lest" and "therefore" are not in the Hebrew. "It" (three times) is masculine in Hebrew and has no clear noun to point to: the holy thing, the bread, or the charge.
- **"There shall no stranger eat of the holy thing: a sojourner of the priest, or an hired servant" → "no stranger shall eat a holy thing; a settler of a priest and a hired man"** (v10) — Hebrew זָר, one who does not belong: here one who is not of a priest's house (not גֵּר "sojourner"); תּוֹשָׁב "settler", one who lives with the priest without belonging to his house; שָׂכִיר "hired man".
- **"if the priest buy any soul with his money" → "a priest, when he buys a soul, the property of his money"** (v11) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ קִנְיַן כַּסְפּוֹ, literally "a soul, the acquisition of his silver": a slave. **"he that is born in his house: they shall eat of his meat" → "those born in his house, they may eat of his bread"** — Hebrew יְלִיד בֵּיתוֹ, a singular for the class, with a plural verb.
- **"If the priest's daughter also be married unto a stranger" → "a priest's daughter, when she becomes a strange man's"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי תִהְיֶה לְאִישׁ זָר, literally "when she is to a strange man" (as 21:3): a man who is not a priest. **"an offering of the holy things" → "the contribution of the holy things"** — Hebrew תְּרוּמָה, what is "lifted off" for Yahweh (v15).
- **"and have no child" → "and she has no seed"** (v13) — Hebrew זֶרַע. **"her father's meat" → "her father's bread"**.
- **"unwittingly" → "in error"** (v14) — Hebrew בִּשְׁגָגָה, by a straying, a mistake (4:2). **"then he shall put the fifth part thereof unto it" → "shall add its fifth to it"** — as 5:16.
- **"which they offer unto the LORD" → "which they lift up to Yahweh"** (v15) — Hebrew יָרִימוּ, the verb of תְּרוּמָה "contribution". Who "they" are (the priests, or the sons of Israel) the verse does not say.
- **"or suffer them to bear the iniquity of trespass" → "and make them bear the iniquity of guilt"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהִשִּׂיאוּ אוֹתָם עֲוֺן אַשְׁמָה, literally "and cause them to bear iniquity of guilt". "Them" may be the people, or (read as "themselves") the priests.
- **"that will offer his oblation" → "who brings near his offering"** (v18) — Hebrew יַקְרִיב קָרְבָּנוֹ, verb and noun of one root: "bring near" and "what is brought near" (so through the chapter). **"of the strangers in Israel" → "of the sojourner in Israel"** — Hebrew הַגֵּר, a singular for the class. The Hebrew passes from "his" to "their".
- **"Ye shall offer at your own will a male without blemish" → "that you may be found pleasing, it shall be without blemish, a male"** (v19) — Hebrew לִרְצֹנְכֶם תָּמִים זָכָר: the verse has no verb; "it shall be" is supplied. לִרְצֹנְכֶם is "for your acceptance": that you may be received with pleasure (19:5); the same root in v20, 21, 23, 25, 27, 29. תָּמִים is "whole, complete". **"of the beeves, of the sheep" → "of the herd, of the lambs"** — Hebrew בָּקָר and כְּשָׂבִים (the sheep kind beside the goats, as 1:10).
- **"to accomplish his vow" → "to make a special vow"** (v21) — Hebrew לְפַלֵּא־נֶדֶר; the verb (of the root "be wonderful, set apart") is used of vows in 27:2; Num 6:2; 15:3, 8; its exact sense is not certain: "make a special vow", or "fulfill a vow". **"it shall be perfect to be accepted" → "it shall be without blemish, to be found pleasing"** — תָּמִים as in v19.
- **"Blind, or broken, or maimed, or having a wen, or scurvy, or scabbed" → "Blind, or broken, or maimed, or with a wart, or itch, or eruption"** (v22) — Hebrew עַוֶּרֶת "blindness"; שָׁבוּר "broken" (with a broken limb); חָרוּץ "cut" (maimed; the exact sense is not certain); יַבֶּלֶת, found only here: "a wart" in Jewish tradition, or "a running sore"; גָּרָב and יַלֶּפֶת, two skin diseases whose kind is not known (21:20).
- **"a bullock or a lamb that hath any thing superfluous or lacking in his parts" → "an ox or a sheep with a limb too long or stunted"** (v23) — Hebrew שׁוֹר וָשֶׂה, one head of cattle and one of the flock; שָׂרוּעַ as in 21:18; קָלוּט is found only here: "drawn in, stunted" (uncertain). **"that mayest thou offer for a freewill offering" → "a freewill offering you may make it"** — Hebrew תַּעֲשֶׂה, "make".
- **"that which is bruised, or crushed, or broken, or cut" → "what is bruised, or crushed, or torn off, or cut"** (v24) — four participles, מָעוּךְ, כָּתוּת, נָתוּק, כָּרוּת; the testicles are meant (so the Jewish tradition and the old versions), but the Hebrew does not name them. **"neither shall ye make any offering thereof in your land" → "and in your land you shall not do it"** — Hebrew וּבְאַרְצְכֶם לֹא תַעֲשׂוּ, literally "and in your land you shall not do": "it" is supplied. Either "you shall not offer such", or "you shall not do this to an animal" (castrate).
- **"Neither from a stranger's hand" → "And from the hand of a foreigner"** (v25) — Hebrew בֶּן־נֵכָר, literally "a son of foreignness". **"because their corruption is in them, and blemishes be in them" → "for their ruin is in them, a blemish is in them"** — Hebrew מָשְׁחָתָם, from שָׁחַת "ruin": their marred state.
- **"a bullock, or a sheep" → "An ox or a lamb"** (v27) — Hebrew שׁוֹר אוֹ־כֶשֶׂב (17:3). **"under the dam" → "under its mother"** — literal. **"it shall be accepted for an offering made by fire" → "it shall be found pleasing as an offering of an offering by fire"** — Hebrew לְקָרְבַּן אִשֶּׁה, two nouns: "an offering" (what is brought near) and "offering by fire" (אִשֶּׁה, commonly taken from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain).
- **"whether it be cow or ewe, ye shall not kill it and her young" → "an ox or a sheep: it and its young you shall not slaughter"** (v28) — Hebrew שׁוֹר אוֹ־שֶׂה אֹתוֹ וְאֶת־בְּנוֹ, literally "an ox or a sheep, it and its son": the words are masculine and cover either sex; the mother is commonly understood. שָׁחַט is "slaughter".
- **"when ye will offer … offer it at your own will" → "when you sacrifice … that you may be found pleasing you shall sacrifice it"** (v29) — as 19:5. תּוֹדָה is "thanksgiving" (7:12).
- **"ye shall leave none of it until the morrow" → "you shall not leave over any of it until morning"** (v30) — Hebrew לֹא־תוֹתִירוּ … עַד־בֹּקֶר.
- **"but I will be hallowed" → "and I will be sanctified"** (v32) — Hebrew וְנִקְדַּשְׁתִּי, the passive (or reflexive: "I will show myself holy") of the verb in "who sanctifies you".
- **"That brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God" → "who brought you out from the land of Egypt to be Elohim to you"** (v33) — Hebrew הַמּוֹצִיא, a participle ("the one bringing you out"); לִהְיוֹת לָכֶם לֵאלֹהִים, literally "to be to you for Elohim".

### Notes
- **Two themes interwoven**: (1) priestly eating of the holy things (v1–16); (2) integrity of offerings (v17–33). The priesthood and the animals must both be unblemished.
- **Priestly household inclusion** (v10–13) — slaves and home-born dependents eat the holy things; daughters married to strangers do not; returned divorced / widowed daughters may resume eating.
- **Eight-day rule** (v27) — newborn animals stay seven days under the mother before being sacrificed, paralleling the eight-day rule for circumcision (Gen 17:12).
- **"It and its young on one day"** (v28) — similar to Deut 22:6 (mother bird and nestlings).
- **Refrain: "I am Yahweh who sanctifies you"** — the chapter's drumbeat; holiness-by-association with Yahweh's own holiness.
