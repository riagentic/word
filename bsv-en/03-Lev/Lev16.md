# Leviticus 16

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ after the death of the two sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, when they came near before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and died.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Speak to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ your brother, and let him not come at any time into the Holy inside the veil, before the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ that is on the ark, and he shall not die; for in the cloud I will appear over the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧.

[3] With this ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall come into the Holy: with a bull of the herd for a sin offering and a ram for a burnt offering.

[4] A holy tunic of linen he shall put on, and breeches of linen shall be on his flesh, and with a sash of linen he shall gird himself, and with a turban of linen he shall wrap his head; they are holy garments; and he shall wash his flesh in water and put them on.

[5] And from the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ he shall take two male goats for a sin offering and one ram for a burnt offering.

[6] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall bring near the bull of the sin offering that is his own, and he shall make atonement on behalf of himself and on behalf of his house.

[7] And he shall take the two male goats and make them stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[8] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall place lots on the two male goats: one lot for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and one lot for Azazel.

[9] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall bring near the male goat on which the lot for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came up, and he shall offer it as a sin offering.

[10] And the male goat on which the lot for Azazel came up shall be made to stand alive before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to make atonement over it, to send it away to Azazel into the wilderness.

[11] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall bring near the bull of the sin offering that is his own, and he shall make atonement on behalf of himself and on behalf of his house; and he shall slaughter the bull of the sin offering that is his own.

[12] And he shall take the firepan full of coals of fire from upon the altar, from before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and his two hands full of fine fragrant incense, and he shall bring them inside the veil.

[13] And he shall place the incense on the fire before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the cloud of the incense shall cover the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ that is over the testimony, and he shall not die.

[14] And he shall take of the blood of the bull and sprinkle with his finger on the face of the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧, eastward; and before the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ he shall sprinkle seven times of the blood with his finger.

[15] And he shall slaughter the male goat of the sin offering that is the people's, and he shall bring its blood inside the veil and do with its blood as he did with the blood of the bull, and he shall sprinkle it on the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ and before the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧.

[16] And he shall make atonement for the Holy, from the uncleannesses of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and from their transgressions, as to all their sins; and so he shall do for the tent of meeting, which abides with them in the midst of their uncleannesses.

[17] And no man shall be in the tent of meeting when he comes in to make atonement in the Holy, until he goes out; and he shall make atonement on behalf of himself and on behalf of his house and on behalf of all the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[18] And he shall go out to the altar that is before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and make atonement for it; and he shall take of the blood of the bull and of the blood of the male goat and place it on the horns of the altar around.

[19] And he shall sprinkle on it of the blood with his finger seven times, and he shall cleanse it and sanctify it from the uncleannesses of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[20] And when he has finished making atonement for the Holy and the tent of meeting and the altar, he shall bring near the live male goat.

[21] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall lay his two hands on the head of the live male goat and confess over it all the iniquities of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and all their transgressions, as to all their sins; and he shall place them on the head of the male goat and send it away by the hand of a man in readiness into the wilderness.

[22] And the male goat shall bear on itself all their iniquities to a land cut off; and he shall send away the male goat in the wilderness.

[23] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall come into the tent of meeting and strip off the garments of linen that he put on when he came into the Holy, and he shall lay them up there.

[24] And he shall wash his flesh in water in a holy place and put on his garments; and he shall go out and offer his burnt offering and the burnt offering of the people, and he shall make atonement on behalf of himself and on behalf of the people.

[25] And the fat of the sin offering he shall turn into smoke on the altar.

[26] And he who sends away the male goat to Azazel shall wash his garments and wash his flesh in water, and afterward he shall come into the camp.

[27] And the bull of the sin offering and the male goat of the sin offering, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the Holy, he shall bring out to outside the camp; and they shall burn with fire their skins and their flesh and their dung.

[28] And he who burns them shall wash his garments and wash his flesh in water, and afterward he shall come into the camp.

[29] And it shall be to you an everlasting statute: in the seventh month, on the tenth of the month, you shall afflict your souls, and no work shall you do, the native and the sojourner who sojourns in your midst.

[30] For on this day he shall make atonement for you, to cleanse you; from all your sins before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ you shall be clean.

[31] It is a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ of ⟦shabbat|shabbaton|Sabbath rest⟧ to you, and you shall afflict your souls: an everlasting statute.

[32] And the priest who is anointed and whose hand is filled to be priest in his father's place shall make atonement; and he shall put on the garments of linen, the holy garments.

[33] And he shall make atonement for the holy sanctuary, and for the tent of meeting and for the altar he shall make atonement, and for the priests and for all the people of the assembly he shall make atonement.

[34] And this shall be to you an everlasting statute, to make atonement for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from all their sins, once in the year." And he did as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when they offered before the LORD" → "when they came near before Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּקָרְבָתָם, "in their coming near"; "offered" (with "strange fire") is the reading of the Septuagint and other versions, cited from memory; the Masoretic Text is translated.
- **"that he come not at all times" → "and let him not come at any time"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאַל־יָבֹא בְכָל־עֵת, a wish form: not at whatever time he chooses. **"into the holy place within the vail" → "into the Holy inside the veil"** (v2, 3, 16, 17, 20, 23, 27) — Hebrew הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "the Holy" ("place" is not in the Hebrew); in this chapter it is the room inside the veil. מִבֵּית לַפָּרֹכֶת is literally "from the house to the veil" (v2, 12, 15). **"mercy seat"** (v2, 13, 14, 15) — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת, the "atonement cover" of the ark (Exod 25:17), of the root of כִּפֶּר. **"that he die not" → "and he shall not die"** (v2, 13) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָמוּת.
- **"Thus shall Aaron come" → "With this Aaron shall come"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּזֹאת. **"a young bullock" → "a bull of the herd"** — Hebrew פַּר בֶּן־בָּקָר, literally "a bull, a son of the herd"; the phrase says nothing of age.
- **"the holy linen coat … linen breeches … a linen girdle … the linen mitre shall he be attired" → "A holy tunic of linen … breeches of linen … a sash of linen … with a turban of linen he shall wrap his head"** (v4) — Hebrew כְּתֹנֶת, מִכְנָסַיִם, אַבְנֵט, מִצְנֶפֶת, as Exod 28; יִצְנֹף is the verb of the noun "turban" ("wind around"). There is no article: "a holy tunic". **"flesh"** (v4, 24, 26, 28) — Hebrew בָּשָׂר. **"wash"** — Hebrew רָחַץ; the washing of garments in v26, 28 is another verb (כִּבֵּס), and English has "wash" for both.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v5, 16, 19, 21, 34) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. **"two kids of the goats" → "two male goats"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׂעִירֵי עִזִּים; in the rest of the chapter the word is שָׂעִיר alone, "male goat" (literally "hairy one"), kept the same each time.
- **"offer his bullock" → "bring near the bull"** (v6, 11) — Hebrew הִקְרִיב, "bring near"; it is slaughtered in v11. So also v9 and v20 ("bring near the male goat"). **"which is for himself" → "that is his own"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ, "which is his". **"make an atonement for himself, and for his house" → "make atonement on behalf of himself and on behalf of his house"** (v6, 11, 17, 24) — Hebrew כִּפֶּר בְּעַד, kept apart from כִּפֶּר עַל "make atonement for" (v16, 18, 30, 33, 34). כִּפֶּר is at its root "cover".
- **"present them" → "make them stand"** (v7) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד; in v10 the passive, "shall be made to stand".
- **"cast lots upon the two goats" → "place lots on the two male goats"** (v8) — Hebrew וְנָתַן … גּוֹרָלוֹת, literally "give lots". **"the other lot for the scapegoat" → "one lot for Azazel"** (v8, 10, 26) — Hebrew לַעֲזָאזֵל. The word stands only in this chapter and its meaning is not known; the verse keeps the Hebrew word. It is set beside "for Yahweh" in the same form, and is often taken as the name of a being of the wilderness; others take it as "the goat that goes away" (עֵז "goat" and אָזַל "go away": so the Vulgate, and from it KJV "scapegoat"), as the name of a place or a rough cliff, or as "entire removal".
- **"upon which the LORD's lot fell" → "on which the lot for Yahweh came up"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew עָלָה עָלָיו הַגּוֹרָל, "the lot came up on it". **"offer him for a sin offering" → "offer it as a sin offering"** (v9) — Hebrew וְעָשָׂהוּ חַטָּאת, literally "make it a sin offering"; עָשָׂה of a sacrifice is "offer" (also v24).
- **"to make an atonement with him, and to let him go for a scapegoat" → "to make atonement over it, to send it away to Azazel"** (v10) — Hebrew לְכַפֵּר עָלָיו: עַל is "on, over, for"; elsewhere in the chapter כִּפֶּר עַל is "make atonement for"; here the goat is not slaughtered, and "over it" keeps the preposition (v21: "confess over it"). שִׁלַּח is "send away" (v21, 22, 26).
- **"a censer full of burning coals of fire" → "the firepan full of coals of fire"** (v12) — Hebrew מְלֹא־הַמַּחְתָּה גַּחֲלֵי־אֵשׁ, literally "the fullness of the firepan, coals of fire". **"his hands full of sweet incense beaten small" → "his two hands full of fine fragrant incense"** — Hebrew מְלֹא חָפְנָיו, "the fullness of his two cupped hands"; קְטֹרֶת סַמִּים דַּקָּה, "incense of fragrant substances, fine". "Them" after "bring" is supplied.
- **"put the incense upon the fire" → "place the incense on the fire"** (v13) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "give" (also v18, 21). **"that the cloud of the incense may cover" → "and the cloud of the incense shall cover"** — Hebrew וְכִסָּה. **"upon the testimony" → "over the testimony"** — Hebrew עַל־הָעֵדוּת.
- **"upon the mercy seat eastward" → "on the face of the kapporet, eastward"** (v14) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי הַכַּפֹּרֶת קֵדְמָה: on its front, the side toward the east.
- **"an atonement for the holy place, because of the uncleanness … and because of their transgressions in all their sins" → "atonement for the Holy, from the uncleannesses … and from their transgressions, as to all their sins"** (v16) — Hebrew מִטֻּמְאֹת … וּמִפִּשְׁעֵיהֶם, with מִן "from" (so also v19, 30, 34), and the nouns in the plural; לְכָל־חַטֹּאתָם, "for / as to all their sins" (v21). **"that remaineth among them" → "which abides with them"** — Hebrew הַשֹּׁכֵן אִתָּם.
- **"there shall be no man" → "no man shall be"** (v17) — Hebrew וְכָל־אָדָם לֹא־יִהְיֶה. **"congregation of Israel" → "assembly of Israel"** (v17, 33) — Hebrew קָהָל; "congregation" (v5) is עֵדָה.
- **"cleanse it, and hallow it" → "cleanse it and sanctify it"** (v19) — Hebrew וְטִהֲרוֹ וְקִדְּשׁוֹ.
- **"when he hath made an end of reconciling the holy place" → "when he has finished making atonement for the Holy"** (v20) — Hebrew מִכַּפֵּר אֶת־הַקֹּדֶשׁ: here and in v33 the verb has a direct object ("atoning the Holy"); English needs "for".
- **"both his hands" → "his two hands"** (v21) — the written text has יָדוֹ "his hand"; the reading tradition (qere), with the numeral "two", has יָדָיו "his hands". **"lay"** — Hebrew סָמַךְ, "lean, press". **"putting them" → "and he shall place them"** — Hebrew וְנָתַן. **"by the hand of a fit man" → "by the hand of a man in readiness"** — Hebrew אִישׁ עִתִּי: the adjective, from עֵת "time", stands only here: a man at hand for the time, ready or appointed; the exact sense is not certain.
- **"unto a land not inhabited" → "to a land cut off"** (v22) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ גְּזֵרָה: the noun stands only here, from גָּזַר "cut": a land cut off; "not inhabited" is an inference. **"he shall let go the goat" → "he shall send away the male goat"** — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּח.
- **"shall put off … and shall leave them there" → "strip off … and he shall lay them up there"** (v23) — Hebrew וּפָשַׁט … וְהִנִּיחָם.
- **"in the holy place" → "in a holy place"** (v24) — Hebrew בְּמָקוֹם קָדוֹשׁ, without the article. **"his garments"** — Hebrew בְּגָדָיו; no word "other" stands in the Hebrew.
- **"shall he burn upon the altar" → "he shall turn into smoke on the altar"** (v25) — Hebrew יַקְטִיר; "burn" in v27, 28 is another verb (שָׂרַף).
- **"he that let go the goat for the scapegoat" → "he who sends away the male goat to Azazel"** (v26) — as v10.
- **"shall one carry forth without the camp" → "he shall bring out to outside the camp"** (v27) — Hebrew יוֹצִיא, a singular with no subject named ("one shall bring out"); the next verb is plural, "they shall burn".
- **"a statute for ever" → "an everlasting statute"** (v29, 31, 34) — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age". **"ye shall afflict your souls"** (v29, 31) — Hebrew תְּעַנּוּ אֶת־נַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶם, word for word; it is commonly understood of fasting (cf. Isa 58:3, 5), which the words themselves do not say. **"one of your own country, or a stranger that sojourneth among you" → "the native and the sojourner who sojourns in your midst"** — Hebrew הָאֶזְרָח וְהַגֵּר הַגָּר בְּתוֹכְכֶם.
- **"shall the priest make an atonement for you" → "he shall make atonement for you"** (v30) — Hebrew יְכַפֵּר עֲלֵיכֶם: "the priest" is not in the Hebrew; the subject is not named (it may also be taken impersonally, "atonement shall be made"). **"that ye may be clean from all your sins before the LORD" → "from all your sins before Yahweh you shall be clean"** — the Hebrew accents put the pause after "to cleanse you".
- **"a sabbath of rest" → "a Shabbat of shabbaton"** (v31) — Hebrew שַׁבַּת שַׁבָּתוֹן, as Exod 31:15.
- **"whom he shall anoint, and whom he shall consecrate to minister in the priest's office in his father's stead" → "who is anointed and whose hand is filled to be priest in his father's place"** (v32) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־יִמְשַׁח אֹתוֹ וַאֲשֶׁר יְמַלֵּא אֶת־יָדוֹ, active verbs with no subject named ("whom one anoints and whose hand one fills"); "fill the hand" is the Hebrew for installing a priest (Exod 28:41). לְכַהֵן is "to be priest".
- **"the holy sanctuary"** (v33) — Hebrew מִקְדַּשׁ הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the sanctuary of the holiness", only here.
- **"once a year" → "once in the year"** (v34) — Hebrew אַחַת בַּשָּׁנָה, as Exod 30:10. **"for all their sins" → "from all their sins"** — Hebrew מִכָּל־חַטֹּאתָם.

### Notes

- **Yom Kippur** — the Day of Atonement (23:27 יוֹם הַכִּפֻּרִים); the name does not stand in this chapter. Once in the year the Holy, the tent of meeting and the altar are atoned for, from the uncleannesses and sins of Israel.
- **Two male goats** — one is slaughtered as a sin offering and its blood is brought inside the veil; over the other all the iniquities of Israel are confessed, and it bears them into the wilderness.
- **Azazel** — see Changes on v8: the meaning of the word is not known, and the text does not explain it.
- **Garments of linen** (v4) — not the garments of gold and colours of Exod 28; they are put off again in v23.
- **Once in the year** (v34) — Heb 9:7–12 sets this beside the entry of Christ "once for all".
- **Jesus and the two goats** — early Christian readings (Barnabas, Tertullian) saw Christ in *both* goats: slain for atonement and bearing sins away. Heb 13:11–13 applies the "outside the camp" of v27 to Jesus' crucifixion.
