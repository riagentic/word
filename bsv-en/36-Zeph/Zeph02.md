# Zephaniah 2

[1] Gather yourselves together, and gather,
nation not longed for,

[2] before the decree gives birth —
like chaff the day has passed —
before there comes upon you
the burning of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
before there comes upon you
the day of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] Seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all the humble of the land,
who have done his judgment;
seek righteousness, seek humility;
perhaps you will be concealed
on the day of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] For ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧ shall be forsaken,
and ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ a desolation;
Ashdod — at noon they shall drive her out,
and ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ shall be uprooted.

[5] Woe, inhabitants of the region of the sea,
nation of the ⟦cherethites|Keretim|Cherethites⟧!
The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is against you,
⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧;
and I will make you perish, so that there is no inhabitant.

[6] And the region of the sea shall become pastures,
shepherds' cisterns,
and folds of the flock.

[7] And the region shall be for the remnant of the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧;
upon them they shall graze;
in the houses of ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ in the evening they shall lie down;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will visit them
and restore their fortunes.

[8] I have heard the reproach of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧
and the revilings of the sons of Ammon,
with which they have reproached my people
and made themselves great against their border.

[9] Therefore, as I live, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be like ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧,
and the sons of Ammon like ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧,
a possession of nettles and a pit of salt,
and a desolation forever;
the remnant of my people shall plunder them,
and the rest of my nation shall inherit them.

[10] This shall be theirs instead of their pride,
for they have reproached and made themselves great
against the people of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[11] Fearsome is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ against them,
for he has made lean all the gods of the earth;
and they shall bow down to him,
each from his place,
all the coastlands of the nations.

[12] You too, ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ —
the slain of my sword are they.

[13] And he will stretch out his hand against the north
and make ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ perish,
and he will set ⟦nineveh|Ninveh|Nineveh⟧ as a desolation,
dryness like the wilderness.

[14] And droves shall lie down in her midst,
all the living things of a nation;
both the desert owl and the hedgehog
shall lodge in her capitals;
a voice shall sing in the window;
dryness is on the threshold;
for he has laid bare the cedar work.

[15] This is the jubilant city
that dwells in security,
that says in her heart,
I am, and there is none besides me.
How she has become a thing that appalls,
a lying-down place for the living things!
Everyone who passes by her
hisses, shakes his hand.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"the LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, the prophets' formula; נְאֻם "declares".
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the quotation marks of v8–9 are dropped (the Hebrew has none).
- **"Gather yourselves together, yea, gather together, O nation not desired" → "Gather yourselves together, and gather, nation not longed for"** (v1) — Hebrew הִתְקוֹשְׁשׁוּ וָקוֹשּׁוּ, a verb from קַשׁ "stubble" ("gather as stubble is gathered"); וָ "and"; no "O"; לֹא נִכְסָף "not longed for" (KJV "not desired"; the former "shameless" takes a later sense of the root).
- **"Before the decree bring forth, before the day pass as the chaff" → "before the decree gives birth — like chaff the day has passed —"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּטֶרֶם לֶדֶת חֹק "before the giving birth of a decree" (חֹק, elsewhere "statute"); כְּמֹץ עָבַר יוֹם, a perfect, a clause by itself (uncertain). **"before the fierce anger of the LORD come upon you, before the day of the LORD's anger come upon you" → "before there comes upon you the burning of the anger of Yahweh, before there comes upon you the day of the anger of Yahweh"** — בְּטֶרֶם לֹא, literally "before not" (the negative belongs to the idiom); חֲרוֹן אַף "the burning of anger".
- **"Seek ye the LORD, all ye meek of the earth, which have wrought his judgment; seek righteousness, seek meekness: it may be ye shall be hid in the day of the LORD's anger" → "Seek Yahweh, all the humble of the land, who have done his judgment; seek righteousness, seek humility; perhaps you will be concealed on the day of the anger of Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew עַנְוֵי "the humble" (not "meek"); מִשְׁפָּטוֹ "his judgment" (singular; the former "just commands" was paraphrase); פָּעָלוּ, a perfect; עֲנָוָה "humility"; אוּלַי "perhaps"; תִּסָּתְרוּ "you will be concealed".
- **"For Gaza shall be forsaken, and Ashkelon a desolation: they shall drive out Ashdod at the noon day, and Ekron shall be rooted up"** (v4) — Hebrew עַזָּה עֲזוּבָה (*Azzah azuvah*) and עֶקְרוֹן תֵּעָקֵר (*Eqron te'aqer*), plays on the names; יְגָרְשׁוּהָ "they shall drive her out" (Ashdod first; the former "Ashdod's people" added "people").
- **"Woe unto the inhabitants of the sea coast, the nation of the Cherethites! the word of the LORD is against you; O Canaan, the land of the Philistines, I will even destroy thee, that there shall be no inhabitant" → "Woe, inhabitants of the region of the sea, nation of the Cherethites! The word of Yahweh is against you, Canaan, land of the Philistines; and I will make you perish, so that there is no inhabitant"** (v5) — Hebrew הוֹי "Woe"; חֶבֶל הַיָּם "the region of the sea" (חֶבֶל, literally "cord", a measured region; also v6, 7); no "O"; הַאֲבַדְתִּיךְ "I will make you perish"; מֵאֵין יוֹשֵׁב "without inhabitant".
- **"And the sea coast shall be dwellings and cottages for shepherds, and folds for flocks" → "And the region of the sea shall become pastures, shepherds' cisterns, and folds of the flock"** (v6) — Hebrew נְוֺת "pastures" (נָוֶה); כְּרֹת, found only here, from כָּרָה "dig": "cisterns" (KJV "cottages"; uncertain); גִּדְרוֹת צֹאן "folds of the flock".
- **"And the coast shall be for the remnant of the house of Judah; they shall feed thereupon: in the houses of Ashkelon shall they lie down in the evening: for the LORD their God shall visit them, and turn away their captivity" → "And the region shall be for the remnant of the house of Judah; upon them they shall graze; in the houses of Ashkelon in the evening they shall lie down; for Yahweh their Elohim will visit them and restore their fortunes"** (v7) — Hebrew עֲלֵיהֶם "upon them"; יִרְעוּן "they shall graze"; יִרְבָּצוּן "lie down"; יִפְקְדֵם "will visit them" (for good; the former "be mindful of" softened the verb); וְשָׁב שְׁבִיתָם (qere; written שבותם), שׁוּב שְׁבוּת "restore the fortunes", literally "turn back their captivity / their turning".
- **"I have heard the reproach of Moab, and the revilings of the children of Ammon, whereby they have reproached my people, and magnified themselves against their border" → "I have heard the reproach of Moab and the revilings of the sons of Ammon, with which they have reproached my people and made themselves great against their border"** (v8) — Hebrew חֶרְפַּת "reproach", חֵרְפוּ "reproached" (one root; the former "taunts" broke it); גִּדּוּפֵי "revilings"; בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן "the sons of Ammon" (the `ammonites` token gave "Ammonites" for words the Hebrew has as "sons of Ammon"; also v9); וַיַּגְדִּילוּ עַל "made themselves great against" (the former "boasts" was interpretation).
- **"Therefore as I live, saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, Surely Moab shall be as Sodom, and the children of Ammon as Gomorrah, even the breeding of nettles, and saltpits, and a perpetual desolation" → "Therefore, as I live, declares Yahweh of Hosts, the Elohim of Israel, Moab shall be like Sodom, and the sons of Ammon like Gomorrah, a possession of nettles and a pit of salt, and a desolation forever"** (v9) — Hebrew חַי־אָנִי "as I live" (literally "living am I"); כִּי after the oath "that" (not rendered); מִמְשַׁק, found only here, "possession" (uncertain); חָרוּל "nettles" (uncertain); מִכְרֵה־מֶלַח "a pit of salt" (singular). **"the residue of my people shall spoil them, and the remnant of my people shall possess them" → "the remnant of my people shall plunder them, and the rest of my nation shall inherit them"** — שְׁאֵרִית "remnant", יֶתֶר "rest"; the qere גּוֹיִי "my nation" (written גוי "a nation"); בָּזַז "plunder"; נָחַל "inherit".
- **"This shall they have for their pride, because they have reproached and magnified themselves against the people of the LORD of hosts" → "This shall be theirs instead of their pride, for they have reproached and made themselves great against the people of Yahweh of Hosts"** (v10) — Hebrew תַּחַת "instead of"; גְּאוֹנָם "their pride"; the verbs of v8.
- **"The LORD will be terrible unto them: for he will famish all the gods of the earth; and men shall worship him, every one from his place, even all the isles of the heathen" → "Fearsome is Yahweh against them, for he has made lean all the gods of the earth; and they shall bow down to him, each from his place, all the coastlands of the nations"** (v11) — Hebrew נוֹרָא "fearsome"; רָזָה, a perfect, "made lean"; אֱלֹהֵי הָאָרֶץ "the gods of the earth" (plural beings: plain); וְיִשְׁתַּחֲווּ "and they shall bow down"; אִישׁ מִמְּקוֹמוֹ "each from his place" (in his own land, not coming to Jerusalem); אִיֵּי "coastlands".
- **"Ye Ethiopians also, ye shall be slain by my sword" → "You too, Cushites — the slain of my sword are they"** (v12) — Hebrew גַּם־אַתֶּם כּוּשִׁים חַלְלֵי חַרְבִּי הֵמָּה: address in the second person, then "they" (as printed); no verb "shall be slain".
- **"And he will stretch out his hand against the north, and destroy Assyria; and will make Nineveh a desolation, and dry like a wilderness" → "And he will stretch out his hand against the north and make Assyria perish, and he will set Nineveh as a desolation, dryness like the wilderness"** (v13) — Hebrew וְיֵט, וִיאַבֵּד, וְיָשֵׂם, short (jussive) forms, which can also be read as a wish ("may he stretch out"); וִיאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד; צִיָּה "dryness"; מִדְבָּר "wilderness".
- **"And flocks shall lie down in the midst of her, all the beasts of the nations: both the cormorant and the bittern shall lodge in the upper lintels of it; their voice shall sing in the windows; desolation shall be in the thresholds: for he shall uncover the cedar work" → "And droves shall lie down in her midst, all the living things of a nation; both the desert owl and the hedgehog shall lodge in her capitals; a voice shall sing in the window; dryness is on the threshold; for he has laid bare the cedar work"** (v14) — Hebrew עֲדָרִים "droves"; כָּל־חַיְתוֹ־גוֹי "all the living things of a nation" (uncertain; גּוֹי of a swarm of animals, as Joel 1:6); קָאַת "desert owl", קִפֹּד "hedgehog" (both uncertain, as Isa 34:11); בְּכַפְתֹּרֶיהָ "in her capitals" (the heads of pillars, as Amos 9:1); קוֹל "a voice"; יְשׁוֹרֵר "shall sing"; חֹרֶב "dryness" (the Septuagint, from memory, "ravens", עֹרֵב; the MT is kept); סַף "threshold"; אַרְזָה, found only here, "cedar work"; עֵרָה, a perfect, "he has laid bare" (subject not named).
- **"This is the rejoicing city that dwelt carelessly, that said in her heart, I am, and there is none beside me: how is she become a desolation, a place for beasts to lie down in! every one that passeth by her shall hiss, and wag his hand" → "This is the jubilant city that dwells in security, that says in her heart, I am, and there is none besides me. How she has become a thing that appalls, a lying-down place for the living things! Everyone who passes by her hisses, shakes his hand"** (v15) — Hebrew הָעַלִּיזָה "the jubilant"; הַיּוֹשֶׁבֶת לָבֶטַח "that dwells in security"; אֲנִי וְאַפְסִי עוֹד "I, and besides me no more" (as Isa 47:8, 10); שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls"; מַרְבֵּץ "a lying-down place"; חַיָּה "living things"; יִשְׁרֹק "hisses"; יָנִיעַ יָדוֹ "shakes his hand".
