# 1 Kings 5

[1] Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon, for he had heard that they had anointed him king in place of his father; for Hiram had always loved David.

[2] Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,

[3] "You know that David my father could not build a house for the name of Yahweh his *Elohim* because of the wars around him on every side, until Yahweh put them under the soles of his feet.

[4] But now Yahweh my *Elohim* has given me rest on every side; there is neither adversary nor occurrence of evil.

[5] Behold, I intend to build a house for the name of Yahweh my *Elohim*, as Yahweh said to David my father, 'Your son whom I will set on your throne in your place, he shall build the house for my name.'

[6] Now therefore command that they cut down for me cedars from Lebanon; my servants shall be with your servants, and I will give you wages for your servants according to all that you say; for you know that there is no one among us who can cut timber like the Sidonians."

[7] When Hiram heard the words of Solomon, he rejoiced greatly and said, "Blessed be Yahweh this day, who has given to David a wise son to be over this great people!"

[8] Hiram sent to Solomon, saying, "I have heard the message you have sent me; I will do all your desire concerning timber of cedar and timber of cypress.

[9] My servants shall bring them down from Lebanon to the sea; I will make them into rafts to go by sea to the place that you shall direct me; I will have them broken up there, and you shall receive them. You shall fulfill my desire by providing food for my household."

[10] So Hiram gave Solomon cedar timber and cypress timber according to all his desire.

[11] Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand kors of wheat as food for his household, and twenty kors of pure oil; thus Solomon gave to Hiram year by year.

[12] Yahweh gave Solomon wisdom, as he had promised him. There was peace between Hiram and Solomon; the two of them made a treaty.

[13] King Solomon raised a levy of forced labor from all Israel; the levy was thirty thousand men.

[14] He sent them to Lebanon ten thousand a month in shifts: a month they were in Lebanon, and two months at home. Adoniram was over the levy.

[15] Solomon had seventy thousand burden-bearers and eighty thousand stonecutters in the hill country,

[16] besides Solomon's chief officers who were over the work, three thousand three hundred, who supervised the people who did the work.

[17] At the king's command, they quarried great stones, costly stones, to lay the foundation of the house with dressed stones.

[18] So Solomon's builders and Hiram's builders and the Gebalites did the hewing and prepared the timber and the stones to build the house.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"fir" → "cypress"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew *berosh*. Cypress more accurate.
- **"floats" → "rafts"** (v9) — archaic.
- **"league" → "treaty"** (v12).
- **"stonesquarers" → "Gebalites"** (v18) — Hebrew הַגִּבְלִים *ha-Giblim*. Men from Gebal (Byblos), renowned stoneworkers.
- **"levy" → "forced labor"** (v13, 14) — *mas*.

### Notes
- **Hiram of Tyre alliance** — continues since David's time (2 Sam 5:11). Phoenician technology and timber; Israelite grain and oil.
- **Cedar and cypress** — Lebanon's prized lumber for royal construction.
- **Forced labor** (v13) — 30,000 rotating among Israelites; plus 70,000 bearers + 80,000 stonecutters (probably Canaanite residents per 9:20–21).
- **Scale** — over 150,000 workers on the temple project. Massive public works.
- **Fulfillment of 2 Sam 7** — Solomon undertakes what was promised his father.
