## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"fir" → "cypress"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew *berosh*. Cypress more accurate.
- **"floats" → "rafts"** (v9) — archaic.
- **"league" → "treaty"** (v12).
- **"stonesquarers" → "Gebalites"** (v18) — Hebrew הַגִּבְלִים *ha-Giblim*. Men from Gebal (Byblos), renowned stoneworkers.
- **"levy" → "forced labor"** (v13, 14) — *mas*.

### Notes
- **Hiram of Tyre alliance** — continues since David's time (2 Sam 5:11). Phoenician technology and timber; Israelite grain and oil.
- **Cedar and cypress** — Lebanon's prized lumber for royal construction.
- **Forced labor** (v13) — 30,000 rotating among Israelites; plus 70,000 bearers + 80,000 stonecutters (probably Canaanite residents per 9:20–21).
- **Scale** — over 150,000 workers on the temple project. Massive public works.
- **Fulfillment of 2 Sam 7** — Solomon undertakes what was promised his father.
