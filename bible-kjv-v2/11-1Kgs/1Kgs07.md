# 1 Kings 7

[1] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ was building his own house thirteen years, and he completed his entire house.

[2] He built also the House of the Forest of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧; its length was a hundred cubits, its width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits, built on four rows of cedar pillars, with cedar beams on the pillars.

[3] It was covered with cedar above over the forty-five side chambers that were on the pillars, fifteen in a row.

[4] There were three rows of window frames, with window opposite window in three tiers.

[5] All the doorways and doorposts had square frames, with window opposite window in three tiers.

[6] He made the Hall of Pillars: its length was fifty cubits, its width thirty cubits; with a vestibule in front of them, and a canopy and pillars before them.

[7] He made the Hall of the Throne, where he was to pronounce judgment, the Hall of Judgment; it was paneled with cedar from floor to rafters.

[8] His own house where he would dwell — in the other court, inward from the Hall — was of like workmanship. ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made also a house like this Hall for ⟦pharaoh|Paroh's|Pharaoh's⟧ daughter, whom he had taken as wife.

[9] All these were of costly stones, hewn to measurement, sawed with saws on the inside and outside, from foundation to coping, and from the outside to the great court.

[10] The foundation was of costly stones, great stones, stones of ten cubits and stones of eight cubits.

[11] Above were costly stones, hewn to measurement, and cedar.

[12] The great court all around had three courses of hewn stone and one course of cedar beams, the same as the inner court of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the vestibule of the house.

[13] King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sent and brought ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ from ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧.

[14] He was the son of a widow from the tribe of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, and his father was a man of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, a worker in bronze. He was filled with wisdom, understanding, and skill to do any work in bronze. He came to King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ and did all his work.

[15] He cast two pillars of bronze, eighteen cubits high apiece; a line of twelve cubits measured the circumference of each.

[16] He made two capitals of cast bronze to set on the tops of the pillars; the height of the one capital was five cubits, and the height of the other capital was five cubits.

[17] There were nets of checker work, with wreaths of chain work, for the capitals that were on the top of the pillars — seven for the one capital and seven for the other capital.

[18] He made the pillars; two rows around on the one network, to cover the capitals that were on the top, with pomegranates; so he did for the other capital.

[19] The capitals that were on the top of the pillars in the vestibule were of lily work, four cubits.

[20] The capitals were on the two pillars above, close to the rounded projection beside the network; there were two hundred pomegranates in rows all around on the second capital also.

[21] He set up the pillars at the vestibule of the temple; he set up the right pillar and called its name ⟦jachin|Yachin|Jachin⟧ — "He establishes"; he set up the left pillar and called its name Boaz — "In strength."

[22] On the tops of the pillars was lily work; so the work of the pillars was finished.

[23] He made the molten sea, ten cubits from brim to brim, circular, five cubits high; a line of thirty cubits measured its circumference.

[24] Under its brim, gourds encircled it all around, ten to the cubit, around the sea; the gourds were cast in two rows when it was cast.

[25] It stood on twelve oxen, three facing north, three facing west, three facing south, and three facing east; the sea was set upon them, and all their hindquarters were inward.

[26] Its thickness was a handbreadth; its brim was made like the brim of a cup, like the petals of a lily; it held two thousand baths.

[27] He made ten bases of bronze; each base was four cubits long, four cubits wide, and three cubits high.

[28] This was the construction of the bases: they had panels, and the panels were set in frames;

[29] on the panels set in the frames were lions, oxen, and ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧; and on the frames, both above and below the lions and the oxen, there were wreaths of hammered work.

[30] Each base had four bronze wheels and bronze axles; at the four corners were supports for a basin, cast with wreaths at each side.

[31] Its opening within the crown at the top was a cubit; its opening was round like the work of a pedestal, a cubit and a half; and on its opening also were engravings. Their panels were square, not round.

[32] The four wheels were underneath the panels; the axles of the wheels were attached to the base; the height of a wheel was a cubit and a half.

[33] The wheels were made like a chariot wheel; their axles, their rims, their spokes, and their hubs were all cast.

[34] There were four supports at the four corners of each base; the supports were of one piece with the base.

[35] On the top of the base was a round band half a cubit high; and on the top of the base its supports and its panels were of one piece with it.

[36] On the surfaces of its supports and on its panels, he carved ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, lions, and palm trees, according to the space of each, with wreaths all around.

[37] After this manner he made the ten bases; all of them were cast alike, of the same measure and the same form.

[38] He made ten basins of bronze; each basin held forty baths; each basin was four cubits; on each of the ten bases was one basin.

[39] He set the bases, five on the south side of the house, and five on the north side of the house; and he set the sea on the southeast corner of the house.

[40] ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ made the pots, the shovels, and the basins. So ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ finished all the work that he did for King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

[41] the two pillars, the two bowls of the capitals that were on the tops of the pillars, and the two networks to cover the two bowls of the capitals that were on the tops of the pillars;

[42] and the four hundred pomegranates for the two networks, two rows of pomegranates for each network, to cover the two bowls of the capitals that were on the pillars;

[43] the ten bases and the ten basins on the bases;

[44] the one sea and the twelve oxen under the sea;

[45] the pots, the shovels, and the basins. All these vessels that ⟦hiram|Chiram|Hiram⟧ made for King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ were of burnished bronze.

[46] In the plain of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ the king cast them in the clay ground between ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ and ⟦zarethan|Tzartan|Zarethan⟧.

[47] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ left all the vessels unweighed, because there were so many; the weight of the bronze could not be ascertained.

[48] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made all the vessels that were in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: the golden altar, the golden table on which was the bread of the Presence,

[49] the lampstands of pure gold, five on the south side and five on the north, before the inner sanctuary — with the flowers, the lamps, and the tongs, of gold;

[50] the basins, the snuffers, the bowls, the dishes for incense, and the fire-pans, of pure gold; and the sockets of gold for the doors of the innermost part of the house — the Most Holy Place — and for the doors of the nave of the temple.

[51] Thus all the work that King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ did on the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was finished. ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ brought in the things that David his father had dedicated — the silver, the gold, and the vessels — and placed them in the treasuries of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (throughout).
- **"chapiters" → "capitals"** (v16, 17, 19, 20, 41) — modernized architectural term.
- **"knops" → "gourds"** (v24).
- **"candlesticks" → "lampstands"** (v49).
- **"shewbread" → "bread of the Presence"** (v48).
- **"laver" → "basin"** (v38).

### Notes
- **Palace vs temple** — 13-year palace + 7-year temple = 20 total. Solomon's palace larger and took longer.
- **Yakhin and Bo'az** — freestanding bronze pillars at the temple entrance. Their names suggest divine establishment and strength.
- **Molten sea** — massive bronze basin on twelve oxen. Symbolic of cosmic waters / temple as cosmos-microcosm.
- **Hiram** — skilled craftsman (namesake of the king of Tyre). Tyrian metalwork for Israelite temple.
- **Bronze vessels uncounted** — scale immense. 2 Kgs 25 tells of their breaking up by Babylonians.
- **Gold interior, bronze exterior** — graduation of sacredness.
