## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"sixscore" → "120"** (v14).
- **"fir" → "cypress"** (v11).
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v26).
- **"nether" → "Lower"** (v17).

### Notes
- **Second theophany** — like Gibeon (ch 3), Yahweh appears to Solomon. Sanction + warning.
- **Conditional covenant** (v6–9) — if Solomon or his sons turn away, the temple will be destroyed. Exile prophecy anticipated from the temple's very dedication.
- **Cabul cities** — Hiram displeased with Solomon's gift. International relations stress point.
- **Chariot cities** — Hazor, Megiddo, Gezer. Archaeology confirms massive Solomonic-era gates at these sites.
- **Ophir gold** — legendary source; location debated (Yemen, Somalia, India). Red Sea expedition.
- **Pharaoh's daughter's new house** (v24) — separated from the ark, possibly for ritual reasons.
