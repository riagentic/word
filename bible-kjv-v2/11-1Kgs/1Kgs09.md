# 1 Kings 9

[1] When Solomon had finished building the house of Yahweh and the king's house, and all that Solomon desired to do,

[2] Yahweh appeared to Solomon a second time, as he had appeared to him at Gibeon.

[3] Yahweh said to him, "I have heard your prayer and your plea that you have made before me; I have consecrated this house that you have built, by putting my name there forever; my eyes and my heart will be there for all time.

[4] As for you, if you will walk before me, as David your father walked, in integrity of heart and in uprightness, doing according to all that I have commanded you, and keeping my statutes and my ordinances,

[5] then I will establish the throne of your kingdom over Israel forever, as I promised David your father, saying, 'You shall not lack a man upon the throne of Israel.'

[6] But if you or your sons turn away from following me and do not keep my commandments and my statutes which I have set before you, but go and serve other gods and worship them,

[7] then I will cut off Israel from the land that I have given them; and the house that I have consecrated for my name I will cast out of my sight. Israel will become a proverb and a byword among all the peoples.

[8] This house which was exalted — everyone passing by will be appalled and will hiss; they will say, 'Why has Yahweh done thus to this land and to this house?'

[9] And they will answer, 'Because they forsook Yahweh their *Elohim*, who brought their fathers out of the land of Egypt, and laid hold of other gods and worshiped them and served them; therefore Yahweh has brought all this evil upon them.'"

[10] At the end of twenty years, when Solomon had built the two houses — the house of Yahweh and the king's house —

[11] (Hiram king of Tyre had supplied Solomon with cedar and cypress timber and with gold according to all his desire), then King Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee.

[12] Hiram came out from Tyre to see the cities that Solomon had given him, and they did not please him.

[13] He said, "What kind of cities are these that you have given me, my brother?" So they are called the land of Cabul ("Worthless") to this day.

[14] Hiram had sent to the king 120 talents of gold.

[15] This is the account of the forced labor that King Solomon conscripted: to build the house of Yahweh, his own house, the Millo, the wall of Jerusalem, Hazor, Megiddo, and Gezer.

[16] (Pharaoh king of Egypt had gone up and captured Gezer, burned it with fire, killed the Canaanites who lived in the city, and given it as a dowry to his daughter, Solomon's wife.

[17] Solomon rebuilt Gezer, Lower Beth-horon,

[18] Baalath, Tamar in the wilderness, within the land,

[19] and all the store cities that Solomon had, and the cities for his chariots and for his horsemen, and whatever Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and in all the land of his dominion.)

[20] All the people who were left of the Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, who were not of the children of Israel —

[21] their descendants who were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel had been unable to devote to destruction — these Solomon drafted to be slave labor, to this day.

[22] But of the children of Israel Solomon made no slaves; they were the soldiers, his servants, his officials, his commanders, his chariot officers, and his horsemen.

[23] These were the chief officers in charge of Solomon's work: five hundred and fifty, who supervised the people who did the work.

[24] Pharaoh's daughter went up from the city of David to her own house that Solomon had built for her. Then he built the Millo.

[25] Three times a year Solomon used to offer burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar that he built to Yahweh, making offerings with it before Yahweh. So he finished the house.

[26] King Solomon built a fleet of ships at Ezion-geber, which is near Eloth on the shore of the Sea of Reeds, in the land of Edom.

[27] Hiram sent in the fleet his servants, sailors who were familiar with the sea, together with the servants of Solomon.

[28] They went to Ophir and brought from there gold — 420 talents — and brought it to King Solomon.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"sixscore" → "120"** (v14).
- **"fir" → "cypress"** (v11).
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v26).
- **"nether" → "Lower"** (v17).

### Notes
- **Second theophany** — like Gibeon (ch 3), Yahweh appears to Solomon. Sanction + warning.
- **Conditional covenant** (v6–9) — if Solomon or his sons turn away, the temple will be destroyed. Exile prophecy anticipated from the temple's very dedication.
- **Cabul cities** — Hiram displeased with Solomon's gift. International relations stress point.
- **Chariot cities** — Hazor, Megiddo, Gezer. Archaeology confirms massive Solomonic-era gates at these sites.
- **Ophir gold** — legendary source; location debated (Yemen, Somalia, India). Red Sea expedition.
- **Pharaoh's daughter's new house** (v24) — separated from the ark, possibly for ritual reasons.
