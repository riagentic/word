# 1 Kings 2

[1] And the days of David drew near to die, and he commanded ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his son, saying,

[2] "I am going in the way of all the earth; and you shall be strong and be a man.

[3] And you shall keep the charge of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to walk in his ways, to keep his statutes, his commandments and his ordinances and his testimonies, as it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so that you may act with insight in all that you do and everywhere you turn,

[4] so that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ may make his word stand that he spoke concerning me, saying, 'If your sons keep their way, to walk before me in truth with all their heart and with all their soul,' saying, 'there shall not be cut off for you a man from upon the throne of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'

[5] And you also know what ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ did to me, what he did to the two commanders of the hosts of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ the son of Ner and to Amasa the son of ⟦jether|Yeter|Jether⟧: and he killed them and put the blood of war in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and he placed the blood of war on his girdle that was on his loins and on his sandal that was on his feet.

[6] And you shall do according to your wisdom, and you shall not let his gray hair go down in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to Sheol.

[7] And to the sons of Barzillai the ⟦gileadite|Giladi|Gileadite⟧ you shall do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, and they shall be among those who eat at your table, for so they came near to me when I fled from the face of ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ your brother.

[8] And behold, with you is ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ the son of Gera, the ⟦benjaminite|son of the Yemini|Benjaminite⟧, from ⟦bahurim|Bachurim|Bahurim⟧, and he cursed me with a grievous curse on the day of my going to ⟦mahanaim|Machanayim|Mahanaim⟧; and he came down to meet me at the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and I swore to him by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, 'I will not put you to death with the sword.'

[9] And now, do not clear him, for you are a wise man, and you will know what you shall do to him, and you shall bring down his gray hair in blood to Sheol."

[10] And David lay with his fathers and was buried in the city of David.

[11] And the days that David reigned over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were forty years: in ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧ he reigned seven years, and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ he reigned thirty-three years.

[12] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sat on the throne of David his father, and his kingdom was established greatly.

[13] And ⟦adonijah|Adoniyahu|Adonijah⟧ the son of ⟦haggith|Chaggit|Haggith⟧ came to ⟦bathsheba|Bat-Sheva|Bathsheba⟧ the mother of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, and she said, "Is your coming ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧?" And he said, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧."

[14] And he said, "I have a word for you." And she said, "Speak."

[15] And he said, "You know that the kingship was mine, and upon me all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had set their faces, to reign; and the kingship turned about and became my brother's, for from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ it became his.

[16] And now, one request I am asking from you; do not turn back my face." And she said to him, "Speak."

[17] And he said, "Say, please, to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ the king — for he will not turn back your face — that he give me ⟦abishag|Avishag|Abishag⟧ the ⟦shunammite|Shunammit|Shunammite⟧ as a wife."

[18] And ⟦bathsheba|Bat-Sheva|Bathsheba⟧ said, "Good. I will speak concerning you to the king."

[19] And ⟦bathsheba|Bat-Sheva|Bathsheba⟧ came to King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ to speak to him concerning ⟦adonijah|Adoniyahu|Adonijah⟧. And the king arose to meet her and bowed down to her, and he sat on his throne and set a throne for the mother of the king, and she sat at his right hand.

[20] And she said, "One small request I am asking from you; do not turn back my face." And the king said to her, "Ask, my mother, for I will not turn back your face."

[21] And she said, "Let ⟦abishag|Avishag|Abishag⟧ the ⟦shunammite|Shunammit|Shunammite⟧ be given to ⟦adonijah|Adoniyahu|Adonijah⟧ your brother as a wife."

[22] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ answered and said to his mother, "And why are you asking ⟦abishag|Avishag|Abishag⟧ the ⟦shunammite|Shunammit|Shunammite⟧ for ⟦adonijah|Adoniyahu|Adonijah⟧? Ask for him the kingship, for he is my brother, older than I — and for him, and for ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ the priest, and for ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧."

[23] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ swore by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "Thus may ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ do to me, and thus may he add: against his own soul ⟦adonijah|Adoniyahu|Adonijah⟧ has spoken this word.

[24] And now, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, who has established me and made me sit on the throne of David my father, and who has made for me a house as he spoke: today ⟦adonijah|Adoniyahu|Adonijah⟧ shall be put to death."

[25] And King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sent by the hand of ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧, and he fell upon him, and he died.

[26] And to ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ the priest the king said, "To ⟦anathoth|Anatot|Anathoth⟧ go, to your fields, for you are a man of death; and on this day I will not put you to death, for you bore the ark of ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ before David my father, and because you were afflicted in all in which my father was afflicted."

[27] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ drove out ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ from being priest to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to fulfill the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he spoke concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

[28] And the report came to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ — for ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ had turned after ⟦adonijah|Adoniyahu|Adonijah⟧, and after ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ he had not turned — and ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ fled to the tent of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and held fast to the horns of the altar.

[29] And it was told King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ that ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ had fled to the tent of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and behold, he was beside the altar. And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sent ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧, saying, "Go, fall upon him."

[30] And ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ came to the tent of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said to him, "Thus says the king: 'Come out.'" And he said, "No, for here I will die." And ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ brought back word to the king, saying, "Thus spoke ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, and thus he answered me."

[31] And the king said to him, "Do as he has spoken, and fall upon him and bury him, and you shall remove the blood without cause, which ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ shed, from upon me and from upon the house of my father.

[32] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will return his blood upon his head, who fell upon two men more righteous and better than he and killed them with the sword, and my father David did not know: ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ the son of Ner, the commander of the host of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and Amasa the son of ⟦jether|Yeter|Jether⟧, the commander of the host of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[34] And ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ went up and fell upon him and put him to death, and he was buried in his house in the wilderness.

[35] And the king put ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ in his place over the host, and ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ the priest the king put in the place of ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧.

[36] And the king sent and called ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ and said to him, "Build for yourself a house in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and dwell there, and you shall not go out from there anywhere.

[37] And it shall be, on the day of your going out, when you pass over the wadi ⟦kidron|Qidron|Kidron⟧, you shall surely know that you shall surely die; your blood shall be on your head."

[38] And ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ said to the king, "The word is good. As my lord the king has spoken, so your servant will do." And ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ dwelt in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ many days.

[39] And it came to pass at the end of three years that two slaves of ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ fled to Achish the son of ⟦maacah|Maachah|Maacah⟧, the king of ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧. And they told ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧, saying, "Behold, your slaves are in ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧."

[40] And ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ arose and saddled his donkey and went to ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧, to Achish, to seek his slaves; and ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ went and brought his slaves from ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧.

[41] And it was told ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ that ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ had gone from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ and had returned.

[42] And the king sent and called ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧ and said to him, "Did I not make you swear by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and warn you, saying, 'On the day of your going out, when you go anywhere, you shall surely know that you shall surely die'? And you said to me, 'The word is good; I have heard.'

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 23) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"drew nigh that he should die; and he charged" → "drew near to die, and he commanded"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרְבוּ … לָמוּת; וַיְצַו, the verb "command" (also v43, 46).
- **"I go the way of all the earth: be thou strong therefore, and shew thyself a man" → "I am going in the way of all the earth; and you shall be strong and be a man"** (v2) — Hebrew הֹלֵךְ, a participle, as Josh 23:14; וְחָזַקְתָּ וְהָיִיתָ לְאִישׁ, literally "and you shall be strong and become a man" (as 1 Sam 4:9).
- **"his judgments" → "his ordinances"** (v3) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים, the word "judgment", here of what has been ruled, as in Deuteronomy. The first two nouns stand without "and": "his statutes, his commandments and his ordinances and his testimonies". **"that thou mayest prosper" → "so that you may act with insight"** — Hebrew תַּשְׂכִּיל, "act with insight" and so succeed (Deut 29:9; Josh 1:7). **"whithersoever thou turnest thyself" → "everywhere you turn"** — literally "all to which you turn there".
- **"That the LORD may continue his word" → "so that Yahweh may make his word stand"** (v4) — Hebrew יָקִים, "make stand", the verb of David's prayer in 2 Sam 7:25; "established" in v12, 24, 45, 46 is another root (כּוּן). **"If thy children take heed to their way" → "If your sons keep their way"** — Hebrew בָּנֶיךָ "your sons"; שָׁמַר "keep". **"there shall not fail thee (said he) a man on the throne" → "saying, 'there shall not be cut off for you a man from upon the throne'"** — Hebrew לֵאמֹר, "saying", stands a second time before the promise; לֹא־יִכָּרֵת, "shall not be cut off".
- **"and what he did" → "what he did"** (v5) — the Hebrew has no "and": the second clause says what the first means. **"the two captains of the hosts" → "the two commanders of the hosts"** (v5, 32) — Hebrew שַׂר; צָבָא "host". **"whom he slew, and shed the blood of war in peace" → "and he killed them and put the blood of war in shalom"** — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׂם, "put, set": he set the blood of war in a time of peace; דְּמֵי is plural ("bloods": blood shed). The second verb, וַיִּתֵּן, is literally "gave", "placed". **"his shoes" → "his sandal"** — Hebrew נַעֲלוֹ, singular. The Hebrew says "his girdle … his sandal", Joab's own; a Greek reading "my girdle … my sandal" is sometimes followed (from memory).
- **"let not his hoar head go down to the grave in peace" → "you shall not let his gray hair go down in shalom to Sheol"** (v6) and **"his hoar head bring thou down to the grave with blood" → "you shall bring down his gray hair in blood to Sheol"** (v9) — Hebrew שֵׂיבָה "gray hair" (as Gen 42:38; 44:29); שְׁאוֹל is the name Sheol, the realm of the dead, not "the grave"; בְּדָם, "in blood".
- **"shew kindness" → "do chesed"** (v7) — Hebrew תַּעֲשֶׂה־חֶסֶד. **"of those that eat at thy table" → "among those who eat at your table"** — Hebrew בְּאֹכְלֵי שֻׁלְחָנֶךָ, "among the eaters of your table". **"they came to me when I fled because of Absalom" → "they came near to me when I fled from the face of Absalom"** — Hebrew קָרְבוּ; מִפְּנֵי, "from the face of".
- **"a Benjamite" → "the Benjaminite"** (v8) — Hebrew בֶן־הַיְמִינִי, "the son of the Yemini". **"which cursed me with a grievous curse"** — Hebrew קִלְלַנִי קְלָלָה נִמְרֶצֶת, verb and noun of one root ("make light of, curse"); נִמְרֶצֶת is a rare word ("grievous, painful"), its exact sense is not certain. **"in the day when I went" → "on the day of my going"**. **"I will not put thee to death"** — the oath formula, אִם־אֲמִיתְךָ, literally "if I put you to death".
- **"hold him not guiltless" → "do not clear him"** (v9) — Hebrew אַל־תְּנַקֵּהוּ. **"and knowest what thou oughtest to do" → "and you will know what you shall do"** — Hebrew וְיָדַעְתָּ אֵת אֲשֶׁר תַּעֲשֶׂה, with no "ought".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׁכַב "lie".
- **"seven years reigned he in Hebron"** (v11) — the Hebrew puts the places first: "in Hebron he reigned … and in Jerusalem he reigned".
- **"his kingdom was established greatly"** (v12) — Hebrew וַתִּכֹּן מַלְכֻתוֹ מְאֹד. "Kingdom" is מַלְכוּת here and מַמְלָכָה in v46; "kingship" (v15, 22) is מְלוּכָה.
- **"Comest thou peaceably? … Peaceably" → "Is your coming shalom? … Shalom"** (v13) — Hebrew הֲשָׁלוֹם בֹּאֶךָ … שָׁלוֹם, the noun "peace" both times (as 1 Sam 16:4).
- **"I have somewhat to say unto thee" → "I have a word for you"** (v14) — Hebrew דָּבָר לִי אֵלָיִךְ, "a word to me, to you".
- **"the kingdom was mine, and that all Israel set their faces on me, that I should reign" → "the kingship was mine, and upon me all Israel had set their faces, to reign"** (v15) — Hebrew מְלוּכָה; לִמְלֹךְ, the bare infinitive. **"for it was his from the LORD" → "for from Yahweh it became his"** — Hebrew הָיְתָה לּוֹ.
- **"I ask one petition of thee, deny me not" → "one request I am asking from you; do not turn back my face"** (v16, 20) and **"he will not say thee nay" → "he will not turn back your face"** (v17, 20) — Hebrew שְׁאֵלָה … שֹׁאֵל, noun and verb of one root ("an asking I am asking"); הֵשִׁיב פָּנִים, "turn back the face": send someone away unheard.
- **"Well; I will speak for thee" → "Good. I will speak concerning you"** (v18, 19) — Hebrew טוֹב; עָלֶיךָ, "concerning you, on your behalf".
- **"caused a seat to be set for the king's mother" → "set a throne for the mother of the king"** (v19) — Hebrew כִּסֵּא, the same word as "his throne" just before; וַיָּשֶׂם, "and he set".
- **"I pray thee, say me not nay"** (v20) — the Hebrew has no "I pray thee".
- **"ask for him the kingdom also; for he is mine elder brother; even for him" → "Ask for him the kingship, for he is my brother, older than I — and for him"** (v22) — Hebrew וְשַׁאֲלִי־לוֹ, with no "also"; הַגָּדוֹל מִמֶּנִּי, literally "the one greater than I". The last words, וְלוֹ וּלְאֶבְיָתָר … וּלְיוֹאָב, are translated as they stand: "and for him and for Abiathar the priest and for Joab". The old versions read "and on his side are Abiathar … and Joab" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"God do so to me, and more also, if Adonijah have not spoken this word against his own life" → "Thus may Elohim do to me, and thus may he add: against his own soul Adonijah has spoken this word"** (v23) — the oath formula כֹּה יַעֲשֶׂה־לִּי אֱלֹהִים וְכֹה יוֹסִיף, with כִּי bringing in what is sworn; בְנַפְשׁוֹ, "at the cost of his soul".
- **"set me on the throne" → "made me sit on the throne"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיּוֹשִׁיבַנִי (qere; the written form has one letter more and means the same). **"as he promised" → "as he spoke"** — Hebrew דִּבֵּר. The oath is taken up by כִּי: "today Adonijah shall be put to death".
- **"he fell upon him that he died" → "he fell upon him, and he died"** (v25, 46) — Hebrew וַיִּפְגַּע־בּוֹ וַיָּמֹת; פָּגַע בְּ "fall upon" also in v29, 31, 32, 34.
- **"Get thee to Anathoth, unto thine own fields; for thou art worthy of death" → "To Anathoth go, to your fields, for you are a man of death"** (v26) — the place stands first; אִישׁ מָוֶת, "a man of death" (as "son of death", 1 Sam 20:31). **"at this time" → "on this day"** — Hebrew וּבַיּוֹם הַזֶּה. **"the ark of the Lord GOD"** — Hebrew אֲרוֹן אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names. **"thou hast been afflicted in all wherein my father was afflicted"** — Hebrew הִתְעַנִּיתָ … הִתְעַנָּה, one verb twice: he bore the affliction that David bore.
- **"thrust out" → "drove out"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיְגָרֶשׁ. **"that he might fulfil" → "to fulfill"** — Hebrew לְמַלֵּא, literally "to fill" (the verb of 1:14). The word spoken concerning the house of Eli: 1 Sam 2:27–36.
- **"Then tidings came to Joab" → "And the report came to Joab"** (v28) — Hebrew הַשְּׁמֻעָה, "what was heard". **"though he turned not after Absalom" → "and after Absalom he had not turned"** — a plain "and". **"the tabernacle of the LORD" → "the tent of Yahweh"** (v28, 29, 30) — Hebrew אֹהֶל. **"caught hold on" → "held fast to"** — as 1:50.
- **"he is by the altar" → "he was beside the altar"** (v29) — Hebrew אֵצֶל "beside"; the clause has no verb ("and behold, beside the altar").
- **"Nay; but I will die here" → "No, for here I will die"** (v30) — Hebrew לֹא כִּי פֹה אָמוּת.
- **"that thou mayest take away the innocent blood" → "and you shall remove the blood without cause"** (v31) — Hebrew דְּמֵי חִנָּם, "blood(s) of without cause": blood shed for nothing.
- **"my father David not knowing thereof" → "and my father David did not know"** (v32). **"captain of the host" → "commander of the host"**.
- **"upon the head of his seed for ever … peace for ever" → "on the head of his seed forever … shalom forever"** (v33) — Hebrew זֶרַע "seed"; לְעֹלָם and עַד־עוֹלָם, two forms, one sense.
- **"slew him" → "put him to death"** (v34) — Hebrew וַיְמִתֵהוּ. **"in his own house"** — the Hebrew has no "own".
- **"in his room … in the room of" → "in his place … in the place of"** (v35) — Hebrew תַּחַת; the verb is נָתַן, "gave, put".
- **"go not forth thence any whither" → "you shall not go out from there anywhere"** (v36) and **"walkest abroad any whither" → "go anywhere"** (v42) — Hebrew אָנֶה וָאָנָה, "whither and whither".
- **"passest over the brook Kidron, thou shalt know for certain that thou shalt surely die" → "pass over the wadi Kidron, you shall surely know that you shall surely die"** (v37, 42) — Hebrew נַחַל "wadi"; יָדֹעַ תֵּדַע and מוֹת תָּמוּת, each verb with its infinitive ("knowing you shall know that dying you shall die").
- **"The saying is good" → "The word is good"** (v38) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר, as in v42.
- **"two of the servants of Shimei ran away" → "two slaves of Shimei fled"** (v39, 40) — Hebrew עֲבָדִים: here bondmen who run from their owner, so "slaves"; "your servant" in v38 is the same word.
- **"saddled his ass" → "saddled his donkey"** (v40).
- **"protested unto thee" → "warn you"** (v42) — Hebrew וָאָעִד בְּךָ, "testified against you": gave solemn warning. **"The word that I have heard is good" → "The word is good; I have heard"** — Hebrew טוֹב הַדָּבָר שָׁמָעְתִּי, divided by the accents after "the word".
- **"the commandment that I have charged thee with" → "the commandment that I commanded upon you"** (v43) — Hebrew הַמִּצְוָה אֲשֶׁר־צִוִּיתִי עָלֶיךָ, noun and verb of one root.
- **"all the wickedness which thine heart is privy to … therefore the LORD shall return thy wickedness" → "all the evil that your heart knows … and Yahweh will return your evil"** (v44) — Hebrew רָעָה "evil" both times; יָדַע "knows"; a plain "and".
- **"king Solomon shall be blessed" → "King Solomon is blessed"** (v45) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ, with no verb.

### Notes
- **David's final charges** — two lines:
  - Walk in Yahweh's ways; *chesed* to Barzillai's sons
  - Joab, for the blood of Abner (2 Sam 3:27) and Amasa (2 Sam 20:10); Shimei, for his curse (2 Sam 16:5–13; 19:16–23)
- **Solomon's first acts** — Adonijah put to death (after asking for Abishag, who had attended David), Abiathar driven out from the priesthood, Joab put to death at the altar, Shimei put to death after breaking his oath.
- **Abiathar** (v27) — the word concerning the house of Eli: 1 Sam 2:27–36. Zadok is put in his place (v35).
- **Joab** — Abner and Amasa killed "and my father David did not know" (v32); Absalom is not named here.
- **Shimei** (v36–46) — bound by oath to stay in Jerusalem; he goes to Gath after two slaves.
- **"The kingdom was established"** (v12, 46) — frame verses.
