## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"hoar head" → "gray head"** (v6, 9) — archaic.
- **"grave" → "Sheol"** (v6, 9).
- **"captain" → "commander"** (v5, 32).
- **"ass" → "donkey"** (v40).

### Notes
- **David's final charges** — two lines:
  - Blessings: walk in Yahweh's ways; kindness to Barzillai's sons
  - Vengeance: kill Joab for double murder; Shimei for cursing
- **Political housecleaning** — Solomon eliminates Adonijah (for requesting Abishag — attempted claim to kingship via royal concubine), Abiathar (banished; fulfills oracle against Eli's house), Joab (at the altar; blood-guilt), Shimei (for oath violation).
- **Abiathar's deposition fulfills 1 Sam 2:31–36** — the house of Eli replaced by Zadok's line.
- **Joab's long shadow** — Abner, Amasa, Absalom killed despite David's wishes. Finally Joab executed even at the altar.
- **Shimei's oath violation** — seemingly over two runaway slaves. But Solomon interprets as binding precedent.
- **"Kingdom was established"** (v12, 46) — frame verses. Solomon's reign secured by decisive action.
