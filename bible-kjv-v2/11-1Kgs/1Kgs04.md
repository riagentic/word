## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v29) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the princes which he had"** (v2) — Hebrew הַשָּׂרִים אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ; שַׂר "prince" is the word for the king's chief men. **"Azariah the son of Zadok the priest"** — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן, "the priest", can belong to Azariah or to Zadok; the comma follows the list, in which each man has his office. In v2–6 the Hebrew has no verb: "was", "were" are not supplied.
- **"the recorder"** (v3) — Hebrew הַמַּזְכִּיר, literally "the one who brings to remembrance": the exact office is not known.
- **"were the priests" → "priests"** (v4) — Hebrew כֹּהֲנִים, without the article. Abiathar is still named (see 2:27, 35).
- **"over the officers" → "over the prefects"** (v5, 7, 27) — Hebrew נִצָּבִים, literally "those set, stationed": the twelve of v7–19. **"principal officer, and the king's friend" → "a priest, the friend of the king"** — Hebrew כֹּהֵן רֵעֶה הַמֶּלֶךְ; כֹּהֵן is the word "priest" (as of David's sons, 2 Sam 8:18).
- **"over the household" → "over the house"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־הַבָּיִת. **"over the tribute" → "over the forced labor"** — Hebrew הַמַּס.
- **"which provided victuals for the king and his household: each man his month in a year made provision" → "and they sustained the king and his house; a month in the year it was upon each one to sustain"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּלְכֵּל "sustain" (with bread: Gen 45:11), twice; the reading tradition (qere) הָאֶחָד "the one", the written text "one".
- **"The son of Hur" → "Ben-hur"** (v8–11, 13) — five of the twelve are named only by the father: בֶּן־חוּר "son of Hur", and so Ben-deker, Ben-hesed, Ben-abinadab, Ben-geber. **"in mount Ephraim" → "in the hill country of Ephraim"** — Hebrew הַר, "mountain", of a region.
- **"Elonbethhanan"** (v9) — Hebrew וְאֵילוֹן בֵּית חָנָן, "and Elon of Beth-hanan" (or "the oak of Beth-hanan"): one place or two is not certain.
- **"to him pertained Sochoh" → "his was Socoh"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew לוֹ, "to him".
- **"in all the region of Dor" → "all Naphath-dor"** (v11) — Hebrew כָּל־נָפַת דֹּאר, with no "in"; נָפָה is found only with Dor (Josh 11:2; 12:23) and its meaning is not known ("height", "region"); the name is kept. **"which had Taphath … to wife" → "Taphath … became his wife"** — Hebrew הָיְתָה לּוֹ לְאִשָּׁה.
- **"to him pertained Taanach and Megiddo" → "Taanach and Megiddo"** (v12) — the Hebrew has only the names, with no "in" and no "to him". **"by Zartanah beneath Jezreel" → "beside Zarethan, below Jezreel"**. **"even unto the place that is beyond Jokneam" → "as far as beyond Jokmeam"** — Hebrew עַד מֵעֵבֶר לְיָקְמֳעָם; the name is Jokmeam.
- **"the towns of Jair" → "the tent villages of Jair"** (v13) — Hebrew חַוֺּת יָאִיר (Num 32:41; Deut 3:14). **"the region of Argob"** — Hebrew חֶבֶל, literally "cord, measured share". **"threescore great cities with walls and brasen bars" → "sixty great cities, with wall and bar of bronze"** — Hebrew חוֹמָה וּבְרִיחַ נְחֹשֶׁת, both singular.
- **"had Mahanaim" → "to Mahanaim"** (v14) — Hebrew מַחֲנָיְמָה, the name with the ending of direction.
- **"in Asher and in Aloth" → "in Asher and Bealoth"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּאָשֵׁר וּבְעָלוֹת, which can be read "and in Aloth" or "and Bealoth"; the place is not known.
- **"king of the Amorites" → "king of the Amorite"** (v19) — Hebrew הָאֱמֹרִי, singular with the article, of the whole people. **"and he was the only officer which was in the land" → "and one prefect who was in the land"** — Hebrew וּנְצִיב אֶחָד אֲשֶׁר בָּאָרֶץ, word for word; the clause is short and obscure ("and there was one prefect in the land": a governor over that land alone, or one over the home land, Judah, which the list does not name). נְצִיב is of one root with נִצָּבִים.
- **"making merry" → "glad"** (v20) — Hebrew שְׂמֵחִים. **"by the sea"** — Hebrew עַל־הַיָּם, "upon the sea".
- **"reigned over all kingdoms from the river unto the land of the Philistines" → "was ruling over all the kingdoms from the River, the land of the Philistines"** (v21) — Hebrew הָיָה מוֹשֵׁל, "was ruling"; the Masoretic Text has no "to" before "the land of the Philistines" (2 Chr 9:26 has it; from memory); the words are left as they stand. "The River" is the Euphrates. **"they brought presents" → "they were bringing forward an offering"** — Hebrew מַגִּשִׁים מִנְחָה: מִנְחָה is the gift brought to a king ("tribute"), the same word as the grain offering.
- **"Solomon's provision" → "the bread of Solomon"** (v22) — Hebrew לֶחֶם, "bread": all that was eaten. **"measures" → "kors"** — Hebrew כֹּר, a large dry measure.
- **"Ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen out of the pastures, and an hundred sheep" → "ten fat ones of the herd, and twenty of the herd from the pasture, and a hundred of the flock"** (v23) — Hebrew בָּקָר "herd" and צֹאן "flock", collectives counted without a word for the single animal; רְעִי "pasture". **"harts, and roebucks, and fallowdeer" → "deer and gazelle and roebuck"** — Hebrew אַיָּל, צְבִי, יַחְמוּר (Deut 14:5); the third is some kind of deer and cannot be proved. **"fatted fowl"** — Hebrew בַּרְבֻּרִים אֲבוּסִים: בַּרְבֻּר is found only here; a bird is commonly understood (geese, cuckoos, fowl), but the meaning is not certain.
- **"on this side the river" → "across the River"** (v24) — Hebrew עֵבֶר הַנָּהָר, "the far side of the River": the land west of the Euphrates, named as it is seen from the east. **"Azzah" → "Gaza"**. **"he had peace on all sides round about him" → "he had shalom from all his sides all around"** — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם; מִכָּל־עֲבָרָיו מִסָּבִיב.
- **"dwelt safely" → "dwelt in security"** (v25) — Hebrew לָבֶטַח.
- **"forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots"** (v26) — so the Masoretic Text; 2 Chr 9:25 has "four thousand" (from memory). אֻרְוֺת is "stalls" or "teams"; מֶרְכָּבוֹ is singular, "his chariotry".
- **"provided victual for" → "sustained"** (v27) — as v7. **"all that came unto king Solomon's table" → "everyone who came near to the table of King Solomon"** — Hebrew הַקָּרֵב. **"they lacked nothing" → "they let nothing be missing"** — Hebrew לֹא יְעַדְּרוּ דָּבָר.
- **"dromedaries" → "steeds"** (v28) — Hebrew רֶכֶשׁ, a kind of horse (Esth 8:10; Mic 1:13); which kind is not certain. **"unto the place where the officers were" → "to the place where it should be"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יִהְיֶה־שָּׁם, "where he (or it) would be": no subject is written; the king, or the horses, or the barley may be meant. **"according to his charge" → "according to his ordinance"** — Hebrew כְּמִשְׁפָּטוֹ, "according to his judgment": what was laid down for him.
- **"wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart" → "wisdom …, and understanding, very much, and breadth of heart"** (v29) — Hebrew תְּבוּנָה; רֹחַב לֵב. **"on the sea shore" → "on the shore of the sea"** — Hebrew שְׂפַת הַיָּם, literally "the lip of the sea".
- **"excelled the wisdom of all the children of the east country" → "was greater than the wisdom of all the sons of the east"** (v30) — Hebrew וַתֵּרֶב, "was more, was many"; בְּנֵי־קֶדֶם.
- **"wiser than all men" → "wiser than all mankind"** (v31) — Hebrew הָאָדָם. **"his fame" → "his name"** — Hebrew שְׁמוֹ.
- **"three thousand proverbs"** (v32) — Hebrew מָשָׁל, a saying built on a likeness: a proverb or a parable. **"his songs were a thousand and five"** — Hebrew שִׁירוֹ, singular, "his song".
- **"he spake of trees … that springeth out of the wall" → "he spoke about the trees … that comes out in the wall"** (v33) — Hebrew עַל "about, concerning"; יֹצֵא בַּקִּיר. The hyssop is a plant not certainly known. **"of beasts, and of fowl" → "about the beasts and about the birds"** — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה, עוֹף, רֶמֶשׂ, each a collective with the article.
- **"there came of all people" → "they came from all the peoples"** (v34). **"which had heard of his wisdom" → "who had heard his wisdom"**.

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew chapter 4 ends with our v20; our 4:21–34 is Hebrew 5:1–14.
- **Solomon's princes** (v2–6) — a list of names and offices, without verbs.
- **The twelve prefects** (v7–19) — each sustains the king and his house for one month in the year.
- **The bread of one day** (v22–23) — thirty kors of fine flour, sixty kors of meal, thirty of the herd, a hundred of the flock.
- **From the River to the border of Egypt** (v21, 24) — the kingdoms over which Solomon rules.
- **"Each man under his vine and under his fig tree"** (v25) — the same words in Mic 4:4.
- **Wisdom** (v29–34) — three thousand proverbs, a thousand and five songs; men come from all the peoples to hear (cf. ch 10).
