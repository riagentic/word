## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v2).
- **"tribute" → "forced labor"** (v6) — *mas*.
- **"brasen" → "bronze"** (v13).
- **"dromedaries" → "swift steeds"** (v28) — Hebrew רֶכֶשׁ *rekhesh*. Fast horses.
- **"Azzah" → "Gaza"** (v24).

### Notes
- **Solomon's administration** — centralized bureaucracy. Twelve administrative districts each supplying royal provisions for one month.
- **Scale of court** — daily ~190 bushels flour + 30+ cattle + 100+ sheep. Vast kingdom bureaucracy.
- **Empire** (v21, 24) — "from the River [Euphrates] to the border of Egypt." The Davidic-Solomonic empire at peak.
- **Peace and prosperity** — "every man under his vine and fig tree" (v25). Iconic OT peace-image; Mic 4:4 echoes.
- **Wisdom-literature attributed to Solomon** — 3,000 proverbs + 1,005 songs. Solomon's writings include parts of Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs.
- **International renown** — kings from afar came to hear (sets up Queen of Sheba, ch 10).
