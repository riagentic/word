## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"strange women" → "foreign women"** (v1, 8).
- **"Ashtoreth / Milcom / Chemosh" — retained with brief etymology** (v5, 7).
- **"perfect" → "wholly true"** (v4).
- **"captain" → "commander"** (v15, 21).
- **"Zidonians" → "Sidonians"** (throughout).

### Notes
- **Solomon's fall** — from wisdom's peak to covenantal failure. Foreign wives lead to idolatry.
- **1000 wives/concubines** — violation of Deut 17:17 ("he shall not multiply wives"). Political alliances, dynastic tools, but spiritually lethal.
- **Foreign gods** — Ashtoreth (Canaanite fertility), Chemosh (Moab), Molech (Ammon). State shrines on the Mount of Olives ("mountain east of Jerusalem").
- **Kingdom divided** — oracle pronounced. Ten tribes to Jeroboam; one (Judah + effectively Benjamin) remain to David's line. Solomon himself spared for David's sake.
- **Three adversaries** — Hadad (Edom), Rezon (Aram), Jeroboam (internal). Yahweh raises them up.
- **Ahijah's torn cloak** — prophetic sign-act. Twelve pieces, ten to Jeroboam. The pattern of dynastic transfer.
- **"Lamp before me in Jerusalem"** (v36) — Davidic-dynasty promise persists despite division.
