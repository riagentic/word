# 1 Kings 20

[1] And ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ gathered all his force, and thirty-two kings were with him, and horse and chariots; and he went up and besieged ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and fought against it.

[2] And he sent messengers to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, into the city, and he said to him, "Thus says ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧:

[3] 'Your silver and your gold, it is mine; and your wives and your sons, the good ones, they are mine.'"

[4] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ answered and said, "According to your word, my lord the king: yours am I, and all that is mine."

[5] And the messengers returned and said, "Thus says ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧, saying: 'I sent to you, saying, "Your silver and your gold and your wives and your sons you shall give to me";

[6] but about this time tomorrow I will send my servants to you, and they shall search your house and the houses of your servants; and it shall be, all the desire of your eyes they shall put in their hand and take.'"

[7] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ called all the elders of the land and said, "Know, please, and see that this one is seeking evil; for he sent to me for my wives and for my sons and for my silver and for my gold, and I did not hold back from him."

[8] And all the elders and all the people said to him, "Do not listen, and do not be willing."

[9] And he said to the messengers of ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧, "Say to my lord the king, 'All that you sent for to your servant at the first I will do, and this thing I am not able to do.'" And the messengers went and brought him back word.

[10] And ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ sent to him and said, "Thus may the gods do to me, and thus may they add: the dust of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ shall not suffice for handfuls for all the people who are at my feet."

[11] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ answered and said, "Speak: 'Let not him who girds on boast like him who ungirds.'"

[12] And it came to pass, when he heard this word — and he was drinking, he and the kings, in the booths — that he said to his servants, "Set yourselves!" And they set themselves against the city.

[13] And behold, one prophet drew near to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Have you seen all this great multitude? Behold, I am giving it into your hand today, and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

[14] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ said, "By whom?" And he said, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'By the young men of the princes of the provinces.'" And he said, "Who shall join the battle?" And he said, "You."

[15] And he numbered the young men of the princes of the provinces, and they were two hundred and thirty-two; and after them he numbered all the people, all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, seven thousand.

[16] And they went out at noon. And ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ was drinking himself drunk in the booths, he and the kings, thirty-two kings, who were helping him.

[17] And the young men of the princes of the provinces went out first. And ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ sent, and they told him, saying, "Men have come out from ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧."

[18] And he said, "If for ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ they have come out, seize them alive; and if for war they have come out, alive seize them."

[19] And these went out from the city: the young men of the princes of the provinces, and the force that was after them.

[20] And they struck down, each man his man, and ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ fled, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ pursued them. And ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ escaped on a horse, with horsemen.

[21] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went out and struck the horse and the chariots, and he struck ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ with a great blow.

[22] And the prophet drew near to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said to him, "Go, strengthen yourself, and know and see what you will do; for at the return of the year the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ is coming up against you."

[23] And the servants of the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ said to him, "A god of mountains is their god; therefore they were stronger than we. But let us fight with them on the level ground; surely we shall be stronger than they.

[24] And do this thing: remove the kings, each man from his place, and put governors in their place.

[25] And you, count for yourself a force like the force that fell from you, and horse like the horse, and chariots like the chariots, and let us fight them on the level ground; surely we shall be stronger than they." And he listened to their voice and did so.

[26] And it came to pass at the return of the year that ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ numbered ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and went up to ⟦aphek|Afeq|Aphek⟧ for the battle with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[27] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were numbered and were sustained, and they went to meet them. And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ camped opposite them like two little flocks of goats, and ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ filled the land.

[28] And the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ drew near and spoke to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Because ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ has said, "A god of mountains is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he is not a god of valleys," I will give all this great multitude into your hand, and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

[29] And they camped, these opposite those, seven days. And it came to pass on the seventh day that the battle drew near, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ struck down ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, a hundred thousand men on foot, in one day.

[30] And those who remained fled to ⟦aphek|Afeq|Aphek⟧, into the city, and the wall fell on twenty-seven thousand men who remained. And ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ fled and came into the city, a chamber within a chamber.

[31] And his servants said to him, "Behold, please, we have heard that the kings of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ are kings of ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧. Let us put, please, sackcloth on our loins and cords on our heads, and let us go out to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; perhaps he will let your soul live."

[32] And they girded sackcloth on their loins and cords on their heads, and they came to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said, "Your servant ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ says, 'Let my soul live, please.'" And he said, "Is he still alive? He is my brother."

[33] And the men were divining, and they hastened and caught it up from him, and they said, "Your brother ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧!" And he said, "Go, take him." And ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ came out to him, and he brought him up onto the chariot.

[34] And he said to him, "The cities that my father took from your father I will return, and streets you shall put for yourself in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, as my father put in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧." "And I, with the covenant I will let you go." And he cut a covenant with him and let him go.

[35] And one man from the sons of the prophets said to his neighbor, by the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "Strike me, please." And the man refused to strike him.

[36] And he said to him, "Because you have not listened to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, behold, you are going from me, and the lion will strike you down." And he went from beside him, and the lion found him and struck him down.

[37] And he found another man and said, "Strike me, please." And the man struck him, striking and wounding.

[38] And the prophet went and stood for the king by the way, and he disguised himself with a bandage over his eyes.

[39] And it came to pass, as the king was passing by, that he cried out to the king and said, "Your servant went out into the midst of the battle, and behold, a man turned aside and brought a man to me and said, 'Keep this man; if he is surely missing, your soul shall be for his soul, or you shall weigh out a talent of silver.'

[40] And it came to pass, your servant was doing here and there, and he was not." And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to him, "So is your judgment; you yourself have decided."

[41] And he hastened and removed the bandage from over his eyes, and the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ recognized him, that he was of the prophets.

[42] And he said to him, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Because you have let go out of hand the man of my devoting to destruction, your soul shall be for his soul, and your people for his people.'"

[43] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went to his house, sullen and dejected, and he came to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v28) — Hebrew אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים, "the man of the Elohim", with the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Syria / the Syrians" → "Aram"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲרָם, the name, for the land and for its people (with singular or plural verbs: v20, 27, 28); "the Arameans" is not written.
- **"all his host … and horses, and chariots" → "all his force … and horse and chariots"** (v1, 19, 25) — Hebrew חַיִל, "force"; סוּס וָרֶכֶב, two collective singulars.
- **"and said unto him"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר, singular: Ben-hadad speaks through his messengers.
- **"thy children, even the goodliest" → "your sons, the good ones"** (v3, 5, 7) — Hebrew בָנֶיךָ הַטּוֹבִים.
- **"I am thine, and all that I have" → "yours am I, and all that is mine"** (v4).
- **"Although I have sent … Yet I will send" → "I sent … but … I will send"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew כִּי … כִּי אִם; the first כִּי only opens the speech. **"whatsoever is pleasant in thine eyes" → "all the desire of your eyes"** (v6) — Hebrew כָּל־מַחְמַד עֵינֶיךָ; the Septuagint has "their eyes" (from memory). **"put it in their hand, and take it away"** — the Hebrew has no "it".
- **"Mark, I pray you, and see how this man seeketh mischief … and I denied him not" → "Know, please, and see that this one is seeking evil … and I did not hold back from him"** (v7) — Hebrew רָעָה "evil"; זֶה "this one"; מָנַע.
- **"Hearken not unto him, nor consent" → "Do not listen, and do not be willing"** (v8) — Hebrew אָבָה.
- **"brought him word again" → "brought him back word"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיְשִׁבֻהוּ דָּבָר. "Your servant" is עֶבֶד, the word "slave" (v9, 32, 39, 40).
- **"The gods do so unto me, and more also, if the dust of Samaria shall suffice for handfuls for all the people that follow me" → "Thus may the gods do to me, and thus may they add: the dust of Samaria shall not suffice for handfuls for all the people who are at my feet"** (v10) — the oath formula of 19:2, with plural verbs; literally "if the dust of Samaria shall suffice": the "if" of an oath means "it shall not". אֲשֶׁר בְּרַגְלָי is "who are at my feet".
- **"Let not him that girdeth on his harness boast himself as he that putteth it off" → "Let not him who girds on boast like him who ungirds"** (v11) — Hebrew חֹגֵר … מְפַתֵּחַ, "one girding … one loosening": no object is named (sword or armor).
- **"in the pavilions" → "in the booths"** (v12, 16) — Hebrew סֻכּוֹת. **"Set yourselves in array. And they set themselves in array against the city" → "Set yourselves! And they set themselves against the city"** — Hebrew שִׂימוּ וַיָּשִׂימוּ, the bare verb "put" twice, with no object.
- **"a prophet" → "one prophet"** (v13) — Hebrew נָבִיא אֶחָד. **"I will deliver it" → "I am giving it"** — Hebrew הִנְנִי נֹתְנוֹ.
- **"the young men of the princes of the provinces"** (v14–19) — Hebrew נַעֲרֵי שָׂרֵי הַמְּדִינוֹת; נַעַר is "boy, young man, attendant". **"Who shall order the battle?" → "Who shall join the battle?"** — Hebrew מִי־יֶאְסֹר הַמִּלְחָמָה, literally "who shall bind the battle?": begin it, close with the enemy.
- **"drinking himself drunk"** (v16) — Hebrew שֹׁתֶה שִׁכּוֹר, "drinking, drunk". "Who were helping him" is a singular participle, "helping him".
- **"Whether they be come out for peace, take them alive" → "If for shalom they have come out, seize them alive … alive seize them"** (v18) — Hebrew לְשָׁלוֹם; the Hebrew turns the order of the two commands around.
- **"they slew every one his man" → "they struck down, each man his man"** (v20) — Hebrew הִכָּה, "strike", here of killing (v20, 21, 29, 36). **"on an horse with the horsemen" → "on a horse, with horsemen"** — Hebrew עַל־סוּס וּפָרָשִׁים, "on a horse and horsemen".
- **"smote the horses and chariots, and slew the Syrians with a great slaughter" → "struck the horse and the chariots, and he struck Aram with a great blow"** (v21) — one verb twice; מַכָּה גְדוֹלָה.
- **"mark, and see what thou doest" → "know and see what you will do"** (v22). **"at the return of the year"** (v22, 26) — Hebrew לִתְשׁוּבַת הַשָּׁנָה: the time of year when kings go out again (2 Sam 11:1).
- **"Their gods are gods of the hills" → "A god of mountains is their god"** (v23) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי הָרִים אֱלֹהֵיהֶם; the form can be "god" or "gods". The singular is taken from v28, where the saying is repeated with "Yahweh" and "he". **"in the plain" → "on the level ground"** (v23, 25) — Hebrew מִישׁוֹר, "level land" (of the tableland east of the Jordan in Deut 3:10). **"surely we shall be stronger"** — Hebrew אִם־לֹא, the oath form.
- **"captains" → "governors"** (v24) — Hebrew פַּחוֹת.
- **"number thee an army, like the army that thou hast lost" → "count for yourself a force like the force that fell from you"** (v25) — Hebrew מָנָה; הַנֹּפֵל מֵאוֹתָךְ.
- **"were all present" → "were sustained"** (v27) — Hebrew כָלְכְּלוּ, "were sustained": given food for the march. **"like two little flocks of kids"** — Hebrew חֲשִׂפֵי עִזִּים; חָשִׂיף is found only here and its meaning is uncertain ("small flocks", things stripped off). "Camped opposite them" is נֶגְדָּם.
- **"a man of God" → "the man of Elohim"** (v28) — with the article. **"The LORD is God of the hills, but he is not God of the valleys" → "A god of mountains is Yahweh, and he is not a god of valleys"**. "You shall know" is plural here, singular in v13.
- **"the battle was joined" → "the battle drew near"** (v29) — Hebrew וַתִּקְרַב. **"footmen" → "men on foot"**.
- **"into an inner chamber" → "a chamber within a chamber"** (v30) — Hebrew חֶדֶר בְּחָדֶר, "chamber in chamber". "The wall" is חוֹמָה, the city wall.
- **"merciful kings" → "kings of chesed"** (v31) — Hebrew מַלְכֵי חֶסֶד. **"ropes" → "cords"** — Hebrew חֲבָלִים. **"he will save thy life" → "he will let your soul live"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew יְחַיֶּה אֶת־נַפְשֶׁךָ; תְּחִי־נָא נַפְשִׁי.
- **"the men did diligently observe whether any thing would come from him, and did hastily catch it" → "the men were divining, and they hastened and caught it up from him"** (v33) — Hebrew יְנַחֲשׁוּ, "divine, look for an omen": they took his word "brother" as a sign. וַיַּחְלְטוּ הֲמִמֶּנּוּ: the verb חָלַט is found only here ("catch up, take as settled"), and the Masoretic division of the words gives literally "and they caught up: was it from him?"; the meaning is uncertain. **"bring him" → "take him"** — Hebrew קָחֻהוּ.
- **"And Benhadad said unto him … Then said Ahab, I will send thee away with this covenant" → "And he said to him … 'And I, with the covenant I will let you go.'"** (v34) — the Hebrew names neither speaker; the names that stood in the verse were supplied and are removed. The first words are Ben-hadad's, and "And I …" is commonly taken as Ahab's reply; it can also be read as the end of Ben-hadad's speech ("and as for me, let me go on these terms"). **"thou shalt make streets for thee in Damascus" → "streets you shall put for yourself"** — Hebrew חוּצוֹת, "streets": quarters for his traders. **"made a covenant" → "cut a covenant"**. שִׁלַּח is "send away, let go" (v34, 42).
- **"a certain man … said unto his neighbour in the word of the LORD" → "one man … said to his neighbor, by the word of Yahweh"** (v35) — Hebrew בִּדְבַר יְהוָה.
- **"Because thou hast not obeyed the voice of the LORD … a lion shall slay thee" → "Because you have not listened to the voice of Yahweh … the lion will strike you down"** (v36) — Hebrew הָאַרְיֵה, with the article. "Strike" is one verb through v35–37.
- **"so that in smiting he wounded him" → "striking and wounding"** (v37) — Hebrew הַכֵּה וּפָצֹעַ, two bare infinitives.
- **"waited for the king … with ashes upon his face" → "stood for the king … with a bandage over his eyes"** (v38, 41) — Hebrew אֲפֵר, found only in these two verses: a covering, a bandage. The letters are those of אֵפֶר "ashes", which older translations read (from memory).
- **"if by any means he be missing, then shall thy life be for his life" → "if he is surely missing, your soul shall be for his soul"** (v39, 42) — Hebrew הִפָּקֵד יִפָּקֵד, the verb with its infinitive; נַפְשְׁךָ תַּחַת נַפְשׁוֹ. "Keep" is שְׁמֹר.
- **"as thy servant was busy here and there, he was gone" → "your servant was doing here and there, and he was not"** (v40) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה הֵנָּה וָהֵנָּה; וְהוּא אֵינֶנּוּ. **"So shall thy judgment be; thyself hast decided it" → "So is your judgment; you yourself have decided"**.
- **"discerned him" → "recognized him"** (v41). "From over his eyes" is the reading tradition (qere).
- **"a man whom I appointed to utter destruction" → "the man of my devoting to destruction"** (v42) — Hebrew אִישׁ־חֶרְמִי, "the man of my *cherem*": חֵרֶם is what is given over to Yahweh beyond recall, as a rule by destroying it (Deut 7:26; Josh 6:17). The Hebrew has the noun with "my", not a verb; the narrative before has no command to devote Ben-hadad. "Out of hand" is מִיָּד, with no "your".
- **"heavy and displeased" → "sullen and dejected"** (v43; 21:4) — Hebrew סַר וְזָעֵף: סַר is found only here, in 21:4 and 21:5 ("sullen, ill-humored"); זָעֵף is "dejected, vexed" (Gen 40:6).

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew, "And he said to him, 'Thus says Ben-hadad:'" opens verse 3.
- **Ben-hadad** — Aramean dynastic name. Aram-Damascus is Israel's chronic northern enemy.
- **Thirty-two kings** — vassal rulers. Confederation common in ANE coalition warfare.
- **Two victories** — "You shall know that I am Yahweh" twice (v13, 28) — the formula of Ezekiel.
- **Mountains and valleys** (v23, 28) — the Arameans think Yahweh is a local mountain god. Their error becomes the reason given for their defeat.
- **Aphek** — likely Afeq east of the Sea of Galilee.
- **Wounded prophet parable** — same technique as Nathan with David (2 Sam 12). King sentences himself.
- **"Your soul for his soul"** — fulfilled at Ramoth-gilead (ch 22).
- **History** — a few years later Ahab and the king of Damascus fight together against Assyria at Qarqar (853 BC, in Shalmaneser III's annals).
