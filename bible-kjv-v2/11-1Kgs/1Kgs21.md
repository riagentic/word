# 1 Kings 21

[1] After these things, Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard in Jezreel, beside the palace of Ahab king of Samaria.

[2] Ahab said to Naboth, "Give me your vineyard, that I may have it for a vegetable garden, because it is near my house; I will give you in its place a better vineyard, or, if it seems good to you, I will give you its worth in silver."

[3] Naboth said to Ahab, "Yahweh forbid that I should give the inheritance of my fathers to you."

[4] Ahab came into his house resentful and sullen because of the word that Naboth the Jezreelite had spoken to him — for he had said, "I will not give you the inheritance of my fathers." He lay down on his bed, turned away his face, and would eat no bread.

[5] Jezebel his wife came to him and said, "Why is your spirit so sullen that you eat no bread?"

[6] He said to her, "Because I spoke to Naboth the Jezreelite and said to him, 'Give me your vineyard for silver, or if it please you I will give you another vineyard in its place'; and he answered, 'I will not give you my vineyard.'"

[7] Jezebel his wife said to him, "Is it you who now governs the kingdom of Israel? Arise, eat bread, and let your heart be cheerful. I will give you the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite."

[8] So she wrote letters in Ahab's name, sealed them with his seal, and sent the letters to the elders and to the nobles who lived with Naboth in his city.

[9] She wrote in the letters, saying, "Proclaim a fast, and seat Naboth at the head of the people.

[10] Seat two worthless men before him to testify against him, saying, 'You blasphemed *Elohim* and the king.' Then carry him out and stone him, that he may die."

[11] The men of his city — the elders and the nobles who lived in his city — did as Jezebel had sent to them, as it was written in the letters that she had sent to them.

[12] They proclaimed a fast and seated Naboth at the head of the people.

[13] The two worthless men came and sat before him; the worthless men testified against Naboth in the presence of the people, saying, "Naboth blasphemed *Elohim* and the king." Then they carried him out of the city and stoned him to death with stones.

[14] Then they sent to Jezebel, saying, "Naboth has been stoned; he is dead."

[15] When Jezebel heard that Naboth had been stoned and was dead, Jezebel said to Ahab, "Arise, take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, which he refused to give you for silver; for Naboth is not alive, but dead."

[16] When Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, Ahab arose to go down to the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, to take possession of it.

[17] The word of Yahweh came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,

[18] "Arise, go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, who is in Samaria; behold, he is in the vineyard of Naboth, where he has gone down to possess it.

[19] You shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says Yahweh: Have you killed and also taken possession?' And you shall speak to him, saying, 'Thus says Yahweh: In the place where the dogs licked up the blood of Naboth, the dogs shall lick up your blood — yes, yours.'"

[20] Ahab said to Elijah, "Have you found me, O my enemy?" He answered, "I have found you, because you have sold yourself to do what is evil in the eyes of Yahweh.

[21] 'Behold, I will bring evil upon you; I will sweep you away, and will cut off from Ahab every male, both bond and free in Israel.

[22] I will make your house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah, because of the provocation with which you have provoked me to anger, and have made Israel to sin.'

[23] Concerning Jezebel also Yahweh spoke, saying, 'The dogs shall eat Jezebel by the rampart of Jezreel.'

[24] Whoever of Ahab dies in the city, the dogs shall eat; and whoever dies in the field, the birds of the sky shall eat."

[25] Indeed there was none like Ahab, who sold himself to do what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, whom Jezebel his wife incited.

[26] He acted most abominably in going after idols according to all that the Amorites did, whom Yahweh cast out before the children of Israel.

[27] When Ahab heard those words, he tore his clothes, put sackcloth on his flesh, fasted, lay in sackcloth, and went about subdued.

[28] The word of Yahweh came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,

[29] "Do you see how Ahab has humbled himself before me? Because he has humbled himself before me, I will not bring the evil in his days; but in his son's days I will bring the evil upon his house."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"The Lord forbid" → "Yahweh forbid"** (v3).
- **"money" → "silver"** (v2, 6, 15) — *kesef*.
- **"set on high" → "seat at the head"** (v9, 12).
- **"him that pisseth against the wall, and him that is shut up and left" → "every male, both bond and free"** (v21) — euphemistic idiom for any male, from servants to freemen.
- **"by the wall of Jezreel" → "by the rampart of Jezreel"** (v23) — Hebrew *chel*; outer defensive ring. (Some MSS *cheleq* = "plot of ground," picked up in 2 Kgs 9:10. MT preserves both readings across ch 21/2 Kgs 9.)
- **"went softly" → "went about subdued"** (v27) — *halak at*; moving in humbled manner.

### Notes
- **Ancestral inheritance (*nachalah*)** — Naboth refuses sale on Torah principle (Lev 25:23, Num 36:7). Land returned to clan at Jubilee; cannot be permanently alienated.
- **Judicial murder** — Jezebel uses Torah legal form (blasphemy → stoning, Lev 24) to commit injustice. Perversion of law by power.
- **Ahab and Jezebel's roles** — Ahab sulks; Jezebel acts. Jezebel is the more active agent, but Ahab is held responsible (sold himself).
- **"Have you found me, O my enemy?"** — Ahab instantly assumes Elijah brings only condemnation. Shows Ahab's awareness of his own guilt.
- **Naboth's blood** — licked up by dogs at Samaria (1 Kgs 22:38) or Jezreel (2 Kgs 9:26); the narrative has layers of fulfillment.
- **Ahab's repentance** — partial but genuine; Yahweh postpones (not cancels) judgment. Judgment falls on Jehoram (2 Kgs 9).
- **"In his son's days"** — Joram / Jehoram dies in Naboth's very field (2 Kgs 9:21–26). Exact fulfillment.
