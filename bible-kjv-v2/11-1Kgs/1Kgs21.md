## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"The Lord forbid" → "Yahweh forbid"** (v3).
- **"money" → "silver"** (v2, 6, 15) — *kesef*.
- **"set on high" → "seat at the head"** (v9, 12).
- **"him that pisseth against the wall, and him that is shut up and left" → "every male, both bond and free"** (v21) — euphemistic idiom for any male, from servants to freemen.
- **"by the wall of Jezreel" → "by the rampart of Jezreel"** (v23) — Hebrew *chel*; outer defensive ring. (Some MSS *cheleq* = "plot of ground," picked up in 2 Kgs 9:10. MT preserves both readings across ch 21/2 Kgs 9.)
- **"went softly" → "went about subdued"** (v27) — *halak at*; moving in humbled manner.

### Notes
- **Ancestral inheritance (*nachalah*)** — Naboth refuses sale on Torah principle (Lev 25:23, Num 36:7). Land returned to clan at Jubilee; cannot be permanently alienated.
- **Judicial murder** — Jezebel uses Torah legal form (blasphemy → stoning, Lev 24) to commit injustice. Perversion of law by power.
- **Ahab and Jezebel's roles** — Ahab sulks; Jezebel acts. Jezebel is the more active agent, but Ahab is held responsible (sold himself).
- **"Have you found me, O my enemy?"** — Ahab instantly assumes Elijah brings only condemnation. Shows Ahab's awareness of his own guilt.
- **Naboth's blood** — licked up by dogs at Samaria (1 Kgs 22:38) or Jezreel (2 Kgs 9:26); the narrative has layers of fulfillment.
- **Ahab's repentance** — partial but genuine; Yahweh postpones (not cancels) judgment. Judgment falls on Jehoram (2 Kgs 9).
- **"In his son's days"** — Joram / Jehoram dies in Naboth's very field (2 Kgs 9:21–26). Exact fulfillment.
