# 1 Kings 17

[1] Elijah the Tishbite, of the settlers of Gilead, said to Ahab, "As Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel lives, before whom I stand, there shall be neither dew nor rain these years, except by my word."

[2] The word of Yahweh came to him, saying,

[3] "Go from here, turn eastward, and hide yourself by the Wadi Cherith, east of the Jordan.

[4] You shall drink from the wadi, and I have commanded the ravens to feed you there."

[5] So he went and did according to the word of Yahweh; he went and lived by the Wadi Cherith, east of the Jordan.

[6] The ravens brought him bread and meat in the morning, and bread and meat in the evening; and he drank from the wadi.

[7] After a while the wadi dried up, because there had been no rain in the land.

[8] The word of Yahweh came to him, saying,

[9] "Arise, go to Zarephath, which belongs to Sidon, and live there. Behold, I have commanded a widow there to sustain you."

[10] So he arose and went to Zarephath. When he came to the gate of the city, behold, a widow was there gathering sticks; he called to her and said, "Bring me, please, a little water in a vessel, that I may drink."

[11] As she was going to bring it, he called to her and said, "Bring me, please, a morsel of bread in your hand."

[12] She said, "As Yahweh your *Elohim* lives, I have no bread, only a handful of flour in a jar and a little oil in a jug; behold, I am gathering a couple of sticks, that I may go in and prepare it for me and my son, that we may eat it and die."

[13] Elijah said to her, "Do not fear; go and do as you have said. But first make me a little cake of it and bring it out to me, and afterward make for yourself and for your son.

[14] For thus says Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel: 'The jar of flour shall not be spent, neither shall the jug of oil fail, until the day that Yahweh sends rain upon the face of the earth.'"

[15] She went and did according to the word of Elijah; she and he and her household ate for many days.

[16] The jar of flour was not spent, neither did the jug of oil fail, according to the word of Yahweh that he spoke by Elijah.

[17] After these things, the son of the woman, the mistress of the house, fell sick; his sickness was so severe that there was no breath left in him.

[18] She said to Elijah, "What have you against me, O man of *Elohim*? Have you come to me to bring my iniquity to remembrance and to kill my son?"

[19] He said to her, "Give me your son." He took him out of her bosom, carried him up to the upper room where he was staying, and laid him upon his own bed.

[20] He cried out to Yahweh and said, "O Yahweh my *Elohim*, have you also brought evil upon the widow with whom I sojourn, by killing her son?"

[21] He stretched himself out upon the child three times, and cried out to Yahweh and said, "O Yahweh my *Elohim*, let this child's soul return into him."

[22] Yahweh listened to the voice of Elijah; the soul of the child returned into him, and he revived.

[23] Elijah took the child, brought him down from the upper room into the house, and delivered him to his mother; Elijah said, "See, your son lives."

[24] The woman said to Elijah, "Now by this I know that you are a man of *Elohim*, and that the word of Yahweh in your mouth is truth."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"brook" → "Wadi"** (v3, 4, 5, 6, 7) — *nachal* = seasonal stream, not a year-round brook.
- **"barrel of meal" → "jar of flour"** (v12, 14, 16) — Hebrew *kad* = clay jar.
- **"cruse of oil" → "jug of oil"** (v12, 14, 16) — *tsappachat*.
- **"loft / chamber" → "upper room"** (v19, 23) — *aliyah*.
- **"Zidon" → "Sidon"** (v9).

### Notes
- **Elijah appears abruptly** — no genealogy, no introduction. Pure prophetic figure.
- **Tishbite of Gilead** — Transjordanian, outsider to court. Some propose *tishbi* = "of Tishbe"; others read as "settler."
- **Drought as polemic** — Baal is the storm-god. Yahweh withholding rain is direct attack on Baal's claimed domain. The Elijah cycle is Yahweh-vs-Baal contest.
- **Ravens feed him** — *orvim*; some later rabbinic readings emend to "Arabs" (same consonants), but MT ravens is the natural sense.
- **Zarephath** — in Phoenicia, Jezebel's home territory. Yahweh sustains his prophet inside Baal-worshipping heartland using a gentile widow. Jesus cites this in Luke 4:25–26.
- **Resurrection of the widow's son** — first resurrection in the Bible. Elisha will later repeat (2 Kgs 4).
- **"Man of *Elohim*" / "word of Yahweh is truth"** — Gentile confession; foreshadows prophetic reach beyond Israel.
