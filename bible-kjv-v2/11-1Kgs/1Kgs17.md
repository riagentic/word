# 1 Kings 17

[1] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ the ⟦tishbite|Tishbi|Tishbite⟧, of the settlers of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, said to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ lives, before whom I stand, there shall not be dew or rain these years, except according to my word."

[2] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to him, saying,

[3] "Go from here and turn yourself eastward and conceal yourself in the wadi ⟦cherith|Kerit|Cherith⟧, which faces the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[4] And it shall be that from the wadi you shall drink, and the ravens I have commanded to sustain you there."

[5] And he went and did according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he went and dwelt in the wadi ⟦cherith|Kerit|Cherith⟧, which faces the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[6] And the ravens were bringing him bread and flesh in the morning and bread and flesh in the evening, and from the wadi he would drink.

[7] And it came to pass at the end of days that the wadi dried out, for there was no rain in the land.

[8] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to him, saying,

[9] "Arise, go to ⟦zarephath|Tzarfat|Zarephath⟧, which belongs to ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, and dwell there. Behold, I have commanded a widow woman there to sustain you."

[10] And he arose and went to ⟦zarephath|Tzarfat|Zarephath⟧ and came to the entrance of the city, and behold, a widow woman was there, gathering wood. And he called to her and said, "Take for me, please, a little water in the vessel, that I may drink."

[11] And she went to take it, and he called to her and said, "Take for me, please, a bit of bread in your hand."

[12] And she said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ lives, I have no cake, except a palmful of meal in the jar and a little oil in the jug. And behold, I am gathering two sticks of wood, and I will go in and make it for me and for my son, and we will eat it and die."

[13] And ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ said to her, "Do not fear. Go in, do according to your word; only make for me from there a small cake first and bring it out to me, and for yourself and for your son you shall make afterward.

[14] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'The jar of meal shall not be finished, and the jug of oil shall not lack, until the day that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gives rain on the face of the ground.'"

[15] And she went and did according to the word of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, and she ate, she and he and her house, for days.

[16] The jar of meal was not finished, and the jug of oil did not lack, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he spoke by the hand of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧.

[17] And it came to pass after these things that the son of the woman, the mistress of the house, became sick, and his sickness was very strong, until no breath remained in him.

[18] And she said to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, "What have I to do with you, man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? You have come to me to bring my iniquity to remembrance and to put my son to death."

[19] And he said to her, "Give me your son." And he took him from her bosom and brought him up to the upper room where he was dwelling, and he laid him on his bed.

[20] And he called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, have you done evil also to the widow with whom I am sojourning, to put her son to death?"

[21] And he stretched himself upon the child three times, and he called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, let the soul of this child return, please, into his midst."

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ listened to the voice of ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, and the soul of the child returned into his midst, and he lived.

[24] And the woman said to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, "Now this I know, that you are a man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in your mouth is truth."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. "Man of *Elohim*" has the article in v18 (אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים) and none in v24.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"of the inhabitants of Gilead" → "of the settlers of Gilead"** (v1) — Hebrew מִתֹּשָׁבֵי, of תּוֹשָׁב "settler", one who lives in a place without being native to it. The letters are those of "the Tishbite" (הַתִּשְׁבִּי); with other vowels they are read "from Tishbe of Gilead", and so the Septuagint is said to have it (from memory). The Masoretic pointing is translated.
- **"there shall not be dew nor rain these years, but according to my word" → "there shall not be dew or rain these years, except according to my word"** (v1) — the Hebrew oath: חַי־יְהוָה … אִם־יִהְיֶה, literally "As Yahweh lives … if there shall be"; the "if" of an oath means a sworn "not". "Before whom I stand" is a perfect, "I have taken my stand". לְפִי דְבָרִי is literally "at the mouth of my word". "Rain" in v1 is מָטָר, in v7, 14 גֶּשֶׁם.
- **"Get thee hence, and turn thee eastward, and hide thyself by the brook Cherith, that is before Jordan" → "Go from here and turn yourself eastward and conceal yourself in the wadi Cherith, which faces the Jordan"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew וּפָנִיתָ לְּךָ "turn for yourself"; קֵדְמָה "eastward", literally "to the front"; נִסְתַּר "conceal oneself"; בְּנַחַל "in the wadi", a stream bed that runs only after rain; עַל־פְּנֵי "upon the face of, facing": commonly taken as "east of".
- **"I have commanded the ravens to feed thee" → "the ravens I have commanded to sustain you"** (v4, 9) — Hebrew כִּלְכֵּל "sustain"; the object stands first.
- **"brought him bread and flesh"** (v6) — Hebrew בָּשָׂר "flesh": meat. The verbs tell what went on day by day ("were bringing … would drink").
- **"after a while, that the brook dried up" → "at the end of days that the wadi dried out"** (v7) — Hebrew מִקֵּץ יָמִים, literally "from the end of days": after some time (Gen 4:3); יָבֵשׁ "be dried out".
- **"I have commanded a widow woman there to sustain thee"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה אַלְמָנָה "a woman, a widow". **"dwell"** — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"the gate of the city" → "the entrance of the city"** (v10) — Hebrew פֶּתַח. **"gathering of sticks" → "gathering wood"** — Hebrew עֵצִים; in v12 "two sticks of wood" is שְׁנַיִם עֵצִים "two woods".
- **"Fetch me, I pray thee, a little water in a vessel" → "Take for me, please, a little water in the vessel"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew לָקַח "take" (here: fetch); נָא "please"; בַּכְּלִי has the article. "It" in v11 is supplied.
- **"a morsel of bread" → "a bit of bread"** (v11) — Hebrew פַּת.
- **"I have not a cake, but an handful of meal in a barrel, and a little oil in a cruse" → "I have no cake, except a palmful of meal in the jar and a little oil in the jug"** (v12, 14, 16) — again the oath, אִם־יֶשׁ־לִי "if I have". מָעוֹג is found only here in this sense: something baked, from the root of עֻגָה "cake" (v13). מְלֹא כַף "the fullness of a palm"; קֶמַח "meal", ordinary flour; כַּד a clay jar; צַפַּחַת a small jug (1 Sam 26:11).
- **"that I may go in and dress it" → "and I will go in and make it"** (v12) — Hebrew וַעֲשִׂיתִיהוּ "and I will make it"; "and … and … and", not "that".
- **"go and do as thou hast said: but make me thereof a little cake first" → "Go in, do according to your word; only make for me from there a small cake first"** (v13) — Hebrew בֹּאִי "come, go in"; כִדְבָרֵךְ "according to your word"; אַךְ "only"; מִשָּׁם "from there".
- **"The barrel of meal shall not waste, neither shall the cruse of oil fail, until the day that the LORD sendeth rain upon the earth" → "The jar of meal shall not be finished, and the jug of oil shall not lack, until the day that Yahweh gives rain on the face of the ground"** (v14, 16) — Hebrew כָּלָה "be finished, come to an end"; חָסֵר "lack"; תֵּת "give" (the written text has the form תתן); עַל־פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה "on the face of the ground".
- **"she, and he, and her house, did eat many days" → "she ate, she and he and her house, for days"** (v15) — the verb is singular and feminine. The reading tradition has "she and he", the written text "he and she". יָמִים "days": for some time; "many" is not written.
- **"which he spake by Elijah" → "that he spoke by the hand of Elijah"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּיַד.
- **"fell sick; and his sickness was so sore, that there was no breath left in him" → "became sick, and his sickness was very strong, until no breath remained in him"** (v17) — Hebrew חָזָק מְאֹד "very strong"; עַד אֲשֶׁר "until"; נוֹתַר "remain"; נְשָׁמָה "breath". בַּעֲלַת הַבָּיִת "the mistress of the house" is the feminine of בַּעַל "owner".
- **"What have I to do with thee, O thou man of God? art thou come unto me to call my sin to remembrance, and to slay my son?" → "What have I to do with you, man of *Elohim*? You have come to me to bring my iniquity to remembrance and to put my son to death."** (v18) — Hebrew מַה־לִּי וָלָךְ, literally "What to me and to you"; no "O". The second sentence has no mark of a question in the Hebrew; it may be heard as one. עָוֺן "iniquity"; הַזְכִּיר, the causative of "remember"; הֵמִית "put to death".
- **"carried him up into a loft, where he abode, and laid him upon his own bed" → "brought him up to the upper room where he was dwelling, and he laid him on his bed"** (v19) — Hebrew הֶעֱלָה "bring up"; עֲלִיָּה, a room on the roof; יֹשֵׁב "dwelling"; "own" is not written.
- **"he cried unto the LORD" → "he called to Yahweh"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew קָרָא "call". **"O LORD my God" → "Yahweh my *Elohim*"** — the Hebrew has no "O".
- **"hast thou also brought evil upon the widow with whom I sojourn, by slaying her son?" → "have you done evil also to the widow with whom I am sojourning, to put her son to death?"** (v20) — Hebrew הֲרֵעוֹתָ "have you done evil", with עַל "upon"; the widow stands first in the Hebrew.
- **"he stretched himself upon the child"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיִּתְמֹדֵד, literally "he measured himself": he laid himself out at full length.
- **"let this child's soul come into him again" → "let the soul of this child return, please, into his midst"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; שׁוּב "return"; נָא "please"; עַל־קִרְבּוֹ, literally "upon his inward part".
- **"the LORD heard the voice of Elijah … and he revived" → "Yahweh listened to the voice of Elijah … and he lived"** (v22) — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל; וַיֶּחִי "and he lived".
- **"delivered him unto his mother" → "gave him to his mother"** (v23) — Hebrew נָתַן. **"thy son liveth"** — Hebrew חַי בְּנֵךְ "alive is your son".
- **"Now by this I know" → "Now this I know"** (v24) — Hebrew עַתָּה זֶה יָדַעְתִּי; זֶה "this" may also only strengthen "now".

### Notes
- **Elijah appears abruptly** — no genealogy, no introduction. Pure prophetic figure.
- **Tishbite of Gilead** — Transjordanian, outsider to court. Some propose *tishbi* = "of Tishbe"; others read as "settler."
- **Drought as polemic** — Baal is the storm-god. Yahweh withholding rain is direct attack on Baal's claimed domain. The Elijah cycle is Yahweh-vs-Baal contest.
- **Ravens sustain him** — *orvim*; some later readings take the same consonants as "Arabs" (from memory), but the Masoretic "ravens" is the natural sense.
- **Zarephath** — in Phoenicia, Jezebel's home territory. Yahweh sustains his prophet inside Baal-worshipping heartland using a gentile widow. Jesus cites this in Luke 4:25–26.
- **The widow's son lives again** — the first such account in the Bible. Elisha will later repeat (2 Kgs 4).
- **"Man of *Elohim*" / "word of Yahweh is truth"** — Gentile confession; foreshadows prophetic reach beyond Israel.
