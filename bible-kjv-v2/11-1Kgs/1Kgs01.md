## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17, 30, 36, 47, 48) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"stricken in years" → "advanced in days"** (v1) — Hebrew בָּא בַּיָּמִים, literally "gone into the days". **"clothes" → "garments"**. **"but he gat no heat" → "and he did not get warm"** — Hebrew וְלֹא יִחַם לוֹ, literally "and it would not grow warm for him".
- **"Let there be sought" → "Let them seek"** (v2) — Hebrew יְבַקְשׁוּ, an active plural with no subject named. **"a young virgin" → "a girl, a virgin"** — Hebrew נַעֲרָה בְתוּלָה, two nouns. **"let her cherish him" → "be an attendant to him"** (v2, 4) — Hebrew סֹכֶנֶת, a participle found only here in the feminine (the masculine סֹכֵן is the "steward" of Isa 22:15): one who is of use, who attends; the exact service is not said. **"that my lord the king may get heat" → "and my lord the king will be warm"** — literally "and it will be warm for my lord the king". The servants speak of the king ("my lord the king") and to him ("your bosom") in one sentence.
- **"a fair damsel throughout all the coasts of Israel" → "a beautiful girl in all the territory of Israel"** (v3) — Hebrew נַעֲרָה "girl"; גְּבוּל "border", here the land within it.
- **"very fair" → "exceedingly beautiful"** (v4) — Hebrew יָפָה עַד־מְאֹד, "beautiful up to very". **"knew her not"** — Hebrew יָדַע, "know", of a man and a woman as in Gen 4:1; nothing is added.
- **"exalted himself … I will be king" → "was exalting himself … I will reign"** (v5) — Hebrew מִתְנַשֵּׂא, a participle: a lasting bearing; אֶמְלֹךְ, the verb "reign". **"prepared him chariots" → "made for himself chariots"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ לוֹ, as of Absalom in 2 Sam 15:1; רֶכֶב is a collective, "chariots" or "a chariot". **"to run before him" → "running before him"** — a participle.
- **"had not displeased him at any time" → "had not grieved him in all his days"** (v6) — Hebrew עֲצָבוֹ, "pained, grieved him"; מִיָּמָיו, literally "from his days". **"a very goodly man" → "very good in form"** — Hebrew טוֹב־תֹּאַר, with no word "man". **"his mother bare him" → "him she had borne"** — Hebrew וְאֹתוֹ יָלְדָה: the verb is feminine and no subject is written. His mother Haggith (v5) is meant, or the verb is impersonal ("one bore him"); "his mother" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"he conferred with Joab" → "his words were with Joab"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ דְבָרָיו עִם. **"they following Adonijah helped him" → "they helped after Adonijah"** — Hebrew וַיַּעְזְרוּ אַחֲרֵי, word for word: they gave their help as his followers.
- **"Adonijah"** (v5, 7, 18) — the name is written here in its short form אֲדֹנִיָּה, elsewhere in the chapter אֲדֹנִיָּהוּ; the verse keeps one form. So also Jonathan (v42, 43), written here יוֹנָתָן.
- **"the mighty men which belonged to David" → "the mighty ones who were David's"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew הַגִּבּוֹרִים אֲשֶׁר לְדָוִד.
- **"slew sheep and oxen and fat cattle" → "sacrificed flock and herd and fatlings"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּזְבַּח, "sacrificed": a sacrificial feast; צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats), בָּקָר "herd" (cattle), מְרִיא "fatling", all three collective singulars. In v19, 25 ("slain oxen and fat cattle and sheep") the words are שׁוֹר "ox", מְרִיא and צֹאן, again singular in form: "oxen and fatlings and flock". **"by the stone of Zoheleth, which is by Enrogel" → "at the stone of Zoheleth, which is beside En-rogel"** — two prepositions, עִם "with, at" and אֵצֶל "beside"; הַזֹּחֶלֶת has the article ("the stone of the Zoheleth": perhaps "the crawling one", a serpent; the meaning is not certain).
- **"called"** (v9, 10, 19, 25, 26) and **"guests" → "called ones"** (v41, 49) — Hebrew קָרָא "call" and הַקְּרֻאִים "the called": those bidden to the feast.
- **"doth reign" → "has begun to reign"** (v11, 13, 18) — Hebrew מָלַךְ, the perfect of the verb "reign", here of the beginning: he has become king.
- **"let me, I pray thee, give thee counsel" → "let me counsel you, please, with counsel"** (v12) — Hebrew אִיעָצֵךְ נָא עֵצָה, verb and noun of one root. **"that thou mayest save thine own life" → "rescue your soul"** — Hebrew וּמַלְּטִי אֶת־נַפְשֵׁךְ, an imperative, literally "and make your soul escape" (as 2 Sam 19:5); נֶפֶשׁ is "soul" both times.
- **"thine handmaid" → "your slave woman"** (v13, 17) — Hebrew אָמָה. **"Assuredly Solomon"** — Hebrew כִּי, which here only opens the words quoted.
- **"confirm thy words" → "fill up your words"** (v14) — Hebrew וּמִלֵּאתִי אֶת־דְּבָרָיִךְ, literally "and I will fill your words": make them full, confirm them. The same verb is "fulfill" in 2:27.
- **"bowed, and did obeisance" → "bowed her head and bowed down"** (v16, 31) — Hebrew קָדַד and הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה; the second verb also in v23, 47, 53. **"What wouldest thou?" → "What ails you?"** (v16) — Hebrew מַה־לָּךְ, literally "What to you?", as Judg 1:14.
- **"and now, my lord the king, thou knowest it not"** (v18) — the Masoretic Text has וְעַתָּה "and now" twice. Many Hebrew manuscripts and the old versions read the second as וְאַתָּה "and you" (from memory); the printed text is kept.
- **"captain of the host" → "commander of the host"** (v19, 25) — Hebrew שַׂר הַצָּבָא; שַׂר is the word "prince".
- **"that thou shouldest tell them" → "to tell them"** (v20) — Hebrew לְהַגִּיד.
- **"Otherwise it shall come to pass … shall sleep with his fathers … shall be counted offenders" → "And it shall be … lies with his fathers … shall be sinners"** (v21) — Hebrew וְהָיָה, with no "otherwise"; שָׁכַב "lie"; חַטָּאִים "sinners", with no "counted": they will stand as the guilty ones.
- **"with his face to the ground" → "on his face to the earth"** (v23) — Hebrew עַל־אַפָּיו אָרְצָה; אַפַּיִם is literally "nostrils".
- **"hast thou said" → "you have said"** (v24) — the Hebrew has no question word: אַתָּה אָמַרְתָּ, "you, you have said". It is commonly read as a question; the verse leaves the words as they stand, and the question comes in v27.
- **"God save king Adonijah" → "May King Adonijah live!"** (v25, 34, 39) — Hebrew יְחִי הַמֶּלֶךְ, "may the king live": no "God", no "save", no "long".
- **"Is this thing done by my lord the king" → "Is it from my lord the king that this thing has come to be"** (v27) — Hebrew אִם מֵאֵת … נִהְיָה. **"thy servant"** — the reading tradition (qere) עַבְדְּךָ, singular; the written text (ketiv) has "your servants".
- **"that hath redeemed my soul" → "who has ransomed my soul"** (v29) — Hebrew פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem").
- **"Even as I sware … even so will I certainly do" → "surely as I swore … surely so I will do"** (v30) — the Hebrew has כִּי twice: the particle that carries what is sworn, taken up again before the main clause.
- **"did reverence to the king" → "bowed down to the king"** (v31) — the same verb as in v16. **"with her face to the earth" → "face to the earth"** — Hebrew אַפַּיִם אֶרֶץ.
- **"mine own mule" → "the she-mule that is mine"** (v33, 38, 44) — Hebrew פִּרְדָּה, the female mule; הַפִּרְדָּה אֲשֶׁר־לִי.
- **"blow ye with the trumpet" → "blow on the shofar"** (v34, 39, 41) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָר, the ram's horn.
- **"that he may come and sit" → "he shall come in and sit"** (v35) — plain future forms. **"for he shall be king in my stead" → "and he shall reign in my place"**. **"I have appointed him to be ruler" → "him I have commanded to be leader"** — Hebrew צִוִּיתִי "I have commanded"; נָגִיד "leader", the word of 1 Sam 9:16; 13:14.
- **"the LORD God of my lord the king say so too" → "So may Yahweh, the Elohim of my lord the king, say"** (v36) — Hebrew כֵּן יֹאמַר יְהוָה, with no "too".
- **"even so be he with Solomon" → "so shall he be with Solomon"** (v37) — the reading tradition (qere) יִהְיֶה "he shall be"; the written text (ketiv) יהי is the wish "may he be". The next verb, וִיגַדֵּל, is a wish in both.
- **"the Cherethites, and the Pelethites"** (v38, 44) — Hebrew הַכְּרֵתִי וְהַפְּלֵתִי, both singular in form with the article, of the whole body: David's guard (2 Sam 8:18).
- **"brought him to Gihon" → "led him to Gihon"** (v38) — Hebrew וַיֹּלִכוּ, "made him go"; עַל־גִּחוֹן, literally "upon Gihon" (v33 has אֶל "to").
- **"an horn of oil out of the tabernacle" → "the horn of oil from the tent"** (v39) — Hebrew אֶת־קֶרֶן הַשֶּׁמֶן מִן־הָאֹהֶל, with the article each time: the tent in which the ark stood (2 Sam 6:17). Which tent is not said here.
- **"piped with pipes, and rejoiced with great joy, so that the earth rent with the sound of them" → "were playing on flutes and were glad with great gladness, and the earth was split by their sound"** (v40) — Hebrew מְחַלְּלִים בַּחֲלִלִים, verb and noun of one root (חָלִיל "flute"); שְׂמֵחִים שִׂמְחָה, again one root; וַתִּבָּקַע "was split", said as it stands; קוֹל is "voice" or "sound".
- **"as they had made an end of eating" → "and they had finished eating"** (v41) — Hebrew וְהֵם כִּלּוּ לֶאֱכֹל. **"Wherefore is this noise of the city being in an uproar?" → "Why is the sound of the town in uproar?"** — Hebrew מַדּוּעַ קוֹל־הַקִּרְיָה הוֹמָה, literally "Why the sound of the town, roaring?"; קִרְיָה "town" (v41, 45) is another word than עִיר "city".
- **"a valiant man" → "a man of ability"** (v42) and **"a worthy man" → "a son of ability"** (v52) — Hebrew אִישׁ חַיִל, בֶּן־חַיִל: חַיִל is ability, worth, strength.
- **"Verily" → "Truly"** (v43) — Hebrew אֲבָל, in the older books a word of assurance ("truly, indeed"), here turning aside what Adonijah has said: "No, rather". **"hath made Solomon king"** — Hebrew הִמְלִיךְ, "made to reign".
- **"so that the city rang again" → "and the town has resounded"** (v45) — Hebrew וַתֵּהֹם, from another root than "in uproar" in v41. **"noise" → "sound"** — Hebrew קוֹל, as in v40, 41.
- **"sitteth on the throne of the kingdom" → "has sat down on the throne of the kingship"** (v46) — Hebrew יָשַׁב, a perfect; מְלוּכָה "kingship".
- **"God make the name of Solomon better"** (v47) — the reading tradition (qere) אֱלֹהִים "Elohim"; the written text (ketiv) has "your Elohim". Hebrew יֵיטֵב, "make good": the verb of "good". **"bowed himself upon the bed" → "bowed down on the bed"** — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּב "bed", what one lies on.
- **"Blessed be the LORD" → "Blessed is Yahweh"** (v48) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה, with no verb. **"hath given one to sit" → "has given … one sitting"** — Hebrew יֹשֵׁב, a participle; no name and no "son" is written. **"mine eyes even seeing it" → "and my eyes are seeing it"** — "it" is supplied.
- **"were afraid" → "trembled"** (v49) — Hebrew חָרַד. **"every man his way" → "each man on his way"**.
- **"feared because of Solomon"** (v50) — Hebrew יָרֵא מִפְּנֵי, literally "feared from the face of"; in v51 the verb has a plain object, "fears King Solomon". **"caught hold on" → "held fast to"** (v50) and **"taken hold of"** (v51) — two verbs, הֶחֱזִיק בְּ and אָחַז בְּ.
- **"swear unto me to day that he will not slay his servant" → "swear to me today that he will not put his servant to death"** (v51) — the oath formula, אִם יָמִית, literally "if he puts to death": the "if" of an oath means "he will not". כַיּוֹם is literally "as the day": "today" or "first of all" (as Gen 25:31).
- **"If he will shew himself a worthy man, there shall not an hair of him fall to the earth" → "If he will be a son of ability, not a hair of his shall fall to the earth"** (v52) — Hebrew אִם יִהְיֶה, "if he will be"; לֹא־יִפֹּל מִשַּׂעֲרָתוֹ אָרְצָה, literally "there shall not fall from his hair to the earth". **"wickedness" → "evil"** — Hebrew רָעָה.
- **"from the altar" → "from upon the altar"** (v53) — Hebrew מֵעַל הַמִּזְבֵּחַ.

### Notes
- **Books of Kings** — originally one book; divided by LXX, Vulgate, and early Hebrew editions. Chronicle of Israelite-Judahite monarchies from Solomon to exile.
- **David's aging** — pathos of a great king's decline. Abishag the Shunammite attends him.
- **Adonijah's coup** — fourth son, eldest surviving after Amnon, Chileab, Absalom. Claims throne in David's waning days. Gathers Joab and Abiathar as supporters; bypasses Solomon.
- **Nathan and Bathsheba** — counter-coup via David's oath. Nathan the prophet, through whom Solomon was named Jedidiah (2 Sam 12:25), now secures his throne.
- **Solomon anointed at Gihon** — the spring below the city of David. Rite of legitimate kingship.
- **Adonijah's refuge at the altar** — horns grasped seeking asylum (Ex 21:14). Solomon grants conditional mercy.
