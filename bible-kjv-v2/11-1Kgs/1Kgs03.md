# 1 Kings 3

[1] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made a marriage alliance with ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; he took ⟦pharaoh|Paroh's|Pharaoh's⟧ daughter and brought her into the city of David, until he had finished building his own house, the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the wall around ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] The people were sacrificing at the high places, however, because no house had been built for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ until those days.

[3] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ loved ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, walking in the statutes of David his father; only he sacrificed and burned incense at the high places.

[4] The king went to ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ to sacrifice there, for that was the great high place. ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ offered a thousand burnt offerings on that altar.

[5] At ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appeared to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in a dream by night; ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Ask what I shall give you."

[6] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ said, "You have shown great lovingkindness to your servant David my father, because he walked before you in faithfulness, in righteousness, and in uprightness of heart toward you; you have kept for him this great lovingkindness, in that you have given him a son to sit on his throne, as it is this day.

[7] Now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you have made your servant king in the place of David my father; I am only a little child — I do not know how to go out or come in.

[8] Your servant is in the midst of your people whom you have chosen, a great people, who cannot be numbered or counted for multitude.

[9] Give your servant therefore a listening heart to judge your people, that I may discern between good and evil; for who is able to judge this great people of yours?"

[10] It pleased ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had asked this thing.

[11] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said to him, "Because you have asked this thing and have not asked for yourself long life, nor have asked riches for yourself, nor have asked the life of your enemies, but have asked for yourself understanding to discern what is right —

[12] behold, I have done according to your word; behold, I have given you a wise and understanding heart, so that there has been none like you before you, and none like you shall arise after you.

[13] I have also given you what you have not asked — both riches and honor — so that there will not be any among the kings like you, all your days.

[14] If you walk in my ways, keeping my statutes and my commandments, as your father David walked, then I will lengthen your days."

[15] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ awoke, and behold, it was a dream. He came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, stood before the ark of the covenant of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, and offered burnt offerings, offered peace offerings, and made a feast for all his servants.

[16] Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood before him.

[17] One woman said, "Oh, my lord, this woman and I live in the same house; I gave birth to a child with her in the house.

[18] On the third day after I gave birth, this woman also gave birth. We were together; there was no one else with us in the house — only the two of us.

[19] This woman's son died in the night, because she lay on him.

[20] She arose at midnight and took my son from beside me, while your maidservant slept, and laid him in her bosom; her dead child she laid in my bosom.

[21] When I rose in the morning to nurse my child, behold, he was dead; but when I looked at him closely in the morning, behold, it was not my son that I had borne."

[22] The other woman said, "No, but the living one is my son, and the dead one is your son." And this one said, "No, but the dead one is your son, and the living one is my son." Thus they spoke before the king.

[23] Then the king said, "The one says, 'This is my son who is alive, and your son is the dead one'; the other says, 'No, but your son is dead, and my son is alive.'"

[24] The king said, "Bring me a sword." They brought a sword before the king.

[25] The king said, "Divide the living child in two, and give half to the one and half to the other."

[26] Then the woman whose son was living spoke to the king, for her compassion yearned for her son, and she said, "Oh, my lord, give her the living child, and by no means kill him!" But the other said, "He shall be neither mine nor yours; divide him!"

[27] Then the king answered, "Give the first woman the living child, and by no means kill him; she is his mother."

[28] All ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ heard of the judgment that the king had rendered; they stood in awe of the king, for they saw that the wisdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was in him to do justice.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"affinity with" → "marriage alliance with"** (v1).
- **"harlots" → "prostitutes"** (v16).
- **"understanding heart" → "listening heart"** (v9) — Hebrew לֵב שֹׁמֵעַ *lev shome'a*. "Hearing heart" — attentive.
- **"mercy" → "lovingkindness"** (v6) — *chesed*.
- **"bowels yearned" → "compassion yearned"** (v26) — Hebrew idiom for maternal love.
- **"overlaid" → "lay on"** (v19) — accidental infant suffocation.

### Notes
- **Pharaoh's daughter** — first of Solomon's many foreign marriages. Political alliance at cost of covenantal distinctiveness (cf. 11:1–2).
- **High places** (v2–3) — pre-temple decentralized worship. Tolerated but later condemned.
- **Gibeon** — tabernacle's location before Jerusalem temple (1 Chr 16:39, 21:29).
- **Dream at Gibeon** — Solomon asks for wisdom. Yahweh grants wisdom + unasked riches/honor.
- **"Listening heart"** — distinctive Hebrew idiom. Not mere intellect but attentive discernment.
- **Judgment of two prostitutes** — paradigmatic wisdom-test. The true mother's self-sacrifice reveals her. Iconic demonstration of divine-wisdom-applied.
- **Social note** — two prostitutes treated with justice like any other citizens. Access to royal court.
