# 1 Kings 14

[1] At that time ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ the son of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ became sick.

[2] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ said to his wife, "Arise, please, and disguise yourself, and they shall not know that you are the wife of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, and go to Shiloh. Behold, ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧ the prophet is there; he spoke concerning me as king over this people.

[3] And take in your hand ten loaves of bread and crumb cakes and a flask of honey, and come to him; he will tell you what shall be for the boy."

[4] And the wife of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ did so, and she arose and went to Shiloh and came to the house of ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧. And ⟦ahijah|Achiyahu|Ahijah⟧ was not able to see, for his eyes were set from his old age.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had said to ⟦ahijah|Achiyahu|Ahijah⟧, "Behold, the wife of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ is coming to seek a word from you concerning her son, for he is sick. Thus and thus you shall speak to her. And it shall be, when she comes, that she is making herself a stranger."

[6] And it came to pass, when ⟦ahijah|Achiyahu|Ahijah⟧ heard the sound of her feet as she came in at the entrance, that he said, "Come in, wife of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧. Why is it that you are making yourself a stranger? And I am sent to you with a hard thing.

[7] Go, say to ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Because I raised you from the midst of the people and made you leader over my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[8] and I tore the kingdom from the house of David and gave it to you, and you have not been like my servant David, who kept my commandments and who walked after me with all his heart, to do only what is right in my eyes,

[9] and you have done more evil than all who were before you, and you went and made for yourself other gods and molten images to vex me, and me you have thrown behind your back,

[10] therefore, behold, I am bringing evil to the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, and I will cut off from ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ one who urinates against a wall, shut in and left, in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I will clear away after the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, as one clears away the dung until it has come to an end.

[11] The one of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam's|Jeroboam's⟧ who dies in the city the dogs shall eat, and the one who dies in the field the birds of the heavens shall eat, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.'

[12] And you, arise, go to your house. When your feet come into the city, the child shall die.

[13] And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall lament for him and bury him, for this one alone of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam's|Jeroboam's⟧ shall come to a grave, because there was found in him a good thing toward ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧.

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall raise up for himself a king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who shall cut off the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ this day. And what? Even now.

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall strike ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, as the reed wavers in the water, and he shall pluck up ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from upon this good ground that he gave to their fathers and strew them beyond the River, because they have made their Asherim, vexing ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] And he shall give ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ up because of the sins of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, which he sinned and with which he caused ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to sin."

[17] And the wife of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ arose and went and came to ⟦tirzah|Tirtzah|Tirzah⟧. She was coming to the threshold of the house, and the boy died.

[18] And they buried him, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ lamented for him, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he spoke by the hand of his servant ⟦ahijah|Achiyahu|Ahijah⟧ the prophet.

[19] And the rest of the acts of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧, how he fought and how he reigned, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[20] And the days that ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ reigned were twenty-two years. And he lay with his fathers, and ⟦nadab|Nadav|Nadab⟧ his son reigned in his place.

[21] And ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ the son of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ reigned in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and seventeen years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the city that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ chose to put his name there, out of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. And the name of his mother was Naamah the ⟦ammonites|Ammonit|Ammonite⟧.

[22] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they made him jealous, more than all that their fathers had done, with their sins that they sinned.

[23] And they also built for themselves high places and pillars and Asherim on every high hill and under every luxuriant tree.

[24] And there were also male cult prostitutes in the land. They did according to all the abominations of the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dispossessed from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[25] And it came to pass in the fifth year of King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ that ⟦shishak|Shishaq|Shishak⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ came up against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[26] And he took the treasures of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the treasures of the house of the king, and he took everything; and he took all the shields of gold that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had made.

[27] And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ made shields of bronze in their place, and he entrusted them to the hand of the chiefs of the runners who kept the entrance of the house of the king.

[28] And it came to pass, as often as the king came to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that the runners would carry them, and they would bring them back to the room of the runners.

[29] And the rest of the acts of ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?

[30] And there was war between ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ all the days.

[31] And ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ lay with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David. And the name of his mother was Naamah the ⟦ammonites|Ammonit|Ammonite⟧. And ⟦abijam|Aviyam|Abijam⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"fell sick" → "became sick"** (v1) — Hebrew חָלָה.
- **"that thou be not known to be the wife of Jeroboam" → "and they shall not know that you are the wife of Jeroboam"** (v2) — Hebrew וְלֹא יֵדְעוּ "and they shall not know". "Disguise yourself" is וְהִשְׁתַּנִּית, literally "change yourself". For "you" the written text has the old form אתי, read as אַתְּ; the meaning is the same.
- **"which told me that I should be king" → "he spoke concerning me as king"** (v2) — Hebrew הוּא־דִבֶּר עָלַי לְמֶלֶךְ, literally "he spoke concerning me for king" (11:29–39).
- **"take with thee ten loaves, and cracknels, and a cruse of honey" → "take in your hand ten loaves of bread and crumb cakes and a flask of honey"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּיָדֵךְ "in your hand"; עֲשָׂרָה לֶחֶם "ten of bread" ("loaves" is supplied); נִקֻּדִים is the word of Josh 9:5, 12 ("crumbs"), here a kind of baked food: the meaning is uncertain; בַּקְבֻּק is a flask with a narrow neck (Jer 19:1), not the "jar" (כַּד) of 17:12.
- **"what shall become of the child" → "what shall be for the boy"** (v3, 17) — Hebrew נַעַר "boy"; in v12 the word is יֶלֶד "child".
- **"Ahijah" / "Ahijahu"** (v4, 5, 6, 18) — from the second half of v4 on the Hebrew writes the long form of the name, אֲחִיָּהוּ.
- **"his eyes were set by reason of his age"** (v4) — Hebrew קָמוּ עֵינָיו מִשֵּׂיבוֹ, literally "his eyes stood from his gray hair" (as 1 Sam 4:15).
- **"And the LORD said" → "And Yahweh had said"** (v5) — the subject stands before the verb (וַיהוָה אָמַר): what was said before she came.
- **"cometh to ask a thing of thee for her son" → "is coming to seek a word from you concerning her son"** (v5) — Hebrew לִדְרֹשׁ דָּבָר "to seek a word"; אֶל־בְּנָהּ is literally "to her son".
- **"she shall feign herself to be another woman" → "she is making herself a stranger"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew מִתְנַכֵּרָה, the verb of Gen 42:7: to act as one unknown.
- **"as she came in at the door" → "as she came in at the entrance"** (v6) — Hebrew פֶּתַח. **"the sound of her feet"** — Hebrew קוֹל "voice, sound".
- **"I am sent to thee with heavy tidings" → "I am sent to you with a hard thing"** (v6) — Hebrew שָׁלוּחַ אֵלַיִךְ קָשָׁה, literally "sent to you, hard"; "with a … thing" is supplied.
- **"Forasmuch as I exalted thee from among the people, and made thee prince" → "Because I raised you from the midst of the people and made you leader"** (v7) — Hebrew הֵרִים "raise"; וָאֶתֶּנְךָ נָגִיד, literally "and I gave you as leader"; נָגִיד is not "prince" (שַׂר).
- **"rent the kingdom away" → "tore the kingdom"** (v8) — Hebrew קָרַע, as 11:11–13, 31; 13:3, 5.
- **"who followed me with all his heart, to do that only which was right in mine eyes" → "who walked after me with all his heart, to do only what is right in my eyes"** (v8) — Hebrew הָלַךְ אַחֲרַי "walked after me"; הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינָי.
- **"hast done evil above all that were before thee" → "have done more evil than all who were before you"** (v9) — Hebrew וַתָּרַע לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "you have done evil in doing, more than all".
- **"molten images, to provoke me to anger, and hast cast me behind thy back" → "molten images to vex me, and me you have thrown behind your back"** (v9, 15) — Hebrew הִכְעִיס "vex" ("anger" is not a second word in the Hebrew); וְאֹתִי "and me" stands first; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw".
- **"will cut off from Jeroboam him that pisseth against the wall, and him that is shut up and left in Israel" → "I will cut off from Jeroboam one who urinates against a wall, shut in and left, in Israel"** (v10) — Hebrew מַשְׁתִּין בְּקִיר, said as the Hebrew says it: every male (1 Sam 25:22, 34). לְיָרָבְעָם is literally "to / belonging to Jeroboam" (so also v11, 13). עָצוּר וְעָזוּב "shut in and left" is a fixed pair (Deut 32:36; 1 Kgs 21:21; 2 Kgs 9:8; 14:26) whose sense is uncertain: perhaps "bond and free", or "under guardianship and on his own"; the wording of Deut 32:36 is kept.
- **"will take away the remnant of the house of Jeroboam, as a man taketh away dung, till it be all gone" → "I will clear away after the house of Jeroboam, as one clears away the dung until it has come to an end"** (v10) — Hebrew בִּעֵר אַחֲרֵי, literally "burn / clear away behind": the verb of "you shall purge the evil from your midst" (Deut 13:5). The Hebrew has no "remnant". עַד־תֻּמּוֹ "until its end".
- **"Him that dieth of Jeroboam … fowls of the air" → "The one of Jeroboam's who dies … the birds of the heavens"** (v11) — Hebrew עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם. **"for the LORD hath spoken it" → "for Yahweh has spoken"** — no object in the Hebrew.
- **"Arise thou therefore, get thee to thine own house" → "And you, arise, go to your house"** (v12) — Hebrew וְאַתְּ קוּמִי לְכִי לְבֵיתֵךְ.
- **"shall mourn for him" → "shall lament for him"** (v13, 18) — Hebrew סָפַד, the loud wailing for the dead.
- **"there is found some good thing" → "there was found in him a good thing"** (v13) — Hebrew נִמְצָא־בוֹ דָּבָר טוֹב; דָּבָר is "word" or "thing".
- **"that day: but what? even now" → "this day. And what? Even now"** (v14) — Hebrew זֶה הַיּוֹם וּמֶה גַּם־עָתָּה, literally "this the day, and what, even now". The words are broken off and their sense is uncertain; they are translated as they stand.
- **"shall smite Israel, as a reed is shaken in the water" → "shall strike Israel, as the reed wavers in the water"** (v15) — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike"; יָנוּד, of נוּד "move to and fro, wander" (Gen 4:12).
- **"shall root up Israel out of this good land … and shall scatter them beyond the river" → "shall pluck up Israel from upon this good ground … and strew them beyond the River"** (v15) — Hebrew נָתַשׁ "pluck up" (Deut 29:28); אֲדָמָה "ground"; זֵרָה "strew, winnow"; הַנָּהָר "the River" is the Euphrates.
- **"groves" → "Asherim"** (v15, 23) — Hebrew אֲשֵׁרִים, plural of *Asherah*: wooden objects of worship that bear the name of the goddess; the word is kept.
- **"who did sin, and who made Israel to sin" → "which he sinned and with which he caused Israel to sin"** (v16) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר חָטָא וַאֲשֶׁר הֶחֱטִיא: the sins are the object of both verbs. בִּגְלַל "because of".
- **"when she came to the threshold of the door, the child died" → "She was coming to the threshold of the house, and the boy died"** (v17) — Hebrew הִיא בָּאָה בְסַף־הַבַּיִת וְהַנַּעַר מֵת, two clauses side by side.
- **"the rest of the acts … how he warred … in the book of the chronicles"** (v19, 29) — Hebrew וְיֶתֶר דִּבְרֵי … עַל־סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, literally "the rest of the words / matters of … upon the book of the matters of the days": the same word דִּבְרֵי stands in both places. נִלְחַם "fight". In v19 the formula has הִנָּם "behold, they"; in v29 the question הֲלֹא־הֵמָּה "are they not".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v20, 31) — Hebrew שָׁכַב "lie, lie down"; "sleep" is another verb.
- **"when he began to reign"** (v21) — Hebrew בְּמָלְכוֹ, literally "at his reigning". "Forty-one years old" is literally "a son of forty-one years".
- **"which the LORD did choose out of all the tribes of Israel, to put his name there" → "that Yahweh chose to put his name there, out of all the tribes of Israel"** (v21) — the Hebrew order. **"an Ammonitess"** (v21, 31) — Hebrew הָעַמֹּנִית, the feminine form with the article: "the Ammonite woman".
- **"Judah did evil in the sight of the LORD, and they provoked him to jealousy" → "Judah did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and they made him jealous"** (v22) — Hebrew הָרַע "the evil", with the article; וַיְקַנְאוּ אֹתוֹ "they made him jealous" (Deut 32:21).
- **"images" → "pillars"** (v23) — Hebrew מַצֵּבוֹת, stones set up. **"under every green tree" → "under every luxuriant tree"** — Hebrew רַעֲנָן "fresh, full of leaves", not the colour.
- **"sodomites" → "male cult prostitutes"** (v24) — Hebrew קָדֵשׁ *qadesh*, literally "consecrated man" (Deut 23:17); the Hebrew is singular, used of the whole class. What these men did is inferred from the word and its feminine (Gen 38:21); "sodomite" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"which the LORD cast out before the children of Israel" → "that Yahweh dispossessed from before the sons of Israel"** (v24) — Hebrew הוֹרִישׁ; בְּנֵי "sons".
- **"Shishak"** (v25) — the written text has שושק (Shushaq), the reading tradition שִׁישַׁק.
- **"the treasures of the house of the LORD"** (v26) — Hebrew אֹצְרוֹת, the word for "treasuries" (15:18): what was stored in them.
- **"brasen shields, and committed them unto the hands of the chief of the guard, which kept the door" → "shields of bronze, and he entrusted them to the hand of the chiefs of the runners who kept the entrance"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze"; הִפְקִיד עַל־יַד, literally "appointed upon the hand of"; שָׂרֵי הָרָצִים "the chiefs of the runners": the runners are the king's guard (1 Sam 22:17). תָּא is a small room (Ezek 40:7).
- **"all their days" → "all the days"** (v30) — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים.

### Notes
- **Abijah's death** — Jeroboam's son dies as sign of his house's doom. Only this son, in whom "a good thing" was found, comes to a grave.
- **Prophecy of northern exile** (v15) — "strew them beyond the River" — anticipates Assyrian deportation (722 BC).
- **Rehoboam's Judah** — also corrupt. Matching apostasy north and south.
- **Shishak's invasion** — first Egyptian pharaoh invasion of Judah. Strips temple of Solomon's treasures. Confirmed in Shishak's Karnak relief (Egypt).
- **Bronze shields replace gold** — symbol of Judah's diminished glory. Solomon's wealth already fading.
- **"The book of the chronicles of the kings"** — lost royal annals, different from biblical Chronicles. Referenced frequently in Kings.
