## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"cracknels" → "cakes"** (v3) — archaic; Hebrew *niqqudim*.
- **"cruse" → "jar"** (v3).
- **"sodomites" → "male cult-prostitutes"** (v24) — Hebrew *qedeshim*. Male pagan-cult prostitutes, not "sodomites" in the modern sense.
- **"brasen" → "bronze"** (v27).

### Notes
- **Abijah's death** — Jeroboam's son dies as sign of his house's doom. Only this "good" son receives honorable burial.
- **Prophecy of northern exile** (v15) — "scatter them beyond the River" — anticipates Assyrian deportation (722 BC).
- **Rehoboam's Judah** — also corrupt. Matching apostasy north and south.
- **Shishak's invasion** — first Egyptian pharaoh invasion of Judah. Strips temple of Solomon's treasures. Confirmed in Shishak's Karnak relief (Egypt).
- **Bronze shields replace gold** — symbol of Judah's diminished glory. Solomon's wealth already fading.
- **"Book of the Chronicles of the Kings"** — lost royal annals, different from biblical Chronicles. Referenced frequently in Kings.
