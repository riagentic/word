# 1 Kings 14

[1] At that time Abijah son of Jeroboam fell sick.

[2] Jeroboam said to his wife, "Arise, please, and disguise yourself, so that you may not be known to be the wife of Jeroboam; go to Shiloh. Behold, Ahijah the prophet is there, who said to me that I should be king over this people.

[3] Take with you ten loaves, cakes, and a jar of honey, and go to him; he will tell you what shall become of the child."

[4] Jeroboam's wife did so; she arose, went to Shiloh, and came to the house of Ahijah. But Ahijah could not see, for his eyes had dimmed because of his age.

[5] Yahweh said to Ahijah, "Behold, the wife of Jeroboam is coming to inquire of you concerning her son, for he is sick; thus and thus shall you say to her, for as she comes in she will pretend to be another woman."

[6] When Ahijah heard the sound of her feet as she came in the door, he said, "Come in, wife of Jeroboam; why do you pretend to be another? I am sent to you with heavy news.

[7] Go, tell Jeroboam: 'Thus says Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel: I exalted you from among the people and made you prince over my people Israel,

[8] tore the kingdom away from the house of David and gave it to you; yet you have not been like my servant David, who kept my commandments and followed me with all his heart, doing only what was right in my eyes,

[9] but have done evil above all who were before you, and have gone and made for yourself other gods and cast images, provoking me to anger, and have cast me behind your back.

[10] Therefore I will bring evil upon the house of Jeroboam; I will cut off from Jeroboam every male, both bond and free in Israel, and will sweep away the house of Jeroboam as a man sweeps away dung until it is gone.

[11] Whoever of Jeroboam dies in the city the dogs shall eat, and whoever dies in the field the birds of the sky shall eat; for Yahweh has spoken it.'

[12] Arise, therefore, go to your house. When your feet enter the city, the child shall die.

[13] All Israel shall mourn for him and bury him; for he alone of Jeroboam shall come to the grave, because in him there is found something good toward Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel, in the house of Jeroboam.

[14] Moreover, Yahweh will raise up for himself a king over Israel who shall cut off the house of Jeroboam — this day, even this very day.

[15] Yahweh will strike Israel, as a reed is shaken in the water; he will uproot Israel out of this good land that he gave to their fathers, and will scatter them beyond the River, because they have made their Asherah poles, provoking Yahweh to anger.

[16] He will give Israel up because of the sins of Jeroboam, which he sinned and made Israel to sin."

[17] Jeroboam's wife arose, departed, and came to Tirzah. When she was entering the threshold of the house, the child died.

[18] They buried him; all Israel mourned for him, according to the word of Yahweh that he spoke by the hand of his servant Ahijah the prophet.

[19] The rest of the acts of Jeroboam — how he warred and how he reigned — behold, they are written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel.

[20] The days that Jeroboam reigned were twenty-two years; he slept with his fathers, and Nadab his son reigned in his place.

[21] Rehoboam son of Solomon reigned in Judah. Rehoboam was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city that Yahweh had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel to put his name there. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonitess.

[22] Judah did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh; they provoked him to jealousy with their sins that they had committed, more than all that their fathers had done.

[23] For they also built for themselves high places, sacred pillars, and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every green tree.

[24] There were also male cult-prostitutes in the land. They did according to all the abominations of the nations that Yahweh cast out before the children of Israel.

[25] In the fifth year of King Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem.

[26] He took away the treasures of the house of Yahweh and the treasures of the king's house; he took away everything. He also took away all the gold shields that Solomon had made.

[27] King Rehoboam made in their place shields of bronze, and committed them to the hands of the officers of the guard who kept the door of the king's house.

[28] Whenever the king went into the house of Yahweh, the guard carried them and brought them back into the guardroom.

[29] The rest of the acts of Rehoboam, and all that he did — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

[30] There was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all their days.

[31] Rehoboam slept with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David. His mother's name was Naamah the Ammonitess. Abijam his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"cracknels" → "cakes"** (v3) — archaic; Hebrew *niqqudim*.
- **"cruse" → "jar"** (v3).
- **"sodomites" → "male cult-prostitutes"** (v24) — Hebrew *qedeshim*. Male pagan-cult prostitutes, not "sodomites" in the modern sense.
- **"brasen" → "bronze"** (v27).

### Notes
- **Abijah's death** — Jeroboam's son dies as sign of his house's doom. Only this "good" son receives honorable burial.
- **Prophecy of northern exile** (v15) — "scatter them beyond the River" — anticipates Assyrian deportation (722 BC).
- **Rehoboam's Judah** — also corrupt. Matching apostasy north and south.
- **Shishak's invasion** — first Egyptian pharaoh invasion of Judah. Strips temple of Solomon's treasures. Confirmed in Shishak's Karnak relief (Egypt).
- **Bronze shields replace gold** — symbol of Judah's diminished glory. Solomon's wealth already fading.
- **"Book of the Chronicles of the Kings"** — lost royal annals, different from biblical Chronicles. Referenced frequently in Kings.
