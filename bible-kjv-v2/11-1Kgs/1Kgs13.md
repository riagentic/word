## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v18).
- **"ass" → "donkey"** (throughout).
- **"ox / lion" language standardized**.

### Notes
- **Josiah prophesied** (v2) — by name, three centuries before fulfillment (2 Kgs 23:15–20). Josiah will destroy this altar and burn priests' bones on it.
- **Jeroboam's withered hand** — immediate confirmation-miracle.
- **The old prophet's deception** — odd narrative. He lies, then oracles truthfully against the man he just deceived.
- **Man of *Elohim* killed by lion** — stern consequence for disobeying his commissioned word, even when deceived.
- **Lion and donkey standing peacefully** — unnatural scene confirms divine judgment, not mere accident.
- **Bones preserved** — by the old prophet's request, the Judahite's bones are preserved when Josiah later desecrates the altar (2 Kgs 23:17–18).
- **Jeroboam's continued sin** — no repentance despite the sign. Seals his house's doom.
