## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "Tent of Meeting"** (v4).
- **"staves" → "poles"** (v7, 8).
- **"mercy" → "lovingkindness"** (v23) — *chesed*.
- **"caterpiller" → "caterpillar"** (v37) — modernized spelling.
- **"perfect" → "wholly true"** (v61) — *shalem*.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v64).

### Notes
- **Temple dedication ceremony** — one of OT's great liturgical moments. Ark installed; Yahweh's glory fills the house.
- **"Cloud filled the house"** — echo of Sinai theophany (Ex 40:34–35). Yahweh's presence in the new sanctuary.
- **Solomon's prayer** (v22–53) — seven specific requests for divine response to prayer: oath-disputes, defeat, drought, plague, foreigners, war, exile. Comprehensive covenantal prayer-theology.
- **"Heaven cannot contain you"** (v27) — acknowledgment that the temple is not Yahweh's cage but a focal point for prayer.
- **Exile anticipated** (v46–51) — remarkable text. Foresees exile and offers theology of exilic prayer. Post-exilic editors likely finalized this.
- **Massive sacrifice** — 22,000 oxen + 120,000 sheep. Largest recorded sacrifice in OT.
- **Fourteen-day feast** — Sukkot + extended celebration.
