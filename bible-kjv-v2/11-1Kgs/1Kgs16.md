## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"I exalted thee out of the dust, and made thee prince" → "I raised you from the dust and made you leader"** (v2) — Hebrew הֵרִים "raise"; נָגִיד "leader" (14:7).
- **"hast made my people Israel to sin, to provoke me to anger with their sins" → "have caused my people Israel to sin, to vex me with their sins"** (v2, 7, 13, 26, 33) — Hebrew הֶחֱטִיא, the causative of "sin"; הִכְעִיס "vex" ("anger" is not a second word in the Hebrew).
- **"I will take away the posterity of Baasha, and the posterity of his house" → "I am clearing away after Baasha and after his house"** (v3) — Hebrew מַבְעִיר אַחֲרֵי, literally "burning / clearing away behind", as 14:10; the Hebrew has no "posterity". "I will make" is וְנָתַתִּי, literally "I will give". The word turns from "Baasha … his house" to "your house" within the verse.
- **"Him that dieth of Baasha … fowls of the air" → "The one of Baasha's who dies … the birds of the heavens"** (v4) — as 14:11.
- **"the rest of the acts … in the book of the chronicles"** (v5, 14, 20, 27) — Hebrew וְיֶתֶר דִּבְרֵי … עַל־סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, literally "the rest of the words / matters of … upon the book of the matters of the days". **"his might"** — Hebrew גְּבוּרָה.
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v6, 28) — Hebrew שָׁכַב "lie, lie down".
- **"came the word of the LORD against Baasha, and against his house, even for all the evil … and because he killed him" → "the word of Yahweh came to Baasha and to his house, both on account of all the evil … and on account of his having struck it down"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶל "to" both times; וְעַל … וְעַל "and on account of … and on account of". The last words are עַל אֲשֶׁר־הִכָּה אֹתוֹ: "him" or "it", that is, the house of Jeroboam (15:27–29).
- **"began Elah … to reign over Israel in Tirzah, two years"** (v8, 23) — Hebrew מָלַךְ "reign": one verb, which here tells both the beginning and the length of the reign; "began to reign", as with every year of accession. In v15 ("reigned seven days") and v29 ("reigned … twenty-two years") the same verb tells only the length. "In the twenty-sixth year" is literally "in the year of twenty-six years".
- **"captain of half his chariots" → "commander of half the chariots"** (v9) — Hebrew שַׂר מַחֲצִית הָרָכֶב; no "his". **"drinking himself drunk"** — Hebrew שֹׁתֶה שִׁכּוֹר, literally "drinking, drunk". **"steward of his house" → "who was over the house"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עַל־הַבַּיִת.
- **"smote him, and killed him" → "struck him and put him to death"** (v10) — Hebrew הִכָּה, הֵמִית.
- **"as soon as he sat on his throne, that he slew all the house" → "as soon as he sat on his throne, that he struck down all the house"** (v11) — Hebrew הִכָּה. "When he began to reign" is בְמָלְכוֹ, literally "at his reigning".
- **"he left him not one that pisseth against a wall, neither of his kinsfolks, nor of his friends" → "he did not leave him one who urinates against a wall, nor his redeemers nor his friend"** (v11) — Hebrew מַשְׁתִּין בְּקִיר, said as the Hebrew says it: every male (14:10; 1 Sam 25:22). גֹּאֲלָיו "his redeemers": the kinsmen whose duty it was to redeem and to avenge. רֵעֵהוּ is singular, "his friend".
- **"which he spake against Baasha" → "that he spoke to Baasha"** (v12) — Hebrew אֶל "to".
- **"For all the sins"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶל, literally "to / with regard to".
- **"with their vanities" → "with their vapors"** (v13, 26) — Hebrew הֲבָלִים, plural of הֶבֶל "vapor, breath": a name of contempt for idols (Deut 32:21).
- **"the people were encamped" → "the people were camping"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew הָעָם "the people"; חָנָה "camp".
- **"hath also slain the king" → "has also struck down the king"** (v16) — Hebrew הִכָּה.
- **"made Omri, the captain of the host, king over Israel" → "made Omri, the commander of the host, reign over Israel"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיַּמְלִכוּ, the causative of "reign"; שַׂר־צָבָא. So also "to make him reign" (v21).
- **"that the city was taken, that he went into the palace of the king's house" → "that the city was captured, that he came into the citadel of the house of the king"** (v18) — Hebrew נִלְכְּדָה; אַרְמוֹן, the fortified part of a palace.
- **"For his sins which he sinned in doing evil" → "for his sins that he sinned, to do the evil"** (v19) — the written text has the singular "his sin", the reading tradition the plural חַטֹּאתָיו; three infinitives follow: "to do … to walk … to cause to sin".
- **"his treason that he wrought" → "his conspiracy that he conspired"** (v20) — Hebrew קִשְׁרוֹ אֲשֶׁר קָשָׁר: noun and verb of one root, as v9, 16.
- **"Then were the people of Israel divided into two parts: half of the people followed Tibni" → "Then the people Israel were divided in half: half of the people were after Tibni"** (v21) — Hebrew הָעָם יִשְׂרָאֵל "the people, Israel"; לַחֵצִי "into the half"; הָיָה אַחֲרֵי "was after".
- **"prevailed against" → "were stronger than"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיֶּחֱזַק … אֶת, of חָזַק "be strong": literally "was strong over".
- **"so Tibni died, and Omri reigned"** (v22) — the Hebrew has "and … and".
- **"he bought the hill Samaria of Shemer … and built on the hill … the owner of the hill" → "he bought the mountain of Samaria from Shemer … and he built the mountain … the lord of the mountain"** (v24) — Hebrew הַר "mountain"; וַיִּבֶן אֶת־הָהָר, literally "he built the mountain" (built on it, fortified it); אֲדֹנֵי "lord". The name שֹׁמְרוֹן (Shomron) is formed from שֶׁמֶר (Shemer). In the Hebrew the name "Samaria" stands last in the verse.
- **"wrought evil in the eyes of the LORD, and did worse than all that were before him" → "did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and he did more evil than all who were before him"** (v25, 30) — Hebrew הָרַע "the evil", with the article; וַיָּרַע מִכֹּל "he did evil more than all".
- **"in his sin wherewith he made Israel to sin"** (v26) — the reading tradition has the singular "his sin", as in 15:26, 34; the written text has the plural "his sins".
- **"his might that he shewed" → "his might that he did"** (v27) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה "that he did", the same words as in the clause before.
- **"as if it had been a light thing for him to walk in the sins of Jeroboam" → "was his walking in the sins of Jeroboam … a light thing?"** (v31) — Hebrew הֲנָקֵל לֶכְתּוֹ, a question: "was it (too) light, his walking …?"
- **"he took to wife Jezebel … served Baal, and worshipped him" → "he took as wife Jezebel … served the Baal and bowed down to him"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל, with the article; הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down".
- **"he reared up an altar for Baal in the house of Baal" → "he raised up an altar for the Baal in the house of the Baal"** (v32) — Hebrew הֵקִים "raise up"; "in" is supplied: the Hebrew has "the house of the Baal" with no preposition.
- **"Ahab made a grove" → "Ahab made the Asherah"** (v33) — Hebrew הָאֲשֵׁרָה, with the article: the wooden object of worship that bears the name of the goddess; the word is kept.
- **"Ahab did more to provoke the LORD … to anger" → "Ahab did yet more to vex Yahweh"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיּוֹסֶף אַחְאָב לַעֲשׂוֹת לְהַכְעִיס, literally "and Ahab added to do, to vex".
- **"he laid the foundation thereof in Abiram his firstborn, and set up the gates thereof in his youngest son Segub" → "With Abiram his firstborn he laid its foundation, and with Segub his youngest he set up its doors"** (v34) — Hebrew בַּאֲבִירָם … וּבִשְׂגוּב, the בְּ of Josh 6:26 ("with his firstborn … with his youngest"): at the price of the son's life, as it is commonly understood; how the sons died is not said. צְעִירוֹ "his young one"; דְּלָתֶיהָ "its doors". For "Segub" the written text has שגיב (Segib).
- **"the Bethelite"** (v34) — Hebrew בֵּית הָאֱלִי, "the one of Bethel".

### Notes
- **Jehu son of Hanani** — not the later king Jehu (ch 9); a prophet. His father Hanani rebukes Asa in 2 Chr 16.
- **Baasha's condemnation** (v7) — the oracle notes that Baasha was both guilty of Jeroboam-style sin *and* of striking down the house of Jeroboam (thereby advancing his own career). Moral ambiguity: instrument of judgment, still judged himself.
- **Zimri's seven days** — shortest Israelite reign. "Zimri" becomes byword for regicide (2 Kgs 9:31).
- **Tibni vs Omri** — civil war of about four years (v15 and v23: the twenty-seventh and the thirty-first year of Asa). The Septuagint is said to have a longer v22 (from memory).
- **The figures of the reigns** — given as the Hebrew has them, also where they are hard to fit together (v23 and v29: twelve years from the thirty-first year of Asa, and Ahab in the thirty-eighth).
- **Omri** — historically far more important than the text admits. Moabite Stone (Mesha Stela) mentions him; Assyrian records call northern kingdom "House of Omri" (Bit-Humri) for centuries afterward. Biblical writer minimizes him theologically.
- **Samaria** — new capital, built on strategic hill. Will be capital until 722 BC Assyrian fall.
- **Ahab and Jezebel** — Jezebel = daughter of Ethbaal (Ittobaal) of Tyre/Sidon. Priest-king of Astarte according to Josephus (from memory). Political marriage brings aggressive Baal cult to Israel.
- **Hiel rebuilds Jericho** — at cost of his sons, fulfilling Joshua's curse (Josh 6:26). Foundation sacrifice attested archaeologically in ANE but here framed as divine judgment.
