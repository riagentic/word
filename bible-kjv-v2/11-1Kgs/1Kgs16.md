## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"him that pisseth against a wall" → "a single male"** (v11) — KJV literal of idiom *mashtin be-qir*; Hebrew figure of speech = any male.
- **"vanities" → "worthless idols"** (v13, 26) — Hebrew *hablim* = vapors/worthless things, term for idols.
- **"Zidonians" → "Sidonians"** (v31).
- **"palace" → "citadel"** (v18) — *armon*.

### Notes
- **Jehu son of Hanani** — not the later king Jehu (ch 9); a prophet. His father Hanani rebukes Asa in 2 Chr 16.
- **Baasha's condemnation** (v7) — the oracle notes that Baasha was both guilty of Jeroboam-style sin *and* of executing Yahweh's judgment on Jeroboam (thereby advancing his own career). Moral ambiguity: instrument of judgment, still judged himself.
- **Zimri's seven days** — shortest Israelite reign. "Zimri" becomes byword for regicide (2 Kgs 9:31).
- **Tibni vs Omri** — civil war ca. 4 years. LXX expands v22.
- **Omri** — historically far more important than the text admits. Moabite Stone (Mesha Stela) mentions him; Assyrian records call northern kingdom "House of Omri" (Bit-Humri) for centuries afterward. Biblical writer minimizes him theologically.
- **Samaria** — new capital, built on strategic hill. Will be capital until 722 BC Assyrian fall.
- **Ahab and Jezebel** — Jezebel = daughter of Ethbaal (Ittobaal) of Tyre/Sidon. Priest-king of Astarte; Josephus corroborates. Political marriage brings aggressive Baal cult to Israel.
- **Hiel rebuilds Jericho** — at cost of his sons, fulfilling Joshua's curse (Josh 6:26). Foundation sacrifice attested archaeologically in ANE but here framed as divine judgment.
