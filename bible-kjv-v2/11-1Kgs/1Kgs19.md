# 1 Kings 19

[1] Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and how he had killed all the prophets with the sword.

[2] Then Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah, saying, "So may the gods do to me, and more also, if I do not make your life as the life of one of them by this time tomorrow."

[3] When he saw that, he arose and fled for his life, and came to Beersheba which belongs to Judah, and left his servant there.

[4] But he himself went a day's journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a broom tree. He asked that he might die, saying, "It is enough. Now, O Yahweh, take away my life; for I am no better than my fathers."

[5] He lay down and slept under the broom tree; behold, then a Messenger touched him and said to him, "Arise and eat."

[6] He looked, and behold, at his head there was a cake baked on hot stones and a jar of water. He ate and drank, and lay down again.

[7] The Messenger of Yahweh came again a second time, touched him, and said, "Arise and eat, for the journey is too great for you."

[8] He arose, ate and drank, and went in the strength of that food forty days and forty nights to Horeb, the mountain of *Elohim*.

[9] He came there to the cave and lodged there; behold, the word of Yahweh came to him, and he said to him, "What are you doing here, Elijah?"

[10] He said, "I have been very jealous for Yahweh, the *Elohim* of hosts; for the children of Israel have forsaken your covenant, thrown down your altars, and killed your prophets with the sword. I, I alone, am left, and they seek my life, to take it away."

[11] He said, "Go out and stand on the mountain before Yahweh." Behold, Yahweh passed by, and a great and strong wind tore the mountains and broke in pieces the rocks before Yahweh; but Yahweh was not in the wind. After the wind an earthquake; but Yahweh was not in the earthquake.

[12] After the earthquake a fire; but Yahweh was not in the fire. After the fire, a sound of thin silence.

[13] When Elijah heard it, he wrapped his face in his mantle, went out, and stood at the entrance of the cave. Behold, a voice came to him and said, "What are you doing here, Elijah?"

[14] He said, "I have been very jealous for Yahweh, the *Elohim* of hosts; for the children of Israel have forsaken your covenant, thrown down your altars, and killed your prophets with the sword. I, I alone, am left, and they seek my life, to take it away."

[15] Yahweh said to him, "Go, return on your way to the wilderness of Damascus; when you arrive, anoint Hazael to be king over Aram.

[16] Jehu son of Nimshi you shall anoint to be king over Israel, and Elisha son of Shaphat of Abel-meholah you shall anoint to be prophet in your place.

[17] Whoever escapes the sword of Hazael, Jehu shall kill; and whoever escapes the sword of Jehu, Elisha shall kill.

[18] Yet I have left me seven thousand in Israel — all the knees that have not bowed to Baal and every mouth that has not kissed him."

[19] So he departed from there and found Elisha son of Shaphat, who was plowing with twelve yoke of oxen before him, and he was with the twelfth. Elijah passed by him and cast his mantle upon him.

[20] He left the oxen and ran after Elijah, and said, "Let me please kiss my father and my mother, and then I will follow you." He said to him, "Go back again; what have I done to you?"

[21] He returned from following him, took the yoke of oxen, and slaughtered them; with the equipment of the oxen he boiled their flesh and gave it to the people, and they ate. Then he arose, went after Elijah, and ministered to him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"juniper tree" → "broom tree"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew *rotem*; a desert shrub (Retama raetam), not juniper.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v5, 7).
- **"still small voice" → "sound of thin silence"** (v12) — Hebrew *qol demamah daqqah*. Literally "voice/sound of thin stillness"; could be whisper, or paradoxical "sound of silence."
- **"cake baken on the coals" → "cake baked on hot stones"** (v6) — *ugah retzaphim*.
- **"cruse" → "jar"** (v6).
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v15).
- **"mount of God" → "mountain of *Elohim*"** (v8).

### Notes
- **Collapse after triumph** — prophetic pattern: spiritual high followed by crash. Elijah just saw fire fall and still despairs.
- **Forty days to Horeb** — Horeb = Sinai. Pilgrimage to the covenant mountain, recapitulating Moses (Ex 34). Forty days echoes Moses on Sinai.
- **Theophany inverts Sinai** — at Sinai Yahweh came in wind, earthquake, fire (Ex 19). Here, explicitly *not* in those. The "thin silence" reframes how God addresses his prophet — not always in spectacle.
- **"What are you doing here?"** — asked twice (v9, 13). Probing question; Elijah's identical answer suggests unchanged despair even after theophany.
- **Three anointings** — Hazael (external instrument of judgment), Jehu (internal purge of Omrides), Elisha (successor). Elijah personally anoints only Elisha; Hazael and Jehu are handled by Elisha later (2 Kgs 8, 9).
- **Seven thousand remnant** — corrective to Elijah's "I alone am left." Paul cites in Rom 11:4. Remnant theology.
- **Mantle on Elisha** — prophetic call-sign. Will pass to Elisha at Elijah's ascension (2 Kgs 2).
- **Twelve yoke** — Elisha is wealthy; burning his plow-gear = radical renunciation, no turning back.
