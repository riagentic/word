## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"tribute" → "forced labor"** (v18) — *mas*.
- **"chastised" → "disciplined"** (v11, 14).
- **"the cause was from the Lord" → "a turn of events brought about by Yahweh"** (v15) — Hebrew סִבָּה מֵעִם יְהוָה *sibah me-im YHWH*.

### Notes
- **Divided monarchy** — Solomon's kingdom splits. Ten northern tribes under Jeroboam; Judah (+Benjamin) under Rehoboam.
- **Shechem assembly** — significant: covenant-site (Josh 24), location of Israel's historic tribal gatherings. Rehoboam goes north for confirmation.
- **"Your father made our yoke heavy"** — Solomon's forced labor bred rebellion.
- **Old vs young counselors** — wisdom literature trope. Rehoboam rejects the senior counselors' moderation for aggressive youth.
- **Scorpions** (v11) — likely barbed whips called "scorpions."
- **Adoram killed** — the tax/labor master, symbol of Solomonic oppression.
- **Jeroboam's calves** — to prevent Israel from returning to Jerusalem's temple, establishes alternative cult at Dan and Bethel. "These are your gods, O Israel" — echoes Ex 32 (Aaron's calf).
- **Foundational sin of the Northern Kingdom** — "the sin of Jeroboam" will be referenced throughout Kings as the reason for northern apostasy.
- **Non-Levite priests** — breaks Torah's exclusive Levite priesthood.
