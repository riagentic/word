# 1 Kings 12

[1] And ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ went to Shechem, for to Shechem all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had come to make him king.

[2] And it came to pass, when ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧ heard — and he was still in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, where he had fled from the face of King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ — that ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ dwelt in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[3] And they sent and called him; and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ and all the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came, and they spoke to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧, saying,

[4] "Your father made our yoke hard; and you, now lighten the hard service of your father and his heavy yoke that he put on us, and we will serve you."

[5] And he said to them, "Go for three days more, and return to me." And the people went.

[6] And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ took counsel with the elders who had been standing before ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his father while he was alive, saying, "How do you counsel to return word to this people?"

[7] And they spoke to him, saying, "If today you will be a servant to this people and serve them and answer them and speak good words to them, then they will be your servants all the days."

[8] And he forsook the counsel of the elders, which they had counseled him, and took counsel with the children who had grown up with him, who were standing before him.

[9] And he said to them, "What do you counsel, that we may return word to this people, who have spoken to me, saying, 'Lighten the yoke that your father put on us'?"

[10] And the children who had grown up with him spoke to him, saying, "Thus you shall say to this people, who have spoken to you, saying, 'Your father made our yoke heavy, and you, lighten it from upon us' — thus you shall speak to them: 'My little finger is thicker than the loins of my father.

[11] And now, my father loaded on you a heavy yoke, and I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, and I will discipline you with scorpions.'"

[12] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ and all the people came to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ on the third day, as the king had spoken, saying, "Return to me on the third day."

[13] And the king answered the people a hard thing, and he forsook the counsel of the elders, which they had counseled him,

[14] and he spoke to them according to the counsel of the children, saying, "My father made your yoke heavy, and I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, and I will discipline you with scorpions."

[15] And the king did not listen to the people, for it was a turn of things from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in order to establish his word, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken by the hand of ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧ the ⟦shilonite|Shiloni|Shilonite⟧ to ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧.

[16] And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ saw that the king had not listened to them, and the people returned word to the king, saying,

"What share have we in David?
And there is no inheritance in the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧.
To your tents, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!
Now see to your house, David."

And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went to its tents.

[17] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who dwelt in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ — ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ reigned over them.

[18] And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ sent Adoram, who was over the forced labor, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ stoned him with stone, and he died. And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ exerted himself to go up into the chariot, to flee to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[19] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has revolted against the house of David to this day.

[20] And it came to pass, when all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ heard that ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ had returned, that they sent and called him to the congregation and made him king over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. There was none after the house of David except the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ alone.

[21] And ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and assembled all the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, a hundred and eighty thousand chosen men who made war, to fight with the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to return the kingship to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ the son of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[22] And the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, saying,

[23] "Say to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ the son of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and to all the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and the rest of the people, saying,

[24] 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: You shall not go up and you shall not fight with your brothers, the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. Return, each man to his house, for from me this thing has come to be.'" And they heard the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and turned back to go, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[25] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ built Shechem in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and dwelt in it; and he went out from there and built Penuel.

[26] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ said in his heart, "Now the kingdom will return to the house of David.

[27] If this people go up to offer sacrifices in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, then the heart of this people will return to their lord, to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they will kill me and return to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧."

[28] And the king took counsel and made two calves of gold. And he said to them, "Enough for you of going up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧! Behold your gods, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who brought you up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧."

[29] And he put the one in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, and the one he placed in Dan.

[30] And this thing became a sin; and the people went before the one, as far as Dan.

[31] And he made a house of high places, and he made priests from the ends of the people, who were not of the sons of Levi.

[32] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ made a feast in the eighth month, on the fifteenth day of the month, like the feast that is in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he went up upon the altar; so he did in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, to sacrifice to the calves that he had made. And he made the priests of the high places that he had made stand in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧.

[33] And he went up upon the altar that he had made in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ on the fifteenth day in the eighth month, in the month that he had devised from his heart; and he made a feast for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he went up upon the altar to make smoke.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v22 the Hebrew has the article both times, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"to make him king"** (v1, 20) — Hebrew הִמְלִיךְ, the causative of "reign".
- **"heard of it … and Jeroboam dwelt in Egypt" → "heard … that Jeroboam dwelt in Egypt"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֵּשֶׁב יָרָבְעָם בְּמִצְרָיִם, word for word; "of it" is not in the Hebrew. The parallel 2 Chr 10:2 has "and Jeroboam returned from Egypt" (וַיָּשָׁב … מִמִּצְרָיִם: other vowels and one letter more), and so this verse is commonly read. The Masoretic Text is kept. **"from the presence of" → "from the face of"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי.
- **"came"** (v3, 12, 21) — the reading tradition (qere) has the singular, "came", with Jeroboam (v3, 12) and Rehoboam (v21); the written text has the plural or a shortened form. The sense is the same. **"congregation" → "assembly"** (v3) — Hebrew קָהָל.
- **"made our yoke grievous" → "made our yoke hard"** (v4) — Hebrew הִקְשָׁה, one root with "the hard service" (הַקָּשָׁה) in the same verse; in v10, 14 the verb is another, הִכְבִּיד "made heavy". **"make thou the grievous service of thy father, and his heavy yoke … lighter" → "lighten the hard service of your father and his heavy yoke"** — Hebrew הָקֵל מִן, literally "make light from": take off some of it (also v9). **"put on us"** (v4, 9) — Hebrew נָתַן, "gave".
- **"Depart yet for three days, then come again to me" → "Go for three days more, and return to me"** (v5) — Hebrew לְכוּ עֹד שְׁלֹשָׁה יָמִים וְשׁוּבוּ.
- **"consulted with the old men" → "took counsel with the elders"** (v6, 8, 13) — Hebrew הַזְּקֵנִים, literally "the old men": the elders, a standing body. **"How do ye advise that I may answer this people" → "How do you counsel to return word to this people"** (v6, 9, 16) — Hebrew לְהָשִׁיב … דָּבָר, "bring back a word"; the verb "counsel" (יָעַץ) and the noun "counsel" (עֵצָה) are one root throughout.
- **"they spake"** (v7) — the qere is plural; the written text has the singular.
- **"a servant unto this people"** (v7) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word "slave"; "serve them" and "your servants" are the same root.
- **"the young men that were grown up with him" → "the children who had grown up with him"** (v8, 10, 14) — Hebrew הַיְלָדִים, the plain word "children, boys" (יֶלֶד), not "young men" (בַּחוּרִים, נְעָרִים), though they are men of the king's own age; the word is kept.
- **"My little finger" kept** (v10) — Hebrew קָטָנִּי, a noun found only here and in 2 Chr 10:10, literally "my little one / my littleness"; the word "finger" is not written, and the little finger is the common understanding.
- **"did lade you with" → "loaded on you"** (v11) — Hebrew הֶעְמִיס עֲלֵיכֶם. **"chastised" → "disciplined"** (v11, 14) — Hebrew יִסַּר. **"scorpions"** — Hebrew עַקְרַבִּים, the animal; perhaps the name of a kind of scourge.
- **"answered the people roughly" → "answered the people a hard thing"** (v13) — Hebrew קָשָׁה, the "hard" of v4 (as 1 Sam 20:10).
- **"hearkened not unto" → "did not listen to"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל.
- **"the cause was from the LORD" → "it was a turn of things from Yahweh"** (v15) — Hebrew סִבָּה מֵעִם יְהוָה: סִבָּה, found only here, is from סָבַב "turn"; literally "a turning from with Yahweh". **"that he might perform his saying" → "in order to establish his word"** — Hebrew הָקִים, "make stand" (as Deut 9:5). **"by"** — Hebrew בְּיַד, "by the hand of".
- **"What portion have we in David? neither have we inheritance" → "What share have we in David? And there is no inheritance"** (v16) — Hebrew חֵלֶק "share" and וְלֹא־נַחֲלָה "and no inheritance". **"O Israel" → "Israel"** — no "O" in the Hebrew. **"see to thine own house" → "see to your house"** — Hebrew רְאֵה בֵיתְךָ, literally "see your house". **"unto their tents" → "to its tents"** — Hebrew לְאֹהָלָיו, "to his tents". The cry takes up that of 2 Sam 20:1 ("We have no share in David …").
- **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** (v17, 24, 33).
- **"tribute" → "forced labor"** (v18) — Hebrew מַס. **"stoned him with stones"** → "with stone" — singular in the Hebrew. **"made speed to get him up to his chariot" → "exerted himself to go up into the chariot"** — Hebrew הִתְאַמֵּץ, literally "made himself strong".
- **"rebelled against" → "has revolted against"** (v19) — Hebrew פָּשַׁע, the verb of פֶּשַׁע "transgression": to break faith with one to whom allegiance is owed, to break away. "Revolt" says that; "transgress" would speak of a law, not of a lord. "Rebel" is kept for מָרַד / מָרָה (reviewer).
- **"congregation"** (v20) — Hebrew עֵדָה. **"there was none that followed the house of David" → "There was none after the house of David"** — Hebrew לֹא הָיָה אַחֲרֵי.
- **"chosen men, which were warriors" → "chosen men who made war"** (v21) — Hebrew בָּחוּר עֹשֵׂה מִלְחָמָה, literally "chosen, making war" (singular, of the whole number). **"fight against" → "fight with"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew נִלְחַם עִם. **"to bring the kingdom again" → "to return the kingship"** — Hebrew הַמְּלוּכָה; in v26 the word is מַמְלָכָה "kingdom".
- **"for this thing is from me" → "for from me this thing has come to be"** (v24) — Hebrew כִּי מֵאִתִּי נִהְיָה הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה. **"hearkened therefore to the word" → "heard the word"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶת. **"returned to depart" → "turned back to go"** — Hebrew וַיָּשֻׁבוּ לָלֶכֶת.
- **"mount Ephraim" → "the hill country of Ephraim"** (v25) — Hebrew הַר אֶפְרַיִם, "the mountain of Ephraim". **"dwelt"** — Hebrew יָשַׁב.
- **"to do sacrifice" → "to offer sacrifices"** (v27) — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת זְבָחִים; the verb is "do / make". **"their lord"** — Hebrew אֲדֹנֵיהֶם.
- **"It is too much for you to go up" → "Enough for you of going up"** (v28) — Hebrew רַב־לָכֶם מֵעֲלוֹת, as Deut 1:6.
- **"behold thy gods, O Israel, which brought thee up" → "Behold your gods, Israel, who brought you up"** (v28) — Hebrew הִנֵּה אֱלֹהֶיךָ יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר הֶעֱלוּךָ: the verb "brought you up" is plural, and there are two calves; the words are those of Exod 32:4, 8 ("These are your gods, Israel, who brought you up"). אֱלֹהִים is plural in form and is the same word as "*Elohim*"; the same words can be heard as "Behold your Elohim, Israel, who brought you up" (the plural of אֱלֹהִים sometimes takes a plural verb when the one God is meant; Neh 9:18 retells the words of Exod 32 in the singular). The verse keeps the plural of the Hebrew. The Septuagint has long additions in this chapter (after v24; from memory); none of them is in the Masoretic Text.
- **"set" / "put" → "put" / "placed"** (v29) — Hebrew שִׂים and נָתַן. **"the other" → "the one"** — Hebrew הָאֶחָד both times.
- **"the people went to worship before the one, even unto Dan" → "the people went before the one, as far as Dan"** (v30) — "to worship" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"an house of high places"** (v31) — Hebrew בֵּית בָּמוֹת, singular "house". **"of the lowest of the people" → "from the ends of the people"** — Hebrew מִקְצוֹת הָעָם, "from the ends / from the whole range": from every part of the people, not "the lowest".
- **"ordained a feast" → "made a feast"** (v32, 33) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ חָג. **"he offered upon the altar" → "he went up upon the altar"** (v32, 33) — Hebrew וַיַּעַל עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ; the form can also be read "he offered up on the altar". **"placed" → "made … stand"** (v32) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד.
- **"which he had devised of his own heart" → "that he had devised from his heart"** (v33) — the reading tradition (qere) has מִלִּבּוֹ "from his heart"; the written text has מלבד "apart". בָּדָא "devise, invent" is found only here and in Neh 6:8.
- **"and burnt incense" → "to make smoke"** (v33) — Hebrew לְהַקְטִיר, "turn into smoke" (of sacrifices and of incense alike), with no object.

### Notes
- **Divided monarchy** — Solomon's kingdom splits. Israel under Jeroboam; Judah (and Benjamin, v21, 23) under Rehoboam.
- **Shechem assembly** — significant: covenant-site (Josh 24). Rehoboam goes north to be made king.
- **"Your father made our yoke hard"** — Solomon's forced labor bred the break.
- **Elders and "children"** — Rehoboam forsakes the counsel of the elders for that of the men who had grown up with him.
- **Scorpions** (v11) — likely barbed whips called "scorpions."
- **Adoram stoned** — the man over the forced labor (cf. 2 Sam 20:24; 1 Kgs 4:6).
- **Jeroboam's calves** — to keep Israel from going up to the house of Yahweh in Jerusalem, he sets calves in Bethel and Dan. "Behold your gods, Israel" echoes Exod 32 (Aaron's calf).
- **Foundational sin of the Northern Kingdom** — "the sin of Jeroboam" is named throughout Kings.
- **Priests not of the sons of Levi** (v31).
