## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Baalim" → "Baals"** (v18) — Hebrew plural.
- **"halt ye between two opinions" → "limp between two opinions"** (v21) — *posechim*; image of a lame man stepping awkwardly on two alternative supports.
- **"Lord of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v15).
- **"knives and lancets" → "swords and lances"** (v28).
- **"brook" → "Wadi"** (v40) — Kishon.
- **Elijah's taunt (v27)** — "musing / wandered aside / on a journey / asleep" — Hebrew *siach* and *sig* (euphemism possibly for "relieving himself"). Direct mockery of Baal-myth tropes (dying/rising god, seasonal absence).

### Notes
- **Obadiah** — not the prophet of the book of Obadiah. A Yahwist official hiding prophets during Jezebel's purge.
- **Mount Carmel** — Phoenician-Israelite border highland. Baal worship established there. Setting the contest on Baal territory maximizes the polemic.
- **450 prophets of Baal + 400 of Asherah** — Asherah prophets drop out of the narrative (v22ff); some LXX mss omit them.
- **"God who answers by fire"** — Baal = storm-lightning god. Elijah challenges Baal on his strongest supposed attribute.
- **Self-laceration** (v28) — Canaanite mourning/ecstatic rite; Ugaritic texts attest cutting for Baal.
- **Twelve stones / trench** — symbolic reclamation of twelve-tribe unity in a divided kingdom. Excess water makes divine fire more impressive.
- **Slaughter at Kishon** — echoes Deborah's victory (Judg 5:21). Carrying out Deut 13's penalty for prophets of other gods.
- **Man's-hand cloud** — classic weather sign over the Mediterranean. End of drought.
- **Running before Ahab** — supernatural endurance; a prophetic honor to escort the king.
