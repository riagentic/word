# 1 Kings 6

[1] In the four hundred and eightieth year after the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, in the fourth year of ⟦solomon|Shlomo's|Solomon's⟧ reign over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, in the month of Ziv, which is the second month, he began to build the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] The house that King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high.

[3] The vestibule in front of the nave of the house was twenty cubits long, corresponding to the width of the house, and ten cubits deep in front of the house.

[4] He made for the house recessed windows with narrowing frames.

[5] Against the wall of the house he built a structure of side rooms all around, against the walls of the house around both the nave and the inner sanctuary; and he made side chambers all around.

[6] The lowest story was five cubits wide, the middle was six cubits wide, and the third was seven cubits wide; for on the outside of the house he made offset ledges all around, so that the beams would not be inserted into the walls of the house.

[7] The house, when it was being built, was built of stone finished at the quarry; so neither hammer nor axe nor any tool of iron was heard in the house while it was being built.

[8] The entrance for the lowest story was on the south side of the house; they went up with winding stairs to the middle story, and from the middle to the third.

[9] So he built the house and finished it; he covered the house with beams and planks of cedar.

[10] He built the side chambers against the whole house, each five cubits high; they were attached to the house with timbers of cedar.

[11] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, saying,

[12] "Concerning this house that you are building, if you will walk in my statutes, obey my ordinances, and keep all my commandments, walking in them, then I will establish my word with you that I spoke to David your father.

[13] I will dwell among the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and will not forsake my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[14] So ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built the house and finished it.

[15] He lined the walls of the house inside with boards of cedar; from the floor of the house to the rafters of the ceiling he covered them on the inside with wood. He covered the floor of the house with planks of cypress.

[16] He built twenty cubits of the rear of the house with boards of cedar from the floor to the walls; he built them for it within, as an inner sanctuary, as the Most Holy Place.

[17] The house — that is, the nave in front — was forty cubits long.

[18] The cedar within the house was carved with gourds and open flowers; all was cedar, no stone was seen.

[19] He prepared an inner sanctuary within the house in which to set the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[20] The inner sanctuary was twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and twenty cubits high; he overlaid it with pure gold. He also made an altar of cedar.

[21] So ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ overlaid the house within with pure gold; he drew chains of gold across, in front of the inner sanctuary, and overlaid it with gold.

[22] He overlaid the whole house with gold, until he had finished all the house; also the whole altar that belonged to the inner sanctuary he overlaid with gold.

[23] In the inner sanctuary he made two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ of olive wood, each ten cubits high.

[24] Five cubits was one wing of the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧, and five cubits the other wing of the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧; from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other was ten cubits.

[25] The other ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ also was ten cubits; both the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ were of the same measurement and shape.

[26] The height of one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ was ten cubits, and so was that of the other ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧.

[27] He put the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ in the innermost part of the house; they spread out the wings of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ so that the wing of the one touched one wall, and the wing of the other ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ touched the other wall; their wings touched each other in the middle of the house.

[28] He overlaid the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ with gold.

[29] On all the walls of the house, all around, he carved engraved figures of ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, palm trees, and open flowers, both in the inner and the outer rooms.

[30] He overlaid the floor of the house with gold, in both the inner and the outer rooms.

[31] For the entrance to the inner sanctuary he made doors of olive wood; the lintel and the doorposts were five-sided.

[32] The two doors also were of olive wood; he carved on them carvings of ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, palm trees, and open flowers; he overlaid them with gold, and spread gold over the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ and over the palm trees.

[33] So also he made for the entrance of the nave doorposts of olive wood, four-sided,

[34] and two doors of cypress wood; the two leaves of the one door were folding, and the two leaves of the other door were folding.

[35] On them he carved ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, palm trees, and open flowers; he overlaid them with gold evenly applied to the carved work.

[36] He built the inner court with three courses of hewn stone and one course of cedar beams.

[37] In the fourth year the foundation of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was laid, in the month of Ziv.

[38] In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul (which is the eighth month), the house was finished in all its parts and according to all its specifications. So he built it in seven years.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"fir" → "cypress"** (v15, 34).
- **"oracle" → "inner sanctuary"** (v5, 16, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 31) — Hebrew דְּבִיר *devir*. The Holy of Holies.
- **"knops" → "gourds"** (v18) — Hebrew פְּקָעִים *peqa'im*. Gourd-shaped decorations.
- **"porch" → "vestibule"** (v3).
- **"temple" (v3) → "nave"** — Hebrew הֵיכָל *heikhal*. Main hall; distinct from inner sanctuary.
- **"month Zif / Bul"** (v1, 38) — Hebrew ancient month names. Ziv = second month (April-May), Bul = eighth month (October-November).

### Notes
- **480 years from Exodus** (v1) — classical chronological marker. Places exodus ~1446 BC if Solomon's 4th year is ~966 BC. Some scholars read as symbolic (12 generations × 40 years).
- **Temple dimensions** — 60×20×30 cubits (~90×30×45 feet). Similar pattern to tabernacle, doubled in size.
- **Seven-year construction** (v38) — contrast with David's palace of 13 years (7:1). Temple completed first.
- **Silent construction** (v7) — no metal tools heard. Stones pre-cut at quarry. Emphasizes sacred quiet.
- **Gold overlay throughout** — cedar-and-gold interior. Temple as royal-cosmic microcosm.
- **Kerubim 10 cubits tall** — massive olive-wood figures overlaid with gold, wings spanning the whole 20-cubit-wide sanctuary. Divine throne-imagery.
- **Conditional promise** (v12–13) — Yahweh's presence depends on covenantal obedience. Sets up later catastrophe when the covenant is broken.
