# 1 Kings 6

[1] And it came to pass in the four hundred and eightieth year of the going out of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv — it is the second month — of the reign of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that he built the house for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] And the house that King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: sixty cubits its length, and twenty its width, and thirty cubits its height.

[3] And the porch facing the temple of the house: twenty cubits its length, facing the width of the house; ten cubits its width, facing the house.

[4] And he made for the house windows of closed frames.

[5] And he built against the wall of the house a side structure around, against the walls of the house around, of the temple and of the inner room; and he made side chambers around.

[6] The lowest side structure, five cubits its width; and the middle, six cubits its width; and the third, seven cubits its width; for he placed offsets on the house around, outside, so as not to take hold in the walls of the house.

[7] And the house, when it was being built, was built of whole stone from the quarry; and hammers and the axe, any article of iron, was not heard in the house when it was being built.

[8] The entrance of the middle side chamber was at the right shoulder of the house; and by winding stairs they would go up to the middle, and from the middle to the third.

[9] And he built the house and finished it; and he covered the house with beams and rows of cedar.

[10] And he built the side structure against all the house, five cubits its height; and it took hold of the house with cedar wood.

[11] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, saying,

[12] "This house that you are building: if you walk in my statutes and do my ordinances and keep all my commandments, to walk in them, then I will establish my word with you, which I spoke to David your father.

[13] And I will abide in the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I will not forsake my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[14] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built the house and finished it.

[15] And he built the walls of the house on the inside with planks of cedar; from the floor of the house to the walls of the ceiling he overlaid with wood on the inside; and he overlaid the floor of the house with planks of cypress.

[16] And he built the twenty cubits from the rear of the house with planks of cedar, from the floor to the walls; and he built for it on the inside, for an inner room, for the Holy of Holies.

[17] And forty cubits was the house, that is, the temple in front.

[18] And cedar was on the house within, a carving of gourds and open blossoms; all was cedar, no stone was seen.

[19] And an inner room in the midst of the house, within, he prepared, to place there the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[20] And before the inner room: twenty cubits in length and twenty cubits in width and twenty cubits its height; and he overlaid it with refined gold. And he overlaid an altar with cedar.

[21] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ overlaid the house within with refined gold, and he drew chains of gold across before the inner room, and he overlaid it with gold.

[22] And all the house he overlaid with gold, until all the house was complete; and all the altar that belonged to the inner room he overlaid with gold.

[23] And he made in the inner room two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ of oil-tree wood; ten cubits its height.

[24] And five cubits was the one wing of the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧, and five cubits the second wing of the ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧: ten cubits from the ends of its wings to the ends of its wings.

[25] And ten cubits was the second ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧; one measure and one form for the two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[26] The height of the one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ was ten cubits, and so the second ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧.

[27] And he placed the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ in the midst of the inner house; and they spread out the wings of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, and the wing of the one touched the wall, and the wing of the second ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ was touching the second wall; and their wings toward the midst of the house were touching, wing to wing.

[28] And he overlaid the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ with gold.

[29] And all the walls of the house around he carved with engravings of carvings of ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ and palm trees and open blossoms, within and without.

[30] And the floor of the house he overlaid with gold, within and without.

[31] And the entrance of the inner room he made with doors of oil-tree wood; the pilaster, the doorposts, a fifth.

[32] And two doors of oil-tree wood: and he carved on them carvings of ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ and palm trees and open blossoms, and he overlaid them with gold; and he spread the gold over the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ and over the palm trees.

[33] And so he made for the entrance of the temple doorposts of oil-tree wood, from a fourth,

[34] and two doors of cypress wood: two sides the one door, turning; and two hangings the second door, turning.

[35] And he carved ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ and palm trees and open blossoms, and he overlaid them with gold, made even upon the incised work.

[36] And he built the inner court, three rows of hewn stone and a row of cut cedars.

[37] In the fourth year the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was founded, in the moon of Ziv.

[38] And in the eleventh year, in the moon of Bul — it is the eighth month — the house was finished, as to all its matters and as to all its ordinances; and he built it seven years.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Measures** (throughout) — cubits as the Hebrew gives them, in the Hebrew order ("sixty cubits its length"). Often the Hebrew says "by the cubit" (בָּאַמָּה: v3, 6, 17, 25, 26); it is the same as "cubits".
- **"after the children of Israel were come out" → "of the going out of the sons of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew לְצֵאת בְּנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל; בְּנֵי is "sons of". The Hebrew repeats "year" ("in the eightieth year and four hundredth year").
- **"he began to build the house of the LORD" → "he built the house for Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּבֶן הַבַּיִת לַיהוָה: "and he built"; "began" is not in the Hebrew. The order of the verse is the Hebrew's: "of the reign of Solomon" belongs to "in the fourth year".
- **"month Zif / Bul"; "month" → "moon"** (v1, 37, 38) — Ziv and Bul are old month names. In v1 the word is חֹדֶשׁ "month"; in v37, 38 it is יֶרַח "moon", and v38 has both ("the moon of Bul — it is the eighth month").
- **"breadth" → "width"** (throughout) — Hebrew רֹחַב, as in the chapters of the tent (Exod 25–27).
- **"porch" kept** (v3) — Hebrew אוּלָם *ulam*: the entrance hall before the house.
- **"before the temple of the house" → "facing the temple of the house"** (v3) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי "upon the face of", three times in the verse. הֵיכָל *heikhal* "temple" (of a king: "palace") names here the great front room of the house, as v5, 17, 33; the whole building is "the house".
- **"windows of narrow lights" → "windows of closed frames"** (v4) — Hebrew חַלּוֹנֵי שְׁקֻפִים אֲטֻמִים. Both words are uncertain: שְׁקֻפִים "frames" (as 7:4, 5), אֲטֻמִים "closed, stopped up" (perhaps latticed, or narrowing). The plainest sense is given.
- **"chambers round about" → "a side structure around"** (v5, 6, 10) — Hebrew יָצִיעַ (qere; the written form is יצוע, "couch"): something laid out against the house, a lean-to of stories. The sense is uncertain. In v6 it takes feminine adjectives, as צֵלָע "side chamber" does.
- **"oracle" → "inner room"** (v5, 16, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 31) — Hebrew דְּבִיר *devir*: the hindmost room of the house, called in v16 "the Holy of Holies". The KJV's "oracle" derives it from דִּבֶּר "speak" (so Jerome, from memory); "back part" is the commonly held sense.
- **"chambers" → "side chambers"** (v5, 8) — Hebrew צְלָעוֹת, the plural of צֵלָע "side". The same word is "planks" in v15, 16 and "sides" (door leaves) in v34.
- **"nethermost chamber" → "The lowest side structure"** (v6) — the Hebrew has הַיָּצִיעַ here, not "chamber".
- **"he made narrowed rests" → "he placed offsets"** (v6) — Hebrew מִגְרָעוֹת, only here, from גָּרַע "diminish": the wall was stepped back. "Placed" is נָתַן "give".
- **"that the beams should not be fastened in" → "so as not to take hold in"** (v6) — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי אֲחֹז; "the beams" is not in the Hebrew. The same verb stands in v10, "it took hold of the house".
- **"stone made ready before it was brought thither" → "whole stone from the quarry"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶבֶן־שְׁלֵמָה מַסָּע: שְׁלֵמָה "whole" (Deut 27:6), מַסָּע "a pulling out", the quarrying (or "the transport"); uncertain.
- **"tool of iron" → "article of iron"** (v7) — Hebrew כְּלִי בַרְזֶל, as Num 35:16. The verb "was heard" is singular.
- **"in the right side of the house" → "at the right shoulder of the house"** (v8) — Hebrew כֶּתֶף "shoulder"; "right" is the south for one who faces east. "Right" is kept here and in chapter 7, where it stands beside "south" (7:39).
- **"The door for the middle chamber" → "The entrance of the middle side chamber"** (v8) — Hebrew פֶּתַח "entrance". The Masoretic Text has "middle"; the Septuagint and the Targum have "lowest" (from memory).
- **"winding stairs" kept** (v8) — Hebrew לוּלִים, only here; the sense is uncertain (winding stairs, or trapdoors).
- **"beams and boards of cedar" → "beams and rows of cedar"** (v9) — Hebrew גֵּבִים וּשְׂדֵרֹת בָּאֲרָזִים. Both nouns are uncertain; שְׂדֵרֹת is "rows, ranks".
- **"they rested on the house" → "it took hold of the house"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיֶּאֱחֹז, singular.
- **"execute my judgments" → "do my ordinances"** (v12) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטַי תַּעֲשֶׂה; מִשְׁפָּט is "judgment", of a ruling "ordinance".
- **"then will I perform my word" → "then I will establish my word"** (v12) — Hebrew וַהֲקִמֹתִי, literally "make stand".
- **"I will dwell among the children of Israel" → "I will abide in the midst of the sons of Israel"** (v13) — Hebrew שָׁכַן "abide", the root of מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling".
- **"boards of cedar" → "planks of cedar"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew צַלְעוֹת (see v5).
- **"both the floor of the house, and the walls of the cieling" → "from the floor of the house to the walls of the ceiling"** (v15) — Hebrew מִקַּרְקַע … עַד־קִירוֹת הַסִּפֻּן. סִפֻּן is "covering" (from סָפַן "cover", v9). The Masoretic Text has "walls"; "beams" (קוֹרוֹת) is read by the Septuagint (from memory).
- **"covered" → "overlaid"** (v15, 20–22, 28, 30, 32, 35) — Hebrew צִפָּה, as in Exod 25.
- **"on the sides of the house" → "from the rear of the house"** (v16) — Hebrew מִיַּרְכְּתֵי (qere), literally "from the two flanks", the far end.
- **"he even built them for it within, even for the oracle, even for the most holy place" → "he built for it on the inside, for an inner room, for the Holy of Holies"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּבֶן לוֹ מִבַּיִת לִדְבִיר לְקֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים. "Place" is not in the Hebrew (as Exod 26:33).
- **"the temple before it" → "the temple in front"** (v17) — Hebrew לִפְנָי, a form found only here (as pointed it could be read "before me"); taken as "in front".
- **"carved with knops and open flowers" → "a carving of gourds and open blossoms"** (v18) — Hebrew מִקְלַעַת פְּקָעִים וּפְטוּרֵי צִצִּים. פְּקָעִים are taken as gourd-shaped ornaments (uncertain); פְּטוּרֵי צִצִּים is literally "opened ones of blossoms".
- **"the forepart" → "before the inner room"** (v20) — Hebrew וְלִפְנֵי הַדְּבִיר, as the Masoretic Text has it; the sentence is difficult.
- **"pure gold" → "refined gold"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew זָהָב סָגוּר, literally "shut-up gold"; the sense is uncertain (pure, solid or stored gold). "Pure gold" in Exodus is זָהָב טָהוֹר.
- **"and so covered the altar which was of cedar" → "And he overlaid an altar with cedar"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיְצַף מִזְבֵּחַ אָרֶז: "altar" is not in the construct form, so "cedar" is what it is overlaid with. The Septuagint has "he made" (from memory).
- **"he made a partition by the chains of gold" → "he drew chains of gold across"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיְעַבֵּר בְּרַתּוּקוֹת זָהָב, literally "he made pass over with chains of gold". רַתּוּקוֹת (qere) is another word than the "chains" of 7:17.
- **"until he had finished all the house" → "until all the house was complete"** (v22) — Hebrew עַד־תֹּם כָּל־הַבָּיִת.
- **"olive tree" → "oil-tree wood"** (v23, 31, 32, 33) — Hebrew עֲצֵי־שֶׁמֶן, literally "wood of oil". Commonly taken as olive wood; Neh 8:15 names the "oil tree" beside the olive, so the tree is not certain.
- **"each ten cubits high" → "ten cubits its height"** (v23) — the Hebrew has the singular "its".
- **"the other" → "the second"** (v24–27) — Hebrew הַשֵּׁנִי / הַשֵּׁנִית.
- **"of one measure and one size" → "one measure and one form"** (v25) — Hebrew קֶצֶב, "cut, shape".
- **"within the inner house" → "in the midst of the inner house"** (v27) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ הַבַּיִת הַפְּנִימִי. "They spread out" has no subject named.
- **"carved figures of cherubims" → "engravings of carvings of cherubim"** (v29) — Hebrew פִּתּוּחֵי מִקְלְעוֹת. תִּמֹרֹת "palm trees" is a form used only of this ornament.
- **"within and without" kept** (v29, 30) — Hebrew מִלִּפְנִים וְלַחִיצוֹן: the inner and the outer room are meant; "rooms" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the lintel and side posts were a fifth part of the wall" → "the pilaster, the doorposts, a fifth"** (v31) — Hebrew הָאַיִל מְזוּזוֹת חֲמִשִׁית, word for word. The meaning is not known: אַיִל is a projecting part of a doorway (as in Ezek 40–41); "a fifth" may be a fifth part or five-sided. "Of the wall" is not in the Hebrew. So also v33, "from a fourth" (מֵאֵת רְבִעִית).
- **"spread gold upon" → "spread the gold over"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיָּרֶד, from רָדַד "beat out, spread" (or "brought down"); uncertain.
- **"door" → "entrance"** (v31, 33) — Hebrew פֶּתַח; "doors" is דְּלָתוֹת.
- **"the two leaves of the one door were folding, and the two leaves of the other door were folding" → "two sides the one door, turning; and two hangings the second door, turning"** (v34) — the Masoretic Text has two different words: צְלָעִים "sides" for the first door and קְלָעִים for the second (elsewhere the "hangings" of the court; or from קָלַע "carve"). Most take the second as a slip for the first; the Masoretic Text is kept. גְּלִילִים is "turning" (on pivots, or folding).
- **"covered them with gold fitted upon the carved work" → "overlaid them with gold, made even upon the incised work"** (v35) — Hebrew מְיֻשָּׁר עַל־הַמְּחֻקֶּה.
- **"a row of cedar beams" → "a row of cut cedars"** (v36) — Hebrew כְּרֻתֹת אֲרָזִים, literally "cut things of cedars": beams.
- **"was the foundation of the house of the LORD laid" → "the house of Yahweh was founded"** (v37) — Hebrew יֻסַּד.
- **"throughout all the parts thereof, and according to all the fashion of it" → "as to all its matters and as to all its ordinances"** (v38) — Hebrew לְכָל־דְּבָרָיו וּלְכָל־מִשְׁפָּטָיו (qere; the written form is singular): מִשְׁפָּט is the "ordinance" of a building also in Exod 26:30.

### Notes
- **480 years from the going out** (v1) — a chronological marker. Some read it as a round figure (12 generations × 40 years).
- **Temple dimensions** — 60 × 20 × 30 cubits; twice the length and width of the dwelling of Exod 26.
- **Seven-year construction** (v38) — compare the thirteen years of Solomon's own house (7:1).
- **Silent construction** (v7) — no iron heard in the house; compare Exod 20:25; Deut 27:5.
- **Cherubim ten cubits high** — two figures whose wings together span the twenty cubits of the inner room.
- **Conditional word** (v12–13) — the promise to David is tied to walking in the statutes.
