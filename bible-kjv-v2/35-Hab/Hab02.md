# Habakkuk 2

[1] Upon my watch let me stand,
and let me take my stand upon the rampart,
and let me watch to see what he will speak with me,
and what I shall return concerning my reproof.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered me and said,
Write the vision,
and make it plain upon the tablets,
so that the one who reads it may run.

[3] For still the vision is for the appointed time,
and it pants toward the end, and it will not lie;
if it lingers, wait for it,
for it will surely come; it will not delay.

[4] Behold, puffed up, not upright is his soul in him;
but **the righteous by his faithfulness shall live**.

[5] And moreover, the wine deals treacherously;
a haughty mighty man, and he does not abide,
who has enlarged his soul like Sheol,
and he is like death and is not sated;
and he has gathered to himself all the nations,
and collected to himself all the peoples.

[6] Shall not these, all of them, lift up a parable against him,
and a figure, riddles for him?
And he shall say:
Woe to him who multiplies what is not his — how long? —
and makes pledges heavy upon himself!

[7] Shall not those who bite you rise up suddenly,
and those who make you tremble awake,
and you shall become pillage for them?

[8] Because you have spoiled many nations,
all the rest of the peoples shall spoil you,
because of the blood of man and the violence of the land,
the town and all who dwell in it.

[9] Woe to him who gains evil gain for his house,
to set his nest on high,
to be delivered from the palm of evil!

[10] You have counseled shame for your house,
cutting off many peoples, and sinning against your soul.

[11] For the stone shall cry out from the wall,
and the beam from the wood shall answer it.

[12] Woe to him who builds a city with bloodguilt,
and establishes a town with injustice!

[13] Behold, is it not from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts
that peoples labor for fire,
and folks grow weary for emptiness?

[14] For the earth shall be filled
to know the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
as the waters cover over the sea.

[15] Woe to him who makes his neighbor drink,
pouring out your fury, and also making drunk,
in order to look upon their nakedness!

[16] You are sated with disgrace instead of glory;
drink, you too, and be shown uncircumcised!
The cup of the right hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall come around to you,
and utter disgrace upon your glory.

[17] For the violence of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ shall cover you,
and the havoc of beasts shall terrify them,
because of the blood of man and the violence of the land,
the town and all who dwell in it.

[18] What does a carved image profit, that its former has carved it,
a molten image and a teacher of falsehood,
that the former trusts upon what he formed,
to make dumb worthless idols?

[19] Woe to him who says to the wood, Awake!
Rouse yourself! to silent stone.
It — shall it teach?
Behold, it is encased in gold and silver,
and there is no breath at all in its midst.

[20] **But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is in his holy temple;
hush before him, all the earth.**

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, the prophets' formula.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the quotation marks are dropped (the Hebrew has none).
- **"I will stand upon my watch, and set me upon the tower, and will watch to see what he will say unto me, and what I shall answer when I am reproved" → "Upon my watch let me stand, and let me take my stand upon the rampart, and let me watch to see what he will speak with me, and what I shall return concerning my reproof"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶעֱמֹדָה, וְאֶתְיַצְּבָה cohortatives ("let me"); מִשְׁמַרְתִּי "my watch"; מָצוֹר, elsewhere "siege", here the place of the watch (a rampart; KJV "tower"); צִפָּה "watch" (as צֹפֶה "watchman"); יְדַבֶּר־בִּי "speak with me" (or "in me"); אָשִׁיב "return" (an answer); תּוֹכַחְתִּי "my reproof" (the reproof spoken to me or by me: open).
- **"Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables, that he may run that readeth it" → "Write the vision, and make it plain upon the tablets, so that the one who reads it may run"** (v2) — Hebrew הַלֻּחוֹת "the tablets" (article kept); בָּאֵר "make plain"; לְמַעַן יָרוּץ קוֹרֵא בוֹ.
- **"For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry" → "For still the vision is for the appointed time, and it pants toward the end, and it will not lie; if it lingers, wait for it, for it will surely come; it will not delay"** (v3) — Hebrew עוֹד "still"; לַמּוֹעֵד "for the appointed time"; וְיָפֵחַ "and it pants" (פּוּחַ "breathe"; or a noun "a witness": uncertain; KJV "speak"); יְכַזֵּב "lie"; יִתְמַהְמַהּ "linger"; חַכֵּה "wait"; בֹא יָבֹא, literally "coming it will come" ("surely"); יְאַחֵר "delay" (two verbs, not "tarry" twice). Heb 10:37 quotes the end of the verse in the Greek of the Septuagint, which has "he who comes" (from memory); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him" → "Behold, puffed up, not upright is his soul in him"** (v4) — Hebrew עֻפְּלָה, found as a pual only here, "puffed up" (or "swollen"; uncertain); the Hebrew order kept. **"but the just shall live by his faith" → "but the righteous by his faithfulness shall live"** — word for word: וְצַדִּיק בֶּאֱמוּנָתוֹ יִחְיֶה; צַדִּיק "righteous"; אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness" (firmness, steadiness, the noun of אָמַן "be firm"; "faith" is the Greek πίστις). Rom 1:17 and Gal 3:11 quote it as "the righteous shall live by faith" (no "his"), Heb 10:38 as "my righteous one shall live by faith"; the Septuagint, from memory, has "by my faithfulness". The Hebrew is translated as it stands; the Greek forms belong to those books.
- **"Yea also, because he transgresseth by wine, he is a proud man, neither keepeth at home, who enlargeth his desire as hell, and is as death, and cannot be satisfied, but gathereth unto him all nations, and heapeth unto him all people" → "And moreover, the wine deals treacherously; a haughty mighty man, and he does not abide, who has enlarged his soul like Sheol, and he is like death and is not sated; and he has gathered to himself all the nations, and collected to himself all the peoples"** (v5) — Hebrew וְאַף כִּי "and moreover"; הַיַּיִן בּוֹגֵד "the wine deals treacherously" (the Habakkuk scroll from Qumran reads הוֹן "wealth", from memory; the MT is kept); גֶּבֶר "mighty man"; יָהִיר "haughty"; יִנְוֶה, from נָוֶה "habitation": "abide" (stay at rest; uncertain); נַפְשׁוֹ "his soul" (the throat, appetite: KJV "desire"); שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (not "hell"); וַיֶּאֱסֹף, וַיִּקְבֹּץ past forms, "gather" and "collect" (two verbs).
- **"Shall not all these take up a parable against him, and a taunting proverb against him, and say" → "Shall not these, all of them, lift up a parable against him, and a figure, riddles for him? And he shall say"** (v6) — Hebrew מָשָׁל יִשָּׂאוּ "lift up a parable"; מְלִיצָה "figure" (a saying whose sense must be drawn out); חִידוֹת "riddles"; וְיֹאמַר singular. **"Woe to him that increaseth that which is not his! how long? and to him that ladeth himself with thick clay!" → "Woe to him who multiplies what is not his — how long? — and makes pledges heavy upon himself!"** — הוֹי "Woe" ("to him who" supplied for the participle); עַבְטִיט, found only here, "pledges" (a mass of pledges taken; KJV "thick clay" divides it as עָב טִיט).
- **"Shall they not rise up suddenly that shall bite thee, and awake that shall vex thee, and thou shalt be for booties unto them?" → "Shall not those who bite you rise up suddenly, and those who make you tremble awake, and you shall become pillage for them?"** (v7) — Hebrew נֹשְׁכֶיךָ "those who bite you" (נָשַׁךְ "bite", also "lend at interest", נֶשֶׁךְ "interest": the creditors); מְזַעְזְעֶיךָ "those who make you tremble"; מְשִׁסּוֹת "pillage" (plural).
- **"Because thou hast spoiled many nations, all the remnant of the people shall spoil thee; because of men's blood, and for the violence of the land, of the city, and of all that dwell therein" → "Because you have spoiled many nations, all the rest of the peoples shall spoil you, because of the blood of man and the violence of the land, the town and all who dwell in it"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׁלּוֹתָ … יְשָׁלּוּךָ, one verb twice; יֶתֶר "the rest"; מִדְּמֵי אָדָם, literally "from the bloods of man"; חֲמַס־אֶרֶץ "the violence of the land" (done to it); קִרְיָה "town" (as v12, 17).
- **"Woe to him that coveteth an evil covetousness to his house, that he may set his nest on high, that he may be delivered from the power of evil!" → "Woe to him who gains evil gain for his house, to set his nest on high, to be delivered from the palm of evil!"** (v9) — Hebrew בֹּצֵעַ בֶּצַע רָע "gaining evil gain" (בֶּצַע "unjust gain"); מִכַּף "from the palm".
- **"Thou hast consulted shame to thy house by cutting off many people, and hast sinned against thy soul" → "You have counseled shame for your house, cutting off many peoples, and sinning against your soul"** (v10) — Hebrew יָעַצְתָּ "you have counseled"; בֹּשֶׁת "shame"; קְצוֹת "cutting off"; וְחוֹטֵא נַפְשֶׁךָ, a participle, "and sinning (against) your soul" (the former "forfeited your life" was paraphrase).
- **"For the stone shall cry out of the wall, and the beam out of the timber shall answer it"** (v11) — Hebrew תִּזְעָק "shall cry out"; כָּפִיס, found only here, "beam" (uncertain); מֵעֵץ "from the wood"; יַעֲנֶנָּה "shall answer it".
- **"Woe to him that buildeth a town with blood, and stablisheth a city by iniquity!" → "Woe to him who builds a city with bloodguilt, and establishes a town with injustice!"** (v12) — Hebrew עִיר … קִרְיָה "city … town"; בְּדָמִים, literally "with bloods" (as Nah 3:1); כּוֹנֵן "establish"; עַוְלָה "injustice".
- **"Behold, is it not of the LORD of hosts that the people shall labour in the very fire, and the people shall weary themselves for very vanity?" → "Behold, is it not from Yahweh of Hosts that peoples labor for fire, and folks grow weary for emptiness?"** (v13) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא הִנֵּה "is it not, behold"; עַמִּים … לְאֻמִּים "peoples … folks"; בְּדֵי־אֵשׁ … בְּדֵי־רִיק, literally "for the need of fire … for the need of emptiness" (= only for fire, for nothing); יָגַע "labor", יָעֵף "grow weary" (Jer 51:58 has the same words).
- **"For the earth shall be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD, as the waters cover the sea" → "For the earth shall be filled to know the glory of Yahweh, as the waters cover over the sea"** (v14) — Hebrew תִּמָּלֵא … לָדַעַת "shall be filled to know" (an infinitive, not the noun "knowledge"); אֶת־כְּבוֹד "the glory"; כַּמַּיִם יְכַסּוּ עַל־יָם "as the waters cover over the sea". Isa 11:9 has other words for the first half (מָלְאָה … דֵּעָה אֶת־יְהוָה "full of the knowledge of Yahweh") and the same picture in the second; each is translated as it stands.
- **"Woe unto him that giveth his neighbour drink, that puttest thy bottle to him, and makest him drunken also, that thou mayest look on their nakedness!" → "Woe to him who makes his neighbor drink, pouring out your fury, and also making drunk, in order to look upon their nakedness!"** (v15) — Hebrew רֵעֵהוּ "his neighbor" (singular); מְסַפֵּחַ "pouring out" (or "joining", "adding"; uncertain); חֲמָתְךָ "your fury" as pointed (KJV "bottle" reads חֵמֶת "skin"; "your" in the middle of the woe, as printed); שַׁכֵּר "making drunk"; מְעוֹרֵיהֶם, found only here, "their nakedness".
- **"Thou art filled with shame for glory: drink thou also, and let thy foreskin be uncovered" → "You are sated with disgrace instead of glory; drink, you too, and be shown uncircumcised!"** (v16) — Hebrew שָׂבַעְתָּ "you are sated"; קָלוֹן "disgrace"; מִכָּבוֹד "rather than glory"; וְהֵעָרֵל, nifal of עָרֵל "uncircumcised" (the Habakkuk scroll and the Septuagint, from memory, read הֵרָעֵל "stagger"; the MT is kept). **"the cup of the LORD's right hand shall be turned unto thee, and shameful spewing shall be on thy glory" → "The cup of the right hand of Yahweh shall come around to you, and utter disgrace upon your glory"** — תִּסּוֹב "shall come around"; קִיקָלוֹן, found only here, a doubled form of קָלוֹן "disgrace" (KJV "shameful spewing" hears קִיא "vomit" in it; uncertain).
- **"For the violence of Lebanon shall cover thee, and the spoil of beasts, which made them afraid" → "For the violence of Lebanon shall cover you, and the havoc of beasts shall terrify them"** (v17) — Hebrew שֹׁד "havoc"; יְחִיתַן "shall terrify them" (feminine plural; the Septuagint, from memory, "you"; the MT is kept). The last line as v8.
- **"What profiteth the graven image that the maker thereof hath graven it; the molten image, and a teacher of lies, that the maker of his work trusteth therein, to make dumb idols?" → "What does a carved image profit, that its former has carved it, a molten image and a teacher of falsehood, that the former trusts upon what he formed, to make dumb worthless idols?"** (v18) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל "carved image" (not "idol"); יֹצְרוֹ "its former" (יָצַר "form"); מַסֵּכָה "molten image"; מוֹרֶה שָּׁקֶר "a teacher of falsehood"; יֹצֵר יִצְרוֹ "the former of his thing formed"; אֱלִילִים "worthless idols"; אִלְּמִים "dumb".
- **"Woe unto him that saith to the wood, Awake; to the dumb stone, Arise, it shall teach! Behold, it is laid over with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all in the midst of it" → "Woe to him who says to the wood, Awake! Rouse yourself! to silent stone. It — shall it teach? Behold, it is encased in gold and silver, and there is no breath at all in its midst"** (v19) — Hebrew הָקִיצָה "Awake"; עוּרִי "rouse yourself" (two verbs); דּוּמָם "silent"; הוּא יוֹרֶה "it shall teach" (a mocking statement, or a question: open); תָּפוּשׂ, תָּפַשׂ "seize": "held fast" in gold and silver (encased); רוּחַ "breath".
- **"But the LORD is in his holy temple: let all the earth keep silence before him" → "But Yahweh is in his holy temple; hush before him, all the earth"** (v20) — Hebrew הֵיכַל קָדְשׁוֹ "the temple of his holiness"; הַס "Hush!" (an interjection, not a verb "keep silence").
