# Habakkuk 2

[1] I will take my stand at my watchpost
and station myself on the tower,
and look out to see what he will say to me,
and what I will answer concerning my complaint.

[2] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered me:

"Write the vision;
make it plain on tablets,
so he may run who reads it.

[3] For still the vision awaits its appointed time;
it hastens to the end — it will not lie.
If it seems slow, wait for it;
it will surely come; it will not delay.

[4] Behold, his soul is puffed up; it is not upright within him,
but **the righteous shall live by his faith**.

[5] Moreover, wine is a traitor,
an arrogant man who is never at rest.
His greed is as wide as Sheol;
like death he has never enough.
He gathers for himself all nations
and collects as his own all peoples."

[6] Shall not all these take up their taunt against him, with scoffing and riddles for him, and say:
"Woe to him who heaps up what is not his own —
for how long? —
and loads himself with pledges!"

[7] Will not your debtors suddenly arise,
and those awake who will make you tremble?
Then you will be spoil for them.

[8] Because you have plundered many nations,
all the remnant of the peoples shall plunder you,
for the blood of man and violence to the earth,
to cities and all who dwell in them.

[9] "Woe to him who gets evil gain for his house,
to set his nest on high,
to be safe from the reach of harm!

[10] You have devised shame for your house
by cutting off many peoples;
you have forfeited your life.

[11] For the stone will cry out from the wall,
and the beam from the woodwork respond.

[12] "Woe to him who builds a town with blood
and founds a city on iniquity!

[13] Behold, is it not from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts
that peoples labor merely for fire,
and nations weary themselves for nothing?

[14] For the earth will be filled
with the knowledge of the glory of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
as the waters cover the sea.

[15] "Woe to him who makes his neighbors drink —
you pour out your wrath and make them drunk,
in order to gaze at their nakedness!

[16] You will have your fill of shame instead of glory.
Drink, yourself, and show your uncircumcision!
The cup in the right hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will come around to you,
and utter shame will come upon your glory!

[17] The violence done to ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ will overwhelm you,
as will the destruction of the beasts that terrified them,
for the blood of man and violence to the earth,
to cities and all who dwell in them.

[18] "What profit is an idol
when its maker has shaped it,
a metal image, a teacher of lies?
For its maker trusts in his own creation
when he makes speechless idols!

[19] Woe to him who says to a wooden thing, Awake;
to a silent stone, Arise!
Can this teach?
Behold, it is overlaid with gold and silver,
and there is no breath at all in it.

[20] **But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is in his holy temple;
let all the earth keep silence before him.**"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the watchman's vigil and woe-oracles are poetry.
- **"I will stand upon my watch, and set me upon the tower, and will watch to see what he will say unto me" → "I will take my stand at my watchpost and station myself on the tower, and look out to see what he will say to me"** (v1) — modernized; "watchpost" for *mishmarti*.
- **"and what I shall answer when I am reproved" → "and what I will answer concerning my complaint"** (v1) — Hebrew עַל תּוֹכַחְתִּי *al tokhachti* = concerning my complaint/reproof; "complaint" more precise.
- **"Write the vision, and make it plain upon tables" → "Write the vision; make it plain on tablets"** (v2) — Hebrew לוּחוֹת *luchot* = tablets; "tablets" more precise; "make it plain" = write it clearly.
- **"For the vision is yet for an appointed time, but at the end it shall speak, and not lie: though it tarry, wait for it; because it will surely come, it will not tarry" → "For still the vision awaits its appointed time; it hastens to the end — it will not lie. If it seems slow, wait for it; it will surely come; it will not delay"** (v3) — Hebrew עוֹד חָזוֹן לַמּוֹעֵד *od chazon lammo'ed*; "appointed time" for *mo'ed*; "it hastens to the end" for *yaphéach laqqetz*; "delay" for *yeacher*.
- **"Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him" → "Behold, his soul is puffed up; it is not upright within him"** (v4) — Hebrew הִנֵּה עֻפְּלָה לֹא יָשְׁרָה נַפְשׁוֹ; "puffed up" for *ufal* = swollen/arrogant.
- **"but the just shall live by his faith" → "but the righteous shall live by his faith"** (v4) — Hebrew וְצַדִּיק בֶּאֱמוּנָתוֹ יִחְיֶה *vetzaddiq be'emunatho yichyeh*; "righteous" for *tzaddiq* (more precise than "just"); "faith/faithfulness" for *emunah*; the famous text retained in full; bold formatting added as key text.
- **"Yea also, because he transgresseth by wine, he is a proud man, neither keepeth at home, who enlargeth his desire as hell, and is as death, and cannot be satisfied" → "Moreover, wine is a traitor, an arrogant man who is never at rest. His greed is as wide as Sheol"** (v5) — Hebrew יִבְגּוֹד הַיַּיִן גֶּבֶר יָהִיר *yivgod hayyayin gever yahir*; "wine is a traitor" (personified); "never at rest" for *welo yinveh*; *Sheol* retained; "greed" for *nefesh*.
- **"and is as death, and cannot be satisfied" → "like death he has never enough"** (v5) — Hebrew כַּמָּוֶת וְלֹא יִשְׂבָּע *kammavet velo yisba'*; "never enough" more vivid.
- **"Woe to him that increaseth that which is not his! how long? and to him that ladeth himself with thick clay!" → "Woe to him who heaps up what is not his own — for how long? — and loads himself with pledges!"** (v6) — Hebrew וּמַכְבִּד עָלָיו עַבְטִיט *umakhbid alav avttit*; *avttit* = pledged goods/debts; "pledges" more precise than "thick clay."
- **"Shall they not rise up suddenly that shall bite thee, and awake that shall vex thee" → "Will not your debtors suddenly arise, and those awake who will make you tremble?"** (v7) — Hebrew נֹשְׁכֶיךָ *noshekha* = your biters/those who bite you = creditors/debtors; "debtors" captures the economic image.
- **"Thou hast consulted shame to thy house by cutting off many people, and hast sinned against thy soul" → "You have devised shame for your house by cutting off many peoples; you have forfeited your life"** (v10) — Hebrew יָעַצְתָּ בֹּשֶׁת לְבֵיתֶךָ *ya'atzta boshet leveitkhah*; "devised shame" for *ya'atz boshet*; "forfeited your life" for *vechatata nafshekha*.
- **"For the stone shall cry out of the wall, and the beam out of the timber shall answer it" → "For the stone will cry out from the wall, and the beam from the woodwork respond"** (v11) — modernized.
- **"that the people shall labour in the very fire, and the people shall weary themselves for very vanity?" → "that peoples labor merely for fire, and nations weary themselves for nothing?"** (v13) — Hebrew יִיגְעוּ עַמִּים בְּדֵי אֵשׁ *yig'u ammim bedey esh*; "labor merely for fire" = their work burns up; "nations" for *le'ummim*.
- **"for the earth shall be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD, as the waters cover the sea" → "For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of Yahweh, as the waters cover the sea"** (v14) — divine name restored; the great eschatological promise.
- **"Woe unto him that giveth his neighbour drink, that puttest thy bottle to him, and makest him drunken also" → "Woe to him who makes his neighbors drink — you pour out your wrath and make them drunk"** (v15) — Hebrew מַסְפֵּה חֲמָתְךָ *maspe chamatkha*; "wrath" for *chemah* = both wrath and poison/heat; the empire forces its conquest-cup on neighbors.
- **"Thou art filled with shame for glory: drink thou also, and let thy foreskin be uncovered" → "You will have your fill of shame instead of glory. Drink, yourself, and show your uncircumcision!"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהֵקֵל עָלֶיךָ מִכָּבוֹד *vehéqel alekhá mikkhavod*; "show your uncircumcision" = be exposed as shameful; "uncircumcision" for *he'arel*.
- **"the cup of the LORD's right hand shall be turned unto thee, and shameful spewing shall be on thy glory" → "The cup in the right hand of Yahweh will come around to you, and utter shame will come upon your glory"** (v16) — Hebrew כּוֹס יְמִין יְהוָה *kos yemin Yahweh*; "come around" for *tissov* = will turn/come around.
- **"What profiteth the graven image that the maker thereof hath graven it; the molten image, and a teacher of lies" → "What profit is an idol when its maker has shaped it, a metal image, a teacher of lies?"** (v18) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל *pesel* = carved image/idol; מַסֵּכָה *massekah* = metal/cast image; "metal image" consistent.
- **"to make dumb idols?" → "when he makes speechless idols!"** (v18) — Hebrew אֱלִילִים אִלְּמִים *elilim illemim* = dumb/mute idols; "speechless idols" more vivid.
- **"But the LORD is in his holy temple: let all the earth keep silence before him" → "But Yahweh is in his holy temple; let all the earth keep silence before him"** (v20) — divine name restored; the climactic contrast with the idol; bold formatting added.

### Notes
- **v4** — "The righteous shall live by faith": quoted Rom 1:17; Gal 3:11; Heb 10:38 — cornerstone of Reformation theology.
- **v14** — parallel to Isa 11:9.
