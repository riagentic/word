# Habakkuk 3

[1] A prayer of ⟦habakkuk|Chavaqquq|Habakkuk⟧ the prophet, according to ⟦shigyonot|Shigyonot|Shigionoth⟧.

[2] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I have heard the report of you,
and your work, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do I fear.
In the midst of the years revive it;
in the midst of the years make it known;
in wrath remember mercy.

[3] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came from Teman,
and the Holy One from Mount Paran. Selah.
His splendor covered the heavens,
and the earth was full of his praise.

[4] His brightness was like the light;
rays flashed from his hand;
and there he veiled his power.

[5] Before him went pestilence,
and plague followed at his heels.

[6] He stood and measured the earth;
he looked and shook the nations;
then the eternal mountains were scattered;
the everlasting hills sank low.
His were the everlasting ways.

[7] I saw the tents of ⟦cushan|Kushan|Cushan⟧ in affliction;
the curtains of the land of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧ did tremble.

[8] Was your wrath against the rivers, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧?
Was your anger against the rivers,
or your indignation against the sea,
when you rode on your horses,
on your chariot of salvation?

[9] You stripped the sheath from your bow,
calling for many arrows. Selah.
You split the earth with rivers.

[10] The mountains saw you and writhed;
the raging waters swept on;
the deep gave forth its voice;
it lifted its hands on high.

[11] The sun and moon stood still in their place
at the light of your arrows as they sped,
at the flash of your glittering spear.

[12] You marched through the earth in fury;
you threshed the nations in anger.

[13] You went out for the salvation of your people,
for the salvation of your anointed.
You crushed the head of the house of the wicked,
laying him bare from thigh to neck. Selah.

[14] You pierced with his own arrows the heads of his warriors,
who came like a whirlwind to scatter me,
rejoicing as if to devour the poor in secret.

[15] You trampled the sea with your horses,
the surging of mighty waters.

[16] I hear, and my body trembles;
my lips quiver at the sound;
rottenness enters into my bones;
my legs tremble beneath me.
Yet I will quietly wait for the day of trouble
to come upon people who invade us.

[17] **Though the fig tree should not blossom,
nor fruit be on the vines,
the produce of the olive fail
and the fields yield no food,
the flock be cut off from the fold
and there be no herd in the stalls,**

**[18] yet I will rejoice in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
I will take joy in the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation.**

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|GOD,⟧ ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord,⟧ is my strength;
he makes my feet like the deer's;
he makes me tread on my high places.

To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Yahweh Adonai"** (v19) — Hebrew יְהוִה אֲדֹנָי, in the MT order.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the entire chapter is a theophanic psalm (liturgical poetry).
- **"O LORD, I have heard thy speech, and was afraid" → "O Yahweh, I have heard the report of you, and your work, O Yahweh, do I fear"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי שִׁמְעֲךָ יָרֵאתִי *shama'ti shim'akha yare'ti*; "report of you" for *shim'akha* = your report/fame; "I fear" more precise than "was afraid."
- **"His glory covered the heavens" → "His splendor covered the heavens"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּסָּה שָׁמַיִם הוֹדוֹ *kissa shamayim hodo*; *hod* = splendor/majesty; "splendor" more vivid than "glory" here.
- **"he had horns coming out of his hand: and there was the hiding of his power" → "rays flashed from his hand; and there he veiled his power"** (v4) — Hebrew וְקַרְנַיִם מִיָּדוֹ לוֹ *veqarnayim miyado lo* = and rays/horns from his hand; *qeren* = horn = ray of light; "rays flashed" captures the light imagery; "veiled his power" for *hevyon uzzon*.
- **"Before him went the pestilence, and burning coals went forth at his feet" → "Before him went pestilence, and plague followed at his heels"** (v5) — Hebrew לִפְנָיו יֵלֶךְ דָּבֶר *lifanav yelekh daver* = before him goes pestilence; רֶשֶׁף *resheph* = fiery plague/arrow; "plague followed at his heels" for *yetze resheph leraglav*.
- **"he stood, and measured the earth: he beheld, and drove asunder the nations" → "He stood and measured the earth; he looked and shook the nations"** (v6) — Hebrew מָדַד אֶרֶץ *madad eretz* = measured/shook the earth; "shook the nations" for *vayyatter goyim* = he made nations tremble.
- **"and the everlasting mountains were scattered, the perpetual hills did bow: his ways are everlasting" → "then the eternal mountains were scattered; the everlasting hills sank low. His were the everlasting ways"** (v6) — Hebrew הֲלִיכוֹת עוֹלָם לוֹ *halikot olam lo* = the ways of eternity are his; "His were the everlasting ways" captures the divine sovereignty.
- **"Was the Lord displeased against the rivers? was thine anger against the rivers? was thy wrath against the sea" → "Was your wrath against the rivers, O Yahweh? Was your anger against the rivers, or your indignation against the sea"** (v8) — reordered for poetry; "indignation" for *ebra* = burning wrath.
- **"Thy bow was made quite naked, according to the oaths of the tribes, even thy word" → "You stripped the sheath from your bow, calling for many arrows"** (v9) — Hebrew עֶרְיָה תֵעוֹר קַשְׁתֶּךָ *erya te'or qashtekha* = nakedly you stripped your bow; מַטּוֹת אֹמֶר *mattot omer* = sworn/called for weapons; modernized.
- **"The mountains saw thee, and they trembled" → "The mountains saw you and writhed"** (v10) — Hebrew יָחִילוּ *yachilu* = writhed/trembled; "writhed" more vivid.
- **"the overflowing of the water passed by: the deep uttered his voice, and lifted up his hands on high" → "the raging waters swept on; the deep gave forth its voice; it lifted its hands on high"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"The sun and moon stood still in their habitation" → "The sun and moon stood still in their place"** (v11) — Hebrew זְבוּלֹה *zevuloh* = their exalted place/dwelling.
- **"at the light of thine arrows they went, and at the shining of thy glittering spear" → "at the light of your arrows as they sped, at the flash of your glittering spear"** (v11) — modernized.
- **"Thou didst march through the land in indignation, thou didst thresh the heathen in anger" → "You marched through the earth in fury; you threshed the nations in anger"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּזַעַם *beza'am* = in indignation/fury; "nations" for *goyim*.
- **"thou woundedst the head out of the house of the wicked, by discovering the foundation unto the neck" → "You crushed the head of the house of the wicked, laying him bare from thigh to neck"** (v13) — Hebrew מָחַצְתָּ רֹאשׁ *machatzta rosh* = you crushed the head; עָרוֹת יְסוֹד עַד צַוָּאר *arot yesod ad tzavar* = laying bare the foundation to the neck.
- **"Thou didst strike through with his staves the head of his villages" → "You pierced with his own arrows the heads of his warriors"** (v14) — Hebrew נָקַבְתָּ בְמַטָּיו רֹאשׁ פְּרָזָיו *naqavta bemattav rosh perazav*; "pierced with his own arrows" = turning the enemy's weapons against themselves.
- **"who came like a whirlwind to scatter me: their rejoicing was as to devour the poor secretly" → "who came like a whirlwind to scatter me, rejoicing as if to devour the poor in secret"** (v14) — modernized.
- **"Thou didst walk through the sea with thine horses, through the heap of great waters" → "You trampled the sea with your horses, the surging of mighty waters"** (v15) — Hebrew דָּרַכְתָּ בְיָם סוּסֶיךָ *darakhtá bayam susekha*; "trampled" for *darak* = to tread/trample.
- **"When I heard, my belly trembled; my lips quivered at the voice: rottenness entered into my bones, and I trembled in myself, that I might rest in the day of trouble: when he cometh up unto the people, he will invade them with his troops" → "I hear, and my body trembles; my lips quiver at the sound; rottenness enters into my bones; my legs tremble beneath me. Yet I will quietly wait for the day of trouble to come upon people who invade us"** (v16) — Hebrew וְתַחְתַּי אֶרְגָּז *vetachtay ergazz* = my legs tremble beneath me; the final clause restructured: "come upon people who invade us."
- **"Yet I will rejoice in the Lord, I will joy in the God of my salvation" → "yet I will rejoice in Yahweh; I will take joy in the *Elohim* of my salvation"** (v18) — Hebrew אָגִילָה בַּיהוָה *aggilah baYahweh*; *Elohim* retained; the famous confession.
- **"The Lord God is my strength" → "Yahweh Adonai is my strength"** (v19) — Hebrew יְהוִה אֲדֹנָי חֵילִי; *Adonai* retained.
- **"he will make me to walk upon mine high places" → "he makes me tread on my high places"** (v19) — Hebrew בָּמֹותַי *bamotay* = my high places; "tread on my high places" = victory over enemies on the hills.
- **"To the chief singer on my stringed instruments" → "To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments"** (v19) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ בִּנְגִינוֹתָי *lamnatzzeach bineginotay*; the liturgical subscription; "choirmaster" for *menatze'ach*.
