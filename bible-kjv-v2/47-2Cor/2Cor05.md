# 2 Corinthians 5

[1] For we know that if the tent that is our earthly home is destroyed, we have a building from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.

[2] For in this tent we groan, longing to put on our heavenly dwelling,

[3] if indeed by putting it on we may not be found naked.

[4] For while we are still in this tent, we groan, being burdened — not that we would be unclothed, but that we would be further clothed, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life.

[5] He who has prepared us for this very thing is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who has given us the Spirit as a guarantee.

[6] So we are always of good courage. We know that while we are at home in the body we are away from the Lord,

[7] for **we walk by faith, not by sight**.

[8] Yes, we are of good courage, and we would rather be away from the body and at home with the Lord.

[9] So whether we are at home or away, we make it our aim to please him.

[10] For we must all appear before the judgment seat of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, so that each one may receive what is due for what he has done in the body, whether good or evil.

[11] Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade others. But what we are is known to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and I hope it is known also to your conscience.

[12] We are not commending ourselves to you again but giving you cause to boast about us, so that you may be able to answer those who boast about outward appearance and not about what is in the heart.

[13] For if we are beside ourselves, it is for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; if we are in our right mind, it is for you.

[14] For the love of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ controls us, because we have concluded this: that one has died for all, therefore all have died;

[15] and he died for all, that those who live might no longer live for themselves but for him who for their sake died and was raised.

[16] From now on, therefore, we regard no one according to the flesh. Even though we once regarded ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ according to the flesh, we regard him thus no longer.

[17] Therefore, **if anyone is in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come.**

[18] All this is from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who through ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ reconciled us to himself and gave us the ministry of reconciliation;

[19] that is, in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was reconciling the world to himself, not counting their trespasses against them, and entrusting to us the message of reconciliation.

[20] Therefore, we are ambassadors for ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ making his appeal through us. We implore you on behalf of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, **be reconciled to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧**.

[21] For our sake he made him to be sin who knew no sin, so that in him we might become the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v10, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"earnest" → "guarantee"** (v5) — ἀρραβών; see 2 Cor 1:22 note.
- **"judgment seat of Christ" → "judgment seat of the *Mashiach*"** (v10) — βήματος τοῦ Χριστοῦ; some MSS read "God" here; majority text and ESV read "Christ/*Mashiach*."
- **"righteousness of God" → "righteousness of *Elohim*"** (v21) — δικαιοσύνη θεοῦ; the pivotal phrase: in him we become God's own righteousness/covenant-faithfulness incarnate.
- **"reconciliation" (v18, 19, 20)** — καταλλαγή *katallagē* = restoration of broken relationship between parties; retained as "reconciliation," the precise theological term.
