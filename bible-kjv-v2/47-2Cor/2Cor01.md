[20] For all the promises of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ find their Yes in him. That is why it is through him that we utter our Amen to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for his glory.

[21] And it is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who establishes us with you in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and has anointed us,

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 14, 19, 21) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"church"** (v1) — systematic.
- **"Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ" → "Blessed be the *Elohim* and Father of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v3) — εὐλογητὸς ὁ θεός = blessed be God; KJV's "even" is archaic explanatory particle; modernized.
- **"grace" (v2, 12, 15)** — χάρις *charis*; in v15 "a second experience of grace" = a second *charin*, meaning "benefit/favor" — God's gracious presence experienced through Paul's visit.
- **"earnest" → "guarantee"** (v22) — ἀρραβών *arrabon* = down payment, pledge, guarantee; "earnest" is archaic/technical; "guarantee" conveys the legal-commercial sense in modern English.
