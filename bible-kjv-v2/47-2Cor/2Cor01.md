# 2 Corinthians 1

[1] Paul, an apostle of the *Mashiach* *Yeshua* by the will of *Elohim*, and Timothy our brother,

To the church of *Elohim* that is at Corinth, with all the saints who are in the whole of Achaia:

[2] Grace to you and peace from *Elohim* our Father and the Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.

[3] Blessed be the *Elohim* and Father of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, the Father of mercies and *Elohim* of all comfort,

[4] who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those who are in any affliction, with the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by *Elohim*.

[5] For as we share abundantly in the *Mashiach*'s sufferings, so through the *Mashiach* we share abundantly in comfort too.

[6] If we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation; and if we are comforted, it is for your comfort, which you experience when you patiently endure the same sufferings that we suffer.

[7] Our hope for you is unshaken, for we know that as you share in our sufferings, you will also share in our comfort.

[8] For we do not want you to be unaware, brothers, of the affliction we experienced in Asia. For we were so utterly burdened beyond our strength that we despaired of life itself.

[9] Indeed, we felt that we had received the sentence of death. But that was to make us rely not on ourselves but on *Elohim* who raises the dead.

[10] He delivered us from such a deadly peril, and he will deliver us. On him we have set our hope that he will deliver us again.

[11] You also must help us by prayer, so that many will give thanks on our behalf for the blessing granted us through the prayers of many.

[12] For our boast is this, the testimony of our conscience, that we behaved in the world with simplicity and godly sincerity, not by earthly wisdom but by the grace of *Elohim*, and supremely so toward you.

[13] For we are not writing to you anything other than what you read and understand and I hope you will fully understand —

[14] just as you did partially understand us — that on the day of our Lord *Yeshua* you will boast of us as we will boast of you.

[15] Because I was sure of this, I wanted to come to you first, so that you might have a second experience of grace.

[16] I wanted to visit you on my way to Macedonia, and to come back to you from Macedonia and have you send me on my way to Judea.

[17] Was I vacillating when I wanted to do this? Do I make my plans according to the flesh, ready to say "Yes, yes" and "No, no" at the same time?

[18] As surely as *Elohim* is faithful, our word to you has not been Yes and No.

[19] For the Son of *Elohim*, *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, whom we proclaimed among you, Silvanus and Timothy and I, was not Yes and No, but in him it is always Yes.

[20] For all the promises of *Elohim* find their Yes in him. That is why it is through him that we utter our Amen to *Elohim* for his glory.

[21] And it is *Elohim* who establishes us with you in the *Mashiach*, and has anointed us,

[22] and who has also put his seal on us and given us his Spirit in our hearts as a guarantee.

[23] But I call *Elohim* to witness against me — it was to spare you that I refrained from coming again to Corinth.

[24] Not that we lord it over your faith, but we work with you for your joy, for you stand firm in your faith.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 14, 19, 21) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"church"** (v1) — systematic.
- **"Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ" → "Blessed be the *Elohim* and Father of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v3) — εὐλογητὸς ὁ θεός = blessed be God; KJV's "even" is archaic explanatory particle; modernized.
- **"grace" (v2, 12, 15)** — χάρις *charis*; in v15 "a second experience of grace" = a second *charin*, meaning "benefit/favor" — God's gracious presence experienced through Paul's visit.
- **"earnest" → "guarantee"** (v22) — ἀρραβών *arrabon* = down payment, pledge, guarantee; "earnest" is archaic/technical; "guarantee" conveys the legal-commercial sense in modern English.
