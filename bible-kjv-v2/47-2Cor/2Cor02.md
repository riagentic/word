# 2 Corinthians 2

[1] For I made up my mind not to make another painful visit to you.

[2] For if I cause you pain, who is there to make me glad but the one whom I have pained?

[3] And I wrote as I did, so that when I came I might not suffer pain from those who should have made me rejoice, for I felt sure of all of you, that my joy would be the joy of you all.

[4] For I wrote to you out of much affliction and anguish of heart and with many tears, not to cause you pain but to let you know the abundant love that I have for you.

[5] Now if anyone has caused pain, he has caused it not to me, but in some measure — not to put it too severely — to all of you.

[6] For such a one, this punishment by the majority is enough,

[7] so you should rather turn to forgive and comfort him, or he may be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow.

[8] So I beg you to reaffirm your love for him.

[9] For this is why I wrote, that I might test you and know whether you are obedient in everything.

[10] Anyone whom you forgive, I also forgive. Indeed, what I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, has been for your sake in the presence of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[11] so that we would not be outwitted by Satan; for we are not ignorant of his designs.

[12] When I came to Troas to preach the gospel of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, even though a door was opened for me in the Lord,

[13] my spirit was not at rest because I did not find my brother Titus there. So I took leave of them and went on to ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧.

[14] But thanks be to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ always leads us in triumphal procession, and through us spreads the fragrance of the knowledge of him everywhere.

[15] For we are the aroma of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing,

[16] to one a fragrance from death to death, to the other a fragrance from life to life. Who is sufficient for these things?

[17] For we are not, like so many, peddlers of ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ word, but as men of sincerity, as commissioned by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in the sight of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we speak in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v10, 12, 14, 15, 17) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v14, 15, 17) — systematic.
- **"Satan" (v11)** — Σατανᾶς retained as proper name; "lest *Satan* should get an advantage of us" modernized to "outwitted."
- **"corrupt the word of God" → "peddlers of *Elohim*'s word"** (v17) — καπηλεύοντες *kapeleuontes* = adulterating/huckstering; "peddlers" captures the commercial corruption sense better than KJV "corrupt."
- **"in the sight of God" → "in the sight of *Elohim*"** (v17) — systematic; Paul's claim to ministerial integrity before the divine gaze.
