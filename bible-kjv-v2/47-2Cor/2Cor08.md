# 2 Corinthians 8

[1] Now we make known to you, brothers, the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that has been given in the churches of ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧,

[2] that in much proof of affliction the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded unto the riches of their simplicity;

[3] that according to power, I testify, and beyond power, of their own accord,

[4] with much appeal beseeching of us the grace and the fellowship of the service to the saints —

[5] and not as we hoped, but they gave themselves first to the Lord and to us through the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[6] so that we exhorted Titus that, as he had begun before, so he would also complete among you this grace also;

[7] but as you abound in everything — in faith and word and knowledge and all diligence and the love from us in you — abound in this grace also.

[8] I do not speak by way of command, but through the diligence of others testing also the genuineness of your love;

[9] for you know the grace of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, that **for your sake he became poor, being rich, so that you by that one's poverty might become rich**.

[10] And I give an opinion in this; for this is profitable for you, who began before, from last year, not only the doing but also the willing;

[11] but now complete the doing also, so that just as there was the readiness of the willing, so there may be also the completing, out of what you have.

[12] For if the readiness is present, it is well-acceptable according to whatever one has, not according to what one does not have.

[13] For it is not that others may have relief and you affliction, but out of equality:

[14] at the present time your abundance for their lack, so that their abundance also may be for your lack, that there may be equality;

[15] as it is written, "**He who had much did not have more, and he who had little did not have less**."

[16] But thanks be to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who gives the same diligence on your behalf in the heart of Titus,

[17] for he accepted the exhortation, but being more diligent, of his own accord he went out to you.

[18] And we sent together with him the brother whose praise in the gospel is through all the churches —

[19] and not only so, but he was also chosen by the churches as our fellow traveler with this grace which is being served by us, for the glory of the Lord himself and our readiness —

[20] avoiding this, that anyone should blame us in this bounty which is being served by us,

[21] for we take forethought for what is good not only before the Lord but also before men.

[22] And we sent together with them our brother, whom we have approved in many things many times as being diligent, but now much more diligent through great confidence toward you.

[23] Whether concerning Titus: my partner and fellow worker toward you; or our brothers: apostles of the churches, the glory of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[24] Therefore demonstrate to them, in the face of the churches, the demonstration of your love and of our boasting on your behalf.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v9) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 16) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v23) — systematic.
- **"grace" (v1, 4, 6, 7, 9, 19)** — χάρις *charis*; Paul uses *charis* throughout chs. 8–9 for the gift of *Elohim*, for the collection and for the act of giving. It is rendered "grace" every time; only χάρις τῷ θεῷ (v16; 9:15) is "thanks be to *Elohim*", the same word.
- **"we do you to wit of" → "we make known to you"** (v1) — Greek Γνωρίζομεν ὑμῖν.
- **"in a great trial of affliction" → "in much proof of affliction"** (v2) — Greek ἐν πολλῇ δοκιμῇ θλίψεως; δοκιμή is the testing and what the testing shows.
- **"their deep poverty" kept** (v2) — Greek ἡ κατὰ βάθους πτωχεία, "poverty down to the depth".
- **"liberality" → "simplicity"** (v2) — Greek ἁπλότης, singleness, an undivided mind; in giving, open-handedness (also 9:11, 13).
- **"to their power … they were willing of themselves" → "according to power … of their own accord"** (v3–5) — Greek κατὰ δύναμιν … παρὰ δύναμιν, αὐθαίρετοι: the sentence has no verb until "they gave themselves" in v5; no "they gave" stands in v3. The break after v4 is kept.
- **"praying us with much intreaty that we would receive the gift" → "with much appeal beseeching of us the grace and the fellowship of the service"** (v4) — Greek μετὰ πολλῆς παρακλήσεως δεόμενοι ἡμῶν τὴν χάριν καὶ τὴν κοινωνίαν τῆς διακονίας; "that we would receive" is not in the Greek.
- **"not as we hoped" kept** (v5) — Greek ἠλπίσαμεν. "And to us": the Greek has no "then".
- **"desired" → "exhorted"** (v6) — Greek παρακαλέσαι, "exhort" for urging in this letter; "the exhortation" (v17) is παράκλησιν. "Begun before" is προενήρξατο (also v10).
- **"see that ye abound" → "abound"** (v7) — Greek ἵνα … περισσεύητε, a ἵνα clause standing as a command.
- **"in your love to us" → "the love from us in you"** (v7) — Greek τῇ ἐξ ἡμῶν ἐν ὑμῖν ἀγάπῃ (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); Treg: ὑμῶν ἐν ἡμῖν, "from you in us".
- **"forwardness" / "sincerity" → "diligence" / "genuineness"** (v8) — Greek σπουδῆς, τὸ γνήσιον. "Testing" is δοκιμάζων.
- **"though he was rich, yet for your sakes he became poor" → "for your sake he became poor, being rich"** (v9) — Greek διʼ ὑμᾶς ἐπτώχευσεν πλούσιος ὤν, in this order; the participle is not marked as "though". "That one's" is ἐκείνου.
- **"advice" → "opinion"** (v10) — Greek γνώμην. "From last year" is ἀπὸ πέρυσι.
- **"a readiness to will … a performance" → "the readiness of the willing … the completing"** (v11) — Greek ἡ προθυμία τοῦ θέλειν … τὸ ἐπιτελέσαι; the clause has no verb, "there was" and "there may be" are supplied.
- **"it is accepted according to that a man hath" → "it is well-acceptable according to whatever one has"** (v12) — Greek εὐπρόσδεκτος (as 6:2); the printed text has no subject for "has" ("one" is supplied).
- **"that other men be eased, and ye burdened" → "that others may have relief and you affliction"** (v13) — Greek ἄλλοις ἄνεσις, ὑμῖν θλῖψις, two nouns without a verb.
- **"abundance" / "want" → "abundance" / "lack"** (v14) — Greek περίσσευμα (in v2 "abundance" is περισσεία), ὑστέρημα. The first half has no verb.
- **"He that had gathered much had nothing over" → "He who had much did not have more"** (v15) — Greek Ὁ τὸ πολὺ οὐκ ἐπλεόνασεν, καὶ ὁ τὸ ὀλίγον οὐκ ἠλαττόνησεν: "he of the much", without a verb ("had" is supplied; "gathered" comes from Exod 16:18). πλεονάζω is elsewhere "increase".
- **"which put" → "who gives"** (v16) — Greek διδόντι, present (SBLGNT, WH, Treg). NA28: δόντι, "who gave". "Diligence" is σπουδήν; "more diligent" (v17, 22) σπουδαιότερος.
- **"he went unto you" → "he went out to you"** (v17) — Greek ἐξῆλθεν, aorist, as are "we sent" (v18, 22); in a letter carried by those sent, the past tense may be that of the reader's time.
- **"chosen" kept** (v19) — Greek χειροτονηθείς, elected by a show of hands. "Fellow traveler" is συνέκδημος, one who is away from home with another (5:6).
- **"administered" → "served"** (v19, 20) — Greek διακονουμένῃ, the verb of διακονία "service". "With this grace" is σὺν τῇ χάριτι ταύτῃ (SBLGNT, NA28; WH, Treg: ἐν, "in this grace"); "the Lord himself" is αὐτοῦ τοῦ κυρίου (SBLGNT, NA28; WH, Treg have no αὐτοῦ: "the Lord"); "our readiness" is προθυμίαν ἡμῶν, as v11, 12.
- **"abundance" → "bounty"** (v20) — Greek ἁδρότητι, fullness, a large amount; a word found only here in the New Testament.
- **"providing for honest things" → "we take forethought for what is good"** (v21) — Greek προνοοῦμεν καλά (Prov 3:4 LXX).
- **"upon the great confidence which I have in you" → "through great confidence toward you"** (v22) — Greek πεποιθήσει πολλῇ τῇ εἰς ὑμᾶς; the Greek does not say whose confidence, the brother's or Paul's.
- **"partner" kept** (v23) — Greek κοινωνός (as Phlm 17; "sharers" in 1:7). The verse has no verb.
- **"messengers of the churches" → "apostles of the churches"** (v23) — Greek ἀπόστολοι ἐκκλησιῶν: the same word as "apostle", here of men sent by churches.
- **"shew ye … the proof" → "demonstrate … the demonstration"** (v24) — Greek τὴν ἔνδειξιν … ἐνδεικνύμενοι, noun and verb of one root; the verb is a participle standing as a command (SBLGNT, Treg, NA28; WH: ἐνδείξασθε, the imperative). "In the face of" is εἰς πρόσωπον.
- **v15** — OT quotation from Ex 16:18 (manna gathering); noted.

### Notes
- **v15** quotes Ex 16:18 (manna).
