# 2 Corinthians 7

[1] Having therefore these promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from every defilement of flesh and spirit, completing holiness in the fear of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] Make room for us; we wronged no one, we corrupted no one, we defrauded no one.

[3] I do not say this for condemnation, for I have said before that you are in our hearts, to die together and to live together.

[4] Great is my boldness toward you, great is my boasting on your behalf; I have been filled with encouragement, I abound beyond measure with joy in all our affliction.

[5] For indeed, when we came into ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧, our flesh has had no relief, but in everything afflicted — outside fights, inside fears —

[6] but he who encourages the humble, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, encouraged us by the coming of Titus;

[7] and not only by his coming, but also by the encouragement with which he was encouraged over you, reporting to us your longing, your lamentation, your zeal on my behalf, so that I rejoiced the more.

[8] For even if I grieved you in the letter, I do not regret it; even if I did regret it (I see that that letter grieved you, even if for a short time),

[9] now I rejoice, not that you were grieved, but that you were grieved unto repentance; for you were grieved according to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, so that you might suffer loss in nothing from us.

[10] For the sorrow according to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ works repentance unto salvation, not to be regretted; but the sorrow of the world works out death.

[11] For behold this very thing, to be grieved according to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: how much diligence it worked out in you, indeed defense, indeed indignation, indeed fear, indeed longing, indeed zeal, indeed vengeance! In everything you commended yourselves as being pure in the matter.

[12] So then, even if I wrote to you, it was not on account of the one who did the wrong, nor on account of the one who was wronged, but on account of your diligence on our behalf being manifested to you before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[13] Because of this we have been encouraged.

And in addition to our encouragement we rejoiced still more abundantly at the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed by all of you;

[14] for if I have boasted anything to him on your behalf, I was not put to shame; but as we spoke all things to you in truth, so also our boasting before Titus became truth.

[15] And his inward parts are more abundantly toward you, as he remembers the obedience of you all, how with fear and trembling you accepted him.

[16] I rejoice that in everything I am of good courage in you.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12) — systematic.
- **"filthiness of the flesh and spirit" → "defilement of flesh and spirit"** (v1) — Greek μολυσμοῦ σαρκὸς καὶ πνεύματος; σάρξ is "flesh", not "body". "Completing" is ἐπιτελοῦντες (as 8:6, 11).
- **"Receive us" → "Make room for us"** (v2) — Greek Χωρήσατε ἡμᾶς; "in your hearts" is not in the Greek. The three verbs are aorist: "wronged" (ἠδικήσαμεν, as v12), "corrupted" (ἐφθείραμεν), "defrauded" (ἐπλεονεκτήσαμεν).
- **"to condemn you" → "for condemnation"** (v3) — Greek πρὸς κατάκρισιν, a noun without "you".
- **"glorying of you" → "boasting on your behalf"** (v4, 14) — Greek καύχησις ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν.
- **"comfort" → "encouragement" / "encourage"** (v4, 6, 7, 13) — Greek παράκλησις, παρακαλέω, as in chapter 1.
- **"I am exceeding joyful" → "I abound beyond measure with joy"** (v4) — Greek ὑπερπερισσεύομαι τῇ χαρᾷ, a compound of περισσεύω "abound". "In all our affliction" is ἐπί with the dative: in it, or on top of it.
- **"our flesh had no rest; but we were troubled on every side" → "our flesh has had no relief, but in everything afflicted —"** (v5) — Greek ἔσχηκεν is perfect; θλιβόμενοι is a participle that fits no word in the sentence, and the sentence breaks off. The break is kept. "Fights" is μάχαι, "fears" φόβοι, both plural.
- **"those that are cast down" → "the humble"** (v6) — Greek τοὺς ταπεινούς, the low, the lowly. The Greek order is kept: "*Elohim*" comes late.
- **"in you" → "over you"** (v7) — Greek ἐφʼ ὑμῖν, on account of you; not "by you".
- **"repent" → "regret"** (v8) — Greek μεταμέλομαι, a different word from "repentance" (μετάνοια, v9, 10). The verse reads βλέπω, "I see" (SBLGNT, WH, Treg), without "for"; NA28: βλέπω γάρ, "for I see". The clause is a parenthesis and the sentence goes on in v9.
- **"for a season" → "for a short time"** (v8) — Greek πρὸς ὥραν, literally "for an hour" (as Phlm 15).
- **"made sorry" / "sorrowed" → "grieved"; "sorrow" kept** (v8–11) — Greek λυπέω (verb) and λύπη (noun), one root.
- **"after a godly manner" / "godly sorrow" → "according to *Elohim*"** (v9, 10, 11) — Greek κατὰ θεόν.
- **"that ye might receive damage by us in nothing" → "so that you might suffer loss in nothing from us"** (v9) — Greek ἵνα … ζημιωθῆτε, a purpose clause.
- **"worketh" / "worketh" → "works" / "works out"** (v10, 11) — Greek ἐργάζεται, then the compound κατεργάζεται, κατειργάσατο.
- **"repentance to salvation not to be repented of" → "repentance unto salvation, not to be regretted"** (v10) — Greek ἀμεταμέλητον can go with "repentance" or with "salvation"; it is from μεταμέλομαι (v8).
- **"sorrow of the world" kept** (v10) — Greek ἡ τοῦ κόσμου λύπη, a genitive; not an adjective "worldly".
- **"carefulness" → "diligence"** (v11, 12) — Greek σπουδή.
- **"yea, what clearing of yourselves" → "indeed defense"** (v11) — Greek ἀλλά six times, each stepping higher; "what" (πόσην) stands only once. ἀπολογία is a defense speech.
- **"revenge" → "vengeance"** (v11) — Greek ἐκδίκησις, the carrying out of justice.
- **"ye have approved yourselves to be clear" → "you commended yourselves as being pure"** (v11) — Greek συνεστήσατε ἑαυτούς, the verb of 5:12 and 6:4, here "show, prove"; ἁγνούς is "pure" (6:6).
- **"that our care for you in the sight of God might appear unto you" → "on account of your diligence on our behalf being manifested to you"** (v12) — Greek τὴν σπουδὴν ὑμῶν τὴν ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν πρὸς ὑμᾶς: their diligence for Paul, made plain to themselves. "It was" is supplied.
- **"in your comfort" → "in addition to our encouragement"** (v13) — Greek Ἐπὶ δὲ τῇ παρακλήσει ἡμῶν; the printed text ends the first sentence after "we have been encouraged". "Still more abundantly" is περισσοτέρως μᾶλλον.
- **"is found a truth" → "became truth"** (v14) — Greek ἀλήθεια ἐγενήθη.
- **"inward affection" → "inward parts"** (v15) — Greek σπλάγχνα. "Accepted" is ἐδέξασθε.
- **"I have confidence in you in all things" → "in everything I am of good courage in you"** (v16) — Greek θαρρῶ ἐν ὑμῖν, the verb of 5:6, 8.
