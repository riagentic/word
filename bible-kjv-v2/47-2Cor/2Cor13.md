# 2 Corinthians 13

[1] This is the third time I am coming to you. "**Every charge must be established by the evidence of two or three witnesses**."

[2] I warned those who sinned before and all the others, and I warn them now while absent, as I did when present on my second visit, that if I come again I will not spare them —

[3] since you seek proof that ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is speaking in me. He is not weak in dealing with you, but is powerful among you.

[4] For he was crucified in weakness, but lives by the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. For we also are weak in him, but in dealing with you we will live with him by the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] **Examine yourselves, to see whether you are in the faith. Test yourselves.** Or do you not realize this about yourselves, that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is in you? — unless indeed you fail to meet the test!

[6] I hope you will find out that we have not failed the test.

[7] But we pray to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that you may not do wrong — not that we may appear to have met the test, but that you may do what is right, though we may seem to have failed.

[8] For we cannot do anything against the truth, but only for the truth.

[9] For we are glad when we are weak and you are strong. Your restoration is what we pray for.

[10] For this reason I write these things while I am away from you, that when I come I may not have to be severe in my use of the authority that the Lord has given me for building up and not for tearing down.

[11] Finally, brothers, rejoice. Aim for restoration, comfort one another, agree with one another, live in peace; and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of love and peace will be with you.

[12] Greet one another with a holy kiss.

[13] All the saints greet you.

[14] **The grace of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and the fellowship of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ be with you all.** Amen.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v5, 14) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v14) — Greek τοῦ ἁγίου πνεύματος; Hebrew רוּחַ הַקֹּדֶשׁ *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness"; preserved in the famous Trinitarian benediction.

### Notes
- **v1** quotes Deut 19:15.
