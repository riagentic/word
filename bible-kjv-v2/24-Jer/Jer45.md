# Jeremiah 45

[1] The word that ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet spoke to Baruch son of ⟦neriah|Neriyah|Neriah⟧, when he wrote these words in a book at the dictation of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, in the fourth year of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧:

[2] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to you, O Baruch:

[3] You said, 'Woe is me! For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has added sorrow to my pain. I am weary with my groaning, and I find no rest.'

[4] Thus shall you say to him, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, what I have built I am breaking down, and what I have planted I am plucking up — that is, the whole land.

[5] And do you seek great things for yourself? Seek them not, for behold, I am bringing disaster upon all flesh, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. But I will give you your life as a prize of war in all places to which you may go."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; brief oracle of consolation to Baruch.

### Notes
- **Baruch** — Jeremiah's faithful scribe; promised survival.
