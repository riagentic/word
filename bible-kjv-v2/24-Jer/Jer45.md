# Jeremiah 45

[1] The word that ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet spoke to Baruch son of ⟦neriah|Neriyah|Neriah⟧, when he wrote these words on a book from the mouth of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, in the fourth year of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying:

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, concerning you, Baruch:

[3] You said, Woe now to me, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has added sorrow to my pain; I am weary with my groaning, and rest I have not found.

[4] Thus you shall say to him: Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, what I have built I am tearing down, and what I have planted I am plucking up, and that is all the land.

[5] And you, you seek great things for yourself? Do not seek! For behold, I am bringing evil upon all flesh, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and I will give you your soul as spoil in all the places where you go.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Layout** — prose, as the Hebrew; the quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where the words of Yahweh to Baruch begin and end, and v3 quotes Baruch inside them.
- **"in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah" → "on a book from the mouth of Jeremiah … saying"** (v1) — Hebrew עַל־סֵפֶר "on a book" (on a scroll; סֵפֶר "book" as its row); מִפִּי "from the mouth of" (as 36:4; "at the dictation of" interprets); לֵאמֹר "saying" at the end of the verse, left out before, kept.
- **"unto thee, O Baruch" → "concerning you, Baruch"** (v2) — Hebrew עָלֶיךָ "upon / concerning you" (not אֵלֶיךָ "to you"); no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it).
- **"Woe is me now" → "Woe now to me"** (v3) — Hebrew אוֹי־נָא לִי: אוֹי "Woe" with לִי "to me" (as Isa 6:5), נָא "now" kept. **"I fainted in my sighing, and I find no rest" → "I am weary with my groaning, and rest I have not found"** — יָגַעְתִּי "I am weary" (stative perfect); אֲנָחָה "groaning"; מְנוּחָה "rest" (here the state, not a "resting place" as Deut 12:9), placed first as in the Hebrew; מָצָאתִי perfect "I have not found". מַכְאֹב "pain" (its row), יָגוֹן "sorrow" (its row), עַל "upon, to".
- **"which I have built will I break down, and that which I have planted I will pluck up, even this whole land" → "what I have built I am tearing down, and what I have planted I am plucking up, and that is all the land"** (v4) — Hebrew הֹרֵס, נֹתֵשׁ participles: "I am tearing down" (הָרַס, as Judg 6:25), "I am plucking up" (נָתַשׁ, as Jer 1:10); the pronoun אֲנִי, set after each verb, stresses "I". וְאֶת־כָּל־הָאָרֶץ הִיא, "and all the land, it (is that)": what is built and planted is the whole land (אֶרֶץ "land" or "earth"; with "all flesh" in v5 "earth" is also possible).
- **"And seekest thou great things for thyself? seek them not … evil … but thy life will I give unto thee for a prey" → "And you, you seek great things for yourself? Do not seek! … evil … and I will give you your soul as spoil"** (v5) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה תְּבַקֶּשׁ־לְךָ, "and you, you seek for yourself" (no question word: a question by tone, or a statement; the pronoun stands first); אַל־תְּבַקֵּשׁ "do not seek" (no object); רָעָה "evil" (its row, not "disaster"); נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (its row; "your life" is the sense: he will escape alive); שָׁלָל "spoil" (as Jer 21:9; 38:2; 39:18: what a man carries away from the battle); וְ "and" (not "but"); תֵּלֶךְ "you go".

### Notes
- **Baruch** — Jeremiah's scribe (Jer 36); the fourth year of Jehoiakim is the year of the scroll of Jer 36:1–4. The word to him is promised survival, nothing more.
