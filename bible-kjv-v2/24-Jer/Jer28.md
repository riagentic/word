# Jeremiah 28

[1] And it came to pass in that year, in the beginning of the reign of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the fourth year, in the fifth month, that ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ son of Azzur, the prophet who was from ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧, said to me in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, before the eyes of the priests and all the people, saying,

[2] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: I have broken the yoke of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[5] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet said to ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ the prophet before the eyes of the priests and before the eyes of all the people who were standing in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[7] Only, hear, please, this word that I am speaking in your ears and in the ears of all the people:

[10] And ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ the prophet took the yoke-bar from upon the neck of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet, and he broke it.

[11] And ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ said before the eyes of all the people, saying, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Even so I will break the yoke of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ within two years of days from upon the neck of all the nations." ## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v2, 14). **"God" → "Elohim"** (v2, 14).
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose.
- **"And it came to pass the same year, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, and in the fifth month" → "And it came to pass in that year, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, in the fifth month"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי; qere בַּשָּׁנָה "in the year" (ketiv בשנת "in the year of"); "in that year" ties the chapter to 27:1. "In the beginning of the reign" and "in the fourth year" stand side by side as printed (the Septuagint has only "in the fourth year of Zedekiah", from memory). **"Hananiah the son of Azur the prophet, which was of Gibeon" → "Hananiah son of Azzur, the prophet who was from Gibeon"** — אֲשֶׁר מִגִּבְעוֹן. **"spake unto me … in the presence of the priests" → "said to me … before the eyes of the priests"** — אָמַר אֵלַי; לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of" (also v5, 11); לֵאמֹר "saying".
- **"Thus speaketh the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, saying" → "Thus says Yahweh of Hosts, the Elohim of Israel, saying"** (v2) — כֹּה־אָמַר … לֵאמֹר, both kept. **"I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon"** — שָׁבַרְתִּי, a perfect: Hananiah speaks of it as done; עֹל "yoke" (27:8; not the מוֹטָה "yoke-bar" of v10).
- **"Within two full years will I bring again into this place all the vessels of the LORD's house" → "Within two years of days I am bringing back to this place all the utensils of the house of Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּעוֹד שְׁנָתַיִם יָמִים "within two years of days" (= two full years; the row of 2 Sam 13:23; Gen 41:1 has "two full years"; also v11); מֵשִׁיב participle "I am bringing back" (also v4); כְּלֵי "utensils" (as 27:16). **"and carried them to Babylon" → "and brought to Babylon"** — וַיְבִיאֵם "and brought them".
- **"And I will bring again to this place Jeconiah … with all the captives of Judah, that went into Babylon" → "And Jeconiah … and all the exiles of Judah who went to Babylon, I am bringing back to this place"** (v4) — the objects placed first, as the Hebrew; גָּלוּת "exile" (collective: "the exiles"); הַבָּאִים "who went". **"for I will break the yoke"** — אֶשְׁבֹּר.
- **"Then the prophet Jeremiah said unto the prophet Hananiah in the presence of the priests, and in the presence of all the people" → "And Jeremiah the prophet said to Hananiah the prophet before the eyes of the priests and before the eyes of all the people"** (v5) — וַיֹּאמֶר; לְעֵינֵי twice; הָעֹמְדִים "who were standing".
- **"Amen: the LORD do so: the LORD perform thy words which thou hast prophesied" → "Amen! So may Yahweh do! May Yahweh make your words stand that you have prophesied"** (v6) — Hebrew כֵּן יַעֲשֶׂה יְהוָה, יָקֵם, jussives (wishes); הֵקִים "make stand" (of a word: confirm, carry out). **"to bring again the vessels of the LORD's house, and all that is carried away captive, from Babylon into this place" → "to bring back the utensils of the house of Yahweh and all the exiles from Babylon to this place"** — הַגּוֹלָה "the exile" (collective).
- **"Nevertheless hear thou now this word that I speak in thine ears" → "Only, hear, please, this word that I am speaking in your ears"** (v7) — Hebrew אַךְ "only"; שְׁמַע־נָא "hear, please"; דֹּבֵר participle; בְּאָזְנֶיךָ "in your ears" (not "hearing").
- **"The prophets that have been before me and before thee of old prophesied both against many countries, and against great kingdoms, of war, and of evil, and of pestilence" → "The prophets who were before me and before you from of old prophesied to many lands and against great kingdoms, of war and of evil and of pestilence"** (v8) — Hebrew מִן־הָעוֹלָם "from of old"; אֶל … וְעַל "to … and against"; לְמִלְחָמָה וּלְרָעָה וּלְדָבֶר "of (for) war and evil and pestilence": the MT reads רָעָה "evil"; many Hebrew manuscripts read רָעָב "famine" (the sword–famine–pestilence triad, 27:8, 13), and the current "famine" followed them (from memory): the MT is restored.
- **"The prophet which prophesieth of peace, when the word of the prophet shall come to pass, then shall the prophet be known, that the LORD hath truly sent him" → "The prophet who prophesies of shalom, when the word of the prophet comes, the prophet shall be known whom Yahweh has sent in truth"** (v9) — Hebrew לְשָׁלוֹם (token `shalom`); בְּבֹא "when … comes"; יִוָּדַע הַנָּבִיא אֲשֶׁר־שְׁלָחוֹ יְהוָה בֶּאֱמֶת, literally "the prophet shall be known whom Yahweh sent him in truth"; the sentence begins with the prophet and does not finish with him (kept). Deut 18:21–22 is the rule behind it.
- **"Then Hananiah the prophet took the yoke from off the prophet Jeremiah's neck, and brake it" → "And Hananiah the prophet took the yoke-bar from upon the neck of Jeremiah the prophet, and he broke it"** (v10) — Hebrew הַמּוֹטָה singular "the yoke-bar" (27:2 has the plural; the current "yoke-bars … them" did not follow the Hebrew); מֵעַל "from upon"; וַיִּשְׁבְּרֵהוּ.
- **"Even so will I break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the neck of all nations within the space of two full years" → "Even so I will break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon within two years of days from upon the neck of all the nations"** (v11) — the Hebrew order; עֹל "yoke" (the thing the bar stood for). **"And the prophet Jeremiah went his way" → "And Jeremiah the prophet went his way"** — וַיֵּלֶךְ … לְדַרְכּוֹ, "and" (not "but").
- **"Then the word of the LORD came unto Jeremiah the prophet, after that Hananiah the prophet had broken the yoke from off the neck of the prophet Jeremiah, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah, after Hananiah the prophet had broken the yoke-bar from upon the neck of Jeremiah the prophet, saying"** (v12) — וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֶל … לֵאמֹר; "Sometime" is not written; אֶל־יִרְמְיָה "to Jeremiah" without "the prophet" (KJV adds it there); הַמּוֹטָה "the yoke-bar"; שְׁבוֹר infinitive.
- **"Go and tell Hananiah, saying" → "Go, and you shall say to Hananiah, saying"** (v13) — Hebrew הָלוֹךְ וְאָמַרְתָּ, an infinitive absolute for the command and a weqatal; לֵאמֹר kept. **"Thou hast broken the yokes of wood; but thou shalt make for them yokes of iron" → "Yoke-bars of wood you have broken, and you shall make in their place yoke-bars of iron"** — מוֹטֹת עֵץ … מֹטוֹת בַּרְזֶל, plural, placed first; וְעָשִׂיתָ "and you shall make" (the Septuagint "I will make", from memory); תַּחְתֵּיהֶן "in their place".
- **"I have put a yoke of iron upon the neck of all these nations, that they may serve Nebuchadnezzar" → "A yoke of iron I have put upon the neck of all these nations, to serve Nebuchadnezzar"** (v14) — the Hebrew order; עֹל בַּרְזֶל "a yoke of iron"; לַעֲבֹד "to serve". **"and I have given him the beasts of the field also" → "and also the living things of the field I have given to him"** — וְגַם "and also" (not "for"); חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה "the living things of the field" (as 27:6).
- **"Hear now, Hananiah; The LORD hath not sent thee; but thou makest this people to trust in a lie" → "Hear, please, Hananiah: Yahweh has not sent you, and you, you have made this people trust in falsehood"** (v15) — שְׁמַע־נָא "hear, please"; וְאַתָּה stressed; הִבְטַחְתָּ … עַל־שָׁקֶר "made trust upon falsehood" (perfect; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood", as 27:10).
- **"Behold, I will cast thee from off the face of the earth" → "Behold, I am sending you away from upon the face of the ground"** (v16) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מְשַׁלֵּחֲךָ, piel participle of שָׁלַח, "send away": it answers לֹא־שְׁלָחֲךָ "has not sent you" (v15), one root; הָאֲדָמָה "the ground". **"this year thou shalt die" → "This year you are dying"** — הַשָּׁנָה אַתָּה מֵת, a participle (what is about to happen). **"because thou hast taught rebellion against the LORD" → "for you have spoken defection against Yahweh"** — סָרָה דִבַּרְתָּ "you have spoken defection" (סָרָה from סוּר "turn aside"; as Deut 13:5).
- **"So Hananiah the prophet died the same year in the seventh month" → "And Hananiah the prophet died in that year, in the seventh month"** (v17) — וַיָּמָת; two months after v1.

### Notes
- **27–28 the yoke** — מוֹטָה "yoke-bar" (27:2; 28:10, 12, 13) and עֹל "yoke" (28:2, 4, 11, 14): the bar Jeremiah wears is the sign; the yoke is the rule of the king of Babylon. The wooden bars broken by Hananiah are answered by bars of iron (v13) and a yoke of iron (v14).
- **v8–9** — the test of a prophet who foretells shalom: the coming of his word (Deut 18:21–22).
