# Jeremiah 28

[1] In that same year, at the beginning of the reign of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the fifth month of the fourth year, ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ son of Azzur, the prophet from ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧, spoke to me in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in the presence of the priests and all the people, saying,

[2] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: I have broken the yoke of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[3] Within two years I will bring back to this place all the vessels of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧ house, which ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ took away from this place and carried to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[4] I will also bring back to this place ⟦jeconiah|Yechonyah|Jeconiah⟧ son of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and all the exiles from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who went to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for I will break the yoke of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧."

[5] Then the prophet ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ spoke to ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ the prophet in the presence of the priests and all the people who were standing in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[6] and the prophet ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said, "Amen! May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ do so; may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ make the words that you have prophesied come true, and bring back to this place from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ the vessels of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and all the exiles.

[7] Yet hear now this word that I speak in your hearing and in the hearing of all the people.

[8] The prophets who preceded you and me from ancient times prophesied war, famine, and pestilence against many countries and great kingdoms.

[9] As for the prophet who prophesies peace, when the word of that prophet comes to pass, then it will be known that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has truly sent the prophet."

[10] Then ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ the prophet took the yoke-bars from the neck of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet and broke them.

[11] And ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ spoke in the presence of all the people, saying, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Even so will I break the yoke of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ from the neck of all the nations within two years." But ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet went his way.

[12] Sometime after the prophet ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ had broken the yoke-bars from off the neck of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧:

[13] "Go, tell ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: You have broken wooden bars, but you have made in their place bars of iron.

[14] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: I have put upon the neck of all these nations an iron yoke to serve ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and they shall serve him, for I have given to him even the beasts of the field.'"

[15] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet said to the prophet ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧, "Listen, ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not sent you, and you have made this people trust in a lie.

[16] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Behold, I will remove you from the face of the earth. This year you shall die, because you have uttered rebellion against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

[17] In that same year, in the seventh month, the prophet ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧ died.

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv2, 14) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 14) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; confrontation between true and false prophecy.
