# Jeremiah 6

[1] Flee for refuge, sons of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧,
from the midst of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!
And in ⟦tekoa|Teqoa|Tekoa⟧ blow the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧,
and over ⟦beth-haccherem|Beit HaKerem|Beth-haccherem⟧ lift up a signal;
for evil looks down from the north,
and a great breaking.

[2] The comely one and the delicate one I have destroyed,
the daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[3] To her shall come shepherds and their droves;
they have pitched tents against her all around;
they have pastured each man his hand.

[4] Sanctify war against her;
arise, and let us go up at noon!
Woe to us, for the day has turned,
for the shadows of evening stretch out!

[5] Arise, and let us go up by night,
and let us ruin her palaces!

[6] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:
Cut down trees,
and pour out a mound against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
she is the city that has been visited;
all of her is extortion in her midst.

[7] As a well keeps its waters cool,
so she keeps her evil cool;
violence and havoc are heard in her;
before my face continually are sickness and blow.

[8] Be disciplined, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
lest my soul be wrenched from you,
lest I make you a desolation,
a land not inhabited.

[9] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:
They shall surely glean, as a vine,
the remnant of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
turn back your hand,
like a grape-gatherer, over the shoots.

[10] To whom shall I speak and warn,
that they may hear?
Behold, their ear is uncircumcised,
and they are not able to give heed;
behold, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has become to them a reproach;
they do not delight in it.

[11] And with the fury of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I am full,
I am weary of holding it in.
Pour it out on the child in the street,
and on the company of young men together;
for also man with woman shall be captured,
the old man with the one full of days.

[12] And their houses shall be turned over to others,
fields and wives together;
for I will stretch out my hand
against the inhabitants of the land, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] For from their least to their greatest,
every one of them is gaining unjust gain;
and from prophet to priest,
every one of them is doing falsehood.

[14] And they have healed the breaking of my people lightly,
saying, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧, ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧,"
and there is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[15] They have acted shamefully, for abomination they have done;
also they are surely not ashamed,
also to show shame they have not known;
therefore they shall fall among the falling;
at the time that I visit them they shall stumble,
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
Stand by the ways and see,
and ask for the paths of old,
where is the good way, and walk in it,
and find rest for your soul.
And they said, "We will not walk."

[17] And I raised up watchmen over you:
"Give heed to the voice of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧!"
And they said, "We will not give heed."

[18] Therefore hear, nations,
and know, congregation, what is among them.

[19] Hear, earth! Behold, I am bringing evil on this people,
the fruit of their thoughts;
for to my words they have not given heed,
and my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, they have rejected it.

[20] Why is this to me, frankincense that comes from ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧,
and the good cane from a land far off?
Your burnt offerings are not for good pleasure,
and your sacrifices are not sweet to me.

[21] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
Behold, I am giving to this people stumbling blocks,
and they shall stumble on them, fathers and sons together;
the neighbor and his friend, and they shall perish.

[22] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
Behold, a people is coming from the land of the north,
and a great nation is stirred up from the remote parts of the earth.

[23] Bow and javelin they hold fast;
cruel it is, and they have no compassion;
their voice roars like the sea,
and on horses they ride,
arrayed as a man for battle,
against you, daughter of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[24] We have heard the report of it;
our hands have gone slack;
distress has taken hold of us,
anguish like a woman giving birth.

[25] Do not go out to the field,
and on the way do not walk;
for the enemy has a sword,
terror on every side.

[26] Daughter of my people, gird on sackcloth,
and roll in the ashes;
make for yourself the mourning of an only one,
a lament of bitterness;
for suddenly the waster
shall come upon us.

[27] A tester I have made you among my people, a fortress,
and you shall know and try their way.

[28] All of them are the most stubborn of the stubborn,
going about as talebearers,
bronze and iron;
all of them, workers of ruin are they.

[29] The bellows blow fiercely;
by the fire the lead has come to an end;
for emptiness he has surely refined,
and the evil are not drawn out.

[30] Rejected silver they have called them,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has rejected them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v6, 9) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, "Hosts" plain.
- **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v12) — נְאֻם יְהוָה. **"saith the LORD" → "says Yahweh"** (v15) — אָמַר יְהוָה, another phrase (the verb "say").
- **Layout** — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents. No outer quotation marks: only the words quoted inside the speech (v14, 16–17) are marked; v4–5 are the cry of the enemy, v24–26 the people's and the prophet's words, without marks.
- **"O ye children of Benjamin, gather yourselves to flee out of the midst of Jerusalem" → "Flee for refuge, sons of Benjamin, from the midst of Jerusalem!"** (v1) — הָעִזוּ "flee for refuge" (bring yourselves to safety); בְּנֵי "sons of"; no "O". **"And in Tekoa blow the shofar"** — וּבִתְקוֹעַ תִּקְעוּ שׁוֹפָר, the Hebrew order (a play of sounds: *tiqoa* … *tiq'u*). **"set up a sign of fire in Bethhaccerem" → "over Beth-haccherem lift up a signal"** — עַל "over"; שְׂאוּ מַשְׂאֵת "lift up a lifting" (a raised signal, fire or smoke: the kind is not said). **"evil appeareth out of the north, and great destruction" → "evil looks down from the north, and a great breaking"** — נִשְׁקְפָה "look down"; רָעָה "evil"; שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as a bone breaks; also v14; 8:11, 21).
- **"I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman" → "The comely one and the delicate one I have destroyed, the daughter of Zion"** (v2) — הַנָּוָה וְהַמְּעֻנָּגָה דָּמִיתִי בַּת־צִיּוֹן: דָּמִיתִי is "I have destroyed / cut off" (דָּמָה II, as Hos 4:5) or "I have likened" (דָּמָה I, KJV; "to" is not written). נָוָה "comely" (as נָאוָה); the same letters can be read "pasture", which would fit v3. Uncertain: the verse keeps the order of the words.
- **"The shepherds with their flocks shall come unto her; they shall pitch … they shall feed every one in his place" → "To her shall come shepherds and their droves; they have pitched … they have pastured each man his hand"** (v3) — יָבֹאוּ imperfect, then the perfects תָּקְעוּ, רָעוּ (what is sure is told as done); עֶדְרֵיהֶם "their droves"; אִישׁ אֶת־יָדוֹ "each man his hand" (= the part beside him; kept).
- **"Prepare ye war against her" → "Sanctify war against her"** (v4) — קַדְּשׁוּ מִלְחָמָה (war is opened with rites: kept). **"Woe unto us! for the day goeth away" → "Woe to us, for the day has turned"** — אוֹי "Woe"; פָּנָה "turn". **"are stretched out" → "stretch out"** — יִנָּטוּ, imperfect.
- **"let us go by night, and let us destroy her palaces" → "let us go up by night, and let us ruin her palaces"** (v5) — וְנַעֲלֶה "let us go up" (as v4); וְנַשְׁחִיתָה (שָׁחַת "ruin"); אַרְמְנוֹת "palaces" (fortified great houses).
- **"For thus hath the LORD of hosts said" → "For thus says Yahweh of Hosts"** (v6) — the prophets' formula. **"Hew ye down trees, and cast a mount" → "Cut down trees, and pour out a mound"** — כִּרְתוּ עֵצָה, the collective "trees" (no suffix); שִׁפְכוּ סֹלְלָה "pour out a mound" (the earth heaped for a siege). **"this is the city to be visited; she is wholly oppression in the midst of her" → "she is the city that has been visited; all of her is extortion in her midst"** — הָפְקַד, hofal perfect of פָּקַד "visit" (the pointing is unusual; "to be visited" reads it as an infinitive); עֹשֶׁק "extortion".
- **"As a fountain casteth out her waters, so she casteth out her wickedness" → "As a well keeps its waters cool, so she keeps her evil cool"** (v7) — the qere בַּיִר "well" (the ketiv בור "cistern"); הָקִיר / הֵקֵרָה, hifil of קָרַר "be cool" (KJV takes it from קוּר "dig, spring": uncertain). **"violence and spoil" → "violence and havoc"** — חָמָס וָשֹׁד. **"before me continually is grief and wounds" → "before my face continually are sickness and blow"** — עַל־פָּנַי "upon my face"; חֳלִי "sickness"; מַכָּה "blow".
- **"Be thou instructed" → "Be disciplined"** (v8) — הִוָּסְרִי, nifal of יָסַר. **"lest my soul depart from thee" → "lest my soul be wrenched from you"** — תֵּקַע, of יָקַע "be wrenched, dislocated" (as Gen 32:25 of the thigh). **"desolate" → "a desolation"** — שְׁמָמָה.
- **"They shall throughly glean … as a vine" → "They shall surely glean, as a vine"** (v9) — עוֹלֵל יְעוֹלְלוּ, the infinitive absolute with the verb (literally "gleaning they shall glean"). **"turn back thine hand as a grapegatherer into the baskets" → "turn back your hand, like a grape-gatherer, over the shoots"** — עַל "over"; סַלְסִלּוֹת is found only here (uncertain: "shoots, tendrils" or "baskets").
- **"give warning" → "warn"** (v10) — אָעִידָה (הֵעִיד). **"their ear is uncircumcised"** — kept, singular. **"they cannot hearken" → "they are not able to give heed"** — הַקְשִׁיב "give heed" (also v17, 19). **"is unto them a reproach" → "has become to them a reproach"** — הָיָה לְ. חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; לֹא יַחְפְּצוּ־בוֹ "they do not delight in it".
- **"Therefore I am full of the fury of the LORD" → "And with the fury of Yahweh I am full"** (v11) — וְאֵת; no "therefore"; חֵמָה "fury". **"I am weary with holding in" → "I am weary of holding it in"** — הָכִיל "hold". **"I will pour it out upon the children abroad" → "Pour it out on the child in the street"** — שְׁפֹךְ is an imperative (Yahweh's word to the prophet) or an infinitive absolute used as a finite verb ("I will pour"); the pointing is the imperative form; עוֹלָל "child", singular; בַּחוּץ "in the street". **"the assembly of young men" → "the company of young men"** — סוֹד. **"the husband with the wife shall be taken" → "man with woman shall be captured"** — אִישׁ עִם־אִשָּׁה; לָכַד "capture". **"the aged with him that is full of days" → "the old man with the one full of days"** — זָקֵן.
- **"with their fields and wives together" → "fields and wives together"** (v12) — no "with", no "their". **"upon the inhabitants of the land" → "against the inhabitants of the land"** — נָטָה יָד עַל.
- **"every one is given to covetousness" → "every one of them is gaining unjust gain"** (v13) — כֻּלּוֹ בּוֹצֵעַ בָּצַע, verb and noun of one root; **"dealeth falsely" → "is doing falsehood"** — עֹשֶׂה שָׁקֶר. מִקְּטַנָּם וְעַד־גְּדוֹלָם "from their least to their greatest" (8:10 has "from the small to the great", without suffixes).
- **"They have healed also the hurt … slightly, saying, Peace, peace; when there is no peace" → "And they have healed the breaking of my people lightly, saying, Shalom, shalom, and there is no shalom"** (v14) — וַיְרַפְּאוּ; שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as v1); עַל־נְקַלָּה "lightly" (as a light thing); שָׁלוֹם three times, the token; וְאֵין "and there is no". Jer 8:11 has the same words with בַּת־עַמִּי "the daughter of my people" and the verb spelled וַיְרַפּוּ; the two verses are rendered alike except for that.
- **"Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination?" → "They have acted shamefully, for abomination they have done"** (v15) — הֹבִישׁוּ is a hifil statement, not a question (no interrogative), "act shamefully" (or "be put to shame", as 8:9); כִּי "for". **"nay, they were not at all ashamed" → "also they are surely not ashamed"** — גַּם־בּוֹשׁ לֹא־יֵבוֹשׁוּ, infinitive absolute with the verb. **"neither could they blush" → "also to show shame they have not known"** — גַּם־הַכְלִים לֹא יָדָעוּ: הַכְלִים, hifil of כָּלַם, here without an object (8:12 has the nifal הִכָּלֵם "to be put to shame"). **"among them that fall" → "among the falling"** — בַנֹּפְלִים. **"at the time that I visit them they shall be cast down" → "at the time that I visit them they shall stumble"** — בְּעֵת־פְּקַדְתִּים (8:12 "in the time of their visitation", another form); כָּשַׁל "stumble" (also v21).
- **"Stand ye in the ways … ask for the old paths, where is the good way" → "Stand by the ways … ask for the paths of old, where is the good way"** (v16) — עַל־דְּרָכִים "by / upon the ways"; נְתִבוֹת עוֹלָם "paths of old" (עוֹלָם here of the past; the "everlasting" of the genitive row would say another thing); אֵי־זֶה דֶּרֶךְ הַטּוֹב "where is this, the way of the good". **"and ye shall find rest for your souls" → "and find rest for your soul"** — וּמִצְאוּ, an imperative like "walk"; מַרְגּוֹעַ "rest"; נַפְשְׁכֶם singular. **"We will not walk therein" → "We will not walk"** — "therein" is not written.
- **"Also I set watchmen over you, saying, Hearken" → "And I raised up watchmen over you: Give heed"** (v17) — וַהֲקִמֹתִי "and I raised up"; צֹפִים "watchmen"; "saying" is not written.
- **"what is among them" → "what is among them"** (v18) — אֶת־אֲשֶׁר־בָּם, kept; obscure (what is done among them, or what will come on them). **"O congregation" → "congregation"** — עֵדָה, no "O".
- **"Hear, O earth: behold, I will bring evil" → "Hear, earth! Behold, I am bringing evil"** (v19) — הִנֵּה אָנֹכִי מֵבִיא, participle. **"the fruit of their thoughts"** — kept; מַחְשְׁבוֹת. **"nor to my law, but rejected it" → "and my torah, they have rejected it"** — וְתוֹרָתִי וַיִּמְאֲסוּ־בָהּ: "my torah" stands first, then "and they rejected it"; מָאַס "reject" (also v30).
- **"To what purpose cometh there to me incense from Sheba" → "Why is this to me, frankincense that comes from Sheba"** (v20) — לָמָּה־זֶּה לִּי; לְבוֹנָה "frankincense". **"the sweet cane" → "the good cane"** — קָנֶה הַטּוֹב. **"are not acceptable" → "are not for good pleasure"** — לֹא לְרָצוֹן (as Isa 56:7). **"nor your sacrifices sweet unto me" → "and your sacrifices are not sweet to me"** — עָרְבוּ.
- **"I will lay stumblingblocks before this people" → "I am giving to this people stumbling blocks"** (v21) — הִנְנִי נֹתֵן אֶל, participle, "give to". **"shall fall upon them" → "shall stumble on them"** — כָּשְׁלוּ בָם. **"the neighbour and his friend shall perish" → "the neighbor and his friend, and they shall perish"** — the qere וְאָבָדוּ "and they shall perish" (the ketiv יאבדו "they shall perish", the same sense); שָׁכֵן "neighbor", רֵעַ "friend".
- **"cometh from the north country … shall be raised from the sides of the earth" → "is coming from the land of the north … is stirred up from the remote parts of the earth"** (v22) — בָּא participle; מֵאֶרֶץ צָפוֹן "from the land of the north"; יֵעוֹר nifal of עוּר "stir up"; יַרְכְּתֵי־אָרֶץ "the remote parts of the earth" (literally "the flanks").
- **"They shall lay hold on bow and spear; they are cruel" → "Bow and javelin they hold fast; cruel it is"** (v23) — the Hebrew order; כִּידוֹן "javelin"; הֶחֱזִיק "hold fast"; אַכְזָרִי הוּא, singular (the nation), then plural verbs. **"have no mercy" → "they have no compassion"** — רִחַם. **"set in array as men for war" → "arrayed as a man for battle"** — עָרוּךְ כְּאִישׁ, singular; מִלְחָמָה "battle" (the fight at hand). No "O" before "daughter of Zion".
- **"the fame thereof" → "the report of it"** (v24) — שָׁמְעוֹ. **"our hands wax feeble" → "our hands have gone slack"** — רָפוּ. **"anguish hath taken hold of us, and pain, as of a woman in travail" → "distress has taken hold of us, anguish like a woman giving birth"** — צָרָה "distress"; חִיל "anguish" (the writhing of one in labor); no "and".
- **"Go not forth … nor walk" → "Do not go out … and on the way do not walk"** (v25) — the qere plural תֵּצְאוּ … תֵּלֵכוּ (the ketiv is feminine singular, "you", the daughter of v23, 26: the same sense). **"for the sword of the enemy and fear is on every side" → "for the enemy has a sword, terror on every side"** — חֶרֶב לְאֹיֵב "a sword is the enemy's"; מָגוֹר מִסָּבִיב "terror on every side" (also 20:3, 10; 46:5; 49:29).
- **"O daughter of my people, gird thee with sackcloth, and wallow thyself in ashes" → "Daughter of my people, gird on sackcloth, and roll in the ashes"** (v26) — no "O"; בָאֵפֶר with the article. **"make thee mourning, as for an only son, most bitter lamentation" → "make for yourself the mourning of an only one, a lament of bitterness"** — אֵבֶל יָחִיד: "son" is not in the word (as Gen 22:2); מִסְפַּד תַּמְרוּרִים "a lament of bitternesses". **"the spoiler shall suddenly come" → "suddenly the waster shall come"** — הַשֹּׁדֵד, participle of שָׁדַד "lay waste".
- **"I have set thee for a tower and a fortress among my people, that thou mayest know and try their way" → "A tester I have made you among my people, a fortress, and you shall know and try their way"** (v27) — בָּחוֹן, a noun of בָּחַן "try" (KJV "tower" reads בַּחַן, Isa 32:14); מִבְצָר "fortress" stands after "among my people" (a common emendation reads "one who assays", from בָּצַר, from memory: not followed); וְתֵדַע "and you shall know".
- **"They are all grievous revolters, walking with slanders: they are brass and iron; they are all corrupters" → "All of them are the most stubborn of the stubborn, going about as talebearers, bronze and iron; all of them, workers of ruin are they"** (v28) — סָרֵי סוֹרְרִים, literally "the sullen of the stubborn" (סַר "sullen", 1 Kgs 20:43; or "turners-aside", from סוּר): a superlative; הֹלְכֵי רָכִיל "going about as talebearers"; נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze"; מַשְׁחִיתִים "workers of ruin" (שִׁחֵת).
- **"The bellows are burned, the lead is consumed of the fire" → "The bellows blow fiercely; by the fire the lead has come to an end"** (v29) — נָחַר "blow hard, snort" (KJV "burn", from חָרַר); the qere מֵאֵשׁ תַּם "by the fire … has come to an end" (the ketiv מאשתם is read "by their fire"). **"the founder melteth in vain" → "for emptiness he has surely refined"** — לַשָּׁוְא "for emptiness" (in vain); צָרַף צָרוֹף, the verb with its infinitive absolute; the subject is not named. **"the wicked are not plucked away" → "the evil are not drawn out"** — רָעִים "evil ones"; נִתָּקוּ "be drawn out".
- **"Reprobate silver shall men call them" → "Rejected silver they have called them"** (v30) — כֶּסֶף נִמְאָס, the participle of מָאַס "reject" (the same verb as "has rejected them"); קָרְאוּ perfect.
