[5] I myself will fight against you with outstretched hand and strong arm, in anger and in fury and in great wrath.

[6] And I will strike down the inhabitants of this city, both man and beast. They shall die of a great pestilence.

[7] Afterward, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, I will give ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and his servants and the people in this city who survive the pestilence, sword, and famine into the hand of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and into the hand of their enemies, into the hand of those who seek their lives. [8] "And to this people you shall say: 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I set before you the way of life and the way of death.

[11] "And to the house of the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ say, 'Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[13] "Behold, I am against you, O inhabitant of the valley,
