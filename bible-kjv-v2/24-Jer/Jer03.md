# Jeremiah 3

[1] Saying: If a man sends away his wife,
and she goes from him
and becomes another man's,
will he turn back to her again?
Would not that land be surely polluted?
And you, you have played the prostitute with many companions;
and turn back to me?
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] Lift up your eyes to the bare heights, and see:
where have you not been lain with?
By the ways you have sat for them,
like an ⟦arabs|Arvi|Arab⟧ in the wilderness;
and you have polluted the land
with your prostitutions and with your evil.

[3] And the showers have been withheld,
and the latter rain has not been;
and the forehead of a prostitute woman you have had;
you have refused to be dishonored.

[4] Have you not from now called to me,
"My father! The close friend of my youth are you!

[5] Will he keep anger forever?
Or will he keep it to the end?"
Behold, you have spoken,
and you have done the evil things, and you have prevailed.

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me in the days of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ the king: Have you seen what turning-away ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has done? She went on every high mountain and to under every luxuriant tree, and played the prostitute there.

[7] And I said, After she has done all these things, to me she will turn back; and she did not turn back. And her sister, treacherous ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saw it.

[8] And I saw that, for all the causes in which turning-away ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ committed adultery, I had sent her away and given her document of cutting off to her; and treacherous ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, her sister, did not fear, and she went and played the prostitute, she also.

[9] And it came to pass, from the lightness of her prostitution, she polluted the land, and committed adultery with the stone and with the tree.

[10] And even in all this her sister, treacherous ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, did not turn back to me with all her heart, but in falsehood, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me: Turning-away ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has shown her soul righteous, more than treacherous ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[12] Go, and call these words toward the north, and say,

Turn back, turning-away ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
I will not make my face fall upon you,
for I am faithful, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
I will not keep anger forever.

[13] Only know your iniquity,
that against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you have transgressed,
and you have scattered your ways to the strangers under every luxuriant tree,
and to my voice you have not listened,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] Turn back, turning-away sons,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
for I, I have become your husband;
and I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan,
and I will bring you to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[15] And I will give you shepherds according to my heart,
and they shall shepherd you with knowledge and insight.

[16] And it shall be, when you multiply and are fruitful in the land, in those days, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, they shall not say again, "The ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧," and it shall not come up on the heart, and they shall not remember it, and they shall not miss it, and it shall not be made again.

[17] At that time they shall call ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ the throne of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and all the nations shall be gathered to it, to the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they shall not walk again after the stubbornness of their evil heart.

[18] In those days the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall walk with the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they shall come together from the land of the north to the land that I made your fathers inherit.

[19] And I, I said,
How I will set you among the sons,
and give you a land of desire,
an inheritance of the beauty of the hosts of nations!
And I said, My father you shall call me,
and from after me you shall not turn back.

[20] Surely, as a woman deals treacherously away from her companion,
so you have dealt treacherously with me, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[21] A voice on the bare heights is heard,
the weeping, the pleas for favor of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
for they have made their way crooked,
they have forgotten ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[22] Turn back, turning-away sons;
I will heal your turnings away.
Here we are; we have come to you,
for you are ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[23] Surely, for falsehood, from the hills —
the multitude of the mountains.
Surely in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
is the salvation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[24] And the shame has devoured
the labor of our fathers from our youth,
their flock and their herd,
their sons and their daughters.

[25] Let us lie down in our shame,
and let our dishonor cover us;
for against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we have sinned,
we and our fathers, from our youth and to this day,
and we have not listened to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13, 21, 22, 23, 25) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** — v1–5, 12b–15 and 19–25 are poetry, one Hebrew line per printed line; v6–12a and 16–18 are prose. The outer quotation marks are dropped: Yahweh speaks through the chapter (v6, 11 name him as the speaker); quotation marks stand only for words cited inside his speech (v4–5, 16).
- **שׁוּב · מְשֻׁבָה · שׁוֹבָב** (v1, 6–8, 10–12, 14, 19, 22; 2:19) — one root (the play is named here; מְשׁוּבָה "turning away" by the reviewer row, as 5:6; 8:5; 14:7): שׁוּב "turn back" (the שׁוּב row: not "return", "repent"); מְשֻׁבָה "turning away" (KJV "backsliding"), used of Israel as a name: "turning-away Israel" (v6, 8, 11, 12); plural מְשׁוּבֹת "turnings away" (v22; 2:19); שׁוֹבָבִים "turning-away" (v14, 22, of the sons). So the play of v12, 14, 22 shows: "Turn back, turning-away Israel"; "I will heal your turnings away". The Hebrew word says turning away from Yahweh; turning back to him is the same verb.
- **בָּגוֹדָה · בֹּגֵדָה** (v7, 8, 10, 11; verb v20) — "treacherous" (the בָּגַד row "deal treacherously"), the epithet of Judah beside Israel's "turning away"; KJV has "treacherous" too.
- **"They say, If a man put away his wife" → "Saying: If a man sends away his wife"** (v1) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר "saying" opens the verse (it joins the words to what goes before; the Septuagint has no word here: from memory); הֵן "if" (also "behold"); שִׁלַּח "send away" (the שִׁלַּח row of a wife, as Deut 24:1–4: not "divorce"); וְהָיְתָה לְאִישׁ־אַחֵר "and becomes another man's" (the הָיָה לְאִישׁ row). **"shall he return unto her again?" → "will he turn back to her again?"** — הֲיָשׁוּב. **"shall not that land be greatly polluted?" → "Would not that land be surely polluted?"** — חָנוֹף תֶּחֱנַף, infinitive absolute with its verb ("polluted, it shall be polluted": the "surely" of the standing decision); חָנֵף "be polluted, profaned" (as the הֶחֱנִיף "pollute" row; also v2, 9). The Septuagint reads "that woman" (from memory); the Hebrew "land". **"thou hast played the harlot with many lovers; yet return again to me" → "you have played the prostitute with many companions; and turn back to me?"** — זָנָה "play the prostitute" (its row); רֵעִים "companions" (רֵעַ, as v20; not "lovers": that is עֹגְבִים, 4:30); וְשׁוֹב אֵלַי, an infinitive absolute: "and turning back to me" — read as a question ("and would you turn back to me?") or as a call ("yet turn back to me!"): the form decides neither; kept open.
- **"Lift up thine eyes unto the high places … where thou hast not been lien with" → "Lift up your eyes to the bare heights … where have you not been lain with?"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׁפָיִם "bare heights" (as Isa 41:18; also v21); שֻׁכַּבְתְּ is the qere, "you have been lain with"; the written form שגלת is a coarser word for the same act ("you have been ravished" in the plain sense; the reviewer's qere row). **"In the ways hast thou sat for them, as the Arabian in the wilderness; and thou hast polluted the land with thy whoredoms and with thy wickedness" → "By the ways you have sat for them, like an Arab in the wilderness; and you have polluted the land with your prostitutions and with your evil"** — זְנוּתַיִךְ "your prostitutions" (the זְנוּת row); רָעָתֵךְ "your evil" (the רָעָה row); הֶחֱנִיף "pollute".
- **"Therefore the showers have been withholden, and there hath been no latter rain; and thou hadst a whore's forehead, thou refusedst to be ashamed" → "And the showers have been withheld, and the latter rain has not been; and the forehead of a prostitute woman you have had; you have refused to be dishonored"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּמָּנְעוּ "and … have been withheld" ("therefore" is not written); רְבִבִים "showers" (its row); מַלְקוֹשׁ "latter rain" (its row); אִשָּׁה זוֹנָה "a woman, a prostitute"; הִכָּלֵם, nifal of כָּלַם, "be dishonored" (the root of כְּלִמָּה "dishonor", v25; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed" is another word).
- **"Wilt thou not from this time cry unto me, My father, thou art the guide of my youth?" → "Have you not from now called to me, My father! The close friend of my youth are you!"** (v4) — Hebrew קָרָאת is the qere, "you have called" (the written form reads like "I called", or an old spelling of "you called"); מֵעַתָּה "from now"; אַלּוּף נְעֻרַי "the close friend of my youth" (the אַלּוּף row of a companion, Prov 2:17; not the "chief" of Edom).
- **"Will he reserve his anger for ever? will he keep it to the end? Behold, thou hast spoken and done evil things as thou couldest" → "Will he keep anger forever? Or will he keep it to the end? Behold, you have spoken, and you have done the evil things, and you have prevailed"** (v5) — Hebrew הֲיִנְטֹר "will he keep (anger)" (the נָטַר row: "anger" supplied) and יִשְׁמֹר "keep" (the שָׁמַר row): English has one word for both; אִם "or"; לָנֶצַח "to the end"; דִּבַּרְתְּ is the qere, "you have spoken" (written form: "I have spoken", or an old spelling); הָרָעוֹת "the evil things"; וַתּוּכָל, literally "and you have been able": you carried it through. The close of the people's words (after v5a) is set by the sense: v4–5a are what Israel says, v5b Yahweh's answer.
- **"The LORD said also unto me in the days of Josiah the king … she is gone up upon every high mountain and under every green tree" → "And Yahweh said to me in the days of Josiah the king … She went on every high mountain and to under every luxuriant tree"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר "And … said" ("also" is not written); הֹלְכָה הִיא "she was going"; אֶל־תַּחַת "to under"; רַעֲנָן "luxuriant" (its row).
- **"And I said after she had done all these things, Turn thou unto me. But she returned not" → "And I said, After she has done all these things, to me she will turn back; and she did not turn back"** (v7) — Hebrew אֵלַי תָּשׁוּב, an imperfect: "she will turn back to me" (KJV makes it a command); וַתֵּרֶא is the qere, "and she saw" (written form ותראה, the same verb). "her sister, treacherous Judah", in the Hebrew order (also v10).
- **"And I saw, when for all the causes whereby backsliding Israel committed adultery I had put her away, and given her a bill of divorce" → "And I saw that, for all the causes in which turning-away Israel committed adultery, I had sent her away and given her document of cutting off to her"** (v8) — Hebrew וָאֵרֶא "And I saw" (the Septuagint and Syriac read "she saw": from memory); עַל־כָּל־אֹדוֹת אֲשֶׁר "for all the causes in which"; נִאֲפָה "committed adultery" (the נָאַף row); סֵפֶר כְּרִיתֻתֶיהָ "her document of cutting off" (the סֵפֶר כְּרִיתוּת row). **"but went and played the harlot also" → "and she went and played the prostitute, she also"** — וַתֵּלֶךְ "and she went"; גַּם־הִיא "she also".
- **"And it came to pass through the lightness of her whoredom, that she defiled the land, and committed adultery with stones and with stocks" → "And it came to pass, from the lightness of her prostitution, she polluted the land, and committed adultery with the stone and with the tree"** (v9) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it came to pass"; מִקֹּל זְנוּתָהּ "from the lightness of her prostitution" (קֹל from קָלַל "be light": she held it light; read as קוֹל, "from the noise of"): uncertain; וַתֶּחֱנַף "she polluted" (qal with an object, as v2 has hifil); הָאֶבֶן · הָעֵץ, singular with the article, as 2:27; אֶת "with".
- **"turned unto me with her whole heart, but feignedly" → "did not turn back to me with all her heart, but in falsehood"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּכָל־לִבָּהּ "with all her heart"; כִּי אִם־בְּשֶׁקֶר "but in falsehood" (שֶׁקֶר, its row).
- **"The backsliding Israel hath justified herself more than treacherous Judah" → "Turning-away Israel has shown her soul righteous, more than treacherous Judah"** (v11) — Hebrew צִדְּקָה נַפְשָׁהּ, piel of צָדַק with "her soul" as object: "has shown herself righteous"; מִן "more than".
- **"proclaim these words toward the north" → "call these words toward the north"** (v12) — Hebrew קָרָא (as 2:2). **"I will not cause mine anger to fall upon you: for I am merciful, saith the LORD, and I will not keep anger for ever" → "I will not make my face fall upon you, for I am faithful, declares Yahweh; I will not keep anger forever"** — לוֹא־אַפִּיל פָּנַי בָּכֶם "I will not make my face fall on you" ("face", not "anger": the face falls on one in anger); חָסִיד "faithful" (one who keeps *chesed*; the חָסִיד row, no token); אֶטּוֹר, the נָטַר row ("anger" supplied).
- **"that thou hast transgressed against the LORD thy God, and hast scattered thy ways to the strangers … and ye have not obeyed my voice" → "that against Yahweh your Elohim you have transgressed, and you have scattered your ways to the strangers … and to my voice you have not listened"** (v13) — Hebrew פָּשַׁע בְּ "transgress against" (as 2:8); דְּרָכַיִךְ "your ways" (no "favors"); זָרִים "strangers"; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of" (the שָׁמַע row: not "obey"); the plural "you" of the last line kept.
- **"Turn, O backsliding children … for I am married unto you: and I will take you one of a city, and two of a family" → "Turn back, turning-away sons … for I, I have become your husband; and I will take you, one from a city and two from a clan"** (v14) — Hebrew בָנִים "sons"; no "O"; כִּי אָנֹכִי בָּעַלְתִּי בָכֶם: the pronoun written; בָּעַל "become the husband of" (lit. "become owner of"; with בְּ the verb is also read "I have been lord over you": its row); מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan".
- **"I will give you pastors according to mine heart, which shall feed you with knowledge and understanding" → "I will give you shepherds according to my heart, and they shall shepherd you with knowledge and insight"** (v15) — Hebrew רֹעִים · וְרָעוּ, noun and verb of one root (the רֹעֶה row: "shepherd … to shepherd"); דֵּעָה "knowledge"; הַשְׂכֵּיל, infinitive of הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight" (its row).
- **"And it shall come to pass, when ye be multiplied and increased in the land … neither shall it come to mind: neither shall they remember it; neither shall they visit it; neither shall that be done any more" → "And it shall be, when you multiply and are fruitful in the land … and it shall not come up on the heart, and they shall not remember it, and they shall not miss it, and it shall not be made again"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהָיָה כִּי "And it shall be, when" (its row); רָבָה · פָּרָה "multiply · be fruitful"; אֲרוֹן "ark" (the word is "chest": its row); יַעֲלֶה עַל־לֵב "come up on the heart"; יִפְקֹדוּ, פָּקַד "visit, attend to", here of what is looked for and not found: "miss" (the פָּקַד row; "visit" in KJV); יֵעָשֶׂה "be made" (or "be done").
- **"all the nations shall be gathered unto it, to the name of the LORD … neither shall they walk any more after the imagination of their evil heart" → "all the nations shall be gathered to it, to the name of Yahweh … and they shall not walk again after the stubbornness of their evil heart"** (v17) — Hebrew לְשֵׁם יְהוָה "to the name of Yahweh" (no "presence"); שְׁרִרוּת לֵב "the stubbornness of the heart" (its row).
- **"the house of Judah shall walk with the house of Israel … to the land that I have given for an inheritance unto your fathers" → "the house of Judah shall walk with the house of Israel … to the land that I made your fathers inherit"** (v18) — Hebrew עַל "upon, beside": "with"; הִנְחַלְתִּי, hifil of נָחַל, "I made … inherit" (the נִחַל row).
- **"But I said, How shall I put thee among the children, and give thee a pleasant land, a goodly heritage of the hosts of nations? and I said, Thou shalt call me, My father; and shalt not turn away from me" → "And I, I said, How I will set you among the sons, and give you a land of desire, an inheritance of the beauty of the hosts of nations! And I said, My father you shall call me, and from after me you shall not turn back"** (v19) — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי "and I", the pronoun written; אֵיךְ "how!" (or "how shall I …?"); בָּנִים "sons"; אֶרֶץ חֶמְדָּה "a land of desire"; נַחֲלַת צְבִי צִבְאוֹת גּוֹיִם "an inheritance of the beauty of the hosts of nations" (צְבִי "beauty", its row; צִבְאוֹת is pointed as "hosts", a play of sound with צְבִי; read as a plural of צְבִי, "the beauty of beauties of the nations"); תִּקְרְאִי … תָשׁוּבִי are the qere, feminine singular, "you shall call … you shall not turn back" (the written forms are plural).
- **"Surely as a wife treacherously departeth from her husband, so have ye dealt treacherously with me, O house of Israel" → "Surely, as a woman deals treacherously away from her companion, so you have dealt treacherously with me, house of Israel"** (v20) — Hebrew אָכֵן "surely"; אִשָּׁה "woman" (the אִשָּׁה row); בָּגַד מִן "deal treacherously away from"; רֵעַ "companion" (as v1; "husband" is the sense); no "O".
- **"weeping and supplications of the children of Israel: for they have perverted their way" → "the weeping, the pleas for favor of the sons of Israel; for they have made their way crooked"** (v21) — Hebrew בְּכִי תַחֲנוּנֵי, two nouns without "and"; תַּחֲנוּנִים "pleas for favor" (its row); בְּנֵי "sons of"; הֶעֱוּוּ, hifil of עָוָה "be bent", the root of עָוֹן "iniquity".
- **"Return, ye backsliding children, and I will heal your backslidings. Behold, we come unto thee" → "Turn back, turning-away sons; I will heal your turnings away. Here we are; we have come to you"** (v22) — Hebrew אֶרְפָּה "I will heal" (רָפָא, its row); הִנְנוּ "here we are" (lit. "behold us": the הִנֵּנִי row), אָתָנוּ "we have come" (אָתָה). The people answer from "Here we are" on (to v25).
- **"Truly in vain is salvation hoped for from the hills, and from the multitude of mountains" → "Surely, for falsehood, from the hills — the multitude of the mountains"** (v23) — Hebrew אָכֵן לַשֶּׁקֶר מִגְּבָעוֹת הָמוֹן הָרִים: no verb; "for falsehood (is what comes) from the hills, the multitude (the noise of the crowds) of the mountains": the words as they stand, the line left without a verb; uncertain. הָמוֹן "multitude" (its row). **"truly in the LORD our God is the salvation of Israel"** — תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation" (its row).
- **"For shame hath devoured the labour of our fathers from our youth" → "And the shame has devoured the labor of our fathers from our youth"** (v24) — Hebrew וְהַבֹּשֶׁת "and the shame", with the article (a name put for the Baal, as in names like Ish-bosheth: said here only); יְגִיעַ "labor"; צֹאן · בָּקָר "flock · herd".
- **"We lie down in our shame, and our confusion covereth us" → "Let us lie down in our shame, and let our dishonor cover us"** (v25) — Hebrew נִשְׁכְּבָה, cohortative, and וּתְכַסֵּנוּ, jussive: wishes, not statements; כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (the reviewer row; בֹּשֶׁת "shame"). **"we have not obeyed the voice of the LORD" → "we have not listened to the voice of Yahweh"** — שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל.
