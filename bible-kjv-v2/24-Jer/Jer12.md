# Jeremiah 12

[1] Righteous are you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
when I contend with you;
yet judgments let me speak with you:
Why does the way of the wicked prosper,
all who deal treacherously with treachery are at ease?

[2] You have planted them; they have also taken root;
they go on; they have also made fruit;
near are you in their mouth,
and far from their kidneys.

[3] But you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, know me;
you see me, and you have tried my heart with you.
Draw them out like a flock for slaughter,
and sanctify them for the day of killing.

[4] How long shall the land mourn,
and the herb of every field be dried out?
For the evil of those who dwell in it
beasts and birds are swept away,
for they said, "He will not see our latter end."

[5] "For with men on foot you have run, and they have wearied you,
and how will you compete with the horses?
And in a land of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ you are trusting,
and how will you do in the pride of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧?

[6] For even your brothers and the house of your father,
even they have dealt treacherously with you;
even they have called after you in full.
Do not believe them,
though they speak good things to you."

[7] "I have forsaken my house;
I have abandoned my inheritance;
I have given the beloved of my soul
into the palm of her enemies.

[8] My inheritance has become to me
like a lion in the forest;
she has given out her voice against me;
therefore I have hated her.

[9] Is my inheritance to me a speckled bird of prey?
Are the birds of prey around against her?
Go, gather all the living things of the field,
bring them to devour.

[10] Many shepherds have ruined my vineyard;
they have trampled my portion;
they have made the portion of my delight
a wilderness of desolation.

[11] One has made it a desolation;
it mourns to me, desolate;
all the land is made desolate,
for there is no man laying it on heart.

[12] Upon all the bare heights in the wilderness
devastators have come,
for a sword of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is devouring
from the end of the land to the end of the land;
there is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ for any flesh.

[13] They have sown wheat, and thorns they have reaped;
they have made themselves sick, they do not profit;
and be ashamed of your yields,
from the burning of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[14] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning all my evil neighbors who touch the inheritance that I made my people, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, inherit: "Behold, I am plucking them up from upon their ground, and the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ I will pluck up from their midst.

[15] And it shall be, after my plucking them up, I will turn back and have compassion on them, and I will bring them back, each man to his inheritance and each man to his land.

[16] And it shall be, if they will surely learn the ways of my people, to swear by my name, 'As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives,' as they taught my people to swear by the Baal, then they shall be built up in the midst of my people.

[17] And if they will not hear, then I will pluck up that nation, plucking up and doing away with it, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** — v1–13 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order; v14–17 are prose, as printed.
- **Vocative** (v1, 3) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"Righteous art thou, O LORD, when I plead with thee" → "Righteous are you, Yahweh, when I contend with you"** (v1) — Hebrew כִּי אָרִיב אֵלֶיךָ; רִיב "contend" (in law). **"yet let me talk with thee of thy judgments" → "yet judgments let me speak with you"** — אַךְ מִשְׁפָּטִים אֲדַבֵּר אוֹתָךְ, the object first; "thy" is not in the Hebrew; אוֹתָךְ "with you". **"Wherefore doth the way of the wicked prosper? wherefore are all they happy that deal very treacherously?" → "Why does the way of the wicked prosper, all who deal treacherously with treachery are at ease?"** — one מַדּוּעַ "why" for both lines (the second "why" was supplied); צָלֵחָה (צָלַח "prosper"); שָׁלוּ "are at ease"; בֹּגְדֵי בָגֶד "those who deal treacherously with treachery", verb and noun of one root.
- **"Thou hast planted them, yea, they have taken root: they grow, yea, they bring forth fruit" → "You have planted them; they have also taken root; they go on; they have also made fruit"** (v2) — Hebrew נְטַעְתָּם גַּם־שֹׁרָשׁוּ יֵלְכוּ גַּם־עָשׂוּ פֶרִי: perfects, גַּם "also" twice; יֵלְכוּ "they go (on)"; עָשָׂה פְּרִי "make fruit". **"and far from their reins" → "and far from their kidneys"** — כִּלְיוֹתֵיהֶם "their kidneys" (the inmost self).
- **"But thou, O LORD, knowest me: thou hast seen me, and tried mine heart toward thee" → "But you, Yahweh, know me; you see me, and you have tried my heart with you"** (v3) — Hebrew יְדַעְתָּנִי (perfect of a state), תִּרְאֵנִי (imperfect), וּבָחַנְתָּ לִבִּי אִתָּךְ: בָּחַן "try"; אִתָּךְ "with you" (= how my heart is toward you). **"pull them out like sheep for the slaughter" → "Draw them out like a flock for slaughter"** — הַתִּקֵם, hifil of נָתַק "draw out, tear away" (as the cords of 10:20); צֹאן "flock". **"and prepare them for the day of slaughter" → "and sanctify them for the day of killing"** — הַקְדִּשֵׁם, hifil of the root of קֹדֶשׁ (set apart as holy, as for a sacrifice); הֲרֵגָה "killing" (הָרַג "kill"), a second word beside טִבְחָה "slaughter".
- **"How long shall the land mourn, and the herbs of every field wither" → "How long shall the land mourn, and the herb of every field be dried out?"** (v4) — Hebrew אָבַל "mourn"; עֵשֶׂב singular; יָבֵשׁ "be dried out". **"for the wickedness of them that dwell therein? the beasts are consumed, and the birds" → "For the evil of those who dwell in it beasts and birds are swept away"** — מֵרָעַת "from the evil"; סָפְתָה (סָפָה "sweep away"). **"because they said, He shall not see our last end" → "for they said, He will not see our latter end"** — אַחֲרִית "latter end"; "he" is not named (God, or the prophet).
- **"If thou hast run with the footmen" → "For with men on foot you have run"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי "for" (or "if": the Hebrew allows both); רַגְלִים "men on foot". **"then how canst thou contend with horses?" → "and how will you compete with the horses?"** — וְאֵיךְ, "and" kept; תְּתַחֲרֶה "compete" (only here and 22:15). **"and if in the land of peace, wherein thou trustedst, they wearied thee" → "And in a land of shalom you are trusting"** — וּבְאֶרֶץ שָׁלוֹם אַתָּה בוֹטֵחַ, participle "trusting"; שָׁלוֹם, the noun (token); "they wearied thee" is not in the Hebrew of this line. **"then how wilt thou do in the swelling of Jordan?" → "and how will you do in the pride of the Jordan?"** — גְּאוֹן הַיַּרְדֵּן, literally "the pride (height) of the Jordan": its thickets.
- **"For even thy brethren, and the house of thy father, even they have dealt treacherously with thee" → "For even your brothers and the house of your father, even they have dealt treacherously with you"** (v6) — Hebrew גַּם three times, kept. **"yea, they have called a multitude after thee" → "even they have called after you in full"** — קָרְאוּ אַחֲרֶיךָ מָלֵא, מָלֵא "full" as an adverb (with full voice; uncertain). **"believe them not, though they speak fair words unto thee" → "Do not believe them, though they speak good things to you"** — הֶאֱמִין בְּ "believe"; טוֹבוֹת "good things".
- **"I have left mine heritage" → "I have abandoned my inheritance"** (v7) — Hebrew נָטַשְׁתִּי (נָטַשׁ "abandon", beside עָזַב "forsake"); נַחֲלָה "inheritance" (also v8, 9, 14, 15). **"the dearly beloved of my soul" → "the beloved of my soul"** — יְדִדוּת נַפְשִׁי, an abstract noun ("the belovedness of my soul"). **"into the hand of her enemies" → "into the palm of her enemies"** — בְּכַף.
- **"it crieth out against me" → "she has given out her voice against me"** (v8) — Hebrew נָתְנָה עָלַי בְּקוֹלָהּ, literally "she has given against me with her voice". **"therefore have I hated it" → "therefore I have hated her"** — שְׂנֵאתִיהָ, perfect; the inheritance is feminine.
- **"Mine heritage is unto me as a speckled bird, the birds round about are against her" → "Is my inheritance to me a speckled bird of prey? Are the birds of prey around against her?"** (v9) — Hebrew הַעַיִט צָבוּעַ … הַעַיִט: two questions with הֲ; עַיִט "bird(s) of prey"; צָבוּעַ "colored, speckled" (only here; the Septuagint read "hyena", from memory: "a hyena's lair" came from it). **"assemble all the beasts of the field, come to devour" → "gather all the living things of the field, bring them to devour"** — חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה "the living things of the field"; הֵתָיוּ "bring", hifil of אָתָה.
- **"Many pastors have destroyed my vineyard, they have trodden my portion under foot" → "Many shepherds have ruined my vineyard; they have trampled my portion"** (v10) — Hebrew רֹעִים "shepherds"; שִׁחֵת "ruin"; בֹּסְסוּ "trampled"; חֶלְקָה "portion" (beside חֵלֶק "share", 10:16). **"they have made my pleasant portion a desolate wilderness" → "they have made the portion of my delight a wilderness of desolation"** — חֶלְקַת חֶמְדָּתִי, חֶמְדָּה "delight, desire"; לְמִדְבַּר שְׁמָמָה; נָתַן לְ "make into", literally "give for".
- **"They have made it desolate" → "One has made it a desolation"** (v11) — Hebrew שָׂמָהּ, singular, no subject named ("they" was supplied). **"and being desolate it mourneth unto me" → "it mourns to me, desolate"** — אָבְלָה עָלַי שְׁמֵמָה; עָלַי "to me, before me". **"because no man layeth it to heart" → "for there is no man laying it on heart"** — אֵין אִישׁ שָׂם עַל־לֵב. שְׁמָמָה, שָׁמֵם three times in the verse, "desolation", "desolate", "made desolate".
- **"The spoilers are come upon all high places through the wilderness" → "Upon all the bare heights in the wilderness devastators have come"** (v12) — Hebrew שְׁפָיִם "bare heights"; שֹׁדְדִים "devastators" (שָׁדַד "lay waste"). **"for the sword of the LORD shall devour from the one end of the land even to the other end of the land" → "for a sword of Yahweh is devouring from the end of the land to the end of the land"** — חֶרֶב לַיהוָה, "a sword belonging to Yahweh"; אֹכְלָה participle. **"no flesh shall have peace" → "there is no shalom for any flesh"** — אֵין שָׁלוֹם לְכָל־בָּשָׂר (token).
- **"They have sown wheat, but shall reap thorns" → "They have sown wheat, and thorns they have reaped"** (v13) — Hebrew קָצָרוּ, perfect, the object first. **"they have put themselves to pain, but shall not profit" → "they have made themselves sick, they do not profit"** — נֶחְלוּ, nifal of חָלָה "be sick" (as 10:19 נַחְלָה); יוֹעִלוּ. **"and they shall be ashamed of your revenues" → "and be ashamed of your yields"** — וּבֹשׁוּ, an imperative "be ashamed" (it matches "your"), or "and they shall be ashamed" (KJV): the form allows both; תְּבוּאֹתֵיכֶם "your yields". **"because of the fierce anger of the LORD" → "from the burning of the anger of Yahweh"** — מֵחֲרוֹן אַף־יְהוָה; אַף "anger".
- **"against all mine evil neighbours, that touch the inheritance which I have caused my people Israel to inherit" → "concerning all my evil neighbors who touch the inheritance that I made my people, Israel, inherit"** (v14) — Hebrew עַל "concerning" (also "against"); הִנְחַלְתִּי "I made inherit". **"Behold, I will pluck them out of their land" → "Behold, I am plucking them up from upon their ground"** — הִנְנִי נֹתְשָׁם, participle; מֵעַל אַדְמָתָם "from upon their ground"; נָתַשׁ "pluck up" (v14–17). **"and pluck out the house of Judah from among them" → "and the house of Judah I will pluck up from their midst"** — the object first.
- **"And it shall come to pass, after that I have plucked them out, I will return, and have compassion on them" → "And it shall be, after my plucking them up, I will turn back and have compassion on them"** (v15) — Hebrew וְהָיָה; אָשׁוּב וְרִחַמְתִּים, שׁוּב with a second verb (= again have compassion: kept). **"and will bring them again, every man to his heritage, and every man to his land" → "and I will bring them back, each man to his inheritance and each man to his land"** — הֲשִׁבֹתִים, אִישׁ … וְאִישׁ.
- **"if they will diligently learn the ways of my people" → "if they will surely learn the ways of my people"** (v16) — Hebrew לָמֹד יִלְמְדוּ, infinitive absolute ("learning they learn"). **"The LORD liveth" → "As Yahweh lives"** — חַי־יְהוָה, the oath formula. **"as they taught my people to swear by Baal" → "as they taught my people to swear by the Baal"** — בַּבָּעַל, the article; לִמֵּד "teach" and לָמַד "learn" are one root. **"then shall they be built in the midst of my people" → "then they shall be built up in the midst of my people"** — וְנִבְנוּ.
- **"But if they will not obey" → "And if they will not hear"** (v17) — Hebrew וְאִם לֹא יִשְׁמָעוּ. **"I will utterly pluck up and destroy that nation" → "then I will pluck up that nation, plucking up and doing away with it"** — וְנָתַשְׁתִּי … נָתוֹשׁ וְאַבֵּד, two infinitives absolute after the verb; אִבֵּד piel "do away with" (literally "make perish").
