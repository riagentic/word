## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2).
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose. **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Jer 40:7–9 and 2 Kgs 25:23–24** — the same events; the wording of Kings is kept where the Hebrew is the same ("the commanders of the forces, they and their men", "appointed", "dwell in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it shall go well with you", "the Mizpah" with the article); the differences are listed below.
- **"after that Nebuzar-adan the captain of the guard had let him go from Ramah, when he had taken him being bound in chains among all that were carried away captive of Jerusalem and Judah, which were carried away captive unto Babylon" → "after Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards had sent him away from the Ramah, when he took him, and he was bound in chains in the midst of all the exiles of Jerusalem and Judah who were being carried into exile to Babylon"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁלַּח "send away" (also v5); הָרָמָה, the name with the article, "the height"; וְהוּא אָסוּר "and he was bound", a clause; אֲזִקִּים "chains" (only here and v4: perhaps manacles); גָּלוּת "exile", here of the people, "the exiles"; הַמֻּגְלִים "being carried into exile" (הִגְלָה). The heading "the word that came" stands, but no word of Yahweh follows until 42:7; what follows is the chief of the guards' speech.
- **"The LORD thy God hath pronounced this evil upon this place" → "Yahweh your Elohim spoke this evil against this place"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיִּקַּח … לְיִרְמְיָהוּ (the object with לְ); דִּבֶּר "spoke"; רָעָה "evil" (as 39:16).
- **"Now the LORD hath brought it, and done according as he hath said: because ye have sinned against the LORD, and have not obeyed his voice, therefore this thing is come upon you" → "And Yahweh has brought it and has done as he spoke; for you have sinned against Yahweh and have not listened to his voice, and this thing has come to you"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיָּבֵא וַיַּעַשׂ יְהוָה, "and he brought and Yahweh did" ("it" supplied: English needs an object); כִּי "for"; חָטָא לְ "sin against"; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of"; וְהָיָה לָכֶם "and it has been to you" ("therefore" is not written). "You" is plural (the people). The reading (qere) is הַדָּבָר "the thing"; the written text (ketiv) דבר, without the article: the same sense.
- **"I loose thee this day from the chains which were upon thine hand … come; and I will look well unto thee: but if it seem ill unto thee … forbear: behold, all the land is before thee: whither it seemeth good and convenient for thee to go, thither go" → "I have set you free today from the chains that are on your hand … come, and I will set my eye on you; and if it is bad in your eyes … forbear. See, all the land is before you; to where it is good and right in your eyes to go, there go"** (v4) — Hebrew פִּתַּחְתִּיךָ, a perfect, "I have set you free" (literally "opened, unbound"); עַל־יָדֶךָ "on your hand", singular; אִם־טוֹב בְּעֵינֶיךָ "if it is good in your eyes" (also v5); אָשִׂים אֶת־עֵינִי עָלֶיךָ "I will set my eye on you" (as 39:12); רַע "bad" (of what displeases); חֲדָל "forbear" (חָדַל "cease, leave off"); רְאֵה "see"; הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינֶיךָ "right in your eyes" (the reviewer's row); the Hebrew order "to the good and to the right in your eyes to go there, go".
- **"Now while he was not yet gone back, he said, Go back also to Gedaliah … whom the king of Babylon hath made governor over the cities of Judah" → "And while he was not yet turning back — 'And return to Gedaliah … whom the king of Babylon has appointed over the cities of Judah'"** (v5) — Hebrew וְעוֹדֶנּוּ לֹא־יָשׁוּב וְשֻׁבָה: the words are hard; literally "and he still would not turn back — and return …". "He said" is not written: the speech runs on without a verb of saying. The sense may be that Jeremiah had not yet answered or turned to go; the Septuagint differs (from memory). הִפְקִיד בְּ "appointed in, over" ("governor" is not written; as 2 Kgs 25:22); וְשֵׁב "and dwell"; בְּתוֹךְ "in the midst of". **"So the captain of the guard gave him victuals and a reward, and let him go" → "And the chief of the guards gave him an allowance and a portion, and sent him away"** — Hebrew אֲרֻחָה "allowance" (as 2 Kgs 25:30); מַשְׂאֵת "portion" (something lifted up and sent, as 2 Sam 11:8); וַיְשַׁלְּחֵהוּ "and sent him away".
- **"Then went Jeremiah unto Gedaliah … to Mizpah; and dwelt with him among the people" → "And Jeremiah came to Gedaliah … to the Mizpah, and dwelt with him in the midst of the people"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיָּבֹא "and came"; הַמִּצְפָּתָה "to the Mizpah" (the name has the article, "the watchtower", also v8, 10, 12, 13, 15; as 2 Kgs 25:23); יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"Now when all the captains of the forces which were in the fields, even they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had made Gedaliah … governor in the land, and had committed unto him men, and women, and children, and of the poor of the land" → "And all the commanders of the forces who were in the field, they and their men, heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah … in the land, and that he had committed to him men and women and little ones, and some of the poor of the land"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַחֲיָלִים "the commanders of the forces" (the שַׂר row; also v13); בַּשָּׂדֶה "in the field" (open country), singular; הִפְקִיד "appointed", then הִפְקִיד אִתּוֹ "committed with him"; טַף "little ones"; וּמִדַּלַּת הָאָרֶץ "and of the poor of the land" ("some" supplied for the partitive מִן); הָגְלוּ "were carried into exile". The sentence runs on into v8.
- **"then they came to Gedaliah to Mizpah, even Ishmael … and Jezaniah the son of a Maachathite" → "and they came to Gedaliah to the Mizpah, and Ishmael … and Jezaniah son of the Maacathite"** (v8) — the Hebrew has וְ "and" before Ishmael (KJV "even"): the list names those who came. "Jonathan" is written יוֹנָתָן here (not in 2 Kgs 25:23). The reading (qere) is עֵיפַי "Ephai"; the written text (ketiv) עופי "Ophai". "Jezaniah" is written יְזַנְיָהוּ (2 Kgs 25:23 יַאֲזַנְיָהוּ "Jaazaniah"). The Hebrew of Kings has "the men" first and "their men" at the end; here "they and their men" both times (v7, 8).
- **"Fear not to serve the Chaldeans: dwell in the land, and serve the king of Babylon, and it shall be well with you" → "Do not fear to serve the Chaldeans; dwell in the land and serve the king of Babylon, and it shall go well with you"** (v9) — Hebrew אַל־תִּירְאוּ מֵעֲבוֹד, literally "do not fear from serving" (2 Kgs 25:24 has מֵעַבְדֵי "from the servants of": "do not fear the servants of the Chaldeans"); "saying" stands for לֵאמֹר; וְיִיטַב לָכֶם "and it shall go well with you".
- **"As for me, behold, I will dwell at Mizpah, to serve the Chaldeans, which will come unto us: but ye, gather ye wine, and summer fruits, and oil, and put them in your vessels, and dwell in your cities that ye have taken" → "And I, behold, I am dwelling at the Mizpah, to stand before the Chaldeans who will come to us; and you, gather wine and summer fruit and oil, and put them in your vessels, and dwell in your cities that you have seized"** (v10) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי הִנְנִי יֹשֵׁב, a participle; לַעֲמֹד לִפְנֵי "to stand before" (to answer for you before them; "serve" is the sense in KJV, not the word); קַיִץ "summer fruit" (also v12); וְשִׂמוּ בִּכְלֵיכֶם "and put in your vessels" ("them" supplied); תָּפַשׂ "seize".
- **"Likewise when all the Jews that were in Moab, and among the Ammonites, and in Edom, and that were in all the countries … had left a remnant of Judah, and that he had set over them Gedaliah" → "And also all the Judeans who were in Moab and among the sons of Ammon and in Edom, and who were in all the lands … had given a remnant to Judah, and that he had appointed over them Gedaliah"** (v11) — Hebrew וְגַם "and also"; בִּבְנֵי־עַמּוֹן "among the sons of Ammon" (also v14); נָתַן שְׁאֵרִית לִיהוּדָה, literally "had given a remnant to Judah" (שְׁאֵרִית "remnant", also v15); הִפְקִיד עַל "appoint over". The sentence runs on into v12.
- **"Even all the Jews returned out of all places whither they were driven … and gathered wine and summer fruits very much" → "and all the Judeans returned from all the places where they had been driven … and they gathered wine and summer fruit, very much"** (v12) — Hebrew נִדְּחוּ "had been driven" (nifal of נדח, "be thrust, driven", the root of the "be drawn away" row; here of being driven out); הַרְבֵּה מְאֹד "very much".
- **"Dost thou certainly know that Baalis the king of the Ammonites hath sent Ishmael … to slay thee? But Gedaliah … believed them not" → "Do you surely know that Baalis king of the sons of Ammon has sent Ishmael … to strike down your soul? And Gedaliah … did not believe them"** (v14) — Hebrew הֲיָדֹעַ תֵּדַע, literally "knowing do you know"; לְהַכֹּתְךָ נָפֶשׁ, literally "to strike you, soul" (= to kill you; the הִכָּה … נֶפֶשׁ row, also v15); הֶאֱמִין לְ "believe".
- **"Then Johanan … spake to Gedaliah in Mizpah secretly, saying, Let me go, I pray thee, and I will slay Ishmael … wherefore should he slay thee, that all the Jews which are gathered unto thee should be scattered" → "And Johanan … said to Gedaliah in secret at the Mizpah, saying, Let me go, please, and strike Ishmael … Why should he strike down your soul, and all Judah who are gathered to you be scattered"** (v15) — Hebrew אָמַר "said" with לֵאמֹר; בַסֵּתֶר "in secret"; אֵלְכָה נָּא וְאַכֶּה, cohortatives, "let me go, please, and strike" (הִכָּה; of killing); וְאִישׁ לֹא יֵדָע "and no man shall know"; כָּל־יְהוּדָה "all Judah" (not "the Jews"); נָפוֹץ "be scattered"; קָבַץ nifal "be gathered"; אָבַד "perish".
- **"Thou shalt not do this thing: for thou speakest falsely of Ishmael" → "Do not do this thing, for falsehood you are speaking about Ishmael"** (v16) — the reading (qere) is תַּעֲשֵׂה "do", the written text (ketiv) תעש: both forbid ("do not do"); שֶׁקֶר "falsehood", in the Hebrew order; אֶל "about".
