# Jeremiah 23

[1] Woe to shepherds who do away with and scatter the flock of my pasture! declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, concerning the shepherds who shepherd my people: You have scattered my flock and driven them away, and you have not visited them. Behold, I am visiting upon you the evil of your doings, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] And I myself will gather the remnant of my flock out of all the lands where I have driven them, and I will bring them back to their habitation, and they shall be fruitful and multiply.

[4] And I will raise up over them shepherds, and they shall shepherd them; and they shall not fear again, and they shall not be dismayed, and they shall not be missing, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] Behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and I will raise up for David a righteous sprout,
and he shall reign as king and act with insight,
and do judgment and righteousness in the land.

[6] In his days ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall be saved,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall abide in security;
and this is his name, which he shall call him:
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ Our Righteousness.

[7] Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they shall no longer say, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, who brought up the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,"

[8] but, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, who brought up and who brought in the seed of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the land of the north and from all the lands where I had driven them"; and they shall dwell on their own ground.

[9] Concerning the prophets:
My heart is broken within me;
all my bones tremble;
I am like a drunken man,
and like a mighty man whom wine has passed over,
because of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and because of his holy words.

[10] For the land is full of adulterers;
for because of the curse the land has mourned;
the pastures of the wilderness are dried out;
and their running has been evil,
and their might is not right.

[11] For both prophet and priest are godless;
even in my house I have found their evil,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] Therefore their way shall be to them
like slippery places in the gloom;
they shall be thrust down and fall in it;
for I will bring evil upon them,
the year of their visitation, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And in the prophets of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧
I have seen unseemliness:
they prophesied by Baal
and caused my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to wander.

[14] And in the prophets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
I have seen a horrible thing:
committing adultery and walking in falsehood;
and they make strong the hands of evildoers,
so that they have not turned back, each man from his evil;
they have all become to me like ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧,
and her inhabitants like ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧.

[15] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts concerning the prophets:

Behold, I am feeding them wormwood,
and I will make them drink water of poison;
for from the prophets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
godlessness has gone out to all the land.

[16] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you;
they are making you vain;
a vision of their own heart they speak,
not from the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] They keep saying to those who spurn me,
"⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken: ⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧ shall be yours";
and to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his heart they have said,
"Evil shall not come upon you."

[18] For who has stood in the secret counsel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and seen and heard his word?
Who has given heed to his word and heard?

[19] Behold, the storm of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
Fury has gone out,
and a whirling storm;
upon the head of the wicked it shall whirl.

[20] The anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall not turn back
until he has done and until he has established the devisings of his heart;
in the latter days
you shall give heed to it with understanding.

[21] I did not send the prophets,
but they ran;
I did not speak to them,
but they prophesied.

[22] And if they had stood in my secret counsel,
then they would have made my people hear my words,
and they would have turned them back from their evil way
and from the evil of their doings.

[23] Am I ⟦elohim|an Elohim|a God⟧ from near, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and not ⟦elohim|an Elohim|a God⟧ from afar?

[24] Can a man hide himself in hiding places,
and I not see him? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
Do I not fill the heavens and the earth? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[25] I have heard what the prophets have said who prophesy falsehood in my name, saying, "I have dreamed, I have dreamed!"

[26] How long? Is it in the heart of the prophets who prophesy falsehood, and the prophets of the deceit of their heart,

[27] who plan to make my people forget my name by their dreams that they recount, each man to his neighbor, as their fathers forgot my name for Baal?

[28] The prophet with whom there is a dream, let him recount a dream; and he with whom my word is, let him speak my word in truth. What has the straw to do with the grain? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[29] Is not my word thus, like fire, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and like a hammer that shatters a crag?

[30] Therefore, behold, I am against the prophets, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who steal my words, each man from his neighbor.

[31] Behold, I am against the prophets, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who take their tongue and declare a declaration.

[32] Behold, I am against those who prophesy dreams of falsehood, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and recount them, and cause my people to wander by their falsehoods and by their recklessness; and I did not send them, and I did not command them, and they shall surely not profit this people, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[33] And when this people, or the prophet or a priest, asks you, saying, "What is the burden of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?" then you shall say to them, "What burden! And I will abandon you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[34] And the prophet and the priest and the people who says, "The burden of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧" — I will visit upon that man and upon his house.

[35] Thus you shall say, each man to his neighbor and each man to his brother: "What has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered?" and, "What has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoken?"

[36] But the burden of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ you shall not remember again; for the burden shall be to each man his own word, and you have overturned the words of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[37] Thus you shall say to the prophet: "What has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered you?" and, "What has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoken?"

[38] But if you say, "The burden of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧," therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Because you have said this word, "The burden of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧," and I sent to you, saying, "You shall not say, 'The burden of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,'"

[39] therefore, behold, I will surely forget you, and I will abandon you and the city that I gave to you and to your fathers, away from my face.

[40] And I will put upon you an everlasting reproach and an everlasting dishonor, which shall not be forgotten.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v15, 16, 36). **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 23, 36). נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v1–5, 7, 11, 12, 23, 24, 28–33).
- **Layout** — v1–4, 7–8, 25–40 are prose; v5–6, v9–24 (v15 and v16 after the opening words) are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order; v20 is laid out anew by the accents ("until he has done and until he has established the purposes of his heart" is one line, "in the latter days" another). Quotation marks are kept only for words spoken inside the oracle.
- **"Woe be unto the pastors that destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture!" → "Woe to shepherds who do away with and scatter the flock of my pasture!"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹי רֹעִים, "shepherds" without the article; הוֹי "Woe" (the prophets' formula row); מְאַבְּדִים, piel of אָבַד, "do away with" (its row); מְפִצִים "scatter" (the הֵפִיץ row, also v2); צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats; also v2, 3); מַרְעִית "pasture".
- **"against the pastors that feed my people" → "concerning the shepherds who shepherd my people"** (v2) — עַל־הָרֹעִים הָרֹעִים, noun and participle of one root (also v4 "shepherds … shall shepherd them"). **"and have not visited them: behold, I will visit upon you the evil of your doings" → "and you have not visited them. Behold, I am visiting upon you the evil of your doings"** — פָּקַד twice, the play kept: they did not attend to the flock; Yahweh will call them to account (the פָּקַד "visit" row). פֹקֵד, a participle; הִדִּיחַ "drive away" (also v3, 8).
- **"And I will gather the remnant" → "And I myself will gather"** (v3) — וַאֲנִי, stressed; קִבֵּץ "gather" (אָסַף is "gather" too: English has one word). **"out of all countries" → "from all the lands"** — הָאֲרָצוֹת. **"to their folds" → "to their habitation"** — נָוֶה "habitation" (pasture, abode: its row), singular. "Them" is feminine (the flock).
- **"and they shall fear no more, nor be dismayed, neither shall they be lacking" → "and they shall not fear again, and they shall not be dismayed, and they shall not be missing"** (v4) — three clauses, each וְלֹא; חָתַת "be dismayed" (its row); יִפָּקֵדוּ, the nifal of פָּקַד, "be missed" — the same verb as v2, a third sense.
- **"I will raise unto David a righteous Branch" → "and I will raise up for David a righteous sprout"** (v5) — Hebrew צֶמַח צַדִּיק, "a righteous sprout": the צֶמַח row (Isa 4:2), plain, no capital; "Branch" as a title (Zech 3:8; 6:12) is the reading of the tradition. The verse opens a weqatal "and I will raise up" after "days are coming". **"and a King shall reign and prosper" → "and he shall reign as king and act with insight"** — וּמָלַךְ מֶלֶךְ, verb and noun of one root ("a king shall reign"); הִשְׂכִּיל "act with insight" (its row; "prosper" is the other sense). **"execute judgment and justice" → "do judgment and righteousness"** — עָשָׂה מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה (as 22:3, 15).
- **"Israel shall dwell safely" → "Israel shall abide in security"** (v6) — יִשְׁכֹּן "abide" (the שָׁכַן row); לָבֶטַח "in security". **"and this is his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS" → "and this is his name, which he shall call him: Yahweh Our Righteousness"** — Hebrew וְזֶה־שְּׁמוֹ אֲשֶׁר־יִקְרְאוֹ, an active verb with no named subject, "which he (one) shall call him". The name is יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ, *Yahweh Tzidqenu*, "Yahweh (is) our righteousness": a play on the name of the king then reigning, צִדְקִיָּהוּ *Tzidqiyahu*, "my righteousness is Yahweh". The accents set a divider (paseq) between the two words; the medieval reading "this is his name which Yahweh shall call him: Our Righteousness" is named here, not followed. Jer 33:16 gives the same name to Jerusalem.
- **Verses 7–8** — the same words as Jer 16:14–15, with small differences in the Hebrew (here "who brought up and who brought in the seed of the house of Israel", "where I had driven them", "and they shall dwell on their own ground"). The Greek places them after v40 (from memory).
- **"Therefore, behold, the days come … that they shall no more say" → "Therefore, behold, days are coming … and they shall no longer say"** (v7) — יָמִים without the article (also v5); וְלֹא־יֹאמְרוּ, a weqatal "and". **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** — בְּנֵי (standing decision).
- **"which brought up and which led the seed of the house of Israel out of the north country" → "who brought up and who brought in the seed … from the land of the north"** (v8) — הֶעֱלָה וַאֲשֶׁר הֵבִיא; זֶרַע "seed"; אֶרֶץ צָפוֹנָה "the land of the north". **"whither I had driven them" kept** — Hebrew הִדַּחְתִּים "I had driven them": the first person, though the sentence quotes what the people will say (16:15 has "he had driven them"). **"they shall dwell in their own land" → "they shall dwell on their own ground"** — עַל־אַדְמָתָם (אֲדָמָה "ground").
- **"Mine heart within me is broken because of the prophets" → "Concerning the prophets: My heart is broken within me"** (v9) — לַנְּבִאִים is a heading for v9–40, as 21:11 (also Jer 46:2; 48:1). **"all my bones shake" → "all my bones tremble"** — רָחֲפוּ, the qal of רָחַף (only here; the piel is "hover", Gen 1:2; Deut 32:11): the sense "tremble, grow weak" is uncertain. **"like a man whom wine hath overcome" → "and like a mighty man whom wine has passed over"** — גֶּבֶר beside אִישׁ "man" (the standing decision); עֲבָרוֹ יָיִן "wine has passed over him" (עָבַר "pass over"). **"because of the LORD, and because of the words of his holiness" → "because of Yahweh and because of his holy words"** — מִפְּנֵי "because of" (its row); דִּבְרֵי קָדְשׁוֹ, "the words of his holiness" (the קֹדֶשׁ genitive row).
- **"the land mourneth … the pleasant places of the wilderness are dried up" → "the land has mourned; the pastures of the wilderness are dried out"** (v10) — אָבְלָה "has mourned" (perfect; אָבַל "mourn"); מִפְּנֵי אָלָה "because of the curse" (the אָלָה row); נְאוֹת "pastures" (its row); יָבְשׁוּ "are dried out" (the יָבֵשׁ row). **"and their course is evil, and their force is not right" → "and their running has been evil, and their might is not right"** — וַתְּהִי, a wayyiqtol; מְרוּצָה "running" (as 2 Sam 18:27); גְּבוּרָה "might"; לֹא־כֵן, literally "not so".
- **"For both prophet and priest are profane" → "For both prophet and priest are godless"** (v11) — חָנֵפוּ, the verb of חָנֵף "godless" (its row; literally "polluted"); also v15 חֲנֻפָּה "godlessness". **"their wickedness" → "their evil"** — רָעָה (also v14, 17, 22).
- **"as slippery ways in the darkness: they shall be driven on, and fall therein" → "like slippery places in the gloom; they shall be thrust down and fall in it"** (v12) — חֲלַקְלַקּוֹת "slippery places"; אֲפֵלָה "gloom" (its row); יִדַּחוּ from דָּחָה "thrust, push down" (not the נָדַח of v2, 3, 8). **"I will bring evil upon them, even the year of their visitation" → "I will bring evil upon them, the year of their visitation"** — שְׁנַת פְּקֻדָּתָם (the פְּקֻדָּה "visitation" row).
- **"And I have seen folly in the prophets of Samaria" → "And in the prophets of Samaria I have seen unseemliness"** (v13) — the Hebrew order; תִּפְלָה "unseemliness" (as Job 1:22). **"they prophesied in Baal" → "they prophesied by Baal"** — בַבַּעַל, with the article ("the Baal"; also v27). **"and caused my people Israel to err" → "and caused my people Israel to wander"** — הִתְעָה "cause to wander" (its row; also v32).
- **"an horrible thing: they commit adultery, and walk in lies" → "a horrible thing: committing adultery and walking in falsehood"** (v14) — שַׁעֲרוּרָה "a horrible thing" (Jer 5:30; 18:13); נָאוֹף וְהָלֹךְ, two infinitives absolute; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (its row; also v25, 26, 32). **"they strengthen also the hands of evildoers, that none doth return from his wickedness" → "and they make strong the hands of evildoers, so that they have not turned back, each man from his evil"** — חִזֵּק "make strong" (its row); לְבִלְתִּי־שָׁבוּ, a perfect: "so as not to have turned back"; אִישׁ "each man". **"they are all of them unto me as Sodom, and the inhabitants thereof as Gomorrah" → "they have all become to me like Sodom, and her inhabitants like Gomorrah"** — הָיוּ "have become"; יֹשְׁבֶיהָ "her inhabitants": Jerusalem's.
- **"I will feed them with wormwood, and make them drink the water of gall" → "Behold, I am feeding them wormwood, and I will make them drink water of poison"** (v15) — הִנְנִי מַאֲכִיל, a participle; לַעֲנָה "wormwood", מֵי־רֹאשׁ "water of poison" (the רֹאשׁ וְלַעֲנָה row; as Jer 9:15). **"profaneness gone forth into all the land" → "godlessness has gone out to all the land"** — חֲנֻפָּה.
- **"Hearken not unto the words of the prophets that prophesy unto you: they make you vain" → "Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you; they are making you vain"** (v16) — שָׁמַע עַל "listen to"; מַהְבִּלִים, a hifil participle of the root of הֶבֶל "vapor": "they make you vapor", they fill you with empty things (as 2 Kgs 17:15; Jer 2:5). **"they speak a vision of their own heart" → "a vision of their own heart they speak"** — the Hebrew order; לֵב "heart".
- **"They say still unto them that despise me, The LORD hath said, Ye shall have peace" → "They keep saying to those who spurn me, 'Yahweh has spoken: Shalom shall be yours'"** (v17) — Hebrew אֹמְרִים אָמוֹר, a participle with the infinitive absolute: they say it again and again; לִמְנַאֲצַי, "to those who spurn me" (נִאֵץ "spurn": its row), as the MT is pointed. The Greek reads "to those who spurn the word of Yahweh" (dividing the letters as לִמְנַאֲצֵי דְבַר יְהוָה: from memory); the MT is kept. דִּבֶּר "has spoken"; שָׁלוֹם token. **"and they say unto every one that walketh after the imagination of his own heart" → "and to everyone who walks in the stubbornness of his heart they have said"** — שְׁרִרוּת לֵב "the stubbornness of the heart" (its row); אָמְרוּ, a perfect. **"No evil shall come upon you" → "Evil shall not come upon you"** — לֹא־תָבוֹא … רָעָה.
- **"For who hath stood in the counsel of the LORD, and hath perceived and heard his word? who hath marked his word, and heard it?" → "For who has stood in the secret counsel of Yahweh and seen and heard his word? Who has given heed to his word and heard?"** (v18) — סוֹד "secret counsel" (its row: the close circle and what is said in it, "council"; also v22); וַיֵּרֶא "and seen"; הִקְשִׁיב "give heed" (its row). The written text (ketiv) has דברי "my word", the reading (qere) דְּבָרוֹ "his word": followed.
- **"Behold, a whirlwind of the LORD is gone forth in fury, even a grievous whirlwind: it shall fall grievously upon the head of the wicked" → "Behold, the storm of Yahweh! Fury has gone out, and a whirling storm; upon the head of the wicked it shall whirl"** (v19) — Hebrew סַעֲרַת יְהוָה "the storm of Yahweh", then חֵמָה יָצְאָה "fury has gone out" as its own clause; סְעָרָה and סַעַר are both "storm" (their rows; English has one word); מִתְחוֹלֵל "whirling" and יָחוּל "it shall whirl" (the חוּל עַל רֹאשׁ row: one verb twice).
- **"The anger of the LORD shall not return, until he have executed, and till he have performed the thoughts of his heart" → "The anger of Yahweh shall not turn back until he has done and until he has established the purposes of his heart"** (v20) — אַף "anger"; עֲשֹׂתוֹ "his doing", הֲקִימוֹ "his establishing" (הֵקִים, literally "make stand"); מְזִמּוֹת "devisings" (the Proverbs row, good or bad by context; reviewer row 2026-10-16: not "schemes", here Yahweh's own plan) (as Jer 30:24; 51:11). **"in the latter days ye shall consider it perfectly" → "in the latter days you shall give heed to it with understanding"** — בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים (its row); תִּתְבּוֹנְנוּ בָהּ בִּינָה, the verb with its own noun (the הִתְבּוֹנֵן row). Jer 30:24 repeats v19–20 with small differences.
- **"yet they ran … yet they prophesied" → "but they ran … but they prophesied"** (v21) — וְהֵם, "and they": the pronoun marks the contrast.
- **"But if they had stood in my counsel, and had caused my people to hear my words, then they should have turned them" → "And if they had stood in my secret counsel, then they would have made my people hear my words, and they would have turned them back"** (v22) — the Hebrew has the condition in one clause (וְאִם־עָמְדוּ), and the two results joined by וְ; הִשְׁמִיעַ "make hear"; הֵשִׁיב "turn back"; מַעֲלָלִים "doings" (also v2).
- **"Am I a God at hand … and not a God afar off?" → "Am I an Elohim from near … and not an Elohim from afar?"** (v23) — מִקָּרֹב · מֵרָחֹק "from near · from afar". The Greek reads this as a statement ("I am a God from near, and not a God from afar": from memory).
- **"Can any hide himself in secret places that I shall not see him?" → "Can a man hide himself in hiding places, and I not see him?"** (v24) — אִם־יִסָּתֵר אִישׁ, a question with אִם; וַאֲנִי "and I"; מִסְתָּרִים "hiding places". **"Do not I fill heaven and earth?" → "Do I not fill the heavens and the earth?"** — אֶת־הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֶת־הָאָרֶץ (the שָׁמַיִם row).
- **"that prophesy lies in my name" → "who prophesy falsehood in my name"** (v25) — שֶׁקֶר "falsehood". **"I have dreamed, I have dreamed"** — חָלַמְתִּי twice, kept.
- **"How long shall this be in the heart of the prophets that prophesy lies? yea, they are prophets of the deceit of their own heart" → "How long? Is it in the heart of the prophets who prophesy falsehood, and the prophets of the deceit of their heart,"** (v26) — Hebrew עַד־מָתַי הֲיֵשׁ בְּלֵב הַנְּבִאִים, word for word "Until when? Is there in the heart of the prophets …": what "is there" is not named, and the sentence runs on into v27; the sense is uncertain ("How long? Is there anything in the heart …"). תַּרְמִת "deceit" (Jer 8:5; 14:14).
- **"Which think to cause my people to forget my name by their dreams which they tell every man to his neighbour, as their fathers have forgotten my name for Baal" → "who plan to make my people forget my name by their dreams that they recount, each man to his neighbor, as their fathers forgot my name for Baal?"** (v27) — הַחֹשְׁבִים "who plan"; הִשְׁכִּיחַ "make forget"; סִפֵּר "recount" (its row; also v28, 32); בַּבָּעַל "for the Baal" (or "through the Baal": the בְּ allows both).
- **"The prophet that hath a dream, let him tell a dream; and he that hath my word, let him speak my word faithfully" → "The prophet with whom there is a dream, let him recount a dream; and he with whom my word is, let him speak my word in truth"** (v28) — אֲשֶׁר־אִתּוֹ, "with whom"; אֱמֶת "truth" (its row). **"What is the chaff to the wheat?" → "What has the straw to do with the grain?"** — מַה־לַתֶּבֶן אֶת־הַבָּר, literally "what to the straw with the grain?"; תֶּבֶן "straw", בַּר "grain" (their rows).
- **"Is not my word like as a fire? … and like a hammer that breaketh the rock in pieces?" → "Is not my word thus, like fire … and like a hammer that shatters a crag?"** (v29) — כֹה "thus"; פַּטִּישׁ "hammer"; יְפֹצֵץ "shatters"; סֶלַע "crag" (its row), without the article.
- **"that steal my words every one from his neighbour" → "who steal my words, each man from his neighbor"** (v30) — the אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ row.
- **"that use their tongues, and say, He saith" → "who take their tongue and declare a declaration"** (v31) — Hebrew הַלֹּקְחִים לְשׁוֹנָם וַיִּנְאֲמוּ נְאֻם: לָקַח "take"; וַיִּנְאֲמוּ, a verb made from נְאֻם (only here), with נְאֻם itself as its object: they use the prophets' formula "declares Yahweh" of their own words.
- **"that prophesy false dreams … and cause my people to err by their lies, and by their lightness" → "who prophesy dreams of falsehood … and cause my people to wander by their falsehoods and by their recklessness"** (v32) — חֲלֹמוֹת שֶׁקֶר "dreams of falsehood"; פַּחֲזוּת "recklessness" (only here; root of פַּחַז "reckless", Gen 49:4). **"yet I sent them not, nor commanded them: therefore they shall not profit this people at all" → "and I did not send them, and I did not command them, and they shall surely not profit this people"** — each clause with וְ; וְהוֹעֵיל לֹא־יוֹעִילוּ, the infinitive absolute.
- **"What is the burden of the LORD? thou shalt then say unto them, What burden? I will even forsake you" → "'What is the burden of Yahweh?' then you shall say to them, 'What burden! And I will abandon you'"** (v33) — מַשָּׂא "burden" (the prophets' formula row: "what is lifted up"; usually rendered "oracle"): the word is used throughout v33–40 as a play on its two senses, a prophet's word and a load to carry. Hebrew אֶת־מַה־מַשָּׂא, as the MT is divided; the Greek and the Latin read אַתֶּם הַמַּשָּׂא "You are the burden" (the same letters divided otherwise: from memory); the MT is kept. נָטַשׁ "abandon" (its row; also v39).
- **"I will even punish that man and his house" → "I will visit upon that man and upon his house"** (v34) — פָּקַד עַל (as v2). The verse begins with three subjects and turns ("— I will visit"), as the Hebrew does.
- **"every one to his neighbour, and every one to his brother" → "each man to his neighbor and each man to his brother"** (v35). **"What hath the LORD answered? and, What hath the LORD spoken?"** — kept (also v37).
- **"And the burden of the LORD shall ye mention no more" → "But the burden of Yahweh you shall not remember again"** (v36) — לֹא תִזְכְּרוּ־עוֹד, the qal of זָכַר "remember" ("mention" is the hifil; the sense here is "speak of"). **"for every man's word shall be his burden" → "for the burden shall be to each man his own word"** — the Hebrew order: what each man says becomes his load. **"for ye have perverted the words of the living God" → "and you have overturned the words of the living Elohim"** — וַהֲפַכְתֶּם, the הָפַךְ "overturn" row; אֱלֹהִים חַיִּים, with a plural adjective (the plural named here, as the row for אֱלֹהִים with a plural word).
- **"and I have sent unto you" → "and I sent to you"** (v38) — וָאֶשְׁלַח, a wayyiqtol. **"Because ye say this word" → "Because you have said this word"** — יַעַן אֲמָרְכֶם, "because of your saying".
- **"I, even I, will utterly forget you, and I will forsake you" → "behold, I will surely forget you, and I will abandon you"** (v39) — Hebrew הִנְנִי וְנָשִׁיתִי אֶתְכֶם נָשֹׁא, as pointed: נָשָׁה "forget" with an infinitive absolute (spelled with aleph). Some Hebrew manuscripts and the Greek, the Syriac and the Latin read "I will surely lift you up" (נָשָׂא, the root of מַשָּׂא: from memory); the MT is kept, and the play on "burden" is heard either way. **"from my presence" → "away from my face"** — מֵעַל פָּנָי, literally "from upon my face".
- **"an everlasting reproach … and a perpetual shame" → "an everlasting reproach and an everlasting dishonor"** (v40) — עוֹלָם twice (the "everlasting" row; literally "of the age"); חֶרְפָּה "reproach", כְּלִמּוּת "dishonor" (only here, a form of כְּלִמָּה: the reviewer's row). נָתַן "put", literally "give".
