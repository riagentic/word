## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv15-16, 36) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 23, 36) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved; "Am I an *Elohim* at hand?"
- **"THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS" → "Yahweh Our Righteousness"** (v6) — Hebrew יְהוָה צִדְקֵנוּ *Yahweh Tzidkenu*; divine messianic name; wordplay with *Tzidqiyahu* (Zedekiah = "Yahweh is my righteousness").
- **Poetic structure** — vv5-6 verse messianic oracle; vv9-14 verse; vv16-24 extended verse oracle against false prophets; prose sections preserved.

### Notes
- **v5-6** — "Branch (*Tzemach*)" messianic title; name "**Yahweh Tzidkenu**" applied to Zedekiah's failure (Zedekiah = "Yahweh is my righteousness") and prophetic messianic anti-type.
- **v33-40 wordplay** — *massa* = both "oracle" and "burden"; people use it derisively.
