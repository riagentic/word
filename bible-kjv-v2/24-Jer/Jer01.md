# Jeremiah 1

[1] The words of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧, of the priests who were in ⟦anathoth|Anatot|Anathoth⟧ in the land of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧,

[2] to whom the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came in the days of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ son of Amon, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the thirteenth year of his reign.

[3] And it came in the days of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, until the end of the eleventh year of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, until the exile of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ in the fifth month.

[4] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[5] "Before I formed you in the belly I knew you,
and before you came out from the womb I consecrated you;
a prophet to the nations I have given you."

[6] And I said, "Alas, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧! Behold, I do not know how to speak, for I am a boy."

[7] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me,

"Do not say, 'I am a boy';
for to all to whom I send you, you shall go,
and all that I command you, you shall speak.

[8] Do not be afraid of them,
for I am with you to deliver you,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ put out his hand and made it touch my mouth. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me,

"Behold, I have put my words in your mouth.

[10] See, I have appointed you this day
over the nations and over the kingdoms,
to pluck up and to break down,
and to make perish and to throw down,
to build and to plant."

[11] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying, "What do you see, ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧?" And I said, "I see a rod of almond."

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "You have done well in seeing, for I am keeping awake over my word to do it."

[13] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me a second time, saying, "What do you see?" And I said, "I see a blown-upon pot, and its face is from the face of the north."

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "From the north the evil shall be opened upon all who dwell in the land.

[15] For behold, I am calling to all the clans of the kingdoms of the north, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they shall come and set each one his throne at the entrance of the gates of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and against all her walls all around, and against all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[16] And I will speak my judgments with them for all their evil, in that they have forsaken me and made smoke to other gods and bowed down to the works of their hands.

[17] And you, you shall gird your loins, and arise, and speak to them all that I, I command you. Do not be dismayed because of them, lest I dismay you before them.

[18] And I, behold, I have made you today a fortified city and a pillar of iron and walls of bronze against all the land: to the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, to its princes, to its priests, and to the people of the land.

[19] And they shall fight against you, and they shall not overcome you, for I am with you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to deliver you."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names (token `adonai-yhwh`).
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose in the Hebrew with poetic lines in v5, 7–8, 9b–10; the existing line layout follows the accents and is kept.
- **"The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests that were in Anathoth" → "The words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, of the priests who were in Anathoth"** (v1) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי יִרְמְיָהוּ … מִן־הַכֹּהֲנִים, literally "from the priests". The heading of the book (v1–3) runs as one sentence.
- **"It came also in the days of Jehoiakim … unto the carrying away of Jerusalem captive" → "And it came in the days of Jehoiakim … until the exile of Jerusalem"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיְהִי "and it was": the word of v2 is the subject; "also" is not written. עַד־תֹּם "until the completion" (the תַּם row); גְּלוֹת, infinitive of גָּלָה "go into exile".
- **"Then the word of the LORD came unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר, the prophets' formula, one wording (also v11, 13).
- **"I formed thee in the belly … before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet" → "I formed you in the belly … before you came out from the womb I consecrated you; a prophet to the nations I have given you"** (v5) — Hebrew בַבֶּטֶן "in the belly" (the בֶּטֶן row) and מֵרֶחֶם "from the womb": two words; הִקְדַּשְׁתִּיךָ, hifil of קדשׁ, "I consecrated you" (set you apart as holy); נְתַתִּיךָ "I have given you" (נָתַן; "appointed" is the sense), in the Hebrew order, with no "and". אֶצָּרְךָ is the qere (the written form spells the same verb with ו).
- **"Then said I, Ah, Lord GOD! behold, I cannot speak: for I am a child" → "And I said, Alas, Lord Yahweh! Behold, I do not know how to speak, for I am a boy"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew וָאֹמַר "And I said"; אֲהָהּ "Alas" (as Judg 6:22); לֹא־יָדַעְתִּי דַּבֵּר, literally "I do not know to speak": "how" is supplied for English; נַעַר "boy" (the נַעַר row).
- **"But the LORD said … thou shalt go to all that I shall send thee, and whatsoever I command thee" → "And Yahweh said … to all to whom I send you, you shall go, and all that I command you"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר "And … said"; עַל־כָּל־אֲשֶׁר אֶשְׁלָחֲךָ, in the Hebrew order; וְאֵת כָּל־אֲשֶׁר "and all that".
- **"Be not afraid of their faces" → "Do not be afraid of them"** (v8) — Hebrew אַל־תִּירָא מִפְּנֵיהֶם: יָרֵא מִפְּנֵי is the plain "be afraid of" (as 2 Kgs 1:15), literally "from before their face".
- **"Then the LORD put forth his hand, and touched my mouth" → "And Yahweh put out his hand and made it touch my mouth"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיַּגַּע עַל־פִּי, hifil of נָגַע (as Isa 6:7).
- **"I have this day set thee over the nations … to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to throw down" → "I have appointed you this day over the nations … to pluck up and to break down, and to make perish and to throw down"** (v10) — Hebrew הִפְקַדְתִּיךָ עַל "I have appointed you over" (the הִפְקִיד עַל row). Six infinitives: נָתַשׁ "pluck up" (its row), נָתַץ "break down" (the reviewer row), הֶאֱבִיד "make perish" (hifil of אָבַד, its row), הָרַס "throw down", בָּנָה "build", נָטַע "plant".
- **"Jeremiah, what seest thou? … I see a rod of an almond tree" → "What do you see, Jeremiah? … I see a rod of almond"** (v11) — the name stands last in the Hebrew; מַקֵּל "rod" (a cut branch: the מַקֵּל row), שָׁקֵד "almond".
- **"Thou hast well seen: for I will hasten my word to perform it" → "You have done well in seeing, for I am keeping awake over my word to do it"** (v12) — Hebrew הֵיטַבְתָּ לִרְאוֹת, literally "you have done well to see"; שֹׁקֵד, participle of שָׁקַד "keep awake" (the שָׁקַד row: watching without sleep, not "hasten"); לַעֲשֹׂתוֹ "to do it". The vision stands on a play of sound: שָׁקֵד *shaqed* "almond" (the "waking" tree, the first to blossom at the end of winter) and שֹׁקֵד *shoqed* "keeping awake". English cannot carry it; the verse gives each word its meaning.
- **"I see a seething pot; and the face thereof is toward the north" → "I see a blown-upon pot, and its face is from the face of the north"** (v13) — Hebrew סִיר נָפוּחַ, passive participle of נָפַח "blow": a pot whose fire is blown on, so boiling. וּפָנָיו מִפְּנֵי צָפוֹנָה, literally "and its face from the face of northward": the pot's mouth is turned from the north side, tilting toward the south, toward the land. Uncertain; kept as written.
- **"Out of the north an evil shall break forth upon all the inhabitants of the land" → "From the north the evil shall be opened upon all who dwell in the land"** (v14) — Hebrew תִּפָּתַח, nifal of פָּתַח "be opened"; הָרָעָה "the evil", with the article; יֹשְׁבֵי, participle of יָשַׁב "dwell". Yahweh's speech runs from v14 to v19.
- **"I will call all the families of the kingdoms … they shall set every one his throne at the entering of the gates" → "I am calling to all the clans of the kingdoms … set each one his throne at the entrance of the gates"** (v15) — Hebrew הִנְנִי קֹרֵא לְ, a participle, "I am calling to"; מִשְׁפְּחוֹת "clans" (the מִשְׁפָּחָה row); וְנָתְנוּ אִישׁ כִּסְאוֹ "and they shall set (lit. "give") each one his throne"; פֶּתַח "entrance" (its row); חוֹמֹתֶיהָ "her walls": Jerusalem is feminine.
- **"I will utter my judgments against them touching all their wickedness, who have … burned incense unto other gods, and worshipped the works of their own hands" → "I will speak my judgments with them for all their evil, in that they have … made smoke to other gods and bowed down to the works of their hands"** (v16) — Hebrew וְדִבַּרְתִּי מִשְׁפָּטַי אוֹתָם: דִּבֶּר "speak"; אוֹתָם here = אִתָּם "with them" (Jeremiah writes "with" so; to speak judgments with someone is to pass sentence on him); רָעָה "evil"; אֲשֶׁר עֲזָבוּנִי "in that they have forsaken me" (or "who have forsaken me"); וַיְקַטְּרוּ, piel of קטר without an object: "made smoke" (the reviewer row; "burned incense" decides for incense); הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" (its row); "own" is not written.
- **"Thou therefore gird up thy loins … be not dismayed at their faces, lest I confound thee before them" → "And you, you shall gird your loins … Do not be dismayed because of them, lest I dismay you before them"** (v17) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה תֶּאְזֹר מָתְנֶיךָ, "and you" with an imperfect: "you shall gird"; אָנֹכִי אֲצַוֶּךָּ, the pronoun written ("I, I command you"). אַל־תֵּחַת … פֶּן־אֲחִתְּךָ: חָתַת "be dismayed" (its row), hifil "dismay": one verb; מִפְּנֵיהֶם "because of them", לִפְנֵיהֶם "before them".
- **"I have made thee this day a defenced city, and an iron pillar, and brasen walls against the whole land, against the kings of Judah, against the princes thereof" → "I have made you today a fortified city and a pillar of iron and walls of bronze against all the land: to the kings of Judah, to its princes"** (v18) — Hebrew נְתַתִּיךָ … לְ "I have given you for" = made you; הַיּוֹם "today" (v10 has הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה "this day"); עִיר מִבְצָר (the מִבְצָר row); נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze" (its row). The four persons are each introduced by לְ "to" (they are the land the prophet stands against), not by עַל "against" of the clause before: the two prepositions are kept apart. שַׂר "prince" (its row).
- **"but they shall not prevail against thee" → "and they shall not overcome you"** (v19) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יוּכְלוּ לָךְ: וְ "and"; יָכֹל לְ "overcome" (lit. "be able for": its row).

### Notes
- **v11–12** — the vision of the almond rod is a play of sound, *shaqed* / *shoqed* (see Changes).
- **627 BC** — Jeremiah's call (13th year of Josiah).
