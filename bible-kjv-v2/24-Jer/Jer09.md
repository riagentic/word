# Jeremiah 9

[1] Would that my head were waters,
and my eye a fountain of tears,
and I would weep day and night
for the slain of the daughter of my people!

[2] Would that I had in the wilderness
a lodging place of wayfarers,
and I would forsake my people
and go away from them!
For all of them are adulterers,
a solemn assembly of treacherous ones.

[3] And they bend their tongue, their bow of falsehood,
and not for faithfulness have they prevailed in the land;
for from evil to evil they have gone out,
and me they have not known,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] Take heed, each man, of his neighbor,
and in any brother do not trust;
for every brother surely supplants,
and every neighbor goes about as a talebearer.

[5] And they cheat, each man his neighbor,
and truth they do not speak;
they have taught their tongue to speak falsehood;
committing iniquity they weary themselves.

[6] Your dwelling is in the midst of deceit;
in deceit they have refused to know me,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

"Behold, I am refining them, and I will try them;
for how shall I do, because of the daughter of my people?

[8] A hammered arrow is their tongue;
it has spoken deceit;
with his mouth he speaks ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with his neighbor,
and in his inward part he sets his ambush.

[9] For these things shall I not visit upon them? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
or on a nation that is like this
shall my soul not avenge itself?"

[10] Over the mountains I will lift up weeping and wailing,
and over the pastures of the wilderness a dirge,
for they are laid waste, without a man passing through,
and they have not heard the voice of livestock;
from the birds of the heavens to the beast
they have fled, they have gone.

[11] "And I will make ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ heaps,
a lair of jackals,
and the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ I will make a desolation,
without inhabitant."

[12] Who is the wise man, that he may understand this, and he to whom the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken, that he may tell it? Why has the land perished, laid waste like the wilderness, without one passing through?

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said: "Because they have forsaken my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ which I set before them, and have not listened to my voice and have not walked in it,

[14] and they went after the stubbornness of their heart and after the Baals, which their fathers taught them,

[15] therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I am feeding them, this people, wormwood, and I will make them drink water of poison.

[16] And I will scatter them among the nations that they have not known, they and their fathers; and I will send the sword after them, until I have made an end of them."

[17] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

"Consider, and call for the women who chant dirges, and let them come;
and send to the wise women, and let them come;

[18] and let them hasten and lift up a wailing over us,
and let our eyes run down with tears,
and our eyelids flow with water.

[19] For a voice of wailing is heard from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧:
'How we are laid waste!
We are greatly ashamed,
for we have left the land,
for they have thrown down our dwellings.'"

[20] For hear, women, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and let your ear take the word of his mouth;
and teach your daughters wailing,
and each woman her neighbor a dirge.

[21] For death has come up into our windows;
it has entered our palaces,
to cut off the child from the street,
the young men from the squares.

[22] Speak: Thus declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
"And the dead body of man shall fall
like dung on the face of the field,
and like a sheaf behind the reaper,
and there is none who gathers."

[23] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom,
and let not the mighty one boast in his might;
let not the rich man boast in his riches;

[24] but in this let him who boasts boast:
having insight and knowing me,
that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, doing ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, judgment, and righteousness in the earth;
for in these I delight, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[25] "Behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will visit upon everyone circumcised in the foreskin:

[26] upon ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and upon ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and upon Edom, and upon the sons of Ammon, and upon ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and upon all who are clipped at the edge, who dwell in the wilderness; for all the nations are uncircumcised, and all the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ are uncircumcised of heart."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v7, 15, 17) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15). **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v3, 6, 9, 22, 24, 25) — Hebrew נְאֻם יְהוָה, "the declaration of Yahweh".
- **Layout** — v1–11 and v17–24 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order; v12–16 and v25–26 are prose, as printed.
- **"Oh that my head were waters, and mine eyes a fountain of tears, that I might weep" → "Would that my head were waters, and my eye a fountain of tears, and I would weep"** (v1) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן, literally "who will give" (also v2); עֵינִי "my eye", singular; מְקוֹר "fountain"; וְאֶבְכֶּה "and I would weep", the "and" kept. אֵת חַלְלֵי "the slain" (חָלָל, one pierced).
- **"Oh that I had in the wilderness a lodging place of wayfaring men; that I might leave my people" → "Would that I had in the wilderness a lodging place of wayfarers, and I would forsake my people"** (v2) — Hebrew מִי־יִתְּנֵנִי בַמִּדְבָּר, literally "who will give me in the wilderness" (or "put me in the wilderness, in a lodging place"); מְלוֹן "lodging place"; עָזַב "forsake". **"an assembly of treacherous men" → "a solemn assembly of treacherous ones"** — עֲצֶרֶת (the word of the feast-day assembly; "solemn" is customary English, not a word of the Hebrew); בֹּגְדִים "treacherous ones" ("men" is not in the Hebrew).
- **"they bend their tongues like their bow for lies" → "And they bend their tongue, their bow of falsehood"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיַּדְרְכוּ אֶת־לְשׁוֹנָם קַשְׁתָּם שֶׁקֶר, literally "they tread their tongue, their bow, falsehood" (a bow is bent by treading); no "like". **"but they are not valiant for the truth upon the earth" → "and not for faithfulness have they prevailed in the land"** — לֶאֱמוּנָה "for faithfulness"; גָּבַר "prevail". **"they proceed from evil to evil, and they know not me" → "from evil to evil they have gone out, and me they have not known"** — perfects יָצָאוּ, יָדָעוּ; the object first, as the Hebrew.
- **"Take ye heed every one of his neighbour" → "Take heed, each man, of his neighbor"** (v4) — Hebrew אִישׁ מֵרֵעֵהוּ הִשָּׁמֵרוּ, literally "keep yourselves, a man from his neighbor". **"every brother will utterly supplant" → "every brother surely supplants"** — עָקוֹב יַעְקֹב, infinitive absolute ("supplanting he supplants"): the verb is the root of the name Jacob (Gen 27:36, "he has supplanted me"). **"walk with slanders" → "goes about as a talebearer"** — רָכִיל יַהֲלֹךְ.
- **"they will deceive every one his neighbour" → "they cheat, each man his neighbor"** (v5) — Hebrew יְהָתֵלּוּ, הֵתֵל "cheat, trifle with"; plural verb, "each man" as subject. **"weary themselves to commit iniquity" → "committing iniquity they weary themselves"** — הַעֲוֵה (infinitive, "doing iniquity, acting perversely") נִלְאוּ, the Hebrew order. שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (also v3).
- **"Thine habitation is in the midst of deceit; through deceit they refuse to know me" → "Your dwelling is in the midst of deceit; in deceit they have refused to know me"** (v6) — Hebrew שִׁבְתְּךָ בְּתוֹךְ מִרְמָה בְּמִרְמָה מֵאֲנוּ, "your sitting / dwelling" (masculine singular: the prophet). The earlier wording "Heaping oppression upon oppression, and deceit upon deceit" follows a different division of the same letters (תֹּךְ בְּתֹךְ מִרְמָה בְּמִרְמָה) and the Septuagint (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"I will melt them, and try them" → "I am refining them, and I will try them"** (v7) — Hebrew הִנְנִי צוֹרְפָם, participle, "behold, I am refining them" (צָרַף "refine"), then וּבְחַנְתִּים (בָּחַן "try"). **"for how shall I do for the daughter of my people?" → "for how shall I do, because of the daughter of my people?"** — כִּי־אֵיךְ אֶעֱשֶׂה מִפְּנֵי בַּת־עַמִּי; "else" is not in the Hebrew; "daughter" restored. The reading "because of the evil of the daughter of my people" is of the Septuagint (from memory).
- **"Their tongue is as an arrow shot out" → "A hammered arrow is their tongue"** (v8) — qere חֵץ שָׁחוּט, passive of שָׁחַט: "hammered" as זָהָב שָׁחוּט "beaten gold" (1 Kgs 10:16), so "sharpened" (uncertain); the ketiv שֹׁחֵט "slaughtering" gives "a deadly arrow". **"it speaketh deceit" → "it has spoken deceit"** — perfect. **"one speaketh peaceably to his neighbour with his mouth" → "with his mouth he speaks shalom with his neighbor"** — שָׁלוֹם, the noun (token); "each" is not in the Hebrew. **"but in heart he layeth his wait" → "and in his inward part he sets his ambush"** — בְּקִרְבּוֹ "in his inward part" (קֶרֶב), אָרְבּוֹ "his ambush".
- **"Shall I not visit them for these things?" → "For these things shall I not visit upon them?"** (v9) — Hebrew הַעַל־אֵלֶּה לֹא־אֶפְקָד־בָּם; פָּקַד "visit" (attend to, here for punishment), with בְּ "upon". **"shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?" → "or on a nation that is like this shall my soul not avenge itself?"** — אִם "or" in a double question; נַפְשִׁי "my soul"; הִתְנַקֵּם reflexive.
- **"For the mountains will I take up a weeping" → "Over the mountains I will lift up weeping and wailing"** (v10) — Hebrew אֶשָּׂא, first person ("Take up", an imperative, is the Septuagint's reading, from memory); עַל "over". **"for the habitations of the wilderness a lamentation" → "over the pastures of the wilderness a dirge"** — נְאוֹת, plural of נָוֶה "pasture, abode"; קִינָה "dirge". **"because they are burned up, so that none can pass through them" → "for they are laid waste, without a man passing through"** — נִצְּתוּ (also v12), taken from נָצָה "be laid waste" (or from יָצַת "be burned": uncertain); מִבְּלִי־אִישׁ עֹבֵר "without a man passing". **"neither can men hear the voice of the cattle" → "and they have not heard the voice of livestock"** — שָׁמְעוּ, perfect, no subject named; מִקְנֶה "livestock". **"both the fowl of the heavens and the beast are fled; they are gone" → "from the birds of the heavens to the beast they have fled, they have gone"** — מֵ … וְעַד "from … to".
- **"I will make Jerusalem heaps, and a den of dragons" → "And I will make Jerusalem heaps, a lair of jackals"** (v11) — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי … לְגַלִּים, literally "I will give … for heaps" (also אֶתֵּן "I will give" of the cities); גַּל "heap" (of stones); תַּנִּים "jackals" (not תַּנִּין "dragon"); "and" before "a lair" is not in the Hebrew. **"desolate, without an inhabitant" → "a desolation, without inhabitant"** — שְׁמָמָה, noun.
- **"Who is the wise man, that may understand this? and who is he to whom the mouth of the LORD hath spoken, that he may declare it" → "Who is the wise man, that he may understand this, and he to whom the mouth of Yahweh has spoken, that he may tell it?"** (v12) — Hebrew מִי … וְיָבֵן … וַאֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר … וְיַגִּדָהּ: one "who"; הִגִּיד "tell". **"for what the land perisheth and is burned up like a wilderness" → "Why has the land perished, laid waste like the wilderness, without one passing through?"** — עַל־מָה "on account of what"; אָבְדָה "has perished"; נִצְּתָה as v10.
- **"And the LORD saith" → "And Yahweh said"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר, past. **"which I set before them" → "which I set before them"** — נָתַתִּי לִפְנֵיהֶם, literally "gave before them". **"have not obeyed my voice, neither walked therein" → "have not listened to my voice and have not walked in it"** — שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of"; "in it", the torah.
- **"But have walked after the imagination of their own heart" → "and they went after the stubbornness of their heart"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיֵּלְכוּ אַחֲרֵי שְׁרִרוּת לִבָּם; "stubbornly followed their own hearts" turned the noun into an adverb. **"and after Baalim, which their fathers taught them" → "and after the Baals, which their fathers taught them"** — הַבְּעָלִים with the article; אֲשֶׁר "which", the Baals being what was taught.
- **"I will feed them, even this people, with wormwood, and give them water of gall to drink" → "Behold, I am feeding them, this people, wormwood, and I will make them drink water of poison"** (v15) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מַאֲכִילָם, participle with הִנֵּה; לַעֲנָה "wormwood", מֵי־רֹאשׁ "water of poison" (רֹאשׁ a poisonous plant, not known which); "bitter food" and "poisonous water" named the plants away.
- **"whom neither they nor their fathers have known" → "that they have not known, they and their fathers"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא יָדְעוּ הֵמָּה וַאֲבוֹתָם, the Hebrew order. **"till I have consumed them" → "until I have made an end of them"** — כַּלּוֹתִי, piel of כָּלָה with persons. שִׁלַּח "send" (piel; elsewhere "send away").
- **"call for the mourning women" → "call for the women who chant dirges"** (v17) — Hebrew לַמְקוֹנְנוֹת, participle of קוֹנֵן "chant a dirge" (קִינָה, v10, 20). **"that they may come … that they may come" → "and let them come … and let them come"** — וּתְבוֹאֶינָה, וְתָבוֹאנָה, wishes. **"the cunning women" → "the wise women"** — הַחֲכָמוֹת. הִתְבּוֹנְנוּ "consider" (pay attention).
- **"let them make haste, and take up a wailing for us, that our eyes may run down with tears" → "and let them hasten and lift up a wailing over us, and let our eyes run down with tears"** (v18) — Hebrew וּתְמַהֵרְנָה וְתִשֶּׂנָה … וְתֵרַדְנָה, each with "and"; תֵּרַדְנָה, literally "come down" (יָרַד); נָזַל "flow".
- **"How are we spoiled! we are greatly confounded" → "How we are laid waste! We are greatly ashamed"** (v19) — Hebrew שֻׁדָּדְנוּ (שָׁדַד "lay waste"), בֹּשְׁנוּ (בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"). **"because we have forsaken the land" → "for we have left the land"** — עָזַב of a place. **"because our dwellings have cast us out" → "for they have thrown down our dwellings"** — כִּי הִשְׁלִיכוּ מִשְׁכְּנוֹתֵינוּ: הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; "they" (unnamed) the subject and the dwellings the object, or "our dwellings have thrown (us) out" (KJV): the Hebrew allows both.
- **"Yet hear the word of the LORD, O ye women" → "For hear, women, the word of Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew כִּי־שְׁמַעְנָה נָשִׁים, כִּי "for" kept; no "O". **"let your ear receive" → "let your ear take"** — לָקַח "take". **"teach your daughters wailing, and every one her neighbour lamentation" → "teach your daughters wailing, and each woman her neighbor a dirge"** — נְהִי "wailing" (v10, 18, 19), אִשָּׁה רְעוּתָהּ "a woman her neighbor", קִינָה "dirge".
- **"to cut off the children from without, and the young men from the streets" → "to cut off the child from the street, the young men from the squares"** (v21) — Hebrew לְהַכְרִית עוֹלָל מִחוּץ בַּחוּרִים מֵרְחֹבוֹת; עוֹלָל singular; no "and"; רְחֹבוֹת "squares" (open places).
- **"Speak, Thus saith the LORD, Even the carcases of men shall fall" → "Speak: Thus declares Yahweh: And the dead body of man shall fall"** (v22) — Hebrew כֹּה נְאֻם־יְהוָה; וְנָפְלָה נִבְלַת הָאָדָם, singular, "and" kept; נְבֵלָה of a man "dead body"; אָדָם "man, mankind". **"as dung upon the open field, and as the handful after the harvestman, and none shall gather them" → "like dung on the face of the field, and like a sheaf behind the reaper, and there is none who gathers"** — עַל־פְּנֵי הַשָּׂדֶה "on the face of the field"; עָמִיר "sheaf" (what the reaper's hand gathers); וְאֵין מְאַסֵּף "and there is none gathering".
- **"Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, neither let the mighty man glory in his might, let not the rich man glory in his riches" → "Let not the wise man boast in his wisdom, and let not the mighty one boast in his might; let not the rich man boast in his riches"** (v23) — Hebrew הִתְהַלֵּל "boast" (also v24); גִּבּוֹר "mighty one", גְּבוּרָה "might", עֹשֶׁר "riches"; the third clause has no "and", as the Hebrew.
- **"But let him that glorieth glory in this, that he understandeth and knoweth me" → "but in this let him who boasts boast: having insight and knowing me"** (v24) — Hebrew כִּי אִם "but" after the negatives; הַשְׂכֵּל וְיָדֹעַ, two infinitives absolute ("having insight, knowing"), הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight". **"which exercise lovingkindness, judgment, and righteousness" → "doing chesed, judgment, and righteousness"** — עֹשֶׂה חֶסֶד מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה (token); "in the earth" (בָּאָרֶץ). **"for in these things I delight"** — חָפַצְתִּי, perfect of a state. The verse is taken up in 1 Cor 1:31 and 2 Cor 10:17 ("let the one who boasts boast in the Lord", Greek, from memory); the Hebrew is translated as it stands, the difference stays here.
- **"Behold, the days come … that I will punish all them which are circumcised with the uncircumcised" → "Behold, days are coming, declares Yahweh, and I will visit upon everyone circumcised in the foreskin"** (v25) — Hebrew יָמִים בָּאִים (no article), וּפָקַדְתִּי עַל "and I will visit upon" (the "and" kept); כָּל־מוּל בְּעָרְלָה "every one circumcised in foreskin": circumcised and yet uncircumcised, or circumcised only in the foreskin (uncertain); "merely in the flesh" was an explanation.
- **"all that are in the utmost corners" → "all who are clipped at the edge"** (v26) — Hebrew קְצוּצֵי פֵאָה, "clipped as to the edge": of the hair at the temples (Lev 19:27), so most take it. **"for all these nations are uncircumcised" → "for all the nations are uncircumcised"** — כָל־הַגּוֹיִם, "these" is not in the Hebrew. **"uncircumcised in the heart" → "uncircumcised of heart"** — עַרְלֵי־לֵב. Each name keeps its עַל "upon".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our v1 is Heb 8:23; our v2–26 are Heb 9:1–25.
- **v23–24** — quoted 1 Cor 1:31; 2 Cor 10:17 ("let him who boasts boast in the Lord").
