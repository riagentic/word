[4] and ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall not escape from the hand of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, for he shall surely be given into the hand of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and his mouth shall speak with his mouth, and his eyes shall see his eyes;

[10] And I wrote in the deed and sealed it, and I called witnesses to witness, and I weighed the silver in the scales.

[11] And I took the deed of the purchase, the sealed one, the commandment and the statutes, and the open one;

[14] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Take these deeds, this deed of the purchase, both the sealed one and this open deed, and put them in a vessel of earthenware, so that they may stand many days.

[21] And you brought your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ with signs and with wonders and with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm and with great fear.

[24] Behold, the siege mounds have come to the city to capture it, and the city has been given into the hand of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ who are fighting against it, because of the sword and the famine and the pestilence; and what you spoke has come to pass, and behold, you are seeing it.

[25] And you, you have said to me, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧: Buy for yourself the field for silver, and call witnesses to witness; and the city has been given into the hand of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧.

[30] For the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ have been only doing the evil in my eyes from their youth; for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ have been only vexing me with the work of their hands, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] For to my anger and to my fury this city has been to me, from the day that they built it and to this day, so that I remove it from before my face,

[32] because of all the evil of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, which they have done to vex me, they, their kings, their princes, their priests and their prophets, and the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and those who dwell in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[33] And they have turned to me the back of the neck and not the face; and I taught them, rising early and teaching, and they are not listening, to take discipline.

[34] And they set their detestable things in the house over which my name is called, to defile it.

[35] And they built the high places of the Baal, which are in the valley of ⟦ben-hinnom|Ben Hinnom|Ben-hinnom⟧, to make their sons and their daughters pass over to Molech, which I did not command them, and it did not come up on my heart, to do this abomination, in order to make ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ sin.

[36] And now therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, concerning this city of which you are saying, It has been given into the hand of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ by the sword and by the famine and by the pestilence:

[37] Behold, I am gathering them from all the lands where I have driven them in my anger and in my fury and in great wrath; and I will bring them back to this place, and I will make them dwell in security.

[38] And they shall be a people to me, and I, I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them.

[42] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: As I have brought to this people all this great evil, so I am bringing upon them all the good that I am speaking concerning them.

[43] And the field shall be bought in this land of which you are saying, It is a desolation, without man and beast; it has been given into the hand of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧.

[44] Fields they shall buy for silver, and write in the deed and seal and call witnesses to witness, in the land of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and in the places around ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the cities of the hill country and in the cities of the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧ and in the cities of the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧; for I will restore their fortunes, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v14, 15, 18) — plain words, as the prophets' row. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v17, 25) — אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names (token `adonai-yhwh`). **"God" → "Elohim"** (v14, 15, 27, 36, 38); **"God" → "El"** (v18) — אֵל. נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v5, 30, 44).
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose; the outer quotation marks are dropped (the speeches are nested: Zedekiah quotes Jeremiah, v3–5; Jeremiah tells of the word and the purchase, v6–25).
- **"in the tenth year … which was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar" → "in the tenth year …; it was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar"** (v1) — Hebrew בַּשָּׁנָה is the qere (the ketiv בשנת "in the year of", the same sense); הִיא הַשָּׁנָה שְׁמֹנֶה־עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה, literally "it is the year eighteen years" (the reviewer's numbers row). The name is written נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר (the token's r form; also v28).
- **"For then the king of Babylon's army besieged Jerusalem: and Jeremiah the prophet was shut up in the court of the prison, which was in the king of Judah's house" → "And then the force of the king of Babylon was besieging Jerusalem, and Jeremiah the prophet was shut up in the court of the guard, which was in the house of the king of Judah"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאָז "And then"; חֵיל "force" (the exile row); צָרִים עַל, a participle, "besieging" (as Jer 21:4); כָּלוּא "shut up" (כָּלָא: "keep back" of its row, here of a person confined: the same verb, v3); חֲצַר הַמַּטָּרָה "the court of the guard" (as Jer 37:21; 38:13); בֵּית "house".
- **"For Zedekiah king of Judah had shut him up, saying, Wherefore dost thou prophesy, and say …" → "whom Zedekiah king of Judah had shut up, saying, Why are you prophesying, saying …"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר כְּלָאוֹ "whom … had shut up"; נִבָּא participle "are you prophesying"; לֵאמֹר "saying", twice; הִנְנִי נֹתֵן a participle (also v28); לָכַד "capture" (its row; also v24, 28).
- **"and shall surely be delivered … and shall speak with him mouth to mouth, and his eyes shall behold his eyes" → "for he shall surely be given into the hand … and his mouth shall speak with his mouth, and his eyes shall see his eyes"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי "for" (not "but"); הִנָּתֹן יִנָּתֵן, the infinitive absolute ("being given he shall be given": the standing decision); נִמְלַט "escape" (its row); וְדִבֶּר־פִּיו עִם־פִּיו … וְעֵינָיו אֶת־עֵינָיו תִּרְאֶינָה, kept literal (= face to face); עֵינָיו "his eyes" is the qere (the ketiv עינו "his eye").
- **"And he shall lead Zedekiah to Babylon … though ye fight with the Chaldeans, ye shall not prosper" → "and to Babylon he shall lead Zedekiah, and there he shall be until I visit him … when you fight with the Chaldeans, you shall not prosper?"** (v5) — the Hebrew order; פָּקַד "visit" (its row: for good or for punishment, here not said); כִּי "when" (or "if", "though"); הִצְלִיחַ "prosper" (its row). The "Why …?" of v3 runs to here.
- **"And Jeremiah said, The word of the LORD came unto me, saying"** (v6) — Hebrew הָיָה דְּבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר: the perfect הָיָה, not the formula's וַיְהִי; the same English.
- **"Behold, Hanameel the son of Shallum thine uncle shall come unto thee, saying, Buy thee my field that is in Anathoth: for the right of redemption is thine to buy it" → "Behold, Hanamel son of Shallum your uncle is coming to you, saying, Buy for yourself my field … for yours is the right of redemption, to buy"** (v7) — Hebrew בָּא participle "is coming"; דֹּדְךָ "your uncle" (Shallum is the uncle: v8 "son of my uncle"); קְנֵה לְךָ "buy for yourself" (קָנָה "buy", its row); כִּי לְךָ מִשְׁפַּט הַגְּאֻלָּה לִקְנוֹת, literally "for to you is the right of the redemption, to buy": מִשְׁפָּט "right" (what is due, the legal claim; the מִשְׁפָּט row: "judgment", of a ruling "ordinance"), גְּאֻלָּה "redemption" (its row: the right and the act of the nearest kinsman, Lev 25:25). The terms of v7–15 are given in their plainest sense.
- **"Hanameel mine uncle's son came to me … for the right of inheritance is thine, and the redemption is thine; buy it for thyself. Then I knew that this was the word of the LORD" → "Hanamel son of my uncle came to me … Buy, please, my field … for yours is the right of possession, and yours the redemption; buy for yourself. And I knew that it was the word of Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew בֶּן־דֹּדִי "son of my uncle" (also v9; "cousin" is not a Hebrew word); כִּדְבַר יְהוָה "according to the word of Yahweh"; קְנֵה נָא "Buy, please" (נָא kept); מִשְׁפַּט הַיְרֻשָּׁה "the right of possession" (יְרֻשָּׁה, noun of יָרַשׁ "possess", its row; "inheritance" is נַחֲלָה); קְנֵה־לָךְ "buy for yourself" ("it" is not written); וָאֵדַע "And I knew"; הוּא "it (was)".
- **"and weighed him the money, even seventeen shekels of silver" → "and I weighed out to him the silver, seventeen shekels of silver"** (v9) — Hebrew וָאֶשְׁקֲלָה־לּוֹ אֶת־הַכֶּסֶף "and I weighed out to him the silver" (שָׁקַל "weigh", its row; כֶּסֶף "silver": the price, weighed out, not coined money; also v10, 25, 44); שִׁבְעָה שְׁקָלִים וַעֲשָׂרָה הַכָּסֶף, literally "seven shekels and ten (of) the silver" = seventeen (the reviewer's numbers row; the order is unusual, the sum is plain). שֶׁקֶל "shekel", a weight.
- **"And I subscribed the evidence, and sealed it, and took witnesses, and weighed him the money in the balances" → "And I wrote in the deed and sealed it, and I called witnesses to witness, and I weighed the silver in the scales"** (v10) — Hebrew וָאֶכְתֹּב בַּסֵּפֶר "and I wrote in the deed" (סֵפֶר, any written document: of a deed "deed", the סֵפֶר row; also v11–16, 44); וָאֶחְתֹּם "and I sealed"; וָאָעֵד עֵדִים, hifil of עוּד, "I made witnesses witness" (the עֵד row; also v25, 44); מֹאזְנַיִם "scales" (a dual, the two pans).
- **"So I took the evidence of the purchase, both that which was sealed according to the law and custom, and that which was open" → "And I took the deed of the purchase, the sealed one, the commandment and the statutes, and the open one"** (v11) — Hebrew סֵפֶר הַמִּקְנָה "the deed of the purchase" (מִקְנָה "purchase", the Gen 23 row); הֶחָתוּם "the sealed (one)"; הַמִּצְוָה וְהַחֻקִּים "the commandment and the statutes" (the מִצְוָה and חֹק rows, set without a verb: the terms written in the sealed copy, or "according to the commandment and the statutes"; KJV "according to the law and custom", modern "terms and conditions"); הַגָּלוּי "the open (one)" (passive participle of גָּלָה "uncover"). Two copies of one deed: one sealed, one open to be read.
- **"I gave the evidence of the purchase unto Baruch … in the sight of Hanameel mine uncle's son, and in the presence of the witnesses that subscribed the book of the purchase, before all the Jews that sat in the court of the prison" → "and I gave the deed of the purchase to Baruch … before the eyes of Hanamel my uncle, and before the eyes of the witnesses who wrote in the deed of the purchase, before the eyes of all the Judeans who were sitting in the court of the guard"** (v12) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of", three times (also v13); דֹּדִי "my uncle" as the MT stands (v8, 9 have "son of my uncle"; the Septuagint and others read "son of my uncle" here, from memory; MT kept); הַכֹּתְבִים בְּסֵפֶר "who wrote in the deed" (their names); הַיֹּשְׁבִים "sitting" (the יָשַׁב row: of the body).
- **"And I charged Baruch before them" → "And I commanded Baruch before their eyes"** (v13) — Hebrew וָאֲצַוֶּה, צִוָּה "command".
- **"Take these evidences … and put them in an earthen vessel, that they may continue many days" → "Take these deeds … and put them in a vessel of earthenware, so that they may stand many days"** (v14) — Hebrew לָקוֹחַ, an infinitive absolute as a command; כְּלִי־חָרֶשׂ "a vessel of earthenware" (its row); יַעַמְדוּ "they may stand" (= last).
- **"Houses and fields and vineyards shall be possessed again in this land" → "Again houses and fields and vineyards shall be bought in this land"** (v15) — Hebrew עוֹד יִקָּנוּ, nifal of קָנָה, "shall be bought" (also v43).
- **"Now when I had delivered the evidence … I prayed unto the LORD, saying" → "And I prayed to Yahweh, after I had given the deed …, saying"** (v16) — the Hebrew order; הִתְפַּלֵּל "pray".
- **"Ah Lord GOD! behold, thou hast made the heaven and the earth by thy great power and stretched out arm, and there is nothing too hard for thee" → "Alas, Lord Yahweh! Behold, you, you made the heavens and the earth by your great power and by your outstretched arm; nothing is too wonderful for you"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲהָהּ "Alas" (as Jer 1:6); אַתָּה written; שָׁמַיִם "heavens" (its row); בְּ before both nouns; לֹא־יִפָּלֵא מִמְּךָ כָּל־דָּבָר "nothing is too wonderful for you" (the נִפְלָא מִן row, which names this verse and v27: from פֶּלֶא "wonder", not "too hard"; as Gen 18:14).
- **"Thou shewest lovingkindness unto thousands … the Great, the Mighty God, the LORD of hosts, is his name" → "who does chesed to thousands, and repays the iniquity of fathers into the bosom of their sons after them; the great, the mighty El, Yahweh of Hosts is his name"** (v18) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה חֶסֶד לַאֲלָפִים, participles "who does chesed" (as Exod 20:6 "doing chesed"; token `steadfast-love`, which the verse lacked); מְשַׁלֵּם "repays" (the שִׁלֵּם row); חֵיק "bosom"; בְּנֵיהֶם "their sons" (the standing decision); הָאֵל הַגָּדוֹל הַגִּבּוֹר "the great, the mighty El" (the אֵל גִּבּוֹר row, with the article as Deut 10:17); no "O": the verse describes, it does not call.
- **"Great in counsel, and mighty in work: for thine eyes are open upon all the ways of the sons of men: to give every one according to his ways, and according to the fruit of his doings" → "great in counsel and abundant in deed, whose eyes are open upon all the ways of the sons of man, to give to each man …"** (v19) — Hebrew גְּדֹל הָעֵצָה "great in counsel" (עֵצָה, its row); רַב הָעֲלִילִיָּה "abundant in deed" (רַב "much, great"; עֲלִילִיָּה only here, from the root of מַעֲלָלִים "doings"); אֲשֶׁר "whose" (not "for"); בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man" (the reviewer row); לְאִישׁ "to each man"; מַעֲלָלִים "doings" (the Ps 78 row).
- **"Which hast set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt, even unto this day, and in Israel, and among other men; and hast made thee a name, as at this day" → "who have set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt to this day, and in Israel and among mankind, and have made for yourself a name, as at this day"** (v20) — Hebrew שַׂמְתָּ "set"; אוֹת "sign", מוֹפֵת "wonder" (their rows; also v21); בָּאָדָם "among mankind" ("other" is not written); וַתַּעֲשֶׂה־לְּךָ שֵׁם "and made yourself a name".
- **"with signs, and with wonders, and with a strong hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with great terror" → "… and with great fear"** (v21) — Hebrew בְּ before each member (the reviewer's "with a strong hand" row); מוֹרָא "fear" (its row, singular as Deut 26:8; 34:12).
- **"which thou didst swear to their fathers to give them, a land flowing with milk and honey"** (v22) — Hebrew נִשְׁבַּע "swear"; זָבַת חָלָב וּדְבָשׁ (its row).
- **"And they came in, and possessed it; but they obeyed not thy voice, neither walked in thy law; they have done nothing of all that thou commandedst them to do: therefore thou hast caused all this evil to come upon them" → "And they came and possessed it, and they did not listen to your voice, and in your Torah they did not walk; all that you commanded them to do they did not do; and you made all this evil meet them"** (v23) — Hebrew יָרַשׁ "possess"; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice" (its row); וּבְתוֹרָתְךָ "and in your Torah" is the qere (the ketiv ובתרותך may be read as a plural, "your torot"); the Hebrew order of the last clauses, each with its own verb; וַתַּקְרֵא, hifil of קָרָא "meet", "you made … meet them"; וְ "and" (not "therefore"); רָעָה "evil".
- **"Behold the mounts, they are come unto the city to take it; and the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans … because of the sword, and of the famine, and of the pestilence: and what thou hast spoken is come to pass" → "Behold, the siege mounds have come to the city to capture it, and the city has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans … because of the sword and the famine and the pestilence; and what you spoke has come to pass, and behold, you are seeing it"** (v24) — Hebrew הַסֹּלְלוֹת "the siege mounds" (סֹלְלָה, as Jer 6:6); נִתְּנָה perfect "has been given" (also v25, 36, 43); בְּיַד singular "hand"; מִפְּנֵי "because of" (its row); וְהִנְּךָ רֹאֶה "and behold, you are seeing".
- **"And thou hast said unto me, O Lord GOD, Buy thee the field for money, and take witnesses; for the city is given into the hand of the Chaldeans" → "And you, you have said to me, Lord Yahweh: Buy for yourself the field for silver, and call witnesses to witness; and the city has been given into the hand of the Chaldeans"** (v25) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה "And you"; no "O"; קְנֵה־לְךָ "buy for yourself"; בַּכֶּסֶף "for silver" (as v9); וְהָעֵד עֵדִים as v10; וְהָעִיר "and the city" (וְ "and": the contrast is left to the reader, "though" in sense).
- **"Then came the word of the LORD unto Jeremiah, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to Jeremiah, saying"** (v26) — the prophets' formula with the name.
- **"Behold, I am the LORD, the God of all flesh: is there any thing too hard for me?" → "Behold, I am Yahweh, the Elohim of all flesh; is any thing too wonderful for me?"** (v27) — Hebrew בָּשָׂר "flesh"; הֲמִמֶּנִּי יִפָּלֵא כָּל־דָּבָר "is any thing too wonderful for me?" (as v17).
- **"And the Chaldeans … shall come and set fire on this city, and burn it with the houses, upon whose roofs they have offered incense unto Baal, and poured out drink offerings unto other gods, to provoke me to anger" → "And the Chaldeans … shall come and set this city on fire with fire and burn it, and the houses on whose roofs they have made smoke to the Baal and poured out drink offerings to other gods, to vex me"** (v29) — Hebrew וְהִצִּיתוּ … בָּאֵשׁ "and set on fire with fire"; שָׂרַף "burn"; קִטְּרוּ without an object "made smoke" (the reviewer row; "offered incense" decides for incense); לַבַּעַל "to the Baal" (the article, as Jer 19:5); הִסִּיכוּ נְסָכִים "poured out drink offerings" (its row; as Jer 19:13); הִכְעִיס "vex" (its row: "provoke to anger" in KJV; "anger" is not a second word; also v30, 32).
- **"For the children of Israel and the children of Judah have only done evil before me from their youth: for the children of Israel have only provoked me to anger with the work of their hands" → "For the sons of Israel and the sons of Judah have been only doing the evil in my eyes from their youth; for the sons of Israel have been only vexing me with the work of their hands"** (v30) — Hebrew בְּנֵי "sons of" (the standing decision; also v32); הָיוּ … אַךְ עֹשִׂים, a participle with "were": "have been only doing"; הָרַע with the article, "the evil" (as Jer 7:30); בְּעֵינַי "in my eyes"; מִנְּעֻרֹתֵיהֶם "from their youth".
- **"For this city hath been to me as a provocation of mine anger and of my fury from the day that they built it even unto this day; that I should remove it from before my face" → "For to my anger and to my fury this city has been to me, from the day that they built it and to this day, so that I remove it from before my face"** (v31) — Hebrew כִּי עַל־אַפִּי וְעַל־חֲמָתִי הָיְתָה לִּי, literally "for upon my anger and upon my fury it has been to me" (an occasion of anger: "provocation" is KJV's supply); אַף "anger" (the standing decision), חֵמָה "fury"; לְמִן־הַיּוֹם … וְעַד הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה; לַהֲסִירָהּ "to remove it"; מֵעַל פָּנַי "from before my face".
- **"they, their kings, their princes, their priests, and their prophets, and the men of Judah, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem"** (v32) — Hebrew הֵמָּה "they"; שָׂרֵיהֶם "their princes" (the שַׂר row); אִישׁ יְהוּדָה "the man of Judah" (a collective: "the men"); יֹשְׁבֵי "those who dwell in" (the יָשַׁב row).
- **"And they have turned unto me the back, and not the face: though I taught them, rising up early and teaching them, yet they have not hearkened to receive instruction" → "And they have turned to me the back of the neck and not the face; and I taught them, rising early and teaching, and they are not listening, to take discipline"** (v33) — Hebrew עֹרֶף "the back of the neck" (its row; as Jer 2:27); וְלַמֵּד אֹתָם, an infinitive, "and teaching them" ("I" is supplied: Yahweh speaks); הַשְׁכֵּם וְלַמֵּד, two infinitives absolute (= again and again, as Jer 7:13, 25); וְאֵינָם שֹׁמְעִים "and they are not listening", a participle; לָקַחַת מוּסָר "to take discipline" (as Jer 7:28; מוּסָר, its row).
- **"But they set their abominations in the house, which is called by my name, to defile it" → "And they set their detestable things in the house over which my name is called, to defile it"** (v34) — the words of Jer 7:30 and their wording: שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things"; אֲשֶׁר נִקְרָא־שְׁמִי עָלָיו "over which my name is called" (the mark of ownership); טִמֵּא "defile".
- **"And they built the high places of Baal, which are in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to cause their sons and their daughters to pass through the fire unto Molech; which I commanded them not, neither came it into my mind, that they should do this abomination, to cause Judah to sin" → "And they built the high places of the Baal, which are in the valley of Ben-hinnom, to make their sons and their daughters pass over to Molech, which I did not command them, and it did not come up on my heart, to do this abomination, in order to make Judah sin"** (v35) — Hebrew בָּמוֹת הַבַּעַל "the high places of the Baal" (as Jer 19:5); גֵּיא בֶן־הִנֹּם "the valley of Ben-hinnom" (the token `ben-hinnom`, as Jer 7:31; literally "of the son of Hinnom"); לְהַעֲבִיר … לַמֹּלֶךְ "to make pass over to Molech" (the מֹלֶךְ row: "through the fire" is not written here; the Hebrew has the article, "the Molech"); וְלֹא עָלְתָה עַל־לִבִּי "and it did not come up on my heart" (as Jer 7:31; 19:5); תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" (its row); הַחֲטִיא is the qere (the ketiv החטי drops the last letter), "to make … sin" (the הֶחֱטִיא row).
- **"And now therefore thus saith the LORD … concerning this city, whereof ye say, It shall be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon by the sword, and by the famine, and by the pestilence" → "And now therefore thus says Yahweh … concerning this city of which you are saying, It has been given into the hand of the king of Babylon by the sword and by the famine and by the pestilence"** (v36) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה לָכֵן "and now therefore"; אֶל "concerning"; אֹמְרִים participle "are saying" (also v43); נִתְּנָה perfect "has been given".
- **"Behold, I will gather them out of all countries, whither I have driven them in mine anger, and in my fury, and in great wrath … and I will cause them to dwell safely" → "Behold, I am gathering them from all the lands where I have driven them in my anger and in my fury and in great wrath … and I will make them dwell in security"** (v37) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מְקַבְּצָם, a participle; אֲרָצוֹת "lands"; הִדִּיחַ "drive" (its row); קֶצֶף "wrath" (its row); הוֹשִׁיב "make dwell"; לָבֶטַח "in security".
- **"And they shall be my people, and I will be their God" → "And they shall be a people to me, and I, I will be Elohim to them"** (v38) — the covenant formula as Jer 30:22; 31:1, 33; אֲנִי written.
- **"And I will give them one heart, and one way, that they may fear me for ever, for the good of them, and of their children after them" → "And I will give them one heart and one way, to fear me all the days, for good to them and to their sons after them"** (v39) — Hebrew לְיִרְאָה אוֹתִי "to fear me"; כָּל־הַיָּמִים "all the days" (not עוֹלָם); לְטוֹב לָהֶם "for good to them"; בְּנֵיהֶם "their sons".
- **"And I will make an everlasting covenant with them, that I will not turn away from them, to do them good; but I will put my fear in their hearts, that they shall not depart from me" → "And I will cut for them an everlasting covenant, that I will not turn back from after them, to do them good; and my fear I will put in their heart, so that they do not turn aside from me"** (v40) — Hebrew וְכָרַתִּי לָהֶם בְּרִית עוֹלָם "and I will cut for them an everlasting covenant" (the בְּרִית and עוֹלָם rows); לֹא־אָשׁוּב מֵאַחֲרֵיהֶם "I will not turn back from after them" (from following them); הֵיטִיב "do good" (its row; also v41); וְ "and" (not "but"); יִרְאָתִי "my fear" (the fear of me); בִּלְבָבָם "in their heart" (singular); לְבִלְתִּי סוּר מֵעָלָי "so as not to turn aside from me" ("they" supplied for English).
- **"Yea, I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land assuredly with my whole heart and with my whole soul" → "And I will exult over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land in truth, with all my heart and with all my soul"** (v41) — Hebrew וְשַׂשְׂתִּי "and I will exult" (the שׂוּשׂ row); בֶּאֱמֶת "in truth" (the אֱמֶת row; KJV "assuredly"); בְּכָל־לִבִּי וּבְכָל־נַפְשִׁי "with all my heart and with all my soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul", plain).
- **"Like as I have brought all this great evil upon this people, so will I bring upon them all the good that I have promised them" → "As I have brought to this people all this great evil, so I am bringing upon them all the good that I am speaking concerning them"** (v42) — Hebrew הֵבֵאתִי אֶל "I have brought to"; מֵבִיא עֲלֵיהֶם, a participle, "I am bringing upon them"; אָנֹכִי דֹּבֵר עֲלֵיהֶם "I am speaking concerning them" (the דִּבֶּר טוֹב עַל row: not "promise").
- **"And fields shall be bought in this land, whereof ye say, It is desolate without man or beast" → "And the field shall be bought in this land of which you are saying, It is a desolation, without man and beast"** (v43) — Hebrew וְנִקְנָה הַשָּׂדֶה, singular with the article ("the field": each field, or the field of v7–9); שְׁמָמָה "a desolation" (its row); מֵאֵין אָדָם וּבְהֵמָה "without man and beast".
- **"Men shall buy fields for money, and subscribe evidences, and seal them, and take witnesses … in the cities of the mountains, and in the cities of the valley, and in the cities of the south: for I will cause their captivity to return" → "Fields they shall buy for silver, and write in the deed and seal and call witnesses to witness … in the cities of the hill country and in the cities of the Shephelah and in the cities of the Negev; for I will restore their fortunes"** (v44) — Hebrew שָׂדוֹת "fields" stands first; וְכָתוֹב … וְחָתוֹם … וְהָעֵד, infinitives absolute carrying on the verb ("and write … and seal … and make witnesses witness", as v10); "men" is not written; הָהָר "the hill country" (the הַר row); הַשְּׁפֵלָה and הַנֶּגֶב keep their tokens; סְבִיבֵי "the places around"; כִּי־אָשִׁיב אֶת־שְׁבוּתָם, hifil, literally "for I will turn back their captivity / their turning": the reviewer row "restore the fortunes".

### Notes
- **v1** — 588/587 BC, during the last siege of Jerusalem (Jer 39:1–2; 52:4–5).
- **v6–15** — the purchase of the field at Anathoth by the right of redemption (Lev 25:25; Ruth 4): a deed in two copies, sealed and open, laid up in an earthen vessel; the sign that "houses and fields and vineyards shall be bought again" (v15).
