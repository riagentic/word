# Jeremiah 35

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the days of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧:

[3] So I took ⟦jaazaniah|Yaazanyahu|Jaazaniah⟧ son of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, son of ⟦habazziniah|Chavatzinyah|Habazziniah⟧, and his brothers, and all his sons, and the whole house of the ⟦rechabites|Rechavim|Rechabites⟧.

[16] The sons of ⟦jonadab|Yonadav|Jonadab⟧ son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧ have kept the command that their father gave them, but this people has not obeyed me.

[18] But to the house of the ⟦rechabites|Rechavim|Rechabites⟧ ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Because you have obeyed the command of ⟦jonadab|Yonadav|Jonadab⟧ your father and kept all his precepts and done all that he commanded you,

[19] therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: ⟦jonadab|Yonadav|Jonadab⟧ son of ⟦rechab|Rechav|Rechab⟧ shall never lack a man to stand before me."

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv13, 17-19) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 13, 17-19) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved; "*Elohim Tzeva'ot*" (v17).
- **"man of God" → "man of *Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים *ish ha-Elohim*; standard title preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; Rechabite sign-act narrative.

### Notes
- **Rechabites** — descendants of Jehonadab (2 Kgs 10:15ff), nomadic tent-dwelling clan; held up as foil to unfaithful Judah.
