# Jeremiah 8

[1] At that time, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, they shall bring out the bones of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and the bones of his princes, and the bones of the priests, and the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, from their graves.

[2] And they shall spread them out to the sun and to the moon and to all the host of the heavens, which they have loved and which they have served and after which they have walked and which they have sought and to which they have bowed down; they shall not be gathered and shall not be buried; dung on the face of the ground they shall be.

[3] And death shall be chosen rather than life by all the remnant that remain of this evil clan, in all the places that remain, where I have driven them, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[4] And you shall say to them, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
Do they fall and not rise?
Or does one turn back and not turn back?

[5] Why has this people turned away,
⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, with an enduring turning away?
They hold fast to deceit;
they refuse to turn back.

[6] I have given heed and heard:
they speak what is not right;
there is no man who is sorry for his evil,
saying, "What have I done?"
Every one of them turns in their running,
like a horse rushing into battle.

[7] Even the stork in the heavens
knows her appointed times,
and the turtledove and the swift and the crane
keep the time of their coming;
but my people do not know
the judgment of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] How do you say, "We are wise,
and the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is with us"?
Surely, behold, for falsehood it has worked,
the lying pen of the scribes.

[9] The wise have been put to shame,
they are dismayed and captured;
behold, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ they have rejected,
and what wisdom is theirs?

[10] Therefore I will give their wives to others,
their fields to those who take possession;
for from the small to the great
every one is gaining unjust gain;
from prophet to priest
every one is doing falsehood.

[11] And they have healed the breaking of the daughter of my people lightly,
saying, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧, ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧,"
and there is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[12] They have acted shamefully, for abomination they have done;
also they are surely not ashamed,
and to be put to shame they have not known;
therefore they shall fall among the falling;
in the time of their visitation they shall stumble,
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] I will surely make an end of them, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
there are no grapes on the vine,
and there are no figs on the fig tree,
and the leaf has withered;
and I have given to them: they shall pass over them.

[14] Why are we sitting?
Gather yourselves, and let us go into the fortified cities,
and let us be still there;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has made us still,
and has made us drink water of poison,
for we have sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] Waiting for ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and there is no good;
for a time of healing, and behold, terror.

[16] From Dan has been heard the snorting of his horses;
at the sound of the neighings of his mighty ones
the whole land has quaked;
and they have come and devoured the land and its fullness,
the city and those who dwell in it.

[17] For behold, I am sending among you serpents,
vipers, for which there is no charming,
and they shall bite you,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] My cheer against sorrow!
Upon me my heart is faint.

[19] Behold, the voice of the cry for help of the daughter of my people
from a land of far distances:
Is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ not in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧?
Or is her king not in her?
Why have they vexed me with their carved images,
with foreign vapors?

[20] The harvest has passed, the summer has ended,
and we, we have not been saved.

[21] Over the breaking of the daughter of my people I am broken;
I have gone dark; astonishment has taken hold of me.

[22] Is there no balm in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧?
Or is there no physician there?
For why has the new flesh of the daughter of my people
not come up?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v3) — יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 13, 17) — נְאֻם יְהוָה; **"saith the LORD" → "says Yahweh"** (v12) — אָמַר יְהוָה (as 6:15).
- **Layout** — v1–3 prose; from v4 one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents. No outer quotation marks: only the words quoted inside the speech (v6, 8, 11) are marked.
- **"they shall bring out the bones … out of their graves"** (v1) — kept, active, with no subject named; the qere יוֹצִיאוּ (the ketiv ויציאו "and they shall bring out"); "the bones of" before each of the five, as the Hebrew; שָׂרָיו "his princes" (the king's: שַׂר "prince"); קֶבֶר "grave".
- **"before the sun, and the moon, and all the host of heaven, whom they have loved" → "to the sun and to the moon and to all the host of the heavens, which they have loved"** (v2) — לַשֶּׁמֶשׁ וְלַיָּרֵחַ וּלְכֹל צְבָא הַשָּׁמַיִם; שָׁטַח "spread out". **"whom they have worshipped" → "to which they have bowed down"** — הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה; דָּרַשׁ "seek". **"nor be buried; they shall be for dung upon the face of the earth" → "and shall not be buried; dung on the face of the ground they shall be"** — לְדֹמֶן "for dung", first; הָאֲדָמָה "the ground".
- **"by all the residue of them that remain of this evil family, which remain in all the places whither I have driven them" → "by all the remnant that remain of this evil clan, in all the places that remain, where I have driven them"** (v3) — נִבְחַר "be chosen"; מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan"; הַנִּשְׁאָרִים stands twice, the second time after "the places" ("the places that remain"; it is often struck as a copying slip, as the Greek lacks it (from memory) — not followed, MT kept; KJV turns it back to the people). הִדִּיחַ "drive".
- **"Moreover thou shalt say … Shall they fall, and not arise? shall he turn away, and not return?" → "And you shall say … Do they fall and not rise? Or does one turn back and not turn back?"** (v4) — וְאָמַרְתָּ; הֲ … אִם, the double question ("or"); יָשׁוּב twice, the same verb שׁוּב "turn back" (it begins the play on the root that runs through v5–6).
- **"Why then is this people of Jerusalem slidden back by a perpetual backsliding?" → "Why has this people turned away, Jerusalem, with an enduring turning away?"** (v5) — שׁוֹבְבָה … מְשֻׁבָה, verb and noun of שׁוּב (as 5:6 "turnings away"); "Jerusalem" stands beside "this people" (no "of"); נִצַּחַת "enduring" (of נֵצַח). **"they hold fast deceit" → "They hold fast to deceit"** — הֶחֱזִיקוּ בַּתַּרְמִית; תַּרְמִית is another word than מִרְמָה "deceit" (5:27), of the same root: English has one word. **"to return" → "to turn back"** — לָשׁוּב.
- **"I hearkened and heard, but they spake not aright" → "I have given heed and heard: they speak what is not right"** (v6) — הִקְשַׁבְתִּי; לוֹא־כֵן יְדַבֵּרוּ "not so they speak". **"no man repented him of his wickedness" → "there is no man who is sorry for his evil"** — נִחַם "be sorry" (not "repent"); רָעָה "evil". **"every one turned to his course, as the horse rusheth into the battle" → "Every one of them turns in their running, like a horse rushing into battle"** — כֻּלֹּה שָׁב "all of it turns" (שׁוּב again); the qere בִּמְרוּצָתָם "in their running" (the ketiv במרצותם is the plural, "in their runnings"); שׁוֹטֵף "rushing" (literally "overflowing", of a flood).
- **"the stork … knoweth her appointed times; and the turtle and the crane and the swallow observe the time of their coming" → "the stork … knows her appointed times, and the turtledove and the swift and the crane keep the time of their coming"** (v7) — מוֹעֲדֶיהָ "her appointed times"; the qere סִיס "swift" (the ketiv סוס, as Isa 38:14); עָגוּר "crane" (or "thrush"); the birds are uncertain and are rendered as Isa 38:14; שָׁמַר "keep". **"but my people know not the judgment of the LORD" → "but my people do not know the judgment of Yahweh"** — וְעַמִּי, set against the birds; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment".
- **"Lo, certainly in vain made he it; the pen of the scribes is in vain" → "Surely, behold, for falsehood it has worked, the lying pen of the scribes"** (v8) — אָכֵן הִנֵּה לַשֶּׁקֶר עָשָׂה עֵט שֶׁקֶר סֹפְרִים: לַשֶּׁקֶר "for falsehood" (KJV "in vain"); עָשָׂה "it has worked", with the pen as subject (or "he has made it", the torah, with an unnamed subject: the Hebrew allows both); עֵט שֶׁקֶר "the lying pen" (literally "the pen of falsehood"), as printed. "it" refers to the torah of the line before.
- **"The wise men are ashamed" → "The wise have been put to shame"** (v9) — הֹבִשׁוּ, the hifil of 6:15; 8:12 ("acted shamefully"): here, of persons brought to nothing, "be put to shame"; the one form, two senses by context. **"they are dismayed and taken" → "they are dismayed and captured"** — חַתּוּ "be dismayed" (literally "be shattered"); וַיִּלָּכֵדוּ, לָכַד "capture". **"they have rejected the word of the LORD" → "the word of Yahweh they have rejected"** — the Hebrew order; מָאַס בְּ. **"and what wisdom is in them?" → "and what wisdom is theirs?"** — וְחָכְמַת־מֶה לָהֶם, "and wisdom of what is to them".
- **"to them that shall inherit them" → "to those who take possession"** (v10) — לְיוֹרְשִׁים, the participle of יָרַשׁ "take possession" (no "them"). **"every one from the least even unto the greatest is given to covetousness" → "from the small to the great every one is gaining unjust gain"** — מִקָּטֹן וְעַד־גָּדוֹל (6:13 has "from their least to their greatest"); בֹּצֵעַ בָּצַע; עֹשֶׂה שָׁקֶר "doing falsehood". v10b–12 = 6:13–15 where the Hebrew is the same; each verse as printed.
- **"For they have healed the hurt of the daughter of my people slightly … when there is no peace" → "And they have healed the breaking of the daughter of my people lightly … and there is no shalom"** (v11) — וַיְרַפּוּ "and they have healed" (the verb of 6:14, spelled without א); שֶׁבֶר "breaking"; בַּת־עַמִּי "the daughter of my people" (6:14 has "my people"); עַל־נְקַלָּה "lightly"; שָׁלוֹם three times, the token; וְאֵין "and there is no". Otherwise the same wording as 6:14.
- **"Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? nay, they were not at all ashamed, neither could they blush" → "They have acted shamefully, for abomination they have done; also they are surely not ashamed, and to be put to shame they have not known"** (v12) — as 6:15, with the Hebrew's differences: גַּם once (6:15 twice); וְהִכָּלֵם, the nifal "to be put to shame" (6:15 the hifil "to show shame"). **"in the time of their visitation they shall be cast down" → "in the time of their visitation they shall stumble"** — בְּעֵת פְּקֻדָּתָם (6:15 "at the time that I visit them"); כָּשַׁל "stumble".
- **"I will surely consume them" → "I will surely make an end of them"** (v13) — אָסֹף אֲסִיפֵם: the infinitive absolute of אָסַף "gather" with the hifil of סוּף "make an end" (a play of sound; literally "gathering I will make an end of them"). **"there shall be no grapes … and the leaf shall fade" → "there are no grapes … and the leaf has withered"** — אֵין "there is not"; נָבֵל perfect, "wither". **"and the things that I have given them shall pass away from them" → "and I have given to them: they shall pass over them"** — וָאֶתֵּן לָהֶם יַעַבְרוּם, word for word; obscure: "what I gave them shall pass from them" (KJV), or "I have given them up to those who shall pass over them"; the verse keeps the words and adds none.
- **"Why do we sit still?" → "Why are we sitting?"** (v14) — יֹשְׁבִים, participle ("still" is not written). **"assemble yourselves, and let us enter into the defenced cities" → "Gather yourselves, and let us go into the fortified cities"** — הֵאָסְפוּ; עָרֵי הַמִּבְצָר. **"let us be silent there: for the LORD our God hath put us to silence" → "let us be still there; for Yahweh our Elohim has made us still"** — וְנִדְּמָה … הֲדִמָּנוּ, of דָּמַם "be still, be silent" (also taken as "perish / make perish", from the same root: say so). **"water of gall" → "water of poison"** — מֵי־רֹאשׁ (רֹאשׁ a poisonous plant: Deut 29:18).
- **"We looked for peace, but no good came; and for a time of health, and behold trouble!" → "Waiting for shalom, and there is no good; for a time of healing, and behold, terror"** (v15) — קַוֵּה, an infinitive absolute without a finite verb or subject ("waiting for", קִוָּה "wait for"); וְאֵין טוֹב "and there is no good"; מַרְפֵּה "healing"; בְעָתָה "terror". The same words stand in 14:19 (there מַרְפֵּא).
- **"The snorting of his horses was heard from Dan" → "From Dan has been heard the snorting of his horses"** (v16) — the Hebrew order; perfects; "his": the enemy of v14. **"the neighing of his strong ones" → "the neighings of his mighty ones"** — מִצְהֲלוֹת plural; אַבִּירִים "mighty ones" (stallions are meant). **"for they are come, and have devoured the land, and all that is in it" → "and they have come and devoured the land and its fullness"** — וַיָּבוֹאוּ וַיֹּאכְלוּ with "and"; מְלוֹאָהּ "its fullness".
- **"I will send serpents, cockatrices, among you, which will not be charmed" → "I am sending among you serpents, vipers, for which there is no charming"** (v17) — מְשַׁלֵּחַ participle; צִפְעֹנִים "vipers" (kind uncertain); אֲשֶׁר אֵין־לָהֶם לָחַשׁ "for which there is no charming" (a noun).
- **"When I would comfort myself against sorrow, my heart is faint in me" → "My cheer against sorrow! Upon me my heart is faint"** (v18) — מַבְלִיגִיתִי, found only here: taken from בָּלַג "brighten, take cheer" (Job 9:27; Ps 39:13), "my cheer"; עֲלֵי יָגוֹן "over / against sorrow"; the line is obscure (the consonants are also divided "without healing", מִבְּלִי גֹהָה: an emendation, from memory; not followed). עָלַי לִבִּי דַוָּי "upon me my heart is faint" (דַּוָּי "sick, faint").
- **"Behold the voice of the cry of the daughter of my people because of them that dwell in a far country" → "Behold, the voice of the cry for help of the daughter of my people from a land of far distances"** (v19) — שַׁוְעַת "cry for help"; מֵאֶרֶץ מַרְחַקִּים "from a land of distances" (from far away, or across the breadth of the land: uncertain). **"is not her king in her?" → "Or is her king not in her?"** — אִם "or"; מַלְכָּהּ "her king", lower case: the Hebrew does not mark it as a title (Yahweh is meant, by the line before). **"with their graven images, and with strange vanities" → "with their carved images, with foreign vapors"** — פְּסִלִים "carved images"; הַבְלֵי נֵכָר "vapors of foreignness" (הֶבֶל "vapor"); no "and".
- **"we are not saved" → "and we, we have not been saved"** (v20) — וַאֲנַחְנוּ, the pronoun stands out; נוֹשָׁעְנוּ perfect.
- **"For the hurt of the daughter of my people am I hurt" → "Over the breaking of the daughter of my people I am broken"** (v21) — עַל־שֶׁבֶר … הָשְׁבָּרְתִּי, noun and verb of one root (שָׁבַר "break"). **"I am black" → "I have gone dark"** — קָדַרְתִּי (dark of face and dress, as mourners). **"astonishment hath taken hold on me"** — kept; שַׁמָּה, here the feeling (as of one appalled; 5:30 "a thing that appalls" is the same word); הֶחֱזִיק "take hold of".
- **"Is there no balm in Gilead; is there no physician there?" → "Is there no balm in Gilead? Or is there no physician there?"** (v22) — הַ … אִם; צֳרִי "balm"; רֹפֵא "physician". **"why then is not the health of the daughter of my people recovered?" → "For why has the new flesh of the daughter of my people not come up?"** — כִּי מַדּוּעַ "for why"; אֲרֻכָה "new flesh" (the new skin that grows over a wound: 30:17; 33:6; Isa 58:8); עָלְתָה "come up".

### Notes

- **Hebrew numbering** — the Hebrew chapter 8 has one verse more: 8:23 is our 9:1.
