# Jeremiah 29

[1] These are the words of the letter that ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet sent from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to the surviving elders of the exiles, and to the priests, the prophets, and all the people, whom ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ had taken into exile from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[2] This was after King ⟦jeconiah|Yechonyah|Jeconiah⟧ and the queen mother, the eunuchs, the officials of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the craftsmen, and the metal workers had departed from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[3] The letter was sent by the hand of Elasah son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ and ⟦gemariah|Gemaryahu|Gemariah⟧ son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧, whom ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ sent to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ to ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧. It said:

[4] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to all the exiles whom I have sent into exile from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧:

[5] **Build houses and live in them; plant gardens and eat their produce.**

[6] Take wives and have sons and daughters; take wives for your sons, and give your daughters in marriage, that they may bear sons and daughters; multiply there, and do not decrease.

[7] But seek the peace of the city where I have sent you into exile, and pray to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on its behalf, for in its peace you will find your peace.

[8] "For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Do not let your prophets and your diviners who are among you deceive you, and do not listen to the dreams that they dream,

[10] "For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: When seventy years are completed for ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, I will visit you, and I will fulfill to you my promise and bring you back to this place.

[11] For I know the plans I have for you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, plans for peace and not for evil, to give you a future and a hope.

[12] Then you will call upon me and come and pray to me, and I will hear you.

[13] You will seek me and find me, when you seek me with all your heart.

[14] I will be found by you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will restore your fortunes and gather you from all the nations and all the places where I have driven you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will bring you back to the place from which I sent you into exile.

[15] "Because you have said, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has raised up prophets for us in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,'

[16] thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the king who sits on the throne of David, and concerning all the people who dwell in this city, your kinsmen who did not go out with you into exile:

[17] 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: behold, I am sending on them sword, famine, and pestilence, and I will make them like vile figs that are so rotten they cannot be eaten.

[18] I will pursue them with sword, famine, and pestilence, and will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, to be a curse, a terror, a hissing, and a reproach among all the nations where I have driven them,

[19] because they did not pay attention to my words, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that I persistently sent to you by my servants the prophets, but you would not listen, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'

[20] "Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all you exiles whom I sent away from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧:

[24] "To ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ of ⟦nehelam|Nechelam|Nehelam⟧ you shall say:

[25] 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: You have sent letters in your name to all the people who are in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and to ⟦zephaniah|Tzefanyah|Zephaniah⟧ the son of ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ the priest, and to all the priests, saying,

[29] ⟦zephaniah|Tzefanyah|Zephaniah⟧ the priest read this letter in the hearing of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet.

[30] Then the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧:

[31] "Send to all the exiles, saying, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ of ⟦nehelam|Nechelam|Nehelam⟧: Because ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ had prophesied to you when I did not send him, and has made you trust in a lie,

He shall not have anyone living among this people, and he shall not see the good that I will do to my people, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he has spoken rebellion against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv4, 8, 17, 21, 25) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 8, 21, 25) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; the famous letter to the exiles.

### Notes
- **v5-7** — exiles instructed to settle, pray for Babylon's welfare; transformative ethic of diaspora.
- **v11** — "plans for peace and not for evil" (מַחְשְׁבוֹת שָׁלוֹם וְלֹא לְרָעָה) — widely quoted promise.
