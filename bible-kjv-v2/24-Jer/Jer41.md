# Jeremiah 41

[1] And it came to pass in the seventh month that ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧, the son of Elishama, of the seed of the kingship, and the chiefs of the king, and ten men with him, came to ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ the son of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧, to the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧; and they ate bread there together at the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧.

[2] And ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧ arose, and the ten men who were with him, and they struck ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ the son of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧, the son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧, with the sword, and he put him to death, him whom the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ had appointed in the land.

[3] And all the ⟦judeans|Yehudim|Judeans⟧ who were with him, with ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧, at the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧, and the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ who were found there, the men of war, ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ struck.

[4] And it came to pass on the second day after the putting to death of ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧, and no man knew,

[5] that men came from Shechem, from Shiloh and from ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, eighty men, with beards shaved and garments torn and cutting themselves, and a grain offering and frankincense in their hand, to bring to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And it came to pass, when they came into the midst of the city, that ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧ slaughtered them into the midst of the pit, he and the men who were with him.

[8] And ten men were found among them, and they said to ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧, "Do not put us to death, for we have hidden stores in the field: wheat and barley and oil and honey." And he ceased, and did not put them to death in the midst of their brothers.

[9] And the pit into which ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ had thrown all the carcasses of the men whom he had struck beside ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ is the one that King Asa made because of ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧ filled it with the slain.

[10] And ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ took captive all the remnant of the people who were at the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧, the daughters of the king and all the people who were left at the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧, over whom ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards had appointed ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ the son of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧; and ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧ took them captive and went to pass over to the sons of Ammon.

[11] And ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ the son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧ and all the commanders of the forces who were with him heard of all the evil that ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧ had done.

[12] And they took all the men and went to fight with ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧; and they found him by the many waters that are in ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧.

[13] And it came to pass, when all the people who were with ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ saw ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ the son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧ and all the commanders of the forces who were with him, that they rejoiced.

[14] And all the people whom ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ had taken captive from the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧ turned around, and returned, and went to ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ the son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧.

[16] And ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ the son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧ and all the commanders of the forces who were with him took all the remnant of the people whom he had brought back from ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧, from the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧, after he had struck ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ the son of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧: grown men, men of war, and women and little ones and eunuchs, whom he had brought back from ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; the killing of Gedaliah and its aftermath.
- **2 Kgs 25:25** — v1–3 tell the same event in other Hebrew words (Kings: "came, and ten men with him, and they struck Gedaliah, and he died"); the wording of 2 Kgs 25:25 is kept where the Hebrew is the same ("the son of Nethaniah, the son of Elishama, of the seed of the kingship", "the Mizpah", "struck"). "The Mizpah" (v1, 3, 6, 10, 14, 16): the Hebrew has the article, "the Watchtower".
- **"Now it came to pass … of the seed royal, and the princes of the king, even ten men with him" → "And it came to pass … of the seed of the kingship, and the chiefs of the king, and ten men with him"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי; זֶרַע הַמְּלוּכָה, literally "the seed of the kingship" (the royal line); וְרַבֵּי הַמֶּלֶךְ "and the chiefs of the king" (רַב "great one, chief", as רַב־טַבָּחִים "chief of the guards", v10): the Hebrew does not say whether Ishmael was one of them or whether they came with him; the words are not in 2 Kgs 25:25. **"there they did eat bread together"** — "they ate bread there together".
- **"and slew him, whom the king of Babylon had made governor over the land" → "and he put him to death, him whom the king of Babylon had appointed in the land"** (v2, 18) — Hebrew וַיָּמֶת אֹתוֹ, singular: Ishmael "put him to death" (הֵמִית); הִפְקִיד בָּאָרֶץ "appointed in the land": "governor" is not written (as 2 Kgs 25:22). הִכָּה "strike" (v2, 3, 9, 16, 18), as 2 Kgs 25:25.
- **"Ishmael also slew all the Jews … and the men of war" → "And all the Judeans … the men of war, Ishmael struck"** (v3) — the Hebrew sets the objects first and the subject last; אֵת אַנְשֵׁי הַמִּלְחָמָה "the men of war" stands without "and", as a further name for those struck; נִמְצְאוּ "were found".
- **"the second day after he had slain Gedaliah, and no man knew it" → "on the second day after the putting to death of Gedaliah, and no man knew"** (v4) — Hebrew לְהָמִית, an infinitive with no subject; no object "it".
- **"there came certain from Shechem … even fourscore men, having their beards shaven … with offerings and incense in their hand, to bring them" → "men came from Shechem … eighty men, with beards shaved and garments torn and cutting themselves, and a grain offering and frankincense in their hand, to bring"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲנָשִׁים "men"; no "and" between Shechem and Shiloh; מִתְגֹּדְדִים, hitpael "cutting themselves" (gashing, a rite of mourning); מִנְחָה "grain offering", singular; לְבוֹנָה "frankincense"; בֵּית יְהוָה "the house of Yahweh".
- **"weeping all along as he went" → "going along and weeping"** (v6) — Hebrew הֹלֵךְ הָלֹךְ וּבֹכֶה, literally "going, going and weeping": the infinitive absolute after the participle says that he went on. **"he said unto them" → "that he said to them"** — וַיְהִי … וַיֹּאמֶר.
- **"slew them, and cast them into the midst of the pit" → "slaughtered them into the midst of the pit"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁחָטֵם … אֶל־תּוֹךְ הַבּוֹר: the verb is "slaughter" (שָׁחַט, of animals cut at the throat), and "into the midst of the pit" goes with it in one clause; there is no verb "cast". What is meant, killed and thrown into the pit, is said by the Hebrew in this short way. בּוֹר "pit" (a cistern).
- **"Slay us not: for we have treasures in the field … So he forbare, and slew them not among their brethren" → "Do not put us to death, for we have hidden stores in the field … And he ceased, and did not put them to death in the midst of their brothers"** (v8) — Hebrew מַטְמֹנִים "hidden stores" (from טָמַן "hide in the ground"); חָדַל "cease"; בְּתוֹךְ אֲחֵיהֶם.
- **"the dead bodies of the men, whom he had slain because of Gedaliah, was it which Asa the king had made for fear of Baasha" → "the carcasses of the men whom he had struck beside Gedaliah is the one that King Asa made because of Baasha"** (v9) — Hebrew פִּגְרֵי "carcasses" (פֶּגֶר, its row); בְּיַד־גְּדַלְיָהוּ, literally "by the hand of Gedaliah": here יָד "side", "beside, along with Gedaliah" (KJV "because of"); "by the hand of" would say that Gedaliah struck them. The Septuagint reads "a great pit" (בּוֹר גָּדוֹל, from memory); the MT is kept. מִפְּנֵי "because of" (1 Kgs 15:22 tells of Asa building against Baasha). חֲלָלִים "the slain". The Hebrew has no verb in the main clause: "is" supplied.
- **"carried away captive all the residue of the people … whom Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard had committed to Gedaliah … and departed to go over to the Ammonites" → "took captive all the remnant of the people … over whom Nebuzaradan the chief of the guards had appointed Gedaliah … and went to pass over to the sons of Ammon"** (v10, 15) — Hebrew שָׁבָה "take captive"; שְׁאֵרִית "remnant"; אֲשֶׁר הִפְקִיד … אֶת־גְּדַלְיָהוּ, literally "whom … appointed Gedaliah": the relative is taken as "over whom", as הִפְקִיד אֶת־גְּדַלְיָהוּ in 40:7 ("over" supplied; KJV and others read "committed to Gedaliah"); רַב־טַבָּחִים "the chief of the guards", as 2 Kgs 25:8; עָבַר "pass over"; בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן "the sons of Ammon".
- **"the captains of the forces" → "the commanders of the forces"** (v11, 13, 16) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַחֲיָלִים, as 2 Kgs 25:23. **"all the evil"** — הָרָעָה "evil".
- **"by the great waters" → "by the many waters"** (v12) — Hebrew מַיִם רַבִּים, "many waters"; the pool of Gibeon (2 Sam 2:13) is named with another word (בְּרֵכָה).
- **"they were glad" → "that they rejoiced"** (v13) — וַיְהִי … וַיִּשְׂמָחוּ.
- **"cast about and returned" → "turned around, and returned"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיָּסֹבּוּ … וַיָּשֻׁבוּ.
- **"escaped from Johanan" → "escaped … from the face of Johanan"** (v15) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי, of fleeing.
- **"whom he had recovered from Ishmael … mighty men of war, and the women, and the children, and the eunuchs" → "whom he had brought back from Ishmael … grown men, men of war, and women and little ones and eunuchs"** (v16) — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב "bring back" (twice); the Hebrew does not name who "he" is in "he had brought back" (Johanan) and in "he had struck" (Ishmael); גְּבָרִים, adult males set beside women and little ones (the גֶּבֶר row), then אַנְשֵׁי הַמִּלְחָמָה "men of war"; טַף "little ones"; סָרִיס "eunuch" (also used of a court officer: its row).
- **"dwelt in the habitation of Chimham … to go to enter into Egypt" → "dwelt in Geruth Chimham … to go to enter Egypt"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּגֵרוּת כִּמְהָם, the qere (ketiv כמוהם, the same name spelled with ו); גֵּרוּת is taken as part of the name (it may mean "lodging place" of Chimham, 2 Sam 19:37–40). יָשַׁב "dwell". לָלֶכֶת לָבוֹא, two infinitives: "to go to enter".
- **"Because of the Chaldeans: for they were afraid of them" → "from the face of the Chaldeans; for they feared them"** (v18) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי הַכַּשְׂדִּים, of their flight (the one word מִפְּנֵי also stands in כִּי יָרְאוּ מִפְּנֵיהֶם "for they feared them", literally "feared from their face", as 2 Kgs 25:26).

### Notes
- **Murder of Gedaliah** — commemorated by Jewish fast (Tzom Gedaliah, 3 Tishri).
