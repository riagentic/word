# Jeremiah 41

[1] In the seventh month, ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧, son of Elishama, of the royal family, one of the chief officers of the king, came with ten men to ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ son of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧, at ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧. [4] On the day after the murder of ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧, before anyone knew of it,

[7] When they came into the city, ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧ and the men with him slaughtered them and cast them into a cistern.

[11] But when ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧ and all the leaders of the forces with him heard of all the evil that ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧ had done,

[14] So all the people whom ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ had carried away captive from ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧ turned around and came back and went to ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧.

- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; assassination of Gedaliah and its aftermath.
