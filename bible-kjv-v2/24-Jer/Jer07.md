# Jeremiah 7

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying:

[2] Stand in the gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and you shall call there this word, and you shall say: Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who come in by these gates to bow down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Make good your ways and your doings, and I will make you abide in this place.

[4] Do not trust for yourselves in the words of falsehood, saying, "The temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are these."

[5] For if you surely make good your ways and your doings, if you surely do judgment between a man and his neighbor,

[6] if you do not extort from the sojourner, the fatherless and the widow, and innocent blood do not shed in this place, and after other gods you do not walk, to evil for yourselves,

[7] then I will make you abide in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers, from age and to age.

[8] Behold, you are trusting for yourselves in the words of falsehood, so as not to profit.

[9] Will you steal, murder and commit adultery, and swear to falsehood, and make smoke to the Baal, and walk after other gods whom you have not known,

[10] and come and stand before me in this house over which my name is called, and say, "We are delivered," in order to do all these abominations?

[11] Is it a den of robbers that this house, over which my name is called, has become in your eyes? Also I, behold, I have seen it, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] For go, please, to my place that was in Shiloh, where I made my name abide at first, and see what I did to it because of the evil of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[13] And now, because you have done all these deeds, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I spoke to you, rising early and speaking, and you did not hear, and I called you, and you did not answer,

[14] then I will do to the house over which my name is called, in which you trust, and to the place that I gave to you and to your fathers, as I did to Shiloh.

[15] And I will throw you out from before my face, as I threw out all your brothers, all the seed of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[16] And you, do not pray for this people, and do not lift up for them a cry and a prayer, and do not entreat me, for I am not hearing you.

[17] Are you not seeing what they are doing in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?

[18] The sons are gathering wood, and the fathers are kindling the fire, and the women are kneading dough, to make cakes for the queen of the heavens, and to pour out drink offerings to other gods, in order to vex me.

[19] Is it me they are vexing? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. Is it not themselves, to the shame of their faces?

[20] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧: Behold, my anger and my fury is poured out on this place, on man and on beast, and on the tree of the field and on the fruit of the ground; and it shall burn and shall not be quenched.

[21] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Your burnt offerings add to your sacrifices, and eat flesh.

[22] For I did not speak with your fathers, and I did not command them, in the day that I brought them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, concerning the matters of burnt offering and sacrifice.

[23] But this word I commanded them, saying: Listen to my voice, and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you, and you shall be a people to me; and walk in all the way that I command you, so that it may go well with you.

[24] And they did not hear and did not incline their ear, and they walked in counsels, in the stubbornness of their evil heart, and they were backward and not forward.

[25] Even from the day that your fathers came out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ until this day — and I sent to you all my servants the prophets, daily rising early and sending.

[26] And they did not listen to me and did not incline their ear, and they made their neck hard; they did more evil than their fathers.

[27] And you shall speak to them all these words, and they will not listen to you; and you shall call to them, and they will not answer you.

[28] And you shall say to them: This is the nation that has not listened to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ its ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and has not taken discipline; faithfulness has perished and is cut off from their mouth.

[29] Shear your crown and throw it away,
and lift up on the bare heights a dirge;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has rejected and abandoned
the generation of his raging.

[30] For the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ have done the evil in my eyes, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; they have set their detestable things in the house over which my name is called, to defile it.

[31] And they have built the high places of the ⟦topheth|Tofet|Topheth⟧, which is in the valley of ⟦ben-hinnom|Ben Hinnom|Ben-hinnom⟧, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire, which I did not command, and it did not come up on my heart.

[32] Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and it shall no more be said, "the ⟦topheth|Tofet|Topheth⟧" and "the valley of ⟦ben-hinnom|Ben Hinnom|Ben-hinnom⟧," but "the valley of the Killing"; and they shall bury in ⟦topheth|Tofet|Topheth⟧ for want of a place.

[33] And the carcasses of this people shall be food for the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth, and there shall be none to make them tremble.

[34] And I will make cease from the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and from the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride; for the land shall become a ruin.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v3, 21) — יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, "Hosts" plain. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v20) — אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both names. **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v11, 13, 19, 30, 32) — נְאֻם יְהוָה.
- **Layout** — prose, as the Hebrew runs on in long clauses; v29 (short lines, a dirge) is set as verse. No outer quotation marks: the whole is Yahweh's word to the prophet; only the words others say (v4, 10, 32) are marked.
- **"The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying"** (v1) — הַדָּבָר אֲשֶׁר הָיָה אֶל, kept; לֵאמֹר "saying".
- **"Stand in the gate of the LORD'S house, and proclaim there this word, and say" → "Stand in the gate of the house of Yahweh, and you shall call there this word, and you shall say"** (v2) — וְקָרָאתָ … וְאָמַרְתָּ, two weqatal forms ("and you shall"); קָרָא "call". **"all ye of Judah, that enter in at these gates to worship the LORD" → "all Judah who come in by these gates to bow down to Yahweh"** — כָּל־יְהוּדָה; הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down".
- **"Amend your ways and your doings" → "Make good your ways and your doings"** (v3, 5) — הֵיטִיבוּ, hifil of יָטַב "be good"; מַעַלְלִים "doings". **"I will cause you to dwell" → "I will make you abide"** (v3, 7) — וַאֲשַׁכְּנָה / וְשִׁכַּנְתִּי, piel of שָׁכַן "abide" (also v12 "where I made my name abide"); the same consonants can be read "and I will abide with you" (a repointing, from memory; not followed).
- **"Trust ye not in lying words" → "Do not trust for yourselves in the words of falsehood"** (v4, 8) — אַל־תִּבְטְחוּ לָכֶם אֶל־דִּבְרֵי הַשֶּׁקֶר: לָכֶם "for yourselves" kept; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood", with the article. **"The temple of the LORD" ×3 "are these"** — הֵיכַל יְהוָה three times, as printed; הֵמָּה "are these" (literally "they").
- **"For if ye throughly amend … if ye throughly execute judgment" → "For if you surely make good … if you surely do judgment"** (v5) — הֵיטֵיב תֵּיטִיבוּ, עָשׂוֹ תַעֲשׂוּ: infinitive absolute with the verb, twice (literally "making good you make good", "doing you do"). מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; בֵּין אִישׁ וּבֵין רֵעֵהוּ "between a man and his neighbor".
- **"If ye oppress not the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow" → "if you do not extort from the sojourner, the fatherless and the widow"** (v6) — עָשַׁק "extort" (לָחַץ is "oppress"); גֵּר "sojourner"; the Hebrew names them first. **"and shed not innocent blood" → "and innocent blood do not shed"** — the Hebrew has אַל, the negative of a prohibition, in the middle of the condition (the other two clauses have לֹא): kept. **"to your hurt" → "to evil for yourselves"** — לְרַע לָכֶם.
- **"for ever and ever" → "from age and to age"** (v7) — לְמִן־עוֹלָם וְעַד־עוֹלָם, without the article, with וְ (the reviewer's row); it may belong to "gave" or to "make you abide": the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"that cannot profit" → "so as not to profit"** (v8) — לְבִלְתִּי הוֹעִיל. **"ye trust" → "you are trusting"** — בֹּטְחִים, participle.
- **"Will ye steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear falsely, and burn incense unto Baal" → "Will you steal, murder and commit adultery, and swear to falsehood, and make smoke to the Baal"** (v9) — the question is made of infinitives absolute (הֲגָנֹב רָצֹחַ וְנָאֹף …), with no finite verb: literally "Stealing, murdering and committing adultery …?"; רָצַח "murder"; לַשֶּׁקֶר "to falsehood" (as 5:2); קַטֵּר without an object, "make smoke"; לַבָּעַל with the article. **"whom ye know not" → "whom you have not known"** — perfect.
- **"which is called by my name" → "over which my name is called"** (v10, 11, 14, 30) — אֲשֶׁר נִקְרָא־שְׁמִי עָלָיו, literally "which my name has been called upon it" (the mark of ownership). **"We are delivered to do all these abominations" → "We are delivered," in order to do all these abominations"** — נִצַּלְנוּ; לְמַעַן עֲשׂוֹת "in order to do" (the purpose is set against them).
- **"Is this house … become a den of robbers in your eyes?" → "Is it a den of robbers that this house, over which my name is called, has become in your eyes?"** (v11) — הַמְעָרַת פָּרִצִים stands first: "a den of robbers" is what is asked. מְעָרָה is "cave" (Gen 23); פָּרִיץ "a violent one, one who breaks in"; "a den of robbers" kept, as the printed English has it. Quoted in Matt 21:13; Mark 11:17; Luke 19:46 (the Greek σπήλαιον λῃστῶν), not levelled. **"Behold, even I have seen it" → "Also I, behold, I have seen it"** — גַּם אָנֹכִי הִנֵּה רָאִיתִי.
- **"But go ye now" → "For go, please"** (v12) — כִּי "for"; נָא "please". **"where I set my name at the first" → "where I made my name abide at first"** — שִׁכַּנְתִּי (as v3). **"for the wickedness" → "because of the evil"** — מִפְּנֵי רָעַת, a cause; רָעָה "evil".
- **"all these works" → "all these deeds"** (v13) — הַמַּעֲשִׂים. **"rising up early and speaking, but ye heard not" → "rising early and speaking, and you did not hear"** — הַשְׁכֵּם וְדַבֵּר, two infinitives absolute (= again and again: as "rising early and sending", v25); each clause with וְ "and"; שָׁמַע "hear".
- **"Therefore will I do unto this house" → "then I will do to the house"** (v14) — וְעָשִׂיתִי, the weqatal that ends the "because" of v13; no "therefore"; לַבַּיִת "to the house" (no "this").
- **"cast you out of my sight … your brethren, even the whole seed of Ephraim" → "throw you out from before my face … your brothers, all the seed of Ephraim"** (v15) — הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; מֵעַל פָּנַי "from upon my face"; זֶרַע "seed".
- **"Therefore pray not thou" → "And you, do not pray"** (v16) — וְאַתָּה. **"cry nor prayer" → "a cry and a prayer"** — רִנָּה וּתְפִלָּה (רִנָּה "cry", as Solomon's prayer, 1 Kgs 8:28). **"neither make intercession to me" → "and do not entreat me"** — פָּגַע בְּ "entreat". **"I will not hear thee" → "I am not hearing you"** — אֵינֶנִּי שֹׁמֵעַ, participle.
- **"Seest thou not what they do" → "Are you not seeing what they are doing"** (v17) — participles.
- **"The children gather wood … knead their dough, to make cakes to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings … that they may provoke me to anger" → "The sons are gathering wood … kneading dough, to make cakes for the queen of the heavens, and to pour out drink offerings … in order to vex me"** (v18) — הַבָּנִים "the sons"; participles; בָּצֵק "dough" (no "their"); כַּוָּנִים "cakes" (a loanword; the kind is not known); מְלֶכֶת הַשָּׁמַיִם: the pointing is unusual (some manuscripts read מְלֶאכֶת "work", i.e. the host of the heavens: from memory), the vowels point to "queen"; who she is, is not said; שָׁמַיִם "heavens"; הַסֵּךְ נְסָכִים "pour out drink offerings"; הִכְעִיס "vex" (also v19).
- **"Do they provoke me to anger? … do they not provoke themselves to the confusion of their own faces?" → "Is it me they are vexing? … Is it not themselves, to the shame of their faces?"** (v19) — הַאֹתִי הֵם מַכְעִסִים … הֲלוֹא אֹתָם: "me" and "them" first; the second question has no verb. לְמַעַן בֹּשֶׁת פְּנֵיהֶם "to the shame of their faces" (לְמַעַן of the result; "on account of" of its row would not fit).
- **"mine anger and my fury shall be poured out" → "my anger and my fury is poured out"** (v20) — נִתֶּכֶת, a singular participle with two subjects; אַף "anger", חֵמָה "fury". **"upon man, and upon beast, and upon the trees of the field" → "on man and on beast, and on the tree of the field"** — הָאָדָם "man" (with the article: mankind); עֵץ singular. **"and shall not be quenched"** — kept.
- **"Put your burnt offerings unto your sacrifices, and eat flesh" → "Your burnt offerings add to your sacrifices, and eat flesh"** (v21) — סְפוּ "add" (of יָסַף, or סָפָה "sweep together"); "burnt offerings" first, as in the Hebrew. בָּשָׂר "flesh" (of meat).
- **"For I spake not unto your fathers, nor commanded them … concerning burnt offerings or sacrifices" → "For I did not speak with your fathers, and I did not command them … concerning the matters of burnt offering and sacrifice"** (v22) — as the Hebrew stands: לֹא־דִבַּרְתִּי … וְלֹא צִוִּיתִים … עַל־דִּבְרֵי עוֹלָה וָזָבַח; the qere הוֹצִיאִי "I brought out" (the ketiv הוציא is the infinitive without the suffix). How this stands beside the sacrifices of Exodus–Leviticus is not explained in the verse; many read it as "not first / not only" (v23 "But this word"): that is an interpretation and is not put into the verse.
- **"But this thing commanded I them, saying, Obey my voice, and I will be your God, and ye shall be my people" → "But this word I commanded them, saying: Listen to my voice, and I will be Elohim to you, and you shall be a people to me"** (v23) — כִּי אִם "but rather" (after the negatives of v22); הַדָּבָר "the word"; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of" ("obey" is not a Hebrew word here); לָכֶם לֵאלֹהִים … לִי לְעָם, literally "to you for Elohim … to me for a people". **"in all the ways that I have commanded you" → "in all the way that I command you"** — singular; אֲצַוֶּה imperfect. **"that it may be well unto you" → "so that it may go well with you"** — לְמַעַן יִיטַב לָכֶם.
- **"But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear" → "And they did not hear and did not incline their ear"** (v24) — שָׁמְעוּ "hear" without an object; הִטָּה אֹזֶן "incline the ear". **"walked in the counsels and in the imagination of their evil heart" → "walked in counsels, in the stubbornness of their evil heart"** — בְּמֹעֵצוֹת without article or suffix; שְׁרִרוּת לִבָּם הָרָע "the stubbornness of their evil heart" (שְׁרִרוּת "stubbornness", not "imagination"). **"and went backward" → "and they were backward"** — וַיִּהְיוּ לְאָחוֹר וְלֹא לְפָנִים.
- **"Since the day … I have even sent unto you all my servants the prophets, daily rising up early and sending them" → "Even from the day … until this day — and I sent to you all my servants the prophets, daily rising early and sending"** (v25) — לְמִן "even from"; וָאֶשְׁלַח keeps its "and" after the fronted time; אֲלֵיכֶם "to you"; עֲבָדַי "my servants" (עֶבֶד of Yahweh's men); יוֹם הַשְׁכֵּם וְשָׁלֹחַ, "day" + two infinitives absolute; "them" is not written.
- **"Yet they hearkened not unto me … but hardened their neck: they did worse than their fathers" → "And they did not listen to me … and they made their neck hard; they did more evil than their fathers"** (v26) — שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to" (also v27); הִקְשָׁה עֹרֶף "make hard the neck" (the הִקְשָׁה row); הֵרֵעוּ מֵאֲבוֹתָם "they did evil more than their fathers".
- **"Therefore thou shalt speak … thou shalt also call" → "And you shall speak … and you shall call"** (v27) — וְדִבַּרְתָּ … וְקָרָאתָ.
- **"This is a nation that obeyeth not the voice of the LORD their God, nor receiveth correction: truth is perished" → "This is the nation that has not listened to the voice of Yahweh its Elohim and has not taken discipline; faithfulness has perished"** (v28) — הַגּוֹי with the article; perfects; אֱלֹהָיו "its Elohim" (the nation); לָקַח מוּסָר "take discipline"; אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness" (as 5:1, 3). **"cut off from their mouth"** — kept.
- **"Cut off thine hair, O Jerusalem, and cast it away" → "Shear your crown and throw it away"** (v29) — גָּזִּי נִזְרֵךְ: נֵזֶר is the crown, and the hair of one kept apart (the nazir, Num 6:5–19): the hair is meant; "O Jerusalem" is not written (the feminine "you" is the city). **"take up a lamentation on high places" → "lift up on the bare heights a dirge"** — נָשָׂא "lift up"; שְׁפָיִם "bare heights"; קִינָה "dirge". **"rejected and forsaken the generation of his wrath" → "rejected and abandoned the generation of his raging"** — נָטַשׁ "abandon" (עָזַב is "forsake"); עֶבְרָה "raging" (literally "overflowing").
- **"For the children of Judah have done evil in my sight" → "For the sons of Judah have done the evil in my eyes"** (v30) — בְנֵי; הָרַע with the article; בְּעֵינַי. **"their abominations … to pollute it" → "their detestable things … to defile it"** — שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things" (תּוֹעֵבָה is "abomination", v10); טִמֵּא "defile".
- **"the high places of Tophet, which is in the valley of the son of Hinnom" → "the high places of the Topheth, which is in the valley of Ben-hinnom"** (v31) — בָּמוֹת הַתֹּפֶת, with the article; גֵּיא בֶן־הִנֹּם, the name as Josh 15:8 (the token; "son of Hinnom" in its meaning). **"to burn their sons … in the fire"** — שָׂרַף "burn". **"which I commanded them not, neither came it into my heart" → "which I did not command, and it did not come up on my heart"** — "them" is not written; עָלָה עַל לֵב "come up on the heart".
- **"the days come … that it shall no more be called Tophet, nor the valley of the son of Hinnom, but the valley of slaughter" → "days are coming … and it shall no more be said, 'the Topheth' and 'the valley of Ben-hinnom,' but 'the valley of the Killing'"** (v32) — הִנֵּה־יָמִים בָּאִים, no article; וְלֹא־יֵאָמֵר "and it shall not be said"; הֲרֵגָה "killing" (of הָרַג "kill"). **"till there be no place" → "for want of a place"** — מֵאֵין מָקוֹם, literally "from there being no place": because there is no other place, or until there is none (uncertain); the verse gives the plainer sense.
- **"the carcases of this people shall be meat for the fowls of the heaven" → "the carcasses of this people shall be food for the birds of the heavens"** (v33) — נִבְלַת, a collective singular ("carcass"); לְמַאֲכָל "for food"; עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם "the birds of the heavens"; בֶּהֱמַת הָאָרֶץ "the beasts of the earth". **"none shall fray them away" → "there shall be none to make them tremble"** — וְאֵין מַחֲרִיד.
- **"Then will I cause to cease … the voice of mirth" → "And I will make cease … the voice of joy"** (v34) — וְהִשְׁבַּתִּי "and I will make cease"; מִן "from"; שָׂשׂוֹן "joy", שִׂמְחָה "gladness" (rows; as 16:9; 25:10; 33:11); חָתָן "bridegroom", כַּלָּה "bride". **"the land shall be desolate" → "the land shall become a ruin"** — לְחָרְבָּה תִּהְיֶה, חָרְבָּה "ruin".

### Notes

- **Temple sermon** (v1–15) — v11 is quoted by Yeshua in the temple ("a den of robbers": Matt 21:13; Mark 11:17; Luke 19:46). Jer 26 tells of the same sermon.
- **v31–32 Topheth, valley of Ben-hinnom** — the valley south of Jerusalem; its name (גֵּי הִנֹּם, Greek *Gehenna*) became the NT word for the place of judgment.
- **v18 the queen of the heavens** — also 44:17–25; commonly taken as a goddess of the stars (Ishtar / Astarte): the text does not name her.
