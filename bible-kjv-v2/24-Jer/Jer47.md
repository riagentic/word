# Jeremiah 47

[1] That which came as the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet concerning the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, before ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ struck ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧.

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
Behold, waters are coming up from the north,
and they shall become a flooding torrent,
and they shall flood the land and its fullness,
the city and those who dwell in it;
and mankind shall cry out,
and every inhabitant of the land shall wail.

[3] At the sound of the stamping of the hoofs of his mighty ones,
at the quaking of his chariots, the din of his wheels,
fathers have not turned to sons,
from slackness of hands,

[4] because of the day that is coming
to lay waste all the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧,
to cut off from ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and from ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧
every survivor who helps;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is laying waste the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧,
the remnant of the coastland of ⟦caphtor|Kaftor|Caphtor⟧.

[5] Baldness has come to ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧,
⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ is undone,
the remnant of their valley;
how long will you gash yourself?

[6] Woe, sword of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
how long will you not be quiet?
Gather yourself into your sheath,
be at rest and be still.

[7] How can you be quiet,
when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded it?
Against ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ and against the shore of the sea,
there he has designated it.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** — v1 prose (the heading); v2–7 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents; "Thus says Yahweh" opens v2 on its own line. The quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark them.
- **"The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet against the Philistines, before that Pharaoh smote Gaza" → "That which came as the word of Yahweh to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הָיָה דְבַר־יְהוָה (the heading form of 46:1); אֶל "concerning" (lit. "to"); הִכָּה "strike" (its row; the city is not said to be taken).
- **"Behold, waters rise up out of the north, and shall be an overflowing flood, and shall overflow the land, and all that is therein; the city, and them that dwell therein: then the men shall cry, and all the inhabitants of the land shall howl" → "Behold, waters are coming up from the north, and they shall become a flooding torrent, and they shall flood the land and its fullness, the city and those who dwell in it; and mankind shall cry out, and every inhabitant of the land shall wail"** (v2) — Hebrew עֹלִים participle "are coming up" (עָלָה, as 46:7–8); נַחַל שׁוֹטֵף "a flooding torrent" (נַחַל "torrent" where the water itself is meant: its row), שָׁטַף "flood" twice (one root); אֶרֶץ וּמְלוֹאָהּ "the land and its fullness" (מְלֹא, its row; as Ps 24:1); וְ "and" each time (no "then"); הָאָדָם "mankind" (with the article; the אָדָם row); זָעַק "cry out"; הֵילִיל "wail" (its row); כֹּל יוֹשֵׁב singular "every inhabitant".
- **"At the noise of the stamping of the hoofs of his strong horses, at the rushing of his chariots, and at the rumbling of his wheels, the fathers shall not look back to their children for feebleness of hands" → "At the sound of the stamping of the hoofs of his mighty ones, at the quaking of his chariots, the din of his wheels, fathers have not turned to sons, from slackness of hands"** (v3) — Hebrew קוֹל "sound" (its row); שַׁעֲטָה, only here, "stamping"; אַבִּירָיו "his mighty ones" (its row: of animals; stallions are meant, as 8:16; 50:11); רַעַשׁ "quaking" (רָעַשׁ "quake", its row); הֲמוֹן "din" (the noise of a multitude, as Isa 5:14); לֹא־הִפְנוּ perfect "have not turned"; אָבוֹת … בָּנִים without article, "fathers … sons" (its row); מֵרִפְיוֹן יָדַיִם "from slackness of hands" (רָפָה "be slack", its row).
- **"Because of the day that cometh to spoil all the Philistines, and to cut off from Tyrus and Zidon every helper that remaineth: for the LORD will spoil the Philistines, the remnant of the country of Caphtor" → "because of the day that is coming to lay waste all the Philistines, to cut off from Tyre and from Sidon every survivor who helps; for Yahweh is laying waste the Philistines, the remnant of the coastland of Caphtor"** (v4) — Hebrew שָׁדַד "lay waste" (its row; the participle שֹׁדֵד "is laying waste"); הִכְרִית "cut off" (its row); לְצֹר וּלְצִידוֹן "for / from Tyre and from Sidon" (the לְ may also mean "belonging to Tyre and Sidon"); שָׂרִיד עֹזֵר "survivor, helper" (its row: "survivor"); אִי "coastland" (its row; Caphtor may be Crete); שְׁאֵרִית "remnant".
- **"Baldness is come upon Gaza; Ashkelon is cut off with the remnant of their valley: how long wilt thou cut thyself?" → "Baldness has come to Gaza, Ashkelon is undone, the remnant of their valley; how long will you gash yourself?"** (v5) — Hebrew קָרְחָה "baldness" (the shaved head of mourning); נִדְמְתָה "is undone" (as Isa 15:1 נִדְמָה: from דָּמָה "cease, be cut off", or "be silenced"); שְׁאֵרִית עִמְקָם "the remnant of their valley" as printed, set by the accents with Ashkelon (the Septuagint reads "the remnant of the Anakim", עֲנָקִים, from memory); תִּתְגּוֹדָדִי "gash yourself" (feminine singular, the cutting of the skin in mourning, as 16:6; 41:5; 48:37; Deut 14:1).
- **"O thou sword of the LORD, how long will it be ere thou be quiet? put up thyself into thy scabbard, rest, and be still" → "Woe, sword of Yahweh, how long will you not be quiet? Gather yourself into your sheath, be at rest and be still"** (v6) — Hebrew הוֹי "Woe" (the prophets' row; here a cry addressed to the sword: "Ah" / "Ho" are the other renderings); no "O"; עַד־אָנָה לֹא תִשְׁקֹטִי "how long will you not be quiet" (שָׁקַט "be quiet", its row); הֵאָסְפִי "gather yourself" (נֶאֱסַף, its row); תַּעַר "sheath" (the same word is "razor", Num 6:5: its row; here the sheath of the sword, as 1 Sam 17:51); הֵרָגְעִי "be at rest"; דֹּמִּי "be still" (דָּמַם, its row).
- **"How can it be quiet, seeing the LORD hath given it a charge against Ashkelon, and against the sea shore? there hath he appointed it" → "How can you be quiet, when Yahweh has commanded it? Against Ashkelon and against the shore of the sea, there he has designated it"** (v7) — Hebrew תִּשְׁקֹטִי "you be quiet" (second person, to the sword, as v6; the versions have "it", from memory) with לָהּ "(to) it" and יְעָדָהּ "he has designated it" in the third person, as printed; צִוָּה "command" (its row); חוֹף הַיָּם "the shore of the sea"; יָעַד "designate" (its row).

### Notes
- **Caphtor** — the land the Philistines came from (Amos 9:7; Deut 2:23), commonly taken to be Crete.
- **Gaza, Ashkelon** — two of the five Philistine cities; the Pharaoh who struck Gaza is not named.
