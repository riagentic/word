# Jeremiah 2

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] Go, and call in the ears of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

I remember for you the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ of your youth,
the love of your bridehood,
your walking after me in the wilderness,
in a land not sown.

[3] ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the first of his yield;
all who ate him would be guilty;
evil would come upon them,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, and all the clans of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

What injustice did your fathers find in me,
that they went far from me,
and went after the vapor and became vapor?

[6] And they did not say, "Where is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who brought us up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
who made us walk in the wilderness,
in a land of steppe and pit,
in a land of dry land and the shadow of death,
in a land that no man passed through,
and where mankind did not dwell?"

[7] And I brought you into the land of the garden land,
to eat its fruit and its good;
and you came in and defiled my land,
and my inheritance you made an abomination.

[8] The priests did not say, "Where is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"
and those who handle the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ did not know me,
and the shepherds transgressed against me,
and the prophets prophesied by the Baal,
and after things that do not profit they went.

[9] Therefore I will yet contend with you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and with the sons of your sons I will contend.

[10] For pass over to the coastlands of Kittim and see,
and to ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧ send, and consider well,
and see whether there has been such a thing.

[11] Has a nation exchanged gods?
And they are not gods!
And my people has exchanged its glory
for what does not profit.

[12] Be appalled, heavens, at this,
and shudder, be dried up exceedingly, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] For two evils my people has done:
me they have forsaken,
the fountain of living waters,
to hew out for themselves cisterns,
broken cisterns that cannot hold the water.

[14] Is ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a slave? Or is he one born in the house?
Why has he become plunder?

[15] Upon him young lions roar;
they have given their voice;
and they have made his land a thing that appalls;
his cities are burned, without one who dwells.

[16] Also the sons of ⟦memphis|Nof|Memphis⟧ and ⟦tahpanhes|Tachpanches|Tahpanhes⟧
shall graze on the crown of your head.

[17] Is it not this that does it to you,
your forsaking ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
at the time when he made you walk in the way?

[18] And now, what have you to do with the way to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
to drink the waters of ⟦shihor-nile|Shichor|the Nile⟧?
And what have you to do with the way to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧,
to drink the waters of the River?

[19] Your evil shall discipline you,
and your turnings away shall reprove you;
and know and see that it is evil and bitter,
your forsaking ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and the dread of me is not upon you,
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts.

[20] For from of old I broke your yoke,
I tore off your bonds,
and you said, "I will not overstep."
For on every high hill
and under every luxuriant tree
you are stooping, playing the prostitute.

[21] And I, I planted you a choice vine,
wholly a seed of truth;
and how have you turned on me
into the turned-aside shoots of a foreign vine?

[22] For if you wash with natron
and multiply soap for yourself,
your iniquity is a stain before me,
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[23] How can you say, "I am not defiled;
after the Baals I have not gone"?
See your way in the valley;
know what you have done —
a swift young she-camel entangling her ways,

[24] a wild donkey taught by the wilderness,
in the desire of her soul she pants after the wind;
her heat — who can turn it back?
All who seek her will not weary themselves;
in her month they will find her.

[25] Hold back your foot from going barefoot,
and your throat from thirst.
And you said, "It is hopeless! No!
For I have loved strangers,
and after them I will go."

[26] As the shame of a thief when he is found,
so the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is put to shame:
they, their kings, their princes,
and their priests and their prophets,

[27] who say to the tree, "You are my father,"
and to the stone, "You have given birth to us."
For they have turned to me the back of the neck, and not the face;
and in the time of their evil they say,
"Arise and save us!"

[28] And where are your gods that you made for yourself?
Let them arise, if they can save you
in the time of your evil;
for as the number of your cities
have your gods been, ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[29] Why do you contend with me?
All of you have transgressed against me,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[30] In vain have I struck your sons;
discipline they did not take;
your sword has devoured your prophets
like a destroying lion.

[31] You, the generation — see the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
Have I been a wilderness to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
or a land of deep gloom?
Why have my people said, "We roam free;
we will not come again to you"?

[32] Can a virgin forget her ornaments,
a bride her sashes?
And my people have forgotten me
days without number.

[33] How well you make your way
to seek love!
Therefore even the evil ones
you have taught your ways.

[34] Also on your corners is found
the blood of the souls of the needy, the clear;
not in breaking in did you find them,
but upon all these.

[35] And you said, "I am clear;
surely his anger has turned back from me."
Behold, I will enter into judgment with you
for your saying, "I have not sinned."

[36] Why do you go about so much,
to change your way?
Also because of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ you shall be ashamed,
as you were ashamed because of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.

[37] Also from this one you shall go out
with your hands upon your head;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has rejected your trusts,
and you shall not prosper by them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17, 19) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים. **"the Lord GOD of hosts" → "the Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת: both names (token `adonai-yhwh`) and "of Hosts" (plain words, as the earlier books); **"the Lord GOD" → "the Lord Yahweh"** (v22).
- **Layout** — v2b–37 are poetry: one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents. The outer quotation marks are dropped: from v2 to v37 Yahweh speaks through the prophet; quotation marks now stand only for the words the people say.
- **"Moreover the word of the LORD came to me" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — the prophets' formula, one wording.
- **"Go and cry in the ears of Jerusalem" → "Go, and call in the ears of Jerusalem"** (v2) — Hebrew הָלֹךְ, infinitive absolute as a command, and וְקָרָאתָ "and call" (קָרָא); בְאָזְנֵי "in the ears of" (its row). **"I remember thee, the kindness of thy youth, the love of thine espousals, when thou wentest after me" → "I remember for you the chesed of your youth, the love of your bridehood, your walking after me"** — זָכַרְתִּי לָךְ, literally "I remember for you" (to your credit); חֶסֶד token; כְּלוּלֹת, only here, the state of a bride ("betrothal"); לֶכְתֵּךְ, infinitive with suffix, "your walking".
- **"Israel was holiness unto the LORD, and the firstfruits of his increase: all that devour him shall offend; evil shall come upon them" → "Israel was holy to Yahweh, the first of his yield; all who ate him would be guilty; evil would come upon them"** (v3) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ, the noun "holiness" as predicate (the קֹדֶשׁ row: "holy"); "was" supplied: the verse looks back to the youth of v2. רֵאשִׁית "first"; תְּבוּאָה "yield" (as Deut 14:22, 28); there is no "and" between. אֹכְלָיו "those eating him" (Israel); יֶאְשָׁמוּ (אָשֵׁם "be guilty", its row) and תָּבֹא are imperfects: what used to happen; a present ("all who eat him are guilty") is also possible.
- **"O house of Jacob" → "house of Jacob"** (v4) — the Hebrew has no "O"; מִשְׁפְּחוֹת "clans".
- **"What iniquity have your fathers found in me, that they are gone far from me, and have walked after vanity, and are become vain?" → "What injustice did your fathers find in me, that they went far from me, and went after the vapor and became vapor?"** (v5) — Hebrew עָוֶל "injustice" (its row; not עָוֹן "iniquity"); הַהֶבֶל "the vapor" with the article, and the verb of the same root, וַיֶּהְבָּלוּ "and became vapor" (the הֶבֶל row: "vapor", the breath that passes; "vanity" here).
- **"Neither said they, Where is the LORD … that led us through the wilderness, through a land of deserts and of pits, through a land of drought, and of the shadow of death, through a land that no man passed through, and where no man dwelt?" → "And they did not say, Where is Yahweh … who made us walk in the wilderness, in a land of steppe and pit, in a land of dry land and the shadow of death, in a land that no man passed through, and where mankind did not dwell?"** (v6) — Hebrew הַמּוֹלִיךְ "who made us walk" (the הוֹלִיךְ row); עֲרָבָה "steppe" and צִיָּה "dry land" (as Isa 35:1); שׁוּחָה "pit"; צַלְמָוֶת "the shadow of death" as pointed (צֵל + מָוֶת; read צַלְמוּת "deep darkness": the Job 3 row); אִישׁ "man" and אָדָם "mankind" kept apart.
- **"I brought you into a plentiful country, to eat the fruit thereof and the goodness thereof; but when ye entered, ye defiled my land, and made mine heritage an abomination" → "I brought you into the land of the garden land, to eat its fruit and its good; and you came in and defiled my land, and my inheritance you made an abomination"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ הַכַּרְמֶל "the land of the garden land" (כַּרְמֶל as Isa 10:18; with the article; the same letters name Mount Carmel); טוּבָהּ "its good"; וַתָּבֹאוּ "and you came in" (not "when"); טִמֵּא "defile"; נַחֲלָה "inheritance" (its row).
- **"they that handle the law knew me not: the pastors also transgressed against me, and the prophets prophesied by Baal, and walked after things that do not profit" → "those who handle the torah did not know me, and the shepherds transgressed against me, and the prophets prophesied by the Baal, and after things that do not profit they went"** (v8) — Hebrew תֹפְשֵׂי הַתּוֹרָה "those who grasp the torah"; רֹעִים "shepherds" (its row); פָּשַׁע בְּ "transgress against" (as Isa 1:2); בַבַּעַל, with the article (the Baal row); לֹא־יוֹעִלוּ "they do not profit", in the Hebrew order. Each line opens with וְ "and".
- **"I will yet plead with you … and with your children's children will I plead" → "I will yet contend with you … and with the sons of your sons I will contend"** (v9) — Hebrew רִיב "contend" (its row: a case at law); בְּנֵי בְנֵיכֶם "sons of your sons".
- **"pass over the isles of Chittim … and consider diligently" → "pass over to the coastlands of Kittim … and consider well"** (v10) — Hebrew אִיֵּי "coastlands" (its row); הִתְבּוֹנֵן מְאֹד, hitpolel of בִּין "look closely" (as Isa 14:16 "consider"), "very"; הֵן "whether" (also "behold").
- **"Hath a nation changed their gods, which are yet no gods? but my people have changed their glory for that which doth not profit" → "Has a nation exchanged gods? And they are not gods! And my people has exchanged its glory for what does not profit"** (v11) — Hebrew הַהֵימִיר "has … exchanged" (הֵמִיר, the Lev 27:10 row); אֱלֹהִים of the nations' gods: plain "gods", without "their"; וְהֵמָּה לֹא אֱלֹהִים "and they are not gods"; כְּבוֹדוֹ "its glory" (the people's glory: Yahweh; the scribes' list of corrections names an earlier "my glory": from memory).
- **"Be astonished, O ye heavens, at this, and be horribly afraid, be ye very desolate" → "Be appalled, heavens, at this, and shudder, be dried up exceedingly"** (v12) — Hebrew שֹׁמּוּ, שָׁמֵם "be appalled" (its row, of persons); no "O"; שַׂעֲרוּ "shudder" (the hair standing on end, root of שֵׂעָר "hair"); חָרְבוּ מְאֹד, חָרֵב "be dried up" (its row).
- **"they have forsaken me the fountain of living waters, and hewed them out cisterns, broken cisterns, that can hold no water" → "me they have forsaken, the fountain of living waters, to hew out for themselves cisterns, broken cisterns that cannot hold the water"** (v13) — Hebrew אֹתִי עָזְבוּ, "me" first; מְקוֹר מַיִם חַיִּים "the fountain of living waters": מָקוֹר "fountain" (its row), מַיִם חַיִּים "living water", running water from a spring (its row; the plural "waters" of the Hebrew kept in this phrase, one wording for Jeremiah, also 17:13); לַחְצֹב, infinitive, "to hew out" (not a second finite verb); בֹּארוֹת "cisterns" (pits hewn to hold rain water); הַמָּיִם "the water".
- **"is he a homeborn slave? why is he spoiled?" → "Or is he one born in the house? Why has he become plunder?"** (v14) — Hebrew אִם "or"; יְלִיד בַּיִת "born in the house" (its row); הָיָה לָבַז "has become plunder" (בַּז, its row).
- **"The young lions roared upon him, and yelled, and they made his land waste: his cities are burned without inhabitant" → "Upon him young lions roar; they have given their voice; and they have made his land a thing that appalls; his cities are burned, without one who dwells"** (v15) — Hebrew יִשְׁאֲגוּ, imperfect, "roar"; נָתְנוּ קוֹלָם "they have given their voice"; שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (the Deut 28:37 row; שְׁמָמָה "desolation" is another word, 4:27); נִצְּתוּ is the qere, "are burned" (from יָצַת; the written נצתה, singular, may be read "is laid waste", from נָצָה); מִבְּלִי יֹשֵׁב "without one who dwells".
- **"have broken the crown of thy head" → "shall graze on the crown of your head"** (v16) — Hebrew יִרְעוּךְ קָדְקֹד, pointed from רָעָה "graze" (they shall feed on you, the crown); read from רָעַע it is "shall break". Uncertain. וְתַחְפַּנְחֵס is the qere (the written form lacks the ח). בְּנֵי "sons of".
- **"Hast thou not procured this unto thyself, in that thou hast forsaken the LORD thy God, when he led thee by the way?" → "Is it not this that does it to you, your forsaking Yahweh your Elohim at the time when he made you walk in the way?"** (v17) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא־זֹאת תַּעֲשֶׂה־לָּךְ: "this" is the subject ("does it to you"; the verb may also be read "you do this to yourself"); עָזְבֵךְ "your forsaking", an infinitive; מוֹלִיכֵךְ "making you walk" (its row).
- **"what hast thou to do in the way of Egypt, to drink the waters of Sihor? … the waters of the river?" → "what have you to do with the way to Egypt, to drink the waters of Shichor? … the waters of the River?"** (v18) — Hebrew מַה־לָּךְ לְדֶרֶךְ, literally "what to you for the way of" (what business have you on it); שִׁחוֹר (token); נָהָר "the River" (the Euphrates is meant): the Hebrew does not name the Euphrates, so the `euphrates` token is removed.
- **"Thine own wickedness shall correct thee, and thy backslidings shall reprove thee … and that my fear is not in thee" → "Your evil shall discipline you, and your turnings away shall reprove you … and the dread of me is not upon you"** (v19) — Hebrew רָעָה "evil"; יִסֵּר "discipline" and הוֹכִיחַ "reprove" (their rows); מְשֻׁבוֹתַיִךְ "your turnings away" (מְשׁוּבָה, from שׁוּב: the WORDS-HE row for Jeremiah); וּדְעִי וּרְאִי "and know and see"; עָזְבֵךְ "your forsaking" (an infinitive, as v17); פַחְדָּתִי, only here, "my dread" (of פַּחַד), with אֵלַיִךְ "toward / upon you".
- **"For of old time I have broken thy yoke, and burst thy bands; and thou saidst, I will not transgress; when upon every high hill and under every green tree thou wanderest, playing the harlot" → "For from of old I broke your yoke, I tore off your bonds, and you said, I will not overstep. For on every high hill and under every luxuriant tree you are stooping, playing the prostitute"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁבַרְתִּי · נִתַּקְתִּי are pointed as "I broke, I tore off" (the forms may also be old spellings of "you broke, you tore off", as the Septuagint reads: from memory); מֵעוֹלָם "from of old" (its row); אֶעֱבוֹר is the qere, "I will not overstep" (עָבַר of a command, its row); the written form אעבד reads "I will not serve". רַעֲנָן "luxuriant" (its row); צֹעָה זֹנָה, two participles, "stooping, playing the prostitute" (the זָנָה row).
- **"Yet I had planted thee a noble vine, wholly a right seed: how then art thou turned into the degenerate plant of a strange vine unto me?" → "And I, I planted you a choice vine, wholly a seed of truth; and how have you turned on me into the turned-aside shoots of a foreign vine?"** (v21) — Hebrew וְאָנֹכִי, the pronoun written; שֹׂרֵק "choice vine" (a red grape, as the valley of Sorek); זֶרַע אֱמֶת "a seed of truth" (the אֱמֶת row); נֶהְפַּכְתְּ לִי "you have turned to me / against me"; סוּרֵי הַגֶּפֶן נָכְרִיָּה, literally "the turned-aside ones of the vine, a foreign one" (סוּר "turn aside"): "shoots" supplied; uncertain.
- **"For though thou wash thee with nitre, and take thee much sope, yet thine iniquity is marked before me" → "For if you wash with natron and multiply soap for yourself, your iniquity is a stain before me"** (v22) — Hebrew כִּבֵּס "wash" (its row); נֶתֶר "natron" (a mineral alkali) and בֹּרִית "soap" (an alkali from plants); נִכְתָּם, only here, "is stained". **"saith the Lord GOD" → "declares the Lord Yahweh"** — נְאֻם, one wording.
- **"I am not polluted … see thy way in the valley … thou art a swift dromedary traversing her ways" → "I am not defiled … See your way in the valley … a swift young she-camel entangling her ways"** (v23) — Hebrew נִטְמֵאתִי (the נִטְמָא row: "be defiled"); הַבְּעָלִים "the Baals" (its row); גַּיְא "valley" (its row); בִּכְרָה "young she-camel"; מְשָׂרֶכֶת, only here, "entangling" (running this way and that); uncertain.
- **"A wild ass used to the wilderness, that snuffeth up the wind at her pleasure; in her occasion who can turn her away? all they that seek her will not weary themselves" → "a wild donkey taught by the wilderness, in the desire of her soul she pants after the wind; her heat — who can turn it back? All who seek her will not weary themselves"** (v24) — Hebrew לִמֻּד מִדְבָּר "taught of the wilderness"; בְּאַוַּת נַפְשָׁהּ "in the desire of her soul" (the אַוָּה row; נַפְשָׁהּ is the qere, the written form נפשו "his soul"); שָׁאֲפָה "pants after" (its row); תַּאֲנָתָהּ, only here, "her heat" (her time of mating; uncertain); מִי יְשִׁיבֶנָּה "who can turn it back" (hifil of שׁוּב); יִיעָפוּ "grow weary" (the יָעֵף row).
- **"Withhold thy foot from being unshod, and thy throat from thirst: but thou saidst, There is no hope: no; for I have loved strangers" → "Hold back your foot from going barefoot, and your throat from thirst. And you said, It is hopeless! No! For I have loved strangers"** (v25) — Hebrew מִנְעִי "hold back" (the מָנַע row); וּגְרוֹנֵךְ is the qere (the written form lacks the ו after ר); וַתֹּאמְרִי "and you said"; נוֹאָשׁ "it is hopeless" (nifal of יאשׁ); זָרִים "strangers" (its row).
- **"As the thief is ashamed when he is found, so is the house of Israel ashamed; they, their kings, their princes" → "As the shame of a thief when he is found, so the house of Israel is put to shame: they, their kings, their princes"** (v26) — Hebrew כְּבֹשֶׁת "as the shame of"; הֹבִישׁוּ, hifil of בּוֹשׁ, "are put to shame"; שַׂר "prince" (its row).
- **"Saying to a stock, Thou art my father; and to a stone, Thou hast brought me forth: for they have turned their back unto me" → "who say to the tree, You are my father, and to the stone, You have given birth to us. For they have turned to me the back of the neck, and not the face"** (v27) — Hebrew לָעֵץ "to the tree" (עֵץ "tree, wood"; with the article, as לָאֶבֶן "to the stone"); יְלִדְתָּנוּ is the qere, "you have given birth to us" (the written form ילדתני "to me"); עֹרֶף "the back of the neck" (its row). **"in the time of their trouble" → "in the time of their evil"** — רָעָתָם (the רָעָה row; also v28).
- **"O Judah" → "Judah"** (v28) — no "O". **"if they can save thee" → "if they can save you"** — אִם־יוֹשִׁיעוּךָ "whether they will save you"; כִּי מִסְפַּר עָרֶיךָ הָיוּ אֱלֹהֶיךָ "for as the number of your cities have your gods been" (the "as" supplied).
- **"Wherefore will ye plead with me?" → "Why do you contend with me?"** (v29) — Hebrew רִיב אֶל (as v9).
- **"In vain have I smitten your children; they received no correction" → "In vain have I struck your sons; discipline they did not take"** (v30) — Hebrew לַשָּׁוְא, literally "for emptiness" (the שָׁוְא row); הִכָּה "strike" (its row); בְּנֵיכֶם "your sons"; מוּסָר "discipline" (its row); לָקַח "take" (its row). **"like a destroying lion"** — כְּאַרְיֵה מַשְׁחִית.
- **"O generation, see ye the word of the LORD … a land of darkness? wherefore say my people, We are lords; we will come no more unto thee?" → "You, the generation — see the word of Yahweh! … a land of deep gloom? Why have my people said, We roam free; we will not come again to you?"** (v31) — Hebrew הַדּוֹר אַתֶּם "the generation, you"; מַאְפֵּלְיָה, only here, "deep gloom" (from the root of אֹפֶל "gloom"; the ending is read as the divine short name, "gloom of Yah", an intensive: uncertain); אָמְרוּ, perfect; רַדְנוּ, from רוּד "roam free" (not "be lords").
- **"Can a maid forget her ornaments, or a bride her attire?" → "Can a virgin forget her ornaments, a bride her sashes?"** (v32) — Hebrew בְּתוּלָה "virgin" (its row); עֲדִי "ornaments" (its row); קִשֻּׁרִים "sashes" (as Isa 3:20); no "or".
- **"Why trimmest thou thy way to seek love? therefore hast thou also taught the wicked ones thy ways" → "How well you make your way to seek love! Therefore even the evil ones you have taught your ways"** (v33) — Hebrew מַה־תֵּיטִבִי "how you make good"; לִמַּדְתְּ is the qere, "you have taught" (the written form reads like "I taught", or an old spelling of "you taught"); אֶת־הָרָעוֹת, feminine plural, "the evil ones": evil women, or "evil things" (you have accustomed your ways to evil): open.
- **"Also in thy skirts is found the blood of the souls of the poor innocents: I have not found it by secret search, but upon all these" → "Also on your corners is found the blood of the souls of the needy, the clear; not in breaking in did you find them, but upon all these"** (v34) — Hebrew נִמְצְאוּ, plural, "are found" (with the plural "souls"); כְּנָפַיִךְ "your corners" (lit. "wings": the כָּנָף row of a garment); אֶבְיוֹנִים "needy" (its row) and נְקִיִּים "clear" (of blood: "innocent", the נָקִי row), two words; מְצָאתִים "you found them" (the thieves were not caught breaking in, Exod 22:2, so their killing was not lawful); כִּי עַל־כָּל־אֵלֶּה, literally "but upon all these": uncertain ("but because of all these").
- **"Yet thou sayest, Because I am innocent, surely his anger shall turn from me. Behold, I will plead with thee" → "And you said, I am clear; surely his anger has turned back from me. Behold, I will enter into judgment with you"** (v35) — Hebrew וַתֹּאמְרִי "and you said"; כִּי introduces the words spoken ("because" is also possible); נִקֵּיתִי "I am clear" (the נָקָה row); שָׁב, perfect, "has turned back"; נִשְׁפָּט אוֹתָךְ "enter into judgment with you" (the נִשְׁפַּט עִם row; אוֹתָךְ = "with you").
- **"Why gaddest thou about so much to change thy way? thou also shalt be ashamed of Egypt" → "Why do you go about so much, to change your way? Also because of Egypt you shall be ashamed"** (v36) — Hebrew מַה־תֵּזְלִי, pointed from אָזַל "go about" (from זָלַל "be cheap": "why do you hold it so cheap"); בּוֹשׁ מִן "be ashamed because of" (as Isa 1:29).
- **"Yea, thou shalt go forth from him … for the LORD hath rejected thy confidences" → "Also from this one you shall go out … for Yahweh has rejected your trusts"** (v37) — Hebrew גַּם מֵאֵת זֶה "also from this one" (Egypt); מָאַס בְּ "reject"; מִבְטַחַיִךְ "your trusts" (what you trust in, as Ps 65:5); הִצְלִיחַ "prosper".

### Notes
- **v13** — "the fountain of living waters": Yahweh's name for himself again in Jer 17:13.
- **v2–3** — the wilderness years seen as Israel's youth and bridal time (Hos 2:15).
