# Jeremiah 30

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying,

[2] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: Write for yourself all the words that I have spoken to you in a book.

[3] For behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will restore the fortunes of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will bring them back to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they shall possess it.

[4] And these are the words that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[5] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
A sound of trembling we have heard,
dread, and there is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[6] Ask, please, and see
whether a male gives birth.
Why have I seen every mighty man
with his hands on his loins like a woman giving birth,
and all faces are turned to paleness?

[7] Woe! For great is that day,
there is none like it;
and it is a time of distress for ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and out of it he shall be saved.

[8] And it shall be in that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
I will break his yoke from upon your neck,
and your bonds I will tear off,
and strangers shall no more make him serve.

[9] And they shall serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
and David their king,
whom I will raise up for them.

[10] And you, do not fear, my servant ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and do not be dismayed, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
for behold, I am saving you from afar,
and your seed from the land of their captivity;
and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ shall return and be quiet and be at ease,
and there shall be none who makes him tremble.

[11] For I am with you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to save you;
for I will make a full end among all the nations
where I have scattered you;
only of you I will not make a full end,
and I will discipline you with judgment,
and I will surely not leave you clear.

[12] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
Incurable is your breaking,
grievous is your blow.

[13] There is none judging your cause, for a sore;
remedies, new skin, there are none for you.

[14] All your lovers have forgotten you;
you they do not seek;
for with the blow of an enemy I have struck you,
the discipline of a cruel one,
because of the abundance of your iniquity;
your sins have become mighty.

[15] Why do you cry out over your breaking?
Incurable is your pain.
Because of the abundance of your iniquity,
your sins have become mighty,
I have done these things to you.

[16] Therefore all who devour you shall be devoured,
and all your adversaries, all of them, shall go into captivity;
and those who pillage you shall become a pillage,
and all who plunder you I will give for plunder.

[17] For I will bring up new flesh for you,
and from your blows I will heal you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
for "Thrust away" they have called you:
"She is ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧; there is none seeking her."

[18] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
Behold, I am restoring the fortunes of the tents of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and on his dwellings I will have compassion;
and the city shall be built on her mound,
and the citadel shall dwell upon its right.

[19] And out of them shall go out thanksgiving
and the sound of those who make merry;
and I will multiply them, and they shall not become few;
and I will make them honored, and they shall not be small.

[20] And his sons shall be as of old,
and his congregation shall be established before me,
and I will visit upon all who oppress him.

[21] And his noble one shall be from him,
and his ruler shall go out from his midst;
and I will bring him near, and he shall approach me;
for who is this who has pledged his heart to approach me?
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] And you shall be a people to me,
and I, I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to you.

[23] Behold, the storm of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
fury has gone out,
a whirling tempest;
upon the head of the wicked it shall whirl.

[24] The burning of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall not turn back
until he has done and until he has established
the devisings of his heart;
in the latter days you shall give heed to it.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v8) — plain words, as the prophets' row. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v2, 9, 22). נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v3, 8, 10, 11, 17, 21); אָמַר יְהוָה "says Yahweh" (v3) is another word, kept apart. No "O" before a name (v10; the reviewer's vocative row).
- **Layout** — v1–4 are prose; from v5 (after "For thus says Yahweh") to v24 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order. v13 is two lines, as its accents divide it (the old layout made three lines out of a rearranged verse).
- **"The word that came to Jeremiah from the LORD, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר אֲשֶׁר הָיָה … לֵאמֹר; "saying" kept (its row; also v2).
- **"Write thee all the words … in a book" → "Write for yourself all the words … in a book"** (v2) — Hebrew כְּתָב־לְךָ, the imperative with לְךָ (the reviewer's row gives the English reflexive; "write yourself" is not English, so "for yourself"); אֶל־סֵפֶר "into a book", in the Hebrew order at the end.
- **"that I will bring again the captivity of my people Israel and Judah" → "and I will restore the fortunes of my people Israel and Judah"** (v3) — Hebrew וְשַׁבְתִּי אֶת־שְׁבוּת, literally "and I will turn back the captivity / the turning of": the reviewer row "restore the fortunes" (traditional "turn the captivity"; also v18). וְ "and", no "that / when". יָרַשׁ "possess" (its row).
- **"concerning Israel and concerning Judah" → "to Israel and to Judah"** (v4) — Hebrew אֶל … וְאֶל "to … and to"; the verse opens with וְ "And".
- **"We have heard a voice of trembling, of fear, and not of peace" → "A sound of trembling we have heard, dread, and there is no shalom"** (v5) — the Hebrew order; קוֹל "sound / voice"; חֲרָדָה "trembling" (noun of חָרַד, its row); פַּחַד "dread" (its row, as Deut 11:25); וְאֵין שָׁלוֹם "and there is no shalom" (token `shalom`).
- **"Ask ye now, and see whether a man doth travail with child? wherefore do I see every man with his hands on his loins, as a woman in travail, and all faces are turned into paleness?" → "Ask, please, and see whether a male gives birth. Why have I seen every mighty man with his hands on his loins like a woman giving birth, and all faces are turned to paleness?"** (v6) — Hebrew שַׁאֲלוּ־נָא "Ask, please" (נָא kept); אִם־יֹלֵד זָכָר "whether a male gives birth" (a participle; זָכָר "male"); רָאִיתִי perfect "have I seen"; גֶּבֶר "mighty man" (the standing decision); חֲלָצָיו "his loins" (its row); כַּיּוֹלֵדָה "like a woman giving birth"; יֵרָקוֹן "paleness" (the word of the Deut 28:22 sickness row, there of grain; here of faces, a sickly yellow-green).
- **"Alas! for that day is great, so that none is like it: it is even the time of Jacob's trouble; but he shall be saved out of it" → "Woe! For great is that day, there is none like it; and it is a time of distress for Jacob, and out of it he shall be saved"** (v7) — Hebrew הוֹי "Woe" (the prophets' row); מֵאַיִן כָּמֹהוּ "there is none like it" (literally "from there being none like it"); וְעֵת־צָרָה הִיא לְיַעֲקֹב "and it is a time of distress for Jacob" (צָרָה "distress", its row); וּמִמֶּנָּה יִוָּשֵׁעַ "and out of it he shall be saved" — וְ "and", no "but" written.
- **"For it shall come to pass in that day … that I will break his yoke from off thy neck, and will burst thy bonds, and strangers shall no more serve themselves of him" → "And it shall be in that day … I will break his yoke from upon your neck, and your bonds I will tear off, and strangers shall no more make him serve"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהָיָה בַיּוֹם הַהוּא "And it shall be in that day" (the formula row); עֻלּוֹ "his yoke" and צַוָּארֶךָ "your neck": the shift of person is in the Hebrew; מוֹסְרוֹתֶיךָ "your bonds", אֲנַתֵּק "I will tear off" in the Hebrew order; יַעַבְדוּ־בוֹ … זָרִים "strangers shall make him serve" (the עָבַד בְּ row; זָר "stranger", its row).
- **"But they shall serve" → "And they shall serve"** (v9) — Hebrew וְעָבְדוּ; אָקִים "I will raise up".
- **"Therefore fear thou not, O my servant Jacob … neither be dismayed, O Israel: for, lo, I will save thee from afar, and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and shall be in rest, and be quiet, and none shall make him afraid" → "And you, do not fear, my servant Jacob … and do not be dismayed, Israel; for behold, I am saving you from afar, and your seed … and Jacob shall return and be quiet and be at ease, and there shall be none who makes him tremble"** (v10) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה "And you"; עַבְדִּי "my servant" (the עֶבֶד row); חָתַת "be dismayed" (its row); הִנְנִי מוֹשִׁיעֲךָ, a participle; זֶרַע "seed" (its row); שִׁבְיָם "their captivity" (the שְׁבִי row); שָׁקַט "be quiet" (its row) and שַׁאֲנַן "be at ease" (the שַׁאֲנָן row); אֵין מַחֲרִיד "there is none making (him) tremble" (hifil of חָרַד; "him" supplied). The same words stand in Jer 46:27–28.
- **"though I make a full end of all nations whither I have scattered thee, yet will I not make a full end of thee: but I will correct thee in measure, and will not leave thee altogether unpunished" → "for I will make a full end among all the nations where I have scattered you; only of you I will not make a full end, and I will discipline you with judgment, and I will surely not leave you clear"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּי "for" (not "though"); עָשָׂה כָלָה בְּ "make a full end among"; הֵפִיץ "scatter" (its row); אַךְ "only"; יִסַּר "discipline" (its row); לַמִּשְׁפָּט "with judgment" (מִשְׁפָּט, its row; "in measure" is the sense); וְנַקֵּה לֹא אֲנַקֶּךָּ, the infinitive absolute, literally "and clearing I will not clear you" (the standing decision: "surely"; נִקָּה "clear", its row: leave guiltless).
- **"Thy bruise is incurable, and thy wound is grievous" → "Incurable is your breaking, grievous is your blow"** (v12) — Hebrew אָנוּשׁ לְשִׁבְרֵךְ, the adjective first; שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (of the body: a fracture; also v15); נַחְלָה "grievous" (niphal participle of חָלָה "be sick"); מַכָּה "blow" (its row; also v14, 17). The "you" of v12–17 is feminine: Zion (v17).
- **"There is none to plead thy cause, that thou mayest be bound up: thou hast no healing medicines" → "There is none judging your cause, for a sore; remedies, new skin, there are none for you"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵין־דָּן דִּינֵךְ "none judging your cause" (the דִּין rows); לְמָזוֹר "for a sore" (מָזוֹר, from זוּר "press": a wound to be pressed together, or the bandage itself; uncertain; the accents set it in the first line); רְפֻאוֹת "remedies"; תְּעָלָה "new skin" (from עָלָה "come up": the flesh that grows over a wound; uncertain).
- **"All thy lovers have forgotten thee; they seek thee not; for I have wounded thee with the wound of an enemy, with the chastisement of a cruel one, for the multitude of thine iniquity; because thy sins were increased" → "All your lovers have forgotten you; you they do not seek; for with the blow of an enemy I have struck you, the discipline of a cruel one, because of the abundance of your iniquity; your sins have become mighty"** (v14) — Hebrew אוֹתָךְ "you" stands first; דָּרַשׁ "seek"; מַכַּת … הִכִּיתִיךְ "with the blow … I have struck you" (one root); מוּסַר "discipline" (the noun of יִסַּר, its row); עָוֹן "iniquity", חַטָּאת "sin"; עָצְמוּ "have become mighty" (the עָצַם row: strong in number, "many"; also v15).
- **"Why criest thou for thine affliction? thy sorrow is incurable" → "Why do you cry out over your breaking? Incurable is your pain"** (v15) — Hebrew זָעַק "cry out" (its row); שִׁבְרֵךְ as v12; מַכְאֹב "pain" (its row).
- **"and they that spoil thee shall be a spoil, and all that prey upon thee will I give for a prey" → "and those who pillage you shall become a pillage, and all who plunder you I will give for plunder"** (v16) — Hebrew שֹׁאסַיִךְ (from שָׁסַס, the שָׁסָה "pillage" row) and מְשִׁסָּה "pillage" (its noun, the 2 Kgs 21:14 row); בֹּזְזַיִךְ … לָבַז "who plunder you … for plunder" (בָּזַז, בַּז, their row); צָרַיִךְ "your adversaries" (צַר, its row); בַּשְּׁבִי "into captivity".
- **"For I will restore health unto thee, and I will heal thee of thy wounds … because they called thee an Outcast, saying, This is Zion, whom no man seeketh after" → "For I will bring up new flesh for you, and from your blows I will heal you … for 'Thrust away' they have called you: 'She is Zion; there is none seeking her'"** (v17) — Hebrew אַעֲלֶה אֲרֻכָה "I will bring up new flesh" (אֲרֻכָה, literally "lengthening": the new flesh drawn over a wound, Jer 8:22; 33:6; the adjective אֲרֻכָּה "long" of Jer 29:28 is from the same root); נִדָּחָה "thrust away" (the נִדַּח row), set first as the name they gave her; צִיּוֹן הִיא "she is Zion"; דֹּרֵשׁ אֵין לָהּ "a seeker there is none for her" (דָּרַשׁ, as v14).
- **"Behold, I will bring again the captivity of Jacob's tents, and have mercy on his dwellingplaces; and the city shall be builded upon her own heap, and the palace shall remain after the manner thereof" → "Behold, I am restoring the fortunes of the tents of Jacob, and on his dwellings I will have compassion; and the city shall be built on her mound, and the citadel shall dwell upon its right"** (v18) — Hebrew הִנְנִי־שָׁב שְׁבוּת, a participle (the reviewer row "restore the fortunes"); מִשְׁכְּנֹתָיו "his dwellings" (the מִשְׁכָּן row); רִחַם "have compassion" (its row); תֵּל "mound" (its row: the heap of the old city, as Josh 11:13); אַרְמוֹן "citadel" (its row); עַל־מִשְׁפָּטוֹ "upon its right" (מִשְׁפָּט of what is due to a thing, as מִשְׁפַּט הַגְּאֻלָּה "the right of redemption", Jer 32:7: where it rightly belongs); יֵשֵׁב "shall dwell" (the יָשַׁב row: of a place, "be inhabited, stand").
- **"And out of them shall proceed thanksgiving and the voice of them that make merry" → "And out of them shall go out thanksgiving and the sound of those who make merry"** (v19) — Hebrew יָצָא "go out"; תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving" (its row); מְשַׂחֲקִים "those who make merry" (שִׂחֵק, the "play" of its rows: laughing, dancing, playing); הִרְבָּה "multiply"; מָעַט "become few" (as Jer 29:6); הִכְבִּיד "make honored" (literally "make heavy", the root of כָּבוֹד "glory"); צָעַר "be small" (insignificant).
- **"Their children also shall be as aforetime, and their congregation … and I will punish all that oppress them" → "And his sons shall be as of old, and his congregation … and I will visit upon all who oppress him"** (v20) — Hebrew בָּנָיו, עֲדָתוֹ, לֹחֲצָיו "his": Jacob (v18) is meant, as v21; בֵּן "son" (the standing decision); כְּקֶדֶם "as of old"; עֵדָה "congregation" (its row); תִּכּוֹן "shall be established"; פָּקַד עַל "visit upon" (its row); לָחַץ "oppress" (its row).
- **"And their nobles shall be of themselves, and their governor shall proceed from the midst of them; and I will cause him to draw near … for who is this that engaged his heart to approach unto me?" → "And his noble one shall be from him, and his ruler shall go out from his midst; and I will bring him near, and he shall approach me; for who is this who has pledged his heart to approach me?"** (v21) — Hebrew אַדִּירוֹ "his noble one" (singular; the אַדִּיר row "nobles" of men); מֹשְׁלוֹ "his ruler"; הִקְרִיב "bring near", נִגַּשׁ "approach"; עָרַב אֶת־לִבּוֹ "pledged his heart" (the עָרַב "be surety" row: who stakes his life). The question has no "would"; "of himself" is not written.
- **"And ye shall be my people, and I will be your God" → "And you shall be a people to me, and I, I will be Elohim to you"** (v22) — Hebrew וִהְיִיתֶם לִי לְעָם וְאָנֹכִי אֶהְיֶה לָכֶם לֵאלֹהִים, literally "you shall be to me for a people, and I, I will be to you for Elohim": the covenant formula (as Jer 7:23; 11:4; 31:33); אָנֹכִי written.
- **"Behold, the whirlwind of the LORD goeth forth with fury, a continuing whirlwind: it shall fall with pain upon the head of the wicked" → "Behold, the storm of Yahweh, fury has gone out, a whirling tempest; upon the head of the wicked it shall whirl"** (v23) — Hebrew סַעֲרַת יְהוָה "the storm of Yahweh" (סְעָרָה, its row) and סַעַר "tempest" (the masculine of the same root: two words); חֵמָה יָצְאָה "fury has gone out" (perfect); מִתְגּוֹרֵר "whirling" (hitpolel of גּוּר, uncertain: "sweeping", "lodging"; Jer 23:19 has מִתְחוֹלֵל in the same verse); עַל רֹאשׁ … יָחוּל "upon the head of … it shall whirl" (the חוּל עַל רֹאשׁ row). Jer 23:19–20 stands almost word for word with v23–24.
- **"The fierce anger of the LORD shall not return, until he have done it, and until he have performed the intents of his heart: in the latter days ye shall consider it" → "The burning of the anger of Yahweh shall not turn back until he has done and until he has established the devisings of his heart; in the latter days you shall give heed to it"** (v24) — Hebrew חֲרוֹן אַף "the burning of anger" (its row); הֵקִים "establish" (literally "make stand", the הֵקִים דָּבָר row); מְזִמּוֹת לִבּוֹ "the devisings of his heart" (the reviewer row for the plural of Yahweh's plan; "scheme" of the מְזִמָּה row is for men's plots). The line stands word for word as Jer 23:20 and is worded the same; בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים "in the latter days" (its row); הִתְבּוֹנֵן בְּ "give heed to" (its row).

### Notes
- **Jer 30–31** — the "Book of Consolation": Yahweh's word for Israel and Judah, written in a book (30:2).
