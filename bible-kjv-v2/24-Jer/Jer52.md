# Jeremiah 52

[1] ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ was twenty-one years old when he became king, and he reigned eleven years in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. His mother's name was ⟦hamutal|Chamutal|Hamutal⟧ daughter of ⟦jeremiah-libnah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ of ⟦libnah|Livnah|Libnah⟧.

[2] And he did what was evil in the sight of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to all that ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ had done.

[3] For because of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ it came to the point in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ that he cast them out from his presence. And ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ rebelled against the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[4] And in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth day of the month, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came with all his army against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and laid siege to it. And they built siegeworks all around it.

[5] So the city was besieged until the eleventh year of King ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧.

[6] On the ninth day of the fourth month the famine was so severe in the city that there was no food for the people of the land.

[7] Then a breach was made in the city, and all the men of war fled and went out from the city by night by the way of the gate between the two walls, by the king's garden, though the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ were around the city. And they went in the direction of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧.

[8] But the army of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ pursued the king and overtook ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ in the plains of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, and all his army was scattered from him.

[9] Then they captured the king and brought him up to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ in the land of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧, and he passed sentence on him.

[10] The king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ slaughtered the sons of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ before his eyes, and also slaughtered all the officials of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧.

[11] He put out the eyes of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧, and bound him in chains, and the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ brought him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and put him in prison till the day of his death.

[12] In the fifth month, on the tenth day of the month — that was the nineteenth year of King ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ — ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard, who served the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, entered ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] And he burned the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the king's house and all the houses of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; every great house he burned down.

[14] And all the army of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, who were with the captain of the guard, broke down all the walls around ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[15] And ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard carried away captive some of the poorest of the people and the rest of the people who were left in the city and the deserters who had deserted to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, together with the rest of the artisans.

[16] But ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard left some of the poorest of the land to be vinedressers and plowmen.

[17] And the pillars of bronze that were in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the stands and the bronze sea that were in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ broke in pieces, and carried all the bronze to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[18] And they took away the pots, the shovels, the snuffers, the basins, the dishes for incense, and all the vessels of bronze used in the temple service;

[19] also the small bowls and the fire pans and the basins and the pots and the lampstands and the dishes for incense and the bowls for drink offerings. What was of gold the captain of the guard took away as gold, and what was of silver, as silver.

[20] As for the two pillars, the one sea, the twelve bronze bulls that were under the sea, and the stands, which ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ the king had made for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the bronze of all these things was beyond weight.

[21] As for the pillars, the height of the one pillar was eighteen cubits, its circumference was twelve cubits, and its thickness was four fingers; it was hollow.

[22] On it was a capital of bronze. The height of the one capital was five cubits. A network and pomegranates, all of bronze, were around the capital. And the second pillar had the same, with pomegranates.

[23] There were ninety-six pomegranates on the sides; all the pomegranates were a hundred upon the network all around.

[24] And the captain of the guard took ⟦seraiah|Serayah|Seraiah⟧ the chief priest, and ⟦zephaniah|Tzefanyah|Zephaniah⟧ the second priest, and the three keepers of the threshold;

[25] and from the city he took an officer who had been in command of the men of war, and seven men of the king's council, who were found in the city; and the secretary of the commander of the army, who mustered the people of the land; and sixty men of the people of the land, who were found in the midst of the city.

[26] And ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard took them and brought them to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧.

[27] And the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ struck them down and put them to death at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ in the land of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧. So ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ was taken into exile out of its land.

[28] This is the number of the people whom ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ carried away captive: in the seventh year, 3,023 ⟦judeans|Yehudim|Judeans⟧;

[29] in the eighteenth year of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ he carried away captive from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ 832 persons;

[30] in the twenty-third year of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard carried away captive of the ⟦judeans|Yehudim|Judeans⟧ 745 persons; all the persons were 4,600.

[31] And in the thirty-seventh year of the exile of ⟦jehoiachin|Yehoyachin|Jehoiachin⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-fifth day of the month, Evil-Merodach king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, in the year that he began to reign, showed favor to ⟦jehoiachin|Yehoyachin|Jehoiachin⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and brought him out of prison.

[32] And he spoke kindly to him, and gave him a seat above the seats of the kings who were with him in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[33] So ⟦jehoiachin|Yehoyachin|Jehoiachin⟧ put off his prison garments. And every day of his life he dined regularly at the king's table.

[34] And as for his allowance, a regular allowance was given him by the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, every day a portion, as long as he lived, till the day of his death.
