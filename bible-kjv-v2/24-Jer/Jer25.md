# Jeremiah 25

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ concerning all the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the fourth year of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ (that was the first year of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧),

[2] which ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet spoke to all the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to all the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying:

[3] "From the thirteenth year of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ son of Amon, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and to this day, this is twenty-three years, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has come to me, and I have spoken to you, rising early and speaking, and you have not listened.

[4] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has sent to you all his servants the prophets, rising early and sending, and you have not listened, and you have not inclined your ear to hear,

[5] saying, 'Turn back, please, each man from his evil way and from the evil of your doings, and dwell on the ground that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given to you and to your fathers from age and to age.

[6] And do not go after other gods to serve them and to bow down to them, and do not vex me with the work of your hands, and I will not do evil to you.'

[7] And you have not listened to me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in order to vex me with the work of your hands, for evil to you.

[8] "Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Because you have not heard my words,

[9] behold, I am sending, and I will take all the clans of the north, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and to ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, my servant, and I will bring them against this land and against its inhabitants and against all these nations around, and I will devote them to destruction, and I will make them a thing that appalls and a hissing and everlasting ruins.

[10] And I will make perish from them the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the sound of the two millstones and the light of the lamp.

[11] And all this land shall become a ruin, a thing that appalls, and these nations shall serve the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ **seventy years**.

[12] And it shall be, when seventy years are fulfilled, I will visit upon the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and upon that nation, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, their iniquity, and upon the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, and I will make it everlasting desolations.

[13] And I will bring upon that land all my words that I have spoken against it, all that is written in this book, which ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ has prophesied against all the nations.

[14] For many nations and great kings have made them serve, them also, and I will repay them according to their deed and according to the work of their hands."

[15] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to me: "Take this cup of the wine of fury from my hand, and make all the nations to whom I am sending you drink it.

[16] And they shall drink, and toss to and fro, and rave, because of the sword that I am sending among them."

[17] And I took the cup from the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I made all the nations drink to whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had sent me:

[18] ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and its kings, its princes, to make them a ruin, a thing that appalls, a hissing and a curse, as at this day;

[19] ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and his servants and his princes and all his people;

[20] and all the mixed people; and all the kings of the land of ⟦uz|Utz|Uz⟧; and all the kings of the land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ and ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧ and ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ and the remnant of Ashdod;

[21] Edom and ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and the sons of Ammon;

[22] and all the kings of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, and all the kings of ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, and the kings of the coastland that is across the sea;

[23] and Dedan and Tema and Buz, and all who are clipped at the edge;

[24] and all the kings of ⟦arabia|Arav|Arabia⟧, and all the kings of the mixed people who abide in the wilderness;

[25] and all the kings of Zimri, and all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧;

[26] and all the kings of the north, the near and the far, each man to his brother; and all the kingdoms of the earth that are on the face of the ground. And the king of ⟦sheshach|Sheshach|Babylon⟧ shall drink after them.

[27] "And you shall say to them, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Drink, and be drunk, and vomit, and fall, and you shall not rise, because of the sword that I am sending among you.'

[28] And it shall be, when they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, that you shall say to them, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: You shall surely drink!

[29] For behold, in the city over which my name is called I am beginning to do evil, and you, shall you be surely clear? You shall not be clear, for a sword I am calling upon all the inhabitants of the earth, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.'

[30] And you, you shall prophesy to them all these words, and say to them:

'⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will roar from on high,
and from the abode of his holiness he will give his voice;
he will surely roar over his habitation;
a shout like those who tread he will answer,
to all the inhabitants of the earth.

[31] A tumult has come to the end of the earth,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has a case against the nations;
he is entering into judgment with all flesh;
the wicked, he has given them to the sword, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

[32] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

"Behold, evil is going out
from nation to nation,
and a great storm is stirred up
from the remote parts of the earth."

[33] And the slain of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be in that day from the end of the earth to the end of the earth. They shall not be lamented, and they shall not be gathered, and they shall not be buried; dung on the face of the ground they shall be.

[34] Wail, shepherds, and cry out,
and roll yourselves, mighty ones of the flock,
for your days are fulfilled for slaughtering,
and your scatterings, and you shall fall like a desirable vessel.

[35] And the place of flight shall perish from the shepherds,
and escape from the mighty ones of the flock.

[36] The voice of the outcry of the shepherds,
and the wailing of the mighty ones of the flock!
For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is laying waste their pasture,

[37] and the pastures of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ are silenced
because of the burning of the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[38] Like a young lion he has left his covert,
for their land has become a thing that appalls
because of the burning of the oppressor,
and because of the burning of his anger.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v8, 27–29, 32) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת. **"God" → "Elohim"** (v15, 27) — אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **Layout** — v1–29 and v33 are prose; v30b–31, 32b and 34–38 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, as the accents divide them. The speech that opens in v27 runs to the end of v31 (the stray opening quotation marks of v28 and v30 removed).
- **"The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah"** (v1) — Hebrew הָיָה עַל־יִרְמְיָהוּ, literally "that was upon Jeremiah" (elsewhere אֶל "to"); עַל־כָּל־עַם "concerning all the people". In v2 the same עַל stands before "all the people": "to" (as the following וְאֶל "and to"). **"that was the first year of Nebuchadrezzar"** — Hebrew הִיא הַשָּׁנָה הָרִאשֹׁנִית, the only synchronism of Jeremiah's dates with Babylon's in the book.
- **"which Jeremiah the prophet spake … saying"** (v2) — לֵאמֹר "saying" kept.
- **"From the thirteenth year … even unto this day, that is the three and twentieth year" → "From the thirteenth year … and to this day, this is twenty-three years"** (v3) — Hebrew וְעַד הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה זֶה שָׁלֹשׁ וְעֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה; the number is a span of years, not "the twenty-third year". **"rising early and speaking"** — אַשְׁכֵּים וְדַבֵּר, two infinitives (the first with an unusual א prefix): "I spoke again and again" is the sense; the literal form kept, as 2 Chr 36:15. **"but ye have not hearkened" → "and you have not listened"** — וְלֹא שְׁמַעְתֶּם, "and".
- **"And the LORD hath sent … rising early and sending them" → "And Yahweh has sent … rising early and sending"** (v4) — Hebrew וְשָׁלַח … הַשְׁכֵּם וְשָׁלֹחַ, no object (the row of 2 Chr 36:15). **"nor inclined your ear" → "and you have not inclined your ear"** — אָזְנְכֶם singular; each וְלֹא kept.
- **"Turn ye again now every one from his evil way, and from the evil of your doings" → "Turn back, please, each man from his evil way and from the evil of your doings"** (v5) — Hebrew שׁוּבוּ־נָא "turn back, please" (נָא kept); אִישׁ "each man", then "your" doings (the change of person is the Hebrew's); מַעֲלָלִים "doings". **"and dwell in the land" → "and dwell on the ground"** — עַל־הָאֲדָמָה "on the ground" (אֲדָמָה, not אֶרֶץ). **"for ever and ever" → "from age and to age"** — לְמִן־עוֹלָם וְעַד־עוֹלָם, no article, with וְ (as Ps 103:17); the given ground is meant to be theirs for all time.
- **"and provoke me not to anger with the works of your hands; and I will do you no hurt" → "and do not vex me with the work of your hands, and I will not do evil to you"** (v6) — Hebrew הִכְעִיס "vex"; מַעֲשֵׂה singular "work"; לֹא אָרַע לָכֶם, hifil of רָעַע "do evil" (as v29). **"to serve them, and to worship them" → "to serve them and to bow down to them"** — הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down".
- **"that ye might provoke me to anger with the works of your hands to your own hurt" → "in order to vex me with the work of your hands, for evil to you"** (v7) — qere הַכְעִיסֵנִי, infinitive "to vex me" (ketiv הכעסוני "you vexed me" or "they vexed me"); לְמַעַן "in order to": their deeds are spoken of as if done for this end. לְרַע לָכֶם "for evil to you" (רַע "evil", not "hurt").
- **"Because ye have not heard my words"** (v8) — Hebrew שְׁמַעְתֶּם אֶת־דְּבָרַי "you have not heard my words" (שָׁמַע "hear"; not "obeyed").
- **"I will send and take all the families of the north … and Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon, my servant" → "I am sending, and I will take all the clans of the north … and to Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, my servant"** (v9) — Hebrew הִנְנִי שֹׁלֵחַ, participle; מִשְׁפְּחוֹת "clans"; וְאֶל "and to" Nebuchadrezzar: the verb "send" is taken up again without being repeated; the Septuagint lacks "and to Nebuchadrezzar … my servant" (from memory). עַבְדִּי "my servant": a king's man and Yahweh's, the one word (also 27:6). **"these nations round about" → "these nations around"** — סָבִיב. **"and will utterly destroy them" → "and I will devote them to destruction"** — הֶחֱרִים, the ban (חֵרֶם). **"an astonishment, and an hissing, and perpetual desolations" → "a thing that appalls and a hissing and everlasting ruins"** — שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (root שָׁמֵם "be desolate / appalled"), שְׁרֵקָה "hissing" (the whistle of one who sees a ruin), חָרְבוֹת עוֹלָם "ruins of the age" = everlasting ruins (עוֹלָם as a genitive; also v12).
- **"Moreover I will take from them" → "And I will make perish from them"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהַאֲבַדְתִּי, hifil of אָבַד "perish". **"the voice of mirth" → "the voice of joy"** — שָׂשׂוֹן "joy", שִׂמְחָה "gladness" (rows; as 7:34; 16:9). **"the sound of the millstones" → "the sound of the two millstones"** — רֵחַיִם, a dual: the hand mill of two stones; קוֹל "voice", of a thing "sound": one word. נֵר "lamp".
- **"And this whole land shall be a desolation, and an astonishment" → "And all this land shall become a ruin, a thing that appalls"** (v11) — Hebrew לְחָרְבָּה לְשַׁמָּה, no "and" between them. **"seventy years"** — שִׁבְעִים שָׁנָה (bold kept); also v12 (2 Chr 36:21; Dan 9:2; Zech 1:12; 7:5 take it up).
- **"when seventy years are accomplished, that I will punish the king of Babylon … for their iniquity" → "when seventy years are fulfilled, I will visit upon the king of Babylon … their iniquity"** (v12) — Hebrew כִמְלֹאות "as the filling of"; פָּקַד עַל … אֶת־עֲוֹנָם "visit upon … their iniquity" (פָּקַד "visit", not "punish"). **"and will make it perpetual desolations" → "and I will make it everlasting desolations"** — שִׁמְמוֹת עוֹלָם, plural; אֹתוֹ "it" (masculine: the nation or the land; not decided).
- **"And I will bring upon that land all my words which I have pronounced against it, even all that is written in this book" → "And I will bring upon that land all my words that I have spoken against it, all that is written in this book"** (v13) — qere וְהֵבֵאתִי (the ketiv differs only in spelling); דִּבַּרְתִּי "I have spoken"; עָלֶיהָ "against it". From here the Septuagint places the words against the nations (chs. 46–51 of the Hebrew) and continues with v15 later, as its ch. 32 (from memory); the MT order is kept.
- **"For many nations and great kings shall serve themselves of them also" → "For many nations and great kings have made them serve, them also"** (v14) — Hebrew עָבְדוּ־בָם גַּם־הֵמָּה, עָבַד בְּ "make serve" (the row of Jer 22:13); a perfect, kept as the English perfect (what is certain is spoken of as done); גַּם־הֵמָּה "them also", the Babylonians. **"and I will recompense them according to their deeds" → "and I will repay them according to their deed"** — שִׁלֵּם "repay"; כְּפָעֳלָם singular "their deed"; "according to" twice, as the Hebrew.
- **"For thus saith the LORD God of Israel unto me" → "For thus says Yahweh, the Elohim of Israel, to me"** (v15) — כִּי "For" kept (the current text dropped it); כֹּה אָמַר "thus says" (as 13:1). **"Take the wine cup of this fury at my hand" → "Take this cup of the wine of fury from my hand"** — Hebrew אֶת־כּוֹס הַיַּיִן הַחֵמָה הַזֹּאת, literally "the cup of the wine, the fury, this": the wine is the fury; הַזֹּאת is feminine and goes with כּוֹס "cup"; חֵמָה "fury" (lit. "heat"), not "wrath". **"and cause all the nations … to drink it" → "and make all the nations … drink it"** — הִשְׁקָה.
- **"And they shall drink, and be moved, and be mad" → "And they shall drink, and toss to and fro, and rave"** (v16) — Hebrew הִתְגֹּעֲשׁוּ, hitpael of גָּעַשׁ "heave" (of the earth, 2 Sam 22:8; of waters, 46:7–8): of drunk men, the body tossing; הִתְהֹלָלוּ "rave" (as 1 Sam 21:13). מִפְּנֵי "because of".
- **"Then took I the cup at the LORD's hand" → "And I took the cup from the hand of Yahweh"** (v17) — וָאֶקַּח, "and". **"and made all the nations to drink, unto whom the LORD had sent me"** — וָאַשְׁקֶה; the list of v18–26 depends on this verb.
- **"the kings thereof, and the princes thereof" → "its kings, its princes"** (v18) — no "and" before "its princes"; שָׂרִים "princes" (also v19; not "officials"). **"to make them a desolation, an astonishment, an hissing, and a curse" → "to make them a ruin, a thing that appalls, a hissing and a curse"** — לָתֵת אֹתָם, literally "to give them" (= to make them); חָרְבָּה "ruin", קְלָלָה "curse". **"as it is this day" → "as at this day"** — כַּיּוֹם הַזֶּה: Jerusalem was not yet destroyed in the fourth year of Jehoiakim; the words look at the state of things when the book was written (or "as it is coming to be"): the Hebrew is kept, the Septuagint lacks it (from memory).
- **"and all the mingled people" → "and all the mixed people"** (v20, 24) — Hebrew הָעֶרֶב "the mixture" (as עֵרֶב רַב, Exod 12:38): "people" supplied; the same consonants as עֲרָב "Arabia" (v24): some read "the Arabs". KJV "among them" is not in the Hebrew. **"the land of Uz"** — אֶרֶץ הָעוּץ, with the article (unusual for a name). **"the remnant of Ashdod"** — שְׁאֵרִית "remnant".
- **"the children of Ammon" → "the sons of Ammon"** (v21) — בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן.
- **"the kings of the isles which are beyond the sea" → "the kings of the coastland that is across the sea"** (v22) — Hebrew הָאִי, singular: the coast or island reached by sea; בְּעֵבֶר הַיָּם "across the sea".
- **"and all that are in the utmost corners" → "and all who are clipped at the edge"** (v23; also 9:26; 49:32) — Hebrew קְצוּצֵי פֵאָה, "clipped as to the edge": פֵּאָה "edge" of the hair or beard (Lev 19:27, which forbids it); the clipped hair at the temples of desert peoples is meant; "corners of the earth" is not the sense.
- **"that dwell in the desert" → "who abide in the wilderness"** (v24) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁכְנִים, שָׁכַן "abide" (beside יָשַׁב "dwell"); מִדְבָּר "wilderness".
- **"far and near, one with another" → "the near and the far, each man to his brother"** (v26) — Hebrew הַקְּרֹבִים וְהָרְחֹקִים, the Hebrew order; אִישׁ אֶל־אָחִיו "each man to his brother" (= one after another). **"all the kingdoms of the world, which are upon the face of the earth" → "all the kingdoms of the earth that are on the face of the ground"** — הַמַּמְלְכוֹת הָאָרֶץ … עַל־פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה: אֶרֶץ "earth", אֲדָמָה "ground". **"and the king of Sheshach shall drink after them"** — Hebrew שֵׁשַׁךְ, printed as the text has it. It is read as a cipher (atbash: the alphabet reversed, ש for ב, ך for ל) for בָּבֶל "Babel" (as 51:41, where "Sheshach" stands beside "Babylon"; לֵב קָמָי 51:1 = כַּשְׂדִּים); the token's traditional form gives "Babylon". The Septuagint lacks the line (from memory).
- **"Drink ye, and be drunken, and spue, and fall, and rise no more" → "Drink, and be drunk, and vomit, and fall, and you shall not rise"** (v27) — Hebrew שְׁתוּ וְשִׁכְרוּ וּקְיוּ וְנִפְלוּ, four imperatives, each with "and"; וְלֹא תָקוּמוּ, a statement with לֹא, not a command; שָׁכַר "be drunk", קִיא "vomit". **"which I will send among you" → "that I am sending among you"** — participle (also v16, 29).
- **"And it shall be, if they refuse to take the cup at thine hand to drink" → "And it shall be, when they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink"** (v28) — וְהָיָה כִּי (when / if); לָקַח "take" (not "accept"). **"Ye shall certainly drink" → "You shall surely drink"** — שָׁתוֹ תִשְׁתּוּ, literally "drinking you shall drink".
- **"For, lo, I begin to bring evil on the city which is called by my name" → "For behold, in the city over which my name is called I am beginning to do evil"** (v29) — Hebrew בָעִיר אֲשֶׁר נִקְרָא־שְׁמִי עָלֶיהָ, literally "on which my name is called" (= which belongs to me), placed first; מֵחֵל participle "beginning"; לְהָרַע "to do evil". **"and should ye be utterly unpunished? Ye shall not be unpunished" → "and you, shall you be surely clear? You shall not be clear"** — וְאַתֶּם הִנָּקֵה תִנָּקוּ, a question without a question word, with the infinitive absolute ("surely"); נִקָּה nifal "be clear" (of guilt), not "go unpunished". **"for I will call for a sword upon all the inhabitants of the earth" → "for a sword I am calling upon all the inhabitants of the earth"** — the Hebrew order; קֹרֵא participle. הָאָרֶץ "the earth" here and v30–33 (all the nations of v15–26); "the land" is also possible.
- **"Therefore prophesy thou against them all these words" → "And you, you shall prophesy to them all these words"** (v30) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה תִּנָּבֵא אֲלֵיהֶם: וְאַתָּה "and you" (not "therefore"), the imperfect "you shall", אֲלֵיהֶם "to them". **"The LORD shall roar from on high, and utter his voice from his holy habitation" → "Yahweh will roar from on high, and from the abode of his holiness he will give his voice"** — the Hebrew order; מְעוֹן קָדְשׁוֹ "the abode of his holiness" (מָעוֹן "abode"); נָתַן קוֹל "give his voice". **"he shall mightily roar upon his habitation" → "he will surely roar over his habitation"** — שָׁאֹג יִשְׁאַג, infinitive absolute; נָוֵהוּ "his habitation" (נָוֶה, literally "pasture, abode": his own land and flock, or his dwelling). **"he shall give a shout, as they that tread the grapes, against all the inhabitants of the earth" → "a shout like those who tread he will answer, to all the inhabitants of the earth"** — Hebrew הֵידָד כְּדֹרְכִים יַעֲנֶה אֶל: הֵידָד "shout" (the treaders' cry, Isa 16:10), עָנָה "answer" (= raise in reply); "grapes" is not written; אֶל "to".
- **"A noise shall come even to the ends of the earth" → "A tumult has come to the end of the earth"** (v31) — Hebrew בָּא, perfect (or participle "is coming"); שָׁאוֹן "tumult"; קְצֵה singular "end". **"for the LORD hath a controversy with the nations" → "for Yahweh has a case against the nations"** — רִיב "case" (a suit at law). **"he will plead with all flesh" → "he is entering into judgment with all flesh"** — נִשְׁפָּט, nifal participle. **"he will give them that are wicked to the sword" → "the wicked, he has given them to the sword"** — הָרְשָׁעִים נְתָנָם, the object placed first and taken up again; a perfect.
- **"Behold, evil shall go forth from nation to nation" → "Behold, evil is going out from nation to nation"** (v32) — Hebrew רָעָה "evil" (not "disaster"); יֹצֵאת participle. **"and a great whirlwind shall be raised up from the coasts of the earth" → "and a great storm is stirred up from the remote parts of the earth"** — סַעַר "storm"; יֵעוֹר nifal; יַרְכְּתֵי־אָרֶץ "the remote parts (lit. thighs, flanks) of the earth".
- **"And the slain of the LORD shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth" → "And the slain of Yahweh shall be in that day from the end of the earth to the end of the earth"** (v33) — Hebrew חַלְלֵי יְהוָה "the slain of Yahweh" (a genitive: those he has slain); "in that day". **"they shall not be lamented, neither gathered, nor buried" → "They shall not be lamented, and they shall not be gathered, and they shall not be buried"** — three verbs, each with וְלֹא; סָפַד "lament". **"they shall be dung upon the ground" → "dung on the face of the ground they shall be"** — the Hebrew order; עַל־פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"Howl, ye shepherds, and cry; and wallow yourselves in the ashes, ye principal of the flock" → "Wail, shepherds, and cry out, and roll yourselves, mighty ones of the flock"** (v34) — Hebrew הֵילִילוּ "wail", זָעַק "cry out"; הִתְפַּלְּשׁוּ "roll yourselves" ("in the ashes" / "in the dust" is not written here; 6:26 has בָאֵפֶר); אַדִּירֵי הַצֹּאן "the mighty ones of the flock" (the leaders, as rams of the flock; also v35, 36). No "O". **"for the days of your slaughter and of your dispersions are accomplished" → "for your days are fulfilled for slaughtering, and your scatterings"** — Hebrew מָלְאוּ יְמֵיכֶם לִטְבוֹחַ "your days are full for slaughtering"; וּתְפוֹצוֹתִיכֶם only here, uncertain: a noun of פּוּץ "scatter" ("your scatterings") or a verb "and I will shatter you"; the word is kept as pointed. **"and ye shall fall like a pleasant vessel" → "and you shall fall like a desirable vessel"** — כִּכְלִי חֶמְדָּה, literally "like a vessel of desire" (a fine vessel dropped and broken).
- **"And the shepherds shall have no way to flee, nor the principal of the flock to escape" → "And the place of flight shall perish from the shepherds, and escape from the mighty ones of the flock"** (v35) — Hebrew וְאָבַד מָנוֹס "and the place of flight shall perish" (מָנוֹס from נוּס "flee"); פְּלֵיטָה "escape".
- **"A voice of the cry of the shepherds, and an howling of the principal of the flock, shall be heard" → "The voice of the outcry of the shepherds, and the wailing of the mighty ones of the flock!"** (v36) — Hebrew קוֹל צַעֲקַת הָרֹעִים, an exclamation without a verb ("shall be heard" is not written); צְעָקָה "outcry", יְלָלָה "wailing". **"for the LORD hath spoiled their pasture" → "For Yahweh is laying waste their pasture"** — שֹׁדֵד participle of שָׁדַד "lay waste"; מַרְעִית "pasture" (the flock's grazing).
- **"And the peaceable habitations are cut down" → "and the pastures of shalom are silenced"** (v37) — Hebrew וְנָדַמּוּ נְאוֹת הַשָּׁלוֹם: נְאוֹת "pastures" (another word than מַרְעִית v36, the same English), הַשָּׁלוֹם the noun (token `shalom`); נָדַמּוּ from דָּמַם "be silent" or דָּמָה "be cut off, destroyed": uncertain, "are silenced" taken. **"because of the fierce anger of the LORD" → "because of the burning of the anger of Yahweh"** — חֲרוֹן אַף, literally "the burning of the nose (anger)".
- **"He hath forsaken his covert, as the lion" → "Like a young lion he has left his covert"** (v38) — Hebrew עָזַב כַּכְּפִיר סֻכּוֹ, the Hebrew order; כְּפִיר "young lion"; עָזַב "forsake", of a place "leave"; סֹךְ "covert, thicket". **"for their land is desolate" → "for their land has become a thing that appalls"** — לְשַׁמָּה, the word of v9, 11, 18. **"because of the fierceness of the oppressor" → "because of the burning of the oppressor"** — MT חֲרוֹן הַיּוֹנָה "the burning of the oppressing (one)": הַיּוֹנָה is a participle of יָנָה "oppress" (feminine, perhaps the sword is meant); some Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint read חֶרֶב "the sword of the oppressor", as 46:16; 50:16 (from memory); the current "sword" followed them: the MT is restored. The Vulgate took הַיּוֹנָה as "the dove" (from memory). **"and because of his fierce anger" → "and because of the burning of his anger"** — חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ.

### Notes
- **v11–12 seventy years** — taken up in 29:10; 2 Chr 36:21–22; Dan 9:2; Zech 1:12; 7:5. The reckonings of the seventy years differ (from the first deportation 605 BC, or from the destruction of the temple 586 BC to its rebuilding 516 BC); the text gives no starting point beyond "these nations shall serve the king of Babylon".
- **v15–29 the cup** — the cup of Yahweh's fury, given by the prophet's hand to the nations in the order of v18–26; the same picture in 49:12; 51:7; Isa 51:17, 22; Lam 4:21; Ezek 23:31–34; Hab 2:16; Ps 75:8.
