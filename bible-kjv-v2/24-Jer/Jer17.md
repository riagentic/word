# Jeremiah 17

[1] "The sin of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ is written with a pen of iron,
with a point of diamond;
engraved on the tablet of their heart,
and on the horns of your altars,

[2] as their sons remember their altars
and their Asherim
by a luxuriant tree,
upon the high hills.

[3] My mountain in the field,
your wealth, all your treasuries, I will give for plunder,
your high places, in sin, in all your borders.

[4] And you shall let drop, even you,
from your inheritance that I gave to you,
and I will make you serve your enemies
in the land that you have not known;
for a fire you have kindled in my anger;
forever it shall burn."

[5] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Cursed is the mighty man who trusts in man
and makes flesh his arm,
and from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his heart turns aside.

[6] And he shall be like a shrub in the steppe,
and shall not see when good comes;
and he shall abide in parched places in the wilderness,
a salt land, and not inhabited.

[7] Blessed is the mighty man who trusts in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be his trust.

[8] And he shall be like a tree planted by water,
and by a watercourse it shall send out its roots,
and it shall not see when heat comes,
and its leaf shall be luxuriant;
and in the year of drought it shall not be anxious,
and it shall not leave off making fruit.

[9] The heart is crooked above all,
and incurable it is;
who can know it?

[10] I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, search out the heart,
try the kidneys,
and to give to each man according to his ways,
according to the fruit of his doings."

[11] A partridge has brooded, and has not borne:
one who makes riches, and not by judgment;
in the half of his days it shall leave him,
and at his end he shall be a fool.

[12] A throne of glory, on high from the first,
is the place of our sanctuary.

[13] The hope of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
all who forsake you shall be ashamed;
and those who turn aside from me shall be written in the earth,
for they have forsaken the fountain of living water, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] Heal me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and I shall be healed;
save me, and I shall be saved,
for you are my praise.

[15] Behold, they are saying to me,
"Where is the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?
Let it come, now!"

[16] And I, I have not hastened from being a shepherd after you,
and the incurable day I have not craved;
you, you know;
what went out of my lips was in front of your face.

[17] Do not be a terror to me;
my refuge are you in the day of evil.

[18] Let those who pursue me be ashamed,
and let not me be ashamed;
let them be dismayed,
and let not me be dismayed;
bring upon them the day of evil,
and with a double breaking break them!

[19] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to me: "Go, and stand in the gate of the sons of the people, by which the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ come in and by which they go out, and in all the gates of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[20] and say to them: 'Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and all the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ who come in by these gates.

[21] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Take heed for your souls, and do not lift up a burden on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day and bring it in by the gates of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[22] And do not bring out a burden from your houses on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day, and do no work; and sanctify the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day, as I commanded your fathers.

[23] And they did not hear and did not incline their ear, and they stiffened their neck so as not to hear and not to take discipline.

[24] "'And it shall be, if you surely listen to me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, so as not to bring in a burden by the gates of this city on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day, and to sanctify the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day, so as not to do any work in it,

[25] then there shall come in by the gates of this city kings and princes sitting on the throne of David, riding in chariots and on horses, they and their princes, the man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and this city shall be inhabited forever.

[26] And they shall come from the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and from the surroundings of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and from the land of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, and from the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧, and from the hill country, and from the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧, bringing burnt offering and sacrifice and grain offering and frankincense, and bringing thanksgiving to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[27] But if you do not listen to me, to sanctify the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day, and so as not to lift up a burden and come in by the gates of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day, then I will kindle a fire in its gates, and it shall devour the citadels of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and shall not be quenched.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (v1–18) — poetry: one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the existing breaks inside a line are kept where the accents mark a pause (v2, 4, 15, 18). v1 and v16 had merged lines across the main pause (atnach): split as the Hebrew has them. vv19–27 stay prose.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v4, 6, 8, 10, 16, 18, 22–23, 26).
- **"with the point of a diamond: it is graven" → "with a point of diamond; engraved"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּצִפֹּרֶן שָׁמִיר ends the first line; חֲרוּשָׁה "engraved" (lit. "plowed, cut in") begins the second. צִפֹּרֶן "nail, point"; שָׁמִיר a very hard stone ("diamond" or "emery"; uncertain: the same word is "thorns" in Isa 5:6). לוּחַ "tablet".
- **"their altars" → "your altars"** (v1) — Hebrew מִזְבְּחוֹתֵיכֶם, "your" (plural), as the MT has it beside "their heart"; many Hebrew manuscripts and versions read "their" (from memory): the MT is kept.
- **vv1–4 in the MT** — the Septuagint has no vv1–4 (from memory); the MT is translated.
- **"Whilst their children remember … by the green trees" → "as their sons remember … by a luxuriant tree"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּזְכֹּר בְּנֵיהֶם, "as their sons remember"; בֵּן "son". עֵץ רַעֲנָן "a luxuriant tree" (the רַעֲנָן row: fresh, full of leaves, not the colour); the Hebrew has no "every" here (unlike Deut 12:2). **"their groves" → "their Asherim"** — אֲשֵׁרִים, the word kept (WORDS-HE.md). **"upon the high hills"** — עַל without "and".
- **"O my mountain in the field, I will give thy substance …" → "My mountain in the field, your wealth, all your treasuries, I will give for plunder"** (v3) — Hebrew הֲרָרִי בַּשָּׂדֶה, "my mountain in the field" (or the old construct "the mountains in the field", ending v2: both readings possible); חַיִל "wealth" (the חַיִל row); אוֹצָרוֹת "treasuries"; לָבַז "for plunder" (בַּז). **"and thy high places for sin" → "your high places, in sin"** — בָּמֹתֶיךָ בְּחַטָּאת: בְּ "in, with, because of"; the same בְּ as "in all your borders". גְּבוּל "border" (also the land within it).
- **"And thou, even thyself, shalt discontinue from thine heritage" → "And you shall let drop, even you, from your inheritance"** (v4) — Hebrew וְשָׁמַטְתָּה וּבְךָ, "and you shall let drop (release), and in / through you": "your hand" (the reading the current text had) is an emendation (וְיָדְךָ), not the MT. שָׁמַט "let drop" as Exod 23:11; Deut 15:2 (the year of release). נַחֲלָה "inheritance". **"in the land which thou knowest not" → "in the land that you have not known"** — perfect, with the article. **"for ye have kindled a fire in mine anger"** — Hebrew קְדַחְתֶּם, "you (plural) have kindled": the current "a fire is kindled" dropped the subject. אַף "nose, anger": "in my anger". Jer 15:13–14 has nearly the same words.
- **"Cursed be the man" → "Cursed is the mighty man"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew אָרוּר הַגֶּבֶר (no verb: "is"); גֶּבֶר "mighty man" beside אָדָם "man" in the same verse (standing decision); v7 keeps the same word for the contrast. **"his arm"** — זְרֹעוֹ, "his arm" (not "strength"). **"departeth" → "turns aside"** — סוּר.
- **"For he shall be like the heath in the desert" → "And he shall be like a shrub in the steppe"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהָיָה; עַרְעָר a desert shrub (kind uncertain; "juniper", "tamarisk"; the same word is "destitute" of a person, Ps 102:17); בָּעֲרָבָה "in the steppe" (the article may also make it the place name, the Arabah, by the Dead Sea). **"shall not see when good cometh"** — כִּי "when". **"inhabit" → "abide"** — שָׁכַן. **"the parched places"** — חֲרֵרִים, found only here. **"a salt land and not inhabited"** — וְלֹא תֵשֵׁב, literally "and it does not sit / dwell" (the land, feminine).
- **"and whose hope the LORD is" → "and Yahweh shall be his trust"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהָיָה יְהוָה מִבְטַחוֹ, a clause of its own with וְהָיָה (as v6, 8); מִבְטָח "trust".
- **v8 beside Ps 1:3** — "And he shall be like a tree planted by" as Ps 1:3, where the Hebrew is the same (וְהָיָה כְּעֵץ שָׁתוּל עַל); Ps 1:3 has פַּלְגֵי מָיִם "streams of water", here plain מַיִם "water". **"by the river" → "by a watercourse"** — יוּבַל, found only here (root "carry, flow"); "stream" stays with פֶּלֶג, "channel" with אָפִיק. **"shall not see when heat cometh"** — the qere יִרְאֶה "see" (as v6); the ketiv ירא can be read "fear" (the current text followed it). **"her leaf shall be green" → "its leaf shall be luxuriant"** — רַעֲנָן (as v2). **"shall not be careful" → "shall not be anxious"** — דָּאַג. **"neither shall cease from yielding fruit" → "and it shall not leave off making fruit"** — מוּשׁ "move away from, leave off"; עָשָׂה פְּרִי "make fruit" (WORDS-HE.md). The suffixes "its" refer to the tree.
- **"The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked" → "The heart is crooked above all, and incurable it is"** (v9) — Hebrew עָקֹב, as printed: "uneven, crooked" (Isa 40:4 "the uneven"), from the root of "heel" and of Jacob's "supplanting" (Gen 27:36), so "deceitful" is also defended; the Septuagint "deep" (from memory) reads another word. אָנֻשׁ "incurable, mortally sick" (as Jer 15:18; Mic 1:9), not "wicked". מִכֹּל "above all" ("things" not written). **"who can know it?"** — יָדַע "know".
- **"search the heart, I try the reins" → "search out the heart, try the kidneys"** (v10) — Hebrew חֹקֵר, בֹּחֵן (participles); חָקַר "search out", בָּחַן "try" (reviewer rows); כְּלָיוֹת "kidneys" (the inner man). **"even to give" → "and to give"** — וְלָתֵת. **"his ways"** — the qere כִּדְרָכָיו (plural); the ketiv כדרכו is singular. **"doings"** — מַעֲלָלִים.
- **"As the partridge sitteth on eggs, and hatcheth them not; so he that getteth riches, and not by right" → "A partridge has brooded, and has not borne: one who makes riches, and not by judgment"** (v11) — the Hebrew has no "as … so": two lines side by side. קֹרֵא "partridge" (lit. "caller"; uncertain); דָּגַר "brood" (or "gather"; uncertain); יָלַד "bear" (she did not lay the eggs she sits on). עֹשֶׂה עֹשֶׁר "makes riches"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment". **"shall leave them" → "it shall leave him"** — יַעַזְבֶנּוּ, singular subject and object (the riches leave him); the qere יָמָיו "his days" (ketiv ימו). נָבָל "fool".
- **"A glorious high throne from the beginning" → "A throne of glory, on high from the first"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּסֵּא כָבוֹד מָרוֹם מֵרִאשׁוֹן; "set" is not written; the copula "is" supplied.
- **"O LORD, the hope of Israel" → "The hope of Israel, Yahweh,"** (v13) — Hebrew order kept; no "O" (no word for it). מִקְוֶה "hope" (the same letters are "a gathering of water", Gen 1:10: the play goes on in "living water"). **"shall be ashamed"** — בּוֹשׁ. **"and they that depart from me" → "and those who turn aside from me"** — the qere וְסוּרַי, "and my turners-aside" (Yahweh speaks); the ketiv יסורי. **"in the earth"** — בָּאָרֶץ (or "in the land"). **"the fountain of living waters, the LORD" → "the fountain of living water, Yahweh"** — מְקוֹר מַיִם־חַיִּים (as Jer 2:13), the name last in apposition, as the Hebrew has it.
- **"Heal me, O LORD" → "Heal me, Yahweh"** (v14) — no "O". רָפָא "heal", יָשַׁע "save".
- **"Behold, they say unto me … let it come now" → "Behold, they are saying to me … Let it come, now!"** (v15) — participle אֹמְרִים; נָא kept.
- **"As for me, I have not hastened from being a pastor to follow thee" → "And I, I have not hastened from being a shepherd after you"** (v16) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי לֹא־אַצְתִּי מֵרֹעֶה אַחֲרֶיךָ; אוּץ "hasten"; מֵרֹעֶה "from a shepherd / from shepherding" (repointed מֵרָעָה it would be "from evil", from memory: the MT is kept). **"the woeful day" → "the incurable day"** — יוֹם אָנוּשׁ, the word of v9. **"desired" → "craved"** — הִתְאַוָּה. **"thou knowest: that which came out of my lips was right before thee" → "you, you know; what went out of my lips was in front of your face"** — אַתָּה יָדָעְתָּ stands alone before the main pause; מוֹצָא שְׂפָתַי "the going out of my lips"; נֹכַח פָּנֶיךָ "in front of your face" (נֹכַח "opposite, in front of"; "right" is not written).
- **"Be not a terror unto me: thou art my hope in the day of evil" → "Do not be a terror to me; my refuge are you in the day of evil"** (v17) — Hebrew מְחִתָּה "terror" (as its row for Jer 17:17); מַחְסֶה "refuge" (not "hope"); יוֹם רָעָה "the day of evil" (also v18; רָעָה "evil": the harm that comes).
- **"Let them be confounded that persecute me" → "Let those who pursue me be ashamed"** (v18) — רֹדְפַי "my pursuers" (רָדַף "pursue"); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed". The Hebrew adds the pronouns, "I … they … I" (אָנִי, הֵמָּה), to set the two sides against each other: "let not me" carries it; "they" is not repeated in English. חָתַת "be dismayed". **"destroy them with double destruction" → "with a double breaking break them"** — מִשְׁנֶה שִׁבָּרוֹן שָׁבְרֵם, one root (שָׁבַר "break") twice.
- **"Thus said the LORD unto me; Go and stand" → "Thus says Yahweh to me: Go, and stand"** (v19) — כֹּה אָמַר "Thus says" (the prophets' formula); הָלֹךְ an infinitive absolute used as a command, and וְעָמַדְתָּ "and stand". **"the gate of the children of the people" → "the gate of the sons of the people"** — the qere הָעָם; the ketiv עם has no article. **"come in"** — בּוֹא.
- **"Take heed to yourselves" → "Take heed for your souls"** (v21) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמְרוּ בְּנַפְשׁוֹתֵיכֶם, literally "keep yourselves in your souls" (= at the cost of your souls). **"bear no burden" → "do not lift up a burden"** — נָשָׂא (also v27); מַשָּׂא "burden" (a load here). **"on the sabbath day"** — בְּיוֹם הַשַּׁבָּת. **"bring it in"** — וַהֲבֵאתֶם, "and bring (it) in": "it" supplied.
- **"carry forth" → "bring out"** (v22) — הוֹצִיא. **"hallow ye the sabbath day" → "sanctify the Shabbat day"** (v22, 24, 27) — קִדֵּשׁ "sanctify"; מְלָאכָה "work".
- **"But they obeyed not" → "And they did not hear"** (v23) — שָׁמַע "hear" (the qere שְׁמוֹעַ "to hear"; the ketiv שומע a participle form). **"made their neck stiff" → "stiffened their neck"** — הִקְשָׁה עֹרֶף (its row). **"nor receive instruction" → "and not to take discipline"** — לָקַח "take", מוּסָר "discipline".
- **"if ye diligently hearken unto me" → "if you surely listen to me"** (v24) — Hebrew שָׁמֹעַ תִּשְׁמְעוּן, literally "hearing you shall hear"; שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to". **"And it shall be"** — וְהָיָה kept. **"to do no work therein" → "so as not to do any work in it"** — the qere בּוֹ "in it" (the day; ketiv בה, feminine).
- **"then shall there enter … kings and princes sitting" → "then there shall come in … kings and princes sitting"** (v25) — יֹשְׁבִים participle. **"their princes"** — שָׂרֵיהֶם, the same word as "princes" before it (not "officials"). **"the men of Judah" → "the man of Judah"** — singular collective אִישׁ. **"shall remain for ever" → "shall be inhabited forever"** — וְיָשְׁבָה, literally "and it shall sit / dwell" (of a city: be inhabited; as v6).
- **"And they shall come from … the places about Jerusalem" → "And they shall come from … the surroundings of Jerusalem"** (v26) — no subject supplied (the current "people"); each place has its "and from". **"from the mountains" → "from the hill country"** — הָהָר. **"burnt offerings, and sacrifices, and meat offerings, and incense" → "burnt offering and sacrifice and grain offering and frankincense"** — singulars, as the Hebrew has them; לְבוֹנָה "frankincense". **"sacrifices of praise" → "thanksgiving"** — תּוֹדָה (the thanksgiving offering).
- **"and to not bear a burden, even entering in" → "and so as not to lift up a burden and come in"** (v27) — וּבֹא, an infinitive, joined by "and". **"the palaces" → "the citadels"** — אַרְמוֹן. **"devour"** — אָכַל, literally "eat".

### Notes
- **v5–8** — beside Psalm 1 (curse / blessing; the tree planted by water); Ps 1:1 has אַשְׁרֵי "Happy", here בָּרוּךְ "Blessed".
- **v12–18** — Jeremiah's prayer; v13 the speaker changes within the verse (the qere "from me").
