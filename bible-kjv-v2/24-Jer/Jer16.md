# Jeremiah 16

[1] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying,

[2] "You shall not take yourself a wife, and you shall not have sons and daughters in this place.

[3] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the sons and concerning the daughters who are born in this place, and concerning their mothers who bear them, and concerning their fathers who beget them in this land:

[4] Deaths of sicknesses they shall die; they shall not be lamented and they shall not be buried; dung on the face of the ground they shall be; and by the sword and by famine they shall be finished; and their dead body shall be food for the birds of the heavens and for the beasts of the earth.

[5] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Do not enter a house of mourning feast, and do not go to lament, and do not show grief for them; for I have gathered my ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ from this people, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and the compassion.

[6] And great and small shall die in this land; they shall not be buried, and they shall not lament for them, and none shall gash himself, and none shall be made bald for them.

[7] And they shall not break bread for them in mourning, to comfort him for the dead; and they shall not give them the cup of consolations to drink for his father and for his mother.

[8] And a house of feasting you shall not enter, to sit with them, to eat and to drink.

[9] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I am making cease from this place, before your eyes and in your days, the voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride.

[10] "And it shall be, when you tell this people all these words, and they say to you, 'Why has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoken against us all this great evil? And what is our iniquity, and what is our sin that we have sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?'

[11] then you shall say to them, 'Because your fathers have forsaken me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and went after other gods and served them and bowed down to them; and me they have forsaken, and my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ they have not kept.

[12] And you, you have done evil more than your fathers; and behold, you are walking, each man after the stubbornness of his evil heart, so as not to listen to me.

[13] And I will hurl you from upon this land onto the land that you have not known, you and your fathers; and you shall serve there other gods day and night, for I will not give you favor.'

[14] "Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and it shall no more be said, 'As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, who brought up the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,'

[15] but, 'As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, who brought up the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the land of the north and from all the lands where he had driven them.' And I will bring them back to their ground that I gave to their fathers.

[16] "Behold, I am sending for many fishers, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they shall fish them; and after this I will send for many hunters, and they shall hunt them from upon every mountain and from upon every hill, and from the clefts of the crags.

[17] For my eyes are on all their ways; they are not concealed from before me, and their iniquity is not hidden away from before my eyes.

[18] And I will repay first the double of their iniquity and their sin, because they have profaned my land; with the carcass of their detestable things and their abominations they have filled my inheritance."

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, my strength and my strong place,
and my place of flight in the day of distress,
to you nations shall come
from the ends of the earth, and say,
"Only falsehood have our fathers inherited,
vapor, and there is nothing among them that profits.

[20] Shall a man make for himself gods,
and they are not gods?"

[21] "Therefore, behold, I am making them know;
this time I will make them know my hand and my might,
and they shall know that my name is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v9) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, "of Hosts" plain (the prophets' formula row).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv9, 10) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved; v11, 13, 20 אֱלֹהִים of other gods: plain "gods".
- **Poetic structure** — vv19–21 set as verse; the prose of vv1–18 stays prose.
- **"The word of the LORD came also unto me, saying" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־יְהוָה אֵלַי לֵאמֹר (the prophets' formula row).
- **"Thou shalt not take thee a wife" → "You shall not take yourself a wife"** (v2) — Hebrew לֹא־תִקַּח לְךָ, לְךָ kept as the reflexive (also "you shall not have", וְלֹא־יִהְיוּ לְךָ).
- **"concerning the sons and concerning the daughters"** (v3) — each with its עַל, as printed; הַיֹּלְדוֹת "who bear", הַמּוֹלִדִים "who beget" (הוֹלִיד "beget", row; not "fathered"); "their mothers … their fathers" (the suffix kept).
- **"They shall die of grievous deaths" → "Deaths of sicknesses they shall die"** (v4) — Hebrew מְמוֹתֵי תַחֲלֻאִים, literally "deaths of sicknesses" (תַּחֲלֻאִים, row), first for emphasis. **"they shall not be lamented; neither shall they be buried" → "they shall not be lamented and they shall not be buried"** — סָפַד "lament" (row). **"they shall be as dung upon the face of the earth" → "dung on the face of the ground they shall be"** — לְדֹמֶן "for dung" ("as" is not written); אֲדָמָה "ground" (row). **"they shall be consumed by the sword" → "by the sword and by famine they shall be finished"** — יִכְלוּ, qal of כָּלָה "be finished" (תַּם "come to an end" is another verb, Jer 14:15). **"their carcases" → "their dead body"** — נִבְלָתָם, singular; נְבֵלָה of dead men "dead body" (row). **"the fowls of heaven" → "the birds of the heavens"** (row).
- **"Enter not into the house of mourning" → "Do not enter a house of mourning feast"** (v5) — Hebrew בֵּית מַרְזֵחַ; מַרְזֵחַ is found only here and in Amos 6:7 ("the revelry of those who sprawl"): a feast of a fellowship, here held for the dead (meaning not certain; "house of mourning" gives only half of it). **"neither go to lament nor bemoan them" → "and do not go to lament, and do not show grief for them"** — סָפַד "lament" (row); נוּד לְ "show grief" (row). **"for I have taken away my peace from this people … even lovingkindness and mercies" → "for I have gathered my shalom from this people … the chesed and the compassion"** — אָסַפְתִּי … מֵאֵת "I have gathered (away) from"; שָׁלוֹם token (reviewer); הַחֶסֶד token (reviewer: in every place; the earlier text had plain "my steadfast love"); הָרַחֲמִים "the compassion" (row; the articles kept).
- **"Both the great and the small shall die in this land" → "And great and small shall die in this land"** (v6) — Hebrew וּמֵתוּ גְדֹלִים וּקְטַנִּים, no article. **"neither shall men lament for them, nor cut themselves, nor make themselves bald for them" → "and they shall not lament for them, and none shall gash himself, and none shall be made bald for them"** — יִסְפְּדוּ, an impersonal plural; יִתְגֹּדַד "gash himself" (הִתְגֹּדֵד, row), יִקָּרֵחַ "be made bald" (nifal of קָרַח, row), both singular: "none".
- **"Neither shall men tear themselves for them in mourning, to comfort them for the dead" → "And they shall not break bread for them in mourning, to comfort him for the dead"** (v7) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִפְרְסוּ לָהֶם עַל־אֵבֶל; פָּרַס "break" (of bread: Isa 58:7; Lam 4:4; "bread" is not written here, and the verb says it; some read לֶחֶם "bread" for לָהֶם "for them", the MT is kept); לְנַחֲמוֹ "to comfort him" (singular; נִחַם piel "comfort", row). **"neither shall men give them the cup of consolation to drink for their father or for their mother" → "and they shall not give them the cup of consolations to drink for his father and for his mother"** — כּוֹס תַּנְחוּמִים "the cup of consolations" (plural); אָבִיו … אִמּוֹ "his father … his mother", with וְ "and".
- **"Thou shalt not also go into the house of feasting" → "And a house of feasting you shall not enter"** (v8) — Hebrew וּבֵית־מִשְׁתֶּה לֹא־תָבוֹא, the object first; מִשְׁתֶּה "feast" (row); לָשֶׁבֶת אוֹתָם "to sit with them".
- **"I will cause to cease out of this place" → "I am making cease from this place"** (v9) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מַשְׁבִּית, a participle after "behold"; הִשְׁבִּית "make cease" (row; the verb of שַׁבָּת). **"the voice of mirth, and the voice of gladness" → "the voice of joy and the voice of gladness"** — קוֹל שָׂשׂוֹן וְקוֹל שִׂמְחָה (row); חָתָן "bridegroom", כַּלָּה "bride" (rows).
- **"And it shall come to pass, when thou shalt shew this people" → "And it shall be, when you tell this people"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהָיָה כִּי (row); הִגִּיד "tell". **"Wherefore hath the LORD pronounced all this great evil against us?" → "Why has Yahweh spoken against us all this great evil?"** — עַל־מֶה "for what, why"; דִּבֶּר עָלֵינוּ "spoken against us"; רָעָה "evil" (row). **"or what is our iniquity? or what is our sin that we have committed" → "And what is our iniquity, and what is our sin that we have sinned"** — וּמֶה … וּמֶה; אֲשֶׁר חָטָאנוּ, one root ("sin … sinned"); חָטָא לְ "sin against" (row).
- **"Because your fathers have forsaken me … and have walked after other gods, and have served them, and have worshipped them" → "Because your fathers have forsaken me … and went after other gods and served them and bowed down to them"** (v11) — Hebrew עַל אֲשֶׁר "because"; three wayyiqtol forms after the perfect: told as past; הָלַךְ אַחֲרֵי אֱלֹהִים אֲחֵרִים "go after other gods" (row); הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" (row). **"and have forsaken me, and have not kept my law" → "and me they have forsaken, and my torah they have not kept"** — the objects first, as printed; תּוֹרָה token; שָׁמַר "keep" (row).
- **"And ye have done worse than your fathers" → "And you, you have done evil more than your fathers"** (v12) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם הֲרֵעֹתֶם לַעֲשׂוֹת מֵאֲבוֹתֵיכֶם, the pronoun written; literally "you have made evil to do, more than your fathers". **"ye walk every one after the imagination of his evil heart, that they may not hearken unto me" → "you are walking, each man after the stubbornness of his evil heart, so as not to listen to me"** — הִנְּכֶם הֹלְכִים, a participle; אִישׁ "each man" (row); שְׁרִרוּת לֵב "stubbornness of the heart" (row); לְבִלְתִּי שְׁמֹעַ אֵלַי "so as not to listen to me" (שָׁמַע אֶל, row).
- **"Therefore will I cast you out of this land into a land that ye know not" → "And I will hurl you from upon this land onto the land that you have not known"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהֵטַלְתִּי "and I will hurl" (hifil of טוּל, of throwing far: as 1 Sam 18:11; 20:33); מֵעַל … עַל "from upon … onto"; הָאָרֶץ "the land" (with the article); לֹא יְדַעְתֶּם, a perfect. **"where I will not shew you favour" → "for I will not give you favor"** — אֲשֶׁר לֹא־אֶתֵּן לָכֶם חֲנִינָה; אֲשֶׁר may be "for" or "where": both are read; חֲנִינָה "favor" (only here; of חָנַן "show favor").
- **"Therefore, behold, the days come" → "Therefore, behold, days are coming"** (v14) — Hebrew הִנֵּה־יָמִים בָּאִים, no article; a participle. **"that it shall no more be said, The LORD liveth" → "and it shall no more be said, 'As Yahweh lives'"** — וְלֹא־יֵאָמֵר עוֹד; חַי־יְהוָה "As Yahweh lives" (the oath, as 1 Sam 14:39). **"the children of Israel" → "the sons of Israel"** — בְּנֵי (Standing decisions; also v15).
- **"But, The LORD liveth" → "but, 'As Yahweh lives'"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי אִם after a negative, one saying put in place of the other: "but" (reviewer row; as 23:8): this alone will be said. **"whither he had driven them" → "where he had driven them"** — הִדִּיחָם, hifil "drive (away)" (the Deut 30:1 verb of the נִדַּח row). **"and I will bring them again into their land" → "And I will bring them back to their ground"** — וַהֲשִׁבֹתִים (שׁוּב hifil); אַדְמָתָם "their ground" (אֲדָמָה, row). Jer 23:7–8 says the same with small differences of its own (יֹאמְרוּ "they shall say"; "who brought up and who brought in the seed of the house of Israel"; "where I had driven them"; "and they shall dwell on their ground"): the wording here is set for it, each verse as printed.
- **"many fishers … many hunters"** (v16) — qere לְדַיָּגִים "for fishers" (the ketiv לדוגים, another form); וְדִיגוּם "and they shall fish them", וְצָדוּם "and they shall hunt them" (weqatal). **"and after will I send" → "and after this I will send"** — וְאַחֲרֵי־כֵן. **"from every mountain, and from every hill, and out of the holes of the rocks" → "from upon every mountain and from upon every hill, and from the clefts of the crags"** — מֵעַל "from upon"; נְקִיקֵי הַסְּלָעִים "the clefts of the crags" (as Jer 13:4; סֶלַע "crag", row).
- **"they are not hid from my face, neither is their iniquity hid from mine eyes" → "they are not concealed from before me, and their iniquity is not hidden away from before my eyes"** (v17) — Hebrew נִסְתְּרוּ "concealed" (נִסְתַּר, row) and נִצְפַּן "hidden away" (צָפַן, row): two verbs; מִלְּפָנַי "from before me", מִנֶּגֶד עֵינָי "from before my eyes" (literally "from in front of").
- **"And first I will recompense their iniquity and their sin double" → "And I will repay first the double of their iniquity and their sin"** (v18) — Hebrew וְשִׁלַּמְתִּי "and I will repay" (שִׁלֵּם, row); מִשְׁנֵה "the double" (row). **"because they have defiled my land" → "because they have profaned my land"** — חַלְּלָם, חִלֵּל "profane" (row; not "defile"). **"with the carcases of their detestable and abominable things" → "with the carcass of their detestable things and their abominations"** — בְּנִבְלַת "with the carcass" (singular; their idols called a lifeless body: נְבֵלָה, row); שִׁקּוּצִים "detestable things" (row), תּוֹעֵבוֹת "abominations" (row).
- **"O LORD, my strength, and my fortress, and my refuge in the day of affliction" → "Yahweh, my strength and my strong place, and my place of flight in the day of distress"** (v19) — Hebrew עֻזִּי "my strength" (עֹז, row); מָעֻזִּי "my strong place" (מָעוֹז, row); מְנוּסִי "my place of flight" (מָנוֹס, row; not "refuge"); צָרָה "distress" (row); no "O" (reviewer). **"the Gentiles shall come unto thee" → "to you nations shall come"** — the Hebrew order; גּוֹיִם "nations", no article (row). **"Surely our fathers have inherited lies, vanity, and things wherein there is no profit" → "Only falsehood have our fathers inherited, vapor, and there is nothing among them that profits"** — אַךְ "only" (or "surely": the one word); שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (row); נָחַל "inherit" (row); הֶבֶל "vapor" (row); וְאֵין־בָּם מוֹעִיל "and there is not among them one that profits" (participle of הוֹעִיל).
- **"Shall a man make gods unto himself, and they are no gods?" → "Shall a man make for himself gods, and they are not gods?"** (v20) — Hebrew הֲיַעֲשֶׂה־לּוֹ אָדָם אֱלֹהִים וְהֵמָּה לֹא אֱלֹהִים; אָדָם "man" (Standing decisions); אֱלֹהִים here of what men make: plain "gods" (the "gods" row); the question covers both halves (the verse is often read as two sentences: "… gods? And they are not gods!").
- **"I will this once cause them to know, I will cause them to know mine hand and my might" → "behold, I am making them know; this time I will make them know my hand and my might"** (v21) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מוֹדִיעָם, a participle after "behold"; בַּפַּעַם הַזֹּאת "this time" (accents: it opens the second line); אֶת־יָדִי "my hand" (not "power"); גְּבוּרָה "might" (row). **"and they shall know that my name is The LORD"** — וְיָדְעוּ כִּי־שְׁמִי יְהוָה (as the "and they shall know that I am Yahweh" formula).

### Notes
- **No wife, no mourning, no feast** (vv1–9) — three commands to the prophet, each a sign of what is coming.
- **Jer 16:14–15 = 23:7–8** — the same saying; Jer 23:7–8 follows this wording where the Hebrew is the same.
