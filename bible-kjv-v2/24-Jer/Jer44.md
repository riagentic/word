# Jeremiah 44

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ for all the ⟦judeans|Yehudim|Judeans⟧ who dwelt in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, who dwelt at Migdol and at ⟦tahpanhes|Tachpanches|Tahpanhes⟧ and at ⟦memphis|Nof|Memphis⟧ and in the land of ⟦pathros|Patros|Pathros⟧, saying:

[2] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: You have seen all the evil that I brought upon ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and upon all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; and behold, they are a ruin this day, and there is no one dwelling in them,

[3] because of their evil that they did to vex me, going to make smoke, to serve other gods whom they did not know, they, you and your fathers.

[4] And I sent to you all my servants the prophets, rising early and sending, saying, 'Please do not do this thing of abomination that I hate.'

[5] And they did not listen, and did not incline their ear, to turn back from their evil, so as not to make smoke to other gods.

[6] And my fury and my anger were poured out and burned in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they became a ruin, a desolation, as at this day.

[7] And now thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Why are you doing great evil against your souls, to cut off from you man and woman, child and suckling, from the midst of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, so as not to leave over a remnant for you,

[8] vexing me with the works of your hands, making smoke to other gods in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, where you are entering to sojourn, in order to cut yourselves off, and in order that you may become a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?

[9] Have you forgotten the evils of your fathers, and the evils of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and the evils of his wives, and your evils, and the evils of your wives, which they did in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?

[10] They have not been crushed to this day, and they have not feared, and they have not walked in my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and in my statutes, which I set before you and before your fathers.

[11] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I am putting my face against you for evil, and to cut off all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[12] And I will take the remnant of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who have set their faces to enter the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to sojourn there, and they shall all come to an end; in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ they shall fall; by the sword, by the famine they shall come to an end; from small to great, by the sword and by the famine they shall die; and they shall become a curse, a thing that appalls, and a cursing and a reproach.

[13] And I will visit upon those who dwell in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, as I visited upon ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, with the sword, with the famine and with the pestilence.

[14] And there shall be no fugitive or survivor for the remnant of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who are entering to sojourn there, in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and to return to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, to which they lift up their soul to return to dwell there; for they shall not return, except fugitives."

[15] And all the men who knew that their wives were making smoke to other gods, and all the women who stood by, a great assembly, and all the people who dwelt in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, in ⟦pathros|Patros|Pathros⟧, answered ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, saying,

[16] "The word that you have spoken to us in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — we are not listening to you.

[17] But we will surely do every word that has gone out of our mouth, to make smoke to the queen of the heavens and to pour out drink offerings to her, as we did, we and our fathers, our kings and our princes, in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; and we were sated with bread, and we were well, and evil we did not see.

[18] And from the time we ceased to make smoke to the queen of the heavens and to pour out drink offerings to her, we have lacked everything, and by the sword and by the famine we have come to an end.

[19] And when we make smoke to the queen of the heavens and pour out drink offerings to her, is it apart from our husbands that we have made cakes for her, to fashion her, and poured out drink offerings to her?"

[20] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said to all the people, to the grown men and to the women and to all the people who were answering him a word, saying,

[21] "Is it not the smoke that you made in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, you and your fathers, your kings and your princes and the people of the land — them ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ remembered, and it came up upon his heart?

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ could no longer bear, because of the evil of your doings, because of the abominations that you did; and your land became a ruin and a thing that appalls and a curse, without an inhabitant, as at this day.

[23] Because you made smoke and because you sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and did not listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and in his ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and in his statutes and in his testimonies you did not walk, therefore this evil has befallen you, as at this day."

[24] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said to all the people and to all the women, "Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who are in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[25] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: You and your wives — you have spoken with your mouth, and with your hands you have fulfilled it, saying, 'We will surely perform our vows that we have vowed, to make smoke to the queen of the heavens and to pour out drink offerings to her.' You shall surely make your vows stand, and you shall surely perform your vows!

[26] Therefore hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who dwell in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧: Behold, I have sworn by my great name, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that my name shall no more be called in the mouth of any man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying, 'As ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ lives,' in all the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[27] Behold, I am watching over them for evil and not for good; and every man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who is in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall come to an end by the sword and by the famine, until they are finished.

[28] And the fugitives of the sword shall return from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, few men; and all the remnant of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who have entered the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to sojourn there shall know whose word shall stand, mine or theirs.

[29] And this is the sign for you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that I am visiting upon you in this place, so that you may know that my words shall surely stand against you for evil:

[30] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am giving ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ ⟦hophra|Chofra|Hophra⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ into the hand of his enemies and into the hand of those who seek his soul, as I gave ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ into the hand of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, his enemy and the one who sought his soul."

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v2, 11, 25) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved; v7 has יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת "Yahweh, the Elohim of Hosts".
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v26) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה preserved; solemn oath formula.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 7, 11, 25) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; Jeremiah's word to the people in Egypt.
- **"concerning all the Jews which dwell in the land of Egypt, which dwell at Migdol" → "for all the Judeans who dwelt in the land of Egypt, who dwelt at Migdol"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶל "to, for" (the word is addressed to them); הַיֹּשְׁבִים twice, kept; נֹף "Nof" (Memphis); "in the land of Pathros" (Upper Egypt).
- **"all the evil … this day they are a desolation, and no man dwelleth therein" → "all the evil … they are a ruin this day, and there is no one dwelling in them"** (v2) — Hebrew רָעָה "evil" (also v11, 17, 23, 27, 29: not "disaster", "harm"); חָרְבָּה "ruin" (also v6, 22); וְהִנָּם "and behold, they".
- **"Because of their wickedness which they have committed to provoke me to anger, in that they went to burn incense, and to serve other gods … neither they, ye, nor your fathers" → "because of their evil that they did to vex me, going to make smoke, to serve other gods whom they did not know, they, you and your fathers"** (v3) — Hebrew הִכְעִיס "vex" (also v8; "anger" is not a second word); קִטֵּר without an object "make smoke" (the reviewer row; "burn incense" decides for incense; also v5, 8, 15, 17–19, 21, 23, 25); לָלֶכֶת לְקַטֵּר לַעֲבֹד, three infinitives with no "and"; הֵמָּה אַתֶּם וַאֲבֹתֵיכֶם "they, you and your fathers", the subjects of "did not know" (KJV's "neither … nor" is the sense, the negation standing once).
- **"Howbeit I sent unto you all my servants the prophets, rising early and sending them, saying, Oh, do not this abominable thing" → "And I sent to you all my servants the prophets, rising early and sending, saying, Please do not do this thing of abomination"** (v4) — Hebrew הַשְׁכֵּים וְשָׁלֹחַ, two infinitives absolute, no object (as Jer 25:4); אַל־נָא "please do not"; דְּבַר־הַתֹּעֵבָה "the thing of abomination" (דָּבָר "thing"); עֶבֶד of Yahweh's prophets "servant".
- **"But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear to turn from their wickedness, to burn no incense" → "And they did not listen, and did not incline their ear, to turn back from their evil, so as not to make smoke"** (v5) — שָׁמַע "listen"; שׁוּב "turn back"; לְבִלְתִּי "so as not to".
- **"Wherefore my fury and mine anger was poured forth, and was kindled … and they are wasted and desolate" → "And my fury and my anger were poured out and burned … and they became a ruin, a desolation"** (v6) — Hebrew וַתִּתַּךְ … וַתִּבְעַר, narrative "and"; the verbs are singular with the two nouns; חֵמָה "fury", אַף "anger"; לְחָרְבָּה לִשְׁמָמָה, no "and".
- **"Wherefore commit ye this great evil against your souls, to cut off from you … to leave you none to remain" → "Why are you doing great evil against your souls, to cut off from you … so as not to leave over a remnant for you"** (v7) — Hebrew רָעָה גְדוֹלָה, no "this"; עֹשִׂים, a participle; לְהַכְרִית לָכֶם, literally "to cut off for you"; עוֹלֵל וְיוֹנֵק "child and suckling"; הוֹתִיר "leave over"; שְׁאֵרִית "remnant".
- **"In that ye provoke me unto wrath … whither ye be gone to dwell, that ye might cut yourselves off" → "vexing me … where you are entering to sojourn, in order to cut yourselves off"** (v8) — Hebrew לְהַכְעִסֵנִי, an infinitive going on from v7; בָּאִים "entering" (בּוֹא "enter", as 42:14–22; also v12, 14, 28), לָגוּר "to sojourn"; לְמַעַן הַכְרִית לָכֶם, literally "in order to cut off for yourselves"; קְלָלָה "curse", חֶרְפָּה "reproach".
- **"the wickedness of your fathers … and the wickedness of their wives" → "the evils of your fathers … and the evils of his wives"** (v9) — Hebrew רָעוֹת, plural, five times; נָשָׁיו "his wives" (each king's: the MT; the Septuagint has "your princes", from memory); עָשׂוּ "they did".
- **"They are not humbled even unto this day" → "They have not been crushed to this day"** (v10) — Hebrew דֻּכְּאוּ, pual of דָּכָא "crush" ("humbled" is the sense: broken in spirit); נָתַן לִפְנֵי "set before" (the reviewer row); חֻקֹּת "statutes".
- **"Behold, I will set my face against you for evil, and to cut off all Judah" → "Behold, I am putting my face against you for evil, and to cut off all Judah"** (v11) — Hebrew הִנְנִי שָׂם, a participle; שִׂים פָּנִים בְּ "put my face against" (the row; נָתַן is "set"); לְרָעָה "for evil". שִׂים פָּנִים לְ of a purpose (v12; 42:15, 17) stays "set their faces to".
- **"and they shall all be consumed, and fall in the land of Egypt; they shall even be consumed by the sword and by the famine: they shall die, from the least even unto the greatest … and they shall be an execration, and an astonishment, and a curse, and a reproach" → "and they shall all come to an end; in the land of Egypt they shall fall; by the sword, by the famine they shall come to an end; from small to great, by the sword and by the famine they shall die; and they shall become a curse, a thing that appalls, and a cursing and a reproach"** (v12, 18, 27) — Hebrew תַּם "come to an end" (three times; not "be consumed": that is אָכַל); the clauses as the accents divide them; אָלָה "curse" and קְלָלָה "cursing" side by side, as 42:18; שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (also v22).
- **"For I will punish them that dwell in the land of Egypt, as I have punished Jerusalem" → "And I will visit upon those who dwell in the land of Egypt, as I visited upon Jerusalem"** (v13, 29) — Hebrew פָּקַד עַל "visit upon" (the פָּקַד row: attend to, here for punishment).
- **"So that none of the remnant of Judah … shall escape or remain, that they should return into the land of Judah, to the which they have a desire to return … for none shall return but such as shall escape" → "And there shall be no fugitive or survivor for the remnant of Judah … and to return to the land of Judah, to which they lift up their soul to return … for they shall not return, except fugitives"** (v14) — Hebrew פָּלִיט "fugitive", שָׂרִיד "survivor"; וְלָשׁוּב "and to return" goes on from "no fugitive or survivor"; מְנַשְּׂאִים אֶת־נַפְשָׁם "lift up their soul" (= long, the נָשָׂא נֶפֶשׁ row); כִּי אִם "except"; פְּלֵטִים "fugitives".
- **"and all the women that stood by, a great multitude, even all the people that dwelt in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah" → "and all the women who stood by, a great assembly, and all the people who dwelt in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying"** (v15) — Hebrew מְקַטְּרוֹת "were making smoke", a participle; קָהָל "assembly"; וְכָל־הָעָם "and all the people". The Hebrew begins with the verb ("And they answered Jeremiah, all the men …"); English puts the subjects first.
- **"As for the word … we will not hearken unto thee" → "The word … — we are not listening to you"** (v16) — Hebrew אֵינֶנּוּ שֹׁמְעִים, a participle.
- **"But we will certainly do whatsoever thing goeth forth out of our own mouth, to burn incense unto the queen of heaven … for then had we plenty of victuals, and were well, and saw no evil" → "But we will surely do every word that has gone out of our mouth, to make smoke to the queen of the heavens … and we were sated with bread, and we were well, and evil we did not see"** (v17) — Hebrew עָשֹׂה נַעֲשֶׂה, the infinitive absolute ("surely do"; literally "doing we will do"; also v25); כָּל־הַדָּבָר "every word"; יָצָא "has gone out"; הִסִּיךְ נְסָכִים "pour out drink offerings" (also v18, 19, 25); שָׂרִים "princes" (also v21); וַנִּשְׂבַּע־לֶחֶם "and we were sated with bread" (no "then"); וַנִּהְיֶה טוֹבִים "and we were well" (literally "we were good").- **"the queen of heaven" → "the queen of the heavens"** (v17, 18, 19, 25; as 7:18) — Hebrew מְלֶכֶת הַשָּׁמַיִם; שָׁמַיִם is plural ("the heavens"). The pointed form מְלֶכֶת is read as a form of מַלְכָּה "queen" (מַלְכַּת "queen of"), and v17–19, 25 speak of "her" (לָהּ): the verse renders it so. Another reading takes the vowels as those of מְלֶאכֶת "work, service": "the work (service) of the heavens", i.e. the host of the heavens, a reading that would avoid naming a goddess; many manuscripts write מלאכת (from memory). No capitals: the Hebrew gives a title, not a name.
- **"But since we left off to burn incense … we have wanted all things, and have been consumed by the sword" → "And from the time we ceased to make smoke … we have lacked everything, and by the sword and by the famine we have come to an end"** (v18) — Hebrew וּמִן־אָז "and from then"; חָדַל "cease"; חָסֵר "lack"; תַּמְנוּ "we have come to an end".
- **"And when we burned incense to the queen of heaven … did we make her cakes to worship her … without our men?" → "And when we make smoke to the queen of the heavens … is it apart from our husbands that we have made cakes for her, to fashion her …?"** (v19) — the Hebrew has no "And the women said" (the Septuagint and others supply it, from memory; v15 and v20 show that women speak with the men); מְקַטְּרִים, a masculine participle in the MT; הֲמִבַּלְעֲדֵי אֲנָשֵׁינוּ "is it apart from our husbands" (אִישׁ "man / husband"); כַּוָּנִים "cakes" (found only here and 7:18; a loan word, uncertain); לְהַעֲצִבָה "to fashion her" (hifil of עָצַב "shape, fashion", Job 10:8: cakes in her likeness; uncertain; others "to grieve her" or "to worship her").
- **"to the men, and to the women, and to all the people which had given him that answer" → "to the grown men and to the women and to all the people who were answering him a word"** (v20) — Hebrew עַל "to" (used for אֶל); הַגְּבָרִים, adult males beside the women (the גֶּבֶר row); הָעֹנִים אֹתוֹ דָּבָר, a participle, literally "answering him a word".
- **"The incense that ye burned … did not the LORD remember them, and came it not into his mind?" → "Is it not the smoke that you made … — them Yahweh remembered, and it came up upon his heart?"** (v21) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא אֶת־הַקִּטֵּר … אֹתָם זָכַר יְהוָה וַתַּעֲלֶה עַל־לִבּוֹ, literally "Is it not the smoke that you made smoke … — them Yahweh remembered, and it came up upon his heart?": the question word stands once, at the head, and the sentence takes up "the smoke" again with "them"; the KJV asks two questions. הַקִּטֵּר, a noun of קִטֵּר, found only here: "the smoke" (of the offering); the verb is rendered "made". עָלָה עַל לֵב "come up upon the heart" (= come to mind).
- **"So that the LORD could no longer bear, because of the evil of your doings … therefore is your land a desolation, and an astonishment, and a curse" → "And Yahweh could no longer bear, because of the evil of your doings, because of the abominations … and your land became a ruin and a thing that appalls and a curse"** (v22) — Hebrew לָשֵׂאת "bear" with no object; מִפְּנֵי "because of" (twice, the second without "and"); רֹעַ מַעַלְלֵיכֶם "the evil of your doings"; וַתְּהִי "and became" (no "therefore"); מֵאֵין יוֹשֵׁב "without an inhabitant" (literally "from there being none dwelling").
- **"Because ye have burned incense … therefore this evil is happened unto you" → "Because you made smoke … therefore this evil has befallen you"** (v23) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי אֲשֶׁר "because"; חָטָא לַיהוָה "sinned against Yahweh"; the Hebrew puts "in his torah and in his statutes and in his testimonies" before "you did not walk"; עֵדְוֹת "testimonies"; קָרָאת, the verb קָרָה "befall" written with א.
- **"Hear the word of the LORD, all Judah that are in the land of Egypt" → "Hear the word of Yahweh, all Judah who are in the land of Egypt"** (v24, 26) — כָּל־יְהוּדָה "all Judah" (no "you of").
- **"Ye and your wives have both spoken with your mouths, and fulfilled with your hand … ye will surely accomplish your vows, and surely perform your vows" → "You and your wives — you have spoken with your mouth, and with your hands you have fulfilled it … You shall surely make your vows stand, and you shall surely perform your vows!"** (v25) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר after "Thus says" is kept ("saying"); וַתְּדַבֵּרְנָה, a feminine plural verb ("you women have spoken"; the Septuagint has "you women", from memory), then מִלֵּאתֶם, masculine plural; בְּפִיכֶם "with your mouth", בִידֵיכֶם "with your hands"; "it" supplied after "fulfilled"; נָדַר נֶדֶר "vow a vow"; הָקֵים תָּקִימְנָה … וְעָשֹׂה תַעֲשֶׂינָה, infinitives absolute with feminine imperfects: "you shall surely make … stand … surely perform" (הֵקִים of a vow "make stand", its row) — a bitter permission: go and keep them.
- **"that my name shall no more be named in the mouth of any man of Judah in all the land of Egypt, saying, The Lord GOD liveth" → "that my name shall no more be called in the mouth of any man of Judah, saying, As the Lord Yahweh lives, in all the land of Egypt"** (v26) — Hebrew אָמַר יְהוָה "says Yahweh" (not נְאֻם); the oath formula אִם־יִהְיֶה עוֹד שְׁמִי נִקְרָא, literally "if my name shall again be called": by its meaning "shall no more be called"; נִקְרָא "be called"; the Hebrew order kept ("in all the land of Egypt" last); חַי־אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה "As the Lord Yahweh lives" (the oath words).
- **"Behold, I will watch over them for evil … and all the men of Judah … shall be consumed … until there be an end of them" → "Behold, I am watching over them for evil … and every man of Judah … shall come to an end … until they are finished"** (v27) — Hebrew שֹׁקֵד, a participle of שָׁקַד "keep awake, watch" (as 1:12; 31:28); כָל־אִישׁ יְהוּדָה "every man of Judah" with a plural verb; עַד־כְּלוֹתָם "until they are finished".
- **"Yet a small number that escape the sword shall return … whose words shall stand, mine, or theirs" → "And the fugitives of the sword shall return … few men … whose word shall stand, mine or theirs"** (v28) — Hebrew פְלִיטֵי חֶרֶב "the fugitives of the sword"; מְתֵי מִסְפָּר "few men" (literally "men of number": its row); דְּבַר־מִי יָקוּם מִמֶּנִּי וּמֵהֶם "whose word shall stand, from me or from them", singular "word".
- **"And this shall be a sign unto you, saith the LORD, that I will punish you in this place, that ye may know that my words shall surely stand against you for evil" → "And this is the sign for you, declares Yahweh, that I am visiting upon you in this place, so that you may know that my words shall surely stand against you for evil"** (v29) — Hebrew וְזֹאת־לָכֶם הָאוֹת, no verb, with the article "the sign"; נְאֻם־יְהוָה "declares Yahweh"; פֹקֵד, a participle; קוֹם יָקוּמוּ, the infinitive absolute ("surely stand").
- **"Behold, I will give Pharaohhophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies, and into the hand of them that seek his life" → "Behold, I am giving Pharaoh Hophra … into the hand of those who seek his soul … his enemy and the one who sought his soul"** (v30) — Hebrew נֹתֵן, a participle; מְבַקְשֵׁי נַפְשׁוֹ "those who seek his soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul", the life sought); אֹיְבוֹ וּמְבַקֵּשׁ נַפְשׁוֹ "his enemy and the one seeking his soul" (no "who was"). The token form "Nevuchadretzar" follows the Hebrew נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר.

### Notes
- **The queen of the heavens** — likely Ishtar / Astarte (from memory).
- **Pharaoh Hophra** (Hebrew חָפְרַע; Greek Apries) — killed about 570 BC (from memory).
