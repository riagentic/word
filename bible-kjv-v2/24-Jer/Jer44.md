# Jeremiah 44

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ concerning all the ⟦judeans|Yehudim|Judeans⟧ who lived in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, at Migdol, at ⟦tahpanhes|Tachpanches|Tahpanhes⟧, at ⟦memphis|Nof|Memphis⟧, and in the land of ⟦pathros|Patros|Pathros⟧:

[2] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: You have seen all the disaster that I brought upon ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and upon all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. Behold, this day they are a desolation, and no one dwells in them,

[3] because of the evil that they committed, provoking me to anger, in that they went to make offerings and serve other gods that they knew not, neither they, nor you, nor your fathers.

[4] Yet I persistently sent to you all my servants the prophets, saying, 'Oh, do not do this abomination that I hate!'

[5] But they did not listen or incline their ear, to turn from their evil and make no offerings to other gods.

[6] Therefore my wrath and my anger were poured out and kindled in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they became a waste and a desolation, as at this day.

[7] "And now thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Why do you commit this great evil against your souls, to cut off from you man and woman, infant and child, from the midst of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, leaving you no remnant?

[8] Why do you provoke me to anger with the works of your hands, making offerings to other gods in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ where you have come to live, that you may be cut off and become a curse and a taunt among all the nations of the earth?

[9] Have you forgotten the evil of your fathers, the evil of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, the evil of their wives, your own evil, and the evil of your wives, which they committed in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?

[10] They have not humbled themselves even to this day, nor have they feared, nor walked in my ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and my statutes that I set before you and before your fathers.

[11] "Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I will set my face against you for harm, to cut off all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[12] I will take the remnant of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who have set their faces to come to the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to live, and they shall all be consumed. In the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ they shall fall; by the sword and by famine they shall be consumed. From the least to the greatest, they shall die by the sword and by famine, and they shall become an oath, a horror, a curse, and a taunt.

[13] I will punish those who dwell in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, as I have punished ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, with the sword, with famine, and with pestilence,

[14] so that none of the remnant of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who have come to live in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall escape or survive or return to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, to which they desire to return to dwell there. For they shall not return, except some fugitives."

[15] Then all the men who knew that their wives had made offerings to other gods, and all the women who stood by, a great assembly, all the people who lived in ⟦pathros|Patros|Pathros⟧ in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, answered ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧:

[16] "As for the word that you have spoken to us in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, we will not listen to you.

[17] But we will do everything that we have vowed, make offerings to the Queen of Heaven and pour out drink offerings to her, as we did, both we and our fathers, our kings and our officials, in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. For then we had plenty of food, and prospered, and saw no disaster.

[18] But since we left off making offerings to the Queen of Heaven and pouring out drink offerings to her, we have lacked everything and have been consumed by the sword and by famine."

[19] And the women said, "When we made offerings to the Queen of Heaven and poured out drink offerings to her, was it without our husbands' approval that we made cakes for her bearing her image and poured out drink offerings to her?"

[20] Then ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said to all the people, men and women, all the people who had given him this answer:

[21] "As for the offerings that you offered in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, you and your fathers, your kings and your officials, and the people of the land, did not ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ remember them? Did it not come into his mind?

[22] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ could no longer bear your evil deeds and the abominations that you committed. Therefore your land has become a desolation and a waste and a curse, without inhabitant, as it is this day.

[23] It is because you made offerings and sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and did not obey the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ or walk in his ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and in his statutes and in his testimonies that this disaster has happened to you, as at this day."

[24] ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said to all the people and all the women, "Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all you of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who are in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[25] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: You and your wives have declared with your mouths, and have fulfilled it with your hands, saying, 'We will surely perform our vows that we have made, to make offerings to the Queen of Heaven and to pour out drink offerings to her.' Then confirm your vows and perform your vows!

[26] Therefore hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all you of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who dwell in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧: Behold, I have sworn by my great name, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that my name shall no more be invoked by the mouth of any man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ in all the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, saying, 'As ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ lives.'

[27] Behold, I am watching over them for disaster and not for good. All the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who are in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall be consumed by the sword and by famine, until there is an end of them.

[28] And those who escape the sword shall return from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, few in number; and all the remnant of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, who came to the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ to live, shall know whose word will stand, mine or theirs.

[29] "This shall be the sign to you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that I will punish you in this place, in order that you may know that my words will surely stand against you for harm:

[30] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I will give ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ ⟦hophra|Chofra|Hophra⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ into the hand of his enemies and into the hand of those who seek his life, as I gave ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ into the hand of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, who was his enemy and sought his life."

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv2, 7, 11, 25) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v26) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה preserved; solemn oath formula.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv7, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; Jeremiah's oracle to the community in Egypt.

### Notes
- **Queen of Heaven** — likely Ishtar/Astarte.
- **Pharaoh Chofra** (Hebrew חָפְרַע; KJV Hophra, Greek Apries) — assassinated 570 BC.
