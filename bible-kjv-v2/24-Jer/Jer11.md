# Jeremiah 11

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying:

[2] "Hear the words of this covenant, and you shall speak them to the man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[3] And you shall say to them, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Cursed is the man who does not hear the words of this covenant,

[4] which I commanded your fathers in the day I brought them out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the furnace of iron, saying, Listen to my voice, and do them, according to all that I command you; and you shall be to me a people, and I will be to you ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[5] in order to establish the oath that I swore to your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as at this day." And I answered and said, "Amen, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧."

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Call out all these words in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying, Hear the words of this covenant, and do them.

[7] For I surely warned your fathers in the day I brought them up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and to this day, rising early and warning, saying, Listen to my voice.

[8] And they did not listen and did not incline their ear, and they walked, each man in the stubbornness of their evil heart; and I brought upon them all the words of this covenant, which I commanded to do, and they did not do."

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "A conspiracy is found among the man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and among the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] They have turned back to the iniquities of their fathers, the former ones, who refused to hear my words; and they have gone after other gods to serve them. The house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ have broken my covenant that I cut with their fathers.

[11] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am bringing evil upon them, from which they will not be able to go out; and they will cry out to me, and I will not listen to them.

[12] And the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ will go and cry out to the gods to whom they make smoke, but they will surely not save them in the time of their evil.

[13] For the number of your cities have been your gods, ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; and the number of the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ you have set altars to the shame, altars to make smoke to the Baal.

[14] And you, do not pray for this people, and do not lift up for them a cry and a prayer, for I am not hearing in the time of their calling to me because of their evil.

[15] What is it to my beloved in my house,
her doing the scheme, the many?
And holy flesh shall pass away from upon you;
for your evil, then you are jubilant.

[16] A luxuriant olive tree, beautiful in fruit, in form,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ called your name.
At the sound of a great roaring
he has kindled fire upon it,
and its branches are broken.

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, who planted you, has spoken evil against you, because of the evil of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and of the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, which they have done for themselves, to vex me by making smoke to the Baal."

[18] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made me know, and I knew; then you showed me their deeds.

[19] And I was like a tame lamb that is led to slaughter; and I did not know that against me they devised devices: "Let us ruin the tree with its bread, and let us cut him off from the land of the living, and his name shall be remembered no more."

[20] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts, who judges righteously, who tries the kidneys and the heart: let me see your vengeance on them, for to you I have uncovered my case.

[21] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the men of ⟦anathoth|Anatot|Anathoth⟧, who seek your soul, saying, "You shall not prophesy in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and you shall not die by our hand" —

[22] therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: "Behold, I am visiting upon them; the young men shall die by the sword, their sons and their daughters shall die by the famine,

[23] and a remnant shall not be to them; for I will bring evil upon the men of ⟦anathoth|Anatot|Anathoth⟧, the year of their visitation."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v17, 20, 22). **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 4).
- **Layout** — v15–16 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order; the rest is prose, as printed.
- **"from the LORD" → "from Yahweh, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר "saying", kept.
- **"and speak unto the men of Judah" → "and you shall speak them to the man of Judah"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ "hear" (plural), וְדִבַּרְתָּם "and you shall speak them" (singular, with the object "them"); אִישׁ יְהוּדָה "the man of Judah", a collective (also v9); עַל "to", literally "upon".
- **"Cursed be the man that obeyeth not" → "Cursed is the man who does not hear"** (v3) — Hebrew אָרוּר הָאִישׁ, no verb ("is", not "be"); שָׁמַע "hear".
- **"from the iron furnace" → "from the furnace of iron"** (v4) — Hebrew מִכּוּר הַבַּרְזֶל (כּוּר, a furnace for smelting). **"Obey my voice, and do them" → "Listen to my voice, and do them"** — שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of"; אוֹתָם "them", the words. **"so shall ye be my people" → "and you shall be to me a people"** — וִהְיִיתֶם לִי לְעָם, "and" kept; לִי "to me".
- **"That I may perform the oath" → "in order to establish the oath"** (v5) — Hebrew לְמַעַן הָקִים, with an infinitive; הֵקִים "establish" (literally "make stand"). **"a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is this day" → "as at this day"** — כַּיּוֹם הַזֶּה. **"Then answered I, and said, So be it, O LORD" → "And I answered and said, Amen, Yahweh"** — וָאַעַן וָאֹמַר אָמֵן, the Hebrew word kept; no "O".
- **"Proclaim all these words" → "Call out all these words"** (v6) — Hebrew קְרָא. **"saying"** — לֵאמֹר, kept.
- **"For I earnestly protested unto your fathers" → "For I surely warned your fathers"** (v7) — Hebrew הָעֵד הַעִדֹתִי, infinitive absolute; הֵעִיד בְּ "warn" (literally "testify against": a formal notice). **"rising early and protesting" → "rising early and warning"** — הַשְׁכֵּם וְהָעֵד, two infinitives absolute. **"when I brought them up" → "in the day I brought them up"** — בְּיוֹם הַעֲלוֹתִי (הֶעֱלָה "bring up").
- **"Yet they obeyed not" → "And they did not listen"** (v8) — Hebrew וְלֹא שָׁמְעוּ, וְ "and". **"but walked every one in the imagination of their evil heart" → "and they walked, each man in the stubbornness of their evil heart"** — אִישׁ "each man" with the plural "their"; שְׁרִירוּת לֵב "stubbornness of the heart". **"therefore I will bring upon them" → "and I brought upon them"** — וָאָבִיא, a past form; "therefore" is not in the Hebrew. **"but they did them not" → "and they did not do"** — וְלֹא עָשׂוּ, no object.
- **"And the LORD said unto me, A conspiracy is found" → "And Yahweh said to me, A conspiracy is found"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר; "Again" is not in the Hebrew; נִמְצָא "is found"; קֶשֶׁר "conspiracy" (קָשַׁר "conspire"); בְּ "among" twice.
- **"the iniquities of their forefathers" → "the iniquities of their fathers, the former ones"** (v10) — Hebrew אֲבוֹתָם הָרִאשֹׁנִים, "their fathers, the first / former" (the adjective goes with "fathers"). **"and they went after other gods" → "and they have gone after other gods"** — וְהֵמָּה הָלְכוּ, the pronoun stressed ("and they"); perfect. **"which I made with their fathers" → "that I cut with their fathers"** — כָּרַתִּי, כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant"; הֵפֵרוּ "have broken".
- **"I will bring evil upon them" → "I am bringing evil upon them"** (v11) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, participle with הִנֵּה; רָעָה "evil" ("disaster" is not the word). **"which they shall not be able to escape" → "from which they will not be able to go out"** — לָצֵאת מִמֶּנָּה. **"and though they shall cry unto me, I will not hearken unto them" → "and they will cry out to me, and I will not listen to them"** — וְזָעֲקוּ … וְלֹא אֶשְׁמַע, two clauses joined by "and", no "though"; שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to".
- **"unto whom they offer incense" → "to whom they make smoke"** (v12, 13, 17) — Hebrew מְקַטְּרִים, קִטֵּר without an object: "make smoke" ("burn incense" would decide what is burned). **"but they shall not save them at all" → "but they will surely not save them"** — הוֹשֵׁעַ לֹא־יוֹשִׁיעוּ, infinitive absolute. **"in the time of their trouble" → "in the time of their evil"** — רָעָתָם, the word of v11 (also v14, 17).
- **"For according to the number of thy cities were thy gods, O Judah" → "For the number of your cities have been your gods, Judah"** (v13) — Hebrew מִסְפַּר עָרֶיךָ הָיוּ אֱלֹהֶיךָ, literally "the number of your cities your gods have been"; "gods" plural verb, plain; no "O". **"ye set up altars to that shameful thing" → "you have set altars to the shame"** — שַׂמְתֶּם; לַבֹּשֶׁת "to the shame" (בֹּשֶׁת "shame", with the article: the Baal is meant). **"altars to burn incense unto Baal" → "altars to make smoke to the Baal"** — לַבָּעַל, the article kept (also v17).
- **"Therefore pray not thou for this people" → "And you, do not pray for this people"** (v14) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה, "and you", stressed; no "therefore". **"neither lift up a cry or prayer for them" → "and do not lift up for them a cry and a prayer"** — רִנָּה וּתְפִלָּה (רִנָּה "a ringing cry", here of pleading). **"for I will not hear them in the time that they cry unto me for their trouble" → "for I am not hearing in the time of their calling to me because of their evil"** — אֵינֶנִּי שֹׁמֵעַ, participle; בְּעַד רָעָתָם "on account of their evil" (בְּעַד, the word of "for this people"); "in the time of" (בְּעֵת) is read here by some Hebrew manuscripts and the versions (from memory): the MT is kept.
- **"What hath my beloved to do in mine house, seeing she hath wrought lewdness with many" → "What is it to my beloved in my house, her doing the scheme, the many?"** (v15) — Hebrew מֶה לִידִידִי בְּבֵיתִי עֲשׂוֹתָהּ הַמְזִמָּתָה הָרַבִּים: יְדִיד "beloved", spoken of a woman; מְזִמָּה "scheme, plot" (root זָמַם "devise"); הָרַבִּים "the many" stands alone. **"and the holy flesh is passed from thee? when thou doest evil, then thou rejoicest" → "And holy flesh shall pass away from upon you; for your evil, then you are jubilant"** — בְּשַׂר־קֹדֶשׁ "holy flesh" (of sacrifices); יַעַבְרוּ, a future form; כִּי רָעָתֵכִי "for / when your evil"; עָלַז "be jubilant". The whole verse is very uncertain; the Septuagint has quite other words (from memory). Word for word kept; nothing supplied.
- **"The LORD called thy name, A green olive tree, fair, and of goodly fruit" → "A luxuriant olive tree, beautiful in fruit, in form, Yahweh called your name"** (v16) — Hebrew זַיִת רַעֲנָן יְפֵה פְרִי־תֹאַר, the Hebrew order; רַעֲנָן "luxuriant" (not the colour); יְפֵה פְרִי־תֹאַר, literally "beautiful of fruit of form"; "once" is not in the Hebrew. **"with the noise of a great tumult he hath kindled fire upon it" → "At the sound of a great roaring he has kindled fire upon it"** — הֲמוּלָּה "roaring" (also Ezek 1:24); הִצִּית, perfect. **"and the branches of it are broken"** — וְרָעוּ, from רָעַע "be broken" (the same letters as "be evil": uncertain).
- **"hath pronounced evil against thee" → "has spoken evil against you"** (v17) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר עָלַיִךְ רָעָה. **"for the evil … which they have done against themselves" → "because of the evil … which they have done for themselves"** — בִּגְלַל "because of"; לָהֶם "for themselves". **"to provoke me to anger" → "to vex me"** — הִכְעִיס "vex".
- **"And the LORD hath given me knowledge of it, and I know it" → "And Yahweh made me know, and I knew"** (v18) — Hebrew הוֹדִיעַנִי וָאֵדָעָה, no "it". **"then thou shewedst me their doings" → "then you showed me their deeds"** — מַעַלְלֵיהֶם.
- **"But I was like a lamb or an ox that is brought to the slaughter" → "And I was like a tame lamb that is led to slaughter"** (v19) — Hebrew כְּכֶבֶשׂ אַלּוּף, אַלּוּף "tame, familiar" (the word of "close friend"; KJV took it as "ox"); יוּבַל "is led"; טָבַח "slaughter". **"they had devised devices against me" → "against me they devised devices"** — חָשְׁבוּ מַחֲשָׁבוֹת, the object first; "saying" is not in the Hebrew (the words follow at once). **"Let us destroy the tree with the fruit thereof" → "Let us ruin the tree with its bread"** — נַשְׁחִיתָה (שָׁחַת "ruin"); בְּלַחְמוֹ "with its bread" (= its fruit: the word kept; "with its sap" by a change of one letter, from memory). **"and let us cut him off from the land of the living, that his name may be no more remembered" → "and let us cut him off from the land of the living, and his name shall be remembered no more"** — וּשְׁמוֹ לֹא־יִזָּכֵר עוֹד, "and" kept.
- **"But, O LORD of hosts, that judgest righteously, that triest the reins and the heart" → "And Yahweh of Hosts, who judges righteously, who tries the kidneys and the heart"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיהוָה צְבָאוֹת, "and"; שֹׁפֵט צֶדֶק "judging righteousness"; בֹּחֵן כְּלָיוֹת וָלֵב: בָּחַן "try"; כְּלָיוֹת "kidneys" (the seat of the inmost thoughts; the gloss "kidneys/emotions" is removed). **"let me see thy vengeance on them" → "let me see your vengeance on them"** — אֶרְאֶה, also "I shall see". **"for unto thee have I revealed my cause" → "for to you I have uncovered my case"** — גִּלִּיתִי (גִּלָּה "uncover"); רִיב "case".
- **"that seek thy life" → "who seek your soul"** (v21) — Hebrew נַפְשְׁךָ "your soul". **"Prophesy not … that thou die not by our hand" → "You shall not prophesy … and you shall not die by our hand"** — לֹא תִנָּבֵא … וְלֹא תָמוּת, two clauses with לֹא and "and": a prohibition, and what follows if it is kept.
- **"Behold, I will punish them" → "Behold, I am visiting upon them"** (v22) — Hebrew הִנְנִי פֹקֵד עֲלֵיהֶם, participle; פָּקַד עַל "visit upon". **"by famine" → "by the famine"** — בָּרָעָב, the article.
- **"And there shall be no remnant of them" → "and a remnant shall not be to them"** (v23) — Hebrew וּשְׁאֵרִית לֹא תִהְיֶה לָהֶם; שְׁאֵרִית "remnant". **"even the year of their visitation"** — שְׁנַת פְּקֻדָּתָם, no "even"; "visitation" (פְּקֻדָּה), not "punishment".

### Notes
- **Jeremiah's own town** (Anathoth) plots against him (v21–23); v18–23 begin the passages often called his "confessions".
