# Jeremiah 11

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

[2] "Hear the words of this covenant, and speak to the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[3] You shall say to them, Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Cursed be the man who does not hear the words of this covenant

[4] that I commanded your fathers when I brought them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the iron furnace, saying, Listen to my voice, and do all that I command you. So shall you be my people, and I will be your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[5] that I may confirm the oath that I swore to your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as at this day." Then I answered, "Amen, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧."

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Proclaim all these words in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: Hear the words of this covenant and do them.

[7] For I solemnly warned your fathers when I brought them up out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, warning them persistently, even to this day, saying, Listen to my voice.

[8] Yet they did not listen or incline their ear, but everyone walked in the stubbornness of his evil heart. Therefore I brought upon them all the words of this covenant, which I commanded them to do, but they did not."

[9] Again ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "A conspiracy exists among the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] They have turned back to the iniquities of their forefathers, who refused to hear my words. They have gone after other gods to serve them. The house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ have broken my covenant that I made with their fathers.

[11] Therefore, thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, Behold, I am bringing disaster upon them that they cannot escape. Though they cry to me, I will not listen to them.

[12] Then the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ will go and cry to the gods to whom they make offerings, but they cannot save them in the time of their trouble.

[13] For your gods have become as many as your cities, O ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and as many as the streets of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ are the altars you have set up to shame, altars to make offerings to Baal.

[14] "Therefore do not pray for this people, or lift up a cry or prayer on their behalf, for I will not listen when they call to me in the time of their trouble.

[15] What right has my beloved in my house,
when she has done many vile deeds?
Can sacrificial flesh avert your doom?
Can you then exult?

[16] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ once called you a green olive tree,
beautiful with good fruit;
but with the roar of a great tempest
he will set fire to it,
and its branches will be consumed.

[17] "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts, who planted you, has decreed disaster against you, because of the evil that the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ have done, provoking me to anger by making offerings to Baal."

[18] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ made it known to me and I knew; then you showed me their deeds.

[19] But I was like a gentle lamb led to the slaughter. I did not know it was against me they devised schemes, saying, "Let us destroy the tree with its fruit, let us cut him off from the land of the living, that his name be remembered no more."

[20] But, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts, who judges righteously, who tests the inmost being — kidneys/emotions — and the heart, let me see your vengeance upon them, for to you have I committed my cause.

[21] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the men of ⟦anathoth|Anatot|Anathoth⟧, who seek your life, and say, "Do not prophesy in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, or you will die by our hand" —

[22] therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: "Behold, I will punish them. The young men shall die by the sword, their sons and their daughters shall die by famine,

[23] and none of them shall be left. For I will bring disaster upon the men of ⟦anathoth|Anatot|Anathoth⟧, the year of their punishment."

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv17, 20, 22) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3-4) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved; "the *Elohim* of Israel."
- **Poetic structure** — vv15-16 formatted as verse oracle within prose narrative; first Jeremianic "confession" begins.

### Notes
- **Jeremiah's own town** (Anathoth) plots against him (v21-23) — begins his "confessions."
