# Jeremiah 22

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Go down to the house of the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and speak there this word,

[2] and say: Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, who sits on the throne of David, you and your servants and your people who come in by these gates.

[3] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Do judgment and righteousness, and deliver the robbed from the hand of the extortioner; and the sojourner, the fatherless and the widow do not wrong, do not do violence to, and innocent blood do not shed in this place.

[4] For if you surely do this word, then there shall come in by the gates of this house kings sitting for David on his throne, riding in chariots and on horses, he and his servants and his people.

[5] But if you do not hear these words, by myself I have sworn, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that this house shall become a ruin.

[6] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the house of the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧:

⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ you are to me,
the head of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧;
surely I will make you a wilderness,
cities that are not inhabited.

[7] And I will sanctify destroyers against you,
each man and his weapons;
and they shall cut down the choicest of your cedars
and make them fall into the fire.

[8] And many nations shall pass by this city, and they shall say, each man to his neighbor, "Why has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ done thus to this great city?"

[9] And they shall say, "Because they forsook the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and bowed down to other gods and served them."

[10] Do not weep for the dead,
and do not show grief for him;
surely weep for him who goes away,
for he shall not return again
and see the land of his kindred.

[11] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning Shallum son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, who reigned in the place of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ his father, who went out from this place: He shall not return there again;

[12] but in the place where they have carried him into exile, there he shall die, and this land he shall not see again.

[13] Woe to him who builds his house without righteousness,
and his upper rooms without judgment,
who makes his neighbor serve for nothing
and does not give him his wages,

[14] who says, "I will build myself a house of measures
and upper rooms made spacious";
and he cuts out windows for it,
and it is paneled with cedar
and painted with vermilion.

[15] Shall you reign
because you are competing in cedar?
Your father, did he not eat and drink
and do judgment and righteousness?
Then it was good for him.

[16] He judged the cause of the afflicted and the needy;
then it was good.
Is not that the knowing of me? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] For your eyes and your heart are on nothing
but your unjust gain,
and on the innocent blood, to shed it,
and on extortion and on crushing, to do it.

[18] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧ son of ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧:

They shall not lament for him,
"Woe, my brother!" and "Woe, sister!"
They shall not lament for him,
"Woe, lord!" and "Woe, his majesty!"

[19] With the burial of a donkey he shall be buried,
dragged and thrown out beyond the gates of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[20] Go up to ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ and cry out,
and in Bashan give your voice;
and cry out from ⟦abarim|Avarim|Abarim⟧,
for all your lovers are broken.

[21] I spoke to you in your ease;
you said, "I will not hear."
This has been your way from your youth,
that you have not listened to my voice.

[22] All your shepherds the wind shall shepherd,
and your lovers shall go into captivity;
for then you shall be ashamed and put to shame
because of all your evil.

[23] You who dwell in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧,
nested in the cedars,
how you shall be pitied when pangs come to you,
anguish as of one giving birth!

[24] As I live, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
though ⟦coniah|Konyahu|Coniah⟧ son of ⟦jehoiakim|Yehoyaqim|Jehoiakim⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
were a seal on my right hand,
yet from there I would tear you off;

[25] and I will give you into the hand of those who seek your soul,
and into the hand of those of whom you are afraid,
and into the hand of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and into the hand of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧.

[26] And I will hurl you and your mother who bore you
onto another land, where you were not born,
and there you shall die.

[27] And to the land to which they lift up their soul to return,
there they shall not return.

[28] Is this man ⟦coniah|Konyahu|Coniah⟧ a despised, shattered pot,
or a vessel in which there is no delight?
Why are they hurled, he and his seed,
and thrown onto the land that they have not known?

[29] Land, land, land,
hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!

[30] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Write this man down childless, a mighty man who shall not prosper in his days; for no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting on the throne of David and ruling again in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9). נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh" (v5, 16, 24).
- **Layout** — v1–5, 8–9, 11–12, 30 are prose; v6 (after the opening words), v7, v10, v13–17, v18 (after the opening words) to v29 are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line in the Hebrew order (v6 "Gilead you are to me", v22 "All your shepherds the wind shall shepherd" in the Hebrew order). Quotation marks are kept only for words spoken inside the oracle.
- **"Go down to the house of the king of Judah"** (v1) — Hebrew רֵד בֵּית־מֶלֶךְ, "go down (to) the house". **"O king of Judah" → "king of Judah"** (v2) — no "O": the Hebrew has the bare vocative (the reviewer's vocative row; also v29). **"that enter in by these gates" → "who come in by these gates"** — הַבָּאִים בַּשְּׁעָרִים.
- **"Execute ye judgment and righteousness" → "Do judgment and righteousness"** (v3; also v15) — עָשָׂה מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה. **"deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor" → "deliver the robbed from the hand of the extortioner"** — גָזוּל "one robbed"; עָשׁוֹק "extortioner" (the עָשַׁק row; as 21:12 עוֹשֵׁק). **"do no wrong, do no violence to the stranger, the fatherless, nor the widow, neither shed innocent blood" → "and the sojourner, the fatherless and the widow do not wrong, do not do violence to, and innocent blood do not shed"** — the Hebrew order: the objects stand first; גֵּר "sojourner"; יָתוֹם "fatherless"; הוֹנָה "wrong", חָמַס "do violence" (verb of חָמָס); דָּם נָקִי "innocent blood" (also v17).
- **"For if ye do this thing indeed" → "For if you surely do this word"** (v4) — Hebrew אִם־עָשׂוֹ תַּעֲשׂוּ, literally "if doing you do": the infinitive absolute; הַדָּבָר "word". **"kings sitting upon the throne of David" → "kings sitting for David on his throne"** — יֹשְׁבִים לְדָוִד עַל־כִּסְאוֹ. **"he, and his servants" → "he and his servants"** — the singular הוּא: each king; the written text (ketiv) has ועבדו "and his servant", the reading (qere) וַעֲבָדָיו "and his servants": followed. רֶכֶב "chariots" (a collective).
- **"But if ye will not hear these words" → "But if you do not hear these words"** (v5) — שָׁמַע "hear". **"I swear by myself" → "by myself I have sworn"** — בִּי נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי, a perfect, "by myself" first. **"a desolation" → "a ruin"** — חָרְבָּה "ruin" (the Lev 26 row).
- **"Thou art Gilead unto me, and the head of Lebanon: yet surely I will make thee a wilderness, and cities which are not inhabited" → "Gilead you are to me, the head of Lebanon; surely I will make you a wilderness, cities that are not inhabited"** (v6) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא אֲשִׁיתְךָ, the oath form "if I do not make you": by its meaning "surely" (the oath row); no "yet", no second "and" in the Hebrew. The written text (ketiv) נושבה is feminine singular, the reading (qere) נוֹשָׁבוּ plural, agreeing with "cities": followed. "You" is masculine singular: the house of the king.
- **"I will prepare destroyers against thee, every one with his weapons" → "And I will sanctify destroyers against you, each man and his weapons"** (v7) — Hebrew וְקִדַּשְׁתִּי עָלֶיךָ, קִדֵּשׁ "sanctify": an army is set apart for the war as for a holy act (Jer 6:4; 51:27, 28; Joel 3:9); "prepare" is the sense, not the word. מַשְׁחִתִים "destroyers"; אִישׁ וְכֵלָיו "a man and his gear" (כְּלִי, here weapons). **"they shall cut down thy choice cedars, and cast them into the fire" → "they shall cut down the choicest of your cedars and make them fall into the fire"** — מִבְחַר "the choicest"; הִפִּיל "make fall" (hifil of נָפַל) עַל־הָאֵשׁ, literally "upon the fire".
- **"every man to his neighbour" → "each man to his neighbor"** (v8) — the אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ row. **"Wherefore hath the LORD done thus unto this great city?" → "Why has Yahweh done thus to this great city?"** — עַל־מֶה, literally "on account of what".
- **"Then they shall answer" → "And they shall say"** (v9) — וְאָמְרוּ "and they shall say" (the verb of v8). **"worshipped other gods" → "bowed down to other gods"** — וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּ, wayyiqtol: "forsook … bowed down … served", past.
- **"neither bemoan him" → "and do not show grief for him"** (v10) — נוּד לְ "show grief" (literally "sway for": the row of Job 2:11). **"weep sore for him that goeth away" → "surely weep for him who goes away"** — בְּכוּ בָכוֹ, literally "weep weeping": the infinitive absolute. **"nor see his native country" → "and see the land of his kindred"** — וְרָאָה, joined by "and" to "he shall not return"; אֶרֶץ מוֹלַדְתּוֹ (the מוֹלֶדֶת row).
- **"touching Shallum the son of Josiah" → "concerning Shallum son of Josiah"** (v11) — אֶל, "to" or "concerning". **"which reigned instead of" → "who reigned in the place of"** — תַּחַת (the מָלַךְ row). **"He shall not return thither any more" → "He shall not return there again"** — שָׁם "there". **"where they have led him captive" → "where they have carried him into exile"** (v12) — הִגְלוּ (the הִגְלָה row).
- **"by unrighteousness … by wrong" → "without righteousness … without judgment"** (v13) — בְּלֹא־צֶדֶק, בְּלֹא מִשְׁפָּט, "with no righteousness, with no judgment". **"that useth his neighbour's service without wages, and giveth him not for his work" → "who makes his neighbor serve for nothing and does not give him his wages"** — בְּרֵעֵהוּ יַעֲבֹד, the עָבַד בְּ "make serve" row (lit. "serves through his neighbor"); חִנָּם "for nothing"; פֹּעַל, literally "his work", the pay for it. Hebrew order of the last line: "and his wages he does not give him".
- **"I will build me a wide house and large chambers" → "I will build myself a house of measures and upper rooms made spacious"** (v14) — בֵּית מִדּוֹת "a house of measures": of large dimensions; עֲלִיּוֹת מְרֻוָּחִים "upper rooms made wide" (a pual participle). **"and cutteth him out windows" → "and he cuts out windows for it"** — Hebrew וְקָרַע לוֹ חַלּוֹנָי: קָרַע "tear (open)"; חַלּוֹנָי is pointed as "my windows", which does not fit the speaker; the ending is taken as a plural form of the noun, without a possessive (uncertain; many emend to "its windows"). **"and it is cieled with cedar, and painted with vermilion" → "and it is paneled with cedar and painted with vermilion"** — סָפוּן "paneled" (the סָפַן row of 1 Kgs 6); מָשׁוֹחַ, literally "smeared, anointed" (מָשַׁח); שָׁשַׁר "vermilion" (red ochre; only here and Ezek 23:14).
- **"Shalt thou reign, because thou closest thyself in cedar?" → "Shall you reign because you are competing in cedar?"** (v15) — מְתַחֲרֶה, from חָרָה "be hot": "heat yourself in rivalry", "compete" (as Jer 12:5); the Ps 37 row "burn with anger" is another sense of the same stem. **"did not thy father eat and drink" → "Your father, did he not eat and drink"** — "your father" stands first. **"then it was well with him" → "Then it was good for him"** — אָז טוֹב לוֹ (also v16 "then it was good").
- **"He judged the cause of the poor and needy" → "He judged the cause of the afflicted and the needy"** (v16) — דָּן דִּין, verb and noun of one root; עָנִי "afflicted", אֶבְיוֹן "needy" (their row). **"was not this to know me?" → "Is not that the knowing of me?"** — הֲלוֹא־הִיא הַדַּעַת אֹתִי, the noun with the article.
- **"But thine eyes and thine heart are not but for thy covetousness" → "For your eyes and your heart are on nothing but your unjust gain"** (v17) — כִּי אֵין … כִּי אִם־עַל; בֶּצַע "unjust gain" (its row). **"and for oppression, and for violence, to do it" → "and on extortion and on crushing, to do it"** — עֹשֶׁק "extortion" (the עָשַׁק row); מְרוּצָה here from רָצַץ "crush" (not the "running" of 2 Sam 18:27, from רוּץ).
- **"They shall not lament for him, saying, Ah my brother! or, Ah sister! … Ah lord! or, Ah his glory!" → "They shall not lament for him, 'Woe, my brother!' and 'Woe, sister!' … 'Woe, lord!' and 'Woe, his majesty!'"** (v18) — הוֹי "Woe" (the prophets' formula row; KJV "Ah"); "saying" is not in the Hebrew; וְ "and"; הוֹדֹה "his majesty" (the הוֹד row).
- **"He shall be buried with the burial of an ass, drawn and cast forth beyond the gates" → "With the burial of a donkey he shall be buried, dragged and thrown out beyond the gates"** (v19) — the Hebrew order; סָחוֹב וְהַשְׁלֵךְ, two infinitives absolute, "a dragging and a throwing"; מֵהָלְאָה לְ "out beyond".
- **"lift up thy voice in Bashan" → "and in Bashan give your voice"** (v20) — נָתַן קוֹל, literally "give voice"; the "you" of v20–23 is feminine singular (Jerusalem). **"cry from the passages" → "cry out from Abarim"** — מֵעֲבָרִים, the mountains of Abarim (Num 27:12; the `abarim` token); KJV took it as a common noun. צָעַק "cry out" both times. **"for all thy lovers are destroyed" → "are broken"** — נִשְׁבְּרוּ (שָׁבַר "break").
- **"I spake unto thee in thy prosperity" → "I spoke to you in your ease"** (v21) — שַׁלְוָה "ease" (its row). **"I will not hear"** — שָׁמַע. **"that thou obeyedst not my voice" → "that you have not listened to my voice"** — שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל (the row: "obey" is not the Hebrew word).
- **"The wind shall eat up all thy pastors" → "All your shepherds the wind shall shepherd"** (v22) — Hebrew כָּל־רֹעַיִךְ תִּרְעֶה־רוּחַ, a play on one root: רֹעֶה "shepherd", רָעָה "shepherd, graze, feed on"; the wind will drive them off as a shepherd drives a flock, or graze them away. **"go into captivity" → "shall go into captivity"** — בַּשְּׁבִי, literally "in the captivity". **"then shalt thou be ashamed and confounded for all thy wickedness" → "for then you shall be ashamed and put to shame because of all your evil"** — כִּי אָז; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", נִכְלַם "be put to shame" (its row); רָעָה "evil".
- **"O inhabitant of Lebanon, that makest thy nest in the cedars" → "You who dwell in Lebanon, nested in the cedars"** (v23) — the written text (ketiv) ישבתי and מקננתי (old forms of the feminine "you"), the reading (qere) יֹשַׁבְתְּ, מְקֻנַּנְתְּ: the same sense. **"how gracious shalt thou be when pangs come upon thee" → "how you shall be pitied when pangs come to you"** — Hebrew מַה־נֵּחַנְתְּ, pointed as a nifal of חָנַן "be shown favor": said with bitter irony. The Greek and the Syriac read "how you will groan" (נֶאֱנַחְתְּ: from memory); the MT is kept. **"the pain as of a woman in travail" → "anguish as of one giving birth"** — חִיל "anguish" (the writhing of one in labor: its row); כַּיֹּלֵדָה "as one who gives birth".
- **"As I live, saith the LORD, though Coniah … were the signet upon my right hand, yet would I pluck thee thence" → "As I live, declares Yahweh, though Coniah … were a seal on my right hand, yet from there I would tear you off"** (v24) — the oath חַי־אָנִי with כִּי אִם … כִּי; כָּנְיָהוּ is the short form of Jeconiah (יְכָנְיָהוּ, Jer 24:1), the king called Jehoiachin in Kings. חוֹתָם "seal" (as Gen 38:18 "your seal"), without the article; יַד יְמִינִי "my right hand"; נָתַק "tear off". The address moves from "Coniah" (third person) to "you" in the Hebrew.
- **"those that seek thy life" → "those who seek your soul"** (v25) — נַפְשֶׁךָ. **"into the hand of them whose face thou fearest, even into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar" → "into the hand of those of whom you are afraid, and into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar"** — יָגוֹר מִפְּנֵיהֶם (the יָגֹר "be afraid of" row; literally "from their face"); וּבְיַד "and into the hand" each time (no "even").
- **"I will cast thee out, and thy mother that bare thee, into another country" → "And I will hurl you and your mother who bore you onto another land"** (v26) — הֵטַלְתִּי "hurl" (also v28 הוּטֲלוּ); עַל הָאָרֶץ אַחֶרֶת, literally "upon the land, another".
- **"But to the land whereunto they desire to return" → "And to the land to which they lift up their soul to return"** (v27) — מְנַשְּׂאִים אֶת־נַפְשָׁם (the נָשָׂא נֶפֶשׁ אֶל row: they long for it).
- **"Is this man Coniah a despised broken idol? is he a vessel wherein is no pleasure?" → "Is this man Coniah a despised, shattered pot, or a vessel in which there is no delight?"** (v28) — עֶצֶב here is a vessel (beside כְּלִי, and with נָפוּץ "shattered", from נָפַץ); the same letters are "idol" elsewhere (עָצָב) and "pain" (Gen 3:16); "pot" is uncertain. חֵפֶץ "delight". אִם "or". **"wherefore are they cast out, he and his seed, and are cast into a land which they know not?" → "Why are they hurled, he and his seed, and thrown onto the land that they have not known?"** — הוּטֲלוּ "hurled" (as v26), הֻשְׁלְכוּ "thrown"; זֶרַע "seed"; עַל־הָאָרֶץ "onto the land".
- **"O earth, earth, earth" → "Land, land, land"** (v29) — אֶרֶץ three times, feminine imperative "hear"; "earth" is the other sense of the word (the אֶרֶץ row).
- **"Write ye this man childless, a man that shall not prosper in his days: for no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David, and ruling any more in Judah" → "Write this man down childless, a mighty man who shall not prosper …"** (v30) — עֲרִירִי "childless" (its row): he is to stand in the record as one without an heir on the throne; 1 Chr 3:17–18 names his sons. גֶּבֶר "mighty man" beside אִישׁ "man" (the standing decision); צָלַח "prosper" both times; זֶרַע "seed"; מֹשֵׁל עוֹד "ruling again".

### Notes
- **Shallum** (v11) is the king called Jehoahaz in 2 Kgs 23:30–34 (1 Chr 3:15), taken to Egypt by Pharaoh Neco (609 BC).
- **Coniah** (v24, 28) is Jeconiah / Jehoiachin, carried into exile to Babylon (597 BC; 2 Kgs 24:8–15).
