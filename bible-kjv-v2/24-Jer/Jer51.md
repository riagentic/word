# Jeremiah 51

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת "Yahweh of Hosts" (v5, 14, 19, 33, 57, 58); אֱלֹהִים token (v5, 10, 33); אֵל token (v56).
- **Layout and speech marks** — v1–58 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents; v59–64 prose. The quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where speech begins and ends (v14, 34–35, 51, 61–64). No "O" before names and titles (v13, 25, 62).
- **"against them that dwell in the midst of them that rise up against me, a destroying wind" → "against the inhabitants of Leb-kamai a destroying wind"** (v1) — לֵב קָמָי, "the heart of those who rise against me", is a cipher (atbash: each letter for its counterpart from the other end of the alphabet) for כַּשְׂדִּים "Chaldeans"; the name is kept, as printed. הִנְנִי מֵעִיר participle; רוּחַ מַשְׁחִית "a destroying wind" (רוּחַ is also "spirit": the same word as v11; the winnowing of v2 suits "wind").
- **"And will send unto Babylon fanners, that shall fan her, and shall empty her land: for in the day of trouble they shall be against her round about" → "And I will send to Babylon strangers, and they shall winnow her, and they shall empty her land; for they shall be against her all around in the day of evil"** (v2) — זָרִים is pointed "strangers"; "winnowers" (זֹרִים) is the reading of the versions (from memory): the play on זֵרוּהָ "winnow her" is the Hebrew's; בָּקַק "empty"; בְּיוֹם רָעָה "in the day of evil".
- **"Against him that bendeth let the archer bend his bow, and against him that lifteth himself up in his brigandine" → "Against him who treads let the treader tread his bow, and against him who rises up in his coat of mail"** (v3) — the MT pointing is אֶל "against"; the written text repeats ידרך (written, not read); many translations read אַל "let not (the archer bend)" (from memory); דָּרַךְ קֶשֶׁת "tread the bow" (as 50:14); סִרְיוֹן "coat of mail"; צָבָא "host"; הֶחֱרִים "devote to destruction".
- **"Thus the slain shall fall … and they that are thrust through in her streets" → "And the slain shall fall … and the pierced in her streets"** (v4) — חֲלָלִים "slain"; מְדֻקָּרִים "pierced"; חוּצוֹת "streets".
- **"For Israel hath not been forsaken, nor Judah of his God, of the LORD of hosts; though their land was filled with sin against the Holy One of Israel" → "For not widowed is Israel, or Judah, from his Elohim, from Yahweh of Hosts; for their land is full of guilt against the Holy One of Israel"** (v5) — אַלְמָן, only here, "widowed"; the second כִּי "for" (not "though"); אָשָׁם "guilt"; מִקְּדוֹשׁ "against (lit. from) the Holy One". "Their land" may be the Chaldeans' (guilt that brings judgment) or Israel's (guilt that did not end the bond): the Hebrew does not say.
- **"deliver every man his soul: be not cut off in her iniquity; for this is the time of the LORD'S vengeance; he will render unto her a recompence" → "let each man rescue his soul; do not be silenced in her iniquity; for it is a time of vengeance for Yahweh; a recompense he is repaying to her"** (v6) — מִלֵּט "rescue" (also v45); נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; תִּדַּמּוּ "be silenced" (as 49:26); עָוֹן "iniquity" (also its punishment); גְּמוּל "recompense" (from גָּמַל "deal"; also v56); מְשַׁלֵּם participle "repaying".
- **"the nations have drunken of her wine; therefore the nations are mad" → "of her wine the nations have drunk; therefore the nations act madly"** (v7) — הִתְהוֹלֵל "act madly" (as 50:38).
- **"Babylon is suddenly fallen and destroyed: howl for her; take balm for her pain, if so be she may be healed" → "Suddenly Babylon has fallen and is broken; wail over her; take balm for her pain; perhaps she may be healed"** (v8) — שָׁבַר "break"; הֵילִיל "wail"; צֳרִי "balm"; מַכְאוֹב "pain"; אוּלַי "perhaps".
- **"We would have healed Babylon … for her judgment reacheth unto heaven, and is lifted up even to the skies" → "We healed Babylon … for her judgment has touched the heavens and is lifted up to the clouds"** (v9) — the qere רִפִּינוּ "we healed" (perfect; the written רפאנו the same sense); "would have" is not in the form; נָגַע אֶל "touch"; שְׁחָקִים "clouds".
- **"The LORD hath brought forth our righteousness: come, and let us declare in Zion" → "Yahweh has brought out our righteousnesses; come, and let us recount in Zion"** (v10) — צִדְקֹתֵינוּ plural "our righteousnesses" (the righteous acts done for us; "vindication" is the sense); סִפֵּר "recount".
- **"Make bright the arrows; gather the shields … for his device is against Babylon, to destroy it" → "Sharpen the arrows, fill the shields … for against Babylon is his devising, to ruin her"** (v11) — הָבֵרוּ "sharpen (polish)"; מִלְאוּ הַשְּׁלָטִים "fill the shields" (שֶׁלֶט: shield, or quiver, which one fills: uncertain); מְזִמָּה "devising" (the row of 2026-10-16: of Yahweh's plan); הִשְׁחִית "ruin"; מָדַי "Madai", the Medes (also v28); נִקְמַת הֵיכָלוֹ "the vengeance of his temple" (as 50:28).
- **"Set up the standard upon the walls of Babylon, make the watch strong, set up the watchmen, prepare the ambushes: for the LORD hath both devised and done" → "Against the walls of Babylon lift up a banner; make the watch strong, set up watchmen, prepare the ambushes; for Yahweh has both devised and done"** (v12) — נָשָׂא נֵס "lift up a banner" (also v27); מִשְׁמָר "watch" (lit. "a keeping": the guard set over a place); הֵקִים "set up (lit. make stand)"; שֹׁמְרִים "watchmen"; אֹרְבִים "ambushes" (the men lying in ambush); זָמַם "devise"; אֶל "concerning".
- **"O thou that dwellest upon many waters, abundant in treasures, thine end is come, and the measure of thy covetousness" → "You who abide by many waters, great in treasures, your end has come, the cubit of your unjust gain"** (v13) — the qere שֹׁכַנְתְּ "you who abide" (fem.; written שכנתי); רַבַּת "great in"; אַמָּה "cubit" (the measure: the length allotted, or the thread cut off at the cubit in weaving: uncertain); בֶּצַע "unjust gain".
- **"The LORD of hosts hath sworn by himself, saying, Surely I will fill thee with men, as with caterpillers; and they shall lift up a shout against thee" → "Yahweh of Hosts has sworn by his soul: Surely I will fill you with men like the hopping locust, and they shall answer a shout over you"** (v14) — בְּנַפְשׁוֹ "by his soul" (= by himself); כִּי אִם oath form "surely"; אָדָם "men" (mankind); יֶלֶק "hopping locust" (also v27); עָנָה הֵידָד "answer a shout" (the treaders' shout, sung back and forth: Isa 16:9–10; Jer 25:30).
- **v15–19** — the Hebrew of Jer 10:12–16, translated in the same words (see the Changes there: עֹשֵׂה participle "the maker", מֵכִין, נָטָה; לְקוֹל תִּתּוֹ "when he gives voice"; וַיַּעַל past "he made … go up", נְשִׂאִים "mists"; נִבְעַר … מִדַּעַת "brutish, without knowledge"; צוֹרֵף "refiner", פֶּסֶל "carved image", נֶסֶךְ "molten image"; הֶבֶל "vapor", תַּעְתֻּעִים "mockeries", פְּקֻדָּה "visitation"; חֵלֶק "share", יוֹצֵר "former", שֵׁבֶט "tribe"). The Hebrew differs in two things: v16 מִקְצֵה־אָרֶץ without the article (English "from the end of the earth" the same), and v19 has no "Israel": וְשֵׁבֶט נַחֲלָתוֹ "and the tribe of his inheritance" (10:16 "and Israel is the tribe of his inheritance"); "Israel" is not supplied.
- **"Thou art my battle axe and weapons of war: for with thee will I break in pieces the nations, and with thee will I destroy kingdoms" → "A shatterer you are to me, weapons of war; and I will shatter with you nations, and I will ruin with you kingdoms"** (v20–23) — מַפֵּץ "shatterer" (a war club; the noun of נִפֵּץ "shatter", repeated through v20–23); כְּלֵי מִלְחָמָה "weapons of war" (plural); the weqatal forms וְנִפַּצְתִּי "and I will shatter" (also read as what is done again and again, "I shatter"); הִשְׁחִית "ruin"; רֶכֶב וְרֹכְבוֹ "chariot and its rider" (the one word "rider" for horse and chariot); זָקֵן "old man", נַעַר "boy", בָּחוּר "young man", בְּתוּלָה "virgin"; עֵדֶר "drove"; אִכָּר "farmer", צֶמֶד "yoke" (a pair of oxen); פַּחוֹת "governors", סְגָנִים "prefects" (also v28, 57).
- **"in your sight" → "before your eyes"** (v24) — לְעֵינֵיכֶם; the Hebrew order kept: "all their evil that they have done in Zion before your eyes".
- **"O destroying mountain … which destroyest all the earth … and will make thee a burnt mountain" → "mountain of the destroyer … the destroyer of all the earth … and make you a mountain of burning"** (v25) — הַר הַמַּשְׁחִית "the mountain of the destroyer" (as 2 Kgs 23:13); הַמַּשְׁחִית participle "the destroyer"; גִּלְגֵּל "roll"; סְלָעִים "crags"; הַר שְׂרֵפָה "a mountain of burning".
- **"but thou shalt be desolate for ever" → "for everlasting desolations you shall be"** (v26, 62) — כִּי "for"; שִׁמְמוֹת עוֹלָם "desolations of the age" (plural); מוֹסָדוֹת "foundations".
- **"Set ye up a standard in the land, blow the trumpet among the nations, prepare the nations against her, call together against her the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashchenaz; appoint a captain against her; cause the horses to come up as the rough caterpillers" → "Lift up a banner in the land, blow the shofar among the nations, sanctify nations against her, make heard against her the kingdoms of Ararat, Minni, and Ashkenaz; appoint against her a marshal; bring up horses like the bristling hopping locust"** (v27) — שׁוֹפָר token; קִדֵּשׁ "sanctify" (set apart for war; also v28); הַשְׁמִיעוּ "make heard" (= summon); טִפְסָר, a loan word, "marshal" (an officer: uncertain); סוּס collective "horses"; יֶלֶק סָמָר "the bristling hopping locust" (סָמָר "bristling", uncertain).
- **"with the captains thereof, and all the rulers thereof, and all the land of his dominion" → "her governors and all her prefects, and all the land of his rule"** (v28) — פַּחוֹתֶיהָ, סְגָנֶיהָ "her governors, her prefects" (of the land of Madai); מֶמְשַׁלְתּוֹ "his rule" (each king's); "kings of the Medes" stands in apposition to "nations", no "with".
- **"And the land shall tremble and sorrow: for every purpose of the LORD shall be performed against Babylon" → "And the land shook and writhed; for the plans of Yahweh rose against Babylon"** (v29) — וַתִּרְעַשׁ … וַתָּחֹל past forms (the prophet sees it as done); חִיל "writhe"; קָמָה "rose (stood)"; מַחְשְׁבוֹת "plans" (as 49:20); שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (also v37, 41, 43).
- **"The mighty men of Babylon have forborn to fight, they have remained in their holds: their might hath failed; they became as women: they have burned her dwellingplaces; her bars are broken" → "The mighty ones of Babylon have ceased to fight; they sit in the strongholds; their might has dried up; they have become women; they have set fire to her dwellings; her bars are broken"** (v30) — חָדַל "cease"; יָשַׁב "sit"; מְצָדוֹת "strongholds" (no "their"); נָשְׁתָה "has dried up"; no "as"; הִצִּיתוּ "they have set fire" (active, no subject named); מִשְׁכְּנֹת "dwellings".
- **"One post shall run to meet another, and one messenger to meet another, to shew the king of Babylon that his city is taken at one end" → "Runner runs to meet runner, and teller to meet teller, to tell the king of Babylon that his city is captured from end to end"** (v31) — רָץ "runner"; מַגִּיד "teller" (the participle of הִגִּיד "tell", the verb that follows); מִקָּצֶה "from end (to end)" (lit. "from the end").
- **"And that the passages are stopped, and the reeds they have burned with fire, and the men of war are affrighted" → "and the fords are seized, and the reed pools they have burned with fire, and the men of war are dismayed"** (v32) — מַעְבָּרוֹת "fords"; נִתְפָּשׂוּ "seized"; אֲגַמִּים "reed pools" (pools of reedy standing water: the city's marsh defences); שָׂרְפוּ "they have burned" (active); נִבְהָלוּ "be dismayed".
- **"it is time to thresh her" → "at the time one treads it"** (v33) — עֵת הִדְרִיכָהּ, "the time of treading it" (the floor is trodden firm before threshing); עוֹד מְעַט "yet a little"; קָצִיר "harvest".
- **"Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon hath devoured me, he hath crushed me, he hath made me an empty vessel, he hath swallowed me up like a dragon, he hath filled his belly with my delicates, he hath cast me out" → "Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon has devoured me, he has thrown me into confusion; he has set me down an empty vessel, he has swallowed me like the dragon, he has filled his belly from my dainties; he has rinsed me out"** (v34) — the five verbs are read with the qere ("me"); the written forms have "us"; הָמַם "throw into confusion"; הִצִּיג "set (down)"; תַּנִּין "dragon"; כְּרֵשׂ "belly"; עֲדָנִים "dainties"; הֱדִיחָנִי "he has rinsed me out" (from דּוּחַ "rinse", as a dish; or from נָדַח "he has thrust me out": uncertain).
- **"The violence done to me and to my flesh be upon Babylon, shall the inhabitant of Zion say" → "The violence done to me and to my flesh be upon Babylon, the inhabitress of Zion shall say"** (v35) — חֲמָסִי "my violence" (the violence done to me); שְׁאֵרִי "my flesh" (as Ps 73:26; "my kin" is also read); יֹשֶׁבֶת צִיּוֹן "the inhabitress of Zion" (feminine); תֹּאמַר "shall say" (may also be "let … say").
- **"Behold, I will plead thy cause, and take vengeance for thee; and I will dry up her sea, and make her springs dry" → "Behold, I am contending your case, and I will avenge your vengeance; and I will dry up her sea and make her fountain dry"** (v36) — הִנְנִי־רָב participle; רִיב "case"; נִקַּמְתִּי אֶת־נִקְמָתֵךְ "avenge your vengeance"; הֶחֱרִיב "dry up", הוֹבִישׁ "make dry"; מָקוֹר "fountain" (singular).
- **"And Babylon shall become heaps, a dwellingplace for dragons, an astonishment, and an hissing, without an inhabitant" → "And Babylon shall become heaps, an abode of jackals, a thing that appalls and a hissing, without inhabitant"** (v37) — גַּלִּים "heaps" (the same word as "waves", v42, 55); מְעוֹן תַּנִּים "an abode of jackals" (as 49:33); שְׁרֵקָה "hissing".
- **"They shall roar together like lions: they shall yell as lions' whelps" → "Together like young lions they shall roar; they growl like the whelps of lions"** (v38) — כְּפִירִים "young lions"; שָׁאַג "roar"; נָעַר "growl" (uncertain); גּוֹרֵי אֲרָיוֹת "whelps of lions".
- **"In their heat I will make their feasts, and I will make them drunken, that they may rejoice, and sleep a perpetual sleep, and not wake" → "In their heat I will set their feast, and I will make them drunk, so that they exult, and sleep an everlasting sleep and not wake"** (v39, 57) — שִׁית "set"; מִשְׁתֶּה "feast" (singular); לְמַעַן "so that"; עָלַז "exult"; שְׁנַת עוֹלָם "a sleep of the age"; הֵקִיץ "wake".
- **"like rams with he goats" → "like rams with he-goats"** (v40) — כָּרִים "lambs"; טָבַח "slaughter"; עַתּוּדִים "he-goats".
- **"How is Sheshach taken! and how is the praise of the whole earth surprised!" → "How Sheshach is captured, and the praise of all the earth is seized!"** (v41) — שֵׁשַׁךְ, printed as a name, is a cipher (atbash) for בָּבֶל "Babylon" (also Jer 25:26), and the second half of the verse names Babylon; the token keeps the printed name. לָכַד "capture", תָּפַשׂ "seize"; תְּהִלָּה "praise".
- **"she is covered with the multitude of the waves thereof" → "with the multitude of its waves she is covered"** (v42) — הֲמוֹן "multitude" (also "roar"); גַּלִּים "waves".
- **"a dry land, and a wilderness, a land wherein no man dwelleth, neither doth any son of man pass thereby" → "a land of dryness and desert plain, a land in which no man dwells, and no son of man passes through them"** (v43) — צִיָּה "dryness", עֲרָבָה "desert plain" (as 50:12); כָּל־אִישׁ under the negative "no man"; בָּהֵן "in them, through them" (the cities); בֶּן־אָדָם "son of man".
- **"And I will punish Bel in Babylon, and I will bring forth out of his mouth that which he hath swallowed up: and the nations shall not flow together any more unto him: yea, the wall of Babylon shall fall" → "And I will visit Bel in Babylon, and bring out what he has swallowed from his mouth; and the nations shall not stream to him anymore; also the wall of Babylon has fallen"** (v44) — פָּקַד עַל "visit (upon)" (also v47, 52); בִּלְעוֹ "what he has swallowed"; נָהַר "stream" (as Isa 2:2); גַּם "also"; נָפָלָה perfect "has fallen".
- **"deliver ye every man his soul from the fierce anger of the LORD" → "rescue each man his soul from the burning of the anger of Yahweh"** (v45) — חֲרוֹן אַף "the burning of anger".
- **"And lest your heart faint, and ye fear for the rumour that shall be heard in the land; a rumour shall both come one year, and after that in another year shall come a rumour, and violence in the land, ruler against ruler" → "and lest your heart grow faint, and you fear at the report that is heard in the land; and the report comes in a year, and after it in a year the report, and violence in the land, ruler against ruler"** (v46) — וּפֶן "and lest" (bound to v45; "Let not" is not the word); רָכַךְ "grow faint (soft)"; שְׁמוּעָה "report" (with the article each time); בַּשָּׁנָה "in a year" (twice; "one … another" is the sense); מֹשֵׁל עַל מֹשֵׁל "ruler against (upon) ruler".
- **"I will do judgment upon the graven images of Babylon: and her whole land shall be confounded" → "I will visit the carved images of Babylon, and all her land shall be ashamed"** (v47, 52) — פָּקַד עַל "visit" (not "do judgment", v52); פְּסִילִים "carved images"; בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"; "days are coming" without article; the weqatal "and I will visit" (no "when").
- **"Then the heaven and the earth, and all that is therein, shall sing for Babylon: for the spoilers shall come unto her from the north" → "And the heavens and the earth and all that is in them shall shout for joy over Babylon; for from the north those who lay waste shall come to her"** (v48) — רִנֵּן "shout for joy"; שֹׁדְדִים "those who lay waste" (שָׁדַד; also v53, 55, 56; "destroyer" stays for מַשְׁחִית, v1, 25).
- **"As Babylon hath caused the slain of Israel to fall, so at Babylon shall fall the slain of all the earth" → "Babylon also is to fall, slain of Israel; also for Babylon the slain of all the earth have fallen"** (v49) — גַּם … גַּם "also … also"; לִנְפֹּל "is to fall"; חַלְלֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל stands without a preposition: an address ("you slain of Israel"), or "(for) the slain of Israel" (= Babylon falls on their account): uncertain, kept as it stands; לְבָבֶל "for (by) Babylon"; נָפְלוּ perfect.
- **"Ye that have escaped the sword, go away, stand not still: remember the LORD afar off, and let Jerusalem come into your mind" → "You who have escaped from the sword, go, do not stand; remember Yahweh from afar, and let Jerusalem come up upon your heart"** (v50) — פְּלֵטִים "those who have escaped"; עָלָה עַל־לֵבָב "come up upon the heart" (as Isa 65:17).
- **"shame hath covered our faces: for strangers are come into the sanctuaries of the LORD'S house" → "dishonor has covered our faces, for strangers have come upon the sanctuaries of the house of Yahweh"** (v51) — בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; כְּלִמָּה "dishonor"; זָרִים "strangers"; עַל "upon"; מִקְדְּשֵׁי plural "sanctuaries" (the holy parts of the house).
- **"and through all her land the wounded shall groan" → "and through all her land the pierced shall groan"** (v52) — חָלָל singular "the pierced (slain)"; אָנַק "groan".
- **"Though Babylon should mount up to heaven, and though she should fortify the height of her strength, yet from me shall spoilers come unto her" → "Though Babylon should go up to the heavens, and though she should make the height of her strength unreachable, from me those who lay waste shall come to her"** (v53) — בִּצֵּר "make unreachable (fortify)"; מְרוֹם עֻזָּהּ "the height of her strength" (עֹז); מֵאִתִּי "from me"; no "yet".
- **"A sound of a cry cometh from Babylon, and great destruction from the land of the Chaldeans" → "A sound of outcry from Babylon, and a great breaking from the land of the Chaldeans!"** (v54) — no verb; זְעָקָה "outcry"; שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as 50:22).
- **"Because the LORD hath spoiled Babylon, and destroyed out of her the great voice; when her waves do roar like great waters, a noise of their voice is uttered" → "For Yahweh is laying Babylon waste, and he makes the great voice perish from her; and their waves roar like many waters; the tumult of their voice is given forth"** (v55) — שֹׁדֵד participle; אִבֵּד piel "make perish"; קוֹל גָּדוֹל "the great voice" (the loud noise of the city); הָמָה "roar"; גַּלִּים "waves" ("their": the attackers'); שָׁאוֹן "tumult"; נִתַּן "is given (forth)".
- **"Because the spoiler is come upon her, even upon Babylon, and her mighty men are taken, every one of their bows is broken: for the LORD God of recompences shall surely requite" → "For upon her, upon Babylon, has come one who lays waste, and her mighty ones are captured, their bows are shattered; for an El of recompenses is Yahweh; he will surely repay"** (v56) — שׁוֹדֵד "one who lays waste"; לָכַד "capture"; חִתְּתָה piel of חָתַת "shatter" (no "every one"); אֵל גְּמֻלוֹת "an El of recompenses" (token `el`: the word is אֵל, not אֱלֹהִים; גְּמוּלָה from גָּמַל "deal"); שַׁלֵּם יְשַׁלֵּם "he will surely repay" (lit. "repaying he will repay").
- **"And I will make drunk her princes … saith the King, whose name is the LORD of hosts" → "And I will make drunk her princes … declares the King, Yahweh of Hosts is his name"** (v57) — שָׂרִים "princes"; נְאֻם "declares"; the clause יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת שְׁמוֹ stands as a clause ("Yahweh of Hosts is his name"), as v19.
- **"The broad walls of Babylon shall be utterly broken, and her high gates shall be burned with fire; and the people shall labour in vain, and the folk in the fire, and they shall be weary" → "The walls of Babylon, the broad one, shall surely be laid bare, and her high gates shall be kindled with fire; and peoples labor for emptiness, and folks for the fire, and they shall be weary"** (v58) — חֹמוֹת plural with the singular הָרְחָבָה "the broad one" (Babylon, or the wall as one); עַרְעֵר תִּתְעַרְעָר infinitive absolute, "shall surely be laid bare" (lit. "laying bare, be laid bare": stripped to the ground); יִצַּתּוּ "be kindled"; יָגַע "labor"; בְּדֵי־רִיק "for emptiness" (lit. "for the enough of emptiness"), בְּדֵי־אֵשׁ "for the fire"; לְאֻמִּים "folks"; יָעֵף "be weary". Hab 2:13 has nearly the same words.
- **"which Jeremiah the prophet commanded Seraiah … when he went with Zedekiah the king of Judah into Babylon in the fourth year of his reign. And this Seraiah was a quiet prince" → "that Jeremiah the prophet commanded Seraiah … when he went with Zedekiah king of Judah to Babylon, in the fourth year of his reign; and Seraiah was prince of the resting place"** (v59) — אֶת־צִדְקִיָּהוּ "with Zedekiah" (the Septuagint has "from Zedekiah", from memory); שַׂר מְנוּחָה "prince of the resting place" (the officer for the halts on the march, "quartermaster": the sense; "a quiet prince" takes מְנוּחָה as "rest"); בְּמָלְכוֹ "of his reign".
- **"So Jeremiah wrote in a book all the evil that should come upon Babylon" → "And Jeremiah wrote all the evil that should come to Babylon in one book"** (v60) — וַיִּכְתֹּב "and he wrote"; רָעָה "evil" (not "disaster"; also v64); אֶל־סֵפֶר אֶחָד "in one book" (lit. "into"); אֶל "concerning".
- **"When thou comest to Babylon, and shalt see, and shalt read all these words" → "When you come to Babylon, then you shall see, and you shall read all these words"** (v61) — וְרָאִיתָ וְקָרָאתָ weqatal "and you shall see and you shall read" ("see to it" is the sense).
- **"O LORD, thou hast spoken against this place, to cut it off, that none shall remain in it, neither man nor beast" → "Yahweh, you have spoken concerning this place, to cut it off, so that there shall be none dwelling in it, from man to beast"** (v62) — no "O"; אֶל "concerning"; הִכְרִית "cut off"; יוֹשֵׁב "one dwelling"; לְמֵאָדָם וְעַד־בְּהֵמָה "from man to beast".
- **"thou shalt bind a stone to it, and cast it into the midst of Euphrates" → "you shall bind a stone upon it and throw it into the midst of the Euphrates"** (v63) — וְהָיָה "And it shall be"; קָשַׁר עַל "bind upon"; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw".
- **"Thus shall Babylon sink, and shall not rise from the evil that I will bring upon her: and they shall be weary. Thus far are the words of Jeremiah" → "Thus shall Babylon sink, and she shall not rise, from the face of the evil that I am bringing upon her; and they shall be weary. Thus far the words of Jeremiah"** (v64) — מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (because of); מֵבִיא participle "I am bringing"; וְיָעֵפוּ "and they shall be weary" stands again as at the end of v58 (a word that marks where Jeremiah's words end: so the closing words say); עַד־הֵנָּה "thus far".

### Notes
- **v15–19** — the Hebrew of Jer 10:12–16 (one wording; v19 without "Israel").
- **v58** — beside Hab 2:13.
