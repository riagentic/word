# Jeremiah 19

[1] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: "Go, and buy a flask of a potter, of earthenware, and some of the elders of the people and some of the elders of the priests,

[2] and go out to the valley of ⟦ben-hinnom|Ben Hinnom|Ben-hinnom⟧, which is at the entrance of the Potsherd Gate, and call out there the words that I speak to you,

[3] and say: 'Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I am bringing evil upon this place, at which the ears of everyone who hears it shall tingle.

[4] Because they have forsaken me and have made this place foreign, and have made smoke in it to other gods whom they had not known, they and their fathers and the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; and they have filled this place with the blood of innocents,

[5] and have built the high places of the Baal, to burn their sons in the fire as burnt offerings to the Baal, which I did not command and did not speak, and it did not come up on my heart.

[6] Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and this place shall no more be called the ⟦topheth|Tofet|Topheth⟧ and the valley of ⟦ben-hinnom|Ben Hinnom|Ben-hinnom⟧, but the valley of the Killing.

[7] And I will empty out the counsel of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ in this place, and I will make them fall by the sword before their enemies and by the hand of those who seek their soul; and I will give their carcasses for food to the birds of the heavens and to the beasts of the earth.

[8] And I will make this city a thing that appalls and a hissing; everyone who passes by it shall be appalled and hiss over all its blows.

[9] And I will make them eat the flesh of their sons and the flesh of their daughters, and each man the flesh of his neighbor they shall eat, in the siege and in the straits with which their enemies and those who seek their soul shall straiten them.'

[10] And you shall break the flask before the eyes of the men who go with you,

[11] and say to them: 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Even so I will break this people and this city, as one breaks the vessel of the potter, which cannot be healed any more; and in ⟦topheth|Tofet|Topheth⟧ they shall bury, for lack of a place to bury.

[12] So I will do to this place, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and to its inhabitants, and to make this city like ⟦topheth|Tofet|Topheth⟧.

[13] And the houses of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and the houses of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall be like the place of the ⟦topheth|Tofet|Topheth⟧, the unclean ones: all the houses on whose roofs they made smoke to all the host of the heavens and poured out drink offerings to other gods.'"

[14] And ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ came from the ⟦topheth|Tofet|Topheth⟧, where ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had sent him to prophesy, and he stood in the court of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said to all the people:

[15] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I am bringing to this city and upon all its cities all the evil that I have spoken against it, because they have stiffened their neck so as not to hear my words."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v3, 11, 15) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"God" → "Elohim"** (v3, 15) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **Layout** — the chapter is prose.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v2–3, 4–9, 13–14).
- **"Go and get a potter's earthen bottle, and take of the ancients" → "Go, and buy a flask of a potter, of earthenware, and some of the elders"** (v1) — Hebrew הָלוֹךְ, an infinitive absolute used as a command; קָנָה "buy"; בַּקְבֻּק יוֹצֵר חָרֶשׂ, word for word "a flask, a potter's, of earthenware" (יוֹצֵר "the one who forms", as Jer 18). The Hebrew has no verb before "some of the elders": "take" (KJV) is supplied by sense and not written here; מִן "some of". זָקֵן "elder".
- **"the valley of the son of Hinnom" → "the valley of Ben-hinnom"** (v2, 6) — Hebrew גֵּיא בֶן־הִנֹּם, "the valley of the son of Hinnom", with the `ben-hinnom` token as Josh 15:8. **"the east gate" → "the Potsherd Gate"** — the qere הַחַרְסִית (ketiv החרסות): from חֶרֶשׂ "earthenware, potsherd" (as v1); the meaning of the name is uncertain. **"proclaim" → "call out"** — קָרָא. **"the words that I shall tell thee" → "that I speak to you"** — אֲדַבֵּר.
- **"Hear ye the word of the LORD, O kings of Judah" → "Hear the word of Yahweh, kings of Judah"** (v3) — no "O". **"I will bring evil upon this place, the which whosoever heareth, his ears shall tingle" → "I am bringing evil upon this place, at which the ears of everyone who hears it shall tingle"** — הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, participle; רָעָה "evil" (not "disaster"); צָלַל "tingle" (as 1 Sam 3:11; 2 Kgs 21:12).
- **"Because they have forsaken me, and have estranged this place" → "Because they have forsaken me and have made this place foreign"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְנַכְּרוּ, piel of נכר, "made strange, foreign" (of the gods brought in). No subject supplied ("the people" of the current text). **"have burned incense in it" → "have made smoke in it"** — קִטֵּר without an object (the "make smoke" row). **"whom neither they nor their fathers have known" → "whom they had not known, they and their fathers and the kings of Judah"** — the Hebrew order. **"the blood of innocents"** — דַּם נְקִיִּם.
- **"the high places of Baal … burnt offerings unto Baal" → "the high places of the Baal … to the Baal"** (v5) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל, the article kept (the Judg 2 row). שָׂרַף "burn". **"which I commanded not, nor spake it, neither came it into my mind" → "which I did not command and did not speak, and it did not come up on my heart"** — עָלָה עַל־לֵב, literally "come up on the heart" (= come into the mind).
- **"that this place shall no more be called" → "and this place shall no more be called"** (v6) — וְ kept. **"Tophet" → "the Topheth"** (v6, 13, 14) — Hebrew הַתֹּפֶת with the article (as 2 Kgs 23:10); v11, 12 have no article. **"the valley of slaughter" → "the valley of the Killing"** — הַהֲרֵגָה, from הָרַג "kill", with the article.
- **"I will make void the counsel" → "I will empty out the counsel"** (v7) — בַּקֹּתִי, from בָּקַק "empty out", a play on בַּקְבֻּק "flask" (v1, 10). **"I will cause them to fall" → "I will make them fall"** — הִפִּיל. **"them that seek their lives" → "those who seek their soul"** — מְבַקְשֵׁי נַפְשָׁם (also v9). **"carcases"** — נִבְלָתָם, a singular collective. **"the fowls of the heaven" → "the birds of the heavens"**; **"the beasts of the earth"** — בְּהֵמָה.
- **"desolate, and an hissing" → "a thing that appalls and a hissing"** (v8) — שַׁמָּה, שְׁרֵקָה. **"shall be astonished" → "shall be appalled"** — יִשֹּׁם. **"plagues" → "blows"** — מַכּוֹת (noun of הִכָּה "strike").
- **"they shall eat every one the flesh of his friend" → "each man the flesh of his neighbor they shall eat"** (v9) — אִישׁ … רֵעֵהוּ; the Hebrew order. **"in the siege and straitness, wherewith their enemies … shall straiten them" → "in the siege and in the straits with which … shall straiten them"** — מָצוֹר, מָצוֹק, הֵצִיק (its row, Deut 28:53).
- **"Then shalt thou break the bottle in the sight of the men" → "And you shall break the flask before the eyes of the men"** (v10) — וְשָׁבַרְתָּ; לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of".
- **"as one breaketh a potter's vessel" → "as one breaks the vessel of the potter"** (v11) — כְּלִי הַיּוֹצֵר, with the article. **"that cannot be made whole again" → "which cannot be healed any more"** — לְהֵרָפֵה, nifal of רָפָא "heal" (of a vessel: be mended). **"and they shall bury them in Tophet, till there be no place to bury" → "and in Topheth they shall bury, for lack of a place to bury"** — no object in the Hebrew; מֵאֵין מָקוֹם "from there being no place".
- **"and even make this city as Tophet" → "and to make this city like Topheth"** (v12) — וְלָתֵת, literally "and to give".
- **"shall be defiled as the place of Tophet" → "shall be like the place of the Topheth, the unclean ones"** (v13) — הַטְּמֵאִים, an adjective with the article after the place (plural: the houses); טָמֵא "unclean". **"because of all the houses" → "all the houses"** — לְכֹל הַבָּתִּים, "as to all the houses". **"they have burned incense" → "they made smoke"** — קִטְּרוּ without an object. **"have poured out drink offerings"** — הַסֵּךְ נְסָכִים, an infinitive absolute carrying on the verb; נֶסֶךְ "drink offering".
- **"Then came Jeremiah" → "And Jeremiah came"** (v14) — וַיָּבֹא. **"in the court of the LORD's house" → "in the court of the house of Yahweh"**.
- **"I will bring upon this city" → "I am bringing to this city"** (v15) — the qere מֵבִיא (ketiv מבי); אֶל "to", then וְעַל "and upon". **"all her towns" → "all its cities"** — עָרֶיהָ. **"pronounced" → "spoken"** — דִּבַּרְתִּי. **"hardened their necks" → "stiffened their neck"** — הִקְשָׁה עֹרֶף (its row). **"that they might not hear" → "so as not to hear"** — לְבִלְתִּי שְׁמוֹעַ.

### Notes
- **vv1–13** — the sign of the broken flask: בַּקְבֻּק "flask" (v1, 10) and בַּקֹּתִי "I will empty out" (v7) sound alike.
