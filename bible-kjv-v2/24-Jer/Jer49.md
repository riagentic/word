# Jeremiah 49

[1] Concerning the sons of Ammon. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

Has ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ no sons?
Or has he no heir?
Why then has their king taken possession of Gad,
and his people dwell in its cities?

[2] Therefore, behold, days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and I will make heard against Rabbah of the sons of Ammon
the shouting of war;
and it shall become a mound of desolation,
and its daughters shall be burned with fire;
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall dispossess those who dispossessed him,
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] Wail, ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, for Ai is laid waste;
cry out, daughters of Rabbah,
gird on sackcloth,
lament, and run to and fro among the fences;
for their king shall go into exile,
his priests and his princes together.

[4] Why do you boast in the valleys?
Your valley is flowing,
turning-away daughter,
who trusts in her treasures:
"Who will come to me?"

[5] Behold, I am bringing dread upon you,
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts,
from all who are around you;
and you shall be driven out, each man before him,
and there is none to gather the fugitive.

[6] And afterward I will restore the fortunes of the sons of Ammon,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] Concerning Edom. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

Is there no more wisdom in Teman?
Has counsel perished from the sons?
Has their wisdom vanished?

[8] Flee, turn back, go deep to dwell,
inhabitants of Dedan;
for the calamity of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ I have brought upon him,
the time that I visit him.

[9] If grape-gatherers came to you,
they would not leave gleanings;
if thieves by night,
they would ruin what was enough for them.

[10] For I, I have stripped ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ bare;
I have uncovered his hidden places,
and he is not able to hide himself;
his seed is laid waste, and his brothers and his neighbors,
and he is not.

[11] Leave your fatherless ones; I will keep them alive;
and your widows, let them trust in me.

[12] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, those whose judgment was not to drink the cup shall surely drink; and are you the one who shall surely go clear? You shall not go clear, for you shall surely drink.

[13] For by myself I have sworn, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧ shall become a thing that appalls, a reproach, a waste, and a curse; and all her cities shall become everlasting ruins.

[14] A report I have heard from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and an envoy is sent among the nations:
Gather yourselves, and come against her,
and rise up for the battle.

[15] For behold, small I have made you among the nations,
despised among mankind.

[16] Your horror has deceived you,
the presumption of your heart,
you who abide in the clefts of the crag,
who hold the height of the hill;
though you make your nest high like the eagle,
from there I will bring you down, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] And Edom shall become a thing that appalls;
everyone who passes by her shall be appalled
and shall hiss over all her blows.

[18] As at the overthrow of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧ and her neighbors, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
no man shall dwell there,
and no son of man shall sojourn in her.

[19] Behold, like a lion he shall come up from the pride of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧
to a lasting habitation;
for in a moment I will make him run from her,
and whoever is chosen, I will appoint him over her.
For who is like me? And who will summon me?
And who is this shepherd who will stand before me?

[20] Therefore hear the counsel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he has counseled against Edom,
and his plans that he has devised against the inhabitants of Teman:
Surely they shall drag them away, the little ones of the flock;
surely their habitation shall be made desolate over them.

[21] At the sound of their fall the earth shakes;
an outcry: at the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧ its sound is heard.

[22] Behold, like the eagle he shall come up and swoop,
and spread his wings over ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧;
and the heart of the mighty ones of Edom shall be in that day
like the heart of a woman in distress.

[23] Concerning ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧:

⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ is put to shame, and Arpad,
for an evil report they have heard; they melt;
in the sea there is anxiety; it cannot be quiet.

[24] ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ has grown slack; she has turned to flee,
and trembling has seized her;
distress and pangs have taken hold of her,
like a woman giving birth.

[25] How is she not forsaken, the city of praise,
the town of my exultation!

[26] Therefore her young men shall fall in her squares,
and all the men of war shall be silenced in that day,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[27] And I will kindle a fire in the wall of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧,
and it shall devour the citadels of ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧.

[28] Concerning ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧ and concerning the kingdoms of ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧, which ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ struck down. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

Arise, go up to ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧,
and lay waste the sons of the east.

[29] Their tents and their flock they shall take;
their curtains and all their articles and their camels they shall carry off for themselves;
and they shall call out against them: Terror on every side!

[30] Flee, wander far, go deep to dwell,
inhabitants of ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
for ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ has counseled counsel against you
and has devised a plan against you.

[31] Arise, go up to a nation at ease,
that dwells securely, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
it has neither doors nor bar;
alone they abide.

[32] And their camels shall be for plunder,
and the multitude of their livestock for spoil;
and I will scatter them to every wind, the ones clipped at the edge,
and from all its sides I will bring their calamity, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[33] And ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧ shall become an abode of jackals,
a desolation forever;
no man shall dwell there,
and no son of man shall sojourn in her.

[34] That which came as the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet concerning Elam, in the beginning of the reign of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying:

[35] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:
Behold, I am breaking the bow of Elam,
the first of their might.

[36] And I will bring to Elam four winds
from the four ends of the heavens,
and I will scatter them to all these winds;
and there shall be no nation
to which the driven out of Elam shall not come.

[37] And I will dismay Elam before their enemies
and before those who seek their soul,
and I will bring upon them evil, the burning of my anger, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
and I will send after them the sword,
until I have made an end of them.

[38] And I will set my throne in Elam,
and I will make perish from there king and princes, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[39] And it shall be in the latter days:
I will restore the fortunes of Elam,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת "Yahweh of Hosts" (v7, 26, 35); אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת "the Lord Yahweh of Hosts" (v5).
- **Layout and speech marks** — the oracles are poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents; v12–13 and the headings (v1a, 7a, 23a, 28a, 34) prose. The outer quotation marks are dropped: the Hebrew does not mark where Yahweh's words begin and end (v14 is the prophet's own "I have heard"). No "O" before names and titles (v3, 4, 8, 30): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"Concerning the Ammonites" → "Concerning the sons of Ammon"** (v1, 2, 6) — Hebrew בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן, "sons of".
- **"Hath Israel no sons? hath he no heir? why then doth their king inherit Gad, and his people dwell in his cities?" → "Has Israel no sons? Or has he no heir? Why then has their king taken possession of Gad, and his people dwell in its cities?"** (v1) — אִם "or" opens the second question. מַלְכָּם as pointed is "their king" (also v3; KJV the same); the Septuagint, Syriac and Vulgate read the god's name Milcom (מִלְכֹּם, 1 Kgs 11:5; from memory): the MT stands, the token is removed. יָרַשׁ with a land "take possession of"; יָשַׁב "dwell"; עָרָיו "its (Gad's) cities".
- **"I will cause an alarm of war to be heard in Rabbah of the Ammonites; and it shall be a desolate heap, and her daughters shall be burned with fire: then shall Israel be heir unto them that were his heirs" → "I will make heard against Rabbah of the sons of Ammon the shouting of war; and it shall become a mound of desolation, and its daughters shall be burned with fire; and Israel shall dispossess those who dispossessed him"** (v2) — תְּרוּעַת מִלְחָמָה: no instrument is named, so "shouting"; תֵּל שְׁמָמָה "a mound of desolation"; בְּנֹתֶיהָ "its daughters" (the towns belonging to it; "villages" is the sense, not the word); יָרַשׁ with persons "dispossess"; the verse opens with וְ (no "when", no "then").
- **"Howl, O Heshbon … gird you with sackcloth; lament, and run to and fro by the hedges; for their king shall go into captivity, and his priests and his princes together" → "Wail, Heshbon … gird on sackcloth, lament, and run to and fro among the fences; for their king shall go into exile, his priests and his princes together"** (v3) — הֵילִיל "wail"; שֻׁדְּדָה "is laid waste" (שָׁדַד); גְּדֵרוֹת "fences" (stone walls of fields); בַּגּוֹלָה יֵלֵךְ "go into exile"; שָׂרִים "princes".
- **"Wherefore gloriest thou in the valleys, thy flowing valley, O backsliding daughter? that trusted in her treasures, saying, Who shall come unto me?" → "Why do you boast in the valleys? Your valley is flowing, turning-away daughter, who trusts in her treasures: 'Who will come to me?'"** (v4) — זָב עִמְקֵךְ, "flowing is your valley" (זוּב: with water, or with blood: uncertain); הַשּׁוֹבֵבָה "turning-away" (the reviewer's שׁוֹבָב row, Jer 3:14, 22; root שׁוּב; not "faithless"); הַבֹּטְחָה participle "who trusts"; there is no "saying"; אֵלַי "to me" (not "against").
- **"I will bring a fear upon thee … and ye shall be driven out every man right forth; and none shall gather up him that wandereth" → "I am bringing dread upon you … and you shall be driven out, each man before him, and there is none to gather the fugitive"** (v5) — הִנְנִי מֵבִיא participle; פַּחַד "dread"; אִישׁ לְפָנָיו "each man before him" (no "straight"); נֹדֵד "fugitive" (נָדַד "flee").
- **"bring again the captivity of" → "restore the fortunes of"** (v6, 39) — Hebrew שׁוּב שְׁבוּת, literally "turn back the captivity / the turning" (שְׁבוּת from שָׁבָה "take captive", or from שׁוּב); traditional "turn the captivity". In v39 the qere אָשִׁיב שְׁבוּת is read; the written text has אשוב "I will return" and שבית.
- **"Is counsel perished from the prudent? is their wisdom vanished?" → "Has counsel perished from the sons? Has their wisdom vanished?"** (v7) — מִבָּנִים as pointed is "from (the) sons"; "the prudent" takes it from בִּין "understand" (נְבֹנִים), which is not the word printed; נִסְרְחָה "vanished" (lit. "be let loose, spill over": uncertain).
- **"Flee ye, turn back, dwell deep, O inhabitants of Dedan; for I will bring the calamity of Esau upon him, the time that I will visit him" → "Flee, turn back, go deep to dwell, inhabitants of Dedan; for the calamity of Esau I have brought upon him, the time that I visit him"** (v8; v30 the same) — הֶעְמִיקוּ לָשֶׁבֶת "make deep for dwelling"; הֵבֵאתִי perfect "I have brought" (a perfect of what is certain to come); אֵיד "calamity"; פָּקַד "visit".
- **"If grapegatherers come to thee, would they not leave some gleaning grapes? if thieves by night, they will destroy till they have enough" → "If grape-gatherers came to you, they would not leave gleanings; if thieves by night, they would ruin what was enough for them"** (v9) — the printed Hebrew has no question word: לֹא יַשְׁאִרוּ "they would not leave", so nothing is left of Edom. Obad 5 has הֲלוֹא "would they not" (a question): the two are not levelled. הִשְׁחִית "ruin"; דַּיָּם "their enough".
- **"But I have made Esau bare, I have uncovered his secret places, and he shall not be able to hide himself: his seed is spoiled, and his brethren, and his neighbours, and he is not" → "For I, I have stripped Esau bare … and he is not able to hide himself; his seed is laid waste, and his brothers and his neighbors, and he is not"** (v10) — כִּי "for" (not "but"), with the stressed אֲנִי; מִסְתָּרָיו "his hidden places"; זֶרַע "seed" (not "offspring"); שֻׁדַּד "is laid waste".
- **"Leave thy fatherless children, I will preserve them alive; and let thy widows trust in me" → "Leave your fatherless ones; I will keep them alive; and your widows, let them trust in me"** (v11) — יְתֹמִים "fatherless ones"; עָלַי "upon me" (= in me); תִּבְטָחוּ may also be read "shall trust".
- **"Behold, they whose judgment was not to drink of the cup have assuredly drunken; and art thou he that shall altogether go unpunished? thou shalt not go unpunished, but thou shalt surely drink of it" → "Behold, those whose judgment was not to drink the cup shall surely drink; and are you the one who shall surely go clear? You shall not go clear, for you shall surely drink"** (v12) — three infinitives absolute: שָׁתוֹ יִשְׁתּוּ, נָקֹה תִּנָּקֶה, שָׁתֹה תִּשְׁתֶּה (lit. "drinking they shall drink", "being clear you shall be clear", "drinking you shall drink"); יִשְׁתּוּ is imperfect "shall drink"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (what was due to them); נָקָה nifal "go clear"; the middle clause has no question word: it is taken as a question, as the next clause answers it.
- **"a desolation, a reproach, a waste, and a curse; and all the cities thereof shall be perpetual wastes" → "a thing that appalls, a reproach, a waste, and a curse; and all her cities shall become everlasting ruins"** (v13) — שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls" (root שָׁמֵם; also v17); חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; חֹרֶב "waste"; קְלָלָה "curse"; חָרְבוֹת עוֹלָם "ruins of the age".
- **"I have heard a rumour from the LORD, and an ambassador is sent unto the heathen, saying, Gather ye together, and come against her, and rise up to the battle" → "A report I have heard from Yahweh, and an envoy is sent among the nations: Gather yourselves, and come against her, and rise up for the battle"** (v14) — שְׁמוּעָה "report"; צִיר "envoy"; שָׁלוּחַ passive participle "is sent"; no "saying".
- **"For, lo, I will make thee small among the heathen, and despised among men" → "For behold, small I have made you among the nations, despised among mankind"** (v15) — נְתַתִּיךָ perfect "I have made (lit. given) you"; אָדָם "mankind".
- **"Thy terribleness hath deceived thee, and the pride of thine heart, O thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, that holdest the height of the hill" → "Your horror has deceived you, the presumption of your heart, you who abide in the clefts of the crag, who hold the height of the hill"** (v16) — תִּפְלַצְתְּךָ, only here, "your horror" (the dread you cause, or a horrid thing you trust: uncertain); הִשִּׁיא "deceive"; זְדוֹן לִבֶּךָ "the presumption of your heart"; שָׁכַן "abide"; סֶלַע "crag" (perhaps also the name Sela); תֹּפְשִׂי "who hold (seize)".
- **"every one that goeth by it shall be astonished, and shall hiss at all the plagues thereof" → "everyone who passes by her shall be appalled and shall hiss over all her blows"** (v17) — יִשֹּׁם "be appalled" (the root of שַׁמָּה); מַכּוֹת "blows" (not "disasters").
- **"As in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and the neighbour cities thereof … no man shall abide there, neither shall a son of man dwell in it" → "As at the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and her neighbors … no man shall dwell there, and no son of man shall sojourn in her"** (v18, 33) — מַהְפֵּכָה "overthrow"; שְׁכֵנֶיהָ "her neighbors" ("cities" is not written); בֶּן־אָדָם "son of man" is kept (it was "no man" twice); גּוּר "sojourn". Jer 50:40 has the same words.
- **"Behold, he shall come up like a lion from the swelling of Jordan against the habitation of the strong: but I will suddenly make him run away from her: and who is a chosen man, that I may appoint over her? for who is like me? and who will appoint me the time? and who is that shepherd that will stand before me?" → "Behold, like a lion he shall come up from the pride of the Jordan to a lasting habitation; for in a moment I will make him run from her, and whoever is chosen, I will appoint him over her. For who is like me? And who will summon me? And who is this shepherd who will stand before me?"** (v19) — גְּאוֹן הַיַּרְדֵּן "the pride of the Jordan" (its thick growth along the banks; the word is "pride"); נְוֵה אֵיתָן "a lasting habitation" (נָוֶה "habitation, pasture"; אֵיתָן "lasting, never failing"); אַרְגִּיעָה "in a moment" (uncertain: "I will make it rest" is also read); "him" is not named (Edom or the lion); מִי בָחוּר … אֶפְקֹד "who is chosen … I will appoint", taken as a statement; הוֹעִיד "summon" (set a time, call to court). Jer 50:44 has the same words.
- **"Surely the least of the flock shall draw them out: surely he shall make their habitations desolate with them" → "Surely they shall drag them away, the little ones of the flock; surely their habitation shall be made desolate over them"** (v20) — אִם־לֹא twice: the oath form, "surely". צְעִירֵי הַצֹּאן may be the subject ("the little ones drag them") or the object ("they drag them away, even the little ones"): the order is kept, the choice left open. יַשִּׁים hifil of שָׁמֵם, with no named subject ("he shall make desolate", or intransitive "shall be desolate"); נָוֶה "habitation". חָשַׁב … מַחֲשָׁבוֹת "devised … plans" (one root; also v30). Jer 50:45 has the same words.
- **"at the cry the noise thereof was heard in the Red sea" → "an outcry: at the Sea of Reeds its sound is heard"** (v21) — צְעָקָה "outcry" stands alone; קוֹלָהּ "its sound"; יַם־סוּף "Sea of Reeds"; רָעֲשָׁה "shakes".
- **"he shall come up and fly as the eagle … and at that day shall the heart of the mighty men of Edom be as the heart of a woman in her pangs" → "like the eagle he shall come up and swoop … and the heart of the mighty ones of Edom shall be in that day like the heart of a woman in distress"** (v22) — דָּאָה "swoop"; גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones"; מְצֵרָה "in distress" (hifil participle of צָרַר; the pangs of birth are meant: the sense, not the word). Jer 48:40–41 has nearly the same words.
- **"Hamath is confounded, and Arpad: for they have heard evil tidings: they are fainthearted; there is sorrow on the sea; it cannot be quiet" → "Hamath is put to shame, and Arpad, for an evil report they have heard; they melt; in the sea there is anxiety; it cannot be quiet"** (v23) — בּוֹשָׁה "is put to shame" (singular verb, Hamath first); נָמֹגוּ "melt" (מוּג); דְּאָגָה "anxiety"; שָׁקַט "be quiet".
- **"Damascus is waxed feeble, and turneth herself to flee, and fear hath seized on her: anguish and sorrows have taken her, as a woman in travail" → "Damascus has grown slack; she has turned to flee, and trembling has seized her; distress and pangs have taken hold of her, like a woman giving birth"** (v24) — רָפְתָה "grown slack" (רָפָה); רֶטֶט, only here, "trembling"; הֶחֱזִיקָה "seized"; צָרָה "distress"; חֲבָלִים "pangs"; אָחַז "take hold".
- **"How is the city of praise not left, the city of my joy!" → "How is she not forsaken, the city of praise, the town of my exultation!"** (v25) — the qere תְּהִלַּת "of praise" is read (the written תהלה, the same sense); עָזַב "forsake"; קִרְיָה "town" (beside עִיר "city"); מָשׂוֹשׂ "exultation". "Famous" (ESV) is not the word.
- **"and all the men of war shall be cut off in that day" → "and all the men of war shall be silenced in that day"** (v26) — יִדַּמּוּ nifal of דָּמַם "be silenced"; אַנְשֵׁי הַמִּלְחָמָה "the men of war" (not "soldiers"); no "her". Jer 50:30 has the same words.
- **"palaces" → "citadels"** (v27) — אַרְמְנוֹת. Amos 1:4 has nearly the same words.
- **"Arise ye, go up to Kedar, and spoil the men of the east" → "Arise, go up to Kedar, and lay waste the sons of the east"** (v28) — וּלְמַמְלְכוֹת "and concerning the kingdoms" (the לְ repeated); the qere נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּר is read; עָלָה אֶל "go up to"; שָׁדַד "lay waste"; בְּנֵי־קֶדֶם "the sons of the east".
- **"Their tents and their flocks shall they take away: they shall take to themselves their curtains, and all their vessels, and their camels; and they shall cry unto them, Fear is on every side" → "Their tents and their flock they shall take; their curtains and all their articles and their camels they shall carry off for themselves; and they shall call out against them: Terror on every side!"** (v29) — יִקָּחוּ is active, "they shall take" (no subject named); צֹאן "flock"; כְּלִי "article"; נָשָׂא לָהֶם "carry off for themselves"; מָגוֹר מִסָּבִיב "terror on every side" (as Jer 6:25; 20:3, 10; 46:5).
- **"hath taken counsel against you, and hath conceived a purpose against you" → "has counseled counsel against you and has devised a plan against you"** (v30) — יָעַץ … עֵצָה, חָשַׁב … מַחֲשָׁבָה (each verb with its own noun); נֻדוּ מְאֹד "wander far"; the qere עֲלֵיכֶם "against you" is read; the written text has עליהם "against them".
- **"the wealthy nation, that dwelleth without care … which have neither gates nor bars, which dwell alone" → "a nation at ease, that dwells securely … it has neither doors nor bar; alone they abide"** (v31) — גּוֹי שְׁלֵיו "a nation at ease" (untroubled, not "wealthy"); יוֹשֵׁב לָבֶטַח "dwells securely"; דְּלָתַיִם "doors" (the two leaves; not "gates": שַׁעַר); בְּרִיחַ "bar" (singular); שָׁכַן "abide", the verb plural.
- **"And their camels shall be a booty, and the multitude of their cattle a spoil: and I will scatter into all winds them that are in the utmost corners; and I will bring their calamity from all sides thereof" → "And their camels shall be for plunder, and the multitude of their livestock for spoil; and I will scatter them to every wind, the ones clipped at the edge, and from all its sides I will bring their calamity"** (v32) — בַּז "plunder", שָׁלָל "spoil"; מִקְנֶה "livestock"; זֵרָה "scatter" (lit. "strew, winnow", as Exod 32:20: of people driven by the wind; also v36); קְצוּצֵי פֵאָה "clipped at the edge" (of the hair at the temples, as Jer 9:26; 25:23); עֲבָרָיו "its sides".
- **"And Hazor shall be a dwelling for dragons, and a desolation for ever" → "And Hazor shall become an abode of jackals, a desolation forever"** (v33) — מְעוֹן "abode"; תַּנִּים "jackals" (not תַּנִּין "dragon").
- **"The word of the LORD that came to Jeremiah the prophet against Elam" → "That which came as the word of Yahweh to Jeremiah the prophet concerning Elam … saying"** (v34) — the clause opens with אֲשֶׁר, as Jer 14:1; 46:1; 47:1 (the Jer 45–48 row); אֶל "concerning"; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept.
- **"I will break the bow of Elam, the chief of their might" → "I am breaking the bow of Elam, the first of their might"** (v35) — שֹׁבֵר participle; רֵאשִׁית "the first" (the chief part; "mainstay" is not the word); גְּבוּרָה "might".
- **"the four winds from the four quarters of heaven … the outcasts of Elam" → "four winds from the four ends of the heavens … the driven out of Elam"** (v36) — אַרְבַּע רוּחוֹת has no article; קְצוֹת "ends"; שָׁמַיִם "the heavens"; נִדְּחֵי "the driven out"; the qere עֵילָם is read; the written text has עולם ("of the age").
- **"For I will cause Elam to be dismayed … and I will bring evil upon them, even my fierce anger … till I have consumed them: And I will set my throne in Elam, and will destroy from thence the king and the princes" → "And I will dismay Elam … and I will bring upon them evil, the burning of my anger … until I have made an end of them. And I will set my throne in Elam, and I will make perish from there king and princes"** (v37–38) — הֵחַת hifil "dismay"; מְבַקְשֵׁי נַפְשָׁם "those who seek their soul"; רָעָה "evil"; חֲרוֹן אַף "the burning of anger"; כִּלָּה piel "make an end of"; הֶאֱבִיד "make perish"; מֶלֶךְ וְשָׂרִים without articles.
- **"But it shall come to pass in the latter days" → "And it shall be in the latter days"** (v39) — וְהָיָה; בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים "in the latter days".

### Notes
- **v7–22** — beside Obadiah 1–9 (v9 ≈ Obad 5; v10 ≈ Obad 6; v14–16 ≈ Obad 1–4); each is translated as printed, and the differences are not levelled (v9).
- **v18–21** — Jer 50:40, 44–46 repeat these lines of Babylon.
