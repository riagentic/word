# Jeremiah 49

[1] Concerning the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Has ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ no sons?
Has he no heir?
Why then has ⟦milcom|Milkom|Milcom⟧ dispossessed Gad,
and his people settled in its cities?

[2] Therefore, behold, the days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
when I will cause the battle cry to be heard
against Rabbah of the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧;
it shall become a desolate mound,
and its villages shall be burned with fire;
then ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall dispossess those who dispossessed him,
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] Wail, O ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, for Ai is laid waste!
Cry out, O daughters of Rabbah!
Put on sackcloth, lament, and run to and fro among the hedges!
For ⟦milcom|Milkom|Milcom⟧ shall go into exile,
with his priests and his officials.

[4] Why do you boast of your valleys,
O faithless daughter,
who trusted in her treasures, saying,
'Who will come against me?'

[5] Behold, I will bring terror upon you,
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts,
from all who are around you,
and you shall be driven out, every man straight before him,
with none to gather the fugitives.

[6] But afterward I will restore the fortunes of the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[7] Concerning Edom. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

"Is wisdom no more in Teman?
Has counsel perished from the prudent?
Has their wisdom vanished?

[8] Flee, turn back, dwell in the depths,
O inhabitants of Dedan!
For I will bring the calamity of ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ upon him,
the time when I punish him.

[9] If grape-gatherers came to you,
would they not leave gleanings?
If thieves came by night,
would they not destroy only enough for themselves?

[10] But I have stripped ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ bare;
I have uncovered his hiding places,
and he is not able to conceal himself.
His offspring are destroyed, and his brothers, and his neighbors;
and he is no more.

[11] Leave your fatherless children; I will keep them alive;
and let your widows trust in me."

[12] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: "If those who did not deserve to drink the cup must drink it, will you go unpunished? You shall not go unpunished, but you must drink.

[13] For I have sworn by myself, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧ shall become a horror, a taunt, a waste, and a curse, and all her cities shall be perpetual wastes."

[14] I have heard a message from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and a messenger has been sent among the nations:
"Gather yourselves together, and come against her,
and rise up for battle!

[15] For behold, I make you small among the nations,
despised among mankind.

[16] The horror you inspire has deceived you,
and the pride of your heart,
O you who live in the clefts of the rock,
who hold the height of the hill.
Though you make your nest as high as the eagle's,
I will bring you down from there, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] Edom shall become a horror.
Everyone who passes by it
will be horrified and will hiss because of all its disasters.

[18] As when ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧
and their neighboring cities were overthrown, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
no man shall dwell there,
no man shall sojourn in her.

[19] Behold, like a lion coming up from the jungle of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧
against a perennial pasture,
I will suddenly make him run away from her,
and I will appoint over her whomever I choose.
For who is like me? Who will summon me?
What shepherd can stand before me?

[20] Therefore hear the counsel that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has taken against Edom,
and the purposes that he has formed against the inhabitants of Teman:
Even the little ones of the flock shall be dragged away;
surely their fold shall be appalled at their fate.

[21] At the sound of their fall the earth shall tremble;
the sound of their cry shall be heard at the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧.

[22] Behold, one shall mount up and fly swiftly like an eagle
and spread his wings against ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧,
and the heart of the warriors of Edom shall be in that day
like the heart of a woman in her birth pangs."

[23] Concerning ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧:

"⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ and Arpad are confounded,
for they have heard bad news;
they melt in fear,
they are troubled like the sea that cannot be quiet.

[24] ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ has become feeble, she turned to flee,
and panic seized her;
anguish and sorrows have taken hold of her,
as of a woman in labor.

[25] How is the famous city not forsaken,
the city of my joy?

[26] Therefore her young men shall fall in her squares,
and all her soldiers shall be destroyed in that day,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[27] And I will kindle a fire in the wall of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧,
and it shall devour the strongholds of ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧."

[28] Concerning ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧ and the kingdoms of ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧ that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ struck down. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Rise up, advance against ⟦kedar|Qedar|Kedar⟧!
Destroy the people of the east!

[29] Their tents and their flocks shall be taken,
their curtains and all their goods;
their camels shall be led away from them,
and men shall cry to them: 'Terror on every side!'

[30] Flee, wander far away, dwell in the depths,
O inhabitants of ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧! declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
For ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ has made a plan against you
and formed a purpose against you.

[31] Rise up, advance against a nation at ease,
that dwells securely, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
that has no gates or bars,
that dwells alone.

[32] Their camels shall become plunder,
their herds of livestock a spoil.
I will scatter to every wind those who cut the corners of their hair,
and I will bring their calamity from every side of them,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[33] ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧ shall become a haunt of jackals,
an everlasting waste;
no man shall dwell there;
no man shall sojourn in her."

[34] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet concerning Elam, in the beginning of the reign of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧:

[35] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:
Behold, I will break the bow of Elam,
the mainstay of their might.

[36] And I will bring upon Elam the four winds
from the four quarters of heaven.
And I will scatter them to all those winds,
and there shall be no nation
to which those driven out of Elam shall not come.

[37] I will terrify Elam before their enemies
and before those who seek their life.
I will bring disaster upon them, my fierce anger, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
I will send the sword after them,
until I have consumed them,

[38] and I will set my throne in Elam
and destroy their king and officials, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[39] But in the latter days I will restore the fortunes of Elam,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv7, 26, 35) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD of hosts" → "Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v5) — Hebrew full triple title preserved.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v21) — Hebrew יַם־סוּף *Yam Suph*.
- **Poetic structure** — oracles against Ammon, Edom, Damascus, Kedar, and Elam formatted as verse; brief prose headings preserved.

### Notes
- **Milkom** — chief Ammonite god (cf. 1 Kgs 11:5).
