# Jeremiah 20

[1] And ⟦pashhur|Pashchur|Pashhur⟧ the son of Immer, the priest — and he was overseer, leader in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — heard ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ prophesying these words.

[2] And ⟦pashhur|Pashchur|Pashhur⟧ struck ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet, and put him in the stocks that were in the upper gate of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, which was in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] And it came to pass on the morrow that ⟦pashhur|Pashchur|Pashhur⟧ brought ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ out from the stocks; and ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ said to him: "Not ⟦pashhur|Pashchur|Pashhur⟧ has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ called your name, but ⟦magor-missabib|Magor Missaviv|Terror on Every Side⟧.

[4] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am making you a terror to yourself and to all who love you; and they shall fall by the sword of their enemies, and your eyes seeing. And all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ I will give into the hand of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and he shall carry them into exile to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and strike them with the sword.

[5] And I will give all the store of this city and all its labor and all its precious things, and all the treasuries of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ I will give into the hand of their enemies, and they shall plunder them and take them and bring them to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[6] And you, ⟦pashhur|Pashchur|Pashhur⟧, and all who dwell in your house, shall go into captivity; and to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ you shall come, and there you shall die, and there you shall be buried, you and all who love you, to whom you have prophesied in falsehood."

[7] You have enticed me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and I was enticed;
you were strong over me,
and you overcame.
I have become a laughingstock all the day;
everyone derides me.

[8] For as often as I speak, I cry out;
"Violence and havoc!" I call out;
for the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has become to me
a reproach and a derision all the day.

[9] And I said, "I will not remember him,
and I will not speak any more in his name";
and it was in my heart like a burning fire,
shut in in my bones;
and I was weary of holding it in,
and I could not.

[10] For I have heard the report of many,
terror on every side:
"Tell, and let us tell of him!"
every mortal man of my ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧,
those who watch for my stumbling:
"Perhaps he will be enticed,
and we shall overcome him,
and take our vengeance on him."

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is with me like a ruthless mighty one;
therefore my pursuers shall stumble,
and shall not overcome;
they are greatly ashamed,
for they have not acted with insight,
an everlasting dishonor
that shall not be forgotten.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts, who tries the righteous,
who sees the kidneys and the heart,
I shall see your vengeance on them,
for to you I have uncovered my case.

[13] Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
For he has delivered the soul of the needy
from the hand of evildoers.

[14] Cursed is the day
on which I was born;
the day when my mother bore me,
let it not be blessed.

[15] Cursed is the man who brought news to my father, saying,
"A son is born to you, a male,"
surely gladdening him.

[16] And that man shall be like the cities
that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ overthrew and did not relent;
and he shall hear an outcry in the morning
and shouting at the time of noon,

[17] because he did not put me to death from the womb,
and my mother would have been my grave,
and her womb with child forever.

[18] Why is it that I came out from the womb,
to see toil and sorrow,
and my days have come to an end in shame?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v12) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **"Pashur" → "Pashhur"** (v1–3, 6) — Hebrew פַּשְׁחוּר.
- **Layout** — vv1–6 prose; vv7–18 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the existing breaks inside a line are kept where the accents mark a pause (v7, 10, 11, 14). "say" (v10) and "making him" (v15), which joined lines, are not in the Hebrew.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v1–6, 9, 11–12, 16–17).
- **"Now Pashur the son of Immer the priest, who was also chief governor" → "And Pashhur the son of Immer, the priest — and he was overseer, leader"** (v1) — Hebrew וְהוּא־פָקִיד נָגִיד, a clause of its own; פָּקִיד "overseer", נָגִיד "leader" (two nouns side by side; "chief officer" joins them). **"prophesied these things" → "prophesying these words"** — נִבָּא, participle; דְּבָרִים "words".
- **"smote Jeremiah" → "struck Jeremiah"** (v2) — הִכָּה "strike". **"put him in the stocks" → "put him in the stocks"** — מַהְפֶּכֶת (uncertain: from הָפַךְ "turn", a frame that held the body twisted; its row). **"in the high gate of Benjamin" → "in the upper gate of Benjamin"** — שַׁעַר בִּנְיָמִן הָעֶלְיוֹן; **"which was by the house of the LORD" → "which was in the house of Yahweh"** — בְּבֵית.
- **"And it came to pass on the morrow"** (v3) — וַיְהִי מִמָּחֳרָת. **"The LORD hath not called thy name Pashur, but Magor-missabib" → "Not Pashhur has Yahweh called your name, but Terror on Every Side"** — the Hebrew order (לֹא פַשְׁחוּר first); the name מָגוֹר מִסָּבִיב "terror on every side", as the Ps 31:13 row and v10 (token `magor-missabib`; the meaning was written into the token before: taken out, it stands here).
- **"I will make thee a terror … to all thy friends" → "I am making you a terror … to all who love you"** (v4) — הִנְנִי נֹתֶנְךָ לְמָגוֹר, literally "I am giving you for a terror" (מָגוֹר, the word of the name); אֹהֲבֶיךָ "those who love you" (also v6). **"and thine eyes shall behold it" → "and your eyes seeing"** — וְעֵינֶיךָ רֹאוֹת, a participle clause. **"carry them captive" → "carry them into exile"** — הִגְלָה. **"slay them with the sword" → "strike them with the sword"** — הִכָּה.
- **"I will deliver all the strength of this city, and all the labours thereof, and all the precious things thereof" → "I will give all the store of this city and all its labor and all its precious things"** (v5) — חֹסֶן "store" (its row); יְגִיעַ "labor" (what is gained by labor); יְקָר "honor" of a king (Esth), here "precious things". The Hebrew has "I will give" twice (וְנָתַתִּי … אֶתֵּן): kept. **"which shall spoil them" → "and they shall plunder them"** — בָּזַז "plunder"; לָקַח "take"; הֵבִיא "bring".
- **"shall go into captivity"** (v6) — בַּשֶּׁבִי, "into the captivity" (שְׁבִי "captives"). **"thou shalt come to Babylon"** — the Hebrew order "and to Babylon you shall come". **"prophesied lies" → "prophesied in falsehood"** — בַּשָּׁקֶר.
- **"O LORD, thou hast deceived me, and I was deceived" → "You have enticed me, Yahweh, and I was enticed"** (v7) — פִּתָּה piel, nifal (the "entice" row: persuade, lead on; "deceive" and "seduce" are both read here: Exod 22:16; 1 Kgs 22:20–22); no "O". **"thou art stronger than I, and hast prevailed" → "you were strong over me, and you overcame"** — חֲזַקְתַּנִי, חָזַק "be strong" with an object ("seized me"); וַתּוּכָל, יָכֹל "overcome" (as v10, 11). **"I am in derision daily, every one mocketh me" → "I have become a laughingstock all the day; everyone derides me"** — הָיִיתִי לִשְׂחוֹק (its row); כֻּלֹּה "all of him" = everyone; לָעַג "deride".
- **"For since I spake, I cried out, I cried violence and spoil" → "For as often as I speak, I cry out; 'Violence and havoc!' I call out"** (v8) — מִדֵּי "as often as"; זָעַק "cry out", קָרָא "call out"; חָמָס "violence", שֹׁד "havoc". **"a reproach unto me, and a derision, daily" → "has become to me a reproach and a derision all the day"** — חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; קֶלֶס "derision"; כָּל־הַיּוֹם "all the day" (also v7).
- **"Then I said, I will not make mention of him" → "And I said, I will not remember him"** (v9) — וְאָמַרְתִּי; אֶזְכְּרֶנּוּ, qal of זָכַר "remember" (KJV "make mention" is the sense of the hifil). **"But his word was in mine heart as a burning fire" → "and it was in my heart like a burning fire"** — וְהָיָה, no subject named ("his word" is supplied in KJV). **"shut up in my bones" → "shut in in my bones"** — עָצֻר (the עָצוּר row). **"I was weary with forbearing, and I could not stay" → "I was weary of holding it in, and I could not"** — כַּלְכֵל, "to contain, hold in" (pilpel of כּוּל; the כִּלְכֵּל "sustain" of Genesis is the same form, another sense: note); "stay" is not written.
- **"For I heard the defaming of many, fear on every side" → "For I have heard the report of many, terror on every side"** (v10) — דִּבָּה "report" (a report that defames: the דִּבָּה row); מָגוֹר מִסָּבִיב "terror on every side" (the Ps 31:13 row; the words of the name, v3). **"Report, say they, and we will report it" → "Tell, and let us tell of him!"** — הַגִּידוּ וְנַגִּידֶנּוּ; הִגִּיד "tell"; "say they" is not written. **"All my familiars watched for my halting" → "every mortal man of my shalom, those who watch for my stumbling"** — כֹּל אֱנוֹשׁ שְׁלוֹמִי (as Ps 41:9 "the man of my shalom"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"); צַלְעִי "my stumbling" (the צֶלַע "limping" row). **"Peradventure he will be enticed, and we shall prevail against him" → "Perhaps he will be enticed, and we shall overcome him"** — יְפֻתֶּה (פִּתָּה, as v7); יָכֹל לְ "overcome"; נְקָמָה "vengeance".
- **"But the LORD is with me as a mighty terrible one" → "And Yahweh is with me like a ruthless mighty one"** (v11) — וַיהוָה; גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; עָרִיץ "ruthless" (its row). **"my persecutors shall stumble, and they shall not prevail" → "my pursuers shall stumble, and shall not overcome"** — רֹדְפַי; כָּשַׁל; יָכֹל (no "me" in the Hebrew). **"they shall be greatly ashamed" → "they are greatly ashamed"** — בֹּשׁוּ, a perfect (what is certain; or "they will be"). **"for they shall not prosper" → "for they have not acted with insight"** — הִשְׂכִּילוּ, perfect of הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight" ("prosper" is a later sense). **"their everlasting confusion shall never be forgotten" → "an everlasting dishonor that shall not be forgotten"** — כְּלִמַּת עוֹלָם (כְּלִמָּה "dishonor", reviewer row; עוֹלָם genitive "everlasting"); "their" is not written.
- **"But, O LORD of hosts, that triest the righteous, and seest the reins and the heart" → "And Yahweh of Hosts, who tries the righteous, who sees the kidneys and the heart"** (v12) — בֹּחֵן "try" (reviewer row); כְּלָיוֹת "kidneys"; no "O". **"let me see thy vengeance on them" → "I shall see your vengeance on them"** — אֶרְאֶה, an imperfect (not the cohortative form; read as a wish it is "let me see", as KJV and Jer 11:20). **"opened my cause" → "uncovered my case"** — גִּלָּה "uncover"; רִיב "case".
- **"for he hath delivered the soul of the poor" → "For he has delivered the soul of the needy"** (v13) — הִצִּיל "deliver"; אֶבְיוֹן "needy". **"evildoers"** — מְרֵעִים.
- **"Cursed be the day" → "Cursed is the day"** (v14, 15) — אָרוּר, no verb in the Hebrew (its row: "is", not "be"). **"let not the day wherein my mother bare me be blessed" → "the day when my mother bore me, let it not be blessed"** — the Hebrew order; אַל־יְהִי בָרוּךְ.
- **"who brought tidings to my father" → "who brought news to my father, saying"** (v15) — בִּשַּׂר; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept. **"A man child is born unto thee" → "A son is born to you, a male"** — בֵּן זָכָר, "a son, a male". **"making him very glad" → "surely gladdening him"** — שַׂמֵּחַ שִׂמְּחָהוּ, literally "gladdening he gladdened him" (infinitive absolute); שִׂמַּח "gladden".
- **"And let that man be as the cities" → "And that man shall be like the cities"** (v16) — וְהָיָה, a perfect with "and" (not a jussive; the wish is carried by the curse it follows). **"which the LORD overthrew, and repented not" → "that Yahweh overthrew and did not relent"** — הָפַךְ "overthrow" (as Sodom, Gen 19:25); נִחַם "relent". **"the cry" → "an outcry"** — זְעָקָה. **"the shouting at noontide" → "shouting at the time of noon"** — תְּרוּעָה (neither voice nor instrument named: "shouting", reviewer row); בְּעֵת צָהֳרָיִם "at the time of noon".
- **"Because he slew me not from the womb" → "because he did not put me to death from the womb"** (v17) — מוֹתְתַנִי, polel of מוּת (its row); the subject is not named ("he": the man of v15–16, or no one in particular). **"or that my mother might have been my grave" → "and my mother would have been my grave"** — וַתְּהִי־לִי אִמִּי קִבְרִי, literally "and my mother would have been to me my grave". **"and her womb to be always great with me" → "and her womb with child forever"** — הֲרַת עוֹלָם, הָרָה "with child"; "with me" is not written.
- **"Wherefore came I forth out of the womb" → "Why is it that I came out from the womb"** (v18) — לָמָּה זֶּה. עָמָל "toil", יָגוֹן "sorrow". **"that my days should be consumed with shame" → "and my days have come to an end in shame"** — וַיִּכְלוּ, wayyiqtol of כָּלָה "come to an end"; בֹּשֶׁת "shame".

### Notes
- **vv7–18** — Jeremiah's last complaint; vv14–18 the curse of the day of his birth (beside Job 3).
- **v10** — the words of the name in v3, now said of Jeremiah by those around him.
