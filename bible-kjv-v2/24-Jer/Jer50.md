# Jeremiah 50

[1] The word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke concerning ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, concerning the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, by ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet:

[2] "Declare among the nations and proclaim,
set up a banner and proclaim,
conceal it not, and say:
'⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ is taken,
Bel is put to shame,
Merodach is dismayed.
Her images are put to shame,
her idols are dismayed.'

[3] For out of the north a nation has come up against her,
which shall make her land a desolation,
and none shall dwell in it;
both man and beast shall flee away.

[4] In those days and in that time, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the children of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall come together,
weeping as they come,
and they shall seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] They shall ask the way to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
with faces turned toward it, saying,
'Come, let us join ourselves to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
in an everlasting covenant that will never be forgotten.'

[6] My people have been lost sheep.
Their shepherds have led them astray,
turning them away on the mountains.
From mountain to hill they have gone.
They have forgotten their fold.

[7] All who found them have devoured them,
and their enemies have said, 'We are not guilty,
for they have sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the habitation of righteousness,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the hope of their fathers.'

[8] Flee from the midst of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and go out of the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧,
and be as male goats before the flock.

[9] For behold, I am stirring up
and bringing against ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧
a gathering of great nations from the north country.
And they shall array themselves against her;
from there she shall be taken.
Their arrows are like a skilled warrior
who does not return empty-handed.

[10] ⟦chaldea|Kasdim|Chaldea⟧ shall be plundered;
all who plunder her shall be sated, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] Though you rejoice, though you exult,
O plunderers of my heritage,
though you frolic like a heifer in the pasture,
and neigh like stallions,

[12] your mother shall be utterly shamed,
and she who bore you shall be disgraced.
Behold, she shall be the last of the nations,
a wilderness, a dry land, and a desert.

[13] Because of the wrath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ she shall not be inhabited
but shall be an utter desolation;
everyone who passes by ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ shall be appalled,
and hiss because of all her wounds.

[14] Set yourselves in array against ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ all around,
all you who bend the bow;
shoot at her, spare no arrows,
for she has sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] Raise a shout against her all around;
she has surrendered;
her bulwarks have fallen;
her walls are thrown down.
For this is the vengeance of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:
take vengeance on her;
do to her as she has done.

[16] Cut off from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ the sower,
and the one who handles the sickle in time of harvest;
because of the sword of the oppressor,
every one shall turn to his own people,
and every one shall flee to his own land.

[17] ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ is a hunted sheep driven away by lions.
First the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ devoured him,
and now at last ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ has gnawed his bones.

[18] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:
Behold, I am bringing punishment on the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and his land,
as I punished the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.

[19] I will restore ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to his pasture,
and he shall feed on ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ and Bashan,
and his soul shall be satisfied
on the hills of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧.

[20] In those days and in that time, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
iniquity shall be sought in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and there shall be none,
and sin in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and none shall be found,
for I will pardon those whom I leave as a remnant.

[21] Go up against the land of Double Rebellion,
and against the inhabitants of ⟦pekod|Peqod|Pekod⟧.
Kill, and devote them to destruction, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and do all that I have commanded you.

[22] The noise of battle is in the land,
and great destruction!

[23] How the hammer of the whole earth
is cut down and broken!
How ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ has become
a horror among the nations!

[24] I set a snare for you and you were taken, O ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and you did not know it;
you were found and caught,
because you opposed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[25] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has opened his armory
and brought out the weapons of his wrath,
for the ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts has a work to do
in the land of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧.

[26] Come against her from every side;
open her granaries;
pile her up like heaps of grain, and devote her to destruction;
let nothing be left of her.

[27] Kill all her bulls;
let them go down to the slaughter.
Woe to them, for their day has come,
the time of their punishment.

[28] A voice! They flee and escape from the land of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
to declare in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ the vengeance of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
vengeance for his temple.

[29] Summon archers against ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, all those who bend the bow.
Encamp around her;
let no one escape.
Repay her according to her deeds;
do to her according to all that she has done.
For she has proudly defied ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[30] Therefore her young men shall fall in her squares,
and all her soldiers shall be destroyed on that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] Behold, I am against you, O proud one,
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts,
for your day has come,
the time when I will punish you.

[32] The proud one shall stumble and fall,

[33] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts:

"The children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ are oppressed,
and the children of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ with them;

they refuse to let them go.

[34] Their Redeemer is strong;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts is his name.

that he may give rest to the earth,
but unrest to the inhabitants of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[35] "A sword against the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and against the inhabitants of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and against her officials and her wise men!

[36] A sword against the diviners,
that they may become fools!
A sword against her warriors,
that they may be destroyed!

[37] A sword against her horses and against her chariots,
and against all the foreign troops in her midst,
that they may become women!
A sword against all her treasures,
that they may be plundered!

[38] A drought against her waters,
that they may be dried up!
For it is a land of images,
and they are mad over idols.

[39] Therefore wild beasts shall dwell with hyenas in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,
and ostriches shall dwell in her;
she shall never again have people,
nor be inhabited for all generations.

[40] As when ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ overthrew ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧
and their neighboring cities, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

and no son of man shall sojourn in her.

[41] Behold, a people comes from the north;
a mighty nation and many kings
are stirring from the farthest parts of the earth.

[42] They lay hold of bow and spear;
they are cruel and have no mercy.
The sound of them is like the roaring of the sea;
they ride on horses,
arrayed as a man for battle

[43] The king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ heard the report of them,

[44] Behold, like a lion coming up from the thicket of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧

[45] Therefore hear the plan that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made against ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧,

[46] At the sound of the capture of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv18, 25, 33-34) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD of hosts" → "Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv25, 31) — Hebrew full triple title preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 28, 40) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entire chapter formatted as extended verse oracle against Babylon; prose header v1 preserved.

### Notes
- **Bel** (v2) = Marduk; **Merodach** = Marduk's name.
- **Eclipses Isa 13 and Rev 18** — one of three great anti-Babylon oracles.
