# Jeremiah 39

[1] In the ninth year of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the tenth month, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and all his army came against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and besieged it.

[2] In the eleventh year of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧, in the fourth month, on the ninth day of the month, a breach was made in the city.

[3] Then all the officials of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came and sat in the middle gate: ⟦nergal-sharezer|Nergal-Sar-Etzer, Samgar-Nevu, Sar-Sechim|Nergal-sar-ezer of Samgar, Nebu-sar-sekim⟧ the ⟦rab-saris|Rav-Saris|Rab-saris⟧ — chief officer, ⟦nergal-sharezer|Nergal-Sar-Etzer|Nergal-sar-ezer⟧ the ⟦rab-mag|Rav-Mag|Rab-mag⟧ — chief magus, with all the rest of the officials of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[4] When ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and all the soldiers saw them, they fled, going out of the city at night by way of the king's garden through the gate between the two walls; and they went toward the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧.

[5] But the army of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ pursued them and overtook ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ in the plains of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧. And when they had taken him, they brought him up to ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ in the land of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧; and he passed sentence on him.

[6] The king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ slaughtered the sons of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ before his eyes, and the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ slaughtered all the nobles of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[7] He put out the eyes of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ and bound him in chains to take him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[8] The ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ burned the king's house and the house of the people, and broke down the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[9] Then ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard carried into exile to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ the rest of the people who were left in the city, those who had deserted to him, and the people who remained.

[10] ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard left in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ some of the poor people who owned nothing, and gave them vineyards and fields at the same time.

[11] ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadretzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ gave command concerning ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ through ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard, saying,

[12] "Take him, look after him well, and do him no harm, but deal with him as he tells you."

[13] So ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the captain of the guard, ⟦nebushazban|Nevushazban|Nebushazban⟧ the ⟦rab-saris|Rav-Saris|Rab-saris⟧, ⟦nergal-sharezer|Nergal-Sar-Etzer|Nergal-sar-ezer⟧ the ⟦rab-mag|Rav-Mag|Rab-mag⟧, and all the chief officers of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧

[14] sent and took ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from the court of the guard. They entrusted him to ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ son of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧ son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧, that he should take him home. So he lived among the people.

[15] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ while he was shut up in the court of the guard:

[16] "Go, and say to ⟦ebed-melech|Eved-Melech|Ebed-melech⟧ the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Ethiopian⟧, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: Behold, I will fulfill my words against this city for disaster and not for good, and they shall be accomplished before you on that day.

[17] But I will deliver you on that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and you shall not be given into the hand of the men of whom you are afraid.

[18] For I will surely save you, and you shall not fall by the sword, but you shall have your life as a prize of war, because you have put your trust in me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v16) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v16) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; fall of Jerusalem narrative.

### Notes
- **586 BC** — fall of Jerusalem after 18-month siege.
