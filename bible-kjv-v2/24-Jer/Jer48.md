# Jeremiah 48

Concerning ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧. Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[1] "Woe to ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧, for it is laid waste!
⟦kiriathaim|Qiryatayim|Kiriathaim⟧ is put to shame, it is taken;
the fortress is put to shame and broken down;

[2] the renown of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is no more.
In ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ they planned disaster against her:
'Come, let us cut her off from being a nation!'
You also, O Madmen, shall be brought to silence;
the sword shall pursue you.

[3] A voice! A cry from ⟦horonaim|Choronayim|Horonaim⟧,
'Desolation and great destruction!'

[4] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is broken;
her little ones have made a cry.

[5] For at the ascent of ⟦luhith|Luchit|Luhith⟧
they go up weeping;
for at the descent of ⟦horonaim|Choronayim|Horonaim⟧
they have heard the distressed cry of destruction.

[6] Flee! Save yourselves!
You will be like a juniper in the desert!

[7] For, because you trusted in your works and your treasures,
you also shall be taken;
and ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧ shall go into exile
with his priests and his officials.

[8] The destroyer shall come upon every city,
and no city shall escape;
the valley shall perish,
and the plain shall be destroyed,
as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[9] Give wings to ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
for she must fly away;
her cities shall become a desolation,
with no inhabitant in them.

[10] Cursed is he who does the work of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with slackness,
and cursed is he who keeps back his sword from bloodshed.

[11] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has been at ease from his youth
and has settled on his dregs;
he has not been emptied from vessel to vessel,
nor has he gone into exile;
so his taste remains in him,
and his scent is not changed.

[12] Therefore, behold, the days are coming, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
when I shall send to him pourers who will pour him,
and empty his vessels and break his jars in pieces.

[13] Then ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be ashamed of ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧,
as the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was ashamed of ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, their confidence.

[14] How do you say, 'We are heroes
and mighty men of war'?

[15] The destroyer of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and his cities has come up,
and the choicest of his young men have gone down to slaughter,
declares the King, whose name is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[16] The calamity of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is near at hand,
and his affliction hastens swiftly.

[17] Grieve for him, all you who are around him,
and all who know his name;
say, 'How the mighty scepter is broken,
the glorious staff!'

[18] Come down from your glory,
and sit on the parched ground,
O inhabitant of ⟦dibon|Divon|Dibon⟧!
For the destroyer of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has come up against you;
he has destroyed your strongholds.

[19] Stand by the way and watch,
O inhabitant of Aroer!
Ask him who flees and her who escapes;
say, 'What has happened?'

[20] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is put to shame, for it is broken;
wail and cry!
Tell it beside the Arnon,
that ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is laid waste.

[21] Judgment has come upon the tableland, upon ⟦holon|Cholon|Holon⟧, and ⟦jahaz|Yahtzah|Jahzah⟧, and ⟦mephaath|Mefaat|Mephaath⟧,

[22] and ⟦dibon|Divon|Dibon⟧, and ⟦nebo|Nevo|Nebo⟧, and ⟦beth-diblathaim|Beit Divlatayim|Beth-diblathaim⟧,

[23] and ⟦kiriathaim|Qiryatayim|Kiriathaim⟧, and ⟦beth-gamul|Beit Gamul|Beth-gamul⟧, and ⟦beth-meon|Beit Meon|Beth-meon⟧,

[24] and ⟦kerioth|Qeriyot|Kerioth⟧, and ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧, and all the cities of the land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, far and near.

[25] The horn of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is cut off,
and his arm is broken, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[26] Make him drunk, because he magnified himself against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
so that ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall wallow in his vomit,
and he too shall be held in derision.

[27] Was not ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a derision to you?
Was he found among thieves,
that whenever you spoke of him you wagged your head?

[28] Leave the cities, and dwell in the rock,
O inhabitants of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧!
Be like the dove
that nests in the sides of the mouth of a gorge.

[29] We have heard of the pride of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ —
he is very proud —
of his loftiness, his pride, and his arrogance,
and the haughtiness of his heart.

[30] I know his insolence, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
his boasts are false,
his deeds are false.

[31] Therefore I wail for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
I cry out for all ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
for the men of ⟦kir-heres|Qir Cheres|Kir-hareseth⟧ I mourn.

[32] More than for ⟦jazer|Yazer|Jazer⟧ I weep for you,
O vine of ⟦sibmah|Sivmah|Sibmah⟧!
Your branches passed over the sea,
reached to the Sea of ⟦jazer|Yazer|Jazer⟧;
on your summer fruits and your grapes
the destroyer has fallen.

[33] Gladness and joy have been taken away
from the fruitful land of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;
I have made the wine cease from the winepresses;
no one treads them with shouts of joy;
the shouting is not the shout of joy.

[34] From the outcry at ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ even to ⟦elealeh|Elaleh|Elealeh⟧,
as far as ⟦jahaz|Yahatz|Jahaz⟧ they utter their voice,
from ⟦zoar|Tzoar|Zoar⟧ to ⟦horonaim|Choronayim|Horonaim⟧ and ⟦eglath-shelishiyah|Eglat Shelishiyah|Eglath-shelishiyah⟧.
For the waters of Nimrim also have become desolate.

[35] And I will bring to an end in ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
him who offers sacrifice in the high place
and makes offerings to his god.

[36] Therefore my heart moans for ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ like flutes,
and my heart moans like flutes for the men of ⟦kir-heres|Qir Cheres|Kir-hareseth⟧;
therefore the riches they gained have perished.

[37] For every head is shaved
and every beard cut off.
On all the hands are gashes,
and around the waist is sackcloth.

[38] On all the housetops of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧
and in the squares there is nothing but lamentation,
for I have broken ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ like a vessel for which no one cares,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[39] How it is broken! How they wail!
How ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has turned his back in shame!
So ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ has become a derision and a horror
to all that are around him.

[40] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Behold, one shall fly swiftly like an eagle
and spread his wings against ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧;

[41] the cities shall be taken
and the strongholds seized.
The heart of the warriors of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be in that day
like the heart of a woman in her birth pangs.

[42] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ shall be destroyed as a people,
because he magnified himself against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[43] Terror, pit, and snare
are before you, O inhabitant of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧!
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[44] He who flees from the terror
shall fall into the pit,
and he who climbs out of the pit
shall be caught in the snare.
For I will bring these things upon ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
the year of their punishment,
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[45] In the shadow of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧
fugitives stop without strength,
for fire came out from ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧,
flame from the house of ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧;
it has destroyed the forehead of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧,
the crown of the sons of tumult.

[46] Woe to you, O ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧!
The people of ⟦chemosh|Kemosh|Chemosh⟧ are undone,
for your sons have been taken captive,
and your daughters into captivity.

[47] Yet I will restore the fortunes of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧
in the latter days, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

Thus far is the judgment on ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v15) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Holon" → "Cholon"** (v21) — Hebrew חֹלוֹן; the other Moabite city names (Nebo, Madmen, Dibon, Jahaz, Mephaath, Beth-Diblathaim) kept as in KJV.
- **Poetic structure** — entire chapter formatted as verse oracle against Moab; prose city list vv21-24 preserved within verse context.

### Notes
- **Kemosh** — chief Moabite god.
- **v47** — even Moab has a future hope under Yahweh.
