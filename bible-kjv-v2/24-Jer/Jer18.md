# Jeremiah 18

[1] The word that came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying:

[2] "Arise, and go down to the house of the potter, and there I will make you hear my words."

[3] And I went down to the house of the potter, and behold, he was doing work on the two stones.

[4] And the vessel that he was making was ruined, with the clay, in the hand of the potter; and he made it again into another vessel, as it was right in the eyes of the potter to make.

[5] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to me, saying:

[6] "Like this potter, am I not able to do to you, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. Behold, like the clay in the hand of the potter, so are you in my hand, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] At a moment I speak concerning a nation and concerning a kingdom, to pluck up and to break down and to make perish;

[8] and that nation turns back from its evil, concerning which I spoke against it; and I relent concerning the evil that I meant to do to it.

[9] And at a moment I speak concerning a nation and concerning a kingdom, to build and to plant;

[10] and it does the evil in my eyes, so as not to listen to my voice; and I relent concerning the good with which I said I would do good to it.

[11] And now, say, please, to the man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying: 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am forming evil against you and devising a design against you. Turn back, please, each man from his evil way, and make good your ways and your doings.'

[12] And they will say, 'It is hopeless! For after our designs we will walk, and each man the stubbornness of his evil heart we will do.'"

[13] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Ask, please, among the nations,
who has heard the like of these?
A very horrible thing she has done,
the virgin of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[14] Does the snow of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ leave
the ⟦rock-of-the-field|rock of the field|crags of Sirion⟧?
Or are the strange waters plucked up,
the cold flowing ones?

[15] For my people have forgotten me;
to emptiness they make smoke;
and they have made them stumble in their ways,
the paths of old,
to walk in tracks,
a way not built up,

[16] to make their land a thing that appalls,
everlasting hissings;
everyone who passes by it shall be appalled
and shake his head.

[17] Like an east wind I will scatter them
before the enemy;
the neck and not the face I will see of them
in the day of their calamity."

[18] And they said, "Come, and let us devise designs against ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧, for ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ shall not perish from the priest, and counsel from the wise, and word from the prophet. Come, and let us strike him with the tongue, and let us not give heed to any of his words."

[19] Give heed, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, to me,
and hear the voice of those who contend with me.

[20] Shall evil be repaid for good?
For they have dug a pit for my soul.
Remember my standing before you
to speak good concerning them,
to turn back your fury from them.

[21] Therefore give their sons to the famine,
and pour them out on the hands of the sword;
and let their wives be bereaved and widows,
and let their men be killed by death,
their young men struck by the sword in the battle.

[22] Let an outcry be heard from their houses,
when you bring a raiding band upon them suddenly;
for they have dug a pit to capture me,
and traps they have hidden for my feet.

[23] And you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you know
all their counsel against me for death.
Do not make atonement for their iniquity,
and their sin from before you do not wipe out;
and let them be made to stumble before you;
in the time of your anger deal with them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** — vv1–12 and v18 prose; vv13–17 and 19–23 poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the existing breaks inside a line are kept where the accents mark a pause (v13, 15, 17, 23).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept (v3–4, 8, 10, 15, 18, 21, 23); the "if … then" of the current text is not written (v7–10).
- **"saying"** (v1, 5, 11) — לֵאמֹר kept. **"Then the word of the LORD came to me" → "And the word of Yahweh came to me, saying"** (v5) — the prophets' formula.
- **"the potter's house" → "the house of the potter"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew בֵּית הַיּוֹצֵר. יוֹצֵר "potter" is the participle of יָצַר "form", "the one who forms" (as Isa 29:16; 64:8): v11 has the same word, "I am forming". **"I will cause thee to hear" → "I will make you hear"** — hifil.
- **"Then I went down … and, behold, he wrought a work on the wheels" → "And I went down … and behold, he was doing work on the two stones"** (v3) — the qere וְהִנֵּה־הוּא "and behold, he" (ketiv והנהו, the same sense); עֹשֶׂה מְלָאכָה "doing work"; הָאָבְנָיִם, a dual, "the two stones" (the potter's wheel, upper and lower: so Exod 1:16 of the birth-stool).
- **"was marred in the hand of the potter: so he made it again" → "was ruined, with the clay, in the hand of the potter; and he made it again"** (v4) — Hebrew וְנִשְׁחַת "was ruined" (שָׁחַת nifal); בַּחֹמֶר "with / in the clay" (some manuscripts כַּחֹמֶר "as clay", from memory: the MT is kept); חֹמֶר "clay" here (the potter's clay; "mortar" of bricks is the same word). וְשָׁב וַיַּעֲשֵׂהוּ, "and he returned and made it" = made it again (the שׁוּב row). **"as seemed good to the potter" → "as it was right in the eyes of the potter"** — יָשַׁר בְּעֵינֵי (reviewer row).
- **"O house of Israel, cannot I do with you as this potter?" → "Like this potter, am I not able to do to you, house of Israel?"** (v6) — the Hebrew order; no "O". יָכֹל "be able".
- **"At what instant I shall speak … to pull down, and to destroy it" → "At a moment I speak … to pluck up and to break down and to make perish"** (v7, 9) — רֶגַע "a moment" (v9 "and at a moment"); נָתַשׁ "pluck up", נָתַץ "break down" (reviewer row), הֶאֱבִיד "make perish"; "it" not written.
- **"If that nation … turn from their evil, I will repent of the evil" → "and that nation turns back from its evil … and I relent concerning the evil"** (v8) — שׁוּב "turn back"; נִחַם עַל הָרָעָה "relent concerning the evil" (its row: רָעָה the evil planned; the same word as "its evil"). **"that I thought to do" → "that I meant to do"** — חָשַׁב (the "mean" row). **"against whom I have pronounced" → "concerning which I spoke against it"** — אֲשֶׁר דִּבַּרְתִּי עָלָיו.
- **"If it do evil in my sight, that it obey not my voice" → "and it does the evil in my eyes, so as not to listen to my voice"** (v10) — the qere הָרַע "the evil" (ketiv הרעה, the same sense); "in my eyes"; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice". **"wherewith I said I would benefit them" → "with which I said I would do good to it"** — הֵיטִיב "do good to", singular "it" (the nation).
- **"to the men of Judah" → "to the man of Judah"** (v11) — singular collective אִישׁ; עַל before "the inhabitants" ("to"). **"I frame evil" → "I am forming evil"** — יוֹצֵר, the word of "the potter" (v2–6). **"devise a device" → "devising a design"** — חֹשֵׁב … מַחֲשָׁבָה (the חָשַׁב מַחֲשָׁבֹת row "devise designs"; also v12, 18). **"return ye now" → "Turn back, please"** — נָא. **"make your ways and your doings good" → "make good your ways and your doings"** — הֵיטִיבוּ; מַעֲלָלִים "doings".
- **"And they said, There is no hope" → "And they will say, It is hopeless!"** (v12) — וְאָמְרוּ, a perfect with "and" after the commands: what they will answer (KJV took it as past). נוֹאָשׁ, literally "it is despaired of". **"we will every one do the imagination of his evil heart" → "each man the stubbornness of his evil heart we will do"** — שְׁרִרוּת לֵב "the stubbornness of the heart"; the Hebrew order.
- **"Ask ye now among the heathen" → "Ask, please, among the nations"** (v13) — נָא; גּוֹיִם "nations". **"such things" → "the like of these"** — כָּאֵלֶּה. **"the virgin of Israel hath done a very horrible thing" → "A very horrible thing she has done, the virgin of Israel"** — the Hebrew order; שַׁעֲרֻרִת "horrible thing" (rare); בְּתוּלַת יִשְׂרָאֵל "the virgin of Israel" (the genitive may also name Israel herself: "virgin Israel").
- **"Will a man leave the snow of Lebanon … ? or shall the cold flowing waters … be forsaken?" → "Does the snow of Lebanon leave the rock of the field? Or are the strange waters plucked up, the cold flowing ones?"** (v14) — Hebrew הֲיַעֲזֹב מִצּוּר שָׂדַי שֶׁלֶג לְבָנוֹן: the snow is the subject; צוּר שָׂדַי, the MT kept ("crags of Sirion" is an emendation); יִנָּתְשׁוּ "are plucked up" (נָתַשׁ, as v7; the emendation "dry up", יִנָּשְׁתוּ, is common: from memory); מַיִם זָרִים "strange waters" (זָר "stranger"; "foreign"); נוֹזְלִים "flowing ones" ("ones" supplied).
- **"they have burned incense to vanity" → "to emptiness they make smoke"** (v15) — יְקַטֵּרוּ, piel without an object (the "make smoke" row); לַשָּׁוְא "to emptiness" (the שָׁוְא row). **"they have caused them to stumble" → "they have made them stumble"** — הִכְשִׁיל, the subject not named. **"from the ancient paths" → "the paths of old"** — שְׁבִילֵי עוֹלָם, literally "paths of the age" (time long past; "everlasting" of the עוֹלָם genitive row would say the reverse here); there is no "from". **"to walk in paths, in a way not cast up" → "to walk in tracks, a way not built up"** — נְתִיבוֹת "tracks" (the נְתִיבָה row); סָלַל "build up" (a road).
- **"desolate, and a perpetual hissing" → "a thing that appalls, everlasting hissings"** (v16) — שַׁמָּה "a thing that appalls"; the qere שְׁרִיקוֹת, plural (ketiv שרוקת); עוֹלָם as a genitive "everlasting" (lit. "of the age"). **"shall be astonished, and wag his head" → "shall be appalled and shake his head"** — יִשֹּׁם (root of שַׁמָּה); הֵנִיד בְּרֹאשׁ "shake with his head".
- **"I will shew them the back, and not the face" → "the neck and not the face I will see of them"** (v17) — Hebrew עֹרֶף "neck" (the back of the neck); אֶרְאֵם is pointed as qal, "I will see them" (they flee, I see their necks); read as hifil אַרְאֵם it is "I will show them" (KJV, the versions, from memory). The MT pointing is kept. **"as with an east wind" → "Like an east wind"** — כְּרוּחַ־קָדִים. **"calamity"** — אֵיד.
- **"Come, and let us devise devices" → "Come, and let us devise designs"** (v18) — וְ kept; "designs" as v11. **"the law shall not perish … nor counsel … nor the word" → "torah shall not perish … and counsel … and word"** — no articles in the Hebrew; each with "and". **"let us smite him with the tongue"** — הִכָּה "strike". **"let us not give heed"** — הִקְשִׁיב (also v19).
- **"Give heed to me, O LORD, and hearken to the voice of them that contend with me" → "Give heed, Yahweh, to me, and hear the voice of those who contend with me"** (v19) — the Hebrew order; no "O"; שָׁמַע לְקוֹל; יְרִיבַי "my contenders" (root רִיב "contend").
- **"Shall evil be recompensed for good?"** (v20) — Hebrew יְשֻׁלַּם (pual of שִׁלֵּם "repay"); תַּחַת "for". **"for they have digged a pit for my soul" → "For they have dug a pit for my soul"** — כָּרָה "dig" (the second verb for digging: note); שׁוּחָה "pit" (another word than בּוֹר and שַׁחַת: English has one); נֶפֶשׁ "soul". **"Remember that I stood before thee to speak good for them" → "Remember my standing before you to speak good concerning them"** — עָמְדִי an infinitive; דִּבֶּר טוֹב עַל "speak good concerning". **"wrath" → "fury"** — חֵמָה; הֵשִׁיב "turn back".
- **"deliver up their children to the famine" → "give their sons to the famine"** (v21) — נָתַן "give"; בֵּן "son". **"pour out their blood by the force of the sword" → "pour them out on the hands of the sword"** — הַגִּרֵם "pour them out" ("blood" is not written); עַל־יְדֵי "on the hands of". **"bereaved of their children" → "bereaved"** — שַׁכֻּלוֹת: the word itself says it (the שָׁכֹל row). **"let their men be put to death" → "let their men be killed by death"** — הֲרֻגֵי מָוֶת, literally "the killed of death" (death as a plague: Jer 15:2). **"young men"** — בַּחוּרִים. **"slain by the sword" → "struck by the sword"** — מֻכֵּי־חֶרֶב (הִכָּה "strike").
- **"Let a cry be heard" → "Let an outcry be heard"** (v22) — זְעָקָה. **"a troop" → "a raiding band"** — גְּדוּד (reviewer row). **"to take me" → "to capture me"** — לָכַד; the qere שׁוּחָה "pit" (ketiv שיחה). **"hid snares" → "traps they have hidden"** — פַּח "trap"; טָמַן "hide"; the Hebrew order.
- **"Yet, LORD, thou knowest all their counsel against me to slay me" → "And you, Yahweh, you know all their counsel against me for death"** (v23) — וְאַתָּה; עֵצָה "counsel"; לַמָּוֶת "for death". **"forgive not" → "Do not make atonement for"** — כִּפֶּר עַל. **"neither blot out their sin from thy sight" → "and their sin from before you do not wipe out"** — the Hebrew order; מָחָה "wipe out" (as Ps 51:1, 9); תֶּמְחִי an unusual form (as if feminine). **"let them be overthrown" → "let them be made to stumble"** — the qere וְיִהְיוּ (ketiv והיו); מֻכְשָׁלִים, hofal of כָּשַׁל (as v15). **"deal thus with them" → "deal with them"** — עֲשֵׂה בָהֶם.

### Notes
- **vv1–12** — the potter: יוֹצֵר "the one who forms" (v2–6) and Yahweh "forming evil" (v11) are one word.
