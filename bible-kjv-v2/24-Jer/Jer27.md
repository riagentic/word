## Changes

- **"Jehoiakim" kept** (v1) — MT reads יְהוֹיָקִים *Yehoyaqim*; the context (vv3, 12; 28:1 "in that same year … Zedekiah") points to Zedekiah, as a few Hebrew mss, the Syriac and Arabic read (LXX lacks the verse) — that is the traditional reading (ESV) only.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv4, 18-19, 21) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 21) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; sign-act of the yoke and oracle to foreign kings and Zedekiah.
