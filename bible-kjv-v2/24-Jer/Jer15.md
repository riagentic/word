# Jeremiah 15

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Though ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ should stand before me, my soul would not be toward this people. Send them away from before my face, and let them go out.

[2] And it shall be, when they say to you, 'Where shall we go out?' then you shall say to them, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"Those who are for death, to death,
and those who are for the sword, to the sword,
and those who are for the famine, to the famine,
and those who are for the captivity, to the captivity."'

[3] And I will appoint over them four kinds, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: the sword to kill, and the dogs to drag away, and the birds of the heavens and the beasts of the earth to devour and to ruin.

[4] And I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth, because of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ son of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, for what he did in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[5] For who will have pity on you, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
and who will show grief for you,
and who will turn aside to ask about your ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧?

[6] You have abandoned me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
backward you go;
and I stretched out my hand against you and ruined you;
I am weary of relenting.

[7] And I winnowed them with a winnowing fork
in the gates of the land;
I have bereaved, I have destroyed my people;
from their ways they have not turned back.

[8] His widows have become more to me
than the sand of the seas;
I have brought to them, against the mother of a young man,
a waster at noonday;
I have made fall upon her suddenly
anguish and terrors.

[9] She who bore seven has languished;
she has breathed out her soul;
her sun has gone down while it was yet day;
she has been ashamed and abashed.
And the remnant of them I will give to the sword
before their enemies, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[10] Woe to me, my mother, that you have borne me,
a man of contention and a man of strife to all the land!
I have not lent, and they have not lent to me;
all of them curse me.

[11] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said, "Surely I have set you free for good; surely I have made the enemy come upon you in a time of evil and in a time of distress.

[12] Does iron shatter, iron from the north, and bronze?

[13] Your wealth and your treasures I will give for plunder, not for a price, and for all your sins, and in all your borders.

[14] And I will make your enemies pass over into a land that you have not known; for a fire has been kindled in my anger; upon you it shall burn."

[15] You know, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
remember me and visit me,
and take vengeance for me on my pursuers.
Do not, in the length of your anger, take me away;
know that for your sake I have borne reproach.

[16] Your words were found, and I ate them,
and your word became to me a joy
and the gladness of my heart,
for your name is called upon me,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts.

[17] I did not sit in the secret counsel of those who make merry, nor did I exult;
because of your hand I sat alone,
for with indignation you have filled me.

[18] Why has my pain been forever,
and my blow incurable, refusing to be healed?
You are surely to me like a deceit,
waters that are not sure.

[19] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"If you return, then I will bring you back;
before me you shall stand.
And if you bring out what is precious from what is worthless,
as my mouth you shall be.
They shall return to you,
but you shall not return to them.

[20] And I will make you to this people
a fortified wall of bronze;
and they shall fight against you,
but they shall not overcome you,
for I am with you
to save you and to deliver you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[21] And I will deliver you from the hand of the evil,
and ransom you from the palm of the ruthless."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v16) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — the four lots of v2 and vv5–10, 15–21 set as verse; the prose of vv1–4, 11–14 stays prose.
- **Vocative** (vv5, 15, 16) — no "O" before the name: the Hebrew has none.
- **"Then the LORD said unto me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before me, yet my mind could not be toward this people" → "And Yahweh said to me, Though Moses and Samuel should stand before me, my soul would not be toward this people"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר "And … said"; אִם־יַעֲמֹד "if … should stand" (a supposition: both were men who stood before Yahweh for the people, Exod 32:11–14; 1 Sam 7:9; 12:23); the names keep their tokens; אֵין נַפְשִׁי אֶל "my soul is not toward" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul", row; not "heart", "mind"). **"cast them out of my sight, and let them go forth" → "Send them away from before my face, and let them go out"** — שַׁלַּח מֵעַל פָּנַי, שִׁלַּח "send away" (row), מֵעַל פָּנַי literally "from upon my face".
- **"And it shall come to pass, if they say unto thee, Whither shall we go forth?" → "And it shall be, when they say to you, 'Where shall we go out?'"** (v2) — Hebrew וְהָיָה כִּי (row); אָנָה נֵצֵא, the same verb as v1 "go out". **"Such as are for death, to death" → "Those who are for death, to death"** — לַמָּוֶת "for death" (the earlier text had "pestilence", which is דֶּבֶר and is not in this verse); לַשְּׁבִי "for the captivity" (שְׁבִי, row); the article on each of the four, as printed.
- **"I will appoint over them four kinds"** (v3) — Hebrew פָּקַדְתִּי עֲלֵיהֶם "I will appoint over them" (פָּקַד עַל, row); אַרְבַּע מִשְׁפָּחוֹת, literally "four clans": "kinds" in the verse (the "clan" row would say a kin group of men; the sword and the beasts are meant). **"the dogs to tear" → "the dogs to drag away"** — סָחַב "drag". **"the fowls of the heaven" → "the birds of the heavens"** — עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם (row); בֶּהֱמַת הָאָרֶץ "the beasts of the earth" (row). **"to devour and destroy" → "to devour and to ruin"** — לְהַשְׁחִית (שָׁחַת hifil "ruin", row; also v6).
- **"And I will cause them to be removed into all kingdoms of the earth" → "And I will make them a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth"** (v4) — qere לְזַעֲוָה "a horror" (row; the ketiv לזועה is another spelling of the same word). **"because of Manasseh … for that which he did in Jerusalem" → "because of Manasseh … for what he did in Jerusalem"** — בִּגְלַל "because of" (row); עַל אֲשֶׁר־עָשָׂה "for what he did".
- **"For who shall have pity upon thee, O Jerusalem?" → "For who will have pity on you, Jerusalem"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי "For" (it was dropped); חָמַל "have pity" (row). **"or who shall bemoan thee" → "and who will show grief for you"** — נוּד לְ "show grief" (literally "sway for", row). **"to ask how thou doest" → "to ask about your shalom"** — שָׁאַל לְשָׁלוֹם (row; token).
- **"Thou hast forsaken me" → "You have abandoned me"** (v6) — Hebrew נָטַשְׁתְּ (נָטַשׁ "abandon", row; עָזַב is "forsake"). **"thou art gone backward" → "backward you go"** — אָחוֹר תֵּלֵכִי, a yiqtol. **"therefore will I stretch out my hand against thee, and destroy thee" → "and I stretched out my hand against you and ruined you"** — וָאַט … וָאַשְׁחִיתֵךְ, wayyiqtol forms: told as done (also v7 "And I winnowed"); נָטָה יָד "stretch out the hand" (row). **"I am weary with repenting" → "I am weary of relenting"** — נִלְאֵיתִי הִנָּחֵם; נִחַם nifal "relent" (row; not "repent").
- **"And I will fan them with a fan" → "And I winnowed them with a winnowing fork"** (v7) — Hebrew וָאֶזְרֵם, wayyiqtol; זָרָה "winnow" (of grain, row); מִזְרֶה "winnowing fork". **"I will bereave them of children, I will destroy my people" → "I have bereaved, I have destroyed my people"** — שִׁכַּלְתִּי אִבַּדְתִּי אֶת־עַמִּי, two perfects, one object (שִׁכֵּל "bereave", row: "of children" is in the verb, not added). **"since they return not from their ways" → "from their ways they have not turned back"** — the Hebrew order; שׁוּב "turn back" (row).
- **"Their widows are increased to me above the sand of the seas" → "His widows have become more to me than the sand of the seas"** (v8) — Hebrew עָצְמוּ "have become mighty (many)" (עָצַם, row); אַלְמְנֹתָו "his widows" (of "my people", v7; the earlier text had "their"). **"I have brought upon them against the mother of the young men a spoiler at noonday" → "I have brought to them, against the mother of a young man, a waster at noonday"** — הֵבֵאתִי לָהֶם עַל־אֵם בָּחוּר; אֵם בָּחוּר "the mother of a young man" (or "mother and young man": the words stand side by side; בָּחוּר "young man", row); שֹׁדֵד "a waster" (participle of שָׁדַד "lay waste", row; as Isa 21:2). **"I have caused him to fall upon it suddenly, and terrors upon the city" → "I have made fall upon her suddenly anguish and terrors"** — הִפַּלְתִּי עָלֶיהָ פִּתְאֹם עִיר וּבֶהָלוֹת; עִיר is taken as "anguish" (agitation; a word like this is not found elsewhere: uncertain); the reading "city", as KJV, makes no sentence with the verb; בֶּהָלוֹת "terrors"; עָלֶיהָ "upon her" (the mother).
- **"She that hath borne seven languisheth" → "She who bore seven has languished"** (v9) — Hebrew אֻמְלְלָה (row). **"she hath given up the ghost" → "she has breathed out her soul"** — נָפְחָה נַפְשָׁהּ (נָפַח "breathe / blow", row; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"). **"her sun is gone down while it was yet day"** — qere בָּא (masculine, as שֶׁמֶשׁ often is), ketiv באה (feminine): the same meaning. **"she hath been ashamed and confounded" → "she has been ashamed and abashed"** — בּוֹשָׁה וְחָפֵרָה (בּוֹשׁ, row; חָפֵר "be abashed", row). **"the residue of them" → "the remnant of them"** — שְׁאֵרִית (row).
- **"Woe is me" → "Woe to me"** (v10) — Hebrew אוֹי־לִי (row). **"a man of strife and a man of contention to the whole earth" → "a man of contention and a man of strife to all the land"** — רִיב "contention" (row), מָדוֹן "strife" (row); כָּל־הָאָרֶץ "all the land" (or "the whole earth": the one word, row). **"I have neither lent on usury, nor men have lent to me on usury" → "I have not lent, and they have not lent to me"** — נָשִׁיתִי … נָשׁוּ־בִי, נָשָׁה בְּ "lend to" (row; no "usury": the word names the loan). **"yet every one of them doth curse me" → "all of them curse me"** — כֻּלֹּה מְקַלְלַוְנִי, an unusual form (literally "all of him is cursing me"); קִלֵּל "curse" (literally "make light of", row).
- **"Verily it shall be well with thy remnant; verily I will cause the enemy to entreat thee well in the time of evil" → "Surely I have set you free for good; surely I have made the enemy come upon you in a time of evil"** (v11) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא … אִם־לֹא, the oath form "if not … if not" = "surely" (Standing decisions); qere שֵׁרִיתִיךָ "I have set you free" (taken from שָׁרָה "loose"; uncertain: "strengthened you" is also given); the ketiv שרותך is read by KJV as "your remnant"; הִפְגַּעְתִּי בְךָ אֶת־הָאֹיֵב "I have made the enemy come upon you" (הִפְגִּיעַ with an object "make come upon", row, as Isa 53:6; the hifil of פָּגַע בְּ "entreat" also allows "I have made the enemy entreat you", as Jer 21:1–2; 37:3 tell: the reading is not settled; the earlier text "pleaded for you before the enemy" said neither); רָעָה "evil" (row), צָרָה "distress" (row). The speaker of vv11–14 is Yahweh; whom vv13–14 address (the prophet or the people) is not said.
- **"Shall iron break the northern iron and the steel?" → "Does iron shatter, iron from the north, and bronze?"** (v12) — Hebrew הֲיָרֹעַ בַּרְזֶל בַּרְזֶל מִצָּפוֹן וּנְחֹשֶׁת, word for word; whether the first "iron" is the subject ("can iron shatter iron from the north") or the verb is impersonal ("can one shatter iron") is not marked; נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze" (row; not "steel").
- **"Thy substance and thy treasures will I give to the spoil without price, and that for all thy sins, even in all thy borders" → "Your wealth and your treasures I will give for plunder, not for a price, and for all your sins, and in all your borders"** (v13) — Hebrew חַיִל "wealth" (of goods, row); אוֹצְרוֹת "treasures" (the אוֹצָר "treasury" row; here what is stored); לָבַז "for plunder" (בַּז, row); לֹא בִמְחִיר "not for a price" (מְחִיר, row); each "and" as printed; גְּבוּל "border" (row: the land within it).
- **"I will make thee to pass with thine enemies into a land which thou knowest not" → "I will make your enemies pass over into a land that you have not known"** (v14) — MT וְהַעֲבַרְתִּי אֶת־אֹיְבֶיךָ, literally "I will make your enemies pass over" (הֶעֱבִיר, row); some Hebrew manuscripts and the parallel Jer 17:4 read וְהַעֲבַדְתִּיךָ "and I will make you serve (your enemies)", which the earlier text followed (from memory): the MT is kept; לֹא יָדָעְתָּ, a perfect. **"for a fire is kindled in mine anger, which shall burn upon you" → "for a fire has been kindled in my anger; upon you it shall burn"** — קָדְחָה "has been kindled" (קָדַח, row); בְאַפִּי "in my anger" (אַף, Standing decisions); עֲלֵיכֶם "upon you" (plural); the earlier "forever" is from Jer 17:4 עַד־עוֹלָם, which is not in this verse.
- **"O LORD, thou knowest" → "You know, Yahweh"** (v15) — Hebrew אַתָּה יָדַעְתָּ, the pronoun first. **"revenge me of my persecutors" → "take vengeance for me on my pursuers"** — וְהִנָּקֶם לִי מֵרֹדְפַי (נָקַם, row; רָדַף "pursue", row). **"take me not away in thy longsuffering" → "Do not, in the length of your anger, take me away"** — אַל־לְאֶרֶךְ אַפְּךָ תִּקָּחֵנִי, literally "do not, according to the length of your anger (= your slowness to anger), take me" (אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם "long of anger" is Yahweh's patience, Exod 34:6). **"know that for thy sake I have suffered rebuke" → "know that for your sake I have borne reproach"** — דַּע שְׂאֵתִי עָלֶיךָ חֶרְפָּה, literally "know: my bearing reproach on your account" (חֶרְפָּה "reproach", row).
- **"and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart" → "and your word became to me a joy and the gladness of my heart"** (v16) — qere דְבָרְךָ "your word" (singular; the ketiv דבריך "your words", as at the start of the verse); וַיְהִי "became"; שָׂשׂוֹן "joy", שִׂמְחָה "gladness" (row). **"for I am called by thy name, O LORD God of hosts" → "for your name is called upon me, Yahweh, Elohim of Hosts"** — כִּי־נִקְרָא שִׁמְךָ עָלַי, as Jer 14:9; יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת, both names (tokens) and "of Hosts" plain (the prophets' formula row).
- **"I sat not in the assembly of the mockers, nor rejoiced" → "I did not sit in the secret counsel of those who make merry, nor did I exult"** (v17) — Hebrew סוֹד "secret counsel" (the close circle, row); מְשַׂחֲקִים "those who make merry" (of שִׂחֵק "play", row; not "mockers"); וָאֶעְלֹז "nor did I exult" (עָלַז, row). **"I sat alone because of thy hand"** — מִפְּנֵי יָדְךָ "because of your hand" (מִפְּנֵי, row). **"for thou hast filled me with indignation" → "for with indignation you have filled me"** — the Hebrew order; זַעַם "indignation" (row).
- **"Why is my pain perpetual" → "Why has my pain been forever"** (v18) — Hebrew לָמָּה הָיָה כְאֵבִי נֶצַח; נֶצַח "forever" (row); כְּאֵב "pain" (of כָּאַב, as the מַכְאֹב row). **"and my wound incurable, which refuseth to be healed" → "and my blow incurable, refusing to be healed"** — מַכָּה "blow" (row); אֲנוּשָׁה "incurable"; מֵאֲנָה "refuses" (מֵאֵן, row). **"wilt thou be altogether unto me as a liar, and as waters that fail?" → "You are surely to me like a deceit, waters that are not sure"** — הָיוֹ תִהְיֶה לִי, literally "being you are to me" (infinitive absolute: "surely"); the Hebrew has no question word: kept as a statement (other versions make it a question); אַכְזָב "a deceit" (a deceptive thing) (a stream that fails in summer is meant: "brook" is not written); מַיִם לֹא נֶאֱמָנוּ "waters that are not sure" (נֶאֱמָן "sure", row; no "and as").
- **"If thou return, then will I bring thee again, and thou shalt stand before me" → "If you return, then I will bring you back; before me you shall stand"** (v19) — Hebrew תָּשׁוּב … וַאֲשִׁיבְךָ, one verb שׁוּב "return / bring back" (row), four times in the verse; the Hebrew order. **"if thou take forth the precious from the vile, thou shalt be as my mouth" → "if you bring out what is precious from what is worthless, as my mouth you shall be"** — תּוֹצִיא "bring out"; יָקָר "precious" (row); זוֹלֵל "worthless" (one who squanders; the "glutton" of its row is another sense); the Hebrew order. **"let them return unto thee; but return not thou unto them" → "They shall return to you, but you shall not return to them"** — יָשֻׁבוּ … לֹא־תָשׁוּב, yiqtol forms: what will be.
- **"a fenced brasen wall" → "a fortified wall of bronze"** (v20) — Hebrew חוֹמַת נְחֹשֶׁת בְּצוּרָה (חוֹמָה "wall", row; בָּצוּר "fortified", row). **"they shall not prevail against thee" → "they shall not overcome you"** — לֹא־יוּכְלוּ לָךְ (יָכֹל לְ "overcome", row). **"to save thee and to deliver thee"** — הוֹשִׁיעַ "save", הִצִּיל "deliver" (row).
- **"out of the hand of the wicked" → "from the hand of the evil"** (v21) — Hebrew רָעִים "evil (ones)" (רַע, row; רְשָׁעִים "the wicked" is another word). **"I will redeem thee out of the hand of the terrible" → "ransom you from the palm of the ruthless"** — פָּדָה "ransom" (row); כַּף "palm" (row); עָרִיץ "ruthless" (row).

### Notes
- **Moses and Samuel** (v1) — both stood before Yahweh to pray for the people (Exod 32:11–14, 30–32; Num 14:13–20; 1 Sam 7:5–9; 12:19–23; Ps 99:6).
- **Manasseh** (v4) — 2 Kgs 21:1–16; 23:26; 24:3–4.
