# Jeremiah 42

[1] Then all the commanders of the forces, and ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧ and ⟦jezaniah|Yezanyahu|Jezaniah⟧ son of ⟦hoshaiah|Hoshayah|Hoshaiah⟧, and all the people from the least to the greatest, came near

[4] ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet said to them, "I have heard you. Behold, I will pray to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ according to your request, and whatever ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answers you I will tell you. I will keep nothing back from you."

[6] Whether it is good or bad, we will obey the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to whom we are sending you, that it may be well with us when we obey the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[7] At the end of ten days the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧.

[8] Then he summoned ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧ and all the commanders of the forces who were with him, and all the people from the least to the greatest,

[10] If you will remain in this land, then I will build you up and not pull you down; I will plant you, and not pluck you up; for I relent of the disaster that I did to you.

[11] Do not fear the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, of whom you are afraid. Do not fear him, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for I am with you, to save you and to deliver you from his hand.

[12] I will grant you mercy, that he may have mercy on you and let you remain in your own land.

[13] "But if you say, 'We will not remain in this land,' disobeying the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧

[18] "For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: As my anger and my wrath were poured out on the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, so my wrath will be poured out on you when you go to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧. You shall become an execration, a horror, a curse, and a taunt. You shall see this place no more.

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has said to you, O remnant of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, 'Do not go to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.' Know for a certainty that I have warned you this day

[22] Now therefore know for a certainty that you shall die by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence in the place where you desire to go to live."

## Changes

- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv15, 18) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת preserved.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2-6, 9, 13, 15, 18-22) — Hebrew *Elohim* preserved throughout.
- **Poetic structure** — entirely prose; the remnant's inquiry and Jeremiah's oracle against going to Egypt.
