# Colossians 3

[1] If then you have been raised with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, seek the things that are above, where ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is, seated at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] Set your minds on things that are above, not on things that are on earth.

[3] For you have died, and your life is hidden with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] When ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ who is your life appears, then you also will appear with him in glory.

[5] Put to death therefore what is earthly in you: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry.

[6] On account of these the wrath of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is coming.

[7] In these you too once walked, when you were living in them.

[8] But now you must put them all away: anger, wrath, malice, slander, and obscene talk from your mouth.

[9] Do not lie to one another, seeing that you have put off the old self with its practices

[10] and have put on the new self, which is being renewed in knowledge after the image of its creator.

[11] Here there is not ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧ and ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, circumcised and uncircumcised, barbarian, ⟦scythian|Skythes|Scythian⟧, slave, free; but ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is all, and in all.

[12] Put on then, as ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ chosen ones, holy and beloved, compassionate hearts, kindness, humility, meekness, and patience,

[13] bearing with one another and, if one has a complaint against another, forgiving each other; as the Lord has forgiven you, so you also must forgive.

[14] And above all these put on **love, which binds everything together in perfect harmony**.

[15] And let the peace of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ rule in your hearts, to which indeed you were called in one body. And be thankful.

[16] Let the word of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ dwell in you richly, teaching and admonishing one another in all wisdom, singing psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, with thankfulness in your hearts to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[17] **And whatever you do, in word or deed, do everything in the name of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, giving thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father through him.**

[18] Wives, submit to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.

[19] Husbands, love your wives, and do not be harsh with them.

[20] Children, obey your parents in everything, for this pleases the Lord.

[21] Fathers, do not provoke your children, lest they become discouraged.

[22] Bondservants, obey in everything those who are your earthly masters, not by way of eye-service, as people-pleasers, but with sincerity of heart, fearing the Lord.

[23] Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men,

[24] knowing that from the Lord you will receive the inheritance as your reward. You are serving the Lord ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[25] For the wrongdoer will be paid back for the wrong he has done, and there is no partiality.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 3, 6, 12, 16, 17) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v17) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
