# Colossians 3

[1] If then you were raised with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, seek the things above, where ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is, sitting at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[2] set your mind on the things above, not on the things on the earth,

[3] for you died, and your life has been hidden with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[4] when ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is manifested, your life, then you also will be manifested with him in glory.

[5] Put to death therefore the members that are on the earth: sexual immorality, uncleanness, passion, evil desire, and the covetousness, which is idolatry,

[6] because of which things the wrath of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is coming upon the sons of disobedience;

[7] in which you also once walked, when you were living in these things;

[8] but now you also, put away all of it: wrath, rage, malice, blasphemy, shameful speech out of your mouth;

[9] do not lie to one another, having put off the old man with his practices

[10] and having put on the new, who is being renewed unto full knowledge according to the image of the one who created him,

[11] where there is no ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧ and ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian, ⟦scythian|Skythes|Scythian⟧, slave, free, but ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is all things and in all.

[12] Put on therefore, as chosen ones of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, holy and beloved, inward parts of compassion, kindness, humility, meekness, patience,

[13] bearing with one another and forgiving each other, if anyone has a complaint against anyone; just as also the Lord forgave you, so also you;

[14] and over all these, **love, which is the bond of perfection**.

[15] And let the peace of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ arbitrate in your hearts, unto which also you were called in one body; and become thankful.

[16] Let the word of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ dwell in you richly, in all wisdom, teaching and admonishing each other, with psalms, hymns, spiritual songs, in the grace, singing in your hearts to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[17] **and everything, whatever you do in word or in deed, all in the name of the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, giving thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father through him.**

[18] Wives, be subject to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.

[19] Husbands, love your wives and do not be embittered against them.

[20] Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is well-pleasing in the Lord.

[21] Fathers, do not provoke your children, that they may not lose heart.

[22] Slaves, obey in all things your lords according to the flesh, not in eye-services, as men-pleasers, but in singleness of heart, fearing the Lord.

[23] Whatever you do, work from the soul, as to the Lord and not to men,

[24] knowing that from the Lord you will receive the recompense of the inheritance. You are slaves to the Lord ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[25] For the one who does wrong will receive back the wrong he did, and there is no partiality.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 3, 6, 12, 16, 17) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v17) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"if ye then be risen" → "if then you were raised"** (v1) — Greek συνηγέρθητε, aorist passive, as in 2:12.
- **"where Christ sitteth" → "where the *Mashiach* is, sitting"** (v1) — Greek οὗ ὁ Χριστός ἐστιν … καθήμενος: "is, sitting" or simply "is sitting".
- **"set your affection" → "set your mind"** (v2) — Greek φρονεῖτε.
- **"ye are dead" → "you died"** (v3) — Greek ἀπεθάνετε, aorist, as in 2:20; then perfect κέκρυπται, "has been hidden".
- **"when Christ, who is our life, shall appear" → "when the *Mashiach* is manifested, your life"** (v4) — Greek φανερωθῇ, the verb of 1:26; "your life" stands in apposition, without "who is"; NA28 ὑμῶν "your", KJV text "our".
- **"mortify … your members which are upon the earth" → "put to death … the members that are on the earth"** (v5) — Greek Νεκρώσατε … τὰ μέλη τὰ ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς; "your" is not in the critical text, and the Greek has no "what is earthly in you".
- **"fornication" → "sexual immorality"** (v5) — Greek πορνεία, every kind of illicit sexual intercourse.
- **"inordinate affection, evil concupiscence" → "passion, evil desire"** (v5) — Greek πάθος, ἐπιθυμίαν κακήν.
- **"on the children of disobedience" → "upon the sons of disobedience"** (v6) — Greek ἐπὶ τοὺς υἱοὺς τῆς ἀπειθείας; the words stand in RP NA28 and are omitted by WH Treg NIV. With them, "in which" (ἐν οἷς, v7) can also be read "among whom".
- **"put off all these" → "put away all of it"** (v8) — Greek ἀπόθεσθε … τὰ πάντα, an imperative; the Greek has no "must".
- **"anger, wrath" → "wrath, rage"** (v8) — Greek ὀργήν (the word of v6), θυμόν.
- **"blasphemy" kept** (v8) — Greek βλασφημίαν, abusive speech against *Elohim* or against people.
- **"filthy communication" → "shameful speech"** (v8) — Greek αἰσχρολογίαν.
- **"seeing that ye have put off" → "having put off"** (v9–10) — Greek participles ἀπεκδυσάμενοι, ἐνδυσάμενοι; the Greek does not say whether they give the reason or are part of the command.
- **"the old man" kept; "with his deeds" → "with his practices"** (v9) — Greek τὸν παλαιὸν ἄνθρωπον σὺν ταῖς πράξεσιν αὐτοῦ.
- **"renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him" → "renewed unto full knowledge according to the image of the one who created him"** (v10) — Greek εἰς ἐπίγνωσιν (as 1:9) κατʼ εἰκόνα τοῦ κτίσαντος αὐτόν.
- **"circumcision nor uncircumcision" → "circumcision and uncircumcision"** (v11) — Greek nouns περιτομὴ καὶ ἀκροβυστία.
- **"Christ is all, and in all" → "the *Mashiach* is all things and in all"** (v11) — Greek τὰ πάντα καὶ ἐν πᾶσιν Χριστός, without a verb; ἐν πᾶσιν is "in all (people)" or "in all things".
- **"bowels of mercies" → "inward parts of compassion"** (v12) — Greek σπλάγχνα οἰκτιρμοῦ; σπλάγχνα as in Phlm 7.
- **"humbleness of mind" → "humility"; "longsuffering" → "patience"** (v12) — Greek ταπεινοφροσύνην (as 2:18, 23), μακροθυμίαν (as 1:11).
- **"if any man have a quarrel" → "if anyone has a complaint"** (v13) — Greek μομφήν.
- **"even as Christ forgave you" → "just as also the Lord forgave you"** (v13) — NA28 ὁ κύριος. The Greek ends "so also you", without a verb.
- **"charity, which is the bond of perfectness" → "love, which is the bond of perfection"** (v14) — Greek τὴν ἀγάπην, ὅ ἐστιν σύνδεσμος τῆς τελειότητος; σύνδεσμος as in 2:19. The Greek has no "put on" here and no "harmony".
- **"let the peace of God rule" → "let the peace of the *Mashiach* arbitrate"** (v15) — NA28 τοῦ Χριστοῦ; βραβευέτω, to act as umpire (see 2:18).
- **"be ye thankful" → "become thankful"** (v15) — Greek γίνεσθε.
- **"in all wisdom; teaching … in psalms … singing with grace"** (v16) — the Greek has no punctuation: "in all wisdom" can go with "dwell" or with "teaching", "with psalms, hymns, spiritual songs" with "admonishing" or with "singing", "in the grace" (ἐν τῇ χάριτι, Treg NA28; the printed SBLGNT has ἐν χάριτι, "in grace", WH RP) with what precedes or with "singing". The commas leave this open. The critical text has no "and" between the three nouns.
- **"to the Lord" → "to *Elohim*"** (v16) — NA28 τῷ θεῷ.
- **"do all in the name" → "all in the name"** (v17) — the Greek has no second verb: πάντα ἐν ὀνόματι κυρίου Ἰησοῦ.
- **"to God and the Father" → "to *Elohim* the Father"** (v17) — NA28 τῷ θεῷ πατρί, without "and".
- **"submit yourselves unto your own husbands" → "be subject to your husbands"** (v18) — Greek ὑποτάσσεσθε τοῖς ἀνδράσιν; "own" is not in the critical text. γυναῖκες / ἄνδρες are "women / men" and "wives / husbands".
- **"as it is fit" → "as is fitting"** (v18) — Greek ἀνῆκεν, an imperfect ("as was fitting") used of a standing duty.
- **"be not bitter" → "do not be embittered"** (v19) — Greek πικραίνεσθε.
- **"well pleasing unto the Lord" → "well-pleasing in the Lord"** (v20) — NA28 ἐν κυρίῳ.
- **"lest they be discouraged" → "that they may not lose heart"** (v21) — Greek ἵνα μὴ ἀθυμῶσιν. "To anger" (KJV) is not in the Greek.
- **"servants" → "slaves"; "masters" → "lords"** (v22) — Greek δοῦλοι and κυρίοις, the same word as "the Lord" (κύριος) in v22–24 and 4:1.
- **"not with eyeservice" → "not in eye-services"** (v22) — Greek ἐν ὀφθαλμοδουλίαις, plural (WH Treg RP): acts of service done to be seen; ἐν ὀφθαλμοδουλίᾳ, singular (NA28).
- **"fearing God" → "fearing the Lord"** (v22) — NA28 τὸν κύριον.
- **"do it heartily" → "work from the soul"** (v23) — Greek ἐκ ψυχῆς ἐργάζεσθε.
- **"the reward of the inheritance" → "the recompense of the inheritance"** (v24) — Greek τὴν ἀνταπόδοσιν τῆς κληρονομίας, a genitive.
- **"for ye serve the Lord Christ" → "You are slaves to the Lord *Mashiach*"** (v24) — Greek τῷ κυρίῳ Χριστῷ δουλεύετε; δουλεύω is the verb of δοῦλος. The form is a statement or a command ("be slaves to …"). The critical text has no "for".
- **"shall receive for the wrong which he hath done" → "will receive back the wrong he did"** (v25) — Greek κομίσεται ὃ ἠδίκησεν.
- **"respect of persons" → "partiality"** (v25) — Greek προσωπολημψία, "accepting of the face".
