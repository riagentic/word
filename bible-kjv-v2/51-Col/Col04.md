# Colossians 4

[1] Masters, treat your bondservants justly and fairly, knowing that you also have a Master in heaven.

[2] Continue steadfastly in prayer, being watchful in it with thanksgiving.

[3] At the same time, pray also for us, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may open to us a door for the word, to declare the mystery of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, on account of which I am in prison —

[4] that I may make it clear, which is how I ought to speak.

[5] Walk in wisdom toward outsiders, making the best use of the time.

[6] Let your speech always be gracious, **seasoned with salt**, so that you may know how you ought to answer each person.

[7] ⟦tychicus|Tychikos|Tychicus⟧ will tell you all about my activities. He is a beloved brother and faithful minister and fellow servant in the Lord.

[8] I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know how we are and that he may encourage your hearts,

[9] and with him ⟦onesimus|Onesimos|Onesimus⟧, our faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will tell you of everything that has taken place here.

[10] ⟦aristarchus|Aristarchos|Aristarchus⟧ my fellow prisoner greets you, and ⟦mark|Marcus|Mark⟧ the cousin of ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ (concerning whom you have received instructions — if he comes to you, welcome him),

[11] and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ who is called Justus. These are the only men of the circumcision among my fellow workers for the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they have been a comfort to me.

[12] Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, greets you, always struggling on your behalf in his prayers, that you may stand mature and fully assured in all the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[13] For I bear him witness that he has worked hard for you and for those in ⟦laodicea|Laodikeia|Laodicea⟧ and in Hierapolis.

[14] ⟦luke|Loukas|Luke⟧ the beloved physician greets you, as does Demas.

[15] Give my greetings to the brothers at ⟦laodicea|Laodikeia|Laodicea⟧, and to Nympha and the church in her house.

[16] And when this letter has been read among you, have it also read in the church of the Laodiceans; and see that you also read the letter from ⟦laodicea|Laodikeia|Laodicea⟧.

[17] And say to ⟦archippus|Archippos|Archippus⟧, "See that you fulfill the ministry that you have received in the Lord."

[18] I, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (vv3, 12) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3, 11, 12) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v11) — "Jesus who is called Justus" = a Jewish believer named *Yeshua*; the name is preserved in Hebrew/Aramaic form.
