# Colossians 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, an apostle of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ through the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ the brother,

[2] to the saints in ⟦colossae|Kolossai|Colossae⟧ and faithful brothers in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧:

Grace to you and peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father.

[3] We give thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the Father of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, always, praying for you,

[4] having heard of your faith in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and of the love which you have toward all the saints,

[5] because of the hope laid up for you in the heavens, of which you heard before in the word of the truth of the gospel,

[6] which has come to you, just as also in all the world it is bearing fruit and growing, just as also among you, from the day you heard and came to know fully the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in truth;

[7] just as you learned from Epaphras our beloved fellow slave, who is a faithful servant of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ on our behalf,

[8] who also made clear to us your love in the Spirit.

[9] For this reason we also, from the day we heard, do not cease praying for you and asking that you may be filled with the full knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding,

[10] to walk worthily of the Lord unto all pleasing, in every good work bearing fruit and growing in the full knowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[11] being empowered with all power according to the might of his glory unto all endurance and patience with joy,

[12] giving thanks to the Father, who qualified you for the portion of the lot of the saints in the light,

[13] who rescued us out of the authority of the darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of the Son of his love,

[14] in whom we have the redemption, the forgiveness of sins;

[15] who is the image of the invisible ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
firstborn of all creation,
[16] for in him all things were created
in the heavens and on the earth, the visible and the invisible,
whether thrones or lordships or principalities or authorities;
all things have been created through him and unto him.
[17] And he is before all things,
and all things in him hold together.
[18] And he is the head of the body, the church;
who is the beginning, firstborn from the dead,
that in all things he himself might become first,
[19] for in him all the fullness was pleased to dwell
[20] and through him to reconcile all things unto him,
having made peace through the blood of his cross,
through him, whether the things on the earth or the things in the heavens.

[21] And you, being once alienated and enemies in mind, in the evil works,

[22] yet now he has reconciled in the body of his flesh through death, to present you holy and without blemish and blameless before him,

[23] if indeed you remain in the faith, founded and firm and not being moved away from the hope of the gospel which you heard, which was proclaimed in all creation under heaven, of which I, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, became a servant.

[24] Now I rejoice in the sufferings for you, and I fill up in turn what is lacking of the afflictions of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ in my flesh for his body, which is the church,

[25] of which I became a servant according to the stewardship of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that was given to me for you, to fulfill the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[26] the mystery that has been hidden from the ages and from the generations — but now it has been manifested to his saints,

[27] to whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ willed to make known what is the riches of the glory of this mystery among the nations, which is **⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ in you, the hope of the glory**;

[28] whom we proclaim, admonishing every man and teaching every man in all wisdom, that we may present every man perfect in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧;

[29] for which I also labor, struggling according to his working, which is working in me in power.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 2, 3, 6, 10, 15, 25, 27) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v3) — in "our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*."
- **"our brother" → "the brother"** (v1) — Greek ὁ ἀδελφός, no "our".
- **"to the saints and faithful brethren in Christ which are at Colosse" → "to the saints in *Kolossai* and faithful brothers in the *Mashiach*"** (v2) — Greek order τοῖς ἐν Κολοσσαῖς ἁγίοις καὶ πιστοῖς ἀδελφοῖς ἐν Χριστῷ. ἁγίοις can also be an adjective: "to the holy and faithful brothers". "And the Lord Jesus Christ" at the end is not in the critical text.
- **"praying always for you" → "always, praying for you"** (v3) — Greek πάντοτε stands between "we give thanks" and "praying" and can belong to either.
- **"since we heard" → "having heard"** (v4) — Greek participle ἀκούσαντες.
- **"in heaven" → "in the heavens"** (v5, 16, 20) — Greek plural ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς.
- **"bringeth forth fruit" → "bearing fruit and growing"** (v6) — NA28 καρποφορούμενον καὶ αὐξανόμενον; "and growing" is missing in the later text the KJV follows. The same pair in v10.
- **"fellowservant" → "fellow slave"; "minister" → "servant"** (v7, 23, 25) — Greek σύνδουλος (δοῦλος, "slave") and διάκονος ("one who serves").
- **"knew" → "came to know fully"; "knowledge" → "full knowledge"** (v6, 9, 10) — Greek ἐπέγνωτε, ἐπίγνωσις, the strengthened forms of "know" and "knowledge" (γνῶσις, 2:3).
- **"for you" → "on our behalf"** (v7) — Greek ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν (WH Treg NIV); ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν, "on your behalf" (RP NA28).
- **"in the Spirit" kept** (v8) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι has no article; it can also be read "in spirit".
- **"to desire" → "asking"** (v9) — Greek αἰτούμενοι.
- **"that ye might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing" → "to walk worthily of the Lord unto all pleasing"** (v10) — Greek infinitive περιπατῆσαι ἀξίως τοῦ κυρίου εἰς πᾶσαν ἀρεσκείαν; the Greek has no "to him".
- **"in the knowledge" → "in the full knowledge"** (v10) — Greek τῇ ἐπιγνώσει, a dative without preposition: "in" or "by the full knowledge".
- **"strengthened with all might, according to his glorious power" → "empowered with all power according to the might of his glory"** (v11) — Greek ἐν πάσῃ δυνάμει δυναμούμενοι (one root) κατὰ τὸ κράτος τῆς δόξης αὐτοῦ; "of his glory" is a noun. "With joy" can also belong to v12.
- **"made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance" → "qualified you for the portion of the lot"** (v12) — Greek τῷ ἱκανώσαντι ὑμᾶς εἰς τὴν μερίδα τοῦ κλήρου; NA28 "you" (ὑμᾶς), KJV text "us".
- **"from the power of darkness" → "out of the authority of the darkness"** (v13) — Greek ἐξουσία, the same word as "authorities" in v16.
- **"his dear Son" → "the Son of his love"** (v13) — Greek τοῦ υἱοῦ τῆς ἀγάπης αὐτοῦ.
- **"through his blood" omitted** (v14) — not in the critical text here (it stands in Eph 1:7).
- **"by him" → "in him"** (v16) — Greek ἐν αὐτῷ; "through him" (διʼ αὐτοῦ) follows at the end of the verse.
- **"dominions, or principalities, or powers" → "lordships or principalities or authorities"** (v16) — Greek κυριότητες, ἀρχαί, ἐξουσίαι.
- **"for him" → "unto him"** (v16, 20) — Greek εἰς αὐτόν. In v20 it can refer to the Son or mean "unto himself".
- **"were created" / "have been created"** (v16) — Greek aorist ἐκτίσθη, then perfect ἔκτισται.
- **"consist" → "hold together"** (v17) — Greek συνέστηκεν.
- **"that in all things he might have the preeminence" → "that in all things he himself might become first"** (v18) — Greek ἵνα γένηται ἐν πᾶσιν αὐτὸς πρωτεύων; ἐν πᾶσιν is "in all things" or "among all".
- **"it pleased the Father that in him should all fulness dwell" → "in him all the fullness was pleased to dwell"** (v19) — Greek ἐν αὐτῷ εὐδόκησεν πᾶν τὸ πλήρωμα κατοικῆσαι. The Greek has neither "the Father" nor "of *Elohim*". The subject can be "all the fullness" or an unnamed "he": "he was pleased that all the fullness should dwell in him"; the participle "having made peace" (εἰρηνοποιήσας, v20) is masculine, "fullness" (πλήρωμα) is neuter.
- **"by him" → "through him"** (v20) — Greek διʼ αὐτοῦ. The second "through him" (WH RP NA28; omitted by Treg NIV) is printed in square brackets; the verse keeps it and keeps the Greek order of the clauses.
- **"enemies in your mind by wicked works" → "enemies in mind, in the evil works"** (v21) — Greek ἐχθροὺς τῇ διανοίᾳ ἐν τοῖς ἔργοις τοῖς πονηροῖς.
- **"hath he reconciled" kept** (v22) — Greek ἀποκατήλλαξεν, "he reconciled" (WH Treg NA28 RP); "you" in v21 is its object, resumed by "to present you". The printed SBLGNT has ἀποκατηλλάγητε, "you were reconciled" (Holmes).
- **"unblameable and unreproveable in his sight" → "without blemish and blameless before him"** (v22) — Greek ἀμώμους, ἀνεγκλήτους (the word of Titus 1:6–7), κατενώπιον αὐτοῦ.
- **"grounded and settled" → "founded and firm"** (v23) — Greek τεθεμελιωμένοι καὶ ἑδραῖοι.
- **"to every creature which is under heaven" → "in all creation under heaven"** (v23) — Greek ἐν πάσῃ κτίσει; κτίσις is "creation" as in v15.
- **"in my sufferings" → "in the sufferings"** (v24) — the critical text has no "my".
- **"fill up that which is behind" → "fill up in turn what is lacking"** (v24) — Greek ἀνταναπληρῶ τὰ ὑστερήματα; ἀντ- "in return, on one's part".
- **"dispensation" → "stewardship"** (v25) — Greek οἰκονομία, the office of a house steward.
- **"to fulfil the word of God" kept** (v25) — Greek πληρῶσαι τὸν λόγον τοῦ θεοῦ.
- **"hid from ages and from generations" → "hidden from the ages and from the generations"** (v26) — Greek ἀπὸ τῶν αἰώνων καὶ ἀπὸ τῶν γενεῶν. The Greek sentence breaks off: after the participle comes a finite verb, "but now it has been manifested".
- **"would make known" → "willed to make known"** (v27) — Greek ἠθέλησεν.
- **"Gentiles" → "nations"** (v27) — Greek ἔθνη.
- **"in you"** (v27) — Greek ἐν ὑμῖν, plural: "in you" or "among you".
- **"warning every man" → "admonishing every man"** (v28) — Greek νουθετοῦντες πάντα ἄνθρωπον; ἄνθρωπος is a human being, man or woman.
- **"perfect" kept** (v28) — Greek τέλειον, "complete, full-grown"; "Jesus" is not in the critical text here.
- **"striving according to his working, which worketh in me mightily" → "struggling according to his working, which is working in me in power"** (v29) — Greek ἀγωνιζόμενος (the root of "struggle", 2:1) κατὰ τὴν ἐνέργειαν αὐτοῦ τὴν ἐνεργουμένην ἐν ἐμοὶ ἐν δυνάμει.

### Notes
- **v15–20** — in Greek one sentence that continues v13–14 with relative clauses ("who is …").
- **v21–22** — the Greek sentence starts with "you" as an object and resumes it with "to present you".
