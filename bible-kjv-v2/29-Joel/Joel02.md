# Joel 2

[1] Blow a ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and sound a blast on my holy mountain!
Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble,
for the **day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧** is coming, for it is near:

[2] a day of darkness and gloom,
a day of cloud and dark cloud!
Like dawn spread upon the mountains,
a people great and mighty;
its like has not been from of old,
and after it there shall not be again,
to the years of generation and generation.

[3] Before it fire has devoured,
and behind it a flame blazes;
like the garden of Eden is the land before it,
and behind it a wilderness of desolation;
and also there has been no escape from it.

[4] Like the appearance of horses is its appearance,
and like horsemen, so they run.

[5] Like the sound of chariots
on the tops of the mountains they leap,
like the sound of a flame of fire devouring stubble,
like a mighty people arrayed for battle.

[6] Before it peoples are in anguish;
all faces gather pallor.

[7] Like mighty ones they run;
like men of war they go up a wall;
and each in his ways they go,
and they do not alter their paths.

[8] And they do not press, each his brother;
each man in his highway they go;
and through the missiles they fall,
they do not break off.

[9] In the city they rush about,
on the wall they run,
into the houses they go up,
through the windows they come in like the thief.

[10] Before it the earth trembles,
the heavens quake;
sun and moon are darkened,
and the stars have gathered in their brightness.

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ utters his voice before his force,
for very great is his camp;
for mighty is he who does his word.
For great is the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and very fearsome,
and who can endure it?

[12] "And even now," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"return to me with all your heart,
and with fasting, and with weeping, and with lamenting;

[13] and rend your heart and not your garments."
And return to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
for gracious and compassionate is he,
slow to anger and abounding in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and relenting concerning the evil.

[14] Who knows? He may turn back and relent,
and leave behind him a blessing,
a grain offering and a drink offering for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[15] Blow a ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
sanctify a fast, call a solemn assembly;

[16] gather people, sanctify an assembly,
assemble elders,
gather children and sucklings of breasts;
let the bridegroom go out from his room,
and the bride from her canopy.

[17] Between the porch and the altar
let the priests weep, the ministers of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and let them say, "Spare, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your people,
and do not give your inheritance to reproach,
that nations should rule over them.
Why should they say among the peoples,
'Where is their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?'"

[18] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was jealous for his land
and had pity on his people.

[19] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered and said to his people, "Behold, I am sending you the grain and the new wine and the fresh oil, and you shall be satisfied with it; and I will not again make you a reproach among the nations.

[20] And the northerner I will remove far from you,
and drive him into a land of dryness and desolation,
his face toward the eastern sea,
and his rear toward the hinder sea;
and his stench shall go up,
and his foul smell shall go up,
for he has done great things.

[21] Do not fear, ground;
be glad and rejoice,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done great things!

[22] Do not fear, beasts of the field,
for the pastures of the wilderness have grown green;
for the tree has borne its fruit,
fig tree and vine have given their strength.

[23] And sons of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, be glad,
and rejoice in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
for he has given you the early rain for righteousness,
and has brought down for you rain,
early rain and latter rain, at the first.

[24] And the threshing floors shall be full of grain,
and the vats shall overflow with new wine and fresh oil.

[25] And I will repay you the years
that the locusts have eaten,
the hopping locust and the devourer and the cutting locust,
my great force that I sent among you.

[26] And you shall surely eat and be satisfied,
and praise the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
who has dealt wonderfully with you;
and my people shall never be ashamed.

[27] And you shall know that in the midst of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ am I,
and I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and there is none else;
and my people shall never be ashamed.

[28] And it shall be afterward:
I will pour out my spirit on all flesh,
and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy;
your old men shall dream dreams,
your young men shall see visions.

[29] And also on the slaves and on the maidservants
in those days I will pour out my spirit.

[30] And I will set wonders in the heavens and on the earth,
blood and fire and columns of smoke.

[31] The sun shall be turned to darkness,
and the moon to blood,
before the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ comes, the great and the fearsome.

[32] And it shall be: everyone
who calls on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall escape;
for on Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
there shall be an escape,
as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has said,
and among the survivors whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ calls."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13, 14, 17, 23, 26, 27) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents: v14, 15, 16 were split and v20 merged against the Hebrew and are now set as its cola.
- **"Blow ye the trumpet in Zion, and sound an alarm in my holy mountain: let all the inhabitants of the land tremble: for the day of the LORD cometh, for it is nigh at hand" → "Blow a shofar in Zion, and sound a blast on my holy mountain! Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble, for the day of Yahweh is coming, for it is near"** (v1) — Hebrew שׁוֹפָר without the article (v15 the same); הֵרִיעַ on an instrument "sound a blast"; רָגַז "tremble" (to be stirred, in fear here); כִּי … כִּי "for … for"; "day" in small letters, as the other prophets.
- **"A day of darkness and of gloominess, a day of clouds and of thick darkness, as the morning spread upon the mountains" → "a day of darkness and gloom, a day of cloud and dark cloud! Like dawn spread upon the mountains"** (v2) — Hebrew חֹשֶׁךְ "darkness", אֲפֵלָה "gloom", עָנָן "cloud", עֲרָפֶל "dark cloud"; כְּשַׁחַר "like dawn" (שַׁחַר, the first light spreading over the hills: the coming host is bright and wide as dawn). Reading the same letters as שְׁחֹר "blackness" changes the pointing; the Masoretic Text is kept. **"a great people and a strong; there hath not been ever the like, neither shall be any more after it, even to the years of many generations" → "a people great and mighty; its like has not been from of old, and after it there shall not be again, to the years of generation and generation"** — עָצוּם "mighty"; מִן־הָעוֹלָם "from of old"; לֹא יוֹסֵף "there shall not be again" (literally "it shall not add"); דּוֹר וָדוֹר "generation and generation".
- **"A fire devoureth before them; and behind them a flame burneth … yea, and nothing shall escape them" → "Before it fire has devoured, and behind it a flame blazes … and also there has been no escape from it"** (v3) — the Hebrew speaks of the people as one, "it" (לְפָנָיו "before it"), through v3–10, with plural verbs; אָכְלָה perfect; תְּלַהֵט "blaze"; מִדְבַּר שְׁמָמָה "a wilderness of desolation"; פְּלֵיטָה "escape", literally "and also an escape has not been to it" (none escapes it, or it lets none escape). Eden is the name kept.
- **"as horsemen, so shall they run" → "like horsemen, so they run"** (v4) — Hebrew פָּרָשִׁים "horsemen"; כֵּן "so" kept.
- **"Like the noise of chariots … like the noise of a flame of fire … as a strong people set in battle array" → "Like the sound of chariots … like the sound of a flame of fire devouring stubble, like a mighty people arrayed for battle"** (v5) — Hebrew קוֹל "sound" (of things), twice; מַרְכָּבוֹת "chariots"; עָרוּךְ מִלְחָמָה "arrayed for battle".
- **"Before their face the people shall be much pained: all faces shall gather blackness" → "Before it peoples are in anguish; all faces gather pallor"** (v6) — Hebrew עַמִּים "peoples"; יָחִילוּ "be in anguish" (the writhing of one in labor); קִבְּצוּ "gather" kept; פָארוּר (only here and Nah 2:10) is uncertain: "pallor" (or "a glow"; KJV "blackness" from פָּרוּר "pot").
- **"They shall run like mighty men; they shall climb the wall like men of war; and they shall march every one on his ways, and they shall not break their ranks" → "Like mighty ones they run; like men of war they go up a wall; and each in his ways they go, and they do not alter their paths"** (v7) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones"; אַנְשֵׁי מִלְחָמָה "men of war"; עָלָה "go up"; אִישׁ "each (man)" with a plural verb; יְעַבְּטוּן only here in this sense, "alter" (or "tangle", "break"); uncertain; אֹרַח "path".
- **"Neither shall one thrust another; they shall walk every one in his path: and when they fall upon the sword, they shall not be wounded" → "And they do not press, each his brother; each man in his highway they go; and through the missiles they fall, they do not break off"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאִישׁ אָחִיו "each … his brother" (not "one another"); גֶּבֶר "each man" beside אִישׁ (the standing "mighty man" does not fit a distributive word; the word named); מְסִלָּה "highway"; שֶׁלַח "missile" (a thrown weapon; collective with the article); יִפֹּלוּ "they fall" (plunge); לֹא יִבְצָעוּ "they do not break off" (בָּצַע "cut off"; or "they are not cut down"; uncertain).
- **"They shall run to and fro in the city … they shall climb up upon the houses; they shall enter in at the windows like a thief" → "In the city they rush about … into the houses they go up, through the windows they come in like the thief"** (v9) — Hebrew בָּעִיר "in the city"; יָשֹׁקּוּ "rush about" (שָׁקַק); the article of כַּגַּנָּב kept.
- **"The earth shall quake before them; the heavens shall tremble: the sun and the moon shall be dark, and the stars shall withdraw their shining" → "Before it the earth trembles, the heavens quake; sun and moon are darkened, and the stars have gathered in their brightness"** (v10) — Hebrew רָגַז "tremble" (v1), רָעַשׁ "quake"; perfects; קָדַר "be darkened"; אָסְפוּ נָגְהָם "have gathered in their brightness" (אָסַף "gather"; נֹגַהּ "brightness"). The same last two lines are 3:15.
- **"And the LORD shall utter his voice before his army: for his camp is very great: for he is strong that executeth his word: for the day of the LORD is great and very terrible; and who can abide it?" → "And Yahweh utters his voice before his force, for very great is his camp; for mighty is he who does his word. For great is the day of Yahweh and very fearsome, and who can endure it?"** (v11) — Hebrew נָתַן קוֹלוֹ, literally "gives his voice" (as 3:16; Amos 1:2); חַיִל "force" (of an army); מַחֲנֶה "camp"; עָצוּם "mighty"; עֹשֵׂה דְבָרוֹ "he who does his word"; נוֹרָא "fearsome"; כּוּל hifil "endure" (literally "contain").
- **"Therefore also now, saith the LORD, turn ye even to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning" → "And even now, declares Yahweh, return to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with lamenting"** (v12) — Hebrew וְגַם־עַתָּה "and even now" (no "therefore", no "yet"); נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh"; שׁוּב עַד "return to" (literally "as far as me"); מִסְפֵּד "lamenting", the noun of סָפַד "lament" (1:13).
- **"And rend your heart, and not your garments, and turn unto the LORD your God: for he is gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repenteth him of the evil" → "and rend your heart and not your garments. And return to Yahweh your Elohim, for gracious and compassionate is he, slow to anger and abounding in chesed, and relenting concerning the evil"** (v13) — Hebrew לְבַבְכֶם singular "your heart"; the words of Exod 34:6 in their own order here: חַנּוּן "gracious", רַחוּם "compassionate" (Exod 34:6 has the two the other way round; Jonah 4:2 as here), אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם "slow to anger" (literally "long of nostrils"), רַב־חֶסֶד "abounding in chesed", and נִחָם עַל־הָרָעָה "relenting concerning the evil" (רָעָה the evil planned; נִחַם is "be sorry, relent", not "repent").
- **"Who knoweth if he will return and repent, and leave a blessing behind him; even a meat offering and a drink offering unto the LORD your God?" → "Who knows? He may turn back and relent, and leave behind him a blessing, a grain offering and a drink offering for Yahweh your Elohim"** (v14) — Hebrew מִי יוֹדֵעַ יָשׁוּב "who knows — he will turn back" (no "if"); שׁוּב "turn back", נִחַם "relent"; הִשְׁאִיר "leave".
- **"Blow the trumpet in Zion, sanctify a fast" → "Blow a shofar in Zion, sanctify a fast"** (v15) — as v1 and 1:14 (קִדֵּשׁ "sanctify").
- **"Gather the people, sanctify the congregation, assemble the elders, gather the children, and those that suck the breasts: let the bridegroom go forth of his chamber, and the bride out of her closet" → "gather people, sanctify an assembly, assemble elders, gather children and sucklings of breasts; let the bridegroom go out from his room, and the bride from her canopy"** (v16) — Hebrew עָם, קָהָל, זְקֵנִים without the article; קָהָל "assembly"; אָסַף "gather", קִבֵּץ "assemble"; עוֹלָלִים "children"; יֹנְקֵי שָׁדָיִם "sucklings of breasts"; חָתָן "bridegroom", כַּלָּה "bride"; חֶדֶר "room"; חֻפָּה "canopy" (the bridal chamber or covering).
- **"Let the priests, the ministers of the LORD, weep between the porch and the altar, and let them say, Spare thy people, O LORD, and give not thine heritage to reproach, that the heathen should rule over them: wherefore should they say among the people, Where is their God?" → "Between the porch and the altar let the priests weep, the ministers of Yahweh, and let them say, Spare, Yahweh, your people, and do not give your inheritance to reproach, that nations should rule over them. Why should they say among the peoples, Where is their Elohim?"** (v17) — the Hebrew order (the place first); no "O"; חוּס "spare" (to look on with pity; חָמַל "have pity" is v18); נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; לִמְשָׁל־בָּם גּוֹיִם as pointed, "for nations to rule over them" (מָשַׁל); "to make a byword of them" (from מָשָׁל) is the other reading, in Changes only; עַמִּים "peoples".
- **"Then will the LORD be jealous for his land, and pity his people" → "And Yahweh was jealous for his land and had pity on his people"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיְקַנֵּא, וַיַּחְמֹל: narrative past (wayyiqtol), the turn of the book; קִנֵּא לְ "be jealous for" ("zealous" in Changes only); חָמַל "have pity".
- **"Yea, the LORD will answer and say unto his people, Behold, I will send you corn, and wine, and oil, and ye shall be satisfied therewith: and I will no more make you a reproach among the heathen" → "And Yahweh answered and said to his people, Behold, I am sending you the grain and the new wine and the fresh oil, and you shall be satisfied with it; and I will not again make you a reproach among the nations"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמֶר past; הִנְנִי שֹׁלֵחַ "behold, I am sending"; דָּגָן "grain", תִּירוֹשׁ "new wine", יִצְהָר "fresh oil", each with the article; אֹתוֹ "with it"; גּוֹיִם "nations".
- **"But I will remove far off from you the northern army, and will drive him into a land barren and desolate, with his face toward the east sea, and his hinder part toward the utmost sea, and his stink shall come up, and his ill savour shall come up" → "And the northerner I will remove far from you, and drive him into a land of dryness and desolation, his face toward the eastern sea, and his rear toward the hinder sea; and his stench shall go up, and his foul smell shall go up"** (v20) — Hebrew הַצְּפוֹנִי "the northerner" (no "army"), first in the line; צִיָּה "dryness"; פָּנָיו "his face", סֹפוֹ "his rear" (literally "his end"); הַיָּם הַקַּדְמֹנִי "the eastern sea" (literally "the front sea"), הַיָּם הָאַחֲרוֹן "the hinder sea" (the west for one facing the sunrise); בְּאֹשׁ "stench", צַחֲנָה "foul smell" (only here), each with its own "shall go up". **"because he hath done great things" → "for he has done great things"** — הִגְדִּיל לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "he has made great to do"; the same words of Yahweh in v21.
- **"Fear not, O land; be glad and rejoice: for the LORD will do great things" → "Do not fear, ground; be glad and rejoice, for Yahweh has done great things!"** (v21) — Hebrew אֲדָמָה "ground"; no "O"; גִּיל "be glad", שָׂמַח "rejoice"; הִגְדִּיל perfect.
- **"Be not afraid, ye beasts of the field: for the pastures of the wilderness do spring, for the tree beareth her fruit, the fig tree and the vine do yield their strength" → "Do not fear, beasts of the field, for the pastures of the wilderness have grown green; for the tree has borne its fruit, fig tree and vine have given their strength"** (v22) — Hebrew שָׂדָי, the old form of שָׂדֶה "field"; דָּשְׁאוּ, the verb of דֶּשֶׁא "vegetation"; נָשָׂא פִרְיוֹ "has borne its fruit"; חַיִל "strength" (of a tree: what it yields; elsewhere "force", "wealth").
- **"Be glad then, ye children of Zion … for he hath given you the former rain moderately, and he will cause to come down for you the rain, the former rain, and the latter rain in the first month" → "And sons of Zion, be glad, and rejoice in Yahweh your Elohim, for he has given you the early rain for righteousness, and has brought down for you rain, early rain and latter rain, at the first"** (v23) — Hebrew וּבְנֵי צִיּוֹן "and sons of Zion"; הַמּוֹרֶה "the early rain" — the same word is "teacher" (Isa 30:20), and "the teacher for righteousness" is the other reading; לִצְדָקָה "for righteousness"; וַיּוֹרֶד past; מוֹרֶה וּמַלְקוֹשׁ "early rain and latter rain"; בָּרִאשׁוֹן "at the first" (no "month"; the Greek reads "as before", from memory).
- **"And the floors shall be full of wheat, and the fats shall overflow with wine and oil" → "And the threshing floors shall be full of grain, and the vats shall overflow with new wine and fresh oil"** (v24) — Hebrew גֳּרָנוֹת "threshing floors"; בָּר "grain"; יְקָבִים "vats" (the trough of the winepress; גַּת is the press, 3:13); תִּירוֹשׁ, יִצְהָר as v19.
- **"And I will restore to you the years that the locust hath eaten, the cankerworm, and the caterpiller, and the palmerworm, my great army which I sent among you" → "And I will repay you the years that the locusts have eaten, the hopping locust and the devourer and the cutting locust, my great force that I sent among you"** (v25) — Hebrew שִׁלֵּם "repay"; the four words of 1:4 (each its own, the kinds uncertain), here in the order אַרְבֶּה, יֶלֶק, חָסִיל, גָּזָם; חֵילִי "my force" (v11).
- **"And ye shall eat in plenty, and be satisfied … that hath dealt wondrously with you: and my people shall never be ashamed" → "And you shall surely eat and be satisfied … who has dealt wonderfully with you; and my people shall never be ashamed"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew וַאֲכַלְתֶּם אָכוֹל וְשָׂבוֹעַ, literally "you shall eat, eating and being satisfied": "surely"; עָשָׂה … לְהַפְלִיא "dealt wonderfully" (the verb of פֶּלֶא "wonder"); לֹא … לְעוֹלָם "never", literally "not … forever".
- **"And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the LORD your God, and none else" → "And you shall know that in the midst of Israel am I, and I am Yahweh your Elohim, and there is none else"** (v27) — Hebrew order, "in the midst of Israel" first; וַאֲנִי "and I" (no second "that"); אֵין עוֹד "there is none else".
- **Joel 2:28–32 = Heb 3:1–5** — the Hebrew Bible makes these verses a chapter of their own (Joel 3); our numbers are kept.
- **"And it shall come to pass afterward, that I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh" → "And it shall be afterward: I will pour out my spirit on all flesh"** (v28) — Hebrew וְהָיָה אַחֲרֵי־כֵן "and it shall be afterward" (no "that"); שָׁפַךְ "pour out"; רוּחִי "my spirit", small letters as the word stands; כָּל־בָּשָׂר "all flesh". **"your old men"** — זְקֵנִים, the word of "elders" (1:2, 14; 2:16), here set against "young men" (בַּחוּרִים): age is meant. חֶזְיֹנוֹת "visions". Peter quotes the passage in Acts 2:17–21 from the Greek, which differs in places ("in the last days", "my slaves", "and they shall prophesy" in v29, "signs" in v30 — from memory); the verses here follow the Hebrew as it stands.
- **"And also upon the servants and upon the handmaids" → "And also on the slaves and on the maidservants"** (v29) — Hebrew הָעֲבָדִים "the slaves" (עֶבֶד), הַשְּׁפָחוֹת "the maidservants" (שִׁפְחָה); no "my" in the Hebrew.
- **"And I will shew wonders in the heavens and in the earth, blood, and fire, and pillars of smoke" → "And I will set wonders in the heavens and on the earth, blood and fire and columns of smoke"** (v30) — Hebrew נָתַן "give, set"; מוֹפְתִים "wonders"; תִּימֲרוֹת עָשָׁן "columns of smoke" (palm-shaped, as Song 3:6).
- **"before the great and the terrible day of the LORD come" → "before the day of Yahweh comes, the great and the fearsome"** (v31) — Hebrew order; נוֹרָא "fearsome".
- **"And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered: for in mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, as the LORD hath said, and in the remnant whom the LORD shall call" → "And it shall be: everyone who calls on the name of Yahweh shall escape; for on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there shall be an escape, as Yahweh has said, and among the survivors whom Yahweh calls"** (v32) — Hebrew יִמָּלֵט "shall escape" (נִמְלַט) and פְלֵיטָה "an escape" (another root, the same English); שְׂרִידִים "survivors" (not "remnant": that is שְׁאֵרִית); קֹרֵא participle "calls". Rom 10:13 and Acts 2:21 have "shall be saved" (σωθήσεται) from the Greek; the Hebrew verb is "escape".

### Notes
- **v28–32** — quoted by Peter at Pentecost (Acts 2:17–21); v32 also in Rom 10:13.
