# Joel 2

[1] Blow a ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
sound an alarm on my holy mountain!
Let all the inhabitants of the land tremble,
for the **Day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧** is coming; it is near:

[2] a day of darkness and gloom,
a day of clouds and thick darkness!
Like blackness there is spread upon the mountains
a great and powerful people;
their like has never been before,
nor will be again after them
through the years of all generations.

[3] Fire devours before them,
and behind them a flame burns.
The land is like the garden of Eden before them,
but behind them a desolate wilderness,
and nothing escapes them.

[4] Their appearance is like the appearance of horses,
and like war horses they run.

[5] As with the rumbling of chariots,
they leap on the tops of the mountains,
like the crackling of a flame of fire devouring the stubble,
like a powerful army drawn up for battle.

[6] Before them peoples are in anguish;
all faces grow pale.

[7] Like warriors they charge;
like soldiers they scale the wall.
They march each on his way;
they do not swerve from their paths.

[8] They do not jostle one another;
each marches in his path;
they burst through the weapons
and are not halted.

[9] They leap upon the city,
they run upon the walls,
they climb up into the houses,
they enter through the windows like a thief.

[10] The earth quakes before them;
the heavens tremble.
The sun and the moon are darkened,
and the stars withdraw their shining.

[11] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ utters his voice before his army,
for his camp is exceedingly great;
he who executes his word is powerful.
For the Day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is great and very awesome;
who can endure it?

[12] "Yet even now," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"return to me with all your heart,
with fasting, with weeping, and with mourning;

[13] and rend your hearts and not your garments."
Return to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
for he is gracious and merciful,
slow to anger, and abounding in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧;
and he relents over disaster.

[14] Who knows whether he will not turn and relent,
and leave a blessing behind him,
a grain offering and a drink offering
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[15] Blow the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
consecrate a fast;
call a solemn assembly;

[16] gather the people.
Consecrate the congregation;
assemble the elders;
gather the children,
even nursing infants.
Let the bridegroom leave his room,
and the bride her chamber.

[17] Between the vestibule and the altar
let the priests, the ministers of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, weep
and say, "Spare your people, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and make not your heritage a reproach,
a byword among the nations.
Why should they say among the peoples,
'Where is their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?'"

[18] Then ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ became jealous for his land
and had pity on his people.

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ answered and said to his people, "Behold, I am sending to you grain, wine, and oil, and you will be satisfied; and I will no more make you a reproach among the nations.

[20] "I will remove the northerner far from you,
and drive him into a parched and desolate land,
his vanguard into the eastern sea,
and his rear guard into the western sea;
the stench and foul smell of him will rise,
for he has done great things.

[21] Fear not, O land;
be glad and rejoice,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done great things!

[22] Fear not, you beasts of the field,
for the pastures of the wilderness are green;
the tree bears its fruit;
the fig tree and vine give their full yield.

[23] Be glad, O children of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and rejoice in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
for he has given the early rain for your vindication;
he has poured down for you abundant rain,
the early and the latter rain, as before.

[24] The threshing floors shall be full of grain;
the vats shall overflow with wine and oil.

[25] I will restore to you the years
that the swarming locust has eaten,
the hopper, the destroyer, and the cutter,
my great army, which I sent among you.

[26] You shall eat in plenty and be satisfied,
and praise the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
who has dealt wondrously with you.
And my people shall never again be put to shame.

[27] You shall know that I am in the midst of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and there is none else.
And my people shall never again be put to shame.

[28] "And it shall come to pass afterward,
that I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh;
your sons and your daughters shall prophesy,
your old men shall dream dreams,
and your young men shall see visions.

[29] Even on the male and female servants
in those days I will pour out my Spirit.

[30] I will show wonders in the heavens and on the earth,
blood and fire and columns of smoke.

[31] The sun shall be turned to darkness,
and the moon to blood,
before the great and awesome Day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ comes.

[32] And it shall come to pass that everyone
who calls on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be saved.
For in Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
there shall be those who escape,
as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has said,
and among the survivors shall be those whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ calls."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv13, 14, 17, 23, 26, 27) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV renders as prose; Hebrew is sustained poetry.
- **"the day of the Lord" → "the Day of Yahweh"** (vv1, 11, 31) — technical eschatological formula consistently capitalized.
- **"as the morning spread upon the mountains" → "Like blackness there is spread upon the mountains"** (v2) — Hebrew כְּשַׁחַר פָּרֻשׂ עַל הֶהָרִים; *shachar* = dawn/blackness (the spreading darkness of a locust swarm); "blackness" more vivid.
- **"a great people and a strong" → "a great and powerful people"** (v2) — modernized word order.
- **"all faces shall gather blackness" → "all faces grow pale"** (v6) — Hebrew פָּארוּר *pa'rur* = pallor/flush; faces draining of color in terror.
- **"they shall run like mighty men" → "Like warriors they charge"** (v7) — modernized.
- **"they shall not break their ranks" → "they do not swerve from their paths"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא יְעַבְּטוּן אֹרְחוֹתָם; "swerve" more vivid.
- **"Neither shall one thrust another" → "They do not jostle one another"** (v8) — modernized.
- **"when they fall upon the sword, they shall not be wounded" → "they burst through the weapons and are not halted"** (v8) — Hebrew בַּעַד הַשֶּׁלַח יִפֹּלוּ; the locusts are not stopped even by weapons.
- **"The Lord shall utter his voice" → "Yahweh utters his voice"** (v11) — divine name restored; present tense for vividness.
- **"he is strong that executeth his word" → "he who executes his word is powerful"** (v11) — modernized.
- **"very terrible" → "very awesome"** (v11) — Hebrew נוֹרָא *nora'* = awe-inspiring; "awesome" retains the sense of overwhelming majesty rather than mere terror.
- **"who can abide it" → "who can endure it"** (v11) — modernized.
- **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** (v12) — Hebrew נְאֻם יְהוָה; "declares" is the standard prophetic formula.
- **"repenteth him of the evil" → "he relents over disaster"** (v13) — Hebrew וְנִחָם עַל הָרָעָה; God does not sin and so cannot "repent"; the Hebrew means he turns back from the calamity he planned.
- **"a meat offering" → "a grain offering"** (v14) — Hebrew מִנְחָה *minchah* = grain offering; "meat" in 1611 meant "food" but now means flesh only.
- **"sanctify a fast" → "consecrate a fast"** (v15) — Hebrew קַדְּשׁוּ *qaddeshu* = consecrate/set apart; consistent with Joe 1:14.
- **"sanctify the congregation" → "Consecrate the congregation"** (v16) — same Hebrew root; consistent.
- **"those that suck the breasts" → "nursing infants"** (v16) — modernized.
- **"let the bride out of her closet" → "the bride her chamber"** (v16) — Hebrew חֻפָּתָהּ *chuppatah* = bridal chamber/canopy; modernized.
- **"between the porch and the altar" → "Between the vestibule and the altar"** (v17) — Hebrew בֵּין הָאוּלָם וְלַמִּזְבֵּחַ; "vestibule" more precise than "porch."
- **"give not thine heritage to reproach, that the heathen should rule over them" → "make not your heritage a reproach, a byword among the nations"** (v17) — Hebrew לְחֶרְפָּה; "byword among the nations" captures the social shame; "heathen should rule over them" is a different reading.
- **"Where is their God?" → "Where is their *Elohim*?"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵיהֶם; *Elohim* retained; the taunt uses the divine name.
- **"corn, and wine, and oil" → "grain, wine, and oil"** (v19) — Hebrew דָּגָן; "grain" not "corn" (American English: corn = maize).
- **"the northern army" → "the northerner"** (v20) — Hebrew הַצְּפוֹנִי *hatzefoni* = "the northerner"; may refer to the locust army from the north or an eschatological invader; singular retained.
- **"with his face toward the east sea, and his hinder part toward the utmost sea" → "his vanguard into the eastern sea, and his rear guard into the western sea"** (v20) — Hebrew פָּנָיו אֶל הַיָּם הַקַּדְמֹנִי וְסֹפוֹ אֶל הַיָּם הָאַחֲרוֹן; "vanguard/rear guard" more vivid than "face/hinder part."
- **"his stink shall come up, and his ill savour shall come up" → "the stench and foul smell of him will rise"** (v20) — modernized; condensed.
- **"for he hath done great things" → "for he has done great things"** (v20) — same ironic echo of v21: the locust/invader "did great things" (devastation); Yahweh also "does great things" (restoration).
- **"the former rain moderately" → "the early rain for your vindication"** (v23) — Hebrew הַמּוֹרֶה לִצְדָקָה *hammoreh litzedaqah*; *moreh* = early rain OR teacher; *tzedaqah* = righteousness/vindication; the rain restores covenant faithfulness.
- **"the fats shall overflow" → "the vats shall overflow"** (v24) — Hebrew יְקָבִים *yeqavim* = wine vats; "vats" more natural than "fats."
- **"the locust … the cankerworm … the caterpiller … the palmerworm" → "the swarming locust … the hopper, the destroyer, and the cutter"** (v25) — Hebrew *arbeh*, *yeleq*, *chasil*, *gazam*; order reversed from Joel 1:4; brief English glosses used here rather than full transliteration, consistent with the summary recall.
- **"pillars of smoke" → "columns of smoke"** (v30) — Hebrew תִּימֲרוֹת עָשָׁן *timarot ashan* = columns/palm-shaped pillars of smoke; "columns" more natural modern English.
- **"the great and the terrible day of the LORD" → "the great and awesome Day of Yahweh"** (v31) — consistent formula; "awesome" for *nora'*.
- **"whosoever shall call on the name of the LORD shall be delivered" → "everyone who calls on the name of Yahweh shall be saved"** (v32) — Hebrew כֹּל אֲשֶׁר יִקְרָא בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה יִמָּלֵט; "saved" (*yimmalet*) used here; quoted Rom 10:13.

### Notes
- **v28-32** — quoted by Peter at Pentecost (Acts 2:17-21) as fulfillment of the Spirit's outpouring.
- **v32** — quoted Rom 10:13.
