# Joel 3

[1] For behold, in those days and at that time, when I restore the fortunes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[2] I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the valley of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, and I will enter into judgment with them there concerning my people and my inheritance ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whom they scattered among the nations; and my land they divided,

[3] and for my people they cast a lot, and they gave the child for the prostitute, and the young girl they sold for wine, and they drank.

[4] And also, what are you to me, ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, and all the regions of ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧? Is it a recompense that you are repaying to me? And if you are dealing it out to me, swiftly, speedily I will return your recompense on your head,

[5] you who have taken my silver and my gold, and my good desirable things you have brought into your temples.

[6] And the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the sons of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ you have sold to the sons of the ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧, in order to remove them far from their border.

[7] Behold, I am rousing them from the place to which you have sold them, and I will return your recompense on your head.

[8] And I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they shall sell them to the ⟦sabeans|Shevaim|Sabeans⟧, to a nation far off; for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[9] Proclaim this among the nations:
Sanctify war,
rouse the mighty ones;
let them draw near, let them come up,
all the men of war.

[10] Beat your mattocks into swords,
and your pruning knives into spears;
let the weak one say, "I am a mighty one."

[11] Hasten and come, all the nations from around,
and gather yourselves.
There bring down, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your mighty ones.

[12] Let the nations be roused
and come up to the valley of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧;
for there I will sit to judge
all the nations from around.

[13] Send in the sickle,
for the harvest is ripe;
come, go down,
for the winepress is full;
the vats overflow,
for great is their evil.

[14] Multitudes, multitudes
in the valley of decision!
For near is the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
in the valley of decision.

[15] Sun and moon are darkened,
and the stars have gathered in their brightness.

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ roars from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ he utters his voice,
and the heavens and the earth quake;
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a refuge for his people,
and a strong place for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[17] And you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who abides in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, my holy mountain; and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall be holy, and strangers shall not pass through it again.

[18] And it shall be in that day:
the mountains shall drip sweet wine,
and the hills shall flow with milk,
and all the channels of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall flow with water;
and a spring shall go out from the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and water the wadi of Shittim.

[19] ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall become a desolation,
and Edom shall become a wilderness of desolation,
for the violence done to the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
in that they shed innocent blood in their land.

[20] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall dwell forever,
and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to generation and generation.

[21] And I will clear their blood that I have not cleared;
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ abides in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Joel 3:1–21 = Heb 4:1–21** — the Hebrew Bible numbers this chapter 4 (our 2:28–32 being its chapter 3); our numbers are kept. The outer quotation marks are dropped: Yahweh speaks through the chapter, and the prophet's lines (v8 "for Yahweh has spoken", v11c, v16) stand within it as the Hebrew has them.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents; v1–8 and v17 are prose; v18 was split against the Hebrew and is now set as its cola.
- **"when I shall bring again the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem" → "when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem"** (v1) — Hebrew שׁוּב שְׁבוּת, literally "turn back the captivity" or "turn the turning" (שְׁבוּת from שָׁבָה "take captive" or from שׁוּב), traditional "turn the captivity"; one idiom, one rendering through the Bible. The qere אָשִׁיב (hifil, "bring back") is read; the written form אשוב (qal) gives the same sense.
- **"and will plead with them there for my people and for my heritage Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations, and parted my land" → "and I will enter into judgment with them there concerning my people and my inheritance Israel, whom they scattered among the nations; and my land they divided"** (v2) — Hebrew נִשְׁפַּט עִם "enter into judgment with"; עַל "concerning"; נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; וְאֶת־אַרְצִי חִלֵּקוּ, the object first; חִלֵּק "divide"; "valley" in small letters (the name is Jehoshaphat: "Yahweh judges", the word of v2, 12).
- **"And they have cast lots for my people; and have given a boy for an harlot, and sold a girl for wine, that they might drink" → "and for my people they cast a lot, and they gave the child for the prostitute, and the young girl they sold for wine, and they drank"** (v3) — Hebrew יַדּוּ גוֹרָל "they cast a lot" (singular); הַיֶּלֶד "the child" (a male child; יֶלֶד) and הַיַּלְדָּה "the young girl" (יַלְדָּה), with the article; בְּ of the price, "for"; זוֹנָה "prostitute"; וַיִּשְׁתּוּ "and they drank" (no "that they might", no "it").
- **"Yea, and what have ye to do with me, O Tyre, and Zidon, and all the coasts of Palestine? will ye render me a recompence? and if ye recompense me, swiftly and speedily will I return your recompence upon your own head" → "And also, what are you to me, Tyre and Sidon, and all the regions of Philistia? Is it a recompense that you are repaying to me? And if you are dealing it out to me, swiftly, speedily I will return your recompense on your head"** (v4) — Hebrew וְגַם "and also"; no "O"; גְּלִילוֹת "regions" (literally "circuits"); הַגְּמוּל … מְשַׁלְּמִים "is it the recompense you are repaying" (גְּמוּל "recompense", שִׁלֵּם "repay"); גֹּמְלִים "dealing out" (גָּמַל, the verb of גְּמוּל: "it" supplied for English); קַל מְהֵרָה "swiftly, speedily" (no "and"); בְּרֹאשְׁכֶם "on your head" (no "own"; also v7).
- **"Because ye have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried into your temples my goodly pleasant things" → "you who have taken my silver and my gold, and my good desirable things you have brought into your temples"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר "who" (or "in that"); מַחֲמַדַּי הַטֹּבִים "my good desirable things" (מַחְמָד "desirable thing", טוֹב "good"); הֵבֵאתֶם "you have brought"; הֵיכָל "temple" (also "palace": a great house of a god or a king).
- **"The children also of Judah and the children of Jerusalem have ye sold unto the Grecians, that ye might remove them far from their border" → "And the sons of Judah and the sons of Jerusalem you have sold to the sons of the Greeks, in order to remove them far from their border"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּנֵי "sons of" three times; לְמַעַן + infinitive "in order to"; הַיְּוָנִים "the Greeks" (Yavan, the Ionians).
- **"Behold, I will raise them out of the place whither ye have sold them" → "Behold, I am rousing them from the place to which you have sold them"** (v7) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מְעִירָם, participle "I am rousing them" (עוּר hifil, as v9, 12).
- **"to a people far off: for the LORD hath spoken it" → "to a nation far off; for Yahweh has spoken"** (v8) — Hebrew גּוֹי "nation"; no "it".
- **"Proclaim ye this among the Gentiles; Prepare war, wake up the mighty men, let all the men of war draw near; let them come up" → "Proclaim this among the nations: Sanctify war, rouse the mighty ones; let them draw near, let them come up, all the men of war"** (v9) — Hebrew גּוֹיִם "nations"; קַדְּשׁוּ מִלְחָמָה "sanctify war" (קִדֵּשׁ, as the fast of 1:14; war begun with rites); הָעִירוּ "rouse"; גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones"; Hebrew order, "all the men of war" last.
- **"Beat your plowshares into swords, and your pruninghooks into spears: let the weak say, I am strong" → "Beat your mattocks into swords, and your pruning knives into spears; let the weak one say, I am a mighty one"** (v10) — the words of Isa 2:4 (= Mic 4:3) turned round, and kept the same: אִתִּים "mattocks" (KJV "plowshares"), מַזְמֵרוֹת "pruning knives", חֶרֶב "sword", רֹמַח "spear"; כָּתַת "beat". הַחַלָּשׁ "the weak one" (only here; the verb חָלַשׁ "weaken"); גִּבּוֹר "a mighty one" (as v9, 11).
- **"Assemble yourselves, and come, all ye heathen, and gather yourselves together round about: thither cause thy mighty ones to come down, O LORD" → "Hasten and come, all the nations from around, and gather yourselves. There bring down, Yahweh, your mighty ones"** (v11) — Hebrew עוּשׁוּ only here: "hasten" (or "help"); uncertain. מִסָּבִיב "from around"; וְנִקְבָּצוּ "and gather yourselves" (the accent ends the line here); שָׁמָּה "there" opens the last line; הַנְחַת, hifil of נָחַת "bring down"; no "O". Many read "let Yahweh shatter your mighty ones" or put "there" with the line before (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Let the heathen be wakened … to judge all the heathen round about" → "Let the nations be roused … to judge all the nations from around"** (v12) — Hebrew יֵעוֹרוּ "be roused" (v7, 9); מִסָּבִיב "from around" (v11); יָשַׁב "sit" (to judge).
- **"Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe: come, get you down; for the press is full, the fats overflow; for their wickedness is great" → "Send in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe; come, go down, for the winepress is full; the vats overflow, for great is their evil"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׁלְחוּ "send"; בָּשַׁל "is ripe" (literally "has boiled": cooked by the sun); רְדוּ "go down" (from יָרַד; the same form from רָדָה is "tread", which many take here); גַּת "winepress", יְקָבִים "vats" (2:24); רָעָה "evil".
- **"Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision: for the day of the LORD is near" → "Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision! For near is the day of Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew הֲמוֹנִים "multitudes"; הֶחָרוּץ "decision" (from חָרַץ "cut, decide"; the same word is the "threshing sledge", Isa 41:15; Amos 1:3 חֲרֻצוֹת — both senses may be heard); קָרוֹב "near" first, as 1:15.
- **"The sun and the moon shall be darkened, and the stars shall withdraw their shining" → "Sun and moon are darkened, and the stars have gathered in their brightness"** (v15) — the Hebrew of 2:10b, the same wording: perfects; no articles; אָסְפוּ נָגְהָם "have gathered in their brightness".
- **"The LORD also shall roar out of Zion, and utter his voice from Jerusalem; and the heavens and the earth shall shake: but the LORD will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel" → "And Yahweh roars from Zion, and from Jerusalem he utters his voice, and the heavens and the earth quake; and Yahweh is a refuge for his people, and a strong place for the sons of Israel"** (v16) — the first two lines are the Hebrew of Amos 1:2, in the same wording there: שָׁאַג "roar"; נָתַן קוֹלוֹ "utter his voice" (literally "give his voice", as 2:11); "from Jerusalem" first in its line, as the Hebrew. The verbs are yiqtol and weqatal: present here (as 2:10, 11), the future equally possible. רָעַשׁ "quake"; מַחֲסֶה "refuge"; מָעוֹז "strong place"; בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "sons of Israel".
- **"So shall ye know that I am the LORD your God dwelling in Zion, my holy mountain: then shall Jerusalem be holy, and there shall no strangers pass through her any more" → "And you shall know that I am Yahweh your Elohim, who abides in Zion, my holy mountain; and Jerusalem shall be holy, and strangers shall not pass through it again"** (v17) — Hebrew וִידַעְתֶּם "and you shall know"; שֹׁכֵן "who abides" (שָׁכַן, beside יָשַׁב "dwell" v20); קֹדֶשׁ "holy" (literally "holiness"); זָרִים "strangers"; עוֹד "again".
- **"And it shall come to pass in that day, that the mountains shall drop down new wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the rivers of Judah shall flow with waters, and a fountain shall come forth of the house of the LORD, and shall water the valley of Shittim" → "And it shall be in that day: the mountains shall drip sweet wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the channels of Judah shall flow with water; and a spring shall go out from the house of Yahweh and water the wadi of Shittim"** (v18) — Hebrew וְהָיָה בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא "and it shall be in that day"; נָטַף "drip"; עָסִיס "sweet wine" (1:5); הָלַךְ "flow" (literally "go": "go milk", "go water"); אֲפִיקִים "channels"; מַעְיָן "spring"; יָצָא "go out"; הִשְׁקָה "water"; נַחַל "wadi" (not "valley"); שִׁטִּים "acacias", the name kept.
- **"Egypt shall be a desolation, and Edom shall be a desolate wilderness, for the violence against the children of Judah" → "Egypt shall become a desolation, and Edom shall become a wilderness of desolation, for the violence done to the sons of Judah, in that they shed innocent blood in their land"** (v19) — Hebrew לְ … תִּהְיֶה "become" twice; מִדְבַּר שְׁמָמָה "a wilderness of desolation" (2:3); מֵחֲמַס בְּנֵי יְהוּדָה, literally "from the violence of the sons of Judah" (the violence done to them, as Gen 16:5); אֲשֶׁר "in that"; דָם נָקִיא "innocent blood"; whose land "their land" is (Judah's or the nations') is not said.
- **"But Judah shall dwell for ever, and Jerusalem from generation to generation" → "And Judah shall dwell forever, and Jerusalem to generation and generation"** (v20) — Hebrew וִיהוּדָה "and Judah"; יָשַׁב "dwell" (of a land "be inhabited"; KJV kept); לְדוֹר וָדוֹר "to generation and generation".
- **"For I will cleanse their blood that I have not cleansed: for the LORD dwelleth in Zion" → "And I will clear their blood that I have not cleared; and Yahweh abides in Zion"** (v21) — Hebrew וְנִקֵּיתִי … לֹא־נִקֵּיתִי, נִקָּה piel "clear" (hold or leave guiltless, as Exod 34:7 "he will surely not clear"); what is said is uncertain: "I will hold their blood innocent, which I had not held innocent", or as a question with its answer, "shall I leave their blood unpunished? I will not". The Greek reads "I will avenge" (from memory); the Hebrew verb is kept. וַיהוָה שֹׁכֵן "and Yahweh abides" (v17).

### Notes
- **v10** — the words of Isa 2:4 / Mic 4:3 turned round: the nations arm for judgment.
- **Valley of Jehoshaphat** (v2, 12) — the name means "Yahweh judges"; v14 calls it "the valley of decision". Where it lies is not said; the Kidron Valley is the later tradition.
