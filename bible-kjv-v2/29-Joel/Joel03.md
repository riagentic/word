# Joel 3

[1] "For behold, in those days and at that time, when I restore the fortunes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[2] I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the Valley of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧. And I will enter into judgment with them there, on behalf of my people and my heritage ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, because they have scattered them among the nations and have divided up my land,

[3] and have cast lots for my people, and have traded a boy for a prostitute, and have sold a girl for wine and have drunk it.

[4] "What are you to me, O ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ and ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧, and all the regions of ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧? Are you paying me back for something? If you are paying me back, I will return your payment on your own head swiftly and speedily.

[5] For you have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried my rich treasures into your temples.

[6] You have sold the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to the ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ in order to remove them far from their own border.

[7] Behold, I will stir them up from the place to which you have sold them, and I will return your payment on your own head.

[8] I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they will sell them to the ⟦sabeans|Shevaim|Sabeans⟧, to a nation far off, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken.

[9] Proclaim this among the nations:
Consecrate for war;
stir up the mighty men.
Let all the men of war draw near;
let them come up.

[10] Beat your plowshares into swords,
and your pruning hooks into spears;
let the weak say, "I am a warrior."

[11] Hasten and come, all you surrounding nations,
and assemble there.
Bring down your warriors, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[12] Let the nations stir themselves up
and come up to the Valley of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧;
for there I will sit to judge
all the surrounding nations.

[13] Put in the sickle,
for the harvest is ripe.
Go in, tread,
for the winepress is full.
The vats overflow,
for their evil is great.

[14] Multitudes, multitudes,
in the Valley of Decision!
For the Day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is near
in the Valley of Decision.

[15] The sun and the moon are darkened,
and the stars withdraw their shining.

[16] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ roars from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and utters his voice from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
and the heavens and the earth quake.
But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a refuge to his people,
a stronghold to the people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[17] "So you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who dwells in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, my holy mountain. And ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall be holy, and strangers shall never again pass through it.

[18] "And in that day
the mountains shall drip sweet wine,
and the hills shall flow with milk,
and all the streambeds of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧
shall flow with water;
and a fountain shall come forth from the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
and water the Valley of Shittim.

[19] "⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ shall become a desolation
and Edom a desolate wilderness,
for the violence done to the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
because they have shed innocent blood in their land.

[20] But ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ shall be inhabited forever,
and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to all generations.

[21] I will avenge their blood, blood I have not avenged,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dwells in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (vv9-16, 18-21) — KJV renders as prose; vv9-16 and 18-21 are poetry.
- **"bring again the captivity of Judah and Jerusalem" → "restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem"** (v1) — Hebrew שׁוּב שְׁבוּת *shuv shevut*; the idiom means "turn the turning/captivity" = restore fortunes; not necessarily literal captives returning.
- **"plead with them" → "enter into judgment with them"** (v2) — Hebrew נִשְׁפַּטְתִּי *nishpatti* = I will enter into legal judgment; forensic language; "plead" too weak.
- **"parted my land" → "divided up my land"** (v2) — Hebrew חִלְּקוּ אֶת אַרְצִי; "divided up" more vivid than "parted."
- **"given a boy for an harlot" → "traded a boy for a prostitute"** (v3) — Hebrew נָתְנוּ הַיֶּלֶד בַּזּוֹנָה; "traded" captures the commercial exchange; "harlot" → "prostitute" modernized.
- **"all the coasts of Palestine" → "all the regions of Philistia"** (v4) — Hebrew כֹּל גְּלִילוֹת פְּלֶשֶׁת; "Philistia" more precise than "Palestine" (anachronistic name); "regions" for *gelilot* = districts.
- **"will ye render me a recompence?" → "Are you paying me back for something?"** (v4) — modernized.
- **"your goodly pleasant things" → "my rich treasures"** (v5) — Hebrew מַחֲמַדַּי הַטּוֹבִים; the suffix shows these are Yahweh's treasures, not the nations'.
- **"the Grecians" → "the Greeks"** (v6) — Hebrew הַיְּוָנִים *hayyevanim* = the Ionians/Greeks; modernized.
- **"I will raise them out of the place" → "I will stir them up from the place"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵעִיר *me'ir* = stirring up/arousing; "stir them up" more vivid.
- **"Prepare war" → "Consecrate for war"** (v9) — Hebrew קַדְּשׁוּ מִלְחָמָה *qadshu milchamah* = "consecrate/sanctify war"; holy war was formally consecrated; "prepare" loses the religious dimension.
- **"let the weak say, I am strong" → "let the weak say, 'I am a warrior'"** (v10) — Hebrew אֲנִי גִבּוֹר *ani gibbor* = "I am a *gibbor*" (mighty man/warrior); ironic reversal of Joel 2's warriors.
- **"Assemble yourselves, and come, all ye heathen" → "Hasten and come, all you surrounding nations"** (v11) — Hebrew עוּשׁוּ וָבֹאוּ כָּל הַגּוֹיִם *ushu va'vu kol hagoyim*; "hasten" for *ushu*; "nations" for *goyim* (not "heathen").
- **"thither cause thy mighty ones to come down, O Lord" → "Bring down your warriors, O Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew הַנְחַת גִּבּוֹרֶיךָ יְהוָה; Yahweh's divine warriors/host called to the valley of judgment.
- **"the heathen be wakened" → "the nations stir themselves up"** (v12) — Hebrew יֵעוֹרוּ הַגּוֹיִם; "stir themselves up" for *ye'oru*; "nations" for *goyim*.
- **"all the heathen round about" → "all the surrounding nations"** (v12) — Hebrew מִסָּבִיב; consistent.
- **"come, get you down; for the press is full" → "Go in, tread, for the winepress is full"** (v13) — Hebrew בֹּאוּ רְדוּ כִּי מָלְאָה הַגַּת; "tread" the winepress; "winepress" explicit.
- **"the fats overflow" → "The vats overflow"** (v13) — Hebrew הֵשִׁיקוּ הַיְקָבִים *heshiqu hayeqavim*; consistent with Joe 2:24.
- **"for their wickedness is great" → "for their evil is great"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי רַבָּה רָעָתָם; "evil" broader than "wickedness."
- **"the day of the LORD" → "the Day of Yahweh"** (v14) — consistent formula.
- **"the Lord will be the hope of his people, and the strength of the children of Israel" → "Yahweh is a refuge to his people, a stronghold to the people of Israel"** (v16) — Hebrew מַחֲסֶה *machseh* = refuge/shelter; מָעוֹז *ma'oz* = fortress/stronghold; more vivid than "hope/strength."
- **"the mountains shall drop down new wine" → "the mountains shall drip sweet wine"** (v18) — Hebrew יִטְּפוּ הֶהָרִים עָסִיס *yittpu heharim asis*; *asis* = sweet grape juice (same word as Joel 1:5); "drip" for *nataph*; consistent with Joe 1:5 translation of *asis*.
- **"all the rivers of Judah shall flow with waters" → "all the streambeds of Judah shall flow with water"** (v18) — Hebrew אֲפִיקֵי יְהוּדָה *afiqei yehudah*; *afiqim* = stream channels/wadis; consistent with Joe 1:20 "water brooks."
- **"for the violence against the children of Judah" → "for the violence done to the people of Judah"** (v19) — modernized.
- **"Judah shall dwell for ever" → "Judah shall be inhabited forever"** (v20) — Hebrew תֵּשֵׁב לְעוֹלָם; "inhabited" more precise.
- **"For I will cleanse their blood that I have not cleansed" → "I will avenge their blood, blood I have not avenged"** (v21) — Hebrew וְנִקֵּיתִי דָּמָם לֹא נִקֵּיתִי *veniqqeti dammam lo niqqeti*; *niqqah* = to avenge/acquit; the verse means Yahweh will settle the bloodguilt he had not yet addressed; "avenge" better than "cleanse."

### Notes
- **v10** — ironic reversal of Isa 2:4 / Mic 4:3 — the nations prepare for judgment, not peace.
- **Valley of Jehoshaphat** — "Yahweh judges"; traditionally identified with the Kidron Valley.
