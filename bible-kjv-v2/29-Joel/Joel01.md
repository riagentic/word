# Joel 1

[1] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that came to ⟦joel|Yoel|Joel⟧ son of ⟦pethuel|Petuel|Pethuel⟧:

[2] Hear this, you elders;
give ear, all inhabitants of the land!
Has such a thing happened in your days,
or in the days of your fathers?

[3] Tell your children of it,
and let your children tell their children,
and their children to another generation.

[4] What the cutting locust left,
the swarming locust has eaten.
What the swarming locust left,
the hopping locust has eaten,
and what the hopping locust left,
the destroying locust has eaten.

[5] Awake, you drunkards, and weep,
and wail, all you drinkers of wine,
because of the sweet wine,
for it is cut off from your mouth.

[6] For a nation has come up against my land,
powerful and beyond number;
its teeth are lions' teeth,
and it has the fangs of a lioness.

[7] It has laid waste my vine
and splintered my fig tree;
it has stripped off their bark and thrown it down;
their branches are made white.

[8] Lament like a virgin wearing sackcloth
for the bridegroom of her youth.

[9] The grain offering and the drink offering are cut off
from the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
The priests mourn,
the ministers of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] The fields are destroyed,
the ground mourns,
because the grain is destroyed,
the wine dries up,
the oil languishes.

[11] Be ashamed, O tillers of the soil;
wail, O vinedressers,
for the wheat and the barley,
because the harvest of the field has perished.

[12] The vine dries up;
the fig tree languishes.
Pomegranate, palm, and apple,
all the trees of the field are dried up,
and gladness dries up
from the children of man.

[13] Put on sackcloth and lament, O priests;
wail, O ministers of the altar.
Go in, pass the night in sackcloth,
O ministers of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!
Because grain offering and drink offering
are withheld from the house of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[14] Consecrate a fast;
call a solemn assembly.
Gather the elders
and all the inhabitants of the land
to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and cry out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] Alas for the day!
For the Day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is near,
and as destruction from ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ it comes.

[16] Is not the food cut off
before our eyes,
joy and gladness
from the house of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[17] The seed shrivels under the clods;
the storehouses are desolate;
the granaries are torn down
because the grain has dried up.

[18] How the beasts groan!
The herds of cattle are perplexed
because there is no pasture for them;
even the flocks of sheep suffer.

[19] To you, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I call.
For fire has devoured
the pastures of the wilderness,
and flame has burned
all the trees of the field.

[20] Even the beasts of the field pant for you
because the water brooks are dried up,
and fire has devoured
the pastures of the wilderness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv13, 14, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (vv2-20) — KJV renders entire chapter as prose; the majority is sustained Hebrew poetry.
- **"ye old men" → "you elders"** (v2) — archaic second-person plural modernized throughout.
- **"the new wine" → "the sweet wine"** (v5) — Hebrew עָסִיס *asis* = sweet/fresh grape juice, distinct from ordinary "new wine" (*yayin*); "sweet wine" better.
- **"he hath the cheek teeth of a great lion" → "it has the fangs of a lioness"** (v6) — Hebrew מְתַלְּעוֹת לָבִיא *metallaoth lavi* = teeth of a lioness; "fangs" more vivid.
- **"barked my fig tree" → "splintered my fig tree"** (v7) — Hebrew חָשַׂף חָשֹׂף *chasaph chasoph* = stripped/peeled; "splintered" or "stripped" better than "barked" (not standard English).
- **"Gird yourselves, and lament" → "Put on sackcloth and lament"** (v13) — Hebrew חִגְרוּ *chigru*; "gird yourselves with sackcloth" → "put on sackcloth."
- **"Sanctify ye a fast" → "Consecrate a fast"** (v14) — Hebrew קַדְּשׁוּ *qaddeshu* = consecrate/set apart; "consecrate" more precise than "sanctify."
- **"the day of the LORD" → "the Day of Yahweh"** (v15) — formula consistently capitalized; Day of Yahweh is a technical eschatological term.
- **"the meat" → "the food"** (v16) — Hebrew אֹכֶל *ochel* = food generally; "meat" in 1611 meant "food" but now means flesh only.
- **"The seed is rotten under their clods" → "The seed shrivels under the clods"** (v17) — Hebrew עָבְשׁוּ פְרֻדוֹת *aveshu perudoth* = seeds/grains dried up/shriveled.
- **"the garners are laid desolate, the barns are broken down" → "the storehouses are desolate; the granaries are torn down"** (v17) — modernized.
- **"the rivers of waters are dried up" → "the water brooks are dried up"** (v20) — Hebrew אֲפִיקֵי מַיִם *afiqei mayim* = channels of water/streams.
