# Joel 1

[1] The word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that came to ⟦joel|Yoel|Joel⟧ son of ⟦pethuel|Petuel|Pethuel⟧.

[2] Hear this, you elders,
and give ear, all inhabitants of the land!
Has this been in your days,
or in the days of your fathers?

[3] Recount of it to your sons,
and your sons to their sons,
and their sons to another generation.

[4] What the cutting locust left, the locusts have eaten;
and what the locusts left, the hopping locust has eaten;
and what the hopping locust left, the devourer has eaten.

[5] Awake, you drunkards, and weep,
and wail, all drinkers of wine,
over the sweet wine,
for it is cut off from your mouth.

[6] For a nation has come up against my land,
mighty and without number;
its teeth are the teeth of a lion,
and it has the jaw teeth of a lioness.

[7] It has made my vine a desolation,
and my fig tree a splintering;
it has surely stripped it bare and thrown it away;
its branches have turned white.

[8] Keen like a virgin girded with sackcloth
for the husband of her youth.

[9] Cut off are the grain offering and the drink offering
from the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
the priests mourn,
the ministers of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] The field is laid waste,
the ground mourns;
for the grain is laid waste,
the new wine is dried up,
the fresh oil languishes.

[11] Be ashamed, plowmen;
wail, vinedressers,
over the wheat and over the barley,
for the harvest of the field has perished.

[12] The vine is dried up,
and the fig tree languishes;
pomegranate, palm also, and apple tree,
all the trees of the field are dried out;
for joy is dried up
from the sons of man.

[13] Gird yourselves and lament, priests;
wail, ministers of the altar;
come, spend the night in sackcloth,
ministers of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
for held back from the house of your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
are grain offering and drink offering.

[14] Sanctify a fast,
call a solemn assembly;
gather elders,
all the inhabitants of the land,
to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and cry out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] Alas for the day!
For near is the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and like havoc from ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ it comes.

[16] Is not food cut off before our eyes,
from the house of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gladness and rejoicing?

[17] The seeds have shriveled under their clods;
the storehouses are desolate,
the granaries are torn down,
for the grain is dried up.

[18] How the beasts sigh!
The droves of the herd are bewildered,
for there is no pasture for them;
even the droves of the flock bear guilt.

[19] To you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I call;
for fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness,
and flame has set ablaze all the trees of the field.

[20] Even the beasts of the field pant for you,
for the channels of water are dried out,
and fire has devoured the pastures of the wilderness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13, 14, 16) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v15) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי, the name kept; its meaning is not certain.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, by the accents: v4, 16, 19, 20 were split into more lines than the Hebrew has and are now set as its cola.
- **"Hear this, ye old men, and give ear, all ye inhabitants of the land. Hath this been in your days, or even in the days of your fathers?" → "Hear this, you elders, and give ear, all inhabitants of the land! Has this been in your days, or in the days of your fathers?"** (v2) — Hebrew הַזְּקֵנִים "the elders"; הֶאֱזִין "give ear"; הֶהָיְתָה זֹּאת "has this been" (no "such a thing happened"); וְאִם "or".
- **"Tell ye your children of it, and let your children tell their children, and their children another generation" → "Recount of it to your sons, and your sons to their sons, and their sons to another generation"** (v3) — Hebrew סַפֵּרוּ "recount" (סִפֵּר), said once and carried through the three lines (no "let … tell" in the Hebrew); בָּנִים "sons".
- **"That which the palmerworm hath left hath the locust eaten; and that which the locust hath left hath the cankerworm eaten; and that which the cankerworm hath left hath the caterpiller eaten" → "What the cutting locust left, the locusts have eaten; and what the locusts left, the hopping locust has eaten; and what the hopping locust left, the devourer has eaten"** (v4) — four Hebrew words, each kept as its own word: גָּזָם "cutting locust" (from גָּזַם "cut off"), אַרְבֶּה "locusts" (a collective, as Exod 10), יֶלֶק "hopping locust", חָסִיל "devourer" (from חָסַל "devour", as 1 Kgs 8:37). Whether they are four kinds of locust or four stages of one is not known; the names are the plainest sense of each word. יֶתֶר "what is left" (literally "the rest of"). The same four stand in 2:25 in another order.
- **"howl, all ye drinkers of wine, because of the new wine" → "wail, all drinkers of wine, over the sweet wine"** (v5) — Hebrew הֵילִיל "wail"; עַל "over"; עָסִיס "sweet wine" (the fresh juice; תִּירוֹשׁ is "new wine", v10).
- **"is come up upon my land, strong, and without number, whose teeth are the teeth of a lion, and he hath the cheek teeth of a great lion" → "has come up against my land, mighty and without number; its teeth are the teeth of a lion, and it has the jaw teeth of a lioness"** (v6) — Hebrew עָצוּם "mighty"; וְאֵין מִסְפָּר, literally "and there is no number"; מְתַלְּעוֹת "jaw teeth" (Job 29:17; Ps 58:6); לָבִיא "lioness" (the gender is not certain).
- **"He hath laid my vine waste, and barked my fig tree: he hath made it clean bare, and cast it away" → "It has made my vine a desolation, and my fig tree a splintering; it has surely stripped it bare and thrown it away"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׂם … לְשַׁמָּה "set … for a desolation"; קְצָפָה only here, "a splintering" (or "a stump"; uncertain); חָשֹׂף חֲשָׂפָהּ, literally "stripping he has stripped her" (the fig tree): "surely"; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw", "it" supplied for English. **"the branches thereof are made white" → "its branches have turned white"** — הִלְבִּינוּ, hifil without object; שָׂרִיג "branch".
- **"Lament" → "Keen"** (v8) — Hebrew אֱלִי, feminine imperative of אָלָה, only here: to wail in grief. Kept apart from סָפַד "lament" (v13), הֵילִיל "wail" (v5, 11, 13) and אָבַל "mourn" (v9, 10). **"for the husband of her youth"** (KJV kept) — בַּעַל נְעוּרֶיהָ, בַּעַל "owner, husband": the betrothed or the husband taken from her young; not "bridegroom" (that is חָתָן, 2:16).
- **"The meat offering and the drink offering is cut off … the priests, the LORD's ministers, mourn" → "Cut off are the grain offering and the drink offering … the priests mourn, the ministers of Yahweh"** (v9) — Hebrew הָכְרַת first in the line; מִנְחָה "grain offering"; מְשָׁרְתֵי "ministers of" (מְשָׁרֵת), in their own line.
- **"The field is wasted, the land mourneth; for the corn is wasted: the new wine is dried up, the oil languisheth" → "The field is laid waste, the ground mourns; for the grain is laid waste, the new wine is dried up, the fresh oil languishes"** (v10) — Hebrew שֻׁדַּד "is laid waste" (שָׁדַד); אֲדָמָה "ground"; דָּגָן "grain"; תִּירוֹשׁ "new wine"; יִצְהָר "fresh oil"; אֻמְלַל "languish". הוֹבִישׁ (v10, 12, 17) may be read from יָבֵשׁ "be dry" or from בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"; the chapter plays on both (v11 הֹבִישׁוּ "be ashamed"); "dried up" is kept for the plants and the grain.
- **"Be ye ashamed, O ye husbandmen; howl, O ye vinedressers, for the wheat and for the barley; because the harvest of the field is perished" → "Be ashamed, plowmen; wail, vinedressers, over the wheat and over the barley, for the harvest of the field has perished"** (v11) — the Hebrew has no "O"; אִכָּרִים "plowmen" (tillers of the soil); עַל … וְעַל "over … and over".
- **"are withered: because joy is withered away from the sons of men" → "are dried out; for joy is dried up from the sons of man"** (v12) — Hebrew יָבֵשׁוּ "are dried out" (יָבֵשׁ) beside הוֹבִישָׁה / הֹבִישׁ "is dried up" (see v10: הֹבִישׁ שָׂשׂוֹן may also be "joy has shown shame"); שָׂשׂוֹן "joy"; בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man"; תַּפּוּחַ "apple tree" (the kind of fruit tree is not certain).
- **"Gird yourselves, and lament, ye priests … come, lie all night in sackcloth … is withholden" → "Gird yourselves and lament, priests … come, spend the night in sackcloth … held back"** (v13) — Hebrew חִגְרוּ "gird" without object ("yourselves" supplied for English; sackcloth is named in the next line, not here); סָפַד "lament"; לִינוּ "spend the night" (לוּן "lodge"); נִמְנַע "is held back" (מָנַע), first in its line.
- **"Sanctify ye a fast … gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land into the house of the LORD your God, and cry unto the LORD" → "Sanctify a fast … gather elders, all the inhabitants of the land, to the house of Yahweh your Elohim, and cry out to Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew קַדְּשׁוּ "sanctify" (קִדֵּשׁ); עֲצָרָה "solemn assembly" (literally "a restraint": a day held back from work); זְקֵנִים without the article and without "and" before כֹּל יֹשְׁבֵי (KJV "and" is not in the Hebrew); זָעַק "cry out".
- **"for the day of the LORD is at hand, and as a destruction from the Almighty shall it come" → "For near is the day of Yahweh, and like havoc from Shaddai it comes"** (v15) — Hebrew קָרוֹב "near" first in the line; שֹׁד "havoc" sounds like שַׁדַּי (the play kept in the order of the words; Isa 13:6 has the same line).
- **"Is not the meat cut off before our eyes, yea, joy and gladness from the house of our God?" → "Is not food cut off before our eyes, from the house of our Elohim gladness and rejoicing?"** (v16) — Hebrew אֹכֶל "food"; שִׂמְחָה "gladness", גִּיל "rejoicing"; no "yea".
- **"The seed is rotten under their clods, the garners are laid desolate, the barns are broken down; for the corn is withered" → "The seeds have shriveled under their clods; the storehouses are desolate, the granaries are torn down, for the grain is dried up"** (v17) — three words found only here: עָבְשׁוּ "have shriveled", פְרֻדוֹת "seeds" (or "grains"), מֶגְרְפֹת "clods" (or "shovels"); all uncertain. אֹצָרוֹת "storehouses" (אוֹצָר, elsewhere "treasury"); נָשַׁמּוּ "are desolate"; נֶהֶרְסוּ "are torn down"; מַמְּגֻרוֹת "granaries" (only here).
- **"How do the beasts groan! the herds of cattle are perplexed, because they have no pasture; yea, the flocks of sheep are made desolate" → "How the beasts sigh! The droves of the herd are bewildered, for there is no pasture for them; even the droves of the flock bear guilt"** (v18) — Hebrew נֶאֶנְחָה "sigh" (נֶאֱנַח); עֶדְרֵי בָקָר "droves of the herd", עֶדְרֵי הַצֹּאן "droves of the flock"; נָבֹכוּ "are bewildered"; נֶאְשָׁמוּ, nifal of אָשֵׁם "be guilty": "bear guilt" (= suffer its penalty). Many read נָשַׁמּוּ "are desolate" (as v17), with the Greek (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"O LORD, to thee will I cry: for the fire … and the flame hath burned" → "To you, Yahweh, I call; for fire … and flame has set ablaze"** (v19) — the Hebrew has no "O" and puts "to you" first; קָרָא "call"; לָהַט "set ablaze" (one root with לֶהָבָה "flame").
- **"The beasts of the field cry also unto thee: for the rivers of waters are dried up" → "Even the beasts of the field pant for you, for the channels of water are dried out"** (v20) — Hebrew גַּם "even" first; תַּעֲרוֹג "pant" (as Ps 42:1), singular after the plural; יָבְשׁוּ "are dried out" (יָבֵשׁ, as v12); אֲפִיקֵי מַיִם "channels of water" (אָפִיק).
