# 1 Thessalonians 3

[1] Therefore, no longer bearing it, we were well pleased to be left behind in ⟦athens|Athenai|Athens⟧ alone,

[2] and we sent ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧, our brother and ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ fellow worker in the gospel of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, to establish you and encourage you for the sake of your faith,

[3] that no one be shaken in these afflictions. For you yourselves know that we are appointed to this;

[4] for also when we were with you, we were telling you beforehand that we are about to be afflicted, just as it also came to pass, and you know.

[5] For this reason I also, no longer bearing it, sent to know your faith, lest somehow the tempter had tempted you and our labor should come to be in vain.

[6] But now, ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧ having come to us from you and having brought us the good news of your faith and love, and that you have good remembrance of us always longing to see us, just as we also you,

[7] for this reason we were encouraged, brothers, over you, in all our distress and affliction, through your faith,

[8] because now we live, if you stand fast in the Lord.

[9] For what thanksgiving are we able to render back to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ concerning you, for all the joy with which we rejoice because of you before our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[10] night and day beseeching beyond all measure to see your face and to complete the things lacking in your faith?

[11] Now may our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father himself and our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ direct our way to you;

[12] and you may the Lord make to increase and abound in love toward one another and toward all, just as we also toward you,

[13] so as to establish your hearts blameless in holiness before our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father at the coming of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ with all his holy ones.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v2) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 9, 11, 13) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv11, 13) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"when we could no longer forbear" → "no longer bearing it"** (v1, 5) — Greek μηκέτι στέγοντες, a participle.
- **"we thought it good" → "we were well pleased"** (v1) — Greek εὐδοκήσαμεν, the same verb as 2:8.
- **"minister of God, and our fellowlabourer" → "*Elohim's* fellow worker"** (v2) — Greek συνεργὸν τοῦ θεοῦ, read by SBLGNT and NA28; Westcott-Hort, Tregelles and RP read διάκονον, "servant of *Elohim*", and RP adds "and our fellow worker", which KJV follows. The editions are divided two against two and NA28 decides. συνεργός as Phlm 1.
- **"to comfort you concerning your faith" → "encourage you for the sake of your faith"** (v2) — Greek παρακαλέσαι (see 2:3); NA28 ὑπὲρ τῆς πίστεως, "on behalf of".
- **"moved by these afflictions" → "shaken in these afflictions"** (v3) — Greek σαίνεσθαι ἐν, "be shaken, disturbed"; the verb can also mean "be fawned upon, beguiled".
- **"we told you before that we should suffer tribulation" → "we were telling you beforehand that we are about to be afflicted"** (v4) — Greek προελέγομεν, imperfect; μέλλομεν θλίβεσθαι, the verb of θλῖψις, "affliction".
- **"lest by some means the tempter have tempted you, and our labour be in vain" → "lest somehow the tempter had tempted you and our labor should come to be in vain"** (v5) — Greek ἐπείρασεν, indicative (a thing that may have happened), then γένηται, subjunctive (a result still feared).
- **"brought us good tidings" → "having brought us the good news"** (v6) — Greek εὐαγγελισαμένου, the verb of "gospel" (εὐαγγέλιον).
- **"charity" → "love"** (v6) — Greek ἀγάπην.
- **"always"** (v6) — Greek πάντοτε stands between "you have good remembrance of us" and "longing to see us"; it can belong to either. μνεία is the word rendered "mention" in 1:2.
- **"Therefore, brethren, we were comforted over you in all our affliction and distress by your faith" → "for this reason we were encouraged, brothers, over you, in all our distress and affliction, through your faith"** (v7) — Greek παρεκλήθημεν (see 2:3); NA28 order ἀνάγκῃ καὶ θλίψει. ἀνάγκη is "necessity, distress".
- **"For now we live" → "because now we live"** (v8) — Greek ὅτι, continuing v7.
- **"for all the joy wherewith we joy" → "for all the joy with which we rejoice"** (v9) — Greek τῇ χαρᾷ ᾗ χαίρομεν, noun and verb of one root.
- **"praying exceedingly" → "beseeching beyond all measure"** (v10) — Greek δεόμενοι, "asking, begging", a different verb from "pray" (προσεύχομαι, 5:17); ὑπερεκπερισσοῦ, as 5:13.
- **"perfect that which is lacking" → "complete the things lacking"** (v10) — Greek καταρτίσαι τὰ ὑστερήματα, plural; καταρτίζω is "mend, fit out, make complete".
- **"God himself and our Father, and our Lord Jesus Christ, direct" → "may our *Elohim* and Father himself and our Lord *Yeshua* direct"** (v11) — Greek κατευθύναι, a wish; the verb is singular with both subjects. "Christ" is not in NA28 (also v13).
- **"toward all men" → "toward all"** (v12) — Greek εἰς πάντας.
- **"before God, even our Father" → "before our *Elohim* and Father"** (v13) — Greek τοῦ θεοῦ καὶ πατρὸς ἡμῶν, one article.
- **"saints" → "holy ones"** (v13) — Greek τῶν ἁγίων αὐτοῦ, "his holy ones": the word can mean holy people or heavenly beings (Zech 14:5), and the verse does not decide. NA28 adds "Amen" (ἀμήν) at the end; SBLGNT, Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NIV and RP do not have it.
