# 1 Thessalonians 5

[1] Now concerning the times and the seasons, brothers, you have no need that anything be written to you,

[2] for you yourselves know accurately that **the day of the Lord so comes as a thief in the night**.

[3] When they say, "Peace and safety," then sudden ruin comes upon them, just as the birth pang upon her who is with child, and they will by no means escape.

[4] But you, brothers, are not in darkness, that the day should overtake you as a thief,

[5] for you are all sons of light and sons of day. We are not of night nor of darkness;

[6] so then let us not sleep as the rest, but let us be awake and be sober.

[7] For those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who get drunk are drunk at night;

[8] but we, being of day, let us be sober, having put on a breastplate of faith and love, and as a helmet the hope of salvation,

[9] because ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not appoint us to wrath, but to the obtaining of salvation through our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[10] who died for us, so that whether we are awake or sleep we might live together with him.

[11] Therefore encourage one another and build up one the other, just as you also do.

[12] Now we request you, brothers, to know those who labor among you and are over you in the Lord and admonish you,

[13] and to esteem them beyond all measure in love because of their work. Be at peace among yourselves.

[14] Now we exhort you, brothers: admonish the disorderly, comfort the fainthearted, hold on to the weak, be patient toward all.

[15] See that no one repays evil for evil to anyone, but always pursue the good toward one another and toward all.

[16] **Rejoice always,**

[17] pray unceasingly,

**[18] in everything give thanks; for this is the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ for you.**

[19] Do not quench the Spirit,

[20] do not despise prophecies;

[21] but test all things, hold fast the good,

[22] abstain from every form of evil.

[23] Now may the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of peace himself sanctify you wholly, and may your spirit and soul and body be kept entire, blamelessly, at the coming of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[24] Faithful is he who calls you, who also will do it.

[25] Brothers, pray also for us.

[26] Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss.

[27] I adjure you by the Lord that the letter be read to all the brothers.

[28] The grace of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ be with you.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"ye have no need that I write unto you" → "you have no need that anything be written to you"** (v1) — Greek ὑμῖν γράφεσθαι, passive; the writer is not named.
- **"perfectly" → "accurately"** (v2) — Greek ἀκριβῶς.
- **"so cometh as a thief in the night" kept** (v2) — Greek ὡς κλέπτης ἐν νυκτὶ οὕτως ἔρχεται, present tense; ἡμέρα κυρίου has no article.
- **"For when they shall say" → "When they say"** (v3) — NA28 ὅταν λέγωσιν, without "for"; the speakers are not named.
- **"sudden destruction cometh upon them" → "sudden ruin comes upon them"** (v3) — Greek ὄλεθρος, "ruin" (a different word from ἀπώλεια, "destruction"); ἐφίσταται, present tense.
- **"as travail upon a woman with child" → "just as the birth pang upon her who is with child"** (v3) — Greek ἡ ὠδίν, singular with the article; τῇ ἐν γαστρὶ ἐχούσῃ.
- **"shall not escape" → "will by no means escape"** (v3) — Greek οὐ μὴ ἐκφύγωσιν, the strong negation, as 4:15.
- **"that day" → "the day"** (v4) — Greek ἡ ἡμέρα.
- **"the children of light, and the children of the day" → "sons of light and sons of day"** (v5) — Greek υἱοὶ φωτός … υἱοὶ ἡμέρας, no article; so also "of night", "of darkness", and "of day" in v8.
- **"as do others" → "as the rest"** (v6) — Greek ὡς οἱ λοιποί, as 4:13.
- **"let us watch" → "let us be awake"** (v6) — Greek γρηγορῶμεν; the same two verbs, γρηγορέω and καθεύδω, stand in v10 ("are awake or sleep").
- **"they that be drunken are drunken" → "those who get drunk are drunk"** (v7) — two Greek verbs, μεθυσκόμενοι and μεθύουσιν.
- **"putting on" → "having put on"** (v8) — Greek ἐνδυσάμενοι, aorist.
- **"to obtain salvation" → "to the obtaining of salvation"** (v9) — Greek εἰς περιποίησιν σωτηρίας, a noun: "obtaining" or "possession". ἔθετο, "set, appointed", as κείμεθα in 3:3.
- **"For" → "because"** (v9) — Greek ὅτι.
- **"for us"** (v10) — Greek περὶ ἡμῶν, "concerning us", read by SBLGNT, Westcott-Hort and Tregelles; NA28 and RP read ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν, "on behalf of us". English "for" covers both.
- **"comfort yourselves together, and edify one another" → "encourage one another and build up one the other"** (v11) — Greek παρακαλεῖτε (see 2:3); οἰκοδομεῖτε εἷς τὸν ἕνα.
- **"we beseech you" → "we request you"** (v12) — Greek ἐρωτῶμεν, as 4:1.
- **"to know" kept** (v12) — Greek εἰδέναι; "respect" is not in the word.
- **"are over you" kept** (v12) — Greek προϊσταμένους ὑμῶν, "stand before": lead, or care for. One article joins the three participles: the same people labor, are over, and admonish.
- **"very highly" → "beyond all measure"** (v13) — Greek ὑπερεκπερισσοῦ, as 3:10.
- **"warn them that are unruly" → "admonish the disorderly"** (v14) — Greek νουθετεῖτε, the same verb as v12; ἀτάκτους, "out of rank, out of order".
- **"comfort the feebleminded" → "comfort the fainthearted"** (v14) — Greek παραμυθεῖσθε, as 2:12; ὀλιγοψύχους, "of little soul".
- **"support the weak" → "hold on to the weak"** (v14) — Greek ἀντέχεσθε, "hold fast to".
- **"toward all men" → "toward all"** (v14, 15) — Greek πρὸς πάντας, εἰς πάντας.
- **"ever follow that which is good, both among yourselves" → "always pursue the good toward one another"** (v15) — Greek τὸ ἀγαθὸν διώκετε. SBLGNT, Westcott-Hort and Tregelles have no "both"; NA28 and RP add καί ("both toward one another and toward all").
- **"without ceasing" → "unceasingly"** (v17) — Greek ἀδιαλείπτως, as 1:2; 2:13.
- **"In every thing" kept** (v18) — Greek ἐν παντί; "circumstances" is not in the Greek.
- **"concerning you" → "for you"** (v18) — Greek εἰς ὑμᾶς, "toward you".
- **"prophesyings" → "prophecies"** (v20) — Greek προφητείας.
- **"Prove all things" → "but test all things"** (v21) — NA28 has δέ, "but"; δοκιμάζετε as 2:4.
- **"that which is good" → "the good"** (v21) — Greek τὸ καλόν; a different word from "the good" (τὸ ἀγαθόν) in v15.
- **"all appearance of evil" → "every form of evil"** (v22) — Greek ἀπὸ παντὸς εἴδους πονηροῦ: εἶδος is "form, kind" or "what is seen"; πονηροῦ can be a noun ("of evil") or an adjective ("every evil kind").
- **"the very God of peace" → "the *Elohim* of peace himself"** (v23) — Greek αὐτὸς δὲ ὁ θεός, as 3:11.
- **"I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto" → "may your spirit and soul and body be kept entire, blamelessly, at"** (v23) — Greek ὁλόκληρον … τηρηθείη, a wish with no "I pray God"; ὁλόκληρον, "entire, with all its parts", is said of what is kept; ἀμέμπτως is an adverb; ἐν τῇ παρουσίᾳ, "at".
- **"Faithful is he that calleth you, who also will do it" kept** (v24) — Greek ὃς καὶ ποιήσει; no "surely".
- **"pray for us" → "pray also for us"** (v25) — "also" (καί) is read by Westcott-Hort and NA28; SBLGNT, Tregelles and RP do not have it ("pray for us"). The editions are divided two against two and NA28 decides.
- **"I charge you by the Lord that this epistle be read unto all the holy brethren" → "I adjure you by the Lord that the letter be read to all the brothers"** (v27) — Greek ἐνορκίζω ὑμᾶς τὸν κύριον, "I put you on oath by the Lord"; τὴν ἐπιστολήν, "the letter"; "holy" is not in NA28.
- **"be with you. Amen." → "be with you."** (v28) — "Amen" is not in NA28. The Greek has no verb; "be" is supplied.
