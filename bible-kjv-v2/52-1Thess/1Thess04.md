# 1 Thessalonians 4

[1] Finally then, brothers, we request you and exhort in the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, that, just as you received from us how you ought to walk and to please ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, just as you also do walk, that you abound more.

[2] For you know what charges we gave you through the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[3] For this is the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, your sanctification: that you abstain from sexual immorality,

[4] that each of you know how to acquire his own vessel in sanctification and honor,

[5] not in passion of desire, just as also the nations that do not know ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[6] not to overstep and defraud his brother in the matter, because the Lord is an avenger concerning all these things, just as we also told you before and solemnly testified.

[7] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not call us for uncleanness, but in sanctification.

[8] Therefore he who rejects, rejects not man but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who also gives his ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ to you.

[9] Now concerning brotherly love you have no need that one write to you, for you yourselves are taught by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to love one another;

[10] for indeed you do it toward all the brothers who are in the whole of ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧. But we exhort you, brothers, to abound more,

[11] and to make it your ambition to be quiet and to do your own business and to work with your hands, just as we charged you,

[12] so that you may walk becomingly toward those outside and have need of nothing.

[13] But we do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, concerning those who are asleep, so that you may not grieve just as also the rest, who have no hope.

[14] For if we believe that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ died and rose, so also ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will bring those who fell asleep through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ with him.

[15] For this we say to you in a word of the Lord, that we the living, who remain until the coming of the Lord, will by no means precede those who fell asleep;

[16] **because the Lord himself, with a shout of command, with the voice of an archangel and with the trumpet of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, will descend from heaven, and the dead in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ will rise first,**

**[17] then we the living, who remain, will be snatched up together with them in clouds to meet the Lord, into the air; and so we will always be with the Lord.**

[18] Therefore encourage one another with these words.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v16) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v8) — Greek τὸ πνεῦμα αὐτοῦ τὸ ἅγιον; Hebrew *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness."
- **"we beseech you … and exhort you by the Lord Jesus" → "we request you and exhort in the Lord *Yeshua*"** (v1) — Greek ἐρωτῶμεν, "request" (also 5:12); παρακαλοῦμεν (see 2:3); ἐν κυρίῳ, "in".
- **"so ye would abound more and more" → "just as you also do walk, that you abound more"** (v1) — NA28 has καθὼς καὶ περιπατεῖτε, which KJV's text lacks; ἵνα stands twice in the Greek and twice in the verse. περισσεύητε μᾶλλον, "abound more"; also v10.
- **"commandments" → "charges"** (v2) — Greek παραγγελίας, an order passed on; the verb παρηγγείλαμεν, "we charged", in v11. A different word from "commandment" (ἐντολή) and "command" (ἐπιταγή).
- **"fornication" → "sexual immorality"** (v3) — Greek πορνείας, as in Matt 19:9.
- **"to possess his vessel" → "to acquire his own vessel"** (v4) — Greek τὸ ἑαυτοῦ σκεῦος κτᾶσθαι. σκεῦος is "vessel, implement": here one's own body or one's wife (1 Pet 3:7); κτάομαι is "acquire, gain possession of". The verse decides neither.
- **"sanctification … holiness" → "sanctification"** (v3, 4, 7) — one Greek word, ἁγιασμός.
- **"the lust of concupiscence" → "passion of desire"** (v5) — Greek ἐν πάθει ἐπιθυμίας; ἐπιθυμία is "desire" as in 2:17.
- **"Gentiles" → "nations"** (v5) — Greek τὰ ἔθνη.
- **"That no man go beyond and defraud his brother in any matter" → "not to overstep and defraud his brother in the matter"** (v6) — Greek τὸ μὴ ὑπερβαίνειν καὶ πλεονεκτεῖν ἐν τῷ πράγματι: infinitives with no "no one"; "the matter" has the article. πλεονεκτέω is "take more, take advantage of" (πλεονεξία, "greed", 2:5).
- **"the avenger of all such" → "an avenger concerning all these things"** (v6) — Greek ἔκδικος κύριος περὶ πάντων τούτων.
- **"forewarned you and testified" → "told you before and solemnly testified"** (v6) — Greek προείπαμεν, διεμαρτυράμεθα.
- **"unto uncleanness, but unto holiness" → "for uncleanness, but in sanctification"** (v7) — two prepositions in the Greek, ἐπί and ἐν.
- **"despiseth" → "rejects"** (v8) — Greek ἀθετῶν, "set aside"; it has no object.
- **"who hath also given unto us" → "who also gives … to you"** (v8) — NA28 διδόντα, present, and εἰς ὑμᾶς, "to you, into you" (Ezek 37:14). "Also" (καί) is read by SBLGNT, NA28 and RP; Westcott-Hort and Tregelles do not have it ("who gives").
- **"ye need not that I write" → "you have no need that one write"** (v9) — Greek οὐ χρείαν ἔχετε γράφειν ὑμῖν; the writer is not named.
- **"taught of God" kept** (v9) — Greek θεοδίδακτοι, one word.
- **"we beseech you … that ye increase more and more" → "we exhort you … to abound more"** (v10) — Greek παρακαλοῦμεν … περισσεύειν μᾶλλον, as v1.
- **"study to be quiet" → "make it your ambition to be quiet"** (v11) — Greek φιλοτιμεῖσθαι, "love honor, be ambitious".
- **"with your own hands" → "with your hands"** (v11) — SBLGNT, Westcott-Hort, Tregelles and NIV have no "own"; NA28 and RP add ἰδίαις ("with your own hands").
- **"honestly" → "becomingly"** (v12) — Greek εὐσχημόνως.
- **"have lack of nothing" → "have need of nothing"** (v12) — Greek μηδενὸς χρείαν ἔχητε; μηδενός can be "nothing" or "no one".
- **"I would not" → "we do not want"** (v13) — NA28 θέλομεν, plural.
- **"them which are asleep" → "those who are asleep"** (v13) — NA28 κοιμωμένων, present; in v14, 15 the aorist κοιμηθέντας, "fell asleep". A different verb from "sleep" (καθεύδω) in 5:6, 7, 10.
- **"others" → "the rest"** (v13) — Greek οἱ λοιποί; also 5:6.
- **"them also which sleep in Jesus will God bring with him" → "*Elohim* will bring those who fell asleep through *Yeshua* with him"** (v14) — Greek διὰ τοῦ Ἰησοῦ, "through *Yeshua*", stands between "those who fell asleep" and "will bring" and can belong to either.
- **"by the word of the Lord" → "in a word of the Lord"** (v15) — Greek ἐν λόγῳ κυρίου.
- **"we which are alive and remain" → "we the living, who remain"** (v15, 17) — Greek οἱ ζῶντες οἱ περιλειπόμενοι.
- **"shall not prevent" → "will by no means precede"** (v15) — Greek οὐ μὴ φθάσωμεν, the strong negation; φθάνω, "come before" (in 2:16 "come upon").
- **"with a shout" → "with a shout of command"** (v16) — Greek ἐν κελεύσματι, the shouted order. ἀρχαγγέλου has no article.
- **"For" → "because"** (v16) — Greek ὅτι; v15–17 are one sentence.
- **"caught up … in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air" → "snatched up … in clouds to meet the Lord, into the air"** (v17) — Greek ἁρπαγησόμεθα, "seize, snatch"; ἐν νεφέλαις, no article; εἰς ἀπάντησιν τοῦ κυρίου εἰς ἀέρα, literally "to a meeting of the Lord, into air".
- **"comfort" → "encourage"** (v18) — Greek παρακαλεῖτε (see 2:3).
