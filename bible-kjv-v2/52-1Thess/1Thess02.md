# 1 Thessalonians 2

[1] For you yourselves know, brothers, our entrance to you, that it has not been empty,

[2] but having suffered before and been shamefully treated, as you know, in Philippi, we were bold in our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to speak to you the gospel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in much struggle.

[3] For our exhortation is not from error nor from uncleanness nor in guile,

[4] but just as we have been approved by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to be entrusted with the gospel, so we speak, not as pleasing people but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who tests our hearts.

[5] For neither did we ever come with a word of flattery, as you know, nor with a pretext of greed — ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is witness —

[6] nor seeking glory from people, neither from you nor from others,

[7] being able to be a burden as apostles of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧; but we became infants in your midst; as when a nurse cherishes her own children,

[8] so, yearning for you, we were well pleased to impart to you not only the gospel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ but also our own souls, because you became beloved to us.

[9] For you remember, brothers, our labor and toil: working night and day so as not to burden any of you, we proclaimed to you the gospel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] You are witnesses, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, how devoutly and righteously and blamelessly we behaved toward you who believe,

[11] just as you know how, each one of you, as a father his own children,

[12] exhorting you and comforting and testifying, that you should walk worthily of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who calls you into his own kingdom and glory.

[13] And for this reason we also give thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ unceasingly, that, having received ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ word of hearing from us, you accepted not a word of people but, just as it truly is, a word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which also is at work in you who believe.

[14] For you became imitators, brothers, of the churches of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that are in ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, because you also suffered the same things from your own countrymen, just as they also from the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧

[15] who both killed the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and the prophets and drove us out, and are not pleasing to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and are contrary to all people,

[16] preventing us from speaking to the nations that they might be saved, so as to fill up their sins always. But the wrath has come upon them to the end.

[17] But we, brothers, having been orphaned from you for the time of an hour, in face, not in heart, strove the more abundantly to see your face, in much desire,

[18] because we wished to come to you — I, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, both once and twice — and Satan hindered us.

[19] For what is our hope or joy or crown of boasting — or is it not you also — before our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ at his coming?

[20] For you are our glory and joy.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (vv7, 14) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv15, 19) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"that it was not in vain" → "that it has not been empty"** (v1) — Greek οὐ κενὴ γέγονεν, a perfect; κενός is "empty" (3:5 εἰς κενόν, "in vain").
- **"even after that we had suffered before" → "having suffered before"** (v2) — Greek προπαθόντες καὶ ὑβρισθέντες, two participles; no "though".
- **"with much contention" → "in much struggle"** (v2) — Greek ἐν πολλῷ ἀγῶνι, the contest of an athlete.
- **"exhortation" kept** (v3) — Greek παράκλησις. The verb παρακαλέω is "exhort" where it urges (2:12; 4:1, 10; 5:14) and "encourage" where it strengthens (3:2, 7; 4:18; 5:11). The noun is elsewhere "encouragement"; here it is the appeal of the preaching itself, set against error and guile, so "exhortation" stays.
- **"of deceit … in guile" → "from error … in guile"** (v3) — Greek ἐκ πλάνης, "wandering, error"; ἐν δόλῳ, "guile". The verse has no verb; "is" is supplied.
- **"allowed" / "trieth" → "approved" / "tests"** (v4) — one Greek verb, δοκιμάζω: "test" and "approve after testing"; also 5:21.
- **"men" → "people"** (v4, 6, 13, 15) — Greek ἄνθρωποι.
- **"used we flattering words" → "come with a word of flattery"** (v5) — Greek ἐν λόγῳ κολακείας ἐγενήθημεν, literally "we became in a word of flattery".
- **"a cloke of covetousness" → "a pretext of greed"** (v5) — Greek προφάσει πλεονεξίας; the genitive is left as it is.
- **"when we might have been burdensome" → "being able to be a burden"** (v7) — Greek δυνάμενοι ἐν βάρει εἶναι, literally "to be in weight": a burden (v9 ἐπιβαρῆσαι, "to burden") or the weight of authority. In this text the clause is in v7.
- **"gentle" → "infants"** (v7) — Greek νήπιοι, "infants", read by Westcott-Hort and NA28; ἤπιοι, "gentle" ("we became gentle in your midst"), is read by SBLGNT, Tregelles, NIV and RP. The editions are divided two against two and NA28 decides. The two words differ by one letter after ἐγενήθημεν.
- **"even as a nurse cherisheth her children" → "as when a nurse cherishes her own children"** (v7) — Greek τροφός, "nurse" (one who feeds); τὰ ἑαυτῆς τέκνα, "her own". The clause can close what stands before ("in your midst, as when a nurse …", the punctuation of SBLGNT) or open v8 ("as when … so"); the verse leaves both possible.
- **"being affectionately desirous of you, we were willing" → "yearning for you, we were well pleased"** (v8) — Greek ὁμειρόμενοι; εὐδοκοῦμεν, the same verb as 3:1.
- **"our own souls" kept** (v8) — Greek τὰς ἑαυτῶν ψυχάς; ψυχή as in 5:23.
- **"ye were dear unto us" → "you became beloved to us"** (v8) — Greek ἀγαπητοὶ ἡμῖν ἐγενήθητε.
- **"because we would not be chargeable" → "so as not to burden"** (v9) — Greek πρὸς τὸ μὴ ἐπιβαρῆσαι.
- **"preached" → "proclaimed"** (v9) — Greek ἐκηρύξαμεν.
- **"holily" → "devoutly"** (v10) — Greek ὁσίως; a different word from ἅγιος, "holy".
- **"we behaved ourselves among you" → "we behaved toward you"** (v10) — Greek ὑμῖν … ἐγενήθημεν, literally "we became to you".
- **"we exhorted and comforted and charged every one of you" → "each one of you … exhorting you and comforting and testifying"** (v11–12) — the Greek has three participles and no main verb; the sentence is left unfinished as in the Greek. παραμυθούμενοι is "comforting" (also 5:14); μαρτυρόμενοι is "testifying, solemnly declaring".
- **"walk worthy of God, who hath called you" → "walk worthily of *Elohim*, who calls you"** (v12) — NA28 καλοῦντος, present.
- **"the word of God which ye heard of us" → "*Elohim's* word of hearing from us"** (v13) — Greek λόγον ἀκοῆς παρʼ ἡμῶν τοῦ θεοῦ, literally "a word of hearing from us, of *Elohim*".
- **"received … received" → "received … accepted"** (v13) — two Greek verbs, παραλαβόντες and ἐδέξασθε.
- **"the word of men … the word of God" → "a word of people … a word of *Elohim*"** (v13) — no article in the Greek; no "as" before "a word of people".
- **"which effectually worketh" → "which also is at work"** (v13) — Greek ὃς καὶ ἐνεργεῖται; ὅς can refer to the word or to *Elohim* ("who").
- **"which in Judaea are in Christ Jesus" → "that are in Judea in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v14) — the Greek order.
- **"the Jews: Who both killed" → "the *Yehudim* who both killed"** (v14–15) — Greek τῶν Ἰουδαίων τῶν … ἀποκτεινάντων, a participle with the article. It can describe all of them or mark out those who did this; the verse puts no comma. Ἰουδαῖοι is also "Judeans", the people of Judea just named.
- **"their own prophets" → "the prophets"** (v15) — NA28 τοὺς προφήτας; "their own" is not in the critical text.
- **"have persecuted us" → "drove us out"** (v15) — Greek ἐκδιωξάντων, "drive out, pursue hard".
- **"Forbidding" → "preventing"** (v16) — Greek κωλυόντων; a different verb from "hindered" (ἐνέκοψεν) in v18.
- **"Gentiles" → "nations"** (v16) — Greek ἔθνεσιν; also 4:5.
- **"to fill up their sins alway" kept** (v16) — Greek ἀναπληρῶσαι αὐτῶν τὰς ἁμαρτίας; no "measure" in the Greek.
- **"for the wrath is come upon them to the uttermost" → "But the wrath has come upon them to the end"** (v16) — Greek ἔφθασεν δὲ … εἰς τέλος: "to the end", "at last" or "fully".
- **"being taken from you for a short time in presence" → "having been orphaned from you for the time of an hour, in face"** (v17) — Greek ἀπορφανισθέντες, "made orphans"; πρὸς καιρὸν ὥρας; προσώπῳ, the same word as "your face".
- **"Wherefore we would have come" → "because we wished to come"** (v18) — NA28 διότι, "because".
- **"once and again; but Satan" → "both once and twice — and Satan"** (v18) — Greek καὶ ἅπαξ καὶ δίς; καί, "and".
- **"crown of rejoicing" → "crown of boasting"** (v19) — Greek στέφανος καυχήσεως; στέφανος is the victor's wreath.
- **"Are not even ye in the presence of our Lord Jesus Christ at his coming?" → "or is it not you also — before our Lord *Yeshua* at his coming?"** (v19) — the Greek order; "Christ" is not in NA28. παρουσία is "presence, arrival".
