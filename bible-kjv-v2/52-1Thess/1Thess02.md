# 1 Thessalonians 2

[1] For you yourselves know, brothers, that our coming to you was not in vain.

[2] But though we had already suffered and been shamefully treated at Philippi, as you know, we had boldness in our *Elohim* to declare to you the gospel of *Elohim* in the midst of much conflict.

[3] For our appeal does not spring from error or impurity or any attempt to deceive,

[4] but just as we have been approved by *Elohim* to be entrusted with the gospel, so we speak, not to please man, but to please *Elohim* who tests our hearts.

[5] For we never came with words of flattery, as you know, nor with a pretext for greed — *Elohim* is witness.

[6] Nor did we seek glory from people, whether from you or from others, though we could have made demands as apostles of the *Mashiach*.

[7] But we were gentle among you, like a nursing mother taking care of her own children.

[8] So, being affectionately desirous of you, we were ready to share with you not only the gospel of *Elohim* but also our own selves, because you had become very dear to us.

[9] For you remember, brothers, our labor and toil: we worked night and day, that we might not be a burden to any of you, while we proclaimed to you the gospel of *Elohim*.

[10] You are witnesses, and *Elohim* also, how holy and righteous and blameless was our conduct toward you believers.

[11] For you know how, like a father with his children,

[12] we exhorted each one of you and encouraged you and charged you to walk in a manner worthy of *Elohim*, who calls you into his own kingdom and glory.

[13] And we also thank *Elohim* constantly for this, that when you received the word of *Elohim*, which you heard from us, you accepted it not as the word of men but as what it really is, the word of *Elohim*, which is at work in you believers.

[14] For you, brothers, became imitators of the churches of *Elohim* in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua* that are in Judea. For you suffered the same things from your own countrymen as they did from the Jews,

[15] who killed both the Lord *Yeshua* and the prophets, and drove us out, and displease *Elohim* and oppose all mankind

[16] by hindering us from speaking to the Gentiles that they might be saved — so as always to fill up the measure of their sins. But wrath has come upon them at last!

[17] But since we were torn away from you, brothers, for a short time, in person not in heart, we endeavored the more eagerly and with great desire to see you face to face,

[18] because we wanted to come to you — I, Paul, again and again — but Satan hindered us.

[19] For what is our hope or joy or crown of boasting before our Lord *Yeshua* at his coming? Is it not you?

[20] For you are our glory and joy.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (vv6, 14) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv15, 19) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
