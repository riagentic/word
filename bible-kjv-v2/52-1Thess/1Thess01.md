# 1 Thessalonians 1

[1] Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy,

To the church of the Thessalonians in *Elohim* the Father and the Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*:

Grace to you and peace.

[2] We give thanks to *Elohim* always for all of you, constantly mentioning you in our prayers,

[3] remembering before our *Elohim* and Father your work of faith and labor of love and steadfastness of hope in our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.

[4] For we know, brothers loved by *Elohim*, that he has chosen you,

[5] because our gospel came to you not only in word, but also in power and in the *Ruach HaQodesh* and with full conviction. You know what kind of men we proved to be among you for your sake.

[6] And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, for you received the word in much affliction, with the joy of the *Ruach HaQodesh*,

[7] so that you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and in Achaia.

[8] For not only has the word of the Lord sounded forth from you in Macedonia and Achaia, but your faith in *Elohim* has gone forth everywhere, so that we need not say anything.

[9] For they themselves report concerning us the kind of reception we had among you, and how you turned to *Elohim* from idols to serve the living and true *Elohim*,

[10] and to wait for his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead, *Yeshua* who delivers us from the wrath to come.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (vv1, 3) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v10) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"Holy Ghost" / "Holy Spirit" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (vv5, 6) — Greek τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον; Hebrew *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness."
