# 1 Thessalonians 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, Silvanus, and ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧,

To the church of the ⟦thessalonians|Thessalonikeis|Thessalonians⟧ in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧:

Grace to you and peace.

[2] We give thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ always for all of you, constantly mentioning you in our prayers,

[3] remembering before our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father your work of faith and labor of love and steadfastness of hope in our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[4] For we know, brothers loved by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that he has chosen you,

[5] because our gospel came to you not only in word, but also in power and in the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ and with full conviction. You know what kind of men we proved to be among you for your sake.

[6] And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, for you received the word in much affliction, with the joy of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧,

[7] so that you became an example to all the believers in ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ and in Achaia.

[8] For not only has the word of the Lord sounded forth from you in ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ and Achaia, but your faith in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has gone forth everywhere, so that we need not say anything.

[9] For they themselves report concerning us the kind of reception we had among you, and how you turned to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from idols to serve the living and true ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[10] and to wait for his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ who delivers us from the wrath to come.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (vv1, 3) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v10) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"Holy Ghost" / "Holy Spirit" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (vv5, 6) — Greek τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον; Hebrew *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness."
