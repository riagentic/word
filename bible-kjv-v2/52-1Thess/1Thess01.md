# 1 Thessalonians 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ and Silvanus and ⟦timothy|Timotheos|Timothy⟧,

to the church of the ⟦thessalonians|Thessalonikeis|Thessalonians⟧ in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧:

Grace to you and peace.

[2] We give thanks to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ always for all of you, making mention in our prayers, unceasingly

[3] remembering your work of faith and labor of love and endurance of the hope of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, before our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father,

[4] knowing, brothers beloved by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, your election,

[5] because our gospel did not come to you in word only, but also in power and in the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ and in much full assurance, just as you know of what sort we became among you for your sake.

[6] And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, having accepted the word in much affliction with joy of the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧,

[7] so that you became a pattern to all who believe in ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ and in Achaia.

[8] For from you the word of the Lord has sounded out not only in ⟦macedonia|Makedonia|Macedonia⟧ and Achaia, but in every place your faith toward ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has gone out, so that we have no need to say anything.

[9] For they themselves report concerning us what kind of entrance we had to you, and how you turned to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from the idols, to be slaves to a living and true ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[10] and to await his Son from the heavens, whom he raised from the dead, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, who delivers us from the coming wrath.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (vv1, 3) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v10) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"Holy Ghost" / "Holy Spirit" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (vv5, 6) — Greek πνεῦμα ἅγιον, here without the article; Hebrew *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness."
- **"Grace be unto you, and peace, from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ" → "Grace to you and peace"** (v1) — NA28 ends at εἰρήνη; the longer ending is not in the critical text.
- **"making mention of you" → "making mention"** (v2) — NA28 μνείαν ποιούμενοι, without "of you" (ὑμῶν).
- **"without ceasing" → "unceasingly"** (v2–3) — Greek ἀδιαλείπτως stands between "making mention in our prayers" and "remembering"; it can belong to either. The verse keeps the Greek order.
- **"patience of hope in our Lord" → "endurance of the hope of our Lord"** (v3) — Greek τῆς ὑπομονῆς τῆς ἐλπίδος τοῦ κυρίου: ὑπομονή is "endurance" (as Titus 2:2); "of our Lord" is a plain genitive, usually understood as hope in him. "Your" (ὑμῶν) stands first and can also be the object of "remembering".
- **"in the sight of God and our Father" → "before our *Elohim* and Father"** (v3) — Greek ἔμπροσθεν τοῦ θεοῦ καὶ πατρὸς ἡμῶν, one article; it stands last in the verse and can go with "remembering" or with "hope".
- **"brethren beloved, your election of God" → "brothers beloved by *Elohim*, your election"** (v4) — Greek ἠγαπημένοι ὑπὸ θεοῦ: "by *Elohim*" belongs to "beloved". ἐκλογή is a noun; the verse has no "he has chosen".
- **"For" → "because"** (v5) — Greek ὅτι, "because" or "that" (the content of the election known).
- **"in much assurance" → "in much full assurance"** (v5) — Greek ἐν πληροφορίᾳ πολλῇ, "fullness, full assurance". "In" (ἐν) is read by Tregelles, NA28 and RP; SBLGNT and Westcott-Hort leave it out ("and much full assurance").
- **"what manner of men we were" → "of what sort we became"** (v5) — Greek οἷοι ἐγενήθημεν; "men" is not in the Greek. γίνομαι is "become" in v5, 6, 7.
- **"followers" → "imitators"** (v6) — Greek μιμηταί; also 2:14.
- **"received" → "accepted"** (v6) — Greek δεξάμενοι; as 2:13, where it stands beside παραλαβόντες, "received".
- **"ensamples" → "a pattern"** (v7) — NA28 τύπον, singular; the same word as Titus 2:7.
- **"to all that believe" kept** (v7) — Greek τοῖς πιστεύουσιν, a present participle; also 2:10, 13.
- **"your faith to God-ward is spread abroad" → "your faith toward *Elohim* has gone out"** (v8) — Greek ἡ πρὸς τὸν θεὸν ἐξελήλυθεν.
- **"what manner of entering in" → "what kind of entrance"** (v9) — Greek εἴσοδον; the same word in 2:1.
- **"to serve the living and true God" → "to be slaves to a living and true *Elohim*"** (v9) — Greek δουλεύειν, the verb of δοῦλος, "slave"; θεῷ ζῶντι καὶ ἀληθινῷ has no article.
- **"from heaven" → "from the heavens"** (v10) — Greek ἐκ τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural.
- **"which delivered us from the wrath to come" → "who delivers us from the coming wrath"** (v10) — Greek τὸν ῥυόμενον, a present participle; τῆς ὀργῆς τῆς ἐρχομένης, "the wrath, the coming one".
