# 2 Samuel 10

[1] After this the king of the children of Ammon died, and ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ his son became king in his place.

[2] David said, "I will show kindness to ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ son of ⟦nahash|Nachash|Nahash⟧, as his father showed kindness to me." So David sent his servants to console him concerning his father. David's servants came into the land of the children of Ammon.

[3] But the princes of the children of Ammon said to ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ their lord, "Do you think that David is honoring your father because he has sent you comforters? Has not David sent his servants to you in order to search the city, to scout it out, and to overthrow it?"

[4] So ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ seized David's servants, shaved off half of each one's beard, cut their garments in the middle up to their buttocks, and sent them away.

[8] The children of Ammon came out and drew up in battle array at the entrance of the gate; and the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧ and of ⟦rehob|Rechov|Rehob⟧, with the men of ⟦ish-tob|Tov|Tob⟧ and ⟦maacah|Maachah|Maacah⟧, were by themselves in the open country.

[11] ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said, "If the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ are too strong for me, then you shall help me; but if the children of Ammon are too strong for you, then I will come to help you.

[13] So ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and the people with him drew near for battle against the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧, and they fled before him.

[14] When the children of Ammon saw that the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ had fled, they also fled before ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ and entered the city. ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ returned from fighting against the children of Ammon and came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[16] Hadadezer sent and brought out the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ who were beyond the River; they came to ⟦helam|Chelam|Helam⟧, with ⟦shobach|Shovach|Shobach⟧ the commander of Hadadezer's army at their head.

[17] When it was told David, he gathered all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ together and crossed the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and came to ⟦helam|Chelam|Helam⟧. The ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ drew up in battle array against David and fought with him.

[18] The ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ fled before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; David killed of the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ seven hundred chariot-teams and forty thousand horsemen, and struck down ⟦shobach|Shovach|Shobach⟧ the commander of their army, who died there.

[19] When all the kings who were servants of Hadadezer saw that they had been defeated by ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, they made peace with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and became their servants. So the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ feared to help the children of Ammon any more.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Syrians" → "Arameans"** — as elsewhere.
- **"Hadarezer" → "Hadadezer"** (v16) — variant; same person as ch 8.

### Notes
- **Diplomatic incident** — David's gesture of kindness to the new Ammonite king is misinterpreted and insulted.
- **Beard shaving + clothes cut** — double humiliation. Beards symbolized honor; exposing buttocks shamed the messengers.
- **Two-phase war** — Ammonite/Aramean alliance defeated in two stages. David's empire consolidates.
- **Joab's generalship** (v9–12) — tactical split-force deployment; mutual-support principle. Pragmatic theology: "do what is good in his eyes."
- **Israel's position** — dominant regional power. Sets up ch 11's Ammonite siege during which the Bathsheba affair occurs.
