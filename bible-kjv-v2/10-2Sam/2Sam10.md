# 2 Samuel 10

[1] And it came to pass after this that the king of the sons of Ammon died, and ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ his son reigned in his place.

[2] And David said, "I will do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ the son of ⟦nahash|Nachash|Nahash⟧, as his father did ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with me." And David sent to comfort him by the hand of his servants concerning his father. And the servants of David came to the land of the sons of Ammon.

[3] And the princes of the sons of Ammon said to ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ their lord, "Is David honoring your father in your eyes, because he has sent comforters to you? Is it not in order to examine the city and to spy it out and to overthrow it that David has sent his servants to you?"

[4] And ⟦hanun|Chanun|Hanun⟧ took the servants of David and shaved off half of their beard and cut off their garments at the half, as far as their buttocks, and he sent them away.

[5] And they told David, and he sent to meet them, for the men were greatly put to shame. And the king said, "Dwell in ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ until your beard springs up, and then return."

[6] And the sons of Ammon saw that they had made themselves stink with David, and the sons of Ammon sent and hired ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ of ⟦beth-rehob|Beit Rechov|Beth-rehob⟧ and ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧, twenty thousand men on foot, and the king of ⟦maacah|Maachah|Maacah⟧, a thousand men, and the men of ⟦ish-tob|Tov|Tob⟧, twelve thousand men.

[7] And David heard, and he sent ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and all the host, the mighty ones.

[8] And the sons of Ammon came out and drew up for battle at the entrance of the gate; and ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧ and ⟦rehob|Rechov|Rehob⟧, and the men of ⟦ish-tob|Tov|Tob⟧ and ⟦maacah|Maachah|Maacah⟧, were by themselves in the field.

[9] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ saw that the face of the battle was toward him from the front and from behind, and he chose from all the chosen ones of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and drew up to meet ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧.

[10] And the rest of the people he gave into the hand of ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ his brother, and he drew up to meet the sons of Ammon.

[11] And he said, "If ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ is stronger than I, then you shall be salvation to me; and if the sons of Ammon are stronger than you, then I will go to save you.

[12] Be strong, and let us strengthen ourselves for our people and for the cities of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will do what is good in his eyes."

[13] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ drew near, and the people who were with him, to the battle against ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, and they fled from his face.

[14] And the sons of Ammon saw that ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ had fled, and they fled from the face of ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ and came into the city. And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ returned from the sons of Ammon and came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[15] And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ saw that it was smitten before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they gathered together.

[16] And Hadadezer sent and brought out ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ that was across the River, and they came to ⟦helam|Chelam|Helam⟧; and ⟦shobach|Shovach|Shobach⟧, the commander of the host of Hadadezer, was before them.

[17] And it was told to David, and he gathered all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and passed over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and came to ⟦helam|Chelam|Helam⟧. And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ drew up to meet David, and they fought with him.

[18] And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ fled from the face of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and David killed of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ seven hundred chariots and forty thousand horsemen; and ⟦shobach|Shovach|Shobach⟧, the commander of its host, he struck, and he died there.

[19] And all the kings, the servants of Hadadezer, saw that they were smitten before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they made peace with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and served them. And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ feared to save the sons of Ammon any more.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"children of Ammon" → "sons of Ammon"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן.
- **"the Syrians" → "Aram"** (v6, 8, 9, 11, 13–19) — the Hebrew has the name אֲרָם "Aram", with verbs now singular, now plural ("Aram saw that it was smitten … and they gathered"); "Aram of Beth-rehob", "Aram of Zoba". Not "the Arameans".
- **"reigned in his stead" → "reigned in his place"** (v1) — מָלַךְ … תַּחְתָּיו.
- **"I will shew kindness unto Hanun … as his father shewed kindness unto me" → "I will do chesed with Hanun … as his father did chesed with me"** (v2) — Hebrew עָשָׂה חֶסֶד עִם (token), as 9:1, 3, 7. **"sent to comfort him by the hand of his servants for his father" → "… concerning his father"** — Hebrew אֶל־אָבִיו, literally "to / toward his father".
- **"Thinkest thou that David doth honour thy father" → "Is David honoring your father in your eyes"** (v3) — Hebrew הַמְכַבֵּד דָּוִד אֶת־אָבִיךָ בְּעֵינֶיךָ. **"to search the city, and to spy it out, and to overthrow it"** → **"to examine the city and to spy it out and to overthrow it"** — חָקַר "examine", רִגֵּל "spy out", הָפַךְ "overthrow"; בַּעֲבוּר "for the sake of", here "in order to".
- **"shaved off the one half of their beards, and cut off their garments in the middle, even to their buttocks" → "shaved off half of their beard and cut off their garments at the half, as far as their buttocks"** (v4) — Hebrew חֲצִי זְקָנָם "half of their beard" (singular); מַדְוֵיהֶם "their garments"; בַּחֵצִי "at the half"; שְׁתוֹתֵיהֶם "their buttocks". Said as plainly as the Hebrew says it. שִׁלַּח "send away".
- **"greatly ashamed" → "greatly put to shame"** (v5) — Hebrew נִכְלָמִים, passive. **"Tarry at Jericho until your beards be grown" → "Dwell in Jericho until your beard springs up"** — יָשַׁב "dwell, sit"; צִמַּח "spring up, sprout".
- **"that they stank before David" → "that they had made themselves stink with David"** (v6) — Hebrew נִבְאֲשׁוּ בְּדָוִד (as 1 Sam 13:4); "made themselves odious" is the sense.
- **"the Syrians of Beth-rehob, and the Syrians of Zoba, twenty thousand footmen, and of king Maacah a thousand men, and of Ish-tob twelve thousand men" → "Aram of Beth-rehob and Aram of Zoba, twenty thousand men on foot, and the king of Maacah, a thousand men, and the men of Tob, twelve thousand men"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ טוֹב, "the man (men) of Tob", or a name "Ish-tob". 1 Chr 19:6–7 has other figures and adds chariots (from memory); the figures here are those of the Masoretic Text.
- **"all the host of the mighty men" → "all the host, the mighty ones"** (v7) — Hebrew כָּל־הַצָּבָא הַגִּבֹּרִים: "the mighty ones" stands beside "the host", not as its genitive.
- **"put the battle in array" → "drew up for battle"** (v8, 9, 10, 17) — Hebrew עָרַךְ (מִלְחָמָה). **"at the entering in of the gate" → "at the entrance of the gate"** — פֶּתַח.
- **"that the front of the battle was against him before and behind, he chose of all the choice men of Israel" → "that the face of the battle was toward him from the front and from behind, and he chose from all the chosen ones of Israel"** (v9) — Hebrew פְּנֵי הַמִּלְחָמָה "the face of the battle"; the written text (ketiv) has "in Israel", the reading tradition (qere) "of Israel"; the qere is translated.
- **"delivered into the hand" → "gave into the hand"** (v10) — נָתַן.
- **"then thou shalt help me … I will come and help thee" → "then you shall be salvation to me … I will go to save you"** (v11) — Hebrew לִישׁוּעָה "for salvation" and לְהוֹשִׁיעַ "to save", one root; so also v19.
- **"Be of good courage, and let us play the men … and the LORD do that which seemeth him good" → "Be strong, and let us strengthen ourselves … and Yahweh will do what is good in his eyes"** (v12) — Hebrew חֲזַק וְנִתְחַזַּק, one root twice; וַיהוָה יַעֲשֶׂה הַטּוֹב בְּעֵינָיו is a statement or a wish ("may Yahweh do"): the form allows both.
- **"fled before him" → "fled from his face"** (v13, 14, 18) — Hebrew מִפָּנָיו, מִפְּנֵי.
- **"returned from the children of Ammon"** (v14) — Hebrew מֵעַל "from upon, from against".
- **"that they were smitten before Israel"** (v15, 19) — Hebrew נִגַּף לִפְנֵי.
- **"Hadarezer" → "Hadadezer"** (v16, 19) — the printed text has הֲדַדְעֶזֶר here as in chapter 8. **"beyond the river" → "across the River"** — הַנָּהָר, the Euphrates. **"and they came to Helam"** — Hebrew וַיָּבֹאוּ חֵילָם; the word can also be read "their force" (חֵילָם); v17 has חֵלָאמָה "to Helam". **"the captain of the host of Hadarezer went before them" → "the commander of the host of Hadadezer, was before them"** — שַׂר "prince", of an army "commander".
- **"passed over Jordan"** (v17) — עָבַר "pass over".
- **"David slew the men of seven hundred chariots of the Syrians, and forty thousand horsemen, and smote Shobach the captain of their host" → "David killed of Aram seven hundred chariots and forty thousand horsemen; and Shobach, the commander of its host, he struck"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיַּהֲרֹג … שְׁבַע מֵאוֹת רֶכֶב, "killed seven hundred chariots": the men of the chariots are meant, but "the men of" is not written. The figures are those of the Masoretic Text; 1 Chr 19:18 has "seven thousand chariots and forty thousand men on foot" (from memory).
- **"they made peace with Israel, and served them"** (v19) — Hebrew הִשְׁלִים אֶת, a verb from the root of *shalom* (no noun, so no token). **"feared to help" → "feared to save"** — לְהוֹשִׁיעַ.

### Notes
- **David's chesed and Hanun's answer** — David sends to comfort; the princes of Ammon take it for spying.
- **The beard and the garments** (v4) — half the beard shaved, the garments cut off at the half as far as the buttocks: the men are "greatly put to shame".
- **Two battles** — first Joab and Abishai against Aram and the sons of Ammon; then David at Helam against Aram from across the River.
- **Joab's words** (v11–12) — each to save the other; "Yahweh will do what is good in his eyes".
- **What follows** — the war with the sons of Ammon goes on in ch 11–12.
