## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"captain" → "commander"** (v8).
- **"roe" → "gazelle"** (v18).
- **"hinder end of the spear" → "butt of his spear"** (v23).
- **"fifth rib" → "belly"** (v23) — Hebrew חֹמֶשׁ *chomesh* = under-the-ribs / belly. Debated; likely the soft-tissue region.
- **"kindness" → "loyalty"** (v5, 6) — *chesed*.

### Notes
- **Civil war** — Saul's commander Abner installs Ishbosheth ("man of shame," likely Esh-baal "man of Baal" modified). David rules Judah; Ishbosheth nominally the north.
- **Hebron** — David's capital for 7.5 years. Southern Judahite center.
- **Jabesh-gilead diplomacy** (v5–7) — David honors those who honored Saul. Political and personal.
- **Twelve-on-twelve contest** (v14–16) — possibly ritual combat / ordeal. All 24 die simultaneously; no decision. Triggers full battle.
- **Asahel's death** — Abner kills David's nephew reluctantly. Creates blood-feud with Joab.
- **Joab's character** introduced — ruthless loyalist-warrior.
- **"Shall the sword devour forever?"** (v26) — Abner's plea ends the day's fighting.
