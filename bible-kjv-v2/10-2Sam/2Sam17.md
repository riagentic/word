# 2 Samuel 17

[1] Moreover Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Let me choose out twelve thousand men; I will arise and pursue David tonight.

[2] I will come upon him while he is weary and discouraged, and will terrify him; all the people who are with him will flee. I will strike down the king only,

[3] and I will bring back all the people to you; when all return, the man whom you seek being dead — so all the people shall be at peace."

[4] The saying pleased Absalom well, and all the elders of Israel.

[5] Then Absalom said, "Call also now Hushai the Archite, and let us hear what he too has to say."

[6] When Hushai came to Absalom, Absalom spoke to him, saying, "Ahithophel has spoken after this manner. Shall we do as he says? If not, you speak."

[7] Hushai said to Absalom, "The counsel that Ahithophel has given is not good at this time."

[8] Hushai said, "You know your father and his men, that they are mighty men, and they are bitter in soul, like a bear robbed of her cubs in the field. Your father is a man of war; he will not spend the night with the people.

[9] Behold, he is hidden by now in some pit or some other place. When some of them are attacked at the first, whoever hears will say, 'There is a slaughter among the people who follow Absalom.'

[10] Even the valiant, whose heart is like the heart of a lion, will utterly melt; for all Israel knows that your father is a mighty man, and those with him are valiant men.

[11] Therefore I counsel that all Israel be gathered to you, from Dan to Beersheba, as the sand by the sea for multitude — and that you go to battle in your own person.

[12] Then we will come upon him in whatever place he is found; we will light upon him as the dew falls on the ground; of him and of all the men who are with him, not one shall be left.

[13] If he withdraws into a city, then all Israel will bring ropes to that city, and we will drag it into the valley until not a pebble is found there."

[14] Absalom and all the men of Israel said, "The counsel of Hushai the Archite is better than the counsel of Ahithophel." For Yahweh had ordained to defeat the good counsel of Ahithophel, in order that Yahweh might bring disaster upon Absalom.

[15] Then Hushai said to Zadok and Abiathar the priests, "Thus and thus did Ahithophel counsel Absalom and the elders of Israel, and thus and thus have I counseled.

[16] Now therefore send quickly and tell David, 'Do not stay this night at the fords of the wilderness, but by all means cross over, lest the king and all the people with him be swallowed up.'"

[17] Now Jonathan and Ahimaaz were staying at En-rogel; a servant-girl would go and tell them, and they would go and tell King David; for they must not be seen coming into the city.

[18] But a young man saw them and told Absalom; so both of them went away quickly and came to the house of a man in Bahurim, who had a well in his courtyard; they went down into it.

[19] The woman took and spread a covering over the mouth of the well and scattered grain on it; nothing was known.

[20] When Absalom's servants came to the woman at the house, they said, "Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?" The woman said to them, "They have gone over the brook of water." When they had sought and could not find them, they returned to Jerusalem.

[21] After they had gone, the two came up out of the well and went and told King David; they said to David, "Arise and cross the water quickly, for thus Ahithophel has counseled against you."

[22] Then David arose, and all the people who were with him, and they crossed the Jordan; by the morning light not one was left who had not crossed the Jordan.

[23] When Ahithophel saw that his counsel was not followed, he saddled his donkey, arose, went to his home in his city, put his house in order, and hanged himself; he died and was buried in the tomb of his father.

[24] David came to Mahanaim. And Absalom crossed the Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him.

[25] Absalom had set Amasa over the army in place of Joab. Amasa was the son of a man named Ithra the Israelite, who had gone in to Abigal the daughter of Nahash, sister of Zeruiah, Joab's mother.

[26] Israel and Absalom encamped in the land of Gilead.

[27] When David came to Mahanaim, Shobi son of Nahash from Rabbah of the children of Ammon, Machir son of Ammiel from Lo-debar, and Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim,

[28] brought beds, basins, earthenware vessels, wheat, barley, flour, roasted grain, beans, lentils,

[29] honey, curds, sheep, and cheese from the herd, for David and the people who were with him to eat; for they said, "The people are hungry, weary, and thirsty in the wilderness."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"wench" → "servant-girl"** (v17) — modernized.
- **"corn" → "grain"** (v19).
- **"parched pulse" → "lentils"** (v28) — MT text uncertain; LXX suggests "lentils" doubled.
- **"Israelite" → "Ishmaelite"** (v25) — 1 Chr 2:17 reads "Ishmaelite." 2 Sam 17:25 MT reads "Israelite." Likely textual variant; here following MT.

### Notes
- **Council of war** — two counselors offer strategies. Ahithophel's (surgical strike at tired David) is militarily superior. Hushai's (massive muster with king leading) flatters Absalom's ego.
- **"Yahweh had ordained"** (v14) — theological commentary. Hushai's persuasion is providentially empowered.
- **Underground network** — priest-sons as spies; the girl at En-rogel as courier; Bahurim well as hiding place. David's counter-intelligence works.
- **Ahithophel's suicide** (v23) — unique OT suicide of a private individual. Parallel with Judas (Mt 27:5): a counselor who betrays his master and hangs himself.
- **Amasa** — Absalom's general, cousin of Joab. Joab will kill him in ch 20 to eliminate rival.
- **Transjordan support** — Shobi, Machir, Barzillai provide food for David's forces. Three supporters from previous generosity: Shobi from defeated Ammon (ch 10-12), Machir who housed Mephibosheth (ch 9), Barzillai (later honored, ch 19).
