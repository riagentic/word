## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"pertained" → "belonged"** (v9).
- **"Micha" → "Mica"** (v12) — modernized.

### Notes
- **Keeping the covenant with Jonathan** — fulfills 1 Sam 20:15–17, 42. David remembers his oath.
- **Mephibosheth's humility** — "dead dog" self-description. Last of Saul's line, crippled, politically powerless.
- **"Always eat at my table"** — permanent royal provision. Functionally both hospitality and house-arrest (surveillance of Saul's heir).
- **Ziba's responsibility** — estate manager for Saul's land. Later (ch 16, 19) he'll become a problematic figure during Absalom's revolt.
- **Mica's descendants** — 1 Chr 8:34–40 lists many generations of Mephibosheth's line continuing. Covenantal loyalty preserved the line.
