# 2 Samuel 9

[1] And David said, "Is there indeed yet one who remains of the house of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, that I may do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with him for the sake of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧?"

[2] And the house of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ had a servant, and his name was ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧; and they called him to David, and the king said to him, "Are you ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧?" And he said, "Your servant."

[3] And the king said, "Is there no man yet of the house of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, that I may do with him the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?" And ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ said to the king, "There is yet a son of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, stricken in the feet."

[4] And the king said to him, "Where is he?" And ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ said to the king, "Behold, he is in the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, in ⟦lo-debar|Lo-Devar|Lo-debar⟧."

[5] And King David sent and took him from the house of Machir the son of Ammiel, from ⟦lo-debar|Lo-Devar|Lo-debar⟧.

[6] And ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧ the son of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, came to David, and he fell on his face and bowed down. And David said, "⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧!" And he said, "Behold, your servant."

[7] And David said to him, "Do not fear, for I will surely do ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with you for the sake of ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ your father, and I will return to you all the field of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ your father; and you, you shall eat bread at my table continually."

[8] And he bowed down and said, "What is your servant, that you have turned to the dead dog that is like me?"

[9] And the king called to ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧, the young man of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and said to him, "All that was ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧'s and all his house's I have given to the son of your lord.

[10] And you shall work the ground for him, you and your sons and your servants, and you shall bring in, and it shall be bread for the son of your lord, and he shall eat it; and ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧ the son of your lord shall eat bread continually at my table." And ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

[11] And ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ said to the king, "According to all that my lord the king commands his servant, so your servant will do." And ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧ is eating at my table, like one of the sons of the king.

[12] And ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧ had a small son, and his name was ⟦mica|Micha|Mica⟧. And all who dwelt in the house of ⟦ziba|Tziva|Ziba⟧ were servants to ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧.

[13] And ⟦mephibosheth|Mefiboshet|Mephibosheth⟧ was dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for at the table of the king he was eating continually; and he was lame in both his feet.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"that I may shew him kindness" → "that I may do chesed with him"** (v1, 3, 7) — Hebrew עָשָׂה חֶסֶד עִם "do *chesed* with" (token); in v3 חֶסֶד אֱלֹהִים "the chesed of Elohim". The word looks back to 1 Sam 20:14–15, where Jonathan asks David for "the chesed of Yahweh" toward his house.
- **"Is there yet any that is left of the house of Saul" → "Is there indeed yet one who remains of the house of Saul"** (v1) — Hebrew הֲכִי יֶשׁ־עוֹד אֲשֶׁר נוֹתַר. הֲכִי is elsewhere "Is it because …?" (Gen 27:36; 29:15); here no cause is asked for, and it strengthens the question. נוֹתַר "remain"; לְבֵית is literally "to the house". **"for Jonathan's sake" → "for the sake of Jonathan"** — בַּעֲבוּר.
- **"servant"** (v2, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word "slave" in every place: Ziba, the slave of Saul's house; "your servant" of one who speaks before the king; and the twenty slaves of Ziba himself. One English word is kept so that the one Hebrew word shows. **"Thy servant is he" → "Your servant"** (v2) — Hebrew עַבְדֶּךָ, one word.
- **"Is there not yet any of the house of Saul" → "Is there no man yet of the house of Saul"** (v3) — Hebrew הַאֶפֶס עוֹד אִישׁ.
- **"which is lame on his feet" → "stricken in the feet"** (v3) — Hebrew נְכֵה רַגְלָיִם, literally "struck of the two feet" (from הִכָּה "strike"; as 4:4). "Lame" is another word, פִּסֵּחַ, in v13.
- **"fetched him" → "took him"** (v5) — Hebrew לָקַח.
- **"did reverence" → "bowed down"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה.
- **"I will surely shew thee kindness … and will restore thee all the land of Saul thy father" → "I will surely do chesed with you … and I will return to you all the field of Saul your father"** (v7) — Hebrew עָשֹׂה אֶעֱשֶׂה, the infinitive with its verb, literally "doing I will do"; הֵשִׁיב "return"; שָׂדֶה "field". "Your father" of Saul is "father" for a grandfather. **"and you, you shall eat"** — the pronoun is written and stressed. תָּמִיד "continually" (v7, 10, 13).
- **"that thou shouldest look upon such a dead dog as I am" → "that you have turned to the dead dog that is like me"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי פָנִיתָ אֶל־הַכֶּלֶב הַמֵּת אֲשֶׁר כָּמוֹנִי, word for word; פָּנָה "turn (the face)".
- **"Saul's servant" → "the young man of Saul"** (v9) — Hebrew נַעַר, "boy", of an attendant "young man"; not the עֶבֶד of v2. **"thy master's son" → "the son of your lord"** (v9, 10) — אָדוֹן.
- **"shall till the land for him, and thou shalt bring in the fruits, that thy master's son may have food to eat" → "shall work the ground for him … and you shall bring in, and it shall be bread for the son of your lord, and he shall eat it"** (v10) — Hebrew עָבַד "serve, work"; אֲדָמָה "ground"; וְהֵבֵאתָ "and you shall bring in" has no object; לֶחֶם "bread".
- **"As for Mephibosheth, said the king, he shall eat at my table" → "And Mephibosheth is eating at my table"** (v11) — the Masoretic Text has עַל־שֻׁלְחָנִי "at my table" in a sentence that follows Ziba's words and has no word of saying; "said the king" was supplied by the KJV, and the earlier verse had "at David's table", which is the reading of the Septuagint (from memory). The printed text is difficult and is translated as it stands, outside the quotation marks; who says "my" is not told.
- **"a young son" → "a small son"** (v12) — Hebrew קָטָן. **"all that dwelt in the house of Ziba"** — Hebrew כֹל מוֹשַׁב בֵּית־צִיבָא, literally "all the dwelling of the house of Ziba".
- **"for he did eat continually at the king's table; and was lame on both his feet" → "for at the table of the king he was eating continually; and he was lame in both his feet"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; פִּסֵּחַ "lame".
- **"Micha" → "Mica"** (v12) — the name as the lexicon has it.

### Notes
- **The chesed with Jonathan's house** — 1 Sam 20:14–17, 42; 24:21–22. David does what he swore.
- **"The dead dog"** (v8) — Mephibosheth of himself (as David of himself, 1 Sam 24:14).
- **"Eat bread at my table continually"** (v7, 10, 13) — said three times.
- **Ziba** — he and his house work the ground of Saul for Mephibosheth. He comes again in ch 16 and 19.
- **Mica** — 1 Chr 8:34–40 gives his line.
