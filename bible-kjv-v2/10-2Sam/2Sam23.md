## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"sweet psalmist of Israel" → "delight of the songs of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew נְעִים זְמִרוֹת יִשְׂרָאֵל *ne'im zemirot Yisrael*. Lit. "pleasant one of Israel's songs."
- **"lionlike men of Moab" → "sons of Ariel of Moab"** (v20) — Hebrew אֲרִיאֵל *Ari'el* = "lion of El." Moabite warriors, possibly chiefs with this title.
- **"Tachmonite / Adino the Eznite" → "Josheb-basshebeth a Tahchemonite"** (v8) — textually difficult. 1 Chr 11:11 clarifies name. MT reading is probably corrupt.
- **"Bethlehem" well (v15–16)** — traditional site; modern tradition preserves it.
- **Uriah the Hittite** (v39) — last name in the list. Pointed: he whom David killed is remembered among the loyal mighty.
- **"God" → "El"** (v5) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.

### Notes
- **Last words of David** (v1–7) — oracle-form poem. Self-description as Yahweh's prophet-king.
- **Just ruler imagery** (v3–4) — the ideal king. Compared to light after rain — life-giving.
- **Everlasting covenant** (v5) — reference to 2 Sam 7. David's testimony that the covenant is sure.
- **Three mighty men** — Josheb-basshebeth, Eleazar, Shammah. Extraordinary individual feats.
- **Bethlehem water incident** (v13–17) — David's restraint; pours out water as offering. Recognizes "blood" of the soldiers' lives.
- **Thirty mighty men** — elite core of David's army. Named for historical memory.
- **Uriah's inclusion** reminds of David's great sin; list both celebrates and implicates.
