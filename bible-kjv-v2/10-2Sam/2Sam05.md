## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"league" → "covenant"** (v3).
- **"fort / strong hold" → "stronghold"** (v7, 9, 17).
- **"gutter" → "water shaft"** (v8) — Hebrew צִנּוֹר *tzinnor*. Warren's Shaft in Jerusalem — a water-access tunnel. Archaeologically plausible route of entry.
- **"Baal-perazim" → "*Baal-perazim* — Lord of breakthroughs"** (v20) — etymology preserved.
- **"mulberry trees" → "balsam trees"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew בְּכָאִים *bekha'im*. Uncertain plant, possibly balsam.
- **"fetch a compass" → "go around"** (v23).

### Notes
- **David king over all Israel** — 7 years after Ishbosheth's fall. Three anointings: Samuel's (1 Sam 16), Judah's (2 Sam 2), all-Israel (here).
- **Jerusalem captured** — the Jebusite stronghold. Neutral between north and south — a political masterstroke. Becomes "City of David" and capital.
- **"Lame and blind"** — Jebusite taunt that David inverts. Possibly a reference to the Jebusites' religious defenses (ritual curses) that David's men overcame.
- **Warren's Shaft / water tunnel** — archaeological discovery matches v8's "gutter" — a vertical shaft giving access to the Gihon spring. Joab reportedly led the assault (1 Chr 11:6).
- **Hiram of Tyre** — Phoenician alliance. David's international standing rises.
- **Eleven sons born in Jerusalem** — Solomon is fourth. Future king.
- **Baal-perazim / balsam-tree battles** — two Philistine repulsions. Yahweh gives unambiguous tactical direction.
