## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"and spake, saying" → "and they said, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמְרוּ לֵאמֹר. "Behold, we are …" is הִנְנוּ … אֲנָחְנוּ, "behold us … we".
- **"Also in time past" → "Both yesterday and the day before"** (v2) — Hebrew גַּם־אֶתְמוֹל גַּם־שִׁלְשׁוֹם: before, for some time past. **"thou wast he that leddest out and broughtest in Israel" → "you were the one who led out and brought in Israel"** — the qere is translated; the ketiv differs only in spelling.
- **"Thou shalt feed my people Israel, and thou shalt be a captain over Israel" → "You shall shepherd my people Israel, and you shall be leader over Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew תִרְעֶה "shepherd"; נָגִיד "leader" (1 Sam 9:16; 13:14; 25:30), kept apart from מֶלֶךְ "king" in v3. No such word of Yahweh to David is told before in these words.
- **"made a league with them" → "cut a covenant for them"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּכְרֹת לָהֶם … בְּרִית, with לְ "for". **"anointed David king" → "anointed David as king"** (v3, 17) — Hebrew לְמֶלֶךְ.
- **"David was thirty years old when he began to reign"** (v4) — Hebrew בֶּן־שְׁלֹשִׁים שָׁנָה … בְּמָלְכוֹ, literally "a son of thirty years … at his reigning". **"thirty and three years" → "thirty-three years"** (v5) — numbers in the ordinary order.
- **"went to Jerusalem unto the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land: which spake unto David" → "went to Jerusalem, to the Jebusite, the inhabitant of the land. And he said to David"** (v6) — Hebrew אֶל־הַיְבֻסִי יוֹשֵׁב הָאָרֶץ וַיֹּאמֶר, all singular: the people as one.
- **"Except thou take away the blind and the lame, thou shalt not come in hither: thinking, David cannot come in hither" → "'You shall not come in here, but the blind and the lame will remove you,' saying, 'David shall not come in here'"** (v6) — Hebrew לֹא־תָבוֹא הֵנָּה כִּי אִם־הֱסִירְךָ הַעִוְרִים וְהַפִּסְחִים לֵאמֹר לֹא־יָבוֹא דָוִד הֵנָּה. The verse is very difficult. As pointed, הֱסִירְךָ is "he has removed you" (a singular verb in the perfect before a plural subject): "but the blind and the lame have removed you" — they are enough to keep you out. The KJV reads it as "unless you remove the blind and the lame", which needs another pointing. לֵאמֹר "saying" may give the meaning of the taunt or a second saying; "thinking" is not in the Hebrew. Who the blind and the lame are is not said. The Masoretic Text is translated word for word.
- **"Nevertheless David took the strong hold of Zion: the same is the city of David" → "And David captured the stronghold of Zion; that is the city of David"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּלְכֹּד; מְצֻדַת צִיּוֹן.
- **"Whosoever getteth up to the gutter, and smiteth the Jebusites, and the lame and the blind, that are hated of David's soul, he shall be chief and captain" → "Everyone who strikes a Jebusite, let him touch the water channel, and the lame and the blind, the hated of the soul of David"** (v8) — Hebrew כָּל־מַכֵּה יְבֻסִי וְיִגַּע בַּצִּנּוֹר וְאֶת־הַפִּסְחִים וְאֶת־הָעִוְרִים שְׂנֻאֵי נֶפֶשׁ דָּוִד. The verse is very difficult and may be broken: the sentence has no end (the KJV's "he shall be chief and captain" is supplied from 1 Chr 11:6, which has other words altogether; nothing is taken from there). וְיִגַּע בְּ is "and let him touch / reach"; צִנּוֹר stands only here and in Ps 42:7 ("your waterspouts") and its meaning is uncertain: a water channel or shaft, a pipe; "hook", "throat" are also given. "And the lame and the blind" are marked as objects (אֶת) with no verb of their own. The qere שְׂנֻאֵי is "hated of" the soul of David; the ketiv שנאו is "they hate" (whom the soul of David hates, or who hate the soul of David). The words are given as they stand.
- **"The blind and the lame shall not come into the house" → "A blind man and a lame man shall not come into the house"** (v8) — Hebrew עִוֵּר וּפִסֵּחַ, singular and without the article. Which house is meant is not said.
- **"David dwelt in the fort" → "David dwelt in the stronghold"** (v9) — Hebrew מְצֻדָה, one word in v7, 9, 17. **"built round about from Millo and inward" → "built all around, from the Millo and inward"** — Hebrew מִן־הַמִּלּוֹא וָבָיְתָה; the Millo ("the filling") has the article; what it was is not known; וָבָיְתָה is "and toward the house".
- **"David went on, and grew great, and the LORD God of hosts was with him" → "David went on growing greater, and Yahweh the Elohim of Hosts was with him"** (v10) — Hebrew הָלוֹךְ וְגָדוֹל (as 3:1); יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת. "For" (the earlier text) is "and" in the Hebrew.
- **"cedar trees, and carpenters, and masons" → "cedar wood and craftsmen in wood and craftsmen in stone for walls"** (v11) — Hebrew עֲצֵי אֲרָזִים; חָרָשֵׁי עֵץ; חָרָשֵׁי אֶבֶן קִיר, literally "craftsmen of stone of wall".
- **"David perceived" → "David knew"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיֵּדַע. **"exalted his kingdom for his people Israel's sake" → "lifted up his kingdom for the sake of his people Israel"** — Hebrew נִשֵּׂא; בַּעֲבוּר.
- **"out of Jerusalem, after he was come from Hebron" → "from Jerusalem after his coming from Hebron"** (v13) — Hebrew מִירוּשָׁלִַם אַחֲרֵי בֹּאוֹ.
- **Names** (v14–16) — joined by "and", as in the Hebrew.
- **"came up to seek David … went down to the hold" → "went up to seek David … went down to the stronghold"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲלוּ; הַמְּצוּדָה.
- **"spread themselves in the valley of Rephaim" → "spread themselves out"** (v18, 22) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּטְשׁוּ (as 1 Sam 30:16).
- **"David enquired of the LORD" → "David asked of Yahweh"** (v19, 23) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאַל … בַּיהוָה, the verb "ask" (2:1). **"I will doubtless deliver" → "I will surely give"** — Hebrew נָתֹן אֶתֵּן, "giving I will give".
- **"The LORD hath broken forth upon mine enemies before me, as the breach of waters" → "Yahweh has breached my enemies before me like a breach of waters"** (v20) — Hebrew פָּרַץ … כְּפֶרֶץ מָיִם, verb and noun of one root. **"Baal-perazim"** — Hebrew בַּעַל פְּרָצִים: בַּעַל is "owner, lord" (and the name Baal), פְּרָצִים "breaches": "lord of breaches". The earlier text had this gloss in the verse; the Hebrew has only the name. **"he called the name of that place"** — Hebrew קָרָא שֵׁם, with a singular verb.
- **"there they left their images, and David and his men burned them" → "they left their idols there, and David and his men took them up"** (v21) — Hebrew עֲצַבֵּיהֶם "their idols" (1 Sam 31:9); וַיִּשָּׂאֵם "lifted them, carried them off". "Burned" is from 1 Chr 14:12, not from this verse.
- **"came up yet again" → "went up yet again"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיֹּסִפוּ עוֹד … לַעֲלוֹת, literally "added yet to go up".
- **"fetch a compass behind them, and come upon them over against the mulberry trees" → "Turn around to behind them, and come to them from over against the balsam trees"** (v23) — Hebrew הָסֵב אֶל־אַחֲרֵיהֶם; מִמּוּל בְּכָאִים. בְּכָאִים (v23, 24; 1 Chr 14:14, 15) is a tree or shrub that is not known: "balsam trees" is the common identification, "mulberry" the KJV's; the root is that of "weep".
- **"when thou hearest the sound of a going in the tops of the mulberry trees, that then thou shalt bestir thyself" → "when you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then you shall act sharply"** (v24) — Hebrew קוֹל צְעָדָה, "the sound of a stepping, a march"; אָז תֶּחֱרָץ, from חָרַץ "cut, be sharp, decide": uncertain here ("be on the alert", "act quickly, with decision"). The ketiv has "in your hearing", the qere "as you hear". **"to smite the host" → "to strike in the camp"** — Hebrew לְהַכּוֹת בְּמַחֲנֵה.
- **"from Geba until thou come to Gazer" → "from Geba until you come to Gezer"** (v25) — Hebrew מִגֶּבַע עַד־בֹּאֲךָ גָזֶר, "as far as your coming to Gezer". 1 Chr 14:16 has "from Gibeon"; the Masoretic Text of Samuel is kept.

### Notes
- **David king over all Israel** — anointed by Samuel (1 Sam 16:13), by the men of Judah (2:4), and here by the elders of Israel.
- **Jerusalem** (v6–9) — the stronghold of Zion becomes "the city of David". The account of 1 Chr 11:4–9 differs in its wording and is not used to fill out v6–8.
- **"The blind and the lame"** (v6, 8) — the Hebrew of both verses is very difficult; see Changes.
- **Hiram of Tyre** (v11) — see also 1 Kgs 5:1.
- **Sons born in Jerusalem** (v14–16) — eleven names; compare 1 Chr 3:5–8; 14:4–7, where the lists differ.
- **Two battles in the valley of Rephaim** (v17–25) — David asks of Yahweh each time.
