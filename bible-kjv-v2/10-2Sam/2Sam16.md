# 2 Samuel 16

[1] When David had passed a little beyond the summit, behold, Ziba the servant of Mephibosheth met him, with a pair of donkeys saddled, bearing two hundred loaves of bread, a hundred clusters of raisins, a hundred of summer fruits, and a skin of wine.

[2] The king said to Ziba, "What do you mean by these?" Ziba said, "The donkeys are for the king's household to ride on; the bread and summer fruit are for the young men to eat; the wine is for those who are faint in the wilderness to drink."

[3] The king said, "And where is your master's son?" Ziba said to the king, "Behold, he remains in Jerusalem; for he said, 'Today the house of Israel will restore to me the kingdom of my father.'"

[4] The king said to Ziba, "Behold, all that belonged to Mephibosheth is yours." Ziba said, "I humbly prostrate myself; let me find favor in your eyes, my lord the king."

[5] When King David came to Bahurim, behold, there came out a man from the clan of the house of Saul, whose name was Shimei son of Gera; he came out cursing continually as he came.

[6] He threw stones at David and at all the servants of King David; all the people and all the mighty men were on his right hand and on his left.

[7] Shimei said as he cursed, "Get out, get out, you man of bloodshed, you worthless man!

[8] Yahweh has returned upon you all the blood of the house of Saul, in whose place you have reigned; and Yahweh has given the kingdom into the hand of Absalom your son. Behold, you are caught in your own evil, because you are a man of bloodshed."

[9] Then Abishai son of Zeruiah said to the king, "Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? Let me go over and take off his head."

[10] The king said, "What have I to do with you, sons of Zeruiah? If he curses because Yahweh has said to him, 'Curse David,' who then shall say, 'Why have you done so?'"

[11] David said to Abishai and to all his servants, "Behold, my own son, who came forth from my own body, seeks my life. How much more now may this Benjamite! Let him alone, and let him curse, for Yahweh has told him to.

[12] It may be that Yahweh will look on the wrong done to me, and that Yahweh will repay me with good for his cursing this day."

[13] So David and his men went on by the road, while Shimei went along the hillside opposite him, cursing as he went, throwing stones at him, and flinging dust.

[14] The king and all the people who were with him arrived exhausted, and he refreshed himself there.

[15] Now Absalom and all the people, the men of Israel, came to Jerusalem; Ahithophel was with him.

[16] When Hushai the Archite, David's friend, came to Absalom, Hushai said to Absalom, "Long live the king! Long live the king!"

[17] Absalom said to Hushai, "Is this your loyalty to your friend? Why did you not go with your friend?"

[18] Hushai said to Absalom, "No; but whom Yahweh and this people and all the men of Israel have chosen, his I will be, and with him I will remain.

[19] Whom should I serve? Should I not serve in the presence of his son? As I have served your father, so I will serve you."

[20] Then Absalom said to Ahithophel, "Give your counsel: what shall we do?"

[21] Ahithophel said to Absalom, "Go in to your father's concubines, whom he has left to keep the house; all Israel will hear that you have made yourself odious to your father, and the hands of all who are with you shall be strengthened."

[22] So they pitched a tent for Absalom on the roof; and Absalom went in to his father's concubines in the sight of all Israel.

[23] The counsel of Ahithophel which he gave in those days was as if one consulted the word of *Elohim*; so was all the counsel of Ahithophel, both to David and to Absalom.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"asses" → "donkeys"** (v1).
- **"bottle of wine" → "skin of wine"** (v1).
- **"God save the king" → "Long live the king"** (v16) — *yechi ha-melekh*.

### Notes
- **Ziba's opportunism** — slanders Mephibosheth to gain his estate. David grants it without investigation — later retracted partially in ch 19.
- **Shimei's curse** — Benjamite (Saul's tribe) grievance emerges publicly. Accusation of "bloodshed" probably refers to Abner, Ishbosheth deaths (though David was not guilty).
- **David's response to Shimei** (v10–12) — remarkable restraint. Reads it as possibly divine chastisement.
- **Ahithophel's advice** (v21–22) — to lie with David's concubines publicly. Fulfills Nathan's prophecy (12:11–12). Symbolic seizure of kingship; also burns bridges with David.
- **Ahithophel's counsel "as if God's word"** (v23) — almost infallible reputation. Makes his defection extraordinarily dangerous.
