# 2 Samuel 8

[1] And it came to pass after this that David struck the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and brought them low; and David took ⟦meteg-ammah|Meteg-HaAmmah|Metheg-ammah⟧ from the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧.

[2] And he struck ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, and he measured them with the cord, making them lie down on the ground; and he measured two cords to put to death, and the fullness of the cord to keep alive. And ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ became servants to David, bearers of an offering.

[3] And David struck Hadadezer the son of ⟦rehob|Rechov|Rehob⟧, king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧, as he went to bring back his hand at the River ⟦euphrates|Perat|Euphrates⟧.

[4] And David captured from him a thousand and seven hundred horsemen and twenty thousand men on foot; and David hamstrung all the chariots, and he left of them a hundred chariots.

[5] And ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ came to help Hadadezer king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧, and David struck down in ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ twenty-two thousand men.

[6] And David put garrisons in ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ became servants to David, bearers of an offering. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saved David in all where he went.

[7] And David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and he brought them to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[8] And from ⟦betah|Betach|Betah⟧ and from ⟦berothai|Berotai|Berothai⟧, cities of Hadadezer, King David took very much bronze.

[9] And Toi king of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ heard that David had struck all the force of Hadadezer.

[10] And Toi sent ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ his son to King David, to ask him about ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer and struck him, for Hadadezer was a man of wars with Toi; and in his hand were vessels of silver and vessels of gold and vessels of bronze.

[11] These also King David sanctified to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, with the silver and the gold that he had sanctified from all the nations that he had subdued:

[12] from ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and from ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and from the sons of Ammon and from the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and from ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧, and from the spoil of Hadadezer the son of ⟦rehob|Rechov|Rehob⟧, king of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧.

[13] And David made a name when he returned from striking down ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ in the Valley of Salt, eighteen thousand.

[14] And he put garrisons in Edom; in all Edom he put garrisons, and all Edom became servants to David. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saved David in all where he went.

[15] And David reigned over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and David was doing judgment and righteousness for all his people.

[16] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧ was over the host; and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ the son of ⟦ahilud|Achilud|Ahilud⟧ was recorder;

[17] and ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ the son of ⟦ahitub|Achituv|Ahitub⟧ and ⟦ahimelech|Achimelech|Ahimelech⟧ the son of ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ were priests; and ⟦seraiah|Serayah|Seraiah⟧ was scribe;

[18] and ⟦benaiah|Benayahu|Benaiah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧, and the ⟦cherethites|Keretim|Cherethites⟧ and the ⟦pelethites|Peletim|Pelethites⟧; and the sons of David were priests.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Numbers** (v4, 5, 13) — written in words, each as the Masoretic Text has it.
- **"smote the Philistines, and subdued them" → "struck the Philistines and brought them low"** (v1) — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike"; הִכְנִיעַ "bring low" (כָּבַשׁ "subdue" stands in v11).
- **"Metheg-ammah"** (v1) — Hebrew מֶתֶג הָאַמָּה, with the article on the second word: literally "the bridle of the *ammah*" ("of the cubit", or "of the mother city"). Whether it is a place name or a figure of speech is not known. The explanation that stood in the verse ("the bridle of the mother-city") is removed. 1 Chr 18:1 has "Gath and its daughters" (from memory).
- **"measured them with a line, casting them down to the ground; even with two lines measured he to put to death, and with one full line to keep alive. And so the Moabites became David's servants, and brought gifts" → "measured them with the cord, making them lie down on the ground; and he measured two cords to put to death, and the fullness of the cord to keep alive. And Moab became servants to David, bearers of an offering"** (v2) — Hebrew חֶבֶל "cord"; הַשְׁכֵּב "making lie down"; מְלֹא הַחֶבֶל "the fullness of the cord". The Hebrew has the name "Moab" (and "Aram", "Edom" in v5, 6, 14), not "the Moabites". עֲבָדִים is "slaves", of those under a king "servants". מִנְחָה is "offering, gift"; here what a subject people brings ("tribute").
- **"as he went to recover his border at the river Euphrates" → "as he went to bring back his hand at the River Euphrates"** (v3) — Hebrew לְהָשִׁיב יָדוֹ, literally "to make his hand return" (= to restore his power, or to turn his hand against); "he" may be Hadadezer or David. 1 Chr 18:3 has "to set up his hand" (from memory). "Euphrates" (פְּרָת) is not in the written text (ketiv), which has only "at the River"; the reading tradition (qere) supplies it, and the qere is translated.
- **"a thousand chariots, and seven hundred horsemen, and twenty thousand footmen" → "a thousand and seven hundred horsemen and twenty thousand men on foot"** (v4) — the Masoretic Text has אֶלֶף וּשְׁבַע־מֵאוֹת פָּרָשִׁים, "a thousand and seven hundred horsemen"; the KJV took its figures from 1 Chr 18:4, which has "a thousand chariots and seven thousand horsemen" (from memory), as the Septuagint also has.
- **"houghed all the chariot horses, but reserved of them for an hundred chariots" → "hamstrung all the chariots, and he left of them a hundred chariots"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְעַקֵּר … אֶת־כָּל־הָרֶכֶב. עִקֵּר is to cut the sinew of the hind leg; רֶכֶב is "chariots, chariotry": the horses of the chariots are meant, but "horses" is not written.
- **"the Syrians of Damascus … slew of the Syrians" → "Aram of Damascus … struck down in Aram"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲרַם דַּמֶּשֶׂק "Aram of Damascus", the name, with a singular verb; וַיַּךְ … בַּאֲרָם "struck in / among Aram".
- **"garrisons"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew נְצִבִים; the word is also read "prefects" (officers set over a land); uncertain.
- **"the LORD preserved David whithersoever he went" → "Yahweh saved David in all where he went"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew וַיֹּשַׁע, the verb "save".
- **"shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer"** (v7) — Hebrew שִׁלְטֵי הַזָּהָב; שֶׁלֶט is a piece of armed gear, "shield" by old understanding (also read "quiver", "bow case"): uncertain. אֶל עַבְדֵי is literally "to / toward the servants"; "carried by" was an interpretation.
- **"Betah … Berothai"** (v8) — as the Masoretic Text has them; 1 Chr 18:8 has "Tibhath" and "Cun" (from memory).
- **"all the host of Hadadezer" → "all the force of Hadadezer"** (v9) — Hebrew חַיִל; "host" is צָבָא (v16).
- **"to salute him" → "to ask him about shalom"** (v10) — Hebrew לִשְׁאָל־לוֹ לְשָׁלוֹם (token). **"for Hadadezer had wars with Toi" → "for Hadadezer was a man of wars with Toi"** — Hebrew אִישׁ מִלְחֲמוֹת תֹּעִי, literally "a man of the wars of Toi". **"And Joram brought with him" → "and in his hand were"** — Hebrew וּבְיָדוֹ הָיוּ; "Joram" is not repeated.
- **"did dedicate" → "sanctified"** (v11) — Hebrew הִקְדִּישׁ.
- **"Of Syria" → "from Aram"** (v12) — the Masoretic Text has אֲרָם "Aram"; 1 Chr 18:11, the Septuagint and some Hebrew manuscripts have "Edom" (from memory; the two names differ by one letter, ד / ר). **"children of Ammon" → "sons of Ammon"** — בְּנֵי.
- **"David gat him a name when he returned from smiting of the Syrians in the valley of salt, being eighteen thousand men" → "David made a name when he returned from striking down Aram in the Valley of Salt, eighteen thousand"** (v13) — the Masoretic Text has אֶת־אֲרָם "Aram" and is translated so. The earlier verse had "Edomites": 1 Chr 18:12 has "Edom" (and names Abishai), and the heading of Ps 60 has "Edom" with Joab and twelve thousand (from memory); the Valley of Salt lies toward Edom, and v14 goes on with Edom. None of this is put into the verse. "Him", "men" are not written.
- **"all they of Edom" → "all Edom"** (v14) — Hebrew כָל־אֱדוֹם.
- **"executed judgment and justice" → "was doing judgment and righteousness"** (v15) — Hebrew עֹשֶׂה מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה.
- **"recorder"** (v16) — Hebrew מַזְכִּיר, literally "one who brings to remembrance". **"scribe"** (v17) — סוֹפֵר.
- **"Ahimelech the son of Abiathar"** (v17) — as the Masoretic Text has it; in 1 Sam 22:20 Abiathar is the son of Ahimelech.
- **"And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over both the Cherethites and the Pelethites" → "and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites and the Pelethites"** (v18) — the Masoretic Text has וְהַכְּרֵתִי "and the Cherethites", with no "over"; the sentence has no verb. 1 Chr 18:17 and 2 Sam 20:23 have "over the Cherethites" (from memory). The printed text is translated. The two names are singular collectives in the Hebrew ("the Cherethite and the Pelethite").
- **"and David's sons were chief rulers" → "and the sons of David were priests"** (v18) — Hebrew כֹּהֲנִים הָיוּ, the same word as "priests" in v17, translated as it is written. 1 Chr 18:17 has instead "the first at the hand of the king" (from memory).

### Notes
- **David's wars** — the Philistines (west), Moab (east), Zobah and Aram (north), Edom (south).
- **The cord in Moab** (v2) — two cords put to death, one kept alive. David's forefather's wife Ruth was a Moabitess (Ruth 4:17).
- **Sanctified to Yahweh** (v11–12) — the silver, gold and bronze taken or received.
- **The men about the king** (v15–18) — Joab over the host, Jehoshaphat recorder, Zadok and Ahimelech priests, Seraiah scribe, Benaiah with the Cherethites and the Pelethites, the sons of David.
- **"Judgment and righteousness"** (v15) — what a king is to do (Jer 22:3, 15).
