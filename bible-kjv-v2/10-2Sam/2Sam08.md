## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"houghed" → "hamstrung"** (v4).
- **"Syrians" → "Arameans"** (v5, 6) — standard modernization.
- **"chief rulers" → "priests"** (v18) — MT reads *kohanim* (priests). 1 Chr 18:17 clarifies "chief officials." The priesthood attribution is theologically awkward since David's sons were not Levites; interpretive translations vary.
- **"Cherethites and Pelethites"** — royal bodyguard, possibly Philistine-origin mercenaries.

### Notes
- **David's empire** — expanded kingdom: Philistines (west), Moab (east), Zobah/Aram (north), Edom (south). Reaches maximum biblical extent.
- **Moabite lot-line** (v2) — harsh: two-thirds killed, one-third kept. Contrast with Ruth's Moabite origin — David's own great-grandmother. Irony of the text.
- **Tribute to Yahweh** (v11–12) — dedicated for future temple. Solomon inherits this wealth.
- **Administration** (v15–18) — formal court structure: military (Joab), recorder (Jehoshaphat), priesthood (Zadok, Abiathar), secretary (Seraiah), bodyguard (Benaiah). Functional monarchy.
- **"Justice and righteousness"** (v15) — Deuteronomic royal ideal. David models it.
