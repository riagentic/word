# 2 Samuel 7

[1] When the king dwelt in his house, and Yahweh had given him rest from all his enemies around him,

[2] the king said to Nathan the prophet, "See now, I dwell in a house of cedar, but the ark of *Elohim* dwells within curtains."

[3] Nathan said to the king, "Go, do all that is in your heart, for Yahweh is with you."

[4] But that night the word of Yahweh came to Nathan, saying,

[5] "Go and say to my servant David, 'Thus says Yahweh: Would you build me a house to dwell in?

[6] For I have not dwelt in a house since the day I brought up the children of Israel out of Egypt to this day, but have moved about in a tent, in a dwelling.

[7] In all places where I have moved about with all the children of Israel, did I speak a word with any of the rulers of Israel whom I commanded to shepherd my people Israel, saying, "Why have you not built me a house of cedar?"'

[8] Now therefore you shall say to my servant David, 'Thus says Yahweh of Hosts: I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, to be prince over my people Israel.

[9] I have been with you wherever you went, and have cut off all your enemies from before you; I will make for you a great name, like the name of the great ones on the earth.

[10] I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in their own place and be disturbed no more; and sons of wickedness shall not afflict them any more, as before,

[11] from the day I commanded judges to be over my people Israel; and I will give you rest from all your enemies. Moreover, Yahweh declares to you that Yahweh will make you a house.

[12] When your days are fulfilled and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your offspring after you, who shall come forth from your body; and I will establish his kingdom.

[13] He shall build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.

[14] I will be his father, and he shall be my son. When he commits iniquity, I will discipline him with the rod of men, with the stripes of the children of men;

[15] but my lovingkindness shall not depart from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I removed from before you.

[16] Your house and your kingdom shall be made sure forever before you; your throne shall be established forever.'"

[17] According to all these words and according to all this vision, Nathan spoke to David.

[18] Then King David went in and sat before Yahweh and said, "Who am I, Lord Yahweh, and what is my house, that you have brought me to this point?

[19] And this was yet a small thing in your eyes, Lord Yahweh; you have spoken also of the distant future of your servant's house. Is this the law for human beings, Lord Yahweh?

[20] What more can David say to you? For you know your servant, Lord Yahweh.

[21] Because of your word and according to your own heart you have done all these great things, to make your servant know them.

[22] Therefore you are great, Lord Yahweh; there is none like you, and there is no *Elohim* besides you, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

[23] Who is like your people Israel, the one nation on earth whom *Elohim* went to redeem for himself as a people, to make for himself a name, and to do for them great and awesome things, by driving out nations and their gods from before your people, whom you redeemed for yourself from Egypt?

[24] You established for yourself your people Israel to be your people forever; and you, Yahweh, became their *Elohim*.

[25] And now, Yahweh *Elohim*, the word that you have spoken concerning your servant and concerning his house — confirm it forever, and do as you have spoken.

[26] Let your name be magnified forever, saying, 'Yahweh of Hosts is *Elohim* over Israel,' and the house of your servant David shall be established before you.

[27] For you, Yahweh of Hosts, *Elohim* of Israel, have made this revelation to your servant, saying, 'I will build you a house'; therefore your servant has found courage to pray this prayer to you.

[28] And now, Lord Yahweh, you are *Elohim*, and your words are truth; you have promised this goodness to your servant.

[29] Now therefore be pleased to bless the house of your servant, that it may continue forever before you; for you, Lord Yahweh, have spoken, and with your blessing shall the house of your servant be blessed forever."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"stablish" → "establish"** (v13).
- **"mercy" → "lovingkindness"** (v15) — *chesed*.

### Notes
- **The Davidic covenant** — foundational text. Yahweh refuses David's temple-building offer, but instead promises:
  - Dynastic "house" (*bayit*) for David
  - Son (Solomon) will build Yahweh's house
  - Eternal throne
  - Father-son relationship with the king
  - Discipline but not rejection (contrast Saul)
- **"I will be his father, and he shall be my son"** (v14) — foundational adoption-formula. Quoted in Heb 1:5 of Christ.
- **Messianic implications** — "forever" dynasty finds fulfillment in David's ultimate descendant. NT reads Jesus as eternal Davidic heir.
- **David's response** — prayer of awe and gratitude. Seven times addresses "Lord Yahweh." Does not demand, only asks for confirmation.
- **"Who am I?"** (v18) — David's characteristic self-humility at the threshold of greatness.
- **Temple-deferred** — David plans; Solomon builds. David's hands are bloody (1 Chr 22:8); peace is his son's portion.
