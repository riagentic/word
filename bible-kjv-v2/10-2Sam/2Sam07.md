# 2 Samuel 7

[1] And it came to pass, when the king dwelt in his house, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had given him rest all around from all his enemies,

[2] that the king said to ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ the prophet, "See, please, I am dwelling in a house of cedars, and the ark of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is dwelling in the midst of the curtain."

[3] And ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ said to the king, "All that is in your heart, go, do, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is with you."

[4] And it came to pass in that night that the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧, saying,

[5] "Go and say to my servant, to David, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Will you build for me a house for my dwelling?

[6] For I have not dwelt in a house from the day that I brought up the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ even to this day, and I have been walking about in a tent and in a dwelling.

[7] In all where I have walked about among all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, did I speak a word with one of the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whom I commanded to shepherd my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "Why have you not built for me a house of cedars?"'

[8] And now, thus you shall say to my servant, to David, 'Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: I took you from the pasture, from after the flock, to be leader over my people, over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[9] And I was with you in all where you went, and I cut off all your enemies from before you; and I will make for you a great name, like the name of the great ones who are on the earth.

[10] And I will set a place for my people, for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I will plant it, and it shall abide in its place and shall be agitated no more; and sons of injustice shall not afflict it again, as at the first,

[11] and from the day that I commanded judges over my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and I will give you rest from all your enemies. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ tells you that a house ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will make for you.

[12] When your days are full and you lie with your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, who shall come out of your inward parts, and I will establish his kingdom.

[13] He shall build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.

[14] I will be to him for a father, and he shall be to me for a son; when he does iniquity, I will reprove him with the rod of men and with the strokes of the sons of man.

[15] And my ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ shall not depart from him, as I removed it from ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, whom I removed from before you.

[16] And your house and your kingdom shall be sure forever before you; your throne shall be established forever.'"

[17] According to all these words and according to all this vision, so ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ spoke to David.

[18] And King David came and sat before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he said, "Who am I, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, and who is my house, that you have brought me as far as here?

[19] And this was yet small in your eyes, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, and you have spoken also to the house of your servant of what is far off; and this is ⟦torah|the torah|the law⟧ of man, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧.

[20] And what more shall David yet speak to you? And you know your servant, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧.

[21] For the sake of your word and according to your heart you have done all this greatness, to make your servant know.

[22] Therefore you are great, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, for there is none like you, and there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ besides you, in all that we have heard with our ears.

[23] And who is like your people, like ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, one nation on the earth, whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ went to ransom for himself as a people, and to set for himself a name, and to do for you the greatness, and fearsome deeds for your land, from before your people, whom you ransomed for yourself from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, nations and its gods?

[24] And you established for yourself your people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ as a people for yourself forever; and you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, became ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them.

[25] And now, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the word that you have spoken concerning your servant and concerning his house, make it stand forever, and do as you have spoken.

[26] And let your name be great forever, saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧'; and the house of your servant David shall be established before you.

[27] For you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, have uncovered the ear of your servant, saying, 'A house I will build for you'; therefore your servant has found his heart to pray to you this prayer.

[28] And now, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, you are ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and your words shall be truth, and you have spoken to your servant this good.

[29] And now, be willing and bless the house of your servant, to be forever before you; for you, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, have spoken, and from your blessing the house of your servant shall be blessed forever."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v2 and v28 the Hebrew has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים "the Elohim".
- **"O Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v18, 19 twice, 20, 22, 28, 29) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai YHWH*, both words, seven times in the prayer. In v22 the earlier text had "Yahweh Elohim"; the printed Hebrew has אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה there too. "Yahweh Elohim" (יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים) stands only in v25.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"house"** (v1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 11, 13, 16, 18, 19, 25, 26, 27, 29) — Hebrew בַּיִת every time, and "house" every time: the house David dwells in, the house he would build for Yahweh, and the house (a line of sons) Yahweh will make for David are one word, and the passage turns on it.
- **"sat in his house … had given him rest round about from all his enemies" → "dwelt in his house … had given him rest all around from all his enemies"** (v1) — Hebrew יָשַׁב "dwell, sit"; מִסָּבִיב "from around".
- **"I dwell in an house of cedar, but the ark of God dwelleth within curtains" → "I am dwelling in a house of cedars, and the ark of Elohim is dwelling in the midst of the curtain"** (v2) — the same verb יָשַׁב for the king and for the ark; אֲרָזִים is plural; הַיְרִיעָה "the curtain" is singular (a length of tent cloth, as Exod 26:1); וְ is "and".
- **"Shalt thou build me an house for me to dwell in?" → "Will you build for me a house for my dwelling?"** (v5) — Hebrew הַאַתָּה תִּבְנֶה: a question, with the stress on "you". 1 Chr 17:4 has a statement, "Not you shall build" (from memory).
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v6, 7) — בְּנֵי.
- **"but have walked in a tent and in a tabernacle" → "and I have been walking about in a tent and in a dwelling"** (v6) — Hebrew מִתְהַלֵּךְ "walking about"; מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling".
- **"with any of the tribes of Israel"** → **"with one of the tribes of Israel"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶת־אַחַד שִׁבְטֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. The earlier verse had "rulers": 1 Chr 17:6 has "judges" (שֹׁפְטֵי for שִׁבְטֵי, one letter; from memory). The Masoretic Text of Samuel has "tribes" and is translated. שֵׁבֶט is also "rod, scepter".
- **"I took thee from the sheepcote, from following the sheep, to be ruler" → "I took you from the pasture, from after the flock, to be leader"** (v8) — Hebrew נָוֶה "pasture, abode"; מֵאַחַר הַצֹּאן "from behind the flock"; נָגִיד "leader" (as 1 Sam 13:14), not "prince", "ruler".
- **"have made thee a great name" → "I will make for you a great name"** (v9) — Hebrew וְעָשִׂתִי: after the past forms of v8–9a the verbs turn to the form that looks forward (v9b–11).
- **"I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in a place of their own, and move no more; neither shall the children of wickedness afflict them any more, as beforetime" → "I will set a place for my people, for Israel, and I will plant it, and it shall abide in its place and shall be agitated no more; and sons of injustice shall not afflict it again, as at the first"** (v10) — Hebrew שִׂים "put, set"; the people is spoken of in the singular ("him / it"); שָׁכַן "abide"; תַּחְתָּיו "in its place" (literally "under itself"); רָגַז "be agitated, tremble"; בְּנֵי־עַוְלָה "sons of injustice"; לֹא־יֹסִיפוּ … לְעַנּוֹתוֹ literally "shall not add to afflict it".
- **"And as since the time that I commanded judges … Also the LORD telleth thee that he will make thee an house" → "and from the day that I commanded judges … And Yahweh tells you that a house Yahweh will make for you"** (v11) — Hebrew וּלְמִן־הַיּוֹם "and from the day", running on from v10. וְהִגִּיד לְךָ יְהוָה: the speech passes into the third person; "Yahweh" stands twice. "A house" is put first in the Hebrew: David is not to build Yahweh a house; Yahweh will make David a house.
- **"when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels" → "When your days are full and you lie with your fathers, I will raise up your seed after you, who shall come out of your inward parts"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁכַב "lie"; הֵקִים "raise up"; זֶרַע "seed", a singular collective word (one son or a line of sons: the word does not decide); מֵעִים "inward parts" (as Gen 15:4).
- **"establish … stablish"** (v12, 13, 16, 24, 26) — Hebrew הֲכִינֹתִי (v12), כֹנַנְתִּי (v13), נָכוֹן (v16, 26), וַתְּכוֹנֵן (v24): forms of the one root כּוּן, "establish" each time. הָקֵם in v25 is another verb, "make stand".
- **"I will be his father, and he shall be my son. If he commit iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the children of men" → "I will be to him for a father, and he shall be to me for a son; when he does iniquity, I will reprove him with the rod of men and with the strokes of the sons of man"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲנִי אֶהְיֶה־לּוֹ לְאָב וְהוּא יִהְיֶה־לִּי לְבֵן, word for word. The second half is אֲשֶׁר בְּהַעֲוֹתוֹ וְהֹכַחְתִּיו, literally "who, in his doing iniquity, I will reprove him": it is said of the same "he" as the first half. הוֹכִיחַ "reprove"; שֵׁבֶט אֲנָשִׁים "the rod of men"; נִגְעֵי "strokes" (נֶגַע, elsewhere "plague": a blow dealt); בְּנֵי אָדָם "the sons of man". Heb 1:5 cites the first half in the Greek form ("I will be to him for a father, and he shall be to me for a son"); the verse here is translated from the Hebrew, both halves alike.
- **"my mercy shall not depart away from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I put away before thee" → "my chesed shall not depart from him, as I removed it from Saul, whom I removed from before you"** (v15) — Hebrew חֶסֶד (token); לֹא־יָסוּר "shall not depart" (qal: the chesed is the subject); הֲסִרֹתִי "I removed", the same verb twice ("it" is supplied the first time; the Hebrew has "as I removed from with Saul"). 1 Chr 17:13 and the Septuagint have "I will not remove" and do not name Saul (from memory).
- **"thine house and thy kingdom shall be established for ever before thee: thy throne shall be established" → "your house and your kingdom shall be sure forever before you; your throne shall be established"** (v16) — two words: נֶאְמַן "sure, firm, faithful" (as 1 Sam 2:35 "a sure house") and נָכוֹן "established". לְפָנֶיךָ "before you" is what the Masoretic Text has; the Septuagint and some Hebrew manuscripts have "before me" (from memory).
- **"so did Nathan speak"** (v17) — כֵּן "so" is kept; חִזָּיוֹן "vision".
- **"Then went king David in … what is my house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?" → "And King David came … who is my house, that you have brought me as far as here?"** (v18) — Hebrew וּמִי בֵיתִי "and who is my house"; עַד־הֲלֹם "to here".
- **"thou hast spoken also of thy servant's house for a great while to come. And is this the manner of man, O Lord GOD?" → "you have spoken also to the house of your servant of what is far off; and this is the torah of man, Lord Yahweh"** (v19) — Hebrew אֶל־בֵּית־עַבְדְּךָ לְמֵרָחוֹק, literally "to the house of your servant, to from-afar" (of time far off). וְזֹאת תּוֹרַת הָאָדָם is translated as it stands: "and this is the torah of the man" (תּוֹרָה "instruction, law", as in "this is the torah of the burnt offering", Lev 6:9; הָאָדָם "man, mankind", with the article). The clause is difficult and its sense is disputed: a statement, an exclamation or a question ("Is this the manner of man?"); the Hebrew has no mark of a question. 1 Chr 17:17 has other words here ("you have looked upon me as the rank of the man on high"; from memory, and itself difficult). Nothing is smoothed and nothing is added.
- **"what can David say more unto thee?" → "what more shall David yet speak to you?"** (v20) — Hebrew וּמַה־יּוֹסִיף דָּוִד עוֹד לְדַבֵּר, literally "what shall David add yet to speak". **"for thou … knowest" → "And you know"** — וְ.
- **"For thy word's sake, and according to thine own heart, hast thou done all these great things, to make thy servant know them" → "For the sake of your word and according to your heart you have done all this greatness, to make your servant know"** (v21) — Hebrew הַגְּדוּלָּה הַזֹּאת "this greatness", singular; "them" is not written. 1 Chr 17:19 has "for the sake of your servant" (from memory).
- **"according to all that we have heard" → "in all that we have heard"** (v22) — Hebrew בְּכֹל.
- **v23** — the Masoretic Text is difficult and is translated word by word: (1) הָלְכוּ־אֱלֹהִים, "Elohim went", has the verb in the plural ("went" of God, or "gods went"); (2) "for himself" (לוֹ, twice) turns into "for you" in the plural (לָכֶם) and then into "your land", "your people", "you ransomed" in the singular, spoken to Elohim; (3) הַגְּדוּלָּה וְנֹרָאוֹת "the greatness and fearsome deeds"; (4) מִפְּנֵי עַמְּךָ "from before your people" has no verb before it; (5) the last words גּוֹיִם וֵאלֹהָיו are "nations and its gods" ("his / its", singular). 1 Chr 17:21 has "to drive out nations from before your people" (from memory), and the Septuagint has other differences; the earlier verse had taken "by driving out" and "for them" from there. Nothing of that is in the verse now. פָּדָה is "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem"). **"what one nation in the earth is like thy people" → "who is like your people, like Israel, one nation on the earth"** — Hebrew וּמִי כְעַמְּךָ כְּיִשְׂרָאֵל גּוֹי אֶחָד בָּאָרֶץ.
- **"thou hast confirmed to thyself thy people Israel to be a people unto thee for ever: and thou, LORD, art become their God" → "you established for yourself your people Israel as a people for yourself forever; and you, Yahweh, became Elohim to them"** (v24) — Hebrew לְךָ לְעָם "for yourself for a people"; הָיִיתָ לָהֶם לֵאלֹהִים literally "became to them for Elohim" (the form of v14).
- **"establish it for ever" → "make it stand forever"** (v25) — Hebrew הָקֵם "make stand, raise up" (the verb of v12 "raise up").
- **"let thy name be magnified" → "let your name be great"** (v26) — Hebrew וְיִגְדַּל (the root of v22 "you are great").
- **"hast revealed to thy servant … therefore hath thy servant found in his heart to pray this prayer unto thee" → "have uncovered the ear of your servant … therefore your servant has found his heart to pray to you this prayer"** (v27) — Hebrew גָּלִיתָה אֶת־אֹזֶן "uncovered the ear" (as 1 Sam 9:15); מָצָא … אֶת־לִבּוֹ "found his heart" ("courage" is an interpretation). **"A house I will build for you"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"thou art that God, and thy words be true, and thou hast promised this goodness" → "you are Elohim, and your words shall be truth, and you have spoken to your servant this good"** (v28) — Hebrew אַתָּה־הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים, literally "you are he, the Elohim"; יִהְיוּ אֱמֶת "shall be / are truth" (the form looks forward); דִּבֶּר "speak" (Hebrew has no word "promise"); הַטּוֹבָה "the good".
- **"let it please thee to bless … that it may continue for ever before thee … with thy blessing let the house of thy servant be blessed" → "be willing and bless … to be forever before you … from your blessing the house of your servant shall be blessed"** (v29) — Hebrew הוֹאֵל וּבָרֵךְ, two imperatives; לִהְיוֹת "to be"; מִבִּרְכָתְךָ "from your blessing"; יְבֹרַךְ "shall be blessed" (or "let it be blessed").

### Notes
- **The promise to David** — Yahweh does not ask David for a house, and promises instead:
  - a "house" (*bayit*) for David
  - David's seed will build a house for Yahweh's name
  - the throne of his kingdom established forever
  - "I will be to him for a father, and he shall be to me for a son"
  - reproof when he does iniquity, but the *chesed* does not depart (unlike Saul)
- **"I will be to him for a father, and he shall be to me for a son"** (v14) — cited in Heb 1:5 (and compare 2 Cor 6:18; Rev 21:7). The parallel account is 1 Chr 17; Ps 89 and Ps 132 take up the promise.
- **"forever"** (v13, 16, 24, 25, 26, 29) — Hebrew עַד־עוֹלָם / לְעוֹלָם, "to the age": time out of sight.
- **David's response** — he sits before Yahweh. Seven times he addresses "Lord Yahweh" (*Adonai YHWH*). He asks only that what was spoken be made to stand.
- **"Who am I?"** (v18) — David before Yahweh.
- **The house deferred** — David's seed builds (1 Kgs 5–8). 1 Chr 22:8 gives a reason that this chapter does not give.
