## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"stablish" → "establish"** (v13).
- **"mercy" → "lovingkindness"** (v15) — *chesed*.
- **"O LORD God" → "Yahweh *Elohim*"** (v22) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים *YHWH Elohim* (as in v25), not אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה *Adonai YHWH* as in v18–20, 28–29.

### Notes
- **The Davidic covenant** — foundational text. Yahweh refuses David's temple-building offer, but instead promises:
  - Dynastic "house" (*bayit*) for David
  - Son (Solomon) will build Yahweh's house
  - Eternal throne
  - Father-son relationship with the king
  - Discipline but not rejection (contrast Saul)
- **"I will be his father, and he shall be my son"** (v14) — foundational adoption-formula. Quoted in Heb 1:5 of Christ.
- **Messianic implications** — "forever" dynasty finds fulfillment in David's ultimate descendant. NT reads Jesus as eternal Davidic heir.
- **David's response** — prayer of awe and gratitude. Six times addresses "Lord Yahweh." Does not demand, only asks for confirmation.
- **"Who am I?"** (v18) — David's characteristic self-humility at the threshold of greatness.
- **Temple-deferred** — David plans; Solomon builds. David's hands are bloody (1 Chr 22:8); peace is his son's portion.
