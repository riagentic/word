## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v17, 20) — *mal'akh*.
- **"revengers of blood" → "avenger of blood"** (v11) — *go'el ha-dam*.

### Notes
- **Joab's ruse** — sends the wise woman of Tekoa with a prepared fictional case that mirrors David's dilemma with Absalom. Same method as Nathan's parable (ch 12).
- **"*Elohim* devises plans that a banished one is not expelled"** (v14) — remarkable theology. Quoted in context of reconciliation-theology; echoes in Christian redemption-readings.
- **Absalom's beauty** (v25) — no blemish. Heavy hair (traditionally vanity-signal) — which later kills him (ch 18, caught in a tree).
- **Partial reconciliation** (v24) — Absalom returns but doesn't see David's face. Two years of suspended status.
- **Joab's field burned** (v30) — Absalom's dramatic force-move to get attention. Pattern of Absalom's aggressive problem-solving.
- **King's kiss** (v33) — formal restoration. But the wound is not healed; Absalom's coming revolt builds.
