## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"on all manner of instruments made of fir wood" → "with all [instruments of] cypress wood"** (v5) — MT בְּכֹל עֲצֵי בְרוֹשִׁים "with all cypress woods" (*berosh*, cypress, as 1 Kgs 5:8); "instruments" is supplied in brackets. 1 Chr 13:8, 4QSam^a and the LXX read "with all (their) might and with songs" (בְּכָל־עֹז וּבְשִׁירִים); MT kept as the base, as it is intelligible in context.
- **"cornets" → "castanets"** (v5) — Hebrew *mena'an'im*. Percussive rattles.
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v5) — *nevalim*.
- **"flagon of wine" → "cake of raisins"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲשִׁישָׁה *ashishah*. Likely a raisin-cake, not a wine flask.
- **"vain fellows" → "vulgar fellows"** (v20).

### Notes
- **First attempt to move the ark** — on an ox-cart, like the Philistines (1 Sam 6). Wrong method: the ark was to be carried by poles on Kohathites' shoulders (Num 4).
- **Uzzah's death** — touched the ark. The Torah's safeguards were not followed. Divine holiness and ritual mishandling collide.
- **Three-month interlude** at Obed-edom's — demonstrates that the ark is a blessing, not a curse, when properly handled.
- **Second attempt** — with correct method (by carriers on shoulders); sacrifices after six paces; David dancing in linen ephod.
- **David's dance** — priestly dress (*linen ephod*) + king's joy. Highly charged with holy-celebration.
- **Michal's contempt** — resents David's "exposure." Deeper tension: Saul's daughter vs David's kingship. Her barrenness (v23) is both personal loss and dynastic signal — Saul's line dies without extending through her.
- **Davidic tent-shrine** (v17) — David's tent houses the ark in Jerusalem. Not yet the Solomonic temple.
