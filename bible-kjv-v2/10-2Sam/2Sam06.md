## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In every place of this chapter (v2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 12) the Hebrew has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים "the Elohim".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **The titles of the ark** — kept as the Hebrew varies them: "the ark of the Elohim" (v2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 12 twice), "the ark" (v4), "the ark of Yahweh" (v9, 10, 11, 13, 15, 16, 17). The word אֲרוֹן is "chest".
- **"Again, David gathered together all the chosen men of Israel" → "And David again gathered every chosen man in Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף עוֹד דָּוִד אֶת־כָּל־בָּחוּר בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל. The verb as pointed looks like "and he added" (יָסַף); with the object it is taken as a short writing of וַיֶּאֱסֹף "and he gathered". בָּחוּר is singular and collective, "chosen man" (or "young man": the same word).
- **"from Baale of Judah" → "from Baale-judah"** (v2) — Hebrew מִבַּעֲלֵי יְהוּדָה, translated as the Masoretic Text prints it. The words can also be read "from the lords of Judah" (בַּעֲלֵי as in Josh 24:11), but "from there" asks for a place. The earlier verse had "Baalah", which is the reading of 1 Chr 13:6 ("to Baalah, to Kiriath-jearim, which belongs to Judah"; from memory), not of this verse.
- **"whose name is called by the name of the LORD of hosts that dwelleth between the cherubims" → "upon which a name is called, the name of Yahweh of Hosts, who sits upon the cherubim"** (v2) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־נִקְרָא שֵׁם שֵׁם יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת יֹשֵׁב הַכְּרֻבִים עָלָיו: the word "name" stands twice, and עָלָיו "upon it" at the end belongs to "is called". The Septuagint and 1 Chr 13:6 have "name" once (from memory). יֹשֵׁב הַכְּרֻבִים has no preposition; "upon" is supplied (as 1 Sam 4:4).
- **"set the ark of God upon a new cart, and brought it out" → "made the ark of Elohim ride on a new wagon, and they carried it"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיַּרְכִּבוּ "made ride"; עֲגָלָה "wagon" (as 1 Sam 6:7); נָשָׂא "lift up, carry". בַּגִּבְעָה "on the hill" may be the place name Gibeah (as 1 Sam 7:1).
- **"drave the new cart" → "were driving the wagon, a new one"** (v3) and **"they carried it … with the ark of Elohim"** (v4) — the Masoretic Text is difficult here. In v3 the last word חֲדָשָׁה "new" has no article after "the wagon"; v4a repeats the words of v3 ("and they carried it from the house of Abinadab, which was on the hill") and then says "with the ark of the Elohim", although "it" is the ark. The Septuagint and a Qumran scroll have a shorter text without the repeated words (from memory), and many take the repetition as a copyist's doubling. The printed text is translated as it stands. וְאַחְיוֹ can also be read "and his brother(s)".
- **"played before the LORD on all manner of instruments made of fir wood" → "were playing before Yahweh with all woods of cypresses"** (v5) — Hebrew מְשַׂחֲקִים "playing, making merry" (the verb of v21); בְּכֹל עֲצֵי בְרוֹשִׁים, word for word "with all woods of cypresses" (*berosh*, a conifer; the kind is not certain). "Instruments" is not written and is no longer supplied. The text is difficult: 1 Chr 13:8 has "with all strength and with songs" (בְּכָל־עֹז וּבְשִׁירִים), and the Septuagint and a Qumran scroll agree with it (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"on harps, and on psalteries, and on timbrels, and on cornets, and on cymbals" → "with lyres and with harps and with timbrels and with rattles and with cymbals"** (v5) — כִּנּוֹר "lyre", נֵבֶל "harp", תֹּף "timbrel" (a hand drum); מְנַעַנְעִים is found only here: from נוּעַ "shake", so a shaken instrument, "rattles" (sistrums); the sense is uncertain. The kinds of all these instruments are uncertain.
- **"Nachon's threshingfloor" → "the threshing floor of Nacon"** (v6) — נָכוֹן may be a name or the word "established, firm" ("a fixed threshing floor"). 1 Chr 13:9 has "Chidon" (from memory).
- **"put forth his hand to the ark" → "stretched out to the ark"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁלַח עֻזָּא אֶל־אֲרוֹן: "his hand" is not in the Masoretic Text here (1 Chr 13:9 has it; from memory) and is not supplied.
- **"for the oxen shook it" → "for the cattle let go"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי שָׁמְטוּ הַבָּקָר. שָׁמַט is "let drop, let loose" (Exod 23:11; Deut 15:2); the verb has no object here, and what the animals did is uncertain ("stumbled", "let it slip", "shook it loose"). בָּקָר is the collective word "herd, cattle".
- **"God smote him there for his error" → "Elohim struck him down there for the error"** (v7) — Hebrew עַל־הַשַּׁל. The word שַׁל is found only here and its meaning is uncertain; "error" is the old understanding and is kept. "His" is not written. 1 Chr 13:10 has in its place "because he stretched out his hand upon the ark" (from memory). **"by the ark"** — Hebrew עִם "with, beside".
- **"the anger of the LORD was kindled"** (v7) — Hebrew אַף "nose", of anger; "burned" is חָרָה.
- **"David was displeased, because the LORD had made a breach upon Uzzah: and he called the name of the place Perez-uzzah" → "David burned with anger because Yahweh had broken out with a breach against Uzzah; and he called that place Perez-uzzah"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר לְדָוִד, literally "it burned to David"; פָּרַץ … פֶּרֶץ "breached a breach". The name *Peretz Uzza* means "the breach of Uzzah"; the explanation that stood in the verse in brackets was not in the Hebrew and is removed. וַיִּקְרָא "and he called": the subject is not named (David, or "one called").
- **"How shall the ark of the LORD come to me?"** (v9) — Hebrew אֵיךְ יָבוֹא; the imperfect is "shall" or "can".
- **"would not remove the ark of the LORD unto him … carried it aside" → "was not willing to remove … to himself … turned it aside"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא־אָבָה "was not willing"; לְהָסִיר "to make turn aside, remove"; וַיַּטֵּהוּ "turned it aside" (another verb).
- **"continued" → "dwelt"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיֵּשֶׁב. **"his household" → "his house"** (v11, 20) — בַּיִת.
- **"with gladness"** (v12) — שִׂמְחָה. **"because of" → "for the sake of"** — בַּעֲבוּר.
- **"when they that bare the ark of the LORD had gone six paces, he sacrificed oxen and fatlings" → "when the carriers of the ark of Yahweh had marched six steps, that he sacrificed an ox and a fatling"** (v13) — Hebrew צָעֲדוּ … צְעָדִים, verb and noun of one root; שׁוֹר וּמְרִיא are singular. "He" is not named.
- **"David danced before the LORD with all his might" → "David was whirling with all strength before Yahweh"** (v14, 16) — Hebrew מְכַרְכֵּר, found only in these two verses: a dance in which one turns round ("whirl"; commonly "dance"). עֹז "strength"; "his" is not written. **"girded with a linen ephod"** — חָגוּר "girded"; בַּד "linen".
- **"with the sound of the trumpet" → "with the voice of a shofar"** (v15) — Hebrew קוֹל שׁוֹפָר, the ram's horn, without the article. תְּרוּעָה of voices is "shouting".
- **"as the ark of the LORD came" → "And it was, as the ark of Yahweh was coming"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהָיָה … בָּא, an unusual form (1 Chr 15:29 has וַיְהִי; from memory). **"leaping"** — מְפַזֵּז, found only here: "be nimble, leap"; uncertain.
- **"offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD" → "offered up burnt offerings before Yahweh, and peace offerings"** (v17) — the Hebrew order; in v18 הָעוֹלָה "the burnt offering" is singular.
- **"he dealt among all the people … as well to the women as men, to every one a cake of bread, and a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine" → "he divided to all the people … from man to woman, to each one cake of bread and one portion and one raisin cake"** (v19) — Hebrew לְמֵאִישׁ וְעַד־אִשָּׁה "from man and to woman". אֶשְׁפָּר is found only here and in 1 Chr 16:3 and its meaning is not known ("a portion", of meat or of dates, is a guess of the old translators; "of meat" is not written). אֲשִׁישָׁה is a pressed cake, most likely of raisins (Hos 3:1), not a flask of wine. חַלָּה and אֲשִׁישָׁה are two words; English has "cake" for both.
- **"How glorious was the king of Israel to day, who uncovered himself to day in the eyes of the handmaids of his servants, as one of the vain fellows shamelessly uncovereth himself!" → "How the king of Israel was glorified today, who uncovered himself today before the eyes of the slave women of his servants, as one of the empty men surely uncovers himself!"** (v20) — Hebrew נִכְבַּד "was glorified, got himself glory" (the root of v22 "let me be glorified"); נִגְלָה "uncovered himself" or "was uncovered"; אָמָה "slave woman"; עֶבֶד of a king's men "servant". כְּהִגָּלוֹת נִגְלוֹת is the doubled infinitive, literally "as uncovering, one uncovers himself"; "shamelessly" was an interpretation. הָרֵקִים "the empty ones" (as Judg 9:4; 11:3).
- **"It was before the LORD, which chose me before thy father … to appoint me ruler … therefore will I play" → "Before Yahweh, who chose me above your father … to command me to be leader … — and I will play"** (v21) — the Hebrew sentence begins "Before Yahweh" and has no "It was"; it breaks off and is taken up by "and I will play before Yahweh". The Septuagint has a longer text ("… I will dance. Blessed be the Lord, who chose me …"; from memory). מִן "from, above, rather than". נָגִיד "leader" (as 1 Sam 13:14), not "prince", "ruler". שִׂחַקְתִּי is the verb of v5.
- **"I will yet be more vile than thus, and will be base in mine own sight: and of the maidservants … of them shall I be had in honour" → "I will be made light of yet more than this, and I will be low in my eyes; and with the slave women … with them let me be glorified"** (v22) — Hebrew וּנְקַלֹּתִי, from קָלַל "be light": the opposite of כָּבֵד "be heavy, glorious"; שָׁפָל "low"; עִם "with"; אִכָּבֵדָה, the form of a wish or resolve. The Septuagint has "in your eyes" (from memory); the Masoretic Text has "in my eyes".
- **"Therefore Michal … had no child" → "And as for Michal … she had no child"** (v23) — Hebrew וּלְמִיכַל … לֹא־הָיָה לָהּ יָלֶד; "therefore" is not written.

### Notes
- **First attempt to move the ark** — on a wagon drawn by cattle, like the Philistines (1 Sam 6). The ark was to be carried by poles on Kohathites' shoulders (Num 4).
- **Uzzah's death** — he took hold of the ark. The verse does not say more than "for the error" (see v7 above).
- **Three months** in the house of Obed-edom — Yahweh blesses the house where the ark dwells.
- **Second attempt** — by carriers; a sacrifice after six steps; David whirling, girded with a linen ephod.
- **The linen ephod** (v14) — the dress of one who serves before Yahweh (1 Sam 2:18; 22:18).
- **Michal's contempt** — she speaks of David's uncovering himself. Saul's daughter against David's kingship. That she has no child (v23) is stated without a reason.
- **David's tent** (v17) — the tent David pitched houses the ark in Jerusalem. Not yet the house that Solomon built.
