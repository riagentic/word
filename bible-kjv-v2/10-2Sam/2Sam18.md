# 2 Samuel 18

[1] And David numbered the people who were with him, and he set over them commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds.

[2] And David sent out the people, the third in the hand of ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, and the third in the hand of ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ the son of ⟦zeruiah|Tzeruyah|Zeruiah⟧, the brother of ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, and the third in the hand of Ittai the ⟦gittite|Gitti|Gittite⟧. And the king said to the people, "I will surely go out, I also, with you."

[3] And the people said, "You shall not go out, for if we surely flee, they will not set their heart on us, and if half of us die, they will not set their heart on us, for now there are ten thousand like us. And now it is good that you be for us from the city, to help."

[4] And the king said to them, "What is good in your eyes I will do." And the king stood beside the gate, and all the people went out by hundreds and by thousands.

[5] And the king commanded ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ and Ittai, saying, "Gently, for me, with the young man, with ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧." And all the people heard when the king commanded all the commanders concerning ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧.

[6] And the people went out to the field to meet ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the battle was in the forest of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[7] And the people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were smitten there before the servants of David, and the smiting there was great on that day: twenty thousand.

[8] And the battle there was scattered over the face of all the land, and the forest devoured more of the people than the sword devoured on that day.

[9] And ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ chanced to be before the servants of David. And ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ was riding on the mule, and the mule came under the tangled branches of the great terebinth, and his head was held fast in the terebinth, and he was set between the heavens and the earth, and the mule that was under him passed on.

[10] And one man saw it and told ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and said, "Behold, I saw ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ hanging in the terebinth."

[11] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said to the man who was telling him, "And behold, you saw! And why did you not strike him there to the earth? And it would have been upon me to give you ten of silver and one belt."

[12] And the man said to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, "And though I were weighing on my palms a thousand of silver, I would not stretch out my hand against the son of the king, for in our ears the king commanded you and ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ and Ittai, saying, 'Keep watch, whoever you are, over the young man, over ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧.'

[13] Or had I done falsehood against my soul — and no thing is hidden from the king — then you would take your stand opposite."

[14] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said, "Not so will I wait before you." And he took three rods in his palm and thrust them into the heart of ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧, while he was still alive in the heart of the terebinth.

[15] And ten young men, the weapon-bearers of ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, went around and struck ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ and put him to death.

[16] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ blew on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, and the people returned from pursuing after ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ withheld the people.

[17] And they took ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ and threw him in the forest into the great pit, and they set up over him a very great heap of stones. And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had fled, each man to his tents.

[18] And ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ had taken and set up for himself in his life the pillar that is in the Valley of the King, for he said, "I have no son, for the sake of making my name remembered." And he called the pillar by his name, and it is called the Monument of ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ to this day.

[19] And ⟦ahimaaz|Achimaatz|Ahimaaz⟧ the son of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ said, "Let me run, please, and bring the news to the king, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has judged him out of the hand of his enemies."

[20] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said to him, "You are not a man of news this day, and you shall bring news on another day; and this day you shall not bring news, because the son of the king is dead."

[21] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said to the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧, "Go, tell the king what you have seen." And the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ bowed down to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and ran.

[22] And ⟦ahimaaz|Achimaatz|Ahimaaz⟧ the son of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ said yet again to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, "And be what may, let me run, please, I also, after the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧." And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said, "Why is it that you would run, my son, and for you there is no news that finds anything?"

[23] "And be what may, I will run." And he said to him, "Run." And ⟦ahimaaz|Achimaatz|Ahimaaz⟧ ran by the way of the circle and passed the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧.

[24] And David was sitting between the two gates. And the watchman went to the roof of the gate, to the wall, and he lifted up his eyes and saw, and behold, a man running alone.

[25] And the watchman called and told the king. And the king said, "If he is alone, there is news in his mouth." And he came on, coming and drawing near.

[26] And the watchman saw another man running, and the watchman called to the gatekeeper and said, "Behold, a man running alone." And the king said, "This one also is a bearer of news."

[27] And the watchman said, "I see the running of the first as the running of ⟦ahimaaz|Achimaatz|Ahimaaz⟧ the son of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧." And the king said, "This is a good man, and to good news he comes."

[28] And ⟦ahimaaz|Achimaatz|Ahimaaz⟧ called and said to the king, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧!" And he bowed down to the king with his face to the earth and said, "Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who has shut up the men who lifted up their hand against my lord the king."

[29] And the king said, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧ with the young man, with ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧?" And ⟦ahimaaz|Achimaatz|Ahimaaz⟧ said, "I saw the great multitude at the sending of the servant of the king by ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, and of your servant, and I did not know what."

[30] And the king said, "Turn about; take your stand here." And he turned about and stood.

[31] And behold, the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ was coming, and the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ said, "Let my lord the king receive the news, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has judged you today out of the hand of all who rose up against you."

[32] And the king said to the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧, "Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with the young man, with ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧?" And the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ said, "May they be as the young man, the enemies of my lord the king and all who have risen up against you for evil."

[33] And the king was agitated, and he went up to the upper room of the gate and wept, and thus he said as he went:

"My son ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧,
my son, my son ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v28) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"captains of thousands" → "commanders of thousands"** (v1, 5) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי, "princes, chiefs of". **"numbered"** — Hebrew פָּקַד, "muster".
- **"a third part … under the hand of" → "the third in the hand of"** (v2) — Hebrew הַשְּׁלִשִׁית בְּיַד. **"I will surely go forth with you myself also"** — literally "going out I will go out".
- **"if we flee away, they will not care for us" → "if we surely flee, they will not set their heart on us"** (v3) — Hebrew נֹס נָנוּס; לֹא־יָשִׂימוּ אֵלֵינוּ לֵב. **"but now thou art worth ten thousand of us" → "for now there are ten thousand like us"** — the Masoretic Text has כִּי־עַתָּה כָמֹנוּ עֲשָׂרָה אֲלָפִים, "for now, like us, ten thousand", and is translated as it stands; it is difficult. The Greek and the Vulgate read "you" (אַתָּה for עַתָּה "now"): "you are like ten thousand of us" (from memory), which the KJV follows. **"that thou succour us out of the city" → "that you be for us from the city, to help"** — "to help" is the reading tradition (qere לַעְזוֹר).
- **"What seemeth you best" → "What is good in your eyes"** (v4). **"by the gate side" → "beside the gate"** — Hebrew אֶל־יַד, "at the hand of".
- **"Deal gently for my sake with the young man, even with Absalom" → "Gently, for me, with the young man, with Absalom"** (v5) — the Hebrew has no verb: לְאַט־לִי לַנַּעַר לְאַבְשָׁלוֹם. נַעַר is "boy", here of a grown man (so v12, 29, 32). **"gave all the captains charge" → "commanded all the commanders"**.
- **"against Israel" → "to meet Israel"** (v6) — Hebrew לִקְרַאת. **"the wood of Ephraim" → "the forest of Ephraim"**.
- **"were slain … a great slaughter" → "were smitten … the smiting there was great"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּגְפוּ, הַמַּגֵּפָה, verb and noun of one root: a blow, a defeat. "Men" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the battle was there scattered over the face of all the country" → "over the face of all the land"** (v8) — "scattered" is the reading tradition (qere נָפֹצֶת). **"devoured"** — Hebrew אָכַל "eat", of the forest and of the sword; literally "the forest did much to eat among the people, more than the sword ate".
- **"Absalom met the servants of David" → "chanced to be before the servants of David"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּקָּרֵא … לִפְנֵי, "happened upon, before". **"rode upon a mule … under the thick boughs of a great oak, and his head caught hold of the oak, and he was taken up between the heaven and the earth; and the mule that was under him went away" → "was riding on the mule … under the tangled branches of the great terebinth, and his head was held fast in the terebinth, and he was set between the heavens and the earth, and the mule that was under him passed on"** — Hebrew שׂוֹבֶךְ is found only here: a tangle of boughs; אֵלָה "terebinth", with the article; וַיֶּחֱזַק, "was strong, held fast"; וַיֻּתַּן, "was given, set" (the Greek and other versions have "was hung", from memory); עָבָר. The verse speaks of his head; his hair (14:26) is not named.
- **"hanged in an oak" → "hanging in the terebinth"** (v10) — Hebrew תָּלוּי בָּאֵלָה.
- **"I would have given thee ten shekels of silver, and a girdle" → "it would have been upon me to give you ten of silver and one belt"** (v11) — Hebrew וְעָלַי לָתֶת; עֲשָׂרָה כֶסֶף, "ten of silver": the unit is not named (so also "a thousand of silver", v12). **"to the ground" → "to the earth"** — Hebrew אָרְצָה.
- **"Though I should receive a thousand shekels of silver in mine hand" → "though I were weighing on my palms a thousand of silver"** (v12) — "though" is the reading tradition (qere וְלוּא; the written text has ולא "and not"); שֹׁקֵל עַל־כַּפַּי, "weighing upon my palms". **"put forth mine hand" → "stretch out my hand"**. **"in our hearing" → "in our ears"**. **"Beware that none touch the young man Absalom" → "Keep watch, whoever you are, over the young man, over Absalom"** — Hebrew שִׁמְרוּ־מִי בַּנַּעַר בְּאַבְשָׁלוֹם: מִי "who(ever)" is difficult here; the Greek and other versions have "for me" (לִי), as in v5 (from memory).
- **"Otherwise I should have wrought falsehood against mine own life … and thou thyself wouldest have set thyself against me" → "Or had I done falsehood against my soul … then you would take your stand opposite"** (v13) — "against my soul" is the reading tradition (qere בְנַפְשִׁי); the written text (ketiv) is "against his soul" (בנפשו, Absalom's). שֶׁקֶר "falsehood"; וְאַתָּה תִּתְיַצֵּב מִנֶּגֶד, "and you would take your stand over against": at a distance, leaving him to it.
- **"I may not tarry thus with thee" → "Not so will I wait before you"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹא־כֵן אֹחִילָה לְפָנֶיךָ. **"three darts in his hand, and thrust them through the heart of Absalom, while he was yet alive in the midst of the oak" → "three rods in his palm and thrust them into the heart of Absalom, while he was still alive in the heart of the terebinth"** — Hebrew שְׁבָטִים, "rods, staffs" (the Greek has "darts", from memory); בְּלֵב … בְּלֵב, "in the heart of" twice.
- **"ten young men that bare Joab's armour compassed about and smote Absalom, and slew him" → "ten young men, the weapon-bearers of Joab, went around and struck Absalom and put him to death"** (v15) — Hebrew נֹשְׂאֵי כְּלֵי, "bearers of the gear of"; וַיָּסֹבּוּ; וַיְמִיתֻהוּ.
- **"blew the trumpet" → "blew on the shofar"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּתְקַע … בַּשֹּׁפָר. **"held back the people" → "withheld the people"** — Hebrew חָשַׂךְ.
- **"cast him into a great pit in the wood, and laid a very great heap of stones upon him" → "threw him in the forest into the great pit, and they set up over him a very great heap of stones"** (v17) — Hebrew אֶל־הַפַּחַת הַגָּדוֹל, with the article; וַיַּצִּבוּ, the verb of v18. **"every one to his tent" → "each man to his tents"** — the reading tradition (qere) is the plural.
- **"had taken and reared up for himself a pillar, which is in the king's dale" → "had taken and set up for himself in his life the pillar that is in the Valley of the King"** (v18) — Hebrew מַצֶּבֶת, a standing stone; עֵמֶק־הַמֶּלֶךְ (Gen 14:17). **"I have no son to keep my name in remembrance" → "I have no son, for the sake of making my name remembered"** — Hebrew בַּעֲבוּר הַזְכִּיר שְׁמִי, the hifil of "remember": "to cause my name to be remembered" or "to mention my name". In 14:27 three sons were born to Absalom; the two statements stand side by side. **"Absalom's place" → "the Monument of Absalom"** — Hebrew יַד אַבְשָׁלֹם, literally "the hand of Absalom" (1 Sam 15:12).
- **"bear the king tidings, how that the LORD hath avenged him of his enemies" → "bring the news to the king, that Yahweh has judged him out of the hand of his enemies"** (v19, 31) — Hebrew בִּשֵּׂר, בְּשֹׂרָה (v19–31), one root: "bring news", "news"; שְׁפָטוֹ … מִיַּד, "judged him out of the hand of": gave judgment for him and so freed him.
- **"Thou shalt not bear tidings this day" → "You are not a man of news this day"** (v20) — Hebrew לֹא אִישׁ בְּשֹׂרָה אַתָּה. **"because"** — the reading tradition (qere) עַל־כֵּן; the written text has only עַל.
- **"Cushi" → "the Cushite"** (v21–23, 31, 32) — Hebrew הַכּוּשִׁי, "the Cushite", with the article; once in v21 without it. **"bowed himself" → "bowed down"**.
- **"But howsoever, let me, I pray thee, also run" → "And be what may, let me run, please, I also"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew וִיהִי מָה, "and let there be what (there may)". **"seeing that thou hast no tidings ready" → "and for you there is no news that finds anything"** — Hebrew וּלְכָה אֵין־בְּשׂוֹרָה מֹצֵאת, literally "and to you there is no news finding": difficult; "finding" is taken as "bringing in (a reward)" or "reaching (its aim)".
- **"by the way of the plain, and overran Cushi" → "by the way of the circle and passed the Cushite"** (v23) — Hebrew הַכִּכָּר, "the round": the district of the lower Jordan (Gen 13:10); וַיַּעֲבֹר.
- **"sat between the two gates"** (v24) — the outer and the inner gate. **"went up to the roof over the gate unto the wall" → "went to the roof of the gate, to the wall"**. **"lifted up his eyes, and looked" → "lifted up his eyes and saw"**.
- **"he came apace, and drew near" → "he came on, coming and drawing near"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ הָלוֹךְ וְקָרֵב.
- **"unto the porter" → "to the gatekeeper"** (v26) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁעֵר. **"Behold another man running alone" → "Behold, a man running alone"** — "another" is not in the watchman's words. **"He also bringeth tidings" → "This one also is a bearer of news"** — Hebrew מְבַשֵּׂר.
- **"Me thinketh the running of the foremost is like" → "I see the running of the first as"** (v27) — Hebrew אֲנִי רֹאֶה. **"cometh with good tidings" → "to good news he comes"** — Hebrew וְאֶל־בְּשׂוֹרָה טוֹבָה יָבוֹא.
- **"All is well" → "Shalom!"** (v28) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם, one word. **"fell down to the earth upon his face" → "bowed down … with his face to the earth"** — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁתַּחוּ … לְאַפָּיו אָרְצָה, literally "to his nostrils, to the earth". **"Blessed be" → "Blessed is"** — the Hebrew has no verb. **"which hath delivered up the men" → "who has shut up the men"** — Hebrew סִגַּר, as 1 Sam 17:46; 24:18; 26:8.
- **"Is the young man Absalom safe?" → "Shalom with the young man, with Absalom?" / "Is it shalom with the young man, with Absalom?"** (v29, 32) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם לַנַּעַר לְאַבְשָׁלוֹם; in v32 with the question particle (הֲשָׁלוֹם), in v29 without it.
- **"When Joab sent the king's servant, and me thy servant, I saw a great tumult" → "I saw the great multitude at the sending of the servant of the king by Joab, and of your servant"** (v29) — Hebrew הֶהָמוֹן הַגָּדוֹל, "the great multitude" (a crowd and its noise); לִשְׁלֹחַ אֶת־עֶבֶד הַמֶּלֶךְ יוֹאָב וְאֶת־עַבְדֶּךָ, word for word "at sending the servant of the king, Joab, and your servant": the order is difficult; "Joab" is taken as the one who sends. "What it was" is only "what" in the Hebrew.
- **"Turn aside, and stand here" → "Turn about; take your stand here"** (v30) — Hebrew סֹב הִתְיַצֵּב כֹּה.
- **"Tidings, my lord the king" → "Let my lord the king receive the news"** (v31) — Hebrew יִתְבַּשֵּׂר, a wish. **"avenged thee this day of all them that rose up against thee" → "judged you today out of the hand of all who rose up against you"**.
- **"be as that young man is" → "May they be as the young man"** (v32) — the Hebrew word order is kept: the wish first. **"to do thee hurt" → "for evil"** — Hebrew לְרָעָה.
- **"the king was much moved, and went up to the chamber over the gate" → "the king was agitated, and he went up to the upper room of the gate"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיִּרְגַּז, "trembled, was shaken"; עֲלִיַּת הַשַּׁעַר.
- **"O my son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom! would God I had died for thee, O Absalom, my son, my son!" → "My son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom! Would that I had died, I, in your place, Absalom, my son, my son!"** (v33) — the cry word for word: בְּנִי אַבְשָׁלוֹם בְּנִי בְנִי אַבְשָׁלוֹם מִי־יִתֵּן מוּתִי אֲנִי תַחְתֶּיךָ אַבְשָׁלוֹם בְּנִי בְנִי. "My son" stands five times and the name three times; the Hebrew has no "O" and no "God". מִי־יִתֵּן is literally "who will give": "who will give my dying, I, in your place".
- **Verse numbers** — v33 is 19:1 in the Hebrew numbering.

### Notes
- **David stays in Mahanaim** — the people keep him from the battle (v3–4).
- **"Gently, for me, with the young man"** (v5) — the king's command, heard by all the people (v12).
- **Absalom in the terebinth** — the text says his head was held fast; that it was by his hair (14:26) is an old inference.
- **Joab and the king's command** — Joab thrusts the rods into Absalom against the command of v5; see 19:5–7.
- **Absalom's monument** — traditionally identified with a monument in the Kidron Valley (that monument is of the Hellenistic period).
- **Ahimaaz** — runs first but does not say what he knows (v29).
- **The Cushite** — brings the word of Absalom's death (v32).
- **David's cry** (v33) — taken up again in 19:4.
