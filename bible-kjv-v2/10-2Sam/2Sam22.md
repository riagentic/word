## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"hell" → "Sheol"** (v6).
- **"froward / unsavoury" → "crooked / shrewd"** (v27) — Hebrew *iqqesh / patel* = twisted / crafty. Yahweh engages the crooked with "shrewd" reciprocity.
- **"bow of steel" → "bow of bronze"** (v35) — Hebrew *qeshet nechushah*. Steel unknown; bronze intended.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v44, 50).
- **"strangers" → "foreigners"** (v45, 46).
- **"mercy" → "lovingkindness"** (v51) — *chesed*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v31, 32, 33, 48) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim* (in v32 only the first "God"; the second is *Elohim*).

### Notes
- **Ps 18 parallel** — this chapter is essentially Ps 18 with minor textual differences. The Psalter preserves a polished version; 2 Sam gives the narrative-embedded version.
- **Theophanic language** (v8–16) — cosmic imagery: earthquake, fire, clouds, thunder. Yahweh depicted as storm-warrior who intervenes on David's behalf.
- **Righteousness claim** (v21–25) — David claims moral integrity. Written prior to the Bathsheba episode, or deliberately glossing over it. The psalm reflects an earlier phase of self-understanding.
- **Personal and dynastic** — closes with reference to "David and his offspring forever" — Davidic covenant reference.
- **Placement** — together with ch 23:1–7 (David's last words) forms a poetic bookend for the book, after the epilogue-material of ch 21.
