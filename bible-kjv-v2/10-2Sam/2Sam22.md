## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 7, 22, 30, 32, 47) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God" → "El"** (v31, 32, 33, 48) — Hebrew אֵל *El*; in v31, 33, 48 with the article, הָאֵל "the El". **"the most High" → "Elyon"** (v14) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן.
- **Tenses** (throughout) — the song alternates the Hebrew perfect, the imperfect and the narrative form with וַ. Where the song tells of the rescue (v5–20, 38–43) the imperfects are given as past ("he sent … he took me", literally "he sends … he takes me"); each וַ is kept as "and", so that the places where Samuel has it and Ps 18 has not (and the reverse) can be seen.
- **"out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul" → "from the palm … from the palm"** (v1) — Hebrew מִכַּף both times (Ps 18 has מִיַּד "from the hand" the second time).
- **"my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer" → "my crag and my stronghold and my rescuer, mine"** (v2) — Hebrew סֶלַע "crag" (צוּר is "rock", v3); מְצוּדָה "stronghold"; מְפַלְטִי from פִּלֵּט "bring to safety" (הִצִּיל is "deliver", v1, 18, 49); לִי "to me, mine", which Ps 18 does not have.
- **"The God of my rock; in him will I trust" → "the Elohim of my Rock, in whom I take refuge"** (v3) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי צוּרִי אֶחֱסֶה־בּוֹ, literally "the Elohim of my Rock, I take refuge in him". "Rock" (צוּר) is written with a capital where it stands for Yahweh (v3, 32, 47; Deut 32:4). **"my high tower, and my refuge" → "my secure height and my place of flight"** — Hebrew מִשְׂגָּב, a high place out of reach; מָנוֹס, a place to flee to (from נוּס "flee"). **"thou savest me from violence"** — Hebrew חָמָס.
- **"I will call on the LORD, who is worthy to be praised" → "I call on Yahweh, the praised one"** (v4) — Hebrew מְהֻלָּל אֶקְרָא יְהוָה, in this order: "praised — I call — Yahweh". "Worthy" is not in the Hebrew. It may also be read "I call Yahweh 'praised'".
- **"the waves of death compassed me, the floods of ungodly men made me afraid" → "the breakers of death encompassed me, the torrents of Beliyyaal (worthlessness) terrified me"** (v5) — Hebrew מִשְׁבְּרֵי־מָוֶת; נַחֲלֵי בְלִיַּעַל, the common noun "worthlessness" (as "sons of Beliyyaal"); "men" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"The sorrows of hell compassed me about; the snares of death prevented me" → "The cords of Sheol surrounded me, the snares of death came to meet me"** (v6) — Hebrew חֶבְלֵי שְׁאוֹל; חֶבֶל is "cord" (another word of the same letters is "pang", whence KJV "sorrows"); קִדְּמֻנִי "came to meet, confronted" (so v19).
- **"my cry did enter into his ears" → "my cry for help was in his ears"** (v7) — the Hebrew has no verb; שַׁוְעָה "cry for help". "Called" is the same verb twice (אֶקְרָא).
- **"shook and trembled … moved and shook, because he was wroth" → "heaved and quaked … trembled, and they heaved, for he burned with anger"** (v8) — Hebrew גָּעַשׁ (twice; the first is qere וַיִּתְגָּעַשׁ, ketiv ותגעש), רָעַשׁ, רָגַז; חָרָה לוֹ, literally "it burned to him".
- **"out of his nostrils" → "in his nose"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּאַפּוֹ; אַף is "nose" and "anger". **"coals were kindled by it" → "from him"** — Hebrew מִמֶּנּוּ, "from him" or "from it".
- **"He bowed the heavens"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיֵּט, from נָטָה "stretch out, bend"; here "bend down". **"darkness" → "a dark cloud"** — Hebrew עֲרָפֶל.
- **"cherub" → "keruv"** (v11) — Hebrew כְּרוּב. **"he was seen"** — Hebrew וַיֵּרָא (Ps 18 has וַיֵּדֶא "he swooped": one letter differs).
- **"he made darkness pavilions round about him, dark waters, and thick clouds of the skies" → "he set darkness around him as booths, a mass of waters, thick clouds of the skies"** (v12) — Hebrew סֻכּוֹת "booths"; חַשְׁרַת־מַיִם: חַשְׁרָה is found only here and its meaning is uncertain ("a gathering, a mass" of waters; others "a sieve"). "As" is supplied.
- **"Through the brightness before him were coals of fire kindled"** (v13) — Hebrew מִנֹּגַהּ נֶגְדּוֹ בָּעֲרוּ גַּחֲלֵי־אֵשׁ.
- **"uttered his voice" → "gave his voice"** (v14) — Hebrew יִתֵּן קוֹלוֹ.
- **"discomfited them" → "threw them into confusion"** (v15) — qere וַיָּהֹם; the ketiv has ויהמם, with "them" written. "Lightning" is singular.
- **"at the rebuking of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils" → "at the rebuke of Yahweh, from the breath of the wind of his nose"** (v16) — Hebrew מִנִּשְׁמַת רוּחַ אַפּוֹ: נְשָׁמָה "breath", רוּחַ "wind, breath, spirit", אַף "nose". **"appeared" → "were seen"**, **"were discovered" → "were uncovered"**.
- **"He sent from above"** (v17) — the Hebrew has no object ("his hand" is understood). **"drew me out"** — Hebrew יַמְשֵׁנִי, the verb of Exod 2:10.
- **"They prevented me" → "They came to meet me"** (v19). **"my stay" → "a support to me"** — Hebrew מִשְׁעָן.
- **"into a large place" → "to the broad place"** (v20) — Hebrew לַמֶּרְחָב. **"delivered me" → "pulled me out"** — Hebrew יְחַלְּצֵנִי.
- **"rewarded me … recompensed me" → "dealt with me … returned to me"** (v21, 25) — Hebrew גָּמַל "deal with"; הֵשִׁיב "make return". **"cleanness" → "purity"** — Hebrew בֹּר, of the root of "pure" in v27.
- **"have not wickedly departed from my God" → "have not done wickedly away from my Elohim"** (v22) — Hebrew וְלֹא רָשַׁעְתִּי מֵאֱלֹהָי, "I was not wicked from my Elohim"; "departed" is not written.
- **"as for his statutes, I did not depart from them" → "and his statutes, I do not turn aside from it"** (v23) — Hebrew וְחֻקֹּתָיו לֹא־אָסוּר מִמֶּנָּה: "from it", feminine singular, after the plural "statutes"; kept as it stands. "Judgments" is the qere מִשְׁפָּטָיו (ketiv: singular).
- **"upright before him" → "blameless toward him"** (v24) — Hebrew תָמִים לוֹ. **"from mine iniquity" kept** — Hebrew מֵעֲוֺנִי.
- **"With the merciful thou wilt shew thyself merciful, and with the upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright" → "With the faithful one you show yourself faithful, with the blameless mighty one you show yourself blameless"** (v26) — Hebrew חָסִיד, one who keeps *chesed*; תִּתְחַסָּד is the verb of the same root. גִּבּוֹר תָּמִים is "a mighty one, blameless".
- **"with the froward thou wilt shew thyself unsavoury" → "with the crooked you show yourself twisted"** (v27) — Hebrew עִקֵּשׁ "crooked"; תִּתַּפָּל is an unusual form. It is taken as a form of פָּתַל "twist", which Ps 18 has in full (תִּתְפַּתָּל); KJV "unsavoury" derives it from תָּפֵל "tasteless". תִּתָּבָר ("show yourself pure") is a like shortened form.
- **"thine eyes are upon the haughty, that thou mayest bring them down" → "your eyes are on the high: you make them low"** (v28) — Hebrew וְעֵינֶיךָ עַל־רָמִים תַּשְׁפִּיל; "them" is supplied.
- **"will lighten my darkness" → "brightens my darkness"** (v29) — Hebrew יַגִּיהַּ, of the root of "brightness" (v13). The Hebrew has no "O".
- **"by thee I have run through a troop" → "by you I run at a raiding band"** (v30) — Hebrew אָרוּץ גְּדוּד, "I run a raiding band", with no preposition.
- **"his way is perfect; the word of the LORD is tried: he is a buckler" → "his way is blameless; the saying of Yahweh is refined; he is a shield"** (v31) — Hebrew תָּמִים (the word of v24, 26, 33); אִמְרָה "saying"; צְרוּפָה "refined" as metal; מָגֵן "shield".
- **"save the LORD … save our God" → "apart from Yahweh … apart from our Elohim"** (v32) — Hebrew מִבַּלְעֲדֵי twice.
- **"God is my strength and power: and he maketh my way perfect" → "The El is my strong place of ability, and he set my way loose, blameless"** (v33) — Hebrew הָאֵל מָעוּזִּי חָיִל וַיַּתֵּר תָּמִים דַּרְכִּי (qere; the ketiv has דרכו "his way"). The verse is difficult. מָעוּזִּי חָיִל is "my strong place — ability (strength)": two nouns, the first with "my". וַיַּתֵּר is taken from הִתִּיר "let loose, set free"; others take it from תּוּר "search out". Ps 18 reads "the El who girds me with ability, and he made my way blameless".
- **"like hinds' feet" → "like the does"** (v34) — Hebrew כָּאַיָּלוֹת, "like the does"; "feet" is not repeated. "My feet" is the qere (ketiv: "his feet").
- **"so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms" → "and my arms bend a bow of bronze"** (v35) — Hebrew וְנִחַת קֶשֶׁת־נְחוּשָׁה זְרֹעֹתָי: נִחַת "press down, bend" (not "break"); the verb is masculine singular in Samuel ("and he presses down a bow of bronze by my arms" is also possible). נְחוּשָׁה is bronze.
- **"thy gentleness hath made me great" → "your answering makes me great"** (v36) — Hebrew וַעֲנֹתְךָ, as printed the infinitive of עָנָה: "your answering", or of the other עָנָה, "your being bowed low". Ps 18 has וְעַנְוַתְךָ, "your humility".
- **"my feet did not slip" → "my ankles have not tottered"** (v37) — Hebrew קַרְסֻלַּי, found only here and in Ps 18.
- **"consumed them, and wounded them" → "made an end of them and struck them through"** (v38, 39) — Hebrew כִּלָּה; מָחַץ.
- **"hast subdued under me" → "made … bow down under me"** (v40) — Hebrew תַּכְרִיעַ. **"strength" → "ability"** — Hebrew חַיִל (v33).
- **"Thou hast also given me the necks of mine enemies" → "my enemies, you gave them to me as a neck"** (v41) — Hebrew תַּתָּה לִּי עֹרֶף: you made them turn their backs to me. **"that I might destroy them" → "and I annihilated them"** — Hebrew וָאַצְמִיתֵם, "and I put an end to them".
- **"They looked"** (v42) — Hebrew יִשְׁעוּ, from שָׁעָה "look (to)"; Ps 18 has יְשַׁוְּעוּ "they cried for help".
- **"did beat them as small as the dust … did stamp them as the mire of the street, and did spread them abroad" → "ground them like the dust of the earth, like the mud of the streets I crushed them, I stamped them"** (v43) — Hebrew שָׁחַק, הֵדֵק, רָקַע.
- **"from the strivings of my people, thou hast kept me to be head of the heathen" → "from the contentions of my people; you kept me for the head of nations"** (v44) — Hebrew רִיבֵי עַמִּי; תִּשְׁמְרֵנִי.
- **"Strangers shall submit themselves unto me: as soon as they hear, they shall be obedient unto me" → "Foreigners cringe before me; at the hearing of the ear they listen to me"** (v45) — Hebrew בְּנֵי נֵכָר, literally "sons of foreignness"; יִתְכַּחֲשׁוּ־לִי, literally "they lie to me" (feign submission); יִשָּׁמְעוּ לִי, "let themselves be heard by me", that is, obey.
- **"Strangers shall fade away, and they shall be afraid out of their close places" → "Foreigners wither, and they gird themselves from their fastnesses"** (v46) — Hebrew וְיַחְגְּרוּ מִמִּסְגְּרוֹתָם. As printed the verb is חָגַר "gird"; the clause is difficult. Ps 18 has וְיַחְרְגוּ, with two letters in the other order, a verb found only there ("come trembling"); some give the Samuel verb the sense "limp" (from memory). מִסְגְּרוֹת are "places shut in" (from סָגַר "shut").
- **"The LORD liveth; and blessed be my rock" → "Yahweh lives, and blessed is my Rock"** (v47) — the Hebrew has no verb. **"exalted be" → "let … be high"** — Hebrew וְיָרֻם.
- **"that avengeth me" → "who gives vengeances to me"** (v48) — Hebrew הַנֹּתֵן נְקָמֹת לִי, plural.
- **"thou also hast lifted me up on high above them that rose up against me" → "above those who rise against me you raise me high"** (v49) — Hebrew תְּרוֹמְמֵנִי. **"the violent man" → "the man of violences"** — Hebrew אִישׁ חֲמָסִים, plural.
- **"I will give thanks unto thee, O LORD, among the heathen, and I will sing praises unto thy name" → "I will give thanks to you, Yahweh, among the nations, and to your name I will make music"** (v50) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ; גּוֹיִם "nations"; אֲזַמֵּר "make music". Rom 15:9 cites the verse in its Greek form; the verse here follows the Hebrew.
- **"He is the tower of salvation for his king" → "A tower of the salvations of his king"** (v51) — qere מִגְדּוֹל "tower"; the ketiv מגדיל is "making great the salvations of his king", which is also what Ps 18:50 reads. "Salvations" is plural. **"sheweth mercy to his anointed" → "doing *chesed* (steadfast love) to his *mashiach* (anointed)"** — Hebrew וְעֹשֶׂה־חֶסֶד לִמְשִׁיחוֹ, a participle. **"his seed"** — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **Differences from Psalm 18** (our verse numbers of Samuel; "Ps" is the wording of the psalm) — v1: the psalm has a heading before these words. v2: the psalm begins "I love you, Yahweh, my strength", which Samuel does not have; Samuel has "mine" (לִי). v3: "the Elohim of my Rock" (Ps "my El, my Rock"); "and my place of flight, my savior; from violence you save me" is only in Samuel. v5: "For the breakers of death" (Ps "the cords of death", no "for"). v7: "I called" twice (Ps "I called … I cried for help"); ### Notes
- **Psalm 18** — the same song stands in the Psalter as Ps 18 (Hebrew 18:1–51), with many small differences. This chapter translates the Hebrew of Samuel; nothing is taken over from the psalm.
- **The coming of Yahweh** (v8–16) — earth, heavens, smoke, fire, cloud, thunder.
- **"According to my righteousness"** (v21–25) — David's words about himself.
- **"To David and to his seed forever"** (v51) — compare 2 Sam 7:12–16.
- **Placement** — the song and the last words of David (23:1–7) stand together near the end of the book.
