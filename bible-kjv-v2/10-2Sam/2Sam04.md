# 2 Samuel 4

[1] When Saul's son heard that Abner had died in Hebron, his hands grew feeble, and all Israel was troubled.

[2] Saul's son had two men who were commanders of raiding bands; the name of one was Baanah, and the name of the other Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, of the children of Benjamin (for Beeroth also is reckoned to Benjamin;

[3] the Beerothites fled to Gittaim and have been sojourners there to this day).

[4] Jonathan, Saul's son, had a son who was crippled in his feet. He was five years old when the news about Saul and Jonathan came from Jezreel; his nurse took him up and fled. As she was hurrying to flee, he fell and became lame. His name was Mephibosheth.

[5] The sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah, set out and came to the house of Ishbosheth about the heat of the day, while he was lying down for his midday rest.

[6] They came into the midst of the house as if to get wheat, and they struck him in the belly. Rechab and Baanah his brother escaped.

[7] They had come into the house while he lay on his bed in his bedchamber; they struck him and killed him and beheaded him. They took his head and went by the way of the Arabah all night.

[8] They brought the head of Ishbosheth to David at Hebron and said to the king, "Behold, the head of Ishbosheth son of Saul, your enemy, who sought your life. Yahweh has avenged my lord the king this day on Saul and on his offspring."

[9] But David answered Rechab and Baanah his brother, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, "As Yahweh lives, who has redeemed my life from every trouble,

[10] when one told me, 'Behold, Saul is dead,' thinking he was bringing good news, I seized him and killed him at Ziklag, instead of giving him a reward for his news;

[11] how much more, when wicked men have killed a righteous man in his own house on his bed — shall I not now require his blood from your hand and sweep you from the earth?"

[12] David commanded his young men, and they killed them; they cut off their hands and their feet and hung them up beside the pool at Hebron. But they took the head of Ishbosheth and buried it in the tomb of Abner at Hebron.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"captains of bands" → "commanders of raiding bands"** (v2).
- **"fifth rib" → "belly"** (v6) — as elsewhere.
- **"lame of his feet" → "crippled in his feet"** (v4).
- **"in Ziklag" (v10)** — 2 Sam 1 event referenced.

### Notes
- **Ishbosheth's death** — without Abner, the house of Saul loses its military leader. Baanah and Rechab assassinate him.
- **Mephibosheth introduced** (v4) — Jonathan's lame son, age 5 at Saul's death. Future subject of David's covenantal loyalty (ch 9).
- **David's consistent treatment** — executes the assassins. Same pattern as with the Amalekite who claimed to kill Saul. Strict protection of the "anointed" line.
- **Mephibosheth's name** — in Chronicles called Merib-baal. *Bosheth* ("shame") regularly substituted for *baal* ("lord") in Samuel when referring to names linked to the Canaanite storm god.
- **Political effect** — David distances himself from any suggestion of benefiting from assassination. The path to the throne is legitimized by his non-complicity.
