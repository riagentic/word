## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"moved David" → "incited David"** (v1) — Hebrew הִסִית *hesit*. 1 Chr 21:1 attributes the incitement to *Satan* (the adversary); 2 Sam to Yahweh. Theological difficulty — OT often says Yahweh did what we would ascribe to proximate causes.
- **"seven years" → "three years"** (v13) — MT reads "seven"; LXX and 1 Chr 21:12 read "three." The parallel three/three/three structure argues for "three."
- **"strait" → "distress"** (v14) — archaic.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v16, 17).
- **"repented him of the evil" → "relented from the evil"** (v16) — *nacham*.

### Notes
- **The census sin** — debated. Was it (1) pride in military strength, (2) failure to pay the census-tax (Ex 30:12), (3) lack of trust in Yahweh? Joab's protest (v3) shows it was recognized as problematic even then.
- **Three-choice test** (v13) — Gad presents three plagues. David chooses pestilence (falling into Yahweh's hand).
- **70,000 die** — massive loss. Far worse than famine or military defeat.
- **"Angel of Yahweh" halted** (v16) — at the future temple site.
- **Araunah's generosity declined** — David insists on paying. "I will not offer that which costs me nothing" — foundational principle of costly worship.
- **Temple site purchased** (v24) — this threshing floor of Araunah becomes the site of Solomon's temple (2 Chr 3:1). Mount Moriah connection (Gen 22). The book of Samuel ends at the sacred ground where the temple will rise.
