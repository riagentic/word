[1] It was told ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, "Behold, the king weeps and mourns for ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧."

[2] The victory that day was turned into mourning for all the people, for the people heard that day, "The king is grieved for his son."

[3] The people stole into the city that day as people stole away when they were ashamed of having fled in battle.

[4] The king covered his face, and the king cried with a loud voice, "My son ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧! ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ my son, my son!"

[6] by loving those who hate you and hating those who love you. For you have made it clear today that princes and servants are nothing to you; for today I perceive that if ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ were alive and all of us were dead today, it would have pleased you.

[7] Now therefore arise, go out and speak kindly to your servants; for I swear by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, if you do not go out, not a man will stay with you this night; that will be worse for you than all the evil that has befallen you from your youth until now."

[8] So the king arose and took his seat in the gate. They told all the people, "Behold, the king is sitting in the gate." Then all the people came before the king. Meanwhile, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had fled each to his own home.

[9] All the people were disputing throughout all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "The king delivered us from the hand of our enemies and saved us from the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; but now he has fled out of the land from ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧.

[14] He inclined the hearts of all the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ as the heart of one man; they sent word to the king, "Return, you and all your servants."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"captain" → "commander"** (v13).
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v27).
- **"fourscore" → "eighty"** (v32, 35).

### Notes
- **Joab rebukes David** — forcefully. The king must resume public function. Joab's political acumen.
- **Amasa replaces Joab** — David names Absalom's former general to replace Joab. Punishment for Joab's insubordination in killing Absalom.
- **Shimei pardoned** — for now. Later David instructs Solomon (1 Kgs 2:8–9) to execute him.
- **Mephibosheth's defense** — contradicts Ziba's story. David splits the estate, possibly suspecting he cannot discern who is truthful.
- **Barzillai's humility** (v34–37) — declines royal favor; recommends his son Chimham instead.
- **North-south tension** (v41–43) — the ten northern tribes feel slighted. Sets up Sheba's rebellion in ch 20 and ultimately the kingdom's division under Rehoboam.
