# 2 Samuel 19

[1] It was told Joab, "Behold, the king weeps and mourns for Absalom."

[2] The victory that day was turned into mourning for all the people, for the people heard that day, "The king is grieved for his son."

[3] The people stole into the city that day as people stole away when they were ashamed of having fled in battle.

[4] The king covered his face, and the king cried with a loud voice, "My son Absalom! Absalom my son, my son!"

[5] Joab came into the house to the king and said, "You have today covered with shame the faces of all your servants, who this day have saved your life, the lives of your sons and daughters, and the lives of your wives and concubines,

[6] by loving those who hate you and hating those who love you. For you have made it clear today that princes and servants are nothing to you; for today I perceive that if Absalom were alive and all of us were dead today, it would have pleased you.

[7] Now therefore arise, go out and speak kindly to your servants; for I swear by Yahweh, if you do not go out, not a man will stay with you this night; that will be worse for you than all the evil that has befallen you from your youth until now."

[8] So the king arose and took his seat in the gate. They told all the people, "Behold, the king is sitting in the gate." Then all the people came before the king. Meanwhile, Israel had fled each to his own home.

[9] All the people were disputing throughout all the tribes of Israel, saying, "The king delivered us from the hand of our enemies and saved us from the hand of the Philistines; but now he has fled out of the land from Absalom.

[10] And Absalom, whom we anointed over us, is dead in the battle. Now therefore why do you say nothing about bringing the king back?"

[11] King David sent to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, saying, "Speak to the elders of Judah, saying, 'Why are you the last to bring the king back to his house, when the talk of all Israel has come to the king, even to his house?

[12] You are my kinsmen, my bone and my flesh; why then should you be the last to bring back the king?'

[13] And say to Amasa, 'Are you not my bone and my flesh? May *Elohim* do so to me and more also, if you are not commander of the army before me continually in place of Joab.'"

[14] He inclined the hearts of all the men of Judah as the heart of one man; they sent word to the king, "Return, you and all your servants."

[15] So the king returned and came to the Jordan. Judah came to Gilgal to meet the king and to bring the king over the Jordan.

[16] Shimei son of Gera the Benjamite, from Bahurim, hurried down with the men of Judah to meet King David.

[17] With him were a thousand men of Benjamin, and Ziba the servant of the house of Saul, and his fifteen sons and twenty servants with him; they rushed over the Jordan ahead of the king

[18] and crossed the ford to bring the king's household over, and to do what was good in his eyes. Shimei son of Gera fell down before the king as he was about to cross the Jordan,

[19] and said to the king, "Let not my lord hold me guilty, nor remember what your servant did wrong on the day my lord the king went out of Jerusalem. Let the king not take it to heart.

[20] For your servant knows that I have sinned; therefore behold, I have come this day, the first of all the house of Joseph, to go down to meet my lord the king."

[21] Abishai son of Zeruiah answered and said, "Shall not Shimei be put to death for this, because he cursed the anointed of Yahweh?"

[22] David said, "What have I to do with you, sons of Zeruiah, that you should this day be my adversaries? Shall anyone be put to death in Israel today? For do I not know that I am this day king over Israel?"

[23] Therefore the king said to Shimei, "You shall not die." And the king swore to him.

[24] Mephibosheth son of Saul came down to meet the king. He had not cared for his feet nor trimmed his beard nor washed his clothes from the day the king departed until the day he came home in peace.

[25] When he came to Jerusalem to meet the king, the king said to him, "Why did you not go with me, Mephibosheth?"

[26] He answered, "My lord, O king, my servant deceived me. For your servant said, 'I will saddle a donkey for myself, that I may ride on it and go with the king' — because your servant is lame.

[27] He has slandered your servant to my lord the king. But my lord the king is like the Messenger of *Elohim*; do therefore what is good in your eyes.

[28] For all my father's house were but men worthy of death before my lord the king; yet you set your servant among those who eat at your own table. What right do I have then to cry any more to the king?"

[29] The king said to him, "Why do you speak any more of your affairs? I say: you and Ziba shall divide the land."

[30] Mephibosheth said to the king, "Let him take it all, since my lord the king has come home in peace."

[31] Barzillai the Gileadite came down from Rogelim; he went on with the king as far as the Jordan, to escort him over the Jordan.

[32] Barzillai was a very aged man, eighty years old. He had provided the king with sustenance while he stayed at Mahanaim, for he was a very great man.

[33] The king said to Barzillai, "Come over with me, and I will provide for you in Jerusalem at my side."

[34] Barzillai said to the king, "How many years have I still to live, that I should go up with the king to Jerusalem?

[35] I am this day eighty years old. Can I discern between good and evil? Can your servant taste what I eat or drink? Can I still listen to the voice of singing men and singing women? Why then should your servant be an added burden to my lord the king?

[36] Your servant will go over the Jordan a little way with the king; why should the king recompense me with such a reward?

[37] Please let your servant return, that I may die in my own city beside the tomb of my father and mother. But here is your servant Chimham; let him go over with my lord the king, and do for him what is good in your eyes."

[38] The king answered, "Chimham shall go over with me, and I will do for him whatever is good in your eyes; and whatever you desire from me, I will do for you."

[39] All the people crossed the Jordan, and the king crossed over. The king kissed Barzillai and blessed him; and Barzillai returned to his own place.

[40] The king went on to Gilgal, and Chimham went on with him; all the people of Judah escorted the king, and also half the people of Israel.

[41] Behold, all the men of Israel came to the king and said to the king, "Why have our kinsmen, the men of Judah, stolen you away and brought the king and his household over the Jordan, and all David's men with him?"

[42] All the men of Judah answered the men of Israel, "Because the king is a close relative of ours. Why then are you angry about this matter? Have we eaten at all at the king's cost? Or has he given us any gift?"

[43] The men of Israel answered the men of Judah, "We have ten shares in the king, and we have also more claim on David than you. Why then did you despise us — was not the first word to bring back our king ours?" But the words of the men of Judah were fiercer than the words of the men of Israel.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"captain" → "commander"** (v13).
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v27).
- **"fourscore" → "eighty"** (v32, 35).

### Notes
- **Joab rebukes David** — forcefully. The king must resume public function. Joab's political acumen.
- **Amasa replaces Joab** — David names Absalom's former general to replace Joab. Punishment for Joab's insubordination in killing Absalom.
- **Shimei pardoned** — for now. Later David instructs Solomon (1 Kgs 2:8–9) to execute him.
- **Mephibosheth's defense** — contradicts Ziba's story. David splits the estate, possibly suspecting he cannot discern who is truthful.
- **Barzillai's humility** (v34–37) — declines royal favor; recommends his son Chimham instead.
- **North-south tension** (v41–43) — the ten northern tribes feel slighted. Sets up Sheba's rebellion in ch 20 and ultimately the kingdom's division under Rehoboam.
