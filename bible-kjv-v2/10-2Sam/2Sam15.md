## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"forty years" → "four years"** (v7) — MT reads "forty," but LXX and Peshitta read "four." Forty years is impossibly long given the narrative chronology; "four" is the likely original.
- **"stranger" → "foreigner"** (v19).
- **"mercy and truth" → "lovingkindness and faithfulness"** (v20) — *chesed ve-emet*.

### Notes
- **Absalom's campaign** — patient 4-year political gathering. "Stole the hearts" idiom: populist appeals.
- **Hebron** — Absalom's birthplace (3:2–3) and David's first capital. Symbolic choice of rebellion-center.
- **Ahithophel joins** — David's top counselor defects. Possibly Bathsheba's grandfather (23:34 suggests); personal grudge over David's sin.
- **David's flight** — leaves Jerusalem without fighting. Prevents bloodshed in the city.
- **Ittai the Gittite** — remarkable loyalty from a Philistine convert. Echoes Ruth: "where you go, I go."
- **Ark sent back** (v25) — David refuses to treat the ark as magic. If Yahweh brings him back, good; if not, he accepts. Theological maturity.
- **Hushai the spy** (v32–37) — David's counter-intelligence in Jerusalem. Sets up the contest of counselors in ch 17.
- **Pss 3, 63** — traditionally linked to David's flight from Absalom.
