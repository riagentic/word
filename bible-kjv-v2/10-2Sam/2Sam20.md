## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Belial" → "worthless fellow"** (v1).
- **"fifth rib" → "belly"** (v10) — as elsewhere.
- **"trench" → "rampart"** (v15).
- **"tribute" → "forced labor"** (v24) — Hebrew *mas*.
- **"chief ruler" → "priest"** (v26) — Hebrew *kohen*. Same textual issue as 8:18.

### Notes
- **Sheba's revolt** — Benjamite-led northern secession. Prefigures the later Israel-Judah split.
- **"No portion in David"** — same cry used when Rehoboam fails (1 Kgs 12:16). The slogan endures.
- **Concubines' permanent isolation** (v3) — Absalom's public violation leaves them in liminal status. Neither widowed nor actively wives.
- **Joab assassinates Amasa** — eliminates his rival with a belt-concealed sword. "Is it well, my brother?" + beard-kiss + belly-thrust. Ruthless professionalism.
- **The wise woman of Abel** (v16–22) — second wise woman in this section (Tekoa was first in ch 14). Negotiates surrender of Sheba; city spared.
- **Court list** (v23–26) — nearly identical to 8:15–18, with some variations. Book's narrative concludes with administrative stability.
