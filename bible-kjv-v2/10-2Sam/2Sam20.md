# 2 Samuel 20

[1] And there chanced to be there a ⟦belial|man of Beliyyaal|man of worthlessness⟧, and his name was ⟦sheba-ben-bichri|Sheva|Sheba⟧ the son of Bichri, a ⟦benjaminite|Yemini|Benjaminite⟧ man. And he blew on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ and said:

"We have no share in David,
and no inheritance have we in the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧;
each man to his tents, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!"

[2] And all the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went up from after David, after ⟦sheba-ben-bichri|Sheva|Sheba⟧ the son of Bichri; and the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ clung to their king, from the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and as far as ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[3] And David came to his house at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And they were bound up until the day of their death, a widowhood of life.

[4] And the king said to Amasa, "Call out for me the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, three days, and you, stand here."

[5] And Amasa went to call out ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he delayed beyond the appointed time that he had designated for him.

[6] And David said to ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧, "Now ⟦sheba-ben-bichri|Sheva|Sheba⟧ the son of Bichri will be more evil for us than ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧. You, take the servants of your lord and pursue after him, lest he has found for himself fortified cities and snatch away our eye."

[7] And the men of ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ went out after him, and the ⟦cherethites|Keretim|Cherethites⟧ and the ⟦pelethites|Peletim|Pelethites⟧ and all the mighty ones; and they went out from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to pursue after ⟦sheba-ben-bichri|Sheva|Sheba⟧ the son of Bichri.

[8] They were by the great stone that is in ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧, and Amasa came before them. And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ was girded with his garment, his clothing, and upon him was the girdle of a sword fastened on his loins in its sheath; and he went out, and it fell.

[9] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ said to Amasa, "Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with you, my brother?" And the right hand of ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ took hold of the beard of Amasa to kiss him.

[10] And Amasa was not on his guard against the sword that was in the hand of ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, and he struck him with it in the belly and poured out his inward parts to the ground, and he did not do it to him a second time, and he died. And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ and ⟦abishai|Avishai|Abishai⟧ his brother pursued after ⟦sheba-ben-bichri|Sheva|Sheba⟧ the son of Bichri.

[11] And a man of the young men of ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ stood over him and said, "Whoever delights in ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, and whoever is for David: after ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧!"

[12] And Amasa was rolling in the blood in the midst of the highway. And the man saw that all the people stood, and he brought Amasa around from the highway into the field and threw a garment over him, as he saw that everyone who came upon him stood.

[13] When he was removed from the highway, every man passed on after ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ to pursue after ⟦sheba-ben-bichri|Sheva|Sheba⟧ the son of Bichri.

[14] And he passed through all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to ⟦abel-beth-maacah|Avel|Abel⟧ and ⟦beth-maacah|Beit Maachah|Beth-maacah⟧, and all the ⟦berites|Berim|Berites⟧; and they assembled and came, yes, after him.

[15] And they came and besieged him in ⟦abel-beth-maacah|Avel|Abel⟧ of ⟦beth-maacah|Beit HaMaachah|Beth-maacah⟧, and they poured out a mound against the city, and it stood at the rampart; and all the people who were with ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ were working ruin, to make the wall fall.

[16] And a wise woman called from the city, "Hear, hear! Say, please, to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, 'Come near to here, and let me speak to you.'"

[17] And he came near to her, and the woman said, "Are you ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧?" And he said, "I am." And she said to him, "Hear the words of your slave woman." And he said, "I am hearing."

[18] And she said, saying, "They used surely to speak at the first, saying, 'Let them surely ask in ⟦abel-beth-maacah|Avel|Abel⟧,' and so they brought it to an end.

[19] I am the peaceable ones of the faithful of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. You are seeking to put to death a city and a mother in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. Why will you swallow up the inheritance of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"

[20] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ answered and said, "Far be it, far be it from me, that I should swallow up or that I should ruin.

[21] The matter is not so; for a man from the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, ⟦sheba-ben-bichri|Sheva|Sheba⟧ the son of Bichri his name, has lifted up his hand against the king, against David. Give him alone, and I will go from the city." And the woman said to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧, "Behold, his head is thrown to you over the wall."

[22] And the woman came to all the people in her wisdom, and they cut off the head of ⟦sheba-ben-bichri|Sheva|Sheba⟧ the son of Bichri and threw it to ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧. And he blew on the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, and they scattered from the city, each man to his tents. And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to the king.

[23] And ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ was over all the host of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and ⟦benaiah|Benayah|Benaiah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ was over the ⟦cherethites|Keretim|Cherethites⟧ and over the ⟦pelethites|Peletim|Pelethites⟧;

[24] and Adoram was over the forced labor; and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ the son of ⟦ahilud|Achilud|Ahilud⟧ was the recorder;

[25] and Sheva was scribe; and ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ and ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ were priests;

[26] and also Ira the ⟦jairite|Yairi|Jairite⟧ was a priest to David.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"there happened to be there" → "there chanced to be there"** (v1) — Hebrew נִקְרָא, "was met with, chanced" (the root of מִקְרֶה "a chance").
- **"a man of Belial" → "a man of Beliyyaal (worthlessness)"** (v1) — Hebrew אִישׁ בְּלִיַּעַל. בְּלִיַּעַל is a common noun, "worthlessness", not a name.
- **"a Benjamite" → "a Yemini (Benjaminite) man"** (v1) — Hebrew אִישׁ יְמִינִי.
- **"trumpet" → "shofar"** (v1, 22) — Hebrew שֹׁפָר, the ram's horn; וַיִּתְקַע בַּשֹּׁפָר "blew on the shofar".
- **"We have no part in David" → "We have no share in David"** (v1) — Hebrew חֵלֶק. The second line keeps the Hebrew order (וְלֹא נַחֲלָה־לָנוּ). **"every man to his tents, O Israel" → "each man to his tents, Israel"** — the Hebrew has no "O". The same cry stands in 1 Kgs 12:16.
- **"went up from after David, and followed Sheba" → "went up from after David, after Sheba"** (v2) — Hebrew מֵאַחֲרֵי דָוִד אַחֲרֵי שֶׁבַע; no second verb. "The men of Israel / of Judah" is the collective singular אִישׁ. **"clave" → "clung"** — Hebrew דָּבַק.
- **"put them in ward" → "put them in a house of watch"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנֵם בֵּית־מִשְׁמֶרֶת, literally "gave them a house of watch"; מִשְׁמֶרֶת is from שָׁמַר, the verb of "to keep the house". **"fed them" → "sustained them"** — Hebrew כִּלְכֵּל. **"concubines"** — the Hebrew has "ten women, concubines"; no "his".
- **"shut up … living in widowhood" → "bound up … a widowhood of life"** (v3) — Hebrew צְרֻרוֹת, "bound, tied up" (shut away); אַלְמְנוּת חַיּוּת, two nouns, "widowhood of life" or "of living". חַיּוּת stands only here and the phrase is difficult: a widowhood while the husband lives, or a widowhood for life. Nothing is added.
- **"Assemble me the men of Judah within three days, and be thou here present" → "Call out for me the men of Judah, three days, and you, stand here"** (v4) — Hebrew הַזְעֶק, "summon by a cry"; שְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים is "three days" with no preposition ("within" is the sense); עֲמֹד "stand".
- **"he tarried longer than the set time which he had appointed him" → "he delayed beyond the appointed time that he had designated for him"** (v5) — qere וַיּוֹחֶר. מוֹעֵד "appointed time" and יְעָדוֹ "designated" are of one root.
- **"do us more harm than did Absalom" → "be more evil for us than Absalom"** (v6) — Hebrew יֵרַע לָנוּ, "will be evil to us".
- **"lest he get him fenced cities, and escape us" → "lest he has found for himself fortified cities and snatch away our eye"** (v6) — Hebrew פֶּן־מָצָא, a perfect; וְהִצִּיל עֵינֵנוּ, literally "and snatch away (deliver) our eye". The phrase is difficult. It is commonly taken as "escape from our sight" (so KJV "escape us"); others take it as "tear out our eye" (do us grievous harm). The Greek has another sense ("overshadow our eyes": from memory). The verse gives the words as printed.
- **"mighty men" → "mighty ones"** (v7) — Hebrew הַגִּבֹּרִים.
- **"went before them" → "came before them"** (v8) — Hebrew בָּא לִפְנֵיהֶם. "When" is not in the Hebrew: "They were by the great stone".
- **"Joab's garment that he had put on was girded unto him, and upon it a girdle with a sword fastened upon his loins in the sheath thereof; and as he went forth it fell out" → "Joab was girded with his garment, his clothing, and upon him was the girdle of a sword fastened on his loins in its sheath; and he went out, and it fell"** (v8) — Hebrew וְיוֹאָב חָגוּר מִדּוֹ לְבֻשׁוּ וְעָלָיו (qere; ketiv ועלו) חֲגוֹר חֶרֶב מְצֻמֶּדֶת עַל־מָתְנָיו בְּתַעְרָהּ וְהוּא יָצָא וַתִּפֹּל. The verse is difficult and is given word for word: "his garment, his clothing" are two nouns side by side (מַד is a soldier's dress, as 1 Sam 17:38); "and he went out" is masculine (Joab; or the sheath), "and it fell" is feminine (the sword). How the sword came into Joab's hand (v10) is not said.
- **"Art thou in health, my brother?" → "Is it shalom (peace) with you, my brother?"** (v9) — Hebrew הֲשָׁלוֹם אַתָּה אָחִי, literally "Are you shalom, my brother?" **"Joab took Amasa by the beard with the right hand" → "the right hand of Joab took hold of the beard of Amasa"** — the hand is the subject in the Hebrew.
- **"took no heed to the sword" → "was not on his guard against the sword"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא־נִשְׁמַר בַּחֶרֶב, nifal of שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"fifth rib" → "belly"** (v10) — Hebrew הַחֹמֶשׁ (also 2:23; 3:27; 4:6); the meaning is not certain: "belly" is the best-supported sense, "fifth rib" takes it from חָמֵשׁ "five".
- **"shed out his bowels" → "poured out his inward parts"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁפֹּךְ מֵעָיו. **"struck him not again" → "did not do it to him a second time"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־שָׁנָה לוֹ, "did not repeat to him". "Pursued" is singular in the Hebrew.
- **"He that favoureth Joab, and he that is for David, let him go after Joab" → "Whoever delights in Joab, and whoever is for David: after Joab!"** (v11) — Hebrew חָפֵץ בְּ "delight in"; the last words are only אַחֲרֵי יוֹאָב, with no verb. "Stood over him" is עָמַד עָלָיו.
- **"wallowed in blood" → "was rolling in the blood"** (v12) — Hebrew מִתְגֹּלֵל בַּדָּם, from גָּלַל "roll". **"removed Amasa" → "brought Amasa around"** — Hebrew וַיַּסֵּב, "made go around". **"stood still" → "stood"** — Hebrew עָמַד, both times.
- **"removed" kept** (v13) — Hebrew הֹגָה, found only here in this form (from יָגָה "thrust away"); the sense is fairly sure from the context. **"all the people" → "every man"** — Hebrew כָּל־אִישׁ.
- **"unto Abel, and to Beth-maachah, and all the Berites" → "to Abel and Beth-maacah, and all the Berites"** (v14) — Hebrew אָבֵלָה וּבֵית מַעֲכָה וְכָל־הַבֵּרִים, with "and" before Beth-maacah; v15 has "Abel of Beth-maacah" (אָבֵלָה בֵּית הַמַּעֲכָה). "The Berites" (הַבֵּרִים) are named only here; the Greek has another word (often read "Bichrites": from memory). The subject "he" is not named (Sheba, or Joab). **"they were gathered together" → "they assembled"** — qere וַיִּקָּהֲלוּ; the ketiv ויקלהו is another form (perhaps "they made light of him"). אַף is "also, even".
- **"cast a bank" → "poured out a mound"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁפְּכוּ סֹלְלָה: the earth of a siege mound is "poured out". **"trench" → "rampart"** — Hebrew חֵל, the outer wall or the ground before the wall. **"battered the wall, to throw it down" → "were working ruin, to make the wall fall"** — Hebrew מַשְׁחִיתִם, the "ruin" verb with no object; some take it as "were planning" (from memory).
- **"Hear the words of thine handmaid" → "your slave woman"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲמָתֶךָ.
- **"They were wont to speak in old time, saying, They shall surely ask counsel at Abel: and so they ended the matter" → "They used surely to speak at the first, saying, 'Let them surely ask in Abel,' and so they brought it to an end"** (v18) — Hebrew דַּבֵּר יְדַבְּרוּ בָרִאשֹׁנָה לֵאמֹר שָׁאֹל יְשָׁאֲלוּ בְּאָבֵל וְכֵן הֵתַמּוּ. Two infinitive absolutes ("speaking they spoke", "asking let them ask"). "Counsel" is not in the Hebrew. The Greek has a longer text here ("in Abel and in Dan …": from memory); the verse keeps the Hebrew.
- **"I am one of them that are peaceable and faithful in Israel" → "I am the peaceable ones of the faithful of Israel"** (v19) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי שְׁלֻמֵי אֱמוּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל: "I" with two plural words in a chain, "the peaceable ones of the faithful ones of Israel". The verse is difficult; "one of" is not written. The woman may speak as the city. שְׁלֻמֵי is of the root of *shalom* but is not the noun.
- **"to destroy a city and a mother" → "to put to death a city and a mother"** (v19) — Hebrew לְהָמִית.
- **"Far be it, far be it from me, that I should swallow up or destroy" → "… that I should swallow up or that I should ruin"** (v20) — Hebrew חָלִילָה חָלִילָה לִי אִם־אֲבַלַּע וְאִם־אַשְׁחִית, the form of an oath ("if I swallow up and if I ruin").
- **"Deliver him only, and I will depart from the city" → "Give him alone, and I will go from the city"** (v21) — Hebrew תְּנוּ־אֹתוֹ לְבַדּוֹ; מֵעַל הָעִיר "from upon the city". **"shall be thrown" → "is thrown"** — Hebrew מֻשְׁלָךְ, a participle. בְּעַד הַחוֹמָה is "through / over the wall".
- **"they retired from the city, every man to his tent" → "they scattered from the city, each man to his tents"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיָּפֻצוּ; אִישׁ לְאֹהָלָיו, the words of v1.
- **"over all the host of Israel"** (v23) — Hebrew אֶל כָּל־הַצָּבָא יִשְׂרָאֵל, with אֶל "to" where עַל "over" is expected, and "the host, Israel" side by side. **"Cherethites"** — qere הַכְּרֵתִי; the ketiv has הכרי ("the Carites", as 2 Kgs 11:4).
- **"tribute" → "forced labor"** (v24) — Hebrew *mas*. **"recorder"** — Hebrew הַמַּזְכִּיר, "the one who brings to remembrance".
- **"Sheva was scribe"** (v25) — qere שְׁוָא; the ketiv has שיא. In 8:17 the scribe is Seraiah.
- **"chief ruler" → "priest"** (v26) — Hebrew *kohen*. The same word as in 8:18.

### Notes
- **Sheba's revolt** — a Benjaminite leads the men of Israel away from David.
- **"No share in David"** — the same cry is raised under Rehoboam (1 Kgs 12:16).
- **The concubines** (v3) — the ten of 15:16; 16:21–22.
- **Joab and Amasa** (v8–10) — Amasa had been set over the host in Joab's place (19:13).
- **The wise woman of Abel** (v16–22) — compare the wise woman of Tekoa (ch 14).
- **Court list** (v23–26) — compare 8:15–18.
