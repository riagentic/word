# Job 8

[1] Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

[2] "How long will you say these things?
How long shall the words of your mouth be a great wind?

[3] Does *Elohim* pervert justice?
Does Shaddai pervert righteousness?

[4] If your children have sinned against him,
he has sent them away for their transgression.

[5] If you would earnestly seek *Elohim*,
and make supplication to Shaddai,

[6] if you are pure and upright,
surely then he will rouse himself for you
and restore your righteous home.

[7] Though your beginning was small,
your latter end will be very great.

[8] For inquire of the former age,
and apply yourself to what their fathers have searched out.

[9] For we are but of yesterday and know nothing,
for our days on earth are a shadow.

[10] Will they not teach you and tell you,
and utter words out of their heart?

[11] Can the papyrus grow where there is no marsh?
Can the reed flourish without water?

[12] While still in its greenness, not cut down,
it withers before any other plant.

[13] Such are the paths of all who forget *Elohim*;
the hope of the godless shall perish.

[14] His confidence is gossamer;
his trust is a spider's web.

[15] He leans on his house, but it does not stand;
he holds fast to it, but it does not endure.

[16] He is well-watered before the sun,
and his shoots spread over his garden;

[17] his roots twine around a stone heap,
he grips a house of stones.

[18] If he is torn from his place,
it will deny him: 'I have never seen you.'

[19] Behold, this is the joy of his way;
from the dust others will sprout.

[20] Behold, *Elohim* will not reject a blameless man,
nor will he take the hand of evildoers.

[21] He will yet fill your mouth with laughter
and your lips with shouts of joy.

[22] Those who hate you will be clothed with shame;
the tent of the wicked shall be no more."

## Changes

- **"hypocrite" → "godless"** (v13) — *chanef*; profane, not a hypocrite per se.
- **"spider's web" — retained** (v14) — *bet-akavish*.
- **"rush... flag" → "papyrus... reed"** (v11) — *gome... achu*.
- **"whose hope shall be cut off" → "His confidence is gossamer"** (v14) — Hebrew *asher yaqot kislo*; his confidence = a thread easily broken; gossamer conveys fragility.

### Notes
- **Bildad's first speech** — appeals to tradition and ancestral wisdom. Straightforward retribution theology.
- **Harsh about Job's children** (v4) — "if they sinned, they deserved it." Callous application.
- **Reed without water** — metaphor for godless flourishing; quick withering.
- **Spider's web** — image for insubstantial hope.
- **Conditional restoration** (v5-7, 20-22) — if Job is truly righteous, prosperity returns. If not, confirmed guilt.
