# Job 8

[1] And Bildad the ⟦shuhite|Shuchi|Shuhite⟧ answered and said:

[2] "How long will you utter these things,
and the sayings of your mouth be a mighty wind?

[3] Does ⟦el|El|God⟧ pervert judgment?
Or does ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ pervert righteousness?

[4] If your sons sinned against him,
then he sent them away into the hand of their transgression.

[5] If you yourself will seek ⟦el|El|God⟧ earnestly,
and plead with ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧,

[6] if you are pure and upright,
surely now he will stir himself up over you
and make whole the habitation of your righteousness.

[7] And your beginning will be small,
and your latter end will grow very great.

[8] For ask, please, of the former generation,
and set yourself to the searching of their fathers.

[9] For we are of yesterday and do not know,
for a shadow are our days upon the earth.

[10] Will not they teach you, say to you,
and bring out words from their heart?

[11] Does papyrus rise high without a marsh?
Does reed grass grow great without water?

[12] While it is still in its shoot, not plucked,
and before any grass it dries out.

[13] So are the paths of all who forget ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and the hope of the godless perishes,

[14] whose confidence is cut off,
and a spider's house is his trust.

[15] He leans on his house, and it does not stand;
he holds fast to it, and it does not stand up.

[16] He is full of sap before the sun,
and over his garden his shoot goes out.

[17] Over a heap his roots are entwined;
a house of stones he beholds.

[18] If he swallows him up from his place,
then it will lie to him: 'I have not seen you.'

[19] Behold, this is the joy of his way,
and from the dust others spring up.

[20] Behold, ⟦el|El|God⟧ does not reject the blameless,
and he does not take hold of the hand of evildoers,

[21] until he fills your mouth with laughter,
and your lips with a shout.

[22] Those who hate you will put on shame,
and the tent of the wicked is no more."

## Changes

- **"Then … answered and said" → "And … answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the speech headings in Job.
- **"God" → "El"** (v3, 5, 13, 20) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*. In v3 the הַ before it is the question particle, not the article. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי; its meaning is not certain.
- **"How long wilt thou speak these things? and how long shall the words of thy mouth be like a strong wind?" → "How long will you utter these things, and the sayings of your mouth be a mighty wind?"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּמַלֵּל (מִלֵּל "utter, speak"); אִמְרֵי־פִיךָ "the sayings of your mouth"; רוּחַ כַּבִּיר "a mighty wind" (רוּחַ is also "spirit, breath"). "How long" stands once; "like" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"pervert judgment … pervert justice" → "pervert judgment … pervert righteousness"** (v3) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", צֶדֶק "righteousness"; the verb עִוֵּת "make crooked" both times. The second line opens with וְאִם, "or".
- **"If thy children have sinned … and he have cast them away for their transgression" → "If your sons sinned … then he sent them away into the hand of their transgression"** (v4) — Hebrew בָּנֶיךָ "your sons"; וַיְשַׁלְּחֵם "and he sent them away" (piel of שָׁלַח); בְּיַד־פִּשְׁעָם "into the hand of their transgression", not "for". The וַ is taken as opening the second half of the condition ("then"); it may also continue the condition: "and he sent them away …".
- **"seek unto God betimes, and make thy supplication to the Almighty" → "you yourself will seek El earnestly, and plead with Shaddai"** (v5) — Hebrew אַתָּה "you" written out; שִׁחֵר אֶל, from שַׁחַר "dawn": "seek early, seek earnestly"; תִּתְחַנָּן, hitpael of חָנַן "show favor": "plead with", literally "seek favor for oneself".
- **"pure and upright; surely now he would awake for thee, and make the habitation of thy righteousness prosperous" → "pure and upright, surely now he will stir himself up over you and make whole the habitation of your righteousness"** (v6) — Hebrew זַךְ "pure"; כִּי עַתָּה "surely now"; יָעִיר עָלֶיךָ, hifil of עוּר without an object, "he will rouse (himself) over you": "himself" is supplied. שִׁלַּם, piel of the root of שָׁלוֹם, "make whole" (the "repay" of the word list does not fit here: nothing is owed); נְוַת צִדְקֶךָ "the habitation of your righteousness" (נָוֶה, "pasture, abode").
- **"Though thy beginning was small, yet thy latter end should greatly increase" → "And your beginning will be small, and your latter end will grow very great"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהָיָה "and it will be" (future, not "though … was"); יִשְׂגֶּה "grow great" (the verb of v11).
- **"enquire, I pray thee, of the former age, and prepare thyself to the search of their fathers" → "ask, please, of the former generation, and set yourself to the searching of their fathers"** (v8) — Hebrew שְׁאַל־נָא, with נָא "please"; דּוֹר "generation"; כּוֹנֵן "set firm, set yourself"; חֵקֶר "searching" (noun of חָקַר "examine").
- **"For we are but of yesterday, and know nothing, because our days upon earth are a shadow" → "For we are of yesterday and do not know, for a shadow are our days upon the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew תְמוֹל אֲנַחְנוּ "we are yesterday"; "but" and "nothing" are not in the Hebrew (וְלֹא נֵדָע "and we do not know"). צֵל "shadow" (of shelter the word list has "shade").
- **"Shall not they teach thee, and tell thee, and utter words out of their heart?" → "Will not they teach you, say to you, and bring out words from their heart?"** (v10) — Hebrew הֵם "they" written out; יֹאמְרוּ "say"; יוֹצִאוּ מִלִּים "bring out words".
- **"Can the rush grow up without mire? can the flag grow without water?" → "Does papyrus rise high without a marsh? Does reed grass grow great without water?"** (v11) — Hebrew גֹּמֶא "papyrus"; בִּצָּה "marsh"; אָחוּ "reed grass", an Egyptian loanword; יִגְאֶה "rise high", יִשְׂגֶּה "grow great".
- **"Whilst it is yet in his greenness, and not cut down, it withereth before any other herb" → "While it is still in its shoot, not plucked, and before any grass it dries out"** (v12) — Hebrew אֵב "shoot, bud" (Song 6:11); יִקָּטֵף "be plucked"; חָצִיר "grass"; יִיבָשׁ "dries out". "Other" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"hypocrite's hope" → "the hope of the godless"** (v13) — Hebrew חָנֵף, one who is profane, polluted (the verb is used of a land defiled, Num 35:33); not "hypocrite". **"So are the paths"** — Hebrew כֵּן "so"; the hope "perishes" (אָבַד). The sentence runs on into v14.
- **"Whose hope shall be cut off, and whose trust shall be a spider's web" → "whose confidence is cut off, and a spider's house is his trust"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־יָקוֹט כִּסְלוֹ. יָקוֹט is uncertain: as pointed a verb, "is cut off" (from קָטַט, or קוּט "loathe"); others read a noun, "summer threads, gossamer" (so the earlier rendering): the pointing is kept. כֶּסֶל "confidence" (also "loins", "folly"). בֵּית עַכָּבִישׁ "a spider's house"; מִבְטָח "trust, what one trusts in".
- **"but it shall not stand … but it shall not endure" → "and it does not stand … and it does not stand up"** (v15) — Hebrew עָמַד "stand", then קוּם "rise, stand up": two verbs. Each וְ is "and".
- **"He is green before the sun, and his branch shooteth forth in his garden" → "He is full of sap before the sun, and over his garden his shoot goes out"** (v16) — Hebrew רָטֹב "moist, full of sap"; יוֹנַקְתּוֹ "his shoot" (singular, a sucker); עַל "over"; תֵצֵא "goes out". Who "he" is (the godless of v13, or another plant set against him) is not said.
- **"His roots are wrapped about the heap, and seeth the place of stones" → "Over a heap his roots are entwined; a house of stones he beholds"** (v17) — Hebrew עַל־גַּל "over a heap" ("stone" is not written in the first line); יְסֻבָּכוּ "are entwined"; בֵּית אֲבָנִים "a house of stones"; יֶחֱזֶה "he beholds" (חָזָה). The earlier rendering "he grips" follows a change of the verb (to יֶאֱחֹז) that some make after the Septuagint (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"If he destroy him from his place, then it shall deny him, saying, I have not seen thee" → "If he swallows him up from his place, then it will lie to him: 'I have not seen you'"** (v18) — Hebrew יְבַלְּעֶנּוּ "he swallows him up"; who "he" is, is not named (it may be El, or "one"). וְכִחֶשׁ בּוֹ "and it will lie to him" (the place speaks). "Never" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Behold, this is the joy of his way, and out of the earth shall others grow" → "Behold, this is the joy of his way, and from the dust others spring up"** (v19) — Hebrew הֵן "behold"; מְשׂוֹשׂ "joy" (said in bitter irony); עָפָר "dust"; אַחֵר "another", singular, with a plural verb יִצְמָחוּ "they spring up".
- **"God will not cast away a perfect man, neither will he help the evil doers" → "El does not reject the blameless, and he does not take hold of the hand of evildoers"** (v20) — Hebrew מָאַס "reject"; תָּם "blameless" ("man" is not written); יַחֲזִיק בְּיַד "take hold of the hand of".
- **"Till he fill thy mouth with laughing, and thy lips with rejoicing" → "until he fills your mouth with laughter, and your lips with a shout"** (v21) — Hebrew עַד "until", joined to v20; the earlier rendering "He will yet fill" reads עוֹד "yet" for עַד: the Masoretic Text is kept. תְּרוּעָה "a shout" (of voices). יְמַלֵּה is a by-form of יְמַלֵּא "fill".
- **"shall be clothed with shame; and the dwelling place of the wicked shall come to nought" → "will put on shame, and the tent of the wicked is no more"** (v22) — Hebrew יִלְבְּשׁוּ "put on, wear"; אֹהֶל "tent"; אֵינֶנּוּ "it is not".

### Notes
- **Bildad's first speech** — appeals to tradition and ancestral wisdom. Straightforward retribution theology.
- **Harsh about Job's sons** (v4) — "if they sinned, he sent them away into the hand of their transgression." Callous application.
- **Papyrus and reed grass without water** (v11–12) — image of the godless: quick withering.
- **Spider's house** (v14) — image for insubstantial trust.
- **Conditional restoration** (v5–7, 20–22) — if Job is truly pure and upright, prosperity returns. If not, confirmed guilt.
