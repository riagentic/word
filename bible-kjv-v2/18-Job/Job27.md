# Job 27

[1] ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ continued his discourse and said:

[2] "As ⟦el|El|God⟧ lives — who has taken away my right —
and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧, who has embittered my soul —

[3] as long as my breath is in me,
and the spirit of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ is in my nostrils,

[4] my lips will not speak falsehood,
nor my tongue utter deceit.

[5] Far be it from me that I should declare you right;
till I die, I will not put away my integrity from me.

[6] I hold fast my righteousness, and will not let it go;
my heart will not reproach me as long as I live.

[7] Let my enemy be as the wicked,
and my adversary as the unrighteous.

[8] For what is the hope of the godless when he is cut off,
when ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ takes away his soul?

[9] Will ⟦el|El|God⟧ hear his cry
when distress comes upon him?

[10] Will he take delight in ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧?
Will he call upon ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ at all times?

[11] I will teach you about the hand of ⟦el|El|God⟧;
what is with ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ I will not conceal.

[12] Behold, all of you have seen it yourselves;
why then have you become so utterly vain?

[13] This is the portion of the wicked man with ⟦el|El|God⟧,
the heritage that oppressors receive from ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧:

[14] If his children are multiplied, it is for the sword;
his offspring shall not be satisfied with bread.

[15] Those who survive him the plague buries;
their widows will not weep.

[16] Though he heaps up silver like dust
and piles up clothing like clay,

[17] he may pile it up, but the righteous will wear it;
the innocent will divide the silver.

[18] He builds his house like a moth,
like a booth that a watchman makes.

[19] Rich he lies down, but does so no more;
he opens his eyes, and it is gone.

[20] Terrors overtake him like a flood;
in the night a whirlwind carries him off.

[21] The east wind lifts him up and he is gone;
it sweeps him out of his place.

[22] It hurls at him without sparing;
he flees headlong from its power.

[23] It claps its hands at him
and hisses at him out of his place."

## Changes

- **"God" → "El"** (v2, 9, 11, 13) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"hypocrite" → "godless"** (v8) — *chanef*.
- **"buried in death" → "the plague buries"** (v15) — *ba-maweth yiqqaveru*; "in death they are buried," likely meaning pestilence.

### Notes
- **Continued discourse** — Job takes up the *mashal* form (teaching poem).
- **Dramatic oath** (v2) — swears by the Eloah whose justice he denies. Heightens honest paradox.
- **Refusal to capitulate** — "Will not put away my integrity till I die."
- **Ironic "wicked man's fate"** (v13-23) — Job describes the fate the friends have been promising. Reads as if he has shifted to orthodoxy, but context (v11-12) suggests he's mocking — the friends see it but remain vain.
- **Some scholars hold v13-23 originally belonged to Zophar's missing third speech** (since Zophar never speaks a third time), redistributed.
