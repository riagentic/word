# Job 27

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ again lifted up his parable and said:

[2] "As ⟦el|El|God⟧ lives, who has taken away my judgment,
and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧, who has made my soul bitter —

[3] for all the while my breath is in me,
and the spirit of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ is in my nostrils,

[4] my lips shall not speak injustice,
and my tongue shall not utter deceit.

[5] Far be it from me that I should justify you;
until I expire I will not remove my integrity from me.

[6] My righteousness I have held fast, and I will not let it go;
my heart shall not reproach any of my days.

[7] Let my enemy be as the wicked,
and he who rises up against me as the unjust.

[8] For what is the hope of the godless when he cuts off,
when ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ draws out his soul?

[9] Will ⟦el|El|God⟧ hear his cry
when distress comes upon him?

[10] Or will he delight himself in ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧,
call on ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ at every time?

[11] I will teach you by the hand of ⟦el|El|God⟧;
what is with ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ I will not hide.

[12] Behold, you yourselves, all of you, have beheld;
and why then do you become vain with vapor?

[13] This is the share of a wicked man with ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and the inheritance of the ruthless, which they take from ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧:

[14] If his sons are many, it is for the sword,
and his offspring shall not be sated with bread.

[15] His survivors shall be buried in death,
and his widows shall not weep.

[16] If he heaps up silver like dust,
and prepares clothing like clay,

[17] he prepares it, and the righteous shall put it on,
and the silver the innocent shall divide.

[18] He builds his house like the moth,
and like a booth that a guard makes.

[19] Rich he lies down, and he is not gathered;
he opens his eyes, and he is not.

[20] Terrors overtake him like the waters;
by night a tempest steals him away.

[21] The east wind lifts him up, and he goes,
and it storms him away from his place.

[22] And it throws at him and does not spare;
from its hand he would surely flee.

[23] It claps its palms at him,
and hisses at him from his place."

## Changes

- **"Moreover Job continued his parable, and said" → "And Job again lifted up his parable and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף אִיּוֹב שְׂאֵת מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר, literally "and Job added to lift up his parable"; the same words open 29:1 and have the same wording there. נָשָׂא מָשָׁל "lift up one's parable" as Num 23:7: מָשָׁל is a saying built on a likeness (a proverb, a figurative poem, a taunt song); the word does not say "discourse".
- **"God" → "El"** (v2, 9, 11, 13) — Hebrew אֵל. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v2, 10, 11, 13) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי; the meaning of the name is not certain. **"God" → "Eloah"** (v3, 8, 10) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ.
- **"who hath taken away my judgment … who hath vexed my soul" → "who has taken away my judgment … who has made my soul bitter"** (v2) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִי "my judgment" (= my right, my case); הֵמַר "made bitter". The oath "As El lives" is kept as it stands.
- **"All the while my breath is in me" → "for all the while …"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי "for" at the head. **"the spirit of God is in my nostrils"** — Hebrew רוּחַ (spirit, wind, breath: one word) beside נְשָׁמָה "breath"; אַף "nose, nostrils".
- **"My lips shall not speak wickedness, nor my tongue utter deceit"** (v4) — the oath form אִם … אִם, literally "if my lips speak injustice, if my tongue utters deceit", means "they shall not"; the verse gives that meaning. עַוְלָה "injustice"; הָגָה "utter"; רְמִיָּה "deceit".
- **"God forbid that I should justify you: till I die" → "Far be it from me that I should justify you; until I expire"** (v5) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לִּי, literally "a profane thing to me"; "God" is not in the Hebrew. אֶגְוָע "I expire" (beside מוּת "die"). **"I will not remove mine integrity from me"** — Hebrew תֻּמָּתִי, as 2:3, 9.
- **"my heart shall not reproach me so long as I live" → "my heart shall not reproach any of my days"** (v6) — Hebrew לֹא־יֶחֱרַף לְבָבִי מִיָּמָי, literally "my heart does not reproach from my days"; "me" is not written. **"I hold fast" → "I have held fast"** — perfect.
- **"him that riseth up against me as the unrighteous" → "he who rises up against me as the unjust"** (v7) — Hebrew עַוָּל, of the root of עַוְלָה "injustice" (v4).
- **"the hypocrite, though he hath gained, when God taketh away his soul" → "the godless when he cuts off, when Eloah draws out his soul"** (v8) — Hebrew חָנֵף, one who is profane (the root is "be polluted"), not "hypocrite". כִּי יִבְצָע: the verb בָּצַע is "cut off" and also "make (unjust) gain" (noun בֶּצַע); who cuts off is not said. יֵשֶׁל is uncertain: from שָׁלָה "draw out", or from נָשַׁל "make drop off".
- **"Will he delight himself in the Almighty? will he always call upon God?" → "Or will he delight himself … call on Eloah at every time?"** (v10) — Hebrew אִם "or" continues the question of v9; בְּכָל־עֵת "at every time".
- **"I will teach you by the hand of God"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּיַד־אֵל, "by" or "concerning" the hand of El. **"will I not conceal" → "I will not hide"** — Hebrew כִּחֵד.
- **"ye yourselves have seen it; why then are ye thus altogether vain?" → "you yourselves, all of you, have beheld; and why then do you become vain with vapor?"** (v12) — Hebrew חֲזִיתֶם "you have beheld", with no object; הֶבֶל תֶּהְבָּלוּ, the noun הֶבֶל "vapor, breath" with its own verb, literally "vapor you vapor".
- **"the portion … the heritage of oppressors, which they shall receive" → "the share … the inheritance of the ruthless, which they take"** (v13) — Hebrew חֵלֶק "share"; נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; עָרִיץ "ruthless, one who strikes terror"; יִקָּחוּ "they take". אָדָם רָשָׁע "a wicked man".
- **"his children" → "his sons"** (v14) — Hebrew בָּנָיו. **"satisfied" → "sated"**.
- **"Those that remain of him shall be buried in death: and his widows" → "His survivors shall be buried in death, and his widows"** (v15) — qere שְׂרִידָיו "his survivors"; the ketiv שרידו is singular, "his survivor". בַּמָּוֶת "in death" is kept; "death" may here mean the plague, as Jer 15:2; 18:21, but the Hebrew has the plain word. The widows are "his", not "their".
- **"prepare raiment as the clay" → "prepares clothing like clay"** (v16) — Hebrew חֹמֶר "clay" (the "mortar" of the brick passages is the same word). **"Though he heap up"** — Hebrew אִם "if".
- **"He may prepare it, but the just shall put it on, and the innocent shall divide the silver" → "he prepares it, and the righteous shall put it on, and the silver the innocent shall divide"** (v17) — Hebrew יָכִין וְצַדִּיק יִלְבָּשׁ; "it" is supplied twice; the Hebrew order of the second line is kept. נָקִי "innocent", the word "clear" of an oath.
- **"as a moth, and as a booth that the keeper maketh" → "like the moth, and like a booth that a guard makes"** (v18) — Hebrew כָעָשׁ "like the moth"; some versions read "spider" (cited from memory), as 8:14; the Hebrew is kept. נֹצֵר "one who guards".
- **"The rich man shall lie down, but he shall not be gathered: he openeth his eyes, and he is not" → "Rich he lies down, and he is not gathered; he opens his eyes, and he is not"** (v19) — Hebrew עָשִׁיר יִשְׁכַּב וְלֹא יֵאָסֵף. The Septuagint is commonly cited as "and he does not add" (from memory: יֹסִף, "he does it no more"); the MT "he is not gathered" is kept. אֵינֶנּוּ "he is not": the man, not his wealth.
- **"Terrors take hold on him as waters, a tempest stealeth him away in the night" → "Terrors overtake him like the waters; by night a tempest steals him away"** (v20) — Hebrew בַּלָּהוֹת "terrors"; כַמַּיִם "like the waters" (not "flood"); סוּפָה "tempest"; גְּנָבַתּוּ "steals him".
- **"and he departeth: and as a storm hurleth him out of his place" → "and he goes, and it storms him away from his place"** (v21) — Hebrew קָדִים "east wind" (literally "the front": the east); וְיֵלַךְ "and he goes" (or "and it goes"); וִישָׂעֲרֵהוּ "and it storms him away".
- **"For God shall cast upon him, and not spare: he would fain flee out of his hand" → "And it throws at him and does not spare; from its hand he would surely flee"** (v22) — "God" is not in the Hebrew; the subject is the east wind of v21, or left open. בָּרוֹחַ יִבְרָח, literally "fleeing he would flee"; "hand" is יָד.
- **"Men shall clap their hands at him, and shall hiss him out of his place" → "It claps its palms at him, and hisses at him from his place"** (v23) — "Men" is not in the Hebrew; the verbs are singular. עָלֵימוֹ כַפֵּימוֹ have the poetic suffix ־מוֹ, plural in form ("at them … their palms") and commonly read as singular here. כַּף "palm".

### Notes
- **The parable** (v1) — Job takes up the *mashal* again; the same heading stands at 29:1.
- **Oath** (v2) — he swears by the El who has, he says, taken away his judgment.
- **Refusal to give way** (v5–6) — "I will not remove my integrity from me" until death.
- **The wicked man's fate** (v13–23) — Job describes the fate the friends have been promising. It reads as if he now speaks their teaching; v11–12 may suggest he turns it back on them.
- **Some scholars hold that v13–23 belonged to Zophar's missing third speech** (Zophar does not speak a third time).
