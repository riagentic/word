# Job 13

[1] "Behold, my eye has seen all;
my ear has heard, and has understood it.

[2] As you know, so I too know;
I do not fall below you.

[3] But I — to ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ I would speak,
and to argue with ⟦el|El|God⟧ I desire.

[4] But you are smearers of falsehood,
healers of worthlessness, all of you.

[5] Would that you would surely be silent,
and it would be wisdom for you!

[6] Hear, please, my argument,
and to the contentions of my lips give heed.

[7] Will you speak injustice for ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and for him speak deceit?

[8] Will you lift up his face?
Or will you contend for ⟦el|El|God⟧?

[9] Is it good when he examines you?
Or as one mocks a mortal man, will you mock him?

[10] He will surely reprove you,
if in secret you lift up faces.

[11] Will not his loftiness terrify you,
and his dread fall upon you?

[12] Your memorials are proverbs of ashes;
ramparts of clay are your ramparts.

[13] Be silent, away from me, and let me speak, I myself,
and let pass over me what may.

[14] Why should I carry my flesh in my teeth,
and put my soul in my palm?

[15] Behold, he will slay me; for him I hope;
only my ways I will argue to his face.

[16] This also is to me for salvation,
that a godless one does not come before him.

[17] Surely hear my word,
and my declaration — in your ears.

[18] Behold now, I have arranged the judgment;
I know that I, I shall be righteous.

[19] Who is he who will contend with me?
For now I would be silent and expire.

[20] Only two things do not do with me;
then from your face I will not hide myself:

[21] your palm put far away from upon me,
and your dread, let it not terrify me.

[22] And call, and I will answer;
or I will speak, and reply to me.

[23] How many iniquities and sins are mine?
My transgression and my sin make known to me.

[24] Why do you hide your face,
and reckon me for an enemy to you?

[25] Will you make a driven leaf tremble?
And dry stubble will you pursue?

[26] For you write bitter things against me,
and make me possess the iniquities of my youth.

[27] And you put my feet in the stocks,
and watch all my paths;
on the roots of my feet you draw a line.

[28] And he, like a rotten thing, wears out,
like a garment that the moth has eaten."

## Changes

- **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v3) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי; the meaning of the name is not certain. **"God" → "El"** (v3, 7, 8) — Hebrew אֵל.
- **"Lo, mine eye hath seen all this" → "Behold, my eye has seen all"** (v1) — Hebrew הֶן־כֹּל, "this" is not written. **"heard and understood it"** — Hebrew וַתָּבֶן לָהּ, "and understood it / for itself".
- **"What ye know, the same do I know also" → "As you know, so I too know"** (v2) — Hebrew כְּדַעְתְּכֶם יָדַעְתִּי גַם־אָנִי, literally "like your knowing, I know, I too". **"I am not inferior unto you" → "I do not fall below you"** — literally "I am not falling from you", as 12:3.
- **"I desire to reason with God" → "to argue with El I desire"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהוֹכֵחַ אֶל־אֵל אֶחְפָּץ. הוֹכִיחַ is the verb "reprove"; of one who brings his case it is "argue" (v3, 15; the noun תּוֹכַחַת "argument", v6); of El it stays "reprove" (v10). One root in v3, 6, 10, 15.
- **"ye are forgers of lies, ye are all physicians of no value" → "you are smearers of falsehood, healers of worthlessness"** (v4) — Hebrew טֹפְלֵי־שָׁקֶר: טָפַל "smear, plaster over" (so 14:17); רֹפְאֵי אֱלִל, אֱלִיל "worthless(ness)", the word of "worthless idols".
- **"O that ye would altogether hold your peace!" → "Would that you would surely be silent"** (v5) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן הַחֲרֵשׁ תַּחֲרִישׁוּן, literally "who will give: being silent you will be silent". **"it should be your wisdom" → "it would be wisdom for you"** — Hebrew וּתְהִי לָכֶם לְחָכְמָה.
- **"my reasoning … the pleadings of my lips" → "my argument … the contentions of my lips"** (v6) — Hebrew תּוֹכַחְתִּי; רִבוֹת, plural of רִיב "contention, case". **"please"** — Hebrew נָא.
- **"Will ye speak wickedly for God? and talk deceitfully for him?" → "Will you speak injustice for El, and for him speak deceit?"** (v7) — Hebrew עַוְלָה "injustice", רְמִיָּה "deceit".
- **"Will ye accept his person?" → "Will you lift up his face?"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew הֲפָנָיו תִּשָּׂאוּן, נָשָׂא פָּנִים "lift up the face" (= show partiality); in v10 "faces", without "his". **"will ye contend for God?"** — Hebrew תְּרִיבוּן, the verb of רִיב (v6, 19).
- **"when he should search you out" → "when he examines you"** (v9) — Hebrew חָקַר. **"as one man mocketh another, do ye so mock him?" → "as one mocks a mortal man, will you mock him?"** — Hebrew כְּהָתֵל בֶּאֱנוֹשׁ תְּהָתֵלּוּ בוֹ; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; הֵתֵל "mock, trifle with, deceive".
- **"He will surely reprove you"** (v10) — Hebrew הוֹכֵחַ יוֹכִיחַ, literally "reproving he will reprove". **"if ye do secretly accept persons" → "if in secret you lift up faces"**.
- **"his excellency" → "his loftiness"** (v11) — Hebrew שְׂאֵתוֹ, literally "his lifting up", from נָשָׂא, the verb of "lift up the face" in v8 and v10. **"make you afraid" → "terrify you"** — Hebrew בִּעֵת, as v21. **"his dread"** — Hebrew פַּחַד (אֵימָה in v21 is also "dread": two words).
- **"Your remembrances are like unto ashes, your bodies to bodies of clay" → "Your memorials are proverbs of ashes; ramparts of clay are your ramparts"** (v12) — Hebrew זִכְרֹנֵיכֶם מִשְׁלֵי־אֵפֶר; מָשָׁל "proverb, parable", "like unto" is not written. גַּב is anything rounded (a back, a mound, the boss of a shield); "ramparts" is the likeliest sense, uncertain. חֹמֶר "clay", the potter's material (elsewhere "mortar").
- **"Hold your peace, let me alone, that I may speak" → "Be silent, away from me, and let me speak, I myself"** (v13) — Hebrew הַחֲרִישׁוּ מִמֶּנִּי וַאֲדַבְּרָה־אָנִי. **"let come on me what will" → "let pass over me what may"** — Hebrew וְיַעֲבֹר עָלַי מָה.
- **"Wherefore do I take my flesh in my teeth, and put my life in mine hand?" → "Why should I carry my flesh in my teeth, and put my soul in my palm?"** (v14) — Hebrew עַל־מָה "on account of what"; נָשָׂא "carry, lift"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; כַּף "palm". Some join עַל־מָה to v13 ("whatever"), which the accents do not.
- **"Though he slay me, yet will I trust in him" → "Behold, he will slay me; for him I hope"** (v15) — the verse follows the qere לוֹ "for him"; the written text (ketiv) is לֹא "not": "Behold, he will slay me; I will not hope (I have no hope)". The two readings say opposite things; the tradition reads לוֹ. הֵן "behold" (also "if"); "though" and "yet" are not written. יִחֵל "hope, wait". **"but I will maintain mine own ways before him" → "only my ways I will argue to his face"** — Hebrew אַךְ־דְּרָכַי אֶל־פָּנָיו אוֹכִיחַ.
- **"He also shall be my salvation" → "This also is to me for salvation"** (v16) — Hebrew גַּם־הוּא־לִי לִישׁוּעָה; הוּא is "he" or "it": "He also is to me for salvation" is possible. **"an hypocrite" → "a godless one"** — Hebrew חָנֵף, of the root "be polluted, profane" (Num 35:33).
- **"Hear diligently my speech" → "Surely hear my word"** (v17) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ שָׁמוֹעַ מִלָּתִי, literally "hear, hearing". **"and my declaration with your ears"** — Hebrew וְאַחֲוָתִי בְּאָזְנֵיכֶם, without a verb.
- **"I have ordered my cause" → "I have arranged the judgment"** (v18) — Hebrew עָרַכְתִּי מִשְׁפָּט, "my" is not written; מִשְׁפָּט is here the case brought to be judged. **"I shall be justified" → "I, I shall be righteous"** — Hebrew כִּי־אֲנִי אֶצְדָּק, the active "be righteous" with the pronoun stressed.
- **"For now, if I hold my tongue, I shall give up the ghost" → "For now I would be silent and expire"** (v19) — Hebrew כִּי־עַתָּה אַחֲרִישׁ וְאֶגְוָע; "if" is not written.
- **"Only do not two things unto me" → "Only two things do not do with me"** (v20) — Hebrew שְׁתַּיִם, "things" supplied; עִמָּדִי "with me". Here Job turns to El ("you" singular, v20–27).
- **"Withdraw thine hand far from me: and let not thy dread make me afraid" → "your palm put far away from upon me, and your dread, let it not terrify me"** (v21) — Hebrew כַּפְּךָ "your palm"; אֵימָה "dread".
- **"answer thou me" → "reply to me"** (v22) — Hebrew וַהֲשִׁיבֵנִי, literally "make return to me" (עָנָה "answer" is the first line).
- **"How many are mine iniquities and sins?"** (v23) — Hebrew כַּמָּה לִי עֲוֹנוֹת וְחַטָּאוֹת, literally "how many to me". **"make me to know" → "make known to me"**.
- **"holdest me for thine enemy" → "reckon me for an enemy to you"** (v24) — Hebrew חָשַׁב "reckon". אוֹיֵב "enemy" sounds like אִיּוֹב "Iyov".
- **"Wilt thou break a leaf driven to and fro?" → "Will you make a driven leaf tremble?"** (v25) — Hebrew תַּעֲרוֹץ, עָרַץ "make tremble, terrify" (בִּעֵת "terrify" is v11, 21). **"the dry stubble"** — Hebrew קַשׁ.
- **"makest me to possess"** (v26) — Hebrew וְתוֹרִישֵׁנִי, hifil of יָרַשׁ.
- **"lookest narrowly unto all my paths" → "watch all my paths"** (v27) — Hebrew תִּשְׁמוֹר. **"thou settest a print upon the heels of my feet" → "on the roots of my feet you draw a line"** — Hebrew עַל־שָׁרְשֵׁי רַגְלַי תִּתְחַקֶּה: שָׁרְשֵׁי רַגְלַי "the roots of my feet" (the soles); תִּתְחַקֶּה, hitpael of חָקָה "carve, inscribe" ("you engrave for yourself"); "line" is supplied, and whether a mark or a boundary is meant is uncertain. סַד "stocks" is found only here and in 33:11.
- **"he, as a rotten thing, consumeth" → "he, like a rotten thing, wears out"** (v28) — Hebrew וְהוּא כְּרָקָב יִבְלֶה; בָּלָה "wear out". "He" is not named: Job speaks of himself in the third person (or of man in general); "man" is not written.

### Notes
- **Shaddai and El** (v3) — Job turns from the friends to the one he wants to meet.
- **"For him I hope"** (v15) — the reading tradition (qere); the written text says "I will not hope". The verse follows the qere.
- **"Do not do two things"** (v20–22) — the hand far away, the dread lifted: then call, and I will answer.
- **Moth-eaten garment** (v28) — leads into chapter 14.
