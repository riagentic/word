# Job 21

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Surely hear my utterance,
and let this be your consolations.

[3] Bear with me, and I, I will speak;
and after my speaking, you may mock.

[4] I — is my complaint to man?
And if so, why should my spirit not be short?

[5] Turn to me and be appalled,
and put hand on mouth.

[6] And if I remember, I am dismayed,
and shuddering takes hold of my flesh.

[7] Why do the wicked live,
grow old, also grow strong in wealth?

[8] Their seed is established before them, with them,
and their offspring before their eyes.

[9] Their houses are ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ away from dread,
and the rod of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ is not upon them.

[10] His bull breeds and does not fail;
his cow brings forth and does not miscarry.

[11] They send out their little ones like a flock,
and their children dance.

[12] They lift up their voice like timbrel and lyre,
and rejoice at the sound of the pipe.

[13] They complete their days in good,
and in a moment they go down to Sheol.

[14] And they say to ⟦el|El|God⟧, 'Depart from us;
and the knowledge of your ways we do not delight in.

[15] What is ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧, that we should serve him?
And what do we profit, that we should entreat him?'

[16] Behold, their good is not in their hand;
the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

[17] How often is the lamp of the wicked put out,
and their calamity comes upon them,
pangs he divides in his anger,

[18] they become like straw before the wind,
and like chaff that the tempest has stolen?

[19] ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ stores up for his sons his wickedness;
let him repay to him, and let him know.

[20] Let his eyes see his ruin,
and from the fury of ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ let him drink.

[21] For what is his delight in his house after him,
and the number of his months cut off?

[22] Shall one teach ⟦el|El|God⟧ knowledge,
and he judges those on high?

[23] This one dies in his very wholeness,
all of him at ease and untroubled.

[24] His pails are full of milk,
and the marrow of his bones is watered.

[25] And this one dies in a bitter soul,
and has not eaten of the good.

[26] Together on the dust they lie down,
and the maggots cover over them.

[27] Behold, I know your thoughts,
and the schemes with which you do violence against me.

[28] For you say, 'Where is the house of the noble one?
And where is the tent of the dwellings of the wicked?'

[29] Have you not asked those who pass along the way,
and their signs you do not treat as strange,

[30] that to the day of calamity the evil one is withheld,
to the day of wraths they are led?

[31] Who will tell his way to his face?
And he has done it — who will repay him?

[32] And he is brought to the graves,
and over the stack he keeps watch.

[33] Sweet to him are the clods of the wadi,
and after him all mankind draws on,
and before him there is no number.

[34] And how do you comfort me with vapor?
And of your answers unfaithfulness remains."

## Changes

- **"But Job answered and said" → "And Job answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of every speech heading.
- **"Hear diligently my speech" → "Surely hear my utterance"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ שָׁמוֹעַ, the imperative with its infinitive, literally "hear hearing"; מִלָּתִי "my utterance" (the poetic word מִלָּה, as 19:2). תַּנְחוּמֹתֵיכֶם "your consolations" (plural).
- **"Suffer me that I may speak; and after that I have spoken, mock on" → "Bear with me, and I, I will speak; and after my speaking, you may mock"** (v3) — Hebrew שָׂאוּנִי, literally "bear me"; וְאָנֹכִי "and I", for emphasis; תַלְעִיג is singular ("you may mock", to one of the three), while "bear" is plural.
- **"As for me, is my complaint to man? and if it were so, why should not my spirit be troubled?" → "I — is my complaint to man? And if so, why should my spirit not be short?"** (v4) — Hebrew הֶאָנֹכִי "is it I …?"; שִׂיחַ "complaint" (lit. "musing"); וְאִם "and if" ("so" is supplied); קָצַר רוּחַ "the spirit is short" (= impatient: as "his soul was short", Judg 16:16; Num 21:4).
- **"Mark me … and lay your hand upon your mouth" → "Turn to me … and put hand on mouth"** (v5) — Hebrew פְּנוּ־אֵלַי "turn to me"; יָד עַל־פֶּה, no "your".
- **"Even when I remember I am afraid" → "And if I remember, I am dismayed"** (v6) — Hebrew וְאִם "and if"; נִבְהַל "be dismayed"; פַּלָּצוּת "shuddering".
- **"yea, are mighty in power" → "also grow strong in wealth"** (v7) — Hebrew גַּם־גָּבְרוּ חָיִל: גָּבַר "be strong, prevail"; חַיִל "ability", of goods "wealth" (as 20:15, 18). עָתְקוּ "grow old" (lit. "move on").
- **"established in their sight with them" → "established before them, with them"** (v8) — Hebrew נָכוֹן לִפְנֵיהֶם עִמָּם; זֶרַע "seed"; צֶאֱצָאִים "offspring" (lit. "what comes out").
- **"Their houses are safe from fear" → "Their houses are shalom away from dread"** (v9) — Hebrew בָּתֵּיהֶם שָׁלוֹם מִפָּחַד: the noun שָׁלוֹם with no verb, "their houses (are) shalom, from dread"; פַּחַד "dread".
- **"Their bull gendereth, and faileth not; their cow calveth, and casteth not her calf" → "His bull breeds and does not fail; his cow brings forth and does not miscarry"** (v10) — Hebrew שׁוֹרוֹ … פָּרָתוֹ "his … his" (singular). שׁוֹר is "ox" elsewhere (one head of cattle); here a breeding male, so "bull" (an ox does not breed). עִבַּר "breed"; לֹא יַגְעִל, hifil of גָּעַל "abhor", only here: "fail" (uncertain). תְּפַלֵּט, literally "lets escape" = brings forth; לֹא תְשַׁכֵּל, of the שִׁכֵּל "bereave" root: "does not miscarry" (as Exod 23:26).
- **"They send forth their little ones like a flock" → "They send out their little ones like a flock"** (v11) — Hebrew עֲוִילִים "little ones" (as 19:18); צֹאן "flock"; יַלְדֵיהֶם "their children"; רִקֵּד "dance, skip".
- **"They take the timbrel and harp, and rejoice at the sound of the organ" → "They lift up their voice like timbrel and lyre, and rejoice at the sound of the pipe"** (v12) — Hebrew יִשְׂאוּ כְּתֹף וְכִנּוֹר: נָשָׂא "lift up" with no object ("their voice" is supplied, as נָשָׂא קוֹל Isa 42:11); כְּ "like" (many Hebrew manuscripts read בְּ "with": from memory). תֹּף "timbrel" (a hand drum), כִּנּוֹר "lyre", עוּגָב "pipe" (the kind uncertain); קוֹל "sound".
- **"They spend their days in wealth, and in a moment go down to the grave" → "They complete their days in good, and in a moment they go down to Sheol"** (v13) — qere יְכַלּוּ "they complete"; the ketiv יבלו "they wear out" says much the same. בַּטּוֹב "in good"; רֶגַע "a moment" (the earlier "in peace" was not in the Hebrew); שְׁאוֹל Sheol; יֵחָתּוּ from נָחַת "go down".
- **"Therefore they say unto God … for we desire not" → "And they say to El … and the knowledge of your ways we do not delight in"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמְרוּ "and they say" (the narrative form, here of what they do); אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*; סוּר מִמֶּנּוּ "depart from us"; וְ "and", not "for"; חָפֵץ "delight in".
- **"what profit should we have, if we pray unto him?" → "what do we profit, that we should entreat him?"** (v15) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי *Shaddai*; הוֹעִיל "profit"; פָּגַע בְּ "entreat" (lit. "meet, fall upon"; not "pray", הִתְפַּלֵּל).
- **"How oft is the candle of the wicked put out! … God distributeth sorrows in his anger" → "How often is the lamp of the wicked put out … pangs he divides in his anger"** (v17) — Hebrew כַּמָּה "how often", a question that runs on through v18; נֵר "lamp"; דָּעַךְ "be put out"; אֵיד "calamity". The Masoretic Text names no subject for יְחַלֵּק "he divides": "God" is not in the Hebrew (KJV and ESV supply it). חֲבָלִים "pangs" (as of birth); the same letters are "cords", measuring lines, so "portions" (uncertain).
- **"They are as stubble … that the storm carrieth away" → "they become like straw … that the tempest has stolen"** (v18) — Hebrew יִהְיוּ "they become" (still under "how often"); תֶּבֶן "straw"; מֹץ "chaff"; גְּנָבַתּוּ "has stolen" (גָּנַב "steal"); סוּפָה "tempest" (סְעָרָה is "storm").
- **"God layeth up his iniquity for his children: he rewardeth him, and he shall know it" → "Eloah stores up for his sons his wickedness; let him repay to him, and let him know"** (v19) — Hebrew אוֹנוֹ "his wickedness" (אָוֶן; the same written form is "his strength" in 20:10); בָּנָיו "his sons"; יְשַׁלֵּם "let him repay" (jussive sense, as v20); וְיֵדָע "and let him know". Many take the first line as the friends' saying, which Job answers in the second (ESV adds "You say"); the Hebrew has no mark of quotation.
- **"His eyes shall see his destruction, and he shall drink" → "Let his eyes see his ruin, and … let him drink"** (v20) — qere עֵינָיו "his eyes" (ketiv עינו "his eye"); both verbs are wishes; כִּיד "ruin" (only here); חֵמָה "fury".
- **"when the number of his months is cut off in the midst" → "and the number of his months cut off"** (v21) — Hebrew חֵפֶץ "delight"; וּמִסְפַּר חֳדָשָׁיו חֻצָּצוּ, "and the number of his months — they are cut off"; "when" and "in the midst" are not written.
- **"Shall any teach God knowledge? seeing he judgeth those that are high" → "Shall one teach El knowledge, and he judges those on high?"** (v22) — Hebrew הַלְאֵל: אֵל *El*; וְהוּא "and he"; רָמִים "high ones".
- **"One dieth in his full strength, being wholly at ease and quiet" → "This one dies in his very wholeness, all of him at ease and untroubled"** (v23) — Hebrew זֶה "this one" (and v25); בְּעֶצֶם תֻּמּוֹ, literally "in the bone of his wholeness" (עֶצֶם = "the very"; תֹּם "integrity", here wholeness of body); כֻּלּוֹ "all of him"; שַׁלְאֲנַן (= שַׁאֲנָן) "at ease", שָׁלֵיו "untroubled".
- **"His breasts are full of milk, and his bones are moistened with marrow" → "His pails are full of milk, and the marrow of his bones is watered"** (v24) — Hebrew עֲטִינָיו, only here: uncertain ("pails", "breasts", "flanks"); חָלָב "milk" as pointed (read as חֵלֶב "fat" by many: the earlier verse had "fat"); יְשֻׁקֶּה "is watered" (lit. "is given drink").
- **"And another dieth in the bitterness of his soul, and never eateth with pleasure" → "And this one dies in a bitter soul, and has not eaten of the good"** (v25) — Hebrew בְּנֶפֶשׁ מָרָה "in a bitter soul"; אָכַל בְּ "eat of"; טוֹבָה "good".
- **"They shall lie down alike in the dust, and the worms shall cover them" → "Together on the dust they lie down, and the maggots cover over them"** (v26) — Hebrew יַחַד "together"; עַל־עָפָר "on the dust"; רִמָּה "maggots" (a collective; תּוֹלֵעָה is "worm").
- **"the devices which ye wrongfully imagine against me" → "the schemes with which you do violence against me"** (v27) — Hebrew מְזִמּוֹת "schemes"; תַּחְמֹסוּ, verb of חָמָס "violence".
- **"the house of the prince? and where are the dwelling places of the wicked" → "the house of the noble one? And where is the tent of the dwellings of the wicked"** (v28) — Hebrew נָדִיב "noble one" (lit. "willing one"); אֹהֶל מִשְׁכְּנוֹת, "the tent of the dwellings".
- **"them that go by the way? and do ye not know their tokens" → "those who pass along the way, and their signs you do not treat as strange"** (v29) — Hebrew עֹבְרֵי דֶרֶךְ "those who pass along the way"; אוֹת "sign"; לֹא תְנַכֵּרוּ, piel of נכר "treat as strange, misconstrue" (the hifil הִכִּיר is "recognize").
- **"That the wicked is reserved to the day of destruction? they shall be brought forth to the day of wrath" → "that to the day of calamity the evil one is withheld, to the day of wraths they are led"** (v30) — Hebrew כִּי "that"; לְיוֹם "to / for the day"; יֵחָשֶׂךְ, nifal of חָשַׂךְ "withhold": "kept back for" or "spared in" the day of calamity (the preposition allows both); רָע "evil one"; עֲבָרוֹת "wraths" (plural); יוּבָלוּ "they are led".
- **"who shall repay him what he hath done?" → "And he has done it — who will repay him?"** (v31) — Hebrew וְהוּא־עָשָׂה, "and he — he has done" ("it" is supplied); הִגִּיד "tell"; שִׁלֵּם "repay".
- **"Yet shall he be brought to the grave, and shall remain in the tomb" → "And he is brought to the graves, and over the stack he keeps watch"** (v32) — Hebrew לִקְבָרוֹת "to the graves" (plural); גָּדִישׁ "stack" (of grain, Judg 15:5; Job 5:26), here the heap over the grave; יִשְׁקוֹד "he keeps watch" (who watches — the dead man or another — is not said).
- **"and every man shall draw after him, as there are innumerable before him" → "and after him all mankind draws on, and before him there is no number"** (v33) — Hebrew רִגְבֵי נָחַל "the clods of the wadi"; כָּל־אָדָם "all mankind"; מָשַׁךְ "draw" (here without object: they follow in a line); אֵין מִסְפָּר "there is no number"; "as" is not written.
- **"How then comfort ye me in vain, seeing in your answers there remaineth falsehood?" → "And how do you comfort me with vapor? And of your answers unfaithfulness remains"** (v34) — Hebrew הֶבֶל "vapor" (lit. "breath"; not "vanity"); "with" is supplied; מַעַל "unfaithfulness" (a breach of trust), not "falsehood"; נִשְׁאַר "remains".

### Notes
- **Job's counter-evidence** — the wicked live, prosper and die in peace.
- **Against the friends' teaching of retribution** — what is seen contradicts it.
- **The words of the wicked to El** (v14–15) — Job quotes their refusal of El and Shaddai.
- **v19–21** — Job answers the saying that Eloah stores up a man's wickedness for his sons: let the man himself be repaid.
- **Death levels all** (v23–26) — the man who dies at ease and the man who dies bitter lie down together on the dust.
- **Travelers' testimony** (v29–33) — those who pass along the way know the evil man's end: a funeral, a watched grave, a long procession.
- **"Unfaithfulness remains"** (v34) — Job's judgment on the friends' answers.
