# Job 24

[1] "Why are times of judgment not kept by Shaddai?
Why do those who know him not see his days?

[2] Men remove landmarks;
they seize flocks and pasture them.

[3] They drive away the donkey of the fatherless;
they take the widow's ox for a pledge.

[4] They thrust the needy off the road;
the poor of the earth all hide themselves.

[5] Behold, like wild donkeys in the desert
the poor go out to their labor,
foraging for food;
the wilderness yields food for their children.

[6] They gather fodder in the field;
they glean the vineyard of the wicked.

[7] They lie all night naked, without clothing,
with no covering in the cold.

[8] They are drenched by the rain of the mountains,
and cling to the rock for want of shelter.

[9] The orphan is snatched from the breast;
the infant of the poor is taken as a pledge.

[10] The poor go about naked, without clothing;
they carry the sheaves, yet hungry.

[11] Among the rows they press out oil;
they tread the winepresses, yet thirst.

[12] From the city the dying groan,
and the soul of the wounded cries out —
yet Eloah charges no one with wrong.

[13] They are among those who rebel against the light;
they do not know its ways
and do not stay in its paths.

[14] At daylight the murderer rises
to kill the poor and needy;
at night he becomes a thief.

[15] The eye of the adulterer waits for twilight;
saying, 'No eye shall see me,'
he sets a cover over his face.

[16] In the dark they break into houses;
by day they shut themselves in;
they do not know the light.

[17] For deep darkness is morning to them;
they are friends with the terrors of deep darkness.

[18] They are swift on the face of the waters;
their portion is cursed in the land;
none turns toward their vineyards.

[19] Drought and heat snatch the snow waters;
so Sheol those who have sinned.

[20] The womb forgets him;
the worm finds him sweet;
he is no more remembered;
wickedness is broken like a tree.

[21] He preys on the barren, childless woman;
he does no good to the widow.

[22] Yet he drags off the mighty by his power;
he rises, and no one is sure of life.

[23] Eloah gives him security on which he relies,
and his eyes are upon their ways.

[24] They are exalted a little while, then gone;
they are brought low, gathered up like all others,
cut off like the heads of grain.

[25] If it is not so, who will prove me a liar
and reduce my words to nothing?"

## Changes

- **"shadow of death" → "deep darkness"** (v17) — *tzalmaweth*.
- **"grave" → "Sheol"** (v19).
- **"corn / fodder" → "fodder / sheaves"** (v6, 10) — *belilo... omer*.
- **"the tops of the ears of corn" → "the heads of grain"** (v24).
- **"God layeth not folly to them" → "Eloah charges no one with wrong"** (v12).

### Notes
- **Job describes systemic evil** — the wicked flourish, poor suffer. Yahweh does not intervene.
- **Catalog of atrocities** (v2-12) — landmark-moving, flock-theft, widow-exploitation, orphan-stripping. Crimes Amos and Isaiah also condemn.
- **Poor's dignified labor yields no sustenance** — structural injustice.
- **"Eloah charges no one with wrong"** (v12) — stinging observation. Job denies retribution works.
- **Night-workers** (v13-17) — murderer, adulterer, burglar all rebel against light.
- **Wicked's death** (v18-24) — finally catches up, but perhaps only in natural death like everyone else.
- **Challenge** (v25) — "prove me wrong."
