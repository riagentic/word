# Job 24

[1] "Why are times not stored up by ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧,
and those who know him do not behold his days?

[2] Borders they move back;
a drove they rob, and they shepherd it.

[3] The donkey of the fatherless they drive away;
they take the ox of the widow in pledge.

[4] They turn the needy aside from the way;
together the afflicted of the land are made to hide.

[5] Behold, wild donkeys in the wilderness,
they go out in their work,
seeking diligently for prey;
the steppe — bread to him for the boys.

[6] In the field they reap his fodder,
and the vineyard of the wicked they glean late.

[7] Naked they spend the night without clothing,
and there is no covering in the cold.

[8] From the downpour of the mountains they are wet,
and for lack of refuge they embrace the rock.

[9] They rob the fatherless from the breast,
and on the afflicted they take a pledge.

[10] Naked they go about without clothing,
and hungry they carry the sheaf.

[11] Between their walls they press out oil;
wine vats they tread, and they thirst.

[12] From the city men groan,
and the soul of the slain cries for help,
and ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ does not charge unseemliness.

[13] They have been among the rebels against light;
they have not recognized its ways,
and they have not dwelt in its paths.

[14] At the light the murderer rises;
he kills the afflicted and the needy,
and in the night he is as the thief.

[15] And the eye of the adulterer waits for the twilight,
saying, 'No eye will watch me,'
and he puts a covering on his face.

[16] He digs through houses in the darkness;
by day they seal themselves in;
they do not know light.

[17] For together, morning to them is the shadow of death,
for he recognizes the terrors of the shadow of death.

[18] Swift is he on the face of the waters;
cursed is their portion in the land;
he does not turn by the way of the vineyards.

[19] Drought, also heat, rob the waters of snow —
Sheol, those who have sinned.

[20] The womb forgets him;
the maggot finds him sweet;
he is no more remembered,
and injustice is broken like a tree.

[21] He feeds on the barren one who does not bear,
and to the widow he does no good.

[22] And he drags off the strong by his power;
he rises, and does not believe in life.

[23] He gives to him in security, and he leans,
and his eyes are on their ways.

[24] They are lifted up a little, and he is not;
and they are brought low; like all, they are drawn in,
and like the head of an ear of grain they are cut off.

[25] And if it is not so, then who will prove me a liar
and set my utterance at nothing?"

## Changes

- **Speaker** — the chapter has no heading of its own: it goes on with Job's answer begun in 23:1. Who speaks in 24:18–25 (and in 25–27) is debated; nothing is added to the Hebrew as printed.
- **"Why, seeing times are not hidden from the Almighty, do they that know him not see his days?" → "Why are times not stored up by Shaddai, and those who know him do not behold his days?"** (v1) — Hebrew מִשַּׁדַּי "from / by Shaddai"; נִצְפְּנוּ, nifal of צָפַן "store up" (as 23:12); "of judgment" is not written. וְיֹדְעָיו is the qere, "and those who know him"; the ketiv וידעו may be read "and they know him". חָזָה "behold". **"Shaddai"** — שַׁדַּי, traditionally "the Almighty"; **"Eloah"** (v12) — אֱלוֹהַּ.
- **"Some remove the landmarks; they violently take away flocks, and feed thereof" → "Borders they move back; a drove they rob, and they shepherd it"** (v2) — Hebrew גְּבֻלוֹת יַשִּׂיגוּ "they move back borders" (no "landmark" stone is named; no subject); עֵדֶר "a drove"; גָּזַל "rob" (also v9, 19; the Lev 6:2 row); וַיִּרְעוּ "and they shepherd" ("it" supplied).
- **"They turn the needy out of the way: the poor of the earth hide themselves together" → "They turn the needy aside from the way; together the afflicted of the land are made to hide"** (v4) — Hebrew עֲנִיֵּי־אָרֶץ "the afflicted of the land"; חֻבְּאוּ pual, "they are made to hide" (only here).
- **"as wild asses in the desert, go they forth to their work; rising betimes for a prey: the wilderness yieldeth food for them and for their children" → "wild donkeys in the wilderness, they go out in their work, seeking diligently for prey; the steppe — bread to him for the boys"** (v5) — Hebrew פְּרָאִים "wild donkeys", with no "as": the men are called wild donkeys; "the poor" is not written. מְשַׁחֲרֵי "seeking diligently" (or "early"); טֶרֶף "prey"; עֲרָבָה "steppe"; לוֹ לֶחֶם לַנְּעָרִים, literally "to him bread for the boys"; "yields" is not written.
- **"They reap every one his corn in the field: and they gather the vintage of the wicked" → "In the field they reap his fodder, and the vineyard of the wicked they glean late"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּלִילוֹ "his fodder" (mixed fodder; also read בְּלִי לוֹ "not his own"); יִקְצוֹרוּ is the qere, "they reap" (ketiv יקצירו, hifil, "they harvest"); לִקֵּשׁ "glean late", from לֶקֶשׁ "late crop" (only here).
- **"They cause the naked to lodge without clothing, that they have no covering in the cold" → "Naked they spend the night without clothing, and there is no covering in the cold"** (v7) — Hebrew עָרוֹם יָלִינוּ, qal "they spend the night", not "cause to lodge".
- **"showers" → "downpour"** (v8) — Hebrew זֶרֶם. **"shelter" → "refuge"** — מַחְסֶה; חִבְּקוּ "embrace".
- **"They pluck the fatherless from the breast, and take a pledge of the poor" → "They rob the fatherless from the breast, and on the afflicted they take a pledge"** (v9) — Hebrew שֹׁד "breast"; וְעַל־עָנִי יַחְבֹּלוּ, "and on the afflicted they take in pledge" (some read עוּל "infant": "the infant of the afflicted", a conjecture).
- **"They cause him to go naked without clothing, and they take away the sheaf from the hungry" → "Naked they go about without clothing, and hungry they carry the sheaf"** (v10) — Hebrew הִלְּכוּ "they go about"; נָשְׂאוּ "they carry"; עֹמֶר "sheaf" (the same word as the measure *omer*).
- **"Which make oil within their walls, and tread their winepresses, and suffer thirst" → "Between their walls they press out oil; wine vats they tread, and they thirst"** (v11) — Hebrew שׁוּרֹתָם "their walls" (or "rows", of olive trees; only here: uncertain); יְקָבִים "wine vats" (not "their").
- **"Men groan from out of the city, and the soul of the wounded crieth out: yet God layeth not folly to them" → "From the city men groan, and the soul of the slain cries for help, and Eloah does not charge unseemliness"** (v12) — Hebrew מְתִים "men" as pointed (not מֵתִים "the dead, the dying"); חֲלָלִים "slain" (pierced); שִׁוַּע "cry for help"; לֹא־יָשִׂים תִּפְלָה, "does not set unseemliness" (תִּפְלָה as 1:22): he lays no wrong to anyone's charge, or pays no heed to it; Syriac is said to read תְּפִלָּה "prayer" (from memory).
- **"They are of those that rebel against the light; they know not the ways thereof, nor abide in the paths thereof" → "They have been among the rebels against light; they have not recognized its ways, and they have not dwelt in its paths"** (v13) — Hebrew הֵמָּה הָיוּ בְּמֹרְדֵי־אוֹר, a perfect, "they have been among"; הִכִּירוּ "recognize"; יָשְׁבוּ "dwell"; מֹרְדֵי־אוֹר "rebels against light".
- **"The murderer rising with the light killeth the poor and needy, and in the night is as a thief" → "At the light the murderer rises; he kills the afflicted and the needy, and in the night he is as the thief"** (v14) — Hebrew לָאוֹר "at the light"; יִקְטָל "kills"; עָנִי "afflicted"; יְהִי has the form of a wish, "let him be", used here as a statement.
- **"waiteth for the twilight … No eye shall see me: and disguiseth his face" → "waits for the twilight … No eye will watch me, and he puts a covering on his face"** (v15) — Hebrew שָׁמְרָה, literally "keeps" (the twilight); שׁוּר "watch"; וְסֵתֶר פָּנִים יָשִׂים "and a covering of face he puts".
- **"In the dark they dig through houses, which they had marked for themselves in the daytime" → "He digs through houses in the darkness; by day they seal themselves in"** (v16) — Hebrew חָתַר singular "he digs through", then plural; חִתְּמוּ־לָמוֹ "they sealed for themselves" (shut themselves in).
- **"For the morning is to them even as the shadow of death: if one know them, they are in the terrors of the shadow of death" → "For together, morning to them is the shadow of death, for he recognizes the terrors of the shadow of death"** (v17) — Hebrew צַלְמָוֶת, as pointed צֵל "shadow" + מָוֶת "death" (the Job row); the reading צַלְמוּת "deep darkness" (from צלם "be dark") is widely held; כִּי־יַכִּיר "for he recognizes"; בַּלְהוֹת "terrors". יַחְדָּו "together".
- **"He is swift as the waters; their portion is cursed in the earth: he beholdeth not the way of the vineyards" → "Swift is he on the face of the waters; cursed is their portion in the land; he does not turn by the way of the vineyards"** (v18) — Hebrew קַל "swift" (literally "light"), and תְּקֻלַּל "is cursed", of the same root (קִלֵּל "make light of, curse"); עַל־פְּנֵי־מַיִם "on the face of the waters"; לֹא־יִפְנֶה "he does not turn".
- **"Drought and heat consume the snow waters: so doth the grave those which have sinned" → "Drought, also heat, rob the waters of snow — Sheol, those who have sinned"** (v19) — Hebrew גַם "also"; יִגְזְלוּ "rob" (as v2, 9); the second line has no verb and no "so": שְׁאוֹל חָטָאוּ "Sheol — they have sinned".
- **"the worm shall feed sweetly on him … wickedness shall be broken as a tree" → "the maggot finds him sweet … injustice is broken like a tree"** (v20) — Hebrew מְתָקוֹ רִמָּה, "the maggot finds him sweet"; רִמָּה "maggot" (תּוֹלֵעָה is "worm", 25:6); עַוְלָה "injustice" (as 22:23).
- **"He evil entreateth the barren that beareth not" → "He feeds on the barren one who does not bear"** (v21) — Hebrew רֹעֶה, of רָעָה "graze, shepherd": he grazes on her as a beast does; others derive it from רָעַע "do evil". יְיֵטִיב "do good".
- **"He draweth also the mighty with his power: he riseth up, and no man is sure of life" → "And he drags off the strong by his power; he rises, and does not believe in life"** (v22) — Hebrew מָשַׁךְ "drag"; אַבִּירִים "strong ones"; וְלֹא־יַאֲמִין בַּחַיִּין "and he does not believe in life" (= has no hope to live); "no man" is not written. Who "he" is (Eloah or the wicked man) is not said.
- **"Though it be given him to be in safety, whereon he resteth; yet his eyes are upon their ways" → "He gives to him in security, and he leans, and his eyes are on their ways"** (v23) — Hebrew יִתֶּן־לוֹ לָבֶטַח וְיִשָּׁעֵן, the MT kept (as fixed before: tools/terms/lex-todo.md): "he gives to him in security, and he leans" (נִשְׁעַן "lean, be supported"); no subject is named ("God" in some versions is supplied).
- **"They are exalted for a little while, but are gone and brought low; they are taken out of the way as all other, and cut off as the tops of the ears of corn" → "They are lifted up a little, and he is not; and they are brought low; like all, they are drawn in, and like the head of an ear of grain they are cut off"** (v24) — Hebrew רוֹמּוּ "they are lifted up"; וְאֵינֶנּוּ "and he is not" (singular); הֻמְּכוּ "brought low"; כַּכֹּל יִקָּפְצוּן "like all they are drawn in" (קָפַץ "shut, draw together"); רֹאשׁ שִׁבֹּלֶת "the head of an ear of grain"; יִמָּלוּ "they are cut off" (or "wither").
- **"And if it be not so now, who will make me a liar, and make my speech nothing worth?" → "And if it is not so, then who will prove me a liar and set my utterance at nothing?"** (v25) — Hebrew אֵפוֹ "then"; יָשֵׂם לְאַל "set to nothing"; מִלָּתִי "my utterance".

### Notes
- **The oppressed** (v2–12) — borders moved, droves robbed, the fatherless and the widow stripped, workers in field and press hungry and thirsty; "and Eloah does not charge unseemliness" (v12).
- **Rebels against light** (v13–17) — the murderer, the adulterer, the burglar: night is their morning.
- **v18–24** — the end of the wicked; because these lines agree with the friends' teaching, many hold that the speech of another speaker has come in here. The Hebrew names no speaker.
- **v25** — Job's challenge: "who will prove me a liar?"
