# Job 23

[1] Then ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Even today my complaint is bitter;
his hand is heavy despite my groaning.

[3] Oh that I knew where I might find him,
that I might come even to his seat!

[4] I would lay my case before him
and fill my mouth with arguments.

[5] I would know the words he would answer me
and understand what he would say to me.

[6] Would he contend with me in his great power?
No; surely he would give me a hearing.

[7] There the upright could reason with him,
and I should be acquitted forever by my judge.

[8] Behold, I go forward, but he is not there;
and backward, but I cannot perceive him;

[9] on the left hand, where he works, but I do not behold him;
he turns to the right hand, but I do not see him.

[10] But he knows the way that I take;
when he has tested me, I shall come forth as gold.

[11] My foot has held fast to his steps;
I have kept his way and not turned aside.

[12] I have not departed from the commandment of his lips;
I have treasured the words of his mouth more than my portion.

[13] But he stands alone — who can turn him back?
What his soul desires, that he does.

[14] For he accomplishes what he has decreed for me,
and many such things are with him.

[15] Therefore I am terrified before his face;
when I consider, I am afraid of him.

[16] For ⟦el|El|God⟧ has made my heart faint;
⟦shaddai|Shaddai|The Almighty⟧ has terrified me —

[17] yet I am not silenced by the darkness,
nor by the thick gloom that covers my face."

## Changes

- **"God" → "El"** (v16) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"my necessary food" → "my portion"** (v12) — Hebrew *mei-chuqqi*; "from my allotted portion."
- **"in one mind" → "stands alone"** (v13) — *hu be-echad*; singular, unparalleled.
- **"I was not cut off before the darkness" → "I am not silenced by the darkness"** (v17) — Hebrew ambiguous; modern reading: Job persists in speech despite terror.

### Notes
- **Longing for audience** — Job wants the legal hearing, confident of acquittal.
- **"If I could find him"** (v3) — theological frustration: God's absence from where he would be found.
- **Testing like gold** (v10) — classic purification image.
- **Claim of integrity** (v11-12) — Job maintains moral rectitude.
- **Divine inscrutability** (v13-14) — Eloah's will unchanged, unchallenged.
- **Terror but not silenced** — Job remains speaking through fear.
