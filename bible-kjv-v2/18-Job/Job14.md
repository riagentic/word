# Job 14

[1] "Man, born of woman,
is short of days and sated with agitation.

[2] Like a blossom he comes out and is cut off,
and he flees like the shadow and does not stand.

[3] Even upon this one you have opened your eye,
and me you bring into judgment with you.

[4] Who will give a clean thing out of an unclean?
Not one.

[5] If his days are determined,
the number of his months is with you;
his limits you have made, and he shall not pass over.

[6] Look away from upon him, and let him cease,
until he pays off, like a hired man, his day.

[7] For there is hope for a tree:
if it is cut down, it will yet sprout again,
and its shoot will not cease.

[8] If its root grows old in the earth,
and in the dust its stump dies,

[9] from the smell of water it will bud,
and make a bough like a planting.

[10] But a mighty man dies and becomes weak;
and man expires, and where is he?

[11] Waters go away from the sea,
and a river dries up and is dried out;

[12] and a man lies down and does not rise;
until the heavens are no more they will not awake,
and they will not be roused out of their sleep.

[13] Would that you would hide me in Sheol,
conceal me until your anger turns back,
set me a limit, and remember me!

[14] If a man dies, will he live?
All the days of my service I will hope,
until my change comes.

[15] You will call, and I will answer you;
for the work of your hands you will long.

[16] For now you number my steps;
you do not keep watch over my sin.

[17] Sealed up in a bag is my transgression,
and you smear over my iniquity.

[18] And yet a falling mountain wears away,
and a rock is moved from its place;

[19] stones the waters grind away;
its outpourings wash away the dust of the earth;
and the hope of mortal man you have made perish.

[20] You overpower him forever, and he goes;
changing his face, you send him away.

[21] His sons come to honor, and he does not know;
and they become small, and he does not discern them.

[22] Only his flesh upon him is in pain,
and his soul upon him mourns."

## Changes

- **"Man that is born of a woman is of few days, and full of trouble" → "Man, born of woman, is short of days and sated with agitation"** (v1) — Hebrew אָדָם יְלוּד אִשָּׁה קְצַר יָמִים וּשְׂבַע־רֹגֶז: קְצַר יָמִים "short of days"; שָׂבֵעַ "sated"; רֹגֶז, noun of רָגַז "be agitated" (turmoil, unrest).
- **"He cometh forth like a flower, and is cut down" → "Like a blossom he comes out and is cut off"** (v2) — Hebrew צִיץ "blossom"; וַיִּמָּל, from מָלַל, "is cut off" or "withers" (uncertain). **"continueth not" → "does not stand"** — Hebrew וְלֹא יַעֲמוֹד. צֵל "shadow" (of protection elsewhere "shade").
- **"And dost thou open thine eyes upon such an one" → "Even upon this one you have opened your eye"** (v3) — Hebrew אַף־עַל־זֶה פָּקַחְתָּ עֵינֶךָ, "eye" singular, no question marker. **"and bringest me into judgment with thee" → "and me you bring …"** — the Hebrew puts וְאֹתִי "and me" first.
- **"Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? not one" → "Who will give a clean thing out of an unclean? Not one"** (v4) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן טָהוֹר מִטָּמֵא לֹא אֶחָד, word for word. מִי יִתֵּן is often the wish "would that" (v13; 13:5); here the answer "not one" shows the question (reviewer); the wish reading ("Would that a clean thing came out of an unclean!") in Changes only.
- **"Seeing his days are determined" → "If his days are determined"** (v5) — Hebrew אִם. **"thou hast appointed his bounds that he cannot pass" → "his limits you have made, and he shall not pass over"** — the verse follows the qere חֻקָּיו "his limits" (plural); the written text (ketiv) is חקו "his limit"; the meaning is the same. חֹק is "statute"; here, as in v13 and 38:10, a fixed limit.
- **"Turn from him, that he may rest" → "Look away from upon him, and let him cease"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׁעֵה מֵעָלָיו וְיֶחְדָּל; חָדַל "cease". **"till he shall accomplish, as an hireling, his day" → "until he pays off, like a hired man, his day"** — Hebrew עַד־יִרְצֶה; רָצָה here as Lev 26:34 "pay off" (or "enjoy, be pleased with"; uncertain).
- **"that it will sprout again" → "it will yet sprout again"** (v7) — Hebrew וְעוֹד יַחֲלִיף, hifil of חָלַף "change, pass on, renew"; its noun is "my change" in v14. **"the tender branch thereof" → "its shoot"** — Hebrew יֹנַקְתּוֹ. **"will not cease"** — Hebrew חָדַל, as v6.
- **"the stock thereof die in the ground" → "in the dust its stump dies"** (v8) — Hebrew וּבֶעָפָר … גִּזְעוֹ.
- **"through the scent of water" → "from the smell of water"** (v9) — Hebrew מֵרֵיחַ מַיִם. **"bring forth boughs like a plant" → "make a bough like a planting"** — Hebrew וְעָשָׂה קָצִיר כְּמוֹ־נָטַע: קָצִיר here is "bough" (Ps 80:11), not the קָצִיר "harvest".
- **"But man dieth, and wasteth away" → "But a mighty man dies and becomes weak"** (v10) — Hebrew וְגֶבֶר יָמוּת וַיֶּחֱלָשׁ: גֶּבֶר "mighty man" beside אָדָם "man" in the second line (in v14 גֶּבֶר stands alone: "a man"); חָלַשׁ "be weak". **"man giveth up the ghost" → "man expires"** — Hebrew וַיִּגְוַע אָדָם.
- **"As the waters fail from the sea" → "Waters go away from the sea"** (v11) — Hebrew אָזְלוּ־מַיִם מִנִּי־יָם; "as" and "so" (v12) are not written: the two pictures stand side by side. **"decayeth and drieth up" → "dries up and is dried out"** — Hebrew יֶחֱרַב וְיָבֵשׁ.
- **"So man lieth down, and riseth not" → "and a man lies down and does not rise"** (v12) — Hebrew וְאִישׁ שָׁכַב וְלֹא־יָקוּם; "again" is not written. **"till the heavens be no more"** — Hebrew עַד־בִּלְתִּי שָׁמַיִם.
- **"O that thou wouldest hide me in the grave" → "Would that you would hide me in Sheol"** (v13) — Hebrew מִי יִתֵּן בִּשְׁאוֹל תַּצְפִּנֵנִי; צָפַן "hide, store away", then סָתַר "conceal" (סָתַר is "hide" in 13:20, 24; here "conceal" keeps the two verbs apart). **"until thy wrath be past" → "until your anger turns back"** — Hebrew עַד־שׁוּב אַפֶּךָ; אַף "anger" (lit. "nose"). **"appoint me a set time" → "set me a limit"** — Hebrew תָּשִׁית לִי חֹק, as v5.
- **"If a man die, shall he live again? all the days of my appointed time will I wait, till my change come" → "If a man dies, will he live? All the days of my service I will hope, until my change comes"** (v14) — word for word: אִם־יָמוּת גֶּבֶר הֲיִחְיֶה כָּל־יְמֵי צְבָאִי אֲיַחֵל עַד־בּוֹא חֲלִיפָתִי. "Again" is not written. צָבָא is "host", here the term of service (as 7:1); "hard" is not written. יִחֵל "hope, wait" (as 13:15); the imperfect may also be "I would hope". חֲלִיפָה "change": the change of a watch or a shift ("relief"), or a renewal (v7 has the verb of a tree); the verse does not say which.
- **"Thou shalt call" → "You will call"** (v15) — the imperfects of v15 may also be read "would". **"thou wilt have a desire to the work of thine hands" → "for the work of your hands you will long"** — Hebrew כָּסַף.
- **"For now thou numberest my steps: dost thou not watch over my sin?" → "For now you number my steps; you do not keep watch over my sin"** (v16) — the Hebrew has no question marker; whether the second line is a question, or says "then you would not watch over my sin" (going on from v15), is uncertain.
- **"thou sewest up mine iniquity" → "you smear over my iniquity"** (v17) — Hebrew וַתִּטְפֹּל עַל, טָפַל "smear, plaster over", as 13:4.
- **"Surely the mountain falling cometh to nought" → "And yet a falling mountain wears away"** (v18) — Hebrew וְאוּלָם הַר־נוֹפֵל יִבּוֹל; נָבֵל "wither, wear away". **"rock"** — Hebrew צוּר.
- **"The waters wear the stones" → "stones the waters grind away"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲבָנִים שָׁחֲקוּ מַיִם. **"thou washest away the things which grow out of the dust of the earth" → "its outpourings wash away the dust of the earth"** — Hebrew תִּשְׁטֹף־סְפִיחֶיהָ עֲפַר־אָרֶץ; סָפִיחַ is elsewhere "what grows of itself" (Lev 25:5); here the subject of "wash away", likely the outpourings of water; uncertain. **"thou destroyest the hope of man" → "the hope of mortal man you have made perish"** — Hebrew אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; הֶאֱבַדְתָּ, hifil of אָבַד "perish"; "so" is not written.
- **"Thou prevailest for ever against him" → "You overpower him forever"** (v20) — Hebrew תִּתְקְפֵהוּ לָנֶצַח. **"countenance" → "face"** — Hebrew פָּנָיו.
- **"His sons come to honour" kept** (v21) — Hebrew יִכְבְּדוּ, literally "are heavy". **"they are brought low" → "they become small"** — Hebrew יִצְעֲרוּ. **"he perceiveth it not of them" → "he does not discern them"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָבִין לָמוֹ.
- **"But his flesh upon him shall have pain, and his soul within him shall mourn" → "Only his flesh upon him is in pain, and his soul upon him mourns"** (v22) — Hebrew עָלָיו "upon him" in both lines ("for himself" is possible).

### Notes
- **Man and the tree** (v7–12) — the tree has hope; man lies down and does not rise "until the heavens are no more".
- **"If a man dies, will he live?"** (v14) — the question stands without answer; the verse gives the Hebrew word for word.
- **"You will call, and I will answer you"** (v15) — the same words as 13:22, now spoken of a time after Sheol.
- **Sheol** (v13) — the place of the dead; Job wishes to be hidden there until the anger turns back.
