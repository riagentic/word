# Job 18

[1] Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

[2] "When will you put an end to words?
Think, and then we will speak.

[3] Why are we counted as beasts,
regarded as stupid in your sight?

[4] You who tear yourself in your anger —
shall the earth be forsaken for you?
Shall a rock be removed from its place?

[5] Indeed, the light of the wicked is put out,
and the flame of his fire shall not shine.

[6] The light is dark in his tent,
and his lamp beside him is put out.

[7] The vigor of his steps is shortened,
and his own plan throws him down.

[8] For he is cast into a net by his own feet,
he walks on a mesh.

[9] A snare seizes him by the heel;
a trap holds him fast.

[10] A noose is hidden in the ground for him,
and a trap for him in the path.

[11] Terrors startle him on every side
and chase him at his heels.

[12] His strength is famished;
calamity is ready at his side.

[13] It consumes the parts of his skin;
the bekhor maweth — firstborn of Death — devours his limbs.

[14] He is torn from the security of his tent
and brought to the melekh balahot — king of terrors.

[15] In his tent dwells what is not his;
brimstone is scattered over his habitation.

[16] Below, his roots dry up;
above, his branch withers.

[17] His memory perishes from the earth;
he has no name in the open country.

[18] He is thrust from light into darkness
and driven out of the world.

[19] He has no posterity or descendant among his people,
no survivor where he sojourned.

[20] Those of the west are appalled at his day,
and those of the east are seized with horror.

[21] Surely such are the dwellings of the unrighteous,
and this is the place of him who does not know *Elohim*."

## Changes

- **"gin" → "snare"** (v9).
- **"nephew" → "descendant"** (v19) — *neked*; grandson/offspring.
- **"they that come after... they that went before" → "those of the west... those of the east"** (v20) — *acharonim... qadmonim*; geographical (sunset-sunrise) readings preferred in modern; also possible "those coming after, those gone before" temporally.

### Notes
- **Bildad's second speech** — doesn't respond to Job's arguments; just repeats wicked-man's-fate catalog.
- **"Firstborn of Death" / "King of Terrors"** — personified death-deities from ANE (Mot in Ugaritic). Hebrew poetry borrowing imagery.
- **Light extinguished** (v5-6) — recurring wicked-man motif.
- **Net, snare, trap, noose** (v8-10) — piled-up trap imagery.
- **Salt/brimstone** (v15) — Sodom-like wasteland.
- **Memory, descendants erased** (v17, 19) — worst ANE fate.
- **Personal attack on Job** implicitly — all these sound like Job's current reality.
