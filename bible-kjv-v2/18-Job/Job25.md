# Job 25

[1] Then Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

[2] "Dominion and dread are with him;
he makes peace in his high places.

[3] Is there any number to his armies?
Upon whom does his light not arise?

[4] How then can man be just before *Elohim*?
How can one born of woman be clean?

[5] Behold, even the moon is not bright,
and the stars are not pure in his sight.

[6] How much less man, a maggot,
and the son of man — a worm!"

## Changes

- **"worm" (first) → "maggot"** (v6) — Hebrew *rimmah*.

### Notes
- **Shortest speech** — only six verses. Bildad sputtering out. Friends running out of material.
- **Third cycle truncated** — Zophar will not speak again. The rhetorical structure shows the friends' exhaustion.
- **Divine transcendence** emphasized — stars and moon impure; how much more humans.
- **Maggot-worm imagery** for humanity — severe anthropology; applied to Job to deflate his claims.
- **Most commentators suspect the third cycle is fragmented** — some of ch 26-27 may originally have been Bildad/Zophar material relocated in transmission.
