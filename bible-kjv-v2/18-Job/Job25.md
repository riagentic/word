# Job 25

[1] And Bildad the ⟦shuhite|Shuchi|Shuhite⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Ruling and dread are with him;
he makes ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ in his heights.

[3] Is there a number to his bands?
And upon whom does his light not rise?

[4] And how can a mortal man be righteous with ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and how can one born of a woman be pure?

[5] Behold, even to the moon — and it does not shine,
and the stars are not pure in his eyes;

[6] how much less a mortal man, a maggot,
and a son of man, a worm!"

## Changes

- **"Then Bildad … answered and said" → "And Bildad … answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר.
- **"Dominion and fear" → "Ruling and dread"** (v2) — Hebrew הַמְשֵׁל, an infinitive used as a noun, "ruling"; פַּחַד "dread". **"peace" → "shalom"** — שָׁלוֹם. **"high places" → "heights"** — מְרוֹמִים.
- **"armies" → "bands"** (v3) — Hebrew גְּדוּדִים, "bands" (troops). **"arise" → "rise"** — יָקוּם.
- **"How then can man be justified with God? or how can he be clean that is born of a woman?" → "And how can a mortal man be righteous with El, and how can one born of a woman be pure?"** (v4) — Hebrew וּמַה … וּמַה "and how … and how"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man" (also v6; beside אָדָם "man"); יִצְדַּק "be righteous" (qal, not "be justified"); עִם־אֵל "with El"; יִזְכֶּה "be pure" (the verb of v5 זַכּוּ). **"God" → "El"** — Hebrew אֵל *El*.
- **"Behold even to the moon, and it shineth not; yea, the stars are not pure in his sight" → "Behold, even to the moon — and it does not shine, and the stars are not pure in his eyes"** (v5) — Hebrew עַד־יָרֵחַ "up to the moon": even the moon; יַאֲהִיל "shine" (only here); בְעֵינָיו "in his eyes".
- **"How much less man, that is a worm? and the son of man, which is a worm?" → "how much less a mortal man, a maggot, and a son of man, a worm!"** (v6) — Hebrew אַף כִּי "how much less"; רִמָּה "maggot" and תּוֹלֵעָה "worm", two words (KJV "worm" for both); בֶּן־אָדָם "son of man".

### Notes
- **Shortest speech** — six verses. Bildad's last words; Zophar does not speak a third time.
- **v4–6** — the thought of Eliphaz in 4:17–19 and 15:14–16 said again.
- **Order of speakers in 24–27** — debated; the Hebrew has the speech headings as printed (25:1; 26:1; 27:1) and nothing is added.
