# Job 15

[1] And ⟦eliphaz|Elifaz|Eliphaz⟧ the ⟦temanite|Temani|Temanite⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Should a wise man answer with knowledge of wind,
and fill his belly with the east wind,

[3] reproving with a word that is of no use,
and with words by which he does no good?

[4] Even you make fear void,
and diminish musing before ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[5] For your iniquity teaches your mouth,
and you choose the tongue of the shrewd.

[6] Your mouth condemns you, and not I,
and your lips answer against you.

[7] Were you born the first man,
and before the hills were you brought forth?

[8] Do you hear in the secret counsel of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧,
and take away wisdom to yourself?

[9] What do you know that we do not know?
What do you understand that is not with us?

[10] Both the gray-haired and the aged are among us,
greater in days than your father.

[11] Are the consolations of ⟦el|El|God⟧ too little for you,
and a word gently with you?

[12] Why does your heart take you away,
and why do your eyes flash,

[13] that you turn your spirit against ⟦el|El|God⟧
and bring words out of your mouth?

[14] What is mortal man, that he should be pure,
and that one born of a woman should be righteous?

[15] Behold, in his holy ones he does not believe,
and the heavens are not pure in his eyes;

[16] how much less one who is abominable and corrupt,
a man who drinks injustice like water!

[17] I will show you; hear me;
and this I have beheld, and I will recount,

[18] what wise men tell,
and have not hidden, from their fathers;

[19] to them alone the land was given,
and no stranger passed in their midst.

[20] All the days of the wicked one, he writhes,
and a number of years are hidden away for the ruthless.

[21] A sound of dreads is in his ears;
in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ the destroyer comes upon him.

[22] He does not believe that he will return from darkness,
and he is watched for, for the sword.

[23] He wanders for bread: 'Where?'
He knows that a day of darkness is ready at his hand.

[24] Distress and straits terrify him;
it overpowers him like a king ready for the onset.

[25] For he stretched out his hand against ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and against ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ he makes himself mighty.

[26] He runs at him with the neck,
with the thickness of the bosses of his shields.

[27] For he covered his face with his fat,
and made fatness upon the flank.

[28] And he abode in cities cut off,
in houses where none dwell,
that are made ready for heaps.

[29] He will not be rich, and his wealth will not stand,
and their possession will not stretch out to the land.

[30] He will not depart from darkness;
a flame will dry up his shoot,
and he will depart by the breath of his mouth.

[31] Let him not believe in emptiness; he is led astray;
for emptiness will be his exchange.

[32] Before his day it will be filled up,
and his palm branch is not luxuriant.

[33] He will wrong, like the vine, his unripe grapes,
and cast off, like the olive, his blossom.

[34] For the congregation of the godless is barren,
and fire has devoured the tents of the bribe.

[35] Conceiving toil and bearing wickedness,
and their belly prepares deceit."

## Changes

- **"Then answered Eliphaz the Temanite, and said" → "And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the book.
- **"God" → "El"** (v4, 11, 13, 25) — Hebrew אֵל *El*; **"God" → "Eloah"** (v8) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ; **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v25) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי (its meaning is not certain). The chapter has no *Elohim*.
- **"utter vain knowledge" → "answer with knowledge of wind"** (v2) — Hebrew יַעֲנֶה דַעַת־רוּחַ, literally "answer knowledge of wind"; "with" is supplied. רוּחַ is "wind, spirit, breath" (here "wind", beside קָדִים "east wind"). בֶּטֶן "belly".
- **"Should he reason with unprofitable talk? or with speeches wherewith he can do no good?" → "reproving with a word that is of no use, and with words by which he does no good?"** (v3) — Hebrew הוֹכֵחַ, an infinitive absolute of הוֹכִיחַ "reprove", running on from v2 (no second "should he"); דָּבָר לֹא יִסְכּוֹן "a word that is of no use"; לֹא־יוֹעִיל "does not profit".
- **"Yea, thou castest off fear, and restrainest prayer before God" → "Even you make fear void, and diminish musing before El"** (v4) — Hebrew אַף־אַתָּה "even you"; תָּפֵר, hifil of פָּרַר "break, make void"; יִרְאָה "fear" stands alone ("of El" is not written in the first line). שִׂיחָה "musing, meditation" (also "complaint"); תִגְרַע "diminish" (the verb of v8).
- **"For thy mouth uttereth thine iniquity" → "For your iniquity teaches your mouth"** (v5) — Hebrew יְאַלֵּף עֲוֺנְךָ פִיךָ; אִלֵּף is "teach". Either noun can be the subject ("your mouth teaches your iniquity"); the order of the Hebrew is followed. **"the crafty" → "the shrewd"** — עֲרוּמִים, the word of 5:12.
- **"Thine own mouth condemneth thee … yea, thine own lips testify against thee" → "Your mouth condemns you, and not I, and your lips answer against you"** (v6) — "own" is not written; הִרְשִׁיעַ "condemn" (declare wicked); עָנָה בְּ "answer against" (= testify against).
- **"Art thou the first man that was born? or wast thou made before the hills?" → "Were you born the first man, and before the hills were you brought forth?"** (v7) — Hebrew הֲרִאישׁוֹן אָדָם תִּוָּלֵד, literally "the first man are you born?"; אָדָם "man" (not the name). חוֹלָלְתָּ, polal of חִיל "writhe", of being brought forth in labor.
- **"Hast thou heard the secret of God? and dost thou restrain wisdom to thyself?" → "Do you hear in the secret counsel of Eloah, and take away wisdom to yourself?"** (v8) — Hebrew הַבְסוֹד אֱלוֹהַּ תִּשְׁמָע: סוֹד "secret counsel" (the close circle, and what is said in it; "council" is also read), with בְּ "in"; the verbs are imperfect. וְתִגְרַע אֵלֶיךָ "and take away to yourself" — the verb "diminish" of v4.
- **"which is not in us" → "that is not with us"** (v9) — Hebrew וְלֹא־עִמָּנוּ הוּא; "what" stands once at the head of the verse and is understood in the second line.
- **"very aged men, much elder than thy father" → "the aged … greater in days than your father"** (v10) — Hebrew יָשִׁישׁ; כַּבִּיר מֵאָבִיךָ יָמִים. בָּנוּ "among us" (literally "in us").
- **"Are the consolations of God small with thee? is there any secret thing with thee?" → "Are the consolations of El too little for you, and a word gently with you?"** (v11) — Hebrew הַמְעַט מִמְּךָ; וְדָבָר לָאַט עִמָּךְ, without a verb: לָאַט "gently, softly" (KJV took it from לוּט "cover", hence "secret").
- **"carry thee away … what do thy eyes wink at" → "take you away … why do your eyes flash"** (v12) — Hebrew יִקָּחֲךָ "take you" ("away" is supplied); יִרְזְמוּן is found only here: "flash" or "wink" (uncertain).
- **"turnest thy spirit against God, and lettest such words go out" → "turn your spirit against El and bring words out"** (v13) — Hebrew תָשִׁיב, hifil of שׁוּב "turn back"; אֶל "to, against"; רוּחַ "spirit" (also "anger, breath"); מִלִּין "words" — "such" is not written.
- **"What is man, that he should be clean?" → "What is mortal man, that he should be pure"** (v14) — Hebrew אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; זָכָה "be pure" (the verb of v15). צָדַק "be righteous".
- **"he putteth no trust in his saints; yea, the heavens are not clean in his sight" → "in his holy ones he does not believe, and the heavens are not pure in his eyes"** (v15) — qere בִּקְדֹשָׁיו "in his holy ones"; the ketiv בקדשו is the singular, "in his holy one". הֶאֱמִין בְּ "believe in" (= trust; so v22, 31). בְּעֵינָיו "in his eyes".
- **"How much more abominable and filthy is man, which drinketh iniquity like water?" → "how much less one who is abominable and corrupt, a man who drinks injustice like water!"** (v16) — Hebrew אַף כִּי "how much less / more"; נִתְעָב "abominable", נֶאֱלָח "corrupt" (as Ps 14:3); אִישׁ "a man"; עַוְלָה "injustice" (not עָוֺן "iniquity").
- **"that which I have seen I will declare" → "this I have beheld, and I will recount"** (v17) — Hebrew זֶה־חָזִיתִי וַאֲסַפֵּרָה: חָזָה "behold", סִפֵּר "recount".
- **"Which wise men have told from their fathers, and have not hid it" → "what wise men tell, and have not hidden, from their fathers"** (v18) — Hebrew יַגִּידוּ (imperfect) "tell"; the Hebrew order is kept: מֵאֲבוֹתָם "from their fathers" closes the verse and may belong to "tell" (handed down from the fathers) or to "not hidden". "It" is not written.
- **"the earth" → "the land"** (v19) — Hebrew הָאָרֶץ, here the land given to one people. **"passed among them" → "passed in their midst"** — בְּתוֹכָם.
- **"The wicked man travaileth with pain all his days, and the number of years is hidden to the oppressor" → "All the days of the wicked one, he writhes, and a number of years are hidden away for the ruthless"** (v20) — Hebrew כָּל־יְמֵי רָשָׁע הוּא מִתְחוֹלֵל: מִתְחוֹלֵל "writhes" (of pangs; "with pain" is not a second word). נִצְפְּנוּ "are hidden away, stored up" (צָפַן); עָרִיץ "ruthless" (one who strikes terror).
- **"A dreadful sound" → "A sound of dreads"** (v21) — Hebrew קוֹל־פְּחָדִים, plural of פַּחַד "dread". **"in prosperity" → "in shalom"** — Hebrew בַּשָּׁלוֹם (whole, at peace). **"the destroyer shall come upon him" → "comes"** — שׁוֹדֵד "destroyer" (one who devastates); imperfect.
- **"he is waited for of the sword" → "he is watched for, for the sword"** (v22) — qere וְצָפוּי, passive of צָפָה "watch, look out for"; the ketiv וצפו lacks the yod. אֱלֵי־חָרֶב "toward the sword".
- **"saying, Where is it? he knoweth that the day of darkness is ready" → "'Where?' … a day of darkness is ready"** (v23) — Hebrew אַיֵּה "where?"; "saying" is not written. יוֹם־חֹשֶׁךְ has no article. נָכוֹן "ready, made firm" (WORDS-HE.md "right / established"; here what is prepared stands at hand). The Greek reads אַיָּה "vulture" (from memory: not checked here); the MT is kept.
- **"Trouble and anguish shall make him afraid; they shall prevail against him, as a king ready to the battle" → "Distress and straits terrify him; it overpowers him like a king ready for the onset"** (v24) — Hebrew צַר "distress" (narrowness; the same letters are "adversary" elsewhere) and מְצוּקָה "straits"; בִּעֵת "terrify". תִּתְקְפֵהוּ is singular, "it overpowers him". כִּידוֹר is found only here: "onset, attack" (uncertain).
- **"stretcheth out his hand … and strengtheneth himself against the Almighty" → "stretched out his hand … and against Shaddai he makes himself mighty"** (v25) — Hebrew נָטָה, perfect; יִתְגַּבָּר, hitpael of גָּבַר "be mighty".
- **"He runneth upon him, even on his neck, upon the thick bosses of his bucklers" → "He runs at him with the neck, with the thickness of the bosses of his shields"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּצַוָּאר "with neck" (= neck stretched out, headlong; "stubbornly" is an interpretation); בַּעֲבִי "with the thickness of"; מָגִנָּיו "his shields". Who runs at whom is not named: the wicked at El (v25), or El at the wicked.
- **"Because he covereth his face with his fatness, and maketh collops of fat on his flanks" → "For he covered his face with his fat, and made fatness upon the flank"** (v27) — Hebrew כִּסָּה (perfect) … וַיַּעַשׂ; חֵלֶב "fat"; פִּימָה (only here) "fatness"; כֶּסֶל "flank, loin", without "his".
- **"he dwelleth in desolate cities, and in houses which no man inhabiteth, which are ready to become heaps" → "he abode in cities cut off, in houses where none dwell, that are made ready for heaps"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכּוֹן "and he abode" (שָׁכַן); נִכְחָדוֹת "cut off, effaced" (the root of "hidden", v18); לֹא־יֵשְׁבוּ לָמוֹ "they do not dwell in them"; הִתְעַתְּדוּ לְגַלִּים "are made ready for heaps".
- **"neither shall his substance continue, neither shall he prolong the perfection thereof upon the earth" → "his wealth will not stand, and their possession will not stretch out to the land"** (v29) — Hebrew חֵילוֹ "his wealth" (חַיִל: ability, force, wealth); יָקוּם "stand". מִנְלָם is found only here; "their possession" (or "gain") is uncertain, and the suffix is plural. יִטֶּה "stretch out, bend".
- **"He shall not depart out of darkness … and by the breath of his mouth shall he go away" → "He will not depart from darkness … and he will depart by the breath of his mouth"** (v30) — Hebrew לֹא־יָסוּר … וְיָסוּר: the one verb twice, kept. יֹנַקְתּוֹ "his shoot" (singular). "His mouth" may be El's or the man's own.
- **"Let not him that is deceived trust in vanity: for vanity shall be his recompence" → "Let him not believe in emptiness; he is led astray; for emptiness will be his exchange"** (v31) — Hebrew אַל־יַאֲמֵן בַּשָּׁו נִתְעָה: the ketiv בשו; the qere as printed is בַּשָּׁיו; both are taken as שָׁוְא "emptiness", the word of the second line. נִתְעָה "he is led astray" (nifal of תָּעָה). תְּמוּרָה "exchange" (what is received in place of a thing).
- **"It shall be accomplished before his time, and his branch shall not be green" → "Before his day it will be filled up, and his palm branch is not luxuriant"** (v32) — Hebrew בְּלֹא־יוֹמוֹ "in not his day"; תִּמָּלֵא "it will be filled up" (feminine: the "exchange" of v31); כִּפָּה "palm branch"; רַעֲנָנָה "luxuriant", without a verb.
- **"He shall shake off his unripe grape as the vine, and shall cast off his flower as the olive" → "He will wrong, like the vine, his unripe grapes, and cast off, like the olive, his blossom"** (v33) — Hebrew יַחְמֹס, the verb of חָמָס "violence": "treat with violence, wrong" (the vine loses its grapes before they ripen); בֹּסֶר "unripe grapes"; הִשְׁלִיךְ "cast"; נִצָּה "blossom".
- **"the congregation of hypocrites shall be desolate, and fire shall consume the tabernacles of bribery" → "the congregation of the godless is barren, and fire has devoured the tents of the bribe"** (v34) — Hebrew עֲדַת חָנֵף, singular "of a godless one"; גַּלְמוּד "barren, stony"; אָכְלָה, perfect; אָהֳלֵי־שֹׁחַד "tents of bribe".
- **"They conceive mischief, and bring forth vanity" → "Conceiving toil and bearing wickedness"** (v35) — Hebrew הָרֹה … וְיָלֹד, infinitives absolute; עָמָל "toil", אָוֶן "wickedness". בִּטְנָם "their belly"; מִרְמָה "deceit".

### Notes
- **Second round begins.** Eliphaz's second speech.
- **"Were you born the first man?"** (v7) — the question sets Job against the first of mankind and the age of the hills; compare Prov 8:25 (wisdom brought forth before the hills).
- **"The secret counsel of Eloah"** (v8) — סוֹד, the circle of those who hear Eloah's counsel (compare Jer 23:18, 22).
- **"His holy ones … the heavens"** (v15) — the thought of 4:17–19, repeated.
- **The fate of the wicked** (v20–35) — Eliphaz's portrait: dread, darkness, the sword, lost wealth, early end.
