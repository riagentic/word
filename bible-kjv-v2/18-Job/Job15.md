# Job 15

[1] Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

[2] "Should a wise man answer with airy knowledge,
and fill his belly with the east wind?

[3] Should he argue in unprofitable talk,
with speeches by which he can do no good?

[4] But you do away with the fear of *Elohim*
and diminish meditation before him.

[5] Your iniquity teaches your mouth,
and you choose the tongue of the crafty.

[6] Your own mouth condemns you, not I;
your own lips testify against you.

[7] Were you the first human born?
Were you brought forth before the hills?

[8] Have you listened in the secret council of Eloah?
Do you hoard wisdom to yourself?

[9] What do you know that we do not know?
What do you understand that is not also ours?

[10] Both the gray-haired and the aged are with us,
older than your father.

[11] Are the consolations of *Elohim* too small for you,
the word that deals gently with you?

[12] Why does your heart carry you away?
Why do your eyes flash —

[13] that you turn your spirit against *Elohim*
and let such words go out of your mouth?

[14] What is man, that he should be clean,
or one born of woman, that he should be righteous?

[15] Behold, he puts no trust in his holy ones —
the heavens are not clean in his eyes.

[16] How much less one who is abominable and corrupt —
man who drinks iniquity like water!

[17] I will show you, hear me;
what I have seen I will declare,

[18] what wise men have told from their fathers
and have not hidden —

[19] to whom alone the land was given,
and no stranger passed among them:

[20] the wicked man writhes in pain all his days,
through all the years reserved for the tyrant.

[21] Dreadful sounds are in his ears;
in prosperity the destroyer will come upon him.

[22] He does not believe he will return out of darkness,
and he is marked for the sword.

[23] He wanders abroad for bread, 'Where is it?'
He knows the day of darkness is at hand.

[24] Distress and anguish terrify him;
they overpower him, like a king ready for battle.

[25] For he stretched out his hand against *Elohim*
and bid defiance to Shaddai,

[26] running stubbornly against him
with thick-bossed shield.

[27] For he has covered his face with his fat,
and gathered fat upon his flanks.

[28] He dwells in ruined cities,
in houses no one inhabits —
destined to become heaps.

[29] He shall not be rich; his wealth will not endure,
nor his possessions stretch out over the land.

[30] He shall not escape from darkness;
the flame shall dry up his shoots,
and by the breath of his mouth he shall go away.

[31] Let him not trust in emptiness, deceiving himself,
for emptiness shall be his recompense.

[32] It shall be accomplished before his time,
and his branch will not be green.

[33] Like a vine he will shake off his unripe grapes,
like an olive tree he will cast off his blossom.

[34] For the company of the godless is barren,
and fire consumes the tents of bribery.

[35] They conceive mischief and bring forth wickedness;
their belly prepares deceit."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"vain knowledge" → "airy knowledge"** (v2) — *da'at ruach*.
- **"hypocrites" → "godless"** (v34) — *chanef*.
- **"restrain prayer" → "diminish meditation"** (v4) — *gara sichah*.

### Notes
- **Second round begins.** Eliphaz more hostile; Job has pushed too far.
- **"Were you the first human?"** (v7) — taunt at Job's supposed wisdom-primacy. Echoes Adam.
- **"Secret council of Eloah"** (v8) — divine council, to which wisdom-teachers claim access. Job doesn't.
- **Even holy ones / heavens unclean** — anthropology of corruption. Argument: certainly Job is guilty.
- **Portrait of the wicked's fate** (v20-35) — extended retribution-imagery directly aimed at Job's situation. Wealth fails, family fails, premature death. Friends diagnose Job as wicked.
