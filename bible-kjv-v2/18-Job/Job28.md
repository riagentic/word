# Job 28

[1] "For there is a source for silver,
and a place for gold, which they refine.

[2] Iron is taken from the dust,
and stone is poured out as copper.

[3] He sets an end to darkness,
and to every limit he searches out
the stone of gloom and the shadow of death.

[4] He breaks open a shaft away from the sojourner,
those forgotten by the foot;
they hang down far from mortal man, they sway.

[5] The earth — out of it comes bread,
and beneath it is overturned as by fire.

[6] Its stones are the place of sapphire,
and it has dust of gold.

[7] A path the bird of prey does not know,
and the eye of the falcon has not caught sight of it;

[8] the sons of pride have not trodden it,
the fierce lion has not passed over it.

[9] Against the flint he stretches out his hand;
he overturns mountains from the root.

[10] In the rocks he splits open channels,
and every precious thing his eye has seen.

[11] From weeping he binds up the rivers,
and what is hidden in it he brings out to light.

[12] And wisdom — from where shall it be found?
And where is the place of understanding?

[13] Mortal man does not know its valuation,
and it is not found in the land of the living.

[14] The deep says, 'It is not in me,'
and the sea says, 'It is not with me.'

[15] Refined gold cannot be given for it,
and silver cannot be weighed as its price.

[16] It cannot be weighed against the gold of ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧,
against precious onyx and sapphire.

[17] Gold and glass do not compare with it,
and vessels of fine gold are not its exchange.

[18] Coral and crystal shall not be mentioned,
and the drawing up of wisdom is above rubies.

[19] The topaz of ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ does not compare with it;
it cannot be weighed against pure gold.

[20] And wisdom — from where does it come?
And where is the place of understanding?

[21] And it is hidden from the eyes of all living,
and from the birds of the heavens it is concealed.

[22] ⟦abaddon|Avaddon|Abaddon⟧ and Death say,
'With our ears we have heard a report of it.'

[23] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ understands its way,
and he knows its place.

[24] For he looks to the ends of the earth;
under all the heavens he sees.

[25] To make a weight for the wind,
and he measured out the waters by measure;

[26] when he made a statute for the rain,
and a way for the thunderbolt of voices,

[27] then he saw it and recounted it;
he established it, and also searched it out.

[28] And he said to man:
'Behold, the fear of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, that is wisdom,
and to turn aside from evil is understanding.'"

## Changes

- **"Surely" → "For"** (v1) — Hebrew כִּי; "surely" is also possible. **"a vein for the silver" → "a source for silver"** — Hebrew מוֹצָא, literally "a going out": the place it comes out from. **"where they fine it" → "which they refine"** — Hebrew יָזֹקּוּ, "they refine", with no named subject.
- **"Iron is taken out of the earth, and brass is molten out of the stone" → "Iron is taken from the dust, and stone is poured out as copper"** (v2) — Hebrew מֵעָפָר "from the dust"; וְאֶבֶן יָצוּק נְחוּשָׁה, literally "and stone — poured, copper": the stone is the subject. נְחוּשָׁה is "bronze" elsewhere; of the ore, "copper".
- **"He setteth an end to darkness"** (v3, 9) — the Hebrew does not name the subject ("Man" is not written): it is the miner, unnamed, through v11. **"searcheth out all perfection: the stones of darkness, and the shadow of death" → "to every limit he searches out the stone of gloom and the shadow of death"** — Hebrew תַּכְלִית "limit, end"; אֶבֶן אֹפֶל "the stone of gloom" (singular); צַלְמָוֶת, pointed as "shadow of death" (צֵל + מָוֶת); many derive it from a root "be dark" and read "deep darkness".
- **"The flood breaketh out from the inhabitant; even the waters forgotten of the foot: they are dried up, they are gone away from men" → "He breaks open a shaft away from the sojourner, forgotten by the foot; they hang down far from mortal man, they sway"** (v4) — a difficult verse, word for word: פָּרַץ נַחַל "he breaks open a נַחַל" — elsewhere "wadi"; here "shaft" is the best-supported sense, but it is uncertain. מֵעִם־גָּר "away from one who sojourns" (uncertain). הַנִּשְׁכָּחִים מִנִּי־רָגֶל "the ones forgotten, away from the foot" (plural; "waters" is not in the Hebrew). דַּלּוּ "they hang low", נָעוּ "they sway" (not "dried up", "gone away"). אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man".
- **"under it is turned up as it were fire" → "beneath it is overturned as by fire"** (v5) — Hebrew תַחְתֶּיהָ נֶהְפַּךְ כְּמוֹ־אֵשׁ, literally "its underparts — overturned like fire"; "by" is supplied. The same verb as "overturns" in v9.
- **"The stones of it are the place of sapphires: and it hath dust of gold"** (v6) — Hebrew סַפִּיר, probably lapis lazuli. עַפְרֹת זָהָב, literally "dusts of gold". לוֹ "to it" or "to him" (the miner).
- **"There is a path which no fowl knoweth, and which the vulture's eye hath not seen" → "A path the bird of prey does not know, and the eye of the falcon has not caught sight of it"** (v7) — Hebrew עַיִט "bird of prey"; אַיָּה "falcon" (kind uncertain); שְׁזָפַתּוּ "caught sight of it".
- **"The lion's whelps have not trodden it, nor the fierce lion passed by it" → "the sons of pride have not trodden it, the fierce lion has not passed over it"** (v8) — Hebrew בְנֵי־שָׁחַץ "sons of pride" (= proud beasts; שַׁחַץ only here and 41:34); שַׁחַל, a poetic word for a lion, kept apart from אַרְיֵה "lion" (4:10) as "fierce lion"; the kind is uncertain.
- **"He putteth forth his hand upon the rock; he overturneth the mountains by the roots" → "Against the flint he stretches out his hand; he overturns mountains from the root"** (v9) — Hebrew חַלָּמִישׁ "flint"; מִשֹּׁרֶשׁ "from the root" (singular).
- **"He cutteth out rivers among the rocks" → "In the rocks he splits open channels"** (v10) — Hebrew יְאֹרִים, the word of the Nile and its arms; here cut channels or galleries. בִּקֵּעַ "split open". **"and his eye seeth" → "has seen"** — perfect.
- **"He bindeth the floods from overflowing; and the thing that is hid bringeth he forth to light" → "From weeping he binds up the rivers, and what is hidden in it he brings out to light"** (v11) — Hebrew מִבְּכִי "from weeping" (= from trickling) as the MT points it; others read מַבּוּכֵי "the sources of" (as נִבְכֵי־יָם, 38:16): not followed. תַעֲלֻמָהּ "its hidden thing".
- **"But where shall wisdom be found? and where is the place of understanding?" → "And wisdom — from where shall it be found? And where is the place of understanding?"** (v12, 20) — Hebrew וְהַחָכְמָה מֵאַיִן "And wisdom, from where"; חָכְמָה "wisdom" and בִּינָה "understanding" are kept apart through the chapter (v12, 18, 20, 28), as in Proverbs.
- **"Man knoweth not the price thereof" → "Mortal man does not know its valuation"** (v13) — Hebrew אֱנוֹשׁ; עֶרְכָּהּ "its valuation" (the word of the valuations of Lev 27). The Septuagint is commonly cited as "its way" (from memory: דַּרְכָּהּ); the MT is kept.
- **"The depth saith" → "The deep says"** (v14) — Hebrew תְּהוֹם.
- **"It cannot be gotten for gold" → "Refined gold cannot be given for it"** (v15) — Hebrew סְגוֹר, of the root of זָהָב סָגוּר "refined gold" (1 Kgs 6:20); literally "shut up" (uncertain). תַּחְתֶּיהָ "for it", literally "in its place". מְחִירָהּ "its price".
- **"It cannot be valued with the gold of Ophir" → "It cannot be weighed against the gold of Ophir"** (v16, 19) — Hebrew תְסֻלֶּה, "be weighed (against)", Lam 4:2. כֶּתֶם is another word for gold beside זָהָב (v17; the difference is not known); both are "gold". **"precious onyx, or the sapphire" → "precious onyx and sapphire"** — Hebrew וְ "and".
- **"The gold and the crystal cannot equal it: and the exchange of it shall not be for jewels of fine gold" → "Gold and glass do not compare with it, and vessels of fine gold are not its exchange"** (v17) — Hebrew יַעַרְכֶנָּה, from עָרַךְ "arrange, set in order": "set beside, compare". זְכוֹכִית "glass" (only here). כְּלִי־פָז "vessels of fine gold": פָּז, of the root of מוּפָז "fine gold" (1 Kgs 10:18). The "not" of the first line is carried into the second.
- **"No mention shall be made of coral, or of pearls: for the price of wisdom is above rubies" → "Coral and crystal shall not be mentioned, and the drawing up of wisdom is above rubies"** (v18) — Hebrew רָאמוֹת "coral" and גָבִישׁ "crystal" (both uncertain; גָּבִישׁ only here); "and", not "or". מֶשֶׁךְ "drawing up" (as a fisher's line or a bag drawn up: the price got for it; uncertain). פְּנִינִים "rubies" (uncertain: red stones or red coral, Lam 4:7; "pearls" in others). "For" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"The topaz of Ethiopia" → "The topaz of Cush"** (v19) — Hebrew פִּטְדַת־כּוּשׁ; פִּטְדָה "topaz" (traditional identification). **"pure gold"** — Hebrew כֶּתֶם טָהוֹר.
- **"seeing it is hid from the eyes of all living, and kept close from the fowls of the air" → "And it is hidden from the eyes of all living, and from the birds of the heavens it is concealed"** (v21) — Hebrew וְנֶעֶלְמָה "and it is hidden"; נִסְתָּרָה "it is concealed"; עוֹף הַשָּׁמַיִם "the birds of the heavens".
- **"Destruction and death say, We have heard the fame thereof with our ears" → "Abaddon and Death say, 'With our ears we have heard a report of it'"** (v22) — Hebrew אֲבַדּוֹן (from אָבַד "perish") as the token has it; שִׁמְעָהּ "a report of it".
- **"God understandeth the way thereof" → "Elohim understands its way"** (v23) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים; הֵבִין, of the root of בִּינָה "understanding".
- **"seeth under the whole heaven" → "under all the heavens he sees"** (v24) — Hebrew תַּחַת כָּל־הַשָּׁמַיִם.
- **"To make the weight for the winds; and he weigheth the waters by measure" → "To make a weight for the wind, and he measured out the waters by measure"** (v25) — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת "to make" (infinitive, with no "when"; it may go with v24 or with v26); רוּחַ "wind"; תִּכֵּן "measured out" (perfect).
- **"When he made a decree for the rain, and a way for the lightning of the thunder" → "when he made a statute for the rain, and a way for the thunderbolt of voices"** (v26) — Hebrew חֹק "statute"; חֲזִיז קֹלוֹת, literally "the thunderbolt of voices": קוֹל "voice, sound", the plural used of thunder.
- **"Then did he see it, and declare it; he prepared it, yea, and searched it out" → "then he saw it and recounted it; he established it, and also searched it out"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיְסַפְּרָהּ "recounted it"; הֱכִינָהּ "established it"; חֲקָרָהּ "searched it out".
- **"And unto man he said, Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding" → "And he said to man: 'Behold, the fear of Adonai, that is wisdom, and to turn aside from evil is understanding'"** (v28) — Hebrew לָאָדָם "to man"; הֵן "behold"; אֲדֹנָי (token `adonai`); וְסוּר מֵרָע "and to turn aside from evil", as "turning aside from evil" in 1:1, 8; 2:3.

### Notes
- **The poem on wisdom** — it stands between Job's speeches; whether it is Job's own words or the poet's is debated.
- **Mining** (v1–11) — shafts, forgotten depths, precious stones, cut channels: man reaches what is hidden in the earth.
- **But wisdom is not dug up** (v12–22) — it is not found in the land of the living, not bought, not known even by Abaddon and Death.
- **Only Elohim knows its way** (v23–27) — he saw it when he set the wind, the waters, the rain and the thunderbolt.
- **The close** (v28) — the fear of Adonai is wisdom, turning aside from evil is understanding (compare Prov 1:7; 9:10).
