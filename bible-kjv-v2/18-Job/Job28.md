# Job 28

[1] "Surely there is a vein for silver,
and a place where gold is refined.

[2] Iron is taken from the earth,
and copper is smelted from stone.

[3] Man sets an end to darkness
and searches to the farthest limit,
the stones of deep gloom.

[4] He sinks a shaft far from where people live,
places forgotten by foot;
they hang and swing far from mankind.

[5] The earth — from it bread comes forth;
but beneath, it is turned as by fire.

[6] Its stones are the place of sapphires;
it has dust of gold.

[7] The path no bird of prey knows,
and the falcon's eye has not seen;

[8] the proud beasts have not trodden it,
nor has the fierce lion passed over it.

[9] Man puts his hand against the flinty rock
and overturns mountains by the roots.

[10] He cuts out channels in the rocks;
his eye sees every precious thing.

[11] He dams up the streams from trickling;
what is hidden he brings out to light.

[12] But where shall wisdom — be found?
Where is the place of understanding?

[13] Man does not know its price,
nor is it found in the land of the living.

[14] The deep says, 'It is not in me';
the sea says, 'It is not with me.'

[15] It cannot be bought for pure gold,
nor can silver be weighed for its price.

[16] It cannot be valued with gold of Ophir,
with precious onyx or sapphire.

[17] Gold and glass cannot equal it,
nor can it be exchanged for articles of fine gold.

[18] Coral and crystal are not to be mentioned;
the pulling out of wisdom is above rubies.

[19] The topaz of Cush cannot equal it,
nor can it be valued in pure gold.

[20] From where, then, does wisdom come?
Where is the place of understanding?

[21] It is hidden from the eyes of all living
and concealed from the birds of the sky.

[22] Abaddon and Death say,
'We have heard a rumor of it with our ears.'

[23] *Elohim* understands the way to it;
he knows its place.

[24] For he looks to the ends of the earth
and sees under the whole heavens.

[25] When he gave to the wind its weight,
and meted out the waters by measure,

[26] when he made a decree for the rain,
and a way for the thunderbolt,

[27] then he saw it and declared it;
he established it and searched it out.

[28] And he said to man:
'Behold, the fear of the Lord — that is wisdom,
and to turn from evil is understanding.'"

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"fine" → "refine"** (v1).
- **"brass" → "copper"** (v2) — *nechoshet*.
- **"shadow of death" → "deep gloom"** (v3) — *tzalmaweth*.
- **"Ethiopia" → "Cush"** (v19).
- **"pearls" → "crystal"** (v18) — *gavish*.
- **"flood breaketh out from the inhabitant" → "sinks a shaft far from where people live"** (v4) — Hebrew difficult; refers to mining shafts.

### Notes
- **Wisdom Interlude** — interrupts the speech cycle. Either a hymn independent or composed by Job/poet.
- **Mining imagery** — brilliantly precise picture of underground mining: shafts, forgotten depths, precious stones, dug rivers.
- **But *chokhmah* is unmineable** — not found in natural world, not bought, not located even by Sheol.
- **Only *Elohim* knows** — wisdom is cosmological, fixed at creation.
- **Classic conclusion (v28)** — fear of *Adonai* is *chokhmah*; turning from evil is understanding. Echoes Prov 1:7 etc.
- **Theological bridge** — prepares for Yahweh's final speech; wisdom is divine, not human.
