# Job 19

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "How long will you grieve my soul
and crush me with utterances?

[3] These ten times you put me to shame;
you are not ashamed to wrong me.

[4] And even if indeed I have erred,
with me lodges my error.

[5] If indeed you magnify yourselves against me
and reprove me by my reproach,

[6] know then that ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ has wronged me
and with his net has encircled me.

[7] Behold, I cry out, 'Violence!' and I am not answered;
I cry for help, and there is no judgment.

[8] My path he has walled up, and I cannot pass over;
and on my tracks he sets darkness.

[9] My glory he has stripped from me
and has removed the crown of my head.

[10] He breaks me down all around, and I go;
and he has pulled up my hope like a tree.

[11] And his anger burned against me,
and he reckoned me to himself as his adversaries.

[12] Together his bands come,
and they have cast up their way against me
and have camped around my tent.

[13] My brothers he has put far from me,
and those who know me are surely estranged from me.

[14] Those near to me have ceased,
and those known to me have forgotten me.

[15] The sojourners of my house and my slave women
reckon me as a stranger;
a foreigner I have become in their eyes.

[16] To my slave I call, and he does not answer;
with my mouth I plead with him for favor.

[17] My breath is strange to my wife,
and I am loathsome to the sons of my belly.

[18] Even little ones reject me;
I rise, and they speak against me.

[19] All the men of my secret counsel abominate me,
and those whom I loved have turned against me.

[20] To my skin and to my flesh my bone clings,
and I have escaped with the skin of my teeth.

[21] Show me favor, show me favor, you my friends,
for the hand of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ has touched me.

[22] Why do you pursue me like ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and from my flesh you are not sated?

[23] Oh that my utterances were now written!
Oh that they were inscribed in a book,

[24] with a stylus of iron and lead,
forever in the rock they were hewn!

[25] And I — I know my redeemer lives,
and last, upon dust he will rise.

[26] And after my skin they have struck off this,
and from my flesh I shall behold ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧,

[27] whom I, I shall behold for myself,
and my eyes have seen, and not a stranger;
my kidneys are worn out in my bosom.

[28] When you say, 'How shall we pursue him?'
and the root of the matter is found in me,

[29] be afraid for yourselves because of the sword,
for fury is iniquities of the sword,
so that you may know there is judgment."

## Changes

- **"Then Job answered" → "And Job answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of every speech heading in the book.
- **"vex my soul, and break me in pieces with words" → "grieve my soul and crush me with utterances"** (v2) — Hebrew תּוֹגְיוּן from יָגָה "grieve"; דִּכָּא "crush". מִלִּים is the poetic word מִלָּה "utterance" (beside דָּבָר "word"), here and in v23.
- **"have ye reproached me: ye are not ashamed that ye make yourselves strange to me" → "you put me to shame; you are not ashamed to wrong me"** (v3) — Hebrew הִכְלִים "put to shame". תַּהְכְּרוּ is found only here and its meaning is uncertain ("wrong", "deal harshly with", "stare at"; KJV "make yourselves strange"). The two verbs stand side by side with no "to" or "that": the second completes the first.
- **"And be it indeed that I have erred, mine error remaineth with myself" → "And even if indeed I have erred, with me lodges my error"** (v4) — Hebrew וְאַף־אָמְנָם, "and even truly"; "if" is not written, the clause is read as a supposition (as v5 has אִם). תָּלִין from לוּן "lodge, spend the night". מְשׁוּגָה "error" only here, of the שָׁגָה "err" root.
- **"plead against me my reproach" → "reprove me by my reproach"** (v5) — Hebrew וְתוֹכִיחוּ עָלַי חֶרְפָּתִּי, literally "reprove against me my reproach": my disgrace is made the proof against me.
- **"God hath overthrown me, and hath compassed me with his net" → "Eloah has wronged me and with his net has encircled me"** (v6) — Hebrew עִוְּתָנִי from עִוֵּת "bend, make crooked" (of a judgment "pervert", Job 8:3); of a person, "wrong" (Lam 3:36). מְצוּדוֹ "his net" (root צוּד "hunt"); עָלַי הִקִּיף, literally "over me he has encircled". הִקִּיף is the root נקף "go around", not the נקף "strike off" of v26.
- **"I cry out of wrong, but I am not heard: I cry aloud" → "I cry out, 'Violence!' and I am not answered; I cry for help"** (v7) — Hebrew צָעַק חָמָס; שִׁוַּע "cry for help"; וְ is "and". מִשְׁפָּט "judgment".
- **"fenced up my way … darkness in my paths" → "My path he has walled up … on my tracks"** (v8) — Hebrew אֹרַח "path", נְתִיבָה "track". לֹא אֶעֱבוֹר "I cannot pass over".
- **"He hath destroyed me on every side, and I am gone: and mine hope hath he removed like a tree" → "He breaks me down all around, and I go; and he has pulled up my hope like a tree"** (v10) — Hebrew נָתַץ "break down" (as of a house); וָאֵלַךְ "and I go" (= I depart); הִסִּיעַ, hifil of נָסַע, "pull up" (as tent pegs; here a tree's roots).
- **"He hath also kindled his wrath against me, and he counteth me unto him as one of his enemies" → "And his anger burned against me, and he reckoned me to himself as his adversaries"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר … אַפּוֹ, "his anger (lit. nose) burned"; חָשַׁב "reckon"; כְצָרָיו "as his adversaries" (plural; "one of" is not written).
- **"His troops … raise up their way … encamp round about my tabernacle" → "his bands … have cast up their way … have camped around my tent"** (v12) — Hebrew גְּדוּדָיו "his bands"; סָלַל "cast up" (a ramp or road, as for a siege); אֹהֶל "tent". The first verb is imperfect ("come"), the next two are the narrative form (past).
- **"mine acquaintance are verily estranged" → "those who know me are surely estranged"** (v13) — Hebrew יֹדְעַי "those who know me"; אַךְ "surely, only"; זָרוּ from זוּר "be strange" (the root of זָר "stranger", v15, 27).
- **"My kinsfolk have failed, and my familiar friends" → "Those near to me have ceased, and those known to me"** (v14) — Hebrew קְרוֹבָי "those near to me" (by kin or by dwelling: not said); חָדְלוּ "have ceased"; מְיֻדָּעַי, literally "my known ones".
- **"They that dwell in mine house, and my maids, count me for a stranger: I am an alien" → "The sojourners of my house and my slave women reckon me as a stranger; a foreigner"** (v15) — Hebrew גָּרֵי בֵיתִי, of גּוּר "sojourn"; אַמְהֹתַי "my slave women"; זָר "stranger", נָכְרִי "foreigner"; בְעֵינֵיהֶם "in their eyes".
- **"I called my servant … I intreated him with my mouth" → "To my slave I call … with my mouth I plead with him for favor"** (v16) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, a man of Job's household: "slave"; אֶתְחַנֶּן, hitpael of חָנַן "show favor", "plead for favor".
- **"though I intreated for the children's sake of mine own body" → "and I am loathsome to the sons of my belly"** (v17) — Hebrew וְחַנֹּתִי: uncertain. It is read from a rare root "be loathsome" (the parallel line); from the common חָנַן it would be "I plead for favor" (KJV). זָרָה "strange", the זוּר of v13 (some read a second root "be loathsome" here too). בְּנֵי בִטְנִי "the sons of my belly": whose belly is not said — Job's own sons, or the sons of the womb he came from (his brothers; "the doors of my belly", 3:10). Both readings stay open.
- **"young children despised me; I arose" → "little ones reject me; I rise"** (v18) — Hebrew עֲוִילִים "little ones" (as 21:11); מָאַס "reject"; אָקוּמָה "I rise" (cohortative form); דִּבֶּר בְּ "speak against".
- **"All my inward friends abhorred me" → "All the men of my secret counsel abominate me"** (v19) — Hebrew מְתֵי סוֹדִי, "the men of my secret counsel" (סוֹד: the close circle and what is said in it; "council" in Changes, as 29:4); תִּעֵב "abominate". וְזֶה־אָהַבְתִּי, literally "and this one I loved", with a plural verb.
- **"My bone cleaveth to my skin" → "To my skin and to my flesh my bone clings"** (v20) — Hebrew עַצְמִי "my bone" (singular), in the Hebrew order; וָאֶתְמַלְּטָה "and I have escaped".
- **"Have pity upon me … O ye my friends; for the hand of God hath touched me" → "Show me favor … you my friends, for the hand of Eloah has touched me"** (v21) — Hebrew חָנֻּנִי from חָנַן "show favor"; נָגַע בְּ "touch" (of a blow: the plain verb kept).
- **"persecute me as God, and are not satisfied with my flesh" → "pursue me like El, and from my flesh you are not sated"** (v22) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*; רָדַף "pursue"; מִבְּשָׂרִי "from my flesh" (= to eat of a man's flesh is to slander him: Ps 27:2; Dan 3:8 Aramaic "ate the pieces of").
- **"Oh that … oh that they were printed in a book!" → "Oh that … Oh that they were inscribed in a book"** (v23) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן, literally "Who will give …?", a wish: the verse gives the meaning. בַּסֵּפֶר "in a book" (any written document; "scroll" is מְגִלָּה). חָקַק "inscribe".
- **"That they were graven with an iron pen and lead in the rock for ever!" → "with a stylus of iron and lead, forever in the rock they were hewn!"** (v24) — Hebrew עֵט "stylus"; עֹפֶרֶת "lead" (poured into the cut letters, or a lead tablet: not said); יֵחָצְבוּן from חָצַב "hew"; לָעַד "forever".
- **"For I know that my redeemer liveth" → "And I — I know my redeemer lives"** (v25) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי יָדַעְתִּי גֹּאֲלִי חָי, word for word: "and I, I know, my redeemer living". וְ is "and" (not "for"); the pronoun אֲנִי stands for emphasis; no "that" (כִּי) is written. גֹּאֵל "redeemer": the one who buys back a kinsman's land or person and stands up for his cause (Lev 25:25; Num 35:12 "the redeemer of blood"; Ruth 3:12; Prov 23:11; of Yahweh Isa 41:14). Who Job means is not said in the verse: Eloah himself (v26), a witness in heaven (16:19), or another. חַי may be the verb "lives" or the adjective "living".
- **"and that he shall stand at the latter day upon the earth" → "and last, upon dust he will rise"** (v25) — Hebrew וְאַחֲרוֹן עַל־עָפָר יָקוּם. אַחֲרוֹן "last" is an adjective: "as the last one" (a title, Isa 44:6 "I am the last"), or "at last, afterward"; "day" is not written. עָפָר is "dust", without the article: the earth's surface, or the dust of the grave (Job 7:21; 17:16; 20:11; 21:26); "the earth" (אֶרֶץ) is not written. יָקוּם "he will rise / stand up" (of a witness standing up in court, Deut 19:15–16; of rising from the dead, Isa 26:14, 19). Each word kept open.
- **"And though after my skin worms destroy this body" → "And after my skin they have struck off this"** (v26) — Hebrew וְאַחַר עוֹרִי נִקְּפוּ־זֹאת, word for word, in the Hebrew order: "and after my skin they have struck off this". "Worms" and "body" are not in the Hebrew (KJV supplied them). נִקְּפוּ: piel of נָקַף "strike off, cut down" (Isa 10:34, of a thicket), plural with no subject (the Hebrew way of saying "has been struck off"). זֹאת "this" is feminine and refers to no noun named; עוֹר "skin" is masculine. "After" may be "after (the loss of) my skin" or "afterward".
- **"yet in my flesh shall I see God" → "and from my flesh I shall behold Eloah"** (v26) — Hebrew וּמִבְּשָׂרִי אֶחֱזֶה אֱלוֹהַּ. מִן may be "from (out of) my flesh" = still in the body, looking out from it; or "away from my flesh" = without the body. "Yet" is not written (וְ "and"). חָזָה "behold". The ancient readings of v26 differ widely (Greek, Latin, Targum: from memory); nothing from them is in the verse.
- **"Whom I shall see for myself, and mine eyes shall behold, and not another" → "whom I, I shall behold for myself, and my eyes have seen, and not a stranger"** (v27) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר אֲנִי אֶחֱזֶה־לִּי: the pronoun repeated; לִּי "for myself" (or "on my side"). רָאוּ is a perfect, "have seen": kept (a perfect of certainty for the future is possible; the reader decides). וְלֹא־זָר "and not a stranger": the word of v13, 15 — no other man sees in my place; or "not as a stranger" (Eloah no longer estranged).
- **"though my reins be consumed within me" → "my kidneys are worn out in my bosom"** (v27) — Hebrew כָּלוּ כִלְיֹתַי בְּחֵקִי, "my kidneys are spent in my bosom": the kidneys as the seat of the inmost feeling, worn out with longing (as eyes "wear out", Deut 28:32). "Though" is not written.
- **"But ye should say, Why persecute we him, seeing the root of the matter is found in me?" → "When you say, 'How shall we pursue him?' and the root of the matter is found in me"** (v28) — Hebrew כִּי "when / if"; מַה "how / what". בִי "in me" is the Masoretic Text; many Hebrew manuscripts and the ancient versions read בוֹ "in him" (from memory), which would make the second line part of the friends' words. Where their words end is not marked. שֹׁרֶשׁ דָּבָר "the root of the matter".
- **"Be ye afraid of the sword: for wrath bringeth the punishments of the sword" → "be afraid for yourselves because of the sword, for fury is iniquities of the sword"** (v29) — Hebrew גּוּרוּ לָכֶם מִפְּנֵי־חֶרֶב; כִּי־חֵמָה עֲוֺנוֹת חָרֶב has no verb ("brings" is supplied in KJV): fury counts among the iniquities that the sword punishes. חֵמָה "fury" (lit. "heat").
- **"that ye may know there is a judgment"** (v29) — qere שַׁדּוּן, "that (there is) judgment" (שֶׁ + דּוּן); the ketiv שדין has the same sense (שֶׁ + דִּין). The written letters are close to שַׁדַּי *Shaddai*; nothing in the verse says that the name is meant. "Judgment", not the "cause" of the דִּין noun row: a judging is meant here.

### Notes
- **Poetry** — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order.
- **Social death** (v13–19) — brothers, those near, household, slaves, wife, little ones and the men of his secret counsel turn from Job.
- **"The skin of my teeth"** (v20) — the phrase has entered English proverb; what part of the body is meant is not certain.
- **v25–27** — the most disputed lines of the book. The verse is kept word for word; every reading choice is listed above. Whether Job speaks of vindication before death or after it, in the body or without it, and who the redeemer is, the Hebrew does not decide. Christian tradition reads the passage of resurrection and of the Messiah (Handel's *Messiah*).
- **Inscription wish** (v23–24) — Job wants his words kept in writing, on rock, forever.
- **Warning to the friends** (v28–29) — their pursuit of Job will bring the sword on them.
