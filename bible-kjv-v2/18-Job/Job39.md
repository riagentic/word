# Job 39

[1] "Do you know the time of giving birth of the ibexes of the crag?
Do you keep watch over the writhing of the does?

[2] Do you number the moons that they fulfill,
and do you know the time of their giving birth?

[3] They bow, they let their young break out,
they send away their pangs.

[4] Their sons grow strong, they grow great in the open field;
they go out and do not return to them.

[5] Who has sent away the wild donkey free,
and the bonds of the onager, who has loosed them,

[6] whose house I have made the steppe,
and his dwelling places the salt land?

[7] He laughs at the multitude of the town;
the shouts of the taskmaster he does not hear.

[8] The range of the mountains is his pasture,
and after every green thing he seeks.

[9] Is the wild ox willing to serve you?
Or will he lodge at your crib?

[10] Can you bind the wild ox in the furrow with his rope?
Or will he harrow the valleys after you?

[11] Will you trust in him because his power is great,
and leave to him your labor?

[12] Will you believe him that he will bring back your seed,
and gather your threshing floor?

[13] The wing of the ostriches rejoices;
is it the pinion of the stork, and feathers?

[14] For she leaves her eggs to the earth,
and on the dust she warms them,

[15] and she forgets that a foot may crush it,
and the living thing of the field trample it.

[16] She is hard toward her sons, as if not hers;
in vain is her labor, without dread,

[17] for ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ has made her forget wisdom
and did not allot her a share in understanding.

[18] At the time she lifts herself on high,
she laughs at the horse and at his rider.

[19] Do you give the horse might?
Do you clothe his neck with a mane?

[20] Do you make him quake like the locusts?
The majesty of his snorting is dread.

[21] They dig in the valley, and he exults in power;
he goes out to meet the weapons.

[22] He laughs at dread and is not dismayed,
and he does not turn back from the face of the sword.

[23] Upon him rattles the quiver,
the flame of spear and javelin.

[24] With quaking and agitation he swallows the earth,
and he does not believe that it is the sound of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧.

[25] At each ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ he says, 'Aha!'
and from afar he smells war,
the thunder of the commanders and the shout.

[26] Is it from your understanding that the hawk soars,
spreads his wings toward the south?

[27] Or is it at your mouth that the eagle mounts up,
and that he raises his nest?

[28] On the crag he abides and lodges,
on the tooth of the crag and the stronghold.

[29] From there he searches out food;
from afar his eyes look.

[30] And his young suck up blood,
and where the slain are, there is he."

## Changes

- **"the wild goats of the rock" → "the ibexes of the crag"** (v1) — Hebrew יַעֲלֵי־סָלַע; יָעֵל is the ibex (Ps 104:18), אַקּוֹ "wild goat" another word; סֶלַע "crag". **"when the hinds do calve" → "the writhing of the does"** — Hebrew חֹלֵל אַיָּלוֹת, "the writhing" of birth; אַיָּלָה "doe" (אַיָּל "deer"). **"mark" → "keep watch over"** — Hebrew שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"months" → "moons"** (v2) — Hebrew יְרָחִים, from יָרֵחַ "moon". **"or" → "and"**.
- **"They bow themselves, they bring forth their young ones, they cast out their sorrows" → "They bow, they let their young break out, they send away their pangs"** (v3) — Hebrew יַלְדֵיהֶן תְּפַלַּחְנָה, literally "they cleave (split) their children" (פִּלַּח "cleave"); חֶבְלֵיהֶם "their pangs" (masculine suffix); שִׁלַּח "send away".
- **"Their young ones are in good liking, they grow up with corn" → "Their sons grow strong, they grow great in the open field"** (v4) — Hebrew יַחְלְמוּ (חָלַם "be strong", not "dream"); בְּנֵיהֶם "their sons"; בַּבָּר, an Aramaic word, "in the open field" (not בָּר "grain"). **"return not unto them" → "do not return to them"** — לָמוֹ kept.
- **"sent out the wild ass free" → "sent away the wild donkey free"** (v5) — Hebrew פֶּרֶא (as Gen 16:12), שִׁלַּח, חָפְשִׁי. **"the wild ass" → "the onager"** — Hebrew עָרוֹד, found only here (Aramaic in Dan 5:21): a second word for a wild donkey, kept apart.
- **"wilderness … barren land" → "steppe … salt land"** (v6) — Hebrew עֲרָבָה (without the article: not the place name) and מְלֵחָה "salt land". **"house"** — Hebrew בַּיִת.
- **"scorneth" → "laughs at"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׂחַק, the verb of v18, 22. **"the crying of the driver" → "the shouts of the taskmaster"** — Hebrew תְּשֻׁאוֹת, a din: of thunder "crashings" (36:29), of a man "shouts" (reviewer) and נֹגֵשׂ "taskmaster" (Exod 3:7).
- **"The range of the mountains is his pasture"** (v8) — Hebrew יְתוּר, a noun as pointed (from תּוּר "explore"); read as a verb, "he explores". **"searcheth" → "seeks"** — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ.
- **"unicorn" → "wild ox"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew רֵים *re'em*, as Num 23:22; a wild ox (aurochs), not certain. **"abide by thy crib" → "lodge at your crib"** — Hebrew יָלִין עַל־אֲבוּסֶךָ. **"Or"** — Hebrew אִם.
- **"bind the unicorn with his band in the furrow" → "bind the wild ox in the furrow with his rope"** (v10) — Hebrew רֵים is repeated; עֲבֹת "rope".
- **"Wilt thou trust him" → "Will you trust in him"** (v11) — Hebrew בָּטַח בְּ. **"strength" → "power"** — Hebrew כֹּחַ. **"labour" → "labor"** — Hebrew יְגִיעַ (also v16; עָמָל "toil" is another word).
- **"Wilt thou believe him, that he will bring home thy seed, and gather it into thy barn?" → "Will you believe him that he will bring back your seed, and gather your threshing floor?"** (v12) — Hebrew הֶאֱמִין "believe" (v11 has בָּטַח "trust"); qere יָשִׁיב "he will bring back" (ketiv ישוב "that your seed returns"); זֶרַע "seed"; וְגָרְנְךָ יֶאֱסֹף "and your threshing floor he gathers" (= its grain).
- **"Gavest thou the goodly wings unto the peacocks? or wings and feathers unto the ostrich?" → "The wing of the ostriches rejoices; is it the pinion of the stork, and feathers?"** (v13) — The Hebrew has no question in the first line and no "you gave". רְנָנִים is found only here: "ostriches" by the context (eggs left in the dust, v14), literally "cries (of joy)"; בַּת הַיַּעֲנָה is the usual word for ostrich (Lev 11:16). נֶעֱלָסָה "rejoices" (others "flaps", uncertain). אֶבְרָה "pinion", חֲסִידָה "stork" (Lev 11:19); some read it as "kindly" (from חֶסֶד: "a kindly pinion"), and נֹצָה "feathers": the line is uncertain.
- **"warmeth them in dust" → "on the dust she warms them"** (v14) — Hebrew עָפָר "dust" (not "sand"); "them" is supplied.
- **"may crush them … may break them" → "may crush it … trample it"** (v15) — the Hebrew suffix is singular. **"the wild beast" → "the living thing of the field"** — Hebrew חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה (חַיָּה "living thing"); תְּדוּשֶׁהָ "trample", the verb of threshing (דּוּשׁ).
- **"She is hardened against her young ones" → "She is hard toward her sons"** (v16) — Hebrew הִקְשִׁיחַ בָּנֶיהָ; the verb is masculine with a feminine subject. **"her labour is in vain without fear" → "in vain is her labor, without dread"** — Hebrew פַּחַד "dread".
- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ. **"neither hath he imparted to her understanding" → "did not allot her a share in understanding"** — Hebrew חָלַק לָהּ בַּבִּינָה; חָלַק "divide, allot" carries "a share".
- **"What time she lifteth up herself on high" → "At the time she lifts herself on high"** (v18) — Hebrew כָּעֵת בַּמָּרוֹם תַּמְרִיא; הִמְרִיא found only here, uncertain ("rouse herself", "beat her wings"). No "to flee". **"scorneth" → "laughs at"** — Hebrew שָׂחַק; רֹכֵב "rider".
- **"strength" → "might"** (v19) — Hebrew גְּבוּרָה. **"thunder" → "mane"** — Hebrew רַעְמָה, found only here, of the root of רַעַם "thunder" (v25): "a quivering (mane)"; uncertain.
- **"make him afraid as a grasshopper" → "make him quake like the locusts"** (v20) — Hebrew הְתַרְעִישֶׁנּוּ (רָעַשׁ "quake"; "leap" in Changes only) and אַרְבֶּה "locusts". **"the glory of his nostrils is terrible" → "the majesty of his snorting is dread"** — Hebrew הוֹד "majesty", נַחְרוֹ "his snorting", אֵימָה "dread".
- **"He paweth in the valley, and rejoiceth in his strength: he goeth on to meet the armed men" → "They dig in the valley, and he exults in power; he goes out to meet the weapons"** (v21) — Hebrew יַחְפְּרוּ "they dig" (plural: his hooves); חָפַר "dig"; כֹּחַ "power"; נָשֶׁק "weapons".
- **"mocketh at fear, and is not affrighted" → "laughs at dread and is not dismayed"** (v22) — Hebrew שָׂחַק; פַּחַד; יֵחָת. **"from the sword" → "from the face of the sword"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי־חָרֶב.
- **"the glittering spear and the shield" → "the flame of spear and javelin"** (v23) — Hebrew לַהַב "flame" (the flashing blade); כִּידוֹן "javelin".
- **"He swalloweth the ground with fierceness and rage: neither believeth he that it is the sound of the trumpet" → "With quaking and agitation he swallows the earth, and he does not believe that it is the sound of the shofar"** (v24) — Hebrew רַעַשׁ "quaking" (the root of v20), רֹגֶז "agitation"; גִּמֵּא "swallow", found in this sense only here; יַאֲמִין "believe" (as v12; others "he does not stand still", from the root sense "be firm").
- **"He saith among the trumpets, Ha, ha" → "At each shofar he says, 'Aha!'"** (v25) — Hebrew בְּדֵי שֹׁפָר, "as often as the shofar"; הֶאָח. **"the battle" → "war"** — Hebrew מִלְחָמָה. **"the thunder of the captains, and the shouting" → "the thunder of the commanders and the shout"** — Hebrew שָׂרִים; תְּרוּעָה of voices.
- **"Doth the hawk fly by thy wisdom" → "Is it from your understanding that the hawk soars"** (v26) — Hebrew מִבִּינָתְךָ "from your understanding"; יַאֲבֶר, from אֵבֶר "pinion" ("take wing"); נֵץ "hawk". **"his wings"** — qere כְּנָפָיו (ketiv כנפו "his wing"). **"toward the south"** — Hebrew לְתֵימָן.
- **"at thy command" → "at your mouth"** (v27) — Hebrew עַל־פִּיךָ, = at your command. **"make his nest on high" → "raises his nest"** — Hebrew יָרִים קִנּוֹ. נֶשֶׁר "eagle" (the word also covers the great vultures, Lev 11:13).
- **"She dwelleth and abideth on the rock, upon the crag of the rock, and the strong place" → "On the crag he abides and lodges, on the tooth of the crag and the stronghold"** (v28) — Hebrew סֶלַע "crag"; שָׁכַן "abide"; שֶׁן־סֶלַע "tooth of the crag"; מְצוּדָה "stronghold".
- **"she seeketh the prey" → "he searches out food"** (v29) — Hebrew חָפַר־אֹכֶל, literally "digs food" (חָפַר "search out" as Deut 1:22); אֹכֶל "food". **"behold afar off" → "from afar … look"** — Hebrew יַבִּיטוּ.
- **"Her young ones also" → "And his young"** (v30) — qere וְאֶפְרֹחָיו (ketiv ואפרחו, singular: "his young one"); אֶפְרֹחַ "young bird". **"suck up"** — Hebrew יְעַלְעוּ, found only here. **"slain"** — Hebrew חֲלָלִים.

### Notes
- **Beasts and birds** — ibex, doe, wild donkey, onager, wild ox, ostrich, horse, hawk, eagle: none of them is ruled by man.
- **Wild donkey free** (v5–8) — far from the town and its taskmaster.
- **Wild ox** (v9–12) — not for man's service.
- **Ostrich** (v13–18) — Eloah has made her forget wisdom; yet she laughs at the horse.
- **Horse** (v19–25) — in battle.
- **Hawk and eagle** (v26–30) — the chapter ends with the eagle's young sucking up blood.
