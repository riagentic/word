# Job 30

[1] "But now those who are younger than I deride me —
whose fathers I would have disdained
to set with the dogs of my flock.

[2] The strength of their hands — of what use to me?
Their vigor has perished.

[3] Through want and hard hunger they gnaw the dry ground,
in the waste and desolate night.

[4] They pluck mallow among the bushes,
and the roots of broom are their food.

[5] They are driven out from among men;
men shout after them as after a thief,

[6] so that they dwell in the gullies of wadis,
in holes of the earth and rocks.

[7] Among the bushes they bray;
under the nettles they huddle.

[8] A senseless, nameless brood,
they were flogged out of the land.

[9] Now I am their mocking song;
I am their byword.

[10] They abhor me, they keep aloof from me;
they do not hesitate to spit at my face.

[11] Because he has loosed my cord and afflicted me,
they have cast off restraint in my presence.

[12] On my right hand a brood rises up;
they drive away my feet
and cast up against me their ways of destruction.

[13] They break up my path;
they promote my calamity;
no one restrains them.

[14] As through a wide breach they come;
amid the crash they roll on.

[15] Terrors are turned upon me;
my dignity is pursued as by the wind;
my prosperity has passed away like a cloud.

[16] And now my soul is poured out within me;
days of affliction have taken hold of me.

[17] The night racks my bones;
the pain that gnaws me takes no rest.

[18] With great force my garment is disfigured;
it binds me like the collar of my tunic.

[19] He has cast me into the mire,
and I have become like dust and ashes.

[20] I cry to you, and you do not answer me;
I stand up, and you merely look at me.

[21] You have turned cruel to me;
with the might of your hand you persecute me.

[22] You lift me up on the wind; you make me ride on it;
you toss me about in the storm.

[23] I know that you will bring me to death,
to the house appointed for all living.

[24] Yet does not one in a heap of ruins stretch out his hand?
Does he not cry for help in his disaster?

[25] Did not I weep for him who had a hard day?
Was not my soul grieved for the needy?

[26] When I looked for good, evil came;
when I waited for light, darkness came.

[27] My inner parts are in turmoil and never still;
days of affliction come to meet me.

[28] I go about mourning without the sun;
I stand up in the assembly and cry for help.

[29] I am a brother of jackals
and a companion of ostriches.

[30] My skin turns black and falls from me;
my bones burn with heat.

[31] My lyre is turned to mourning,
and my flute to the voice of those who weep."

## Changes

- **"dragons" → "jackals"** (v29) — *tannim*.
- **"owls" → "ostriches"** (v29) — *benot ya'anah*.
- **"viler than the earth" → "flogged out of the land"** (v8) — Hebrew *nikke'u min-ha-aretz*.
- **"mallows by the bushes" → "mallow among the bushes"** (v4) — *malluach*; salt-plant.
- **"juniper roots" → "broom roots"** (v4) — *retem*; broom-bush (same as 1 Kgs 19:4).
- **"house appointed for all living" — retained** (v23) — Sheol.
- **"harp... organ" → "lyre... flute"** (v31).

### Notes
- **Reversal of ch 29** — total collapse of Job's honor.
- **Mocked by the dregs** — the poorest class now deride him.
- **Desert-dwelling outcasts** (v3-8) — survival in caves, roots and bushes for food.
- **God as persecutor** (v19-22) — direct address: turned cruel, tossing in storm.
- **"Jackals and ostriches"** — desert creatures known for wailing sounds, metaphor for mourning company.
- **Blackened skin** — disease's physical manifestation.
- **Musical instruments turned to lament** — whole life inverted.
