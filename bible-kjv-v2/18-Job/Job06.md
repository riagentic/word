# Job 6

[1] Then Job answered and said:

[2] "Oh that my grief were truly weighed,
and my calamity laid on the scales together!

[3] For now it would be heavier than the sand of the seas —
therefore my words have been wild.

[4] For the arrows of Shaddai are in me;
my spirit drinks their poison;
the terrors of Eloah are arrayed against me.

[5] Does the wild ass bray when it has grass?
Does the ox low over its fodder?

[6] Can what is tasteless be eaten without salt?
Is there flavor in the slime of the mallow?

[7] My soul refuses to touch them;
they are like loathsome food to me.

[8] Oh that I might have my request,
that Eloah would grant what I long for —

[9] that it would please Eloah to crush me,
that he would loose his hand and cut me off!

[10] This would still be my comfort —
I would exult in relentless pain —
that I have not denied the words of the Holy One.

[11] What is my strength, that I should wait?
What is my end, that I should be patient?

[12] Is my strength the strength of stones?
Is my flesh bronze?

[13] Is my help not within me?
Has resourcefulness been driven from me?

[14] To the despairing, steadfast love should be shown by his friend —
or he forsakes the fear of Shaddai.

[15] My brothers have dealt deceitfully as a wadi,
as the course of wadis that run dry —

[16] dark with ice,
their snow hidden in them;

[17] when warmed, they vanish;
when hot, they disappear from their place.

[18] The paths of their way wind off;
they go into the wasteland and perish.

[19] The caravans of Tema looked;
the travelers of Sheba hoped for them.

[20] They were confounded because they had trusted;
they came there and were disappointed.

[21] So now you have become nothing;
you see a terror, and are afraid.

[22] Did I say, 'Give me something'?
or, 'From your wealth offer a bribe for me'?

[23] or, 'Deliver me from the enemy's hand'?
or, 'Redeem me from the hand of tyrants'?

[24] Teach me, and I will be silent;
make me understand how I have erred.

[25] How forceful are honest words!
But what does your reproof rebuke?

[26] Do you intend to rebuke words,
when the speeches of a despairing man are wind?

[27] You would cast lots over the orphan
and bargain over your friend.

[28] Now, please look at me —
I will not lie to your face.

[29] Relent! Let there be no injustice.
Relent! My righteousness — is at stake.

[30] Is there injustice on my tongue?
Can my palate not discern calamity?"

## Changes

- **"my words are swallowed up" → "my words have been wild"** (v3) — *la'u*; faltered/rash.
- **"white of an egg" → "slime of the mallow"** (v6) — Hebrew *rir chalamut*; disputed — either "slime of an egg's white" or "juice of mallow plant."
- **"brook / stream of brooks" → "wadi / course of wadis"** (v15) — *nachal*.

### Notes
- **Job's first response** — directed at Eliphaz's comfort.
- **Heavier than sand** — classic hyperbole for grief.
- **Arrows of *Shaddai*** (v4) — first use; divine-assault imagery.
- **Wanting death** — wishes Yahweh would just finish him.
- **"Brothers deceitful as wadi"** — seasonal streambeds dry when travelers most need them. Caravans of Tema (Arabia) and Sheba reach the wadi in vain. Image for friends' failure.
- **Accuses friends of profiteering from his misery** (v27) — harsh rhetoric, reflects pain.
- **"I will not lie to your face"** — refuses their implied diagnostic that he has sinned.
