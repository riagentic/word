# Job 32

[1] And these three men ceased from answering ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧, because he was righteous in his own eyes.

[2] And the anger of Elihu the son of Barachel the ⟦buzite|Buzi|Buzite⟧, of the clan of Ram, burned. Against ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ his anger burned, because he justified his soul rather than ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] And against his three friends his anger burned, because they had not found an answer, and they condemned ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧.

[4] And Elihu had waited for ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ with words, because they were older than he in days.

[5] And Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of the three men, and his anger burned.

[6] And Elihu the son of Barachel the ⟦buzite|Buzi|Buzite⟧ answered and said:

"Young am I in days, and you are aged;
therefore I shrank back and was afraid to declare my knowledge to you.

[7] I said, 'Let days speak,
and let the multitude of years make wisdom known.'

[8] Truly, it is a spirit in mortal man,
and the breath of ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ gives them understanding.

[9] Not the many are wise,
nor do the old understand judgment.

[10] Therefore I say, 'Hear me;
I will declare my knowledge, I also.'

[11] Behold, I waited for your words;
I gave ear to your understandings,
while you searched out utterances.

[12] And up to you I paid attention,
and behold, there was none of you who reproved ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧,
answering his sayings.

[13] Lest you say, 'We have found wisdom;
⟦el|El|God⟧ will drive him away, not man.'

[14] And he has not arrayed utterances against me,
and with your sayings I will not reply to him.

[15] They are dismayed, they answer no more;
utterances have moved away from them.

[16] And I waited, for they do not speak,
for they stand still, they answer no more.

[17] I will answer, I also, my share;
I will declare my knowledge, I also.

[18] For I am full of utterances;
the spirit of my belly constrains me.

[19] Behold, my belly is like wine that is not opened;
like new wineskins it will be split open.

[20] Let me speak, and I shall have relief;
I will open my lips and answer.

[21] Let me not, please, lift up the face of a man,
and to mankind I will not give titles.

[22] For I do not know how to give titles;
in a little my Maker would carry me off."

## Changes

- **"God" → "Elohim"** (v2) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים; **"God" → "El"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵל; **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי (its meaning is not certain).
- **Prose and poetry** — v1–6a are prose; Elihu's speech (v6b–22) is poetry, one line to each Hebrew line.
- **"So these three men ceased to answer Job" → "And these three men ceased from answering Job"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁבְּתוּ "and they ceased" (שָׁבַת); מֵעֲנוֹת "from answering".
- **"Then was kindled the wrath of Elihu … of the kindred of Ram: against Job was his wrath kindled, because he justified himself rather than God" → "And the anger of Elihu … of the clan of Ram, burned. Against Job his anger burned, because he justified his soul rather than Elohim"** (v2, 3, 5) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר אַף, literally "and the nose burned" (אַף "nose, anger"), four times in v2–5; מִשְׁפַּחַת "clan"; צַדְּקוֹ נַפְשׁוֹ "his justifying his soul" (= himself); מֵאֱלֹהִים "more than Elohim", or "before / from Elohim" (that he was in the right against Elohim).
- **"and yet had condemned Job" → "and they condemned Job"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיַּרְשִׁיעוּ אֶת־אִיּוֹב "and they condemned (declared wicked) Job" (as 15:6); "yet" is not written. Jewish tradition counts this verse among the "corrections of the scribes" (*tiqqune sopherim*): the text is said to have read "and they condemned Elohim" (from memory; not in the WLC, which is followed).
- **"Now Elihu had waited till Job had spoken" → "And Elihu had waited for Job with words"** (v4) — Hebrew חִכָּה אֶת־אִיּוֹב בִּדְבָרִים "waited for Job with words" (= waited to speak to Job); זְקֵנִים … לְיָמִים "older … in days".
- **"these three men" → "the three men"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁלֹשֶׁת הָאֲנָשִׁים, without "these" (v1 has הָאֵלֶּה).
- **"Elihu … answered and said"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the book. **"I am young, and ye are very old; wherefore I was afraid, and durst not shew you mine opinion" → "Young am I in days, and you are aged; therefore I shrank back and was afraid to declare my knowledge to you"** — Hebrew לְיָמִים "in days"; יְשִׁישִׁים "aged"; זָחַלְתִּי "I shrank back" (literally "I crept"; uncertain); מֵחַוֺּת דֵּעִי "from declaring my knowledge"; דֵּעַ, from יָדַע "know", only in Elihu's speeches (v6, 10, 17; 36:3; 37:16): "my knowledge" (= what I know; KJV "opinion").
- **"Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom" → "Let days speak, and let the multitude of years make wisdom known"** (v7) — Hebrew יְדַבֵּרוּ … יֹדִיעוּ, imperfects with the force of a wish; יֹדִיעוּ, hifil of יָדַע "make known".
- **"But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding" → "Truly, it is a spirit in mortal man, and the breath of Shaddai gives them understanding"** (v8) — Hebrew אָכֵן "truly, surely"; רוּחַ־הִיא "it is a spirit" (רוּחַ is "spirit, wind, breath"); אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; נִשְׁמַת "the breath of"; תְּבִינֵם "makes them understand".
- **"Great men are not always wise: neither do the aged understand judgment" → "Not the many are wise, nor do the old understand judgment"** (v9) — Hebrew לֹא־רַבִּים "not many / not the great" (רַבִּים of age, as Gen 25:23 רַב "the elder", is also read: "not the old"); "always" is not written; the לֹא governs both lines; זְקֵנִים "old men"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment".
- **"Therefore I said, Hearken to me" → "Therefore I say, 'Hear me'"** (v10) — Hebrew אָמַרְתִּי, perfect ("I say" as of a decision now made); שִׁמְעָה, singular imperative "hear", although Elihu has spoken to the three in the plural (v6); אַף־אָנִי "I also".
- **"I gave ear to your reasons, whilst ye searched out what to say" → "I gave ear to your understandings, while you searched out utterances"** (v11) — Hebrew הוֹחַלְתִּי, hifil of יָחַל "wait" (the piel is "hope", 30:26); אָזִין "I gave ear"; תְּבוּנוֹתֵיכֶם "your understandings"; מִלִּין "utterances" (the Aramaic plural of מִלָּה; beside דְּבָרִים "words" in the same verse).
- **"Yea, I attended unto you, and, behold, there was none of you that convinced Job, or that answered his words" → "And up to you I paid attention, and behold, there was none of you who reproved Job, answering his sayings"** (v12) — Hebrew וְעָדֵיכֶם אֶתְבּוֹנָן "and up to you I paid attention" (the verb "gaze at" of 30:20; 31:1); מוֹכִיחַ "one who reproves" (הוֹכִיחַ); עוֹנֶה אֲמָרָיו מִכֶּם "answering his sayings, of you"; אֵמֶר "saying".
- **"Lest ye should say, We have found out wisdom: God thrusteth him down, not man" → "Lest you say, 'We have found wisdom; El will drive him away, not man'"** (v13) — Hebrew יִדְּפֶנּוּ, from נָדַף "drive away" (as wind drives chaff or smoke); אִישׁ "man".
- **"Now he hath not directed his words against me" → "And he has not arrayed utterances against me"** (v14) — Hebrew עָרַךְ "set in order, array" (of battle lines and of a case); מִלִּין "utterances" ("his" is not written); אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּ "I will reply to him" (hifil of שׁוּב, as 31:14); אִמְרֵיכֶם "your sayings".
- **"They were amazed, they answered no more: they left off speaking" → "They are dismayed, they answer no more; utterances have moved away from them"** (v15) — Hebrew חַתּוּ "they are dismayed" (literally "shattered"); הֶעְתִּיקוּ מֵהֶם מִלִּים "utterances have moved away from them" (or, with "they" as subject, "they have removed utterances from themselves"). From here Elihu speaks of the three in the third person.
- **"When I had waited, (for they spake not, but stood still, and answered no more;)" → "And I waited, for they do not speak, for they stand still, they answer no more"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהוֹחַלְתִּי, perfect with וְ; many read it as a question, "And shall I wait, because they do not speak …?"; the Hebrew has no question word. כִּי … כִּי "for … for"; עָמְדוּ "they stand / stood".
- **"I said, I will answer also my part" → "I will answer, I also, my share"** (v17) — "I said" is not written; Hebrew אַעֲנֶה אַף־אֲנִי חֶלְקִי "I will answer, I also, my share"; חֵלֶק "share".
- **"For I am full of matter, the spirit within me constraineth me" → "For I am full of utterances; the spirit of my belly constrains me"** (v18) — Hebrew מִלִּים "utterances"; רוּחַ בִּטְנִי "the spirit (or wind, breath) of my belly"; בֶּטֶן "belly"; הֱצִיקַתְנִי "presses, constrains me".
- **"Behold, my belly is as wine which hath no vent; it is ready to burst like new bottles" → "Behold, my belly is like wine that is not opened; like new wineskins it will be split open"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא־יִפָּתֵחַ "is not opened"; אֹבוֹת "wineskins" (the same word as אוֹב "ghost", in another sense; נֹאד is the usual "skin"); יִבָּקֵעַ "will be split open" (בָּקַע). "Ready" is not written.
- **"I will speak, that I may be refreshed" → "Let me speak, and I shall have relief"** (v20) — Hebrew אֲדַבְּרָה, cohortative "let me speak"; וְיִרְוַח־לִי, literally "and it will be wide for me"; שְׂפָתַי "my lips".
- **"Let me not, I pray you, accept any man's person, neither let me give flattering titles unto man" → "Let me not, please, lift up the face of a man, and to mankind I will not give titles"** (v21) — Hebrew אַל־נָא "please, not"; אֶשָּׂא פְנֵי־אִישׁ "lift up the face of a man" (= show him partiality; kept); אֲכַנֶּה, piel of כָּנָה "give an honorific name" (Isa 44:5; 45:4), KJV "flattering titles"; אִישׁ "man" and אָדָם "mankind" are two words.
- **"For I know not to give flattering titles; in so doing my maker would soon take me away" → "For I do not know how to give titles; in a little my Maker would carry me off"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹא יָדַעְתִּי אֲכַנֶּה, literally "I do not know (that) I would give titles"; כִּמְעַט "in a little" (= soon); יִשָּׂאֵנִי, the verb נָשָׂא of v21 ("lift up"): "would carry me off"; עֹשֵׂנִי "my Maker" (literally "the one who makes me").

### Notes
- **Elihu introduced** — a new voice, not in the prologue. His speeches (32–37) are not mentioned in the closing judgment of 42:7–9.
- **Elihu the Buzite** — Buz (Gen 22:21).
- **His anger burned four times** (v2, 3, 5) — the same phrase each time.
- **Youth speaking** — wisdom is from the spirit and the breath of Shaddai (v8), not from years alone.
- **The wineskin** — Elihu is full and must speak.
- **No titles** — he will show no partiality to the friends or to Job.
