# Job 40

[1] Yahweh said to Job:

[2] "Shall a faultfinder contend with Shaddai?
He who argues with Eloah, let him answer it."

[3] Then Job answered Yahweh and said:

[4] "Behold, I am of small account;
what shall I answer you?
I lay my hand on my mouth.

[5] I have spoken once, and I will not answer;
twice, but I will proceed no further."

[6] Then Yahweh answered Job out of the whirlwind and said:

[7] "Gird up your loins like a man;
I will question you, and you shall declare to me.

[8] Will you even annul my justice?
Will you condemn me that you may be justified?

[9] Have you an arm like *Elohim*,
and can you thunder with a voice like his?

[10] Adorn yourself with majesty and dignity;
clothe yourself with glory and splendor.

[11] Pour out the overflowings of your anger;
look on everyone who is proud and abase him.

[12] Look on everyone who is proud and bring him low;
tread down the wicked where they stand.

[13] Hide them in the dust together;
bind their faces in the hidden place.

[14] Then I will also acknowledge to you
that your own right hand can save you.

[15] Behold Behemoth, which I made along with you;
he eats grass like an ox.

[16] Behold, his strength is in his loins,
his power in the muscles of his belly.

[17] He stiffens his tail like a cedar;
the sinews of his thighs are knit together.

[18] His bones are tubes of bronze,
his limbs like bars of iron.

[19] He is the first of the ways of *Elohim*;
let his Maker draw near with his sword!

[20] The mountains yield him food,
where all the beasts of the field play.

[21] Under the lotus plants he lies,
in the cover of the reeds and marsh.

[22] The lotus trees cover him with shade;
the willows of the brook surround him.

[23] Behold, if the river surges, he is not alarmed;
he is confident though Jordan rushes against his mouth.

[24] Can anyone capture him when he is on watch,
or pierce his nose with a snare?"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"vile" → "of small account"** (v4) — *qalloti*; "I am slight/light."
- **"unicorn" category animals — here new**: ***Behemoth*** (v15) — retained; Hebrew plural-intensive of *behemah*; large beast. Commonly identified as hippopotamus (lotus habitat, river confidence), occasionally as mythological chaos-monster.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v18).
- **"chief of the ways" → "first of the ways"** (v19) — *reshit darkei el*.
- **"he taketh it with his eyes: his nose pierceth through snares" → "Can anyone capture him when he is on watch, or pierce his nose with a snare?"** (v24) — Hebrew rhetorical question rendered as question.

### Notes
- **Yahweh pauses** (v1-2) — demands Job's response.
- **Job's first response** (v4-5) — surrender of argument. But not yet complete submission.
- **Yahweh resumes** — more forceful. Demands Job try governing creation.
- **"Condemn me to justify yourself"** (v8) — core of the case. Job's righteousness cannot stand on divine injustice.
- **Behemoth** (v15-24) — vast herbivore. Lotus-dwelling identifies hippo; "tail like cedar" strains natural identification (some see mythological overtones).
- **"His Maker draw near with sword"** — only Yahweh can handle Behemoth.
- **River confidence** — even Jordan flooding can't move him.
