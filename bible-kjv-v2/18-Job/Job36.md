# Job 36

[1] And Elihu continued and said:

[2] "Wait for me a little, and I will show you,
for there are yet words for ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧.

[3] I will lift up my knowledge from afar,
and to my Maker I will give righteousness.

[4] For truly my words are not falsehood;
one perfect in knowledge is with you.

[5] Behold, ⟦el|El|God⟧ is mighty, and he does not reject;
mighty in strength of heart.

[6] He does not let the wicked live,
and the judgment of the afflicted he gives.

[7] He does not take away his eyes from the righteous;
and kings on the throne —
and he seats them forever, and they are exalted.

[8] And if they are bound in fetters,
they are captured in cords of affliction,

[9] then he tells them their work
and their transgressions, that they make themselves mighty.

[10] And he uncovers their ear to discipline,
and says that they turn back from wickedness.

[11] If they hear and serve,
they finish their days in good,
and their years in pleasantness.

[12] And if they do not hear, they pass over by the missile,
and they expire without knowledge.

[13] And the godless of heart lay up anger;
they do not cry for help when he has bound them.

[14] Their soul dies in youth,
and their life among the male cult prostitutes.

[15] He pulls out the afflicted one by his affliction,
and uncovers their ear by oppression.

[16] And he also lured you out of the mouth of narrowness
into breadth, no constraint beneath it;
and the setting of your table was full of fatness.

[17] And you are full of the cause of the wicked;
cause and judgment hold fast.

[18] For fury — lest it lure you with scoffing;
and let not the greatness of the ransom turn you aside.

[19] Will your cry for help be set in order, not in distress,
and all the exertions of power?

[20] Do not pant for the night,
for peoples to go up in their place.

[21] Take heed: do not turn to wickedness,
for this you have chosen rather than affliction.

[22] Behold, ⟦el|El|God⟧ acts loftily in his power;
who is a teacher like him?

[23] Who has appointed over him his way,
and who has said, 'You have done injustice'?

[24] Remember that you magnify his work,
of which men have sung.

[25] All mankind has beheld it;
mortal man looks from afar.

[26] Behold, ⟦el|El|God⟧ is great, and we do not know;
the number of his years — and there is no searching out.

[27] For he takes away the drops of water;
they refine rain for his stream,

[28] which the skies pour down;
they drip upon mankind abundantly.

[29] Indeed, can one understand the spreadings of the thick cloud,
the crashings of his booth?

[30] Behold, he spreads over him his light,
and the roots of the sea he covers.

[31] For by them he judges peoples;
he gives food in abundance.

[32] Upon the palms he covers light,
and commands it as one who makes it strike.

[33] His noise tells concerning him;
the livestock also, concerning what goes up."

## Changes

- **"God" → "El" / "Eloah"** (v2; v5, 22, 26) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah* (v2) and אֵל *El* (v5, 22, 26), not *Elohim*. "My Maker" (v3) is פֹּעֲלִי, "the one who works me", no divine name.
- **"Elihu also proceeded" → "And Elihu continued"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף, literally "and he added".
- **"Suffer me a little … I have yet to speak on God's behalf" → "Wait for me a little … there are yet words for Eloah"** (v2) — Hebrew כַּתַּר "wait, surround"; זְעֵיר "a little" (both Aramaic-like); לֶאֱלוֹהַּ מִלִּים "for Eloah words": "for" may be "on behalf of" or "about". No verb "speak".
- **"fetch … ascribe righteousness" → "lift up … give righteousness"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶשָּׂא, נָתַן; to "give righteousness" is to declare him righteous. דֵּעַ "knowledge" is a rare form (also v4 plural).
- **"perfect in knowledge"** (v4; 37:16) — Hebrew תְּמִים דֵּעוֹת, plural "knowledges" (37:16 דֵּעִים, said of El); תָּמִים is "whole, blameless", here of knowledge "perfect". Elihu says of himself in v4 what he says of El in 37:16; the words are kept the same.
- **"despiseth not any" → "does not reject"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא יִמְאָס, no object; "any" is supplied in KJV. **"strength and wisdom" → "strength of heart"** — Hebrew כֹּחַ לֵב; "of understanding" or "of purpose" are readings of לֵב.
- **"preserveth not the life" → "does not let live"** (v6) — piel of חָיָה. **"giveth right to the poor" → "the judgment of the afflicted he gives"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפַּט עֲנִיִּים; עָנִי "afflicted".
- **"withdraweth not" → "does not take away"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא־יִגְרַע (as v27). **"with kings are they on the throne"** — Hebrew וְאֶת־מְלָכִים לַכִּסֵּא: אֶת may be "with" (the righteous set with kings) or mark the object (kings, set on the throne). The line is kept without deciding: "and kings on the throne". וַיֹּשִׁיבֵם is a narrative form, "and he seated them", here of what he does.
- **"holden" → "captured"** (v8) — Hebrew יִלָּכְדוּן (לָכַד). חֶבֶל "cord"; עֳנִי "affliction".
- **"that they have exceeded" → "that they make themselves mighty"** (v9) — hitpael of גָּבַר "be strong, prevail". פֹּעַל "work, deed".
- **"openeth also their ear" → "uncovers their ear"** (v10, 15) — Hebrew גָּלָה "uncover". **"instruction" → "discipline"** — Hebrew מוּסָר. **"iniquity" → "wickedness"** (v10, 21) — Hebrew אָוֶן.
- **"spend their days in prosperity" → "finish their days in good"** (v11) — Hebrew יְכַלּוּ "they bring to an end"; בַּטּוֹב "in good". נְעִימִים "pleasant things".
- **"perish by the sword" → "pass over by the missile"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּשֶׁלַח יַעֲבֹרוּ: שֶׁלַח is a thrown weapon (Joel 2:8), not חֶרֶב "sword"; some take it as "the channel" (of death). The same words stand in 33:18. **"die" → "expire"** — Hebrew גָּוַע. כִּבְלִי־דָעַת "as without knowledge".
- **"hypocrites in heart heap up wrath" → "the godless of heart lay up anger"** (v13) — Hebrew חַנְפֵי־לֵב יָשִׂימוּ אָף. **"when he bindeth them" → "when he has bound them"** — perfect אֲסָרָם.
- **"among the unclean" → "among the male cult prostitutes"** (v14) — Hebrew קְדֵשִׁים, "consecrated men" of a pagan cult (Deut 23:17); what they did is inferred from those places. The line says they die young as these do; how is not said.
- **"delivereth the poor in his affliction" → "pulls out the afflicted one by his affliction"** (v15) — Hebrew יְחַלֵּץ (as 2 Sam 22:20); בְּ "by" or "in". **"in oppression"** — Hebrew לַחַץ.
- **"removed thee out of the strait into a broad place" → "lured you out of the mouth of narrowness into breadth"** (v16) — Hebrew הֲסִיתְךָ מִפִּי־צָר רַחַב: הֵסִית elsewhere "incite" (2:3); here and in v18 of drawing a man away, so "lure" in both; "into" is supplied before רַחַב. מוּצָק here is from צוּק "press": "constraint" (as 37:10). **"that which should be set on thy table" → "the setting of your table"** — Hebrew נַחַת "what is laid down". דֶּשֶׁן "fatness". The verse is hard; whether the luring is past, offered or wished is not marked.
- **"thou hast fulfilled the judgment of the wicked: judgment and justice take hold on thee" → "you are full of the cause of the wicked; cause and judgment hold fast"** (v17) — Hebrew דִּין "cause" and מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; יִתְמֹכוּ "they hold fast", no object ("on you" is supplied in KJV).
- **"Because there is wrath, beware lest he take thee away with his stroke" → "For fury — lest it lure you with scoffing"** (v18) — Hebrew כִּי־חֵמָה פֶּן־יְסִיתְךָ בְסָפֶק: "beware" is not written. סֵפֶק is uncertain: "clapping" (scorn, 34:37), or "abundance" (as שֵׂפֶק 20:22). **"a great ransom cannot deliver thee" → "let not the greatness of the ransom turn you aside"** — Hebrew רָב־כֹּפֶר אַל־יַטֶּךָּ.
- **"Will he esteem thy riches?" → "Will your cry for help be set in order"** (v19) — Hebrew הֲיַעֲרֹךְ שׁוּעֲךָ: שׁוּעַ is "cry for help" (as v13 שִׁוַּע) or "riches" (34:19); עָרַךְ "set in order, arrange" (as 37:19). The verse is uncertain. **"forces of strength" → "exertions of power"** — Hebrew מַאֲמַצֵּי־כֹחַ.
- **"Desire not the night, when people are cut off in their place" → "Do not pant for the night, for peoples to go up in their place"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁאַף "pant"; לַעֲלוֹת "to go up" (vanish); "cut off" is a reading of the sense.
- **"Take heed" kept** (v21) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֶר "keep yourself". **"this hast thou chosen rather than affliction"** — Hebrew עַל־זֶה בָּחַרְתָּ מֵעֹנִי; עַל "on account of" or "this".
- **"exalteth by his power" → "acts loftily in his power"** (v22) — hifil יַשְׂגִּיב without an object. מוֹרֶה "teacher".
- **"enjoined him" → "appointed over him"** (v23) — Hebrew פָּקַד עָלָיו. **"Thou hast wrought iniquity" → "You have done injustice"** — Hebrew עַוְלָה.
- **"magnify his work, which men behold" → "magnify his work, of which men have sung"** (v24) — Hebrew שֹׁרְרוּ, from שִׁיר "sing"; some derive it from שׁוּר "watch".
- **"Every man may see it" → "All mankind has beheld it"** (v25) — Hebrew כָּל־אָדָם חָזוּ, perfect. **"man" → "mortal man"** — Hebrew אֱנוֹשׁ; יַבִּיט "looks".
- **"we know him not" → "we do not know"** (v26) — Hebrew לֹא נֵדָע, no object. **"neither can the number of his years be searched out" → "the number of his years — and there is no searching out"** — Hebrew חֵקֶר.
- **"maketh small the drops" → "takes away the drops"** (v27) — Hebrew יְגָרַע (as v7): he draws them off. **"they pour down rain according to the vapour thereof" → "they refine rain for his stream"** — Hebrew יָזֹקּוּ "refine, distil" (of metal); אֵד "stream" (as Gen 2:6; meaning uncertain, also "mist").
- **"the clouds do drop" → "the skies pour down"** (v28) — Hebrew שְׁחָקִים. **"distil upon man abundantly" → "drip upon mankind abundantly"** — Hebrew אָדָם רָב, "much mankind": רָב may belong to "mankind" ("many men") or be "abundantly".
- **"Also can any understand" → "Indeed, can one understand"** (v29) — Hebrew אַף אִם־יָבִין, no subject. **"clouds" → "thick cloud"** — Hebrew עָב (עָנָן is "cloud", 37:11, 15). **"the noise of his tabernacle" → "the crashings of his booth"** — Hebrew תְּשֻׁאוֹת "crashings, roarings"; סֻכָּה "booth" (as 2 Sam 22:12).
- **"his light"** (v30, 32; 37:3, 11, 15) — Hebrew אוֹר "light" every time; lightning is meant (בָּרָק "lightning" stands in 38:35). **"Behold, he spreadeth his light upon it" → "spreads over him his light"** (v30) — עָלָיו "over him" or "over it". **"the bottom of the sea" → "the roots of the sea"** — Hebrew שָׁרְשֵׁי הַיָּם.
- **"in abundance"** (v31) — Hebrew לְמַכְבִּיר. אֹכֶל "food".
- **"With clouds he covereth the light" → "Upon the palms he covers light"** (v32) — Hebrew עַל־כַּפַּיִם "upon (the) palms": no "clouds", no "his". **"commandeth it not to shine by the cloud that cometh betwixt" → "commands it as one who makes it strike"** — Hebrew וַיְצַו עָלֶיהָ בְמַפְגִּיעַ: מַפְגִּיעַ is the hifil participle of פָּגַע "meet, strike" (Isa 53:12 "intercede"); "against the mark" is another reading. Uncertain.
- **"The noise thereof sheweth concerning it, the cattle also concerning the vapour" → "His noise tells concerning him; the livestock also, concerning what goes up"** (v33) — Hebrew רֵעוֹ "his noise" (from רוּעַ "shout"; the same letters are "his friend"); מִקְנֶה "livestock"; עוֹלֶה "what goes up" (the storm rising). The verse is obscure; a common emendation reads "anger against iniquity", not followed.

### Notes
- **Elihu's fourth speech** (36–37) — suffering as discipline (v8–15); then El's greatness in the rain and the storm (36:26–37:24).
- **Weather words** (36–37; 38) — one rendering each: סְעָרָה "storm", סוּפָה "tempest", בָּרָק "lightning", אוֹר "light" (also where lightning is meant), קֶרַח "ice", שֶׁלֶג "snow", עָב "thick cloud", עָנָן "cloud", שְׁחָקִים "skies".
- **Obscurity** — chapters 36–37 are among the hardest in Job (v16–20, 32–33); where the Hebrew is uncertain the verse keeps its words and Changes gives the readings.
