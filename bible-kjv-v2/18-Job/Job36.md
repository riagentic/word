# Job 36

[1] Elihu continued and said:

[2] "Bear with me a little, and I will show you,
for I have yet more to say for Eloah.

[3] I will bring my knowledge from afar,
and ascribe righteousness to my Maker.

[4] For truly my words are not false;
one perfect in knowledge is with you.

[5] Behold, *Elohim* is mighty, and despises no one;
he is mighty in strength of understanding.

[6] He does not let the wicked live,
but gives the afflicted their due.

[7] He does not withdraw his eyes from the righteous;
with kings upon the throne
he sets them forever, and they are exalted.

[8] If they are bound in fetters
and caught in cords of affliction,

[9] then he declares to them their deeds
and their transgressions, that they have behaved arrogantly.

[10] He opens their ear to instruction
and commands that they return from iniquity.

[11] If they listen and serve,
they spend their days in prosperity
and their years in pleasantness.

[12] But if they do not listen, they pass away by the sword;
they die without knowledge.

[13] The godless in heart cherish anger;
they do not cry for help when he binds them.

[14] Their soul dies in youth,
their life among the male temple-prostitutes.

[15] He delivers the afflicted by their affliction
and opens their ear by adversity.

[16] He also allured you out of distress
into a broad place where there was no constraint;
what was set on your table was full of richness.

[17] But you are full of the judgment on the wicked;
judgment and justice seize you.

[18] Beware that wrath does not entice you into scoffing;
let not the greatness of the ransom turn you aside.

[19] Will your cry of distress avail —
no, nor all your strenuous power?

[20] Do not long for the night
when peoples are cut off in their place.

[21] Take care; do not turn to iniquity,
for this you have chosen rather than affliction.

[22] Behold, *Elohim* is exalted in his power;
who is a teacher like him?

[23] Who has appointed him his way,
or who can say, 'You have done wrong'?

[24] Remember to extol his work,
of which men have sung.

[25] All mankind has looked on it;
man gazes from afar.

[26] Behold, *Elohim* is great, and we do not know him;
the number of his years is unsearchable.

[27] For he draws up the drops of water;
they distill rain to the mist,

[28] which the skies pour down
and drop upon mankind abundantly.

[29] Can anyone understand the spreading of the clouds,
the thunderings of his pavilion?

[30] Behold, he scatters his lightning about him,
and covers the depths of the sea.

[31] For by these he judges peoples;
he gives food in abundance.

[32] He covers his hands with the lightning
and commands it to strike the mark.

[33] Its crashing declares concerning him —
even the cattle concerning what is coming up."

## Changes

- **"hypocrites" → "godless"** (v13) — *chanef*.
- **"unclean" → "male temple-prostitutes"** (v14) — *qedeshim*.
- **"tabernacle" → "pavilion"** (v29) — *sukkato*; divine tent.

### Notes
- **Elihu's theology developed** — suffering as discipline. Even kings learn via fetters.
- **"Allured you out of distress"** (v16) — Elihu claims Yahweh had offered Job escape, which he declined.
- **Lightning imagery** at end — transition to creation-theology as Yahweh speech approaches.
- **Obscurity** — chapters 36-37 are textually difficult; translations vary.
