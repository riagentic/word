# Job 34

[1] Elihu answered and said:

[2] "Hear my words, you wise men;
give ear to me, you who have knowledge.

[3] For the ear tests words,
as the palate tastes food.

[4] Let us choose for ourselves what is right;
let us know among ourselves what is good.

[5] For ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ has said, 'I am righteous,
and ⟦el|El|God⟧ has taken away my right.

[6] Though I am right, I am held as a liar;
my wound is incurable, though without transgression.'

[7] What man is like ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧,
who drinks up scorn like water,

[8] travels in company with evildoers
and walks with wicked men?

[9] For he has said, 'A man gains nothing
when he is pleased with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'

[10] Therefore hear me, you men of understanding:
far be it from ⟦el|El|God⟧ to do wickedness,
from ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ to do wrong.

[11] For the work of a man he repays to him,
and he makes every man reap according to his way.

[12] Surely ⟦el|El|God⟧ will not act wickedly,
and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ will not pervert justice.

[13] Who gave him charge over the earth?
Who laid on him the whole world?

[14] If he should set his heart against one,
gather to himself his spirit and his breath,

[15] all flesh would perish together,
and man would return to dust.

[16] If you have understanding, hear this;
listen to the voice of my words.

[17] Can one who hates justice govern?
Can you condemn the righteous Mighty One?

[18] — who says to a king, '⟦belial|Beliyyaal|Worthless one⟧,'
and to nobles, 'Wicked one';

[19] who shows no partiality to princes,
nor regards the rich more than the poor,
for they are all the work of his hands.

[20] In a moment they die — at midnight
the people are shaken and pass away;
the mighty are taken away by no human hand.

[21] For his eyes are on the ways of a man,
and he sees all his steps.

[22] There is no darkness, no deep gloom,
where evildoers may hide.

[23] For he has not appointed a time for any man
to come before ⟦el|El|God⟧ in judgment.

[24] He shatters the mighty without investigation,
and sets others in their place.

[25] Because he knows their works,
he overturns them in the night,
and they are crushed.

[26] He strikes them as wicked men
in the open sight of others,

[27] because they turned aside from following him,
and had no regard for any of his ways,

[28] so that they caused the cry of the poor to come to him,
and he heard the cry of the afflicted.

[29] When he is quiet, who then can condemn?
When he hides his face, who can behold him?
Yet it is over both nation and man alike —

[30] that the godless should not reign,
that he should not ensnare the people.

[31] Has anyone said to ⟦el|El|God⟧,
'I have borne chastisement; I will not offend any more.

[32] What I do not see, teach me;
if I have done iniquity, I will do no more'?

[33] Will he then recompense as you see fit
because you reject his way?
You must choose, and not I;
so declare what you know.

[34] Men of understanding will tell me,
and the wise man who hears me will say,

[35] '⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ speaks without knowledge;
his words are without insight.'

[36] Oh that ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ were tested to the end,
because he answers like wicked men.

[37] For he adds rebellion to his sin;
he claps his hands among us
and multiplies his words against ⟦el|El|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v5, 10, 12, 23, 31, 37) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"hypocrite" → "godless"** (v30) — *chanef*.
- **"shadow of death" → "deep gloom"** (v22) — *tzalmaweth*.

### Notes
- **Elihu's second speech** — defends divine justice.
- **Indicts Job** for claiming Yahweh has taken away his right (v5).
- **Divine impartiality** (v18-19) — no kings or nobles privileged; all are work of his hands.
- **"Without investigation"** (v24) — divine judgment doesn't require legal process; Yahweh sees directly.
- **Sees all, rescues afflicted** — poor's cry heard.
- **Repentance formula** (v31-32) — the proper prayer Job should offer. Elihu prescribes.
- **Harsh verdict on Job** (v35-37) — ignorant rebel.
