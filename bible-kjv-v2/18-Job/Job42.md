# Job 42

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said:

[2] "I know that you can do all things,
and no purpose is withheld from you.

[3] Who is this who hides counsel without knowledge?
Therefore I have told, and I did not understand,
things too wonderful for me, and I did not know.

[4] Hear, please, and I will speak;
I will ask you, and you make me know.

[5] By the hearing of the ear I had heard of you,
and now my eye has seen you.

[6] Therefore I reject, and I am sorry,
upon dust and ashes."

[7] And it came to pass, after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken these words to ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦eliphaz|Elifaz|Eliphaz⟧ the ⟦temanite|Temani|Temanite⟧, "My anger has burned against you and against your two friends, for you have not spoken to me what is right, as my servant ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ has.

[8] And now take for yourselves seven bulls and seven rams, and go to my servant ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧, and offer up a burnt offering on your behalf; and ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ my servant shall pray for you, for surely his face I will lift up, so as not to do with you an outrage; for you have not spoken to me what is right, as my servant ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ has."

[9] And ⟦eliphaz|Elifaz|Eliphaz⟧ the ⟦temanite|Temani|Temanite⟧ and Bildad the ⟦shuhite|Shuchi|Shuhite⟧, ⟦zophar|Tzofar|Zophar⟧ the ⟦naamathite|Naamati|Naamathite⟧, went and did as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken to them; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lifted up the face of ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧.

[10] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ restored the fortunes of ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ when he prayed for his friends; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ added to all that ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ had, to double.

[11] And there came to him all his brothers and all his sisters and all who had known him before, and they ate bread with him in his house; and they showed him grief and comforted him over all the evil that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had brought upon him; and they gave him each one ⟦qesitah|qesitah|piece of money⟧, and each one ring of gold.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ blessed the latter end of ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ more than his beginning; and he had fourteen thousand of the flock and six thousand camels and a thousand yoke of the herd and a thousand she-donkeys.

[13] And he had seven sons and three daughters.

[14] And he called the name of the first ⟦jemimah|Yemimah|Jemimah⟧, and the name of the second ⟦keziah|Qetziah|Keziah⟧, and the name of the third ⟦keren-happuch|Keren HaPuch|Keren-happuch⟧.

[15] And no women were found so beautiful as the daughters of ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ in all the land; and their father gave them an inheritance in the midst of their brothers.

[16] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ lived after this a hundred and forty years, and saw his sons and his sons' sons, four generations.

[17] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ died, old and full of days.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Then Job answered the LORD, and said" → "And Job answered Yahweh and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the book.
- **"I know that thou canst do every thing, and that no thought can be withholden from thee" → "I know that you can do all things, and no purpose is withheld from you"** (v2) — the verse follows the qere יָדַעְתִּי "I know"; the written form (ketiv) ידעת reads "you know". לֹא־יִבָּצֵר מִמְּךָ מְזִמָּה "no purpose is withheld from you" (as Gen 11:6); "that" is not repeated.
- **"Who is he that hideth counsel without knowledge? therefore have I uttered that I understood not" → "Who is this who hides counsel without knowledge? Therefore I have told, and I did not understand"** (v3) — Job takes up Yahweh's question of 38:2 (there מַחְשִׁיךְ "darkens", here מַעְלִים "hides"); the Hebrew has no quotation marks, and none are added. הִגַּדְתִּי "I have told"; וְלֹא אָבִין, וְלֹא אֵדָע "and I did not understand", "and I did not know" (each with "and"); נִפְלָאוֹת מִמֶּנִּי "things too wonderful for me".
- **"Hear, I beseech thee, and I will speak: I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me" → "Hear, please, and I will speak; I will ask you, and you make me know"** (v4) — the words of 38:3; 40:7, now in Job's mouth (no quotation marks in the Hebrew); נָא "please"; וְהוֹדִיעֵנִי an imperative.
- **"I have heard of thee by the hearing of the ear: but now mine eye seeth thee" → "By the hearing of the ear I had heard of you, and now my eye has seen you"** (v5) — Hebrew order kept; שְׁמַעְתִּיךָ "I heard you" ("of" for the suffix); רָאָתְךָ a perfect, "has seen"; וְעַתָּה "and now".
- **"Wherefore I abhor myself, and repent in dust and ashes" → "Therefore I reject, and I am sorry, upon dust and ashes"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־כֵּן אֶמְאַס וְנִחַמְתִּי עַל־עָפָר וָאֵפֶר, word for word. אֶמְאַס "I reject" (מָאַס, as Lev 26): the object is not written and nothing is supplied. It is read (1) "I reject (my words / what I said)", (2) "I despise (myself)" (so KJV "abhor myself"), (3) "I melt away", taking the verb as a form of מָסַס (as 7:5 is read by some; from memory). נִחַמְתִּי nifal of נִחַם: "be sorry" (as Gen 6:6; Exod 32:14: regret, a change of mind); the same stem with עַל is also "be comforted over" (Gen 24:67; 38:12; so v11 piel "comforted him over"): "I am comforted concerning dust and ashes" is the other reading. עַל "upon" (Job sits in the ashes, 2:8) or "concerning" (dust and ashes = the mortal state, Gen 18:27 "I am dust and ashes"). "Repent" adds the sense of turning from sin, which the word does not carry by itself.
- **"And it was so, that after" → "And it came to pass, after"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיְהִי. **"My wrath is kindled against thee" → "My anger has burned against you"** — Hebrew חָרָה אַפִּי, literally "my nose has burned". **"for ye have not spoken of me the thing that is right" → "for you have not spoken to me what is right"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew אֵלַי "to me" (also read "about me", as KJV); נְכוֹנָה "what is right, established" (נָכוֹן). **"as my servant Job hath"** — עַבְדִּי "my servant" (עֶבֶד "slave"; of Yahweh's man "servant").
- **"Therefore take unto you now seven bullocks" → "And now take for yourselves seven bulls"** (v8) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה "and now"; קְחוּ־לָכֶם "take for yourselves"; פָּרִים "bulls". **"offer up for yourselves a burnt offering" → "offer up a burnt offering on your behalf"** — Hebrew בַּעַדְכֶם. **"my servant Job" → "Job my servant"** — Hebrew order (the verse has both orders). **"for him will I accept" → "for surely his face I will lift up"** — Hebrew כִּי אִם־פָּנָיו אֶשָּׂא, literally "for surely his face I will lift up" (= show him regard; also read "for only his face"); the same words in v9 ("lifted up the face of Job"). **"lest I deal with you after your folly" → "so as not to do with you an outrage"** — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי עֲשׂוֹת עִמָּכֶם נְבָלָה: נְבָלָה is the deed of the נָבָל, the base fool ("outrage" as Gen 34:7); "your" is not written: the outrage is what Yahweh would do to them, or the folly they would be treated as.
- **"So Eliphaz … and Zophar" → "And Eliphaz … and Bildad the Shuhite, Zophar the Naamathite"** (v9) — the Hebrew has no "and" before Zophar. **"according as the LORD commanded them" → "as Yahweh had spoken to them"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר "spoke". **"the LORD also accepted Job" → "and Yahweh lifted up the face of Job"** — Hebrew וַיִּשָּׂא יְהוָה אֶת־פְּנֵי אִיּוֹב (as v8).
- **"And the LORD turned the captivity of Job" → "And Yahweh restored the fortunes of Job"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׁב אֶת־שְׁבוּת אִיּוֹב (qere; ketiv שבית), literally "turned back the shevut of Job". שְׁבוּת is uncertain: from שָׁבָה "take captive" ("captivity", as Deut 30:3, where the scattering supports it) or from שׁוּב "turn" ("turn the turning" = restore the fortunes). Job was never taken captive: the verse gives the second sense; the literal "turned back the captivity" stands here. **"when he prayed for his friends"** — Hebrew רֵעֵהוּ, a singular form, "his friend" (said of each of them, as 1 Sam 30:26). **"also the LORD gave Job twice as much as he had before" → "and Yahweh added to all that Job had, to double"** — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף יְהוָה אֶת־כָּל־אֲשֶׁר לְאִיּוֹב לְמִשְׁנֶה, literally "and Yahweh added all that was Job's to a double".
- **"all they that had been of his acquaintance before" → "all who had known him before"** (v11) — Hebrew יֹדְעָיו לְפָנִים. אַחְיוֹתָיו "his sisters" (qere; the ketiv is spelled otherwise). **"they bemoaned him" → "they showed him grief"** — Hebrew וַיָּנֻדוּ לוֹ, literally "they swayed for him" (the shaking of the head in grief; as 2:11). **"every man also gave him a piece of money, and every one an earring of gold" → "and they gave him each one qesitah, and each one ring of gold"** — Hebrew אִישׁ קְשִׂיטָה אֶחָת וְאִישׁ נֶזֶם זָהָב אֶחָד: אִישׁ "each" (literally "a man", of the brothers and sisters alike); קְשִׂיטָה *qesitah*, a weight or piece of silver of unknown value (Gen 33:19; Josh 24:32), the word kept; נֶזֶם "ring" (of the nose or the ear: the word says only "ring"); אֶחָד "one" both times.
- **"So the LORD blessed the latter end of Job" → "And Yahweh blessed the latter end of Job"** (v12) — Hebrew אַחֲרִית "latter end", רֵאשִׁית "beginning". **"fourteen thousand sheep … a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand she asses" → "fourteen thousand of the flock … a thousand yoke of the herd and a thousand she-donkeys"** — Hebrew צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats), צֶמֶד בָּקָר "yoke of the herd" (cattle), אֲתוֹנוֹת "she-donkeys": as 1:3, each number doubled. Each "and" of the list kept.
- **"seven sons"** (v13) — Hebrew שִׁבְעָנָה, an unusual form of "seven", only here; some take it as "twice seven" (from memory). The number of sons and daughters is the number of 1:2.
- **"Jemima / Kezia / Kerenhappuch"** (v14) — the names are taken to mean "dove" (uncertain), "cassia" (a spice), "horn of antimony" (eye paint). **"And he called the name of the first"** — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא שֵׁם "called the name of".
- **"And in all the land were no women found so fair as the daughters of Job" → "And no women were found so beautiful as the daughters of Job in all the land"** (v15) — Hebrew order of the phrase "in all the land" kept at the end; יָפוֹת "beautiful"; נִמְצָא a singular verb with "women". **"among their brethren" → "in the midst of their brothers"** — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ אֲחֵיהֶם; נַחֲלָה "inheritance".
- **"saw his sons, and his sons' sons, even four generations"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיַּרְא (qere; the ketiv is spelled otherwise); "a hundred and forty years"; בָּנָיו "his sons".
- **"So Job died, being old and full of days" → "And Job died, old and full of days"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיָּמָת; זָקֵן וּשְׂבַע יָמִים "old and full (sated) of days", as Gen 35:29.

### Notes
- **Job's answer** (v1–6) — the end of the poetry: he takes up Yahweh's words (38:2, 3; 40:7) and speaks of what he heard and what he has now seen.
- **The prose frame** (v7–17) — the narrative of 1–2 resumes; Yahweh says twice that the friends have not spoken what is right, as Job has.
- **Job prays for the friends** (v8–10) — the friends' burnt offering and Job's prayer; Yahweh "lifts up the face" of Job, and the turn of his fortunes comes "when he prayed".
- **The latter end** (v12–17) — the flocks and herds of 1:3 doubled; seven sons and three daughters as before; the daughters are named and receive an inheritance with their brothers.
