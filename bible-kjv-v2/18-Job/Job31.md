# Job 31

[1] "I made a covenant with my eyes —
how then could I gaze at a virgin?

[2] What is the portion of Eloah above,
the heritage of Shaddai from on high?

[3] Is not calamity for the unrighteous,
disaster for the workers of iniquity?

[4] Does he not see my ways
and count all my steps?

[5] If I have walked with falsehood,
if my foot has hastened to deceit —

[6] let him weigh me in an honest balance,
and let Eloah know my integrity.

[7] If my step has turned aside from the way,
and my heart has gone after my eyes,
if any spot has cleaved to my hands,

[8] then let me sow, and another eat;
let what grows for me be uprooted.

[9] If my heart has been enticed toward a woman,
and I have lurked at my neighbor's door,

[10] then let my wife grind for another,
and let others kneel over her.

[11] For that would be a heinous crime,
an iniquity to be punished by the judges;

[12] for it is a fire that consumes to Abaddon,
and would uproot all my increase.

[13] If I have rejected the cause of my manservant
or my maidservant when they brought a complaint against me,

[14] what then shall I do when *Elohim* rises up?
When he makes inquiry, what shall I answer him?

[15] Did not he who made me in the womb make him?
Did not one fashion us in the womb?

[16] If I have withheld from the poor what they desired,
or caused the eyes of the widow to grow dim,

[17] or eaten my morsel alone,
while the fatherless did not share it —

[18] (for from my youth the orphan grew up with me as a father,
and from my mother's womb I guided the widow) —

[19] if I have seen anyone perish for lack of clothing,
or a poor man without covering,

[20] if his loins have not blessed me,
and if he was not warmed with the fleece of my sheep;

[21] if I have raised my hand against the orphan,
when I saw my support in the gate —

[22] let my shoulder fall from its socket,
and my arm be broken from its bone.

[23] For calamity from *Elohim* was a terror to me;
because of his majesty I could do nothing.

[24] If I have made gold my confidence,
or said to fine gold, 'My security,'

[25] if I have rejoiced because my wealth was great,
or because my hand had found much;

[26] if I have looked at the sun when it shone,
or the moon moving in splendor,

[27] and my heart was secretly enticed,
and my mouth kissed my hand —

[28] this also would be an iniquity for the judges,
for I would have denied *Elohim* above.

[29] If I rejoiced at the ruin of him who hated me,
or exulted when evil overtook him —

[30] I have not let my mouth sin
by asking for his life with a curse —

[31] if the men of my tent have not said,
'Who can find one not filled with his meat?'

[32] the sojourner did not spend the night in the street;
I opened my doors to the traveler.

[33] If I have concealed my transgressions as man does,
hiding my iniquity in my bosom,

[34] because I feared the great multitude,
and the contempt of families terrified me,
so that I kept silent and did not go out of my door —

[35] oh that I had one to hear me!
Here is my signature — let Shaddai answer me,
and let my adversary write an indictment.

[36] Surely I would carry it on my shoulder;
I would bind it on me like a crown.

[37] I would give him an account of my steps;
like a prince I would approach him.

[38] If my land has cried out against me,
and its furrows have wept together,

[39] if I have eaten its yield without payment,
and made its owners breathe their last —

[40] let thorns grow instead of wheat,
and noxious weeds instead of barley."

The words of Job are ended.

## Changes

- **"kissed my hand" — retained** (v27) — Hebrew *tishshaq yadi le-fi*; ritual gesture of sun/moon worship.
- **"oh that one would hear me!" → "oh that I had one to hear me! Here is my signature"** (v35) — Hebrew *hen tavi*; "behold my *tav*" = mark/signature (final letter of Hebrew alphabet). Legal: Job "signs" his affidavit.
- **"cockle" → "noxious weeds"** (v40) — *bo'shah*; stinkweed.

### Notes
- **Oath of innocence** — elaborate self-malediction across multiple ethical domains.
- **Each "if" followed by a curse** — covenant self-curse form.
- **Sexual ethics** (v1-12) — "covenant with eyes"; sexual purity.
- **Servant justice** (v13-15) — radical claim: slaves have legal case against master.
- **Social justice** (v16-23) — widow, orphan, poor, naked.
- **Idolatry** (v24-28) — gold-worship and astral cults both denied.
- **Loving enemies** (v29-30) — refuses to rejoice at enemies' fall or curse them.
- **Hospitality** (v31-32) — traveler always welcomed.
- **Hidden sin** (v33-34) — no Adam-like concealment.
- **Land crimes** (v38-40) — agricultural exploitation denied.
- **"My words are ended"** — formal close of Job's speeches. Elihu begins next.
- **Challenge to El** (v35) — "write your indictment." Job demands legal contest. Pivotal moment before Yahweh's eventual answer.
