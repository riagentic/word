# Job 31

[1] "A covenant I cut for my eyes;
and how should I gaze at a virgin?

[2] And what is the share of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ from above,
and the inheritance of ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ from the heights?

[3] Is it not calamity for the unjust one,
and misfortune for the workers of wickedness?

[4] Does he not see my ways,
and number all my steps?

[5] If I have walked with emptiness,
and my foot has hastened to deceit,

[6] let him weigh me in balances of righteousness,
and let ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ know my integrity.

[7] If my step turns from the way,
and after my eyes my heart has walked,
and to my palms a blemish has clung,

[8] let me sow, and let another eat,
and let my offspring be uprooted.

[9] If my heart has been enticed toward a woman,
and at the entrance of my neighbor I have lain in ambush,

[10] let my wife grind for another,
and over her let others bow down.

[11] For that is depravity,
and that is an iniquity for arbiters;

[12] for it is a fire that devours to ⟦abaddon|Avaddon|Abaddon⟧,
and among all my yield it would uproot.

[13] If I rejected the judgment of my slave and of my slave woman,
in their contention with me,

[14] and what shall I do when ⟦el|El|God⟧ rises up?
And when he visits, what shall I reply to him?

[15] Did not he who made me in the belly make him?
And did not one establish us in the womb?

[16] If I have held back from the desire of the poor,
and the eyes of the widow I have worn out,

[17] and I have eaten my morsel alone,
and the fatherless has not eaten of it —

[18] for from my youth he grew up with me as with a father,
and from my mother's belly I led her —

[19] if I have seen one perishing without clothing,
and no covering for the needy,

[20] if his loins have not blessed me,
and from the fleece of my lambs he has not warmed himself,

[21] if I have waved my hand against the fatherless,
because I saw my help in the gate,

[22] let my shoulder fall from its shoulder blade,
and my arm be broken from its shaft.

[23] For a terror to me is the calamity of ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and because of his majesty I could not.

[24] If I have set gold as my confidence,
and to gold I have said, 'My trust,'

[25] if I rejoiced because my wealth was great,
and because my hand had found much,

[26] if I saw the light when it shone,
and the moon walking in splendor,

[27] and my heart was enticed in secret,
and my hand kissed my mouth,

[28] that too is an iniquity for arbiters,
for I would have lied to ⟦el|El|God⟧ above.

[29] I have not rejoiced at the disaster of him who hates me,
nor roused myself when evil found him;

[30] and I have not given my palate to sin,
to ask for his soul with a curse.

[31] Surely the men of my tent have said,
'Would that there were one who has not been sated from his flesh!'

[32] In the street the sojourner did not lodge;
my doors I opened to the road.

[33] I have not covered my transgressions like Adam,
hiding my iniquity in my bosom,

[34] because I dreaded the great multitude,
and the contempt of clans terrified me,
and I kept still, I did not go out of the entrance.

[35] Would that I had one who hears me!
Behold my mark — let ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ answer me —
and the document the man of my case has written!

[36] Surely on my shoulder I would carry it;
I would bind it on me as crowns.

[37] The number of my steps I would tell him;
like a leader I would approach him.

[38] If my ground cries out against me,
and its furrows weep together,

[39] if I have eaten its power without money,
and the soul of its owners I have made to breathe out,

[40] instead of wheat let the thistle come out,
and instead of barley stinkweed."

The words of ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ have come to an end.

## Changes

- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v2, 6) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ; **"God" → "El"** (v14, 23, 28) — Hebrew אֵל; **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v2, 35) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי (its meaning is not certain). The chapter has no *Elohim*.
- **The oath form** (v5–40) — Job swears by "if": "If I have done this, let that happen to me". Where a curse or a consequence follows (v5–6, 7–8, 9–10, 13–14, 16–22, 24–28, 38–40), the "if" is kept. Where none follows, the oath means "I have not" / "surely I have", and the verse gives that meaning: v29 (אִם־אֶשְׂמַח, literally "if I rejoiced"), v31 (אִם־לֹא אָמְרוּ, literally "if the men of my tent have not said"), v33 (אִם־כִּסִּיתִי, literally "if I have covered"), v36 (אִם־לֹא, literally "if I do not carry it").
- **"I made a covenant with mine eyes; why then should I think upon a maid?" → "A covenant I cut for my eyes; and how should I gaze at a virgin?"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּרִית כָּרַתִּי לְעֵינָי, "cut a covenant" for כָּרַת, "for my eyes"; וּמָה "and how / and what"; אֶתְבּוֹנֵן עַל "gaze at" (the verb of 30:20); בְּתוּלָה "virgin".
- **"For what portion of God is there from above? and what inheritance of the Almighty from on high?" → "And what is the share of Eloah from above, and the inheritance of Shaddai from the heights?"** (v2) — Hebrew וּמֶה "and what"; חֵלֶק "share" (what Eloah allots); מִמָּעַל "from above"; מְרוֹמִים "the heights".
- **"Is not destruction to the wicked? and a strange punishment to the workers of iniquity?" → "Is it not calamity for the unjust one, and misfortune for the workers of wickedness?"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵיד "calamity"; עַוָּל "unjust one" (not רָשָׁע "wicked"); נֵכֶר, found only here and in Obad 12, "misfortune" (from "strange"; uncertain); פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness".
- **"count all my steps" → "number all my steps"** (v4) — Hebrew יִסְפּוֹר, סָפַר "number".
- **"If I have walked with vanity" → "with emptiness"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׁוְא "emptiness". מִרְמָה "deceit".
- **"Let me be weighed in an even balance, that God may know mine integrity" → "let him weigh me in balances of righteousness, and let Eloah know my integrity"** (v6) — Hebrew יִשְׁקְלֵנִי "let him weigh me" (active); מֹאזְנֵי־צֶדֶק "balances of righteousness"; וְיֵדַע "and let him know" (not "that"); תֻּמָּה "integrity" (as 2:3, 9; 27:5).
- **"If my step hath turned out of the way … if any blot hath cleaved to mine hands" → "If my step turns from the way … and to my palms a blemish has clung"** (v7) — Hebrew תִּטֶּה, imperfect of נָטָה "turn"; מְאוּם "blemish" (= מוּם); כַּפַּי "my palms".
- **"Then let me sow" → "let me sow"** (v8) — "then" is not written. **"let my offspring be rooted out" → "let my offspring be uprooted"** — Hebrew צֶאֱצָאַי "what comes out of me": "offspring" (5:25; 21:8; 27:14); beside "sow" it may also mean what comes out of the ground, the crops.
- **"If mine heart have been deceived by a woman, or if I have laid wait at my neighbour's door" → "If my heart has been enticed toward a woman, and at the entrance of my neighbor I have lain in ambush"** (v9) — Hebrew נִפְתָּה "be enticed" (the verb of v27); עַל "toward, over"; פֶּתַח "entrance"; אָרַבְתִּי "I lay in ambush". "or if" is "and".
- **"let others bow down upon her" → "over her let others bow down"** (v10) — Hebrew כָּרַע "bow"; אֲחֵרִין, with the Aramaic plural ending.
- **"For this is an heinous crime; yea, it is an iniquity to be punished by the judges" → "For that is depravity, and that is an iniquity for arbiters"** (v11) — qere הִיא … וְהוּא; the ketiv has the two pronouns the other way round (הוא … והיא); the meaning is the same. זִמָּה "depravity"; עָוֺן פְּלִילִים "an iniquity of arbiters" (= for arbiters to judge; פְּלִילִים is uncertain: "arbiters" or "assessment"; not שֹׁפְטִים "judges").
- **"consumeth to destruction" → "devours to Avaddon"** (v12) — Hebrew תֹּאכֵל "devours"; אֲבַדּוֹן "Avaddon" (place of destruction, as 26:6; 28:22). **"and would root out all mine increase" → "and among all my yield it would uproot"** — Hebrew וּבְכָל־תְּבוּאָתִי תְשָׁרֵשׁ, with בְּ "in, among".
- **"If I did despise the cause of my manservant or of my maidservant, when they contended with me" → "If I rejected the judgment of my slave and of my slave woman, in their contention with me"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶמְאַס "reject" (imperfect); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (= their case); עֶבֶד "slave", וַאֲמָתִי "and my slave woman" (KJV "or"); בְּרִבָם עִמָּדִי, the noun רִיב "contention".
- **"What then shall I do when God riseth up? and when he visiteth, what shall I answer him?" → "and what shall I do when El rises up? And when he visits, what shall I reply to him?"** (v14) — Hebrew וּמָה "and what" ("then" is not written); פָּקַד "visit"; אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּ, hifil of שׁוּב, "return to him" (a word): "reply".
- **"Did not he that made me in the womb make him? and did not one fashion us in the womb?" → "Did not he who made me in the belly make him? And did not one establish us in the womb?"** (v15) — Hebrew בַבֶּטֶן "in the belly"; רֶחֶם "womb" in the second line; וַיְכֻנֶנּוּ, polel of כּוּן "establish", "us" or "him" (the form allows both); אֶחָד "one".
- **"If I have withheld the poor from their desire, or have caused the eyes of the widow to fail" → "If I have held back from the desire of the poor, and the eyes of the widow I have worn out"** (v16) — Hebrew אֶמְנַע מֵחֵפֶץ דַּלִּים, literally "hold back from the desire of the poor" (= keep back what they desire; the poor are not the object); דַּלִּים "the poor"; חֵפֶץ "desire"; אֲכַלֶּה, piel of כָּלָה, of eyes "wear out".
- **"Or have eaten my morsel myself alone, and the fatherless hath not eaten thereof" → "and I have eaten my morsel alone, and the fatherless has not eaten of it"** (v17) — "or" is "and"; יָתוֹם "fatherless".
- **"For from my youth he was brought up with me, as with a father, and I have guided her from my mother's womb" → "for from my youth he grew up with me as with a father, and from my mother's belly I led her"** (v18) — Hebrew גְּדֵלַנִי כְאָב, literally "he grew up me like a father": the fatherless grew up with Job as with a father (or "he raised me": the form is hard; uncertain); בֶּטֶן "belly"; אַנְחֶנָּה "I led her" (the widow, v16).
- **"If I have seen any perish for want of clothing, or any poor without covering" → "if I have seen one perishing without clothing, and no covering for the needy"** (v19) — Hebrew אוֹבֵד "perishing"; מִבְּלִי "without"; וְאֵין כְּסוּת לָאֶבְיוֹן "and there is no covering for the needy".
- **"If his loins have not blessed me, and if he were not warmed with the fleece of my sheep" → "if his loins have not blessed me, and from the fleece of my lambs he has not warmed himself"** (v20) — qere חֲלָצָיו "his loins" (the ketiv חלצו is the same word written short). The "not" of the first line governs the second (the Hebrew has it once). גֵּז "fleece"; כְּבָשַׂי "my lambs"; יִתְחַמָּם "warmed himself".
- **"If I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless, when I saw my help in the gate" → "if I have waved my hand against the fatherless, because I saw my help in the gate"** (v21) — Hebrew הֲנִיפוֹתִי, hifil of נוּף "wave"; כִּי "because"; עֶזְרָתִי "my help".
- **"Then let mine arm fall from my shoulder blade, and mine arm be broken from the bone" → "let my shoulder fall from its shoulder blade, and my arm be broken from its shaft"** (v22) — Hebrew כְּתֵפִי "my shoulder"; מִשִּׁכְמָה, שְׁכֶם "shoulder, upper back" (English has one word for the two; "shoulder blade" for the second); אֶזְרֹעִי "my arm"; מִקָּנָה, קָנֶה "reed, shaft": the bone of the upper arm.
- **"For destruction from God was a terror to me, and by reason of his highness I could not endure" → "For a terror to me is the calamity of El, and because of his majesty I could not"** (v23) — Hebrew כִּי פַחַד אֵלַי אֵיד אֵל, no verb ("is" supplied); אֵיד אֵל "the calamity of El" (a construct: the calamity El sends; "from" is not written); אֵיד "calamity" (v3); שְׂאֵת "majesty" (as 13:11); לֹא אוּכָל "I could not" ("endure" is not written).
- **"If I have made gold my hope, or have said to the fine gold, Thou art my confidence" → "If I have set gold as my confidence, and to gold I have said, 'My trust'"** (v24) — Hebrew כֶּסֶל "confidence" (the word is also "loins" and "folly"); two words for gold, זָהָב and then כֶּתֶם (a poetic word; KJV "fine gold"); מִבְטָחִי "my trust".
- **"If I rejoiced because my wealth was great, and because mine hand had gotten much" → "and because my hand had found much"** (v25) — Hebrew חֵילִי "my wealth"; כַבִּיר "much"; מָצְאָה "found".
- **"If I beheld the sun when it shined, or the moon walking in brightness" → "if I saw the light when it shone, and the moon walking in splendor"** (v26) — Hebrew אוֹר "light" (the sun is meant; the word is "light"); יָהֵל "shone"; וְיָרֵחַ יָקָר הֹלֵךְ, literally "and the moon, precious, walking".
- **"And my heart hath been secretly enticed, or my mouth hath kissed my hand" → "and my heart was enticed in secret, and my hand kissed my mouth"** (v27) — Hebrew וַתִּשַּׁק יָדִי לְפִי, literally "and my hand kissed to my mouth": the hand is the subject; a kiss blown from the hand to the sun or moon.
- **"This also were an iniquity to be punished by the judge: for I should have denied the God that is above" → "that too is an iniquity for arbiters, for I would have lied to El above"** (v28) — Hebrew עָוֺן פְּלִילִי, the adjective of פְּלִילִים (v11); כִּחַשְׁתִּי לְ "lie to"; מִמָּעַל "above".
- **"If I rejoiced at the destruction of him that hated me, or lifted up myself when evil found him" → "I have not rejoiced at the disaster of him who hates me, nor roused myself when evil found him"** (v29) — the oath form (see above); פִּיד "disaster" (30:24); הִתְעֹרַרְתִּי "I roused myself".
- **"Neither have I suffered my mouth to sin by wishing a curse to his soul" → "and I have not given my palate to sin, to ask for his soul with a curse"** (v30) — Hebrew וְלֹא־נָתַתִּי … חִכִּי "and I have not given my palate"; לִשְׁאֹל בְּאָלָה נַפְשׁוֹ "to ask his soul with a curse" (= ask for his death).
- **"If the men of my tabernacle said not, Oh that we had of his flesh! we cannot be satisfied" → "Surely the men of my tent have said, 'Would that there were one who has not been sated from his flesh!'"** (v31) — the oath אִם־לֹא, "surely" (see above); מְתֵי אָהֳלִי "the men of my tent"; מִי־יִתֵּן, literally "who will give", "Would that"; מִבְּשָׂרוֹ לֹא נִשְׂבָּע "from his flesh not sated": no one at Job's table went unfed (it can also be read as a question, "who can find one …?"). "His" is Job's.
- **"The stranger did not lodge in the street: but I opened my doors to the traveller" → "In the street the sojourner did not lodge; my doors I opened to the road"** (v32) — Hebrew גֵּר "sojourner"; לָאֹרַח, as pointed, "to the road" (the doors open on the road); the common repointing לָאֹרֵחַ "to the traveler" is not followed.
- **"If I covered my transgressions as Adam, by hiding mine iniquity in my bosom" → "I have not covered my transgressions like Adam, hiding my iniquity in my bosom"** (v33) — the oath form (see above); כְאָדָם "like Adam" (without the article: the name, Gen 3:8–10; also read "like a man, as men do"); חֻבִּי "my bosom" (found only here).
- **"Did I fear a great multitude, or did the contempt of families terrify me, that I kept silence, and went not out of the door?" → "because I dreaded the great multitude, and the contempt of clans terrified me, and I kept still, I did not go out of the entrance"** (v34) — Hebrew כִּי "because" (not a question); אֶעֱרוֹץ "I dreaded"; הָמוֹן "multitude"; מִשְׁפָּחוֹת "clans"; יְחִתֵּנִי "terrified me"; וָאֶדֹּם "and I kept still"; פֶּתַח "entrance". The whole of v33–34 stands under the oath: Job denies that he hid his sin out of fear.
- **"Oh that one would hear me! behold, my desire is, that the Almighty would answer me, and that mine adversary had written a book" → "Would that I had one who hears me! Behold my mark — let Shaddai answer me — and the document the man of my case has written!"** (v35) — Hebrew מִי יִתֶּן־לִי, literally "who will give me"; הֶן־תָּוִי "behold my tav": תָּו, the last letter, a mark or signature set under a statement (KJV "my desire"); וְסֵפֶר כָּתַב אִישׁ רִיבִי, literally "and a document (which) the man of my case wrote": the charge written by the other party.
- **"bind it as a crown to me" → "I would bind it on me as crowns"** (v36) — Hebrew עֲטָרוֹת, plural; אֶעֶנְדֶנּוּ "I would bind it on"; שְׁכֶם "shoulder".
- **"I would declare unto him the number of my steps; as a prince would I go near unto him" → "The number of my steps I would tell him; like a leader I would approach him"** (v37) — Hebrew אַגִּידֶנּוּ "I would tell him"; נָגִיד "leader" (not "prince"; the two words sound alike: אַגִּיד … נָגִיד); אֲקָרֲבֶנּוּ "I would bring it near / approach him".
- **"If my land cry against me, or that the furrows likewise thereof complain" → "If my ground cries out against me, and its furrows weep together"** (v38) — Hebrew אַדְמָתִי "my ground"; תִזְעָק "cries out"; יַחַד "together"; יִבְכָּיוּן "weep".
- **"If I have eaten the fruits thereof without money, or have caused the owners thereof to lose their life" → "if I have eaten its power without money, and the soul of its owners I have made to breathe out"** (v39) — Hebrew כֹּחָהּ "its power" (its yield, as Gen 4:12); בְלִי־כֶסֶף "without silver", = without payment; בְּעָלֶיהָ "its owners"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; הִפַּחְתִּי, hifil of נָפַח "breathe": "made to breathe out (the soul)".
- **"Let thistles grow instead of wheat, and cockle instead of barley" → "instead of wheat let the thistle come out, and instead of barley stinkweed"** (v40) — Hebrew חוֹחַ "thistle" (singular); יֵצֵא "come out"; בָאְשָׁה, from בָּאַשׁ "stink", a weed of unknown kind. **"The words of Job are ended" → "have come to an end"** — Hebrew תַּמּוּ, the verb תַּם.

### Notes
- **Oath of innocence** — a sworn self-curse across many fields of conduct.
- **"If" and the curse** (v5–40) — most "if" clauses run on to a curse on himself (v8, 10, 22, 40) or a judgment he accepts (v6, 14, 28); where no curse follows (v29–34, 36) the sworn "if" is given as a denial or an assurance.
- **Sexual ethics** (v1–12) — a covenant for the eyes; the neighbor's wife.
- **Slaves' case** (v13–15) — slave and master made in the same belly by the same one.
- **The poor** (v16–23) — widow, fatherless, poor, needy.
- **Gold and the heavenly lights** (v24–28) — trust in wealth and the kiss to the sun or moon both denied.
- **The enemy** (v29–30) — no joy at his disaster, no curse on him.
- **Hospitality** (v31–32) — no one unfed, no sojourner outside.
- **Hidden sin** (v33–34) — no concealment like Adam's.
- **The signature** (v35–37) — Job signs his statement and calls for the written charge of the other party.
- **The land** (v38–40) — the last oath; then "the words of Job have come to an end". Elihu begins next.
