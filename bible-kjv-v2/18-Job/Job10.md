# Job 10

[1] "My soul loathes my life;
I will give free course to my complaint;
I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.

[2] I will say to ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧: Do not condemn me;
let me know why you contend with me.

[3] Is it good to you to oppress,
to despise the work of your hands,
while you shine upon the counsel of the wicked?

[4] Do you have eyes of flesh?
Do you see as man sees?

[5] Are your days as the days of man,
or your years as a man's days,

[6] that you inquire after my iniquity
and search after my sin —

[7] knowing that I am not guilty,
and that none can deliver from your hand?

[8] Your hands fashioned me and made me —
all of me together —
and now you destroy me.

[9] Remember that you have made me like clay;
will you return me to dust?

[10] Did you not pour me out like milk
and curdle me like cheese?

[11] You clothed me with skin and flesh;
you knit me together with bones and sinews.

[12] You granted me life and steadfast love;
your visitation has kept my spirit.

[13] Yet these things you hid in your heart;
I know that this was with you:

[14] If I sin, you watch me;
you will not acquit me of my iniquity.

[15] If I am guilty, woe to me!
If I am righteous, I cannot lift up my head,
filled with shame, looking on my affliction.

[16] It keeps rising; you hunt me like a lion;
you display your marvels against me.

[17] You renew your witnesses against me
and increase your vexation toward me;
wave after wave of troops come upon me.

[18] Why then did you bring me out of the womb?
Would that I had died and no eye had seen me,

[19] that I had been as though I had not been,
carried from the womb to the grave.

[20] Are not my days few? Then cease;
turn away from me, that I may have a little gladness

[21] before I go — whence I shall not return —
to the land of darkness and deep darkness,

[22] the land of gloom, like deep darkness,
of deep darkness without order,
where light itself is like darkness."

## Changes

- **"changes and war are against me" → "wave after wave of troops come upon me"** (v17) — Hebrew *chalifot ve-tzava*.

### Notes
- **Address to *Elohim* direct** throughout.
- **Clay-pottery imagery** (v9-11) — mid-poetic embryology, beautiful and tender: poured out like milk, curdled like cheese, clothed with skin.
- **"You knit me together"** echoed in Ps 139:13.
- **Paradox of divine attention** — Yahweh hunts Job *because* he made him. The tender-maker becomes the relentless-hunter.
- **"Land of darkness"** — Sheol described with intensive doubling of *tzalmaweth* — "darkness without order, light as darkness."
