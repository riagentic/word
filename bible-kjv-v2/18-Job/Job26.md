# Job 26

[1] Then Job answered and said:

[2] "How you have helped him who has no power!
How you have saved the arm that has no strength!

[3] How you have counseled one who has no wisdom,
and revealed the truth in abundance!

[4] With whose help have you uttered words?
Whose breath has come forth from you?

[5] The shades tremble
beneath the waters and their inhabitants.

[6] Sheol is naked before him;
Abaddon has no covering.

[7] He stretches out tzaphon — the north — over empty space;
he hangs the earth on nothing.

[8] He binds the waters in his thick clouds,
and the cloud is not torn beneath them.

[9] He covers the face of the full moon;
he spreads his cloud upon it.

[10] He has inscribed a circle on the face of the waters,
at the boundary between light and darkness.

[11] The pillars of heaven tremble,
astonished at his rebuke.

[12] By his power he stilled the sea;
by his understanding he shattered Rahab.

[13] By his spirit the heavens were made fair;
his hand pierced the fleeing serpent.

[14] Behold, these are but the fringes of his ways;
how small a whisper do we hear of him!
The thunder of his power — who can understand?"

## Changes

- **"hell" → "Sheol"** (v6).
- **"crooked serpent" → "fleeing serpent"** (v13) — *nachash bariach*; same phrase Isa 27:1 applies to Leviathan.
- **"throne" → "full moon"** (v9) — Hebrew *kisseh*; Qere *keseh* = full moon.

### Notes
- **Job's sarcasm** (v2-4) — mock-praise of Bildad's "helpful" speech.
- **Creation hymn** (v5-14) — Job one-ups Bildad with a greater theological display.
- **"Hangs earth on nothing"** — extraordinary cosmology; pre-scientific but evocative.
- **Rahab / fleeing serpent** — defeated chaos-monsters at creation.
- **"These are but the fringes"** (v14) — even vast creation-display is mere edge. True divine mystery far beyond.
