# Job 26

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "How you have helped the one without power!
You have saved the arm without strength!

[3] How you have counseled the one without wisdom,
and sound wisdom in abundance you have made known!

[4] To whom have you told utterances?
And whose breath has gone out from you?

[5] The shades writhe
beneath the waters and their dwellers.

[6] Naked is Sheol before him,
and there is no covering for ⟦abaddon|Avaddon|Abaddon⟧.

[7] He stretches out the north over emptiness;
he hangs the earth on nothing.

[8] He binds up waters in his thick clouds,
and the cloud is not split under them.

[9] He shuts in the face of the throne;
he spreads his cloud over it.

[10] A limit he has circled on the face of the waters,
to the end of light with darkness.

[11] The pillars of the heavens shake
and are astounded at his rebuke.

[12] By his power he stirred up the sea,
and by his understanding he struck through ⟦rahab-monster|Rahav|Rahab⟧.

[13] By his wind the heavens are fair;
his hand has pierced the fleeing serpent.

[14] Behold, these are the ends of his ways,
and what a whisper of a word is heard of him!
And the thunder of his mighty deeds — who can understand?"

## Changes

- **"Then Job answered and said" → "And Job answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר.
- **"How hast thou helped him that is without power? how savest thou the arm that hath no strength?" → "How you have helped the one without power! You have saved the arm without strength!"** (v2) — Hebrew מֶה־עָזַרְתָּ, an exclamation; לְלֹא־כֹחַ "to no-power"; הוֹשַׁעְתָּ a perfect, "you have saved"; זְרוֹעַ לֹא־עֹז "an arm of no strength" (כֹּחַ "power", עֹז "strength"). "you" is singular, as in v3–4: Job answers Bildad.
- **"and how hast thou plentifully declared the thing as it is?" → "and sound wisdom in abundance you have made known!"** (v3) — Hebrew תּוּשִׁיָּה "sound wisdom" (uncertain: sound counsel, success, lasting help; another word than חָכְמָה "wisdom"); לָרֹב "in abundance"; הוֹדַעְתָּ "you have made known".
- **"To whom hast thou uttered words? and whose spirit came from thee?" → "To whom have you told utterances? And whose breath has gone out from you?"** (v4) — Hebrew אֶת־מִי, "whom" (also read "with whose help"); הִגַּדְתָּ "you have told"; מִלִּין "utterances" (beside דָּבָר "word"); נִשְׁמַת־מִי "whose breath" (נְשָׁמָה, not רוּחַ).
- **"Dead things are formed from under the waters, and the inhabitants thereof" → "The shades writhe beneath the waters and their dwellers"** (v5) — Hebrew הָרְפָאִים, the shades of the dead (*rephaim*, the same word as the ancient people of that name; no token: the people are not meant); יְחוֹלָלוּ "writhe" (polal of חוּל), not "are formed"; שֹׁכְנֵיהֶם "their dwellers".
- **"Hell is naked before him, and destruction hath no covering" → "Naked is Sheol before him, and there is no covering for Abaddon"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל "Sheol"; אֲבַדּוֹן *Abaddon*, "destruction", the place of the dead (the token keeps the name).
- **"He stretcheth out the north over the empty place" → "He stretches out the north over emptiness"** (v7) — Hebrew צָפוֹן "north" (also the name of the mountain Zaphon; the plain word is kept); תֹּהוּ "emptiness" (the "waste" of Gen 1:2: here what is not land); בְּלִי־מָה, literally "without-what": "nothing".
- **"rent" → "split"** (v8) — Hebrew נִבְקַע. עָבִים "thick clouds", עָנָן "cloud".
- **"He holdeth back the face of his throne" → "He shuts in the face of the throne"** (v9) — Hebrew מְאַחֵז, piel of אָחַז "take hold": "shut in, enclose"; כִסֵּה as pointed is כִּסֵּא "throne" in another spelling, without "his"; many read כֶּסֶה "full moon" (as Ps 81:3), a change of the pointing. פַּרְשֵׁז "spreading" (only here).
- **"He hath compassed the waters with bounds, until the day and night come to an end" → "A limit he has circled on the face of the waters, to the end of light with darkness"** (v10) — Hebrew חֹק־חָג: חֹק "statute", here a fixed limit (as Job 38:10; Prov 8:29), חָג "he has circled" (the verb of חוּג "circle", 22:14); עַל־פְּנֵי־מָיִם "on the face of the waters"; עַד־תַּכְלִית אוֹר עִם־חֹשֶׁךְ "to the end of light with darkness", where light and darkness meet.
- **"tremble and are astonished at his reproof" → "shake and are astounded at his rebuke"** (v11) — Hebrew יְרוֹפָפוּ "shake" (only here); יִתְמְהוּ "are astounded"; גְּעָרָה "rebuke".
- **"He divideth the sea with his power, and by his understanding he smiteth through the proud" → "By his power he stirred up the sea, and by his understanding he struck through Rahav"** (v12) — Hebrew רָגַע "stir up" (as Isa 51:15; Jer 31:35; also taken here as "still"); וּבִתְבוּנָתוֹ is the qere (the ketiv spells it ובתובנתו, the same word); מָחַץ "strike through"; רַהַב, the sea monster (the token keeps the name).
- **"By his spirit he hath garnished the heavens; his hand hath formed the crooked serpent" → "By his wind the heavens are fair; his hand has pierced the fleeing serpent"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּרוּחוֹ "by his wind" (רוּחַ wind, breath, spirit; with the clearing sky "wind"); שִׁפְרָה "fairness", a noun: "the heavens are fairness"; חֹלֲלָה "pierced" (of חָלַל; KJV took it from חוּל "bring forth"); נָחָשׁ בָּרִיחַ "fleeing serpent" (also "gliding"; as Isa 27:1).
- **"Lo, these are parts of his ways: but how little a portion is heard of him? but the thunder of his power who can understand?" → "Behold, these are the ends of his ways, and what a whisper of a word is heard of him! And the thunder of his mighty deeds — who can understand?"** (v14) — Hebrew קְצוֹת "ends"; דְּרָכָיו "his ways" is the qere (ketiv דרכו "his way"); שֵׁמֶץ דָּבָר "a whisper of a word" ("little" is not written); גְּבוּרוֹתָיו "his mighty deeds" is the qere (ketiv גבורתו "his might"); יִתְבּוֹנָן "understand".

### Notes
- **Job's answer to Bildad** (v2–4) — irony: what has such counsel done for the one without power?
- **Below and above** (v5–14) — the shades, Sheol and Abaddon lie open before him; the north stretched out, the earth hung on nothing, the waters held in the clouds, the sea and Rahav struck down.
- **"The ends of his ways"** (v14) — all this is only the edge; his full might no one can understand.
