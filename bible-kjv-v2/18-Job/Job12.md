# Job 12

[1] Then Job answered and said:

[2] "Truly you are the people,
and wisdom will die with you!

[3] But I have understanding as well as you;
I am not inferior to you.
Who does not know such things as these?

[4] I am a laughingstock to my friend,
I who called on Eloah and he answered —
a just, blameless man a laughingstock!

[5] The one at ease has contempt for misfortune,
a ready thrust for those whose feet slip.

[6] The tents of raiders prosper,
and those who provoke *Elohim* are secure —
those who bring their god in their hand.

[7] But ask the beasts, and they will teach you;
the birds of the sky, and they will tell you;

[8] or speak to the earth, and it will teach you;
the fish of the sea will declare to you.

[9] Who does not know of all these
that the hand of Yahweh has done this?

[10] In his hand is the soul of every living thing,
and the spirit of all humankind.

[11] Does not the ear test words,
as the palate tastes food?

[12] Wisdom is with the aged,
and understanding in length of days.

[13] With him are wisdom and might;
counsel and understanding are his.

[14] Behold, he tears down, and none can rebuild;
he shuts up a man, and none can open.

[15] Behold, he withholds the waters, and they dry up;
he sends them out, and they overturn the earth.

[16] With him are strength and sound wisdom;
the deceived and the deceiver are his.

[17] He leads counselors away stripped,
and makes fools of judges.

[18] He looses the bonds of kings,
and binds a waistcloth on their loins.

[19] He leads priests away stripped,
and overthrows the mighty.

[20] He removes speech from the trusted,
and takes away the discernment of elders.

[21] He pours contempt on nobles,
and loosens the belt of the strong.

[22] He uncovers deep things out of darkness,
and brings deep gloom to light.

[23] He makes nations great, and destroys them;
he enlarges nations, and leads them away.

[24] He takes away the understanding of the leaders of the peoples of the earth,
and makes them wander in a trackless wilderness.

[25] They grope in the dark without light;
he makes them stagger like a drunkard."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"shadow of death" → "deep gloom"** (v22) — *tzalmaweth*.
- **"princes" → "priests"** (v19) — Hebrew *kohanim*; priestly class, not generic princes.
- **"chief of the people" → "leaders of the peoples"** (v24) — *rashei am ha-aretz*.
- **"the just upright man" → "a just, blameless man"** (v4).
- **"he that is ready to slip" → "those whose feet slip"** (v5).

### Notes
- **Sarcasm** (v2) — "Wisdom will die with you!"
- **"Raiders prosper"** (v6) — direct counter to friends: the wicked, not just the good, flourish.
- **"Ask the beasts"** (v7-8) — common creation-theology accessible to animals. Anyone could see this.
- **Creation catalog of divine reversals** (v13-25) — mirroring ancient wisdom psalms. Yahweh's sovereignty reverses human structures.
- **Stark creation-theology** but with dark twist: Yahweh governs, but chaotically from human perspective.
