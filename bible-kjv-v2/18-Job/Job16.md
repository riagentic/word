# Job 16

[1] Then Job answered and said:

[2] "I have heard many such things;
miserable comforters are you all.

[3] Is there no end to windy words?
What provokes you that you answer?

[4] I also could speak as you do,
if your soul were in my nephesh's place;
I could heap up words against you
and shake my head at you.

[5] But I would strengthen you with my mouth,
and the consolation of my lips would ease your pain.

[6] If I speak, my pain is not eased;
and if I keep silent, how much of it leaves me?

[7] Now he has worn me out;
you have devastated all my company.

[8] You have shriveled me up — it has become a witness —
my leanness rises up in me, testifying to my face.

[9] His wrath tears and hates me;
he gnashes his teeth at me;
my adversary sharpens his eyes against me.

[10] They gape at me with their mouths;
they slap me on the cheek with reproach;
they mass together against me.

[11] *Elohim* delivers me over to the ungodly
and casts me into the hands of the wicked.

[12] I was at ease, but he has shattered me;
he seized me by the neck and dashed me to pieces;
he has set me up as his target.

[13] His archers surround me;
he pierces my kidneys without mercy;
he pours out my gall on the ground.

[14] He breaks me breach upon breach;
he runs upon me like a warrior.

[15] I have sewed sackcloth upon my skin
and laid my horn in the dust.

[16] My face is red with weeping,
and on my eyelids is deep shadow —

[17] although there is no violence in my hands,
and my prayer is pure.

[18] O earth, do not cover my blood,
and let my cry find no resting place.

[19] Even now, behold, my witness is in heaven,
and he who testifies for me is on high.

[20] My friends are my scoffers;
my eye pours out tears to Eloah —

[21] that a man might plead for a man before Eloah,
as a son of man for his neighbor.

[22] For when a few years have come,
I shall go on the way whence I shall not return."

## Changes

- **"shadow of death" → "deep shadow"** (v16) — *tzalmaweth*.
- **"reins" → "kidneys"** (v13) — *kilyot*.
- **"giant" → "warrior"** (v14) — *gibbor*.
- **"my record" → "he who testifies for me"** (v19) — *sahadi*; Aramaism for witness.

### Notes
- **"Miserable comforters"** (v2) — Job's devastating assessment.
- **Cosmic witness** (v19) — enigmatic figure "in heaven" who testifies for Job. Progressive anticipation of a heavenly advocate/mediator. Part of Job's emerging hope.
- **"Do not cover my blood"** (v18) — echoes Cain: blood crying from the ground (Gen 4:10). Job demands his suffering not be erased.
- **El as enemy** (v9-14) — most intense anti-God imagery: tears, gnashes, shatters, pierces, breaks. Hyperbolic lament theology.
- **Wish for arbiter** (v21) — extends ch 9's longing. The theme grows.
