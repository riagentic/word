# Job 16

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ answered and said:

[2] "I have heard many things like these;
comforters of toil are you all.

[3] Is there an end to words of wind?
Or what provokes you, that you answer?

[4] I also would speak like you,
if your soul were in the place of my soul;
I would join words together against you,
and shake my head at you.

[5] I would strengthen you with my mouth,
and the moving of my lips would withhold.

[6] If I speak, my pain is not withheld;
and if I cease, what goes from me?

[7] Surely now he has wearied me;
you have made desolate all my congregation.

[8] And you have seized me; it has become a witness;
and my leanness has risen up against me; to my face it answers.

[9] His anger has torn, and he has borne a grudge against me;
he has gnashed at me with his teeth;
my adversary sharpens his eyes at me.

[10] They have gaped at me with their mouth;
with reproach they have struck my cheeks;
together they mass themselves against me.

[11] ⟦el|El|God⟧ hands me over to the unjust one,
and into the hands of the wicked he throws me.

[12] I was untroubled, and he shattered me;
and he seized me by my neck and dashed me to pieces,
and set me up for himself as a target.

[13] His archers surround me;
he splits my kidneys and does not have pity;
he pours out my gall to the earth.

[14] He breaks me with breach upon the face of breach;
he runs at me like a mighty one.

[15] Sackcloth I have sewn upon my skin,
and I have thrust my horn into the dust.

[16] My face is red from weeping,
and on my eyelids is the shadow of death,

[17] although there is no violence in my palms,
and my prayer is pure.

[18] Earth, do not cover my blood,
and let there be no place for my outcry.

[19] Even now, behold, in the heavens is my witness,
and he who testifies for me is in the heights.

[20] My scorners are my friends;
to ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ my eye drips,

[21] and may he decide for a mighty man with ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧,
and a son of man for his friend.

[22] For a few years will come,
and I shall go a path by which I shall not return.

## Changes

- **"Then Job answered and said" → "And Job answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the book.
- **"God" → "El"** (v11) — Hebrew אֵל *El*; **"God" → "Eloah"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ. The chapter has no *Elohim*.
- **"miserable comforters" → "comforters of toil"** (v2) — Hebrew מְנַחֲמֵי עָמָל; עָמָל "toil, trouble": comforters who bring toil. "Many things like these" — כְאֵלֶּה רַבּוֹת.
- **"Shall vain words have an end? or what emboldeneth thee that thou answerest?" → "Is there an end to words of wind? Or what provokes you, that you answer?"** (v3) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי־רוּחַ "words of wind" (as 15:2); יַמְרִיצְךָ, hifil of מָרַץ "be grievous": "provokes, galls you" (singular: Job turns to the one who has just spoken).
- **"I also could speak as ye do: if your soul were in my soul's stead, I could heap up words against you" → "I also would speak like you, if your soul were in the place of my soul; I would join words together against you"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲדַבֵּרָה, אַחְבִּירָה, אָנִיעָה, cohortatives ("I would"); תַּחַת "in the place of"; אַחְבִּירָה from חָבַר "join": string words together. **"shake mine head at you"** — Hebrew הֵנִיעַ בְּמוֹ רֹאשִׁי.
- **"But I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the moving of my lips should asswage your grief" → "I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the moving of my lips would withhold"** (v5) — Hebrew אִמֵּץ "strengthen"; נִיד "moving" (of the lips; or "consolation": uncertain); יַחְשֹׂךְ "withhold, hold back", with no object written ("your grief" is supplied in KJV; the verb returns in v6 with "my pain" as subject). "But" is not written.
- **"Though I speak, my grief is not asswaged: and though I forbear, what am I eased?" → "If I speak, my pain is not withheld; and if I cease, what goes from me?"** (v6) — Hebrew לֹא־יֵחָשֵׂךְ כְּאֵבִי, the verb of v5; וְאַחְדְּלָה "and I cease"; מַה־מִנִּי יַהֲלֹךְ "what goes from me?"
- **"But now he hath made me weary: thou hast made desolate all my company" → "Surely now he has wearied me; you have made desolate all my congregation"** (v7) — Hebrew אַךְ "surely, only"; הֶלְאָנִי "he has wearied me", then הֲשִׁמּוֹתָ "you have made desolate" (hifil of שָׁמֵם): the change from "he" to "you" is the Hebrew's. עֵדָה "congregation" (the gathered household; the word of 15:34).
- **"And thou hast filled me with wrinkles, which is a witness against me: and my leanness rising up in me beareth witness to my face" → "And you have seized me; it has become a witness; and my leanness has risen up against me; to my face it answers"** (v8) — Hebrew וַתִּקְמְטֵנִי: קָמַט is "seize" (22:16) or "shrivel" (uncertain); לְעֵד הָיָה "it has become a witness"; כַּחַשׁ "leanness" (the same letters are "lying"); בִּי "in me / against me"; בְּפָנַי יַעֲנֶה "answers to my face" (= testifies).
- **"He teareth me in his wrath, who hateth me" → "His anger has torn, and he has borne a grudge against me"** (v9) — Hebrew אַפּוֹ טָרַף וַיִּשְׂטְמֵנִי: אַף "anger" (literally "nose"); טָרַף "tear to pieces", with no object; שָׂטַם "bear a grudge against" (not שָׂנֵא "hate"). **"mine enemy sharpeneth his eyes upon me" → "my adversary sharpens his eyes at me"** — צָרִי "my adversary"; לָטַשׁ "sharpen".
- **"they have smitten me upon the cheek reproachfully; they have gathered themselves together against me" → "with reproach they have struck my cheeks; together they mass themselves against me"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּחֶרְפָּה הִכּוּ לְחָיָי; יִתְמַלָּאוּן, literally "they fill themselves up" (in full number). "Mouth" is singular.
- **"God hath delivered me to the ungodly, and turned me over into the hands of the wicked" → "El hands me over to the unjust one, and into the hands of the wicked he throws me"** (v11) — Hebrew יַסְגִּירֵנִי אֶל "hands me over to" (literally "shuts me up to"), imperfect; עֲוִיל, taken as עַוָּל "unjust one" (18:21); the same letters mean "young boy" in 19:18; 21:11 (uncertain). יִרְטֵנִי, from יָרַט "throw headlong" (uncertain).
- **"I was at ease, but he hath broken me asunder: he hath also taken me by my neck, and shaken me to pieces, and set me up for his mark" → "I was untroubled, and he shattered me; and he seized me by my neck and dashed me to pieces, and set me up for himself as a target"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁלֵו "untroubled"; וַיְפַרְפְּרֵנִי, וַיְפַצְפְּצֵנִי, two doubled verbs of breaking; עֹרֶף "neck" (the back of the neck); וַיְקִימֵנִי לוֹ לְמַטָּרָה "set me up for himself as a target".
- **"His archers compass me round about, he cleaveth my reins asunder, and doth not spare" → "His archers surround me; he splits my kidneys and does not have pity"** (v13) — Hebrew רַבָּיו "his archers" (רַב as in Jer 50:29; or "his many ones": uncertain); כִּלְיוֹתַי "my kidneys"; חָמַל "have pity (spare)". **"upon the ground" → "to the earth"** — לָאָרֶץ.
- **"He breaketh me with breach upon breach: he runneth upon me like a giant" → "He breaks me with breach upon the face of breach; he runs at me like a mighty one"** (v14) — Hebrew יִפְרְצֵנִי פֶרֶץ עַל־פְּנֵי־פָרֶץ, verb and noun of one root; גִּבּוֹר "mighty one".
- **"I have sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and defiled my horn in the dust" → "Sackcloth I have sewn upon my skin, and I have thrust my horn into the dust"** (v15) — Hebrew גֶּלֶד "skin" (only here); עוֹלַלְתִּי, poel of עָלַל "thrust in" (KJV "defiled", from another root); קַרְנִי "my horn" (strength, standing).
- **"My face is foul with weeping, and on my eyelids is the shadow of death" → "My face is red from weeping, and on my eyelids is the shadow of death"** (v16) — qere חֳמַרְמְרוּ, plural (with פָּנַי, a plural noun); the ketiv חמרמרה is feminine singular; the meaning is the same: "is red" (or "inflamed"). צַלְמָוֶת as pointed, "the shadow of death"; read צַלְמוּת it is "deep darkness".
- **"Not for any injustice in mine hands: also my prayer is pure" → "although there is no violence in my palms, and my prayer is pure"** (v17) — Hebrew עַל לֹא־חָמָס בְּכַפָּי: חָמָס "violence"; כַּף "palm".
- **"let my cry have no place" → "let there be no place for my outcry"** (v18) — Hebrew זְעָקָה "outcry" (the cry for help of the wronged; Gen 4:10 has צְעָקָה).
- **"my witness is in heaven, and my record is on high" → "in the heavens is my witness, and he who testifies for me is in the heights"** (v19) — Hebrew בַשָּׁמַיִם "in the heavens"; עֵדִי "my witness"; שָׂהֲדִי, the Aramaic word for a witness, "my witness" (kept apart from עֵד in English); בַּמְּרוֹמִים "in the heights".
- **"My friends scorn me: but mine eye poureth out tears unto God" → "My scorners are my friends; to Eloah my eye drips"** (v20) — Hebrew מְלִיצַי רֵעָי, without a verb. מְלִיצַי is the participle of הֵלִיץ, "mock" (Ps 119:51); the same form is "interpreter, go-between" (Gen 42:23; Job 33:23), and "My interpreters are my friends" (or "my interpreter is my friend") is also read. דָּלְפָה "drips" ("tears" is not written).
- **"O that one might plead for a man with God, as a man pleadeth for his neighbour!" → "and may he decide for a mighty man with Eloah, and a son of man for his friend"** (v21) — Hebrew וְיוֹכַח, jussive of הוֹכִיחַ: "decide" between two parties (as Gen 31:37); who "he" is, is not named (the witness of v19, or Eloah). גֶּבֶר "mighty man" beside בֶּן־אָדָם "son of man" (two words kept apart); רֵעֵהוּ "his friend" (the word of v20). "O that one might" and "as" are not written.
- **"When a few years are come" → "For a few years will come"** (v22) — Hebrew שְׁנוֹת מִסְפָּר, literally "years of number" (= few). **"I shall go the way whence I shall not return" → "I shall go a path by which I shall not return"** — אֹרַח "path".

### Notes
- **"Comforters of toil"** (v2) — Job's answer to the friends.
- **The witness in the heavens** (v19) — Job names a witness "in the heavens" and one who testifies for him "in the heights"; who it is, is not said.
- **"Do not cover my blood"** (v18) — compare Gen 4:10 (the voice of blood crying from the ground) and Isa 26:21.
- **El as assailant** (v9–14) — Job's images: tearing, gnashing, shattering, archers, breach upon breach.
- **An arbiter** (v21) — compare 9:33.
