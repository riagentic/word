# Job 38

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ out of the storm and said:

[2] "Who is this who darkens counsel
by utterances without knowledge?

[3] Gird up your loins, please, like a man;
and I will ask you, and you shall make me know.

[4] Where were you when I founded the earth?
Tell, if you know understanding.

[5] Who set its measurements, for you know?
Or who stretched a line over it?

[6] On what were its sockets sunk,
or who cast its cornerstone,

[7] when the morning stars sang together
and all the sons of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shouted,

[8] and shut in the sea with doors,
when it burst forth, came out from the womb,

[9] when I made the cloud its garment
and dark cloud its swaddling band,

[10] and I broke my limit upon it,
and set a bar and doors,

[11] and said, 'Thus far you shall come, and no farther,
and here shall one set a stop to the exaltation of your waves'?

[12] Have you, from your days, commanded the morning,
made the dawn know its place,

[13] to take hold of the corners of the earth,
and the wicked be shaken out of it?

[14] It is changed like the clay of a seal,
and they take their stand like a garment.

[15] And from the wicked their light is held back,
and the raised arm is broken.

[16] Have you come to the springs of the sea,
and walked about in the recesses of the deep?

[17] Have the gates of death been uncovered to you,
and do you see the gates of the shadow of death?

[18] Have you considered the breadths of the earth?
Tell, if you know it all.

[19] Where is the way where light abides,
and darkness, where is its place,

[20] that you should take it to its border,
and that you should discern the paths to its house?

[21] You know, for then you were born,
and the number of your days is great!

[22] Have you come into the treasuries of the snow,
and do you see the treasuries of the hail,

[23] which I have withheld for the time of distress,
for the day of battle and war?

[24] Where is the way where the light is divided,
the east wind scatters upon the earth?

[25] Who has divided a channel for the flood,
and a way for the flash of thunders,

[26] to cause it to rain on a land where no man is,
a wilderness with no human in it,

[27] to satisfy the waste and the desolation,
and to make the going forth of vegetation spring up?

[28] Has the rain a father,
or who has begotten the drops of dew?

[29] Out of whose belly came the ice,
and the frost of the heavens, who has borne it?

[30] As stone the waters hide themselves,
and the face of the deep is caught fast.

[31] Can you bind the bands of ⟦pleiades|Kimah|the Pleiades⟧,
or loose the cords of ⟦orion|Kesil|Orion⟧?

[32] Can you bring out ⟦mazzaroth|Mazzarot|the Mazzaroth⟧ in its season,
and lead ⟦bear|Ayish|the Bear⟧ with her sons?

[33] Do you know the statutes of the heavens?
Can you set its rule on the earth?

[34] Can you raise your voice to the thick cloud,
and an abundance of waters cover you?

[35] Can you send lightnings, and they go,
and say to you, 'Here we are'?

[36] Who has put wisdom in the inward parts,
or who has given understanding to the mind?

[37] Who counts the skies by wisdom,
and the jars of the heavens, who can tilt them,

[38] when the dust is poured into a casting
and the clods cling together?

[39] Do you hunt prey for the lioness,
and fill the appetite of the young lions,

[40] when they crouch in the dens,
sit in the covert for ambush?

[41] Who prepares for the raven its game,
when its young cry for help to ⟦el|El|God⟧,
wander for lack of food?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind" → "And Yahweh answered Job out of the storm"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … מִן הַסְּעָרָה; סְעָרָה is "storm" (no "whirl" in the word; as 2 Kgs 2:1, 11 and Job 40:6). The qere מִן הַסְּעָרָה is two words; the written text joins them (מנהסערה); the meaning is the same.
- **"words" → "utterances"** (v2) — Hebrew מִלִּין, plural of מִלָּה "utterance" (דָּבָר is "word").
- **"Gird up now thy loins like a man; for I will demand of thee, and answer thou me" → "Gird up your loins, please, like a man; and I will ask you, and you shall make me know"** (v3) — Hebrew נָא "please"; גֶּבֶר "man" (alone here: not "mighty man"); וְהוֹדִיעֵנִי "and make me know" (hifil of יָדַע).
- **"when I laid the foundations of the earth" → "when I founded the earth"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּיָסְדִי־אָרֶץ, one verb. **"if thou hast understanding" → "if you know understanding"** — Hebrew אִם־יָדַעְתָּ בִינָה (also v18 "if you know it all").
- **"if thou knowest" → "for you know"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי תֵדָע, ironic. **"the line"** — Hebrew קָו "line"; "over it" עָלֶיהָ.
- **"foundations" → "sockets"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲדָנִים, the word for the sockets of the tabernacle boards (Exod 26). **"laid" → "cast"** — Hebrew יָרָה "throw, cast".
- **"shouted for joy" → "shouted"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיָּרִיעוּ; "for joy" is not written. **"sons of God" → "sons of Elohim"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי אֱלֹהִים, without the article (Job 1:6; 2:1 have it); "angels" in Changes only.
- **"Or who shut up the sea with doors" → "and shut in the sea with doors"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיָּסֶךְ "and he shut in": the Hebrew has no "who"; the verse runs on from "who cast" (v6). Reading מִי שָׂךְ "who shut in" is a common correction (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. **"as if it had issued out of the womb" → "came out from the womb"** — Hebrew מֵרֶחֶם יֵצֵא, a second verb, no "as if".
- **"thick darkness" → "dark cloud"** (v9) — Hebrew עֲרָפֶל. "Cloud" is singular (עָנָן).
- **"brake up for it my decreed place" → "I broke my limit upon it"** (v10) — Hebrew וָאֶשְׁבֹּר עָלָיו חֻקִּי: חֹק, elsewhere "statute", here the bound set for the sea (Job 26:10; Prov 8:29). שָׁבַר is "break"; how a limit is "broken" (cut out, measured off) is not certain. **"bars" → "a bar"** — Hebrew בְּרִיחַ, singular.
- **"no further" kept** (v11) — Hebrew וְלֹא תֹסִיף, literally "and you shall not add". **"here shall thy proud waves be stayed" → "here shall one set a stop to the exaltation of your waves"** — Hebrew וּפֹא־יָשִׁית בִּגְאוֹן גַּלֶּיךָ, "and here one sets in (against) the exaltation of your waves": an active verb with no object; "a stop" is supplied. גָּאוֹן "exaltation, pride".
- **"since thy days" → "from your days"** (v12) — Hebrew הְמִיָּמֶיךָ, = in your lifetime. **"caused the dayspring to know his place" → "made the dawn know its place"** — qere יִדַּעְתָּה הַשַּׁחַר (ketiv ידעתה שחר, without the article; the same sense).
- **"the ends of the earth" → "the corners of the earth"** (v13) — Hebrew כַּנְפוֹת הָאָרֶץ, literally "the wings", as of a garment's corners.
- **"It is turned as clay to the seal; and they stand as a garment" → "It is changed like the clay of a seal, and they take their stand like a garment"** (v14) — Hebrew כְּחֹמֶר חוֹתָם "like the clay of a seal"; וְיִתְיַצְּבוּ "they take their stand". Who "they" are is not said.
- **"withholden" → "held back"** (v15) — Hebrew יִמָּנַע (מָנַע "hold back"; חָשַׂךְ "withhold" is v23). **"the high arm" → "the raised arm"** — Hebrew זְרוֹעַ רָמָה; no "their".
- **"entered into" → "come to"** (v16) — Hebrew בָּאתָ עַד. **"springs"** — Hebrew נִבְכֵי, found only here: meaning uncertain. **"or hast thou walked in the search of the depth" → "and walked about in the recesses of the deep"** — Hebrew חֵקֶר, literally "searching" (what can only be searched out); הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about"; "and", not "or".
- **"opened" → "uncovered"** (v17) — Hebrew נִגְלוּ. **"shadow of death" kept** — Hebrew צַלְמָוֶת, pointed as צֵל + מָוֶת "shadow of death"; many read the consonants as צַלְמוּת "deep darkness". The Masoretic pointing is followed (one rendering through Job).
- **"perceived" → "considered"** (v18) — Hebrew הִתְבֹּנַנְתָּ עַד־רַחֲבֵי־אָרֶץ, "considered as far as the breadths of the earth". **"if thou knowest it all" → "if you know it all"** — כֻלָּהּ "all of it".
- **"Where is the way where light dwelleth" → "Where is the way where light abides"** (v19) — Hebrew יִשְׁכָּן (שָׁכַן "abide").
- **"bound" → "border"** (v20) — Hebrew גְּבוּל. **"house"** — Hebrew בַּיִת.
- **"Knowest thou it, because thou wast then born?" → "You know, for then you were born"** (v21) — the Hebrew has no question; irony.
- **"treasures" → "treasuries"** (v22, twice) — Hebrew אוֹצָר. **"or" → "and"** — Hebrew וְ.
- **"reserved" → "withheld"** (v23) — Hebrew חָשַׂךְ. **"trouble" → "distress"** — Hebrew צָר.
- **"By what way is the light parted, which scattereth the east wind upon the earth?" → "Where is the way where the light is divided, the east wind scatters upon the earth?"** (v24) — Hebrew אֵי־זֶה הַדֶּרֶךְ יֵחָלֶק אוֹר יָפֵץ קָדִים עֲלֵי־אָרֶץ; יָפֵץ is active, "scatters", with קָדִים "east wind" as subject and no object.
- **"divided a watercourse for the overflowing of waters" → "divided a channel for the flood"** (v25) — Hebrew פִּלַּג לַשֶּׁטֶף תְּעָלָה. **"the lightning of thunder" → "the flash of thunders"** — Hebrew חֲזִיז קֹלוֹת; חֲזִיז uncertain ("flash" or "thundercloud", Job 28:26; Zech 10:1); קֹלוֹת "thunders", literally "voices".
- **"no man is … wherein there is no man" → "no man is … no human in it"** (v26) — Hebrew לֹא־אִישׁ … לֹא־אָדָם: two words for man, kept apart. **"wilderness"** — Hebrew מִדְבָּר.
- **"the desolate and waste ground" → "the waste and the desolation"** (v27) — Hebrew שֹׁאָה וּמְשֹׁאָה, two nouns of one root (as Job 30:3); "ground" is not written. **"to cause the bud of the tender herb to spring forth" → "to make the going forth of vegetation spring up"** — Hebrew מֹצָא דֶשֶׁא, "the going forth (source) of vegetation"; reading מִצִּיָּה "dry ground" for מֹצָא is a correction (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Out of whose womb" → "Out of whose belly"** (v29) — Hebrew בֶּטֶן. **"hoary frost of heaven, who hath gendered it" → "frost of the heavens, who has borne it"** — Hebrew יְלָדוֹ (יָלַד of the mother).
- **"The waters are hid as with a stone" → "As stone the waters hide themselves"** (v30) — Hebrew כָּאֶבֶן מַיִם יִתְחַבָּאוּ. **"is frozen" → "is caught fast"** — Hebrew יִתְלַכָּדוּ, literally "take one another captive" (לָכַד), of the ice locking the surface.
- **"sweet influences of Pleiades" → "bands of Kimah"** (v31) — Hebrew מַעֲדַנּוֹת, uncertain: "bands, fetters" (as if from עָנַד "bind") or "delights" (from עֵדֶן). מֹשְׁכוֹת "cords" (from מָשַׁךְ "draw"). כִּימָה *Kimah* and כְּסִיל *Kesil* keep their tokens; "Pleiades" and "Orion" are traditional identifications, not certain.
- **"Mazzaroth in his season" → "Mazzarot in its season"** (v32) — Hebrew מַזָּרוֹת, found only here; perhaps constellations of the zodiac, perhaps the same as מַזָּלוֹת (2 Kgs 23:5): uncertain. **"Arcturus with his sons" → "Ayish with her sons"** — Hebrew עַיִשׁ (Job 9:9 has עָשׁ), traditionally the Bear; uncertain. בָּנֶיהָ "her sons" (not "cubs"); "guide" → "lead" (נָחָה); "or" → "and".
- **"ordinances" → "statutes"** (v33) — Hebrew חֻקּוֹת. **"dominion" → "rule"** — Hebrew מִשְׁטָר, found only here.
- **"clouds" → "thick cloud"** (v34) — Hebrew עָב, singular. **"that abundance of waters may cover thee" → "and an abundance of waters cover you"** — Hebrew וְשִׁפְעַת־מַיִם תְּכַסֶּךָּ, with "and".
- **"Here we are"** (v35) — Hebrew הִנֵּנוּ, literally "behold us". **"send forth" → "send"**; **"that they may go" → "and they go"** — Hebrew וְיֵלֵכוּ.
- **"the inward parts … the heart" → "the inward parts … the mind"** (v36) — Hebrew טֻחוֹת (elsewhere only Ps 51:6) and שֶׂכְוִי (only here): both uncertain. Others take them as "the ibis … the rooster" (birds that foretell weather) or as signs in the sky (from memory). לֵב "heart" is not written.
- **"number the clouds" → "counts the skies"** (v37) — Hebrew שְׁחָקִים "skies". **"stay the bottles of heaven" → "tilt the jars of the heavens"** — Hebrew נִבְלֵי שָׁמַיִם; נֵבֶל "jar" or "skin" (uncertain); יַשְׁכִּיב, literally "make lie down".
- **"groweth into hardness" → "is poured into a casting"** (v38) — Hebrew בְּצֶקֶת עָפָר לַמּוּצָק, from יָצַק "pour": the dust set hard like cast metal. **"cleave fast together" → "cling together"** — Hebrew יְדֻבָּקוּ.
- **"Wilt thou hunt the prey for the lion" → "Do you hunt prey for the lioness"** (v39) — Hebrew לָבִיא (gender not certain, as Num 23:24). **"appetite"** — Hebrew חַיַּת, literally "the life of".
- **"dens" kept** (v40) — Hebrew מְעוֹנוֹת, "abodes", of beasts a den. **"abide in the covert to lie in wait" → "sit in the covert for ambush"** — Hebrew יֵשְׁבוּ בַסֻּכָּה לְמוֹ־אָרֶב; סֻכָּה, elsewhere "booth", is here a thicket.
- **"provideth for the raven his food" → "prepares for the raven its game"** (v41) — Hebrew יָכִין; צַיִד "game" (what is hunted). **"they wander for lack of meat" → "wander for lack of food"** — Hebrew יִתְעוּ לִבְלִי־אֹכֶל: the third line is kept. "Its young" is the qere יְלָדָיו (plural; ketiv ילדו "its young one"). **"God" → "El"** — Hebrew אֵל.

### Notes
- **Yahweh speaks** — after all the human speeches, Yahweh answers. He does not answer Job's questions directly.
- **Out of the storm** (v1) — the speech comes from the storm; Elihu has just spoken of storm and weather (36–37).
- **Questions** — Yahweh questions Job.
- **Creation tour** — earth (v4–7), sea (v8–11), morning (v12–15), depths and death (v16–18), light and darkness (v19–21), weather (v22–30, 34–38), stars (v31–33), beasts (v39–41).
- **"The morning stars sang together"** (v7) — at the founding of the earth.
- **Feeding the raven** (v41) — its young cry to El.
- **No direct justification of Job's suffering** — the answer is creation itself.
