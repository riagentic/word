# Job 37

[1] "At this also my heart trembles
and leaps from its place.

[2] Listen, listen to the roar of his voice,
and the rumbling that comes from his mouth.

[3] Under the whole heaven he lets it go,
and his lightning to the corners of the earth.

[4] After it a voice roars;
he thunders with the voice of his majesty,
and he does not restrain the lightnings when his voice is heard.

[5] *Elohim* thunders wondrously with his voice;
he does great things that we cannot comprehend.

[6] For he says to the snow, 'Fall on the earth,'
and to the shower of rain, 'Be strong' — his mighty shower.

[7] He seals up the hand of every man,
that all whom he has made may know his work.

[8] The beasts go into their lairs
and stay in their dens.

[9] From its chamber comes the whirlwind,
and cold from the scattering winds.

[10] By the breath of *Elohim* ice is given,
and the broad waters are frozen.

[11] He loads the thick cloud with moisture;
the clouds scatter his lightning.

[12] They turn round about by his guidance,
to do whatever he commands them
on the face of the habitable world.

[13] Whether for correction, for his land,
or for steadfast love —
he causes it to happen.

[14] Hear this, O Job;
stop and consider the wondrous works of *Elohim*.

[15] Do you know how *Elohim* lays his command upon them,
and causes the lightning of his cloud to shine?

[16] Do you know the balancing of the clouds,
the wondrous works of him who is perfect in knowledge —

[17] you whose clothes are hot
when the earth is still because of the south wind?

[18] Can you, like him, spread out the skies,
hard as a cast metal mirror?

[19] Teach us what we shall say to him;
we cannot draw up our case because of darkness.

[20] Shall it be told him that I would speak?
Did ever a man say he wished to be swallowed up?

[21] Now no one can look at the light
when it is bright in the clouds,
when the wind has passed and cleared them.

[22] Out of the north comes golden splendor;
around Eloah is awesome majesty.

[23] Shaddai — we cannot find him out;
he is excellent in power;
he will not violate justice and abundant righteousness.

[24] Therefore men fear him;
he does not regard any who are wise in their own heart."

## Changes

- **"molten looking glass" → "cast metal mirror"** (v18) — ancient mirrors of polished bronze.
- **"he will not afflict" → "he will not violate justice"** (v23) — Hebrew *lo ya'aneh*; connected to *mishpat* — "will not answer/violate judgment."

### Notes
- **Elihu closing speech** — climactic thunder-imagery. Voice of Yahweh in storm.
- **Divine utterance in storm** — preparing for actual voice from the whirlwind in ch 38.
- **Creation evidence** — humans cannot control, understand, or replicate natural phenomena.
- **"Golden splendor from the north"** — unusual expression; aurora? Sunset-gold illuminating storm-clouds?
- **"Around Eloah awesome majesty"** — terror-and-beauty theme.
- **"He does not regard any wise in their own heart"** — closing rebuke; Yahweh's sovereignty over sages.
- **Transition to theophany** — Elihu's words cease; Yahweh answers next.
