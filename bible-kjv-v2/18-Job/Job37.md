# Job 37

[1] "Also at this my heart trembles
and leaps from its place.

[2] Surely hear the agitation of his voice,
and the rumbling that goes out from his mouth.

[3] Under all the heavens he lets it loose,
and his light to the corners of the earth.

[4] After it a voice roars;
he thunders with the voice of his exaltation,
and he does not hold them back when his voice is heard.

[5] ⟦el|El|God⟧ thunders with his voice wondrously,
doing great things, and we do not know.

[6] For to the snow he says, 'Fall on the earth,' and the shower of rain,
and the shower of the rains of his strength.

[7] On the hand of every man he sets a seal,
that all the men of his work may know.

[8] And the living thing goes into a lurking place
and abides in its dens.

[9] Out of the chamber comes the tempest,
and from the scatterers, cold.

[10] From the breath of ⟦el|El|God⟧ ice is given,
and the breadth of the waters is in constraint.

[11] Also with moisture he loads the thick cloud;
he scatters the cloud of his light.

[12] And it turns about in circlings by his guidance,
for their work,
all that he commands them on the face of the world, earthward.

[13] Whether for a rod, whether for his land,
whether for ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
he makes it come.

[14] Give ear to this, O ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧;
stand and consider the wonderful deeds of ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[15] Do you know when ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ lays charge upon them,
and makes the light of his cloud shine?

[16] Do you know about the balancings of the thick cloud,
the wonders of the one perfect in knowledge,

[17] you whose garments are hot
when the earth is made quiet from the south?

[18] Will you with him beat out the skies,
strong as a cast mirror?

[19] Make us know what we shall say to him;
we cannot set in order because of darkness.

[20] Shall it be recounted to him that I would speak?
If a man has spoken, surely he shall be swallowed up.

[21] And now they have not seen the light;
bright it is in the skies,
and a wind has passed and cleansed them.

[22] Out of the north gold comes;
upon ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ is fearsome majesty.

[23] ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|The Almighty⟧ — we have not found him,
great in power;
and judgment and abundance of righteousness he does not afflict.

[24] Therefore men fear him;
he does not see any of the wise of heart."

## Changes

- **"God" → "El" / "Eloah"** (v5, 10, 14; v15, 22) — Hebrew אֵל *El* and אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*, not *Elohim*. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v23) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי; its meaning is not certain.
- **"is moved out of his place" → "leaps from its place"** (v1) — Hebrew יִתַּר, from נָתַר "leap". חָרַד "tremble".
- **"Hear attentively the noise of his voice" → "Surely hear the agitation of his voice"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ שָׁמוֹעַ, literally "hear, hearing"; רֹגֶז "agitation, raging" (verb רָגַז). **"the sound" → "the rumbling"** — Hebrew הֶגֶה (verb הָגָה, of a low sound).
- **"He directeth it" → "he lets it loose"** (v3) — Hebrew יִשְׁרֵהוּ (שָׁרָה "let loose"). **"lightning" → "light"** (v3, 11, 15) — Hebrew אוֹר, as 36:30, 32; lightning is meant. **"ends" → "corners"** — Hebrew כַּנְפוֹת, literally "wings".
- **"the voice of his excellency" → "the voice of his exaltation"** (v4) — Hebrew גְּאוֹן. **"he will not stay them" → "he does not hold them back"** — Hebrew יְעַקְּבֵם; "them" is not named (lightnings, rains or the sounds).
- **"marvellously" → "wondrously"** (v5) — Hebrew נִפְלָאוֹת "wonders", used as an adverb. **"which we cannot comprehend" → "and we do not know"** — Hebrew וְלֹא נֵדָע (as 36:26).
- **"Be thou on the earth; likewise to the small rain, and to the great rain of his strength" → "Fall on the earth,' and the shower of rain, and the shower of the rains of his strength"** (v6) — Hebrew הֱוֵא אָרֶץ: הֱוֵא is "fall" (Arabic *hawā*) or "be" (Aramaic); uncertain. גֶּשֶׁם and מָטָר are both "rain": גֶּשֶׁם is "shower" here, because "rain of rain" is not English. The Hebrew line has no verb for the rains; "Be strong" is not in it. עֹז "strength".
- **"He sealeth up the hand of every man" → "On the hand of every man he sets a seal"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּיַד … יַחְתּוֹם, "in/on the hand … he seals". **"that all men may know his work" → "that all the men of his work may know"** — Hebrew לָדַעַת כָּל־אַנְשֵׁי מַעֲשֵׂהוּ: "the men he made", or "that all men may know his work"; the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"Then the beasts go into dens, and remain in their places" → "the living thing goes into a lurking place and abides in its dens"** (v8) — Hebrew חַיָּה "living thing" (singular); אֶרֶב "lurking place" (from אָרַב "lie in ambush"); שָׁכַן "abide"; מְעוֹנָה "den" (of God's dwelling "abode").
- **"Out of the south cometh the whirlwind" → "Out of the chamber comes the tempest"** (v9) — Hebrew מִן־הַחֶדֶר "from the chamber"; סוּפָה "tempest" (סְעָרָה is "storm", 38:1). **"cold out of the north" → "from the scatterers, cold"** — Hebrew מִמְּזָרִים, found only here: "scatterers" (winds that scatter), or a name of stars (cf. מַזָּרוֹת 38:32); uncertain.
- **"By the breath of God frost is given" → "ice is given"** (v10) — Hebrew קֶרַח "ice"; יִתֶּן is active, "he (or it) gives". **"the breadth of the waters is straitened" → "is in constraint"** — Hebrew בְּמוּצָק, from צוּק "press" (as 36:16, set beside רֹחַב as there); others derive it from יָצַק "cast" (as v18): "is cast", frozen.
- **"by watering he wearieth the thick cloud" → "with moisture he loads the thick cloud"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּרִי, found only here: "moisture" (uncertain); יַטְרִיחַ "burden". **"he scattereth his bright cloud" → "he scatters the cloud of his light"** — Hebrew עֲנַן אוֹרוֹ. עָב "thick cloud", עָנָן "cloud".
- **"by his counsels" → "by his guidance"** (v12) — qere בְּתַחְבּוּלֹתָיו "his guidances" (ketiv singular, the same sense); the word is from the rope of a steersman. **"that they may do" → "for their work"** — Hebrew לְפָעֳלָם. **"upon the face of the world in the earth" → "earthward"** — Hebrew אָרְצָה "to the earth". וְהוּא "and it" (the cloud) or "and he".
- **"correction" → "a rod"** (v13) — Hebrew שֵׁבֶט "rod". **"mercy" → "chesed"** — Hebrew חֶסֶד (token). **"he causeth it to come"** — Hebrew יַמְצִאֵהוּ, literally "he makes it find" (its place).
- **"Hearken unto this" → "Give ear to this"** (v14) — Hebrew הַאֲזִינָה. **"stand still" → "stand"** — Hebrew עֲמֹד. **"the wondrous works" → "the wonderful deeds"** — Hebrew נִפְלְאוֹת.
- **"when God disposed them" → "when Eloah lays charge upon them"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּשׂוּם־אֱלוֹהַּ עֲלֵיהֶם, "in Eloah's setting upon them": "charge" is supplied for English (no object is written). הוֹפִיעַ "make shine".
- **"the balancings of the clouds" → "the balancings of the thick cloud"** (v16) — Hebrew מִפְלְשֵׂי־עָב, found only here; perhaps the same as מִפְרְשֵׂי "spreadings" (36:29), one letter different; uncertain. **"the wondrous works" → "the wonders"** — Hebrew מִפְלָאוֹת, another word than v14, found only here. **"perfect in knowledge"** — Hebrew תְּמִים דֵּעִים, plural; the words of 36:4.
- **"How thy garments are warm" → "you whose garments are hot"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־בְּגָדֶיךָ חַמִּים. **"when he quieteth the earth by the south wind" → "when the earth is made quiet from the south"** — Hebrew בְּהַשְׁקִט אֶרֶץ מִדָּרוֹם: an active infinitive without a subject; דָּרוֹם "south"; "wind" is not written.
- **"spread out the sky" → "beat out the skies"** (v18) — Hebrew תַּרְקִיעַ, "beat out" (as metal: the root of רָקִיעַ "expanse"); no question word, the question is in the sense. **"strong, and as a molten looking glass" → "strong as a cast mirror"** — Hebrew חֲזָקִים כִּרְאִי מוּצָק (מוּצָק "cast", from יָצַק).
- **"Teach us" → "Make us know"** (v19) — Hebrew הוֹדִיעֵנוּ. **"we cannot order our speech by reason of darkness" → "we cannot set in order because of darkness"** — Hebrew לֹא־נַעֲרֹךְ מִפְּנֵי־חֹשֶׁךְ; no object is written ("words" or "a case" is understood); עָרַךְ as 36:19.
- **"Shall it be told him" → "Shall it be recounted to him"** (v20) — Hebrew סֻפַּר. **"if a man speak, surely he shall be swallowed up"** — Hebrew אִם־אָמַר אִישׁ כִּי יְבֻלָּע: also read as a question, "Has a man ever said that he would be swallowed up?"; uncertain.
- **"men see not the bright light" → "they have not seen the light; bright it is"** (v21) — Hebrew לֹא רָאוּ אוֹר בָּהִיר הוּא, "they" without a subject. **"the wind passeth, and cleanseth them" → "a wind has passed and cleansed them"** — perfect and narrative form; טִהֵר "cleanse".
- **"Fair weather cometh out of the north" → "Out of the north gold comes"** (v22) — Hebrew זָהָב "gold"; יֶאֱתֶה "comes" (a poetic verb). Whether golden light or gold itself is meant is not said. **"with God is terrible majesty" → "upon Eloah is fearsome majesty"** — Hebrew עַל "upon"; נוֹרָא "fearsome"; הוֹד "majesty".
- **"we cannot find him out" → "we have not found him"** (v23) — Hebrew לֹא־מְצָאנֻהוּ, perfect. **"he is excellent in power" → "great in power"** — Hebrew שַׂגִּיא־כֹחַ (as 36:26). **"and in judgment, and in plenty of justice: he will not afflict" → "and judgment and abundance of righteousness he does not afflict"** — Hebrew וּמִשְׁפָּט וְרֹב־צְדָקָה לֹא יְעַנֶּה: piel of עָנָה "afflict". "Judgment and righteousness" may be what he does not do violence to, or the manner in which he does not afflict ("in judgment … he does not oppress"); "violate" is not in the word.
- **"he respecteth not any that are wise of heart" → "he does not see any of the wise of heart"** (v24) — Hebrew לֹא־יִרְאֶה כָּל־חַכְמֵי־לֵב, "he does not see all the wise of heart" (= he regards none of them); "in their own heart" is not written. With another vowel the line reads "all the wise of heart do not see (him)".

### Notes
- **Elihu's closing words** — the thunder as El's voice (v2–5), the winter (v6–10), the clouds at his command (v11–13), then questions to Job (v14–20) that Yahweh's speech takes up in 38.
- **"Gold from the north"** (v22) — the Hebrew says only "gold"; light, a golden sky or gold from the northern mountains have been proposed.
- **"Perfect in knowledge"** (v16; 36:4) — Elihu uses the same words of himself (36:4) and of El (37:16).
