# Job 41

[1] "Can you draw out ⟦leviathan|Livyatan|Leviathan⟧ with a hook,
and with a cord press down his tongue?

[2] Can you put a rush in his nose,
and with a thorn pierce his jaw?

[3] Will he multiply supplications to you?
Or will he speak tender things to you?

[4] Will he cut a covenant with you?
Will you take him for a slave forever?

[5] Will you play with him as with a bird,
and will you bind him for your girls?

[6] Will companions trade over him?
Will they divide him among the merchants?

[7] Can you fill his skin with barbs,
and his head with a fish spear?

[8] Put your palm upon him;
remember the battle — do not do it again!

[9] Behold, his hope is proved a lie;
is one hurled down even at the sight of him?

[10] None is so fierce that he would stir him up;
and who is he who will take his stand before me?

[11] Who has come before me, that I should repay?
Under all the heavens, it is mine.

[12] Concerning him shall I keep silent — his limbs,
and the word of mighty deeds, and the grace of his arrangement?

[13] Who has uncovered the face of his garment?
Into the double of his bridle who will come?

[14] The doors of his face, who has opened them?
Around his teeth is dread.

[15] A pride are the channels of shields,
shut up with a tight seal.

[16] One draws near to one,
and wind does not come between them.

[17] Each to its brother they are made to cling;
they hold together and are not parted.

[18] His sneezings make light flash,
and his eyes are like the eyelids of dawn.

[19] From his mouth go torches;
sparks of fire escape.

[20] From his nostrils goes out smoke,
as a pot blown upon, and a rush.

[21] His soul kindles coals,
and a flame goes out from his mouth.

[22] In his neck lodges strength,
and before him dances dismay.

[23] The folds of his flesh cling;
cast upon him, it does not totter.

[24] His heart is cast like stone,
and cast like a lower millstone.

[25] From his rising up gods are afraid;
from the crashings they purge themselves of sin.

[26] One who reaches him with the sword — it does not stand,
the spear, the dart, and the lance.

[27] He reckons iron as straw,
bronze as rotten wood.

[28] The son of the bow does not make him flee;
into stubble are turned for him the stones of the sling.

[29] As stubble are clubs reckoned,
and he laughs at the rattling of the javelin.

[30] Under him are the sharpest of potsherds;
he spreads a threshing sledge upon the mud.

[31] He makes the depths boil like a pot;
the sea he makes like a pot of perfume.

[32] Behind him he makes a path shine;
one would reckon the deep to be gray hair.

[33] There is not upon the dust his like,
the one made to be without dread.

[34] Every high thing he sees;
he is king over all the sons of pride."

## Changes

- **"Canst thou draw out leviathan with an hook? or his tongue with a cord which thou lettest down?" → "Can you draw out Leviathan with a hook, and with a cord press down his tongue?"** (v1) — Hebrew תִּמְשֹׁךְ … וּבְחֶבֶל תַּשְׁקִיעַ לְשֹׁנוֹ: no question word (read as a question, as the next verses); הִשְׁקִיעַ "make sink, press down"; חַכָּה "hook".
- **"Canst thou put an hook into his nose? or bore his jaw through with a thorn?" → "Can you put a rush in his nose, and with a thorn pierce his jaw?"** (v2) — Hebrew אַגְמוֹן "rush" (a cord of rush is meant; the same word in v20); חוֹחַ "thorn" (a hook of thorn); נָקַב "pierce", as 40:24.
- **"supplications … soft words" → "supplications … tender things"** (v3) — Hebrew תַּחֲנוּנִים "supplications" (pleas for favor); רַכּוֹת "tender things" ("words" is not written); אִם "or".
- **"Will he make a covenant with thee? wilt thou take him for a servant for ever?" → "Will he cut a covenant with you? Will you take him for a slave forever?"** (v4) — Hebrew יִכְרֹת בְּרִית "cut a covenant"; עֶבֶד עוֹלָם "a slave forever" (as Deut 15:17).
- **"or wilt thou bind him for thy maidens?" → "and will you bind him for your girls?"** (v5) — Hebrew וְ "and"; נַעֲרוֹת "girls".
- **"Shall the companions make a banquet of him? shall they part him among the merchants?" → "Will companions trade over him? Will they divide him among the merchants?"** (v6) — Hebrew יִכְרוּ עָלָיו: כָּרָה is "buy, trade" here (Deut 2:6; Hos 3:2), also read "make a feast" (2 Kgs 6:23); uncertain. חַבָּרִים "companions" (partners in a trade). כְּנַעֲנִים, literally "Canaanites": the word for traders (Prov 31:24; Isa 23:8); the people are not meant.
- **"barbed irons … fish spears" → "barbs … a fish spear"** (v7) — Hebrew שֻׂכּוֹת, only here (as שֵׂךְ "thorn", Num 33:55: uncertain); צִלְצַל דָּגִים, literally "a whirring thing of fish" (the word of Deut 28:42), a harpoon (uncertain). The question has הֲ.
- **"Lay thine hand upon him, remember the battle, do no more" → "Put your palm upon him; remember the battle — do not do it again!"** (v8) — Hebrew כַּפְּךָ "your palm"; מִלְחָמָה "battle" (no article: "a battle"); אַל־תּוֹסַף, a prohibition, literally "do not add".
- **"the hope of him is in vain: shall not one be cast down even at the sight of him?" → "his hope is proved a lie; is one hurled down even at the sight of him?"** (v9) — Hebrew תֹּחַלְתּוֹ "his hope" (the hope of the one who would take him, or the hope set on him); נִכְזָבָה "is proved a lie"; הֲגַם … יֻטָל "is one hurled down even …?" — the question expects "yes"; "not" is not written.
- **"None is so fierce that dare stir him up: who then is able to stand before me?" → "None is so fierce that he would stir him up; and who is he who will take his stand before me?"** (v10) — Hebrew לְפָנַי "before me" (MT; "before him", read by some Hebrew manuscripts (from memory), is not taken); הִתְיַצֵּב "take one's stand"; וּמִי "and who".
- **"Who hath prevented me, that I should repay him? whatsoever is under the whole heaven is mine" → "Who has come before me, that I should repay? Under all the heavens, it is mine"** (v11) — Hebrew הִקְדִּימַנִי "has come before me / confronted me" (KJV "prevented" = "gone before"; Rom 11:35 takes it as "first given to me"); וַאֲשַׁלֵּם "and I should repay" (שִׁלֵּם); לִי־הוּא "it is mine" ("whatever" is not written); הַשָּׁמַיִם "the heavens".
- **"I will not conceal his parts, nor his power, nor his comely proportion" → "Concerning him shall I keep silent — his limbs, and the word of mighty deeds, and the grace of his arrangement?"** (v12) — the verse follows the qere לוֹ "concerning him"; the written form (ketiv) is לֹא "not": "I will not keep silent (about) his limbs". With the qere there is no "not", and the line is read as a question that expects "no" (no question word is written, as v1); uncertain. בַּדָּיו "his parts, limbs"; דְּבַר־גְּבוּרוֹת "the word of mighty deeds" (the telling of them); חִין, only here, "grace, comeliness" (uncertain); עֶרְכּוֹ "his arrangement" (his build, the order of his parts).
- **"Who can discover the face of his garment? or who can come to him with his double bridle?" → "Who has uncovered the face of his garment? Into the double of his bridle who will come?"** (v13) — Hebrew גִּלָּה "uncover"; כֶּפֶל רִסְנוֹ "the double of his bridle" (his jaws; MT). "His double coat of mail" reads סִרְיֹנוֹ for רִסְנוֹ, a conjecture (from memory): not taken.
- **"his teeth are terrible round about" → "Around his teeth is dread"** (v14) — Hebrew סְבִיבוֹת שִׁנָּיו אֵימָה; "them" supplied in the first line.
- **"His scales are his pride, shut up together as with a close seal" → "A pride are the channels of shields, shut up with a tight seal"** (v15) — Hebrew גַּאֲוָה "pride" (MT); "his back" reads גֵּוָה, a conjecture (from memory): not taken. אֲפִיקֵי מָגִנִּים "the channels of shields" (the rows of his scales; אָפִיק as 40:18); סָגוּר חוֹתָם צָר "shut up, a tight seal" ("with" supplied).
- **"One is so near to another, that no air can come between them" → "One draws near to one, and wind does not come between them"** (v16) — Hebrew נָגַשׁ "draw near"; רוּחַ "wind" (also "breath, spirit": one word).
- **"They are joined one to another" → "Each to its brother they are made to cling"** (v17) — Hebrew אִישׁ־בְּאָחִיהוּ יְדֻבָּקוּ, literally "a man to his brother they are made to cling" (of the scales: "each"); יִתְלַכְּדוּ "they grip one another"; יִתְפָּרָדוּ "they are parted".
- **"By his neesings a light doth shine" → "His sneezings make light flash"** (v18) — Hebrew תָּהֶל "makes shine"; שַׁחַר "dawn".
- **"burning lamps … leap out" → "torches … escape"** (v19) — Hebrew לַפִּידִים "torches"; כִּידוֹדֵי, only here, "sparks"; יִתְמַלָּטוּ "slip away, escape".
- **"as out of a seething pot or caldron" → "as a pot blown upon, and a rush"** (v20) — Hebrew כְּדוּד נָפוּחַ וְאַגְמֹן: a pot fanned (to boiling) and a burning rush; אַגְמֹן is the "rush" of v2.
- **"His breath kindleth coals" → "His soul kindles coals"** (v21) — Hebrew נַפְשׁוֹ "his soul" (the word is also used of the throat and breath).
- **"and sorrow is turned into joy before him" → "and before him dances dismay"** (v22) — Hebrew תָּדוּץ, only here, "leaps, dances"; דְּאָבָה, only here, "dismay, faintness" (root "languish"); יָלִין "lodges".
- **"The flakes of his flesh are joined together: they are firm in themselves" → "The folds of his flesh cling; cast upon him, it does not totter"** (v23) — Hebrew מַפְּלֵי "folds" (uncertain); דָּבֵקוּ "cling"; יָצוּק "poured, cast" (of metal); בַּל־יִמּוֹט "it does not totter".
- **"His heart is as firm as a stone; yea, as hard as a piece of the nether millstone" → "His heart is cast like stone, and cast like a lower millstone"** (v24) — Hebrew יָצוּק twice, the word of v23 ("hard" is not written); פֶּלַח תַּחְתִּית "a lower millstone" (lit. "a lower slice").
- **"When he raiseth up himself, the mighty are afraid: by reason of breakings they purify themselves" → "From his rising up gods are afraid; from the crashings they purge themselves of sin"** (v25) — Hebrew אֵלִים, plural of אֵל: "gods" (plain word; also taken "the mighty", as אֵילִים "leaders", Exod 15:15); יָגוּרוּ "are afraid"; שְׁבָרִים "breakings, crashings"; יִתְחַטָּאוּ, hitpael of חָטָא, elsewhere "purge oneself of sin" (Num 19:12); here also taken "they miss (their way), are beside themselves" (the root sense "miss"): uncertain.
- **"The sword of him that layeth at him cannot hold: the spear, the dart, nor the habergeon" → "One who reaches him with the sword — it does not stand, the spear, the dart, and the lance"** (v26) — Hebrew מַשִּׂיגֵהוּ חֶרֶב "one reaching him — a sword"; בְּלִי תָקוּם "it does not stand"; מַסָּע "dart" (only here in this sense: uncertain); שִׁרְיָה, only here, "lance" (also taken as שִׁרְיוֹן "coat of mail", whence KJV "habergeon"): uncertain.
- **"He esteemeth iron as straw, and brass as rotten wood" → "He reckons iron as straw, bronze as rotten wood"** (v27) — Hebrew חָשַׁב "reckon"; נְחוּשָׁה "bronze".
- **"The arrow cannot make him flee: slingstones are turned with him into stubble" → "The son of the bow does not make him flee; into stubble are turned for him the stones of the sling"** (v28) — Hebrew בֶן־קָשֶׁת "son of the bow" = the arrow (kept); Hebrew order kept.
- **"Darts are counted as stubble: he laugheth at the shaking of a spear" → "As stubble are clubs reckoned, and he laughs at the rattling of the javelin"** (v29) — Hebrew תוֹתָח, only here, "club" (a singular with a plural verb); כִּידוֹן "javelin"; רַעַשׁ "rattling, quaking".
- **"Sharp stones are under him: he spreadeth sharp pointed things upon the mire" → "Under him are the sharpest of potsherds; he spreads a threshing sledge upon the mud"** (v30) — Hebrew חַדּוּדֵי חָרֶשׂ "the sharp ones of earthenware"; חָרוּץ "threshing sledge"; no "like" in the Hebrew: his underside leaves the marks of a sledge.
- **"He maketh the deep to boil like a pot: he maketh the sea like a pot of ointment" → "He makes the depths boil like a pot; the sea he makes like a pot of perfume"** (v31) — Hebrew מְצוּלָה "depths" (תְּהוֹם "the deep" is the word of v32); מֶרְקָחָה "a pot of perfume" (root רָקַח "blend perfume").
- **"one would think the deep to be hoary" → "one would reckon the deep to be gray hair"** (v32) — Hebrew יַחְשֹׁב (the verb of v27); לְשֵׂיבָה "as gray hair".
- **"Upon earth there is not his like, who is made without fear" → "There is not upon the dust his like, the one made to be without dread"** (v33) — Hebrew עַל־עָפָר "upon the dust"; מָשְׁלוֹ "his like"; חַת "dread".
- **"He beholdeth all high things: he is a king over all the children of pride" → "Every high thing he sees; he is king over all the sons of pride"** (v34) — Hebrew יִרְאֶה "sees"; בְּנֵי־שָׁחַץ "sons of pride" (as 28:8; the proud beasts are meant).

### Notes
- **The Hebrew numbering** — our 41:1–8 are Heb 40:25–32; our 41:9–34 are Heb 41:1–26. Our verse numbers are kept.
- **Leviathan** — the sea beast (Ps 74:14; 104:26; Isa 27:1); in this chapter described as a beast no man can take, tame or trade.
- **"Who has come before me, that I should repay?"** (v11) — Yahweh owes no man; Rom 11:35 has the thought.
- **End of Yahweh's speeches** — 42:1–6 is Job's answer.
