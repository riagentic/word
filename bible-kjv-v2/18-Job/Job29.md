# Job 29

[1] And ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ again lifted up his parable and said:

[2] "Would that I were as in the months of old,
as in the days when ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ kept me,

[3] when he made his lamp shine above my head,
by his light I walked through darkness;

[4] as I was in the days of my autumn,
when the secret counsel of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ was over my tent,

[5] when ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ was yet with me,
my boys around me,

[6] when my steps were washed in curds,
and a rock poured out beside me streams of oil!

[7] When I went out to the gate, up to the town,
in the square I prepared my seat;

[8] the young men saw me and hid themselves,
and the aged rose up, stood;

[9] princes held back from utterances,
and laid the palm on their mouth;

[10] the voice of leaders hid itself,
and their tongue clung to their palate.

[11] For the ear heard, and called me happy,
and the eye saw, and bore witness to me;

[12] for I rescued the afflicted who cried for help,
and the fatherless, and him who had no helper.

[13] The blessing of one perishing came upon me,
and the heart of the widow I made to sing for joy.

[14] I put on righteousness, and it put me on;
like a robe and a turban was my judgment.

[15] Eyes I was to the blind,
and feet to the lame was I.

[16] A father I was to the needy,
and the case that I did not know I searched out.

[17] And I broke in pieces the jaw-teeth of the unjust,
and from his teeth I threw away the prey.

[18] And I said, 'With my nest I shall expire,
and like the sand I shall multiply days.

[19] My root is opened to the waters,
and the dew lodges in my bough.

[20] My glory is new with me,
and my bow is renewed in my hand.'

[21] To me they listened, and hoped,
and were still for my counsel.

[22] After my word they did not speak again,
and upon them my utterance dropped.

[23] And they hoped for me as for the rain,
and their mouth they opened wide for the latter rain.

[24] I laughed to them — they did not believe;
and the light of my face they did not cause to fall.

[25] I chose their way, and I sat as head,
and I abode like a king in the band,
as one who comforts mourners."

## Changes

- **"Moreover Job continued his parable, and said" → "And Job again lifted up his parable and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף אִיּוֹב שְׂאֵת מְשָׁלוֹ וַיֹּאמַר, literally "and Job added to lift up his parable", word for word as 27:1 and with the same wording. נָשָׂא מָשָׁל "lift up one's parable", as Num 23:7.
- **"Oh that I were as in months past" → "Would that I were as in the months of old"** (v2) — Hebrew מִי־יִתְּנֵנִי, literally "who will give me"; יַרְחֵי־קֶדֶם "months of old". **"God" → "Eloah"** (v2, 4) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ. **"preserved me" → "kept me"** — Hebrew יִשְׁמְרֵנִי.
- **"When his candle shined upon my head, and when by his light I walked through darkness" → "when he made his lamp shine above my head, by his light I walked through darkness"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּהִלּוֹ, hifil of הָלַל "shine": "when he made shine"; עֲלֵי רֹאשִׁי "above my head". The second "when" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"As I was in the days of my youth" → "as I was in the days of my autumn"** (v4) — Hebrew חָרְפִּי, from חֹרֶף "autumn" (the season of ripe fruit and the first rains, also "winter"); "my prime" is meant; "youth" is not the word. **"the secret of God was upon my tabernacle" → "the secret counsel of Eloah was over my tent"** — Hebrew סוֹד, the close circle and what is said in it ("friendship", "council" in other versions); אֹהֶל "tent".
- **"When the Almighty was yet with me, when my children were about me" → "when Shaddai was yet with me, my boys around me"** (v5) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי (the meaning of the name is not certain); נְעָרַי "my boys" (children or servants: the word does not decide); the second "when" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"When I washed my steps with butter" → "when my steps were washed in curds"** (v6) — Hebrew בִּרְחֹץ הֲלִיכַי בְּחֵמָה, "in the washing of my steps in curds": חֵמָה here is a short spelling of חֶמְאָה "curds", not חֵמָה "fury". **"and the rock poured me out rivers of oil" → "and a rock poured out beside me streams of oil"** — Hebrew צוּר, without the article; עִמָּדִי "with me, beside me"; פַּלְגֵי "streams, channels".
- **"When I went out to the gate through the city, when I prepared my seat in the street!" → "When I went out to the gate, up to the town, in the square I prepared my seat"** (v7) — Hebrew עֲלֵי־קָרֶת "up to / by the town" (קֶרֶת, a poetic word for town); רְחוֹב "square" (the open place at the gate, not a street); מוֹשָׁב "seat" (the word of "dwelling place", here the place where one sits).
- **"The young men saw me, and hid themselves: and the aged arose, and stood up" → "… and the aged rose up, stood"** (v8) — Hebrew נֶחְבָּאוּ "hid themselves"; קָמוּ עָמָדוּ, two verbs with no "and".
- **"The princes refrained talking, and laid their hand on their mouth" → "princes held back from utterances, and laid the palm on their mouth"** (v9) — Hebrew עָצְרוּ בְמִלִּים, literally "restrained in utterances"; מִלָּה "utterance" (beside דָּבָר "word", v22); כַף "palm".
- **"The nobles held their peace, and their tongue cleaved to the roof of their mouth" → "the voice of leaders hid itself, and their tongue clung to their palate"** (v10) — Hebrew קוֹל־נְגִידִים נֶחְבָּאוּ, the verb of v8 (plural, agreeing with "leaders"); נָגִיד "leader"; חֵךְ "palate".
- **"When the ear heard me, then it blessed me; and when the eye saw me, it gave witness to me" → "For the ear heard, and called me happy, and the eye saw, and bore witness to me"** (v11) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; וַתְּאַשְּׁרֵנִי "and called me happy" (not בֵּרַךְ "bless"); "me" after "heard" and "saw" is not written.
- **"Because I delivered the poor that cried, and the fatherless, and him that had none to help him" → "for I rescued the afflicted who cried for help, and the fatherless, and him who had no helper"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲמַלֵּט "I rescued"; עָנִי "afflicted"; מְשַׁוֵּעַ "crying for help"; וְלֹא־עֹזֵר לוֹ, literally "and no helper to him": a third person, or the fatherless himself.
- **"The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me: and I caused the widow's heart to sing for joy" → "The blessing of one perishing came upon me, and the heart of the widow I made to sing for joy"** (v13) — Hebrew אֹבֵד "perishing"; אַרְנִן "I made sing for joy".
- **"I put on righteousness, and it clothed me: my judgment was as a robe and a diadem" → "I put on righteousness, and it put me on; like a robe and a turban was my judgment"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיִּלְבָּשֵׁנִי, the same verb as "I put on": "and it put me on". מִשְׁפָּטִי "my judgment" (= the right I gave). צָנִיף "turban" (of the root of מִצְנֶפֶת, the priest's turban).
- **"I was eyes to the blind, and feet was I to the lame" → "Eyes I was to the blind, and feet to the lame was I"** (v15) — the Hebrew order (each line opens and closes on the word that matters).
- **"I was a father to the poor: and the cause which I knew not I searched out" → "A father I was to the needy, and the case that I did not know I searched out"** (v16) — Hebrew אֶבְיוֹנִים "needy"; רִיב "case"; אֶחְקְרֵהוּ "I searched it out" (חָקַר "examine"). "Which I knew not" may be the case of one I did not know: the Hebrew leaves it open.
- **"And I brake the jaws of the wicked, and plucked the spoil out of his teeth" → "And I broke in pieces the jaw-teeth of the unjust, and from his teeth I threw away the prey"** (v17) — Hebrew וָאֲשַׁבְּרָה "and I broke in pieces"; מְתַלְּעוֹת "jaw-teeth, fangs"; עַוָּל "unjust" (as 27:7); אַשְׁלִיךְ "I threw"; טֶרֶף "prey".
- **"Then I said, I shall die in my nest, and I shall multiply my days as the sand" → "And I said, 'With my nest I shall expire, and like the sand I shall multiply days'"** (v18) — Hebrew עִם־קִנִּי "with my nest" (with my household); אֶגְוָע "I shall expire"; כַחוֹל "like the sand" (a Jewish tradition reads חוֹל here as the long-lived bird, the phoenix: cited from memory); יָמִים "days", without "my".
- **"My root was spread out by the waters, and the dew lay all night upon my branch" → "My root is opened to the waters, and the dew lodges in my bough"** (v19) — Hebrew פָתוּחַ "opened"; יָלִין "lodges, spends the night"; קָצִיר here "bough" (the same letters as "harvest").
- **"My glory was fresh in me" → "My glory is new with me"** (v20) — Hebrew חָדָשׁ "new"; עִמָּדִי "with me". **"my bow was renewed in my hand"** — Hebrew תַחֲלִיף, hifil of חָלַף "change, renew".
- **"Unto me men gave ear, and waited, and kept silence at my counsel" → "To me they listened, and hoped, and were still for my counsel"** (v21) — "men" is not in the Hebrew; יִחֵלּוּ "they hoped" (קִוָּה is "wait for"); יִדְּמוּ "they were still"; עֵצָה "counsel".
- **"After my words they spake not again; and my speech dropped upon them" → "After my word they did not speak again, and upon them my utterance dropped"** (v22) — Hebrew דְבָרִי "my word" (singular); לֹא יִשְׁנוּ "they did not repeat"; מִלָּתִי "my utterance" (beside דָּבָר "word").
- **"And they waited for me as for the rain; and they opened their mouth wide as for the latter rain" → "And they hoped for me as for the rain, and their mouth they opened wide for the latter rain"** (v23) — Hebrew יִחֲלוּ "they hoped" (as v21); מַלְקוֹשׁ "latter rain"; the second "as" is not written.
- **"If I laughed on them, they believed it not; and the light of my countenance they cast not down" → "I laughed to them — they did not believe; and the light of my face they did not cause to fall"** (v24) — Hebrew אֶשְׂחַק אֲלֵהֶם "I laughed to them", with no "if"; לֹא יַאֲמִינוּ "they did not believe" (that I would laugh to them; or "when they had no confidence"); יַפִּילוּן "they caused to fall". פָּנִים "face".
- **"I chose out their way, and sat chief, and dwelt as a king in the army, as one that comforteth the mourners" → "I chose their way, and I sat as head, and I abode like a king in the band, as one who comforts mourners"** (v25) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ "head"; אֶשְׁכּוֹן "I abode" (שָׁכַן, beside יָשַׁב "sit, dwell" in the same verse); גְּדוּד "band" (a body of fighting men).

### Notes
- **Job's look back** (ch. 29–31) — he recalls his former days, then his present (ch. 30), then swears his innocence (ch. 31).
- **The elder at the gate** (v7–10) — young and old, princes and leaders gave way before him.
- **What he did** (v12–17) — the afflicted, the fatherless, the widow, the blind, the lame, the needy: set against Eliphaz's charges (ch. 22).
- **Curds and oil** (v6) — pictures of plenty.
- **Glory new, bow renewed** (v20) — strength that did not fail. The reversal follows in ch. 30.
