# Job 29

[1] ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ continued his parable and said:

[2] "Oh that I were as in months past,
as in the days when ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ watched over me,

[3] when his lamp shone upon my head,
and by his light I walked through darkness;

[4] as I was in the autumn of my days,
when the friendship of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ was upon my tent,

[5] when ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ was yet with me,
when my young ones were around me,

[6] when my steps were bathed in curds,
and the rock poured out streams of oil for me!

[7] When I went out to the gate of the city,
when I prepared my seat in the square,

[8] the young men saw me and withdrew,
and the aged arose and stood;

[9] officials refrained from talking
and laid their hand on their mouth;

[10] the voices of nobles were hushed,
their tongue cleaved to the roof of their mouth.

[11] When the ear heard me, it blessed me;
when the eye saw me, it approved of me,

[12] because I rescued the poor who cried,
and the fatherless who had none to help.

[13] The blessing of him who was about to perish came upon me;
I made the widow's heart sing for joy.

[14] I put on righteousness — and it clothed me;
my justice was like a robe and turban.

[15] I was eyes to the blind
and feet to the lame.

[16] I was a father to the needy;
the case of one I did not know I investigated.

[17] I broke the fangs of the unrighteous
and snatched prey from his teeth.

[18] Then I thought, 'I shall die in my nest;
I shall multiply my days as the sand.

[19] My roots spread out to the waters,
with the dew all night on my branches.

[20] My glory was ever fresh with me,
and my bow was renewed in my hand.'

[21] To me men listened and waited;
they kept silence for my counsel.

[22] After I spoke they did not speak again;
my word dropped upon them.

[23] They waited for me as for the rain;
they opened their mouths as for the latter rain.

[24] If I smiled at them, they could not believe it;
the light of my face they did not let fall.

[25] I chose out their way, sat as chief,
and lived like a king among his troops,
one who comforts mourners."

## Changes

- **"butter" → "curds"** (v6) — *chemah*.
- **"judgment" → "justice"** (v14) — *mishpat*.
- **"diadem" → "turban"** (v14) — *tzaniph*.
- **"the days of my youth" → "the autumn of my days"** (v4) — Hebrew *chorfi*; debated. Either "my autumn/ripe days" (prosperity-season) or "my youth"; moderns often prefer autumn imagery fitting with "harvest of blessing."
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v9) — *sarim*.

### Notes
- **Job's autobiography begins** — recalling former prosperity.
- **Honored elder at city-gate** (v7-10) — Job chaired civic affairs; all deferred to him.
- **Social justice record** (v12-17) — rescues poor, fatherless, widows, blind, lame. Defies Eliphaz's false accusations (ch 22).
- **"Bathed in curds and oil"** — prosperity imagery.
- **Fresh glory, bow renewed** (v20) — never weakening. The tragic reversal will come in ch 30.
- **"As a king among troops"** — communal leadership, not just wealth.
