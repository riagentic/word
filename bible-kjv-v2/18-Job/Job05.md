# Job 5

[1] "Call now — is there anyone to answer you?
To which of the holy ones — will you turn?

[2] For vexation kills the fool,
and envy slays the simple.

[3] I have seen the fool taking root,
but suddenly I cursed his dwelling.

[4] His children are far from safety;
they are crushed in the gate,
and no one delivers them.

[5] The hungry eat his harvest —
taking it even from among the thorns;
the thirsty gulp their wealth.

[6] Affliction does not come from the dust,
nor does trouble sprout from the ground;

[7] but man is born to trouble,
as the sparks fly upward.

[8] But as for me, I would seek *Elohim*,
and to *Elohim* I would commit my cause —

[9] who does great things beyond searching,
wondrous things beyond number,

[10] who gives rain upon the earth,
sends water upon the fields,

[11] setting the lowly on high,
so those who mourn are lifted to safety.

[12] He frustrates the schemes of the crafty,
so their hands cannot perform their enterprise.

[13] He catches the wise in their craftiness;
the counsel of the cunning is swept away.

[14] They meet with darkness by day,
and grope at noon as in the night.

[15] But he saves the needy from the sword of their mouth,
and from the hand of the mighty.

[16] So the poor have hope,
and injustice shuts its mouth.

[17] Behold, blessed is the man whom Eloah reproves.
Therefore do not despise the discipline — of Shaddai;

[18] for he wounds, but he binds up;
he strikes, but his hands heal.

[19] He will deliver you from six troubles;
in seven no evil will touch you.

[20] In famine he will redeem you from death,
and in war from the power of the sword.

[21] You shall be hidden from the scourge of the tongue,
and shall not fear destruction when it comes.

[22] At destruction and famine you will laugh,
and you shall not fear the beasts of the earth.

[23] For you shall be in league with the stones of the field,
and the beasts of the field shall be at peace with you.

[24] You shall know that your tent is in peace;
you shall visit your fold and miss nothing.

[25] You shall know that your offspring will be many,
your descendants as the grass of the earth.

[26] You shall come to the grave in ripe old age,
as a stack of sheaves gathered in its season.

[27] Behold, we have searched this out, and it is so;
hear it, and know it for yourself."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"tabernacle" → "tent"** (v24).
- **"shock of corn" → "stack of sheaves"** (v26).

### Notes
- **Eliphaz continues** — points to universal providence. Humans born to trouble like sparks flying up (v7) — famous image.
- **"Six... seven troubles"** (v19) — numerical parallelism; encompassing.
- **Promise pattern** — if Job submits to divine discipline, restoration. The ground of Job's problem: he already is innocent; friends' counsel assumes the opposite.
- **1 Cor 3:19 cites v13** — "He catches the wise in their craftiness" (Paul quotes this verse directly).
