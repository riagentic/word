# Job 35

[1] Elihu continued and said:

[2] "Do you think this to be right —
do you say, 'My righteousness is more than ⟦el|El|God⟧'s'?

[3] For you ask, 'What advantage is it to you?
What do I profit more than if I had sinned?'

[4] I will answer you and your companions with you.

[5] Look to the heavens and see;
observe the clouds that are higher than you.

[6] If you have sinned, what do you do against him?
If your transgressions are many, what harm do you do him?

[7] If you are righteous, what do you give him?
What does he receive from your hand?

[8] Your wickedness affects a man like yourself,
and your righteousness a son of man.

[9] Because of the multitude of oppressions men cry out;
they cry for help because of the arm of the mighty.

[10] But none says, 'Where is ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ my Maker,
who gives songs in the night,

[11] who teaches us more than the beasts of the earth,
and makes us wiser than the birds of the heavens?'

[12] There they cry out, but he does not answer,
because of the pride of evil men.

[13] Surely ⟦el|El|God⟧ does not listen to an empty cry;
⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ regards it not.

[14] How much less when you say you do not see him,
that your case is before him,
and you are waiting for him!

[15] And now, because he has not visited in his anger,
and has not greatly noticed transgression,

[16] ⟦job|Iyov|Job⟧ opens his mouth in empty talk;
he multiplies words without knowledge."

## Changes

- **"God" → "El"** (v2, 13) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"vanity" → "empty cry"** (v13) — *shav*.
- **"in great extremity" → "noticed transgression"** (v15) — Hebrew disputed.
- **"wait" / "trust" in v14 rephrased** for clarity.

### Notes
- **Elihu's third speech** — divine impassibility theme.
- **Eloah is unaffected by human sin/righteousness** (v5-8) — only other humans feel impact. Philosophical claim: divine transcendence.
- **"Songs in the night"** (v10) — beautiful phrase; Yahweh as joy-giver in darkness.
- **Hollow cries ignored** (v13) — only sincere cries reach Yahweh.
- **Charges Job with ignorance** (v16) — repeats the concluding judgment of 34:35-37.
