# Job 11

[1] And ⟦zophar|Tzofar|Zophar⟧ the ⟦naamathite|Naamati|Naamathite⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Shall a multitude of words not be answered?
And shall a man of lips be righteous?

[3] Your empty talk makes men silent,
and you mock, and there is none who puts to shame.

[4] And you say, 'My teaching is pure,
and I am clean in your eyes.'

[5] But oh that ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ would speak,
and open his lips with you,

[6] and tell you the hidden things of wisdom,
for sound wisdom is twofold!
And know that ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ lets part of your iniquity be forgotten for you.

[7] Can you find the searching out of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧?
Or can you find unto the limit of ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧?

[8] Heights of the heavens — what can you work?
Deeper than Sheol — what can you know?

[9] Longer than the earth is its measure,
and broader than the sea.

[10] If he passes on and shuts up,
and calls an assembly, then who can turn him back?

[11] For he knows empty men,
and he sees wickedness and does not consider.

[12] And a hollow man will get a heart,
and a wild donkey's colt is born a human.

[13] If you yourself prepare your heart
and spread out your palm to him —

[14] if wickedness is in your hand, put it far away,
and let not injustice abide in your tents —

[15] for then you will lift up your face without blemish,
and you will be firmly cast and will not fear.

[16] For you yourself will forget toil;
as waters that have passed over you will remember it.

[17] And a lifetime will rise above the noonday;
should it grow dark, it will be like the morning.

[18] And you will trust, for there is hope,
and you will search about, in security you will lie down.

[19] And you will lie, and there is none who makes you tremble,
and many will beseech your face.

[20] And the eyes of the wicked will wear out,
and a place of flight has perished from them,
and their hope is the breathing out of the soul."

## Changes

- **"Then … answered and said" → "And … answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the speech headings in Job.
- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v5, 6, 7) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ. **"the Almighty" → "Shaddai"** (v7) — Hebrew שַׁדַּי.
- **"Should not the multitude of words be answered? and should a man full of talk be justified?" → "Shall a multitude of words not be answered? And shall a man of lips be righteous?"** (v2) — Hebrew רֹב דְּבָרִים "a multitude of words"; אִישׁ שְׂפָתַיִם "a man of lips" (= a talker; the Hebrew idiom kept); יִצְדָּק "be righteous".
- **"Should thy lies make men hold their peace? and when thou mockest, shall no man make thee ashamed?" → "Your empty talk makes men silent, and you mock, and there is none who puts to shame"** (v3) — Hebrew בַּדֶּיךָ "your empty talk, your boasts" (בַּדִּים, not "lies" in itself); מְתִים "men"; יַחֲרִישׁוּ "make silent". The verse has no question particle; most versions carry on the questions of v2 (so KJV); as written it is a statement. מַכְלִם "one who puts to shame" ("you" is not written).
- **"My doctrine is pure, and I am clean in thine eyes" → "My teaching is pure, and I am clean in your eyes"** (v4) — Hebrew לִקְחִי "my teaching" (lit. "what is received"); זַךְ "pure"; בַּר "clean"; הָיִיתִי "I am (have been)". "Your eyes" are those of Eloah, to whom Job is said to speak.
- **"But oh that God would speak, and open his lips against thee" → "But oh that Eloah would speak, and open his lips with you"** (v5) — Hebrew מִי־יִתֵּן "who will give" = "oh that"; עִמָּךְ "with you".
- **"And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is! Know therefore that God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth" → "and tell you the hidden things of wisdom, for sound wisdom is twofold! And know that Eloah lets part of your iniquity be forgotten for you"** (v6) — Hebrew וְיַגֶּד "and tell"; תַּעֲלֻמוֹת "hidden things"; כִּי־כִפְלַיִם לְתוּשִׁיָּה, literally "for double to sound wisdom" (כִּפְלַיִם "double, twofold"; תּוּשִׁיָּה "sound wisdom, success"); the sense is uncertain. יַשֶּׁה, hifil of נָשָׁה "forget" (as 39:17), "causes to be forgotten"; מֵעֲוֺנֶךָ "from (= part of) your iniquity". KJV and the earlier rendering take the verb from נָשָׁה "lend, exact" ("exacts of you less than"). The earlier text split the third line in two; the Hebrew has three lines.
- **"Canst thou by searching find out God? canst thou find out the Almighty unto perfection?" → "Can you find the searching out of Eloah? Or can you find unto the limit of Shaddai?"** (v7) — Hebrew הַחֵקֶר אֱלוֹהַּ, "the searching (what is to be searched out) of Eloah" (חֵקֶר as 8:8; 9:10; KJV "by searching"); אִם "or"; עַד־תַּכְלִית "unto the limit".
- **"It is as high as heaven; what canst thou do? deeper than hell; what canst thou know?" → "Heights of the heavens — what can you work? Deeper than Sheol — what can you know?"** (v8) — Hebrew גָּבְהֵי שָׁמַיִם "heights of the heavens" ("it is" is not written); תִּפְעָל "can you work" (פָּעַל); שְׁאוֹל "Sheol", not "hell".
- **"If he cut off, and shut up, or gather together, then who can hinder him?" → "If he passes on and shuts up, and calls an assembly, then who can turn him back?"** (v10) — Hebrew יַחֲלֹף "passes on" (as 9:11); יַסְגִּיר "shuts up" (no object written: a man is meant); יַקְהִיל "assembles", the hifil of קָהָל "assembly" (= for judgment): "an assembly" is supplied. וּמִי "and who". The earlier text put three verbs in one line; the Hebrew divides after the second.
- **"For he knoweth vain men: he seeth wickedness also; will he not then consider it?" → "For he knows empty men, and he sees wickedness and does not consider"** (v11) — Hebrew מְתֵי־שָׁוְא "men of emptiness"; אָוֶן "wickedness"; וְלֹא יִתְבּוֹנָן "and does not consider". There is no question particle: it is read as "without needing to consider" (he sees at once), or, with most versions, as a question "and will he not consider?"; the words are kept as written.
- **"For vain man would be wise, though man be born like a wild ass's colt" → "And a hollow man will get a heart, and a wild donkey's colt is born a human"** (v12) — Hebrew אִישׁ נָבוּב "a hollow man"; יִלָּבֵב "get a heart" (= understanding; the verb is made from לֵבָב "heart"); עַיִר פֶּרֶא "a colt, a wild donkey"; אָדָם "a human" (אִישׁ is "man": the two words kept apart). The two lines are joined only by "and"; the proverb says that the one is as likely as the other.
- **"If thou prepare thine heart, and stretch out thine hands toward him" → "If you yourself prepare your heart and spread out your palm to him"** (v13) — Hebrew אַתָּה "you" written out; הֲכִינוֹתָ "prepare, make firm"; פָרַשְׂתָּ "spread out"; כַּפֶּךָ "your palm" (singular). The condition runs through v14 to v15.
- **"If iniquity be in thine hand, put it far away, and let not wickedness dwell in thy tabernacles" → "if wickedness is in your hand, put it far away, and let not injustice abide in your tents"** (v14) — Hebrew אָוֶן "wickedness"; עַוְלָה "injustice"; תַּשְׁכֵּן "let abide" (hifil of שָׁכַן "abide"); אֹהָלֶיךָ "your tents".
- **"For then shalt thou lift up thy face without spot; yea, thou shalt be stedfast, and shalt not fear" → "for then you will lift up your face without blemish, and you will be firmly cast and will not fear"** (v15) — Hebrew מִמּוּם "from blemish" (= without); מֻצָק "cast, poured" (as metal; passive of יָצַק "pour"): firm.
- **"Because thou shalt forget thy misery, and remember it as waters that pass away" → "For you yourself will forget toil; as waters that have passed over you will remember it"** (v16) — Hebrew אַתָּה "you" written out; עָמָל "toil" (no "your"); עָבְרוּ "have passed over"; "it" after "remember" is supplied.
- **"And thine age shall be clearer than the noonday; thou shalt shine forth, thou shalt be as the morning" → "And a lifetime will rise above the noonday; should it grow dark, it will be like the morning"** (v17) — Hebrew חֶלֶד "lifetime, duration" (no "your"); יָקוּם "will rise"; מִצָּהֳרַיִם "beyond (more than) the noonday" ("brighter" is the sense); תָּעֻפָה, from עוּף "be dark" (Amos 4:13 עֵיפָה): "(though) it grows dark"; KJV takes it from יָפַע "shine". The word is uncertain.
- **"And thou shalt be secure, because there is hope; yea, thou shalt dig about thee, and thou shalt take thy rest in safety" → "And you will trust, for there is hope, and you will search about, in security you will lie down"** (v18) — Hebrew בָטַחְתָּ "you will trust"; חָפַרְתָּ "you will search about" (lit. "dig", the word of searching out a land); לָבֶטַח "in security"; תִּשְׁכָּב "you will lie down".
- **"Also thou shalt lie down, and none shall make thee afraid; yea, many shall make suit unto thee" → "And you will lie, and there is none who makes you tremble, and many will beseech your face"** (v19) — Hebrew רָבַצְתָּ "you will lie" (stretched out at rest, as a beast; שָׁכַב "lie down" is v18); מַחֲרִיד "one who makes tremble"; חִלּוּ פָנֶיךָ "beseech your face" (lit. "make your face soft").
- **"But the eyes of the wicked shall fail, and they shall not escape, and their hope shall be as the giving up of the ghost" → "And the eyes of the wicked will wear out, and a place of flight has perished from them, and their hope is the breathing out of the soul"** (v20) — Hebrew תִּכְלֶינָה "wear out"; מָנוֹס "a place of flight"; אָבַד "has perished"; מַפַּח־נָפֶשׁ "the breathing out of the soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul"); "only" is not written.

### Notes
- **Zophar's first speech** — harshest of the three friends. Dismisses Job's words as empty talk.
- **"Lets part of your iniquity be forgotten"** (v6) — says Job's guilt is heavier than his suffering.
- **Divine inscrutability** (v7–9) — cosmic dimensions; Eloah cannot be searched out.
- **"A wild donkey's colt born a human"** (v12) — proverbial impossibility. Zophar doubts that Job can learn.
- **Standard repentance-prosperity formula** (v13–19) — if Job purifies himself, blessings return.
- **The breathing out of the soul** (v20) — stark contrast, closing warning.
