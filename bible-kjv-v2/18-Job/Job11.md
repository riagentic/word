# Job 11

[1] Then Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

[2] "Should not this multitude of words be answered?
Should a man full of talk be justified?

[3] Should your empty boasts silence men?
When you mock, shall no one shame you?

[4] For you say, 'My doctrine is pure,
and I am clean in your eyes.'

[5] But oh that Eloah would speak,
and open his lips against you,

[6] and tell you the secrets of wisdom,
for wisdom is many-sided!
Know then that Eloah extracts from you
less than your iniquity deserves.

[7] Can you fathom the depths of Eloah?
Can you find the limit of Shaddai?

[8] It is higher than the heavens — what can you do?
Deeper than Sheol — what can you know?

[9] Its measure is longer than the earth
and wider than the sea.

[10] If he passes by, imprisons, and summons judgment,
who can turn him back?

[11] For he knows deceitful men;
he sees iniquity; will he not consider it?

[12] A hollow man will get understanding
when a wild donkey's colt is born a man.

[13] If you set your heart right
and stretch out your hands to him,

[14] if iniquity is in your hand, put it far away;
let no injustice dwell in your tent.

[15] Then you shall lift up your face without blemish;
you shall be secure and not fear.

[16] You will forget your trouble;
you will remember it as waters that have passed by.

[17] Your life will be brighter than noon;
its darkness will be like the morning.

[18] You will have confidence because there is hope;
you will look around and lie down in safety.

[19] You will lie down, and no one will make you afraid;
many will court your favor.

[20] But the eyes of the wicked will fail;
all refuge is lost to them;
their only hope is to breathe their last."

## Changes

- **"hell" → "Sheol"** (v8).
- **"vain man" → "hollow man"** (v12) — *ish navuv*.
- **"giving up of the ghost" → "to breathe their last"** (v20) — *mappach-nefesh*.
- **"double to that which is" → "many-sided"** (v6) — *kiflayim le-tushiyyah*; doubled/multi-faceted.

### Notes
- **Zophar's first speech** — harshest of the three friends. Dismisses Job's words as chatter.
- **"Extracts less than you deserve"** (v6) — accuses Job of guilt heavier than the suffering.
- **Divine inscrutability** (v7-9) — cosmic dimensions; can't know Yahweh fully.
- **"Wild donkey's colt become man"** (v12) — proverbial impossibility. Zophar doubts Job's humility.
- **Standard repentance-prosperity formula** (v13-19) — if Job purifies himself, blessings restored.
- **Wicked's hope = exhaling last breath** — stark contrast, closing warning.
