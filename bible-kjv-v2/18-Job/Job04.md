# Job 4

[1] Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

[2] "If one ventures a word with you, will you be weary?
But who can restrain himself from speaking?

[3] Behold, you have instructed many,
and you have strengthened the weak hands.

[4] Your words have upheld him who was stumbling;
you have made firm the feeble knees.

[5] But now it has come upon you, and you are weary;
it touches you, and you are dismayed.

[6] Is not your fear of *Elohim* your confidence,
and the integrity of your ways your hope?

[7] Remember: who, being innocent, ever perished?
Where were the upright cut off?

[8] As I have seen, those who plow iniquity
and sow trouble reap the same.

[9] By the breath of Eloah they perish;
by the blast of his nostril they are consumed.

[10] The roar of the lion, the voice of the fierce lion,
and the teeth of the young lions are broken.

[11] The old lion perishes for lack of prey,
and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.

[12] A word came to me in secret;
my ear caught a whisper of it.

[13] In disquieting thoughts from the visions of the night,
when deep sleep falls on men,

[14] fear came upon me, and trembling,
which made all my bones to shake.

[15] A spirit glided past my face;
the hair of my flesh stood up.

[16] It stood still, but I could not discern its appearance;
a form was before my eyes;
there was silence, then I heard a voice:

[17] 'Can man — mortal man — be more righteous than Eloah?
Can a man be more pure than his Maker?

[18] Behold, he puts no trust in his servants;
his Messengers he charges with error.

[19] How much more those who dwell in houses of clay,
whose foundation is in the dust,
who are crushed like a moth!

[20] Between morning and evening they are broken in pieces;
without anyone regarding it, they perish forever.

[21] Is not their tent-cord plucked up within them?
They die, yet without wisdom.'"

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"assay to commune with thee" → "ventures a word with you"** (v2).
- **"angels" → "Messengers"** (v18).
- **"charged with folly" → "charges with error"** (v18) — *tohŏlah*; rare word; possibly "folly" or "error."
- **"excellency which is in them" → "tent-cord"** (v21) — Hebrew *yitram*; debated — either "their excellence" or "their tent-cord"; the latter matches the mortal-impermanence imagery.

### Notes
- **Eliphaz first** — opens the cycles of speeches. Tactful opening; moves to theological challenge.
- **"Who ever perished, being innocent?"** (v7) — retributive orthodoxy: suffering = guilt, prosperity = righteousness. The challenge of Job is precisely this equation.
- **Lion imagery** (v10-11) — wicked are as predators now fallen.
- **Visionary experience** (v12-16) — Eliphaz reports a nocturnal revelation. *Ruach* passing; voice speaking.
- **"Can mortal be more righteous than Eloah?"** — rhetorical; points to universal human inadequacy. But Job's question is different: can a just person suffer without cause?
- **Angels charged with error** — even divine servants fallible; certainly humans.
- **"Houses of clay" / "foundation in dust"** — human mortality theme.
