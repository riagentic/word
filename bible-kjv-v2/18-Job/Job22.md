# Job 22

[1] And ⟦eliphaz|Elifaz|Eliphaz⟧ the ⟦temanite|Temani|Temanite⟧ answered and said:

[2] "Can a man be of use to ⟦el|El|God⟧?
For one who has insight is of use to himself.

[3] Is it a delight to ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ that you are righteous,
or gain, that you make your ways blameless?

[4] Is it from your fear that he reproves you,
that he comes with you into judgment?

[5] Is not your evil great,
and no end to your iniquities?

[6] For you take pledges from your brothers for nothing,
and strip off the garments of the naked.

[7] You give no water to the weary to drink,
and from the hungry you hold back bread.

[8] And the man of arm — the land is his,
and the one whose face is lifted up dwells in it.

[9] Widows you have sent away empty,
and the arms of the fatherless are crushed.

[10] Therefore traps are all around you,
and dread dismays you suddenly;

[11] or darkness — you do not see,
and an abundance of waters covers you.

[12] Is not ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ in the height of the heavens?
And see the top of the stars, how high they are!

[13] And you say, 'What does ⟦el|El|God⟧ know?
Does he judge through the dark cloud?

[14] Thick clouds are a covering to him, and he does not see,
and he walks about on the circle of the heavens.'

[15] Will you keep the path of old
that men of wickedness have trodden,

[16] who were seized when it was not the time,
their foundation poured out as a river,

[17] who said to ⟦el|El|God⟧, 'Depart from us,'
and, 'What can ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ do for them?'

[18] And he filled their houses with good —
and the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

[19] The righteous see and are glad,
and the innocent derides them:

[20] 'Surely the one risen against us is cut off,
and what is left of them fire has consumed.'

[21] Get acquainted with him, please, and be at peace;
by them good shall come to you.

[22] Take, please, ⟦torah|Torah|instruction⟧ from his mouth,
and put his sayings in your heart.

[23] If you return to ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧, you shall be built up;
you put injustice far from your tent,

[24] and set gold ore on the dust,
and ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧ among the rock of the wadis,

[25] and ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ shall be your gold ore,
and silver of the heights to you.

[26] For then you shall delight yourself in ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧
and lift up your face to ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧.

[27] You shall plead with him, and he will hear you,
and your vows you shall repay.

[28] And you shall decree a saying, and it shall stand for you,
and on your ways light shall shine.

[29] When they make low, you say, 'Lifting up!' —
and the one bowed down of eyes he saves.

[30] He rescues one not innocent,
and he escapes by the purity of your palms."

## Changes

- **"Then … answered and said" → "And … answered and said"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמַר, the one formula of the book.
- **"God" → "El"** (v2, 13, 17) — Hebrew אֵל *El*; **"Eloah"** (v12, 26) — אֱלוֹהַּ; **"Shaddai"** (v3, 17, 23, 25, 26) — שַׁדַּי, traditionally "the Almighty" (the meaning is not certain).
- **"Can a man be profitable unto God, as he that is wise may be profitable unto himself?" → "Can a man be of use to El? For one who has insight is of use to himself"** (v2) — Hebrew גֶּבֶר, a man in his strength ("mighty man" where אֱנוֹשׁ, אָדָם or אִישׁ stands beside it; alone "man", as the Job row); סָכַן "be of use"; מַשְׂכִּיל "one who has insight"; כִּי "for", also read "surely" or "though"; עָלֵימוֹ, literally "upon them", a poetic form used of one person.
- **"Is it any pleasure" → "Is it a delight"** (v3) — Hebrew חֵפֶץ. **"gain"** — בֶּצַע, elsewhere "unjust gain" (WORDS-HE.md); here only profit is meant, so plain "gain". **"makest thy ways perfect" → "make your ways blameless"** — תַתֵּם, hifil of תמם, the root of תָּם "blameless".
- **"Will he reprove thee for fear of thee? will he enter with thee into judgment?" → "Is it from your fear that he reproves you, that he comes with you into judgment?"** (v4) — Hebrew הֲמִיִּרְאָתְךָ "is it from your fear": Job's fear of Eloah, his piety (as 4:6); "of him" and "of thee" are not written. יָבוֹא "comes".
- **"thy wickedness" → "your evil"** (v5) — Hebrew רָעָה. **"infinite" → "no end to"** — וְאֵין־קֵץ, literally "and there is no end".
- **Tenses** (v6–7) — the Hebrew has imperfects (תַחְבֹּל, תַּפְשִׁיט, תַּשְׁקֶה, תִּמְנַע): what Job does, or used to do; KJV's "hast taken" turns them into one past act. v9 has a perfect, "you have sent away".
- **"taken a pledge from thy brother for nought" → "take pledges from your brothers for nothing"** (v6) — Hebrew תַחְבֹּל אַחֶיךָ חִנָּם, literally "you pledge your brothers for nothing" (plural; with no debt behind it). **"stripped the naked of their clothing" → "strip off the garments of the naked"** — בִּגְדֵי עֲרוּמִּים תַּפְשִׁיט.
- **"withholden" → "hold back"** (v7) — Hebrew מָנַע.
- **"But as for the mighty man, he had the earth; and the honourable man dwelt in it" → "And the man of arm — the land is his, and the one whose face is lifted up dwells in it"** (v8) — Hebrew אִישׁ זְרוֹעַ "a man of arm" (of strength); לוֹ הָאָרֶץ "to him is the land", no verb; נְשׂוּא פָנִים "lifted up of face" (held in honor; the phrase of נָשָׂא פָנִים "show regard"). The verse breaks the address to Job; whether it describes Job's favorites or the powerful generally is not said.
- **"have been broken" → "are crushed"** (v9) — Hebrew יְדֻכָּא, singular verb with the plural "arms".
- **"snares" → "traps"** (v10) — Hebrew פַּחִים. **"sudden fear troubleth thee" → "dread dismays you suddenly"** — פַּחַד "dread", בִּהֵל "dismay", פִּתְאֹם "suddenly".
- **"Or darkness, that thou canst not see" → "or darkness — you do not see"** (v11) — Hebrew אוֹ־חֹשֶׁךְ לֹא־תִרְאֶה; the MT "or" is kept (a common conjecture reads אוֹר "light"). שִׁפְעַת־מַיִם "an abundance of waters".
- **"in the height of heaven … the height of the stars" → "in the height of the heavens … the top of the stars"** (v12) — Hebrew גֹּבַהּ שָׁמַיִם, literally "the height of the heavens", "in" supplied; רֹאשׁ כּוֹכָבִים, literally "the head of the stars", the highest of them.
- **"How doth God know? can he judge through the dark cloud?" → "What does El know? Does he judge through the dark cloud?"** (v13) — Hebrew מַה־יָּדַע "what does he know"; עֲרָפֶל "dark cloud".
- **"he walketh in the circuit of heaven" → "he walks about on the circle of the heavens"** (v14) — Hebrew חוּג "circle" (as Isa 40:22); הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about". עָבִים "thick clouds"; סֵתֶר "covering, hiding place".
- **"Hast thou marked the old way" → "Will you keep the path of old"** (v15) — Hebrew הַאֹרַח עוֹלָם תִּשְׁמֹר, "the path of the age" (of old); תִּשְׁמֹר "keep". מְתֵי־אָוֶן "men of wickedness".
- **"Which were cut down out of time, whose foundation was overflown with a flood" → "who were seized when it was not the time, their foundation poured out as a river"** (v16) — Hebrew קֻמְּטוּ, of קָמַט (also 16:8): "seize" (uncertain; also taken as "shrivel"); וְלֹא־עֵת "and not the time"; נָהָר יוּצַק יְסוֹדָם, literally "a river — their foundation is poured out": "as" is supplied; also read "a river was poured out on their foundation".
- **"what can the Almighty do for them?" kept** (v17) — Hebrew לָמוֹ "for them / to them", inside the words of the wicked; many versions read "for us" (from memory). סוּר מִמֶּנּוּ "depart from us".
- **"good things" → "good"** (v18) — Hebrew טוֹב. The second line repeats 21:16 word for word.
- **"laugh them to scorn" → "derides them"** (v19) — Hebrew יִלְעַג־לָמוֹ.
- **"Whereas our substance is not cut down, but the remnant of them the fire consumeth" → "Surely the one risen against us is cut off, and what is left of them fire has consumed"** (v20) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא, the oath form, literally "if not": by its force "surely". קִימָנוּ is found only here: "the one risen against us" (from קוּם; uncertain; KJV "our substance"). יִתְרָם "their remainder" (or "their abundance").
- **"Acquaint now thyself with him, and be at peace: thereby good shall come unto thee" → "Get acquainted with him, please, and be at peace; by them good shall come to you"** (v21) — Hebrew הַסְכֶּן־נָא, hifil of the סָכַן "be of use" of v2 (the one root, here "be acquainted"); נָא "please"; וּשְׁלם the verb "be whole, be at peace" (not the noun שָׁלוֹם: no token); בָּהֶם "by them" (plural). תְּבוֹאַתְךָ is an unusual form: "it shall come to you", or the noun "your yield (shall be) good".
- **"Receive, I pray thee, the law … lay up his words" → "Take, please, Torah … put his sayings"** (v22) — Hebrew קַח־נָא; תּוֹרָה "instruction, teaching"; אֲמָרָיו "his sayings"; שִׂים "put".
- **"thou shalt put away iniquity far from thy tabernacles" → "you put injustice far from your tent"** (v23) — Hebrew תַּרְחִיק עַוְלָה מֵאָהֳלֶךָ, singular "tent"; no "if" or "then" is written: the clause may go on with the condition or begin the promise. עַד־שַׁדַּי "to Shaddai", literally "as far as".
- **"Then shalt thou lay up gold as dust, and the gold of Ophir as the stones of the brooks" → "and set gold ore on the dust, and Ophir among the rock of the wadis"** (v24) — Hebrew וְשִׁית imperative "and set"; עַל־עָפָר "on the dust"; בֶּצֶר "gold ore" (only here and v25; uncertain); אוֹפִיר alone, the gold named by its land; בְּצוּר נְחָלִים "among the rock of the wadis". There is no "as" in the Hebrew.
- **"Yea, the Almighty shall be thy defence, and thou shalt have plenty of silver" → "and Shaddai shall be your gold ore, and silver of the heights to you"** (v25) — Hebrew בְּצָרֶיךָ, the word בֶּצֶר of v24 with "your"; תּוֹעָפוֹת is uncertain ("heights, peaks", hence "choicest"; in Num 23:22 "horns", its row; another sense here).
- **"have thy delight" → "delight yourself"** (v26) — Hebrew תִּתְעַנָּג (as Isa 58:14).
- **"make thy prayer" → "plead"** (v27) — Hebrew תַּעְתִּיר. **"pay thy vows" → "your vows you shall repay"** — תְשַׁלֵּם.
- **"decree a thing, and it shall be established" → "decree a saying, and it shall stand"** (v28) — Hebrew תִגְזַר־אוֹמֶר, literally "cut a saying"; יָקָם "shall stand". נָגַהּ is a perfect, "light has shone", in a row of promises.
- **"When men are cast down, then thou shalt say, There is lifting up; and he shall save the humble person" → "When they make low, you say, 'Lifting up!' — and the one bowed down of eyes he saves"** (v29) — Hebrew הִשְׁפִּילוּ "they make low" (no object, no "men"); גֵּוָה "lifting up" (also "pride"); שַׁח עֵינַיִם "bowed down of eyes". The verse is obscure; its words are kept as they stand.
- **"He shall deliver the island of the innocent: and it is delivered by the pureness of thine hands" → "He rescues one not innocent, and he escapes by the purity of your palms"** (v30) — Hebrew אִי־נָקִי: אִי "not" (as אִי־כָבוֹד, 1 Sam 4:21), so "one not innocent"; KJV took אִי as "island". מִלֵּט "rescue", נִמְלַט "escape"; בֹר "purity"; כַּפֶּיךָ "your palms".

### Notes
- **Third round** — Eliphaz now names Job's sins (v5–9); the narrator and Yahweh have called Job blameless (1:1, 8).
- **"What does El know?"** (v13–14) — Eliphaz puts these words in Job's mouth.
- **v18b** — the line repeats Job's own words of 21:16.
- **v30** — the one "not innocent" is rescued through the purity of another man's palms: Job 42:8 shows Job praying for Eliphaz himself.
