# Job 17

[1] "My spirit is broken, my days are extinguished;
the grave awaits me.

[2] Surely mockers are with me,
and my eye dwells on their provocations.

[3] Lay down a pledge for me with yourself;
who else will strike hands with me?

[4] For you have shut their heart from understanding;
therefore you will not let them triumph.

[5] He who denounces his friends for a share of spoil —
the eyes of his children shall fail.

[6] He has made me a byword of the peoples;
I am one in whose face men spit.

[7] My eye grows dim from grief,
and all my limbs are like a shadow.

[8] Upright men are astonished at this,
and the innocent rise up against the godless.

[9] Yet the righteous holds to his way,
and he who has clean hands grows stronger and stronger.

[10] As for all of you, come back now;
I will not find a wise man among you.

[11] My days are past, my plans are shattered,
the desires of my heart.

[12] These turn night into day;
'Light,' they say, 'is near, in the face of darkness.'

[13] If I look to Sheol as my house,
if I make my bed in darkness,

[14] if I say to the pit, 'You are my father,'
and to the worm, 'My mother and my sister,'

[15] where then is my hope?
Who will see my hope?

[16] Will it go down with me to Sheol?
Shall we descend together into the dust?"

## Changes

- **"hypocrite" → "godless"** (v8) — *chanef*.
- **"aforetime I was as a tabret" → "I am one in whose face men spit"** (v6) — Hebrew *topet le-panim eheyeh*; disputed — "spitting" from *toph* or "object of spitting"; KJV read as "tabret" (timbrel) from same consonants. Modern reading clearly "spittle / object of spitting."
- **"corruption" → "the pit"** (v14) — *shachat*.
- **"bars of the pit" → "Sheol"** (v16).

### Notes
- **Continued anguish** — days end, spirit broken, grave awaits.
- **"Pledge with yourself"** (v3) — Job asks El to stand pledge for Job before El. Mediator-theme: God the guarantor of the case against God.
- **Ironic — righteous hold on** (v9) — even in Job's case, assertion of perseverance.
- **Addressed to kin Sheol** (v14) — corruption, worm as family. Final abasement imagery.
- **Descent together into dust** — hope itself seems to die.
