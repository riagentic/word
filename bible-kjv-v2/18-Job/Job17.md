# Job 17

[1] "My spirit is ruined, my days are extinguished,
graves are for me.

[2] Surely mockeries are with me,
and on their rebelling my eye lodges.

[3] Set it down, please; be surety for me with yourself;
who is he who will be struck to my hand?

[4] For their heart you have hidden from insight;
therefore you will not raise them high.

[5] For a share he tells friends,
and the eyes of his sons will wear out.

[6] And he has set me for a proverb of the peoples,
and a spitting in the face I become.

[7] And my eye has grown dim from vexation,
and my members are like the shadow, all of them.

[8] The upright are appalled at this,
and the innocent stirs himself up against the godless.

[9] And the righteous holds to his way,
and the clean of hands adds firmness.

[10] But all of them, return and come, please,
and I will not find a wise man among you.

[11] My days have passed by, my devisings are torn apart,
the possessions of my heart.

[12] Night into day they set:
'Light is near from the face of darkness.'

[13] If I wait, Sheol is my house;
in the darkness I have spread my couch.

[14] To the pit I have called, 'You are my father';
'My mother and my sister,' to the maggot.

[15] And where, then, is my hope?
And my hope, who will watch it?

[16] To the bars of Sheol they will go down,
if together upon the dust there is rest."

## Changes

- **"My breath is corrupt, my days are extinct, the graves are ready for me" → "My spirit is ruined, my days are extinguished, graves are for me"** (v1) — Hebrew רוּחִי "my spirit" (also "breath"); חֻבָּלָה, pual of חָבַל "ruin"; נִזְעָכוּ "are extinguished" (only here; as דָּעַךְ); קְבָרִים לִי "graves are for me": "ready" is not written.
- **"Are there not mockers with me? and doth not mine eye continue in their provocation?" → "Surely mockeries are with me, and on their rebelling my eye lodges"** (v2) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא, literally "if not": the form of an oath, "surely". הֲתֻלִים "mockeries" (of the root of הֵתֵל "mock"), not persons. הַמְּרוֹתָם, hifil of מָרָה "rebel against, provoke"; תָּלַן, of לִין "lodge, spend the night".
- **"Lay down now, put me in a surety with thee; who is he that will strike hands with me?" → "Set it down, please; be surety for me with yourself; who is he who will be struck to my hand?"** (v3) — Hebrew שִׂימָה־נָּא "set, please" ("it", the pledge, is supplied); עָרְבֵנִי עִמָּךְ "be surety for me with you"; לְיָדִי יִתָּקֵעַ, nifal of תָּקַע "strike": "be struck to my hand", the handclasp that seals a pledge (Prov 6:1).
- **"therefore shalt thou not exalt them" → "therefore you will not raise them high"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא תְרֹמֵם, without an object: "them" is supplied. צָפַן "hide"; שֵׂכֶל "insight".
- **"He that speaketh flattery to his friends, even the eyes of his children shall fail" → "For a share he tells friends, and the eyes of his sons will wear out"** (v5) — Hebrew לְחֵלֶק יַגִּיד רֵעִים: חֵלֶק "share" (KJV "flattery" from the same letters, "smoothness"); הִגִּיד "tell". It may mean that he informs against friends for a share of their goods, or that he invites friends to a share; the Hebrew does not say. בָּנָיו "his sons"; תִּכְלֶנָה "wear out" (of the eyes, as Deut 28:32).
- **"He hath made me also a byword of the people; and aforetime I was as a tabret" → "And he has set me for a proverb of the peoples, and a spitting in the face I become"** (v6) — Hebrew הִצִּגַנִי "he has set me"; לִמְשֹׁל עַמִּים "for a proverb of the peoples" (מְשֹׁל, of the root of מָשָׁל); וְתֹפֶת לְפָנִים אֶהְיֶה: תֹּפֶת "spitting" (only here: uncertain); לְפָנִים "to the face" (also "formerly", whence KJV "aforetime"; KJV read תֹּף "timbrel").
- **"Mine eye also is dim by reason of sorrow, and all my members are as a shadow" → "And my eye has grown dim from vexation, and my members are like the shadow, all of them"** (v7) — Hebrew וַתֵּכַהּ, wayyiqtol of כָּהָה "grow dim"; כַּעַשׂ "vexation"; יְצֻרַי "my members", literally "my formed parts" (root יָצַר "form").
- **"Upright men shall be astonied at this, and the innocent shall stir up himself against the hypocrite" → "The upright are appalled at this, and the innocent stirs himself up against the godless"** (v8) — Hebrew יָשֹׁמּוּ, of שָׁמֵם (of persons "be appalled"); נָקִי "innocent" (WORDS-HE.md "clear": "the clear one" would not be English); חָנֵף "godless" (as 15:34).
- **"The righteous also shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger" → "And the righteous holds to his way, and the clean of hands adds firmness"** (v9) — Hebrew וְיֹאחֵז "and holds"; טְהָר־יָדַיִם "clean of hands"; יֹסִיף אֹמֶץ "adds firmness" (אֹמֶץ, of אָמֵץ "be firm").
- **"But as for you all, do ye return, and come now: for I cannot find one wise man among you" → "But all of them, return and come, please, and I will not find a wise man among you"** (v10) — Hebrew וְאוּלָם כֻּלָּם, "all of them", with the verbs in the second person (תָּשֻׁבוּ "you return", וּבֹאוּ "and come"); kept as written. נָא "please". "For" is not written: וְלֹא־אֶמְצָא "and I will not find".
- **"My days are past, my purposes are broken off, even the thoughts of my heart" → "My days have passed by, my devisings are torn apart, the possessions of my heart"** (v11) — Hebrew עָבְרוּ "passed by"; זִמֹּתַי "my devisings" (זִמָּה, here without blame); נִתְּקוּ "are torn apart" (as cords; WORDS-HE.md "be drawn out", of men drawn away from a town, does not fit a thing that snaps); מוֹרָשֵׁי לְבָבִי "the possessions of my heart" (of יָרַשׁ "possess": what the heart holds as its own).
- **"They change the night into day: the light is short because of darkness" → "Night into day they set: 'Light is near from the face of darkness'"** (v12) — Hebrew לַיְלָה לְיוֹם יָשִׂימוּ; who "they" are is not said (the friends?). אוֹר קָרוֹב מִפְּנֵי־חֹשֶׁךְ "light is near from the face of darkness" (= nearer than darkness? or near, before darkness comes?); "short" is not the word (קָרוֹב "near"). The quotation marks are supplied.
- **"If I wait, the grave is mine house: I have made my bed in the darkness" → "If I wait, Sheol is my house; in the darkness I have spread my couch"** (v13) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (not "the grave"); it may also be read "If I wait for Sheol as my house". רִפַּדְתִּי "I have spread"; יָצוּעַ "couch".
- **"I have said to corruption … to the worm" → "To the pit I have called … to the maggot"** (v14) — Hebrew לַשַּׁחַת "to the pit" (also taken from שָׁחַת "ruin": "corruption"); קָרָאתִי "I have called"; רִמָּה "maggot" (a collective).
- **"as for my hope, who shall see it?" → "And my hope, who will watch it?"** (v15) — Hebrew יְשׁוּרֶנָּה, of שׁוּר "watch" (as 7:8). אֵפוֹ "then".
- **"They shall go down to the bars of the pit, when our rest together is in the dust" → "To the bars of Sheol they will go down, if together upon the dust there is rest"** (v16) — Hebrew בַּדֵּי שְׁאֹל "the bars of Sheol" (בַּד "pole", here the bars of a gate: "pole" of WORDS-HE.md would not say a gate); תֵּרַדְנָה, feminine plural, "they" — the hopes of v15. אִם־יַחַד עַל־עָפָר נָחַת: נַחַת "rest" (or "descent", of נָחַת "go down"); "our" is not written. The sense of the line is uncertain.

### Notes
- **Job's speech continues** from ch 16.
- **"Be surety for me with yourself"** (v3) — Job asks the one he addresses to stand pledge for him with himself.
- **The pit as father, the maggot as mother and sister** (v14) — the grave as Job's kin.
- **Hope going down to Sheol** (v15–16).
