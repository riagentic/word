# Daniel 12

[1] "And at that time **Michael shall stand up, the great prince who stands over the sons of your people**. And there shall be a time of distress such as has not been since there was a nation until that time. And at that time your people shall escape, everyone who is found written in the book.

[2] And many of those who sleep in the ground of dust shall awake,
these to everlasting life,
and these to reproaches, to everlasting abhorrence.

[3] And those with insight shall shine like the shining of the expanse;
and those who justify the many,
like the stars forever and ever.

[4] "And you, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, shut up the words and seal the book, until a time of the end. Many shall roam about, and the knowledge shall increase."

[5] And I, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, looked, and behold, two others were standing, one here on the shore of the stream and one there on the shore of the stream.

[6] And he said to the man clothed in the linen, who was above the waters of the stream, "Until when is the end of the wonders?"

[7] And I heard the man clothed in the linen, who was above the waters of the stream; and he raised his right hand and his left hand to the heavens and swore by him who lives forever that it is for an appointed time, appointed times, and a half; and when the shattering of the hand of a holy people is finished, all these things shall be finished.

[8] And I heard, but I did not understand. And I said, "My lord, what is the latter end of these things?"

[9] And he said, "Go, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, for the words are shut up and sealed until a time of the end.

[10] Many shall purify themselves and make themselves white and be refined; and the wicked shall act wickedly, and none of the wicked shall understand; and those with insight shall understand.

[11] And from the time that the continual is taken away, and to set up a detestable thing desolating: one thousand two hundred and ninety days.

[12] Happy is the one who waits and reaches one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days.

[13] And you, go to the end; and you shall rest, and stand up for your lot at the end of the days."

## Changes

- **"shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people" → "Michael shall stand up, the great prince who stands over the sons of your people"** (v1) — Hebrew הָעֹמֵד עַל־בְּנֵי עַמֶּךָ: עַל "over / for"; בְּנֵי "sons of" (the standing decision). **"a time of trouble" → "a time of distress"** — Hebrew צָרָה (the row). **"such as never was" → "such as has not been"**. **"shall be delivered" → "shall escape"** — Hebrew יִמָּלֵט (the נִמְלַט row). **"every one that shall be found written" → "everyone who is found written"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַנִּמְצָא כָּתוּב, a participle.
- **"them that sleep in the dust of the earth" → "those who sleep in the ground of dust"** (v2) — Hebrew מִיְּשֵׁנֵי אַדְמַת־עָפָר: אֲדָמָה "ground", עָפָר "dust" (the rows). **"some … and some" → "these … and these"** — Hebrew אֵלֶּה … וְאֵלֶּה. **"everlasting life"** — Hebrew לְחַיֵּי עוֹלָם, literally "to life of the age" (the עוֹלָם-genitive row; so "everlasting abhorrence"). **"to shame and everlasting contempt" → "to reproaches, to everlasting abhorrence"** — Hebrew לַחֲרָפוֹת לְדִרְאוֹן עוֹלָם: חֲרָפוֹת plural of חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (the row), without "and"; דֵּרָאוֹן "abhorrence", found only here and Isa 66:24, rendered as there.
- **"they that be wise" → "those with insight"** (v3, 10) — Hebrew הַמַּשְׂכִּלִים, the participle of הִשְׂכִּיל (the row; 11:33, 35). **"shall shine as the brightness of the firmament" → "shall shine like the shining of the expanse"** — Hebrew יַזְהִרוּ כְּזֹהַר הָרָקִיעַ, verb and noun of one root; רָקִיעַ "expanse" (the row, Gen 1:6–8). **"they that turn many to righteousness" → "those who justify the many"** — Hebrew מַצְדִּיקֵי הָרַבִּים, the hifil of צָדַק (the הִצְדִּיק "justify" row): those who make the many righteous; "the many" with the article, as 11:33. **"for ever and ever"** — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם וָעֶד.
- **"But thou, O Daniel" → "And you, Daniel"** (v4) — no "O". **"shut up the words, and seal the book"** — Hebrew סְתֹם … וַחֲתֹם, two verbs (as 8:26 סְתֹם; v9). **"even to the time of the end" → "until a time of the end"** (v4, 9) — Hebrew עַד־עֵת קֵץ, without the article (as 11:35, 40). **"many shall run to and fro" → "Many shall roam about"** — Hebrew יְשֹׁטְטוּ (the שׁוֹטֵט row). **"knowledge shall be increased" → "the knowledge shall increase"** — Hebrew וְתִרְבֶּה הַדָּעַת, with the article; the Greek reads "and wickedness shall be filled" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"there stood other two" → "two others were standing"** (v5) — a participle. **"the one on this side of the bank of the river" → "one here on the shore of the stream"** (v5–7) — Hebrew הֵנָּה לִשְׂפַת הַיְאֹר: שָׂפָה "lip", of water "shore" (the row); יְאֹר, the word that names the Nile elsewhere (the `nile` row), here the river of 10:4: "stream", no token.
- **"And one said" → "And he said"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר, the speaker not named; the Greek reads "and I said" (from memory). **"clothed in linen" → "clothed in the linen"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew לְבוּשׁ הַבַּדִּים, with the article (10:5 without). **"which was upon the waters" → "who was above the waters"** — Hebrew מִמַּעַל לְמֵימֵי. **"How long shall it be to the end of these wonders?" → "Until when is the end of the wonders?"** — Hebrew עַד־מָתַי קֵץ הַפְּלָאוֹת; "these" is not written.
- **"held up" → "raised"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיָּרֶם. **"unto heaven" → "to the heavens"**. **"him that liveth for ever"** — Hebrew בְּחֵי הָעוֹלָם, literally "by the life of the age". **"for a time, times, and an half" → "for an appointed time, appointed times, and a half"** — Hebrew לְמוֹעֵד מוֹעֲדִים וָחֵצִי: the Hebrew word is מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (the row), not the Aramaic עִדָּן of 7:25. **"when he shall have accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people" → "when the shattering of the hand of a holy people is finished"** — Hebrew וּכְכַלּוֹת נַפֵּץ יַד־עַם־קֹדֶשׁ: יָד "hand" (= power), עַם־קֹדֶשׁ without the article; the subject of "finish" is not named. **"all these things shall be finished"** — Hebrew תִּכְלֶינָה כָל־אֵלֶּה (כָּלָה twice in the verse).
- **"but I understood not: then said I" → "but I did not understand. And I said"** (v8). **"O my Lord" → "My lord"** — Hebrew אֲדֹנִי, a man's title (as 10:16), not the divine name; no "O". **"what shall be the end" → "what is the latter end"** — Hebrew אַחֲרִית (as 8:19, 23).
- **"Go thy way" → "Go"** (v9, 13) — Hebrew לֵךְ. **"closed up" → "shut up"** — Hebrew סְתֻמִים, as v4.
- **"Many shall be purified, and made white, and tried" → "Many shall purify themselves and make themselves white and be refined"** (v10) — Hebrew יִתְבָּרֲרוּ וְיִתְלַבְּנוּ, reflexive stems, and וְיִצָּרְפוּ, nifal of צָרַף "refine" (the row; 11:35). **"but the wicked shall do wickedly" → "and the wicked shall act wickedly"** — Hebrew וְהִרְשִׁיעוּ רְשָׁעִים. **"but the wise" → "and those with insight"**.
- **"from the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away" → "from the time that the continual is taken away"** (v11) — Hebrew הוּסַר הַתָּמִיד (as 8:11; 11:31). **"and the abomination that maketh desolate set up" → "and to set up a detestable thing desolating"** — Hebrew וְלָתֵת שִׁקּוּץ שֹׁמֵם, as printed: an infinitive; without the article; שֹׁמֵם the plain participle (8:13; 9:27 has the polel מְשֹׁמֵם, 11:31 מְשׁוֹמֵם). **"there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days" → "one thousand two hundred and ninety days"** — Hebrew יָמִים אֶלֶף מָאתַיִם וְתִשְׁעִים, no verb; the Hebrew sets "days" first (the numbers row).
- **"Blessed" → "Happy"** (v12) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי (the reviewer's row, as the Psalms). **"cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days" → "reaches one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days"** — Hebrew וְיַגִּיעַ לְיָמִים אֶלֶף שְׁלֹשׁ מֵאוֹת שְׁלֹשִׁים וַחֲמִשָּׁה; the numbers stand as printed (1,290 in v11, 1,335 here, beside the 2,300 of 8:14).
- **"But go thou thy way till the end be" → "And you, go to the end"** (v13) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה לֵךְ לַקֵּץ; the Greek lacks לַקֵּץ (from memory). **"for thou shalt rest" → "and you shall rest"** — וְ "and". **"stand in thy lot" → "stand up for your lot"** — Hebrew וְתַעֲמֹד לְגֹרָלְךָ (עָמַד, as v1). **"at the end of the days"** — Hebrew לְקֵץ הַיָּמִין, with the Aramaic-like plural ending -in.

### Notes
- **v2** — the plainest word in the Old Testament of a rising from the dust to two ends; Isa 26:19; 66:24 stand beside it; John 5:28–29 takes it up.
- **v3** — echoed Matt 13:43.
- **"An appointed time, appointed times, and a half"** (v7) — the Hebrew form of 7:25; Rev 12:14.
- **1,290 / 1,335 days** (v11–12) — about three and a half years and somewhat more; the verses do not say how the figures relate to each other or to 8:14.
