# Daniel 2

[1] And in the second year of the reign of ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧, ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ dreamed dreams; and his spirit was troubled, and his sleep was ended upon him.

[2] And the king said to call the magicians and the enchanters and the sorcerers and the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, to tell the king his dreams. And they came and stood before the king.

[3] And the king said to them, "A dream I have dreamed, and my spirit is troubled to know the dream."

[4] And the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ spoke to the king in ⟦aramaic|Aramit|Aramaic⟧: "King, live forever! Tell the dream to your servants, and we will show the interpretation."

[5] The king answered and said to the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧, "The word from me is firm: if you do not make known to me the dream and its interpretation, you shall be made into limbs, and your houses shall be made a dunghill.

[6] But if you show the dream and its interpretation, gifts and a reward and great honor you shall receive from before me. Therefore show me the dream and its interpretation."

[7] They answered a second time and said, "Let the king tell the dream to his servants, and we will show its interpretation."

[8] The king answered and said, "I know of a certainty that you are buying time, because you have seen that the word from me is firm,

[9] that if you do not make known to me the dream, one is your law; and a lying and corrupt word you have agreed together to speak before me, until the time is changed. Therefore tell me the dream, and I shall know that you will show me its interpretation."

[10] The ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ answered before the king and said, "There is not a man on the dry land who can show the matter of the king, because no king, great and ruling, has asked such a matter of any magician or enchanter or ⟦chaldeans|Kasdi|Chaldean⟧.

[11] And the matter that the king asks is difficult, and there is no other who can show it before the king except gods, whose dwelling is not with flesh."

[12] Because of this the king was angry and very wrathful, and said to destroy all the wise men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[13] And the law went out, and the wise men were being killed; and they sought ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ and his companions, to be killed.

[14] Then ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ returned counsel and discretion to ⟦arioch|Aryoch|Arioch⟧, the chief of the guards of the king, who had gone out to kill the wise men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[15] He answered and said to ⟦arioch|Aryoch|Arioch⟧, the captain of the king, "Why is the law so harsh from before the king?" Then ⟦arioch|Aryoch|Arioch⟧ made the matter known to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧.

[16] And ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ went in and sought from the king that he would give him a time, and to show the interpretation to the king.

[17] Then ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ went to his house, and to ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧, Mishael, and ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧, his companions, he made the matter known,

[18] that they should seek compassion from before the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens concerning this mystery, that ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ and his companions should not perish with the rest of the wise men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[19] Then to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ in a vision of the night the mystery was revealed. Then ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ blessed the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens.

[20] ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ answered and said:

"Let the name of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ be blessed from the age and to the age,
for wisdom and might, they are his.

[21] And he changes times and seasons;
he removes kings and sets up kings;
he gives wisdom to the wise
and knowledge to those who know understanding;

[22] he reveals the deep and the hidden things;
he knows what is in the darkness,
and the light dwells with him.

[23] To you, ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of my fathers,
I give thanks and praise,
for wisdom and might you have given me,
and now you have made known to me what we sought from you,
for the matter of the king you have made known to us."

[24] Because of this ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ went in to ⟦arioch|Aryoch|Arioch⟧, whom the king had appointed to destroy the wise men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧; he went, and thus he said to him: "Do not destroy the wise men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧; bring me in before the king, and the interpretation I will show to the king."

[25] Then ⟦arioch|Aryoch|Arioch⟧ in haste brought ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ in before the king, and thus he said to him: "I have found a man of the sons of the exile of ⟦judah|Yehud|Judah⟧ who will make known the interpretation to the king."

[26] The king answered and said to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, whose name was ⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧, "Are you able to make known to me the dream that I have seen, and its interpretation?"

[27] ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ answered before the king and said, "The mystery that the king asks, wise men, enchanters, magicians, determiners are not able to show to the king.

[28] But there is an ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ in the heavens who reveals mysteries, and he has made known to King ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ what shall be in the latter days. Your dream and the visions of your head upon your bed, this it is:

[29] You, King, your thoughts came up upon your bed, of what shall be after this; and he who reveals mysteries has made known to you what shall be.

[30] And as for me, not by wisdom that is in me more than all the living has this mystery been revealed to me, but for the sake that the interpretation should be made known to the king, and that you may know the thoughts of your heart.

[31] You, King, were seeing, and behold, one great image. That image was mighty, and its brightness excellent; it stood before you, and its appearance was fearsome.

[32] That image: its head was of good gold, its breast and its arms of silver, its belly and its thighs of bronze,

[33] its legs of iron, its feet, part of them of iron and part of them of clay.

[34] You were seeing until a stone was cut out, not by hands, and it struck the image on its feet of iron and of clay, and crushed them.

[35] Then were crushed together the iron, the clay, the bronze, the silver, and the gold, and they became like chaff from the threshing floors of summer; and the wind carried them away, and no place was found for them. And the stone that struck the image became a great mountain and filled all the earth.

[36] This is the dream; and its interpretation we will tell before the king.

[37] You, King, king of kings, to whom the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens has given the kingdom, the might, and the strength, and the honor,

[38] and wherever the sons of man dwell, the beasts of the field and the birds of the heavens he has given into your hand, and has made you rule over them all — **you are the head of gold**.

[39] And after you shall arise another kingdom, lower than you; and another, a third kingdom, of bronze, which shall rule over all the earth.

[40] And a fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron, because iron crushes and shatters everything; and as iron that breaks, all these it shall crush and break.

[41] And as you saw the feet and the toes, part of them potter's clay and part of them iron, it shall be a divided kingdom; and some of the firmness of iron shall be in it, because you saw the iron mixed with miry clay.

[42] And the toes of the feet, part of them iron and part of them clay: part of the kingdom shall be strong, and part of it shall be brittle.

[43] And as you saw the iron mixed with miry clay, they shall mix themselves with the seed of man, but they shall not cling, this to this, even as iron does not mix with clay.

[44] **And in the days of those kings the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens will set up a kingdom that shall never be destroyed, and the kingdom shall not be left to another people. It shall crush and make an end of all these kingdoms, and it shall stand forever,**

**[45] because you saw that from the mountain a stone was cut out, not by hands, and that it crushed the iron, the bronze, the clay, the silver, and the gold.** A great ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ has made known to the king what shall be after this; and the dream is certain, and its interpretation trustworthy."

[46] Then King ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ fell upon his face, and to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ he did homage, and he said to pour out to him an offering and soothing smells.

[47] The king answered ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ and said, "Of a truth it is that your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, he is the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of gods and the Lord of kings, and a revealer of mysteries, because you have been able to reveal this mystery."

[48] Then the king made ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ great, and many great gifts he gave him, and he made him rule over all the province of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and chief of the prefects over all the wise men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[49] And ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ sought from the king, and he appointed over the work of the province of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ Shadrach, Meshach, and ⟦abednego|Aved-nego|Abednego⟧; and ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ was in the gate of the king.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elah*"** (v18, 19, 20, 23, 28, 37, 44, 45, 47) — from v4b the chapter is Aramaic; אֱלָהּ *Elah* (with the emphatic ending אֱלָהָא, v20) is the Aramaic word for "God", with its own token (as Ezra 4–7), not the Hebrew *Elohim*. **"the God of heaven" → "the Elah of the heavens"** (v18, 19, 37, 44) — אֱלָהּ שְׁמַיָּא, plural in form as Hebrew שָׁמַיִם (v28 בִּשְׁמַיָּא "in the heavens"). Plural אֱלָהִין "gods" (v11, 47) is plain.
- **"dreamed dreams"** (v1) — חָלַם … חֲלֹמוֹת, plural (v2 "his dreams"; v3 "a dream"). **"his sleep brake from him" → "his sleep was ended upon him"** — וּשְׁנָתוֹ נִהְיְתָה עָלָיו, literally "his sleep came to be (was done) upon him"; the sense is uncertain ("left him", "was finished", or "came back upon him").
- **"commanded to call" → "said to call"** (v2; v12 "said to destroy", v46 "said to pour out") — אָמַר / אֲמַר with an infinitive. **"the astrologers, and the sorcerers" → "the enchanters and the sorcerers"** — אַשָּׁפִים "enchanters" (as 1:20), מְכַשְּׁפִים "sorcerers"; חַרְטֻמִּים "magicians" (exact sense uncertain). **"the Chaldeans"** — here a class of learned men (v2, 4, 5, 10), the people's name used for them.
- **"I have dreamed a dream" → "A dream I have dreamed"** (v3) — חֲלוֹם חָלַמְתִּי, the noun first.
- **"in Syriack" → "in Aramaic"** (v4) — אֲרָמִית; here the text itself changes into Aramaic, which goes on to 7:28. **"O king, live for ever" → "King, live forever!"** (v4) — מַלְכָּא לְעָלְמִין חֱיִי; Aramaic has no word for "O" (so v29, 31, 37 "You, King"). **"to thy servants"** — qere לְעַבְדָךְ (ketiv לעבדיך, the same word spelled as in Hebrew). **"shew"** — חַוִּי "show" (v4–11, 16, 24, 27); הוֹדַע "make known" (v5, 9, 15, 17, 23, 25, 26, 28–30, 45) kept apart.
- **"The thing is gone from me" → "The word from me is firm"** (v5, 8) — מִלְּתָא מִנִּי אַזְדָּא; אַזְדָּא is a Persian loanword, "certain, publicly declared" (uncertain); KJV's "gone" follows a guess from Hebrew אָזַל. מִלָּה is "word" where speech is meant (v5, 8, 9), "matter" where the thing asked or told is meant (v10, 11, 15, 17, 23). **"ye shall be cut in pieces" → "you shall be made into limbs"** — הַדָּמִין תִּתְעַבְדוּן, הַדָּם a Persian loanword "limb": torn apart. **"a dunghill"** — נְוָלִי, sense uncertain ("a heap of refuse", "a ruin"). **qere** לְכַשְׂדָּאֵי (v5), כַשְׂדָּאֵי (v10).
- **"gifts and rewards and great honour" → "gifts and a reward and great honor"** (v6) — מַתְּנָן וּנְבִזְבָּה וִיקָר שַׂגִּיא, נְבִזְבָּה singular (a loanword, uncertain). **"of me" → "from before me"** — מִן־קֳדָמָי. **"therefore"** — לָהֵן (v6, 9; "except" in v11; "but" in v30).
- **"that ye would gain the time" → "that you are buying time"** (v8) — עִדָּנָא אַנְתּוּן זָבְנִין, literally "you are buying the time". **"because ye see" → "because you have seen"** — חֲזֵיתוֹן, perfect.
- **"there is but one decree for you" → "one is your law"** (v9) — חֲדָה־הִיא דָתְכוֹן; דָּת, a Persian loanword, "law" (also v13, 15; not *torah*). **"ye have prepared lying and corrupt words" → "a lying and corrupt word you have agreed together"** — qere הִזְדְּמִנְתּוּן (ketiv הזמנתון, another stem of the same verb), "agree together, conspire". **"till the time be changed"** — עִדָּנָא יִשְׁתַּנֵּא. **"I shall know that ye can shew me" → "I shall know that you will show me"** — imperfect.
- **"upon the earth" → "on the dry land"** (v10) — עַל־יַבֶּשְׁתָּא. **"the king's matter" → "the matter of the king"** — מִלַּת מַלְכָּא (also v23). **"therefore there is no king, lord, nor ruler" → "because no king, great and ruling"** — כָּל־קֳבֵל דִּי כָּל־מֶלֶךְ רַב וְשַׁלִּיט … לָא, "every king, great and ruling, has not asked": the adjectives qualify "king".
- **"a rare thing" → "difficult"** (v11) — יַקִּירָה, "heavy, precious": hard to come by. **"except the gods" → "except gods"** — אֱלָהִין, no article.
- **"was angry and very furious" → "was angry and very wrathful"** (v12) — בְּנַס וּקְצַף שַׂגִּיא; קְצַף as Hebrew קָצַף "be wrathful". **"destroy" … "perish"** (v12, 18, 24) — one root, אֲבַד: haphel הוֹבֵד "destroy" (v12, 24), peal "perish" (v18).
- **"the decree" → "the law"** (v13, 15) — דָּתָא (v9). **"the wise men should be slain" → "the wise men were being killed"** — מִתְקַטְּלִין, participle; "to be killed" (לְהִתְקְטָלָה) for Daniel and his companions.
- **"answered with counsel and wisdom" → "returned counsel and discretion"** (v14) — הֲתִיב עֵטָא וּטְעֵם; טְעֵם of a person's sense, "discretion" (the word is "decree" elsewhere, 3:10). **"the captain of the king's guard" → "the chief of the guards of the king"** — רַב־טַבָּחַיָּא, as Hebrew שַׂר הַטַּבָּחִים (Gen 37:36), literally "of the slaughterers".
- **"the king's captain" → "the captain of the king"** (v15) — שַׁלִּיטָא, the root of שַׁלִּיט "ruling" (v10) and הַשְׁלֵט "make rule" (v38, 48): one in authority. **"Why is the decree so hasty" → "Why is the law so harsh"** — מְהַחְצְפָה, "be harsh, insolent" (or "urgent"; uncertain).
- **"desired of the king" → "sought from the king"** (v16, 49) — בְּעָה, the verb of v13, 18, 23 ("sought", "seek").
- **"desire mercies of the God of heaven" → "that they should seek compassion from before the Elah of the heavens"** (v18) — וְרַחֲמִין לְמִבְעֵא מִן־קֳדָם, an infinitive; "that they should" supplied. רַחֲמִין "compassion" (as Hebrew רַחֲמִים). **"secret" → "mystery"** (v18, 19, 27–30, 47) — רָז, a Persian loanword, "secret, mystery".
- **"for ever and ever" → "from the age and to the age"** (v20) — מִן־עָלְמָא וְעַד־עָלְמָא, with the article (the reviewer's row; Dan 2:20 named). **"for wisdom and might are his" → "for wisdom and might, they are his"** — דִּי … דִּי לֵהּ־הִיא, the pronoun kept. **"Blessed be" → "Let … be blessed"** — לֶהֱוֵא, jussive.
- **"to them that know understanding"** (v21) — יָדְעֵי בִינָה. **"and the light"** (v22) — qere וּנְהוֹרָא (ketiv ונהירא, another spelling of the noun).
- **"O thou God of my fathers" → "To you, Elah of my fathers"** (v23) — no "O". **"what we desired of thee" → "what we sought from you"** — בְעֵינָא.
- **"Destroy not" → "Do not destroy"** (v24) — אַל־תְּהוֹבֵד.
- **"of the captives of Judah" → "of the sons of the exile of Judah"** (v25) — מִן־בְּנֵי גָלוּתָא דִּי יְהוּד; יְהוּד, the Aramaic name of the province.
- **"Art thou able"** (v26) — qere הַאִיתָךְ (ketiv האיתיך, another spelling).
- **"cannot the wise men, the astrologers, the magicians, the soothsayers, shew" → "wise men, enchanters, magicians, determiners are not able to show"** (v27) — no "the" in the Aramaic; גָּזְרִין, literally "cutters, deciders": those who determine fate (uncertain; also 4:7; 5:7, 11).
- **"in the latter days"** (v28) — בְּאַחֲרִית יוֹמַיָּא (as Hebrew בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים). **"are these" → "this it is"** — דְּנָה הוּא. **"King Nebuchadnezzar"** — לְמַלְכָּא נְבוּכַדְנֶצַּר, "King" first as the Aramaic order (so v46).
- **"As for thee, O king, thy thoughts came into thy mind upon thy bed" → "You, King, your thoughts came up upon your bed"** (v29) — רַעְיוֹנָךְ … סְלִקוּ.
- **"for any wisdom that I have" → "by wisdom that is in me"** (v30). **"for their sakes that shall make known the interpretation" → "for the sake that the interpretation should be made known"** — עַל־דִּבְרַת דִּי … יְהוֹדְעוּן, a plural verb with no subject: "that they should make known", i.e. impersonal. **"of thy heart"** — לִבְבָךְ.
- **"sawest, and behold a great image" → "were seeing, and behold, one great image"** (v31) — חָזֵה הֲוַיְתָ וַאֲלוּ צְלֵם חַד שַׂגִּיא; אֲלוּ "behold". צְלֵם "image" (as Hebrew צֶלֶם). **"whose brightness was excellent" → "its brightness excellent"** — זִיוֵהּ יַתִּיר. **"terrible" → "fearsome"** — דְּחִיל.
- **"fine gold" → "good gold"** (v32) — דְהַב טָב. **"breast … belly"** — חֲדוֹהִי … מְעוֹהִי. **"brass" → "bronze"** (v32, 35, 39, 45) — נְחָשׁ.
- **"part of iron and part of clay" → "part of them of iron and part of them of clay"** (v33, 41, 42) — qere מִנְּהֵין (ketiv מנהון, masculine form), "of them". **"clay"** — חֲסַף, earthenware clay; v41 חֲסַף דִּי־פֶחָר "potter's clay"; v41, 43 חֲסַף טִינָא "miry clay".
- **"Thou sawest till" → "You were seeing until"** (v34). **"without hands" → "not by hands"** (v34, 45) — דִּי־לָא בִידַיִן. **"brake them to pieces" → "crushed them"** — הַדֵּקֶת; דְּקַק "crush" each time (v35, 40, 44, 45).
- **"the chaff of the summer threshingfloors" → "chaff from the threshing floors of summer"** (v35) — כְּעוּר מִן־אִדְּרֵי־קַיִט. **"no place was found for them"** — וְכָל־אֲתַר לָא־הִשְׁתֲּכַח לְהוֹן, literally "and any place was not found for them". **"the whole earth" → "all the earth"** — כָּל־אַרְעָא.
- **"we will tell the interpretation thereof before the king" → "its interpretation we will tell before the king"** (v36).
- **"a kingdom, power, and strength, and glory" → "the kingdom, the might, and the strength, and the honor"** (v37) — מַלְכוּתָא חִסְנָא וְתָקְפָּא וִיקָרָא; יְקָר "honor" as v6.
- **"wheresoever the children of men dwell" → "wherever the sons of man dwell"** (v38) — qere דָּיְרִין (ketiv דארין, another spelling); בְּנֵי־אֲנָשָׁא "the sons of man". **"the fowls of the heaven" → "the birds of the heavens"** — עוֹף־שְׁמַיָּא. **"made thee ruler" → "made you rule"** — הַשְׁלְטָךְ.
- **"inferior to thee" → "lower than you"** (v39) — qere אֲרַע "lower" (ketiv ארעא, "the earth"); qere תְלִיתָאָה.
- **"forasmuch as iron breaketh in pieces and subdueth all things" → "because iron crushes and shatters everything"** (v40) — מְהַדֵּק וְחָשֵׁל; qere רְבִיעָאָה. **"as iron that breaketh all these" → "as iron that breaks, all these it shall crush and break"** — מְרָעַע … תַּדִּק וְתֵרֹעַ.
- **"strength of the iron" → "firmness of iron"** (v41) — נִצְבְּתָא, "firmness" (only here). **"forasmuch as" → "because"** — כָּל־קֳבֵל דִּי (v8, 10, 12, 24, 40, 41, 45).
- **"partly broken" → "brittle"** (v42) — תְבִירָה.
- **"they shall mingle themselves with the seed of men" → "they shall mix themselves with the seed of man"** (v43) — בִּזְרַע אֲנָשָׁא (qere וְדִי); "in marriage" is not in the Aramaic. **"cleave one to another" → "cling, this to this"** — דָּבְקִין דְּנָה עִם־דְּנָה; "cling" as Hebrew דָּבַק.
- **"and the kingdom shall not be left to other people" → "and the kingdom shall not be left to another people"** (v44) — וּמַלְכוּתָה; **"consume" → "make an end of"** — תָסֵיף.
- **"out of the mountain" → "from the mountain"** (v45). **"the great God" → "A great Elah"** — אֱלָהּ רַב, no article. **"sure" → "trustworthy"** — מְהֵימַן.
- **"worshipped Daniel" → "to Daniel he did homage"** (v46) — וּלְדָנִיֵּאל סְגִד; סְגִד is bowing to the ground; "fall down" (the Isa 44–47 row) is not used where נְפַל "fell" stands beside it (also ch. 3). **"commanded that they should offer an oblation and sweet odours unto him" → "he said to pour out to him an offering and soothing smells"** — מִנְחָה וְנִיחֹחִין אֲמַר לְנַסָּכָה לֵהּ; נִיחֹחִין as Hebrew נִיחֹחַ "soothing"; נְסַךְ "pour out".
- **"Of a truth it is, that your God is a God of gods" → "Of a truth it is that your Elah, he is the Elah of gods"** (v47) — מִן־קְשֹׁט דִּי אֱלָהֲכוֹן הוּא אֱלָהּ אֱלָהִין; "your" is plural. **"a Lord of kings" → "the Lord of kings"** — מָרֵא מַלְכִין.
- **"made Daniel a great man" → "made Daniel great"** (v48) — רַבִּי. **"chief of the governors" → "chief of the prefects"** — רַב־סִגְנִין (as Hebrew סְגָנִים, "prefects"). **"province"** — מְדִינָה.
- **"over the affairs" → "over the work"** (v49) — עֲבִידְתָּא. **"sat in the gate of the king" → "was in the gate of the king"** — בִּתְרַע מַלְכָּא: the gate as the seat of the court.

### Notes
- **Language** — 2:1–4a is Hebrew; from "King, live forever!" (v4b) the book is in Aramaic to 7:28.
- **Four kingdoms** — traditionally Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome (or Babylon, Media, Persia, Greece); the text names only the first (v38).
- **Stone cut not by hands** (v34, 45) — the kingdom set up by the Elah of the heavens (v44).
