# Daniel 7

[1] In the first year of ⟦belshazzar|Belshatzar|Belshazzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ saw a dream, and visions of his head upon his bed. Then he wrote the dream; the head of the words he told.

[2] ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧ answered and said, "I was looking in my vision by night, and behold, the four winds of the heavens were stirring up the great sea.

[3] And four great beasts were coming up from the sea, different one from another.

[4] "The first was like a lion and had wings of an eagle. I was looking until its wings were plucked off, and it was lifted up from the earth and made to stand on two feet like a man, and the heart of a man was given to it.

[5] And behold, another beast, a second one, resembling a bear. And on one side it was raised up, and three ribs were in its mouth between its teeth; and thus they were saying to it, 'Arise, devour much flesh.'

[6] After this I was looking, and behold, another, like a leopard, and it had four wings of a bird on its back. And the beast had four heads, and dominion was given to it.

[7] After this I was looking in the visions of the night, and behold, a fourth beast, fearsome and terrible and exceedingly strong. And it had great teeth of iron; it devoured and crushed, and the rest it trampled with its feet. And it was different from all the beasts that were before it, and it had ten horns.

[8] I was considering the horns, and behold, another horn, a little one, came up among them, and three of the first horns were uprooted before it. And behold, in this horn were eyes like the eyes of man, and a mouth speaking great things.

[9] "I was looking until
thrones were placed,
and ⟦ancient-of-days|Attiq Yomin|an Ancient of Days⟧ sat;
his clothing was white as snow,
and the hair of his head like pure wool;
his throne was flames of fire;
its wheels were burning fire.

[10] A river of fire was flowing
and coming out from before him;
a thousand thousands ministered to him,
and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him;
the court sat,
and books were opened.

[11] "I was looking then because of the voice of the great words that the horn was speaking. I was looking until the beast was slain, and its body destroyed and given to the burning of fire.

[12] And the rest of the beasts, they took away their dominion; and a lengthening in life was given to them, until a season and a time.

[13] "I was looking in the visions of the night,
and behold, with the clouds of the heavens
one like a son of man was coming,
and up to ⟦ancient-of-days|Attiq Yomayya|the Ancient of Days⟧ he came,
and before him they brought him near.

[14] And to him was given dominion
and honor and a kingdom,
and all the peoples, nations, and languages
shall serve him;
his dominion is an everlasting dominion,
which shall not pass away,
and his kingdom one
that shall not be destroyed.

[15] "My spirit was distressed, I, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, in the midst of the sheath, and the visions of my head alarmed me.

[16] I came near to one of those who were standing, and the truth I asked of him concerning all this. And he spoke to me and made known to me the interpretation of the matters.

[17] 'These great beasts, which are four: four kings shall arise out of the earth.

[18] And the holy ones of ⟦elyon|Elyonin|the Most High⟧ shall receive the kingdom, and they shall possess the kingdom to the age, and to the age of the ages.'

[19] "Then I wished to make certain about the fourth beast, which was different from all of them, exceedingly fearsome, its teeth of iron and its claws of bronze, which devoured, crushed, and trampled the rest with its feet,

[20] and about the ten horns that were on its head, and the other that came up, and before which three fell — and that horn had eyes and a mouth speaking great things, and its look was greater than its companions.

[21] I was looking, and that horn was making war with the holy ones and prevailing over them,

[22] until ⟦ancient-of-days|Attiq Yomayya|the Ancient of Days⟧ came, and judgment was given to the holy ones of ⟦elyon|Elyonin|the Most High⟧, and the season arrived, and the holy ones possessed the kingdom.

[23] "Thus he said: 'The fourth beast: a fourth kingdom shall be on the earth, which shall be different from all the kingdoms, and it shall devour all the earth, and tread it down, and crush it.

[24] And the ten horns: out of this kingdom ten kings shall arise, and another shall arise after them; and he shall be different from the former ones, and three kings he shall bring low.

[25] And words against ⟦elyon|Illaya|the Most High⟧ he shall speak, and the holy ones of ⟦elyon|Elyonin|the Most High⟧ he shall wear out, and he shall think to change seasons and law; and they shall be given into his hand until **a time and times and half a time**.

[26] And the court shall sit, and his dominion they shall take away, to destroy and to make perish to the end.

[27] And the kingdom and the dominion and the greatness of the kingdoms under all the heavens has been given to the people of the holy ones of ⟦elyon|Elyonin|the Most High⟧; his kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all the dominions shall serve him and obey.'

[28] "Here is the end of the matter. As for me, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, my thoughts greatly alarmed me, and my brightness was changed upon me; and the matter I kept in my heart."

## Changes

- **"Belshazzar" retained** (v1) — standard KJV English transliteration.
- **"In the first year"** (v1) — Aramaic בִּשְׁנַת חֲדָה, literally "in year one". **"had a dream … upon his bed" → "saw a dream, and visions of his head upon his bed"** — Aramaic חֵלֶם חֲזָה "saw a dream"; "as he lay" is not written. **"told the sum of the matters" → "the head of the words he told"** — Aramaic רֵאשׁ מִלִּין, literally "the head of words": the chief points; the order of the Aramaic kept.
- **"spake and said" → "answered and said"** (v2) — Aramaic עָנֵה וְאָמַר, the formula that opens speech; no question has been asked. **"I saw" → "I was looking"** (v2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13, 21) — Aramaic חָזֵה הֲוֵית, a participle with "to be": the ongoing look of the vision. **"the four winds of the heaven strove upon" → "the four winds of the heavens were stirring up"** — Aramaic שְׁמַיָּא plural in form (so v13, 27); מְגִיחָן "bursting forth upon, stirring up" (uncertain).
- **"came up"** (v3) — Aramaic סָלְקָן, a participle: "were coming up".
- **"eagle's wings" → "wings of an eagle"** (v4) — Aramaic גַפִּין דִּי־נְשַׁר. **"I beheld till" → "I was looking until"** (v4, 9, 11) — Aramaic עַד דִּי. **"upon the feet" → "on two feet"** — Aramaic רַגְלַיִן, dual. **"a man's heart" → "the heart of a man"** — Aramaic לְבַב אֱנָשׁ "heart" (not "mind"); אֱנָשׁ is the Aramaic word for a man / mankind (v4, 8, 13).
- **"like to a bear" → "resembling a bear"** (v5) — Aramaic דָּמְיָה לְדֹב. **"it raised up itself on one side" → "on one side it was raised up"** — Aramaic הֳקִמַת, passive. **"between its teeth"** — qere שִׁנַּהּ; the ketiv spells the same word otherwise. **"they said thus unto it" → "thus they were saying to it"** — Aramaic אָמְרִין, a plural participle with no subject named.
- **"a fowl" → "a bird"** (v6) — Aramaic עוֹף. **"upon the back"** — qere גַּבַּהּ "its back"; the ketiv reads a plural.
- **"dreadful and terrible" → "fearsome and terrible"** (v7, 19) — Aramaic דְּחִילָה (root "fear") · אֵימְתָנִי. **"brake in pieces" → "crushed"** (v7, 19, 23) — Aramaic הַדֵּק. **"with the feet of it"** — qere בְּרַגְלַהּ; the ketiv reads a plural.
- **"another little horn" → "another horn, a little one"** (v8) — Aramaic קֶרֶן אָחֳרִי זְעֵירָה; qere בֵּינֵיהֵן, אֶתְעֲקַרָה, קֳדָמַהּ (feminine forms of the same words). **"plucked up by the roots" → "uprooted"** — Aramaic אֶתְעֲקַרָה, one verb.
- **"the thrones were cast down" → "thrones were placed"** (v9) — Aramaic כָרְסָוָן רְמִיו, literally "thrones were thrown": set down in place, not removed; no article. **"the Ancient of days did sit" → "an Ancient of Days sat"** (v9) — Aramaic עַתִּיק יוֹמִין *Attiq Yomin*, without the article: "one advanced in days"; in v13, 22 עַתִּיק יוֹמַיָּא *Attiq Yomayya*, with the article, "the Ancient of Days". Czech has no article. **"like the fiery flame" → "flames of fire"** — Aramaic שְׁבִיבִין דִּי־נוּר, plural; "like" is not written.
- **"A fiery stream issued and came forth" → "A river of fire was flowing and coming out"** (v10) — Aramaic נְהַר דִּי־נוּר נָגֵד וְנָפֵק, participles. **"thousand thousands … ten thousand times ten thousand"** — qere אַלְפִין, רִבְבָן. **"the judgment was set" → "the court sat"** (v10, 26) — Aramaic דִּינָא יְתִב, literally "the judgment sat": the court. **"the books" → "books"** — Aramaic סִפְרִין, without the article.
- **"the voice of the great words"** (v11) — Aramaic קָל. **"given to the burning flame" → "given to the burning of fire"** — Aramaic לִיקֵדַת אֶשָּׁא.
- **"they had their dominion taken away" → "they took away their dominion"** (v12) — Aramaic הֶעְדִּיו, active with no subject named (so v26). **"yet their lives were prolonged" → "and a lengthening in life was given to them"** — Aramaic אַרְכָה בְחַיִּין יְהִיבַת לְהוֹן. **"for a season and time" → "until a season and a time"** — Aramaic עַד־זְמַן וְעִדָּן; זְמָן "season" (v12, 22, 25 "seasons"), עִדָּן "time" (v12, 25).
- **"one like the Son of man" → "one like a son of man"** (v13) — Aramaic כְּבַר אֱנָשׁ, "like a son of man", without the article: one in the likeness of a man (as the beasts are "like" a lion, a bear, a leopard); no capital. **"came with the clouds of heaven" → "with the clouds of the heavens … was coming"** — Aramaic אָתֵה הֲוָה, a participle. **"came to" → "up to … he came"** — Aramaic וְעַד־… מְטָה, "reach, arrive" (v22 "arrived"). **"they brought him near before him"** — Aramaic הַקְרְבוּהִי, active with no subject named.
- **"glory" → "honor"** (v14) — Aramaic יְקָר (Hebrew כָּבוֹד is "glory"). **"that all people … should serve him" → "and all the peoples … shall serve him"** — Aramaic וְכֹל … לֵהּ יִפְלְחוּן; "that" is not written. **"an everlasting dominion"** (v14, 27 "kingdom") — Aramaic שָׁלְטָן עָלַם, literally "a dominion of the age". **"destroyed"** — Aramaic תִתְחַבַּל.
- **"I Daniel was grieved in my spirit in the midst of my body" → "My spirit was distressed, I, Daniel, in the midst of the sheath"** (v15) — Aramaic אֶתְכְּרִיַּת רוּחִי אֲנָה דָנִיֵּאל בְּגוֹא נִדְנֶה: the spirit is the subject; נִדְנֶה "sheath" (the body as the sheath of the spirit; as 1 Chr 21:27 נָדָן of a sword). A reading "because of this" is found in a Qumran copy (from memory); the MT is kept. **"troubled me" → "alarmed me"** (v15, 28) — Aramaic בְּהַל.
- **"one of them that stood by" → "one of those who were standing"** (v16) — Aramaic קָאֲמַיָּא. **"the truth"** (v16) — Aramaic יַצִּיבָא, "what is certain"; v19 the verb of the same root, "to make certain". **"So he told me" → "And he spoke to me"** — Aramaic וַאֲמַר־לִי. **"of the things" → "of the matters"** — Aramaic מִלַּיָּא; the same word is "words" in v1, 11, 25 and "matter" in v28.
- **"These great beasts, which are four, are four kings" → "These great beasts, which are four: four kings shall arise"** (v17) — the Aramaic sets the beasts first and has no "are".
- **"But the saints of the most High" → "And the holy ones of the Most High"** (v18, 22, 25, 27) — Aramaic וִיקַבְּלוּן … קַדִּישֵׁי עֶלְיוֹנִין: וְ "and"; עֶלְיוֹנִין is plural in form ("Most High ones"), a plural of majesty, set beside the singular עִלָּאָה of v25 (qere); the token keeps the plural form. **"take" → "receive"** — Aramaic קַבֵּל. **"for ever, even for ever and ever" → "to the age, and to the age of the ages"** — Aramaic עַד־עָלְמָא וְעַד עָלַם עָלְמַיָּא.
- **"Then I would know the truth of" → "Then I wished to make certain about"** (v19) — Aramaic צְבִית לְיַצָּבָא. **"from all the others" → "from all of them"** — qere כָּלְּהֵין. **"exceeding dreadful, whose teeth were of iron, and his nails of brass" → "exceedingly fearsome, its teeth of iron and its claws of bronze"** — no "were"; qere שִׁנַּהּ.
- **"that were in his head" → "that were on its head"** (v20) — Aramaic בְרֵאשַׁהּ. **"before whom three fell"** — qere וּנְפַלָה, קֳדָמַהּ. **"even of that horn that had eyes" → "and that horn had eyes"** — Aramaic וְקַרְנָא דִכֵּן וְעַיְנִין לַהּ. **"very great things" → "great things"** — Aramaic רַבְרְבָן, as v8. **"whose look was more stout than his fellows" → "its look was greater than its companions"** — Aramaic חֶזְוַהּ רַב מִן־חַבְרָתַהּ.
- **"made war with the saints, and prevailed" → "was making war with the holy ones and prevailing"** (v21) — Aramaic עָבְדָה … וְיָכְלָה, participles.
- **"judgment was given to the saints"** (v22) — Aramaic דִינָא יְהִב לְקַדִּישֵׁי: לְ "to" or "for": the judgment given to them to exercise, or in their favor; both are possible. **"the time came that" → "the season arrived, and"** — Aramaic וְזִמְנָא מְטָה … וּמַלְכוּתָא הֶחֱסִנוּ.
- **"The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom" → "The fourth beast: a fourth kingdom shall be on the earth"** (v23) — the Aramaic sets the beast first; qere רְבִיעָאָה; no article. **"from all kingdoms" → "from all the kingdoms"**; **"the whole earth" → "all the earth"**.
- **"the ten horns out of this kingdom are ten kings" → "the ten horns: out of this kingdom ten kings shall arise"** (v24) — Aramaic מִנַּהּ מַלְכוּתָה, literally "out of it, the kingdom". **"from the first" → "from the former ones"** — Aramaic קַדְמָיֵא, plural. **"subdue" → "bring low"** — Aramaic הַשְׁפֵּל.
- **"great words" → "words"** (v25) — Aramaic מִלִּין, without "great". **"against"** — Aramaic לְצַד, literally "at the side of". **"the most High"** — qere עִלָּאָה (ketiv עליא). **"times and laws" → "seasons and law"** — Aramaic זִמְנִין וְדָת: דָּת singular (the Persian loan word, not *torah*). **"until a time and times and the dividing of time" → "until a time and times and half a time"** — Aramaic עַד־עִדָּן וְעִדָּנִין וּפְלַג עִדָּן; the plural עִדָּנִין may be meant as "two times" (pointed as a plural).
- **"to consume and to destroy it" → "to destroy and to make perish"** (v26) — Aramaic לְהַשְׁמָדָה וּלְהוֹבָדָה.
- **"shall be given" → "has been given"** (v27) — Aramaic יְהִיבַת, a perfect: the gift stated as done. **"of the kingdom" → "of the kingdoms"** — Aramaic מַלְכְוָת, plural. **"whose kingdom is" → "his kingdom is"** — Aramaic מַלְכוּתֵהּ: "his" may be the Most High's or the people's (עַם is singular); the Aramaic does not decide. **"and obey him" → "and obey"** — Aramaic יִשְׁתַּמְּעוּן, the reflexive of "hear": "make themselves hearers"; לֵהּ "him" stands once, before both verbs.
- **"Hitherto is the end" → "Here is the end"** (v28) — Aramaic עַד־כָּה, literally "until here". **"my cogitations" → "my thoughts"** — Aramaic רַעְיוֹנַי. **"my countenance changed in me" → "my brightness was changed upon me"** — Aramaic זִיוַי, "my brightness" (as 2:31; 5:6, 9): the color of his face.

### Notes
- **Language** — the chapter is Aramaic, the last of the Aramaic section 2:4b–7:28; Hebrew resumes at 8:1.
- **"One like a son of man"** (v13) — in the Gospels "the Son of Man" is Yeshua's own name for himself (80+ times), and Matt 24:30; 26:64; Mark 14:62; Rev 1:7, 13 take up this verse. The Aramaic itself says "one like a son of man".
- **Ancient of Days** / throne vision — echoed in Rev 1; 4–5.
- **"A time and times and half a time"** (v25) — commonly read as three and a half years; the same words in Hebrew in 12:7; echoed in Rev 12:14.
