# Daniel 9

[1] In the first year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ son of ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, of the seed of ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧, who was made king over the kingdom of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ —

[2] in the first year of his reign, I, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, understood in the books the number of the years, of which the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ the prophet, to fill up for the ruins of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ seventy years.

[3] And I set my face to ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to seek by prayer and pleas for favor, with fasting and sackcloth and ashes.

[4] And I prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and confessed, and said, "Ah, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, the great and fearsome ⟦el|El|God⟧, who keeps the covenant and the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ for those who love him and for those who keep his commandments,

[5] we have sinned, and we have done iniquity, and we have done wickedly, and we have rebelled, and turned aside from your commandments and from your judgments.

[6] And we have not listened to your servants the prophets, who spoke in your name to our kings, our princes, and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.

[7] To you, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, is the righteousness, and to us the shame of face, as at this day, to the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and to the dwellers of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, the near and the far, in all the lands where you have driven them, for their unfaithfulness that they committed against you.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, to us is the shame of face, to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers, because we have sinned against you.

[9] To ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ belong the compassions and the forgivenesses, for we have rebelled against him;

[10] and we have not listened to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to walk in his ⟦torah|torot|laws⟧, which he set before us by the hand of his servants the prophets.

[11] And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ have overstepped your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, and turned aside, so as not to listen to your voice; and the curse and the oath that is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has been poured out upon us, for we have sinned against him.

[12] And he has made his word stand, which he spoke against us and against our judges who judged us, to bring upon us a great evil; for under all the heavens there has not been done what has been done in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] As it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, all this evil has come upon us; and we have not besought the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to turn back from our iniquity and to have insight in your truth.

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ kept awake over the evil and brought it upon us; for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is righteous in all his works that he has done, and we have not listened to his voice.

[15] "And now, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought your people out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ with a strong hand, and made for yourself a name, as at this day: we have sinned, we have been wicked.

[16] ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, according to all your righteousnesses, let your anger and your fury turn back, please, from your city ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, your holy mountain; for because of our sins and the iniquities of our fathers, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and your people are a reproach to all who are around us.

[17] And now, listen, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to the prayer of your servant and to his pleas for favor, and make your face shine upon your sanctuary that is desolate, on account of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧.

[18] Incline, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, your ear and hear; open your eyes and see our desolations, and the city over which your name is called; for not on account of our righteousnesses are we making our pleas for favor fall before you, but on account of your great compassions.

[19] ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, hear! ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, pardon! ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, give heed and act! Do not delay, on your own account, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for your name is called over your city and over your people."

[20] And while I was still speaking and praying, and confessing my sin and the sin of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and making my plea for favor fall before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for the holy mountain of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[21] and while I was still speaking in the prayer, the man ⟦gabriel|Gavriel|Gabriel⟧, whom I had seen in the vision at the first, flying swiftly, touched me about the time of the evening grain offering.

[22] And he gave understanding, and spoke with me, and said, "⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, now I have come out to give you insight in understanding.

[23] At the beginning of your pleas for favor a word went out, and I have come to tell it, for you are greatly desired. So consider the word, and understand the sight.

[24] "**Seventy sevens** are decreed upon your people
and upon your holy city,
to shut up the transgression,
and to complete sin,
and to make atonement for iniquity,
and to bring in righteousness of the ages,
and to seal vision and prophet,
and to anoint a holy of holies.

[25] And you shall know and have insight:
from the going out of a word
to restore and to build ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
until ⟦anointed|a mashiach|an anointed one⟧, a leader,
are seven sevens;
and sixty-two sevens
it shall return and be built, square and moat,
and in the distress of the times.

[26] And after the sixty-two sevens
⟦anointed|a mashiach|an anointed one⟧ shall be cut off and shall have nothing;
and the city and the holy place
the people of a leader who comes shall ruin;
and its end shall be in the flood,
and until the end of war
desolations are decided.

[27] And he shall make a covenant strong for the many, one seven;
and half of the seven
he shall make sacrifice and grain offering cease;
and upon a wing of detestable things, one making desolate,
and until a full end, and one decided,
it shall be poured out upon the desolating one."

## Changes

- **"Lord" → "Yahweh" / "Adonai"** (throughout) — the prayer has both names: יְהוָה (v2, 4, 8, 10, 13, 14, 20) and אֲדֹנָי alone (v3, 4, 7, 9, 15, 16, 17, 19), each with its own token; KJV prints "Lord" for both. **"God" → "*Elohim*" / "El"** — אֱלֹהִים (v3 הָאֱלֹהִים with the article, as the reviewer's הָאֱלֹהִים row: no "the" in the verse; v11 the same); v4 הָאֵל "the great and fearsome El".
- **"of the seed of the Medes" → "of the seed of Media"** (v1) — Hebrew מִזֶּרַע מָדָי, the name of the land and people (as 8:20), not the gentilic "Mede" (מָדִי, 11:1): the token is `media`. **"which was made king over the realm" → "who was made king over the kingdom"** — Hebrew הָמְלַךְ, passive (the הִמְלִיךְ row); מַלְכוּת.
- **"I Daniel understood by books" → "I, Daniel, understood in the books"** (v2) — Hebrew בִּינֹתִי בַּסְּפָרִים, with the article. **"whereof the word of the Lord came" → "of which the word of Yahweh came"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הָיָה דְבַר־יְהוָה. **"that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations" → "to fill up for the ruins of Jerusalem seventy years"** — Hebrew לְמַלֹּאות לְחָרְבוֹת: מִלֵּא "fill"; חָרְבָּה "ruin" (the Lev 26 row; שֹׁמֵמוֹת "desolations" in v18, 26).
- **"set my face unto" → "set my face to"** (v3) — Hebrew וָאֶתְּנָה אֶת־פָּנַי, literally "gave my face". **"supplications" → "pleas for favor"** (v3, 17, 18, 23) — Hebrew תַּחֲנוּנִים (the 2 Chr 6:21 row); תְּחִנָּה "plea for favor" (v20).
- **"made my confession" → "confessed"** (v4, 20) — Hebrew הִתְוַדָּה. **"O Lord" → "Ah, Adonai"** — Hebrew אָנָּא (the אָנָּא row "Ah"), as Neh 1:5; no "O" (the vocative row) here or in v7, 8, 15–19, 22. **"dreadful" → "fearsome"** — Hebrew נוֹרָא. **"keeping the covenant and mercy" → "who keeps the covenant and the steadfast love"** — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר הַבְּרִית וְהַחֶסֶד, with the article on both (Neh 1:5 has וָחֶסֶד).
- **"have committed iniquity" → "we have done iniquity"** (v5) — Hebrew עָוִינוּ, the verb of עָוֹן. **"have done wickedly"** — qere הִרְשַׁעְנוּ (ketiv spelled otherwise, the same word). **"even by departing from thy precepts and from thy judgments" → "and turned aside from your commandments and from your judgments"** — Hebrew וְסוֹר מִמִּצְוֹתֶךָ וּמִמִּשְׁפָּטֶיךָ: an infinitive "and turning aside" (also v11); מִצְוָה "commandment".
- **"hearkened unto" → "listened to"** (v6) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל (the row). **"and to all the people of the land"** — עַם הָאָרֶץ.
- **"righteousness belongeth unto thee" → "To you … is the righteousness"** (v7) — Hebrew לְךָ אֲדֹנָי הַצְּדָקָה, with the article. **"confusion of faces" → "the shame of face"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew בֹּשֶׁת הַפָּנִים. **"the men of Judah"** — Hebrew לְאִישׁ יְהוּדָה, a collective singular ("the man of Judah"). **"inhabitants" → "dwellers"** — יֹשְׁבֵי (the יָשַׁב row). **"that are near, and that are far off" → "the near and the far"**. **"through all the countries whither" → "in all the lands where"**. **"because of their trespass that they have trespassed" → "for their unfaithfulness that they committed"** — Hebrew בְּמַעֲלָם אֲשֶׁר מָעֲלוּ (the מָעַל מַעַל row).
- **"O Lord" → "Yahweh"** (v8) — the Hebrew has יְהוָה here; KJV prints "Lord" as for אֲדֹנָי.
- **"mercies and forgivenesses" → "the compassions and the forgivenesses"** (v9) — Hebrew הָרַחֲמִים וְהַסְּלִחוֹת, plural and with the article (the רַחֲמִים and סְלִיחָה rows). **"though" → "for"** — Hebrew כִּי.
- **"obeyed the voice" → "listened to the voice"** (v10, 11, 14) — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל (the row: "obey" is not the Hebrew word). **"set before us" → "set before us"** — Hebrew נָתַן לְפָנֵינוּ, literally "gave before us" (the reviewer's row). **"by his servants" → "by the hand of his servants"** — Hebrew בְּיַד.
- **"have transgressed thy law" → "have overstepped your Torah"** (v11) — Hebrew עָבְרוּ אֶת־תּוֹרָתֶךָ (the עָבַר "overstep" row). **"that they might not obey" → "so as not to listen to"** — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי שְׁמוֹעַ. **"therefore the curse is poured upon us, and the oath" → "and the curse and the oath … has been poured out upon us"** — Hebrew וַתִּתַּךְ עָלֵינוּ הָאָלָה וְהַשְּׁבֻעָה, one verb before both nouns; אָלָה "curse" (the row), שְׁבוּעָה "oath".
- **"he hath confirmed his words" → "he has made his word stand"** (v12) — qere דְּבָרוֹ "his word" (ketiv "his words"); הֵקִים "make stand" (the row). **"our judges that judged us"** — שֹׁפְטֵינוּ (the שָׁפַט row; "rulers" is not the word). **"a great evil"** (v12, 13, 14) — Hebrew רָעָה. **"under the whole heaven" → "under all the heavens"**. **"upon Jerusalem" → "in Jerusalem"** — Hebrew בִּירוּשָׁלִַם.
- **"yet made we not our prayer before" → "and we have not besought the face of"** (v13) — Hebrew חִלִּינוּ אֶת־פְּנֵי (the חִלָּה פְּנֵי row). **"that we might turn from our iniquities" → "to turn back from our iniquity"** — Hebrew לָשׁוּב מֵעֲוֹנֵנוּ, singular. **"and understand thy truth" → "and to have insight in your truth"** — Hebrew וּלְהַשְׂכִּיל בַּאֲמִתֶּךָ (the הִשְׂכִּיל row).
- **"hath the Lord watched upon the evil" → "Yahweh kept awake over the evil"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁקֹד (the שָׁקַד row, as Jer 1:12; 31:28). **"which he doeth" → "that he has done"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה. **"for we obeyed not" → "and we have not listened"** — וְ "and".
- **"with a mighty hand" → "with a strong hand"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּיָד חֲזָקָה (the row). **"hast gotten thee renown" → "made for yourself a name"** — Hebrew וַתַּעַשׂ־לְךָ שֵׁם. **"we have done wickedly" → "we have been wicked"** — Hebrew רָשָׁעְנוּ, the plain stem (v5 has the causative).
- **"according to all thy righteousness" → "according to all your righteousnesses"** (v16, 18) — Hebrew צִדְקֹתֶךָ, plural: the righteous acts. **"I beseech thee, let … be turned away" → "let … turn back, please"** — Hebrew יָשָׁב־נָא. **"fury"** — חֵמָה. **"thy holy mountain"** (v16, 20) — Hebrew הַר־קָדְשֶׁךָ, literally "the mountain of your holiness". **"are become a reproach" → "are a reproach"** — no verb in the Hebrew; חֶרְפָּה "reproach".
- **"hear the prayer" → "listen … to the prayer"** (v17) — Hebrew שְׁמַע … אֶל. **"cause thy face to shine"** — Hebrew וְהָאֵר פָּנֶיךָ. **"for the Lord's sake" → "on account of Adonai"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן אֲדֹנָי, the third person in the address (the לְמַעַן row); the Greek of Theodotion reads "for your sake, Lord" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"incline thine ear … open thine eyes" → "Incline, my Elohim, your ear … open your eyes"** (v18) — the name stands after the first verb; qere פְּקַח (ketiv a longer imperative, the same sense). **"behold" → "see"** — רְאֵה. **"which is called by thy name" → "over which your name is called"** (v18, 19) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־נִקְרָא שִׁמְךָ עָלֶיהָ. **"we do not present our supplications" → "are we making our pleas for favor fall"** (v18, 20) — Hebrew מַפִּילִים (the Jer 36:7 row). **"for our righteousnesses … but for thy great mercies" → "on account of our righteousnesses … but on account of your great compassions"** — Hebrew עַל … כִּי עַל.
- **"forgive" → "pardon"** (v19) — Hebrew סְלָחָה (סָלַח, said only of God). **"hearken and do" → "give heed and act"** — Hebrew הַקֲשִׁיבָה וַעֲשֵׂה (the הִקְשִׁיב row). **"defer not, for thine own sake" → "Do not delay, on your own account"** — Hebrew לְמַעַנְךָ. **"thy city and thy people are called by thy name" → "your name is called over your city and over your people"**.
- **"whiles I was speaking" → "while I was still speaking"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew וְעוֹד. **"presenting my supplication" → "making my plea for favor fall"** — Hebrew וּמַפִּיל תְּחִנָּתִי.
- **"Yea, whiles I was speaking in prayer" → "and while I was still speaking in the prayer"** (v21). **"being caused to fly swiftly" → "flying swiftly"** — Hebrew מֻעָף בִּיעָף, uncertain: from עוּף "fly" ("made to fly in flight") or from יָעֵף "be weary" ("wearied with weariness", of Daniel or of Gabriel); the verse follows the first. **"touched me"** — Hebrew נֹגֵעַ אֵלַי, a participle: "touching / reaching me". **"the evening oblation" → "the evening grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחַת־עָרֶב (the מִנְחָה row).
- **"he informed me" → "he gave understanding"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיָּבֶן, with no object; the Greek and Syriac read "and he came" (from memory); the MT is kept. **"O Daniel" → "Daniel"**. **"I am now come forth to give thee skill and understanding" → "now I have come out to give you insight in understanding"** — Hebrew לְהַשְׂכִּילְךָ בִינָה, one verb with בִינָה as its object; "and" is not written.
- **"the commandment came forth" → "a word went out"** (v23, 25) — Hebrew יָצָא דָבָר, without the article; v25 מֹצָא דָבָר "the going out of a word". **"to shew thee" → "to tell it"** — Hebrew לְהַגִּיד, no object. **"thou art greatly beloved" → "you are greatly desired"** — Hebrew חֲמוּדוֹת אָתָּה, literally "you are desirable things", of the root חָמַד "desire" (so 10:11, 19); "loved" is not the word. **"understand the matter, and consider the vision" → "consider the word, and understand the sight"** — Hebrew וּבִין בַּדָּבָר וְהָבֵן בַּמַּרְאֶה: בִּין qal then hifil; מַרְאֶה "sight", as 8:16, 26, 27.
- **"Seventy weeks" → "Seventy sevens"** (v24–27) — Hebrew שָׁבֻעִים שִׁבְעִים: שָׁבוּעַ is a unit of seven ("week" where days are meant, as Gen 29:27 and 10:2–3 "three sevens of days"); here the unit is not named; "weeks" in KJV. **"are determined upon" → "are decreed upon"** — Hebrew נֶחְתַּךְ, only here, singular ("cut off, decided"). **"to finish the transgression" → "to shut up the transgression"** — Hebrew לְכַלֵּא, as printed of כָּלָא "keep back, shut up"; KJV takes it as כַּלֵּה "finish" (the spelling of the two can meet). **"to make an end of sins" → "to complete sin"** — qere וּלְהָתֵם חַטָּאת (תמם, as 8:23 "come to the full"); the ketiv reads וְלַחְתֹּם חַטָּאוֹת "to seal sins". **"to make reconciliation for iniquity" → "to make atonement for iniquity"** — Hebrew לְכַפֵּר (the כִּפֶּר row). **"everlasting righteousness" → "righteousness of the ages"** — Hebrew צֶדֶק עֹלָמִים, plural of עוֹלָם. **"to seal up the vision and prophecy" → "to seal vision and prophet"** — Hebrew חָזוֹן וְנָבִיא, without articles; נָבִיא is "prophet", not "prophecy". **"to anoint the most Holy" → "to anoint a holy of holies"** — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, without the article: "holiness of holinesses", the words of the most holy things and of the inner room (the קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים rows); whether a place, a thing or a person is meant the Hebrew does not say.
- **"Know therefore and understand" → "And you shall know and have insight"** (v25) — Hebrew וְתֵדַע וְתַשְׂכֵּל, imperfects. **"the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build" → "the going out of a word to restore and to build"** — Hebrew לְהָשִׁיב, the hifil of שׁוּב "bring back, restore". **"unto the Messiah the Prince" → "until an anointed one, a leader"** — Hebrew עַד־מָשִׁיחַ נָגִיד, both nouns without the article: מָשִׁיחַ "anointed" (of priests, kings, Cyrus in Isa 45:1), נָגִיד "leader" (the נָגִיד row); no capital and no title: who is meant the verse does not say. **"shall be seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks" → "are seven sevens; and sixty-two sevens"** — the accents put the main pause (atnah) after שָׁבֻעִים שִׁבְעָה: seven sevens run to the anointed leader, and sixty-two sevens belong to the building; KJV joins the two numbers. **"the street shall be built again, and the wall" → "it shall return and be built, square and moat"** — Hebrew תָּשׁוּב וְנִבְנְתָה: "return" and a second verb (the שׁוּב row: = "be built again"); רְחוֹב "square" (the row); חָרוּץ "moat, trench" (only here in this sense; uncertain). **"even in troublous times" → "and in the distress of the times"** — Hebrew וּבְצוֹק הָעִתִּים.
- **"after threescore and two weeks" → "after the sixty-two sevens"** (v26) — with the article. **"shall Messiah be cut off" → "an anointed one shall be cut off"** — Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ, without the article, as v25. **"but not for himself" → "and shall have nothing"** — Hebrew וְאֵין לוֹ, literally "and there is not to him" (what he lacks is not named). **"the people of the prince that shall come" → "the people of a leader who comes"** — Hebrew עַם נָגִיד הַבָּא: נָגִיד as in v25, without the article; הַבָּא "the one coming" may belong to the leader or to the people. **"the sanctuary" → "the holy place"** — Hebrew הַקֹּדֶשׁ, "the holy (thing)"; "place" is supplied; מִקְדָּשׁ "sanctuary" is v17. **"shall destroy" → "shall ruin"** — Hebrew יַשְׁחִית (the הִשְׁחִית row). **"the end thereof shall be with a flood" → "its end shall be in the flood"** — Hebrew וְקִצּוֹ בַשֶּׁטֶף, "its" or "his". **"unto the end of the war desolations are determined" → "until the end of war desolations are decided"** — Hebrew וְעַד קֵץ מִלְחָמָה נֶחֱרֶצֶת שֹׁמֵמוֹת; the accent pause after מִלְחָמָה.
- **"he shall confirm the covenant with many" → "he shall make a covenant strong for the many"** (v27) — Hebrew וְהִגְבִּיר בְּרִית לָרַבִּים: hifil of גָּבַר "prevail, be strong"; בְּרִית without the article, רַבִּים with it. **"for one week" → "one seven"**. **"in the midst of the week" → "half of the seven"** — Hebrew וַחֲצִי הַשָּׁבוּעַ: "for half" or "at the half". **"the sacrifice and the oblation" → "sacrifice and grain offering"** — without articles; הִשְׁבִּית "make cease". **"for the overspreading of abominations he shall make it desolate" → "upon a wing of detestable things, one making desolate"** — Hebrew וְעַל כְּנַף שִׁקּוּצִים מְשֹׁמֵם, as printed: כָּנָף "wing" (of a building, a corner, or of the altar: not said); שִׁקּוּץ "detestable thing" (the 1 Kgs 11 row; תּוֹעֵבָה is "abomination"); מְשֹׁמֵם, the polel participle of שָׁמֵם, "one making desolate". **"even until the consummation, and that determined shall be poured upon the desolate" → "and until a full end, and one decided, it shall be poured out upon the desolating one"** — Hebrew וְעַד־כָּלָה וְנֶחֱרָצָה תִּתַּךְ עַל־שֹׁמֵם, as Isa 10:23; 28:22 "a full end, and one decided"; שֹׁמֵם, the plain participle, "the desolating one" or "the desolate one": the Hebrew does not decide.

### Notes
- **Jeremiah's seventy years** (v2) — Jer 25:11–12; 29:10.
- **The prayer** (v4–19) — close to Neh 1:5–11; 9:6–37; Ezra 9:6–15.
- **"Seventy sevens"** (v24) — commonly taken as seventy sevens of years (490 years), the sevens of Lev 25:8. The anointed one of v25 and of v26 is read of Joshua the high priest or Zerubbabel, of Onias III, or of Yeshua in Christian reading; the verses themselves name no one.
- **"Detestable things … one making desolate"** (v27) — the same words stand in 11:31 and 12:11; Matt 24:15 and Mark 13:14 take them up in the Greek form ("the abomination of desolation").
