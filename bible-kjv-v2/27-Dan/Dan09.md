# Daniel 9

[1] In the first year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ son of ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, by descent a ⟦medes|Madi|Mede⟧, who was made king over the realm of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ —

[3] Then I turned my face to ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, seeking him by prayer and pleas for mercy with fasting and sackcloth and ashes.

[4] I prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and made confession, saying, "O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, the great and awesome ⟦el|El|God⟧, who keeps covenant and ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ with those who love him and keep his commandments,

[5] we have sinned and done wrong and acted wickedly and rebelled, turning aside from your commandments and rules.

[6] We have not listened to your servants the prophets, who spoke in your name to our kings, our princes, our fathers, and all the people of the land.

[7] To you, O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, belongs righteousness, but to us open shame, as at this day, to the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, to the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, those who are near and those who are far away, in all the lands to which you have driven them, because of the treachery that they have committed against you.

[8] To us, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, belongs open shame, to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers, because we have sinned against you.

[9] To ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ belong mercy and forgiveness, for we have rebelled against him

[10] and have not obeyed the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ by walking in his ⟦torah|torot|laws⟧, which he set before us by his servants the prophets.

[11] All ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has transgressed your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and turned aside, refusing to obey your voice. And the curse and oath that are written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ have been poured out upon us, because we have sinned against him.

[12] He has confirmed his words, which he spoke against us and against our rulers who ruled us, by bringing upon us a great calamity. For under the whole heaven there has not been done anything like what has been done against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] As it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, all this calamity has come upon us; yet we have not entreated the favor of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, turning from our iniquities and gaining insight by your truth.

[14] Therefore ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has kept ready the calamity and has brought it upon us, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is righteous in all the works that he has done, and we have not obeyed his voice.

[15] "And now, O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought your people out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ with a mighty hand, and have made a name for yourself, as at this day, we have sinned; we have done wickedly.

[18] O my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, incline your ear and hear. Open your eyes and see our desolations, and the city that is called by your name. For we do not present our pleas before you because of our righteousness, but because of your great mercy.

[19] O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, hear; O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, forgive. O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, pay attention and act. Delay not, for your own sake, O my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because your city and your people are called by your name."

[20] While I was speaking and praying, confessing my sin and the sin of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and presenting my plea before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for the holy hill of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[21] while I was speaking in prayer, the man ⟦gabriel|Gavriel|Gabriel⟧, whom I had seen in the vision at the first, came to me in swift flight at the time of the evening sacrifice.

[22] He made me understand, speaking with me and saying, "O ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, I have now come out to give you insight and understanding.

[23] At the beginning of your pleas for mercy a word went out, and I have come to tell it to you, for you are greatly loved. Therefore consider the word and understand the vision.

[24] "**Seventy weeks** are decreed about your people

[26] And after the sixty-two weeks,
⟦anointed|a Mashiach|an anointed one⟧ shall be cut off

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly; Daniel's prayer repeatedly invokes the covenant name of Israel's God.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*" / "El"** (throughout; v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; used throughout Daniel's prayer; v4 has הָאֵל *ha-El* ("the great and awesome El").
- **"Messiah" → "Anointed One"** (vv25, 26) — Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ *mashiach*, "anointed one"; KJV's "Messiah" is a Latinized transliteration; translated as "Anointed One."

### Notes
- **Seventy weeks = 490 years (seventy weeks of years)**.
- **v26 "Anointed One cut off"**: Christian interpretation points to Yeshua's crucifixion.
- **v27 "abomination that makes desolate"**: echoed Matt 24:15; Mark 13:14; 2 Thess 2:3-4.
