# Daniel 10

[1] In the third year of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧, a word was revealed to ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, whose name was called ⟦belteshazzar|Belteshatzar|Belteshazzar⟧; and the word was truth, and a great warfare. And he understood the word, and understanding was his in the sight.

[2] In those days I, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, was mourning three sevens of days.

[3] Desirable bread I did not eat, and flesh and wine did not come into my mouth, and I did not anoint myself at all, until the three sevens of days were fulfilled.

[4] And on the twenty-fourth day of the first month, I was by the side of the great river, that is, ⟦tigris|Chiddeqel|Tigris⟧.

[5] And I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, a man, one, clothed in linen, and his loins were girded with fine gold of ⟦uphaz|Ufaz|Uphaz⟧.

[6] And his body was like beryl, and his face like the appearance of lightning, and his eyes like torches of fire, and his arms and his feet like the look of burnished bronze, and the voice of his words like the voice of a multitude.

[7] And I, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, alone saw the vision, and the men who were with me did not see the vision; but a great trembling fell upon them, and they fled to hide themselves.

[8] And I was left alone and saw this great vision, and no power was left in me; and my majesty was turned upon me to ruin, and I retained no power.

[9] And I heard the voice of his words; and when I heard the voice of his words, I was in a deep sleep on my face, with my face to the earth.

[10] And behold, a hand touched me and set me swaying on my knees and the palms of my hands.

[11] And he said to me, "⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, man greatly desired, understand the words that I am speaking to you, and stand where you stand, for now I have been sent to you." And when he spoke this word with me, I stood up trembling.

[12] And he said to me, "Do not fear, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, for from the first day that you gave your heart to understand and to humble yourself before your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, your words were heard; and I have come because of your words.

[13] And the prince of the kingdom of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧ was standing against me twenty-one days; and behold, Michael, one of the first princes, came to help me, and I remained there beside the kings of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧.

[14] And I have come to make you understand what shall befall your people in the latter days; for there is yet a vision for the days."

[15] And when he spoke with me according to these words, I set my face to the earth and was made mute.

[16] And behold, one like the likeness of the sons of man was touching my lips; and I opened my mouth and spoke, and said to the one standing before me, "My lord, through the vision my pangs have turned upon me, and I have retained no power.

[17] And how can the servant of this my lord speak with this my lord? And as for me, from now no power stands in me, and no breath is left in me."

[18] And again one like the appearance of a man touched me and made me strong.

[19] And he said, "Do not fear, man greatly desired; ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to you; be strong, and be strong." And when he spoke with me, I strengthened myself and said, "Let my lord speak, for you have made me strong."

[20] And he said, "Do you know why I have come to you? And now I shall return to fight with the prince of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧; and I am going out, and behold, the prince of ⟦greece|Yavan|Greece⟧ is coming.

[21] But I will tell you what is inscribed in the writing of truth; and there is not one who strengthens himself with me against these, except Michael, your prince."

## Changes

- **"a thing was revealed" → "a word was revealed"** (v1) — Hebrew דָּבָר נִגְלָה (the נִגְלָה row); "word" each time in v1. **"whose name was called"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־נִקְרָא שְׁמוֹ. **"the thing was true" → "the word was truth"** — Hebrew וֶאֱמֶת הַדָּבָר, the noun. **"but the time appointed was long" → "and a great warfare"** — Hebrew וְצָבָא גָדוֹל: צָבָא, the word of "host" (the row), here of a hard service or a war (as Job 7:1; Isa 40:2); KJV takes it of a long time. **"had understanding of the vision" → "understanding was his in the sight"** — Hebrew וּבִינָה לוֹ בַּמַּרְאֶה: מַרְאֶה "sight" as 8:16, 26, 27; מַרְאָה "vision" (the row) in v7, 8, 16.
- **"three full weeks" → "three sevens of days"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew שְׁלֹשָׁה שָׁבֻעִים יָמִים: שָׁבוּעַ "a seven" as in 9:24–27, here named as days.
- **"pleasant bread" → "Desirable bread"** (v3) — Hebrew לֶחֶם חֲמֻדוֹת, literally "bread of desirable things" (the word of 9:23; v11, 19). **"neither came flesh nor wine in my mouth" → "and flesh and wine did not come into my mouth"** — Hebrew אֶל־פִּי. **"neither did I anoint myself at all"** — Hebrew וְסוֹךְ לֹא־סָכְתִּי, literally "and anointing I did not anoint myself" (the infinitive with its verb; the סוּךְ row). **"till three whole weeks were fulfilled" → "until the three sevens of days were fulfilled"** — Hebrew עַד־מְלֹאת, literally "until the filling".
- **"in the four and twentieth day" → "on the twenty-fourth day"** (v4). **"as I was by the side of" → "I was by the side of"** — Hebrew וַאֲנִי הָיִיתִי עַל יַד, literally "by the hand of". **"which is Hiddekel" → "that is, Chiddeqel"** — Hebrew הוּא חִדָּקֶל, the Hebrew name of the Tigris (as Gen 2:14).
- **"a certain man" → "a man, one"** (v5) — Hebrew אִישׁ־אֶחָד. **"clothed in linen"** — Hebrew לָבוּשׁ בַּדִּים, plural, as Ezek 9:2. **"whose loins were girded" → "and his loins were girded"** — Hebrew וּמָתְנָיו. **"with fine gold"** — Hebrew כֶּתֶם.
- **"His body also was like the beryl" → "And his body was like beryl"** (v6) — Hebrew כְתַרְשִׁישׁ, "like Tarshish (stone)": beryl as the breastpiece row (identification uncertain). **"lamps of fire" → "torches of fire"** — Hebrew לַפִּידֵי אֵשׁ (the לַפִּיד row). **"his feet"** — Hebrew מַרְגְּלֹתָיו, literally "the parts at his feet" (in Ruth 3 "the place of his feet"): here the feet or legs themselves. **"like in colour to polished brass" → "like the look of burnished bronze"** — Hebrew כְּעֵין נְחֹשֶׁת קָלָל, as Ezek 1:7 (עַיִן "look", literally "eye"). **"the voice of a multitude"** — Hebrew הָמוֹן.
- **"for the men … saw not" → "and the men … did not see"** (v7) — Hebrew וְ "and". **"a great quaking" → "a great trembling"** — Hebrew חֲרָדָה (the חָרַד row). **"so that they fled to hide themselves" → "and they fled to hide themselves"** — Hebrew וַיִּבְרְחוּ בְּהֵחָבֵא.
- **"Therefore I was left alone" → "And I was left alone"** (v8). **"there remained no strength in me" → "no power was left in me"** (v8, 16, 17) — Hebrew כֹּחַ "power" (the row). **"my comeliness was turned in me into corruption" → "my majesty was turned upon me to ruin"** — Hebrew וְהוֹדִי נֶהְפַּךְ עָלַי לְמַשְׁחִית: הוֹד "majesty" (the row); מַשְׁחִית "ruin", of the root שָׁחַת. **"I retained no strength" → "I retained no power"** (v8, 16) — Hebrew עָצַר כֹּחַ (the row).
- **"Yet heard I" → "And I heard"** (v9). **"then was I in a deep sleep on my face, and my face toward the ground" → "I was in a deep sleep on my face, with my face to the earth"** — Hebrew וּפָנַי אָרְצָה.
- **"an hand touched me, which set me upon my knees" → "a hand touched me and set me swaying on my knees"** (v10) — Hebrew וַתְּנִיעֵנִי, hifil of נוּעַ "sway, stagger" (the row).
- **"O Daniel, a man greatly beloved" → "Daniel, man greatly desired"** (v11, 19) — Hebrew אִישׁ־חֲמֻדוֹת, "man of desirable things" (as 9:23); no "O". **"that I speak" → "that I am speaking"** — a participle. **"stand upright" → "stand where you stand"** — Hebrew עֲמֹד עַל־עָמְדֶךָ, literally "stand upon your standing" (as 8:18). **"when he had spoken … I stood trembling" → "when he spoke this word with me, I stood up trembling"** — Hebrew מַרְעִיד, of the root רָעַד "tremble" (another root than חָרַד in v7: English has one word).
- **"Then said he" → "And he said"** (v12). **"Fear not" → "Do not fear"** (v12, 19). **"thou didst set thine heart" → "you gave your heart"** — Hebrew נָתַתָּ אֶת־לִבְּךָ (the נָתַן לֵב לְ row). **"to chasten thyself" → "to humble yourself"** — Hebrew לְהִתְעַנּוֹת (the הִתְעַנָּה row). **"I am come for thy words" → "I have come because of your words"** — Hebrew בִּדְבָרֶיךָ.
- **"But the prince … withstood me" → "And the prince … was standing against me"** (v13) — Hebrew עֹמֵד לְנֶגְדִּי, a participle. **"one of the chief princes" → "one of the first princes"** — Hebrew הַשָּׂרִים הָרִאשֹׁנִים. **"I remained there with the kings of Persia" → "I remained there beside the kings of Persia"** — Hebrew נוֹתַרְתִּי … אֵצֶל (the נוֹתַר row); the Greek reads "and I left him there" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"Now I am come" → "And I have come"** (v14). **"what shall befall"** — Hebrew יִקְרָה (the קָרָה row). **"in the latter days"** — the בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים row. **"for yet the vision is for many days" → "for there is yet a vision for the days"** — Hebrew כִּי־עוֹד חָזוֹן לַיָּמִים: "many" is not written; חָזוֹן without the article.
- **"when he had spoken such words unto me" → "when he spoke with me according to these words"** (v15) — Hebrew כַּדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה. **"I set my face toward the ground" → "I set my face to the earth"** — Hebrew נָתַתִּי פָנַי, literally "gave my face". **"I became dumb" → "was made mute"** — Hebrew וְנֶאֱלָמְתִּי, nifal.
- **"one like the similitude of the sons of men" → "one like the likeness of the sons of man"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּדְמוּת בְּנֵי אָדָם (the דְּמוּת row; the reviewer's בְּנֵי אָדָם row). **"touched my lips" → "was touching my lips"** — a participle. **"O my lord" → "My lord"** — Hebrew אֲדֹנִי, a man's title, not the divine name. **"by the vision my sorrows are turned upon me" → "through the vision my pangs have turned upon me"** — Hebrew צִירַי, the pangs of birth (the row).
- **"For how can the servant of this my lord talk" → "And how can the servant of this my lord speak"** (v17) — Hebrew וְהֵיךְ; עֶבֶד of the speaker before a superior (the row; the word is "slave"). **"straightway there remained no strength in me" → "from now no power stands in me"** — Hebrew מֵעַתָּה לֹא־יַעֲמָד־בִּי כֹחַ. **"breath"** — Hebrew נְשָׁמָה.
- **"Then there came again and touched me" → "And again … touched me"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיֹּסֶף וַיִּגַּע־בִּי, literally "and he added and touched" (the יָסַף row). **"and he strengthened me" → "and made me strong"** (v18, 19) — piel חִזֵּק "make strong" (the row); הִתְחַזֵּק "strengthen oneself" (v19, 21).
- **"peace be unto thee" → "shalom to you"** (v19) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם לָךְ (the reviewer's שָׁלוֹם row: the token). **"be strong, yea, be strong" → "be strong, and be strong"** — Hebrew חֲזַק וַחֲזָק. **"when he had spoken unto me, I was strengthened" → "when he spoke with me, I strengthened myself"** — Hebrew הִתְחַזַּקְתִּי.
- **"Knowest thou wherefore I come" → "Do you know why I have come"** (v20). **"and now will I return to fight with the prince of Persia" → "And now I shall return to fight with the prince of Persia"**. **"and when I am gone forth, lo, the prince of Grecia shall come" → "and I am going out, and behold, the prince of Greece is coming"** — Hebrew וַאֲנִי יוֹצֵא וְהִנֵּה שַׂר־יָוָן בָּא, participles.
- **"I will shew thee that which is noted in the scripture of truth" → "I will tell you what is inscribed in the writing of truth"** (v21) — Hebrew הָרָשׁוּם בִּכְתָב אֱמֶת (כְּתָב "writing", the Esther row). **"there is none that holdeth with me in these things" → "there is not one who strengthens himself with me against these"** — Hebrew מִתְחַזֵּק עִמִּי עַל־אֵלֶּה. **"but Michael your prince" → "except Michael, your prince"** — כִּי אִם after a negative (the row); שַׂרְכֶם "your" plural.

### Notes
- **The princes of Persia and Greece** (v13, 20) and **Michael** (v13, 21; 12:1) — beings set over the nations; Michael "your prince", of Israel. Cf. Jude 9; Rev 12:7.
- **Chapters 10–12** are one vision: the word of 10:1 is given in 11:2–12:4.
