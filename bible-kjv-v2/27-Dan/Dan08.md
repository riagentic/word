# Daniel 8

[1] In the third year of the reign of ⟦belshazzar|Belshatzar|Belshazzar⟧ the king, a vision appeared to me, I, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, after the one that appeared to me at the first.

[2] And I saw in the vision; and it came to pass, when I saw, that I was in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel, which is in Elam the province; and I saw in the vision, and I was by the watercourse of Ulai.

[3] And I lifted up my eyes and saw, and behold, a ram, one, standing before the watercourse, and it had two horns; and the two horns were high, and the one was higher than the second, and the higher one was coming up last.

[4] I saw the ram goring westward and northward and southward, and no living things could stand before him, and there was none who could deliver out of his hand; and he did according to his will and magnified himself.

[5] And I was considering, and behold, the he-goat of the goats was coming from the west over the face of all the earth, and not touching the earth; and the he-goat had a horn of note between his eyes.

[6] And he came up to the ram having the two horns, which I had seen standing before the watercourse, and he ran at him in the fury of his power.

[7] And I saw him reaching the side of the ram, and he was embittered against him and struck the ram and broke in pieces his two horns; and there was no power in the ram to stand before him, and he threw him to the earth and trampled him; and there was none who could deliver the ram out of his hand.

[8] And the he-goat of the goats magnified himself exceedingly; and when he was mighty, the great horn was broken, and there came up four of note in its place toward the four winds of the heavens.

[9] And out of the one of them came forth one horn, from littleness, and it grew exceedingly great toward the south and toward the east and toward the beauty.

[10] And it grew great as far as the host of the heavens, and it made some of the host and some of the stars fall to the earth, and trampled them.

[11] And as far as the prince of the host he magnified himself, and from him the continual was taken away, and the established place of his sanctuary was thrown down.

[12] And a host shall be given over the continual through transgression, and it shall throw truth to the earth, and it shall act and prosper.

[13] And I heard one holy one speaking, and one holy one said to the certain one who was speaking, "Until when is the vision, the continual and the desolating transgression, to give both holiness and host to trampling?"

[14] And he said to me, "Until two thousand three hundred evening-morning; and holiness shall be justified."

[15] And it came to pass, when I, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, had seen the vision, that I sought understanding; and behold, standing before me was one like the appearance of a mighty man.

[16] And I heard the voice of a man between the banks of Ulai, and he called and said, "⟦gabriel|Gavriel|Gabriel⟧, make this one understand the sight."

[17] And he came beside where I stood, and when he came I was terrified and fell on my face. And he said to me, "Understand, son of man, for the vision is for the time of the end."

[18] And as he was speaking with me, I fell into a deep sleep on my face to the earth; and he touched me and made me stand where I stood.

[19] And he said, "Behold, I am making known to you what shall be in the latter part of the indignation, for it is for the appointed time of the end.

[20] The ram that you saw having the two horns: the kings of ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧ and ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧.

[21] And the he-goat, the shaggy one, is the king of ⟦greece|Yavan|Greece⟧; and the great horn that is between his eyes, it is the first king.

[22] And the broken one, in whose place four stood up: four kingdoms shall stand up from a nation, but not with his power.

[23] "And in the latter part of their kingdom, when the transgressors have come to the full, there shall stand up a king strong of face and understanding riddles.

[24] And his power shall be mighty, but not by his own power; and wondrously he shall ruin, and he shall prosper and act, and he shall ruin mighty ones and a people of holy ones.

[25] And through his insight he shall make deceit prosper in his hand, and in his heart he shall magnify himself, and in ease he shall ruin many; and against the prince of princes he shall stand up, and without a hand he shall be broken.

[26] And the sight of the evening and the morning that was told is truth; and you, shut up the vision, for it is for many days."

[27] And I, ⟦daniel|Daniyel|Daniel⟧, was undone and was sick for days; and I rose and did the work of the king. And I was appalled at the sight, and there was none who understood.

## Changes

- **"In the third year of the reign of king Belshazzar" → "of the reign of Belshazzar the king"** (v1) — Hebrew בִּשְׁנַת שָׁלוֹשׁ, literally "in year three"; הַמֶּלֶךְ after the name. **"even unto me Daniel" → "to me, I, Daniel"** — Hebrew אֵלַי אֲנִי דָנִיֵּאל. **"appeared unto"** — נִרְאָה אֶל (v1 twice).
- **"I saw in a vision" → "I saw in the vision"** (v2) — Hebrew בֶּחָזוֹן, with the article. **"at Shushan in the palace" → "in Susa the citadel"** — Hebrew בְּשׁוּשַׁן הַבִּירָה, as Neh 1:1; Esth 1:2. **"in the province of Elam" → "in Elam the province"** — the Hebrew order. **"the river of Ulai" → "the watercourse of Ulai"** (v2, 3, 6) — Hebrew אוּבַל, only here: a stream or channel (from יָבַל "carry, flow"; uncertain; "gate" in some versions, from memory).
- **"there stood before the river a ram" → "a ram, one, standing before the watercourse"** (v3) — Hebrew אַיִל אֶחָד, "one ram". **"two horns … the two horns"** — dual קְרָנָיִם. **"the higher came up last" → "the higher one was coming up last"** — Hebrew עֹלָה, a participle.
- **"pushing" → "goring"** (v4) — Hebrew מְנַגֵּחַ (the נָגַח row). **"westward … southward"** — literally "seaward" (יָמָּה) and "to the Negev" (נֶגְבָּה). **"no beasts" → "no living things"** — Hebrew חַיּוֹת. **"so that … neither" → "and … and"** — the Hebrew joins with וְ. **"became great" → "magnified himself"** (v4, 8, 11, 25) — Hebrew הִגְדִּיל, the causative stem; the plain stem גָּדַל is "grow great" (v9, 10).
- **"as I was considering" → "I was considering"** (v5) — Hebrew הָיִיתִי מֵבִין, the verb "understand" (v15–17, 23, 27). **"an he goat" → "the he-goat of the goats"** (v5, 8) — Hebrew צְפִיר־הָעִזִּים, with the article (the 2 Chr 29:21 row); צָפִיר alone "he-goat" (v5–8, 21). **"on the face of the whole earth" → "over the face of all the earth"**. **"touched not the ground" → "not touching the earth"** — Hebrew אָרֶץ both times. **"a notable horn" → "a horn of note"** (v5, 8) — Hebrew קֶרֶן חָזוּת, literally "a horn of sight": one to be looked at; in v8 חָזוּת alone ("four of note": "horns" is not written).
- **"the ram that had two horns" → "the ram having the two horns"** (v6, 20) — Hebrew בַּעַל הַקְּרָנַיִם, literally "the owner of the two horns" (בַּעַל of one marked by a thing, as the reviewer's 2 Kgs 1:8 row). **"the fury of his power"** — חֵמָה · כֹּחַ.
- **"come close unto" → "reaching the side of"** (v7) — Hebrew מַגִּיעַ אֵצֶל. **"he was moved with choler" → "he was embittered"** — Hebrew וַיִּתְמַרְמַר, of מָרַר "be bitter". **"brake" → "broke in pieces"** — piel שִׁבֵּר; the nifal נִשְׁבַּר "was broken" (v8, 22, 25). **"cast him down to the ground" → "threw him to the earth"** — הִשְׁלִיךְ.
- **"Therefore the he goat waxed very great" → "And the he-goat of the goats magnified himself exceedingly"** (v8) — וּ "and". **"when he was strong" → "when he was mighty"** — Hebrew כְּעָצְמוֹ. **"for it" → "in its place"** (v8, 22) — Hebrew תַּחְתֶּיהָ. **"of heaven" → "of the heavens"** (v8, 10).
- **"a little horn" → "one horn, from littleness"** (v9) — Hebrew קֶרֶן־אַחַת מִצְּעִירָה, literally "one horn from littleness": it began small (the form is unusual; a correction to "another, a small one" is often proposed, from memory). **"waxed exceeding great" → "grew exceedingly great"** — Hebrew וַתִּגְדַּל־יֶתֶר. **"the south … the east"** — Hebrew הַנֶּגֶב · הַמִּזְרָח. **"the pleasant land" → "the beauty"** — Hebrew הַצֶּבִי, without "land" (as 11:16, 41 אֶרֶץ־הַצְּבִי "the land of the beauty"): the land of Israel is meant; צְבִי is also "gazelle".
- **"it cast down some of the host and of the stars" → "it made some of the host and some of the stars fall"** (v10) — Hebrew וַתַּפֵּל, hifil of נָפַל.
- **"Yea, he magnified himself even to the prince of the host" → "And as far as the prince of the host he magnified himself"** (v11) — the verb is masculine here, the horn feminine (v9, 10, 12): the one the horn stands for. שַׂר "prince" (the שַׂר row; of an army "commander"); no capital: whom the words mean is not said. **"by him the daily sacrifice was taken away" → "from him the continual was taken away"** (v11, 12, 13) — qere הוּרַם "was taken away" (ketiv הרים "he took away"); הַתָּמִיד "the continual", the word alone: the continual offering of the תָּמִיד row is meant; "sacrifice" is not written. **"the place of his sanctuary was cast down" → "the established place of his sanctuary was thrown down"** — Hebrew מְכוֹן (as Exod 15:17) · הֻשְׁלַךְ.
- **"an host was given him against the daily sacrifice … and it cast down the truth … practised, and prospered" → "a host shall be given over the continual … shall throw truth … shall act and prosper"** (v12) — Hebrew תִּנָּתֵן, an imperfect, with weqatal forms after it: the narrative past of v10 is left; the verse may also be read of the past ("was being given"). עַל "over, against, together with": the Hebrew does not decide. **"by reason of transgression" → "through transgression"** — Hebrew בְּפָשַׁע. **"the truth" → "truth"** — Hebrew אֱמֶת, without the article. The whole verse is difficult; it is translated word for word.
- **"one saint" → "one holy one"** (v13) — Hebrew קָדוֹשׁ. **"that certain saint which spake" → "the certain one who was speaking"** — Hebrew לַפַּלְמוֹנִי הַמְדַבֵּר, "so-and-so", one not named. **"How long shall be the vision concerning" → "Until when is the vision"** — Hebrew עַד־מָתַי הֶחָזוֹן; "concerning" is not written. **"the transgression of desolation" → "the desolating transgression"** — Hebrew וְהַפֶּשַׁע שֹׁמֵם, a participle of שָׁמֵם "be desolate / appalled" (the שָׁמֵם row; 9:27; 11:31; 12:11). **"to give both the sanctuary and the host to be trodden under foot" → "to give both holiness and host to trampling"** — Hebrew תֵּת וְקֹדֶשׁ וְצָבָא מִרְמָס: קֹדֶשׁ "holiness, holy thing", without the article (v13, 14); the sanctuary of v11 is מִקְדָּשׁ.
- **"he said unto me"** (v14) — Hebrew אֵלַי; the Greek and Syriac read "to him" (from memory); the MT is kept. **"Unto two thousand and three hundred days" → "Until two thousand three hundred evening-morning"** — Hebrew עַד עֶרֶב בֹּקֶר אַלְפַּיִם וּשְׁלֹשׁ מֵאוֹת, literally "until evening morning, two thousand and three hundred": the noun pair stands as printed, singular and without "and"; whether 2,300 days are meant or 2,300 evening and morning offerings (1,150 days) the Hebrew does not say (see v26). **"then shall the sanctuary be cleansed" → "and holiness shall be justified"** — Hebrew וְנִצְדַּק קֹדֶשׁ, the nifal of צָדַק "be righteous", only here: "be put right, be vindicated"; "cleansed" follows the Greek (from memory).
- **"when I, even I Daniel, had seen the vision, and sought for the meaning" → "when I, Daniel, had seen the vision, that I sought understanding"** (v15) — Hebrew בִינָה. **"as the appearance of a man" → "one like the appearance of a mighty man"** — Hebrew כְּמַרְאֵה־גָבֶר: גֶּבֶר "mighty man" beside אָדָם "man" in v16 (the standing decision); the word sounds in the name גַּבְרִיאֵל.
- **"a man's voice between the banks of Ulai" → "the voice of a man between the banks of Ulai"** (v16) — Hebrew קוֹל־אָדָם בֵּין אוּלָי, literally "between Ulai": "the banks" is supplied. **"which called, and said" → "and he called and said"**. **"make this man to understand the vision" → "make this one understand the sight"** — Hebrew לְהַלָּז "this one" ("man" not written); הַמַּרְאֶה "the sight, what is seen" (v16, 26, 27; in v15 "appearance"), beside חָזוֹן "vision".
- **"near where I stood" → "beside where I stood"** (v17) — Hebrew אֵצֶל עָמְדִי. **"I was afraid" → "I was terrified"** — Hebrew נִבְעַתִּי. **"Understand, O son of man" → "Understand, son of man"** — no "O" (the vocative row); בֶּן־אָדָם "son of man". **"for at the time of the end shall be the vision" → "for the vision is for the time of the end"** — Hebrew כִּי לְעֶת־קֵץ הֶחָזוֹן.
- **"I was in a deep sleep" → "I fell into a deep sleep"** (v18) — Hebrew נִרְדַּמְתִּי. **"set me upright" → "made me stand where I stood"** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמִידֵנִי עַל־עָמְדִי, literally "upon my standing".
- **"I will make thee know" → "I am making known to you"** (v19) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מוֹדִיעֲךָ, a participle. **"in the last end of the indignation" → "in the latter part of the indignation"** (v19, 23) — Hebrew בְּאַחֲרִית, as the בְּאַחֲרִית הַיָּמִים row; זַעַם "indignation". **"for at the time appointed the end shall be" → "for it is for the appointed time of the end"** — Hebrew כִּי לְמוֹעֵד קֵץ, without a verb; "it" is supplied; KJV's reading is possible.
- **"are the kings" → "the kings"** (v20) — a sentence without a verb.
- **"the rough goat" → "the he-goat, the shaggy one"** (v21) — Hebrew הַצָּפִיר הַשָּׂעִיר: שָׂעִיר "hairy", the word that is "male goat" elsewhere. **"Grecia" → "Greece"** — Hebrew יָוָן. **"is the first king" → "it is the first king"** — Hebrew הוּא.
- **"Now that being broken, whereas four stood up for it" → "And the broken one, in whose place four stood up"** (v22) — Hebrew וְהַנִּשְׁבֶּרֶת וַתַּעֲמֹדְנָה אַרְבַּע תַּחְתֶּיהָ. **"out of the nation" → "from a nation"** — Hebrew מִגּוֹי, without the article ("his nation" in the Greek, from memory). **"in his power" → "with his power"**.
- **"in the latter time" → "in the latter part"** (v23). **"when the transgressors are come to the full"** — Hebrew כְּהָתֵם הַפֹּשְׁעִים; the Greek reads "the transgressions" (from memory). **"of fierce countenance" → "strong of face"** — Hebrew עַז־פָּנִים. **"dark sentences" → "riddles"** — Hebrew חִידוֹת. **"shall stand up"** (v22, 23, 25) — Hebrew עָמַד.
- **"shall destroy wonderfully" → "wondrously he shall ruin"** (v24) — Hebrew נִפְלָאוֹת יַשְׁחִית: נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful things" (the row), here as an adverb or as the object; הִשְׁחִית "ruin" (v24 twice, 25). **"practise" → "act"** (v12, 24) — עָשָׂה. **"the mighty and the holy people" → "mighty ones and a people of holy ones"** — Hebrew עֲצוּמִים וְעַם־קְדֹשִׁים, without articles.
- **"through his policy also" → "through his insight"** (v25) — Hebrew שִׂכְלוֹ, of the הִשְׂכִּיל root. **"craft" → "deceit"** — Hebrew מִרְמָה. **"by peace" → "in ease"** — Hebrew בְּשַׁלְוָה (the שַׁלְוָה row): while all are at ease. **"the Prince of princes" → "the prince of princes"** — Hebrew שַׂר־שָׂרִים; no capital, as v11. **"without hand" → "without a hand"** — Hebrew בְּאֶפֶס יָד.
- **"the vision of the evening and the morning" → "the sight of the evening and the morning"** (v26) — Hebrew מַרְאֵה הָעֶרֶב וְהַבֹּקֶר, here with articles and "and" (v14 has neither). **"is true" → "is truth"** — Hebrew אֱמֶת הוּא. **"shut thou up the vision"** — Hebrew סְתֹם "stop up, shut up" (not חָתַם "seal", which stands beside it in 12:4). **"for it shall be for many days" → "for it is for many days"**.
- **"fainted" → "was undone"** (v27) — Hebrew נִהְיֵיתִי, the nifal of הָיָה "be": "I was finished" (uncertain). **"certain days" → "for days"** — Hebrew יָמִים. **"did the king's business" → "did the work of the king"** — Hebrew מְלֶאכֶת. **"I was astonished at the vision" → "I was appalled at the sight"** — Hebrew וָאֶשְׁתּוֹמֵם, of שָׁמֵם. **"but none understood it" → "and there was none who understood"** — Hebrew וְאֵין מֵבִין (or "and I was without understanding": the Hebrew does not name the person).

### Notes
- **Language** — Hebrew resumes at 8:1 and runs to the end of the book; 2:4b–7:28 is Aramaic.
- **Ram, he-goat, horns** — the chapter names Media, Persia and Greece (v20, 21); the first king of Greece is commonly taken as Alexander, the four horns as his successors, and the horn of v9–12, 23–25 as Antiochus IV (167–164 BC). The verses themselves name no one else.
- **Gabriel** is first named here.
