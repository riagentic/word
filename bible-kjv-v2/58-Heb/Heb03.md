# Hebrews 3

[1] Therefore, holy brothers, you who share in a heavenly calling, consider ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, the apostle and high priest of our confession,

[2] who was faithful to him who appointed him, just as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ also was faithful in all ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ house.

[3] For ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ has been counted worthy of more glory than ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ — as much more glory as the builder of a house has more honor than the house itself.

[4] (For every house is built by someone, but the builder of all things is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.)

[5] Now ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ was faithful in all ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ house as a servant, to testify to the things that were to be spoken later,

[6] but ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is faithful over ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ house as a son. And we are his house, if indeed we hold fast our confidence and our boasting in our hope.

[7] Therefore, as the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧ says,

"**Today, if you hear his voice,**

**[8] do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion,
on the day of testing in the wilderness,**

**[9] where your fathers put me to the test
and saw my works**

**[10] for forty years.
Therefore I was provoked with that generation,
and said, 'They always go astray in their heart;
they have not known my ways.'**

**[11] As I swore in my wrath,
'They shall not enter my rest.'**"

[12] Take care, brothers, lest there be in any of you an evil, unbelieving heart, leading you to fall away from the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[13] But exhort one another every day, as long as it is called "today," that none of you may be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.

[14] For we have come to share in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, if indeed we hold our original confidence firm to the end.

[15] As it is said,

"**Today, if you hear his voice,
do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion**."

[16] For who were those who heard and yet rebelled? Was it not all those who left ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ led by ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧?

[17] And with whom was he provoked for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the wilderness?

[18] And to whom did he swear that they would not enter his rest, but to those who were disobedient?

[19] So we see that they were unable to enter because of unbelief.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v1, 3) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ *Mashiach*, "anointed one"; Greek Χριστός *Christos* is a translation of the Hebrew; restored to the original.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v7) — systematic.
- **"the Holy Ghost saith" → "as the *Ruach HaQodesh* says"** (v7) — framing modernized.
- **"in the provocation, in the day of temptation" → "as in the rebellion, on the day of testing"** (v8) — OT quotation (Ps 95:8); Greek ἐν τῷ παραπικρασμῷ *en tō parapikrasmō* = "in the rebellion / embitterment"; "provocation" is archaic. Greek ἐν τῇ ἡμέρᾳ τοῦ πειρασμοῦ = "on the day of testing" ("temptation" in English now implies sexual/moral temptation, but the Hebrew *massah* = trial/test).
- **"Wherefore I was grieved with that generation" → "I was provoked with that generation"** (v10) — OT quotation (Ps 95:10); Greek προσώχθισα *prosōchthisa* = "I was provoked / angry"; "grieved" in KJV misses the anger component.
- **"They do alway err in their heart" → "They always go astray in their heart"** (v10) — modernized; "alway" → "always."
- **"lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief" → "lest there be in any of you an evil, unbelieving heart"** (v12) — restructured for natural English.
- **"through the deceitfulness of sin" → "by the deceitfulness of sin"** (v13) — minor modernization.
- **"partakers of Christ" → "to share in the *Mashiach*"** (v14) — Greek μέτοχοι τοῦ Χριστοῦ *metochoi tou Christou*; "partakers" → "to share in."

### Notes
- **v7-11** quotes Ps 95:7-11.
