# Hebrews 1

[1] Long ago, at many times and in many ways, *Elohim* spoke to our fathers by the prophets,

[2] but in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son, whom he appointed the heir of all things, through whom also he created the world.

[3] He is the radiance of the glory of *Elohim* and the exact imprint of his nature, and he upholds the universe by the word of his power. After making purification for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high,

[4] having become as much superior to angels as the name he has inherited is more excellent than theirs.

[5] For to which of the angels did *Elohim* ever say,

"**You are my Son,
today I have begotten you**"?

Or again,

"**I will be to him a father,
and he shall be to me a son**"?

[6] And again, when he brings the firstborn into the world, he says,

"**Let all *Elohim*'s angels worship him**."

[7] Of the angels he says,

"**He makes his angels winds,
and his ministers a flame of fire**."

[8] But of the Son he says,

"**Your throne, O *Elohim*, is forever and ever,
the scepter of uprightness is the scepter of your kingdom.
[9] You have loved righteousness and hated wickedness;
therefore *Elohim*, your *Elohim*, has anointed you
with the oil of gladness beyond your companions**."

[10] And,

"**You, Lord, laid the foundation of the earth in the beginning,
and the heavens are the work of your hands;
[11] they will perish, but you remain;
they will all wear out like a garment,
[12] like a robe you will roll them up,
like a garment they will be changed.
But you are the same,
and your years will have no end**."

[13] And to which of the angels has he ever said,

"**Sit at my right hand
until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet**"?

[14] Are they not all ministering spirits sent out to serve for the sake of those who are to inherit salvation?

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic substitution of the Hebrew divine name. See gen/gen01.md for full rationale.
- **"God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake" → "Long ago, at many times and in many ways, *Elohim* spoke"** (v1) — KJV archaic forms modernized; "sundry times and divers manners" → "many times and in many ways."
- **"the worlds" → "the world"** (v2) — KJV "worlds" renders Greek αἰῶνας *aiōnas* ("ages / eons"); "world" chosen for readability in context.
- **"the brightness of his glory" → "the radiance of the glory"** (v3) — Greek ἀπαύγασμα *apaugasma* = "outshining / effulgence," light radiating outward; "brightness" is adequate but "radiance" better captures the emanation sense.
- **"the express image of his person" → "the exact imprint of his nature"** (v3) — Greek χαρακτὴρ τῆς ὑποστάσεως *charaktēr tēs hypostaseōs*; *charaktēr* = engraved stamp/impression on a coin; *hypostasis* = underlying substance/essence, not merely "person" in the English personal sense.
- **"purged our sins" → "making purification for sins"** (v3) — Greek participle καθαρισμὸν ποιησάμενος; "purged" is archaic; participial phrase restored.
- **"hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name" → "the name he has inherited is more excellent"** (v4) — modernized verbal form.
- **"firstbegotten" → "firstborn"** (v6) — Greek πρωτότοκον *prōtotokon*; "firstborn" is the standard modern rendering; "firstbegotten" is KJV archaism.
- **"Let all the angels of God worship him" → "Let all *Elohim*'s angels worship him"** (v6) — OT quotation (Deut 32:43 LXX / Ps 97:7); "God" → "*Elohim*."
- **"Who maketh his angels spirits" → "He makes his angels winds"** (v7) — OT quotation (Ps 104:4); Greek/Hebrew πνεύματα *pneumata* / רוּחוֹת *ruchot* = "winds" or "spirits." In the original Psalm context God commands the weather; "winds" fits the cosmic imagery better.
- **"a sceptre of righteousness" → "the scepter of uprightness"** (v8) — OT quotation (Ps 45:6-7); Greek εὐθύτητος *euthytētos* = "straightness / uprightness," slightly more concrete than KJV "righteousness."
- **"hated iniquity" → "hated wickedness"** (v9) — OT quotation (Ps 45:7); "iniquity" modernized.
- **"above thy fellows" → "beyond your companions"** (v9) — modernized thee/thy forms throughout quotations.
- **"as a vesture shalt thou fold them up" → "like a robe you will roll them up"** (v12) — OT quotation (Ps 102:26); "vesture" is archaic; "roll them up" more literal than "fold."
- **"thy years shall not fail" → "your years will have no end"** (v12) — modernized.
- **"Sit on my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool" → "Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet"** (v13) — OT quotation (Ps 110:1); thee/thy modernized; "footstool for your feet" restores the literal image.

### Notes

- **v7** quotes Ps 104:4.
- **v8-9** quotes Ps 45:6-7.
- **v10-12** quotes Ps 102:25-27.
