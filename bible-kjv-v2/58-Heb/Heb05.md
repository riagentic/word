# Hebrews 5

[1] For every high priest chosen from among men is appointed to act on behalf of men in relation to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to offer gifts and sacrifices for sins.

[2] He can deal gently with the ignorant and wayward, since he himself is beset with weakness.

[3] Because of this he is obligated to offer sacrifice for his own sins just as he does for those of the people.

[4] And no one takes this honor for himself, but only when called by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, just as ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ was.

[5] So also ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ did not exalt himself to be made a high priest, but was appointed by him who said to him,

"**You are my Son,
today I have begotten you**";

[6] as he says also in another place,

"**You are a priest forever,
after the order of ⟦melchizedek|Malki-Tzedeq|Melchizedek⟧**."

[7] In the days of his flesh, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ offered up prayers and supplications, with loud cries and tears, to him who was able to save him from death, and he was heard because of his reverence.

[8] Although he was a son, he learned obedience through what he suffered.

[9] And being made perfect, he became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey him,

[10] being designated by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ a high priest after the order of ⟦melchizedek|Malki-Tzedeq|Melchizedek⟧.

[11] About this we have much to say, and it is hard to explain, since you have become dull of hearing.

[12] For though by this time you ought to be teachers, you need someone to teach you again the basic principles of the oracles of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. You need milk, not solid food,

[13] for everyone who lives on milk is unskilled in the word of righteousness, since he is a child.

[14] But solid food is for the mature, for those who have their powers of discernment trained by constant practice to distinguish good from evil.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v5) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v7) — systematic.
- **"Who can have compassion on the ignorant, and on them that are out of the way" → "He can deal gently with the ignorant and wayward"** (v2) — Greek μετριοπαθεῖν *metriopathein* = "to deal moderately / gently with" (a philosophical term for measured emotional response); "have compassion on them that are out of the way" is weak; "deal gently with the ignorant and wayward" is more precise.
- **"Thou art my Son, to day have I begotten thee" → "You are my Son, today I have begotten you"** (v5) — OT quotation (Ps 2:7); thee/thou modernized.
- **"Who in the days of his flesh … was heard in that he feared" → "he was heard because of his reverence"** (v7) — Greek εὐλάβεια *eulabeia* = "reverence / godly fear / piety"; KJV "in that he feared" is terse to the point of ambiguity; "reverence" more precise.
- **"author of eternal salvation" → "source of eternal salvation"** (v9) — Greek αἴτιος *aitios* = "cause / source / originator"; "author" in modern English implies writing, which is misleading.
- **"called of God an high priest" → "being designated by *Elohim* a high priest"** (v10) — Greek προσαγορευθείς *prosagoreuetheis* = "designated / acclaimed / addressed"; "called" is weaker.
- **"oracles of God" → "oracles of *Elohim*"** (v12) — "God" → "*Elohim*."

### Notes
- **v5** quotes Ps 2:7.
- **v6** quotes Ps 110:4. *Malki-Tzedek* = Melchizedek, "my king is righteousness" / "king of righteousness."
