# Hebrews 8

[1] Now the chief point upon the things being said: we have such a high priest, who sat down at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens,

[2] a minister of the holy places and of the true tent, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ pitched, not man.

[3] For every high priest is appointed to offer both gifts and sacrifices; whence it is necessary that this one also have something which he may offer.

[4] If then he were on earth, he would not even be a priest, there being those who offer the gifts according to ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧

[5] (who serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things, just as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ has been divinely instructed when about to complete the tent; for he says, "**See, you shall make all things according to the pattern that was shown to you on the mountain**").

[6] But now he has obtained a more excellent ministry, by as much as he is also mediator of a better covenant, which has been enacted upon better promises.

[7] For if that first one had been faultless, no place would have been sought for a second.

[8] For finding fault with them he says:

"**Behold, days are coming, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧,
and I will consummate upon the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧
and upon the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ a new covenant,
[9] not according to the covenant which I made for their fathers
in the day of my taking hold of their hand to lead them out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
because they did not abide in my covenant,
and I neglected them, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧.
[10] For this is the covenant which I will covenant with the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧
after those days, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧:
giving my laws into their mind,
and upon their hearts I will write them,
and I will be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to them,
and they shall be a people to me.
[11] And they shall by no means teach, each his fellow citizen
and each his brother, saying, 'Know ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧,'
because all shall know me,
from the small to the great of them.
[12] For I will be propitious toward their unrighteousnesses,
and their sins I will by no means remember any more**."

[13] In saying "new" he has made the first old; and that which is growing old and aging is near to vanishing.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10) — systematic.
- **"the Lord" → "*Yahweh*"** (v2, 8–11) — Greek κύριος; in v8–11 it stands for the divine name of Jer 31:31–34, in v2 the wording echoes Num 24:6 in the Septuagint.
- **"Now of the things which we have spoken this is the sum" → "Now the chief point upon the things being said"** (v1) — Greek κεφάλαιον δὲ ἐπὶ τοῖς λεγομένοις, without a verb; κεφάλαιον is "chief point" or "sum"; λεγομένοις is a present participle.
- **"who is set on the right hand" → "who sat down at the right hand"** (v1) — Greek ἐκάθισεν, aorist (1:3; 10:12; 12:2).
- **"A minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man" → "a minister of the holy places and of the true tent, which *Yahweh* pitched, not man"** (v2) — Greek τῶν ἁγίων λειτουργός, a genitive, not "in"; τὰ ἅγια is plural, "the holy places" (9:8, 12, 24, 25); it can also be "of the holy things". σκηνή is "tent" throughout Hebrews.
- **"ordained" → "appointed"; "wherefore it is of necessity that this man have somewhat also to offer" → "whence it is necessary that this one also have something which he may offer"** (v3) — Greek καθίσταται (5:1); τοῦτον, "this one": the Greek has no "priest" and no "man".
- **"For if he were on earth, he should not be a priest, seeing that there are priests that offer gifts according to the law" → "If then he were on earth, he would not even be a priest, there being those who offer the gifts according to *Torah*"** (v4) — the printed text has οὖν, "then", οὐδέ, "not even", and no "priests"; κατὰ νόμον is without the article.
- **"Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things" → "who serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things"** (v5) — Greek ὑποδείγματι καὶ σκιᾷ λατρεύουσιν. λατρεύω is "worship" elsewhere (9:9, 14); here its dative names the thing the service is given to, as in 13:10, so "serve". ὑπόδειγμα is something shown, an outline or figure of a thing ("example" in 4:11); "copy" also in 9:23. It has the root of "shown" (δειχθέντα) later in the verse.
- **"as Moses was admonished of God when he was about to make the tabernacle" → "just as *Moshe* has been divinely instructed when about to complete the tent"** (v5) — Greek κεχρημάτισται, perfect passive of χρηματίζω, "give a divine answer or instruction" (11:7; 12:25); the word "God" is not in the Greek. ἐπιτελεῖν is "complete" (9:6).
- **"See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount" → "for he says, 'See, you shall make all things according to the pattern that was shown to you on the mountain'"** (v5) — Greek Ὅρα γάρ, φησίν, ποιήσεις, a future; Ex 25:40 as the Greek prints it ("all things" is not in the Hebrew). τύπος is "pattern".
- **"But now hath he obtained a more excellent ministry" kept** (v6) — Greek διαφορωτέρας … λειτουργίας; the Greek does not name the subject and has no "than the old".
- **"by how much also he is the mediator of a better covenant, which was established upon better promises" → "by as much as he is also mediator of a better covenant, which has been enacted upon better promises"** (v6) — Greek ὅσῳ καὶ κρείττονός ἐστιν διαθήκης μεσίτης; νενομοθέτηται, perfect passive of the verb of 7:11, "has been given as law".
- **"if that first covenant had been faultless, then should no place have been sought for the second" → "if that first one had been faultless, no place would have been sought for a second"** (v7) — Greek ἡ πρώτη ἐκείνη, without the noun; δευτέρας … τόπος, "place for a second".
- **"For finding fault with them, he saith" kept** (v8) — Greek μεμφόμενος γὰρ αὐτοὺς λέγει (SBLGNT, WH, NA28); with αὐτοῖς (Treg, RP) it can also be "finding fault, he says to them"; the root of "faultless" (ἄμεμπτος, v7).
- **"I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah" → "I will consummate upon the house of *Yisrael* and upon the house of *Yehudah* a new covenant"** (v8) — Greek συντελέσω ἐπὶ τὸν οἶκον, "bring to completion upon" (the root of "consummation", 9:26). The Hebrew of Jer 31:31 has "cut a covenant with".
- **"Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took them by the hand" → "not according to the covenant which I made for their fathers in the day of my taking hold of their hand"** (v9) — Greek ἐποίησα τοῖς πατράσιν, a dative; ἐπιλαβομένου μου τῆς χειρὸς αὐτῶν.
- **"because they continued not in my covenant, and I regarded them not" → "because they did not abide in my covenant, and I neglected them"** (v9) — Greek ἐνέμειναν, a compound of μένω "abide"; ἠμέλησα, "neglected, paid no heed to" (2:3). The Hebrew of Jer 31:32 has "which covenant of mine they broke, though I was a husband to them"; the verse follows the Greek.
- **"the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel" → "the covenant which I will covenant with the house of *Yisrael*"** (v10) — Greek διαθήκη ἣν διαθήσομαι, noun and verb of one root; a different verb from "made" (v9). τῷ οἴκῳ is a dative.
- **"I will put my laws into their mind, and write them in their hearts" → "giving my laws into their mind, and upon their hearts I will write them"** (v10) — Greek διδούς, a participle; διάνοιαν, singular; ἐπὶ καρδίας can be "upon their hearts" or "upon their heart". The Hebrew has "my *Torah*", singular; the Greek has the plural νόμους.
- **"I will be to them a God, and they shall be to me a people" → "I will be *Elohim* to them, and they shall be a people to me"** (v10) — Greek ἔσομαι αὐτοῖς εἰς θεόν … μοι εἰς λαόν, literally "for a God … for a people".
- **"they shall not teach every man his neighbour" → "they shall by no means teach, each his fellow citizen"** (v11) — Greek οὐ μὴ διδάξωσιν, the strong negation; the printed text has πολίτην, "fellow citizen".
- **"for all shall know me, from the least to the greatest" → "because all shall know me, from the small to the great of them"** (v11) — Greek ἀπὸ μικροῦ ἕως μεγάλου αὐτῶν. "Know" translates two verbs: γνῶθι (γινώσκω) and εἰδήσουσιν (οἶδα).
- **"I will be merciful to their unrighteousness, and their sins and their iniquities will I remember no more" → "I will be propitious toward their unrighteousnesses, and their sins I will by no means remember any more"** (v12) — Greek ἵλεως, "propitious, gracious" (the root of ἱλάσκομαι, 2:17, and ἱλαστήριον, 9:5), a different word from "mercy" (ἔλεος); ἀδικίαις, plural of ἀδικία "unrighteousness"; οὐ μὴ μνησθῶ ἔτι. The printed text does not have "and their iniquities".
- **"In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old" → "In saying 'new' he has made the first old"** (v13) — Greek ἐν τῷ λέγειν Καινήν; the noun "covenant" is not repeated; πεπαλαίωκεν, perfect of παλαιόω (1:11).
- **"that which decayeth and waxeth old is ready to vanish away" → "that which is growing old and aging is near to vanishing"** (v13) — Greek τὸ παλαιούμενον καὶ γηράσκον ἐγγὺς ἀφανισμοῦ; ἐγγύς is "near" (6:8).

### Notes
- **v5** quotes Ex 25:40.
- **v8–12** quote Jer 31:31–34 (Jer 38:31–34 in the Septuagint).
- **v1** takes up Ps 110:1.
