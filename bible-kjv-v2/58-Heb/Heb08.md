# Hebrews 8

[1] Now the point in what we are saying is this: we have such a high priest, one who is seated at the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in heaven,

[2] a minister in the holy places, in the true tent that the Lord set up, not man.

[3] For every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices; thus it is necessary for this priest also to have something to offer.

[4] Now if he were on earth, he would not be a priest at all, since there are priests who offer gifts according to the Torah.

[5] They serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things. For when Moses was about to erect the tent, he was instructed by *Elohim*, saying, "**See that you make everything according to the pattern that was shown you on the mountain**."

[6] But as it is, the *Mashiach* has obtained a ministry that is as much more excellent than the old as the covenant he mediates is better, since it is enacted on better promises.

[7] For if that first covenant had been faultless, there would have been no occasion to look for a second.

[8] For he finds fault with them when he says:

"**Behold, the days are coming, declares the Lord,
when I will establish a new covenant with the house of Israel
and with the house of Judah,
[9] not like the covenant that I made with their fathers
on the day when I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt.
For they did not continue in my covenant,
and so I showed no concern for them, declares the Lord.
[10] For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel
after those days, declares the Lord:
I will put my laws into their minds,
and write them on their hearts,
and I will be their *Elohim*,
and they shall be my people.
[11] And they shall not teach, each one his neighbor
and each one his brother, saying, 'Know the Lord,'
for they shall all know me,
from the least of them to the greatest.
[12] For I will be merciful toward their iniquities,
and I will remember their sins no more**."

[13] In speaking of a new covenant, he makes the first one obsolete. And what is becoming obsolete and growing old is ready to vanish away.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v6) — systematic.
- **"the law" → "the Torah"** (v4) — Greek κατὰ νόμον *kata nomon*; in context of Mosaic priestly law, "Torah" more accurately conveys the scope.
- **"See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount" → "See that you make everything according to the pattern that was shown you on the mountain"** (v5) — OT quotation (Ex 25:40); thee/thou modernized.
- **"who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things" → "They serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things"** (v5) — Greek ὑποδείγματι καὶ σκιᾷ *hypodeigmati kai skiā* = "example/copy and shadow"; "copy" better than "example" for the spatial/architectural sense.
- **"by how much also he is the mediator of a better covenant" → "the covenant he mediates is better"** (v6) — restructured for clarity.
- **"I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah" → "I will establish a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah"** (v8) — OT quotation (Jer 31:31); Greek συντελέσω *syntelēsō* = "I will complete / bring to fulfillment / establish."
- **"Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt" → "not like the covenant that I made with their fathers on the day when I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt"** (v9) — OT quotation (Jer 31:32); modernized.
- **"because they continued not in my covenant, and I regarded them not, saith the Lord" → "For they did not continue in my covenant, and so I showed no concern for them, declares the Lord"** (v9) — Greek ἠμέλησα αὐτῶν *ēmelēsa autōn* = "I showed no concern for them / I neglected them"; KJV "regarded them not" is similar but the active neglect sense is clearer with "showed no concern."
- **"I will be to them a God, and they shall be to me a people" → "I will be their *Elohim*, and they shall be my people"** (v10) — OT quotation (Jer 31:33); "God" → "*Elohim*"; restructured possessively.
- **"they shall not teach every man his neighbour" → "they shall not teach, each one his neighbor"** (v11) — modernized.
- **"for I will be merciful to their unrighteousness" → "For I will be merciful toward their iniquities"** (v12) — OT quotation (Jer 31:34); "iniquities" restored over "unrighteousness" to match the OT original more closely.

### Notes
- **v5** quotes Ex 25:40.
- **v8-12** quotes Jer 31:31-34.
