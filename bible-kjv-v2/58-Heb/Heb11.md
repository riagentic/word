# Hebrews 11

[1] **Now faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen;**

[2] for in this the elders were testified to.

[3] By faith we understand that the ages have been fitted together by a word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, so that what is seen has not come to be out of things that appear.

[4] By faith ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧ offered to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ a greater sacrifice than ⟦cain|Qayin|Cain⟧, through which he was testified to as being righteous, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ testifying over his gifts, and through it, having died, he still speaks.

[5] By faith ⟦enoch|Chanoch|Enoch⟧ was removed so as not to see death, and he was not found, because ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ removed him; for before the removal he has been testified to as having been well-pleasing to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[6] and without faith it is impossible to be well-pleasing, for he who approaches ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ must believe that he is, and that he becomes a recompenser to those who seek him out.

[7] By faith ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, having been divinely instructed concerning the things not yet seen, in reverence prepared an ark for the salvation of his house, through which he condemned the world and became heir of the righteousness that is according to faith.

[8] By faith ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, being called, obeyed to go out to a place which he was about to receive for an inheritance, and he went out not knowing where he was going.

[9] By faith he dwelt as an alien in the land of the promise as in a foreign land, dwelling in tents with ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, the fellow heirs of the same promise;

[10] for he was waiting for the city that has the foundations, whose craftsman and maker is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[11] By faith also Sarah herself received power for the laying down of seed, even beyond the time of age, since she counted faithful him who had promised;

[12] therefore also from one, and that one having been made dead, there were begotten even as the stars of heaven in multitude and as the sand that is by the shore of the sea, the innumerable.

[13] According to faith these all died, not having received the promises, but having seen them from afar and greeted them, and having confessed that they are strangers and sojourners on the earth.

[14] For those who say such things make it plain that they seek after a fatherland.

[15] And if indeed they had been remembering that one from which they went out, they would have had time to turn back;

[16] but now they reach for a better one, that is, a heavenly one. Therefore ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is not ashamed of them, to be called their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for he prepared a city for them.

[17] By faith ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, being tested, has offered ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧, and he who had accepted the promises was offering his only one,

[18] to whom it was spoken, "**In ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ shall seed be called for you**,"

[19] considering that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is powerful to raise even from the dead; from where he also carried him off in a parable.

[20] By faith also concerning things to come ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ blessed ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ and ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧.

[21] By faith ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, dying, blessed each of the sons of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, and bowed down upon the top of his staff.

[22] By faith ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧, coming to his end, made mention of the exodus of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and gave commandment concerning his bones.

[23] By faith ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, having been born, was hidden three months by his parents, because they saw that the young child was beautiful, and they did not fear the edict of the king.

[24] By faith ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, having become great, refused to be called son of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh's|Pharaoh's⟧ daughter,

[25] choosing rather to be ill-treated together with the people of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ than to have a temporary enjoyment of sin,

[26] counting the reproach of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ greater riches than the treasures of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, for he was looking away to the recompense.

[27] By faith he left ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ behind, not fearing the rage of the king, for he persevered as seeing the invisible one.

[28] By faith he has kept the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ and the pouring on of the blood, so that the one bringing ruin on the firstborn might not touch them.

[29] By faith they passed through the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧ as through dry land, of which the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧, taking trial, were swallowed up.

[30] By faith the walls of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ fell, having been encircled for seven days.

[31] By faith ⟦rahab|Rachav|Rahab⟧ the prostitute did not perish together with those who disobeyed, having accepted the spies with peace.

[32] And what more shall I say? For the time will fail me recounting concerning ⟦gideon|Gidon|Gideon⟧, ⟦barak|Baraq|Barak⟧, ⟦samson|Shimshon|Samson⟧, ⟦jephthah|Yiftach|Jephthah⟧, both David and ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ and the prophets,

[33] who through faith subdued kingdoms, worked righteousness, attained promises, stopped the mouths of lions,

[34] quenched the power of fire, fled the mouths of the sword, were made powerful out of weakness, became strong in war, turned back the camps of foreigners;

[35] women received their dead by resurrection; but others were tortured, not accepting the redemption, that they might attain a better resurrection;

[36] and others took trial of mockings and scourgings, and further of chains and prison;

[37] they were stoned, they were sawn in two, they died by slaughter of the sword; they went about in sheepskins, in goatskins, being in lack, afflicted, ill-treated —

[38] of whom the world was not worthy — wandering over deserts and mountains and caves and the holes of the earth;

[39] and these all, having been testified to through the faith, did not carry off the promise,

[40] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ having foreseen something better concerning us, that apart from us they should not be perfected.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v26) — systematic; Greek τοῦ Χριστοῦ, with the article.
- **"the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen" → "the assurance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen"** (v1) — Greek ὑπόστασις, literally "what stands under": "substance, underlying reality" (so 1:3) or "assurance, firm confidence" (3:14); the one word is rendered "assurance" here. ἔλεγχος is "proof, evidence", from ἐλέγχω (12:5).
- **"For by it the elders obtained a good report" → "for in this the elders were testified to"** (v2) — Greek ἐν ταύτῃ … ἐμαρτυρήθησαν οἱ πρεσβύτεροι; μαρτυρέω is "testify" throughout (v4, 5, 39; 7:8, 17; 10:15).
- **"the worlds were framed by the word of God" → "the ages have been fitted together by a word of *Elohim*"** (v3) — Greek κατηρτίσθαι (10:5; 13:21) τοὺς αἰῶνας (1:2) ῥήματι θεοῦ: "ages", not "universe"; ῥῆμα, a spoken word (1:3), without the article.
- **"things which are seen were not made of things which do appear" → "what is seen has not come to be out of things that appear"** (v3) — printed text τὸ βλεπόμενον, singular; γεγονέναι, "come to be"; φαινομένων, a different word from "seen".
- **"a more excellent sacrifice" → "a greater sacrifice"** (v4) — Greek πλείονα, "more, greater".
- **"he obtained witness that he was righteous, God testifying of his gifts" → "he was testified to as being righteous, *Elohim* testifying over his gifts"** (v4) — Greek ἐμαρτυρήθη … μαρτυροῦντος ἐπὶ τοῖς δώροις: one verb twice. "Through which" and "through it" can refer to the faith or to the sacrifice.
- **"being dead yet speaketh" → "having died, he still speaks"** (v4) — Greek ἀποθανών; the Greek has no "though".
- **"was translated" → "was removed"; "translation" → "removal"** (v5) — Greek μετετέθη, μετέθηκεν, μεταθέσεως: one root, three times; μετάθεσις also in 12:27. In 7:12 the same words are "change" (of the priesthood and the law); here and in 12:27 the sense is a moving away to another place. The wording is that of Gen 5:24 LXX.
- **"he had this testimony, that he pleased God" → "he has been testified to as having been well-pleasing to *Elohim*"** (v5) — Greek μεμαρτύρηται, perfect; εὐαρεστηκέναι, as in v6; 12:28; 13:16, 21.
- **"to please him" → "to be well-pleasing"** (v6) — the Greek has no "him".
- **"he that cometh to God" → "he who approaches *Elohim*"** (v6) — Greek τὸν προσερχόμενον (10:1, 22); the Greek has no "would".
- **"that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him" → "that he becomes a recompenser to those who seek him out"** (v6) — Greek μισθαποδότης γίνεται, the noun of μισθαποδοσία (v26; 10:35), with γίνεται "becomes"; ἐκζητοῦσιν (12:17).
- **"being warned of God" → "having been divinely instructed"** (v7) — Greek χρηματισθείς, said of a divine answer or warning (8:5; 12:25); the Greek has no word "God".
- **"moved with fear" → "in reverence"** (v7) — Greek εὐλαβηθείς, a participle, "having become reverent, having taken heed"; the noun εὐλάβεια is "reverence" in 5:7; 12:28.
- **"to the saving of his house" → "for the salvation of his house"** (v7) — Greek εἰς σωτηρίαν τοῦ οἴκου; "house" as in 3:2–6; 10:21.
- **"by the which" → "through which"; "the righteousness which is by faith" → "the righteousness that is according to faith"** (v7) — Greek διʼ ἧς (the faith, the ark, or the salvation); κατὰ πίστιν, as in v13.
- **"which he should after receive" → "which he was about to receive"; "obeyed" kept** (v8) — Greek ἤμελλεν λαμβάνειν; ὑπήκουσεν ἐξελθεῖν, "obeyed to go out". The printed text has "a place" without the article.
- **"he sojourned in the land of promise, as in a strange country" → "he dwelt as an alien in the land of the promise as in a foreign land"** (v9) — Greek παρῴκησεν (the life of a πάροικος, a resident without citizen's rights) beside κατοικήσας, "dwelling"; τῆς ἐπαγγελίας has the article; ἀλλοτρίαν, "belonging to others", as "foreigners" in v34.
- **"he looked for a city which hath foundations, whose builder and maker" → "he was waiting for the city that has the foundations, whose craftsman and maker"** (v10) — Greek ἐξεδέχετο (10:13); τὴν τοὺς θεμελίους ἔχουσαν πόλιν, with articles; τεχνίτης καὶ δημιουργός.
- **"Through faith also Sara herself received strength to conceive seed, and was delivered of a child when she was past age, because she judged him faithful" → "By faith also Sarah herself received power for the laying down of seed, even beyond the time of age, since she counted faithful him who had promised"** (v11) — the verse follows αὐτὴ Σάρρα, nominative (WH Treg RP). Other readings: αὐτὴ Σάρρα στεῖρα, "Sarah herself, being barren" (NA28); αὐτῇ Σάρρᾳ, a dative (Holmes, WH margin; printed in the SBLGNT): "together with Sarah herself he received power", with Abraham as the subject, as in v8–10, 12. The letters are the same; the old manuscripts have no iota subscript. καταβολὴ σπέρματος, "the laying down of seed", is elsewhere the expression for the father's part; καταβολή is "foundation" in 4:3; 9:26. "Was delivered of a child" (ἔτεκεν, RP) is not in the critical editions.
- **"of one, and him as good as dead" → "from one, and that one having been made dead"** (v12) — Greek νενεκρωμένου, a perfect participle (Rom 4:19); the Greek has no "as good as". **"sprang there" → "there were begotten"** — Greek ἐγεννήθησαν; the Greek names no subject ("descendants" is not there).
- **"as the sand which is by the sea shore innumerable" kept as "as the sand that is by the shore of the sea, the innumerable"** (v12) — Greek χεῖλος, literally "lip".
- **"in faith" → "according to faith"** (v13) — Greek κατὰ πίστιν, not the dative of the other verses.
- **"were persuaded of them, and embraced them" → "greeted them"** (v13) — the critical text has only ἰδόντες καὶ ἀσπασάμενοι.
- **"strangers and pilgrims" → "strangers and sojourners"** (v13) — Greek ξένοι καὶ παρεπίδημοι: people staying for a time in a place not their own. "They are" is present tense in Greek. ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς is "on the earth" or "in the land".
- **"declare plainly that they seek a country" → "make it plain that they seek after a fatherland"** (v14) — Greek ἐμφανίζουσιν; πατρίδα; ἐπιζητοῦσιν (13:14).
- **"if they had been mindful of that country" → "if indeed they had been remembering that one"** (v15) — the verse follows ἐμνημόνευον, imperfect (WH NA28 RP); the SBLGNT prints μνημονεύουσιν, present, "if they remember" (Treg). "That one" is the fatherland. **"opportunity to have returned" → "time to turn back"** — Greek καιρόν, as in v11.
- **"they desire a better country" → "they reach for a better one"** (v16) — Greek ὀρέγονται, "stretch oneself toward"; no noun.
- **"God is not ashamed to be called their God" → "*Elohim* is not ashamed of them, to be called their *Elohim*"** (v16) — Greek οὐκ ἐπαισχύνεται αὐτούς.
- **"when he was tried, offered up Isaac" → "being tested, has offered *Yitzchaq*"** (v17) — Greek προσενήνοχεν, perfect, then προσέφερεν, imperfect, "was offering". πειραζόμενος is the verb elsewhere rendered "tempt" (2:18; 4:15; Jas 1:13); here it is the test of Gen 22:1, so "tested".
- **"his only begotten son" → "his only one"** (v17) — Greek τὸν μονογενῆ, without "son". **"he that had received the promises" → "he who had accepted the promises"** — Greek ἀναδεξάμενος, a compound of δέχομαι.
- **"Of whom it was said, That in Isaac shall thy seed be called" → "to whom it was spoken, 'In *Yitzchaq* shall seed be called for you'"** (v18) — Greek πρὸς ὃν ἐλαλήθη: "to whom" (Abraham) or "with regard to whom" (the only one). The quotation is Gen 21:12 LXX.
- **"Accounting that God was able to raise him up" → "considering that *Elohim* is powerful to raise even from the dead"** (v19) — Greek λογισάμενος; δυνατός; the Greek has no "him" after "raise".
- **"from whence also he received him in a figure" → "from where he also carried him off in a parable"** (v19) — Greek ἐκομίσατο (v39; 10:36); ἐν παραβολῇ, the word of 9:9.
- **"blessed Jacob and Esau concerning things to come" → "also concerning things to come … blessed"** (v20) — printed text καὶ περὶ μελλόντων; the Greek has no "invoked".
- **"when he was a dying" → "dying"; "worshipped, leaning upon the top of his staff" → "bowed down upon the top of his staff"** (v21) — Greek προσεκύνησεν ἐπὶ τὸ ἄκρον τῆς ῥάβδου αὐτοῦ, the wording of Gen 47:31 LXX (the Hebrew text has "the head of the bed"); the Greek has no "leaning". ῥάβδος is the "rod" of 1:8; 9:4.
- **"when he died, made mention of the departing of the children of Israel" → "coming to his end, made mention of the exodus of the sons of *Yisrael*"** (v22) — Greek τελευτῶν; ἐξόδου; τῶν υἱῶν Ἰσραήλ. ἐμνημόνευσεν is the verb "remember" of v15; 13:7. **"gave commandment" kept** — Greek ἐνετείλατο.
- **"when he was born" → "having been born"; "a proper child" → "the young child was beautiful"; "commandment" → "edict"** (v23) — Greek γεννηθείς; ἀστεῖον τὸ παιδίον (Exod 2:2 LXX); διάταγμα. "Parents" is literally "fathers" (πατέρων).
- **"when he was come to years" → "having become great"** (v24) — Greek μέγας γενόμενος (Exod 2:11 LXX). **"refused" kept** — Greek ἠρνήσατο, elsewhere "deny"; with an infinitive, "refuse".
- **"to suffer affliction with" → "to be ill-treated together with"; "to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season" → "to have a temporary enjoyment of sin"** (v25) — Greek συγκακουχεῖσθαι (as κακουχέω, v37; 13:3); πρόσκαιρον ἔχειν ἁμαρτίας ἀπόλαυσιν.
- **"Esteeming" → "counting"; "he had respect unto the recompence of the reward" → "he was looking away to the recompense"** (v26) — Greek ἡγησάμενος; ἀπέβλεπεν εἰς τὴν μισθαποδοσίαν.
- **"forsook" → "left … behind"; "wrath" → "rage"; "endured" → "persevered"; "him who is invisible" → "the invisible one"** (v27) — Greek κατέλιπεν; θυμόν (ὀργή is "wrath", 3:11); ἐκαρτέρησεν, a different verb from "endure" (ὑπομένω); τὸν ἀόρατον.
- **"Through faith he kept the passover, and the sprinkling of blood" → "By faith he has kept the *Pesach* and the pouring on of the blood"** (v28) — Greek πεποίηκεν, perfect, with two objects; πρόσχυσις, "pouring on", a different word from "sprinkling" (ῥαντισμός, 12:24).
- **"he that destroyed the firstborn" → "the one bringing ruin on the firstborn"** (v28) — Greek ὁ ὀλοθρεύων (Exod 12:23 LXX), the verb of ὄλεθρος "ruin"; τὰ πρωτότοκα is neuter, the firstborn of people and animals.
- **"they passed through the Red sea as by dry land" → "they passed through the Sea of Reeds as through dry land"** (v29) — the Greek names no subject ("the people" is not there); it has Ἐρυθρὰν Θάλασσαν, "Red Sea", the Septuagint's name for the Hebrew *Yam Suf*.
- **"which the Egyptians assaying to do were drowned" → "of which the *Mitzrim*, taking trial, were swallowed up"** (v29) — Greek ἧς πεῖραν λαβόντες; the same expression in v36; κατεπόθησαν.
- **"fell down, after they were compassed about" → "fell, having been encircled"** (v30) — Greek κυκλωθέντα.
- **"the harlot" → "the prostitute"; "them that believed not" → "those who disobeyed"; "when she had received the spies with peace" → "having accepted the spies with peace"** (v31) — Greek πόρνη; ἀπειθήσασιν (3:18); δεξαμένη … μετʼ εἰρήνης.
- **"the time would fail me to tell of" → "the time will fail me recounting concerning"** (v32) — Greek ἐπιλείψει, future; διηγούμενον.
- **"wrought righteousness, obtained promises" → "worked righteousness, attained promises"** (v33) — Greek εἰργάσαντο δικαιοσύνην; ἐπέτυχον (6:15), a different verb from "receive" (v13) and "carry off" (v39). **"subdued" kept** — Greek κατηγωνίσαντο, "struggled down", from ἀγών (12:1).
- **"the violence of fire" → "the power of fire"; "the edge of the sword" → "the mouths of the sword"** (v34) — Greek δύναμιν; στόματα μαχαίρης, the word of "mouths of lions" in v33.
- **"out of weakness were made strong, waxed valiant in fight, turned to flight the armies of the aliens" → "were made powerful out of weakness, became strong in war, turned back the camps of foreigners"** (v34) — Greek ἐδυναμώθησαν; ἰσχυροὶ ἐν πολέμῳ; παρεμβολάς (13:11, 13) ἔκλιναν ἀλλοτρίων.
- **"raised to life again" → "by resurrection"** (v35) — Greek ἐξ ἀναστάσεως, the noun that returns at the end of the verse; the Greek has no "to life".
- **"were tortured, not accepting deliverance; that they might obtain a better resurrection" → "were tortured, not accepting the redemption, that they might attain a better resurrection"** (v35) — Greek ἐτυμπανίσθησαν, "stretched on the τύμπανον and beaten"; ἀπολύτρωσιν, release for a ransom, the word of 9:15; τύχωσιν.
- **"had trial of cruel mockings and scourgings, yea, moreover of bonds and imprisonment" → "took trial of mockings and scourgings, and further of chains and prison"** (v36) — Greek πεῖραν ἔλαβον (v29); the Greek has no "cruel"; δεσμῶν; φυλακῆς.
- **"were tempted, were slain with the sword" → "they died by slaughter of the sword"** (v37) — the printed text has no "were tempted" (ἐπειράσθησαν); ἐν φόνῳ μαχαίρης ἀπέθανον.
- **"being destitute, afflicted, tormented" → "being in lack, afflicted, ill-treated"** (v37) — Greek ὑστερούμενοι, θλιβόμενοι, κακουχούμενοι.
- **"they wandered in deserts" → "wandering over deserts"** (v38) — Greek πλανώμενοι, a participle; πλανάω is here literal, "wander", elsewhere "deceive, lead astray". **"dens and caves of the earth" → "caves and the holes of the earth"** — Greek σπηλαίοις καὶ ταῖς ὀπαῖς.
- **"these all, having obtained a good report through faith, received not the promise" → "these all, having been testified to through the faith, did not carry off the promise"** (v39) — the verse follows καὶ οὗτοι πάντες (WH Treg NA28 RP); the SBLGNT prints καὶ πάντες, "and all" (Holmes). μαρτυρηθέντες as v2; διὰ τῆς πίστεως, with the article; ἐκομίσαντο.
- **"God having provided some better thing for us" → "*Elohim* having foreseen something better concerning us"** (v40) — Greek προβλεψαμένου, "foresee, see to beforehand"; περὶ ἡμῶν. **"be made perfect" → "be perfected"** — Greek τελειωθῶσιν.

### Notes
- **v18** quotes Gen 21:12.
- **v5–6**, **v9–12**, **v32–38** — each is one sentence in Greek; the stops between the verses follow the printed text.
- **v37–38** — "of whom the world was not worthy" stands in the Greek between "ill-treated" and "wandering".
