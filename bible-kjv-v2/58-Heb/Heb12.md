# Hebrews 12

[1] Therefore we also, having so great a cloud of witnesses lying around us, having laid aside every weight and the sin that so easily besets, let us run through endurance the struggle that lies before us,

[2] looking off to the author and perfecter of the faith, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, who for the joy that lay before him endured a cross, despising shame, and has sat down at the right hand of the throne of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] For consider him who has endured such contradiction by the sinners against himself, that you may not be worn out, fainting in your souls.

[4] Not yet unto blood have you resisted, struggling against sin,

[5] and you have forgotten the encouragement which reasons with you as with sons:

"**My son, do not make light of the training of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧,
nor faint when reproved by him;
[6] for whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ loves he trains,
and he scourges every son whom he accepts**."

[7] For training you endure; ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is dealing with you as with sons; for what son is there whom a father does not train?

[8] But if you are without training, of which all have become partakers, then you are illegitimate, and not sons.

[9] Furthermore, we had the fathers of our flesh as trainers and we respected them; shall we not much more be subject to the Father of the spirits and live?

[10] For they indeed trained for a few days as seemed good to them, but he for what is profitable, that we may share in his holiness.

[11] Now all training for the present does not seem to be of joy but of sorrow, yet afterward it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness to those who have been exercised through it.

[12] Therefore straighten up the slackened hands and the paralyzed knees,

[13] and make straight tracks for your feet, that what is lame may not be turned aside, but rather be healed.

[14] Pursue peace with all, and the sanctification without which no one will see the Lord,

[15] overseeing lest anyone be coming short of the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, lest any root of bitterness growing upward cause trouble and through it many be defiled,

[16] lest there be any sexually immoral or profane person, like ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧, who for one meal sold his own birthright.

[17] For you know that also afterward, wanting to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no place of repentance, although he sought it out with tears.

[18] For you have not approached something that is touched, and a fire that has been kindled, and blackness and gloom and tempest,

[19] and a sound of a trumpet and a voice of words, the hearers of which refused that any word be added to them;

[20] for they did not bear what was being enjoined: "**If even a beast touches the mountain, it shall be stoned**";

[21] and — so fearful was what appeared — ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said, "**I am terrified and trembling**."

[22] But you have approached Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ and the city of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, heavenly ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and myriads of angels, a festal gathering

[23] and church of the firstborn who are enrolled in the heavens, and a judge, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of all, and the spirits of righteous ones who have been perfected,

[24] and the mediator of a new covenant, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and the blood of sprinkling that speaks better than ⟦abel|Hevel|Abel⟧.

[25] See that you do not refuse him who speaks; for if those did not escape, having refused on earth him who divinely instructed, much more shall we not escape, who turn away from him who is from the heavens;

[26] whose voice shook the earth then, but now he has promised, saying, "**Yet once I will cause not only the earth to quake but also the heaven**."

[27] Now the "Yet once" makes clear the removal of the things that are shaken, as of things that have been made, that the things that are not shaken may remain.

[28] Therefore, receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us have grace, through which let us worship ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in a well-pleasing way, with reverence and awe,

[29] for indeed **our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is a consuming fire**.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v2, 24) — systematic.
- **"seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses" → "we also, having so great a cloud of witnesses lying around us"** (v1) — Greek ἔχοντες περικείμενον ἡμῖν; "we also" belongs to "let us run".
- **"the sin which doth so easily beset us" → "the sin that so easily besets"** (v1) — Greek τὴν εὐπερίστατον ἁμαρτίαν; the adjective occurs only here: "easily standing around, easily encircling"; the Greek has no "us".
- **"let us run with patience the race that is set before us" → "let us run through endurance the struggle that lies before us"** (v1) — Greek διʼ ὑπομονῆς; ἀγῶνα, a contest (here a footrace), the word rendered "struggle" elsewhere (Phil 1:30; Col 2:1), with ἀνταγωνιζόμενοι in v4; προκείμενον, as in v2.
- **"Looking unto Jesus the author and finisher of our faith" → "looking off to the author and perfecter of the faith, *Yeshua*"** (v2) — Greek ἀφορῶντες, "looking away (from other things) to"; ἀρχηγόν, "leader, originator" (2:10); τελειωτήν, from τελειόω "perfect"; τῆς πίστεως has no "our"; the name stands last.
- **"for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame" → "for the joy that lay before him endured a cross, despising shame"** (v2) — Greek ἀντὶ τῆς προκειμένης αὐτῷ χαρᾶς: ἀντί is "in exchange for, for the sake of" or "instead of" (so in v16). σταυρόν and αἰσχύνης have no article.
- **"is set down" → "has sat down"** (v2) — Greek κεκάθικεν, perfect.
- **"him that endured such contradiction of sinners against himself" → "him who has endured such contradiction by the sinners against himself"** (v3) — the verse follows εἰς ἑαυτόν (NA28 Treg; RP αὐτόν); the SBLGNT prints εἰς ἑαυτούς, plural, "against themselves" (WH). ὑπομεμενηκότα is perfect; τῶν ἁμαρτωλῶν has the article. ἀντιλογία as 6:16; 7:7; Jude 11.
- **"lest ye be wearied and faint in your minds" → "that you may not be worn out, fainting in your souls"** (v3) — Greek κάμητε … ἐκλυόμενοι (the verb of v5); ταῖς ψυχαῖς ὑμῶν, "in your souls", can go with either verb.
- **"Ye have not yet resisted unto blood, striving against sin" kept as "Not yet unto blood have you resisted, struggling against sin"** (v4) — Greek μέχρις αἵματος; the Greek has no "shedding" and no "your".
- **"the exhortation which speaketh unto you as unto children" → "the encouragement which reasons with you as with sons"** (v5) — Greek παρακλήσεως (13:22); διαλέγεται; υἱοῖς. The clause can be a statement or a question.
- **"despise not thou the chastening of the Lord" → "do not make light of the training of *Yahweh*"** (v5) — Greek μὴ ὀλιγώρει; παιδεία and παιδεύω are the bringing up of a child, with correction: "training", "train" throughout v5–11 (as Titus 2:12). The quotation is Prov 3:11–12 LXX; the Hebrew has the Name.
- **"nor faint when thou art rebuked of him" → "nor faint when reproved by him"** (v5) — Greek ἐκλύου; ἐλεγχόμενος.
- **"chasteneth" → "trains"; "scourgeth" → "scourges"; "receiveth" → "accepts"** (v6) — Greek παιδεύει; μαστιγοῖ (as μαστίγων, 11:36); παραδέχεται.
- **"If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons" → "For training you endure; *Elohim* is dealing with you as with sons"** (v7) — printed text εἰς παιδείαν ὑπομένετε, "unto training"; ὑπομένετε is a statement or a command ("endure!"). προσφέρεται, "bears himself toward", is the verb elsewhere rendered "offer".
- **"whom the father chasteneth not" → "whom a father does not train"** (v7) — Greek πατήρ, without the article and without "his".
- **"whereof all are partakers" → "of which all have become partakers"; "bastards" → "illegitimate"** (v8) — Greek μέτοχοι γεγόνασι; νόθοι, born outside lawful marriage; the Greek has no "children".
- **"we have had fathers of our flesh which corrected us, and we gave them reverence" → "we had the fathers of our flesh as trainers and we respected them"** (v9) — Greek τοὺς τῆς σαρκὸς ἡμῶν πατέρας … παιδευτάς, a noun; ἐνετρεπόμεθα.
- **"be in subjection unto the Father of spirits" → "be subject to the Father of the spirits"** (v9) — Greek ὑποταγησόμεθα τῷ πατρὶ τῶν πνευμάτων, with the article.
- **"after their own pleasure" → "as seemed good to them"; "for our profit" → "for what is profitable"; "that we might be partakers of his holiness" → "that we may share in his holiness"** (v10) — Greek κατὰ τὸ δοκοῦν αὐτοῖς; ἐπὶ τὸ συμφέρον, without "our"; μεταλαβεῖν, a different verb from μετέχω "partake"; ἁγιότητος. The Greek has no object "us".
- **"no chastening for the present seemeth to be joyous, but grievous" → "all training for the present does not seem to be of joy but of sorrow"** (v11) — Greek χαρᾶς … λύπης, two nouns.
- **"exercised thereby" → "exercised through it"** (v11) — Greek γεγυμνασμένοις (5:14).
- **"lift up the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees" → "straighten up the slackened hands and the paralyzed knees"** (v12) — Greek ἀνορθώσατε, one verb for both; παρειμένας; παραλελυμένα; the Greek has no "your" (Isa 35:3).
- **"paths" → "tracks"; "be turned out of the way" → "be turned aside"** (v13) — Greek τροχιάς, wheel tracks (Prov 4:26 LXX); ἐκτραπῇ, "turn aside", in medical use "be dislocated".
- **"Follow peace with all men, and holiness" → "Pursue peace with all, and the sanctification"** (v14) — Greek διώκετε; τὸν ἁγιασμόν, a different word from "holiness" in v10.
- **"Looking diligently" → "overseeing"; "lest any man fail of the grace of God" → "lest anyone be coming short of the grace of *Elohim*"** (v15) — Greek ἐπισκοποῦντες, the verb of ἐπίσκοπος "overseer"; ὑστερῶν ἀπό (4:1).
- **"springing up trouble you" → "growing upward cause trouble"** (v15) — Greek ἄνω φύουσα ἐνοχλῇ (Deut 29:18 LXX); the Greek has no "you".
- **"fornicator" → "sexually immoral … person"; "for one morsel of meat sold his birthright" → "for one meal sold his own birthright"** (v16) — Greek πόρνος (as πορνεία); βέβηλος, "profane", kept; ἀντὶ βρώσεως μιᾶς; τὰ πρωτοτόκια ἑαυτοῦ.
- **"when he would have inherited the blessing, he was rejected" → "wanting to inherit the blessing, he was rejected"** (v17) — Greek θέλων; ἀπεδοκιμάσθη, "rejected after testing" (as ἀδόκιμος "unapproved").
- **"no place of repentance" kept; "though he sought it carefully" → "although he sought it out"** (v17) — Greek μετανοίας τόπον; ἐκζητήσας αὐτήν: "it" can be the repentance or the blessing (both are feminine in Greek).
- **"ye are not come unto the mount that might be touched, and that burned with fire" → "you have not approached something that is touched, and a fire that has been kindled"** (v18) — the printed text has no "mountain"; ψηλαφωμένῳ καὶ κεκαυμένῳ πυρί can also be read as one phrase, "a fire that is touched and has been kindled". προσεληλύθατε is "approach" as 10:22.
- **"blackness, and darkness, and tempest" → "blackness and gloom and tempest"** (v18) — Greek γνόφῳ καὶ ζόφῳ καὶ θυέλλῃ; ζόφος is "gloom" (2 Pet 2:4; Jude 6).
- **"the voice of words; which voice they that heard intreated that the word should not be spoken to them any more" → "a voice of words, the hearers of which refused that any word be added to them"** (v19) — Greek παρῃτήσαντο, the verb of v25 ("refuse"); with μή and an infinitive it is "begged that … not". προστεθῆναι, "be added" (Deut 5:22, 25 LXX). "Words" is ῥημάτων, "word" is λόγον.
- **"they could not endure that which was commanded" → "they did not bear what was being enjoined"** (v20) — Greek οὐκ ἔφερον; τὸ διαστελλόμενον.
- **"it shall be stoned, or thrust through with a dart" → "it shall be stoned"** (v20) — the critical text has only λιθοβοληθήσεται (Exod 19:12–13).
- **"so terrible was the sight" → "so fearful was what appeared"** (v21) — Greek φοβερόν (10:27, 31); τὸ φανταζόμενον.
- **"I exceedingly fear and quake" → "I am terrified and trembling"** (v21) — Greek Ἔκφοβός εἰμι καὶ ἔντρομος; the first word is from Deut 9:19 LXX.
- **"ye are come unto mount Sion" → "you have approached Mount *Tziyon*"** (v22) — Greek προσεληλύθατε, perfect, as v18.
- **"an innumerable company of angels, to the general assembly and church of the firstborn" → "myriads of angels, a festal gathering and church of the firstborn"** (v22–23) — Greek μυριάσιν ἀγγέλων, πανηγύρει καὶ ἐκκλησίᾳ πρωτοτόκων. The printed text puts the comma after "angels", so that "festal gathering" goes with "church"; it can also be read "myriads of angels in festal gathering, and the church …". ἐκκλησία is "church" as elsewhere (2:12), literally "assembly".
- **"which are written in heaven" → "who are enrolled in the heavens"** (v23) — Greek ἀπογεγραμμένων ἐν οὐρανοῖς.
- **"to God the Judge of all" → "a judge, *Elohim* of all"** (v23) — Greek κριτῇ θεῷ πάντων, in this order: "a judge who is God of all" or "God, the judge of all".
- **"the spirits of just men made perfect" → "the spirits of righteous ones who have been perfected"** (v23) — Greek δικαίων τετελειωμένων.
- **"to Jesus the mediator of the new covenant" → "the mediator of a new covenant, *Yeshua*"** (v24) — Greek διαθήκης νέας μεσίτῃ Ἰησοῦ; νέα, "new, young", a different word from καινή (8:8; 9:15).
- **"that speaketh better things than that of Abel" → "that speaks better than *Hevel*"** (v24) — Greek κρεῖττον λαλοῦντι παρὰ τὸν Ἅβελ: "than Abel" (11:4), not "than the blood of Abel".
- **"if they escaped not who refused him that spake on earth" → "if those did not escape, having refused on earth him who divinely instructed"** (v25) — printed text ἐπὶ γῆς παραιτησάμενοι τὸν χρηματίζοντα: "on earth" goes with "refused"; χρηματίζω as 8:5; 11:7.
- **"much more shall not we escape, if we turn away from him that speaketh from heaven" → "much more shall we not escape, who turn away from him who is from the heavens"** (v25) — Greek οἱ τὸν ἀπʼ οὐρανῶν ἀποστρεφόμενοι: no "if", no second "speaks"; "shall not escape" is supplied from the first half.
- **"Yet once more I shake not the earth only, but also heaven" → "Yet once I will cause not only the earth to quake but also the heaven"** (v26) — Greek Ἔτι ἅπαξ (ἅπαξ is "once", 9:26–28); σείσω, future, a different verb from "shook" (ἐσάλευσεν). The quotation is Hag 2:6 LXX.
- **"signifieth the removing of those things that are shaken" → "makes clear the removal of the things that are shaken"** (v27) — Greek δηλοῖ (9:8); τὴν … μετάθεσιν, with the article (WH Treg NA28; the SBLGNT prints no article), the word of 11:5; σαλευομένων, a present participle ("being shaken"); the Greek has no "cannot".
- **"remain" kept** (v27) — Greek μείνῃ, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of things: "remain".
- **"we receiving a kingdom which cannot be moved, let us have grace, whereby we may serve God acceptably" → "receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us have grace, through which let us worship *Elohim* in a well-pleasing way"** (v28) — Greek ἀσάλευτον, from σαλεύω; ἔχωμεν χάριν is "let us have grace" or "let us have gratitude, be thankful"; λατρεύωμεν, "worship, serve (God)" (9:14); εὐαρέστως (11:5–6; 13:16, 21).
- **"with reverence and godly fear" → "with reverence and awe"** (v28) — printed text μετὰ εὐλαβείας καὶ δέους.
- **"For our God is a consuming fire" → "for indeed our *Elohim* is a consuming fire"** (v29) — Greek καὶ γάρ; Deut 4:24.

### Notes
- **v5-6** quotes Prov 3:11-12 (LXX).
- **v20** quotes Exod 19:12-13.
- **v26** quotes Hag 2:6 (LXX).
- **v29** echoes Deut 4:24.
- **v1–2**, **v14–16**, **v18–24** — each is one sentence in Greek. In v22–24 the Greek has no "to" before the single items (plain datives after "approached").
