# Hebrews 12

[1] Therefore, since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses, let us also lay aside every weight, and sin which clings so closely, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us,

[2] looking to *Yeshua*, the founder and perfecter of our faith, who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame, and is seated at the right hand of the throne of *Elohim*.

[3] Consider him who endured from sinners such hostility against himself, so that you may not grow weary or fainthearted.

[4] In your struggle against sin you have not yet resisted to the point of shedding your blood.

[5] And have you forgotten the exhortation that addresses you as sons?

"**My son, do not regard lightly the discipline of the Lord,
nor be weary when reproved by him.
[6] For the Lord disciplines the one he loves,
and chastises every son whom he receives**."

[7] It is for discipline that you have to endure. *Elohim* is treating you as sons. For what son is there whom his father does not discipline?

[8] If you are left without discipline, in which all have participated, then you are illegitimate children and not sons.

[9] Besides this, we have had earthly fathers who disciplined us and we respected them. Shall we not much more be subject to the Father of spirits and live?

[10] For they disciplined us for a short time as it seemed best to them, but he disciplines us for our good, that we may share his holiness.

[11] For the moment all discipline seems painful rather than pleasant, but later it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness to those who have been trained by it.

[12] Therefore lift your drooping hands and strengthen your weak knees,

[13] and make straight paths for your feet, so that what is lame may not be put out of joint but rather be healed.

[14] Strive for peace with everyone, and for the holiness without which no one will see the Lord.

[15] See to it that no one fails to obtain the grace of *Elohim*; that no "root of bitterness" springs up and causes trouble, and by it many become defiled;

[16] that no one is sexually immoral or unholy like Esau, who sold his birthright for a single meal.

[17] For you know that afterward, when he desired to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no chance to repent, though he sought it with tears.

[18] For you have not come to what may be touched, a blazing fire and darkness and gloom and a tempest

[19] and the sound of a trumpet and a voice whose words made the hearers beg that no further messages be spoken to them.

[20] For they could not endure the order that was given, "**If even a beast touches the mountain, it shall be stoned**."

[21] Indeed, so terrifying was the sight that Moses said, "**I tremble with fear**."

[22] But you have come to Mount Zion and to the city of the living *Elohim*, the heavenly Jerusalem, and to innumerable angels in festal gathering,

[23] and to the assembly of the firstborn who are enrolled in heaven, and to *Elohim*, the judge of all, and to the spirits of the righteous made perfect,

[24] and to *Yeshua*, the mediator of a new covenant, and to the sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than the blood of Abel.

[25] See that you do not refuse him who is speaking. For if they did not escape when they refused him who warned them on earth, much less will we escape if we reject him who warns from heaven.

[26] At that time his voice shook the earth, but now he has promised, "**Yet once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heavens**."

[27] This phrase, "Yet once more," indicates the removal of things that are shaken — that is, things that have been made — in order that the things that cannot be shaken may remain.

[28] Therefore let us be grateful for receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, and thus let us offer to *Elohim* acceptable worship, with reverence and awe,

[29] for **our *Elohim* is a consuming fire**.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v2, 24) — systematic.
- **"sin which doth so easily beset us" → "sin which clings so closely"** (v1) — Greek εὐπερίστατον *euperistaton* = "easily besetting / surrounding / clinging closely"; "easily beset" is archaic; "clings so closely" is more vivid.
- **"the author and finisher of our faith" → "the founder and perfecter of our faith"** (v2) — Greek ἀρχηγὸν καὶ τελειωτὴν *archēgon kai teleiōtēn*; *archēgos* = "founder / pioneer / trailblazer"; *teleiōtēs* = "one who completes/perfects"; "author … finisher" in KJV is possible but "author" in modern English implies writing.
- **"My son, despise not thou the chastening of the Lord, nor faint when thou art rebuked of him" → "My son, do not regard lightly the discipline of the Lord, nor be weary when reproved by him"** (v5) — OT quotation (Prov 3:11-12); thee/thou modernized; "despise not" → "do not regard lightly" (Greek μὴ ὀλιγώρει *mē oligōrei* = "do not make light of").
- **"For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth" → "For the Lord disciplines the one he loves"** (v6) — OT quotation (Prov 3:12); modernized.
- **"bastards, and not sons" → "illegitimate children and not sons"** (v8) — Greek νόθοι *nothoi* = "illegitimate / born outside lawful marriage"; "bastards" is crude in modern English.
- **"the father of spirits" → "the Father of spirits"** (v9) — capitalized appropriately.
- **"Lift up the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees" → "lift your drooping hands and strengthen your weak knees"** (v12) — echoes Isa 35:3; "drooping" and "weak" are more vivid than "hang down" and "feeble."
- **"And make straight paths for your feet" → "and make straight paths for your feet"** (v13) — echoes Prov 4:26; unchanged; appropriate.
- **"lest that which is lame be turned out of the way; but let it rather be healed" → "so that what is lame may not be put out of joint but rather be healed"** (v13) — Greek ἵνα μὴ τὸ χωλὸν ἐκτραπῇ *hina mē to chōlon ektrapē* = "that the lame not be dislocated/turned out of the way"; "put out of joint" is a valid medical rendering.
- **"And if so much as a beast touch the mountain, it shall be stoned, or thrust through with a dart" → "If even a beast touches the mountain, it shall be stoned"** (v20) — OT quotation (Ex 19:12-13); "or thrust through with a dart" follows some MSS; the translation streamlines.
- **"I exceedingly fear and quake" → "I tremble with fear"** (v21) — Greek Ἔκφοβός εἰμι καὶ ἔντρομος *Ekphobos eimi kai entromos* = "I am greatly frightened and trembling"; simplified for natural English.
- **"the general assembly and church of the firstborn" → "the assembly of the firstborn"** (v23) — Greek πανηγύρει καὶ ἐκκλησίᾳ πρωτοτόκων *panēgyrei kai ekklēsia prōtotokōn*; πανήγυρις *panēgyris* = "festal gathering" (preserved in the preceding phrase "innumerable angels in festal gathering"); ἐκκλησία = "assembly/congregation"; not rendered "church" here because the referent is the heavenly assembly of the firstborn, not a local congregation.
- **"the Mediator of the new covenant" → "*Yeshua*, the mediator of a new covenant"** (v24) — *Yeshua* named explicitly; "new covenant" = the Jeremiah 31 covenant.
- **"And to the blood of sprinkling, that speaketh better things than that of Abel" → "the sprinkled blood that speaks a better word than the blood of Abel"** (v24) — restructured for clarity; the sprinkled blood of *Yeshua* speaks forgiveness rather than the blood of Abel which cried for vengeance (Gen 4:10).
- **"Yet once more I shake not the earth only, but also heaven" → "Yet once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heavens"** (v26) — OT quotation (Hag 2:6); modernized.
- **"for our God is a consuming fire" → "our *Elohim* is a consuming fire"** (v29) — echoes Deut 4:24; "God" → "*Elohim*."

### Notes
- **v5-6** quotes Prov 3:11-12.
- **v20** quotes Ex 19:12-13.
- **v26** quotes Hag 2:6.
- **v29** echoes Deut 4:24.
