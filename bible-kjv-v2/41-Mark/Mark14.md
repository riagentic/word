# Mark 14

[1] Now after two days it was the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ and the ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧. And the high priests and the scribes were seeking how, taking hold of him by guile, they might kill him,

[2] for they were saying, "Not in the feast, lest there be a commotion of the people."

[3] And while he was in ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧, in the house of ⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧ the leper, as he was reclining, there came a woman having an alabaster flask of ointment of genuine nard, very costly; breaking the alabaster flask, she poured it down over his head.

[4] Now there were some being indignant among themselves: "For what has this waste of the ointment come to be?

[5] For this ointment could have been sold for more than three hundred denarii and given to the poor." And they were speaking sternly to her.

[6] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Let her be; why do you give her troubles? She has worked a good work in me;

[7] for the poor you always have with yourselves, and whenever you want you are able to do good to them; but me you do not always have.

[8] What she had, she did; she anointed my body beforehand for the burial.

[9] And amen, I say to you, wherever the gospel is proclaimed into the whole world, also what this woman did will be spoken of for a memorial of her."

[10] And ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧ Iscariot, the one of the twelve, went away to the high priests that he might deliver him up to them.

[11] And they, hearing, rejoiced and promised to give him silver. And he was seeking how he might deliver him up at an opportune time.

[12] And on the first day of the ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧, when they were sacrificing the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧, his disciples say to him, "Where do you want us to go away and make ready, that you may eat the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧?"

[13] And he sends two of his disciples and says to them, "Go into the city, and a man carrying a jar of water will meet you; follow him,

[14] and wherever he enters, say to the master of the house, 'The Teacher says, "Where is my guest room, where I may eat the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ with my disciples?"'

[15] And he himself will show you a large upper room, spread and ready; and there make ready for us."

[16] And the disciples went out and came into the city and found even as he had said to them, and they made ready the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[17] And when evening had come, he comes with the twelve.

[18] And as they were reclining and eating, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Amen, I say to you that one of you will deliver me up, the one eating with me."

[19] They began to be grieved and to say to him one by one, "Surely not I?"

[20] But he said to them, "One of the twelve, the one dipping with me into the bowl;

[21] because the Son of Man indeed goes, even as it is written about him, but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is delivered up! It would be good for him if that man had not been born."

[22] And as they were eating, taking bread, having blessed, he broke it and gave to them and said, "Take; this is my body."

[23] And taking a cup, having given thanks, he gave to them, and they all drank from it.

[24] And he said to them, "This is my blood of the covenant, which is being poured out for many.

[25] Amen, I say to you that I shall by no means drink any more from the produce of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[26] And having sung a hymn, they went out to ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧.

[27] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "You will all be made to stumble, because it is written, '**I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered**.'

[28] But after I have been raised, I will go before you into ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧."

[29] But ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ said to him, "Even if all will be made to stumble, yet not I."

[30] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to him, "Amen, I say to you that you, today, this night, before a rooster crows twice, will deny me three times."

[31] But he was speaking beyond measure, "If I must die together with you, I will by no means deny you." And likewise all also were saying.

[32] And they come to a plot of land whose name is ⟦gethsemane|Gethsemani|Gethsemane⟧, and he says to his disciples, "Sit here while I pray."

[33] And he takes along ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ with him, and he began to be utterly astounded and distressed,

[34] and he says to them, "My soul is deeply grieved, unto death; remain here and be awake."

[35] And going forward a little, he was falling upon the ground and was praying that, if it is possible, the hour might pass away from him,

[36] and he was saying, "***Abba***, Father, all things are possible to you; take this cup away from me; but not what I want, but what you."

[37] And he comes and finds them sleeping, and he says to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "⟦simon|Shimon|Simon⟧, are you sleeping? Did you not have strength to be awake one hour?

[38] Be awake and pray, that you may not come into trial; the spirit indeed is ready, but the flesh is weak."

[39] And again going away he prayed, saying the same word.

[40] And again coming he found them sleeping, for their eyes were being weighed down, and they did not know what they should answer him.

[41] And he comes the third time and says to them, "Sleep on now and rest. It is enough; the hour has come; behold, the Son of Man is delivered up into the hands of the sinners.

[42] Arise, let us go; behold, the one delivering me up has drawn near."

[43] And immediately, while he was still speaking, ⟦judas|Yehudah|Judas⟧, one of the twelve, arrives, and with him a crowd with swords and clubs, from the high priests and the scribes and the elders.

[44] Now the one delivering him up had given them a signal, saying, "Whomever I kiss, he it is; take hold of him and lead him away securely."

[45] And coming, immediately approaching him he says, "Rabbi," and he kissed him.

[46] And they laid hands on him and took hold of him.

[47] But a certain one of those standing by, drawing the sword, struck the slave of the high priest and took off his ear.

[48] And answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "As against a robber you came out with swords and clubs to apprehend me?

[49] Daily I was with you in the temple teaching, and you did not take hold of me; but that the Scriptures might be fulfilled —"

[50] And leaving him, they all fled.

[51] And a certain young man was following along with him, clothed in a linen cloth over his naked body, and they take hold of him,

[52] but he, leaving the linen cloth behind, fled naked.

[53] And they led ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ away to the high priest, and all the high priests and the elders and the scribes come together.

[54] And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ from afar followed him, as far as inside, into the court of the high priest, and he was sitting together with the officers and warming himself at the light.

[55] Now the high priests and the whole ⟦sanhedrin|Sanhedrin|council⟧ were seeking testimony against ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ to put him to death, and they were not finding any;

[56] for many were testifying falsely against him, and the testimonies were not alike.

[57] And some, rising, were testifying falsely against him, saying,

[58] "We heard him saying, 'I will tear down this sanctuary made with hands, and in three days I will build another, not made with hands.'"

[59] And not even thus was their testimony alike.

[60] And the high priest, rising into the midst, asked ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, saying, "Do you answer nothing? What is it that these testify against you?"

[61] But he was silent and answered nothing. Again the high priest was asking him and says to him, "Are you the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧, the Son of the Blessed One?"

[62] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "**I am, and you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of the Power and coming with the clouds of heaven.**"

[63] And the high priest, tearing his tunics, says, "What need do we still have of witnesses?

[64] You heard the blasphemy; how does it appear to you?" And they all condemned him to be guilty of death.

[65] And some began to spit on him and to cover his face and to buffet him and to say to him, "Prophesy!" And the officers received him with slaps.

[66] And while ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ was below in the court, there comes one of the slave women of the high priest,

[67] and seeing ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ warming himself, looking at him she says, "You also were with the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazarene⟧, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧."

[68] But he denied, saying, "I neither know nor understand what you are saying." And he went out outside into the forecourt, and a rooster crowed.

[69] And the slave woman, seeing him, began again to say to those standing by, "This one is of them."

[70] But again he was denying. And after a little, again those standing by were saying to ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, "Truly you are of them, for you are also a Galilean."

[71] But he began to curse and to swear, "I do not know this man of whom you speak."

[72] And immediately a rooster crowed a second time. And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ remembered the word, how ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had said to him, "Before a rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times." And thinking upon it, he was weeping.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v33) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved. The name is not in the critical text in v22 (WH NA28).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v25) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v61) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (throughout) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"And" / "immediately" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — Mark's καί at the start of sentences, καὶ εὐθύς "and immediately" (v43, 45, 72) and his present tenses in narrative ("say", v12; "sends … says", v13; "comes", v17; "says", v27, 30, 32, 34, 37, 41, 45, 61, 63, 67; "come", v32; "takes along", v33; "comes … finds", v37; "arrives", v43; "take hold", v51; "come together", v53; "comes", v66) are kept as they are.
- **"After two days was the feast of the passover, and of unleavened bread" → "Now after two days it was the Passover and the Unleavened Bread"** (v1) — Greek τὸ πάσχα καὶ τὰ ἄζυμα, "the Pesach and the unleavened (loaves)"; the Greek has no word "feast" here.
- **"the chief priests" → "the high priests"** (v1, 10, 43, 53, 55) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of the word for the high priest (v47, 53, 54, 60, 61, 63, 66), as 8:31.
- **"how they might take him by craft, and put him to death" → "how, taking hold of him by guile, they might kill him"** (v1) — Greek ἐν δόλῳ κρατήσαντες ἀποκτείνωσιν; κρατέω, "take hold of", is the verb of v44, 46, 49, 51.
- **"But they said, Not on the feast day, lest there be an uproar of the people" → "for they were saying, 'Not in the feast, lest there be a commotion of the people'"** (v2) — Greek ἔλεγον γάρ (WH Treg NA28); ἐν τῇ ἑορτῇ, "in the feast" (during it, or in the festival crowd); θόρυβος as 5:38.
- **"as he sat at meat … an alabaster box of ointment of spikenard very precious; and she brake the box, and poured it on his head" → "as he was reclining … an alabaster flask of ointment of genuine nard, very costly; breaking the alabaster flask, she poured it down over his head"** (v3) — Greek κατακειμένου (as 2:15); ἀλάβαστρον, a flask of alabaster; μύρου νάρδου πιστικῆς πολυτελοῦς: πιστικός is found only here and in John 12:3, probably "genuine, trustworthy" (from πίστις), by others "liquid"; κατέχεεν, "poured down".
- **"that had indignation within themselves, and said, Why was this waste of the ointment made?" → "being indignant among themselves: 'For what has this waste of the ointment come to be?'"** (v4) — "and said" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28. Greek ἀπώλεια, elsewhere "destruction" (the noun of ἀπόλλυμι, "destroy, lose"); here the loss of the ointment, so "waste" is kept.
- **"it might have been sold … And they murmured against her" → "this ointment could have been sold … And they were speaking sternly to her"** (v5) — Greek ἠδύνατο τοῦτο τὸ μύρον πραθῆναι; ἐνεβριμῶντο αὐτῇ, the verb of 1:43, "snort at".
- **"Let her alone; why trouble ye her? she hath wrought a good work on me" → "Let her be; why do you give her troubles? She has worked a good work in me"** (v6) — Greek ἄφετε αὐτήν (ἀφίημι, "leave, let"); κόπους παρέχετε, as Gal 6:17; καλὸν ἔργον ἠργάσατο ἐν ἐμοί, verb and noun of one root, and ἐν, "in / on me".
- **"ye have the poor with you always, and whensoever ye will ye may do them good" → "the poor you always have with yourselves, and whenever you want you are able to do good to them"** (v7) — Greek μεθ' ἑαυτῶν; δύνασθε αὐτοῖς εὖ ποιῆσαι (WH adds πάντοτε, "always", after αὐτοῖς; Treg NA28 do not).
- **"She hath done what she could: she is come aforehand to anoint my body to the burying" → "What she had, she did; she anointed my body beforehand for the burial"** (v8) — Greek ὃ ἔσχεν ἐποίησεν, "what she had (it in her to do), she did"; προέλαβεν μυρίσαι, literally "she took beforehand to anoint"; ἐνταφιασμός, the preparing of a body for burial.
- **"Verily I say unto you, Wheresoever this gospel shall be preached throughout the whole world, this also that she hath done shall be spoken of for a memorial of her" → "And amen, I say to you, wherever the gospel is proclaimed into the whole world, also what this woman did will be spoken of for a memorial of her"** (v9) — Greek ἀμὴν δέ (WH Treg NA28); τὸ εὐαγγέλιον, without "this" (RP); εἰς ὅλον τὸν κόσμον. "Amen" also in v18, 25, 30.
- **"Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, went unto the chief priests, to betray him" → "Judas Iscariot, the one of the twelve, went away to the high priests that he might deliver him up"** (v10) — Greek Ἰσκαριώθ (WH NA28); ὁ εἷς τῶν δώδεκα, with the article, literally "the one of the twelve"; παραδοῖ, "hand over, deliver up", the verb of 3:19 (v11, 18, 21, 41, 42, 44): the word "betray" (προδίδωμι) is not used.
- **"they were glad, and promised to give him money. And he sought how he might conveniently betray him" → "rejoiced and promised to give him silver. And he was seeking how he might deliver him up at an opportune time"** (v11) — Greek ἐχάρησαν; ἀργύριον, "silver, money"; εὐκαίρως (as εὔκαιρος, 6:21).
- **"when they killed the passover … Where wilt thou that we go and prepare" → "when they were sacrificing the Passover … Where do you want us to go away and make ready"** (v12) — Greek ἔθυον, "were sacrificing" (a custom); τὸ πάσχα is the Passover lamb and meal; the Greek has no word "lamb". ἑτοιμάζω, "make ready" (v12, 15, 16), as 1:3.
- **"a pitcher of water" → "a jar of water"** (v13) — Greek κεράμιον, an earthen vessel.
- **"the goodman of the house … Where is the guestchamber" → "the master of the house … Where is my guest room"** (v14) — Greek οἰκοδεσπότης; τὸ κατάλυμά μου, "my lodging, guest room" (WH Treg NA28 have "my").
- **"a large upper room furnished and prepared" → "a large upper room, spread and ready"** (v15) — Greek ἐστρωμένον, "spread" (with couches or carpets); ἕτοιμον, "ready"; καὶ ἐκεῖ, "and there" (WH Treg NA28).
- **"as they sat and did eat … One of you which eateth with me shall betray me" → "as they were reclining and eating … one of you will deliver me up, the one eating with me"** (v18) — Greek ἀνακειμένων; ὁ ἐσθίων μετ' ἐμοῦ (Ps 41:9).
- **"they began to be sorrowful, and to say unto him one by one, Is it I? and another said, Is it I?" → "They began to be grieved and to say to him one by one, 'Surely not I?'"** (v19) — Greek λυπεῖσθαι; μήτι ἐγώ, a question expecting "no". "and another said, Is it I?" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"that dippeth with me in the dish" → "the one dipping with me into the bowl"** (v20) — Greek ὁ ἐμβαπτόμενος … εἰς τὸ τρύβλιον (Treg NA28; WH "into the one bowl"); ἐμβάπτω is "dip in", of the root of βαπτίζω "immerse". The Greek has no word "bread".
- **"The Son of man indeed goeth … by whom the Son of man is betrayed! good were it for that man if he had never been born" → "because the Son of Man indeed goes … through whom the Son of Man is delivered up! It would be good for him if that man had not been born"** (v21) — Greek ὅτι, "because" (WH Treg NA28); δι' οὗ, "through whom"; καλὸν αὐτῷ, without a verb, "good for him" (not a comparative); ἐγεννήθη is the passive of γεννάω "beget", here of birth.
- **"Jesus took bread, and blessed, and brake it, and gave to them, and said, Take, eat" → "taking bread, having blessed, he broke it and gave to them and said, 'Take'"** (v22) — Greek λαβὼν ἄρτον εὐλογήσας ἔκλασεν; ἄρτον is "bread" or "a loaf"; "it" is supplied. "eat" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"he took the cup, and when he had given thanks" → "taking a cup, having given thanks"** (v23) — Greek ποτήριον, without the article; εὐχαριστήσας (v22 εὐλογήσας, "having blessed").
- **"my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many" → "my blood of the covenant, which is being poured out for many"** (v24) — Greek τὸ αἷμά μου τῆς διαθήκης, without "new" (RP καινῆς); διαθήκη is "covenant"; ἐκχυννόμενον, a present participle; ὑπὲρ πολλῶν.
- **"I will drink no more of the fruit of the vine" → "I shall by no means drink any more from the produce of the vine"** (v25) — Greek οὐκέτι οὐ μὴ πίω, the strong negation (as 13:2, 30); γένημα, "produce, what comes of", not καρπός "fruit".
- **"when they had sung an hymn" → "having sung a hymn"** (v26) — Greek ὑμνήσαντες, one word, "having hymned": at the Passover the Hallel psalms (Ps 113–118).
- **"All ye shall be offended because of me this night … I will smite" → "You will all be made to stumble … I will strike"** (v27) — Greek σκανδαλισθήσεσθε, as 4:17; 6:3; "because of me this night" (RP) is not in the critical text. πατάξω (Zech 13:7).
- **"after that I am risen" → "after I have been raised"** (v28) — Greek μετὰ τὸ ἐγερθῆναί με, a passive.
- **"Although all shall be offended, yet will not I" → "Even if all will be made to stumble, yet not I"** (v29) — Greek εἰ καὶ πάντες σκανδαλισθήσονται, ἀλλ' οὐκ ἐγώ.
- **"this day, even in this night, before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice" → "you, today, this night, before a rooster crows twice, will deny me three times"** (v30) — Greek σύ, emphatic; ἀλέκτορα, without the article; φωνῆσαι, "sound, call" (the verb "call" of 1:26), of a rooster "crow" (v68, 72); ἀπαρνήσῃ, as 8:34.
- **"he spake the more vehemently … I will not deny thee in any wise. Likewise also said they all" → "he was speaking beyond measure … I will by no means deny you. And likewise all also were saying"** (v31) — Greek ἐκπερισσῶς ἐλάλει (WH Treg NA28), "exceedingly, beyond measure" (as 6:51), that is, with great vehemence; συναποθανεῖν, "die together with"; οὐ μή σε ἀπαρνήσομαι.
- **"they came to a place which was named Gethsemane" → "they come to a plot of land whose name is Gethsemane"** (v32) — Greek χωρίον, a piece of land, an enclosed field; Γεθσημανί, Hebrew / Aramaic "oil press".
- **"began to be sore amazed, and to be very heavy" → "began to be utterly astounded and distressed"** (v33) — Greek ἐκθαμβεῖσθαι, a strengthened form of θαμβέομαι "be astounded" (1:27): to be struck with awe or terror; ἀδημονεῖν, as Phil 2:26. Editions: Ἰάκωβον καὶ Treg RP (and the printed SBLGNT) ] τὸν Ἰάκωβον καὶ τὸν WH NA28 — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides; the translation is the same.
- **"My soul is exceeding sorrowful unto death: tarry ye here, and watch" → "My soul is deeply grieved, unto death; remain here and be awake"** (v34) — Greek περίλυπος, as 6:26 (Ps 42:5, 11; 43:5); μείνατε, the verb μένω, of a stay in a place "remain" (as 6:10); γρηγορεῖτε, "be awake" (v37, 38), as 13:35, 37.
- **"he went forward a little, and fell on the ground, and prayed that, if it were possible" → "going forward a little, he was falling upon the ground and was praying that, if it is possible"** (v35) — Greek ἔπιπτεν, προσηύχετο, imperfects (WH NA28; Treg RP ἔπεσεν, "fell"); εἰ δυνατόν ἐστιν.
- **"Abba, Father … take away this cup from me: nevertheless not what I will, but what thou wilt" → "*Abba*, Father … take this cup away from me; but not what I want, but what you"** (v36) — Greek Αββα ὁ πατήρ: the Aramaic אַבָּא, "Father", kept by Mark and followed by its Greek translation ὁ πατήρ, "Father" (literally "the Father", a nominative used in address). παρένεγκε, "carry past, take away". ἀλλ' οὐ τί ἐγὼ θέλω ἀλλὰ τί σύ: the second clause has no verb, and the verse leaves it so.
- **"sleepest thou? couldest not thou watch one hour?" → "are you sleeping? Did you not have strength to be awake one hour?"** (v37) — Greek οὐκ ἴσχυσας, from ἰσχύς "strength" (as 5:4).
- **"Watch ye and pray, lest ye enter into temptation. The spirit truly is ready" → "Be awake and pray, that you may not come into trial; the spirit indeed is ready"** (v38) — Greek ἵνα μὴ ἔλθητε (WH NA28; Treg RP εἰσέλθητε, "enter"); πειρασμός is "trial, testing" and "temptation", one word (the noun of πειράζω, "tempt", 1:13; 8:11); πρόθυμον, "ready, willing"; σάρξ, "flesh".
- **"spake the same words" → "saying the same word"** (v39) — Greek τὸν αὐτὸν λόγον, singular.
- **"for their eyes were heavy, neither wist they what to answer him" → "for their eyes were being weighed down, and they did not know what they should answer him"** (v40) — Greek καταβαρυνόμενοι (WH Treg NA28).
- **"Sleep on now, and take your rest: it is enough" → "Sleep on now and rest. It is enough"** (v41) — Greek καθεύδετε τὸ λοιπὸν καὶ ἀναπαύεσθε: the forms are imperatives or statements, and the printed text has no question mark; as a question, "Are you still sleeping and resting?". τὸ λοιπόν is "for the rest (of the time), henceforth". ἀπέχει is one word of uncertain meaning: "it is enough"; or "it is settled, paid in full" (ἀπέχω, "have received in full"); or "it (the hour) is far off?".
- **"is betrayed into the hands of sinners" → "is delivered up into the hands of the sinners"** (v41) — Greek παραδίδοται; τῶν ἁμαρτωλῶν, with the article.
- **"Rise up, let us go; lo, he that betrayeth me is at hand" → "Arise, let us go; behold, the one delivering me up has drawn near"** (v42) — Greek ἐγείρεσθε; ὁ παραδιδούς με; ἤγγικεν, a perfect, as 1:15.
- **"cometh Judas … a great multitude with swords and staves" → "Judas … arrives, and with him a crowd with swords and clubs"** (v43) — Greek παραγίνεται; ὄχλος, without "great" (RP); ξύλων, pieces of wood, clubs.
- **"he that betrayed him had given them a token … Whomsoever I shall kiss, that same is he; take him, and lead him away safely" → "the one delivering him up had given them a signal … Whomever I kiss, he it is; take hold of him and lead him away securely"** (v44) — Greek σύσσημον, a sign agreed on; φιλήσω, the verb φιλέω, "love, show affection", here "kiss"; in v45 the compound κατεφίλησεν, "kissed (warmly)". ἀσφαλῶς, "securely, safely".
- **"saith, Master, master; and kissed him" → "says, 'Rabbi,' and he kissed him"** (v45) — Greek Ῥαββί, once (WH Treg NA28), the Hebrew address "my great one, my teacher", kept by Mark.
- **"laid their hands on him, and took him" → "laid hands on him and took hold of him"** (v46) — Greek ἐπέβαλαν τὰς χεῖρας αὐτῷ (WH Treg NA28); ἐκράτησαν.
- **"one of them that stood by drew a sword, and smote a servant of the high priest, and cut off his ear" → "a certain one of those standing by, drawing the sword, struck the slave of the high priest and took off his ear"** (v47) — Greek εἷς δέ τις; τὴν μάχαιραν, "the sword" (his own); ἔπαισεν (another verb than "strike" in v27); τὸν δοῦλον, "the slave"; ἀφεῖλεν, "took away"; ὠτάριον, a diminutive form of "ear".
- **"Are ye come out, as against a thief, with swords and with staves to take me?" → "As against a robber you came out with swords and clubs to apprehend me?"** (v48) — Greek λῃστής, a robber, a bandit (also an insurgent; 15:27), not κλέπτης "thief"; συλλαβεῖν, "take, apprehend", another verb than "take hold of" (κρατέω, v44, 46, 49).
- **"ye took me not: but the scriptures must be fulfilled" → "you did not take hold of me; but that the Scriptures might be fulfilled —"** (v49) — Greek ἀλλ' ἵνα πληρωθῶσιν αἱ γραφαί: a clause of purpose with no main verb; the sentence is left unfinished ("but this happens that …"), or the clause is a command, "but let the Scriptures be fulfilled".
- **"they all forsook him, and fled" → "leaving him, they all fled"** (v50) — Greek ἀφέντες, as 1:18.
- **"there followed him a certain young man, having a linen cloth cast about his naked body; and the young men laid hold on him" → "a certain young man was following along with him, clothed in a linen cloth over his naked body, and they take hold of him"** (v51) — Greek συνηκολούθει, an imperfect (as 5:37); σινδόνα ἐπὶ γυμνοῦ, "a linen cloth upon (his) naked (body)"; κρατοῦσιν, a present; "the young men" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"he left the linen cloth, and fled from them naked" → "he, leaving the linen cloth behind, fled naked"** (v52) — "from them" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"with him were assembled all the chief priests" → "all the high priests … come together"** (v53) — Greek συνέρχονται, a present; "with him" (Treg RP αὐτῷ) is not in WH NA28.
- **"even into the palace of the high priest: and he sat with the servants, and warmed himself at the fire" → "as far as inside, into the court of the high priest, and he was sitting together with the officers and warming himself at the light"** (v54) — Greek ἕως ἔσω εἰς τὴν αὐλήν, the open court of the house (v66; the word of 15:16); ὑπηρέται, "officers" (v65), the men under the orders of the high priest and the Sanhedrin (the word "assistant" of 1 Cor 4:1); πρὸς τὸ φῶς, "at the light" of the fire.
- **"all the council sought for witness … and found none" → "the whole Sanhedrin were seeking testimony … and they were not finding any"** (v55) — Greek συνέδριον, the supreme council of the Jews in Jerusalem; μαρτυρία, "testimony" (v55, 56, 59); ἐζήτουν, ηὕρισκον, imperfects.
- **"many bare false witness against him, but their witness agreed not together" → "many were testifying falsely against him, and the testimonies were not alike"** (v56, 57, 59) — Greek ἐψευδομαρτύρουν; ἴσαι … οὐκ ἦσαν, "were not equal", did not match (Deut 19:15).
- **"I will destroy this temple that is made with hands, and within three days" → "I will tear down this sanctuary made with hands, and in three days"** (v58) — Greek καταλύσω, as 13:2; ναός "sanctuary", the temple building itself (13:1, 3 and 14:49 have ἱερόν "temple", the whole precinct); διὰ τριῶν ἡμερῶν, literally "through three days"; ἀχειροποίητον.
- **"stood up in the midst … Answerest thou nothing? what is it which these witness against thee?" → "rising into the midst … Do you answer nothing? What is it that these testify against you?"** (v60) — Greek ἀναστὰς εἰς μέσον. The two questions can also be read as one: "Do you answer nothing to what these testify against you?"
- **"he held his peace … asked him, and said unto him" → "he was silent … was asking him and says to him"** (v61) — Greek ἐσιώπα, ἐπηρώτα, imperfects; λέγει, a present.
- **"the Son of the Blessed" → "the Son of the Blessed One"** (v61) — Greek τοῦ εὐλογητοῦ, "the Blessed", a reverent Jewish way of naming God without saying the name.
- **"sitting on the right hand of power, and coming in the clouds of heaven" → "sitting at the right hand of the Power and coming with the clouds of heaven"** (v62) — Greek ἐκ δεξιῶν καθήμενον τῆς δυνάμεως, with the article: "the Power" stands for God; μετὰ τῶν νεφελῶν, "with the clouds" (Dan 7:13). Ἐγώ εἰμι, "I am", as 6:50; 13:6.
- **"rent his clothes, and saith, What need we any further witnesses?" → "tearing his tunics, says, 'What need do we still have of witnesses?'"** (v63) — Greek τοὺς χιτῶνας, plural of the word "tunic" (6:9), the garments worn under the cloak.
- **"Ye have heard the blasphemy: what think ye?" → "You heard the blasphemy; how does it appear to you?"** (v64) — Greek ἠκούσατε, a past tense; τί ὑμῖν φαίνεται, literally "what appears to you?".
- **"condemned him to be guilty of death" kept** (v64) — Greek κατέκριναν αὐτὸν ἔνοχον εἶναι θανάτου; ἔνοχος is "held by, liable to" (as 3:29).
- **"to buffet him … and the servants did strike him with the palms of their hands" → "to buffet him … And the officers received him with slaps"** (v65) — Greek περικαλύπτειν, "cover all around"; κολαφίζειν, "strike with the fist" (as 2 Cor 12:7); ῥαπίσμασιν αὐτὸν ἔλαβον (WH Treg NA28), "took him with blows" of the hand or of a rod.
- **"one of the maids" → "one of the slave women"** (v66, 69) — Greek παιδίσκη, a female slave (as Gal 4:22), often a young one.
- **"she looked upon him, and said, And thou also wast with Jesus of Nazareth" → "looking at him she says, 'You also were with the Nazarene, Yeshua'"** (v67) — Greek μετὰ τοῦ Ναζαρηνοῦ ἦσθα τοῦ Ἰησοῦ (WH Treg NA28), "the Nazarene" (as 1:24) first.
- **"But he denied, saying, I know not, neither understand I what thou sayest. And he went out into the porch; and the cock crew" → "But he denied, saying, 'I neither know nor understand what you are saying.' And he went out outside into the forecourt, and a rooster crowed"** (v68) — Greek ἠρνήσατο, without an object; οὔτε οἶδα οὔτε ἐπίσταμαι σὺ τί λέγεις: ἐπίσταμαι is "know, be acquainted with, understand"; the words can also be divided "I neither know nor understand. What are you saying?". προαύλιον, the space before the court. Editions: καὶ ἀλέκτωρ ἐφώνησεν Treg NA28 RP (printed) ] not in WH — 2–2, NA28 decides, so the clause stands.
- **"a maid saw him again, and began to say to them that stood by, This is one of them" → "the slave woman, seeing him, began again to say to those standing by, 'This one is of them'"** (v69) — Greek ἡ παιδίσκη, "the slave woman" (the same one); οὗτος ἐξ αὐτῶν ἐστιν.
- **"he denied it again … Surely thou art one of them: for thou art a Galilaean, and thy speech agreeth thereto" → "again he was denying … Truly you are of them, for you are also a Galilean"** (v70) — Greek ἠρνεῖτο, an imperfect; καὶ γὰρ Γαλιλαῖος εἶ. Editions: καὶ ἡ λαλιά σου ὁμοιάζει, "and your speech is like", RP (and the printed SBLGNT) ] not in WH Treg NA28 — the verse follows the three editions and does not have it.
- **"he began to curse and to swear, saying" → "he began to curse and to swear"** (v71) — Greek ἀναθεματίζειν, "to put under a curse (ἀνάθεμα)", without an object: he calls a curse down on himself if he lies, or curses others.
- **"the second time the cock crew. And Peter called to mind the word that Jesus said unto him … And when he thought thereon, he wept" → "immediately a rooster crowed a second time. And Kefa remembered the word, how Yeshua had said to him … And thinking upon it, he was weeping"** (v72) — Greek καὶ εὐθύς (WH Treg NA28); τὸ ῥῆμα ὡς εἶπεν; ἔκλαιεν, an imperfect. ἐπιβαλών is literally "having thrown upon" (the verb of v46) with nothing added, and its sense is uncertain: "having set (his mind) upon it"; or "having begun"; or "having broken down"; or "having covered (his head)". The verse keeps the first, with the KJV.
