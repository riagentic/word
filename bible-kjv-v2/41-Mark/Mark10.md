# Mark 10

[1] And from there, rising, he comes into the borders of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ and beyond the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and crowds come together to him again, and as he was accustomed, again he was teaching them.

[2] And ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, coming to him, were asking him whether it is permitted for a man to send away a wife, tempting him.

[3] But he, answering, said to them, "What commandment did ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ give you?"

[4] And they said, "⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ permitted to write a document of divorce and to send away."

[5] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "With regard to your hardness of heart he wrote you this commandment;

[6] but from the beginning of creation, 'male and female he made them';

[7] 'on account of this a man will leave his father and mother and will be joined to his wife,

[8] and the two will be one flesh'; so that they are no longer two, but one flesh.

[9] What therefore ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ yoked together, let man not separate."

[10] And in the house the disciples again were asking him about this.

[11] And he says to them, "Whoever sends away his wife and marries another commits adultery against her,

[12] and if she herself, having sent away her husband, marries another, she commits adultery."

[13] And they were bringing young children to him that he might touch them; but the disciples rebuked them.

[14] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, seeing it, was indignant and said to them, "**Let the young children come to me; do not hinder them, for of such is the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**

**[15] Amen, I say to you, whoever does not accept the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ as a young child will by no means enter into it.**"

[16] And taking them in his arms, he was blessing them, putting his hands upon them.

[17] And as he was going out into the way, one, running up and kneeling to him, was asking him, "Good Teacher, what shall I do that I may inherit eternal life?"

[18] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Why do you call me good? No one is good except one, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[19] You know the commandments: '**Do not murder, Do not commit adultery, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Do not deprive, Honor your father and mother**.'"

[20] But he said to him, "Teacher, all these things I guarded from my youth."

[21] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, looking at him, loved him and said to him, "One thing is lacking to you: go, sell whatever you have and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; and come, follow me."

[22] But he, becoming gloomy at the word, went away grieved, for he was one having many possessions.

[23] And looking around, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to his disciples, "With what difficulty will those who have wealth enter into the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!"

[24] But the disciples were astounded at his words. But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, answering again, says to them, "Children, how difficult it is to enter into the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!

[25] It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of the needle than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[26] But they were astonished beyond measure, saying among themselves, "And who is able to be saved?"

[27] Looking at them, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says, "With men it is impossible, but not with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; for all things are possible with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[28] ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ began to say to him, "Behold, we left all things and have followed you."

[29] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Amen, I say to you, there is no one who left house or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or fields for the sake of me and for the sake of the gospel,

[30] who will not receive a hundredfold now in this time, houses and brothers and sisters and mothers and children and fields, with persecutions, and in the age that is coming eternal life.

[31] But many first will be last, and the last first."

[32] Now they were on the way, going up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was going before them, and they were astounded, but those following were afraid. And taking along the twelve again, he began to tell them the things about to happen to him:

[33] "Behold, we are going up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and the Son of Man will be delivered up to the high priests and to the scribes, and they will condemn him to death and will deliver him up to the nations,

[34] and they will mock him and spit on him and scourge him and kill, and after three days he will rise."

[35] And ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, the sons of ⟦zebedee|Zebedaios|Zebedee⟧, come up to him, saying to him, "Teacher, we want that whatever we ask you, you would do for us."

[36] But he said to them, "What do you want me to do for you?"

[37] And they said to him, "Give to us that we may sit, one at your right hand and one at the left, in your glory."

[38] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "You do not know what you are asking. Are you able to drink the cup which I drink, or to be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ with the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ with which I am ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧?"

[39] And they said to him, "We are able." But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "The cup which I drink you will drink, and with the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ with which I am ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ you will be ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧;

[40] but to sit at my right hand or at the left is not mine to give, but to those for whom it has been prepared."

[41] And hearing it, the ten began to be indignant about ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧.

[42] And calling them to himself, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "You know that those who are reputed to rule the nations lord it over them, and their great ones exercise authority over them.

[43] But it is not so among you; **but whoever wants to become great among you shall be your servant,**

[44] and whoever wants to be first among you shall be slave of all;

**[45] for even the Son of Man did not come to be served but to serve, and to give his soul as a ransom for many.**"

[46] And they come into ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧. And as he was going out from ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, and his disciples and a considerable crowd, the son of ⟦timaeus|Timaios|Timaeus⟧, ⟦bartimaeus|Bartimaios|Bartimaeus⟧, a blind beggar, was sitting beside the way.

[47] And hearing that it is ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the ⟦nazarenes|Notzri|Nazarene⟧, he began to cry out and to say, "Son of David, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, have mercy on me!"

[48] And many were rebuking him, that he should be silent; but he was crying out much more, "Son of David, have mercy on me!"

[49] And standing still, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "Call him." And they call the blind man, saying to him, "Be of good courage; arise, he is calling you."

[50] But he, throwing off his garment, leaping up, came to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[51] And answering him, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said, "What do you want me to do for you?" And the blind man said to him, "⟦rabbouni|Rabbouni|Rabbi⟧, that I may receive sight."

[52] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Go; your faith has saved you." And immediately he received sight, and he was following him on the way.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v35, 41) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv9, 14, 15, 18, 23, 24, 25, 27) — divine name retained throughout. In v6 the critical text has no "God" (RP adds ὁ θεός): "he made them".
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (vv28) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"baptized … baptism" → "immersed … immersion"** (vv38-39) — Greek βαπτίζω, βάπτισμα, "immerse, immersion", here of what is coming upon *Yeshua*. "I drink", "I am immersed" are present tenses.
- **"And" / "immediately" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — Mark's καί at the start of sentences, εὐθύς "immediately" (v52) and his present tenses ("comes … come together", v1; "says", v11, 23, 24, 27, 42; "come up", v35; "come", v46; "call", v49) are kept as they are.
- **"into the coasts of Judaea by the farther side of Jordan" → "into the borders of Judea and beyond the Jordan"** (v1) — Greek εἰς τὰ ὅρια τῆς Ἰουδαίας καὶ πέραν τοῦ Ἰορδάνου (WH Treg NA28; RP "through the other side of the Jordan").
- **"the Pharisees came to him, and asked him, Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife?" → "Pharisees, coming to him, were asking him whether it is permitted for a man to send away a wife"** (v2) — Greek ἔξεστιν (as 2:24, 26; 3:4); ἀπολῦσαι, "release, send away" (the verb of 6:36, 45; 8:3, 9), here of divorce; the Greek has no "his". Editions: + προσελθόντες Φαρισαῖοι WH Treg NA28 RP ] without these words ("And they were asking him") the printed SBLGNT (Holmes), WH-margin — the verse follows the majority.
- **"man" / "wife" / "husband"** (v2, 7, 11, 12) — "man" in v2 and "husband" in v12 are ἀνήρ; "man" in v7 and v9 is ἄνθρωπος, a human being; "wife" is γυνή, the word for "woman".
- **"What did Moses command you?" → "What commandment did Moses give you?"** (v3) — Greek ἐνετείλατο, the verb of "commandment" (ἐντολή, v5, 19).
- **"Moses suffered to write a bill of divorcement, and to put her away" → "Moses permitted to write a document of divorce and to send away"** (v4) — Greek ἐπέτρεψεν (as 5:13); βιβλίον ἀποστασίου (Deut 24:1); the Greek has no "her".
- **"For the hardness of your heart … precept" → "With regard to your hardness of heart … commandment"** (v5) — Greek πρὸς τὴν σκληροκαρδίαν ὑμῶν; ἐντολήν.
- **"For this cause shall a man leave … and cleave to his wife" → "on account of this a man will leave … and will be joined to his wife"** (v7) — Greek ἕνεκεν τούτου; προσκολληθήσεται, as Eph 5:31. WH does not have "and will be joined to his wife"; Treg NA28 RP and the SBLGNT have it.
- **"they twain shall be one flesh" → "the two will be one flesh"** (v8) — Greek ἔσονται οἱ δύο εἰς σάρκα μίαν, literally "will be unto one flesh" (Gen 2:24).
- **"hath joined together, let not man put asunder" → "yoked together, let man not separate"** (v9) — Greek συνέζευξεν, "yoked together" (a different verb from "be joined", v7); χωριζέτω, the verb of 1 Cor 7:10.
- **"in the house" kept** (v10) — Greek εἰς τὴν οἰκίαν, literally "into the house" (WH Treg NA28; RP ἐν).
- **"committeth adultery against her"** (v11) — Greek μοιχᾶται ἐπ' αὐτήν: "against her" (the first wife) or "with her" (the second).
- **"if a woman shall put away her husband, and be married to another" → "if she herself, having sent away her husband, marries another"** (v12) — Greek αὐτὴ ἀπολύσασα τὸν ἄνδρα αὐτῆς γαμήσῃ ἄλλον (WH Treg NA28).
- **"rebuked those that brought them" → "rebuked them"** (v13) — Greek ἐπετίμησαν αὐτοῖς (WH NA28; Treg RP "were rebuking those who brought them").
- **"was much displeased" → "was indignant"** (v14, 41) — Greek ἀγανακτέω.
- **"Suffer the little children … forbid them not" → "Let the young children … do not hinder them"** (v14) — Greek ἄφετε; παιδία (as 9:36); κωλύετε (as 9:38–39).
- **"receive … as a little child, he shall not enter therein" → "accept … as a young child will by no means enter into it"** (v15) — Greek δέξηται (as 9:37); οὐ μή, the strong negation.
- **"put his hands upon them, and blessed them" → "he was blessing them, putting his hands upon them"** (v16) — Greek κατευλόγει τιθεὶς τὰς χεῖρας ἐπ' αὐτά (WH NA28): an imperfect and a present participle; κατευλογέω is a strengthened form of "bless" (6:41; 8:7).
- **"there came one running, and kneeled to him" → "one, running up and kneeling to him"** (v17) — Greek προσδραμὼν εἷς καὶ γονυπετήσας αὐτόν; the Greek says only "one", not "a man".
- **"there is none good but one, that is, God" → "No one is good except one, *Elohim*"** (v18) — Greek οὐδεὶς ἀγαθὸς εἰ μὴ εἷς ὁ θεός, the wording of 2:7.
- **"Do not commit adultery, Do not kill … Defraud not" → "Do not murder, Do not commit adultery … Do not deprive"** (v19) — the order of WH NA28 (Treg RP: adultery first). ἀποστερήσῃς is "deprive (someone of what is his)", the verb of 1 Cor 6:7–8; "defraud" is another verb (πλεονεκτέω). The Greek has "your father and the mother".
- **"observed" → "guarded"** (v20) — Greek ἐφυλαξάμην, a simple past.
- **"beholding him loved him … One thing thou lackest" → "looking at him, loved him … One thing is lacking to you"** (v21) — Greek ἐμβλέψας (as 8:25); ἕν σε ὑστερεῖ.
- **"take up the cross, and follow me" → "follow me"** (v21) — "taking up the cross" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"was sad at that saying … had great possessions" → "becoming gloomy at the word … was one having many possessions"** (v22) — Greek στυγνάσας, "becoming dark, gloomy"; ἐπὶ τῷ λόγῳ; ἦν … ἔχων κτήματα πολλά.
- **"How hardly shall they that have riches" → "With what difficulty will those who have wealth"** (v23) — Greek δυσκόλως; τὰ χρήματα, "property, money" (a different word from "riches", πλοῦτος, 4:19).
- **"astonished" → "astounded"** (v24, 32) — Greek ἐθαμβοῦντο, as 1:27; "astonished" (v26) is ἐκπλήσσομαι.
- **"how hard is it for them that trust in riches to enter" → "how difficult it is to enter"** (v24) — WH NA28 and the SBLGNT do not have "for those who trust in riches" (Treg RP).
- **"the eye of a needle" → "the eye of the needle"** (v25) — Greek διὰ τῆς τρυμαλιᾶς τῆς ῥαφίδος, "through the hole of the needle" (NA28 RP and the SBLGNT; WH Treg without the articles).
- **"astonished out of measure, saying among themselves, Who then can be saved?" → "astonished beyond measure, saying among themselves, And who is able to be saved?"** (v26) — Greek περισσῶς; πρὸς ἑαυτούς (Treg NA28 RP; WH πρὸς αὐτόν, "to him"); Καὶ τίς.
- **"we have left all" → "we left all things and have followed you"** (v28) — Greek ἀφήκαμεν, a simple past; ἠκολουθήκαμεν, a perfect (WH Treg NA28).
- **"or father, or mother, or wife … or lands, for my sake, and the gospel's" → "or mother or father … or fields for the sake of me and for the sake of the gospel"** (v29) — "or wife" (RP) is not in the critical text; ἀγρούς, "fields"; ἕνεκεν twice.
- **"But he shall receive" → "who will not receive"** (v30) — Greek ἐὰν μὴ λάβῃ, literally "if he does not receive": no one has left these things without receiving.
- **"in the world to come" → "in the age that is coming"** (v30) — Greek ἐν τῷ αἰῶνι τῷ ἐρχομένῳ; "this time" is καιρός.
- **"as they followed, they were afraid" → "but those following were afraid"** (v32) — Greek οἱ δὲ ἀκολουθοῦντες (WH Treg NA28): the wording can mean a second group besides those who "were astounded".
- **"took again the twelve" → "taking along the twelve again"** (v32) — Greek παραλαβών, as 4:36; 5:40; 9:2.
- **"delivered" → "delivered up"** (v33) — Greek παραδοθήσεται, παραδώσουσιν, as 9:31.
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v33) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς, as 8:31.
- **"Gentiles" → "nations"** (v33, 42) — Greek τὰ ἔθνη.
- **"shall scourge him, and shall spit upon him, and shall kill him: and the third day" → "spit on him and scourge him and kill, and after three days"** (v34) — the order and wording of WH Treg NA28; "him" after "kill" is not in WH NA28. μετὰ τρεῖς ἡμέρας as 8:31; 9:31.
- **"we would that thou shouldest do for us whatsoever we shall desire" → "we want that whatever we ask you, you would do for us"** (v35) — Greek θέλομεν ἵνα ὃ ἐὰν αἰτήσωμέν σε ποιήσῃς ἡμῖν.
- **"What would ye that I should do for you?" → "What do you want me to do for you?"** (v36, 51) — Greek Τί θέλετε ποιήσω ὑμῖν (WH Treg and the SBLGNT; NA28 adds με).
- **"Grant unto us … one on thy right hand, and the other on thy left hand" → "Give to us … one at your right hand and one at the left"** (v37, 40) — Greek δός; ἐκ δεξιῶν … ἐξ ἀριστερῶν (v37), ἐξ εὐωνύμων (v40): two words for "left". ἐκ δεξιῶν is "at the right hand" as in Heb 1:13 (the word "hand" is in the English idiom, not in the Greek); the Greek has no second "your".
- **"but it shall be given to them for whom it is prepared" → "but to those for whom it has been prepared"** (v40) — Greek ἀλλ' οἷς ἡτοίμασται; "it shall be given" is not in the Greek. The same letters can be divided ἄλλοις, "for others it has been prepared".
- **"they which are accounted to rule over the Gentiles exercise lordship over them" → "those who are reputed to rule the nations lord it over them"** (v42) — Greek οἱ δοκοῦντες ἄρχειν, "those who seem / are held to rule"; κατακυριεύουσιν, κατεξουσιάζουσιν, strengthened forms of "be lord" and "have authority".
- **"so shall it not be among you" → "it is not so among you"** (v43) — Greek ἐστιν, a present (WH Treg NA28; RP ἔσται, "shall be").
- **"minister" → "servant"; "servant" → "slave"** (v43, 44) — Greek διάκονος, δοῦλος; "shall be" (ἔσται) is a future, as 9:35.
- **"to be ministered unto, but to minister" → "to be served but to serve"** (v45) — Greek διακονηθῆναι, διακονῆσαι, of one root with "servant" (v43).
- **"his life" → "his soul"** (v45) — Greek ψυχήν, as 8:35–37: his own life and self.
- **"a ransom for many" kept** (v45) — Greek λύτρον ἀντὶ πολλῶν: λύτρον is the price paid to release a slave or captive; ἀντί is "in exchange for, in place of" (not ὑπέρ, "on behalf of").
- **"a great number of people, blind Bartimaeus, the son of Timaeus, sat by the highway side begging" → "a considerable crowd, the son of Timaeus, Bartimaeus, a blind beggar, was sitting beside the way"** (v46) — Greek ὄχλου ἱκανοῦ; ὁ υἱὸς Τιμαίου Βαρτιμαῖος τυφλὸς προσαίτης (WH Treg NA28). *Bar-Timai* is Aramaic for "son of Timai"; Mark gives the Greek first.
- **"Jesus of Nazareth" → "Yeshua the Nazarene"** (v47) — Greek Ἰησοῦς ὁ Ναζαρηνός, as 1:24; the place name is not in the verse.
- **"Jesus, thou Son of David" → "Son of David, Yeshua"** (v47) — the Greek order Υἱὲ Δαυὶδ Ἰησοῦ.
- **"charged him that he should hold his peace: but he cried the more a great deal" → "were rebuking him, that he should be silent; but he was crying out much more"** (v48) — Greek ἐπετίμων (as 8:30), σιωπήσῃ, πολλῷ μᾶλλον ἔκραζεν.
- **"commanded him to be called … Be of good comfort, rise" → "said, Call him … Be of good courage; arise"** (v49) — Greek Φωνήσατε αὐτόν (WH Treg NA28); Θάρσει, as 6:50; ἔγειρε.
- **"casting away his garment, rose" → "throwing off his garment, leaping up"** (v50) — Greek ἀναπηδήσας (WH Treg NA28; RP ἀναστάς, "rising").
- **"Lord, that I might receive my sight" → "Rabbouni, that I may receive sight"** (v51, 52) — Greek Ραββουνι, Aramaic "my master / my teacher"; ἀναβλέψω, ἀνέβλεψεν: the verb is "look up" (6:41; 7:34; 8:24) and, of the blind, "see again".
- **"Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole" → "Go; your faith has saved you"** (v52) — Greek σέσωκέν σε, as 5:34.
- **"followed Jesus in the way" → "was following him on the way"** (v52) — Greek ἠκολούθει αὐτῷ (WH Treg NA28), an imperfect.

### Notes
- **v6** quotes Gen 1:27.
- **v7-8** quotes Gen 2:24.
- **v19** cites Ex 20:12-16; Deut 5:16-20.
- **v51** — *Rabbouni*: an Aramaic form of address, "my master"; in the New Testament only here and John 20:16.
