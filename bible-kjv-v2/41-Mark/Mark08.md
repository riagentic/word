# Mark 8

[1] In those days, when there was again a great crowd and they had nothing to eat, calling the disciples to himself he says to them,

[2] "I am moved with compassion for the crowd, because already three days they remain with me and have nothing to eat;

[3] and if I send them away fasting to their house, they will faint on the way; and some of them have come from afar."

[4] And his disciples answered him, "From where will anyone be able to fill these with loaves here in a desert?"

[5] And he was asking them, "How many loaves do you have?" And they said, "Seven."

[6] And he charges the crowd to recline on the ground; and taking the seven loaves, having given thanks he broke them and was giving them to his disciples that they might set them before them, and they set them before the crowd.

[7] And they had a few small fish; and having blessed them, he said that these also be set before them.

[8] And they ate and were filled, and they took up the abundance of fragments, seven large baskets.

[9] Now they were about four thousand. And he sent them away.

[10] And immediately, getting into the boat with his disciples, he came into the parts of Dalmanutha.

[11] And the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ came out and began to dispute with him, seeking from him a sign from heaven, tempting him.

[12] And groaning deeply in his spirit he says, "Why does this generation seek a sign? Amen, I say to you, no sign shall be given to this generation."

[13] And leaving them, getting in again he went away to the other side.

[14] And they forgot to take loaves, and except for one loaf they had none with them in the boat.

[15] And he was enjoining them, saying, "See, beware of the leaven of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and the leaven of ⟦herod|Herodes|Herod⟧."

[16] And they were reasoning with one another that they had no loaves.

[17] And knowing it, he says to them, "Why do you reason that you have no loaves? Do you not yet perceive nor understand? Do you have your heart hardened?

[18] Having eyes, do you not see, and having ears, do you not hear? And do you not remember,

[19] when I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many baskets full of fragments you took up?" They say to him, "Twelve."

[20] "When the seven for the four thousand, the fullness of how many large baskets of fragments did you take up?" And they say to him, "Seven."

[21] And he was saying to them, "Do you not yet understand?"

[22] And they come to ⟦bethsaida|Beit Tzaidah|Bethsaida⟧. And they bring to him a blind man and appeal to him that he would touch him.

[23] And laying hold of the hand of the blind man, he brought him out outside the village, and having spit into his eyes, having laid his hands on him, he was asking him, "Do you see anything?"

[24] And looking up he was saying, "I see the men, for as trees I see them walking."

[25] Then again he laid his hands upon his eyes, and he saw clearly and was restored, and he was looking at all things distinctly.

[26] And he sent him away to his house, saying, "Do not even enter into the village."

[27] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ went out, and his disciples, into the villages of Caesarea Philippi; and on the way he was asking his disciples, saying to them, "Who do men say that I am?"

[28] And they told him, saying, "⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the ⟦immerser|Immerser|Baptist⟧; and others, ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧; but others, one of the prophets."

[29] And he himself was asking them, "But you, who do you say that I am?" ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, answering, says to him, "**You are the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧.**"

[30] And he rebuked them, that they should tell no one about him.

[31] And he began to teach them that the Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders and the high priests and the scribes and be killed and after three days rise;

[32] and he was speaking the word openly. And ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, taking him to himself, began to rebuke him.

[33] But he, turning around and seeing his disciples, rebuked ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and says, "Go behind me, Satan, because you do not think the things of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ but the things of men."

[34] And calling the crowd to himself with his disciples, he said to them, "**If anyone wants to follow after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me.**

[35] For whoever wants to save his soul will lose it; but whoever will lose his soul for the sake of me and of the gospel will save it.

[36] For what does it profit a man to gain the whole world and to suffer the loss of his soul?

**[37] For what would a man give as an exchange for his soul?**

[38] For whoever is ashamed of me and of my words in this adulterous and sinful generation, the Son of Man also will be ashamed of him when he comes in the glory of his Father with the holy angels."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v27) — Hebrew name preserved. The Greek names him only in v27; in v17 and v25 it has "he", and so does the verse (v17: WH NA28 and the SBLGNT; Treg RP add ὁ Ἰησοῦς).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v33) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v29) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (vv29, 32, 33) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"John the Baptist" → "John the Immerser"** (v28) — "Immerser" renders *baptistēs*; see Mark 1.
- **"holy angels" → "the holy angels"** (v38) — retained as is; consistent.
- **"And" / "immediately" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — Mark's καί at the start of sentences, καὶ εὐθύς "and immediately" (v10) and his present tenses ("says", v1, 12, 17, 29, 33; "charges", v6; "say", v19, 20; "come … bring … appeal", v22) are kept as they are.
- **"I have compassion on" → "I am moved with compassion for"** (v2) — Greek σπλαγχνίζομαι, from σπλάγχνα, the inward parts as the seat of feeling.
- **"to their own houses" → "to their house"** (v3) — Greek εἰς οἶκον αὐτῶν, singular. "Fasting": Greek νήστεις. "Have come": ἥκασιν (Treg NA28; WH εἰσίν, "are").
- **"satisfy these men with bread here in the wilderness" → "fill these with loaves here in a desert"** (v4) — Greek χορτάσαι ἄρτων ἐπ' ἐρημίας; the Greek has no word "men".
- **"commanded the people to sit down" → "charges the crowd to recline"** (v6) — Greek παραγγέλλει (a present), ἀναπεσεῖν.
- **"the broken meat that was left" → "the abundance of fragments"** (v8) — Greek περισσεύματα κλασμάτων, what was over and above. "Seven large baskets": σπυρίς, a larger basket than the κόφινος of 6:43 and 8:19.
- **"they that had eaten" dropped** (v9) — WH NA28 and the SBLGNT read only "Now they were about four thousand"; Treg RP add οἱ φαγόντες.
- **"to question with him" → "to dispute with him"** (v11) — Greek συζητεῖν.
- **"sighed deeply" → "groaning deeply"** (v12) — Greek ἀναστενάξας, a strengthened form of the verb in 7:34.
- **"Verily" → "Amen"** (v12) — Greek ἀμήν, left untranslated as Mark leaves it.
- **"There shall no sign be given" → "no sign shall be given"** (v12) — Greek εἰ δοθήσεται … σημεῖον, literally "if a sign shall be given to this generation": the Hebrew oath formula, in which "if" with the consequence left unsaid is a sworn "not". The verse gives the meaning.
- **"entering into the ship again" → "getting in again"** (v13) — WH NA28 and the SBLGNT have no "into the boat"; Treg RP add it.
- **"had forgotten to take bread" → "forgot to take loaves"** (v14) — Greek ἄρτους, plural, and then "one loaf" (ἕνα ἄρτον), the same word. So also in v16, 17.
- **"charged" → "was enjoining"** (v15) — Greek διεστέλλετο, as in 5:43; 7:36.
- **"Take heed, beware" → "See, beware"** (v15) — Greek Ὁρᾶτε, βλέπετε ἀπό: two verbs of seeing, "See, look away from".
- **"saying, It is because we have no bread" → "that they had no loaves"** (v16) — Greek ὅτι ἄρτους οὐκ ἔχουσιν, "they have" (WH Treg NA28; RP "saying … we have"). ὅτι can be "that" or "because".
- **"perceive" / "understand" kept** (v17, 21) — Greek νοεῖτε, συνίετε.
- **"how many baskets full of fragments" → "the fullness of how many large baskets of fragments"** (v20) — Greek πόσων σπυρίδων πληρώματα κλασμάτων; v19 has κοφίνους … πλήρεις, "baskets full". The verse follows the reading without "and" at its beginning (WH NA28; the SBLGNT reads ὅτε καί, Treg RP ὅτε δέ). The translation is the same.
- **"How is it that ye do not understand?" → "Do you not yet understand?"** (v21) — Greek Οὔπω συνίετε; (WH NA28; Treg Πῶς, RP Πῶς οὐ).
- **"besought" → "appeal to"** (v22) — Greek παρακαλοῦσιν.
- **"took the blind man by the hand" → "laying hold of the hand of the blind man"** (v23) — Greek ἐπιλαβόμενος.
- **"if he saw ought" → "Do you see anything?"** (v23) — Greek Εἴ τι βλέπεις; a direct question (WH NA28; Treg RP βλέπει, "whether he sees anything"). "Eyes" in v23 is ὄμματα, in v25 ὀφθαλμούς.
- **"I see men as trees, walking" → "I see the men, for as trees I see them walking"** (v24) — Greek Βλέπω τοὺς ἀνθρώπους ὅτι ὡς δένδρα ὁρῶ περιπατοῦντας: two verbs of seeing (βλέπω, ὁράω) and the conjunction ὅτι. "Looking up" (ἀναβλέψας) can also mean "regaining sight".
- **"made him look up: and he was restored, and saw every man clearly" → "he saw clearly and was restored, and he was looking at all things distinctly"** (v25) — Greek διέβλεψεν καὶ ἀπεκατέστη καὶ ἐνέβλεπεν τηλαυγῶς ἅπαντα (WH Treg NA28): three verbs, "he looked through" (saw clearly), "was restored", "was looking at" (an imperfect); τηλαυγῶς is "shining far", clearly even at a distance. "Laid": ἐπέθηκεν (NA28 RP and the SBLGNT; WH Treg ἔθηκεν, "put").
- **"nor tell it to any in the town" dropped** (v26) — WH NA28 and the SBLGNT end the verse with "Do not even enter into the village"; Treg RP add μηδὲ εἴπῃς τινὶ ἐν τῇ κώμῃ.
- **"towns" → "villages"** (v23, 26, 27) — Greek κώμη.
- **"he charged them" → "he rebuked them"** (v30) — Greek ἐπετίμησεν, the same verb as in v32 and v33: here a stern warning.
- **"chief priests" → "high priests"** (v31) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς, the plural of the word for the high priest: the high priest, former high priests and the members of the high-priestly families.
- **"rise again" → "rise"** (v31) — Greek ἀναστῆναι.
- **"he spake that saying openly" → "he was speaking the word openly"** (v32) — Greek παρρησίᾳ, "with openness", without concealment; the word is "boldness" in the letters.
- **"took him" → "taking him to himself"** (v32) — Greek προσλαβόμενος.
- **"Get thee behind me" → "Go behind me"** (v33) — Greek Ὕπαγε ὀπίσω μου; "after me" in v34 is the same ὀπίσω μου.
- **"thou savourest not the things that be of God" → "you do not think the things of *Elohim*"** (v33) — Greek οὐ φρονεῖς τὰ τοῦ θεοῦ.
- **"will come after me" → "wants to follow after me"** (v34) — the verse follows ἀκολουθεῖν, "follow" (Treg NA28 RP); WH and the SBLGNT read ἐλθεῖν, "come". With this reading the verb "follow" stands twice in the verse.
- **"life" → "soul"** (v35) — Greek ψυχή *psychē*, four times in v35–37; KJV has "life" in v35 and "soul" in v36–37 for the one word. "Soul" here is the person's own life and self.
- **"lose his own soul" → "suffer the loss of his soul"** (v36) — Greek ζημιωθῆναι, to suffer loss or damage; "lose" in v35 is another verb (ἀπόλλυμι). The verse follows the infinitives κερδῆσαι … ζημιωθῆναι (WH NA28).
- **"what shall a man give in exchange" → "what would a man give as an exchange"** (v37) — Greek δοῖ (a subjunctive; WH Treg NA28), ἀντάλλαγμα.

### Notes
- **v18** — the words echo Jer 5:21; Ezek 12:2.
- **v19–20** — the question of v18 runs on into v19 in the printed Greek ("do you not remember, when I broke …, how many baskets … you took up?").
- **v33** — "Satan": Greek Σατανᾶ, the Hebrew word שָׂטָן, "adversary".
