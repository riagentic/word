# Mark 11

[1] And when they draw near to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to ⟦bethphage|Beit Pagei|Bethphage⟧ and ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧, at ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧, he sends two of his disciples

[2] and says to them, "Go into the village opposite you, and immediately, entering into it, you will find a colt tied, upon which no one of men has yet sat; loose it and bring it.

[3] And if anyone says to you, 'Why are you doing this?' say, 'The Lord has need of it, and immediately he sends it here again.'"

[4] And they went away and found a colt tied at a door, outside on the street, and they loose it.

[5] And some of those standing there were saying to them, "What are you doing, loosing the colt?"

[6] And they said to them even as ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said; and they let them.

[7] And they bring the colt to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and they throw their garments upon it, and he sat upon it.

[8] And many spread their garments on the way, and others leafy branches, having cut them from the fields.

[9] And those going before and those following were crying out, "**Hosanna**! **Blessed is the one coming in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧!**

[10] Blessed is the coming kingdom of our father David! Hosanna in the highest!"

[11] And he entered into ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, into the temple; and having looked around at all things, the hour being already late, he went out to ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧ with the twelve.

[12] And on the next day, when they had gone out from ⟦bethany|Bethania|Bethany⟧, he was hungry.

[13] And seeing from afar a fig tree having leaves, he came, if perhaps he will find anything on it; and coming to it he found nothing except leaves, for it was not the time of figs.

[14] And answering, he said to it, "May no one eat fruit from you any more forever." And his disciples were hearing.

[15] And they come into ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And entering into the temple, he began to cast out those selling and those buying in the temple, and the tables of the money changers and the seats of those selling the doves he overturned,

[16] and he was not letting anyone carry a vessel through the temple,

[17] and he was teaching and was saying to them, "Is it not written, '**My house shall be called a house of prayer for all the nations**'? But you have made it **a cave of robbers**."

[18] And the high priests and the scribes heard, and they were seeking how they might destroy him; for they feared him, for all the crowd was astonished at his teaching.

[19] And whenever it became late, they were going out outside the city.

[20] And going along early, they saw the fig tree withered from the roots.

[21] And remembering, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ says to him, "Rabbi, see, the fig tree which you cursed has withered."

[22] And answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "Have faith of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[23] Amen, I say to you that whoever says to this mountain, 'Be taken up and be cast into the sea,' and does not doubt in his heart but believes that what he speaks comes to pass, it will be for him.

[24] Because of this I say to you, all things whatever you pray and ask for, believe that you received them, and it will be for you.

[25] And whenever you stand praying, forgive, if you have anything against anyone, that your Father also, who is in the heavens, may forgive you your trespasses."

[27] And they come again into ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And as he is walking in the temple, the high priests and the scribes and the elders come to him,

[28] and they were saying to him, "By what authority are you doing these things? Or who gave you this authority, that you should do these things?"

[29] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "I will ask you one word, and answer me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things:

[30] the ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ of ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ — was it from heaven or from men? Answer me."

[31] And they were reasoning among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' he will say, 'Why then did you not believe him?'

[32] But shall we say, 'From men'?" — they feared the crowd, for all held ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, that he really was a prophet.

[33] And answering ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, they say, "We do not know." And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v6, 7, 22, 29, 33) — Hebrew name preserved. The Greek does not name him in v1 (nor in v11, 14, 15, where RP adds the name); there it has "he", and so does the verse.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v22) — divine name retained.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v21) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"baptism" → "immersion"** (v30) — Greek *baptisma*; consistent rendering.
- **"And" / "immediately" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — Mark's καί at the start of sentences, εὐθύς "immediately" (v2, 3) and his present tenses ("draw near … sends … says", v1–2; "loose", v4; "bring … throw", v7; "come", v15, 27; "says", v21, 22, 33; "say", v33) are kept as they are.
- **"over against you … as soon as ye be entered into it … whereon never man sat; loose him, and bring him" → "opposite you … immediately, entering into it … upon which no one of men has yet sat; loose it and bring it"** (v2) — Greek κατέναντι; εὐθὺς εἰσπορευόμενοι; οὐδεὶς οὔπω ἀνθρώπων ἐκάθισεν (WH NA28); λύσατε, as 1:7. πῶλος is the young of a horse or a donkey.
- **"say ye that the Lord hath need of him; and straightway he will send him hither" → "say, 'The Lord has need of it, and immediately he sends it here again'"** (v3) — Greek Ὁ κύριος αὐτοῦ χρείαν ἔχει: "the Lord has need of it"; the words can also be taken "its lord (owner) has need". ἀποστέλλει πάλιν, "sends again / back", a present tense (WH Treg NA28; RP has no πάλιν: "he sends it here", said of the man who is asked). With πάλιν the words are a promise to send the colt back, and belong to what the disciples are to say. The verse follows the reading without ὅτι after "say" (WH Treg NA28; RP and the SBLGNT have it); the translation is the same.
- **"by the door without in a place where two ways met" → "at a door, outside on the street"** (v4) — Greek πρὸς θύραν ἔξω ἐπὶ τοῦ ἀμφόδου; ἄμφοδον is a street or a block of houses with streets around it.
- **"even as Jesus had commanded: and they let them go" → "even as Yeshua said; and they let them"** (v6) — Greek εἶπεν (WH Treg NA28); ἀφῆκαν αὐτούς, the verb of 1:34; 5:19, 37.
- **"cast their garments on him" → "throw their garments upon it"** (v7) — Greek ἐπιβάλλουσιν, a present; ἱμάτια as 5:27–30.
- **"others cut down branches off the trees, and strawed them in the way" → "others leafy branches, having cut them from the fields"** (v8) — Greek ἄλλοι δὲ στιβάδας κόψαντες ἐκ τῶν ἀγρῶν (WH Treg NA28); στιβάς is a layer of leaves, reeds or straw to lie on. The verb "spread" is said once.
- **"cried, saying, Hosanna; Blessed is he that cometh" → "were crying out, Hosanna! Blessed is the one coming"** (v9) — Greek ἔκραζον, imperfect; "saying" (RP) is not in the critical text. Ὡσαννά is Hebrew *hoshia-na*, "save, please" (Ps 118:25), left untranslated as Mark leaves it. ἐν ὀνόματι κυρίου: "in the name of the Lord", in Ps 118:26 the name *Yahweh*.
- **"Blessed be the kingdom of our father David, that cometh in the name of the Lord" → "Blessed is the coming kingdom of our father David"** (v10) — "in the name of the Lord" here (RP) is not in the critical text. "In the highest": ἐν τοῖς ὑψίστοις, plural, "in the highest places".
- **"and into the temple … and now the eventide was come" → "into the temple … the hour being already late"** (v11) — Greek εἰς Ἱεροσόλυμα εἰς τὸ ἱερόν; ὀψίας ἤδη οὔσης τῆς ὥρας (Treg NA28 RP; WH and the SBLGNT ὀψέ, the same sense). "Temple" is ἱερόν, the whole temple precinct, throughout the chapter.
- **"if haply he might find any thing thereon" → "if perhaps he will find anything on it"** (v13) — Greek εἰ ἄρα τι εὑρήσει, a future.
- **"for the time of figs was not yet" → "for it was not the time of figs"** (v13) — Greek ὁ γὰρ καιρὸς οὐκ ἦν σύκων.
- **"No man eat fruit of thee hereafter for ever" → "May no one eat fruit from you any more forever"** (v14) — Greek φάγοι, a wish; εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα, literally "unto the age". "Answering": ἀποκριθείς, although no one has spoken. "Were hearing": ἤκουον, imperfect.
- **"overthrew … them that sold doves" → "overturned … those selling the doves"** (v15) — Greek κατέστρεψεν; τὰς περιστεράς with the article. "Cast out" is ἐκβάλλειν, the verb used of demons.
- **"would not suffer that any man should carry any vessel" → "was not letting anyone carry a vessel"** (v16) — Greek οὐκ ἤφιεν (as 1:34); σκεῦος is a vessel or any object carried.
- **"My house shall be called of all nations the house of prayer" → "My house shall be called a house of prayer for all the nations"** (v17) — Greek οἶκος προσευχῆς κληθήσεται πᾶσιν τοῖς ἔθνεσιν (Isa 56:7).
- **"den of thieves" → "cave of robbers"** (v17) — Greek σπήλαιον λῃστῶν (Jer 7:11): σπήλαιον is a cave; λῃστής is a robber or bandit who uses violence (Josephus uses the word for armed rebels), not a thief (κλέπτης). "You have made": πεποιήκατε, a perfect.
- **"scribes and chief priests" → "high priests and the scribes"** (v18, 27) — Greek ἀρχιερεῖς, as 8:31; the order of WH Treg NA28.
- **"sought" → "were seeking"; "people" → "crowd"; "doctrine" → "teaching"** (v18) — Greek ἐζήτουν, ὄχλος, διδαχή.
- **"when even was come, he went out of the city" → "whenever it became late, they were going out outside the city"** (v19) — Greek ὅταν ὀψὲ ἐγένετο, ἐξεπορεύοντο (WH Treg NA28; RP "when … he was going out"): ὅταν is "whenever" (each evening); it can also be a plain "when".
- **"in the morning, as they passed by" → "going along early"** (v20) — Greek παραπορευόμενοι πρωΐ, as 1:35; 2:23.
- **"dried up" / "withered away" → "withered"** (v20, 21) — Greek ξηραίνω both times, as 3:1; 4:6.
- **"Master, behold" → "Rabbi, see"** (v21) — Greek Ῥαββί, ἴδε.
- **"Have faith in God" → "Have faith of *Elohim*"** (v22) — Greek Ἔχετε πίστιν θεοῦ, a plain genitive, kept as it stands (as "faith of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*", Rom 3:22; Gal 2:16). It is most naturally "faith in God"; it can also be "the faith(fulness) that God has" or "faith that comes from God".
- **"Verily" → "Amen"** (v23) — Greek ἀμήν; "For" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28.
- **"Be thou removed … shall believe that those things which he saith shall come to pass; he shall have whatsoever he saith" → "Be taken up … believes that what he speaks comes to pass, it will be for him"** (v23) — Greek Ἄρθητι; πιστεύῃ ὅτι ὃ λαλεῖ γίνεται, present tenses; ἔσται αὐτῷ, literally "it will be to him" (WH Treg NA28; "whatever he says" at the end is RP). "Doubt": διακριθῇ, as Jas 1:6.
- **"What things soever ye desire, when ye pray, believe that ye receive them, and ye shall have them" → "all things whatever you pray and ask for, believe that you received them, and it will be for you"** (v24) — Greek πάντα ὅσα προσεύχεσθε καὶ αἰτεῖσθε, two verbs; ἐλάβετε, a past tense (WH Treg NA28; RP λαμβάνετε, "you receive"); ἔσται ὑμῖν.
- **"when ye stand praying" → "whenever you stand praying"** (v25) — Greek ὅταν στήκετε; στήκω is "stand" (in the letters "stand fast"). "In the heavens": ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς, plural. "Trespasses": παραπτώματα.
- **"as he was walking" → "as he is walking"** (v27) — Greek περιπατοῦντος αὐτοῦ, with the present "come".
- **"By what authority" kept** (v28, 29, 33) — Greek ἐν ποίᾳ ἐξουσίᾳ, literally "in what kind of authority".
- **"I will also ask of you one question" → "I will ask you one word"** (v29) — Greek ἕνα λόγον; "also I" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"of men" → "from men"** (v30, 32) — Greek ἐξ ἀνθρώπων, as ἐξ οὐρανοῦ "from heaven".
- **"reasoned with themselves" → "were reasoning among themselves"** (v31) — Greek διελογίζοντο πρὸς ἑαυτούς. Editions: the printed SBLGNT (Holmes) adds Τί εἴπωμεν; "What should we say?" before "If we say"; WH Treg NA28 RP do not have it — the verse follows the majority.
- **"But if we shall say, Of men; they feared the people: for all men counted John, that he was a prophet indeed" → "But shall we say, 'From men'? — they feared the crowd, for all held John, that he really was a prophet"** (v32) — Greek ἀλλὰ εἴπωμεν· Ἐξ ἀνθρώπων; a question in the printed text; the Greek has no "if". The sentence breaks off and the narrator finishes it. ὄχλον (WH NA28; Treg RP λαόν, "people"); ὄντως, "really", can go with "held" or with "was a prophet".
- **"We cannot tell … Neither do I tell you" → "We do not know … Neither do I tell you"** (v33) — Greek Οὐκ οἴδαμεν; λέγω, a present.

### Notes
- **v9-10** echoes Ps 118:25-26; *Hoshia-na* = "save now."
- **v17** quotes Isa 56:7 + Jer 7:11.
- **v26** — not in the text: WH, Treg and NA28 do not have it; RP adds "But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father who is in the heavens forgive your trespasses."
