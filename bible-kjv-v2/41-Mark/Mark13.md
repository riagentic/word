# Mark 13

[1] And as he was going out of the temple, one of his disciples says to him, "Teacher, see what manner of stones and what manner of buildings!"

[2] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to him, "Do you see these great buildings? There shall by no means be left here stone upon stone which shall not be torn down."

[3] And as he was sitting on ⟦mount-of-olives|Har HaZeitim|the Mount of Olives⟧ opposite the temple, ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ and ⟦andrew|Andreas|Andrew⟧ were asking him privately,

[4] "Tell us, when will these things be, and what is the sign when all these things are about to be consummated?"

[5] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ began to say to them, "Look, lest anyone deceive you.

[6] Many will come upon my name, saying, 'I am,' and they will deceive many.

[7] But when you hear of wars and reports of wars, do not be alarmed; it must come to pass, but the end is not yet.

[8] For nation will arise against nation and kingdom against kingdom; there will be earthquakes in places, there will be famines; these things are a beginning of birth pains.

[9] But you, look to yourselves: they will deliver you up to ⟦sanhedrin|sanhedrins|councils⟧, and in synagogues you will be beaten, and before governors and kings you will stand for my sake, for a testimony to them.

[10] And unto all the nations the gospel must first be proclaimed.

[11] And when they lead you, delivering you up, do not be anxious beforehand about what you should speak, but whatever is given to you in that hour, this speak; for it is not you who are speaking, but the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧.

[12] And brother will deliver up brother to death, and a father a child, and children will rise up against parents and put them to death;

[13] and you will be hated by all because of my name. But the one who has endured to the end, this one will be saved.

[14] But when you see the abomination of desolation standing where he must not (let the one reading perceive), then let those in ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ flee into the mountains,

[15] and let the one on the housetop not go down nor enter to take anything out of his house,

[16] and let the one in the field not turn back to take his garment.

[17] But woe to those who are with child and to those who are nursing in those days!

[18] And pray that it may not come to pass in winter;

[19] for those days will be affliction, such as there has not been the like from the beginning of the creation which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ created until now, and by no means shall be.

[20] And if the Lord had not cut short the days, no flesh would have been saved; but for the sake of the elect whom he chose, he cut short the days.

[21] And then if anyone says to you, 'See, here is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧!' 'See, there!' — do not believe;

[22] for ⟦mashiach|false mashiachs|false christs⟧ and false prophets will arise and will give signs and wonders, to lead astray, if possible, the elect.

[23] But you, look; I have told you all things beforehand.

[24] But in those days, after that affliction, the sun will be darkened,
and the moon will not give its light,
[25] and the stars will be falling out of heaven,
and the powers that are in the heavens will be shaken.

[26] And then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with much power and glory.

[27] And then he will send the angels and will gather together his elect out of the four winds, from the end of earth to the end of heaven.

[28] But from the fig tree learn the parable: when its branch has already become tender and puts forth the leaves, you know that the summer is near;

[29] so also you, when you see these things coming to pass, know that it is near, at the doors.

[30] Amen, I say to you that this generation shall by no means pass away until all these things come to pass.

[31] The heaven and the earth will pass away, but my words shall by no means pass away.

[32] But about that day or the hour **no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, except the Father**.

[33] Look, keep watch; for you do not know when the time is.

[34] As a man away from home, having left his house and given the authority to his slaves, to each his work — and to the doorkeeper he gave commandment to be awake.

[35] Be awake therefore, for you do not know when the lord of the house comes, whether late or at midnight or at cockcrow or early,

[36] lest coming suddenly he find you sleeping.

[37] And what I say to you I say to all: **Be awake.**"

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v3) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v2, 5) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v19) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"; "false Christs" → "false mashiachs"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew Anointed; ψευδόχριστοι *pseudochristoi* is the same word with ψευδο- "false", so the same rendering stands in both verses.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v3) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v11) — Hebrew divine Spirit. Greek τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον, with the article.
- **"saith" → "says"** (v1) — Greek λέγει, Mark's present tense in narrative, kept as a present.
- **"Master, see what manner of stones and what buildings are here" → "Teacher, see what manner of stones and what manner of buildings"** (v1) — Greek διδάσκαλε; ἴδε; ποταποί … ποταπαί, twice. "temple" here and in v3 is ἱερόν, the whole precinct (ναός, the sanctuary itself, stands in 14:58; 15:29, 38).
- **"there shall not be left one stone upon another, that shall not be thrown down" → "There shall by no means be left here stone upon stone which shall not be torn down"** (v2) — Greek οὐ μὴ ἀφεθῇ ὧδε λίθος ἐπὶ λίθον ὃς οὐ μὴ καταλυθῇ: the strong negation οὐ μή stands twice (also v19, 30, 31; "by no means"); ὧδε "here" (WH Treg NA28); καταλύω is "tear down", the verb of 14:58; 15:29.
- **"asked him privately" → "were asking him privately"** (v3) — Greek ἐπηρώτα, an imperfect (singular, with four subjects). εἰς τὸ ὄρος is literally "unto / at the mount".
- **"what shall be the sign when all these things shall be fulfilled" → "what is the sign when all these things are about to be consummated"** (v4) — Greek τί τὸ σημεῖον, without a verb; ὅταν μέλλῃ … συντελεῖσθαι, "are about to be brought to completion" (the verb of Heb 8:8).
- **"Take heed lest any man deceive you" → "Look, lest anyone deceive you"** (v5) — Greek βλέπετε, "look, see", four times in the chapter (v5, 9, 23, 33): be on the watch. πλανάω is "deceive, lead astray" (v5, 6); in v22 ἀποπλανᾶν, "lead astray, away".
- **"shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ" → "will come upon my name, saying, 'I am'"** (v6) — Greek ἐπὶ τῷ ὀνόματί μου, "upon my name" (as 9:37, 39): resting on it, claiming it; "in my name" is ἐν (16:17). λέγοντες ὅτι Ἐγώ εἰμι, "I am" (the words of 6:50, "it is I", and 14:62): "I am he", the one expected. The KJV adds "Christ" from Matt 24:5. "For" (Treg RP γάρ) is not in WH NA28.
- **"rumours of wars, be ye not troubled: for such things must needs be; but the end shall not be yet" → "reports of wars, do not be alarmed; it must come to pass, but the end is not yet"** (v7) — Greek ἀκοὰς πολέμων (ἀκοή as 1:28); μὴ θροεῖσθε (as 2 Thess 2:2); δεῖ γενέσθαι, without "for" (WH NA28); οὔπω τὸ τέλος has no verb.
- **"nation shall rise against nation … in divers places … and troubles: these are the beginnings of sorrows" → "nation will arise against nation … in places … these things are a beginning of birth pains"** (v8) — Greek ἐγερθήσεται, a passive form, "will be raised, will arise"; κατὰ τόπους, "place by place"; "and troubles" (RP) is not in the critical text; ἀρχὴ ὠδίνων, singular and without the article, "a beginning of birth pains".
- **"take heed to yourselves: for they shall deliver you up to councils; and in the synagogues ye shall be beaten: and ye shall be brought before rulers and kings" → "look to yourselves: they will deliver you up to councils, and in synagogues you will be beaten, and before governors and kings you will stand"** (v9) — Greek παραδώσουσιν, "hand over, deliver up", the verb of 1:14; 3:19 and of v11, 12; συνέδρια, the plural of the word "Sanhedrin" (14:55): local councils that sat as courts; εἰς συναγωγὰς δαρήσεσθε, literally "into synagogues you will be beaten". The Greek has no stop: "to councils and to synagogues; you will be beaten" is also possible. ἡγεμόνων, "governors"; σταθήσεσθε, "you will be made to stand, will stand". "For" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28.
- **"for a testimony against them" → "for a testimony to them"** (v9) — Greek εἰς μαρτύριον αὐτοῖς, as 1:44; 6:11.
- **"the gospel must first be published among all nations" → "unto all the nations the gospel must first be proclaimed"** (v10) — Greek εἰς πάντα τὰ ἔθνη, put first; κηρυχθῆναι.
- **"when they shall lead you, and deliver you up, take no thought beforehand what ye shall speak, neither do ye premeditate" → "when they lead you, delivering you up, do not be anxious beforehand about what you should speak"** (v11) — Greek ἄγωσιν ὑμᾶς παραδιδόντες; μὴ προμεριμνᾶτε, from μεριμνάω "be anxious"; "neither do ye premeditate" (RP) is not in the critical text. λαλέω "speak" stands four times.
- **"the brother shall betray the brother to death, and the father the son … shall cause them to be put to death" → "brother will deliver up brother to death, and a father a child … put them to death"** (v12) — Greek παραδώσει; τέκνον, "child"; no articles; θανατώσουσιν, "will put to death".
- **"for my name's sake: but he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved" → "because of my name. But the one who has endured to the end, this one will be saved"** (v13) — Greek διὰ τὸ ὄνομά μου; ὁ ὑπομείνας, a past participle; οὗτος.
- **"the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing where it ought not, (let him that readeth understand,)" → "the abomination of desolation standing where he must not (let the one reading perceive)"** (v14) — "spoken of by Daniel the prophet" (RP) is not in the critical text. ἑστηκότα (WH Treg NA28) is a masculine participle beside the neuter noun βδέλυγμα: the abomination is spoken of as a person, hence "he". ὅπου οὐ δεῖ, "where it is not right, where he must not" (δεῖ as v7, 10). νοείτω, "perceive", as 7:18; 8:17. The brackets are the editors' punctuation.
- **"And let him that is on the housetop not go down into the house, neither enter therein" → "and let the one on the housetop not go down nor enter"** (v15) — "into the house" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28. Editions: ὁ WH (and the printed SBLGNT) ] + δὲ Treg RP NA28 — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides: "and (but) let the one".
- **"let him that is in the field not turn back again for to take up his garment" → "let the one in the field not turn back to take his garment"** (v16) — Greek ὁ εἰς τὸν ἀγρόν, literally "the one (gone) into the field"; εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω, "to the things behind"; ἱμάτιον, "garment".
- **"them that are with child, and to them that give suck" → "those who are with child and to those who are nursing"** (v17) — Greek ταῖς ἐν γαστρὶ ἐχούσαις, literally "those having in the womb".
- **"pray ye that your flight be not in the winter" → "pray that it may not come to pass in winter"** (v18) — "your flight" (RP) is not in the critical text; the verb has no expressed subject.
- **"in those days shall be affliction, such as was not from the beginning of the creation … neither shall be" → "those days will be affliction, such as there has not been the like from the beginning of the creation … and by no means shall be"** (v19) — Greek ἔσονται αἱ ἡμέραι ἐκεῖναι θλῖψις, "those days will be affliction" (θλῖψις also v24); οἵα οὐ γέγονεν τοιαύτη, "such as has not come to be, of such a kind"; οὐ μὴ γένηται.
- **"except that the Lord had shortened those days, no flesh should be saved: but for the elect's sake, whom he hath chosen, he hath shortened" → "if the Lord had not cut short the days, no flesh would have been saved; but for the sake of the elect whom he chose, he cut short the days"** (v20) — Greek ἐκολόβωσεν, "cut short, docked", twice, a past tense; κύριος without the article; οὐκ ἂν ἐσώθη πᾶσα σάρξ, literally "all flesh would not have been saved", a Hebrew way of saying "no flesh"; τοὺς ἐκλεκτοὺς οὓς ἐξελέξατο, noun and verb of one root ("the chosen whom he chose").
- **"Lo, here is Christ; or, lo, he is there; believe him not" → "'See, here is the Mashiach!' 'See, there!' — do not believe"** (v21) — Greek Ἴδε, twice; "or" (Treg RP ἤ) is not in WH NA28; ἴδε ἐκεῖ has no verb, and μὴ πιστεύετε has no object.
- **"shall rise, and shall shew signs and wonders, to seduce, if it were possible, even the elect" → "will arise and will give signs and wonders, to lead astray, if possible, the elect"** (v22) — Greek ἐγερθήσονται (as v8); δώσουσιν, "will give"; πρὸς τὸ ἀποπλανᾶν; "even" (Treg RP καί) is not in WH NA28.
- **"But take ye heed: behold, I have foretold you all things" → "But you, look; I have told you all things beforehand"** (v23) — Greek ὑμεῖς δὲ βλέπετε; "behold" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"tribulation" → "affliction"** (v24) — Greek θλῖψις, as v19 and 4:17.
- **"the stars of heaven shall fall" → "the stars will be falling out of heaven"; "the powers that are in heaven" → "the powers that are in the heavens"** (v25) — Greek ἔσονται ἐκ τοῦ οὐρανοῦ πίπτοντες (WH Treg NA28); ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς, plural.
- **"in the clouds with great power" → "in clouds with much power"** (v26) — Greek ἐν νεφέλαις, without the article; μετὰ δυνάμεως πολλῆς.
- **"shall he send his angels, and shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from the uttermost part of the earth to the uttermost part of heaven" → "he will send the angels and will gather together his elect out of the four winds, from the end of earth to the end of heaven"** (v27) — "his" with "angels" (RP) is not in the critical text. Editions: ἐκλεκτοὺς Treg (and the printed SBLGNT), "the elect" ] + αὐτοῦ WH NA28 RP, "his elect" — the verse follows the majority. ἀπ' ἄκρου γῆς ἕως ἄκρου οὐρανοῦ, singular and without articles: "from the tip of earth to the tip of heaven".
- **"Now learn a parable of the fig tree; When her branch is yet tender, and putteth forth leaves" → "But from the fig tree learn the parable: when its branch has already become tender and puts forth the leaves"** (v28) — Greek ἀπὸ δὲ τῆς συκῆς μάθετε τὴν παραβολήν; ἤδη, "already"; ἁπαλὸς γένηται. ἐκφύῃ, "puts forth"; with another accent (ἐκφυῇ) "and the leaves sprout".
- **"come to pass, know that it is nigh, even at the doors" → "coming to pass, know that it is near, at the doors"** (v29) — Greek γινώσκετε, which is "you know" (so v28) or "know!"; ἐγγύς ἐστιν has no expressed subject: "it is near" or "he is near".
- **"Verily I say unto you" → "Amen, I say to you"** (v30) — Greek ἀμήν, left untranslated as Mark leaves it.
- **"shall not pass" → "shall by no means pass away"** (v30, 31) — Greek οὐ μὴ παρέλθῃ; οὐ μὴ παρελεύσονται (NA28 RP; WH Treg have only οὐ, "will not pass away" — 2–2, NA28 decides). ὁ οὐρανὸς καὶ ἡ γῆ, both with the article.
- **"of that day and that hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels which are in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father" → "about that day or the hour no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, except the Father"** (v32) — Greek περὶ δὲ τῆς ἡμέρας ἐκείνης ἢ τῆς ὥρας; εἰ μὴ ὁ πατήρ, "except the Father".
- **"Take ye heed, watch and pray" → "Look, keep watch"** (v33) — Greek βλέπετε ἀγρυπνεῖτε; ἀγρυπνέω is "be sleepless, keep watch", a different verb from γρηγορέω "be awake" (v34, 35, 37). "and pray" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28. πότε ὁ καιρός ἐστιν, "when the time is".
- **"For the Son of man is as a man taking a far journey, who left his house, and gave authority to his servants, and to every man his work, and commanded the porter to watch" → "As a man away from home, having left his house and given the authority to his slaves, to each his work — and to the doorkeeper he gave commandment to be awake"** (v34) — Greek ὡς ἄνθρωπος ἀπόδημος (as ἀπεδήμησεν, 12:1): the comparison has no main clause and is left unfinished; "the Son of man is" is the KJV's addition. δούλοις, "slaves"; τὴν ἐξουσίαν, "the authority"; ἐνετείλατο ἵνα γρηγορῇ.
- **"Watch ye therefore … the master of the house cometh, at even, or at midnight, or at the cockcrowing, or in the morning" → "Be awake therefore … the lord of the house comes, whether late or at midnight or at cockcrow or early"** (v35) — Greek γρηγορεῖτε, "be awake" (as 1 Thess 5:6); ὁ κύριος τῆς οἰκίας; ὀψέ "late", πρωΐ "early" (as 1:35): the four watches of the night.
- **"Watch" → "Be awake"** (v37) — Greek γρηγορεῖτε.

### Notes
- **v14** refers to Dan 9:27; 11:31; 12:11.
- **v24-25** echoes Isa 13:10; 34:4.
- **v26** echoes Dan 7:13.
