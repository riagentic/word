# Mark 5

[1] And they came to the other side of the sea, into the country of the Gerasenes.

[2] And when he had come out of the boat, immediately there met him out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit,

[3] who had his dwelling in the graves; and no one was able to bind him any longer, not even with a chain,

[4] because he had often been bound with shackles and chains, and the chains had been torn apart by him and the shackles broken in pieces, and no one had strength to tame him.

[5] And always, night and day, in the graves and in the mountains, he was crying out and cutting himself with stones.

[6] And seeing ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ from afar, he ran and bowed down to him,

[7] and crying out with a loud voice he says, "What have I to do with you, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, Son of the Most High ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? I adjure you by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not torment me."

[8] For he was saying to him, "Come out, unclean spirit, from the man."

[9] And he was asking him, "What is your name?" And he says to him, "Legion is my name, for we are many."

[10] And he was appealing to him much that he would not send them out of the country.

[11] Now there was there by the mountain a great herd of pigs feeding;

[12] and they appealed to him, saying, "Send us into the pigs, that we may enter into them."

[13] And he permitted them. And the unclean spirits, coming out, entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea, about two thousand, and they were drowning in the sea.

[14] And those feeding them fled and reported it to the city and to the farms; and they came to see what it was that had happened.

[15] And they come to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, and they behold the demonized man sitting, clothed and of sound mind, the one who had had the legion, and they were afraid.

[16] And those who had seen it related to them how it had happened to the demonized man, and about the pigs.

[17] And they began to appeal to him to depart from their borders.

[18] And as he was getting into the boat, the man who had been demonized was appealing to him that he might be with him.

[19] And he did not let him, but says to him, "Go to your house, to your own, and report to them how much the Lord has done for you and had mercy on you."

[20] And he went away and began to proclaim in the Decapolis how much ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had done for him, and all were marveling.

[21] And when ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ had crossed over in the boat again to the other side, a great crowd was gathered to him, and he was by the sea.

[22] And there comes one of the synagogue rulers, ⟦jairus|Yair|Jairus⟧ by name, and seeing him he falls at his feet

[23] and appeals to him much, saying, "My little daughter is at the point of death — that you would come and lay your hands on her, that she may be saved and live."

[24] And he went away with him. And a great crowd was following him, and they were pressing upon him.

[25] And a woman who had been in a flow of blood twelve years,

[26] and had suffered many things from many physicians, and had spent all that she had, and had been helped in nothing but rather had become worse,

[27] having heard about ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, came in the crowd behind and touched his garment.

[28] For she was saying, "If I touch even his garments, I will be saved."

[29] And immediately the fountain of her blood was dried up, and she knew in her body that she had been healed from the scourge.

[30] And immediately ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, recognizing in himself that the power from him had gone out, turned around in the crowd and was saying, "Who touched my garments?"

[31] And his disciples were saying to him, "You see the crowd pressing upon you, and you say, 'Who touched me?'"

[32] And he was looking around to see her who had done this.

[33] But the woman, fearing and trembling, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before him and told him all the truth.

[34] But he said to her, "Daughter, your faith has saved you; go in peace, and be healthy from your scourge."

[35] While he was still speaking, they come from the synagogue ruler's house, saying, "Your daughter has died; why do you still bother the Teacher?"

[36] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, overhearing the word being spoken, says to the synagogue ruler, "**Do not fear, only believe.**"

[37] And he did not let anyone follow along with him except ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ and ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ the brother of ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧.

[38] And they come into the house of the synagogue ruler, and he beholds a commotion, and people weeping and wailing much,

[39] and having entered he says to them, "Why are you making a commotion and weeping? The young child has not died but sleeps."

[40] And they were laughing at him. But he, having cast them all out, takes along the father of the young child and the mother and those with him, and goes in where the young child was.

[41] And taking hold of the hand of the young child, he says to her, "**⟦talitha-kumi|Talitha kum|Talitha cumi⟧**," which is, being translated, "Girl, I say to you, arise."

[42] And immediately the girl rose and was walking, for she was twelve years old. And they were immediately amazed with great amazement.

[43] And he enjoined them much that no one should know this, and said that something be given her to eat.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v37) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v6, 7, 15, 20, 21, 27, 30, 36) — Hebrew name preserved. The name stands only where the Greek has Ἰησοῦς; in v2, 9, 17, 38 the Greek has "he" / "him" and so does the verse.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7) — divine name retained.
- **"most high God" → "the Most High *Elohim*"** (v7) — Greek τοῦ Θεοῦ τοῦ ὑψίστου *tou Theou tou hypsistou* = Hebrew אֵל עֶלְיוֹן *El Elyon*.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v37) — Aramaic name preserved.
- **"And" / "immediately" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — Mark begins almost every sentence with καί "And", says καὶ εὐθύς "and immediately" (v2, 29, 30, 42 twice), and tells the story in the present tense ("he says", v7, 9, 19, 36, 39, 41; "they come", v15, 35, 38; "comes … falls … appeals", v22–23; "takes … goes in", v40). The verse keeps all three as they are.
- **"with an unclean spirit" kept** (v2) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι ἀκαθάρτῳ, literally "in an unclean spirit".
- **"tombs" → "tombs" / "graves"** (v2 / v3, 5) — two Greek words: μνημεῖον (v2) and μνῆμα (v3, 5).
- **"worshipped him" → "bowed down to him"** (v6) — Greek προσεκύνησεν *prosekynēsen*, to bow down before someone. The verse follows αὐτῷ (Treg NA28 RP); WH and the SBLGNT read αὐτόν. The translation is the same.
- **"What have I to do with thee" kept** (v7) — Greek Τί ἐμοὶ καὶ σοί, literally "What to me and to you?", a Hebrew idiom (Judg 11:12; 1 Kgs 17:18).
- **"besought" → "appealed to"** (v10, 12, 17, 18, 23) — Greek παρακαλέω *parakaleō*, one word throughout the chapter for the request of the spirits, of the people, of the healed man and of Yair.
- **"choked" → "were drowning"** (v13) — Greek ἐπνίγοντο, an imperfect: "were being choked", that is, drowning.
- **"in the country" → "to the farms"** (v14) — Greek εἰς τοὺς ἀγρούς, literally "into the fields": the farms and hamlets outside the city.
- **"possessed with the devil" → "demonized"** (v15, 16, 18) — Greek δαιμονιζόμενος / δαιμονισθείς, one word, "demonized"; the Greek has no word "possessed". The word "demons" (*daimonia*) does not occur in this chapter: v2, 8, 13 have "unclean spirit(s)".
- **"in his right mind" → "of sound mind"** (v15) — Greek σωφρονοῦντα *sōphronounta*, the verb of σώφρων ("sober-minded" in the letters). Here it describes the man's restored sanity, so "of sound mind".
- **"suffer" → "let"** (v19, 37) — Greek ἀφίημι; "permitted" in v13 is another word (ἐπιτρέπω).
- **"Go home to thy friends" → "Go to your house, to your own"** (v19) — Greek εἰς τὸν οἶκόν σου πρὸς τοὺς σούς, "to your own (people)"; the Greek has no word "friends".
- **"and hath had compassion on thee" → "and had mercy on you"** (v19) — Greek καὶ ἠλέησέν σε, the verb of ἔλεος "mercy"; "has done" is a perfect (πεποίηκεν), "had mercy" an aorist.
- **"lieth at the point of death" → "is at the point of death"** (v23) — Greek ἐσχάτως ἔχει, literally "has it at the last".
- **"I pray thee, come and lay thy hands on her" → "that you would come and lay your hands on her"** (v23) — Greek ἵνα ἐλθὼν ἐπιθῇς, a broken sentence: a clause with "that" follows without a verb of asking. The Greek has "the hands", without "your".
- **"healed" / "made whole" → "saved"** (v23, 28, 34) — Greek σῴζω *sōzō*, "save", the same verb as for salvation; it also means to rescue from illness or death. "Healed" in v29 is another verb (ἴαται), and "healthy" in v34 another word (ὑγιής).
- **"thronged" → "were pressing upon"** (v24, 31) — Greek συνθλίβω, to press together from all sides.
- **"an issue of blood" → "a flow of blood"** (v25) — Greek ἐν ῥύσει αἵματος, literally "in a flow of blood". v25–27 are one sentence in Greek, a chain of seven participles before the verb "touched".
- **"plague" → "scourge"** (v29, 34) — Greek μάστιξ *mastix*, a whip; used of a tormenting disease.
- **"that virtue had gone out of him" → "that the power from him had gone out"** (v30) — Greek τὴν ἐξ αὐτοῦ δύναμιν ἐξελθοῦσαν, "the power from him having gone out".
- **"go in peace" kept** (v34) — Greek ὕπαγε εἰς εἰρήνην, literally "go into peace" (Hebrew לְשָׁלוֹם).
- **"from the ruler of the synagogue's house" → "from the synagogue ruler's house"** (v35) — Greek ἀπὸ τοῦ ἀρχισυναγώγου, literally "from the synagogue ruler"; "house" is supplied, because English cannot say it otherwise (the man himself is standing with Yeshua).
- **"As soon as Jesus heard" → "overhearing"** (v36) — Greek παρακούσας (WH Treg NA28; RP εὐθέως ἀκούσας, "immediately hearing"). The word means either "overhearing" or "paying no heed to" (so in Matt 18:17); both fit here.
- **"damsel" → "young child" / "girl"** (v39–41 / v41, 42) — two Greek words: παιδίον *paidion* (v39, 40, 41) and κοράσιον *korasion* (v41, 42), a diminutive in form.
- **"Talitha cumi" → "Talitha kum"** (v41) — the Greek prints the Aramaic as Ταλιθα κουμ (WH Treg NA28; RP κοῦμι). *Talitha* = "girl", *kum* = "arise".
- **"arise" / "arose" → "arise" / "rose"** (v41, 42) — two Greek verbs: ἔγειρε (ἐγείρω, elsewhere "raise") and ἀνέστη (ἀνίστημι "rise").
- **"astonished with a great astonishment" → "amazed with great amazement"** (v42) — Greek ἐξέστησαν … ἐκστάσει μεγάλῃ, verb and noun of one root (*ekstasis*, "standing outside oneself").
- **"charged them straitly" → "enjoined them much"** (v43) — Greek διεστείλατο αὐτοῖς πολλά.

### Notes
- **v41** preserves Aramaic; the Greek then translates it: "which is, being translated".
- **v34, 36** — the Greek tense changes: "he said" (v34), "says" (v36).
