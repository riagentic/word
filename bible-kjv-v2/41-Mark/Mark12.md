# Mark 12

[1] And he began to speak to them in parables: "A man planted a vineyard, and put a fence around it and dug a vat for the winepress and built a tower, and let it out to farmers, and went away from home.

[2] And he sent to the farmers at the time a slave, that he might receive from the farmers some of the fruits of the vineyard;

[3] and taking him, they beat him and sent him away empty.

[4] And again he sent to them another slave; and that one they struck on the head and dishonored.

[5] And he sent another; and that one they killed, and many others, beating some and killing some.

[6] He had yet one, a beloved son; he sent him last to them, saying, 'They will respect my son.'

[7] But those farmers said among themselves, 'This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.'

[8] And taking him, they killed him and cast him out outside the vineyard.

[9] What then will the lord of the vineyard do? He will come and destroy the farmers and will give the vineyard to others.

[10] Did you not even read this scripture:
'**The stone which the builders rejected,
this became the head of the corner;
[11] from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ this came to be,
and it is marvelous in our eyes**'?"

[12] And they were seeking to take hold of him, and they feared the crowd, for they knew that he spoke the parable with regard to them. And leaving him, they went away.

[13] And they send to him some of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ and of the ⟦herodians|Herodianoi|Herodians⟧, that they might catch him in a word.

[14] And coming, they say to him, "Teacher, we know that you are true, and it is no concern to you about anyone, for you do not look at the face of men, but in truth you teach the way of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Is it permitted to give the poll tax to Caesar or not? Should we give, or should we not give?"

[15] But he, knowing their hypocrisy, said to them, "Why do you tempt me? Bring me a denarius, that I may see it."

[16] And they brought it. And he says to them, "Whose is this image and the inscription?" And they said to him, "Caesar's."

[17] But ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "**The things of Caesar render to Caesar, and the things of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**" And they were marveling greatly at him.

[18] And ⟦sadducees|Tzedukim|Sadducees⟧ come to him, who say there is no resurrection, and they were asking him, saying,

[19] "Teacher, ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ wrote for us that if someone's brother dies and leaves behind a wife and does not leave a child, that his brother should take the wife and raise up seed for his brother.

[20] There were seven brothers; and the first took a wife, and dying he did not leave seed;

[21] and the second took her, and died not leaving behind seed; and the third likewise;

[22] and the seven did not leave seed. Last of all the woman also died.

[23] In the resurrection, when they rise, of which of them will she be wife? For the seven had her as wife."

[24] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Do you not err because of this, not knowing the scriptures nor the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[25] For when they rise from the dead, they neither marry nor are given in marriage, but are as angels in the heavens.

[26] But concerning the dead, that they are raised, did you not read in the book of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, at the bush, how ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke to him, saying, '**I am the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧**'?

[27] He is not ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the dead, but of the living; you err much."

[28] And one of the scribes, coming up, having heard them disputing, seeing that he answered them well, asked him, "Which commandment is first of all?"

[29] ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ answered, "The first is, '**Hear, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ is one;**
**[30] and you shall love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from your whole heart and from your whole soul and from your whole mind and from your whole strength**.'

[31] The second is this: '**You shall love your neighbor as yourself**.' Greater than these there is no other commandment."

[32] And the scribe said to him, "Well, Teacher; in truth you said that he is one, and there is no other besides him;

[33] and to love him from the whole heart and from the whole understanding and from the whole strength, and to love the neighbor as oneself, is more abundant than all the whole burnt offerings and sacrifices."

[34] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, seeing him, that he answered thoughtfully, said to him, "You are not far from the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧." And no one any longer was daring to ask him.

[35] And answering, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ was saying, teaching in the temple, "How do the scribes say that the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ is son of David?

[36] David himself said in the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧,
'**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ said to my Lord,
"Sit at my right hand
until I put your enemies beneath your feet."**'

[37] David himself calls him Lord, and from where is he his son?" And the great crowd was hearing him gladly.

[38] And in his teaching he was saying, "Beware of the scribes, who want to walk about in robes, and greetings in the marketplaces

[39] and first seats in the synagogues and first couches at the suppers,

[40] those who devour the houses of the widows and for a pretext pray long; these will receive a more abundant sentence."

[41] And sitting down opposite the treasury, he was beholding how the crowd casts copper into the treasury; and many rich ones were casting in much;

[42] and one poor widow, coming, cast in two lepta, which is a quadrans.

[43] And calling his disciples to himself, he said to them, "Amen, I say to you that this poor widow cast in more than all those casting into the treasury;

[44] for all cast in out of what abounds to them, but she out of her lack cast in all, as much as she had, her whole livelihood."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v17, 24, 29, 34, 35) — Hebrew name preserved. In v41 the critical text has "he" (RP adds the name).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — divine name retained.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v35) — Hebrew Anointed.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v36) — Greek ἐν τῷ πνεύματι τῷ ἁγίῳ, "in the Spirit, the Holy" (with the articles: WH Treg NA28).
- **"the Lord" → "*Yahweh*"** (v11, 29, 30, 36) — Greek κύριος, without the article, standing in these quotations for the name YHWH of the Hebrew text (Ps 118:23; Deut 6:4–5; Ps 110:1). "My Lord" in v36 is τῷ κυρίῳ μου, Hebrew *adoni*.
- **"And" / present tenses kept** (throughout) — Mark's καί at the start of sentences and his present tenses ("send", v13; "say", v14; "says", v16; "come", v18) are kept as they are.
- **"A certain man planted a vineyard, and set an hedge about it, and digged a place for the winefat … let it out to husbandmen, and went into a far country" → "A man planted a vineyard, and put a fence around it and dug a vat for the winepress … let it out to farmers, and went away from home"** (v1) — Greek ὑπολήνιον, the trough under the winepress into which the juice runs; γεωργοῖς, "workers of the ground" (as 2 Tim 2:6), here tenants; ἀπεδήμησεν, "went away from his own people"; the Greek has no "far country". The wording echoes Isa 5:1–2.
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v2, 4) — Greek δοῦλος.
- **"at the season … of the fruit" → "at the time … some of the fruits"** (v2) — Greek τῷ καιρῷ; ἀπὸ τῶν καρπῶν, "from the fruits" (WH Treg NA28).
- **"caught him" → "taking him"** (v3, 8) — Greek λαβόντες.
- **"at him they cast stones, and wounded him in the head, and sent him away shamefully handled" → "that one they struck on the head and dishonored"** (v4) — Greek κἀκεῖνον ἐκεφαλίωσαν καὶ ἠτίμασαν (WH NA28); "casting stones" and "sent away" are RP. ἐκεφαλίωσαν is found only here; its form is from κεφαλή "head", and "struck on the head" is the likely sense.
- **"Having yet therefore one son, his wellbeloved, he sent him also last" → "He had yet one, a beloved son; he sent him last"** (v6) — Greek ἔτι ἕνα εἶχεν, υἱὸν ἀγαπητόν (WH Treg NA28); ἀγαπητός as 1:11; 9:7.
- **"reverence" → "respect"** (v6) — Greek ἐντραπήσονται, literally "they will be turned in (shamed) before", as Heb 12:9.
- **"cast him out of the vineyard"** (v8) — Greek ἐξέβαλον … ἔξω; the order "killed him and cast him out" is that of the Greek.
- **"What shall therefore the lord of the vineyard do?" → "What then will the lord of the vineyard do?"** (v9) — the verse follows τί οὖν (Treg NA28 RP); WH and the SBLGNT have no οὖν, "then".
- **"have ye not read this scripture … is become the head of the corner" → "Did you not even read this scripture … this became the head of the corner"** (v10) — Greek οὐδὲ … ἀνέγνωτε; Λίθον ὃν ἀπεδοκίμασαν οἱ οἰκοδομοῦντες, οὗτος ἐγενήθη εἰς κεφαλὴν γωνίας, the wording of Ps 118:22 in the Septuagint, as 1 Pet 2:7. "Head of the corner" is the chief stone at a corner.
- **"This was the Lord's doing" → "from Yahweh this came to be"** (v11) — Greek παρὰ κυρίου ἐγένετο αὕτη; αὕτη, "this", is feminine, following the Hebrew.
- **"sought to lay hold on him, but feared the people … spoken the parable against them" → "were seeking to take hold of him, and they feared the crowd … spoke the parable with regard to them"** (v12) — Greek κρατῆσαι (as 3:21; 6:17); καί, "and"; ὄχλον; πρὸς αὐτούς, "toward them, with regard to them", here against them.
- **"to catch him in his words" → "that they might catch him in a word"** (v13) — Greek ἀγρεύσωσιν, "catch" as in hunting; λόγῳ, "by a word".
- **"carest for no man: for thou regardest not the person of men" → "it is no concern to you about anyone, for you do not look at the face of men"** (v14) — Greek οὐ μέλει σοι περὶ οὐδενός (as 4:38); οὐ γὰρ βλέπεις εἰς πρόσωπον ἀνθρώπων: he shows no partiality.
- **"Is it lawful to give tribute" → "Is it permitted to give the poll tax"** (v14) — Greek ἔξεστιν (as 2:24); κῆνσον, the Latin word *census*: the tax paid per head to Rome.
- **"penny" → "denarius"** (v15) — Greek δηνάριον, the Roman silver coin.
- **"superscription" → "inscription"** (v16) — Greek ἐπιγραφή.
- **"Render to Caesar the things that are Caesar's" → "The things of Caesar render to Caesar"** (v17) — Greek Τὰ Καίσαρος ἀπόδοτε Καίσαρι, in this order (WH Treg NA28); ἀποδίδωμι is "give back, pay what is owed", as Rom 13:7.
- **"they marvelled" → "they were marveling greatly"** (v17) — Greek ἐξεθαύμαζον (WH NA28), a strengthened form of θαυμάζω, imperfect.
- **"which say there is no resurrection"** (v18) — kept; "were asking": ἐπηρώτων, imperfect.
- **"die, and leave his wife behind him, and leave no children" → "dies and leaves behind a wife and does not leave a child"** (v19) — Greek καταλίπῃ … μὴ ἀφῇ τέκνον (WH NA28): two verbs, "leave behind" and "leave", kept apart in v19–22. The sentence changes construction ("wrote that if … that his brother should take"), as in the Greek. "Seed" is σπέρμα (Deut 25:5; Gen 38:8).
- **"had her to wife"** (v23) — Greek ἔσχον αὐτὴν γυναῖκα. "When they rise" (ὅταν ἀναστῶσιν) is in NA28 RP and the SBLGNT; WH and Treg do not have it.
- **"Do ye not therefore err … do greatly err" → "Do you not err because of this … you err much"** (v24, 27) — Greek πλανᾶσθε, "you wander, go astray": the verb is "deceive" in the active; here no deceiver is named. διὰ τοῦτο, "because of this", points forward to "not knowing".
- **"as the angels which are in heaven" → "as angels in the heavens"** (v25) — Greek ὡς ἄγγελοι ἐν τοῖς οὐρανοῖς (WH NA28), without the article, plural "heavens".
- **"as touching the dead, that they rise … how in the bush God spake unto him" → "concerning the dead, that they are raised … at the bush, how Elohim spoke to him"** (v26) — Greek ἐγείρονται, a passive; ἐπὶ τοῦ βάτου, "at the bush": the place in the scroll that tells of the bush (Exod 3). The Greek of the quotation has no verb: "I the *Elohim* of Avraham …"; "am" is supplied.
- **"He is not the God of the dead, but the God of the living" → "He is not Elohim of the dead, but of the living"** (v27) — Greek οὐκ ἔστιν θεὸς νεκρῶν ἀλλὰ ζώντων, without articles and without a second "God" (WH Treg NA28).
- **"Which is the first commandment of all?" → "Which commandment is first of all?"** (v28) — Greek ποία … πρώτη πάντων. "Seeing": ἰδών (Treg NA28 and the SBLGNT; WH RP εἰδώς, "knowing"). "Disputing": συζητούντων, as 8:11; 9:14.
- **"The first of all the commandments is" → "The first is"** (v29) — Greek Πρώτη ἐστίν (WH Treg NA28).
- **"The Lord our God is one Lord" → "Yahweh our Elohim, Yahweh is one"** (v29) — Greek κύριος ὁ θεὸς ἡμῶν κύριος εἷς ἐστιν, the wording of Deut 6:4 in the Septuagint. It can be divided "the Lord our God, the Lord is one" or "the Lord our God is one Lord".
- **"with all thy heart" → "from your whole heart"** (v30, 33) — Greek ἐξ ὅλης τῆς καρδίας σου, "out of"; so four times. "Mind" is διάνοια (as Heb 8:10); "understanding" in v33 is σύνεσις (as Col 1:9). "This is the first commandment" at the end of v30 (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28.
- **"the second is like, namely this" → "The second is this"** (v31) — Greek δευτέρα αὕτη (WH NA28).
- **"Well, Master, thou hast said the truth: for there is one God" → "Well, Teacher; in truth you said that he is one"** (v32) — Greek Καλῶς, διδάσκαλε, ἐπ' ἀληθείας εἶπες ὅτι εἷς ἐστιν: the Greek printed here has no word "God". It can also be read "Well, Teacher, you said in truth that …".
- **"and with all the soul" dropped; "is more than" → "is more abundant than"** (v33) — "and from the whole soul" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28; περισσότερον (WH Treg NA28), the word of v40. "His neighbour" → "the neighbor": τὸν πλησίον.
- **"answered discreetly" → "answered thoughtfully"** (v34) — Greek νουνεχῶς, "having understanding (νοῦς)"; only here in the New Testament.
- **"no man after that durst ask him any question" → "no one any longer was daring to ask him"** (v34) — Greek οὐδεὶς οὐκέτι ἐτόλμα, imperfect.
- **"How say the scribes that Christ is the Son of David?" → "How do the scribes say that the Mashiach is son of David?"** (v35) — Greek ὁ χριστός with the article; υἱὸς Δαυίδ without it. "Answering": ἀποκριθείς, although no one has asked.
- **"The LORD said to my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footstool" → "Yahweh said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand until I put your enemies beneath your feet"** (v36) — Greek ὑποκάτω τῶν ποδῶν σου, "underneath your feet" (WH NA28); Treg RP ὑποπόδιον, "a footstool", the wording of Ps 110:1 in the Septuagint (Heb 1:13).
- **"whence is he then his son? And the common people heard him gladly" → "from where is he his son? And the great crowd was hearing him gladly"** (v37) — Greek πόθεν; ὁ πολὺς ὄχλος, "the large crowd" (or "the mass of the people"); ἤκουεν … ἡδέως, as 6:20.
- **"in his doctrine" → "in his teaching"** (v38) — Greek διδαχῇ.
- **"which love to go in long clothing, and love salutations" → "who want to walk about in robes, and greetings"** (v38) — Greek τῶν θελόντων ἐν στολαῖς περιπατεῖν καὶ ἀσπασμούς: one verb, "want", governs both the infinitive and the nouns of v38–39. στολή is a long outer robe.
- **"the chief seats … the uppermost rooms at feasts" → "first seats … first couches at the suppers"** (v39) — Greek πρωτοκαθεδρίας, πρωτοκλισίας (the first places for reclining at table), δείπνοις.
- **"Which devour widows' houses, and for a pretence make long prayers: these shall receive greater damnation" → "those who devour the houses of the widows and for a pretext pray long; these will receive a more abundant sentence"** (v40) — Greek οἱ κατεσθίοντες, a nominative that breaks the construction of v38; προφάσει μακρὰ προσευχόμενοι; περισσότερον κρίμα: κρίμα is the sentence of a judge.
- **"Jesus sat over against the treasury, and beheld how the people cast money" → "sitting down opposite the treasury, he was beholding how the crowd casts copper"** (v41) — Greek ἐθεώρει; ὁ ὄχλος βάλλει χαλκόν, a present tense; χαλκός is copper (coin), as 6:8.
- **"two mites, which make a farthing" → "two lepta, which is a quadrans"** (v42) — Greek λεπτὰ δύο, ὅ ἐστιν κοδράντης: the lepton was the smallest copper coin; κοδράντης is the Latin *quadrans*, the smallest Roman coin.
- **"Verily" → "Amen"** (v43) — Greek ἀμήν.
- **"hath cast more in" → "cast in more"** (v43) — Greek ἔβαλεν (WH Treg NA28), a simple past.
- **"of their abundance; but she of her want did cast in all that she had, even all her living" → "out of what abounds to them, but she out of her lack cast in all, as much as she had, her whole livelihood"** (v44) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ περισσεύοντος αὐτοῖς; ἐκ τῆς ὑστερήσεως αὐτῆς; ὅλον τὸν βίον αὐτῆς.
