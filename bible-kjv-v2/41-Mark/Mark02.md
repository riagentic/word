# Mark 2

[1] And when he entered again into ⟦capernaum|Kfar Nachum|Capernaum⟧ after some days, it was heard that he is at home;

[2] and many were gathered together, so that there was no longer room, not even at the door; and he was speaking the word to them.

[3] And they come, bringing to him a paralytic carried by four.

[4] And not being able to bring him to him because of the crowd, they unroofed the roof where he was, and having dug through, they lower the pallet where the paralytic was lying.

[5] And seeing their faith, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to the paralytic, "Child, your sins are forgiven."

[6] Now there were some of the scribes sitting there and reasoning in their hearts,

[7] "Why does this one speak thus? He blasphemes! Who is able to forgive sins except one, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[8] And immediately ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, recognizing in his spirit that they are reasoning thus within themselves, says to them, "Why do you reason these things in your hearts?

[9] Which is easier, to say to the paralytic, 'Your sins are forgiven,' or to say, 'Arise and take up your pallet and walk'?

[10] But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority to forgive sins upon the earth" — he says to the paralytic —

[11] "To you I say, arise, take up your pallet and go to your house."

[12] And he arose, and immediately taking up the pallet he went out before all, so that all were amazed and glorified ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, saying, "We never saw it thus."

[13] And he went out again beside the sea; and all the crowd was coming to him, and he was teaching them.

[14] And passing along, he saw Levi the son of ⟦alphaeus|Alphaios|Alphaeus⟧ sitting at the toll booth, and he says to him, "Follow me." And rising, he followed him.

[15] And it comes to pass that he reclines in his house, and many toll collectors and sinners were reclining with ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ and his disciples; for they were many, and they were following him.

[16] And the scribes of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧, seeing that he eats with the sinners and toll collectors, were saying to his disciples, "Why does he eat with the toll collectors and sinners?"

[17] And hearing it, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ says to them, "**Those who are strong have no need of a physician, but those who are ill. I did not come to call righteous ones, but sinners.**"

[18] And ⟦john|Yochanan's|John's⟧ disciples and the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ were fasting. And they come and say to him, "Why do ⟦john|Yochanan's|John's⟧ disciples and the disciples of the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ fast, but your disciples do not fast?"

[19] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ said to them, "Are the sons of the bridal chamber able to fast while the bridegroom is with them? As long a time as they have the bridegroom with them, they are not able to fast.

[20] But days will come when the bridegroom is taken away from them, and then they will fast in that day.

[21] No one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth upon an old garment; otherwise the piece that fills it takes away from it, the new from the old, and a worse tear comes to be.

[22] And no one puts young wine into old wineskins; otherwise the wine will burst the wineskins, and the wine perishes, and the wineskins. But young wine into new wineskins!"

[23] And it came to pass that he was going along through the grainfields on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, and his disciples began to make their way, plucking the ears.

[24] And the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ were saying to him, "See, why are they doing on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ what is not permitted?"

[25] And he says to them, "Did you never read what David did when he had need and was hungry, he and those with him:

[26] how he entered into the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the time of ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ the high priest and ate the loaves of the setting forth, which it is not permitted to eat except for the priests, and gave also to those who were with him?"

[27] And he was saying to them, "**The ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ came to be for the sake of man, and not man for the sake of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧;**

**[28] so then the Son of Man is lord also of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧.**"

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Hebrew name preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv7, 12, 26) — divine name retained in original form.
- **"sabbath" → "Sabbath"** (vv23, 24, 27, 28) — capitalized consistently as a proper name/institution. In v23, 24 the Greek has the plural form τοῖς σάββασιν with singular meaning.
- **"it was noised that he was in the house" → "it was heard that he is at home"** (v1) — Greek ἠκούσθη ὅτι ἐν οἴκῳ ἐστίν: "it was heard", a present "is", and ἐν οἴκῳ without the article, "at home" or "in a house" (RP εἰς οἶκον).
- **"straightway many were gathered" → "many were gathered"** (v2) — "straightway" (Treg RP εὐθέως) is not in WH NA28. "he preached the word" → "he was speaking the word": Greek ἐλάλει.
- **"they come … they let down … he said … saith" → presents kept** (v3, 4, 5, 8, 10, 14, 15, 17, 18, 25) — Greek ἔρχονται, χαλῶσι, λέγει, γίνεται, λέγουσιν: Mark's present tense in narrative.
- **"sick of the palsy" → "paralytic"; "borne of four" → "carried by four"** (v3) — Greek παραλυτικὸν αἰρόμενον ὑπὸ τεσσάρων; αἴρω is the verb "take up" of v9, 11, 12 and "takes away" of v21.
- **"when they could not come nigh unto him for the press" → "not being able to bring him to him because of the crowd"** (v4) — Greek προσενέγκαι, "bring to" (WH NA28); the KJV follows προσεγγίσαι, "come near" (Treg RP). ὄχλος is "crowd".
- **"uncovered the roof … when they had broken it up … the bed" → "unroofed the roof … having dug through … the pallet"** (v4) — Greek ἀπεστέγασαν τὴν στέγην, ἐξορύξαντες "having dug out", κράβαττος, a poor man's bedding (v9, 11, 12).
- **"Son, thy sins be forgiven thee" → "Child, your sins are forgiven"** (v5, 9) — Greek τέκνον; ἀφίενται, a present tense, "are (being) forgiven" (WH Treg NA28); RP has the perfect ἀφέωνται, "have been forgiven".
- **"reasoning" kept** (v6, 8) — Greek διαλογίζομαι, three times.
- **"Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but God only?" → "Why does this one speak thus? He blasphemes! Who is able to forgive sins except one, *Elohim*?"** (v7) — Greek βλασφημεῖ, a verb (WH Treg NA28; RP βλασφημίας). εἰ μὴ εἷς ὁ θεός is literally "except one, God" (compare Deut 6:4): "one" can also be taken as "alone".
- **"when Jesus perceived in his spirit" → "recognizing in his spirit"** (v8) — Greek ἐπιγνοὺς τῷ πνεύματι αὐτοῦ, "having come to know fully"; the dative is "in" or "by his spirit".
- **"Arise" kept; "bed" → "pallet"** (v9, 11, 12) — Greek ἔγειρε; ἠγέρθη "he arose" (v12), a passive form: "he was raised" is also possible.
- **"power on earth to forgive sins" → "authority to forgive sins upon the earth"** (v10) — Greek ἐξουσία, "authority", as 1:22, 27. Editions: ἀφιέναι ἁμαρτίας ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς WH NA28 ] ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς ἀφιέναι ἁμαρτίας Treg (and the printed SBLGNT), "authority upon the earth to forgive sins"; ἀφιέναι ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς ἁμαρτίας RP — 2–2, NA28 decides.
- **"go thy way into thine house" → "go to your house"** (v11) — Greek ὕπαγε εἰς τὸν οἶκόν σου.
- **"We never saw it on this fashion" → "We never saw it thus"** (v12) — Greek οὕτως οὐδέποτε εἴδομεν; "it" is supplied. ἐξίστασθαι "were amazed" is literally "stood out of themselves" (the verb of 3:21).
- **"the receipt of custom" → "the toll booth"; "publicans" → "toll collectors"** (v14, 15, 16) — Greek τελώνιον, τελῶναι, from τέλος "toll, customs duty" (Rom 13:7): collectors of tolls and duties.
- **"And it came to pass, that, as Jesus sat at meat in his house" → "And it comes to pass that he reclines in his house"** (v15) — Greek γίνεται κατακεῖσθαι αὐτόν, a present; κατάκειμαι is "lie down" (v4; 1:30), here at a meal. The name is not in this clause, and "his house" can be Levi's or Yeshua's.
- **"for there were many, and they followed him" → "for they were many, and they were following him"** (v15) — Greek ἦσαν γὰρ πολλοὶ καὶ ἠκολούθουν αὐτῷ.
- **"the scribes and Pharisees" → "the scribes of the Pharisees"** (v16) — Greek οἱ γραμματεῖς τῶν Φαρισαίων (WH Treg NA28); RP "the scribes and the Pharisees".
- **"How is it that he eateth and drinketh with publicans and sinners?" → "Why does he eat with the toll collectors and sinners?"** (v16) — "and drinks" (Treg RP) is not in WH NA28. Greek ὅτι … ἐσθίει; printed as a question ("Why …?"); ὅτι can also introduce a statement: "He eats with the toll collectors and sinners!"
- **"They that are whole … they that are sick" → "Those who are strong … those who are ill"** (v17) — Greek οἱ ἰσχύοντες (from ἰσχύς "strength"), οἱ κακῶς ἔχοντες (as 1:32, 34).
- **"the righteous, but sinners to repentance" → "righteous ones, but sinners"** (v17) — Greek δικαίους, without the article; "to repentance" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"the disciples of John and of the Pharisees used to fast" → "John's disciples and the Pharisees were fasting"** (v18) — Greek ἦσαν … νηστεύοντες, "were fasting" (then, or as a custom); οἱ Φαρισαῖοι, "the Pharisees" themselves (WH Treg NA28).
- **"the children of the bridechamber" → "the sons of the bridal chamber"** (v19) — Greek οἱ υἱοὶ τοῦ νυμφῶνος, a Hebrew way of speaking for the wedding guests or the bridegroom's attendants. δύνανται is "are able", twice.
- **"in those days" → "in that day"** (v20) — Greek ἐν ἐκείνῃ τῇ ἡμέρᾳ, singular (WH Treg NA28).
- **"a piece of new cloth" → "a patch of unshrunk cloth"** (v21) — Greek ἐπίβλημα ῥάκους ἀγνάφου, "a patch of unfulled rag".
- **"else the new piece that filled it up taketh away from the old, and the rent is made worse" → "otherwise the piece that fills it takes away from it, the new from the old, and a worse tear comes to be"** (v21) — Greek αἴρει τὸ πλήρωμα ἀπ' αὐτοῦ τὸ καινὸν τοῦ παλαιοῦ. πλήρωμα is "fullness" elsewhere; here "that which fills up" the hole. It can be the subject ("the filling takes away from it") or the object ("it takes the filling away from it"). σχίσμα "tear" is from the verb of 1:10.
- **"new wine into old bottles … new bottles" → "young wine into old wineskins … new wineskins"** (v22) — two Greek words: οἶνον νέον ("new, young") and ἀσκοὺς καινούς ("new", the word of v21 and 1:27). ἀσκός is a skin bag.
- **"the wine is spilled, and the bottles will be marred: but new wine must be put into new bottles" → "the wine perishes, and the wineskins. But young wine into new wineskins!"** (v22) — Greek ὁ οἶνος ἀπόλλυται καὶ οἱ ἀσκοί (WH Treg NA28); the last sentence has no verb in WH NA28 (Treg RP add βλητέον, "must be put").
- **"he went through the corn fields … his disciples began, as they went, to pluck the ears of corn" → "he was going along through the grainfields … his disciples began to make their way, plucking the ears"** (v23) — Greek ἤρξαντο ὁδὸν ποιεῖν τίλλοντες τοὺς στάχυας: the main verb is "to make (their) way", the plucking is a participle. "corn" in KJV 1611 = any grain, not specifically maize. Editions: παραπορεύεσθαι "go along" NA28 RP (printed) ] διαπορεύεσθαι "go through" WH Treg — 2–2, NA28 decides.
- **"that which is not lawful" → "what is not permitted"** (v24, 26) — Greek οὐκ ἔξεστιν.
- **"in the days of Abiathar the high priest" → "in the time of Abiathar the high priest"** (v26) — Greek ἐπὶ Ἀβιαθὰρ ἀρχιερέως, without the article: "in the time of Abiathar, high priest", or "at (the passage about) Abiathar".
- **"the shewbread" → "the loaves of the setting forth"** (v26) — Greek τοὺς ἄρτους τῆς προθέσεως, as Heb 9:2; Hebrew לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים, "bread of the Presence" (1 Sam 21:6).
- **"The sabbath was made for man" → "The Sabbath came to be for the sake of man"** (v27) — Greek ἐγένετο, "came into being"; διὰ τὸν ἄνθρωπον, "on account of, for the sake of man".
- **"Therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath" → "so then the Son of Man is lord also of the Sabbath"** (v28) — Greek ὥστε, the consequence of v27; καί is "also" or "even".

### Notes
- **v26** — historical note: 1 Sam 21 names Ahimelech (Abiathar's father) as high priest at the time; Mark may reference "the section about Abiathar."
- **v9** — "Arise": ἔγειρε NA28 (printed); WH Treg ἐγείρου, RP ἔγειραι; the meaning is the same.
