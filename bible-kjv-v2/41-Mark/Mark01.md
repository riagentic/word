[4] ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ appeared, the one ⟦immerse|immersing|baptizing⟧ in the wilderness and proclaiming ⟦immersion|an immersion|a baptism⟧ of repentance unto forgiveness of sins.

[19] And going on a little, he saw ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the son of ⟦zebedee|Zebedaios|Zebedee⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧ his brother, them also in the boat, mending the nets,

[28] And the report of him went out immediately everywhere, into the whole surrounding region of ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧.

[31] And coming to her, he raised her, taking hold of her hand; and the fever left her, and she was serving them.

[32] And when evening had come, when the sun set, they were bringing to him all who were ill and those who were demonized;

[33] and the whole city was gathered together at the door.

[34] And he healed many who were ill with various diseases, and cast out many demons, and he was not letting the demons speak, because they knew him.

[39] And he went into their synagogues, into all ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧, proclaiming and casting out the demons.

[40] And a leper comes to him, appealing to him and kneeling, saying to him, "If you are willing, you are able to cleanse me."

[41] And moved with compassion, he stretched out his hand and touched him, and says to him, "I am willing; be cleansed."

[42] And immediately the leprosy went away from him, and he was cleansed.

[43] And speaking sternly to him, he immediately cast him out,

[44] and says to him, "See that you say nothing to anyone, but go, show yourself to the priest and offer concerning your purification the things which ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ prescribed, for a testimony to them."

[45] But he, going out, began to proclaim much and to spread the word abroad, so that he was no longer able to enter openly into a city, but was outside in desert places; and they were coming to him from every side.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v19, 29) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus Christ, the Son of God" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, Son of *Elohim*"** (v1) — Hebrew/Aramaic name *Yeshua* ("Yahweh saves"); Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach* ("Anointed One"); *Elohim* retained as divine name throughout. Greek υἱοῦ θεοῦ has no article. Editions: χριστοῦ alone WH (and the printed SBLGNT) ] + υἱοῦ θεοῦ Treg NA28; υἱοῦ τοῦ θεοῦ RP — 2–2 among the four editions, NA28 decides.
- **"in the prophets" → "in Isaiah the prophet"** (v2) — Greek ἐν τῷ Ἠσαΐᾳ τῷ προφήτῃ (WH Treg NA28); the Byzantine text (RP) reads τοῖς προφήταις, "in the prophets", which the KJV follows. The quotation joins Mal 3:1 / Exod 23:20 to Isa 40:3.
- **"prepare … Prepare" → "prepare … Make ready"** (v2, 3) — two Greek verbs, κατασκευάσει and ἑτοιμάσατε. "before thee" at the end of v2 (RP ἔμπροσθέν σου) is not in the critical text.
- **"The voice" → "a voice"** (v3) — Greek φωνὴ βοῶντος, without the article.
- **"John did baptize in the wilderness, and preach" → "John appeared, the one immersing in the wilderness and proclaiming"** (v4) — Greek ἐγένετο Ἰωάννης ὁ βαπτίζων ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ καὶ κηρύσσων; ἐγένετο is "came to be, came on the scene". Editions: ἐρήμῳ WH (and the printed SBLGNT), "John the Immerser appeared in the wilderness, proclaiming" ] + καὶ Treg NA28 RP — 2–2, NA28 decides. "baptize" renders Greek *baptizo* = "to dip/immerse," translated literally throughout.
- **"baptism of repentance for the remission of sins" → "immersion of repentance unto forgiveness of sins"** (v4) — Greek *baptisma* = immersion; εἰς ἄφεσιν ἁμαρτιῶν, "unto / into forgiveness of sins".
- **"all the land of Judaea, and they of Jerusalem, and were all baptized" → "all the country of Judea and all those of Jerusalem, and they were being immersed"** (v5) — Greek οἱ Ἱεροσολυμῖται πάντες, "all the Jerusalemites"; "all" belongs to them in the critical text. ἐξεπορεύετο, ἐβαπτίζοντο are imperfects.
- **"with a girdle of a skin about his loins" → "a leather belt around his loins"** (v6) — Greek ζώνην δερματίνην περὶ τὴν ὀσφὺν αὐτοῦ; "clothed with" governs both the hair and the belt.
- **"preached" → "was proclaiming"** (v7; also v4, 14, 38, 39, 45) — Greek κηρύσσω, "proclaim as a herald".
- **"mightier" → "stronger"; "worthy" → "fit"; "unloose" → "loose"** (v7) — Greek ἰσχυρότερος (as "the strong man", 3:27); ἱκανός, "sufficient, fit", not ἄξιος "worthy"; "latchet of whose shoes" → "strap of whose sandals".
- **"I indeed have baptized you with water … with the Holy Ghost" → "I immersed you with water … in *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v8) — Greek ἐβάπτισα, a simple past; ὕδατι, a dative without a preposition; ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ, "in", and without the article (WH has no ἐν). Greek *Pneuma Hagion* translates Hebrew *Ruach HaQodesh*.
- **"of John in Jordan" → "into the Jordan by John"** (v9) — Greek εἰς τὸν Ἰορδάνην, "into".
- **"straightway" / "immediately" / "forthwith" / "anon" → "immediately"** (v10, 12, 18, 20, 21, 23, 28, 29, 30, 42, 43) — Greek εὐθύς, Mark's characteristic word; one rendering throughout.
- **"the heavens opened" → "the heavens being torn"** (v10) — Greek σχιζομένους, "being split, torn" (the word of 15:38), not "opened".
- **"descending upon him" → "coming down unto him"** (v10) — Greek εἰς αὐτόν (WH Treg NA28), "into / to him"; the Byzantine text has ἐπ' αὐτόν, "upon him".
- **"from heaven … my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased" → "out of the heavens … my Son, the beloved; in you I have taken pleasure"** (v11) — Greek ἐκ τῶν οὐρανῶν, plural; ὁ υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός; ἐν σοὶ εὐδόκησα, a past tense ("I took pleasure"). "in whom" is the Byzantine ἐν ᾧ.
- **"driveth him" → "casts him out"** (v12) — Greek ἐκβάλλει, present tense; the same verb as "cast out" demons (v34, 39) and v43.
- **"ministered unto" → "were serving"** (v13, 31) — Greek διακονέω.
- **"was put in prison" → "was delivered up"** (v14) — Greek παραδοθῆναι, "be handed over, delivered up" (the verb of 3:19); no prison is named.
- **"the gospel of the kingdom of God" → "the gospel of *Elohim*"** (v14) — "of the kingdom" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"is fulfilled … is at hand" → "has been fulfilled … has drawn near"** (v15) — Greek perfects πεπλήρωται, ἤγγικεν; καιρός is "the (appointed) time".
- **"believe the gospel" → "believe in the gospel"** (v15) — Greek πιστεύετε ἐν.
- **"casting a net into the sea" → "casting a net in the sea"** (v16) — Greek ἀμφιβάλλοντας ἐν τῇ θαλάσσῃ, "casting around in the sea"; the net is in the verb.
- **"fishers" kept** (v16, 17) — Greek ἁλιεῖς, one word in both verses.
- **"forsook" / "left" → "leaving"** (v18, 20) — Greek ἀφέντες both times; the same verb is "left" (v31), "letting" (v34) and "forgive" (2:5).
- **"who also were in the ship mending their nets" → "them also in the boat, mending the nets"** (v19) — Greek καὶ αὐτοὺς ἐν τῷ πλοίῳ καταρτίζοντας τὰ δίκτυα. καταρτίζω is "put in order, restore"; of nets "mend" (or "make ready").
- **"they went into … he entered … and taught" → "they go into … entering into the synagogue, he was teaching"** (v21) — Greek εἰσπορεύονται, a present tense in narrative (so also v12, 30, 37, 38, 40, 41, 44: kept as presents). τοῖς σάββασιν is a plural form with singular meaning. Editions: εἰσελθὼν εἰς τὴν συναγωγὴν ἐδίδασκεν WH Treg NA28 RP ] ἐδίδασκεν εἰς τὴν συναγωγήν, "he was teaching in the synagogue", SBLGNT WH-margin — the verse follows the majority.
- **"doctrine" → "teaching"** (v22, 27) — Greek *didachē*.
- **"as one that had authority" → "as one having authority"** (v22, 27) — Greek ἐξουσία, the right and the power to act; "power" is δύναμις.
- **"with an unclean spirit" kept** (v23) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι ἀκαθάρτῳ, literally "in an unclean spirit", the same construction as "in *Ruach HaQodesh*" (v8).
- **"Let us alone; what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth? art thou come" → "What have we to do with you, Yeshua Nazarene? Did you come"** (v24) — Greek τί ἡμῖν καὶ σοί, literally "what to us and to you?"; "Let us alone" (RP ἔα) is not in the critical text. Ναζαρηνέ is the form of address of Ναζαρηνός, "Nazarene", without an article (the other form, Ναζωραῖος, "Nazorean", stands in Matt 2:23); the place name is not in the verse. ἦλθες ἀπολέσαι ἡμᾶς can also be a statement: "You came to destroy us."
- **"Hold thy peace" → "Be muzzled"** (v25) — Greek φιμώθητι, from φιμός "muzzle"; so also 4:39.
- **"had torn him, and cried with a loud voice" → "convulsing him and calling with a loud voice"** (v26) — Greek σπαράξαν, φωνῆσαν (RP κράξαν, "cried").
- **"amazed" → "astounded"** (v27) — Greek ἐθαμβήθησαν, awe mixed with fear; a different word from "astonished" (v22, ἐκπλήσσομαι) and "amazed" (2:12, ἐξίσταμαι).
- **"questioned … What new doctrine is this? for with authority commandeth he" → "disputed … A new teaching! With authority he orders"** (v27) — Greek διδαχὴ καινή· κατ' ἐξουσίαν καὶ τοῖς πνεύμασι … ἐπιτάσσει (WH Treg NA28). The stop after καινή is the editors'; without it: "A new teaching with authority! He commands even …".
- **"his fame spread abroad throughout all the region round about Galilee" → "the report of him went out immediately everywhere, into the whole surrounding region of Galilee"** (v28) — Greek ἡ ἀκοὴ αὐτοῦ, "the hearing / report of him"; εἰς ὅλην τὴν περίχωρον τῆς Γαλιλαίας can also mean the region around Galilee.
- **"lay sick of a fever, and anon they tell him of her" → "was lying down with a fever, and immediately they speak to him about her"** (v30).
- **"took her by the hand, and lifted her up; and immediately the fever left her" → "he raised her, taking hold of her hand; and the fever left her"** (v31) — Greek ἤγειρεν αὐτὴν κρατήσας τῆς χειρός; "immediately" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"diseased" / "sick" → "ill"** (v32, 34) — Greek κακῶς ἔχοντας, literally "having it badly".
- **"possessed with devils" → "demonized"; "devils" → "demons"** (v32, 34, 39) — Greek δαιμονιζομένους, one verb from δαιμόνιον; the Greek has no word "possessed".
- **"suffered not the devils to speak" → "was not letting the demons speak"** (v34) — Greek οὐκ ἤφιεν, imperfect. WH adds χριστὸν εἶναι, "knew him to be the Mashiach"; Treg NA28 RP do not.
- **"in the morning, rising up a great while before day" → "early, while it was still deep night, rising"** (v35) — Greek πρωῒ ἔννυχα λίαν, "early, very much in the night".
- **"solitary place" → "desert place"; "desert places" kept** (v35, 45) — Greek ἔρημος τόπος; the noun ἡ ἔρημος is "the wilderness" (v3, 4, 12, 13).
- **"followed after him" → "pursued him"** (v36) — Greek κατεδίωξεν, "hunted down".
- **"into the next towns … for therefore came I forth" → "elsewhere, into the neighboring towns … for unto this I came out"** (v38) — Greek ἀλλαχοῦ (not in RP); κωμοπόλεις, "village-towns", only here in the New Testament; εἰς τοῦτο γὰρ ἐξῆλθον.
- **"he preached in their synagogues throughout all Galilee" → "he went into their synagogues, into all Galilee, proclaiming"** (v39) — Greek ἦλθεν κηρύσσων εἰς τὰς συναγωγὰς αὐτῶν εἰς ὅλην τὴν Γαλιλαίαν (RP ἦν … ἐν, "he was … in").
- **"beseeching him, and kneeling down to him … If thou wilt, thou canst make me clean" → "appealing to him and kneeling … If you are willing, you are able to cleanse me"** (v40) — Greek παρακαλῶν; ἐὰν θέλῃς δύνασαί με καθαρίσαι; καθαρίζω is "cleanse" in v40, 41, 42.
- **"And Jesus, moved with compassion, put forth his hand" → "And moved with compassion, he stretched out his hand"** (v41) — Greek σπλαγχνισθείς, from σπλάγχνα "inward parts": to be stirred in one's inward parts. The name is not in the critical text here, nor in v43, 45. Editions: σπλαγχνισθεὶς WH Treg NA28 RP ] ὀργισθείς, "being angered", SBLGNT WH-margin — the verse follows the majority.
- **"as soon as he had spoken … departed" → "went away"** (v42) — "as soon as he had spoken" (RP) is not in the critical text.
- **"straitly charged him, and forthwith sent him away" → "speaking sternly to him, he immediately cast him out"** (v43) — Greek ἐμβριμησάμενος, literally "snorting at" (strong feeling, a stern warning); ἐξέβαλεν, the verb of v12, 34, 39.
- **"for thy cleansing those things which Moses commanded" → "concerning your purification the things which Moses prescribed"** (v44) — Greek περὶ τοῦ καθαρισμοῦ σου ἃ προσέταξεν Μωϋσῆς (Lev 14).
- **"to publish it much, and to blaze abroad the matter, insomuch that Jesus could no more" → "to proclaim much and to spread the word abroad, so that he was no longer able"** (v45) — Greek κηρύσσειν πολλὰ καὶ διαφημίζειν τὸν λόγον. The Greek has only pronouns: "he, going out" is the cleansed man, "he was no longer able" is Yeshua.

### Notes
- **v2-3** — composite quotation: Mal 3:1 + Ex 23:20 + Isa 40:3.
- **v11** — echoes Ps 2:7 + Isa 42:1.
- **v14** — NA28 and RP begin "Now after" (μετὰ δέ); WH, Treg and the SBLGNT "And after" (καὶ μετά), which the verse follows.
- **v40** — NA28 adds "and" after "kneeling" (καὶ γονυπετῶν καὶ λέγων); Treg and RP read "kneeling to him and".
