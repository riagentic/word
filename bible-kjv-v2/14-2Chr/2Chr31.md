# 2 Chronicles 31

[1] When all this was finished, all Israel who were present went out to the cities of Judah, broke the sacred pillars in pieces, cut down the Asherah poles, and tore down the high places and the altars throughout Judah and Benjamin, and also in Ephraim and Manasseh, until they had utterly destroyed them all. Then all the children of Israel returned, each to his possession, to their own cities.

[2] Hezekiah appointed the divisions of the priests and the Levites according to their divisions, each according to his service — priests and Levites — for burnt offerings, for peace offerings, to minister, to give thanks, and to praise in the gates of the camp of Yahweh.

[3] He appointed also the king's portion from his own possessions for the burnt offerings — for the morning and evening burnt offerings, and for the burnt offerings of the Sabbaths, new moons, and appointed feasts, as it is written in the Law of Yahweh.

[4] Moreover, he commanded the people who lived in Jerusalem to give the portion of the priests and Levites, that they might devote themselves to the Law of Yahweh.

[5] As soon as the command came, the children of Israel gave in abundance the firstfruits of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and all the produce of the field; they brought in the tithe of all things abundantly.

[6] The children of Israel and Judah who lived in the cities of Judah also brought in the tithe of oxen and sheep, and the tithe of consecrated things that were consecrated to Yahweh their *Elohim*, and laid them in heaps.

[7] In the third month they began to lay the heaps, and in the seventh month they finished.

[8] When Hezekiah and the officials came and saw the heaps, they blessed Yahweh and his people Israel.

[9] Hezekiah questioned the priests and the Levites about the heaps.

[10] Azariah the chief priest, of the house of Zadok, answered him, "Since the people began to bring the contributions into the house of Yahweh, we have eaten and had plenty to spare; for Yahweh has blessed his people, and what is left is this great store."

[11] Then Hezekiah commanded them to prepare storerooms in the house of Yahweh; so they prepared them,

[12] and brought in the contributions, the tithes, and the dedicated things faithfully. Conaniah the Levite was in charge of them, and Shimei his brother was second.

[13] Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were overseers under the hand of Conaniah and Shimei his brother, by the appointment of King Hezekiah and Azariah the chief officer of the house of *Elohim*.

[14] Kore son of Imnah the Levite, the eastern gatekeeper, was over the freewill offerings of *Elohim*, to apportion the contributions reserved for Yahweh and the most holy things.

[15] Under him were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah, in the cities of the priests, as a trust-office, to distribute to their brothers by divisions, to the great as well as to the small —

[16] besides those males, from three years old and upward, enrolled in the genealogies, who came into the house of Yahweh as each day required, for their service in their charges according to their divisions;

[17] and to the enrolled priests by their fathers' houses, and to the Levites from twenty years old and upward, in their charges by their divisions;

[18] and to the enrolled of all their little ones, their wives, their sons, and their daughters, in the whole assembly — for as a trust-office they had sanctified themselves in holiness.

[19] Also for the sons of Aaron the priests who were in the pasture lands of their cities, in every city, there were men designated by name to distribute portions to all the males among the priests and to all who were enrolled by genealogies among the Levites.

[20] Thus Hezekiah did throughout all Judah; he did what was good, right, and true before Yahweh his *Elohim*.

[21] Every work that he undertook in the service of the house of *Elohim*, in the Law, and in the commandment — to seek his *Elohim* — he did with all his heart, and prospered.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"images" → "sacred pillars"** (v1) — *matzevot*.
- **"tents of the Lord" → "camp of Yahweh"** (v2) — *machanot*.
- **"set feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (v3) — *mo'adim*.
- **"corn" → "grain"** (v5).
- **"chambers" → "storerooms"** (v11) — *leshakhot*.
- **"porter" → "gatekeeper"** (v14).
- **"set office" → "trust-office"** (v15, 18) — *be-emunah*.
- **"suburbs" → "pasture lands"** (v19).

### Notes
- **Unique to Chronicler** — reforms of Hezekiah. Kings summarizes 2 Kgs 18:4.
- **Nation-wide cultic purge** (v1) — extends into the former Northern Kingdom. Assyrian weakening enables Hezekiah's religious reach.
- **Priestly support system** — Hezekiah restores tithe-support. Economic infrastructure for full priestly ministry. Similar reform in Neh 10:35-39.
- **Three-to-seven month harvest** — begins with grain and ends with olive/wine season.
- **Enrolled from three years old** (v16) — all male dependents of priests included in distribution. Comprehensive support.
- **"Good, right, and true"** (v20) — Deuteronomic triad for ideal king.
- **Hezekiah's success formula** (v21) — wholehearted devotion + Torah-obedience = prosperity. Classic Chronicler theology.
