# 2 Chronicles 36

[1] The people of the land took Jehoahaz son of Josiah and made him king in his father's place in Jerusalem.

[2] Jehoahaz was twenty-three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem.

[3] The king of Egypt deposed him at Jerusalem and imposed on the land a fine of one hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.

[4] The king of Egypt made Eliakim his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem, and changed his name to Jehoiakim; Neco took Jehoahaz his brother and brought him to Egypt.

[5] Jehoiakim was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh his *Elohim*.

[6] Against him Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up and bound him in bronze fetters to carry him to Babylon.

[7] Nebuchadnezzar also carried some of the vessels of the house of Yahweh to Babylon, and put them in his palace in Babylon.

[8] The rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, his abominations that he did, and what was found against him, behold, they are written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah; Jehoiachin his son reigned in his place.

[9] Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem. He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh.

[10] At the turn of the year, King Nebuchadnezzar sent and brought him to Babylon, with the precious vessels of the house of Yahweh, and made Zedekiah his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem.

[11] Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.

[12] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh his *Elohim*, and did not humble himself before Jeremiah the prophet, who spoke from the mouth of Yahweh.

[13] He also rebelled against King Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him swear by *Elohim*; but he stiffened his neck and hardened his heart against turning to Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel.

[14] All the officials of the priests and the people acted with great unfaithfulness, following all the abominations of the nations; they polluted the house of Yahweh that he had consecrated in Jerusalem.

[15] Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers sent to them by the hand of his messengers, rising early and sending them, because he had compassion on his people and on his dwelling place.

[16] But they mocked the messengers of *Elohim*, despised his words, and scoffed at his prophets, until the wrath of Yahweh rose against his people, until there was no marpe — healing.

[17] Therefore he brought upon them the king of the Chaldeans, who killed their young men with the sword in the house of their sanctuary, and had no compassion on young man or virgin, on the aged or the bent with age; he gave them all into his hand.

[18] All the vessels of the house of *Elohim*, great and small, the treasures of the house of Yahweh, the treasures of the king and of his officials — all these he brought to Babylon.

[19] They burned the house of *Elohim*, tore down the wall of Jerusalem, burned all its palaces with fire, and destroyed all its precious vessels.

[20] Those who had escaped from the sword he carried away to Babylon, where they became servants to him and to his sons, until the rise of the kingdom of Persia —

[21] to fulfill the word of Yahweh by the mouth of Jeremiah, until the land had enjoyed its shabbatot — Sabbaths. All the days it lay desolate it kept Sabbath, to fulfill seventy years.

[22] In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, in order that the word of Yahweh by the mouth of Jeremiah might be accomplished, Yahweh stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, so that he made a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and also put it in writing:

[23] "Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: 'Yahweh the *Elohim* of heaven has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and he has charged me to build him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Whoever is among you of all his people — may Yahweh his *Elohim* be with him — let him go up.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jehoiachin was eight years old" → "eighteen years old"** (v9) — 2 Kgs 24:8 and LXX give 18; MT 8 is scribal error. Rendered as 18 for coherence.
- **"Zedekiah his brother" → "his brother"** (v10) — actually his uncle (1 Kgs 24:17); *ach* can mean kinsman. Chronicler may use broadly.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v14).
- **"him that stooped for age" → "the bent with age"** (v17).
- **"his temple" → "his palace"** (v7) — *heikalo*; in Nebuchadnezzar's context, the royal palace-complex.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 24-25** — Chronicler compresses greatly. Four kings in 23 verses.
- **Three deportations** implied: 605 (Jehoiakim-era temple vessels + Daniel), 597 (Jehoiachin), 586 (Zedekiah/full destruction).
- **"Seventy years"** (v21) — Jer 25:11, 29:10. Interpretation: 586 to 516 BC (destruction to rebuilt temple) fits ~70; or from 605 to 539 BC (first deportation to Cyrus's decree). Sabbath-year interpretation: land repays 490 years of neglected Sabbath-years (Lev 26:34-35).
- **Cyrus's edict (539 BC)** — Chronicler ends with this hopeful pivot. Ezra 1 picks up the text verbatim and continues. Hebrew Bible order places Chronicles last — this is the final word: return, rebuild, hope.
- **Jeremiah's prophecy** cited twice (v12, 21-22); Chronicler emphasizes prophetic fulfillment. Even exile fits Yahweh's plan.
- **Cyrus Cylinder** — Persian imperial document supports this decree-style; Cyrus granted restoration to many displaced peoples. Historically corroborated.
- **Chronicler's theology** — destruction is covenantal judgment, but not final. Davidic line preserved (Jeconiah released, 2 Kgs 25:27); Sabbath debt paid; Yahweh stirs Gentile king; return begins. Hope, not despair, closes the Hebrew canon.
