# 2 Chronicles 21

[1] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ lay with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David, and ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ his son reigned in his place.

[2] And he had brothers, the sons of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧: ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ and ⟦jehiel|Yechiel|Jehiel⟧ and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ and ⟦azariah|Azaryahu|Azariah⟧ and Michael and ⟦shephatiah|Shefatyah|Shephatiah⟧; all these were the sons of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] And their father gave them many gifts, of silver and of gold and of precious things, together with cities of defense in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; and the kingdom he gave to ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧, for he was the firstborn.

[4] And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ rose up over the kingdom of his father, and he strengthened himself, and he killed all his brothers with the sword, and also some of the princes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and eight years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[6] And he walked in the way of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, as the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ had done, for the daughter of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ was his wife; and he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was not willing to ruin the house of David, on account of the covenant that he had cut for David, and as he had said, to give a lamp to him and to his sons all the days.

[8] In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they made a king reign over them.

[9] And ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ passed over with his commanders, and all the chariots with him. And it came to pass that he arose by night and struck Edom, who was surrounding him, and the commanders of the chariots.

[10] And Edom has revolted from under the hand of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to this day. Then ⟦libnah|Livnah|Libnah⟧ revolted, at that time, from under his hand, for he had forsaken ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|the Elohim|the God⟧ of his fathers.

[11] He also made high places in the mountains of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he made the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ play the prostitute, and he drew ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ away.

[12] And a writing came to him from ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧ the prophet, saying, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|the Elohim|the God⟧ of David your father: 'Because you have not walked in the ways of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ your father and in the ways of Asa king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,

[13] and you have walked in the way of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and have made ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ play the prostitute, as the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ made them play the prostitute, and also your brothers, the house of your father, who were better than you, you have killed:

[14] behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is smiting with a great plague your people and your sons and your wives and all your goods.

[15] And you shall be in many sicknesses, in a sickness of your inward parts, until your inward parts come out because of the sickness, days upon days.'"

[16] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ stirred up against ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ the spirit of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and the ⟦arabians|Arvim|Arabians⟧ who are alongside the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧.

[17] And they went up into ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and split it open, and they took captive all the goods that were found belonging to the house of the king, and also his sons and his wives; and there was not left to him a son except ⟦jehoahaz|Yehoachaz|Jehoahaz⟧, the youngest of his sons.

[18] And after all this ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ smote him in his inward parts with a sickness for which there was no healing.

[19] And it came to pass, as days followed days, and about the time when the end of two years came, that his inward parts came out with his sickness, and he died in evil sicknesses. And his people did not make for him a burning like the burning of his fathers.

[20] He was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and eight years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And he went without being desired, and they buried him in the city of David, but not in the graves of the kings.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallels: 1 Kgs 22:50 (v1) and 2 Kgs 8:17–22 (v5–10)** — where the Hebrew is the same, the wording is that of Kings; every difference named below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Verses 2–4 and 11–20 have no parallel in Kings.
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכַּב. **"the city of David"** — Kings adds "his father".
- **"Azariah … and Azariah"** (v2) — two sons bear the name, written עֲזַרְיָה and עֲזַרְיָהוּ (a shorter and a longer form). The names are joined by "and". **"Jehoshaphat king of Israel"** — so the Masoretic Text, of the king of Judah (as 12:6; 28:19). Some Hebrew manuscripts and the old versions are reported to read "Judah" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"great gifts of silver, and of gold, and of precious things, with fenced cities" → "many gifts, of silver and of gold and of precious things, together with cities of defense"** (v3) — Hebrew מַתָּנוֹת רַבּוֹת; מִגְדָּנוֹת "precious things" (Gen 24:53); עָרֵי מְצֻרוֹת "cities of defense" (11:10, 23; 12:4): places shut in, another word than "fortified" (17:2, 19).
- **"when Jehoram was risen up to the kingdom of his father, he strengthened himself, and slew" → "Jehoram rose up over the kingdom of his father, and he strengthened himself, and he killed"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיָּקָם … עַל; וַיִּתְחַזַּק, the word of 17:1; וַיַּהֲרֹג "kill". **"divers also of the princes of Israel" → "also some of the princes of Israel"** — Hebrew מִשָּׂרֵי; "Israel" as in v2.
- **"Jehoram was thirty and two years old"** (v5, 20) — literally "a son of thirty-two years"; the verb "reign" is used here of the beginning. Kings has no name in the verse and dates the reign by Joram of Israel (2 Kgs 8:16).
- **"for he had the daughter of Ahab to wife" → "for the daughter of Ahab was his wife"** (v6) — Hebrew הָיְתָה לּוֹ אִשָּׁה (Kings: לְאִשָּׁה, "had become his wife"). **"that which was evil" → "the evil"** — Hebrew הָרַע, with the article.
- **"the LORD would not destroy the house of David, because of the covenant that he had made with David, and as he promised to give a light to him and to his sons for ever" → "Yahweh was not willing to ruin the house of David, on account of the covenant that he had cut for David, and as he had said, to give a lamp to him and to his sons all the days"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא־אָבָה; לְהַשְׁחִית "ruin"; כָּרַת לְדָוִיד "cut for David"; אָמַר "said"; כָּל־הַיָּמִים "all the days". נִיר is uncertain: "lamp", or "dominion, a field to till" (1 Kgs 11:36). Kings has "to ruin Judah, on account of David his servant, as he had said to him, to give him a lamp for his sons".
- **"the Edomites revolted from under the dominion of Judah" → "Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew פָּשַׁע אֱדוֹם מִתַּחַת יַד־יְהוּדָה: פָּשַׁע is to break away from a lord (the verb of פֶּשַׁע "transgression"); אֱדוֹם is the people named as one, as in Kings, in plain text. **"made themselves a king" → "made a king reign over them"** — Hebrew וַיַּמְלִיכוּ.
- **"Jehoram went forth with his princes" → "Jehoram passed over with his commanders"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבֹר … עִם־שָׂרָיו; שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander", as in "the commanders of the chariots" at the end of the verse. Kings has צָעִירָה "to Zair" where Chronicles has "with his commanders" (the letters are alike), and the short name Joram. **"smote the Edomites which compassed him in" → "struck Edom, who was surrounding him"**. Kings ends "and the people fled to their tents", which Chronicles lacks.
- **"The same time also did Libnah revolt from under his hand; because he had forsaken the LORD God of his fathers" → "Then Libnah revolted, at that time, from under his hand, for he had forsaken Yahweh, the Elohim of his fathers"** (v10) — "from under his hand" and the reason are not in Kings. עָזַב "forsake" is a word of Chronicles (12:5; 15:2; 24:20).
- **"Moreover he made high places" → "He also made high places"** (v11) — Hebrew גַּם־הוּא "he too". **"caused the inhabitants of Jerusalem to commit fornication, and compelled Judah thereto" → "made the inhabitants of Jerusalem play the prostitute, and he drew Judah away"** — Hebrew וַיֶּזֶן, the causative of זָנָה, the word for unfaithfulness to Yahweh (Exod 34:16); וַיַּדַּח "thrust, draw away" (Deut 13:5, 10; 2 Kgs 17:21). Some manuscripts and versions are reported to read "in the cities of Judah" for "in the mountains" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"there came a writing to him from Elijah the prophet"** (v12) — Hebrew מִכְתָּב "a writing". Elijah is named only here in Chronicles. **"the LORD God of David thy father" → "Yahweh, the Elohim of David your father"**.
- **"hast made Judah … to go a whoring, like to the whoredoms of the house of Ahab" → "have made Judah … play the prostitute, as the house of Ahab made them play the prostitute"** (v13) — Hebrew וַתַּזְנֶה … כְּהַזְנוֹת בֵּית אַחְאָב, the same causative verb twice; "them" is supplied. **"hast slain thy brethren of thy father's house, which were better than thyself" → "your brothers, the house of your father, who were better than you, you have killed"** — the object stands first in the Hebrew; בֵית־אָבִיךָ "the house of your father" stands beside "your brothers" without "of".
- **"with a great plague will the LORD smite thy people, and thy children, and thy wives, and all thy goods" → "Yahweh is smiting with a great plague your people and your sons and your wives and all your goods"** (v14) — Hebrew הִנֵּה יְהוָה נֹגֵף מַגֵּפָה גְדוֹלָה בְּ, a participle with the noun of its own root: "smiting a great smiting on your people"; מַגֵּפָה is a blow from God, a plague. "People", "sons", "wives" and "goods" all have בְּ.
- **"thou shalt have great sickness by disease of thy bowels, until thy bowels fall out by reason of the sickness day by day" → "you shall be in many sicknesses, in a sickness of your inward parts, until your inward parts come out because of the sickness, days upon days"** (v15) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה בָּחֳלָיִים רַבִּים בְּמַחֲלֵה מֵעֶיךָ עַד־יֵצְאוּ מֵעֶיךָ מִן־הַחֹלִי יָמִים עַל־יָמִים. There is no verb in the first half ("and you, in many sicknesses"); "shall be" is supplied. מֵעִים is the inward parts of the belly, the bowels; יָצָא is "go out". יָמִים עַל־יָמִים "days upon days" may also mean "year upon year" (v19).
- **"the Arabians, that were near the Ethiopians" → "the Arabians who are alongside the Cushites"** (v16) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר עַל־יַד כּוּשִׁים, "at the hand of, beside". **"stirred up … the spirit of"** — Hebrew וַיָּעַר … אֵת רוּחַ (1 Chr 5:26).
- **"brake into it, and carried away all the substance that was found in the king's house" → "split it open, and they took captive all the goods that were found belonging to the house of the king"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיִּבְקָעוּהָ "split, break open" (2 Kgs 25:4); וַיִּשְׁבּוּ "take captive", of goods and persons alike; הַנִּמְצָא לְבֵית־הַמֶּלֶךְ "found belonging to the house of the king" (or "in"). **"Jehoahaz, the youngest of his sons"** — Hebrew קְטֹן בָּנָיו, literally "the small one of his sons". He is the Ahaziah of 22:1: the same name with its two parts in the other order.
- **"the LORD smote him in his bowels with an incurable disease" → "Yahweh smote him in his inward parts with a sickness for which there was no healing"** (v18) — Hebrew נְגָפוֹ … בְּמֵעָיו לָחֳלִי לְאֵין מַרְפֵּא, literally "for a sickness, for there being no healing".
- **"in process of time, after the end of two years, his bowels fell out by reason of his sickness: so he died of sore diseases" → "as days followed days, and about the time when the end of two years came, that his inward parts came out with his sickness, and he died in evil sicknesses"** (v19) — Hebrew לְיָמִים מִיָּמִים, literally "to days from days"; וּכְעֵת צֵאת הַקֵּץ לְיָמִים שְׁנַיִם, literally "and about the time of the going out of the end, of two days". יָמִים "days" is also the word for a year (1 Sam 27:7), and "two years" is the common understanding; "two days" (the last two days of the sickness) is also possible, and the Hebrew does not decide. עִם־חָלְיוֹ "with his sickness"; בְּתַחֲלֻאִים רָעִים "in evil sicknesses" (Deut 29:22).
- **"his people made no burning for him" → "his people did not make for him a burning"** (v19) — Hebrew שְׂרֵפָה: the burning of spices at the burial of a king (16:14; Jer 34:5).
- **"departed without being desired" → "he went without being desired"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ בְּלֹא חֶמְדָּה, literally "and he went with no desire": חֶמְדָּה is "desire, what is desirable". It is commonly taken of others (no one desired him, he was not regretted); it may also be read of his own life ("he walked without delight"). **"sepulchres" → "graves"** — Hebrew קִבְרוֹת. Kings says that he "was buried with his fathers in the city of David" (2 Kgs 8:24); Chronicles says "in the city of David, but not in the graves of the kings". Each is translated as it stands.
- **Numbers** (v5, 20) — written out in the ordinary English order.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 8:16-24** — Chronicler expands considerably (v4 fratricide, v11 apostasy, v12-15 Elijah's letter, v16-19 disease).
- **"Jehoshaphat king of Israel"** (v2) — MT, for the king of Judah; the Chronicler calls Judah the true "Israel" (cf. 12:6, 28:19). Some LXX mss, Syriac and Vulgate read "Judah." MT kept.
- **Fratricide** — Jehoram executes his brothers. Typical ANE securing-of-throne but against Davidic norm.
- **Elijah's letter** — unique to Chronicler. Chronologically difficult: Elijah was translated around Jehoshaphat's time (2 Kgs 2); but Chronicles' chronology is flexible, or Elijah's letter was written earlier and delivered now.
- **"Better than you"** (v13) — the six brothers deserved the throne more than Jehoram.
- **Philistine/Arabian raid** — destroys royal family except Ahaziah (=Jehoahaz here, same person, name-variant).
- **Ignominious death** — bowel disease (dysentery?); no royal burial-burning (v19); not buried in royal tombs (v20). Chronicler's message: even Davidic kings face full judgment for apostasy.
- **Lamp of David preserved** — despite devastation, one son survives. Covenant holds by a thread.
