## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v3).
- **"princes" → "officials / commanders"** (v4, 9) — *sarim*.
- **"Ethiopians" → "Cushites"** (v16).
- **"departed without being desired"** (v20) — Hebrew *ba-lo chemdah*; left without regret.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 8:16-24** — Chronicler expands considerably (v4 fratricide, v11 apostasy, v12-15 Elijah's letter, v16-19 disease).
- **"Jehoshaphat king of Israel"** (v2) — MT, for the king of Judah; the Chronicler calls Judah the true "Israel" (cf. 12:6, 28:19). Some LXX mss, Syriac and Vulgate read "Judah." MT kept.
- **Fratricide** — Jehoram executes his brothers. Typical ANE securing-of-throne but against Davidic norm.
- **Elijah's letter** — unique to Chronicler. Chronologically difficult: Elijah was translated around Jehoshaphat's time (2 Kgs 2); but Chronicles' chronology is flexible, or Elijah's letter was written earlier and delivered now.
- **"Better than yourself"** (v13) — the six brothers deserved the throne more than Jehoram.
- **Philistine/Arabian raid** — destroys royal family except Ahaziah (=Jehoahaz here, same person, name-variant).
- **Ignominious death** — bowel disease (dysentery?); no royal burial-burning (v19); not buried in royal tombs (v20). Chronicler's message: even Davidic kings face full judgment for apostasy.
- **Lamp of David preserved** — despite devastation, one son survives. Covenant holds by a thread.
