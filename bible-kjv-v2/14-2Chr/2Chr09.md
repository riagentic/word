## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"prove" → "test"** (v1).
- **"peacocks" → "baboons"** (v21) — *tukkiyim* disputed; same debate as 1 Kgs 10:22.
- **"targets" → "large shields"** (v15); **"shields" → "small shields"** (v16) — *tzinnot / maginnim*.
- **"harness" → "weapons"** (v24) — *nesheq*.
- **"the river" → "the Euphrates River"** (v26) — *ha-nahar*.
- **"low plains" → "Shephelah"** (v27).
- **"book of Nathan the prophet... visions of Iddo" → "Records of Nathan the Prophet... Visions of Iddo the Seer"** (v29).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 10** — Chronicler reproduces, tightening. Omits 1 Kgs 10:28-29 on horse-import pricing.
- **Queen of Sheba** — Saba; modern Yemen. Jesus references her wisdom-seeking (Matt 12:42).
- **"Throne of Yahweh"** (v8) — Chronicler's theology: Solomon sits on Yahweh's throne. Davidic king is Yahweh's viceroy.
- **666 talents** (v13) — famously referenced Rev 13:18. Likely original wealth-marker of excessive power.
- **Deut 17 violations** — Chronicler simply reports Solomon's horses, gold, international alliances without condemnation. 1 Kgs 11 (not in Chronicles) records the resulting apostasy.
- **Chronicler omits Solomon's decline** — entire 1 Kgs 11 (foreign wives, idolatry, adversaries) is absent. Solomon's kingdom passes to Rehoboam without explanation. Chronicler presents an idealized Solomon.
- **Three sources cited** — Nathan, Ahijah, Iddo. Prophet-historians; several of Chronicles' source-works are otherwise lost.
