# 2 Chronicles 13

[1] In the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam, Abijah began to reign over Judah.

[2] He reigned three years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Micaiah the daughter of Uriel of Gibeah. There was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.

[3] Abijah joined battle with an army of valiant warriors — four hundred thousand chosen men. Jeroboam drew up in battle array against him with eight hundred thousand chosen men, mighty warriors.

[4] Abijah stood up on Mount Zemaraim, which is in the hill country of Ephraim, and said: "Hear me, Jeroboam and all Israel!

[5] Do you not know that Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel gave the kingdom over Israel to David forever — to him and to his sons by a berit melach — covenant of salt?

[6] Yet Jeroboam son of Nebat, a servant of Solomon son of David, rose up and rebelled against his lord.

[7] Worthless men, sons of Belial, gathered to him and strengthened themselves against Rehoboam son of Solomon, when Rehoboam was young and irresolute and could not withstand them.

[8] Now you think to withstand the kingdom of Yahweh in the hand of the sons of David, because you are a great multitude and have with you the golden calves that Jeroboam made you for gods.

[9] Have you not driven out the priests of Yahweh, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, and made priests for yourselves as the peoples of other lands do? Whoever comes to consecrate himself with a young bull and seven rams may become a priest of what are not gods.

[10] But as for us, Yahweh is our *Elohim*; we have not forsaken him. Our priests, who minister to Yahweh, are sons of Aaron; the Levites attend to their work.

[11] They burn to Yahweh every morning and every evening burnt offerings and sweet incense; they set the bread of the Presence in order on the pure table, and the golden lampstand with its lamps to burn every evening; for we keep the charge of Yahweh our *Elohim*. But you have forsaken him.

[12] Behold, *Elohim* is with us at our head, and his priests with the alarm-trumpets to sound the alarm against you. O children of Israel, do not fight against Yahweh the *Elohim* of your fathers, for you shall not succeed."

[13] But Jeroboam had sent an ambush around to come in from behind them; so they were in front of Judah, and the ambush was behind them.

[14] When Judah looked back, behold, the battle was in front and behind. They cried out to Yahweh, and the priests blew the trumpets.

[15] Then the men of Judah raised a shout; as the men of Judah shouted, *Elohim* struck Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah.

[16] The children of Israel fled before Judah, and *Elohim* gave them into their hand.

[17] Abijah and his people struck them with a great blow; five hundred thousand chosen men of Israel fell slain.

[18] Thus the children of Israel were subdued at that time, and the children of Judah prevailed, because they relied on Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers.

[19] Abijah pursued after Jeroboam and took cities from him: Bethel with its villages, Jeshanah with its villages, and Ephron with its villages.

[20] Jeroboam did not recover strength again in the days of Abijah; Yahweh struck him, and he died.

[21] But Abijah grew strong; he took fourteen wives and fathered twenty-two sons and sixteen daughters.

[22] The rest of the acts of Abijah, his ways, and his sayings are written in the commentary of the prophet Iddo.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"tenderhearted" → "irresolute"** (v7) — *rak-levav*.
- **"captain" → "at our head"** (v12) — *be-rosh*.
- **"shewbread" → "bread of the Presence"** (v11).
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"** (v11) — *menorah*.
- **"Ephrain" → "Ephron"** (v19) — Qere.

### Notes
- **Chronicler expands** — 1 Kgs 15:1-8 gives Abijam a negative two-verse notice; Chronicler calls him Abijah and makes him the spokesman of Davidic covenant theology.
- **Double-sized 1 Kgs vs 2 Chr portraits** — Chronicles accentuates Abijah's single battle as religious triumph.
- **"Covenant of salt"** — unbreakable; preserves. Num 18:19, Lev 2:13.
- **Non-Levite priesthood polemic** — a pig-offering sacrilege joke: "comes with a young bull and seven rams" mockingly echoes priestly consecration; north's priests could be anyone (1 Kgs 12:31).
- **500,000 casualties** — hyperbolic, typical of ancient war accounts.
- **Bethel captured** — Judah briefly takes the northern cult center. Later retaken.
- **Iddo's *Midrash*** — a commentary-genre source. Chronicler's sources include interpretive writings.
