# 2 Chronicles 27

[1] ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. His mother's name was ⟦jerushah|Yerushah|Jerushah⟧ daughter of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧.

[2] He did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to all that his father ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ had done; however, he did not enter the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. The people still acted corruptly.

[3] He built the Upper Gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; on the wall of the ⟦ophel|Ofel|Ophel⟧ he built extensively.

[4] Moreover, he built cities in the hill country of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; in the forests he built fortresses and towers.

[5] He fought with the king of the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧ and prevailed against them. The children of Ammon gave him that year a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand kor of wheat, and ten thousand of barley; this the children of Ammon paid him also in the second and third years.

[6] So ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ became mighty, because he ordered his ways before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[7] The rest of the acts of ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧, all his wars, and his ways, behold, they are written in the Book of the Kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[8] He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and reigned sixteen years in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[9] ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"castles" → "fortresses"** (v4) — *biraniyyot*.
- **"prepared" → "ordered"** (v6) — *hekhin*, "set firm, put in order."

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 15:32-38** — Chronicler adds Jotham's restraint ("did not enter the temple," contrast with Uzziah), Ammonite war, building projects.
- **"Did not enter the temple"** — deliberate contrast with his father Uzziah's transgression. Jotham respects priestly boundary.
- **"People still acted corruptly"** — good king doesn't automatically produce righteous nation.
- **Ammonite subjugation** — tribute: 100 talents silver + 10,000 *kor* wheat + 10,000 *kor* barley. Economic power.
- **Building Upper Gate and Ophel wall** — fortification program; Ophel = the hill between the temple and the City of David.
- **Brief chapter** — Jotham gets less space than his father Uzziah, though comparable virtues. Chronicler simply doesn't have more material.
