# 2 Chronicles 27

[1] ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and sixteen years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦jerushah|Yerushah|Jerushah⟧ the daughter of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧.

[2] And he did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to all that ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ his father had done; only, he did not go into the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And the people were still working ruin.

[3] He built the upper gate of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and on the wall of the ⟦ophel|Ofel|Ophel⟧ he built in abundance.

[4] And cities he built in the hill country of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and in the wooded places he built citadels and towers.

[5] And he fought with the king of the sons of Ammon and was strong over them. And the sons of Ammon gave him in that year a hundred talents of silver and ten thousand kors of wheat, and of barley ten thousand. This the sons of Ammon returned to him, and in the second year and the third.

[6] And ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ strengthened himself, for he established his ways before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[7] And the rest of the acts of ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧, and all his wars and his ways, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[8] He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and sixteen years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[9] And ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ lay with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David, and ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"was five and twenty years old" → "was twenty-five years old"** (v1, 8) — literally "a son of twenty and five years"; the number in ordinary English order. "Began to reign" is the verb מָלַךְ "reign", here of the beginning.
- **"howbeit he entered not" → "only, he did not go into"** (v2) — רַק "only", as in 2 Kgs 15:35; בָּא אֶל "go into".
- **"the people did yet corruptly" → "the people were still working ruin"** (v2) — מַשְׁחִיתִים, the participle of הִשְׁחִית "ruin" with no object; "acted corruptly" is the other common rendering. Kings has other words here ("the people were still sacrificing and making smoke on the high places"); nothing from Kings is brought in.
- **"he built much" → "he built in abundance"** (v3) — לָרֹב.
- **"in the forests he built castles" → "in the wooded places he built citadels"** (v4) — חֳרָשִׁים, another word than יַעַר "forest"; בִּירָנִיּוֹת (only here and 2 Chr 17:12), from בִּירָה "citadel".
- **"prevailed against them" → "was strong over them"** (v5) — וַיֶּחֱזַק עֲלֵיהֶם, the verb חָזַק "be strong".
- **"the children of Ammon" → "the sons of Ammon"** (v5) — בְּנֵי עַמּוֹן, three times.
- **"ten thousand measures of wheat" → "ten thousand kors of wheat"** (v5) — כֹּרִים, the measure kept. With the barley the Hebrew does not repeat the measure.
- **"So much did the children of Ammon pay unto him, both the second year, and the third" → "This the sons of Ammon returned to him, and in the second year and the third"** (v5) — הֵשִׁיבוּ "returned, brought back"; וּ "and" = also.
- **"became mighty" → "strengthened himself"** (v6) — וַיִּתְחַזֵּק, the reflexive of חָזַק, one rendering through Chronicles.
- **"prepared his ways" → "established his ways"** (v6) — הֵכִין "make firm, establish".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v9) — שָׁכַב "lie".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 15:32-38** — v1–2a, 3a, 7, 9 stand beside the Kings verses and are worded like them where the Hebrew is the same. Chronicles alone has "he did not go into the temple", the building (v3b–4), the war with Ammon (v5), v6 and v8; it has "the book of the kings of Israel and Judah" where Kings names "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah", and "behold, they are written" where Kings asks "are they not written".
- **"Did not go into the temple"** — set against his father Uzziah, who went into the temple to burn incense (26:16).
- **Ammon** — a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand kors of wheat and ten thousand of barley, for three years.
- **Upper gate and Ophel wall** — the Ophel is the hill between the temple and the City of David.
- **v8** repeats v1a; Kings has no such second notice.
