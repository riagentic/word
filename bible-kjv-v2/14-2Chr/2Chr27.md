# 2 Chronicles 27

[1] Jotham was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jerushah daughter of Zadok.

[2] He did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that his father Uzziah had done; however, he did not enter the temple of Yahweh. The people still acted corruptly.

[3] He built the Upper Gate of the house of Yahweh; on the wall of the Ophel he built extensively.

[4] Moreover, he built cities in the hill country of Judah; in the forests he built fortresses and towers.

[5] He fought with the king of the Ammonites and prevailed against them. The children of Ammon gave him that year a hundred talents of silver, ten thousand kor of wheat, and ten thousand of barley; this the children of Ammon paid him also in the second and third years.

[6] So Jotham became mighty, because he ordered his ways before Yahweh his *Elohim*.

[7] The rest of the acts of Jotham, all his wars, and his ways, behold, they are written in the Book of the Kings of Israel and Judah.

[8] He was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem.

[9] Jotham slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; Ahaz his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"castles" → "fortresses"** (v4) — *biraniyyot*.
- **"prepared" → "ordered"** (v6) — *hekhin*, "set firm, put in order."

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 15:32-38** — Chronicler adds Jotham's restraint ("did not enter the temple," contrast with Uzziah), Ammonite war, building projects.
- **"Did not enter the temple"** — deliberate contrast with his father Uzziah's transgression. Jotham respects priestly boundary.
- **"People still acted corruptly"** — good king doesn't automatically produce righteous nation.
- **Ammonite subjugation** — tribute: 100 talents silver + 10,000 *kor* wheat + 10,000 *kor* barley. Economic power.
- **Building Upper Gate and Ophel wall** — fortification program; Ophel = the hill between the temple and the City of David.
- **Brief chapter** — Jotham gets less space than his father Uzziah, though comparable virtues. Chronicler simply doesn't have more material.
