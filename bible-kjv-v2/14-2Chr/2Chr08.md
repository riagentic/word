# 2 Chronicles 8

[1] And it came to pass at the end of twenty years, in which ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had built the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and his house,

[2] that the cities that ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ had given to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧, ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ built them, and he made the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ dwell there.

[3] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ went to ⟦hamath-zobah|Chamat-tzovah|Hamath-zobah⟧ and was strong against it.

[4] And he built Tadmor in the wilderness, and all the store cities that he built in ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧.

[5] And he built Upper ⟦beth-horon|Beit Choron|Beth-horon⟧ and Lower ⟦beth-horon|Beit Choron|Beth-horon⟧, cities of defense: walls, doors and a bar;

[6] and ⟦baalath|Baalat|Baalath⟧, and all the store cities that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had, and all the cities of the chariots and the cities of the horsemen, and all the longing of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ that he longed to build in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ and in all the land of his rule.

[7] All the people who remained from the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦hivites|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧, who were not of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧

[8] — of their sons who remained after them in the land, whom the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had not made an end of — ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ brought them up for forced labor, to this day.

[9] And of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whom ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ did not make slaves for his work — for they were men of war and the commanders of his officers and the commanders of his chariots and his horsemen.

[10] And these were the chiefs of the prefects whom King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had, two hundred and fifty, who had dominion over the people.

[11] And the daughter of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ brought up from the city of David to the house that he had built for her, for he said, "A wife of mine shall not dwell in the house of David king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for they are holy, those to which the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has come."

[12] Then ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ offered up burnt offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that he had built before the porch,

[13] and by the matter of a day in a day, to offer up according to the commandment of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, for the ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ and for the months and for the appointed times, three times in the year: at the Feast of ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ and at the Feast of ⟦weeks|Shavuot|Weeks⟧ and at the Feast of ⟦booths|Sukkot|Booths⟧.

[14] And he made stand, according to the ordinance of David his father, the divisions of the priests over their service, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ at their charges, to praise and to minister in front of the priests, for the matter of a day in its day, and the gatekeepers in their divisions for gate and gate; for so was the commandment of David, the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[15] And they did not turn aside from the commandment of the king concerning the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, for any matter and for the treasuries.

[16] And all the work of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ was established, to the day of the founding of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and to its finishing; the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was complete.

[17] Then ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ went to ⟦ezion-geber|Etzyon Gever|Ezion-geber⟧ and to ⟦eloth|Eilot|Eloth⟧, on the shore of the sea, in the land of Edom.

[18] And ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ sent to him by the hand of his servants ships, and servants who knew the sea; and they came with the servants of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ to ⟦ophir|Ofir|Ophir⟧ and took from there four hundred and fifty talents of gold and brought them to King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v14 with the article, אִישׁ־הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **The parallel in Kings** (throughout) — the chapter runs beside 1 Kgs 9:10–28, with much of its own. Where the Hebrew of the two books is the same, the wording is that of 1 Kings 9; where Chronicles has another word, form, number or order, the verse follows Chronicles. The differences that touch the meaning are named below.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"And it came to pass"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי.
- **"wherein Solomon had built the house of the LORD, and his own house" → "in which Solomon had built the house of Yahweh and his house"** (v1) — Hebrew וְאֶת־בֵּיתוֹ (Kings: "the two houses, the house of Yahweh and the house of the king").
- **"That the cities which Huram had restored to Solomon, Solomon built them, and caused the children of Israel to dwell there" → "that the cities that Churam had given to Solomon, Solomon built them, and he made the sons of Israel dwell there"** (v2) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר נָתַן חוּרָם לִשְׁלֹמֹה: "gave", not "restored". In 1 Kgs 9:11–13 it is Solomon who gives twenty cities to Hiram; Chronicles says only what stands here, and the two are not brought together in the verse. The name is חוּרָם in Chronicles (Kings: חִירָם).
- **"prevailed against it" → "was strong against it"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיֶּחֱזַק עָלֶיהָ: חָזַק "be strong"; he overcame it. V3–4a stand only in Chronicles.
- **"which he built in Hamath" kept** (v4) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר בָּנָה בַּחֲמָת. Kings has "Tadmor in the wilderness, in the land" (its written text has "Tamar").
- **"fenced cities, with walls, gates, and bars" → "cities of defense: walls, doors and a bar"** (v5) — Hebrew עָרֵי מָצוֹר: מָצוֹר is "siege" and a place shut in against it, as 11:5 ("fortified" is another word, מִבְצָר); חוֹמוֹת דְּלָתַיִם וּבְרִיחַ, as Deut 3:5; the Hebrew has no "with", and "bar" is singular. Kings names only Lower Beth-horon (and Gezer).
- **"chariot cities … all that Solomon desired to build" → "the cities of the chariots … all the longing of Solomon that he longed to build"** (v6) — Hebrew חֵשֶׁק … אֲשֶׁר חָשַׁק, noun and verb of one root. "Store cities" is עָרֵי הַמִּסְכְּנוֹת (Exod 1:11); "rule" is מֶמְשָׁלָה. Chronicles has "all" three times where Kings has it once.
- **"the Hittites, and the Amorites …" → "the Hittite and the Amorite …"** (v7) — the Hebrew names each people in the singular with the article; Chronicles puts the Hittite first and joins all five with "and"; "who were not of Israel" (Kings: "of the sons of Israel").
- **"of their children, who were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel consumed not, them did Solomon make to pay tribute" → "of their sons who remained after them in the land, whom the sons of Israel had not made an end of — Solomon brought them up for forced labor"** (v8) — Hebrew מִן־בְּנֵיהֶם "of their sons"; נוֹתַר "remain"; לֹא־כִלּוּם, כִּלָּה "make an end of" (Kings: "were not able to devote to destruction"); וַיַּעֲלֵם … לְמַס, literally "made them go up for forced labor" (Kings: "to be slaves at forced labor"). מַס is forced labor, not a tax.
- **"But of the children of Israel did Solomon make no servants for his work; but they were men of war, and chief of his captains, and captains of his chariots and horsemen" → "And of the sons of Israel, whom Solomon did not make slaves for his work — for they were men of war and the commanders of his officers and the commanders of his chariots and his horsemen"** (v9) — Hebrew וּמִן־בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר לֹא־נָתַן שְׁלֹמֹה לַעֲבָדִים: the אֲשֶׁר "whom" leaves the sentence without a main clause; it is kept as it stands (Kings has no אֲשֶׁר: "Solomon made no one a slave"). נָתַן is "give"; עֲבָדִים "slaves". שָׂרֵי שָׁלִישָׁיו "the commanders of his officers" (Kings: "his servants and his commanders and his officers"); שָׁלִישׁ is literally "third man" (uncertain).
- **"the chief of king Solomon's officers, even two hundred and fifty, that bare rule over the people" → "the chiefs of the prefects whom King Solomon had, two hundred and fifty, who had dominion over the people"** (v10) — the reading tradition (qere) has הַנִּצָּבִים "the prefects" (those set in place), as 1 Kgs 9:23; the written form (ketiv) is הנציבים, the word of "garrison, prefect" (1 Kgs 4:19). רָדָה "have dominion". Chronicles has two hundred and fifty; Kings has five hundred and fifty. Each number is as printed.
- **"And Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh … unto the house" → "And the daughter of Pharaoh Solomon brought up … to the house"** (v11) — the Hebrew puts the object first. In Kings she "went up … to her house"; the reason given in the second half of the verse stands only in Chronicles.
- **"My wife shall not dwell in the house of David king of Israel, because the places are holy, whereunto the ark of the LORD hath come" → "A wife of mine shall not dwell in the house of David king of Israel, for they are holy, those to which the ark of Yahweh has come"** (v11) — Hebrew לֹא־תֵשֵׁב אִשָּׁה לִי, literally "a woman / wife shall not dwell for me": "a wife of mine" (or "no wife shall dwell, as far as I am concerned"). כִּי־קֹדֶשׁ הֵמָּה: "holy" is the noun "holiness"; "they" is plural and has no noun — "places" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"offered burnt offerings" → "offered up burnt offerings"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew הֶעֱלָה עֹלוֹת, literally "made go up goings-up"; one root. "Porch" is אוּלָם. Kings (9:25) has "three times in the year burnt offerings and peace offerings on the altar that he had built for Yahweh, and make smoke with it"; v13–16a stand only in Chronicles.
- **"Even after a certain rate every day, offering according to the commandment of Moses, on the sabbaths, and on the new moons, and on the solemn feasts, three times in the year" → "and by the matter of a day in a day, to offer up according to the commandment of Moses, for the Shabbatot and for the months and for the appointed times, three times in the year"** (v13) — Hebrew וּבִדְבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹם, word for word (v14 has the fuller דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ, as Exod 5:13; 1 Kgs 8:59): what each day called for. חֳדָשִׁים is "months"; the new moon, the first day of the month, is meant (Num 28:11). מוֹעֲדוֹת "appointed times". The Hebrew has לְ "for" three times and בְּ "at" three times.
- **"the feast of unleavened bread … the feast of weeks … the feast of tabernacles" → "the Feast of Matzot … the Feast of Shavuot … the Feast of Sukkot"** (v13) — Hebrew חַג הַמַּצּוֹת, חַג הַשָּׁבֻעוֹת, חַג הַסֻּכּוֹת, as Deut 16:16.
- **"he appointed, according to the order of David his father, the courses of the priests to their service, and the Levites to their charges" → "he made stand, according to the ordinance of David his father, the divisions of the priests over their service, and the Levites at their charges"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֵד, literally "made stand" (1 Chr 6:31); כְּמִשְׁפַּט "according to the judgment / ordinance of"; מַחְלְקוֹת "divisions" (1 Chr 23:6); מִשְׁמְרוֹת "charges" (7:6).
- **"before the priests, as the duty of every day required: the porters also by their courses at every gate" → "in front of the priests, for the matter of a day in its day, and the gatekeepers in their divisions for gate and gate"** (v14) — Hebrew נֶגֶד; לִדְבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ; לְשַׁעַר וָשָׁעַר.
- **"for so had David the man of God commanded" → "for so was the commandment of David, the man of Elohim"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי כֵן מִצְוַת דָּוִיד, a noun, the word of v13 and v15.
- **"they departed not from the commandment of the king unto the priests and Levites concerning any matter, or concerning the treasures" → "they did not turn aside from the commandment of the king concerning the priests and the Levites, for any matter and for the treasuries"** (v15) — Hebrew וְלֹא סָרוּ מִצְוַת הַמֶּלֶךְ עַל: the Hebrew has no "from" before "the commandment"; עַל "concerning" (or "upon"); אֹצָרוֹת "treasuries".
- **"Now all the work of Solomon was prepared unto the day of the foundation of the house of the LORD, and until it was finished. So the house of the LORD was perfected" → "And all the work of Solomon was established, to the day of the founding of the house of Yahweh and to its finishing; the house of Yahweh was complete"** (v16) — Hebrew וַתִּכֹּן, of the root "establish, make ready"; עַד־הַיּוֹם מוּסַד … וְעַד־כְּלֹתוֹ, "as far as the day of the founding … and as far as its finishing", word for word: the sentence is hard, and "from the day" (so the old versions, from memory) is not in the Masoretic Text. שָׁלֵם "whole, complete" (1 Kgs 9:25: "and he completed the house").
- **"Then went Solomon to Ezion-geber, and to Eloth, at the sea side" → "Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Eloth, on the shore of the sea"** (v17) — Hebrew עַל־שְׂפַת הַיָּם, "lip". Chronicles has "the sea" (Kings: "the Sea of Reeds") and says that Solomon went there; Kings says that he made a fleet there.
- **"And Huram sent him by the hands of his servants ships, and servants that had knowledge of the sea" → "And Churam sent to him by the hand of his servants ships, and servants who knew the sea"** (v18) — Hebrew בְּיַד "by the hand of", singular; "ships" is the qere אֳנִיּוֹת (the ketiv is the fuller spelling of the same word). Kings: "sent in the fleet his servants, men of ships who knew the sea".
- **"four hundred and fifty talents of gold" kept** (v18) — Kings has four hundred and twenty. Each number is as printed.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 9:10–28** — Chronicles has the cities given by Churam (v2), Hamath-zobah (v3), the reason for the house of the daughter of Pharaoh (v11) and the order of the service (v13–16); it does not have Hiram's displeasure, Cabul, the gold, the Millo or Gezer (1 Kgs 9:11–16, 24b).
- **Tadmor** (v4) — in the wilderness; with the store cities of Hamath.
- **The three feasts** (v13) — Matzot, Shavuot, Sukkot (Exod 23:14–17; Deut 16:16).
- **The numbers** (v10, 18) — 250 chiefs (1 Kgs 9:23: 550); 450 talents (1 Kgs 9:28: 420).
