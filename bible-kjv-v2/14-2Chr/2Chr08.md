# 2 Chronicles 8

[1] At the end of the twenty years during which Solomon had built the house of Yahweh and his own house,

[2] Solomon rebuilt the cities that Huram had given him, and caused the children of Israel to dwell there.

[3] Solomon went to Hamath-zobah and prevailed against it.

[4] He built Tadmor in the wilderness and all the store cities he built in Hamath.

[5] He also built Upper Beth-horon and Lower Beth-horon, fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars;

[6] and Baalath and all the store cities that Solomon had, all the chariot cities, the cities of the horsemen, and everything that Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and throughout all the land of his dominion.

[7] As for all the people left of the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, who were not of Israel —

[8] their descendants who were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel had not destroyed — Solomon conscripted to forced labor, as it is to this day.

[9] But of the children of Israel, Solomon made no slaves for his work; they were warriors, his officers, commanders, commanders of his chariots, and of his horsemen.

[10] These were the chief officials of King Solomon, two hundred fifty, who had authority over the people.

[11] Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh from the city of David to the house that he had built for her, for he said, "My wife shall not live in the house of David king of Israel, because the places to which the ark of Yahweh has come are holy."

[12] Then Solomon offered burnt offerings to Yahweh on the altar of Yahweh that he had built before the portico,

[13] as each day required — offering according to the commandment of Moses, on the Sabbaths, the new moons, and the appointed feasts three times a year: the Feast of Unleavened Bread, the Feast of Weeks, and the Feast of Booths.

[14] He appointed, according to the ordinance of David his father, the divisions of the priests for their service, and the Levites for their duties — to praise and to minister before the priests as each day required; also the gatekeepers by their divisions at every gate; for so had David the man of *Elohim* commanded.

[15] They did not depart from the commandment of the king to the priests and Levites concerning any matter or concerning the treasuries.

[16] All the work of Solomon was carried out from the day the foundation of the house of Yahweh was laid until it was finished; so the house of Yahweh was perfected.

[17] Then Solomon went to Ezion-geber and to Eloth on the seashore in the land of Edom.

[18] Huram sent him ships by the hand of his servants, servants who knew the sea; they went with Solomon's servants to Ophir and brought from there four hundred fifty talents of gold, and brought them to King Solomon.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v5).
- **"tribute" → "forced labor"** (v8) — *mas oved*.
- **"no servants" → "no slaves"** (v9) — *avadim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v14) — *sho'arim*.
- **"solemn feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (v13) — *mo'adim*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 9:10-28** — Chronicler modifies subtly. Most notably: 1 Kgs 9:11-14 has Solomon giving Hiram twenty cities (which Hiram disliked); Chronicler reverses it — *Huram* gave cities to Solomon. Likely reflects a different tradition or deliberate reframing.
- **Hamath-zobah campaign** (v3) — Chronicler alone records. Extends Solomon's rule into northern Syria.
- **Tadmor** — later Palmyra; oasis on trade route. Attributed to Solomon (traditional) or Tadmor of Yehuda (alternative).
- **Pharaoh's daughter moved** (v11) — house of David too holy for Egyptian queen; separate palace. Chronicler preserves what some later rabbis viewed as cultic scrupulosity.
- **No Israelite slavery** (v9) — Chronicler emphasizes this; only foreigners conscripted. Answers tension with 1 Kgs 5:13 (where Solomon did use Israelite corvée).
- **Three pilgrimage feasts** (v13) — Pesach/Massot, Shavuot, Sukkot.
- **Ezion-geber / Eloth** — Red Sea ports, for Ophir gold trade.
- **450 talents of gold** — 1 Kgs 9:28 = 420 talents. Scribal variant.
