# 2 Chronicles 18

[1] Jehoshaphat had riches and honor in abundance, and allied himself by marriage with Ahab.

[2] After some years he went down to Ahab at Samaria. Ahab slaughtered sheep and oxen for him in abundance, and for the people with him, and induced him to go up against Ramoth-gilead.

[3] Ahab king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, "Will you go with me to Ramoth-gilead?" He answered him, "I am as you are, my people as your people; we will be with you in the war."

[4] Jehoshaphat said to the king of Israel, "Inquire, please, for the word of Yahweh today."

[5] Therefore the king of Israel gathered the prophets — four hundred men — and said to them, "Shall we go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I refrain?" They said, "Go up; for *Elohim* will deliver it into the king's hand."

[6] But Jehoshaphat said, "Is there no longer a prophet of Yahweh here, of whom we may inquire?"

[7] The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "There is still one man through whom we may inquire of Yahweh, but I hate him; for he never prophesies good concerning me, but always evil. He is Micaiah son of Imlah." Jehoshaphat said, "Let the king not say so."

[8] The king of Israel called one of his eunuchs and said, "Bring quickly Micaiah son of Imlah."

[9] The king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah each sat on his throne, clothed in their robes, and were sitting on the threshing floor at the entrance of the gate of Samaria; all the prophets were prophesying before them.

[10] Zedekiah son of Chenaanah had made himself horns of iron, and said, "Thus says Yahweh: 'With these you shall gore Aram until they are destroyed.'"

[11] All the prophets prophesied so, saying, "Go up to Ramoth-gilead and prosper; Yahweh will deliver it into the king's hand."

[12] The messenger who went to call Micaiah spoke to him, saying, "Behold, the words of the prophets with one voice declare good to the king. Let your word therefore be like one of theirs, and speak good."

[13] Micaiah said, "As Yahweh lives, what my *Elohim* says, that I will speak."

[14] When he came to the king, the king said to him, "Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I refrain?" He said, "Go up and prosper; they shall be given into your hand."

[15] The king said to him, "How many times must I adjure you that you speak nothing but truth to me in the name of Yahweh?"

[16] Then he said, "I saw all Israel scattered on the mountains, like sheep that have no shepherd; Yahweh said, 'These have no master; let each return to his house in peace.'"

[17] The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "Did I not tell you that he would not prophesy good concerning me, but evil?"

[18] He said, "Therefore hear the word of Yahweh: I saw Yahweh sitting on his throne, with all the host of heaven standing at his right hand and at his left.

[19] Yahweh said, 'Who will entice Ahab king of Israel to go up and fall at Ramoth-gilead?' One spoke saying one thing, and another saying another.

[20] Then a spirit came forward and stood before Yahweh, and said, 'I will entice him.' Yahweh said, 'By what means?'

[21] He said, 'I will go out and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets.' He said, 'You shall entice him, and you shall also prevail; go out and do so.'

[22] Now therefore, behold, Yahweh has put a lying spirit in the mouth of these your prophets; Yahweh has spoken disaster against you."

[23] Then Zedekiah son of Chenaanah came near and struck Micaiah on the cheek, and said, "Which way did the spirit of Yahweh go from me to speak to you?"

[24] Micaiah said, "Behold, you shall see on that day when you go into an inner chamber to hide yourself."

[25] The king of Israel said, "Take Micaiah and return him to Amon the governor of the city and to Joash the king's son;

[26] say, 'Thus says the king: Put this one in prison, and feed him with bread of affliction and water of affliction, until I return in peace.'"

[27] Micaiah said, "If you return at all in peace, Yahweh has not spoken by me." And he said, "Hear, all you peoples!"

[28] So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah went up to Ramoth-gilead.

[29] The king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, "I will disguise myself and go into battle, but you wear your robes." So the king of Israel disguised himself, and they went into battle.

[30] The king of Aram had commanded the chariot commanders who were with him, saying, "Fight with no one small or great, but only with the king of Israel."

[31] When the chariot commanders saw Jehoshaphat, they said, "It is the king of Israel." So they surrounded him to fight; but Jehoshaphat cried out, and Yahweh helped him; *Elohim* moved them to depart from him.

[32] When the chariot commanders saw that it was not the king of Israel, they turned back from pursuing him.

[33] A certain man drew his bow without taking aim and struck the king of Israel between the scale-armor and the breastplate. He said to his chariot-driver, "Turn around, and carry me out of the camp, for I am wounded."

[34] The battle grew hot that day; the king of Israel held himself up in his chariot facing the Arameans until evening; about the time of sunset he died.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- Largely parallel to 1 Kgs 22 with same translation choices (Aram, *ruach*, threshing floor, scale-armor, etc.)
- **"officers" → "eunuchs"** (v8) — *sarisim*.
- **"void place" → "threshing floor"** (v9).
- **"persuaded" → "induced"** (v2).
- **"Yahweh helped him" / "*Elohim* moved them"** (v31) — Chronicler's expansion over 1 Kgs 22; emphasizes divine preservation of Jehoshaphat.
- **"all ye people" → "all you peoples"** (v27) — *ammim kullam*, plural.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 22:1-35** — Chronicler reproduces with minor shifts.
- **Marriage alliance** (v1) — Jehoshaphat's son Jehoram marries Athaliah daughter of Ahab-Jezebel (ch 21). Chronicler marks this as the fatal compromise.
- **Divine rescue of Jehoshaphat** (v31) — Chronicler's addition: "Yahweh helped him." Even in a bad alliance, Yahweh rescues the basically faithful king.
- **400 prophets** — court prophets, Yahweh-named but compromised. Micaiah alone stands.
- **Divine council / lying spirit** — OT depiction of Yahweh's heavenly court orchestrating judgment.
- **Arrow "without taking aim"** (v33) — providence executing Yahweh's decree (1 Kgs 21 oracle on Ahab).
- **Chronicler's agenda** — presents Jehoshaphat's role with redeeming flourish; Ahab dies as judgment; Jehoshaphat survives by Yahweh's intervention.
