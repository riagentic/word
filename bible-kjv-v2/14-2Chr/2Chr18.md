# 2 Chronicles 18

[1] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ had riches and glory in abundance, and he intermarried with ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧.

[2] And at the end of some years he came down to ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ slaughtered for him flock and herd in abundance, and for the people who were with him, and he incited him to go up to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧.

[3] And ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, "Will you go with me to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧?" And he said to him, "As I am, so are you; and as your people, so my people; and with you in the battle."

[4] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "Seek, please, today the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[5] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ gathered the prophets, four hundred men, and said to them, "Shall we go to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧ to battle, or shall I cease?" And they said, "Go up, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will give it into the hand of the king."

[6] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said, "Is there not here a prophet of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ still, that we may seek from him?"

[7] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "There is still one man to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from him, and I hate him, for he is not prophesying concerning me for good, but all his days for evil: he is ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ the son of ⟦imlah|Yimlah|Imlah⟧." And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ said, "Let not the king say so."

[8] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ called to one eunuch and said, "Hasten ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ the son of ⟦imlah|Yimlah|Imlah⟧."

[9] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ were sitting, each man on his throne, clothed in garments, and they were sitting at a threshing floor at the entrance of the gate of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and all the prophets were prophesying before them.

[10] And ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ the son of ⟦chenaanah|Kenaanah|Chenaanah⟧ made for himself horns of iron and said, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'With these you shall gore ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ until you have made an end of them.'"

[11] And all the prophets were prophesying so, saying, "Go up to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧ and prosper, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give it into the hand of the king."

[12] And the messenger who went to call ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ spoke to him, saying, "Behold, the words of the prophets, with one mouth, are good toward the king. And let your word, please, be like one of them, and speak good."

[13] And ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, surely what my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ says, that I will speak."

[14] And he came to the king, and the king said to him, "⟦micaiah|Michah|Micah⟧, shall we go to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧ to battle, or shall I cease?" And he said, "Go up and prosper, and they shall be given into your hand."

[15] And the king said to him, "How many times am I making you swear that you shall speak to me nothing but truth in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"

[16] And he said, "I saw all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ scattered upon the mountains, like the flock that has no shepherd. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, 'These have no lords; let them return, each man to his house, in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.'"

[17] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "Did I not say to you, 'He will not prophesy good concerning me, but for evil'?"

[18] And he said, "Therefore hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: I saw ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sitting on his throne, and all the host of the heavens standing on his right and his left.

[19] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, 'Who will entice ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and let him go up and fall at ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧?' And one said, this one saying thus and that one saying thus.

[20] And the spirit came out and stood before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and said, 'I, I will entice him.' And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, 'By what?'

[21] And he said, 'I will go out and become a spirit of falsehood in the mouth of all his prophets.' And he said, 'You shall entice, and you shall also be able. Go out and do so.'

[22] And now, behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given a spirit of falsehood in the mouth of these your prophets, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken evil concerning you."

[23] And ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ the son of ⟦chenaanah|Kenaanah|Chenaanah⟧ drew near and struck ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ on the cheek and said, "Which is the way the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ passed over from me to speak with you?"

[24] And ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ said, "Behold, you shall see on that day when you go into a chamber within a chamber to hide yourself."

[25] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said, "Take ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ and return him to Amon the prince of the city and to ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ the son of the king,

[26] and you shall say, 'Thus says the king: Put this one in the house of confinement, and feed him bread of oppression and water of oppression until I return in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.'"

[27] And ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧ said, "If you surely return in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not spoken by me." And he said, "Hear, peoples, all of them!"

[28] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went up to ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧.

[29] And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ said to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, "I will disguise myself and go into the battle; and you, put on your garments." And the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ disguised himself, and they went into the battle.

[30] And the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ had commanded the commanders of the chariots that were his, saying, "You shall not fight with the small or the great, but with the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ alone."

[31] And it came to pass, when the commanders of the chariots saw ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, that they said, "He is the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧," and they surrounded him to fight; and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ cried out, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ helped him, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ incited them away from him.

[32] And it came to pass, when the commanders of the chariots saw that he was not the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that they turned back from after him.

[33] And a man drew the bow in his innocence and struck the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ between the joints and the armor. And he said to the charioteer, "Turn your hand and bring me out of the camp, for I am wounded."

[34] And the battle went up on that day, and the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was keeping himself standing in the chariot opposite ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ until the evening, and he died at the time of the going in of the sun.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 13, 31) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v5 with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים), where 1 Kgs 22:6 has אֲדֹנָי "the Lord".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel: 1 Kgs 22:1–35** — where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the wording is that of 1 Kings 22 (v4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 15, 20, 24, 27, 28 are word for word the same Hebrew, apart from spelling); every difference named below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Nothing is taken over from Kings.
- **"joined affinity with Ahab" → "intermarried with Ahab"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּתְחַתֵּן לְ, "made himself kin by marriage to": his son Jehoram had the daughter of Ahab as wife (21:6). Verses 1–2 are Chronicles' own; Kings begins "And it came to pass in the third year" and has the king of Israel speak to his servants first (1 Kgs 22:2, 3).
- **"after certain years he went down" → "at the end of some years he came down"** (v2) — Hebrew לְקֵץ שָׁנִים, literally "at the end of years". **"killed sheep and oxen" → "slaughtered flock and herd"** — Hebrew וַיִּזְבַּח, the verb "sacrifice", here of slaughter for a feast (Deut 12:15); צֹאן וּבָקָר, the two collective words. **"persuaded him" → "incited him"** — Hebrew וַיְסִיתֵהוּ, הֵסִית "incite, stir up" (1 Sam 26:19; 1 Kgs 21:25); the same verb comes back in v31 with Elohim as the subject.
- **"I am as thou art, and my people as thy people; and we will be with thee in the war" → "As I am, so are you; and as your people, so my people; and with you in the battle"** (v3) — Hebrew כָּמוֹנִי כָמוֹךָ וּכְעַמְּךָ עַמִּי וְעִמְּךָ בַּמִּלְחָמָה; no verb. Kings has "as my people, so your people; as my horses, so your horses" and no "with you in the battle". Ahab is named here; in Kings he is "the king of Israel". **"Enquire … at the word" → "Seek … the word"** (v4, 6, 7) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ "seek", the word of Chronicles.
- **"four hundred men" (v5)** — Kings has "about four hundred". **"Shall we go to Ramoth-gilead to battle, or shall I forbear" → "Shall we go … or shall I cease"** — Hebrew הֲנֵלֵךְ … אִם־אֶחְדָּל: the first verb is plural, the second singular (Kings: "Shall I go against … or shall I cease"); the same in v14 (Kings there: "shall we go … or shall we cease"). "It" in "give it" is supplied, as in Kings.
- **"a prophet of the LORD besides" → "a prophet of Yahweh still"** (v6) — Hebrew עוֹד.
- **"he never prophesied good unto me, but always evil" → "he is not prophesying concerning me for good, but all his days for evil"** (v7) — Hebrew אֵינֶנּוּ מִתְנַבֵּא עָלַי לְטוֹבָה כִּי כָל־יָמָיו לְרָעָה (Kings: "he does not prophesy good concerning me, but evil"). **"the same is Micaiah" → "he is Micaiah"** — Hebrew הוּא. Imla is written יִמְלָא here (Kings יִמְלָה).
- **"one of his officers" → "one eunuch"** (v8) — Hebrew סָרִיס אֶחָד: "eunuch", also used of a court officer. **"Fetch quickly" → "Hasten"** — Hebrew מַהֵר, with the name as its object. The written form of the name (ketiv) is מיכהו; the reading tradition (qere) is "Micaiah".
- **"sat either of them on his throne, clothed in their robes, and they sat in a void place" → "were sitting, each man on his throne, clothed in garments, and they were sitting at a threshing floor"** (v9) — Hebrew יוֹשְׁבִים … וְיֹשְׁבִים בְּגֹרֶן: the participle stands twice (once in Kings); גֹּרֶן is "threshing floor".
- **"thou shalt push Syria until they be consumed" → "you shall gore Aram until you have made an end of them"** (v10) — Hebrew תְּנַגַּח, of a horned beast; עַד־כַּלּוֹתָם; אֲרָם is the people named as one, "Aram".
- **"Behold, the words of the prophets"** (v12) — no נָא "please" after "behold" here (Kings has it). **"let thy word … be like one of theirs" → "And let your word, please, be like one of them"** — Hebrew וִיהִי־נָא דְבָרְךָ כְּאַחַד מֵהֶם (Kings: "like the word of one of them", without "and").
- **"even what my God saith" → "surely what my Elohim says"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־יֹאמַר אֱלֹהַי; Kings has "what Yahweh says to me".
- **"Micaiah" → "Micah"** (v14) — the Hebrew has the short form מִיכָה here. **"Go ye up, and prosper, and they shall be delivered into your hand" → "Go up and prosper, and they shall be given into your hand"** — Hebrew עֲלוּ וְהַצְלִיחוּ וְיִנָּתְנוּ בְּיֶדְכֶם: the commands and "your" are plural, and the verb is "be given" (Kings: "Go up and prosper", singular, "and Yahweh will give it into the hand of the king").
- **"adjure thee" → "making you swear"** (v15) — Hebrew מַשְׁבִּיעֶךָ.
- **"scattered upon the mountains, as sheep that have no shepherd" → "scattered upon the mountains, like the flock that has no shepherd"** (v16) — Hebrew עַל־הֶהָרִים (Kings אֶל "to"); צֹאן "flock". **"These have no master" → "These have no lords"** — Hebrew אֲדֹנִים, plural. **"in peace" → "in shalom"** (v16, 26, 27).
- **"but evil" → "but for evil"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־לְרָע (Kings: "but evil").
- **"hear the word of the LORD" (v18)** — the command is plural here (שִׁמְעוּ), singular in Kings. **"standing on his right hand and on his left" → "standing on his right and his left"** — Hebrew עֹמְדִים עַל־יְמִינוֹ וּשְׂמֹאלוֹ; Kings has "standing by him, on his right and on his left".
- **"Who shall entice Ahab king of Israel" kept** (v19–21) — Hebrew פִּתָּה (piel) "entice, persuade", three times, as in 1 Kgs 22:20–22; "king of Israel" is not in Kings. **"And one spake saying after this manner, and another saying after that manner" → "And one said, this one saying thus and that one saying thus"** — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר זֶה אֹמֵר כָּכָה וְזֶה אֹמֵר כָּכָה, literally "and he said: this one saying thus, and this one saying thus"; the wording differs from Kings.
- **"there came out a spirit" → "the spirit came out"** (v20) — Hebrew הָרוּחַ, with the article. **"Wherewith?" → "By what?"** — Hebrew בַּמָּה.
- **"be a lying spirit" → "become a spirit of falsehood"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew וְהָיִיתִי לְרוּחַ שֶׁקֶר: with לְ, "become" (Kings without it: "be"); רוּחַ שֶׁקֶר "a spirit of falsehood" ("a lying spirit"). **"thou shalt also prevail" → "you shall also be able"** — Hebrew וְגַם־תּוּכָל. **"the LORD hath put" → "Yahweh has given"** (v22) — Hebrew נָתַן. "These your prophets": Kings has "all these your prophets".
- **"smote Micaiah upon the cheek" → "struck Micaiah on the cheek"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ. **"Which way went the Spirit of the LORD from me" → "Which is the way the spirit of Yahweh passed over from me"** — Hebrew אֵי זֶה הַדֶּרֶךְ עָבַר; Kings has no "the way". אֹתָךְ may be "with you" or "to you".
- **"an inner chamber" → "a chamber within a chamber"** (v24) — Hebrew חֶדֶר בְּחֶדֶר.
- **"Take ye Micaiah, and carry him back to Amon the governor of the city" → "Take Micaiah and return him to Amon the prince of the city"** (v25) — the commands are plural here, and so is "you shall say" in v26 (singular in Kings); שַׂר "prince".
- **"in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction and with water of affliction, until I return in peace" → "in the house of confinement, and feed him bread of oppression and water of oppression until I return in shalom"** (v26) — Hebrew בֵּית הַכֶּלֶא; לַחַץ "oppression, pressing"; עַד שׁוּבִי "until my returning" (Kings: "until I come").
- **"If thou certainly return" → "If you surely return"** (v27) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb: שׁוֹב תָּשׁוּב, literally "returning you return". **"then hath not the LORD spoken by me"** — Hebrew בִּי "by me, in me". **"Hearken, all ye people" → "Hear, peoples, all of them!"** — Hebrew שִׁמְעוּ עַמִּים כֻּלָּם, plural "peoples"; the same words open Mic 1:2.
- **"I will disguise myself, and will go to the battle" → "I will disguise myself and go into the battle"** (v29) — Hebrew הִתְחַפֵּשׂ וָבוֹא, two infinitives: "to disguise oneself and to go". **"and they went to the battle" → "and they went into the battle"** — plural here; Kings has "and he went".
- **"the captains of the chariots that were with him" → "the commanders of the chariots that were his"** (v30) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ; Kings adds "thirty-two". **"Fight ye not with small or great" → "You shall not fight with the small or the great"** — Hebrew אֶת־הַקָּטֹן אֶת־הַגָּדוֹל, with the article and without "and": "with the small, with the great" (Kings: "with small or with great").
- **"It is the king of Israel. Therefore they compassed about him to fight" → "He is the king of Israel," and they surrounded him to fight** (v31) — Kings has "Surely he is" and וַיָּסֻרוּ "they turned aside against him"; Chronicles has וַיָּסֹבּוּ "they surrounded".
- **"and the LORD helped him; and God moved them to depart from him" → "and Yahweh helped him, and Elohim incited them away from him"** (v31) — these words are not in Kings. Hebrew וַיְסִיתֵם אֱלֹהִים מִמֶּנּוּ: the verb of v2, הֵסִית "incite", with מִן "from": Ahab incited Jehoshaphat to go up, Elohim incited the commanders away from him.
- **"that it was not the king of Israel, they turned back again from pursuing him" → "that he was not the king of Israel, that they turned back from after him"** (v32) — Hebrew מֵאַחֲרָיו "from after him".
- **"drew a bow at a venture" → "drew the bow in his innocence"** (v33) — Hebrew לְתֻמּוֹ, "in his integrity, innocence": without knowing whom he aimed at. **"between the joints of the harness" → "between the joints and the armor"** — Hebrew בֵּין הַדְּבָקִים וּבֵין הַשִּׁרְיָן; what the "joints" (lit. "things that cling") were is not certain. **"to his chariot man" → "to the charioteer"** — Hebrew לָרַכָּב (Kings: "his charioteer"). "Your hand" is the reading tradition (qere); the written form (ketiv) is plural, "your hands". **"I am wounded"** — Hebrew הָחֳלֵיתִי, "I have been made sick / weak".
- **"the battle increased that day: howbeit the king of Israel stayed himself up in his chariot against the Syrians until the even: and about the time of the sun going down he died" → "the battle went up on that day, and the king of Israel was keeping himself standing in the chariot opposite Aram until the evening, and he died at the time of the going in of the sun"** (v34) — Hebrew וַתַּעַל "went up"; הָיָה מַעֲמִיד, a hifil participle, "was making stand" with no object (Kings has the passive, "was kept standing"); נֹכַח אֲרָם "opposite Aram"; לְעֵת בּוֹא הַשָּׁמֶשׁ (Kings: "in the evening"). Chronicles does not have the blood in the chariot, the cry through the camp, the burial, and the dogs (1 Kgs 22:35–38).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 22:1-35** — Chronicler reproduces with minor shifts.
- **Marriage alliance** (v1) — Jehoshaphat's son Jehoram marries Athaliah daughter of Ahab-Jezebel (ch 21). Chronicler marks this as the fatal compromise.
- **Divine rescue of Jehoshaphat** (v31) — Chronicler's addition: "Yahweh helped him." Even in a bad alliance, Yahweh rescues the basically faithful king.
- **400 prophets** — court prophets, Yahweh-named but compromised. Micaiah alone stands.
- **Divine council / spirit of falsehood** — OT depiction of Yahweh's heavenly court orchestrating judgment.
- **Arrow "in his innocence"** (v33) — providence executing Yahweh's decree (1 Kgs 21 oracle on Ahab).
- **Chronicler's agenda** — presents Jehoshaphat's role with redeeming flourish; Ahab dies as judgment; Jehoshaphat survives by Yahweh's intervention.
