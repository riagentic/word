## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 14) — Hebrew *Elohim*, both times with the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Thus all the work … was finished" → "And all the work … was completed"** (v1) — Hebrew וַתִּשְׁלַם, as 1 Kgs 7:51. Chronicles has "Solomon" (Kings "King Solomon") and "for the house of Yahweh" (Kings "in the house"). **"the things that David his father had dedicated" → "the holy things of David his father"** — Hebrew קָדְשֵׁי דָּוִיד. **"and the silver, and the gold, and all the instruments, put he among the treasures" → "and the silver and the gold and all the utensils he placed in the treasuries"** — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "gave"; אֹצְרוֹת are treasuries. Chronicles adds "and" before "the silver" and "all" before "the utensils", and ends "the house of Elohim" (Kings "the house of Yahweh").
- **"the chief of the fathers of the children of Israel" → "the chieftains of the fathers of the sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאֵי הָאָבוֹת לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, as 1 Kgs 8:1. The verse has the wording of Kings; Chronicles adds "and" before "all the heads" and has "to Jerusalem" where Kings has "to King Solomon in Jerusalem".
- **"assembled themselves unto the king in the feast which was in the seventh month" → "assembled to the king at the feast — it is the seventh month"** (v3) — Hebrew בֶּחָג הוּא הַחֹדֶשׁ הַשְּׁבִעִי, as 1 Kgs 8:2, which names the king and the month ("King Solomon in the moon of the Ethanim").
- **"the Levites took up the ark" → "the Levites lifted up the ark"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂאוּ. 1 Kgs 8:3 has "the priests lifted up the ark".
- **"the tabernacle of the congregation" → "the tent of meeting"** (v5) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"the holy vessels" → "the holy utensils"** — Hebrew כְּלֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the utensils of holiness". **"these did the priests and the Levites bring up" → "the priests, the Levites, brought them up"** — Hebrew הַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם, two nouns side by side with no "and", as Deut 17:9; 1 Kgs 8:4 has "the priests and the Levites" and "the ark of Yahweh".
- **"all the congregation of Israel that were assembled unto him" → "all the congregation of Israel who had met together to him"** (v6) — Hebrew הַנּוֹעָדִים עָלָיו, the verb of "tent of meeting". The verse is the same as 1 Kgs 8:5, word for word, only without "with him". **"sheep and oxen" → "flock and herd"** — Hebrew צֹאן וּבָקָר. **"which could not be told nor numbered"** — Hebrew לֹא־יִסָּפְרוּ וְלֹא יִמָּנוּ, two verbs: "numbered" and "counted".
- **"into the oracle of the house, into the most holy place, even under the wings" → "to the inner room of the house, to the Holy of Holies, to beneath the wings"** (v7) — Hebrew דְּבִיר "inner room"; קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים. The verse is the same as 1 Kgs 8:6, word for word.
- **"For the cherubims spread forth their wings over the place of the ark" → "And the cherubim were spreading out wings over the place of the ark"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ … פֹּרְשִׂים כְּנָפַיִם עַל; 1 Kgs 8:7 has "For" and "toward" (אֶל). **"covered the ark"** — Hebrew וַיְכַסּוּ … עַל "covered over"; 1 Kgs 8:7 has another verb, "screened".
- **"they drew out the staves of the ark, that the ends of the staves were seen from the ark before the oracle" → "the poles were long, and the heads of the poles were seen from the ark, facing the inner room"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיַּאֲרִיכוּ הַבַּדִּים, "the poles were long" (or "they made the poles long"); רָאשֵׁי "heads". 1 Kgs 8:8 has "from the Holy" where Chronicles has "from the ark". **"And there it is unto this day" → "and it has been there to this day"** — Hebrew וַיְהִי־שָׁם, singular (the ark); 1 Kgs 8:8 has the plural, "they have been there".
- **"save the two tables which Moses put therein at Horeb, when the LORD made a covenant" → "only the two tablets that Moses placed at Horeb, where Yahweh cut a covenant"** (v10) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־נָתַן מֹשֶׁה, literally "gave"; "therein" is not in the Hebrew. 1 Kgs 8:9 has "the two tablets of stone that Moses laid up there". כָּרַת is "cut"; the second אֲשֶׁר is "where" (or "which Yahweh cut": a covenant is not named in the Hebrew, "a covenant" is supplied in English as in 1 Kgs 8:9). **"when they came out of Egypt"** — Kings has "from the land of Egypt".
- **"out of the holy place" → "from the Holy"** (v11) — Hebrew מִן־הַקֹּדֶשׁ; "place" is not in the Hebrew. The first words are the same as 1 Kgs 8:10a; what follows to the middle of v13 is not in Kings. **"all the priests that were present were sanctified, and did not then wait by course" → "all the priests who were found had sanctified themselves; there was no keeping to divisions"** — Hebrew הַנִּמְצְאִים "who were found"; הִתְקַדָּשׁוּ is reflexive; אֵין לִשְׁמוֹר לְמַחְלְקוֹת is literally "there was not to keep for divisions": the turns of service (1 Chr 24) were not kept that day.
- **"the Levites which were the singers" → "the Levites, the singers"** (v12) — Hebrew הַלְוִיִּם הַמְשֹׁרֲרִים. **"all of them of Asaph, of Heman, of Jeduthun, with their sons and their brethren" → "all of them, Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, and their sons and their brothers"** — the Hebrew sets לְ before each member of the list. **"arrayed in white linen" → "clothed in fine linen"** — Hebrew בּוּץ; "white" is not in the Hebrew. **"psalteries and harps" → "harps and lyres"** — Hebrew נְבָלִים and כִּנֹּרוֹת; the kinds of instrument are not certain. **"an hundred and twenty priests sounding with trumpets" → "priests, to a hundred and twenty, sounding the trumpets"** — Hebrew לְמֵאָה וְעֶשְׂרִים מַחְצְרִים בַּחֲצֹצְרוֹת: verb and noun of one root; the trumpet is the חֲצֹצְרָה, not the shofar. The written text and the reading tradition differ only in the form of the participle (also v13).
- **"as the trumpeters and singers were as one, to make one sound to be heard" → "those sounding the trumpets and the singers being as one, to make one voice heard"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיְהִי כְאֶחָד לַמְחַצְּרִים וְלַמְשֹׁרֲרִים לְהַשְׁמִיעַ קוֹל־אֶחָד, literally "and it was as one for those sounding the trumpets and for the singers, to make one voice heard"; קוֹל is "voice" or "sound". **"in praising and thanking the LORD" → "to praise and to give thanks to Yahweh"** — Hebrew לְהַלֵּל וּלְהֹדוֹת. **"when they lifted up their voice" → "when they raised a voice"** — Hebrew וּכְהָרִים קוֹל; no "their". **"instruments of musick" → "instruments of song"** — Hebrew כְלֵי הַשִּׁיר.
- **"For he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever" → "for he is good, for his chesed is forever"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי טוֹב כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ, the refrain of 1 Chr 16:34. The Hebrew has no verb ("is", not "endures"); חֶסֶד is kept as the term. The refrain is set on two lines.
- **"then the house was filled with a cloud, even the house of the LORD" → "that the house was filled with a cloud, the house of Yahweh"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהַבַּיִת מָלֵא עָנָן בֵּית יְהוָה; no article with "cloud". 1 Kgs 8:10 has "the cloud filled the house of Yahweh".
- **"So that the priests could not stand to minister by reason of the cloud" → "and the priests were not able to stand to minister because of the cloud"** (v14) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָכְלוּ … מִפְּנֵי הֶעָנָן; the verse is the same as 1 Kgs 8:11, word for word, to its last words, where Chronicles has "the house of Elohim" and Kings "the house of Yahweh".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 7:51–8:11** — where the Hebrew is the same as in Kings, word for word, the wording is that of Kings (v6, 7, 11a word for word; v1–5, 8–10, 14 with each difference of the Hebrew shown). v11b–13a (the priests and the Levite singers) is not in Kings.
- **The tent of meeting** (v5) — the tent that was at Gibeon (1:3) is brought up with the ark.
- **The seventh month** (v3) — 1 Kgs 8:2 calls it "the moon of the Ethanim"; "the feast" of that month is the Feast of Sukkot (7:8–10).
- **"The Levites lifted up the ark"** (v4) — Kings has "the priests"; in v7 the priests bring it to its place, as in Kings.
- **What was in the ark** (v10) — only the two tablets. Heb 9:4 also names the jar of manna and Aaron's rod; Exod 16:33–34 and Num 17:10 set these "before the testimony".
- **The refrain** (v13) — "for he is good, for his chesed is forever": 1 Chr 16:34, 41; 2 Chr 7:3, 6; 20:21; Ezra 3:11; Ps 106:1; 107:1; 118; 136.
- **The cloud and the glory** (v13–14) — as at the dwelling in the wilderness (Exod 40:34–35).
