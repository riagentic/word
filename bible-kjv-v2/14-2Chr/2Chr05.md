## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "Tent of Meeting"** (v5).
- **"oracle" → "inner sanctuary"** (v7, 9) — *devir*.
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v12); **"harps" → "lyres"** (v12).
- **"course" → "divisions"** (v11) — *machlaqot*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 8:1-11** — Chronicler adds substantial detail about Levitical music (v11-13).
- **Tabernacle at Gibeon brought too** (v5) — both cult objects consolidated at Solomon's temple. Chronicler unifies the dual worship of David's era.
- **Seventh month** — Tishri (September/October); season of Feast of Booths. Temple dedication coincides with major pilgrimage festival.
- **Ark contents** (v10) — "only the tablets." Implicit correction: 1 Kgs 8:9 same; both emphasize the covenant document. Note Heb 9:4 remembers manna-jar and Aaron's rod as well (drawing on Exodus/Numbers storage inside ark).
- **Unity of music and worship** (v13) — Chronicler's theological climax: harmonious choir → glory-cloud. Worship done right draws divine presence.
- **"His chesed endures forever"** — liturgical refrain; Ps 136 is entire psalm on this. Core Israelite doxology.
- **Glory-cloud / presence** — same phenomenon as Sinai (Ex 40:35) and Moses's tabernacle. Temple is legitimated by divine presence.
