# 2 Chronicles 25

[1] Amaziah was twenty-five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jehoaddan of Jerusalem.

[2] He did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, but not with a whole heart.

[3] When the kingdom was firmly established in his hand, he put to death his servants who had killed the king his father.

[4] But he did not put their children to death, but did as it is written in the Law, in the book of Moses, where Yahweh commanded, saying, "The fathers shall not die for the sons, nor the sons die for the fathers; but each shall die for his own sin."

[5] Amaziah assembled Judah and made them stand by their fathers' houses, with commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds, for all Judah and Benjamin. He numbered those twenty years old and upward, and found them to be three hundred thousand chosen men, able to go to war, who could handle spear and shield.

[6] He also hired one hundred thousand mighty warriors from Israel for one hundred talents of silver.

[7] But a man of *Elohim* came to him and said, "O king, let the army of Israel not go with you, for Yahweh is not with Israel — with any of the children of Ephraim.

[8] But if you do go — act, be strong for the battle — *Elohim* will cause you to fall before the enemy; for *Elohim* has power to help and to cast down."

[9] Amaziah said to the man of *Elohim*, "But what shall we do about the hundred talents that I have given to the army of Israel?" The man of *Elohim* answered, "Yahweh is able to give you much more than this."

[10] Then Amaziah separated out the army that had come to him from Ephraim, to go home; so their anger was greatly kindled against Judah, and they returned home in great anger.

[11] Amaziah strengthened himself and led out his people; he went to the Valley of Salt and struck down ten thousand of the children of Seir.

[12] The children of Judah captured ten thousand more alive; they brought them to the top of a rock and threw them down from the top of the rock, so that they were all dashed to pieces.

[13] But the men of the army that Amaziah had sent back from going with him to battle raided the cities of Judah, from Samaria to Beth-horon, struck down three thousand of them, and took much spoil.

[14] After Amaziah came from striking down the Edomites, he brought the gods of the children of Seir, set them up as his own gods, bowed down to them, and burned incense to them.

[15] Therefore the anger of Yahweh was kindled against Amaziah, and he sent to him a prophet who said to him, "Why have you sought the gods of a people who could not deliver their own people out of your hand?"

[16] As he was speaking to him, the king said to him, "Have we made you the king's counselor? Stop! Why should you be struck down?" So the prophet stopped, but said, "I know that *Elohim* has determined to destroy you, because you have done this and have not listened to my counsel."

[17] Then Amaziah king of Judah took counsel, and sent to Joash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu king of Israel, saying, "Come, let us look one another in the face."

[18] Joash king of Israel sent word to Amaziah king of Judah, saying, "The thistle that was in Lebanon sent to the cedar that was in Lebanon, saying, 'Give your daughter to my son in marriage.' But a wild beast of Lebanon passed by and trampled the thistle.

[19] You say, 'Behold, I have struck down Edom'; and your heart has lifted you up to boast. Now stay at home. Why stir up trouble, so that you fall, you and Judah with you?"

[20] But Amaziah would not listen; for it was of *Elohim*, that he might deliver them into the hand of their enemies, because they had sought the gods of Edom.

[21] So Joash king of Israel went up, and he and Amaziah king of Judah looked one another in the face at Beth-shemesh, which belongs to Judah.

[22] Judah was defeated before Israel, and each man fled to his tent.

[23] Joash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, son of Joash son of Jehoahaz, at Beth-shemesh, and brought him to Jerusalem; he broke down the wall of Jerusalem from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate, four hundred cubits.

[24] He took all the gold and silver and all the vessels that were found in the house of *Elohim* with Obed-edom, the treasuries of the king's house, and hostages; then he returned to Samaria.

[25] Amaziah son of Joash king of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of Joash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel.

[26] The rest of the acts of Amaziah, first and last — are they not written in the Book of the Kings of Judah and Israel?

[27] From the time that Amaziah turned away from following Yahweh, they made a conspiracy against him in Jerusalem; he fled to Lachish, but they sent to Lachish after him and killed him there.

[28] They brought him back on horses and buried him with his fathers in the city of Judah.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"perfect heart" → "whole heart"** (v2) — *lev shalem*.
- **"captains" → "commanders"** (v5).
- **"meddle to thy hurt" → "stir up trouble"** (v19).
- **"city of Judah" — retained** (v28) — KJV also preserves this; may be textual variant for "city of David."

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 14:1-20** — Chronicler expands on Edom campaign and hired-Israelite-army incident (unique content v5-13), and Amaziah's idolatry (v14-16).
- **Deut 24:16 cited directly** (v4) — same as 2 Kgs 14:6; individual-responsibility principle.
- **Hired northern mercenaries** — Amaziah pays 100 talents, prophet objects, Amaziah obeys. But dismissed soldiers plunder returning.
- **Amaziah's fatal idolatry** — brings back Edomite gods as spoils of war. Chronicler's distinct irony: he worships defeated gods.
- **Chronicler's theology** — defeat at Beth-shemesh is explicit judgment for Edomite idolatry (v20).
- **Rock-top execution** (v12) — Petra = "rock"; likely the cliff of Sela/Petra.
- **Assassination** — like his father Joash, Amaziah dies by conspiracy.
