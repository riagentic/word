# 2 Chronicles 23

[1] And in the seventh year ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ strengthened himself and took the commanders of the hundreds, ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the son of ⟦jeroham|Yerocham|Jeroham⟧ and ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦jehohanan|Yehochanan|Jehohanan⟧ and ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the son of ⟦obed|Oved|Obed⟧ and ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ the son of ⟦adaiah|Adayah|Adaiah⟧ and ⟦elishaphat|Elishafat|Elishaphat⟧ the son of Zichri, with him into the covenant.

[2] And they went around in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and gathered the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ from all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and the heads of the fathers of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[3] And all the assembly cut a covenant in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with the king. And he said to them, "Behold, the son of the king shall reign, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke concerning the sons of David.

[4] This is the thing that you shall do: the third of you, who come in on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, of the priests and of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, for gatekeepers of the thresholds,

[5] and the third in the house of the king, and the third at the gate of the foundation, and all the people in the courts of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And let no one come into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ except the priests and those of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ who minister; they shall come in, for they are holy; and all the people shall keep the charge of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ shall encircle the king all around, each man with his weapons in his hand, and whoever comes into the house shall be put to death. And be with the king in his coming in and in his going out."

[8] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ did according to all that ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest commanded, and they took each man his men, those coming in on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ with those going out on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, for ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest had not set free the divisions.

[9] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest gave to the commanders of the hundreds the spears and the shields and the bucklers that were King David's, which were in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] And he made all the people stand, and each man with his missile in his hand, from the right shoulder of the house to the left shoulder of the house, toward the altar and toward the house, around the king.

[11] And they brought out the son of the king and put on him the crown and the testimony, and they made him king, and ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ and his sons anointed him and said, "May the king live!"

[12] And ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ heard the sound of the people, the runners and those praising the king, and she came to the people, to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And she saw, and behold, the king was standing by his pillar at the entrance, and the commanders and the trumpets were by the king, and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing on trumpets, and the singers were with the instruments of song and were making known to praise. And ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ tore her garments and said, "Conspiracy! Conspiracy!"

[14] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest brought out the commanders of the hundreds, those appointed over the force, and said to them, "Bring her out to within the ranks, and whoever comes after her shall be put to death with the sword." For the priest said, "You shall not put her to death in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[15] And they put hands on her, and she came to the entrance of the gate of the horses, to the house of the king, and they put her to death there.

[16] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ cut a covenant between himself and all the people and the king, to be a people for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] And all the people came to the house of the Baal and broke it down, and his altars and his images they broke in pieces, and Mattan the priest of the Baal they killed before the altars.

[18] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ set the oversight of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the hand of the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, whom David had divided over the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to offer up the burnt offerings of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, with gladness and with song, at the hands of David.

[19] And he made the gatekeepers stand at the gates of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and no one unclean in any thing was to come in.

[20] And he took the commanders of the hundreds and the nobles and those ruling among the people and all the people of the land, and he brought the king down from the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they came through the midst of the upper gate to the house of the king, and they made the king sit on the throne of the kingdom.

[21] And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; and ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ they had put to death with the sword.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article (v3, 9).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Beside 2 Kgs 11:4–20** — the chapter retells Kings; where the Hebrew is the same the wording is that of Kings, and where Chronicles has its own words (the Levites, the gatekeepers, the singers, "the house of Elohim") they are translated as printed. Nothing is taken over from Kings; its differing readings are named below.
- **"Jehoiada strengthened himself, and took the captains of hundreds … into covenant with him" → "strengthened himself and took the commanders of the hundreds … with him into the covenant"** (v1) — Hebrew הִתְחַזַּק (hitpael of חָזַק "be strong"); Kings has "sent". שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander". The first three names have לְ before them, the last two אֵת, with no difference of meaning. בַבְּרִית has the article.
- **"went about" → "went around"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיָּסֹבּוּ. **"the chief of the fathers" → "the heads of the fathers"** — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת, the heads of the fathers' houses.
- **"made a covenant" → "cut a covenant"** (v3, 16) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית. **"as the LORD hath said of the sons of David" → "as Yahweh spoke concerning the sons of David"** — Hebrew דִּבֶּר עַל. Who says "Behold" is not named: Jehoiada.
- **"A third part of you entering on the sabbath, of the priests and of the Levites, shall be porters of the doors" → "the third of you, who come in on the Shabbat, of the priests and of the Levites, for gatekeepers of the thresholds"** (v4) — the Hebrew has no verb in v4, 5 (as 2 Kgs 11:5); לְשֹׁעֲרֵי הַסִּפִּים "for gatekeepers of the thresholds". דָּבָר "word" is here "thing".
- **"the gate of the foundation"** (v5) — Hebrew שַׁעַר הַיְסוֹד; Kings has "the gate Sur" (סוּר). Neither gate is known.
- **"But let none come" → "And let no one come"** (v6) — the Hebrew verb has no subject ("and let there not come"). **"they that minister of the Levites" → "those of the Levites who minister"** — Hebrew הַמְשָׁרְתִים לַלְוִיִּם. **"for they are holy"** — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ, the noun "holiness". **"shall keep the watch of the LORD" → "shall keep the charge of Yahweh"** — Hebrew מִשְׁמֶרֶת יְהוָה, what Yahweh has given to be kept (Lev 8:35); of guards the same word is "watch" (2 Kgs 11:5–7).
- **"compass the king round about" → "encircle the king all around"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהִקִּיפוּ … סָבִיב; Kings has "you shall encircle", here the Levites are named. **"weapons"** — Hebrew כֵּלָיו, literally "his articles / gear". **"whosoever else cometh into the house" → "whoever comes into the house"** — no "else"; Kings has "into the ranks". "In his coming in and in his going out": Kings has the other order.
- **"dismissed not the courses" → "had not set free the divisions"** (v8) — Hebrew פָּטַר "set free, let go" (1 Chr 9:33); מַחְלְקוֹת "divisions", the classes of priests and Levites that served in turn (1 Chr 24–26). Kings has "the commanders of the hundreds did", here "the Levites and all Judah".
- **"spears, and bucklers, and shields" → "the spears and the shields and the bucklers"** (v9) — Hebrew הַחֲנִיתִים (plural; Kings has the singular), הַמָּגִנּוֹת "the shields" (not in Kings), הַשְּׁלָטִים. שֶׁלֶט is elsewhere "shield" (2 Kgs 11:10; 2 Sam 8:7); here it stands beside מָגֵן "shield" and has a second word, "bucklers". Its exact sense is not certain (also understood as "quivers"). "Which were in the house of Elohim": the Hebrew has no "in".
- **"he set all the people" → "he made all the people stand"** (v10, 19) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֵד (hifil of עָמַד "stand"). **"every man having his weapon in his hand" → "and each man with his missile in his hand"** — Hebrew שִׁלְחוֹ, from שָׁלַח "send": a weapon that is thrown, or a weapon in general (2 Chr 32:5; Neh 4:17); another word than the "weapons" of v7; the exact sense is not certain. **"from the right side of the temple to the left side" → "from the right shoulder of the house to the left shoulder of the house"** — Hebrew כֶּתֶף "shoulder"; לַמִּזְבֵּחַ וְלַבָּיִת "toward the altar and toward the house". Kings has "the runners stood".
- **"put upon him the crown, and gave him the testimony" → "put on him the crown and the testimony"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנוּ עָלָיו אֶת־הַנֵּזֶר וְאֶת־הָעֵדוּת: one verb for both; there is no "gave him" and no second "testimony" (the earlier text had "the testimony — covenant testimony"). נֵזֶר is the mark of one set apart, "crown". What "the testimony" put on the king was is not said (see 2 Kgs 11:12). **"made him king"** — Hebrew וַיַּמְלִיכוּ אֹתוֹ, literally "made him reign". "Jehoiada and his sons" is not in Kings, and Kings has "they clapped their hands", which is not here.
- **"God save the king" → "May the king live!"** (v11) — Hebrew יְחִי הַמֶּלֶךְ, a wish; no "God" and no "long".
- **"the noise of the people running and praising the king" → "the sound of the people, the runners and those praising the king"** (v12) — Hebrew קוֹל הָעָם הָרָצִים וְהַמְהַלְלִים אֶת־הַמֶּלֶךְ. הָרָצִים is the word for the king's guard, "the runners" (2 Kgs 11:4, 13); it can also be read as a participle, "the people who were running and praising the king". "To the house of Yahweh" has no preposition in the Hebrew.
- **"stood at his pillar at the entering in" → "was standing by his pillar at the entrance"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־עַמּוּדוֹ בַּמָּבוֹא, literally "upon his pillar, at the entrance"; Kings has "by the pillar, according to the ordinance". **"the princes and the trumpets" → "the commanders and the trumpets"** — Hebrew הַשָּׂרִים (the word of v1), הַחֲצֹצְרוֹת "the trumpets", the instruments named for those who blow them.
- **"also the singers with instruments of musick, and such as taught to sing praise" → "and the singers were with the instruments of song and were making known to praise"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהַמְשׁוֹרֲרִים בִּכְלֵי הַשִּׁיר וּמוֹדִיעִים לְהַלֵּל; מוֹדִיעִים is "making known" (hifil of יָדַע "know"): they gave the sign for the praise, or led it. The words are not in Kings. **"rent her clothes, and said, Treason, Treason" → "tore her garments and said, 'Conspiracy! Conspiracy!'"** — Hebrew קֶשֶׁר; Kings has "called".
- **"brought out the captains of hundreds that were set over the host" → "brought out the commanders of the hundreds, those appointed over the force"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיּוֹצֵא "brought out"; Kings has וַיְצַו "commanded", and many read that here; the printed text is kept. פְּקוּדֵי הַחַיִל, literally "the appointed ones of the force". **"Have her forth of the ranges" → "Bring her out to within the ranks"** — Hebrew אֶל־מִבֵּית הַשְּׂדֵרוֹת, literally "to from-the-inside of the ranks"; שְׂדֵרוֹת "ranks" is not certain (2 Kgs 11:8, 15). **"let him be slain" → "shall be put to death"** — Hebrew יוּמַת (Kings has an infinitive, "put to death"). **"Slay her not" → "You shall not put her to death"** — Hebrew לֹא תְמִיתוּהָ; Kings has "Let her not be put to death". **"kill / slay" → "put to death"** (v14, 15, 21) — Hebrew הֵמִית.
- **"they laid hands on her" → "they put hands on her"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיָּשִׂימוּ לָהּ יָדַיִם, literally "they put for her hands"; also understood "they made room for her". Uncertain. **"to the entering of the horse gate by the king's house" → "to the entrance of the gate of the horses, to the house of the king"** — Hebrew אֶל־מְבוֹא שַׁעַר־הַסּוּסִים בֵּית הַמֶּלֶךְ; "to" before "the house" is supplied. Kings has no "gate" and has "she was put to death".
- **"between him, and between all the people, and between the king, that they should be the LORD's people" → "between himself and all the people and the king, to be a people for Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew בֵּינוֹ וּבֵין כָּל־הָעָם וּבֵין הַמֶּלֶךְ לִהְיוֹת לְעָם לַיהוָה. Kings has "between Yahweh and the king and the people".
- **"went to the house of Baal, and brake it down" → "came to the house of the Baal and broke it down"** (v17) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל, with the article; נָתַץ "break down", שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces", הָרַג "kill". "His altars and his images": the Baal's. Kings has "all the people of the land" and "thoroughly".
- **"appointed the offices of the house of the LORD by the hand of the priests the Levites, whom David had distributed in the house of the LORD" → "set the oversight of the house of Yahweh in the hand of the priests, the Levites, whom David had divided over the house of Yahweh"** (v18) — Hebrew פְּקֻדֹּת, the plural of פְּקֻדָּה "oversight": posts of oversight (2 Kgs 11:18). הַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם is two nouns side by side, "the priests, the Levites", not "the Levitical priests". חָלַק "divide": set in divisions (v8). **"with rejoicing and with singing, as it was ordained by David" → "with gladness and with song, at the hands of David"** — Hebrew עַל יְדֵי דָוִיד, literally "upon the hands of David": under his direction, as he had ordered it (1 Chr 25:2, 6). The verse from "in the hand of" on is not in Kings.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v4, 19) — Hebrew שֹׁעֲרִים. **"that none which was unclean in any thing should enter in" → "and no one unclean in any thing was to come in"** (v19) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָבֹא טָמֵא לְכָל־דָּבָר, literally "and there shall not come in one unclean as to any thing".
- **"the nobles, and the governors of the people" → "the nobles and those ruling among the people"** (v20) — Hebrew הָאַדִּירִים "the mighty, the nobles"; הַמּוֹשְׁלִים בָּעָם "those ruling among the people". Kings has "the Carites and the runners". **"brought down the king"** — Hebrew וַיּוֹרֶד, singular: "he brought down" (Kings has the plural). **"through the high gate into the king's house, and set the king upon the throne" → "through the midst of the upper gate to the house of the king, and they made the king sit on the throne"** — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ־שַׁעַר הָעֶלְיוֹן, literally "in the midst of the upper gate"; Kings has "by the way of the gate of the runners". וַיּוֹשִׁיבוּ "made sit"; Kings has "he sat on the throne of the kings".
- **"after that they had slain Athaliah" → "and Athaliah they had put to death"** (v21) — the Hebrew has "and", not "after"; the object stands first. שָׁקָטָה "was quiet". Kings adds "at the house of the king".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 11** — Chronicler's distinctive edit: expands Levitical role significantly (Kings had primarily secular guard), de-emphasizing the Carite mercenaries present in Kings. Chronicler casts the coup as primarily a priestly-Levitical action.
- **"Pillar at the entrance"** — royal coronation spot by Jachin or Boaz.
- **Six-year hidden** preparation; seventh year = sabbatical year coup timing perhaps intentional.
- **Baal temple in Jerusalem** (v17) — Athaliah's Tyrian-Baal shrine destroyed, Mattan the Baal-priest killed. Parallels Jehu's purge of northern Baalism (2 Kgs 10).
- **Tripartite covenant** (v16) — Jehoiada-king-people covenant, slight reworking of 2 Kgs 11:17 (which has Yahweh-king-people + king-people).
- **Chronicler's theological shape** — priest Jehoiada as temporary savior; Davidic line preserved through priest-royal marriage alliance.
