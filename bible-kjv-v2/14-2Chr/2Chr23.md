## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v4, 19) — *sho'arim*.
- **"God save the king" → "Long live the king"** (v11) — *yechi ha-melek*.
- **"ranges" → "ranks"** (v14).
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v13) — *sarim*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 11** — Chronicler's distinctive edit: expands Levitical role significantly (Kings had primarily secular guard), de-emphasizing the Carite mercenaries present in Kings. Chronicler casts the coup as primarily a priestly-Levitical action.
- **"Pillar at the entrance"** — royal coronation spot by Jachin or Boaz.
- **Six-year hidden** preparation; seventh year = sabbatical year coup timing perhaps intentional.
- **Baal temple in Jerusalem** (v17) — Athaliah's Tyrian-Baal shrine destroyed, Mattan the Baal-priest killed. Parallels Jehu's purge of northern Baalism (2 Kgs 10).
- **Tripartite covenant** (v16) — Jehoiada-king-people covenant, slight reworking of 2 Kgs 11:17 (which has Yahweh-king-people + king-people).
- **Chronicler's theological shape** — priest Jehoiada as temporary savior; Davidic line preserved through priest-royal marriage alliance.
