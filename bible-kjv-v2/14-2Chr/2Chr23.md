# 2 Chronicles 23

[1] In the seventh year Jehoiada strengthened himself and took into covenant with him the commanders of hundreds: Azariah son of Jeroham, Ishmael son of Jehohanan, Azariah son of Obed, Maaseiah son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat son of Zichri.

[2] They went about in Judah and gathered the Levites from all the cities of Judah, and the heads of the fathers' houses of Israel; they came to Jerusalem.

[3] All the assembly made a covenant with the king in the house of *Elohim*. He said to them, "Behold, the king's son shall reign, as Yahweh spoke concerning the sons of David.

[4] This is what you shall do: a third of you who come in on the Sabbath — of the priests and the Levites — shall be gatekeepers of the thresholds;

[5] a third at the king's house; a third at the Foundation Gate; and all the people in the courts of the house of Yahweh.

[6] Let no one enter the house of Yahweh except the priests and the Levites who minister; they may go in, for they are holy. All the people shall keep the watch of Yahweh.

[7] The Levites shall surround the king on every side, each with his weapons in his hand; whoever enters the house shall be put to death. Be with the king when he goes out and when he comes in."

[8] So the Levites and all Judah did according to all that Jehoiada the priest had commanded. Each took his men who were to come on duty on the Sabbath, along with those who were to go off duty on the Sabbath, for Jehoiada the priest did not dismiss the divisions.

[9] Moreover Jehoiada the priest gave to the commanders of hundreds the spears, bucklers, and shields that had been King David's, which were in the house of *Elohim*.

[10] He stationed all the people, each with his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the house to the left side, by the altar and by the house, around the king.

[11] Then they brought out the king's son, put the crown on him, gave him the testimony — covenant testimony — and made him king. Jehoiada and his sons anointed him; and they said, "Long live the king!"

[12] When Athaliah heard the sound of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people in the house of Yahweh.

[13] She looked, and behold, the king was standing by his pillar at the entrance, with the officials and the trumpeters beside the king; all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing trumpets — and the singers with instruments of music were leading the praise. Then Athaliah tore her clothes and cried, "Treason, treason!"

[14] Jehoiada the priest commanded the commanders of hundreds who were set over the army, and said to them, "Bring her out between the ranks; whoever follows her, let him be killed with the sword." For the priest had said, "Do not kill her in the house of Yahweh."

[15] So they laid hands on her; and when she came to the entrance of the Horse Gate of the king's house, they killed her there.

[16] Jehoiada made a covenant between himself, all the people, and the king, that they should be Yahweh's people.

[17] All the people went to the house of Baal and tore it down; they broke its altars and its images to pieces, and killed Mattan the priest of Baal in front of the altars.

[18] Jehoiada assigned the offices of the house of Yahweh to the charge of the Levitical priests, whom David had distributed in the house of Yahweh to offer the burnt offerings of Yahweh, as it is written in the Law of Moses, with rejoicing and with song, according to the order of David.

[19] He set the gatekeepers at the gates of the house of Yahweh, so that no one unclean in any respect should enter.

[20] He took the commanders of hundreds, the nobles, the governors of the people, and all the people of the land; they brought the king down from the house of Yahweh. They came through the Upper Gate into the king's house and set the king on the throne of the kingdom.

[21] All the people of the land rejoiced; the city was quiet, after they had killed Athaliah with the sword.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v4, 19) — *sho'arim*.
- **"God save the king" → "Long live the king"** (v11) — *yechi ha-melek*.
- **"ranges" → "ranks"** (v14).
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v13) — *sarim*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 11** — Chronicler's distinctive edit: expands Levitical role significantly (Kings had primarily secular guard), de-emphasizing the Carite mercenaries present in Kings. Chronicler casts the coup as primarily a priestly-Levitical action.
- **"Pillar at the entrance"** — royal coronation spot by Jachin or Boaz.
- **Six-year hidden** preparation; seventh year = sabbatical year coup timing perhaps intentional.
- **Baal temple in Jerusalem** (v17) — Athaliah's Tyrian-Baal shrine destroyed, Mattan the Baal-priest killed. Parallels Jehu's purge of northern Baalism (2 Kgs 10).
- **Tripartite covenant** (v16) — Jehoiada-king-people covenant, slight reworking of 2 Kgs 11:17 (which has Yahweh-king-people + king-people).
- **Chronicler's theological shape** — priest Jehoiada as temporary savior; Davidic line preserved through priest-royal marriage alliance.
