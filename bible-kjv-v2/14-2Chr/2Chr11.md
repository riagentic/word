## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel: 1 Kgs 12:21–24** (v1–4) — where the Hebrew of the two passages is the same, the wording is that of 1 Kings 12; every difference below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Nothing is taken over from Kings.
- **"he gathered of the house of Judah and Benjamin" → "assembled the house of Judah and Benjamin"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיַּקְהֵל אֶת־בֵּית יְהוּדָה וּבִנְיָמִן; Kings has "all the house of Judah and the tribe of Benjamin". **"chosen men, which were warriors" → "chosen men who made war"** — Hebrew בָּחוּר עֹשֵׂה מִלְחָמָה, a collective singular. **"against Israel" → "with Israel"** — Hebrew עִם־יִשְׂרָאֵל; Kings has "the house of Israel". **"that he might bring the kingdom again" → "to return the kingdom"** — Hebrew לְהָשִׁיב אֶת־הַמַּמְלָכָה "the kingdom"; Kings has הַמְּלוּכָה "the kingship" and adds "the son of Solomon".
- **"the word of the LORD"** (v2) — Hebrew דְּבַר־יְהוָה; Kings has "the word of Elohim". The name is written in the long form שְׁמַעְיָהוּ here (Kings and 12:5, 7, 15: שְׁמַעְיָה).
- **"Speak unto" → "Say to"** (v3) — Hebrew אֱמֹר. **"to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin"** — Kings has "to all the house of Judah and Benjamin and the rest of the people".
- **"Ye shall not go up, nor fight against your brethren" → "You shall not go up and you shall not fight with your brothers"** (v4) — Kings adds "the sons of Israel". **"this thing is done of me" → "from me this thing has come to be"** — Hebrew מֵאִתִּי נִהְיָה הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה. **"they obeyed the words" → "they heard the words"** — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמְעוּ אֶת־דִּבְרֵי, the plural (Kings: "the word"). **"returned from going against Jeroboam" → "turned back from going against Jeroboam"** — Hebrew וַיָּשֻׁבוּ מִלֶּכֶת אֶל־יָרָבְעָם, literally "from going to Jeroboam": אֶל of a march against someone. Kings has "turned back to go, according to the word of Yahweh".
- **"dwelt"** (v5) — Hebrew יָשַׁב "dwell". **"cities for defence" kept, "fenced cities" → "cities of defense", "the strong holds" → "the defenses"** (v5, 10, 11, 23) — Hebrew לְמָצוֹר (v5) and מְצֻרוֹת (v10, 11, 23), two forms of one word from צוּר "enclose, besiege": a place shut in against an enemy. מָצוֹר is elsewhere "siege" (Deut 20:19); "cities for siege" would say in English cities to besiege. It is another word than מִבְצָר "fortification".
- **The list of cities** (v6–10) — each name is joined by "and" (וְאֶת), as in the Hebrew. **"which are in Judah and in Benjamin"** (v10) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר בִּיהוּדָה וּבְבִנְיָמִן.
- **"he fortified the strong holds" → "he made the defenses strong"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיְחַזֵּק, "make strong", the same verb in v12 and v17. **"captains" → "leaders"** — Hebrew נְגִידִים (v22 the singular). **"store of victual" → "treasuries of food"** — Hebrew אֹצְרוֹת, the same word as the "treasures" of 12:9.
- **"in every several city he put shields" → "in each and every city, large shields"** (v12) — Hebrew וּבְכָל־עִיר וָעִיר, literally "in every city and city"; the clause has no verb, and the earlier text supplied "he put". צִנּוֹת is the large shield, another word than מָגֵן (12:9, 10). **"having Judah and Benjamin on his side" → "And Judah and Benjamin were his"** — Hebrew וַיְהִי־לוֹ.
- **"resorted to him out of all their coasts" → "took their stand with him, from all their territory"** (v13) — Hebrew הִתְיַצְּבוּ עָלָיו "took their stand by him"; גְּבוּל "border", here the land within it.
- **"suburbs" → "pasture lands"** (v14) — Hebrew מִגְרָשׁ, the open land round a city. **"left"** — Hebrew עָזַב, the same verb as "forsake" in 12:1, 5. **"came" → "went"** — Hebrew וַיֵּלְכוּ. **"had cast them off from executing the priest's office" → "had cast them off from being priests"** — Hebrew הִזְנִיחָם … מִכַּהֵן; the verb is singular, with "Jeroboam and his sons" as subject.
- **"he ordained him priests" → "he made priests stand for himself"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֶד־לוֹ, "make stand" (so also v22). **"devils" → "male goats"** — Hebrew שְׂעִירִים, the plain word for male goats (Lev 4:24; 17:7). That beings in goat form are meant ("goat-demons", "satyrs") is an interpretation; the word itself says only "male goats".
- **"such as set their hearts to seek" → "those who gave their heart to seek"** (v16) — Hebrew הַנֹּתְנִים אֶת־לְבָבָם, "give", and "heart" in the singular (1 Chr 22:19). לְבַקֵּשׁ "to seek" is another verb than דָּרַשׁ "seek" (12:14); English has one word for both.
- **"strengthened … made strong" → "made strong … strengthened"** (v17) — Hebrew חִזֵּק and אִמֵּץ, two verbs (Deut 3:28). "For three years" stands twice, לְשָׁנִים שָׁלוֹשׁ.
- **"the daughter of Jerimoth"** (v18) — the reading tradition (qere) is בַּת "daughter"; the written text (ketiv) has בן "son". **"and Abihail" → "Abihail"** — the Hebrew sets the name down with no "and" and no object marker. Whether Abihail is a second wife or the mother of Mahalath ("daughter of Jerimoth … and of Abihail") is not said; v19–20 go on with "she" and "after her" of one woman.
- **"above all his wives" → "more than all his wives and his concubines"** (v21) — Hebrew מִכָּל־נָשָׁיו וּפִילַגְשָׁיו. **"took"** — Hebrew נָשָׂא, literally "lifted up" (Ruth 1:4). **"begat" → "begot"** — Hebrew וַיּוֹלֶד. The Hebrew order of the numbers is "wives eighteen … twenty and eight sons".
- **"made Abijah … the chief, to be ruler among his brethren: for he thought to make him king" → "made Abijah … stand as the head, as leader among his brothers, for it was to make him king"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֵד לָרֹאשׁ … לְנָגִיד; כִּי לְהַמְלִיכוֹ is literally "for to make him reign": "it was" is supplied, "he thought / intended" is not written.
- **"he dealt wisely" → "he acted with understanding"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיָּבֶן, from בִּין "understand". **"dispersed of all his children throughout all the countries" → "spread abroad some of all his sons to all the lands"** — Hebrew וַיִּפְרֹץ מִכָּל־בָּנָיו: פָּרַץ "break out, spread abroad" (Gen 28:14); מִן "of, from among", so "some" is supplied; אֲרָצוֹת "lands". **"victual in abundance" → "the nourishment in abundance"** — Hebrew הַמָּזוֹן, with the article.
- **"he desired many wives" → "he asked for a multitude of wives"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאַל הֲמוֹן נָשִׁים: שָׁאַל "ask", הָמוֹן "multitude". For whom he asked, for his sons or for himself, is not said; the earlier text supplied "for them".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 12:21-24** — Chronicler adds extensive unique material (v5-23) on Rehoboam's defenses, Levite migration, wives, and sons.
- **Shemaiah's oracle** — prevents civil war. "From me this thing has come to be."
- **Judean chain of defenses** — fifteen cities mostly along western/southern Judah, defensive ring against Egypt and Philistia.
- **Levitical migration south** — Jeroboam's non-Levite priesthood displaced northern Levites; Chronicler notes mass southward movement. Strengthened Judah religiously.
- **"Male goats / *se'irim*"** (v15) — polemical: northern worship set beside the goat-worship forbidden in Lev 17:7. Could be goat-idols in physical form or beings thought of in goat form.
- **Three-year faithful start** — turns apostate thereafter (2 Chr 12:1).
- **Abijah via Maacah** — successor chosen by paternal favoritism, not birth order. "Maacah daughter of Absalom" = granddaughter via Tamar daughter of Absalom (2 Sam 14:27, 2 Chr 13:2 calls her "Micaiah daughter of Uriel of Gibeah"). Genealogy complicated.
- **Wife/concubine count** — 18 + 60 = 78 vs Solomon's 1,000. Modest by comparison.
