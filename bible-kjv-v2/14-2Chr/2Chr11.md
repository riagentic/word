## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"devils" → "goat-demons"** (v15) — Hebrew שְׂעִירִים *se'irim*; hairy/goat-shaped demons; KJV "devils" obscures the image.
- **"coasts" → "territory"** (v13).
- **"suburbs" → "pasture lands"** (v14) — *migrashim*; Levitical common lands.
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v10, 23).
- **"victual" → "food"** (v11).
- **"desired many wives" → "took for them many wives"** (v23) — Hebrew ambiguous; could refer either to Rehoboam or to his sons; translating for sons fits context better.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 12:21-24** — Chronicler adds extensive unique material (v5-23) on Rehoboam's fortifications, Levite migration, wives, and sons.
- **Shemaiah's oracle** — prevents civil war. "This thing is from me."
- **Judean fortress chain** — fifteen cities mostly along western/southern Judah, defensive ring against Egypt and Philistia.
- **Levitical migration south** — Jeroboam's non-Levite priesthood displaced northern Levites; Chronicler notes mass southward movement. Strengthened Judah religiously.
- **"Goat-demons / *se'irim*"** (v15) — polemical: northern worship called demonic. Could be goat-idols physical form or spiritual entities.
- **Three-year faithful start** — turns apostate thereafter (2 Chr 12:1).
- **Abijah via Maacah** — successor chosen by paternal favoritism, not birth order. "Maacah daughter of Absalom" = granddaughter via Tamar daughter of Absalom (2 Sam 14:27, 2 Chr 13:2 calls her "Micaiah daughter of Uriel of Gibeah"). Genealogy complicated.
- **Wife/concubine count** — 18 + 60 = 78 vs Solomon's 1,000. Modest by comparison.
