# 2 Chronicles 11

[1] When Rehoboam came to Jerusalem, he assembled from the house of Judah and Benjamin one hundred eighty thousand chosen warriors to fight against Israel, to bring the kingdom back to Rehoboam.

[2] But the word of Yahweh came to Shemaiah the man of *Elohim*, saying,

[3] "Speak to Rehoboam son of Solomon king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, saying,

[4] 'Thus says Yahweh: You shall not go up to fight against your brothers. Each man return to his house, for this thing is from me.'" They obeyed the words of Yahweh, and returned from going against Jeroboam.

[5] Rehoboam lived in Jerusalem and built cities for defense in Judah.

[6] He built Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa,

[7] Beth-zur, Soco, Adullam,

[8] Gath, Mareshah, Ziph,

[9] Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah,

[10] Zorah, Aijalon, and Hebron — fortified cities in Judah and in Benjamin.

[11] He strengthened the strongholds and placed commanders in them, with stores of food, oil, and wine.

[12] In each city he put shields and spears, and made them exceedingly strong. Judah and Benjamin were his.

[13] The priests and the Levites throughout all Israel presented themselves to him from every territory.

[14] The Levites left their pasture lands and their possession and came to Judah and Jerusalem, for Jeroboam and his sons had rejected them from serving as priests to Yahweh.

[15] He appointed for himself priests for the high places, for the goat-demons, and for the calves that he had made.

[16] After them, from all the tribes of Israel, those who set their hearts to seek Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel came to Jerusalem to sacrifice to Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers.

[17] So they strengthened the kingdom of Judah and made Rehoboam son of Solomon strong for three years, for they walked in the way of David and Solomon for three years.

[18] Rehoboam took as a wife Mahalath daughter of Jerimoth son of David, and of Abihail daughter of Eliab son of Jesse.

[19] She bore him sons: Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham.

[20] After her he took Maacah daughter of Absalom; she bore him Abijah, Attai, Ziza, and Shelomith.

[21] Rehoboam loved Maacah daughter of Absalom above all his wives and concubines; for he had taken eighteen wives and sixty concubines, and fathered twenty-eight sons and sixty daughters.

[22] Rehoboam appointed Abijah son of Maacah as the chief, as leader among his brothers; for he intended to make him king.

[23] He dealt wisely and distributed some of his sons throughout all the territories of Judah and Benjamin, to every fortified city; he gave them provisions in abundance, and took for them many wives.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv2, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"devils" → "goat-demons"** (v15) — Hebrew שְׂעִירִים *se'irim*; hairy/goat-shaped demons; KJV "devils" obscures the image.
- **"coasts" → "territory"** (v13).
- **"suburbs" → "pasture lands"** (v14) — *migrashim*; Levitical common lands.
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v10, 23).
- **"victual" → "food"** (v11).
- **"desired many wives" → "took for them many wives"** (v23) — Hebrew ambiguous; could refer either to Rehoboam or to his sons; translating for sons fits context better.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 12:21-24** — Chronicler adds extensive unique material (v5-23) on Rehoboam's fortifications, Levite migration, wives, and sons.
- **Shemaiah's oracle** — prevents civil war. "This thing is from me."
- **Judean fortress chain** — fifteen cities mostly along western/southern Judah, defensive ring against Egypt and Philistia.
- **Levitical migration south** — Jeroboam's non-Levite priesthood displaced northern Levites; Chronicler notes mass southward movement. Strengthened Judah religiously.
- **"Goat-demons / *se'irim*"** (v15) — polemical: northern worship called demonic. Could be goat-idols physical form or spiritual entities.
- **Three-year faithful start** — turns apostate thereafter (2 Chr 12:1).
- **Abijah via Maacah** — successor chosen by paternal favoritism, not birth order. "Maacah daughter of Absalom" = granddaughter via Tamar daughter of Absalom (2 Sam 14:27, 2 Chr 13:2 calls her "Micaiah daughter of Uriel of Gibeah"). Genealogy complicated.
- **Wife/concubine count** — 18 + 60 = 78 vs Solomon's 1,000. Modest by comparison.
