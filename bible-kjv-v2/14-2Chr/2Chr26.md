# 2 Chronicles 26

[1] All the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in the place of his father Amaziah.

[2] He rebuilt Eloth and restored it to Judah, after the king slept with his fathers.

[3] Uzziah was sixteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.

[4] He did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that his father Amaziah had done.

[5] He sought *Elohim* in the days of Zechariah, who had understanding in the visions of *Elohim*; as long as he sought Yahweh, *Elohim* made him prosper.

[6] He went out and made war against the Philistines, broke down the wall of Gath, the wall of Jabneh, and the wall of Ashdod; and he built cities in the territory of Ashdod and among the Philistines.

[7] *Elohim* helped him against the Philistines, against the Arabians who lived at Gur-baal, and against the Meunites.

[8] The Ammonites paid tribute to Uzziah; his fame spread even to the entrance of Egypt, for he had grown exceedingly strong.

[9] Moreover, Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the Corner Gate, the Valley Gate, and at the Angle, and fortified them.

[10] He built towers in the wilderness and hewed out many cisterns, for he had much livestock in the Shephelah and in the plain; he had farmers and vinedressers in the hills and in Carmel, for he loved the soil.

[11] Uzziah had an army of fighting men, who went out to war in companies according to the number of their muster by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer, under the hand of Hananiah, one of the king's commanders.

[12] The total number of the heads of fathers' houses, of mighty warriors, was two thousand six hundred.

[13] Under their hand was an army of three hundred seven thousand five hundred, who waged war with mighty power to help the king against the enemy.

[14] Uzziah provided for all the army shields, spears, helmets, coats of mail, bows, and stones for slinging.

[15] In Jerusalem he made engines, invented by skilled men, to be on the towers and on the corners, to shoot arrows and great stones. His fame spread far, for he was marvelously helped, until he was strong.

[16] But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up, to his destruction; he acted unfaithfully against Yahweh his *Elohim* and entered the temple of Yahweh to burn incense on the altar of incense.

[17] Azariah the priest went in after him, and with him eighty priests of Yahweh, men of valor;

[18] they withstood King Uzziah and said to him, "It is not for you, Uzziah, to burn incense to Yahweh, but for the priests, the sons of Aaron, who are consecrated to burn incense. Go out of the sanctuary, for you have acted unfaithfully; there will be no honor for you from Yahweh *Elohim*."

[19] Then Uzziah was angry; he had a censer in his hand to burn incense. While he was angry with the priests, skin disease broke out on his forehead in the presence of the priests in the house of Yahweh, by the altar of incense.

[20] When Azariah the chief priest and all the priests looked at him, behold, he had skin disease on his forehead, and they thrust him out from there; he himself hurried to go out, because Yahweh had struck him.

[21] King Uzziah had skin disease until the day of his death; he lived in a quarantined house, for he was cut off from the house of Yahweh. Jotham his son was over the king's house, judging the people of the land.

[22] The rest of the acts of Uzziah, first and last, Isaiah the prophet son of Amoz wrote.

[23] Uzziah slept with his fathers; they buried him with his fathers in the burial field that belonged to the kings, for they said, "He has skin disease." Jotham his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Mehunims" → "Meunites"** (v7) — Arabian tribe.
- **"gave gifts" → "paid tribute"** (v8) — *minchah*.
- **"low country... plains" → "Shephelah... plain"** (v10).
- **"husbandry" → "the soil"** (v10) — *adamah*.
- **"habergeons" → "coats of mail"** (v14) — *shiryonim*.
- **"bulwarks" → "corners"** (v15) — *pinnot*.
- **"several house" → "quarantined house"** (v21).
- **"turning of the wall" → "the Angle"** (v9) — *miqtzoa*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 15:1-7** — Chronicler greatly expands on Uzziah (= Azariah). Kings brief; Chronicler rich.
- **Zechariah teacher** (v5) — different from Zechariah of ch 24. Spiritual mentor.
- **Engineering feats** — towers, cisterns, siege-engines. Uzziah's technological innovation. Some scholars identify these with the first catapult-style weapons in the ancient world.
- **Early reign prosperous; late reign hubristic.** Same pattern as several Judean kings in Chronicles.
- **Temple offense** (v16-20) — Uzziah usurps priestly prerogative. Parallels Saul (1 Sam 13). Priesthood-kingship boundary violated.
- **Incense-burning** was Aaronic exclusively (Num 16:40, 18:7).
- **Struck with *tzara'at* in the temple** — dramatic divine sign; priests intervene. Uzziah stays contagious-cut-off until death.
- **Uzziah's year of death** — Isaiah 6:1's framing point. Earthquake in his reign (Amos 1:1, Zech 14:5) likely tied to this event in later tradition.
- **Isaiah's chronicle** (v22) — source cited; implies written prophetic account now partially lost.
