# 2 Chronicles 26

[1] And all the people of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ took ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧, and he was sixteen years old, and they made him king in place of his father ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧.

[2] He built ⟦eloth|Eilot|Eloth⟧ and returned it to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, after the king lay with his fathers.

[3] ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ was sixteen years old when he began to reign, and fifty-two years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦jecoliah|Yecholyahu|Jecoliah⟧ of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[4] And he did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to all that ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ his father had done.

[5] And he was seeking ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the days of ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧, who had understanding in the seeing of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and in the days of his seeking ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ made him prosper.

[6] And he went out and fought against the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and he breached the wall of ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ and the wall of ⟦jabneh|Yavneh|Jabneh⟧ and the wall of Ashdod, and he built cities in Ashdod and among the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧.

[7] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ helped him against the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and against the ⟦arabians|Arvim|Arabians⟧ who dwelt in ⟦gur-baal|Gur Baal|Gur-baal⟧, and the ⟦meunites|Meunim|Meunites⟧.

[8] And the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧ gave an offering to ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧, and his name went as far as the entrance of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, for he grew exceedingly strong.

[9] And ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ built towers in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, at the Gate of the Corner and at the Gate of the Valley and at the Angle, and he made them strong.

[10] And he built towers in the wilderness and hewed many pits, for he had much livestock, both in the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧ and in the tableland; farmers and vinedressers in the mountains and in ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧, for he was a lover of the ground.

[11] And ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ had a force making war, who went out to the host by bands, by the number of their numbering by the hand of ⟦jeiel|Yeiel|Jeiel⟧ the scribe and ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ the officer, at the hand of ⟦hananiah|Chananyah|Hananiah⟧, one of the princes of the king.

[12] The whole number of the heads of the fathers, of the mighty ones of ability, was two thousand six hundred.

[13] And at their hand was a force of the host, three hundred and seven thousand five hundred, making war with the power of ability, to help the king against the enemy.

[14] And ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ prepared for them, for all the host, shields and spears and helmets and coats of armor and bows, and stones for slings.

[15] And he made in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ devices, the design of a designer, to be on the towers and on the corners, to shoot with arrows and with great stones. And his name went out far away, for he was wonderfully helped, until he was strong.

[16] And when he was strong, his heart was high, even to working ruin, and he acted unfaithfully against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he came into the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to make smoke on the altar of incense.

[17] And ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the priest came in after him, and with him eighty priests of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, sons of ability.

[18] And they stood against ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ the king and said to him, "It is not for you, ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧, to make smoke to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, but for the priests, the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, who are sanctified to make smoke. Go out of the sanctuary, for you have acted unfaithfully, and it is not for glory to you from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[19] And ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ raged, and in his hand was a censer to make smoke; and in his raging with the priests, the leprosy rose on his forehead before the priests in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, beside the altar of incense.

[20] And ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the head priest and all the priests turned to him, and behold, he was leprous on his forehead; and they hurried him from there, and he himself also hastened to go out, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had plagued him.

[21] And ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ the king was a leper until the day of his death, and he dwelt in the house of freedom, a leper, for he was cut off from the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ his son was over the house of the king, judging the people of the land.

[22] And the rest of the acts of ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧, the first and the last, ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧, the prophet, wrote.

[23] And ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ lay with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the field of the burial place that belonged to the kings, for they said, "He is a leper." And ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v5 (the second and the third time) and v7 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"Then all the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was sixteen years old" → "And all the people of Judah took Uzziah, and he was sixteen years old"** (v1–4) — the Hebrew of v1–4 is that of 2 Kgs 14:21–22; 15:2–3, and the wording is the same. Chronicles differs in these points: the king is "Uzziah" (Kings: "Azariah"); "Eloth" (Kings: "Elath"); v3 names him, with no "was" (Kings: "He was sixteen years old"). בֶּן־שֵׁשׁ עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה is literally "a son of sixteen years". מָלַךְ with בְּ is "when he began to reign"; with the number of years "reigned".
- **"Jecoliah"** (v3) — the reading tradition (qere) has יְכָלְיָה, "Yecholyah"; the written form (ketiv) is יכיליה, "Yechilyah". 2 Kgs 15:2 has the longer form יְכָלְיָהוּ.
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v2, 23) — Hebrew שָׁכַב.
- **"he sought God" → "he was seeking Elohim"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיְהִי לִדְרֹשׁ, literally "and he was to seek": he was given to seeking. דָּרַשׁ is "seek", twice in the verse.
- **"who had understanding in the visions of God" → "who had understanding in the seeing of Elohim"** (v5) — Hebrew בִּרְאֹת הָאֱלֹהִים, the infinitive of "see": either seeing Elohim or what Elohim lets one see. A reading "in the fear of Elohim" (בְּיִרְאַת) is often followed (other Hebrew manuscripts and old versions: from memory); the verse keeps the Masoretic Text.
- **"brake down the wall" → "breached the wall"** (v6) — Hebrew פָּרַץ. **"about Ashdod" → "in Ashdod"** — Hebrew בְּאַשְׁדּוֹד; no word for "territory".
- **"Mehunims" → "Meunites"** (v7) — Hebrew הַמְּעוּנִים, without a second "against". "Arabians": the qere הָעַרְבִים; the ketiv הערביים has the same sense.
- **"gave gifts" → "gave an offering"** (v8) — Hebrew מִנְחָה, the offering a subject people brings: tribute.
- **"his name spread abroad even to the entering in of Egypt; for he strengthened himself exceedingly" → "his name went as far as the entrance of Egypt, for he grew exceedingly strong"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ שְׁמוֹ "his name went"; הֶחֱזִיק עַד־לְמָעְלָה, literally "he showed strength, even upward". The root חזק runs through the chapter: "made them strong" (v9), "until he was strong" (v15), "when he was strong" (v16; literally "at his strength").
- **"at the corner gate, and at the valley gate, and at the turning of the wall, and fortified them" → "at the Gate of the Corner and at the Gate of the Valley and at the Angle, and he made them strong"** (v9) — Hebrew עַל "upon / at"; הַמִּקְצוֹעַ "the Angle", a place in the wall (Neh 3:19); חִזֵּק "make strong".
- **"digged many wells" → "hewed many pits"** (v10) — Hebrew בֹּרוֹת, pits hewn in the rock to hold water: cisterns.
- **"both in the low country, and in the plains: husbandmen also" → "both in the Shephelah and in the tableland; farmers and vinedressers"** (v10) — Hebrew הַמִּישׁוֹר "the level land", elsewhere the tableland east of the Jordan. The second half has no verb. "The mountains": Hebrew plural.
- **"in Carmel"** (v10) — Hebrew בַּכַּרְמֶל, with the article. Either the place Carmel (in Judah: 1 Sam 25:2) or the common noun "garden land, fruitful land". Not decided by the Hebrew.
- **"he loved husbandry" → "he was a lover of the ground"** (v10) — Hebrew אֹהֵב אֲדָמָה, a participle.
- **"an host of fighting men, that went out to war by bands, according to the number of their account" → "a force making war, who went out to the host by bands, by the number of their numbering"** (v11) — Hebrew חַיִל "force"; יוֹצְאֵי צָבָא "those who go out to the host" (Num 1:3); גְּדוּד, the band that goes out on a raid (singular: "by band"); פְּקֻדָּה, from פָּקַד "number, muster".
- **"Jeiel"** (v11) — the qere יְעִיאֵל; the ketiv יעואל is "Jeuel". **"the ruler" → "the officer"** — Hebrew שֹׁטֵר. **"under the hand of" → "at the hand of"** — Hebrew עַל יַד. **"one of the king's captains" → "one of the princes of the king"** — Hebrew שַׂר, "prince", of an army "commander": the one word.
- **"The whole number of the chief of the fathers of the mighty men of valour" → "The whole number of the heads of the fathers, of the mighty ones of ability"** (v12) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת; גִּבּוֹרֵי חָיִל.
- **"under their hand was an army … that made war with mighty power" → "at their hand was a force of the host … making war with the power of ability"** (v13) — Hebrew חֵיל צָבָא; בְּכֹחַ חָיִל. חַיִל is one word here for "force" (v11, 13) and "ability" (v12, 13, 17).
- **"habergeons … and slings to cast stones" → "coats of armor … and stones for slings"** (v14) — Hebrew שִׁרְיֹנוֹת, the plural of "armor" ("coats of" supplied); וּלְאַבְנֵי קְלָעִים, literally "and for stones of slings".
- **"engines, invented by cunning men" → "devices, the design of a designer"** (v15) — Hebrew חִשְּׁבֹנוֹת מַחֲשֶׁבֶת חוֹשֵׁב, three words of the one root חָשַׁב "think, devise". What they were is not said: machines for shooting, or structures on the walls from which men shot. Uncertain.
- **"bulwarks" → "corners"** (v15) — Hebrew פִּנּוֹת.
- **"his name spread far abroad; for he was marvellously helped" → "his name went out far away, for he was wonderfully helped"** (v15) — Hebrew עַד־לְמֵרָחוֹק, literally "as far as to from-afar"; הִפְלִיא לְהֵעָזֵר, literally "he did wonderfully in being helped".
- **"his heart was lifted up to his destruction: for he transgressed" → "his heart was high, even to working ruin, and he acted unfaithfully"** (v16) — Hebrew גָּבַהּ "be high"; עַד־לְהַשְׁחִית, the verb "ruin" with no object: whether he ruined himself or did a ruinous deed is not said ("his" is not in the Hebrew). מָעַל is a breach of trust (v16, 18; 1 Chr 10:13).
- **"to burn incense" → "to make smoke"** (v16, 18 twice, 19) — Hebrew הִקְטִיר with no object, the literal sense of the verb. The altar of incense (מִזְבַּח הַקְּטֹרֶת) and the censer show that incense is meant; the verb and the noun are of one root.
- **"valiant men" → "sons of ability"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־חָיִל. "Priests of Yahweh": literally "priests to Yahweh, eighty".
- **"they withstood Uzziah" → "they stood against Uzziah"** (v18) — Hebrew עָמַד עַל. **"It appertaineth not unto thee" → "It is not for you"** — Hebrew לֹא־לְךָ, "not to you". **"consecrated" → "sanctified"** — Hebrew הַמְקֻדָּשִׁים. **"neither shall it be for thine honour" → "it is not for glory to you"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־לְךָ לְכָבוֹד, with no verb.
- **"Uzziah was wroth … while he was wroth" → "Uzziah raged … in his raging"** (v19) — Hebrew זָעַף, here of anger; in Gen 40:6 the same verb is "be dejected".
- **"the leprosy even rose up in his forehead" → "the leprosy rose on his forehead"** (v19) — Hebrew זָרְחָה, the verb of the rising sun (Deut 33:2 "dawn"). צָרַעַת and מְצֹרָע (v20, 21, 23) are "leprosy", "leper / leprous" as in Lev 13: a scaly outbreak that makes unclean. It is not the leprosy of modern medicine, and no diagnosis is put in the verse.
- **"from beside the incense altar" → "beside the altar of incense"** (v19) — Hebrew מֵעַל לְ, literally "from upon to".
- **"looked upon him" → "turned to him"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּפֶן. **"the chief priest" → "the head priest"** — Hebrew כֹהֵן הָרֹאשׁ (2 Kgs 25:18).
- **"they thrust him out from thence; yea, himself hasted also to go out, because the LORD had smitten him" → "they hurried him from there, and he himself also hastened to go out, for Yahweh had plagued him"** (v20) — Hebrew הִבְהִיל "hurry" (also "dismay"); נִדְחַף, literally "was pushed" (Esth 6:12); נִגַּע, the piel of "touch": strike with a plague (נֶגַע), as 2 Kgs 15:5.
- **"dwelt in a several house, being a leper" → "dwelt in the house of freedom, a leper"** (v21) — Hebrew בֵּית הַחָפְשִׁית (the qere; the ketiv is החפשות), word for word, as 2 Kgs 15:5; חָפְשִׁי is "free" (Exod 21:2). What the name says is not known. Uncertain. The verse stands beside 2 Kgs 15:5; Chronicles alone has "Uzziah the king", the second "a leper", "for he was cut off from the house of Yahweh", "his son" (Kings: "the son of the king") and "the house of the king" (Kings: "the house"); Kings alone has "And Yahweh plagued the king" (here v20).
- **"cut off"** (v21) — Hebrew נִגְזַר, the nifal of גָּזַר "cut".
- **"the rest of the acts of Uzziah, first and last, did Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, write" → "the rest of the acts of Uzziah, the first and the last, Isaiah the son of Amoz, the prophet, wrote"** (v22) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי "words / matters". 2 Kgs 15:6 names "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah" instead.
- **"in the field of the burial which belonged to the kings" → "in the field of the burial place that belonged to the kings"** (v23) — Hebrew בִּשְׂדֵה הַקְּבוּרָה. 2 Kgs 15:7 has "in the city of David" and no "for they said, He is a leper".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 14:21–22; 15:1–7** — Chronicles greatly expands on Uzziah (= Azariah). Kings is brief; v5–20 stand only here.
- **Zechariah** (v5) — different from Zechariah of ch 24.
- **Works** — towers, pits, devices on the walls (v9–10, 15).
- **Early reign prosperous; late reign proud.** Same pattern as several kings of Judah in Chronicles.
- **Temple offense** (v16–20) — Uzziah takes what belongs to the priests. Compare Saul (1 Sam 13).
- **The smoke of incense** was for the sons of Aaron only (Num 16:40; 18:7).
- **Struck with *tzara'at* in the house of Yahweh** — the priests see it; Uzziah stays cut off until his death.
- **The year of Uzziah's death** — Isa 6:1. An earthquake in his days: Amos 1:1; Zech 14:5.
- **Isaiah** (v22) — named as the writer of the source.
