# 2 Chronicles 12

[1] And it came to pass, when the kingdom of ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ was established and when he was strong, that he forsook the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ with him.

[2] And it came to pass in the fifth year of King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ that ⟦shishak|Shishaq|Shishak⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ came up against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, for they had acted unfaithfully against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[3] with a thousand two hundred chariots and with sixty thousand horsemen; and there was no number to the people who came with him from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧: ⟦libyans|Luvim|Libyans⟧, ⟦sukkites|Sukkiyim|Sukkiim⟧ and ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧.

[4] And he captured the cities of defense that belonged to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he came as far as ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[5] And ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ the prophet came to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ and the princes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, who had gathered themselves to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from the face of ⟦shishak|Shishaq|Shishak⟧, and he said to them, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'You have forsaken me, and I also have forsaken you in the hand of ⟦shishak|Shishaq|Shishak⟧.'"

[6] And the princes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the king brought themselves low, and they said, "Righteous is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[7] And when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saw that they had brought themselves low, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧, saying, "They have brought themselves low; I will not destroy them, and I will give them a little for an escape, and my fury shall not be poured out on ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ by the hand of ⟦shishak|Shishaq|Shishak⟧.

[8] But they shall be servants to him, and they shall know my service and the service of the kingdoms of the lands."

[9] And ⟦shishak|Shishaq|Shishak⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ came up against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and he took the treasures of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the treasures of the house of the king; he took everything; and he took the shields of gold that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had made.

[10] And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ made shields of bronze in their place, and he entrusted them to the hand of the chiefs of the runners who kept the entrance of the house of the king.

[11] And it came to pass, as often as the king came to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that the runners would come and carry them, and they would bring them back to the room of the runners.

[12] And when he brought himself low, the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ turned back from him, and it was not to destroy to a full end; and also in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ there were good things.

[13] And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ strengthened himself in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and reigned; for ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ was forty-one years old when he began to reign, and seventeen years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, the city that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ chose to put his name there, out of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. And the name of his mother was Naamah the ⟦ammonites|Ammonit|Ammonite⟧.

[14] And he did the evil, for he did not establish his heart to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] And the acts of ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧, the first and the last, are they not written in the words of ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ the prophet and Iddo the seer, to be enrolled by genealogy? And the wars of ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ were all the days.

[16] And ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ lay with his fathers and was buried in the city of David. And ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel: 1 Kgs 14:21–31** (v2, 9–11, 13, 15–16) — where the Hebrew of the two chapters is the same, the wording is that of 1 Kings 14; every difference below is a difference in the Hebrew of Chronicles. Nothing is taken over from Kings, and the figures stand as Chronicles prints them.
- **"when Rehoboam had established the kingdom, and had strengthened himself" → "when the kingdom of Rehoboam was established and when he was strong"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּהָכִין מַלְכוּת רְחַבְעָם וּכְחֶזְקָתוֹ, literally "at the establishing of the kingdom of Rehoboam and at his strength": the infinitive names no one who establishes, and חֶזְקָה is a noun. **"forsook"** — Hebrew עָזַב (v1, 5).
- **"because they had transgressed against the LORD" → "for they had acted unfaithfully against Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew מָעֲלוּ בַּיהוָה, a breach of trust (1 Chr 10:13). The rest of the verse is 1 Kgs 14:25 word for word; Kings does not have this clause.
- **"twelve hundred chariots, and threescore thousand horsemen" → "a thousand two hundred chariots and … sixty thousand horsemen"** (v3) — Hebrew "a thousand and two hundred … sixty thousand". **"the people were without number" → "there was no number to the people"** — Hebrew וְאֵין מִסְפָּר לָעָם. **"the Lubims, the Sukkiims, and the Ethiopians" → "Libyans, Sukkiim and Cushites"** — Hebrew לוּבִים סֻכִּיִּים וְכוּשִׁים, without articles and with one "and". Who the Sukkiim were is not said.
- **"he took the fenced cities which pertained to Judah, and came to Jerusalem" → "he captured the cities of defense that belonged to Judah, and he came as far as Jerusalem"** (v4) — Hebrew לָכַד "capture"; עָרֵי הַמְּצֻרוֹת as in 11:10, 23; עַד "as far as".
- **"that were gathered together to Jerusalem because of Shishak" → "who had gathered themselves to Jerusalem from the face of Shishak"** (v5) — Hebrew נֶאֶסְפוּ … מִפְּנֵי שִׁישָׁק. **"princes"** — Hebrew שָׂרֵי (v5, 6); the same word is "chiefs" in v10.
- **"Ye have forsaken me, and therefore have I also left you in the hand of Shishak" → "You have forsaken me, and I also have forsaken you in the hand of Shishak"** (v5) — Hebrew אַתֶּם עֲזַבְתֶּם אֹתִי וְאַף־אֲנִי עָזַבְתִּי אֶתְכֶם: one verb, עָזַב, said twice, and both pronouns written out. "Therefore" is not in the Hebrew. "Forsaken you in the hand of": left you to his power.
- **"humbled themselves" → "brought themselves low"** (v6, 7, 12) — Hebrew נִכְנַע, the passive-reflexive of הִכְנִיעַ "bring low" (Judg 3:30; 1 Kgs 21:29). "Humble oneself" renders another verb (הִתְעַנָּה). **"The LORD is righteous" → "Righteous is Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew צַדִּיק יְהוָה, in this order. Here the princes are "of Israel"; in v5 "of Judah".
- **"the word of the LORD came"** (v7) — Hebrew הָיָה דְבַר־יְהוָה. **"I will grant them some deliverance" → "I will give them a little for an escape"** — Hebrew וְנָתַתִּי לָהֶם כִּמְעַט לִפְלֵיטָה, literally "and I will give to them as a little, for escape". פְּלֵיטָה is "escape" (Gen 45:7), not "deliverance". כִּמְעַט may be a measure ("some little escape") or a time ("in a little while"); the Hebrew does not decide. **"my wrath" → "my fury"** — Hebrew חֵמָה, literally "heat". **"destroy"** — Hebrew הִשְׁחִית (v7, 12).
- **"Nevertheless they shall be his servants; that they may know my service, and the service of the kingdoms of the countries" → "But they shall be servants to him, and they shall know my service and the service of the kingdoms of the lands"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי, "but" after the negatives of v7 (also "for"); וְיֵדְעוּ "and they shall know" (also "that they may know"). עֲבוֹדָתִי וַעֲבוֹדַת מַמְלְכוֹת הָאֲרָצוֹת is "my service and the service of the kingdoms of the lands" and no more; "the difference between" of the earlier text is not written.
- **"So Shishak … came up"** (v9) — the clause of v2 is taken up again. **"he took all" → "he took everything"** — Hebrew אֶת־הַכֹּל לָקָח, with no "and" before it (Kings: "and he took everything"). **"he carried away also the shields of gold" → "and he took the shields of gold"** — Hebrew וַיִּקַּח, the same verb three times; Kings has "all the shields of gold".
- **"committed them to the hands of the chief of the guard, that kept the entrance of the king's house" → "entrusted them to the hand of the chiefs of the runners who kept the entrance of the house of the king"** (v10) — the verse is 1 Kgs 14:27 (Chronicles writes תַּחְתֵּיהֶם for תַּחְתָּם, with the same meaning). הָרָצִים is "the runners", the king's guard (1 Sam 22:17); שָׂרֵי is plural, "chiefs", and יַד singular, "hand". **"brass" → "bronze"** — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת.
- **"the guard came and fetched them, and brought them again into the guard chamber" → "the runners would come and carry them, and they would bring them back to the room of the runners"** (v11) — Hebrew בָּאוּ הָרָצִים וּנְשָׂאוּם, of what was done each time; Kings has only "the runners would carry them". תָּא is a room.
- **"when he humbled himself, the wrath of the LORD turned from him, that he would not destroy him altogether" → "when he brought himself low, the anger of Yahweh turned back from him, and it was not to destroy to a full end"** (v12) — Hebrew אַף "anger" (literally "nose"), another word than the "fury" of v7; וְלֹא לְהַשְׁחִית לְכָלָה is literally "and not to destroy to completion": no "him" is written, and "it was" is supplied. **"in Judah things went well" → "in Judah there were good things"** — Hebrew הָיָה דְּבָרִים טוֹבִים; דְּבָרִים is "things" or "words".
- **"strengthened himself"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּתְחַזֵּק. **"for Rehoboam was one and forty years old …"** — from here the verse is 1 Kgs 14:21 word for word, joined on by כִּי "for". "Forty-one years old" is literally "a son of forty and one years"; מָלַךְ "reign" is "began to reign" of the accession and "reigned" with the number of years.
- **"he did evil, because he prepared not his heart to seek the LORD" → "he did the evil, for he did not establish his heart to seek Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew הָרָע with the article; Kings says it of Judah and adds "in the eyes of Yahweh" (1 Kgs 14:22). הֵכִין לִבּוֹ "establish his heart" (1 Sam 7:3), the verb of v1; לִדְרוֹשׁ "to seek" (1 Chr 28:9).
- **"the acts of Rehoboam, first and last, are they not written in the book of Shemaiah the prophet, and of Iddo the seer concerning genealogies?" → "… in the words of Shemaiah the prophet and Iddo the seer, to be enrolled by genealogy?"** (v15) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי twice: literally "the words of Rehoboam … in the words of Shemaiah"; no "book" is written (1 Chr 29:29). Kings names another source, "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah". לְהִתְיַחֵשׂ, "to be enrolled by genealogy" (1 Chr 9:1), is set down as it stands; what it refers to here is not known.
- **"there were wars between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually" → "the wars of Rehoboam and Jeroboam were all the days"** (v15) — Hebrew וּמִלְחֲמוֹת רְחַבְעָם וְיָרָבְעָם כָּל־הַיָּמִים, with no verb; Kings has "there was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכַּב. Kings has "was buried with his fathers" and gives the name of his mother once more; the son is אֲבִיָּה "Abijah" here, "Abijam" in Kings.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 14:21-31** — Chronicler expands on Shishak with theological framing.
- **Shishak = Sheshonq I** — first Libyan pharaoh, 22nd Dynasty. Campaign ca. 925 BC. Karnak temple reliefs list conquered Levantine cities — historical confirmation.
- **Troop composition** — Libyans, Sukkiim (often taken as Egyptian mercenaries), Cushites (Nubians). Matches Sheshonq's mixed Egyptian force.
- **Bringing oneself low → partial reprieve** — Chronicler's repeated theological pattern (cf. 7:14). Judgment averted but consequences retained (vassalage, tribute).
- **"They shall know my service and the service of the kingdoms"** (v8) — educational judgment: Judah learns by contrast. Proto-exile lesson.
- **Bronze for gold** — diminished splendor; symbolic of Rehoboam's compromised reign.
- **"To be enrolled by genealogy"** (v15) — Chronicler's pronounced interest in lineage and census documentation.
