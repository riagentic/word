## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"grievous" → "harsh"** (v4) — *qasheh*.
- **"tribute" → "forced labor"** (v18) — *mas*.
- **"chastised" → "disciplined"** (v11, 14).
- **"the cause was of God" → "a turn of events from *Elohim*"** (v15) — *nesibbah me-im ha-elohim*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 12:1-19** — nearly identical to Kings. Chronicler reproduces the division-of-kingdom narrative.
- **Shechem** — site of tribal covenants (Josh 24); significant northern center. Rehoboam must go north for ratification.
- **Jeroboam** — Chronicler simply notes his return; omits backstory of Ahijah's oracle (1 Kgs 11) since he omitted Solomon's apostasy.
- **Chronicler's interest** — the division is still divine providence, fulfilling Ahijah's word.
- **Hadoram = Adoniram / Adoram** — same tax official.
- **"To this day"** (v19) — Chronicler reuses Kings' formula; his "this day" is post-exilic, yet ten tribes never reunited with Judah.
- **Chronicler's ambivalence** — Northern tribes stand for rebellion; yet Chronicler still calls them Israel, anticipating reunion under reformer kings (2 Chr 30).
