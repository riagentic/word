# 2 Chronicles 10

[1] And ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ went to Shechem, for to Shechem all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had come to make him king.

[2] And it came to pass, when ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧ heard — and he was in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, where he had fled from the face of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ the king — that ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ returned from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[3] And they sent and called him; and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came, and they spoke to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧, saying,

[4] "Your father made our yoke hard; and now, lighten the hard service of your father and his heavy yoke that he put on us, and we will serve you."

[5] And he said to them, "Three days more, and return to me." And the people went.

[6] And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ took counsel with the elders who had been standing before ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his father while he was alive, saying, "How do you counsel to return word to this people?"

[7] And they spoke to him, saying, "If you will be for good to this people and be pleased with them and speak good words to them, then they will be your servants all the days."

[8] And he forsook the counsel of the elders, which they had counseled him, and took counsel with the children who had grown up with him, who were standing before him.

[9] And he said to them, "What do you counsel, that we may return word to this people, who have spoken to me, saying, 'Lighten the yoke that your father put on us'?"

[10] And the children who had grown up with him spoke with him, saying, "Thus you shall say to the people who have spoken to you, saying, 'Your father made our yoke heavy, and you, lighten it from upon us' — thus you shall say to them: 'My little finger is thicker than the loins of my father.

[11] And now, my father loaded on you a heavy yoke, and I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, and I with scorpions.'"

[12] And ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ and all the people came to ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ on the third day, as the king had spoken, saying, "Return to me on the third day."

[13] And the king answered them a hard thing, and King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ forsook the counsel of the elders,

[14] and he spoke to them according to the counsel of the children, saying, "I will make your yoke heavy, and I, I will add to it; my father disciplined you with whips, and I with scorpions."

[15] And the king did not listen to the people, for it was a turn of things from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in order that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ might establish his word, which he had spoken by the hand of ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧ the ⟦shilonite|Shiloni|Shilonite⟧ to ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧.

[16] And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — for the king had not listened to them — the people replied to the king, saying,

"What share have we in David?
And there is no inheritance in the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧.
Each man to your tents, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!
Now see to your house, David."

And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went to its tents.

[17] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who dwelt in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ — ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ reigned over them.

[18] And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ sent Hadoram, who was over the forced labor, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ stoned him with stone, and he died. And King ⟦rehoboam|Rechavam|Rehoboam⟧ exerted himself to go up into the chariot, to flee to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[19] And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ has revolted against the house of David to this day.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v15 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **The parallel in Kings** (throughout) — the chapter runs beside 1 Kgs 12:1–19. Where the Hebrew of the two books is the same, the wording is that of 1 Kings 12; where Chronicles has another word, form or order, the verse follows Chronicles. The differences that touch the meaning are named below; nothing is taken over from Kings.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"And it came to pass"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיְהִי.
- **"for to Shechem were all Israel come to make him king" → "for to Shechem all Israel had come to make him king"** (v1) — Hebrew הִמְלִיךְ, the causative of "reign".
- **"who was in Egypt, whither he had fled from the presence of Solomon the king, heard it, that Jeroboam returned out of Egypt" → "heard — and he was in Egypt, where he had fled from the face of Solomon the king — that Jeroboam returned from Egypt"** (v2) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי "from the face of"; "it" is not in the Hebrew. Chronicles has וַיָּשָׁב יָרָבְעָם מִמִּצְרָיִם "and Jeroboam returned from Egypt"; Kings, as it is pointed, has "dwelt in Egypt". Chronicles has no "still" and has "Solomon the king" (Kings: "King Solomon").
- **"So Jeroboam and all Israel came" → "and Jeroboam and all Israel came"** (v3) — Kings has "all the assembly of Israel".
- **"Thy father made our yoke grievous: now therefore ease thou somewhat the grievous servitude of thy father" → "Your father made our yoke hard; and now, lighten the hard service of your father"** (v4) — Hebrew הִקְשָׁה "made hard"; הָקֵל מִן, literally "make light from"; קָשָׁה "hard", the same word. Chronicles has "and now" (Kings: "and you, now").
- **"Come again unto me after three days" → "Three days more, and return to me"** (v5) — Hebrew עוֹד שְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים וְשׁוּבוּ אֵלָי; Chronicles has no "Go" (Kings has it).
- **"the old men that had stood before Solomon … What counsel give ye me to return answer to this people" → "the elders who had been standing before Solomon … How do you counsel to return word to this people"** (v6) — Hebrew הַזְּקֵנִים; לְהָשִׁיב … דָּבָר.
- **"If thou be kind to this people, and please them" → "If you will be for good to this people and be pleased with them"** (v7) — Hebrew אִם־תִּהְיֶה לְטוֹב לְהָעָם הַזֶּה וּרְצִיתָם: literally "be for good"; רָצָה is "be pleased with, show favor to". Kings has "If today you will be a servant to this people and serve them and answer them". **"for ever" → "all the days"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים.
- **"the young men that were brought up with him" → "the children who had grown up with him"** (v8, 10, 14) — Hebrew הַיְלָדִים, the word for "children".
- **"What advice give ye that we may return answer" → "What do you counsel, that we may return word"** (v9) — Hebrew נוֹעָצִים; וְנָשִׁיב דָּבָר. **"Ease somewhat the yoke" → "Lighten the yoke"** — Hebrew הָקֵל מִן־הָעֹל.
- **"spake unto him … Thus shalt thou answer the people that spake unto thee … make thou it somewhat lighter for us; thus shalt thou say unto them" → "spoke with him … Thus you shall say to the people who have spoken to you … lighten it from upon us — thus you shall say to them"** (v10) — Hebrew אִתּוֹ "with him" (Kings: "to him"); "the people" (Kings: "this people"); תֹּאמַר "say" both times (Kings: "speak" the second time). קָטָנִּי is literally "my little one"; a finger is commonly understood.
- **"For whereas my father put a heavy yoke upon you, I will put more to your yoke: my father chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions" → "And now, my father loaded on you a heavy yoke, and I will add to your yoke; my father disciplined you with whips, and I with scorpions"** (v11, 14) — Hebrew הֶעְמִיס "load"; יִסַּר "discipline". Chronicles has only וַאֲנִי בָּעֲקְרַבִּים, "and I with scorpions"; the verb is not said again (Kings says it).
- **"So Jeroboam and all the people came … Come again to me on the third day" → "And Jeroboam and all the people came … Return to me on the third day"** (v12) — Hebrew שׁוּבוּ.
- **"the king answered them roughly; and king Rehoboam forsook the counsel of the old men" → "the king answered them a hard thing, and King Rehoboam forsook the counsel of the elders"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲנֵם … קָשָׁה, the word of v4. Chronicles has "them" (Kings: "the people") and names King Rehoboam, and does not have "which they had counseled him".
- **"My father made your yoke heavy, but I will add thereto" → "I will make your yoke heavy, and I, I will add to it"** (v14) — the Masoretic Text has אַכְבִּיד, "I will make heavy"; Kings has "My father made your yoke heavy", and many manuscripts and printed editions have that here also (from memory). The printed text is translated. עָלָיו "to it" (Kings: "to your yoke").
- **"for the cause was of God, that the LORD might perform his word" → "for it was a turn of things from Elohim, in order that Yahweh might establish his word"** (v15) — Hebrew נְסִבָּה, found only here, from סָבַב "turn": a turn of affairs (Kings has the form סִבָּה). מֵעִם הָאֱלֹהִים "from Elohim" (Kings: "from Yahweh"); הָקִים "make stand". In Chronicles the name Yahweh stands with "establish", in Kings with "had spoken". The name is אֲחִיָּהוּ here (Kings: אֲחִיָּה). "By the hand of" is בְּיַד. The word of Ahijah (1 Kgs 11:29–39) is not told in Chronicles.
- **"And when all Israel saw that the king would not hearken unto them, the people answered the king" → "And all Israel — for the king had not listened to them — the people replied to the king"** (v16) — Chronicles does not have "saw": the sentence begins with "And all Israel" and takes a new subject. וַיָּשִׁיבוּ … אֶת־הַמֶּלֶךְ is "returned … the king", without the "word" that Kings has.
- **"What portion have we in David? and we have none inheritance in the son of Jesse: every man to your tents, O Israel: and now, David, see to thine own house" → "What share have we in David? And there is no inheritance in the son of Jesse. Each man to your tents, Israel! Now see to your house, David"** (v16) — Hebrew חֵלֶק "share"; וְלֹא־נַחֲלָה. אִישׁ לְאֹהָלֶיךָ: "each man" stands only in Chronicles, and "your" is singular. The Hebrew has no "O". רְאֵה בֵיתְךָ is literally "see your house". **"So all Israel went to their tents" → "And all Israel went to its tents"** — Hebrew לְאֹהָלָיו, "to his tents"; "all" stands only in Chronicles.
- **"But as for the children of Israel that dwelt in the cities of Judah" → "And the sons of Israel who dwelt in the cities of Judah"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"Hadoram that was over the tribute; and the children of Israel stoned him with stones, that he died. But king Rehoboam made speed to get him up to his chariot" → "Hadoram, who was over the forced labor, and the sons of Israel stoned him with stone, and he died. And King Rehoboam exerted himself to go up into the chariot"** (v18) — Hebrew מַס "forced labor"; אֶבֶן, singular; הִתְאַמֵּץ, literally "made himself strong". The name is הֲדֹרָם in Chronicles (Kings: אֲדֹרָם, Adoram); Chronicles has "the sons of Israel" (Kings: "all Israel").
- **"And Israel rebelled against the house of David unto this day" → "And Israel has revolted against the house of David to this day"** (v19) — Hebrew פָּשַׁע בְּ, the verb of פֶּשַׁע "transgression": to break faith with one to whom allegiance is owed, to break away. "Revolt" says that; "rebel" is kept for מָרַד / מָרָה.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 12:1–19** — almost word for word; the differences are named above.
- **Shechem** (v1) — as Josh 24:1.
- **Jeroboam** (v2, 15) — his flight to Egypt and the word of Ahijah stand in 1 Kgs 11:26–40, which Chronicles does not have.
- **"What share have we in David?"** (v16) — the cry takes up that of 2 Sam 20:1.
- **Hadoram** (v18) — the Adoram of 1 Kgs 12:18.
