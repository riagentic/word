## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v7) — *sarim*.
- **"castles" → "fortresses"** (v12) — *biraniyyot*.
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v2, 19).
- **"tribute silver" — retained**.

### Notes
- **Jehoshaphat** — Chronicler's favorite after David-Solomon-Hezekiah. Long, rich narrative (ch 17-20) expands minimal 1 Kgs 22 detail.
- **Teaching tour** (v7-9) — systematic Torah-instruction program. Officials + Levites + priests travel together teaching in cities. Unique to Chronicler.
- **"Book of Torah"** — portable scroll. Implies standardized text by Jehoshaphat's time.
- **Peace via dread** (v10) — Yahweh's reputation deters aggression; diplomatic power of religious integrity.
- **Tribute from gentiles** — Philistines, Arabians bring wealth. International standing.
- **Army numbers** — 300k + 280k + 200k (Judah) + 200k + 180k (Benjamin) = 1.16 million. Hyperbolic; Chronicler's numeric magnification.
- **"Willingly offered himself"** (v16) — Amasiah dedicated for military service; echoes priestly consecration terminology.
