# 2 Chronicles 17

[1] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ his son reigned in his place, and he strengthened himself against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] And he placed a force in all the fortified cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he placed garrisons in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the cities of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ that Asa his father had captured.

[3] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was with ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, for he walked in the first ways of David his father, and he did not seek the Baals.

[4] But he sought ⟦elohim|the Elohim|the God⟧ of his father, and in his commandments he walked, and not according to the doing of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ established the kingdom in his hand, and all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ gave an offering to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧, and he had riches and glory in abundance.

[6] And his heart was high in the ways of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and moreover he removed the high places and the Asherim from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[7] And in the third year of his reign he sent his princes, ⟦ben-hail|Ben-chayil|Ben-hail⟧ and ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧ and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ and ⟦nethanel|Netanel|Nethanel⟧ and ⟦micaiah|Michayehu|Micaiah⟧, to teach in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[8] And with them the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧: ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ and ⟦nethaniah|Netanyahu|Nethaniah⟧ and ⟦zebadiah|Zevadyah|Zebadiah⟧ and Asahel and ⟦shemiramoth|Shemiramot|Shemiramoth⟧ and ⟦jehonathan|Yehonatan|Jehonathan⟧ and ⟦adonijah|Adoniyahu|Adonijah⟧ and ⟦tobijah|Toviyah|Tobijah⟧ and ⟦tob-adonijah|Tov-adoniyah|Tob-adonijah⟧, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧; and with them Elishama and ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧, the priests.

[9] And they taught in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and with them was the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they went around in all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and taught among the people.

[10] And the dread of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were around ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they did not fight with ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧.

[11] And some of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ were bringing to ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ an offering and silver, a burden; the ⟦arabians|Arvim|Arabians⟧ also were bringing to him flocks: seven thousand seven hundred rams and seven thousand seven hundred he-goats.

[12] And ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ went on growing great, exceedingly, and he built in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ citadels and store cities.

[13] And he had much work in the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and men of war, mighty ones of ability, in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[14] And this was their numbering by their fathers' houses. Of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, commanders of thousands: ⟦adnah|Adnach|Adnah⟧ the commander, and with him three hundred thousand mighty ones of ability.

[15] And next to him ⟦jehohanan|Yehochanan|Jehohanan⟧ the commander, and with him two hundred and eighty thousand.

[16] And next to him ⟦amasiah|Amasyah|Amasiah⟧ the son of Zichri, who offered himself willingly to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and with him two hundred thousand mighty ones of ability.

[17] And of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧: a mighty one of ability, ⟦eliada|Elyada|Eliada⟧, and with him two hundred thousand armed with bow and shield.

[18] And next to him ⟦jehozabad|Yehozavad|Jehozabad⟧, and with him a hundred and eighty thousand armed for the host.

[19] These were the ones ministering to the king, besides those whom the king had placed in the fortified cities in all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי אָבִיו "the Elohim of his father".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. The earlier text had "Therefore" (v5), "moreover … also" and "so that" (v10): none of these is written.
- **"strengthened himself against Israel" kept** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּתְחַזֵּק עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל: הִתְחַזֵּק "strengthen oneself", a word of Chronicles (1:1; 12:13; 13:21; 21:4). עַל is "against" or "over"; "Israel" may be the northern kingdom (as v4) or the people he ruled. "Against" fits v2.
- **"placed forces … set garrisons" → "placed a force … placed garrisons"** (v2) — the same verb twice, נָתַן (lit. "give"), as in v19. חַיִל is the word "ability", of an army "force"; נְצִיבִים "garrisons" may also be "prefects" (1 Kgs 4:19). **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v2, 19) — בְּצֻרוֹת in v2, עָרֵי הַמִּבְצָר "the cities of fortification" in v19. **"had taken" → "had captured"** — Hebrew לָכַד.
- **"the first ways of his father David" → "the first ways of David his father"** (v3) — Hebrew הָרִאשֹׁנִים "the first, the former" (ways). Some manuscripts and the Septuagint lack "David" (from memory), which makes the father Asa and his earlier years; the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"sought not unto Baalim" → "did not seek the Baals"; "sought to the LORD God of his father" → "sought the Elohim of his father"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ לְ "seek to", twice; "seek" is the word of the whole book (1 Chr 10:14; 28:9; 2 Chr 14:4; 15:2). "Yahweh" (KJV "the LORD God") is not written in v4. **"But"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי after a negative.
- **"after the doings of Israel" → "according to the doing of Israel"** (v4) — Hebrew כְּמַעֲשֵׂה, singular: מַעֲשֶׂה "work, deed".
- **"stablished" → "established"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיָּכֶן, הֵכִין. **"brought … presents" → "gave an offering"** — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנוּ … מִנְחָה: the verb is "give"; מִנְחָה (singular) is the "offering" brought to a lord or a king ("tribute", "gifts"). The same word in v11. **"honour" → "glory"** — Hebrew כָּבוֹד.
- **"his heart was lifted up" → "his heart was high"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּגְבַּהּ לִבּוֹ, the verb "be high". Elsewhere the phrase is said of pride (26:16; 32:25); here "in the ways of Yahweh". **"moreover" kept** — Hebrew וְעוֹד "and yet, and again". **"groves" → "the Asherim"** — Hebrew הָאֲשֵׁרִים, the word kept: wooden objects of worship bearing the name of the goddess Asherah. 20:33 says that the high places did not depart.
- **"he sent to his princes, even to Ben-hail …" → "he sent his princes, Ben-hail …"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׁלַח לְשָׂרָיו לְבֶן־חַיִל …: each name has לְ. In the late Hebrew of Chronicles לְ often marks the object (as in v3, 4); it may also be read "he sent to his princes … to teach". שַׂר is "prince" (of an army "commander", v14, 15: one word). **"of his reign"** — Hebrew לְמָלְכוֹ "of his reigning".
- **"And with them he sent Levites" → "And with them the Levites"** (v8) — no verb in the Hebrew; "the Levites" stands twice, before and after the names. Shemiramoth is the reading tradition (qere); the written form (ketiv) is another spelling of the name.
- **"had the book of the law of the LORD with them" → "with them was the book of the Torah of Yahweh"** (v9) — Hebrew וְעִמָּהֶם סֵפֶר תּוֹרַת יְהוָה. **"went about throughout" → "went around in"** — Hebrew וַיָּסֹבּוּ בְּ.
- **"the fear of the LORD fell upon" → "the dread of Yahweh was upon"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיְהִי פַּחַד יְהוָה עַל: פַּחַד "dread" (another word than יִרְאָה "fear"); the verb is "was". **"made no war against" → "did not fight with"** — Hebrew נִלְחֲמוּ עִם.
- **"brought Jehoshaphat presents, and tribute silver" → "were bringing to Jehoshaphat an offering and silver, a burden"** (v11) — Hebrew מְבִיאִים, a participle (twice); מִנְחָה וְכֶסֶף מַשָּׂא: מַשָּׂא is "a burden, a load". It may mean silver by the load or silver laid on them as a due ("tribute"); the Hebrew does not decide. **"flocks"** — Hebrew צֹאן, the one collective word for sheep and goats. **"he goats" kept** — Hebrew תְּיָשִׁים. The Hebrew repeats the number in full: "rams seven thousand and seven hundred, and he-goats seven thousand and seven hundred".
- **"waxed great exceedingly" → "went on growing great, exceedingly"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיְהִי … הֹלֵךְ וְגָדֵל עַד־לְמָעְלָה, literally "was going and growing great, up to above". **"castles" → "citadels"** — Hebrew בִּירָנִיּוֹת (only here and 27:4), from בִּירָה "citadel". **"cities of store" → "store cities"** — Hebrew עָרֵי מִסְכְּנוֹת (Exod 1:11; 1 Kgs 9:19).
- **"much business" → "much work"** (v13) — Hebrew מְלָאכָה רַבָּה: "work"; also what work has made, "goods, supplies". **"the men of war, mighty men of valour" → "men of war, mighty ones of ability"** (v13, 14, 16, 17) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי מִלְחָמָה גִּבּוֹרֵי חַיִל; in v16, 17 the singular גִּבּוֹר חַיִל, in v16 of the whole body.
- **"these are the numbers of them according to the house of their fathers" → "this was their numbering by their fathers' houses"** (v14) — Hebrew וְאֵלֶּה פְקֻדָּתָם, literally "and these, their numbering": the noun of פָּקַד "number, muster". **"captains of thousands; Adnah the chief" → "commanders of thousands: Adnah the commander"** — the one word שַׂר (v14, 15).
- **"next to him"** (v15, 16, 18) — Hebrew וְעַל־יָדוֹ, literally "and at his hand".
- **"who willingly offered himself unto the LORD" → "who offered himself willingly to Yahweh"** (v16) — Hebrew הַמִּתְנַדֵּב לַיהוָה, as Judg 5:2, 9.
- **"armed men with bow and shield" → "armed with bow and shield"** (v17) — Hebrew נֹשְׁקֵי־קֶשֶׁת וּמָגֵן; "men" is not written.
- **"ready prepared for the war" → "armed for the host"** (v18) — Hebrew חֲלוּצֵי צָבָא (Num 31:5; Josh 4:13): the root sense is uncertain ("stripped" for action, or "girded"). The word differs from "armed" in v17.
- **"These waited on the king" → "These were the ones ministering to the king"** (v19) — Hebrew הַמְשָׁרְתִים אֶת־הַמֶּלֶךְ, שֵׁרֵת "minister". **"put" → "had placed"** — נָתַן, as v2.
- **Numbers** (v11, 14–18) — written out in the ordinary English order.

### Notes
- **Jehoshaphat** — Chronicler's favorite after David-Solomon-Hezekiah. Long, rich narrative (ch 17-20) expands minimal 1 Kgs 22 detail.
- **Teaching tour** (v7-9) — systematic Torah-instruction program. Princes + Levites + priests travel together teaching in cities. Unique to Chronicler.
- **"Book of Torah"** — portable scroll. Implies standardized text by Jehoshaphat's time.
- **Peace via dread** (v10) — Yahweh's reputation deters aggression; diplomatic power of religious integrity.
- **Offerings from gentiles** — Philistines, Arabians bring wealth. International standing.
- **Army numbers** — 300k + 280k + 200k (Judah) + 200k + 180k (Benjamin) = 1.16 million. Hyperbolic; Chronicler's numeric magnification.
- **"Offered himself willingly"** (v16) — Amasiah dedicated for military service; echoes priestly consecration terminology.
