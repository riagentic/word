# 2 Chronicles 2

[1] Solomon determined to build a house for the name of Yahweh, and a royal palace for himself.

[2] Solomon counted out seventy thousand men to bear burdens, eighty thousand to hew in the mountain, and three thousand six hundred to oversee them.

[3] Solomon sent to Huram king of Tyre, saying, "As you dealt with David my father, sending him cedars to build him a house to dwell in, so deal with me.

[4] Behold, I am building a house for the name of Yahweh my *Elohim*, to dedicate it to him, to burn before him incense of sweet spices, for the continual bread of the Presence, for the burnt offerings morning and evening on the Sabbaths, on the new moons, and on the appointed feasts of Yahweh our *Elohim*. This is an everlasting ordinance for Israel.

[5] The house that I am building is great, for our *Elohim* is great above all gods.

[6] But who is able to build him a house, since the heavens and the heaven of heavens cannot contain him? Who am I then that I should build him a house, except to burn sacrifice before him?

[7] Therefore send me a man skilled in working in gold, silver, bronze, and iron, and in purple, crimson, and blue fabrics, and trained to engrave, to work with the skilled men who are with me in Judah and Jerusalem, whom David my father provided.

[8] Send me also cedar, cypress, and algum trees from Lebanon, for I know that your servants know how to cut timber in Lebanon. Behold, my servants will be with your servants,

[9] to prepare me timber in abundance; for the house that I am about to build shall be great and wonderful.

[10] Behold, I will give to your servants, the woodcutters who cut the timber, twenty thousand kor of beaten wheat, twenty thousand kor of barley, twenty thousand bath of wine, and twenty thousand bath of oil."

[11] Huram king of Tyre answered in a letter that he sent to Solomon: "Because Yahweh loves his people, he has made you king over them."

[12] Huram further said, "Blessed be Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel, who made heaven and earth, who has given to King David a wise son endowed with discretion and understanding, who will build a house for Yahweh and a royal palace for himself.

[13] Now I send you a skilled man, endowed with understanding, Huram-abi,

[14] son of a woman of the daughters of Dan; his father was a man of Tyre. He is trained to work in gold, silver, bronze, iron, stone, and wood, and in purple, blue, and fine linen and crimson; also to engrave every manner of engraving and to produce any design that may be given him — along with your skilled men and the skilled men of my lord David your father.

[15] Now let my lord send to his servants the wheat, barley, oil, and wine that he has mentioned.

[16] We will cut timber from Lebanon, as much as you shall need; we will bring it to you in rafts by sea to Joppa, and you shall carry it up to Jerusalem."

[17] Solomon counted all the resident foreigners who were in the land of Israel, after the counting with which David his father had counted them; they were found to be one hundred fifty-three thousand six hundred.

[18] He set seventy thousand of them as bearers of burdens, eighty thousand as hewers in the mountain, and three thousand six hundred as overseers to set the people at work.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 5, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*; v5 retains plural *elohim* (gods) for contrast with the true *Elohim*.
- **"house for his kingdom" → "royal palace for himself"** (v1, 12) — *bet malkuto*.
- **"sweet incense" → "incense of sweet spices"** (v4) — *qetoret sammim*.
- **"shewbread" → "bread of the Presence"** (v4).
- **"solemn feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (v4) — *mo'adim*.
- **"Huram my father's" → "Huram-abi"** (v13) — proper name, not Huram's father. Same man as 1 Kgs 7:13's Hiram the craftsman; "*abi*" here is part of his name ("my father" = honorific title like "Rabbi").
- **"cunning" → "skilled"** (throughout).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 5** — Chronicler adapts. 1 Kgs 5 calls the Tyrian king Hiram; Chronicler spells Huram. Artisan named "Huram" (same as king) in Chronicles vs "Hiram" in Kings; both derive from the common name.
- **"Huram-abi"** (v13) — Chronicler names the artisan with an honorific attached. His mother's tribe differs: here "Dan"; in 1 Kgs 7:14 "Naphtali." Not necessarily contradictory — perhaps widow of a Danite remarried Naphtali, or scribal variant.
- **Solomon's theology** (v5-6) — acknowledges Yahweh's transcendence. Temple is symbolic sacrifice-location, not residence.
- **Foreign labor** (v17-18) — Chronicler is explicit: Israelites are not forced laborers; only the *gerim* are. Protects Solomon's reputation vs 1 Kgs 5:13 which suggests Israelite corvée.
- **Huram's blessing** (v11-12) — Gentile king praising Yahweh as creator. Theological expansion; beyond 1 Kgs 5.
- **"Algum trees"** (v8) — species debated (sandalwood? juniper?); 1 Kgs 10:11 "almug."
