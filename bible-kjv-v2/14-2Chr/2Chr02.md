# 2 Chronicles 2

[1] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ meant to build a house for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and a house for his kingdom.

[2] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ numbered seventy thousand men, burden-bearers, and eighty thousand men, hewers in the mountain, and overseers over them, three thousand six hundred.

[3] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ sent to ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ king of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, saying, "As you did with David my father, and sent him cedars to build for himself a house to dwell in it —

[4] Behold, I am building a house for the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to sanctify it to him, to burn before him fragrant incense, and a continual arrangement, and burnt offerings for the morning and for the evening, for the ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ and for the months and for the appointed times of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Forever this is upon ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[5] And the house that I am building is great, for greater is our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ than all the gods.

[6] And who retains power to build him a house, for the heavens and the heavens of the heavens cannot contain him? And who am I, that I should build him a house, except to make smoke before him?

[7] And now, send me a wise man to work in gold and in silver and in bronze and in iron, and in purple and crimson and blue, and who knows how to engrave engravings, with the wise men who are with me in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, whom David my father prepared.

[8] And send me cedar, cypress and algum wood from ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, for I, I know that your servants know how to cut the trees of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧; and behold, my servants shall be with your servants,

[9] and to prepare for me wood in abundance, for the house that I am building is great and wonderful.

[10] And behold, for the woodcutters, for those who cut the trees, I have given beaten wheat for your servants, twenty thousand kors, and barley, twenty thousand kors, and wine, twenty thousand baths, and oil, twenty thousand baths."

[11] And ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ king of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧ said in writing, and sent to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧: "Because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loves his people, he has placed you over them as king."

[12] And ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ said, "Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who made the heavens and the earth, who has given to David the king a wise son, knowing insight and understanding, who shall build a house for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and a house for his kingdom.

[13] And now, I have sent a wise man, knowing understanding, ⟦huram-abi|Churam-avi|Huram-abi⟧,

[14] the son of a woman from the daughters of Dan, and his father was a man of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, who knows how to work in gold and in silver, in bronze, in iron, in stones and in wood, in purple, in blue and in fine linen and in crimson, and to engrave any engraving and to devise any design that may be given to him, with your wise men and the wise men of my lord David your father.

[15] And now, the wheat and the barley, the oil and the wine, of which my lord has said, let him send to his servants.

[16] And we, we will cut trees from ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧, according to all your need, and we will bring them to you as floats upon the sea of ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧; and you, you shall bring them up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

[17] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ numbered all the men, the sojourners, who were in the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, after the numbering with which David his father had numbered them; and there were found a hundred and fifty-three thousand six hundred.

[18] And he made of them seventy thousand burden-bearers and eighty thousand hewers in the mountain, and three thousand six hundred overseers to make the people serve.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 5, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v5b "all the gods" (הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article) is the same word, of the gods of the nations.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"determined to build" → "meant to build"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … לִבְנוֹת, literally "said to build", as 1 Kgs 5:5. **"an house for his kingdom"** (v1, 12) — Hebrew בַּיִת לְמַלְכוּתוֹ, word for word; a royal house (palace) is meant.
- **"told out threescore and ten thousand men to bear burdens" → "numbered seventy thousand men, burden-bearers"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיִּסְפֹּר "numbered"; אִישׁ סַבָּל is "man, burden-bearer". 1 Kgs 5:15 has the same numbers with another wording ("bearers of burdens") and no "men". **"to hew in the mountain" → "hewers in the mountain"** — Hebrew חֹצֵב בָּהָר, as 1 Kgs 5:15. **"three thousand and six hundred to oversee them" → "overseers over them, three thousand six hundred"** — Hebrew מְנַצְּחִים עֲלֵיהֶם; 1 Kgs 5:16 has "three thousand three hundred" and other words.
- **"Huram"** (v3, 11, 12) — Hebrew חוּרָם, the form of the name in Chronicles; Kings has חִירָם "Hiram". The token is the one for Huram, as 1 Chr 14:1.
- **"As thou didst deal with David my father, and didst send him cedars to build him an house to dwell therein, even so deal with me" → "As you did with David my father, and sent him cedars to build for himself a house to dwell in it —"** (v3) — the Hebrew sentence is not finished: no "so deal with me" follows. It is left unfinished.
- **"to dedicate it to him" → "to sanctify it to him"** (v4) — Hebrew לְהַקְדִּישׁ לוֹ; "it" is supplied. **"to burn before him sweet incense" → "to burn before him fragrant incense"** — Hebrew לְהַקְטִיר … קְטֹרֶת־סַמִּים, literally "to make smoke … incense of fragrant substances". **"for the continual shewbread" → "and a continual arrangement"** — Hebrew וּמַעֲרֶכֶת תָּמִיד: the arrangement of the bread set before Yahweh (Lev 24:6–8; 1 Chr 9:32); the word "bread" is not here, and no "for". The three nouns (incense, arrangement, burnt offerings) follow the one verb. **"on the new moons" → "for the months"** — Hebrew חֳדָשִׁים "months": the first day of the month is meant, as 1 Chr 23:31. **"solemn feasts" → "appointed times"** — Hebrew מוֹעֲדֵי. **"This is an ordinance for ever to Israel" → "Forever this is upon Israel"** — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם זֹאת עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל; "ordinance" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"great is our God above all gods" → "greater is our Elohim than all the gods"** (v5) — Hebrew גָדוֹל … מִכָּל־הָאֱלֹהִים: the same word "great" as of the house, with מִן "more than".
- **"But who is able to build him an house" → "And who retains power to build him a house"** (v6) — Hebrew יַעֲצָר־כֹּחַ, as 1 Chr 29:14. **"the heaven and heaven of heavens cannot contain him"** — Hebrew לֹא יְכַלְכְּלֻהוּ, as 1 Kgs 8:27. **"save only to burn sacrifice before him" → "except to make smoke before him"** — Hebrew לְהַקְטִיר without an object, "to make smoke"; the same verb as "burn" with incense in v4. "Sacrifice" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"a man cunning to work" → "a wise man to work"** (v7; also v13, 14) — Hebrew חָכָם "wise", here of skill in a craft; לַעֲשׂוֹת is "to do, make". **"crimson"** (v7, 14) — Hebrew כַּרְמִיל, a late word found only in 2 Chr 2:7, 14; 3:14; the older books say תּוֹלַעַת שָׁנִי "scarlet". "Purple" is written אַרְגְּוָן in v7 and אַרְגָּמָן in v14: one word in two forms. The three are dyes and stand for stuff dyed with them. **"that can skill to grave" → "who knows how to engrave engravings"** — Hebrew לְפַתֵּחַ פִּתּוּחִים. **"whom David my father did provide" → "whom David my father prepared"** — Hebrew הֵכִין.
- **"cedar trees, fir trees, and algum trees" → "cedar, cypress and algum wood"** (v8) — Hebrew עֲצֵי אֲרָזִים בְּרוֹשִׁים וְאַלְגּוּמִּים, literally "woods of cedars, cypresses and algums". The algum is not known (1 Kgs 10:11 writes אַלְמֻגִּים "almug"). **"I know" → "I, I know"** — the Hebrew has the pronoun. **"can skill to cut timber in Lebanon" → "know how to cut the trees of Lebanon"** — Hebrew לִכְרוֹת עֲצֵי לְבָנוֹן. **"my servants shall be with thy servants"** — the Hebrew has no verb here; 1 Kgs 5:6 has "shall be".
- **"Even to prepare me timber in abundance" → "and to prepare for me wood in abundance"** (v9) — Hebrew וּלְהָכִין. **"shall be wonderful great" → "is great and wonderful"** — Hebrew גָּדוֹל וְהַפְלֵא, literally "great, and making wonderful" (an infinitive used as an adverb).
- **"the hewers that cut timber" → "the woodcutters, … those who cut the trees"** (v10) — Hebrew לַחֹטְבִים לְכֹרְתֵי הָעֵצִים: חֹטֵב is the "hewer of wood" of Josh 9:21, another word than the "hewers" (חֹצֵב) of v2, 18. **"I will give" → "I have given"** — Hebrew נָתַתִּי, a perfect: the gift is settled as he speaks. **"beaten wheat"** — Hebrew חִטִּים מַכּוֹת, literally "wheat, blows"; the meaning is not certain. 1 Kgs 5:11 has חִטִּים מַכֹּלֶת "wheat as food" (one letter otherwise), and the verse is commonly corrected by it; the Masoretic Text is kept. **"measures" → "kors"**, **"baths"** — Hebrew כֹּר, a dry measure, and בַּת, a liquid measure. The amounts differ from 1 Kgs 5:11.
- **"answered in writing, which he sent to Solomon" → "said in writing, and sent to Solomon"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … בִּכְתָב וַיִּשְׁלַח. **"Because the LORD hath loved his people, he hath made thee king over them" → "Because Yahweh loves his people, he has placed you over them as king"** — Hebrew בְּאַהֲבַת יְהוָה אֶת־עַמּוֹ נְתָנְךָ עֲלֵיהֶם מֶלֶךְ, literally "in Yahweh's loving his people he has given you over them as king", as 1 Kgs 10:9.
- **"Huram said moreover, Blessed be the LORD God of Israel, that made heaven and earth" → "And Huram said, Blessed is Yahweh the Elohim of Israel, who made the heavens and the earth"** (v12) — the Hebrew has no verb with בָּרוּךְ and no "moreover"; שָׁמַיִם is plural. **"endued with prudence and understanding" → "knowing insight and understanding"** — Hebrew יוֹדֵעַ שֵׂכֶל וּבִינָה. 1 Kgs 5:7 has "Blessed is Yahweh today, who has given to David a wise son over this numerous people".
- **"I have sent a cunning man, endued with understanding, of Huram my father's" → "I have sent a wise man, knowing understanding, Huram-abi"** (v13) — Hebrew לְחוּרָם אָבִי, literally "Huram my father": "my father" may be a title of honor for a master craftsman (cf. Gen 45:8) or part of the name; the existing name form is kept. The לְ marks the man who is sent, not his owner.
- **"The son of a woman of the daughters of Dan"** (v14) — 1 Kgs 7:14 has "the son of a widow woman, from the tribe of Naphtali"; "and his father was a man of Tyre" is the same in both. **"skilful to work" → "who knows how to work"** — Hebrew יוֹדֵעַ לַעֲשׂוֹת. **"in stone, and in timber" → "in stones and in wood"** — both plural in the Hebrew. **"fine linen"** — Hebrew בּוּץ, the later word (the older is שֵׁשׁ). **"to grave any manner of graving, and to find out every device which shall be put to him" → "to engrave any engraving and to devise any design that may be given to him"** — Hebrew וּלְפַתֵּחַ כָּל־פִּתּוּחַ וְלַחְשֹׁב כָּל־מַחֲשָׁבֶת אֲשֶׁר יִנָּתֶן־לוֹ, as Exod 31:4. **"cunning men" → "wise men"** — Hebrew חֲכָמִים.
- **"which my lord hath spoken of" → "of which my lord has said"** (v15) — Hebrew אָמַר "said".
- **"we will cut wood out of Lebanon, as much as thou shalt need" → "we, we will cut trees from Lebanon, according to all your need"** (v16) — Hebrew כְּכָל־צָרְכֶּךָ; צֹרֶךְ "need" is found only here. **"in flotes by sea to Joppa" → "as floats upon the sea of Joppa"** — Hebrew רַפְסֹדוֹת עַל־יָם יָפוֹ. רַפְסֹדוֹת is found only here (1 Kgs 5:9 has דֹּבְרוֹת "rafts"); "as" is supplied. יָם יָפוֹ can be "the sea of Joppa" (as Ezra 3:7) or "the sea, to Joppa". **"carry it up" → "bring them up"** — Hebrew תַּעֲלֶה אֹתָם.
- **"the strangers" → "the men, the sojourners"** (v17) — Hebrew הָאֲנָשִׁים הַגֵּירִים. **"after the numbering wherewith David his father had numbered them"** — Hebrew סְפָר, found only here in this sense (1 Chr 22:2). **"an hundred and fifty thousand and three thousand and six hundred" → "a hundred and fifty-three thousand six hundred"** — the Hebrew counts "a hundred and fifty thousand and three thousand and six hundred".
- **"he set threescore and ten thousand of them to be bearers of burdens" → "he made of them seventy thousand burden-bearers"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ "made". **"overseers to set the people a work" → "overseers to make the people serve"** — Hebrew לְהַעֲבִיד, the causative of "serve".

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew, v1 is 1:18 and v2–18 are 2:1–17.
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 5** — Chronicles tells the exchange with the king of Tyre in its own words; only short stretches are the same Hebrew (v2 "hewers in the mountain"; v12 "who has given to David … a wise son"; v14 "and his father was a man of Tyre"). Kings calls the king Hiram; Chronicles writes Huram, for the king and for the craftsman.
- **"Huram-abi"** (v13) — the craftsman of 1 Kgs 7:13–14. His mother is "from the daughters of Dan" here, "from the tribe of Naphtali" in 1 Kgs 7:14; the two statements are not explained in the text.
- **Solomon's words** (v5–6) — the house is not a dwelling that could contain Yahweh; it is a place "to make smoke before him".
- **The sojourners** (v17–18) — Chronicles says that the labor force was made of the sojourners in the land; 1 Kgs 5:13 tells of forced labor "from all Israel".
- **Huram's blessing** (v11–12) — a foreign king blesses Yahweh as the maker of the heavens and the earth; 1 Kgs 5:7 has a shorter blessing.
- **"Algum"** (v8) — the tree is not known; 1 Kgs 10:11 has "almug" and brings it from Ophir, not from Lebanon.
