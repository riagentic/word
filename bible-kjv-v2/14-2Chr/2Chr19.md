# 2 Chronicles 19

[1] Jehoshaphat the king of Judah returned in peace to his house in Jerusalem.

[2] Jehu son of Hanani the seer went out to meet him, and said to King Jehoshaphat, "Should you help the wicked and love those who hate Yahweh? Because of this, wrath has gone out against you from Yahweh.

[3] Nevertheless, some good things are found in you, in that you have swept away the Asherah poles from the land and have set your heart to seek *Elohim*."

[4] Jehoshaphat dwelt at Jerusalem; he went out again among the people from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim, and brought them back to Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers.

[5] He set judges in the land throughout all the fortified cities of Judah, city by city,

[6] and said to the judges, "Consider what you do, for you do not judge for man, but for Yahweh; he is with you in rendering judgment.

[7] Now let the dread of Yahweh be upon you. Take care and act, for with Yahweh our *Elohim* there is no injustice, no partiality, no taking of bribes."

[8] Moreover, in Jerusalem Jehoshaphat appointed some of the Levites, priests, and heads of fathers' houses of Israel to render judgment for Yahweh and to decide disputes; they resided at Jerusalem.

[9] He commanded them, saying, "Thus shall you act in the dread of Yahweh, faithfully, and with a whole heart.

[10] Whatever case comes to you from your brothers who live in their cities — between bloodshed and bloodshed, between Law and commandment, statute and ordinance — you shall warn them not to incur guilt before Yahweh, lest wrath come upon you and upon your brothers. Do this, and you shall not incur guilt.

[11] Behold, Amariah the chief priest is over you in all matters of Yahweh, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, the ruler of the house of Judah, in all the king's matters. The Levites shall be officers before you. Be strong and act, and may Yahweh be with the good."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"ungodly" → "wicked"** (v2) — *rasha*.
- **"respect of persons" → "partiality"** (v7) — *massa panim*.
- **"taking of gifts" → "taking of bribes"** (v7) — *miqqach shochad*.
- **"perfect heart" → "whole heart"** (v9).
- **"trespass" → "incur guilt"** (v10) — *ashem*.
- **"controversies" → "disputes"** (v8) — *rib*.

### Notes
- **Unique to Chronicler** — no parallel in Kings. Jehoshaphat's judicial reforms, oracle of Jehu, national judicial system.
- **Jehu son of Hanani** — son of the seer who rebuked Asa (ch 16). Prophetic family, consistent ministry.
- **Judicial reform** — fortified cities get local judges; Jerusalem gets appellate Levite-priest-elder panel. Two-tier system.
- **Bipartite oversight** (v11) — Amariah (religious cases) and Zebadiah (civil/royal cases). Separation of domains.
- **"No partiality, no bribery"** — classic Deuteronomic judicial virtues (Deut 10:17, 16:19).
- **Chronicler's educational project** — Jehoshaphat = teaching king, judicial king. Models post-exilic theocratic governance structure.
