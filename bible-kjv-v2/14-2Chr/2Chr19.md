## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"ungodly" → "wicked"** (v2) — *rasha*.
- **"respect of persons" → "partiality"** (v7) — *massa panim*.
- **"taking of gifts" → "taking of bribes"** (v7) — *miqqach shochad*.
- **"perfect heart" → "whole heart"** (v9).
- **"trespass" → "incur guilt"** (v10) — *ashem*.
- **"controversies" → "disputes"** (v8) — *rib*.

### Notes
- **Unique to Chronicler** — no parallel in Kings. Jehoshaphat's judicial reforms, oracle of Jehu, national judicial system.
- **Jehu son of Hanani** — son of the seer who rebuked Asa (ch 16). Prophetic family, consistent ministry.
- **Judicial reform** — fortified cities get local judges; Jerusalem gets appellate Levite-priest-elder panel. Two-tier system.
- **Bipartite oversight** (v11) — Amariah (religious cases) and Zebadiah (civil/royal cases). Separation of domains.
- **"No partiality, no bribery"** — classic Deuteronomic judicial virtues (Deut 10:17, 16:19).
- **Chronicler's educational project** — Jehoshaphat = teaching king, judicial king. Models post-exilic theocratic governance structure.
