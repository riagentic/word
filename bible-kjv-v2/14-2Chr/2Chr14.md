# 2 Chronicles 14

[1] Abijah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; Asa his son reigned in his place. In his days the land was quiet for ten years.

[2] Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of Yahweh his *Elohim*.

[3] He removed the altars of foreign gods and the high places, broke down the sacred pillars, and cut down the Asherah poles.

[4] He commanded Judah to seek Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers and to do the Law and the commandment.

[5] He also removed from all the cities of Judah the high places and the incense altars; the kingdom had quiet before him.

[6] He built fortified cities in Judah, for the land was at rest, and he had no war in those years, because Yahweh had given him rest.

[7] He said to Judah, "Let us build these cities and surround them with walls and towers, gates and bars, while the land is still before us; because we have sought Yahweh our *Elohim* — we have sought him and he has given us rest on every side." So they built and prospered.

[8] Asa had an army: from Judah, bearing large shields and spears, three hundred thousand; from Benjamin, bearing small shields and drawing bows, two hundred eighty thousand — all these were valiant warriors.

[9] Zerah the Cushite came out against them with an army of a million men and three hundred chariots; he came to Mareshah.

[10] Asa went out to meet him, and they drew up in battle array in the Valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.

[11] Asa cried out to Yahweh his *Elohim* and said, "Yahweh, it is nothing for you to help, whether the many or those who have no strength. Help us, O Yahweh our *Elohim*, for we lean on you, and in your name we have come against this multitude. O Yahweh, you are our *Elohim*; let not man — mortal man — prevail against you."

[12] Yahweh struck the Cushites before Asa and before Judah; the Cushites fled.

[13] Asa and the people who were with him pursued them as far as Gerar; so many Cushites fell that they could not recover, for they were crushed before Yahweh and his host. They carried away very much spoil.

[14] They struck all the cities around Gerar, for the dread of Yahweh had come upon them; they plundered all the cities, for there was much spoil in them.

[15] They also struck the tents of those who had livestock, and carried away sheep and camels in abundance. Then they returned to Jerusalem.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"strange gods" → "foreign gods"** (v3) — *elohei ha-nekar*.
- **"images" → "sacred pillars"** (v3, 5) — *matzevot*.
- **"sun images" or similar reading retained as "incense altars"** (v5) — *chammanim*; incense-stands, solar symbol.
- **"Ethiopian / Ethiopians" → "Cushite / Cushites"** (v9, 12, 13).
- **"targets" → "large shields"** (v8); **"shields" → "small shields"** (v8) — *tzinnah / magen*.

### Notes
- **Chapter numbering** — MT numbers this chapter starting at 14:1 with "Abijah slept"; English follows. Chapter divisions are later additions.
- **Asa's reforms** — begins with comprehensive religious purge. Chronicler commends without qualification.
- **Zerah the Cushite** — likely a Cushite (Nubian) general; possibly under Egyptian command (Osorkon I after Sheshonq?). Historical identification uncertain.
- **"A million men"** — hyperbolic; Hebrew *elef alafim* = "thousand thousands" / huge numbers. Literal or symbolic.
- **Asa's prayer** (v11) — pattern of pious trust: Yahweh aids the weak, humbled king relies on him.
- **Gerar** — western Negev; Abimelech territory (Gen 20). Philistine-area spoiled.
- **Chronicler's theme** — faithful king + prayer → victory even against overwhelming odds.
