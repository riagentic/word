# 2 Chronicles 14

[1] ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David; Asa his son reigned in his place. In his days the land was quiet for ten years.

[2] Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] He removed the altars of foreign gods and the high places, broke down the sacred pillars, and cut down the Asherah poles.

[4] He commanded ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|the Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers and to do the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and the commandment.

[5] He also removed from all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ the high places and the incense altars; the kingdom had quiet before him.

[6] He built fortified cities in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, for the land was at rest, and he had no war in those years, because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had given him rest.

[7] He said to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, "Let us build these cities and surround them with walls and towers, gates and bars, while the land is still before us; because we have sought ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ — we have sought him and he has given us rest on every side." So they built and prospered.

[8] Asa had an army: from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, bearing large shields and spears, three hundred thousand; from ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, bearing small shields and drawing bows, two hundred eighty thousand — all these were valiant warriors.

[9] ⟦zerah|Zerach|Zerah⟧ the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ came out against them with an army of a million men and three hundred chariots; he came to Mareshah.

[10] Asa went out to meet him, and they drew up in battle array in the Valley of ⟦zephathah|Tzefatah|Zephathah⟧ at Mareshah.

[11] Asa cried out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧, it is nothing for you to help, whether the many or those who have no strength. Help us, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for we lean on you, and in your name we have come against this multitude. O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you are our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; let not man — mortal man — prevail against you."

[12] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ struck the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ before Asa and before ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ fled.

[13] Asa and the people who were with him pursued them as far as Gerar; so many ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ fell that they could not recover, for they were crushed before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and his host. They carried away very much spoil.

[14] They struck all the cities around Gerar, for the dread of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had come upon them; they plundered all the cities, for there was much spoil in them.

[15] They also struck the tents of those who had livestock, and carried away sheep and camels in abundance. Then they returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"strange gods" → "foreign gods"** (v3) — *elohei ha-nekar*.
- **"images" → "sacred pillars"** (v3, 5) — *matzevot*.
- **"sun images" or similar reading retained as "incense altars"** (v5) — *chammanim*; incense-stands, solar symbol.
- **"Ethiopian / Ethiopians" → "Cushite / Cushites"** (v9, 12, 13).
- **"targets" → "large shields"** (v8); **"shields" → "small shields"** (v8) — *tzinnah / magen*.

### Notes
- **Chapter numbering** — MT numbers "Abijah slept" as 13:23, so MT 14:1–14 = English 14:2–15; English starts the chapter one verse earlier. Chapter divisions are later additions.
- **Asa's reforms** — begins with comprehensive religious purge. Chronicler commends without qualification.
- **Zerah the Cushite** — likely a Cushite (Nubian) general; possibly under Egyptian command (Osorkon I after Sheshonq?). Historical identification uncertain.
- **"A million men"** — hyperbolic; Hebrew *elef alafim* = "thousand thousands" / huge numbers. Literal or symbolic.
- **Asa's prayer** (v11) — pattern of pious trust: Yahweh aids the weak, humbled king relies on him.
- **Gerar** — western Negev; Abimelech territory (Gen 20). Philistine-area spoiled.
- **Chronicler's theme** — faithful king + prayer → victory even against overwhelming odds.
