# 2 Chronicles 14

[1] And ⟦abijah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ lay with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David, and Asa his son reigned in his place. In his days the land was quiet ten years.

[2] And Asa did what was good and right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] And he removed the foreign altars and the high places, and he broke in pieces the pillars and hewed down the Asherim.

[4] And he said to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers, and to do the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and the commandment.

[5] And he removed from all the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ the high places and the incense altars; and the kingdom was quiet before him.

[6] And he built cities of defense in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, for the land was quiet, and there was no war with him in these years, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had given him rest.

[7] And he said to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, "Let us build these cities and set a wall around, and towers, doors and bars; the land is still before us, for we have sought ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; we have sought, and he has given us rest all around." And they built and prospered.

[8] And Asa had a force bearing large shield and spear: from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ three hundred thousand; and from ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, bearing shield and treading the bow, two hundred and eighty thousand. All these were mighty ones of ability.

[9] And ⟦zerah|Zerach|Zerah⟧ the ⟦cushite|Kushi|Cushite⟧ came out to them with a force of a thousand thousands and three hundred chariots, and he came as far as Mareshah.

[10] And Asa went out before him, and they drew up for battle in the valley of ⟦zephathah|Tzefatah|Zephathah⟧ at Mareshah.

[11] And Asa called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, there is none with you to help between the many and the one with no power. Help us, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for on you we lean, and in your name we have come against this multitude. ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you are our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; let not mortal man retain power with you."

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ smote the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ before Asa and before ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ fled.

[13] And Asa and the people who were with him pursued them as far as Gerar; and there fell of the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ so that there was no preservation of life for them, for they were broken before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and before his camp. And they carried off very much spoil.

[14] And they struck all the cities around Gerar, for the dread of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon them; and they plundered all the cities, for there was much plunder in them.

[15] And also the tents of livestock they struck, and they took captive flocks in abundance, and camels; and they returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"So Abijah slept with his fathers … In his days the land was quiet ten years"** (v1) — וַיִּשְׁכַּב "lay"; the sentence is that of 1 Kgs 15:8 with the name Abijah for Abijam; the last clause, שָׁקְטָה "was quiet", is only in Chronicles.
- **"that which was good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God"** (v2) — הַטּוֹב וְהַיָּשָׁר. 1 Kgs 15:11 has "what was right in the eyes of Yahweh, like David his father"; each as printed.
- **"the altars of the strange gods" → "the foreign altars"** (v3) — מִזְבְּחוֹת הַנֵּכָר, literally "the altars of foreignness"; the Hebrew has no word "gods".
- **"brake down the images, and cut down the groves" → "broke in pieces the pillars and hewed down the Asherim"** (v3) — מַצֵּבוֹת, stones set up; גִּדַּע "hew down"; אֲשֵׁרִים, wooden objects of worship bearing the name of the goddess Asherah.
- **"commanded Judah to seek" → "said to Judah to seek"** (v4) — וַיֹּאמֶר "said". דָּרַשׁ אֶת־יְהוָה "seek Yahweh" (also v7), a leading word of Chronicles.
- **"the law" → "the Torah"** (v4) — הַתּוֹרָה וְהַמִּצְוָה.
- **"the images" → "the incense altars"** (v5) — חַמָּנִים; the meaning is uncertain (also "sun pillars"), one rendering as Lev 26:30.
- **"fenced cities" → "cities of defense"** (v6) — עָרֵי מְצוּרָה; the noun, from a root "enclose, besiege", names a place made fast; another word than מִבְצָר "fortification".
- **"for the land had rest … because the LORD had given him rest" → "for the land was quiet … for Yahweh had given him rest"** (v6) — two verbs: שָׁקַט "be quiet" (v1, 5, 6) and הֵנִיחַ "give rest" (v6, 7). "in those years" → "in these years": הָאֵלֶּה.
- **"Therefore he said … and make about them walls, and towers, gates, and bars, while the land is yet before us" → "And he said … and set a wall around, and towers, doors and bars; the land is still before us"** (v7) — וְנָסֵב חוֹמָה, "wall" singular, no "them"; דְּלָתַיִם, a dual, "double doors"; the Hebrew has no "while".
- **"we have sought him, and he hath given us rest on every side" → "we have sought, and he has given us rest all around"** (v7) — the second דָּרַשְׁנוּ has no object.
- **"an army of men that bare targets and spears … that bare shields and drew bows … mighty men of valour" → "a force bearing large shield and spear … bearing shield and treading the bow … mighty ones of ability"** (v8) — צִנָּה the large shield, מָגֵן the shield; דֹּרְכֵי קֶשֶׁת, literally "treading the bow": the bow is bent with the foot. The numbers are as printed.
- **"Zerah the Ethiopian with an host of a thousand thousand" → "Zerah the Cushite with a force of a thousand thousands"** (v9, 12, 13) — כּוּשִׁי, a man of Cush; אֶלֶף אֲלָפִים, as printed. אֲלֵיהֶם "to them", that is, against them.
- **"went out against him" → "went out before him"** (v10) — לְפָנָיו. גֵּיא "valley" is a narrow valley; לְמָרֵשָׁה "at Mareshah", literally "belonging to Mareshah". (From memory: the Greek reads "north of Mareshah"; the Hebrew is kept.)
- **"cried unto the LORD" → "called to Yahweh"** (v11) — וַיִּקְרָא אֶל.
- **"it is nothing with thee to help, whether with many, or with them that have no power" → "there is none with you to help between the many and the one with no power"** (v11) — אֵין־עִמְּךָ לַעְזוֹר בֵּין רַב לְאֵין כֹּחַ, word for word "there is not with you to help between many (or: great) to no power". The sentence is hard. It is understood as "there is none besides you to help in a fight between the mighty and the powerless", or as "with you it makes no difference to help the many or the powerless". The verse keeps the words and does not decide.
- **"for we rest on thee, and in thy name we go" → "for on you we lean, and in your name we have come"** (v11) — נִשְׁעַנּוּ, as 13:18; בָאנוּ, a perfect.
- **"O LORD, thou art our God; let not man prevail against thee" → "Yahweh, you are our Elohim; let not mortal man retain power with you"** (v11) — no "O"; אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; אַל־יַעְצֹר עִמְּךָ: the verb alone is "restrain, hold back"; it stands for עָצַר כֹּחַ "retain power" (13:20), so "power" is supplied from that expression. "With you" is in a contest with you, that is, against you.
- **"they were overthrown, that they could not recover themselves; for they were destroyed before the LORD, and before his host; and they carried away very much spoil" → "there fell of the Cushites so that there was no preservation of life for them, for they were broken before Yahweh and before his camp"** (v13) — לְאֵין לָהֶם מִחְיָה: either none of them was left alive, or they could not revive; נִשְׁבְּרוּ "were broken"; מַחֲנֵהוּ "his camp".
- **"the fear of the LORD came upon them … exceeding much spoil in them" → "the dread of Yahweh was upon them … much plunder in them"** (v14) — פַּחַד; בִּזָּה "plunder" is of the root of "plundered" and another word than שָׁלָל "spoil" in v13.
- **"the tents of cattle, and carried away sheep and camels in abundance" → "the tents of livestock … took captive flocks in abundance, and camels"** (v15) — אָהֳלֵי מִקְנֶה; וַיִּשְׁבּוּ, the verb of taking captives; צֹאן, sheep and goats, one collective word; "in abundance" stands with the flocks.

### Notes
- **Chapter numbering** — the Hebrew numbers "Abijah lay with his fathers" as 13:23, so Hebrew 14:1–14 = our 14:2–15.
- **Kings** — 1 Kgs 15:9–24 tells of Asa without this war and with other figures and details; each book is translated as printed.
- **Zerah the Cushite** — not known from another source.
- **"A thousand thousands"** — the number as the Hebrew prints it.
- **Gerar** — in the south-west, toward the land of the Philistines (Gen 20:1; 26:1).
