# 2 Chronicles 34

[1] ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ was eight years old when he began to reign, and thirty-one years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] And he did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and walked in the ways of David his father, and he did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

[3] And in the eighth year of his reign, while he was still a boy, he began to seek the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of David his father; and in the twelfth year he began to cleanse ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from the high places and the Asherim and the carved images and the molten images.

[4] And they broke down before him the altars of the Baals; and the incense altars that were above them he hewed down; and the Asherim and the carved images and the molten images he broke in pieces and crushed, and he tossed them upon the face of the graves of those who had sacrificed to them.

[5] And the bones of priests he burned on their altars, and he cleansed ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[6] And in the cities of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧, and as far as ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, with their swords all around —

[7] And he broke down the altars; and the Asherim and the carved images he beat in pieces, crushing them; and all the incense altars he hewed down in all the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and he returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[8] And in the eighteenth year of his reign, to cleanse the land and the house, he sent ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the son of ⟦azaliah|Atzalyahu|Azaliah⟧ and ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ the prince of the city and ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ the son of ⟦joahaz|Yoachaz|Joahaz⟧, the recorder, to repair the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] And they came to ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the great priest and gave the silver that had been brought into the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, the keepers of the threshold, had gathered from the hand of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and from all the remnant of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and from all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧; and they returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] And they gave it into the hand of the doers of the work who were appointed in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and the doers of the work who were working in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave it to mend and to repair the house.

[11] And they gave it to the craftsmen and to the builders, to buy hewn stones, and wood for the joinings and to lay the beams of the houses that the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ had ruined.

[12] And the men were acting in faithfulness in the work; and over them were appointed ⟦jahath|Yachat|Jahath⟧ and ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, from the sons of Merari, and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ and Meshullam, from the sons of the ⟦kohathites|Qehati|Kohathites⟧, to oversee; and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, everyone who had understanding in instruments of song,

[13] and over the bearers of burdens, and overseers of every doer of work for service and service; and from the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ were scribes and officers and gatekeepers.

[14] And when they brought out the silver that had been brought into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the priest found the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[15] And ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ answered and said to ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe, "The book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ I have found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ gave the book to ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧.

[16] And ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ brought the book to the king, and further brought back word to the king, saying, "All that was given into the hand of your servants, they are doing.

[17] And they have poured out the silver that was found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and have given it into the hand of those who are appointed and into the hand of the doers of the work."

[18] And ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe told the king, saying, "A book ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the priest has given me." And ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ read in it before the king.

[19] And it came to pass, when the king heard the words of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, that he tore his garments.

[20] And the king commanded ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ and ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧ the son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ and ⟦abdon|Avdon|Abdon⟧ the son of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧ and ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe and ⟦asaiah|Asayah|Asaiah⟧ the servant of the king, saying,

[21] "Go, seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for me and for what is left in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, concerning the words of the book that has been found; for great is the fury of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that is poured out on us, because our fathers did not keep the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to do according to all that is written on this book."

[22] And ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ went, and those of the king, to ⟦huldah|Chuldah|Huldah⟧ the prophetess, the wife of Shallum the son of ⟦tokhath|Toqhat|Tokhath⟧, the son of ⟦hasrah|Chasrah|Hasrah⟧, the keeper of the garments; and she was dwelling in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, in the Second quarter; and they spoke to her according to this.

[23] And she said to them, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'Say to the man who sent you to me,

[24] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Behold, I am bringing evil upon this place and upon its inhabitants, all the curses that are written on the book that they have read before the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,

[25] because they have forsaken me and have made smoke to other gods, in order to vex me with all the works of their hands; and my fury shall be poured out on this place and shall not be quenched.

[26] And to the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, who is sending you to seek by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, thus you shall say to him: Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: The words that you have heard —

[27] because your heart was soft and you brought yourself low before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ when you heard his words against this place and against its inhabitants, and you brought yourself low before me and tore your garments and wept before me, I also have heard, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[28] Behold, I am gathering you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered to your graves in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and your eyes shall not look on all the evil that I am bringing upon this place and upon its inhabitants.'" And they brought back word to the king.

[29] And the king sent and gathered all the elders of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[30] And the king went up to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and every man of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and all the people, from great to small; and he read in their ears all the words of the book of the covenant that had been found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] And the king stood in his place and cut the covenant before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to walk after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and to keep his commandments and his testimonies and his statutes with all his heart and with all his soul, to do the words of the covenant that are written on this book.

[32] And he made all who were found in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ stand; and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ did according to the covenant of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers.

[33] And ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ removed all the abominations from all the lands that belonged to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he made all who were found in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ serve, to serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. All his days they did not turn aside from after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign"** (v1) — Hebrew בֶּן־שְׁמוֹנֶה שָׁנִים … בְמָלְכוֹ, literally "a son of eight years was Josiah at his reigning"; the verb is "reign", here of the beginning. Worded as 2 Kgs 22:1; Chronicles does not have the name of his mother.
- **"declined neither to the right hand, nor to the left" → "did not turn aside to the right or to the left"** (v2) — Hebrew הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינֵי "what was right in the eyes of"; יָמִין וּשְׂמֹאול, literally "right and left", with no preposition. "In the ways of David": Hebrew בְּדַרְכֵי, plural; 2 Kgs 22:2 has "in all the way of David".
- **"while he was yet young" → "while he was still a boy"** (v3) — Hebrew נַעַר; he was sixteen. "In the eighth year of his reign": Hebrew בִשְׁמוֹנֶה שָׁנִים לְמָלְכוֹ, literally "in eight years of his reigning". "Seek the Elohim of David": Hebrew דָּרַשׁ לְ, "seek" with "to".
- **"to purge" → "to cleanse"** (v3, 5, 8) — Hebrew טִהֵר "make clean".
- **"the groves, and the carved images, and the molten images" → "the Asherim and the carved images and the molten images"** (v3, 4, 7) — Hebrew אֲשֵׁרִים, wooden objects of worship bearing the name of the goddess Asherah, the word kept; פְּסִלִים "carved images"; מַסֵּכוֹת "molten images".
- **"they brake down the altars of Baalim in his presence" → "they broke down before him the altars of the Baals"** (v4, 7) — Hebrew נִתֵּץ "break down"; לְפָנָיו "before him"; הַבְּעָלִים, plural with the article.
- **"the images, that were on high above them, he cut down" → "the incense altars that were above them he hewed down"** (v4, 7) — Hebrew חַמָּנִים, commonly "incense altars"; the meaning is not certain. לְמַעְלָה מֵעֲלֵיהֶם, literally "upward from upon them". גִּדַּע "hew down".
- **"brake in pieces, and made dust of them, and strowed it upon the graves of them that had sacrificed unto them" → "broke in pieces and crushed, and he tossed them upon the face of the graves of those who had sacrificed to them"** (v4) — Hebrew שִׁבַּר "break in pieces"; הֵדַק "crush fine" (the word "dust" is not written); וַיִּזְרֹק "tossed", with no object: "them" is supplied. הַקְּבָרִים הַזֹּבְחִים לָהֶם is literally "the graves, those who sacrificed to them".
- **"their altars"** (v5) — the reading tradition (qere) מִזְבְּחוֹתָם; the written text (ketiv) has an irregular spelling of the same word. "Priests" has no article.
- **"with their mattocks round about" → "with their swords all around —"** (v6) — the reading tradition (qere) is בְּחַרְבֹתֵיהֶם, pointed as the plural of חֶרֶב "sword" (the word is also used of a cutting tool, Exod 20:25); whose swords is not said. With other vowels the same letters are commonly read "in their ruins". The written text (ketiv) is two words, בהר בתיהם, "in the mountain, their houses", or with ח for ה "he chose their houses". The meaning is uncertain. The verse has no verb; the sentence goes on in v7.
- **"had beaten the graven images into powder" → "the carved images he beat in pieces, crushing them"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּתַּת "beat in pieces"; לְהֵדַק, an infinitive, literally "to crush".
- **"when he had purged the land, and the house" → "to cleanse the land and the house"** (v8) — Hebrew לְטַהֵר, an infinitive: "to cleanse", or "in cleansing"; commonly taken as "when he had cleansed". 2 Kgs 22:3 sends Shaphan alone and names no cleansing.
- **"the governor of the city" → "the prince of the city"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׂר־הָעִיר, as 2 Kgs 23:8. **"Joah the son of Joahaz the recorder"** — Hebrew הַמַּזְכִּיר, literally "the one who brings to remembrance". The name is written מַעֲשֵׂיָהוּ here. **"to repair"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew חִזֵּק, literally "make strong".
- **"the high priest" → "the great priest"** (v9) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל, as 2 Kgs 22:4. **"delivered the money" → "gave the silver"** (v9, 14, 17) — Hebrew נָתַן "give"; כֶּסֶף "silver".
- **"the Levites that kept the doors" → "the Levites, the keepers of the threshold"** (v9) — Hebrew שֹׁמְרֵי הַסַּף, as 2 Kgs 22:4, where the Levites are not named and the silver is "from the people".
- **"and they returned to Jerusalem"** (v9) — the reading tradition (qere) וַיָּשֻׁבוּ; the written text (ketiv) is וישבי "and the inhabitants of Jerusalem". The qere is translated.
- **"they put it in the hand of the workmen that had the oversight of the house of the LORD, and they gave it to the workmen that wrought in the house" → "they gave it into the hand of the doers of the work who were appointed in the house of Yahweh; and the doers of the work who were working in the house of Yahweh gave it"** (v10) — Hebrew נָתַן עַל־יַד "give upon the hand of", with no object in the first clause: "it" is supplied (so also v11). הַמֻּפְקָדִים "the appointed ones"; בְּבֵית "in the house" (2 Kgs 22:5 qere "over the house"). In the second clause עוֹשֵׂי הַמְּלָאכָה has no "to" before it, so it is the subject; 2 Kgs 22:5 has "to the doers of the work". עֹשִׂים "were working" is the verb of "doers".
- **"to repair and amend the house" → "to mend and to repair the house"** (v10) — Hebrew לִבְדּוֹק, a verb found only here, from בֶּדֶק "breach" (2 Kgs 22:5 "to repair the breach of the house"): to mend the breaches.
- **"to the artificers and builders gave they it, to buy hewn stone, and timber for couplings, and to floor the houses which the kings of Judah had destroyed" → "to the craftsmen and to the builders, to buy hewn stones, and wood for the joinings and to lay the beams of the houses that the kings of Judah had ruined"** (v11) — Hebrew חָרָשִׁים "craftsmen"; אַבְנֵי מַחְצֵב, literally "stones of hewing"; מְחַבְּרוֹת "joinings", as 1 Chr 22:3; לְקָרוֹת, a verb from "beam"; הִשְׁחִיתוּ "ruined".
- **"the men did the work faithfully" → "the men were acting in faithfulness in the work"** (v12) — Hebrew עֹשִׂים בֶּאֱמוּנָה, as 2 Kgs 22:7, where it is said of the silver.
- **"the overseers of them … to set it forward" → "over them were appointed … to oversee"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew מֻפְקָדִים "appointed"; נִצֵּחַ "oversee". **"all that could skill of instruments of musick" → "everyone who had understanding in instruments of song"** — Hebrew מֵבִין; כְּלֵי־שִׁיר.
- **"in any manner of service" → "for service and service"** (v13) — Hebrew לַעֲבוֹדָה וַעֲבוֹדָה, the word said twice: for each kind of service. The Hebrew of v12b–13a has no verb: "and the Levites … and over the bearers of burdens, and overseers …". **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** — Hebrew שׁוֹעֲרִים.
- **"a book of the law of the LORD given by Moses" → "the book of the Torah of Yahweh by the hand of Moses"** (v14) — Hebrew סֵפֶר תּוֹרַת־יְהוָה בְּיַד־מֹשֶׁה: "given" is not written. סֵפֶר is any written document, "book" (its form was a scroll); תּוֹרָה "instruction, law".
- **"I have found the book of the law" → "The book of the Torah I have found"** (v15) — the Hebrew order, as 2 Kgs 22:8; there Hilkiah is "the great priest", he does not "answer", and Shaphan reads the book.
- **"Shaphan carried the book to the king" → "Shaphan brought the book to the king"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיָּבֵא "brought"; 2 Kgs 22:9 has "came to the king". עוֹד "further". **"All that was committed to thy servants, they do it" → "All that was given into the hand of your servants, they are doing"** — Hebrew נִתַּן בְּיַד; עֹשִׂים, a participle. "Your servants": the word is "slaves", the court way of speaking of oneself.
- **"they have gathered together the money" → "they have poured out the silver"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּתִּיכוּ, the causative of נתך "pour out": emptied it out, or melted it down; the verb of v21, 25. **"overseers" → "those who are appointed"** — Hebrew הַמֻּפְקָדִים; here they stand beside "the doers of the work", in 2 Kgs 22:9 they are the same men.
- **"read it before the king" → "read in it before the king"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא־בוֹ; 2 Kgs 22:10 has "read it".
- **"rent his clothes" → "tore his garments"** (v19, 27) — Hebrew בְּגָדִים. "The words of the Torah": 2 Kgs 22:11 has "the words of the book of the Torah".
- **"Abdon the son of Micah"** (v20) — 2 Kgs 22:12 has "Achbor the son of Micaiah", and calls Hilkiah "the priest".
- **"enquire of the LORD" → "seek Yahweh"** (v21) — Hebrew דָּרַשׁ אֶת־יְהוָה. **"for them that are left in Israel and in Judah" → "for what is left in Israel and in Judah"** — Hebrew הַנִּשְׁאָר, a singular: those who are left, taken as one. 2 Kgs 22:13 has "for the people and for all Judah".
- **"great is the wrath of the LORD that is poured out upon us" → "great is the fury of Yahweh that is poured out on us"** (v21, 25) — Hebrew חֵמָה, literally "heat"; נִתְּכָה "is poured out", with בְּ. 2 Kgs 22:13, 17 has "kindled".
- **"have not kept the word of the LORD, to do after all that is written in this book" → "did not keep the word of Yahweh, to do according to all that is written on this book"** (v21) — Hebrew עַל־הַסֵּפֶר "upon the book" (so also v24, 31). 2 Kgs 22:13 has "did not listen to the words of this book … written concerning us".
- **"they that the king had appointed" → "those of the king"** (v22) — Hebrew וַאֲשֶׁר הַמֶּלֶךְ, literally "and whom the king", with no verb. A verb ("had sent", "had told") is commonly supplied; the Masoretic Text is kept. 2 Kgs 22:14 names the four men.
- **"Tikvath, the son of Hasrah" → "Tokhath, the son of Hasrah"** (v22) — the reading tradition (qere) תָּקְהַת; the written text (ketiv) is תוקהת. 2 Kgs 22:14 has "Tikvah, the son of Harhas".
- **"keeper of the wardrobe" → "the keeper of the garments"** (v22) — Hebrew שׁוֹמֵר הַבְּגָדִים. **"she dwelt in Jerusalem in the college" → "she was dwelling in Jerusalem, in the Second quarter"** — Hebrew בַּמִּשְׁנֶה "in the Second": a part of the city (Zeph 1:10); "quarter" is supplied. **"they spake to her to that effect" → "they spoke to her according to this"** — Hebrew כָּזֹאת, literally "like this"; not in 2 Kgs 22:14.
- **"I will bring evil upon this place" → "I am bringing evil upon this place"** (v24, 28) — Hebrew הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, a participle.
- **"all the curses that are written in the book which they have read before the king of Judah"** (v24) — Hebrew הָאָלוֹת "the curses"; קָרְאוּ "they have read". 2 Kgs 22:16 has "all the words of the book that the king of Judah has read".
- **"have burned incense unto other gods, that they might provoke me to anger with all the works of their hands" → "have made smoke to other gods, in order to vex me with all the works of their hands"** (v25) — Hebrew קִטֵּר without an object: "make smoke", of any offering burned (the qere is the piel וַיְקַטְּרוּ, the ketiv the causative form of the same verb); לְמַעַן הַכְעִיסֵנִי "in order to vex me" ("anger" is not a second word); מַעֲשֵׂי "works", plural (2 Kgs 22:17 "work"). "Because": Hebrew תַּחַת אֲשֶׁר, literally "in the place of this, that". "Forsaken": Hebrew עָזַב.
- **"therefore my wrath shall be poured out" → "and my fury shall be poured out"** (v25) — Hebrew וְתִתַּךְ, with "and".
- **"who sent you to enquire of the LORD" → "who is sending you to seek by Yahweh"** (v26) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁלֵחַ, a participle; לִדְרוֹשׁ בַּיהוָה, "seek" with בְּ "by, through", as 1 Chr 10:14 (2 Kgs 22:18 has "to seek Yahweh"). **"concerning the words which thou hast heard" → "The words that you have heard —"** — Hebrew הַדְּבָרִים אֲשֶׁר שָׁמָעְתָּ, with nothing before it; the sentence is left open as in the Hebrew.
- **"thine heart was tender, and thou didst humble thyself before God … and humbledst thyself before me" → "your heart was soft and you brought yourself low before Elohim … and you brought yourself low before me"** (v27) — Hebrew רַךְ "soft"; נִכְנַע, said twice, "be brought low", here of his own doing, as 2 Kgs 22:19 and 2 Chr 12:6, 7, 12; מִלִּפְנֵי "from before", then לְפָנַי "before me". 2 Kgs 22:19 has the verb once, "before Yahweh", and "what I spoke … that they should become a thing that appalls and a curse" where Chronicles has "his words".
- **"I have even heard thee also, saith the LORD" → "I also have heard, declares Yahweh"** (v27) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי without an object; נְאֻם־יְהוָה, literally "the declaration of Yahweh".
- **"I will gather thee to thy fathers, and thou shalt be gathered to thy grave in peace, neither shall thine eyes see" → "I am gathering you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered to your graves in shalom, and your eyes shall not look on"** (v28) — Hebrew אֹסִפְךָ, a participle; קִבְרֹתֶיךָ "your graves", plural; שָׁלוֹם; רָאָה בְּ "look on". 2 Kgs 22:20 begins with "Therefore" and does not have "and upon its inhabitants".
- **"gathered together all the elders"** (v29) — Hebrew וַיֶּאֱסֹף, the king gathers; in 2 Kgs 23:1 "they gathered to him".
- **"great and small" → "from great to small"** (v30) — Hebrew מִגָּדוֹל וְעַד־קָטָן; 2 Kgs 23:2 has "from small to great", "all the inhabitants of Jerusalem with him", and "the prophets" where Chronicles has "the Levites". **"book of the covenant"** — Hebrew סֵפֶר הַבְּרִית.
- **"the king stood in his place"** (v31) — Hebrew עַל־עָמְדוֹ, literally "upon his standing place"; 2 Kgs 23:3 has "by the pillar". **"made a covenant" → "cut the covenant"** — Hebrew כָּרַת אֶת־הַבְּרִית. **"to perform the words of the covenant" → "to do the words of the covenant"** — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת; 2 Kgs 23:3 has "to establish the words of this covenant", "with all the heart and with all the soul" without "his", and "all the people stood in the covenant".
- **"he caused all that were present in Jerusalem and Benjamin to stand to it" → "he made all who were found in Jerusalem and Benjamin stand"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֵד, the causative of "stand" (v31); "to it" is not written. הַנִּמְצָא "who were found", a singular (so also v33).
- **"took away all the abominations out of all the countries that pertained to the children of Israel" → "removed all the abominations from all the lands that belonged to the sons of Israel"** (v33) — Hebrew הֵסִיר; הָאֲרָצוֹת "the lands", plural; בְּנֵי "sons".
- **"made all that were present in Israel to serve, even to serve the LORD" → "made all who were found in Israel serve, to serve Yahweh"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבֵד … לַעֲבוֹד: the verb twice, first as a causative.
- **"they departed not from following the LORD" → "they did not turn aside from after Yahweh"** (v33) — Hebrew סָרוּ מֵאַחֲרֵי, the verb of v2.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 22-23:20** — Chronicler alters chronology. In Kings, the finding of the book triggers reform; in Chronicles, Josiah begins reform at age 20 (12th year of reign, v3) and the book is found six years later (18th year, v8), deepening rather than initiating the reform.
- **Early seeking at 16** (v3) — Chronicler's spiritual biography: personal devotion → national reform → finding of the book → covenantal ratification.
- **Reform extends into northern territory** (v6-7) — Manasseh, Ephraim, Simeon, Naphtali. Josiah's religious reach wider than his political control (Assyria weakening).
- **Northern contribution** (v9) — Manasseh, Ephraim, remnant of Israel contributing to temple. Post-722 survivors still oriented to Jerusalem.
- **Huldah's oracle** — nearly verbatim from 2 Kgs 22. Female prophet, senior Jerusalem residence, consulted over Jeremiah.
