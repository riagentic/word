# 2 Chronicles 34

[1] ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty-one years in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] He did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and walked in the ways of David his father; he did not turn aside to the right or to the left.

[3] In the eighth year of his reign, while he was yet young, he began to seek the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of David his father; and in the twelfth year he began to purge ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from the high places, the Asherah poles, the carved images, and the cast images.

[4] They tore down the altars of the Baals in his presence; the incense altars that were above them he cut down; the Asherah poles, the carved images, and the cast images he broke in pieces, ground to dust, and scattered upon the graves of those who had sacrificed to them.

[5] He burned the bones of the priests on their altars, and cleansed ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[6] So he did in the cities of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, ⟦simeon|Shimon|Simeon⟧, and as far as ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, in their ruins on every side.

[7] When he had broken down the altars and the Asherah poles, ground the carved images to powder, and cut down all the incense altars throughout all the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, he returned to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[8] In the eighteenth year of his reign, when he had purged the land and the house, he sent ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ son of ⟦azaliah|Atzalyahu|Azaliah⟧, ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ the governor of the city, and ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ son of ⟦joahaz|Yoachaz|Joahaz⟧ the recorder, to repair the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] They came to ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the high priest and delivered the silver that had been brought into the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which the ⟦levites|Levi|Levite⟧ gatekeepers had gathered from the hand of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, from all the remnant of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, from all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, and from the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] They delivered it into the hand of the workmen who had oversight of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; they gave it to the workmen who were working in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to repair and restore the house.

[11] They gave it to the craftsmen and builders to buy hewn stone and timber for the couplings, and to beam the houses that the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ had let go to ruin.

[12] The men did the work faithfully. Their overseers were ⟦jahath|Yachat|Jahath⟧ and ⟦obadiah|Ovadyahu|Obadiah⟧, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ of the sons of Merari; and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ and Meshullam, of the sons of the ⟦kohathites|Qehati|Kohathites⟧, to direct; other ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, all who were skilled with instruments of music,

[13] were over the bearers of burdens, and were overseers of all who did the work in any kind of service. Of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ there were scribes, officers, and gatekeepers.

[14] When they brought out the silver that had been brought into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the priest found the Scroll of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ given through the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[15] ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ answered and said to ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe, "I have found the Scroll of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ delivered the scroll to ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧.

[16] ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ brought the scroll to the king, and brought back further word to the king, saying, "All that was committed to your servants they are doing.

[17] They have emptied out the silver that was found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and have delivered it into the hand of the overseers and of the workmen."

[18] Then ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe told the king, saying, "⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧ the priest has given me a scroll." ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ read it before the king.

[19] When the king heard the words of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, he tore his clothes.

[20] The king commanded ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧, ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧ son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧, ⟦abdon|Avdon|Abdon⟧ son of ⟦micah|Michah|Micah⟧, ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧ the scribe, and ⟦asaiah|Asayah|Asaiah⟧ a servant of the king, saying,

[26] But to the king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who sent you to inquire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, thus shall you say to him: Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ concerning the words that you have heard:

[29] Then the king sent and gathered all the elders of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[32] He made all who were present in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ take their stand. The inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ did according to the covenant of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers.

[33] ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ removed all the abominations from all the territory that belonged to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and made all who were present in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. All his days they did not depart from following ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of their fathers.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"images" on altars → "incense altars"** (v4, 7) — *chammanim*.
- **"mattocks" → "ruins"** (v6) — Hebrew *char'votehem*; MT obscure; possibly "in their ruins" (= destroyed cities) rather than tools.
- **"money" → "silver"** (throughout).
- **"Tikvath" → "Tokhath"** (v22) — 2 Kgs 22:14 has Tikvah; minor variant.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 22-23:20** — Chronicler alters chronology. In Kings, scroll-finding triggers reform; in Chronicles, Josiah begins reform at age 20 (12th year of reign, v3) and scroll is found six years later (18th year, v8), deepening rather than initiating the reform.
- **Early seeking at 16** (v3) — Chronicler's spiritual biography: personal devotion → national reform → scroll-discovery → covenantal ratification.
- **Reform extends into northern territory** (v6-7) — Manasseh, Ephraim, Simeon, Naphtali. Josiah's religious reach wider than his political control (Assyria weakening).
- **Northern contribution** (v9) — Manasseh, Ephraim, remnant of Israel contributing to temple. Post-722 survivors still oriented to Jerusalem.
- **Huldah's oracle** — nearly verbatim from 2 Kgs 22. Female prophet, senior Jerusalem residence, consulted over Jeremiah.
