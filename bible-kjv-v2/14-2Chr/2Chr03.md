# 2 Chronicles 3

[1] Solomon began to build the house of Yahweh at Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where Yahweh had appeared to David his father — in the place that David had prepared, on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.

[2] He began to build on the second day of the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.

[3] These are the foundations Solomon laid for the building of the house of *Elohim*: the length, by cubits of the old standard, was sixty cubits, and the breadth twenty cubits.

[4] The portico in front of the house was according to the breadth of the house, twenty cubits, and the height one hundred twenty; he overlaid it on the inside with pure gold.

[5] The greater house he paneled with cypress; he overlaid it with fine gold, and set upon it palms and chains.

[6] He adorned the house with precious stones for beauty; the gold was gold of Parvaim.

[7] He overlaid the house — the beams, the thresholds, its walls, and its doors — with gold; and he carved cherubim on the walls.

[8] He made the most holy place; its length was according to the breadth of the house, twenty cubits, and its breadth twenty cubits; he overlaid it with fine gold, amounting to six hundred talents.

[9] The weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold. He also overlaid the upper rooms with gold.

[10] In the most holy place he made two cherubim of sculpted work, and overlaid them with gold.

[11] The wings of the cherubim together were twenty cubits long: one wing of the one cherub, five cubits, reached to the wall of the house; the other wing, five cubits, reached the wing of the other cherub.

[12] One wing of the other cherub, five cubits, reached the wall of the house; the other wing, five cubits, joined the wing of the other cherub.

[13] The wings of these cherubim extended twenty cubits; they stood on their feet, with their faces toward the main hall.

[14] He made the veil of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and wrought cherubim on it.

[15] In front of the house he made two pillars thirty-five cubits high; the capital on top of each was five cubits.

[16] He made chains like those in the inner sanctuary, and put them on the tops of the pillars; he made a hundred pomegranates and put them on the chains.

[17] He set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and the other on the left; he called the name of the right one Jachin and the name of the left one Boaz.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Moriah" — retained** (v1) — connects to Abraham's binding of Isaac (Gen 22:2). Chronicler makes the link explicit: temple site = Moriah.
- **"porch" → "portico"** (v4) — *ulam*.
- **"cieled" → "paneled"** (v5) — *chippah*.
- **"fir" → "cypress"** (v5) — *berosh*.
- **"faces were inward" → "faces toward the main hall"** (v13) — *penehem la-bayit*; facing outward toward the nave, not toward each other.
- **"oracle" → "inner sanctuary"** (v16) — *devir*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 6-7** — Chronicler abridges severely. 1 Kgs gives much more detail; here ~20 verses to 1 Kgs's 60+.
- **Moriah identification** (v1) — Chronicler uniquely ties David's Ornan site with Abraham's Moriah. Theological conflation: place of Isaac's near-sacrifice, David's altar, Solomon's temple.
- **"Old cubit"** (v3) — references a longer cubit (probably 52 cm vs short 44 cm).
- **Porch height 120 cubits** (v4) — likely textual corruption; 1 Kgs 6:2 gives temple height 30 cubits. Some MSS and LXX read "twenty cubits."
- **Gold of Parvaim** — location uncertain; proverbial fine gold.
- **Cherubim facing the nave** — Chronicler's distinctive detail: faced *outward*, guarding the holy place like guardian figures.
- **Pillars 35 cubits** (v15) — 1 Kgs 7:15 says 18 cubits each. Chronicler may give combined length, or textual variant.
- **Jachin and Boaz** — freestanding bronze pillars flanking the entrance; iconic temple features.
