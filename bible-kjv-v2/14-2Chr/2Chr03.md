# 2 Chronicles 3

[1] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ began to build the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, on Mount ⟦moriah|Moriyyah|Moriah⟧, where he had appeared to David his father, which he had prepared in the place of David, on the threshing floor of Ornan the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧.

[2] And he began to build in the second month, on the second, in the fourth year of his reign.

[3] And these are the foundations of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ for building the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: the length, in cubits by the former measure, sixty cubits, and the width twenty cubits.

[4] And the porch that was facing the length, facing the width of the house: twenty cubits; and the height a hundred and twenty; and he overlaid it within with pure gold.

[5] And the great house he lined with cypress wood, and he lined it with good gold, and he set up on it palm trees and chains.

[6] And he overlaid the house with precious stone for beauty; and the gold was gold of ⟦parvaim|Parvayim|Parvaim⟧.

[7] And he lined the house, the beams, the thresholds, and its walls and its doors, with gold; and he engraved ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ on the walls.

[8] And he made the house of the Holy of Holies: its length, facing the width of the house, twenty cubits, and its width twenty cubits; and he lined it with good gold, to six hundred talents.

[9] And the weight for the nails was fifty shekels of gold; and the upper rooms he lined with gold.

[10] And he made in the house of the Holy of Holies two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, sculptured work, and they overlaid them with gold.

[11] And the wings of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧: their length was twenty cubits; the wing of the one, of five cubits, reaching to the wall of the house, and the other wing, five cubits, reaching to the wing of the other ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧.

[12] And the wing of the one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧, five cubits, reaching to the wall of the house, and the other wing, five cubits, clinging to the wing of the other ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧.

[13] The wings of these ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ were spreading out twenty cubits; and they were standing on their feet, and their faces were toward the house.

[14] And he made the veil of blue and purple and crimson and fine linen, and he set up on it ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[15] And he made before the house two pillars, thirty-five cubits in length, and the cap that was on its top, five cubits.

[16] And he made chains in the inner room and placed them on the top of the pillars; and he made a hundred pomegranates and placed them in the chains.

[17] And he raised up the pillars facing the temple, one on the right and one on the left; and he called the name of the right one ⟦jachin|Yachin|Jachin⟧ and the name of the left one Boaz.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*, here with the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"in mount Moriah" → "on Mount Moriah"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּהַר הַמּוֹרִיָּה, "on the mount of the Moriah", with the article as in Gen 22:2 ("the land of the Moriah"). **"where the LORD appeared unto David his father" → "where he had appeared to David his father"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר נִרְאָה לְדָוִיד: no subject is named ("the LORD" is supplied in the KJV). The verb can also be read "which was shown to David".
- **"in the place that David had prepared" → "which he had prepared in the place of David"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר הֵכִין בִּמְקוֹם דָּוִיד, word for word; who prepared and what "the place of David" is are not said. The old versions have the words in another order, "in the place that David had prepared" (from memory), and so the verse is commonly read; the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"in the second day of the second month" → "in the second month, on the second"** (v2) — Hebrew בַּחֹדֶשׁ הַשֵּׁנִי בַּשֵּׁנִי; "day" is not written. **"in the fourth year of his reign"** — Hebrew לְמַלְכוּתוֹ, the word "kingdom", of the time he was king. 1 Kgs 6:1 names the month Ziv and the four hundred and eightieth year after the going out from Egypt; Chronicles has neither.
- **"these are the things wherein Solomon was instructed for the building" → "these are the foundations of Solomon for building"** (v3) — Hebrew וְאֵלֶּה הוּסַד שְׁלֹמֹה לִבְנוֹת: הוּסַד is a passive form of יָסַד "found", literally "these — the being founded of Solomon": the measures of the ground plan. The construction is difficult. **"after the first measure" → "by the former measure"** — Hebrew בַּמִּדָּה הָרִאשׁוֹנָה, literally "the first measure": an older cubit. **"breadth" → "width"** (v3, 4, 8) — Hebrew רֹחַב, as 1 Kgs 6:2, which also gives the height (thirty cubits).
- **"the porch that was in the front of the house, the length of it was according to the breadth of the house" → "the porch that was facing the length, facing the width of the house"** (v4) — Hebrew וְהָאוּלָם אֲשֶׁר עַל־פְּנֵי הָאֹרֶךְ עַל־פְּנֵי רֹחַב־הַבַּיִת, word for word; the sentence is difficult. It is commonly understood "the porch in front: its length, facing the width of the house, twenty cubits", as 1 Kgs 6:3. "Of the house" after "front" and "of it" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"the height was an hundred and twenty"** (v4) — so the Masoretic Text; no unit is named. Some Greek and Syriac witnesses read "twenty cubits" (from memory). הַגֹּבַהּ is another word for "height" than קוֹמָה in 1 Kgs 6:2.
- **"pure gold"** (v4) — Hebrew זָהָב טָהוֹר, literally "clean gold"; 1 Kgs 6:20, 21 has זָהָב סָגוּר "refined gold".
- **"the greater house he cieled with fir tree, which he overlaid with fine gold" → "the great house he lined with cypress wood, and he lined it with good gold"** (v5) — Hebrew חִפָּה, a verb found only in this chapter (v5, 7, 8, 9), beside צִפָּה "overlay" (v4, 6, 10); it is rendered "line" to keep the two apart. הַבַּיִת הַגָּדוֹל is "the great house": the front room, the "temple" of 1 Kgs 6:17. זָהָב טוֹב is "good gold". **"set thereon palm trees and chains" → "set up on it palm trees and chains"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַל עָלָיו, literally "made go up on it" (also v14).
- **"garnished the house with precious stones" → "overlaid the house with precious stone"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיְצַף "overlaid"; אֶבֶן יְקָרָה is singular. **"Parvaim"** — the place is not known.
- **"the posts" → "the thresholds"** (v7) — Hebrew הַסִּפִּים. **"graved cherubims on the walls" → "engraved cherubim on the walls"** — Hebrew פִּתַּח; 1 Kgs 6:29 has "carved" (קָלַע) and adds palm trees and open blossoms.
- **"the most holy house" → "the house of the Holy of Holies"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew בֵּית־קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים; 1 Kgs 6 calls the room דְּבִיר "inner room" and "the Holy of Holies" (6:16). **"amounting to six hundred talents" → "to six hundred talents"** — Hebrew לְכִכָּרִים.
- **"the upper chambers" → "the upper rooms"** (v9) — Hebrew הָעֲלִיּוֹת.
- **"two cherubims of image work" → "two cherubim, sculptured work"** (v10) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה צַעֲצֻעִים; the word is found only here and its meaning is not certain (something shaped, cast or carved). 1 Kgs 6:23 has "of oil-tree wood". **"overlaid them" → "they overlaid them"** — the Hebrew verb is plural.
- **"reaching to"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew מַגִּיעַ, the causative of נָגַע "touch"; 1 Kgs 6:27 has "touch". **"joining to" → "clinging to"** (v12) — Hebrew דְּבֵקָה. **"one wing of the other cherub" → "the wing of the one cherub"** (v12) — the Hebrew says הַכְּרוּב הָאֶחָד "the one cherub" again; the second cherub is meant.
- **"spread themselves forth" → "were spreading out"** (v13) — Hebrew פֹּרְשִׂים, a participle. **"their faces were inward" → "their faces were toward the house"** — Hebrew וּפְנֵיהֶם לַבָּיִת: toward the main room, not toward each other.
- **"vail" → "veil"** (v14) — Hebrew פָּרֹכֶת; 1 Kgs 6 names no veil. **"crimson"**, **"fine linen"** — Hebrew כַּרְמִיל and בּוּץ, the later words (see 2:7, 14).
- **"thirty and five cubits high" → "thirty-five cubits in length"** (v15) — Hebrew אֹרֶךְ "length"; 1 Kgs 7:15 has "eighteen cubits the height of the one pillar". **"the chapiter that was on the top of each of them" → "the cap that was on its top"** — Hebrew הַצֶּפֶת אֲשֶׁר־עַל־רֹאשׁוֹ: צֶפֶת is found only here and is another word than כֹּתֶרֶת "capital" (4:12; 1 Kgs 7:16); the suffix is singular.
- **"he made chains, as in the oracle" → "he made chains in the inner room"** (v16) — Hebrew בַּדְּבִיר, "in the inner room", as it stands; "as" is not written. Chains for the pillars made "in the inner room" are hard to understand, and the word is commonly corrected ("like a necklace"); the Masoretic Text is kept. **"put them on the heads of the pillars" → "placed them on the top of the pillars"** — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן עַל־רֹאשׁ הָעַמֻּדִים, as 1 Kgs 7:17.
- **"reared up the pillars before the temple" → "raised up the pillars facing the temple"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיָּקֶם … עַל־פְּנֵי הַהֵיכָל; 1 Kgs 7:21 has "at the porch of the temple". **"called the name of that on the right hand Jachin"** — "the right one" follows the reading tradition (qere הַיְמָנִי); the written form differs in spelling only.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 6; 7:15–22** — Chronicles is much shorter and uses its own words; no whole verse is the same Hebrew as in Kings.
- **Moriah** (v1) — only here is the place of the house called Mount Moriah; Gen 22:2 has "the land of Moriah". The threshing floor of Ornan is the place of 1 Chr 21:15–22:1.
- **"The former measure"** (v3) — an older cubit; its length is not given.
- **Height a hundred and twenty** (v4) — 1 Kgs 6:2 gives the height of the house as thirty cubits and no height for the porch.
- **Gold of Parvaim** (v6) — a place name not found elsewhere.
- **Pillars of thirty-five cubits** (v15) — 1 Kgs 7:15 has eighteen cubits for each pillar; the two figures are not explained in the text.
- **Jachin and Boaz** (v17) — the two pillars before the house; the names are commonly understood "he establishes" and "in him is strength".
