# 2 Chronicles 15

[1] The spirit of *Elohim* came upon Azariah son of Oded.

[2] He went out to meet Asa and said to him, "Hear me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin! Yahweh is with you while you are with him; if you seek him, he will be found by you; but if you forsake him, he will forsake you.

[3] For a long time Israel was without the true *Elohim*, without a teaching priest, and without Law.

[4] But when they in their distress turned to Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel and sought him, he was found by them.

[5] In those times there was no peace to him who went out or came in, but great disturbances were upon all the inhabitants of the lands.

[6] Nation was crushed by nation, and city by city, for *Elohim* troubled them with every kind of distress.

[7] But be strong, and do not let your hands be slack, for your work shall be rewarded."

[8] When Asa heard these words and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage and put away the detestable things from all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and from the cities that he had captured in the hill country of Ephraim; he renewed the altar of Yahweh that was before the portico of Yahweh.

[9] He gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and those sojourning with them from Ephraim, Manasseh, and Simeon; for they fell to him out of Israel in abundance, when they saw that Yahweh his *Elohim* was with him.

[10] So they gathered at Jerusalem in the third month, in the fifteenth year of Asa's reign.

[11] On that day they sacrificed to Yahweh seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep from the spoil they had brought.

[12] They entered into a covenant to seek Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers with all their heart and with all their soul;

[13] and whoever would not seek Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel was to be put to death, whether small or great, whether man or woman.

[14] They swore to Yahweh with a loud voice, with shouting, with trumpets — and with shofarot.

[15] All Judah rejoiced over the oath, for they had sworn with all their heart and sought him with their whole desire, and he was found by them; Yahweh gave them rest on every side.

[16] King Asa even removed Maacah his grandmother from being queen mother — because she had made an abominable image for Asherah; Asa cut down her image, crushed it, and burned it at the Wadi Kidron.

[17] But the high places were not taken away out of Israel; nevertheless the heart of Asa was wholly true all his days.

[18] He brought into the house of *Elohim* the things that his father had dedicated and that he himself had dedicated — silver, gold, and vessels.

[19] There was no more war until the thirty-fifth year of Asa's reign.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"abominable idols" → "detestable things"** (v8) — *shiqqutzim*.
- **"strangers" → "those sojourning"** (v9) — *gerim*; here northerners coming as sojourners to Judah's worship.
- **"perfect" → "wholly true"** (v17) — *shalem*.
- **"mother" → "grandmother"** (v16) — *em* = ancestress.
- **"the brook Kidron" → "the Wadi Kidron"** (v16).

### Notes
- **Azariah son of Oded** — prophet unique to Chronicler. Not the same as Oded of 2 Chr 28:9.
- **"Teaching priest"** (v3) — Levites as torah-teachers (Deut 33:10). Pre-temple Israel described as periods of anarchy; likely references Judges.
- **Covenant renewal** (v12) — mass national re-commitment. Public, with death penalty for apostasy (Deut 13, 17).
- **Fifteenth year of Asa** — ~895 BC. Mid-reign reform.
- **Northern defection to Judah** (v9) — Chronicler's recurring theme: faithful from North seek Yahweh at Jerusalem.
- **"Thirty-fifth year of Asa" peace ends** (v19) — introduces ch 16. Chronological note for the Baasha conflict.
- **"Queen mother" deposition** — Asa's remarkable move against royal matriarch; takes Yahwistic exclusivism seriously within own family.
