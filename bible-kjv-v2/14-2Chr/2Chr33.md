# 2 Chronicles 33

[1] ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ was twelve years old when he began to reign, and fifty-five years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] And he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to the abominations of the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dispossessed from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] And he built again the high places that ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ his father had broken down, and he raised up altars for the Baals and made Asheroth, and he bowed down to all the host of the heavens and served them.

[4] And he built altars in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, of which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had said, "In ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ shall my name be forever."

[5] And he built altars for all the host of the heavens in the two courts of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And he himself made his sons pass through the fire in the valley of the son of Hinnom, and he practiced soothsaying and divined and practiced sorcery, and he set up a ghost and a knowing spirit; he did much of the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to vex him.

[7] And he put the carved image of the figure that he had made in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, of which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had said to David and to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his son, "In this house and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, I will put my name forever.

[8] And I will not again remove the foot of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from upon the ground that I appointed for your fathers, only if they keep to do all that I have commanded them, as to all the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and the statutes and the ordinances, by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧."

[9] And ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ caused ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to wander, to do evil more than the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ destroyed from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[10] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ and to his people, and they did not give heed.

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought upon them the commanders of the host of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, and they captured ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ with hooks and bound him with bronze fetters and led him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[12] And when he was distressed, he besought the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and brought himself low greatly before the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of his fathers.

[13] And he prayed to him, and he let himself be pleaded with by him and heard his plea for favor and brought him back to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to his kingdom. And ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ knew that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[14] And after this he built an outer wall for the city of David, west of ⟦gihon|Gichon|Gihon⟧, in the wadi, and to the entering in at the Fish Gate, and he went around the ⟦ophel|Ofel|Ophel⟧ and made it very high. And he put commanders of a force in all the fortified cities in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[15] And he removed the foreign gods and the figure from the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and all the altars that he had built on the mountain of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and he threw them outside the city.

[16] And he built the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and sacrificed on it sacrifices of peace offerings and of thanksgiving, and he said to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[17] But the people were still sacrificing on the high places, only to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[18] And the rest of the acts of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, and his prayer to his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the words of the seers who spoke to him in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, behold, they are in the words of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[19] And his prayer, and how he let himself be pleaded with by him, and all his sin and his unfaithfulness, and the places in which he built high places and made the Asherim and the carved images stand, before he brought himself low, behold, they are written in the words of Hozai.

[20] And ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ lay with his fathers, and they buried him in his house, and Amon his son reigned in his place.

[21] Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and two years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[22] And he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ his father had done; and to all the carved images that ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ his father had made Amon sacrificed, and he served them.

[23] And he did not bring himself low before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ as ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ his father had brought himself low, for he, Amon, multiplied guilt.

[24] And his servants conspired against him and put him to death in his house.

[25] And the people of the land struck down all who had conspired against King Amon, and the people of the land made ⟦josiah|Yoshiyahu|Josiah⟧ his son king in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v7 and v13 the Hebrew has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **Parallel 2 Kgs 21** — v1–9 and v20–25 run beside 2 Kgs 21:1–9, 18–24. Where the Hebrew is the same the wording is the same; each difference of Chronicles is translated as printed and named below. Nothing is taken over from Kings.
- **"Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign"** (v1, 21) — Hebrew בֶּן־שְׁתֵּים עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה … בְמָלְכוֹ, literally "a son of twelve years was Manasseh at his reigning". The verb מָלַךְ is "reign"; with the age at accession it tells the beginning of the reign. Chronicles does not name the mothers (2 Kgs 21:1, 19).
- **"did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD" → "did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v2, 6, 22) — Hebrew הָרַע "the evil", with the article; בְּעֵינֵי "in the eyes of".
- **"heathen, whom the LORD had cast out before the children of Israel" → "nations that Yahweh dispossessed from before the sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew גּוֹיִם; הוֹרִישׁ "dispossess"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of"; בְּנֵי "sons of".
- **"he built again"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיָּשָׁב וַיִּבֶן, literally "and he returned and built". **"had broken down"** — Hebrew נִתַּץ; 2 Kgs 21:3 has אִבַּד "had done away with". The name is written יְחִזְקִיָּהוּ here.
- **"altars for Baalim, and made groves" → "altars for the Baals and made Asheroth"** (v3) — Hebrew לַבְּעָלִים and אֲשֵׁרוֹת, both plural; 2 Kgs 21:3 has the singular "for the Baal … an Asherah" and adds "as Ahab king of Israel had done". אֲשֵׁרָה is the wooden object of worship bearing the goddess's name; "pole" is not in the Hebrew. The plural is אֲשֵׁרוֹת here and אֲשֵׁרִים in v19 ("Asherim").
- **"the host of heaven" → "the host of the heavens"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם is plural. "Bowed down": Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה.
- **"And he built altars"** (v4) — Hebrew וּבָנָה, a perfect with "and" where the narrative form is expected (so also וְעוֹנֵן, וְנִחֵשׁ, וְכִשֵּׁף, וְעָשָׂה in v6); translated as simple past. **"shall my name be forever"** — Hebrew יִהְיֶה־שְּׁמִי לְעוֹלָם; 2 Kgs 21:4 has "I will put my name".
- **"And he caused his children to pass through the fire in the valley of the son of Hinnom" → "And he himself made his sons pass through the fire in the valley of the son of Hinnom"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהוּא הֶעֱבִיר, with the pronoun written out ("and he, he made pass"); בָּנָיו "his sons", plural (2 Kgs 21:6 "his son"); the place is named only in Chronicles. הֶעֱבִיר is literally "make pass over".
- **"observed times, and used enchantments, and used witchcraft, and dealt with a familiar spirit, and with wizards" → "practiced soothsaying and divined and practiced sorcery, and he set up a ghost and a knowing spirit"** (v6) — Hebrew עוֹנֵן (exact sense unknown) and נִחֵשׁ "divine, seek omens", as Deut 18:10; כִּשֵּׁף "practice sorcery" stands only in Chronicles. עָשָׂה אוֹב וְיִדְּעוֹנִי is literally "made a ghost and a knowing spirit": the verb is "make / do", here of providing or appointing the means of asking the dead, so "set up"; the two nouns name the spirits (Lev 20:27), and their exact sense is uncertain ("mediums and wizards", "familiar spirits" are other renderings). The second noun is singular here, plural in 2 Kgs 21:6.
- **"he wrought much evil in the sight of the LORD, to provoke him to anger" → "he did much of the evil in the eyes of Yahweh, to vex him"** (v6) — Hebrew הִרְבָּה לַעֲשׂוֹת הָרַע, literally "he made much the doing of the evil"; לְהַכְעִיסוֹ "to vex him": "anger" is not a second word in the Hebrew. Here "him" is written (2 Kgs 21:6 has the bare verb).
- **"a carved image, the idol which he had made" → "the carved image of the figure that he had made"** (v7, 15) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל הַסֶּמֶל, a construct: "the carved image of the figure"; פֶּסֶל "carved image", סֶמֶל "figure", a statue (Deut 4:16; Ezek 8:3, 5), exact sense uncertain. 2 Kgs 21:7 has "the carved image of the Asherah". "Idol" is not the word.
- **"in the house of God, of which God had said"** (v7) — Chronicles has בְּבֵית הָאֱלֹהִים … אֱלֹהִים where 2 Kgs 21:7 has "in the house of which Yahweh had said". **"will I put my name"** — Hebrew שִׂים "put". "Forever" is written לְעֵילוֹם here, a spelling found only in this place.
- **"Neither will I any more remove the foot of Israel from out of the land which I have appointed for your fathers" → "And I will not again remove the foot of Israel from upon the ground that I appointed for your fathers"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא אוֹסִיף לְהָסִיר, literally "I will not add to remove"; רֶגֶל "foot"; מֵעַל הָאֲדָמָה "from upon the ground"; הֶעֱמַדְתִּי, literally "I made stand" (the verb of v19), here of fixing or assigning. 2 Kgs 21:8 has "make wander", "gave" and "their fathers".
- **"so that they will take heed to do all that I have commanded them, according to the whole law and the statutes and the ordinances by the hand of Moses" → "only if they keep to do all that I have commanded them, as to all the Torah and the statutes and the ordinances, by the hand of Moses"** (v8) — Hebrew רַק אִם "only if"; שָׁמַר לַעֲשׂוֹת "keep to do"; לְכָל־הַתּוֹרָה with לְ "to, as to"; מִשְׁפָּטִים "judgments", here rulings: "ordinances"; בְּיַד "by the hand of". No verb "given" is written.
- **"So Manasseh made Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to err, and to do worse than the heathen" → "And Manasseh caused Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to wander, to do evil more than the nations"** (v9) — Hebrew הִתְעָה, the causative of "wander, go astray"; רָע "evil", here without the article (2 Kgs 21:9 "the evil"); מִן "more than".
- **"but they would not hearken" → "and they did not give heed"** (v10) — Hebrew וְלֹא הִקְשִׁיבוּ, the plain past of הִקְשִׁיב "give heed"; "would not" is not written.
- **"Wherefore the LORD brought upon them the captains of the host of the king of Assyria" → "And Yahweh brought upon them the commanders of the host of the king of Assyria"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיָּבֵא "and he brought": "therefore" is not written. שָׂרֵי הַצָּבָא אֲשֶׁר לְמֶלֶךְ אַשּׁוּר is literally "the commanders of the host that the king of Assyria had".
- **"took Manasseh among the thorns" → "captured Manasseh with hooks"** (v11) — Hebrew בַּחֹחִים. The word חוֹחַ is "thistle, thorn" (2 Kgs 14:9) and is used of a hook through the jaw in Job 41:2; the sense here is uncertain: "with hooks" (as captives were led) or "among the thorns". לָכַד "capture".
- **"bound him with fetters, and carried him to Babylon" → "bound him with bronze fetters and led him to Babylon"** (v11) — Hebrew נְחֻשְׁתַּיִם, a dual of "bronze" (2 Kgs 25:7); הוֹלִיךְ, the causative of "go, walk".
- **"when he was in affliction, he besought the LORD his God" → "when he was distressed, he besought the face of Yahweh his Elohim"** (v12) — Hebrew וּכְהָצֵר לוֹ, literally "and at the distressing to him" (the root of צָרָה "distress"); חִלָּה אֶת־פְּנֵי, literally "made the face soft / sweet": "face" is kept.
- **"humbled himself" → "brought himself low"** (v12, 19, 23) — Hebrew נִכְנַע, the passive-reflexive of הִכְנִיעַ "bring low": "was brought low", of a man before God "brought himself low" (so 1 Kgs 21:29; 2 Kgs 22:19; 2 Chr 12:6, 7, 12). In v12 and v23 with מִלִּפְנֵי "from before"; in v12 with מְאֹד "greatly".
- **"and he was intreated of him, and heard his supplication" → "and he let himself be pleaded with by him and heard his plea for favor"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיֵּעָתֶר לוֹ, the passive-reflexive of עָתַר "plead with"; the one who lets himself be pleaded with is not named: the verse has no word "God" here. תְּחִנָּה "plea for favor", from חָנַן "show favor". "Brought him back": Hebrew וַיְשִׁיבֵהוּ; "again" is not written.
- **"Then Manasseh knew that the LORD he was God" → "And Manasseh knew that Yahweh, he is Elohim"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהוָה הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים, the confession of Deut 4:35 and 1 Kgs 18:39, with the article.
- **"he built a wall without the city of David, on the west side of Gihon, in the valley, even to the entering in at the fish gate, and compassed about Ophel, and raised it up a very great height" → "he built an outer wall for the city of David, west of Gihon, in the wadi, and to the entering in at the Fish Gate, and he went around the Ophel and made it very high"** (v14) — Hebrew חוֹמָה חִיצוֹנָה "an outer wall"; נַחַל "wadi", a stream bed; וְלָבוֹא בְשַׁעַר הַדָּגִים, literally "and to the coming in at the gate of the fishes"; וְסָבַב לָעֹפֶל "and he went around (to) the Ophel", the verb masculine: its subject is the king, not the wall; הָעֹפֶל has the article ("the swelling", a hill). "It" in "made it very high" is feminine in the Hebrew: the wall. The course of the wall is not clear in every point.
- **"put captains of war in all the fenced cities" → "put commanders of a force in all the fortified cities"** (v14) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי־חַיִל; שַׂר is the word for "prince", of an army "commander" (so v11, where the Hebrew has צָבָא "host"); חַיִל "force".
- **"he took away the strange gods, and the idol" → "he removed the foreign gods and the figure"** (v15) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי הַנֵּכָר, literally "the gods of foreignness"; הַסֶּמֶל as v7. **"cast them out of the city" → "threw them outside the city"** — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁלֵךְ חוּצָה לָעִיר; "them" is supplied. "The mountain of the house of Yahweh": Hebrew הַר.
- **"he repaired the altar of the LORD" → "he built the altar of Yahweh"** (v16) — the reading tradition (qere) has וַיִּבֶן "and he built"; the written text (ketiv) has ויכן "and he set up / established".
- **"sacrificed thereon peace offerings and thank offerings, and commanded Judah to serve" → "sacrificed on it sacrifices of peace offerings and of thanksgiving, and he said to Judah to serve"** (v16) — Hebrew זִבְחֵי שְׁלָמִים וְתוֹדָה; וַיֹּאמֶר "and he said".
- **"Nevertheless the people did sacrifice still in the high places, yet unto the LORD their God only" → "But the people were still sacrificing on the high places, only to Yahweh their Elohim"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲבָל, in the late books "but"; זֹבְחִים, a participle; רַק "only".
- **"the rest of the acts of Manasseh … behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel" → "the rest of the acts of Manasseh … behold, they are in the words of the kings of Israel"** (v18) — Hebrew הִנָּם עַל־דִּבְרֵי מַלְכֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל: neither "written" nor "book" stands here. One word, דִּבְרֵי, is "acts" of Manasseh and "words" of the seers and of the kings (literally "the words / matters of"). 2 Kgs 21:17 names "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah". "Seers": Hebrew חֹזִים.
- **"and how God was intreated of him" → "and how he let himself be pleaded with by him"** (v19) — Hebrew וְהֵעָתֶר־לוֹ, an infinitive: literally "and the letting himself be pleaded with by him"; "how" stands for the infinitive. No word "God" is written.
- **"all his sins, and his trespass" → "all his sin and his unfaithfulness"** (v19) — Hebrew חַטָּאתוֹ, singular; מַעַל "unfaithfulness", a breach of trust.
- **"the places wherein he built high places, and set up groves and graven images, before he was humbled" → "the places in which he built high places and made the Asherim and the carved images stand, before he brought himself low"** (v19) — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד "make stand"; הָאֲשֵׁרִים, the other plural of "Asherah" (v3); פְּסִלִים "carved images"; לִפְנֵי הִכָּנְעוֹ, literally "before his bringing himself low".
- **"among the sayings of the seers" → "in the words of Hozai"** (v19) — Hebrew עַל דִּבְרֵי חוֹזָי. As pointed, חוֹזָי is a name, "Hozai", or "my seers"; the Masoretic Text is kept as a name. The Septuagint is commonly cited as reading "the seers" (from memory), which would be הַחֹזִים as in v18.
- **"slept with his fathers, and they buried him in his own house" → "lay with his fathers, and they buried him in his house"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁכַב "lie, lie down"; בֵּיתוֹ "his house" stands without a preposition, and "own" is not written. 2 Kgs 21:18 has "was buried in the garden of his house, in the garden of Uzza".
- **"for he sacrificed unto all the carved images" → "and to all the carved images … Amon sacrificed, and he served them"** (v22) — Hebrew וּלְכָל־הַפְּסִילִים, "and", with the object first; "for" is not written.
- **"but Amon trespassed more and more" → "for he, Amon, multiplied guilt"** (v23) — Hebrew כִּי הוּא אָמוֹן הִרְבָּה אַשְׁמָה: כִּי "for"; הִרְבָּה "made much" (the verb of v6); אַשְׁמָה "guilt".
- **"slew him in his own house" → "put him to death in his house"** (v24) — Hebrew הֵמִית; "own" is not written. "Servants": Hebrew עֲבָדִים, the word for "slaves", here the king's men. 2 Kgs 21:23 has "the servants of Amon … put the king to death".
- **"slew all them that had conspired" → "struck down all who had conspired"** (v25) — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike", where killing is meant; the verb is plural here, singular in 2 Kgs 21:24. "Made … king": Hebrew הִמְלִיךְ, literally "made reign".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 21:1-18** — Chronicler uniquely adds Manasseh's Babylonian captivity, repentance, restoration, and reform (v11-17). Kings paints him unrelievedly black.
- **Manasseh's exile to Babylon** — no direct external attestation; some scholars propose he was among vassals summoned by Ashurbanipal after Shamash-shum-ukin's Babylonian revolt (ca. 652 BC), or earlier by Esarhaddon. Manasseh's name appears in Assyrian tribute lists.
- **"Prayer of Manasseh"** — separate apocryphal text in LXX/Vulgate tradition, inspired by v18-19; not canonical.
- **People still on high places but to Yahweh** (v17) — incomplete reform; later requires Josiah.
- **Chronicler's theology** — repentance always possible; even the worst king can turn. Reframes 55 years of reign as concluded in genuine conversion.
- **Amon unchanged** — compared unfavorably: does not humble himself. Quick regicide follows.
