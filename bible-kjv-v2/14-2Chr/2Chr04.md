# 2 Chronicles 4

[1] He made an altar of bronze: twenty cubits in length, twenty cubits in breadth, and ten cubits in height.

[2] He made the cast-metal Sea, ten cubits from brim to brim, circular in shape, five cubits high; a line of thirty cubits encircled it.

[3] Under it were figures of oxen encircling it round about — ten per cubit, surrounding the Sea. The oxen were in two rows, cast when it was cast.

[4] It stood on twelve oxen: three facing north, three facing west, three facing south, and three facing east; the Sea was set on top of them, and all their rear parts pointed inward.

[5] Its thickness was a handbreadth; its brim was made like the brim of a cup, like the flower of a lily. It received and held three thousand bath.

[6] He made ten basins: he placed five on the right and five on the left, for washing; in them they would rinse what was offered for the burnt offering. But the Sea was for the priests to wash in.

[7] He made ten golden lampstands according to their specification; he set them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left.

[8] He made ten tables and placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left; he made a hundred golden basins.

[9] He made the court of the priests and the great court, and doors for the court; he overlaid its doors with bronze.

[10] He set the Sea on the south side of the east end, facing the south.

[11] ⟦hiram|Churam|Hiram⟧ made the pots, shovels, and basins. So ⟦hiram|Churam|Hiram⟧ finished the work that he did for King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ for the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧:

[12] the two pillars, the globes and capitals on top of the two pillars; the two lattices to cover the two globes of the capitals on top of the pillars;

[13] the four hundred pomegranates for the two lattices (two rows of pomegranates for each lattice, to cover the two globes of the capitals on the pillars);

[14] the stands and the basins on the stands;

[15] the one Sea and the twelve oxen under it;

[16] the pots, shovels, forks, and all their utensils, ⟦huram-abi|Churam-avi|Huram-abi⟧ made for King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, of burnished bronze.

[17] In the plain of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ the king cast them, in the clay ground between ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ and ⟦zeredah|Tzeredah|Zeredah⟧.

[18] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made all these vessels in great abundance, for the weight of the bronze was beyond reckoning.

[19] ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made all the vessels that were for the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: the golden altar; the tables on which the bread of the Presence was set;

[20] the lampstands with their lamps of pure gold, to burn before the inner sanctuary as prescribed;

[21] the flowers, the lamps, and the tongs — of pure gold;

[22] the snuffers, basins, pans, and firepans of pure gold; and the entrance of the house, its inner doors for the most holy place, and the doors of the house — of the nave — of gold.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (throughout) — *nechoshet*.
- **"molten sea" → "cast-metal Sea"** (v2).
- **"lavers" → "basins"** (v6, 14).
- **"candlesticks" → "lampstands"** (v7, 20) — *menorot*.
- **"basons" → "basins"** (v8, 11, 22) — *mizraqot*.
- **"pommels" → "globes"** (v12, 13) — *gullot*; rounded capital-tops.
- **"wreaths" → "lattices"** (v12, 13) — *sevakot*; interlaced work.
- **"fleshhooks" → "forks"** (v16) — *mizlegot*.
- **"Zeredathah" → "Zeredah"** (v17) — 1 Kgs 7:46 has Zarethan; Chronicler's variant.
- **"temple... most holy place... temple" → "nave... most holy place... nave"** (v22) — *hekal* vs *qodesh qodashim*.
- **"on the right side of the east end, over against the south" → "on the south side of the east end, facing the south"** (v10).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 7** — Chronicler abridged. Chronicler's figures sometimes differ: 10 basins (Chron), 10 lavers (Kgs) matches; ten lampstands and ten tables (Chron), matching 1 Kgs 7:49.
- **Great bronze altar** (v1) — 20×20×10 cubits. Not in 1 Kgs 7's inventory; Chronicler supplies.
- **"Sea"** — enormous laver (~45,000 L if 3,000 *bat*). Priestly washing basin; symbolizes cosmic sea beneath firmament.
- **Twelve oxen facing outward** — cardinal-direction symbolism; echoes tribal arrangement (Num 2). Ahaz later removes them (2 Kgs 16:17).
- **Huram-abi** (v16) — master craftsman; here "Huram his father" = "Huram-abi" proper name.
- **Plain of Jordan casting** — Succoth-Zarethan clay good for bronze-molding; east of Jordan, away from Jerusalem for industrial work.
- **Golden altar** (v19) — altar of incense; bronze altar (v1) is for burnt offering.
