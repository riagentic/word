# 2 Chronicles 4

[1] And he made an altar of bronze: twenty cubits its length and twenty cubits its width and ten cubits its height.

[2] And he made the sea, cast: ten cubits from its lip to its lip, round all around, and five cubits its height; and a line of thirty cubits would go around it.

[3] And the likeness of cattle was under it, all around, going around it, ten by the cubit, circling the sea around; two rows was the herd, cast in its casting.

[4] It was standing on twelve of the herd: three facing north and three facing west and three facing south and three facing east; and the sea was upon them above, and all their hinder parts were inward.

[5] And its thickness was a handbreadth, and its lip was like the work of the lip of a cup, a lily flower; taking in baths, three thousand it would hold.

[6] And he made ten basins, and he placed five on the right and five on the left, to wash in them; the work of the burnt offering they would rinse off in them; and the sea was for the priests to wash in it.

[7] And he made the lampstands of gold, ten, according to their ordinance, and he placed them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left.

[8] And he made ten tables and set them in the temple, five on the right and five on the left; and he made a hundred bowls of gold.

[9] And he made the court of the priests, and the great enclosure, and doors for the enclosure, and their doors he overlaid with bronze.

[10] And the sea he placed at the right shoulder, eastward, over against the south.

[11] And ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ made the pots and the shovels and the bowls. And ⟦huram|Churam|Huram⟧ finished doing the work that he did for King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧:

[12] two pillars, and the globes and the capitals on the top of the pillars, two; and the lattices, two, to cover the two globes of the capitals that were on the top of the pillars;

[13] and the pomegranates, four hundred, for the two lattices, two rows of pomegranates for the one lattice, to cover the two globes of the capitals that were on the face of the pillars;

[14] and the bases he made, and the basins he made on the bases;

[15] the sea, one, and the twelve of the herd under it;

[16] and the pots and the shovels and the forks and all their utensils ⟦huram-abi|Churam-avi|Huram-abi⟧ made for King ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, of scoured bronze.

[17] In the circle of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ the king cast them, in the thickness of the ground, between ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧ and ⟦zeredah|Tzeredah|Zeredah⟧.

[18] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made all these utensils in very great abundance, for the weight of the bronze was not examined.

[19] And ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ made all the utensils that were in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the altar of gold, and the tables, and on them the bread of the face;

[20] and the lampstands and their lamps, to kindle them according to the ordinance before the inner room, of refined gold;

[21] and the flower and the lamps and the tongs, of gold: it was the perfection of gold;

[22] and the snuffers and the bowls and the pans and the firepans, of refined gold; and the entrance of the house, its inner doors for the Holy of Holies and the doors of the house for the temple, of gold.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v16) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v11, 19) — Hebrew *Elohim*, both times with the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (throughout) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת.
- **"twenty cubits the length thereof" → "twenty cubits its length"** (v1) — the Hebrew word order; "breadth" → "width" (רֹחַב). 1 Kgs 7 has no altar of bronze in its list.
- **"a molten sea of ten cubits from brim to brim, round in compass" → "the sea, cast: ten cubits from its lip to its lip, round all around"** (v2) — Hebrew הַיָּם מוּצָק … מִשְּׂפָתוֹ אֶל־שְׂפָתוֹ עָגוֹל סָבִיב; שָׂפָה is "lip". The verse is the same as 1 Kgs 7:23, word for word (only the preposition "to" is another word, with the same sense), and has its wording. **"did compass it" → "would go around it"** — Hebrew יָסֹב.
- **"under it was the similitude of oxen, which did compass it round about" → "the likeness of cattle was under it, all around, going around it"** (v3) — Hebrew וּדְמוּת בְּקָרִים תַּחַת לוֹ סָבִיב סָבִיב: דְּמוּת "likeness", בְּקָרִים the plural of בָּקָר "herd"; "around" is written twice. 1 Kgs 7:24 has "gourds under its lip" (פְּקָעִים) where Chronicles has "the likeness of cattle under it"; the rest of the verse is the same and has the wording of Kings. **"Two rows of oxen were cast, when it was cast" → "two rows was the herd, cast in its casting"** — Hebrew הַבָּקָר, the collective singular, as v4; בְּמֻצַקְתּוֹ "in its casting" is another form of the word in Kings.
- **"It stood upon twelve oxen" → "It was standing on twelve of the herd"** (v4) — Hebrew בָּקָר "herd, cattle", a collective; the verse is the same as 1 Kgs 7:25, word for word. **"three looking toward the north" → "three facing north"** — Hebrew פֹנִים. The four directions are literally "northward, seaward, toward the Negev, toward the sunrise". **"hinder parts"** — Hebrew אֲחֹרֵיהֶם.
- **"the brim of it like the work of the brim of a cup, with flowers of lilies" → "its lip was like the work of the lip of a cup, a lily flower"** (v5) — as 1 Kgs 7:26; פֶּרַח is singular. **"it received and held three thousand baths" → "taking in baths, three thousand it would hold"** — Hebrew מַחֲזִיק בַּתִּים שְׁלֹשֶׁת אֲלָפִים יָכִיל: two verbs, מַחֲזִיק (literally "holding fast") and יָכִיל "would hold". 1 Kgs 7:26 has "two thousand baths it would hold" and not the first verb.
- **"lavers" → "basins"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew כִּיּוֹר. **"put five on the right hand" → "placed five on the right"** — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן, literally "gave"; the Hebrew has no "hand". **"such things as they offered for the burnt offering they washed in them" → "the work of the burnt offering they would rinse off in them"** — Hebrew אֶת־מַעֲשֵׂה הָעוֹלָה יָדִיחוּ בָם: מַעֲשֶׂה is "work, what is made" (what was made ready for the burnt offering); הֵדִיחַ is another verb than רָחַץ "wash" in the same verse (and than שָׁטַף "rinse", Lev 6:28). 1 Kgs 7:38 gives the size of the basins and not their use.
- **"candlesticks" → "lampstands"** (v7, 20) — Hebrew מְנֹרוֹת. **"according to their form" → "according to their ordinance"** — Hebrew כְּמִשְׁפָּטָם: מִשְׁפָּט, "judgment", here the rule laid down for them.
- **"placed them in the temple" → "set them in the temple"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיַּנַּח, another verb than "placed" (נָתַן) in v6, 7, 10. **"basons" → "bowls"** (v8, 11, 22) — Hebrew מִזְרָק, the bowl for dashing the blood.
- **"the great court" → "the great enclosure"** (v9) — Hebrew הָעֲזָרָה, a late word, beside חָצֵר "court" in the same verse. **"overlaid the doors of them" → "their doors he overlaid"** — the suffix is plural.
- **"he set the sea on the right side of the east end, over against the south" → "the sea he placed at the right shoulder, eastward, over against the south"** (v10) — Hebrew מִכֶּתֶף הַיְמָנִית קֵדְמָה מִמּוּל נֶגְבָּה, as 1 Kgs 7:39b, which has "the right shoulder of the house". "Right" is kept (it is the south side, as the verse itself says); קֵדְמָה is literally "toward the front".
- **"Huram"** (v11) — the name is written twice in the verse: first חוּרָם "Huram"; the second time the written text (ketiv) has חירם "Hiram" and the reading tradition (qere) חוּרָם "Huram". The qere is translated. **"the pots"** — Hebrew הַסִּירוֹת; 1 Kgs 7:40 has הַכִּיֹּרוֹת "the basins". **"finished the work that he was to make" → "finished doing the work that he did"** — Hebrew וַיְכַל … לַעֲשׂוֹת אֶת־הַמְּלָאכָה אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה; 1 Kgs 7:40 has "all the work" and "in the house of Yahweh".
- **"the pommels, and the chapiters which were on the top of the two pillars" → "the globes and the capitals on the top of the pillars, two"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהַגֻּלּוֹת וְהַכֹּתָרוֹת עַל־רֹאשׁ הָעַמּוּדִים שְׁתָּיִם; 1 Kgs 7:41 has "the globes of the capitals that were on the top of the pillars". גֻּלָּה "globe" is uncertain (a bowl-shaped or rounded part). **"wreaths" → "lattices"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew שְׂבָכוֹת.
- **"upon the pillars" → "on the face of the pillars"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי; the verse is the same as 1 Kgs 7:42, word for word.
- **"He made also bases, and lavers made he upon the bases" → "and the bases he made, and the basins he made on the bases"** (v14) — the Masoretic Text has עָשָׂה "he made" twice; 1 Kgs 7:43 has עֶשֶׂר / עֲשָׂרָה "ten" in the same places (the words differ by one letter). The Masoretic Text of Chronicles is translated.
- **"One sea, and twelve oxen under it" → "the sea, one, and the twelve of the herd under it"** (v15) — the nouns are definite in the Hebrew; 1 Kgs 7:44 has "under the sea".
- **"fleshhooks" → "forks"** (v16) — Hebrew הַמִּזְלָגוֹת; 1 Kgs 7:45 has "the bowls". **"all their instruments" → "all their utensils"** — Hebrew כְּלֵיהֶם. **"did Huram his father make" → "Huram-abi made"** — Hebrew חוּרָם אָבִיו, literally "Huram his father" (see 2:13: "father" as a title of a master, or part of the name; the existing name form is kept). **"bright brass" → "scoured bronze"** — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת מָרוּק, from מָרַק "scour" (Lev 6:28); 1 Kgs 7:45 has מְמֹרָט "polished".
- **"In the plain of Jordan" → "In the circle of the Jordan"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּכַּר הַיַּרְדֵּן, the round district of the lower Jordan. **"in the clay ground" → "in the thickness of the ground"** — Hebrew בַּעֲבִי הָאֲדָמָה, the word "thickness" of v5 (1 Kgs 7:46 has another form of it); the heavy, clayey soil is meant. **"Zeredathah" → "Zeredah"** — Hebrew צְרֵדָתָה; 1 Kgs 7:46 has "Zarethan". The rest of the verse is the same as in Kings.
- **"Solomon made all these vessels in great abundance" → "Solomon made all these utensils in very great abundance"** (v18) — Hebrew לָרֹב מְאֹד. 1 Kgs 7:47 has "Solomon left all the utensils". **"could not be found out" → "was not examined"** — Hebrew לֹא נֶחְקַר, as 1 Kgs 7:47.
- **"the golden altar also, and the tables whereon the shewbread was set" → "and the altar of gold, and the tables, and on them the bread of the face"** (v19) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים, "bread of the face": bread set before the face of Yahweh ("showbread", "bread of the Presence"). 1 Kgs 7:48 has one table, "of gold".
- **"that they should burn after the manner before the oracle, of pure gold" → "to kindle them according to the ordinance before the inner room, of refined gold"** (v20) — Hebrew לְבַעֲרָם כַּמִּשְׁפָּט; דְּבִיר is the "inner room"; זָהָב סָגוּר is literally "shut-up gold" (the sense is uncertain), as 1 Kgs 7:49.
- **"the flowers" → "the flower"** (v21) — singular in the Hebrew, as 1 Kgs 7:49. **"and that perfect gold" → "it was the perfection of gold"** — Hebrew הוּא מִכְלוֹת זָהָב; מִכְלוֹת is found only here (from כָּלָה "be complete") and its meaning is not certain. The words are not in Kings.
- **"basons, and the spoons, and the censers" → "the bowls and the pans and the firepans"** (v22) — Hebrew מִזְרָקוֹת, כַּפּוֹת (literally "palms"), מַחְתּוֹת. 1 Kgs 7:50 begins with "the basins" (הַסִּפּוֹת), which Chronicles does not have. **"the entry of the house, the inner doors thereof for the most holy place" → "the entrance of the house, its inner doors for the Holy of Holies"** — Hebrew וּפֶתַח הַבַּיִת; 1 Kgs 7:50 has וְהַפֹּתוֹת "and the hinges" (the words differ by one letter). **"the doors of the house of the temple" → "the doors of the house for the temple"** — Hebrew לַהֵיכָל.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 7:23–26, 38–50** — where the Hebrew is the same as in Kings, word for word, the wording is the same (v2, 4, 5a, 13, 17); where Chronicles has another word, form or number, the translation shows it (v3, 5b, 10–12, 14–16, 18–22).
- **The altar of bronze** (v1) — twenty by twenty by ten cubits; it is not in the list of 1 Kgs 7 (but see 1 Kgs 8:64).
- **The sea** — a very large basin; three thousand baths here, two thousand in 1 Kgs 7:26. Its use is given only here (v6).
- **Cattle and gourds** (v3) — Chronicles has "the likeness of cattle" where Kings has "gourds"; the two Hebrew words (בְּקָרִים, פְּקָעִים) look alike.
- **Huram-abi** (v16) — the craftsman of 2:13–14.
- **The circle of the Jordan** (v17) — the casting was done in the Jordan valley, between Succoth and Zeredah.
- **The altar of gold** (v19) — the altar of incense; the altar of bronze (v1) is for the burnt offering.
