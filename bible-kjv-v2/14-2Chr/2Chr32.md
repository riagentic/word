# 2 Chronicles 32

[1] After these things and this truth, ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ came; and he came into ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and camped against the fortified cities, and he meant to split them open for himself.

[2] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ saw that ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ had come and that his face was for war against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[3] and he took counsel with his princes and his mighty ones to stop up the waters of the springs that were outside the city; and they helped him.

[4] And a numerous people were gathered, and they stopped up all the springs and the wadi that was streaming in the midst of the land, saying, "Why should the kings of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ come and find many waters?"

[5] And he strengthened himself and built all the wall that was breached, and he went up upon the towers, and outside was the other wall; and he strengthened the Millo, the city of David, and made missiles in abundance, and shields.

[6] And he set commanders of war over the people and gathered them to himself, to the square of the gate of the city, and spoke to their heart, saying,

[7] "Be strong and be firm; do not fear and do not be dismayed because of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and because of all the multitude that is with him, for more is with us than with him.

[8] With him is an arm of flesh, and with us is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to help us and to fight our wars." And the people leaned on the words of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[9] After this ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ sent his servants to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ — and he was against Lachish, and all his rule with him — to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ that was in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying,

[10] "Thus says ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧: On what are you trusting, and dwelling in siege in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?

[11] Is not ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ inciting you, to give you to die by famine and by thirst, saying, '⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will deliver us from the palm of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧'?

[12] Is it not he, ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, who has removed his high places and his altars, and he said to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying, 'Before one altar you shall bow down, and upon it you shall make smoke'?

[13] Do you not know what I have done, I and my fathers, to all the peoples of the lands? Were the gods of the nations of the lands surely able to deliver their land from my hand?

[14] Who among all the gods of these nations that my fathers devoted to destruction was able to deliver his people from my hand, that your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ should be able to deliver you from my hand?

[15] And now, let not ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ deceive you, and let him not incite you like this, and do not believe him; for no ⟦eloah|eloah|god⟧ of any nation or kingdom is able to deliver his people from my hand and from the hand of my fathers. How much less will your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ deliver you from my hand!"

[16] And his servants spoke yet more against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and against ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ his servant.

[17] And he wrote letters to reproach ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and to say against him, saying, "Like the gods of the nations of the lands, who did not deliver their people from my hand, so the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ will not deliver his people from my hand."

[18] And they called with a loud voice in ⟦judahite|Yehudit|the language of Judah⟧ to the people of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ who were on the wall, to frighten them and to dismay them, so that they might capture the city.

[19] And they spoke of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ as of the gods of the peoples of the earth, the work of the hands of man.

[20] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ the king and ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧, the prophet, prayed concerning this and cried out to the heavens.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sent an angel, and he annihilated every mighty one of ability and leader and commander in the camp of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧. And he returned with shame of face to his land; and he came into the house of his god, and some of those who came out of his inward parts made him fall there by the sword.

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saved ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from the hand of ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and from the hand of all, and he guided them on every side.

[23] And many were bringing an offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and precious things to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; and he was lifted up before the eyes of all the nations after that.

[24] In those days ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ became sick, even to the point of death; and he prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he said to him, and he gave him a wonder.

[25] But ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ did not return according to what was dealt to him, for his heart was high; and there was wrath upon him and upon ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[26] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ brought himself low in the height of his heart, he and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and the wrath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did not come upon them in the days of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧.

[27] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ had riches and glory, very much; and he made for himself treasuries for silver and for gold and for precious stone and for spices and for shields and for all desirable utensils,

[28] and storehouses for the produce of grain and new wine and fresh oil, and stalls for each kind of beast, and droves for the stalls.

[29] And he made cities for himself, and livestock of flock and herd in abundance, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had given him very many goods.

[30] And he, ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, stopped up the upper going out of the waters of ⟦gihon|Gichon|Gihon⟧ and directed them straight down, to the west of the city of David. And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ prospered in all his work.

[31] And so, with the interpreters of the princes of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, who sent to him to seek the wonder that had been in the land, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ forsook him, to test him, to know all that was in his heart.

[32] And the rest of the acts of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, and his ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|acts of steadfast love⟧, behold, they are written in the vision of ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧, the prophet, in the book of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[33] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ lay with his fathers, and they buried him in the ascent of the graves of the sons of David, and all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ did him glory at his death. And ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v16 and v31 the Hebrew has the article (הָאֱלֹהִים), which the token does not show.
- **"the establishment thereof" → "this truth"** (v1) — הָאֱמֶת, the word of 31:20 ("the truth"); its sense here is faithfulness, the faithful acts just told. דְּבָרִים "things" is the word "words / matters".
- **"thought to win them for himself" → "meant to split them open for himself"** (v1) — וַיֹּאמֶר לְבִקְעָם אֵלָיו, literally "and he said to split them open to him"; בָּקַע is "split" (of a city broken into, 2 Kgs 25:4). 2 Kgs 18:13 says that he "seized them"; Chronicles says only what he meant.
- **"that he was purposed to fight against Jerusalem" → "that his face was for war against Jerusalem"** (v2) — וּפָנָיו לַמִּלְחָמָה, the Hebrew way of speaking kept.
- **"his mighty men" → "his mighty ones"** (v3) — גִּבֹּרָיו; "princes" is שָׂרָיו. The same word שַׂר is "commanders" in v6 and v21 (of an army) and "princes" in v31.
- **"fountains" → "springs"** (v3, 4) — two Hebrew words, עֲיָנוֹת (v3) and מַעְיָנוֹת (v4), both "springs".
- **"much people" → "a numerous people"** (v4) — עַם־רָב.
- **"the brook that ran through the midst of the land" → "the wadi that was streaming in the midst of the land"** (v4) — נַחַל is a stream bed; הַשּׁוֹטֵף is literally "overflowing, flooding".
- **"much water" → "many waters"** (v4) — מַיִם רַבִּים.
- **"raised it up to the towers" → "he went up upon the towers"** (v5) — וַיַּעַל עַל־הַמִּגְדָּלוֹת as printed, with no object; the form can also be read "and he brought up upon the towers" (so the KJV, supplying "it"). It is commonly corrected to "and raised towers upon it"; the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"and another wall without" → "and outside was the other wall"** (v5) — וְלַחוּצָה הַחוֹמָה אַחֶרֶת, literally "and to the outside the wall, another"; "was" is supplied.
- **"repaired Millo in the city of David" → "strengthened the Millo, the city of David"** (v5) — וַיְחַזֵּק, the same root as "strengthened himself" (וַיִּתְחַזַּק) at the head of the verse; the Hebrew has no "in": "the city of David" stands beside "the Millo".
- **"darts" → "missiles"** (v5) — שֶׁלַח, a singular used of the whole, as 23:10: a weapon that is thrown, or any weapon (uncertain).
- **"captains of war" → "commanders of war"** (v6) — שָׂרֵי מִלְחָמוֹת, literally "of wars".
- **"in the street of the gate" → "to the square of the gate"** (v6) — רְחוֹב is the broad open place of a town; אֶל is "to".
- **"spake comfortably to them" → "spoke to their heart"** (v6) — וַיְדַבֵּר עַל־לְבָבָם, as Gen 50:21; Ruth 2:13.
- **"Be strong and courageous" → "Be strong and be firm"** (v7) — חִזְקוּ וְאִמְצוּ, two verbs for being strong, as Deut 31:6.
- **"for the king … nor for all the multitude" → "because of the king … and because of all the multitude"** (v7) — מִפְּנֵי … וּמִלִּפְנֵי, literally "from the face of … and from before".
- **"there be more with us than with him" → "more is with us than with him"** (v7) — רַב is singular: "more", or "one greater"; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"our battles" → "our wars"** (v8) — מִלְחֲמֹתֵנוּ.
- **"rested themselves upon" → "leaned on"** (v8) — וַיִּסָּמְכוּ, as 2 Kgs 18:21.
- **"(but he himself laid siege against Lachish, and all his power with him,)" → "and he was against Lachish, and all his rule with him"** (v9) — וְהוּא עַל־לָכִישׁ וְכָל־מֶמְשַׁלְתּוֹ עִמּוֹ: the Hebrew has no verb "laid siege"; מֶמְשָׁלָה is "rule, dominion", here of all the might under him. 2 Kgs 18:17 names the men sent and says "from Lachish … with a heavy force".
- **"that ye abide in the siege" → "and dwelling in siege"** (v10) — two participles joined by "and" (בֹּטְחִים וְיֹשְׁבִים); יָשַׁב is "dwell / sit".
- **"persuade" → "inciting"** (v11, 15) — הֵסִית, as 2 Kgs 18:32.
- **"to give over yourselves to die" → "to give you to die"** (v11) — לָתֵת אֶתְכֶם לָמוּת.
- **"out of the hand of the king" → "from the palm of the king"** (v11) — מִכַּף, not יָד "hand" (v13–15, 17, 22), as 2 Kgs 20:6.
- **"Hath not the same Hezekiah taken away" → "Is it not he, Hezekiah, who has removed"** (v12) — הֲלֹא־הוּא יְחִזְקִיָּהוּ הֵסִיר. 2 Kgs 18:22 reads "is it not he whose high places and whose altars Hezekiah has removed" and "Before this altar you shall bow down in Jerusalem"; Chronicles has "one altar" and adds the smoke.
- **"commanded" → "he said … saying"** (v12) — וַיֹּאמֶר … לֵאמֹר.
- **"Ye shall worship" → "you shall bow down"** (v12) — תִּשְׁתַּחֲווּ.
- **"burn incense upon it" → "upon it you shall make smoke"** (v12) — תַּקְטִירוּ without an object: the verb says neither incense nor sacrifice.
- **"all the people of other lands" → "all the peoples of the lands"** (v13); **"the nations of other lands" → "the nations of the lands"** (v17) — "other" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"any ways able" → "surely able"** (v13) — הֲיָכוֹל יָכְלוּ, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "being able were they able"; as 2 Kgs 18:33 "surely delivered".
- **"their lands" → "their land"** (v13) — אַרְצָם is singular.
- **"utterly destroyed" → "devoted to destruction"** (v14) — הֶחֱרִימוּ, as 2 Kgs 19:11.
- **"your God" → "your *Elohim*"** (v14, 15) — the Assyrian speaks of the God of Jerusalem with the same word as of "the gods" of the nations (אֱלֹהֵי). In v15 the verb with אֱלֹהֵיכֶם is plural (לֹא־יַצִּילוּ): "your Elohim will not deliver", or, as he may mean it, "your gods will not deliver".
- **"no god of any nation" → "no *eloah* of any nation"** (v15) — כָּל־אֱלוֹהַּ, the singular word Eloah, here of any god of the nations (small letter); elsewhere in the chapter "gods" is אֱלֹהֵי.
- **"was able" → "is able"** (v15) — לֹא יוּכַל, an imperfect: "is not able" or "could not".
- **"nor persuade you on this manner" → "let him not incite you like this"** (v15) — כָּזֹאת.
- **"his servant Hezekiah" → "Hezekiah his servant"** (v16) — עֶבֶד, of Yahweh's man "servant".
- **"to rail on" → "to reproach"** (v17) — לְחָרֵף, as 2 Kgs 19:4, 16.
- **"to speak against him" → "to say against him"** (v17) — וְלֵאמֹר עָלָיו לֵאמֹר, the verb "say" twice.
- **"cried with a loud voice in the Jews' speech" → "called with a loud voice in Yehudit"** (v18) — וַיִּקְרְאוּ בְקוֹל־גָּדוֹל יְהוּדִית, the words of 2 Kgs 18:28 with the verb in the plural; "loud" is literally "great".
- **"to affright them, and to trouble them" → "to frighten them and to dismay them"** (v18) — לְיָרְאָם וּלְבַהֲלָם.
- **"take the city" → "capture the city"** (v18) — לָכַד.
- **"spake against the God of Jerusalem, as against the gods" → "spoke of the Elohim of Jerusalem as of the gods"** (v19) — אֶל … כְּעַל, literally "to … as upon"; "against" (as עַל in v16, 17) is also possible.
- **"the people of the earth" → "the peoples of the earth"** (v19) — עַמֵּי is plural; הָאָרֶץ may also be "the land".
- **"which were the work of the hands of man"** (v19) — מַעֲשֵׂה יְדֵי הָאָדָם, as 2 Kgs 19:18 (there without the article).
- **"for this cause … prayed and cried to heaven" → "prayed concerning this and cried out to the heavens"** (v20) — עַל־זֹאת; הַשָּׁמָיִם is plural. 2 Kgs 19:15 has only Hezekiah praying "before Yahweh", and gives the prayer.
- **"an angel"** (v21) — מַלְאָךְ, the word "messenger"; Chronicles has no "of Yahweh" (2 Kgs 19:35 "the angel of Yahweh") and no number of the dead.
- **"cut off all the mighty men of valour, and the leaders and captains" → "annihilated every mighty one of ability and leader and commander"** (v21) — וַיַּכְחֵד, literally "made disappear"; the three nouns are singular.
- **"they that came forth of his own bowels slew him" → "some of those who came out of his inward parts made him fall … by the sword"** (v21) — the qere וּמִיצִיאֵי מֵעָיו (the ketiv ומיציאו is another form of the same word); מִן is "some of"; הִפִּילֻהוּ is "made him fall". 2 Kgs 19:37 names the god (Nisroch) and the sons and has "struck him down with the sword".
- **"from the hand of all other" → "from the hand of all"** (v22) — וּמִיַּד־כֹּל; "other" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"guided them on every side"** (v22) — וַיְנַהֲלֵם מִסָּבִיב as printed. It is commonly corrected to "and gave them rest on every side" (as 14:7; 15:15; 20:30; so, from memory, the Septuagint); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"brought gifts" → "were bringing an offering"** (v23) — מְבִיאִים is a participle, מִנְחָה a singular.
- **"presents" → "precious things"** (v23) — מִגְדָּנוֹת, as Gen 24:53.
- **"he was magnified in the sight of all nations from thenceforth" → "he was lifted up before the eyes of all the nations after that"** (v23) — וַיִּנַּשֵּׂא לְעֵינֵי.
- **"was sick to the death" → "became sick, even to the point of death"** (v24) — חָלָה … עַד־לָמוּת, literally "unto dying"; 2 Kgs 20:1 has לָמוּת without עַד ("to the point of death").
- **"he spake unto him, and he gave him a sign" → "he said to him, and he gave him a wonder"** (v24) — וַיֹּאמֶר לוֹ is "said", and what was said is not given; no subject is named in the Hebrew. מוֹפֵת is "wonder"; 2 Kgs 20:8–9 has אוֹת "sign" and tells the whole. Nothing is supplied from Kings.
- **"rendered not again according to the benefit done unto him" → "did not return according to what was dealt to him"** (v25) — וְלֹא־כִגְמֻל עָלָיו הֵשִׁיב, literally "and not according to the dealing upon him did he return"; גְּמוּל is from גָּמַל "deal" and does not itself say "benefit".
- **"his heart was lifted up" → "his heart was high"** (v25); **"for the pride of his heart" → "in the height of his heart"** (v26) — גָּבַהּ לִבּוֹ … בְּגֹבַהּ לִבּוֹ, verb and noun of one root; the preposition is בְּ "in" (= as to, in the matter of).
- **"therefore there was wrath upon him" → "and there was wrath upon him"** (v25) — וַיְהִי, a plain "and"; whose wrath is said only in v26.
- **"Notwithstanding Hezekiah humbled himself" → "And Hezekiah brought himself low"** (v26) — וַיִּכָּנַע, the nifal of כָּנַע "be brought low" (Judg 3:30), of a man before Yahweh "bring oneself low", as 12:6, 7, 12 and 2 Kgs 22:19; set against the "height" of his heart.
- **"so that the wrath … came not" → "and the wrath … did not come"** (v26) — וְלֹא־בָא.
- **"exceeding much riches and honour" → "riches and glory, very much"** (v27) — כָּבוֹד is "glory"; so also in v33 ("did him honour" → "did him glory", כָבוֹד עָשׂוּ־לוֹ).
- **"precious stones" → "precious stone"** (v27) — אֶבֶן יְקָרָה, a singular for the whole.
- **"all manner of pleasant jewels" → "all desirable utensils"** (v27) — כְּלֵי חֶמְדָּה, literally "utensils of desire", as 36:10.
- **"Storehouses" → "storehouses"** (v28) — מִסְכְּנוֹת, the word of the "store cities" (1 Kgs 9:19).
- **"corn, and wine, and oil" → "grain and new wine and fresh oil"** (v28) — דָּגָן וְתִירוֹשׁ וְיִצְהָר.
- **"stalls for all manner of beasts" → "stalls for each kind of beast"** (v28) — לְכָל־בְּהֵמָה וּבְהֵמָה, literally "for every beast and beast".
- **"cotes for flocks" → "droves for the stalls"** (v28) — וַעֲדָרִים לָאֲוֵרוֹת in the order of the Masoretic Text; אֲוֵרוֹת is found only here and is taken as another form of אֻרָוֹת "stalls" (meaning uncertain). The order is commonly turned round ("stalls for the droves").
- **"he provided him cities, and possessions of flocks and herds" → "he made cities for himself, and livestock of flock and herd"** (v29) — עָשָׂה is "made"; עָרִים "cities" as printed.
- **"substance very much" → "very many goods"** (v29) — רְכוּשׁ רַב מְאֹד.
- **"This same Hezekiah also stopped the upper watercourse of Gihon" → "And he, Hezekiah, stopped up the upper going out of the waters of Gihon"** (v30) — מוֹצָא מֵימֵי גִיחוֹן, as 2 Kgs 2:21 "the going out of the waters". 2 Kgs 20:20 speaks of "the pool and the conduit".
- **"brought it straight down to the west side of the city of David" → "directed them straight down, to the west of the city of David"** (v30) — וַיַּישְּׁרֵם לְמַטָּה־מַּעְרָבָה לְעִיר דָּוִיד, literally "made them straight downward, westward, to / of the city of David"; "down westward to the city of David" is also possible.
- **"Howbeit in the business of the ambassadors" → "And so, with the interpreters"** (v31) — וְכֵן is "and so / and thus"; "the business" is not in the Hebrew (בְּ "in, with"); מְלִיצֵי is the word of Gen 42:23 "interpreter", here of spokesmen sent between two courts ("envoys").
- **"to enquire of the wonder that was done in the land" → "to seek the wonder that had been in the land"** (v31) — לִדְרֹשׁ, the verb "seek" of the whole book (= ask after); הָיָה is "was".
- **"God left him, to try him, that he might know" → "Elohim forsook him, to test him, to know"** (v31) — עֲזָבוֹ הָאֱלֹהִים לְנַסּוֹתוֹ לָדַעַת: עָזַב is "forsake", נִסָּה "test"; who is to know is not said. 2 Kgs 20:12–19 tells the visit and has none of this.
- **"his goodness" → "his *chasadim*"** (v32) — וַחֲסָדָיו, the plural of *chesed*: his acts of steadfast love (as 35:26).
- **"and in the book" → "in the book"** (v32) — the Hebrew has no "and" before עַל־סֵפֶר (literally "upon the book"). 2 Kgs 20:20 has "and all his might, and how he made the pool and the conduit", asks "are they not written", and names "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah"; Chronicles has "behold, they are written" and names the vision of Isaiah and "the book of the kings of Judah and Israel". "Acts" is the word "words / matters".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v33) — וַיִּשְׁכַּב, as 2 Kgs 20:21; "reigned in his stead" → "reigned in his place".
- **"in the chiefest of the sepulchres" → "in the ascent of the graves"** (v33) — בְּמַעֲלֵה קִבְרֵי; מַעֲלֶה is a way going up. Kings does not have the burial.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 18:13–20:21 / Isa 36–39** — Chronicles tells in one chapter what Kings tells in three; where its Hebrew is the same, the wording here is that of Kings, and where it differs, the difference is kept (see Changes).
- **The name** — Chronicles writes יְחִזְקִיָּהוּ (Yechizqiyahu) in this chapter, and חִזְקִיָּהוּ once (v15); the token has one form.
- **"Arm of flesh"** (v8) — set against Yahweh as helper; only Chronicles has the speech of v7–8.
- **The waters** (v3–4, 30) — the stopping of the springs and the leading of the waters of Gihon; 2 Kgs 20:20 names "the pool and the conduit".
- **The wonder and the high heart** (v24–26) — Chronicles gives the sickness, the prayer and the answer in one verse and does not say what the wonder was or in what the heart was high.
- **The envoys of Babylon** (v31) — in Chronicles a testing by Elohim; the showing of the treasuries (2 Kgs 20:13) is not told.
