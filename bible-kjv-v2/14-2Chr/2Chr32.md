# 2 Chronicles 32

[1] After these things and this faithfulness, ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ came, entered ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, encamped against the fortified cities, and planned to break into them for himself.

[2] When ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ saw that ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ had come and intended to fight against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[3] he took counsel with his officials and his warriors to stop the waters of the springs outside the city; and they helped him.

[4] Many people were gathered, and they stopped all the springs and the wadi that flowed through the land, saying, "Why should the kings of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ come and find much water?"

[5] ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ set to work resolutely, built up all the wall that was broken, raised towers on it, and another wall outside; he strengthened the Millo in the city of David and made abundance of weapons and shields.

[6] He set military commanders over the people, gathered them to him in the square at the gate of the city, and spoke encouragingly to them, saying:

[7] "Be strong and courageous; do not be afraid or dismayed because of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ or because of all the multitude that is with him; for there are more with us than with him.

[8] With him is an arm of flesh; but with us is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to help us and to fight our battles." The people relied on the words of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[9] After this, ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, while he was besieging Lachish with all his forces, sent his servants to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and to all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ who were at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying,

[10] "Thus says ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧: 'On what are you trusting, that you remain in the siege of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧?

[11] Is not ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ misleading you, to give you over to die by famine and thirst, saying, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will deliver us from the hand of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧"?

[12] Is it not this same ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ who has removed his high places and his altars, commanding ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying, "You shall worship before one altar and burn incense on it"?

[13] Do you not know what I and my fathers have done to all the peoples of other lands? Were the gods of the nations of those lands in any way able to deliver their lands from my hand?

[14] Who among all the gods of those nations whom my fathers utterly destroyed was able to deliver his people from my hand, that your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ should be able to deliver you from my hand?

[15] Now therefore, do not let ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ deceive or mislead you in this way, and do not believe him; for no god of any nation or kingdom was able to deliver his people from my hand or the hand of my fathers. How much less shall your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ deliver you from my hand!'"

[16] His servants spoke still more against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and against his servant ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧.

[17] He also wrote letters to mock ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and speak against him, saying, "As the gods of the nations of other lands did not deliver their people from my hand, so the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ shall not deliver his people from my hand."

[18] Then they called out with a loud voice in ⟦judahite|Yehudit|the language of Judah⟧ to the people of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ who were on the wall, to frighten and terrify them, so that they might take the city.

[19] They spoke of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ as though of the gods of the peoples of the earth — the work of human hands.

[20] Because of this King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ and ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧ prayed and cried out to heaven.

[21] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ sent a Messenger who cut off every valiant warrior, leader, and officer in the camp of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧. So he returned with shame of face to his own land; when he entered the house of his god, those who came from his own body struck him down there with the sword.

[22] Thus ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saved ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from the hand of ⟦sennacherib|Sancheriv|Sennacherib⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ and from the hand of all others, and gave them rest on every side.

[23] Many brought gifts to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and precious things to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧; from then on he was magnified in the sight of all nations.

[24] In those days ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ was sick to the point of death; he prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he spoke to him and gave him a sign.

[25] But ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ did not render gratitude commensurate with the benefit done to him, for his heart was lifted up; therefore wrath came upon him and upon ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[26] Nevertheless ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ humbled himself from the pride of his heart, he and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, so that the wrath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ did not come upon them in the days of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧.

[27] ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ had very great riches and honor; he made for himself treasuries for silver, gold, precious stones, spices, shields, and all kinds of valuable articles;

[28] storehouses also for the produce of grain, new wine, and oil; and stalls for all kinds of cattle, and sheepfolds for the flocks.

[29] He provided for himself cities and possessions of flocks and herds in abundance, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had given him very great substance.

[30] This same ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ closed up the upper outlet of the waters of ⟦gihon|Gichon|Gihon⟧ and directed them down to the west side of the city of David. ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ prospered in all his works.

[31] Nevertheless in the matter of the envoys of the officials of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ who were sent to him to inquire about the sign that had been done in the land, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ left him to test him, to know all that was in his heart.

[32] The rest of the acts of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ and his acts of devotion, behold, they are written in the vision of ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧, in the Book of the Kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[33] ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the ascent of the tombs of the sons of David. All ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ did him honor at his death; ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"establishment thereof" → "this faithfulness"** (v1) — *emet*; here of steadfast conduct.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v21).
- **"Jews' speech" → "Judahite language"** (v18) — *Yehudit*.
- **"goodness" → "acts of devotion"** (v32) — *chasadaw*.
- **"chiefest of the sepulchres" → "ascent of the tombs"** (v33) — *ba-ma'aleh qivrei*; upper section of royal tombs.
- **"Spake comfortably" → "spoke encouragingly"** (v6).
- **"brook" → "wadi"** (v4).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 18-20 / Isa 36-39** — Chronicler compresses three chapters to one, summarizing Sennacherib, illness, Babylonian envoys.
- **"Arm of flesh vs Yahweh"** (v8) — Chronicler's theological summary.
- **Hezekiah's Tunnel** (v30) — historical Siloam Tunnel; archaeologically confirmed. Brought Gihon's water inside Jerusalem for siege defense.
- **Illness + pride** — Chronicler distinctively blames Hezekiah's late pride; repents; wrath deferred.
- **Babylonian embassy** (v31) — Chronicler notes as Yahweh's *test*, not Hezekiah's simple failure. The showing-off of treasures becomes seed of Babylonian aggression centuries later.
- **Royal honor at burial** — reverses Ahaz's dishonorable interment. Hezekiah buried in elevated portion of royal tombs.
