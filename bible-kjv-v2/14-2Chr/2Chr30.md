# 2 Chronicles 30

[1] Hezekiah sent word to all Israel and Judah, and wrote letters also to Ephraim and Manasseh, that they should come to the house of Yahweh at Jerusalem, to keep the Passover to Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel.

[2] The king had taken counsel with his officials and all the assembly in Jerusalem to keep the Passover in the second month —

[3] for they had not been able to keep it at the proper time, because the priests had not sanctified themselves sufficiently, and the people had not gathered to Jerusalem.

[4] The thing seemed right in the eyes of the king and of all the assembly.

[5] So they decreed to make proclamation throughout all Israel, from Beersheba to Dan, that they should come to keep the Passover to Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel at Jerusalem; for they had not for a long time kept it as it was prescribed.

[6] So the runners went with the letters from the king and his officials throughout all Israel and Judah, according to the king's command, saying: "Children of Israel, return to Yahweh the *Elohim* of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, that he may return to the remnant of you who have escaped from the hand of the kings of Assyria.

[7] Do not be like your fathers and your brothers, who acted unfaithfully against Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers, so that he gave them up to desolation, as you see.

[8] Do not now be stiff-necked as your fathers were, but give your hand to Yahweh, and come to his sanctuary, which he has sanctified forever; and serve Yahweh your *Elohim*, that the fierceness of his wrath may turn from you.

[9] For if you return to Yahweh, your brothers and your children will find compassion before their captors, so that they will return to this land; for Yahweh your *Elohim* is gracious and merciful, and will not turn his face from you if you return to him."

[10] So the runners passed from city to city through the country of Ephraim and Manasseh as far as Zebulun; but they laughed them to scorn and mocked them.

[11] Nevertheless, some of Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humbled themselves and came to Jerusalem.

[12] Also in Judah the hand of *Elohim* was on them to give them lev echad — one heart — to do the commandment of the king and of the officials, according to the word of Yahweh.

[13] Many people assembled at Jerusalem to keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread in the second month — a very great assembly.

[14] They arose and took away the altars that were in Jerusalem; all the incense altars they took away and cast into the Wadi Kidron.

[15] Then they killed the Passover lamb on the fourteenth day of the second month. The priests and the Levites were ashamed; they sanctified themselves and brought burnt offerings into the house of Yahweh.

[16] They stood in their place according to their custom, according to the Law of Moses the man of *Elohim*; the priests dashed the blood that they received from the hand of the Levites.

[17] For many in the assembly had not sanctified themselves; therefore the Levites had charge of the slaughter of the Passover for everyone who was not clean, to sanctify them to Yahweh.

[18] For a multitude of the people — many from Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun — had not cleansed themselves, yet they ate the Passover otherwise than as it was prescribed. But Hezekiah prayed for them, saying, "May the good Yahweh pardon every one

[19] who sets his heart to seek *Elohim*, Yahweh the *Elohim* of his fathers, even though not cleansed according to the rule of the sanctuary."

[20] Yahweh listened to Hezekiah and healed the people.

[21] The children of Israel who were present at Jerusalem kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days with great gladness; the Levites and the priests praised Yahweh day by day, singing with loud instruments to Yahweh.

[22] Hezekiah spoke encouragingly to all the Levites who showed good understanding of Yahweh. So they ate throughout the feast seven days, offering peace offerings and making todah — thanksgiving confession — to Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers.

[23] The whole assembly took counsel to keep another seven days; so they kept another seven days with gladness.

[24] For Hezekiah king of Judah gave to the assembly a thousand bulls and seven thousand sheep; the officials gave to the assembly a thousand bulls and ten thousand sheep. A great number of priests sanctified themselves.

[25] All the assembly of Judah, with the priests and the Levites, the whole assembly that came out of Israel, and the sojourners — who came out of the land of Israel and who lived in Judah, rejoiced.

[26] There was great joy in Jerusalem, for since the time of Solomon son of David king of Israel there had been nothing like this in Jerusalem.

[27] Then the Levitical priests arose and blessed the people; their voice was heard, and their prayer came up to his holy dwelling, to heaven.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"posts" → "runners"** (v6, 10) — *ratzim*.
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v2, 6, 12, 24) — *sarim*.
- **"comfortably" → "encouragingly"** (v22) — *al-lev*.
- **"brook" → "Wadi"** (v14).

### Notes
- **Unique to Chronicler** — no parallel in Kings for Hezekiah's Passover.
- **Second-month Passover** — allowed by Num 9:9-11 for those ceremonially unable in first month. Unprecedented national delay.
- **Northern invitation** — following the Assyrian devastation of 722 BC, Hezekiah invites surviving northerners. Chronicler's theology: exile opens repentance-door.
- **Mixed reception** — Ephraim/Manasseh mostly scorn; Asher/Manasseh/Zebulun individuals come.
- **Ritual improvisation** (v18-20) — uncleansed celebrants, Levites performing slaughter (priestly task), Hezekiah's mercy-prayer accepted. Heart-oriented worship over strict ceremony.
- **Extended fourteen-day festival** — matches Solomon's dedication. Chronicler compares Hezekiah's reform to Solomon's temple-dedication.
- **Levitical priests** — Chronicler's fluid term (v27), reflecting post-exilic perspective when priestly lines blurred.
- **Incense altars cast out** (v14) — Ahaz's proliferation undone.
