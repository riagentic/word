## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; with the article in v12, 16, 19.
- **"keep the passover" → "make a Pesach" / "make the Pesach"** (v1, 2, 3, 5) — עָשָׂה פֶּסַח, as Num 9 and 2 Kgs 23:21; the article as the Hebrew has it. **"keep the feast" → "make the Feast"** (v13, 21) — עָשָׂה חַג. פֶּסַח names the animal, the meal and the feast alike; no "lamb" is added (v15, 17, 18). The sense of the verb פָּסַח is not certain ("pass over", "spare" or "protect").
- **"had taken counsel" → "took counsel"** (v2, 23) — נוֹעַץ. **"princes"** (v2, 6, 12, 24) — שָׂרִים.
- **"sufficiently"** (v3) — לְמַדַּי, literally "to what is enough": not in sufficient number, or not enough.
- **"the thing pleased the king" → "the thing was right in the eyes of the king"** (v4) — יָשַׁר בְּעֵינֵי.
- **"they established a decree to make proclamation" → "they made a word stand, to make a voice pass"** (v5) — וַיַּעֲמִידוּ דָבָר לְהַעֲבִיר קוֹל, word for word.
- **"they had not done it of a long time in such sort as it was written" → "not in abundance had they made it as it is written"** (v5) — לָרֹב "in abundance", as in v13, 24: not in great number; "not for a long time" is the other reading.
- **"posts" → "runners"** (v6, 10) — רָצִים. **"from the king" → "from the hand of the king"**.
- **"the remnant of you, that are escaped out of the hand" → "the escape that is left to you from the palm"** (v6) — פְּלֵיטָה "escape", those who have escaped; כַּף "palm".
- **"trespassed" → "acted unfaithfully"** (v7) — מָעַל.
- **"gave them up to desolation" → "made them a thing that appalls"** (v7) — literally "gave them for"; שַׁמָּה, as 29:8.
- **"be ye not stiffnecked" → "do not stiffen your neck"** (v8) — הִקְשָׁה עֹרֶף. **"yield yourselves" → "give the hand"** — תְּנוּ־יָד, the gesture of submission or pledge.
- **"the fierceness of his wrath" → "his burning anger"** (v8) — חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ.
- **"shall find compassion before them that lead them captive, so that they shall come again" → "will be for compassion before their captors, and for returning"** (v9) — לְרַחֲמִים … וְלָשׁוּב, literally. **"if ye turn again" → "when you return"** — literally "in your returning"; שׁוּב runs through v6–9: "return", and of the anger "turn back" (v8).
- **"gracious and merciful" → "gracious and compassionate"** (v9) — חַנּוּן וְרַחוּם; חַנּוּן is the adjective of חָנַן "show favor". **"will not turn away his face" → "will not remove his face"** — הֵסִיר.
- **"laughed them to scorn, and mocked them" → "were laughing at them and deriding them"** (v10) — two participles.
- **"humbled themselves" → "brought themselves low"** (v11) — נִכְנַע, one rendering through Chronicles.
- **"much people" → "a numerous people"**, **"a very great congregation" → "an assembly in very great abundance"** (v13) — קָהָל לָרֹב מְאֹד.
- **"altars for incense" → "altars for making smoke"** (v14) — מְקַטְּרוֹת, found only here; from קִטֵּר "make smoke"; "altars" is supplied.
- **"killed" → "slaughtered"** (v15, 17) — שָׁחַט. **"were ashamed" → "were put to shame"** — נִכְלְמוּ.
- **"after their manner" → "according to their ordinance"** (v16) — מִשְׁפָּט. **"sprinkled the blood, which they received of the hand" → "were dashing the blood from the hand"** — זָרַק; "received" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the passovers" → "the Pesachim"** (v17) — plural in the Hebrew, הַפְּסָחִים, as 35:7–9; "offerings" stands only in the traditional form. **"to sanctify them"** — the Hebrew has no object.
- **"a multitude of the people" → "the greater part of the people"** (v18) — מַרְבִּית.
- **"yet did they eat … But Hezekiah prayed" → "for they ate … for Hezekiah prayed"** (v18) — כִּי both times.
- **"The good LORD pardon every one" → "May Yahweh, the good one, make atonement on behalf of everyone"** (v18–19) — יְכַפֵּר בְּעַד, the verb of atonement (literally "cover"), not סָלַח "pardon". The verse division of the Hebrew, which ends v18 at "on behalf of", is kept.
- **"that prepareth his heart" → "who has established his heart"** (v19) — literally "all his heart he has established"; הֵכִין. **"seek"** — דָּרַשׁ.
- **"though he be not cleansed according to the purification of the sanctuary" → "though not according to the cleanness of the Holy"** (v19) — וְלֹא כְּטָהֳרַת הַקֹּדֶשׁ, no verb.
- **"hearkened to" → "listened to"** (v20) — שָׁמַע אֶל. **"healed"** — רָפָא; what the healing was is not said.
- **"loud instruments" → "instruments of strength"** (v21) — כְלֵי־עֹז.
- **"spake comfortably unto" → "spoke to the heart of"** (v22) — עַל־לֵב. **"that taught the good knowledge of the LORD" → "who had good insight for Yahweh"** — הַמַּשְׂכִּילִים שֵׂכֶל־טוֹב, verb and noun of one root.
- **"they did eat throughout the feast seven days" → "they ate the appointed feast, the seven days"** (v22) — וַיֹּאכְלוּ אֶת־הַמּוֹעֵד, literally "they ate the appointed time".
- **"making confession" → "confessing"** (v22) — מִתְוַדִּים, hitpael of ידה; also read "giving thanks". The noun תּוֹדָה does not stand in the verse.
- **"did give to the congregation" → "lifted up for the assembly"** (v24) — הֵרִים, the verb of תְּרוּמָה "contribution": set apart as a gift. **"sheep" → "of the flock"** — צֹאן.
- **"strangers" → "sojourners"** (v25) — גֵּרִים.
- **"joy" → "gladness"** (v21, 23, 26) — שִׂמְחָה.
- **"the priests the Levites"** (v27) — two nouns side by side. **"his holy dwelling place" → "his holy abode"** — מָעוֹן.

### Notes
- **Only in Chronicles** — Kings has no account of this Pesach.
- **The second month** — Num 9:9–11 allows it for one who is unclean or on a far way.
- **The north** — the runners go as far as Zebulun; most deride them, some from Asher, Manasseh and Zebulun come.
- **v18–20** — many eat without having cleansed themselves; Hezekiah prays, and Yahweh listens.
- **Fourteen days** — as at Solomon's dedication (7:8–9); v26 names Solomon.
- **The altars** (v14) — those of Ahaz (28:24).
