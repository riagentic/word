# 2 Chronicles 29

[1] ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ began to reign when he was twenty-five years old, and he reigned twenty-nine years in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. His mother's name was ⟦abiah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ daughter of ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧.

[2] He did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to all that David his father had done.

[3] In the first year of his reign, in the first month, he opened the doors of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and repaired them.

[4] He brought in the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, gathered them in the square on the east,

[6] For our fathers have acted unfaithfully, done what was evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and forsaken him. They turned their faces from the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and turned their backs.

[7] They also shut up the doors of the portico, put out the lamps, and did not burn incense or offer burnt offerings in the holy place to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] Therefore the wrath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; he has given them over to terror, astonishment, and hissing, as you see with your eyes.

[9] Our fathers have fallen by the sword, and our sons, our daughters, and our wives are in captivity for this.

[10] Now it is in my heart to make a covenant with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that his fierce wrath may turn away from us.

[13] of the sons of ⟦elizaphan|Elitzafan|Elizaphan⟧: Shimri and ⟦jeiel|Yeiel|Jeiel⟧; of the sons of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧: ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ and ⟦mattaniah|Mattanyah|Mattaniah⟧;

[23] Then they brought the male goats for the sin offering before the king and the assembly, and they laid their hands on them.

[26] The ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ stood with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.

[27] ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ commanded that the burnt offering be offered on the altar. When the burnt offering began, the song to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ also began, with the trumpets and with the instruments of David king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[29] When they had finished offering, the king and all who were present with him bowed and worshiped.

[30] Moreover King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ and the officials commanded the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ to sing praise to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with the words of David and of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧ the seer. They sang praises with gladness, bowed their heads, and worshiped.

[32] The number of the burnt offerings the assembly brought was seventy bulls, one hundred rams, and two hundred lambs — all these for a burnt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[33] The consecrated things were six hundred oxen and three thousand sheep.

[34] But the priests were too few, so that they could not flay all the burnt offerings; therefore their brothers the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ helped them until the work was finished and until the other priests had sanctified themselves — for the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ had been more upright in heart to sanctify themselves than the priests.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"brook Kidron" → "Wadi Kidron"** (v16).
- **"shewbread table" → "table of the bread of the Presence"** (v18).
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v25); **"harps" → "lyres"** (v25).
- **"reconciliation" → "sin offering / atonement"** (v24) — *chitte'u*.

### Notes
- **Unique to Chronicler** — Hezekiah's temple-rededication occupies three chapters (29-31). Kings summarizes briefly.
- **First month of first year** — immediate action after accession. Contrast with Ahaz's shut doors.
- **Levitical priority** (v34) — Levites more faithful in self-sanctification than priests. Chronicler's noteworthy critique of the priestly class's laxity.
- **Covenantal restoration** — temple-reopening with sin offering for kingdom, sanctuary, nation. Corporate atonement.
- **Musical restoration** — David-Gad-Nathan ordinance revived. Chronicler's emphasis on musical worship.
- **"Prepared the people" (v36)** — revival is *Elohim*'s preparatory work.
- **Sixteenth day of first month** — overlaps Passover timing (ch 30 extends feast to second month).
