# 2 Chronicles 29

[1] ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ began to reign when he was twenty-five years old, and twenty-nine years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦abiah|Aviyah|Abijah⟧ the daughter of ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧.

[2] And he did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to all that David his father had done.

[3] He, in the first year of his reign, in the first month, opened the doors of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and repaired them.

[4] And he brought the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and gathered them to the square of the east.

[5] And he said to them, "Hear me, ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧! Now sanctify yourselves and sanctify the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of your fathers, and bring out the impurity from the Holy.

[6] For our fathers acted unfaithfully and did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and forsook him, and they turned their faces away from the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and gave the neck.

[7] Also they shut the doors of the porch and quenched the lamps, and incense they did not burn, and a burnt offering they did not offer up in the Holy to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[8] And the wrath of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was upon ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and he made them a horror, a thing that appalls and a hissing, as you are seeing with your eyes.

[9] And behold, our fathers have fallen by the sword, and our sons and our daughters and our wives are in captivity on account of this.

[10] Now it is with my heart to cut a covenant to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that his burning anger may turn back from us.

[11] My sons, now do not be negligent, for you has ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ chosen to stand before him, to minister to him, and to be to him ministers and makers of smoke."

[12] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ rose up: ⟦mahath|Machat|Mahath⟧ the son of Amasai and ⟦joel|Yoel|Joel⟧ the son of ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧, from the sons of the ⟦kohathites|Qehati|Kohathites⟧; and from the sons of Merari, ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧ the son of ⟦abdi|Avdi|Abdi⟧ and ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the son of ⟦jehallelel|Yehallelel|Jehallelel⟧; and from the ⟦gershonites|Gershunni|Gershonites⟧, ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧ the son of Zimmah and Eden the son of ⟦joah|Yoach|Joah⟧;

[13] and from the sons of ⟦elizaphan|Elitzafan|Elizaphan⟧, Shimri and ⟦jeiel|Yeiel|Jeiel⟧; and from the sons of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧, ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ and ⟦mattaniah|Mattanyah|Mattaniah⟧;

[14] and from the sons of Heman, ⟦jehiel|Yechiel|Jehiel⟧ and ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧; and from the sons of ⟦jeduthun|Yedutun|Jeduthun⟧, ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ and Uzziel.

[15] And they gathered their brothers and sanctified themselves and came, according to the commandment of the king by the words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to cleanse the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] And the priests came to the inside of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to cleanse, and they brought out all the uncleanness that they found in the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to the court of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ received it, to bring it out to the wadi ⟦kidron|Qidron|Kidron⟧, outside.

[17] And they began on the first of the first month to sanctify, and on the eighth day of the month they came to the porch of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they sanctified the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in eight days, and on the sixteenth day of the first month they finished.

[18] And they came inside to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ the king and said, "We have cleansed all the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the altar of the burnt offering and all its utensils and the table of the arrangement and all its utensils.

[19] And all the utensils that King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ cast off in his reign, in his unfaithfulness, we have prepared and sanctified, and behold, they are before the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[20] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ the king rose early and gathered the princes of the city and went up to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[21] And they brought seven bulls and seven rams and seven lambs and seven he-goats of the goats for a sin offering, for the kingdom and for the sanctuary and for ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and he said to the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, the priests, to offer them up on the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] And they slaughtered the herd, and the priests received the blood and dashed it against the altar; and they slaughtered the rams and dashed the blood against the altar; and they slaughtered the lambs and dashed the blood against the altar.

[23] And they brought forward the male goats of the sin offering before the king and the assembly, and they laid their hands on them.

[24] And the priests slaughtered them and offered their blood as a sin offering on the altar, to make atonement for all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, for the king had said, "For all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ the burnt offering and the sin offering."

[25] And he made the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ stand in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with cymbals, with harps and with lyres, by the commandment of David and of Gad the seer of the king and of ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ the prophet, for by the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was the commandment, by the hand of his prophets.

[26] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ stood with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.

[27] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said to offer up the burnt offering on the altar. And at the time the burnt offering began, the song of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ began, and the trumpets, and at the hands of the instruments of David king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[28] And all the assembly were bowing down, and the song was being sung, and the trumpets were sounding, all of it until the burnt offering was finished.

[29] And when they finished offering up, the king and all who were found with him stooped and bowed down.

[30] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ the king and the princes said to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ to praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with the words of David and of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧ the seer, and they praised unto gladness, and they bowed the head and bowed down.

[31] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ answered and said, "Now you have filled your hand for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; draw near and bring sacrifices and thanksgivings to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And the assembly brought sacrifices and thanksgivings, and everyone willing of heart, burnt offerings.

[32] And the number of the burnt offering that the assembly brought was seventy of the herd, a hundred rams, two hundred lambs; for a burnt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ were all these.

[33] And the holy things were six hundred of the herd and three thousand of the flock.

[34] Only, the priests were too few and were not able to flay all the burnt offerings, and their brothers the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ strengthened them until the work was finished and until the priests sanctified themselves, for the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ were more upright of heart to sanctify themselves than the priests.

[35] And also the burnt offering was in abundance, with the fat of the peace offerings and with the drink offerings for the burnt offering. And the service of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was established.

[36] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ rejoiced, and all the people, over what ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had prepared for the people, for the thing came to be suddenly.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v36 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"Hezekiah"** — written יְחִזְקִיָּהוּ, Yechizqiyahu, in v1, 20, 30, 31, 36 and חִזְקִיָּהוּ in v18, 27; one token.
- **"began to reign when he was five and twenty years old"** (v1) — literally "Hezekiah reigned, a son of twenty and five years": the order differs from 2 Kgs 18:2 and is kept. The mother is אֲבִיָּה "Abijah" here, אֲבִי "Abi" in Kings.
- **"repaired them"** (v3) — חִזֵּק, literally "made them strong".
- **"the east street" → "the square of the east"** (v4) — רְחוֹב, the open place of a town.
- **"carry forth the filthiness out of the holy place" → "bring out the impurity from the Holy"** (v5) — נִדָּה "impurity"; הַקֹּדֶשׁ "the Holy" ("place" is not in the Hebrew; so also v7).
- **"have trespassed" → "acted unfaithfully"** (v6); **"in his transgression" → "in his unfaithfulness"** (v19) — מָעַל / מַעַל, one rendering through Chronicles.
- **"done that which was evil" → "did the evil"** (v6) — הָרַע with the article, as in the formula of Judges and Kings.
- **"turned their backs" → "gave the neck"** (v6) — וַיִּתְּנוּ־עֹרֶף, literally; = turned their backs.
- **"put out the lamps" → "quenched the lamps"** (v7) — כִּבָּה. **"burned incense"** — הִקְטִיר with קְטֹרֶת "incense" as object: one root.
- **"offer up a burnt offering"** (v7, 21, 27, 29) — הֶעֱלָה עֹלָה, literally "make go up a going-up".
- **"delivered them to trouble, to astonishment, and to hissing" → "made them a horror, a thing that appalls and a hissing"** (v8) — literally "gave them for …"; the qere לְזַעֲוָה is translated (the ketiv לזועה is another form of the same word).
- **"fierce wrath" → "burning anger"** (v10) — חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ, literally "the burning of his nose".
- **"it is in mine heart to make a covenant with" → "it is with my heart to cut a covenant to"** (v10) — כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant"; לַיהוָה "to / for Yahweh", not "with".
- **"be not now negligent"** (v11) — תִּשָּׁלוּ, a rare verb ("be at ease, be slack"); the sense is fairly sure.
- **"to serve him, and that ye should minister unto him, and burn incense" → "to minister to him, and to be to him ministers and makers of smoke"** (v11) — שֵׁרֵת twice; מַקְטִרִים has no object, so "make smoke".
- **v13, 14** — the qere "Jeiel" and "Jehiel" are translated (ketiv: Jeuel, Jehuel).
- **"to cleanse"** (v15, 16, 18) — טִהַר, "make clean". **"the inner part" → "the inside"** (v16) — לִפְנִימָה.
- **"took it" → "received it"** (v16), **"received the blood"** (v22) — קִבֵּל, a late verb; elsewhere "accept".
- **"brook Kidron" → "wadi Kidron"** (v16) — נַחַל.
- **"shewbread table" → "the table of the arrangement"** (v18) — שֻׁלְחַן הַמַּעֲרֶכֶת; the word of "the bread of the arrangement".
- **"vessels" → "utensils"** (v18, 19) — כְּלִי; of the singers "instruments" (v26, 27). **"did cast away" → "cast off"** (v19) — הִזְנִיחַ.
- **"rulers" → "princes"** (v20, 30) — שָׂרִים.
- **"bullocks" → "bulls"**, **"he goats" → "he-goats of the goats"** (v21) — צְפִירֵי עִזִּים; צָפִיר is a late word for the he-goat; in v23 the same animals are שְׂעִירֵי הַחַטָּאת "the male goats of the sin offering".
- **"commanded" → "said"** (v21, 27, 30) — אָמַר.
- **"killed the bullocks" → "slaughtered the herd"** (v22) — שָׁחַט; הַבָּקָר "the herd", the cattle (the bulls of v21). **"sprinkled" → "dashed"** — זָרַק, to throw from a bowl; הַמִּזְבֵּחָה "to the altar".
- **"brought forth" → "brought forward"** (v23) — הִגִּישׁ.
- **"made reconciliation with their blood upon the altar" → "offered their blood as a sin offering on the altar"** (v24) — וַיְחַטְּאוּ, the verb of חַטָּאת "sin offering", as Lev 9:15.
- **"make atonement"** (v24) — כִּפֶּר, literally "cover".
- **"the king commanded that the burnt offering … should be made for all Israel" → "the king had said, 'For all Israel the burnt offering and the sin offering'"** (v24) — "for all Israel" stands first in the Hebrew.
- **"psalteries" → "harps"**, **"harps" → "lyres"** (v25) — נֵבֶל, כִּנּוֹר; the kinds are not certain. **"the king's seer"** — חֹזֶה.
- **"of the LORD by his prophets" → "by the hand of Yahweh … by the hand of his prophets"** (v25) — בְּיַד twice.
- **"with the instruments ordained by David" → "at the hands of the instruments of David"** (v27) — וְעַל־יְדֵי, literally "and upon the hands of": led or accompanied by them.
- **"worshipped" → "were bowing down" / "bowed down"** (v28, 29, 30) — הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה.
- **"the singers sang, and the trumpeters sounded" → "the song was being sung, and the trumpets were sounding"** (v28) — הַשִּׁיר מְשׁוֹרֵר, literally "the song singing"; הַחֲצֹצְרוֹת "the trumpets" with the qere מַחְצְרִים "sounding". Both nouns name the thing, not the men.
- **"bowed themselves" → "stooped"** (v29) — כָּרַע. **"bowed their heads" → "bowed the head"** (v30) — קָדַד.
- **"sang praises with gladness" → "praised unto gladness"** (v30) — עַד־לְשִׂמְחָה.
- **"ye have consecrated yourselves" → "you have filled your hand"** (v31) — מִלֵּאתֶם יֶדְכֶם, the expression for installing a priest (Exod 28:41); kept.
- **"thank offerings" → "thanksgivings"** (v31) — תּוֹדוֹת, the one word "thanksgiving", used of the sacrifice (Lev 7:12).
- **"as many as were of a free heart" → "everyone willing of heart"** (v31) — נְדִיב לֵב.
- **"bullocks" → "of the herd"** (v32, 33) — בָּקָר; **"sheep" → "of the flock"** (v33) — צֹאן, sheep and goats.
- **"consecrated things" → "holy things"** (v33) — הַקֳּדָשִׁים.
- **"did help them" → "strengthened them"** (v34) — חִזֵּק, "make strong". **"more upright in heart"** — יִשְׁרֵי לֵבָב.
- **"the fat"** (v35) — plural in the Hebrew, "the fats". **"was set in order" → "was established"** — וַתִּכּוֹן.
- **"that God had prepared the people" → "over what Elohim had prepared for the people"** (v36) — הֵכִין with לָעָם "for the people".
- **"was done suddenly" → "came to be suddenly"** (v36) — בְּפִתְאֹם.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 18:1–3** — v1–2 only; the rest of chapters 29–31 is found only in Chronicles.
- **First month of the first year** — set against the doors that Ahaz shut (28:24).
- **The Levites and the priests** (v34) — the Levites are said to be more upright of heart to sanctify themselves.
- **The sin offering** — for the kingdom, the sanctuary and Judah (v21); the king says "for all Israel" (v24).
- **The song** — by the commandment of David, Gad and Nathan (v25).
- **Sixteenth day of the first month** (v17) — past the day of the Pesach; chapter 30 keeps it in the second month.
