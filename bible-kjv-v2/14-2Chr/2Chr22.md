# 2 Chronicles 22

[1] And the inhabitants of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ made ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ his youngest son king in his place, for the raiding band that came with the ⟦arabians|Arvim|Arabians⟧ to the camp had killed all the first ones. And ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ began to reign.

[2] ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ was forty-two years old when he began to reign, and one year he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ the daughter of Omri.

[3] He also walked in the ways of the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, for his mother was his counselor to do wickedly.

[4] And he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, like the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, for they were his counselors after the death of his father, to his destruction.

And ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went down to see ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ in ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, for he was sick.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v12 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And" kept** (throughout) — the Hebrew וַ / וְ at the head of a clause is translated each time; v3 and v5 begin with גַּם, "also".
- **"all the eldest" → "all the first ones"** (v1) — Hebrew הָרִאשֹׁנִים, "the first": the sons born before him (21:17). **"his youngest son"** — הַקָּטֹן, literally "the small one". **"with the Arabians"** — בָעַרְבִים, "among / with the Arabians". **"reigned" → "began to reign"** — the verb is מָלַךְ, "reigned", here of the beginning, as in 2 Kgs 8:25, which has the same words after a year of accession.
- **"Forty and two years old was Ahaziah" → "Ahaziah was forty-two years old"** (v2) — literally "a son of forty-two years". The Hebrew of Chronicles reads אַרְבָּעִים וּשְׁתַּיִם, forty-two, and the verse keeps it. 2 Kgs 8:26 has twenty-two. Forty-two makes Ahaziah older than his father Jehoram, who was thirty-two when he began to reign and reigned eight years (21:20), so the number is commonly corrected from Kings; the correction is not put into the verse.
- **"daughter of Omri"** (v2) — Hebrew בַּת־עָמְרִי, "daughter of Omri", kept as it stands. Athaliah was the daughter of Ahab or of his house (21:6), so בַּת is commonly understood here as "granddaughter" or "woman of the line of"; the word itself says "daughter". 2 Kgs 8:26 adds "king of Israel"; Chronicles does not.
- **"his counsellor to do wickedly"** (v3) — Hebrew לְהַרְשִׁיעַ, the hifil of the root of רָשָׁע "wicked", here without an object: "to act wickedly". Elsewhere the same form with an object is "condemn". "Ways" is plural (2 Kgs 8:27 "the way").
- **"Wherefore he did evil" → "And he did the evil"** (v4) — the regnal formula as in Kings, with the article. **"to his destruction"** — Hebrew לְמַשְׁחִית לוֹ, literally "for a destroyer / destruction to him", from the verb שָׁחַת "ruin" (Exod 12:13 "to destroy").
- **"He walked also after their counsel" → "Also in their counsel he walked"** (v5) — the Hebrew order kept; "walked" and "went" are the one verb הָלַךְ. **"to war" → "to the battle"** — as 2 Kgs 8:28; Chronicles has עַל "against" where Kings has עִם "with", and "Jehoram … king of Israel" where Kings has "Joram". **"the Syrians smote Joram" → "the Arameans struck Joram"** — Hebrew הָרַמִּים, a short form of הָאֲרַמִּים (2 Kgs 8:28 אֲרַמִּים); the verb is הִכָּה "strike". The form could also be read "the archers"; the meaning "Arameans" is the common one.
- **"because of the wounds which were given him" → "for the wounds with which they had struck him"** (v6) — the Hebrew has כִּי הַמַּכִּים, "for / because the wounds", and the clause has no verb of its own; 2 Kgs 8:29 has מִן־הַמַּכִּים, "from the wounds", and names the Arameans. The text of Chronicles is translated as it stands. מַכִּים is the noun of "strike" ("strokes"). **"at Ramah"** — Hebrew בָּרָמָה, "at the Ramah" ("the height"): Ramoth-gilead. **"Azariah"** — as printed, see Notes. **"because he was sick"** — חֹלֶה, here of a wounded man.
- **"the destruction of Ahaziah was of God by coming to Joram" → "from Elohim was the downfall of Ahaziah, to come to Joram"** (v7) — Hebrew תְּבוּסָה stands only here; it is from בּוּס "trample down", literally "a trampling down"; the meaning is not certain. לָבוֹא is the plain infinitive "to come": that he came to Joram was from Elohim. **"against Jehu" → "to Jehu"** — Hebrew אֶל, "to".
- **"when Jehu was executing judgment upon" → "when Jehu was entering into judgment with"** (v8) — Hebrew כְּהִשָּׁפֵט … עִם, the nifal of "judge" with "with". **"princes"** — שָׂרִים. **"the sons of the brethren of Ahaziah, that ministered" → "the sons of the brothers of Ahaziah, ministering"** — בְּנֵי אֲחֵי, "sons of the brothers" (not "kinsmen"); 2 Kgs 10:13 has "the brothers of Ahaziah". מְשָׁרְתִים "ministering to".
- **"and when they had slain him, they buried him" → "and put him to death. And they buried him"** (v9) — the verbs are plural: "they brought him … and they put him to death" (not Jehu alone). הֵמִית "put to death" is kept apart from הָרַג "kill" (v1, 8). **"sought"** — both בִּקֵּשׁ (he sought Ahaziah) and דָּרַשׁ (Jehoshaphat sought Yahweh) are "seek" in English. **"for he was hid in Samaria" → "and he was hiding himself in Samaria"** — Hebrew וְהוּא מִתְחַבֵּא. **"had no power to keep still the kingdom" → "had no one to retain power for the kingdom"** — literally "and there was not to the house of Ahaziah to retain power for kingdom" (עָצַר כֹּחַ as 1 Chr 29:14; 2 Chr 13:20).
- **"destroyed all the seed royal" → "destroyed all the seed of the kingdom"** (v10) — Chronicles has וַתְּדַבֵּר, which as the common verb would be "and she spoke"; it is taken as a rare verb דבר, "destroy, make away with" (the hifil "subdue" stands in Ps 18:47; 47:3). The meaning is uncertain. 2 Kgs 11:1 has וַתְּאַבֵּד, "did away with", and many correct Chronicles from it; the verse keeps the text of Chronicles. "Of the house of Judah" is in Chronicles only.
- **"Jehoshabeath" — retained** (v11) — =Jehosheba of 2 Kgs 11; Chronicler adds she was *wife of Jehoiada the priest*, making priest-king alliance explicit. **"and put him and his nurse in a bedchamber" → "and she placed him and his nurse in the chamber of the beds"** — the verb is נָתַן, literally "gave"; חֲדַר הַמִּטּוֹת as 2 Kgs 11:2: a bedroom, or a room where bedding was kept. **"that were slain" → "who were being put to death"** — the participle הַמּוּמָתִים. **"hid him from Athaliah, so that she slew him not" → "concealed him from the face of Athaliah, and she did not put him to death"** — הִסְתִּיר "conceal" beside הִתְחַבֵּא "hide oneself" (v9, 12); מִפְּנֵי "from the face of"; Kings has the plural "they concealed him" and the passive "he was not put to death".
- **"he was with them hid in the house of God" → "he was with them in the house of Elohim, hiding himself"** (v12) — Chronicles has "with them" and "the house of Elohim" where 2 Kgs 11:3 has "with her" and "the house of Yahweh".

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 8:25-29, 9:27-28, 11:1-3** — Chronicler compresses Jehu's purge. Where the Hebrew is the same, the wording is that of the Kings verses; where it differs, the difference is kept.
- **Chronicler's age problem** — MT 2 Chr 22:2 says Ahaziah was 42 when he began to reign, older than his father Jehoram (32 at accession + 8 years = 40 at death). Usually emended to 22 following 2 Kgs 8:26. The verse keeps the Hebrew number, forty-two.
- **"Azariah son of Jehoram"** (v6) — MT *Azaryahu*; the king is Ahaziah (v1–2, 7), and 2 Kgs 8:29 has "Ahaziah". MT kept as printed (cf. "Jehoahaz," 21:17).
- **Ahaziah's death** — Chronicler differs from 2 Kgs 9:27-28: Kings has Ahaziah fleeing via Beth-haggan, dying at Megiddo; Chronicles has him found hiding in Samaria. Possibly two phases of the same event.
- **Honorable burial** (v9) — out of respect for Jehoshaphat, his grandfather. Chronicler's unique detail.
- **Jehoshabeath = Jehosheba** — priest's wife; this priest-royal marriage preserves the Davidic line through the Jehoiada-Athaliah confrontation.
- **Six-year hiding** sets up ch 23 coup.
