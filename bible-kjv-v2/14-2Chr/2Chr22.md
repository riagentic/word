# 2 Chronicles 22

[1] The inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah his youngest son king in his place, for the raiding band that came with the Arabians into the camp had killed all the older ones. So Ahaziah son of Jehoram king of Judah reigned.

[2] Ahaziah was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Athaliah the granddaughter of Omri.

[3] He also walked in the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother was his counselor in doing wickedly.

[4] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, like the house of Ahab; for they were his counselors after the death of his father, to his destruction.

[5] He also walked according to their counsel, and went with Jehoram son of Ahab king of Israel to war against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead; the Arameans wounded Joram.

[6] He returned to be healed at Jezreel from the wounds that had been inflicted on him at Ramah when he fought against Hazael king of Aram. Azariah son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to see Jehoram son of Ahab at Jezreel, because he was sick.

[7] The downfall of Ahaziah was from *Elohim*, through his visit to Joram; for when he came, he went out with Jehoram against Jehu son of Nimshi, whom Yahweh had anointed to cut off the house of Ahab.

[8] As Jehu was executing judgment on the house of Ahab, he met the officials of Judah and the kinsmen of Ahaziah's brothers who were serving Ahaziah, and he killed them.

[9] He searched for Ahaziah; they caught him (he was hiding in Samaria) and brought him to Jehu, who killed him. Then they buried him, for they said, "He is the son of Jehoshaphat, who sought Yahweh with all his heart." The house of Ahaziah had no one able to hold the kingdom.

[10] When Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the royal seed of the house of Judah.

[11] But Jehoshabeath the daughter of the king took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him from among the king's sons who were being killed; she put him and his nurse in a bedchamber. So Jehoshabeath daughter of King Jehoram, wife of Jehoiada the priest — for she was the sister of Ahaziah — hid him from Athaliah, so she did not kill him.

[12] He was with them, hidden in the house of *Elohim*, for six years while Athaliah reigned over the land.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"forty-two years old" — retained** (v2) — textually difficult: 2 Kgs 8:26 has 22, which matches context (father Jehoram reigned 8 years, so son must be under 32). Most ancient versions and commentators follow 2 Kgs; "forty-two" likely scribal error but MT preserved here. Rendered as "twenty-two" to reflect the correct age; note preserves the issue.

Correction applied: "twenty-two years old" (following 2 Kgs 8:26 reading, against MT 42 which is age of mother + son not his own).
- **"daughter of Omri" → "granddaughter of Omri"** (v2) — *bat* = descendant.
- **"seed royal" → "royal seed"** (v10).
- **"Jehoshabeath" — retained** (v11) — =Jehosheba of 2 Kgs 11; Chronicler adds she was *wife of Jehoiada the priest*, making priest-king alliance explicit.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 8:25-29, 9:27-28, 11:1-3** — Chronicler compresses Jehu's purge.
- **Chronicler's age problem** — MT 2 Chr 22:2 says Ahaziah was 42 when he began to reign, older than his father Jehoram (32 at accession + 8 years = 40 at death). Usually emended to 22 following 2 Kgs 8:26. I've rendered the emended reading.
- **Ahaziah's death** — Chronicler differs from 2 Kgs 9:27-28: Kings has Ahaziah fleeing via Beth-haggan, dying at Megiddo; Chronicles has him found hiding in Samaria. Possibly two phases of the same event.
- **Honorable burial** (v9) — out of respect for Jehoshaphat, his grandfather. Chronicler's unique detail.
- **Jehoshabeath = Jehosheba** — priest's wife; this priest-royal marriage preserves the Davidic line through the Jehoiada-Athaliah confrontation.
- **Six-year hiding** sets up ch 23 coup.
