## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"forty-two years old" → "twenty-two years old"** (v2) — departs from MT (42), an evident scribal error: it makes Ahaziah older than his father Jehoram (32 at accession + 8 years = 40 at death, 21:20). 2 Kgs 8:26 and the Syriac read 22 (LXX 20).
- **"daughter of Omri" → "granddaughter of Omri"** (v2) — *bat* = descendant.
- **"seed royal" → "royal seed"** (v10).
- **"Jehoshabeath" — retained** (v11) — =Jehosheba of 2 Kgs 11; Chronicler adds she was *wife of Jehoiada the priest*, making priest-king alliance explicit.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 8:25-29, 9:27-28, 11:1-3** — Chronicler compresses Jehu's purge.
- **Chronicler's age problem** — MT 2 Chr 22:2 says Ahaziah was 42 when he began to reign, older than his father Jehoram (32 at accession + 8 years = 40 at death). Usually emended to 22 following 2 Kgs 8:26. I've rendered the emended reading.
- **"Azariah son of Jehoram"** (v6) — MT *Azaryahu*; the king is Ahaziah (v1–2, 7). Many Hebrew mss, LXX, Syriac and Vulgate read "Ahaziah"; MT kept as a name-variant (cf. "Jehoahaz," 21:17).
- **Ahaziah's death** — Chronicler differs from 2 Kgs 9:27-28: Kings has Ahaziah fleeing via Beth-haggan, dying at Megiddo; Chronicles has him found hiding in Samaria. Possibly two phases of the same event.
- **Honorable burial** (v9) — out of respect for Jehoshaphat, his grandfather. Chronicler's unique detail.
- **Jehoshabeath = Jehosheba** — priest's wife; this priest-royal marriage preserves the Davidic line through the Jehoiada-Athaliah confrontation.
- **Six-year hiding** sets up ch 23 coup.
