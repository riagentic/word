# 2 Chronicles 1

[1] Solomon son of David strengthened himself in his kingdom; Yahweh his *Elohim* was with him and magnified him exceedingly.

[2] Solomon spoke to all Israel — to the commanders of thousands and hundreds, to the judges, and to every leader in all Israel, the heads of fathers' houses.

[3] Solomon and all the assembly with him went to the high place at Gibeon; for the Tent of Meeting of *Elohim* was there, which Moses the servant of Yahweh had made in the wilderness.

[4] But the ark of *Elohim* David had brought up from Kiriath-jearim to the place that David had prepared for it, for he had pitched a tent for it in Jerusalem.

[5] Moreover the bronze altar that Bezalel son of Uri son of Hur had made was there before the dwelling of Yahweh; Solomon and the assembly sought it.

[6] Solomon went up there to the bronze altar before Yahweh, which was at the Tent of Meeting, and offered a thousand burnt offerings upon it.

[7] That night *Elohim* appeared to Solomon and said to him, "Ask what I shall give you."

[8] Solomon said to *Elohim*, "You have shown great steadfast love — to David my father, and have made me king in his place.

[9] Now, O Yahweh *Elohim*, let your promise to David my father be confirmed; for you have made me king over a people like the dust of the earth in multitude.

[10] Give me now wisdom and knowledge, that I may go out and come in before this people; for who can judge this your people, so great?"

[11] *Elohim* said to Solomon, "Because this was in your heart — and you have not asked for riches, wealth, honor, the life of your enemies, nor long life, but have asked for wisdom and knowledge for yourself, that you may judge my people over whom I have made you king —

[12] wisdom and knowledge are granted to you; and I will give you riches and wealth and honor such as no kings who were before you had, nor shall any after you have the like."

[13] Then Solomon came from the high place at Gibeon, from before the Tent of Meeting, to Jerusalem; and he reigned over Israel.

[14] Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen: he had 1,400 chariots and 12,000 horsemen, whom he stationed in the chariot cities and with the king at Jerusalem.

[15] The king made silver and gold as common in Jerusalem as stones; cedars he made as plentiful as the sycamores in the Shephelah.

[16] Solomon had horses imported from Egypt and from Kue; the king's traders received them from Kue at a price.

[17] They imported a chariot from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for one hundred fifty; likewise through their hand they were exported to all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Aram.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "Tent of Meeting"** (v3, 6, 13).
- **"tabernacle of the Lord" → "dwelling of Yahweh"** (v5).
- **"brasen" → "bronze"** (v5, 6).
- **"governor" → "leader"** (v2).
- **"linen yarn" → "from Kue"** (v16) — same correction as 1 Kgs 10:28: *miqveh* = Kue (Cilicia), not linen yarn.
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v17).
- **"sycomore trees that are in the vale" → "sycamores in the Shephelah"** (v15).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 3** — Chronicler's version of Solomon's Gibeon visit and wisdom-choice. Omits the dream; presents nighttime appearance without dream-frame.
- **Gibeon** — tabernacle + bronze altar location. Chronicler explains Solomon's unusual out-of-Jerusalem worship: the Mosaic tabernacle hadn't yet moved to the permanent temple.
- **Dual worship during transition** — ark at Zion, tabernacle at Gibeon. Chronicler's explanation for this pre-temple split.
- **Solomon's wisdom request** — identical to 1 Kgs 3 theologically; Chronicler emphasizes judicial application ("go out and come in before this people").
- **Chariot cities, horse trade** — Deut 17:16 forbids multiplying horses and Egyptian imports. Chronicler does not comment critically; emphasizes Solomon's commercial splendor.
- **Kue / Cilicia** — Anatolian horse-breeding region.
- **Chronicler opens Solomon's reign** with divine favor, wisdom, and wealth — omits Adonijah crisis entirely, skipping 1 Kgs 1-2.
