# 2 Chronicles 16

[1] In the thirty-sixth year of the reign of Asa, ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came up against ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and built Ramah, so as not to let anyone go out or come in to Asa king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[2] And Asa brought out silver and gold from the treasuries of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and of the house of the king, and he sent to ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, who dwelt in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, saying,

[3] "There is a covenant between me and you, and between my father and your father. Behold, I have sent you silver and gold. Go, break your covenant with ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that he may go up from me."

[4] And ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ listened to King Asa, and he sent the commanders of the forces that were his to the cities of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they struck ⟦ijon|Iyyon|Ijon⟧ and Dan and ⟦abel-maim|Avel-mayim|Abel-maim⟧ and all the stores of the cities of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧.

[5] And it came to pass, when ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ heard, that he ceased from building Ramah, and he made his work cease.

[6] And Asa the king took all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they carried away the stones of Ramah and its wood with which ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ had built, and he built with them ⟦geba|Geva|Geba⟧ and ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧.

[7] And at that time ⟦hanani|Chanani|Hanani⟧ the seer came to Asa king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and said to him, "Because you leaned on the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and did not lean on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, therefore the force of the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ has escaped from your hand.

[8] Were not the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ and the ⟦libyans|Luvim|Libyans⟧ a force in abundance, with chariots and horsemen exceedingly many? And because you leaned on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he gave them into your hand.

[9] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, his eyes roam to and fro in all the earth, to strengthen himself with those whose heart is whole toward him. You have acted foolishly over this, for from now on there are wars with you."

[10] And Asa was vexed at the seer and put him in the house of the stocks, for he was in a rage with him over this. And Asa crushed some of the people at that time.

[11] And behold, the acts of Asa, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[12] And Asa became sick in the thirty-ninth year of his reign, in his feet, until his sickness was exceedingly great. And also in his sickness he did not seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, but sought by the physicians.

[13] And Asa lay with his fathers, and he died in the forty-first year of his reign.

[14] And they buried him in his graves, which he had dug for himself in the city of David, and they laid him on the bed that he had filled with spices and kinds, blended in a blend of workmanship, and they burned for him an exceedingly great burning.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — every Hebrew clause that opens with וְ / וַ opens with "And"; "Then", "yet", "therefore" of the KJV are not in the Hebrew.
- **"In the six and thirtieth year … to the intent that he might let none go out or come in" → "In the thirty-sixth year … so as not to let anyone go out or come in"** (v1) — from "Baasha" on the Hebrew is that of 1 Kgs 15:17, and so is the wording: לְבִלְתִּי תֵּת יוֹצֵא וָבָא לְאָסָא, literally "so as not to give one going out and coming in to Asa". The date is only in Chronicles and is translated as printed (see Notes). "Ramah" has the article in the Hebrew, "the Height"; so also "the Mizpah" (v6).
- **"out of the treasures of the house of the LORD and of the king's house" → "from the treasuries of the house of Yahweh and of the house of the king"** (v2) — Hebrew אֹצְרוֹת. **"Syria" → "Aram"** — Hebrew *Aram*. **"that dwelt at Damascus" → "who dwelt in Damascus"** — Hebrew יָשַׁב; the name is spelled דַּרְמֶשֶׂק (*Darmeseq*) here, the later form of דַּמֶּשֶׂק.
- **"There is a league between me and thee, as there was between my father and thy father … break thy league … that he may depart from me" → "There is a covenant between me and you, and between my father and your father … break your covenant … that he may go up from me"** (v3) — as 1 Kgs 15:19: Hebrew בְּרִית "covenant"; the first sentence has no verb ("is" is supplied; it can also be heard as a wish, "Let there be"); "as there was" is not in the Hebrew. וְיַעֲלֶה מֵעָלָי, literally "go up from upon me". Chronicles has "and" before the second "between" and has no "bribe" (שֹׁחַד, 1 Kgs 15:19).
- **"hearkened unto king Asa, and sent the captains of his armies against the cities of Israel; and they smote" → "listened to King Asa, and he sent the commanders of the forces that were his to the cities of Israel, and they struck"** (v4) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל; שָׂרֵי הַחֲיָלִים אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ; אֶל "to" (1 Kgs 15:20 has עַל "against"); וַיַּכּוּ "they struck", plural (1 Kgs 15:20 singular).
- **"Abelmaim, and all the store cities of Naphtali" → "Abel-maim and all the stores of the cities of Naphtali"** (v4) — Hebrew כָּל־מִסְכְּנוֹת עָרֵי נַפְתָּלִי, "stores" first (elsewhere עָרֵי מִסְכְּנוֹת "store cities"). 1 Kgs 15:20 has "Abel-beth-maacah and all Chinneroth, together with all the land of Naphtali".
- **"he left off building of Ramah, and let his work cease" → "he ceased from building Ramah, and he made his work cease"** (v5) — Hebrew חָדַל "cease" and הִשְׁבִּית "make cease" (of שָׁבַת): two verbs, one English word. 1 Kgs 15:21 ends "and he dwelt in Tirzah".
- **"Then Asa the king took all Judah … the timber thereof, wherewith Baasha was building" → "And Asa the king took all Judah … its wood with which Baasha had built"** (v6) — Hebrew אָסָא הַמֶּלֶךְ "Asa the king" (1 Kgs 15:22 הַמֶּלֶךְ אָסָא "King Asa"); לָקַח "took" (1 Kgs 15:22 "made all Judah hear, none was clear"); עֵצִים "wood"; בָּנָה "had built". Chronicles has "Geba", without "of Benjamin".
- **"Because thou hast relied on the king of Syria, and not relied on the LORD" → "Because you leaned on the king of Aram and did not lean on Yahweh"** (v7, 8) — Hebrew נִשְׁעַן עַל "lean on", as a man leans on a staff or on another's hand (2 Sam 1:6; 2 Kgs 7:2); so also 13:18; 14:11. בְּהִשָּׁעֶנְךָ, literally "in your leaning", both times. **"the host" → "the force"** — Hebrew חַיִל. **"is … escaped out of thine hand"** — Hebrew נִמְלַט.
- **"Ethiopians and the Lubims a huge host, with very many chariots and horsemen? yet" → "Cushites and the Libyans a force in abundance, with chariots and horsemen exceedingly many? And"** (v8) — Hebrew לְחַיִל לָרֹב; לְהַרְבֵּה מְאֹד; וּ "and". **"he delivered them" → "he gave them"** — Hebrew נָתַן.
- **"the eyes of the LORD run to and fro throughout the whole earth, to shew himself strong in the behalf of them whose heart is perfect toward him" → "Yahweh, his eyes roam to and fro in all the earth, to strengthen himself with those whose heart is whole toward him"** (v9) — word for word: the Hebrew sets "Yahweh" first and takes it up with "his eyes"; מְשֹׁטְטוֹת, of שׁוּט "roam" (2 Sam 24:2, 8), in the stem of repeated movement; לְהִתְחַזֵּק עִם "to strengthen himself with" (as 15:8; 1 Chr 11:10: to stand strongly by; "to show himself strong on behalf of" is the common understanding); "those whose" is supplied (literally "with their heart whole toward him"); שָׁלֵם "whole", as 15:17.
- **"Herein thou hast done foolishly: therefore from henceforth thou shalt have wars" → "You have acted foolishly over this, for from now on there are wars with you"** (v9) — Hebrew נִסְכַּלְתָּ (as 1 Sam 13:13; 1 Chr 21:8); עַל־זֹאת "over this", as in v10; כִּי "for"; יֵשׁ עִמְּךָ מִלְחָמוֹת "there are wars with you".
- **"Asa was wroth with the seer, and put him in a prison house … And Asa oppressed some of the people the same time" → "Asa was vexed at the seer and put him in the house of the stocks … And Asa crushed some of the people at that time"** (v10) — Hebrew כָּעַס "be vexed"; בֵּית הַמַּהְפֶּכֶת, from הָפַךְ "turn over": a device that bends or twists the body (as Jer 20:2; 29:26); the exact meaning is uncertain. בְּזַעַף "in a rage" (the noun of the raging of the sea, Jonah 1:15; the adjective is "dejected" in Gen 40:6; 1 Kgs 20:43). רִצֵּץ "crush".
- **"the acts of Asa, first and last, lo, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel" → "the acts of Asa, the first and the last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel"** (v11) — Hebrew הִנֵּה twice; עַל־סֵפֶר הַמְּלָכִים לִיהוּדָה וְיִשְׂרָאֵל, literally "upon the book of the kings, of Judah and Israel". דִּבְרֵי "acts", literally "words, matters". 1 Kgs 15:23 names "the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah"; each book's own title is translated.
- **"Asa in the thirty and ninth year of his reign was diseased in his feet, until his disease was exceeding great" → "Asa became sick in the thirty-ninth year of his reign, in his feet, until his sickness was exceedingly great"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיֶּחֱלֶא; חֳלִי "sickness"; עַד־לְמַעְלָה, literally "up to upward" ("great" is supplied).
- **"yet in his disease he sought not to the LORD, but to the physicians" → "And also in his sickness he did not seek Yahweh, but sought by the physicians"** (v12) — Hebrew וְגַם "and also"; לֹא־דָרַשׁ אֶת־יְהוָה כִּי בָּרֹפְאִים, literally "he did not seek Yahweh, but by the physicians": the second "sought" is supplied. דָּרַשׁ בְּ "seek by" is said of asking a god or a spirit for a word (1 Chr 10:14; 2 Kgs 1:2). רֹפְאִים "physicians", literally "healers" (Gen 50:2).
- **"Asa slept with his fathers" → "Asa lay with his fathers"** (v13) — Hebrew שָׁכַב. "Of his reign" is here לְמָלְכוֹ, literally "of his reigning"; v1 and v12 have the noun מַלְכוּת.
- **"in his own sepulchres, which he had made for himself" → "in his graves, which he had dug for himself"** (v14) — Hebrew קִבְרֹתָיו, a plural; כָּרָה "dig".
- **"laid him in the bed which was filled with sweet odours and divers kinds of spices prepared by the apothecaries' art" → "laid him on the bed that he had filled with spices and kinds, blended in a blend of workmanship"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר מִלֵּא "which he had filled", an active verb with no subject named (it can also mean "which one had filled"); בְּשָׂמִים "spices"; זְנִים "kinds" (of spices: the Hebrew says no more); מְרֻקָּחִים בְּמִרְקַחַת מַעֲשֶׂה, literally "blended in a blend of work", that is, made by the perfumer's craft (Exod 30:25, 35).
- **"they made a very great burning for him" → "they burned for him an exceedingly great burning"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׂרְפוּ־לוֹ שְׂרֵפָה, verb and noun of one root; עַד־לִמְאֹד "exceedingly".

### Notes

- **Beside 1 Kgs 15:16–24** — v1–6 run with 1 Kgs 15:17–22 and v11–14 with 15:23–24; where the Hebrew is the same the wording is the same, and each difference is translated as printed. The word of Hanani (v7–10) is only in Chronicles.
- **The thirty-sixth year** (v1; 15:19 "the thirty-fifth year") — the figures are translated as printed. By 1 Kgs 16:6, 8 Baasha died in the twenty-sixth year of Asa. Some count the years from the division of the kingdom; the text says "of the reign of Asa".
- **Hanani the seer** (v7) — Jehu the son of Hanani speaks against Baasha (1 Kgs 16:1) and to Jehoshaphat (2 Chr 19:2).
- **The Cushites and the Libyans** (v8) — the force of Zerah (14:9–13), where only the Cushites are named.
- **The burning** (v14) — a burning of spices in honor of a dead king (2 Chr 21:19; Jer 34:5); the verse does not say that the body was burned.
