# 2 Chronicles 16

[1] In the thirty-sixth year of Asa's reign, ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ came up against ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and built Ramah, to prevent anyone from going out or coming in to Asa king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[2] Then Asa brought out silver and gold from the treasuries of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and of the king's house, and sent to ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ who lived in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, saying,

[3] "There is a treaty between me and you, as between my father and your father. Behold, I have sent you silver and gold. Go, break your treaty with ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that he may withdraw from me."

[4] ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ listened to King Asa and sent the commanders of his armies against the cities of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; they struck ⟦ijon|Iyyon|Ijon⟧, Dan, ⟦abel-maim|Avel-mayim|Abel-maim⟧, and all the store cities of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧.

[5] When ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ heard of it, he stopped building Ramah and let his work cease.

[6] Then King Asa took all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they carried away the stones of Ramah and its timber, with which ⟦baasha|Basha|Baasha⟧ had been building; with them he built ⟦geba|Geva|Geba⟧ and ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧.

[7] At that time ⟦hanani|Chanani|Hanani⟧ the seer came to Asa king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and said to him, "Because you relied on the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and did not rely on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the army of the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ has escaped from your hand.

[8] Were not the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ and the ⟦libyans|Luvim|Libyans⟧ a vast army, with very many chariots and horsemen? Yet because you relied on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he gave them into your hand.

[9] For the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ range throughout the whole earth, to show himself strong on behalf of those whose heart is whole toward him. You have acted foolishly in this; from now on you shall have wars."

[10] Then Asa was angry with the seer and put him in the stocks-house, for he was enraged with him because of this thing. Asa also oppressed some of the people at the same time.

[11] Behold, the acts of Asa, first and last, are written in the Book of the Kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[12] In the thirty-ninth year of his reign Asa was diseased in his feet; his disease became very severe. Yet even in his disease he did not seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, but the rofe'im — physicians.

[13] Asa slept with his fathers and died in the forty-first year of his reign.

[14] They buried him in his own tomb, which he had cut out for himself in the city of David; they laid him on a bed that was filled with sweet spices prepared by the perfumer's art, and they made a very great burning for him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v2, 3, 7).
- **"Ethiopians / Lubims" → "Cushites / Libyans"** (v8).
- **"league" → "treaty"** (v3) — *berit*.
- **"perfect" → "whole"** (v9) — *shalem*.
- **"prison house" → "stocks-house"** (v10) — *bet-mahpeket*; pillory-prison.
- **"sweet odours and divers kinds of spices" → "sweet spices"** (v14) — *besamim*.
- **"apothecaries" → "perfumer's"** (v14) — *roqchim*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 15:16-24** — Chronicler adds the Hanani confrontation, Asa's imprisonment of the prophet, and the foot-disease detail.
- **Thirty-sixth year** — chronologically difficult (Baasha died 24th year of Asa per 1 Kgs 16:8). Possible reading: "thirty-sixth year of the divided kingdom" rather than of Asa's reign.
- **Chronicler's reversal** — early Asa relies on Yahweh (ch 14) and wins; late Asa relies on Aram (here) and gets mixed results.
- **Hanani the seer** — father of Jehu (son of Hanani) who will oppose Baasha (1 Kgs 16:1).
- **Asa oppresses people** (v10) — signs of character breakdown late in reign.
- **Seeking physicians instead of Yahweh** — not anti-medicine polemic; Chronicler's point is Asa's continued faithless reliance on human means.
- **Royal cremation** (v14) — "great burning" = spice-pyre, royal honor (cf. Jer 34:5). Not cremation of body.
