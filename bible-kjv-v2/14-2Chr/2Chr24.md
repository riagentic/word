# 2 Chronicles 24

[1] Joash was seven years old when he began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Zibiah of Beersheba.

[2] Joash did what was right in the eyes of Yahweh all the days of Jehoiada the priest.

[3] Jehoiada took for him two wives; he fathered sons and daughters.

[4] After this, Joash determined to repair the house of Yahweh.

[5] He gathered together the priests and the Levites and said to them, "Go out to the cities of Judah and gather from all Israel silver to repair the house of your *Elohim*, year by year; hasten the matter." But the Levites did not hasten it.

[6] The king summoned Jehoiada the chief, and said to him, "Why have you not required the Levites to bring in from Judah and Jerusalem the levy ordained by Moses the servant of Yahweh and by the assembly of Israel for the Tent of the Testimony?"

[7] For the sons of Athaliah, that wicked woman, had broken into the house of *Elohim*; and all the dedicated things of the house of Yahweh they had used for the Baals.

[8] At the king's command, they made a chest and set it outside at the gate of the house of Yahweh.

[9] They made a proclamation throughout Judah and Jerusalem to bring in to Yahweh the levy that Moses the servant of *Elohim* had imposed on Israel in the wilderness.

[10] All the officials and all the people rejoiced, and brought in and cast into the chest until they had finished.

[11] Whenever the chest was brought to the king's office by the hand of the Levites, when they saw that there was much silver, the king's scribe and the officer of the chief priest would come, empty the chest, take it up, and carry it back to its place. Thus they did day by day, and gathered silver in abundance.

[12] The king and Jehoiada gave it to those who did the work of the service of the house of Yahweh; they hired masons and carpenters to restore the house of Yahweh, and also those who worked in iron and bronze to repair the house of Yahweh.

[13] So those who did the work labored, and the work of restoration went forward under their hand; they restored the house of *Elohim* to its proper condition and strengthened it.

[14] When they had finished, they brought the rest of the silver before the king and Jehoiada; with it were made vessels for the house of Yahweh — vessels for service and for offering, basins, and vessels of gold and silver. They offered burnt offerings in the house of Yahweh continually all the days of Jehoiada.

[15] Jehoiada grew old and was full of days, and he died; he was one hundred thirty years old when he died.

[16] They buried him in the city of David among the kings, because he had done good in Israel, toward *Elohim* and toward his house.

[17] Now after the death of Jehoiada, the officials of Judah came and did obeisance to the king; then the king listened to them.

[18] They forsook the house of Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers and served the Asherah poles and the idols. Wrath came upon Judah and Jerusalem because of this their guilt.

[19] Yet Yahweh sent prophets among them, to bring them back to Yahweh; they testified against them, but they would not listen.

[20] The spirit of *Elohim* clothed Zechariah son of Jehoiada the priest. He stood above the people and said to them, "Thus says *Elohim*: 'Why do you transgress the commandments of Yahweh so that you cannot prosper? Because you have forsaken Yahweh, he has also forsaken you.'"

[21] They conspired against him and stoned him with stones at the king's command, in the court of the house of Yahweh.

[22] Thus King Joash did not remember the steadfast love that Jehoiada his father had shown him, but killed his son. As he was dying, he said, "May Yahweh see and avenge!"

[23] At the turn of the year, the army of Aram came up against him; they came to Judah and Jerusalem, and destroyed all the officials of the people from among the people, and sent all the spoil from them to the king of Damascus.

[24] Though the army of the Arameans had come with few men, Yahweh delivered a very great army into their hand, because they had forsaken Yahweh the *Elohim* of their fathers. So they executed judgment upon Joash.

[25] When they had departed from him, leaving him with severe wounds, his own servants conspired against him for the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest, and killed him on his bed; so he died, and they buried him in the city of David, but did not bury him in the tombs of the kings.

[26] These are those who conspired against him: Zabad son of Shimeath the Ammonitess, and Jehozabad son of Shimrith the Moabitess.

[27] Regarding his sons, the many oracles concerning him, and the rebuilding of the house of *Elohim*, behold, they are written in the Midrash of the Book of the Kings. Amaziah his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"money" → "silver"** (throughout) — *kesef*.
- **"tabernacle of witness" → "Tent of the Testimony"** (v6).
- **"Baalim" → "Baals"** (v7).
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v10, 17) — *sarim*.
- **"Syria / Syrians" → "Aram / Arameans"** (v23, 24).
- **"commanded the Levites to bring in... the collection... for the tabernacle of witness" → "required the Levites to bring in... the levy ordained by Moses... for the Tent of the Testimony"** (v6) — references the half-shekel tax (Ex 30:11-16).

### Notes
- **Parallel to 2 Kgs 12** — Chronicler expands significantly: Jehoiada's age and honorable burial, apostasy after his death, Zechariah's martyrdom, Aramean punishment, conspiracy details.
- **Jehoiada's unique royal burial** (v16) — exceptional honor for a priest; acknowledgment of his regency-like role.
- **Zechariah son of Jehoiada** — likely the "Zechariah son of Berachiah" in Matt 23:35 (Jesus links Abel and Zechariah as bookends of prophetic martyrdom — Zechariah ben Jehoiada is the last martyr in Hebrew canonical order, 2 Chr being last book). Some identify with Zech 1:1's prophet; most commentators see Jesus referring to this Chronicles figure.
- **Stoning in the temple court** (v21) — profanation; hence "may Yahweh avenge."
- **Joash's fall** — Chronicler's theological structure: faithful while Jehoiada lived, apostatized after, divine retribution through Aramean invasion and assassination. Exactly inverse of the start of his reign.
- **Jesus echoes** (Matt 23:35-36) — Abel to Zechariah frames all righteous blood.
- **Dishonorable burial** (v25) — not in royal tombs; covenant-punishment motif.
