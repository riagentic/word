## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"meat offerings" → "grain offerings"** (v7) — *menachot*.
- **"brasen" → "bronze"** (v7).
- **"entering in of Hamath" → "Lebo-hamath"** (v8).
- **"river of Egypt" → "Wadi of Egypt"** (v8).
- **"high" → "exalted"** (v21) — *elyon*.

### Notes
- **Parallel to 1 Kgs 9** — Chronicler adds the fire from heaven (v1-3), unique. Fire confirms the sacrifice and divine reception.
- **22,000 oxen + 120,000 sheep** — matches 1 Kgs 8:63. Enormous numbers; likely includes fellowship offerings shared in feast.
- **Eight-day dedication** + 7-day Feast of Booths = 14-day festival, plus conclusion on 23rd.
- **"If my people..." (v14)** — Chronicler's signature verse; classic pattern: humble-pray-seek-turn → hear-forgive-heal. Applied to post-exilic restoration.
- **Conditional Davidic covenant** (v17-22) — Solomon and sons face covenant conditionality. If faithful → throne; if faithless → temple destroyed. Chronicler writes after the temple's destruction already; this passage interprets it.
- **"Proverb and byword"** — fulfilled in the Babylonian exile; Chronicler's audience knows this fulfillment.
- **Restoration-implicit** — the promise implies the possibility of return if repentance happens.
