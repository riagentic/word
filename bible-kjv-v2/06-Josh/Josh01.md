[1] After the death of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of Nun, ⟦moses|Moshe's|Moses'⟧ attendant, saying,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"minister" → "attendant"** (v1) — Hebrew מְשָׁרֵת *mesharet*. Personal servant / aide.
- **"coast" → "territory"** (v4) — as in Deuteronomy.
- **"be of good courage" → "be courageous"** (v6, 7, 9, 18) — *chazaq ve-ematz*, as in Deut 31.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v7, 8).
- **"victuals" → "provisions"** (v11) — archaic.
- **"host" → "camp"** (v11) — Hebrew מַחֲנֶה *machaneh*.

### Notes
- **Opening of the Former Prophets** — Joshua begins the "Former Prophets" (Joshua, Judges, Samuel, Kings) in Jewish canon; "Historical Books" in Christian Bibles.
- **"Servant of Yahweh"** (v1, 13, 15) — Moses' title. Joshua is not called that yet — he's "Moses' attendant." Only at book's end (Josh 24:29) does he earn the same title.
- **Be strong and courageous** — four-fold repetition (v6, 7, 9, 18). Joshua must internalize it before leading.
- **Meditate day and night** (v8) — Hebrew הָגָה *hagah*. Low muttering of Torah — rumination, memorization-by-recitation. Ps 1:2 echoes.
- **Transjordan tribes' commitment** (v12–18) — they remember their Num 32 promise: fight across the Jordan first, then return home.
- **"Whoever rebels shall die"** (v18) — the people affirm Joshua's authority even to capital sentence. Transition of leadership confirmed.
