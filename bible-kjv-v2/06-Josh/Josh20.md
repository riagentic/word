# Joshua 20

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, 'Appoint for yourselves the cities of refuge of which I spoke to you by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧,

[3] that the manslayer who kills any person unintentionally and without knowing it may flee there. They shall be for you a refuge from the avenger of blood.

[4] The one who flees to one of these cities shall stand at the entrance of the gate of the city and declare his case in the hearing of the elders of that city; they shall take him into the city to themselves and give him a place, so that he may dwell among them.

[5] If the avenger of blood pursues after him, they shall not deliver the manslayer into his hand, because he struck his neighbor unintentionally and did not hate him beforehand.

[6] He shall dwell in that city until he stands before the congregation for judgment, and until the death of the high priest who is in office in those days. Then the manslayer may return to his own city and to his own house, to the city from which he fled.'"

[7] So they set apart ⟦kedesh|Qedesh|Kedesh⟧ in ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ in the hill country of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, Shechem in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and ⟦kiriath-arba|Qiryat Arba|Kiriath-arba⟧ (that is, ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧) in the hill country of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[8] Beyond the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, east of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, they assigned ⟦bezer|Betzer|Bezer⟧ in the wilderness on the tableland, from the tribe of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧; ⟦ramoth|Ramot|Ramoth⟧ in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, from the tribe of Gad; and Golan in Bashan, from the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧.

[9] These were the cities appointed for all the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and for the foreigner who sojourns among them, so that whoever kills any person unintentionally may flee there and not die by the hand of the avenger of blood, until he stands before the congregation.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"unawares and unwittingly" → "unintentionally and without knowing it"** (v3) — Hebrew *bi-shegagah bi-vli-da'at*.
- **"revenger of blood" → "avenger of blood"** (v3, 5, 9) — as in Num 35.
- **"beforetime" → "beforehand"** (v5) — archaic.
- **"stranger" → "foreigner"** (v9) — *ger*.

### Notes
- **Fulfillment of Num 35 / Deut 19** — six cities of refuge formally established. Three east of Jordan (already designated by Moses, Deut 4:41–43): Bezer, Ramoth-gilead, Golan. Three west of Jordan designated now: Kedesh (north), Shechem (center), Hebron (south).
- **Strategic distribution** — roughly equidistant across the land. Any Israelite can reach a refuge city relatively quickly. Rabbinic tradition claims roads were maintained and signs posted to speed access.
- **Case procedure** — (1) Manslayer flees to city; (2) elders hear case at the gate; (3) if accepted, sheltered; (4) stands before congregation for judgment; (5) if acquitted of intentional murder, remains in city until high priest dies; (6) then returns home.
- **High priest's death as release** — continues the Torah law (Num 35:25, 28). The death of the high priest functions as amnesty / atonement-marker.
- **Foreigners included** (v9) — the protection extends to resident aliens. Same standard of justice.
