# Joshua 20

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, 'Give for yourselves the cities of refuge, of which I spoke to you by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧,

[3] for a murderer to flee there, one who strikes down a soul in error, without knowledge; and they shall be for you for refuge from the redeemer of blood.

[4] And he shall flee to one of these cities and stand at the entrance of the gate of the city and speak his words in the ears of the elders of that city; and they shall gather him into the city to themselves and give him a place, and he shall dwell with them.

[5] And when the redeemer of blood pursues after him, they shall not hand over the murderer into his hand, for without knowledge he struck his neighbor, and he was not hating him yesterday and the day before.

[6] And he shall dwell in that city until he stands before the congregation for judgment, until the death of the great priest who shall be in those days; then the murderer shall return and come to his city and to his house, to the city from which he fled.'"

[7] And they sanctified ⟦kedesh|Qedesh|Kedesh⟧ in ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧ in the hill country of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧, and Shechem in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and ⟦kiriath-arba|Qiryat Arba|Kiriath-arba⟧, that is ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧, in the hill country of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[8] And across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, eastward, they gave ⟦bezer|Betzer|Bezer⟧ in the wilderness, on the tableland, from the tribe of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧, and ⟦ramoth|Ramot|Ramoth⟧ in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ from the tribe of Gad, and Golan in the Bashan from the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧.

[9] These were the cities of designation for all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and for the sojourner who sojourns in their midst, for everyone who strikes down a soul in error to flee there, and he shall not die by the hand of the redeemer of blood until he stands before the congregation.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** (v2, 9) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"Appoint out for you cities of refuge" → "Give for yourselves the cities of refuge"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּנוּ לָכֶם, "give", the verb of Num 35:6, 13, 14 and of v8 here; "the cities" has the article: the ones already spoken of. מִקְלָט *miqlat* is "refuge" as Num 35.
- **"the slayer that killeth any person unawares and unwittingly" → "a murderer … one who strikes down a soul in error, without knowledge"** (v3) — Hebrew רוֹצֵחַ מַכֵּה־נֶפֶשׁ בִּשְׁגָגָה בִּבְלִי־דָעַת. רֹצֵחַ is the participle of רָצַח, the verb of "You shall not murder" (Exod 20:13); as in Num 35 and Deut 19 the one word is used also of the man who killed in error (v3, 5, 6): the name goes with the deed until the congregation has judged. מַכֵּה־נֶפֶשׁ is "a striker of a soul" (Num 35:11, 15); נֶפֶשׁ "soul", a living person. The two expressions stand side by side with no "and": בִּשְׁגָגָה "in error" is the word of Num 35:11, 15; בִּבְלִי־דַעַת, literally "in the lack of knowledge", is the word of Deut 4:42; 19:4. "Unintentionally" is the sense of both.
- **"your refuge from the avenger of blood" → "for you for refuge from the redeemer of blood"** (v3, 5, 9) — Hebrew גֹּאֵל הַדָּם *go'el ha-dam*, as Num 35:19–27; Deut 19:6, 12. גֹּאֵל is the participle of גָּאַל "redeem": the kinsman who buys back his brother's land and person (Lev 25:25, 48) is the same man who claims back his blood. "Avenger" (נֹקֵם) is another word and is not used here.
- **"when he that doth flee unto one of those cities shall stand" → "And he shall flee to one of these cities and stand"** (v4) — Hebrew וְנָס … וְעָמַד, two verbs in a chain. **"shall declare his cause in the ears of the elders" → "speak his words in the ears of the elders"** — Hebrew וְדִבֶּר … אֶת־דְּבָרָיו, "speak his words"; בְּאָזְנֵי "in the ears of". **"they shall take him into the city unto them" → "they shall gather him into the city to themselves"** — Hebrew וְאָסְפוּ, אָסַף "gather, take in". **"that he may dwell among them" → "and he shall dwell with them"** — Hebrew וְיָשַׁב עִמָּם.
- **"they shall not deliver the slayer up into his hand" → "they shall not hand over the murderer into his hand"** (v5) — Hebrew יַסְגִּרוּ, literally "shut up" (into the hand of another), as Deut 23:15. **"because he smote his neighbour unwittingly" → "for without knowledge he struck his neighbor"** — Hebrew בִבְלִי־דַעַת stands first. **"hated him not beforetime" → "he was not hating him yesterday and the day before"** — Hebrew לֹא־שֹׂנֵא הוּא לוֹ מִתְּמוֹל שִׁלְשׁוֹם, a participle, and literally "from yesterday, the third day" = before now (Deut 4:42; 19:4, 6).
- **"and until the death of the high priest that shall be in those days" → "until the death of the great priest who shall be in those days"** (v6) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל, "the great priest", as Num 35:25, 28 (the "high priest" of later usage). The two "until" clauses stand side by side with no "and" between them; how the judgment before the congregation and the death of the priest are related is told in Num 35:24–28. **"then shall the slayer return, and come unto his own city" → "then the murderer shall return and come to his city"** — the Hebrew has no "own".
- **"they appointed Kedesh" → "they sanctified Qedesh"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיַּקְדִּשׁוּ, the hifil of קדשׁ, "make holy, set apart as holy"; the verb has the same root as the name of the first city, קֶדֶשׁ. **"in mount Naphtali … mount Ephraim … the mountain of Judah" → "in the hill country of"** — Hebrew הַר, "mountain", three times, of a region. "Galilee" has the article in Hebrew, הַגָּלִיל, "the circuit".
- **"on the other side Jordan by Jericho eastward, they assigned" → "across the Jordan of Jericho, eastward, they gave"** (v8) — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לְיַרְדֵּן יְרִיחוֹ, a genitive, as Num 22:1; 35:1; נָתְנוּ "gave", the verb of v2. **"upon the plain" → "on the tableland"** — Hebrew מִישׁוֹר, "level land": the plateau east of the Dead Sea (Deut 4:43). **"tribe"** (three times) is מַטֶּה, literally "staff". **"Golan"** — the written form (ketiv) is גלון "Galon"; the reading tradition (qere) is גּוֹלָן "Golan", as Deut 4:43; the qere is translated.
- **"the cities appointed" → "the cities of designation"** (v9) — Hebrew עָרֵי הַמּוּעָדָה; מוּעָדָה is found only here, a noun from יָעַד "designate, appoint": the cities set by appointment. **"the stranger that sojourneth among them" → "the sojourner who sojourns in their midst"** — Hebrew הַגֵּר הַגָּר בְּתוֹכָם. **"that whosoever killeth any person at unawares might flee thither, and not die" → "for everyone who strikes down a soul in error to flee there, and he shall not die"** — as v3; here only בִּשְׁגָגָה stands.

### Notes
- **Fulfillment of Num 35 / Deut 19** — the six cities of refuge are given. Three across the Jordan (separated by Moses, Deut 4:41–43): Bezer, Ramoth in Gilead, Golan. Three west of the Jordan: Kedesh (north), Shechem (center), Hebron (south).
- **Distribution** — the six lie across the land, so that no part of it is far from one (Deut 19:3). Rabbinic tradition says that roads were kept up and signs posted.
- **Procedure** — (1) the one who killed flees to the city; (2) he speaks his words to the elders at the gate; (3) they take him in; (4) he stands before the congregation for judgment; (5) he dwells in the city until the great priest dies; (6) then he returns to his own city.
- **The great priest's death as release** — as Num 35:25, 28. The text gives no reason for it.
- **Sojourners included** (v9) — the same protection holds for the sojourner (Num 35:15).
