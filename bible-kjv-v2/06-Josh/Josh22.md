# Joshua 22

[1] Then ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ called the ⟦reubenites|Reuveni|Reubenite⟧ and the ⟦gadites|Gadi|Gadite⟧ and the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧,

[2] and he said to them, "You have kept all that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded you, and you have listened to my voice in all that I commanded you.

[3] You have not forsaken your brothers these many days, to this day, and you have kept the charge of the commandment of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] And now ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given rest to your brothers, as he spoke to them; and now turn and get yourselves to your tents, to the land of your possession, which ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave you across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[6] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ blessed them and sent them away, and they went to their tents.

[7] And to the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ had given in the Bashan, and to its half ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ gave with their brothers across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, westward. And also, when ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ sent them away to their tents, he blessed them,

[8] and he said to them, saying, "With many riches return to your tents, and with very much livestock, with silver and with gold and with bronze and with iron and with very many cloaks; divide the spoil of your enemies with your brothers."

[9] And the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and the sons of Gad and the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ returned and went from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, from Shiloh, which is in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, to go to the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, to the land of their possession, in which they had taken possession at the mouth of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[10] And they came to the districts of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, which are in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, and the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and the sons of Gad and the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ built there an altar by the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, an altar great in appearance.

[11] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ heard, saying, "Behold, the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and the sons of Gad and the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ have built the altar opposite the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, at the districts of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, at the side of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[12] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ heard, and all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ assembled at Shiloh to go up against them as a host.

[13] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sent to the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and to the sons of Gad and to the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, to the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧ the son of ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest,

[14] and ten chieftains with him, one chieftain for each father's house, for all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and each of them was a head of the house of their fathers, for the thousands of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] And they came to the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and to the sons of Gad and to the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, to the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, and they spoke with them, saying,

[16] "Thus says all the congregation of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'What is this unfaithfulness that you have committed against the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to turn back today from after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, in your building for yourselves an altar, to rebel today against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?

[17] Is the iniquity of Peor too little for us, from which we have not cleansed ourselves to this day, and the plague was on the congregation of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[18] and you, you turn back today from after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧? And it shall be: you rebel today against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and tomorrow he will be wrathful with all the congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[19] But if the land of your possession is unclean, pass over to the land of the possession of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, where the dwelling of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ abides, and take possession in our midst; and against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ do not rebel, and against us do not rebel, in your building for yourselves an altar apart from the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] Did not Achan the son of ⟦zerah|Zerach|Zerah⟧ commit unfaithfulness in the devoted thing, and on all the congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ there was wrath? And he, one man, did not expire alone in his iniquity.'"

[21] And the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and the sons of Gad and the half of the tribe of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ answered and spoke with the heads of the thousands of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[22] "⟦el|El|God⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧! ⟦el|El|God⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he knows, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, he shall know: if in rebellion and if in unfaithfulness against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — do not save us this day —

[23] to build for ourselves an altar, to turn back from after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and if to offer up on it burnt offering and grain offering, and if to offer on it sacrifices of peace offerings, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ himself shall seek it.

[24] But surely from anxiety, because of a matter, we have done this, saying, 'Tomorrow your sons will say to our sons, saying, "What have you to do with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[25] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has set a border between us and you, sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and sons of Gad: the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧. You have no share in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And your sons will make our sons cease from fearing ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'

[26] And we said, 'Let us now act for ourselves, to build the altar, not for burnt offering and not for sacrifice;

[27] for it is a witness between us and you and between our generations after us, to serve the service of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ before him with our burnt offerings and with our sacrifices and with our peace offerings; and your sons shall not say tomorrow to our sons, "You have no share in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."'

[28] And we said, 'And it shall be, when they say this to us and to our generations tomorrow, that we shall say, "See the pattern of the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that our fathers made, not for burnt offering and not for sacrifice; for it is a witness between us and you."'

[29] Far be it from us to rebel against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and to turn back today from after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to build an altar for burnt offering, for grain offering and for sacrifice, besides the altar of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that is before his dwelling."

[30] And ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧ the priest and the chieftains of the congregation and the heads of the thousands of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who were with him heard the words that the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and the sons of Gad and the sons of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ spoke, and it was good in their eyes.

[31] And ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧ the son of ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest said to the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and to the sons of Gad and to the sons of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, "Today we know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is in our midst, because you have not committed this unfaithfulness against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; then you have delivered the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[32] And ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧ the son of ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest and the chieftains returned from the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and from the sons of Gad, from the land of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧, to the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they brought them back word.

[33] And the word was good in the eyes of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ blessed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they did not say to go up against them as a host, to ruin the land in which the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and the sons of Gad were dwelling.

[34] And the sons of ⟦reuben|Reuven|Reuben⟧ and the sons of Gad called the altar: "For it is a witness between us that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children" → "sons"** (throughout, also v24, 25, 27) — Hebrew בְּנֵי, בָּנִים.
- **"the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh" → "the Reubenite and the Gadite and the half of the tribe of Manasseh"** (v1) — the Hebrew has singulars with the article. "Tribe" is מַטֶּה (literally "staff") in v1 and v14, and שֵׁבֶט (literally "rod") in v7, 9, 10, 11, 13, 15, 21; the two words name the same thing. In v7, 9, 10, 11, 21 "Manasseh" has the article, הַמְנַשֶּׁה.
- **"have obeyed my voice" → "you have listened to my voice"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל. **"servant"** (v2, 4, 5) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, "slave"; of Yahweh's man "servant".
- **"Ye have not left" → "You have not forsaken"** (v3) — Hebrew עָזַב. **"kept the charge of the commandment"** — Hebrew שְׁמַרְתֶּם אֶת־מִשְׁמֶרֶת, "kept the keeping": verb and noun of one root.
- **"as he promised them" → "as he spoke to them"** (v4) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר. **"return ye, and get you unto your tents" → "turn and get yourselves to your tents"** — Hebrew פְּנוּ וּלְכוּ לָכֶם, literally "turn and go for yourselves". **"on the other side Jordan" → "across the Jordan"** (v4, 7) — Hebrew בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן: the same words name the east side in v4 and the west side in v7.
- **"But take diligent heed to do" → "Only keep very much to do"** (v5) — Hebrew שִׁמְרוּ מְאֹד לַעֲשׂוֹת: שָׁמַר "keep" with מְאֹד "very, greatly", as Deut 24:8. **"to cleave unto him" → "to cling to him"** — Hebrew דָּבַק, as Deut 10:20; 11:22; 13:4. The five infinitives are joined by "and", as Deut 11:22; 30:20.
- **"Now to the one half … but unto the other half thereof" → "And to the half … and to its half"** (v7) — Hebrew וְלַחֲצִי … וּלְחֶצְיוֹ; "one" and "other" are not written. "Possession" is not written after "given". **"on this side Jordan westward" → "across the Jordan, westward"** — the written form (ketiv) is מעבר "from across"; the reading tradition (qere) is בְּעֵבֶר "across"; the sense is the same. "Westward" is literally "seaward".
- **"Return with much riches … with very much cattle … with very much raiment" → "With many riches return … with very much livestock … with very many cloaks"** (v8) — Hebrew נְכָסִים "riches, goods" (a word of the later books); מִקְנֶה "livestock"; שְׂלָמוֹת, the outer garments. "And he said to them, saying": both verbs are in the Hebrew.
- **"whereof they were possessed, according to the word of the LORD" → "in which they had taken possession at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v9) — Hebrew נֹאחֲזוּ־בָהּ, the verb of אֲחֻזָּה "possession" (so v19 "take possession"); עַל־פִּי יְהוָה. **"the country of Gilead" → "the land of Gilead"** — Hebrew אֶרֶץ; "Gilead" has the article.
- **"the borders of Jordan" → "the districts of the Jordan"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew גְּלִילוֹת, "circles, circuits" (the word of "Galilee"); some take it as a place name, Geliloth (18:17). **"a great altar to see to" → "an altar great in appearance"** — Hebrew גָּדוֹל לְמַרְאֶה, literally "great for the sight".
- **"have built an altar over against the land of Canaan, in the borders of Jordan, at the passage of the children of Israel" → "have built the altar opposite the land of Canaan, at the districts of the Jordan, at the side of the sons of Israel"** (v11) — Hebrew אֶל־מוּל "toward the front of"; אֶל־עֵבֶר בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, "toward the side of the sons of Israel": on which bank the altar stood the words do not settle (v10 says "in the land of Canaan"). "The altar" has the article.
- **"gathered themselves together … to go up to war against them" → "assembled … to go up against them as a host"** (v12, 33) — Hebrew וַיִּקָּהֲלוּ; לַצָּבָא, literally "for the host" (the army in the field). **"congregation"** (v12, 16, 17, 18, 20, 30) — Hebrew עֵדָה.
- **"ten princes, of each chief house a prince throughout all the tribes" → "ten chieftains … one chieftain for each father's house, for all the tribes"** (v14) — Hebrew נָשִׂיא "chieftain"; נָשִׂיא אֶחָד נָשִׂיא אֶחָד לְבֵית אָב, literally "one chieftain, one chieftain for a father's house". **"an head of the house of their fathers among the thousands of Israel" → "a head of the house of their fathers, for the thousands of Israel"** (v14, 21, 30) — Hebrew אַלְפֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, "the thousands of Israel" (Num 1:16; 10:4): the divisions of the people; "clans" is another word.
- **"trespass" → "unfaithfulness"** (v16, 20, 22, 31) — Hebrew מַעַל *ma'al*, a breach of trust (Lev 5:15; Num 5:6); in v16, 20, 31 verb and noun stand together, מָעַל מַעַל, "commit unfaithfulness". **"to turn away this day from following the LORD" → "to turn back today from after Yahweh"** (v16, 18, 23, 29) — Hebrew לָשׁוּב … מֵאַחֲרֵי יְהוָה, as Num 14:43. **"that ye might rebel" → "to rebel"** (v16) — Hebrew לִמְרָדְכֶם, literally "for your rebelling".
- **"rebel" / "rebellion"** (v16, 18, 19, 22, 29) — Hebrew מָרַד and its noun מֶרֶד (v22), the word for revolt against an overlord (Num 14:9). מָרָה (Num 20:10; Deut 1:26) is another verb; English has one word for both.
- **"Is the iniquity of Peor too little for us, from which we are not cleansed until this day, although there was a plague" → "… from which we have not cleansed ourselves to this day, and the plague was"** (v17) — Hebrew הִטַּהַרְנוּ, reflexive; וַיְהִי הַנֶּגֶף, "and the plague was", with no "although"; נֶגֶף is literally "a blow" (Num 25:8–9, 18). **"But that ye must turn away … and it will be, seeing ye rebel to day" → "and you, you turn back today … And it shall be: you rebel today … and tomorrow he will be wrathful"** (v18) — the Hebrew sets the clauses side by side; קָצַף "be wrathful", noun קֶצֶף "wrath" (v20).
- **"then pass ye over unto the land" → "pass over to the land"** (v19) — Hebrew עִבְרוּ לָכֶם, the imperative with "for yourselves", as v4; English cannot carry it here ("pass yourselves over"). **"wherein the LORD's tabernacle dwelleth" → "where the dwelling of Yahweh abides"** — Hebrew שָׁכַן־שָׁם מִשְׁכַּן יְהוָה: verb and noun of one root. **"nor rebel against us"** — the Hebrew is וְאֹתָנוּ אֶל־תִּמְרֹדוּ: the verb takes here a direct object, "us" (elsewhere "rebel against" has בְּ, as in the clause before), and the printed text points אֶל where "do not" is אַל; it is read as "do not". Some take it as "and do not make us rebels". **"beside the altar" → "apart from the altar"** — Hebrew מִבַּלְעֲדֵי; v29 has מִלְּבַד "besides".
- **"commit a trespass in the accursed thing" → "commit unfaithfulness in the devoted thing"** (v20) — Hebrew מָעַל מַעַל בַּחֵרֶם, the words of 7:1; חֵרֶם is what is devoted to Yahweh beyond recall, here the spoil of Jericho that was to be destroyed. **"and that man perished not alone in his iniquity" → "And he, one man, did not expire alone in his iniquity"** — Hebrew וְהוּא אִישׁ אֶחָד לֹא גָוַע בַּעֲוֺנוֹ, literally "and he, one man, did not expire in his iniquity": he was one man, and yet he was not the one man who died for it (7:5, 24–25). "Alone" is supplied, because without it the English would say that he did not die. גָּוַע is "expire".
- **"The LORD God of gods" → "El, Elohim, Yahweh"** (v22) — Hebrew אֵל אֱלֹהִים יְהוָה, twice: three divine names side by side, each divided from the next by a stroke (*paseq*) in the Masoretic Text; so also Ps 50:1. The first two can also be read together as "the El of gods" ("God of gods"). **"he knoweth, and Israel he shall know"** — Hebrew הוּא יֹדֵעַ וְיִשְׂרָאֵל הוּא יֵדָע; "let him know" is the sense. **"save us not this day" → "do not save us this day"** — Hebrew אַל־תּוֹשִׁיעֵנוּ, singular "you": the speakers turn to Yahweh in the middle of their sentence. The sentence runs on into v23: "if in rebellion … to build for ourselves an altar".
- **"That we have built us an altar" → "to build for ourselves an altar"** (v23) — Hebrew לִבְנוֹת, an infinitive. **"to offer thereon burnt offering or meat offering, or … to offer peace offerings" → "to offer up on it burnt offering and grain offering … to offer on it sacrifices of peace offerings"** — Hebrew לְהַעֲלוֹת עוֹלָה, literally "to make a going-up go up"; מִנְחָה "grain offering"; לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "to make", of sacrifices; זִבְחֵי שְׁלָמִים. **"let the LORD himself require it" → "Yahweh himself shall seek it"** — Hebrew יְבַקֵּשׁ "seek", here to call to account.
- **"And if we have not rather done it for fear of this thing" → "But surely from anxiety, because of a matter, we have done this"** (v24) — Hebrew וְאִם־לֹא, the form of an oath, literally "and if not …": "surely". דְּאָגָה is "anxiety, concern"; מִדָּבָר is literally "from a word / a thing". **"In time to come" → "Tomorrow"** (v24, 27, 28) — Hebrew מָחָר, "tomorrow" (so v18), here of the days to come. **"What have ye to do with the LORD"** — literally "What is there to you and to Yahweh".
- **"the LORD hath made Jordan a border" → "Yahweh has set a border … the Jordan"** (v25) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "gave"; "the Jordan" stands at the end. **"ye have no part in the LORD" → "you have no share in Yahweh"** (v25, 27) — Hebrew חֵלֶק. **"make our children cease from fearing"** — Hebrew הִשְׁבִּיתוּ, the causative of שָׁבַת "cease".
- **"Let us now prepare to build us an altar" → "Let us now act for ourselves, to build the altar"** (v26) — Hebrew נַעֲשֶׂה־נָּא לָנוּ לִבְנוֹת אֶת־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ, literally "let us do / make now for ourselves, to build the altar"; the sentence is rough in Hebrew. "Sacrifice" is זֶבַח.
- **"But that it may be a witness" → "for it is a witness"** (v27, 28, 34) — Hebrew כִּי עֵד הוּא; עֵד is "witness". **"that we might do the service of the LORD" → "to serve the service of Yahweh"** (v27) — Hebrew לַעֲבֹד אֶת־עֲבֹדַת יְהוָה, verb and noun of one root.
- **"Behold the pattern of the altar"** (v28) — Hebrew תַּבְנִית, a thing built after the shape of another, as Exod 25:9. "This" is supplied after "when they say".
- **"God forbid that we should rebel" → "Far be it from us to rebel"** (v29) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לָּנוּ מִמֶּנּוּ, literally "a profane thing to us, from it"; no divine name is in the phrase. **"meat offerings" → "grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה. **"that is before his tabernacle" → "that is before his dwelling"** — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן.
- **"it pleased them" → "it was good in their eyes"** (v30, 33) — Hebrew וַיִּיטַב בְּעֵינֵיהֶם. **"princes" → "chieftains"** (v30, 32) — as v14.
- **"This day we perceive" → "Today we know"** (v31) — Hebrew יָדַעְנוּ. **"now ye have delivered" → "then you have delivered"** — Hebrew אָז "then": by this. **"Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest"** (v13, 31, 32) — the Hebrew leaves open whether "the priest" is Eleazar or Phinehas; v30 has "Phinehas the priest".
- **"brought them word again" → "brought them back word"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיָּשִׁבוּ אוֹתָם דָּבָר.
- **"did not intend to go up against them in battle, to destroy the land" → "did not say to go up against them as a host, to ruin the land"** (v33) — Hebrew וְלֹא אָמְרוּ "did not say"; לְשַׁחֵת "to ruin". "The matter" is דָּבָר, "word".
- **"called the altar Ed: for it shall be a witness between us that the LORD is God" → "called the altar: 'For it is a witness between us that Yahweh is Elohim'"** (v34) — the Masoretic Text gives no name: וַיִּקְרְאוּ … לַמִּזְבֵּחַ כִּי עֵד הוּא בֵּינֹתֵינוּ כִּי יְהוָה הָאֱלֹהִים, "they called (to) the altar, for it is a witness between us that Yahweh is the Elohim". Either the whole sentence is what they called it, or a name has dropped out. The King James Version supplies "Ed" (עֵד "witness") in italics; some Hebrew manuscripts and old versions have it (stated from memory). Nothing is supplied here; the earlier "Ed" in this verse is removed. "Elohim" has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים: the true God (Deut 4:35; 1 Kgs 18:39).

### Notes
- **The tribes across the Jordan** — they had kept their word (Num 32; Josh 1:12–18) and return with their share of the spoil.
- **The altar** — built by the Jordan, not for sacrifice, as a witness that those across the river have a share in Yahweh.
- **War turned aside** (v12) — the congregation assembles for war and first sends Phinehas and ten chieftains; the answer ends the matter.
- **Phinehas** — the priest of Num 25:7–13, when the plague of Peor (v17) was stopped.
- **One altar** (v19, 29) — the altar before the dwelling of Yahweh, then at Shiloh; so Deut 12:5–14; Lev 17:8–9.
