# Joshua 4

[1] When all the nation had finished crossing the Jordan, Yahweh said to Joshua,

[2] "Take twelve men from the people, one man from each tribe,

[3] and command them, 'Take twelve stones from here, from the middle of the Jordan, from the place where the priests' feet stood firm, and carry them over with you, and leave them at the lodging place where you will lodge tonight.'"

[4] Joshua called the twelve men he had appointed from the children of Israel, one man from each tribe,

[5] and Joshua said to them, "Pass over before the ark of Yahweh your *Elohim* into the middle of the Jordan, and each of you lift up a stone on his shoulder — according to the number of the tribes of the children of Israel —

[6] that this may be a sign among you. When your children ask in time to come, 'What do these stones mean to you?',

[7] then you shall tell them, 'The waters of the Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant of Yahweh; when it passed over the Jordan, the waters of the Jordan were cut off.' These stones shall be for the children of Israel a memorial forever."

[8] The children of Israel did so, as Joshua commanded; they took up twelve stones out of the middle of the Jordan, as Yahweh had said to Joshua, according to the number of the tribes of the children of Israel. They carried them over with them to the lodging place and laid them down there.

[9] And Joshua set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, in the place where the feet of the priests who bore the ark of the covenant had stood; and they are there to this day.

[10] The priests who bore the ark stood in the middle of the Jordan until everything was finished that Yahweh commanded Joshua to speak to the people, according to all that Moses had commanded Joshua. And the people hurried and crossed over.

[11] When all the people had finished crossing over, the ark of Yahweh passed on before the people, with the priests.

[12] The children of Reuben, the children of Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over armed before the children of Israel, as Moses had told them.

[13] About forty thousand equipped for war crossed over before Yahweh to battle, to the plains of Jericho.

[14] On that day Yahweh exalted Joshua in the sight of all Israel, and they stood in awe of him, as they had stood in awe of Moses, all the days of his life.

[15] Yahweh said to Joshua,

[16] "Command the priests who bear the ark of the Testimony to come up out of the Jordan."

[17] So Joshua commanded the priests, "Come up out of the Jordan."

[18] When the priests who bore the ark of the covenant of Yahweh came up out of the middle of the Jordan, and the soles of the priests' feet were lifted up onto the dry ground, the waters of the Jordan returned to their place and flowed over all its banks, as before.

[19] The people came up out of the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month, and they encamped in Gilgal, on the eastern edge of Jericho.

[20] Those twelve stones that they took out of the Jordan, Joshua set up at Gilgal.

[21] He said to the children of Israel, "When your children ask their fathers in time to come, saying, 'What do these stones mean?',

[22] then you shall let your children know, 'Israel crossed over this Jordan on dry ground.'

[23] For Yahweh your *Elohim* dried up the waters of the Jordan from before you until you crossed over, as Yahweh your *Elohim* did to the Sea of Reeds, which he dried up from before us until we crossed over,

[24] that all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of Yahweh is mighty, and that you may fear Yahweh your *Elohim* forever."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"hasted" → "hurried"** (v10) — archaic.
- **"feared him" → "stood in awe of him"** (v14) — Hebrew יָרְאוּ *yar'u*. Reverence, not terror.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v23).

### Notes
- **Twelve-stone memorial** — the most prominent aspect of the crossing. The stones are meant to prompt intergenerational questions (v6, 21). Central to Deuteronomic pedagogy: catechesis by monument.
- **Two stone piles** — (1) twelve stones *from* the Jordan, set at Gilgal (v8, 20); (2) twelve stones placed *in* the Jordan bed where the priests stood (v9). Two witnesses to the event.
- **Dated crossing** (v19) — 10th day of 1st month — exactly four days before Passover. Matches timing for the firstborn-sparing Passover lamb to be selected (Ex 12:3). Passover will be celebrated at Gilgal.
- **"Dried up like the Red Sea"** (v23) — explicit parallel. The generation that crossed the Red Sea mostly died in the wilderness; their children cross the Jordan. Exodus begins and ends with miraculous water-crossings.
- **Joshua exalted** (v14) — fulfillment of 3:7. The succession is validated by repeating the exodus-miracle.
- **"That all the peoples of the earth may know"** (v24) — missional dimension. The crossing is not merely for Israel's benefit; it has international witness-value.
