## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"hasted" → "hurried"** (v10) — archaic.
- **"feared him" → "stood in awe of him"** (v14) — Hebrew יָרְאוּ *yar'u*. Reverence, not terror.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v23).

### Notes
- **Twelve-stone memorial** — the most prominent aspect of the crossing. The stones are meant to prompt intergenerational questions (v6, 21). Central to Deuteronomic pedagogy: catechesis by monument.
- **Two stone piles** — (1) twelve stones *from* the Jordan, set at Gilgal (v8, 20); (2) twelve stones placed *in* the Jordan bed where the priests stood (v9). Two witnesses to the event.
- **Dated crossing** (v19) — 10th day of 1st month — exactly four days before Passover. Matches timing for the firstborn-sparing Passover lamb to be selected (Ex 12:3). Passover will be celebrated at Gilgal.
- **"Dried up like the Red Sea"** (v23) — explicit parallel. The generation that crossed the Red Sea mostly died in the wilderness; their children cross the Jordan. Exodus begins and ends with miraculous water-crossings.
- **Joshua exalted** (v14) — fulfillment of 3:7. The succession is validated by repeating the exodus-miracle.
- **"That all the peoples of the earth may know"** (v24) — missional dimension. The crossing is not merely for Israel's benefit; it has international witness-value.
