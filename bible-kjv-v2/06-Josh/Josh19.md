## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Ramath of the south" → "Ramah of the Negev"** (v8).
- **"Japho" → "Joppa"** (v46) — Hebrew יָפוֹ *Yafo* / Greek *Ioppe*. Modern Jaffa.
- **"the strong city Tyre" → "the fortified city of Tyre"** (v29).
- **"Zidon" → "Sidon"** (v28) — standard English.
- **"Leshem" renamed "Dan"** (v47) — same event told in Judges 18, where the city is called Laish.
- **"fenced cities" → "fortified cities"** (v35).

### Notes
- **Simeon absorbed in Judah** (v1, 9) — Simeon's territory is an enclave within Judah. Fulfills Jacob's prophecy (Gen 49:7) that Simeon would be "scattered in Israel." Simeon soon disappears as a distinct political unit.
- **Zebulun and Issachar in Galilee** — their portions in the Jezreel valley region.
- **Asher** — northwestern coastline, near Phoenicia. Overlaps with Sidonian/Tyrian territory, which Israel does not fully control.
- **Naphtali** — upper Galilee, east of Asher.
- **Dan's migration** (v47) — originally assigned coastal area near the Philistines; could not hold it; migrated north and captured Leshem/Laish, renaming it Dan. Judg 18 narrates in detail. Dan becomes the northernmost town of Israel (hence "from Dan to Beersheba").
- **Joshua's inheritance** (v49–50) — Timnath-serah in Ephraim. Joshua is Ephraimite (Num 13:8).
- **Book's literary structure** — seven lots (Benjamin, Simeon, Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, Dan). Seven is covenantal-completion number.
