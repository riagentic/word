## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. In the lists of towns "and" stands exactly where the Hebrew has it. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"families" → "clans"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחוֹת. "Tribe" is מַטֶּה (literally "staff") in every place in this chapter.
- **"the second lot came forth to Simeon" → "the second lot came out for Simeon"** (v1, 17, 24, 32, 40) — Hebrew יָצָא, "go out"; in v10 the Hebrew has וַיַּעַל, "came up". **"within the inheritance" → "in the midst of the inheritance"** (v1, 9) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ.
- **"Beer-sheba, and Sheba" → "Beersheba and Sheba"** (v2) — Hebrew וְשֶׁבַע, "and Sheba"; "or Sheba" is not what the Hebrew says. **Counts and names** — the Hebrew numbers are kept as they stand. In v2–6 the list has fourteen names, the sum "thirteen"; in v15 five names follow the border, the sum "twelve"; in v35–38 there are sixteen names, the sum "nineteen". In v22 the sum "sixteen" agrees with the list if the three names of the border in that verse are counted with it; in v25–30 the sum "twenty-two" can be reached in more than one way. How each sum was reckoned is not said.
- **"Ramath of the south" → "Ramah of the Negev"** (v8) — Hebrew רָאמַת נֶגֶב.
- **"Out of the portion of the children of Judah … for the part of the children of Judah was too much for them" → "From the portion of the sons of Judah … for the share of the sons of Judah was too large for them"** (v9) — two Hebrew words: חֶבֶל "portion" (literally a measuring "cord") and חֵלֶק "share". **"had their inheritance" → "inherited"** — Hebrew וַיִּנְחֲלוּ.
- **"went up … reached … turned … went out … compasseth" → "goes up … touches … turns back … goes out … turns about"** (v11–14, 22, 26–29, 34) — the verbs of a border, one English verb for each Hebrew verb, as in Num 34 and Josh 15: עָלָה "go up", יָצָא "go out", עָבַר "pass on", נָסַב "turn", פָּגַע בְּ "touch" (literally "meet, come upon"), שָׁב "turn back" (v12, 27, 29, 34). The Hebrew uses its descriptive forms, not the narrative past; they are rendered by the English present, and "was / were" stands where the Hebrew has the narrative form (v10, 18, 25, 33, 41). תּוֹצָאוֹת is "outgoings": where the border comes out, its end. In v29 the written text has ויהיו, the reading tradition וְהָיוּ; the meaning is the same.
- **"toward the sea, and Maralah" → "westward and to Maralah"** (v11) — Hebrew לַיָּמָּה וּמַרְעֲלָה; "sea" is the west. **"the river that is before Jokneam" → "the wadi that is facing Jokneam"** — Hebrew נַחַל; עַל־פְּנֵי, literally "upon the face of". The second "touches" has אֶל, "to".
- **"eastward toward the sunrising unto the border of Chisloth-tabor" → "eastward, toward the rising of the sun, to the border of Chisloth-tabor"** (v12) — Hebrew קֵדְמָה מִזְרַח הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ, two expressions for the east ("frontward", "the rising of the sun"); עַל־גְּבוּל, literally "upon the border of". "Daberath" has the article.
- **"to Remmon-methoar to Neah" → "to Rimmon-methoar, to Neah"** (v13) — Hebrew רִמּוֹן הַמְּתֹאָר הַנֵּעָה. הַמְּתֹאָר is commonly taken not as part of the name but as a form of the verb תָּאַר "bend" (15:9, 11; 18:14, 17): "Rimmon, which bends to Neah". The name is left in its usual form; which is right is not certain. "Gath-hepher" and "Eth-kazin" stand in the Hebrew with the ending of direction ("to Gath-hepher, to Eth-kazin").
- **"the border compasseth it on the north side to Hannathon" → "the border turns about it from the north of Hannathon"** (v14) — Hebrew וְנָסַב אֹתוֹ הַגְּבוּל מִצְּפוֹן חַנָּתֹן; "on the north, to Hannathon" is also possible. **"valley"** (v14, 27) — גַּיְא, a narrow valley.
- **"their border was toward Jezreel" → "their border was to Jezreel"** (v18) — Hebrew יִזְרְעֶאלָה, with the ending of direction. "Chesulloth" (v18) and "Rabbith" (v20) have the article.
- **"Shahazimah"** (v22) — the reading tradition (שַׁחֲצִימָה); the written text has שחצומה, "Shahazumah", the form the name keeps here.
- **"reacheth to Carmel westward, and to Shihor-libnath" → "touches Carmel westward, and Shihor-libnath"** (v26) — Hebrew וּפָגַע בְּ.
- **"turneth toward the sunrising … on the left hand" → "turns back toward the rising of the sun … on the north"** (v27) — Hebrew מִשְּׂמֹאל, literally "from the left", which is the north for one who faces the sunrise. How "Beth-emek and Neiel" join the sentence is not said in the Hebrew.
- **"great Zidon" → "Great Sidon"** (v28) — Hebrew צִידוֹן רַבָּה. "Ebron" is the reading of the Leningrad Codex (עֶבְרֹן).
- **"the strong city Tyre" → "the fortified city of Tyre"** (v29) — Hebrew עִיר מִבְצַר־צֹר, "the city of the fortification of Tyre". **"from the coast to Achzib" → "from the region to Achzib"** — Hebrew מֵחֶבֶל אַכְזִיבָה; חֶבֶל is a measured stretch of land (the "portion" of v9); the phrase is obscure, and some read a place name in it.
- **"from Allon to Zaanannim" → "from the oak in Zaanannim"** (v33) — Hebrew מֵאֵלוֹן בְּצַעֲנַנִּים; אֵלוֹן is a great tree, "oak" (the kind is not certain). **"the outgoings thereof were at Jordan"** — here the Hebrew has the narrative form (וַיְהִי).
- **"and to Judah upon Jordan toward the sunrising" → "and Judah, the Jordan, toward the rising of the sun"** (v34) — Hebrew וּבִיהוּדָה הַיַּרְדֵּן מִזְרַח הַשָּׁמֶשׁ, "and (it touches) Judah, the Jordan, at the rising of the sun". How Naphtali's border touches Judah is not explained; the words are translated as they stand.
- **"the fenced cities" → "the fortified cities"** (v35) — Hebrew עָרֵי מִבְצָר. "Ziddim" has the article; "Ramah" (v29, 36) also.
- **"with the border before Japho" → "with the border over against Joppa"** (v46) — Hebrew מוּל יָפוֹ. "Me-jarkon" is "the waters of the Jarkon".
- **"the coast of the children of Dan went out too little for them" → "the border of the sons of Dan went out from them"** (v47) — Hebrew וַיֵּצֵא גְבוּל־בְּנֵי־דָן מֵהֶם, literally "went out from them": it was lost to them, or it went out beyond them. "Too little" and "too narrow" are not in the Hebrew. **"to fight against Leshem, and took it, and smote it … and possessed it" → "fought with Leshem and captured it and struck it … and took possession of it"** — Hebrew לָכַד, הִכָּה, יָרַשׁ. "With the edge of the sword" is literally "to the mouth of the sword". The same event is told in Judg 18, where the city is called Laish. **"Dan their father"** — Hebrew אֲבִיהֶם.
- **"When they had made an end of dividing the land for inheritance by their coasts" → "And they finished inheriting the land by its borders"** (v49) — Hebrew לִנְחֹל, the simple stem "inherit" (as Num 34:17, 18). **"among them" → "in their midst"** — Hebrew בְּתוֹכָם.
- **"According to the word of the LORD" → "At the mouth of Yahweh"** (v50) — Hebrew עַל־פִּי יְהוָה. **"which he asked" → "that he asked"**. **"mount Ephraim" → "the hill country of Ephraim"** — הַר of a region. **"he built the city"** — Hebrew וַיִּבְנֶה; "rebuilt" is not said.
- **"which … divided for an inheritance by lot" → "that … made to be inherited by lot"** (v51) — Hebrew נִחֲלוּ, the intensive stem of נָחַל, "make inherit", as 13:32; 14:1. **"the heads of the fathers of the tribes"** — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת לְמַטּוֹת. **"at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "at the entrance of the tent of meeting"** — Hebrew פֶּתַח אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"made an end of dividing the country" → "finished dividing the land"** — Hebrew מֵחַלֵּק, the verb of חֵלֶק "share".

### Notes
- **Simeon within Judah** (v1, 9) — Simeon's inheritance lies in the midst of Judah's. Compare Gen 49:7.
- **Zebulun and Issachar** — their inheritances lie in the region of the valley of Jezreel.
- **Asher** — the north-western coast, toward Tyre and Sidon.
- **Naphtali** — upper Galilee, east of Asher.
- **Dan** (v47) — Judg 18 tells the taking of Leshem (Laish) at length. Dan becomes the northernmost town of Israel (hence "from Dan to Beersheba").
- **Joshua's inheritance** (v49–50) — Timnath-serah in Ephraim. Joshua is of Ephraim (Num 13:8).
- **Seven lots** — Benjamin, Simeon, Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, Dan (18:11–19:48).
