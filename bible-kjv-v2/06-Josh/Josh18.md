# Joshua 18

[1] The whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled at Shiloh and set up the Tent of Meeting there; and the land was subdued before them.

[2] But there remained among the children of Israel seven tribes whose inheritance had not yet been apportioned.

[3] Joshua said to the children of Israel, "How long will you be slack to go in and possess the land that Yahweh the *Elohim* of your fathers has given you?

[4] Appoint three men from each tribe, and I will send them out. They shall rise and go through the land, describing it according to their inheritances; then they shall come back to me.

[5] They shall divide it into seven portions. Judah shall stay in its territory in the south, and the house of Joseph shall stay in its territory in the north.

[6] You shall describe the land in seven portions and bring the description here to me; and I will cast lots for you here before Yahweh our *Elohim*.

[7] The Levites have no portion among you, for the priesthood of Yahweh is their inheritance; and Gad and Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance beyond the Jordan on the east, which Moses the servant of Yahweh gave them."

[8] The men rose and went out, and Joshua charged those who went to describe the land, saying, "Go and walk through the land, describe it, and come back to me. I will cast lots for you here before Yahweh in Shiloh."

[9] So the men went and passed through the land, described it by cities in seven portions in a scroll, and returned to Joshua at the camp at Shiloh.

[10] Joshua cast lots for them in Shiloh before Yahweh; and there Joshua divided the land to the children of Israel according to their divisions.

[11] The lot for the tribe of the children of Benjamin came up by their clans; the territory of their lot came out between the children of Judah and the children of Joseph.

[12] Their border on the north side began at the Jordan; the border went up to the slope north of Jericho, then up through the hill country westward, ending at the wilderness of Beth-aven.

[13] From there the border crossed over toward Luz, to the southern slope of Luz (that is, Bethel); the border went down to Ataroth-addar, to the hill south of Lower Beth-horon.

[14] Then the border bent around the west side southward, from the hill that lies south of Beth-horon; it ended at Kiriath-baal (that is, Kiriath-jearim), a city of the children of Judah. This was the western side.

[15] The southern side began at the outskirts of Kiriath-jearim, and the border went out on the west, and went to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah.

[16] The border went down to the edge of the mountain facing the Valley of Ben-hinnom, which is in the Valley of Rephaim on the north; it went down to the Valley of Hinnom, to the south slope of the Jebusite, and descended to En-rogel.

[17] It turned north and went on to En-shemesh, and continued to Geliloth, which is opposite the ascent of Adummim; it descended to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben.

[18] It passed along to the northern slope of Beth-arabah and went down to the Arabah;

[19] then the border passed along to the north slope of Beth-hoglah, and it ended at the north bay of the Salt Sea, at the south end of the Jordan. This was the southern border.

[20] The Jordan was its border on the east side. This was the inheritance of the children of Benjamin, according to the boundaries around it, by their clans.

[21] The cities of the tribe of the children of Benjamin, by their clans, were: Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz,

[22] Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel,

[23] Avvim, Parah, Ophrah,

[24] Chephar-ammoni, Ophni, and Geba — twelve cities with their villages.

[25] Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth,

[26] Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah,

[27] Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah,

[28] Zela, Eleph, the Jebusite (that is, Jerusalem), Gibeath, and Kiriath — fourteen cities with their villages. This is the inheritance of the children of Benjamin according to their clans.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "Tent of Meeting"** (v1).
- **"valley of the giants" → "Valley of Rephaim"** (v16).
- **"Jebusi" → "the Jebusite"** (v16, 28) — gentilic.
- **"south coast" → "southern border"** (v19).

### Notes
- **Shiloh becomes the center** (v1) — the Tent of Meeting moves from Gilgal to Shiloh. Shiloh remains the sanctuary-center until the ark is captured by Philistines (1 Sam 4).
- **Seven tribes unsettled** (v2) — Judah and Joseph (Ephraim/half-Manasseh) already settled. Seven remaining: Benjamin, Simeon, Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, Dan.
- **Benjamin's inheritance** — small but strategically placed between Judah and Joseph. Contains Jerusalem (officially), though it will remain Jebusite until David.
- **Bethel on the border** — Bethel in Benjamin (this chapter), but functionally part of the northern kingdom later. Jeroboam's golden-calf shrine at Bethel becomes a northern-kingdom sanctuary (1 Kgs 12:29).
