## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"children of" → "sons of"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"And these"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew וְאֵלֶּה; the "and" is kept.
- **"which the children of Israel smote, and possessed their land on the other side Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from the river Arnon unto mount Hermon, and all the plain on the east" → "whom the sons of Israel struck down, and they took possession of their land across the Jordan, toward the rising of the sun, from the wadi Arnon as far as Mount Hermon, and all the Arabah eastward"** (v1) — the words of Deut 4:47–49 in the same English: הִכָּה "strike down", יָרַשׁ "take possession of", בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן "across the Jordan" (here the east side; in v7 the same words are the west side), מִזְרְחָה הַשָּׁמֶשׁ "toward the rising of the sun", נַחַל "wadi", הָעֲרָבָה, the name of the Jordan rift.
- **"Sihon king of the Amorites, who dwelt in Heshbon, and ruled from Aroer, which is upon the bank of the river Arnon, and from the middle of the river, and from half Gilead, even unto the river Jabbok, which is the border of the children of Ammon" → "Sihon king of the Amorite, who dwelt in Heshbon, ruling from Aroer, which is on the edge of the wadi Arnon, and the middle of the wadi, and half of Gilead, and as far as the Jabbok, the wadi, the border of the sons of Ammon"** (v2) — the words of Deut 2:36; 3:16. הָאֱמֹרִי is a singular with the article; מֹשֵׁל, a participle, "ruling" (so v5); "from" stands only before Aroer: "the middle of the wadi" and "half of Gilead" are set beside it without a preposition, as the Hebrew has them (וְתוֹךְ הַנַּחַל: the middle of the stream bed as the line, Deut 3:16). שְׂפַת, literally "lip". "Gilead" has the article.
- **"from the plain to the sea of Chinneroth on the east, and unto the sea of the plain, even the salt sea on the east, the way to Beth-jeshimoth; and from the south, under Ashdoth-pisgah" → "the Arabah as far as the sea of Chinneroth eastward, and as far as the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, eastward, by the way of Beth-jeshimoth, and on the south under the slopes of the Pisgah"** (v3) — the words of Deut 3:17; 4:49. דֶּרֶךְ בֵּית הַיְשִׁמוֹת, "the way of Beth-jeshimoth": in the direction of it. מִתֵּימָן, literally "from Teman", the south. אַשְׁדּוֹת "slopes" (the sense is not certain); "the Pisgah" has the article. The Salt Sea is the Dead Sea; the sea of Chinneroth is the Sea of Galilee (כִּנְרוֹת here, כִּנֶּרֶת in 13:27).
- **"the coast of Og king of Bashan, which was of the remnant of the giants, that dwelt at Ashtaroth and at Edrei" → "the border of Og king of the Bashan, of the rest of the Rephaim, who dwelt in Ashtaroth and in Edrei"** (v4) — Hebrew גְּבוּל "border", also the land inside it (so v5); it stands here where a second king's name is expected ("and Og …"). מִיֶּתֶר הָרְפָאִים, the words of Deut 3:11; הָרְפָאִים is the name of a people; "the Bashan" has the article.
- **"reigned in mount Hermon … unto the border of the Geshurites and the Maachathites, and half Gilead, the border of Sihon" → "ruling in Mount Hermon … as far as the border of the Geshurite and the Maacathite, and half of Gilead, the border of Sihon"** (v5) — Hebrew מֹשֵׁל בְּ "ruling in"; the peoples are singulars with the article (Deut 3:14).
- **"Them did Moses the servant of the LORD and the children of Israel smite … gave it for a possession unto the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh" → "Moses the servant of Yahweh and the sons of Israel struck them down … gave it as a possession to the Reubenite and to the Gadite and to the half of the tribe of Manasseh"** (v6) — Hebrew יְרֻשָּׁה "possession" (Deut 2:5; 3:20; so v7), from יָרַשׁ (v1); אֲחֻזָּה has the same English. שֵׁבֶט "tribe" (literally "rod"); "Manasseh" has the article. עֶבֶד is "slave"; of one who serves Yahweh, "servant".
- **"on this side Jordan on the west, from Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon even unto the mount Halak, that goeth up to Seir; which Joshua gave unto the tribes of Israel for a possession" → "across the Jordan westward, from Baal-gad in the plain of the Lebanon and as far as Mount Halak, which goes up to Seir; and Joshua gave it to the tribes of Israel as a possession"** (v7) — the words of 11:17, 23, from north to south here. יָמָּה, literally "seaward"; בִּקְעָה "plain"; וַיִּתְּנָהּ, a main clause.
- **"In the mountains, and in the valleys, and in the plains, and in the springs, and in the wilderness, and in the south country" → "in the hill country and in the Shephelah and in the Arabah and in the slopes and in the wilderness and in the Negev"** (v8) — the regions by their names (10:40; 11:16); אֲשֵׁדוֹת "slopes" (v3). The six peoples are singulars with the article, and "and" stands where the Hebrew has it (Deut 20:17).
- **"the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel" → "the king of Ai, which is at the side of Bethel"** (v9) — Hebrew מִצַּד. "Ai" has the article (הָעַי, "the heap of ruins").
- **"one"** (v9–24) — Hebrew אֶחָד after each king, as in a tally.
- **"the king of Lasharon"** (v18) — Hebrew מֶלֶךְ לַשָּׁרוֹן. The form is "to / of the Sharon"; it may be the name of a town (so the Masoretic tally, which counts this king as one) or belong to Aphek before it, "Aphek of the Sharon". The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Shimron-meron"** (v20) — written שִׁמְרוֹן מְראוֹן; in 11:1 the town is Shimron and the king of Madon is named beside it.
- **"Jokneam of Carmel … Dor in the coast of Dor … the nations of Gilgal" → "Jokneam of the Carmel … Dor of Naphath-dor … Goiim of Gilgal"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew לַכַּרְמֶל, לְנָפַת דּוֹר, לְגִלְגָּל: לְ "belonging to", the district in which the town lies. נָפַת דּוֹר is a singular here (11:2 has the plural); the meaning of נָפָה is not certain ("height"?). גּוֹיִם *Goyim* is the word "nations": "the king of nations of Gilgal", or the name of a people or place (Gen 14:1). The Septuagint has "Galilee" for Gilgal here (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"all the kings thirty and one" → "All the kings: thirty-one"** (v24) — Hebrew כָּל־מְלָכִים שְׁלֹשִׁים וְאֶחָד, "all kings, thirty and one"; the list has thirty-one names.

### Notes
- **Summary of conquest** — ch 12 is a list of the kings struck down: two across the Jordan eastward (Sihon, Og; Num 21; Deut 2–3) and thirty-one westward.
- **Geographic range** (v7–8) — from north (Baal-gad, under Mount Hermon) to south (Mount Halak, which goes up to Seir), as 11:17.
- **Structure of Joshua** — chs 1–12 tell of the taking of the land; chs 13–22 of its division; chs 23–24 are Joshua's last words and the covenant at Shechem.
- **Kings not told of before** — several of these towns (Geder, Adullam, Tappuah, Hepher, Aphek, Taanach, Megiddo, Kedesh, Jokneam, Tirzah) are not named in chs 6–11.
