## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"giants" → "Rephaim"** (v4) — as elsewhere.
- **"nations of Gilgal" → "Goiim in Gilgal"** (v23) — Hebrew גּוֹיִם *Goyim*. Possibly a place-name or "nations." LXX: "Galilee of the Gentiles."
- **"coast of Dor" → "Naphath-dor"** (v23) — proper name / region.

### Notes
- **Summary of conquest** — ch 12 is a king-list of conquests: two Transjordan kings (Sihon, Og) + thirty-one west of Jordan = thirty-three total.
- **Geographic range** (v7–8) — spans the entire land from north (Baal-gad, near Mt Hermon) to south (Mt Halak, leading to Seir/Edom).
- **Structure of Joshua** — chs 1–12 describe conquest; chs 13–22 distribute the land; chs 23–24 are Joshua's farewell and covenant-renewal.
- **Cities identified with archaeological sites** — many of these names (Megiddo, Taanach, Lachish, Hazor) are well-identified and excavated. Their destruction layers have been used to debate conquest chronology.
- **"One" formulaic** — each king listed with "one" (Heb *echad*), a scribal enumeration pattern also found in Egyptian king-lists.
