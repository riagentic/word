## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"the flood" → "the River"** (v2, 3, 14, 15) — Hebrew *ha-nahar*. The Euphrates, not Noah's flood. KJV archaic.
- **"in sincerity and in truth" → "in sincerity and in faithfulness"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּתָמִים וּבֶאֱמֶת *be-tamim u-ve-emet*.
- **"the men of Jericho" → "the lords of Jericho"** (v11) — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי יְרִיחוֹ *ba'alei Yericho*. The ruling nobles.
- **"God forbid" → "Far be it from us"** (v16) — idiomatic.
- **"book of the law of God" → "book of the Torah of *Elohim*"** (v26).
- **"overlived" → "survived"** (v31) — archaic.
- **"a jealous God" → "a jealous El"** (v19) — Hebrew אֵל קַנּוֹא *El qanno*; *El*, not *Elohim* (the "holy *Elohim*" just before it is אֱלֹהִים).

### Notes
- **Shechem covenant ceremony** — a second, more solemn farewell. Shechem: the site where Abraham first entered the land (Gen 12:6–7), where Jacob built an altar (Gen 33:20), where Joseph's bones are buried. Deep-memory location.
- **Historical recital** (v2–13) — Yahweh-narrates salvation history in the first person. Six chapters of Torah compressed. Structured like ancient Near Eastern covenant preambles.
- **"Your fathers served other gods"** (v2, 14) — remarkable: Abraham's origins in polytheism. Jewish tradition fills in the gap: Abraham recognized the one true God while still in Ur.
- **"Choose this day"** (v15) — pivotal covenantal choice. The famous "as for me and my house" speech. Classic statement of covenantal commitment.
- **"He will not forgive"** (v19) — shocking pastoral theology. Joshua warns that Yahweh's forgiveness-capacity is not to be presumed. Not a denial of Yahweh's mercy but a warning against covenant frivolity.
- **Stone-witness** (v26–27) — under the oak, by the sanctuary. Same location as Jacob's oak (Gen 35:4). Recurring covenant-spot.
- **Closure of Joshua** (v29–33):
  - Joshua dies at 110.
  - Joseph's bones finally buried (fulfilling Gen 50:25, Ex 13:19).
  - Eleazar dies.
- **"Servant of Yahweh"** (v29) — Joshua finally receives Moses' title. Earlier called "attendant" (1:1).
- **Joseph's bones** — traveled 400+ years in Egypt, 40+ in the wilderness, finally interred in the land. Fulfillment of oath.
