# Joshua 8

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "Do not fear and do not be dismayed. Take with you all the people of war, and arise, go up to Ai. See, I have given into your hand the king of Ai and his people and his city and his land.

[2] And you shall do to Ai and to its king as you did to ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ and to its king; only its spoil and its beasts you shall plunder for yourselves. Set for yourself an ambush for the city, behind it."

[3] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ arose, and all the people of war, to go up to Ai; and ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ chose thirty thousand men, the mighty ones of ability, and sent them by night.

[4] And he commanded them, saying, "See, you are lying in ambush for the city, behind the city. Do not go very far from the city, and be, all of you, ready.

[5] And I and all the people who are with me will come near to the city; and it shall be, when they come out to meet us as at the first, that we will flee before them.

[6] And they will come out after us until we have drawn them out from the city, for they will say, 'They are fleeing before us as at the first.' And we will flee before them.

[7] And you, you shall rise from the ambush and take possession of the city, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will give it into your hand.

[8] And it shall be, when you have seized the city, that you shall set the city on fire; according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ you shall do. See, I have commanded you."

[9] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ sent them, and they went to the place of ambush and sat between ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and Ai, west of Ai; and ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ lodged that night in the midst of the people.

[10] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ rose early in the morning and numbered the people, and he went up, he and the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, before the people to Ai.

[11] And all the people of war who were with him went up and drew near and came in front of the city, and they camped north of Ai, and the valley was between him and Ai.

[12] And he took about five thousand men and set them as an ambush between ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ and Ai, west of the city.

[13] And the people set all the camp that was north of the city, and its heel west of the city; and ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ went that night into the midst of the valley.

[14] And it came to pass, when the king of Ai saw this, that they hastened and rose early, and the men of the city went out to meet ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ for battle, he and all his people, at the appointed time, before the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧; and he did not know that there was an ambush for him behind the city.

[15] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were struck before them, and they fled by the way of the wilderness.

[16] And all the people who were in Ai were called out to pursue after them; and they pursued after ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and were drawn out from the city.

[17] And there was not a man left in Ai and ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ who did not go out after ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and they left the city open and pursued after ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[18] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "Stretch out with the javelin that is in your hand toward Ai, for into your hand I will give it." And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ stretched out with the javelin that was in his hand toward the city.

[19] And the ambush rose quickly from its place, and they ran when he stretched out his hand, and they came into the city and captured it; and they hastened and set the city on fire.

[20] And the men of Ai turned behind them and saw, and behold, the smoke of the city went up toward the heavens, and there were no hands in them to flee this way or that way; and the people that was fleeing to the wilderness turned upon the pursuer.

[21] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ saw that the ambush had captured the city and that the smoke of the city went up, and they turned back and struck down the men of Ai.

[22] And these came out from the city to meet them, and they were in the midst for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, these on this side and these on that side; and they struck them down until no survivor and no fugitive was left to him.

[23] And the king of Ai they seized alive, and they brought him near to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧.

[24] And it came to pass, when ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had finished killing all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, in the wilderness in which they pursued them, and they had all fallen by the edge of the sword until they came to an end, that all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ returned to Ai and struck it with the edge of the sword.

[25] And all who fell on that day, from man to woman, were twelve thousand, all the men of Ai.

[26] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ did not bring back his hand, which he had stretched out with the javelin, until he had devoted to destruction all the inhabitants of Ai.

[27] Only the beasts and the spoil of that city ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ plundered for themselves, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ which he commanded ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧.

[28] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ burned Ai and made it an everlasting mound, a desolation, to this day.

[29] And the king of Ai he hanged on the tree until the time of evening; and at the going in of the sun ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ commanded, and they took down his dead body from the tree and threw it at the entrance of the gate of the city, and they raised up over him a great heap of stones, to this day.

[30] Then ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ built an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, on Mount ⟦ebal|Eival|Ebal⟧,

[31] as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, as it is written in the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧: an altar of whole stones, over which no one had waved iron; and they offered up on it burnt offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and sacrificed peace offerings.

[32] And he wrote there on the stones a copy of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, which he wrote before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[33] And all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and its elders and officers and its judges were standing on this side and on that side of the ark, in front of the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, who carried the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as the sojourner so the native, half of it toward the front of Mount Gerizim and the half of it toward the front of Mount ⟦ebal|Eival|Ebal⟧, as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded, to bless the people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ at the first.

[34] And afterward he read all the words of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, the blessing and the curse, according to all that is written in the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧.

[35] There was not a word of all that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded which ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ did not read in front of all the assembly of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the women and the little ones and the sojourner who went in their midst.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept. **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of וַ / וְ is kept.
- **"Fear not, neither be thou dismayed"** (v1) — as Deut 1:21; 31:8. **"the people of war"** (v1, 3, 11) — Hebrew עַם הַמִּלְחָמָה; in v11 both nouns have the article ("the people, the war"). **"Ai"** — Hebrew הָעַי, with the article.
- **"the cattle thereof, shall ye take for a prey" → "its beasts you shall plunder"** (v2, 27) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה and בָּזַז. "You shall do" is singular, "you shall plunder for yourselves" plural.
- **"lay thee an ambush" / "liers in wait" → "ambush"** (v2, 4, 7, 12, 14, 19, 21) — Hebrew אֹרֵב, "one lying in ambush", used of the whole body; in v9 מַאְרָב, "the place of ambush".
- **"mighty men of valour" → "the mighty ones of ability"** (v3) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹרֵי הַחַיִל, as 6:2.
- **"seize upon the city" → "take possession of the city"** (v7) — Hebrew הוֹרִישׁ, the causative of "possess": it is also "dispossess" (those in it). **"taken" → "seized"** (v8, 23) — Hebrew תָּפַשׂ; "capture" (v19, 21) is לָכַד.
- **"abode" → "sat"** (v9) — Hebrew יָשַׁב "sit, dwell". **"on the west side of" → "west of"**, **"on the north side of" → "north of"** (v9, 11, 12, 13) — "west" is literally "from the sea".
- **"numbered"** (v10) — Hebrew פָּקַד: muster, review.
- **"a valley" → "the valley"** (v11, 13) — two Hebrew words: הַגַּי in v11, a narrow valley, and הָעֵמֶק in v13; English has one word. **"the servant of Yahweh"** (v31, 33) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word for "slave". **"between him and Ai"** — written and read forms differ only in spelling.
- **"their liers in wait" → "its heel"** (v13) — Hebrew עֲקֵבוֹ, literally "its heel": the hindmost part, the body set behind the city (v12). **"went"** — so the Masoretic Text; "lodged" (as v9) differs by one letter and is often read here.
- **"at a time appointed" kept** (v14) — Hebrew לַמּוֹעֵד: an appointment of time or of place; "to the appointed place" is also read. **"before the plain" → "before the Arabah"** — Hebrew הָעֲרָבָה, the dry valley of the Jordan. "Saw" has no object in the Hebrew; "this" is supplied. The verbs keep the Hebrew order: "they hastened and rose early and the men of the city went out".
- **"made as if they were beaten" → "were struck"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּגְעוּ, passive of "touch, strike": they let themselves be driven back; that it was feigned is told by v5–6, not by the verb.
- **"in Ai"** (v16) — the reading tradition; the written text has "in the city". **"were called together" → "were called out"** — Hebrew וַיִּזָּעֲקוּ, from "cry out". **"drawn away" → "drawn out"** (v6, 16) — Hebrew נָתַק "tear away, pull off" (4:18).
- **"spear" → "javelin"** (v18, 26) — Hebrew כִּידוֹן *kidon*; the kind of weapon is not certain (javelin, or a curved sword). The Hebrew is "stretch out with the javelin".
- **"they had no power to flee" → "there were no hands in them to flee"** (v20) — Hebrew וְלֹא־הָיָה בָהֶם יָדַיִם, literally so: "hands" is strength, or room on either hand.
- **"so they were in the midst of Israel, some on this side, and some on that side" → "and they were in the midst for Israel, these on this side and these on that side"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ לְיִשְׂרָאֵל בַּתָּוֶךְ. **"they let none of them remain or escape" → "until no survivor and no fugitive was left to him"** — Hebrew עַד־בִּלְתִּי הִשְׁאִיר־לוֹ שָׂרִיד וּפָלִיט, as Num 21:35 with "and fugitive" added; "to him" is singular.
- **"slaying" → "killing"** (v24) — Hebrew הָרַג. **"until they were consumed" → "until they came to an end"** — Hebrew עַד־תֻּמָּם. **"by the edge of the sword … with the edge of the sword"** — one Hebrew phrase, לְפִי־חֶרֶב, literally "to the mouth of the sword".
- **"both of men and women" → "from man to woman"** (v25) — as 6:21. **"all the men of Ai"** — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי.
- **"drew not his hand back" → "did not bring back his hand"** (v26) — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב. **"utterly destroyed" → "devoted to destruction"** — Hebrew הֶחֱרִים, the verb of *cherem* (6:17, 21).
- **"an heap for ever, even a desolation" → "an everlasting mound, a desolation"** (v28) — Hebrew תֵּל־עוֹלָם, as Deut 13:16; תֵּל is the mound of a ruined town, and the name Ai itself means "the ruin".
- **"on a tree" → "on the tree"** (v29) — Hebrew הָעֵץ, with the article: a tree, or a wooden stake. **"as soon as the sun was down" → "at the going in of the sun"**. **"carcase" → "dead body"** — Hebrew נְבֵלָה, as Deut 21:23, which this verse follows. **"over him"** — the Hebrew pronoun is masculine.
- **"Then Joshua built"** (v30) — Hebrew אָז with the imperfect: the time is joined loosely to what goes before.
- **"an altar of whole stones, over which no man hath lift up any iron" → "over which no one had waved iron"** (v31) — the words of Deut 27:5, 6: אֲבָנִים שְׁלֵמוֹת "whole stones", הֵנִיף "wave". "Burnt offerings" and "peace offerings" as Deut 27:6, 7.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v31, 32, 34) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה. **"a copy"** (v32) — Hebrew מִשְׁנֵה, literally "a double, a second" (Deut 17:18). **"in the presence of" → "before"**. Who "wrote" the second time (Moses, or Joshua) is not said.
- **"the priests the Levites"** (v33) — two nouns side by side. **"as well the stranger, as he that was born among them" → "as the sojourner so the native"** — Hebrew כַּגֵּר כָּאֶזְרָח. **"over against" → "toward the front of"** — Hebrew אֶל־מוּל. **"had commanded before, that they should bless" → "commanded, to bless the people Israel at the first"** — Hebrew בָּרִאשֹׁנָה stands last: "at the first" may go with "commanded" or with "bless".
- **"blessings and cursings" → "the blessing and the curse"** (v34) — Hebrew singular; קְלָלָה, as Deut 11:26–29.
- **"the congregation" → "the assembly"** (v35) — Hebrew קְהַל. **"the strangers that were conversant among them" → "the sojourner who went in their midst"** — Hebrew הַגֵּר הַהֹלֵךְ, singular; הָלַךְ is "go, walk".

### Notes
- **The ambush** — in v3–9 thirty thousand men are sent by night; in v12 about five thousand are set between Bethel and Ai. How the two numbers belong together the text does not say.
- **"Stretch out with the javelin"** (v18, 26) — compare the hand of Moses in Exod 17:11, 12.
- **King hanged until evening** (v29) — Deut 21:22, 23: the dead body shall not lodge on the tree.
- **Mount Ebal and Mount Gerizim** (v30–35) — Deut 11:29; 27:2–13. The altar of whole stones, the Torah written on the stones, the people in two halves.
- **Sojourner and native** (v33, 35) — the *ger* stands with the native and hears the reading (Deut 31:12).
- **Place of v30–35** — the Septuagint is cited as placing these verses after 9:2, and a Qumran scroll of Joshua as placing them earlier (from memory); the Masoretic order is kept.
