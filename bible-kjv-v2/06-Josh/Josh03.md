# Joshua 3

[1] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ rose early in the morning, and they set out from Shittim and came as far as the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, he and all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they lodged there before they passed over.

[2] And it came to pass at the end of three days that the officers passed through the midst of the camp,

[3] and they commanded the people, saying, "When you see the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, carrying it, then you, you shall set out from your place and go after it.

[4] Only, there shall be a distance between you and it, about two thousand cubits by measure; do not come near to it, so that you may know the way in which you shall go, for you have not passed over in the way yesterday and the day before."

[5] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to the people, "Sanctify yourselves, for tomorrow ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will do wonderful deeds in your midst."

[6] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to the priests, saying, "Lift up the ark of the covenant and pass over before the people." And they lifted up the ark of the covenant and went before the people.

[7] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "This day I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that they may know that as I was with ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, I will be with you.

[8] And you, you shall command the priests, the carriers of the ark of the covenant, saying, 'When you come as far as the edge of the waters of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, in the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ you shall stand.'"

[9] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "Draw near here and hear the words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[10] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said, "By this you shall know that a living ⟦el|El|God⟧ is in your midst, and he will surely dispossess from before you the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦hivite|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦perizzite|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦girgashite|Girgashi|Girgashite⟧ and the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧.

[11] Behold, the ark of the covenant, the Lord of all the earth, is passing over before you in the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

[12] And now, take for yourselves twelve men from the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, one man for each tribe.

[13] And it shall be, when the soles of the feet of the priests, the carriers of the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the Lord of all the earth, rest in the waters of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, the waters of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ shall be cut off, the waters that come down from above, and they shall stand as one heap."

[14] And it came to pass, when the people set out from their tents to pass over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and the priests, the carriers of the ark of the covenant, were before the people,

[15] and when the carriers of the ark came as far as the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and the feet of the priests, the carriers of the ark, were dipped in the edge of the waters — and the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ is full over all its banks all the days of harvest —

[16] that the waters that come down from above stood; they rose up as one heap, very far off, from Adam, the city that is at the side of ⟦zarethan|Tzartan|Zarethan⟧; and those going down to the sea of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, the Salt Sea, came to an end, were cut off. And the people passed over opposite ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧.

[17] And the priests, the carriers of the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, stood on the dry ground in the midst of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, firmly, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were passing over on the dry ground, until all the nation had come to an end of passing over the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **The titles of the ark** — the Hebrew varies them and they are kept as they stand: "the ark of the covenant of Yahweh your Elohim" (v3), "the ark of the covenant" (v6, 8, 11, 14), "the ark of Yahweh, the Lord of all the earth" (v13), "the ark" (v15), "the ark of the covenant of Yahweh" (v17). The word אֲרוֹן is "chest". In v14 and v17 "the ark" has the article although a genitive follows (הָאָרוֹן הַבְּרִית, הָאָרוֹן בְּרִית־יְהוָה), literally "the ark, the covenant".
- **"removed from Shittim, and came to Jordan" → "set out from Shittim and came as far as the Jordan"** (v1) — Hebrew נָסַע "pull up, set out"; עַד "as far as" (so v8, 15); "Shittim" has the article. **"lodged there before they passed over"** — Hebrew לוּן "lodge"; עָבַר "pass over", the verb of the whole account (v1, 2, 4, 6, 11, 14, 16, 17).
- **"after three days, that the officers went through the host" → "at the end of three days that the officers passed through the midst of the camp"** (v2) — Hebrew מִקְצֵה "from the end of"; בְּקֶרֶב הַמַּחֲנֶה, the words of 1:11.
- **"the priests the Levites bearing it" → "the priests, the Levites, carrying it"** (v3) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם, two nouns side by side, as in Deuteronomy (Deut 17:9; 18:1). **"then ye shall remove" → "then you, you shall set out"** — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם תִּסְעוּ, the pronoun written out.
- **"Yet there shall be a space between you and it" → "Only, there shall be a distance between you and it"** (v4) — Hebrew אַךְ רָחוֹק יִהְיֶה, literally "only, far shall it be". "Between it": the written form (ketiv) and the reading tradition (qere) differ only in spelling. **"come not near unto it"** — Hebrew קָרַב אֶל. "It" may also be read "him" each time: the pronoun is masculine, as "ark" is.
- **"for ye have not passed this way heretofore" → "for you have not passed over in the way yesterday and the day before"** (v4) — Hebrew מִתְּמוֹל שִׁלְשׁוֹם, literally "from yesterday, the day before": before now.
- **"wonders" → "wonderful deeds"** (v5) — Hebrew נִפְלָאוֹת (Exod 3:20); "wonder" is another word (מוֹפֵת). **"among you" → "in your midst"** (v5, 10) — Hebrew בְּקִרְבְּכֶם.
- **"Take up the ark … and pass over … And they took up" → "Lift up the ark … and pass over … And they lifted up"** (v6) — Hebrew נָשָׂא "lift up, carry"; the same verb is "carrying" and "the carriers of" in v3, 8, 13–17.
- **"This day will I begin to magnify thee in the sight of all Israel" → "This day I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel"** (v7) — Hebrew גַּדֶּלְךָ, from גָּדַל "be great"; בְּעֵינֵי. **"so I will be with thee" → "I will be with you"** — Hebrew אֶהְיֶה עִמָּךְ, with no "so"; the words of 1:5.
- **"the priests that bear the ark" → "the priests, the carriers of the ark"** (v8, 13, 14, 15, 17) — Hebrew נֹשְׂאֵי, a participle used as a noun (Deut 31:25). **"When ye are come to the brink of the water of Jordan, ye shall stand still in Jordan" → "When you come as far as the edge of the waters of the Jordan, in the Jordan you shall stand"** — Hebrew קְצֵה, literally "the end of" (so v15); עָמַד "stand", the verb said of the waters in v13, 16; "still" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Come hither" → "Draw near here"** (v9) — Hebrew גֹּשׁוּ, from נָגַשׁ.
- **"the living God is among you, and that he will without fail drive out" → "a living El is in your midst, and he will surely dispossess"** (v10) — Hebrew אֵל חַי *El chay*, without the article; הוֹרֵשׁ יוֹרִישׁ, literally "dispossessing he will dispossess", the causative of יָרַשׁ "possess". The seven peoples are singulars with the article, each joined by "and"; the first five have the object marker.
- **"the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth" → "the ark of the covenant, the Lord of all the earth"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲרוֹן הַבְּרִית אֲדוֹן כָּל־הָאָרֶץ: "the covenant" has the article and a dividing accent, so "the Lord of all the earth" is not joined to it as a genitive but stands beside it. It is commonly read "the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth", as v13 has "the ark of Yahweh, the Lord of all the earth"; the words are left as they stand. אָדוֹן is "lord, master"; הָאָרֶץ is "the earth" or "the land". **"passeth over before you into Jordan" → "is passing over before you in the Jordan"** — Hebrew עֹבֵר, a participle; בַּיַּרְדֵּן "in / into the Jordan".
- **"out of every tribe a man" → "one man for each tribe"** (v12) — Hebrew אִישׁ־אֶחָד אִישׁ־אֶחָד לַשָּׁבֶט, literally "one man, one man for the tribe"; שֵׁבֶט "tribe", literally "rod". **"take you" → "take for yourselves"** — Hebrew קְחוּ לָכֶם.
- **"as soon as the soles of the feet … shall rest … that the waters of Jordan shall be cut off from the waters that come down from above; and they shall stand upon an heap" → "when the soles of the feet … rest … the waters of the Jordan shall be cut off, the waters that come down from above, and they shall stand as one heap"** (v13) — Hebrew יִכָּרֵתוּן, from כָּרַת "cut" (the verb of "cut a covenant"); הַמַּיִם הַיֹּרְדִים מִלְמָעְלָה stands beside "the waters of the Jordan" with no "from"; נֵד אֶחָד "one heap" (Exod 15:8), the heap of waters, another word than the heap of stones.
- **"when the people removed from their tents"** (v14) — one sentence runs from v14 to v16: "And it came to pass, when … and when … that the waters stood".
- **"were dipped in the brim of the water, (for Jordan overfloweth all his banks all the time of harvest,)" → "were dipped in the edge of the waters — and the Jordan is full over all its banks all the days of harvest —"** (v15) — Hebrew וְהַיַּרְדֵּן מָלֵא עַל־כָּל־גְּדוֹתָיו, literally "and the Jordan is full upon all its banks"; כֹּל יְמֵי קָצִיר "all the days of harvest".
- **"stood and rose up upon an heap very far from the city Adam, that is beside Zaretan" → "stood; they rose up as one heap, very far off, from Adam, the city that is at the side of Zarethan"** (v16) — the reading tradition (qere) מֵאָדָם "from Adam" is translated; the written form (ketiv) is בְאָדָם "at Adam". With the qere the heap is "very far off from Adam" (or the distance is counted from Adam); with the ketiv it stands "very far off, at Adam". מִצַּד "at the side of".
- **"toward the sea of the plain, even the salt sea, failed, and were cut off" → "to the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, came to an end, were cut off"** (v16) — Hebrew תַּמּוּ נִכְרָתוּ, two verbs with no "and": "were complete, were cut off"; הָעֲרָבָה *ha-Aravah*, the dry plain of the Jordan valley; the Dead Sea. **"right against Jericho" → "opposite Jericho"** — Hebrew נֶגֶד.
- **"stood firm on dry ground in the midst of Jordan" → "stood on the dry ground in the midst of the Jordan, firmly"** (v17) — Hebrew הָכֵן, an infinitive used as an adverb, at the end of the clause: "firmly, steadily". חָרָבָה "dry ground" (Exod 14:21). **"all the Israelites passed over … until all the people were passed clean over Jordan" → "all Israel were passing over … until all the nation had come to an end of passing over the Jordan"** — Hebrew עֹבְרִים, a participle; הַגּוֹי "the nation", said of Israel; תַּמּוּ, the verb of v16.

### Notes
- **Three-day preparation** — parallels Sinai (Ex 19:11). Sacred preparation before major theophany.
- **Ark leads the crossing** — contrast with Red Sea: there Moses' staff parted the waters; here, the ark-priests standing in the river halts it. Yahweh's presence, mediated by the covenant-ark, accomplishes the crossing.
- **2,000 cubits** (v4) — ~900 m / 3,000 feet. Distance ensures reverence; no one may approach the holy.
- **Jordan at flood** (v15) — spring harvest = snow-melt + rain flooding. The crossing is at the worst-case time, making the miracle unmistakable.
- **Parallel to Red Sea** — Yahweh repeats the exodus-crossing pattern for the new generation. Joshua repeats Moses; the people repeat their parents. The transition-through-water initiates the land-inheritance.
- **Adam and Zarethan** (v16) — Adam is placed about 30 km north of the crossing. Landslides have stopped the Jordan at this location several times in recorded history (1267, 1906, 1927). Miracle and natural event are not mutually exclusive in biblical narrative.
