[1] ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ rose early in the morning; they set out from Shittim and came to the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ — he and all the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — and they lodged there before crossing over.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"sea of the plain, even the salt sea" → "sea of the Arabah — the Salt Sea"** (v16) — the Dead Sea.
- **"right against Jericho" → "opposite Jericho"** (v16).
- **"living God" → "living El"** (v10) — Hebrew אֵל חַי *El chay*.
- **"Adam / Zaretan / Zarethan"** (v16) — archaeological location ~30 km north of where the crossing took place. Geological blockage of Jordan at Adam periodically occurs from landslides (recorded 1267, 1906, 1927).

### Notes
- **Three-day preparation** — parallels Sinai (Ex 19:11). Sacred preparation before major theophany.
- **Ark leads the crossing** — contrast with Red Sea: there Moses' staff parted the waters; here, the ark-priests standing in the river halts it. Yahweh's presence, mediated by the covenant-ark, accomplishes the crossing.
- **2,000 cubits** (v4) — ~900 m / 3,000 feet. Distance ensures reverence; no one may approach the holy.
- **Jordan at flood** (v15) — spring harvest = snow-melt + rain flooding. The crossing is at the worst-case time, making the miracle unmistakable.
- **Parallel to Red Sea** — Yahweh repeats the exodus-crossing pattern for the new generation. Joshua repeats Moses; the people repeat their parents. The transition-through-water initiates the land-inheritance.
- **Water-stoppage at Adam** — historical reality: landslides have stopped the Jordan at exactly this location multiple times in recorded history. Miracle and natural event are not mutually exclusive in biblical narrative.
