## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"families" → "clans"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחוֹת. "Tribe" is מַטֶּה (v1).
- **"There was also a lot for the tribe of Manasseh … to wit, for Machir … therefore he had Gilead and Bashan" → "And the lot was for the tribe of Manasseh … for Machir … and he had Gilead and the Bashan"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הַגּוֹרָל … לְמָכִיר … וַיְהִי־לוֹ, literally "and there was to him". The Hebrew has "for" (כִּי) twice and no "therefore". "Gilead" and "Bashan" have the article in v1, 5, 6 ("the Gilead", "the Bashan"): the lands; English keeps it with "the Bashan", as in Josh 12–13.
- **"There was also a lot for the rest of the children of Manasseh" → "And it was for the remaining sons of Manasseh"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיְהִי לִבְנֵי מְנַשֶּׁה הַנּוֹתָרִים; "a lot" is not repeated in the Hebrew. נוֹתָר is "remaining" (v2, 6). **"the male children" → "the males"** — Hebrew הַזְּכָרִים.
- **"had no sons, but daughters" → "had no sons, except daughters"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי אִם, as Num 26:33. The names stand in the order and with the "and" of the Hebrew.
- **"princes" → "chieftains"** (v4) — Hebrew נְשִׂיאִים. **"among our brethren" → "in the midst of our brothers"** — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ, as Num 27:4, 7. **"according to the commandment of the LORD" → "at the mouth of Yahweh"** — Hebrew אֶל־פִּי יְהוָה, literally "to the mouth of". "He gave": the Hebrew does not name the giver.
- **"there fell ten portions to Manasseh, beside" → "the portions of Manasseh fell, ten, apart from"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיִּפְּלוּ חַבְלֵי־מְנַשֶּׁה עֲשָׂרָה. חֶבֶל is literally a "cord", a measuring line, and so a measured portion of land (so also v14); another word than חֵלֶק "share". **"on the other side Jordan" → "across the Jordan"** — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן.
- **"had an inheritance" → "inherited an inheritance"** (v6) — Hebrew נָחֲלוּ נַחֲלָה, verb and noun of one root.
- **"the coast of Manasseh was from Asher to Michmethah, that lieth before Shechem; and the border went along on the right hand" → "the border of Manasseh was from Asher to Michmethath, which is facing Shechem; and the border goes to the south"** (v7) — Hebrew מֵאָשֵׁר הַמִּכְמְתָת, literally "from Asher, the Michmethath": no "to" stands in the Hebrew, and the words may also be one name. עַל־פְּנֵי is literally "upon the face of". אֶל־הַיָּמִין is literally "to the right", which is the south for one who faces the sunrise; it may also be read as a place name. The verbs of the border (הָלַךְ "go", יָרַד "go down", v9; פָּגַע בְּ "touch", v10) are in the descriptive form and are rendered by the present; "was / were" stands where the Hebrew has the narrative form (v7, 9, 10).
- **"Now Manasseh had the land of Tappuah: but Tappuah on the border of Manasseh belonged to" → "To Manasseh belonged the land of Tappuah; and Tappuah, at the border of Manasseh, belonged to"** (v8) — the Hebrew order; אֶל־גְּבוּל is "at / toward the border". No "town" is in the Hebrew.
- **"the river Kanah, southward of the river" → "the wadi Kanah, southward of the wadi"** (v9) — Hebrew נַחַל, a stream bed. **"these cities of Ephraim are among the cities of Manasseh" → "These cities are Ephraim's, in the midst of the cities of Manasseh"** — Hebrew עָרִים הָאֵלֶּה לְאֶפְרַיִם; which cities are meant is not said. **"the outgoings of it were at the sea"** — תּוֹצָאוֹת, "outgoings": where the border comes out, its end.
- **"they met together in Asher on the north" → "they touch Asher on the north"** (v10) — Hebrew וּבְאָשֵׁר יִפְגְּעוּן מִצָּפוֹן, "and Asher they touch from the north". "His border": Manasseh's.
- **"and her towns" → "and its daughters"** (v11, 16) — Hebrew בְּנוֹתֶיהָ, the settlements that belong to a city, as Num 21:25. From Dor on the Hebrew names "the inhabitants of" each city. **"even three countries" → "the three of the Napheth"** — Hebrew שְׁלֹשֶׁת הַנָּפֶת. נֶפֶת stands only here; it belongs with נָפַת דּוֹר (12:23) and נָפוֹת דּוֹר (11:2), a word of unknown meaning ("height", "ridge", "district" are proposed). What "the three of" counts is not said. The word is kept. The old rendering "the third being Naphath-dor" put "Dor" into the verse, which the Hebrew does not have here.
- **"could not drive out the inhabitants of those cities" → "could not take possession of these cities"** (v12) — Hebrew לְהוֹרִישׁ אֶת־הֶעָרִים: the object of הוֹרִישׁ is the cities, so "take possession of"; with persons as object the same verb is "dispossess" (v13, 18). **"would dwell" → "undertook to dwell"** — Hebrew וַיּוֹאֶל, "resolved, set himself to", as Exod 2:21.
- **"were waxen strong" → "became strong"** (v13) — Hebrew חָזְקוּ. **"put the Canaanites to tribute" → "put the Canaanite to forced labor"** — Hebrew לָמַס; literally "gave … to". **"but did not utterly drive them out" → "but they surely did not dispossess him"** — Hebrew וְהוֹרֵשׁ לֹא הוֹרִישׁוֹ, the infinitive with its verb, literally "and dispossessing they did not dispossess him"; it either strengthens the "not", or says "not completely". In 16:10 the text has the plain "did not dispossess"; in v12 and 15:63 "could not".
- **"spake unto Joshua" → "spoke with Joshua"** (v14) — Hebrew אֶת־יְהוֹשֻׁעַ. **"but one lot and one portion to inherit" → "as an inheritance one lot and one portion"** — "but" is not in the Hebrew. **"a great people" → "a numerous people"** (v14, 15, 17) — Hebrew עַם־רָב. **"forasmuch as the LORD hath blessed me hitherto" → "inasmuch as Yahweh has blessed me until now"** — Hebrew עַד אֲשֶׁר־עַד־כֹּה, literally "until that until thus"; the exact sense of the phrase is not certain.
- **"get thee up to the wood country, and cut down for thyself" → "get yourself up to the forest, and cut down for yourself"** (v15) — Hebrew עֲלֵה לְךָ, "go up for yourself"; בֵּרֵאתָ, an intensive form used of cutting (v18; Ezek 23:47), another stem than בָּרָא "create". No object stands in the Hebrew. **"giants" → "Rephaim"** — Hebrew הָרְפָאִים. **"if mount Ephraim be too narrow for thee" → "for the hill country of Ephraim is too narrow for you"** — Hebrew כִּי־אָץ לְךָ, literally "for it presses on you" (אוּץ "press, hurry").
- **"The hill is not enough for us" → "The hill country is not enough for us"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא־יִמָּצֵא לָנוּ הָהָר, literally "the hill country will not be found for us" (as Num 11:22). **"chariots of iron"** — Hebrew רֶכֶב בַּרְזֶל; בְּכָל is "in / with all". **"the land of the valley"** — עֵמֶק, a broad valley.
- **"thou shalt not have one lot only" → "you shall not have one lot"** (v17) — "only" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"But the mountain shall be thine … though they have iron chariots, and though they be strong" → "For a hill country shall be yours … for he has chariots of iron, for he is strong"** (v18) — the Hebrew has כִּי five times in the verse; it is "for" each time. The last two are commonly taken as "though"; the Hebrew word does not decide it, and the reasons may also be heard as: he must be dispossessed, for he is strong. הַר has no article here. **"the outgoings of it"** — תֹּצְאֹתָיו, as of a border: its ends.

### Notes
- **Zelophehad's daughters receive land** (v3–6) — as Num 27 and 36.
- **Manasseh's territory spans the Jordan** — half the tribe east of the Jordan (Bashan and Gilead, Machir); the rest west of it, north of Ephraim.
- **Canaanites remain in the valleys** — the chariots of iron (v16) are named as the reason.
- **Forest-clearing counsel** (v15) — Joshua's answer: go up and cut down the forest.
- **"Chariots of iron"** — also Judg 1:19; 4:3.
