## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"giants" → "Rephaim"** (v15).
- **"waxen strong" → "grew stronger"** (v13) — archaic.
- **"three countries" → "the third being Naphath-dor"** (v11) — Hebrew שְׁלֹשֶׁת הַנָּפֶת *sheloshet ha-nafet*. Likely naming a specific third-region ("the Nafoth"), not "three countries."

### Notes
- **Zelophehad's daughters receive land** (v3–6) — fulfillment of Num 27 and 36. Legal precedent applied.
- **Manasseh's territory spans Jordan** — half-tribe in Transjordan (Bashan/Gilead, under Machir); the rest in Cisjordan north of Ephraim.
- **Canaanites remain in the plains** — iron chariots (v16) give them military superiority on flat terrain. Israel dominates the hills but cannot clear the lowlands.
- **Forest-clearing counsel** (v15) — Joshua's pragmatic response: go clear more forest land rather than complain. Northern Palestine was more forested in the Bronze Age than now.
- **"Iron chariots"** — first mention in Joshua. The Canaanites' technological advantage. Israel's victories in Judges (Judg 4–5, Barak/Deborah) turn on divine intervention that neutralizes this advantage.
