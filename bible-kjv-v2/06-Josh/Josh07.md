## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"committed a trespass" → "committed a breach of faith"** (v1) — Hebrew *ma'al*.
- **"accursed thing / accursed" → "devoted things / devoted to destruction"** (v1, 11, 12, 13, 15) — *cherem*.
- **"dissembled" → "lied"** (v11) — Hebrew *kichesh*. Concealed / denied.
- **"goodly Babylonish garment" → "beautiful cloak from Shinar"** (v21) — Hebrew אַדֶּרֶת שִׁנְעָר *adderet Shin'ar*. Mesopotamian luxury cloak.
- **"wedge of gold" → "bar of gold"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew לְשׁוֹן זָהָב *leshon zahav*. Lit. "tongue of gold" — an ingot.
- **"wrought folly" → "done a shameful thing"** (v15) — Hebrew *nevalah*.
- **"valley of Achor / troubled / trouble"** (v24–26) — Hebrew wordplay: Achor from *akhar* "to trouble." Achan's name in 1 Chr 2:7 is spelled "Achar" (troubler), intensifying the wordplay.
- **"Shebarim" → "quarries"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁבָרִים *Shevarim*. Possibly proper name or "quarries / stone-breakings."

### Notes
- **Communal consequences of individual sin** — one man's breach of *cherem* brings defeat upon the whole army. Ancient Israelite corporate solidarity: individuals represent and affect the whole community.
- **Thirty-six casualties** (v5) — the first Israelite military deaths in the land. Breaks the momentum of unbroken success.
- **Joshua's prayer** (v7–9) — like Moses, Joshua intercedes. Argument: (1) redemptive history will be undone; (2) Yahweh's reputation suffers. Same rhetoric as Moses' intercessions (Ex 32, Num 14).
- **Divine lot** (v14) — tribes cast lots, each "taken" narrows to the guilty party. Possibly Urim and Thummim (cf. 1 Sam 14:41 LXX).
- **"Give glory to Yahweh"** (v19) — idiom for truthful confession, making divine justice visible. Lk 17:18 uses similar phrasing.
- **Collective punishment** (v24–25) — Achan's family also executed with him. Troubling modern ethical reading; explanations suggest complicity (hidden things in the family tent), but collective-guilt assumptions of the age also operate. Later Deut 24:16 restricts children from bearing father's guilt.
- **Valley of Achor** — later prophetic reversal: Hos 2:15 "I will make the Valley of Achor a door of hope." Trouble transformed into renewed expectation.
