## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept. **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of וַ / וְ is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v1, 12, 23).
- **"committed a trespass in the accursed thing" → "committed unfaithfulness in the devoted thing"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּמְעֲלוּ … מַעַל, verb and noun of one root: a breach of trust (Lev 5:15). חֵרֶם *cherem* is what was devoted to destruction in 6:17–19. The Hebrew says "the sons of Israel" did it, and then names the one man.
- **"tribe"** (v1, 14, 16, 18) — two Hebrew words: מַטֶּה in v1, 18 and שֵׁבֶט in v14, 16. **"the anger of Yahweh burned"** (v1) — Hebrew אַף, literally "nose".
- **"Ai"** (v2–5) — Hebrew הָעַי, always with the article: "the Ai" ("the ruin"). **"beside Beth-aven"** — Hebrew עִם "with". **"on the east side of" → "east of"** — literally "from the front of". **"view" → "spy out"** — Hebrew רִגֵּל, the word of 6:22–25.
- **"make not all the people to labour thither" → "Do not weary all the people there"** (v3) — Hebrew יִגַּע (piel).
- **"chased them from before the gate even unto Shebarim" → "pursued them before the gate as far as the Shebarim"** (v5) — Hebrew לִפְנֵי הַשַּׁעַר "before the gate". הַשְּׁבָרִים has the article and means "the breakings"; whether it is a place name or "the quarries" is not certain. **"in the going down" → "on the descent"**. **"the hearts … melted, and became as water" → "the heart … melted and became water"** — singular; the Hebrew has no "as".
- **"clothes" → "cloaks"** (v6) — Hebrew שִׂמְלֹתָיו. **"put dust"** — literally "made dust go up on their head".
- **"wherefore hast thou at all brought this people over" → "why have you surely made this people pass over"** (v7) — Hebrew הֵעֲבַרְתָּ הַעֲבִיר, literally "making pass over you made pass over": the infinitive with its verb, here in a question ("why ever"). **"Amorites" → "Amorite"**, **"Canaanites" → "Canaanite"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew singular with the article. **"would to God we had been content, and dwelt on the other side Jordan" → "would that we had been willing and had dwelt across the Jordan"** — the Hebrew has no "God"; הוֹאִיל is "be willing, undertake" (Exod 2:21; Deut 1:5).
- **"O Lord" → "Oh, Adonai"** (v8) — Hebrew בִּי אֲדֹנָי, literally "upon me, Adonai": asking leave to speak. **"turneth their backs" → "turned the neck"** (v8, 12) — Hebrew עֹרֶף, the back of the neck.
- **"environ us round" → "surround us"** (v9) — Hebrew נָסַבּוּ, the root of "go around" in chapter 6. **"the land … the earth"** — one Hebrew word, הָאָרֶץ.
- **"Get thee up" → "Get yourself up"** (v10) — Hebrew קֻם לָךְ, literally "rise for yourself". **"wherefore liest thou thus" → "Why is it that you are fallen"** — Hebrew לָמָּה זֶּה … נֹפֵל, the verb "fall" of v6.
- **"transgressed" → "overstepped"** (v11, 15) — Hebrew עָבַר, literally "pass over", the verb of v7. **"dissembled" → "lied"** — Hebrew כִּחֵשׁ: deny, deceive. **"their own stuff" → "their vessels"** — Hebrew כְּלֵיהֶם: any made article, their baggage; "it" is supplied. The Hebrew has "also" five times.
- **"they were accursed" → "they have become a thing devoted to destruction"** (v12) — Hebrew הָיוּ לְחֵרֶם. **"neither will I be with you any more" → "I will not be with you again"** — literally "I will not add to be with you".
- **"There is an accursed thing in the midst of thee" → "A devoted thing is in your midst"** (v13) — "your midst" and "you will not be able" are singular, "until you remove … from your midst" plural.
- **"taketh" → "captures"** (v14–18) — Hebrew לָכַד, the verb for capturing a city (6:20); "take" is another verb (v1, 11, 21). How the one was marked out is not said. **"households" → "houses"**. **"man by man" → "by men"** — Hebrew לַגְּבָרִים, from גֶּבֶר, a grown man.
- **"the family of Judah" → "the clan of Judah"** (v17) — Hebrew singular. **"he took" → "he captured"** — active here. **"by men"** (v17) — so the Masoretic Text, where "by houses" is expected from v14.
- **"folly" → "outrage"** (v15) — Hebrew נְבָלָה (Gen 34:7).
- **"give, I pray thee, glory … and make confession unto him" → "give, please, glory … and give him thanksgiving"** (v19) — two Hebrew verbs, שִׂים "put, set" (glory) and נָתַן "give". תּוֹדָה is thanksgiving, praise (Lev 7:12); it is also taken as "confession" here and in Ezra 10:11, since its verb means both "give thanks" and "confess". **"hide it"** — "it" is supplied.
- **"Indeed I have sinned" → "Truly, I, I have sinned"** (v20) — the Hebrew writes the pronoun.
- **"a goodly Babylonish garment" → "one good mantle of Shinar"** (v21) — Hebrew אַדֶּרֶת שִׁנְעָר אַחַת טוֹבָה; Shinar is the land of Babylon (Gen 11:2). **"wedge of gold" → "tongue of gold"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew לְשׁוֹן זָהָב: a bar of that shape. The shekel is a weight, not a coin. **"And I saw"** — the reading tradition; the written form differs only in spelling. **"my tent"** — Hebrew הָאָהֳלִי, with the article and the suffix together. **"under it"** (v21, 22) — feminine: under the mantle.
- **"laid them out" → "poured them out"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיַּצִּקֻם, from יָצַק "pour".
- **"his oxen, and his asses, and his sheep" → "his ox and his donkey and his flock"** (v24) — Hebrew singulars. **"son of Zerah"** — his forefather (v1).
- **"Why hast thou troubled us?"** (v25) — Hebrew מֶה, "what / how / why": also "How you have troubled us!" The verb עָכַר "trouble" (6:18) gives the valley its name: עָכוֹר *Achor*, "troubling". **"stoned"** — two Hebrew verbs, רָגַם (him) and סָקַל (them); English has one word. "With stones" is first singular, "with stone". "Him … them … them" as the Hebrew has it.
- **"the fierceness of his anger" → "the burning of his anger"** (v26) — Hebrew חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ. **"was called"** — literally "he / one called the name of that place".

### Notes
- **One man and all Israel** — one man takes of the devoted thing, and the text says "the sons of Israel committed unfaithfulness" (v1) and "Israel has sinned" (v11).
- **Thirty-six men** (v5) — the first Israelite dead in the land.
- **Joshua's prayer** (v7–9) — compare Moses in Exod 32:11–13 and Num 14:13–16: the name of Yahweh among the nations.
- **How the tribe was "captured"** (v14–18) — the means is not named; the lot is commonly supposed (cf. 1 Sam 14:41, 42).
- **"Give glory to Yahweh"** (v19) — compare John 9:24.
- **Achan's sons and daughters** (v24, 25) — the text says "they burned them … and stoned them". Compare Deut 24:16.
- **Valley of Achor** — Hos 2:15; Isa 65:10. In 1 Chr 2:7 the man's name is written Achar (from memory).
