# Joshua 7

[1] But the children of Israel committed a breach of faith regarding the devoted things: for Achan son of Carmi son of Zabdi son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of the devoted things; and the anger of Yahweh burned against the children of Israel.

[2] Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is near Beth-aven, east of Bethel, and said to them, "Go up and scout out the land." The men went up and scouted out Ai.

[3] They returned to Joshua and said to him, "Let not all the people go up; let only about two or three thousand men go up and strike Ai. Do not weary all the people there, for they are few."

[4] So about three thousand men of the people went up there; but they fled before the men of Ai.

[5] The men of Ai struck down about thirty-six of them and chased them from the gate as far as the quarries, and struck them down on the slope. The hearts of the people melted and became as water.

[6] Joshua tore his clothes and fell to the ground on his face before the ark of Yahweh until evening, he and the elders of Israel; and they put dust on their heads.

[7] Joshua said, "Alas, Lord Yahweh, why have you brought this people across the Jordan at all, to give us into the hand of the Amorites, to destroy us? Would that we had been content and remained beyond the Jordan!

[8] O Lord, what shall I say, when Israel has turned their backs before their enemies?

[9] For the Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land will hear of it and will surround us and cut off our name from the earth. And what will you do for your great name?"

[10] Yahweh said to Joshua, "Get up! Why have you fallen on your face?

[11] Israel has sinned; they have also transgressed my covenant that I commanded them. They have taken some of the devoted things; they have stolen; they have lied; they have put them among their own belongings.

[12] Therefore the children of Israel cannot stand before their enemies; they turn their backs before their enemies, because they have become devoted to destruction. I will be with you no more unless you destroy the devoted thing from among you.

[13] Get up! Consecrate the people, and say, 'Consecrate yourselves for tomorrow; for thus says Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel: "Devoted things are in your midst, O Israel; you cannot stand before your enemies until you remove the devoted things from among you."

[14] In the morning therefore you shall be brought forward by your tribes; and the tribe that Yahweh takes shall come forward by clans; and the clan that Yahweh takes shall come forward by households; and the household that Yahweh takes shall come forward man by man.

[15] He who is taken with the devoted things shall be burned with fire — he and all that he has — because he has transgressed the covenant of Yahweh and because he has done a shameful thing in Israel.'"

[16] So Joshua rose early in the morning and brought Israel near by their tribes; and the tribe of Judah was taken.

[17] He brought near the clans of Judah, and the clan of the Zerahites was taken; he brought near the clan of the Zerahites man by man, and Zabdi was taken.

[18] He brought near his household man by man, and Achan son of Carmi son of Zabdi son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, was taken.

[19] Joshua said to Achan, "My son, give glory to Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel and make confession to him. Tell me now what you have done; do not hide it from me."

[20] Achan answered Joshua and said, "Truly I have sinned against Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel, and this is what I did:

[21] when I saw among the spoil a beautiful cloak from Shinar, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a bar of gold weighing fifty shekels, I coveted them and took them. Behold, they are hidden in the earth inside my tent, with the silver underneath."

[22] So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran to the tent, and behold, it was hidden in his tent, with the silver underneath.

[23] They took them out of the tent and brought them to Joshua and to all the children of Israel, and laid them out before Yahweh.

[24] Then Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan son of Zerah, with the silver, the cloak, the bar of gold, his sons, his daughters, his oxen, his donkeys, his sheep, his tent, and all that he had; and they brought them up to the Valley of Achor.

[25] Joshua said, "Why have you troubled us? Yahweh will trouble you today." And all Israel stoned him with stones; they burned them with fire and stoned them with stones.

[26] They raised over him a great heap of stones, which stands to this day. Then Yahweh turned from the fierceness of his anger. Therefore the name of that place was called the Valley of Achor ("Trouble") to this day.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"committed a trespass" → "committed a breach of faith"** (v1) — Hebrew *ma'al*.
- **"accursed thing / accursed" → "devoted things / devoted to destruction"** (v1, 11, 12, 13, 15) — *cherem*.
- **"dissembled" → "lied"** (v11) — Hebrew *kichesh*. Concealed / denied.
- **"goodly Babylonish garment" → "beautiful cloak from Shinar"** (v21) — Hebrew אַדֶּרֶת שִׁנְעָר *adderet Shin'ar*. Mesopotamian luxury cloak.
- **"wedge of gold" → "bar of gold"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew לְשׁוֹן זָהָב *leshon zahav*. Lit. "tongue of gold" — an ingot.
- **"wrought folly" → "done a shameful thing"** (v15) — Hebrew *nevalah*.
- **"valley of Achor / troubled / trouble"** (v24–26) — Hebrew wordplay: Achor from *akhar* "to trouble." Achan's name in 1 Chr 2:7 is spelled "Achar" (troubler), intensifying the wordplay.
- **"Shebarim" → "quarries"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁבָרִים *Shevarim*. Possibly proper name or "quarries / stone-breakings."

### Notes
- **Communal consequences of individual sin** — one man's breach of *cherem* brings defeat upon the whole army. Ancient Israelite corporate solidarity: individuals represent and affect the whole community.
- **Thirty-six casualties** (v5) — the first Israelite military deaths in the land. Breaks the momentum of unbroken success.
- **Joshua's prayer** (v7–9) — like Moses, Joshua intercedes. Argument: (1) redemptive history will be undone; (2) Yahweh's reputation suffers. Same rhetoric as Moses' intercessions (Ex 32, Num 14).
- **Divine lot** (v14) — tribes cast lots, each "taken" narrows to the guilty party. Possibly Urim and Thummim (cf. 1 Sam 14:41 LXX).
- **"Give glory to Yahweh"** (v19) — idiom for truthful confession, making divine justice visible. Lk 17:18 uses similar phrasing.
- **Collective punishment** (v24–25) — Achan's family also executed with him. Troubling modern ethical reading; explanations suggest complicity (hidden things in the family tent), but collective-guilt assumptions of the age also operate. Later Deut 24:16 restricts children from bearing father's guilt.
- **Valley of Achor** — later prophetic reversal: Hos 2:15 "I will make the Valley of Achor a door of hope." Trouble transformed into renewed expectation.
