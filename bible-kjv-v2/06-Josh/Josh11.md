# Joshua 11

[1] And it came to pass, when ⟦jabin|Yavin|Jabin⟧ king of ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧ heard, that he sent to ⟦jobab|Yovav|Jobab⟧ king of Madon and to the king of Shimron and to the king of ⟦achshaph|Achshaf|Achshaph⟧,

[2] and to the kings who were on the north, in the hill country and in the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧ south of ⟦chinneroth|Kinnerot|Chinneroth⟧ and in the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧ and in ⟦naphath-dor|Nafot Dor|Naphoth-dor⟧ on the west:

[3] the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ on the east and on the west, and the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦perizzite|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧ in the hill country, and the ⟦hivite|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ under ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧ in the land of the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧.

[4] And they went out, they and all their camps with them, a numerous people, as the sand that is on the shore of the sea for multitude, and horse and chariot, very many.

[5] And all these kings met, and they came and camped together at the waters of Merom, to fight with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[6] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "Do not fear because of them, for tomorrow at this time I am setting all of them, slain, before ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; their horses you shall hamstring, and their chariots you shall burn with fire."

[7] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ came, and all the people of war with him, upon them at the waters of Merom suddenly, and they fell upon them.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave them into the hand of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and they struck them down and pursued them as far as Great ⟦sidon|Tzidon|Sidon⟧ and as far as ⟦misrephoth-maim|Misrefot Mayim|Misrephoth-maim⟧ and as far as the plain of ⟦mizpeh|Mitzpeh|Mizpeh⟧ eastward; and they struck them down until no survivor was left to them.

[9] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ did to them as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had said to him: their horses he hamstrung, and their chariots he burned with fire.

[10] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ returned at that time and captured ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧, and its king he struck down with the sword; for ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧ formerly was the head of all these kingdoms.

[11] And they struck down every soul that was in it with the edge of the sword, devoting to destruction; no breath remained; and ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧ he burned with fire.

[12] And all the cities of these kings and all their kings ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ captured, and he struck them down with the edge of the sword; he devoted them to destruction, as ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded.

[13] Only all the cities that stood on their mound, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did not burn them; except that ⟦hazor|Chatzor|Hazor⟧ alone ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ burned.

[14] And all the spoil of these cities and the beasts the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ plundered for themselves; only all the men they struck down with the edge of the sword until they had destroyed them; they left no breath.

[15] As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ his servant, so ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, and so ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ did; he did not remove a word from all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[16] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ took all this land: the hill country and all the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ and all the land of Goshen and the ⟦shephelah|Shefelah|Shephelah⟧ and the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, and the hill country of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and its lowland,

[17] from Mount ⟦halak|Chalaq|Halak⟧, which goes up to Seir, and as far as ⟦baal-gad|Baal Gad|Baal-gad⟧ in the plain of the ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ under Mount ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧; and all their kings he captured, and he struck them down and put them to death.

[18] Many days ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ made war with all these kings.

[19] There was not a city that made peace with the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ except the ⟦hivites|Chivvi|Hivite⟧, those who dwelt in ⟦gibeon|Givon|Gibeon⟧; the whole they took in war.

[20] For it was from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to make their heart strong to meet the war with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, in order to devote them to destruction, that there might be no favor for them, but in order to destroy them, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[21] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ came at that time and cut off the ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧ from the hill country, from ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧, from ⟦debir|Devir|Debir⟧, from ⟦anab|Anav|Anab⟧, and from all the hill country of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and from all the hill country of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; with their cities ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ devoted them to destruction.

[22] No ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧ remained in the land of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; only in ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧, in ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ and in Ashdod were they left.

[23] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ took all the land, according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had spoken to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧; and ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ gave it as an inheritance to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ according to their divisions, by their tribes. And the land was quiet from war.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"when Jabin king of Hazor had heard those things" → "when Jabin king of Hazor heard"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי כִּשְׁמֹעַ, "and it came to pass, at the hearing of Jabin": the verb has no object.
- **"on the north of the mountains, and of the plains south of Chinneroth, and in the valley, and in the borders of Dor" → "on the north, in the hill country and in the Arabah south of Chinneroth and in the Shephelah and in Naphoth-dor"** (v2) — Hebrew הָהָר, "the mountain", of a region "the hill country" (so v3, 16, 21); הָעֲרָבָה, the name of the Jordan rift; הַשְּׁפֵלָה *ha-Shefelah*, "the lowland", the name of the low hills toward the coast (as Deut 1:7); נָפוֹת דּוֹר, a plural here ("heights of Dor"?; the meaning of נָפָה is not certain), in 12:23 the singular נָפַת דּוֹר. The quarters of the sky are literally "from the hidden side" (north), "Negev" (south), "from the sea" (west), "from the rising" (east, v3).
- **"And to the Canaanite … and to the Amorite … and to the Hivite" → "the Canaanite … and the Amorite … and the Hivite"** (v3) — the Hebrew has no "to" in this verse: the peoples stand beside "the kings" of v2. Each is a singular with the article. הַמִּצְפָּה has the article, "the Mizpah" ("the watchtower"); in v8 the form is מִצְפֶּה without it.
- **"they and all their hosts with them, much people, even as the sand that is upon the sea shore in multitude, with horses and chariots very many" → "they and all their camps with them, a numerous people, as the sand that is on the shore of the sea for multitude, and horse and chariot, very many"** (v4) — Hebrew מַחֲנֶה "camp"; שְׂפַת הַיָּם, literally "the lip of the sea" (Gen 22:17); סוּס וָרֶכֶב, two collective singulars, as Deut 20:1.
- **"when all these kings were met together, they came and pitched together" → "all these kings met, and they came and camped together"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיִּוָּעֲדוּ, "met by appointment" (the verb of מוֹעֵד); חָנָה "camp". **"to fight against" → "to fight with"** — Hebrew עִם "with" (Exod 17:8).
- **"Be not afraid because of them: for to morrow about this time will I deliver them up all slain" → "Do not fear because of them, for tomorrow at this time I am setting all of them, slain, before Israel"** (v6) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵיהֶם, literally "from their face"; אָנֹכִי נֹתֵן, "I" written out with a participle; נָתַן … לִפְנֵי "set before" (the verb is "give": Deut 2:33; 7:2); חֲלָלִים "slain", literally "pierced".
- **"hough" → "hamstring"** (v6, 9) — Hebrew עִקֵּר: to cut the sinew of the hind leg.
- **"all the people of war with him, against them … and they fell upon them"** (v7) — Hebrew עַם הַמִּלְחָמָה "the people of war"; פִּתְאֹם "suddenly" stands at the end of the clause.
- **"who smote them, and chased them unto great Zidon … and unto the valley of Mizpeh eastward; and they smote them, until they left them none remaining" → "and they struck them down and pursued them as far as Great Sidon … and as far as the plain of Mizpeh eastward; and they struck them down until no survivor was left to them"** (v8) — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike", here of killing; רָדַף "pursue"; בִּקְעָה "plain", a broad valley floor (so v17); עַד־בִּלְתִּי הִשְׁאִיר־לָהֶם שָׂרִיד, the words of Num 21:35; Deut 3:3, literally "until not leaving to them a survivor". מִשְׂרְפוֹת מַיִם *Misrefot Mayim* is literally "burnings of water" (or "burnings at the water"); the meaning of the name is not certain.
- **"as the LORD bade him" → "as Yahweh had said to him"** (v9) — Hebrew אָמַר "say".
- **"turned back, and took Hazor, and smote the king thereof with the sword: for Hazor beforetime was the head" → "returned at that time and captured Hazor, and its king he struck down with the sword; for Hazor formerly was the head"** (v10) — Hebrew שׁוּב "return"; לָכַד "capture" (v10, 12, 17), beside לָקַח "take" (v16, 19, 23).
- **"all the souls that were therein … utterly destroying them: there was not any left to breathe" → "every soul that was in it … devoting to destruction; no breath remained"** (v11) — Hebrew כָּל־הַנֶּפֶשׁ, a singular, "every soul"; הַחֲרֵם, an infinitive without an object, from חֵרֶם *cherem*: what is given over to Yahweh beyond recall, here by destroying it (so v12, 20, 21; Deut 7:2; 20:17); לֹא נוֹתַר כָּל־נְשָׁמָה, literally "there did not remain any breath" (נְשָׁמָה, the word of Deut 20:16; so v14). לְפִי־חֶרֶב is literally "to the mouth of the sword" (v11, 12, 14).
- **"he burnt Hazor with fire"** (v11) — the verbs change from "they struck" to "he burned" as the Hebrew has them.
- **"But as for the cities that stood still in their strength, Israel burned none of them, save Hazor only; that did Joshua burn" → "Only all the cities that stood on their mound, Israel did not burn them; except that Hazor alone Joshua burned"** (v13) — Hebrew עַל־תִּלָּם, "on their mound", a singular: תֵּל *tel* is the heap on which a town stands, built up from the ruins of the towns before it (Deut 13:16; Josh 8:28 of a ruin). רַק "only" opens the verse and v14b.
- **"the cattle, the children of Israel took for a prey unto themselves; but every man they smote … neither left they any to breathe" → "the beasts the sons of Israel plundered for themselves; only all the men they struck down … they left no breath"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה "beasts"; בָּזַז "plunder" (Deut 3:7); כָּל־הָאָדָם, "all the mankind": human beings, not males only (Num 16:32); הִשְׁמִיד "destroy".
- **"he left nothing undone of all" → "he did not remove a word from all"** (v15) — Hebrew לֹא־הֵסִיר דָּבָר, literally "he did not make a word / a thing turn aside".
- **"the hills, and all the south country, and all the land of Goshen, and the valley, and the plain, and the mountain of Israel, and the valley of the same" → "the hill country and all the Negev and all the land of Goshen and the Shephelah and the Arabah, and the hill country of Israel and its lowland"** (v16) — the regions by their names. "Goshen" has the article (אֶרֶץ הַגֹּשֶׁן). The last word, שְׁפֵלָתֹה, is the same word as "the Shephelah" with a suffix, "its lowland": the low land that belongs to the hill country of Israel.
- **"Even from the mount Halak, that goeth up to Seir, even unto Baal-gad in the valley of Lebanon … and smote them, and slew them" → "from Mount Halak, which goes up to Seir, and as far as Baal-gad in the plain of the Lebanon … and he struck them down and put them to death"** (v17) — Hebrew הָהָר הֶחָלָק, "the smooth (bare) mountain"; "Lebanon" has the article; הֵמִית "put to death".
- **"Joshua made war a long time with all those kings" → "Many days Joshua made war with all these kings"** (v18) — Hebrew יָמִים רַבִּים, first in the sentence.
- **"save the Hivites the inhabitants of Gibeon: all other they took in battle" → "except the Hivite, those who dwelt in Gibeon; the whole they took in war"** (v19) — Hebrew הַחִוִּי, a singular; אֶת־הַכֹּל "the whole" (Deut 2:36); "other" is not in the Hebrew. מִלְחָמָה is "war" in v18, 19, 20, 23. הִשְׁלִימָה "made peace" is the verb of שָׁלוֹם *shalom* (Deut 20:12).
- **"For it was of the LORD to harden their hearts, that they should come against Israel in battle, that he might destroy them utterly, and that they might have no favour, but that he might destroy them" → "For it was from Yahweh, to make their heart strong to meet the war with Israel, in order to devote them to destruction, that there might be no favor for them, but in order to destroy them"** (v20) — Hebrew לְחַזֵּק אֶת־לִבָּם, the verb חִזֵּק "make strong" used of the heart of Pharaoh (Exod 4:21; 9:12; 14:4): "harden" is another verb (הִקְשָׁה). לִקְרַאת הַמִּלְחָמָה, literally "toward the war"; "come" is not in the Hebrew. The two infinitives הַחֲרִימָם, הַשְׁמִידָם have no subject: "he" (Yahweh) or "they" (Israel) is not said. תְּחִנָּה, from חָנַן "show favor" (Deut 7:2 "you shall not show them favor"); elsewhere the word is a plea for favor.
- **"cut off the Anakims from the mountains … Joshua destroyed them utterly with their cities" → "cut off the Anakim from the hill country … with their cities Joshua devoted them to destruction"** (v21) — Hebrew הִכְרִית; עֲנָקִים is already a plural.
- **"There was none of the Anakims left … there remained" → "No Anakim remained … were they left"** (v22) — Hebrew נוֹתַר "remain" and נִשְׁאַר "be left", two verbs.
- **"according to their divisions by their tribes. And the land rested from war" → "according to their divisions, by their tribes. And the land was quiet from war"** (v23) — Hebrew מַחְלְקֹת, from חָלַק "divide"; שֵׁבֶט "tribe" (literally "rod"); שָׁקְטָה "was quiet, undisturbed": "rest" is another verb (נוּחַ).

### Notes
- **Northern campaign** — parallels the southern campaign of ch 10. Hazor was Canaan's leading city; its fall is decisive.
- **Hazor's historical significance** — the largest Canaanite city-state, ~200 acres, strategic north-south crossroads. Yadin's excavations (1955–1969) found a massive Late Bronze Age destruction layer.
- **Horses and chariots** (v4, 6, 9) — the first major chariot force Israel encounters; Yahweh commands the horses to be hamstrung and the chariots burned.
- **"Land of Goshen"** (v16) — a region of the south of the land (10:41; 15:51), distinct from the Egyptian Goshen of Genesis and Exodus.
- **"To make their heart strong"** (v20) — the same verb as of the heart of Pharaoh (Exod 4–14): the resistance of the kings is said to be from Yahweh.
- **Anakim left** (v22) — in Gaza, Gath, Ashdod, later cities of the Philistines. Goliath is of Gath (1 Sam 17).
- **"The land was quiet from war"** (v23) — repeated in 14:15. Ch 12 lists the kings, ch 13 and following give the land.
