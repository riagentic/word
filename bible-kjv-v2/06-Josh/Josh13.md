[14] Only to the tribe of Levi he gave no inheritance; the offerings by fire of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that is his inheritance, as he spoke to him.

[21] and all the cities of the tableland, and all the kingdom of ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ king of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧, who reigned in ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧, whom ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ struck down, him and the chieftains of ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧, Evi and ⟦rekem|Reqem|Rekem⟧ and ⟦zur|Tzur|Zur⟧ and ⟦hur|Chur|Hur⟧ and ⟦reba|Reva|Reba⟧, the princes of ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧, who dwelt in the land.

[26] and from ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧ as far as ⟦ramath-mizpeh|Ramat HaMitzpeh|Ramath-mizpeh⟧ and Betonim, and from ⟦mahanaim|Machanayim|Mahanaim⟧ as far as the border of ⟦debir|Devir|Debir⟧;

[30] And their border was from ⟦mahanaim|Machanayim|Mahanaim⟧: all the Bashan, all the kingdom of Og king of the Bashan, and all ⟦havvoth-jair|Chavvot Yair|Havvoth-jair⟧, which are in the Bashan, sixty cities;

[31] and half of ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ and ⟦ashtaroth|Ashtarot|Ashtaroth⟧ and Edrei, the cities of the kingdom of Og in the Bashan, were for the sons of Machir the son of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, for the half of the sons of Machir by their clans.

[32] These are what ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ made to be inherited in the plains of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧, across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ eastward.

[33] And to the tribe of Levi ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ gave no inheritance; ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, he is their inheritance, as he spoke to them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"old and stricken in years … Thou art old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed" → "old, advanced in days … You have grown old, you are advanced in days, and of the land very much is left, to possess it"** (v1) — Hebrew זָקֵן בָּא בַּיָּמִים, literally "old, gone into the days" (Gen 24:1); in the speech the two words are verbs, זָקַנְתָּה בָּאתָ בַיָּמִים. נִשְׁאֲרָה "is left" (so v2); לְרִשְׁתָּהּ "to possess it" (Deut 3:18).
- **"all the borders of the Philistines, and all Geshuri" → "all the districts of the Philistines and all the Geshurite"** (v2) — Hebrew גְּלִילוֹת, literally "circles" (the word of "Galilee"); another word than גְּבוּל "border". הַגְּשׁוּרִי, a singular with the article; a people of the south here (1 Sam 27:8), another than the Geshurite of v11, 13.
- **"From Sihor, which is before Egypt, even unto the borders of Ekron northward, which is counted to the Canaanite: five lords of the Philistines; the Gazathites, and the Ashdothites, the Eshkalonites, the Gittites, and the Ekronites; also the Avites" → "from the Shihor, which is facing Egypt, and as far as the border of Ekron northward — it is reckoned to the Canaanite —, the five lords of the Philistines: the Gazite and the Ashdodite, the Ashkelonite, the Gittite and the Ekronite; and the Avvim"** (v3) — Hebrew הַשִּׁיחוֹר, with the article, a water on the side of Egypt (which one is not certain); עַל־פְּנֵי "on the face of", facing (Deut 34:1); תֵּחָשֵׁב "is reckoned" (the verb of Gen 15:6). סַרְנֵי, a word used only of the rulers of the five Philistine cities; "lords" is kept (the same English as for אָדוֹן). The five are named by singulars with the article, "the Gazite …", and "and" stands where the Hebrew has it. The Avvim: Deut 2:23.
- **"From the south, all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah that is beside the Sidonians, unto Aphek, to the borders of the Amorites" → "on the south, all the land of the Canaanite, and Mearah, which belongs to the Sidonians, as far as Aphek, as far as the border of the Amorite"** (v4) — Hebrew מִתֵּימָן, "from Teman", the south. The Masoretic verse division puts these words at the head of v4; many join them to "the Avvim" of v3 ("the Avvim on the south", Deut 2:23). מְעָרָה is the word "cave"; it is taken here as a name, *Mearah* (or "from Arah", מֵעָרָה); the meaning is not certain. אֲשֶׁר לַצִּידֹנִים, "which is the Sidonians'".
- **"And the land of the Giblites, and all Lebanon, toward the sunrising … unto the entering into Hamath" → "and the land of the Gebalite, and all the Lebanon toward the rising of the sun … as far as Lebo-hamath"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהָאָרֶץ הַגִּבְלִי, literally "and the land, the Gebalite" (the man of Gebal, Byblos); "Lebanon" has the article; לְבוֹא חֲמָת, "the coming to Hamath", commonly taken as a place name (Num 34:8).
- **"All the inhabitants of the hill country … them will I drive out from before the children of Israel: only divide thou it by lot unto the Israelites for an inheritance" → "all those who dwell in the hill country … I myself will dispossess them from before the sons of Israel. Only make it fall to Israel as an inheritance"** (v6) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי "I", written out; הוֹרִישׁ "dispossess" (so v12, 13); הַפִּלֶהָ, "make it fall": the causative of the verb of Num 34:2 ("the land that shall fall to you as an inheritance"), said of the lot; "by lot" is not a word of the Hebrew here. צִידֹנִים has no article in "all the Sidonians".
- **"Now therefore divide this land for an inheritance unto the nine tribes, and the half tribe of Manasseh" → "And now, divide this land as an inheritance to the nine tribes and the half of the tribe of Manasseh"** (v7) — Hebrew חַלֵּק "divide"; שֵׁבֶט "tribe" (literally "rod") in v7, 14, 29a, 33; the other word, מַטֶּה (literally "staff"), in v15, 24, 29b. Both are "tribe"; they stand side by side in v29: "the half of the tribe (שֵׁבֶט) of Manasseh … the half of the tribe (מַטֶּה) of the sons of Manasseh". "Manasseh" has the article in v7.
- **"With whom the Reubenites and the Gadites have received their inheritance … beyond Jordan eastward, even as Moses the servant of the LORD gave them" → "With it the Reubenite and the Gadite have taken their inheritance … across the Jordan eastward, as Moses the servant of Yahweh gave to them"** (v8) — Hebrew עִמּוֹ, "with him / with it": the word looks back to "the half of the tribe of Manasseh" in v7, though the half that took its land across the Jordan is the other half; the Hebrew does not say "the other half", and nothing is supplied. לָקְחוּ "have taken" (Num 34:14, 15); the two tribes are singulars with the article.
- **"From Aroer, that is upon the bank of the river Arnon, and the city that is in the midst of the river, and all the plain of Medeba unto Dibon" → "from Aroer, which is on the edge of the wadi Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the wadi, and all the tableland, Medeba as far as Dibon"** (v9, 16) — the words of Deut 2:36; 3:12. נַחַל "wadi"; הַמִּישֹׁר "the tableland" (Deut 3:10) has the article and so is not joined to "Medeba" as "the tableland of": the name stands beside it; in v16 the Hebrew has עַל־מֵידְבָא, "by (upon) Medeba".
- **"unto the border of the children of Ammon; and Gilead, and the border of the Geshurites and Maachathites" → "as far as the border of the sons of Ammon; and Gilead, and the border of the Geshurite and the Maacathite"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew גְּבוּל "border" in every place in this chapter (v3, 4, 10, 11, 16, 23, 25, 26, 27, 30); the word also means the land inside a border (so in v11, 16, 25, 30, where KJV has "coast").
- **"which reigned in Ashtaroth and in Edrei, who remained of the remnant of the giants: for these did Moses smite, and cast them out" → "who reigned in Ashtaroth and in Edrei; he was left of the rest of the Rephaim. And Moses struck them down and dispossessed them"** (v12) — the words of Deut 3:11; הָרְפָאִים, the name of a people; וַיַּכֵּם … וַיֹּרִשֵׁם, main clauses.
- **"the Geshurites and the Maachathites dwell among the Israelites until this day" → "Geshur and Maacath dwelt in the midst of Israel, to this day"** (v13) — the second time the Hebrew has the names of the lands, גְּשׁוּר וּמַעֲכָת, with a singular verb, וַיֵּשֶׁב.
- **"Only unto the tribe of Levi he gave none inheritance; the sacrifices of the LORD God of Israel made by fire are their inheritance, as he said unto them" → "Only to the tribe of Levi he gave no inheritance; the offerings by fire of Yahweh the Elohim of Israel, that is his inheritance, as he spoke to him"** (v14) — Hebrew אִשֵּׁי יְהוָה, "the offerings by fire of Yahweh" (Deut 18:1; the word is commonly derived from אֵשׁ "fire"; the derivation is not certain). The pronouns are singular: הוּא נַחֲלָתוֹ … דִּבֶּר־לוֹ, "that is his inheritance … spoke to him" (Deut 18:2); in v33 they are plural. Who "gave" is not named in this verse (Moses, v33). שֵׁבֶט הַלֵּוִי, "the tribe of the Levite", with the article (Deut 10:8).
- **"Moses gave unto the tribe of the children of Reuben inheritance according to their families" → "Moses gave to the tribe of the sons of Reuben by their clans"** (v15, 24, 29) — the Hebrew verb has no object in these verses; "inheritance" is not supplied. מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan".
- **"And their coast was from Aroer" → "And they had the border from Aroer"** (v16, 25) — Hebrew וַיְהִי לָהֶם הַגְּבוּל, literally "and the border was for them" (Num 34:3).
- **"Heshbon, and all her cities that are in the plain; Dibon, and Bamoth-baal, and Beth-baal-meon, and Jahaza …" → "Heshbon and all its cities that are on the tableland: Dibon and Bamoth-baal and Beth-baal-meon and Jahaz …"** (v17–20) — each name is joined by "and" as in the Hebrew. יַהְצָה is "Jahaz" with the ending of direction grown onto the name (Num 21:23). **"in the mount of the valley" → "on the mountain of the valley"** (v19) — Hebrew בְּהַר הָעֵמֶק. **"Ashdoth-pisgah" → "the slopes of the Pisgah"** (v20) — Deut 3:17.
- **"whom Moses smote with the princes of Midian … which were dukes of Sihon, dwelling in the country" → "whom Moses struck down, him and the chieftains of Midian … the princes of Sihon, who dwelt in the land"** (v21) — Hebrew אֹתוֹ וְאֶת־נְשִׂיאֵי מִדְיָן, "him and the chieftains of Midian" (נָשִׂיא; in Num 31:8 the five are "kings of Midian"); נְסִיכֵי סִיחוֹן, a rare word for a ruler (perhaps "one installed"), here of men under Sihon; "princes" is also the English of שַׂר.
- **"Balaam also the son of Beor, the soothsayer, did the children of Israel slay with the sword among them that were slain by them" → "And Balaam the son of Beor, the diviner, the sons of Israel killed with the sword, along with their slain"** (v22) — Hebrew הַקּוֹסֵם, the one who practices קֶסֶם "divination" (Num 22:7; Deut 18:10); הָרַג "kill"; אֶל־חַלְלֵיהֶם, literally "to their slain": added to them. Num 31:8 has עַל־חַלְלֵיהֶם "together with their slain".
- **"And the border of the children of Reuben was Jordan, and the border thereof … the cities and the villages thereof" → "And the border of the sons of Reuben was the Jordan and the border … the cities and their villages"** (v23, 27, 28) — Hebrew הַיַּרְדֵּן וּגְבוּל, literally "the Jordan and border", as Num 34:6; Deut 3:17: what "and border" adds is not certain (the river and its bank land, or "the Jordan, as a border"). חָצֵר "village", an unwalled settlement.
- **"unto Aroer that is before Rabbah" → "as far as Aroer, which is facing Rabbah"** (v25) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי; another Aroer than that of v9, 16.
- **"unto the border of Debir"** (v26) — Hebrew גְּבוּל לִדְבִר. The form may be the name "Lidebir", or "Debir" with לְ; it is commonly thought to be the Lo-debar of 2 Sam 9:4. Not the Debir of 11:21.
- **"And in the valley, Beth-aram … the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon, Jordan and his border, even unto the edge of the sea of Chinnereth on the other side Jordan eastward" → "and in the valley: Beth-haram … the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon, the Jordan and the border, as far as the end of the sea of Chinnereth, across the Jordan eastward"** (v27) — Hebrew עֵמֶק "valley"; קְצֵה "end" (Num 34:3); עֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן "across the Jordan".
- **"And Moses gave inheritance unto the half tribe of Manasseh: and this was the possession of the half tribe of the children of Manasseh" → "And Moses gave to the half of the tribe of Manasseh; and it was for the half of the tribe of the sons of Manasseh"** (v29) — Hebrew וַיְהִי לַ, "and it was for"; no word "possession". The first "tribe" is שֵׁבֶט, the second מַטֶּה.
- **"all the towns of Jair, which are in Bashan, threescore cities" → "all Havvoth-jair, which are in the Bashan, sixty cities"** (v30) — Hebrew חַוֺּת יָאִיר, "the tent villages of Jair" (Num 32:41; Deut 3:14).
- **"were pertaining unto the children of Machir the son of Manasseh, even to the one half of the children of Machir" → "were for the sons of Machir the son of Manasseh, for the half of the sons of Machir"** (v31) — Hebrew לִבְנֵי … לַחֲצִי.
- **"These are the countries which Moses did distribute for inheritance in the plains of Moab, on the other side Jordan, by Jericho, eastward" → "These are what Moses made to be inherited in the plains of Moab, across the Jordan of Jericho eastward"** (v32) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־נִחַל, the causative-intensive stem of נָחַל "inherit" (Num 34:29): "countries" and "distribute" are not in the Hebrew. עַרְבוֹת מוֹאָב, the dry steppe of Moab; מֵעֵבֶר לְיַרְדֵּן יְרִיחוֹ, "across the Jordan of Jericho" (Num 22:1; 34:15).
- **"But unto the tribe of Levi Moses gave not any inheritance: the LORD God of Israel was their inheritance, as he said unto them" → "And to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance; Yahweh the Elohim of Israel, he is their inheritance, as he spoke to them"** (v33) — Hebrew הוּא נַחֲלָתָם, "he is their inheritance", without a verb (Deut 10:9; 18:2); דִּבֶּר "spoke".

### Notes
- **Joshua old, land left** (v1–6) — the land that is left: the districts of the Philistines in the south, and the north as far as Lebo-hamath.
- **The tribes across the Jordan** (v8–32) — what Moses gave (Num 32; Deut 3:8–17) is told again: Reuben in the south, Gad in Gilead, the half of Manasseh in the Bashan.
- **Balaam's death** (v22) — Num 31:8; his words are in Num 22–24.
- **Geshur and Maacath** (v13) — peoples not dispossessed; Judg 1 has a like list for the land west of the Jordan.
- **Levi** (v14, 33) — no inheritance of land; said twice in this chapter, once of the offerings by fire of Yahweh, once of Yahweh himself.
