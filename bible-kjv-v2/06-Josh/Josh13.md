# Joshua 13

[1] Now Joshua was old and advanced in years, and Yahweh said to him, "You are old and advanced in years, and very much of the land still remains to be possessed.

[2] This is the land that still remains: all the regions of the Philistines and all those of the Geshurites,

[3] from the Shihor, which is east of Egypt, to the border of Ekron on the north, which is counted as Canaanite territory; the five lords of the Philistines — those of Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron — and those of the Avvim,

[4] in the south; all the land of the Canaanites; and Mearah, which belongs to the Sidonians, to Aphek, to the border of the Amorites;

[5] and the land of the Gebalites, and all of Lebanon toward the sunrise, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath;

[6] all the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, all the Sidonians. I myself will drive them out from before the children of Israel. Only allot the land to Israel for an inheritance, as I have commanded you.

[7] Now therefore apportion this land for an inheritance to the nine tribes and to the half-tribe of Manasseh."

[8] With the other half the Reubenites and the Gadites had received their inheritance, which Moses gave them beyond the Jordan eastward, as Moses the servant of Yahweh gave them:

[9] from Aroer on the edge of the wadi Arnon — and the city in the middle of the valley — and all the tableland of Medeba as far as Dibon;

[10] and all the cities of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the border of the children of Ammon;

[11] and Gilead, and the region of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salcah;

[12] all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ashtaroth and Edrei — he alone remained of the remnant of the Rephaim — these Moses had struck down and dispossessed.

[13] But the children of Israel did not drive out the Geshurites or the Maacathites; the Geshurites and the Maacathites dwell in the midst of Israel to this day.

[14] To the tribe of Levi alone he gave no inheritance; the offerings made by fire to Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel are their inheritance, as he said to them.

[15] Moses gave to the tribe of the children of Reuben their inheritance by their clans.

[16] Their territory was from Aroer, on the edge of the wadi Arnon — and the city in the middle of the valley — and all the tableland by Medeba;

[17] Heshbon, and all its cities that are on the tableland; Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon,

[18] Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,

[19] Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill of the valley,

[20] Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, Beth-jeshimoth,

[21] and all the cities of the tableland, and all the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who reigned in Heshbon, whom Moses struck down with the chiefs of Midian — Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, the princes of Sihon, who dwelt in the land.

[22] And Balaam son of Beor, the diviner, the children of Israel killed with the sword among the rest of their slain.

[23] The border of the children of Reuben was the Jordan and its bank. This was the inheritance of the children of Reuben by their clans — the cities and their villages.

[24] Moses gave inheritance to the tribe of Gad, to the children of Gad, by their clans.

[25] Their territory was Jazer, and all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the children of Ammon, as far as Aroer, which is east of Rabbah;

[26] and from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the border of Debir;

[27] and in the valley, Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon — the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon — the Jordan and its bank, as far as the edge of the Sea of Chinnereth, beyond the Jordan to the east.

[28] This was the inheritance of the children of Gad by their clans — the cities and their villages.

[29] Moses gave inheritance to the half-tribe of Manasseh; it went to the half-tribe of the children of Manasseh by their clans.

[30] Their territory was from Mahanaim, all of Bashan, all the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the Havvoth-jair that are in Bashan — sixty cities.

[31] Half of Gilead, Ashtaroth, and Edrei — cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan — belonged to the children of Machir son of Manasseh, to half the children of Machir by their clans.

[32] These are the allotments that Moses made for inheritance in the plains of Moab, beyond the Jordan, east of Jericho.

[33] But to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance; Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel was their inheritance, as he had said to them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Sihor" (v3)** — Egyptian border waterway; likely the eastern Nile branch or the *nachal* of Egypt.
- **"Giblites" → "Gebalites"** (v5) — from Gebal (Byblos).
- **"giants" → "Rephaim"** (v12).
- **"soothsayer" → "diviner"** (v22).
- **"dukes" → "princes"** (v21) — archaic.
- **"coast" → "territory"** (v16, 25, 27, 30) — archaic.

### Notes
- **Joshua old, land unfinished** (v1–6) — the conquest is incomplete. Five Philistine cities and parts of the north remain. The book acknowledges limits.
- **Transjordan tribes reviewed** (v8–32) — Moses' earlier allotments (Num 32) summarized. Reuben: southern Transjordan. Gad: central Gilead. Half-Manasseh: northern Bashan.
- **Balaam's death** (v22) — mentioned among the slain. Closes the Balaam-arc from Num 22–24, 31.
- **Incomplete conquest** — Judg 1 opens with a list of peoples *not* dispossessed. Joshua records the ideal inheritance; Judges documents the failure to complete it.
- **Levite exception** (v14, 33) — no land. Yahweh is their inheritance. Repeated doubly in this chapter.
