## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"suburbs" → "pasturelands"** (throughout) — *migrash*, as in Numbers.

### Notes
- **48 Levitical cities** (v41) — fulfills Num 35:7. Six are cities of refuge; 42 additional. Scattered across all 12 tribes.
- **Aaronic priests within Judah** (v13–19) — priests concentrated near what will become Jerusalem's temple zone. Strategic clustering.
- **1 Chr 6:54–81** offers a parallel listing with slight variations in names.
- **Climactic affirmation** (v43–45) — triple declaration of covenant-fulfillment: land given, rest secured, no enemy stood, every promise kept. The land-gift is complete.
- **Tension with later history** — v43–45 declares absolute fulfillment; Judges will narrate continued struggles. Two theological readings: (1) divine gift was complete; failure to possess was Israel's; (2) the text is idealized summary; conquest was incremental.
- **Pasturelands 2000 cubits** (cf. Num 35:5) — each Levitical city came with a green-belt for livestock.
