## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, and in the lists each "and" stands where the Hebrew has one. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"the heads of the fathers"** (v1) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי אֲבוֹת, as Exod 6:25; Num 31:26: the heads of the fathers' houses; "houses" is not written. **"came near" → "drew near"** — Hebrew נָגַשׁ. **"tribe(s)"** is מַטֶּה, literally "staff", throughout the chapter, except in v16, where "these two tribes" has שֵׁבֶט, literally "rod"; the two words name the same thing.
- **"to give us cities to dwell in, with the suburbs thereof for our cattle" → "to give us cities to dwell in, and their pasture lands for our beasts"** (v2) — Hebrew לָתֶת "to give"; בְּהֵמָה "beasts", as Num 35:3.
- **"suburbs" → "pasture lands"** (throughout) — Hebrew מִגְרָשׁ *migrash*, the open land around a city (Num 35:2–5); not modern suburbs. With a city's name the form is מִגְרָשֶׁהָ, a plural written short (v42 writes it in full, מִגְרָשֶׁיהָ): "its pasture lands". The Hebrew says each time "and its pasture lands", not "with".
- **"at the commandment of the LORD" → "at the mouth of Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶל־פִּי יְהוָה.
- **"the children of Aaron the priest … had by lot" → "the sons of Aaron the priest … had … by lot"** (v4–7) — the Hebrew is "and there were for the sons of Aaron … thirteen cities" (v4), and in v5–7 no verb at all: "and for the sons of Kohath who remained … ten cities"; "had" renders the "for". The order of the Hebrew is kept: the tribes first, the number last. **"the tribe of Simeon"** (v4) — the Hebrew has here the gentilic with the article, מַטֵּה הַשִּׁמְעֹנִי, "the tribe of the Simeonite"; so also "the Kohathite" (v4, 10) and "the Gershonite" (v33) are singulars with the article. **"the rest of the children of Kohath" → "the sons of Kohath who remained"** (v5, 20, 26, 34, 40) — Hebrew הַנּוֹתָרִים. **"the half tribe" → "the half of the tribe"** (v5, 6, 25, 27) — Hebrew חֲצִי מַטֵּה; in v25 מַחֲצִית, another form of the same word.
- **"which are here mentioned by name" → "which one calls by name"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־יִקְרָא אֶתְהֶן בְּשֵׁם, an active verb with no subject named. **"Which the children of Aaron … had: for theirs was the first lot" → "and they were for the sons of Aaron … for theirs was the lot first"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיְהִי לִבְנֵי אַהֲרֹן, literally "and it was for the sons of Aaron"; רִיאשֹׁנָה is an adverb, "first".
- **"the city of Arba the father of Anak" → "Qiryat Arba, the father of the Anaq"** (v11) — Hebrew קִרְיַת אַרְבַּע אֲבִי הָעֲנוֹק: the name of the city means "city of Arba" (or "of four"); "the father of" stands beside the name Arba (15:13; 14:15 "the great man among the Anakim"). The name has the article and is spelled here עֲנוֹק *Anoq*. **"with the suburbs thereof round about it" → "and its pasture lands around it"**.
- **"the fields of the city, and the villages thereof, gave they to Caleb … for his possession" → "the field of the city and its villages they gave to Caleb … as his possession"** (v12) — Hebrew שְׂדֵה, singular; בַּאֲחֻזָּתוֹ, literally "in his possession". חָצֵר is an unwalled settlement.
- **"Hebron with her suburbs, to be a city of refuge for the slayer" → "the city of refuge of the murderer, Hebron, and its pasture lands"** (v13, 21, 27, 32, 38) — Hebrew אֶת־עִיר מִקְלַט הָרֹצֵחַ, a genitive chain that stands before the name of the city. רֹצֵחַ is "murderer", the word of Num 35 and Josh 20:3, used there also of the man who killed in error.
- **Counts and names** (v13–39) — every name and number was set against the Hebrew: nine (v16), four (v18), thirteen (v19), four (v22), four (v24), two (v25), ten (v26), two (v27), four (v29), four (v31), three (v32), thirteen (v33), four (v35), four (v37), four (v39), twelve (v40), forty-eight (v41). In v33 the Hebrew says "thirteen city" (עִיר, singular). **"Golan"** (v27) — the written form (ketiv) is גלון "Galon"; the reading tradition (qere) is גּוֹלָן, as 20:8; the qere is translated. **"Jahazah" → "Yahtzah"** (v36) — Hebrew יַהְצָה, the longer form of the name Jahaz (13:18). Gath-rimmon is named twice, for Dan (v24) and for the half of Manasseh (v25); the Hebrew is kept (1 Chr 6:70 has another name in the second place: from memory).
- **Verses 36–37** — the Hebrew text printed for this pass (the Westminster Leningrad Codex as our tool gives it) has both verses, without brackets, and they are translated as printed: Bezer, Yahtzah, Kedemoth, Mephaath, "four cities". They are known to be missing from the first hand of a number of Masoretic manuscripts and from some printed Hebrew Bibles, which then count 43 verses in this chapter; without them the twelve cities of v40 and the forty-eight of v41 do not add up (stated from memory, not checked with the tools). As printed, v36 does not call Bezer "the city of refuge of the murderer" (so 20:8) and does not say "in the wilderness"; nothing is supplied.
- **"four cities in all" → "all the cities, four"** (v39) — Hebrew כָּל־עָרִים אַרְבַּע. **"So all the cities for the children of Merari … were by their lot twelve cities" → "All the cities for the sons of Merari … their lot was twelve cities"** (v40) — Hebrew וַיְהִי גּוֹרָלָם עָרִים שְׁתֵּים עֶשְׂרֵה; the sentence begins anew.
- **"within the possession" → "in the midst of the possession"** (v41) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ אֲחֻזַּת.
- **"These cities were every one with their suburbs round about them: thus were all these cities" → "These cities were city by city, each with its pasture lands around it; so it was for all these cities"** (v42) — Hebrew תִּהְיֶינָה הֶעָרִים הָאֵלֶּה עִיר עִיר וּמִגְרָשֶׁיהָ סְבִיבֹתֶיהָ, literally "these cities shall be / were, city, city, and its pasture lands around it; so for all these cities"; "each with" and "it was" are supplied.
- **"sware" → "swore"; "they possessed it" → "they took possession of it"** (v43) — Hebrew וַיִּרָשׁוּהָ, יָרַשׁ with the land as object.
- **"gave them rest round about" → "gave them rest all around"** (v44) — Hebrew וַיָּנַח … מִסָּבִיב (Deut 12:10). **"there stood not a man of all their enemies before them; the LORD delivered all their enemies into their hand" → "not a man of all their enemies stood before them; all their enemies Yahweh gave into their hand"** — Hebrew בִּפְנֵיהֶם, literally "in their face"; "all their enemies" stands first; נָתַן "gave".
- **"There failed not ought of any good thing which the LORD had spoken … all came to pass" → "Not a word fell of all the good word that Yahweh spoke … all of it came"** (v45) — Hebrew לֹא־נָפַל דָּבָר מִכֹּל הַדָּבָר הַטּוֹב … הַכֹּל בָּא: דָּבָר "word" twice, the second time a singular for the whole promise; נָפַל "fall" = fail, fall to the ground; בָּא "came" = came to pass. So 23:14.

### Notes
- **48 cities of the Levites** (v41) — as Num 35:7: the six cities of refuge and forty-two more, taken from all the tribes.
- **The sons of Aaron in Judah, Simeon and Benjamin** (v13–19) — the priests' cities lie in the south, around the later Jerusalem.
- **1 Chr 6:54–81** gives the same list with some differences in names.
- **The summary** (v43–45) — the land given, rest all around, no enemy standing, no word fallen. Josh 13:1–6; 23:4–13 and Judges 1 speak of land and nations that remain; the book sets both side by side.
- **The pasture lands** — measured in Num 35:4–5.
