# Joshua 6

[1] Now ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ was tightly shut up because of the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; no one went out and no one came in.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "See, I have given ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧ and its king — mighty warriors — into your hand.

[4] Seven priests shall bear seven rams'-horn trumpets before the ark; and on the seventh day you shall march around the city seven times, and the priests shall blow the trumpets.

[5] When they make a long blast with the ram's horn, when you hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people shall shout with a great shout; the wall of the city shall fall down flat, and the people shall go up, each one straight ahead."

[7] He said to the people, "Pass on, and march around the city; let the armed men pass on before the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[8] So when ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ had spoken to the people, the seven priests bearing the seven rams'-horn trumpets before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ passed on and blew the trumpets, with the ark of the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ following them.

[10] But ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ commanded the people, "You shall not shout or let your voice be heard, nor shall any word come out of your mouth, until the day I tell you to shout. Then you shall shout."

[11] So he had the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ go around the city, circling it once, and they came into the camp and lodged in the camp.

[12] ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] The seven priests bearing the seven rams'-horn trumpets before the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went on continually, blowing the trumpets. The armed men went before them, but the rearguard came after the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, while the priests went on blowing the trumpets.

[15] On the seventh day, they rose early at the dawn of day and marched around the city in the same manner seven times; only on that day did they march around the city seven times.

[16] At the seventh time, when the priests blew the trumpets, ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said to the people, "Shout! For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given you the city.

[20] So the people shouted, and the trumpets were blown. When the people heard the sound of the trumpet, the people shouted with a great shout, and the wall fell down flat, so that the people went up into the city, each one straight ahead, and they captured the city.

[21] They devoted to destruction all that was in the city — man and woman, young and old, ox and sheep and donkey — with the edge of the sword.

[22] To the two men who had scouted the land, ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ said, "Go into the prostitute's house and bring out from there the woman and all that she has, as you swore to her."

[25] But ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ spared ⟦rahab|Rachav|Rahab⟧ the prostitute, her father's household, and all that she had; she has dwelt in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to this day, because she hid the messengers whom ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ sent to scout ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧.

[26] At that time ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ made them swear an oath: "Cursed before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be the man who rises up and rebuilds this city, ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧. At the cost of his firstborn he shall lay its foundation; at the cost of his youngest he shall set up its gates."

[27] So ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was with ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, and his fame was throughout all the land.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"straitly shut up" → "tightly shut up"** (v1) — archaic intensifier.
- **"accursed / the accursed thing" → "devoted to destruction / devoted things"** (v17, 18) — *cherem*.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v19, 24).
- **"harlot" → "prostitute"** (v17, 22, 25).
- **"rereward" → "rearguard"** (v9, 13).
- **"noised" → "was throughout"** (v27) — modernized.

### Notes
- **Seven-day siege** — not a military assault but a liturgical procession. Seven priests + seven trumpets + seven days + seven circuits on the seventh day = sevenfold sanctification of the capture.
- **The shout and the wall** — the wall falls not by battering ram but by voice and trumpet. Yahweh's *cherem* on Jericho is delivered not by Israel but through Israel.
- **Rahab saved** (v22–25) — the scarlet-cord promise is kept. She and her household are preserved; she "dwells in Israel to this day" — actually absorbed into the tribe of Judah (Mt 1:5).
- **Cherem violated in ch 7** — the silver and gold being "devoted" establishes the coming Achan narrative; his secret appropriation of silver and gold leads to defeat at Ai.
- **Jericho curse** (v26) — 1 Kgs 16:34 fulfills: Hiel of Bethel rebuilt Jericho in Ahab's time; his firstborn and youngest died.
- **Archaeology of Jericho** — much debated. Tell es-Sultan has Bronze Age fortifications that appear to have collapsed suddenly; dating contested between 15th-cent (matching traditional chronology) and Late Bronze Age. The site has been abandoned several times across history.
- **"House of Yahweh"** (v24) — anachronistic reference (the Temple did not yet exist). The portable-tabernacle's treasury; later read back as proto-Temple treasury.
