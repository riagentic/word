# Joshua 5

[1] And it came to pass, when all the kings of the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ who were across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ westward, and all the kings of the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ who were by the sea, heard how ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had dried up the waters of the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ until they had passed over, that their heart melted, and there was no more spirit in them because of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] At that time ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "Make for yourself swords of flint, and return, circumcise the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a second time."

[3] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ made for himself swords of flint and circumcised the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ at ⟦gibeath-haaraloth|Givat HaAralot|Gibeath-haaraloth⟧.

[4] And this is the matter for which ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ circumcised: all the people that came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, the males, all the men of war, had died in the wilderness on the way, when they came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[5] For all the people who came out were circumcised; and all the people who were born in the wilderness on the way, when they came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, they had not circumcised.

[6] For forty years the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ walked in the wilderness, until all the nation came to an end, the men of war who came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, who had not listened to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had sworn not to let them see the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had sworn to their fathers to give to us, a land flowing with milk and honey.

[7] And their sons he raised up in their place; them ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ circumcised, for they were uncircumcised, for they had not circumcised them on the way.

[8] And it came to pass, when all the nation had come to an end of being circumcised, that they dwelt in their place in the camp until they revived.

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "Today I have rolled away the reproach of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ from upon you." And he called the name of that place Gilgal, to this day.

[10] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ camped at Gilgal, and they made the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ on the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, in the plains of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧.

[11] And they ate of the produce of the land on the morrow of the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧, unleavened bread and parched grain, on that very day.

[12] And the manna ceased on the morrow, when they ate of the produce of the land, and there was no more manna for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and they ate of the yield of the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧ in that year.

[13] And it came to pass, when ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ was at ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, that he lifted up his eyes and saw, and behold, a man standing opposite him, and his sword drawn in his hand. And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ went to him and said to him, "Are you for us, or for our adversaries?"

[14] And he said, "No, for I am the commander of the host of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; now I have come." And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ fell on his face to the earth and bowed down, and he said to him, "What is my lord speaking to his servant?"

[15] And the commander of the host of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧, "Take off your sandal from your foot, for the place on which you are standing, it is holy." And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ did so.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"all the kings of the Amorites, which were on the side of Jordan westward, and all the kings of the Canaanites" → "all the kings of the Amorite who were across the Jordan westward, and all the kings of the Canaanite"** (v1) — Hebrew הָאֱמֹרִי, הַכְּנַעֲנִי, singulars with the article; בְּעֵבֶר הַיַּרְדֵּן יָמָּה: "across the Jordan", here of the west side, with יָמָּה, literally "seaward".
- **"heard that the LORD had dried up … until we were passed over" → "heard how Yahweh had dried up … until they had passed over"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵת אֲשֶׁר "that which" (2:10). The reading tradition (qere) עָבְרָם "they had passed over" is translated; the written form (ketiv) is עברנו "we had passed over". KJV follows the ketiv.
- **"their heart melted, neither was there spirit in them any more"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּמַּס לְבָבָם, the words of 2:11; "heart" is singular; רוּחַ "spirit, breath". **"because of the children of Israel" → "because of the sons of Israel"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי "from the face of", the same word as "from before" earlier in the verse; בְּנֵי "sons of" (throughout).
- **"Make thee sharp knives" → "Make for yourself swords of flint"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew חַרְבוֹת צֻרִים *charvot tzurim*: חֶרֶב is the word for "sword", used also of a cutting tool (Exod 20:25); here knives are meant. צֹר is "flint" (Exod 4:25, of the same act); "sharp" is KJV's reading of it.
- **"circumcise again the children of Israel the second time" → "return, circumcise the sons of Israel a second time"** (v2) — Hebrew וְשׁוּב מֹל … שֵׁנִית: "return" with a second verb is the Hebrew way of saying "do again" (Deut 30:3, 8, 9), so "again" is said twice, by the verb and by שֵׁנִית "a second time". No man is circumcised twice: the people as a body is circumcised again (v4–7). The Septuagint reads the first word otherwise (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"at the hill of the foreskins" → "at Givat HaAralot"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶל־גִּבְעַת הָעֲרָלוֹת *el-Giv'at ha-aralot*, "at (or to) the hill of the foreskins". It is taken as the name of the place; the Hebrew says the words once, so the gloss is given here and not in the verse.
- **"this is the cause why Joshua did circumcise" → "this is the matter for which Joshua circumcised"** (v4) — Hebrew וְזֶה הַדָּבָר אֲשֶׁר־מָל; דָּבָר is "word" or "matter"; the verb has no object. **"that were males, even all the men of war"** → "the males, all the men of war" — הַזְּכָרִים, כֹּל אַנְשֵׁי הַמִּלְחָמָה, set side by side. **"by the way, after they came out of Egypt" → "on the way, when they came out from Egypt"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew בְּצֵאתָם, literally "in their coming out".
- **"Now all the people that came out were circumcised: but all the people that were born … them they had not circumcised" → "For all the people who came out were circumcised; and all the people who were born … they had not circumcised"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; מֻלִים, a passive participle; לֹא־מָלוּ, an active verb with no subject named, "they had not circumcised" (so also v7).
- **"till all the people that were men of war … were consumed, because they obeyed not the voice of the LORD" → "until all the nation came to an end, the men of war …, who had not listened to the voice of Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew עַד־תֹּם כָּל־הַגּוֹי, the verb of 3:16, 17; 4:1, 10, 11 (Deut 2:14–16); הַגּוֹי "the nation"; אֲשֶׁר לֹא־שָׁמְעוּ בְּקוֹל, a relative clause, "who did not listen to the voice of".
- **"unto whom the LORD sware that he would not shew them the land" → "to whom Yahweh had sworn not to let them see the land"** (v6) — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי הַרְאוֹתָם, the causative of "see" (Num 14:23). **"that he would give us"** → "to give to us" — Hebrew לָתֶת לָנוּ: "to their fathers", yet "to us". **"a land that floweth with milk and honey"** — Hebrew זָבַת חָלָב וּדְבָשׁ.
- **"their children, whom he raised up in their stead, them Joshua circumcised" → "their sons he raised up in their place; them Joshua circumcised"** (v7) — Hebrew הֵקִים תַּחְתָּם: the subject is not named (Yahweh, v6). **"because they had not circumcised them by the way" → "for they had not circumcised them on the way"** — כִּי twice in the verse.
- **"when they had done circumcising all the people, that they abode in their places in the camp, till they were whole" → "when all the nation had come to an end of being circumcised, that they dwelt in their place in the camp until they revived"** (v8) — Hebrew תַּמּוּ כָל־הַגּוֹי לְהִמּוֹל, a passive ("to be circumcised"); וַיֵּשְׁבוּ תַחְתָּם, literally "they sat under themselves": each where he was; עַד חֲיוֹתָם, literally "until their living": until they were well again (Gen 45:27).
- **"This day have I rolled away the reproach of Egypt from off you. Wherefore the name of the place is called Gilgal unto this day" → "Today I have rolled away the reproach of Egypt from upon you. And he called the name of that place Gilgal, to this day"** (v9) — Hebrew גַּלּוֹתִי *gallothi* "I have rolled", from גָּלַל "roll" (Gen 29:3, 10, of a stone), and גִּלְגָּל *Gilgal*: the name is heard as "rolling" (the word itself is "circle, wheel"; a circle of stones is commonly thought of). "(Rolling)" stood in the verse as a gloss and is taken out; the Hebrew has only the name. וַיִּקְרָא "and he called", with no subject: "one called", "it was called". "Wherefore" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the reproach of Egypt"** (v9) — Hebrew חֶרְפַּת מִצְרַיִם, a genitive that the Hebrew does not explain: the reproach that came from Egypt or was borne in Egypt (slavery), or the reproach that Egypt cast on them (Exod 32:12; Num 14:13–16), or the state of being uncircumcised (Gen 34:14 has the same word of that).
- **"encamped in Gilgal, and kept the passover" → "camped at Gilgal, and they made the Pesach"** (v10) — Hebrew בַּגִּלְגָּל, with the article; וַיַּעֲשׂוּ אֶת־הַפֶּסַח, the verb "do, make" (Num 9:5; Deut 16:1). **"at even" → "in the evening"** — Hebrew בָּעֶרֶב.
- **"they did eat of the old corn of the land on the morrow after the passover, unleavened cakes, and parched corn in the selfsame day" → "they ate of the produce of the land on the morrow of the Pesach, unleavened bread and parched grain, on that very day"** (v11) — Hebrew עֲבוּר, found only in v11, 12: "produce"; "old" is not in the word. מִמָּחֳרַת הַפֶּסַח "from the morrow of the Pesach"; מַצּוֹת וְקָלוּי; בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "in the bone of this day". The clauses are left in the Hebrew order.
- **"the manna ceased on the morrow after they had eaten of the old corn of the land" → "the manna ceased on the morrow, when they ate of the produce of the land"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁבֹּת, the verb of "Shabbat"; בְּאָכְלָם "in their eating"; the Hebrew word is מָן *man*. **"the fruit of the land of Canaan" → "the yield of the land of Canaan"** — Hebrew תְּבוּאָה, another word than "produce".
- **"when Joshua was by Jericho" → "when Joshua was at Jericho"** (v13) — Hebrew בִּירִיחוֹ, literally "in Jericho": in its land; the city is not yet taken (6:1). **"looked" → "saw"** — Hebrew וַיַּרְא. **"there stood a man over against him with his sword drawn in his hand" → "a man standing opposite him, and his sword drawn in his hand"** — Hebrew עֹמֵד לְנֶגְדּוֹ; the words of Num 22:23. **"Art thou for us, or for our adversaries?"** — Hebrew הֲלָנוּ אַתָּה אִם־לְצָרֵינוּ, literally "Are you ours, or our adversaries'?"
- **"Nay; but as captain of the host of the LORD am I now come" → "No, for I am the commander of the host of Yahweh; now I have come"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew לֹא כִּי אֲנִי שַׂר־צְבָא־יְהוָה עַתָּה בָאתִי, word for word: שַׂר "prince, chief", of an army "commander"; צָבָא "host", the word of "Yahweh of hosts". The accents divide after "Yahweh": "I am the commander … ; now I have come"; "as" is not in the Hebrew. Some manuscripts and old versions read לוֹ "to him" for לֹא "No" ("And he said to him"; cited from memory); the printed text is kept.
- **"fell on his face to the earth, and did worship, and said unto him, What saith my lord unto his servant?" → "fell on his face to the earth and bowed down, and he said to him, 'What is my lord speaking to his servant?'"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁתָּחוּ "bowed down", said before men and before God alike; אֲדֹנִי *adoni* "my lord", the form used to a man or an angel, not the divine *Adonai*; מְדַבֵּר, a participle of "speak".
- **"Loose thy shoe from off thy foot; for the place whereon thou standest is holy" → "Take off your sandal from your foot, for the place on which you are standing, it is holy"** (v15) — the words of Exod 3:5, with "sandal" and "foot" in the singular and without the word "ground": Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ הוּא, literally "it is holiness".

### Notes
- **Circumcision en masse** (v2–9) — the wilderness generation had not been circumcised in the 40-year journey. The new generation is now ritually prepared for the covenant-land. Circumcision and Passover-celebration together re-inaugurate covenantal identity.
- **"Second time"** (v2) — debated: circumcision was not "literally" second — unless referring to Israel's collective covenantal re-circumcision after exodus.
- **Manna ceases** (v12) — the day after Passover (the "second day of unleavened bread"). Wilderness provisions end; land-produce begins. Transition complete.
- **"Commander of the host of Yahweh"** (v13–15) — theophanic figure. Parallels Moses at Sinai. Jewish tradition identifies as the archangel Michael or the "Angel of Yahweh." Early Christian tradition often reads christologically.
- **"Are you for us or them?" — "No"** (v13–14) — the commander is for neither; he is Yahweh's. Joshua must join *his* side, not vice versa.
- **Holy ground** (v15) — same words as to Moses. Continuity of theophany and commission.
