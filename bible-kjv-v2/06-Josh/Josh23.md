# Joshua 23

[1] And it came to pass after many days, after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had given rest to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from all their enemies all around, and ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ was old, advanced in days,

[2] that ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ called all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, its elders and its heads and its judges and its officers, and he said to them, "I have grown old; I am advanced in days.

[3] And you, you have seen all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has done to all these nations from before you, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is the one fighting for you.

[4] See, I have made fall to you these nations that are left, as an inheritance for your tribes, from the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and all the nations that I have cut off, and the Great Sea, the going in of the sun.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he will push them from before you and dispossess them from before you, and you shall take possession of their land, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke to you.

[6] And you shall be very strong to keep and to do all that is written in the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so as not to turn aside from it, right or left,

[7] so as not to go in among these nations, these that are left with you; and by the name of their gods you shall not make mention, and you shall not make swear, and you shall not serve them, and you shall not bow down to them.

[8] But to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ you shall cling, as you have done to this day.

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has dispossessed from before you great and mighty nations; and you, not a man has stood before you to this day.

[10] One man of you pursues a thousand, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is the one fighting for you, as he spoke to you.

[11] And you shall take great heed for your souls, to love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[12] For if you surely turn back and cling to the rest of these nations, these that are left with you, and intermarry with them and go in among them, and they among you,

[13] you shall surely know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will no more dispossess these nations from before you; and they shall be to you a trap and a snare, and a whip in your sides and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from off this good ground that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given you.

[14] And behold, I am going today in the way of all the earth; and you shall know with all your heart and with all your soul that not one word has fallen of all the good words that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke concerning you; all have come to you; not one word of it has fallen.

[15] And it shall be, as all the good word has come upon you that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke to you, so ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will bring upon you all the evil word, until he has destroyed you from off this good ground that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given you,

[16] when you overstep the covenant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, which he commanded you, and go and serve other gods and bow down to them; and the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will burn against you, and you shall perish quickly from off the good land that he has given you."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"a long time after" → "after many days"** (v1) — Hebrew מִיָּמִים רַבִּים. **"had given rest … from all their enemies round about" → "… all around"** — Hebrew הֵנִיחַ … מִסָּבִיב, the words of 21:44; Deut 12:10; 25:19. **"waxed old and stricken in age" → "was old, advanced in days"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew זָקֵן בָּא בַּיָּמִים, literally "old, gone into the days" (13:1; Gen 24:1); in v2 the verb זָקַנְתִּי, "I have grown old".
- **"called for all Israel, and for their elders, and for their heads" → "called all Israel, its elders and its heads"** (v2) — the Hebrew has no "and" before "elders": the four groups stand for all Israel; the suffixes are singular ("his", of Israel). **"officers"** — Hebrew שֹׁטְרִים, as Deut 16:18.
- **"because of you" → "from before you"** (v3) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵיכֶם, the word of v5, 9, 13 ("from before you"); "because of you" is the other sense of the same word. **"he it is that fighteth for you" → "he is the one fighting for you"** (v3, 10) — Hebrew הוּא הַנִּלְחָם לָכֶם, a participle (Deut 3:22).
- **"Behold, I have divided unto you by lot these nations that remain, to be an inheritance for your tribes, from Jordan, with all the nations that I have cut off, even unto the great sea westward" → "See, I have made fall to you these nations that are left, as an inheritance for your tribes, from the Jordan, and all the nations that I have cut off, and the Great Sea, the going in of the sun"** (v4) — Hebrew רְאוּ "see"; הִפַּלְתִּי, "I have made fall" (the lot is not named; 13:6); הַנִּשְׁאָרִים "that are left". The second half of the verse is rough in Hebrew and is given word for word: "from the Jordan, and all the nations that I have cut off, and the Great Sea"; "even unto" is not written. מְבוֹא הַשָּׁמֶשׁ, "the going in of the sun", is the west (Deut 11:30). "Tribes" is here שֵׁבֶט.
- **"he shall expel them from before you, and drive them from out of your sight; and ye shall possess their land, as the LORD your God hath promised" → "he will push them from before you and dispossess them from before you, and you shall take possession of their land, as Yahweh your Elohim spoke"** (v5) — Hebrew הָדַף "push" (Deut 6:19; 9:4); הוֹרִישׁ "dispossess" and יָרַשׁ "take possession" are one root; מִפְּנֵיכֶם and מִלִּפְנֵיכֶם are two forms, both "from before you"; דִּבֶּר "spoke" (v5, 10, 14, 15).
- **"Be ye therefore very courageous" → "And you shall be very strong"** (v6) — Hebrew וַחֲזַקְתֶּם מְאֹד, as 1:7. **"that ye turn not aside therefrom to the right hand or to the left" → "so as not to turn aside from it, right or left"** — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי סוּר־מִמֶּנּוּ יָמִין וּשְׂמֹאול (Deut 17:20; 28:14).
- **"That ye come not among these nations" → "so as not to go in among these nations"** (v7) — Hebrew לְבִלְתִּי־בוֹא בַּגּוֹיִם; the same verb and preposition in v12, "go in among them, and they among you". **"neither make mention of the name of their gods, nor cause to swear by them" → "by the name of their gods you shall not make mention, and you shall not make swear"** — Hebrew וּבְשֵׁם אֱלֹהֵיהֶם לֹא־תַזְכִּירוּ וְלֹא תַשְׁבִּיעוּ: "by the name" stands first and belongs to both verbs; הִזְכִּיר is "cause to be remembered, mention" (Exod 23:13), הִשְׁבִּיעַ "make swear", and no object is named: none is supplied. **"these that remain among you" → "these that are left with you"** (v7, 12) — Hebrew הַנִּשְׁאָרִים … אִתְּכֶם.
- **"cleave" → "cling"** (v8, 12) — Hebrew דָּבַק, the word of Deut 10:20; 11:22; 13:4 and of Gen 2:24. In v8 "to Yahweh your Elohim" stands first.
- **"hath driven out" → "has dispossessed"** (v9, 13) — Hebrew הוֹרִישׁ, as v5. **"no man hath been able to stand before you" → "not a man has stood before you"** — Hebrew לֹא־עָמַד אִישׁ בִּפְנֵיכֶם, as 21:44; "been able" is not written. "And you" stands alone at the head of the clause.
- **"One man of you shall chase a thousand" → "One man of you pursues a thousand"** (v10) — Hebrew יִרְדָּף, רָדַף "pursue" (Lev 26:8; Deut 32:30); the form can also be read "shall pursue".
- **"Take good heed therefore unto yourselves" → "And you shall take great heed for your souls"** (v11) — Hebrew וְנִשְׁמַרְתֶּם מְאֹד לְנַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶם, the words of Deut 4:15; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"Else if ye do in any wise go back" → "For if you surely turn back"** (v12) — Hebrew שׁוֹב תָּשׁוּבוּ, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "turning back you turn back"; so v13 יָדוֹעַ תֵּדְעוּ, "knowing you shall know": "you shall surely know". **"the remnant of these nations" → "the rest of these nations"** — Hebrew יֶתֶר. **"shall make marriages with them" → "intermarry with them"** — Hebrew הִתְחַתֵּן, as Deut 7:3.
- **"will no more drive out any of these nations" → "will no more dispossess these nations"** (v13) — Hebrew לֹא יוֹסִיף … לְהוֹרִישׁ, literally "will not add to dispossess". **"snares and traps unto you, and scourges in your sides, and thorns in your eyes" → "a trap and a snare, and a whip in your sides and thorns in your eyes"** — Hebrew פַּח, the fowler's trap; מוֹקֵשׁ "snare", the word of Exod 23:33; 34:12; Deut 7:16; שֹׁטֵט, a form found only here, taken as שׁוֹט "whip"; צְנִנִים "thorns", only here and Num 33:55 ("thorns in your sides"); the last two are uncertain. The first three nouns are singular. **"from off this good land" → "from off this good ground"** (v13, 15) — Hebrew אֲדָמָה; v16 has אֶרֶץ "land".
- **"this day I am going the way of all the earth" → "I am going today in the way of all the earth"** (v14) — Hebrew הוֹלֵךְ … בְּדֶרֶךְ כָּל־הָאָרֶץ (1 Kgs 2:2): the way that all the earth goes, death. **"ye know in all your hearts and in all your souls" → "you shall know with all your heart and with all your soul"** — Hebrew וִידַעְתֶּם, which may also be rendered "and you know"; "heart" and "soul" are singular. **"not one thing hath failed of all the good things" → "not one word has fallen of all the good words"** — Hebrew לֹא־נָפַל דָּבָר אֶחָד מִכֹּל הַדְּבָרִים הַטּוֹבִים, as 21:45: דָּבָר "word", נָפַל "fall" = fail. **"all are come to pass unto you, and not one thing hath failed thereof" → "all have come to you; not one word of it has fallen"** — Hebrew הַכֹּל בָּאוּ לָכֶם לֹא־נָפַל מִמֶּנּוּ דָּבָר אֶחָד; "of it" is singular.
- **"as all good things are come upon you … so shall the LORD bring upon you all evil things" → "as all the good word has come upon you … so Yahweh will bring upon you all the evil word"** (v15) — Hebrew כָּל־הַדָּבָר הַטּוֹב … כָּל־הַדָּבָר הָרָע, singular, "word" as in v14: what he spoke for good and what he spoke for evil (Deut 28). **"which the LORD your God promised you" → "spoke to you"**. **"destroyed"** is הִשְׁמִיד.
- **"When ye have transgressed the covenant" → "when you overstep the covenant"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּעָבְרְכֶם, literally "in your passing over"; the verse continues the sentence of v15. **"then shall the anger of the LORD be kindled against you" → "and the anger of Yahweh will burn against you"** — Hebrew וְחָרָה אַף־יְהוָה; אַף is literally "nose". The second half of the verse has the words of Deut 11:16–17.

### Notes
- **Joshua's words to Israel** — the chapter is built of the words of Deuteronomy (Deut 4:15; 7:1–5, 16; 10:20; 11:16–17, 22–25; 28).
- **"The way of all the earth"** (v14) — so David in 1 Kgs 2:2.
- **Not one word has fallen** (v14) — as 21:45; then v15–16 set the evil word beside the good.
- **Intermarriage** (v12–13) — Deut 7:3–4; Judg 3:5–6.
- **The nations that are left** — 13:1–6; Judg 2:20–23.
