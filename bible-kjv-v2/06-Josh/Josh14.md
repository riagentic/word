# Joshua 14

[1] And these are what the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ inherited in the land of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧, which ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ the priest and ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ made them inherit,

[2] by the lot of their inheritance, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, for the nine tribes and the half of the tribe.

[3] For ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ had given the inheritance of the two tribes and the half of the tribe across the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧; and to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ he gave no inheritance in their midst.

[4] For the sons of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ were two tribes, ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ and ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧; and they gave no share to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ in the land, except cities to dwell in, and their pasture lands for their livestock and for their property.

[5] As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did, and they divided the land.

[6] And the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ drew near to ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ in Gilgal, and ⟦caleb|Kalev|Caleb⟧ the son of ⟦jephunneh|Yefunneh|Jephunneh⟧ the ⟦kenizzites|Qenizzi|Kenizzite⟧ said to him, "You know the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ concerning me and concerning you in ⟦kadesh-barnea|Qadesh Barnea|Kadesh-barnea⟧.

[7] Forty years old was I when ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sent me from ⟦kadesh-barnea|Qadesh Barnea|Kadesh-barnea⟧ to spy out the land, and I brought him back word as it was with my heart.

[8] And my brothers who went up with me made the heart of the people melt, but I followed fully after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ swore on that day, saying, 'Surely the land on which your foot has trodden, to you it shall be as an inheritance, and to your sons, forever, for you have followed fully after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'

[10] And now, behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has kept me alive, as he spoke, these forty-five years, from the time that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke this word to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, while ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ walked in the wilderness; and now, behold, I am today eighty-five years old.

[11] I am still strong today, as on the day that ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ sent me; as my power was then, so is my power now, for war and for going out and for coming in.

[12] And now, give me this hill country of which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke on that day; for you yourself heard on that day that ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧ were there, and cities great and fortified. Perhaps ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will be with me, and I shall dispossess them, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke."

[13] And ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ blessed him, and he gave ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧ to ⟦caleb|Kalev|Caleb⟧ the son of ⟦jephunneh|Yefunneh|Jephunneh⟧ as an inheritance.

[14] Therefore ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧ became the inheritance of ⟦caleb|Kalev|Caleb⟧ the son of ⟦jephunneh|Yefunneh|Jephunneh⟧ the ⟦kenizzites|Qenizzi|Kenizzite⟧, to this day, because he followed fully after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[15] And the name of ⟦hebron|Chevron|Hebron⟧ formerly was ⟦kiriath-arba|Qiryat Arba|Kiriath-arba⟧; he was the great man among the ⟦anakim|Anaqim|Anakim⟧. And the land was quiet from war.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v6 with the article, אִישׁ־הָאֱלֹהִים "the man of the Elohim" (Deut 33:1).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of" → "sons of"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"And these are the countries which the children of Israel inherited … which Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed for inheritance to them" → "And these are what the sons of Israel inherited … which Eleazar the priest and Joshua the son of Nun and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the sons of Israel made them inherit"** (v1) — Hebrew נָחֲלוּ "inherited" and נִחֲלוּ אוֹתָם "made them inherit", two stems of one verb (Num 34:17, 29); "countries" and "distributed" are not in the Hebrew. רָאשֵׁי אֲבוֹת הַמַּטּוֹת, the words of Num 32:28. "Tribe" is מַטֶּה (literally "staff") in every place in this chapter (v1, 2, 3, 4); the other word, שֵׁבֶט, does not stand in it.
- **"By lot was their inheritance" → "by the lot of their inheritance"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּגוֹרַל נַחֲלָתָם: as pointed, גּוֹרַל is joined to the next word, "by the lot of their inheritance", and the words go on from v1. With another pointing (בְּגוֹרָל) they read "by lot was their inheritance"; the Masoretic Text is kept. בְּיַד־מֹשֶׁה "by the hand of Moses" (Num 34:13; 36:13).
- **"the inheritance of two tribes and an half tribe on the other side Jordan: but unto the Levites he gave none inheritance among them" → "the inheritance of the two tribes and the half of the tribe across the Jordan; and to the Levites he gave no inheritance in their midst"** (v3) — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר לַיַּרְדֵּן (Num 34:15); בְּתוֹכָם.
- **"therefore they gave no part unto the Levites in the land, save cities to dwell in, with their suburbs for their cattle and for their substance" → "and they gave no share to the Levites in the land, except cities to dwell in, and their pasture lands for their livestock and for their property"** (v4) — Hebrew חֵלֶק "share"; כִּי אִם "except"; מִגְרָשׁ, the open land round a city (Num 35:2, 3); מִקְנֶה "livestock"; קִנְיָן "property", what one has got. "Therefore" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"and they divided the land"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיַּחְלְקוּ, from חָלַק "divide" (13:7).
- **"Then the children of Judah came unto Joshua in Gilgal … Thou knowest the thing that the LORD said unto Moses the man of God concerning me and thee" → "And the sons of Judah drew near to Joshua in Gilgal … You know the word that Yahweh spoke to Moses the man of Elohim concerning me and concerning you"** (v6) — Hebrew נָגַשׁ "draw near"; בַּגִּלְגָּל, "in the Gilgal", with the article; הַדָּבָר "the word"; דִּבֶּר "spoke"; עַל אֹדוֹתַי וְעַל אֹדוֹתֶיךָ, twice written out: עַל אֹדוֹת is elsewhere "on account of" (Gen 21:11; Num 12:1), here of the matter spoken about, "concerning". The word of Yahweh: Num 14:24, 30; Deut 1:36.
- **"Forty years old was I … to espy out the land; and I brought him word again as it was in mine heart" → "Forty years old was I … to spy out the land, and I brought him back word as it was with my heart"** (v7) — Hebrew בֶּן־אַרְבָּעִים שָׁנָה, literally "a son of forty years" (so v10); רִגֵּל "spy out" (Deut 1:24; Num 13 has תּוּר "explore"); הֵשִׁיב דָּבָר "bring back word" (Num 13:26; Deut 1:22, 25); עִם־לְבָבִי "with my heart".
- **"my brethren that went up with me made the heart of the people melt: but I wholly followed the LORD my God" → "my brothers who went up with me made the heart of the people melt, but I followed fully after Yahweh my Elohim"** (v8, 9, 14) — Hebrew מִלֵּאתִי אַחֲרֵי, literally "I filled after": the expression of Num 14:24; 32:11, 12; Deut 1:36, in the same English. הִמְסִיו "made melt" (Deut 1:28).
- **"Surely the land whereon thy feet have trodden shall be thine inheritance, and thy children's for ever" → "Surely the land on which your foot has trodden, to you it shall be as an inheritance, and to your sons, forever"** (v9) — Hebrew אִם־לֹא, the oath formula, literally "if not the land … shall be yours": a sworn "surely". רַגְלְךָ "your foot", singular; דָּרַךְ "tread" (Deut 1:36; 11:24); the order of the Hebrew is kept. Such an oath of Moses is not told in Numbers; Deut 1:36 gives the words as Yahweh's.
- **"the LORD hath kept me alive, as he said, these forty and five years, even since the LORD spake this word unto Moses, while the children of Israel wandered in the wilderness: and now, lo, I am this day fourscore and five years old" → "Yahweh has kept me alive, as he spoke, these forty-five years, from the time that Yahweh spoke this word to Moses, while Israel walked in the wilderness; and now, behold, I am today eighty-five years old"** (v10) — Hebrew הֶחֱיָה "keep alive"; הָלַךְ "walk" (Deut 8:2); "children of" is not in the Hebrew here; הִנֵּה "behold", twice.
- **"As yet I am as strong this day as I was in the day that Moses sent me: as my strength was then, even so is my strength now, for war, both to go out, and to come in" → "I am still strong today, as on the day that Moses sent me; as my power was then, so is my power now, for war and for going out and for coming in"** (v11) — Hebrew כֹּחַ "power"; כְּכֹחִי אָז וּכְכֹחִי עָתָּה, literally "as my power then and as my power now"; לָצֵאת וְלָבוֹא "to go out and to come in" (Num 27:17; Deut 31:2).
- **"give me this mountain, whereof the LORD spake in that day; for thou heardest in that day how the Anakims were there, and that the cities were great and fenced: if so be the LORD will be with me, then I shall be able to drive them out, as the LORD said" → "give me this hill country of which Yahweh spoke on that day; for you yourself heard on that day that Anakim were there, and cities great and fortified. Perhaps Yahweh will be with me, and I shall dispossess them, as Yahweh spoke"** (v12) — Hebrew הָהָר הַזֶּה, "this mountain", of a region "hill country" (11:21); אַתָּה "you", written out; עֲנָקִים without the article; עָרִים גְּדֹלוֹת בְּצֻרוֹת, the words of Deut 1:28; אוּלַי "perhaps"; אוֹתִי stands here for אִתִּי "with me"; "be able" is not in the Hebrew; הוֹרִישׁ "dispossess".
- **"Hebron therefore became the inheritance of Caleb … unto this day, because that he wholly followed the LORD God of Israel"** (v14) — Hebrew הָיְתָה … לְכָלֵב … לְנַחֲלָה, literally "became Caleb's, for an inheritance"; יַעַן אֲשֶׁר "because".
- **"And the name of Hebron before was Kirjath-arba; which Arba was a great man among the Anakims. And the land had rest from war" → "And the name of Hebron formerly was Kiriath-arba; he was the great man among the Anakim. And the land was quiet from war"** (v15) — Hebrew הָאָדָם הַגָּדוֹל בָּעֲנָקִים הוּא, "the great man among the Anakim was he": the Hebrew does not repeat the name; "he" is the Arba of the city's name ("the city of Arba"; Arba is "the father of the Anak" in 15:13). אַרְבַּע is also the word "four". שָׁקְטָה "was quiet" (11:23): "rest" is another verb (נוּחַ).
- **Numbers** (v7, 10) — in the ordinary English order; the Hebrew has "forty and five years", "five and eighty years".

### Notes
- **Caleb's claim** (v6–12) — forty-five years after he was sent from Kadesh-barnea (Num 13–14), Caleb at eighty-five asks for the hill country of which Yahweh spoke.
- **Kenizzite** (v6, 14) — Num 32:12; Kenaz is a name among the sons of Esau (Gen 36:11, 15), and the Kenizzite is among the peoples of Gen 15:19.
- **Hebron given** (v13–15) — Hebron and the Anakim: 11:21; 15:13, 14.
- **"The land was quiet from war"** (v15) — repeated from 11:23.
