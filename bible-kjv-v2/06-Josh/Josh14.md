## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"suburbs" → "pasturelands"** (v4) — *migrash*, as in Num 35.
- **"espy out" → "scout out"** (v7).
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v12).
- **"fourscore and five" → "eighty-five"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"as yet I am as strong" → "I am still as strong"** (v11) — modernized.

### Notes
- **Caleb's claim** — 45 years after the scouting mission (40 wandering + 5 into conquest), Caleb at 85 claims his reward. Faithful from the scouting mission to the distribution.
- **Kenizzite** — a clan name associated with Edomite / pre-Israelite heritage (Gen 15:19). Caleb's family may have been absorbed into Judah through the exodus.
- **Hebron given** — Caleb's inheritance is the southern hill-country stronghold, previously home of Anakim giants. Hebron had been captured in ch 10; Caleb takes the territory as personal inheritance.
- **Caleb the younger contender** — despite being 85, Caleb volunteers to take the hardest territory. Symbol of tenacious faithfulness.
- **"Land had rest from war"** (v15) — closing-formula repeated from 11:23. Settlement phase.
