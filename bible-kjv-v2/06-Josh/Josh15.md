## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"valley of the giants" → "Valley of Rephaim"** (v8) — specific geographic name.
- **"fetched a compass / compassed" → "turned about / bends around"** (v3, 10) — archaic.
- **"south coast" → "southern boundary"** (v2, 4) — modernized.
- **"nether" → "lower"** (v19) — modernized.

### Notes
- **Judah's territory** — the largest tribal inheritance. Reaches from the Dead Sea (east) to the Mediterranean (west), and from the Negev (south) to the Jerusalem area (north).
- **Achsah narrative** (v16–19) — anecdote about Caleb's daughter requesting springs. Shows women asserting rights to water-land. Parallels Num 27 (Zelophehad's daughters).
- **Othniel** — becomes the first judge (Judg 3:9–11), delivering Israel from Cushan-rishathaim.
- **"Could not drive out"** (v63) — the failure to conquer Jerusalem. The Jebusites held out until David (2 Sam 5:6–9).
- **Jerusalem's frontier status** — sits on Judah/Benjamin border. Technically assigned to Benjamin (18:28), but politically and defensively tied to Judah.
- **City-list format** — classic tribal-geography document; matches ancient-Near-Eastern administrative district lists. Four groups: Negev (29 cities), Shephelah (divided into sections), hill country (divided), wilderness (6 cities).
