# Joshua 2

[1] Joshua son of Nun sent out two men from Shittim as scouts in secret, saying, "Go, view the land, especially Jericho." So they went and came to the house of a prostitute named Rahab, and lodged there.

[2] It was told to the king of Jericho, "Behold, men from the children of Israel have come here tonight to scout out the land."

[3] Then the king of Jericho sent to Rahab, saying, "Bring out the men who have come to you, who have entered your house, for they have come to scout out all the land."

[4] But the woman had taken the two men and hidden them. And she said, "Yes, the men came to me, but I did not know where they were from.

[5] When the gate was about to be shut, at dark, the men went out. I do not know where the men went. Pursue them quickly, for you may overtake them."

[6] But she had brought them up to the roof and hidden them among the stalks of flax that she had laid in order on the roof.

[7] So the men pursued after them on the way to the Jordan, to the fords; and as soon as the pursuers had gone out, they shut the gate.

[8] Before the men lay down, she came up to them on the roof

[9] and said to the men, "I know that Yahweh has given you the land; the dread of you has fallen upon us, and all the inhabitants of the land melt in fear before you.

[10] For we have heard how Yahweh dried up the water of the Sea of Reeds before you when you came out of Egypt, and what you did to the two kings of the Amorites who were beyond the Jordan — Sihon and Og — whom you devoted to destruction.

[11] When we heard it, our hearts melted, and no spirit remained in any man because of you; for Yahweh your *Elohim* — he is *Elohim* in the heavens above and on the earth beneath.

[12] Now then, please swear to me by Yahweh that, since I have shown you kindness, you also will show kindness to my father's house, and give me a reliable sign

[13] that you will spare the lives of my father and my mother, my brothers and my sisters, and all who belong to them, and that you will deliver our lives from death."

[14] The men said to her, "Our life for yours — if you do not reveal this business of ours! And when Yahweh gives us the land, we will deal kindly and faithfully with you."

[15] Then she let them down by a rope through the window, for her house was built into the city wall, and she lived in the wall.

[16] She said to them, "Go to the hill country, lest the pursuers meet you; hide yourselves there three days until the pursuers have returned. Then afterward you may go on your way."

[17] The men said to her, "We shall be guiltless of this oath of yours which you have made us swear —

[18] unless, when we come into the land, you tie this cord of scarlet thread in the window from which you let us down, and gather into your house your father, your mother, your brothers, and all your father's household.

[19] Anyone who goes out of the doors of your house into the street, his blood shall be on his own head, and we shall be guiltless. But if a hand is laid on any who are with you in the house, his blood shall be on our head.

[20] But if you reveal this business of ours, we shall be free of the oath that you have made us swear."

[21] She said, "According to your words, so be it." Then she sent them away, and they departed. And she tied the scarlet cord in the window.

[22] They went and came to the hill country and remained there three days, until the pursuers had returned. The pursuers sought them all along the way but did not find them.

[23] The two men then returned, came down from the hill country, crossed over, and came to Joshua son of Nun; they told him all that had befallen them.

[24] They said to Joshua, "Truly Yahweh has given all the land into our hands; and indeed all the inhabitants of the land melt in fear before us."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"harlot" → "prostitute"** (v1) — Hebrew זֹנָה *zonah*. Modernized.
- **"spy secretly" → "scouts in secret"** (v1) — Hebrew מְרַגְּלִים חֶרֶשׁ *meragglim cheresh*.
- **"wist / wot not" → "did not know / do not know"** (v4, 5) — archaic.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v10).
- **"utterly destroyed" → "devoted to destruction"** (v10) — *cherem*.
- **"our life for yours, if ye utter not" → "Our life for yours — if you do not reveal"** (v14) — self-imprecatory oath formula.
- **"cord / line" → "rope / cord"** (v15, 18, 21) — rope to descend; cord/thread as sign.

### Notes
- **Rahab, the unlikely heroine** — a Canaanite prostitute becomes the saver of Israelite spies. Multiple NT resonances:
  - Heb 11:31 — listed among heroes of faith
  - James 2:25 — justified by works
  - Mt 1:5 — Rahab in Jesus' genealogy (mother of Boaz, ancestor of David)
- **"Melted hearts"** — threefold repetition (v9, 11, 24). The Canaanites' dread fulfills Yahweh's prediction (Ex 15:15–16, Deut 2:25).
- **Rahab's confession** (v11) — "Yahweh your *Elohim* — he is *Elohim* in the heavens above and on the earth beneath." The same formula used by Moses (Deut 4:39). Remarkable monotheistic profession from a Canaanite.
- **Scarlet cord** (v18, 21) — sign of preservation; early Christian interpretation (1 Clement 12) compared to the Passover blood and to Christ's blood.
- **Rahab's deception** (v4–6) — she lies to protect the spies. Ancient and modern ethicists debate; her deception is not explicitly approved, but her faith-act is praised.
- **Parallel to Passover protection** — Rahab's household marked and kept safe amid general destruction, like Israel's houses at Egypt's tenth plague.
