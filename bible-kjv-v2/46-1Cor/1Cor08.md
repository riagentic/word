# 1 Corinthians 8

[1] Now concerning food offered to idols: we know that "all of us possess knowledge." This "knowledge" puffs up, but love builds up.

[2] If anyone imagines that he knows something, he does not yet know as he ought to know.

[3] But if anyone loves *Elohim*, he is known by *Elohim*.

[4] Therefore, as to the eating of food offered to idols, we know that "an idol has no real existence," and that "**there is no *Elohim* but one**."

[5] For although there may be so-called gods in heaven or on earth — as indeed there are many "gods" and many "lords" —

[6] yet for us there is **one *Elohim*, the Father, from whom are all things and for whom we exist, and one Lord, *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, through whom are all things and through whom we exist**.

[7] However, not all possess this knowledge. But some, through former association with idols, eat food as really offered to an idol, and their conscience, being weak, is defiled.

[8] Food will not commend us to *Elohim*. We are no worse off if we do not eat, and no better off if we do.

[9] But take care that this right of yours does not somehow become a stumbling block to the weak.

[10] For if anyone sees you who have knowledge eating in an idol's temple, will he not be encouraged, if his conscience is weak, to eat food offered to idols?

[11] And so by your knowledge this weak person is destroyed, the brother for whom the *Mashiach* died.

[12] Thus, sinning against your brothers and wounding their conscience when it is weak, you sin against the *Mashiach*.

[13] Therefore, if food makes my brother stumble, I will never eat meat, lest I make my brother stumble.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v6) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (vv6, 11, 12) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **v4 *Shema* echo** — "there is no *Elohim* but one" echoes Deut 6:4's שְׁמַע יִשְׂרָאֵל *Shema Yisrael*; the *Shema* notation is preserved in the Notes rather than altered in the text.
