# 1 Corinthians 3

[1] But I, brothers, could not address you as spiritual people, but as people of the flesh, as infants in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[2] I fed you with milk, not solid food, for you were not ready for it. And even now you are not yet ready,

[3] for you are still of the flesh. For while there is jealousy and strife among you, are you not of the flesh and behaving only in a human way?

[4] For when one says, "I follow ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧," and another, "I follow Apollos," are you not being merely human?

[5] What then is Apollos? What is ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧? Servants through whom you believed, as the Lord assigned to each.

[6] I planted, Apollos watered, but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave the growth.

[7] So neither he who plants nor he who waters is anything, but only ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who gives the growth.

[8] He who plants and he who waters are one, and each will receive his wages according to his labor.

[9] For we are ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ fellow workers. You are ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ field, ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ building.

[10] According to the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ given to me, like a skilled master builder I laid a foundation, and someone else is building upon it. Let each one take care how he builds upon it.

[11] For no one can lay a foundation other than that which is laid, which is ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[12] Now if anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw —

[13] each one's work will become manifest, for the Day will disclose it, because it will be revealed by fire, and the fire will test what sort of work each one has done.

[14] If the work that anyone has built on the foundation survives, he will receive a reward.

[15] If anyone's work is burned up, he will suffer loss, though he himself will be saved, but only as through fire.

[16] Do you not know that **you are ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ temple and that ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ Spirit dwells in you**?

[17] If anyone destroys ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ temple, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will destroy him. For ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ temple is holy, and you are that temple.

[18] Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks that he is wise in this age, let him become a fool that he may become wise.

[19] For the wisdom of this world is folly with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. For it is written, "**He catches the wise in their craftiness**,"

[20] and again, "**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile**."

[21] So let no one boast in men. For all things are yours,

[22] whether ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧ or Apollos or ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧ or the world or life or death or the present or the future — all are yours,

[23] and you are ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach's|Christ's⟧, and ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*; divine name preserved.
- **"Cephas" → "*Kefa*"** (v22) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kefa* ("rock"); preserved in Aramaic form.

### Notes
- **v19** quotes Job 5:13.
- **v20** quotes Ps 94:11.
