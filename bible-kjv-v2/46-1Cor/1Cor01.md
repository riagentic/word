## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Greek Ἰησοῦς *Iēsous* is a transliteration of Hebrew/Aramaic יֵשׁוּעַ *Yeshua* ("Yahweh saves"); he was called *Yeshua* by those who knew him.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek Χριστός *Christos* = Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ *Mashiach*, "Anointed One." Preserving the Hebrew term keeps the royal-priestly-prophetic anointing concept explicit.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek θεός *theos* renders Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*. The underlying divine name is preserved rather than flattened to the generic English "God."
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (vv13–17) — Greek βαπτίζω *baptizō* means literally "to immerse/dip"; "baptize" is a transliteration, not a translation. "Immerse" renders the Greek verb accurately.
- **"Cephas" → "*Kefa*"** (v12) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kefa* ("rock/stone"), the name Yeshua gave Simon; Greek *Kēphas* is itself a transliteration of Aramaic. Preserved in its Aramaic form.

### Notes
- **v19** quotes Isa 29:14.
- **v31** echoes Jer 9:24.
