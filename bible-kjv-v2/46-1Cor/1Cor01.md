# 1 Corinthians 1

[1] Paul, called by the will of *Elohim* to be an apostle of the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*, and our brother Sosthenes,

[2] To the church of *Elohim* that is in Corinth, to those sanctified in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*, called to be saints together with all those who in every place call upon the name of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, both their Lord and ours:

[3] Grace to you and peace from *Elohim* our Father and the Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.

[4] I give thanks to my *Elohim* always for you because of the grace of *Elohim* that was given you in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*,

[5] that in every way you were enriched in him in all speech and all knowledge —

[6] even as the testimony about the *Mashiach* was confirmed among you —

[7] so that you are not lacking in any gift, as you wait for the revealing of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*,

[8] who will sustain you to the end, guiltless in the day of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.

[9] *Elohim* is faithful, by whom you were called into the fellowship of his Son, *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* our Lord.

[10] I appeal to you, brothers, by the name of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, that all of you agree, and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be united in the same mind and the same judgment.

[11] For it has been reported to me by Chloe's people that there is quarreling among you, my brothers.

[12] What I mean is that each one of you says, "I follow Paul," or "I follow Apollos," or "I follow *Kefa*," or "I follow the *Mashiach*."

[13] Is the *Mashiach* divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you immersed in the name of Paul?

[14] I thank *Elohim* that I immersed none of you except Crispus and Gaius,

[15] so that no one may say that you were immersed in my name.

[16] (I did immerse also the household of Stephanas. Beyond that, I do not know whether I immersed anyone else.)

[17] For the *Mashiach* did not send me to immerse but to preach the gospel, and not with words of eloquent wisdom, lest the cross of the *Mashiach* be emptied of its power.

[18] For the word of the cross is folly to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of *Elohim*.

[19] For it is written,

"**I will destroy the wisdom of the wise,
and the discernment of the discerning I will thwart**."

[20] Where is the one who is wise? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has not *Elohim* made foolish the wisdom of the world?

[21] For since, in the wisdom of *Elohim*, the world did not know *Elohim* through wisdom, it pleased *Elohim* through the folly of what we preach to save those who believe.

[22] For Jews demand signs and Greeks seek wisdom,

[23] but we preach the *Mashiach* crucified, a stumbling block to Jews and folly to Gentiles,

[24] but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, the *Mashiach* the power of *Elohim* and the wisdom of *Elohim*.

[25] For the foolishness of *Elohim* is wiser than men, and the weakness of *Elohim* is stronger than men.

[26] For consider your calling, brothers: not many of you were wise according to worldly standards, not many were powerful, not many were of noble birth.

[27] But *Elohim* chose what is foolish in the world to shame the wise; *Elohim* chose what is weak in the world to shame the strong;

[28] *Elohim* chose what is low and despised in the world, even things that are not, to bring to nothing things that are,

[29] so that no human being might boast in the presence of *Elohim*.

[30] And because of him you are in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*, who became to us wisdom from *Elohim*, righteousness and sanctification and redemption,

[31] so that, as it is written, "**Let the one who boasts, boast in the Lord**."

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (throughout) — Greek Ἰησοῦς *Iēsous* is a transliteration of Hebrew/Aramaic יֵשׁוּעַ *Yeshua* ("Yahweh saves"); he was called *Yeshua* by those who knew him.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek Χριστός *Christos* = Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ *Mashiach*, "Anointed One." Preserving the Hebrew term keeps the royal-priestly-prophetic anointing concept explicit.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek θεός *theos* renders Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*. The underlying divine name is preserved rather than flattened to the generic English "God."
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (vv13–17) — Greek βαπτίζω *baptizō* means literally "to immerse/dip"; "baptize" is a transliteration, not a translation. "Immerse" renders the Greek verb accurately.
- **"Cephas" → "*Kefa*"** (v12) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kefa* ("rock/stone"), the name Yeshua gave Simon; Greek *Kēphas* is itself a transliteration of Aramaic. Preserved in its Aramaic form.

### Notes
- **v19** quotes Isa 29:14.
- **v31** echoes Jer 9:24.
