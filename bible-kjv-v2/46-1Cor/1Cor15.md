# 1 Corinthians 15

[1] Now I make known to you, brothers, the gospel which I preached to you, which also you received, in which also you stand,

[2] through which also you are saved, with what word I preached the gospel to you, if you hold fast — unless you believed in vain.

[3] For I delivered to you among the first things what I also received: **that ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ died for our sins according to the Scriptures,**

[4] **and that he was buried, and that he has been raised on the third day according to the Scriptures,**

[5] **and that he appeared to ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧, then to the twelve**;

[6] then he appeared to over five hundred brothers at one time, of whom the greater part remain until now, but some fell asleep;

[7] then he appeared to ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧, then to all the apostles;

[8] and last of all, as to the one untimely born, he appeared also to me.

[9] For I am the least of the apostles, who am not fit to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[10] but by the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ I am what I am, and his grace which was unto me did not become empty, but I labored more abundantly than they all — yet not I, but the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ which is with me.

[11] Whether then I or they, so we proclaim and so you believed.

[12] But if ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is proclaimed, that he has been raised from the dead, how do some among you say that there is no resurrection of the dead?

[13] But if there is no resurrection of the dead, neither has ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ been raised;

[14] and if ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ has not been raised, then empty is our proclamation, empty also your faith,

[15] and we are also found false witnesses of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because we testified against ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that he raised ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, whom he did not raise, if indeed the dead are not raised.

[16] For if the dead are not raised, neither has ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ been raised;

[17] and if ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ has not been raised, your faith is vain; you are still in your sins.

[18] Then also those who fell asleep in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ perished.

[19] If in this life we have hoped in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ only, we are more pitiable than all men.

[20] But now ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ has been raised from the dead, firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.

[21] For since death is through a man, resurrection of the dead is also through a man;

[22] for as in Adam all die, so also in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ all will be made alive.

[23] But each in his own rank: ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ the firstfruits, then those who are ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach's|Christ's⟧, at his coming;

[24] then the end, when he delivers the kingdom to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father, when he has abolished all principality and all authority and power.

[25] For he must reign until he has put all the enemies under his feet.

[26] As the last enemy, death is abolished,

[27] for "**he subjected all things under his feet**." But when he says that all things have been subjected, it is clear that the one who subjected all things to him is excepted.

[28] But when all things have been subjected to him, then the Son himself also will be subjected to the one who subjected all things to him, **that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may be all in all**.

[29] Otherwise, what will they do who are ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ for the dead? If the dead are actually not raised, why are they also ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ for them?

[30] Why are we also in danger every hour?

[31] I die daily — by the boasting in you, brothers, which I have in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our Lord.

[32] If according to man I fought with beasts in ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧, what is the profit to me? If the dead are not raised, "**Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die**."

[33] Do not be deceived: "**Bad associations corrupt good morals**."

[34] Sober up righteously and do not sin, for some have ignorance of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; I speak to your shame.

[35] But someone will say, "How are the dead raised? And with what kind of body do they come?"

[36] Senseless one! What you yourself sow is not made alive unless it dies;

[37] and what you sow — you do not sow the body that will come to be, but a naked grain, it may be of wheat or of one of the rest;

[38] but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gives it a body as he willed, and to each of the seeds a body of its own.

[39] Not all flesh is the same flesh, but there is one of men, and another flesh of beasts, and another flesh of birds, and another of fish.

[40] There are also heavenly bodies and earthly bodies; but the glory of the heavenly is one, and that of the earthly is another.

[41] There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars; for star differs from star in glory.

[42] So also is the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption;

[43] it is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory; it is sown in weakness, it is raised in power;

[44] it is sown a soulish body, it is raised a spiritual body. If there is a soulish body, there is also a spiritual one.

[45] So also it is written, "**The first man Adam became a living ⟦nephesh|nephesh|being⟧**"; the last Adam became a spirit that makes alive.

[46] But the spiritual is not first, but the soulish; then the spiritual.

[47] The first man is from the earth, of dust; the second man is from heaven.

[48] As is the one of dust, such also are those of dust; and as is the heavenly one, such also are the heavenly ones;

[49] and just as we bore the image of the one of dust, we shall also bear the image of the heavenly one.

[50] But this I say, brothers, that flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, nor does corruption inherit incorruption.

[51] Behold, I tell you a mystery: **we shall not all fall asleep, but we shall all be changed,**

[52] **in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet; for the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed.**

[53] For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal must put on immortality.

[54] But when this corruptible has put on incorruption and this mortal has put on immortality, then will come to pass the word that is written:

"**Death was swallowed up into victory.**"
[55] "**Where, death, is your victory?
Where, death, is your sting?**"

[56] Now the sting of death is sin, and the power of sin is the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧;

[57] but thanks be to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who gives us the victory through our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[58] So then, my beloved brothers, become firm, immovable, abounding in the work of the Lord always, knowing that your labor is not empty in the Lord.

## Changes

- **"James" → "*Yaaqov*"** (v7) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic. Χριστός has no article in v3, 12–14, 16–20, 23a and has it in v15, 22, 23b.
- **"Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v57) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Cephas" → "*Kefa*"** (v5) — Aramaic; systematic.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v29) — βαπτίζω; systematic.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v56) — Greek ὁ νόμος, with the article.
- **"I declare unto you" → "I make known to you"** (v1) — Greek γνωρίζω (as Gal 1:11); not "remind".
- **"By which also ye are saved, if ye keep in memory what I preached unto you" → "through which also you are saved, with what word I preached the gospel to you, if you hold fast"** (v2) — Greek διʼ οὗ καὶ σῴζεσθε, τίνι λόγῳ εὐηγγελισάμην ὑμῖν, εἰ κατέχετε, kept in the Greek order. σῴζεσθε is a present ("are saved, are being saved"). The middle clause, "with what word (or: in what wording) I preached the gospel to you", can depend on "if you hold fast" ("if you hold fast the word with which I preached to you") or on "I make known" (v1). κατέχετε has no object in the Greek.
- **"first of all" → "among the first things"** (v3) — Greek ἐν πρώτοις: first in order or first in weight; the verse does not decide.
- **"delivered" kept; "received" kept** (v3) — Greek παρέδωκα, παρέλαβον: the pair of words for passing on and receiving a tradition (11:23).
- **"rose again" → "has been raised"** (v4, 12–14, 16–17, 20) — Greek ἐγήγερται, a perfect passive, seven times in the chapter; beside it the aorists "died", "was buried", "appeared". ἐγείρω is "raise" throughout (v15 active, of *Elohim*); "resurrection" is ἀνάστασις.
- **"he was seen of" → "he appeared to"** (v5–8) — Greek ὤφθη with a dative, four times: "was seen by", "appeared to".
- **"at once" → "at one time"** (v6) — Greek ἐφάπαξ, elsewhere "once for all" (Heb 7:27); here of one occasion.
- **"remain unto this present" → "remain until now"; "are fallen asleep" → "fell asleep"** (v6) — Greek μένουσιν ("abide", 13:13), ἐκοιμήθησαν, an aorist (so also v18; v20 is a perfect, v51 a future).
- **"as of one born out of due time" → "as to the one untimely born"** (v8) — Greek ὡσπερεὶ τῷ ἐκτρώματι, with the article: ἔκτρωμα is a miscarriage, a child born dead or before its time.
- **"not meet" → "not fit"** (v9) — Greek ἱκανός, "sufficient" (2 Cor 3:5), "fit"; not "worthy" (ἄξιος).
- **"was not in vain; but I laboured more abundantly" → "did not become empty, but I labored more abundantly"** (v10) — Greek οὐ κενὴ ἐγενήθη; κενός is "empty" in v10, 14 (twice) and 58, a different word from "in vain" (εἰκῇ, v2) and "vain" (ματαία, v17). No "On the contrary" in the Greek.
- **"which was with me" → "which is with me"** (v10) — the printed text has ἡ σὺν ἐμοί (NA28 RP); without the article (WH Treg) the words read "the grace of *Elohim* with me".
- **"preach" / "preaching" → "proclaim" / "proclamation"** (v11, 12, 14) — Greek κηρύσσω, κήρυγμα; "preached the gospel" (v1–2) is εὐαγγελίζομαι.
- **"how say some among you" kept; "no resurrection of the dead"** (v12–13, 15–16, 21, 29, 32) — Greek ἀνάστασις νεκρῶν, νεκροὶ οὐκ ἐγείρονται: "dead" has no article in these verses ("dead ones"); with the article in v29a, 35, 42, 52.
- **"false witnesses of God; because we have testified of God" → "false witnesses of *Elohim*, because we testified against *Elohim*"** (v15) — Greek ψευδομάρτυρες τοῦ θεοῦ … κατὰ τοῦ θεοῦ: κατά with the genitive is "against" (so 4:6); some take it here as "concerning".
- **"they also which are fallen asleep in Christ are perished" → "those who fell asleep in the *Mashiach* perished"** (v18) — Greek κοιμηθέντες, ἀπώλοντο, two aorists.
- **"If in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are of all men most miserable" → "If in this life we have hoped in the *Mashiach* only, we are more pitiable than all men"** (v19) — Greek ἠλπικότες ἐσμὲν μόνον: "only" stands last and can go with "in this life" or with "have hoped" ("have only hoped"). ἐλεεινότεροι is a comparative, "more to be pitied".
- **"and become the firstfruits" → "firstfruits"** (v20) — "become" (ἐγένετο) is not in the critical text. ἀπαρχή is the first portion of the harvest, offered to God (Lev 23:10).
- **"by man came death, by man came also the resurrection" → "death is through a man, resurrection of the dead is also through a man"** (v21) — Greek διʼ ἀνθρώπου, "through"; the verse has no verb and no articles.
- **"order" → "rank"** (v23) — Greek τάγμα, a body of troops, a rank; "order" (τάξις) is the word of 14:40.
- **"they that are Christ's at his coming" → "those who are the *Mashiach's*, at his coming"** (v23) — Greek οἱ τοῦ Χριστοῦ ἐν τῇ παρουσίᾳ αὐτοῦ.
- **"Then cometh the end, when he shall have delivered up the kingdom to God, even the Father" → "then the end, when he delivers the kingdom to the *Elohim* and Father"** (v24) — Greek εἶτα τὸ τέλος, without a verb; παραδιδῷ, a present ("deliver", the verb of v3; "deliver up" is kept for a person); τῷ θεῷ καὶ πατρί, one article for both nouns (as Eph 5:20).
- **"put down all rule" → "abolished all principality"; "destroyed" → "abolished"** (v24, 26) — Greek καταργέω, as 13:8–11; ἀρχή, ἐξουσία, δύναμις as Eph 1:21.
- **"all enemies" → "all the enemies"** (v25) — Greek πάντας τοὺς ἐχθρούς; no "his". The subject of "has put" can be the *Mashiach* or *Elohim* (Ps 110:1).
- **"The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death" → "As the last enemy, death is abolished"** (v26) — Greek ἔσχατος ἐχθρὸς καταργεῖται ὁ θάνατος, a present.
- **"he hath put all things under his feet" → "he subjected all things under his feet"** (v27) — Greek ὑπέταξεν; the Greek names no subject (the earlier text supplied "*Elohim*"). ὑποτάσσω is "subject" six times in v27–28.
- **"when he saith" kept** (v27) — Greek ὅταν δὲ εἴπῃ, without a subject: "when it (the Scripture) says", or "when he shall have said".
- **"then shall the Son also himself be subject" → "then the Son himself also will be subjected"** (v28) — Greek ὑποταγήσεται, a future passive, which can also mean "will subject himself". The verse follows τότε καί (WH RP NA28; the printed text omits καί with Treg).
- **"all in all" kept** (v28) — the printed text has πάντα ἐν πᾶσιν (WH Treg); NA28 and RP have τὰ πάντα. ἐν πᾶσιν is "in all things" or "in all persons".
- **"Else what shall they do which are baptized for the dead" → "Otherwise, what will they do who are immersed for the dead"** (v29) — Greek οἱ βαπτιζόμενοι ὑπὲρ τῶν νεκρῶν: ὑπέρ with the genitive, "for, on behalf of", the preposition of v3 ("for our sins"). What practice is meant is not said; the verse adds no "people" and no "mean".
- **"if the dead rise not at all" → "If the dead are actually not raised"** (v29) — Greek εἰ ὅλως νεκροὶ οὐκ ἐγείρονται; ὅλως as 5:1; 6:7, with a negative "not at all". The printed punctuation is followed; the clause can also close the first question.
- **"I protest by your rejoicing which I have … I die daily" → "I die daily — by the boasting in you, brothers, which I have"** (v31) — Greek καθʼ ἡμέραν ἀποθνῄσκω, νὴ τὴν ὑμετέραν καύχησιν … ἣν ἔχω: νή is the particle of an oath, "by"; there is no verb "protest". τὴν ὑμετέραν καύχησιν is literally "your boasting": the boasting about you, as "which I have" shows. The verse follows the reading with ἀδελφοί, "brothers" (WH Treg NA28; RP and the printed text omit it).
- **"after the manner of men" → "according to man"; "what advantageth it me" → "what is the profit to me"** (v32) — Greek κατὰ ἄνθρωπον (as Gal 1:11), τί μοι τὸ ὄφελος. "If the dead are not raised" can also end the question before it.
- **"evil communications corrupt good manners" → "Bad associations corrupt good morals"** (v33) — Greek φθείρουσιν ἤθη χρηστὰ ὁμιλίαι κακαί: ὁμιλία is company and talk; φθείρω "corrupt", the root of "corruption" (v42, 50).
- **"Awake to righteousness, and sin not" → "Sober up righteously and do not sin"** (v34) — Greek ἐκνήψατε δικαίως: ἐκνήφω, "become sober" (νήφω, "be sober"); δικαίως, "righteously, as is right" (Titus 2:12).
- **"some have not the knowledge of God" → "some have ignorance of *Elohim*"** (v34) — Greek ἀγνωσίαν … ἔχουσιν.
- **"Thou fool" → "Senseless one"** (v36) — Greek ἄφρων, as 2 Cor 11:16; "fool" is μωρός (3:18). σύ, "you", is stressed: "what you yourself sow".
- **"is not quickened" → "is not made alive"** (v22, 36, 45) — Greek ζῳοποιέω.
- **"that body that shall be, but bare grain, it may chance of wheat, or of some other grain" → "the body that will come to be, but a naked grain, it may be of wheat or of one of the rest"** (v37) — Greek τὸ σῶμα τὸ γενησόμενον, γυμνὸν κόκκον, εἰ τύχοι (as 14:10) … τινος τῶν λοιπῶν.
- **"as it hath pleased him" → "as he willed"** (v38) — Greek καθὼς ἠθέλησεν, an aorist of θέλω.
- **"All flesh is not the same flesh" → "Not all flesh is the same flesh"** (v39) — Greek οὐ πᾶσα σὰρξ ἡ αὐτὴ σάρξ; "flesh" stands four times in the critical text. κτηνῶν is "beasts, cattle".
- **"celestial" / "terrestrial" → "heavenly" / "earthly"** (v40, 48–49) — Greek ἐπουράνιος, ἐπίγειος. In v40 "one … another" is ἑτέρα … ἑτέρα ("different"), in v39 and 41 ἄλλη.
- **"in corruption … in incorruption" kept** (v42, 50, 53–54) — Greek φθορά, ἀφθαρσία; "corruptible", "incorruptible" (v52–54) are φθαρτός, ἄφθαρτος. The Greek of v42–44 names no subject of "is sown … is raised".
- **"a natural body" → "a soulish body"** (v44, 46) — Greek σῶμα ψυχικόν, from ψυχή "soul": a body of the soul, as the "living soul" of v45; over against πνευματικόν, "spiritual", from πνεῦμα "spirit". "Natural" hides the link with v45 (as 2:14; Jas 3:15; Jude 19).
- **"There is a natural body, and there is a spiritual body" → "If there is a soulish body, there is also a spiritual one"** (v44) — the critical text has εἰ ἔστιν … ἔστιν καὶ πνευματικόν.
- **"was made a living soul; the last Adam was made a quickening spirit" → "became a living *nephesh*; the last Adam became a spirit that makes alive"** (v45) — Greek ἐγένετο … εἰς ψυχὴν ζῶσαν … εἰς πνεῦμα ζῳοποιοῦν: the verb stands once; the second "became" is supplied. The Greek word is ψυχή, "soul", which renders Hebrew *nephesh* in Gen 2:7; "soulish" (ψυχικόν, v44, 46) is formed from it, a link that "being" does not show. "First" and "Adam" are not in Gen 2:7. πνεῦμα has no article.
- **"of the earth, earthy" → "from the earth, of dust"** (v47–49) — Greek ἐκ γῆς χοϊκός: χοϊκός is "made of dust (χοῦς)", the word of Gen 2:7 in the Septuagint. The verse has no verb.
- **"the second man is the Lord from heaven" → "the second man is from heaven"** (v47) — "the Lord" (ὁ κύριος) is not in the critical text.
- **"we shall also bear" kept** (v49) — the printed text has φορέσομεν, a future (NA28); the other reading is φορέσωμεν, "let us also bear" (WH Treg RP). The two differ by one letter.
- **"We shall not all sleep" → "we shall not all fall asleep"** (v51) — Greek πάντες οὐ κοιμηθησόμεθα, literally "all of us shall not fall asleep".
- **"in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye" kept** (v52) — Greek ἐν ἀτόμῳ, "in an indivisible (instant)"; ἐν ῥιπῇ ὀφθαλμοῦ, "in the cast (glance) of an eye". σαλπίσει is "it (or: he) will trumpet"; "the trumpet" is supplied from the verb.
- **"this corruptible" / "this mortal" kept** (v53–54) — Greek τὸ φθαρτὸν τοῦτο, τὸ θνητὸν τοῦτο, neuter adjectives; no "body" in the Greek.
- **"the saying that is written" → "the word that is written"** (v54) — Greek ὁ λόγος ὁ γεγραμμένος.
- **"Death is swallowed up in victory" → "Death was swallowed up into victory"** (v54) — Greek κατεπόθη … εἰς νῖκος, an aorist; translated as printed. The Hebrew of Isa 25:8 has "he will swallow up death forever".
- **"O death, where is thy sting? O grave, where is thy victory?" → "Where, death, is your victory? Where, death, is your sting?"** (v55) — the critical text has θάνατε, "death", twice and "victory" first; "grave" (ᾅδη, Hades) is the later text (RP). Translated as printed; the wording differs from the Hebrew of Hos 13:14.
- **"be ye stedfast, unmoveable" → "become firm, immovable"** (v58) — Greek ἑδραῖοι γίνεσθε, "become"; ἑδραῖος, "seated, firm", as 7:37; Col 1:23.
- **"your labour is not in vain" → "your labor is not empty"** (v58) — Greek κενός, as v10, 14.

### Notes
- **v27** quotes Ps 8:6.
- **v32** quotes Isa 22:13.
- **v33** — quotation from the poet Menander (*Thais*).
- **v45** quotes Gen 2:7.
- **v54-55** quotes Isa 25:8 + Hos 13:14.
