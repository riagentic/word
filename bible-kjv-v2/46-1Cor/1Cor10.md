# 1 Corinthians 10

[1] For I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that our fathers were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea,

[2] and all had themselves ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ into ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ in the cloud and in the sea,

[3] and all ate the same spiritual food,

[4] and all drank the same spiritual drink; for they were drinking from a spiritual rock that followed, and the rock was ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[5] But not in the greater number of them did ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ take pleasure, for they were laid low in the wilderness.

[6] Now these things became patterns of us, so that we should not be desirers of evil things, as those also desired.

[7] And do not become idolaters, as some of them; as it is written, "**The people sat down to eat and to drink, and they rose up to play**."

[8] And let us not commit sexual immorality, as some of them committed it, and there fell in one day twenty-three thousand.

[9] And let us not put ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ to the test, as some of them tempted, and were perishing by the serpents.

[10] And do not murmur, even as some of them murmured, and perished by the bringer of ruin.

[11] Now these things were happening to those as a pattern, and they were written for our admonition, unto whom the ends of the ages have arrived.

[12] So then, let the one who thinks he stands look lest he fall.

[13] **No trial has taken you except a human one; but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is faithful, who will not let you be tempted beyond what you are able, but will make with the trial also the way out, so as to be able to bear it.**

[14] Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.

[15] I speak as to sensible ones; judge for yourselves what I say.

[16] The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not a fellowship of the blood of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧? The bread which we break, is it not a fellowship of the body of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧?

[17] Because there is one bread, we, the many, are one body, for we all partake of the one bread.

[18] Look at ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ according to the flesh: are not those who eat the sacrifices sharers of the altar?

[19] What then do I say? That a thing sacrificed to idols is anything, or that an idol is anything?

[20] But that the things which they sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and I do not want you to become sharers of the demons.

[21] You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons; you cannot partake of the table of the Lord and of the table of demons.

[22] Or do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than he?

[23] All things are permitted, but not all things are profitable. All things are permitted, but not all things build up.

[24] Let no one seek his own, but that of the other.

[25] Eat everything that is sold in the meat market, examining nothing because of conscience,

[26] for "**the earth is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the Lord's⟧, and its fullness**."

[27] If one of the unbelievers invites you and you want to go, eat everything that is set before you, examining nothing because of conscience.

[28] But if anyone says to you, "This is a sacred sacrifice," do not eat, because of that one who disclosed it, and because of conscience —

[29] conscience, I say, not one's own, but that of the other. For why is my liberty judged by another conscience?

[30] If I partake with thanks, why am I blasphemed for that for which I give thanks?

[31] Whether therefore you eat or drink or do anything, **do all things unto the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧**.

[32] Become without offense both to ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and to ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ and to the church of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[33] even as I also please all in all things, not seeking my own profit but that of the many, that they may be saved.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (vv4, 9, 16) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v2) — Greek βαπτίζω *baptizō* means "to immerse/dip"; "immersed into Moses" preserves the Greek verb's literal meaning and the typological parallel with water-immersion ritual.
- **"were all baptized" → "all had themselves immersed"** (v2) — Greek ἐβαπτίσαντο, a middle form (SBLGNT, Westcott-Hort, Tregelles); NA28 prints the passive ἐβαπτίσθησαν, "were immersed".
- **"church"** (v32) — Greek ἐκκλησία *ekklēsia*, "assembly/congregation."
- **"Moreover" → "For"** (v1) — Greek γάρ (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the chapter continues 9:24–27.
- **"that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ" → "a spiritual rock that followed, and the rock was the *Mashiach*"** (v4) — Greek ἐκ πνευματικῆς ἀκολουθούσης πέτρας, without the article and without "them"; ἔπινον is an imperfect, "they were drinking".
- **"with many of them God was not well pleased" → "not in the greater number of them did *Elohim* take pleasure"** (v5) — Greek οὐκ ἐν τοῖς πλείοσιν αὐτῶν ηὐδόκησεν; οἱ πλείονες as 9:19.
- **"overthrown" → "laid low"** (v5) — Greek κατεστρώθησαν, "were spread out, strewn on the ground" (Num 14:16 in the Septuagint).
- **"these things were our examples" → "these things became patterns of us"** (v6) — Greek τύποι ἡμῶν ἐγενήθησαν; τύπος "pattern" (v11 τυπικῶς, "as a pattern"). The genitive is "of us": patterns that concern us, or patterns for us.
- **"we should not lust after evil things, as they also lusted" → "we should not be desirers of evil things, as those also desired"** (v6) — Greek ἐπιθυμητὰς κακῶν … ἐπεθύμησαν, the root of ἐπιθυμία "desire".
- **"Neither be ye idolaters" → "And do not become idolaters"** (v7) — Greek μηδὲ … γίνεσθε, a command to "you"; v8–9 change to "let us", v10 back to "you". The quotation is the Greek as printed; "they rose up" is plural (ἀνέστησαν).
- **"commit fornication" → "commit sexual immorality"** (v8) — Greek πορνεύωμεν, the verb of πορνεία.
- **"Neither let us tempt Christ, as some of them also tempted" → "let us not put the *Mashiach* to the test, as some of them tempted"** (v9) — Greek ἐκπειράζωμεν, a stronger form, then ἐπείρασαν, the plain verb ("tempt", v13; one root with "trial"). "The *Mashiach*" (Χριστόν) is read by SBLGNT and NA28; Westcott-Hort and Tregelles read κύριον, "the Lord".
- **"were destroyed of serpents" → "were perishing by the serpents"** (v9) — Greek ἀπώλλυντο, an imperfect; v10 ἀπώλοντο, "perished".
- **"destroyed of the destroyer" → "perished by the bringer of ruin"** (v10) — Greek ὑπὸ τοῦ ὀλοθρευτοῦ, from ὄλεθρος "ruin" (as Heb 11:28); the Greek does not say who this is.
- **"all these things happened unto them for ensamples" → "these things were happening to those as a pattern"** (v11) — Greek τυπικῶς συνέβαινεν ἐκείνοις, an adverb and an imperfect; "all" is absent from the critical text.
- **"upon whom the ends of the world are come" → "unto whom the ends of the ages have arrived"** (v11) — Greek τὰ τέλη τῶν αἰώνων κατήντηκεν, both plural.
- **"take heed" → "look"** (v12) — Greek βλεπέτω.
- **"no temptation … but such as is common to man" → "no trial … except a human one"** (v13) — Greek πειρασμός, "trial, temptation", and the verb πειρασθῆναι "be tempted" are one root; ἀνθρώπινος is "human". εἴληφεν is "has taken, seized".
- **"a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear it" → "the way out, so as to be able to bear it"** (v13) — Greek τὴν ἔκβασιν τοῦ δύνασθαι ὑπενεγκεῖν; "you" is absent from the critical text and "it" is supplied. ὑποφέρω "bear" as 2 Tim 3:11.
- **"wise men" → "sensible ones"** (v15) — Greek φρονίμοις.
- **"the communion of" → "a fellowship of"** (v16) — Greek κοινωνία, without the article, with a plain genitive: a sharing in the blood and the body, or a fellowship that the blood and the body make. The same root in v18, 20 ("sharers").
- **"For we being many are one bread, and one body" → "Because there is one bread, we, the many, are one body"** (v17) — Greek ὅτι εἷς ἄρτος, ἓν σῶμα οἱ πολλοί ἐσμεν, without a verb in the first clause; it can also be read as the KJV has it, "because we, the many, are one bread, one body".
- **"Behold Israel after the flesh" → "Look at *Yisrael* according to the flesh"** (v18) — Greek βλέπετε τὸν Ἰσραὴλ κατὰ σάρκα; the Greek has no word "people".
- **"partakers of the altar" → "sharers of the altar"; "have fellowship with devils" → "become sharers of the demons"** (v18, 20) — Greek κοινωνοί, twice (as 2 Cor 1:7); "partake" (v17, 21, 30) is a different verb, μετέχω.
- **"What say I then?" kept** (v19) — Greek τί οὖν φημι; the same verb as v15. The order "a thing sacrificed to idols … an idol" follows Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28. εἰδωλόθυτον has no word "food".
- **"the things which the Gentiles sacrifice" → "the things which they sacrifice"** (v20) — "the nations" (τὰ ἔθνη) is read by Westcott-Hort and Tregelles and is absent from SBLGNT and NA28. The Greek has no "No" and no second "I say". "Not to *Elohim*" (οὐ θεῷ) echoes Deut 32:17 and can also be "to a no-god".
- **"devils" → "demons"** (v20, 21) — Greek δαιμόνια.
- **"All things are lawful for me" → "All things are permitted"; "expedient" → "profitable"; "edify" → "build up"** (v23) — "for me" is absent from the critical text. The Greek has no quotation marks; many read the words as a saying of the Corinthians which Paul answers. ἔξεστιν as 6:12; συμφέρει as 2 Cor 8:10; οἰκοδομεῖ as οἰκοδομή "building up".
- **"but every man another's wealth" → "but that of the other"** (v24) — Greek τὸ τοῦ ἑτέρου; the Greek has no word "good" and no "neighbor".
- **"shambles" → "meat market"; "asking no question for conscience sake" → "examining nothing because of conscience"** (v25, 27) — Greek ἐν μακέλλῳ; μηδὲν ἀνακρίνοντες, the verb of 9:3.
- **"the earth is the Lord's, and the fulness thereof" → "the earth is Yahweh's, and its fullness"** (v26) — the Greek has κυρίου, "the Lord's", for the divine name of Ps 24:1. The KJV repeats the line in v28, following the later text.
- **"bid you to a feast, and ye be disposed to go" → "invites you and you want to go"** (v27) — Greek καλεῖ ὑμᾶς … καὶ θέλετε πορεύεσθαι; no word "feast" or "dinner".
- **"This is offered in sacrifice unto idols" → "This is a sacred sacrifice"** (v28) — Greek ἱερόθυτον (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28), "sacrificed as sacred": the word a worshipper of the gods would use, not εἰδωλόθυτον (v19).
- **"for his sake that shewed it" → "because of that one who disclosed it"** (v28) — Greek δι' ἐκεῖνον τὸν μηνύσαντα.
- **"not thine own, but of the other" → "not one's own, but that of the other"** (v29) — Greek οὐχὶ τὴν ἑαυτοῦ, a third-person form: the conscience of the one addressed. "Another conscience" is ἄλλης συνειδήσεως.
- **"if I by grace be a partaker" → "If I partake with thanks"** (v30) — Greek χάριτι, the dative of χάρις: "with thanks" (the root of εὐχαριστῶ, "I give thanks", in the same verse) or "by grace".
- **"evil spoken of" → "blasphemed"** (v30) — Greek βλασφημοῦμαι.
- **"Give none offence" → "Become without offense"** (v32) — Greek ἀπρόσκοποι … γίνεσθε (as Phil 1:10): giving no cause to stumble.
- **"Gentiles" → "Greeks"** (v32) — Greek Ἕλλησιν.
- **"I please all men" → "I also please all"** (v33) — Greek πάντα πᾶσιν ἀρέσκω, a plain present: the sense is "seek to please". σύμφορον "profit" is the root of "profitable" (v23).

### Notes
- **v7** quotes Ex 32:6.
- **v26** quotes Ps 24:1.
- **v1–10** — the events are those of Exod 13–17; 32; Num 11; 14; 16; 21; 25. Num 25:9 gives twenty-four thousand (v8).
