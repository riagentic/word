# 1 Corinthians 12

[1] Now concerning the spiritual things, brothers, I do not want you to be ignorant.

[2] You know that when you were nations, you were being led away to the voiceless idols, however you might be led.

[3] Therefore I make known to you that no one speaking in the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ says, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is accursed," and no one can say, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is Lord," except in the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧.

[4] Now there are distributions of gifts, but the same Spirit;

[5] and there are distributions of services, and the same Lord;

[6] and there are distributions of workings, but the same ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who works all things in all.

[7] But to each one is given the manifestation of the Spirit for what is profitable.

[8] For to one is given through the Spirit a word of wisdom, and to another a word of knowledge according to the same Spirit,

[9] to a different one faith in the same Spirit, and to another gifts of healings in the one Spirit,

[10] and to another workings of powers, and to another prophecy, and to another discernings of spirits, to a different one kinds of tongues, and to another interpretation of tongues;

[11] but all these things the one and the same Spirit works, distributing to each one individually as it wishes.

[12] For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, being many, are one body, so also is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧;

[13] for indeed in one Spirit we all were ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ into one body, whether ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ or ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧, whether slaves or free, and we all were given one Spirit to drink.

[14] For indeed the body is not one member but many.

[15] If the foot says, "Because I am not a hand, I am not of the body," it is not on account of this not of the body.

[16] And if the ear says, "Because I am not an eye, I am not of the body," it is not on account of this not of the body.

[17] If the whole body were an eye, where would the hearing be? If the whole were hearing, where would the smelling be?

[18] But now ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ set the members, each one of them, in the body, even as he wanted.

[19] And if they all were one member, where would the body be?

[20] But now there are many members, but one body.

[21] And the eye cannot say to the hand, "I have no need of you," or again the head to the feet, "I have no need of you."

[22] But much more, the members of the body that seem to be weaker are necessary,

[23] and those of the body which we think to be less honorable, around these we put more abundant honor, and our unbecoming members have more abundant becomingness,

[24] but our becoming members have no need. But ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ blended the body together, having given more abundant honor to the one that is in lack,

[25] that there may be no schism in the body, but that the members may be anxious in the same way on behalf of one another.

[26] And if one member suffers, all the members suffer with it; if a member is glorified, all the members rejoice with it.

[27] Now you are the body of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and members in part.

[28] And some ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ set in the church: first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then powers, then gifts of healings, helps, governings, kinds of tongues.

[29] Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Are all powers?

[30] Do all have gifts of healings? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret?

[31] But be zealous for the greater gifts. And still I show you an exceeding way.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v12, 27) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 6, 18, 24, 28) — systematic.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v3) — systematic; πνεῦμα ἅγιον *pneuma hagion*.
- **"church"** (v28) — systematic.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v13) — βαπτίζω; systematic.
- **"Jesus is Lord" → "*Yeshua* is Lord"** (v3) — systematic.
- **"spiritual gifts" → "the spiritual things"** (v1) — Greek τῶν πνευματικῶν, an adjective with no noun: "the spiritual things" (as 14:1) or "the spiritual persons" (as 14:37). The word "gifts" (χαρίσματα) first comes in v4.
- **"ye were Gentiles, carried away unto these dumb idols, even as ye were led" → "when you were nations, you were being led away to the voiceless idols, however you might be led"** (v2) — Greek ὅτε ἔθνη ἦτε … ὡς ἂν ἤγεσθε ἀπαγόμενοι: the sentence is loosely built, with the two verbs "led" and "led away" side by side. ἔθνη is "nations" (as Eph 2:11); ἄφωνα is "without voice".
- **"I give you to understand" → "I make known to you"** (v3) — Greek γνωρίζω (as Gal 1:11).
- **"by the Spirit of God … by the Holy Ghost" → "in the Spirit of *Elohim* … in the *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v3) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι θεοῦ … ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ, both without the article.
- **"calleth Jesus accursed … that Jesus is the Lord" → "says, 'Yeshua is accursed' … 'Yeshua is Lord'"** (v3) — Greek Ἀνάθεμα Ἰησοῦς … Κύριος Ἰησοῦς, two cries of two words each, without a verb (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28). ἀνάθεμα as Gal 1:8. The Greek has no "ever".
- **"diversities of gifts" / "differences of administrations" / "diversities of operations" → "distributions of gifts" / "of services" / "of workings"** (v4–6) — Greek διαιρέσεις three times, the noun of διαιροῦν "distributing" in v11; it can also mean "differences, varieties". χάρισμα "gift" is from χάρις "grace"; διακονία "service"; ἐνέργημα, "a thing worked", is the noun of "works" (ἐνεργῶν, v6, 11).
- **"it is the same God which worketh all in all" → "the same *Elohim*, who works all things in all"** (v6) — Greek ὁ ἐνεργῶν τὰ πάντα ἐν πᾶσιν; "in all" is "in all persons" or "in all things".
- **"manifestation of the Spirit" kept** (v7) — Greek φανέρωσις (as 2 Cor 4:2), from φανερόω "manifest"; not ἀποκάλυψις "revelation".
- **"to profit withal" → "for what is profitable"** (v7) — Greek πρὸς τὸ συμφέρον (as 10:23); the Greek has no word "common".
- **"the word of wisdom … the word of knowledge" → "a word of wisdom … a word of knowledge"** (v8) — Greek λόγος σοφίας, λόγος γνώσεως, without the article.
- **"to another" / "to a different one"** (v8–10) — Greek ἄλλῳ and, twice, ἑτέρῳ (v9 "faith", v10 "kinds of tongues"); the two words are kept apart, as Gal 1:6–7. "And" (δέ) before "to another" in v9–10 follows Westcott-Hort and NA28 (Tregelles too in three of the five places); the SBLGNT omits it.
- **"by the same Spirit" → "in the same Spirit … in the one Spirit"** (v9) — Greek ἐν τῷ αὐτῷ πνεύματι … ἐν τῷ ἑνὶ πνεύματι (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28).
- **"the gifts of healing" → "gifts of healings"** (v9, 28, 30) — Greek χαρίσματα ἰαμάτων, both plural.
- **"the working of miracles" → "workings of powers"** (v10) — Greek ἐνεργήματα δυνάμεων; δυνάμεις "powers" as 2 Cor 12:12 (also v28, 29).
- **"discerning of spirits" → "discernings of spirits"** (v10) — Greek διακρίσεις πνευμάτων, plural; διακρίνω "discern" as 11:29, 31. The Greek has no "ability".
- **"divers kinds of tongues" → "kinds of tongues"** (v10, 28) — Greek γένη γλωσσῶν; no "various".
- **"dividing to every man severally as he will" → "distributing to each one individually as it wishes"** (v11) — Greek διαιροῦν ἰδίᾳ ἑκάστῳ καθὼς βούλεται. "It": the Greek participle is neuter, agreeing with πνεῦμα; the gender is grammatical. βούλομαι "wish" beside θέλω "want" in v18.
- **"so also is Christ" → "so also is the *Mashiach*"** (v12) — Greek οὕτως καὶ ὁ Χριστός, without a verb; the Greek has no "it is with".
- **"by one Spirit" → "in one Spirit"** (v13) — Greek ἐν ἑνὶ πνεύματι, "in" or "by".
- **"have been all made to drink into one Spirit" → "we all were given one Spirit to drink"** (v13) — Greek ἓν πνεῦμα ἐποτίσθημεν (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28, without "into"); ποτίζω as 3:2, also "water" (3:6).
- **"Gentiles" → "Greeks"; "bond" → "slaves"** (v13) — Greek Ἕλληνες, δοῦλοι.
- **"is it therefore not of the body?" → "it is not on account of this not of the body"** (v15, 16) — Greek οὐ παρὰ τοῦτο οὐκ ἔστιν ἐκ τοῦ σώματος, two negatives: a statement, "not for this reason does it cease to be of the body", or a question, as the KJV has it. The SBLGNT prints v15 as a question and v16 as a statement; the sense is the same either way.
- **"If the whole were hearing"** (v17) — Greek εἰ ὅλον ἀκοή; "body" is not repeated, and ἀκοή is "hearing", not "ear".
- **"hath God set … as it hath pleased him" → "*Elohim* set … even as he wanted"** (v18) — Greek ἔθετο (as v28), ἠθέλησεν.
- **"Nay, much more" → "But much more"; "more feeble" → "weaker"** (v22) — Greek ἀλλὰ πολλῷ μᾶλλον; ἀσθενέστερα.
- **"upon these we bestow more abundant honour" → "around these we put more abundant honor"** (v23) — Greek περιτίθεμεν, "put around", as a garment. "We think" (δοκοῦμεν) is the verb of "seem" in v22.
- **"uncomely parts … comeliness … comely parts" → "unbecoming members … becomingness … becoming members"** (v23–24) — Greek τὰ ἀσχήμονα, εὐσχημοσύνην, τὰ εὐσχήμονα, one root (as 14:40 "becomingly"). The Greek has no noun here; "members" is supplied from v22. No "modesty" and no "treated".
- **"tempered the body together" → "blended the body together"** (v24) — Greek συνεκέρασεν, "mixed together".
- **"that part which lacked" → "the one that is in lack"** (v24) — Greek τῷ ὑστερουμένῳ (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28; the SBLGNT prints the active ὑστεροῦντι, "that comes short"); ὑστερέω as 2 Cor 11:5, 9.
- **"should have the same care one for another" → "may be anxious in the same way on behalf of one another"** (v25) — Greek τὸ αὐτὸ ὑπὲρ ἀλλήλων μεριμνῶσι; μεριμνάω as 7:32–34; Phil 2:20.
- **"or one member be honoured" → "if a member is glorified"** (v26) — Greek δοξάζεται, "is glorified"; "one" is read by NA28 and absent from SBLGNT, Westcott-Hort, Tregelles. "Honor" (τιμή, v23–24) is a different word.
- **"members in particular" → "members in part"** (v27) — Greek μέλη ἐκ μέρους (as 13:9–12): each a member for his part. σῶμα Χριστοῦ has no article: "the body" or "a body" of the *Mashiach*. The Greek has no "of it".
- **"God hath set some in the church" → "some *Elohim* set in the church"** (v28) — Greek καὶ οὓς μὲν ἔθετο ὁ θεός, "and some indeed …": the sentence begins as if "and others" were to follow and goes on with "first … second …".
- **"miracles" → "powers"; "governments" → "governings"; "diversities of tongues" → "kinds of tongues"** (v28) — Greek δυνάμεις; ἀντιλήμψεις "helps"; κυβερνήσεις, the steering of a ship; γένη γλωσσῶν as v10. The Greek has no "and" in the list.
- **"are all workers of miracles?" → "Are all powers?"** (v29) — Greek μὴ πάντες δυνάμεις; without a verb. δυνάμεις can be a nominative, as the three before it, or an accusative with "have" of v30 ("do all have powers?"). All seven questions begin with μή and expect "no".
- **"do all speak with tongues?" → "Do all speak in tongues?"** (v30) — Greek γλώσσαις λαλοῦσιν, a dative (as 14:5).
- **"covet earnestly the best gifts" → "be zealous for the greater gifts"** (v31) — Greek ζηλοῦτε (as 14:1), a command or a statement ("you are zealous for"); τὰ μείζονα, "the greater" (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28).
- **"and yet shew I unto you a more excellent way" → "And still I show you an exceeding way"** (v31) — Greek καὶ ἔτι καθ' ὑπερβολὴν ὁδὸν ὑμῖν δείκνυμι: "a way according to excess", a way beyond all measure (as 2 Cor 4:17); not a comparative, and δείκνυμι is a present.

### Notes
- **v12–27** — the body and its members: compare Rom 12:4–5; Eph 4:15–16.
