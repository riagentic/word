# 1 Corinthians 11

[1] Become imitators of me, even as I also am of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[2] Now I praise you that you remember me in all things and hold fast the traditions, even as I delivered them to you.

[3] But I want you to know that the head of every man is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and the head of a woman is the man, and the head of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] Every man praying or prophesying, having something down over his head, puts his head to shame;

[5] but every woman praying or prophesying with the head uncovered puts her head to shame; for it is one and the same thing as the woman who is shaven.

[6] For if a woman does not cover herself, let her also be shorn; but if it is shameful for a woman to be shorn or shaven, let her cover herself.

[7] For a man indeed ought not to cover the head, being the image and glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; but the woman is the glory of man.

[8] For man is not from woman, but woman from man;

[9] for indeed man was not created because of the woman, but woman because of the man.

[10] Because of this the woman ought to have authority on the head, because of the angels.

[11] Nevertheless, neither is woman apart from man nor man apart from woman in the Lord;

[12] for as the woman is from the man, so also the man is through the woman; and all things are from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[13] Judge in your own selves: is it befitting for a woman to pray to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ uncovered?

[14] Does not even nature itself teach you that if a man has long hair, it is a dishonor to him,

[15] but if a woman has long hair, it is a glory to her? Because the long hair has been given to her instead of a cloak.

[16] But if anyone seems to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor do the churches of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[17] But in charging this I do not praise, because you come together not for the better but for the worse.

[18] For first, when you come together in church, I hear that schisms exist among you, and in some part I believe it.

[19] For there must also be sects among you, that also the approved may become manifest among you.

[20] When therefore you come together to the same place, it is not to eat the Lord's supper,

[21] for in the eating each one takes his own supper beforehand, and one is hungry and another is drunk.

[22] For have you no houses for eating and drinking? Or do you despise the church of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and put to shame those who have not? What shall I say to you? Shall I praise you? In this I do not praise.

[23] For I received from the Lord that which I also delivered to you, that the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, in the night in which he was being delivered up, took bread,

[24] and having given thanks he broke it and said, "This is my body, which is for you; do this unto my remembrance."

[25] In the same way also the cup, after the supper, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in my blood; do this, as often as you drink, unto my remembrance."

[26] For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the death of the Lord, until he comes.

[27] So then, whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord unworthily will be guilty of the body and of the blood of the Lord.

[28] But let a person test himself, and so let him eat of the bread and drink of the cup;

[29] for the one who eats and drinks eats and drinks a sentence to himself, not discerning the body.

[30] Because of this many among you are weak and infirm, and a considerable number are asleep.

[31] But if we discerned ourselves, we would not be judged;

[32] but being judged by the Lord we are trained, that we may not be condemned with the world.

[33] So then, my brothers, coming together to eat, wait for one another.

[34] If anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, that you may not come together unto a sentence. And the rest I will ordain when I come.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v23) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"church(es)" → "church / churches"** (vv16, 18, 22) — Greek ἐκκλησία / ἐκκλησίαι *ekklēsia / ekklēsiai*; "congregation/assembly," not an institutional building.
- **"Be ye followers of me" → "Become imitators of me"** (v1) — Greek μιμηταί μου γίνεσθε (as Eph 5:1); the second half has no verb ("am" is supplied).
- **"keep the ordinances, as I delivered them" → "hold fast the traditions, even as I delivered them"** (v2) — Greek τὰς παραδόσεις κατέχετε … παρέδωκα: noun and verb are one root (as 2 Thess 2:15). "Brethren" is absent from the critical text.
- **"man" / "woman" kept** (v3–15) — Greek ἀνήρ and γυνή throughout. ἀνήρ is "man" or "husband", γυνή "woman" or "wife"; Greek has no separate words, and nothing in these verses marks one sense ("her husband", "a wife"). The verses keep "man" and "woman" everywhere.
- **"the head" kept** (v3) — Greek κεφαλή, "head", three times; v4–5 then speak of the head of the body. What "head" means in v3 is disputed: one who is over another (as Eph 1:22; 5:23), or the source from which another comes (compare v8, 12). The verse gives the word only. "The head of a woman is the man": κεφαλὴ γυναικὸς ὁ ἀνήρ, "woman" without the article, "the man" with it.
- **"having his head covered" → "having something down over his head"** (v4) — Greek κατὰ κεφαλῆς ἔχων, literally "having down from the head", with no object; "something" is supplied. It is read of a covering that hangs down from the head, or of long hair (compare v14).
- **"dishonoureth his head" → "puts his head to shame"** (v4, 5) — Greek καταισχύνει (as v22), one root with "shameful" (αἰσχρόν, v6). "His head" / "her head" can be the head of the body or the "head" named in v3.
- **"that is even all one as if she were shaven" → "it is one and the same thing as the woman who is shaven"** (v5) — Greek ἓν γάρ ἐστιν καὶ τὸ αὐτὸ τῇ ἐξυρημένῃ.
- **"be not covered … let her be covered" → "does not cover herself … let her cover herself"** (v6) — Greek κατακαλύπτεται, κατακαλυπτέσθω; the Greek has no word "head" in this verse, and no "will not".
- **"forasmuch as he is the image and glory of God" → "being the image and glory of *Elohim*"** (v7) — Greek εἰκὼν καὶ δόξα θεοῦ ὑπάρχων, a participle (as Phil 2:6).
- **"the glory of the man" → "the glory of man"** (v7) — Greek δόξα ἀνδρός, without the article.
- **"the man is not of the woman" → "man is not from woman"** (v8) — Greek ἐκ, "out of, from"; the Greek has no "was made" and no articles here.
- **"for the woman … for the man" → "because of the woman … because of the man"** (v9) — Greek διά with the accusative, as "because of this" and "because of the angels" in v10.
- **"to have power on her head" → "to have authority on the head"** (v10) — Greek ἐξουσίαν ἔχειν ἐπὶ τῆς κεφαλῆς. ἐξουσία is "authority, right" (the word of 9:4–18). The Greek has no "symbol of", no "sign of" and no "her". It is read (1) of a covering as the sign of an authority, hers or one she stands under, or (2) "to have authority over her head", control of her own head. The verse gives the words as they stand.
- **"because of the angels" kept** (v10) — Greek διὰ τοὺς ἀγγέλους; ἄγγελος is "angel" or "messenger". The Greek does not explain the reason.
- **"neither is the man without the woman, neither the woman without the man" → "neither is woman apart from man nor man apart from woman"** (v11) — the order of Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28; χωρίς is "apart from, without". The Greek has no word "independent".
- **"of the man … by the woman … of God" → "from the man … through the woman … from *Elohim*"** (v12) — Greek ἐκ, διά, ἐκ; no "was made", no "is born", no "now".
- **"Judge in yourselves: is it comely" → "Judge in your own selves: is it befitting"** (v13) — Greek ἐν ὑμῖν αὐτοῖς, "in yourselves" or "among yourselves"; πρέπον as Titus 2:1. "Uncovered" (ἀκατακάλυπτον) describes the woman; no "head" here.
- **"shame" → "dishonor"** (v14) — Greek ἀτιμία, the opposite of honor; not the word of v4–6. φύσις is "nature".
- **"her hair is given her for a covering" → "the long hair has been given to her instead of a cloak"** (v15) — Greek ἡ κόμη ἀντὶ περιβολαίου δέδοται αὐτῇ. κόμη is the long hair of v14–15 (κομᾷ); περιβόλαιον is something thrown around, a cloak (Heb 1:12), a different word from "cover" in v5–7, 13; ἀντί is "instead of" or "as, answering to". "To her" (αὐτῇ) follows Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28; the SBLGNT omits it.
- **"seem to be contentious" kept** (v16) — Greek δοκεῖ φιλόνεικος εἶναι; δοκεῖ is "seems" or "thinks (fit)". "Custom": συνήθεια.
- **"in this that I declare unto you I praise you not" → "in charging this I do not praise"** (v17) — Greek τοῦτο δὲ παραγγέλλων οὐκ ἐπαινῶ (Westcott-Hort, NA28; Tregelles "this I charge, not praising"). "This" can point back or forward. The Greek has no "you" after "praise".
- **"in the church" → "in church"; "divisions" → "schisms"** (v18) — Greek ἐν ἐκκλησίᾳ, without the article: "in assembly". σχίσματα as 1:10; 12:25.
- **"heresies" → "sects"** (v19) — Greek αἱρέσεις (as Gal 5:20), groups formed by choice.
- **"they which are approved may be made manifest" → "also the approved may become manifest"** (v19) — Greek ἵνα καὶ οἱ δόκιμοι φανεροὶ γένωνται.
- **"into one place, this is not to eat the Lord's supper" → "to the same place, it is not to eat the Lord's supper"** (v20) — Greek ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό, "to the same (place)", "together"; οὐκ ἔστιν … φαγεῖν is "it is not (an) eating of" or "it is not possible to eat". κυριακὸν δεῖπνον is "a supper belonging to the Lord", without the article.
- **"taketh before other his own supper" → "takes his own supper beforehand"** (v21) — Greek προλαμβάνει; "supper" is the word of v20.
- **"shame them that have not" → "put to shame those who have not"** (v22) — Greek καταισχύνετε τοὺς μὴ ἔχοντας; no "nothing". The first question begins μὴ γὰρ … οὐκ, "for is it that you have no …?"; the Greek has no "What!".
- **"shall I praise you in this? I praise you not" → "Shall I praise you? In this I do not praise."** (v22) — the punctuation of the printed text; "in this" can also go with the question.
- **"delivered … betrayed" → "delivered … was being delivered up"** (v23) — Greek παρέδωκα and παρεδίδετο are one verb, παραδίδωμι: "deliver" of a tradition, "deliver up" of a person. The second is an imperfect passive and does not say by whom: by Judas ("betrayed"), or by *Elohim* (Rom 8:32).
- **"Take, eat: this is my body, which is broken for you" → "This is my body, which is for you"** (v24) — "Take, eat" and "broken" are absent from Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28. "It" after "broke" is supplied.
- **"in remembrance of me" → "unto my remembrance"** (v24, 25) — Greek εἰς τὴν ἐμὴν ἀνάμνησιν; ἀνάμνησις as Heb 10:3.
- **"he took the cup, when he had supped" → "the cup, after the supper"** (v25) — Greek ὡσαύτως καὶ τὸ ποτήριον μετὰ τὸ δειπνῆσαι, without a verb.
- **"new testament" → "new covenant"** (v25) — Greek διαθήκη, "covenant" (also "will, testament").
- **"as oft as ye drink it" → "as often as you drink"** (v25) — the Greek has no "it".
- **"ye do shew the Lord's death" → "you proclaim the death of the Lord"** (v26) — Greek καταγγέλλετε (as 9:14); statement or command. "This cup" and "this bread" (v27) follow the later text.
- **"guilty of the body and blood" → "guilty of the body and of the blood"** (v27) — Greek ἔνοχος … τοῦ σώματος καὶ τοῦ αἵματος, two articles; ἔνοχος with the genitive names that against which one is guilty.
- **"let a man examine himself" → "let a person test himself"** (v28) — Greek ἄνθρωπος, a human being, not ἀνήρ; δοκιμαζέτω, "test, approve" (as δόκιμοι, v19).
- **"eateth and drinketh unworthily, eateth and drinketh damnation to himself, not discerning the Lord's body" → "eats and drinks a sentence to himself, not discerning the body"** (v29) — "unworthily" and "the Lord's" are absent from Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28. κρίμα is "sentence" (also v34). "The body" is read of the body of the Lord (v24, 27) or of the church as the one body (10:17).
- **"sickly … many sleep" → "infirm … a considerable number are asleep"** (v30) — Greek ἄρρωστοι; κοιμῶνται ἱκανοί, the word used of the death of believers (1 Thess 4:13).
- **"judge ourselves" → "discerned ourselves"** (v31) — Greek διεκρίνομεν, the verb of v29, beside ἐκρινόμεθα "be judged"; v29–34 play on one root: κρίμα, διακρίνω, κρίνω, κατακρίνω.
- **"when we are judged, we are chastened of the Lord" → "being judged by the Lord we are trained"** (v32) — Greek κρινόμενοι δὲ ὑπὸ τοῦ κυρίου παιδευόμεθα; "by the Lord" can go with "judged" or with "trained". παιδεύω as Titus 2:12; Heb 12:6.
- **"tarry one for another" → "wait for one another"** (v33) — Greek ἐκδέχεσθε; some read "receive one another".
- **"unto condemnation" → "unto a sentence"; "set in order" → "ordain"** (v34) — Greek εἰς κρίμα; διατάξομαι as 9:14.

### Notes
- **v23–25** — compare Luke 22:19–20; Matt 26:26–28; Mark 14:22–24.
- **v7–9, 12** — Gen 1:26–27; 2:18–23.
