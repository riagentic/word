# 1 Corinthians 5

[1] Actually sexual immorality is heard of among you, and such sexual immorality as is not even among the nations, so that someone has his father's wife.

[2] And you are puffed up, and did not rather mourn, that the one who practiced this deed might be removed from your midst?

[3] For I indeed, being absent in the body but present in the spirit, have already judged, as being present, the one who has thus worked this,

[4] in the name of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, when you and my spirit are gathered together, with the power of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[5] to deliver up such a one to Satan for ruin of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord.

[6] Your boast is not good. Do you not know that **a little leaven leavens the whole lump**?

[7] Cleanse out the old leaven, that you may be a new lump, even as you are unleavened. For also our ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ was sacrificed, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧;

[8] so then let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, nor with leaven of malice and evil, but with unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

[9] I wrote to you in the letter not to mix with the sexually immoral,

[10] not altogether with the sexually immoral of this world, or with the covetous and extortioners, or idolaters, since in that case you would have to go out of the world.

[11] But now I wrote to you not to mix, if anyone called a brother is a sexually immoral person or covetous or an idolater or a reviler or a drunkard or an extortioner — with such a one not even to eat.

[12] For what is it to me to judge those outside? Do you not judge those inside,

[13] but those outside ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ judges? "**Remove the evil one from among yourselves.**"

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v4) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v7) — Greek *Christos* = Hebrew *Mashiach*, "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"It is reported commonly" → "Actually … is heard of"** (v1) — Greek Ὅλως ἀκούεται ἐν ὑμῖν πορνεία. ὅλως is "altogether, actually" (as 6:7); "among you" can go with "is heard" or with "sexual immorality". The Greek has no "it is reported that there is".
- **"fornication" → "sexual immorality"** (v1) — Greek πορνεία, twice; πόρνος (v9–11) is "sexually immoral".
- **"as is not so much as named among the Gentiles" → "as is not even among the nations"** (v1) — Greek ἥτις οὐδὲ ἐν τοῖς ἔθνεσιν, without a verb. "Named" (ὀνομάζεται) is a later addition (RP); WH, Treg and NA28 do not have it. No "tolerated" in the Greek.
- **"that one should have his father's wife" → "so that someone has his father's wife"** (v1) — Greek ὥστε γυναῖκά τινα τοῦ πατρὸς ἔχειν, literally "a wife of the father"; ἔχειν is present tense, a lasting state.
- **"puffed up" kept** (v2) — Greek πεφυσιωμένοι, as 4:6, 18–19; 8:1.
- **"and have not rather mourned" → "and did not rather mourn"** (v2) — Greek ἐπενθήσατε, a past tense; the verse is one question in the printed text. It can also be read as a statement, or "that he might be removed" as a separate command.
- **"he that hath done this deed" → "the one who practiced this deed"** (v2) — the verse follows πράξας "practiced" (WH NA28); ποιήσας "did" is read by SBLGNT, Treg and RP.
- **"taken away" → "removed"** (v2) — Greek ἀρθῇ, from αἴρω; the same root as "Remove" (ἐξάρατε) in v13.
- **"as absent in body" → "being absent in the body"** (v3) — Greek ἀπὼν τῷ σώματι; "as" (ὡς) is a later addition (RP). τῷ πνεύματι, "in the spirit", is Paul's own spirit, as v4 shows.
- **"concerning him that hath so done this deed" → "the one who has thus worked this"** (v3) — Greek τὸν οὕτως τοῦτο κατεργασάμενον, a direct object of "judged"; no "deed" here. κατεργάζομαι is "work out" elsewhere; "worked" here, because "worked this out" would say something else in English. A third verb beside "practiced" (v2).
- **v3–5** — one sentence. "In the name of our Lord *Yeshua*" can go with "have judged", with "gathered together" or with "to deliver up"; "with the power of our Lord *Yeshua*" can go with "gathered together" or with "to deliver up". The verse keeps the Greek order and decides nothing. "The one who has thus worked this" is taken up again by "such a one" in v5.
- **"our Lord Jesus Christ" → "our Lord *Yeshua*"** (v4, twice) — "Christ" is a later addition (RP); WH, Treg and NA28 do not have it.
- **"To deliver" → "to deliver up"** (v5) — Greek παραδοῦναι, as 1 Tim 1:20: a person handed into another's power.
- **"destruction" → "ruin"** (v5) — Greek ὄλεθρον; "destruction" is ἀπώλεια. The Greek has no "his" before "spirit" and no "this man".
- **"the day of the Lord Jesus" → "the day of the Lord"** (v5) — the verse follows τοῦ κυρίου (WH NA28, also SBLGNT); "Jesus" is added by Treg and RP.
- **"glorying" → "boast"** (v6) — Greek καύχημα.
- **"Purge out therefore" → "Cleanse out"** (v7) — Greek ἐκκαθάρατε, from καθαρ- "clean"; the printed Greek has no "therefore".
- **"For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us" → "For also our *Pesach* was sacrificed, the *Mashiach*"** (v7) — Greek καὶ γὰρ τὸ πάσχα ἡμῶν ἐτύθη Χριστός, in this order; ἐτύθη is a past tense. πάσχα is the Passover, here the Passover animal; the Greek has no word "lamb". "For us" (ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν) is a later addition (RP).
- **"Therefore let us keep the feast" → "so then let us keep the feast"** (v8) — Greek ὥστε ἑορτάζωμεν, one verb, "keep a feast".
- **"with" kept** (v8) — Greek ἐν, "in", three times.
- **"neither with the leaven of malice and wickedness" → "nor with leaven of malice and evil"** (v8) — Greek μηδὲ ἐν ζύμῃ κακίας καὶ πονηρίας; πονηρία is the noun of "the evil one" (v13).
- **"unleavened bread" kept** (v8) — Greek ἀζύμοις, a plural: "unleavened things, unleavened loaves"; the word of "you are unleavened" (v7).
- **"in an epistle" → "in the letter"** (v9) — Greek ἐν τῇ ἐπιστολῇ, with the article: a letter the readers know.
- **"not to company with" → "not to mix with"** (v9, 11) — Greek συναναμίγνυσθαι, "mix oneself together with"; as 2 Thess 3:14.
- **"fornicators" → "the sexually immoral"** (v9, 10, 11) — Greek πόρνος.
- **"Yet not altogether" → "not altogether"** (v10) — Greek οὐ πάντως: "not in every case, not absolutely" or "not at all meaning". The Greek has no "meaning".
- **"extortioners" kept** (v10, 11) — Greek ἅρπαξ, from ἁρπάζω "seize": one who seizes what belongs to others. The Greek joins "the covetous and extortioners" with one article.
- **"for then must ye needs go out of the world" → "since in that case you would have to go out of the world"** (v10) — Greek ἐπεὶ ὠφείλετε ἄρα, "since you would then be obliged"; ἐπεὶ ἄρα as 7:14.
- **"But now I have written" → "But now I wrote"** (v11) — Greek νῦν δὲ ἔγραψα, the same past tense as v9. It is either "but as it is, what I wrote was …" or the past tense of a letter writer, "but now I write".
- **"a railer" → "a reviler"** (v11) — Greek λοίδορος, the noun of λοιδορέω "revile".
- **"if any man that is called a brother be a fornicator" → "if anyone called a brother is a sexually immoral person"** (v11) — Greek ἐάν τις ἀδελφὸς ὀνομαζόμενος ᾖ πόρνος. The Greek has no "guilty of".
- **"For what have I to do to judge them also that are without?" → "For what is it to me to judge those outside?"** (v12) — Greek τί γάρ μοι τοὺς ἔξω κρίνειν; "also" (καί) is a later addition (RP).
- **"them that are within" → "those inside"** (v12) — Greek τοὺς ἔσω; the Greek has no "church" here.
- **"God judgeth" → "*Elohim* judges"; one question** (v12–13) — the printed text (κρίνει, present: SBLGNT WH Treg) makes v12b–13a one question. The old manuscripts have no accents: NA28 and RP accent κρινεῖ, "will judge".
- **"Therefore put away from among yourselves that wicked person" → "Remove the evil one from among yourselves"** (v13) — Greek ἐξάρατε τὸν πονηρὸν ἐξ ὑμῶν αὐτῶν, a command (WH Treg NA28); RP reads Καὶ ἐξαρεῖτε, "And you shall remove". τὸν πονηρόν is masculine, "the evil man"; the Greek has no word "person".

### Notes
- **v13** — the wording of Deut 17:7 in the Septuagint (also Deut 19:19; 22:21, 24; 24:7); the Greek here has no formula of quotation.
- **v6** — the same sentence in Gal 5:9.
