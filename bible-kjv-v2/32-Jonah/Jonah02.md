# Jonah 2

[1] Then ⟦jonah|Yonah|Jonah⟧ prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from the belly of the fish,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 6) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (vv2-9) — KJV prose; Jonah's prayer is Hebrew poetry modeled on the Psalms.
- **"out of the belly of hell" → "out of the belly of Sheol"** (v2) — Hebrew מִבֶּטֶן שְׁאוֹל *mibeten Sheol* = from the belly of Sheol; the underworld; "hell" is theological interpretation; *Sheol* retained.
- **"I cried by reason of mine affliction unto the LORD, and he heard me" → "I called out to Yahweh, out of my distress, and he answered me"** (v2) — Hebrew מִצָּרָה לִי *mitzarah li* = out of my distress; restructured for poetic flow; "answered" for *wayya'aneni*.
- **"the floods compassed me about" → "the flood surrounded me"** (v3) — Hebrew נָהָר יְסֹבְבֵנִי *nahar yesobeveni* = a river/current surrounded me; "flood" singular; "surrounded" consistent.
- **"all thy billows and thy waves passed over me" → "all your waves and your billows passed over me"** (v3) — modernized; order reversed to "waves and billows" (cf. Ps 42:7 which has the same phrase in reverse — following Jonah's order).
- **"I am cast out of thy sight" → "I am driven away from your sight"** (v4) — Hebrew נִגְרַשְׁתִּי *nigrashti* = I am driven out/expelled; "driven away" more vivid.
- **"The waters compassed me about, even to the soul" → "The waters closed in over me to take my life"** (v5) — Hebrew עַד נֶפֶשׁ *ad nefesh* = to the point of life/soul; "to take my life" captures the mortal danger.
- **"the depth closed me round about" → "the deep surrounded me"** (v5) — Hebrew תְּהוֹם יְסֹבְבֵנִי *tehom yesobeveni*; *tehom* = deep/abyss; "the deep."
- **"I went down to the bottoms of the mountains" → "I went down to the land whose bars closed upon me forever"** (v6) — Hebrew יָרַדְתִּי לְקִצְבֵי הָרִים *yaradti leqitzevey harim* = I went down to the bases of the mountains; בְּרִיחִים *berichim* = bars (of the underworld gates); the two-verse unit restructured to flow.
- **"yet hast thou brought up my life from corruption" → "yet you brought up my life from the pit"** (v6) — Hebrew מִשַּׁחַת *mishachat* = from the pit/corruption; "pit" more vivid and consistent with Psalms usage; "corruption" is KJV's reading.
- **"Salvation is of the LORD" → "**Yeshuah belongs to Yahweh!**"** (v9) — Hebrew לַיהוָה הַיְשׁוּעָה *laYahweh hayeshuah* = salvation/deliverance belongs to Yahweh; *Yeshuah* = salvation (cognate with *Yeshua*); Hebrew term retained; emphasized as climactic declaration.
