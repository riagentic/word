## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton.
- **Layout** — the prayer (v2b–9) is poetry, one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line by the accents; v1, 2a and 10 prose. No "O" before Yahweh (v6): the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"Then Jonah prayed unto the LORD his God out of the fish's belly" → "And Jonah prayed to Yahweh his Elohim from the inward parts of the fish"** (v1) — מִמְּעֵי "from the inward parts" (as 1:17); here the feminine הַדָּגָה, in 1:17 and v10 דָּג: the same fish, the form named only here.
- **"And said, I cried by reason of mine affliction unto the LORD, and he heard me; out of the belly of hell cried I, and thou heardest my voice" → "And he said: I called out of my distress to Yahweh, and he answered me; out of the belly of Sheol I cried for help; you heard my voice"** (v2) — וַיֹּאמֶר "and he said" (not "saying"); מִצָּרָה לִי "out of distress to me", the Hebrew order; עָנָה "answer"; שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (not "hell"); שִׁוַּע "cry for help" (beside קָרָא "call"); "you heard" with no "and".
- **"For thou hadst cast me into the deep, in the midst of the seas; and the floods compassed me about: all thy billows and thy waves passed over me" → "And you cast me into the depth, into the heart of the seas, and the current surrounded me; all your breakers and your waves passed over me"** (v3) — וַ "and" (not "for"); מְצוּלָה "depth" (beside תְּהוֹם "deep", v5); בִּלְבַב יַמִּים "in the heart of the seas"; נָהָר "river", here of the sea's current; מִשְׁבָּרֶיךָ וְגַלֶּיךָ "your breakers and your waves", the Hebrew order and words of Ps 42:7.
- **"Then I said, I am cast out of thy sight; yet I will look again toward thy holy temple" → "And I, I said, I am driven out from before your eyes; yet I will again look to your holy temple"** (v4) — the stressed וַאֲנִי "and I"; נִגְרַשְׁתִּי "I am driven out"; מִנֶּגֶד עֵינֶיךָ "from before your eyes"; אַךְ "yet" (the Greek of Theodotion is said to read אֵיךְ "how shall I …?", from memory; the MT kept); הֵיכַל קָדְשֶׁךָ "your holy temple" (lit. "the temple of your holiness", as v7).
- **"The waters compassed me about, even to the soul: the depth closed me round about, the weeds were wrapped about my head" → "Waters encompassed me, to the soul; the deep surrounded me; reeds were bound about my head"** (v5) — אָפַף "encompass"; עַד־נֶפֶשׁ "to the soul" (= to the throat, the life itself: the soul row; "to take my life" was not the words); תְּהוֹם "deep"; סוּף "reeds" (the word of Exod 2:3, 5; here plants of the sea); חָבוּשׁ "bound".
- **"I went down to the bottoms of the mountains; the earth with her bars was about me for ever: yet hast thou brought up my life from corruption" → "To the bases of the mountains I went down; the earth, her bars were about me forever; and you brought up my life from the pit"** (v6) — the Hebrew verse begins with לְקִצְבֵי הָרִים (קְצָבִים only here: "the bases, the cut-off ends"); הָאָרֶץ בְּרִחֶיהָ בַעֲדִי "the earth, her bars about me" (no verb; "were" supplied); וַתַּעַל "and you brought up"; שַׁחַת "pit" (the Ps 28–31 row; KJV "corruption" reads it from שָׁחַת "ruin").
- **"When my soul fainted within me I remembered the LORD: and my prayer came in unto thee, into thine holy temple" → "When my soul fainted within me, Yahweh I remembered; and my prayer came to you, to your holy temple"** (v7) — בְּהִתְעַטֵּף עָלַי נַפְשִׁי "when my soul fainted upon me"; the object first ("Yahweh I remembered"); תָּבוֹא אֶל "came to".
- **"They that observe lying vanities forsake their own mercy" → "Those who keep empty vapors forsake their chesed"** (v8) — מְשַׁמְּרִים "keep" (piel of שָׁמַר); הַבְלֵי־שָׁוְא "empty vapors" as Ps 31:6 (הֶבֶל "vapor", the word for what has no substance; of idols); חַסְדָּם "their chesed": their own loyalty, or the One who is their chesed (Ps 144:2): open in the Hebrew.
- **"But I will sacrifice unto thee with the voice of thanksgiving; I will pay that that I have vowed. Salvation is of the LORD" → "But I, with the voice of thanksgiving, let me sacrifice to you; what I have vowed, let me pay. Yeshuah belongs to Yahweh!"** (v9) — the stressed וַאֲנִי; אֶזְבְּחָה, אֲשַׁלֵּמָה cohortatives ("let me"); תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving"; יְשׁוּעָתָה, the long form of יְשׁוּעָה "salvation", keeps its token; לַיהוָה "to Yahweh" (= belongs to).
- **"And the LORD spake unto the fish, and it vomited out Jonah upon the dry land" → "And Yahweh spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah out onto the dry land"** (v10) — וַיֹּאמֶר is "said" (not דִּבֵּר "speak"); with no words following, English needs "spoke"; הַיַּבָּשָׁה "the dry land" (as 1:9, 13).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — our 2:1–10 = Heb 2:2–11 (our 1:17 = Heb 2:1).
- **Jonah's prayer** (v2–9) — a psalm of thanksgiving built from the Psalms' words (Ps 18:6; 31:6, 22; 42:7; 120:1; 142:3).
