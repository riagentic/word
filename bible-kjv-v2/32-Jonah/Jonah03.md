## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. הָאֱלֹהִים with the article (v9, 10) and אֱלֹהִים without it (v3, 5, 8) are both the plain Elohim token (the reviewer's row).
- **"And the word of the LORD came unto Jonah the second time" → "And the word of Yahweh came to Jonah a second time, saying"** (v1) — the formula of 1:1; שֵׁנִית "a second time".
- **"that great city, and preach unto it the preaching that I bid thee" → "the great city, and call out to it the call that I am speaking to you"** (v2) — קְרָא … הַקְּרִיאָה, the verb with its own noun ("call … the call"; הַקְּרִיאָה only here); אֵלֶיהָ "to it" (1:2 has עָלֶיהָ "against it"); אָנֹכִי דֹּבֵר participle "I am speaking".
- **"So Jonah arose … Now Nineveh was an exceeding great city of three days' journey" → "And Jonah arose … And Nineveh was a great city to Elohim, a walk of three days"** (v3) — עִיר־גְּדוֹלָה לֵאלֹהִים is printed: "a great city to Elohim" (= great in the eyes of Elohim; or, as a superlative, "an exceedingly great city", the reading of KJV and most versions: the sense is open, the words are kept); מַהֲלַךְ "a walk" (lit. "a going"); "in breadth" is not in the Hebrew. הָיְתָה "was".
- **"And Jonah began to enter into the city a day's journey, and he cried, and said, Yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown" → "And Jonah began to go into the city, a walk of one day; and he called out and said, Yet forty days, and Nineveh is overthrown!"** (v4) — וַיִּקְרָא וַיֹּאמַר "called out and said" (both verbs); נֶהְפָּכֶת participle "is overthrown" (= is being overturned, of what is at hand; הָפַךְ also "turn": the word may carry a turn of another kind).
- **"So the people of Nineveh believed God, and proclaimed a fast" → "And the men of Nineveh believed in Elohim; and they called a fast"** (v5) — אַנְשֵׁי "men of"; הֶאֱמִין בְּ "believe in"; קָרָא צוֹם "call a fast" (the verb of v2, 4).
- **"For word came unto the king … and he laid his robe from him … and sat in ashes" → "And the word reached the king … and removed his robe from him … and sat on the ash"** (v6) — וַיִּגַּע "reached"; הֶעֱבִיר "removed" (lit. "made pass"); עַל־הָאֵפֶר "on the ash" (singular, the article).
- **"And he caused it to be proclaimed and published through Nineveh by the decree of the king and his nobles, saying, Let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock, taste any thing: let them not feed, nor drink water" → "And he had it cried out, and he said in Nineveh, By the decree of the king and his great ones, saying: Man and beast, herd and flock, let them not taste anything; let them not graze, and water let them not drink"** (v7) — וַיַּזְעֵק hifil "had it cried out"; וַיֹּאמֶר "and he said"; טַעַם "decree" (the word for "taste": יִטְעֲמוּ "taste" follows); גְּדֹלָיו "his great ones"; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept; the nouns stand first and the jussives follow ("let them not …").
- **"But let man and beast be covered with sackcloth, and cry mightily unto God: yea, let them turn every one from his evil way, and from the violence that is in their hands" → "And let them cover themselves with sackcloth, man and beast, and let them call to Elohim with strength; and let them turn, each man from his evil way and from the violence that is in their palms"** (v8) — וְ "and" (not "but"); הִתְכַּסָּה "cover oneself"; בְחָזְקָה "with strength"; אִישׁ "each man"; כַּפֵּיהֶם "their palms" (plural: the people).
- **"Who can tell if God will turn and repent, and turn away from his fierce anger, that we perish not?" → "Who knows? He may turn, and Elohim relent, and turn back from the burning of his anger, and we shall not perish"** (v9) — מִי־יוֹדֵעַ "who knows"; יָשׁוּב … וְשָׁב, the one verb twice ("turn … turn back"); נִחַם "relent" (not "repent"; the שׁוּב row); חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ "the burning of his anger"; וְלֹא נֹאבֵד as 1:6.
- **"God saw their works … and God repented of the evil, that he had said that he would do unto them; and he did it not" → "And Elohim saw their works … and Elohim relented concerning the evil that he had spoken of doing to them, and he did not do it"** (v10) — נִחַם עַל הָרָעָה "relent concerning the evil" (רָעָה "evil": the harm planned, the same word as "their evil way"; not "disaster"); דִּבֶּר "spoken".

### Notes
- **Nineveh's size** (v3) — "a walk of three days" may be the time to cross it or to go through its streets and districts; the Hebrew does not say which.
