# Jude 1

[1] Jude, a servant of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* and brother of Jacob,

To those who are called, beloved in *Elohim* the Father and kept for *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*:

[2] May mercy, peace, and love be multiplied to you.

[3] Beloved, although I was very eager to write to you about our common salvation, I found it necessary to write appealing to you to contend for the faith that was once for all delivered to the saints.

[4] For certain people have crept in unnoticed who long ago were designated for this condemnation, ungodly people, who pervert the grace of our *Elohim* into sensuality and deny our only Master and Lord, *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.

[5] Now I want to remind you, although you once fully knew it, that the Lord, who saved a people out of the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed those who did not believe.

[6] And the angels who did not stay within their own position of authority, but left their proper dwelling, he has kept in eternal chains under gloomy darkness until the judgment of the great day —

[7] just as Sodom and Gomorrah and the surrounding cities, which likewise indulged in sexual immorality and pursued unnatural desire, serve as an example by undergoing a punishment of eternal fire.

[8] Yet in like manner these people also, relying on their dreams, defile the flesh, reject authority, and blaspheme the glorious ones.

[9] But when the archangel Michael, contending with the devil, was disputing about the body of Moses, he did not presume to pronounce a blasphemous judgment, but said, "**The Lord rebuke you**."

[10] But these people blaspheme all that they do not understand, and they are destroyed by all that they, like unreasoning animals, understand instinctively.

[11] Woe to them! For they walked in the way of Cain and abandoned themselves for the sake of gain to Balaam's error and perished in Korach's rebellion.

[12] These are hidden reefs at your love feasts, as they feast with you without fear, shepherds feeding themselves; waterless clouds, swept along by winds; fruitless trees in late autumn, twice dead, uprooted;

[13] wild waves of the sea, casting up the foam of their own shame; wandering stars, for whom the gloom of utter darkness has been reserved forever.

[14] It was also about these that Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied, saying, "**Behold, the Lord comes with ten thousands of his holy ones,**

**[15] to execute judgment on all and to convict all the ungodly of all their deeds of ungodliness that they have committed in such an ungodly way, and of all the harsh things that ungodly sinners have spoken against him**."

[16] These are grumblers, malcontents, following their own sinful desires; they are loud-mouthed boasters, showing favoritism to gain advantage.

[17] But you must remember, beloved, the predictions of the apostles of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.

[18] They said to you, "In the last time there will be scoffers, following their own ungodly passions."

[19] It is these who cause divisions, worldly people, devoid of the Spirit.

[20] But you, beloved, building yourselves up in your most holy faith and praying in the *Ruach HaQodesh*,

[21] keep yourselves in the love of *Elohim*, waiting for the mercy of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* that leads to eternal life.

[22] And have mercy on those who doubt;

[23] save others by snatching them out of the fire; to others show mercy with fear, hating even the garment stained by the flesh.

[24] Now to him who is able to keep you from stumbling and to present you blameless before the presence of his glory with great joy,

[25] to the only *Elohim*, our Savior, through *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* our Lord, be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority, before all time and now and forever. Amen.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v1) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 4, 17, 21) — systematic.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v20) — systematic.
- **"the grace of our God" → "the grace of our *Elohim*"** (v4) — "God" → "*Elohim*."
- **"turning the grace of our God into lasciviousness" → "who pervert the grace of our *Elohim* into sensuality"** (v4) — "lasciviousness" → "sensuality"; modernized.
- **"dignities" → "the glorious ones"** (v8) — Greek δόξας *doxas* = "glories / glorious beings"; KJV "dignities" is too mild; the reference is to angelic or exalted beings.
- **"spots in your feasts of charity" → "hidden reefs at your love feasts"** (v12) — Greek σπιλάδες *spilades* = "rocks hidden underwater / reefs" (a nautical metaphor for hidden dangers that wreck ships); "spots" misunderstands the word; "love feasts" = Greek ἀγάπαις *agapais* (the communal *agape* meals); "charity" in KJV = "love."
- **"raging waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame" → "wild waves of the sea, casting up the foam of their own shame"** (v13) — "raging" → "wild"; "foaming out" → "casting up the foam of"; rendered more vividly.
- **"clouds without water" → "waterless clouds"** (v12) — Greek νεφέλαι ἄνυδροι *nephelai anydroi*; restructured.
- **"walking after their own lusts" → "following their own sinful desires"** (v16, 18) — systematic; "lusts" → "sinful desires."
- **"murmurers, complainers" → "grumblers, malcontents"** (v16) — Greek γογγυσταί, μεμψίμοιροι *gongystai, mempsimoiroi* = "grumblers, fault-finders / discontented ones"; "malcontents" is a good modern equivalent.
- **"Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling" → "Now to him who is able to keep you from stumbling"** (v24) — Greek ἀπταίστους *aptaistous* = "without stumbling / without falling."
- **"with exceeding joy" → "with great joy"** (v24) — Greek ἐν ἀγαλλιάσει *en agalliasei* = "with exultation / great joy"; modernized.
- **"To the only wise God our Saviour" → "to the only *Elohim*, our Savior"** (v25) — "God" → "*Elohim*"; "wise" dropped following the shorter text supported by many MSS (σοφῷ "wise" is absent in several witnesses).

### Notes
- **v9** draws on the lost *Assumption of Moses*.
- **v14-15** quotes *1 Enoch* 1:9.
