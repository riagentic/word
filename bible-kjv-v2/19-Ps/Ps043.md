# Psalm 43

[1] Judge me, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and contend my case
against a nation not faithful;
from a man of deceit and injustice you will rescue me.

[2] For you are the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my strong place;
why have you rejected me?
Why do I walk about darkened
in the oppression of the enemy?

[3] Send your light and your truth;
they — let them lead me;
let them bring me to your holy mountain
and to your dwellings.

[4] And let me come to the altar of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
to ⟦el|El|God⟧, the gladness of my joy,
and let me give thanks to you with the lyre,
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] Why are you bowed down, my soul,
and why are you in turmoil within me?
Hope for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for I shall yet give thanks to him,
the salvations of my face and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 4, 5) — Hebrew *Elohim*, as printed (the Elohistic psalms, as Ps 42).
- **"God" → "El"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"Judge me, O God, and plead my cause against an ungodly nation" → "Judge me, Elohim, and contend my case against a nation not faithful"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׁפְטֵנִי אֱלֹהִים וְרִיבָה רִיבִי מִגּוֹי לֹא־חָסִיד: שָׁפַט "judge"; רִיב verb and noun, "contend my case"; מִן "from", here "against" (the case is brought against them); לֹא־חָסִיד "not faithful" (חָסִיד "faithful one", as 2 Sam 22:26; not "ungodly").
- **"O deliver me from the deceitful and unjust man" → "from a man of deceit and injustice you will rescue me"** (v1) — Hebrew מֵאִישׁ־מִרְמָה וְעַוְלָה תְפַלְּטֵנִי: מִרְמָה "deceit", עַוְלָה "injustice"; פִּלֵּט "rescue". תְפַלְּטֵנִי is an imperfect, not an imperative: "you will rescue me" (it may also be read as a wish, "may you rescue me").
- **"For thou art the God of my strength: why dost thou cast me off?" → "For you are the Elohim of my strong place; why have you rejected me?"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי־אַתָּה אֱלֹהֵי מָעוּזִּי לָמָה זְנַחְתָּנִי; מָעוֹז "strong place" (as 2 Sam 22:33); זָנַח "reject" (42:9 has שְׁכַחְתָּנִי "forgotten me").
- **"why go I mourning because of the oppression of the enemy?" → "Why do I walk about darkened in the oppression of the enemy?"** (v2) — Hebrew לָמָּה־קֹדֵר אֶתְהַלֵּךְ בְּלַחַץ אוֹיֵב; הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about" (42:9 has אֵלֵךְ "go"); קֹדֵר "darkened" (in mourning, as 42:9); בְּ "in".
- **"O send out thy light and thy truth: let them lead me; let them bring me unto thy holy hill, and to thy tabernacles" → "Send your light and your truth; they — let them lead me; let them bring me to your holy mountain and to your dwellings"** (v3) — Hebrew שְׁלַח־אוֹרְךָ וַאֲמִתְּךָ הֵמָּה יַנְחוּנִי יְבִיאוּנִי אֶל־הַר־קָדְשְׁךָ וְאֶל־מִשְׁכְּנוֹתֶיךָ: שָׁלַח "send"; the pronoun הֵמָּה "they" is written; נָחָה "lead" (the imperfects may also be "they will lead me … bring me"); הַר "mountain"; מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling", plural.
- **"Then will I go unto the altar of God, unto God my exceeding joy: yea, upon the harp will I praise thee" → "And let me come to the altar of Elohim, to El, the gladness of my joy, and let me give thanks to you with the lyre"** (v4) — Hebrew וְאָבוֹאָה … אֶל־אֵל שִׂמְחַת גִּילִי וְאוֹדְךָ בְכִנּוֹר: cohortative with "and" (not "then"); בּוֹא "come"; שִׂמְחַת גִּילִי "the gladness of my joy" (שִׂמְחָה "gladness", גִּיל "joy", two words of rejoicing); הוֹדָה "give thanks to"; כִּנּוֹר "lyre".
- **"O God my God" → "Elohim, my Elohim"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים אֱלֹהָי.
- **Refrain (v5)** — Hebrew word for word as 42:11, and so the same wording; see Ps 42 for the words.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Ps 43 has no heading: our numbers = the Hebrew.
- **Continuation of Ps 42** — the same refrain (42:5, 11; 43:5).
