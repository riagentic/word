# Psalm 43

[1] Vindicate me, O *Elohim*, and defend my cause
against an ungodly people,
from the deceitful and unjust man deliver me!

[2] For you are the *Elohim* in whom I take refuge;
why have you rejected me?
Why do I go about mourning
because of the oppression of the enemy?

[3] Send out your light and your truth;
let them lead me;
let them bring me to your holy mountain
and to your dwelling!

[4] Then I will go to the altar of *Elohim*,
to *Elohim* my exceeding joy,
and I will praise you with the lyre,
O *Elohim*, my *Elohim*.

[5] Why are you cast down, O my soul,
and why are you in turmoil within me?
Hope in *Elohim*; for I shall again praise him,
my salvation and my *Elohim*.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 4) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — continuation of Ps 42 without superscription; closes with identical refrain (v5).

### Notes
- **Continuation of Ps 42** — same refrain.
