# Psalm 150

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Praise ⟦el|El|God⟧ in his sanctuary;
praise him in his mighty heavens!

[2] Praise him for his mighty deeds;
praise him according to his excellent greatness!

[3] Praise him with the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧;
praise him with lute and harp!

[4] Praise him with tambourine and dance;
praise him with strings and pipe!

[5] Praise him with sounding cymbals;
praise him with loud clashing cymbals!

[6] Let everything that has breath praise ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧!
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"El"** (v1) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **"Yah"** (v6) — shortened Yahweh; retained.
- **Poetic structure** — thirteen imperatives to praise; instruments catalog; every breathing creature (v6) as climax and close of entire Psalter.

### Notes
- **Closing doxology** of the Psalter.
- **Thirteen imperatives** to praise.
