# Psalm 150

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Praise ⟦el|El|God⟧ in his holiness;
praise him in the expanse of his strength!

[2] Praise him for his mighty deeds;
praise him according to the abundance of his greatness!

[3] Praise him with the blowing of ⟦shofar|a shofar|a trumpet⟧;
praise him with harp and lyre!

[4] Praise him with timbrel and dance;
praise him with strings and pipe!

[5] Praise him with cymbals of sound;
praise him with cymbals of shouting!

[6] Let all breath praise ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧!
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"El"** (v1) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **"Yah"** (v6) — Hebrew יָהּ, the short form of the name Yahweh, with its token.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v1, 6) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ, "praise Yah", with its token (in v1 written as two words, הַלְלוּ יָהּ).
- **"Praise God in his sanctuary: praise him in the firmament of his power" → "Praise El in his holiness; praise him in the expanse of his strength"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּקָדְשׁוֹ "in his holiness" (קֹדֶשׁ; also of a place, "his holy place, his sanctuary": the word is kept and the sense left open); רְקִיעַ "expanse" (as Gen 1:6; Ps 19:1; not "firmament"); עֻזּוֹ "his strength" (עֹז; "power" is כֹּחַ).
- **"Praise him for his mighty acts: praise him according to his excellent greatness" → "Praise him for his mighty deeds; praise him according to the abundance of his greatness"** (v2) — Hebrew בִגְבוּרֹתָיו "for his mighty deeds" (גְּבוּרוֹת, lit. "mights": "deeds" supplied; בְּ "in, for"); כְּרֹב גֻּדְלוֹ "according to the abundance of his greatness" (רֹב "abundance"; "excellent" is not written).
- **"Praise him with the sound of the trumpet: praise him with the psaltery and harp" → "Praise him with the blowing of a shofar; praise him with harp and lyre"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּתֵקַע שׁוֹפָר: תֵּקַע "blowing" (noun of תָּקַע "blow", only here); שׁוֹפָר "shofar", the ram's horn, with its token; נֵבֶל "harp", כִּנּוֹר "lyre" (as 2 Sam 6:5; 1 Chr 15:16: the earlier "lute and harp" put the words on the wrong instruments). The kinds of these instruments are uncertain.
- **"Praise him with the timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and organs" → "Praise him with timbrel and dance; praise him with strings and pipe"** (v4) — Hebrew תֹּף "timbrel" (a hand drum); מָחוֹל "dance"; מִנִּים "strings" (as Ps 45:8); עוּגָב "pipe" (a wind instrument; the exact sense is uncertain; as Gen 4:21).
- **"Praise him upon the loud cymbals: praise him upon the high sounding cymbals" → "Praise him with cymbals of sound; praise him with cymbals of shouting"** (v5) — Hebrew צִלְצְלֵי־שָׁמַע "cymbals of sound" (שֵׁמַע "hearing, what is heard": loud cymbals); צִלְצְלֵי תְרוּעָה "cymbals of shouting" (תְּרוּעָה: on a trumpet or shofar "a blast", but cymbals are struck, not blown: the shout of the festival, or a loud clash, may be meant; the two kinds of cymbal cannot be told apart with certainty). צֶלְצְלִים "cymbals" as 2 Sam 6:5 (1 Chr has מְצִלְתַּיִם, the same English).
- **"Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD" → "Let all breath praise Yah"** (v6) — Hebrew כֹּל הַנְּשָׁמָה, lit. "all the breath" (נְשָׁמָה "breath": all that breathes); תְּהַלֵּל, imperfect, here a wish ("let"; also "shall praise"); the object is יָהּ "Yah".

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew; our verse numbers are the Hebrew ones.
- **Closing doxology** of the Psalter: where (v1), why (v2), with what (v3–5), who (v6).
