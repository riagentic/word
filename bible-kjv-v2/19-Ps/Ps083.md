# Psalm 83

A Song. A Psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not keep silence;
do not hold your peace or be still, O ⟦el|El|God⟧!

[2] For behold, your enemies make an uproar;
those who hate you have raised their heads.

[3] They lay crafty plans against your people;
they consult together against your ⟦tzefunim|tzefunim — hidden ones|treasured ones⟧.

[4] They say, "Come, let us wipe them out as a nation;
let the name of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ be remembered no more!"

[5] For they conspire with one accord;
against you they make a covenant —

[6] the tents of Edom and the ⟦ishmaelites|Yishmeelim|Ishmaelites⟧,
⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and the ⟦hagrites|Hagrim|Hagrites⟧,

[7] ⟦gebal|Geval|Gebal⟧ and Ammon and ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧,
⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧ with the inhabitants of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧;

[8] ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ also has joined them;
they are the strong arm of the children of Lot. Selah.

[9] Do to them as you did to ⟦midian|Midyan|Midian⟧,
as to Sisera and ⟦jabin|Yavin|Jabin⟧ at the river ⟦kishon|Qishon|Kishon⟧,

[10] who were destroyed at ⟦en-dor|Ein Dor|En-dor⟧,
who became dung for the ground.

[11] Make their nobles like ⟦oreb|Orev|Oreb⟧ and ⟦zeeb|Zeev|Zeeb⟧,
all their princes like ⟦zebah|Zevach|Zebah⟧ and ⟦zalmunna|Tzalmunna|Zalmunna⟧,

[12] who said, "Let us take possession for ourselves
of the pastures of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[13] O my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, make them like a whirling dust,
like chaff before the wind.

[14] As fire consumes the forest,
as the flame sets the mountains ablaze,

[15] so may you pursue them with your tempest
and terrify them with your hurricane!

[16] Fill their faces with shame,
that they may seek your name, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[17] Let them be put to shame and dismayed forever;
let them perish in disgrace,

[18] that they may know that you alone,
whose name is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
are ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ over all the earth.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 12, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"El"** (v1) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v16, 18) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — enemies' plot (v1-8), appeal to historical precedents of victory (v9-12), imprecatory prayer (v13-18).

### Notes
- **Last Asaph psalm.**
- **Ten-nation coalition** — composite of foes over history.
