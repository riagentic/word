# Psalm 127

A song of the ascents. Of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[1] If ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ does not build a house,
in vain have its builders toiled on it;
if ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ does not keep a city,
in vain has the watchman kept awake.

[2] In vain it is for you who rise up early,
who sit down late,
eaters of the bread of pains;
so he gives to his beloved sleep.

[3] Behold, the inheritance of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is sons,
a reward is the fruit of the belly.

[4] Like arrows in the hand of a mighty one,
so are the sons of youth.

[5] Happy is the man who has filled his quiver with them!
They shall not be ashamed
when they speak with enemies in the gate.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees for Solomon" → "A song of the ascents. Of Solomon"** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת לִשְׁלֹמֹה, the first words of v1; מַעֲלָה "a going up, a step", with the article. לְ may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" Solomon; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Except the LORD build the house, they labour in vain that build it" → "If Yahweh does not build a house, in vain have its builders toiled on it"** (v1) — Hebrew אִם־יְהוָה לֹא־יִבְנֶה בַיִת: "a house", "a city" (no article); שָׁוְא "in vain" (literally "emptiness"), first in each line as in the Hebrew; עָמְלוּ, perfect of עָמַל "toil"; בוֹנָיו "its builders"; בּוֹ "on it". **"except the LORD keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain" → "if Yahweh does not keep a city, in vain has the watchman kept awake"** — Hebrew שָׁמַר "keep" and שׁוֹמֵר "watchman" (literally "keeper", the same root); שָׁקַד "keep awake, be watchful", a perfect.
- **"It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows" → "In vain it is for you who rise up early, who sit down late, eaters of the bread of pains"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁוְא לָכֶם, then three participles: מַשְׁכִּימֵי קוּם, literally "who make early to rise" (הִשְׁכִּים "rise early"); מְאַחֲרֵי־שֶׁבֶת "who delay sitting", that is, who sit down to rest late; אֹכְלֵי לֶחֶם הָעֲצָבִים "eaters of the bread of the pains" (עֶצֶב "pain", toil that hurts, as Gen 3:16, 17; not "sorrow"). **"for so he giveth his beloved sleep" → "so he gives to his beloved sleep"** — Hebrew כֵּן "so" (no "for"); לִידִידוֹ "to his beloved" (יָדִיד, the root of Solomon's name Jedidiah, 2 Sam 12:25). שֵׁנָא "sleep" is printed with א for ה (שֵׁנָה); it may also be taken as "in sleep": he gives to his beloved while he sleeps.
- **"Lo, children are an heritage of the LORD: and the fruit of the womb is his reward" → "Behold, the inheritance of Yahweh is sons, a reward is the fruit of the belly"** (v3) — Hebrew הִנֵּה "behold"; נַחֲלַת יְהוָה בָּנִים, "the inheritance of Yahweh" first, as in the Hebrew; נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; בָּנִים "sons"; שָׂכָר "reward" (no "his"); בֶּטֶן "belly" (רֶחֶם is "womb").
- **"As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man; so are children of the youth" → "Like arrows in the hand of a mighty one, so are the sons of youth"** (v4) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; בְּנֵי הַנְּעוּרִים "the sons of the youth", sons born while the father is young.
- **"Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them" → "Happy is the man who has filled his quiver with them"** (v5) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "happy" ("blessed" is בָּרוּךְ); הַגֶּבֶר "the man" (גֶּבֶר, which elsewhere is "mighty man", stands here alone); מִלֵּא "has filled", a perfect. **"they shall not be ashamed, but they shall speak with the enemies in the gate" → "They shall not be ashamed when they speak with enemies in the gate"** — Hebrew לֹא־יֵבֹשׁוּ, plural (the man with his sons); כִּי "when" or "for", not "but"; דִּבֶּר אֶת "speak with"; אוֹיְבִים "enemies", no article; the gate is where cases were heard.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew too; the numbers agree.
- **Poetic structure** — two sayings: building and keeping (v1–2), sons as Yahweh's inheritance (v3–5).
