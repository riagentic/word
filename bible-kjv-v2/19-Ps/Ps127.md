# Psalm 127

A Song of Ascents. Of Solomon.

[1] Unless Yahweh builds the house,
those who build it labor in vain.
Unless Yahweh watches over the city,
the watchman stays awake in vain.

[2] It is in vain that you rise up early
and go late to rest,
eating the bread of anxious toil;
for he gives to his beloved sleep.

[3] Behold, children are a heritage from Yahweh,
the fruit of the womb a reward.

[4] Like arrows in the hand of a warrior
are the children of one's youth.

[5] Blessed is the man who fills his quiver with them!
He shall not be put to shame
when he speaks with his enemies in the gate.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — two wisdom maxims: building/watching (v1-2) and children as heritage (v3-5).
