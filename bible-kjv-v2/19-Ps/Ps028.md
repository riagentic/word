# Psalm 28

Of David.

[1] To you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I call;
my Rock, do not be deaf to me,
lest you be silent to me,
and I become like those who go down to the pit.

[2] Hear the voice of my pleas for favor,
when I cry to you for help,
when I lift up my hands
toward your holy inner room.

[3] Do not drag me away with the wicked,
and with the workers of wickedness,
who speak ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with their friends,
and evil is in their heart.

[4] Give to them according to their work,
and according to the evil of their deeds;
according to the work of their hands give to them;
return to them their dealing.

[5] For they do not understand the works of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and the work of his hands;
he will tear them down and will not build them up.

[6] Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for he has heard the voice of my pleas for favor.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is my strength and my shield;
in him my heart trusted,
and I was helped, and my heart was jubilant,
and from my song I will give thanks to him.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is strength to them,
and the strong place of the salvations of his ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧ is he.

[9] Save your people, and bless your inheritance,
and shepherd them, and carry them forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 6, 7, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Heading "A Psalm of David" → "Of David"** — the heading is the first word of the Hebrew v1, לְדָוִד; לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and "Of David" leaves it open.
- **"O LORD my rock; be not silent to me: lest, if thou be silent to me, I become like them" → "my Rock, do not be deaf to me, lest you be silent to me, and I become like those"** (v1) — Hebrew אַל־תֶּחֱרַשׁ "do not be deaf / mute" and תֶּחֱשֶׁה "be still" are two verbs; מִמֶּנִּי "from me" both times; וְנִמְשַׁלְתִּי "and I be made like". "Rock" (צוּר) has a capital where it names Yahweh. בּוֹר "pit".
- **"supplications" → "pleas for favor"** (v2, 6) — Hebrew תַּחֲנוּנִים, of the root חָנַן "show favor". **"cry" → "cry for help"** — שִׁוַּע. **"thy holy oracle" → "your holy inner room"** — דְּבִיר קָדְשֶׁךָ, the inner room of the house (1 Kgs 6), literally "the inner room of your holiness".
- **"Draw me not away … which speak peace to their neighbours, but mischief is in their hearts" → "Do not drag me away … who speak shalom with their friends, and evil is in their heart"** (v3) — Hebrew מָשַׁךְ "draw, drag"; פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness"; עִם "with"; רֵעַ "friend, companion"; וְרָעָה "and evil"; בִּלְבָבָם singular "their heart".
- **"their deeds … their endeavours … the work of their hands; render to them their desert" → "their work … their deeds … the work of their hands … return to them their dealing"** (v4) — Hebrew פֹּעַל "work" (verb פָּעַל "work, do"), רֹעַ מַעַלְלֵיהֶם "the evil of their deeds", מַעֲשֵׂה יְדֵיהֶם "the work of their hands" (another word for "work"); the third line keeps the Hebrew order. הָשֵׁב גְּמוּלָם "return their dealing", from גָּמַל "deal with" (as 2 Sam 22:21).
- **"they regard not the works of the LORD, nor the operation of his hands, he shall destroy them" → "they do not understand the works … and the work of his hands; he will tear them down"** (v5) — Hebrew לֹא יָבִינוּ אֶל, from בִּין "understand" (lit. "understand toward"); פְּעֻלֹּת "works" is the root of פֹּעַל in v4, מַעֲשֵׂה the word of v4 line 3. וְאֶל "and", not "nor". הָרַס "tear down"; "no more" (in the earlier wording) is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Blessed be the LORD, because" → "Blessed is Yahweh, for"** (v6) — no verb in the Hebrew (בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה): "is", as in Deut 28:3.
- **"my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him" → "in him my heart trusted, and I was helped, and my heart was jubilant, and from my song I will give thanks to him"** (v7) — Hebrew perfect בָּטַח, perfect וְנֶעֱזָרְתִּי, narrative וַיַּעֲלֹז "and was jubilant" (עָלַז), imperfect אֲהוֹדֶנּוּ "I will give thanks to him" (הוֹדָה). "Therefore" and "greatly" are not written; מִשִּׁירִי "from my song". The lines follow the Hebrew accents (the main break after the second לִבִּי).
- **"their strength … the saving strength of his anointed" → "strength to them … the strong place of the salvations of his anointed is he"** (v8) — Hebrew עֹז־לָמוֹ "strength to them" (the earlier wording "of his people" follows the Greek and Syriac, which read לְעַמּוֹ, from memory; the Masoretic Text is kept); מָעוֹז "strong place" (beside עֹז "strength"); יְשׁוּעוֹת plural "salvations"; מְשִׁיחוֹ "his anointed" with its token.
- **"feed them also, and lift them up for ever" → "and shepherd them, and carry them forever"** (v9) — Hebrew וּרְעֵם "and shepherd them" (רָעָה); וְנַשְּׂאֵם piel of נָשָׂא "lift up, carry"; עַד־הָעוֹלָם "until the age" = forever. נַחֲלָה "inheritance".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — the heading לְדָוִד is part of the Hebrew v1; the verse numbers are the same in Hebrew and English.
- **Plea + thanksgiving** — the plea (v1–5) turns to thanks for an answered prayer (v6–9); v6 takes up "the voice of my pleas for favor" of v2.
- **"Shepherd them"** (v9) — as Ps 23.
