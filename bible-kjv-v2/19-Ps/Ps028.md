# Psalm 28

Of David.

[1] To you, O Yahweh, I call;
my rock, be not deaf to me,
lest, if you be silent to me,
I become like those who go down to the pit.

[2] Hear the voice of my pleas for mercy,
when I cry to you for help,
when I lift up my hands
toward your most holy sanctuary.

[3] Do not drag me off with the wicked,
with the workers of evil,
who speak peace with their neighbors
while evil is in their hearts.

[4] Give them according to their work
and according to the evil of their deeds;
give them according to the work of their hands;
render them their due reward.

[5] Because they do not regard the works of Yahweh
or the work of his hands,
he will tear them down and build them up no more.

[6] Blessed be Yahweh!
For he has heard the voice of my pleas for mercy.

[7] Yahweh is my strength and my shield;
in him my heart trusts, and I am helped;
my heart exults,
and with my song I give thanks to him.

[8] Yahweh is the strength of his people;
he is the saving stronghold of his anointed.

[9] O save your people and bless your heritage!
Be their shepherd and carry them forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — lament (v1-5) pivots to thanksgiving at v6; v8-9 broaden to community intercession.

### Notes
- **Plea + thanksgiving** — transition mid-psalm from lament (v1-5) to answered prayer (v6-9).
- **"Shepherd them"** — echoes Ps 23.
