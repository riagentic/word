# Psalm 96

[1] Oh sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a new song;
sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all the earth!

[2] Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, bless his name;
tell of his salvation from day to day.

[3] Declare his glory among the nations,
his marvelous works among all the peoples!

[4] For great is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and greatly to be praised;
he is to be feared above all gods.

[5] For all the gods of the peoples are worthless idols,
but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made the heavens.

[6] Splendor and majesty are before him;
strength and beauty are in his sanctuary.

[7] Ascribe to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, O families of the peoples,
ascribe to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ glory and strength!

[8] Ascribe to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the glory due his name;
bring an offering, and come into his courts!

[9] Worship ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the splendor of holiness;
tremble before him, all the earth!

[10] Say among the nations, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ reigns!
Yes, the world is established; it shall never be moved;
he will judge the peoples with equity."

[11] Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice;
let the sea roar, and all that fills it;

[12] let the field exult, and everything in it!
Then shall all the trees of the forest sing for joy

[13] before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he comes,
for he comes to judge the earth.
He will judge the world in righteousness,
and the peoples in his faithfulness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"gods"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew *elohim*; here the pagan gods, rendered "gods" in lowercase.
- **Poetic structure** — new-song call (v1-3), Yahweh's supremacy over the gods (v4-6), summons of nations to worship (v7-10), creation's joy at coming judgment (v11-13).

### Notes
- **Parallels 1 Chr 16** — enthronement poetry.
