# Psalm 96

[1] Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a new song;
sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all the earth.

[2] Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, bless his name;
bring the news of his salvation from day to day.

[3] Recount among the nations his glory,
among all the peoples his wonderful deeds.

[4] For great is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and greatly praised;
fearsome is he above all gods.

[5] For all the gods of the peoples are worthless idols,
but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made the heavens.

[6] Majesty and splendor are before him;
strength and beauty are in his sanctuary.

[7] Give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, clans of the peoples,
give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ glory and strength.

[8] Give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the glory of his name;
lift up an offering and come to his courts.

[9] Bow down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the splendor of holiness;
be in anguish before him, all the earth.

[10] Say among the nations, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ reigns;
yes, the world is established, it shall not be shaken;
he will judge peoples in uprightness."

[11] Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad;
let the sea thunder, and its fullness;

[12] let the field be jubilant, and all that is in it.
Then all the trees of the forest shall shout for joy

[13] before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he comes,
for he comes to judge the earth.
He will judge the world in righteousness,
and peoples in his faithfulness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"gods"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew *elohim*; here other gods, plain text.
- **The wording of 1 Chr 16:23–33** — v1b–13a stand also in 1 Chr 16:23–33 and keep its wording where the Hebrew is the same; the differences are named below, each as this psalm has it.
- **"O sing unto the LORD a new song: sing unto the LORD, all the earth" → "Sing to Yahweh a new song; sing to Yahweh, all the earth"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁירוּ לַיהוָה שִׁיר חָדָשׁ; "O" is not written. 1 Chr 16:23 has only "Sing to Yahweh, all the earth".
- **"bless his name; shew forth his salvation from day to day" → "bless his name; bring the news of his salvation from day to day"** (v2) — the line "Sing to Yahweh, bless his name" is not in 1 Chr 16. בַּשְּׂרוּ "bring the news of" (as 1 Chr 16:23); מִיּוֹם־לְיוֹם "from day to day" (1 Chr מִיּוֹם־אֶל־יוֹם: another preposition, the same English).
- **"Declare his glory among the heathen, his wonders among all people" → "Recount among the nations his glory, among all the peoples his wonderful deeds"** (v3) — Hebrew סַפְּרוּ "recount"; נִפְלְאוֹתָיו "his wonderful deeds", as 1 Chr 16:24.
- **"greatly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods" → "greatly praised; fearsome is he above all gods"** (v4) — Hebrew מְהֻלָּל, a passive participle; נוֹרָא "fearsome". 1 Chr 16:25 has וְנוֹרָא "and fearsome"; here there is no "and".
- **"idols" → "worthless idols"** (v5) — Hebrew אֱלִילִים; the word says "worthless" and sounds like אֵל.
- **"Honour and majesty are before him: strength and beauty are in his sanctuary" → "Majesty and splendor are before him; strength and beauty are in his sanctuary"** (v6) — Hebrew הוֹד וְהָדָר "majesty and splendor"; עֹז "strength"; תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" and בְּמִקְדָּשׁוֹ "in his sanctuary", where 1 Chr 16:27 has חֶדְוָה "joy" and בִּמְקֹמוֹ "in his place".
- **"Give unto the LORD, O ye kindreds of the people" → "Give to Yahweh, clans of the peoples"** (v7) — Hebrew הָבוּ "give" (the KJV "give" kept, not the later "ascribe"); מִשְׁפְּחוֹת עַמִּים "clans of the peoples", as 1 Chr 16:28.
- **"the glory due unto his name: bring an offering, and come into his courts" → "the glory of his name; lift up an offering and come to his courts"** (v8) — Hebrew כְּבוֹד שְׁמוֹ, "due" is not written; שְׂאוּ מִנְחָה "lift up an offering"; וּבֹאוּ לְחַצְרוֹתָיו "and come to his courts", where 1 Chr 16:29 has לְפָנָיו "before him".
- **"O worship the LORD in the beauty of holiness: fear before him, all the earth" → "Bow down to Yahweh in the splendor of holiness; be in anguish before him, all the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲווּ "bow down"; הַדְרַת־קֹדֶשׁ "the splendor of holiness": the sense is not certain ("in holy adornment / attire" is the other way of taking it). In 1 Chr 16:29 this line closes the verse of the offering. חִילוּ "be in anguish", lit. "writhe" (as one in labor); commonly "tremble". "before him" is מִפָּנָיו, lit. "from his face"; 1 Chr 16:30 has מִלְּפָנָיו "from before him".
- **"Say among the heathen that the LORD reigneth: the world also shall be established that it shall not be moved: he shall judge the people righteously" → "Say among the nations, 'Yahweh reigns; yes, the world is established, it shall not be shaken; he will judge peoples in uprightness'"** (v10) — Hebrew אִמְרוּ "say", a command; 1 Chr 16:31 has וְיֹאמְרוּ "and let them say", after the line of the heavens. מָלָךְ, a perfect: also "has become king". אַף "yes"; תִּכּוֹן "is established"; בַּל־תִּמּוֹט "it shall not be shaken" (as 1 Chr 16:30). The third line is not in 1 Chr 16: יָדִין "he will judge" (דִּין, beside שָׁפַט "judge" in v13: English has one word); עַמִּים "peoples", no article (also v13); מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness". Where the quotation ends is not marked in the Hebrew.
- **"Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof" → "Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; let the sea thunder, and its fullness"** (v11) — Hebrew שָׂמַח "rejoice", גִּיל "be glad"; יִרְעַם "thunder"; מְלֹאוֹ "its fullness" (what fills it). In 1 Chr 16:31–32 the two lines stand in two verses.
- **"Let the field be joyful … then shall all the trees of the wood rejoice" → "let the field be jubilant, and all that is in it. Then all the trees of the forest shall shout for joy"** (v12) — Hebrew יַעֲלֹז "be jubilant" (עָלַז; 1 Chr 16:32 has יַעֲלֹץ, a by-form, the same English); שָׂדַי, a poetic form without the article (1 Chr הַשָּׂדֶה); יְרַנְּנוּ "shout for joy"; כָּל־עֲצֵי־יָעַר "all the trees of the forest", where 1 Chr 16:33 has עֲצֵי הַיָּעַר, without "all".
- **"Before the LORD: for he cometh, for he cometh to judge the earth: he shall judge the world with righteousness, and the people with his truth" → "before Yahweh, for he comes, for he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness, and peoples in his faithfulness"** (v13) — Hebrew לִפְנֵי "before" (1 Chr 16:33 מִלִּפְנֵי "from before"); כִּי בָא "for he comes" (or "he has come"), twice here, once in 1 Chr. The last two lines are not in 1 Chr 16: יִשְׁפֹּט "he will judge"; תֵּבֵל "world"; בֶּאֱמוּנָתוֹ "in his faithfulness".

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew.
- **Parallel: 1 Chr 16:23–33** — the song of David's day; the differences are named in Changes and kept as each book has them.
- **Structure** — new-song call (v1–3), Yahweh above the gods (v4–6), the clans of the peoples called (v7–10), the joy of heaven and earth at his coming (v11–13).
