# Psalm 148

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the heavens;
praise him in the heights!

[2] Praise him, all his angels;
praise him, all his hosts!

[3] Praise him, sun and moon;
praise him, all stars of light!

[4] Praise him, heavens of the heavens,
and the waters that are above the heavens!

[5] Let them praise the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for he commanded and they were created.

[6] And he made them stand perpetually, forever;
a statute he gave, and it shall not pass away.

[7] Praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the earth,
sea monsters and all deeps,

[8] fire and hail, snow and smoke,
storm wind doing his word,

[9] the mountains and all hills,
fruit tree and all cedars,

[10] the living things and every beast,
creeping thing and winged bird,

[11] kings of the earth and all folks,
princes and all judges of the earth,

[12] young men and also virgins,
old men with boys!

[13] Let them praise the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for his name alone is set on high;
his majesty is over earth and heavens.

[14] And he raised a horn for his people,
praise for all his faithful ones,
for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, a people near to him.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 7, 13) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v1, 14) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ, "praise Yah", with its token (in v1 written as two words, הַלְלוּ יָהּ).
- **"Praise ye him, all his angels: praise ye him, all his hosts" → "Praise him, all his angels; praise him, all his hosts"** (v2) — Hebrew מַלְאָכָיו "his angels" (the word is "messengers"); the qere צְבָאָיו "his hosts" is followed; the written text (ketiv) צבאו is "his host".
- **"praise him, all ye stars of light" → "praise him, all stars of light"** (v3) — Hebrew כָּל־כּוֹכְבֵי אוֹר, word for word.
- **"Praise him, ye heavens of heavens, and ye waters that be above the heavens" → "Praise him, heavens of the heavens, and the waters that are above the heavens"** (v4) — Hebrew שְׁמֵי הַשָּׁמָיִם "the heavens of the heavens" (as Deut 10:14); וְהַמַּיִם אֲשֶׁר מֵעַל הַשָּׁמָיִם, with the article.
- **"He hath also stablished them for ever and ever: he hath made a decree which shall not pass" → "And he made them stand perpetually, forever; a statute he gave, and it shall not pass away"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמִידֵם "and he made them stand" (הֶעֱמִיד); לָעַד לְעוֹלָם "perpetually, forever" (two words, kept apart; lit. "to perpetuity, to the age"); חָק־נָתַן "a statute he gave" (חֹק "statute"); וְלֹא יַעֲבוֹר "and it shall not pass away": the subject is not named — the statute does not pass away, or "and none passes over it" (it is not transgressed); the verb is singular.
- **"ye dragons, and all deeps" → "sea monsters and all deeps"** (v7) — Hebrew תַּנִּינִים "sea monsters" (great creatures of the waters; the exact sense is uncertain); תְּהֹמוֹת "deeps"; "ye" is not written.
- **"Fire, and hail; snow, and vapour; stormy wind fulfilling his word" → "fire and hail, snow and smoke, storm wind doing his word"** (v8) — Hebrew קִיטוֹר "smoke" (as Gen 19:28; here perhaps mist or cloud: the sense in the weather is not certain); רוּחַ סְעָרָה "storm wind" (lit. "wind of storm"; as Ps 107:25); עֹשָׂה דְבָרוֹ "doing his word" (עָשָׂה "do, make").
- **"Mountains, and all hills; fruitful trees, and all cedars" → "the mountains and all hills, fruit tree and all cedars"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶהָרִים, with the article; גְּבָעוֹת "hills"; עֵץ פְּרִי "fruit tree", singular (lit. "tree of fruit").
- **"Beasts, and all cattle; creeping things, and flying fowl" → "the living things and every beast, creeping thing and winged bird"** (v10) — Hebrew הַחַיָּה "the living thing" (a collective; the word says "living", not "wild"); כָּל־בְּהֵמָה "every beast" (four-footed animals); רֶמֶשׂ "creeping thing"; צִפּוֹר כָּנָף "bird of wing", "winged bird".
- **"and all people" → "and all folks"** (v11) — Hebrew לְאֻמִּים "folks" (beside עַם "people"); שָׂרִים "princes"; שֹׁפְטֵי אָרֶץ "judges of the earth".
- **"Both young men, and maidens; old men, and children" → "young men and also virgins, old men with boys"** (v12) — Hebrew בַּחוּרִים וְגַם־בְּתוּלוֹת "young men and also virgins" (בְּתוּלָה "virgin"); זְקֵנִים עִם־נְעָרִים "old men with boys" (עִם "with", not "and").
- **"for his name alone is excellent; his glory is above the earth and heaven" → "for his name alone is set on high; his majesty is over earth and heavens"** (v13) — Hebrew נִשְׂגָּב "is set on high" (nifal of the שִׂגֵּב "set on high" row); הוֹדוֹ "his majesty" (not "glory": that is כָּבוֹד); עַל "over"; אֶרֶץ וְשָׁמָיִם without articles, "heavens" plural.
- **"He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him" → "And he raised a horn for his people, praise for all his faithful ones, for the sons of Israel, a people near to him"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיָּרֶם, a wayyiqtol, "and he raised" (past); קֶרֶן לְעַמּוֹ "a horn for his people" (no article; a horn = strength); תְּהִלָּה לְכָל־חֲסִידָיו "praise for all his faithful ones": "praise" may stand beside "horn" (what he raised) or begin a new clause, "praise (belongs) to all his faithful ones" / "he is the praise of …" — the Hebrew leaves it open; חֲסִידִים "faithful ones" (from חֶסֶד); לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "for the sons of Israel"; עַם־קְרֹבוֹ "a people near to him" (lit. "a people of his nearness").

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew; our verse numbers are the Hebrew ones.
- **Structure** — praise from the heavens (v1–6), praise from the earth (v7–12), the reason: his name and the horn raised for Israel (v13–14).
