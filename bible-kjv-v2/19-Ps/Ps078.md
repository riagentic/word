# Psalm 78

A Maskil. Of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] Give ear, my people, to ⟦torah|my torah|my teaching⟧;
incline your ears to the sayings of my mouth.

[2] Let me open my mouth in a parable;
let me pour forth riddles from of old,

[3] which we have heard and have known,
and our fathers have recounted to us.

[4] We will not hide them from their sons,
recounting to a later generation the praises of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and his might and his wonderful deeds that he has done.

[5] And he established a testimony in ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and a ⟦torah|torah|law⟧ he set in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
which he commanded our fathers,
to make them known to their sons,

[6] so that a later generation might know,
sons who would be born;
they would rise and recount to their sons,

[7] and they would set their confidence in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
and not forget the doings of ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and his commandments they would guard;

[8] and they would not be like their fathers,
a generation stubborn and rebellious,
a generation that did not establish its heart,
and whose spirit was not faithful with ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[9] The sons of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, armed, shooters of the bow,
turned back in the day of battle.

[10] They did not keep the covenant of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and in his ⟦torah|torah|law⟧ they refused to walk.

[11] And they forgot his doings
and his wonderful deeds that he had shown them.

[12] Before their fathers he did a wonder
in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, the field of ⟦zoan|Tzoan|Zoan⟧.

[13] He split the sea and made them pass over,
and he set up the waters like a heap.

[14] And he led them with the cloud by day,
and all the night with a light of fire.

[15] He split rocks in the wilderness
and gave drink like the deeps, abundantly.

[16] And he brought out streams from the crag
and brought down waters like rivers.

[17] And they went on still to sin against him,
rebelling against ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ in the parched land.

[18] And they tested ⟦el|El|God⟧ in their heart
by asking food for their soul.

[19] And they spoke against ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; they said,
"Is ⟦el|El|God⟧ able to arrange a table in the wilderness?

[20] Behold, he struck the rock, and waters flowed
and torrents overflowed.
Is he able also to give bread,
or will he provide meat for his people?"

[21] Therefore ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ heard and was enraged,
and a fire was kindled against ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and anger also went up against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[22] for they did not believe in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
and did not trust in his salvation.

[23] And he commanded the skies above
and opened the doors of the heavens,

[24] and he caused manna to rain upon them to eat,
and the grain of the heavens he gave to them.

[25] Man ate the bread of the mighty;
provision he sent to them to the full.

[26] He made the east wind set out in the heavens,
and he drove by his strength the south wind.

[27] And he caused meat to rain upon them like dust,
and like the sand of the seas winged birds.

[28] And he made them fall in the midst of his camp,
all around his dwellings.

[29] And they ate and were greatly sated,
and their craving he brought to them.

[30] They were not estranged from their craving;
their food was still in their mouths,

[31] and the anger of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ went up against them,
and he killed among their fat ones
and made the young men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ bow down.

[32] In all this they sinned still,
and they did not believe in his wonderful deeds.

[33] And he finished their days in vapor,
and their years in dismay.

[34] When he killed them, they sought him,
and they returned and sought ⟦el|El|God⟧ eagerly.

[35] And they remembered that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was their Rock,
and ⟦el-elyon|El Elyon|God Most High⟧ their redeemer.

[36] And they enticed him with their mouth,
and with their tongue they lied to him.

[37] And their heart was not established with him,
and they were not faithful in his covenant.

[38] But he, compassionate,
would make atonement for iniquity
and would not ruin;
and many times he turned back his anger
and would not stir up all his fury.

[39] And he remembered that they were flesh,
a wind that goes and does not return.

[40] How often they rebelled against him in the wilderness,
grieved him in the desert!

[41] And they turned back and tested ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and the Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ they pained.

[42] They did not remember his hand,
the day that he ransomed them from the adversary,

[43] when he set his signs in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧
and his wonders in the field of ⟦zoan|Tzoan|Zoan⟧.

[44] And he turned their ⟦nile|Yeorim|Niles⟧ into blood,
and their streams they could not drink.

[45] He sent among them a swarm, and it ate them,
and frogs, and they ruined them.

[46] And he gave their produce to the devourer
and their labor to the locusts.

[47] He killed their vine with hail
and their sycamores with frost.

[48] And he handed over their animals to the hail
and their livestock to the flames.

[49] He sent against them the burning of his anger,
raging and indignation and distress,
a sending of angels of evils.

[50] He leveled a path for his anger;
he did not withhold their soul from death,
and their life he handed over to the pestilence.

[51] And he struck every firstborn in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
the beginning of vigor in the tents of ⟦ham|Cham|Ham⟧.

[52] And he made his people set out like the flock
and led them like a drove in the wilderness.

[53] And he led them in security, and they were not in dread,
and their enemies the sea covered.

[54] And he brought them to his holy territory,
this mountain that his right hand got.

[55] And he drove out nations from before them,
and made them fall by the line as an inheritance,
and made the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ abide in their tents.

[56] And they tested and rebelled against ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧,
and his testimonies they did not keep.

[57] And they turned back and dealt treacherously like their fathers;
they were turned like a bow of treachery.

[58] And they vexed him with their high places,
and with their carved images they made him jealous.

[59] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ heard and was enraged,
and he rejected ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ greatly.

[60] And he abandoned the dwelling of Shiloh,
the tent that he had made abide among mankind.

[61] And he gave his strength to captivity,
and his beauty into the hand of the adversary.

[62] And he handed over his people to the sword,
and with his inheritance he was enraged.

[63] His young men fire consumed,
and his virgins were not praised.

[64] His priests fell by the sword,
and his widows did not weep.

[65] And ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ awoke like one asleep,
like a mighty one shouting because of wine.

[66] And he struck his adversaries backward;
an everlasting reproach he gave to them.

[67] And he rejected the tent of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧,
and the tribe of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ he did not choose,

[68] and he chose the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, which he loved.

[69] And he built his sanctuary like the heights,
like the earth that he founded forever.

[70] And he chose David his servant
and took him from the folds of the flock.

[71] From after the nursing ones he brought him
to shepherd ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ his people
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his inheritance.

[72] And he shepherded them according to the integrity of his heart,
and with the understanding of his palms he led them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 21) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*" / "El"** (throughout) — this is an Elohistic psalm: אֱלֹהִים (v7, 10, 19, 22, 31, 35, 56, 59) and אֵל (v7, 8, 18, 19, 34, 41) are kept apart, each as printed; יהוה stands only in v4, 21, אֲדֹנָי in v65.
- **"Maschil of Asaph" → "A Maskil. Of Asaph."** (heading) — Hebrew מַשְׂכִּיל לְאָסָף, in this order. מַשְׂכִּיל, a hifil participle of שָׂכַל ("give insight"), is kept as the heading term; its meaning is uncertain ("a psalm of insight"). לְאָסָף: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" Asaph; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Give ear, O my people, to my law" → "Give ear, my people, to my torah"** (v1) — Hebrew הַאֲזִינָה עַמִּי תּוֹרָתִי; no "O" (the Hebrew has no word for it). תּוֹרָה "instruction" (the teacher's own here; the *torah* of Sinai in v5, 10, the same word). אִמְרֵי־פִי "the sayings of my mouth" (אֵמֶר, not דָּבָר "word").
- **"I will open my mouth in a parable: I will utter dark sayings of old" → "Let me open my mouth in a parable; let me pour forth riddles from of old"** (v2) — Hebrew אֶפְתְּחָה … אַבִּיעָה, two cohortatives ("let me", a resolve). מָשָׁל "parable" (a saying built on a likeness; the whole psalm is meant, so not "proverb"); חִידוֹת "riddles"; הִבִּיעַ "pour forth, bubble out" (of a spring); מִנִּי־קֶדֶם "from of old" (קֶדֶם "front, east, ancient time"). Matt 13:35 cites the verse from the Greek, "I will open my mouth in parables (plural); I will utter things hidden from the foundation (of the world)"; the Hebrew is not levelled to it.
- **"Which we have heard and known, and our fathers have told us" → "which we have heard and have known, and our fathers have recounted to us"** (v3) — Hebrew וַנֵּדָעֵם "and we have known them"; סִפֵּר "recount" (also v4, 6).
- **"We will not hide them from their children, shewing to the generation to come the praises of the LORD, and his strength" → "We will not hide them from their sons, recounting to a later generation the praises of Yahweh, and his might"** (v4) — Hebrew בָּנִים "sons" (also v5, 6); דּוֹר אַחֲרוֹן "a later generation" (also v6); "them" is supplied (נְכַחֵד has no object). The lines follow the accents (the main break after "Yahweh"). נִפְלְאוֹת "wonderful deeds" (also v11, 32), עֱזוּז "might".
- **"For he established a testimony … that they should make them known to their children" → "And he established a testimony … to make them known to their sons"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיָּקֶם "and he raised up, established" (no "for"); תּוֹרָה "a torah" stands first in its line: "and a torah he set"; לְהוֹדִיעָם "to make them known".
- **"even the children which should be born; who should arise and declare them" → "sons who would be born; they would rise and recount to their sons"** (v6) — Hebrew יָקֻמוּ וִיסַפְּרוּ, a new clause without "who". לְמַעַן "so that". יֵדְעוּ has no object ("them" not supplied).
- **"That they might set their hope in God" → "and they would set their confidence in Elohim"** (v7) — Hebrew וְיָשִׂימוּ בֵאלֹהִים כִּסְלָם, a chain of "and" clauses; כֶּסֶל "confidence" (Job 8:14; Prov 3:26; not תִּקְוָה "hope"). מַעַלְלֵי־אֵל "the doings of El" (מַעֲלָל, from עָלַל; v11 עֲלִילוֹת, the same root, "doings"). נָצַר "guard".
- **"a generation that set not their heart aright, and whose spirit was not stedfast with God" → "a generation that did not establish its heart, and whose spirit was not faithful with El"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא־הֵכִין לִבּוֹ (the הֵכִין לֵב of 1 Sam 7:3, "establish the heart"; v37 נָכוֹן, the same root, "established"); נֶאֶמְנָה אֶת־אֵל "was faithful with El" (אֶת "with"). סוֹרֵר וּמוֹרֶה "stubborn and rebellious" (as Deut 21:18, 20).
- **"The children of Ephraim, being armed, and carrying bows" → "The sons of Ephraim, armed, shooters of the bow"** (v9) — Hebrew נוֹשְׁקֵי רוֹמֵי־קָשֶׁת, two participles side by side: "armed ones" (נָשַׁק "be armed", 1 Chr 12:2) and "throwers / shooters of the bow" (רָמָה). Many take the pair as a doubled reading; the MT is kept. הָפְכוּ "turned (back)"; קְרָב "battle" (a poetic word beside מִלְחָמָה). What failure of Ephraim is meant is not said.
- **"refused to walk in his law" → "in his torah they refused to walk"** (v10) — Hebrew order.
- **"his works, and his wonders" → "his doings and his wonderful deeds"** (v11) — Hebrew עֲלִילוֹתָיו, נִפְלְאוֹתָיו.
- **"Marvellous things did he … in the field of Zoan" → "he did a wonder … the field of Zoan"** (v12) — Hebrew עָשָׂה פֶלֶא, a singular noun (as Exod 15:11 "doing wonders", where English has the plural); v43 מוֹפֵת "wonders" is another word with the same English. שְׂדֵה "field" (singular; also v43).
- **"caused them to pass through; and he made the waters to stand as an heap" → "made them pass over, and he set up the waters like a heap"** (v13) — Hebrew הֶעֱבִיר "make pass over"; וַיַּצֶּב, hifil of נצב, "set up" (Exod 15:8 נִצְּבוּ כְמוֹ־נֵד "stood like a heap"). בָּקַע "split" (also v15).
- **"In the daytime also he led them with a cloud" → "And he led them with the cloud by day"** (v14) — Hebrew בֶּעָנָן, with the article (the cloud of Exod 13:21). נָחָה "lead" (also v53, 72).
- **"gave them drink as out of the great depths" → "gave drink like the deeps, abundantly"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁקְ כִּתְהֹמוֹת רַבָּה: the verb has no object; כְּ "like" (not "out of"); רַבָּה, a singular beside the plural תְּהֹמוֹת, is taken as an adverb, "abundantly" (or "the great deep", as Gen 7:11 תְּהוֹם רַבָּה). יְבַקַּע is an imperfect in a past setting.
- **"He brought streams also out of the rock" → "And he brought out streams from the crag"** (v16) — Hebrew נוֹזְלִים "streams" (as Exod 15:8; also v44); סֶלַע "crag" (the word of Num 20:8–11; v15 and v20 have צוּר "rock", the word of Exod 17:6). הוֹרִיד "bring down".
- **"And they sinned yet more against him by provoking the most High in the wilderness" → "And they went on still to sin against him, rebelling against the Most High in the parched land"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיּוֹסִיפוּ עוֹד לַחֲטֹא־לוֹ, literally "and they added still to sin against him". לַמְרוֹת עֶלְיוֹן "to rebel against Elyon" (מָרָה, also v40, 56). צִיָּה "parched land" (beside מִדְבָּר "wilderness").
- **"And they tempted God in their heart by asking meat for their lust" → "And they tested El in their heart by asking food for their soul"** (v18) — Hebrew נִסָּה "test" (also v41, 56); אֹכֶל "food"; לְנַפְשָׁם "for their soul" (נֶפֶשׁ here the appetite).
- **"Yea, they spake against God; they said, Can God furnish a table" → "And they spoke against Elohim; they said, Is El able to arrange a table"** (v19) — Hebrew הֲיוּכַל אֵל לַעֲרֹךְ שֻׁלְחָן; עָרַךְ "arrange" (as of the table of the bread, Exod 40:4, 23).
- **"Behold, he smote the rock, that the waters gushed out, and the streams overflowed; can he give bread also? can he provide flesh" → "Behold, he struck the rock, and waters flowed and torrents overflowed. Is he able also to give bread, or will he provide meat"** (v20) — Hebrew הֵן "behold"; זוּב "flow" (the verb of "flowing with milk and honey"); נְחָלִים "wadis", here the water itself: "torrents"; שָׁטַף "overflow". אִם "or" in a double question. שְׁאֵר "meat" (also v27; Exod 21:10 "food"); v39 בָּשָׂר "flesh" is another word, and Num 11:4–33 has בָּשָׂר.
- **"the LORD heard this, and was wroth: so a fire was kindled against Jacob, and anger also came up against Israel" → "Yahweh heard and was enraged, and a fire was kindled against Jacob, and anger also went up against Israel"** (v21) — Hebrew הִתְעַבֵּר "be enraged" (also v59, 62); "this" not in the Hebrew; עָלָה "go up" (also v31); אַף "anger" (lit. "nose", also v31, 38, 49, 50).
- **"trusted not in his salvation" — kept** (v22) — Hebrew יְשׁוּעָה "salvation"; הֶאֱמִין בְּ "believe in" (also v32).
- **"Though he had commanded the clouds from above, and opened the doors of heaven" → "And he commanded the skies above and opened the doors of the heavens"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיְצַו, "and he commanded": a new clause, not "though he had". שְׁחָקִים "skies" (2 Sam 22:12); שָׁמַיִם is plural, "the heavens" (also v24, 26).
- **"And had rained down manna upon them to eat, and had given them of the corn of heaven" → "and he caused manna to rain upon them to eat, and the grain of the heavens he gave to them"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיַּמְטֵר "caused to rain" (also v27; Exod 16:4 "I am causing bread to rain"). מָן "manna": the Hebrew is *man* (Exod 16:15, 31). דְּגַן־שָׁמַיִם "the grain of the heavens" (דָּגָן "grain"), as printed: John 6:31 cites "bread from heaven", which is Exod 16:4 and the Greek of this verse, not the Hebrew of this line.
- **"Man did eat angels' food: he sent them meat to the full" → "Man ate the bread of the mighty; provision he sent to them to the full"** (v25) — Hebrew לֶחֶם אַבִּירִים אָכַל אִישׁ: אַבִּירִים "mighty ones" (plural of אַבִּיר; the אָבִיר of "the Mighty One of Jacob"); the Hebrew does not say "angels". The Greek has "bread of angels" (from memory; so KJV). אִישׁ "man" (each one). צֵידָה "provision" (food for a journey, Josh 9:11); לָשֹׂבַע "to the full".
- **"He caused an east wind to blow in the heaven: and by his power he brought in the south wind" → "He made the east wind set out in the heavens, and he drove by his strength the south wind"** (v26) — Hebrew יַסַּע, hifil of נָסַע "set out" (Num 11:31 "a wind set out from Yahweh"); וַיְנַהֵג "drove" (Exod 10:13; also v52); עֹז "strength" (also v61). קָדִים "east" and תֵּימָן "south" are, literally, "front" and "right-hand side".
- **"He rained flesh also upon them as dust, and feathered fowls like as the sand of the sea" → "And he caused meat to rain upon them like dust, and like the sand of the seas winged birds"** (v27) — Hebrew עוֹף כָּנָף "birds of wing"; יַמִּים "seas", plural. The quail of Num 11:31 (the word שְׂלָו is not used here).
- **"And he let it fall in the midst of their camp, round about their habitations" → "And he made them fall in the midst of his camp, all around his dwellings"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיַּפֵּל has no object: "them" is supplied. מַחֲנֵהוּ, מִשְׁכְּנֹתָיו "his camp, his dwellings": the people as one ("his"), or God's own camp; the suffix is kept singular (KJV "their").
- **"So they did eat, and were well filled: for he gave them their own desire" → "And they ate and were greatly sated, and their craving he brought to them"** (v29) — Hebrew שָׂבַע "be sated" with מְאֹד "greatly"; וְ "and" (not "for"); תַּאֲוָה "craving" (as Num 11:4, 34; also v30); יָבִא "he brought".
- **"They were not estranged from their lust. But while their meat was yet in their mouths" → "They were not estranged from their craving; their food was still in their mouths"** (v30) — Hebrew לֹא־זָרוּ מִתַּאֲוָתָם, "they had not turned strangers to their craving": it had not left them. עוֹד אָכְלָם בְּפִיהֶם, a nominal clause (Num 11:33 "the flesh was still between their teeth").
- **"The wrath of God came upon them, and slew the fattest of them, and smote down the chosen men of Israel" → "and the anger of Elohim went up against them, and he killed among their fat ones and made the young men of Israel bow down"** (v31) — Hebrew אַף "anger" with עָלָה (as v21); הָרַג "kill" (also v34, 47); בְּמִשְׁמַנֵּיהֶם "among their fat ones" (the stoutest); בַּחוּרִים "young men" (also v63); הִכְרִיעַ "make bow down".
- **"believed not for his wondrous works" → "did not believe in his wonderful deeds"** (v32) — Hebrew הֶאֱמִין בְּ.
- **"Therefore their days did he consume in vanity, and their years in trouble" → "And he finished their days in vapor, and their years in dismay"** (v33) — Hebrew וַיְכַל "and he finished" (כִּלָּה); הֶבֶל "vapor" ("vanity" in KJV; the word of Ecclesiastes); בֶּהָלָה "dismay" (as Lev 26:16).
- **"When he slew them, then they sought him: and they returned and enquired early after God" → "When he killed them, they sought him, and they returned and sought El eagerly"** (v34) — Hebrew אִם "when" (or "if"); דָּרַשׁ "seek"; שׁוּב "return, turn back" (not "repent"); שִׁחֵר "seek eagerly" (from the root of שַׁחַר "dawn": KJV "early").
- **"God was their rock, and the high God their redeemer" → "Elohim was their Rock, and El Elyon their redeemer"** (v35) — Hebrew צוּר as a name of God, with a capital; אֵל עֶלְיוֹן, both words kept (token `el-elyon`, as Gen 14:18–22); גֹּאֵל "redeemer".
- **"Nevertheless they did flatter him with their mouth" → "And they enticed him with their mouth"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיְפַתּוּהוּ, piel of פָּתָה "entice, persuade, fool" (not "flatter"); וְ "and" (not "nevertheless"). כִּזֵּב "lie".
- **"For their heart was not right with him, neither were they stedfast in his covenant" → "And their heart was not established with him, and they were not faithful in his covenant"** (v37) — Hebrew נָכוֹן "established" (the root of v8 הֵכִין); נֶאֶמְנוּ "faithful" (as v8).
- **"But he, being full of compassion, forgave their iniquity, and destroyed them not: yea, many a time turned he his anger away, and did not stir up all his wrath" → "But he, compassionate, would make atonement for iniquity and would not ruin; and many times he turned back his anger and would not stir up all his fury"** (v38) — Hebrew רַחוּם "compassionate"; יְכַפֵּר עָוֹן "make atonement for iniquity" (כִּפֶּר, literally perhaps "cover"; not "forgive"); "their" and "them" are not in the Hebrew. The three imperfects (יְכַפֵּר, יַשְׁחִית, יָעִיר) are taken as what he would do again and again, beside the perfect וְהִרְבָּה "and he did many times". הִשְׁחִית "ruin" (also v45); הֵשִׁיב "turn back"; חֵמָה "fury"; הֵעִיר "stir up".
- **"For he remembered that they were but flesh; a wind that passeth away" → "And he remembered that they were flesh, a wind that goes"** (v39) — Hebrew וַיִּזְכֹּר "and he remembered"; "but" (only) is not in the Hebrew. רוּחַ "wind" (also "breath, spirit": the one word); הוֹלֵךְ "goes".
- **"How oft did they provoke him in the wilderness, and grieve him in the desert" → "How often they rebelled against him in the wilderness, grieved him in the desert"** (v40) — Hebrew מָרָה "rebel against"; the second line has no "and". יְשִׁימוֹן "desert" (Deut 32:10). Both verbs are imperfects: what they did again and again.
- **"Yea, they turned back and tempted God, and limited the Holy One of Israel" → "And they turned back and tested El, and the Holy One of Israel they pained"** (v41) — Hebrew וַיָּשׁוּבוּ וַיְנַסּוּ, "turned back and tested" (= tested again). הִתְווּ is found only here: from a verb תָּוָה "pain, wound" (so here), or from תָּוָה "make a mark" (Ezek 9:4: "set a limit to", KJV "limited"); the meaning is uncertain.
- **"nor the day when he delivered them from the enemy" → "the day that he ransomed them from the adversary"** (v42) — Hebrew פָּדָה "ransom"; צָר "adversary" (also v61, 66); no "nor".
- **"How he had wrought his signs in Egypt, and his wonders in the field of Zoan" → "when he set his signs in Egypt and his wonders in the field of Zoan"** (v43) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־שָׂם "when / that he set"; אוֹת "sign", מוֹפֵת "wonder" (as Exod 7:3).
- **"And had turned their rivers into blood; and their floods, that they could not drink" → "And he turned their Niles into blood, and their streams they could not drink"** (v44) — Hebrew יְאֹרֵיהֶם, the plural of יְאֹר "the Nile" (its arms and canals: the "Niles" of Exod 7:19; 8:5); נֹזְלִים "streams" (as v16). בַּל־יִשְׁתָּיוּן "they could not drink" (Exod 7:21, 24). The plagues are not told in the order of Exodus, and not all of them.
- **"He sent divers sorts of flies among them, which devoured them; and frogs, which destroyed them" → "He sent among them a swarm, and it ate them, and frogs, and they ruined them"** (v45) — Hebrew עָרֹב "swarm" (singular, without the article, as Exod 8:24 "a heavy swarm"); what the swarm was is uncertain ("a mixture", of insects or of beasts); "flies" is not in the word. צְפַרְדֵּעַ "frogs" (a collective singular). וַתַּשְׁחִיתֵם "and they ruined them" (Exod 8:24 "the land was being ruined because of the swarm").
- **"He gave also their increase unto the caterpiller, and their labour unto the locust" → "And he gave their produce to the devourer and their labor to the locusts"** (v46) — Hebrew יְבוּל "produce"; חָסִיל "devourer" (a kind of locust, from חָסַל "devour"; as 1 Kgs 8:37); יְגִיעַ "labor" (what labor yields); אַרְבֶּה "locusts" (a collective singular, Exod 10).
- **"He destroyed their vines with hail, and their sycomore trees with frost" → "He killed their vine with hail and their sycamores with frost"** (v47) — Hebrew יַהֲרֹג "kill" (the verb of v31, 34, used of a vine); גֶּפֶן "vine", singular; שִׁקְמָה "sycamore" (the sycamore fig). חֲנָמַל is found only here: "frost" is the best-supported sense ("hailstones", "flood" are also proposed); uncertain. בָּרָד "hail" (Exod 9:18–34; also v48).
- **"He gave up their cattle also to the hail, and their flocks to hot thunderbolts" → "And he handed over their animals to the hail and their livestock to the flames"** (v48) — Hebrew וַיַּסְגֵּר "handed over" (literally "shut up to"; also v50, 62); בְּעִיר "animals" (beasts of burden); מִקְנֶה "livestock" (Exod 9:3). רְשָׁפִים, plural of רֶשֶׁף "flame" (Song 8:6), also a word for plague (Deut 32:24 "burning plague"; Hab 3:5): here beside the hail, the fire that went with it (Exod 9:23–24) is likely meant; "lightning bolts" is also proposed.
- **"He cast upon them the fierceness of his anger, wrath, and indignation, and trouble, by sending evil angels among them" → "He sent against them the burning of his anger, raging and indignation and distress, a sending of angels of evils"** (v49) — Hebrew יְשַׁלַּח "he sent" (also v45); חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ "the burning of his anger"; עֶבְרָה "raging", זַעַם "indignation", צָרָה "distress". מִשְׁלַחַת מַלְאֲכֵי רָעִים "a sending (a band sent) of angels of evils": מַלְאָךְ is "messenger", here sent by God; רָעִים "evils" (or "evil ones"): angels who bring harm, as the destroyer of Exod 12:23, not angels evil in themselves.
- **"He made a way to his anger; he spared not their soul from death, but gave their life over to the pestilence" → "He leveled a path for his anger; he did not withhold their soul from death, and their life he handed over to the pestilence"** (v50) — Hebrew יְפַלֵּס נָתִיב "he leveled a path"; חָשַׂךְ "withhold"; וְ "and"; דֶּבֶר "pestilence" (Exod 9:3). חַיָּתָם may also be "their living things" (their beasts, as Exod 9:3); "their life" beside "their soul" is the more likely sense.
- **"And smote all the firstborn in Egypt; the chief of their strength in the tabernacles of Ham" → "And he struck every firstborn in Egypt, the beginning of vigor in the tents of Ham"** (v51) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ כָּל־בְּכוֹר "struck every firstborn" (Exod 12:29); רֵאשִׁית אוֹנִים "the beginning of vigor" (Gen 49:3 רֵאשִׁית אוֹנִי "the beginning of my vigor"; the firstborn); "their" is not in the Hebrew. אֹהֶל "tent". Ham as the father of Mitzrayim (Gen 10:6).
- **"But made his own people to go forth like sheep, and guided them in the wilderness like a flock" → "And he made his people set out like the flock and led them like a drove in the wilderness"** (v52) — Hebrew וַיַּסַּע, hifil of נָסַע (as v26); צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats); וַיְנַהֲגֵם, the piel of v26 ("drove" of the wind), of a shepherd "lead" (as Ps 48:14; 80:1); v14, 53, 72 נָחָה is another verb with the same English; עֵדֶר "drove" (a single flock).
- **"And he led them on safely, so that they feared not: but the sea overwhelmed their enemies" → "And he led them in security, and they were not in dread, and their enemies the sea covered"** (v53) — Hebrew לָבֶטַח "in security"; פָּחַד "be in dread"; כִּסָּה "cover" (Exod 14:28; 15:5, 10).
- **"to the border of his sanctuary, even to this mountain, which his right hand had purchased" → "to his holy territory, this mountain that his right hand got"** (v54) — Hebrew גְּבוּל קָדְשׁוֹ, literally "the border of his holiness" (גְּבוּל "border", and the land within it); הַר־זֶה "this mountain" (the land of hills, or Zion); קָנָה "get" (as Exod 15:16 "the people that you got").
- **"He cast out the heathen also before them, and divided them an inheritance by line" → "And he drove out nations from before them, and made them fall by the line as an inheritance"** (v55) — Hebrew גֵּרֵשׁ "drive out"; גּוֹיִם "nations"; וַיַּפִּילֵם בְּחֶבֶל נַחֲלָה, literally "and he made them fall with a cord of inheritance": the land of the nations allotted by the measuring line (הִפִּיל as Josh 13:6). וַיַּשְׁכֵּן "and made abide" (שָׁכַן, as Josh 18:1).
- **"Yet they tempted and provoked the most high God" → "And they tested and rebelled against Elohim Elyon"** (v56) — Hebrew אֶת־אֱלֹהִים עֶלְיוֹן, two divine words, each with its token. עֵדוֹת "testimonies".
- **"But turned back, and dealt unfaithfully like their fathers: they were turned aside like a deceitful bow" → "And they turned back and dealt treacherously like their fathers; they were turned like a bow of treachery"** (v57) — Hebrew סוּג "turn back"; בָּגַד "deal treacherously"; נֶהְפְּכוּ "they were turned" (the הָפַךְ of v9, 44); קֶשֶׁת רְמִיָּה "a bow of treachery" (רְמִיָּה, beside מִרְמָה "deceit"; also "slackness": a bow that fails the archer). The English "treach-" stands for two Hebrew roots here.
- **"For they provoked him to anger with their high places, and moved him to jealousy with their graven images" → "And they vexed him with their high places, and with their carved images they made him jealous"** (v58) — Hebrew וְ "and" (not "for"); הִכְעִיס "vex" (Deut 32:16, 21); בָּמוֹת "high places"; פְּסִילִים "carved images"; הִקְנִיא "make jealous" (Deut 32:16, 21).
- **"When God heard this, he was wroth, and greatly abhorred Israel" → "Elohim heard and was enraged, and he rejected Israel greatly"** (v59) — Hebrew מָאַס "reject" (also v67); מְאֹד "greatly"; no "when", no "this".
- **"So that he forsook the tabernacle of Shiloh, the tent which he placed among men" → "And he abandoned the dwelling of Shiloh, the tent that he had made abide among mankind"** (v60) — Hebrew נָטַשׁ "abandon"; מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling"; שִׁכֵּן "made abide" (the verb of Josh 18:1 "they made the tent of meeting abide" at Shiloh); בָּאָדָם "among mankind" (or "in man"). The verse with v61 looks to 1 Sam 4 (the ark taken; Jer 7:12).
- **"And delivered his strength into captivity, and his glory into the enemy's hand" → "And he gave his strength to captivity, and his beauty into the hand of the adversary"** (v61) — Hebrew עֹז "strength", תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (not כָּבוֹד "glory"): the ark is meant (Ps 132:8 "the ark of your strength"; 1 Sam 4:21–22 "glory has gone into exile"), but not named. שְׁבִי "captivity".
- **"was wroth with his inheritance" → "with his inheritance he was enraged"** (v62) — Hebrew order; הִתְעַבֵּר בְּ (as v21, 59).
- **"The fire consumed their young men; and their maidens were not given to marriage" → "His young men fire consumed, and his virgins were not praised"** (v63) — Hebrew בַּחוּרָיו "his young men" (the people as one: "his", also v64); אֵשׁ without the article; בְּתוּלֹתָיו "his virgins"; לֹא הוּלָּלוּ, pual of הָלַל "they were not praised": no wedding song was sung for them (so the KJV's sense).
- **"and their widows made no lamentation" → "and his widows did not weep"** (v64) — Hebrew לֹא תִבְכֶּינָה "did not weep" (the dead were not mourned).
- **"Then the Lord awaked as one out of sleep, and like a mighty man that shouteth by reason of wine" → "And Adonai awoke like one asleep, like a mighty one shouting because of wine"** (v65) — Hebrew כְּיָשֵׁן "like one asleep"; אֲדֹנָי `adonai`; גִּבּוֹר "mighty one". מִתְרוֹנֵן is found only here: from רָנַן "shout (for joy)" (so here), or from רוּן "be overcome" ("overcome by wine"); uncertain.
- **"And he smote his enemies in the hinder parts: he put them to a perpetual reproach" → "And he struck his adversaries backward; an everlasting reproach he gave to them"** (v66) — Hebrew צָרָיו "his adversaries"; אָחוֹר "backward" (he drove them back; or "from behind", KJV "in the hinder parts"); חֶרְפַּת עוֹלָם "a reproach of the age", "everlasting".
- **"Moreover he refused the tabernacle of Joseph" → "And he rejected the tent of Joseph"** (v67) — Hebrew מָאַס "reject" (as v59); אֹהֶל "tent". שֵׁבֶט "tribe" (also v68).
- **"the mount Zion which he loved" → "Mount Zion, which he loved"** (v68) — Hebrew אָהֵב, a perfect (it may also be read "which he loves").
- **"And he built his sanctuary like high palaces" → "And he built his sanctuary like the heights"** (v69) — Hebrew כְּמוֹ־רָמִים "like high ones": the heights (of the heavens or of the mountains); "palaces" is not in the word. יְסָדָהּ "he founded it" (the earth); לְעוֹלָם "forever".
- **"He chose David also his servant, and took him from the sheepfolds" → "And he chose David his servant and took him from the folds of the flock"** (v70) — Hebrew עַבְדּוֹ "his servant" (עֶבֶד of Yahweh); מִכְלְאֹת צֹאן "folds of the flock".
- **"From following the ewes great with young he brought him to feed Jacob his people, and Israel his inheritance" → "From after the nursing ones he brought him to shepherd Jacob his people and Israel his inheritance"** (v71) — Hebrew מֵאַחַר עָלוֹת: עָלוֹת "nursing ones", the ewes giving suck (Gen 33:13; Isa 40:11); "ewes" is not a separate word. רָעָה "shepherd" (also v72; 2 Sam 5:2 "you shall shepherd my people Israel").
- **"So he fed them according to the integrity of his heart; and guided them by the skilfulness of his hands" → "And he shepherded them according to the integrity of his heart, and with the understanding of his palms he led them"** (v72) — Hebrew כְּתֹם לְבָבוֹ "according to the integrity of his heart"; תְּבוּנוֹת "understanding" (plural, "understandings": skill); כַּפָּיו "his palms" (כַּף, not יָד "hand").

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading stands in Hebrew v1 with the first words of the psalm: the numbers do not shift.
- **Long historical psalm** — from the exodus and the wilderness (Exod 7–17; Num 11; 20) to the loss of Shiloh (1 Sam 4) and the choice of David and Zion; the plague list (v44–51) and the wilderness events keep the words of the Torah where the Hebrew uses the same words.
- **Didactic intention** — teaching the next generation (v4–8).
- **Choice of Judah over Ephraim** — the rejection of "the tent of Joseph" and Shiloh (v60, 67) and the choice of Zion and David (v68–72) close the psalm.
