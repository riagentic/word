# Psalm 86

A prayer of David.

[1] Incline, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your ear, answer me,
for afflicted and needy am I.

[2] Keep my soul, for I am a faithful one;
save your servant, you my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ — the one who trusts in you.

[3] Show me favor, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧,
for to you I call all the day.

[4] Gladden the soul of your servant,
for to you, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, I lift up my soul.

[5] For you, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, are good and pardoning,
and abounding in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ to all who call on you.

[6] Give ear, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, to my prayer,
and give heed to the voice of my pleas for favor.

[7] In the day of my distress I call on you,
for you will answer me.

[8] There is none like you among the gods, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧,
and there is nothing like your works.

[9] All nations whom you have made will come
and bow down before you, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧,
and give honor to your name.

[10] For great are you, and doing wonderful deeds;
you are ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you alone.

[11] Teach me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your way;
I will walk in your truth;
unite my heart to fear your name.

[12] I will give thanks to you, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, with all my heart,
and let me honor your name forever.

[13] For your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is great upon me,
and you have delivered my soul from Sheol beneath.

[14] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the presumptuous have risen against me,
and a congregation of ruthless ones has sought my soul,
and they have not set you before them.

[15] But you, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, are ⟦el|El|God⟧ compassionate and gracious,
slow to anger and abounding in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth.

[16] Turn to me and show me favor;
give your strength to your servant,
and save the son of your slave woman.

[17] Make with me a sign for good,
and let those who hate me see and be ashamed,
for you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, have helped me and comforted me.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 6, 11, 17) — Tetragrammaton. **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 12, 15) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 10, 12, 14) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God" → "El"** (v15) — Hebrew אֵל *El*. No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"A Prayer of David"** (heading) — Hebrew תְּפִלָּה לְדָוִד, the first words of v1 in the Hebrew (as Ps 17); the לְ of לְדָוִד may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and is not decided here.
- **"Bow down thine ear, O LORD, hear me: for I am poor and needy" → "Incline, Yahweh, your ear, answer me, for afflicted and needy am I"** (v1) — Hebrew הַטֵּה "incline" (נָטָה "turn, stretch out"), the name before the object; עֲנֵנִי "answer me", no "and"; עָנִי "afflicted", אֶבְיוֹן "needy", before אָנִי "I".
- **"Preserve my soul; for I am holy: O thou my God, save thy servant that trusteth in thee" → "Keep my soul, for I am a faithful one; save your servant, you my Elohim — the one who trusts in you"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁמְרָה "keep"; חָסִיד "a faithful one" (one who keeps *chesed*; not "holy", "godly"); אַתָּה אֱלֹהַי, "you, my Elohim", in the Hebrew order between "your servant" and "the one who trusts"; עַבְדְּךָ "your servant" (the word is "slave", said of oneself before Elohim, also v4, 16).
- **"Be merciful unto me … for I cry unto thee daily" → "Show me favor … for to you I call all the day"** (v3) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי "show me favor" (חָנַן, also v16); כָּל־הַיּוֹם "all the day".
- **"Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul"** (v4) — Hebrew שַׂמֵּחַ "gladden"; נַפְשִׁי אֶשָּׂא "I lift up my soul" (to long for, to count on).
- **"good, and ready to forgive; and plenteous in mercy" → "good and pardoning, and abounding in chesed"** (v5) — Hebrew סַלָּח, only here, adjective of סָלַח "pardon" (said only of God); וְרַב־חֶסֶד "and abounding in *chesed*" (as Exod 34:6), with its token (also v13, 15).
- **"attend to the voice of my supplications" → "give heed to the voice of my pleas for favor"** (v6) — Hebrew הַאֲזִינָה "give ear"; הַקְשִׁיבָה "give heed"; תַּחֲנוּנוֹתָי "my pleas for favor" (from חָנַן; as Ps 28:2; 31:22).
- **"In the day of my trouble I will call upon thee: for thou wilt answer me" → "In the day of my distress I call on you, for you will answer me"** (v7) — Hebrew צָרָה "distress".
- **"Among the gods there is none like unto thee … neither are there any works like unto thy works" → "There is none like you among the gods … and there is nothing like your works"** (v8) — Hebrew בָאֱלֹהִים, plural beings: "gods", plain text; וְאֵין כְּמַעֲשֶׂיךָ "and there is nothing like your works".
- **"All nations whom thou hast made shall come and worship before thee, O Lord; and shall glorify thy name" → "All nations whom you have made will come and bow down before you, Adonai, and give honor to your name"** (v9) — Hebrew וְיִשְׁתַּחֲווּ "bow down"; וִיכַבְּדוּ לִשְׁמֶךָ, כִּבֵּד "honor" with לְ: "give honor to your name" (v12 has the object without לְ).
- **"For thou art great, and doest wondrous things: thou art God alone" → "For great are you, and doing wonderful deeds; you are Elohim, you alone"** (v10) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה נִפְלָאוֹת, a participle, "doing wonderful deeds"; אַתָּה אֱלֹהִים לְבַדֶּךָ.
- **"I will walk in thy truth: unite my heart to fear thy name" → "I will walk in your truth; unite my heart to fear your name"** (v11) — Hebrew הוֹרֵנִי "teach me", the name before the object; אֲהַלֵּךְ "I will walk", a statement (not "that I may"); יַחֵד "unite" (only here in this form; the versions read "let my heart rejoice", from memory).
- **"I will praise thee … and I will glorify thy name for evermore" → "I will give thanks to you … and let me honor your name forever"** (v12) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ "I will give thanks to you"; וַאֲכַבְּדָה, cohortative of כִּבֵּד "honor"; לְעוֹלָם "forever".
- **"great is thy mercy toward me: and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell" → "your chesed is great upon me, and you have delivered my soul from Sheol beneath"** (v13) — Hebrew עָלָי "upon me"; וְהִצַּלְתָּ "and you have delivered"; שְׁאוֹל תַּחְתִּיָּה "Sheol beneath" (as Deut 32:22), plain word.
- **"the proud are risen against me, and the assemblies of violent men have sought after my soul" → "the presumptuous have risen against me, and a congregation of ruthless ones has sought my soul"** (v14) — Hebrew זֵדִים "the presumptuous"; עֲדַת עָרִיצִים "a congregation of ruthless ones" (עֵדָה singular; עָרִיץ, one who strikes terror); נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; שָׂמוּךָ "set you". Ps 54:3 has זָרִים "strangers" where this verse has זֵדִים.
- **"But thou, O Lord, art a God full of compassion, and gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth" → "But you, Adonai, are El compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in chesed and truth"** (v15) — the words of Exod 34:6 (אֵל רַחוּם וְחַנּוּן אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם וְרַב־חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת), rendered as there; אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם, literally "long of nostrils"; אֱמֶת "truth" (not "faithfulness").
- **"the son of thine handmaid" → "the son of your slave woman"** (v16) — Hebrew אָמָה "slave woman"; עֹז "strength".
- **"Shew me a token for good; that they which hate me may see it, and be ashamed" → "Make with me a sign for good, and let those who hate me see and be ashamed"** (v17) — Hebrew עֲשֵׂה־עִמִּי אוֹת, "make with me a sign"; וְיִרְאוּ … וְיֵבֹשׁוּ, jussives with "and" (no "it"); כִּי "for".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is the first words of v1 in the Hebrew; the verse numbers are the same.
- **A prayer of David in Book III** (Ps 73–89), set among the psalms of the sons of Korah (84–85; 87–88); many of its lines stand in other psalms and in Exod 34:6 (v15).
