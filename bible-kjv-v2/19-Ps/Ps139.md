# Psalm 139

To the overseer. Of David. A psalm.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you have searched me out and known.

[2] You know my sitting down and my rising up;
you understand my thought from afar.

[3] My path and my lying down you have winnowed,
and with all my ways you are acquainted.

[4] For there is not a word on my tongue —
behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you know all of it.

[5] Behind and before you have hemmed me in,
and you have set your palm upon me.

[6] Too wonderful is knowledge for me;
it is set on high; I am not equal to it.

[7] Where shall I go from your spirit,
and where shall I flee from your face?

[8] If I go up to the heavens, there you are;
and should I make my bed in Sheol, behold, you!

[9] Should I take up the wings of the dawn,
should I abide in the far end of the sea,

[10] even there your hand will lead me,
and your right hand will hold me.

[11] And I said, "Surely darkness will bruise me,
and night is the light around me" —

[12] even darkness is not dark to you,
and night shines as the day;
as the darkness, so the light.

[13] For you, you got my kidneys;
you wove me together in the belly of my mother.

[14] I will give thanks to you, because fearsomely I am set apart;
wonderful are your works,
and my soul knows very well.

[15] My frame was not hidden from you,
when I was made in the hiding place,
embroidered in the lowest parts of the earth.

[16] My unformed substance your eyes saw,
and upon your book all of them were written:
days were formed,
and to it was one among them.

[17] And to me, how precious are your thoughts, ⟦el|El|God⟧!
How mighty are their sums!

[18] Should I number them, they are more than the sand;
I awake, and I am still with you.

[19] If only you would slay the wicked, ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧!
And men of blood, turn aside from me!

[20] They who speak of you for a scheme —
your adversaries lift up for emptiness.

[21] Do I not hate those who hate you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and loathe those who rise up against you?

[22] With complete hatred I hate them;
they have become enemies to me.

[23] Search me out, ⟦el|El|God⟧, and know my heart;
try me and know my disquieting thoughts;

[24] And see if there is a way of pain in me,
and lead me in the everlasting way.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 21) — Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "El"** (v17, 23) — Hebrew אֵל *El*. **"God" → "Eloah"** (v19) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*, singular. No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. Of David. A psalm."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר, in this order, the first words of v1. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"thou hast searched me, and known me" → "you have searched me out and known"** (v1) — Hebrew חֲקַרְתַּנִי וַתֵּדָע; חָקַר "search out" (also v23, as Ps 44:21); וַתֵּדָע "and you have known" has no object: "me" is not written.
- **"Thou knowest my downsitting and mine uprising, thou understandest my thought afar off" → "You know my sitting down and my rising up; you understand my thought from afar"** (v2) — Hebrew אַתָּה יָדַעְתָּ, with the pronoun "you" set first for weight; בִּין "understand"; רֵעַ "thought", in this sense only here and v17 (an Aramaic word; elsewhere רֵעַ is "friend").
- **"Thou compassest my path and my lying down, and art acquainted with all my ways" → "My path and my lying down you have winnowed, and with all my ways you are acquainted"** (v3) — Hebrew זֵרִיתָ, piel of זָרָה "strew, winnow": to sift as grain is sifted; the objects stand first, as in the Hebrew; הִסְכַּנְתָּה, hifil of סָכַן "be familiar with".
- **"For there is not a word in my tongue, but, lo, O LORD, thou knowest it altogether" → "For there is not a word on my tongue — behold, Yahweh, you know all of it"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי אֵין מִלָּה בִּלְשׁוֹנִי, no "yet" and no "but"; הֵן "behold"; כֻלָּהּ "all of it".
- **"Thou hast beset me behind and before, and laid thine hand upon me" → "Behind and before you have hemmed me in, and you have set your palm upon me"** (v5) — Hebrew צַרְתָּנִי, from צוּר "bind up, shut in"; וַתָּשֶׁת, שִׁית "set"; כַּפֶּכָה "your palm" (כַּף, not יָד "hand").
- **"Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; it is high, I cannot attain unto it" → "Too wonderful is knowledge for me; it is set on high; I am not equal to it"** (v6) — qere פְּלִיאָה "wonderful" (ketiv פלאיה, the same word in another spelling); דַעַת מִמֶּנִּי, מִן of comparison, as Gen 18:14 "too wonderful for"; no "such"; נִשְׂגְּבָה, nifal of שָׂגַב "be set on high"; לֹא־אוּכַל לָהּ, literally "I am not able for it".
- **"Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy presence?" → "Where shall I go from your spirit, and where shall I flee from your face?"** (v7) — Hebrew רוּחַ "spirit" (the one word for spirit, wind and breath); וְאָנָה "and where", not "or"; מִפָּנֶיךָ "from your face"; בָּרַח "flee".
- **"If I ascend up into heaven, thou art there: if I make my bed in hell, behold, thou art there" → "If I go up to the heavens, there you are; and should I make my bed in Sheol, behold, you!"** (v8) — Hebrew אֶסַּק, from סָלַק "go up" (an Aramaic word, only here in Hebrew); שָׁמַיִם "heavens", plural; וְאַצִּיעָה "and should I spread my bed"; שְׁאוֹל "Sheol", plain word; הִנֶּךָּ "behold, you", no "there".
- **"If I take the wings of the morning, and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea" → "Should I take up the wings of the dawn, should I abide in the far end of the sea"** (v9) — Hebrew אֶשָּׂא, נָשָׂא "lift up, take up"; שַׁחַר "dawn"; אֶשְׁכְּנָה, שָׁכַן "abide"; אַחֲרִית יָם "the far end (literally the after-part) of the sea": the west.
- **"If I say, Surely the darkness shall cover me; even the night shall be light about me" → "And I said, 'Surely darkness will bruise me, and night is the light around me'"** (v11) — Hebrew וָאֹמַר, a past form with "and": "And I said" (taken by most as "If I say"); יְשׁוּפֵנִי, from שׁוּף "bruise" (as Gen 3:15; meaning uncertain), kept as printed: "cover me" (KJV) rests on a change of one letter (יְשׂוּכֵּנִי), not followed; וְלַיְלָה אוֹר בַּעֲדֵנִי, "and night is the light around me".
- **"Yea, the darkness hideth not from thee … the darkness and the light are both alike to thee" → "even darkness is not dark to you … as the darkness, so the light"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא־יַחְשִׁיךְ מִמֶּךָ, literally "does not make dark from you"; יָאִיר "shines"; כַּחֲשֵׁיכָה כָּאוֹרָה, literally "as the darkness, as the light" ("so" for the second "as"); חֲשֵׁיכָה is another word for darkness beside חֹשֶׁךְ.
- **"For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast covered me in my mother's womb" → "For you, you got my kidneys; you wove me together in the belly of my mother"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי־אַתָּה קָנִיתָ, the pronoun set first; קָנָה "get, buy, possess" (as Deut 32:6 "who got you"; the sense "create" is also defended); כִּלְיוֹת "kidneys", the inner man; תְּסֻכֵּנִי "you wove me together" (as Job 10:11; a root "screen, cover" is also possible: KJV "covered"); בֶּטֶן "belly".
- **"I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well" → "I will give thanks to you, because fearsomely I am set apart; wonderful are your works, and my soul knows very well"** (v14) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ "I will give thanks to you"; עַל כִּי "because"; נוֹרָאוֹת "fearsome things", used as an adverb (נוֹרָא "fearsome"); נִפְלֵיתִי, nifal of פָּלָה "set apart, make distinct" (as Exod 11:7), not "made": the Hebrew has no verb "make" here; the form is also taken from פָּלָא "be wonderful", and the versions read "you are wonderful" (from memory); נִפְלָאִים "wonderful"; no "that".
- **"My substance was not hid from thee, when I was made in secret, and curiously wrought in the lowest parts of the earth" → "My frame was not hidden from you, when I was made in the hiding place, embroidered in the lowest parts of the earth"** (v15) — Hebrew עָצְמִי "my frame" (one root with עֶצֶם "bone"; also read "my strength"); בַסֵּתֶר "in the hiding place"; רֻקַּמְתִּי, pual of רָקַם "embroider, weave in colors"; תַּחְתִּיּוֹת אָרֶץ "the lowest parts of the earth".
- **"Thine eyes did see my substance, yet being unperfect; and in thy book all my members were written, which in continuance were fashioned, when as yet there was none of them" → "My unformed substance your eyes saw, and upon your book all of them were written: days were formed, and to it was one among them"** (v16) — Hebrew גָּלְמִי, found only here, "my unformed substance" (from גָּלַם "roll up": an embryo; uncertain); עַל־סִפְרְךָ "upon your book"; כֻּלָּם "all of them": "my members" (KJV) is not in the Hebrew; יָמִים יֻצָּרוּ "days were formed" (יָצַר "form"). The qere וְלוֹ "and to it" is translated: "it" is taken as the unformed substance (the pronoun may also be "him"); the meaning of the line is uncertain. The ketiv וְלֹא reads "and not one among them" (KJV "when as yet there was none of them").
- **"How precious also are thy thoughts unto me, O God! how great is the sum of them!" → "And to me, how precious are your thoughts, El! How mighty are their sums!"** (v17) — Hebrew וְלִי "and to me", first; רֵעֶיךָ "your thoughts" (as v2); עָצְמוּ, עָצַם "be mighty"; רָאשֵׁיהֶם, literally "their heads": their sums.
- **"If I should count them, they are more in number than the sand: when I awake, I am still with thee" → "Should I number them, they are more than the sand; I awake, and I am still with you"** (v18) — Hebrew אֶסְפְּרֵם, סָפַר "number"; יִרְבּוּן, רָבָה "be many"; הֱקִיצֹתִי "I awake", no "when" (read by some as "I come to the end", a change of the pointing, not followed).
- **"Surely thou wilt slay the wicked, O God: depart from me therefore, ye bloody men" → "If only you would slay the wicked, Eloah! And men of blood, turn aside from me!"** (v19) — Hebrew אִם־תִּקְטֹל, אִם of a wish; רָשָׁע singular, "the wicked"; וְאַנְשֵׁי דָמִים "and men of blood" (דָּמִים plural, "bloods"), no "therefore"; סוּרוּ "turn aside", an imperative as printed.
- **"For they speak against thee wickedly, and thine enemies take thy name in vain" → "They who speak of you for a scheme — your adversaries lift up for emptiness"** (v20) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יֹאמְרֻךָ, literally "who say you": "speak of you" (KJV "against"); לִמְזִמָּה "for a scheme"; נָשֻׂא לַשָּׁוְא, "lift up for emptiness" (as Exod 20:7), but the Hebrew has no "your name": it is not supplied; עָרֶיךָ "your adversaries" (עָר, an Aramaic word, as 1 Sam 28:16; uncertain: also "your cities").
- **"Do not I hate them, O LORD, that hate thee? and am not I grieved with those that rise up against thee?" → "Do I not hate those who hate you, Yahweh, and loathe those who rise up against you?"** (v21) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא once, over both halves; אֶתְקוֹטָט, hitpolel of קוּט "loathe" (not "be grieved").
- **"I hate them with perfect hatred: I count them mine enemies" → "With complete hatred I hate them; they have become enemies to me"** (v22) — Hebrew תַּכְלִית שִׂנְאָה, literally "the completeness of hatred"; לְאוֹיְבִים הָיוּ לִי "they have become enemies to me", not "I count them".
- **"Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts" → "Search me out, El, and know my heart; try me and know my disquieting thoughts"** (v23) — two verbs, two renderings: חָקַר "search out" (as v1) and בָּחַן "try"; שַׂרְעַפִּים "disquieting thoughts" (as Ps 94:19).
- **"And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting" → "And see if there is a way of pain in me, and lead me in the everlasting way"** (v24) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ־עֹצֶב, עֹצֶב "pain" (one root with עֶצֶב, Gen 3:16), not "wicked"; a word of the same letters means "idol" (Isa 48:5), hence also "a way of an idol"; no "any"; נָחָה "lead"; דֶּרֶךְ עוֹלָם, literally "the way of the age" (Jer 6:16 has the "ancient paths").

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is the first words of v1 in the Hebrew; the verse numbers are the same.
- **Structure** — knowledge (v1–6), presence (v7–12), forming (v13–18), the wicked and the plea (v19–24); v1 and v23–24 frame the psalm with "search out" and "know".
