# Psalm 99

[1] Yahweh reigns; let the peoples tremble!
He sits enthroned upon the cherubim; let the earth quake!

[2] Yahweh is great in Zion;
he is exalted over all the peoples.

[3] Let them praise your great and awesome name!
Holy is he!

[4] The King in his might loves justice;
you have established equity;
you have executed justice
and righteousness in Jacob.

[5] Exalt Yahweh our *Elohim*;
worship at his footstool!
Holy is he!

[6] Moses and Aaron were among his priests,
Samuel also was among those who called upon his name.
They called to Yahweh, and he answered them.

[7] In the pillar of the cloud he spoke to them;
they kept his testimonies
and the statute that he gave them.

[8] O Yahweh our *Elohim*, you answered them;
you were an *Elohim* who forgave them,
but an avenger of their wrongdoings.

[9] Exalt Yahweh our *Elohim*,
and worship at his holy mountain;
for Yahweh our *Elohim* is holy!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 9) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 8, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — trisagion structure (v3, 5, 9); Yahweh's kingship (v1-5), Moses/Aaron/Samuel precedents (v6-8), closing exaltation (v9).

### Notes
- **Trisagion psalm** — "Holy is he" three times (v3, 5, 9).
