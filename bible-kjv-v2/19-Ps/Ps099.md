## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 9) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 8, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v8) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"The LORD reigneth; let the people tremble" → "Yahweh reigns; let the peoples be agitated"** (v1) — Hebrew יְהוָה מָלָךְ, a perfect (also "has become king"); יִרְגְּזוּ עַמִּים: רָגַז "be agitated" (in fear or in anger: the verse does not say which; "tremble" is the common rendering); the imperfect may also be read "the peoples are agitated". עַמִּים plural.
- **"he sitteth between the cherubims; let the earth be moved" → "He sits upon cherubim; let the earth shake"** (v1) — Hebrew יֹשֵׁב כְּרוּבִים, literally "sitting the cherubim", with no preposition and no article (as 1 Sam 4:4 "who sits upon the cherubim"; "upon" is supplied; יָשַׁב "sit", as a king sits, also "dwell"). תָּנוּט is found only here: "shake, totter" is the best-supported sense.
- **"and he is high above all the people" → "and high is he over all the peoples"** (v2) — Hebrew וְרָם הוּא עַל־כָּל־הָעַמִּים, "high" first; עַמִּים plural.
- **"Let them praise thy great and terrible name; for it is holy" → "Let them give thanks to your name, great and fearsome! Holy is he!"** (v3) — Hebrew יוֹדוּ שִׁמְךָ גָּדוֹל וְנוֹרָא קָדוֹשׁ הוּא: הוֹדָה "give thanks to"; נוֹרָא "fearsome" (nifal participle of יָרֵא "fear"); קָדוֹשׁ הוּא "holy is he" (or "it", the name: הוּא is both); no "for". The same words close v5.
- **"The king's strength also loveth judgment; thou dost establish equity, thou executest judgment and righteousness in Jacob" → "And the strength of a king loves judgment; you have established uprightness; judgment and righteousness in Jacob you have done"** (v4) — Hebrew וְעֹז מֶלֶךְ מִשְׁפָּט אָהֵב אַתָּה כּוֹנַנְתָּ מֵישָׁרִים מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה בְּיַעֲקֹב אַתָּה עָשִׂיתָ. The first clause is word for word; מֶלֶךְ has no article ("a king"); its sense is not certain ("the strength of the King [is that he] loves judgment", or "and a mighty King loves judgment"). אַתָּה stands twice before the verbs (emphatic "you"); both verbs are perfects; מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness"; עָשָׂה "do".
- **"Exalt ye … and worship at his footstool; for he is holy" → "Extol … and bow down at the footstool of his feet! Holy is he!"** (v5) — Hebrew רוֹמְמוּ (רוֹמֵם "extol", as Exod 15:2) … וְהִשְׁתַּחֲווּ לַהֲדֹם רַגְלָיו: הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" (not "worship"); לְ, literally "to", "toward"; הֲדֹם רַגְלָיו "the footstool of his feet". So also v9 לְהַר קָדְשׁוֹ, literally "to the mountain of his holiness".
- **"Moses and Aaron among his priests, and Samuel among them that call upon his name; they called upon the LORD, and he answered them" → "Moses and Aaron were among his priests, and Samuel among those who call on his name; they called to Yahweh, and he answered them"** (v6) — Hebrew: no verb in the first two lines ("were" supplied); קֹרְאֵי שְׁמוֹ "those who call on his name" and קֹרִאים אֶל־יְהוָה "calling to Yahweh" are participles (the time is set by the context); וְהוּא יַעֲנֵם "and he, he answered them", an imperfect (repeatedly: "he would answer them").
- **"He spake unto them in the cloudy pillar" → "In the pillar of cloud he spoke to them"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּעַמּוּד עָנָן יְדַבֵּר אֲלֵיהֶם, in this order; יְדַבֵּר imperfect, as יַעֲנֵם in v6. **"the ordinance" → "the statute"** — Hebrew חֹק.
- **"Thou answeredst them, O LORD our God" → "Yahweh our Elohim, you answered them"** (v8) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵינוּ אַתָּה עֲנִיתָם, the address first; no "O" (no such word in the Hebrew).
- **"thou wast a God that forgavest them, though thou tookest vengeance of their inventions" → "a forgiving El you were to them, and one taking vengeance on their deeds"** (v8) — Hebrew אֵל נֹשֵׂא הָיִיתָ לָהֶם וְנֹקֵם עַל־עֲלִילוֹתָם. נֹשֵׂא, participle of נָשָׂא "lift up, bear", here "forgive" (as Exod 34:7 נֹשֵׂא עָוֹן); נֹקֵם participle of נָקַם "avenge"; וְ "and" (no "though"); עֲלִילוֹת "deeds" (as 1 Chr 16:8; whether the deeds are wrongs is said only by the vengeance). Whose deeds are meant (Moses', Aaron's, Samuel's, or the people's) the Hebrew does not say.
- **"worship at his holy hill" → "bow down at his holy mountain"** (v9) — Hebrew הַר "mountain".

### Notes
- **"Holy is he"** — three times (v3, 5, 9), the last as "holy is Yahweh our Elohim".
- **Enthronement psalm** — "Yahweh reigns" (v1), as Ps 93:1; 96:10; 97:1.
