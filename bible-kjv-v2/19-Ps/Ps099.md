# Psalm 99

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ reigns; let the peoples tremble!
He sits enthroned upon the ⟦cherub|keruvim|cherubim⟧; let the earth quake!

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is great in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
he is exalted over all the peoples.

[3] Let them praise your great and awesome name!
Holy is he!

[4] The King in his might loves justice;
you have established equity;
you have executed justice
and righteousness in ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[5] Exalt ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
worship at his footstool!
Holy is he!

[6] ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ were among his priests,
⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ also was among those who called upon his name.
They called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he answered them.

[7] In the pillar of the cloud he spoke to them;
they kept his testimonies
and the statute that he gave them.

[8] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you answered them;
you were ⟦el|an El|a God⟧ who forgave them,
but an avenger of their wrongdoings.

[9] Exalt ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and worship at his holy mountain;
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is holy!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 9) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 8, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v8) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — trisagion structure (v3, 5, 9); Yahweh's kingship (v1-5), Moses/Aaron/Samuel precedents (v6-8), closing exaltation (v9).

### Notes
- **Trisagion psalm** — "Holy is he" three times (v3, 5, 9).
