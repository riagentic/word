# Psalm 22

To the overseer. Upon "⟦aijeleth-shahar|Ayyelet HaShachar|The Doe of the Dawn⟧". A psalm of David.

[1] ⟦eli-eli|Eli, Eli, lamah azavtani — My El, my El, why have you forsaken me?|My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?⟧
Far from my salvation are the words of my roaring.

[2] My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, I call by day, and you do not answer,
and by night, and there is no silence for me.

[3] But you are holy,
dwelling in the praises of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] In you our fathers trusted;
they trusted, and you rescued them.

[5] To you they cried out and escaped;
in you they trusted and were not ashamed.

[6] But I am a worm and not a man,
a reproach of mankind and despised of the people.

[7] All who see me deride me;
they gape with the lip, they shake the head:

[8] "Roll it to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — let him rescue him;
let him deliver him, for he delights in him!"

[9] For you are the one who drew me from the belly,
making me trust upon the breasts of my mother.

[10] Upon you I was thrown from the womb;
from the belly of my mother you are my ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[11] Do not be far from me, for distress is near,
for there is none who helps.

[12] Many bulls have surrounded me;
the mighty ones of Bashan have encircled me.

[13] They have opened their mouth against me,
a lion tearing and roaring.

[14] Like water I am poured out,
and all my bones are parted;
my heart has become like wax,
it is melted in the midst of my inward parts.

[15] Dried up like a potsherd is my power,
and my tongue is made to cling to my jaws,
and into the dust of death you lay me.

[16] For dogs have surrounded me;
a congregation of evildoers has closed around me;
like a lion my hands and my feet.

[17] I count all my bones;
they look, they stare at me.

[18] They divide my garments among them,
and over my clothing they cast a lot.

[19] But you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do not be far off;
my strength, hasten to my help!

[20] Deliver my soul from the sword,
my only one from the hand of the dog!

[21] Save me from the mouth of the lion,
and from the horns of wild oxen you have answered me!

[22] I will recount your name to my brothers;
in the midst of the assembly I will praise you.

[23] You who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, praise him!
All the seed of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, honor him,
and be afraid of him, all the seed of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!

[24] For he has not despised and has not detested
the affliction of the afflicted,
and he has not hidden his face from him,
and when he cried for help to him, he heard.

[25] From you is my praise in a great assembly;
my vows I will repay before those who fear him.

[26] The humble shall eat and be sated;
those who seek him shall praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
may your heart live forever!

[27] They shall remember and turn back to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
all the ends of the earth,
and they shall bow down before you,
all the clans of the nations.

[28] For the kingship is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧'s,
and he rules over the nations.

[29] They have eaten and bowed down, all the fat ones of the earth;
before him shall bow all who go down to the dust,
and his soul he did not keep alive.

[30] A seed shall serve him;
it shall be recounted of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ to the generation.

[31] They shall come and tell his righteousness
to a people that shall be born, that he has acted.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew אֱלֹהַי, "my Elohim". **"God" → "El"** (v1, 10) — Hebrew אֵלִי *Eli*, "my El", not *Elohim*; in v1 the translation is joined to the Hebrew line as its gloss. **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v30) — Hebrew לַאדֹנָי.
- **"To the chief Musician upon Aijeleth Shahar, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. Upon "Ayyelet HaShachar". A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ (as Ps 19); עַל־אַיֶּלֶת הַשַּׁחַר, literally "upon the doe of the dawn": most likely the name of a tune; what it was is not known. לְדָוִד: לְ "of" (by, for or belonging to: not decided).
- **The Hebrew as it stands** (throughout) — the New Testament cites this psalm (v1 Matt 27:46; Mark 15:34; v18 John 19:24; v22 Heb 2:12; from memory); each verse here is translated from the Hebrew, nothing is levelled to the Greek quotations.
- **"why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring?" → "Far from my salvation are the words of my roaring"** (v1) — Hebrew רָחוֹק מִישׁוּעָתִי דִּבְרֵי שַׁאֲגָתִי, with no verb: literally "far from my salvation — the words of my roaring". "Are" is supplied; the line may also be read "(you are) far from my salvation, (from) the words of my roaring". "Why art thou" and "from" are not written. שַׁאֲגָה is the roaring of a lion (שָׁאַג, v13). In the cry עֲזַבְתָּנִי is the Hebrew *azavtani*; the Gospels give the cry in an Aramaic form (from memory).
- **"in the daytime, but thou hearest not; and in the night season, and am not silent" → "by day, and you do not answer, and by night, and there is no silence for me"** (v2) — Hebrew תַעֲנֶה "answer"; וְלֹא־דוּמִיָּה לִי "and there is no silence to me". "O" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel" → "dwelling in the praises of Israel"** (v3) — Hebrew יוֹשֵׁב תְּהִלּוֹת יִשְׂרָאֵל, a participle with no preposition: "dwelling (in)" or "sitting (enthroned) on" the praises. **"But"** (v3, 6, 19) — the וְ stands before the pronoun, set against what goes before.
- **"thou didst deliver them … were delivered … were not confounded" → "you rescued them … escaped … were not ashamed"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew וַתְּפַלְּטֵמוֹ (פִּלֵּט "rescue"); זָעֲקוּ "cried out"; נִמְלָטוּ (נִמְלַט "escape"); בּוֹשׁוּ "be ashamed".
- **"a reproach of men, and despised of the people" → "a reproach of mankind"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ "man" and אָדָם "mankind" stand side by side; חֶרְפָּה "reproach"; תּוֹלַעַת "worm".
- **"laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head, saying" → "deride me; they gape with the lip, they shake the head"** (v7) — Hebrew יַלְעִגוּ לִי (לָעַג לְ "deride"); יַפְטִירוּ בְשָׂפָה "they open wide with the lip"; יָנִיעוּ רֹאשׁ. "Saying" is not written.
- **"He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him" → "Roll it to Yahweh — let him rescue him"** (v8) — Hebrew גֹּל אֶל־יְהוָה: גֹּל as pointed is the imperative (or infinitive) of גָּלַל "roll", as Ps 37:5 "roll your way upon Yahweh": commit it to him. "It" is supplied; the object is not written. The Septuagint and Matt 27:43 have "he trusted" (from memory). **"seeing he delighted in him" → "for he delights in him"** — Hebrew כִּי חָפֵץ בּוֹ. יְפַלְּטֵהוּ "rescue", יַצִּילֵהוּ "deliver" (הִצִּיל).
- **"But thou art he that took me out of the womb: thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother's breasts" → "For you are the one who drew me from the belly, making me trust upon the breasts of my mother"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; גֹחִי, a participle found only here (from גִּיחַ "burst forth": "who brought me out"; uncertain); בֶּטֶן "belly" (v10 beside רֶחֶם "womb"); מַבְטִיחִי, hifil of בָּטַח "trust" (v4, 5).
- **"I was cast upon thee" → "Upon you I was thrown"** (v10) — Hebrew הָשְׁלַכְתִּי, hofal of הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw".
- **"trouble" → "distress"** (v11) — Hebrew צָרָה. **"none to help" → "none who helps"** — Hebrew עוֹזֵר, a participle.
- **"compassed me … strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round" → "surrounded me … the mighty ones of Bashan have encircled me"** (v12, 16) — Hebrew סָבַב "surround"; אַבִּירֵי בָשָׁן "the mighty ones of Bashan": "bulls" is not repeated; כִּתְּרוּנִי "encircled me" (the root of "crown").
- **"They gaped upon me with their mouths, as a ravening and a roaring lion" → "They have opened their mouth against me, a lion tearing and roaring"** (v13) — Hebrew פָּצוּ עָלַי פִּיהֶם; אַרְיֵה טֹרֵף וְשֹׁאֵג with no "as": the line puts the lion beside them. טָרַף "tear to pieces".
- **"out of joint … in the midst of my bowels" → "are parted … in the midst of my inward parts"** (v14) — Hebrew וְהִתְפָּרְדוּ "and they are parted, separated"; מֵעִים "inward parts". **"is like wax" → "has become like wax"** — Hebrew הָיָה. נָמֵס "melt".
- **"My strength is dried up … my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death" → "Dried up … is my power, and my tongue is made to cling to my jaws, and into the dust of death you lay me"** (v15) — Hebrew כֹּחַ "power"; מֻדְבָּק, hofal of דָּבַק "cling"; מַלְקוֹחָי "my jaws" (a dual; the same letters as מַלְקוֹחַ "booty", another sense); תִּשְׁפְּתֵנִי, an imperfect of שָׁפַת "set, lay".
- **"the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet" → "a congregation of evildoers has closed around me; like a lion my hands and my feet"** (v16) — Hebrew עֲדַת מְרֵעִים (עֵדָה "congregation"; מְרֵעִים "those who do evil"); הִקִּיפוּנִי "closed around me". The third line is כָּאֲרִי יָדַי וְרַגְלָי, as printed "like a lion (at) my hands and my feet", with no verb; it is translated as it stands. The reading "they pierced" (KJV) comes from the versions and from a written form כארו "they dug / pierced" with one letter more (Septuagint ὤρυξαν "they dug"; some Hebrew manuscripts and a scroll from the Judean desert: all from memory); the WLC prints כָּאֲרִי and marks no qere. "Pierced" is not in the verse.
- **"I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me" → "I count all my bones; they look, they stare at me"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲסַפֵּר, the verb "recount" of v22, here in the sense "count"; יַבִּיטוּ (הִבִּיט "look at"); יִרְאוּ־בִי, "they see on me", look on with pleasure.
- **"They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture" → "They divide my garments among them, and over my clothing they cast a lot"** (v18) — Hebrew יְחַלְּקוּ "divide"; בֶּגֶד "garment"; לְבוּשׁ "clothing"; יַפִּילוּ גוֹרָל, literally "they make a lot fall", singular.
- **"O my strength, haste thee to help me" → "my strength, hasten to my help"** (v19) — Hebrew אֱיָלוּתִי, found only here: "my strength" (as אֱיָל, Ps 88:4); uncertain. חוּשָׁה "hasten"; לְעֶזְרָתִי "to my help".
- **"my darling from the power of the dog" → "my only one from the hand of the dog"** (v20) — Hebrew יְחִידָתִי, feminine of יָחִיד "only one": the one life the speaker has, set beside "my soul"; מִיַּד "from the hand".
- **"for thou hast heard me from the horns of the unicorns" → "and from the horns of wild oxen you have answered me"** (v21) — Hebrew וּמִקַּרְנֵי רֵמִים עֲנִיתָנִי, "and from the horns … you have answered me"; the prayer turns here to what has been done. רְאֵם "wild ox". The Septuagint reads another word, "my lowliness" (from memory).
- **"I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation" → "I will recount your name to my brothers; in the midst of the assembly"** (v22, 25) — Hebrew אֲסַפְּרָה (סִפֵּר "recount"), a cohortative; קָהָל "assembly" (עֵדָה, v16, is "congregation").
- **"glorify him; and fear him" → "honor him, and be afraid of him"** (v23) — Hebrew כַּבְּדוּהוּ (כִּבֵּד "honor"); וְגוּרוּ מִמֶּנּוּ (גּוּר מִן "be afraid of"; יִרְאֵי is "you who fear"). **"seed"** kept — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **"nor abhorred … but when he cried unto him, he heard" → "and has not detested … and when he cried for help to him, he heard"** (v24) — Hebrew שִׁקַּץ "detest"; עֱנוּת "affliction", a noun found only here (of the root of עָנִי "afflicted"); וּבְשַׁוְּעוֹ "and in his crying for help".
- **"My praise shall be of thee in the great congregation: I will pay my vows" → "From you is my praise in a great assembly; my vows I will repay"** (v25) — Hebrew מֵאִתְּךָ "from (with) you"; בְּקָהָל רָב, no article; שִׁלֵּם נֶדֶר "repay a vow".
- **"The meek shall eat and be satisfied … your heart shall live for ever" → "The humble shall eat and be sated … may your heart live forever"** (v26) — Hebrew עֲנָוִים (עָנָו "humble"); יִשְׂבָּעוּ "be sated"; יְחִי, a jussive; לְבַבְכֶם "your (plural) heart".
- **"All the ends of the world … all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee" → "They shall remember and turn back … all the clans of the nations … bow down before you"** (v27) — Hebrew אַפְסֵי־אָרֶץ "the ends of the earth"; מִשְׁפְּחוֹת "clans"; הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down". The lines keep the Hebrew order (verb first, subject after). "Before you" as the MT prints (the Septuagint has "before him", from memory).
- **"For the kingdom is the LORD's: and he is the governor among the nations" → "the kingship is Yahweh's, and he rules over the nations"** (v28) — Hebrew הַמְּלוּכָה "the kingship"; וּמֹשֵׁל בַּגּוֹיִם, a participle, "and ruling over the nations"; "he" is supplied.
- **"All they that be fat upon earth shall eat and worship … and none can keep alive his own soul" → "They have eaten and bowed down, all the fat ones of the earth … and his soul he did not keep alive"** (v29) — Hebrew אָכְלוּ וַיִּשְׁתַּחֲווּ, a perfect and the narrative form (past); דִּשְׁנֵי־אֶרֶץ "the fat ones of the earth"; יִכְרְעוּ (כָּרַע "bow"); וְנַפְשׁוֹ לֹא חִיָּה, literally "and his soul he did not keep alive" (חִיָּה piel "let live"): whose soul is meant is not said. The Septuagint has "and my soul lives for him" (from memory).
- **"it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation" → "it shall be recounted of Adonai to the generation"** (v30) — Hebrew יְסֻפַּר, pual of סִפֵּר "recount"; לַאדֹנָי; לַדּוֹר "to the generation" ("coming" is not written).
- **"declare his righteousness … that he hath done this" → "tell his righteousness … that he has acted"** (v31) — Hebrew וְיַגִּידוּ (הִגִּיד "tell"); לְעַם נוֹלָד "to a people that shall be born"; כִּי עָשָׂה, with no object: "that he has acted (done it)".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v31 = Heb v32.
- **Cry from the cross** (v1) — quoted by Jesus (Matt 27:46, Mark 15:34) in Aramaic form.
- **v18 — dividing garments, lots** — quoted John 19:24.
- **Dense messianic prophecy of Passion** — verses 1, 6-8, 14-18 paralleled at crucifixion.
- **Ends in universal worship** — suffering individual → nations turn to Yahweh.
