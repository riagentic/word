# Psalm 108

A Song. A Psalm of David.

[1] My heart is steadfast, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!
I will sing and make melody with all my glory!

[2] Awake, O harp and lyre!
I will awake the dawn!

[3] I will give thanks to you, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, among the peoples;
I will sing praises to you among the nations.

[4] For your steadfast love is great above the heavens;
your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.

[5] Be exalted, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, above the heavens!
Let your glory be over all the earth!

[6] That your beloved ones may be delivered,
give salvation by your right hand and answer me!

[7] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has promised in his holiness:
"With exultation I will divide up Shechem
and portion out the Valley of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧.

[8] ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ is mine; ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ is mine;
⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is my helmet,
⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ my scepter.

[9] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is my washbasin;
upon Edom I cast my shoe;
over ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧ I shout in triumph."

[10] Who will bring me to the fortified city?
Who will lead me to Edom?

[11] Have you not rejected us, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you do not go out with our armies.

[12] Oh grant us help against the foe,
for vain is the salvation of man!

[13] With ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we shall do valiantly;
it is he who will tread down our foes.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 7, 11, 12, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — composite of Ps 57:7-11 and Ps 60:5-12; praise (v1-5) + oracle (v7-9) + plea (v10-13).

### Notes
- **Composite** — v1-5 from Ps 57:7-11; v6-13 from Ps 60:5-12.
