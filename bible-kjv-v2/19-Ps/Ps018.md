# Psalm 18

To the overseer. Of the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, of David, who spoke to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the words of this song in the day that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ delivered him from the palm of all his enemies and from the hand of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[1] And he said:

"I love you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, my strength.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is my crag and my stronghold and my rescuer,
my ⟦el|El|God⟧, my Rock, in whom I take refuge,
my shield and the horn of my salvation, my secure height.

[3] I call on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the praised one,
and from my enemies I am saved.

[4] The cords of death encompassed me,
and the torrents ⟦belial|of Beliyyaal|of worthlessness⟧ terrified me.

[5] The cords of Sheol surrounded me,
the snares of death came to meet me.

[6] In my distress I called on ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and to my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ I cried for help;
he heard my voice from his temple,
and my cry for help before him came into his ears.

[7] And the earth heaved and quaked,
and the foundations of the mountains trembled,
and they heaved, for he burned with anger.

[8] Smoke went up in his nose,
and fire from his mouth devoured;
coals were kindled from him.

[9] And he bowed the heavens and came down,
and a dark cloud was under his feet.

[10] And he rode on a ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ and flew,
and he swooped on the wings of the wind.

[11] He set darkness as his hiding place, around him his booth,
darkness of waters, thick clouds of the skies.

[12] From the brightness before him his thick clouds passed on,
hail and coals of fire.

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ thundered in the heavens,
and ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ gave his voice,
hail and coals of fire.

[14] And he sent his arrows and scattered them,
and lightnings, many, and threw them into confusion.

[15] And the channels of waters were seen,
and the foundations of the world were uncovered,
from your rebuke, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
from the breath of the wind of your nose.

[16] He sent from on high, he took me,
he drew me out from many waters.

[17] He delivered me from my strong enemy,
and from those who hate me, for they were stronger than I.

[18] They came to meet me in the day of my calamity,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ became a support to me.

[19] And he brought me out to the broad place,
he pulled me out, for he delighted in me.

[20] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ dealt with me according to my righteousness,
according to the purity of my hands he returned to me.

[21] For I have kept the ways of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and I have not done wickedly away from my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[22] For all his judgments are before me,
and his statutes I do not remove from me.

[23] And I was blameless with him,
and I kept myself from my iniquity.

[24] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ returned to me according to my righteousness,
according to the purity of my hands before his eyes.

[25] With the faithful one you show yourself faithful,
with the blameless mighty man you show yourself blameless.

[26] With the pure you show yourself pure,
and with the crooked you show yourself twisted.

[27] For you save an afflicted people,
and high eyes you make low.

[28] For you light my lamp;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ brightens my darkness.

[29] For by you I run at a raiding band,
and by my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ I leap a wall.

[30] The ⟦el|El|God⟧, his way is blameless;
the saying of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is refined;
he is a shield to all who take refuge in him.

[31] For who is ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ apart from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?
And who is a Rock except our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[32] The ⟦el|El|God⟧ who girds me with ability,
and he made my way blameless.

[33] He makes my feet like the does,
and on my high places he makes me stand.

[34] He teaches my hands for war,
and my arms bend a bow of bronze.

[35] And you gave me the shield of your salvation,
and your right hand sustains me,
and your humility makes me great.

[36] You enlarge my step under me,
and my ankles have not tottered.

[37] I pursued my enemies and I overtook them,
and I did not turn back until I had made an end of them.

[38] I struck them through, and they were not able to rise;
they fell under my feet.

[39] And you girded me with ability for war;
you made those who rose against me bow down under me.

[40] And my enemies, you gave them to me as a neck,
and those who hate me, I annihilated them.

[41] They cried for help, and there was no savior,
to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he did not answer them.

[42] And I ground them like dust on the face of the wind,
like the mud of the streets I emptied them out.

[43] You rescued me from the contentions of the people;
you set me for the head of nations;
a people that I did not know serve me.

[44] At the hearing of the ear they listen to me;
foreigners cringe before me.

[45] Foreigners wither,
and they come trembling from their fastnesses.

[46] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ lives, and blessed is my Rock,
and let the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation be high,

[47] the ⟦el|El|God⟧ who gives vengeances to me
and subdued peoples under me,

[48] my rescuer from my enemies;
even above those who rise against me you raise me high,
from the man of violence you deliver me.

[49] Therefore I will give thanks to you among the nations, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and to your name let me make music.

[50] Making great the salvations of his king,
and doing ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ to his ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧,
to David and to his seed forever."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6, 21, 28, 29, 31, 46) — Hebrew *Elohim* (v46 written אֱלוֹהֵי). **"God" → "El"** (v2, 30, 32, 47) — Hebrew אֵל *El*; in v30, 32, 47 with the article, הָאֵל "the El". **"God" → "Eloah"** (v31) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ. **"the Highest" → "Elyon"** (v13) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן.
- **The same song as 2 Sam 22** — the wording of 2 Sam 22 is used wherever the Hebrew is the same; every place where the Hebrew of the psalm differs is rendered as the psalm has it (listed at the end). The tenses follow 2 Sam 22: where the song tells of the rescue (v4–19, 37–42) the imperfects are given as past; each וַ is kept as "and".
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David, the servant of the LORD" → "To the overseer. Of the servant of Yahweh, of David"** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ, from the verb "oversee" (1 Chr 15:21; traditionally "to the chief musician"); לְעֶבֶד יְהוָה לְדָוִד, "of the servant of Yahweh, of David": לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to"; the heading has no word for "psalm" (מִזְמוֹר). עֶבֶד is "slave"; of Yahweh's man "servant". **"from the hand of all his enemies" → "from the palm of all his enemies"** — Hebrew מִכַּף; "from the hand of Shaul" is מִיַּד. The heading is v1 in the Hebrew.
- **"I will love thee, O LORD, my strength" → "And he said: I love you, Yahweh, my strength"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמַר stands at the head of the verse (KJV puts "And he said" in the heading). אֶרְחָמְךָ: the verb רָחַם, otherwise "have compassion" (piel), is found in the qal only here; "love" is its sense here. חֵזֶק "strength" is found only here.
- **"my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler … and my high tower" → "my crag and my stronghold and my rescuer, my El, my Rock, in whom I take refuge, my shield … my secure height"** (v2) — Hebrew סֶלַע "crag"; מְצוּדָה "stronghold"; מְפַלֵּט "rescuer"; צוּר "Rock" (capital where it names Yahweh, v2, 31, 46); אֶחֱסֶה־בּוֹ, literally "I take refuge in him"; מָגֵן "shield"; מִשְׂגָּב "secure height" (a high place out of reach).
- **"I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved" → "I call on Yahweh, the praised one, and from my enemies I am saved"** (v3) — Hebrew מְהֻלָּל אֶקְרָא יְהוָה, in this order: "praised — I call — Yahweh"; "worthy" is not written.
- **"The sorrows of death compassed me, and the floods of ungodly men made me afraid" → "The cords of death encompassed me, and the torrents of Beliyyaal (worthlessness) terrified me"** (v4) — Hebrew חֶבְלֵי־מָוֶת: חֶבֶל "cord" (another word of the same letters is "pang", whence "sorrows"); נַחֲלֵי בְלִיַּעַל, the common noun "worthlessness"; "men" is not written.
- **"The sorrows of hell compassed me about: the snares of death prevented me" → "The cords of Sheol surrounded me, the snares of death came to meet me"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל "Sheol"; קִדְּמוּנִי "came to meet, confronted" (so v18).
- **"cried unto my God … my cry came before him, even into his ears" → "to my Elohim I cried for help … my cry for help before him came into his ears"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲשַׁוֵּעַ "cry for help" and its noun שַׁוְעָה.
- **"shook and trembled … moved and were shaken, because he was wroth" → "heaved and quaked … trembled, and they heaved, for he burned with anger"** (v7) — Hebrew גָּעַשׁ (twice), רָעַשׁ, רָגַז; חָרָה לוֹ, literally "it burned to him". **"hills" → "mountains"** — Hebrew הָרִים.
- **"out of his nostrils … coals were kindled by it" → "in his nose … from him"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּאַפּוֹ; אַף is "nose" and "anger". מִמֶּנּוּ "from him" or "from it".
- **"darkness was under his feet" → "a dark cloud"** (v9) — Hebrew עֲרָפֶל. וַיֵּט, from נָטָה, here "bend down".
- **"cherub" → "keruv"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּרוּב. **"yea, he did fly" → "and he swooped"** — Hebrew וַיֵּדֶא, from דָּאָה "swoop" (of a bird of prey); 2 Sam 22:11 has וַיֵּרָא "he was seen".
- **"He made darkness his secret place; his pavilion round about him were dark waters" → "He set darkness as his hiding place, around him his booth, darkness of waters"** (v11) — Hebrew יָשֶׁת "he set" (no "and"); סֵתֶר "hiding place"; סֻכָּה "booth"; "as" is supplied. חֹשֶׁךְ and חֶשְׁכַת (from חֲשֵׁכָה) are two words for "darkness".
- **"his thick clouds passed, hail stones" → "his thick clouds passed on, hail"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew עָבָיו עָבְרוּ; בָּרָד "hail". The line "hail and coals of fire" stands in v12 and again in v13; it is not in 2 Sam 22:14, and some hold the second to be a copying repeat (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"also thundered … the Highest gave his voice" → "And Yahweh thundered … and Elyon gave his voice"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיַּרְעֵם; יִתֵּן קֹלוֹ.
- **"he shot out lightnings, and discomfited them" → "and lightnings, many, and threw them into confusion"** (v14) — Hebrew וּבְרָקִים רָב: רָב as written is "many, much"; KJV "shot out" takes it as a verb (רָבַב, as Gen 49:23 "shot", from memory). וַיְהֻמֵּם "threw them into confusion".
- **"were discovered at thy rebuke, O LORD, at the blast of the breath of thy nostrils" → "were uncovered, from your rebuke, Yahweh, from the breath of the wind of your nose"** (v15) — Hebrew מִגַּעֲרָתְךָ … מִנִּשְׁמַת רוּחַ אַפֶּךָ: מִן "from" both times; נְשָׁמָה "breath", רוּחַ "wind, breath, spirit".
- **"He sent from above"** (v16) — no object is written ("his hand" is understood). יַמְשֵׁנִי "drew me out" (Exod 2:10).
- **"from them which hated me: for they were too strong for me" → "and from those who hate me, for they were stronger than I"** (v17) — Hebrew אָמְצוּ מִמֶּנִּי.
- **"They prevented me … but the LORD was my stay" → "They came to meet me … and Yahweh became a support to me"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיְהִי … לְמִשְׁעָן, "became (lit. was for) a support"; "but" is וַ, "and". אֵיד "calamity".
- **"into a large place; he delivered me" → "to the broad place, he pulled me out"** (v19) — Hebrew לַמֶּרְחָב; יְחַלְּצֵנִי.
- **"rewarded me … recompensed me … the cleanness of my hands" → "dealt with me … returned to me … the purity of my hands"** (v20, 24) — Hebrew גָּמַל "deal with"; הֵשִׁיב "make return"; בֹּר "purity" (the root of "pure", v26). צֶדֶק "righteousness" (2 Sam 22 צְדָקָה, the same word in English).
- **"have not wickedly departed from my God" → "have not done wickedly away from my Elohim"** (v21) — Hebrew לֹא־רָשַׁעְתִּי מֵאֱלֹהָי; "departed" is not written.
- **"I did not put away his statutes from me" → "his statutes I do not remove from me"** (v22) — Hebrew לֹא־אָסִיר מֶנִּי, an imperfect.
- **"I was also upright before him" → "And I was blameless with him"** (v23) — Hebrew תָמִים עִמּוֹ. **"mine iniquity"** — Hebrew עָוֹן.
- **"With the merciful … with an upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright … with the froward thou wilt shew thyself froward" → "With the faithful one … with the blameless mighty man you show yourself blameless … with the crooked you show yourself twisted"** (v25, 26) — Hebrew חָסִיד, one who keeps *chesed*, with the verb of the same root; גְּבַר תָּמִים "a blameless mighty man" (גֶּבֶר; 2 Sam 22 גִּבּוֹר); עִקֵּשׁ "crooked"; תִּתְפַּתָּל "you show yourself twisted".
- **"thou wilt save the afflicted people; but wilt bring down high looks" → "For you save an afflicted people, and high eyes you make low"** (v27) — Hebrew עַם־עָנִי (no article); וְעֵינַיִם רָמוֹת "and high eyes".
- **"thou wilt light my candle … will enlighten my darkness" → "you light my lamp … brightens my darkness"** (v28) — Hebrew נֵר "lamp"; יַגִּיהַּ "brightens".
- **"by thee I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall" → "by you I run at a raiding band, and by my Elohim I leap a wall"** (v29) — Hebrew אָרֻץ גְּדוּד, with no preposition.
- **"As for God, his way is perfect: the word of the LORD is tried: he is a buckler to all those that trust in him" → "The El, his way is blameless; the saying of Yahweh is refined; he is a shield to all who take refuge in him"** (v30) — Hebrew תָּמִים; אִמְרָה "saying"; צְרוּפָה "refined"; חָסָה "take refuge".
- **"save the LORD … save our God" → "apart from Yahweh … except our Elohim"** (v31) — Hebrew מִבַּלְעֲדֵי, then זוּלָתִי: two words.
- **"It is God that girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect" → "The El who girds me with ability, and he made my way blameless"** (v32) — Hebrew הַמְאַזְּרֵנִי חָיִל; חַיִל "ability" (strength, as v39); וַיִּתֵּן, literally "and he gave", a narrative form.
- **"like hinds' feet, and setteth me upon my high places" → "like the does, and on my high places he makes me stand"** (v33) — Hebrew כָּאַיָּלוֹת; "feet" is not repeated.
- **"He teacheth my hands to war, so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms" → "He teaches my hands for war, and my arms bend a bow of bronze"** (v34) — Hebrew וְנִחֲתָה, from נָחַת "press down, bend" (not "break"), here feminine singular (2 Sam 22 masculine): the arms may be the subject, or the bow ("and a bow of bronze is bent by my arms"). נְחוּשָׁה "bronze".
- **"thy right hand hath holden me up, and thy gentleness hath made me great" → "your right hand sustains me, and your humility makes me great"** (v35) — Hebrew תִסְעָדֵנִי; עַנְוָה "humility" (of the root of עָנָו "humble"; 2 Sam 22 has "your answering").
- **"Thou hast enlarged my steps under me, that my feet did not slip" → "You enlarge my step under me, and my ankles have not tottered"** (v36) — Hebrew צַעַד singular; קַרְסֻלַּיִם "ankles", found only here and in 2 Sam 22.
- **"neither did I turn again till they were consumed" → "I did not turn back until I had made an end of them"** (v37) — Hebrew כַּלּוֹתָם.
- **"I have wounded them that they were not able to rise: they are fallen" → "I struck them through, and they were not able to rise; they fell"** (v38) — Hebrew מָחַץ "strike through"; all three verbs are imperfects of the told rescue.
- **"thou hast subdued under me" → "you made … bow down under me"** (v39) — Hebrew תַּכְרִיעַ. **"strength" → "ability"** — חַיִל.
- **"Thou hast also given me the necks of mine enemies; that I might destroy them that hate me" → "And my enemies, you gave them to me as a neck, and those who hate me, I annihilated them"** (v40) — Hebrew נָתַתָּה לִּי עֹרֶף: you made them turn their backs to me; אַצְמִיתֵם "I annihilated them".
- **"They cried, but there was none to save them: even unto the LORD" → "They cried for help, and there was no savior, to Yahweh"** (v41) — Hebrew יְשַׁוְּעוּ "they cried for help"; מוֹשִׁיעַ "savior"; עַל־יְהוָה "to (upon) Yahweh".
- **"Then did I beat them small as the dust before the wind: I did cast them out as the dirt in the streets" → "And I ground them like dust on the face of the wind, like the mud of the streets I emptied them out"** (v42) — Hebrew שָׁחַק "grind"; עַל־פְּנֵי־רוּחַ "on the face of the wind"; טִיט "mud"; אֲרִיקֵם "I emptied them out" (2 Sam 22 אֲדִקֵּם "I crushed them": one letter differs).
- **"from the strivings of the people; and thou hast made me the head of the heathen: a people whom I have not known shall serve me" → "from the contentions of the people; you set me for the head of nations; a people that I did not know serve me"** (v43) — Hebrew רִיבֵי עָם: עָם has no article and no "my" (2 Sam 22 "my people"); "the" is supplied. תְּשִׂימֵנִי "you set me". גּוֹיִם "nations".
- **"As soon as they hear of me, they shall obey me: the strangers shall submit themselves unto me" → "At the hearing of the ear they listen to me; foreigners cringe before me"** (v44) — Hebrew לְשֵׁמַע אֹזֶן; יִשָּׁמְעוּ לִי, "they let themselves be heard by me", that is, obey; בְּנֵי־נֵכָר, literally "sons of foreignness"; יְכַחֲשׁוּ־לִי, literally "they lie to me" (feign submission).
- **"The strangers shall fade away, and be afraid out of their close places" → "Foreigners wither, and they come trembling from their fastnesses"** (v45) — Hebrew נָבֵל "wither"; וְיַחְרְגוּ, a verb found only here, uncertain ("come trembling", "come out in fear"); מִסְגְּרוֹת "places shut in" (2 Sam 22 has וְיַחְגְּרוּ "they gird themselves").
- **"blessed be my rock; and let the God of my salvation be exalted" → "blessed is my Rock, and let the Elohim of my salvation be high"** (v46) — the first clause has no verb; וְיָרוּם "let … be high".
- **"It is God that avengeth me, and subdueth the people under me" → "the El who gives vengeances to me and subdued peoples under me"** (v47) — Hebrew הַנּוֹתֵן נְקָמוֹת לִי, plural; וַיַּדְבֵּר "and subdued", a narrative form; עַמִּים "peoples".
- **"He delivereth me from mine enemies: yea, thou liftest me up … thou hast delivered me from the violent man" → "my rescuer from my enemies; even above those who rise against me you raise me high, from the man of violence you deliver me"** (v48) — Hebrew מְפַלְּטִי "my rescuer" (a noun, as v2); אַף "even"; תְּרוֹמְמֵנִי; אִישׁ חָמָס "the man of violence".
- **"Therefore will I give thanks unto thee, O LORD, among the heathen, and sing praises unto thy name" → "Therefore I will give thanks to you among the nations, Yahweh, and to your name let me make music"** (v49) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ בַגּוֹיִם יְהוָה, in this order; אֲזַמֵּרָה is a cohortative ("let me make music"). Rom 15:9 cites the verse in its Greek form; the verse here follows the Hebrew.
- **"Great deliverance giveth he to his king; and sheweth mercy to his anointed, to David, and to his seed for evermore" → "Making great the salvations of his king, and doing *chesed* (steadfast love) to his *mashiach* (anointed), to David and to his seed forever"** (v50) — qere מַגְדִּיל "making great" (ketiv מגדל "a tower", which is the qere of 2 Sam 22:51); יְשׁוּעוֹת "salvations", plural; עֹשֶׂה, a participle; זֶרַע "seed".
- **Differences from 2 Sam 22** (our verse numbers of the psalm; "Sam" is the wording of 2 Sam 22) — heading: "who spoke" (Sam "And David spoke"); "from the hand of Shaul" (Sam "the palm"). v1: "I love you, Yahweh, my strength" is only in the psalm. v2: "my El, my Rock" (Sam "the Elohim of my Rock", v3); ### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to our v50 = Heb v51.
- **2 Samuel 22** — the same song stands in the book of Samuel, with many small differences. This psalm translates its own Hebrew; nothing is taken over from Samuel where the Hebrew differs.
- **Theophany** (v7–15) — earth, mountains, smoke, fire, cloud, thunder, hail.
- **"To David and to his seed forever"** (v50) — compare 2 Sam 7:12–16.
