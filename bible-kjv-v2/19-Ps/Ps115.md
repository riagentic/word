# Psalm 115

[1] Not to us, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, not to us,
but to your name give glory,
for the sake of your steadfast love and your faithfulness!

[2] Why should the nations say,
"Where is their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[3] Our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in the heavens;
he does all that he pleases.

[4] Their idols are silver and gold,
the work of human hands.

[5] They have mouths, but do not speak;
eyes, but do not see.

[6] They have ears, but do not hear;
noses, but do not smell.

[7] They have hands, but do not feel;
feet, but do not walk;
they do not make a sound in their throat.

[8] Those who make them become like them;
so do all who trust in them.

[9] O ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
He is their help and their shield.

[10] O house of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
He is their help and their shield.

[11] You who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
He is their help and their shield.

[12] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has remembered us; he will bless us;
he will bless the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
he will bless the house of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧;

[13] he will bless those who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
both the small and the great.

[14] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ give you increase,
you and your children!

[15] May you be blessed by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who made heaven and earth!

[16] The heavens are ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧ heavens,
but the earth he has given to the children of men.

[17] The dead do not praise ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧,
nor do any who go down into silence.

[18] But we will bless ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧
from this time forth and forevermore.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Yah"** (v17, 18) — shortened Yahweh; retained.
- **Poetic structure** — not to us (v1-2), idol polemic (v3-8), threefold call to trust (v9-11), blessing (v12-15), praise-vow (v16-18).

### Notes
- **Idol polemic** (v4-8) — satirizing lifeless images.
