# Psalm 8

To the overseer, upon the ⟦gittith|Gittit|Gittith⟧. A psalm of David.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, our Lord,
how mighty is your name in all the earth,
you who set your majesty upon the heavens!

[2] From the mouth of children and sucklings
you have founded strength
on account of your adversaries,
to make the enemy and the avenger cease.

[3] When I see your heavens, the works of your fingers,
the moon and the stars, which you have established —

[4] what is mortal man, that you remember him,
and the son of man, that you visit him?

[5] And you have made him lack a little of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and with glory and splendor you crown him.

[6] You make him rule over the works of your hands;
all things you have set under his feet:

[7] flocks and cattle, all of them,
and also the beasts of the field,

[8] the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea,
what passes along the paths of the seas.

[9] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, our Lord,
how mighty is your name in all the earth!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"O LORD our Lord" → "Yahweh, our Lord"** (v1, 9) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֲדֹנֵינוּ: the name, and אָדוֹן "lord" with "our" (not the form אֲדֹנָי); there is no "O".
- **"To the chief Musician upon Gittith, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer, upon the Gittit. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ, from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"); עַל־הַגִּתִּית "upon the Gittit": the meaning is unknown (taken as "of Gath": an instrument or a tune from Gath, or a "winepress" song from גַּת); לְדָוִד "of David": the לְ may say "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and is not decided here.
- **"how excellent is thy name" → "how mighty is your name"** (v1, 9) — Hebrew אַדִּיר "mighty" (as Exod 15:10). **"who hast set thy glory above the heavens" → "you who set your majesty upon the heavens"** — Hebrew הוֹד "majesty" (not כָּבוֹד "glory", v5); עַל "upon". אֲשֶׁר תְּנָה is difficult: תְּנָה is pointed as the imperative of נָתַן "give, set" ("set your majesty upon the heavens!"), which does not join well to אֲשֶׁר "who, which". The verse takes the line, as most versions do, as said of Yahweh ("you who set"); the imperative form is named here, and no letter is changed.
- **"Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thine enemies, that thou mightest still the enemy and the avenger" → "From the mouth of children and sucklings you have founded strength on account of your adversaries, to make the enemy and the avenger cease"** (v2) — Hebrew עוֹלְלִים "children", יֹנְקִים "sucklings"; יִסַּדְתָּ "you have founded"; עֹז "strength" (Matt 21:16 quotes the Greek "praise", as the Septuagint has it); לְמַעַן "on account of"; צוֹרְרֶיךָ "your adversaries" (as Ps 6:7); לְהַשְׁבִּית "to make cease"; מִתְנַקֵּם, hitpael, "one who takes vengeance for himself".
- **"When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers … which thou hast ordained" → "When I see your heavens, the works of your fingers … which you have established"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶרְאֶה "I see"; מַעֲשֵׂי, plural, "works"; כּוֹנָנְתָּה, from כּוֹנֵן "establish".
- **"What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him?" → "what is mortal man, that you remember him, and the son of man, that you visit him?"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man" beside בֶּן־אָדָם "son of man" (two words kept apart, as Job 7:17; 25:6); תִזְכְּרֶנּוּ "you remember him"; תִפְקְדֶנּוּ "you visit him" (attend to). Heb 2:6–8 quotes v4–6 from the Greek.
- **"For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels" → "And you have made him lack a little of Elohim"** (v5) — Hebrew וַתְּחַסְּרֵהוּ מְּעַט מֵאֱלֹהִים, "and you made him lack a little from Elohim": חִסֵּר "make lack", מְעַט "a little" (of degree, or "for a little while"), מִן "from, than". The word is אֱלֹהִים as printed, with its token; whether it means Elohim himself or divine beings ("gods") is not said by the verse. The Septuagint has "angels" (from memory), and Heb 2:7, 9 quotes the Greek; KJV follows it. **"and hast crowned him with glory and honour" → "and with glory and splendor you crown him"** — Hebrew כָּבוֹד "glory", הָדָר "splendor"; תְּעַטְּרֵהוּ, imperfect of עִטֵּר "crown" (the root of "surround" in Ps 5:12).
- **"Thou madest him to have dominion … thou hast put all things under his feet" → "You make him rule … all things you have set under his feet"** (v6) — Hebrew תַּמְשִׁילֵהוּ, hifil of מָשַׁל "rule", imperfect; כֹּל שַׁתָּה, perfect of שִׁית "set" (1 Cor 15:27 and Heb 2:8 quote this line).
- **"All sheep and oxen, yea, and the beasts of the field" → "flocks and cattle, all of them, and also the beasts of the field"** (v7) — Hebrew צֹנֶה, a form of צֹאן "flock (sheep and goats)"; אֲלָפִים "cattle"; כֻּלָּם "all of them"; וְגַם "and also"; בַּהֲמוֹת שָׂדָי "the beasts of the field" (שָׂדַי, a poetic form of שָׂדֶה).
- **"The fowl of the air … and whatsoever passeth through the paths of the seas" → "the birds of the heavens … what passes along the paths of the seas"** (v8) — Hebrew צִפּוֹר שָׁמַיִם, "bird" as a collective, "of the heavens"; עֹבֵר "passing", singular participle (the fish, or anything that passes); the Hebrew has no "and" before it.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v9 = Heb v10.
- **Creation psalm** — human dignity within the cosmos.
- **v2 cited by Jesus** (Matt 21:16).
- **v4–6 cited in Heb 2:6–8** — messianic application.
