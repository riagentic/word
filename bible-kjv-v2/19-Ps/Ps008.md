# Psalm 8

To the chief musician, upon the Gittith. A Psalm of David.

[1] O Yahweh, our Lord,
how majestic is your name in all the earth!
You have set your glory above the heavens.

[2] From the mouth of infants and nursing babes
you have established strength
because of your adversaries,
to silence the enemy and the avenger.

[3] When I consider your heavens, the work of your fingers,
the moon and the stars that you have set in place —

[4] what is man that you are mindful of him,
and the son of man that you care for him?

[5] Yet you have made him a little lower than the heavenly beings
and crowned him with glory and honor.

[6] You have given him dominion over the works of your hands;
you have put all things under his feet:

[7] all sheep and oxen,
and also the beasts of the field,

[8] the birds of the heavens and the fish of the sea,
whatever passes along the paths of the seas.

[9] O Yahweh, our Lord,
how majestic is your name in all the earth!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord" → "Yahweh, our Lord"** (v1, 9) — two divine names.
- **"angels" → "heavenly beings"** (v5) — Hebrew *elohim*; LXX reads "angels"; ambiguous — could mean God himself or divine beings. Heb 2:7 follows LXX "angels."

### Notes
- **Creation psalm** — human dignity within cosmos.
- **v2 cited by Jesus** (Matt 21:16).
- **v4-6 cited Heb 2:6-8** — messianic application.
- **Gittith** — musical term, perhaps "Gittite instrument."
