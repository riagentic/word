# Psalm 69

To the overseer. Upon ⟦shoshannim|Shoshannim|Lilies⟧. Of David.

[1] Save me, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
for waters have come to the soul.

[2] I have sunk in the mire of the depth, and there is no foothold;
I have come into the deep places of waters, and the current has swept over me.

[3] I am weary with my calling; my throat is parched;
my eyes are spent,
hoping for my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] More than the hairs of my head
are those who hate me without cause;
mighty are those who annihilate me,
those who are falsely my enemies;
what I did not rob, then I restore.

[5] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you know my folly,
and my guilts are not hidden from you.

[6] Let them not be ashamed because of me, those who wait for you,
⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts;
let them not be put to shame because of me, those who seek you,
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] For because of you I have borne reproach;
dishonor has covered my face.

[8] Estranged I have become to my brothers,
and a foreigner to the sons of my mother.

[9] For the zeal of your house has consumed me,
and the reproaches of those who reproach you have fallen upon me.

[10] And I wept in the fasting of my soul,
and it became reproaches to me.

[11] And I made my clothing sackcloth,
and I became to them a proverb.

[12] They muse on me, those who sit in the gate,
and the songs of those who drink strong drink.

[13] And I, my prayer is to you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, at a time of good pleasure;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in the abundance of your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
answer me in the truth of your salvation.

[14] Deliver me from the mud, and let me not sink;
let me be delivered from those who hate me
and from the deep places of waters.

[15] Let not the current of waters sweep over me,
and let not the depth swallow me,
and let not the well shut its mouth upon me.

[16] Answer me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for good is your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧;
according to the abundance of your compassion turn to me.

[17] And do not hide your face from your servant,
for I am in distress; quickly answer me.

[18] Come near to my soul, redeem it;
on account of my enemies ransom me.

[19] You know my reproach,
and my shame and my dishonor;
before you are all who harass me.

[20] Reproach has broken my heart, and I am sick;
and I waited for one to show grief, and there was none,
and for comforters, and I found none.

[21] And they put poison in my food,
and for my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.

[22] Let their table before them become a trap,
and for those at ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, a snare.

[23] Let their eyes grow dark from seeing,
and make their loins totter continually.

[24] Pour out upon them your indignation,
and let the burning of your anger overtake them.

[25] Let their encampment be made desolate;
in their tents let there be no one who dwells.

[26] For the one you yourself have struck, they have pursued,
and of the pain of your slain they recount.

[27] Put iniquity upon their iniquity,
and let them not come into your righteousness.

[28] Let them be blotted out of the book of life,
and with the righteous let them not be written.

[29] And I am afflicted and in pain;
your salvation, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, will set me on high.

[30] Let me praise the name of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with a song,
and let me make him great with thanksgiving.

[31] And it will be better to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ than an ox, a bull,
horned, hoofed.

[32] The humble have seen, they will rejoice;
you who seek ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and let your heart live.

[33] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ listens to the needy,
and his prisoners he has not despised.

[34] Let heavens and earth praise him,
the seas and everything that creeps in them.

[35] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will save ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
and build the cities of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,
and they will dwell there and possess it;

[36] and the seed of his servants will inherit it,
and those who love his name will abide in it.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 3, 5, 6, 13, 29, 30, 32, 35) — Hebrew *Elohim*; an Elohistic psalm (42–83), the names stand as printed.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v13, 16, 31, 33) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"O Lord GOD of hosts" → "Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (v6) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה צְבָאוֹת, all three words. No vocative "O" anywhere: the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. Upon Shoshannim. Of David"** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל־שׁוֹשַׁנִּים לְדָוִד; מְנַצֵּחַ, participle of the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "the chief musician"); שׁוֹשַׁנִּים "lilies", a tune or instrument (meaning uncertain); the word "psalm" is not in this heading; לְ in לְדָוִד may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and "Of David" leaves it open.
- **"the waters are come in unto my soul" → "waters have come to the soul"** (v1) — Hebrew בָאוּ מַיִם עַד־נָפֶשׁ, no article and no "my"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (here also read "throat, neck", as Jonah 2:5; the earlier "neck" interprets).
- **"I sink in deep mire, where there is no standing … where the floods overflow me" → "I have sunk in the mire of the depth, and there is no foothold; I have come into the deep places of waters, and the current has swept over me"** (v2) — Hebrew טָבַעְתִּי perfect "I have sunk"; בִּיוֵן מְצוּלָה "in the mire of the depth" (יָוֵן "mire", as Ps 40:2; מְצוּלָה "depth", as Exod 15:5); מָעֳמָד "a place to stand"; מַעֲמַקֵּי־מַיִם "the deep places of waters" (another word than מְצוּלָה); שִׁבֹּלֶת "current" (of a stream, also v15); שָׁטַף "sweep over" (of water; "rinse" in Lev 15 is the same verb of washing).
- **"I am weary of my crying: my throat is dried: mine eyes fail while I wait for my God" → "I am weary with my calling; my throat is parched; my eyes are spent, hoping for my Elohim"** (v3) — Hebrew יָגַעְתִּי בְקָרְאִי "I am weary with my calling" (קָרָא "call"); נִחַר "is parched" (lit. "is burned"); כָּלוּ עֵינַי "my eyes are spent" (כָּלָה "come to an end"); מְיַחֵל, participle of יִחֵל "hope" ("wait for" is קִוָּה, v6, 20). The lines follow the accents: "my eyes are spent" closes the first half of the verse.
- **"They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of mine head: they that would destroy me, being mine enemies wrongfully, are mighty: then I restored that which I took not away" → "More than the hairs of my head are those who hate me without cause; mighty are those who annihilate me, those who are falsely my enemies; what I did not rob, then I restore"** (v4) — Hebrew רַבּוּ "they are many" (more than); שֹׂנְאַי חִנָּם "those who hate me without cause" (חִנָּם "for nothing": with no payment, no ground); עָצְמוּ "are mighty"; מַצְמִיתַי, participle of הִצְמִית "annihilate" (as 2 Sam 22:41); אֹיְבַי שֶׁקֶר "my enemies of falsehood" (as Ps 35:19); גָּזַל "rob"; אָז אָשִׁיב, an imperfect after "then": "then I restore" (= I must restore; "then I restored", KJV, takes it as past). John 15:25 quotes "they hated me without cause" (ἐδωρεὰν, from memory); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"O God, thou knowest my foolishness; and my sins are not hid from thee" → "Elohim, you know my folly, and my guilts are not hidden from you"** (v5) — Hebrew אַתָּה יָדַעְתָּ "you, you know" (the pronoun stands, for weight); אִוֶּלֶת "folly"; אַשְׁמוֹתַי, plural of אַשְׁמָה "guilt" (not חַטָּאת "sin"); נִכְחָדוּ "are hidden".
- **"Let not them that wait on thee … be ashamed for my sake: let not those that seek thee be confounded for my sake" → "Let them not be ashamed because of me, those who wait for you … let them not be put to shame because of me, those who seek you"** (v6) — Hebrew אַל־יֵבֹשׁוּ בִי קֹוֶיךָ: בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", נִכְלַם "be put to shame" (two verbs, kept apart); קֹוֶיךָ from קִוָּה "wait for"; בִּי, lit. "in me" (through what happens to me); the Hebrew order, the verb first and the subject after it, is kept.
- **"Because for thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face" → "For because of you I have borne reproach; dishonor has covered my face"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי־עָלֶיךָ "for upon you" (on your account; לְמַעַן is "on account of", v18); חֶרְפָּה "reproach", כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (also v19; בֹּשֶׁת "shame" is v19).
- **"I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto my mother's children" → "Estranged I have become to my brothers, and a foreigner to the sons of my mother"** (v8) — Hebrew מוּזָר, hofal participle of זוּר, "one made a stranger", first in the line; נָכְרִי "foreigner"; לִבְנֵי אִמִּי "to the sons of my mother".
- **"For the zeal of thine house hath eaten me up" → "For the zeal of your house has consumed me"** (v9) — Hebrew קִנְאַת בֵּיתְךָ, קִנְאָה "jealousy, zeal" (here zeal for your house: the genitive is kept as in the Hebrew); אֲכָלָתְנִי, perfect of אָכַל "eat, consume". John 2:17 quotes the first line with a future, "will consume me" (καταφάγεται, from memory), Rom 15:3 the second line; the Hebrew perfect is kept. חֶרְפּוֹת חוֹרְפֶיךָ "the reproaches of those who reproach you" (one root).
- **"When I wept, and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach" → "And I wept in the fasting of my soul, and it became reproaches to me"** (v10) — Hebrew וָאֶבְכֶּה בַצּוֹם נַפְשִׁי, lit. "and I wept in the fasting of my soul" (or "I wept, my soul, with fasting"); "chastened" is not written: the Greek "I bowed down my soul" reads another verb (from memory); וַתְּהִי לַחֲרָפוֹת לִי "and it became reproaches (plural) to me".
- **"I made sackcloth also my garment; and I became a proverb to them" → "And I made my clothing sackcloth, and I became to them a proverb"** (v11) — Hebrew וָאֶתְּנָה "and I made" (lit. "gave"); "also" is not written; מָשָׁל "proverb" (a by-word).
- **"They that sit in the gate speak against me; and I was the song of the drunkards" → "They muse on me, those who sit in the gate, and the songs of those who drink strong drink"** (v12) — Hebrew יָשִׂיחוּ בִי, שִׂיחַ "muse" (talk over, ponder aloud; "speak against" interprets); the second line has no verb ("I was", "they make songs" are supplied by others): נְגִינוֹת "songs" (sung to strings, the word of the heading term בִּנְגִינוֹת; of a taunt song, Lam 3:14); שׁוֹתֵי שֵׁכָר "drinkers of strong drink".
- **"But as for me, my prayer is unto thee, O LORD, in an acceptable time: O God, in the multitude of thy mercy hear me, in the truth of thy salvation" → "And I, my prayer is to you, Yahweh, at a time of good pleasure; Elohim, in the abundance of your chesed, answer me in the truth of your salvation"** (v13) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי "and I"; עֵת רָצוֹן "a time of good pleasure" ("at" is supplied: no preposition; as Isa 49:8); בְּרָב־חַסְדֶּךָ "in the abundance of your chesed" (token; the earlier "steadfast love" was plain text); עֲנֵנִי "answer me"; בֶּאֱמֶת יִשְׁעֶךָ "in the truth of your salvation". The lines follow the accents ("in the abundance of your chesed" closes the first half).
- **"Deliver me out of the mire … let me be delivered from them that hate me" → "Deliver me from the mud … let me be delivered from those who hate me"** (v14) — Hebrew הַצִּילֵנִי מִטִּיט: טִיט "mud" (as Ps 40:2; יָוֵן "mire" is v2); אֶטְבָּעָה, cohortative "let me (not) sink"; אִנָּצְלָה, nifal cohortative of הִצִּיל "deliver".
- **"Let not the waterflood overflow me, neither let the deep swallow me up, and let not the pit shut her mouth upon me" → "Let not the current of waters sweep over me, and let not the depth swallow me, and let not the well shut its mouth upon me"** (v15) — Hebrew שִׁבֹּלֶת מַיִם "the current of waters"; מְצוּלָה "depth" (as v2); וְאַל … וְאַל "and let not … and let not"; בְּאֵר "well" (not בּוֹר "pit"); תֶּאְטַר "shut", found only here (root of אִטֵּר "bound", Judg 3:15).
- **"Hear me, O LORD; for thy lovingkindness is good: turn unto me according to the multitude of thy tender mercies" → "Answer me, Yahweh, for good is your chesed; according to the abundance of your compassion turn to me"** (v16) — Hebrew עֲנֵנִי "answer me" (as v13, 17); כִּי־טוֹב חַסְדֶּךָ (token); רַחֲמֶיךָ "your compassion" (רַחֲמִים); Hebrew order kept.
- **"for I am in trouble: hear me speedily" → "for I am in distress; quickly answer me"** (v17) — Hebrew כִּי־צַר־לִי, lit. "for it is narrow for me" (as Ps 31:9); מַהֵר עֲנֵנִי "quickly answer me". עַבְדְּךָ "your servant" (the word is "slave").
- **"Draw nigh unto my soul, and redeem it: deliver me because of mine enemies" → "Come near to my soul, redeem it; on account of my enemies ransom me"** (v18) — Hebrew קָרְבָה אֶל "come near to"; גְאָלָהּ "redeem it" (גָּאַל), פְּדֵנִי "ransom me" (פָּדָה): two verbs, kept apart; לְמַעַן "on account of".
- **"Thou hast known my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonour: mine adversaries are all before thee" → "You know my reproach, and my shame and my dishonor; before you are all who harass me"** (v19) — Hebrew אַתָּה יָדַעְתָּ (as v5); חֶרְפָּה "reproach", בֹּשֶׁת "shame", כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" — three words, three renderings; נֶגְדְּךָ "before you" (in front of you); צוֹרְרָי "those who harass me" (צָרַר, as Ps 31:11).
- **"Reproach hath broken my heart; and I am full of heaviness: and I looked for some to take pity, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none" → "Reproach has broken my heart, and I am sick; and I waited for one to show grief, and there was none, and for comforters, and I found none"** (v20) — Hebrew וָאָנוּשָׁה, from נוּשׁ, found only here: "be sick" (as the adjective אָנוּשׁ "incurable"; uncertain); וָאֲקַוֶּה לָנוּד "and I waited to (someone's) showing grief": קִוָּה "wait for", נוּד "sway" (the head, in grief: "show grief", as Job 2:11; "pity" interprets); "one" is supplied; וָאַיִן "and there was none"; "but" is "and" in each place.
- **"They gave me also gall for my meat; and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink" → "And they put poison in my food, and for my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנוּ "and they gave, put"; בְּבָרוּתִי "in my food" (בָּרוּת only here); רֹאשׁ "poison", a poisonous plant (Deut 29:18; which plant is not known; "gall" is the traditional English); יַשְׁקוּנִי "they gave me to drink"; חֹמֶץ "vinegar" (soured wine). Matt 27:34, 48 and John 19:29 tell of wine mixed with gall (χολή) and of sour wine (ὄξος) at the cross (from memory); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"Let their table become a snare before them: and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap" → "Let their table before them become a trap, and for those at shalom, a snare"** (v22) — Hebrew יְהִי־שֻׁלְחָנָם לִפְנֵיהֶם לְפָח וְלִשְׁלוֹמִים לְמוֹקֵשׁ: פַּח "trap" (the fowler's), מוֹקֵשׁ "snare"; שְׁלוֹמִים, plural of שָׁלוֹם, "those at peace" (or "their peace, their well-being"; KJV paraphrases "that which should have been for their welfare"; the token marks the noun). Rom 11:9 quotes the verse after the Greek, which reads "for a recompense" (as from שִׁלּוּמִים) and adds "a stumbling block" (from memory); the Hebrew is kept.
- **"Let their eyes be darkened, that they see not; and make their loins continually to shake" → "Let their eyes grow dark from seeing, and make their loins totter continually"** (v23) — Hebrew תֶּחְשַׁכְנָה עֵינֵיהֶם מֵרְאוֹת "let their eyes grow dark from seeing" (so that they do not see); הַמְעַד, hifil imperative of מָעַד "totter" (as 2 Sam 22:37), spoken to Elohim; תָּמִיד "continually". Rom 11:10 has "bend their back always" (the Greek, from memory).
- **"Pour out thine indignation upon them, and let thy wrathful anger take hold of them" → "Pour out upon them your indignation, and let the burning of your anger overtake them"** (v24) — Hebrew זַעַם "indignation" (verb זָעַם "be indignant", Ps 7:11); חֲרוֹן אַפְּךָ "the burning of your anger" (as Deut 13:17); יַשִּׂיגֵם "let it overtake them" (הִשִּׂיג). Hebrew order kept.
- **"Let their habitation be desolate; and let none dwell in their tents" → "Let their encampment be made desolate; in their tents let there be no one who dwells"** (v25) — Hebrew טִירָתָם "their encampment" (טִירָה, Gen 25:16: a walled camp of herdsmen); נְשַׁמָּה, nifal participle of שָׁמֵם, "made desolate"; יֹשֵׁב "one who dwells". Acts 1:20 quotes the verse in the singular, "his homestead" (ἔπαυλις, from memory); the Hebrew plural is kept.
- **"For they persecute him whom thou hast smitten; and they talk to the grief of those whom thou hast wounded" → "For the one you yourself have struck, they have pursued, and of the pain of your slain they recount"** (v26) — Hebrew כִּי־אַתָּה אֲשֶׁר־הִכִּיתָ רָדָפוּ, lit. "for you — whom you have struck, they have pursued": אַתָּה stands first for weight; "the one" is supplied (the relative has no noun); הִכָּה "strike"; רָדַף "pursue", perfect; מַכְאוֹב "pain"; חֲלָלֶיךָ "your slain" (חָלָל, one pierced, fatally wounded); יְסַפֵּרוּ "they recount" (the Greek reads "they added", יֹסִיפוּ, from memory).
- **"Add iniquity unto their iniquity" → "Put iniquity upon their iniquity"** (v27) — Hebrew תְּנָה־עָוֹן עַל־עֲוֹנָם, נָתַן "give, put" ("add" interprets); עָוֹן "iniquity" (the guilt and what it brings: both are meant); בּוֹא בְּ "come into".
- **"of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous" → "of the book of life, and with the righteous let them not be written"** (v28) — Hebrew מִסֵּפֶר חַיִּים, חַיִּים "life" (no article: "the living" would be הַחַיִּים); מָחָה "blot out" (of writing); Hebrew order of the second line kept.
- **"But I am poor and sorrowful: let thy salvation, O God, set me up on high" → "And I am afflicted and in pain; your salvation, Elohim, will set me on high"** (v29) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי "and I"; עָנִי "afflicted"; כוֹאֵב "in pain" (participle); תְּשַׂגְּבֵנִי, imperfect of שִׂגֵּב "set on high" (also read as a wish, "let it set me on high").
- **"I will praise … and will magnify him" → "Let me praise … and let me make him great"** (v30) — Hebrew אֲהַלְלָה … וַאֲגַדְּלֶנּוּ, cohortatives; גִּדֵּל "make great"; בְתוֹדָה "with thanksgiving".
- **"This also shall please the LORD better than an ox or bullock that hath horns and hoofs" → "And it will be better to Yahweh than an ox, a bull, horned, hoofed"** (v31) — Hebrew וְתִיטַב לַיהוָה מִשּׁוֹר פָּר, יָטַב "be good" with מִן "more than": "be better"; "please" interprets; "or" is not written: שׁוֹר "ox", פָּר "bull" stand side by side; מַקְרִן מַפְרִיס, hifil participles, "putting out horns, parting the hoof" (a full-grown animal, clean by Lev 11:3); "this" is not written ("it", the song of v30).
- **"The humble shall see this, and be glad: and your heart shall live that seek God" → "The humble have seen, they will rejoice; you who seek Elohim, and let your heart live"** (v32) — Hebrew רָאוּ, perfect "they have seen" (with no object); עֲנָוִים "humble"; יִשְׂמָחוּ "they will rejoice" (or "let them rejoice"); דֹּרְשֵׁי אֱלֹהִים "seekers of Elohim", a call to them; וִיחִי לְבַבְכֶם "and let your heart live" (as Ps 22:26).
- **"For the LORD heareth the poor, and despiseth not his prisoners" → "For Yahweh listens to the needy, and his prisoners he has not despised"** (v33) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵעַ אֶל "listens to" (participle); אֶבְיוֹנִים "needy"; אֲסִירָיו "his prisoners" (the earlier "his own people who are prisoners" adds); בָזָה, perfect "has (not) despised".
- **"Let the heaven and earth praise him, the seas, and every thing that moveth therein" → "Let heavens and earth praise him, the seas and everything that creeps in them"** (v34) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם וָאָרֶץ, no article; רֹמֵשׂ "creeps" (רָמַשׂ, as Gen 1:21; of all that moves about).
- **"For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah: that they may dwell there, and have it in possession" → "For Elohim will save Zion and build the cities of Judah, and they will dwell there and possess it"** (v35) — Hebrew וְיָשְׁבוּ שָׁם וִירֵשׁוּהָ "and they will dwell there and possess it": no subject ("people") is written; יָרַשׁ "possess"; בָּנָה "build" ("up" is not written).
- **"The seed also of his servants shall inherit it: and they that love his name shall dwell therein" → "and the seed of his servants will inherit it, and those who love his name will abide in it"** (v36) — Hebrew וְזֶרַע "and the seed"; נָחַל "inherit"; יִשְׁכְּנוּ, שָׁכַן "abide" (beside יָשַׁב "dwell", v35).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v36 = Heb v37.
- **The Hebrew as it stands** — the New Testament quotes this psalm (v4 John 15:25; v9 John 2:17, Rom 15:3; v21 Matt 27:34, 48, John 19:29; v22–23 Rom 11:9–10; v25 Acts 1:20; from memory), mostly after the Greek; each verse here is translated from the Hebrew, nothing levelled to the quotations (the differences are at each verse above).
- **Structure** — a cry from deep waters (v1–21), a curse on the enemies (v22–28), a turn to praise (v29–36).
