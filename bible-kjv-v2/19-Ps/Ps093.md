# Psalm 93

[1] Yahweh reigns; he is robed in majesty;
Yahweh is robed; he has put on strength as his belt.
Indeed, the world is established; it shall never be moved.

[2] Your throne is established from of old;
you are from everlasting.

[3] The floods have lifted up, O Yahweh,
the floods have lifted up their voice;
the floods lift up their roaring.

[4] Mightier than the thunders of many waters,
mightier than the waves of the sea,
Yahweh on high is mighty!

[5] Your testimonies are very sure;
holiness befits your house,
O Yahweh, forevermore.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 4, 5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — enthronement proclamation (v1-2), sea-chaos imagery subdued (v3-4), holiness of house (v5).

### Notes
- **Enthronement psalm** (one of Pss 93, 95-99).
- **"Yahweh reigns"** — opening of each enthronement psalm.
