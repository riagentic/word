# Psalm 93

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ reigns; he has put on exaltation;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has put on — with strength he has girded himself;
yes, the world is established; it will not be shaken.

[2] Your throne is established from long ago;
from of old you are.

[3] The rivers have lifted up, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
the rivers have lifted up their voice;
the rivers lift up their pounding.

[4] More than the voices of many waters,
mighty breakers of the sea,
mighty on high is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] Your testimonies are very sure;
to your house holiness is fitting,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for length of days.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 4, 5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"The LORD reigneth, he is clothed with majesty; the LORD is clothed with strength, wherewith he hath girded himself" → "Yahweh reigns; he has put on exaltation; Yahweh has put on — with strength he has girded himself"** (v1) — Hebrew מָלָךְ, a perfect: "reigns" (also read "has become king"); גֵּאוּת "exaltation" (of the root of גָּאָה "be exalted", Exod 15:1; of the proud "pride", Ps 17:10); לָבֵשׁ "put on" (clothe oneself), twice, the second time with no object written: עֹז "strength" belongs to the next verb, הִתְאַזָּר "he has girded himself".
- **"the world also is stablished, that it cannot be moved" → "yes, the world is established; it will not be shaken"** (v1) — Hebrew אַף "yes, also"; תִּכּוֹן nifal of כּוּן "be established"; תֵּבֵל "world"; בַּל־תִּמּוֹט, מוֹט nifal "be shaken" (בַּל a poetic "not").
- **"Thy throne is established of old: thou art from everlasting" → "Your throne is established from long ago; from of old you are"** (v2) — Hebrew נָכוֹן "established" first; מֵאָז "from long ago" (lit. "from then") beside מֵעוֹלָם "from of old": two words; "from of old you are" keeps the Hebrew order.
- **"The floods … lift up their waves" → "The rivers … lift up their pounding"** (v3) — Hebrew נְהָרוֹת "rivers" (נָהָר), three times; נָשְׂאוּ perfect twice, יִשְׂאוּ imperfect once ("lift up"); דָּכְיָם, from דֳּכִי, found only here: the pounding (crashing) of the waters, from דָּכָה "crush"; uncertain.
- **"The LORD on high is mightier than the noise of many waters, yea, than the mighty waves of the sea" → "More than the voices of many waters, mighty breakers of the sea, mighty on high is Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew in this order: מִקֹּלוֹת מַיִם רַבִּים "more than the voices (sounds) of many waters" (קוֹל "voice", as v3); אַדִּירִים מִשְׁבְּרֵי־יָם "mighty, the breakers of the sea", in apposition to the waters (KJV carries the "than" over to them; "yea" is not written); אַדִּיר "mighty" once more of Yahweh; בַּמָּרוֹם "on high".
- **"Thy testimonies are very sure: holiness becometh thine house, O LORD, for ever" → "Your testimonies are very sure; to your house holiness is fitting, Yahweh, for length of days"** (v5) — Hebrew עֵדֹתֶיךָ "your testimonies"; נֶאֶמְנוּ "are sure" (נֶאֱמַן, as 2 Sam 7:16); לְבֵיתְךָ "to your house" first; נַאֲוָה "is fitting" (נָאָה); לְאֹרֶךְ יָמִים "for length of days" (as Ps 91:16; 23:6), not לְעוֹלָם "forever".

### Notes
- **No heading** — the psalm has none in the Hebrew.
- **Yahweh reigns** — the cry opens also Ps 97 and 99 (Ps 96:10).
