## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 6, 7, 11, 12) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 7, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v1, 20) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ, "praise Yah", with its token (in v1 written as two words, הַלְלוּ יָהּ).
- **"for it is good to sing praises unto our God; for it is pleasant; and praise is comely" → "For it is good to make music to our Elohim; for it is pleasant; praise is fitting"** (v1) — Hebrew זַמְּרָה, infinitive of זִמֵּר "make music"; כִּי־נָעִים "for it is pleasant" (נָעִים may also go with what follows: "for pleasant, fitting is praise"); נָאוָה "fitting"; תְהִלָּה "praise". There is no "and".
- **"The LORD doth build up Jerusalem: he gathereth together the outcasts of Israel" → "Yahweh builds Jerusalem; the driven-away ones of Israel he gathers"** (v2) — Hebrew בּוֹנֵה, a participle, "builds" ("up" is not written); נִדְחֵי, nifal participle of נָדַח, "driven-away ones" (as Deut 30:4); יְכַנֵּס "he gathers".
- **"He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds" → "He heals the broken of heart and binds up their hurts"** (v3) — Hebrew הָרֹפֵא לִשְׁבוּרֵי לֵב "the one healing the broken of heart"; עַצְּבוֹתָם "their hurts" (עַצֶּבֶת, a pain or grief; here what is bound up).
- **"He telleth the number of the stars; he calleth them all by their names" → "He counts out a number for the stars; to all of them he calls names"** (v4) — Hebrew מוֹנֶה מִסְפָּר לַכּוֹכָבִים, lit. "counting a number for the stars" (מָנָה "count"); לְכֻלָּם שֵׁמוֹת יִקְרָא "to all of them names he calls" ("their" is not written).
- **"and of great power: his understanding is infinite" → "and abundant in power; to his understanding there is no number"** (v5) — Hebrew רַב־כֹּחַ "abundant in power"; לִתְבוּנָתוֹ אֵין מִסְפָּר, word for word, the same מִסְפָּר "number" as v4. אֲדוֹנֵינוּ "our Lord" is אָדוֹן with a suffix (not אֲדֹנָי), plain text, as Ps 8:1.
- **"The LORD lifteth up the meek: he casteth the wicked down to the ground" → "Yahweh upholds the humble; he makes the wicked low, down to the earth"** (v6) — Hebrew מְעוֹדֵד "upholds" (as Ps 146:9; the sense is not certain: relieve, restore); עֲנָוִים "humble" (not "meek"); מַשְׁפִּיל "makes low" (as 1 Sam 2:7); עֲדֵי־אָרֶץ "down to the earth".
- **"Sing unto the LORD with thanksgiving; sing praise upon the harp unto our God" → "Sing to Yahweh with thanksgiving; make music to our Elohim with the lyre"** (v7) — Hebrew עֱנוּ, from ענה "sing in answer" (as Num 21:17; the same letters as "answer"); בְּתוֹדָה "with thanksgiving"; זַמְּרוּ "make music"; כִּנּוֹר "lyre" (not "harp": that is נֵבֶל).
- **"Who covereth the heaven with clouds … who maketh grass to grow upon the mountains" → "who covers the heavens with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who makes the mountains sprout grass"** (v8) — Hebrew three participles with the article ("the one who covers …"), set after "our Elohim" of v7; שָׁמַיִם plural; מָטָר "rain"; הַמַּצְמִיחַ הָרִים חָצִיר, הִצְמִיחַ "cause to spring up" with two objects: "makes the mountains sprout grass" (not "upon": no preposition is written).
- **"He giveth to the beast his food, and to the young ravens which cry" → "who gives to the beast its food, to the young of the raven which call"** (v9) — Hebrew נוֹתֵן, a participle; לַחְמָהּ "its food", lit. "its bread" (לֶחֶם; "bread" of an animal would mislead in English); לִבְנֵי עֹרֵב, lit. "to the sons of the raven": "young" because animals are meant; אֲשֶׁר יִקְרָאוּ "which call" (קָרָא; "cry" is another word). There is no "and".
- **"He delighteth not in the strength of the horse: he taketh not pleasure in the legs of a man" → "Not in the might of the horse does he delight; not in the legs of the man is he pleased"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא … לֹא stand first; גְּבוּרָה "might"; חָפֵץ בְּ "delight in"; שׁוֹקֵי הָאִישׁ "the legs of the man" (שׁוֹק "leg"; the article, as with "the horse"); רָצָה "be pleased with".
- **"The LORD taketh pleasure in them that fear him, in those that hope in his mercy" → "Yahweh is pleased with those who fear him, with those who hope for his chesed"** (v11) — Hebrew רוֹצֶה "is pleased with" (as v10); הַמְיַחֲלִים לְחַסְדּוֹ "those who hope for his chesed" (as Ps 33:18). The earlier "but" is not written.
- **"Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Zion" → "Laud Yahweh, Jerusalem! Praise your Elohim, Zion!"** (v12) — Hebrew שַׁבְּחִי (שִׁבַּח "laud", as Ps 117:1, where it stands beside הִלֵּל) and הַלְלִי (הִלֵּל "praise"): two verbs kept apart; both are feminine singular, to the city; "O" is not written.
- **"For he hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; he hath blessed thy children within thee" → "For he has made strong the bars of your gates; he has blessed your sons in your midst"** (v13) — Hebrew חִזַּק "made strong" (piel of חָזַק); בָּנַיִךְ "your sons"; בְּקִרְבֵּךְ "in your midst".
- **"He maketh peace in thy borders, and filleth thee with the finest of the wheat" → "He makes your border shalom; with the fat of wheat he satisfies you"** (v14) — Hebrew הַשָּׂם־גְּבוּלֵךְ שָׁלוֹם, lit. "who sets your border (as) shalom": no "in"; גְּבוּל, singular, "border" (also the land within it); חֵלֶב חִטִּים "the fat of wheat" (= the best, as Deut 32:14; Ps 81:16); יַשְׂבִּיעֵךְ "he satisfies you"; there is no "and".
- **"He sendeth forth his commandment upon earth: his word runneth very swiftly" → "He sends his saying to the earth; very swiftly his word runs"** (v15) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁלֵחַ אִמְרָתוֹ אָרֶץ: אִמְרָה "saying" (not "commandment"); אֶרֶץ without a preposition, "to the earth"; עַד־מְהֵרָה, lit. "unto swiftness".
- **"he scattereth the hoarfrost like ashes" → "frost like ashes he scatters"** (v16) — Hebrew כְּפוֹר "frost" (as Job 38:29); אֵפֶר "ashes"; the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"He casteth forth his ice like morsels" → "He throws his ice like bits"** (v17) — Hebrew מַשְׁלִיךְ "throws"; קֶרַח "ice"; פִּתִּים "bits" (פַּת, a broken piece; hail is meant); קָרָה "cold"; מִי יַעֲמֹד "who can stand" (lit. "who will stand").
- **"he causeth his wind to blow, and the waters flow" → "he makes his wind blow, the waters flow"** (v18) — Hebrew יַשֵּׁב רוּחוֹ יִזְּלוּ־מָיִם, no "and"; רוּחַ "wind" (the same word as "spirit"). "Them" are the snow, frost and ice of v16–17.
- **"He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel" → "He tells his words to Jacob, his statutes and his ordinances to Israel"** (v19) — Hebrew מַגִּיד (הִגִּיד) "tells"; the qere דְּבָרָיו "his words" is followed; the written text (ketiv) דברו is "his word"; מִשְׁפָּטִים of rulings given "ordinances" (the חֻקִּים וּמִשְׁפָּטִים pair, as in Deuteronomy).
- **"He hath not dealt so with any nation: and as for his judgments, they have not known them" → "He has not done so for any nation, and ordinances they have not known"** (v20) — Hebrew לֹא עָשָׂה כֵן לְכָל־גּוֹי "he has not done so for any nation" (the earlier "other" is not written); וּמִשְׁפָּטִים, without "his" or the article; בַּל־יְדָעוּם "they have not known them" ("them" is the suffix, left out in English after the object).

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew; our verse numbers are the Hebrew ones.
- **Structure** — Yahweh rebuilds and heals (v1–6), feeds the creatures and delights in those who fear him (v7–11), Jerusalem called to praise for its peace, the weather and the word given to Israel (v12–20).
