# Psalm 147

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
For it is good to sing praises to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
for it is pleasant, and a song of praise is fitting.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ builds up ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
he gathers the outcasts of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] He heals the brokenhearted
and binds up their wounds.

[4] He determines the number of the stars;
he gives to all of them their names.

[5] Great is our Lord, and abundant in power;
his understanding is beyond measure.

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ lifts up the humble;
he casts the wicked to the ground.

[7] Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with thanksgiving;
make melody to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ on the lyre!

[8] He covers the heavens with clouds;
he prepares rain for the earth;
he makes grass grow on the hills.

[9] He gives to the beasts their food,
and to the young ravens that cry.

[10] His delight is not in the strength of the horse,
nor his pleasure in the legs of a man,

[11] but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ takes pleasure in those who fear him,
in those who hope in his steadfast love.

[12] Praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, O ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!
Praise your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, O ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!

[13] For he strengthens the bars of your gates;
he blesses your children within you.

[14] He makes peace in your borders;
he fills you with the finest of the wheat.

[15] He sends out his command to the earth;
his word runs swiftly.

[16] He gives snow like wool;
he scatters frost like ashes.

[17] He hurls down his crystals of ice like crumbs;
who can stand before his cold?

[18] He sends out his word, and melts them;
he makes his wind blow and the waters flow.

[19] He declares his word to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
his statutes and rules to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[20] He has not dealt thus with any other nation;
they do not know his rules.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 6, 7, 11, 12) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 7, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — praise for Jerusalem's rebuilding (v1-6), natural provision (v7-11), Zion addressed directly (v12-20).
