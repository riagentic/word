# Psalm 13

To the chief musician. A Psalm of David.

[1] How long, O Yahweh? Will you forget me forever?
How long will you hide your face from me?

[2] How long must I take counsel in my soul,
have sorrow in my heart all the day?
How long shall my enemy be exalted over me?

[3] Consider and answer me, O Yahweh my *Elohim*;
light up my eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death,

[4] lest my enemy say, "I have prevailed over him,"
lest my foes rejoice because I am shaken.

[5] But I have trusted in your steadfast love;
my heart shall rejoice in your salvation.

[6] I will sing to Yahweh,
because he has dealt bountifully with me.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 6) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — four "how long" laments (v1-2) set as parallel lines; pivot to trust (v5-6) as its own stanza.

### Notes
- **Classic lament** — four "how longs" move to trust.
- **Short but dense** — pattern of lament moving to praise.
