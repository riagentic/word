# Psalm 129

A song of the ascents.

[1] "Much have they harassed me from my youth" —
let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ now say —

[2] "much have they harassed me from my youth,
yet they have not overcome me.

[3] On my back the plowers plowed;
they made long their furrows."

[4] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is righteous;
he has cut the ropes of the wicked.

[5] Let them be ashamed and turned backward,
all who hate ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[6] Let them be like the grass of the roofs,
which, before one pulls it out, has dried up,

[7] with which the reaper has not filled his palm,
nor the binder of sheaves his lap.

[8] And those who pass by have not said,
"The blessing of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to you!
We bless you in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees" → "A song of the ascents"** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת, the first words of v1; מַעֲלָה "a going up, a step", with the article.
- **"Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth, may Israel now say" → "Much have they harassed me from my youth — let Israel now say"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew רַבַּת "much, many times"; צְרָרוּנִי, צָרַר "harass, show hostility" (as Ps 23:5 צֹרְרָי; עִנָּה is "afflict"); יֹאמַר־נָא, a wish, with נָא "now".
- **"yet they have not prevailed against me" → "yet they have not overcome me"** (v2) — Hebrew גַּם לֹא־יָכְלוּ לִי, literally "also they were not able for me" (יָכֹל לְ "overcome", as Gen 32:25; גָּבַר is "prevail").
- **"The plowers plowed upon my back" → "On my back the plowers plowed"** (v3) — the Hebrew order (עַל־גַּבִּי first). **"they made long their furrows"** kept — Hebrew הֶאֱרִיכוּ לְמַעֲנִיתָם: the written text (ketiv) has למענותם, the reading (qere) לְמַעֲנִיתָם; two spellings of the plural of מַעֲנָה "furrow", the meaning does not differ. The לְ before the object is not rendered.
- **"he hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked" → "he has cut the ropes of the wicked"** (v4) — Hebrew קִצֵּץ "cut (in pieces)"; עֲבוֹת "rope" (as Exod 28:14; after v3 the ropes of the plow team may be meant); רְשָׁעִים "wicked".
- **"Let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Zion" → "Let them be ashamed and turned backward, all who hate Zion"** (v5) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ וְיִסֹּגוּ אָחוֹר כֹּל שֹׂנְאֵי צִיּוֹן, the Hebrew order: the wish first, "all" at the end. בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"; נָסוֹג אָחוֹר "be turned backward".
- **"Let them be as the grass upon the housetops, which withereth afore it groweth up" → "Let them be like the grass of the roofs, which, before one pulls it out, has dried up"** (v6) — Hebrew חֲצִיר גַּגּוֹת "grass of roofs"; שֶׁקַּדְמַת שָׁלַף יָבֵשׁ, literally "which before (one) draws out, has dried up": שָׁלַף is "draw out" (a sword, Num 22:23), here of pulling up grass; no subject is named. "before it shoots up" (KJV "groweth up") takes שָׁלַף of the grass putting out its stalk; the sense is uncertain. יָבֵשׁ "dry up, wither", a perfect.
- **"Wherewith the mower filleth not his hand; nor he that bindeth sheaves his bosom" → "with which the reaper has not filled his palm, nor the binder of sheaves his lap"** (v7) — Hebrew קוֹצֵר "reaper" (as Ps 126:5 "shall reap"); כַּף "palm"; מְעַמֵּר "one who binds sheaves", from עֹמֶר "sheaf"; חֹצֶן "lap", the fold of the garment where things were carried (as Neh 5:13). מִלֵּא is a perfect.
- **"Neither do they which go by say, The blessing of the LORD be upon you" → "And those who pass by have not said, 'The blessing of Yahweh to you!'"** (v8) — Hebrew וְלֹא אָמְרוּ הָעֹבְרִים, a perfect; אֲלֵיכֶם "to you" (no verb). **"we bless you"** kept — Hebrew בֵּרַכְנוּ, a perfect of speaking: the blessing is given in the saying. Whether this second line is still the words of those who pass by or the reapers' answer is not said.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew too; the numbers agree.
- **Poetic structure** — Israel harassed but not overcome (v1–4), the wish that those who hate Zion wither like roof grass, unblessed (v5–8).
