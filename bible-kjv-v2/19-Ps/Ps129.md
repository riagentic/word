# Psalm 129

A Song of Ascents.

[1] "Greatly have they afflicted me from my youth" —
let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ now say —

[2] "Greatly have they afflicted me from my youth,
yet they have not prevailed against me.

[3] The plowers plowed upon my back;
they made long their furrows."

[4] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is righteous;
he has cut the cords of the wicked.

[5] May all who hate ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
be put to shame and turned backward!

[6] Let them be like the grass on the housetops,
which withers before it grows up,

[7] with which the reaper does not fill his hand,
nor the binder of sheaves his arms,

[8] nor do those who pass by say,
"The blessing of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be upon you!
We bless you in the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — Israel's suffering persisted but not overcome (v1-4); imprecatory prayer for enemies' fruitlessness (v5-8).
