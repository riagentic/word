# Psalm 149

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a new song,
his praise in the assembly of the faithful ones!

[2] Let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ be glad in his Maker;
let the children of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ rejoice in their King!

[3] Let them praise his name with dancing,
making melody to him with tambourine and lyre!

[4] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ takes pleasure in his people;
he adorns the humble with salvation.

[5] Let the faithful ones exult in glory;
let them sing for joy on their beds.

[6] Let the high praises of ⟦el|El|God⟧ be in their throats
and two-edged swords in their hands,

[7] to execute vengeance on the nations
and punishments on the peoples,

[8] to bind their kings with chains
and their nobles with fetters of iron,

[9] to execute on them the judgment written!
This is honor for all his faithful ones.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"El"** (v6) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **Poetic structure** — praise with dancing (v1-4), *chasidim* on beds with swords (v5-9); military-praise fusion.
