# Psalm 149

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a new song,
his praise in the assembly of faithful ones!

[2] Let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ rejoice in his Maker;
let the sons of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ be glad in their King!

[3] Let them praise his name with dance;
with timbrel and lyre let them make music to him!

[4] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is pleased with his people;
he adorns the humble with salvation.

[5] Let faithful ones be jubilant in glory;
let them shout for joy upon their beds,

[6] the high praises of ⟦el|El|God⟧ in their throat
and a two-edged sword in their hand,

[7] to do vengeance on the nations,
rebukes on the folks,

[8] to bind their kings with chains
and their honored ones with fetters of iron,

[9] to do on them a judgment written!
A splendor it is for all his faithful ones.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"El"** (v6) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v1, 9) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ, "praise Yah", with its token (in v1 written as two words, הַלְלוּ יָהּ).
- **"Sing unto the LORD a new song, and his praise in the congregation of saints" → "Sing to Yahweh a new song, his praise in the assembly of faithful ones"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁירוּ לַיהוָה שִׁיר חָדָשׁ, as Ps 96:1; there is no "and"; בִּקְהַל "in the assembly" (קָהָל); חֲסִידִים "faithful ones" (from חֶסֶד), without the article (so also v5).
- **"Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: let the children of Zion be joyful in their King" → "Let Israel rejoice in his Maker; let the sons of Zion be glad in their King"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּעֹשָׂיו, a participle with a plural suffix form ("his makers"; commonly a plural of majesty, or a spelling of the singular); בְּנֵי־צִיּוֹן "the sons of Zion"; שָׂמַח "rejoice", גִּיל "be glad" (as Ps 96:11).
- **"Let them praise his name in the dance: let them sing praises unto him with the timbrel and harp" → "Let them praise his name with dance; with timbrel and lyre let them make music to him"** (v3) — Hebrew בְמָחוֹל "with dance"; תֹּף "timbrel" (a hand drum); כִּנּוֹר "lyre" (not "harp": that is נֵבֶל); יְזַמְּרוּ "let them make music"; the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"For the LORD taketh pleasure in his people: he will beautify the meek with salvation" → "For Yahweh is pleased with his people; he adorns the humble with salvation"** (v4) — Hebrew רוֹצֶה בְּ "is pleased with"; יְפָאֵר "adorns" (פֵּאֵר "beautify, glorify"), imperfect; עֲנָוִים "humble" (not "meek"); יְשׁוּעָה "salvation".
- **"Let the saints be joyful in glory: let them sing aloud upon their beds" → "Let faithful ones be jubilant in glory; let them shout for joy upon their beds"** (v5) — Hebrew יַעְלְזוּ "be jubilant" (עָלַז); בְּכָבוֹד "in glory" (or "with glory"); יְרַנְּנוּ "shout for joy" (רִנֵּן); מִשְׁכָּב "bed".
- **"Let the high praises of God be in their mouth, and a twoedged sword in their hand" → "the high praises of El in their throat and a two-edged sword in their hand"** (v6) — Hebrew has no verb: the line hangs on v5 ("let … be" is not written); רוֹמְמוֹת "high praises" (lit. "exaltings", from רוֹמֵם "extol"; only here); בִּגְרוֹנָם "in their throat" (not "mouth"); חֶרֶב פִּיפִיּוֹת, lit. "a sword of mouths", the doubled plural of פֶּה "mouth": "two-edged"; בְּיָדָם "in their hand", singular.
- **"To execute vengeance upon the heathen, and punishments upon the people" → "to do vengeance on the nations, rebukes on the folks"** (v7) — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת נְקָמָה "to do vengeance" (עָשָׂה); תּוֹכֵחֹת "rebukes" (reproofs; "punishments" interprets); the Hebrew writes בַּל־אֻמִּים, taken as בַּלְאֻמִּים "on the folks" (as Ps 44:14; 57:9): the written division would give בַּל "not". There is no "and".
- **"and their nobles with fetters of iron" → "and their honored ones with fetters of iron"** (v8) — Hebrew זִקִּים "chains"; נִכְבָּדִים "honored ones" (nifal of כָּבֵד, "be heavy, honored"); כַּבְלֵי בַרְזֶל "fetters of iron" (כֶּבֶל "fetter").
- **"To execute upon them the judgment written: this honour have all his saints" → "to do on them a judgment written! A splendor it is for all his faithful ones"** (v9) — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת בָּהֶם מִשְׁפָּט כָּתוּב "to do on them a judgment written" (no article); הָדָר הוּא "a splendor it is" (הָדָר "splendor", not "honor"); הוּא "it" (the doing of the judgment) or "he": "he is the splendor of all his faithful ones" — the Hebrew leaves it open.

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew; our verse numbers are the Hebrew ones.
- **Structure** — the new song of the faithful (v1–4), their praise and the sword for judgment on the nations (v5–9).
