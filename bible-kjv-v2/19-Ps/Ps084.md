# Psalm 84

To the overseer, upon the ⟦gittith|Gittit|Gittith⟧. Of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧. A psalm.

[1] How beloved are your dwellings,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts!

[2] My soul has longed, and has even been spent,
for the courts of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
my heart and my flesh shout for joy
to the living ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[3] Even a bird has found a house,
and a swallow a nest for herself,
where she has put her young —
your altars, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts,
my King and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] Happy are those who dwell in your house;
they will still praise you. Selah.

[5] Happy is the man whose strength is in you;
highways are in their heart.

[6] Passing through the valley of the ⟦baca|Bacha|Baca⟧,
they set it as a spring;
the early rain also wraps it in blessings.

[7] They go from strength to strength;
he appears to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, hear my prayer;
give ear, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧. Selah.

[9] See our shield, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and look at the face of your ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧.

[10] For better is a day in your courts
than a thousand;
I have chosen to stand at the threshold in the house of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
rather than to dwell in tents of wickedness.

[11] For a sun and a shield is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
favor and glory ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gives;
he does not hold back good
from those who walk blamelessly.

[12] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts,
happy is the man who trusts in you!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 8, 11, 12) — Tetragrammaton preserved; it stands also in this Elohistic psalm. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God" → "El"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*. **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v1, 3, 12; v8 "Yahweh Elohim of Hosts") — Hebrew יְהוָה (אֱלֹהִים) צְבָאוֹת. No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"To the chief Musician upon Gittith, A Psalm for the sons of Korah" → "To the overseer, upon the Gittit. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל־הַגִּתִּית לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח מִזְמוֹר, in this order. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21; traditional "to the chief musician"); עַל־הַגִּתִּית "upon the Gittit" (as Ps 8; 81): the meaning is unknown (an instrument or tune of Gath, or a "winepress" song from גַּת); the לְ of לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח may be "by", "for" or "belonging to", and is not decided here.
- **"How amiable are thy tabernacles" → "How beloved are your dwellings"** (v1) — Hebrew יְדִידוֹת "beloved" (יָדִיד, as Ps 127:2); מִשְׁכְּנוֹתֶיךָ, plural of מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling".
- **"My soul longeth, yea, even fainteth … my heart and my flesh crieth out" → "My soul has longed, and has even been spent … my heart and my flesh shout for joy"** (v2) — Hebrew נִכְסְפָה וְגַם־כָּלְתָה, perfects; כָּלָה "be spent, come to an end"; יְרַנְּנוּ "shout for joy". The Hebrew accents put "my heart and my flesh" in the first half of the verse; the lines keep the common reading, which makes them the subject of "shout for joy". אֵל־חָי "the living El".
- **"the sparrow hath found an house … where she may lay her young, even thine altars" → "a bird has found a house … where she has put her young — your altars"** (v3) — Hebrew צִפּוֹר "bird" (not a kind); דְּרוֹר "swallow" (the kind is not certain); שָׁתָה, perfect, "she has put"; אֶת־מִזְבְּחוֹתֶיךָ, with the object marker: "at" is not written, and the altars stand as an exclamation or as the place meant.
- **"Blessed … they will be still praising thee" → "Happy … they will still praise you"** (v4, 5, 12) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "happy" (not בָּרוּךְ "blessed"); עוֹד יְהַלְלוּךָ, "still / yet they will praise you".
- **"in whose heart are the ways of them" → "highways are in their heart"** (v5) — Hebrew מְסִלּוֹת בִּלְבָבָם, "highways (raised roads) in their heart": "to Zion" is not in the Hebrew. אָדָם "man", singular, then "their" plural, as printed.
- **"Who passing through the valley of Baca make it a well; the rain also filleth the pools" → "Passing through the valley of the Baca, they set it as a spring; the early rain also wraps it in blessings"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּעֵמֶק הַבָּכָא, with the article: בָּכָא is a kind of tree or shrub (plural בְּכָאִים, 2 Sam 5:23, 24; kind uncertain), heard also as בָּכָה "weep" ("the valley of weeping"); the name is kept. מַעְיָן "spring"; שִׁית "set". בְּרָכוֹת as pointed is "blessings"; the same letters read בְּרֵכוֹת are "pools" (KJV). יַעְטֶה "wraps" (עָטָה "wrap"); "it" is supplied. מוֹרֶה "early rain" (the same word means "teacher").
- **"They go from strength to strength, every one of them in Zion appeareth before God" → "They go from strength to strength; he appears to Elohim in Zion"** (v7) — Hebrew חַיִל "strength" (the word of "ability", "force"; v5 has another word, עֹז); יֵרָאֶה אֶל־אֱלֹהִים, singular, "he appears (is seen) to Elohim": "every one" and "before" are not in the Hebrew. The Septuagint reads "the God of gods will be seen" (from memory).
- **"Behold, O God our shield, and look upon the face of thine anointed" → "See our shield, Elohim, and look at the face of your mashiach"** (v9) — Hebrew מָגִנֵּנוּ רְאֵה אֱלֹהִים, "our shield" first: taken as the object (the king, as the second line), or as a title of Elohim ("our shield, see"); רָאָה "see" (not "behold", which is הִנֵּה); הַבֵּט "look at"; מְשִׁיחֶךָ "your anointed" (the `anointed` token).
- **"For a day in thy courts is better than a thousand. I had rather be a doorkeeper … than to dwell" → "For better is a day in your courts than a thousand; I have chosen to stand at the threshold … rather than to dwell"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי טוֹב־יוֹם … מֵאֶלֶף, "elsewhere" is not written; בָּחַרְתִּי "I have chosen"; הִסְתּוֹפֵף, only here, from סַף "threshold": "be at the threshold"; מִדּוּר "rather than dwelling" (דּוּר, only here as a verb); רֶשַׁע "wickedness" (noun of רָשָׁע "wicked").
- **"the LORD will give grace and glory: no good thing will he withhold from them that walk uprightly" → "favor and glory Yahweh gives; he does not hold back good from those who walk blamelessly"** (v11) — Hebrew חֵן "favor" (not "grace"); כָּבוֹד "glory"; מָנַע "hold back"; בְּתָמִים "in blamelessness" (תָּמִים "blameless", not "upright"). The Hebrew order of the first two lines is kept.
- **"blessed is the man that trusteth in thee" → "happy is the man who trusts in you"** (v12) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי אָדָם בֹּטֵחַ בָּךְ.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v12 = Heb v13.
- **A pilgrim's song** — longing for the courts (v1–4), the way through the valley (v5–7), prayer for the king (v8–9), the day in the courts (v10–12).
