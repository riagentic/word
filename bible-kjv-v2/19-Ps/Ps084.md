# Psalm 84

To the chief musician upon ⟦gittith|Gittit|Gittith⟧. A Psalm of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧.

[1] How lovely is your dwelling place,
O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts!

[2] My soul longs, indeed it faints,
for the courts of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
my heart and flesh sing for joy
to the living ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[3] Even the sparrow finds a home,
and the swallow a nest for herself,
where she may lay her young,
at your altars, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts,
my King and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] Blessed are those who dwell in your house,
ever singing your praise! Selah.

[5] Blessed are those whose strength is in you,
in whose heart are the highways to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[6] As they go through the Valley of ⟦baca|Bacha|Baca⟧
they make it a place of springs;
the early rain also covers it with pools.

[7] They go from strength to strength;
each one appears before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[8] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, hear my prayer;
give ear, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧! Selah.

[9] Behold our shield, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
look on the face of your anointed!

[10] For a day in your courts is better
than a thousand elsewhere.
I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
than dwell in the tents of wickedness.

[11] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is a sun and shield;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ bestows favor and honor.
No good thing does he withhold
from those who walk uprightly.

[12] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ of Hosts,
blessed is the one who trusts in you!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v1, 3, 8, 12) — compound divine name.
- **"LORD God" → "Yahweh *Elohim*"** (v8, 11) — compound retained.
- **Gittith** — musical term; retained.
- **Poetic structure** — longing for sanctuary (v1-4), pilgrim's journey (v5-8), royal prayer (v9), Yahweh's provision (v10-12).
