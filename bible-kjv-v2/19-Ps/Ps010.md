# Psalm 10

[1] Why, O Yahweh, do you stand afar off?
Why do you hide yourself in times of trouble?

[2] In arrogance the wicked hotly pursues the poor;
let them be caught in the schemes they have devised.

[3] For the wicked boasts of the desires of his soul,
and the greedy blesses himself, rejecting Yahweh.

[4] In the pride of his face the wicked does not seek him;
"There is no *Elohim*" — so run all his thoughts.

[5] His ways prosper at all times;
your judgments are on high, out of his sight;
as for all his foes, he sneers at them.

[6] He says in his heart, "I shall not be moved;
throughout all generations I shall not meet adversity."

[7] His mouth is filled with cursing, deceit, and oppression;
under his tongue are trouble and iniquity.

[8] He sits in ambush in the villages;
in hiding places he murders the innocent;
his eyes stealthily watch for the helpless.

[9] He lies in wait in his covert like a lion in his thicket;
he lies in wait to seize the poor;
he seizes the poor when he draws him into his net.

[10] The helpless are crushed, sink down,
and fall by his mighty ones.

[11] He says in his heart, "El has forgotten;
he has hidden his face; he will never see it."

[12] Arise, O Yahweh! O El, lift up your hand;
do not forget the afflicted.

[13] Why does the wicked spurn *Elohim*,
saying in his heart, "You will not call to account"?

[14] But you do see, for you note mischief and vexation,
that you may take it into your hand;
to you the helpless commits himself;
you have been the helper of the fatherless.

[15] Break the arm of the wicked and evildoer;
call his wickedness to account till you find none.

[16] Yahweh is king forever and ever;
the nations perish from his land.

[17] O Yahweh, you hear the desire of the afflicted;
you will strengthen their heart;
you will incline your ear

[18] to do justice to the fatherless and the oppressed,
so that man — mortal man — of the earth may no more strike terror.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 12, 16, 17) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v11) — Hebrew *El*.
- **Poetic structure** — wicked man's boasting preserved as strophes; Yahweh's response (v12-18) set off as counter-movement.

### Notes
- **No superscription** — continues Ps 9. Together they form one acrostic.
- **"No *Elohim*"** (v4) — the practical atheism of the arrogant.
- **Classic complaint against injustice** — wicked seem to prosper, confident Yahweh doesn't see.
