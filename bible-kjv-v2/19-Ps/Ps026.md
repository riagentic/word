# Psalm 26

Of David.

[1] Judge me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for I have walked in my integrity,
and in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I have trusted; I shall not totter.

[2] Try me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and test me;
refine my kidneys and my heart.

[3] For your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is before my eyes,
and I walk in your truth.

[4] I have not sat with men of emptiness,
and with those who conceal themselves I will not go in.

[5] I hate the assembly of evildoers,
and with the wicked I will not sit.

[6] I will wash my palms in innocence,
and I will go around your altar, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,

[7] to make heard the voice of thanksgiving
and to recount all your wonderful deeds.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I love the abode of your house
and the place of the dwelling of your glory.

[9] Do not gather my soul with sinners,
and my life with men of blood,

[10] in whose hands is depravity,
and their right hand is full of a bribe.

[11] But I, in my integrity I will walk;
ransom me and show me favor.

[12] My foot stands on a level place;
in the assemblies I will bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 6, 8, 12) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "Of David"** (heading) — Hebrew לְדָוִד, the first word of v1: "by", "for" or "belonging to David".
- **"I have trusted also in the LORD; therefore I shall not slide" → "and in Yahweh I have trusted; I shall not totter"** (v1) — Hebrew וּבַיהוָה בָּטַחְתִּי לֹא אֶמְעָד, "and", with no "therefore"; מָעַד "totter".
- **"Examine me … and prove me; try my reins" → "Try me … and test me; refine my kidneys"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּחָנֵנִי … וְנַסֵּנִי; בָּחַן "try", נִסָּה "test" (two verbs). The qere צָרְפָה is the command "refine" (צָרַף, of metal); the written form צרופה reads "refined are my kidneys". כְּלָיוֹת "kidneys", with the heart the seat of the inner man.
- **"and I have walked in thy truth" → "and I walk in your truth"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהִתְהַלַּכְתִּי, "and" with the perfect of the hitpael "walk about, go to and fro": an ongoing way of life.
- **"vain persons" → "men of emptiness"** (v4) — Hebrew מְתֵי־שָׁוְא. **"neither will I go in with dissemblers" → "and with those who conceal themselves I will not go in"** — Hebrew נַעֲלָמִים, the nifal participle of עָלַם "hide": those who hide what they are.
- **"I have hated the congregation of evil doers" → "I hate the assembly of evildoers"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׂנֵאתִי, a perfect of a verb of state; קְהַל "assembly".
- **"I will wash mine hands in innocency: so will I compass thine altar" → "I will wash my palms in innocence, and I will go around your altar"** (v6) — Hebrew כַּפָּי "my palms"; וַאֲסֹבְבָה, "and" with the cohortative "let me go around".
- **"That I may publish with the voice of thanksgiving" → "to make heard the voice of thanksgiving"** (v7) — Hebrew לַשְׁמִעַ בְּקוֹל תּוֹדָה, the hifil of שָׁמַע "make heard", literally "with a voice of thanksgiving". **"and tell of all thy wondrous works" → "and to recount all your wonderful deeds"** — Hebrew סִפֵּר; נִפְלָאוֹת.
- **"I have loved the habitation of thy house, and the place where thine honour dwelleth" → "I love the abode of your house and the place of the dwelling of your glory"** (v8) — Hebrew אָהַבְתִּי (a perfect of state), מְעוֹן "abode" (as Deut 26:15), מִשְׁכַּן כְּבוֹדֶךָ: מִשְׁכָּן is the noun "dwelling" (of שָׁכַן), not a verb; כָּבוֹד "glory".
- **"Gather not my soul" kept** (v9) — Hebrew אַל־תֶּאֱסֹף, אָסַף "gather" (to be gathered = to die, Gen 25:8). **"nor my life with bloody men" → "and my life with men of blood"** — Hebrew וְעִם־אַנְשֵׁי דָמִים, literally "men of bloods".
- **"In whose hands is mischief" → "in whose hands is depravity"** (v10) — Hebrew זִמָּה, a shameful design (root "devise"). **"bribes" → "a bribe"** — Hebrew שֹׁחַד, singular; "right hand" is singular too.
- **"be merciful unto me" → "show me favor"** (v11) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי. **"redeem me" → "ransom me"** — Hebrew פְּדֵנִי, from פָּדָה.
- **"standeth in an even place" → "stands on a level place"** (v12) — Hebrew עָמְדָה בְמִישׁוֹר, a perfect ("has stood, stands"); מִישׁוֹר, of the land "tableland", here level ground where the foot does not slip. **"in the congregations" → "in the assemblies"** — Hebrew בְּמַקְהֵלִים, plural (the earlier "great assembly" was singular).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first word of v1.
- **Plea of a man who is in the right** — he asks to be judged, and names what he has not done (v4–5) and what he does (v6–8).
- **Washing the palms in innocence** (v6; Deut 21:6; Ps 73:13).
