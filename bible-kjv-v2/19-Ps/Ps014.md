# Psalm 14

To the chief musician. A Psalm of David.

[1] The fool — says in his heart, "There is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."
They are corrupt, they do abominable deeds;
there is none who does good.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ looks down from heaven on the children of men,
to see if there are any who understand,
who seek after ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] They have all turned aside; together they have become corrupt;
there is none who does good, not even one.

[4] Have they no knowledge, all the evildoers
who eat up my people as they eat bread,
and do not call upon ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?

[5] There they are in great terror,
for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is with the generation of the righteous.

[6] You would shame the counsel of the afflicted,
but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his refuge.

[7] Oh, that salvation for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would come out of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!
When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ restores the fortunes of his people,
let ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ rejoice, let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ be glad.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"captivity" → "fortunes"** (v7).

### Notes
- **Near-duplicate of Ps 53** — with variations.
- **v1-3 cited Rom 3:10-12** — universal depravity.
