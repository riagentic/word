# Psalm 14

To the overseer. Of David.

[1] A fool has said in his heart, "There is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."
They have worked ruin, they have made deeds abominable;
there is none who does good.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ from the heavens has looked down upon the sons of man,
to see whether there is one who has insight,
one who seeks ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] All have turned aside, together they have become foul; there is none who does good,
there is not even one.

[4] Have they not known, all the workers of wickedness,
who eat my people — they have eaten bread?
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ they have not called on.

[5] There they dreaded a dread,
for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in the generation of the righteous.

[6] The counsel of the afflicted you shame,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his refuge.

[7] Would that from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ the salvation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were given!
When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ restores the fortunes of his people,
let ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ be glad, let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ rejoice.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 4, 6, 7) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 5) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. Of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לְדָוִד: there is no word "psalm" here (Ps 53 has מַשְׂכִּיל). לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21; traditional "to the chief musician"); the לְ of לְדָוִד may be "by", "for" or "belonging to".
- **"The fool hath said" → "A fool has said"** (v1) — Hebrew אָמַר נָבָל, perfect, no article. נָבָל is the base, senseless man (as Deut 32:6 "foolish"; the deed of the נָבָל is נְבָלָה "outrage").
- **"They are corrupt, they have done abominable works" → "They have worked ruin, they have made deeds abominable"** (v1) — Hebrew הִשְׁחִיתוּ, hifil of שָׁחַת without an object, "they have acted ruinously" (the one verb for KJV "corrupt" and "destroy"); הִתְעִיבוּ עֲלִילָה, "they have made abominable (their) deed", עֲלִילָה singular as a collective. Ps 53:1 has "and have made abominable injustice" (וְהִתְעִיבוּ עָוֶל).
- **"looked down from heaven upon the children of men" → "from the heavens has looked down upon the sons of man"** (v2) — Hebrew יְהוָה מִשָּׁמַיִם הִשְׁקִיף עַל־בְּנֵי־אָדָם, perfect, in this order; בְּנֵי־אָדָם "sons of man". Ps 53:2 has *Elohim*.
- **"if there were any that did understand, and seek God" → "whether there is one who has insight, one who seeks Elohim"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲיֵשׁ מַשְׂכִּיל דֹּרֵשׁ, two participles: מַשְׂכִּיל from הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight, act with insight"; דָּרַשׁ "seek". Rom 3:11 has the Greek wording.
- **"They are all gone aside, they are all together become filthy" → "All have turned aside, together they have become foul"** (v3) — Hebrew הַכֹּל סָר, "the whole has turned aside" (singular verb); נֶאֱלָחוּ, nifal of אלח, "become tainted, foul" (Job 15:16), another word than שָׁחַת of v1. Ps 53:3 has כֻּלּוֹ סָג "all of it has turned back".
- **Line division** (v3) — the Hebrew half-verse ends after "there is none who does good"; "there is not even one" stands alone (אֵין גַּם־אֶחָד).
- **"Have all the workers of iniquity no knowledge? who eat up my people as they eat bread" → "Have they not known, all the workers of wickedness, who eat my people — they have eaten bread?"** (v4) — Hebrew הֲלֹא יָדְעוּ, perfect "have they not known"; אָוֶן "wickedness"; אֹכְלֵי עַמִּי אָכְלוּ לֶחֶם, "eaters of my people, they have eaten bread": there is no "as" in the Hebrew. The common reading "(they eat my people) as they eat bread" supplies it; another is "they eat bread (= feast) while they eat my people". Ps 53:4 lacks "all" (כָּל).
- **"and call not upon the LORD" → "Yahweh they have not called on"** (v4) — Hebrew יְהוָה לֹא קָרָאוּ, the name first, a perfect.
- **"There were they in great fear" → "There they dreaded a dread"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׁם פָּחֲדוּ פָחַד, the verb with its own noun (as Job 3:25). Ps 53:5 adds "where there was no dread" and goes on with other words.
- **"God is in the generation of the righteous"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּדוֹר צַדִּיק, "in" (not "with"); צַדִּיק singular ("the righteous one" or collective).
- **"Ye have shamed the counsel of the poor, because the LORD is his refuge" → "The counsel of the afflicted you shame, for Yahweh is his refuge"** (v6) — Hebrew עֲצַת־עָנִי תָבִישׁוּ, the object first; imperfect, "you shame" (or "you would shame"); עָנִי "afflicted". כִּי "for"; many take it as "but". מַחְסֶה "refuge", from חָסָה "take refuge". This verse is not in Ps 53.
- **"Oh that the salvation of Israel were come out of Zion!" → "Would that from Zion the salvation of Israel were given!"** (v7) — Hebrew מִי יִתֵּן מִצִּיּוֹן יְשׁוּעַת יִשְׂרָאֵל, literally "Who will give from Zion the salvation of Israel?", a wish. Ps 53:6 has the plural יְשֻׁעוֹת "salvations".
- **"bringeth back the captivity of his people" → "restores the fortunes of his people"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּשׁוּב יְהוָה שְׁבוּת עַמּוֹ, literally "when Yahweh turns back the captivity / the turning of his people": the verb and the noun from one sound; שְׁבוּת is from שָׁבָה "take captive" or from שׁוּב itself. One idiom, one rendering, as Job 42:10, where no captivity is meant; traditional "turn the captivity".
- **"Jacob shall rejoice, and Israel shall be glad" → "let Jacob be glad, let Israel rejoice"** (v7) — Hebrew יָגֵל, short (jussive) form of גִּיל "be glad", then יִשְׂמַח "rejoice"; the two verbs in the Hebrew order.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew too; the numbers agree.
- **Near-duplicate of Ps 53** — with *Elohim* where Ps 14 has Yahweh, and with the differences named above (v1–7).
- **v1–3 cited Rom 3:10–12** — universal depravity; the Greek there follows its own wording.
