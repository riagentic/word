# Psalm 7

A ⟦shiggaion|Shiggayon|Shiggaion⟧ of David, which he sang to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ concerning the words of ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧, a ⟦benjaminite|son of Yemini|Benjaminite⟧.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in you I have taken refuge;
save me from all my pursuers and deliver me,

[2] lest he tear my soul to pieces like a lion,
tearing away, and there is none who delivers.

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, if I have done this,
if there is injustice in my palms,

[4] if I have dealt evil to the one at peace with me,
and have pulled out my adversary without cause —

[5] let the enemy pursue my soul and overtake it,
and let him trample my life to the earth,
and make my glory abide in the dust. Selah.

[6] Arise, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, in your anger;
lift yourself up against the ragings of my adversaries;
and awake to me — judgment you have commanded.

[7] And the congregation of the folks shall surround you,
and over it return to the height.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ judges peoples; judge me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
according to my righteousness
and according to my integrity upon me.

[9] Let the evil of the wicked come to an end, please,
and may you establish the righteous one;
and he who tries hearts and kidneys
is the righteous ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] My shield is upon ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the savior of the upright of heart.

[11] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is a righteous judge,
and ⟦el|an El|a God⟧ who is indignant every day.

[12] If he does not turn back, he will sharpen his sword;
he has trodden his bow and made it ready.

[13] And for him he has prepared the instruments of death;
his arrows he works into burning ones.

[14] Behold, he writhes in labor with wickedness,
and he conceives toil and bears falsehood.

[15] A pit he has dug, and he has dug it,
and he has fallen into the pit that he works.

[16] His toil will return upon his head,
and upon his crown his violence will come down.

[17] I will give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ according to his righteousness,
and I will make music to the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elyon|Elyon|Most High⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 3, 9, 10, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God" → "El"** (v11) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*. **"most high" → "Elyon"** (v17) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן. The Hebrew vocatives have no "O".
- **"Shiggaion of David … concerning the words of Cush the Benjamite" → "A Shiggayon of David … concerning the words of Kush, a son of Yemini"** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁגָּיוֹן, found only here (plural Hab 3:1); the meaning is unknown (often derived from שָׁגָה "stray, reel": a song of strong feeling). לְדָוִד "of David": the לְ may say "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and is not decided here. עַל־דִּבְרֵי "concerning the words of" (lit. "upon the words of"). בֶּן־יְמִינִי "a son of Yemini" (= a Benjaminite, as 1 Sam 9:21), with no article.
- **"in thee do I put my trust" → "in you I have taken refuge"** (v1) — Hebrew חָסִיתִי, perfect of חָסָה "take refuge". **"them that persecute me" → "my pursuers"** — Hebrew רֹדְפַי.
- **"Lest he tear my soul like a lion, rending it in pieces, while there is none to deliver" → "lest he tear my soul to pieces like a lion, tearing away, and there is none who delivers"** (v2) — Hebrew יִטְרֹף "tear to pieces" (of a beast), singular "he"; פֹּרֵק "tearing away", with no object ("it" is not written); וְאֵין מַצִּיל "and there is none delivering".
- **"if there be iniquity in my hands" → "if there is injustice in my palms"** (v3) — Hebrew עָוֶל "injustice" (not עָוֹן "iniquity"); בְּכַפָּי "in my palms". The "if" clauses of v3–4 are a real condition, answered by the wishes of v5 (not the oath formula).
- **"If I have rewarded evil unto him that was at peace with me; (yea, I have delivered him that without cause is mine enemy:)" → "if I have dealt evil to the one at peace with me, and have pulled out my adversary without cause —"** (v4) — Hebrew גָּמַלְתִּי … רָע "dealt evil"; שׁוֹלְמִי "the one at peace with me". וָאֲחַלְּצָה צוֹרְרִי רֵיקָם is difficult: חִלֵּץ is "pull out, rescue" (Ps 6:4), and the clause as printed says "and I pulled out my adversary without cause" (רֵיקָם, lit. "emptily"). KJV makes it a statement in brackets ("yea, I have delivered …"); many take חִלֵּץ in its other sense, "strip, plunder" ("or plundered my adversary without cause"). The verse keeps the verb of Ps 6:4 and the "and" of the Hebrew. צוֹרֵר "adversary" (as Ps 6:7).
- **"Let the enemy persecute my soul, and take it" → "let the enemy pursue my soul and overtake it"** (v5) — Hebrew יִרַדֹּף, a mixed form of רָדַף "pursue"; וְיַשֵּׂג "and overtake" ("it" is supplied). **"tread down my life upon the earth" → "trample my life to the earth"** — Hebrew וְיִרְמֹס לָאָרֶץ. **"lay mine honour in the dust" → "make my glory abide in the dust"** — Hebrew כְּבוֹדִי "my glory"; יַשְׁכֵּן, hifil of שָׁכַן "abide".
- **"lift up thyself because of the rage of mine enemies" → "lift yourself up against the ragings of my adversaries"** (v6) — Hebrew הִנָּשֵׂא; בְּעַבְרוֹת, plural of עֶבְרָה "raging" (lit. "overflowing"; beside אַף "anger"). **"awake for me to the judgment that thou hast commanded" → "awake to me — judgment you have commanded"** — Hebrew וְעוּרָה אֵלַי מִשְׁפָּט צִוִּיתָ: "to me" (the Septuagint reads אֵלִי "my El", from memory); the last words stand without a joining word: "judgment you have commanded".
- **"So shall the congregation of the people compass thee about: for their sakes therefore return thou on high" → "And the congregation of the folks shall surround you, and over it return to the height"** (v7) — Hebrew וַעֲדַת לְאֻמִּים "and the congregation of folks" (לְאֹם "folk", beside עַם "people", v8); תְּסוֹבְבֶךָּ "shall surround you" (or a wish, "let … surround"); וְעָלֶיהָ "and over it" (the congregation); לַמָּרוֹם "to the height"; שׁוּבָה "return".
- **"The LORD shall judge the people" → "Yahweh judges peoples"** (v8) — Hebrew יָדִין עַמִּים, plural, no article; דִּין and שָׁפַט ("judge me") are two verbs, English has one word. **"mine integrity that is in me" → "my integrity upon me"** — Hebrew וּכְתֻמִּי עָלָי, lit. "upon me". The lines follow the Hebrew accents (the main break after the second "Yahweh").
- **"Oh let the wickedness of the wicked come to an end; but establish the just: for the righteous God trieth the hearts and reins" → "Let the evil of the wicked come to an end, please, and may you establish the righteous one; and he who tries hearts and kidneys is the righteous Elohim"** (v9) — Hebrew יִגְמָר־נָא, with נָא "please"; רַע "evil"; וּתְכוֹנֵן "and may you establish" (וְ, not "but"); וּבֹחֵן "and one who tries" (assays, as metal; no "for"); כְּלָיוֹת "kidneys" (the inmost self). The last words may also be read as an address: "and you who try hearts and kidneys, righteous Elohim". The article before "righteous" is supplied.
- **"My defence is of God, which saveth the upright in heart" → "My shield is upon Elohim, the savior of the upright of heart"** (v10) — Hebrew מָגִנִּי "my shield"; עַל־אֱלֹהִים "upon Elohim" (he bears it); מוֹשִׁיעַ "savior, one who saves"; יִשְׁרֵי־לֵב "the upright of heart".
- **"God judgeth the righteous, and God is angry with the wicked every day" → "Elohim is a righteous judge, and an El who is indignant every day"** (v11) — Hebrew שׁוֹפֵט צַדִּיק, "a righteous judge" or "judging the righteous"; זֹעֵם "being indignant", with no object ("with the wicked" is not written). The word list has "denounce" for זָעַם where words are spoken against someone (Num 23:7–8); here there is no object and no speech, and "denounce" would add both.
- **"If he turn not, he will whet his sword; he hath bent his bow" → "If he does not turn back, he will sharpen his sword; he has trodden his bow"** (v12) — Hebrew יָשׁוּב "turn back" (not "repent"); who "he" is in each clause is not named (the wicked man who does not turn back; Elohim who sharpens); יִלְטוֹשׁ "sharpen"; דָרַךְ "tread" (a bow was bent by treading on it); וַיְכוֹנְנֶהָ "and made it ready" (כּוֹנֵן, "establish" in v9).
- **"He hath also prepared for him the instruments of death; he ordaineth his arrows against the persecutors" → "And for him he has prepared the instruments of death; his arrows he works into burning ones"** (v13) — Hebrew וְלוֹ "and for him" (for the one who does not turn back, or "for himself"); כְּלֵי־מָוֶת "instruments of death" (כְּלִי, any made article); לְדֹלְקִים "into burning ones" (from דָּלַק "burn"; KJV takes it in its sense "hotly pursue": "against the persecutors"); יִפְעָל "he works".
- **"he travaileth with iniquity, and hath conceived mischief, and brought forth falsehood" → "he writhes in labor with wickedness, and he conceives toil and bears falsehood"** (v14) — Hebrew יְחַבֶּל "writhe in labor"; אָוֶן "wickedness"; עָמָל "toil" (as Job 15:35, where הָרָה עָמָל stands as here); שֶׁקֶר "falsehood".
- **"He made a pit, and digged it, and is fallen into the ditch which he made" → "A pit he has dug, and he has dug it, and he has fallen into the pit that he works"** (v15) — Hebrew בּוֹר כָּרָה וַיַּחְפְּרֵהוּ: two verbs for "dig" (כָּרָה, חָפַר), English has one word; two nouns for "pit": בּוֹר (a pit, a cistern) and שַׁחַת (a pit; KJV "ditch"); יִפְעָל "he works" (as v13).
- **"His mischief shall return upon his own head, and his violent dealing shall come down upon his own pate" → "His toil will return upon his head, and upon his crown his violence will come down"** (v16) — Hebrew עֲמָלוֹ "his toil" (v14); קָדְקֳדוֹ "the crown of his head"; חֲמָסוֹ "his violence"; "own" is not written.
- **"I will praise the LORD … and will sing praise to the name" → "I will give thanks to Yahweh … and I will make music to the name"** (v17) — Hebrew אוֹדֶה, from הוֹדָה "give thanks"; וַאֲזַמְּרָה, from זִמֵּר "make music".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v17 = Heb v18.
- **Kush the son of Yemini** — unknown; likely an adversary of Saul's time.
- **Conditional self-curse** (v3–5) — if I am guilty, let me perish.
- **Digging a pit** (v15) — proverbial self-trap.
