# Psalm 7

A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to Yahweh concerning the words of Cush a Benjaminite.

[1] O Yahweh my *Elohim*, in you I take refuge;
save me from all my pursuers and deliver me,

[2] lest like a lion they tear my soul apart,
rending it, while there is none to rescue.

[3] O Yahweh my *Elohim*, if I have done this,
if there is wrong in my hands,

[4] if I have repaid my friend with evil,
or plundered my adversary without cause —

[5] let the enemy pursue my soul and overtake it,
let him trample my life to the ground,
and lay my glory in the dust. Selah.

[6] Arise, O Yahweh, in your anger;
lift yourself against the fury of my enemies;
awake for me the judgment you have commanded.

[7] Let the assembly of peoples be gathered about you;
over it return on high.

[8] Yahweh judges the peoples;
judge me, O Yahweh, according to my righteousness
and according to the integrity that is in me.

[9] Oh, let the evil of the wicked come to an end,
and establish the righteous;
you test minds and hearts,
O righteous *Elohim*.

[10] My shield is with *Elohim*,
who saves the upright in heart.

[11] *Elohim* is a righteous judge,
an *Elohim* who has indignation every day.

[12] If one does not repent, he will sharpen his sword;
he has bent his bow and made it ready.

[13] He has prepared for him his deadly weapons,
making his arrows fiery shafts.

[14] Behold, he is pregnant with evil,
conceives mischief, and gives birth to lies.

[15] He makes a pit, digging it out,
and falls into the hole that he has made.

[16] His mischief returns upon his own head,
and on his own skull his violence descends.

[17] I will give thanks to Yahweh according to his righteousness,
and will sing praise to the name of Yahweh Most High.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Shiggaion" — retained** — musical-genre term, possibly "wandering/impassioned song."
- **"reins" → "minds"** (v9) — *kelayot*; inward emotional seat.

### Notes
- **Cush the Benjaminite** — unknown; likely Saul-era adversary.
- **Self-imprecatory oath** (v3-5) — if I am guilty, let me perish.
- **Digging a pit** (v15) — proverbial self-trap.
