# Psalm 35

Of David.

[1] Contend, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, with those who contend with me;
fight against those who fight against me.

[2] Take hold of shield and large shield,
and rise up as my help.

[3] And draw out the spear, and shut, to meet my pursuers;
say to my soul, "I am your salvation."

[4] Let them be ashamed and be put to shame,
those who seek my soul;
let them be turned back and be abashed,
those who devise my evil.

[5] Let them be like chaff before the wind,
and the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ thrusting them.

[6] Let their way be darkness and slippery places,
and the angel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ pursuing them.

[7] For without cause they hid for me the pit of their net;
without cause they dug for my soul.

[8] Let devastation come upon him that he does not know,
and his net that he hid, let it capture him;
in devastation let him fall into it.

[9] And my soul shall be glad in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
it shall exult in his salvation.

[10] All my bones shall say,
"⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, who is like you,
delivering the afflicted from one stronger than he,
and the afflicted and the needy from him who robs him?"

[11] Witnesses of violence rise up;
what I do not know they ask me.

[12] They repay me evil for good,
bereavement to my soul.

[13] But I, when they were sick,
my clothing was sackcloth;
I afflicted my soul with the fast,
and my prayer would return upon my bosom.

[14] As though a friend, as though a brother to me, I walked about;
as one mourning a mother,
darkened, I bowed down.

[15] And at my stumbling they rejoiced and gathered;
they gathered against me, stricken ones, and I did not know;
they tore and were not still.

[16] With the godless, the mockers for a cake,
gnashing at me with their teeth.

[17] ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, how long will you look on?
Bring back my soul from their devastations,
from the young lions my only one.

[18] I will give thanks to you in a great assembly;
among a mighty people I will praise you.

[19] Let not those who are falsely my enemies rejoice over me,
nor those who hate me without cause wink the eye.

[20] For they do not speak ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧,
and against the quiet ones of the land
they devise words of deceits.

[21] And they opened their mouth wide against me;
they said, "Aha, aha!
Our eyes have seen!"

[22] You have seen, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧; do not be silent!
⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, do not be far from me!

[23] Stir up and awake for my judgment,
my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and ⟦adonai|Adonai|my Lord⟧, for my case!

[24] Judge me according to your righteousness,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and let them not rejoice over me.

[25] Let them not say in their heart,
"Aha, our soul!"
Let them not say, "We have swallowed him up."

[26] Let them be ashamed and be abashed together,
those who rejoice at my evil;
let them be clothed with shame and dishonor,
those who make themselves great against me.

[27] Let them shout for joy and rejoice,
those who delight in my righteousness,
and let them say continually,
"Let ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be great,
who delights in the ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ of his servant!"

[28] And my tongue shall softly recite your righteousness,
all the day your praise.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 6, 9, 10, 22, 24, 27) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v17, 22, 23) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"O LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 10, 22, 24) — the vocative stands without "O" in the Hebrew.
- **"Of David" (heading)** — Hebrew לְדָוִד, part of v1 (the numbering is the same as the Hebrew). The לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Plead my cause … with them that strive with me" → "Contend … with those who contend with me"** (v1) — Hebrew רִיבָה … אֶת־יְרִיבַי, one root (רִיב "contend") twice, as לְחַם אֶת־לֹחֲמָי "fight against those who fight against me".
- **"shield and buckler" → "shield and large shield"** (v2) — Hebrew מָגֵן וְצִנָּה; the צִנָּה is the large shield, a "buckler" is a small one.
- **"stand up for mine help" → "rise up as my help"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּעֶזְרָתִי, "in my help": the בְּ read as "as" (= be my help); KJV "for".
- **"Draw out also the spear, and stop the way against them" → "And draw out the spear, and shut, to meet my pursuers"** (v3) — Hebrew וְהָרֵק חֲנִית וּסְגֹר לִקְרַאת רֹדְפָי. הֵרִיק "draw" (lit. "empty out", as of a sword); סְגֹר is pointed as the imperative "shut", with no object ("the way" is KJV's addition). Many read the same letters as a noun *segor*, an axe (from memory: compared with Greek *sagaris*); the Masoretic Text is kept. לִקְרַאת "to meet". "Also" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"be confounded and put to shame … brought to confusion" → "be ashamed and be put to shame … be abashed"** (v4, 26) — Hebrew בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", נִכְלַם "be put to shame" and חָפֵר "be abashed" (another root than חָפַר "dig" of v7): three verbs, three renderings; nouns בֹּשֶׁת "shame" and כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (v26).
- **"seek after my soul" → "seek my soul"** (v4) — Hebrew מְבַקְשֵׁי נַפְשִׁי; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" throughout (v3, 4, 7, 9, 12, 13, 17, 25), not "life".
- **"devise my hurt" → "devise my evil"** (v4; v26 "rejoice at my evil") — Hebrew רָעָתִי, "my evil": the evil meant for me, word for word.
- **"let the angel of the LORD chase them" → "and the angel of Yahweh thrusting them"** (v5, 6) — Hebrew וּמַלְאַךְ יְהוָה דּוֹחֶה / רֹדְפָם, a noun clause with a participle, no "let". דּוֹחֶה has no object: "them" is supplied from v6 רֹדְפָם. מַלְאָךְ is the word "messenger"; "the angel of Yahweh" as in the rest of the Old Testament.
- **"dark and slippery" → "darkness and slippery places"** (v6) — Hebrew חֹשֶׁךְ וַחֲלַקְלַקּוֹת, two nouns.
- **"have they hid for me their net in a pit" → "they hid for me the pit of their net"** (v7) — Hebrew טָמְנוּ־לִי שַׁחַת רִשְׁתָּם, "they hid for me a pit, their net"; the second line חָפְרוּ לְנַפְשִׁי "they dug for my soul" has no object. Many move שַׁחַת "pit" to the second line ("they dug a pit for my soul"); the Masoretic Text is kept, nothing supplied. חִנָּם "without cause" twice.
- **"Let destruction come upon him at unawares" → "Let devastation come upon him that he does not know"** (v8) — Hebrew תְּבוֹאֵהוּ שׁוֹאָה לֹא־יֵדָע; שׁוֹאָה "devastation" (also v17, plural); לֹא־יֵדָע read as a relative clause ("that he does not know"), or "while he does not know". The verbs are imperfects read as wishes (as v4–6); "it shall come …" is also possible. לָכַד "capture". Last line Hebrew בְּשׁוֹאָה יִפָּל־בָּהּ, "in devastation let him fall in it": "it" (feminine) is the net or the devastation.
- **"my soul shall be joyful … it shall rejoice" → "my soul shall be glad … it shall exult"** (v9) — Hebrew וְנַפְשִׁי תָּגִיל … תָּשִׂישׂ; וְ "And" (not "Then"); גִּיל "be glad", שׂוּשׂ "exult", שָׂמַח "rejoice" (v15, 19, 24, 26, 27) kept apart.
- **"the poor … the poor and the needy" → "the afflicted … and the afflicted and the needy"** (v10) — Hebrew עָנִי (bowed down, afflicted) twice, אֶבְיוֹן "needy"; the second line has וְ "and". "from him that is too strong for him" → "from one stronger than he" (מֵחָזָק מִמֶּנּוּ).
- **"False witnesses" → "Witnesses of violence"** (v11) — Hebrew עֵדֵי חָמָס; חָמָס "violence". "they laid to my charge things that I knew not" → "what I do not know they ask me" (שָׁאַל "ask"; Hebrew order).
- **"to the spoiling of my soul" → "bereavement to my soul"** (v12) — Hebrew שְׁכוֹל לְנַפְשִׁי, a noun, "bereavement" (loss of children).
- **"my clothing was sackcloth … humbled my soul with fasting … returned into mine own bosom" → "my clothing was sackcloth; I afflicted my soul with the fast, and my prayer would return upon my bosom"** (v13) — Hebrew לְבוּשִׁי שָׂק (no verb); עִנֵּיתִי בַצּוֹם נַפְשִׁי, "I afflicted with the fast my soul" (the עִנָּה נֶפֶשׁ row); וּתְפִלָּתִי עַל־חֵיקִי תָשׁוּב, an imperfect: here a repeated past ("would return"); it may also be a wish ("let it return") or a future. What the line means (prayer with the head bowed to the chest, or prayer coming back unanswered) is not said.
- **"I behaved myself as though he had been my friend or brother: I bowed down heavily, as one that mourneth for his mother" → "As though a friend, as though a brother to me, I walked about; as one mourning a mother, darkened, I bowed down"** (v14) — Hebrew כְּרֵעַ־כְּאָח לִי הִתְהַלָּכְתִּי, הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about"; כַּאֲבֶל־אֵם, "as the mourning of a mother" = one who mourns a mother ("his" is not in the Hebrew); קֹדֵר "being dark" (in mourning dress or face; KJV "heavily"); "or" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"in mine adversity they rejoiced" → "at my stumbling they rejoiced"** (v15) — Hebrew וּבְצַלְעִי, צֶלַע "limping, stumbling" (Ps 38:17; another word than צֵלָע "side"). נֶאֶסְפוּ עָלַי נֵכִים: נֵכִים (only here) "stricken ones" (from נכה, as נְכֵה רַגְלַיִם "stricken in the feet"); meaning uncertain: KJV "abjects", others "smiters"; a change to נָכְרִים "strangers" is a conjecture, not followed. וְלֹא יָדַעְתִּי "and I did not know" (whom, or what, is not said). קָרְעוּ וְלֹא־דָמּוּ "they tore and were not still" (דָּמַם "be still").
- **"With hypocritical mockers in feasts, they gnashed upon me with their teeth" → "With the godless, the mockers for a cake, gnashing at me with their teeth"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּחַנְפֵי לַעֲגֵי מָעוֹג חָרֹק עָלַי שִׁנֵּימוֹ, word for word: חָנֵף "godless" (lit. "polluted"); לַעֲגֵי מָעוֹג "mockers of a cake" (מָעוֹג "cake", 1 Kgs 17:12: men who mock for a bite of bread); חָרֹק is an infinitive ("gnashing"), with no finite verb and no tense. Uncertain; nothing supplied.
- **"rescue my soul from their destructions, my darling from the lions" → "Bring back my soul from their devastations, from the young lions my only one"** (v17) — Hebrew הָשִׁיבָה נַפְשִׁי מִשֹּׁאֵיהֶם מִכְּפִירִים יְחִידָתִי; הֵשִׁיב "bring back"; שֹׁאָה "devastation" as v8; כְּפִיר "young lion"; יְחִידָה "only one" (feminine, set beside נֶפֶשׁ: = my one life; as Ps 22:20).
- **"I will give thee thanks in the great congregation: I will praise thee among much people" → "I will give thanks to you in a great assembly; among a mighty people I will praise you"** (v18) — Hebrew בְּקָהָל רָב, no article; קָהָל "assembly"; בְּעַם עָצוּם, עָצוּם "mighty" (strong in number).
- **"them that are mine enemies wrongfully … neither let them wink with the eye that hate me without a cause" → "those who are falsely my enemies … nor those who hate me without cause wink the eye"** (v19) — Hebrew אֹיְבַי שֶׁקֶר, "my enemies — falsehood" (שֶׁקֶר "falsehood", an adverbial noun); the אַל "not" of the first line governs the second ("nor"); Hebrew order kept. שֹׂנְאַי חִנָּם is cited in John 15:25 (also Ps 69:4).
- **"they speak not peace" → "they do not speak shalom"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם (token). וְעַל רִגְעֵי־אֶרֶץ "and against the quiet ones of the land" (רָגֵעַ only here; "and", not "but"); דִּבְרֵי מִרְמוֹת "words of deceits", plural.
- **"Yea, they opened their mouth wide … our eye hath seen it" → "And they opened their mouth wide … Our eyes have seen!"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיַּרְחִיבוּ, narrative "And"; עֵינֵינוּ "our eyes", with no object ("it" is not in the Hebrew); הֶאָח twice "Aha, aha".
- **"keep not silence" → "do not be silent"** (v22) — Hebrew אַל־תֶּחֱרַשׁ.
- **"Stir up thyself, and awake to my judgment, even unto my cause" → "Stir up and awake for my judgment … for my case"** (v23) — Hebrew הָעִירָה וְהָקִיצָה, two imperatives without an object ("thyself" is not in the Hebrew); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", רִיב "case" (the noun of v1 "contend").
- **"Judge me … according to thy righteousness" → "Judge me according to your righteousness, Yahweh my Elohim"** (v24) — Hebrew שָׁפְטֵנִי כְצִדְקְךָ יְהוָה אֱלֹהָי, Hebrew order; שָׁפַט "judge" (not "vindicate").
- **"Ah, so would we have it" → "Aha, our soul!"** (v25) — Hebrew הֶאָח נַפְשֵׁנוּ, "aha, our soul" (= our desire: נֶפֶשׁ of craving); בְלִבָּם "in their heart", singular.
- **"that magnify themselves against me" → "those who make themselves great against me"** (v26) — Hebrew הַמַּגְדִּילִים עָלָי, hifil of גָּדַל without an object, "who make great against me"; "themselves" is supplied.
- **"Let them shout for joy, and be glad, that favour my righteous cause … Let the LORD be magnified, which hath pleasure in the prosperity of his servant" → "Let them shout for joy and rejoice, those who delight in my righteousness … Let Yahweh be great, who delights in the shalom of his servant!"** (v27) — Hebrew יָרֹנּוּ וְיִשְׂמְחוּ חֲפֵצֵי צִדְקִי, verbs first as in the Hebrew; צֶדֶק "righteousness" ("cause" is not in the Hebrew); תָּמִיד "continually"; יִגְדַּל יְהוָה may be a wish ("let Yahweh be great") or a statement ("Yahweh is great": the same form); שְׁלוֹם עַבְדּוֹ "the shalom of his servant" (token; KJV "prosperity"); עֶבֶד "servant" (the word is "slave").
- **"And my tongue shall speak of thy righteousness and of thy praise all the day long" → "And my tongue shall softly recite your righteousness, all the day your praise"** (v28) — Hebrew תֶּהְגֶּה, הָגָה "softly recite" (as Ps 1:2); no "of"; וְ "And" (not "Then").

### Notes
- **Imprecatory psalm** — calls for divine judgment against false witnesses (v11).
- **Three rounds** — lament and petition (v1–10, v11–18, v19–28), each ending with a vow of praise (v9–10, 18, 27–28).
- **v19 cited John 15:25** — "they hated me without cause".
