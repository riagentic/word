# Psalm 47

To the overseer. Of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧. A psalm.

[1] All the peoples, clap the palm!
Shout to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with the voice of a ringing cry!

[2] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elyon|Elyon|Most High⟧ is fearsome,
a great King over all the earth.

[3] He subdues peoples under us,
and folks under our feet.

[4] He chooses our inheritance for us,
the exaltation of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ whom he has loved. Selah.

[5] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has gone up with shouting,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with the sound of a ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧.

[6] Sing praises to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, sing praises!
Sing praises to our King, sing praises!

[7] For the King of all the earth is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
sing praises, a maskil.

[8] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ reigns over nations;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sits upon his holy throne.

[9] The nobles of the peoples have gathered,
the people of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧;
for to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ are the shields of the earth;
he is greatly exalted.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD most high" → "Yahweh Elyon"** (v2) — Tetragrammaton + עֶלְיוֹן *Elyon*.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm for the sons of Korah" → "To the overseer. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח מִזְמוֹר, in this order. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" the sons of Korah; "of" leaves it open.
- **"O clap your hands, all ye people" → "All the peoples, clap the palm!"** (v1) — Hebrew כָּל־הָעַמִּים תִּקְעוּ־כָף: the address stands first; עַמִּים is plural, "peoples"; תָּקַע כַּף, literally "strike (thrust) the palm" (the verb of blowing a horn), כַּף singular, no "your".
- **"shout unto God with the voice of triumph" → "Shout to Elohim with the voice of a ringing cry"** (v1) — Hebrew הָרִיעוּ (הֵרִיעַ of voices: "shout") בְּקוֹל רִנָּה. רִנָּה is a ringing cry, here of joy (Ps 17:1 "cry", of prayer: the same word); "triumph" is not in it.
- **"terrible" → "fearsome"** (v2) — Hebrew נוֹרָא, nifal participle of יָרֵא "fear".
- **"He shall subdue the people under us, and the nations" → "He subdues peoples under us, and folks"** (v3) — Hebrew יַדְבֵּר, imperfect (also future, or a wish "may he subdue"); עַמִּים "peoples" and לְאֻמִּים "folks" are two words.
- **"He shall choose … the excellency of Jacob whom he loved" → "He chooses … the exaltation of Jacob whom he has loved"** (v4) — Hebrew יִבְחַר imperfect, as v3; נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; גְּאוֹן "exaltation" (as Exod 15:7; "pride" in the good sense: what Jacob is proud of, the land); אָהֵב perfect.
- **"God is gone up with a shout, the LORD with the sound of a trumpet" → "Elohim has gone up with shouting, Yahweh with the sound of a shofar"** (v5) — Hebrew עָלָה … בִּתְרוּעָה … בְּקוֹל שׁוֹפָר. תְּרוּעָה: the line names neither voice nor instrument, so "shouting" (with the shofar of the next line it may be the horn's blast); קוֹל of a thing is "sound".
- **"For God is the King of all the earth: sing ye praises with understanding" → "For the King of all the earth is Elohim; sing praises, a maskil"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי מֶלֶךְ כָּל־הָאָרֶץ אֱלֹהִים: "King of all the earth" stands first. זַמְּרוּ מַשְׂכִּיל: מַשְׂכִּיל, with no preposition, is the object of the verb, the word of the psalm headings (Ps 32; 42; 44; its meaning is uncertain: "a skilful song", "an instructive song"); "with understanding" takes it as an adverb.
- **"God reigneth over the heathen: God sitteth upon the throne of his holiness" → "Elohim reigns over nations; Elohim sits upon his holy throne"** (v8) — Hebrew מָלַךְ … יָשַׁב, both perfects ("has become king", "has taken his seat"); גּוֹיִם "nations", without the article; כִּסֵּא קָדְשׁוֹ, literally "the throne of his holiness".
- **"The princes of the people are gathered together, even the people of the God of Abraham" → "The nobles of the peoples have gathered, the people of the Elohim of Abraham"** (v9) — Hebrew נְדִיבֵי עַמִּים נֶאֱסָפוּ עַם אֱלֹהֵי אַבְרָהָם. נָדִיב "noble" (literally "willing one", as 1 Sam 2:8); עַמִּים plural; נֶאֱסָפוּ perfect. עַם is pointed "people", standing beside the nobles with no "even" or "as"; the same letters read עִם give "with the people of the Elohim of Abraham" (the Septuagint has "with", from memory): the MT is kept.
- **"for the shields of the earth belong unto God: he is greatly exalted" → "for to Elohim are the shields of the earth; he is greatly exalted"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי לֵאלֹהִים מָגִנֵּי־אֶרֶץ מְאֹד נַעֲלָה: no verb in the first clause, "to Elohim" first; "shields" are the rulers; נַעֲלָה is the nifal of עָלָה, the verb of v5 "has gone up".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–9 = Heb v2–10.
- **Enthronement psalm** — Yahweh's universal kingship.
- **"*Elohim* has gone up"** (v5) — Ascension reference in Christian tradition.
