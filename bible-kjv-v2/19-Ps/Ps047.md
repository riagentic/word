# Psalm 47

To the chief musician. A Psalm of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧.

[1] Clap your hands, all peoples!
Shout to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with loud songs of joy!

[2] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elyon|Elyon|Most High⟧ is awesome,
a great King over all the earth.

[3] He subdued peoples under us,
and nations under our feet.

[4] He chose our heritage for us,
the pride of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ whom he loves. Selah.

[5] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has gone up with a shout,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with the sound of a ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧.

[6] Sing praises to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, sing praises!
Sing praises to our King, sing praises!

[7] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is the King of all the earth;
sing praises with understanding.

[8] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ reigns over the nations;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sits on his holy throne.

[9] The princes of the peoples gather
as the people of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.
For the shields of the earth belong to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
he is highly exalted!

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD most high" → "Yahweh Most High"** (v2) — Tetragrammaton + *Elyon*.
- **Poetic structure** — universal call to praise (v1-4), proclamation of kingship (v5-9).

### Notes
- **Enthronement psalm** — Yahweh's universal kingship.
- **"God is gone up"** — Ascension reference in Christian tradition.
