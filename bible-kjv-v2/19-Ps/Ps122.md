# Psalm 122

A song of the ascents. Of David.

[1] I rejoiced in those who said to me,
"To the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ let us go."

[2] Our feet have been standing
within your gates, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[3] ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, which is built
as a city that is joined together,

[4] where the tribes went up, the tribes of ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧,
a testimony for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
to give thanks to the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] For there thrones for judgment sat,
thrones for the house of David.

[6] Ask for the ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
may those who love you be untroubled.

[7] Let there be ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ within your rampart,
ease within your citadels.

[8] On account of my brothers and my friends
let me speak now: "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧ be within you."

[9] On account of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
let me seek good for you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 9) — Tetragrammaton. **"LORD" → "Yah"** (v4) — Hebrew יָהּ. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"A Song of degrees of David" → "A song of the ascents. Of David."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת לְדָוִד, the first words of v1 (see Ps 120). לְדָוִד "Of David": לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the LORD" → "I rejoiced in those who said to me, 'To the house of Yahweh let us go'"** (v1) — Hebrew שָׂמַחְתִּי בְּאֹמְרִים לִי: שָׂמַח "rejoice"; בְּאֹמְרִים, "in (those) saying", a participle with בְּ (also read "when they said"). בֵּית יְהוָה נֵלֵךְ, "the house of Yahweh" first, נֵלֵךְ "let us go".
- **"Our feet shall stand within thy gates, O Jerusalem" → "Our feet have been standing within your gates, Jerusalem"** (v2) — Hebrew עֹמְדוֹת הָיוּ רַגְלֵינוּ, a participle with the perfect of הָיָה, "have been / were standing" (not a future). No "O": the Hebrew vocative has none.
- **"Jerusalem is builded as a city that is compact together" → "Jerusalem, which is built as a city that is joined together"** (v3) — Hebrew יְרוּשָׁלִַם הַבְּנוּיָה, "Jerusalem, the built one" (the article makes it a relative); שֶׁחֻבְּרָה־לָּהּ יַחְדָּו, pual of חָבַר "join", with לָּהּ "to it / for itself" (not carried into English) and יַחְדָּו "together". No verb "is" is written; v2–5 run on as one sentence.
- **"Whither the tribes go up, the tribes of the LORD, unto the testimony of Israel" → "where the tribes went up, the tribes of Yah, a testimony for Israel"** (v4) — Hebrew שֶׁשָּׁם עָלוּ שְׁבָטִים, "that there tribes went up": a perfect (or "have gone up"), no article. עֵדוּת לְיִשְׂרָאֵל "a testimony for Israel" (עֵדוּת "testimony", not "decree"; how the going up is a testimony is not explained). לְהֹדוֹת "to give thanks" (הוֹדָה).
- **"For there are set thrones of judgment, the thrones of the house of David" → "For there thrones for judgment sat, thrones for the house of David"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי שָׁמָּה יָשְׁבוּ כִסְאוֹת לְמִשְׁפָּט: כִּי "for" (dropped in the former wording); יָשַׁב "sit", here of the thrones themselves (KJV "are set", passive); a perfect. כִּסֵּא "throne"; לְמִשְׁפָּט "for judgment", לְבֵית דָּוִיד "for (of) the house of David".
- **"Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee" → "Ask for the shalom of Jerusalem; may those who love you be untroubled"** (v6) — Hebrew שַׁאֲלוּ שְׁלוֹם, "ask the *shalom* of" (שָׁאַל "ask"; "pray" interprets). יִשְׁלָיוּ, an imperfect of שָׁלָה "be untroubled, at ease" (as Job 12:6), taken as a wish after the command (or "they shall be untroubled"), a sound-play on שָׁלוֹם and יְרוּשָׁלִַם; not "prosper". The quotation marks of the former wording are not in the text.
- **"Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces" → "Let there be shalom within your rampart, ease within your citadels"** (v7) — Hebrew יְהִי־שָׁלוֹם בְּחֵילֵךְ: יְהִי "let there be"; חֵיל "rampart" (singular, as Ps 48:13); שַׁלְוָה "ease" (as Ps 30:6); אַרְמוֹן "citadel" (as Ps 48:3).
- **"For my brethren and companions' sakes, I will now say, Peace be within thee" → "On account of my brothers and my friends let me speak now: 'Shalom be within you'"** (v8) — Hebrew לְמַעַן "on account of" (as v9); רֵעַ "friend"; אֲדַבְּרָה־נָּא, cohortative of דִּבֵּר "speak" with נָא "now"; שָׁלוֹם בָּךְ "*shalom* within you" ("be" supplied; or "is").
- **"Because of the house of the LORD our God I will seek thy good" → "On account of the house of Yahweh our Elohim, let me seek good for you"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲבַקְשָׁה, cohortative of בִּקֵּשׁ "seek"; טוֹב לָךְ "good for you".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **"You"** — from v2 on, Jerusalem is addressed (feminine "you").
