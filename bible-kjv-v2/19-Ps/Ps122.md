# Psalm 122

A Song of Ascents. Of David.

[1] I was glad when they said to me,
"Let us go to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!"

[2] Our feet have been standing
within your gates, O ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!

[3] ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ — built as a city
that is bound firmly together,

[4] to which the tribes go up, the tribes of ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧,
as was decreed for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
to give thanks to the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[5] There thrones for judgment were set,
the thrones of the house of David.

[6] Pray for the peace of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!
"May they be secure who love you!

[7] Peace be within your walls
and security within your towers!"

[8] For my brothers and companions' sake
I will say, "Peace be within you!"

[9] For the sake of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
I will seek your good.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 9) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Yah"** (v4) — shortened Yahweh; retained.
- **Poetic structure** — joy at arriving Jerusalem (v1-2), Zion's civic life (v3-5), prayer for *shalom* (v6-9).
