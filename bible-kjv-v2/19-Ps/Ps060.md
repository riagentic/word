# Psalm 60

To the chief musician. According to ⟦shushan-eduth|Shushan Edut|Shushan Eduth⟧. A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧ of David, for instruction, when he fought against ⟦aram-naharaim|Aram Naharayim|Aram-naharaim⟧ and ⟦aram-zobah|Aram Tzovah|Aram-zobah⟧, and ⟦joab|Yoav|Joab⟧ on his return struck down twelve thousand of Edom in the Valley of Salt.

[1] O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you have rejected us, broken our defenses;
you have been angry; oh, restore us.

[2] You have made the land to quake; you have torn it open;
repair its breaches, for it totters.

[3] You have made your people see hard things;
you have given us wine to drink that made us stagger.

[4] You have set up a banner for those who fear you,
that they may flee to it from the bow. Selah.

[5] That your beloved ones may be delivered,
save with your right hand and answer us!

[6] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has spoken in his holiness:
"With exultation I will divide Shechem
and portion out the Valley of ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧.

[7] ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧ is mine; ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ is mine;
⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ is my helmet;
⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ is my scepter.

[8] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ is my washbasin;
upon Edom I cast my shoe;
over ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧ I shout in triumph."

[9] Who will bring me to the fortified city?
Who will lead me to Edom?

[10] Have you not rejected us, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?
You do not go forth, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, with our armies.

[11] Oh, grant us help against the foe,
for vain is the salvation of man!

[12] With ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ we shall do valiantly;
it is he who will tread down our foes.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 6, 10, 11, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Miktam** — retained in superscription.
- **Shushan-eduth** — musical term, retained.
- **Poetic structure** — national lament (v1-5), divine oracle claiming territories (v6-8), renewed plea (v9-12).

### Notes
- **2 Sam 8** — David's Aramean-Edomite campaigns.
- **Divine oracle** (v6-8) — Yahweh claims tribes and foreign lands.
