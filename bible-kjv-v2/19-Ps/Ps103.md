# Psalm 103

Of David.

[1] Bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my soul,
and all my inward parts, his holy name.

[2] Bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my soul,
and do not forget all his dealings,

[3] who pardons all your iniquities,
who heals all your diseases,

[4] who redeems your life from the pit,
who crowns you with ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and compassion,

[5] who sates your ornament with good;
your youth renews itself like the eagle.

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ does righteous acts
and judgments for all who are extorted from.

[7] He made known his ways to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧,
to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his deeds.

[8] Compassionate and gracious is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
slow to anger and abounding in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧.

[9] He will not contend forever,
and he will not keep anger forever.

[10] Not according to our sins has he done to us,
and not according to our iniquities has he dealt with us.

[11] For as the heavens are high above the earth,
his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ prevails over those who fear him.

[12] As the east is far from the west,
he has put our transgressions far from us.

[13] As a father has compassion on sons,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has compassion on those who fear him.

[14] For he knows our frame;
he remembers that we are dust.

[15] Mortal man — his days are like grass;
like a blossom of the field, so he blossoms.

[16] For a wind passes over it, and it is not,
and its place recognizes it no more.

[17] But the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is from age and to age upon those who fear him,
and his righteousness to sons' sons,

[18] to those who keep his covenant
and to those who remember his precepts, to do them.

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has established his throne in the heavens,
and his kingdom rules over all.

[20] Bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, his messengers,
mighty ones in power, who do his word,
to listen to the voice of his word.

[21] Bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all his hosts,
his ministers, who do his good pleasure.

[22] Bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all his works,
in all the places of his rule.
Bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, my soul.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 6, 8, 13, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Heading "A Psalm of David" → "Of David"** — Hebrew לְדָוִד, the first word of v1; there is no word "psalm". The לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "Of David" leaves it open.
- **"Bless the LORD, O my soul: and all that is within me, bless his holy name" → "Bless Yahweh, my soul, and all my inward parts, his holy name"** (v1) — Hebrew בָּרֲכִי נַפְשִׁי אֶת־יְהוָה וְכָל־קְרָבַי אֶת־שֵׁם קָדְשׁוֹ: no "O" (also v2, 20–22); the verb stands once and serves both lines (the second "bless" is not written); קֶרֶב "inward part", plural; שֵׁם קָדְשׁוֹ, lit. "the name of his holiness". בָּרֲכִי is feminine singular, said to the soul; "your" in v3–5 is the soul too.
- **"forget not all his benefits" → "do not forget all his dealings"** (v2) — Hebrew גְּמוּלָיו, noun of גָּמַל "deal" (v10): what he has dealt; the word itself does not say "benefit" (as 2 Chr 32:25).
- **"Who forgiveth all thine iniquities" → "who pardons all your iniquities"** (v3) — Hebrew סָלַח "pardon", said only of God; v3–5 are participles with the article ("the one who pardons …").
- **"from destruction … with lovingkindness and tender mercies" → "from the pit … with chesed and compassion"** (v4) — Hebrew מִשַּׁחַת "from the pit" (שַׁחַת, as Ps 30:9); עִטֵּר "crown"; רַחֲמִים "compassion".
- **"Who satisfieth thy mouth with good things; so that thy youth is renewed like the eagle's" → "who sates your ornament with good; your youth renews itself like the eagle"** (v5) — Hebrew הַמַּשְׂבִּיעַ בַּטּוֹב עֶדְיֵךְ: עֲדִי is "ornament" (as Ps 32:9); its sense here is uncertain ("mouth" is a guess, as in Ps 32:9; others take it as "your years" or "your desire", from memory). טוֹב "good", singular. The second line has no "so that": תִּתְחַדֵּשׁ, hitpael, "renews itself". נֶשֶׁר is the eagle or the griffon vulture.
- **"executeth righteousness and judgment for all that are oppressed" → "does righteous acts and judgments for all who are extorted from"** (v6) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה צְדָקוֹת … וּמִשְׁפָּטִים, both plural (צְדָקוֹת "righteous acts", as 1 Sam 12:7); עֲשׁוּקִים, passive of עָשַׁק "extort", those wronged by force or fraud (לָחַץ is "oppress").
- **"He made known … his acts unto the children of Israel" → "He made known … to the sons of Israel his deeds"** (v7) — Hebrew יוֹדִיעַ, an imperfect, of what he did for Moses (Exod 33:13); the Hebrew order; עֲלִילוֹת "deeds" (as Ps 9:11).
- **"The LORD is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and plenteous in mercy" → "Compassionate and gracious is Yahweh, slow to anger and abounding in chesed"** (v8) — the words of Exod 34:6, as there: רַחוּם "compassionate" (adjective of רַחֲמִים), חַנּוּן "gracious" (of חֵן "favor"); אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם, lit. "long of nostrils" (אַף "nose" = anger). The Hebrew puts the adjectives first.
- **"He will not always chide: neither will he keep his anger for ever" → "He will not contend forever, and he will not keep anger forever"** (v9) — Hebrew לָנֶצַח "forever" and לְעוֹלָם "forever", two words (Czech keeps them apart); רִיב "contend"; נָטַר "keep, guard", without an object: "anger" is supplied (as Lev 19:18; Jer 3:5); "his" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"He hath not dealt with us after our sins; nor rewarded us according to our iniquities" → "Not according to our sins has he done to us, and not according to our iniquities has he dealt with us"** (v10) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "do", then גָּמַל עַל "deal upon" (the verb of גְּמוּלָיו, v2); the Hebrew order.
- **"For as the heaven is high above the earth, so great is his mercy toward them that fear him" → "For as the heavens are high above the earth, his chesed prevails over those who fear him"** (v11) — Hebrew כִגְבֹהַּ שָׁמַיִם, "as the height of the heavens"; there is no "so"; גָּבַר עַל "prevail over, be strong over" (the root of גִּבּוֹר), not "be great".
- **"As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us" → "As the east is far from the west, he has put our transgressions far from us"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּרְחֹק … הִרְחִיק, one root twice: "be far … put far"; no "so".
- **"Like as a father pitieth his children" → "As a father has compassion on sons"** (v13) — Hebrew רִחַם, verb of רַחֲמִים (v4); בָּנִים "sons", without "his"; no "so" in the second line.
- **"For he knoweth our frame; he remembereth that we are dust" → "For he knows our frame; he remembers that we are dust"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי־הוּא יָדַע יִצְרֵנוּ, the pronoun stressed ("he, he knows"); יֵצֶר, of יָצַר "form", is what is formed (of the heart, Gen 6:5, "inclination"); "frame" says the made thing. זָכוּר, a passive participle, "remembered", here with the sense "mindful".
- **"As for man, his days are as grass: as a flower of the field, so he flourisheth" → "Mortal man — his days are like grass; like a blossom of the field, so he blossoms"** (v15) — Hebrew אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; צִיץ "blossom" and its verb יָצִיץ "he blossoms", one root.
- **"For the wind passeth over it, and it is gone; and the place thereof shall know it no more" → "For a wind passes over it, and it is not, and its place recognizes it no more"** (v16) — Hebrew רוּחַ "wind" (also "spirit, breath"), without the article; עָבְרָה־בּוֹ, lit. "passes in it"; "it" may be the blossom or the man ("him"); וְאֵינֶנּוּ "and it is not"; הִכִּיר "recognize".
- **"from everlasting to everlasting … unto children's children" → "from age and to age … to sons' sons"** (v17) — Hebrew מֵעוֹלָם וְעַד־עוֹלָם, no article, with וְ: "from age and to age" (as 1 Chr 29:10). לִבְנֵי בָנִים "to sons of sons" (as Exod 34:7). וְ at the head: "But", against v15–16. The two lines are the two halves of the Hebrew verse.
- **"his commandments" → "his precepts"** (v18) — Hebrew פִּקֻּדָיו "his precepts" (as Ps 19:8; מִצְוָה is "commandment"); לְ before both participles, "to those who keep … and to those who remember".
- **"hath prepared his throne" → "has established his throne"** (v19) — Hebrew הֵכִין "establish, make firm"; מָשַׁל "rule".
- **"ye his angels, that excel in strength, that do his commandments, hearkening unto the voice of his word" → "his messengers, mighty ones in power, who do his word, to listen to the voice of his word"** (v20) — Hebrew מַלְאָכָיו "his messengers" (the word for "angel"); גִּבֹּרֵי כֹחַ "mighty ones of power"; עֹשֵׂי דְבָרוֹ "doers of his word" (not "commandments"); לִשְׁמֹעַ בְּקוֹל "to listen to the voice of".
- **"ye ministers of his, that do his pleasure" → "his ministers, who do his good pleasure"** (v21) — Hebrew מְשָׁרְתָיו, of שֵׁרֵת "minister"; רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (as Ps 30:5).
- **"in all places of his dominion" → "in all the places of his rule"** (v22) — Hebrew מֶמְשָׁלָה "rule", noun of מָשַׁל (v19).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — the heading לְדָוִד is part of Hebrew v1; the verse numbers are the same.
- **Frame** — the psalm opens and closes with "Bless Yahweh, my soul" (v1, 22); v20–22 call the messengers, the hosts and all his works to do the same.
- **Exod 34:6** — v8 is the formula of Exod 34:6 without "and truth"; v9–13 unfold it.
