# Psalm 33

[1] Shout for joy in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, O you righteous!
Praise is fitting for the upright.

[2] Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with the lyre;
make melody to him with the harp of ten strings.

[3] Sing to him a new song;
play skillfully on the strings, with loud shouts.

[4] For the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is upright,
and all his work is done in faithfulness.

[5] He loves righteousness and justice;
the earth is full of the steadfast love of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] By the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the heavens were made,
and by the breath of his mouth all their host.

[7] He gathers the waters of the sea as a heap;
he puts the deeps in storehouses.

[8] Let all the earth fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him!

[9] For he spoke, and it came to be;
he commanded, and it stood firm.

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ brings the counsel of the nations to nothing;
he frustrates the plans of the peoples.

[11] The counsel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ stands forever,
the plans of his heart to all generations.

[12] Blessed is the nation whose ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the people whom he has chosen as his heritage!

[13] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ looks down from heaven;
he sees all the children of men;

[14] from where he sits enthroned he looks out
on all the inhabitants of the earth,

[15] he who fashions the hearts of them all
and observes all their deeds.

[16] The king is not saved by his great army;
a warrior is not delivered by his great strength.

[17] The war horse is a false hope for salvation,
and by its great might it cannot rescue.

[18] Behold, the eye of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is on those who fear him,
on those who hope in his steadfast love,

[19] that he may deliver their soul from death
and keep them alive in famine.

[20] Our soul waits for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
he is our help and our shield.

[21] For our heart is glad in him,
because we trust in his holy name.

[22] Let your steadfast love, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, be upon us,
even as we hope in you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 18, 20, 21, 22) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — call to praise (v1-3), theological hymn on Yahweh's word (v4-9), sovereignty (v10-17), closing trust (v18-22).

### Notes
- **No superscription** in MT — pairs with Ps 32.
- **Creation-providence hymn** — Yahweh as sovereign.
