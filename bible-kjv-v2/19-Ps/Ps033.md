# Psalm 33

[1] Shout for joy, righteous ones, in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
for the upright praise is fitting.

[2] Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with the lyre;
with a harp of ten strings make music to him.

[3] Sing to him a new song;
play well, with shouting.

[4] For upright is the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and all his work is in faithfulness.

[5] He loves righteousness and judgment;
of the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the earth is full.

[6] By the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the heavens were made,
and by the breath of his mouth all their host.

[7] He gathers as a heap the waters of the sea;
he puts the deeps in treasuries.

[8] Let all the earth fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
of him let all who dwell in the world be afraid.

[9] For he said, and it was;
he commanded, and it stood.

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has made void the counsel of nations;
he has restrained the plans of peoples.

[11] The counsel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall stand forever,
the plans of his heart to generation and generation.

[12] Happy is the nation whose ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
the people he has chosen for an inheritance for himself.

[13] From the heavens ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has looked;
he has seen all the sons of man.

[14] From the established place of his dwelling he has gazed
on all who dwell in the earth,

[15] he who forms their heart together,
he who gives heed to all their deeds.

[16] The king is not saved by the abundance of force;
a mighty one is not delivered by the abundance of power.

[17] A falsehood is the horse for salvation,
and by the abundance of its force it does not rescue.

[18] Behold, the eye of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is toward those who fear him,
toward those who hope for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,

[19] to deliver their soul from death
and to keep them alive in famine.

[20] Our soul has awaited ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
our helper and our shield is he.

[21] For in him our heart rejoices,
for in his holy name we have trusted.

[22] Let your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, be upon us,
as we have hoped for you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 18, 20, 22) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Rejoice in the LORD, O ye righteous: for praise is comely for the upright" → "Shout for joy, righteous ones, in Yahweh; for the upright praise is fitting"** (v1) — Hebrew רַנְּנוּ "shout for joy" (piel of the root of הִרְנִין), in the Hebrew order. נָאוָה "fitting" (lovely, seemly).
- **"Praise the LORD with harp: sing unto him with the psaltery and an instrument of ten strings" → "Give thanks to Yahweh with the lyre; with a harp of ten strings make music to him"** (v2) — Hebrew הוֹדוּ "give thanks"; כִּנּוֹר "lyre", נֵבֶל "harp" (the kinds are uncertain); נֵבֶל עָשׂוֹר is "a harp of ten": "strings" is supplied. זִמֵּר "make music".
- **"play skilfully with a loud noise" → "play well, with shouting"** (v3) — Hebrew הֵיטִיבוּ נַגֵּן בִּתְרוּעָה, "do well at playing" (נִגֵּן of a stringed instrument). תְּרוּעָה names neither voice nor instrument here: "shouting" (it may be the blast of an instrument).
- **"all his works are done in truth" → "all his work is in faithfulness"** (v4) — Hebrew וְכָל־מַעֲשֵׂהוּ בֶּאֱמוּנָה, singular; no verb ("done" is not in the Hebrew). אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness".
- **"He loveth righteousness and judgment: the earth is full of the goodness of the LORD" → "He loves righteousness and judgment; of the steadfast love of Yahweh the earth is full"** (v5) — Hebrew אֹהֵב, a participle with no subject ("loving"): "He" is supplied (so also v7 כֹּנֵס, נֹתֵן). מִשְׁפָּט "judgment". חֶסֶד *chesed* stands first, as in the Hebrew. רוּחַ (v6) is "breath" here (the one word of "spirit", "wind").
- **"He gathereth the waters of the sea together as an heap: he layeth up the depth in storehouses" → "He gathers as a heap the waters of the sea; he puts the deeps in treasuries"** (v7) — Hebrew כֹּנֵס כַּנֵּד מֵי הַיָּם נֹתֵן בְּאֹצָרוֹת תְּהוֹמוֹת. נֵד "heap" (as Exod 15:8); the reading כַּנֹּאד "as in a skin" (as the Greek, "as a wineskin", from memory) is not followed. אוֹצָר "treasury"; תְּהוֹמוֹת "deeps" (plural).
- **"let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him" → "of him let all who dwell in the world be afraid"** (v8) — Hebrew מִמֶּנּוּ יָגוּרוּ כָּל־יֹשְׁבֵי תֵבֵל: גּוּר מִן "be afraid of" (another verb than יָרֵא "fear" in the first line); "of him" first, as in the Hebrew. יִירְאוּ מֵיְהוָה is literally "fear from Yahweh".
- **"he spake, and it was done; he commanded, and it stood fast" → "he said, and it was; he commanded, and it stood"** (v9) — Hebrew הוּא אָמַר וַיֶּהִי הוּא־צִוָּה וַיַּעֲמֹד; אָמַר "say", הָיָה "be", עָמַד "stand".
- **"The LORD bringeth the counsel of the heathen to nought: he maketh the devices of the people of none effect" → "Yahweh has made void the counsel of nations; he has restrained the plans of peoples"** (v10) — Hebrew הֵפִיר עֲצַת־גּוֹיִם הֵנִיא מַחְשְׁבוֹת עַמִּים, two perfects (also v13, 14: "has looked", "has seen", "has gazed"; a hymn may use them of what is always so: "looks", "sees"). הֵפֵר עֵצָה "make void the counsel"; הֵנִיא "restrain" (as Num 32:7). מַחְשָׁבוֹת "plans" (from חָשַׁב "devise"), also v11. Both nouns plural, without the article: "nations", "peoples".
- **"to all generations" → "to generation and generation"** (v11) — Hebrew לְדֹר וָדֹר. תַּעֲמֹד "shall stand" (imperfect).
- **"Blessed" → "Happy"** (v12) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי (as Deut 33:29; 1 Kgs 10:8); "blessed" is בָּרוּךְ. לְנַחֲלָה לוֹ "for an inheritance for himself".
- **"he beholdeth all the sons of men" → "he has seen all the sons of man"** (v13) — Hebrew רָאָה אֶת־כָּל־בְּנֵי הָאָדָם; הִבִּיט "look".
- **"From the place of his habitation he looketh upon all the inhabitants of the earth" → "From the established place of his dwelling he has gazed on all who dwell in the earth"** (v14) — Hebrew מִמְּכוֹן־שִׁבְתּוֹ (as 1 Kgs 8:39); הִשְׁגִּיחַ "gaze" (only here, Song 2:9; Isa 14:16).
- **"He fashioneth their hearts alike; he considereth all their works" → "he who forms their heart together, he who gives heed to all their deeds"** (v15) — Hebrew הַיֹּצֵר יַחַד לִבָּם הַמֵּבִין אֶל־כָּל־מַעֲשֵׂיהֶם, two participles with the article; יָצַר "form"; יַחַד "together" (the heart of them all alike, or all at once); לִבָּם is singular. הֵבִין אֶל "give heed to".
- **"There is no king saved by the multitude of an host: a mighty man is not delivered by much strength" → "The king is not saved by the abundance of force; a mighty one is not delivered by the abundance of power"** (v16) — Hebrew הַמֶּלֶךְ with the article; רָב־חָיִל, רָב־כֹּחַ "abundance of force / of power" (and v17 רֹב חֵילוֹ); חַיִל "force" (of an army; of the horse its strength: one word). גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; נִצַּל "be delivered".
- **"An horse is a vain thing for safety: neither shall he deliver any by his great strength" → "A falsehood is the horse for salvation, and by the abundance of its force it does not rescue"** (v17) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶר הַסּוּס לִתְשׁוּעָה: שֶׁקֶר "falsehood", first; תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation". מִלֵּט "rescue", without an object.
- **"upon them that hope in his mercy" → "toward those who hope for his steadfast love"** (v18) — Hebrew אֶל … לַמְיַחֲלִים לְחַסְדּוֹ; יִחֵל "hope" (also v22); חֶסֶד *chesed* (also v22).
- **"Our soul waiteth for the LORD: he is our help and our shield" → "Our soul has awaited Yahweh; our helper and our shield is he"** (v20) — Hebrew חִכְּתָה, a perfect of חִכָּה "await"; עֵזֶר "helper", in the Hebrew order.
- **"we have trusted in his holy name" → kept** (v21) — Hebrew בְשֵׁם קָדְשׁוֹ, literally "in the name of his holiness". שָׂמַח "rejoice".
- **"according as we hope in thee" → "as we have hoped for you"** (v22) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר יִחַלְנוּ לָךְ, a perfect.

### Notes
- **No superscription** in MT — pairs with Ps 32 (it takes up 32:11 "righteous", "upright").
- **Creation-providence hymn** — call to praise (v1–3), Yahweh's word and work (v4–9), his counsel over the nations (v10–17), closing trust (v18–22).
- **Twenty-two verses** — as many as the letters of the Hebrew alphabet, but not an acrostic.
