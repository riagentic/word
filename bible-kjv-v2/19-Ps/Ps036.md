# Psalm 36

To the chief musician. Of David, the servant of Yahweh.

[1] Transgression speaks to the wicked
deep in his heart;
there is no fear of *Elohim* before his eyes.

[2] For he flatters himself in his own eyes
that his iniquity will not be found out and hated.

[3] The words of his mouth are trouble and deceit;
he has ceased to be wise and to do good.

[4] He plots trouble on his bed;
he sets himself in a way that is not good;
he does not reject evil.

[5] Your steadfast love, O Yahweh, extends to the heavens,
your faithfulness to the clouds.

[6] Your righteousness is like the mountains of El;
your judgments are like the great deep;
man and beast you save, O Yahweh.

[7] How precious is your steadfast love, O *Elohim*!
The children of men take refuge in the shadow of your wings.

[8] They feast on the abundance of your house,
and you give them drink from the river of your delights.

[9] For with you is the fountain of life;
in your light do we see light.

[10] Continue your steadfast love to those who know you,
and your righteousness to the upright of heart!

[11] Let not the foot of arrogance come upon me,
nor the hand of the wicked drive me away.

[12] There the evildoers lie fallen;
they are thrust down, unable to rise.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5, 6) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"great mountains" → "mountains of El"** (v6) — Hebrew *harerei-El*.
- **Poetic structure** — wicked man's portrait (v1-4) contrasted with Yahweh's cosmic love (v5-9); closing prayer (v10-12).

### Notes
- **Contrast** — wicked's nature (v1-4) vs Yahweh's cosmic love (v5-9).
- **"Fountain of life... in your light we see light"** (v9) — classic phrasing.
