# Psalm 36

To the overseer. Of the servant of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, of David.

[1] The declaration of transgression to the wicked
in the midst of my heart:
there is no dread of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ before his eyes.

[2] For he flatters himself in his eyes
to find his iniquity, to hate.

[3] The words of his mouth are wickedness and deceit;
he has ceased to have insight, to do good.

[4] Wickedness he devises on his bed;
he stations himself on a way that is not good;
evil he does not reject.

[5] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, in the heavens is your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
your faithfulness up to the skies.

[6] Your righteousness is like the mountains of ⟦el|El|God⟧;
your judgment is a great deep;
man and beast you save, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[7] How precious is your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!
And the sons of man
take refuge in the shade of your wings.

[8] They drink their fill of the fatness of your house,
and the torrent of your delights you give them to drink.

[9] For with you is the fountain of life;
in your light we see light.

[10] Draw out your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ to those who know you,
and your righteousness to the upright of heart.

[11] Let not the foot of pride come upon me,
and let not the hand of the wicked make me wander.

[12] There the workers of wickedness have fallen;
they have been thrust down and are not able to rise.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (heading, v5, 6) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"O LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5, 6); **"O God" → "Elohim"** (v7) — the vocative stands without "O" in the Hebrew.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David the servant of the LORD" → "To the overseer. Of the servant of Yahweh, of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לְעֶבֶד־יְהוָה לְדָוִד, in the Hebrew order; "A Psalm" (מִזְמוֹר) is not in this heading. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). The two לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to"; "of" leaves it open. עֶבֶד "servant" (the word is "slave").
- **"The transgression of the wicked saith within my heart" → "The declaration of transgression to the wicked in the midst of my heart:"** (v1) — Hebrew נְאֻם־פֶּשַׁע לָרָשָׁע בְּקֶרֶב לִבִּי, word for word. נְאֻם is the word of the prophets' "declares Yahweh" (נְאֻם יְהוָה); only here is its owner "transgression" (פֶּשַׁע). לָרָשָׁע "to the wicked" (or "concerning the wicked"). The Masoretic Text has לִבִּי "my heart"; some Hebrew manuscripts and the ancient versions read "his heart" (from memory), which with "Transgression declares to the wicked within his heart" gives a plain sense; the MT is kept. How the line is meant is uncertain: an oracle about the wicked in the psalmist's heart, or transgression speaking like an oracle to the wicked.
- **"fear of God" → "dread of Elohim"** (v1) — Hebrew פַּחַד אֱלֹהִים (פַּחַד "dread", not יִרְאָה "fear"). The line is cited in Rom 3:18.
- **"For he flattereth himself in his own eyes, until his iniquity be found to be hateful" → "For he flatters himself in his eyes to find his iniquity, to hate"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי־הֶחֱלִיק אֵלָיו בְּעֵינָיו לִמְצֹא עֲוֹנוֹ לִשְׂנֹא, word for word: הֶחֱלִיק "make smooth" (to himself = flatter himself); then two infinitives, לִמְצֹא "to find" and לִשְׂנֹא "to hate", without "and" and without an object for "hate". The sense is uncertain: "too much to find out his iniquity and hate it", or "that his iniquity will (not) be found and hated" (KJV); nothing is supplied.
- **"iniquity and deceit … to be wise, and to do good" → "wickedness and deceit … to have insight, to do good"** (v3) — Hebrew אָוֶן וּמִרְמָה; אָוֶן "wickedness" (also v4, 12; kept apart from עָוֹן "iniquity" of v2); לְהַשְׂכִּיל לְהֵיטִיב, two infinitives without "and"; הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight".
- **"He deviseth mischief upon his bed" → "Wickedness he devises on his bed"** (v4) — Hebrew אָוֶן יַחְשֹׁב, object first, as in the Hebrew (also "evil he does not reject", מָאַס "reject"). הִתְיַצֵּב "station oneself".
- **"Thy mercy, O LORD, is in the heavens" → "Yahweh, in the heavens is your chesed"** (v5) — Hebrew יְהוָה בְּהַשָּׁמַיִם חַסְדֶּךָ; בְּ "in" (not "extends to"); חֶסֶד (token). עַד־שְׁחָקִים "up to the skies" (שְׁחָקִים "skies").
- **"great mountains" → "mountains of El"** (v6) — Hebrew הַרְרֵי־אֵל; אֵל may also give a superlative ("mighty mountains", KJV "great"): the divine name is kept, the reading in Changes.
- **"thy judgments are a great deep" → "your judgment is a great deep"** (v6) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטֶךָ singular; תְּהוֹם רַבָּה without "like" and without the article.
- **"How excellent is thy lovingkindness, O God! therefore the children of men put their trust under the shadow of thy wings" → "How precious is your chesed, Elohim! And the sons of man take refuge in the shade of your wings"** (v7) — Hebrew מַה־יָּקָר חַסְדְּךָ אֱלֹהִים וּבְנֵי אָדָם בְּצֵל כְּנָפֶיךָ יֶחֱסָיוּן; יָקָר "precious"; וּ "And" (not "therefore"); בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man"; צֵל "shade"; חָסָה בְּ "take refuge in". The accents divide the verse in three; some join "Elohim and the sons of man" (gods and men); the accents are followed.
- **"They shall be abundantly satisfied with the fatness of thy house; and thou shalt make them drink of the river of thy pleasures" → "They drink their fill of the fatness of your house, and the torrent of your delights you give them to drink"** (v8) — Hebrew יִרְוְיֻן מִדֶּשֶׁן בֵּיתֶךָ וְנַחַל עֲדָנֶיךָ תַשְׁקֵם; רָוָה "drink one's fill"; דֶּשֶׁן "fatness" (the word of the altar's "fat ashes": here rich food); נַחַל "torrent" (the water itself is meant); עֲדָנִים "delights" (the letters of Eden). Hebrew order of the second line kept.
- **"fountain of life" kept** (v9) — Hebrew מְקוֹר חַיִּים; מָקוֹר "fountain".
- **"O continue thy lovingkindness" → "Draw out your chesed"** (v10) — Hebrew מְשֹׁךְ חַסְדְּךָ, מָשַׁךְ "draw out" (= prolong).
- **"Let not the foot of pride come against me, and let not the hand of the wicked remove me" → "Let not the foot of pride come upon me, and let not the hand of the wicked make me wander"** (v11) — Hebrew אַל־תְּבוֹאֵנִי רֶגֶל גַּאֲוָה; גַּאֲוָה "pride" (of the haughty man; elsewhere "exaltation", the majesty of Yahweh, Deut 33:26, 29); אַל־תְּנִדֵנִי, hifil of נוּד "wander": "make me a wanderer" (KJV "remove").
- **"There are the workers of iniquity fallen: they are cast down, and shall not be able to rise" → "There the workers of wickedness have fallen; they have been thrust down and are not able to rise"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁם נָפְלוּ פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן דֹּחוּ וְלֹא־יָכְלוּ קוּם, three perfects (said as done); דָּחָה "thrust"; פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–12 = Heb v2–13.
- **Contrast** — the wicked man's way (v1–4) set against Yahweh's chesed (v5–9); closing prayer (v10–12).
- **"Fountain of life … in your light we see light"** (v9) — classic phrasing.
