# Psalm 6

To the overseer, with stringed instruments, upon the ⟦sheminith|Sheminit|Sheminith⟧. A psalm of David.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do not reprove me in your anger,
and do not discipline me in your fury.

[2] Show me favor, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for I am languishing;
heal me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for my bones are dismayed.

[3] And my soul is greatly dismayed;
and you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ — how long?

[4] Return, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, pull out my soul;
save me on account of your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧.

[5] For in death there is no remembrance of you;
in Sheol who will give thanks to you?

[6] I am weary with my sighing;
every night I make my bed swim;
with my tears I dissolve my couch.

[7] My eye has been wasted from vexation;
it has grown old because of all my adversaries.

[8] Depart from me, all workers of wickedness,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has heard the voice of my weeping.

[9] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has heard my plea for favor;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ takes my prayer.

[10] All my enemies shall be ashamed and greatly dismayed;
they shall turn back, they shall be ashamed in a moment.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. The Hebrew vocatives have no "O".
- **"To the chief Musician on Neginoth upon Sheminith, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer, with stringed instruments, upon the Sheminit. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ, from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"); בִּנְגִינוֹת "with stringed instruments"; עַל־הַשְּׁמִינִית "upon the Sheminit": the word means "the eighth" (an eighth string, a lower octave, or a tune: the sense is not known); לְדָוִד "of David": the לְ may say "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and is not decided here.
- **"rebuke me not … neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure" → "do not reprove me … do not discipline me in your fury"** (v1) — Hebrew תוֹכִיחֵנִי, from הוֹכִיחַ "reprove"; תְיַסְּרֵנִי, from יִסֵּר "discipline"; בַּחֲמָתְךָ, from חֵמָה "fury" (lit. "heat"); אַף "anger" (also "nose"). In the Hebrew "in your anger" and "in your fury" stand before the verbs.
- **"Have mercy upon me … for I am weak … my bones are vexed" → "Show me favor … for I am languishing … my bones are dismayed"** (v2) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי, from חָנַן "show favor"; אֻמְלַל "languishing" (as 1 Sam 2:5); נִבְהֲלוּ, from נִבְהַל "be dismayed" (also v3, 10).
- **"My soul is also sore vexed: but thou" → "And my soul is greatly dismayed; and you"** (v3) — Hebrew וְנַפְשִׁי … וְאַתָּה, each with וְ. "And you" is the qere וְאַתָּה; the ketiv ואת is a shorter spelling of the same word. **"how long?"** — Hebrew עַד־מָתָי, lit. "until when?".
- **"deliver my soul: oh save me for thy mercies' sake" → "pull out my soul; save me on account of your chesed"** (v4) — Hebrew חַלְּצָה, from חִלֵּץ "pull out"; לְמַעַן חַסְדֶּךָ "on account of your *chesed*" (singular).
- **"in the grave" → "in Sheol"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל. **"remembrance of thee"** — Hebrew זִכְרֶךָ, lit. "your remembrance". **"give thee thanks"** — Hebrew יוֹדֶה, from הוֹדָה "give thanks".
- **"I am weary with my groaning; all the night make I my bed to swim; I water my couch with my tears" → "I am weary with my sighing; every night I make my bed swim; with my tears I dissolve my couch"** (v6) — Hebrew יָגַעְתִּי "I am weary" (the verb "labor": here the weariness that comes from it); בְּאַנְחָתִי, from אֲנָחָה "sighing" (root of נֶאֱנַח "sigh"); בְכָל־לַיְלָה "every night"; אַשְׂחֶה, hifil of שָׂחָה "swim"; אַמְסֶה, hifil of מָסָה "melt, dissolve"; דִּמְעָה "tears". The verse is printed in three lines as before; the Hebrew divides after "my bed".
- **"Mine eye is consumed because of grief; it waxeth old because of all mine enemies" → "My eye has been wasted from vexation; it has grown old because of all my adversaries"** (v7) — Hebrew עָשְׁשָׁה, from עָשֵׁשׁ, found only here and Ps 31:9–10 (meaning uncertain: "be wasted", "grow dim"); כַּעַס "vexation"; עָתְקָה "it has grown old" (lit. "moved on"); צוֹרְרָי, participle of צָרַר "show hostility" (the root of צַר "adversary"; not אֹיֵב "enemy", v10).
- **"all ye workers of iniquity" → "all workers of wickedness"** (v8) — Hebrew כָּל־פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן, as Ps 5:5 (no "you"). **"the voice of my weeping"** — Hebrew קוֹל בִּכְיִי.
- **"my supplication" → "my plea for favor"** (v9) — Hebrew תְּחִנָּתִי, noun of חָנַן (v2). **"will receive" → "takes"** — Hebrew יִקָּח, from לָקַח "take".
- **"Let all mine enemies be ashamed and sore vexed: let them return and be ashamed suddenly" → "All my enemies shall be ashamed and greatly dismayed; they shall turn back, they shall be ashamed in a moment"** (v10) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ "they shall be ashamed" (the same verb twice; may also be read as a wish, "let them be ashamed"); יָשֻׁבוּ "they shall turn back", with no "and"; רָגַע "in a moment".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v10 = Heb v11.
- **First penitential psalm** (of seven: 6, 32, 38, 51, 102, 130, 143).
- **v8 cited by Jesus** (Matt 7:23, Luke 13:27).
