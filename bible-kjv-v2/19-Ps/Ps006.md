# Psalm 6

To the chief musician with stringed instruments, upon the ⟦sheminith|sheminit|Sheminith⟧. A Psalm of David.

[1] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, rebuke me not in your anger,
nor discipline me in your wrath.

[2] Be gracious to me, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for I am languishing;
heal me, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for my bones are troubled.

[3] My soul also is greatly troubled.
But you, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ — how long?

[4] Turn, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, deliver my soul;
save me for the sake of your steadfast love.

[5] For in death there is no remembrance of you;
in Sheol who will give you thanks?

[6] I am weary with my groaning;
every night I flood my bed with tears;
I drench my couch with my weeping.

[7] My eye wastes away from grief;
it grows weak because of all my foes.

[8] Depart from me, all you workers of evil,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has heard the sound of my weeping.

[9] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has heard my plea;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ accepts my prayer.

[10] All my enemies shall be ashamed and greatly troubled;
they shall turn back and be put to shame in a moment.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"grave" → "Sheol"** (v5).
- **"sheminith" — retained** — musical term; "upon the eighth," perhaps octave-register.

### Notes
- **First penitential psalm** (of seven: 6, 32, 38, 51, 102, 130, 143).
- **v8 cited by Jesus** (Matt 7:23, Luke 13:27).
