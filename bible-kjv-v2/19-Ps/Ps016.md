# Psalm 16

A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧ of David.

[1] Preserve me, O ⟦el|El|God⟧, for in you I take refuge.

[2] I say to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "You are ⟦adonai|Adonai|my Lord⟧;
I have no good apart from you."

[3] As for the holy ones — in the land,
they are the excellent, in whom is all my delight.

[4] The sorrows of those who run after another god shall multiply;
their drink offerings of blood I will not pour out,
nor take their names upon my lips.

[5] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is my chosen portion and my cup;
you hold my lot.

[6] The lines have fallen for me in pleasant places;
indeed, I have a beautiful inheritance.

[7] I bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who gives me counsel;
in the night also my inmost being instructs me.

[8] I have set ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ always before me;
because he is at my right hand, I shall not be shaken.

[9] Therefore my heart is glad, and my glory rejoices;
my flesh also dwells secure.

[10] For you will not abandon my soul to Sheol,
or let your faithful one see the pit.

[11] You make known to me the path of life;
in your presence is fullness of joy;
at your right hand are pleasures forevermore.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "El"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"Miktam" — retained** — superscription term; uncertain meaning (perhaps "golden psalm").
- **"hope" → "dwell secure"** (v9) — *la-betach*.
- **"corruption" → "the pit"** (v10) — *shachat*; MT reads "pit," LXX "corruption." Acts 2:27, 13:35 follow LXX for messianic reading.

### Notes
- **Classic Davidic confidence** psalm.
- **v10 cited in Acts 2** — Peter applies to Christ's resurrection.
- **"My right hand" and "path of life"** — deep messianic resonance.
