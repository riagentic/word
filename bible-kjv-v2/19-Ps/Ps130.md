# Psalm 130

A Song of Ascents.

[1] Out of the depths I cry to you, O Yahweh!

[2] O Lord, hear my voice!
Let your ears be attentive
to the voice of my pleas for mercy!

[3] If you, O Yah, should mark iniquities,
O Lord, who could stand?

[4] But with you there is forgiveness,
that you may be feared.

[5] I wait for Yahweh, my soul waits,
and in his word I hope;

[6] my soul waits for the Lord
more than watchmen for the morning,
more than watchmen for the morning.

[7] O Israel, hope in Yahweh!
For with Yahweh there is steadfast love,
and with him is plentiful redemption.

[8] And he will redeem Israel
from all his iniquities.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 7) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Yah"** (v3) — shortened Yahweh; retained.
- **Poetic structure** — depth-cry (v1-2), impossibility of standing before Yah (v3-4), watchman waiting (v5-6), Israel hope (v7-8).

### Notes
- **Sixth penitential psalm** — *De Profundis*.
