# Psalm 42

To the chief musician, a Maskil of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧.

[1] As a deer pants for flowing streams,
so pants my soul for you, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[2] My soul thirsts for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for the living ⟦el|El|God⟧.
When shall I come and appear before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[3] My tears have been my food day and night,
while they say to me all the day long,
"Where is your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[4] These things I remember,
as I pour out my soul:
how I would go with the throng
and lead them in procession to the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
with glad shouts and songs of praise,
a multitude keeping festival.

[5] Why are you cast down, O my soul,
and why are you in turmoil within me?
Hope in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; for I shall again praise him,
my salvation

[6] and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.
My soul is cast down within me;
therefore I remember you
from the land of ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ and of ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧,
from Mount ⟦mizar|Mitzar|Mizar⟧.

[7] Deep calls to deep
at the roar of your waterfalls;
all your breakers and your waves
have gone over me.

[8] By day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commands his steadfast love,
and at night his song is with me,
a prayer to the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of my life.

[9] I say to ⟦el|El|God⟧, my rock:
"Why have you forgotten me?
Why do I go mourning
because of the oppression of the enemy?"

[10] As with a deadly wound in my bones,
my adversaries taunt me,
while they say to me all the day long,
"Where is your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"

[11] Why are you cast down, O my soul,
and why are you in turmoil within me?
Hope in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; for I shall again praise him,
my salvation and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*; Elohist Psalter, Pss 42-72 prefer *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v2, 8, 9) — Hebrew *El*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Maskil** — Hebrew term for instructional/meditative poem; retained in superscription.
- **Poetic structure** — two stanzas each closing with the same refrain (v5 = v11); paired with Ps 43.

### Notes
- **Opens Book II** of the Psalter (Pss 42-72). "Elohist Psalter" — prefers *Elohim* to Yahweh.
- **Sons of Korah** — Levitical singers.
- **Pair with Ps 43** — likely originally one psalm (same refrain).
- **"As a deer pants"** — iconic opening.
