# Psalm 136

[1] Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he is good,
for his steadfast love endures forever.

[2] Give thanks to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of gods,
for his steadfast love endures forever.

[3] Give thanks to the Lord of lords,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[4] to him who alone does great wonders,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[5] to him who by understanding made the heavens,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[6] to him who spread out the earth above the waters,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[7] to him who made the great lights,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[8] the sun to rule over the day,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[9] the moon and stars to rule over the night,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[10] to him who struck down the firstborn of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[11] and brought ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ out from among them,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[12] with a strong hand and an outstretched arm,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[13] to him who divided the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧ in two,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[14] and made ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ pass through the midst of it,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[15] but overthrew ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and his host in the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[16] to him who led his people through the wilderness,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[17] to him who struck down great kings,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[18] and killed mighty kings,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[19] ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧, king of the ⟦amorites|Emorim|Amorites⟧,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[20] and Og, king of Bashan,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[21] and gave their land as a heritage,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[22] a heritage to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his servant,
for his steadfast love endures forever.

[23] It is he who remembered us in our low estate,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[24] and rescued us from our foes,
for his steadfast love endures forever;

[25] he who gives food to all flesh,
for his steadfast love endures forever.

[26] Give thanks to the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of heaven,
for his steadfast love endures forever.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God of gods" → "*Elohim* of gods"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohei ha-Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v26) — Hebrew *El*.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v13, 15) — Hebrew יַם־סוּף *Yam Suph*.
- **Poetic structure** — antiphonal structure; every verse answered by "*ki le'olam chasdo*" (for his *chesed* endures forever); 26-fold repetition.

### Notes
- **"Great Hallel"** — antiphonal response for every verse.
