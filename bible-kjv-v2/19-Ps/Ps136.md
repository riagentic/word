## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"for his mercy endureth for ever" → "for his chesed is forever"** (every verse) — Hebrew כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ, the refrain of 1 Chr 16:34; 2 Chr 5:13: no verb is written ("is" supplied, not "endures"); חֶסֶד is kept as the term; one wording in all 26 verses.
- **"O give thanks unto the God of gods … the Lord of lords" → "Give thanks to the Elohim of gods … the Lord of lords"** (v1–3, 26) — Hebrew הוֹדוּ "give thanks" (the hifil of ידה; no "O"); אֱלֹהֵי הָאֱלֹהִים, אֲדֹנֵי הָאֲדֹנִים as Deut 10:17, each pair one Hebrew word twice (the second word has the article: "of the gods", "of the lords"); אֲדֹנֵי is the construct of אָדוֹן "lord", not the divine form אֲדֹנָי.
- **"To him who alone doeth great wonders" → "to him who does great wonderful deeds alone"** (v4) — Hebrew לְעֹשֵׂה נִפְלָאוֹת גְּדֹלוֹת לְבַדּוֹ: נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds" (as Exod 3:20; מוֹפֵת is "wonder"); לְבַדּוֹ "alone" stands last. The לְ of לְעֹשֵׂה (v4–7, 10, 13, 16, 17) carries on the "give thanks to" of v3.
- **"by wisdom" → "by understanding"** (v5) — Hebrew בִּתְבוּנָה "by understanding" (חָכְמָה is "wisdom").
- **"stretched out the earth above the waters" → "spread out the earth upon the waters"** (v6) — Hebrew רֹקַע, the verb of רָקִיעַ "vault": to beat or stamp flat (piel "beat out" of metal, Exod 39:3); עַל "upon" (or "above").
- **"The sun to rule by day … The moon and stars to rule by night" → "the sun for rule in the day … the moon and stars for rule in the night"** (v7–9) — Hebrew אוֹרִים גְּדֹלִים "great lights", no article (Gen 1:16 has it); לְמֶמְשֶׁלֶת בַּיּוֹם, the noun "rule" with לְ, "in the day" (Gen 1:16 לְמֶמְשֶׁלֶת הַיּוֹם "for the rule of the day"); v9 has the plural לְמֶמְשְׁלוֹת "for rules".
- **"smote Egypt in their firstborn" → "struck the Egyptians in their firstborn"** (v10) — Hebrew לְמַכֵּה מִצְרַיִם בִּבְכוֹרֵיהֶם: the word is מִצְרַיִם, "Egypt", with a plural pronoun "their": the plural is given as "the Egyptians" (as Exod 14). הִכָּה "strike" (also v17).
- **"And brought out Israel from among them" → "and brought out Israel from their midst"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיּוֹצֵא, a narrative form with "and" (also v18, 24; v14, 15, 21 have "and" with the perfect); no subject is written; מִתּוֹכָם "from their midst".
- **"With a strong hand, and with a stretched out arm"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּיָד חֲזָקָה וּבִזְרוֹעַ נְטוּיָה, as Deut 4:34; 5:15: "with … and with".
- **"divided the Red sea into parts" → "cut the Sea of Reeds into pieces"** (v13, 15) — Hebrew לְגֹזֵר יַם־סוּף לִגְזָרִים: גָּזַר "cut" and its noun גְּזָרִים "pieces" (as the covenant pieces, Gen 15:17); יַם־סוּף *Yam Suph*, "Sea of Reeds".
- **"And made Israel to pass through the midst of it" → "and made Israel pass over in its midst"** (v14) — Hebrew הֶעֱבִיר "make pass over"; בְּתוֹכוֹ "in its midst".
- **"But overthrew Pharaoh and his host" → "and shook off Pharaoh and his force"** (v15) — Hebrew וְנִעֵר "and shook off" (as Exod 14:27), with "and", not "but"; חֵילוֹ "his force" (חַיִל of an army).
- **"led his people through the wilderness" → "made his people walk in the wilderness"** (v16) — Hebrew לְמוֹלִיךְ, hifil of הָלַךְ, "make walk" (as Deut 8:2, 15); בַּמִּדְבָּר "in the wilderness".
- **"slew famous kings" → "killed mighty kings"** (v18) — Hebrew הָרַג "kill"; אַדִּירִים "mighty" (as Ps 8:1; Exod 15:10).
- **"Sihon king of the Amorites" → "Sihon, king of the Amorite"** (v19) — Hebrew לְסִיחוֹן מֶלֶךְ הָאֱמֹרִי, the people named in the singular with the article, as Num 21:21; the לְ of v19–20 marks the object of v18 ("killed … Sihon"). In v20 הַבָּשָׁן has the article ("the Bashan"), not shown in English.
- **"for an heritage … Even an heritage" → "for an inheritance … an inheritance"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew נַחֲלָה "inheritance", twice; "Even" is not written. עַבְדּוֹ "his servant" (עֶבֶד, of Yahweh's servant).
- **"Who remembered us in our low estate" kept** (v23) — Hebrew שֶׁבְּשִׁפְלֵנוּ זָכַר לָנוּ: שֶׁ "who" (no "It is he"); זָכַר לָנוּ, lit. "remembered for us"; שֵׁפֶל "low estate" (lowliness).
- **"And hath redeemed us from our enemies" → "and tore us away from our adversaries"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיִּפְרְקֵנוּ, פָּרַק "tear away" (as Ps 7:2; not גָּאַל "redeem"); צָרֵינוּ "our adversaries" (צַר; אֹיֵב is "enemy").
- **"Who giveth food to all flesh" → "who gives bread to all flesh"** (v25) — Hebrew לֶחֶם "bread" (here of food in general).
- **"the God of heaven" → "the El of the heavens"** (v26) — Hebrew לְאֵל הַשָּׁמָיִם: *El*; הַשָּׁמַיִם "the heavens", plural with the article.

### Notes
- **"Great Hallel"** — the refrain answers every line; the psalm moves from creation (v4–9) through the exodus and the conquest (v10–22, as Exod 12–15; Num 21) to the present (v23–25).
