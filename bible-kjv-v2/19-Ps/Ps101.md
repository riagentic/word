# Psalm 101

Of David, a psalm.

[1] ⟦steadfast-love|Chesed|Steadfast love⟧ and judgment let me sing;
to you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, let me make music.

[2] Let me act with insight in a blameless way;
when will you come to me?
I will walk about in the integrity of my heart
within my house.

[3] I will not set before my eyes
a thing ⟦belial|of Beliyyaal|of worthlessness⟧;
the doing of those who swerve I hate;
it shall not cling to me.

[4] A crooked heart shall turn aside from me;
evil I will not know.

[5] One who slanders his neighbor in secret,
him I will annihilate;
one high of eyes and broad of heart,
him I cannot bear.

[6] My eyes are on the faithful of the land,
that they may dwell with me;
he who walks in a blameless way,
he shall minister to me.

[7] There shall not dwell within my house
one who does treachery;
one who speaks lies
shall not be established before my eyes.

[8] Morning by morning I will annihilate
all the wicked of the land,
to cut off from the city of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
all the workers of wickedness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "Of David, a psalm"** (heading) — Hebrew לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר, in this order (as Ps 24). לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open. The heading is part of Hebrew v1.
- **"I will sing of mercy and judgment: unto thee, O LORD, will I sing" → "Chesed and judgment let me sing; to you, Yahweh, let me make music"** (v1) — Hebrew חֶסֶד־וּמִשְׁפָּט אָשִׁירָה לְךָ יְהוָה אֲזַמֵּרָה: the two nouns are the object and stand first; אָשִׁירָה and אֲזַמֵּרָה are cohortatives ("let me"); זִמֵּר "make music"; no "O" (no such word in the Hebrew). Whose chesed and judgment (Yahweh's, or the king's toward his people, v2–8) the verse does not say.
- **"I will behave myself wisely in a perfect way" → "Let me act with insight in a blameless way"** (v2) — Hebrew אַשְׂכִּילָה בְּדֶרֶךְ תָּמִים: הִשְׂכִּיל "act with insight" (also "give heed to"), cohortative; דֶּרֶךְ תָּמִים "a way of the blameless" or "a blameless way" (תָּמִים "blameless", literally "whole").
- **"O when wilt thou come unto me?" → "when will you come to me?"** (v2) — Hebrew מָתַי תָּבוֹא אֵלָי: no "O"; תָּבוֹא may also be "will it come" (the way, feminine), as the verb has the same form.
- **"I will walk within my house with a perfect heart" → "I will walk about in the integrity of my heart within my house"** (v2) — Hebrew אֶתְהַלֵּךְ בְּתָם־לְבָבִי בְּקֶרֶב בֵּיתִי, in this order; הִתְהַלֵּךְ "walk about" (hitpael: habitually); תֹּם "integrity" (root of תָּמִים); בְּקֶרֶב "within" (literally "in the inward part of"), as v7.
- **"I will set no wicked thing before mine eyes" → "I will not set before my eyes a thing of worthlessness"** (v3) — Hebrew לֹא־אָשִׁית לְנֶגֶד עֵינַי דְּבַר־בְּלִיָּעַל: דָּבָר "thing" (or "word"); בְּלִיַּעַל "worthlessness" (token, as Ps 41:8).
- **"I hate the work of them that turn aside; it shall not cleave to me" → "the doing of those who swerve I hate; it shall not cling to me"** (v3) — Hebrew עֲשֹׂה־סֵטִים שָׂנֵאתִי לֹא יִדְבַּק בִּי: עֲשֹׂה, infinitive, "the doing of"; סֵטִים is found only here, taken from שׂוּט / סוּט "swerve" (either "those who swerve" or "swervings"; uncertain; "turn aside" is סוּר, v4); the object stands first; דָּבַק "cling".
- **"A froward heart shall depart from me: I will not know a wicked person" → "A crooked heart shall turn aside from me; evil I will not know"** (v4) — Hebrew לֵבָב עִקֵּשׁ יָסוּר מִמֶּנִּי רָע לֹא אֵדָע: עִקֵּשׁ "crooked"; סוּר "turn aside"; רָע "evil" (also "an evil one": the Hebrew does not say which).
- **"Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour, him will I cut off" → "One who slanders his neighbor in secret, him I will annihilate"** (v5) — Hebrew מְלָשְׁנִי בַסֵּתֶר רֵעֵהוּ אוֹתוֹ אַצְמִית: the qere מְלָשְׁנִי and the ketiv מלושני are two spellings of the participle "one who slanders"; the meaning is the same. בַּסֵּתֶר "in secret"; הִצְמִית "annihilate" (as 2 Sam 22:41; v8; "cut off" is הִכְרִית, v8).
- **"him that hath an high look and a proud heart will not I suffer" → "one high of eyes and broad of heart, him I cannot bear"** (v5) — Hebrew גְּבַהּ־עֵינַיִם וּרְחַב לֵבָב אֹתוֹ לֹא אוּכָל: word for word "high of eyes and broad of heart" (the proud and the arrogant); לֹא אוּכָל is "I am not able", with "him" as its object and no other verb: "bear" is supplied.
- **"Mine eyes shall be upon the faithful of the land" → "My eyes are on the faithful of the land"** (v6) — Hebrew עֵינַי בְּנֶאֶמְנֵי־אֶרֶץ, no verb ("are" supplied); נֶאֱמָן "faithful". **"he shall serve me" → "he shall minister to me"** — Hebrew שֵׁרֵת "minister to" (עָבַד is "serve").
- **"He that worketh deceit shall not dwell within my house" → "There shall not dwell within my house one who does treachery"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא־יֵשֵׁב בְּקֶרֶב בֵּיתִי עֹשֵׂה רְמִיָּה, the verb first; רְמִיָּה "treachery". עֹשֵׂה is "one who does" (Ps 52:2 "worker"): here פֹּעֲלֵי "workers" follows in v8, so the two verbs are kept apart.
- **"he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight" → "one who speaks lies shall not be established before my eyes"** (v7) — Hebrew דֹּבֵר שְׁקָרִים לֹא־יִכּוֹן לְנֶגֶד עֵינָי: דִּבֶּר "speak"; יִכּוֹן nifal of כּוּן "be established"; לְנֶגֶד עֵינַי "before my eyes", as v3.
- **"I will early destroy all the wicked of the land; that I may cut off all wicked doers" → "Morning by morning I will annihilate all the wicked of the land, to cut off … all the workers of wickedness"** (v8) — Hebrew לַבְּקָרִים "at the mornings", that is, every morning; הִצְמִית as v5; לְהַכְרִית "to cut off" (an infinitive); פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness" (as Ps 5:5; 6:8).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of Hebrew v1; the verse numbers are the same.
- **The king's resolve** — the speaker's way, house (v2–7) and city (v8).
