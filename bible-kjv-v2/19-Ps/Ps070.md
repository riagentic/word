# Psalm 70

To the overseer. Of David. To bring to remembrance.

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to deliver me;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, hasten to my help.

[2] Let them be ashamed and be abashed,
those who seek my soul;
let them be turned back and be put to shame,
those who delight in my evil.

[3] Let them turn back because of their shame,
those who say, "Aha! Aha!"

[4] Let them exult and rejoice in you,
all who seek you;
and let them say continually, "Let ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be great,"
those who love your salvation.

[5] And I am afflicted and needy;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, hasten to me.
My help and my rescuer are you;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do not delay.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 4, 5) — Hebrew *Elohim*; an Elohistic psalm (42–83), the names stand as printed.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance" → "To the overseer. Of David. To bring to remembrance"** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לְדָוִד לְהַזְכִּיר; the word "psalm" is not in this heading; מְנַצֵּחַ, participle of the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "the chief musician"); לְהַזְכִּיר, hifil infinitive of זָכַר, "to bring to remembrance" (as Ps 38): what is brought to remembrance, and before whom, is not said; לְ in לְדָוִד may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and "Of David" leaves it open.
- **Ps 70 = Ps 40:13–17** — the wording of Ps 40 is kept where the Hebrew is the same; Ps 70 keeps its own words where they differ (at each verse below), nothing levelled.
- **"Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O LORD" → "Elohim, to deliver me; Yahweh, hasten to my help"** (v1) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים לְהַצִּילֵנִי יְהוָה לְעֶזְרָתִי חוּשָׁה: the first line has no verb (Ps 40:13 has רְצֵה "be pleased" and יְהוָה; "make haste" in the first line is supplied from the second); חוּשׁ "hasten"; הִצִּיל "deliver"; Hebrew order kept.
- **"Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul: let them be turned backward, and put to confusion, that desire my hurt" → "Let them be ashamed and be abashed, those who seek my soul; let them be turned back and be put to shame, those who delight in my evil"** (v2) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ וְיַחְפְּרוּ מְבַקְשֵׁי נַפְשִׁי יִסֹּגוּ אָחוֹר וְיִכָּלְמוּ חֲפֵצֵי רָעָתִי: jussives ("let them"); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", חָפֵר "be abashed" (another root than חָפַר "dig"), נִכְלַם "be put to shame" (as Ps 35:4; Ps 40:14 has "be humiliated"); חָפֵץ "delight in"; רָעָתִי "my evil" (the evil meant for me). Ps 40:14 adds יַחַד "together" and לִסְפּוֹתָהּ "to sweep it away".
- **"Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha" → "Let them turn back because of their shame, those who say, 'Aha! Aha!'"** (v3) — Hebrew יָשׁוּבוּ עַל־עֵקֶב בָּשְׁתָּם; שׁוּב "turn back" (qal, active); עַל־עֵקֶב lit. "on the heel of" (as the result of); הֶאָח "Aha". Ps 40:15 has יָשֹׁמּוּ "let them be appalled" and "to me".
- **"Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified" → "Let them exult and rejoice in you, all who seek you; and let them say continually, 'Let Elohim be great,' those who love your salvation"** (v4) — Hebrew יָשִׂישׂוּ וְיִשְׂמְחוּ בְּךָ כָּל־מְבַקְשֶׁיךָ: שׂוּשׂ "exult", שָׂמַח "rejoice"; וְיֹאמְרוּ "and let them say" (Ps 40:16 has no "and"); תָּמִיד "continually"; יִגְדַּל אֱלֹהִים, a wish, "let Elohim be great" (Ps 40:16 has יְהוָה); יְשׁוּעָתֶךָ "your salvation" (Ps 40:16 תְּשׁוּעָתֶךָ, another word with the same English). The Hebrew order of the lines is kept.
- **"But I am poor and needy: make haste unto me, O God: thou art my help and my deliverer; O LORD, make no tarrying" → "And I am afflicted and needy; Elohim, hasten to me. My help and my rescuer are you; Yahweh, do not delay"** (v5) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן: עָנִי "afflicted", אֶבְיוֹן "needy"; אֱלֹהִים חוּשָׁה־לִּי "Elohim, hasten to me" (Ps 40:17 אֲדֹנָי יַחֲשָׁב לִי "Adonai thinks of me"); עֶזְרִי "my help" (Ps 40:17 עֶזְרָתִי); מְפַלְטִי "my rescuer" (פִּלֵּט); אַל־תְּאַחַר "do not delay" (אִחֵר); יְהוָה in the last line (Ps 40:17 אֱלֹהַי "my Elohim").

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v5 = Heb v6.
- **One psalm in two places** — Ps 70 is Ps 40:13–17 with the differences above; in this Elohistic part of the book אֱלֹהִים stands where Ps 40 has יְהוָה (v1, 4) or אֲדֹנָי (v5), but in the last line of v5 the change goes the other way (Ps 70 יְהוָה, Ps 40 אֱלֹהַי).
