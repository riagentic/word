# Psalm 70

To the chief musician. Of David, for the memorial offering.

[1] Make haste, O *Elohim*, to deliver me!
O Yahweh, make haste to help me!

[2] Let them be put to shame and confusion
who seek my life!
Let them be turned back and brought to dishonor
who delight in my hurt!

[3] Let them turn back because of their shame
who say, "Aha, Aha!"

[4] May all who seek you
rejoice and be glad in you!
May those who love your salvation
say evermore, "*Elohim* is great!"

[5] But I am poor and needy;
hasten to me, O *Elohim*!
You are my help and my deliverer;
O Yahweh, do not delay!

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 4, 5) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — near-duplicate of Ps 40:13-17; brief urgent cry.
