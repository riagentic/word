# Psalm 58

To the overseer. ⟦al-tashcheth|Al-tashchet|Do Not Destroy⟧. Of David. A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧.

[1] Do you indeed in silence speak righteousness?
Do you judge the sons of man in uprightness?

[2] Even in heart you work injustices;
in the land you weigh out the violence of your hands.

[3] The wicked have been estranged from the womb;
they have gone astray from the belly, speakers of a lie.

[4] Venom is theirs like the likeness of the venom of a serpent,
like a deaf cobra that stops its ear,

[5] which does not listen to the voice of charmers,
of one who binds spells, one made skilled.

[6] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, tear down their teeth in their mouth;
break down the fangs of the young lions, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[7] Let them melt away like waters; they go off by themselves;
let him tread his arrows: as though they were cut off.

[8] Like a snail that goes along in melting,
the miscarriage of a woman — they have not beheld the sun.

[9] Before your pots perceive the bramble,
like the living, like burning anger, he will storm it away.

[10] The righteous shall rejoice, for he has beheld vengeance;
he shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked.

[11] And man shall say, "Surely there is fruit for the righteous;
surely there is ⟦elohim|Elohim|a God⟧ judging in the earth."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v6) — Tetragrammaton (Elohistic psalm: it stands here).
- **"To the chief Musician, Altaschith, Michtam of David" → "To the overseer. Al-tashchet. Of David. A Miktam."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת לְדָוִד מִכְתָּם, the first verse of the Hebrew; as Ps 57 (לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", traditional "to the chief musician"; אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת "do not ruin", kept as a name; the לְ of לְדָוִד may be "by", "for" or "belonging to"; *Miktam* of unknown meaning).
- **"Do ye indeed speak righteousness, O congregation?" → "Do you indeed in silence speak righteousness?"** (v1) — Hebrew הַאֻמְנָם אֵלֶם צֶדֶק תְּדַבֵּרוּן, "truly? — silence — righteousness do you speak?": אֵלֶם as printed is "silence" (as the heading of Ps 56), here an adverbial accusative ("in" is supplied). The meaning is uncertain: many read the same letters אֵלִם "gods, mighty ones" (the judges addressed, as Ps 82:1, 6; this is the earlier rendering here), or אֻלָּם "but"; KJV "O congregation" (from memory). The verse follows the printed pointing; the doubt stays open.
- **"do ye judge uprightly, O ye sons of men?" → "Do you judge the sons of man in uprightness?"** (v1) — Hebrew מֵישָׁרִים תִּשְׁפְּטוּ בְּנֵי אָדָם: בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man" taken as the object; it can also be the ones addressed ("O sons of man", KJV). מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness" (plural noun, used as an adverb).
- **"Yea, in heart ye work wickedness; ye weigh the violence of your hands in the earth" → "Even in heart you work injustices; in the land you weigh out the violence of your hands"** (v2) — Hebrew אַף "even" (not "no"); בְּלֵב "in heart" (no "your"); עוֹלֹת "injustices" (עַוְלָה, the noun of עָוֶל "injustice"); פָּעַל "work"; פִּלֵּס "weigh out, make level"; בָּאָרֶץ stands at the head of the second line (Hebrew accents): "in the land" or "on the earth".
- **"they go astray as soon as they be born, speaking lies" → "they have gone astray from the belly, speakers of a lie"** (v3) — Hebrew זֹרוּ (זוּר "be estranged") and תָּעוּ, perfects; מֵרָחֶם "from the womb" · מִבֶּטֶן "from the belly"; דֹּבְרֵי כָזָב "speakers of a lie".
- **"Their poison is like the poison of a serpent: they are like the deaf adder that stoppeth her ear" → "Venom is theirs like the likeness of the venom of a serpent, like a deaf cobra that stops its ear"** (v4) — Hebrew חֲמַת־לָמוֹ כִּדְמוּת חֲמַת־נָחָשׁ: חֵמָה "heat, fury", of a snake "venom" (one word, the "fury" row: note); דְּמוּת "likeness"; פֶּתֶן "cobra" (kind uncertain). The verb יַאְטֵם is imperfect: "stops (shuts)".
- **"charming never so wisely" → "of one who binds spells, one made skilled"** (v5) — Hebrew מְלַחֲשִׁים "charmers" (lit. "whisperers"); חוֹבֵר חֲבָרִים "a binder of bindings" (a caster of spells); מְחֻכָּם pual "made wise, skilled".
- **"Break their teeth, O God, in their mouth: break out the great teeth of the young lions, O LORD" → "Elohim, tear down their teeth in their mouth; break down the fangs of the young lions, Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew הֲרָס "tear down" (הָרַס) and נְתֹץ "break down" (נָתַץ), two verbs; מַלְתְּעוֹת "fangs, jaw teeth" (a transposed form of מְתַלְּעוֹת); כְּפִירִים "young lions".
- **"Let them melt away as waters which run continually: when he bendeth his bow to shoot his arrows, let them be as cut in pieces" → "Let them melt away like waters; they go off by themselves; let him tread his arrows: as though they were cut off"** (v7) — Hebrew יִמָּאֲסוּ, written as the nifal of מָאַס "reject" ("let them be rejected"); taken as a by-form of מָסַס "melt" (as Job 7:5). יִתְהַלְּכוּ־לָמוֹ "they walk off for themselves". יִדְרֹךְ "let him tread (bend)": the bow is not named; the subject is not named. חִצָּיו, the qere, "his arrows"; the written form חצו is "his arrow". כְּמוֹ יִתְמֹלָלוּ "as though they were cut off" (or "withered"). The whole verse is uncertain; the bare imperfects may be wishes or statements.
- **"As a snail which melteth, let every one of them pass away: like the untimely birth of a woman, that they may not see the sun" → "Like a snail that goes along in melting, the miscarriage of a woman — they have not beheld the sun"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׁבְּלוּל "snail" (only here; uncertain); תֶּמֶס "melting" (only here; noun of מָסַס); יַהֲלֹךְ "goes"; נֵפֶל אֵשֶׁת "the miscarriage of a woman" (נֵפֶל, from נָפַל "fall"; אֵשֶׁת construct); בַּל־חָזוּ, perfect plural of חָזָה "behold": "they have not beheld".
- **"Before your pots can feel the thorns, he shall take them away as with a whirlwind, both living, and in his wrath" → "Before your pots perceive the bramble, like the living, like burning anger, he will storm it away"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּטֶרֶם יָבִינוּ סִּירֹתֵיכֶם אָטָד, "before your pots perceive (בִּין) the bramble" (סִירוֹת "pots"; the same letters can be "thorns"; אָטָד "bramble", as Judg 9:14); כְּמוֹ־חַי כְּמוֹ־חָרוֹן "like the living, like burning (anger)" (חַי "living", of flesh "raw"; חָרוֹן "burning anger"); יִשְׂעָרֶנּוּ "he will storm it away" (שָׂעַר "storm"; "it" singular). The meaning of the verse is very uncertain.
- **"The righteous shall rejoice when he seeth the vengeance" → "The righteous shall rejoice, for he has beheld vengeance"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי־חָזָה נָקָם, כִּי "for", perfect of חָזָה "behold"; נָקָם without the article. פְּעָמָיו "his feet" (as 57:6); רָחַץ "wash".
- **"So that a man shall say, Verily there is a reward for the righteous: verily he is a God that judgeth in the earth" → "And man shall say, 'Surely there is fruit for the righteous; surely there is Elohim judging in the earth'"** (v11) — Hebrew וְיֹאמַר אָדָם "and man shall say"; פְּרִי "fruit" (not "reward"); אַךְ "surely" twice; אֱלֹהִים שֹׁפְטִים with a plural participle: "Elohim judging" (the one God, as the plural agrees with the form of the word) or "gods judging"; both readings are possible.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–11 = Heb v2–12.
- **Imprecatory psalm** against unjust judges: v1–5 the charge, v6–9 the curses, v10–11 the outcome.
- **v1** — the word אֵלֶם is the main doubt of the psalm: "silence" as printed, "gods / mighty ones" as often read.
