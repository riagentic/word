# Psalm 97

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ reigns; let the earth be glad;
let the many coastlands rejoice.

[2] Cloud and dark cloud are around him;
righteousness and judgment are the established place of his throne.

[3] Fire goes before him
and sets ablaze his adversaries all around.

[4] His lightnings have lit up the world;
the earth has seen and has been in anguish.

[5] The mountains like wax have melted before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
before the Lord of all the earth.

[6] The heavens have told his righteousness,
and all the peoples have seen his glory.

[7] Let all who serve a carved image be ashamed,
who glory in worthless idols;
bow down to him, all gods.

[8] ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ has heard and has rejoiced,
and the daughters of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ have been glad,
on account of your judgments, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[9] For you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, are ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ over all the earth;
greatly you have been exalted above all gods.

[10] You who love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, hate evil!
He keeps the souls of his faithful ones;
from the hand of the wicked he delivers them.

[11] Light is sown for the righteous,
and for the upright of heart gladness.

[12] Rejoice, righteous ones, in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and give thanks to the remembrance of his holiness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 8, 9, 10, 12) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"gods"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew *elohim* (plural); other gods, plain text.
- **Vocative** (v8, 9) — no "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"The LORD reigneth; let the earth rejoice; let the multitude of isles be glad thereof" → "Yahweh reigns; let the earth be glad; let the many coastlands rejoice"** (v1) — Hebrew מָלָךְ, a perfect: also "has become king" (as Ps 96:10). תָּגֵל "be glad" (גִּיל), יִשְׂמְחוּ "rejoice" (שָׂמַח): the earlier wording had the two verbs exchanged. אִיִּים "coastlands" (coasts or islands: lands reached by sea).
- **"Clouds and darkness are round about him: righteousness and judgment are the habitation of his throne" → "Cloud and dark cloud are around him; righteousness and judgment are the established place of his throne"** (v2) — Hebrew עָנָן "cloud" (singular), עֲרָפֶל "dark cloud" (as Exod 20:21); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; מְכוֹן "established place" (as Exod 15:17; Ps 33:14).
- **"and burneth up his enemies round about" → "and sets ablaze his adversaries all around"** (v3) — Hebrew וּתְלַהֵט, piel of לָהַט "blaze" (the root of לַהַט "flame"); צָרָיו "his adversaries", in the Hebrew order after "all around".
- **"His lightnings enlightened the world: the earth saw, and trembled" → "His lightnings have lit up the world; the earth has seen and has been in anguish"** (v4) — Hebrew הֵאִירוּ, רָאֲתָה, perfects, and וַתָּחֵל: a hymn may use them of what is always so ("light up", "sees"). חִיל "be in anguish", lit. "writhe" (as Ps 96:9).
- **"The hills melted like wax at the presence of the LORD" → "The mountains like wax have melted before Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew הָרִים "mountains"; כַּדּוֹנַג "like wax", in the Hebrew order; נָמַסּוּ "have melted" (נָמֵס); מִלִּפְנֵי, lit. "from before", twice. אֲדוֹן כָּל־הָאָרֶץ "the Lord of all the earth" (אָדוֹן, not אֲדֹנָי; as Josh 3:11, 13).
- **"The heavens declare his righteousness, and all the people see his glory" → "The heavens have told his righteousness, and all the peoples have seen his glory"** (v6) — Hebrew הִגִּידוּ "have told" (הִגִּיד) and רָאוּ, perfects; עַמִּים "peoples" (plural).
- **"Confounded be all they that serve graven images, that boast themselves of idols: worship him, all ye gods" → "Let all who serve a carved image be ashamed, who glory in worthless idols; bow down to him, all gods"** (v7) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ "let … be ashamed" (בּוֹשׁ; or "they shall be ashamed"); עָבַד "serve"; פֶּסֶל "carved image" (singular); הִתְהַלֵּל בְּ "glory in" (as 1 Chr 16:10); אֱלִילִים "worthless idols". הִשְׁתַּחֲווּ־לוֹ may be a command ("bow down to him") or a perfect ("all gods have bowed down to him"); "you" is not written. Heb 1:6 "let all the angels of God worship him" follows the Greek (of Deut 32:43, or of this verse, where the Greek has "all his angels": from memory).
- **"Zion heard, and was glad; and the daughters of Judah rejoiced because of thy judgments" → "Zion has heard and has rejoiced, and the daughters of Judah have been glad, on account of your judgments"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁמְעָה וַתִּשְׂמַח "has heard and has rejoiced" (שָׂמַח), וַתָּגֵלְנָה "have been glad" (גִּיל); לְמַעַן "on account of".
- **"For thou, LORD, art high above all the earth: thou art exalted far above all gods" → "For you, Yahweh, are Elyon over all the earth; greatly you have been exalted above all gods"** (v9) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן *Elyon* ("Most High"); מְאֹד "greatly", first, as in the Hebrew; נַעֲלֵיתָ, nifal perfect of עָלָה "go up": "you have been exalted" (lit. "taken up").
- **"Ye that love the LORD, hate evil: he preserveth the souls of his saints" → "You who love Yahweh, hate evil! He keeps the souls of his faithful ones"** (v10) — Hebrew אֹהֲבֵי יְהוָה "lovers of Yahweh" (no "O"); שֹׁמֵר "keeps" (שָׁמַר); נַפְשׁוֹת "souls" (not "lives"); חֲסִידָיו "his faithful ones", those who keep *chesed* (no token: the token is for the noun). הִצִּיל "deliver".
- **"and gladness for the upright in heart" → "and for the upright of heart gladness"** (v11) — Hebrew וּלְיִשְׁרֵי־לֵב שִׂמְחָה, in the Hebrew order (as Ps 32:11 "upright of heart"); שִׂמְחָה "gladness"; לַצַּדִּיק "for the righteous" (singular, the righteous man). זָרֻעַ "sown"; many read זָרַח "has dawned", as the Greek and other versions (from memory): the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Rejoice in the LORD, ye righteous; and give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness" → "Rejoice, righteous ones, in Yahweh, and give thanks to the remembrance of his holiness"** (v12) — Hebrew שִׂמְחוּ צַדִּיקִים בַּיהוָה, in the Hebrew order; צַדִּיקִים without the article "righteous ones" (as Ps 33:1); the second line as Ps 30:4: לְזֵכֶר "to the remembrance" (זֵכֶר, how he is remembered and named, Exod 3:15).

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew.
- **Structure** — the appearing of Yahweh the king (v1–6), the gods and their servants put to shame, Zion glad (v7–9), the call to the righteous (v10–12).
