# Psalm 117

[1] Praise Yahweh, all nations!
Extol him, all peoples!

[2] For great is his steadfast love toward us,
and the faithfulness of Yahweh endures forever.
Hallelujah!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — shortest psalm; universal call (v1) + reason: *chesed* endures (v2).

### Notes
- **Shortest psalm** (and shortest chapter in Bible).
- **Cited Rom 15:11** on Gentile praise.
