# Psalm 126

A song of the ascents.

[1] When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ restored the fortunes of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
we were like those who dream.

[2] Then our mouth was filled with laughter,
and our tongue with a ringing cry;
then they said among the nations,
"⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has done great things with these."

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has done great things with us;
we were glad.

[4] Restore our fortunes, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
like channels in the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧.

[5] Those who sow in tears
with a ringing cry shall reap.

[6] He surely goes, and weeps,
bearing the drawing of the seed;
he surely comes with a ringing cry,
bearing his sheaves.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 4) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees" → "A song of the ascents"** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת, the first words of v1; מַעֲלָה "a going up, a step", with the article.
- **"When the LORD turned again the captivity of Zion" → "When Yahweh restored the fortunes of Zion"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּשׁוּב יְהוָה אֶת־שִׁיבַת צִיּוֹן, literally "when Yahweh turned back the return / turning of Zion". שִׁיבָה stands only here, from שׁוּב "return, turn back", where the idiom elsewhere has שְׁבוּת (v4). One idiom, one rendering: "restore the fortunes"; the traditional "turned the captivity" reads the noun from שָׁבָה "take captive".
- **"Then was our mouth filled … then said they among the heathen" → "Then our mouth was filled … then they said among the nations"** (v2) — Hebrew אָז יִמָּלֵא … אָז יֹאמְרוּ, imperfects after אָז "then", which speak here of the time of v1 (past); a future reading, "then our mouth will be filled", is also defended. **"singing" → "a ringing cry"** — Hebrew רִנָּה (also v5, 6). **"hath done great things for them" → "has done great things with these"** — Hebrew הִגְדִּיל לַעֲשׂוֹת עִם־אֵלֶּה, literally "has made great to do with these": עִם "with", אֵלֶּה "these".
- **"hath done great things for us; whereof we are glad" → "has done great things with us; we were glad"** (v3) — Hebrew עִמָּנוּ "with us"; הָיִינוּ שְׂמֵחִים, the same "we were" as v1; no "whereof".
- **"Turn again our captivity, O LORD, as the streams in the south" → "Restore our fortunes, Yahweh, like channels in the Negev"** (v4) — Hebrew שׁוּבָה יְהוָה אֶת־שְׁבִיתֵנוּ: the written text (ketiv) has שבותנו, the reading (qere) שְׁבִיתֵנוּ; the same word in two spellings, the meaning does not differ. One rendering with v1 (literally "turn back our turning / our captivity"). No "O". אֲפִיקִים "channels", stream beds of the dry south that fill with the rains; הַנֶּגֶב the region (the token keeps the name).
- **"shall reap in joy" → "with a ringing cry shall reap"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּרִנָּה יִקְצֹרוּ, the Hebrew order kept (בְּדִמְעָה "in tears" against בְּרִנָּה "with a ringing cry").
- **"He that goeth forth and weepeth … shall doubtless come again with rejoicing" → "He surely goes, and weeps … he surely comes with a ringing cry"** (v6) — Hebrew הָלוֹךְ יֵלֵךְ וּבָכֹה … בֹּא־יָבוֹא בְרִנָּה, infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "going he goes, and weeping … coming he comes"; "surely" gives its force (here also of going on and on). No subject is named, and יֵלֵךְ is "goes", not "goes forth". Both verbs are imperfects; "he shall surely come" is also possible. **"bearing precious seed" → "bearing the drawing of the seed"** — Hebrew מֶשֶׁךְ הַזָּרַע, from מָשַׁךְ "draw": what is drawn out for sowing, or the bag it is drawn from (as Amos 9:13 מֹשֵׁךְ הַזָּרַע "the one who draws the seed"); the meaning is uncertain. **"bringing his sheaves with him" → "bearing his sheaves"** — the same נֹשֵׂא "bearing" as the second line; "with him" is not in the Hebrew. אֲלֻמָּה "sheaf" (as Gen 37:7).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew too; the numbers agree.
- **Poetic structure** — the restoration remembered (v1–3), prayer for restoration (v4), sowing in tears and reaping with a ringing cry (v5–6).
