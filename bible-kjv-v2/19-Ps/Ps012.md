# Psalm 12

To the overseer, upon the ⟦sheminith|Sheminit|Sheminith⟧. A psalm of David.

[1] Save, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for the faithful one has come to an end;
for the trustworthy have vanished from the sons of man.

[2] Emptiness they speak, each man with his neighbor;
with a lip of smooth things, with a heart and a heart they speak.

[3] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut off all lips of smooth things,
the tongue that speaks great things,

[4] who have said, "With our tongue we will prevail;
our lips are with us; who is lord to us?"

[5] "Because of the despoiling of the afflicted, because of the groaning of the needy,
now I will arise," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
"I will set in salvation the one he puffs at."

[6] The sayings of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are pure sayings,
silver refined in a furnace of the earth,
purified sevenfold.

[7] You, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, will keep them;
you will guard him from this generation forever.

[8] All around the wicked walk about,
when vileness is exalted for the sons of man.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"To the chief Musician upon Sheminith, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer, upon the Sheminit. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ "To the overseer" (נִצַּח "oversee", 1 Chr 15:21; traditionally "the chief musician"); עַל־הַשְּׁמִינִית "upon the Sheminit", with the article: "the eighth" (an eighth string, or a lower octave: meaning unknown; as Ps 6; 1 Chr 15:21). לְדָוִד "of David": לְ can say "by", "for" or "belonging to".
- **"Help, LORD; for the godly man ceaseth; for the faithful fail from among the children of men" → "Save, Yahweh, for the faithful one has come to an end; for the trustworthy have vanished from the sons of man"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹשִׁיעָה "save"; גָמַר "come to an end"; חָסִיד "faithful one" (one who keeps *chesed*); אֱמוּנִים "the trustworthy" (from אָמַן, another word: kept apart from חָסִיד), or "faithfulness"; פַּסּוּ "vanish" (only here); בְּנֵי אָדָם "the sons of man" (also v8).
- **"They speak vanity every one with his neighbour: with flattering lips and with a double heart do they speak" → "Emptiness they speak, each man with his neighbor; with a lip of smooth things, with a heart and a heart they speak"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁוְא "emptiness" (what has nothing in it, false or worthless); אִישׁ אֶת־רֵעֵהוּ "each man with his neighbor"; שְׂפַת חֲלָקוֹת "a lip of smooth things" (= flattery; "with" supplied: the Hebrew sets the noun without a preposition); בְּלֵב וָלֵב "with a heart and a heart" (= one heart for the words, another within).
- **"The LORD shall cut off all flattering lips, and the tongue that speaketh proud things" → "May Yahweh cut off all lips of smooth things, the tongue that speaks great things"** (v3) — Hebrew יַכְרֵת, the short form (jussive): a wish; שִׂפְתֵי חֲלָקוֹת as v2; גְּדֹלוֹת "great things".
- **"With our tongue will we prevail; our lips are our own: who is lord over us?" → "With our tongue we will prevail; our lips are with us; who is lord to us?"** (v4) — Hebrew לִלְשֹׁנֵנוּ נַגְבִּיר, hifil of גָּבַר "prevail" (lit. "we will show might to / by our tongue"); שְׂפָתֵינוּ אִתָּנוּ "our lips are with us"; מִי אָדוֹן לָנוּ "who is lord to us".
- **"For the oppression of the poor, for the sighing of the needy … I will set him in safety from him that puffeth at him" → "Because of the despoiling of the afflicted, because of the groaning of the needy … I will set in salvation the one he puffs at"** (v5) — Hebrew מִשֹּׁד "because of the despoiling" (שֹׁד "violence, ruin"); עֲנִיִּים "afflicted"; אֶבְיוֹנִים "needy"; אֲנָקָה "groaning". אָשִׁית בְּיֵשַׁע יָפִיחַ לוֹ, lit. "I will set in salvation — he puffs at him": "the one" is supplied (a relative clause without אֲשֶׁר); יָפִיחַ as 10:5 "puff at" (= scorn). Others read "for which he pants" or "he shall testify for him"; KJV "from him that puffeth" supplies "from". Uncertain. יֵשַׁע "salvation".
- **"The words of the LORD are pure words: as silver tried in a furnace of earth, purified seven times" → "The sayings of Yahweh are pure sayings, silver refined in a furnace of the earth, purified sevenfold"** (v6) — Hebrew אִמְרוֹת … אֲמָרוֹת "sayings" (אִמְרָה, as 2 Sam 22:31; דָּבָר is "word"); "as" is not written; צָרוּף "refined"; בַּעֲלִיל (only here) "in a furnace" or "crucible" (uncertain); לָאָרֶץ "of / to the earth" (uncertain: a furnace on the ground, or refined "to the earth"); מְזֻקָּק "purified"; שִׁבְעָתָיִם "sevenfold".
- **"thou shalt preserve them from this generation for ever" → "you will guard him from this generation forever"** (v7) — Hebrew תִּשְׁמְרֵם "you will keep them"; תִּצְּרֶנּוּ "you will guard him" (the suffix is third person singular: each one of them, or the afflicted of v5; "us" would be another form). שָׁמַר "keep", נָצַר "guard"; הַדּוֹר זוּ "this generation".
- **"The wicked walk on every side, when the vilest men are exalted" → "All around the wicked walk about, when vileness is exalted for the sons of man"** (v8) — Hebrew סָבִיב "all around"; יִתְהַלָּכוּן "walk about"; כְּרֻם "when (it) is exalted"; זֻלּוּת (only here) "vileness" (uncertain; "the vilest men" makes it persons); לִבְנֵי אָדָם "for the sons of man" (or "among").

### Notes
- **The Hebrew numbering** — the Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–8 = Heb v2–9. Our verse numbers are kept.
- **Lips and sayings** — the smooth lips of men (v2–4) are set against the sayings of Yahweh, tried like silver (v6).
