# Psalm 12

To the chief musician upon the ⟦sheminith|sheminit|Sheminith⟧. A Psalm of David.

[1] Save, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for the faithful one — is gone;
the faithful have vanished from among the children of men.

[2] Everyone utters lies to his neighbor;
with flattering lips and a double heart they speak.

[3] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut off all flattering lips,
the tongue that speaks great things,

[4] those who say, "With our tongue we will prevail;
our lips are with us; who is master over us?"

[5] "Because the poor are plundered, because the needy groan,
I will now arise," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
"I will place him in the safety for which he longs."

[6] The words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are pure words,
like silver refined in a furnace on the ground,
purified seven times.

[7] You, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, will keep them;
you will guard us from this generation forever.

[8] On every side the wicked prowl,
as vileness is exalted among the children of men.
