# Psalm 116

[1] I love, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ hears
my voice, my pleas for favor.

[2] For he has inclined his ear to me,
and in my days I will call.

[3] The cords of death encompassed me,
and the straits of Sheol found me;
distress and sorrow I found.

[4] And on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I called:
"Ah, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, rescue my soul!"

[5] Gracious is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and righteous;
and our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has compassion.

[6] A keeper of the simple is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
I was brought low, and he saved me.

[7] Return, my soul, to your resting places,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has dealt with you.

[8] For you have pulled out my soul from death,
my eye from tears,
my foot from stumbling.

[9] I will walk before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
in the lands of the living.

[10] I believed, for I speak;
I, I was greatly afflicted.

[11] I, I said in my alarm,
"All mankind is a liar."

[12] What shall I return to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
all his dealings toward me?

[13] The cup of salvations I will lift up,
and on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will call.

[14] My vows to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will repay,
in the presence, please, of all his people.

[15] Precious in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
is the death of his faithful ones.

[16] Ah, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for I am your servant;
I am your servant, the son of your slave woman;
you have loosed my bonds.

[17] To you I will sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving,
and on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will call.

[18] My vows to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I will repay,
in the presence, please, of all his people,

[19] in the courts of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
in your midst, ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"hell/grave" → "Sheol"** (v3) — Hebrew *Sheol*.
- **"I love the LORD, because he hath heard my voice and my supplications" → "I love, for Yahweh hears my voice, my pleas for favor"** (v1) — Hebrew אָהַבְתִּי כִּי־יִשְׁמַע יְהוָה: "I love" has no object ("the LORD" is supplied in KJV; also possible: "I love it that Yahweh hears"). יִשְׁמַע is an imperfect, "hears" (KJV past). קוֹלִי תַּחֲנוּנָי side by side, no "and"; תַּחֲנוּנִים "pleas for favor" (as Ps 28:2).
- **"therefore will I call upon him as long as I live" → "and in my days I will call"** (v2) — Hebrew וּבְיָמַי אֶקְרָא, "and in my days"; "upon him" is not written. הִטָּה אָזְנוֹ "inclined his ear", a perfect.
- **"The sorrows of death compassed me, and the pains of hell gat hold upon me: I found trouble and sorrow" → "The cords of death encompassed me, and the straits of Sheol found me; distress and sorrow I found"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲפָפוּנִי חֶבְלֵי־מָוֶת as Ps 18:4 ("cords", the חֶבֶל row; not "pangs", "sorrows"). מְצָרֵי שְׁאוֹל "the straits of Sheol" (מֵצַר "a narrow place, distress"; as Ps 118:5; Lam 1:3). מְצָאוּנִי … אֶמְצָא, the one verb מָצָא "find" twice. צָרָה "distress", יָגוֹן "sorrow" (rows); אֶמְצָא and אֶקְרָא (v4) are imperfects, told as past.
- **"Then called I … O LORD, I beseech thee, deliver my soul" → "And on the name of Yahweh I called: 'Ah, Yahweh, rescue my soul!'"** (v4, 16) — Hebrew וּבְשֵׁם־יְהוָה אֶקְרָא, "and" (not "then"), the Hebrew order; קָרָא בְּשֵׁם "call on the name" (row; also v13, 17). אָנָּה "Ah" (an earnest plea, as 2 Kgs 20:3); מִלֵּט "rescue".
- **"our God is merciful" → "and our Elohim has compassion"** (v5) — Hebrew וֵאלֹהֵינוּ מְרַחֵם, "and", a participle of רִחַם "have compassion" (row). חַנּוּן "gracious" (row).
- **"The LORD preserveth the simple: I was brought low, and he helped me" → "A keeper of the simple is Yahweh; I was brought low, and he saved me"** (v6) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר פְּתָאיִם יְהוָה, a participle first; שָׁמַר "keep". דַּלּוֹתִי "I was brought low" (דָּלַל, the root of דַּל "poor"); וְלִי יְהוֹשִׁיעַ "and he saved me" (an imperfect after the perfect, written with the ה kept; "saves" is also possible). הוֹשִׁיעַ "save" (not "help").
- **"Return unto thy rest, O my soul; for the LORD hath dealt bountifully with thee" → "Return, my soul, to your resting places, for Yahweh has dealt with you"** (v7) — Hebrew שׁוּבִי נַפְשִׁי לִמְנוּחָיְכִי: no "O", the Hebrew order, מְנוּחָה "resting place" (as Ps 95:11) in the plural; the ‑יְכִי suffixes (also v19) are an old feminine form. גָּמַל עַל "deal with" (as Ps 13:6): "bountifully" is not written.
- **"For thou hast delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, and my feet from falling" → "For you have pulled out my soul from death, my eye from tears, my foot from stumbling"** (v8) — Hebrew חִלַּצְתָּ "you have pulled out" (the חִלֵּץ row, as Ps 18:19); עֵינִי, רַגְלִי singular; דִּמְעָה "tears" (a collective singular); דֶּחִי "stumbling" (a push, a fall).
- **"in the land of the living" → "in the lands of the living"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּאַרְצוֹת הַחַיִּים, plural. אֶתְהַלֵּךְ "walk before" (hitpael, row).
- **"I believed, therefore have I spoken: I was greatly afflicted" → "I believed, for I speak; I, I was greatly afflicted"** (v10) — Hebrew הֶאֱמַנְתִּי כִּי אֲדַבֵּר אֲנִי עָנִיתִי מְאֹד, as it stands: הֶאֱמִין "believe" (perfect), כִּי "for", אֲדַבֵּר an imperfect ("I speak" or "I will speak"; not "therefore I have spoken"). The second line may be what he speaks or a statement of its own: no quotation marks are set. אֲנִי "I" stands before the verb (also v11), given as "I, I". עָנָה "be afflicted" (root of עָנִי, עֳנִי). 2 Cor 4:13 quotes the Greek of this verse, ἐπίστευσα, διὸ ἐλάλησα, "I believed, therefore I spoke" (from memory): it is not levelled here.
- **"I said in my haste, All men are liars" → "I, I said in my alarm, 'All mankind is a liar'"** (v11) — Hebrew בְחָפְזִי, from חָפַז "be alarmed" (row: to start away in alarm; "haste" is the other sense). כָּל־הָאָדָם "all mankind"; כֹּזֵב, a participle in the singular, "a liar".
- **"for all his benefits toward me" → "all his dealings toward me"** (v12) — Hebrew כָּל־תַּגְמוּלוֹהִי עָלָי: no "for"; תַּגְמוּל (only here, with an Aramaic-like suffix) is the noun of גָּמַל (v7) "deal": "what he has dealt" (here good; "benefits" in KJV). The line stands beside the question as its object: "What shall I return … — all his dealings toward me?". הֵשִׁיב "return" (bring back).
- **"I will take the cup of salvation" → "The cup of salvations I will lift up"** (v13) — Hebrew כּוֹס־יְשׁוּעוֹת, plural (as Ps 18:50 "salvations"); נָשָׂא "lift up"; the Hebrew order.
- **"I will pay my vows unto the LORD now in the presence of all his people" → "My vows to Yahweh I will repay, in the presence, please, of all his people"** (v14, 18) — Hebrew שִׁלֵּם "repay" (as Ps 50:14); נֶגְדָה־נָּא "in front of, please": נָא, the particle of entreaty, stands on the preposition (not the "now" of time).
- **"Precious in the sight of the LORD is the death of his saints" → "Precious in the eyes of Yahweh is the death of his faithful ones"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּעֵינֵי "in the eyes of"; הַמָּוְתָה "the death" (with a lengthened ending), לַחֲסִידָיו "of his faithful ones" (חָסִיד, the "faithful one" of Ps 18:25; 1 Sam 2:9). יָקָר "precious" (also "weighty, costly": a death that is not cheap to him).
- **"O LORD, truly I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid" → "Ah, Yahweh, for I am your servant; I am your servant, the son of your slave woman"** (v16) — אָנָּה "Ah" (as v4); כִּי "for" (or "truly"); no "and". עֶבֶד "servant" (the word is "slave": of Yahweh's men "servant", its row); אָמָה "slave woman" (row: not "maidservant", which is שִׁפְחָה). מוֹסֵרָי "my bonds"; פִּתַּח לְ "loose".
- **"I will offer to thee the sacrifice of thanksgiving" → "To you I will sacrifice a sacrifice of thanksgiving"** (v17) — Hebrew לְךָ־אֶזְבַּח זֶבַח תּוֹדָה: the verb זָבַח and its noun זֶבַח "sacrifice" (rows), "to you" first; זֶבַח תּוֹדָה without the article.
- **"in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem" → "in your midst, Jerusalem"** (v19) — no "O". חָצֵר "court" (of the sanctuary, row).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours. The Greek divides the psalm in two (v1–9 and v10–19, from memory).
- **v10** — quoted in 2 Cor 4:13 from the Greek (see Changes); the Hebrew of the verse is difficult and its link with v11 is not certain.
- **v13 "cup of salvations"** — a cup lifted in thanks at the paying of vows (v14, 17–18); later read at Passover (the Hallel, Pss 113–118).
