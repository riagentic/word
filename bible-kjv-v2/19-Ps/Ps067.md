# Psalm 67

To the chief musician upon stringed instruments. A Psalm. A Song.

[1] May ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be gracious to us and bless us
and make his face to shine upon us, Selah

[2] that your way may be known on earth,
your saving power among all nations.

[3] Let the peoples praise you, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
let all the peoples praise you!

[4] Let the nations be glad and sing for joy,
for you judge the peoples with equity
and guide the nations upon earth. Selah.

[5] Let the peoples praise you, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
let all the peoples praise you!

[6] The earth has yielded its increase;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, shall bless us.

[7] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall bless us;
let all the ends of the earth fear him!

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 3, 5, 6, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — Aaronic-benediction echo (v1), dual refrain calling nations to praise (v3, 5), harvest blessing and universal fear (v6-7).

### Notes
- **Aaronic benediction echoes** (v1; Num 6:24-26).
- **Missionary scope** — all nations to know Yahweh.
