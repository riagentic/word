# Psalm 54

To the chief musician with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David, when the ⟦ziphites|Zifim|Ziphites⟧ went and said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "Is not David hiding among us?"

[1] O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, save me by your name,
and vindicate me by your might.

[2] O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, hear my prayer;
give ear to the words of my mouth.

[3] For strangers have risen against me;
ruthless men seek my life;
they do not set ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ before themselves. Selah.

[4] Behold, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my helper;
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is the upholder of my soul.

[5] He will return the evil to my enemies;
in your faithfulness put an end to them.

[6] With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to you;
I will give thanks to your name, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for it is good.

[7] For he has delivered me from every trouble,
and my eye has looked in triumph on my enemies.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3, 4) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v6) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Maskil** — retained in superscription.
- **Poetic structure** — brief lament (v1-3), confidence (v4-5), vow of thanksgiving (v6-7).

### Notes
- **Ziphite betrayal** — 1 Sam 23:19.
- **Short personal lament.**
