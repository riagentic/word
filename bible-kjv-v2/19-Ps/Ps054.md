# Psalm 54

To the overseer. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David, when the ⟦ziphites|Zifim|Ziphites⟧ came and said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "Is not David hiding himself with us?"

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, by your name save me,
and by your might judge me.

[2] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, hear my prayer;
give ear to the sayings of my mouth.

[3] For strangers have risen up against me,
and ruthless ones have sought my soul;
they have not set ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ before them. Selah.

[4] Behold, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is a helper to me;
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is among those who uphold my soul.

[5] He will return the evil to those who watch for me;
in your truth put an end to them.

[6] With a freewill offering let me sacrifice to you;
I will give thanks to your name, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for it is good.

[7] For from every distress he has delivered me,
and on my enemies my eye has looked.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3, 4) — Hebrew *Elohim*, as printed (the Elohistic psalms).
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew יהוה, the one place in this psalm.
- **"To the chief Musician on Neginoth, Maschil, A Psalm of David, when the Ziphims came and said to Saul, Doth not David hide himself with us?" → "To the overseer. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David, when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, 'Is not David hiding himself with us?'"** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ בִּנְגִינֹת מַשְׂכִּיל לְדָוִד בְּבוֹא הַזִּיפִים וַיֹּאמְרוּ לְשָׁאוּל הֲלֹא דָוִד מִסְתַּתֵּר עִמָּנוּ: no word "psalm"; בִּנְגִינֹת "with stringed instruments" (נִגֵּן "play a stringed instrument"); מַשְׂכִּיל, from הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight", a kind of psalm of unknown meaning: kept as *Maskil*. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (traditional "to the chief musician"); לְ of לְדָוִד "by / for / belonging to": "of" leaves it open. בּוֹא "came" (not "went"); מִסְתַּתֵּר hitpael participle "hiding himself"; עִמָּנוּ "with us" (= among us). The event: 1 Sam 23:19; 26:1.
- **"Save me, O God, by thy name, and judge me by thy strength" → "Elohim, by your name save me, and by your might judge me"** (v1) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים בְּשִׁמְךָ הוֹשִׁיעֵנִי וּבִגְבוּרָתְךָ תְדִינֵנִי, in this order; גְּבוּרָה "might"; דִּין "judge" (its row; = do justice to me, give judgment for me). תְדִינֵנִי is an imperfect beside the imperative, rendered as a request ("you will judge me" read as a future).
- **"give ear to the words of my mouth" → "give ear to the sayings of my mouth"** (v2) — Hebrew הַאֲזִינָה לְאִמְרֵי־פִי; הֶאֱזִין "give ear to", אֵמֶר "saying" (their rows; not דָּבָר "word").
- **"oppressors seek after my soul" → "ruthless ones have sought my soul"** (v3) — Hebrew וְעָרִיצִים בִּקְשׁוּ נַפְשִׁי: עָרִיץ "ruthless" (its row; "men" not written), perfect, with "and"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul". זָרִים "strangers" (the זָר row); the near-same line Ps 86:14 has זֵדִים "insolent ones" (one letter apart; some manuscripts read it here too, from memory): the MT is kept.
- **"they have not set God before them" → "they have not set Elohim before them"** (v3) — Hebrew לֹא שָׂמוּ אֱלֹהִים לְנֶגְדָּם, perfect.
- **"God is mine helper" → "Elohim is a helper to me"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים עֹזֵר לִי; עֵזֶר / עֹזֵר "helper".
- **"the Lord is with them that uphold my soul" → "Adonai is among those who uphold my soul"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי בְּסֹמְכֵי נַפְשִׁי: בְּ "among"; many take it as "Adonai is the upholder of my soul" (בְּ of identity); both in the Hebrew. סָמַךְ "uphold".
- **"He shall reward evil unto mine enemies" → "He will return the evil to those who watch for me"** (v5) — qere יָשִׁיב "he will return (bring back)", hifil of שׁוּב (the הֵשִׁיב row); the written form ישוב reads "the evil shall return to …". הָרַע with the article, "the evil". שֹׁרְרַי "those who watch for me" (with hostile intent; from שׁוּר "watch", the Ps 5–8 row), not אֹיְבַי "my enemies" (v7).
- **"cut them off in thy truth" → "in your truth put an end to them"** (v5) — Hebrew בַּאֲמִתְּךָ הַצְמִיתֵם, in this order; אֱמֶת "truth" (its row); הִצְמִית "put an end to, silence".
- **"I will freely sacrifice unto thee" → "With a freewill offering let me sacrifice to you"** (v6) — Hebrew בִּנְדָבָה אֶזְבְּחָה־לָּךְ: נְדָבָה "freewill offering" (its row); אֶזְבְּחָה cohortative "let me sacrifice".
- **"I will praise thy name, O LORD; for it is good" → "I will give thanks to your name, Yahweh, for it is good"** (v6) — Hebrew אוֹדֶה שִּׁמְךָ יְהוָה כִּי־טוֹב: הוֹדָה "give thanks to" (its row); "it" supplied for the bare כִּי־טוֹב.
- **"For he hath delivered me out of all trouble" → "For from every distress he has delivered me"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי מִכָּל־צָרָה הִצִּילָנִי, in this order; צָרָה "distress" (its row); הִצִּיל "deliver". The subject is not named ("he", or "it", the name of v6).
- **"and mine eye hath seen his desire upon mine enemies" → "and on my enemies my eye has looked"** (v7) — Hebrew וּבְאֹיְבַי רָאֲתָה עֵינִי: רָאָה בְּ "look on" (in the Psalms, to look with satisfaction on the fall of the foe: said here, not in the verse); KJV "his desire" and "in triumph" are not written.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is Heb v1–2: our v1 = Heb v3, v7 = Heb v9.
- **The Ziphites** — 1 Sam 23:19–24; 26:1.
