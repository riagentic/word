# Psalm 72

Of ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧.

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, your judgments give to the king,
and your righteousness to the king's son.

[2] May he judge your people with righteousness,
and your afflicted with judgment.

[3] May the mountains bear ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to the people,
and the hills, in righteousness.

[4] May he judge the afflicted of the people,
may he save the sons of the needy,
and may he crush the extortioner.

[5] May they fear you with the sun,
and before the moon, generation of generations.

[6] May he come down like rain upon mown grass,
like showers, a sprinkling of the earth.

[7] May the righteous one bud in his days,
and abundance of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ until there is no moon.

[8] And may he have dominion from sea to sea,
and from river to the ends of the earth.

[9] Before him may desert dwellers bow,
and his enemies lick dust.

[10] May the kings of Tarshish and the coastlands bring back an offering;
may the kings of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧ and ⟦seba|Seva|Seba⟧ bring near tribute.

[11] And may all kings bow down to him,
all nations serve him.

[12] For he will deliver the needy one who cries for help,
and the afflicted, and there is no helper for him.

[13] He will have pity on the poor and the needy,
and the souls of the needy he will save.

[14] From oppression and from violence he will redeem their soul,
and precious will their blood be in his eyes.

[15] And may he live,
and may he give to him from the gold of ⟦sheba|Sheva|Sheba⟧,
and may he pray for him continually;
all the day may he bless him.

[16] May there be abundance of grain in the land,
on the top of the mountains;
may its fruit quake like ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧,
and may they blossom from the city like the plants of the earth.

[17] May his name be forever;
before the sun may his name increase;
and may they bless themselves in him;
may all nations call him happy.

[18] Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
who alone does wonderful deeds.

[19] And blessed is the name of his glory forever,
and may the whole earth be filled with his glory.
Amen and amen.

[20] The prayers of David son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧ are ended.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 18) — Hebrew *Elohim*; v18 has both names, יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים, and then אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל: the verse now has all three (it had dropped one).
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v18) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Psalm for Solomon" → "Of Solomon"** (heading) — Hebrew לִשְׁלֹמֹה, the first word of v1, as לְדָוִד elsewhere: לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to Solomon"; "of" leaves it open. "A Psalm" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"O God" → "Elohim"** (v1) — the vocative stands without "O" in the Hebrew. The object stands first, as in the Hebrew ("your judgments give to the king"). לְמֶלֶךְ, לְבֶן־מֶלֶךְ have no article (poetry): "to the king", "to the king's son".
- **"He shall judge … The mountains shall bring … He shall judge" → "May he judge … May the mountains bear … May he judge"** (v2–11, 15–17) — the verbs are imperfects, which can be wishes or statements ("he shall"). The frame is a prayer (v1 imperative "give"), and v8 וְיֵרְדְּ, v15 וִיחִי, v16–17 יְהִי are clear jussive forms: so the verse gives wishes. v12–14 give the ground (כִּי "For"): there the imperfects are statements, "he will".
- **"thy poor with judgment" → "your afflicted with judgment"** (v2) — Hebrew יָדִין … וַעֲנִיֶּיךָ בְמִשְׁפָּט; דִּין "judge"; עָנִי "afflicted" (also v4, 12); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment".
- **"bring peace … the little hills, by righteousness" → "bear shalom … the hills, in righteousness"** (v3) — Hebrew יִשְׂאוּ הָרִים שָׁלוֹם לָעָם וּגְבָעוֹת בִּצְדָקָה; נָשָׂא "lift up, bear"; שָׁלוֹם (token, also v7); בְּ "in".
- **"he shall save the children of the needy, and shall break in pieces the oppressor" → "may he save the sons of the needy, and may he crush the extortioner"** (v4) — Hebrew יוֹשִׁיעַ לִבְנֵי אֶבְיוֹן וִידַכֵּא עוֹשֵׁק; בְּנֵי "sons"; דִּכָּא "crush"; עָשַׁק "extort" (Lev 6:2–4; another verb than לָחַץ "oppress", and another word than תֹּךְ "oppression" in v14). יִשְׁפֹּט "judge" (not "defend the cause").
- **"They shall fear thee as long as the sun and moon endure, throughout all generations" → "May they fear you with the sun, and before the moon, generation of generations"** (v5) — Hebrew יִירָאוּךָ עִם־שָׁמֶשׁ וְלִפְנֵי יָרֵחַ דּוֹר דּוֹרִים, word for word: "with the sun" and "before (= in the presence of) the moon" mean as long as they shine; "endure" is not in the Hebrew. The Septuagint has "and he shall endure" (from memory); the MT "they shall fear you" is kept.
- **"upon the mown grass: as showers that water the earth" → "upon mown grass, like showers, a sprinkling of the earth"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל־גֵּז כִּרְבִיבִים זַרְזִיף אָרֶץ; גֵּז "shearing": mown grass, or fleece (Judg 6:37 has גִּזָּה; the Septuagint "fleece", from memory); זַרְזִיף, only here, "sprinkling, drops" (uncertain); no verb "water".
- **"shall the righteous flourish … so long as the moon endureth" → "May the righteous one bud … until there is no moon"** (v7) — Hebrew יִפְרַח … צַדִּיק … עַד־בְּלִי יָרֵחַ; פָּרַח "bud" (kept apart from צִיץ "blossom", v16); "until without moon".
- **"from the river unto the ends of the earth" → "from river to the ends of the earth"** (v8) — Hebrew וּמִנָּהָר, no article (as מִיָּם עַד־יָם "from sea to sea"); the Euphrates is usually understood (Exod 23:31); nothing is supplied. וְיֵרְדְּ, from רָדָה "have dominion".
- **"They that dwell in the wilderness shall bow" → "may desert dwellers bow"** (v9) — Hebrew צִיִּים, from צִיָּה "dry land": those of the dry places, men or beasts (also Ps 74:14); a reading "adversaries" (צָרִים) is proposed (from memory), the MT is kept. כָּרַע "bow" (kept apart from הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down", v11). "the dust" — עָפָר without article.
- **"of the isles shall bring presents … shall offer gifts" → "the coastlands bring back an offering … bring near tribute"** (v10) — Hebrew מַלְכֵי תַרְשִׁישׁ וְאִיִּים מִנְחָה יָשִׁיבוּ … אֶשְׁכָּר יַקְרִיבוּ; אִיִּים "coastlands"; מִנְחָה to a king "offering"; הֵשִׁיב "bring back" (= render what is due: say so); אֶשְׁכָּר (also Ezek 27:15) "tribute" (uncertain: a payment or gift); הִקְרִיב "bring near". One Hebrew colon per line (the verse was split into four lines).
- **"Yea, all kings shall fall down before him" → "And may all kings bow down to him"** (v11) — Hebrew וְיִשְׁתַּחֲווּ־לוֹ, "and", "bow down to him".
- **"when he crieth; the poor also, and him that hath no helper" → "who cries for help, and the afflicted, and there is no helper for him"** (v12) — Hebrew אֶבְיוֹן מְשַׁוֵּעַ וְעָנִי וְאֵין־עֹזֵר לוֹ: שִׁוַּע "cry for help"; the last clause, word for word, may describe the afflicted ("who has no helper") or name a third ("him who has no helper", KJV); the verse keeps both open. הִצִּיל "deliver".
- **"He shall spare the poor and needy, and shall save the souls of the needy" → "He will have pity on the poor and the needy, and the souls of the needy he will save"** (v13) — Hebrew יָחֹס עַל־דַּל וְאֶבְיוֹן וְנַפְשׁוֹת אֶבְיוֹנִים יוֹשִׁיעַ; חוּס עַל "have pity on"; דַּל "poor", אֶבְיוֹן "needy"; נַפְשׁוֹת "souls"; object first, as in the Hebrew.
- **"from deceit and violence: and precious shall their blood be in his sight" → "From oppression and from violence … in his eyes"** (v14) — Hebrew מִתּוֹךְ וּמֵחָמָס; תֹּךְ "oppression" (as Ps 10:7); the second "from" is in the Hebrew; גָּאַל "redeem"; בְּעֵינָיו "in his eyes".
- **"And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Sheba: prayer also shall be made for him continually; and daily shall he be praised" → "And may he live, and may he give to him from the gold of Sheba, and may he pray for him continually; all the day may he bless him"** (v15) — Hebrew וִיחִי וְיִתֶּן־לוֹ מִזְּהַב שְׁבָא וְיִתְפַּלֵּל בַּעֲדוֹ תָמִיד כָּל־הַיּוֹם יְבָרֲכֶנְהוּ: all verbs are active, third person singular, and no subject is named. Most take the king as the one who lives and the delivered poor man (v12–14) as the one who gives, prays and blesses; an impersonal "one" is also possible. Nothing is supplied. בֵּרַךְ "bless" (not "praise"); כָּל־הַיּוֹם "all the day".
- **"There shall be an handful of corn in the earth upon the top of the mountains; the fruit thereof shall shake like Lebanon: and they of the city shall flourish like grass of the earth" → "May there be abundance of grain in the land, on the top of the mountains; may its fruit quake like Lebanon, and may they blossom from the city like the plants of the earth"** (v16) — Hebrew יְהִי פִסַּת־בַּר בָּאָרֶץ בְּרֹאשׁ הָרִים יִרְעַשׁ כַּלְּבָנוֹן פִּרְיוֹ וְיָצִיצוּ מֵעִיר כְּעֵשֶׂב הָאָרֶץ; פִּסָּה, only here, "abundance" (uncertain; KJV "handful"); בַּר "grain"; רָעַשׁ "quake" (= rustle, wave); צִיץ "blossom"; מֵעִיר "from the city" (no article; "they" unnamed: no "people" supplied); עֵשֶׂב "plant" (not "grass"), a collective singular. The lines follow the accents: "on the top of the mountains" closes the first part, "quake" belongs to "its fruit".
- **"His name shall endure for ever: his name shall be continued as long as the sun: and men shall be blessed in him: all nations shall call him blessed" → "May his name be forever; before the sun may his name increase; and may they bless themselves in him; may all nations call him happy"** (v17) — Hebrew יְהִי שְׁמוֹ לְעוֹלָם לִפְנֵי־שֶׁמֶשׁ יִנּוֹן שְׁמוֹ וְיִתְבָּרְכוּ בוֹ כָּל־גּוֹיִם יְאַשְּׁרוּהוּ; qere יִנּוֹן, ketiv ינין (the same verb, another stem): "increase, sprout" (uncertain; related to נִין "offspring"); "endure" is not in the Hebrew. הִתְבָּרֵךְ, hitpael: "bless themselves in him" (as Gen 22:18), no "men" supplied; by the accents "all nations" belongs to the last verb. אִשֵּׁר "call happy" (the root of אַשְׁרֵי "Happy"; not "blessed": that is בָּרוּךְ).
- **"Blessed be … who only doeth wondrous things" → "Blessed is … who alone does wonderful deeds"** (v18) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ … עֹשֵׂה נִפְלָאוֹת לְבַדּוֹ; בָּרוּךְ has no verb: "is", as Ps 28:6; נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds"; לְבַדּוֹ "he alone".
- **"And blessed be his glorious name for ever: and let the whole earth be filled with his glory; Amen, and Amen" → "And blessed is the name of his glory forever, and may the whole earth be filled with his glory. Amen and amen."** (v19) — Hebrew וּבָרוּךְ שֵׁם כְּבוֹדוֹ לְעוֹלָם; שֵׁם כְּבוֹדוֹ "the name of his glory"; לְעוֹלָם "forever" (no "from the age to the age" here). וְיִמָּלֵא כְבוֹדוֹ אֶת־כֹּל הָאָרֶץ, as Num 14:21: the verb agrees with "his glory" ("may his glory fill the whole earth" is also read). אָמֵן וְאָמֵן "Amen and amen".
- **"The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended" → "The prayers of David son of Jesse are ended"** (v20) — Hebrew כָּלּוּ תְּפִלּוֹת דָּוִד בֶּן־יִשָׁי, translated as printed: כָּלּוּ "are ended" (pual of כָּלָה "be finished"); no article before "son".
- **Poetic structure** — royal prayer for justice (v1-7), universal dominion (v8-11), care for poor (v12-14), abundance and blessing (v15-17), doxology of Book II (v18-20).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first word of v1.
- **Royal/Messianic psalm** — ideal king portrayal.
- **Closing doxology of Book II** (v18–19), with the colophon of v20: psalms headed "Of David" stand also after it (e.g. Ps 86; 101; 103; 138–145).
