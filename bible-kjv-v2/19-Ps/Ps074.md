# Psalm 74

A Maskil. Of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] Why, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, have you cast off forever?
Why does your anger smoke against the flock of your pasture?

[2] Remember your congregation, which you got of old,
which you redeemed, the tribe of your inheritance,
Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, in which you have abided.

[3] Lift up your steps to the endless ruins;
the enemy has done evil to all in the holy place.

[4] Those who harass you have roared in the midst of your meeting place;
they have set their signs as signs.

[5] It is known like one who brings upward
axes in a thicket of trees.

[6] And now its engravings together
with hatchet and picks they hammer.

[7] They have set your sanctuary on fire;
to the ground they have profaned the dwelling of your name.

[8] They said in their heart, "Let us wrong them together."
They have burned all the meeting places of ⟦el|El|God⟧ in the land.

[9] Our signs we have not seen;
there is no longer a prophet,
and there is none with us who knows how long.

[10] How long, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, shall the adversary reproach?
Shall the enemy spurn your name forever?

[11] Why do you bring back your hand, and your right hand?
From the midst of your bosom — make an end!

[12] And ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my King from of old,
working salvations in the midst of the earth.

[13] You divided the sea by your strength;
you broke in pieces the heads of sea monsters on the waters.

[14] You crushed the heads of ⟦leviathan|Livyatan|Leviathan⟧;
you gave him as food to a people, to desert dwellers.

[15] You split open spring and torrent;
you dried up ever-flowing rivers.

[16] Yours is the day, also yours is the night;
you established the light and the sun.

[17] You set up all the borders of the earth;
summer and winter — you formed them.

[18] Remember this: the enemy has reproached, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and a foolish people has spurned your name.

[19] Do not give to the living thing the soul of your turtledove;
the life of your afflicted do not forget forever.

[20] Look to the covenant,
for the dark places of the land are full of pastures of violence.

[21] Let not the crushed turn back put to shame;
let the afflicted and the needy praise your name.

[22] Arise, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, contend your case;
remember your reproach from the foolish one all the day.

[23] Do not forget the voice of those who harass you,
the tumult of those who rise against you, going up continually.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 10, 12, 22) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v18) — Tetragrammaton (in this Elohistic psalm only here).
- **"El"** (v8) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **"O God … O LORD" → "Elohim … Yahweh"** (v1, 10, 18, 22) — the vocative stands without "O" in the Hebrew.
- **"Maschil of Asaph" → "A Maskil. Of Asaph."** (heading) — Hebrew מַשְׂכִּיל לְאָסָף, the first words of v1, in this order. מַשְׂכִּיל, a hifil participle of שָׂכַל ("give insight"); as a heading term its meaning is uncertain (Ps 32); the word is kept. לְאָסָף: "by", "for" or "belonging to Asaph"; "of" leaves it open.
- **"why hast thou cast us off for ever? why doth thine anger smoke against the sheep of thy pasture?" → "Why, Elohim, have you cast off forever? Why does your anger smoke against the flock of your pasture?"** (v1) — Hebrew לָמָה אֱלֹהִים זָנַחְתָּ לָנֶצַח יֶעְשַׁן אַפְּךָ בְּצֹאן מַרְעִיתֶךָ; זָנַח "cast off" has no object ("us" is not in the Hebrew); לָנֶצַח "forever" (also v10, 19); the second "Why" is supplied from the first (one לָמָה stands for both lines); אַף "anger" (lit. "nose"); צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats); מַרְעִית "pasture" (from רָעָה "shepherd"; another word than נְאוֹת "pastures", v20; CZ keeps them apart).
- **"which thou hast purchased of old; the rod of thine inheritance, which thou hast redeemed; this mount Zion, wherein thou hast dwelt" → "which you got of old, which you redeemed, the tribe of your inheritance, Mount Zion, in which you have abided"** (v2) — Hebrew עֲדָתְךָ קָנִיתָ קֶּדֶם גָּאַלְתָּ שֵׁבֶט נַחֲלָתֶךָ הַר־צִיּוֹן זֶה שָׁכַנְתָּ בּוֹ; עֵדָה "congregation"; קָנָה "get" (the "create" sense is also defended where God is the subject); the two relative clauses have no אֲשֶׁר ("which" supplied); שֵׁבֶט "tribe" (the word is also "rod", KJV); זֶה … בּוֹ "this … in it" = in which (זֶה as a relative); שָׁכַן "abide".
- **"Lift up thy feet unto the perpetual desolations; even all that the enemy hath done wickedly in the sanctuary" → "Lift up your steps to the endless ruins; the enemy has done evil to all in the holy place"** (v3) — Hebrew הָרִימָה פְעָמֶיךָ לְמַשֻּׁאוֹת נֶצַח כָּל־הֵרַע אוֹיֵב בַּקֹּדֶשׁ; פְּעָמִים "steps" (the word is "feet": walking is meant); מַשּׁוּאוֹת "ruins" (Ps 73:18); נֶצַח as a genitive, lit. "ruins of perpetuity": "endless" (the "forever" of v1); הֵרַע "do evil" (its row); קֹדֶשׁ "holiness": here the holy place.
- **"Thine enemies roar in the midst of thy congregations; they set up their ensigns for signs" → "Those who harass you have roared in the midst of your meeting place; they have set their signs as signs"** (v4) — Hebrew שָׁאֲגוּ צֹרְרֶיךָ בְּקֶרֶב מוֹעֲדֶךָ שָׂמוּ אוֹתֹתָם אֹתוֹת; צֹרְרִים "those who harass" (צָרַר; also v23; kept apart from צָר "adversary", v10); perfects; מוֹעֵד "meeting place" (singular; root יָעַד, as אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד "tent of meeting"; also v8); אוֹת "sign" twice.
- **"A man was famous according as he had lifted up axes upon the thick trees" → "It is known like one who brings upward axes in a thicket of trees"** (v5) — Hebrew יִוָּדַע כְּמֵבִיא לְמָעְלָה בִּסֲבָךְ־עֵץ קַרְדֻּמּוֹת, word for word; יִוָּדַע nifal "it / he is known" (no subject named; "a man", "famous" are not in the Hebrew); כְּמֵבִיא "like one who brings"; לְמָעְלָה "upward"; סְבָךְ "thicket"; קַרְדֹּם "axe". The sense is uncertain (the enemy seen like woodcutters; the verse is often emended, from memory).
- **"But now they break down the carved work thereof at once with axes and hammers" → "And now its engravings together with hatchet and picks they hammer"** (v6) — Hebrew qere וְעַתָּה "and now" (ketiv ועת "and the time"); פִּתּוּחִים "engravings" (as פִּתּוּחֵי חֹתָם); יַחַד "together"; כַּשִּׁיל and כֵּילַפּוֹת are found only here: "hatchet" and "picks" (both uncertain; קַרְדֹּם is "axe", v5); הָלַם "hammer". The imperfects of v5–6 stand among the perfects of v4, 7: the scene is told as it goes on.
- **"They have cast fire into thy sanctuary, they have defiled by casting down the dwelling place of thy name to the ground" → "They have set your sanctuary on fire; to the ground they have profaned the dwelling of your name"** (v7) — Hebrew שִׁלְּחוּ בָאֵשׁ מִקְדָּשֶׁךָ, lit. "they have sent your sanctuary into the fire" (as Judg 1:8 "set on fire"); לָאָרֶץ חִלְּלוּ, "to the ground they have profaned" (= profaned it by throwing it down; nothing supplied); חִלֵּל "profane" (not "defile"); מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling". Two lines, as the Hebrew (the verse had three).
- **"Let us destroy them together: they have burned up all the synagogues of God in the land" → "'Let us wrong them together.' They have burned all the meeting places of El in the land"** (v8) — Hebrew נִינָם יָחַד, pointed as a verb of יָנָה "wrong, oppress" (the hifil הוֹנָה, its row) with "them"; the same letters can be read as the noun נִין "offspring": "their offspring together" (from memory); בְלִבָּם "in their heart" (singular); מוֹעֲדֵי־אֵל "the meeting places of El" (no "synagogues").
- **"We see not our signs: there is no more any prophet: neither is there among us any that knoweth how long" → "Our signs we have not seen; there is no longer a prophet, and there is none with us who knows how long"** (v9) — Hebrew אוֹתֹתֵינוּ לֹא רָאִינוּ (perfect, object first) … וְלֹא־אִתָּנוּ יֹדֵעַ עַד־מָה; אִתָּנוּ "with us".
- **"shall the adversary reproach? shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?" → "shall the adversary reproach? Shall the enemy spurn your name forever?"** (v10) — Hebrew יְחָרֶף צָר יְנָאֵץ אוֹיֵב; חֵרֵף "reproach" (also v18; noun חֶרְפָּה v22); נִאֵץ "spurn" (also v18; not "blaspheme").
- **"Why withdrawest thou thy hand, even thy right hand? pluck it out of thy bosom" → "Why do you bring back your hand, and your right hand? From the midst of your bosom — make an end!"** (v11) — Hebrew לָמָּה תָשִׁיב יָדְךָ וִימִינֶךָ מִקֶּרֶב חֵיקְךָ כַלֵּה; הֵשִׁיב "bring back" (draw back); וִימִינֶךָ "and your right hand"; qere חֵיקְךָ "your bosom" (ketiv חוקך, another spelling of the same word, or "your statute"); כַּלֵּה piel imperative of כָּלָה "make an end", without object ("pluck it out", "destroy them" are not in the Hebrew); the line is short and hard.
- **"For God is my King of old, working salvation" → "And Elohim is my King from of old, working salvations"** (v12) — Hebrew וֵאלֹהִים "And Elohim" (not "For"); מִקֶּדֶם "from of old" (קֶדֶם "front, ancient time"; also v2 "of old"); יְשׁוּעוֹת plural "salvations" (as Ps 28:8).
- **"Thou didst divide the sea by thy strength: thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters" → "You divided the sea by your strength; you broke in pieces the heads of sea monsters on the waters"** (v13) — Hebrew אַתָּה פוֹרַרְתָּ בְעָזְּךָ יָם שִׁבַּרְתָּ רָאשֵׁי תַנִּינִים עַל־הַמָּיִם; אַתָּה "you" stands out in v13–17 (five times); פּוֹרֵר "divide" (lit. "break up, split"; בָּקַע "split" is v15); עֹז "strength"; שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces"; תַּנִּין in the waters "sea monster" (its row; a great serpent-like creature, exact sense uncertain; KJV "dragons"); עַל "on" (not "in").
- **"Thou brakest the heads of leviathan in pieces, and gavest him to be meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness" → "You crushed the heads of Leviathan; you gave him as food to a people, to desert dwellers"** (v14) — Hebrew רִצַּצְתָּ רָאשֵׁי לִוְיָתָן תִּתְּנֶנּוּ מַאֲכָל לְעָם לְצִיִּים; רִצֵּץ "crush" (another verb than שִׁבֵּר v13); לִוְיָתָן (token; also Job 3:8; 41:1; Ps 104:26; Isa 27:1), here with "heads" in the plural; תִּתְּנֶנּוּ imperfect in a past frame; לְעָם לְצִיִּים word for word, "to a people, to desert dwellers" (צִיִּים as Ps 72:9: men or beasts of the dry places; "the people of the desert dwellers" is also read).
- **"Thou didst cleave the fountain and the flood: thou driedst up mighty rivers" → "You split open spring and torrent; you dried up ever-flowing rivers"** (v15) — Hebrew בָקַעְתָּ מַעְיָן וָנָחַל … הוֹבַשְׁתָּ נַהֲרוֹת אֵיתָן; בָּקַע "split"; מַעְיָן "spring"; נַחַל "torrent" (the water itself is meant: the "wadi" of its row); אֵיתָן "ever-flowing" (Deut 21:4 row); נָהָר "river".
- **"thou hast prepared the light and the sun" → "you established the light and the sun"** (v16) — Hebrew הֲכִינוֹתָ מָאוֹר וָשָׁמֶשׁ; הֵכִין "establish"; מָאוֹר "light" (a thing that gives light, here the moon or the lights; English has no second plain word for אוֹר: note); אַף "also".
- **"Thou hast set all the borders of the earth: thou hast made summer and winter" → "You set up all the borders of the earth; summer and winter — you formed them"** (v17) — Hebrew הִצַּבְתָּ "set up"; גְּבוּלוֹת "borders"; קַיִץ וָחֹרֶף אַתָּה יְצַרְתָּם, object first, "you formed them" (יָצַר "form", not "make").
- **"Remember this, that the enemy hath reproached, O LORD, and that the foolish people have blasphemed thy name" → "Remember this: the enemy has reproached, Yahweh, and a foolish people has spurned your name"** (v18) — Hebrew זְכָר־זֹאת אוֹיֵב חֵרֵף יְהוָה וְעַם נָבָל נִאֲצוּ שְׁמֶךָ; no "that"; the paseq after חֵרֵף sets יְהוָה apart, so it is read as an address; "the enemy has reproached Yahweh" is also possible; עַם נָבָל "a foolish people" (no article).
- **"O deliver not the soul of thy turtledove unto the multitude of the wicked: forget not the congregation of thy poor for ever" → "Do not give to the living thing the soul of your turtledove; the life of your afflicted do not forget forever"** (v19) — Hebrew אַל־תִּתֵּן לְחַיַּת נֶפֶשׁ תּוֹרֶךָ חַיַּת עֲנִיֶּיךָ אַל־תִּשְׁכַּח לָנֶצַח; נָתַן "give"; חַיָּה twice: "living thing" (a beast; no "wicked" in the Hebrew), then "the life of your afflicted" (the word may also mean a company, as 2 Sam 23:11; one word, a play: say so); נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; תּוֹר "turtledove" (the Septuagint read תּוֹדֶךָ "who confesses you", from memory); עָנִי "afflicted"; the second line in the Hebrew order.
- **"Have respect unto the covenant: for the dark places of the earth are full of the habitations of cruelty" → "Look to the covenant, for the dark places of the land are full of pastures of violence"** (v20) — Hebrew הַבֵּט לַבְּרִית כִּי מָלְאוּ מַחֲשַׁכֵּי־אֶרֶץ נְאוֹת חָמָס; הִבִּיט "look"; נְאוֹת "pastures" (as Ps 23:2; the word is also used of places where men or flocks lodge: "habitations", KJV); חָמָס "violence".
- **"O let not the oppressed return ashamed" → "Let not the crushed turn back put to shame"** (v21) — Hebrew אַל־יָשֹׁב דַּךְ נִכְלָם; דַּךְ "crushed" (Ps 9:9; English has one word with רִצֵּץ "crush" of v14); נִכְלָם "put to shame" (kept apart from בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"); עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן "the afflicted and the needy".
- **"plead thine own cause: remember how the foolish man reproacheth thee daily" → "contend your case; remember your reproach from the foolish one all the day"** (v22) — Hebrew רִיבָה רִיבֶךָ, lit. "contend your contention" (verb and noun of one root); זְכֹר חֶרְפָּתְךָ מִנִּי־נָבָל כָּל־הַיּוֹם: "your reproach" = the reproach of you; מִנִּי "from"; נָבָל "the foolish one"; כָּל־הַיּוֹם "all the day".
- **"Forget not the voice of thine enemies: the tumult of those that rise up against thee increaseth continually" → "Do not forget the voice of those who harass you, the tumult of those who rise against you, going up continually"** (v23) — Hebrew קוֹל צֹרְרֶיךָ שְׁאוֹן קָמֶיךָ עֹלֶה תָמִיד; קוֹל "voice"; צֹרְרִים "those who harass" (v4); שְׁאוֹן "tumult" (uncertain: the roar of waters or crowds); עֹלֶה "going up" (not "increaseth"); תָּמִיד "continually".
- **Poetic structure** — national lament over sanctuary destruction (v1-11), appeal to creation-cosmic power (v12-17), renewed plea (v18-23).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **Post-destruction lament** — possibly after 586 BC or earlier desecration.
- **Creation-cosmic imagery** invoked for present appeal.
