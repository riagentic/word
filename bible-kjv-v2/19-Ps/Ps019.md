# Psalm 19

To the chief musician. A Psalm of David.

[1] The heavens declare the glory of ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and the firmament proclaims the work of his hands.

[2] Day to day pours out speech,
and night to night reveals knowledge.

[3] There is no speech, there are no words,
whose voice is not heard.

[4] Their measuring line goes out through all the earth,
and their words to the end of the world.
In them he has set a tent for the sun,

[5] which comes out like a bridegroom from his chamber,
and like a strong man runs its course with joy.

[6] Its rising is from the end of the heavens,
and its circuit to the end of them;
and nothing is hidden from its heat.

[7] The ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is perfect, reviving the soul;
the testimony of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is sure, making wise the simple.

[8] The precepts of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are right, rejoicing the heart;
the commandment of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is pure, enlightening the eyes;

[9] the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is clean, enduring forever;
the rules of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are true, and righteous altogether.

[10] More to be desired are they than gold,
even much fine gold;
sweeter also than honey and drippings of the honeycomb.

[11] Moreover, by them is your servant warned;
in keeping them there is great reward.

[12] Who can discern his errors?
Declare me innocent from hidden faults.

[13] Keep back your servant also from presumptuous sins;
let them not have dominion over me!
Then I shall be blameless,
and innocent of great transgression.

[14] Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart
be acceptable in your sight,
O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, my Rock and my redeemer.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "El"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"strength" → "Rock"** (v14) — *tzur*.

### Notes
- **Two revelation themes** — general (creation, v1-6) and special (Torah, v7-11).
- **Famous opening** — "The heavens declare the glory of El."
- **Benediction** — v14 used widely in worship.
