# Psalm 98

A psalm.

[1] Sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a new song,
for he has done wonderful deeds;
his right hand has saved for him,
and his holy arm.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has made known his salvation;
before the eyes of the nations he has uncovered his righteousness.

[3] He has remembered his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and his faithfulness
to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
all the ends of the earth have seen
the salvation of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] Shout to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all the earth;
break forth and shout for joy and make music.

[5] Make music to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with the lyre,
with the lyre and the sound of music.

[6] With trumpets and the sound of a ⟦shofar|shofar|horn⟧
sound a blast before the King, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] Let the sea thunder, and its fullness,
the world and those who dwell in it.

[8] Let the rivers clap the palm;
together let the mountains shout for joy

[9] before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he comes to judge the earth.
He will judge the world in righteousness,
and peoples in uprightness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 9) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"A Psalm" → "A psalm"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר, the one word of the heading; it is part of Hebrew v1.
- **"O sing unto the LORD a new song; for he hath done marvellous things" → "Sing to Yahweh a new song, for he has done wonderful deeds"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁירוּ לַיהוָה שִׁיר חָדָשׁ כִּי־נִפְלָאוֹת עָשָׂה: no "O" in the Hebrew; נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds" (as 1 Chr 16:9, 24). Ps 96:1 opens with the same words.
- **"his right hand, and his holy arm, hath gotten him the victory" → "his right hand has saved for him, and his holy arm"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹשִׁיעָה־לּוֹ יְמִינוֹ וּזְרוֹעַ קָדְשׁוֹ: the verb is הוֹשִׁיעַ "save" (the root of יְשׁוּעָה "salvation", v2, 3), with לּוֹ "for him"; the verb is singular and stands with "his right hand", and "his holy arm" (literally "the arm of his holiness") follows as a second subject. "the victory" is not written.
- **"his righteousness hath he openly shewed in the sight of the heathen" → "before the eyes of the nations he has uncovered his righteousness"** (v2) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי הַגּוֹיִם גִּלָּה צִדְקָתוֹ, in this order; לְעֵינֵי "before the eyes of"; גִּלָּה "uncover" (reveal).
- **"his mercy and his truth" → "his chesed and his faithfulness"** (v3) — Hebrew חַסְדּוֹ וֶאֱמוּנָתוֹ; אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness" (אֱמֶת is "truth").
- **"Make a joyful noise … make a loud noise, and rejoice, and sing praise" → "Shout … break forth and shout for joy and make music"** (v4) — Hebrew הָרִיעוּ (הֵרִיעַ of voices: "shout") … פִּצְחוּ וְרַנְּנוּ וְזַמֵּרוּ: three verbs, joined by "and"; פָּצַח "break forth" (as Isa 44:23), רִנֵּן "shout for joy" (1 Chr 16:33), זִמֵּר "make music" (1 Chr 16:9; Ps 33:2).
- **"Sing unto the LORD with the harp; with the harp, and the voice of a psalm" → "Make music to Yahweh with the lyre, with the lyre and the sound of music"** (v5) — Hebrew זַמְּרוּ … בְּכִנּוֹר … וְקוֹל זִמְרָה: the verb of v4; כִּנּוֹר "lyre" (kind uncertain); זִמְרָה is the noun of זִמֵּר, so "music … music" keeps the one root.
- **"With trumpets and sound of cornet make a joyful noise before the LORD, the King" → "With trumpets and the sound of a shofar sound a blast before the King, Yahweh"** (v6) — Hebrew בַּחֲצֹצְרוֹת וְקוֹל שׁוֹפָר הָרִיעוּ לִפְנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ יְהוָה. חֲצֹצְרָה is the metal trumpet, שׁוֹפָר the horn: two instruments. הָרִיעוּ is the verb of v4; with the instruments named it is the blast on them ("sound a blast"); "shout" with the instruments is also possible. "the King" stands first, then "Yahweh".
- **"Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof" → "Let the sea thunder, and its fullness"** (v7) — Hebrew יִרְעַם הַיָּם וּמְלֹאוֹ, as 1 Chr 16:32 = Ps 96:11; תֵּבֵל וְיֹשְׁבֵי בָהּ "the world and those who dwell in it", as Ps 24:1.
- **"Let the floods clap their hands: let the hills be joyful together" → "Let the rivers clap the palm; together let the mountains shout for joy"** (v8) — Hebrew נְהָרוֹת יִמְחֲאוּ־כָף יַחַד הָרִים יְרַנֵּנוּ. נָהָר "river"; מָחָא כַף "clap the palm" (כַּף singular, no "their"; Ps 47:1 has another verb, תָּקַע, with the same noun); יַחַד "together" stands at the head of the second line (the accents); הָרִים "mountains"; רִנֵּן as v4.
- **"for he cometh to judge the earth: with righteousness shall he judge the world, and the people with equity" → "for he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world in righteousness, and peoples in uprightness"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּי בָא לִשְׁפֹּט הָאָרֶץ (בָא "he comes" or "he has come"), as 1 Chr 16:33; יִשְׁפֹּט־תֵּבֵל בְּצֶדֶק וְעַמִּים בְּמֵישָׁרִים: עַמִּים "peoples" without the article; מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness" (plural of the root of יָשָׁר). Ps 96:13 has "he comes" twice and "in his faithfulness" for the last word.

### Notes
- **Parallels** — v1 begins as Ps 96:1; v7–9 stand close to Ps 96:11–13 (= 1 Chr 16:32–33). The Hebrew of each is translated as it stands.
- **Enthronement psalm** — praise of Yahweh as King (v6) and judge (v9).
