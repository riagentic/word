# Psalm 20

To the chief musician. A Psalm of David.

[1] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answer you in the day of trouble!
May the name of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ protect you!

[2] May he send you help from the sanctuary
and give you support from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!

[3] May he remember all your offerings
and accept your burnt sacrifice! Selah.

[4] May he grant you your heart's desire
and fulfill all your plans!

[5] May we shout for joy over your salvation,
and in the name of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ set up our banners!
May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ fulfill all your petitions!

[6] Now I know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saves his anointed;
he will answer him from his holy heaven
with the saving might of his right hand.

[7] Some trust in chariots and some in horses,
but we will remember the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] They collapse and fall,
but we rise and stand upright.

[9] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, save the king!
May he answer us when we call.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 6, 7, 9) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — community petition (v1-5) distinct from single voice of confidence (v6-8); royal plea (v9) as brief closing.

### Notes
- **Royal blessing before battle** — congregation prays for the king.
- **"Chariots and horses vs name of Yahweh"** — famous contrast.
