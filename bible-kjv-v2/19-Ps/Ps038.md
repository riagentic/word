# Psalm 38

A psalm of David. To bring to remembrance.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do not in your wrath reprove me,
nor in your fury discipline me.

[2] For your arrows have come down into me,
and your hand has come down upon me.

[3] There is no soundness in my flesh because of your indignation;
there is no ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ in my bones because of my sin.

[4] For my iniquities have passed over my head;
like a heavy burden they are too heavy for me.

[5] My stripes stink, they rot away,
because of my folly.

[6] I am bent, I am bowed down exceedingly;
all the day I have gone about darkened.

[7] For my flanks are filled with burning,
and there is no soundness in my flesh.

[8] I have grown numb and am crushed exceedingly;
I have roared because of the groaning of my heart.

[9] ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, before you is all my desire,
and my sighing is not hidden from you.

[10] My heart throbs, my power has forsaken me,
and the light of my eyes — they also are not with me.

[11] Those who love me and my friends stand opposite my plague,
and those near me stand far off.

[12] And those who seek my soul lay snares,
and those who seek my evil speak ruin,
and deceits all the day they utter.

[13] But I am like a deaf man — I do not hear,
and like a mute man who does not open his mouth.

[14] And I became like a man who does not hear,
and in whose mouth are no reproofs.

[15] For for you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I have hoped;
you will answer, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[16] For I said, "Lest they be glad over me;
when my foot slips, they make themselves great against me."

[17] For I am ready for stumbling,
and my pain is before me continually.

[18] For my iniquity I declare;
I am anxious because of my sin.

[19] And my enemies are alive, they are mighty,
and many are those who hate me falsely.

[20] And those who repay evil for good
are adversaries to me for my pursuing good.

[21] Do not forsake me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not be far from me.

[22] Hasten to my help,
⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, my salvation.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 15, 21) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15, 21) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance" → "A psalm of David. To bring to remembrance."** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד לְהַזְכִּיר. לְהַזְכִּיר, hifil infinitive of זָכַר, "to bring to remembrance" (as 1 Chr 16:4; also the heading of Ps 70): what is brought to remembrance, and before whom, is not said; a link with the "memorial portion" (*azkarah*, the same root) of the offerings is a guess. לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"O LORD, rebuke me not in thy wrath: neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure" → "Yahweh, do not in your wrath reprove me, nor in your fury discipline me"** (v1) — Hebrew אַל־בְּקֶצְפְּךָ תוֹכִיחֵנִי וּבַחֲמָתְךָ תְיַסְּרֵנִי: "in your wrath" set before the verb, as in the Hebrew; the one אַל "do not" governs both lines. קֶצֶף "wrath", חֵמָה "fury" (lit. "heat"), הוֹכִיחַ "reprove", יִסֵּר "discipline". Ps 6:1 has אַף "anger" in the first line.
- **"thine arrows stick fast in me, and thy hand presseth me sore" → "your arrows have come down into me, and your hand has come down upon me"** (v2) — Hebrew נִחֲתוּ … וַתִּנְחַת, one verb נָחַת "come down, sink" twice (nifal, then qal).
- **"because of thine anger … neither is there any rest in my bones" → "because of your indignation … there is no shalom in my bones"** (v3) — Hebrew מְתֹם "soundness" (root תמם "whole"); זַעַם "indignation"; שָׁלוֹם *shalom* (wholeness, the token as everywhere); מִפְּנֵי "because of".
- **"are gone over mine head: as an heavy burden they are too heavy for me" → "have passed over my head; like a heavy burden they are too heavy for me"** (v4) — Hebrew עָבְרוּ רֹאשִׁי "have passed over my head"; כְּמַשָּׂא כָבֵד יִכְבְּדוּ מִמֶּנִּי, "like a heavy burden they are heavy, more than I".
- **"My wounds stink and are corrupt because of my foolishness" → "My stripes stink, they rot away, because of my folly"** (v5) — Hebrew הִבְאִישׁוּ נָמַקּוּ חַבּוּרֹתָי, two verbs without "and"; חַבּוּרָה "stripe" (the mark of a blow, Exod 21:25; Isa 53:5); נָמַק "rot away"; אִוֶּלֶת "folly".
- **"I am troubled; I am bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long" → "I am bent, I am bowed down exceedingly; all the day I have gone about darkened"** (v6) — Hebrew נַעֲוֵיתִי, nifal of עָוָה "bend, twist" (the root of עָוֹן "iniquity"); שַׁחֹתִי "I am bowed down"; עַד־מְאֹד "exceedingly" (also v8); קֹדֵר "darkened" (the Ps 35–36 row: dark of face or in dark mourning dress, as Ps 35:14); הִלָּכְתִּי piel "walk about".
- **"my loins are filled with a loathsome disease" → "my flanks are filled with burning"** (v7) — Hebrew כְסָלַי, the loin muscles by the kidneys ("flanks", as Lev 3:4); נִקְלֶה, nifal participle of קָלָה "roast, burn": "what is burned" (a root קָלָה "be dishonored", hence KJV "loathsome", is also possible: uncertain).
- **"I am feeble and sore broken: I have roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart" → "I have grown numb and am crushed exceedingly; I have roared because of the groaning of my heart"** (v8) — Hebrew נְפוּגוֹתִי "I have grown numb, faint" (פּוּג); נִדְכֵּיתִי "crushed"; שָׁאַג "roar" (as a lion); נַהֲמָה "growling, groaning" (of a lion, of the sea).
- **"Lord, all my desire is before thee; and my groaning is not hid from thee" → "Adonai, before you is all my desire, and my sighing is not hidden from you"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי (*Adonai*); נֶגְדְּךָ "before you", set first; תַּאֲוָה "desire" (the word for craving, here of a longing not blamed); אֲנָחָה "sighing".
- **"My heart panteth, my strength faileth me: as for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me" → "My heart throbs, my power has forsaken me, and the light of my eyes — they also are not with me"** (v10) — Hebrew סְחַרְחַר, a doubled form of סָחַר "go around": "throbs" (lit. "goes round and round"); עֲזָבַנִי כֹחִי "my power has forsaken me" (כֹּחַ "power"); גַּם־הֵם אֵין אִתִּי "they also are not with me", plural (the eyes, or "the light of my eyes" taken as many).
- **"My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my sore; and my kinsmen stand afar off" → "Those who love me and my friends stand opposite my plague, and those near me stand far off"** (v11) — Hebrew אֹהֲבַי "those who love me"; רֵעַ "friend"; מִנֶּגֶד "opposite" (over against, at some distance); נֶגַע "plague" (lit. "a stroke, touch"); קְרוֹבַי "those near me" (by kin or by dwelling: not decided); יַעֲמֹדוּ imperfect, עָמָדוּ perfect.
- **"They also that seek after my life lay snares for me: and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and imagine deceits all the day long" → "And those who seek my soul lay snares, and those who seek my evil speak ruin, and deceits all the day they utter"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיְנַקְשׁוּ "and they lay snares" ("for me" not in the Hebrew); נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; מְבַקְשֵׁי (בִּקֵּשׁ) and דֹרְשֵׁי (דָּרַשׁ) are two Hebrew verbs, both "seek" in English; רָעָתִי "my evil" (רָעָה); הַוּוֹת "ruin, destruction"; מִרְמוֹת "deceits"; יֶהְגּוּ, הָגָה of plotting: "utter".
- **"But I, as a deaf man, heard not; and I was as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth" → "But I am like a deaf man — I do not hear, and like a mute man who does not open his mouth"** (v13) — Hebrew כְחֵרֵשׁ לֹא אֶשְׁמָע וּכְאִלֵּם לֹא יִפְתַּח־פִּיו; "man" supplied for English (twice).
- **"Thus I was as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs" → "And I became like a man who does not hear, and in whose mouth are no reproofs"** (v14) — Hebrew וָאֱהִי, wayyiqtol "and I became"; תּוֹכָחוֹת "reproofs" (noun of הוֹכִיחַ "reprove", v1).
- **"For in thee, O LORD, do I hope: thou wilt hear, O Lord my God" → "For for you, Yahweh, I have hoped; you will answer, Adonai my Elohim"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּי־לְךָ יְהוָה הוֹחָלְתִּי, hifil of יָחַל "hope, wait in hope" (קִוָּה is "wait for"), perfect; לְךָ "for you", set first; אַתָּה תַעֲנֶה "you will answer", the pronoun stressed; אֲדֹנָי (*Adonai*).
- **"Hear me, lest otherwise they should rejoice over me: when my foot slippeth, they magnify themselves against me" → "Lest they be glad over me; when my foot slips, they make themselves great against me"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי־אָמַרְתִּי פֶּן־יִשְׂמְחוּ־לִי: "I said, lest …" ("Hear me" is not in the Hebrew); מוֹט of the foot "slip"; הִגְדִּילוּ עָלַי "they make (themselves) great against me". Where the words said end is not marked: the second line may also be the psalmist's own report.
- **"For I am ready to halt, and my sorrow is continually before me" → "For I am ready for stumbling, and my pain is before me continually"** (v17) — Hebrew לְצֶלַע נָכוֹן, "ready for stumbling (limping)" (צֶלַע here a noun of the root "limp", not צֵלָע "side": the Ps 35–36 row); נָכוֹן "ready" (lit. "established, prepared"); מַכְאוֹב "pain"; תָּמִיד "continually".
- **"For I will declare mine iniquity; I will be sorry for my sin" → "For my iniquity I declare; I am anxious because of my sin"** (v18) — Hebrew עֲוֺנִי אַגִּיד, the object set first; אֶדְאַג, דָּאַג "be anxious, worry" (not "be sorry"); מֵחַטָּאתִי "because of my sin".
- **"But mine enemies are lively, and they are strong: and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied" → "And my enemies are alive, they are mighty, and many are those who hate me falsely"** (v19) — Hebrew חַיִּים "living, alive", as printed (a common conjecture reads חִנָּם "without cause", as Ps 35:19, from memory: not followed); עָצֵמוּ "they are mighty" (עָצַם); שֹׂנְאַי שָׁקֶר, "those who hate me (with) falsehood".
- **"They also that render evil for good are mine adversaries; because I follow the thing that good is" → "And those who repay evil for good are adversaries to me for my pursuing good"** (v20) — Hebrew מְשַׁלְּמֵי (שִׁלֵּם "repay"); יִשְׂטְנוּנִי, verb of שָׂטָן "adversary": "they are adversaries to me" (accuse / oppose me); תַּחַת "for" both times (תַּחַת רָדְפִי־טוֹב "for my pursuing good", in return for it); רָדַף "pursue". Qere רָדְפִי, ketiv רדופי: the same meaning.
- **"O LORD … O my God" → "Yahweh … my Elohim"** (v21) — Hebrew has no "O"; the line break follows the Hebrew (Yahweh closes the first line).
- **"Make haste to help me, O Lord my salvation" → "Hasten to my help, Adonai, my salvation"** (v22) — Hebrew חוּשָׁה לְעֶזְרָתִי, חוּשׁ "hasten"; עֶזְרָה "help"; אֲדֹנָי (*Adonai*); תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–22 = Heb v2–23.
- **Third penitential psalm** (with Ps 6; 32; 51; 102; 130; 143).
- **Structure** — the body in its sickness (v1–10), friends and enemies (v11–16), confession and plea (v17–22).
