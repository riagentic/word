# Psalm 38

A Psalm of David, for remembrance.

[1] O Yahweh, rebuke me not in your anger,
nor discipline me in your wrath!

[2] For your arrows have sunk into me,
and your hand has come down on me.

[3] There is no soundness in my flesh because of your indignation;
there is no health in my bones because of my sin.

[4] For my iniquities have gone over my head;
like a heavy burden, they are too heavy for me.

[5] My wounds stink and fester
because of my foolishness,

[6] I am utterly bowed down and prostrate;
all the day I go about mourning.

[7] For my sides are filled with burning,
and there is no soundness in my flesh.

[8] I am feebly crushed;
I groan because of the tumult of my heart.

[9] O Lord, all my longing is before you;
my sighing is not hidden from you.

[10] My heart throbs; my strength fails me,
and the light of my eyes — it also has gone from me.

[11] My friends and companions stand aloof from my plague,
and my nearest kin stand far off.

[12] Those who seek my life lay their snares;
those who seek my hurt speak of ruin
and meditate treachery all day long.

[13] But I am like a deaf man; I do not hear,
like a mute man who does not open his mouth.

[14] I have become like a man who does not hear,
and in whose mouth are no rebukes.

[15] But for you, O Yahweh, do I wait;
you will answer, O Lord my *Elohim*.

[16] For I said, "Let them not rejoice over me,
who boast against me when my foot slips!"

[17] For I am ready to fall,
and my pain is ever before me.

[18] I confess my iniquity;
I am sorry for my sin.

[19] But my foes are vigorous, they are mighty,
and many are those who hate me wrongfully.

[20] Those who render me evil for good
accuse me because I follow after good.

[21] Do not forsake me, O Yahweh!
O my *Elohim*, be not far from me!

[22] Make haste to help me,
O Lord, my salvation!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 9, 15, 21, 22) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — body-symptom catalog (v1-10) transitions to social isolation (v11-16) and confession (v17-22).

### Notes
- **Third penitential psalm**.
- **"For remembrance"** — liturgical use; possibly accompanied *azkarah* memorial offering.
