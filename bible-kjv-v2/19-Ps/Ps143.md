# Psalm 143

A psalm of David.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, hear my prayer;
give ear to my pleas for favor;
in your faithfulness answer me, in your righteousness.

[2] And do not enter into judgment with your servant,
for no one living is righteous before you.

[3] For the enemy has pursued my soul;
he has crushed my life to the ground;
he has made me dwell in dark places
like the everlasting dead.

[4] And my spirit grows faint within me;
in my midst my heart is appalled.

[5] I have remembered the days from of old;
I have softly recited all your work;
on the work of your hands I muse.

[6] I have spread out my hands to you;
my soul is like a weary land for you. Selah.

[7] Quickly answer me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
my spirit is spent.
Do not hide your face from me,
and I would become like those who go down to the pit.

[8] Make me hear in the morning your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
for in you I have trusted.
Make me know the way in which I should go,
for to you I have lifted up my soul.

[9] Deliver me from my enemies, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
to you I have taken cover.

[10] Teach me to do your will,
for you are my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.
Your spirit is good;
may it lead me in a level land.

[11] On account of your name, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, make me live;
in your righteousness bring my soul out of distress.

[12] And in your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ annihilate my enemies,
and make perish all who harass my soul,
for I am your servant.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 7, 9, 11) — Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהָי, "my Elohim", written with a full vowel letter (as Ps 145:1; not אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*). No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "A psalm of David"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד, the first words of v1 in the Hebrew. לְ "by / for / belonging to": "of" leaves it open.
- **"Hear my prayer, O LORD, give ear to my supplications: in thy faithfulness answer me, and in thy righteousness" → "Yahweh, hear my prayer; give ear to my pleas for favor; in your faithfulness answer me, in your righteousness"** (v1) — Hebrew יְהוָה שְׁמַע תְּפִלָּתִי, the name first; הַאֲזִינָה "give ear to" (הֶאֱזִין); תַּחֲנוּנִים "pleas for favor" (of the root חָנַן, as Ps 28:2; 86:6); אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness"; צְדָקָה "righteousness"; no "and" before the last phrase.
- **"And enter not into judgment with thy servant: for in thy sight shall no man living be justified" → "And do not enter into judgment with your servant, for no one living is righteous before you"** (v2) — Hebrew וְאַל־תָּבוֹא בְמִשְׁפָּט אֶת־עַבְדֶּךָ: בּוֹא בְמִשְׁפָּט אֶת "come into judgment with" (to take to law); עֶבֶד "slave", of the speaker before Yahweh "servant" (its row; also v12). כִּי לֹא־יִצְדַּק לְפָנֶיךָ כָל־חָי: צָדַק qal "be righteous" (its row), an imperfect ("will be righteous" is also possible); not the passive "be justified"; לְפָנֶיךָ "before you". Rom 3:20 and Gal 2:16 take up the line in Greek ("by works of law no flesh will be justified before him"): "works of law" and "flesh" are Paul's words; the verse here follows the Hebrew.
- **"For the enemy hath persecuted my soul; he hath smitten my life down to the ground; he hath made me to dwell in darkness, as those that have been long dead" → "For the enemy has pursued my soul; he has crushed my life to the ground; he has made me dwell in dark places like the everlasting dead"** (v3) — Hebrew רָדַף "pursue"; אוֹיֵב has no article ("the" is supplied); דִּכָּא "crush" (as Ps 94:5); חַיָּה "life"; הוֹשִׁיבַנִי "he has made me dwell" (lit. "sit"); מַחֲשַׁכִּים "dark places", plural (as Ps 88:6). כְּמֵתֵי עוֹלָם, literally "like the dead of the age": עוֹלָם means time out of sight, past or to come, so "dead long ago" or "dead for ever"; "everlasting" is the row for עוֹלָם as a genitive. Lam 3:6 has the same words.
- **"Therefore is my spirit overwhelmed within me; my heart within me is desolate" → "And my spirit grows faint within me; in my midst my heart is appalled"** (v4) — Hebrew וַתִּתְעַטֵּף, with "and" (not "therefore"); עָטַף hitpael "grow faint" (as Ps 77:3; 142:3); עָלַי "within me" (lit. "upon me"), then בְּתוֹכִי "in my midst": two expressions; יִשְׁתּוֹמֵם, hitpolel of שָׁמֵם "be appalled" (of persons, as Ps 40:15).
- **"I remember the days of old; I meditate on all thy works; I muse on the work of thy hands" → "I have remembered the days from of old; I have softly recited all your work; on the work of your hands I muse"** (v5) — Hebrew זָכַרְתִּי, הָגִיתִי, perfects, then the imperfect אֲשׂוֹחֵחַ; יָמִים מִקֶּדֶם "days from of old" (as Ps 77:5). הָגָה "softly recite" (the reviewer row; "meditate" names only thinking), the בְּ of the object not rendered, as Ps 77:12, whose line is the same; פֹּעַל "work". בְּמַעֲשֵׂה יָדֶיךָ: מַעֲשֶׂה "work" (another word than פֹּעַל: English has one); שִׂיחַ "muse" (its row).
- **"I stretch forth my hands unto thee: my soul thirsteth after thee, as a thirsty land" → "I have spread out my hands to you; my soul is like a weary land for you"** (v6) — Hebrew פֵּרַשְׂתִּי "I have spread out", a perfect; נַפְשִׁי כְּאֶרֶץ־עֲיֵפָה לְךָ has no verb ("is" supplied): "thirsts" is not written; עָיֵף "weary" (as Ps 63:1), of a land: dry, exhausted. לְךָ "for you" (= longing for you).
- **"Hear me speedily, O LORD: my spirit faileth: hide not thy face from me, lest I be like unto them that go down into the pit" → "Quickly answer me, Yahweh; my spirit is spent. Do not hide your face from me, and I would become like those who go down to the pit"** (v7) — Hebrew מַהֵר עֲנֵנִי, as Ps 69:17 ("quickly answer me"); עָנָה "answer" (not "hear"). כָּלְתָה "is spent" (כָּלָה, as Ps 84:2), a perfect. וְנִמְשַׁלְתִּי, "and I become like" (as Ps 28:1, where פֶּן "lest" stands before it): with "and", the outcome if the face is hidden; "would" says that. בּוֹר "pit".
- **"Cause me to hear thy lovingkindness in the morning … cause me to know the way wherein I should walk; for I lift up my soul unto thee" → "Make me hear in the morning your chesed … Make me know the way in which I should go, for to you I have lifted up my soul"** (v8) — Hebrew הַשְׁמִיעֵנִי בַבֹּקֶר חַסְדֶּךָ, in this order; חֶסֶד "chesed" (token). בְךָ בָטָחְתִּי "in you I have trusted", a perfect (as Ps 25:2). דֶּרֶךְ־זוּ אֵלֵךְ, "the way in which I shall go" (זוּ "which", as Ps 142:3); הָלַךְ qal "go". נָשָׂאתִי נַפְשִׁי, perfect: "lift up one's soul to" (= long for, count on; its row).
- **"I flee unto thee to hide me" → "to you I have taken cover"** (v9) — Hebrew אֵלֶיךָ כִסִּתִי, literally "to you I have covered", a perfect of כִּסָּה "cover", with no object ("myself" is understood). Many read חָסִיתִי "I have taken refuge" (one letter differs) with the Greek "I fled" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God: thy spirit is good; lead me into the land of uprightness" → "Teach me to do your will, for you are my Elohim. Your spirit is good; may it lead me in a level land"** (v10) — Hebrew לַמְּדֵנִי "teach me"; רְצוֹנֶךָ "your will" (רָצוֹן, what pleases you; as Ps 40:8). רוּחֲךָ טוֹבָה: the adjective has no article after a noun made definite by "your", so it is the predicate: "your spirit is good", not "your good spirit" (the earlier text). תַּנְחֵנִי, an imperfect: "it (the spirit, feminine) will lead me" or "you will lead me" (the same form); taken with the spirit and rendered as a wish, the other reading in this note. בְּאֶרֶץ מִישׁוֹר "in a land of level ground" (מִישׁוֹר, as Ps 26:12; 27:11); "uprightness" is the moral sense of the same word.
- **"Quicken me, O LORD, for thy name's sake: for thy righteousness' sake bring my soul out of trouble" → "On account of your name, Yahweh, make me live; in your righteousness bring my soul out of distress"** (v11) — Hebrew לְמַעַן־שִׁמְךָ "on account of your name" (as Ps 23:3; 31:3), in the Hebrew order; תְּחַיֵּנִי and תוֹצִיא are imperfects in a prayer, rendered as requests ("you will make me live … you will bring out"); חִיָּה piel "make live" (as Ps 71:20). בְּצִדְקָתְךָ "in your righteousness" (not "for … sake"); צָרָה "distress".
- **"And of thy mercy cut off mine enemies, and destroy all them that afflict my soul" → "And in your chesed annihilate my enemies, and make perish all who harass my soul"** (v12) — Hebrew וּבְחַסְדְּךָ תַּצְמִית … וְהַאֲבַדְתָּ, an imperfect and a perfect with "and", rendered as requests with v11 ("you will annihilate … you will make perish"); הִצְמִית "annihilate" (as Ps 18:40; 73:27); הֶאֱבִיד "make perish" (its row); צֹרְרִים "those who harass" (צָרַר, as Ps 23:5); כִּי אֲנִי עַבְדֶּךָ "for I am your servant".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew; the verse numbers are the same.
- **Seventh of the seven penitential psalms** of church tradition (6, 32, 38, 51, 102, 130, 143); the title is not in the Hebrew.
- **v2 taken up in Rom 3:20; Gal 2:16** (see Changes).
