# Psalm 92

A psalm, a song for the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧.

[1] It is good to give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and to make music to your name, ⟦elyon|Elyon|Most High⟧,

[2] to tell in the morning your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and your faithfulness in the nights,

[3] upon the ten and upon the harp,
upon ⟦higgaion|Higgayon|Higgaion⟧ with the lyre.

[4] For you have made me glad, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, by your work;
in the works of your hands I will shout for joy.

[5] How great are your works, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧!
Very deep are your plans.

[6] A brutish man does not know,
and a fool does not understand this:

[7] when the wicked bud like plants,
and all the workers of wickedness blossom,
it is for their being destroyed perpetually.

[8] And you are on high forever, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧.

[9] For behold, your enemies, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
for behold, your enemies will perish;
all the workers of wickedness will be parted.

[10] And you have raised my horn like a wild ox;
I am moistened with fresh oil.

[11] And my eye has looked on those who watch for me;
of those who rise up against me, doing evil, my ears will hear.

[12] The righteous one will bud like the palm tree;
like a cedar in ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ he will grow great.

[13] Planted in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
in the courts of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ they will bud.

[14] They will still bear fruit in old age;
fat and luxuriant they will be,

[15] to tell that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is upright;
my Rock, and there is no injustice in him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 5, 8, 9, 13, 15) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"O most High" → "Elyon"** (v1) — Hebrew עֶלְיוֹן; the Hebrew vocatives have no "O".
- **"A Psalm or Song for the sabbath day" → "A psalm, a song for the day of the Shabbat"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר שִׁיר לְיוֹם הַשַּׁבָּת: "a psalm, a song" in the Hebrew order ("or" is not written); לְיוֹם "for the day of"; הַשַּׁבָּת with the article.
- **"It is a good thing … and to sing praises unto thy name" → "It is good … and to make music to your name"** (v1) — Hebrew טוֹב "good"; הוֹדָה "give thanks"; זִמֵּר "make music" (as Ps 9:2, with Elyon).
- **"To shew forth thy lovingkindness in the morning, and thy faithfulness every night" → "to tell in the morning your chesed (steadfast love), and your faithfulness in the nights"** (v2) — Hebrew לְהַגִּיד "to tell" (הִגִּיד, also v15); חֶסֶד; אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness"; בַּלֵּילוֹת "in the nights", plural.
- **"Upon an instrument of ten strings, and upon the psaltery; upon the harp with a solemn sound" → "upon the ten and upon the harp, upon Higgayon with the lyre"** (v3) — Hebrew עֲלֵי־עָשׂוֹר "upon the ten" (an instrument of ten strings, as Ps 33:2 נֵבֶל עָשׂוֹר; "strings" is not written); נֵבֶל "harp", כִּנּוֹר "lyre" (the kinds are uncertain); הִגָּיוֹן, noun of הָגָה "softly recite" (Ps 1:2), a low sounding, here a musical term (as Ps 9:16): the meaning is uncertain; KJV "a solemn sound".
- **"thou, LORD, hast made me glad through thy work: I will triumph in the works of thy hands" → "you have made me glad, Yahweh, by your work; in the works of your hands I will shout for joy"** (v4) — Hebrew שִׂמַּחְתַּנִי "you have made me glad"; פֹּעַל "work" (also Ps 90:16) beside מַעֲשֶׂה "works" (v4, 5): English has one word; אֲרַנֵּן "I will shout for joy" (רִנֵּן).
- **"and thy thoughts are very deep" → "Very deep are your plans"** (v5) — Hebrew מְאֹד עָמְקוּ מַחְשְׁבֹתֶיךָ, "very" first, no "and"; מַחֲשָׁבָה "plan" (as Ps 33:11).
- **"A brutish man knoweth not; neither doth a fool understand this" → "A brutish man does not know, and a fool does not understand this"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ־בַּעַר "a brutish man"; כְּסִיל "fool"; וּ "and".
- **"When the wicked spring as the grass, and when all the workers of iniquity do flourish; it is that they shall be destroyed for ever" → "when the wicked bud like plants, and all the workers of wickedness blossom, it is for their being destroyed perpetually"** (v7) — Hebrew בִּפְרֹחַ "when … bud" (פָּרַח, also v12, 13); עֵשֶׂב "plants" (not חָצִיר "grass"); וַיָּצִיצוּ "and … blossom" (הֵצִיץ), a narrative form after the infinitive, given as present with it; פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "the workers of wickedness" (also v9); לְהִשָּׁמְדָם "for their being destroyed"; עֲדֵי־עַד "perpetually" (as לָעַד; לְעוֹלָם "forever" is v8).
- **"But thou, LORD, art most high for evermore" → "And you are on high forever, Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה מָרוֹם לְעֹלָם יְהוָה: מָרוֹם "height", "on high" (as Ps 93:4); the name at the end. Hebrew v9 stands alone, a single line.
- **"For, lo, thine enemies … shall be scattered" → "For behold, your enemies … will be parted"** (v9) — Hebrew הִנֵּה "behold"; אָבַד "perish"; יִתְפָּרְדוּ, hitpael of פָּרַד "part": they are parted from one another.
- **"But my horn shalt thou exalt like the horn of an unicorn: I shall be anointed with fresh oil" → "And you have raised my horn like a wild ox; I am moistened with fresh oil"** (v10) — Hebrew וַתָּרֶם, narrative form, "and you have raised" (past, not "shalt"); כִּרְאֵים "like a wild ox" (רְאֵם; "unicorn" follows the Greek, from memory; "the horn of" is not written). בַּלֹּתִי as pointed is "I am moistened, mixed" (בָּלַל, as flour with oil, Lev 2:4): "anointed" is another verb; the Greek has "my old age", from בָּלָה "wear out" (from memory): uncertain. שֶׁמֶן רַעֲנָן "fresh oil" (רַעֲנָן "luxuriant" of trees, v14; of oil "fresh").
- **"Mine eye also shall see my desire on mine enemies, and mine ears shall hear my desire of the wicked that rise up against me" → "And my eye has looked on those who watch for me; of those who rise up against me, doing evil, my ears will hear"** (v11) — Hebrew וַתַּבֵּט עֵינִי בְּשׁוּרָי, narrative form, "and my eye has looked on"; שׁוּרָי "those who watch for me" (as שׁוֹרְרַי, Ps 5:8); בַּקָּמִים עָלַי מְרֵעִים "of those who rise up against me, doing evil" (מְרֵעִים "evildoers", as Ps 37:1); תִּשְׁמַעְנָה "will hear". "my desire" (KJV, twice) and "the downfall" or "the doom" are not written: what eye and ear take in is not said.
- **"The righteous shall flourish like the palm tree: he shall grow like a cedar in Lebanon" → "The righteous one will bud like the palm tree; like a cedar in Lebanon he will grow great"** (v12) — Hebrew צַדִּיק singular; יִפְרָח "bud" (פָּרַח, as v7: the wicked and the righteous with one verb); תָּמָר "palm tree"; יִשְׂגֶּה "grow great" (שָׂגָה).
- **"Those that be planted in the house of the LORD shall flourish in the courts of our God" → "Planted in the house of Yahweh, in the courts of our Elohim they will bud"** (v13) — Hebrew שְׁתוּלִים "planted" (as Ps 1:3), with no "those that be"; חַצְרוֹת "courts"; יַפְרִיחוּ, hifil of פָּרַח, "they will bud" (put forth buds).
- **"They shall still bring forth fruit in old age; they shall be fat and flourishing" → "They will still bear fruit in old age; fat and luxuriant they will be"** (v14) — Hebrew יְנוּבוּן "bear fruit" (נוּב); שֵׂיבָה "old age" (lit. "gray hair"); דְּשֵׁנִים "fat" (rich, full of sap); רַעֲנַנִּים "luxuriant" (fresh, full of leaves).
- **"To shew that the LORD is upright: he is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in him" → "to tell that Yahweh is upright; my Rock, and there is no injustice in him"** (v15) — Hebrew לְהַגִּיד as v2; יָשָׁר "upright"; צוּרִי "my Rock", with no "he is"; עַוְלָתָה (qere) "injustice" (עַוְלָה, as עָוֶל); the ketiv עלתה is a shorter spelling of the same word.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v15 = Heb v16.
- **Shabbat psalm** — the only psalm whose heading names a day.
