# Psalm 40

To the overseer. Of David. A psalm.

[1] I surely waited for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and he turned to me and heard my cry for help.

[2] And he brought me up from the pit of tumult,
from the mud of the mire,
and he made my feet stand on a crag;
he established my steps.

[3] And he put in my mouth a new song,
praise to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
many will see and fear,
and will trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] Happy is the man who has made ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his trust,
and has not turned to the arrogant,
and to those who stray to a lie.

[5] Many things you have done, you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
your wonderful deeds and your thoughts toward us;
there is no setting in order before you;
let me tell and let me speak:
they are too mighty to recount.

[6] Sacrifice and grain offering you have not delighted in;
ears you have dug for me;
burnt offering and sin offering you have not asked.

[7] Then I said, "Behold, I have come;
in the scroll of a book it is written upon me.

[8] To do your will, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, I delight,
and your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ is in the midst of my inward parts."

[9] I have brought good news of righteousness
in a great assembly;
behold, my lips I do not keep back;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, you yourself know.

[10] Your righteousness I have not covered in the midst of my heart;
your faithfulness and your salvation I have declared;
I have not hidden your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and your truth
from a great assembly.

[11] You, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, will not keep back your compassion from me;
your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and your truth will continually guard me.

[12] For evils have encompassed me
beyond number;
my iniquities have overtaken me,
and I am not able to see;
they are mightier than the hairs of my head,
and my heart has forsaken me.

[13] Be pleased, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, to deliver me;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, hasten to my help.

[14] Let them be ashamed and be abashed together,
those who seek my soul, to sweep it away;
let them be turned back and be put to shame,
those who delight in my evil.

[15] Let them be appalled because of their shame,
those who say to me, "Aha! Aha!"

[16] Let them exult and rejoice in you,
all who seek you;
let them say continually, "Let ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be great,"
those who love your salvation.

[17] And I am afflicted and needy;
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ thinks of me.
My help and my rescuer are you;
my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not delay.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 4, 5, 9, 11, 13, 16) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 5, 8, 17) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. Of David. A psalm."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר, in this order. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"I waited patiently for the LORD" → "I surely waited for Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew קַוֹּה קִוִּיתִי, infinitive absolute with its verb, lit. "waiting I waited" (rendered by its force, "surely"); "patiently" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"he inclined unto me, and heard my cry" → "he turned to me and heard my cry for help"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיֵּט אֵלַי, נָטָה אֶל "turn to" (the verb of "stretch out, bend": no object, "his ear", is written); שַׁוְעָה "cry for help".
- **"out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay" → "from the pit of tumult, from the mud of the mire"** (v2) — Hebrew מִבּוֹר שָׁאוֹן מִטִּיט הַיָּוֵן. שָׁאוֹן "tumult, roar" (of waters, Ps 65:7; "destruction", "desolation" are also given, uncertain); טִיט "mud"; יָוֵן "mire" (Ps 69:2).
- **"set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings" → "he made my feet stand on a crag; he established my steps"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיָּקֶם עַל־סֶלַע רַגְלַי כּוֹנֵן אֲשֻׁרָי; הֵקִים "make stand"; סֶלַע "crag" (צוּר is "Rock"); כּוֹנֵן "establish", a perfect without "and".
- **"even praise unto our God" → "praise to our Elohim"** (v3) — Hebrew תְּהִלָּה לֵאלֹהֵינוּ; "a song of praise" added a second "song". יִרְאוּ … וְיִירָאוּ "see … fear", a play on sound.
- **"Blessed is that man" → "Happy is the man"** (v4) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי הַגֶּבֶר; אַשְׁרֵי "happy" (never "blessed": that is בָּרוּךְ). גֶּבֶר, which elsewhere is "mighty man", stands here alone: "man". שָׂם … מִבְטַחוֹ "made … his trust" (שִׂים "set, make"); מִבְטָח "trust".
- **"respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies" → "has not turned to the arrogant, and to those who stray to a lie"** (v4) — Hebrew וְלֹא־פָנָה אֶל־רְהָבִים וְשָׂטֵי כָזָב: one "not" governs both; רְהָבִים "the arrogant" (only here; from רָהַב "storm, be arrogant"); שָׂטֵי from שׂוּט / שָׂטָה "stray, turn aside"; כָּזָב "lie", singular.
- **"Many, O LORD my God, are thy wonderful works which thou hast done" → "Many things you have done, you, Yahweh my Elohim, your wonderful deeds"** (v5) — Hebrew רַבּוֹת עָשִׂיתָ אַתָּה יְהוָה אֱלֹהַי נִפְלְאֹתֶיךָ, Hebrew order, with the pronoun אַתָּה kept; נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds".
- **"they cannot be reckoned up in order unto thee" → "there is no setting in order before you"** (v5) — Hebrew אֵין עֲרֹךְ אֵלֶיךָ, lit. "there is no arranging to you" (עָרַךְ "arrange"): either "none can be set in order beside you" (none compares with you) or "they cannot be set out in order before you". The Hebrew allows both; the verse keeps the words.
- **"if I would declare and speak of them, they are more than can be numbered" → "let me tell and let me speak: they are too mighty to recount"** (v5) — Hebrew אַגִּידָה וַאֲדַבֵּרָה עָצְמוּ מִסַּפֵּר: two cohortatives (no "if", no "of them"); עָצַם "be mighty" (strong in number), also v12; מִסַּפֵּר "beyond recounting".
- **"Sacrifice and offering thou didst not desire; mine ears hast thou opened" → "Sacrifice and grain offering you have not delighted in; ears you have dug for me"** (v6) — Hebrew זֶבַח וּמִנְחָה לֹא־חָפַצְתָּ אָזְנַיִם כָּרִיתָ לִּי. מִנְחָה "grain offering"; חָפֵץ "delight in"; אָזְנַיִם "ears" (dual, no article, no "my"); כָּרָה "dig" (as Ps 7:15; 57:6). The Hebrew is kept as it stands. Heb 10:5 quotes the Greek of the Psalm, "a body you prepared for me" (the Septuagint as Hebrews has it; from memory); that is not the Hebrew wording and is not put into the verse.
- **"burnt offering and sin offering hast thou not required" → "burnt offering and sin offering you have not asked"** (v6) — Hebrew עוֹלָה וַחֲטָאָה לֹא שָׁאָלְתָּ; חֲטָאָה is the word "sin" (here of the offering, as חַטָּאת); שָׁאַל "ask".
- **"in the volume of the book it is written of me" → "in the scroll of a book it is written upon me"** (v7) — Hebrew בִּמְגִלַּת־סֵפֶר כָּתוּב עָלָי: מְגִלַּת־סֵפֶר "a scroll of a book" (no article); עָלָי "upon me" — "about me" or "prescribed for me"; the Hebrew leaves it open.
- **"thy law is within my heart" → "your Torah is in the midst of my inward parts"** (v8) — Hebrew וְתוֹרָתְךָ בְּתוֹךְ מֵעָי; מֵעִים "inward parts" (not לֵב "heart", which v10 has); רָצוֹן "will, pleasure"; חָפַצְתִּי "I delight" (perfect of a state).
- **"I have preached righteousness in the great congregation" → "I have brought good news of righteousness in a great assembly"** (v9) — Hebrew בִּשַּׂרְתִּי צֶדֶק בְּקָהָל רָב; בִּשַּׂר "bring good news"; קָהָל "assembly"; רָב "great, many", no article (also v10).
- **"I have not refrained my lips, O LORD, thou knowest" → "my lips I do not keep back; Yahweh, you yourself know"** (v9) — Hebrew שְׂפָתַי לֹא אֶכְלָא יְהוָה אַתָּה יָדָעְתָּ; כָּלָא "keep back" (imperfect), the same verb as v11 "you will not keep back"; the pronoun אַתָּה stands with the verb.
- **"I have declared thy faithfulness … I have not concealed thy lovingkindness" → "your faithfulness and your salvation I have declared; I have not hidden your chesed"** (v10) — Hebrew אָמָרְתִּי "I have said" (אָמַר: "said" will not stand with an object in English, so "declared"); כִּסָּה "cover"; כִּחֵד "hide"; חֶסֶד carries its token; לְקָהָל רָב "from a great assembly" (lit. "to, for": with respect to).
- **"Withhold not thou thy tender mercies from me, O LORD: let thy lovingkindness and thy truth continually preserve me" → "You, Yahweh, will not keep back your compassion from me; your chesed and your truth will continually guard me"** (v11) — Hebrew אַתָּה יְהוָה לֹא־תִכְלָא … יִצְּרוּנִי: לֹא with the imperfect is a statement (not the prohibition אַל); the second verb may be read as a wish ("let … guard me"); נָצַר "guard"; רַחֲמִים "compassion". The two cola stand on two lines.
- **"innumerable evils have compassed me about … so that I am not able to look up … therefore my heart faileth me" → "evils have encompassed me beyond number … I am not able to see … and my heart has forsaken me"** (v12) — Hebrew אָפְפוּ־עָלַי רָעוֹת עַד־אֵין מִסְפָּר, lit. "until there is no number"; לִרְאוֹת "to see" ("look up" was added); עָצְמוּ "they are mightier" (as v5); וְלִבִּי עֲזָבָנִי "and my heart has forsaken me" (עָזַב "forsake").
- **"make haste to help me" → "hasten to my help"** (v13) — Hebrew לְעֶזְרָתִי חוּשָׁה; חוּשׁ "hasten"; רְצֵה "be pleased" (Ps 70:1 has no רְצֵה and reads אֱלֹהִים in the first colon).
- **"Let them be ashamed and confounded together that seek after my soul to destroy it; let them be driven backward and put to shame that wish me evil" → "Let them be ashamed and be abashed together, those who seek my soul, to sweep it away; let them be turned back and be put to shame, those who delight in my evil"** (v14) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ וְיַחְפְּרוּ יַחַד מְבַקְשֵׁי נַפְשִׁי לִסְפּוֹתָהּ יִסֹּגוּ אָחוֹר וְיִכָּלְמוּ חֲפֵצֵי רָעָתִי: jussives ("let them"); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", חָפֵר "be abashed" (another root than חָפַר "dig"), נִכְלַם "be put to shame" (three verbs, three renderings, as the Psalms rows); סָפָה "sweep away"; רָעָתִי "my evil" (the evil done to me). Ps 70:2 has no יַחַד and no לִסְפּוֹתָהּ.
- **"Let them be desolate for a reward of their shame" → "Let them be appalled because of their shame"** (v15) — Hebrew יָשֹׁמּוּ עַל־עֵקֶב בָּשְׁתָּם; שָׁמֵם of persons "be appalled"; עַל־עֵקֶב lit. "on the heel of" (as the result of). Ps 70:3 has יָשׁוּבוּ "let them turn back" and no לִי.
- **"The LORD be magnified" → "Let Yahweh be great"** (v16) — Hebrew יִגְדַּל יְהוָה, a wish (as Ps 35:27); שׂוּשׂ "exult", שָׂמַח "rejoice"; תָּמִיד "continually"; the Hebrew order of the cola is kept. Ps 70:4 has וְיֹאמְרוּ "and let them say", אֱלֹהִים for יְהוָה, and יְשׁוּעָתֶךָ for תְּשׁוּעָתֶךָ (two words for "salvation").
- **"But I am poor and needy; yet the Lord thinketh upon me" → "And I am afflicted and needy; Adonai thinks of me"** (v17) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי עָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן אֲדֹנָי יַחֲשָׁב לִי; עָנִי "afflicted", אֶבְיוֹן "needy"; יַחֲשָׁב imperfect, "thinks of me" (or a wish, "may Adonai think of me"); מְפַלֵּט "rescuer"; אִחֵר "delay". Ps 70:5 has אֱלֹהִים חוּשָׁה־לִּי "Elohim, hasten to me", עֶזְרִי for עֶזְרָתִי, and יְהוָה for אֱלֹהַי in the last colon.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–17 = Heb v2–18.
- **v6–8 in Heb 10:5–7** — Hebrews follows the Greek ("a body you prepared for me"); the Hebrew has "ears you have dug for me".
- **v13–17 = Ps 70** — the same psalm with small differences (listed above at each verse); Ps 70 keeps its own wording.
