# Psalm 40

To the chief musician. A Psalm of David.

[1] I waited patiently for Yahweh;
he inclined to me and heard my cry.

[2] He drew me up from the pit of destruction,
out of the miry bog,
and set my feet upon a rock,
making my steps secure.

[3] He put a new song in my mouth,
a song of praise to our *Elohim*.
Many will see and fear,
and put their trust in Yahweh.

[4] Blessed is the man who makes Yahweh his trust,
who does not turn to the proud,
to those who go astray after lies!

[5] You have multiplied, O Yahweh my *Elohim*,
your wondrous deeds and your thoughts toward us;
none can compare with you!
I will proclaim and tell of them,
yet they are more than can be numbered.

[6] Sacrifice and offering you have not desired,
but you have given me an open ear.
Burnt offering and sin offering — you have not required.

[7] Then I said, "Behold, I have come;
in the scroll of the book it is written of me.

[8] I delight to do your will, O my *Elohim*;
your Law is within my heart."

[9] I have told the glad news of righteousness
in the great congregation;
behold, I have not restrained my lips,
as you know, O Yahweh.

[10] I have not hidden your righteousness within my heart;
I have spoken of your faithfulness and your salvation;
I have not concealed your steadfast love and your truth
from the great congregation.

[11] As for you, O Yahweh, you will not restrain
your compassion from me;
your steadfast love and your truth will continually preserve me.

[12] For evils have encompassed me
beyond number;
my iniquities have overtaken me,
and I cannot see;
they are more than the hairs of my head;
my heart fails me.

[13] Be pleased, O Yahweh, to deliver me!
O Yahweh, make haste to help me!

[14] Let those be put to shame and disappointed altogether
who seek to snatch away my life;
let those be turned back and brought to dishonor
who delight in my hurt!

[15] Let those be appalled because of their shame
who say to me, "Aha, Aha!"

[16] But may all who seek you
rejoice and be glad in you;
may those who love your salvation
say continually, "Great is Yahweh!"

[17] As for me, I am poor and needy,
but Lord takes thought for me.
You are my help and my deliverer;
do not delay, O my *Elohim*!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 4, 5, 9, 11, 13, 16) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 5, 8) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — thanksgiving for deliverance (v1-10) pivots to renewed lament (v11-17); reuse in Ps 70.

### Notes
- **v6-8 cited Heb 10:5-7** — messianic application to Christ's obedience.
- **"Open ear"** (v6) — Hebrew "dug my ears"; LXX renders "prepared a body," which Hebrews follows.
