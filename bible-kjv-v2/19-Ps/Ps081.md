# Psalm 81

To the chief musician upon ⟦gittith|Gittit|Gittith⟧. A Psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] Sing aloud to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our strength;
shout for joy to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧!

[2] Raise a song; sound the tambourine,
the sweet lyre with the harp.

[3] Blow the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ at the new moon,
at the full moon, on our feast day.

[4] For it is a statute for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
an ordinance of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[5] He made it a decree in ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧
when he went out over the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.
I hear a language I had not known:

[6] "I relieved your shoulder of the burden;
your hands were freed from the basket.

[7] In distress you called, and I delivered you;
I answered you in the secret place of thunder;
I tested you at the waters of ⟦meribah|Merivah|Meribah⟧. Selah.

[8] Hear, O my people, while I admonish you!
O ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, if you would but listen to me!

[9] There shall be no strange god among you;
you shall not bow down to a foreign god.

[10] I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
who brought you up out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.
Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.

[11] But my people did not listen to my voice;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would not submit to me.

[12] So I gave them over to their stubborn hearts,
to follow their own counsels.

[13] Oh, that my people would listen to me,
that ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would walk in my ways!

[14] I would soon subdue their enemies
and turn my hand against their foes.

[15] Those who hate ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ would cringe toward him,
and their time would last forever.

[16] But he would feed you with the finest of the wheat,
and with honey from the rock I would satisfy you."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 4, 10) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v10, 15) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Gittith** — musical term; retained.
- **Poetic structure** — festival call to worship (v1-5), divine oracle recalling Exodus (v6-16).
