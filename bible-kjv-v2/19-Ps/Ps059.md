# Psalm 59

To the chief musician. ⟦al-tashcheth|Al-tashchet|Do Not Destroy⟧. A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧ of David, when ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ sent men to watch his house to kill him.

[1] Deliver me from my enemies, O my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
protect me from those who rise up against me;

[2] deliver me from those who work evil,
and save me from bloodthirsty men.

[3] For behold, they lie in wait for my life;
fierce men stir up strife against me.
For no transgression or sin of mine, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,

[4] for no fault of mine, they run and make ready.
Awake, come to meet me, and see!

[5] You, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
rouse yourself to punish all the nations;
spare none of those who treacherously plot evil. Selah.

[6] Each evening they come back,
howling like dogs
and prowling about the city.

[7] There they are, bellowing with their mouths,
with swords in their lips —
for "Who," they think, "will hear us?"

[8] But you, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, laugh at them;
you hold all the nations in derision.

[9] O my Strength, I will watch for you,
for you, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, are my fortress.

[10] My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in his steadfast love will meet me;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will let me look in triumph on my enemies.

[11] Kill them not, lest my people forget;
make them totter by your power and bring them down,
O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, our shield!

[12] For the sin of their mouths, the words of their lips,
let them be trapped in their pride.
For the cursing and lies that they utter,

[13] consume them in wrath;
consume them till they are no more,
that they may know that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rules over ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
to the ends of the earth. Selah.

[14] Each evening they come back,
howling like dogs
and prowling about the city.

[15] They wander about for food
and growl if they do not get their fill.

[16] But I will sing of your strength;
I will sing aloud of your steadfast love in the morning.
For you have been to me a fortress
and a refuge in the day of my distress.

[17] O my Strength, I will sing praises to you,
for you, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, are my fortress,
my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of steadfast love.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 9, 10, 13, 17) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 5, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"LORD God of hosts" → "Yahweh *Elohim* of Hosts"** (v5) — triple divine name.
- **Miktam** — retained in superscription.
- **Al-tashcheth** — retained in superscription.
- **Poetic structure** — nocturnal-dog imagery as refrain (v6, 14); two lament cycles each ending in confidence.

### Notes
- **1 Sam 19** — Saul's agents watch David's house.
- **Dog imagery** — enemies as scavenging mongrels prowling at night.
