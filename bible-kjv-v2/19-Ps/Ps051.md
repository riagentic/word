# Psalm 51

To the overseer. A psalm of David, when ⟦nathan|Natan|Nathan⟧ the prophet came to him, after he had gone in to ⟦bathsheba|Bat-Sheva|Bathsheba⟧.

[1] Show me favor, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
according to your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧;
according to the abundance of your compassion
wipe out my transgressions.

[2] Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity,
and from my sin make me clean.

[3] For my transgressions I — I know,
and my sin is in front of me continually.

[4] Against you, you alone, I have sinned,
and what is evil in your eyes I have done,
so that you are righteous in your speaking,
pure in your judging.

[5] Behold, in iniquity I was brought forth in travail,
and in sin my mother conceived me.

[6] Behold, truth you delight in, in the inward parts,
and in the hidden part you make me know wisdom.

[7] Purge me of sin with hyssop, and I shall be clean;
wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.

[8] Make me hear joy and gladness;
let the bones you have crushed rejoice.

[9] Hide your face from my sins,
and all my iniquities wipe out.

[10] A clean heart create for me, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and a right spirit renew within me.

[11] Do not cast me away from before you,
and your holy spirit do not take from me.

[12] Bring back to me the joy of your salvation,
and with a willing spirit uphold me.

[13] Let me teach transgressors your ways,
and sinners shall return to you.

[14] Deliver me from bloodguilt, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation;
my tongue shall ring out your righteousness.

[15] ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, open my lips,
and my mouth shall tell your praise.

[16] For you do not delight in sacrifice, that I should give it;
with burnt offering you are not pleased.

[17] The sacrifices of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ are a broken spirit;
a heart broken and crushed, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you will not despise.

[18] Do good in your pleasure to ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
build the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[19] Then you will delight in sacrifices of righteousness,
burnt offering and whole offering;
then bulls shall go up on your altar.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 10, 14, 17) — Hebrew *Elohim*, as printed (the Elohistic psalms, 42–83).
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David, when Nathan the prophet came unto him, after he had gone in to Bathsheba" → "To the overseer. A psalm of David, when Nathan the prophet came to him, after he had gone in to Bathsheba."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד בְּבוֹא־אֵלָיו נָתָן הַנָּבִיא כַּאֲשֶׁר־בָּא אֶל־בַּת־שָׁבַע. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21; traditional "to the chief musician"); the לְ of לְדָוִד may be "by", "for" or "belonging to" David: "of" leaves it open. One verb בּוֹא stands twice: "came to him" of Nathan, "gone in to" of Bathsheba (the בּוֹא אֶל row); כַּאֲשֶׁר "when, as" with a perfect, "after" (2 Sam 11–12).
- **"Have mercy upon me" → "Show me favor"** (v1) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי, the חָנַן row ("show favor"; not "mercy").
- **"according to thy lovingkindness" → "according to your *chesed*"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּחַסְדֶּךָ; token `steadfast-love`.
- **"the multitude of thy tender mercies" → "the abundance of your compassion"** (v1) — Hebrew רֹב רַחֲמֶיךָ; רַחֲמִים "compassion" (its row).
- **"blot out" → "wipe out"** (v1, 9) — Hebrew מְחֵה, the מָחָה row ("blot out" only of writing or a name; here the transgressions are wiped away). In v9 the object stands first, as in the Hebrew.
- **"Wash me throughly" → "Wash me thoroughly"** (v2) — qere הֶרֶב, imperative "make much"; the written form הרבה (*harbeh*) "greatly" says the same. כִּבֵּס is the washing of clothes (the כִּבֵּס row), used of the man himself (also v7).
- **"cleanse me from my sin" → "from my sin make me clean"** (v2) — Hebrew וּמֵחַטָּאתִי טַהֲרֵנִי, in this order; טִהַר "make clean", root of טָהוֹר "clean" (v7, 10).
- **"For I acknowledge my transgressions" → "For my transgressions I — I know"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־פְשָׁעַי אֲנִי אֵדָע, the object first and the pronoun אֲנִי written out; יָדַע "know".
- **"my sin is ever before me" → "my sin is in front of me continually"** (v3) — Hebrew נֶגְדִּי תָמִיד; תָּמִיד "continually" (its row).
- **"Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight" → "Against you, you alone, I have sinned, and what is evil in your eyes I have done"** (v4) — Hebrew לְךָ לְבַדְּךָ חָטָאתִי וְהָרַע בְּעֵינֶיךָ עָשִׂיתִי: "what is evil" (הָרַע, with the article; no "this"), "in your eyes", the object before the verb.
- **"that thou mightest be justified when thou speakest, and be clear when thou judgest" → "so that you are righteous in your speaking, pure in your judging"** (v4) — Hebrew לְמַעַן תִּצְדַּק בְּדָבְרֶךָ תִּזְכֶּה בְשָׁפְטֶךָ: לְמַעַן "so that" (its row); צָדַק qal "be righteous" (its row; not "be justified", which is passive); זָכָה "be pure"; בְּ + infinitive "in your speaking", "in your judging", no "and". Rom 3:4 quotes the Greek wording (from memory: "when you are judged"); the Hebrew is not levelled to it.
- **"I was shapen in iniquity" → "in iniquity I was brought forth in travail"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּעָווֹן חוֹלָלְתִּי, polal of חִיל "writhe": "I was brought forth with writhing" (the birth pangs); not "shaped".
- **"did my mother conceive me" → "my mother conceived me"** (v5) — Hebrew יֶחֱמַתְנִי, of יָחַם "be hot" (the יָחַם row: of the mother "conceive"; lit. "was in heat with me").
- **"thou desirest truth in the inward parts: and in the hidden part thou shalt make me to know wisdom" → "truth you delight in, in the inward parts, and in the hidden part you make me know wisdom"** (v6) — Hebrew הֵן־אֱמֶת חָפַצְתָּ בַטֻּחוֹת וּבְסָתֻם חָכְמָה תוֹדִיעֵנִי: הֵן "behold" (as הִנֵּה); חָפֵץ "delight in", a perfect of state; טֻחוֹת "inward parts" (uncertain, as Job 38:36); סָתֻם "what is hidden". תוֹדִיעֵנִי is an imperfect: "you make (will make) me know"; it may also be a request, "make me know".
- **"Purge me with hyssop" → "Purge me of sin with hyssop"** (v7) — Hebrew תְּחַטְּאֵנִי, piel of חָטָא, "de-sin me" (the חִטֵּא row, as the rite of Num 19:18–19).
- **Request forms** (v7, 8, 12, 15, 18) — Hebrew תְּחַטְּאֵנִי, תְּכַבְּסֵנִי (v7), תַּשְׁמִיעֵנִי (v8), תִסְמְכֵנִי (v12), תִּפְתָּח (v15), תִּבְנֶה (v18) are imperfects among imperatives; in a prayer the form serves as a request, and it is rendered so ("purge me", "wash me", "make me hear", "uphold me", "open", "build"). Read as futures they say "you will purge me … you will open my lips": the one form carries both.
- **"Make me to hear joy and gladness; that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice" → "Make me hear joy and gladness; let the bones you have crushed rejoice"** (v8) — Hebrew תָּגֵלְנָה עֲצָמוֹת דִּכִּיתָ, a wish ("let … rejoice"), no "that"; דִּכָּה "crush", the root of נִדְכֶּה in v17 ("crushed", one word in both verses).
- **"Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me" → "A clean heart create for me, Elohim, and a right spirit renew within me"** (v10) — Hebrew לֵב טָהוֹר בְּרָא־לִי … וְרוּחַ נָכוֹן חַדֵּשׁ בְּקִרְבִּי, the objects first; לִי "for me" (not "in me"); בָּרָא "create" (Gen 1:1); נָכוֹן "right, established" (its row).
- **"Cast me not away from thy presence" → "Do not cast me away from before you"** (v11) — Hebrew מִלְּפָנֶיךָ, "from before you" (lit. "from your face").
- **"thy holy spirit" → "your holy spirit"** (v11) — Hebrew רוּחַ קָדְשְׁךָ, "the spirit of your holiness" (קֹדֶשׁ as a genitive "holy", its row); with Isa 63:10, 11 the only places in the OT. The Hebrew has no capital: "Holy Spirit" (as a name) is a reading of the later faith, not in the word.
- **"Restore unto me" → "Bring back to me"** (v12) — Hebrew הָשִׁיבָה לִּי, the hifil of שׁוּב (the הֵשִׁיב row).
- **"uphold me with thy free spirit" → "with a willing spirit uphold me"** (v12) — Hebrew וְרוּחַ נְדִיבָה תִסְמְכֵנִי: no "your" (KJV "thy" is added); נְדִיבָה "willing" (the נָדִיב row). רוּחַ (feminine) may also be the subject: "and let a willing spirit uphold me".
- **"Then will I teach" → "Let me teach"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲלַמְּדָה, cohortative; there is no "then". יָשׁוּבוּ "shall return" (the שׁוּב row).
- **"from bloodguiltiness" → "from bloodguilt"** (v14) — Hebrew מִדָּמִים, "from bloods" (plural; the דָּמִים row).
- **"thou God of my salvation" → "Elohim of my salvation"** (v14) — Hebrew תְּשׁוּעָתִי; v12 has יִשְׁעֶךָ, another noun of the same root: English has one word, "salvation".
- **"and my tongue shall sing aloud of thy righteousness" → "my tongue shall ring out your righteousness"** (v14) — Hebrew תְּרַנֵּן לְשׁוֹנִי צִדְקָתֶךָ, no "and"; רִנֵּן "ring out, cry aloud", with "your righteousness" as its direct object.
- **"O Lord" → "Adonai"** (v15) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"shall shew forth thy praise" → "shall tell your praise"** (v15) — Hebrew יַגִּיד, the הִגִּיד row ("tell").
- **"For thou desirest not sacrifice; else would I give it: thou delightest not in burnt offering" → "For you do not delight in sacrifice, that I should give it; with burnt offering you are not pleased"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי לֹא־תַחְפֹּץ זֶבַח וְאֶתֵּנָה עוֹלָה לֹא תִרְצֶה: וְאֶתֵּנָה cohortative "and I would give" ("it" supplied for English); רָצָה "be pleased with" (its row; not "accept").
- **"a broken and a contrite heart" → "a heart broken and crushed"** (v17) — Hebrew לֵב־נִשְׁבָּר וְנִדְכֶּה, in this order; נִדְכֶּה "crushed", as v8.
- **"Do good in thy good pleasure unto Zion: build thou the walls" → "Do good in your pleasure to Zion; build the walls"** (v18) — Hebrew הֵיטִיבָה בִרְצוֹנְךָ אֶת־צִיּוֹן תִּבְנֶה חוֹמוֹת: הֵיטִיב "do good to" (its row); רָצוֹן "pleasure, what pleases" ("good" not written); בָּנָה "build" (no "up").
- **"the sacrifices of righteousness, with burnt offering and whole burnt offering" → "sacrifices of righteousness, burnt offering and whole offering"** (v19) — Hebrew זִבְחֵי־צֶדֶק עוֹלָה וְכָלִיל: no article, no "with"; כָּלִיל "whole" (its row; "offering" supplied: a whole offering, all of it burnt).
- **"then shall they offer bullocks upon thine altar" → "then bulls shall go up on your altar"** (v19) — Hebrew יַעֲלוּ … פָרִים, qal of עָלָה "go up" with the bulls as subject (the offering "goes up").

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is Heb v1–2: our v1 = Heb v3, v19 = Heb v21.
- **David and Bathsheba** — 2 Sam 11–12.
- **Fourth of the penitential psalms** (by church tradition: 6, 32, 38, 51, 102, 130, 143).
