# Psalm 1

[1] Blessed is the man
who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked,
nor stand in the way of sinners,
nor sit in the seat of scoffers.

[2] But his delight is in the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
on his ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ he meditates day and night.

[3] He is like a tree planted by streams of water,
yielding its fruit in its season;
its leaf does not wither,
and all that he does prospers.

[4] Not so the wicked;
they are like chaff that the wind drives away.

[5] Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment,
nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous.

[6] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ knows the way of the righteous,
but the way of the wicked perishes.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"ungodly" → "wicked"** (v1, 4, 5, 6) — *resha'im*.

### Notes
- **Introductory psalm** — Wisdom theme; the Two Ways.
- **Tree by waters** — Jer 17:8 parallel. Hope in rootedness.
- **Without author-superscription** — editorial introduction to Psalter.
