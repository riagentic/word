# Psalm 17

A prayer of David.

[1] Hear, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, righteousness; give heed to my cry;
give ear to my prayer, without lips of deceit.

[2] From before you let my judgment go out;
let your eyes behold uprightness.

[3] You have tried my heart, you have visited by night,
you have refined me — you do not find;
I have purposed: my mouth shall not overstep.

[4] As for the deeds of man, by the word of your lips
I have kept from the paths of the violent one.

[5] My steps have held fast in your courses;
my footsteps have not slipped.

[6] I have called on you, for you will answer me, ⟦el|El|God⟧;
incline your ear to me, hear my saying.

[7] Set apart your ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|acts of steadfast love⟧, savior of those who take refuge,
from those who rise up, by your right hand.

[8] Keep me as the pupil, the daughter of the eye;
in the shade of your wings you will hide me,

[9] from the face of the wicked who have devastated me;
my enemies against the soul close around me.

[10] Their fat they have shut;
with their mouth they have spoken in pride.

[11] Our steps — now they have surrounded us;
their eyes they set, to bend down to the earth.

[12] His likeness is as a lion that longs to tear to pieces,
and as a young lion sitting in secret places.

[13] Arise, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, come to meet his face, make him bow down;
rescue my soul from the wicked one, your sword,

[14] from men, your hand, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
from men, from the world — their portion is in life;
and with your treasure you fill their belly;
they are sated with sons,
and they leave their remainder to their children.

[15] I — in righteousness I will behold your face;
let me be sated, when I awake, with your form.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "El"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"A Prayer of David"** (heading) — Hebrew תְּפִלָּה לְדָוִד, the first words of v1 in the Hebrew. "Of David" is לְדָוִד: לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; the heading does not say which.
- **"Hear the right … attend unto my cry … that goeth not out of feigned lips" → "Hear, Yahweh, righteousness; give heed to my cry … without lips of deceit"** (v1) — Hebrew צֶדֶק "righteousness" with no article and no "cause" (a just case is meant); הַקְשִׁיבָה "give heed"; רִנָּה "cry" (a ringing cry); בְּלֹא שִׂפְתֵי מִרְמָה, literally "in no lips of deceit".
- **"Let my sentence come forth from thy presence; let thine eyes behold the things that are equal" → "From before you let my judgment go out; let your eyes behold uprightness"** (v2) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness" (plural, "what is level, upright"). Both verbs are imperfect and may also be read as statements ("goes out … behold").
- **"Thou hast proved mine heart; thou hast visited me in the night … and shalt find nothing; I am purposed that my mouth shall not transgress" → "You have tried my heart, you have visited by night … you do not find; I have purposed: my mouth shall not overstep"** (v3) — Hebrew בָּחַן "try" (put to the proof, as metal; נִסָּה is "test", חָקַר "examine"). "Me" after "visited" and "nothing" after "find" are not written. צָרַף "refine" (as metal). זַמֹּתִי is pointed as "I have purposed" (זָמַם "devise" of the word list, here of a good purpose); others join it to the first line ("you find no evil plan in me") or read "my plan does not pass my mouth". עָבַר "pass over, overstep".
- **"I have kept me from the paths of the destroyer" → "I have kept from the paths of the violent one"** (v4) — Hebrew שָׁמַרְתִּי אָרְחוֹת פָּרִיץ, literally "I have kept the paths of the violent one": watched (and so avoided) them; "from" gives that sense, since the literal words would say he walked in them. אֲנִי "I" stands emphatically. פְּעֻלּוֹת אָדָם "the deeds of man".
- **"Hold up my goings in thy paths, that my footsteps slip not" → "My steps have held fast in your courses; my footsteps have not slipped"** (v5) — Hebrew תָּמֹךְ is an infinitive absolute ("holding fast"), read here as the verb of "my steps"; KJV takes it as a command. אֲשֻׁר "step", מַעְגָּל "track", פַּעַם "footstep"; בַּל־נָמוֹטּוּ perfect "have not slipped".
- **"Shew thy marvellous lovingkindness … that savest by thy right hand them which put their trust in thee from those that rise up against them" → "Set apart your *chasadim* (acts of steadfast love), savior of those who take refuge, from those who rise up, by your right hand"** (v7) — Hebrew הַפְלֵה, as pointed from פָּלָה "set apart, make distinct" (Ps 4:3); "show wonderfully" (KJV) reads פָּלָא, which some manuscripts have (from memory). חֲסָדֶיךָ is plural. "In thee" and "against them" are not written; "by your right hand" may go with "savior", with "take refuge" or with "rise up". The line is set as the Hebrew divides it.
- **"the apple of the eye" → "the pupil, the daughter of the eye"** (v8) — Hebrew אִישׁוֹן בַּת־עָיִן, literally "the little man, the daughter of the eye" (as Deut 32:10). **"hide me" → "you will hide me"** — imperfect תַּסְתִּירֵנִי; it may carry the force of a request. צֵל "shade".
- **"From the wicked that oppress me, from my deadly enemies, who compass me about" → "from the face of the wicked who have devastated me; my enemies against the soul close around me"** (v9) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי "from the face of"; שַׁדּוּנִי "they have devastated me"; אֹיְבַי בְּנֶפֶשׁ, literally "my enemies in soul": enemies against the life (KJV "deadly"), or enemies with greed of soul; יַקִּיפוּ עָלָי "they close around upon me" is a new clause, not "who".
- **"They are inclosed in their own fat: with their mouth they speak proudly" → "Their fat they have shut; with their mouth they have spoken in pride"** (v10) — Hebrew חֶלְבָּמוֹ סָגְרוּ, "their fat they have shut": a heart grown fat and closed (KJV "inclosed in their own fat"); "hearts" is not written. גֵּאוּת "pride". Both verbs are perfect.
- **"They have now compassed us in our steps: they have set their eyes bowing down to the earth" → "Our steps — now they have surrounded us; their eyes they set, to bend down to the earth"** (v11) — Hebrew אַשֻּׁרֵינוּ "our steps" stands first, as an object of place; qere סְבָבוּנוּ "they have surrounded us" (ketiv סבבוני "me"). לִנְטוֹת בָּאָרֶץ "to bend (down) to the earth": like the crouching lion of v12, or (with "us" supplied) "to cast us to the ground"; no object is written.
- **"Like as a lion that is greedy of his prey … lurking in secret places" → "His likeness is as a lion that longs to tear to pieces, and as a young lion sitting in secret places"** (v12) — Hebrew דִּמְיֹנוֹ "his likeness" (found only here); כָּסַף "long"; טָרַף "tear to pieces"; יֹשֵׁב "sitting"; מִסְתָּרִים "secret places". The wicked are now spoken of as one ("his").
- **"disappoint him, cast him down: deliver my soul from the wicked, which is thy sword" → "come to meet his face, make him bow down; rescue my soul from the wicked one, your sword"** (v13) — Hebrew קַדְּמָה פָנָיו "come to meet his face" (confront him); הַכְרִיעֵהוּ "make him bow down"; פִּלֵּט "rescue". חַרְבֶּךָ "your sword" stands without a preposition: the wicked one as Yahweh's sword (KJV), or "with your sword"; so "your hand" in v14.
- **"From men which are thy hand … from men of the world, which have their portion in this life, and whose belly thou fillest with thy hid treasure: they are full of children, and leave the rest of their substance to their babes" → "from men, your hand, Yahweh, from men, from the world — their portion is in life; and with your treasure you fill their belly; they are sated with sons, and they leave their remainder to their children"** (v14) — Hebrew מְתִים "men" twice; מֵחֶלֶד "from the world" (חֶלֶד "the span of life, the world"; not "men of"); "this" is not written. Qere וּצְפוּנְךָ "and your treasured (thing)" (ketiv וצפינך "your treasure"), with no preposition: "with" is supplied; some read "your treasured ones" (from memory). בֶּטֶן "belly" (KJV; not "womb"). יִשְׂבְּעוּ בָנִים: "they are sated with sons", or "sons are sated". יִתְרָם "their remainder"; עוֹלָל "child". The verse is hard; the men may be the wicked, or the line may speak of those Yahweh feeds.
- **"As for me, I will behold … I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness" → "I — in righteousness I will behold your face; let me be sated, when I awake, with your form"** (v15) — אֲנִי "I" stands first; אֶשְׂבְּעָה is a cohortative ("let me be sated"); תְּמוּנָה "form" (Num 12:8; Deut 4:12; "likeness" is דְּמוּת).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is the first words of v1 in the Hebrew; the verse numbers are the same.
- **Innocent's appeal** (v1–5) — the speaker's claim of a clean way, before the plea against the enemy (v6–14).
- **"When I awake"** (v15) — the hope to behold the face of Yahweh.
