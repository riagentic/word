# Psalm 17

A Prayer of David.

[1] Hear a just cause, O Yahweh; attend to my cry;
give ear to my prayer from lips that do not deceive.

[2] From your presence let my vindication come;
let your eyes behold what is right.

[3] You have tested my heart, you have visited me by night,
you have tried me, and you find nothing;
I have purposed that my mouth shall not transgress.

[4] As for the works of men, by the word of your lips
I have kept from the paths of the violent.

[5] My steps have held fast to your paths;
my feet have not slipped.

[6] I call upon you, for you will answer me, O *Elohim*;
incline your ear to me; hear my words.

[7] Wondrously show your steadfast love,
O Savior of those who take refuge by your right hand
from those who rise up against them.

[8] Keep me as the apple of your eye;
hide me in the shadow of your wings,

[9] from the wicked who despoil me,
my deadly enemies who surround me.

[10] They close their fat hearts;
with their mouths they speak arrogantly.

[11] They have tracked me down; now they surround me;
they set their eyes to cast me to the ground.

[12] He is like a lion eager to tear,
like a young lion lurking in ambush.

[13] Arise, O Yahweh! Confront him, subdue him!
Deliver my soul from the wicked by your sword,

[14] from men by your hand, O Yahweh,
from men of the world whose portion is in this life.
You fill their womb with what you have stored up for them;
their children shall have more than enough;
they leave abundance to their babes.

[15] As for me, I shall behold your face in righteousness;
when I awake, I shall be satisfied with your likeness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"belly" → "womb"** (v14) — *tzafun*; "stored up," here likely metonymy for womb.
- **"apple of the eye"** (v8) — Hebrew *ishon bat-ayin* ("little man, daughter of the eye"); pupil.

### Notes
- **Innocent's appeal** — oath of integrity.
- **"When I awake, I shall behold your face"** — hope of divine vision.
