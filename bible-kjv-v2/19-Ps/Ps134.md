# Psalm 134

A song of the ascents.

[1] Behold, bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all servants of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who stand in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the nights.

[2] Lift up your hands to the holy place,
and bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ bless you from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
the maker of heavens and earth.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees" → "A song of the ascents."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת (the heading row).
- **"Behold, bless ye the LORD, all ye servants of the LORD, which by night stand in the house of the LORD" → "Behold, bless Yahweh, all servants of Yahweh, who stand in the house of Yahweh in the nights"** (v1) — Hebrew הִנֵּה "behold" ("Come" replaced it). עַבְדֵי יְהוָה "servants of Yahweh" (the עֶבֶד row: the word is "slave"; of Yahweh's men "servant"). הָעֹמְדִים "who stand", a participle; בַּלֵּילוֹת "in the nights", plural, in the Hebrew order at the end.
- **"Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the LORD" → "Lift up your hands to the holy place, and bless Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׂאוּ־יְדֵכֶם קֹדֶשׁ: קֹדֶשׁ stands with no preposition, "(toward) the holy place" or "(in) holiness"; both readings are possible, the first is given (the word is not מִקְדָּשׁ "sanctuary"). וּבָרְכוּ "and bless".
- **"The LORD that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion" → "May Yahweh bless you from Zion, the maker of heavens and earth"** (v3) — Hebrew יְבָרֶכְךָ, a jussive, "may he bless you" (you singular: each one addressed, or the people as one); עֹשֵׂה שָׁמַיִם וָאָרֶץ "maker of heavens and earth", a participle, without the article (שָׁמַיִם plural, its row); in the Hebrew order after "from Zion".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading stands in Hebrew v1 with the first words of the psalm: the numbers do not shift.
- **The last song of the ascents** (Ps 120–134): a call to those who stand in the house at night (v1–2), and a blessing from Zion in answer (v3).
