# Psalm 134

A Song of Ascents.

[1] Come, bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all you servants of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
who stand by night in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!

[2] Lift up your hands to the sanctuary
and bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!

[3] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ bless you from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
he who made heaven and earth!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — closing of Songs of Ascents; call to night-watchers to bless (v1-2); blessing returned from Zion (v3).
