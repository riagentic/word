# Psalm 106

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for he is good,
for his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever.

[2] Who can utter the mighty deeds of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
make heard all his praise?

[3] Happy are those who keep judgment,
he who does righteousness at every time.

[4] Remember me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, with the good pleasure toward your people;
visit me with your salvation,

[5] to look upon the good of your chosen ones,
to rejoice in the gladness of your nation,
to glory with your inheritance.

[6] We have sinned with our fathers;
we have done iniquity, we have done wickedly.

[7] Our fathers in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧
did not have insight into your wonderful deeds;
they did not remember the abundance of your ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|steadfast love⟧,
and they rebelled at the sea, at the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧.

[8] And he saved them on account of his name,
to make known his might.

[9] And he rebuked the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧, and it dried up,
and he made them walk through the deeps as through the wilderness.

[10] And he saved them from the hand of the hater,
and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.

[11] And the waters covered their adversaries;
not one of them remained.

[12] And they believed in his words;
they sang his praise.

[13] Quickly they forgot his works;
they did not wait for his counsel.

[14] And they craved a craving in the wilderness,
and they tested ⟦el|El|God⟧ in the desert.

[15] And he gave them their request,
and sent leanness into their soul.

[16] And they were jealous of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ in the camp,
of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, the holy one of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] The earth opened and swallowed ⟦dathan|Datan|Dathan⟧,
and covered over the congregation of ⟦abiram|Aviram|Abiram⟧.

[18] And a fire burned in their congregation;
a flame set the wicked aflame.

[19] They made a calf at ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧
and bowed down to a molten image.

[20] And they exchanged their glory
for the pattern of an ox that eats plants.

[21] They forgot ⟦el|El|God⟧ their savior,
who did great things in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,

[22] wonderful deeds in the land of ⟦ham|Cham|Ham⟧,
fearsome deeds at the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧.

[23] And he said he would destroy them —
had not ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ his chosen one
stood in the breach before him,
to turn back his fury from ruining.

[24] And they rejected the desirable land;
they did not believe his word.

[25] And they murmured in their tents;
they did not listen to the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[26] And he lifted up his hand to them,
to make them fall in the wilderness,

[27] and to make their seed fall among the nations,
and to strew them in the lands.

[28] And they yoked themselves to ⟦baal-peor|Baal Peor|Baal of Peor⟧,
and ate the sacrifices of the dead.

[29] And they caused vexation with their doings,
and a plague broke out among them.

[30] And ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧ stood and intervened,
and the plague was restrained.

[31] And it was reckoned to him for righteousness,
to generation and generation, forever.

[32] And they caused wrath at the waters of ⟦meribah|Merivah|Meribah⟧,
and it went evil for ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ for their sake;

[33] for they rebelled against his spirit,
and he uttered rashly with his lips.

[34] They did not destroy the peoples,
as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had said to them,

[35] and they mixed themselves among the nations
and learned their works.

[36] And they served their idols,
and they became a snare to them.

[37] And they sacrificed their sons
and their daughters to the demons.

[38] And they shed innocent blood,
the blood of their sons and their daughters,
whom they sacrificed to the idols of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧;
and the land was polluted with bloodshed.

[39] And they became unclean through their works,
and played the prostitute in their doings.

[40] And the anger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ burned against his people,
and he abominated his inheritance.

[41] And he gave them into the hand of nations,
and those who hated them ruled over them.

[42] And their enemies oppressed them,
and they were brought low under their hand.

[43] Many times he would deliver them,
but they rebelled in their counsel,
and they sank low in their iniquity.

[44] And he looked upon their distress,
when he heard their cry.

[45] And he remembered for them his covenant,
and relented according to the abundance of his ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|steadfast love⟧.

[46] And he gave them for compassion
before all their captors.

[47] Save us, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and gather us from the nations,
to give thanks to your holy name,
to boast in your praise.

[48] Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
from the age and to the age.
And all the people shall say, "Amen."
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 16, 25, 34, 40, 47, 48) — Tetragrammaton. In v29 and v32 the Hebrew has no divine name and no object (see there).
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v47, 48) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"El"** (v14, 21) — Hebrew אֵל.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Hallelujah"** (v1, 48) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ, literally "praise Yah" (in v1 written as two words). v48 closes Book IV of the Psalter.
- **"O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever" → "Give thanks to Yahweh, for he is good, for his chesed is forever"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹדוּ לַיהוָה כִּי־טוֹב כִּי לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ, the same words as 1 Chr 16:34; no verb in the last clause ("is", not "endures"); חֶסֶד carries its token.
- **"Who can utter … who can shew forth all his praise?" → "Who can utter … make heard all his praise?"** (v2) — Hebrew מִי יְמַלֵּל … יַשְׁמִיעַ, two imperfects ("can" for the question), the second without "who" or "or"; הִשְׁמִיעַ "make heard". גְּבוּרוֹת "mighty deeds" ("deeds" is supplied: the plural of "might"); v8 has the singular, "his might".
- **"Blessed are they that keep judgment, and he that doeth righteousness" → "Happy are those who keep judgment, he who does righteousness"** (v3) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "happy" (never "blessed": that is בָּרוּךְ); שֹׁמְרֵי plural, עֹשֵׂה singular, as printed; בְכָל־עֵת "at every time".
- **"with the favour that thou bearest unto thy people: O visit me with thy salvation" → "with the good pleasure toward your people; visit me with your salvation"** (v4) — Hebrew בִּרְצוֹן עַמֶּךָ, literally "in the good pleasure of your people": the favor Yahweh has toward them (רָצוֹן outside the cult, as Deut 33:16). פָּקַד "visit"; no "when you" in either line.
- **"That I may see the good of thy chosen" → "to look upon the good of your chosen ones"** (v5) — Hebrew three infinitives, לִרְאוֹת בְּ … לִשְׂמֹחַ … לְהִתְהַלֵּל; "that I may" is not written. טוֹבָה "good"; הִתְהַלֵּל "glory" (the reflexive of "praise").
- **"We have sinned with our fathers, we have committed iniquity, we have done wickedly"** (v6) — Hebrew חָטָאנוּ עִם־אֲבוֹתֵינוּ הֶעֱוִינוּ הִרְשָׁעְנוּ: "with" our fathers ("both we and" was not written); the three verbs as 1 Kgs 8:47 and 2 Chr 6:37, where the third is the simple stem; here the causative הִרְשָׁעְנוּ.
- **"understood not thy wonders" → "did not have insight into your wonderful deeds"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא־הִשְׂכִּילוּ נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָ; הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight"; נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds" (also v22). **"the multitude of thy mercies" → "the abundance of your chesed"** — Hebrew רֹב חֲסָדֶיךָ, the plural *chasadim* (token as Ps 25:6). **"provoked him at the sea" → "rebelled at the sea"** — Hebrew וַיַּמְרוּ עַל־יָם, the hifil of מָרָה "rebel against" (also v33, 43); "him" is not written.
- **"for his name's sake" → "on account of his name"** (v8) — Hebrew לְמַעַן שְׁמוֹ, as Ps 23:3.
- **"he led them through the depths, as through the wilderness" → "he made them walk through the deeps as through the wilderness"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיּוֹלִיכֵם בַּתְּהֹמוֹת כַּמִּדְבָּר; הוֹלִיךְ "make walk" (Deut 8:2); תְּהֹמוֹת "deeps" (Exod 15:5). גָּעַר "rebuke"; חָרֵב "dry up".
- **"from the hand of him that hated them … from the hand of the enemy"** (v10) — Hebrew מִיַּד שׂוֹנֵא … מִיַּד אוֹיֵב: "hand" both times, "them" not written after "hater"; גָּאַל "redeem".
- **"there was not one of them left" → "not one of them remained"** (v11) — Hebrew לֹא נוֹתָר (Exod 14:28 has another verb, נִשְׁאַר "was left").
- **"Then believed they his words" → "And they believed in his words"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיַּאֲמִינוּ בִדְבָרָיו, הֶאֱמִין בְּ "believe in". יָשִׁירוּ is an imperfect, told of the past.
- **"They soon forgat his works" → "Quickly they forgot his works"** (v13) — Hebrew מִהֲרוּ שָׁכְחוּ, literally "they hastened, they forgot" (מִהַר "hasten"). חִכָּה לְ "wait for"; עֵצָה "counsel".
- **"lusted exceedingly … tempted God" → "craved a craving … tested El"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיִּתְאַוּוּ תַאֲוָה, as Num 11:4; נִסָּה "test" (not "tempt"). יְשִׁימוֹן "desert" beside מִדְבָּר "wilderness".
- **"their request" kept; "leanness into their soul"** (v15) — Hebrew שְׁאֵלָה "request"; רָזוֹן "leanness" (wasting; KJV "leanness"), בְּנַפְשָׁם "into their soul" (נֶפֶשׁ, also "their life / their throat").
- **"They envied Moses also in the camp" → "And they were jealous of Moses in the camp"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיְקַנְאוּ לְמֹשֶׁה, קִנֵּא "be jealous" (here of a rival: "envy"); "men" and "also" are not written. **"the saint of the LORD" → "the holy one of Yahweh"** — Hebrew קְדוֹשׁ יְהוָה.
- **"The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered the company of Abiram" → "… and covered over the congregation of Abiram"** (v17) — Hebrew תִּפְתַּח, an imperfect, told of the past; וַתְּכַס עַל "covered over" (Num 16:33); עֵדָה "congregation" (also v18), as Num 16.
- **"A fire was kindled … the flame burned up the wicked" → "a fire burned … a flame set the wicked aflame"** (v18) — Hebrew בָּעַר "burn"; לֶהָבָה תְּלַהֵט רְשָׁעִים, לִהֵט "set aflame" (Deut 32:22); רְשָׁעִים "the wicked".
- **"the molten image" → "a molten image"** (v19) — Hebrew לְמַסֵּכָה, no article; מַסֵּכָה "molten image", the word of Exod 32:4, 8 ("molten calf"). הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down".
- **"Thus they changed their glory into the similitude of an ox that eateth grass" → "And they exchanged their glory for the pattern of an ox that eats plants"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיָּמִירוּ אֶת־כְּבוֹדָם בְּתַבְנִית שׁוֹר אֹכֵל עֵשֶׂב; הֵמִיר "exchange"; תַּבְנִית "pattern" (a thing built after a shape, as Deut 4:17); שׁוֹר "ox"; עֵשֶׂב "plant" (not "grass"). "Their glory" is their Elohim (Jer 2:11); a scribal tradition says the text was once "my glory" or "his glory" (from memory).
- **"God their saviour, which had done great things" → "El their savior, who did great things"** (v21) — Hebrew מוֹשִׁיעַ "savior"; עֹשֶׂה, a participle.
- **"terrible things by the Red sea" → "fearsome deeds at the Sea of Reeds"** (v22) — Hebrew נוֹרָאוֹת "fearsome (deeds)": "deeds" is supplied; יַם־סוּף "Sea of Reeds" (also v7, 9).
- **"Therefore he said that he would destroy them" → "And he said he would destroy them"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר לְהַשְׁמִידָם, literally "and he said, to destroy them"; no "therefore". **"had not Moses his chosen stood before him in the breach"** — Hebrew לוּלֵי … עָמַד בַּפֶּרֶץ לְפָנָיו. **"to turn away his wrath, lest he should destroy them" → "to turn back his fury from ruining"** — Hebrew לְהָשִׁיב חֲמָתוֹ מֵהַשְׁחִית, as Num 25:11 "turned back my fury"; חֵמָה "fury"; הִשְׁחִית "ruin", another verb than הִשְׁמִיד "destroy"; "them" is not written.
- **"they despised the pleasant land, they believed not his word" → "they rejected the desirable land; they did not believe his word"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיִּמְאֲסוּ בְּאֶרֶץ חֶמְדָּה, מָאַס "reject" (Num 14:31); אֶרֶץ חֶמְדָּה "a land of desire", without the article; לִדְבָרוֹ "his word" (not "promise").
- **"murmured … hearkened not unto the voice of the LORD" → "murmured … did not listen to the voice of Yahweh"** (v25) — Hebrew נִרְגַּן "murmur", as Deut 1:27 "murmured in your tents"; שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of".
- **"he lifted up his hand against them, to overthrow them" → "he lifted up his hand to them, to make them fall"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיִּשָּׂא יָדוֹ לָהֶם, the lifted hand of an oath (Num 14:30): he swore it to them; הִפִּיל "make fall" (also v27).
- **"To overthrow their seed also among the nations, and to scatter them" → "and to make their seed fall among the nations, and to strew them"** (v27) — Hebrew וּלְהַפִּיל זַרְעָם … וּלְזָרוֹתָם; זָרָה "strew" (Exod 32:20). Many read לְהָפִיץ "to scatter" for the first verb, as Ezek 20:23 (a conjecture; from memory): the MT stands.
- **"They joined themselves also unto Baal-peor, and ate the sacrifices of the dead" → "And they yoked themselves to Baal Peor, and ate the sacrifices of the dead"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיִּצָּמְדוּ, as Num 25:3, 5; זִבְחֵי מֵתִים "sacrifices of the dead" ("offered to" is not written).
- **"Thus they provoked him to anger with their inventions" → "And they caused vexation with their doings"** (v29) — Hebrew וַיַּכְעִיסוּ בְּמַעַלְלֵיהֶם: הִכְעִיס "vex", with no object written (the one vexed is Yahweh); מַעֲלָלִים "doings" (also v39; as Ps 78:7). **"the plague brake in upon them" → "a plague broke out among them"** — Hebrew וַתִּפְרָץ־בָּם מַגֵּפָה, no article; בָּם "among / against them".
- **"Then stood up Phinehas, and executed judgment: and so the plague was stayed" → "And Phinehas stood and intervened, and the plague was restrained"** (v30) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֹד … וַיְפַלֵּל; פִּלֵּל is the verb of 1 Sam 2:25 "arbitrate": to step in between and decide (here what Num 25:7–8 tells: "executed judgment" in KJV). וַתֵּעָצַר הַמַּגֵּפָה "the plague was restrained", as Num 25:8.
- **"And that was counted unto him for righteousness unto all generations for evermore" → "And it was reckoned to him for righteousness, to generation and generation, forever"** (v31) — Hebrew וַתֵּחָשֶׁב לוֹ לִצְדָקָה (חָשַׁב "reckon", as Gen 15:6; here with לְ "for"); לְדֹר וָדֹר "to generation and generation"; עַד־עוֹלָם "forever".
- **"They angered him also" → "And they caused wrath"** (v32) — Hebrew וַיַּקְצִיפוּ, the causative of קָצַף "be wrathful", with no object written. **"so that it went ill with Moses for their sakes" → "and it went evil for Moses for their sake"** — Hebrew וַיֵּרַע לְמֹשֶׁה בַּעֲבוּרָם; רַע "evil"; בַּעֲבוּר "for the sake of".
- **"Because they provoked his spirit, so that he spake unadvisedly with his lips" → "for they rebelled against his spirit, and he uttered rashly with his lips"** (v33) — Hebrew כִּי־הִמְרוּ אֶת־רוּחוֹ וַיְבַטֵּא בִּשְׂפָתָיו; הִמְרָה "rebel against" (as v7); "his spirit" may be Moses' or the spirit of Elohim (Isa 63:10 "they rebelled and grieved his holy spirit"): the Hebrew does not say. בִּטֵּא "utter rashly" (Lev 5:4; Num 30:6).
- **"concerning whom the LORD commanded them" → "as Yahweh had said to them"** (v34) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר אָמַר יְהוָה לָהֶם: אָמַר "say" (not "command"); אֲשֶׁר "which / as": "the peoples of whom Yahweh had spoken to them".
- **"But were mingled among the heathen, and learned their works" → "and they mixed themselves among the nations and learned their works"** (v35) — Hebrew הִתְעָרֵב "mix oneself"; גּוֹיִם "nations"; מַעֲשִׂים "works" (also v13, 39).
- **"which were a snare unto them" → "and they became a snare to them"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ לָהֶם לְמוֹקֵשׁ; עֲצַבִּים "idols"; מוֹקֵשׁ "snare".
- **"unto devils" → "to the demons"** (v37) — Hebrew לַשֵּׁדִים, as Deut 32:17 (the sense "demons" is the best supported, not certain).
- **"and the land was polluted with blood" → "with bloodshed"** (v38) — Hebrew וַתֶּחֱנַף הָאָרֶץ בַּדָּמִים: חָנַף "be polluted" (Num 35:33 "pollute the land"); דָּמִים, the plural "bloods", is blood shed. שָׁפַךְ "shed"; נָקִי "innocent"; "their daughters" has its own "their".
- **"Thus were they defiled with their own works, and went a whoring with their own inventions" → "And they became unclean through their works, and played the prostitute in their doings"** (v39) — Hebrew וַיִּטְמְאוּ "became unclean" (טָמֵא qal); זָנָה "play the prostitute"; "own" is not written.
- **"was the wrath of the LORD kindled against his people, insomuch that he abhorred his own inheritance" → "the anger of Yahweh burned against his people, and he abominated his inheritance"** (v40) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף יְהוָה בְּעַמּוֹ, אַף "anger" (literally "nose"), as the frame sentences of Judges (Judg 2:14, 20); תִּעֵב "abominate".
- **"into the hand of the heathen" → "into the hand of nations"** (v41) — Hebrew בְּיַד־גּוֹיִם, no article; as Judg 2:14 "gave them into the hand of".
- **"they were brought into subjection under their hand" → "they were brought low under their hand"** (v42) — Hebrew וַיִּכָּנְעוּ תַּחַת יָדָם, נִכְנַע "be brought low" (Judg 3:30; 8:28); לָחַץ "oppress".
- **"Many times did he deliver them; but they provoked him with their counsel, and were brought low for their iniquity" → "Many times he would deliver them, but they rebelled in their counsel, and they sank low in their iniquity"** (v43) — Hebrew יַצִּילֵם, an imperfect of what happened again and again; הִצִּיל "deliver"; וְהֵמָּה יַמְרוּ בַעֲצָתָם, "they" stressed, מָרָה "rebel", no "him"; מָכַךְ "sink low" (only here, Job 24:24 and Eccl 10:18), another verb than נִכְנַע (v42); בַּעֲוֺנָם "in their iniquity".
- **"Nevertheless he regarded their affliction" → "And he looked upon their distress"** (v44) — Hebrew וַיַּרְא בַּצַּר לָהֶם, literally "and he looked upon the distress to them"; צַר "distress"; רִנָּה "cry" (as Ps 61:1).
- **"And he remembered for them his covenant, and repented according to the multitude of his mercies" → "… and relented according to the abundance of his chasadim"** (v45) — Hebrew וַיִּנָּחֵם, נִחַם "relent" (a planned act given up; not "repent"). כְּרֹב חֲסָדָיו is the qere, plural (token as v7); the ketiv חסדו is the singular, "his chesed".
- **"He made them also to be pitied of all those that carried them captives" → "And he gave them for compassion before all their captors"** (v46) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן אוֹתָם לְרַחֲמִים לִפְנֵי כָּל־שׁוֹבֵיהֶם, word for word as 1 Kgs 8:50 "give them for compassion before their captors".
- **"Save us, O LORD our God … to give thanks unto thy holy name, and to triumph in thy praise" → "Save us, Yahweh our Elohim … to give thanks to your holy name, to boast in your praise"** (v47) — Hebrew הוֹשִׁיעֵנוּ יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵינוּ וְקַבְּצֵנוּ מִן־הַגּוֹיִם לְהֹדוֹת לְשֵׁם קָדְשֶׁךָ לְהִשְׁתַּבֵּחַ בִּתְהִלָּתֶךָ. 1 Chr 16:35 has "And say," "Elohim of our salvation" (here "Yahweh our Elohim") and "and deliver us" (not here). הִשְׁתַּבֵּחַ "boast", the reflexive of "laud"; no "and" before it.
- **"Blessed be the LORD God of Israel from everlasting to everlasting: and let all the people say, Amen" → "Blessed is Yahweh the Elohim of Israel, from the age and to the age. And all the people shall say, 'Amen'"** (v48) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ־יְהוָה, no verb ("is", as 1 Chr 16:36); מִן־הָעוֹלָם וְעַד הָעוֹלָם, with the article and וְ, "from the age and to the age" (traditional "from everlasting to everlasting"). וְאָמַר is a perfect with "and", pointing ahead: "shall say" (also read as a wish, "let … say"); 1 Chr 16:36 has "and all the people said", with וְהַלֵּל לַיהוָה where this verse has "Hallelujah".

### Notes
- **1 Chr 16:34–36** — v1, 47–48 stand also at the end of David's song in 1 Chr 16. The Hebrew differs in v47 (see there) and v48 ("shall say" / "said"; "Hallelujah" / "and praised Yahweh"); each verse follows its own text.
- **Ps 105 and 106** — Ps 105 tells Yahweh's deeds; Ps 106 the fathers' sins against them, from Egypt (v7–12) through the wilderness (v13–33: Num 11; 16; Exod 32; Num 14; 25; 20) into the land (v34–46, as the frame of Judges).
- **Close of Book IV of the Psalter** (Ps 90–106); v48 is the doxology that ends the book.
