# Psalm 111

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
I will give thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with my whole heart,
in the company of the upright, in the congregation.

[2] Great are the works of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
studied by all who delight in them.

[3] Full of splendor and majesty is his work,
and his righteousness endures forever.

[4] He has caused his wondrous works to be remembered;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is gracious and merciful.

[5] He provides food for those who fear him;
he remembers his covenant forever.

[6] He has shown his people the power of his works,
in giving them the inheritance of the nations.

[7] The works of his hands are faithful and just;
all his precepts are trustworthy;

[8] they are established forever and ever,
to be performed with faithfulness and uprightness.

[9] He sent redemption to his people;
he has commanded his covenant forever.
Holy and awesome is his name!

[10] The fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the beginning of wisdom;
all those who practice it have a good understanding.
His praise endures forever!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 10) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — acrostic hymn of Yahweh's works and covenant; closes with wisdom-saying (v10).

### Notes
- **Acrostic** — each half-verse begins with successive Hebrew letters.
- **"Fear of Yahweh" = beginning of wisdom** — shared with Proverbs.
