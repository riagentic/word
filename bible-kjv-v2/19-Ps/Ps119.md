# Psalm 119

## א Aleph

[1] Happy are the blameless in the way,
who walk in the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[2] Happy are those who guard his testimonies;
with the whole heart they seek him.

[3] Also they have not done injustice;
in his ways they have walked.

[4] You have commanded your precepts,
to keep them greatly.

[5] Oh that my ways were established
to keep your statutes!

[6] Then I shall not be ashamed,
when I look to all your commandments.

[7] I will give thanks to you with uprightness of heart,
when I learn the judgments of your righteousness.

[8] Your statutes I will keep;
do not forsake me utterly.

## ב Beth

[9] By what shall a young man make his path pure?
By keeping according to your word.

[10] With my whole heart I have sought you;
do not make me err from your commandments.

[11] In my heart I have hidden away your saying,
so that I may not sin against you.

[12] Blessed are you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
teach me your statutes.

[13] With my lips I have recounted
all the judgments of your mouth.

[14] In the way of your testimonies I have exulted
as over all wealth.

[15] On your precepts I will muse,
and I will look to your paths.

[16] In your statutes I will delight myself;
I will not forget your word.

## ג Gimel

[17] Deal with your servant, that I may live,
and I will keep your word.

[18] Uncover my eyes, and I will look upon
wonderful things out of your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[19] A sojourner am I in the land;
do not hide your commandments from me.

[20] My soul is crushed with longing
for your judgments at every time.

[21] You have rebuked the presumptuous, the cursed,
who err from your commandments.

[22] Roll away from me reproach and contempt,
for your testimonies I have guarded.

[23] Even princes have sat; against me they have spoken together;
your servant muses on your statutes.

[24] Also your testimonies are my delight,
the men of my counsel.

## ד Daleth

[25] My soul has clung to the dust;
make me live according to your word.

[26] My ways I have recounted, and you answered me;
teach me your statutes.

[27] The way of your precepts make me understand,
and I will muse on your wonderful deeds.

[28] My soul drips away for grief;
make me stand according to your word.

[29] The way of falsehood remove from me,
and with your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ favor me.

[30] The way of faithfulness I have chosen;
your judgments I have set.

[31] I have clung to your testimonies;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, do not put me to shame.

[32] The way of your commandments I will run,
for you will enlarge my heart.

## ה He

[33] Teach me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, the way of your statutes,
and I will guard it to the end.

[34] Make me understand, and I will guard your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧,
and I will keep it with the whole heart.

[35] Make me tread in the track of your commandments,
for in it I delight.

[36] Turn my heart to your testimonies,
and not to unjust gain.

[37] Make my eyes pass on from seeing emptiness;
in your ways make me live.

[38] Make your saying stand for your servant,
which is for the fear of you.

[39] Make my reproach pass away, which I am afraid of,
for your judgments are good.

[40] Behold, I have longed for your precepts;
in your righteousness make me live.

## ו Waw

[41] And let your ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|steadfast love⟧ come to me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
your salvation according to your saying,

[42] and I will answer him who reproaches me a word,
for I have trusted in your word.

[43] And do not snatch from my mouth a word of truth utterly,
for I have hoped for your judgment.

[44] And I will keep your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ continually,
forever and ever.

[45] And I will walk about in a wide place,
for your precepts I have sought.

[46] And I will speak of your testimonies before kings,
and I shall not be ashamed.

[47] And I will delight myself in your commandments,
which I love.

[48] And I will lift up my palms to your commandments, which I love,
and I will muse on your statutes.

## ז Zayin

[50] This is my comfort in my affliction,
that your saying has made me live.

[51] The presumptuous have mocked me utterly;
from your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ I have not turned.

[52] I have remembered your judgments from of old, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,

[53] Scorching has seized me because of the wicked,
who forsake your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[54] Songs to me have your statutes been
in the house of my sojournings.

[55] I have remembered in the night your name, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and I have kept your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[56] This has been mine,
that your precepts I have guarded.

## ח Heth

[57] My portion is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; I have said,
to keep your words.

[58] I have besought your face with the whole heart;
show me favor according to your saying.

[59] I have thought on my ways,
and I turned back my feet to your testimonies.

[60] I have hastened and I have not delayed
to keep your commandments.

[61] The cords of the wicked have surrounded me;
your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ I have not forgotten.

[62] At the middle of the night I rise to give thanks to you
because of the judgments of your righteousness.

[63] A companion am I to all who fear you,
and to those who keep your precepts.

[64] Of your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, the earth is full;

## ט Teth

[65] Good you have done with your servant,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, according to your word.

[66] Goodness of discernment and knowledge teach me,
for in your commandments I have believed.

[67] Before I was afflicted I was erring,
and now your saying I have kept.

[68] Good are you and doing good;
teach me your statutes.

[69] The presumptuous have smeared falsehood over me;

[70] Their heart is gross like fat;
I — your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ I have delighted in.

[71] It is good for me that I have been afflicted,

[72] Better to me is the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of your mouth

[82] My eyes are worn out for your saying,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 12, 31, 33, 41, 52, 55, 57, 64, 65, 75, 89, 107, 108, 126, 137, 145, 149, 151, 156, 159, 166, 169, 174) — Tetragrammaton, 24 times. **"O LORD" → "Yahweh"** — the Hebrew has no word for "O".
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v115) — Hebrew אֱלֹהָי "my Elohim", the only אֱלֹהִים in the psalm.
- **The line above the first stanza removed** ("Longest psalm — 176 verses …") — it is not Hebrew text (the psalm has no heading); its content is in the Notes. **Letter headings** (Aleph–Taw) kept as the file has them: they name the letter each stanza begins with and are not words of the Hebrew text.
- **The eight words, one rendering each through the psalm** — תּוֹרָה the `torah` token; עֵדוֹת / עֵדְוֹת "testimonies" (singular עֵדוּת v88 "testimony"); פִּקּוּדִים "precepts"; חֻקִּים "statutes" (v16 has the feminine חֻקּוֹת, the same word); מִצְוֹת "commandments" (singular v96, 98); מִשְׁפָּטִים "judgments" (the earlier text had "rules"; the singular stands in v43, 84, 121, 132, 149, 160, 175); דָּבָר "word" (plural "words"); אִמְרָה "saying" (the earlier text had "promise" and "word": the noun of אָמַר "say" says nothing of a promise, and "word" is דָּבָר, which stands beside it).
- **"your servant"** (v17, 23, 38, 49, 65, 76, 84, 122, 124, 125, 135, 140, 176) — Hebrew עַבְדְּךָ, the word is "slave"; of the speaker before Yahweh "servant", as the row has it (v91 "your servants" of all things, the same word).
- **"quicken me" → "make me live"** (v25, 37, 40, 88, 107, 149, 154, 156, 159; "has / you have made me live" v50, 93) — Hebrew חַיֵּנִי, the piel of חָיָה "live": "make live" (as Ps 71:20; 85:6; the earlier text had "give me life").
- **"meditate" → "muse"** (v15, 23, 27, 48, 78, 148; noun "musing" v97, 99) — Hebrew שִׂיחַ, שִׂיחָה (הָגָה "softly recite" does not stand in this psalm).
- **"Blessed" → "Happy"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי (בָּרוּךְ "blessed" is v12).
- **"delight" kept for two roots** — שַׁעֲשֻׁעִים "delight" (a plural noun: v24, 77, 92, 143, 174) and its verb "delight oneself / delight in" (v16, 47, 70); חָפֵץ "delight in" (v35) is another verb: English has one word.
- **"keep" and "guard"** — שָׁמַר "keep" (v4, 5, 8, 9, 17, 34, 44, 55, 57, 60, 63, 67, 88, 101, 106, 134, 136, 146, 158, 167, 168); נָצַר "guard" (v2, 22, 33, 34, 56, 69, 100, 115, 129, 145): the earlier text had "keep" for both.
- **"utterly"** (v8, 43, 51, 107) — Hebrew עַד־מְאֹד; מְאֹד alone "greatly" / "very" (v4, 96, 138, 140, 167).
- **"hope"** — יִחֵל "hope" (v43, 49, 74, 81, 114, 147); שִׂבֵּר (v166) and the noun שֵׂבֶר (v116) are another root with the same sense: English has one word.
- **"Blessed are the undefiled in the way" → "Happy are the blameless in the way"** (v1) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי תְמִימֵי־דָרֶךְ, literally "the blameless of way"; תָּמִים "blameless".
- **"that seek him with the whole heart" → "with the whole heart they seek him"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּכָל־לֵב יִדְרְשׁוּהוּ, a finite verb, not a second participle.
- **"They also do no iniquity: they walk in his ways" → "Also they have not done injustice; in his ways they have walked"** (v3) — Hebrew אַף לֹא־פָעֲלוּ עַוְלָה … הָלָכוּ, two perfects; עַוְלָה "injustice" (as עָוֶל).
- **"Thou hast commanded us to keep thy precepts diligently" → "You have commanded your precepts, to keep them greatly"** (v4) — Hebrew אַתָּה צִוִּיתָה פִקֻּדֶיךָ לִשְׁמֹר מְאֹד: the pronoun אַתָּה is written out (stress on "you"); no "us"; "them" is supplied for English; מְאֹד "greatly".
- **"O that my ways were directed" → "Oh that my ways were established"** (v5) — Hebrew אַחֲלַי יִכֹּנוּ, nifal of כּוּן "be established".
- **"Then shall I not be ashamed, when I have respect unto" → "Then I shall not be ashamed, when I look to"** (v6) — Hebrew לֹא־אֵבוֹשׁ (בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed"; so v46, 78, 80); בְּהַבִּיטִי אֶל, הִבִּיט "look to" (so v15, 18).
- **"I will praise thee with uprightness of heart … thy righteous judgments" → "I will give thanks to you with uprightness of heart … the judgments of your righteousness"** (v7) — Hebrew אוֹדְךָ, from הוֹדָה "give thanks" (so v62); מִשְׁפְּטֵי צִדְקֶךָ, a genitive (so v62, 106, 160, 164).
- **"O forsake me not utterly" → "do not forsake me utterly"** (v8) — Hebrew אַל־תַּעַזְבֵנִי עַד־מְאֹד.
- **"Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word" → "By what shall a young man make his path pure? By keeping according to your word"** (v9) — Hebrew בַּמֶּה יְזַכֶּה־נַּעַר אֶת־אָרְחוֹ לִשְׁמֹר כִּדְבָרֶךָ: זִכָּה "make pure"; אֹרַח "path" (דֶּרֶךְ "way" is another word); לִשְׁמֹר, the infinitive "to keep", has no object. נַעַר "boy", here "young man".
- **"With my whole heart have I sought thee" kept** (v10) — Hebrew דְרַשְׁתִּיךָ, a perfect. **"O let me not wander" → "do not make me err"** — Hebrew אַל־תַּשְׁגֵּנִי, hifil of שָׁגָה "err" (so v21, 118; v67 שֹׁגֵג); "wander" is תָּעָה (v110, 176).
- **"Thy word have I hid in mine heart" → "In my heart I have hidden away your saying"** (v11) — Hebrew צָפַנְתִּי אִמְרָתֶךָ; צָפַן "hide away". **"that I might not sin" → "so that I may not sin"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן לֹא אֶחֱטָא (so v71, 80, 101).
- **"Blessed art thou, O LORD" → "Blessed are you, Yahweh"** (v12) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְהוָה.
- **"have I declared" → "I have recounted"** (v13) — Hebrew סִפַּרְתִּי, from סִפֵּר "recount" (so v26).
- **"I have rejoiced … as much as in all riches" → "I have exulted as over all wealth"** (v14) — Hebrew שַׂשְׂתִּי כְּעַל כָּל־הוֹן, from שׂוּשׂ "exult" (so v162; שָׂמַח "be glad" is v74); כְּעַל "as over".
- **"have respect unto thy ways" → "I will look to your paths"** (v15) — Hebrew וְאַבִּיטָה אֹרְחֹתֶיךָ, אֹרַח "path".
- **"I will delight myself in thy statutes" kept** (v16) — Hebrew בְּחֻקֹּתֶיךָ אֶשְׁתַּעֲשָׁע, the reflexive stem of the root of שַׁעֲשֻׁעִים "delight"; חֻקּוֹת, the feminine of חֻקִּים, "statutes" as everywhere.
- **"Deal bountifully with thy servant, that I may live, and keep thy word" → "Deal with your servant, that I may live, and I will keep your word"** (v17) — Hebrew גְּמֹל עַל־עַבְדְּךָ אֶחְיֶה וְאֶשְׁמְרָה: גָּמַל "deal" has no "bountifully" (the good is understood, not written); אֶחְיֶה may also be "I shall live"; וְאֶשְׁמְרָה is a resolve, "and I will keep".
- **"Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold" → "Uncover my eyes, and I will look upon"** (v18) — Hebrew גַּל־עֵינַי וְאַבִּיטָה, from גָּלָה "uncover" and הִבִּיט. **"wondrous things" → "wonderful things"** — Hebrew נִפְלָאוֹת, the "wonderful deeds" of the row; here no deeds are meant ("things" supplied); v27 "wonderful deeds".
- **"I am a stranger in the earth" → "A sojourner am I in the land"** (v19) — Hebrew גֵּר אָנֹכִי בָאָרֶץ: גֵּר, from גּוּר "sojourn"; אֶרֶץ "land" or "earth" (so v64, 87, 90, 119).
- **"My soul breaketh for the longing that it hath unto thy judgments at all times" → "My soul is crushed with longing for your judgments at every time"** (v20) — Hebrew גָּרְסָה נַפְשִׁי לְתַאֲבָה … בְכָל־עֵת; גָּרַס "be crushed" (Lam 3:16); תַּאֲבָה "longing" (only here; the verb תָּאַב v40, 174).
- **"Thou hast rebuked the proud that are cursed, which do err" → "You have rebuked the presumptuous, the cursed, who err"** (v21) — Hebrew גָּעַרְתָּ זֵדִים אֲרוּרִים הַשֹּׁגִים: זֵדִים "the presumptuous" (so v51, 69, 78, 85, 122); the accents close the first half after אֲרוּרִים, so "cursed" belongs with the presumptuous; "cursed are those who err" is the other reading.
- **"Remove from me" → "Roll away from me"** (v22) — Hebrew גַּל, from גָּלַל "roll". **"reproach and contempt"** — חֶרְפָּה וָבוּז. **"I have kept" → "I have guarded"** — נָצַרְתִּי.
- **"Princes also did sit and speak against me: but thy servant did meditate" → "Even princes have sat; against me they have spoken together; your servant muses"** (v23) — Hebrew גַּם יָשְׁבוּ שָׂרִים בִּי נִדְבָּרוּ עַבְדְּךָ יָשִׂיחַ: נִדְבַּר "speak together"; no "but"; יָשִׂיחַ an imperfect.
- **"Thy testimonies also are my delight and my counsellors" → "Also your testimonies are my delight, the men of my counsel"** (v24) — Hebrew גַּם־עֵדֹתֶיךָ שַׁעֲשֻׁעָי אַנְשֵׁי עֲצָתִי, literally "the men of my counsel"; no "and".
- **"My soul cleaveth unto the dust" → "My soul has clung to the dust"** (v25) — Hebrew דָּבְקָה, from דָּבַק "cling" (so v31). **"quicken thou me" → "make me live"** — see above.
- **"I have declared my ways, and thou heardest me" → "My ways I have recounted, and you answered me"** (v26) — Hebrew סִפַּרְתִּי … וַתַּעֲנֵנִי, from עָנָה "answer".
- **"Make me to understand" kept "make me understand"** (v27) — Hebrew הֲבִינֵנִי, hifil of בִּין (so v34, 73, 125, 144, 169). **"I shall talk of thy wondrous works" → "I will muse on your wonderful deeds"** — Hebrew וְאָשִׂיחָה בְּנִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָ.
- **"My soul melteth for heaviness" → "My soul drips away for grief"** (v28) — Hebrew דָּלְפָה נַפְשִׁי מִתּוּגָה: דָּלַף "drip, leak" (Job 16:20 of the eye; Eccl 10:18 of a roof): the soul runs away in tears; תּוּגָה "grief". **"strengthen thou me" → "make me stand"** — Hebrew קַיְּמֵנִי, piel of קוּם "make stand" (so v106).
- **"Remove from me the way of lying: and grant me thy law graciously" → "The way of falsehood remove from me, and with your Torah favor me"** (v29) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ־שֶׁקֶר הָסֵר מִמֶּנִּי וְתוֹרָתְךָ חָנֵּנִי: שֶׁקֶר "falsehood"; חָנַן with two objects, "favor me with" (as Gen 33:5).
- **"the way of truth" → "the way of faithfulness"** (v30) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ־אֱמוּנָה (אֱמֶת "truth" is v43, 142, 151, 160). **"thy judgments have I laid before me" → "your judgments I have set"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָ שִׁוִּיתִי: שִׁוָּה "set, place"; "before me" is not written (Ps 16:8 has לְנֶגְדִּי); the verb may also mean "I have counted fitting".
- **"I have stuck unto thy testimonies: O LORD, put me not to shame" → "I have clung to your testimonies; Yahweh, do not put me to shame"** (v31) — Hebrew דָּבַקְתִּי … אַל־תְּבִישֵׁנִי, hifil of בּוֹשׁ (so v116).
- **"I will run the way of thy commandments, when thou shalt enlarge my heart" → "The way of your commandments I will run, for you will enlarge my heart"** (v32) — Hebrew כִּי תַרְחִיב לִבִּי; כִּי "for" or "when".
- **"Teach me, O LORD" → "Teach me, Yahweh"** (v33) — Hebrew הוֹרֵנִי, from הוֹרָה "teach", the root of תּוֹרָה (so v102 "you have taught me"; לִמֵּד "teach" is v12 and the rest). **"I shall keep it unto the end" → "I will guard it to the end"** — Hebrew וְאֶצְּרֶנָּה עֵקֶב: נָצַר "guard"; עֵקֶב, literally "heel", here "to the end" (so v112); "as a reward" is the other reading.
- **"Give me understanding, and I shall keep thy law; yea, I shall observe it" → "Make me understand, and I will guard your Torah, and I will keep it"** (v34) — Hebrew הֲבִינֵנִי וְאֶצְּרָה … וְאֶשְׁמְרֶנָּה: two verbs, נָצַר "guard" and שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"Make me to go in the path" → "Make me tread in the track"** (v35) — Hebrew הַדְרִיכֵנִי בִּנְתִיב, hifil of דָּרַךְ "tread"; נָתִיב "track" (so v105 נְתִיבָה; אֹרַח "path" is v9, 15). **"therein do I delight" → "in it I delight"** — חָפַצְתִּי.
- **"Incline my heart unto thy testimonies, and not to covetousness" → "Turn my heart to your testimonies, and not to unjust gain"** (v36) — Hebrew הַט־לִבִּי אֶל … וְאַל אֶל־בָּצַע: הִטָּה לֵב "turn the heart" ("incline"); בֶּצַע "unjust gain".
- **"Turn away mine eyes from beholding vanity" → "Make my eyes pass on from seeing emptiness"** (v37) — Hebrew הַעֲבֵר עֵינַי מֵרְאוֹת שָׁוְא: הֶעֱבִיר "make pass over" (so v39); שָׁוְא "emptiness". **"in thy way" → "in your ways"** — Hebrew בִּדְרָכֶךָ, a plural; many manuscripts and the Targum read בִּדְבָרֶךָ "by your word" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Stablish thy word unto thy servant, who is devoted to thy fear" → "Make your saying stand for your servant, which is for the fear of you"** (v38) — Hebrew הָקֵם לְעַבְדְּךָ אִמְרָתֶךָ אֲשֶׁר לְיִרְאָתֶךָ: הֵקִים "make stand"; אֲשֶׁר may point to the saying (which leads to fear of you) or to the servant (who is for the fear of you): left open.
- **"Turn away my reproach which I fear" → "Make my reproach pass away, which I am afraid of"** (v39) — Hebrew הַעֲבֵר חֶרְפָּתִי אֲשֶׁר יָגֹרְתִּי; יָגֹר "be afraid of" (not יָרֵא "fear", v120).
- **"I have longed after" kept "I have longed for"** (v40) — Hebrew תָּאַבְתִּי (so v174; v131 the related יָאַב).
- **"And" kept at the head of every verse of the stanza** (v41–48) — each begins with וְ / וַ (the letter of the stanza).
- **"Let thy mercies come also unto me" → "And let your chasadim come to me"** (v41) — Hebrew וִיבֹאֻנִי חֲסָדֶךָ, the plural of *chesed* with a plural verb. **"according to thy word" → "according to your saying"** — אִמְרָה, as everywhere; תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation".
- **"So shall I have wherewith to answer him that reproacheth me" → "and I will answer him who reproaches me a word"** (v42) — Hebrew וְאֶעֱנֶה חֹרְפִי דָבָר, literally "and I will answer my reproacher a word". **"for I trust" → "for I have trusted"** — בָטַחְתִּי, a perfect.
- **"take not the word of truth utterly out of my mouth" → "do not snatch from my mouth a word of truth utterly"** (v43) — Hebrew וְאַל־תַּצֵּל מִפִּי דְבַר־אֱמֶת עַד־מְאֹד: הִצִּיל, elsewhere "deliver", here "snatch away" (the one verb; as Gen 31:9); דְבַר־אֱמֶת without the article. **"for I have hoped in thy judgments" → "for I have hoped for your judgment"** — Hebrew לְמִשְׁפָּטֶךָ, singular.
- **"for ever and ever" kept** (v44) — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם וָעֶד; תָּמִיד "continually".
- **"I will walk at liberty: for I seek thy precepts" → "I will walk about in a wide place, for your precepts I have sought"** (v45) — Hebrew וְאֶתְהַלְּכָה בָרְחָבָה … דָרָשְׁתִּי: hitpael "walk about"; רְחָבָה "a wide place"; a perfect.
- **"which I have loved" → "which I love"** (v47, 48) — Hebrew אָהָבְתִּי, a perfect of a lasting state, in English a present (so v97, 113, 119, 127, 159, 163, 167). **"I will delight myself"** — אֶשְׁתַּעֲשַׁע, as v16.
- **"My hands also will I lift up" → "And I will lift up my palms"** (v48) — Hebrew וְאֶשָּׂא־כַפַּי, כַּף "palm". The words "your commandments, which I love" stand twice (v47, 48), as in the Hebrew.
- **"Remember the word unto thy servant, upon which thou hast caused me to hope" → "Remember the word to your servant, on which you have made me hope"** (v49) — Hebrew זְכֹר־דָּבָר לְעַבְדֶּךָ עַל אֲשֶׁר יִחַלְתָּנִי: דָּבָר without "your"; the piel of יִחֵל "make hope".
- **"for thy word hath quickened me" → "that your saying has made me live"** (v50) — Hebrew כִּי אִמְרָתְךָ חִיָּתְנִי: כִּי "that" (what the comfort is) or "for"; נֶחָמָה "comfort"; עֳנִי "affliction" (so v92, 153).
- **"The proud have had me greatly in derision: yet have I not declined" → "The presumptuous have mocked me utterly; from your Torah I have not turned"** (v51) — Hebrew הֱלִיצֻנִי עַד־מְאֹד, hifil of לִיץ "mock"; לֹא נָטִיתִי, נָטָה "turn" (so v157; סוּר "depart" is v102, 115); no "yet".
- **"I remembered thy judgments of old, O LORD; and have comforted myself" → "I have remembered your judgments from of old, Yahweh, and I have been comforted"** (v52) — Hebrew זָכַרְתִּי … מֵעוֹלָם … וָאֶתְנֶחָם, hitpael "be comforted"; "from of old" may belong to the judgments or to the remembering.
- **"Horror hath taken hold upon me" → "Scorching has seized me"** (v53) — Hebrew זַלְעָפָה אֲחָזַתְנִי: זַלְעָפָה "burning heat" (Ps 11:6 "a wind of scorchings"; Lam 5:10); מִן "because of".
- **"Thy statutes have been my songs in the house of my pilgrimage" → "Songs to me have your statutes been in the house of my sojournings"** (v54) — Hebrew זְמִרוֹת הָיוּ־לִי חֻקֶּיךָ בְּבֵית מְגוּרָי: זְמִרוֹת "songs" (sung to music); מְגוּרִים, plural, from גּוּר "sojourn".
- **"and have kept thy law" → "and I have kept your Torah"** (v55) — Hebrew וָאֶשְׁמְרָה, a past (the narrative form with the lengthened ending).
- **"This I had, because I kept thy precepts" → "This has been mine, that your precepts I have guarded"** (v56) — Hebrew זֹאת הָיְתָה־לִּי כִּי פִקֻּדֶיךָ נָצָרְתִּי; the earlier "blessing" is not in the Hebrew; כִּי "that" or "because".
- **"Thou art my portion, O LORD: I have said that I would keep thy words" → "My portion is Yahweh; I have said, to keep your words"** (v57) — Hebrew חֶלְקִי יְהוָה אָמַרְתִּי לִשְׁמֹר דְּבָרֶיךָ: no "you are"; also read "My portion, Yahweh — I have said — is to keep your words": left open as the Hebrew leaves it.
- **"I intreated thy favour with my whole heart: be merciful unto me" → "I have besought your face with the whole heart; show me favor"** (v58) — Hebrew חִלִּיתִי פָנֶיךָ, literally "I have made your face soft"; חָנֵּנִי, from חָנַן "show favor" (so v132).
- **"I thought on my ways, and turned my feet" → "I have thought on my ways, and I turned back my feet"** (v59) — Hebrew חִשַּׁבְתִּי … וָאָשִׁיבָה, הֵשִׁיב "turn back" (root שׁוּב).
- **"I made haste, and delayed not" → "I have hastened and I have not delayed"** (v60) — Hebrew חַשְׁתִּי וְלֹא הִתְמַהְמָהְתִּי.
- **"The bands of the wicked have robbed me" → "The cords of the wicked have surrounded me"** (v61) — Hebrew חֶבְלֵי רְשָׁעִים עִוְּדֻנִי: חֶבֶל "cord" (also "band, company": the snare is the likelier sense); עִוֵּד "surround" (uncertain; "rob" in older versions). **"but I have not forgotten" → "your Torah I have not forgotten"** — no "but" (so v83, 109, 141, 153).
- **"At midnight" → "At the middle of the night"** (v62) — Hebrew חֲצוֹת־לַיְלָה. **"I will rise to give thanks unto thee" → "I rise to give thanks to you"** — אָקוּם, an imperfect of what is done.
- **"I am a companion of all them that fear thee, and of them that keep" → "A companion am I to all who fear you, and to those who keep"** (v63) — Hebrew חָבֵר אָנִי לְכָל־ … וּלְשֹׁמְרֵי.
- **"The earth, O LORD, is full of thy mercy" → "Of your chesed, Yahweh, the earth is full"** (v64) — Hebrew חַסְדְּךָ יְהוָה מָלְאָה הָאָרֶץ, the Hebrew order. **"teach me thy statutes" → "your statutes teach me"** — the object first, as written (so v108, 124).
- **"Thou hast dealt well with thy servant" → "Good you have done with your servant"** (v65) — Hebrew טוֹב עָשִׂיתָ עִם־עַבְדְּךָ, as עָשָׂה חֶסֶד עִם "do chesed with".
- **"Teach me good judgment" → "Goodness of discernment … teach me"** (v66) — Hebrew טוּב טַעַם וָדַעַת: טוּב "goodness", a noun; טַעַם "taste, discernment" (1 Sam 25:33). **"for I have believed thy commandments" → "for in your commandments I have believed"** — הֶאֱמַנְתִּי בְּ.
- **"Before I was afflicted I went astray: but now have I kept thy word" → "Before I was afflicted I was erring, and now your saying I have kept"** (v67) — Hebrew טֶרֶם אֶעֱנֶה אֲנִי שֹׁגֵג וְעַתָּה: אֶעֱנֶה, qal of עָנָה "be afflicted"; שֹׁגֵג a participle, "erring" (the שָׁגָה root of v10); וְ "and".
- **"Thou art good, and doest good" → "Good are you and doing good"** (v68) — Hebrew טוֹב־אַתָּה וּמֵטִיב, adjective and participle.
- **"The proud have forged a lie against me" → "The presumptuous have smeared falsehood over me"** (v69) — Hebrew טָפְלוּ עָלַי שֶׁקֶר, טָפַל "smear over" (Job 13:4). **"but I will keep" → "I … will guard"** — אֲנִי … אֱצֹּר, the pronoun written out; no "but" (so v70, 78).
- **"Their heart is as fat as grease; but I delight in thy law" → "Their heart is gross like fat; I — your Torah I have delighted in"** (v70) — Hebrew טָפַשׁ כַּחֵלֶב לִבָּם אֲנִי תּוֹרָתְךָ שִׁעֲשָׁעְתִּי: טָפַשׁ "be gross, insensible" (only here); the stressed אֲנִי kept; a perfect.
- **"It is good for me that I have been afflicted; that I might learn" → "… so that I may learn"** (v71) — Hebrew עֻנֵּיתִי, pual of עָנָה "be afflicted"; לְמַעַן.
- **"The law of thy mouth is better unto me than thousands of gold and silver" → "Better to me is the Torah of your mouth than thousands of gold and silver"** (v72) — Hebrew טוֹב־לִי … מֵאַלְפֵי זָהָב וָכָסֶף, "good to me … more than": no "pieces".
- **"Thy hands have made me and fashioned me: give me understanding, that I may learn" → "Your hands made me and established me; make me understand, and I will learn"** (v73) — Hebrew עָשׂוּנִי וַיְכוֹנְנוּנִי, כּוֹנֵן "establish" (so v90); וְאֶלְמְדָה, a resolve.
- **"They that fear thee will be glad when they see me" → "Those who fear you will see me and be glad"** (v74) — Hebrew יִרְאוּנִי וְיִשְׂמָחוּ, two verbs; שָׂמַח "be glad". **"because I have hoped in thy word" → "for I have hoped for your word"** — לִדְבָרְךָ יִחָלְתִּי (so v81, 114, 147).
- **"that thy judgments are right" → "that your judgments are righteousness"** (v75) — Hebrew כִּי־צֶדֶק מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָ, the noun צֶדֶק (so v138, 144, 172). **"and that thou in faithfulness hast afflicted me" → "and in faithfulness you have afflicted me"** — Hebrew וֶאֱמוּנָה עִנִּיתָנִי, from עִנָּה "afflict".
- **"Let, I pray thee, thy merciful kindness be for my comfort" → "Let, please, your chesed be for my comfort"** (v76) — Hebrew יְהִי־נָא חַסְדְּךָ לְנַחֲמֵנִי, נָא "please"; literally "to comfort me".
- **"Let thy tender mercies come unto me, that I may live" → "Let your compassion come to me, and I shall live"** (v77) — Hebrew רַחֲמֶיךָ (always plural: "compassion"; so v156); וְאֶחְיֶה.
- **"Let the proud be ashamed; for they dealt perversely with me without a cause: but I will meditate" → "Let the presumptuous be ashamed, for with falsehood they have dealt crookedly with me; I will muse"** (v78) — Hebrew כִּי־שֶׁקֶר עִוְּתוּנִי אֲנִי אָשִׂיחַ: שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" (not "without a cause"); עִוֵּת "make crooked, pervert" (Job 8:3); no "but". The verse stands on two lines, as the Hebrew has two halves.
- **"Let those that fear thee turn unto me, and those that have known thy testimonies" → "and those who know your testimonies"** (v79) — qere וְיֹדְעֵי "and those who know"; the ketiv וידעו (וְיֵדְעוּ) is "that they may know".
- **"Let my heart be sound in thy statutes" → "Let my heart be blameless in your statutes"** (v80) — Hebrew תָמִים "blameless" (as v1).
- **"My soul fainteth for thy salvation: but I hope in thy word" → "My soul is worn out for your salvation; for your word I have hoped"** (v81) — Hebrew כָּלְתָה … נַפְשִׁי, כָּלָה "be finished, wear out" (so of the eyes v82, 123); no "but"; a perfect.
- **"Mine eyes fail for thy word, saying" → "My eyes are worn out for your saying, saying"** (v82) — Hebrew כָּלוּ עֵינַי לְאִמְרָתֶךָ לֵאמֹר; לֵאמֹר "saying" kept.
- **"like a bottle in the smoke; yet do I not forget" → "like a skin in the smoke; your statutes I have not forgotten"** (v83) — Hebrew כְּנֹאד בְּקִיטוֹר: נֹאד "skin" (a skin bottle); קִיטוֹר "smoke" (a thick smoke); no "yet"; a perfect.
- **"How many are the days of thy servant? when wilt thou execute judgment on them that persecute me?" → "… When will you do judgment on those who pursue me?"** (v84) — Hebrew תַּעֲשֶׂה בְרֹדְפַי מִשְׁפָּט, "do judgment on"; רָדַף "pursue" (so v86, 150, 157, 161).
- **"The proud have digged pits for me, which are not after thy law" → "The presumptuous have dug pits for me, which is not according to your Torah"** (v85) — Hebrew כָּרוּ־לִי זֵדִים שִׁיחוֹת אֲשֶׁר לֹא כְתוֹרָתֶךָ: כָּרָה "dig"; אֲשֶׁר may point to the deed, the pits or the presumptuous ("who are not according to your Torah"): left open.
- **"All thy commandments are faithful" → "All your commandments are faithfulness"** (v86) — Hebrew אֱמוּנָה, a noun. **"they persecute me wrongfully" → "with falsehood they have pursued me"** — שֶׁקֶר רְדָפוּנִי, a perfect.
- **"They had almost consumed me upon earth; but I forsook not" → "Almost they made an end of me in the land, and I have not forsaken"** (v87) — Hebrew כִּמְעַט כִּלּוּנִי בָאָרֶץ וַאֲנִי לֹא־עָזַבְתִּי: כִּלָּה with a person "make an end of"; וַאֲנִי "and I".
- **"Quicken me after thy lovingkindness; so shall I keep the testimony of thy mouth" → "According to your chesed make me live, and I will keep the testimony of your mouth"** (v88) — Hebrew עֵדוּת פִּיךָ, singular.
- **"For ever, O LORD, thy word is settled in heaven" → "Forever, Yahweh, your word stands in the heavens"** (v89) — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם יְהוָה דְּבָרְךָ נִצָּב בַּשָּׁמָיִם: the accents divide after יְהוָה; נִצָּב "stand (be stationed)"; שָׁמַיִם "heavens". Also read "Yahweh is forever".
- **"Thy faithfulness is unto all generations: thou hast established the earth, and it abideth" → "To generation and generation is your faithfulness; you have established the earth, and it stands"** (v90) — Hebrew לְדֹר וָדֹר; כּוֹנַנְתָּ; וַתַּעֲמֹד, the narrative form, "and it stood (and stands)"; עָמַד "stand" (so v91; נִצָּב in v89 is another verb).
- **"They continue this day according to thine ordinances: for all are thy servants" → "By your judgments they stand this day, for all things are your servants"** (v91) — Hebrew לְמִשְׁפָּטֶיךָ עָמְדוּ הַיּוֹם כִּי הַכֹּל עֲבָדֶיךָ: מִשְׁפָּטִים "judgments" as everywhere ("ordinances" in KJV); לְ "by, according to" (or "for your judgments they stand", as servants waiting); הַכֹּל "all things".
- **"Unless thy law had been my delights, I should then have perished" → "If your Torah had not been my delight, then I would have perished"** (v92) — Hebrew לוּלֵי … אָז אָבַדְתִּי; אָז "then" kept; אָבַד "perish".
- **"I will never forget" → "Forever I will not forget"** (v93) — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם לֹא־אֶשְׁכַּח. **"for with them thou hast quickened me" → "for by them you have made me live"** — חִיִּיתָנִי.
- **"I am thine, save me" → "Yours am I; save me"** (v94) — Hebrew לְךָ־אֲנִי הוֹשִׁיעֵנִי.
- **"The wicked have waited for me to destroy me: but I will consider thy testimonies" → "For me the wicked have waited, to do away with me; to your testimonies I give heed"** (v95) — Hebrew לִי קִוּוּ רְשָׁעִים לְאַבְּדֵנִי עֵדֹתֶיךָ אֶתְבּוֹנָן: קִוָּה "wait for"; אִבֵּד piel "do away with"; הִתְבּוֹנֵן "give heed to" with an object, "gain understanding" without one (v100, 104); no "but".
- **"I have seen an end of all perfection: but thy commandment is exceeding broad" → "To all perfection I have seen an end; your commandment is very broad"** (v96) — Hebrew לְכָל תִּכְלָה רָאִיתִי קֵץ רְחָבָה מִצְוָתְךָ מְאֹד: תִּכְלָה only here ("completeness", root כָּלָה); singular מִצְוָה; no "but".
- **"O how love I thy law! it is my meditation all the day" → "How I love your Torah! All the day it is my musing"** (v97) — Hebrew מָה־אָהַבְתִּי … כָּל־הַיּוֹם הִיא שִׂיחָתִי; no "O"; שִׂיחָה "musing" (so v99).
- **"Thou through thy commandments hast made me wiser than mine enemies: for they are ever with me" → "Than my enemies your commandments make me wiser, for forever it is mine"** (v98) — Hebrew מֵאֹיְבַי תְּחַכְּמֵנִי מִצְוֺתֶךָ כִּי לְעוֹלָם הִיא־לִי: the noun is plural, the verb and הִיא "it" singular (the commandments taken as one whole); לְעוֹלָם "forever"; הִיא־לִי "it is to me".
- **"I have more understanding than all my teachers" → "Than all my teachers I have more insight"** (v99) — Hebrew הִשְׂכַּלְתִּי, from הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight". **"for thy testimonies are my meditation" → "for your testimonies are a musing to me"** — שִׂיחָה לִי.
- **"I understand more than the ancients, because I keep thy precepts" → "Than the elders I gain more understanding, for your precepts I have guarded"** (v100) — Hebrew מִזְּקֵנִים אֶתְבּוֹנָן … נָצָרְתִּי: זְקֵנִים "elders, old men".
- **"I have refrained my feet from every evil way" → "From every evil path I have kept back my feet"** (v101) — Hebrew מִכָּל־אֹרַח רָע כָּלִאתִי רַגְלָי: אֹרַח "path"; כָּלָא "keep back".
- **"I have not departed from thy judgments: for thou hast taught me" → "From your judgments I have not departed, for you yourself have taught me"** (v102) — Hebrew לֹא־סָרְתִּי, from סוּר "depart" (so v115); כִּי־אַתָּה הוֹרֵתָנִי, the pronoun written out; הוֹרָה "teach", as v33.
- **"How sweet are thy words unto my taste! yea, sweeter than honey to my mouth" → "How smooth to my palate is your saying, more than honey to my mouth"** (v103) — Hebrew מַה־נִּמְלְצוּ לְחִכִּי אִמְרָתֶךָ מִדְּבַשׁ לְפִי: נִמְלַץ only here, "be smooth, pleasant" (uncertain); the verb is plural with the singular אִמְרָתֶךָ (some manuscripts have the plural noun; from memory); חֵךְ "palate"; no "yea, sweeter".
- **"Through thy precepts I get understanding: therefore I hate every false way" → "From your precepts I gain understanding; therefore I hate every path of falsehood"** (v104) — Hebrew מִפִּקּוּדֶיךָ אֶתְבּוֹנָן … כָּל־אֹרַח שָׁקֶר (so v128).
- **"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path" → "A lamp to my foot is your word, and a light to my track"** (v105) — Hebrew נֵר־לְרַגְלִי … לִנְתִיבָתִי: רֶגֶל singular; נְתִיבָה "track" (as v35).
- **"I have sworn, and I will perform it" → "I have sworn, and I have made it stand"** (v106) — Hebrew נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי וָאֲקַיֵּמָה, piel of קוּם "make stand" (= confirm; as v28), a past.
- **"I am afflicted very much" → "I am afflicted utterly"** (v107) — Hebrew נַעֲנֵיתִי עַד־מְאֹד, nifal of עָנָה.
- **"Accept, I beseech thee, the freewill offerings of my mouth, O LORD" → "In the freewill offerings of my mouth take pleasure, please, Yahweh"** (v108) — Hebrew נִדְבוֹת פִּי רְצֵה־נָא יְהוָה: רָצָה "take pleasure in, be pleased with"; נָא "please"; the earlier "of praise" is not written.
- **"My soul is continually in my hand: yet do I not forget" → "My soul is in my palm continually, and your Torah I have not forgotten"** (v109) — Hebrew נַפְשִׁי בְכַפִּי תָמִיד: כַּף "palm" (as v48); נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (the earlier "life"); וְ "and".
- **"The wicked have laid a snare for me: yet I erred not from thy precepts" → "The wicked have set a trap for me, and from your precepts I have not wandered"** (v110) — Hebrew נָתְנוּ … פַּח לִי וּמִפִּקּוּדֶיךָ לֹא תָעִיתִי: פַּח "trap"; תָּעָה "wander" (so v176).
- **"Thy testimonies have I taken as an heritage for ever: for they are the rejoicing of my heart" → "I have inherited your testimonies forever, for the exultation of my heart are they"** (v111) — Hebrew נָחַלְתִּי, נָחַל "inherit"; שָׂשׂוֹן "exultation" (noun of שׂוּשׂ, v14).
- **"I have inclined mine heart to perform thy statutes always, even unto the end" → "I have turned my heart to do your statutes, forever, to the end"** (v112) — Hebrew נָטִיתִי לִבִּי לַעֲשׂוֹת חֻקֶּיךָ לְעוֹלָם עֵקֶב: נָטָה "turn" ("incline"; v36 the hifil); עֵקֶב as v33.
- **"I hate vain thoughts: but thy law do I love" → "The double-minded I hate, and your Torah I love"** (v113) — Hebrew סֵעֲפִים שָׂנֵאתִי וְתוֹרָתְךָ אָהָבְתִּי: סֵעֲפִים only here, "the divided ones" (persons; one root with סְעִפִּים "branches", 1 Kgs 18:21; uncertain); וְ "and".
- **"Thou art my hiding place and my shield: I hope in thy word" → "My hiding place and my shield are you; for your word I have hoped"** (v114) — Hebrew סִתְרִי וּמָגִנִּי אָתָּה, the Hebrew order; סֵתֶר "hiding place", מָגֵן "shield".
- **"Depart from me, ye evildoers: for I will keep" → "Depart from me, evildoers, and I will guard"** (v115) — Hebrew סוּרוּ־מִמֶּנִּי מְרֵעִים וְאֶצְּרָה: מְרֵעִים "evildoers"; וְ "and" with a resolve; נָצַר "guard"; אֱלֹהָי "my Elohim".
- **"Uphold me according unto thy word, that I may live: and let me not be ashamed of my hope" → "… according to your saying, and I shall live, and do not make me ashamed of my hope"** (v116) — Hebrew סָמְכֵנִי כְאִמְרָתְךָ וְאֶחְיֶה וְאַל־תְּבִישֵׁנִי מִשִּׂבְרִי: hifil of בּוֹשׁ (as v31); שֵׂבֶר "hope" (only here and Ps 146:5); מִן "of, because of".
- **"Hold thou me up, and I shall be safe: and I will have respect unto thy statutes continually" → "Support me, and I shall be saved, and I will look upon your statutes continually"** (v117) — Hebrew סְעָדֵנִי וְאִוָּשֵׁעָה וְאֶשְׁעָה: סָעַד "support"; nifal of יָשַׁע "be saved"; שָׁעָה "look upon" (Gen 4:4; הִבִּיט "look to" is v6).
- **"Thou hast trodden down all them that err from thy statutes: for their deceit is falsehood" → "You have spurned all who err from your statutes, for falsehood is their deceit"** (v118) — Hebrew סָלִיתָ, סָלָה "spurn, toss aside" (Lam 1:15; uncertain); שֹׁגִים, from שָׁגָה "err"; כִּי־שֶׁקֶר תַּרְמִיתָם, the Hebrew order; the Greek has "their thought" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Thou puttest away all the wicked of the earth like dross" → "As dross you have made all the wicked of the earth cease"** (v119) — Hebrew סִגִים הִשְׁבַּתָּ כָל־רִשְׁעֵי־אָרֶץ: סִגִים "dross" stands first without "as" or "like" ("as" is supplied); הִשְׁבִּית "make cease, put an end to". Some manuscripts read חָשַׁבְתָּ "you have reckoned" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"My flesh trembleth for fear of thee; and I am afraid of thy judgments" → "My flesh bristles from dread of you, and your judgments I fear"** (v120) — Hebrew סָמַר מִפַּחְדְּךָ בְשָׂרִי וּמִמִּשְׁפָּטֶיךָ יָרֵאתִי: סָמַר "bristle, shudder" (Job 4:15 of hair); פַּחַד "dread"; יָרֵא "fear".
- **"I have done judgment and justice: leave me not to mine oppressors" → "I have done judgment and righteousness; do not leave me to my oppressors"** (v121) — Hebrew עָשִׂיתִי מִשְׁפָּט וָצֶדֶק: מִשְׁפָּט here of right dealing (the one word kept); צֶדֶק "righteousness"; עֹשְׁקַי, עָשַׁק "oppress" (so v122, 134).
- **"Be surety for thy servant for good" kept** (v122) — Hebrew עֲרֹב עַבְדְּךָ לְטוֹב, עָרַב "be surety for". **"let not the proud oppress me" → "let not the presumptuous oppress me"** — זֵדִים.
- **"Mine eyes fail for thy salvation, and for the word of thy righteousness" → "My eyes are worn out for your salvation, and for the saying of your righteousness"** (v123) — Hebrew כָּלוּ … וּלְאִמְרַת צִדְקֶךָ, a genitive; the earlier "fulfillment" is not written.
- **"Deal with thy servant according unto thy mercy" → "Do with your servant according to your chesed"** (v124) — Hebrew עֲשֵׂה עִם־עַבְדְּךָ, as v65.
- **"I am thy servant; give me understanding, that I may know" → "Your servant am I; make me understand, and I will know"** (v125) — Hebrew וְאֵדְעָה, a resolve.
- **"It is time for thee, LORD, to work: for they have made void thy law" → "It is time to act for Yahweh; they have made void your Torah"** (v126) — Hebrew עֵת לַעֲשׂוֹת לַיהוָה הֵפֵרוּ תּוֹרָתֶךָ: "for Yahweh to act" or "to act for Yahweh" (the speaker's act on his behalf): left open; הֵפֵר "make void"; an active verb ("they"), not "has been broken"; no "for".
- **"above gold; yea, above fine gold" → "more than gold and more than fine gold"** (v127) — Hebrew מִזָּהָב וּמִפָּז.
- **"Therefore I esteem all thy precepts concerning all things to be right" → "Therefore all the precepts of all things I count right"** (v128) — Hebrew עַל־כֵּן כָּל־פִּקּוּדֵי כֹל יִשָּׁרְתִּי: פִּקּוּדֵי כֹל "the precepts of all (things)" has no "your" (difficult; a common emendation reads "all your precepts"); יִשַּׁר piel "count right, keep straight". **"and I hate every false way" → "every path of falsehood I hate"** — as v104.
- **"Thy testimonies are wonderful: therefore doth my soul keep them" → "Wonders are your testimonies; therefore my soul has guarded them"** (v129) — Hebrew פְּלָאוֹת עֵדְוֺתֶיךָ … נְצָרָתַם נַפְשִׁי: פֶּלֶא "wonder", a plural noun; נָצַר "guard", a perfect.
- **"The entrance of thy words giveth light; it giveth understanding unto the simple" → "The opening of your words gives light, giving understanding to the simple"** (v130) — Hebrew פֵּתַח דְּבָרֶיךָ יָאִיר מֵבִין פְּתָיִים: פֵּתַח only here, "opening, unfolding" (another pointing than פֶּתַח "entrance"); מֵבִין a participle; פְּתָיִים "the simple".
- **"I opened my mouth, and panted: for I longed" → "My mouth I opened wide and panted, for I longed"** (v131) — Hebrew פִּי־פָעַרְתִּי וָאֶשְׁאָפָה: פָּעַר "open wide"; שָׁאַף "pant"; יָאַב "long for" (only here; as תָּאַב v40).
- **"Look thou upon me, and be merciful unto me, as thou usest to do unto those that love thy name" → "Turn to me and show me favor, as is the judgment for those who love your name"** (v132) — Hebrew פְּנֵה־אֵלַי וְחָנֵּנִי כְּמִשְׁפָּט לְאֹהֲבֵי שְׁמֶךָ: פָּנָה "turn"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", here what is due or customary (the one word kept).
- **"Order my steps in thy word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me" → "My steps establish in your saying, and let no wickedness have power over me"** (v133) — Hebrew פְּעָמַי הָכֵן בְּאִמְרָתֶךָ וְאַל־תַּשְׁלֶט־בִּי כָל־אָוֶן: פַּעַם "foot, step"; הֵכִין "establish"; אָוֶן "wickedness"; שָׁלַט בְּ "have power over".
- **"Deliver me from the oppression of man: so will I keep" → "Ransom me from the oppression of man, and I will keep"** (v134) — Hebrew פְּדֵנִי מֵעֹשֶׁק אָדָם: פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל "redeem" is v154); אָדָם "man, mankind".
- **"Make thy face to shine upon thy servant" → "Your face make shine upon your servant"** (v135) — Hebrew פָּנֶיךָ הָאֵר בְּעַבְדֶּךָ, the Hebrew order.
- **"Rivers of waters run down mine eyes, because they keep not thy law" → "Streams of water have run down my eyes, because they have not kept your Torah"** (v136) — Hebrew פַּלְגֵי־מַיִם יָרְדוּ עֵינָי עַל לֹא־שָׁמְרוּ: פַּלְגֵי מַיִם "streams of water"; "my eyes" may also be the subject ("my eyes have run down in streams of water"); עַל "because"; no "people" (the subject is unnamed).
- **"Righteous art thou, O LORD, and upright are thy judgments" → "Righteous are you, Yahweh, and right are your judgments"** (v137) — Hebrew צַדִּיק אַתָּה יְהוָה וְיָשָׁר מִשְׁפָּטֶיךָ: יָשָׁר of things "right" ("upright" is said of persons); the adjective is singular with the plural noun.
- **"Thy testimonies that thou hast commanded are righteous and very faithful" → "You have commanded righteousness, your testimonies, and faithfulness greatly"** (v138) — Hebrew צִוִּיתָ צֶדֶק עֵדֹתֶיךָ וֶאֱמוּנָה מְאֹד, word for word: צֶדֶק and אֱמוּנָה are nouns; they may describe the testimonies ("in righteousness … in faithfulness") or stand beside them as what was commanded: left open.
- **"My zeal hath consumed me, because mine enemies have forgotten" → "My zeal has put an end to me, for my adversaries have forgotten"** (v139) — Hebrew צִמְּתַתְנִי קִנְאָתִי: צִמֵּת "put an end to, silence" (Ps 88:16); קִנְאָה "zeal" (also "jealousy"); צָרַי "my adversaries".
- **"Thy word is very pure: therefore thy servant loveth it" → "Very refined is your saying, and your servant loves it"** (v140) — Hebrew צְרוּפָה אִמְרָתְךָ מְאֹד וְעַבְדְּךָ אֲהֵבָהּ: צָרַף "refine" (as 2 Sam 22:31); וְ "and", not "therefore".
- **"I am small and despised: yet do not I forget" → "Small am I and despised; your precepts I have not forgotten"** (v141) — Hebrew צָעִיר אָנֹכִי וְנִבְזֶה: צָעִיר "small, young"; בָּזָה "despise"; no "yet"; a perfect.
- **"Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth" → "Your righteousness is righteousness forever, and your Torah is truth"** (v142) — Hebrew צִדְקָתְךָ צֶדֶק לְעוֹלָם וְתוֹרָתְךָ אֱמֶת: צְדָקָה and צֶדֶק, the two forms of one word ("righteousness"); לְעוֹלָם "forever".
- **"Trouble and anguish have taken hold on me: yet thy commandments are my delights" → "Distress and straits have found me; your commandments are my delight"** (v143) — Hebrew צַר־וּמָצוֹק מְצָאוּנִי: צַר "distress"; מָצוֹק "straits"; מָצָא "find" (as Deut 31:17); no "yet".
- **"The righteousness of thy testimonies is everlasting: give me understanding, and I shall live" → "Your testimonies are righteousness forever; make me understand, and I shall live"** (v144) — Hebrew צֶדֶק עֵדְוֺתֶיךָ לְעוֹלָם, a noun clause.
- **"I cried with my whole heart; hear me, O LORD: I will keep thy statutes" → "I have called with the whole heart; answer me, Yahweh; your statutes I will guard"** (v145) — Hebrew קָרָאתִי … עֲנֵנִי … חֻקֶּיךָ אֶצֹּרָה: קָרָא "call"; עָנָה "answer"; נָצַר "guard".
- **"I cried unto thee; save me, and I shall keep thy testimonies" → "I have called to you; save me, and I will keep your testimonies"** (v146) — Hebrew קְרָאתִיךָ הוֹשִׁיעֵנִי וְאֶשְׁמְרָה.
- **"I prevented the dawning of the morning, and cried: I hoped in thy word" → "I have gone before the twilight and cried for help; for your word I have hoped"** (v147) — Hebrew קִדַּמְתִּי בַנֶּשֶׁף וָאֲשַׁוֵּעָה: קִדֵּם "go before, be early"; נֶשֶׁף "twilight" (of evening or of dawn); שִׁוַּע "cry for help". Qere לִדְבָרְךָ "for your word"; the ketiv לדבריך is "for your words".
- **"Mine eyes prevent the night watches, that I might meditate in thy word" → "My eyes have gone before the watches, to muse on your saying"** (v148) — Hebrew קִדְּמוּ עֵינַי אַשְׁמֻרוֹת לָשִׂיחַ בְּאִמְרָתֶךָ: the verb of v147; אַשְׁמֻרוֹת "watches" (of the night: "night" is not written).
- **"Hear my voice according unto thy lovingkindness: O LORD, quicken me according to thy judgment" → "My voice hear according to your chesed; Yahweh, according to your judgment make me live"** (v149) — Hebrew קוֹלִי שִׁמְעָה … כְּמִשְׁפָּטֶךָ, singular (v156 the plural).
- **"They draw nigh that follow after mischief: they are far from thy law" → "They have come near, the pursuers of depravity; from your Torah they are far"** (v150) — Hebrew קָרְבוּ רֹדְפֵי זִמָּה: זִמָּה "depravity"; רֹדְפֵי "pursuers of" (many manuscripts read רֹדְפַי "my pursuers", from memory; the Masoretic Text is kept); רָחַק "be far".
- **"Thou art near, O LORD; and all thy commandments are truth" → "Near are you, Yahweh, and all your commandments are truth"** (v151) — Hebrew קָרוֹב אַתָּה; no "but".
- **"Concerning thy testimonies, I have known of old that thou hast founded them for ever" → "Of old I have known from your testimonies that forever you have founded them"** (v152) — Hebrew קֶדֶם יָדַעְתִּי מֵעֵדֹתֶיךָ כִּי לְעוֹלָם יְסַדְתָּם: מִן "from"; יָסַד "found".
- **"Consider mine affliction, and deliver me: for I do not forget" → "See my affliction and free me, for your Torah I have not forgotten"** (v153) — Hebrew רְאֵה … וְחַלְּצֵנִי: רָאָה "see" (so v159); חִלֵּץ "free" (הִצִּיל "deliver" is v170); a perfect.
- **"Plead my cause, and deliver me: quicken me according to thy word" → "Contend my case and redeem me; according to your saying make me live"** (v154) — Hebrew רִיבָה רִיבִי וּגְאָלֵנִי לְאִמְרָתְךָ: רִיב verb and noun, "contend … case"; גָּאַל "redeem"; לְ "according to, for" (not the כְּ of v58 and the rest).
- **"for they seek not thy statutes" → "for your statutes they have not sought"** (v155) — Hebrew לֹא דָרָשׁוּ, a perfect; יְשׁוּעָה "salvation".
- **"Great are thy tender mercies, O LORD: quicken me according to thy judgments" → "Your compassion is great, Yahweh; according to your judgments make me live"** (v156) — Hebrew רַחֲמֶיךָ רַבִּים, literally "many" (the plural noun); כְּמִשְׁפָּטֶיךָ plural.
- **"Many are my persecutors and mine enemies; yet do I not decline" → "Many are my pursuers and my adversaries; from your testimonies I have not turned"** (v157) — Hebrew רֹדְפַי וְצָרָי … לֹא נָטִיתִי: צַר "adversary"; נָטָה as v51; no "yet".
- **"I beheld the transgressors, and was grieved; because they kept not thy word" → "I have seen the treacherous, and I loathed them, who have not kept your saying"** (v158) — Hebrew רָאִיתִי בֹגְדִים וָאֶתְקוֹטָטָה אֲשֶׁר: בָּגַד "deal treacherously"; הִתְקוֹטֵט, the reflexive of קוּט "loathe" ("them" is supplied); אֲשֶׁר "who" (or "because").
- **"Consider how I love thy precepts: quicken me, O LORD" → "See that I love your precepts; Yahweh, according to your chesed make me live"** (v159) — Hebrew רְאֵה כִּי־פִקּוּדֶיךָ אָהָבְתִּי: כִּי "that"; the accents put יְהוָה in the second half.
- **"Thy word is true from the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments endureth for ever" → "The head of your word is truth, and forever is every judgment of your righteousness"** (v160) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ־דְּבָרְךָ אֱמֶת וּלְעוֹלָם כָּל־מִשְׁפַּט צִדְקֶךָ: רֹאשׁ "head" (the sum, or the beginning: not decided); a noun clause, no "endures".
- **"Princes have persecuted me without a cause: but my heart standeth in awe of thy word" → "Princes have pursued me without cause, and of your word my heart has been in dread"** (v161) — Hebrew שָׂרִים רְדָפוּנִי חִנָּם וּמִדְּבָרְךָ פָּחַד לִבִּי: חִנָּם "without cause"; פָּחַד "be in dread" (the verb of פַּחַד, v120); וְ "and". Qere וּמִדְּבָרְךָ "and of your word"; the ketiv ומדבריך is "and of your words".
- **"I rejoice at thy word, as one that findeth great spoil" → "I exult over your saying, like one who finds great spoil"** (v162) — Hebrew שָׂשׂ אָנֹכִי עַל־אִמְרָתֶךָ כְּמוֹצֵא שָׁלָל רָב: שׂוּשׂ "exult" (as v14); שָׁלָל "spoil".
- **"I hate and abhor lying: but thy law do I love" → "Falsehood I hate and abominate; your Torah I love"** (v163) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶר שָׂנֵאתִי וַאֲתַעֵבָה: תִּעֵב "abominate" (verb of תּוֹעֵבָה); no "but".
- **"Seven times a day do I praise thee because of thy righteous judgments" → "Seven times in the day I praise you, because of the judgments of your righteousness"** (v164) — Hebrew שֶׁבַע בַּיּוֹם הִלַּלְתִּיךָ עַל: "times" is supplied; הִלַּלְתִּי a perfect of what is done again and again; הִלֵּל "praise" (הוֹדָה "give thanks" v7, 62).
- **"Great peace have they which love thy law: and nothing shall offend them" → "Great shalom is for those who love your Torah, and there is no stumbling block for them"** (v165) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם רָב לְאֹהֲבֵי תוֹרָתֶךָ וְאֵין־לָמוֹ מִכְשׁוֹל: the `shalom` token; לְ "for"; מִכְשׁוֹל "stumbling block".
- **"LORD, I have hoped for thy salvation, and done thy commandments" → "I have hoped for your salvation, Yahweh, and your commandments I have done"** (v166) — Hebrew שִׂבַּרְתִּי לִישׁוּעָתְךָ יְהוָה וּמִצְוֺתֶיךָ עָשִׂיתִי: שִׂבֵּר "hope" (Ruth 1:13; Esth 9:1); עָשָׂה "do".
- **"My soul hath kept thy testimonies; and I love them exceedingly" → "My soul has kept your testimonies, and I love them greatly"** (v167) — Hebrew שָׁמְרָה נַפְשִׁי … וָאֹהֲבֵם מְאֹד.
- **"I have kept thy precepts and thy testimonies" kept** (v168) — Hebrew שָׁמַרְתִּי פִקּוּדֶיךָ וְעֵדֹתֶיךָ, "your" with both. **"for all my ways are before thee"** — Hebrew נֶגְדֶּךָ "in front of you".
- **"Let my cry come near before thee, O LORD: give me understanding according to thy word" → "Let my cry come near before you, Yahweh; according to your word make me understand"** (v169) — Hebrew תִּקְרַב רִנָּתִי לְפָנֶיךָ: רִנָּה "cry" (as Solomon's prayer, 1 Kgs 8:28; of joy "ringing cry"); קָרַב "come near".
- **"Let my supplication come before thee: deliver me according to thy word" → "Let my plea for favor come before you; according to your saying deliver me"** (v170) — Hebrew תְּחִנָּתִי, תְּחִנָּה "plea for favor" (1 Kgs 8:28); הִצִּיל "deliver".
- **"My lips shall utter praise, when thou hast taught me" → "Let my lips pour forth praise, for you teach me"** (v171) — Hebrew תַּבַּעְנָה שְׂפָתַי תְּהִלָּה כִּי תְלַמְּדֵנִי: הִבִּיעַ "pour forth"; the form may be a wish or a future (taken as a wish, with v169–170, 172–173); כִּי "for".
- **"My tongue shall speak of thy word: for all thy commandments are righteousness" → "Let my tongue answer your saying, for all your commandments are righteousness"** (v172) — Hebrew תַּעַן לְשׁוֹנִי אִמְרָתֶךָ: עָנָה "answer" (also taken as "sing", another root of the same letters); צֶדֶק a noun.
- **"Let thine hand help me" → "Let your hand be to help me"** (v173) — Hebrew תְּהִי־יָדְךָ לְעָזְרֵנִי. **"for I have chosen thy precepts" → "for your precepts I have chosen"** — בָחָרְתִּי.
- **"I have longed for thy salvation, O LORD" → "… Yahweh"** (v174) — Hebrew תָּאַבְתִּי, as v40; **"and thy law is my delight"** — שַׁעֲשֻׁעָי.
- **"Let my soul live, and it shall praise thee; and let thy judgments help me" → "Let my soul live, and it shall praise you, and let your judgment help me"** (v175) — Hebrew תְּחִי־נַפְשִׁי וּתְהַלְלֶךָּ וּמִשְׁפָּטֶךָ יַעֲזְרֻנִי: the noun is singular, the verb plural (the singular may be read as a collective, or the noun as a defective plural).
- **"I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek thy servant; for I do not forget" → "I have wandered like a lost sheep; seek your servant, for your commandments I have not forgotten"** (v176) — Hebrew תָּעִיתִי כְּשֶׂה אֹבֵד בַּקֵּשׁ עַבְדֶּךָ: תָּעָה "wander" (as v110); שֶׂה "sheep" (one of the flock); אֹבֵד "lost, perishing" (from אָבַד "perish", v92); בִּקֵּשׁ "seek"; a perfect.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours; the psalm has no heading.
- **Acrostic** — 22 stanzas of 8 verses; every verse of a stanza begins with the same letter, the stanzas in the order of the Hebrew alphabet (א v1–8 … ת v169–176). The translation does not imitate the letters.
- **The eight words** for what Yahweh has spoken: *torah*, *edot* (testimonies), *piqqudim* (precepts), *chuqqim* (statutes), *mitzvot* (commandments), *mishpatim* (judgments), *davar* (word), *imrah* (saying). Almost every verse has one of them (none in v90, 122; in v84, 121, 132 מִשְׁפָּט stands in another sense).
- ***Chesed*** — v41 (plural), 64, 76, 88, 124, 149, 159.
- **Qere** — v79, 147, 161: the reading tradition stands in the verse, the written form (ketiv) in Changes.
