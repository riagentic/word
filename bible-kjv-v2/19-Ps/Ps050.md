# Psalm 50

A psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] ⟦el|El|God⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
has spoken and called the earth
from the rising of the sun to its setting.

[2] Out of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, the perfection of beauty,
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has shone forth.

[3] Let our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ come, and let him not keep silent;
fire devours before him,
and around him it storms greatly.

[4] He calls to the heavens above
and to the earth, to judge his people:

[5] "Gather to me my faithful ones,
who cut my covenant over sacrifice!"

[6] And the heavens have told his righteousness,
for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he is judge. Selah.

[7] "Hear, my people, and let me speak;
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and let me testify against you:
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, am I.

[8] Not for your sacrifices do I reprove you,
and your burnt offerings are before me continually.

[9] I will not take a bull from your house,
he-goats from your folds.

[10] For mine is every living thing of the forest,
the beasts on a thousand mountains.

[11] I know every bird of the mountains,
and what moves in the field is with me.

[12] If I were hungry, I would not say it to you,
for mine is the world and its fullness.

[13] Do I eat the flesh of mighty ones,
and drink the blood of he-goats?

[14] Sacrifice thanksgiving to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and repay your vows to ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧,

[15] and call me in the day of distress;
I will pull you out, and you shall honor me."

[16] But to the wicked ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has said:
"What is it to you to recount my statutes
and lift up my covenant upon your mouth?

[17] And you — you hate discipline,
and you have thrown my words behind you.

[18] If you saw a thief, you were pleased with him,
and with adulterers is your share.

[19] Your mouth you have sent out into evil,
and your tongue yokes deceit.

[20] You sit, against your brother you speak;
against the son of your mother you lay a blemish.

[21] These things you have done, and I have kept silent;
you thought that I would surely be like you.
I will reprove you
and arrange it before your eyes.

[22] Understand this, please, you who forget ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧,
lest I tear to pieces, and there is none to deliver!

[23] He who sacrifices thanksgiving honors me,
and he who sets a way —
I will make him see the salvation of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 14, 16, 23) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"The mighty God, even the LORD" → "El, *Elohim*, Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל אֱלֹהִים יְהוָה, three divine names, each kept (as Josh 22:22); "Elohim of gods" is not written.
- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v22) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ, the singular form.
- **"A Psalm of Asaph" → "A psalm of Asaph."** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְאָסָף, the start of v1 in the Hebrew. לְאָסָף: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" Asaph; "of" leaves it open.
- **"hath spoken, and called the earth … unto the going down thereof" → "has spoken and called the earth … to its setting"** (v1) — Hebrew דִּבֶּר perfect, וַיִּקְרָא; מְבֹאוֹ "its setting", literally "its coming in".
- **"God hath shined" → "Elohim has shone forth"** (v2) — Hebrew הוֹפִיעַ (as Deut 33:2).
- **"Our God shall come, and shall not keep silence: a fire shall devour before him" → "Let our Elohim come, and let him not keep silent; fire devours before him"** (v3) — Hebrew יָבֹא … וְאַל־יֶחֱרַשׁ: אַל is the negative of a wish, so both verbs are taken as wishes (יָבֹא may also be a plain "comes", and וְאַל a strong "and he will not"). חָרַשׁ is kept "keep silent" against the word list's "be deaf" (Ps 28:1): here Elohim comes to speak (v1, 7), and v21 has the hifil "I have kept silent" of the same verb. אֵשׁ־לְפָנָיו תֹּאכֵל, "fire before him devours"; וּסְבִיבָיו נִשְׂעֲרָה מְאֹד, impersonal, "and around him it storms greatly".
- **"He shall call to the heavens from above … that he may judge his people" → "He calls to the heavens above … to judge his people"** (v4) — Hebrew יִקְרָא imperfect; לָדִין, infinitive of דִּין "judge" (beside שֹׁפֵט v6).
- **"Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice" → "Gather to me my faithful ones, who cut my covenant over sacrifice"** (v5) — Hebrew חֲסִידַי "my faithful ones" (חָסִיד, one who keeps *chesed*); כֹּרְתֵי בְרִיתִי עֲלֵי־זָבַח, "the cutters of my covenant upon sacrifice": כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant", "my covenant" (not "with me"), עֲלֵי "upon, over".
- **"And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God is judge himself" → "And the heavens have told his righteousness, for Elohim, he is judge"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיַּגִּידוּ, wayyiqtol of הִגִּיד "tell"; כִּי־אֱלֹהִים שֹׁפֵט הוּא, "for Elohim — judge is he" (שֹׁפֵט also "judging").
- **"I will speak … I will testify against thee: I am God, even thy God" → "let me speak … let me testify against you: Elohim, your Elohim, am I"** (v7) — Hebrew וַאֲדַבֵּרָה … וְאָעִידָה בָּךְ, cohortatives; הֵעִיד בְּ "testify against" (the court of v4–6; elsewhere "warn", Exod 19:21): kept against the word list's "warn" for the sense of a witness in a suit. אֱלֹהִים אֱלֹהֶיךָ אָנֹכִי, "I am" last, as in the Hebrew.
- **"I will not reprove thee for thy sacrifices or thy burnt offerings, to have been continually before me" → "Not for your sacrifices do I reprove you, and your burnt offerings are before me continually"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא עַל־זְבָחֶיךָ אוֹכִיחֶךָ וְעוֹלֹתֶיךָ לְנֶגְדִּי תָמִיד: "not for your sacrifices" first; the second clause is its own, with no verb (copula); הוֹכִיחַ "reprove".
- **"I will take no bullock … nor he goats" → "I will not take a bull … he-goats"** (v9) — Hebrew לֹא־אֶקַּח, לָקַח "take" (not "accept"); פַּר "bull"; עַתּוּדִים "he-goats" (as v13); no "nor".
- **"For every beast of the forest is mine, and the cattle upon a thousand hills" → "For mine is every living thing of the forest, the beasts on a thousand mountains"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי־לִי כָל־חַיְתוֹ־יָעַר: "mine" first; חַיָּה "living thing"; בְּהֵמוֹת "beasts" (not "cattle"); בְּהַרְרֵי־אָלֶף, literally "on the mountains of a thousand"; no "and".
- **"I know all the fowls of the mountains: and the wild beasts of the field are mine" → "I know every bird of the mountains, and what moves in the field is with me"** (v11) — Hebrew כָּל־עוֹף הָרִים; וְזִיז שָׂדַי עִמָּדִי: זִיז only here and Ps 80:13, "what moves" (uncertain); עִמָּדִי "with me" (KJV "mine").
- **"I would not tell thee: for the world is mine, and the fulness thereof" → "I would not say it to you, for mine is the world and its fullness"** (v12) — Hebrew לֹא־אֹמַר לָךְ, אָמַר "say", no object in the Hebrew ("it" supplied); כִּי־לִי תֵבֵל וּמְלֹאָהּ, "mine" first; מְלֹא "fullness".
- **"Will I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?" → "Do I eat the flesh of mighty ones, and drink the blood of he-goats?"** (v13) — Hebrew הַאוֹכַל בְּשַׂר אַבִּירִים: אַבִּירִים "mighty ones", a word for strong animals, here bulls (as Ps 22:12 "mighty ones of Bashan"; Isa 34:7); וְדַם "and the blood".
- **"Offer unto God thanksgiving; and pay thy vows unto the most High" → "Sacrifice thanksgiving to Elohim, and repay your vows to Elyon"** (v14) — Hebrew זְבַח, the verb זָבַח "sacrifice"; תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving" (the offering of thanks); שַׁלֵּם "repay" (piel of the root of שָׁלוֹם); עֶלְיוֹן *Elyon*.
- **"And call upon me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me" → "and call me in the day of distress; I will pull you out, and you shall honor me"** (v15) — Hebrew וּקְרָאֵנִי; צָרָה "distress"; אֲחַלֶּצְךָ, חִלֵּץ "pull out" (הִצִּיל "deliver" stands in v22); כִּבֵּד "honor" (as v23).
- **"What hast thou to do to declare my statutes, or that thou shouldest take my covenant in thy mouth?" → "What is it to you to recount my statutes and lift up my covenant upon your mouth?"** (v16) — Hebrew אָמַר perfect; מַה־לְּךָ לְסַפֵּר חֻקָּי, "what to you, to recount my statutes"; וַתִּשָּׂא בְרִיתִי עֲלֵי־פִיךָ, "and you have lifted up my covenant upon your mouth" (a wayyiqtol; "and" not "or").
- **"Seeing thou hatest instruction, and castest my words behind thee" → "And you — you hate discipline, and you have thrown my words behind you"** (v17) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה שָׂנֵאתָ מוּסָר, the pronoun set out; no "seeing"; מוּסָר "discipline"; הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw".
- **"When thou sawest a thief, then thou consentedst with him, and hast been partaker with adulterers" → "If you saw a thief, you were pleased with him, and with adulterers is your share"** (v18) — Hebrew וַתִּרֶץ עִמּוֹ, as pointed from רָצָה "be pleased with"; read from רוּץ it would be "you ran with him" (the Septuagint, from memory): the MT is kept. וְעִם מְנָאֲפִים חֶלְקֶךָ, חֵלֶק "share", no verb.
- **"Thou givest thy mouth to evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit" → "Your mouth you have sent out into evil, and your tongue yokes deceit"** (v19) — Hebrew פִּיךָ שָׁלַחְתָּ בְרָעָה, שָׁלַח "send", perfect; תַּצְמִיד, hifil of צמד (only here; צֶמֶד "a yoke, a pair"), "yoke, bind together" (KJV "frame"); מִרְמָה "deceit".
- **"Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother's son" → "You sit, against your brother you speak; against the son of your mother you lay a blemish"** (v20) — Hebrew תֵּשֵׁב בְּאָחִיךָ תְדַבֵּר, no "and"; דִּבֶּר בְּ "speak against"; תִּתֶּן־דֹּפִי, "you give a blemish": דֹּפִי only here, "fault, blemish" (= slander).
- **"thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes" → "you thought that I would surely be like you. I will reprove you and arrange it before your eyes"** (v21) — Hebrew וְהֶחֱרַשְׁתִּי, the verb "keep silent" of v3; דִּמִּיתָ "you thought" (as 48:9); הֱיוֹת־אֶהְיֶה כָמוֹךָ, infinitive absolute with אֶהְיֶה "I will be" ("surely"; literally "being I will be"); no "but", no "now". וְאֶעֶרְכָה, cohortative of עָרַךְ "arrange" (of a case set out in order); the object is not written ("it" supplied).
- **"Now consider this … lest I tear you in pieces" → "Understand this, please … lest I tear to pieces"** (v22) — Hebrew בִּינוּ־נָא זֹאת, נָא "please"; פֶּן־אֶטְרֹף, טָרַף "tear to pieces", no object; וְאֵין מַצִּיל "and there is none delivering".
- **"Whoso offereth praise glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his conversation aright will I shew the salvation of God" → "He who sacrifices thanksgiving honors me, and he who sets a way — I will make him see the salvation of Elohim"** (v23) — Hebrew זֹבֵחַ תּוֹדָה יְכַבְּדָנְנִי: זָבַח "sacrifice", תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving" (as v14), כִּבֵּד "honor". וְשָׂם דֶּרֶךְ, "and one who sets a way" (MT; "rightly" is not in the Hebrew; read וְשָׁם "and there" or תָּם "blameless" by some, from memory); אַרְאֶנּוּ בְּיֵשַׁע אֱלֹהִים, "I will make him look upon the salvation of Elohim".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours (the heading is the start of v1).
- **Asaph psalm** — first of 12 (Pss 50, 73-83).
- **Divine trial** — Yahweh's case against his people.
- **"The beasts on a thousand mountains"** (v10) — Yahweh needs no sacrifice.
