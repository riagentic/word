# Psalm 37

Of David.

[1] Do not burn with anger because of evildoers;
do not envy the doers of injustice.

[2] For like the grass they will quickly wither,
and like the green of vegetation they will fade.

[3] Trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and do good;
abide in the land and feed on faithfulness.

[4] And delight yourself in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and he will give you the requests of your heart.

[5] Roll your way upon ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and trust in him, and he will act.

[6] And he will bring out your righteousness like the light,
and your judgment like the noonday.

[7] Be silent for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and wait longingly for him;
do not burn with anger over the one who makes his way prosper,
over a man who carries out schemes.

[8] Let go of anger and forsake fury;
do not burn with anger — only to do evil.

[9] For evildoers shall be cut off,
but those who wait for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — they shall possess the land.

[10] And yet a little while, and the wicked is no more;
and you will look closely at his place, and he is not.

[11] But the humble shall possess the land
and delight themselves in the abundance of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[12] The wicked devises against the righteous
and gnashes his teeth at him.

[13] ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ laughs at him,
for he has seen that his day is coming.

[14] A sword the wicked have drawn, and they have bent their bow,
to make the afflicted and the needy fall,
to slaughter the upright of way.

[15] Their sword shall go into their heart,
and their bows shall be broken.

[16] Better is a little for the righteous
than the multitude of many wicked.

[17] For the arms of the wicked shall be broken,
but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ upholds the righteous.

[18] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ knows the days of the blameless,
and their inheritance shall be forever.

[19] They shall not be ashamed in a time of evil,
and in the days of famine they shall be sated.

[20] For the wicked shall perish,
and the enemies of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are like the splendor of the pastures;
they vanish — in smoke they vanish.

[21] The wicked borrows and does not repay,
but the righteous shows favor and gives.

[22] For those blessed by him shall possess the land,
but those cursed by him shall be cut off.

[23] From ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the steps of a man are established,
and in his way he delights.

[24] Though he falls, he shall not be hurled down,
for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ upholds his hand.

[25] A young man I have been, and also I have grown old,
and I have not seen the righteous forsaken,
or his seed seeking bread.

[26] All the day he shows favor and lends,
and his seed is for a blessing.

[27] Turn aside from evil and do good,
and abide forever.

[28] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loves judgment,
and he will not forsake his faithful ones.
Forever they are kept,
but the seed of the wicked is cut off.

[29] The righteous shall possess the land
and abide upon it perpetually.

[30] The mouth of the righteous softly recites wisdom,
and his tongue speaks judgment.

[31] The ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in his heart;
his steps do not slip.

[32] The wicked watches for the righteous
and seeks to put him to death.

[33] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will not leave him in his hand,
and will not condemn him when he is judged.

[34] Wait for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and keep his way,
and he will raise you high to possess the land;
when the wicked are cut off, you will see it.

[35] I have seen a wicked man, ruthless,
and spreading himself out like a luxuriant native tree.

[36] And he passed, and behold, he was not;
and I sought him, and he was not found.

[37] Watch the blameless and see the upright,
for there is a future for the man of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[38] But transgressors are destroyed together;
the future of the wicked is cut off.

[39] And the salvation of the righteous is from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
their strong place in the time of distress.

[40] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ helped them and rescued them;
he rescues them from the wicked and saves them,
for they have taken refuge in him.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v31) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 17, 18, 20, 23, 24, 28, 33, 34, 39, 40) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "Of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לְדָוִד only; the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Fret not thyself" → "Do not burn with anger"** (v1, 7, 8) — Hebrew אַל־תִּתְחַר, hitpael of חָרָה "burn (with anger)": literally "do not heat yourself". "Fret" (worry) is not in the word.
- **"neither be thou envious against the workers of iniquity" → "do not envy the doers of injustice"** (v1) — Hebrew קִנֵּא בְּ "envy" (of a rival); עַוְלָה "injustice" (not עָוֹן "iniquity").
- **"they shall soon be cut down … and wither" → "they will quickly wither … they will fade"** (v2) — Hebrew יִמָּלוּ, from מָלַל "wither" (taken by KJV from a root "cut off"; uncertain), and יִבּוֹלוּן "fade" (נָבֵל). יֶרֶק דֶּשֶׁא "the green of vegetation".
- **"verily thou shalt be fed" → "feed on faithfulness"** (v3) — Hebrew וּרְעֵה אֱמוּנָה, an imperative of רָעָה "pasture, feed" with אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness" as its object, with no preposition: "feed on" (as a flock), or "shepherd faithfulness"; others take רָעָה "be a friend" ("befriend faithfulness"). שְׁכָן "abide" (שָׁכַן); "in" supplied.
- **"the desires of thine heart" → "the requests of your heart"** (v4) — Hebrew מִשְׁאֲלֹת, from שָׁאַל "ask". The verse opens with "And" (וְ).
- **"Commit thy way unto the LORD" → "Roll your way upon Yahweh"** (v5) — Hebrew גּוֹל עַל, "roll upon" (KJV margin "roll"). וְהוּא יַעֲשֶׂה "and he will act" (lit. "he will do"; no object in the Hebrew: "bring it to pass" adds one).
- **"he shall bring forth … thy judgment" → "he will bring out … your judgment"** (v6) — Hebrew הוֹצִיא "bring out"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment".
- **"Rest in the LORD, and wait patiently for him" → "Be silent for Yahweh and wait longingly for him"** (v7) — Hebrew דּוֹם לַיהוָה, "be silent / still to Yahweh"; הִתְחוֹלֵל לוֹ, from the root חִיל "writhe": "wait longingly / in anguish" (uncertain).
- **"him who prospereth in his way … the man who bringeth wicked devices to pass" → "the one who makes his way prosper … a man who carries out schemes"** (v7) — Hebrew מַצְלִיחַ דַּרְכּוֹ, hifil: "makes his way prosper"; מְזִמּוֹת "schemes" (no word "wicked"). אִישׁ "man".
- **"Cease from anger" → "Let go of anger"; "wrath" → "fury"; "in any wise to do evil" → "only to do evil"** (v8) — Hebrew הֶרֶף מֵאַף, hifil of רָפָה with מִן; חֵמָה "fury" (lit. "heat"); אַךְ־לְהָרֵעַ "only to do evil", without a verb: the burning leads only to evil-doing, or "it is only for doing evil".
- **"shall inherit the earth" → "shall possess the land"** (v9, 11, 22, 29, 34) — Hebrew יָרַשׁ אֶרֶץ, "take possession of the land"; אֶרֶץ without the article, of the land promised (as v3 "abide in the land"). Matt 5:5 has Greek *klēronomēsousin tēn gēn* "shall inherit the earth" (cited from v11).
- **"For yet a little while … yea, thou shalt diligently consider his place, and it shall not be" → "And yet a little while … and he is not"** (v10) — Hebrew וְעוֹד מְעַט, "and yet a little" ("while" supplied); וְהִתְבּוֹנַנְתָּ "and you will look closely"; וְאֵינֶנּוּ "and he is not" (of the wicked, not of the place).
- **"the meek" → "the humble"** (v11) — Hebrew עֲנָוִים (the עָנָו row: "humble", bowed low); Matt 5:5 has Greek *praeis* "meek". וְהִתְעַנְּגוּ "delight themselves" (as v4); שָׁלוֹם *shalom*.
- **"plotteth against the just" → "devises against the righteous"** (v12) — Hebrew זֹמֵם "devise"; צַדִּיק "righteous".
- **"The Lord shall laugh at him" → "Adonai laughs at him"; "seeth" → "has seen"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי (*Adonai*); לוֹ "at him" (not "the wicked"); רָאָה perfect.
- **"The wicked have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, and to slay such as be of upright conversation" → "A sword the wicked have drawn, and they have bent their bow, to make the afflicted and the needy fall, to slaughter the upright of way"** (v14) — Hebrew חֶרֶב פָּתְחוּ, "a sword they have opened" (= drawn), set first; דָּרְכוּ קַשְׁתָּם "trodden their bow" (= bent it, by stepping on it to string it), singular; לְהַפִּיל "to make fall"; עָנִי "afflicted"; לִטְבוֹחַ "to slaughter"; יִשְׁרֵי־דָרֶךְ "the upright of way".
- **"their own heart" → "their heart"** (v15) — Hebrew בְלִבָּם, no "own"; תָּבוֹא "shall go into".
- **"A little that a righteous man hath is better than the riches of many wicked" → "Better is a little for the righteous than the multitude of many wicked"** (v16) — Hebrew הֲמוֹן "multitude" (abundance, of goods or of people; "riches" is not the word).
- **"their inheritance shall be for ever" → "their inheritance shall be forever"** (v18) — Hebrew נַחֲלָה "inheritance"; תְמִימִם "the blameless".
- **"They shall not be ashamed in the evil time: and in the days of famine they shall be satisfied" → "They shall not be ashamed in a time of evil, and in the days of famine they shall be sated"** (v19) — Hebrew בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed" (the Ps 23–27 row); בְּעֵת רָעָה, "in a time of evil"; שָׂבַע "be sated".
- **"But the wicked shall perish … as the fat of lambs: they shall consume; into smoke shall they consume away" → "For the wicked shall perish … like the splendor of the pastures; they vanish — in smoke they vanish"** (v20) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; כִּיקַר כָּרִים, "like the preciousness / splendor of כָּרִים": כַּר is "pasture" and also "lamb" (KJV "the fat of lambs", taking יְקָר as the choice part): uncertain. כָּלוּ בֶעָשָׁן כָּלוּ, "they are finished, in smoke they are finished", perfect; בְּ "in", not "like".
- **"sheweth mercy, and giveth" → "shows favor and gives"** (v21, 26) — Hebrew חוֹנֵן (the חָנַן row: "show favor"); יְשַׁלֵּם "repay".
- **"such as be blessed of him … they that be cursed of him" → "those blessed by him … those cursed by him"** (v22) — Hebrew מְבֹרָכָיו … וּמְקֻלָּלָיו, "his blessed ones … his cursed ones". The Hebrew has no name: "him" is Yahweh (v20) or the righteous of v21 (Gen 12:3); left open. The token "Yahweh" that stood here is removed.
- **"The steps of a good man are ordered by the LORD: and he delighteth in his way" → "From Yahweh the steps of a man are established, and in his way he delights"** (v23) — Hebrew מֵיְהוָה "from Yahweh", set first; גֶּבֶר "man" (standing alone, no other word for man beside it; there is no "good"); כּוֹנָנוּ "are established"; who delights in whose way (Yahweh in the man's, or the man in Yahweh's) is not said: no "when".
- **"he shall not be utterly cast down" → "he shall not be hurled down"** (v24) — Hebrew לֹא־יוּטָל, hofal of טוּל "hurl". סוֹמֵךְ יָדוֹ "upholds his hand" (or "upholds with his hand").
- **"I have been young, and now am old … nor his seed begging bread" → "A young man I have been, and also I have grown old … or his seed seeking bread"** (v25) — Hebrew נַעַר הָיִיתִי, the noun set first (the נַעַר row); גַּם "also"; זֶרַע "seed" (also v26, 28); מְבַקֶּשׁ "seeking" (בִּקֵּשׁ).
- **"He is ever merciful … and his seed is blessed" → "All the day he shows favor and lends, and his seed is for a blessing"** (v26) — Hebrew כָּל־הַיּוֹם "all the day"; לִבְרָכָה "for a blessing".
- **"Depart from evil … and dwell for evermore" → "Turn aside from evil … and abide forever"** (v27) — Hebrew סוּר "turn aside"; וּשְׁכֹן, a third imperative, "and abide" (not "so shall you dwell").
- **"judgment … his saints; they are preserved for ever: but the seed of the wicked shall be cut off" → "judgment … his faithful ones. Forever they are kept, but the seed of the wicked is cut off"** (v28) — Hebrew חֲסִידָיו "his faithful ones" (no token); נִשְׁמָרוּ perfect nifal of שָׁמַר "keep"; נִכְרָת "is cut off". The Septuagint has here, from memory, a line on the lawless driven out that would give the missing ע strophe (Hebrew *ʿawwalim nishmadu*); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"dwell therein for ever" → "abide upon it perpetually"** (v29) — Hebrew וְיִשְׁכְּנוּ לָעַד עָלֶיהָ; לָעַד "to perpetuity", another word than לְעוֹלָם "forever" (v18, 27, 28); עָלֶיהָ "upon it".
- **"speaketh wisdom … talketh of judgment" → "softly recites wisdom … speaks judgment"** (v30) — Hebrew יֶהְגֶּה, הָגָה "softly recite" (low speech, as Ps 1:2; KJV "speaketh"); תְּדַבֵּר מִשְׁפָּט "speaks judgment".
- **"none of his steps shall slide" → "his steps do not slip"** (v31) — Hebrew לֹא תִמְעַד אֲשֻׁרָיו, a singular verb with the plural noun ("not one of his steps slips"). תּוֹרָה *torah*.
- **"seeketh to slay him" → "seeks to put him to death"** (v32) — Hebrew לַהֲמִיתוֹ, hifil of מוּת.
- **"will not leave him in his hand, nor condemn him when he is judged" → "will not leave him in his hand, and will not condemn him when he is judged"** (v33) — Hebrew לֹא־יַעַזְבֶנּוּ בְיָדוֹ "will not leave (forsake) him in his hand"; "power" interprets יָד. יַרְשִׁיעֶנּוּ "condemn him".
- **"shall exalt thee to inherit the land: when the wicked are cut off, thou shalt see it" → "will raise you high to possess the land; when the wicked are cut off, you will see it"** (v34) — Hebrew וִירוֹמִמְךָ "raise you high" (רוֹמֵם); תִּרְאֶה "you will see" ("it" supplied).
- **"in great power, and spreading himself like a green bay tree" → "ruthless, and spreading himself out like a luxuriant native tree"** (v35) — Hebrew עָרִיץ "ruthless"; מִתְעָרֶה, hitpael of עָרָה "lay bare, pour out": "spreading himself out" (uncertain); כְּאֶזְרָח רַעֲנָן, "like a native, luxuriant": אֶזְרָח is "native" (one born in the land), "tree" supplied for English. The Septuagint has, from memory, "like the cedars of Lebanon". "man" supplied: רָשָׁע "a wicked one".
- **"Yet he passed away, and, lo, he was not: yea, I sought him, but he could not be found" → "And he passed, and behold, he was not; and I sought him, and he was not found"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבֹר "and he passed" (some old versions, from memory, have "I passed by"; the Masoretic Text is kept); הִנֵּה "behold"; נִמְצָא "was found".
- **"Mark the perfect man, and behold the upright: for the end of that man is peace" → "Watch the blameless and see the upright, for there is a future for the man of shalom"** (v37) — Hebrew שְׁמָר־תָּם, שָׁמַר "keep, watch" (here of watching a person: "keep" would say guard); תָּם "blameless" (no "man"); כִּי־אַחֲרִית לְאִישׁ שָׁלוֹם, "for there is an after (a future) for the man of *shalom*". אַחֲרִית is "what comes after": future, end, or posterity (as זֶרַע v28); the KJV reads "the end of the man is peace".
- **"shall be destroyed together: the end of the wicked shall be cut off" → "are destroyed together … the future of the wicked is cut off"** (v38) — Hebrew נִשְׁמְדוּ … נִכְרָתָה, two perfects, given as English present (what is sure to happen); יַחְדָּו "together"; פֹשְׁעִים "transgressors".
- **"he is their strength in the time of trouble" → "their strong place in the time of distress"** (v39) — Hebrew מָעוּזָּם "their strong place" (מָעוֹז), standing beside "Yahweh" with no verb; צָרָה "distress". The verse opens with "And" (וּ).
- **"And the LORD shall help them, and deliver them … because they trust in him" → "And Yahweh helped them and rescued them; he rescues them … for they have taken refuge in him"** (v40) — Hebrew וַיַּעְזְרֵם … וַיְפַלְּטֵם, two wayyiqtol forms (past), then the imperfect יְפַלְּטֵם "he rescues them"; פִּלֵּט "rescue"; הוֹשִׁיעַ "save"; חָסוּ בוֹ, perfect, "they have taken refuge in him".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew: the verse numbers are the same.
- **Alphabetic acrostic** — each strophe (mostly two verses) opens with the next letter of the alphabet: א v1, ב v3, ג v5, ד v7, ה v8, ו v10, ז v12, ח v14, ט v16, י v18, כ v20, ל v21, מ v23, נ v25, ס v27, (ע v28b, where לְעוֹלָם has ע as its second letter), פ v30, צ v32, ק v34, ר v35, ש v37, ת v39. The English does not imitate the scheme.
- **"The humble shall possess the land"** (v11) — cited by Jesus (Matt 5:5, Greek *praeis* "meek", *tēn gēn* "the earth").
- **Wisdom psalm** — long counsel of patience against envy of the wicked.
