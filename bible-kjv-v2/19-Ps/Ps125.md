# Psalm 125

A song of the ascents.

[1] Those who trust in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are like Mount ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧:
it shall not be shaken; forever it dwells.

[2] ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ — mountains are around her,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is around his people,
from now and forever.

[3] For the scepter of wickedness shall not rest
on the lot of the righteous,
so that the righteous do not stretch out
their hands to injustice.

[4] Do good, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, to the good,
and to the upright in their hearts.

[5] But those who turn aside to their winding ways,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will make them walk with the workers of wickedness.
⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧ upon ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 4, 5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees" → "A song of the ascents"** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת, the first words of v1; מַעֲלָה "a going up, a step" (from עָלָה "go up"), with the article. Whether the ascents are the pilgrims' going up to Jerusalem or steps is not said.
- **"shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever" → "are like Mount Zion: it shall not be shaken; forever it dwells"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּהַר־צִיּוֹן, no verb (a copula supplied); לֹא־יִמּוֹט, מוֹט nifal "be shaken" (as Ps 15:5); לְעוֹלָם יֵשֵׁב "forever it dwells" (יָשַׁב "dwell, sit": of a mountain, "it stays seated"). The accents join "forever" to "dwells"; "shall not be shaken forever" is the other reading. No "but" in the Hebrew.
- **"As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people" → "Jerusalem — mountains are around her, and Yahweh is around his people"** (v2) — Hebrew יְרוּשָׁלִַם הָרִים סָבִיב לָהּ וַיהוָה סָבִיב לְעַמּוֹ: Jerusalem stands first, then "mountains around her" (no article, no "as … so"); וְ "and". **"from henceforth even for ever" → "from now and forever"** — Hebrew מֵעַתָּה וְעַד־עוֹלָם, literally "from now and until the age".
- **"the rod of the wicked" → "the scepter of wickedness"** (v3) — Hebrew שֵׁבֶט הָרֶשַׁע: שֵׁבֶט "rod", the rod of one who rules (as Gen 49:10); רֶשַׁע "wickedness", a noun. גּוֹרָל "lot" (the share given by lot). **"lest the righteous put forth their hands unto iniquity" → "so that the righteous do not stretch out their hands to injustice"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן לֹא, "so that … not"; שָׁלַח יָד "stretch out the hand"; עַוְלָתָה, עַוְלָה "injustice" (with a lengthened ending).
- **"Do good, O LORD, unto those that be good, and to them that are upright in their hearts" → "Do good, Yahweh, to the good, and to the upright in their hearts"** (v4) — Hebrew הֵיטִיבָה, imperative of הֵיטִיב "do good"; no "O" (no such word in the Hebrew); טוֹבִים "good ones", יְשָׁרִים "upright ones" (בְּלִבּוֹתָם "in their hearts", plural).
- **"As for such as turn aside unto their crooked ways, the LORD shall lead them forth with the workers of iniquity" → "But those who turn aside to their winding ways, Yahweh will make them walk with the workers of wickedness"** (v5) — Hebrew וְהַמַּטִּים עֲקַלְקַלּוֹתָם: נָטָה hifil "turn aside", with עֲקַלְקַלּוֹת "windings" as its object or goal ("to" and "ways" supplied; the word as Judg 5:6). יוֹלִיכֵם, hifil of הָלַךְ "make walk" (as Deut 8:2); the form can also be a wish, "may Yahweh make them walk". אֶת "with"; פֹּעֲלֵי הָאָוֶן "the workers of wickedness" (אָוֶן). **"but peace shall be upon Israel" → "Shalom upon Israel!"** — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל, no verb and no "but".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew too; the numbers agree.
- **Poetic structure** — the stability of those who trust (v1–3), prayer for the good and the end of the crooked (v4–5).
