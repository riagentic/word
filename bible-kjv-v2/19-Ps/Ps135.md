## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 13, 14, 19, 20, 21) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v1, 3, 21) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ־יָהּ, literally "praise Yah" (in v1 written as two words), with the `praise-the-lord` token each time; in v3 the same word opens the sentence "for Yahweh is good" (the earlier "Praise Yah" there is now the token). יָהּ alone (v4) keeps the `yah` token.
- **"praise him, O ye servants of the LORD" → "praise, servants of Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ עַבְדֵי יְהוָה: no "him", no "O". עֶבֶד "servant" of Yahweh's men (its row: the word is "slave"; also v9 of Pharaoh's, v14).
- **"Ye that stand in the house of the LORD" → "who stand in the house of Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew שֶׁעֹמְדִים, a participle with the relative שֶׁ. אֱלֹהֵינוּ "our Elohim".
- **"sing praises unto his name; for it is pleasant" → "make music to his name, for it is pleasant"** (v3) — Hebrew זַמְּרוּ "make music" (זִמֵּר); כִּי נָעִים "for (it is) pleasant": the subject is not written — the name, the music, or Yahweh: uncertain. נָעִים "pleasant" (its row).
- **"For the LORD hath chosen Jacob unto himself, and Israel for his peculiar treasure" → "For Jacob Yah has chosen for himself, Israel for his treasured possession"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי־יַעֲקֹב בָּחַר לוֹ יָהּ: "Jacob" first, as the Hebrew stands; no "and". סְגֻלָּה "treasured possession" (its row; Exod 19:5; Deut 7:6).
- **"and that our Lord is above all gods" → "and our Lord more than all gods"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי אֲנִי יָדַעְתִּי "for I, I know" (the pronoun written: emphasis); וַאֲדֹנֵינוּ מִכָּל־אֱלֹהִים: מִן of comparison, "more than" (= greater than), the "great" of the first line carried over; "above" is not the word. אֲדֹנֵינוּ "our Lord" (the plural of majesty of אָדוֹן with "our"; not the name אֲדֹנָי). אֱלֹהִים "gods" (plural sense; plain text).
- **"Whatsoever the LORD pleased, that did he" → "All that Yahweh delights in he does"** (v6) — Hebrew כֹּל אֲשֶׁר־חָפֵץ … עָשָׂה, two perfects of a general truth ("has delighted in … has done"); חָפֵץ "delight in" (its row). בַּשָּׁמַיִם וּבָאָרֶץ "in the heavens and in the earth" (שָׁמַיִם plural); וְכָל־תְּהֹמוֹת "and all deeps" (no article; תְּהוֹם "deep").
- **"He causeth the vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth; he maketh lightnings for the rain; he bringeth the wind out of his treasuries" → "He makes vapors go up from the end of the earth; lightnings for the rain he makes; he brings out the wind from his treasuries"** (v7) — Hebrew מַעֲלֶה נְשִׂאִים: a participle, "making go up"; נְשִׂאִים "vapors" (literally "things lifted up"; "clouds" in other versions; the same word means "princes"). מִקְצֵה "from the end" (singular; קָצֶה row). עָשָׂה a perfect, in the Hebrew order. מוֹצֵא a participle, "bringing out"; אוֹצָר "treasury" (its row; not "storehouse"). The verse stands also in Jer 10:13; 51:16 (from memory).
- **"Who smote the firstborn of Egypt, both of man and beast" → "It is he who struck down the firstborn of Egypt, from man to beast"** (v8) — Hebrew שֶׁהִכָּה "who struck" (a relative clause with no head word, going back to Yahweh of v5–6; "It is he" supplied for English, also v10) (הִכָּה; "struck down" where killing is meant, its row; also v10); מֵאָדָם עַד־בְּהֵמָה "from man to beast" (its row).
- **"Who sent tokens and wonders into the midst of thee, O Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his servants" → "He sent signs and wonders into your midst, Egypt, against Pharaoh and against all his servants"** (v9) — Hebrew שָׁלַח without the relative שֶׁ (v8, 10 have it): a main clause. אוֹתוֹת וּמֹפְתִים "signs and wonders" (אוֹת · מוֹפֵת rows), in the Hebrew order before "into your midst". בְּתוֹכֵכִי "into your midst" (an old feminine form, Egypt addressed); no "O". בְּפַרְעֹה וּבְכָל־עֲבָדָיו: בְּ before each, "against … and against".
- **"Who smote great nations, and slew mighty kings" → "It is he who struck down many nations and killed mighty kings"** (v10) — Hebrew גּוֹיִם רַבִּים "many nations"; וְהָרַג "and killed" (הָרַג "kill"); עֲצוּמִים "mighty" (עָצוּם, its row).
- **"Sihon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan"** (v11) — kept: each name has לְ before it (לְסִיחוֹן … וּלְעוֹג … וּלְכֹל), the late mark of the object of "killed"; not "to".
- **"And gave their land for an heritage, an heritage unto Israel his people" → "and he gave their land as an inheritance, an inheritance to Israel his people"** (v12) — Hebrew וְנָתַן "and he gave"; נַחֲלָה "inheritance" (its row), twice.
- **"Thy name, O LORD, endureth for ever; and thy memorial, O LORD, throughout all generations" → "Yahweh, your name is forever; Yahweh, your remembrance is to generation and generation"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהוָה שִׁמְךָ לְעוֹלָם יְהוָה זִכְרְךָ לְדֹר־וָדֹר: no verb ("endures" supplied; "is" only), no "and", no "O"; the name first in each line, as the Hebrew. זֵכֶר "remembrance" (its row), as Exod 3:15 "This is my name forever, and this is my remembrance from generation to generation" (Exodus has לְדֹר דֹּר without וְ; here לְדֹר וָדֹר "to generation and generation").
- **"For the LORD will judge his people, and he will repent himself concerning his servants" → "For Yahweh will judge his people, and for his servants he will be sorry"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי־יָדִין יְהוָה עַמּוֹ וְעַל־עֲבָדָיו יִתְנֶחָם, word for word Deut 32:36a; the wording of Deut 32:36. דִּין "judge" (its row; here to do right by them); הִתְנֶחָם עַל "be sorry for" (the נִחַם row: to have pity, or to relent).
- **"The idols of the heathen are silver and gold, the work of men's hands" → "The idols of the nations are silver and gold, the work of the hands of man"** (v15) — Hebrew עֲצַבֵּי הַגּוֹיִם "the idols of the nations" (גּוֹי "nation"); מַעֲשֵׂה יְדֵי אָדָם "the work of the hands of man" (as Deut 4:28). Ps 115:4–8 has the same lines with differences (115:4 "their idols"; more members in 115:5–7); the shared words have the wording of Ps 115 (135:16 = 115:5; 135:18 = 115:8).
- **"They have mouths, but they speak not; eyes have they, but they see not" → "A mouth they have, and they do not speak; eyes they have, and they do not see"** (v16) — Hebrew פֶּה־לָהֶם "a mouth (is) to them", singular; וְלֹא "and not" (not "but"); the Hebrew order.
- **"They have ears, but they hear not; neither is there any breath in their mouths" → "ears they have, and they do not give ear; also there is no breath in their mouth"** (v17) — Hebrew יַאֲזִינוּ, hifil of the root of אֹזֶן "ear": "give ear" (הֶאֱזִין, its row; not שָׁמַע "hear", as Ps 115:6). אַף "also, yea" (here not "nose", as in 115:6); אֵין־יֶשׁ "there is not" (both words: "there is no … at all"); רוּחַ "breath" (its row: one word for spirit, wind, breath); בְּפִיהֶם "in their mouth", singular noun.
- **"They that make them are like unto them: so is every one that trusteth in them" → "Like them shall be those who make them, everyone who trusts in them"** (v18) — Hebrew כְּמוֹהֶם יִהְיוּ עֹשֵׂיהֶם: יִהְיוּ an imperfect, "shall be" (or a wish, "let them be": the form does not decide); "Like them" first, as the Hebrew. כֹּל אֲשֶׁר־בֹּטֵחַ בָּהֶם "everyone who trusts in them": "so is" is supplied in other versions.
- **"Bless the LORD, O house of Israel" → "House of Israel, bless Yahweh"** (v19–20) — Hebrew בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל בָּרֲכוּ אֶת־יְהוָה, the address first; no "O".
- **"Bless the LORD, O house of Levi" → "house of the Levite, bless Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew בֵּית הַלֵּוִי, with the article: "the Levite" (the tribe named as one man), with the `levites` token, as Deut 14:27. יִרְאֵי יְהוָה "fearers of Yahweh" ("you who fear").
- **"Blessed be the LORD out of Zion, which dwelleth at Jerusalem" → "Blessed is Yahweh from Zion, who abides in Jerusalem"** (v21) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה, no verb: "is" (the בָּרוּךְ row; as Ps 106:48). שֹׁכֵן "who abides" (שָׁכַן, its row; beside יָשַׁב "dwell").

### Notes
- **No heading** — the psalm begins with הַלְלוּ־יָהּ; the Hebrew verse numbers are ours.
- **Shared lines** — v7 = Jer 10:13; 51:16; v14 = Deut 32:36a; v15–18 = Ps 115:4–8 (with differences); v19–20 beside Ps 115:9–13; 118:2–4 (from memory).
