# Psalm 135

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Praise the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
praise him, O servants of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[2] who stand in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
in the courts of the house of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!

[3] Praise ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is good;
sing to his name, for it is pleasant!

[4] For ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧ has chosen ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ for himself,
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ as his own possession.

[5] For I know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is great,
and that our Lord is above all gods.

[6] Whatever ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ pleases, he does,
in heaven and on earth,
in the seas and all deeps.

[7] He it is who makes the clouds rise at the end of the earth,
who makes lightnings for the rain
and brings forth the wind from his storehouses.

[8] He it was who struck down the firstborn of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
both of man and of beast;

[9] who in your midst, O ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
sent signs and wonders
against ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and all his servants;

[10] who struck down many nations
and killed mighty kings —

[11] ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧, king of the ⟦amorites|Emorim|Amorites⟧,
and Og, king of Bashan,
and all the kingdoms of ⟦canaan|Kenaan|Canaan⟧ —

[12] and gave their land as a heritage,
a heritage to his people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[13] Your name, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, endures forever,
your memorial, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, throughout all generations.

[14] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will vindicate his people
and have compassion on his servants.

[15] The idols of the nations are silver and gold,
the work of human hands.

[16] They have mouths, but do not speak;
they have eyes, but do not see;

[17] they have ears, but do not hear,
nor is there any breath in their mouths.

[18] Those who make them become like them,
so do all who trust in them!

[19] O house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
O house of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!

[20] O house of Levi, bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!
You who fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!

[21] Blessed be ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
he who dwells in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧!
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 4, 6, 10, 13, 14, 19, 20, 21) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Yah"** (v3, 4) — shortened Yahweh; retained.
- **"gods" → "*elohim*"** (v5) — Hebrew *elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — praise call (v1-4), Yahweh's uniqueness (v5-7), historical victories (v8-12), idol polemic (v15-18), communal blessing (v19-21).
