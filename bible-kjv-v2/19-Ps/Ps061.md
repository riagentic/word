# Psalm 61

To the chief musician. Upon Neginah. Of David.

[1] Hear my cry, O *Elohim*;
attend to my prayer.

[2] From the end of the earth I call to you
when my heart is faint.
Lead me to the rock
that is higher than I,

[3] for you have been my refuge,
a strong tower against the enemy.

[4] Let me dwell in your tent forever!
Let me take refuge under the shelter of your wings! Selah.

[5] For you, O *Elohim*, have heard my vows;
you have given me the heritage of those who fear your name.

[6] Prolong the life of the king;
may his years endure to all generations!

[7] May he be enthroned forever before *Elohim*;
appoint steadfast love and faithfulness to watch over him!

[8] So will I ever sing praises to your name,
as I perform my vows day after day.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Neginah** — musical term (stringed instrument), retained.
- **Poetic structure** — personal plea from exile/distance (v1-4), royal intercession (v5-8).

### Notes
- **Rock higher than I** — beloved phrase.
- **Royal intercession** — for the king's long reign.
