# Psalm 61

To the overseer, upon ⟦neginah|Neginat|stringed instruments⟧. Of David.

[1] Hear, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my cry;
attend to my prayer.

[2] From the end of the earth to you I call, when my heart grows faint;
to a rock that is higher than I, lead me.

[3] For you have been a refuge to me,
a tower of strength from the face of the enemy.

[4] Let me sojourn in your tent forever;
let me take refuge in the hiding place of your wings. Selah.

[5] For you, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, have heard my vows;
you have given the possession of those who fear your name.

[6] Days upon the days of a king you will add;
his years like generation and generation.

[7] He shall dwell forever before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth appoint, that they may guard him.

[8] So let me make music to your name perpetually,
as I repay my vows day by day.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"To the chief Musician upon Neginah, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer, upon Neginat. Of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל־נְגִינַת לְדָוִד. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). עַל "upon"; נְגִינַת is the singular (construct) of נְגִינָה "stringed music", where Ps 4 has the plural בִּנְגִינוֹת; what "upon" a stringed instrument means here is not known. The heading has no מִזְמוֹר "psalm": only לְדָוִד "Of David" (לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open).
- **No "O"** (v1, 5) — the Hebrew vocatives have none.
- **"Hear my cry, O God" → "Hear, Elohim, my cry"** (v1) — Hebrew שִׁמְעָה אֱלֹהִים רִנָּתִי, in this order; רִנָּה "cry" beside תְּפִלָּה "prayer", as in Solomon's prayer (1 Kgs 8:28). הַקְשִׁיבָה "attend".
- **"when my heart is overwhelmed: lead me to the rock that is higher than I" → "when my heart grows faint; to a rock that is higher than I, lead me"** (v2) — Hebrew בַּעֲטֹף לִבִּי, "in the fainting of my heart" (עָטַף "be faint"). בְּצוּר־יָרוּם מִמֶּנִּי תַנְחֵנִי: no article ("a rock"), "rock" stands first. תַּנְחֵנִי has the form of the imperfect, "you will lead me"; for this verb the jussive looks the same, so the line may be a request (as given) or a statement of trust. Layout: the Hebrew verse has two lines (the break after "faint"), not four.
- **"For thou hast been a shelter for me, and a strong tower from the enemy" → "For you have been a refuge to me, a tower of strength from the face of the enemy"** (v3) — Hebrew מַחְסֶה "refuge" (from חָסָה "take refuge"); מִגְדַּל־עֹז "a tower of strength" (עֹז "strength"); מִפְּנֵי אוֹיֵב "from the face of the enemy" (the tower is a place to flee to from him).
- **"I will abide in thy tabernacle for ever: I will trust in the covert of thy wings" → "Let me sojourn in your tent forever; let me take refuge in the hiding place of your wings"** (v4) — Hebrew אָגוּרָה, cohortative of גּוּר "sojourn" (live as a guest), and אֶחֱסֶה "let me take refuge" (חָסָה, not "trust": that is בָּטַח). אֹהֶל "tent". עוֹלָמִים is a plural, "ages"; "forever" gives its sense. סֵתֶר "hiding place".
- **"thou hast given me the heritage of those that fear thy name" → "you have given the possession of those who fear your name"** (v5) — Hebrew נָתַתָּ יְרֻשַּׁת יִרְאֵי שְׁמֶךָ: "me" is not in the Hebrew. יְרֻשָּׁה "possession" (from יָרַשׁ). שָׁמַעְתָּ לִנְדָרָי "you have heard my vows", a perfect.
- **"Thou wilt prolong the king's life: and his years as many generations" → "Days upon the days of the king you will add; his years like generation and generation"** (v6) — Hebrew יָמִים עַל־יְמֵי־מֶלֶךְ תּוֹסִיף, "days upon the days of a king you will add" (מֶלֶךְ has no article; the king of v7 is meant): תּוֹסִיף is an imperfect (not a jussive). The second line has no verb: שְׁנוֹתָיו כְּמוֹ־דֹר וָדֹר, "his years like generation and generation" (as Ps 33:11).
- **"He shall abide before God for ever: O prepare mercy and truth, which may preserve him" → "He shall dwell forever before Elohim; steadfast love and truth appoint, that they may guard him"** (v7) — Hebrew יֵשֵׁב עוֹלָם, יָשַׁב "dwell"; the form may be imperfect or jussive ("may he dwell"); "sit (enthroned)" is also possible of a king before Elohim. חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת "*chesed* and truth" stand first, as the object. מַן is an unusual form, taken as the imperative of מִנָּה "appoint" (uncertain; some read it as "who?" or remove it, from memory). יִנְצְרֻהוּ, נָצַר "guard".
- **"So will I sing praise unto thy name for ever, that I may daily perform my vows" → "So let me make music to your name perpetually, as I repay my vows day by day"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲזַמְּרָה, cohortative of זִמֵּר "make music" (as Ps 7:17; 9:2). לָעַד "perpetually" (another word than לְעוֹלָם "forever", as Ps 37:29). לְשַׁלְּמִי נְדָרַי "for my repaying of my vows" (שִׁלֵּם "repay", as Ps 22:25); יוֹם יוֹם "day, day" = "day by day".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v8 = Heb v9.
- **Poetic structure** — personal plea from far away (v1–4), prayer for the king (v5–8).
- **Rock higher than I** — beloved phrase.
- **Royal intercession** — for the king's long reign.
