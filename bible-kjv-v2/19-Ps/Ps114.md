# Psalm 114

[1] When ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ from a people of strange language,

[2] ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ became his sanctuary,
⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ his dominion.

[3] The sea saw it and fled;
⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ turned back.

[4] The mountains skipped like rams,
the hills like lambs.

[5] What ails you, O sea, that you flee?
O ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, that you turn back?

[6] O mountains, that you skip like rams,
O hills, like lambs?

[7] Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the Lord,
at the presence of the ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,

[8] who turns the rock into a pool of water,
the flint into a spring of water.

## Changes

- **"God" → "Eloah"** (v7) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*.
- **Poetic structure** — eight-verse meditation on Exodus; nature personified as fleeing; rhetorical questions addressed to sea, Jordan, mountains.

### Notes
- **Exodus poetic celebration** — part of Hallel.
