# Psalm 15

A Psalm of David.

[1] O Yahweh, who shall sojourn in your tent?
Who shall dwell on your holy mountain?

[2] He who walks blamelessly and does what is right
and speaks truth in his heart;

[3] who does not slander with his tongue
and does no evil to his neighbor,
nor takes up a reproach against his friend;

[4] in whose eyes a reprobate is despised,
but who honors those who fear Yahweh;
who swears to his own hurt and does not change;

[5] who does not put out his money at interest
and does not take a bribe against the innocent.
He who does these things shall never be moved.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"usury" → "interest"** (v5) — *neshekh*.
- **"vile person" → "reprobate"** (v4) — *nim'as*.

### Notes
- **Entrance liturgy** — temple-gate question-and-answer. Ten ethical qualifications for worshiper.
- **Sworn to his own hurt** — integrity even at personal cost.
