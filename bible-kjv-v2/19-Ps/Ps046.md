# Psalm 46

To the chief musician. Of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧. Upon ⟦alamoth|Alamot|Alamoth⟧. A Song.

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is our refuge and strength,
a very present help in trouble.

[2] Therefore we will not fear, though the earth give way,
though the mountains be moved into the heart of the sea,

[3] though its waters roar and foam,
though the mountains tremble at its swelling. Selah.

[4] There is a river whose streams make glad the city of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the holy dwelling of ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧.

[5] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in her midst; she shall not be moved;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will help her when morning dawns.

[6] The nations rage, the kingdoms totter;
he utters his voice, the earth melts.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts is with us;
the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ is our fortress. Selah.

[8] Come, behold the works of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
how he has brought desolations on the earth.

[9] He makes wars cease to the end of the earth;
he breaks the bow and shatters the spear;
he burns the chariots with fire.

[10] "Be still, and know that I am ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.
I will be exalted among the nations;
I will be exalted in the earth!"

[11] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ of Hosts is with us;
the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ is our fortress. Selah.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 4, 5, 7, 10, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v7, 11) — compound divine name; Tetragrammaton + *Tzeva'ot*.
- **"Alamoth" (musical term)** — soprano voices; retained in superscription.
- **Poetic structure** — three stanzas (v1-3, v4-7, v8-11), each with the refrain about *Tzeva'ot* (vv7, 11).

### Notes
- **Classic refuge-psalm** — basis of Luther's "A Mighty Fortress."
- **"Be still and know"** (v10) — famous line.
