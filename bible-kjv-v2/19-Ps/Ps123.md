# Psalm 123

A Song of Ascents.

[1] To you I lift up my eyes,
O you who are enthroned in the heavens!

[2] Behold, as the eyes of servants
look to the hand of their master,
as the eyes of a maidservant
to the hand of her mistress,
so our eyes look to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
till he has mercy upon us.

[3] Have mercy upon us, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, have mercy upon us,
for we have had more than enough of contempt.

[4] Our soul has had more than enough
of the scorn of those who are at ease,
of the contempt of the proud.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — servant/maidservant analogy (v2) as extended metaphor; communal "have mercy" (v3).
