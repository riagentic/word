# Psalm 137

[1] By the rivers of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ —
there we sat down and wept,
when we remembered ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.

[2] On the willows there
we hung up our lyres.

[3] For there our captors
required of us songs,
and our tormentors, mirth, saying,
"Sing us one of the songs of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!"

[4] How shall we sing ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧ song
in a foreign land?

[5] If I forget you, O ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
let my right hand forget its skill!

[6] Let my tongue stick to the roof of my mouth,
if I do not remember you,
if I do not set ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
above my highest joy!

[7] Remember, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, against the children of Edom
the day of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
how they said, "Lay it bare, lay it bare,
down to its foundations!"

[8] O daughter of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, doomed to be destroyed,
blessed shall he be who repays you
with what you have done to us!

[9] Blessed shall he be who takes your little ones
and dashes them against the rock!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 7) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — Babylon lament (v1-4), Jerusalem vow (v5-6), imprecatory close (v7-9); shocking ending preserved intact.

### Notes
- **Exile lament** — 586 BC aftermath.
- **Difficult ending** — covenant-vengeance plea. Ancient context: retaliation for atrocity.
