# Psalm 90

A prayer of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[1] ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, an abode you have been for us
in generation and generation.

[2] Before mountains were born,
and you were in labor with earth and world,
and from age to age you are ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[3] You make mortal man return to what is crushed,
and you say, "Return, sons of man."

[4] For a thousand years in your eyes
are like the day of yesterday when it passes by,
and a watch in the night.

[5] You have flooded them away; they become a sleep;
in the morning like grass it sprouts again.

[6] In the morning it blossoms and sprouts again;
at evening it withers and dries out.

[7] For we have come to an end in your anger,
and in your fury we have been dismayed.

[8] You have set our iniquities before you,
our secret in the light of your face.

[9] For all our days have turned away in your raging;
we have finished our years like a murmur.

[10] The days of our years — in them are seventy years,
and if by might, eighty years;
and their pride is toil and trouble,
for it passes over quickly, and we fly away.

[11] Who knows the strength of your anger,
and, as the fear of you, your raging?

[12] To count our days — so make known,
that we may bring in a heart of wisdom.

[13] Return, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧! How long?
And be sorry for your servants.

[14] Sate us in the morning with your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and let us shout for joy and be glad in all our days.

[15] Make us glad according to the days you have afflicted us,
the years we have seen evil.

[16] Let your work appear to your servants,
and your splendor upon their sons.

[17] And let the pleasantness of ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be upon us,
and the work of our hands establish upon us,
and the work of our hands, establish it.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (heading, v17) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v13) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v1, 17) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי *Adonai*, not the Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "El"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל *El*.
- **"A Prayer of Moses the man of God" → "A prayer of Moses, the man of Elohim"** (heading) — Hebrew תְּפִלָּה לְמֹשֶׁה אִישׁ־הָאֱלֹהִים, as Deut 33:1; תְּפִלָּה "a prayer"; לְ may say "by", "for" or "belonging to" Moses and is not decided here; הָאֱלֹהִים has the article, given as "Elohim" (as everywhere).
- **"Lord, thou hast been our dwelling place in all generations" → "Adonai, an abode you have been for us in generation and generation"** (v1) — Hebrew מָעוֹן אַתָּה הָיִיתָ לָּנוּ: מָעוֹן "abode" (as Deut 26:15) first; the pronoun אַתָּה is written beside the verb (stress: "you yourself"); לָּנוּ "for us"; בְּדֹר וָדֹר "in generation and generation".
- **"Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting" → "Before mountains were born, and you were in labor with earth and world, and from age to age"** (v2) — Hebrew הָרִים יֻלָּדוּ, pual of יָלַד "be born", no article; וַתְּחוֹלֵל, polel of חוּל "be in labor with, writhe" (as Deut 32:18), here read as "you" (the same form can be "she": "and earth and world were in labor", which would need another pointing for the passive); אֶרֶץ וְתֵבֵל without article. וּמֵעוֹלָם עַד־עוֹלָם: וּ "and" at the head of the line, no article and no וְ before עַד, so "from age to age" (the reviewer's row; Ps 41:13 has both and reads "from the age and to the age"). אַתָּה אֵל "you are El".
- **"Thou turnest man to destruction; and sayest, Return, ye children of men" → "You make mortal man return to what is crushed, and you say, 'Return, sons of man'"** (v3) — Hebrew תָּשֵׁב, hifil of שׁוּב "make return", and שׁוּבוּ "return": one verb twice. אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man"; דַּכָּא "what is crushed" (crushed to powder; the earlier "dust" is עָפָר, another word); בְנֵי־אָדָם "sons of man". וַתֹּאמֶר is a narrative form ("and you said"), given as present with the line before it (a general truth).
- **"are but as yesterday when it is past, and as a watch in the night" → "are like the day of yesterday when it passes by, and a watch in the night"** (v4) — Hebrew בְּעֵינֶיךָ "in your eyes"; כְּיוֹם אֶתְמוֹל "like the day of yesterday"; כִּי יַעֲבֹר "when it passes by" (עָבַר); "as" before "a watch" is not written (the כְּ of the line before carries over).
- **"Thou carriest them away as with a flood; they are as a sleep: in the morning they are like grass which groweth up" → "You have flooded them away; they become a sleep; in the morning like grass it sprouts again"** (v5) — Hebrew זְרַמְתָּם, from זָרַם "pour in a flood" (the flood is in the verb; "as with" is not written); שֵׁנָה יִהְיוּ "a sleep they become"; יַחֲלֹף singular, "it": חָלַף "sprout again" is the lexicons' sense for v5–6, its usual sense "pass on, pass away" also fits ("in the morning it passes away like grass": uncertain).
- **"flourisheth, and groweth up; in the evening it is cut down, and withereth" → "blossoms and sprouts again; at evening it withers and dries out"** (v6) — Hebrew יָצִיץ "blossom" (הֵצִיץ); וְחָלָף as v5; יְמוֹלֵל "wither" (מָלַל, as Ps 37:2; KJV takes it from a root "cut off"); וְיָבֵשׁ "dry out".
- **"For we are consumed by thine anger, and by thy wrath are we troubled" → "For we have come to an end in your anger, and in your fury we have been dismayed"** (v7) — Hebrew כָלִינוּ, qal of כָּלָה "come to an end" (piel "finish" in v9); בְ "in"; חֵמָה "fury" (lit. "heat"); נִבְהַל "be dismayed". אַף "anger" (also "nose"; v7, 11).
- **"our secret sins in the light of thy countenance" → "our secret in the light of your face"** (v8) — Hebrew עֲלֻמֵנוּ, singular, "our hidden thing": "sins" is not written; לִמְאוֹר "in the light" (מָאוֹר, a source of light); פָּנֶיךָ "your face". שַׁתָּה is the qere; the ketiv שת reads שַׁתָּ, the same "you have set".
- **"are passed away in thy wrath: we spend our years as a tale that is told" → "have turned away in your raging; we have finished our years like a murmur"** (v9) — Hebrew פָּנוּ "turned" (away, as the day declines); עֶבְרָה "raging" (v9, 11; beside אַף and חֵמָה); כִּלִּינוּ piel "we have finished" (the root of v7); הֶגֶה "murmur", a low sound (noun of הָגָה, Ps 1:2): "a tale that is told" interprets.
- **"are threescore years and ten; and if by reason of strength they be fourscore years, yet is their strength labour and sorrow; for it is soon cut off" → "in them are seventy years, and if by might, eighty years; and their pride is toil and trouble, for it passes over quickly"** (v10) — Hebrew יְמֵי־שְׁנוֹתֵינוּ בָהֶם "the days of our years — in them"; בִּגְבוּרֹת "by might" (plural of גְּבוּרָה); רָהְבָּם "their pride" (רֹהַב, the swelling of the proud; KJV "strength"); עָמָל "toil", אָוֶן "trouble"; גָז "pass over" (uncertain; KJV "cut off"); וַנָּעֻפָה "and we fly away".
- **"Who knoweth the power of thine anger? even according to thy fear, so is thy wrath" → "Who knows the strength of your anger, and, as the fear of you, your raging?"** (v11) — Hebrew עֹז "strength"; וּכְיִרְאָתְךָ עֶבְרָתֶךָ, no verb: "and like the fear of you — your raging" (your raging is as great as the fear due to you; "so is" is not written). One question covers both lines.
- **"So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom" → "To count our days — so make known, that we may bring in a heart of wisdom"** (v12) — Hebrew לִמְנוֹת יָמֵינוּ כֵּן הוֹדַע, in this order: מָנָה "count"; הוֹדַע "make known" (hifil of יָדַע), with no object ("us" is not written). וְנָבִא "that we may bring (in)", as a harvest; לְבַב חָכְמָה "a heart of wisdom".
- **"and let it repent thee concerning thy servants" → "And be sorry for your servants"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהִנָּחֵם עַל־עֲבָדֶיךָ, nifal of נִחַם with עַל of persons, as Deut 32:36 ("be sorry for": feel sorrow for them, or relent toward them; one rendering). עֲבָדֶיךָ "your servants" (the word is "slaves").
- **"O satisfy us early with thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days" → "Sate us in the morning with your chesed (steadfast love), and let us shout for joy and be glad in all our days"** (v14) — Hebrew שַׂבְּעֵנוּ, piel of שָׂבַע "be sated"; בַבֹּקֶר "in the morning"; חֶסֶד; וּנְרַנְּנָה וְנִשְׂמְחָה two cohortatives, "let us" (רִנֵּן "shout for joy", as Ps 33:1); בְּכָל־יָמֵינוּ "in all our days".
- **"according to the days wherein thou hast afflicted us" → "according to the days you have afflicted us"** (v15) — Hebrew כִּימוֹת עִנִּיתָנוּ (עִנָּה "afflict"); שְׁנוֹת רָאִינוּ רָעָה "the years we have seen evil", "according to" not repeated.
- **"thy glory unto their children" → "your splendor upon their sons"** (v16) — Hebrew יֵרָאֶה "let it appear" (jussive); פֹּעַל "work"; הֲדָרְךָ "your splendor" (הָדָר, not כָּבוֹד "glory"); עַל־בְּנֵיהֶם "upon their sons".
- **"let the beauty of the LORD our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it" → "let the pleasantness of Adonai our Elohim be upon us, and the work of our hands establish upon us, and the work of our hands, establish it"** (v17) — Hebrew וִיהִי "and let … be"; נֹעַם "pleasantness" (as Ps 27:4); אֲדֹנָי, not the Tetragrammaton. כּוֹנְנָה and כּוֹנְנֵהוּ, polel of כּוּן "establish"; the object stands first both times; the third line opens with וּ "and" (not "yea"), and only the third has "it".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — the heading stands within Hebrew v1; the verse numbers are the same.
- **Opens Book IV** (90–106).
- **Prayer of Moses** — the only psalm whose heading names Moses; "the man of Elohim" as Deut 33:1; Josh 14:6.
