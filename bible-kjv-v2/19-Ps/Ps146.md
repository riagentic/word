# Psalm 146

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, O my soul!

[2] I will praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ as long as I live;
I will sing praises to my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ while I have my being.

[3] Put not your trust in princes,
in a son of man, in whom there is no salvation.

[4] When his breath departs, he returns to the earth;
on that very day his plans perish.

[5] Blessed is he whose help is the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
whose hope is in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[6] who made heaven and earth,
the sea, and all that is in them;
who keeps faith forever;

[7] who executes justice for the oppressed,
who gives food to the hungry.
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ sets the prisoners free;

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ opens the eyes of the blind.
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ lifts up those who are bowed down;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loves the righteous.

[9] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ watches over the sojourners;
he upholds the widow and the fatherless,
but the way of the wicked he brings to ruin.

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will reign forever,
your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, O ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧, to all generations.
⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 5, 10) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"El"** (v5) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **Poetic structure** — opens Final Hallel (Pss 146-150); contrast princes with Yahweh as maker of heaven and earth (v3-6); seven social-justice acts of Yahweh (v7-9).

### Notes
- **First of Final Hallel** (Pss 146-150) — five "Hallelu-yah" psalms.
