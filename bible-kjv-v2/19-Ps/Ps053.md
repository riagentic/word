# Psalm 53

To the chief musician. Upon ⟦mahalath|Machalat|Mahalath⟧. A Maskil of David.

[1] The fool — says in his heart, "There is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."
They are corrupt, doing abominable iniquity;
there is none who does good.

[2] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ looks down from heaven on the children of men
to see if there are any who understand,
who seek after ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] They have all fallen away;
together they have become corrupt;
there is none who does good,
not even one.

[4] Have those who work evil no knowledge,
who eat up my people as they eat bread,
and do not call upon ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[5] There they are, in great terror,
where there was no terror!
For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ scatters the bones of him who encamps against you;
you put them to shame, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has rejected them.

[6] Oh, that salvation for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ would come out of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!
When ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ restores the fortunes of his people,
let ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ rejoice, let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ be glad.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 4, 5, 6) — Hebrew *Elohim*; replaces Yahweh throughout vs. Ps 14.
- **Maskil** — retained in superscription; **Mahalath** — musical term, retained.
- **Poetic structure** — near-duplicate of Ps 14 with *Elohim* substitution.

### Notes
- **Near-duplicate of Ps 14** — with *Elohim* substituted for Yahweh (Elohist Psalter).
