# Psalm 53

To the overseer. Upon ⟦mahalath-tune|machalat|Mahalath⟧. A Maskil of David.

[1] A fool has said in his heart, "There is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."
They have worked ruin, and have made injustice abominable;
there is none who does good.

[2] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from the heavens has looked down upon the sons of man,
to see whether there is one who has insight,
one who seeks ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] All of it has turned back, together they have become foul; there is none who does good,
there is not even one.

[4] Have they not known, the workers of wickedness,
who eat my people — they have eaten bread?
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ they have not called on.

[5] There they dreaded a dread —
there was no dread!
For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has scattered the bones of him who camps against you;
you have put them to shame, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has rejected them.

[6] Would that from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ the salvations of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were given!
When ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ restores the fortunes of his people,
let ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ be glad, let ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ rejoice.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 4, 5, 6) — Hebrew *Elohim*, as printed: seven times, where Ps 14 has יהוה in v2, 4, 7 (the Elohistic psalms keep אֱלֹהִים; nothing is levelled).
- **"To the chief Musician upon Mahalath, Maschil, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. Upon Mahalath. A Maskil of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל־מָחֲלַת מַשְׂכִּיל לְדָוִד: no word "psalm"; Ps 14 has only לַמְנַצֵּחַ לְדָוִד. מָחֲלַת (also Ps 88) is a musical term or tune name of unknown meaning (taken from חָלָה "be sick" or from מָחוֹל "dance"): kept, token `mahalath-tune` (the `mahalath` key is Esau's wife). מַשְׂכִּיל, a participle of הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight", as the name of a kind of psalm of unknown meaning: kept as *Maskil*. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (traditional "to the chief musician"); לְ of לְדָוִד "by / for / belonging to": "of" leaves it open.
- **"The fool hath said" → "A fool has said"** (v1) — Hebrew אָמַר נָבָל, perfect, no article; נָבָל the base, senseless man (as Ps 14:1).
- **"Corrupt are they, and have done abominable iniquity" → "They have worked ruin, and have made injustice abominable"** (v1) — Hebrew הִשְׁחִיתוּ וְהִתְעִיבוּ עָוֶל: הִשְׁחִית without an object "work ruin" (the שִׁחֵת row); הִתְעִיב "act abominably, make abominable" (its row), with עָוֶל "injustice" (its row). Ps 14:1 has no "and" and has עֲלִילָה "deed" for עָוֶל.
- **"God looked down from heaven upon the children of men" → "Elohim from the heavens has looked down upon the sons of man"** (v2) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים מִשָּׁמַיִם הִשְׁקִיף עַל־בְּנֵי אָדָם, perfect, in this order; בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man". Ps 14:2 has יהוה.
- **"if there were any that did understand, that did seek God" → "whether there is one who has insight, one who seeks Elohim"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲיֵשׁ מַשְׂכִּיל דֹּרֵשׁ אֶת־אֱלֹהִים, two participles: מַשְׂכִּיל from הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight" (the word of the heading), דָּרַשׁ "seek".
- **"Every one of them is gone back: they are altogether become filthy" → "All of it has turned back; together they have become foul"** (v3) — Hebrew כֻּלּוֹ סָג יַחְדָּו נֶאֱלָחוּ: כֻּלּוֹ "all of it" (the whole, singular verb), סָג from סוּג "turn back"; נֶאֱלָחוּ "have become foul" (the Ps 13–16 row). Ps 14:3 has הַכֹּל סָר "all have turned aside".
- **"there is none that doeth good, no, not one" → "there is none who does good, there is not even one"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵין עֹשֵׂה־טוֹב אֵין גַּם־אֶחָד, "there is not" twice; as Ps 14:3, with the line break at the half-verse (after "does good").
- **"Have the workers of iniquity no knowledge? who eat up my people as they eat bread" → "Have they not known, the workers of wickedness, who eat my people — they have eaten bread?"** (v4) — Hebrew הֲלֹא יָדְעוּ פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן אֹכְלֵי עַמִּי אָכְלוּ לֶחֶם: perfect "have they not known"; אָוֶן "wickedness"; "eaters of my people, they have eaten bread": no "as" in the Hebrew (the readings as at Ps 14:4). Ps 14:4 has כָּל־ "all the workers".
- **"they have not called upon God" → "Elohim they have not called on"** (v4) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים לֹא קָרָאוּ, the name first, a perfect. Ps 14:4 has יהוה.
- **"There were they in great fear, where no fear was" → "There they dreaded a dread — there was no dread!"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׁם פָּחֲדוּ־פַחַד לֹא־הָיָה פָחַד: the verb with its own noun (as Ps 14:5), then "there was not dread", without "where": the dread came where there was nothing to dread (or: no dread like it had been). The line is Ps 53's own: Ps 14:5 goes on "for Elohim is in the generation of the righteous".
- **"for God hath scattered the bones of him that encampeth against thee" → "For Elohim has scattered the bones of him who camps against you"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי־אֱלֹהִים פִּזַּר עַצְמוֹת חֹנָךְ, perfect; חֹנָךְ "the one camping against you" (חָנָה "camp", its row), singular. Not in Ps 14.
- **"thou hast put them to shame, because God hath despised them" → "you have put them to shame, for Elohim has rejected them"** (v5) — Hebrew הֱבִשֹׁתָה כִּי־אֱלֹהִים מְאָסָם: hifil of בּוֹשׁ, 2nd person masculine singular; מָאַס "reject". Ps 14:6 has instead "The counsel of the afflicted you shame, for Yahweh is his refuge" (the same verb בּוֹשׁ).
- **"Oh that the salvation of Israel were come out of Zion!" → "Would that from Zion the salvations of Israel were given!"** (v6) — Hebrew מִי יִתֵּן מִצִּיּוֹן יְשֻׁעוֹת יִשְׂרָאֵל, literally "Who will give from Zion …?", a wish; יְשֻׁעוֹת plural "salvations" (Ps 14:7 singular יְשׁוּעַת).
- **"When God bringeth back the captivity of his people" → "When Elohim restores the fortunes of his people"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּשׁוּב אֱלֹהִים שְׁבוּת עַמּוֹ (reviewer row 2026-10-16: one idiom, "restore the fortunes"); literally "turn back the captivity / the turning" (שְׁבוּת from שָׁבָה or from שׁוּב), traditional "bring back the captivity". Ps 14:7 has יהוה.
- **"Jacob shall rejoice, and Israel shall be glad" → "let Jacob be glad, let Israel rejoice"** (v6) — Hebrew יָגֵל, short (jussive) form of גִּיל "be glad", then יִשְׂמַח "rejoice"; the two verbs in the Hebrew order.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is Heb v1: our v1 = Heb v2, v6 = Heb v7.
- **Ps 14 and Ps 53** — the same psalm in two forms: Ps 53 has אֱלֹהִים for יהוה (v2, 4, 6), a heading of its own, the wording of v1, 3, 4 and 6 as named above, and in v5 its own lines in place of Ps 14:5b–6.
- **v1–3 cited in Rom 3:10–12** (with Ps 14:1–3); the Greek there follows its own wording.
