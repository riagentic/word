# Psalm 100

A psalm for thanksgiving.

[1] Shout to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all the earth!

[2] Serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with gladness;
come before him with a ringing cry.

[3] Know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
he made us, and his we are,
his people and the flock of his pasture.

[4] Enter his gates with thanksgiving,
his courts with praise;
give thanks to him, bless his name.

[5] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is good;
his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is forever,
and to generation and generation his faithfulness.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"A Psalm of praise" → "A psalm for thanksgiving"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְתוֹדָה: תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving" (the word of v4; also the thank offering, Lev 7:12: whether the psalm was sung with that offering the heading does not say). The heading is part of Hebrew v1.
- **"Make a joyful noise unto the LORD, all ye lands" → "Shout to Yahweh, all the earth"** (v1) — Hebrew הָרִיעוּ (הֵרִיעַ of voices: "shout") לַיהוָה כָּל־הָאָרֶץ, as Ps 98:4.
- **"come before his presence with singing" → "come before him with a ringing cry"** (v2) — Hebrew בֹּאוּ לְפָנָיו (literally "before his face") בִּרְנָנָה; רְנָנָה "ringing cry" (as Job 3:7; Ps 63:5).
- **"it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves" → "he made us, and his we are"** (v3) — Hebrew הוּא־עָשָׂנוּ וְלוֹ אֲנַחְנוּ: the qere (the reading tradition) וְלוֹ "and to him (his) we are" is translated; the written text (ketiv) וְלֹא "and not we", which the KJV follows, says "he made us and not we (ourselves)". The two words sound alike.
- **"we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture" → "his people and the flock of his pasture"** (v3) — Hebrew עַמּוֹ וְצֹאן מַרְעִיתוֹ: no second "we are"; צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats); מַרְעִית "pasture" (from רָעָה "shepherd").
- **"Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name" → "Enter his gates with thanksgiving, his courts with praise; give thanks to him, bless his name"** (v4) — Hebrew בֹּאוּ שְׁעָרָיו בְּתוֹדָה חֲצֵרֹתָיו בִּתְהִלָּה הוֹדוּ־לוֹ בָּרֲכוּ שְׁמוֹ: no "and" in either pair; הוֹדָה "give thanks to".
- **"his mercy is everlasting; and his truth endureth to all generations" → "his chesed is forever, and to generation and generation his faithfulness"** (v5) — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם חַסְדּוֹ וְעַד־דֹּר וָדֹר אֱמוּנָתוֹ: no verb is written ("is" supplied once); אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness"; עַד־דֹּר וָדֹר "to generation and generation".

### Notes
- **Hymn of entry** — summons to come into the gates and courts (v2, 4), each followed by the reason (v3, 5).
