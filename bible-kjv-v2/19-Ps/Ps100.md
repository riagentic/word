# Psalm 100

A Psalm for giving thanks.

[1] Make a joyful noise to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all the earth!

[2] Serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with gladness!
Come into his presence with singing!

[3] Know that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!
It is he who made us, and we are his;
we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.

[4] Enter his gates with thanksgiving,
and his courts with praise!
Give thanks to him; bless his name!

[5] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is good;
his steadfast love endures forever,
and his faithfulness to all generations.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — brief five-verse hymn; two imperatives-then-reason stanzas; "Enter his gates" (v4) as climax.
