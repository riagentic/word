# Psalm 80

To the chief musician. Upon ⟦shoshannim-eduth|Shoshannim Edut|Lilies. A Testimony⟧. A Psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] Give ear, O Shepherd of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
you who lead ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ like a flock.
You who sit enthroned upon the ⟦cherub|keruvim|cherubim⟧, shine forth.

[2] Before ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧,
stir up your might
and come to save us!

[3] Restore us, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
let your face shine, that we may be saved!

[4] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts,
how long will you be angry with your people's prayers?

[5] You have fed them with the bread of tears
and given them tears to drink in full measure.

[6] You make us an object of contention for our neighbors,
and our enemies laugh among themselves.

[7] Restore us, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts;
let your face shine, that we may be saved!

[8] You brought a vine out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
you drove out the nations and planted it.

[9] You cleared the ground for it;
it took deep root and filled the land.

[10] The mountains were covered with its shade,
the mighty cedars with its branches.

[11] It sent out its branches to the sea
and its shoots to the River.

[12] Why then have you broken down its walls,
so that all who pass along the way pluck its fruit?

[13] The boar from the forest ravages it,
and all that move in the field feed on it.

[14] Turn again, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts!
Look down from heaven, and see;
have regard for this vine,

[15] the stock that your right hand planted,
and for the son whom you made strong for yourself.

[16] They have burned it with fire; they have cut it down;
may they perish at the rebuke of your face!

[17] But let your hand be on the man of your right hand,
the son of man whom you have made strong for yourself!

[18] Then we shall not turn back from you;
give us life, and we will call upon your name!

[19] Restore us, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts!
Let your face shine, that we may be saved!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 19) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 4, 7, 14, 19) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD God of hosts" → "Yahweh *Elohim* of Hosts"** (v4, 19) — compound divine name.
- **"God of hosts" → "*Elohim* of Hosts"** (v7, 14) — compound divine name.
- **Shoshannim-eduth** — musical term; retained.
- **Poetic structure** — threefold refrain "Restore us" (v3, 7, 19) structures the psalm; vine allegory in middle.

### Notes
- **Threefold refrain** — "Restore us" (v3, 7, 19).
- **Vine of Israel** — iconic metaphor.
