# Psalm 80

To the overseer, to the ⟦shoshannim-eduth|Shoshannim Edut|Lilies. A Testimony⟧. Of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧, a psalm.

[1] Shepherd of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, give ear,
you who lead ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧ like a flock!
You who sit upon the ⟦cherub|keruvim|cherubim⟧, shine forth!

[2] Before ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧
stir up your might,
and come for salvation for us!

[3] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, bring us back,
and make your face shine, and we shall be saved!

[4] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts,
how long have you smoked against the prayer of your people?

[5] You have fed them the bread of tears,
and made them drink of tears a third-measure.

[6] You set us as strife for our neighbors,
and our enemies mock among themselves.

[7] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, bring us back,
and make your face shine, and we shall be saved!

[8] A vine out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ you pulled up;
you drove out nations and planted it.

[9] You cleared before it,
and it made its roots take root, and it filled the land.

[10] Mountains were covered with its shade,
and with its branches the cedars of ⟦el|El|God⟧.

[11] It sent out its boughs to the sea,
and to the River its shoots.

[12] Why have you breached its fences,
and all who pass along the way pluck it?

[13] A swine from the forest ravages it,
and what moves in the field feeds on it.

[14] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, return, please!
Look from the heavens and see,
and visit this vine,

[15] and the stock that your right hand planted,
and upon the son you made strong for yourself.

[16] It is burned with fire, cut down;
at the rebuke of your face they perish.

[17] Let your hand be upon the man of your right hand,
upon the son of man you made strong for yourself.

[18] And we will not turn back from you;
you will make us live, and on your name we will call.

[19] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, bring us back;
make your face shine, and we shall be saved!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v4, 19) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 4, 7, 14, 19) — Hebrew *Elohim*. Ps 80 is one of the Elohistic psalms (42–83): the divine names stand as printed.
- **"O LORD God of hosts" → "Yahweh *Elohim* of Hosts"** (v4, 19) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים צְבָאוֹת: *Elohim* stands before צְבָאוֹת as if it were a construct, which the word does not form elsewhere; kept word for word. **"O God of hosts" → "*Elohim* of Hosts"** (v7, 14) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים צְבָאוֹת.
- **No "O"** (v1, 3, 4, 7, 14, 19) — the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim-Eduth, A Psalm of Asaph" → "To the overseer, to the Shoshannim Edut. Of Asaph, a psalm."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ אֶל־שֹׁשַׁנִּים עֵדוּת לְאָסָף מִזְמוֹר: לַמְנַצֵּחַ from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"); אֶל "to" (as Ps 5); שֹׁשַׁנִּים "lilies", עֵדוּת "testimony": a tune name, meaning unknown; the accents put עֵדוּת with what follows ("A testimony of Asaph, a psalm"), the token keeps the two words together; לְאָסָף מִזְמוֹר in this order (לְ: by, for or belonging to; not decided).
- **"Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, thou that leadest Joseph like a flock; thou that dwellest between the cherubims" → "Shepherd of Israel, give ear, you who lead Joseph like a flock! You who sit upon the cherubim"** (v1) — Hebrew רֹעֵה יִשְׂרָאֵל הַאֲזִינָה, in this order; נֹהֵג "lead" (as a shepherd, Ps 78:52); יֹשֵׁב הַכְּרוּבִים "sitting (on) the cherubim", as 1 Sam 4:4 ("between" is not written); הוֹפִיעָה "shine forth".
- **"stir up thy strength, and come and save us" → "stir up your might, and come for salvation for us"** (v2) — Hebrew גְּבוּרָתֶךָ "your might"; וּלְכָה לִישֻׁעָתָה לָּנוּ, literally "and go to salvation for us".
- **Refrain "Turn us again … cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved" → "bring us back, and make your face shine, and we shall be saved"** (v3, 7, 19) — Hebrew הֲשִׁיבֵנוּ, hifil of שׁוּב "make us return"; וְהָאֵר פָּנֶיךָ "and make your face shine"; וְנִוָּשֵׁעָה, a cohortative after the imperative: "and we shall be saved" (the result; not "that"). One wording in all three; the Hebrew differs only in the names (v3 *Elohim*, v7 *Elohim* of Hosts, v19 Yahweh *Elohim* of Hosts) and in v19, which has הָאֵר without וְ ("make your face shine", no "and").
- **"how long wilt thou be angry against the prayer of thy people?" → "how long have you smoked against the prayer of your people?"** (v4) — Hebrew עַד־מָתַי עָשַׁנְתָּ, a perfect of עָשַׁן "smoke": the smoke of anger (as Ps 74:1; Deut 29:20); בִּתְפִלַּת "against the prayer" (singular).
- **"Thou feedest them with the bread of tears; and givest them tears to drink in great measure" → "You have fed them the bread of tears, and made them drink of tears a third-measure"** (v5) — Hebrew הֶאֱכַלְתָּם, a perfect; לֶחֶם דִּמְעָה, literally "bread of tear" (a collective singular); וַתַּשְׁקֵמוֹ בִּדְמָעוֹת שָׁלִישׁ: שָׁלִישׁ "a third", a measure (Isa 40:12) of unknown size; "great measure" is a guess.
- **"Thou makest us a strife unto our neighbours: and our enemies laugh among themselves" → "You set us as strife for our neighbors, and our enemies mock among themselves"** (v6) — Hebrew תְּשִׂימֵנוּ "you set us" (imperfect); מָדוֹן "strife" (another word than מְרִיבָה); יִלְעֲגוּ "mock"; לָמוֹ "for themselves" (or "at it": not certain).
- **"Thou hast brought a vine out of Egypt: thou hast cast out the heathen" → "A vine out of Egypt you pulled up; you drove out nations"** (v8) — Hebrew גֶּפֶן מִמִּצְרַיִם תַּסִּיעַ, in this order; הִסִּיעַ, literally "make set out" (the verb of pulling up tent pegs and breaking camp, Ps 78:52); תְּגָרֵשׁ "drive out"; גּוֹיִם without the article.
- **"Thou preparedst room before it, and didst cause it to take deep root" → "You cleared before it, and it made its roots take root"** (v9) — Hebrew פִּנִּיתָ לְפָנֶיהָ "you cleared before it" ("room" is not written); וַתַּשְׁרֵשׁ שָׁרָשֶׁיהָ "it made its roots take root" ("deep" is not written). וַתַּשְׁרֵשׁ and וַתְּמַלֵּא may be "it" (the vine) or "you"; the vine is taken as subject, as in v10–11.
- **"The hills were covered with the shadow of it, and the boughs thereof were like the goodly cedars" → "Mountains were covered with its shade, and with its branches the cedars of El"** (v10) — Hebrew הָרִים "mountains" without the article; עֲנָפֶיהָ "its branches"; אַרְזֵי־אֵל "the cedars of El" (token `el`), often taken as "mighty cedars" (as "the mountains of El", Ps 36:6); "like" is not written. The line may also be read "and its branches were cedars of El".
- **"She sent out her boughs unto the sea, and her branches unto the river" → "It sent out its boughs to the sea, and to the River its shoots"** (v11) — Hebrew קְצִירֶהָ "its boughs" (קָצִיר, elsewhere "harvest"); יוֹנְקוֹתֶיהָ "its shoots" (literally "sucklings"); נָהָר without the article, the Euphrates ("the River").
- **"Why hast thou then broken down her hedges, so that all they which pass by the way do pluck her?" → "Why have you breached its fences, and all who pass along the way pluck it?"** (v12) — Hebrew פָּרַצְתָּ "breached" (פָּרַץ); גְדֵרֶיהָ "its fences"; וְאָרוּהָ "and … pluck it" ("then", "so that" and "fruit" are not written).
- **"The boar out of the wood doth waste it, and the wild beast of the field doth devour it" → "A swine from the forest ravages it, and what moves in the field feeds on it"** (v13) — Hebrew חֲזִיר "pig, swine" (no article; "boar" says more than the word); יְכַרְסְמֶנָּה, found only here: "ravage" or "gnaw" (uncertain); זִיז שָׂדַי "what moves in the field" (uncertain; as Ps 50:11); יִרְעֶנָּה "feeds on it".
- **"Return, we beseech thee … look down from heaven, and behold" → "return, please! Look from the heavens and see"** (v14) — Hebrew שׁוּב־נָא; הַבֵּט מִשָּׁמַיִם וּרְאֵה; פְקֹד "visit" (as Gen 21:1).
- **"And the vineyard which thy right hand hath planted, and the branch that thou madest strong for thyself" → "and the stock that your right hand planted, and upon the son you made strong for yourself"** (v15) — Hebrew וְכַנָּה, a word found only here, read as printed as a noun: "stock, plant" (meaning uncertain; some take it as a verb, "and protect", from memory); וְעַל־בֵּן "and upon the son": בֵּן is "son" (also said of a young branch, Gen 49:22); אִמַּצְתָּה לָּךְ "you made strong for yourself" (as v17). The verse goes on from "visit" in v14.
- **"It is burned with fire, it is cut down: they perish at the rebuke of thy countenance" → "It is burned with fire, cut down; at the rebuke of your face they perish"** (v16) — Hebrew שְׂרֻפָה … כְּסוּחָה, feminine participles: the vine; מִגַּעֲרַת פָּנֶיךָ "at the rebuke of your face"; יֹאבֵדוּ "they perish" (imperfect; who "they" are is not said; a wish "may they perish" is also possible).
- **"Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand" → "Let your hand be upon the man of your right hand"** (v17) — Hebrew תְּהִי־יָדְךָ, a jussive; no "but". בֶּן־אָדָם "son of man".
- **"So will not we go back from thee: quicken us" → "And we will not turn back from you; you will make us live"** (v18) — Hebrew וְלֹא־נָסוֹג "and we will not turn back"; תְּחַיֵּנוּ, an imperfect: "you will make us live" (it may be a request: "make us live"); וּבְשִׁמְךָ נִקְרָא "and on your name we will call".
- **Shoshannim-eduth** — musical term; token kept.
- **Poetic structure** — threefold refrain (v3, 7, 19) structures the psalm; vine allegory in middle.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v19 = Heb v20.
- **Threefold refrain** — "bring us back" (v3, 7, 19).
- **Vine of Israel** — iconic metaphor.
