# Psalm 112

[1] ⟦praise-the-lord|Halleluyah|Praise the LORD⟧!
Happy is the man who fears ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
in his commandments he delights greatly.

[2] Mighty in the land shall his seed be;
the generation of the upright shall be blessed.

[3] Wealth and riches are in his house,
and his righteousness stands perpetually.

[4] Light has dawned in the darkness for the upright:
gracious and compassionate and righteous.

[5] Good is the man who shows favor and lends;
he maintains his matters with judgment.

[6] For forever he shall not be shaken;
for an everlasting remembrance the righteous shall be.

[7] Of an evil report he shall not be afraid;
his heart is established, trusting in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[8] His heart is upheld, he shall not be afraid,
until he looks on his adversaries.

[9] He has scattered, he has given to the needy;
his righteousness stands perpetually;
his horn shall be raised high in glory.

[10] The wicked shall see and be vexed;
his teeth he shall gnash, and melt away;
the craving of the wicked shall perish.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 7) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Praise ye the LORD" → "Halleluyah"** (v1) — Hebrew הַלְלוּ יָהּ (printed as two words), literally "Praise Yah", before the first line of the acrostic (as Ps 111:1).
- **"Blessed is the man that feareth the LORD, that delighteth greatly in his commandments" → "Happy is the man who fears Yahweh; in his commandments he delights greatly"** (v1) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "Happy" (not "Blessed": that is בָּרוּךְ; as Ps 1:1); אִישׁ "man"; יָרֵא "fears". בְּמִצְוֺתָיו חָפֵץ מְאֹד, a clause of its own, "in his commandments" first; מִצְוָה "commandment"; חָפֵץ בְּ "delight in".
- **"His seed shall be mighty upon earth" → "Mighty in the land shall his seed be"** (v2) — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר בָּאָרֶץ יִהְיֶה זַרְעוֹ, in this order; גִּבּוֹר "mighty"; זֶרַע "seed"; בָּאָרֶץ "in the land" (or "on the earth"). דּוֹר יְשָׁרִים יְבֹרָךְ "the generation of the upright shall be blessed".
- **"Wealth and riches shall be in his house: and his righteousness endureth for ever" → "Wealth and riches are in his house, and his righteousness stands perpetually"** (v3) — Hebrew הוֹן־וָעֹשֶׁר בְּבֵיתוֹ, without a verb ("are"); וְצִדְקָתוֹ עֹמֶדֶת לָעַד, the same words as Ps 111:3, there of Yahweh, here of the man (also v9). לָעַד "perpetually" (as Ps 111:3).
- **"Unto the upright there ariseth light in the darkness: he is gracious, and full of compassion, and righteous" → "Light has dawned in the darkness for the upright: gracious and compassionate and righteous"** (v4) — Hebrew זָרַח בַּחֹשֶׁךְ אוֹר לַיְשָׁרִים, a perfect, "(it) has dawned in the darkness, light, for the upright" (זָרַח "dawn, rise", of the sun). חַנּוּן וְרַחוּם וְצַדִּיק, three adjectives without a subject: "he is" is not in the Hebrew. Of whom they are said (Yahweh, as Ps 111:4; the upright man; or the light) is not named.
- **"A good man sheweth favour, and lendeth: he will guide his affairs with discretion" → "Good is the man who shows favor and lends; he maintains his matters with judgment"** (v5) — Hebrew טוֹב־אִישׁ חוֹנֵן וּמַלְוֶה, "good (is) a man showing favor and lending" (or "it is well with the man who ..."); חוֹנֵן, of חָנַן "show favor"; מַלְוֶה, of הִלְוָה "lend". יְכַלְכֵּל דְּבָרָיו בְּמִשְׁפָּט: כִּלְכֵּל "sustain" (as Gen 45:11), here of matters "maintain" (hold up, carry through); דָּבָר "matter" (no speech is meant); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (or "in the judgment", his case in court).
- **"Surely he shall not be moved for ever: the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance" → "For forever he shall not be shaken; for an everlasting remembrance the righteous shall be"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי־לְעוֹלָם לֹא־יִמּוֹט, כִּי "for" (or "surely"); מוֹט nifal "be shaken" (as Ps 15:5). לְזֵכֶר עוֹלָם יִהְיֶה צַדִּיק, "for a remembrance of the age (everlasting) the righteous shall be"; זֵכֶר "remembrance" (as Ps 111:4).
- **"He shall not be afraid of evil tidings: his heart is fixed, trusting in the LORD" → "Of an evil report he shall not be afraid; his heart is established, trusting in Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew מִשְּׁמוּעָה רָעָה "of an evil report" first (שְׁמוּעָה "report, what is heard"; רָעָה "evil"); נָכוֹן לִבּוֹ "his heart is established" (נָכוֹן, of a thing made firm); בָּטֻחַ "trusting" (בָּטַח).
- **"His heart is established, he shall not be afraid, until he see his desire upon his enemies" → "His heart is upheld, he shall not be afraid, until he looks on his adversaries"** (v8) — Hebrew סָמוּךְ לִבּוֹ, סָמוּךְ "upheld" (as Ps 111:8; נָכוֹן "established" is v7). עַד אֲשֶׁר־יִרְאֶה בְצָרָיו "until he looks on his adversaries" (רָאָה בְּ, looking on what befalls them); "his desire" (KJV) and "in triumph" are not in the Hebrew. צַר "adversary" (the word of Ps 109 is שָׂטַן).
- **"He hath dispersed, he hath given to the poor; his righteousness endureth for ever; his horn shall be exalted with honour" → "He has scattered, he has given to the needy; his righteousness stands perpetually; his horn shall be raised high in glory"** (v9) — Hebrew פִּזַּר נָתַן, two perfects without "and": פִּזֵּר "scatter"; לָאֶבְיוֹנִים "to the needy" (אֶבְיוֹן). קַרְנוֹ "his horn" (his strength); תָּרוּם "shall be raised high" (רוּם); בְּכָבוֹד "in glory". 2 Cor 9:9 quotes the first two lines from the Greek (ἐσκόρπισεν, ἔδωκεν τοῖς πένησιν, "he scattered, he gave to the poor"; from memory), of the giver; the verse keeps the Hebrew.
- **"The wicked shall see it, and be grieved; he shall gnash with his teeth, and melt away: the desire of the wicked shall perish" → "The wicked shall see and be vexed; his teeth he shall gnash, and melt away; the craving of the wicked shall perish"** (v10) — Hebrew רָשָׁע יִרְאֶה וְכָעָס, "the wicked" (singular, no article) "shall see", without "it"; כָּעַס "be vexed" (not "grieved"). שִׁנָּיו יַחֲרֹק "his teeth he shall gnash" (חָרַק); וְנָמָס, נָמֵס "melt". תַּאֲוַת רְשָׁעִים "the craving of the wicked" (תַּאֲוָה "craving"); אָבַד "perish".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours.
- **Alphabetic acrostic** — as Ps 111: after "Halleluyah", each line (half-verse; three lines in v9 and v10) opens with the next letter, all twenty-two in order: א אַשְׁרֵי (v1a), ב בְּמִצְוֺתָיו (v1b), ג (v2a), ד (v2b), ה (v3a), ו (v3b), ז (v4a), ח (v4b), ט (v5a), י (v5b), כ (v6a), ל (v6b), מ (v7a), נ (v7b), ס (v8a), ע (v8b), פ (v9a), צ (v9b), ק (v9c), ר (v10a), ש (v10b), ת (v10c). The English does not imitate the scheme.
- **Twin of Ps 111** — what Ps 111 says of Yahweh (v3 "his righteousness stands perpetually", v4 "gracious and compassionate") Ps 112 says of the man who fears him (v3, 4, 9).
- **v9 cited 2 Cor 9:9** — Paul on giving.
