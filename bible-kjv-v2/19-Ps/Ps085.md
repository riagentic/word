# Psalm 85

To the overseer. Of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧. A psalm.

[1] You have been pleased, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, with your land;
you have restored the fortunes of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[2] You have forgiven the iniquity of your people;
you have covered all their sin. Selah.

[3] You have gathered in all your raging;
you have turned back from the burning of your anger.

[4] Turn us back, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our salvation,
and break off your vexation with us.

[5] Will you be angry with us forever?
Will you draw out your anger to generation and generation?

[6] Will you not return, make us live,
and your people will rejoice in you?

[7] Show us, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and your salvation may you give to us.

[8] Let me hear what the ⟦el|El|God⟧, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, will speak,
for he will speak ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to his people and to his faithful ones;
and let them not turn back to folly.

[9] Surely near to those who fear him is his salvation,
that glory may abide in our land.

[10] ⟦steadfast-love|Chesed|Steadfast love⟧ and truth have met;
righteousness and ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ have kissed.

[11] Truth from the earth springs up,
and righteousness from the heavens has looked down.

[12] Also ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give what is good,
and our land will give its produce.

[13] Righteousness will walk before him,
and will set his steps for a way.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 7, 8, 12) — Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God the LORD" → "the El, Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew הָאֵל יְהוָה, אֵל with the article, then the name (as Ps 18:30 "the El"). No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Psalm for the sons of Korah" → "To the overseer. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח מִזְמוֹר, in this order. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer" (the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21; traditional "to the chief musician"); the לְ of לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח may be "by", "for" or "belonging to", and is not decided here.
- **"thou hast been favourable unto thy land" → "You have been pleased, Yahweh, with your land"** (v1) — Hebrew רָצִיתָ יְהוָה אַרְצֶךָ, perfect, the name between verb and object; רָצָה "be pleased with". The perfects of v1–3 stay perfects.
- **"thou hast brought back the captivity of Jacob" → "you have restored the fortunes of Jacob"** (v1) — Hebrew שַׁבְתָּ שְׁבִית יַעֲקֹב (qere; the written form is שבות): literally "you have turned back the captivity / the turning of Jacob", the verb and the noun from one sound; שְׁבוּת / שְׁבִית is from שָׁבָה "take captive" or from שׁוּב itself. One idiom, one rendering (as Ps 14:7; Job 42:10, where no captivity is meant); traditional "turn the captivity". Qere and ketiv do not differ in meaning.
- **"Thou hast forgiven the iniquity of thy people, thou hast covered all their sin" → "You have forgiven … you have covered"** (v2) — Hebrew נָשָׂאתָ עֲוֹן, literally "you have lifted (taken away) the iniquity"; כִּסִּיתָ "you have covered".
- **"Thou hast taken away all thy wrath: thou hast turned thyself from the fierceness of thine anger" → "You have gathered in all your raging; you have turned back from the burning of your anger"** (v3) — Hebrew אָסַפְתָּ "you have gathered (in)"; עֶבְרָה "raging" (beside אַף "anger"); הֱשִׁיבוֹתָ, hifil of שׁוּב, here without an object, "turned back"; חֲרוֹן אַפֶּךָ, "the burning of your anger" (אַף, literally "nose").
- **"Turn us, O God of our salvation, and cause thine anger toward us to cease" → "Turn us back, Elohim of our salvation, and break off your vexation with us"** (v4) — Hebrew שׁוּבֵנוּ, the plain stem of שׁוּב with "us" as object ("turn us back", "restore us"; some read "return to us"); הָפֵר, from הֵפֵר "break, make void"; כַּעַס "vexation"; עִמָּנוּ "with us".
- **"wilt thou draw out thine anger to all generations?" → "Will you draw out your anger to generation and generation?"** (v5) — Hebrew תֶּאֱנַף "be angry" (verb of אַף); תִּמְשֹׁךְ "draw out"; לְדֹר וָדֹר "to generation and generation".
- **"Wilt thou not revive us again: that thy people may rejoice in thee?" → "Will you not return, make us live, and your people will rejoice in you?"** (v6) — Hebrew הֲלֹא־אַתָּה תָּשׁוּב תְּחַיֵּנוּ, with the pronoun "you" stressed: "return" with a second verb, without "and" (= "make us live again"); חִיָּה "make live"; וְעַמְּךָ יִשְׂמְחוּ־בָךְ "and your people will rejoice in you" (no "that").
- **"Shew us thy mercy, O LORD, and grant us thy salvation" → "Show us, Yahweh, your chesed, and your salvation may you give to us"** (v7) — Hebrew הַרְאֵנוּ יְהוָה חַסְדֶּךָ, the name before the object; חֶסֶד with its token; וְיֶשְׁעֲךָ תִּתֶּן־לָנוּ, the object first, an imperfect after the command (a wish).
- **"I will hear what God the LORD will speak: for he will speak peace unto his people, and to his saints: but let them not turn again to folly" → "Let me hear what the El, Yahweh, will speak, for he will speak shalom to his people and to his faithful ones; and let them not turn back to folly"** (v8) — Hebrew אֶשְׁמְעָה, cohortative "let me hear"; שָׁלוֹם with its token; חֲסִידָיו "his faithful ones" (חָסִיד, one who keeps *chesed*); וְאַל־יָשׁוּבוּ "and let them not turn back"; כִּסְלָה "folly" (the same word is "confidence" in Job 4:6). The Septuagint has another last line (from memory).
- **"that glory may dwell in our land" → "that glory may abide in our land"** (v9) — Hebrew לִשְׁכֹּן כָּבוֹד, שָׁכַן "abide"; the Hebrew order of the first line (the predicate first) is kept.
- **"Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other" → "Chesed and truth have met; righteousness and shalom have kissed"** (v10) — Hebrew נִפְגָּשׁוּ, נָשָׁקוּ, perfects; חֶסֶד and שָׁלוֹם with their tokens; "together", "each other" are in the verbs' sense, not written.
- **"Truth shall spring out of the earth; and righteousness shall look down from heaven" → "Truth from the earth springs up, and righteousness from the heavens has looked down"** (v11) — Hebrew תִּצְמָח imperfect, נִשְׁקָף perfect, as printed; אֶרֶץ "earth" (set against the heavens; "land" in v1, 9, 12: one word); שָׁמַיִם "heavens", plural.
- **"Yea, the LORD shall give that which is good; and our land shall yield her increase" → "Also Yahweh will give what is good, and our land will give its produce"** (v12) — Hebrew גַּם "also"; the one verb נָתַן "give" in both lines; יְבוּל "produce".
- **"Righteousness shall go before him; and shall set us in the way of his steps" → "Righteousness will walk before him, and will set his steps for a way"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהַלֵּךְ "walk"; וְיָשֵׂם לְדֶרֶךְ פְּעָמָיו, literally "and set for a way his steps": no "us"; the sense (righteousness makes a way for his steps, or he sets his steps on the way of righteousness) is not certain.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v13 = Heb v14.
- **Structure** — what Yahweh has done (v1–3), plea (v4–7), the word that is heard (v8–13).
