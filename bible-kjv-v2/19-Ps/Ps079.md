# Psalm 79

A psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, nations have come into your inheritance;
they have defiled your holy temple;
they have set ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ as heaps of ruins.

[2] They have given the dead bodies of your servants
as food to the birds of the heavens,
the flesh of your faithful ones to the living things of the earth.

[3] They have poured out their blood like water
all around ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
and there was none to bury.

[4] We have become a reproach to our neighbors,
a mockery and a derision to those around us.

[5] How long, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧? Will you be angry forever?
Will your jealousy burn like fire?

[6] Pour out your fury on the nations that have not known you,
and on kingdoms that have not called on your name!

[7] For he has devoured ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and his habitation they have laid waste.

[8] Do not remember against us former iniquities;
quickly let your compassion come to meet us,
for we have been brought very low.

[9] Help us, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our salvation,
because of the glory of your name;
and deliver us, and make atonement for our sins,
on account of your name!

[10] Why should the nations say,
"Where is their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"
Let there be known among the nations before our eyes
the avenging of the shed blood of your servants!

[11] Let the groaning of the prisoner come before you;
according to the greatness of your arm
leave over the sons of death!

[12] And return to our neighbors sevenfold into their bosom
their reproach with which they have reproached you, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧!

[13] But we, your people and the flock of your pasture,
will give thanks to you forever;
to generation and generation we will recount your praise.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 9, 10) — Hebrew *Elohim*. Ps 79 is one of the Elohistic psalms (42–83), which have אֱלֹהִים where others have יהוה: kept as printed.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"O Lord" → "Adonai"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"O God", "O LORD", "O Lord" → no "O"** (v1, 5, 9, 12) — the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"A Psalm of Asaph" → "A psalm of Asaph"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְאָסָף, the first words of v1; לְ "of" (by, for or belonging to: not decided).
- **"the heathen are come … thy holy temple have they defiled; they have laid Jerusalem on heaps" → "nations have come … they have defiled your holy temple; they have set Jerusalem as heaps of ruins"** (v1) — Hebrew גוֹיִם, without the article (v6, 10 have הַגּוֹיִם "the nations"); הֵיכַל קָדְשֶׁךָ, literally "the temple of your holiness"; שָׂמוּ … לְעִיִּים "they set … as heaps of ruins" (עִי, a heap of ruins).
- **"The dead bodies of thy servants … to be meat unto the fowls of the heaven, the flesh of thy saints unto the beasts of the earth" → "the dead bodies of your servants as food to the birds of the heavens, the flesh of your faithful ones to the living things of the earth"** (v2) — Hebrew נִבְלַת, a singular said of them all (the dead body of a man, as Deut 21:23); חֲסִידֶיךָ "your faithful ones" (חָסִיד, one who keeps *chesed*; not "saints"); חַיְתוֹ־אָרֶץ "the living things of the earth" (the word says "living", not "beasts"). עֲבָדֶיךָ (v2, 10): the word is "slaves", said of the servants of God.
- **"Their blood have they shed … and there was none to bury them" → "They have poured out their blood … and there was none to bury"** (v3) — Hebrew שָׁפְכוּ "they poured out"; וְאֵין קוֹבֵר, literally "and there is none burying": "them" is not written.
- **"a scorn and derision" → "a mockery and a derision"** (v4) — Hebrew לַעַג וָקֶלֶס. חֶרְפָּה "reproach" (v4, 12).
- **"How long, LORD? wilt thou be angry for ever?" → "How long, Yahweh? Will you be angry forever?"** (v5) — Hebrew עַד־מָה, literally "until what"; לָנֶצַח "forever" (CZ „navždy“, apart from לְעוֹלָם in v13). קִנְאָתֶךָ "your jealousy" ("zeal" is also possible).
- **"thy wrath upon the heathen … upon the kingdoms" → "your fury on the nations … on kingdoms"** (v6) — Hebrew חֲמָתְךָ "your fury" (literally "heat"); מַמְלָכוֹת without the article; perfects "have not known … have not called on your name" (קָרָא בְּשֵׁם). Jer 10:25 has nearly the same words (עַל for אֶל, מִשְׁפָּחוֹת "clans" for מַמְלָכוֹת).
- **"they have devoured Jacob, and laid waste his dwelling place" → "he has devoured Jacob, and his habitation they have laid waste"** (v7) — Hebrew אָכַל, a singular verb (the enemy as one), then the plural הֵשַׁמּוּ; Jer 10:25 has the plural אָכְלוּ. נָוֵהוּ "his habitation" (literally "pasture, abode").
- **"O remember not against us former iniquities: let thy tender mercies speedily prevent us: for we are brought very low" → "Do not remember against us former iniquities; quickly let your compassion come to meet us, for we have been brought very low"** (v8) — Hebrew עֲוֹנֹת רִאשֹׁנִים: "former iniquities", or "the iniquities of the former ones" (the fathers); the form allows both. רַחֲמֶיךָ "your compassion" (a plural noun); יְקַדְּמוּנוּ "come to meet us" (קִדֵּם; "prevent" in its old sense). דַּלּוֹנוּ, literally "we are low" (דָּלַל; its row has "become poor", Judg 6:6, but no loss of goods is named here).
- **"for the glory of thy name: and deliver us, and purge away our sins, for thy name's sake" → "because of the glory of your name; and deliver us, and make atonement for our sins, on account of your name"** (v9) — Hebrew עַל־דְּבַר, literally "upon the matter of"; וְהַצִּילֵנוּ opens the second line with "and"; כַּפֵּר עַל "make atonement for" (the root is often taken as "cover"); לְמַעַן "on account of".
- **"Wherefore should the heathen say … let him be known among the heathen in our sight by the revenging of the blood of thy servants which is shed" → "Why should the nations say … Let there be known among the nations before our eyes the avenging of the shed blood of your servants"** (v10) — Hebrew יִוָּדַע "let it be known", with נִקְמַת "the avenging" as its subject ("him" and "by" are not written); לְעֵינֵינוּ "before our eyes"; הַשָּׁפוּךְ "that is shed". The written text has בגיים, the qere בַּגּוֹיִם: one word in two spellings, no change of meaning.
- **"the sighing of the prisoner … the greatness of thy power preserve thou those that are appointed to die" → "the groaning of the prisoner … the greatness of your arm leave over the sons of death"** (v11) — Hebrew אֶנְקַת "groaning"; זְרוֹעֲךָ "your arm"; הוֹתֵר "leave over" (let remain, keep from being cut off); בְּנֵי תְמוּתָה "sons of death" (תְּמוּתָה, a rarer word than מָוֶת: those marked for death).
- **"And render unto our neighbours sevenfold into their bosom their reproach" → "And return to our neighbors sevenfold into their bosom their reproach"** (v12) — Hebrew וְהָשֵׁב "and return"; חֶרְפָּתָם אֲשֶׁר חֵרְפוּךָ "their reproach with which they have reproached you" (noun and verb of one root).
- **"So we thy people and sheep of thy pasture … we will shew forth thy praise to all generations" → "But we, your people and the flock of your pasture, … to generation and generation we will recount your praise"** (v13) — Hebrew וַאֲנַחְנוּ: the וְ before "we" sets the people against the nations; וְצֹאן "and the flock" (sheep and goats); לְדֹר וָדֹר "to generation and generation"; נְסַפֵּר "we will recount".
- **Poetic structure** — description of destruction (v1-4), lament and plea (v5-12), closing vow (v13).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **Lament after Jerusalem's destruction** — likely 586 BC context.
