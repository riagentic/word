# Psalm 79

A Psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧.

[1] O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the nations have come into your inheritance;
they have defiled your holy temple;
they have laid ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ in ruins.

[2] They have given the bodies of your servants
to the birds of the heavens for food,
the flesh of your faithful ones to the beasts of the earth.

[3] They have poured out their blood like water
all around ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,
and there was no one to bury them.

[4] We have become a taunt to our neighbors,
mocked and derided by those around us.

[5] How long, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧? Will you be angry forever?
Will your jealousy burn like fire?

[6] Pour out your anger on the nations that do not know you,
and on the kingdoms that do not call upon your name!

[7] For they have devoured ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
and laid waste his habitation.

[8] Do not remember against us our former iniquities;
let your compassion come speedily to meet us,
for we are brought very low.

[9] Help us, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our salvation,
for the glory of your name;
deliver us, and atone for our sins,
for your name's sake!

[10] Why should the nations say,
"Where is their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?"
Let the avenging of the outpoured blood of your servants
be known among the nations before our eyes!

[11] Let the groans of the prisoner come before you;
according to the greatness of your arm,
preserve those doomed to die!

[12] Return sevenfold into the lap of our neighbors
the taunts with which they have taunted you, O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧!

[13] But we your people, the flock of your pasture,
will give thanks to you forever;
from generation to generation we will recount your praise.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — description of destruction (v1-4), lament and plea (v5-12), closing vow (v13).

### Notes
- **Lament after Jerusalem's destruction** — likely 586 BC context.
