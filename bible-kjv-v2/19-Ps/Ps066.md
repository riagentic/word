# Psalm 66

To the overseer. A song, a psalm.

[1] Shout to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, all the earth!

[2] Make music to the glory of his name;
make glory his praise.

[3] Say to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "How fearsome are your works!
In the greatness of your strength
your enemies cringe before you.

[4] All the earth shall bow down to you
and shall make music to you;
they shall make music to your name." Selah.

[5] Come and see the doings of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
fearsome in deed toward the sons of man.

[6] He turned the sea into dry land;
through the river they passed over on foot.
There let us rejoice in him,

[7] who rules by his might forever;
his eyes keep watch on the nations;
the stubborn — let them not rise up for themselves. Selah.

[8] Bless our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, peoples,
and make the voice of his praise heard,

[9] who sets our soul in life
and has not given our foot to slip.

[10] For you have tried us, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
you have refined us as silver is refined.

[11] You brought us into the net;
you laid pressure on our loins.

[12] You made mortal man ride over our head;
we came into fire and into water;
and you brought us out to saturation.

[13] I will come into your house with burnt offerings;
I will repay to you my vows,

[14] which my lips opened
and my mouth spoke in my distress.

[15] Burnt offerings of fatlings I will offer up to you,
with the smoke of rams;
I will offer cattle with he-goats. Selah.

[16] Come, hear, and let me recount, all who fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
what he has done for my soul.

[17] To him I called with my mouth,
and extolling was under my tongue.

[18] If I had seen wickedness in my heart,
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ would not hear.

[19] Surely ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has heard;
he has given heed to the voice of my prayer.

[20] Blessed is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
who has not removed my prayer
and his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ from me.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 3, 5, 8, 10, 16, 19, 20) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*. An Elohistic psalm: אֱלֹהִים as printed. **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v18) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **No "O"** (v8, 10) — the Hebrew vocatives have none.
- **"To the chief Musician, A Song or Psalm" → "To the overseer. A song, a psalm."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ שִׁיר מִזְמוֹר, the first words of v1: לַמְנַצֵּחַ, participle of נִצַּח "oversee" (1 Chr 15:21), traditional "To the chief musician"; שִׁיר "a song", מִזְמוֹר "a psalm", in this order; there is no "or".
- **"Make a joyful noise unto God, all ye lands" → "Shout to Elohim, all the earth!"** (v1) — Hebrew הָרִיעוּ, the verb of תְּרוּעָה "shout"; כָּל־הָאָרֶץ "all the earth" (as v4).
- **"Sing forth the honour of his name" → "Make music to the glory of his name"** (v2) — Hebrew זַמְּרוּ כְבוֹד־שְׁמוֹ: זִמֵּר "make music" (with voice and instrument), with the glory as its direct object ("to" is supplied); כָּבוֹד "glory". **"make his praise glorious" → "make glory his praise"** — Hebrew שִׂימוּ כָבוֹד תְּהִלָּתוֹ, literally "put glory, his praise", in this order: "make glory (to be) his praise" or "make his praise (into) glory"; the verse keeps the Hebrew order.
- **"How terrible art thou in thy works!" → "How fearsome are your works!"** (v3) — Hebrew מַה־נּוֹרָא מַעֲשֶׂיךָ: נוֹרָא "fearsome" (niphal participle of יָרֵא "fear") with "your works" as subject. **"through the greatness of thy power shall thine enemies submit themselves unto thee" → "In the greatness of your strength your enemies cringe before you"** — Hebrew בְּרֹב עֻזְּךָ יְכַחֲשׁוּ לְךָ אֹיְבֶיךָ: עֹז "strength"; כִּחֵשׁ לְ, literally "lie to": feign submission (as Deut 33:29; Ps 18:44).
- **"All the earth shall worship thee, and shall sing unto thee" → "All the earth shall bow down to you and shall make music to you"** (v4) — Hebrew יִשְׁתַּחֲווּ "bow down" (not "worship"); זִמֵּר as v2. The imperfects may also be read as wishes ("let all the earth bow down"). The Hebrew has no quotation marks: where the words of v3 end is not marked; the verse closes them at v4 by the sense.
- **"the works of God: he is terrible in his doing toward the children of men" → "the doings of Elohim, fearsome in deed toward the sons of man"** (v5) — Hebrew מִפְעֲלוֹת אֱלֹהִים נוֹרָא עֲלִילָה עַל־בְּנֵי אָדָם, with no "he is": מִפְעָלוֹת "doings" (from פָּעַל; v3 has מַעֲשֶׂה "works"); עֲלִילָה "deed"; בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man" (not "children"); עַל "toward" (or "over").
- **"they went through the flood on foot" → "through the river they passed over on foot"** (v6) — Hebrew בַּנָּהָר יַעַבְרוּ בְרָגֶל, in this order; נָהָר "river"; עָבַר "pass over". **"there did we rejoice in him" → "There let us rejoice in him"** — Hebrew נִשְׂמְחָה, a cohortative: "let us rejoice" (it may also be read in the past of the story).
- **"He ruleth by his power for ever; his eyes behold the nations" → "who rules by his might forever; his eyes keep watch on the nations"** (v7) — Hebrew מֹשֵׁל בִּגְבוּרָתוֹ עוֹלָם, a participle running on from "him" in v6; גְּבוּרָה "might"; צָפָה "keep watch". **"let not the rebellious exalt themselves" → "the stubborn — let them not rise up for themselves"** — qere יָרוּמוּ "let them not rise up"; the ketiv ירימו is "let them not lift up (themselves)". סוֹרֵר "stubborn" (as Deut 21:18 "stubborn and rebellious"); לָמוֹ "for themselves".
- **"O bless our God, ye people" → "Bless our Elohim, peoples"** (v8) — Hebrew עַמִּים, plural. **"make the voice of his praise to be heard" → "make the voice of his praise heard"** — Hebrew וְהַשְׁמִיעוּ קוֹל תְּהִלָּתוֹ.
- **"Which holdeth our soul in life, and suffereth not our feet to be moved" → "who sets our soul in life and has not given our foot to slip"** (v9) — Hebrew הַשָּׂם נַפְשֵׁנוּ בַּחַיִּים, "who sets"; וְלֹא־נָתַן לַמּוֹט רַגְלֵנוּ, literally "has not given our foot to slipping"; רֶגֶל singular.
- **"For thou, O God, hast proved us: thou hast tried us, as silver is tried" → "For you have tried us, Elohim; you have refined us as silver is refined"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּחַנְתָּנוּ (בָּחַן "try") and צְרַפְתָּנוּ כִּצְרָף־כָּסֶף (צָרַף "refine", the smelting of metal), two verbs.
- **"Thou broughtest us into the net; thou laidst affliction upon our loins" → "You brought us into the net; you laid pressure on our loins"** (v11) — Hebrew מְצוּדָה, here the hunter's "net" (the same word is "stronghold" in 2 Sam 22:2; Ps 18:2: "into the stronghold" = prison is also read); מוּעָקָה "pressure" is found only here (from עוּק "press"): uncertain; מָתְנַיִם "loins".
- **"Thou hast caused men to ride over our heads" → "You made mortal man ride over our head"** (v12) — Hebrew הִרְכַּבְתָּ אֱנוֹשׁ לְרֹאשֵׁנוּ: אֱנוֹשׁ "mortal man", singular; "head" singular. **"we went through fire and through water" → "we came into fire and into water"** — Hebrew בָּאנוּ־בָאֵשׁ וּבַמַּיִם, בּוֹא "come". **"but thou broughtest us out into a wealthy place" → "and you brought us out to saturation"** — Hebrew וַתּוֹצִיאֵנוּ לָרְוָיָה, "and"; רְוָיָה "saturation" (drinking one's fill, as Ps 23:5 "my cup is brimful"). The Greek and other versions have "to refreshment" (from memory); the verse keeps the MT.
- **"I will go into thy house" → "I will come into your house"** (v13) — Hebrew אָבוֹא, as v12 "we came". **"I will pay thee my vows" → "I will repay to you my vows"** — Hebrew אֲשַׁלֵּם, שִׁלֵּם "repay, make whole"; נֶדֶר "vow".
- **"Which my lips have uttered, and my mouth hath spoken, when I was in trouble" → "which my lips opened and my mouth spoke in my distress"** (v14) — Hebrew פָּצוּ שְׂפָתָי, פָּצָה "open (wide)", of the mouth in a vow (Judg 11:35, 36); בַּצַּר־לִי "in distress to me".
- **"I will offer unto thee burnt sacrifices of fatlings, with the incense of rams" → "Burnt offerings of fatlings I will offer up to you, with the smoke of rams"** (v15) — Hebrew עֹלוֹת מֵחִים אַעֲלֶה־לָּךְ, in this order; הֶעֱלָה "offer up", literally "make go up", the verb of עֹלָה. קְטֹרֶת is elsewhere "incense"; here of rams on the altar: the smoke of their burning (the one word: note). **"I will offer bullocks with goats" → "I will offer cattle with he-goats"** — Hebrew אֶעֱשֶׂה בָקָר עִם־עַתּוּדִים: עָשָׂה of a sacrifice "offer" (literally "do, make"); בָּקָר "herd, cattle" (a collective; not "bullocks"); עַתּוּד "he-goat".
- **"Come and hear, all ye that fear God, and I will declare" → "Come, hear, and let me recount, all who fear Elohim,"** (v16) — Hebrew לְכוּ־שִׁמְעוּ וַאֲסַפְּרָה כָּל־יִרְאֵי אֱלֹהִים, in this order; וַאֲסַפְּרָה is a cohortative, סִפֵּר "recount".
- **"I cried unto him with my mouth" → "To him I called with my mouth"** (v17) — Hebrew אֵלָיו פִּי־קָרָאתִי, "to him" first; קָרָא "call". **"and he was extolled with my tongue" → "and extolling was under my tongue"** — Hebrew וְרוֹמַם תַּחַת לְשׁוֹנִי: רוֹמַם taken as a noun, "extolling, high praise" (as רוֹמְמוֹת Ps 149:6); it may also be read as a verb, "and he was extolled" (KJV). תַּחַת "under" (= ready on the tongue).
- **"If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me" → "If I had seen wickedness in my heart, Adonai would not hear"** (v18) — Hebrew אָוֶן אִם־רָאִיתִי בְלִבִּי לֹא יִשְׁמַע אֲדֹנָי, "wickedness" first; אָוֶן "wickedness" (not עָוֹן "iniquity"); רָאָה "see" (= look on with favor); "me" is not in the Hebrew. A perfect and an imperfect: the past "had seen … would not" by the sense of v19; "if I see … will not hear" is also possible.
- **"But verily God hath heard me" → "Surely Elohim has heard"** (v19) — Hebrew אָכֵן "surely, truly" (adversative: "but truly"); no "me". **"he hath attended to" → "he has given heed to"** — Hebrew הִקְשִׁיב בְּ.
- **"Blessed be God" → "Blessed is Elohim"** (v20) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ אֱלֹהִים, no verb ("is", as Ps 28:6). **"which hath not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me" → "who has not removed my prayer and his chesed from me"** — Hebrew לֹא־הֵסִיר תְּפִלָּתִי וְחַסְדּוֹ מֵאִתִּי: one verb for two objects, "and his chesed" (no "nor"); הֵסִיר "remove". Token on *chesed*.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the same as ours: the heading is the first words of v1.
- **Structure** — all the earth (v1–7), the people tried and brought out (v8–12), the speaker's vows (v13–20).
