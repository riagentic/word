# Psalm 66

To the chief musician. A Song. A Psalm.

[1] Shout for joy to *Elohim*, all the earth;

[2] sing the glory of his name;
give to him glorious praise!

[3] Say to *Elohim*, "How awesome are your deeds!
So great is your power
that your enemies come cringing to you.

[4] All the earth worships you
and sings praises to you;
they sing praises to your name." Selah.

[5] Come and see what *Elohim* has done:
he is awesome in his deeds toward the children of men.

[6] He turned the sea into dry land;
they passed through the river on foot.
There did we rejoice in him,

[7] who rules by his might forever,
whose eyes keep watch on the nations —
let not the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah.

[8] Bless our *Elohim*, O peoples;
let the sound of his praise be heard,

[9] who has kept our soul among the living
and has not let our feet slip.

[10] For you, O *Elohim*, have tested us;
you have tried us as silver is tried.

[11] You brought us into the net;
you laid a crushing burden on our backs;

[12] you let men ride over our heads;
we went through fire and through water;
yet you have brought us out to a place of abundance.

[13] I will come into your house with burnt offerings;
I will perform my vows to you,

[14] that which my lips uttered
and my mouth promised when I was in trouble.

[15] I will offer to you burnt offerings of fattened animals,
with the smoke of the sacrifice of rams;
I will make an offering of bulls and goats. Selah.

[16] Come and hear, all you who fear *Elohim*,
and I will tell what he has done for my soul.

[17] I cried to him with my mouth,
and high praise was on my tongue.

[18] If I had cherished iniquity in my heart,
Lord would not have listened.

[19] But truly *Elohim* has listened;
he has attended to the voice of my prayer.

[20] Blessed be *Elohim*,
because he has not rejected my prayer
or removed his steadfast love from me!

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 3, 5, 8, 10, 16, 19, 20) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — universal praise (v1-7), community thanksgiving (v8-12), individual vow (v13-20).
