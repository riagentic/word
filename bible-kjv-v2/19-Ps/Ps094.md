# Psalm 94

[1] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦el|El|God⟧ of vengeance,
O ⟦el|El|God⟧ of vengeance, shine forth!

[2] Rise up, O Judge of the earth;
repay to the proud what they deserve!

[3] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, how long shall the wicked,
how long shall the wicked exult?

[4] They pour out their arrogant words;
all the evildoers boast.

[5] They crush your people, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and afflict your heritage.

[6] They kill the widow and the sojourner,
and murder the fatherless;

[7] and they say, "⟦yah|Yah|The LORD⟧ does not see;
the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ does not perceive."

[8] Understand, O stupid ones among the people!
Fools, when will you be wise?

[9] He who planted the ear, does he not hear?
He who formed the eye, does he not see?

[10] He who disciplines the nations, does he not rebuke?
He who teaches man knowledge —

[11] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ knows the thoughts of man,
that they are but a breath.

[12] Blessed is the man whom you discipline, O ⟦yah|Yah|LORD⟧,
and whom you teach out of your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧,

[13] to give him rest from days of trouble,
until a pit is dug for the wicked.

[14] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will not forsake his people;
he will not abandon his heritage;

[15] for justice will return to the righteous,
and all the upright in heart will follow it.

[16] Who rises up for me against the wicked?
Who stands up for me against evildoers?

[17] If ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had not been my help,
my soul would soon have lived in the land of silence.

[18] When I thought, "My foot slips,"
your steadfast love, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, held me up.

[19] When the cares of my heart are many,
your consolations cheer my soul.

[20] Can wicked rulers be allied with you,
those who frame injustice by statute?

[21] They band together against the life of the righteous
and condemn the innocent to death.

[22] But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has become my stronghold,
and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the rock of my refuge.

[23] He will bring back on them their iniquity
and wipe them out for their wickedness;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will wipe them out.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 5, 11, 14, 17, 18, 22, 23) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"El"** (v1) — Hebrew *El*, retained.
- **"Yah"** (v7, 12) — shortened Yahweh; retained.
- **Poetic structure** — appeal to divine avenger (v1-7), wisdom polemic against atheism (v8-11), personal testimony of sustenance (v12-23).
