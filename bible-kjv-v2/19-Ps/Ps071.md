# Psalm 71

[1] In you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I have taken refuge;
let me never be ashamed.

[2] In your righteousness you will deliver me and rescue me;
incline your ear to me and save me.

[3] Be to me a rock of abode,
to come to continually;
you have commanded to save me,
for my crag and my stronghold are you.

[4] My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, rescue me from the hand of the wicked,
from the palm of the unjust and the ruthless.

[5] For you are my hope;
⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, my trust from my youth.

[6] Upon you I have leaned from the belly;
from the inward parts of my mother you are the one who severed me.
In you is my praise continually.

[7] Like a wonder I have become to many,
and you are my refuge of strength.

[8] Let my mouth be filled with your praise,
all the day with your beauty.

[9] Do not throw me away at the time of old age;
when my power comes to an end, do not forsake me.

[10] For my enemies have spoken of me,
and those who watch my soul have taken counsel together,

[11] saying, "⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has forsaken him;
pursue and seize him,
for there is no one who delivers."

[12] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not be far from me;
my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, hasten to my help.

[13] Let them be ashamed, let them come to an end, those who are adversaries to my soul;
let them be wrapped in reproach and dishonor,
those who seek my evil.

[14] And I, continually I will hope,
and I will add to all your praise.

[15] My mouth will recount your righteousness,
all the day your salvation,
for I do not know the numbers.

[16] I will come with the mighty deeds of ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧;
I will mention your righteousness, yours alone.

[17] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you have taught me from my youth,
and until now I tell your wonderful deeds.

[18] And even until old age and gray hair,
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not forsake me,
until I tell your arm to a generation,
to all who are to come your might.

[19] And your righteousness, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, is up to the height,
you who have done great things;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who is like you?

[20] You who have made me see distresses many and evil,
you will return, you will make me live;
and from the deeps of the earth
you will return, you will bring me up.

[21] Increase my greatness,
and turn around, comfort me.

[22] I also will give thanks to you with an instrument of harp,
for your truth, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
let me make music to you with the lyre,
Holy One of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[23] My lips will shout for joy when I make music to you,
and my soul, which you have ransomed.

[24] My tongue also all the day will softly recite your righteousness,
for they are ashamed, for they are abashed,
those who seek my evil.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 11, 12, 17, 18, 19, 22) — Hebrew *Elohim* (v4, 12b, 22 אֱלֹהַי "my Elohim"); an Elohistic psalm (42–83), the names stand as printed.
- **"O Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v5, 16) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words. No vocative "O" anywhere: the Hebrew has no such word.
- **v1–3 beside Ps 31:1–3** — the wording of Ps 31 is kept where the Hebrew is the same (v1 = Ps 31:1a; "incline your ear to me", v2; "for my crag and my stronghold are you", v3 = Ps 31:3a); the differences are at each verse below.
- **"do I put my trust: let me never be put to confusion" → "I have taken refuge; let me never be ashamed"** (v1) — Hebrew חָסִיתִי, perfect of חָסָה "take refuge"; אַל־אֵבוֹשָׁה לְעוֹלָם, lit. "let me not be ashamed forever" (= never); בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed". The same words as Ps 31:1.
- **"Deliver me in thy righteousness, and cause me to escape: incline thine ear unto me" → "In your righteousness you will deliver me and rescue me; incline your ear to me"** (v2) — Hebrew תַּצִּילֵנִי וּתְפַלְּטֵנִי, imperfects (also readable as a wish: "deliver me"; Ps 31:1 has the imperative פַלְּטֵנִי and no תַּצִּילֵנִי); הִצִּיל "deliver", פִּלֵּט "rescue" (two verbs, kept apart); הַטֵּה "incline".
- **"Be thou my strong habitation, whereunto I may continually resort: thou hast given commandment to save me" → "Be to me a rock of abode, to come to continually; you have commanded to save me"** (v3) — Hebrew הֱיֵה לִי לְצוּר מָעוֹן "be to me a rock of abode": מָעוֹן "abode" (as Ps 26:8; Ps 31:2 has מָעוֹז "a strong place", one letter apart, and many manuscripts read it here too, from memory; the Masoretic Text is kept); לָבוֹא תָּמִיד "to come continually" (the "to" after "come" is English idiom; "I may" is not written); צִוִּיתָ "you have commanded" (the Greek has a different line here, from memory); כִּי־סַלְעִי וּמְצוּדָתִי אָתָּה as Ps 31:3: סֶלַע "crag", מְצוּדָה "stronghold" (צוּר is "rock").
- **"Deliver me, O my God, out of the hand of the wicked, out of the hand of the unrighteous and cruel man" → "My Elohim, rescue me from the hand of the wicked, from the palm of the unjust and the ruthless"** (v4) — Hebrew פַּלְּטֵנִי "rescue me" (פִּלֵּט); רָשָׁע "wicked" (singular); מִכַּף "from the palm" (beside יָד "hand"); מְעַוֵּל "one who does injustice" (עָוֶל); חוֹמֵץ, found only here, "the ruthless" (from חָמֵץ "be sour"; or a form of חָמַס "do violence"; uncertain); "man" is not written.
- **"thou art my trust from my youth" → "my trust from my youth"** (v5) — Hebrew תִקְוָתִי "my hope", then אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה מִבְטַחִי מִנְּעוּרָי with no verb; the lines follow the accents (the first half ends at "my hope"). מִבְטָח "trust" (that in which one trusts).
- **"By thee have I been holden up from the womb: thou art he that took me out of my mother's bowels: my praise shall be continually of thee" → "Upon you I have leaned from the belly; from the inward parts of my mother you are the one who severed me. In you is my praise continually"** (v6) — Hebrew עָלֶיךָ נִסְמַכְתִּי, nifal of סָמַךְ "lean, be supported"; מִבֶּטֶן "from the belly" (as Ps 22:9, 10); מִמְּעֵי אִמִּי "from the inward parts of my mother"; גּוֹזִי, found only here: from גָּזָה / גָּזַז "cut" (the cutting at birth); the sense is uncertain ("who took me out" follows Ps 22:9 גֹּחִי, another word); בְּךָ תְהִלָּתִי "in you is my praise", no verb.
- **"I am as a wonder unto many; but thou art my strong refuge" → "Like a wonder I have become to many, and you are my refuge of strength"** (v7) — Hebrew כְּמוֹפֵת "like a wonder" (מוֹפֵת, a sign that astonishes: for good or for ill, not said); הָיִיתִי "I have become"; וְאַתָּה "and you"; מַחֲסִי־עֹז "my refuge of strength" (מַחְסֶה "refuge", עֹז "strength").
- **"Let my mouth be filled with thy praise and with thy honour all the day" → "Let my mouth be filled with your praise, all the day with your beauty"** (v8) — Hebrew יִמָּלֵא, jussive; תִּפְאַרְתֶּךָ "your beauty" (תִּפְאֶרֶת; not "honour"); "and" is not written; Hebrew order kept.
- **"Cast me not off in the time of old age; forsake me not when my strength faileth" → "Do not throw me away at the time of old age; when my power comes to an end, do not forsake me"** (v9) — Hebrew תַּשְׁלִיכֵנִי, הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw"; זִקְנָה "old age" (שֵׂיבָה "gray hair" stands beside it in v18); כִּכְלוֹת כֹּחִי "when my power comes to an end" (כָּלָה; כֹּחַ "power"); Hebrew order kept.
- **"For mine enemies speak against me; and they that lay wait for my soul take counsel together" → "For my enemies have spoken of me, and those who watch my soul have taken counsel together"** (v10) — Hebrew אָמְרוּ … לִי, perfect, "have spoken of (or to, against) me"; שֹׁמְרֵי נַפְשִׁי "those who watch my soul" (שָׁמַר "keep, watch": here of watching for it; "lay wait" interprets); נוֹעֲצוּ, nifal of יָעַץ "take counsel"; יַחְדָּו "together".
- **"persecute and take him; for there is none to deliver him" → "pursue and seize him, for there is no one who delivers"** (v11) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר "saying"; רִדְפוּ "pursue" (רָדַף); תִפְשׂוּהוּ "seize him" (תָּפַשׂ); אֵין מַצִּיל "there is no one who delivers" ("him" is not written).
- **"O my God, make haste for my help" → "my Elohim, hasten to my help"** (v12) — Hebrew אֱלֹהַי לְעֶזְרָתִי חוּשָׁה (qere; the written form חישה is another spelling of the same verb, no difference of sense); as Ps 70:1 and Ps 40:13.
- **"Let them be confounded and consumed that are adversaries to my soul; let them be covered with reproach and dishonour that seek my hurt" → "Let them be ashamed, let them come to an end, those who are adversaries to my soul; let them be wrapped in reproach and dishonor, those who seek my evil"** (v13) — Hebrew יֵבֹשׁוּ יִכְלוּ, two jussives without "and": בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", כָּלָה "come to an end"; שֹׂטְנֵי נַפְשִׁי, participle of שָׂטַן "be an adversary to"; יַעֲטוּ "let them wrap themselves" (עָטָה); חֶרְפָּה "reproach", כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (as Ps 69:19); רָעָתִי "my evil" (as Ps 70:2). The lines follow the accents.
- **"But I will hope continually, and will yet praise thee more and more" → "And I, continually I will hope, and I will add to all your praise"** (v14) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי "and I"; תָּמִיד first; יִחֵל "hope"; וְהוֹסַפְתִּי עַל־כָּל־תְּהִלָּתֶךָ "and I will add upon all your praise" (יָסַף hifil; "more and more" interprets).
- **"My mouth shall shew forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof" → "My mouth will recount your righteousness, all the day your salvation, for I do not know the numbers"** (v15) — Hebrew יְסַפֵּר "will recount" (סִפֵּר); תְּשׁוּעָתֶךָ "your salvation"; "and" is not written; סְפֹרוֹת, found only here, "numbers" (the same root as "recount": a play on words; also read "writings", so "I do not know letters"; uncertain); "thereof" is not written.
- **"I will go in the strength of the Lord GOD: I will make mention of thy righteousness, even of thine only" → "I will come with the mighty deeds of Lord Yahweh; I will mention your righteousness, yours alone"** (v16) — Hebrew אָבוֹא "I will come"; בִּגְבֻרוֹת "with the mighty deeds" (plural of גְּבוּרָה "might"; "deeds" is supplied); אַזְכִּיר "I will mention" (הִזְכִּיר, lit. "cause to be remembered"); לְבַדֶּךָ "yours alone".
- **"O God, thou hast taught me from my youth: and hitherto have I declared thy wondrous works" → "Elohim, you have taught me from my youth, and until now I tell your wonderful deeds"** (v17) — Hebrew לִמַּדְתַּנִי "you have taught me" (לִמֵּד); וְעַד־הֵנָּה "and until now"; אַגִּיד, imperfect of הִגִּיד "tell" (also v18); נִפְלְאוֹת "wonderful deeds".
- **"Now also when I am old and greyheaded, O God, forsake me not; until I have shewed thy strength unto this generation, and thy power to every one that is to come" → "And even until old age and gray hair, Elohim, do not forsake me, until I tell your arm to a generation, to all who are to come your might"** (v18) — Hebrew וְגַם עַד־זִקְנָה וְשֵׂיבָה "and even until old age and gray hair" (שֵׂיבָה lit. "gray hair": here beside זִקְנָה "old age", so the literal word); זְרוֹעֲךָ "your arm" ("strength" interprets); לְדוֹר "to a generation" ("this" is not written); לְכָל־יָבוֹא "to all who will come"; גְּבוּרָתֶךָ "your might"; Hebrew order kept.
- **"Thy righteousness also, O God, is very high, who hast done great things: O God, who is like unto thee!" → "And your righteousness, Elohim, is up to the height, you who have done great things; Elohim, who is like you?"** (v19) — Hebrew וְצִדְקָתְךָ "and your righteousness"; עַד־מָרוֹם "up to the height" (no verb; "heavens" is not written); אֲשֶׁר־עָשִׂיתָ גְדֹלוֹת "you who have done great things"; מִי כָמוֹךָ "who is like you?". The lines follow the accents.
- **"Thou, which hast shewed me great and sore troubles, shalt quicken me again, and shalt bring me up again from the depths of the earth" → "You who have made me see distresses many and evil, you will return, you will make me live; and from the deeps of the earth you will return, you will bring me up"** (v20) — Hebrew הִרְאִיתַנִי "you have made me see" and תְּחַיֵּינִי "you will make me live" are the qere; the written forms (ketiv) are plural, הראיתנו "you have made us see", תחיינו "you will make us live"; צָרוֹת רַבּוֹת וְרָעוֹת "distresses many and evil" (צָרָה "distress"); תָּשׁוּב … תָּשׁוּב "you will return" with a second verb, twice (= do again: "you will again make me live", "again bring me up"); חִיָּה piel "make live" (keep alive, revive); תְּהֹמוֹת הָאָרֶץ "the deeps of the earth" (תְּהוֹם, as Gen 1:2); הֶעֱלָה "bring up".
- **"Thou shalt increase my greatness, and comfort me on every side" → "Increase my greatness, and turn around, comfort me"** (v21) — Hebrew תֶּרֶב, the short (jussive) form of הִרְבָּה "increase": a wish; גְּדֻלָּתִי "my greatness"; וְתִסֹּב "and turn around" (סָבַב; KJV "on every side" takes it as "surround"); תְּנַחֲמֵנִי "comfort me", without "and" (the Greek reads "and comfort me", from memory).
- **"I will also praise thee with the psaltery, even thy truth, O my God: unto thee will I sing with the harp, O thou Holy One of Israel" → "I also will give thanks to you with an instrument of harp, for your truth, my Elohim; let me make music to you with the lyre, Holy One of Israel"** (v22) — Hebrew גַּם־אֲנִי "I also"; אוֹדְךָ, הוֹדָה "give thanks to"; בִכְלִי־נֶבֶל "with an instrument of harp" (נֵבֶל "harp", כִּנּוֹר "lyre"; kinds uncertain); אֲמִתְּךָ "your truth" stands after "you" with no preposition: "for" is supplied (or "I will praise you, your truth"); אֲזַמְּרָה, cohortative of זִמֵּר "make music"; קְדוֹשׁ יִשְׂרָאֵל "Holy One of Israel" (a name: Isa 1:4).
- **"My lips shall greatly rejoice when I sing unto thee; and my soul, which thou hast redeemed" → "My lips will shout for joy when I make music to you, and my soul, which you have ransomed"** (v23) — Hebrew תְּרַנֵּנָּה "will shout for joy" (רִנֵּן, as Ps 20:5; "greatly" is not written); כִּי "when, for"; אֲזַמְּרָה "I make music"; פָּדִיתָ "you have ransomed" (פָּדָה; גָּאַל is "redeem").
- **"My tongue also shall talk of thy righteousness all the day long: for they are confounded, for they are brought unto shame, that seek my hurt" → "My tongue also all the day will softly recite your righteousness, for they are ashamed, for they are abashed, those who seek my evil"** (v24) — Hebrew תֶּהְגֶּה, הָגָה "softly recite" (as Ps 1:2); בֹשׁוּ … חָפְרוּ, perfects: בּוֹשׁ "be ashamed", חָפֵר "be abashed" (another root than חָפַר "dig"; as Ps 70:2); כִּי … כִי "for … for"; רָעָתִי "my evil".

### Notes
- **No heading** — the Hebrew has none; the psalm runs on from Ps 70 in many manuscripts (from memory). Our verse numbers are the Hebrew ones.
- **Structure** — trust from the belly to gray hair (v1–13), hope and praise (v14–24); the shame wished on the enemies in v13 is told as done in v24.
