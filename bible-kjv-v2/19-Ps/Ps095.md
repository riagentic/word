# Psalm 95

[1] Oh come, let us sing to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation!

[2] Let us come into his presence with thanksgiving;
let us make a joyful noise to him with songs of praise!

[3] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a great ⟦el|El|God⟧,
and a great King above all gods.

[4] In his hand are the depths of the earth;
the heights of the mountains are his also.

[5] The sea is his, for he made it,
and his hands formed the dry land.

[6] Oh come, let us worship and bow down;
let us kneel before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our Maker!

[7] For he is our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and we are the people of his pasture,
and the sheep of his hand.
Today, if you hear his voice,

[8] do not harden your hearts, as at ⟦meribah|Merivah|Meribah⟧,
as on the day at Massah in the wilderness,

[9] when your fathers put me to the test
and put me to the proof, though they had seen my work.

[10] For forty years I loathed that generation
and said, "They are a people who go astray in their heart,
and they have not known my ways."

[11] Therefore I swore in my wrath,
"They shall not enter my rest."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 6) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"gods"** (v3) — Hebrew *elohim* (plural); the pagan gods.
- **Poetic structure** — call to worship (v1-7a), divine warning against hardened hearts (v7b-11).

### Notes
- **Invitation to worship** with warning (v7-11).
- **v7-11 cited Heb 3-4** — Christian exhortation.
