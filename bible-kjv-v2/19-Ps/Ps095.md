# Psalm 95

[1] Come, let us shout for joy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
let us shout to the Rock of our salvation.

[2] Let us come to meet his face with thanksgiving;
with songs let us shout to him.

[3] For a great ⟦el|El|God⟧ is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and a great King above all gods,

[4] in whose hand are the depths of the earth,
and the heights of the mountains are his,

[5] whose is the sea, and he made it,
and the dry land his hands formed.

[6] Come in, let us bow down and bow;
let us kneel before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our Maker.

[7] For he is our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
and we are the people of his pasture
and the flock of his hand.
Today, if you will listen to his voice,

[8] do not make your heart hard, as at ⟦meribah|Merivah|Meribah⟧,
as on the day of Massah in the wilderness,

[9] when your fathers tested me;
they tried me; they also saw my work.

[10] Forty years I loathed a generation,
and I said, "A people straying of heart are they,
and they have not known my ways."

[11] Therefore I swore in my anger,
"They shall not enter into my resting place."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 6) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"gods"** (v3) — Hebrew *elohim* (plural); other gods, plain text.
- **"O come, let us sing unto the LORD: let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation" → "Come, let us shout for joy to Yahweh; let us shout to the Rock of our salvation"** (v1) — Hebrew לְכוּ "come" (lit. "go"; no "O"); נְרַנְּנָה "let us shout for joy" (רִנֵּן, as Ps 33:1); נָרִיעָה "let us shout" (הֵרִיעַ of voices, also v2); צוּר of Yahweh "Rock" (capital); יֶשַׁע "salvation".
- **"Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms" → "Let us come to meet his face with thanksgiving; with songs let us shout to him"** (v2) — Hebrew נְקַדְּמָה פָנָיו: קִדֵּם "come to meet" (as Deut 23:4), פָּנִים "face"; תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving"; זְמִרוֹת "songs" (from זִמֵּר "make music"), first, as in the Hebrew.
- **"For the LORD is a great God" → "For a great El is Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי אֵל גָּדוֹל יְהוָה, in the Hebrew order.
- **"In his hand are the deep places of the earth: the strength of the hills is his also" → "in whose hand are the depths of the earth, and the heights of the mountains are his"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר בְּיָדוֹ, a relative clause on v3 (also v5 אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ "whose"); מֶחְקְרֵי־אָרֶץ "the depths of the earth", only here, lit. "the searched-out places" (from חָקַר "search out"); תּוֹעֲפוֹת הָרִים "the heights of the mountains": the word of Num 23:22 ("horns", uncertain; "strength", "heights" are the other senses), here of mountains, so "heights"; "also" is not written.
- **"The sea is his, and he made it: and his hands formed the dry land" → "whose is the sea, and he made it, and the dry land his hands formed"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ הַיָּם; "for" of the earlier wording is not written (וְ "and"); יַבֶּשֶׁת "dry land" (a form of יַבָּשָׁה); יָצַר "form".
- **"O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the LORD our maker" → "Come in, let us bow down and bow; let us kneel before Yahweh our Maker"** (v6) — Hebrew בֹּאוּ "come in" (another verb than לְכוּ, v1); נִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה "let us bow down" (הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה), וְנִכְרָעָה "and let us bow" (כָּרַע, to bend the knees: the two verbs as Esth 3:2); נִבְרְכָה "let us kneel" (בָּרַךְ, the root of "bless" and "knee"); עֹשֵׂנוּ "our Maker" (עָשָׂה "make").
- **"the sheep of his hand" → "the flock of his hand"** (v7) — Hebrew צֹאן "flock" (sheep and goats). **"To day if ye will hear his voice" → "Today, if you will listen to his voice"** — Hebrew הַיּוֹם אִם־בְּקֹלוֹ תִשְׁמָעוּ: שָׁמַע בְּקוֹל "listen to the voice of"; אִם may be "if" (the condition of v8) or a wish ("Oh that today you would listen to his voice!"). Heb 3:7, 15; 4:7 quote it as "Today, if you hear his voice" (from the Greek).
- **"Harden not your heart, as in the provocation, and as in the day of temptation in the wilderness" → "do not make your heart hard, as at Meribah, as on the day of Massah in the wilderness"** (v8) — Hebrew אַל־תַּקְשׁוּ לְבַבְכֶם: הִקְשָׁה "make hard" (as Exod 7:3); לְבַבְכֶם "your heart" (singular; "you" plural). כִּמְרִיבָה כְּיוֹם מַסָּה, lit. "as Meribah, as the day of Massah": the two place names of Exod 17:7 (מְרִיבָה "strife", מַסָּה "testing", Num 20:13; Deut 6:16; 33:8). The KJV and the Greek (Heb 3:8 "the provocation", "the trial") translate the names.
- **"When your fathers tempted me, proved me, and saw my work" → "when your fathers tested me; they tried me; they also saw my work"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר "when" (or "where"); נִסָּה "test" (not "tempt"); בָּחַן "try"; גַּם־רָאוּ פָעֳלִי "also they saw my work": גַּם may also be "although" ("though they had seen my work"). פֹּעַל "work", singular. Heb 3:9–10 joins "forty years" to the seeing (from the Greek).
- **"Forty years long was I grieved with this generation, and said, It is a people that do err in their heart, and they have not known my ways" → "Forty years I loathed a generation, and I said, 'A people straying of heart are they, and they have not known my ways'"** (v10) — Hebrew אָקוּט בְּדוֹר: קוּט "loathe" (to feel disgust at); דּוֹר without the article: "this" and "that" are not written. עַם תֹּעֵי לֵבָב הֵם "a people straying (תָּעָה "wander, go astray") of heart are they", in the Hebrew order; וְהֵם "and they".
- **"Unto whom I sware in my wrath that they should not enter into my rest" → "Therefore I swore in my anger, 'They shall not enter into my resting place'"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי בְאַפִּי: אֲשֶׁר "so that, therefore" (the KJV "unto whom" takes it as a relative on "a generation"); אַף "anger" (lit. "nose"). אִם־יְבֹאוּן is the oath formula, lit. "if they shall enter": a sworn "they shall not enter". מְנוּחָה "resting place" (as Deut 12:9, of the land); Heb 3:11; 4:3, 5 have "my rest" (κατάπαυσις, from the Greek).

### Notes
- **No heading** in the Hebrew.
- **Structure** — call to praise (v1–5), call to bow down (v6–7a), the warning from the wilderness (v7b–11).
- **v7–11 quoted in Heb 3:7–11; 4:3–7** — the verses here follow the Hebrew; the differences of the Greek quotation are named under v7–11.
