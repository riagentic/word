# Psalm 141

A psalm of David.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I have called you; hasten to me;
give ear to my voice when I call to you.

[2] Let my prayer be established, incense before you,
the lifting up of my palms, a grain offering of evening.

[3] Set, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, a watch for my mouth;
guard over the door of my lips.

[4] Do not turn my heart to an evil thing,
to practise doings in wickedness
with men who work wickedness;
and let me not eat of their delicacies.

[5] Let a righteous one strike me: it is ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧;
and let him reprove me: oil of the head;
let my head not refuse,
for still — and my prayer is in their evils.

[6] Their judges have been let drop by the sides of the crag,
and they will hear my sayings, for they are pleasant.

[7] As one who cleaves and splits on the earth,
our bones have been scattered at the mouth of Sheol.

[8] For to you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|GOD⟧, ⟦adonai|Adonai|my Lord⟧, are my eyes;
in you I have taken refuge; do not pour out my soul.

[9] Keep me from the hands of the trap they have laid for me,
and the snares of the workers of wickedness.

[10] Let the wicked fall into his nets
together — as for me, until I pass over.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3) — Tetragrammaton. **"GOD the Lord" → "Yahweh, Adonai"** (v8) — Hebrew יְהֹוִה אֲדֹנָי, in this order (the name pointed to be read *Elohim*), both words. No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word.
- **"A Psalm of David" → "A psalm of David"** (heading) — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד, the first words of v1. לְדָוִד may mean "by David", "for David" or "belonging to David"; "of David" leaves it open.
- **"LORD, I cry unto thee: make haste unto me; give ear unto my voice, when I cry unto thee" → "Yahweh, I have called you; hasten to me; give ear to my voice when I call to you"** (v1) — Hebrew קְרָאתִיךָ "I have called you" (קָרָא "call", both times); חוּשָׁה "hasten"; הַאֲזִינָה "give ear".
- **"Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense; and the lifting up of my hands as the evening sacrifice" → "Let my prayer be established, incense before you, the lifting up of my palms, a grain offering of evening"** (v2) — Hebrew תִּכּוֹן, nifal of כּוּן "be established" (as 140:11); קְטֹרֶת "incense" and מִנְחַת־עָרֶב "a grain offering of evening" stand beside the nouns without "as" (כְּ is not written) and without "and"; כַּפַּי "my palms"; מִנְחָה "grain offering", not "sacrifice" (that is זֶבַח).
- **"Set a watch, O LORD, before my mouth; keep the door of my lips" → "Set, Yahweh, a watch for my mouth; guard over the door of my lips"** (v3) — Hebrew שִׁיתָה "set"; שָׁמְרָה "a watch" (noun of שָׁמַר "keep"); לְפִי "for my mouth"; נִצְּרָה, נָצַר "guard", with עַל "over"; דַּל "door", in this form only here (= דֶּלֶת).
- **"Incline not my heart to any evil thing, to practise wicked works with men that work iniquity: and let me not eat of their dainties" → "Do not turn my heart to an evil thing, to practise doings in wickedness with men who work wickedness; and let me not eat of their delicacies"** (v4) — Hebrew אַל־תַּט, hifil of נָטָה "turn, incline"; לְדָבָר רָע "to an evil thing" (דָּבָר, also "word": "an evil word" is possible); לְהִתְעוֹלֵל עֲלִלוֹת, "to practise doings" (עֲלִילָה "doing", one root with the verb); בְּרֶשַׁע "in wickedness" (רֶשַׁע, noun of רָשָׁע "wicked"); פֹּעֲלֵי־אָוֶן "who work wickedness" (אָוֶן; English has one word for both: רֶשַׁע and אָוֶן stand in the same verse); אִישִׁים, a rare plural of אִישׁ "man"; אֶלְחַם, לָחַם "eat"; מַנְעַמִּים, found only here, "delicacies" (from נָעֵם "be pleasant", as v6).
- **"Let the righteous smite me; it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me; it shall be an excellent oil, which shall not break my head: for yet my prayer also shall be in their calamities" → "Let a righteous one strike me: it is chesed; and let him reprove me: oil of the head; let my head not refuse, for still — and my prayer is in their evils"** (v5) — hard text, the Hebrew kept as printed. יֶהֶלְמֵנִי "let him strike me" (הָלַם "strike, hammer"); צַדִּיק "a righteous one"; חֶסֶד stands alone after it, with its token: "it is *chesed*" (or "in *chesed*", "as *chesed*": the Hebrew has no word between); וְיוֹכִיחֵנִי, הוֹכִיחַ "reprove"; שֶׁמֶן רֹאשׁ "oil of the head" (finest oil, or oil for the head); אַל־יָנִי, from נוּא "restrain, refuse" (as Num 32:7), no "it" and not "break" (that reading needs another root); כִּי־עוֹד וּתְפִלָּתִי, "for still, and my prayer", the "and" kept; בְּרָעוֹתֵיהֶם "in their evils": against their evil deeds, or in the time of their calamities (KJV) — the Hebrew does not decide. The versions read differently (from memory); not followed.
- **"When their judges are overthrown in stony places, they shall hear my words; for they are sweet" → "Their judges have been let drop by the sides of the crag, and they will hear my sayings, for they are pleasant"** (v6) — hard text, kept as printed. נִשְׁמְטוּ, nifal of שָׁמַט "let drop" (thrown down), a perfect: no "when"; בִידֵי־סֶלַע, literally "by the hands of the crag" (יָד of a side, as לְיַד 140:5); סֶלַע "crag"; וְשָׁמְעוּ "and they will hear"; אֲמָרַי "my sayings" (אֵמֶר); נָעֵמוּ "they are pleasant". Who "they" are (the judges, or the people) is not said.
- **"Our bones are scattered at the grave's mouth, as when one cutteth and cleaveth wood upon the earth" → "As one who cleaves and splits on the earth, our bones have been scattered at the mouth of Sheol"** (v7) — hard text, kept as printed. כְּמוֹ פֹלֵחַ וּבֹקֵעַ, participles, "as one who cleaves and splits" (בָּקַע "split"); the object is not named: "wood" (KJV) and the furrows of a ploughman are both supplied by readers; בָּאָרֶץ "on the earth"; נִפְזְרוּ "have been scattered"; עֲצָמֵינוּ "our bones" (some read "their bones", from memory); לְפִי שְׁאוֹל "at the mouth of Sheol", plain word.
- **"But mine eyes are unto thee, O GOD the Lord: in thee is my trust; leave not my soul destitute" → "For to you, Yahweh, Adonai, are my eyes; in you I have taken refuge; do not pour out my soul"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי "for", not "but"; חָסִיתִי "I have taken refuge" (חָסָה בְּ); אַל־תְּעַר נַפְשִׁי, עָרָה piel "pour out, lay bare" (as Isa 53:12 "poured out his soul"), not "leave destitute"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"Keep me from the snares which they have laid for me, and the gins of the workers of iniquity" → "Keep me from the hands of the trap they have laid for me, and the snares of the workers of wickedness"** (v9) — Hebrew שָׁמְרֵנִי "keep me"; מִידֵי פַח "from the hands of the trap"; פַּח "trap", מוֹקְשׁוֹת "snares" (two words); יָקְשׁוּ, verb of מוֹקֵשׁ: "they have laid (as a snare)"; פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "the workers of wickedness" (as v4; Ps 5:5).
- **"Let the wicked fall into their own nets, whilst that I withal escape" → "Let the wicked fall into his nets together — as for me, until I pass over"** (v10) — Hebrew בְמַכְמֹרָיו "into his nets" (singular "his": Yahweh's, or each one's own; no "own"); יַחַד "together", set by the accents before אָנֹכִי; אָנֹכִי "I", standing alone: "as for me"; עַד־אֶעֱבוֹר "until I pass over" (עָבַר), not "escape"; the clause has no verb of its own.

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is the first words of v1 in the Hebrew; the verse numbers are the same.
- **v5–7** — among the hardest lines of the Psalter; the verses give the Masoretic Text word for word, and Changes names the open points.
