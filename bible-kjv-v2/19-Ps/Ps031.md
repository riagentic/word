# Psalm 31

To the overseer. A psalm. Of David.

[1] In you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I have taken refuge;
let me never be ashamed;
in your righteousness rescue me.

[2] Incline your ear to me; quickly deliver me;
be to me a rock of a strong place,
a house of strongholds, to save me.

[3] For my crag and my stronghold are you;
and on account of your name you will lead me and guide me.

[4] You will bring me out of the net that they have hidden for me,
for you are my strong place.

[5] Into your hand I commit my spirit;
you have ransomed me, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦el|El|God⟧ of truth.

[6] I hate those who keep empty vapors;
and I, in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ I trust.

[7] Let me rejoice and be glad in your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
that you have seen my affliction;
you have known the distresses of my soul,

[8] and you have not handed me over into the hand of the enemy;
you have made my feet stand in the broad place.

[9] Show me favor, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, for I am in distress;
my eye is wasted with vexation,
my soul and my belly.

[10] For my life is spent in sorrow,
and my years in sighing;
my power stumbles because of my iniquity,
and my bones are wasted.

[11] Because of all who harass me I have become a reproach,
and to my neighbors exceedingly,
and a dread to my acquaintances;
those who see me in the street have fled from me.

[12] I am forgotten like a dead man, out of the heart;
I have become like a perishing vessel.

[13] For I have heard the report of many,
terror on every side,
when they took counsel together against me;
to take my soul they devised.

[14] And I, on you I trust, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
I say, "You are my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[15] In your hand are my times;
deliver me from the hand of my enemies and from my pursuers.

[16] Make your face shine upon your servant;
save me in your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧.

[17] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, let me not be ashamed,
for I have called on you;
let the wicked be ashamed;
let them be silent to Sheol.

[18] Let the lips of falsehood be mute,
which speak arrogance against the righteous
in pride and contempt.

[19] How abundant is your goodness,
which you have hidden away for those who fear you,
you have worked for those who take refuge in you,
before the sons of man!

[20] You hide them in the hiding place of your face
from the plots of man;
you hide them away in a booth from the contention of tongues.

[21] Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for he has made his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ wonderful to me
in a city of siege.

[22] And I, I said in my alarm,
"I am cut off from before your eyes."
Surely you heard the voice of my pleas for favor
when I cried to you for help.

[23] Love ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, all his faithful ones;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ guards the trustworthy,
and repays in abundance the one who acts with pride.

[24] Be strong, and let your heart be firm,
all who hope for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 5, 6, 9, 14, 17, 21, 23, 24) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v14) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v5) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"mercy" → "chesed"** (v7, 16, 21) — Hebrew חֶסֶד, with its token (the earlier "steadfast love" was plain text).
- **Heading "To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer. A psalm. Of David"** — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד; מְנַצֵּחַ is the participle of the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "the chief musician"); לְ in לְדָוִד may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and "Of David" leaves it open.
- **"do I put my trust; let me never be ashamed: deliver me" → "I have taken refuge; let me never be ashamed; … rescue me"** (v1) — Hebrew חָסִיתִי, perfect of חָסָה "take refuge"; אַל־אֵבוֹשָׁה לְעוֹלָם, lit. "let me not be ashamed forever" (= never); פַלְּטֵנִי "rescue me" (פִּלֵּט; הִצִּיל "deliver" is v2, 15). v1–3 are nearly the same as Ps 71:1–3.
- **"Bow down thine ear … deliver me speedily: be thou my strong rock, for an house of defence" → "Incline your ear … quickly deliver me; be to me a rock of a strong place, a house of strongholds"** (v2) — Hebrew הַטֵּה "incline"; הַצִּילֵנִי "deliver me"; לְצוּר־מָעוֹז "a rock of a strong place", לְבֵית מְצוּדוֹת "a house of strongholds" (the names of 2 Sam 22:2–3).
- **"thou art my rock and my fortress; therefore for thy name's sake lead me, and guide me" → "my crag and my stronghold are you; and on account of your name you will lead me and guide me"** (v3) — Hebrew סַלְעִי "my crag" (צוּר "rock" is v2), מְצוּדָתִי "my stronghold", in the Hebrew order; וּלְמַעַן שִׁמְךָ "and on account of your name"; תַּנְחֵנִי וּתְנַהֲלֵנִי, imperfects (also readable as a wish: "lead me").
- **"Pull me out of the net that they have laid privily for me: for thou art my strength" → "You will bring me out of the net that they have hidden for me, for you are my strong place"** (v4) — Hebrew תּוֹצִיאֵנִי, imperfect of הוֹצִיא "bring out"; טָמְנוּ "they have hidden" (in the ground); מָעוּזִּי "my strong place".
- **"redeemed" → "ransomed"** (v5) — Hebrew פָּדִיתָה, from פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem"). אַפְקִיד "I commit, deposit". Luke 23:46 has the first line in Greek (παρατίθεμαι); the Hebrew is translated as it stands.
- **"I have hated them that regard lying vanities: but I trust" → "I hate those who keep empty vapors; and I, in Yahweh I trust"** (v6) — Hebrew שָׂנֵאתִי "I hate" (perfect of a state, as בָּטַחְתִּי "I trust", v6, 14, and אָמַרְתִּי "I say", v14); הַשֹּׁמְרִים "those who keep" (שָׁמַר); הַבְלֵי־שָׁוְא "vapors of emptiness" — הֶבֶל "vapor, breath", the earlier "worthless idols" interprets; the Greek and Syriac read "you hate" (from memory; the Masoretic Text is kept). וַאֲנִי "and I"; אֶל־יְהוָה "to Yahweh".
- **"I will be glad and rejoice … for thou hast considered my trouble; thou hast known my soul in adversities" → "Let me rejoice and be glad … that you have seen my affliction; you have known the distresses of my soul"** (v7) — Hebrew אָגִילָה וְאֶשְׂמְחָה, cohortatives ("let me"); אֲשֶׁר "that"; רָאִיתָ "you have seen"; עָנְיִי "my affliction"; יָדַעְתָּ בְּצָרוֹת נַפְשִׁי, יָדַע בְּ "know of, take note of"; צָרוֹת plural "distresses".
- **"hast not shut me up into the hand of the enemy: thou hast set my feet in a large room" → "you have not handed me over into the hand of the enemy; you have made my feet stand in the broad place"** (v8) — Hebrew הִסְגַּרְתַּנִי בְּיַד, lit. "shut me up into the hand", as 1 Sam 23:11; הֶעֱמַדְתָּ "made stand"; בַמֶּרְחָב "in the broad place" (2 Sam 22:20).
- **"Have mercy upon me … for I am in trouble: mine eye is consumed with grief, yea, my soul and my belly" → "Show me favor … for I am in distress; my eye is wasted with vexation, my soul and my belly"** (v9) — Hebrew חָנֵּנִי (חָנַן "show favor"); צַר־לִי, lit. "it is narrow for me"; עָשְׁשָׁה "is wasted" (also v10, עָשֵׁשׁוּ; the sense "grow weak, waste away" is not certain); כַּעַס "vexation"; בֶּטֶן "belly" (the earlier "body" interprets); "also" is not written.
- **"my strength faileth because of mine iniquity, and my bones are consumed" → "my power stumbles because of my iniquity, and my bones are wasted"** (v10) — Hebrew כָּשַׁל "stumble"; כֹּחַ "power"; בַּעֲוֺנִי "because of / in my iniquity" (the Greek reads "in poverty", בָּעֳנִי, from memory). יָגוֹן "sorrow", אֲנָחָה "sighing".
- **"I was a reproach among all mine enemies, but especially among my neighbours, and a fear to mine acquaintance: they that did see me without fled from me" → "Because of all who harass me I have become a reproach, and to my neighbors exceedingly, and a dread to my acquaintances; those who see me in the street have fled from me"** (v11) — Hebrew מִכָּל־צֹרְרַי, participle of צָרַר "harass"; וְלִשֲׁכֵנַי מְאֹד "and to my neighbors exceedingly" (the line is hard: the earlier "especially" smooths it; an emendation reads "a burden" for מְאֹד, from memory); פַּחַד "dread"; מְיֻדָּעַי "those known to me"; בַּחוּץ "outside, in the street"; נָדְדוּ, perfect, "have fled".
- **"as a dead man out of mind: I am like a broken vessel" → "like a dead man, out of the heart; I have become like a perishing vessel"** (v12) — Hebrew מִלֵּב "from the heart"; הָיִיתִי "I have become"; כְּלִי אֹבֵד, אָבַד "perish, be lost" (the earlier "broken" interprets).
- **"the slander of many: fear was on every side: while they took counsel together against me, they devised to take away my life" → "the report of many, terror on every side, when they took counsel together against me; to take my soul they devised"** (v13) — Hebrew דִּבַּת רַבִּים, דִּבָּה "report", one that defames (as Num 13:32); מָגוֹר מִסָּבִיב "terror from around", no verb; בְּהִוָּסְדָם "in their taking counsel"; נַפְשִׁי "my soul"; זָמָמוּ "they devised", last, as in the Hebrew.
- **"But I trusted in thee" → "And I, on you I trust"** (v14) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי עָלֶיךָ בָטַחְתִּי, עַל "upon".
- **"them that persecute me" → "my pursuers"** (v15) — Hebrew רֹדְפַי, from רָדַף "pursue". הַצִּילֵנִי "deliver me".
- **"save me for thy mercies' sake" → "save me in your chesed"** (v16) — Hebrew בְחַסְדֶּךָ "in your chesed". עַבְדְּךָ "your servant" (the word is "slave").
- **"let them be silent in the grave" → "let them be silent to Sheol"** (v17) — Hebrew יִדְּמוּ לִשְׁאוֹל; "go" (in the earlier "go silently to Sheol") is not written. קְרָאתִיךָ "I have called on you".
- **"Let the lying lips be put to silence; which speak grievous things proudly and contemptuously against the righteous" → "Let the lips of falsehood be mute, which speak arrogance against the righteous in pride and contempt"** (v18) — Hebrew שִׂפְתֵי שָׁקֶר "lips of falsehood"; תֵּאָלַמְנָה "be mute"; עָתָק "arrogance" (as 1 Sam 2:3); צַדִּיק singular; בְּגַאֲוָה וָבוּז "in pride and contempt".
- **"Oh how great is thy goodness, which thou hast laid up … which thou hast wrought for them that trust in thee before the sons of men" → "How abundant is your goodness, which you have hidden away … you have worked for those who take refuge in you, before the sons of man"** (v19) — Hebrew מָה רַב "how much / abundant", no "Oh"; צָפַנְתָּ, צָפַן "hide (away), treasure up" (as v20); פָּעַלְתָּ without "which"; לַחֹסִים בָּךְ "those who take refuge in you" (as v1); בְּנֵי אָדָם "sons of man".
- **"Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy presence from the pride of man: thou shalt keep them secretly in a pavilion" → "You hide them in the hiding place of your face from the plots of man; you hide them away in a booth"** (v20) — Hebrew תַּסְתִּירֵם בְּסֵתֶר פָּנֶיךָ "you hide them in the hiding place of your face" (סָתַר; "presence" interprets פָּנִים "face"); רֻכְסֵי אִישׁ, רֹכֶס found only here, meaning uncertain ("plots", from רָכַס "bind"; KJV "pride"; others "rough places"); תִּצְפְּנֵם, צָפַן "hide away" (as v19); סֻכָּה "booth"; רִיב "contention".
- **"Blessed be the LORD: for he hath shewed me his marvellous kindness in a strong city" → "Blessed is Yahweh, for he has made his chesed wonderful to me in a city of siege"** (v21) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה, no verb ("is"); הִפְלִיא חַסְדּוֹ לִי "he made his chesed wonderful to me"; עִיר מָצוֹר "a city of siege" (מָצוֹר also "fortification": "a fortified city", as KJV, is possible).
- **"For I said in my haste, I am cut off from before thine eyes: nevertheless thou heardest the voice of my supplications" → "And I, I said in my alarm, 'I am cut off from before your eyes.' Surely you heard the voice of my pleas for favor"** (v22) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי אָמַרְתִּי "And I, I said"; בְחָפְזִי, from חָפַז "be alarmed" (Deut 20:3); נִגְרַזְתִּי, found only here, is taken as "I am cut off" (many manuscripts have נִגְזַרְתִּי, the usual verb, from memory); אָכֵן "surely" (as Gen 28:16); תַּחֲנוּנַי "my pleas for favor" (as Ps 28:2, 6); בְּשַׁוְּעִי "when I cried for help".
- **"O love the LORD, all ye his saints: for the LORD preserveth the faithful, and plentifully rewardeth the proud doer" → "Love Yahweh, all his faithful ones; Yahweh guards the trustworthy, and repays in abundance the one who acts with pride"** (v23) — Hebrew חֲסִידָיו "his faithful ones" (חָסִיד, one who keeps *chesed*); אֱמוּנִים, of the root אמן "be firm": "the trustworthy" (to keep it apart from חָסִיד; also read as "faithfulness"), placed first in the Hebrew ("the trustworthy Yahweh guards"); "for" is not written; נֹצֵר "guards" (נָצַר); מְשַׁלֵּם עַל־יֶתֶר "repays in abundance", lit. "upon excess" (שִׁלֵּם "repay"); עֹשֵׂה גַאֲוָה lit. "a doer of pride".
- **"Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the LORD" → "Be strong, and let your heart be firm, all who hope for Yahweh"** (v24) — Hebrew חִזְקוּ "be strong"; וְיַאֲמֵץ לְבַבְכֶם, hifil of אָמֵץ "be firm", with "your heart" as its subject ("and may he make your heart firm" is the other way of taking it); הַמְיַחֲלִים, from יִחֵל "hope, wait" ("wait for" is קִוָּה).

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v24 = Heb v25.
- **v5 "Into your hand I commit my spirit"** — quoted by Jesus on the cross (Luke 23:46).
- **Structure** — a long plea (v1–18) turns at v19 to praise; the call to the faithful closes (v23–24).
