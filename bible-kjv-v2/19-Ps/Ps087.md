# Psalm 87

A Psalm of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧. A Song.

[1] On the holy mount stands the city he founded;

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loves the gates of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
more than all the dwelling places of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[3] Glorious things of you are spoken,
O city of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Selah.

[4] Among those who know me I mention ⟦rahab-monster|Rahav|Rahab⟧ and ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧;
behold, ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧ and ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, with ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ —
"This one was born there," they say.

[5] And of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ it shall be said,
"This one and that one were born in her";
for ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧ himself will establish her.

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ records as he registers the peoples:
"This one was born there." Selah.

[7] Singers and dancers alike say,
"All my springs are in you."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 6) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Rahab"** — poetic name for Egypt; retained with note.
- **Poetic structure** — brief, dense oracle of Zion's universal motherhood; abrupt register shifts.

### Notes
- **Zion as birthplace** — even enemy nations (Rahab = Egypt, Babylon, Philistia, Tyre, Cush) counted as born there.
- **Universal citizenship in Zion** — remarkable inclusive vision.
