# Psalm 87

Of the sons of ⟦korah|Qorach|Korah⟧. A psalm, a song.

[1] His foundation is on the holy mountains.

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ loves the gates of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
more than all the dwellings of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[3] Glorious things are spoken of you,
city of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Selah.

[4] I will mention ⟦rahab-monster|Rahav|Rahab⟧ and ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ to those who know me;
behold, ⟦philistia|Peleshet|Philistia⟧ and ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧, with ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧:
"This one was born there."

[5] And of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ it shall be said,
"Man and man was born in her";
and he shall establish her, ⟦elyon|Elyon|the Most High⟧.

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ shall number, in writing up the peoples,
"This one was born there." Selah.

[7] And singers as well as dancers:
all my springs are in you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 6) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Psalm or Song for the sons of Korah" → "Of the sons of Korah. A psalm, a song."** (heading) — Hebrew לִבְנֵי־קֹרַח מִזְמוֹר שִׁיר, in this order. לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to"; "of" leaves it open. מִזְמוֹר שִׁיר "a psalm, a song" (the heading row).
- **"His foundation is in the holy mountains" → "His foundation is on the holy mountains"** (v1) — Hebrew יְסוּדָתוֹ בְּהַרְרֵי־קֹדֶשׁ, the whole of the first line; literally "his foundation (is) in / on mountains of holiness" (קֹדֶשׁ as a genitive: "holy"). "His" (Yahweh's, named in v2) is the only subject: the earlier "stands the city he founded" supplied a verb, a city and a relative clause. יְסוּדָה "foundation" is found only here.
- **"The LORD loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob"** — kept (v2): אֹהֵב is a participle ("is loving"); מִשְׁכְּנוֹת "dwellings" (מִשְׁכָּן).
- **"Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God"** (v3) — Hebrew נִכְבָּדוֹת מְדֻבָּר בָּךְ עִיר הָאֱלֹהִים: נִכְבָּדוֹת, "honoured, glorious things" (root of כָּבוֹד); מְדֻבָּר "is spoken" (a singular participle with a plural subject); בָּךְ "of you" (or "in you"). No "O": the Hebrew has no word for it. הָאֱלֹהִים has the article.
- **"I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me: behold Philistia, and Tyre, with Ethiopia; this man was born there" → "I will mention Rahab and Babylon to those who know me; behold, Philistia and Tyre, with Cush: 'This one was born there'"** (v4) — Hebrew אַזְכִּיר (hifil of זָכַר, "mention"), an imperfect. לְיֹדְעָי "to those who know me" (also read "among" or "as those who know me": the לְ does not decide). רַהַב "Rahab", literally "arrogance, storming": here a name for Egypt (beside Babylon; as Isa 30:7, from memory), the same word as the sea monster of 89:10; the `rahab-monster` token as the lexicon has it. כּוּשׁ "Cush" (the Nile lands south of Egypt). זֶה "this one". "They say" was supplied in the earlier text; the Hebrew has no verb of saying.
- **"This and that man was born in her: and the highest himself shall establish her" → "Man and man was born in her; and he shall establish her, Elyon"** (v5) — Hebrew אִישׁ וְאִישׁ "a man and a man" (= each and every one); יֻלַּד singular, "was born". וְהוּא יְכוֹנְנֶהָ עֶלְיוֹן "and he shall establish her — Elyon", the name last, as in the Hebrew; וְ is "and" (not "for"). כּוֹנֵן "establish" (polel of כּוּן). לְצִיּוֹן "of Zion" (or "to Zion").
- **"The LORD shall count, when he writeth up the people" → "Yahweh shall number, in writing up the peoples"** (v6) — Hebrew יִסְפֹּר (סָפַר "number"), an imperfect; בִּכְתוֹב "in writing", an infinitive with no subject ("he" is not written); עַמִּים "peoples", plural.
- **"As well the singers as the players on instruments shall be there: all my springs are in thee" → "And singers as well as dancers: all my springs are in you"** (v7) — Hebrew וְשָׁרִים כְּחֹלְלִים, literally "and singers like dancers"; the line has no verb ("shall be there", "say" are supplied in other versions). חֹלְלִים is "dancers" (from חוּל "whirl") or "pipers" (from חָלִיל "pipe"): uncertain. Whether the last line is what they sing is not marked: no quotation marks. מַעְיָן "spring".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading stands in Hebrew v1 with the first words of the psalm: the numbers do not shift.
- **Zion as birthplace** — the nations named (Rahab = Egypt, Babylon, Philistia, Tyre, Cush) are counted as born there.
