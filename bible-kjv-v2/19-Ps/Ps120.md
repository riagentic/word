# Psalm 120

A Song of Ascents.

[1] In my distress I called to Yahweh,
and he answered me.

[2] Deliver me, O Yahweh,
from lying lips, from a deceitful tongue.

[3] What shall be given to you,
and what more shall be done to you,
you deceitful tongue?

[4] A warrior's sharp arrows,
with glowing coals of the broom tree!

[5] Woe to me, that I sojourn in Meshech,
that I dwell among the tents of Kedar!

[6] Too long have I had my dwelling
among those who hate peace.

[7] I am for peace,
but when I speak, they are for war.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Song of degrees/ascents"** — Hebrew *shir ha-ma'alot*; rendered "Song of Ascents" in superscription.
- **Poetic structure** — briefest opening of Ascents collection; exile/alien locale contrasted with desire for peace.

### Notes
- **First of fifteen Songs of Ascents** (Pss 120-134) — pilgrim-songs sung going up to Jerusalem.
