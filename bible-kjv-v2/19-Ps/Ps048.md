# Psalm 48

A Song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah.

[1] Great is Yahweh, and greatly to be praised
in the city of our *Elohim*,
his holy mountain.

[2] Beautiful in elevation,
the joy of all the earth,
Mount Zion, in the far north,
the city of the great King.

[3] *Elohim* has made himself known
in her citadels as a refuge.

[4] For behold, the kings assembled;
they passed by together.

[5] As they saw it, they were astounded;
they were dismayed; they took to flight.

[6] Trembling took hold of them there,
pain as of a woman in labor.

[7] By the east wind you shattered
the ships of Tarshish.

[8] As we have heard, so have we seen
in the city of Yahweh of Hosts,
in the city of our *Elohim*,
which *Elohim* will establish forever. Selah.

[9] We have thought on your steadfast love, O *Elohim*,
in the midst of your temple.

[10] As your name, O *Elohim*,
so your praise reaches to the ends of the earth.
Your right hand is filled with righteousness.

[11] Let Mount Zion be glad!
Let the daughters of Judah rejoice
because of your judgments!

[12] Walk about Zion, go around her,
number her towers,

[13] consider well her ramparts,
go through her citadels,
that you may tell the next generation

[14] that this is *Elohim*,
our *Elohim* forever and ever.
He will guide us forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 3, 8, 9, 10, 14) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v8) — compound divine name.
- **Poetic structure** — praise of Zion (v1-3), narrative of kings' defeat (v4-8), liturgical application (v9-14).

### Notes
- **Zion hymn** — praise of Jerusalem.
- **"Far north"** (v2) — *yarketei tzafon*; mythological Mount-of-assembly (cf. Isa 14:13).
