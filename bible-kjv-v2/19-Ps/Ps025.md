# Psalm 25

Of David.

[1] To you, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, I lift up my soul.

[2] O my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in you I trust; let me not be put to shame;
let not my enemies triumph over me.

[3] Indeed, none who wait for you shall be put to shame;
they shall be ashamed who treacherously betray without cause.

[4] Make me know your ways, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
teach me your paths.

[5] Lead me in your truth and teach me,
for you are the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of my salvation;
for you I wait all the day long.

[6] Remember your mercies, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, and your steadfast love,
for they have been from of old.

[7] Remember not the sins of my youth or my transgressions;
according to your steadfast love remember me,
for the sake of your goodness, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧!

[8] Good and upright is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
therefore he instructs sinners in the way.

[9] He leads the humble in what is right,
and teaches the humble his way.

[10] All the paths of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are steadfast love and truth,
for those who keep his covenant and his testimonies.

[11] For your name's sake, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
pardon my iniquity, for it is great.

[12] Who is the man who fears ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?
Him will he instruct in the way he should choose.

[13] His soul shall abide in well-being,
and his offspring shall inherit the land.

[14] The ⟦sod|sod Yahweh — secret counsel of Yahweh —|friendship of the LORD⟧ is with those who fear him,
and he makes known to them his covenant.

[15] My eyes are ever toward ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for he will pluck my feet out of the net.

[16] Turn to me and be gracious to me,
for I am lonely and afflicted.

[17] The troubles of my heart are enlarged;
bring me out of my distresses.

[18] Consider my affliction and my trouble,
and forgive all my sins.

[19] Consider how many are my foes,
and with what violent hatred they hate me.

[20] Guard my soul and deliver me;
let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in you.

[21] May integrity and uprightness preserve me,
for I wait for you.

[22] Redeem ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, out of all his troubles.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 22) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 5) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"secret" → "*sod Yahweh*"** (v14) — Hebrew *sod Yahweh*, secret counsel; inline gloss added.
- **Poetic structure** — acrostic structure followed; petition and trust braided throughout.

### Notes
- **Acrostic** (each verse begins with successive Hebrew letters, with minor variations).
- ***Chesed*** threaded throughout.
- **Closing intercession** for national deliverance.
