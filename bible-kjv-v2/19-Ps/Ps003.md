# Psalm 3

A psalm of David, when he fled from the face of ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ his son.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, how many are my adversaries!
Many are rising against me.

[2] Many are saying of my soul,
"There is no salvation for him in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧." Selah.

[3] But you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, are a shield about me,
my glory, and the lifter of my head.

[4] With my voice I called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and he answered me from his holy mountain. Selah.

[5] I lay down and slept;
I awoke, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sustains me.

[6] I will not fear ten thousands of people
who all around have set themselves against me.

[7] Arise, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧!
Save me, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!
For you have struck all my enemies on the cheek;
the teeth of the wicked you have broken in pieces.

[8] To ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ belongs the salvation;
on your people your blessing. Selah.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 7) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **The heading** — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד: מִזְמוֹר "a psalm"; לְדָוִד "of David": לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; the Hebrew does not decide. **"when he fled from Absalom his son" → "when he fled from the face of Absalom his son"** — Hebrew בְּבָרְחוֹ מִפְּנֵי, "in his fleeing from the face of".
- **"O LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 3, 7) — the Hebrew vocative has no "O"; so "my Elohim" (v7).
- **"how are they increased that trouble me" → "how many are my adversaries"** (v1) — Hebrew מָה־רַבּוּ צָרָי, a verb, "how many they have become"; צַר "adversary". רַבִּים "many" stands three times (v1, 2).
- **"There is no help for him in God" → "There is no salvation for him"** (v2) — Hebrew יְשׁוּעָתָה, the word of v8.
- **"Selah"** (v2, 4, 8) — Hebrew סֶלָה, a term of the psalm's performance; its meaning is not known.
- **"a shield for me" → "a shield about me"** (v3) — Hebrew מָגֵן בַּעֲדִי, "a shield around / on behalf of me". **"the lifter up of mine head" → "the lifter of my head"** — Hebrew וּמֵרִים רֹאשִׁי, a participle.
- **"I cried unto the LORD with my voice, and he heard me out of his holy hill" → "With my voice I called to Yahweh, and he answered me from his holy mountain"** (v4) — Hebrew קוֹלִי אֶל־יְהוָה אֶקְרָא וַיַּעֲנֵנִי: literally "my voice — to Yahweh I call", an imperfect, followed by the narrative form "and he answered me"; the pair is given as past. עָנָה "answer" (not "hear").
- **"I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the LORD sustained me" → "I lay down and slept; I awoke, for Yahweh sustains me"** (v5) — Hebrew אֲנִי שָׁכַבְתִּי וָאִישָׁנָה הֱקִיצוֹתִי, with "I" written out; יִסְמְכֵנִי is an imperfect, "sustains / will sustain me".
- **"I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, that have set themselves against me round about" → "I will not fear ten thousands of people who all around have set themselves against me"** (v6) — Hebrew רִבְבוֹת עָם, "myriads of a people"; שָׁתוּ "they have set", with no object: "themselves" is supplied (or "set [their camp]").
- **"thou hast smitten all mine enemies upon the cheek bone; thou hast broken the teeth of the ungodly" → "you have struck all my enemies on the cheek; the teeth of the wicked you have broken in pieces"** (v7) — Hebrew perfects הִכִּיתָ, שִׁבַּרְתָּ (piel, "break in pieces"); לֶחִי "cheek, jaw"; the second line keeps the Hebrew order. Some take the perfects as a wish ("strike … break"); the verse keeps them as past.
- **"Salvation belongeth unto the LORD: thy blessing is upon thy people" → "To Yahweh belongs the salvation; on your people your blessing"** (v8) — Hebrew לַיהוָה הַיְשׁוּעָה, "salvation" with the article. The second line has no verb: "is" (a statement) or "be" (a wish); the verse leaves it without one.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v8 = Heb v9.
- **Heading** — flight from Absalom (2 Sam 15–17).
- **Morning psalm** — "I awoke, for Yahweh sustains me".
- **First "selah" in the Psalter.**
