# Psalm 3

A Psalm of David, when he fled from ⟦absalom|Avshalom|Absalom⟧ his son.

[1] O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, how many are my foes!
Many are rising against me.

[2] Many are saying of my soul,
"There is no salvation for him in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧." Selah.

[3] But you, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, are a shield about me,
my glory, and the lifter of my head.

[4] With my voice I cry out to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and he answers me from his holy mountain. Selah.

[5] I lay down and slept;
I awoke, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sustains me.

[6] I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people
who have set themselves against me round about.

[7] Arise, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧!
Save me, O my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!
For you strike all my enemies on the cheek;
you break the teeth of the wicked.

[8] Salvation belongs to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
your blessing be on your people! Selah.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 7) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"Selah" — retained** — musical/liturgical term, meaning uncertain.

### Notes
- **Superscription** — flight from Absalom (2 Sam 15-17).
- **Morning psalm** — "I awoke, for Yahweh sustains me."
- **First "selah" in Psalter** — possibly musical interlude or "lift up" the voice.
