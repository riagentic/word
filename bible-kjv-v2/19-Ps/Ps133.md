# Psalm 133

A Song of Ascents. Of David.

[1] Behold, how good and how pleasant it is
when brothers dwell in unity!

[2] It is like the precious oil on the head,
running down on the beard,
on the beard of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧,
running down on the collar of his robes!

[3] It is like the dew of ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧,
which falls on the mountains of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧!
For there ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded the blessing,
life forevermore.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Poetic structure** — two similes (precious oil, Hermon dew) illustrating fraternal unity; three-verse gem.
