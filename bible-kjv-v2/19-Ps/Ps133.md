# Psalm 133

A song of the ascents. Of David.

[1] Behold, how good and how pleasant it is
for brothers to dwell also together!

[2] Like the good oil upon the head,
coming down upon the beard,
the beard of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧,
which comes down upon the mouth of his garments;

[3] like the dew of ⟦hermon|Chermon|Hermon⟧,
which comes down upon the mountains of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧.
For there ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded the blessing,
life forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"A Song of degrees of David" → "A song of the ascents. Of David."** (heading) — Hebrew שִׁיר הַמַּעֲלוֹת לְדָוִד (the heading row). לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to"; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!" → "Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brothers to dwell also together!"** (v1) — Hebrew הִנֵּה "behold"; נָעִים "pleasant" (its row); שֶׁבֶת אַחִים, an infinitive of יָשַׁב "dwell" (its row), "for brothers to dwell"; גַּם־יָחַד "also together" ("even together"): "in unity" said it twice over, and "also" was dropped. Brothers by blood or the brothers of one people: the word does not decide.
- **"It is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard, even Aaron's beard: that went down to the skirts of his garments" → "Like the good oil upon the head, coming down upon the beard, the beard of Aaron, which comes down upon the mouth of his garments"** (v2) — Hebrew כַּשֶּׁמֶן הַטּוֹב "like the good oil" (טוֹב "good", not "precious"); "It is" is not written (also v3). יֹרֵד · שֶׁיֹּרֵד, participles of יָרַד "come down", the same verb three times (also v3). עַל־פִּי מִדּוֹתָיו "upon the mouth of his garments": פֶּה of a garment is the opening for the head (as Exod 28:32; its row), so the collar, not the "skirts". Whether "which comes down" is the beard or the oil is not marked: uncertain.
- **"As the dew of Hermon, and as the dew that descended upon the mountains of Zion" → "like the dew of Hermon, which comes down upon the mountains of Zion"** (v3) — Hebrew כְּטַל־חֶרְמוֹן שֶׁיֹּרֵד עַל־הַרְרֵי צִיּוֹן: one dew, "of Hermon", coming down on the mountains of Zion; "and as the dew" is supplied in other versions (KJV) to separate the two places. Hermon's dew on Zion is a picture of plenty; the Hebrew does not explain it.
- **"for there the LORD commanded the blessing, even life for evermore" → "For there Yahweh has commanded the blessing, life forever"** (v3) — Hebrew צִוָּה "command" (its row), a perfect; אֶת־הַבְּרָכָה "the blessing" (the article kept); חַיִּים עַד־הָעוֹלָם "life forever" (literally "until the age", as Ps 28:9).

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading stands in Hebrew v1 with the first words of the psalm: the numbers do not shift.
- **Three pictures** — brothers dwelling together, oil coming down the beard of the anointed priest, dew coming down upon Zion; the one verb יָרַד "come down" ties them.
