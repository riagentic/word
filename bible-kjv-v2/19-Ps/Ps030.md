# Psalm 30

A Psalm. A Song at the dedication of the house. Of David.

[1] I will extol you, O Yahweh, for you have drawn me up
and have not let my foes rejoice over me.

[2] O Yahweh my *Elohim*, I cried to you for help,
and you have healed me.

[3] O Yahweh, you have brought up my soul from Sheol;
you restored me to life from among those who go down to the pit.

[4] Sing praises to Yahweh, O you his faithful ones,
and give thanks to his holy remembrance.

[5] For his anger is but for a moment,
and his favor is for a lifetime.
Weeping may tarry for the night,
but joy comes with the morning.

[6] As for me, I said in my prosperity,
"I shall never be moved."

[7] By your favor, O Yahweh,
you made my mountain stand strong;
you hid your face; I was dismayed.

[8] To you, O Yahweh, I cry,
and to Yahweh I plead for mercy:

[9] "What profit is there in my death,
if I go down to the pit?
Will the dust praise you?
Will it tell of your faithfulness?

[10] Hear, O Yahweh, and be merciful to me!
O Yahweh, be my helper!"

[11] You have turned for me my mourning into dancing;
you have loosed my sackcloth
and clothed me with gladness,

[12] that my glory may sing your praise and not be silent.
O Yahweh my *Elohim*, I will give thanks to you forever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"hell/grave" → "Sheol"** (v3) — Hebrew *Sheol*, realm of the dead.
- **Poetic structure** — grateful retrospect (v1-5) gives way to dramatic flashback of the crisis (v6-10) then resolution (v11-12).

### Notes
- **Temple/house dedication** — likely Second Temple usage.
- **"Joy comes with the morning"** (v5) — famous saying.
