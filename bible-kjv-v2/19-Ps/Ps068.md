# Psalm 68

To the chief musician. A Psalm. A Song of David.

[1] Let ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ arise,
let his enemies be scattered;
let those who hate him flee before him!

[2] As smoke is driven away, so you shall drive them away;
as wax melts before fire,
so the wicked shall perish before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!

[3] But the righteous shall be glad;
they shall exult before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
they shall be jubilant with joy!

[4] Sing to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, sing praises to his name;
lift up a song to him who rides through the deserts;
his name is ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧, exult before him!

[5] Father of the fatherless and protector of widows
is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in his holy habitation.

[6] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ settles the solitary in a home;
he leads out the prisoners to prosperity,
but the rebellious dwell in a parched land.

[7] O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, when you went out before your people,
when you marched through the wilderness, Selah,

[8] the earth quaked, the heavens poured down rain,
before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the one of Sinai,
before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[9] Rain in abundance, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you shed abroad;
you restored your inheritance as it languished;

[10] your flock found a dwelling in it;
in your goodness, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, you provided for the needy.

[11] ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ gives the word;
the women who announce the news are a great host:

[12] "The kings of the armies — they flee, they flee!"
The women at home divide the spoil —

[13] though you men lie among the sheepfolds —
the wings of a dove covered with silver,
its pinions with shimmering gold.

[14] When ⟦shaddai|Shaddai|the Almighty⟧ scatters kings there,
let snow fall on ⟦zalmon|Tzalmon|Zalmon⟧.

[15] O mountain of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, mountain of Bashan;
O many-peaked mountain, mountain of Bashan!

[16] Why do you look with envy, O many-peaked mountains,
at the mount that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ desired for his abode,
yes, where ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will dwell forever?

[17] The chariots of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ are twice ten thousand, thousands upon thousands;
⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ is among them; Sinai is now in the sanctuary.

[18] You ascended on high, leading a host of captives in your train
and receiving gifts among men,
even among the rebellious, that ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may dwell there.

[19] Blessed be ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, who daily bears us up;
⟦el|El|God⟧ is our salvation. Selah.

[20] Our ⟦el|El|God⟧ is ⟦el|an El|a God⟧ of ⟦moshaot|moshaot — saving acts|salvation⟧;
and to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|GOD,⟧ ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ belong deliverances from death.

[21] But ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will strike the heads of his enemies,
the hairy crown of him who walks in his guilty ways.

[22] ⟦adonai|Adonai|The Lord⟧ said,
"I will bring them back from Bashan,
I will bring them back from the depths of the sea,

[23] that you may strike your feet in their blood,
that the tongues of your dogs may have their portion from the foe."

[24] Your procession is seen, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the procession of my ⟦el|El|God⟧, my King, into the sanctuary —

[25] the singers in front, the musicians last,
between them young women playing tambourines:

[26] "Bless ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the great congregation,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, O you who are of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧'s fountain!"

[27] There is ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, the least of them, in the lead,
the princes of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ in their throng,
the princes of ⟦zebulun|Zevulun|Zebulun⟧, the princes of ⟦naphtali|Naftali|Naphtali⟧.

[28] Summon your power, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the power, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, by which you have worked for us.

[29] Because of your temple at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧
kings shall bear gifts to you.

[30] Rebuke the beasts that dwell among the reeds,
the herd of bulls with the calves of the peoples.
Trample underfoot those who lust after tribute;
scatter the peoples who delight in war.

[31] Nobles shall come from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧ shall hasten to stretch out her hands to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[32] O kingdoms of the earth, sing to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
sing praises to ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, Selah,

[33] to him who rides in the heavens, the ancient heavens;
behold, he sends out his voice, his mighty voice.

[34] Ascribe power to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
whose majesty is over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and whose power is in the skies.

[35] Awesome is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ from his sanctuary;
the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — he is the one who gives power and strength to his people.
Blessed be ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 24, 28, 31, 32, 34, 35) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v19, 20, 24, 35) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v16, 18, 20, 26) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"Yah"** — shortened form of Yahweh; retained (v4, 18).
- **Poetic structure** — ark-procession march through wilderness (v1-10), conquest and tribute (v11-18), doxology and appeal (v19-35).

### Notes
- **Difficult/complex psalm** — extended procession/victory hymn.
- **v18 cited Eph 4:8** — Christ's ascension leading captives.
- **"Yah"** — shortened form of Yahweh.
