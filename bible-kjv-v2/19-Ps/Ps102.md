# Psalm 102

A prayer of an afflicted one, when he grows faint and pours out his complaint before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, hear my prayer,
and let my cry for help come to you.

[2] Do not hide your face from me
in the day when I am in distress;
incline your ear to me;
in the day I call, hasten, answer me.

[3] For my days have come to an end in smoke,
and my bones are scorched like a hearth.

[4] My heart has been struck like a plant and has withered,
for I have forgotten to eat my bread.

[5] From the voice of my sighing
my bone clings to my flesh.

[6] I am like a desert owl of the wilderness;
I have become like a little owl of the ruins.

[7] I keep awake, and I have become
like a bird alone upon a roof.

[8] All the day my enemies have reproached me;
those who rave against me swear by me.

[9] For I have eaten ashes like bread,
and mixed my drink with weeping,

[10] because of your indignation and your wrath;
for you have lifted me up and thrown me down.

[11] My days are like a shadow stretched out,
and I, like a plant, I wither.

[12] But you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, sit forever,
and your remembrance is to generation and generation.

[13] You will arise, you will have compassion on ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
for it is the time to show her favor,
for the appointed time has come.

[14] For your servants are pleased with her stones,
and to her dust they show favor.

[15] And nations shall fear the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and all the kings of the earth your glory.

[16] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has built ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧;
he has appeared in his glory;

[17] he has turned to the prayer of the destitute,
and has not despised their prayer.

[18] This shall be written for a generation to come,
and a people created shall praise ⟦yah|Yah|the LORD⟧,

[19] for he has looked down from his holy height;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the heavens has looked upon the earth,

[20] to hear the groaning of the prisoner,
to set free the sons of death,

[21] to recount in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and his praise in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[22] when peoples are gathered together,
and kingdoms, to serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[23] He has afflicted my power in the way;
he has shortened my days.

[24] I say, "My ⟦el|El|God⟧, do not take me up
in the half of my days;
in the generation of generations are your years."

[25] Of old you founded the earth,
and the heavens are the work of your hands.

[26] They will perish, but you will stand;
and all of them will wear out like a garment;
like clothing you will change them, and they will change.

[27] But you are he,
and your years will not come to an end.

[28] The sons of your servants will abide,
and their seed will be established before you.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (heading; v1, 12, 15, 16, 19, 21, 22) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "El"** (v24) — Hebrew אֵלִי *Eli*, "my El".
- **"Yah"** (v18) — יָהּ, the short form of the name; token `yah`.
- **"A Prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed, and poureth out his complaint before the LORD" → "A prayer of an afflicted one, when he grows faint and pours out his complaint before Yahweh"** (heading) — Hebrew תְּפִלָּה לְעָנִי כִי־יַעֲטֹף וְלִפְנֵי יְהוָה יִשְׁפֹּךְ שִׂיחוֹ. לְעָנִי "of an afflicted one" (לְ as in the "of David" headings: "by", "for" or "belonging to"); עָנִי "afflicted" without the article; עָטַף "grow faint" (as Ps 61:2); שִׂיחַ "complaint" ("musing": the word for the speech of one who is troubled).
- **"Hear my prayer, O LORD, and let my cry come unto thee" → "Yahweh, hear my prayer, and let my cry for help come to you"** (v1) — Hebrew יְהוָה שִׁמְעָה תְפִלָּתִי וְשַׁוְעָתִי אֵלֶיךָ תָבוֹא: the name first; no "O"; שַׁוְעָה "cry for help"; תָבוֹא jussive "let … come".
- **"in the day when I am in trouble" → "in the day when I am in distress"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם צַר לִי, literally "in the day it is narrow for me". **"in the day when I call answer me speedily" → "in the day I call, hasten, answer me"** — Hebrew בְּיוֹם אֶקְרָא מַהֵר עֲנֵנִי, in this order; מַהֵר "hasten" (as an adverb "quickly").
- **"For my days are consumed like smoke, and my bones are burned as an hearth" → "For my days have come to an end in smoke, and my bones are scorched like a hearth"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־כָלוּ בְעָשָׁן יָמָי וְעַצְמוֹתַי כְּמוֹ־קֵד נִחָרוּ: כָּלָה "come to an end"; the MT has בְּ "in smoke" (some Hebrew manuscripts have כְּ "like smoke", from memory); נִחַר "be scorched" (from חָרַר "burn"); קֵד, found only here, "hearth" (or "firebrand": uncertain).
- **"My heart is smitten, and withered like grass; so that I forget to eat my bread" → "My heart has been struck like a plant and has withered, for I have forgotten to eat my bread"** (v4) — Hebrew הוּכָּה־כָעֵשֶׂב וַיִּבַשׁ לִבִּי כִּי־שָׁכַחְתִּי מֵאֲכֹל לַחְמִי: הוּכָּה hofal of הִכָּה "strike"; עֵשֶׂב "plant" (the seed-bearing herb, also v11; "grass" is דֶּשֶׁא); יָבֵשׁ "wither" (also v11); כִּי "for" (or "so that"); שָׁכַחְתִּי perfect.
- **"By reason of the voice of my groaning my bones cleave to my skin" → "From the voice of my sighing my bone clings to my flesh"** (v5) — Hebrew מִקּוֹל אַנְחָתִי דָּבְקָה עַצְמִי לִבְשָׂרִי: מִן "from" (= because of); אֲנָחָה "sighing" (noun of נֶאֱנַח "sigh"); עֶצֶם singular "my bone"; בָּשָׂר "flesh".
- **"I am like a pelican of the wilderness: I am like an owl of the desert" → "I am like a desert owl of the wilderness; I have become like a little owl of the ruins"** (v6) — Hebrew דָּמִיתִי לִקְאַת מִדְבָּר הָיִיתִי כְּכוֹס חֳרָבוֹת: two verbs, "I am like" and "I have become"; קָאַת and כּוֹס are birds of Lev 11:17–18 (the identifications cannot be proved: "pelican", "owl" in other versions); חֳרָבוֹת "ruins".
- **"I watch, and am as a sparrow alone upon the house top" → "I keep awake, and I have become like a bird alone upon a roof"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׁקַדְתִּי וָאֶהְיֶה כְּצִפּוֹר בּוֹדֵד עַל־גָּג: שָׁקַד "keep awake, watch"; צִפּוֹר "bird" (the kind is not named); גָּג "roof", without the article.
- **"Mine enemies reproach me all the day; and they that are mad against me are sworn against me" → "All the day my enemies have reproached me; those who rave against me swear by me"** (v8) — Hebrew כָּל־הַיּוֹם חֵרְפוּנִי אוֹיְבָי מְהוֹלָלַי בִּי נִשְׁבָּעוּ: חֵרֵף "reproach", a perfect; מְהוֹלָלַי, participle of הָלַל "be mad", "those who rave against me" (or "who mock me"); נִשְׁבַּע בְּ "swear by": they use my name in their curses (my lot as the pattern of a curse).
- **"and mingled my drink with weeping" → "and mixed my drink with weeping"** (v9) — Hebrew וְשִׁקֻּוַי בִּבְכִי מָסָכְתִּי: בְּכִי "weeping" (not "tears").
- **"Because of thine indignation and thy wrath: for thou hast lifted me up, and cast me down" → "because of your indignation and your wrath; for you have lifted me up and thrown me down"** (v10) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי־זַעַמְךָ וְקִצְפֶּךָ: מִפְּנֵי "because of"; זַעַם "indignation"; קֶצֶף "wrath"; נָשָׂא "lift up", הִשְׁלִיךְ "throw".
- **"My days are like a shadow that declineth; and I am withered like grass" → "My days are like a shadow stretched out, and I, like a plant, I wither"** (v11) — Hebrew יָמַי כְּצֵל נָטוּי וַאֲנִי כָּעֵשֶׂב אִיבָשׁ: נָטוּי passive participle of נָטָה "stretch out" (the long shadow of evening); אִיבָשׁ imperfect, with the "I" set first.
- **"But thou, O LORD, shalt endure for ever; and thy remembrance unto all generations" → "But you, Yahweh, sit forever, and your remembrance is to generation and generation"** (v12) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה יְהוָה לְעוֹלָם תֵּשֵׁב: יָשַׁב "sit" (as a king sits; also "dwell", "remain"); no "O"; זֵכֶר "remembrance" (how one is remembered and named); לְדֹר וָדֹר "to generation and generation".
- **"Thou shalt arise, and have mercy upon Zion: for the time to favour her, yea, the set time, is come" → "You will arise, you will have compassion on Zion, for it is the time to show her favor, for the appointed time has come"** (v13) — Hebrew אַתָּה תָקוּם תְּרַחֵם צִיּוֹן כִּי־עֵת לְחֶנְנָהּ כִּי־בָא מוֹעֵד: two verbs without "and"; רִחַם "have compassion"; חָנַן "show favor"; כִּי twice ("for … for"); מוֹעֵד "appointed time".
- **"For thy servants take pleasure in her stones, and favour the dust thereof" → "For your servants are pleased with her stones, and to her dust they show favor"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי־רָצוּ עֲבָדֶיךָ אֶת־אֲבָנֶיהָ וְאֶת־עֲפָרָהּ יְחֹנֵנוּ: רָצָה "be pleased with"; יְחֹנֵנוּ, of חָנַן, the verb of v13 ("show favor", here also "pity"); עֶבֶד "servant" (the word is "slave").
- **"So the heathen shall fear the name of the LORD" → "And nations shall fear the name of Yahweh"** (v15) — Hebrew וְיִירְאוּ גוֹיִם: "and" (not "so"); גּוֹיִם without the article; the second line has no verb ("shall fear" is not repeated).
- **"When the LORD shall build up Zion, he shall appear in his glory" → "For Yahweh has built Zion; he has appeared in his glory"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי־בָנָה יְהוָה צִיּוֹן נִרְאָה בִּכְבוֹדוֹ: כִּי "for" (or "when"); בָּנָה and נִרְאָה are perfects (the speaker sees it done, or the future told as done); נִרְאָה "appear" (literally "be seen").
- **"He will regard the prayer of the destitute, and not despise their prayer" → "he has turned to the prayer of the destitute, and has not despised their prayer"** (v17) — Hebrew פָּנָה אֶל־תְּפִלַּת הָעַרְעָר וְלֹא־בָזָה אֶת־תְּפִלָּתָם: perfects as v16; פָּנָה אֶל "turn to"; עַרְעָר "destitute" (literally "stripped"; a desert shrub in Jer 17:6), singular, then "their prayer" plural.
- **"This shall be written for the generation to come: and the people which shall be created shall praise the LORD" → "This shall be written for a generation to come, and a people created shall praise Yah"** (v18) — Hebrew תִּכָּתֶב זֹאת לְדוֹר אַחֲרוֹן וְעַם נִבְרָא יְהַלֶּל־יָהּ: imperfect (also "let this be written"); דּוֹר אַחֲרוֹן "a later generation"; נִבְרָא nifal participle of בָּרָא "create" ("being created" or "to be created").
- **"For he hath looked down from the height of his sanctuary; from heaven did the LORD behold the earth" → "for he has looked down from his holy height; Yahweh from the heavens has looked upon the earth"** (v19) — Hebrew כִּי־הִשְׁקִיף מִמְּרוֹם קָדְשׁוֹ: כִּי "for" (also "that": what is written, v18); מְרוֹם קָדְשׁוֹ "the height of his holiness"; הִבִּיט "look upon"; שָׁמַיִם "heavens".
- **"To hear the groaning of the prisoner; to loose those that are appointed to death" → "to hear the groaning of the prisoner, to set free the sons of death"** (v20) — Hebrew אֶנְקַת אָסִיר (singular, as Ps 79:11); לְפַתֵּחַ "to set free" (literally "to open, loose"); בְּנֵי תְמוּתָה "the sons of death": תְּמוּתָה "dying", from מוּת (not מָוֶת), as Ps 79:11; "son of" = one marked for it.
- **"To declare the name of the LORD in Zion" → "to recount in Zion the name of Yahweh"** (v21) — Hebrew לְסַפֵּר, an infinitive "to recount", in the Hebrew order; no "that they may".
- **"When the people are gathered together, and the kingdoms, to serve the LORD" → "when peoples are gathered together, and kingdoms, to serve Yahweh"** (v22) — Hebrew בְּהִקָּבֵץ עַמִּים יַחְדָּו וּמַמְלָכוֹת לַעֲבֹד: עַמִּים plural, no article; עָבַד "serve".
- **"He weakened my strength in the way" → "He has afflicted my power in the way"** (v23) — Hebrew עִנָּה בַדֶּרֶךְ כֹּחִי: עִנָּה "afflict" (humble, bring low); the qere כֹּחִי "my power" is translated; the ketiv כחו "his power" would say "he has afflicted his power in the way"; כֹּחַ "power".
- **"I said, O my God, take me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are throughout all generations" → "I say, 'My El, do not take me up in the half of my days; in the generation of generations are your years'"** (v24) — Hebrew אֹמַר אֵלִי אַל־תַּעֲלֵנִי בַּחֲצִי יָמָי בְּדוֹר דּוֹרִים שְׁנוֹתֶיךָ: אֹמַר imperfect "I say"; no "O"; הֶעֱלָה "take up" (hifil of עָלָה "go up"; "away" is not written); חֲצִי "half"; בְּדוֹר דּוֹרִים "in the generation of generations" (= through all generations).
- **"Of old hast thou laid the foundation of the earth" → "Of old you founded the earth"** (v25) — Hebrew לְפָנִים הָאָרֶץ יָסַדְתָּ: לְפָנִים "of old" (literally "formerly"); יָסַד "found". Heb 1:10 quotes the Greek: Σὺ κατʼ ἀρχάς, κύριε, "You, at the beginnings, Lord": "Lord" is not in this verse of the Hebrew; the Hebrew stands here as it is.
- **"They shall perish, but thou shalt endure: yea, all of them shall wax old like a garment; as a vesture shalt thou change them, and they shall be changed" → "They will perish, but you will stand; and all of them will wear out like a garment; like clothing you will change them, and they will change"** (v26) — Hebrew הֵמָּה יֹאבֵדוּ וְאַתָּה תַעֲמֹד וְכֻלָּם כַּבֶּגֶד יִבְלוּ כַּלְּבוּשׁ תַּחֲלִיפֵם וְיַחֲלֹפוּ: אָבַד "perish"; עָמַד "stand" (Heb 1:11 διαμένεις "remain"); וְכֻלָּם "and all of them"; בָּלָה "wear out"; לְבוּשׁ "clothing" (beside בֶּגֶד "garment"); הֶחֱלִיף "change" and חָלַף "change, pass on": one root, both kept (Heb 1:12 has ἑλίξεις "you will roll up", ὡς ἱμάτιον "like a garment" once more, and ἀλλαγήσονται "they will be changed").
- **"But thou art the same, and thy years shall have no end" → "But you are he, and your years will not come to an end"** (v27) — Hebrew וְאַתָּה־הוּא וּשְׁנוֹתֶיךָ לֹא יִתָּמּוּ: אַתָּה־הוּא "you are he" (as Ps 44:4; Heb 1:12 ὁ αὐτὸς εἶ "you are the same"); תַּם "come to an end" (Heb 1:12 ἐκλείψουσιν "fail").
- **"The children of thy servants shall continue, and their seed shall be established before thee" → "The sons of your servants will abide, and their seed will be established before you"** (v28) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־עֲבָדֶיךָ יִשְׁכּוֹנוּ: שָׁכַן "abide" (beside יָשַׁב "dwell, sit", v12); no "secure"; זֶרַע "seed"; יִכּוֹן nifal of כּוּן "be established".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1–28 = Heb v2–29.
- **Penitential psalm** — the fifth of the seven in church tradition (6; 32; 38; 51; 102; 130; 143).
- **v25–27 in the New Testament** — Heb 1:10–12 quotes them from the Greek and applies them to the Son.
