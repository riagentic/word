# Psalm 29

A Psalm of David.

[1] Ascribe to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, O sons of ⟦el|elim|God⟧ — sons of heavenly beings —
ascribe to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ glory and strength.

[2] Ascribe to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the glory due his name;
worship ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the splendor of holiness.

[3] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is over the waters;
the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of glory thunders;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is over many waters.

[4] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is powerful;
the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is full of majesty.

[5] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ breaks the cedars;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ breaks the cedars of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧.

[6] He makes ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ skip like a calf,
and ⟦sirion|Siryon|Sirion⟧ like a young wild ox.

[7] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ flashes forth flames of fire.

[8] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shakes the wilderness;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shakes the wilderness of ⟦kadesh|Qadesh|Kadesh⟧.

[9] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ makes the deer give birth
and strips the forests bare,
and in his temple all cry, "Glory!"

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ sits enthroned over the flood;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sits enthroned as King forever.

[11] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ give strength to his people!
May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ bless his people with peace!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "El"** (v3) and **"mighty" → "*elim*"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵל *El* and בְּנֵי אֵלִים *bnei elim*, not *Elohim*.
- **"unicorn" → "wild ox"** (v6).

### Notes
- **Storm theophany** — "voice of Yahweh" seven times.
- **Possible Canaanite storm-hymn adapted** — Baal-imagery redirected to Yahweh.
