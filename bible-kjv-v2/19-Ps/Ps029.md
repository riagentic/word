# Psalm 29

[1] Give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, sons of ⟦el|elim|God⟧,
give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ glory and strength.

[2] Give to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the glory of his name;
bow down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the splendor of holiness.

[3] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is over the waters;
the ⟦el|El|God⟧ of glory thunders;
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is over many waters.

[4] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is in power;

[5] The voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ breaks cedars,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Heading "A Psalm of David" → "A psalm. Of David"** — Hebrew מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד, the first words of the Hebrew v1; לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and "Of David" leaves it open.
- **"Give unto the LORD, O ye mighty" → "Give to Yahweh, sons of elim"** (v1) — Hebrew הָבוּ "give" (as 1 Chr 16:28); בְּנֵי אֵלִים as printed: אֵלִים is the plural of אֵל, so "sons of gods", heavenly beings; whether the ־ים is a plural or "sons of El" is debated (from memory). The gloss "sons of heavenly beings" of the earlier wording is not in the Hebrew. עֹז "strength".
- **"the glory due unto his name; worship the LORD in the beauty of holiness" → "the glory of his name; bow down to Yahweh in the splendor of holiness"** (v2) — Hebrew כְּבוֹד שְׁמוֹ, "due" is not written; הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down"; הַדְרַת־קֹדֶשׁ, as 1 Chr 16:29; the sense is not certain ("in holy adornment / attire" is the other way of taking it).
- **"God" → "El"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵל־הַכָּבוֹד. **"upon" → "over"** — עַל. הִרְעִים "thunders" is a perfect: the psalm mixes perfect, imperfect, participle and the narrative form with וַ (v5, 6, 9, 10); all are given as present, the form named here once.
- **"is powerful … is full of majesty" → "is in power … is in splendor"** (v4) — Hebrew בַּכֹּחַ "in power", בֶּהָדָר "in splendor".
- **"breaketh the cedars; yea, the LORD breaketh" → "breaks cedars, and Yahweh breaks in pieces"** (v5) — Hebrew שֹׁבֵר אֲרָזִים (no article), then וַיְשַׁבֵּר, the piel "break in pieces".
- **"He maketh them also to skip like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young unicorn" → "And he makes them skip like a calf, Lebanon and Sirion like a son of wild oxen"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיַּרְקִידֵם "and he makes them skip"; the line break follows the Hebrew (the earlier wording moved Lebanon into the first line); בֶן־רְאֵמִים "a son of wild oxen" (= a young wild ox); רְאֵם "wild ox", not "unicorn".
- **"divideth the flames of fire" → "hews flames of fire"** (v7) — Hebrew חֹצֵב "hew, cut out" (of stone); the earlier "flashes forth" is an interpretation.
- **"shaketh the wilderness" → "makes the wilderness writhe"** (v8) — Hebrew יָחִיל, hifil of חִיל "writhe" (1 Chr 16:30 "Be in anguish"). The same root in v9.
- **"maketh the hinds to calve, and discovereth the forests: and in his temple doth every one speak of his glory" → "makes the does writhe, and strips the forests bare, and in his temple all of it says, 'Glory'"** (v9) — Hebrew יְחוֹלֵל אַיָּלוֹת, polel of חִיל: the does writhe in giving birth; with other vowels אֵלוֹת "terebinths" is read, to stand beside "forests" (from memory; the Masoretic pointing is kept). חָשַׂף "strip bare". כֻּלּוֹ אֹמֵר כָּבוֹד "all of it says: glory" — "his" before "glory" is not written.
- **"sitteth upon the flood; yea, the LORD sitteth King for ever" → "sits at the flood, and Yahweh sits as king forever"** (v10) — Hebrew לַמַּבּוּל יָשָׁב, perfect, then וַיֵּשֶׁב; מַבּוּל is the word of the flood of Gen 6–11; לְ "at" (of place or of time; "sat at the flood" is also possible). יָשַׁב "sit" (of a king: on the throne); "enthroned" is not written. מֶלֶךְ without the article: "king".
- **"will give … will bless his people with peace" → "will give … will bless his people with shalom"** (v11) — Hebrew imperfects יִתֵּן, יְבָרֵךְ (also readable as wishes: "may Yahweh give … bless"); בַשָּׁלוֹם with its token.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — the heading is part of the Hebrew v1; the verse numbers are the same in Hebrew and English.
- **Storm theophany** — "the voice of Yahweh" seven times (v3, 4 twice, 5, 7, 8, 9); the name Yahweh eighteen times.
- **v1–2 = 1 Chr 16:28–29 / Ps 96:7–9** in part: the same wording where the Hebrew is the same; Chronicles has "clans of the peoples" where this psalm has "sons of elim", and its v29 has a line more.
