# Psalm 62

To the overseer, upon ⟦jeduthun|Yedutun|Jeduthun⟧. A psalm of David.

[1] Only toward ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my soul silence;
from him is my salvation.

[2] Only he is my Rock and my salvation,
my secure height; I shall not be shaken greatly.

[3] How long will you assail a man?
You shall be murdered, all of you, like a leaning wall,
a fence pushed down.

[4] Only from his height they counsel to thrust down;
they are pleased with a lie; with his mouth they bless,
and in their inward part they curse. Selah.

[5] Only to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be silent, my soul,
for from him is my hope.

[6] Only he is my Rock and my salvation,
my secure height; I shall not be shaken.

[7] Upon ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my salvation and my glory;
the Rock of my strength, my refuge is in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] Trust in him at every time, people;
pour out your heart before him;
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is a refuge for us. Selah.

[9] Only vapor are the sons of man,
a lie are the sons of man;
in the balances they go up;
they together are lighter than vapor.

[10] Do not trust in extortion,
and in robbery do not become vain;
wealth, when it bears fruit,
do not set your heart on it.

[11] Once ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has spoken; twice I have heard this:
that strength belongs to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[12] and to you, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, belongs ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
for you repay a man according to his work.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 7, 8, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Lord" → "Adonai"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"To the chief Musician, to Jeduthun, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer, upon Yedutun. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל־יְדוּתוּן מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). עַל "upon" (Ps 39 has לִ "to" Jeduthun); whether the name here stands for the singer, his guild or a tune is not known. לְדָוִד: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" David; "of" leaves it open.
- **אַךְ "only"** (v1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 9) — the same word six times, one rendering; it may also be read as "surely, truly".
- **"Truly my soul waiteth upon God" → "Only toward Elohim is my soul silence"** (v1) — Hebrew אַךְ אֶל־אֱלֹהִים דּוּמִיָּה נַפְשִׁי, a clause without a verb: דּוּמִיָּה is a noun, "silence" (as Ps 22:2; 39:2; 65:1): the soul is (in) silence toward Elohim. "Waits" is not in the Hebrew. In v5 the verb of the same root stands (דּוֹמִּי "be silent"). **"from him cometh my salvation" → "from him is my salvation"** — no verb "comes".
- **"my defence; I shall not be greatly moved" → "my secure height; I shall not be shaken greatly"** (v2) — Hebrew מִשְׂגַּבִּי, מִשְׂגָּב "secure height" (as Ps 18:2); צוּר "Rock" with a capital, as it names Elohim (v2, 6, 7). לֹא־אֶמּוֹט רַבָּה, מוֹט nifal "be shaken"; רַבָּה "much, greatly". v6 has the same line without רַבָּה.
- **"How long will ye imagine mischief against a man? ye shall be slain all of you: as a bowing wall shall ye be, and as a tottering fence" → "How long will you assail a man? You shall be murdered, all of you, like a leaning wall, a fence pushed down"** (v3) — Hebrew תְּהוֹתְתוּ, found only here: the sense "assail, rush upon, shout at" is uncertain. תְּרָצְּחוּ is pointed in this manuscript as a passive (pual) of רָצַח "murder": "you shall be murdered" (so KJV); other manuscripts and editions point it active (piel), "will you murder (him)", and then the man is the leaning wall (from memory). The verse follows the printed pointing. גָּדֵר "fence"; הַדְּחוּיָה "the pushed one" (passive participle of דָּחָה "push"). Layout: the Hebrew break falls after "wall".
- **"They only consult to cast him down from his excellency: they delight in lies: they bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly" → "Only from his height they counsel to thrust down; they are pleased with a lie; with his mouth they bless, and in their inward part they curse"** (v4) — Hebrew מִשְּׂאֵתוֹ "from his lifting up" (his height, dignity); יָעֲצוּ "counsel"; לְהַדִּיחַ "to thrust down", with no object ("him" not in the Hebrew). יִרְצוּ כָזָב, רָצָה "be pleased with"; כָּזָב "a lie", singular. בְּפִיו "with his mouth": singular (each one's mouth), beside the plural "they" and "their inward part" (קֶרֶב). קִלֵּל "curse". Layout: the Hebrew break falls after "bless".
- **"My soul, wait thou only upon God" → "Only to Elohim be silent, my soul"** (v5) — Hebrew דּוֹמִּי, imperative of דָּמַם "be silent" (as Ps 4:4), feminine, said to the soul. **"my expectation" → "my hope"** — Hebrew תִּקְוָה "hope".
- **"In God is my salvation and my glory: the rock of my strength, and my refuge, is in God" → "Upon Elohim is my salvation and my glory; the Rock of my strength, my refuge is in Elohim"** (v7) — Hebrew עַל־אֱלֹהִים "upon Elohim" (resting on him); יִשְׁעִי, יֵשַׁע "salvation" (in v1, 2, 6 יְשׁוּעָה, the same English). צוּר־עֻזִּי "the Rock of my strength" (עֹז "strength"); מַחְסִי "my refuge"; no "and".
- **"at all times; ye people" → "at every time, people"** (v8) — Hebrew בְכָל־עֵת "at every time"; עָם "people", no "O". בִּטְחוּ and שִׁפְכוּ are plural; לְבַבְכֶם "your heart", singular. מַחֲסֶה־לָּנוּ "a refuge for us".
- **"Surely men of low degree are vanity, and men of high degree are a lie" → "Only vapor are the sons of man, a lie are the sons of man"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶבֶל בְּנֵי־אָדָם כָּזָב בְּנֵי אִישׁ. Both are "sons of man" (English has one word for the two): the first line has בְּנֵי אָדָם (אָדָם "mankind"), the second בְּנֵי אִישׁ (אִישׁ "a man"). Many take the pair as "low and high" (common people and men of rank, so KJV); the Hebrew does not say "low" or "high". הֶבֶל "vapor" (a breath; traditional "vanity"); כָּזָב "a lie". **"to be laid in the balance, they are altogether lighter than vanity" → "in the balances they go up; they together are lighter than vapor"** — Hebrew בְּמֹאזְנַיִם לַעֲלוֹת, "in the balances to go up" (they rise: they weigh nothing); הֵמָּה מֵהֶבֶל יָחַד, "they, than vapor, together": "lighter" is supplied from the image of the scale ("less than vapor", "made of vapor" are also read).
- **"Trust not in oppression, and become not vain in robbery: if riches increase, set not your heart upon them" → "Do not trust in extortion, and in robbery do not become vain; wealth, when it bears fruit, do not set your heart on it"** (v10) — Hebrew עֹשֶׁק "extortion"; גָּזֵל "robbery"; תֶּהְבָּלוּ, verb of הֶבֶל (v9): "become vapor, become vain". חַיִל "wealth"; כִּי־יָנוּב, נוּב "bear fruit" (grow). אַל־תָּשִׁיתוּ לֵב "do not set the heart": "your" and "on it" are supplied. Layout: the Hebrew break falls after "bears fruit", so the last line is two.
- **"God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this; that power belongeth unto God" → "Once Elohim has spoken; twice I have heard this: that strength belongs to Elohim"** (v11) — Hebrew אַחַת … שְׁתַּיִם־זוּ, "one … two (times), this": the feminine numbers used as adverbs (as Ps 89:35 "once"). עֹז "strength" (כֹּחַ is "power").
- **"Also unto thee, O Lord, belongeth mercy: for thou renderest to every man according to his work" → "and to you, Adonai, belongs steadfast love, for you repay a man according to his work"** (v12) — Hebrew וּלְךָ־אֲדֹנָי חָסֶד, "and to you, Adonai, *chesed*" (the "that" of v11 may also govern this line). תְשַׁלֵּם, שִׁלֵּם "repay"; לְאִישׁ "to a man" (each one); מַעֲשֶׂה "work".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v12 = Heb v13.
- **Poetic structure** — trust in *Elohim* only (v1–2, refrain v5–6), the attackers (v3–4), widening to the people (v7–12).
- **Six-fold "only"** — *Elohim*-alone theme.
- **Quiet confidence** psalm.
