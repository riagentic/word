# Psalm 62

To the chief musician, upon Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.

[1] For *Elohim* alone my soul waits in silence;
from him comes my salvation.

[2] He only is my rock and my salvation,
my fortress; I shall not be greatly shaken.

[3] How long will you attack a man
to batter him, all of you,
like a leaning wall, a tottering fence?

[4] They only plan to thrust him down from his high position.
They take pleasure in falsehood.
They bless with their mouths, but inwardly they curse. Selah.

[5] For *Elohim* alone, O my soul, wait in silence,
for my hope is from him.

[6] He only is my rock and my salvation,
my fortress; I shall not be shaken.

[7] On *Elohim* rests my salvation and my glory;
my mighty rock, my refuge is *Elohim*.

[8] Trust in him at all times, O people;
pour out your heart before him;
*Elohim* is a refuge for us. Selah.

[9] Those of low estate are but a breath;
those of high estate are a delusion;
in the balances they go up;
together they are lighter than a breath.

[10] Put no trust in extortion;
set no vain hopes on robbery;
if riches increase, set not your heart on them.

[11] Once *Elohim* has spoken; twice have I heard this:
that power belongs to *Elohim*,

[12] and that to you, O Lord, belongs steadfast love.
For you will render to a man according to his work.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 5, 7, 8, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Jeduthun** — musical director, retained in superscription.
- **Poetic structure** — trust in *Elohim*-alone (v1-2, refrain v5-6), denunciation of attackers (v3-4), broadening to community (v8-12).

### Notes
- **Six-fold "alone/only"** — *Elohim*-alone theme.
- **Quiet confidence** psalm.
