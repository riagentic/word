# Psalm 5

To the overseer, to the ⟦nehiloth|Nechilot|flutes⟧. A psalm of David.

[1] Give ear to my sayings, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
understand my murmuring.

[2] Give heed to the voice of my cry for help, my King and my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
for to you I pray.

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, in the morning you hear my voice;
in the morning I arrange for you, and I keep watch.

[4] For you are not ⟦el|an El|a God⟧ who delights in wickedness;
evil does not sojourn with you.

[5] The boastful shall not take their stand before your eyes;
you hate all workers of wickedness.

[6] You do away with those who speak a lie;
a man of blood and deceit ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ abominates.

[7] But I, in the abundance of your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧, will come into your house;
I will bow down toward your holy temple in the fear of you.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, lead me in your righteousness
on account of those who watch for me;
make straight your way before me.

[9] For there is nothing right in his mouth;
their inward part is destructions;
their throat is an open grave;
they make their tongue smooth.

[10] Hold them guilty, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
let them fall from their counsels.
In the abundance of their transgressions drive them away,
for they have rebelled against you.

[11] And let all who take refuge in you rejoice;
forever let them shout for joy;
and may you screen over them,
and let those who love your name be jubilant in you.

[12] For you bless the righteous one, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧;
like the shield you surround him with good pleasure.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 10) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"a God" → "an El"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*. The Hebrew vocatives have no "O".
- **"To the chief Musician upon Nehiloth, A Psalm of David" → "To the overseer, to the Nechilot. A psalm of David."** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ, from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"); אֶל־הַנְּחִילוֹת "to the Nechilot": אֶל is "to, toward"; the meaning of נְחִילוֹת is unknown (often taken as "flutes", from חָלִיל "flute"; others "inheritances"); מִזְמוֹר "a psalm"; לְדָוִד "of David": the לְ may say "by", "for" or "belonging to" David, and is not decided here.
- **"Give ear to my words … consider my meditation" → "Give ear to my sayings … understand my murmuring"** (v1) — Hebrew הַאֲזִינָה "give ear"; אֲמָרַי, plural of אֵמֶר "saying" (not דָּבָר "word"); בִּינָה, imperative of בִּין "understand, discern"; הֲגִיגִי, found only here and Ps 39:3, from the root of הָגָה "softly recite": a low sound, here of the one praying ("meditation", "sighing", "groaning" in other versions). Its exact sense is uncertain.
- **"Hearken unto the voice of my cry" → "Give heed to the voice of my cry for help"** (v2) — Hebrew הַקְשִׁיבָה "give heed"; שַׁוְעִי "my cry for help".
- **"My voice shalt thou hear in the morning … will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up" → "in the morning you hear my voice; in the morning I arrange for you, and I keep watch"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶעֱרָךְ־לְךָ "I arrange (set in order) for you": the verb has no object; "my prayer" is not written (the verb is used of setting out a sacrifice, Lev 1:7–8, and of setting out words, Job 32:14); וַאֲצַפֶּה "and I keep watch" (watch for an answer). תִּשְׁמַע may also be read as a wish: "may you hear".
- **"neither shall evil dwell with thee" → "evil does not sojourn with you"** (v4) — Hebrew לֹא יְגֻרְךָ רָע, from גּוּר "sojourn" (dwell as a guest); רָע may be "evil" or "an evil one". **"hath pleasure in wickedness"** — Hebrew חָפֵץ רֶשַׁע "delighting in wickedness".
- **"The foolish shall not stand in thy sight" → "The boastful shall not take their stand before your eyes"** (v5) — Hebrew הוֹלְלִים "the boastful"; יִתְיַצְּבוּ "take their stand"; לְנֶגֶד עֵינֶיךָ "before your eyes". **"workers of iniquity" → "workers of wickedness"** — Hebrew פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן, as in Job 31:3; 34:8, 22 (עָוֹן is "iniquity"). English has "wickedness" for both רֶשַׁע (v4) and אָוֶן (v5).
- **"Thou shalt destroy them that speak leasing" → "You do away with those who speak a lie"** (v6) — Hebrew תְּאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד "perish" (lit. "you make perish"); כָזָב "a lie". **"the bloody and deceitful man" → "a man of blood and deceit"** — Hebrew אִישׁ־דָּמִים, lit. "a man of bloods" (one who sheds blood); מִרְמָה "deceit". **"abhor" → "abominates"** — Hebrew יְתָעֵב, the verb of תּוֹעֵבָה.
- **"in the multitude of thy mercy" → "in the abundance of your chesed"** (v7) — Hebrew חֶסֶד. **"worship toward thy holy temple" → "bow down toward your holy temple"** — Hebrew אֶשְׁתַּחֲוֶה "bow down"; הֵיכַל קָדְשְׁךָ, lit. "the temple of your holiness". **"in thy fear" → "in the fear of you"** — Hebrew בְּיִרְאָתֶךָ. The verse is printed in two lines, by the Hebrew accents.
- **"because of mine enemies" → "on account of those who watch for me"** (v8) — Hebrew שׁוֹרְרָי, from שׁוּר "watch, lie in wait" (not אֹיֵב "enemy"). **"before my face" → "before me"** — Hebrew לְפָנַי. "Make straight" is the qere הַיְשַׁר; the ketiv הושר is another spelling of the same verb.
- **"no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part is very wickedness" → "nothing right in his mouth; their inward part is destructions"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּפִיהוּ "in his mouth", singular (the rest of the verse is plural); נְכוֹנָה, from כּוּן, "right, firm, established"; קִרְבָּם "their inward part"; הַוּוֹת, plural, "destructions, ruin". **"sepulchre" → "grave"** — Hebrew קֶבֶר. **"they flatter with their tongue" → "they make their tongue smooth"** — Hebrew לְשׁוֹנָם יַחֲלִיקוּן, lit. "their tongue they make smooth" (= they flatter).
- **"Destroy thou them" → "Hold them guilty"** (v10) — Hebrew הַאֲשִׁימֵם, hifil of אָשֵׁם "be guilty". **"let them fall by their own counsels" → "let them fall from their counsels"** — Hebrew מִמֹּעֲצוֹתֵיהֶם: "because of" or "away from" their counsels; "own" is not written. **"cast them out in the multitude" → "In the abundance … drive them away"** — Hebrew בְּרֹב (as v7); הַדִּיחֵמוֹ "drive them away". **"rebelled against thee"** — Hebrew מָרוּ בָךְ.
- **"But let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice" → "And let all who take refuge in you rejoice"** (v11) — Hebrew וְיִשְׂמְחוּ; חוֹסֵי בָךְ "those taking refuge in you". **"let them ever shout for joy, because thou defendest them" → "forever let them shout for joy; and may you screen over them"** — Hebrew לְעוֹלָם "forever"; וְתָסֵךְ עָלֵימוֹ "and may you screen over them", from סָכַךְ "screen, cover" (no "because"). **"let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee" → "let those who love your name be jubilant in you"** — Hebrew וְיַעְלְצוּ, from עָלַץ "be jubilant".
- **"with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield" → "like the shield you surround him with good pleasure"** (v12) — Hebrew כַּצִּנָּה "like the shield" (צִנָּה is the large shield that covers the whole body; מָגֵן is the smaller "shield": English has one word); רָצוֹן "good pleasure"; תַּעְטְרֶנּוּ, from עָטַר "surround" (the verb of "crown" in Ps 8:5). צַדִּיק is singular, "the righteous one".

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v12 = Heb v13.
- **Morning prayer** — "in the morning you hear my voice."
- **v9 cited in Rom 3:13** for universal sinfulness (the Greek follows the Septuagint).
