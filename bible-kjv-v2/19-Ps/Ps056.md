# Psalm 56

To the chief musician. Upon ⟦jonath-elem-rehokim|Yonat elem rechoqim|The Dove on Far-off Terebinths⟧. A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧ of David, when the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ seized him in ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧.

[1] Be gracious to me, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for man tramples on me;
all day long an attacker oppresses me;

[2] my enemies trample on me all day long,
for many attack me proudly.

[3] When I am afraid,
I put my trust in you.

[4] In ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, whose word I praise,
in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ I trust; I shall not be afraid.
What can flesh do to me?

[5] All day long they injure my cause;
all their thoughts are against me for evil.

[6] They stir up strife, they lurk;
they watch my steps,
as they have waited for my life.

[7] For their crime will they escape?
In wrath cast down the peoples, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!

[8] You have kept count of my tossings;
put my tears in your bottle.
Are they not in your book?

[9] Then my enemies will turn back
in the day when I call.
This I know, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is for me.

[10] In ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, whose word I praise,
in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, whose word I praise,

[11] in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ I trust; I shall not be afraid.
What can man do to me?

[12] I must perform my vows to you, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
I will render thank offerings to you.

[13] For you have delivered my soul from death,
yes, my feet from falling,
that I may walk before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the light of life.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 4, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v10) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Miktam** — retained in superscription.
- **Jonath-elem-rehokim** — musical term, retained.
- **Poetic structure** — enemy pressure (v1-7), confidence refrain (v4, 10-11), vow (v12-13).

### Notes
- **Gath capture** — 1 Sam 21.
- **"Tears in your bottle"** — *no'd*; Yahweh collects tears.
- **Refrain repeated** — *Elohim* and his word, trust over fear.
