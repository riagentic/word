# Psalm 41

To the chief musician. A Psalm of David.

[1] Blessed is the one who considers the poor!
In the day of trouble ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ delivers him;

[2] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ protects him and keeps him alive;
he is called blessed in the land;
you do not give him up to the will of his enemies.

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ sustains him on his sickbed;
in his illness you restore him to full health.

[4] As for me, I said, "O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, be gracious to me;
heal my soul, for I have sinned against you!"

[5] My enemies say of me in malice,
"When will he die, and his name perish?"

[6] When one comes to see me, he utters empty words,
while his heart gathers iniquity;
when he goes out, he tells it abroad.

[7] All who hate me whisper together about me;
they imagine the worst for me.

[8] "A deadly thing is poured out on him;
he will not rise again from where he lies."

[9] Even my close friend in whom I trusted,
who ate my bread,
has lifted his heel against me.

[10] But you, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, be gracious to me,
and raise me up, that I may repay them!

[11] By this I know that you delight in me:
my enemy will not shout in triumph over me.

[12] But you have upheld me because of my integrity,
and set me in your presence forever.

[13] Blessed be ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
from everlasting to everlasting!
Amen and Amen.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 10, 13) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — *ashrei* blessing (v1-3), personal lament (v4-9), confidence and doxology (v10-13).

### Notes
- **Close of Book I of the Psalter** (Ps 1-41).
- **v9 cited John 13:18** — Judas's betrayal echoes.
- **"Amen and Amen"** — closing doxology of Book I.
