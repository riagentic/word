# Psalm 75

To the chief musician. ⟦al-tashcheth|Al-tashchet|Do Not Destroy⟧. A Psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧. A Song.

[1] We give thanks to you, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
we give thanks, for your name is near.
We recount your wondrous deeds.

[2] "At the set time that I appoint
I will judge with equity.

[3] When the earth totters, and all its inhabitants,
it is I who keep steady its pillars." Selah.

[4] I say to the boastful, "Do not boast,"
and to the wicked, "Do not lift up your horn;

[5] do not lift up your horn on high,
or speak with haughty neck."

[6] For not from the east or from the west
and not from the wilderness comes lifting up,

[7] but it is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who executes judgment,
putting down one and lifting up another.

[8] For in the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ there is a cup
with foaming wine, well mixed,
and he pours out from it,
and all the wicked of the earth
shall drain it down to the dregs.

[9] But I will declare it forever;
I will sing praises to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[10] All the horns of the wicked I will cut off,
but the horns of the righteous shall be lifted up.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 7, 9) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **Al-tashcheth** — retained in superscription.
- **Poetic structure** — communal thanksgiving (v1), divine oracle of judgment (v2-5), psalmist's response (v6-10).

### Notes
- **Cup of wrath** — cited NT often.
- **"Horn"** = strength/power.
