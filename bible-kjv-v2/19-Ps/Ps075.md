# Psalm 75

To the overseer. ⟦al-tashcheth|Al-tashchet|Do Not Destroy⟧. A psalm of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧, a song.

[1] We give thanks to you, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;
we give thanks, and near is your name;
they recount your wonderful deeds.

[2] "When I take an appointed time,
I, in uprightness I will judge.

[3] Melting away are the earth and all who dwell in it;
I, I have measured out its pillars." Selah.

[4] I said to the boastful, "Do not boast,"
and to the wicked, "Do not raise the horn;

[5] do not raise your horn on high;
nor speak with a neck of arrogance."

[6] For not from the east and from the west,
and not from the wilderness, is the raising up,

[7] but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is judge:
this one he makes low, and this one he raises.

[8] For a cup is in the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and the wine foams; it is full of mixture,
and he pours out from this;
surely its dregs they shall drain, they shall drink,
all the wicked of the earth.

[9] And I, I will tell forever;
let me make music to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧.

[10] And all the horns of the wicked I will hew off;
raised high shall be the horns of the righteous.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 7, 9) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים, as printed (Ps 42–83 use it where other psalms have the Tetragrammaton). **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v8) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"To the chief Musician, Al-taschith, A Psalm or Song of Asaph" → "To the overseer. Al-tashchet. A psalm of Asaph, a song"** (heading) — Hebrew לַמְנַצֵּחַ אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת מִזְמוֹר לְאָסָף שִׁיר, in this order. לַמְנַצֵּחַ "to the overseer", from the verb "oversee" of 1 Chr 15:21 (traditional "to the chief musician"). אַל־תַּשְׁחֵת "Do not destroy" (as Deut 9:26), kept as a name with its token: most take it as the name of a tune; the meaning is not known. There is no "or": מִזְמוֹר and שִׁיר stand side by side. לְאָסָף: the לְ may mean "by", "for" or "belonging to" Asaph; "of" leaves it open.
- **"Unto thee … do we give thanks, unto thee do we give thanks: for that thy name is near thy wondrous works declare" → "We give thanks to you, Elohim; we give thanks, and near is your name; they recount your wonderful deeds"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹדִינוּ, perfect ("we have given thanks", here of the present act), הוֹדָה "give thanks"; וְקָרוֹב שְׁמֶךָ "and near is your name" (the earlier "for" is not written); סִפְּרוּ, 3rd person plural "they recount" (the earlier "we recount" changes the subject; who "they" are is not said); נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָ "your wonderful deeds" (the Exod 3:20 row). No "O": the Hebrew has no word for it.
- **"When I shall receive the congregation I will judge uprightly" → "When I take an appointed time, I, in uprightness I will judge"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי אֶקַּח מוֹעֵד: מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (KJV took it as "congregation"); כִּי "when" (also "for"). אֲנִי מֵישָׁרִים אֶשְׁפֹּט: the pronoun stressed; מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness" stands without a preposition (an adverbial accusative: "in uprightness", as בְּמֵישָׁרִים Ps 9:8). The speaker of v2–3 is Elohim; the Hebrew has no quotation marks, and where his words end (v3 or v5) is not marked.
- **"The earth and all the inhabitants thereof are dissolved: I bear up the pillars of it" → "Melting away are the earth and all who dwell in it; I, I have measured out its pillars"** (v3) — Hebrew נְמֹגִים, nifal participle of מוּג "melt away" (the Exod 15:15 row); יֹשְׁבֶיהָ "who dwell in it"; אָנֹכִי, stressed; תִכַּנְתִּי, piel perfect of תָּכַן "measure out, fix the measure" (as Isa 40:12; the נִתְכַּן "be measured" of 1 Sam 2:3): the pillars stand because he set their measure (the earlier "keep steady", "bear up" interpret).
- **"Selah"** (v3) — Hebrew סֶלָה, a term of the psalm's performance; its meaning is not known.
- **"I said unto the fools, Deal not foolishly … Lift not up the horn" → "I said to the boastful, 'Do not boast,' … 'Do not raise the horn'"** (v4) — Hebrew אָמַרְתִּי, perfect "I said"; הוֹלְלִים "the boastful" and אַל־תָּהֹלּוּ "do not boast", one root (the Ps 5:5 row); הֵרִים "raise" (its row), the same verb as v5, 7 and the noun of v6; קֶרֶן "the horn", with no "your" (that comes in v5). Who says "I said" (Elohim or the psalmist) is not said.
- **"Lift not up your horn on high: speak not with a stiff neck" → "do not raise your horn on high; nor speak with a neck of arrogance"** (v5) — Hebrew תְּדַבְּרוּ בְצַוָּאר עָתָק, word for word "you speak with neck arrogance": the אַל "not" of the first line is understood ("nor" supplied). עָתָק "arrogance" (as 1 Sam 2:3); it may also be the object, "speak arrogance with the neck" (the neck held high). The Greek reads "against God" (בְּצוּר, from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"For promotion cometh neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south" → "For not from the east and from the west, and not from the wilderness, is the raising up"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּי לֹא מִמּוֹצָא וּמִמַּעֲרָב וְלֹא מִמִּדְבַּר הָרִים: מוֹצָא "going out" (of the sun) and מַעֲרָב "setting" are the east and the west (the literal words here once); מִדְבָּר "wilderness" (KJV "south", the wilderness south of Judah, interprets). הָרִים is read as the hifil infinitive of רוּם, "raising up", the word of v4, 5, 7, 10 ("is" supplied); the same letters and vowels can be "mountains", "the wilderness of the mountains" — the sentence is then left unfinished, and the sense of the line is not certain.
- **"But God is the judge: he putteth down one, and setteth up another" → "but Elohim is judge: this one he makes low, and this one he raises"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי after the negatives of v6: "but" (also "for"); שֹׁפֵט "judge" (a participle: "is judging"); הִשְׁפִּיל "make low" and יָרִים "raise", the pair of 1 Sam 2:7; זֶה … וְזֶה "this one … and this one".
- **"and the wine is red; it is full of mixture; and he poureth out of the same: but the dregs thereof, all the wicked of the earth shall wring them out, and drink them" → "and the wine foams; it is full of mixture, and he pours out from this; surely its dregs they shall drain, they shall drink, all the wicked of the earth"** (v8) — Hebrew וְיַיִן חָמַר, a verb, "the wine foams" (KJV "is red" takes another sense of the root); מָלֵא מֶסֶךְ "full of mixture" (mixed, spiced wine; whether the cup or the wine is "full" is not said); וַיַּגֵּר מִזֶּה, narrative form of הִגִּיר "pour out", "and he pours out from this" (of the present, as the verse); אַךְ "surely" (or "only": the dregs and nothing else); יִמְצוּ יִשְׁתּוּ, two verbs side by side, "they shall drain, they shall drink"; the subject, "all the wicked of the earth", comes last, as in the Hebrew. The lines follow the Hebrew order.
- **"But I will declare for ever; I will sing praises" → "And I, I will tell forever; let me make music"** (v9) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי, stressed; אַגִּיד "I will tell" (הִגִּיד), with no object written ("it" removed); אֲזַמְּרָה, cohortative of זִמֵּר "make music". The Greek has "I will rejoice" (אָגִיל, from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"All the horns of the wicked also will I cut off; but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted" → "And all the horns of the wicked I will hew off; raised high shall be the horns of the righteous"** (v10) — Hebrew וְכָל "And all" (KJV "also"); אֲגַדֵּעַ, piel of גָּדַע "hew down" (its row); תְּרוֹמַמְנָה, polal of רוּם "be raised high"; there is no "but". צַדִּיק is singular, "the righteous one" (collective, or one man); the speaker of "I will hew off" (Elohim or the psalmist, or the king) is not named.

### Notes
- **Numbering** — Hebrew counts the heading as v1: our v1 = Heb v2, and so on to v10 = Heb v11.
- **Structure** — thanks (v1), the words of Elohim on judgment (v2–3, perhaps to v5), the warning to the boastful (v4–5), the judge who raises and makes low (v6–8), the vow (v9–10). One root runs through the psalm: רוּם "raise" (v4, 5, 6, 7, 10), and "horn" (v4, 5, 10).
- **"Horn"** — the horn of an animal as a picture of strength and pride (as 1 Sam 2:1, 10).
- **The cup** (v8) — the cup of Yahweh's fury in the prophets (Isa 51:17; Jer 25:15).
