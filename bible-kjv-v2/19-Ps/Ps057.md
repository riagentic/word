# Psalm 57

To the chief musician. ⟦al-tashcheth|Al-tashchet|Do Not Destroy⟧. A ⟦miktam|Michtam|Miktam⟧ of David, when he fled from ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ in the cave.

[1] Be merciful to me, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, be merciful to me,
for in you my soul takes refuge;
in the shadow of your wings I will take refuge,
till the storms of destruction pass by.

[2] I cry out to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ ⟦elyon|Elyon|Most High⟧,
to ⟦el|El|God⟧ who fulfills his purpose for me.

[3] He will send from heaven and save me;
he will put to shame him who tramples on me. Selah.
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will send out his steadfast love and his faithfulness!

[4] My soul is in the midst of lions;
I lie down amid fiery beasts —
the children of men, whose teeth are spears and arrows,
whose tongues are sharp swords.

[5] Be exalted, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, above the heavens!
Let your glory be over all the earth!

[6] They set a net for my steps;
my soul was bowed down.
They dug a pit in my way,
but they have fallen into it themselves. Selah.

[7] My heart is steadfast, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my heart is steadfast!
I will sing and make melody!

[8] Awake, my glory!
Awake, O harp and lyre!
I will awake the dawn!

[9] I will give thanks to you, O ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, among the peoples;
I will sing praises to you among the nations.

[10] For your steadfast love is great to the heavens,
your faithfulness to the clouds.

[11] Be exalted, O ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, above the heavens!
Let your glory be over all the earth!

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God" → "El"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל *El*, not *Elohim*.
- **"God most high" → "*Elohim* Most High"** (v2) — *Elyon* retained.
- **Miktam** — retained in superscription.
- **Al-tashcheth** — Hebrew "do not destroy"; retained in superscription.
- **Poetic structure** — lament from cave (v1-6) pivots to praise refrain (v5, 11 identical); concludes with universal praise (v9-11).
