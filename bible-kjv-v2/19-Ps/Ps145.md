# Psalm 145

A praise of David.

[1] I will raise you high, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the King,
and let me bless your name forever and ever.

[2] Every day I will bless you,
and let me praise your name forever and ever.

[3] Great is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and greatly praised,
and to his greatness there is no searching.

[4] Generation to generation shall praise your works,
and your mighty deeds they shall tell.

[5] The splendor of the glory of your majesty,
and the matters of your wonderful deeds, let me muse on.

[6] And the might of your fearsome deeds they shall speak of,
and your greatness I will recount.

[7] The remembrance of your abundant goodness they shall pour forth,
and your righteousness they shall ring out.

[8] Gracious and compassionate is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
slow to anger and great in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧.

[9] Good is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to all,
and his compassion is over all his works.

[10] All your works shall give thanks to you, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and your faithful ones shall bless you.

[11] The glory of your kingdom they shall speak of,
and of your might they shall talk,

[12] to make known to the sons of man his mighty deeds,
and the glory of the splendor of his kingdom.

[13] Your kingdom is a kingdom of all ages,
and your rule is in every generation and generation.

[14] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ upholds all who fall,
and straightens all who are bent down.

[15] The eyes of all hope toward you,
and you give them their food in its time.

[16] You open your hand,
and satisfy every living thing with pleasure.

[17] Righteous is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in all his ways,
and faithful in all his works.

[18] Near is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to all who call on him,
to all who call on him in truth.

[19] The pleasure of those who fear him he does,
and their cry for help he hears, and he saves them.

[20] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ keeps all who love him,
and all the wicked he will destroy.

[21] The praise of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my mouth shall speak,
and let all flesh bless his holy name forever and ever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 8, 9, 10, 14, 17, 18, 20, 21) — Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַי, "my Elohim", written with a full vowel letter (as Ps 143:10; not אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*). No vocative "O": the Hebrew has no such word (v1, 10).
- **"David's Psalm of praise" → "A praise of David"** (heading) — Hebrew תְּהִלָּה לְדָוִד, the first words of v1 in the Hebrew: תְּהִלָּה "praise" (the noun of הִלֵּל, as in v21 "the praise of Yahweh"), the one heading in the Psalter with this word; not מִזְמוֹר "psalm". לְ "by / for / belonging to": "of" leaves it open.
- **"I will extol thee, my God, O king; and I will bless thy name for ever and ever" → "I will raise you high, my Elohim, the King, and let me bless your name forever and ever"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲרוֹמִמְךָ "I will raise you high" (רוֹמֵם, the same form as Ps 30:1; Exod 15:2 "extol"); הַמֶּלֶךְ with the article, "the King"; וַאֲבָרֲכָה, a cohortative, "and let me bless" (so וַאֲהַלְלָה v2); לְעוֹלָם וָעֶד "forever and ever" (also v2, 21).
- **"Every day will I bless thee" → "Every day I will bless you"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּכָל־יוֹם, literally "in every day"; אֲבָרֲכֶךָּ, imperfect; הִלֵּל "praise".
- **"and his greatness is unsearchable" → "and to his greatness there is no searching"** (v3) — Hebrew וְלִגְדֻלָּתוֹ אֵין חֵקֶר (חֵקֶר "searching", as Job 5:9; 9:10). וּמְהֻלָּל מְאֹד "and greatly praised", a participle (as Ps 48:1; 96:4; "to be" is not written).
- **"One generation shall praise thy works to another, and shall declare thy mighty acts" → "Generation to generation shall praise your works, and your mighty deeds they shall tell"** (v4) — Hebrew דּוֹר לְדוֹר יְשַׁבַּח: "generation to generation" is the subject ("one … another" interprets); שִׁבַּח "praise" (laud; its row: another verb than הִלֵּל of v2, English has one word). גְּבוּרוֹת "mighty deeds" (its row; "deeds" supplied; also v12); יַגִּידוּ "they shall tell" (הִגִּיד), plural.
- **"I will speak of the glorious honour of thy majesty, and of thy wondrous works" → "The splendor of the glory of your majesty, and the matters of your wonderful deeds, let me muse on"** (v5) — Hebrew הֲדַר כְּבוֹד הוֹדֶךָ: הָדָר "splendor", כָּבוֹד "glory", הוֹד "majesty", three nouns in a chain (v12 has כְּבוֹד הֲדַר, the other order). וְדִבְרֵי נִפְלְאוֹתֶיךָ, literally "and the words of your wonderful deeds": דָּבָר "matter" where no speech is meant (its row; "the accounts of" is also possible); נִפְלָאוֹת "wonderful deeds". אָשִׂיחָה, cohortative of שִׂיחַ "muse" (its row). The Greek and the Dead Sea Psalms Scroll read "they shall speak … and I will recount" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"And men shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts: and I will declare thy greatness" → "And the might of your fearsome deeds they shall speak of, and your greatness I will recount"** (v6) — Hebrew וֶעֱזוּז נוֹרְאֹתֶיךָ יֹאמֵרוּ: עֱזוּז "might" (as Ps 78:4); נוֹרָאוֹת "fearsome deeds" (נוֹרָא "fearsome"); "men" is not written ("they"). אָמַר "say", here "speak of" (also v11; דִּבֶּר is "speak", v21). וּגְדוּלָּתְךָ "your greatness", the qere, singular; the written form (ketiv) וגדולתיך is plural, "your great deeds". אֲסַפְּרֶנָּה "I will recount" (סִפֵּר).
- **"They shall abundantly utter the memory of thy great goodness, and shall sing of thy righteousness" → "The remembrance of your abundant goodness they shall pour forth, and your righteousness they shall ring out"** (v7) — Hebrew זֵכֶר "remembrance" (its row; how one is remembered and named); רַב־טוּבְךָ "the abundance of your goodness" (as Ps 31:19); יַבִּיעוּ "they shall pour forth" (הִבִּיעַ, as Ps 59:7; "abundantly" is the verb, not a second word). וְצִדְקָתְךָ יְרַנֵּנוּ, the object with no preposition, as Ps 51:14 "my tongue shall ring out your righteousness" (רִנֵּן "shout for joy").
- **"The LORD is gracious, and full of compassion; slow to anger, and of great mercy" → "Gracious and compassionate is Yahweh, slow to anger and great in chesed"** (v8) — Hebrew חַנּוּן וְרַחוּם יְהוָה אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם וּגְדָל־חָסֶד: the words of Exod 34:6 ("El compassionate and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in chesed"), rendered as there, with the psalm's own differences kept: the order חַנּוּן וְרַחוּם (as 2 Chr 30:9; Neh 9:17, 31), Yahweh named after the two words, and גְּדָל "great" for רַב "abounding" (as Nah 1:3 "great in power"). חַנּוּן "gracious" (adjective of חָנַן "show favor"); רַחוּם "compassionate"; אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם, literally "long of nostrils"; חֶסֶד "chesed" (token).
- **"The LORD is good to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works" → "Good is Yahweh to all, and his compassion is over all his works"** (v9) — Hebrew טוֹב־יְהוָה לַכֹּל, in this order; רַחֲמִים "compassion" (the plural noun of the רַחוּם root); עַל־כָּל־מַעֲשָׂיו "over all his works" (מַעֲשֶׂה, as v4, 10, 17).
- **"All thy works shall praise thee, O LORD; and thy saints shall bless thee" → "All your works shall give thanks to you, Yahweh, and your faithful ones shall bless you"** (v10) — Hebrew יוֹדוּךָ "they shall give thanks to you" (הוֹדָה, not הִלֵּל "praise"); חֲסִידִים "faithful ones" (those who keep *chesed*; חָסִיד, as Ps 52:9).
- **"They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom, and talk of thy power" → "The glory of your kingdom they shall speak of, and of your might they shall talk"** (v11) — Hebrew יֹאמֵרוּ "speak of" (אָמַר, as v6) and יְדַבֵּרוּ "talk" (דִּבֶּר): two verbs, given two English words here; גְּבוּרָה "might" (its row; singular, where v4, 12 have the plural "mighty deeds"); מַלְכוּת "kingdom".
- **"To make known to the sons of men his mighty acts, and the glorious majesty of his kingdom" → "to make known to the sons of man his mighty deeds, and the glory of the splendor of his kingdom"** (v12) — Hebrew לִבְנֵי הָאָדָם "to the sons of man" (the reviewer row); גְּבוּרֹתָיו "his mighty deeds", וּכְבוֹד הֲדַר מַלְכוּתוֹ "and the glory of the splendor of his kingdom": "his", where v11 and v13 speak to Yahweh ("your"); the Hebrew changes the person, and the verse keeps it.
- **"Thy kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations" → "Your kingdom is a kingdom of all ages, and your rule is in every generation and generation"** (v13) — Hebrew מַלְכוּת כָּל־עֹלָמִים, "a kingdom of all ages" (עוֹלָם plural, as Ps 77:5 "the years of the ages"); מֶמְשָׁלָה "rule" (its row); בְּכָל־דּוֹר וָדוֹר "in every generation and generation" (as Ps 45:17); "endureth" is not written.
- **No verse for נ (nun)** (after v13) — the Masoretic Text has no line beginning with the letter נ (see Notes). One Hebrew manuscript, the Dead Sea Psalms Scroll (11QPs^a) and the Greek and Syriac have one, "Faithful is Yahweh in all his words, and faithful in all his works" (נֶאֱמָן … ; its second half is v17b; from memory). It is not supplied here.
- **"The LORD upholdeth all that fall, and raiseth up all those that be bowed down" → "Yahweh upholds all who fall, and straightens all who are bent down"** (v14) — Hebrew סוֹמֵךְ … וְזוֹקֵף, participles; סָמַךְ "uphold" (as Ps 37:24); זָקַף "straighten, raise upright" (found only here and Ps 146:8, rendered as there); הַכְּפוּפִים "those bent down" (כָּפַף, as Ps 146:8); the objects take לְ (a late use).
- **"The eyes of all wait upon thee; and thou givest them their meat in due season" → "The eyes of all hope toward you, and you give them their food in its time"** (v15) — Hebrew אֵלֶיךָ יְשַׂבֵּרוּ "toward you they hope" (שִׂבֵּר "hope", as Esth 9:1; to wait in hope); אֹכֶל "food"; בְּעִתּוֹ "in its time" (as Deut 11:14), or "in his time" (each one's): the suffix is singular.
- **"Thou openest thine hand, and satisfiest the desire of every living thing" → "You open your hand, and satisfy every living thing with pleasure"** (v16) — Hebrew פּוֹתֵחַ אֶת־יָדֶךָ וּמַשְׂבִּיעַ לְכָל־חַי רָצוֹן, participles ("you" is carried on from v15 and the "your" of the hand). רָצוֹן "pleasure" (what pleases; as Ps 51:18): with good will (Yahweh's), or with what each one desires (the earlier "the desire"): the word does not say whose; also v19.
- **"The LORD is righteous in all his ways, and holy in all his works" → "Righteous is Yahweh in all his ways, and faithful in all his works"** (v17) — Hebrew צַדִּיק יְהוָה, in this order; חָסִיד, the word of v10 "faithful ones", here of Yahweh: one who keeps *chesed* ("holy" is קָדוֹשׁ, not written).
- **"The LORD is nigh unto all them that call upon him" → "Near is Yahweh to all who call on him"** (v18) — Hebrew קָרוֹב יְהוָה, in this order (as Ps 34:18); בֶאֱמֶת "in truth".
- **"He will fulfil the desire of them that fear him: he also will hear their cry, and will save them" → "The pleasure of those who fear him he does, and their cry for help he hears, and he saves them"** (v19) — Hebrew רְצוֹן־יְרֵאָיו יַעֲשֶׂה, literally "he does the pleasure of" (רָצוֹן as v16; the English of Ps 103:21 "who do his pleasure", KJV); שַׁוְעָה "cry for help" (as Ps 34:15); "also" is not written; imperfects, present.
- **"The LORD preserveth all them that love him" → "Yahweh keeps all who love him"** (v20) — Hebrew שׁוֹמֵר "keep" (שָׁמַר); רְשָׁעִים "wicked" with the article ("all the wicked"); יַשְׁמִיד "he will destroy".
- **"My mouth shall speak the praise of the LORD: and let all flesh bless his holy name for ever and ever" → "The praise of Yahweh my mouth shall speak, and let all flesh bless his holy name forever and ever"** (v21) — Hebrew תְּהִלַּת יְהוָה first (the word of the heading); וִיבָרֵךְ "and let … bless"; בָּשָׂר "flesh"; שֵׁם קָדְשׁוֹ, literally "the name of his holiness" (as Ps 33:21).

### Notes
- **Acrostic** — the verses begin with the letters of the Hebrew alphabet in order: v1 א, v2 ב, v3 ג … v13 מ, v14 ס, and on to v21 ת. The letter נ (nun) has no verse in the Masoretic Text, so the psalm has 21 verses for 22 letters (see Changes, after v13). The English does not imitate the scheme.
- **Hebrew numbering** — the heading is part of v1 in the Hebrew; the verse numbers are the same.
- **v8** — the words of Exod 34:6, said of Yahweh throughout the Scriptures (Num 14:18; Neh 9:17; Joel 2:13; Jonah 4:2; Ps 86:15; 103:8).
