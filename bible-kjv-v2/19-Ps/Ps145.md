# Psalm 145

A Song of Praise. Of David.

[1] I will extol you, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and King,
and bless your name forever and ever.

[2] Every day I will bless you
and praise your name forever and ever.

[3] Great is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and greatly to be praised,
and his greatness is unsearchable.

[4] One generation shall commend your works to another,
and shall declare your mighty acts.

[5] On the glorious splendor of your majesty,
and on your wondrous works, I will meditate.

[6] They shall speak of the might of your awesome deeds,
and I will declare your greatness.

[7] They shall pour forth the fame of your abundant goodness
and shall sing aloud of your righteousness.

[8] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is gracious and merciful,
slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love.

[9] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is good to all,
and his compassion is over all that he has made.

[10] All your works shall give thanks to you, O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧,
and all your faithful ones shall bless you!

[11] They shall speak of the glory of your kingdom
and tell of your power,

[12] to make known to the children of man your mighty deeds,
and the glorious splendor of your kingdom.

[13] Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom,
and your dominion endures throughout all generations.

[14] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ upholds all who are falling
and raises up all who are bowed down.

[15] The eyes of all look to you,
and you give them their food in due season.

[16] You open your hand;
you satisfy the desire of every living thing.

[17] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is righteous in all his ways
and kind in all his works.

[18] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is near to all who call on him,
to all who call on him in truth.

[19] He fulfills the desire of those who fear him;
he also hears their cry and saves them.

[20] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ preserves all who love him,
but all the wicked he will destroy.

[21] My mouth will speak the praise of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and let all flesh bless his holy name forever and ever.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 8, 9, 10, 14, 17, 18, 21) — Tetragrammaton.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — alphabetic acrostic; alternates personal praise (I will exalt) with communal declaration; "you open your hand" (v16) as liturgical center.

### Notes
- **Acrostic** (22 letters, with missing *nun* supplied in some MSS and DSS).
- **"You open your hand" (v16)** — classic benedictional verse.
