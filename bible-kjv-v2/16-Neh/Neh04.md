# Nehemiah 4

[1] And it came to pass, when ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ heard that we were building the wall, he burned with anger and was greatly vexed, and he derided the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[2] And he spoke before his brothers and the force of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and he said, "What are the feeble ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ doing? Will they leave it to themselves? Will they sacrifice? Will they finish in a day? Will they bring the stones to life out of the heaps of dust — and they are burned?"

[3] And ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧ was beside him, and he said, "Even what they are building — if a fox goes up, he will breach their wall of stones!"

[4] Hear, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for we have become a contempt; and turn back their reproach on their own head, and give them for plunder in a land of captivity.

[5] And do not cover over their iniquity, and let not their sin be wiped out from before you, for they have caused vexation in front of the builders.

[6] And we built the wall, and all the wall was bound together up to its half; and the people had a heart to do it.

[7] And it came to pass, when ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ and ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ and the ⟦arabians|Arvim|Arabs⟧ and the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧ and the ⟦ashdodites|Ashdodim|Ashdodites⟧ heard that healing had come up on the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, that the breaches had begun to be stopped, they burned with anger greatly.

[8] And they all conspired together to come to fight against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and to cause confusion for it.

[9] And we prayed to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and set a guard against them day and night because of them.

[10] And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ said, "The strength of the burden-bearer has stumbled, and the dust is much, and we are not able to build on the wall."

[11] And our adversaries said, "They will not know and will not see, until we come into their midst and kill them and make the work cease."

[12] And it came to pass, when the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were dwelling beside them came, they said to us ten times, "From all the places where you turn back — upon us!"

[13] And I stationed, in the lowest parts of the place behind the wall, in the bare places — and I stationed the people by clans, with their swords, their spears and their bows.

[14] And I saw, and I arose and said to the nobles and to the prefects and to the rest of the people, "Do not be afraid of them. Remember ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, the great and the fearsome, and fight for your brothers, your sons and your daughters, your wives and your houses."

[15] And it came to pass, when our enemies heard that it was known to us, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had made their counsel void, that we returned, all of us, to the wall, each man to his work.

[16] And it came to pass from that day, half of my young men were doing the work, and half of them were holding both the spears, the shields and the bows and the coats of mail; and the commanders were behind all the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[17] Those who were building on the wall and those who carried the load were loading: with one of his hands each was doing the work, and the other was holding the weapon.

[18] And the builders, each man had his sword bound on his loins, and they were building. And the one who blew the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ was beside me.

[19] And I said to the nobles and to the prefects and to the rest of the people, "The work is great and broad, and we are separated on the wall, far off each man from his brother.

[20] In the place where you hear the sound of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, there gather to us. Our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will fight for us."

[21] And we were doing the work, and half of them were holding fast the spears from the going up of the dawn until the coming out of the stars.

[22] Also at that time I said to the people, "Let each man and his young man lodge in the midst of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and they shall be for us a guard by night and work by day."

[23] And neither I nor my brothers nor my young men nor the men of the guard who were after me — we were not taking off our garments; each man his weapon, the water.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 9, 15, 20) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v15 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים. **"the Lord" → "Adonai"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("but", "so", "therefore", "nevertheless" of the KJV are not in the Hebrew).
- **"he was wroth, and took great indignation" → "he burned with anger and was greatly vexed"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר לוֹ, literally "it burned to him" (no word "anger"; also v7); וַיִּכְעַס הַרְבֵּה, כָּעַס "be vexed". **"mocked" → "derided"** — Hebrew וַיַּלְעֵג עַל, as 2:19.
- **"he spake before his brethren and the army of Samaria" → "he spoke before his brothers and the force of Samaria"** (v2) — Hebrew חֵיל, חַיִל "force". **"What do these feeble Jews?" → "What are the feeble Jews doing?"** — Hebrew הָאֲמֵלָלִים, with the article, no "these"; עֹשִׂים a participle. **"will they fortify themselves?" → "Will they leave it to themselves?"** — Hebrew הֲיַעַזְבוּ לָהֶם, עָזַב "leave", as 3:8; the sense is uncertain ("leave [it] to themselves", "leave [it] to [their god]", or a second root "restore"); "it" is supplied. **"will they make an end" → "Will they finish"** — Hebrew הַיְכַלּוּ, כִּלָּה "finish". **"will they revive the stones out of the heaps of the rubbish which are burned?" → "Will they bring the stones to life out of the heaps of dust — and they are burned?"** — Hebrew הַיְחַיּוּ "make live"; עָפָר "dust" (also v10); וְהֵמָּה שְׂרוּפוֹת "and they are burned".
- **"Now Tobiah … was by him" → "And Tobiah … was beside him"** (v3) — Hebrew אֶצְלוֹ. **"Even that which they build, if a fox go up, he shall even break down their stone wall" → "Even what they are building — if a fox goes up, he will breach their wall of stones!"** — Hebrew גַּם אֲשֶׁר־הֵם בּוֹנִים, a participle; פָרַץ "breach", as 1:3; חוֹמַת אַבְנֵיהֶם "the wall of their stones".
- **"Hear, O our God; for we are despised" → "Hear, our Elohim, for we have become a contempt"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי־הָיִינוּ בוּזָה, a noun: "we have become contempt". **"turn their reproach upon their own head" → "turn back their reproach on their own head"** — Hebrew הָשֵׁב, hifil of שׁוּב; "own" is supplied for English. **"for a prey in the land of captivity" → "for plunder in a land of captivity"** — Hebrew לְבִזָּה, of בָּזַז "plunder"; בְּאֶרֶץ שִׁבְיָה, without the article.
- **"let not their sin be blotted out" → "let not their sin be wiped out"** (v5) — Hebrew מָחָה "wipe out". **"for they have provoked thee to anger before the builders" → "for they have caused vexation in front of the builders"** — Hebrew כִּי הִכְעִיסוּ לְנֶגֶד הַבּוֹנִים: הִכְעִיס "vex" has no object here; "thee" is not in the Hebrew. Whom they vexed (Elohim, or the builders to their face) is not said; לְנֶגֶד "in front of, in sight of".
- **"So built we the wall; and all the wall was joined together unto the half thereof" → "And we built the wall, and all the wall was bound together up to its half"** (v6) — Hebrew וַתִּקָּשֵׁר, nifal of קָשַׁר "bind" (the verb of "conspire", v8). **"for the people had a mind to work" → "and the people had a heart to do it"** — Hebrew וַיְהִי לֵב לָעָם לַעֲשׂוֹת, literally "and there was a heart to the people to do"; "it" is supplied.
- **"the Arabians … the Ashdodites"** (v7) — Hebrew הָעַרְבִים, הָאַשְׁדּוֹדִים. **"that the walls of Jerusalem were made up" → "that healing had come up on the walls of Jerusalem"** — Hebrew כִּי־עָלְתָה אֲרוּכָה לְחֹמוֹת, אֲרוּכָה the new flesh that grows over a wound (Jer 8:22), said of the walls; עָלָה "come up". **"the breaches began to be stopped" → "that the breaches had begun to be stopped"** — Hebrew כִּי־הֵחֵלּוּ, a second "that". **"then they were very wroth" → "they burned with anger greatly"** — Hebrew וַיִּחַר לָהֶם מְאֹד, as v1.
- **"conspired all of them together … and to hinder it" → "they all conspired together … and to cause confusion for it"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיִּקְשְׁרוּ, קָשַׁר "conspire" (literally "bind"); לַעֲשׂוֹת לוֹ תּוֹעָה, תּוֹעָה "error, confusion" (from תָּעָה "go astray"); לוֹ "for it, for him".
- **"Nevertheless we made our prayer unto our God, and set a watch" → "And we prayed to our Elohim and set a guard"** (v9) — Hebrew וַנִּתְפַּלֵּל; מִשְׁמָר, literally "a keeping", here of men on watch ("guard", also v22, 23). **"against them … because of them"** — Hebrew עֲלֵיהֶם … מִפְּנֵיהֶם.
- **"The strength of the bearers of burdens is decayed, and there is much rubbish; so that we are not able to build the wall" → "The strength of the burden-bearer has stumbled, and the dust is much, and we are not able to build on the wall"** (v10) — Hebrew כָּשַׁל "stumble"; הַסַּבָּל, a singular collective; וְהֶעָפָר הַרְבֵּה; לִבְנוֹת בַּחוֹמָה, literally "to build in the wall". The words fall in short lines like a lament.
- **"our adversaries" (v11)** — Hebrew צָרֵינוּ, צַר "adversary" (אוֹיֵב "enemy", v15). **"till we come in the midst among them, and slay them, and cause the work to cease" → "until we come into their midst and kill them and make the work cease"** — Hebrew הָרַג "kill"; הִשְׁבִּית "make cease".
- **"the Jews which dwelt by them" → "the Jews who were dwelling beside them"** (v12) — Hebrew הַיֹּשְׁבִים אֶצְלָם, a participle. **"From all places whence ye shall return unto us they will be upon you" → "From all the places where you turn back — upon us!"** — Hebrew מִכָּל־הַמְּקֹמוֹת אֲשֶׁר־תָּשׁוּבוּ עָלֵינוּ, literally "from all the places that you turn back upon us": the sentence is broken and its sense is uncertain. It is read as a warning that the enemy will come from every side ("they will be upon us"), or as a call to the men to come home ("return to us"); the verse keeps the words and supplies nothing.
- **"Therefore set I in the lower places behind the wall, and on the higher places, I even set the people after their families" → "And I stationed, in the lowest parts of the place behind the wall, in the bare places — and I stationed the people by clans"** (v13) — Hebrew וָאַעֲמִיד "and I made stand" twice, the first time with no object; מִתַּחְתִּיּוֹת לַמָּקוֹם "in the lowest parts of the place"; the qere בַּצְּחִיחִים "in the bare (exposed) places", of צָחִיחַ "bare, glaring" (ketiv בצחחיים, another spelling); "higher" is not the word. מִשְׁפָּחוֹת "clans".
- **"And I looked, and rose up" → "And I saw, and I arose"** (v14) — Hebrew וָאֵרֶא "and I saw". **"the nobles, and to the rulers" → "the nobles and to the prefects"** (v14, 19) — Hebrew חֹרִים, סְגָנִים, as 2:16. **"Be not ye afraid of them" → "Do not be afraid of them"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵיהֶם, literally "from their face". **"which is great and terrible" → "the great and the fearsome"** — Hebrew הַגָּדוֹל וְהַנּוֹרָא, as 1:5. **"your houses"** — Hebrew בָּתֵּיכֶם.
- **"God had brought their counsel to nought" → "Elohim had made their counsel void"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיָּפֶר, הֵפֵר "make void, break". **"that we returned all of us" → "that we returned, all of us"** — the qere וַנָּשָׁב "and we returned"; the ketiv ונשוב reads "and we return". **"every one unto his work" → "each man to his work"** — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶל־מְלַאכְתּוֹ.
- **"from that time forth" → "from that day"** (v16) — Hebrew מִן־הַיּוֹם הַהוּא. **"the half of my servants wrought in the work" → "half of my young men were doing the work"** (v16, 22, 23) — Hebrew נְעָרַי, נַעַר "boy, young man", of attendants; עֹשִׂים בַּמְּלָאכָה, a participle. **"held both the spears … and the habergeons" → "were holding both the spears … and the coats of mail"** — Hebrew מַחֲזִיקִים, the hifil of חָזַק, the verb of "repair" in ch. 3, here "hold" (v16, 17, 21; with בְּ in v21 "hold fast"); the Hebrew has "and" before "the spears"; שִׁרְיֹן "coat of mail". **"the rulers were behind all the house of Judah" → "the commanders were behind all the house of Judah"** — Hebrew הַשָּׂרִים, שַׂר "prince", of men set over armed men "commander" (in ch. 3, of districts, "chief": the one word).
- **"They which builded on the wall, and they that bare burdens, with those that laded, every one with one of his hands wrought in the work, and with the other hand held a weapon" → "Those who were building on the wall and those who carried the load were loading: with one of his hands each was doing the work, and the other was holding the weapon"** (v17) — Hebrew הַנֹּשְׂאִים בַּסֶּבֶל עֹמְשִׂים, עֹמְשִׂים a by-form of עָמַס "load"; בְּאַחַת יָדוֹ "with one his hand"; הַשָּׁלַח "the weapon" (a thrown weapon or a sword; also v23). "Each" is supplied for English.
- **"For the builders, every one had his sword girded by his side, and so builded" → "And the builders, each man had his sword bound on his loins, and they were building"** (v18) — Hebrew אֲסוּרִים עַל־מָתְנָיו, אָסַר "bind"; מָתְנַיִם "loins". **"he that sounded the trumpet" → "the one who blew the shofar"** (v18, 20) — Hebrew הַתּוֹקֵעַ בַּשּׁוֹפָר.
- **"great and large" → "great and broad"** (v19) — Hebrew רְחָבָה. **"one far from another" → "far off each man from his brother"** — Hebrew רְחוֹקִים אִישׁ מֵאָחִיו.
- **"In what place therefore ye hear" → "In the place where you hear"** (v20) — Hebrew בִּמְקוֹם אֲשֶׁר, no "therefore". **"resort ye thither unto us" → "there gather to us"** — Hebrew תִּקָּבְצוּ, nifal of קָבַץ "gather".
- **"So we laboured in the work" → "And we were doing the work"** (v21) — Hebrew עֹשִׂים, a participle. **"from the rising of the morning till the stars appeared" → "from the going up of the dawn until the coming out of the stars"** — Hebrew מֵעֲלוֹת הַשַּׁחַר עַד צֵאת הַכּוֹכָבִים.
- **"Let every one with his servant lodge within Jerusalem, that in the night they may be a guard to us, and labour on the day" → "Let each man and his young man lodge in the midst of Jerusalem, and they shall be for us a guard by night and work by day"** (v22) — Hebrew יָלִינוּ, לוּן "lodge"; וְהָיוּ־לָנוּ הַלַּיְלָה מִשְׁמָר וְהַיּוֹם מְלָאכָה, literally "and they shall be to us the night a guard and the day work".
- **"So neither I … none of us put off our clothes, saving that every one put them off for washing" → "And neither I … — we were not taking off our garments; each man his weapon, the water"** (v23) — Hebrew וְאֵין אֲנִי … אֵין־אֲנַחְנוּ פֹשְׁטִים בְּגָדֵינוּ, "there was not … we were not taking off", a participle; בֶּגֶד "garment"; אֲשֶׁר אַחֲרַי "who were after me". The last words, אִישׁ שִׁלְחוֹ הַמָּיִם, are literally "each man his weapon, the water": their sense is not known. They are read "each kept his weapon (even when he went to) the water", or "each had his weapon in his right hand" by a change of letters (הַיָּמִין for הַמָּיִם; cited from memory); "for washing" of the KJV is a guess. The Masoretic words are kept, and nothing is supplied.

### Notes
- **Chapter numbering** — the Hebrew Bible ends chapter 3 six verses later: our 4:1–6 = Heb 3:33–38, and our 4:7–23 = Heb 4:1–17. Our numbers are the KJV's.
- **Opposition escalates** — verbal mockery → conspiracy → armed threat. Nehemiah's counter-measures: prayer + defensive organization + arming builders.
- **Fox on the wall** — Tobiah's joke: even a slight fox would breach this wall. Exaggerated dismissal.
- **"Adversary coalition"** — Sanballat (Samaria/N), Tobiah (Ammon/E), Arabs (S), Ashdodites (W). Jerusalem encircled by hostile officials.
- **"One hand work, one hand weapon"** (v17) — iconic image; proverb of embattled labor.
- **Shofar as rallying signal** — wall is too long for sight-lines; audio-summons central command.
- **Constant vigilance** — Nehemiah's entourage sleeps clothed and armed.
- **Imprecation (v4–5)** — Nehemiah turns from narrative to prayer without an introduction ("Hear, our Elohim").
