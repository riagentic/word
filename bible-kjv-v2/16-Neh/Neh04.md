# Nehemiah 4

[1] When ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ heard that we were building the wall, he was angry and greatly enraged, and he mocked the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[2] He spoke before his kinsmen and the army of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and said, "What are these feeble ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ doing? Will they fortify themselves? Will they offer sacrifice? Will they finish in a day? Will they revive the stones out of the heaps of rubbish — and those burned?"

[3] ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧ was beside him; he said, "Whatever they are building, if even a fox goes up on it, he will break down their stone wall."

[4] Hear, O our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for we are despised. Turn their taunt back on their own head; give them as plunder in a land of captivity.

[5] Do not cover over their iniquity, and let not their sin be blotted out from before you, for they have provoked you to anger before the builders.

[6] So we built the wall; all the wall was joined together to half its height, for the people had a heart to work.

[7] When ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧, ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧, the ⟦arabians|Arvim|Arabs⟧, the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧, and the ⟦ashdodites|Ashdodim|Ashdodites⟧ heard that the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ were being restored and the breaches were beginning to be closed, they were very angry.

[8] They all conspired together to come and fight against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, to cause it confusion.

[9] But we prayed to our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and set a guard against them day and night because of them.

[10] ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ said, "The strength of those who bear burdens is failing, and there is so much rubbish that we are not able to rebuild the wall."

[11] Our enemies said, "They will not know or see until we come among them, kill them, and stop the work."

[12] When the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who lived near them came, they said to us ten times over, "From every place where you turn, they will come upon us."

[13] Therefore I stationed men in the lowest parts of the space behind the wall, in exposed places; I stationed the people by families, with their swords, spears, and bows.

[14] I looked, rose up, and said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, "Do not be afraid of them; remember ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧, who is great and awesome, and fight for your brothers, your sons and daughters, your wives and your homes."

[15] When our enemies heard that it was known to us, and that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had brought their counsel to nothing, all of us returned to the wall, each to his work.

[16] From that time on, half of my men worked on the construction, while half held the spears, shields, bows, and coats of mail; the officials stood behind all the house of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[17] Those who were rebuilding the wall and those who carried burdens worked with one hand doing the work, and with the other holding a weapon.

[18] Each of the builders wore his sword at his side as he built. The man who sounded the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ was beside me.

[19] I said to the nobles, the officials, and the rest of the people, "The work is great and widespread, and we are separated on the wall, far from one another.

[20] In whatever place you hear the sound of the ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧, rally to us there; our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will fight for us."

[21] So we labored at the work, and half of them held the spears from the break of dawn until the stars came out.

[22] At that time I also said to the people, "Let each man and his servant lodge inside ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, so that they may be a guard for us by night and a worker by day."

[23] So neither I, nor my brothers, nor my men, nor the men of the guard who followed me — none of us took off our clothes; each kept his weapon even when going for water.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"indignation" → "enraged"** (v1) — *charah lo me'od*.
- **"habergeons" → "coats of mail"** (v16).
- **"none of us put off our clothes, saving that every one put them off for washing" → "none of us took off our clothes; each kept his weapon even when going for water"** (v23) — Hebrew text ambiguous; traditional reading "for washing," but text may imply continuous armament. Translated to preserve the sense of constant readiness.
- **"great and terrible" → "great and awesome"** (v14) — *ha-gadol ve-ha-nora*.

### Notes
- **Chapter numbering** — Hebrew Bible ends chapter 3 six verses later: MT 3:33-38 = English 4:1-6, MT 4:1-17 = English 4:7-23.
- **Opposition escalates** — verbal mockery → conspiracy → armed threat. Nehemiah's counter-measures: prayer + defensive organization + arming builders.
- **Fox on the wall** — Tobiah's joke: even a slight fox would collapse this wall. Exaggerated dismissal.
- **"Adversary coalition"** — Sanballat (Samaria/N), Tobiah (Ammon/E), Arabs (S), Ashdodites (W). Jerusalem encircled by hostile officials.
- **"One hand work, one hand weapon"** (v17) — iconic image; proverb of embattled labor.
- **Shofar as rallying signal** — wall is too long for sight-lines; audio-summons central command.
- **Constant vigilance** — Nehemiah's entourage sleeps clothed and armed.
