# Nehemiah 5

[1] And there was a great outcry of the people and of their wives against their brothers the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[2] And there were those who were saying, "Our sons and our daughters, we are many; and let us get grain, and let us eat and live."

[3] And there were those who were saying, "Our fields and our vineyards and our houses we are pledging, and let us get grain in the famine."

[4] And there were those who were saying, "We have borrowed silver for the tribute of the king — our fields and our vineyards.

[5] And now, as the flesh of our brothers is our flesh, as their sons are our sons; and behold, we are subduing our sons and our daughters to be slaves, and some of our daughters are subdued, and it is not in the power of our hand, and our fields and our vineyards are for others."

[6] And it burned to me greatly when I heard their outcry and these words.

[7] And my heart took counsel upon me, and I contended with the nobles and with the prefects, and I said to them, "A loan, each man against his brother, you are lending!" And I set a great assembly against them.

[8] And I said to them, "We have bought back our brothers the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were sold to the nations, as far as was in us; and will you also sell your brothers, and they be sold to us?" And they were silent and did not find a word.

[9] And I said, "The thing that you are doing is not good. Should you not walk in the fear of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because of the reproach of the nations, our enemies?

[10] And also I, my brothers and my young men are lending to them silver and grain. Let us forsake, please, this loan.

[11] Return to them, please, this very day, their fields, their vineyards, their olive trees and their houses, and the hundredth of the silver and the grain, the new wine and the fresh oil, that you are lending to them."

[12] And they said, "We will return, and from them we will not seek; so we will do, as you are saying." And I called the priests and made them swear to do according to this word.

[13] Also my lap I shook out, and I said, "So may ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shake out every man who does not establish this word, from his house and from his labor; and so shall he be shaken out and empty." And all the assembly said, "Amen," and they praised ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And the people did according to this word.

[14] Also, from the day that he commanded me to be their governor in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, from the twentieth year to the thirty-second year of ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ the king, twelve years, I and my brothers did not eat the bread of the governor.

[15] And the former governors who were before me made it heavy on the people, and they took from them for bread and wine, besides forty shekels of silver; also their young men had power over the people. And I did not do so, because of the fear of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[16] And also in the work of this wall I held fast, and a field we did not buy; and all my young men were gathered there for the work.

[17] And the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and the prefects, a hundred and fifty men, and those who came to us from the nations that were around us, were at my table.

[18] And what was made for one day was one ox, six choice ones of the flock, and birds were made for me; and once in ten days all wine in abundance. And with this, the bread of the governor I did not seek, for the service was heavy on this people.

[19] Remember for me, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for good, all that I have done for this people.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v13) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9, 13, 15, 19) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v13 with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"a great cry … against their brethren the Jews" → "a great outcry … against their brothers the Jews"** (v1, 6) — Hebrew צַעֲקָה (v1) and זַעֲקָה (v6): one word in two spellings, "outcry". אֶל is "to" or "against". "fellow Jews" → "brothers": אַחֵיהֶם (also v5, 8, 10, 14).
- **"there were that said" → "there were those who were saying"** (v2, 3, 4) — Hebrew וְיֵשׁ אֲשֶׁר אֹמְרִים, a participle. **"We, our sons, and our daughters, are many" → "Our sons and our daughters, we are many"** (v2) — the Hebrew puts the sons and daughters first. Many read עֹרְבִים "pledging" (as v3) for רַבִּים "many" (a conjecture, not in any Hebrew text); the Masoretic Text is kept. **"take up corn" → "let us get grain"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew לָקַח, "take"; "and let us eat and live" are two more wishes (cohortatives).
- **"We have mortgaged" → "we are pledging"** (v3) — Hebrew עֹרְבִים, a participle of עָרַב, here "give in pledge" (not "be surety for", Gen 43:9, which has a person as object). **"because of the dearth" → "in the famine"** — Hebrew בָּרָעָב.
- **"for the king's tribute, and that upon our lands and vineyards" → "for the tribute of the king — our fields and our vineyards"** (v4) — Hebrew לְמִדַּת הַמֶּלֶךְ שְׂדֹתֵינוּ וּכְרָמֵינוּ: the fields and vineyards stand after the tribute with no preposition (= the land that was put up as security); "upon" is not written.
- **"we bring into bondage our sons … to be servants" → "we are subduing our sons and our daughters to be slaves"** (v5) — Hebrew כֹבְשִׁים … לַעֲבָדִים: כָּבַשׁ is "subdue" (Gen 1:28), and עֶבֶד "slave". **"some of our daughters are brought unto bondage already" → "some of our daughters are subdued"** — the same verb in the nifal; "already" is not written. The verb is used of forcing a woman in Esth 7:8; whether that is meant here is not said. **"neither is it in our power to redeem them" → "it is not in the power of our hand"** — Hebrew וְאֵין לְאֵל יָדֵנוּ, literally "there is not to the power of our hand" (as Gen 31:29; Deut 28:32): the noun אֵל here is "power", not the divine name; "to redeem them" is not written. **"other men have our lands" → "our fields and our vineyards are for others"** — Hebrew לַאֲחֵרִים.
- **"I was very angry" → "it burned to me greatly"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר לִי מְאֹד, no אַף: "it burned to me" = I burned with anger.
- **"I consulted with myself" → "my heart took counsel upon me"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיִּמָּלֵךְ לִבִּי עָלַי: נִמְלַךְ "take counsel" (a late word, from Aramaic). **"rebuked" → "contended with"** — Hebrew רִיב. **"the nobles, and the rulers" → "the nobles and … the prefects"** (v7, 17) — Hebrew הַחֹרִים and הַסְּגָנִים: סְגָן is a deputy or prefect, an office under the governor.
- **"Ye exact usury, every one of his brother" → "A loan, each man against his brother, you are lending!"** (v7) — Hebrew מַשָּׁא אִישׁ־בְּאָחִיו אַתֶּם נֹשִׁים: מַשָּׁא is a loan, the debt itself (as מַשֶּׁה Deut 15:2; מַשָּׁאת Deut 24:10), from the verb נָשָׁה "lend". Whether interest was taken is not said by the word; "usury" and "interest" read that in (the one thing named is the hundredth, v11). The written form (ketiv) is נשאים "bearing, lifting"; the reading (qere) נֹשִׁים "lending" is translated. **"And I set a great assembly against them"** — Hebrew וָאֶתֵּן עֲלֵיהֶם קְהִלָּה גְדוֹלָה, literally "gave against them"; קְהִלָּה (here) and קָהָל (v13) are two forms of one root: "assembly".
- **"We after our ability have redeemed" → "We have bought back … as far as was in us"** (v8) — Hebrew קָנִינוּ "we have bought" (קָנָה; "redeem" is גָּאַל), and כְּדֵי בָנוּ, literally "according to the sufficiency in us". **"or shall they be sold unto us?" → "and they be sold to us?"** — Hebrew וְנִמְכְּרוּ־לָנוּ, "and they will be sold to us"; there is no question word: the question is in the tone (the whole sentence may also be read as a statement of fact). **"found nothing to answer" → "did not find a word"** — Hebrew דָּבָר.
- **"Also I said" → "And I said"** (v9) — the reading (qere) וָאוֹמַר "and I said" is translated; the written form (ketiv) is וַיֹּאמֶר "and he said". **"It is not good that ye do" → "The thing that you are doing is not good"** — Hebrew הַדָּבָר. **"because of the reproach" (v9)** — Hebrew מֵחֶרְפַּת, literally "from the reproach": so as not to give the nations ground for it.
- **"my servants" → "my young men"** (v10, 15, 16) — Hebrew נְעָרַי, נַעֲרֵיהֶם: the word for a boy or young man, used of attendants. **"might exact of them money and corn" → "are lending to them silver and grain"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew נֹשִׁים בָּהֶם, a participle: they are doing it now. **"I pray you, let us leave off this usury" → "Let us forsake, please, this loan"** — Hebrew נַעַזְבָה־נָּא אֶת־הַמַּשָּׁא הַזֶּה: עָזַב "forsake" (give up the claim), נָא "please" (also v11).
- **"Restore, I pray you, to them, even this day" → "Return to them, please, this very day"** (v11) — Hebrew הָשִׁיבוּ נָא … כְּהַיּוֹם, literally "as the day". **"oliveyards" → "olive trees"** — Hebrew זֵיתֵיהֶם. **"the hundredth part" → "the hundredth"** (v11) — Hebrew וּמְאַת: the hundredth part of the silver and of the produce, commonly taken as interest of one in a hundred (by the month); some change the word to מַשַּׁאת "the loan of" (a conjecture). The Masoretic Text is kept. **"the wine, and the oil" → "the new wine and the fresh oil"** — Hebrew הַתִּירוֹשׁ וְהַיִּצְהָר.
- **"We will restore them, and will require nothing of them" → "We will return, and from them we will not seek"** (v12) — Hebrew נָשִׁיב וּמֵהֶם לֹא נְבַקֵּשׁ; "them" and "nothing" are not written after the verbs. **"took an oath of them" → "made them swear"** — Hebrew וָאַשְׁבִּיעֵם: "them" is either the priests just named or the nobles and prefects of v7; the Hebrew does not say. **"according to this promise" → "according to this word"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew כַּדָּבָר הַזֶּה (three times).
- **"Also I shook my lap" → "Also my lap I shook out"** (v13) — Hebrew חָצְנִי, the fold of the garment over the chest, where things were carried. **"that performeth not this promise" → "who does not establish this word"** — Hebrew לֹא־יָקִים אֶת־הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה, "make stand" (הֵקִים דָּבָר "establish a word"). **"and from his labour" (v13)** — Hebrew יְגִיעוֹ, what his toil has gained. **"even thus be he shaken out, and emptied" → "and so shall he be shaken out and empty"** — Hebrew יִהְיֶה נָעוּר וָרֵק, imperfect ("shall be"), and רֵק an adjective, "empty".
- **"from the time that I was appointed" → "from the day that he commanded me"** (v14) — Hebrew מִיּוֹם אֲשֶׁר־צִוָּה אֹתִי: the subject (the king) is not named. **"have not eaten the bread of the governor"** kept (v14, 18) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַפֶּחָה, the governor's allowance of food; the verb is singular, "I did not eat", after "I and my brothers".
- **"were chargeable unto the people" → "made it heavy on the people"** (v15) — Hebrew הִכְבִּידוּ עַל־הָעָם, the hifil of כָּבֵד "be heavy" (also v18 "the service was heavy"). **"had taken of them bread and wine" → "took from them for bread and wine"** — Hebrew בְּלֶחֶם וָיַיִן, "in bread and wine". **"beside forty shekels of silver"** — Hebrew אַחַר, literally "after": the sense "besides, in addition" is not certain. **"bare rule over" → "had power over"** — Hebrew שָׁלְטוּ עַל, שָׁלַט "have power over" (beside מָשַׁל "rule"; as Esth 9:1). **"because of the fear of God"** — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי יִרְאַת אֱלֹהִים.
- **"I continued in the work" → "I held fast"** (v16) — Hebrew הֶחֱזַקְתִּי, the hifil of חָזַק, the verb that names the "repairing" of the wall in ch. 3 ("made strong"). **"neither bought we any land" → "a field we did not buy"** — Hebrew וְשָׂדֶה לֹא קָנִינוּ, "we" (plural).
- **"at my table … beside those that came" → "… were at my table"** (v17) — Hebrew עַל־שֻׁלְחָנִי stands at the end; "were" is supplied.
- **"that which was prepared for me daily" → "what was made for one day"** (v18) — Hebrew נַעֲשֶׂה לְיוֹם אֶחָד, עָשָׂה "make" (twice: "and birds were made for me"). **"six choice sheep" → "six choice ones of the flock"** — Hebrew צֹאן שֵׁשׁ־בְּרֻרוֹת: צֹאן is the flock of sheep and goats. **"once in ten days store of all sorts of wine" → "once in ten days all wine in abundance"** — Hebrew וּבֵין עֲשֶׂרֶת יָמִים בְּכָל־יַיִן לְהַרְבֵּה, literally "between ten days, with all wine, to make much": "all wine" = every kind. **"required not I" → "I did not seek"** — Hebrew בִקַּשְׁתִּי. **"the bondage was heavy" → "the service was heavy"** — Hebrew הָעֲבֹדָה.
- **"Think upon me, my God, for good" → "Remember for me, my *Elohim*, for good"** (v19) — Hebrew זָכְרָה־לִּי אֱלֹהַי לְטוֹבָה: זָכַר "remember"; לִּי is "for me" (to my account), and the object is "all that I have done". "O" is not written. The same words open 13:31; 6:14; 13:14, 22 have זָכְרָה … לְ as well.

### Notes
- **Internal crisis** — while battling external enemies, Nehemiah confronts economic injustice within.
- **Three complaints** — food for many mouths (v2), the pledging of property for grain (v3), borrowing for the Persian tribute, and sons and daughters made slaves (v4–5).
- **Nehemiah's solution** — the loans are given up and the pledged fields returned at once. Compare the release of loans in the seventh year (Deut 15:1–3) and the law against interest among brothers (Exod 22:25; Lev 25:36–37; Deut 23:19–20).
- **Oath + symbolic lap-shake** (v12–13) — covenant by gesture: the emptied fold = may *Elohim* empty the man who breaks it.
- **12-year service** (v14) — 445–433 BC. Nehemiah declines the governor's revenue.
- **Table of 150** — Nehemiah hosts at his own expense. Generous hospitality as public service.
- **"Remember for me" prayers** (v19) — recurring Nehemiah phrase (also 6:14; 13:14, 22, 29, 31). Appeal to divine memory for his deeds.
