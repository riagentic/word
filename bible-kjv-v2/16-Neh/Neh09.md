# Nehemiah 9

[1] And on the twenty-fourth day of this month the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were gathered with fasting and with sackcloth, and ground upon them.

[2] And the seed of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ separated themselves from all foreigners, and they stood and confessed their sins and the iniquities of their fathers.

[3] And they stood up in their place and read in the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ a fourth of the day, and a fourth they were confessing and bowing down to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[4] And there stood up on the ascent of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ and Bani, ⟦kadmiel|Qadmiel|Kadmiel⟧, ⟦shebaniah|Shevanyah|Shebaniah⟧, Bunni, ⟦sherebiah|Sherevyah|Sherebiah⟧, Bani, ⟦chenani|Kenani|Chenani⟧, and they cried out with a loud voice to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ said, ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ and ⟦kadmiel|Qadmiel|Kadmiel⟧, Bani, ⟦hashabneiah|Chashavneyah|Hashabneiah⟧, ⟦sherebiah|Sherevyah|Sherebiah⟧, ⟦hodiah|Hodiyah|Hodiah⟧, ⟦shebaniah|Shevanyah|Shebaniah⟧, ⟦pethahiah|Petachyah|Pethahiah⟧:

"Stand up, bless ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧
from the age to the age!
And let them bless the name of your glory,
which is exalted above all blessing and praise.

[6] You are he, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, you alone.
You have made the heavens,
the heavens of the heavens and all their host,
the earth and all that is on it,
the seas and all that is in them;
and you give life to all of them,
and the host of the heavens bows down to you.

[7] You are he, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who chose ⟦abram|Avram|Abram⟧
and brought him out from Ur of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧
and set his name ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[8] And you found his heart faithful before you,
and cut with him the covenant
to give the land of the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧, the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧, the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧
and the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧ and the ⟦girgashites|Girgashi|Girgashite⟧,
to give it to his seed;
and you have established your words,
for you are righteous.

[9] And you saw the affliction of our fathers in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and their cry you heard at the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧.

[10] And you gave signs and wonders on ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧
and on all his servants and on all the people of his land,
for you knew that they acted presumptuously against them;
and you made for yourself a name, as at this day.

[11] And the sea you split before them,
and they passed over in the midst of the sea on the dry land;
and their pursuers you cast into the depths,
like a stone into strong waters.

[12] And by a pillar of cloud you led them by day,
and by a pillar of fire by night,
to light for them the way in which they should go.

[13] And upon Mount Sinai you came down
and spoke with them from the heavens,
and gave them right ordinances and true ⟦torah|torot|laws⟧,
good statutes and commandments.

[14] And your holy ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ you made known to them,
and commandments and statutes and ⟦torah|torah|law⟧ you commanded them
by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ your servant.

[15] And bread from the heavens you gave them for their hunger,
and water from the crag you brought out for them for their thirst;
and you said to them to go in to take possession of the land
that you had lifted up your hand to give them.

[16] And they and our fathers acted presumptuously,
and they stiffened their neck
and did not listen to your commandments.

[17] And they refused to hear
and did not remember your wonderful deeds that you did with them;
and they stiffened their neck
and appointed a head to return to their slavery in their rebellion.
But you are an ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ of forgiveness,
gracious and compassionate,
slow to anger and abounding in ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and you did not forsake them.

[18] Even when they made for themselves a molten calf
and said, 'This is your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who brought you up from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,'
and did great acts of contempt,

[19] you in your great compassion
did not forsake them in the wilderness.
The pillar of cloud did not turn aside from over them by day,
to lead them on the way,
and the pillar of fire by night,
to light for them the way in which they should go.

[20] And your good spirit you gave to make them wise,
and your manna you did not hold back from their mouth,
and water you gave them for their thirst.

[21] And forty years you sustained them in the wilderness; they did not lack.
Their cloaks did not wear out,
and their feet did not swell.

[22] And you gave them kingdoms and peoples,
and divided them out to the edge;
and they took possession of the land of ⟦sihon|Sichon|Sihon⟧ and the land of the king of ⟦heshbon|Cheshbon|Heshbon⟧,
and the land of Og king of the Bashan.

[23] And their sons you multiplied like the stars of the heavens,
and brought them into the land
that you had said to their fathers to go in to take possession of.

[24] And the sons went in and took possession of the land,
and you brought low before them
the inhabitants of the land, the ⟦canaanites|Kenaanim|Canaanites⟧,
and gave them into their hand, and their kings and the peoples of the land,
to do with them according to their pleasure.

[25] And they captured fortified cities and a fat ground,
and took possession of houses full of every good thing,
hewn pits, vineyards and olive trees and trees for food in abundance;
and they ate and were sated and grew fat,
and delighted themselves in your great goodness.

[26] And they were rebellious and rebelled against you,
and they threw your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ behind their back,
and your prophets they killed, who warned them
to bring them back to you;
and they did great acts of contempt.

[27] And you gave them into the hand of their adversaries, and they distressed them.
And in the time of their distress they would cry out to you,
and you would hear from the heavens,
and according to your great compassion
you would give them saviors,
and they would save them from the hand of their adversaries.

[28] And when they had rest, they would turn back to do evil before you,
and you would leave them in the hand of their enemies,
and they had dominion over them.
And they would turn back and cry out to you,
and you would hear from the heavens
and deliver them according to your compassion, many times.

[29] And you warned them, to bring them back to your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧;
and they acted presumptuously
and did not listen to your commandments,
and against your ordinances they sinned,
which a man shall do and live by them;
and they gave a stubborn shoulder, and their neck they stiffened,
and did not hear.

[30] And you drew out many years over them,
and warned them by your spirit by the hand of your prophets,
and they did not give ear;
and you gave them into the hand of the peoples of the lands.

[31] And in your great compassion you did not make a full end of them and did not forsake them,
for you are an ⟦el|El|God⟧ gracious and compassionate.

[32] And now, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,
the great ⟦el|El|God⟧, the mighty and the fearsome,
keeping the covenant and the ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
let not all the hardship be little before you
that has found us, our kings, our princes
and our priests and our prophets and our fathers and all your people,
from the days of the kings of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ to this day.

[33] And you are righteous in all that has come upon us,
for you have done truth,
and we have done wickedly.

[34] And our kings, our princes, our priests and our fathers
have not done your ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and have not given heed to your commandments
and to your testimonies with which you warned them.

[35] And they, in their kingdom and in your great goodness that you gave them,
and in the broad and fat land that you set before them,
did not serve you
and did not turn back from their evil deeds.

[36] Behold, we today are slaves,
and the land that you gave to our fathers
to eat its fruit and its good —
behold, we are slaves upon it.

[37] And its yield multiplies for the kings whom you have set over us in our sins,
and over our bodies they rule,
and over our beasts, according to their pleasure,
and in great distress are we."

[38] And in all this we are cutting a firm agreement and writing it; and on the sealed document are our princes, our ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, our priests.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v7 the Hebrew has the article, יְהוָה הָאֱלֹהִים "Yahweh the Elohim".
- **"God" → "Eloah" / "El"** (v17; v31, 32) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah* (v17) and אֵל *El* (v31, 32), not *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, and "Then", "So", "Therefore", "Yet", "But" are not added where the Hebrew has only "and" (v5, 24, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31). "But" stands in v17 for וְאַתָּה "and you", where the clause turns against the one before.
- **The prayer retells the Torah** — where the Hebrew uses the words of an earlier verse, the finished wording of that verse is used: Gen 15:18–21 (v8, "cut … the covenant", the peoples in the singular), Exod 3:7 (v9), Exod 14:16, 22; 15:5 (v11), Exod 13:21 (v12, 19), Exod 19:20; 20:22 (v13), Exod 6:8 (v15), Num 14:4 (v17), Exod 34:6 (v17, 31), Exod 32:4 (v18), Deut 8:4; 29:5 (v21), Deut 6:11 (v25), Lev 18:5 (v29), Deut 10:17 and 7:9 (v32).
- **"were assembled with fasting, in sackcloth, and with dust on them" → "were gathered with fasting and with sackcloth, and ground upon them"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּצוֹם וּבְשַׂקִּים וַאֲדָמָה עֲלֵיהֶם: שַׂקִּים is plural, "sackcloths"; אֲדָמָה is "ground, soil", as 1 Sam 4:12; 2 Sam 1:2 ("dust" is עָפָר). **"children" → "sons"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"strangers" → "foreigners"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּנֵי נֵכָר, literally "sons of foreignness", as Exod 12:43.
- **"stood up in their place, and read in the book" → "stood up in their place and read in the book"** (v3) — Hebrew עַל־עָמְדָם "at their standing place"; סֵפֶר "book". **"worshipped" → "bowing down"** — Hebrew מִשְׁתַּחֲוִים, the participle of הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down"; "confessing" is a participle too.
- **"stairs" → "the ascent"** (v4) — Hebrew מַעֲלֵה, "ascent" (as Josh 10:10); a raised place reached by steps. Whether it was a platform or stairs is not said. **"cried with a loud voice"** — Hebrew זָעַק "cry out"; קוֹל גָּדוֹל, literally "great voice".
- **"Stand up and bless" → "Stand up, bless"** (v5) — Hebrew קוּמוּ בָּרֲכוּ, two imperatives without "and".
- **"for ever and ever" → "from the age to the age"** (v5) — Hebrew מִן־הָעוֹלָם עַד־הָעוֹלָם, with the article, as 1 Chr 16:36 (the reviewer's row); here the Hebrew has no "and" before עַד, so none is written. "From everlasting to everlasting" is the traditional rendering.
- **"blessed be thy glorious name" → "And let them bless the name of your glory"** (v5) — Hebrew וִיבָרְכוּ שֵׁם כְּבוֹדֶךָ, a third person plural wish ("let them bless"), not a passive; the speech turns to address Yahweh. **"which is exalted"** — Hebrew וּמְרוֹמַם, a participle, "and exalted"; "which is" supplied for the English.
- **"Thou, even thou, art LORD alone" → "You are he, Yahweh, you alone"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew אַתָּה־הוּא יְהוָה לְבַדֶּךָ, "you (are) he, Yahweh, by yourself"; v7 opens with the same אַתָּה־הוּא. **"You have made"** — the written text (ketiv) has את, the reading tradition (qere) אַתָּה "you"; the qere is followed ("you, you have made"); את is an old spelling of the same word, so the meaning does not differ.
- **"heaven of heavens" → "the heavens of the heavens"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׁמֵי הַשָּׁמַיִם, as Deut 10:14. **"thou preservest them all" → "you give life to all of them"** — Hebrew מְחַיֶּה, the piel participle of חָיָה "live": "making live, keeping alive". **"worshippeth" → "bows down"** — Hebrew מִשְׁתַּחֲוִים, as v3.
- **"didst choose Abram" → "chose Abram"; "gavest him the name of Abraham" → "set his name Abraham"** (v7) — Hebrew וְשַׂמְתָּ שְּׁמוֹ, literally "put his name".
- **"madest a covenant with him" → "cut with him the covenant"** (v8) — Hebrew וְכָרוֹת עִמּוֹ הַבְּרִית, an infinitive absolute continuing the verb before it; "cut a covenant" as Gen 15:18; the article "the covenant" is in the Hebrew. **"to give, I say, to his seed" → "to give … to give it to his seed"** — the Hebrew says לָתֵת twice. **The peoples** — Hebrew הַכְּנַעֲנִי הַחִתִּי … in the singular with the article, "the Canaanite, the Hittite …", as Gen 15:19–21; only the last three are joined by "and".
- **"hast performed thy words" → "have established your words"** (v8) — Hebrew וַתָּקֶם אֶת־דְּבָרֶיךָ, literally "made your words stand", as Deut 9:5.
- **"saw the affliction" kept; "heardest their cry by the Red sea" → "their cry you heard at the Sea of Reeds"** (v9) — Hebrew עֳנִי as Exod 3:7; זַעֲקָה "cry"; יַם־סוּף "sea of reeds" (token `red-sea`).
- **"shewedst signs and wonders upon Pharaoh" → "gave signs and wonders on Pharaoh"** (v10) — Hebrew וַתִּתֵּן אֹתֹת וּמֹפְתִים בְּ, "gave … on", as Deut 6:22. **"they dealt proudly against them" → "they acted presumptuously against them"** — Hebrew הֵזִידוּ עֲלֵיהֶם, as Exod 18:11 (also v16, 29). **"get thee a name, as it is this day" → "made for yourself a name, as at this day"** — Hebrew כְּהַיּוֹם הַזֶּה.
- **"didst divide the sea" → "the sea you split"** (v11) — Hebrew בָּקַעְתָּ, as Exod 14:16. **"went through" → "passed over in the midst of the sea on the dry land"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַבְרוּ בְתוֹךְ־הַיָּם בַּיַּבָּשָׁה, as Exod 14:22. **"threwest into the deeps" → "cast into the depths"** — Hebrew בִמְצוֹלֹת, as Exod 15:5 ("like a stone"). **"mighty waters" → "strong waters"** — Hebrew מַיִם עַזִּים, adjective עַז "strong"; Exod 15:10 "mighty waters" is another word (אַדִּירִים).
- **"Moreover thou leddest them" → "And … you led them"** (v12) — Hebrew הִנְחִיתָם, of נָחָה "lead". **"to give them light in the way" → "to light for them the way"** (v12, 19) — Hebrew לְהָאִיר לָהֶם אֶת־הַדֶּרֶךְ, "the way" is the object.
- **"right judgments, and true laws, good statutes and commandments" → "right ordinances and true torot, good statutes and commandments"** (v13) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים יְשָׁרִים וְתוֹרוֹת אֱמֶת, literally "torot of truth" (plural of *torah*, as Lev 26:46). **"from heaven" → "from the heavens"** (v13, 15, 27, 28) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם, always plural.
- **"madest known unto them thy holy sabbath" → "your holy Shabbat you made known to them"** (v14) — Hebrew שַׁבַּת קָדְשְׁךָ, literally "the Shabbat of your holiness". **"precepts" → "commandments"; "law" → "torah"** — Hebrew מִצְווֹת … וְתוֹרָה, without the article. **"by the hand of Moses thy servant"** — Hebrew בְּיַד; עֶבֶד "slave", of Yahweh's man "servant".
- **"out of the rock" → "from the crag"** (v15) — Hebrew סֶלַע, as Num 20:8. **"promisedst … that they should go in to possess" → "said to them to go in to take possession of"** (v15, 23) — Hebrew אָמַר "say"; יָרַשׁ "take possession". **"thou hadst sworn to give them" → "you had lifted up your hand to give them"** — Hebrew נָשָׂאתָ אֶת־יָדְךָ, the gesture of an oath (Exod 6:8).
- **"But they and our fathers" → "And they and our fathers"** (v16) — Hebrew וְהֵם וַאֲבֹתֵינוּ. The "and" may join two groups ("they", the generation just named, "and our fathers") or explain ("they, that is, our fathers"); the Hebrew is kept as it stands. **"hardened their necks" → "stiffened their neck"** (v16, 17, 29) — Hebrew הִקְשׁוּ אֶת־עָרְפָּם, singular "neck", as 2 Kgs 17:14. **"hearkened not to" → "did not listen to"** (v16, 29) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל / לְ.
- **"refused to obey" → "refused to hear"** (v17) — Hebrew לִשְׁמֹעַ "to hear". **"wonders" → "wonderful deeds"** — Hebrew נִפְלְאוֹת, as Exod 3:20; v10 has another word, מוֹפְתִים "wonders". **"that thou didst among them" → "that you did with them"** — Hebrew עִמָּהֶם.
- **"in their rebellion appointed a captain to return to their bondage" → "appointed a head to return to their slavery in their rebellion"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנוּ־רֹאשׁ לָשׁוּב לְעַבְדֻתָם בְּמִרְיָם, in that order; "appoint a head" as Num 14:4 (lit. "give a head"); עַבְדֻת "slavery". The Masoretic Text has בְּמִרְיָם "in their rebellion"; some Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint read בְּמִצְרַיִם "in Egypt", as Num 14:4 (cited from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"a God ready to pardon" → "an Eloah of forgiveness"** (v17) — Hebrew אֱלוֹהַּ סְלִיחוֹת, literally "an Eloah of forgivenesses" (plural). **"gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness" → "gracious and compassionate, slow to anger and abounding in chesed"** (v17, 31) — Hebrew חַנּוּן וְרַחוּם אֶרֶךְ־אַפַּיִם וְרַב־חֶסֶד, the words of Exod 34:6 in another order; אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם literally "long of nostrils". The written text (ketiv) has וחסד "and chesed", the qere חֶסֶד; the meaning does not differ. *Chesed* carries its token here and in v32.
- **"Yea, when they had made them a molten calf" → "Even when they made for themselves a molten calf"** (v18) — Hebrew אַף כִּי. **"This is thy God that brought thee up" kept: "This is your Elohim who brought you up"** — Hebrew זֶה אֱלֹהֶיךָ אֲשֶׁר הֶעֶלְךָ, with a singular pronoun and verb; Exod 32:4, 8 has the plural, "These are your gods … who brought you up". **"provocations" → "acts of contempt"** (v18, 26) — Hebrew נֶאָצוֹת, plural noun of נִאֵץ "spurn"; "acts of" is supplied.
- **"manifold mercies" → "great compassion"** (v19, 27, 28, 31) — Hebrew רַחֲמִים "compassion" (from רֶחֶם "womb"). **"departed not from them" → "did not turn aside from over them"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא־סָר מֵעֲלֵיהֶם. The Hebrew marks "the pillar of cloud" and "the pillar of fire" with אֶת and has no second "not": "and the pillar of fire by night".
- **"to instruct them" → "to make them wise"** (v20) — Hebrew לְהַשְׂכִּילָם, hifil of שָׂכַל "have insight". **"withheldest not thy manna" → "your manna you did not hold back"** — Hebrew מָנַע; "manna" is the English name of מָן *man*.
- **"didst thou sustain them" kept** (v21) — Hebrew כִּלְכֵּל. **"so that they lacked nothing" → "they did not lack"** — Hebrew לֹא חָסֵרוּ, without an object. **"clothes" → "cloaks"** — Hebrew שַׂלְמֹתֵיהֶם, as Deut 8:4; 29:5.
- **"didst divide them into corners" → "divided them out to the edge"** (v22) — Hebrew וַתַּחְלְקֵם לְפֵאָה, literally "you divided them to a side / edge". The sense is uncertain: the kingdoms divided out to Israel to the last corner, or the peoples divided away; the Hebrew is kept. **"the land of Sihon, and the land of the king of Heshbon"** — the Hebrew repeats "and the land of"; Sihon was king of Heshbon (Deut 2:30), so one land may be meant. **"king of Bashan" → "king of the Bashan"** — the Hebrew has the article, as Deut 3:1.
- **"children" → "sons"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew בְּנֵיהֶם; הַבָּנִים.
- **"subduedst" → "brought low"** (v24) — Hebrew הִכְנִיעַ. **"as they would" → "according to their pleasure"** (v24, 37) — Hebrew כִּרְצוֹנָם.
- **"strong cities" → "fortified cities"; "a fat land" → "a fat ground"** (v25) — Hebrew עָרִים בְּצֻרוֹת; אֲדָמָה. **"wells digged" → "hewn pits"; "oliveyards" → "olive trees"; "fruit trees" → "trees for food"** — Hebrew בֹּרוֹת חֲצוּבִים כְּרָמִים וְזֵיתִים, as Deut 6:11; עֵץ מַאֲכָל, literally "tree of food". **"were filled" → "were sated"** — Hebrew שָׂבַע.
- **"Nevertheless they were disobedient, and rebelled" → "And they were rebellious and rebelled"** (v26) — two verbs: וַיַּמְרוּ (מָרָה "rebel against") and וַיִּמְרְדוּ (מָרַד "rebel"); English has one word. **"cast thy law behind their backs" → "threw your torah behind their back"** — Hebrew גַּו "back", singular. **"which testified against them" → "who warned them"** (v26, 29, 30, 34) — Hebrew הֵעִיד בְּ, literally "testify against": a formal warning before witnesses.
- **"enemies, who vexed them" → "adversaries, and they distressed them"** (v27) — Hebrew צָרֵיהֶם וַיָּצֵרוּ לָהֶם … צָרָתָם: "adversaries … distressed … distress" are one root. **"they cried … thou heardest … thou gavest"** → **"they would cry out … you would hear … you would give"** (v27, 28) — the Hebrew uses the imperfect (יִצְעֲקוּ, תִּשְׁמָע, תִּתֵּן): what happened again and again. **"saviours"** — Hebrew מוֹשִׁיעִים, as Judges; not "deliverers".
- **"they did evil again" → "they would turn back to do evil"** (v28) — Hebrew יָשׁוּבוּ לַעֲשׂוֹת רַע, literally "they would return to do evil": they did it again. **"leftest thou them" → "you would leave them"** — Hebrew וַתַּעַזְבֵם, עָזַב "forsake, leave". **"so that they had the dominion over them" → "and they had dominion over them"** — Hebrew וַיִּרְדּוּ, of רָדָה. **"deliver"** — Hebrew הִצִּיל.
- **"testifiedst against them" → "warned them"** (v29). **"(which if a man do, he shall live in them)" → "which a man shall do and live by them"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־יַעֲשֶׂה אָדָם וְחָיָה בָהֶם, the words of Lev 18:5, no parenthesis. **"withdrew the shoulder" → "gave a stubborn shoulder"** — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנוּ כָתֵף סוֹרֶרֶת, literally "gave", as Zech 7:11 has the same picture. **"would not hear" → "did not hear"** — Hebrew וְלֹא שָׁמֵעוּ.
- **"many years didst thou forbear them" → "you drew out many years over them"** (v30) — Hebrew וַתִּמְשֹׁךְ עֲלֵיהֶם שָׁנִים רַבּוֹת, literally "you drew out (pulled long) over them many years": you bore with them. **"by thy spirit in thy prophets" → "by your spirit by the hand of your prophets"** — Hebrew בְּרוּחֲךָ בְּיַד־נְבִיאֶיךָ. **"people of the lands" → "peoples of the lands"** — Hebrew עַמֵּי הָאֲרָצֹת.
- **"thou didst not utterly consume them" → "you did not make a full end of them"** (v31) — Hebrew לֹא־עֲשִׂיתָם כָּלָה, "you did not make them a complete end". **"a gracious and merciful God" → "an El gracious and compassionate"** — Hebrew אֵל־חַנּוּן וְרַחוּם, the order of Exod 34:6 ("El compassionate and gracious").
- **"the great, the mighty, and the terrible God" → "the great El, the mighty and the fearsome"** (v32) — Hebrew הָאֵל הַגָּדוֹל הַגִּבּוֹר וְהַנּוֹרָא, the words of Deut 10:17. **"who keepest covenant and mercy" → "keeping the covenant and the chesed"** — Hebrew שׁוֹמֵר הַבְּרִית וְהַחֶסֶד, as Deut 7:9. **"let not all the trouble seem little" → "let not all the hardship be little"** — Hebrew תְּלָאָה "hardship" (literally "weariness"); אַל־יִמְעַט "let it not be little". **"that hath come upon us" → "that has found us"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־מְצָאַתְנוּ; the list after it is joined by לְ ("for our kings …"). **"since the time of" → "from the days of"; "unto this day" → "to this day"**.
- **"princes" kept** (v32, 34, 38) — Hebrew שָׂרִים (the earlier "officials" is withdrawn: שַׂר is "prince").
- **"thou hast done right" → "you have done truth"** (v33) — Hebrew אֱמֶת עָשִׂיתָ: you have acted faithfully. **"we have done wickedly"** — Hebrew הִרְשָׁעְנוּ.
- **"kept thy law" → "have not done your torah"; "hearkened unto" → "given heed to"; "testimonies" kept** (v34) — Hebrew לֹא עָשׂוּ תּוֹרָתֶךָ; הִקְשִׁיבוּ; עֵדְוֹתֶיךָ "your testimonies", with which you warned them (הַעִידֹתָ, one root). The kings stand with אֵת, the object marker, at the head of the sentence.
- **"the large and fat land which thou gavest before them" → "the broad and fat land that you set before them"** (v35) — Hebrew נָתַתָּ לִפְנֵיהֶם, literally "gave before them" (as Deut 1:8). **"turned they from" → "did not turn back from"** — Hebrew שָׁב מִן.
- **"servants" → "slaves"** (v36) — Hebrew עֲבָדִים: bondage under foreign kings is meant (the word is also "servants"). **"the good thereof" → "its good"; "in it" → "upon it"** — Hebrew טוּבָהּ; עָלֶיהָ.
- **"it yieldeth much increase unto the kings" → "its yield multiplies for the kings"** (v37) — Hebrew וּתְבוּאָתָהּ מַרְבָּה, the hifil participle of רָבָה "be many". **"set over us because of our sins" → "set over us in our sins"** — Hebrew נָתַתָּה עָלֵינוּ בְּחַטֹּאותֵינוּ: "set" is literally "given"; בְּ "in", also "because of". **"bodies"** — Hebrew גְּוִיֹּת. **"cattle" → "beasts"** — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה.
- **"because of all this we make a sure covenant, and write it" → "in all this we are cutting a firm agreement and writing it"** (v38) — Hebrew וּבְכָל־זֹאת "and in (with) all this"; כֹּרְתִים אֲמָנָה, participles: "cutting" as of a covenant, but the noun is אֲמָנָה "firm agreement" (root of אָמֵן "be firm"), not בְּרִית "covenant". "it" is supplied. **"our princes, Levites, and priests, seal unto it" → "on the sealed document are our princes, our Levites, our priests"** — Hebrew וְעַל הֶחָתוּם, "and on the sealed (one)": "document" supplied. The names stand on the sealed writing (10:1 continues "And on the sealed ones").

### Notes

- **Verse numbers** — our 9:38 is 10:1 in the Hebrew Bible; our 10:1–39 are Hebrew 10:2–40.
- **One of the longest prayers in Scripture** — comprehensive national confession.
- **Historical survey** — creation, Abraham, exodus, Sinai, wilderness, conquest, judges, monarchy, exile, present distress.
- **"We are slaves"** (v36) — despite return, still under Persian rule. Honest acknowledgment of incomplete restoration.
- **Structure** — creator (v6) → patriarch (v7-8) → exodus-conquest (v9-25) → apostasy-judges-exile cycle (v26-31) → present petition (v32-37) → agreement (v38).
- **Sealing the agreement** (v38) — chapter 10 enumerates signatories. Response in action to historical reflection.
- **"Do them and live"** (v29) — Lev 18:5 echoed. Chosen not for obedience-as-merit but in contrast with their actual disobedience.
