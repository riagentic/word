# Nehemiah 1

[1] The words of ⟦nehemiah|Nechemyah|Nehemiah⟧ son of ⟦hachaliah|Chachalyah|Hacaliah⟧.

And it came to pass in the month ⟦kislev|Kislev|Chislev⟧, in the twentieth year, and I was in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel,

[2] and ⟦hanani|Chanani|Hanani⟧, one of my brothers, came, he and men from ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧. And I asked them about the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, the escape who were left from the captivity, and about ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[3] And they said to me, "Those left who are left from the captivity there in the province are in great evil and in reproach, and the wall of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ is breached, and its gates have been burned with fire."

[4] And it came to pass, when I heard these words, I sat down and wept and mourned days; and I was fasting and praying before the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the heavens.

[5] And I said, "Ah, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the heavens, the great and fearsome ⟦el|El|God⟧, who keeps the covenant and ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ for those who love him and for those who keep his commandments,

[6] let your ear, please, be attentive and your eyes open, to listen to the prayer of your servant that I am praying before you today, day and night, for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ your servants, and confessing the sins of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ that we have sinned against you: both I and the house of my father have sinned.

[7] We have surely acted corruptly against you, and we have not kept the commandments and the statutes and the ordinances that you commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ your servant.

[8] Remember, please, the word that you commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ your servant, saying, 'You will act unfaithfully; I will scatter you among the peoples.

[9] And you will return to me and keep my commandments and do them: if your driven-away one is at the end of the heavens, from there I will gather them and bring them to the place that I have chosen, to make my name abide there.'

[10] And they are your servants and your people, whom you have ransomed with your great power and with your strong hand.

[11] Ah, ⟦adonai|Adonai|Lord⟧, let your ear, please, be attentive to the prayer of your servant and to the prayer of your servants who delight to fear your name; and prosper your servant, please, today, and give him for compassion before this man."

And I was cupbearer to the king.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5) — Tetragrammaton preserved. **"O Lord" → "Adonai"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God of heaven" → "Elohim of the heavens"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי הַשָּׁמַיִם; שָׁמַיִם is always plural.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Chisleu" → "Kislev"** (v1) — the qere כִּסְלֵיו; the ketiv writes כסלו, the same name. The ninth month (Nov–Dec).
- **"And it came to pass … as I was in Shushan the palace, That Hanani … came" → "And it came to pass … and I was in Shushan the citadel, and Hanani … came"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew וַיְהִי … וַאֲנִי הָיִיתִי … וַיָּבֹא; בִּירָה "citadel".
- **"certain men of Judah" → "men from Judah"** (v2) — Hebrew אֲנָשִׁים, no "certain". **"the Jews that had escaped, which were left of the captivity" → "the Jews, the escape who were left from the captivity"** — Hebrew הַפְּלֵיטָה "the escape": a noun, those who have escaped, standing beside "the Jews"; שֶׁבִי "captivity", a collective ("the captives").
- **"The remnant that are left" → "Those left who are left"** (v3) — Hebrew הַנִּשְׁאָרִים אֲשֶׁר־נִשְׁאֲרוּ, the one verb twice (not שְׁאֵרִית "remnant"). **"in great affliction" → "in great evil"** — Hebrew בְּרָעָה גְדֹלָה: רָעָה "evil", here the evil they suffer (misery, distress). **"broken down" → "breached"** — Hebrew מְפֹרָצֶת, of פָּרַץ "break through, breach". **"are burned" → "have been burned"** — Hebrew נִצְּתוּ, a perfect.
- **"mourned certain days, and fasted, and prayed" → "mourned days; and I was fasting and praying"** (v4) — Hebrew יָמִים "days", no "certain"; וָאֱהִי צָם וּמִתְפַּלֵּל, "and I was" with two participles: a lasting state.
- **"I beseech thee, O LORD" → "Ah, Yahweh"** (v5, 11) — Hebrew אָנָּא, a cry of entreaty, as 2 Kgs 20:3. **"great and terrible God" → "great and fearsome El"** — Hebrew הָאֵל הַגָּדוֹל וְהַנּוֹרָא, as Deut 7:21; נוֹרָא is a participle of יָרֵא "fear". **"that keepeth covenant and mercy for them that love him and observe his commandments" → "who keeps the covenant and steadfast love for those who love him and for those who keep his commandments"** — the words of Deut 7:9, rendered as there: שֹׁמֵר הַבְּרִית וָחֶסֶד; Nehemiah has "for those who keep" with its own לְ, and the one verb שָׁמַר "keep" twice.
- **"Let thine ear now be attentive" → "let your ear, please, be attentive"** (v6, 11) — Hebrew תְּהִי נָא, with נָא. **"that thou mayest hear" → "to listen to"** — Hebrew לִשְׁמֹעַ אֶל. **"which I pray before thee now, day and night" → "that I am praying before you today, day and night"** — Hebrew מִתְפַּלֵּל, a participle; הַיּוֹם "today". **"your servant" / "your servants"** (v6, 8, 10, 11) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word for "slave"; of one who stands before Yahweh, "servant". **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"and confess" → "and confessing"** — Hebrew וּמִתְוַדֶּה, a participle. **"my father's house" → "the house of my father"**.
- **"We have dealt very corruptly against thee" → "We have surely acted corruptly against you"** (v7) — Hebrew חֲבֹל חָבַלְנוּ לָךְ, literally "acting corruptly we have acted corruptly against you": an infinitive absolute with its verb. The root חבל here is "act corruptly, ruin" (not the חָבַל "take in pledge"). **"the judgments" → "the ordinances"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים, here of rulings, as Deut 5:1; 7:11. **"nor … nor" → "and … and"** — the Hebrew joins the three nouns with וְאֶת.
- **"Remember, I beseech thee" → "Remember, please"** (v8) — Hebrew זְכָר־נָא. **"If ye transgress, I will scatter you abroad among the nations" → "You will act unfaithfully; I will scatter you among the peoples"** — Hebrew אַתֶּם תִּמְעָלוּ אֲנִי אָפִיץ אֶתְכֶם בָּעַמִּים, two clauses side by side with "you" and "I" set against each other and no "if": the sense is a condition ("if you act unfaithfully") or a forecast, as Deut 4:25–27; 28:64; 30:1–3 say both. מָעַל "act unfaithfully", a breach of trust; עַמִּים "peoples".
- **"But if ye turn unto me" → "And you will return to me"** (v9) — Hebrew וְשַׁבְתֶּם, a perfect with "and": no "but", no "if"; שׁוּב "return". **"though there were of you cast out unto the uttermost part of the heaven, yet will I gather them from thence" → "if your driven-away one is at the end of the heavens, from there I will gather them"** — the words of Deut 30:4, rendered as there: נִדַּחֲכֶם "your driven-away one", a singular collective noun with "your" plural; בִּקְצֵה הַשָּׁמַיִם "at the end of the heavens"; then "them". **"will bring them"** — the qere וַהֲבִיאוֹתִים; the ketiv והבואתים is another spelling of the same word. **"to set my name there" → "to make my name abide there"** — Hebrew לְשַׁכֵּן אֶת־שְׁמִי שָׁם, the piel of שָׁכַן "abide", as Deut 12:11.
- **"Now these are" → "And they are"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהֵם. **"redeemed" → "ransomed"** — Hebrew פָּדָה "ransom" (גָּאַל is "redeem"). **"by thy great power, and by thy strong hand" → "with your great power and with your strong hand"** — Hebrew בְּכֹחֲךָ הַגָּדוֹל (the "great power" of Deut 9:29) וּבְיָדְךָ הַחֲזָקָה, the "strong hand" of Exodus and Deuteronomy, with one preposition "with".
- **"who desire to fear thy name" → "who delight to fear your name"** (v11) — Hebrew הַחֲפֵצִים לְיִרְאָה, חָפֵץ "delight in". **"prosper, I pray thee, thy servant this day" → "prosper your servant, please, today"** — Hebrew הַצְלִיחָה־נָּא לְעַבְדְּךָ הַיּוֹם, literally "make prosper, please, for your servant". **"grant him mercy in the sight of this man" → "give him for compassion before this man"** — Hebrew וּתְנֵהוּ לְרַחֲמִים לִפְנֵי, as 1 Kgs 8:50.
- **"For I was the king's cupbearer" → "And I was cupbearer to the king"** (v11) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי הָיִיתִי מַשְׁקֶה לַמֶּלֶךְ: "and", not "for"; מַשְׁקֶה, literally "one who gives drink".

### Notes
- **Nehemiah's memoirs** — book written largely in first person. Memoir genre; authentic Persian-era setting.
- **Kislev 20th year of Artaxerxes I** — ca. 445 BC, roughly 13 years after Ezra's mission (458 BC). The verse names no king; 2:1 does.
- **Susa** — Persian winter capital; cold-season residence of the king.
- **Hanani** = Nehemiah's brother (7:2). Travels between Jerusalem and Susa.
- **Wall breached, gates burned** — possibly *recent* damage, not 586 BC (distant past); perhaps the Artaxerxes-era setback of Ezra 4:23. The text does not say when.
- **Cupbearer** (v11) — high-status role; intimate access to king. Position of trust.
- **Prayer echoes Deuteronomy** — v8–9 join the scattering of Deut 4:27; 28:64 with the gathering of Deut 30:1–4 and the place of Deut 12:5, 11.
- **Covenant-*chesed* linkage** — the Deut 7:9 formula (also 1 Kgs 8:23; Dan 9:4).
