# Nehemiah 8

[1] All the people gathered together as one man in the square before the Water Gate; they told Ezra the scribe to bring the Scroll of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[2] On the first day of the seventh month, Ezra the priest brought the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ before the assembly, both men and women and all who could understand with attentiveness.

[3] He read from it in the square before the Water Gate, from morning until midday, before the men, the women, and those who could understand; the ears of all the people were attentive to the Scroll of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧.

[4] Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden platform that had been made for the purpose; beside him stood ⟦mattithiah|Mattityah|Mattithiah⟧, Shema, ⟦anaiah|Anayah|Anaiah⟧, ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧, ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧, and ⟦maaseiah|Maaseyah|Maaseiah⟧ on his right hand; and on his left, ⟦pedaiah|Pedayah|Pedaiah⟧, Mishael, ⟦malchijah|Malkiyah|Malchijah⟧, ⟦hashum|Chashum|Hashum⟧, ⟦hashbaddanah|Chashbaddanah|Hashbaddanah⟧, ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧, and Meshullam.

[5] Ezra opened the scroll in the sight of all the people (for he was above all the people). When he opened it, all the people stood up.

[6] Ezra blessed ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the great ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. All the people answered, "Amen! Amen!" with lifting up of their hands; they bowed down, and prostrated themselves before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ with their faces to the ground.

[8] They read from the scroll, from the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, with interpretation; they gave the sense, so that the people understood the reading.

[10] Then he said to them, "Go your way, eat the fat, drink the sweet, and send portions to those for whom nothing is prepared, for this day is holy to our Lord. Do not be grieved, for the joy of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is your strength."

[11] So the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ calmed all the people, saying, "Be still, for the day is holy; do not be grieved."

[12] All the people went their way to eat, drink, send portions, and make great rejoicing, because they had understood the words that had been declared to them.

[13] On the second day the heads of fathers' houses of all the people, the priests, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ gathered to Ezra the scribe to give attention to the words of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧.

[16] So the people went out, brought them, and made themselves booths — each on the roof of his house, in their courts, in the courts of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in the square of the Water Gate, and in the square of the Gate of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧.

[17] All the assembly of those who had returned from the captivity made booths and dwelt in them; for from the days of ⟦joshua|Yeshua|Joshua⟧ son of Nun until that day the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had not done so. There was very great gladness.

[18] Day by day, from the first day to the last day, he read from the Scroll of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. They kept the feast seven days; on the eighth day was a solemn assembly, according to the ordinance.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"pulpit" → "platform"** (v4) — *migdal-etz*.
- **"Jeshua the son of Nun" → "Joshua son of Nun"** (v17) — Joshua; both names same Hebrew.
- **"pine branches" → "branches of wild olive"** (v15) — Hebrew *etz shemen*; wild olive / oleaster, not pine. MT reads "olive branches and wild-olive branches."
- **"distinctly... gave the sense" → "with interpretation... gave the sense"** (v8) — *meforash*; traditionally read as hint of early Aramaic translation practice (targum).
- **"our Lord" — retained** (v10) — *adonenu* (אֲדֹנֵינוּ), "our Lord"; not the divine title *Adonai*.

### Notes
- **First day of seventh month** — Feast of Trumpets / Rosh Hashanah (Lev 23:24). Public Torah-reading unique development.
- **Square before Water Gate** — open plaza, inclusive of men, women, and children-old-enough.
- **Ezra on a platform** — visible, ceremonial. Reading from morning to noon (~6 hours).
- **Levites interpreting** (v7-8) — "with interpretation"; rabbinic tradition sees origin of Targum (Aramaic translation) and rabbinic exposition here.
- **"Joy of Yahweh is your strength"** (v10) — proverbial line. Festivals as strengthening, not mournful.
- **"Fat... sweet... portions"** — inclusive feast: share with those who have nothing prepared.
- **Feast of Booths** (v14) — Sukkot; not observed in this form since Joshua's day. Emphasizes return-from-wandering theme.
- **Daily Torah-reading** during the feast — institutional innovation of public literacy in scripture.
