## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. Many verses set the object first ("And the Fish Gate the sons of Hassenaah built"); the order is kept.
- **"repaired"** (v4–32) — Hebrew הֶחֱזִיק, the hifil of חָזַק "be strong", literally "made strong": of a wall or gate, "repair". One rendering through the chapter; v19 has the piel וַיְחַזֵּק, the same sense (as 2 Kgs 12:5–12). בָּנָה "build" (v1–3, 13–15) is another verb.
- **"next unto him / them" → "next to him / them"** (v2–12, 17, 19) — Hebrew עַל־יָדוֹ / עַל־יָדָם, literally "at his / their hand"; אַחֲרָיו "after him" (v16–31) is another word. In v2 the Hebrew has "next to him" both times.
- **"the high priest" → "the great priest"** (v1, 20) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל, as Num 35:25. **"rose up with his brethren" → "arose, and his brothers"** — Hebrew וַיָּקָם … וְאֶחָיו.
- **"the sheep gate" → "the Flock Gate"** (v1, 32) — Hebrew שַׁעַר הַצֹּאן: צֹאן is the flock, sheep and goats together.
- **"set up the doors" (v1, 3, 6, 13–15)** — Hebrew הֶעֱמִיד, literally "made stand". **"even unto the tower of Meah … unto the tower of Hananeel" → "and as far as the Tower of the Hundred … as far as the Tower of Chananel"** (v1) — Hebrew מִגְדַּל הַמֵּאָה, "the hundred" with the article; what the hundred were (cubits, men) is not said.
- **"who also laid the beams thereof" → "they laid its beams"** (v3, 6) — Hebrew קֵרוּהוּ, the piel of קָרָה, as 2:8. **"the locks thereof, and the bars thereof"** — Hebrew מַנְעוּל "lock" (from נָעַל "lock"); בְּרִיחַ "bar".
- **"son of Koz" → "son of HaQotz"** (v4, 21) — Hebrew הַקּוֹץ, with the article.
- **"their nobles put not their necks to the work of their Lord" → "their mighty ones did not bring their neck into the service of their lords"** (v5) — Hebrew אַדִּירֵיהֶם, אַדִּיר "mighty, majestic" (not חֹרִים "nobles", 2:16); לֹא־הֵבִיאוּ צַוָּרָם, "did not bring their neck" (singular), as an ox to the yoke; עֲבֹדָה "service"; אֲדֹנֵיהֶם is plural: "their lords" (the overseers, or Nehemiah), or a plural of majesty for "their Lord"; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"the old gate" → "the Gate of the Old"** (v6) — Hebrew שַׁעַר הַיְשָׁנָה: הַיְשָׁנָה is the feminine adjective "the old" with the article; what is old (the city, the wall) is not said. It is also read as a name, "the Jeshanah Gate" (a town Jeshanah, 2 Chr 13:19); the article favours the adjective. **"Jehoiada" → "Joiada"** — Hebrew יוֹיָדָע.
- **"unto the throne of the governor on this side the river" → "for the seat of the governor across the River"** (v7) — Hebrew לְכִסֵּא פַּחַת עֵבֶר הַנָּהָר: כִּסֵּא "throne / seat"; the sense of לְ here is uncertain: the men belonged to the governor's jurisdiction, or they repaired as far as his residence. "the Mizpah" — the Hebrew has the article (v7, 15, 19).
- **"of the goldsmiths" → "goldsmiths"** (v8) — Hebrew צוֹרְפִים, a plural in apposition: one of the guild. **"the son of one of the apothecaries" → "a son of the perfumers"** — Hebrew בֶּן־הָרַקָּחִים, a member of the perfumers' guild (as 31 "a son of the goldsmith"). **"they fortified Jerusalem" → "they left Jerusalem"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַזְבוּ, עָזַב "leave, forsake": the sense here is uncertain. It may say that they left that part as it stood (the Broad Wall needed no work), or a second root עזב "restore, pave" may be meant (KJV "fortified"); the plain word is kept.
- **"the ruler of the half part of Jerusalem" → "chief of half the district of Jerusalem"** (v9, 12, 14–18) — Hebrew שַׂר חֲצִי פֶּלֶךְ: שַׂר "prince, chief" (of a district "chief": the one word); פֶּלֶךְ "district", a loanword (another פֶּלֶךְ is "spindle", 2 Sam 3:29). "Part" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"even over against his house" → "and opposite his house"** (v10) — Hebrew וְנֶגֶד בֵּיתוֹ, with "and".
- **"repaired the other piece" → "a second measure … repaired"** (v11, 19–21, 24, 27, 30) — Hebrew מִדָּה שֵׁנִית, literally "a second measure": another stretch measured out. **"the tower of the furnaces" → "the Tower of the Ovens"** — Hebrew תַּנּוּר "oven".
- **"he and his daughters"** (v12) — Hebrew הוּא וּבְנוֹתָיו. בָּנוֹת of a town also means its "daughters" = dependent villages (Num 21:25); here it may say that, but the plain word is kept.
- **"the inhabitants of Zanoah" → "the dwellers of Zanoah"** (v13) — Hebrew יֹשְׁבֵי, יָשַׁב "dwell". **"a thousand cubits on the wall unto the dung gate" → "a thousand cubits in the wall, as far as the Gate of the Ash Heaps"** — Hebrew בַּחוֹמָה; שַׁעַר הָשְׁפוֹת, a short spelling of שַׁעַר הָאַשְׁפֹּת (v14; 2:13): אַשְׁפֹּת "ash heap, refuse heap", traditionally "Dung Gate".
- **"he built it, and set up" (v14, 15)** — Hebrew יִבְנֶנּוּ וְיַעֲמִיד, imperfects: the work described as going on ("he was building it"). **"the ruler of part of Bethhaccerem" → "chief of the district of Beth-haccherem"** — no "part" in v14, 15.
- **"the gate of the fountain" → "the Spring Gate"** (v15) — Hebrew שַׁעַר הָעַיִן, עַיִן "spring", as 2:14. **"covered it" → "roofed it"** — Hebrew וִיטַלְלֶנּוּ, found only here, from טֵל "shade"(?): "roof, cover over" (uncertain). **"and set up"** — the qere וְיַעֲמִיד "and he set up"; the ketiv ויעמידו has "and they set up". **"the pool of Siloah" → "the Pool of the Shelach"** — Hebrew בְּרֵכַת הַשֶּׁלַח, with the article; שֶׁלַח "sending" (a conduit), the Siloam of John 9:7. **"by the king's garden" → "at the King's Garden"** — Hebrew לְגַן־הַמֶּלֶךְ. **"the stairs" → "the steps"** — Hebrew הַמַּעֲלוֹת.
- **"unto the place over against the sepulchres of David" → "as far as opposite the graves of David"** (v16) — Hebrew עַד־נֶגֶד קִבְרֵי דָוִיד; "the place" is not in the Hebrew (also v26). **"the house of the mighty" → "the House of the Mighty Ones"** — Hebrew בֵּית הַגִּבֹּרִים.
- **"in his part" → "for his district"** (v17) — Hebrew לְפִלְכּוֹ.
- **"Bavai" → "Bavvai"** (v18) — the Masoretic Text has בַּוַּי. Some Hebrew manuscripts and the Greek read "Binnui", as v24 (cited from memory); the Masoretic name is kept.
- **"over against the going up to the armoury at the turning of the wall" → "from opposite the going up to the armory, the Angle"** (v19) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶד עֲלֹת הַנֶּשֶׁק הַמִּקְצֹעַ: נֶשֶׁק "weapons"; מִקְצוֹעַ "angle" (a turn or buttress of the wall; פִּנָּה is "corner", v24, 31, 32), set beside "the armory" without a preposition. **"the ruler of Mizpah" → "chief of the Mizpah"** — Hebrew שַׂר הַמִּצְפָּה, here with no "district".
- **"Baruch the son of Zabbai earnestly repaired" → "Baruch son of Zakkai zealously repaired"** (v20) — the qere זַכַּי "Zakkai"; the ketiv writes זבי "Zabbai". Hebrew הֶחֱרָה הֶחֱזִיק, literally "he burned, he repaired": חָרָה "burn" (of anger), here of zeal. Some manuscripts read הֶהָרָה "toward the mountain" (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. **"the door" → "the entrance"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew פֶּתַח.
- **"the men of the plain" → "the men of the Circle"** (v22) — Hebrew הַכִּכָּר, "the round", the district of the lower Jordan (Gen 13:10), or the region around Jerusalem; which is meant is not said.
- **"by his house" → "beside his house"** (v23) — Hebrew אֵצֶל.
- **"unto the turning of the wall, even unto the corner" → "as far as the Angle and as far as the corner"** (v24) — Hebrew עַד־הַמִּקְצוֹעַ וְעַד־הַפִּנָּה.
- **"Palal … over against the turning of the wall, and the tower which lieth out from the king's high house, that was by the court of the prison" → "Palal son of Uzai, from opposite the Angle and the tower that goes out from the house of the king, the upper one, which belongs to the court of the guard"** (v25) — the Hebrew has no verb in the verse ("repaired" is understood); מִנֶּגֶד "from opposite"; הַיּוֹצֵא "that goes out" (juts out, v25–27); הָעֶלְיוֹן "the upper" may belong to "the house" or to "the tower": the order is kept; חֲצַר הַמַּטָּרָה "the court of the guard" (Jer 32:2). "After him Pedaiah son of Parosh" also has no verb.
- **"Moreover the Nethinims dwelt in Ophel" → "And the Netinim were dwelling in the Ophel"** (v26) — Hebrew הָיוּ יֹשְׁבִים, "were" with a participle; הָעֹפֶל with the article ("the swelling, the mound"; also v27). **"toward the east"** (v26, 29) — Hebrew לַמִּזְרָח / שַׁעַר הַמִּזְרָח, מִזְרָח literally "the rising (of the sun)".
- **"From above the horse gate" → "Above the Horse Gate"** (v28) — Hebrew מֵעַל. **"every one over against his house" → "each man opposite his house"** — Hebrew אִישׁ לְנֶגֶד בֵּיתוֹ.
- **"Hanun the sixth son of Zalaph" → "Hanun son of Zalaph, the sixth"** (v30) — Hebrew חָנוּן בֶּן־צָלָף הַשִּׁשִּׁי, "the sixth" after the name: the sixth son, or the sixth of a line of workers; the Hebrew does not say. **"After him"** (v30, 31) — the qere אַחֲרָיו; the ketiv writes אחרי, which reads "after me" or is a short spelling. **"his chamber"** — Hebrew נִשְׁכָּה, another form of לִשְׁכָּה.
- **"Malchiah the goldsmith's son" → "Malchijah, a son of the goldsmith"** (v31) — Hebrew בֶּן־הַצֹּרְפִי, a member of the goldsmiths' guild, as v8. **"unto the place of the Nethinims, and of the merchants" → "as far as the house of the Netinim and of the traders"** — Hebrew עַד־בֵּית; רֹכְלִים "traders" (v31, 32). **"the gate Miphkad" → "the Muster Gate"** — Hebrew שַׁעַר הַמִּפְקָד, of פָּקַד "muster, appoint". **"the going up of the corner" → "the upper room of the corner"** (v31, 32) — Hebrew עֲלִיַּת הַפִּנָּה, עֲלִיָּה "upper room".

### Notes
- **Wall-rebuilders catalogued** — ~40 distinct work teams. Counter-clockwise tour from the Flock Gate (N) around to the same gate.
- **Eliashib the great priest** — leads with his priests; later compromised (13:4).
- **Shallum's daughters** (v12) — rare positive mention of women working on the wall (if "daughters" is not "villages").
- **Tekoite mighty ones refuse** (v5) — shame recorded permanently in text.
- **Goldsmiths and perfumers** — craftsmen working as masons; temporary mobilization.
- **District chiefs** — Jerusalem divided into districts; each governor contributes. Also Keilah, Mizpah, Beth-haccherem, Beth-zur — wider Judean involvement.
- **Broad Wall** — excavated in modern Jewish Quarter; 7-meter thick stretch from Hezekiah's era re-used.
- **Ophel** — slope between temple and city of David.
- **Horse Gate** — eastern wall.
- **Chapter numbering** — the Hebrew chapter 3 runs on six more verses: Heb 3:33–38 = our 4:1–6 (see ch. 4).
