## Changes

- **"Meah" → "the Hundred"** (v1) — *Me'ah*; probably "Tower of the Hundred (cubits)."
- **"old gate" → "Jeshanah Gate"** (v6) — possibly "Old Gate" or proper-name "Jeshanah Gate"; debated.
- **"apothecaries" → "perfumers"** (v8) — *raqqachim*.
- **"throne of the governor" → "under the authority of the governor"** (v7) — *kisse pachat*; "jurisdiction of."
- **"pool of Siloah" → "Pool of Shelah"** (v15) — *berekhat ha-shelach*; Siloam.
- **"place over against" — throughout** → "opposite / as far as" for clarity.
- **"Miphkad" → "Muster"** (v31) — *sha'ar ha-miphqad*; gate of the muster/inspection.

### Notes
- **Wall-rebuilders catalogued** — ~40 distinct work teams. Clockwise tour from Sheep Gate (N) around to the same gate.
- **Eliashib the high priest** — leads with his priests; later compromised (13:4).
- **Shallum's daughters** (v12) — rare positive mention of women working on the wall.
- **Tekoite nobles refuse** (v5) — shame recorded permanently in text.
- **Goldsmiths and perfumers** — craftsmen working as masons; temporary mobilization.
- **District rulers** — Jerusalem divided into districts; each governor contributes. Also Keilah, Mizpah, Beth-haccerem, Beth-zur — wider Judean involvement.
- **Broad Wall** — excavated in modern Jewish Quarter; 7-meter thick stretch from Hezekiah's era re-used.
- **Ophel** — slope between temple and city of David.
- **Horse Gate** — eastern wall.
