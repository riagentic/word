# Nehemiah 6

[1] When Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and the rest of our enemies heard that I had rebuilt the wall and that there was no breach left in it (though at that time I had not yet set up the doors in the gates),

[2] Sanballat and Geshem sent to me, saying, "Come, let us meet together in one of the villages in the plain of Ono." But they were planning to do me harm.

[3] I sent messengers to them, saying, "I am doing a great work and cannot come down. Why should the work stop while I leave it and come down to you?"

[4] They sent to me four times in this manner, and I answered them the same way.

[5] Then Sanballat sent his servant to me in the same manner a fifth time, with an open letter in his hand.

[6] It was written: "It is reported among the nations — and Gashmu says it — that you and the Jews intend to rebel; that is why you are rebuilding the wall; and you are to be their king, according to these reports.

[7] You have also set up prophets to proclaim concerning you in Jerusalem, saying, 'There is a king in Judah!' Now this will be reported to the king according to these words. Therefore come and let us take counsel together."

[8] Then I sent to him, saying, "No such things as you say are being done; you are inventing them out of your own heart."

[9] For they were all trying to make us afraid, saying, "Their hands will be weakened from the work, and it will not be done." But now, O *Elohim*, strengthen my hands.

[10] I went to the house of Shemaiah son of Delaiah son of Mehetabel, who was confined there; he said, "Let us meet together in the house of *Elohim*, within the temple; let us close the temple doors, for they will come to kill you — by night they will come to kill you."

[11] But I said, "Should a man like me flee? Who that is as I am would go into the temple to save his life? I will not go in."

[12] I recognized that *Elohim* had not sent him, but that he had uttered this prophecy against me because Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.

[13] He was hired for this purpose — to intimidate me so that I would act in this way and sin, and so they could give me a bad name to discredit me.

[14] My *Elohim*, remember Tobiah and Sanballat according to these deeds of theirs, and also Noadiah the prophetess and the rest of the prophets who were trying to make me afraid.

[15] So the wall was finished on the twenty-fifth day of the month Elul, in fifty-two days.

[16] When all our enemies heard, and all the nations around us saw these things, they fell greatly in their own esteem, for they perceived that this work had been done by our *Elohim*.

[17] Moreover, in those days the nobles of Judah were sending many letters to Tobiah, and letters from Tobiah were coming to them.

[18] For many in Judah were bound by oath to him, because he was the son-in-law of Shecaniah son of Arah, and his son Jehohanan had married the daughter of Meshullam son of Berechiah.

[19] They also kept speaking of his good deeds in my presence and reporting my words to him. Tobiah sent letters to intimidate me.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v6, 16).
- **"temple" → "house of *Elohim* / temple"** (v10) — *heikhal*.
- **"Gashmu saith it" — retained** (v6) — dialectal form of Geshem (Arabian-style ending); preserves the rhetorical naming.
- **"mischief" → "harm"** (v2).
- **"feignest them out of thine own heart" → "inventing them out of your own heart"** (v8).

### Notes
- **Four diplomatic traps** then a fifth open-letter threat. Psychological warfare.
- **"Open letter"** (v5) — deliberately unsealed for maximum embarrassment; gossip-circulation strategy.
- **Charge of rebellion** — standard accusation; echoes Ezra 4:12-16. Fear of Persian-king reprisal.
- **Shemaiah the false prophet** (v10) — hired pseudo-prophet tries to lure Nehemiah into temple sanctuary — a layman entering sanctuary = grave violation (Num 18:7). Would have discredited him.
- **"Noadiah the prophetess"** (v14) — one of the few female prophets named in Second Temple; rare negative association for such.
- **52 days** (v15) — rapid completion; Sept-Oct 445 BC. Historically plausible for rebuilding portions already partly standing.
- **Tobiah's inside access** (v17-19) — intermarried into priestly/elite Jerusalem families; information flow works both ways. Persistent opposition from within.
