# Nehemiah 6

[1] And it came to pass, when it was heard by ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ and ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ and by Geshem the ⟦arabs|Arvi|Arab⟧ and by the rest of our enemies that I had built the wall and there remained no breach in it — also until that time I had not set up doors in the gates —

[2] that ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ and Geshem sent to me, saying, "Come, and let us meet together in the villages in the plain of Ono." And they were planning to do me evil.

[3] And I sent messengers to them, saying, "A great work I am doing, and I am not able to come down. Why should the work cease while I let it go and come down to you?"

[4] And they sent to me according to this word four times, and I replied to them according to this word.

[5] And ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ sent to me according to this word a fifth time his young man, and an open letter in his hand.

[6] Written in it: "Among the nations it is heard, and Gashmu is saying, that you and the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ are planning to rebel; therefore you are building the wall; and you are becoming their king, according to these words.

[7] And also prophets you have set up to proclaim concerning you in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, saying, 'There is a king in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧!' And now it will be heard by the king according to these words. And now come, and let us take counsel together."

[8] And I sent to him, saying, "There has not been anything according to these words that you are saying, for out of your heart you are inventing them."

[9] For all of them were making us afraid, saying, "Their hands will let go of the work, and it will not be done." And now, make strong my hands.

[10] And I came to the house of ⟦shemaiah|Shemayah|Shemaiah⟧ son of ⟦delaiah|Delayah|Delaiah⟧ son of ⟦mehetabel|Mehetavel|Mehetabel⟧, and he was shut in. And he said, "Let us meet at the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, inside the temple, and let us shut the doors of the temple, for they are coming to kill you, and by night they are coming to kill you."

[11] And I said, "Should a man like me flee? And who like me would go into the temple and live? I will not go in."

[12] And I recognized, and behold, it was not ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who had sent him, for the prophecy he spoke against me, and ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ and ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ had hired him.

[13] For this he was hired: so that I would be afraid and do so and sin, and it would be for them an evil name, so that they could reproach me.

[14] Remember, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ and ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ according to these deeds of his, and also ⟦noadiah-prophetess|Noadyah|Noadiah⟧ the prophetess and the rest of the prophets who were making me afraid.

[15] And the wall was completed on the twenty-fifth of Elul, in fifty-two days.

[16] And it came to pass, when all our enemies heard, that all the nations that were around us were afraid, and they fell greatly in their own eyes; and they knew that it was from our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that this work had been done.

[17] Also in those days the nobles of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ were multiplying their letters going to ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧, and those of ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ were coming to them.

[18] For many in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ were sworn to him, for he was son-in-law of ⟦shecaniah|Shechanyah|Shecaniah⟧ son of ⟦arah|Arach|Arah⟧, and ⟦jehohanan|Yehochanan|Jehohanan⟧ his son had taken the daughter of Meshullam son of ⟦berechiah|Berechyah|Berechiah⟧.

[19] Also his good things they were saying before me, and my words they were bringing out to him. Letters ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ sent to make me afraid.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 12, 14, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v10 with the article (בֵּית הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"it came to pass"** (v1, 16) — Hebrew וַיְהִי, kept.
- **"when … heard" → "when it was heard by"** (v1) — Hebrew נִשְׁמַע לְ, the nifal, "it was heard to". **"builded" → "built"** — Hebrew בָּנִיתִי, "built" (not "rebuilt"; also v6). **"there was no breach left therein" → "there remained no breach in it"** — Hebrew נוֹתַר. **"(though at that time I had not set up the doors upon the gates)" → "— also until that time I had not set up doors in the gates —"** — Hebrew גַּם עַד־הָעֵת הַהִיא, "also until that time"; דְּלָתוֹת without the article. Verse 1 opens the sentence; verse 2 ends it.
- **"in some one of the villages" → "in the villages"** (v2) — Hebrew בַּכְּפִירִים, with the article, "in the villages"; "one of" is not written. The word may also be a place name, Kephirim ("Hakkephirim"), in the plain of Ono. **"they thought to do me mischief" → "they were planning to do me evil"** — Hebrew חֹשְׁבִים, a participle (also v6), and רָעָה "evil".
- **"I am doing a great work" → "A great work I am doing"** (v3) — the Hebrew puts the work first. **"I cannot come down" → "I am not able to come down"** — Hebrew לֹא אוּכַל. **"why should the work cease" kept** — Hebrew תִשְׁבַּת, שָׁבַת "cease". **"whilst I leave it" → "while I let it go"** — Hebrew אַרְפֶּהָ, hifil of רָפָה, "let drop, let go" (the same root as v9).
- **"after this sort" / "in like manner" → "according to this word"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew כַּדָּבָר הַזֶּה (three times); in v6–7 כַּדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה "according to these words" (three times, also v8). **"I answered them" → "I replied to them"** (v4) — Hebrew וָאָשִׁיב אוֹתָם, "I returned them" (a word).
- **"his servant" → "his young man"** (v5) — Hebrew נַעֲרוֹ, נַעַר "boy, young man", of an attendant. **"with an open letter" → "and an open letter"** — Hebrew וְאִגֶּרֶת פְּתוּחָה, "and"; פָּתוּחַ "opened": the letter was not sealed.
- **"It is reported among the heathen" → "Among the nations it is heard"** (v6) — Hebrew בַּגּוֹיִם נִשְׁמָע; the same verb in v7 ("it will be heard by the king"). **"Gashmu saith it" → "Gashmu is saying"** — Hebrew וְגַשְׁמוּ אֹמֵר, a participle; גַּשְׁמוּ is a longer form of the name Geshem (v1, 2). **"that thou mayest be their king" → "you are becoming their king"** — Hebrew הֹוֶה לָהֶם לְמֶלֶךְ, a participle of the late verb הָוָה "become, be".
- **"thou hast also appointed prophets to preach of thee" → "also prophets you have set up to proclaim concerning you"** (v7) — Hebrew הֶעֱמַדְתָּ, "made stand", and לִקְרֹא עָלֶיךָ, "call concerning you". **"There is a king in Judah"** — Hebrew מֶלֶךְ בִּיהוּדָה, "a king in Judah", no verb; "there is" supplied. **"Come now therefore" → "And now come"** — Hebrew וְעַתָּה לְכָה; "let us take counsel together" — Hebrew נִוָּעֲצָה (v2 has נִוָּעֲדָה "let us meet": two verbs that differ by one letter).
- **"There are no such things done" → "There has not been anything according to these words"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא נִהְיָה כַּדְּבָרִים הָאֵלֶּה. **"thou feignest them" → "you are inventing them"** — Hebrew בּוֹדָאם, the verb בָּדָא (only here and 1 Kgs 12:33).
- **"they all made us afraid" → "all of them were making us afraid"** (v9) — Hebrew מְיָרְאִים, a participle of the piel "make afraid" (also v14, 19). **"Their hands shall be weakened from the work" → "Their hands will let go of the work"** — Hebrew יִרְפּוּ יְדֵיהֶם מִן, רָפָה with מִן "let go of" (as v3). **"Now therefore, O God, strengthen my hands" → "And now, make strong my hands"** — Hebrew וְעַתָּה חַזֵּק אֶת־יָדָי: no address stands in the Hebrew ("O God" in KJV and ESV is supplied). The form חַזֵּק is either a command (a prayer without a name) or an infinitive ("and now, to strengthen my hands" = so I strengthened them); the Hebrew does not decide. חִזֵּק piel "make strong".
- **"Afterward I came" → "And I came"** (v10) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי־בָאתִי, "and I, I came". **"who was shut up" → "and he was shut in"** — Hebrew וְהוּא עָצוּר, "shut in, held back": why (in fear, under a vow, by uncleanness, or as a sign) is not said. **"Let us meet together in the house of God, within the temple" → "Let us meet at the house of Elohim, inside the temple"** — Hebrew נִוָּעֵד אֶל־בֵּית הָאֱלֹהִים אֶל־תּוֹךְ הַהֵיכָל; הֵיכָל is the temple building itself, where only priests went in (Num 18:7). **"they will come to slay thee; yea, in the night will they come" → "they are coming to kill you, and by night they are coming to kill you"** — Hebrew בָּאִים לְהָרְגֶךָ, twice; "yea" for וְ.
- **"who is there, that, being as I am, would go into the temple to save his life?" → "And who like me would go into the temple and live?"** (v11) — Hebrew וּמִי כָמוֹנִי אֲשֶׁר־יָבוֹא אֶל־הַהֵיכָל וָחָי, "and live": either "to save his life", or "and live" after a layman's going in (Num 18:7). The Hebrew allows both; the words are kept.
- **"I perceived" → "I recognized"** (v12) — Hebrew וָאַכִּירָה, הִכִּיר. **"that God had not sent him" → "behold, it was not Elohim who had sent him"** — Hebrew וְהִנֵּה לֹא־אֱלֹהִים שְׁלָחוֹ: הִנֵּה "behold", and the negative stands before *Elohim* (not *Elohim* sent him, but others); "it was … who" carries that order. **"he pronounced this prophecy against me" → "the prophecy he spoke against me"** — Hebrew הַנְּבוּאָה דִּבֶּר עָלַי, the object first; עַל "against" or "concerning". **"for Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him"** — Hebrew וְטוֹבִיָּה וְסַנְבַלַּט שְׂכָרוֹ, "and Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him".
- **"Therefore was he hired, that I should be afraid" → "For this he was hired: so that I would be afraid"** (v13) — Hebrew לְמַעַן שָׂכוּר הוּא לְמַעַן־אִירָא, literally "for the sake of — hired he was — for the sake of: I would fear"; the first לְמַעַן has no clause after it. **"that they might have matter for an evil report" → "it would be for them an evil name"** — Hebrew וְהָיָה לָהֶם לְשֵׁם רָע. **"reproach me" kept** — Hebrew יְחָרְפוּנִי, חֵרֵף (the noun חֶרְפָּה "reproach", 5:9).
- **"My God, think thou upon Tobiah" → "Remember, my Elohim, Tobiah …"** (v14) — Hebrew זָכְרָה אֱלֹהַי לְטוֹבִיָּה וּלְסַנְבַלַּט: זָכַר "remember", with לְ before each person ("remember to Tobiah"), as לִּי "for me" in 5:19; whether for good or for evil the word does not say; the sense is given by "according to these deeds of his". **"according to these their works" → "according to these deeds of his"** — Hebrew כְּמַעֲשָׂיו אֵלֶּה, "his" (singular). **"Noadiah the prophetess"** — Hebrew נוֹעַדְיָה הַנְּבִיאָה, a woman (the Septuagint makes the name masculine, from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"So the wall was finished in the twenty and fifth day of the month Elul" → "And the wall was completed on the twenty-fifth of Elul"** (v15) — Hebrew וַתִּשְׁלַם, שָׁלַם "be complete, be whole" (the root of *shalom*); "day of the month" is not written: בְּעֶשְׂרִים וַחֲמִשָּׁה לֶאֱלוּל.
- **"all the heathen … saw these things" → "all the nations … were afraid"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּרְאוּ: the same letters and vowels read as "and they feared" (from יָרֵא, as v9, 13, 14, 19) or "and they saw" (from רָאָה); "these things" is not written. "Were afraid" fits "fell … in their own eyes"; "saw" is the other reading. **"they were much cast down in their own eyes" → "they fell greatly in their own eyes"** — Hebrew וַיִּפְּלוּ מְאֹד בְּעֵינֵיהֶם. **"for they perceived" → "and they knew"** — Hebrew וַיֵּדְעוּ, יָדַע. **"this work was wrought of our God" → "it was from our Elohim that this work had been done"** — Hebrew מֵאֵת אֱלֹהֵינוּ, "from with our Elohim", stands first.
- **"sent many letters" → "were multiplying their letters going to Tobiah"** (v17) — Hebrew מַרְבִּים … אִגְּרֹתֵיהֶם הוֹלְכוֹת, two participles.
- **"were sworn unto him" kept** (v18) — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי שְׁבוּעָה לוֹ, literally "owners of an oath to him": men bound to him by oath. **"had taken" kept** — Hebrew לָקַח, of taking a wife.
- **"they reported his good deeds" → "his good things they were saying"** (v19) — Hebrew טוֹבֹתָיו, "his good things" (his good deeds, or his good words); "deeds" is not written. **"uttered my words to him" → "my words they were bringing out to him"** — Hebrew מוֹצִיאִים, "bringing out". **"to put me in fear" → "to make me afraid"** — the same verb as v9, 14.

### Notes
- **Four invitations**, then a fifth, an open letter. A war of fear (the verb "make afraid" runs through v9, 13, 14, 19).
- **"Open letter"** (v5) — unsealed, so that anyone could read it.
- **Charge of rebellion** — standard accusation; echoes Ezra 4:12–16. Fear of Persian-king reprisal.
- **Shemaiah** (v10) — a hired prophet tries to draw Nehemiah into the temple building, where a layman may not go (Num 18:7). It would have discredited him.
- **"Noadiah the prophetess"** (v14) — one of the few prophetesses named in the Hebrew Bible, and here among the opponents.
- **52 days** (v15) — Elul is the sixth month (Aug–Sept); 445 BC.
- **Tobiah's inside access** (v17–19) — bound by marriage and oath to leading Jerusalem families; letters run both ways.
