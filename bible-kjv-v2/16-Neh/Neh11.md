## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v19).
- **"ruler of the house of God" → "chief officer of the house of *Elohim*"** (v11).
- **"valley of craftsmen" → "Valley of Craftsmen"** (v35).
- **"son of one of the great men" → "son of Haggedolim"** (v14) — *ben ha-gedolim*; could be a name or "son of the great-ones."

### Notes
- **Repopulation of Jerusalem** — empty after 586 BC; volunteers lotteried to fill. Tenth to the city, ninety percent to farmland.
- **"Willingly offered"** (v2) — genuine sacrifice; leaving ancestral lands for insecure capital.
- **Provincial governors in Jerusalem** — administrative centralization.
- **Judahites, Benjaminites, priests, Levites, gatekeepers, Netinim** — six classes form Jerusalem community. Parallel to Ezra 2 order.
- **Territorial extent** (v25-36) — south to Beersheba, northeast to Michmash and Bethel. Smaller than pre-exilic Judah.
- **"King's commandment" over singers** (v23) — Persian royal salary for temple musicians. Extraordinary state-religion arrangement.
- **List largely overlaps with 1 Chr 9** — Chronicler incorporated a version of this post-exilic settlement record.
