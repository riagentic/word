# Nehemiah 11

[1] The officials of the people lived at Jerusalem. The rest of the people cast lots to bring one of every ten to live in Jerusalem the holy city, while the nine remained in the other cities.

[2] The people blessed all the men who willingly offered themselves to live in Jerusalem.

[3] These are the chiefs of the province who lived in Jerusalem — but in the cities of Judah each lived on his property in their cities: Israel, the priests, the Levites, the temple servants, and the descendants of Solomon's servants.

[4] In Jerusalem lived some of the children of Judah and of the children of Benjamin.

Of the children of Judah: Athaiah son of Uzziah son of Zechariah son of Amariah son of Shephatiah son of Mahalalel, of the children of Perez;

[5] and Maaseiah son of Baruch son of Col-hozeh son of Hazaiah son of Adaiah son of Joiarib son of Zechariah, a descendant of the Shilonite.

[6] All the sons of Perez who lived at Jerusalem were 468 valiant men.

[7] These are the sons of Benjamin: Sallu son of Meshullam son of Joed son of Pedaiah son of Kolaiah son of Maaseiah son of Ithiel son of Jeshaiah;

[8] and after him Gabbai, Sallai — 928.

[9] Joel son of Zichri was their overseer; Judah son of Hassenuah was second over the city.

[10] Of the priests: Jedaiah son of Joiarib, Jachin,

[11] Seraiah son of Hilkiah son of Meshullam son of Zadok son of Meraioth son of Ahitub, chief officer of the house of *Elohim*;

[12] their brothers who did the work of the house — 822. And Adaiah son of Jeroham son of Pelaliah son of Amzi son of Zechariah son of Pashhur son of Malchijah,

[13] with his brothers, heads of fathers' houses — 242. And Amashsai son of Azarel son of Ahzai son of Meshillemoth son of Immer,

[14] with their brothers, valiant warriors — 128; their overseer was Zabdiel son of Haggedolim.

[15] Of the Levites: Shemaiah son of Hasshub son of Azrikam son of Hashabiah son of Bunni;

[16] Shabbethai and Jozabad, chiefs of the Levites, had oversight of the outside business of the house of *Elohim*;

[17] Mattaniah son of Mica son of Zabdi son of Asaph was the leader who began the thanksgiving at prayer; and Bakbukiah second among his brothers; and Abda son of Shammua son of Galal son of Jeduthun.

[18] All the Levites in the holy city were 284.

[19] The gatekeepers: Akkub, Talmon, and their brothers, who kept watch at the gates — 172.

[20] The rest of Israel, the priests, the Levites, were in all the cities of Judah, each on his inheritance.

[21] The temple servants lived on the Ophel; Ziha and Gishpa were over the temple servants.

[22] The overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem was Uzzi son of Bani son of Hashabiah son of Mattaniah son of Mica, of the sons of Asaph, the singers who were over the business of the house of *Elohim*.

[23] For the king's commandment was over them, an ordinance for the singers, each day's provision for its day.

[24] Pethahiah son of Meshezabel, of the children of Zerah son of Judah, was at the king's hand in all matters concerning the people.

[25] As for the villages with their fields, some of the children of Judah lived at Kiriath-arba and its villages, Dibon and its villages, Jekabzeel and its villages,

[26] Jeshua, Moladah, Beth-pelet,

[27] Hazar-shual, Beersheba and its villages,

[28] Ziklag, Meconah and its villages,

[29] En-rimmon, Zorah, Jarmuth,

[30] Zanoah, Adullam and their villages, Lachish and its fields, Azekah and its villages. So they lived from Beersheba to the Valley of Hinnom.

[31] The children of Benjamin lived from Geba onward: Michmash, Aija, Bethel and its villages,

[32] Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,

[33] Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,

[34] Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,

[35] Lod, Ono, the Valley of Craftsmen.

[36] Of the Levites, certain divisions of Judah were joined to Benjamin.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v19).
- **"ruler of the house of God" → "chief officer of the house of *Elohim*"** (v11).
- **"valley of craftsmen" → "Valley of Craftsmen"** (v35).
- **"son of one of the great men" → "son of Haggedolim"** (v14) — *ben ha-gedolim*; could be a name or "son of the great-ones."

### Notes
- **Repopulation of Jerusalem** — empty after 586 BC; volunteers lotteried to fill. Tenth to the city, ninety percent to farmland.
- **"Willingly offered"** (v2) — genuine sacrifice; leaving ancestral lands for insecure capital.
- **Provincial governors in Jerusalem** — administrative centralization.
- **Judahites, Benjaminites, priests, Levites, gatekeepers, Netinim** — six classes form Jerusalem community. Parallel to Ezra 2 order.
- **Territorial extent** (v25-36) — south to Beersheba, northeast to Michmash and Bethel. Smaller than pre-exilic Judah.
- **"King's commandment" over singers** (v23) — Persian royal salary for temple musicians. Extraordinary state-religion arrangement.
- **List largely overlaps with 1 Chr 9** — Chronicler incorporated a version of this post-exilic settlement record.
