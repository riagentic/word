## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Ginnetho" → "Ginnethon"** (v4); **"Miamin" → "Mijamin"** (v5); **"Melicu" → "Malluchi"** (v14) — minor spelling variants.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v25, 45, 47).
- **"psalteries" → "harps"** (v27); **"harps" → "lyres"** (v27).
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v31, 32, 40) — *sarim*.
- **"old gate" → "Jeshanah Gate"** (v39) — same question as 3:6.
- **"prison gate" → "Gate of the Guard"** (v39) — *sha'ar ha-mattara*.

### Notes
- **Priestly and Levitical lineages** — three generations: Zerubbabel/Jeshua era, Joiakim era, Eliashib-Johanan-Jaddua era. Spans roughly 150 years (late 6th to mid-4th century BC).
- **Jaddua** (v11, 22) — last high priest mentioned; his era overlaps Alexander the Great (ca. 330 BC) per Josephus. Indicates Nehemiah contains some later editorial notes.
- **Wall dedication procession** — two choirs circuit wall in opposite directions, meeting at temple. Symbolic encircling ritual.
- **Nehemiah with one choir** ("I followed them") — memoir-style; v38, 40 preserve first-person voice.
- **Tithe infrastructure** (v44-47) — storehouse system organized; tithes flow from fields to temple.
- **David/Solomon's ordinance** — Chronistic pattern of Davidic temple-music and temple-administration as enduring institutional foundation.
