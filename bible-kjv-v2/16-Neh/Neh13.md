# Nehemiah 13

[1] On that day they read in the Book of Moses in the hearing of the people, and it was found written that no Ammonite or Moabite should ever enter the assembly of *Elohim*,

[2] because they did not meet the children of Israel with bread and water, but hired Balaam against them to curse them — yet our *Elohim* turned the curse into a blessing.

[3] When they heard the Law, they separated from Israel all the mixed multitude.

[4] Before this, Eliashib the priest, who had been put in charge of the chambers of the house of our *Elohim*, and who was related to Tobiah,

[5] had prepared for him a large chamber where they had previously kept the grain offerings, the frankincense, the vessels, the tithes of the grain, the new wine, and the oil ordained for the Levites, the singers, and the gatekeepers, as well as the contributions for the priests.

[6] During all this I was not in Jerusalem; for in the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes king of Babylon, I had come to the king. Then after some time I obtained leave from the king;

[7] I came to Jerusalem and learned of the evil that Eliashib had done for Tobiah, preparing him a chamber in the courts of the house of *Elohim*.

[8] It displeased me greatly; I threw all the household furniture of Tobiah out of the chamber.

[9] I ordered, and they cleansed the chambers; I brought back into them the vessels of the house of *Elohim*, with the grain offering and the frankincense.

[10] I also learned that the portions of the Levites had not been given to them, so that the Levites and the singers who did the work had gone back, each to his field.

[11] Then I confronted the officials, saying, "Why is the house of *Elohim* forsaken?" I gathered them together and set them in their places.

[12] Then all Judah brought the tithe of the grain, the new wine, and the oil into the storehouses.

[13] I appointed as treasurers over the storehouses Shelemiah the priest, Zadok the scribe, and Pedaiah of the Levites; next to them was Hanan son of Zaccur son of Mattaniah, for they were considered faithful; their office was to distribute to their brothers.

[14] Remember me for this, O my *Elohim*, and do not wipe out the kindnesses I have done for the house of my *Elohim* and for its services.

[15] In those days I saw in Judah some treading winepresses on the Sabbath, bringing in sheaves, loading donkeys with them — also wine, grapes, figs, and every kind of burden — and bringing them into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day. I warned them on the day they sold food.

[16] Men of Tyre also lived there, who brought fish and every kind of ware and sold them on the Sabbath to the children of Judah and in Jerusalem.

[17] Then I confronted the nobles of Judah and said to them, "What evil thing is this that you are doing, profaning the Sabbath day?

[18] Did not your fathers do thus, and did not our *Elohim* bring all this evil upon us and upon this city? Yet you are adding to the wrath upon Israel by profaning the Sabbath."

[19] When the gates of Jerusalem began to cast shadows before the Sabbath, I commanded that the doors be shut and ordered that they not be opened until after the Sabbath. I stationed some of my servants at the gates, so that no burden should be brought in on the Sabbath day.

[20] So the merchants and sellers of all kinds of wares lodged outside Jerusalem once or twice.

[21] I warned them and said to them, "Why do you lodge by the wall? If you do so again, I will lay hands on you." From that time on, they did not come on the Sabbath.

[22] I commanded the Levites to purify themselves and to come and guard the gates, to sanctify the Sabbath day. Remember me for this also, O my *Elohim*, and have compassion on me according to the greatness of your steadfast love.

[23] In those days I also saw Jews who had married women from Ashdod, Ammon, and Moab.

[24] Half of their children spoke the language of Ashdod; none of them could speak the Judahite language; they spoke only the language of one of those peoples.

[25] I confronted them, cursed them, struck some of them, pulled out their hair, and made them swear by *Elohim*: "You shall not give your daughters to their sons, nor take their daughters for your sons or for yourselves.

[26] Did not Solomon king of Israel sin on account of such things? Among many nations there was no king like him — he was loved by his *Elohim*, and *Elohim* made him king over all Israel; yet even him the foreign women caused to sin.

[27] Shall we then listen to you and do all this great evil by acting unfaithfully against our *Elohim* by marrying foreign women?"

[28] One of the sons of Joiada son of Eliashib the high priest was son-in-law to Sanballat the Horonite; therefore I chased him from me.

[29] Remember them, O my *Elohim*, for they have defiled the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and of the Levites.

[30] Thus I cleansed them from everything foreign, and I established the duties of the priests and the Levites, each in his work;

[31] and I provided for the wood offering at appointed times, and for the firstfruits.

Remember me, O my *Elohim*, for good.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v5, 9) — *minchah*.
- **"corn" → "grain"** (v5, 12).
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v5).
- **"outlandish women / strange wives" → "foreign women"** (v26, 27) — *nashim nakhriyyot*.
- **"Jews' language" → "Judahite language"** (v24).

### Notes
- **Second term** — Nehemiah returns after a stint back in Persia (thirty-second year of Artaxerxes ≈ 433 BC). Finds multiple reforms unraveled.
- **Tobiah in the temple** — ironic: the long-time enemy given chambers by priestly family ties. Eliashib's family had deep Tobiad connections.
- **Levites unpaid, back to fields** — temple system had broken down.
- **Sabbath violations** — market day on Sabbath; Tyrian fishmongers exploiting. Nehemiah shuts gates.
- **Intermarriage resurfaces** — despite Ezra 10's reform. Chronic temptation.
- **Linguistic observation** (v24) — first historical note on Hebrew-loss in community; "Judahite language" identified as distinctive.
- **Joiada's son marries Sanballat's daughter** — top-tier priestly-Samarian alliance. Some scholars link this expelled priest (Manasseh in Josephus) with the origin of the Mt. Gerizim Samaritan priesthood.
- **"Remember me" refrain** — Nehemiah's four-fold "remember me for good" (5:19, 13:14, 22, 31) closes his memoirs.
