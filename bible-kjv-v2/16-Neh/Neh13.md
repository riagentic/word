# Nehemiah 13

[1] On that day it was read in the book of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ in the ears of the people; and there was found written in it that an ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧ and a ⟦moabites|Moavi|Moabite⟧ shall not come into the assembly of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, forever,

[2] because they did not come to meet the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ with bread and with water, and he hired against him ⟦balaam|Bilam|Balaam⟧ to curse him; and our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ turned the curse into a blessing.

[3] And it came to pass, when they heard the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, that they separated all the mixed people from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] And before this, ⟦eliashib|Elyashiv|Eliashib⟧ the priest, who was set over the chamber of the house of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, being near to ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧,

[5] made for him a great chamber, where formerly they were putting the grain offering, the frankincense and the utensils, and the tithe of the grain, the new wine and the fresh oil, the commandment of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and the singers and the gatekeepers, and the contribution of the priests.

[6] And in all this I was not in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; for in the thirty-second year of ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ I came to the king, and at the end of days I asked leave of the king.

[7] And I came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and I understood the evil that ⟦eliashib|Elyashiv|Eliashib⟧ had done for ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧, in making for him a chamber in the courts of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] And it was very evil to me, and I threw all the utensils of the house of ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ outside, out of the chamber.

[9] And I said, and they cleansed the chambers; and I brought back there the utensils of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the grain offering and the frankincense.

[10] And I came to know that the portions of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ had not been given; and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and the singers, the doers of the work, had fled, each man to his field.

[11] And I contended with the prefects and said, "Why is the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ forsaken?" And I gathered them and made them stand in their place.

[12] And all ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ brought the tithe of the grain and the new wine and the fresh oil into the treasuries.

[13] And I set as treasurers over the treasuries ⟦shelemiah|Shelemyah|Shelemiah⟧ the priest and ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧ the scribe and ⟦pedaiah|Pedayah|Pedaiah⟧ of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and next to them ⟦hanan|Chanan|Hanan⟧ the son of ⟦zaccur|Zakkur|Zaccur⟧, the son of ⟦mattaniah|Mattanyah|Mattaniah⟧; for they were reckoned faithful, and it was on them to divide to their brothers.

[14] Remember for me, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, concerning this, and do not wipe out my ⟦steadfast-love|chasadim|acts of steadfast love⟧ that I have done in the house of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and in its watches.

[15] In those days I saw in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ men treading winepresses on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, and bringing in heaps and loading them on the donkeys, and also wine, grapes and figs and every burden, and bringing them into ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧; and I warned them on the day they sold provision.

[16] And the ⟦tyrians|Tzorim|Tyrians⟧ dwelt in it, bringing fish and every ware, and selling on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ to the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[17] And I contended with the nobles of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and said to them, "What is this evil thing that you are doing, and profaning the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧?

[18] Did not your fathers do thus, and our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ brought on us all this evil, and on this city? And you are adding burning anger on ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ by profaning the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧."

[19] And it came to pass, when the gates of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ grew dark before the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, I said, and the doors were shut; and I said that they should not open them until after the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧. And I stationed some of my young men at the gates: no burden shall come in on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧.

[20] And the merchants and the sellers of every ware lodged outside ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ once and twice.

[21] And I warned them and said to them, "Why are you lodging in front of the wall? If you do it again, I will stretch out a hand against you." From that time they did not come on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧.

[22] And I said to the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ that they should be cleansing themselves and coming, keeping the gates, to sanctify the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧. This also remember for me, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and pity me according to the abundance of your ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧.

[23] Also in those days I saw the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who had made women of Ashdod, of Ammon, of ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ dwell with them.

[24] And of their sons, half spoke the language of Ashdod, and they did not know how to speak ⟦judahite|Yehudit|the language of Judah⟧, but according to the tongue of each people.

[25] And I contended with them and cursed them and struck some men of them and plucked out their hair; and I made them swear by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: "You shall not give your daughters to their sons, and you shall not take of their daughters for your sons and for yourselves.

[26] Did not ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sin on account of these? And among the many nations there was no king like him, and he was loved by his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave him as king over all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; even him the foreign women caused to sin.

[27] And shall we listen to you, to do all this great evil, to act unfaithfully against our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ by making foreign women dwell with us?"

[28] And of the sons of ⟦joiada|Yoyada|Joiada⟧ the son of ⟦eliashib|Elyashiv|Eliashib⟧ the great priest, one was son-in-law to ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ the ⟦horonite|Choroni|Horonite⟧; and I chased him from me.

[29] Remember for them, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, concerning the defilings of the priesthood and the covenant of the priesthood and of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧.

[30] And I cleansed them from everything foreign, and I made watches stand for the priests and for the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, each man in his work,

[31] and for the offering of the wood at appointed times, and for the firstfruits.

Remember for me, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for good.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; with the article in v1, 7, 9, 11 (הָאֱלֹהִים). **"O my God" → "my Elohim"** (v14, 22, 29, 31) — Hebrew אֱלֹהַי, no "O".
- **"they read in the book of Moses in the audience of the people" → "it was read in the book of Moses in the ears of the people"** (v1) — Hebrew נִקְרָא, passive with no subject; בְּאָזְנֵי "in the ears of". **"that the Ammonite and the Moabite should not come into the congregation of God for ever" → "that an Ammonite and a Moabite shall not come into the assembly of Elohim, forever"** — Deut 23:3 in the wording of Deuteronomy (לֹא־יָבוֹא … בִּקְהַל … עַד־עוֹלָם); Deuteronomy has "of Yahweh", Nehemiah "of the Elohim".
- **"Because they met not the children of Israel with bread and with water, but hired Balaam against them, that he should curse them: howbeit our God turned" → "because they did not come to meet the sons of Israel with bread and with water, and he hired against him Balaam to curse him; and our Elohim turned"** (v2) — Deut 23:4–5 in the wording of Deuteronomy where the Hebrew is the same (קִדֵּם "come to meet"; וַיַּהֲפֹךְ … הַקְּלָלָה לִבְרָכָה "turned the curse into a blessing"). Nehemiah says "the sons of Israel" for Deuteronomy's "you", and "he hired" (שָׂכַר, singular: Moab or its king) "against him … to curse him", Israel as one man.
- **"it came to pass … that they separated from Israel all the mixed multitude" → "that they separated all the mixed people from Israel"** (v3) — Hebrew כָל־עֵרֶב, literally "every mixture", without רַב "great" (as Exod 12:38 has it): those of mixed descent.
- **"having the oversight of the chamber" → "who was set over the chamber"** (v4) — Hebrew נָתוּן בְּלִשְׁכַּת, literally "given in the chamber"; singular "chamber" (the previous "chambers" is not in the Hebrew). **"allied unto Tobiah" → "being near to Tobiah"** — קָרוֹב לְ: near by kin or by dwelling (not decided). v4–5 are one sentence in the Hebrew.
- **"they laid the meat offerings" → "they were putting the grain offering"** (v5) — participle; מִנְחָה. **"the vessels" → "the utensils"** (v5, 8, 9) — כְּלִי. **"the corn, the new wine, and the oil" → "the grain, the new wine and the fresh oil"** (v5, 12) — דָּגָן, תִּירוֹשׁ, יִצְהָר. **"which was commanded to be given to the Levites" → "the commandment of the Levites"** — Hebrew מִצְוַת הַלְוִיִּם, a noun: what was commanded for them. **"the offerings of the priests" → "the contribution of the priests"** — תְּרוּמָה.
- **"after certain days obtained I leave" → "at the end of days I asked leave"** (v6) — וּלְקֵץ יָמִים; נִשְׁאַלְתִּי, "I asked for myself".
- **"understood"** (v7) — וָאָבִינָה, from בִּין. **"in preparing him a chamber" → "in making for him a chamber"** — לַעֲשׂוֹת; נִשְׁכָּה is another spelling of לִשְׁכָּה (so v44 of ch. 12).
- **"it grieved me sore" → "it was very evil to me"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיֵּרַע לִי מְאֹד, the רַע "evil" of v7. **"cast forth all the household stuff of Tobiah" → "threw all the utensils of the house of Tobiah outside"** — הִשְׁלִיךְ; כְּלֵי בֵית־טוֹבִיָּה.
- **"I commanded" → "I said"** (v9, 19 twice, 22) — Hebrew וָאֹמְרָה "and I said"; "command" is צִוָּה.
- **"I perceived" → "I came to know"** (v10) — וָאֵדְעָה. **"were fled every one" → "had fled, each man"** — בָּרַח "flee"; the previous "gone back" is not the word. **"that did the work"** — עֹשֵׂי הַמְּלָאכָה "the doers of the work".
- **"contended"** (v11, 17, 25) — Hebrew רִיב. **"rulers" → "prefects"** (v11) — סְגָנִים, as 12:40. **"set them in their place" → "made them stand in their place"** — וָאַעֲמִדֵם עַל־עָמְדָם, literally "on their standing".
- **"into the treasuries"** (v12, 13) — אוֹצָרוֹת. **"I made treasurers" → "I set as treasurers"** (v13) — וָאוֹצְרָה, a verb made from the noun, only here. **"next to them"** — עַל־יָדָם, literally "at their hand". **"they were counted faithful" → "they were reckoned faithful"** — נֶאֱמָנִים נֶחְשָׁבוּ. **"their office was to distribute" → "it was on them to divide"** — וַעֲלֵיהֶם לַחֲלֹק.
- **"Remember me, O my God, concerning this" → "Remember for me, my Elohim, concerning this"** (v14); **"Remember me, O my God, concerning this also" → "This also remember for me, my Elohim"** (v22); **"Remember them, O my God, because" → "Remember for them, my Elohim, concerning"** (v29); **"Remember me, O my God, for good" → "Remember for me, my Elohim, for good"** (v31) — Hebrew זָכְרָה־לִּי / לָהֶם אֱלֹהַי: the לְ "for me / for them" kept, one wording each time, as 5:19. In v14 and v29 עַל "concerning" follows; in v22 the Hebrew order is kept (גַּם־זֹאת זָכְרָה־לִּי, זֹאת the object).
- **"wipe not out my good deeds" → "do not wipe out my acts of steadfast love"** (v14) — Hebrew חֲסָדַי, the plural of *chesed*: the token as 2 Chr 32:32; 35:26. **"for the offices thereof" → "in its watches"** — מִשְׁמָרָיו.
- **"sheaves" → "heaps"** (v15) — Hebrew עֲרֵמוֹת, heaps (of grain), as 2 Chr 31:6–9; "sheaves" is עֳמָרִים. **"lading asses" → "loading them on the donkeys"** — עֹמְסִים עַל־הַחֲמֹרִים; "them" is supplied. **"all manner of burdens" → "every burden"** — מַשָּׂא. **"I testified against them in the day wherein they sold victuals" → "I warned them on the day they sold provision"** — וָאָעִיד, as v21 (הֵעִיד "warn"); "them" is supplied here; צַיִד, the word for game, here food for sale.
- **"There dwelt men of Tyre also therein" → "And the Tyrians dwelt in it"** (v16) — Hebrew הַצֹּרִים, the gentilic: token `tyrians` for the previous `tyre`. **"all manner of ware" → "every ware"** (v16, 20) — מֶכֶר, מִמְכָּר.
- **"profane the sabbath day" → "profaning the day of the Shabbat"** (v17) — חִלֵּל, participle joined with "and".
- **"and did not our God bring" → "and our Elohim brought"** (v18) — וַיָּבֵא, narrative form, after the one question. **"more wrath" → "burning anger"** — חָרוֹן.
- **"began to be dark" → "grew dark"** (v19) — צָלֲלוּ "became shadowed". **"some of my servants" → "some of my young men"** — נְעָרַי. **"that there should no burden be brought in" → "no burden shall come in"** — the words of the order, no "that" in the Hebrew.
- **"once or twice" → "once and twice"** (v20) — פַּעַם וּשְׁתָּיִם.
- **"about the wall" → "in front of the wall"** (v21) — נֶגֶד. **"I will lay hands on you" → "I will stretch out a hand against you"** — יָד אֶשְׁלַח בָּכֶם, the שָׁלַח יָד of Gen 22:10.
- **"that they should cleanse themselves, and that they should come and keep the gates" → "that they should be cleansing themselves and coming, keeping the gates"** (v22) — participles. **"spare me according to the greatness of thy mercy" → "pity me according to the abundance of your steadfast love"** — חוּס "pity"; כְּרֹב חַסְדֶּךָ.
- **"had married wives" → "had made women … dwell with them"** (v23); **"in marrying strange wives" → "by making foreign women dwell with us"** (v27) — Hebrew הֹשִׁיבוּ נָשִׁים, לְהֹשִׁיב נָשִׁים, the hifil of יָשַׁב: they took them into the house as wives (as Ezra 10:2). "with them / us" is supplied. **"of Ashdod, of Ammon, of Moab"** — the qere אַשְׁדֳּדִיּוֹת עַמֳּנִיּוֹת is translated; the ketiv differs only in spelling. The words are adjectives: "Ashdodite, Ammonite, Moabite women".
- **"their children" → "of their sons"** (v24) — בְּנֵיהֶם. **"spake in the speech of Ashdod" → "spoke the language of Ashdod"** — אַשְׁדּוֹדִית, literally "Ashdodite", as יְהוּדִית "Judahite". **"could not speak … but according to the language of each people"** — וְאֵינָם מַכִּירִים, literally "they did not recognize (how) to speak"; כִּלְשׁוֹן עַם וָעָם, literally "the tongue of people and people".
- **"cursed them"** (v25) — קִלֵּל, the word "make light of", here "curse". **"smote certain of them" → "struck some men of them"** — אֲנָשִׁים. **"Ye shall not give … nor take" → "You shall not give … and you shall not take"** — the oath formula אִם … וְאִם, literally "if you give … and if you take": a sworn "you shall not".
- **"by these things" → "on account of these"** (v26) — עַל־אֵלֶּה. **"who was beloved of his God" → "he was loved by his Elohim"** — אָהוּב לֵאלֹהָיו (cf. 2 Sam 12:24, 25). **"God made him king" → "Elohim gave him as king"** — וַיִּתְּנֵהוּ … מֶלֶךְ, literally "gave him (as) king". **"outlandish women" → "the foreign women"** — הַנָּשִׁים הַנָּכְרִיּוֹת. **"did … cause to sin"** — הֶחֱטִיאוּ.
- **"Shall we then hearken unto you" → "And shall we listen to you"** (v27) — וְלָכֶם הֲנִשְׁמַע; the form can also be read as passive: "and is it heard of you (that you do) …". **"to transgress against our God" → "to act unfaithfully against our Elohim"** — מָעַל בְּ.
- **"the high priest" → "the great priest"** (v28) — הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל, as 2 Kgs 22:4. **"one of the sons … was son in law" → "of the sons … one was son-in-law"** — the Hebrew has no "one"; it is supplied for the sense. **"chased"** — הִבְרִיחַ, "made him flee".
- **"because they have defiled the priesthood" → "concerning the defilings of the priesthood"** (v29) — עַל גָּאֳלֵי הַכְּהֻנָּה, a noun, plural.
- **"strangers" → "everything foreign"** (v30) — נֵכָר. **"appointed the wards" → "made watches stand"** — הֶעֱמִיד מִשְׁמָרוֹת. **"every one in his business" → "each man in his work"** — מְלָאכָה.
- **"the wood offering" → "the offering of the wood"** (v31) — קֻרְבַּן הָעֵצִים, literally "the offering of the woods"; קָרְבָּן "what is brought near". **"at times appointed"** — בְּעִתִּים מְזֻמָּנוֹת. "I provided" is not in the Hebrew; v31 continues v30 ("I made watches stand … and for the offering").

### Notes
- **Second term** — Nehemiah returns after a stint back in Persia (thirty-second year of Artaxerxes ≈ 433 BC). Finds multiple reforms unraveled.
- **Tobiah in the temple** — ironic: the long-time enemy given chambers by priestly family ties. Eliashib's family had deep Tobiad connections.
- **Levites unpaid, back to fields** — temple system had broken down.
- **Sabbath violations** — market day on Sabbath; Tyrian fishmongers exploiting. Nehemiah shuts gates.
- **Intermarriage resurfaces** — despite Ezra 10's reform. Chronic temptation.
- **Linguistic observation** (v24) — first historical note on Hebrew-loss in community; "Judahite language" identified as distinctive.
- **Joiada's son marries Sanballat's daughter** — top-tier priestly-Samarian alliance. Some scholars link this expelled priest (Manasseh in Josephus) with the origin of the Mt. Gerizim Samaritan priesthood.
- **"Remember me" refrain** — Nehemiah's "remember for me, my Elohim" (5:19; 13:14, 22, 31) closes his memoirs.
