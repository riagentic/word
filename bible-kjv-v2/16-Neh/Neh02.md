# Nehemiah 2

[1] In the month Nisan, in the twentieth year of King ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧, when wine was before him, I took up the wine and gave it to the king. Now I had never been sad in his presence before.

[2] So the king said to me, "Why is your face sad, seeing you are not sick? This is nothing but sorrow of heart." Then I was very much afraid,

[3] and said to the king, "Let the king live forever! Why should my face not be sad, when the city, the place of my fathers' tombs, lies waste, and its gates are consumed by fire?"

[4] The king said to me, "What are you requesting?" So I prayed to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of heaven,

[5] and said to the king, "If it pleases the king, and if your servant has found favor in your sight, send me to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, to the city of my fathers' tombs, that I may rebuild it."

[6] The king said to me (while the queen consort sat beside him), "How long shall your journey be, and when will you return?" It pleased the king to send me, and I set him a time.

[7] Moreover I said to the king, "If it pleases the king, let letters be given me to the governors of the province Beyond the River, that they may allow me passage until I come into ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧;

[8] and a letter to ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧ the keeper of the king's forest, that he may give me timber to make beams for the gates of the fortress by the temple, for the wall of the city, and for the house that I will occupy." The king granted me this, for the good hand of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was upon me.

[9] Then I came to the governors of the province Beyond the River and gave them the king's letters. The king had sent with me army commanders and horsemen.

[10] When ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ the ⟦horonite|Choroni|Horonite⟧ and ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧ official heard of it, they were greatly displeased that someone had come to seek the welfare of the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[11] So I came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and was there three days.

[12] Then I arose by night, I and a few men with me; I had told no one what my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had put in my heart to do for ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. There was no animal with me except the one I rode.

[13] I went out by night through the Valley Gate, toward the Dragon Spring and the Dung Gate, and I inspected the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ that were broken down, and its gates that had been consumed by fire.

[14] Then I went on to the Fountain Gate and to the King's Pool, but there was no room for the animal that was under me to pass.

[15] So I went up in the night by the wadi, inspecting the wall; I turned back and entered by the Valley Gate, and so returned.

[16] The officials did not know where I had gone or what I was doing; I had not yet told the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, the priests, the nobles, the officials, or the rest who did the work.

[17] Then I said to them, "You see the trouble we are in, how ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ lies waste with its gates burned with fire. Come, let us rebuild the wall of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, that we may no longer be a reproach."

[18] I told them of the hand of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that had been good upon me, and also of the king's words that he had spoken to me. They said, "Let us rise up and build." So they strengthened their hands for the good work.

[19] But when ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ the ⟦horonite|Choroni|Horonite⟧, ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧ official, and Geshem the ⟦arabs|Arvi|Arab⟧ heard it, they mocked us and despised us, saying, "What is this thing you are doing? Are you rebelling against the king?"

[20] Then I answered them, "The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of heaven — he will give us success; and we his servants will arise and build; but you have no portion, no right, and no memorial in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"queen" → "queen consort"** (v6) — *ha-shegal*; king's consort/companion.
- **"palace which appertained to the house" → "fortress by the temple"** (v8) — *birah ashe la-bayit*; temple-fortress (later Antonia's predecessor).
- **"beyond the river" → "province Beyond the River"** (v7, 9).
- **"servant" (re: Tobiah) → "official"** (v10, 19) — *eved*; here bureaucratic sense; Tobiah was a Persian official of Ammonite region.
- **"dung port" → "Dung Gate"** (v13).
- **"brook" → "wadi"** (v15).
- **"rulers" → "officials"** (v16) — *seganim*.

### Notes
- **Nisan, 20th year of Artaxerxes** — spring 445 BC. Four months after Kislev report (1:1 chronology suggests a new regnal year or double-reference).
- **Cupbearer prayer** — Nehemiah shoots a prayer mid-conversation (v4). Brevity as piety.
- **Persian imperial support** — letters, military escort, timber from royal forest. Top-down state endorsement.
- **Sanballat the Horonite** — Elephantine papyri identify Sanballat as governor of Samaria (ca. 407 BC mentions his sons). Historical figure.
- **Tobiah the Ammonite** — powerful Transjordanian family; later Tobiads archive. In-laws among Jerusalem priesthood (13:4).
- **Geshem the Arab** — possibly "Gashmu" in inscriptions of Dedan; Arabian chieftain under Persian suzerainty.
- **Nighttime wall-inspection** — strategic discretion. Nehemiah surveys without alerting opponents.
- **Valley Gate, Dragon Spring, Dung Gate** — southeastern/southern circuit; water-sources and refuse disposal area.
- **Response "no portion, no right, no memorial"** (v20) — legal language excluding the three opponents from Jerusalem's community-life, rights, and inheritance.
