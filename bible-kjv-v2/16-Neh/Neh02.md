# Nehemiah 2

[1] And it came to pass in the month Nisan, in the twentieth year of ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ the king, wine was before him, and I lifted up the wine and gave it to the king. And I had not been sad before him.

[2] And the king said to me, "Why is your face bad, when you are not sick? This is nothing but badness of heart." And I was very greatly afraid.

[3] And I said to the king, "The king, may he live forever! Why should my face not be bad, when the city, the house of the graves of my fathers, is waste, and its gates have been consumed by fire?"

[4] And the king said to me, "For what is this that you are seeking?" And I prayed to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the heavens.

[5] And I said to the king, "If it is good to the king, and if your servant is good before you, that you send me to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, to the city of the graves of my fathers, and let me build it."

[6] And the king said to me — and the consort was sitting beside him — "Until when will your journey be, and when will you return?" And it was good before the king, and he sent me, and I gave him a set time.

[7] And I said to the king, "If it is good to the king, let letters be given to me for the governors across the River, that they may make me pass over until I come to ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧,

[8] and a letter to ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧ the keeper of the park that is the king's, that he may give me wood to lay the beams of the gates of the citadel that belongs to the house, and for the wall of the city, and for the house that I shall come into." And the king gave to me, according to the good hand of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ upon me.

[9] And I came to the governors across the River and gave them the letters of the king. And the king sent with me commanders of the force and horsemen.

[10] And ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ the ⟦horonite|Choroni|Horonite⟧ and ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ the servant, the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧, heard, and it was evil to them, a great evil, that a man had come to seek good for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[11] And I came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and was there three days.

[12] And I arose by night, I and a few men with me, and I did not tell a man what my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was putting into my heart to do for ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And there was no beast with me except the beast that I was riding on.

[13] And I went out by the Valley Gate by night, toward the Dragon Spring and to the Gate of the Ash Heaps, and I was inspecting the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, which were breached, and its gates had been consumed by fire.

[14] And I passed on to the Spring Gate and to the King's Pool, and there was no place for the beast under me to pass.

[15] And I was going up in the wadi by night, and I was inspecting the wall; and I turned back and came in by the Valley Gate, and I turned back.

[16] And the prefects did not know where I had gone and what I was doing; and to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and to the priests and to the nobles and to the prefects and to the rest who did the work I had not told it until then.

[17] And I said to them, "You see the evil that we are in, that ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ is waste and its gates have been burned with fire. Come, and let us build the wall of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and let us no longer be a reproach."

[18] And I told them of the hand of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that it was good upon me, and also of the words of the king that he had said to me. And they said, "Let us arise and build!" And they strengthened their hands for the good.

[19] And ⟦sanballat|Sanvallat|Sanballat⟧ the ⟦horonite|Choroni|Horonite⟧ and ⟦tobiah|Toviyah|Tobiah⟧ the servant, the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧, and Geshem the ⟦arabs|Arvi|Arab⟧ heard, and they derided us and despised us and said, "What is this thing that you are doing? Are you rebelling against the king?"

[20] And I returned word to them and said to them, "The ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the heavens, he will prosper us, and we his servants will arise and build; but you have no share and no right and no memorial in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 8, 12, 18, 20) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God of heaven" → "Elohim of the heavens"** (v4, 20) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם, plural.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept ("so", "then", "but", "therefore" of the KJV are not in the Hebrew).
- **"that wine was before him: and I took up the wine" → "wine was before him, and I lifted up the wine"** (v1) — Hebrew יַיִן לְפָנָיו וָאֶשָּׂא, נָשָׂא "lift up". **"Now I had not been beforetime sad in his presence" → "And I had not been sad before him"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־הָיִיתִי רַע לְפָנָיו, literally "and I had not been bad before him": רַע, the word that runs through v1–3 ("bad" of the face, as Gen 40:7); "beforetime" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Why is thy countenance sad … this is nothing else but sorrow of heart" → "Why is your face bad … This is nothing but badness of heart"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew פָּנֶיךָ רָעִים, רֹעַ לֵב; v3 לֹא־יֵרְעוּ פָנַי "my face not be bad": of a downcast look. **"very sore afraid" → "very greatly afraid"** — Hebrew הַרְבֵּה מְאֹד.
- **"Let the king live for ever" → "The king, may he live forever!"** (v3) — Hebrew הַמֶּלֶךְ לְעוֹלָם יִחְיֶה, a wish (jussive). **"the place of my fathers' sepulchres, lieth waste" → "the house of the graves of my fathers, is waste"** — Hebrew בֵּית־קִבְרוֹת אֲבֹתַי "the house of the graves"; חֲרֵבָה "waste, laid waste". **"consumed with fire" → "consumed by fire"** (v3, 13) — Hebrew אֻכְּלוּ בָאֵשׁ, literally "were eaten by the fire".
- **"For what dost thou make request?" → "For what is this that you are seeking?"** (v4) — Hebrew עַל־מַה־זֶּה אַתָּה מְבַקֵּשׁ, בִּקֵּשׁ "seek".
- **"If it please the king, and if thy servant have found favour in thy sight" → "If it is good to the king, and if your servant is good before you"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew אִם־עַל־הַמֶּלֶךְ טוֹב, literally "if upon the king it is good"; וְאִם־יִיטַב עַבְדְּךָ לְפָנֶיךָ, יָטַב "be good": "found favour" is another phrase (מָצָא חֵן). **"that I may build it" → "and let me build it"** — Hebrew וְאֶבְנֶנָּה, a cohortative; בָּנָה "build" (no "re-").
- **"the queen also sitting by him" → "and the consort was sitting beside him"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהַשֵּׁגַל יוֹשֶׁבֶת אֶצְלוֹ: שֵׁגַל, a royal wife (Ps 45:9), not the usual word for "queen" (מַלְכָּה). **"For how long" → "Until when"** — Hebrew עַד־מָתַי. **"So it pleased the king to send me; and I set him a time" → "And it was good before the king, and he sent me, and I gave him a set time"** — Hebrew וַיִּיטַב לִפְנֵי־הַמֶּלֶךְ וַיִּשְׁלָחֵנִי וָאֶתְּנָה לוֹ זְמָן: three verbs in a row; זְמָן "a set time".
- **"to the governors beyond the river, that they may convey me over" → "for the governors across the River, that they may make me pass over"** (v7, 9) — Hebrew פַּחֲווֹת עֵבֶר הַנָּהָר, "the far side of the River", the land west of the Euphrates named as it is seen from Persia (as 1 Kgs 4:24); "province" is not in the Hebrew. יַעֲבִירוּנִי, hifil of עָבַר "pass over".
- **"the keeper of the king's forest, that he may give me timber to make beams for the gates of the palace which appertained to the house" → "the keeper of the park that is the king's, that he may give me wood to lay the beams of the gates of the citadel that belongs to the house"** (v8) — Hebrew פַּרְדֵּס, a Persian word, an enclosed park or orchard (Eccl 2:5; Song 4:13), not יַעַר "forest"; עֵצִים "wood, trees"; לְקָרוֹת, piel of קָרָה "lay beams"; הַבִּירָה "the citadel" as 1:1; "the house" is the temple. **"that I shall enter into" → "that I shall come into"** — Hebrew אָבוֹא אֵלָיו. **"granted me" → "gave to me"** — Hebrew וַיִּתֶּן־לִי. **"according to the good hand of my God upon me"** — Hebrew כְּיַד־אֱלֹהַי הַטּוֹבָה עָלָי, kept.
- **"Now the king had sent captains of the army" → "And the king sent with me commanders of the force"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁלַח, a narrative past; שָׂרֵי חַיִל: שַׂר of an army "commander", חַיִל "force".
- **"Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite" → "Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite"** (v10, 19) — Hebrew הָעֶבֶד, "the slave": of a king's man "servant", here perhaps a title of office; the Hebrew does not say whose servant.
- **"it grieved them exceedingly that there was come a man to seek the welfare" → "it was evil to them, a great evil, that a man had come to seek good"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיֵּרַע לָהֶם רָעָה גְדֹלָה, the verb and the noun of one root (= it displeased them greatly); טוֹבָה "good"; אָדָם "a man".
- **"some few men" → "a few men"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲנָשִׁים מְעַט. **"neither told I any man" → "I did not tell a man"** — Hebrew לְאָדָם. **"had put in my heart" → "was putting into my heart"** — Hebrew נֹתֵן אֶל־לִבִּי, a participle, literally "giving to my heart". **"to do at Jerusalem" → "to do for Jerusalem"** — Hebrew לִירוּשָׁלִַם. **"neither was there any beast … save the beast that I rode upon" → "there was no beast … except the beast that I was riding on"** — Hebrew בְּהֵמָה, a four-footed animal; רֹכֵב, a participle.
- **"by the gate of the valley, even before the dragon well, and to the dung port" → "by the Valley Gate … toward the Dragon Spring and to the Gate of the Ash Heaps"** (v13) — Hebrew שַׁעַר הַגַּיא; וְאֶל־פְּנֵי עֵין הַתַּנִּין, literally "and to the face of the spring of the dragon" (תַּנִּין "dragon"; a spring and not a well: עַיִן); שַׁעַר הָאַשְׁפֹּת, אַשְׁפֹּת "ash heap, refuse heap" (1 Sam 2:8), traditionally "Dung Gate". **"viewed" → "was inspecting"** (v13, 15) — Hebrew וָאֱהִי שֹׂבֵר, "and I was" with a participle; שָׂבַר "inspect" is found only in these two verses. **"which were broken down" → "which were breached"** — the qere אֲשֶׁר־הֵם פְּרוּצִים "which were breached", of פָּרַץ as 1:3; the ketiv writes המפרוצים as one word.
- **"Then I went on to the gate of the fountain" → "And I passed on to the Spring Gate"** (v14) — Hebrew וָאֶעֱבֹר; שַׁעַר הָעַיִן, עַיִן "spring". **"for the beast that was under me to pass" → "for the beast under me to pass"** — Hebrew לַבְּהֵמָה לַעֲבֹר תַּחְתָּי; עָבַר twice in the verse.
- **"Then went I up in the night by the brook, and viewed the wall" → "And I was going up in the wadi by night, and I was inspecting the wall"** (v15) — Hebrew וָאֱהִי עֹלֶה בַנַּחַל, participles with "I was"; נַחַל "wadi", a stream bed (the Kidron). **"and turned back … and so returned" → "and I turned back … and I turned back"** — Hebrew וָאָשׁוּב … וָאָשׁוּב, the one verb שׁוּב at both ends of the clause.
- **"the rulers" → "the prefects"** (v16) — Hebrew סְגָנִים, a loanword from Akkadian: deputies, officials set over a city or province. **"nobles"** — Hebrew חֹרִים. **"neither had I as yet told it … nor … nor" → "and to … and to … I had not told it until then"** — Hebrew וְלַיְּהוּדִים וְלַכֹּהֲנִים … עַד־כֵּן לֹא הִגַּדְתִּי; "it" is supplied for English. **"the rest that did the work"** — Hebrew יֶתֶר עֹשֵׂה הַמְּלָאכָה.
- **"the distress that we are in" → "the evil that we are in"** (v17) — Hebrew הָרָעָה, as 1:3. **"lieth waste" → "is waste"**. **"come, and let us build up … that we be no more a reproach" → "Come, and let us build … and let us no longer be a reproach"** — Hebrew לְכוּ וְנִבְנֶה … וְלֹא־נִהְיֶה עוֹד חֶרְפָּה, two verbs of the first person plural joined by "and".
- **"the hand of my God which was good upon me" → "the hand of my Elohim, that it was good upon me"** (v18) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־הִיא טוֹבָה עָלַי. **"Let us rise up and build" → "Let us arise and build!"** (v18, 20) — Hebrew נָקוּם וּבָנִינוּ. **"So they strengthened their hands for this good work" → "And they strengthened their hands for the good"** — Hebrew וַיְחַזְּקוּ יְדֵיהֶם לַטּוֹבָה: חִזֵּק "strengthen" (lit. "make strong"); "work" and "this" are not in the Hebrew.
- **"Geshem the Arabian" → "Geshem the Arab"** (v19) — Hebrew הָעַרְבִי. **"they laughed us to scorn, and despised us" → "they derided us and despised us"** — Hebrew וַיַּלְעִגוּ לָנוּ וַיִּבְזוּ עָלֵינוּ, הִלְעִיג "deride", בָּזָה "despise". **"will ye rebel" → "Are you rebelling"** — Hebrew הַעַל הַמֶּלֶךְ אַתֶּם מֹרְדִים, a participle; מָרַד "rebel".
- **"Then answered I them" → "And I returned word to them"** (v20) — Hebrew וָאָשִׁיב אוֹתָם דָּבָר. **"he will prosper us; therefore we" → "he will prosper us, and we"** — Hebrew יַצְלִיחַ לָנוּ, literally "make prosper for us"; וַאֲנַחְנוּ "and we", no "therefore". **"no portion, nor right, nor memorial" → "no share and no right and no memorial"** — Hebrew חֵלֶק "share"; צְדָקָה "righteousness", here a just claim (as 2 Sam 19:28): "right"; זִכָּרוֹן "memorial".

### Notes
- **Nisan, 20th year of Artaxerxes** — spring 445 BC. Four months after Kislev report (1:1 chronology suggests a new regnal year or double-reference).
- **Cupbearer prayer** — Nehemiah shoots a prayer mid-conversation (v4). Brevity as piety.
- **Persian imperial support** — letters, military escort, wood from the royal park. Top-down state endorsement.
- **Sanballat the Horonite** — Elephantine papyri identify Sanballat as governor of Samaria (ca. 407 BC mentions his sons). Historical figure.
- **Tobiah the Ammonite** — powerful Transjordanian family; later Tobiads archive. In-laws among Jerusalem priesthood (13:4).
- **Geshem the Arab** — possibly "Gashmu" in inscriptions of Dedan; Arabian chieftain under Persian suzerainty.
- **Nighttime wall-inspection** — strategic discretion. Nehemiah surveys without alerting opponents.
- **Valley Gate, Dragon Spring, Gate of the Ash Heaps** — southeastern/southern circuit; water-sources and refuse disposal area.
- **Response "no share, no right, no memorial"** (v20) — legal language excluding the three opponents from Jerusalem's community-life, rights, and inheritance.
