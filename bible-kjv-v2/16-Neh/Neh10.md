## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v28, 29 the Hebrew has the article, תּוֹרַת הָאֱלֹהִים, עֶבֶד־הָאֱלֹהִים "the torah / the servant of the Elohim".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, and the "and" before a name in v9, 26 stays.
- **Names** — the list is given as the Hebrew prints it; nothing is added or levelled with the lists of Neh 12 or Ezra 2. In v19 the written text (ketiv) has נובי *Nobai*, the reading tradition (qere) נֵיבָי *Nebai*; the qere is followed.
- **"Now those that sealed were" → "And on the sealed ones"** (v1) — Hebrew וְעַל הַחֲתוּמִים, "and upon the sealed (documents)", continuing 9:38 ("on the sealed document"): the names stood on the sealed writings. No verb "sealed" is in the Hebrew. **"the Tirshatha" kept** — Hebrew הַתִּרְשָׁתָא, a Persian title (perhaps "the one to be revered"); a governor is meant, but it is kept apart from פֶּחָה "governor" (as Ezra 2:63; Neh 8:9).
- **"these were the priests" → "these are the priests"** (v8) — Hebrew אֵלֶּה הַכֹּהֲנִים, no verb.
- **"The Levites" → "And the Levites: and Jeshua"** (v9) — Hebrew וְהַלְוִיִּם וְיֵשׁוּעַ.
- **"And the rest of the people"** (v28) — Hebrew וּשְׁאָר הָעָם, followed by the classes without "and". **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v28, 39) — Hebrew שׁוֹעֲרִים, from שַׁעַר "gate". **"Nethinims" → "Netinim"** — token `nethinim`. **"every one having knowledge, and having understanding" → "everyone who knew, who understood"** — Hebrew כֹּל יוֹדֵעַ מֵבִין, two participles without "and".
- **"clave to their brethren, their nobles" → "were holding fast to their brothers, their nobles"** (v29) — Hebrew מַחֲזִיקִים עַל, a participle (חָזַק hifil "take hold"). The subject is v28; the sentence runs on without a main verb in the past. **"entered into a curse, and into an oath" → "were entering into a curse and into an oath"** — Hebrew בָּאִים בְּאָלָה וּבִשְׁבוּעָה, a participle; אָלָה "curse" (the oath that calls a curse on the breaker), beside שְׁבוּעָה "oath". **"God's law, which was given by Moses" → "the torah of Elohim that was given by the hand of Moses"** — Hebrew בְּיַד. **"to observe and do" → "to keep and to do"** — Hebrew לִשְׁמוֹר וְלַעֲשׂוֹת. **"the LORD our Lord"** — Hebrew יְהוָה אֲדֹנֵינוּ, "our lord" (אָדוֹן with a suffix; not the divine title אֲדֹנָי). **"judgments" → "ordinances"** — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּטִים.
- **"And that we would not give" → "and that we will not give"** (v30) — Hebrew וַאֲשֶׁר לֹא־נִתֵּן, the content of the oath in the first person; no quotation marks are set, as the Hebrew passes into "we" without a verb of saying (v30–39). **"the people of the land" → "the peoples of the land"** (v30, 31) — Hebrew עַמֵּי הָאָרֶץ, "peoples" plural, "land" singular (v28 has "the peoples of the lands").
- **"bring ware or any victuals" → "bring the wares and any grain"** (v31) — Hebrew הַמַּקָּחוֹת (things bought, from לָקַח "take") וְכָל־שֶׁבֶר "and any grain" (what is bought as a ration). **"we would not buy it of them" → "we will not take from them"** — Hebrew לֹא־נִקַּח, "take", that is, buy. **"we would leave the seventh year" → "we will abandon the seventh year"** — Hebrew וְנִטֹּשׁ אֶת־הַשָּׁנָה הַשְּׁבִיעִית, the verb of Exod 23:11 ("abandon it"): the land left to itself in the seventh year. **"the exaction of every debt" → "the loan of every hand"** — Hebrew וּמַשָּׁא כָל־יָד, as Deut 15:2 מַשֵּׁה יָדוֹ "the loan of his hand"; here spelled with א (the same letters as מַשָּׂא "burden"): the debts released in the seventh year.
- **"we made ordinances for us, to charge ourselves" → "we made commandments stand upon ourselves, to put upon ourselves"** (v32) — Hebrew וְהֶעֱמַדְנוּ עָלֵינוּ מִצְוֹת לָתֵת עָלֵינוּ, literally "made commandments stand upon us, to give upon us". **"yearly with the third part of a shekel" → "a third of the shekel in the year"** — Hebrew שְׁלִשִׁית הַשֶּׁקֶל בַּשָּׁנָה; the shekel is a weight, not a coin (Exod 30:13 has "half the shekel").
- **"the shewbread" → "the bread of the arrangement"** (v33) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַמַּעֲרֶכֶת, as 1 Chr 9:32. **"the continual meat offering" → "the continual grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחַת הַתָּמִיד. **"of the sabbaths, of the new moons, for the set feasts" → "the Shabbats, the new moons, for the appointed times"** — Hebrew הַשַּׁבָּתוֹת הֶחֳדָשִׁים לַמּוֹעֲדִים, without "of" or "and": whether the Shabbats and new moons go with "the continual burnt offering" is not marked. מוֹעֲדִים "appointed times", not "feasts". **"holy things"** — Hebrew הַקֳּדָשִׁים. **"for all the work" → "and all the work"** — Hebrew וְכֹל.
- **"we cast the lots … for the wood offering" → "the lots we cast … for the offering of the wood"** (v34) — Hebrew הִפַּלְנוּ, literally "made fall"; קֻרְבַּן הָעֵצִים, קָרְבָּן "offering" (what is brought near). **"after the houses of our fathers" → "by the house of our fathers"** — Hebrew לְבֵית־אֲבֹתֵינוּ, singular. **"at times appointed" → "at fixed times"** — Hebrew לְעִתִּים מְזֻמָּנִים, from זָמַן "fix a time"; another word than מוֹעֵד "appointed time" (v33). **"to burn"** — Hebrew לְבַעֵר.
- **"And to bring"** (v35) — Hebrew וּלְהָבִיא continues the infinitives of v29 and v34; "We obligated ourselves" is not in the Hebrew. **"the firstfruits of all fruit of all trees" → "the firstfruits of all the fruit of every tree"**.
- **"of our cattle" → "of our beasts"; "the firstlings of our herds and of our flocks" → "the firstborn of our herds and of our flocks"** (v36) — Hebrew בְּהֶמְתֵּנוּ; בְּכוֹרֵי, the same word as "firstborn" before; בָּקָר "herd", צֹאן "flock". **"to bring"** — Hebrew לְהָבִיא, kept.
- **"the firstfruits of our dough, and our offerings" → "the first of our dough and our contributions"** (v37) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית עֲרִיסֹתֵינוּ וּתְרוּמֹתֵינוּ, as Num 15:20 ("the first of your dough … a contribution"); עֲרִיסָה is "dough" or coarse meal (uncertain). **"of wine and of oil" → "new wine and fresh oil"** — Hebrew תִּירוֹשׁ וְיִצְהָר. **"tithes" → "tithe"**, the due of the law (v37, 38) — Hebrew מַעְשַׂר "tenth". **"that the same Levites might have the tithes" → "and they, the Levites, are the ones who take the tithe"** — Hebrew וְהֵם הַלְוִיִּם הַמְעַשְּׂרִים, a piel participle of עִשֵּׂר "tithe": here of receiving it. **"in all the cities of our tillage" → "of our service"** — Hebrew עָרֵי עֲבֹדָתֵנוּ, עֲבֹדָה "service, work" (of the ground "work": the farming towns are meant).
- **"the son of Aaron … when the Levites take tithes" → "the son of Aaron … when the Levites take the tithe"** (v38) — Hebrew בַּעְשֵׂר הַלְוִיִּם. **"the tithe of the tithes" → "the tithe of the tithe"** — Hebrew מַעֲשַׂר הַמַּעֲשֵׂר, as Num 18:26. **"into the treasure house" → "to the house of the treasury"** — Hebrew לְבֵית הָאוֹצָר.
- **"the children of Israel and the children of Levi" → "the sons of Israel and the sons of Levi"** (v39) — Hebrew בְנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל וּבְנֵי הַלֵּוִי, "the Levi" with the article (the tribe). **"the offering of the corn" → "the contribution of the grain"** — Hebrew תְּרוּמַת הַדָּגָן. **"vessels" → "utensils"** — Hebrew כְּלֵי הַמִּקְדָּשׁ. **"and we will not forsake"** — Hebrew וְלֹא נַעֲזֹב.

### Notes

- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew Bible begins this chapter with our 9:38; our 10:1–39 are Hebrew 10:2–40.
- **Signatories** — Nehemiah and Zedekiah (v1); twenty-one names closed by "these are the priests" (v2–8); seventeen Levites (v9–13); forty-four heads of the people (v14–27): eighty-four names.
- **Nehemiah first** — governor heads the list.
- **Four commitments** — (1) no intermarriage with the peoples of the land; (2) Shabbat observance + the seventh year (land and loans); (3) third-shekel for the house; (4) firstfruits, firstborn, and tithes regularly.
- **Third of a shekel** (v32) — Exod 30:13 has half a shekel; the Hebrew gives no reason for the difference.
- **Wood offering** (v34) — altar-wood supply by lot; ongoing fuel for continual offerings.
- **Tithe of the tithe** (v38) — as Num 18:26: the Levites give a tenth of what they receive to the house.
- **"We will not forsake the house of our *Elohim*"** (v39) — summative pledge; taken up in 13:11.
