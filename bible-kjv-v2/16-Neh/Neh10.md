## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v28, 39).
- **"ware or any victuals" → "wares or any grain"** (v31) — *maqqachot ve-kol-shever*.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v33) — *minchah*.
- **"shewbread" → "bread of the Presence"** (v33).
- **"set feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (v33) — *mo'adim*.
- **"curse" → "imprecation"** (v29) — *alah*; covenant self-curse.
- **"dough" → "coarse meal"** (v37) — *arisot*; grain flour.

### Notes
- **Covenant signatories** — 21 priests (v2-8), 17 Levites (v9-13), 44 heads of the people (v14-27); with Nehemiah and Zedekiah (v1), 84 named signatories.
- **Nehemiah first** — governor heads the list.
- **Four commitments** — (1) no intermarriage with gentiles; (2) Sabbath observance + debt release (7th year); (3) third-shekel temple tax; (4) firstfruits, firstborn, and tithes regularly.
- **Third-shekel tax** (v32) — reduction from Exod 30:13's half-shekel; post-exilic economic realities.
- **Wood offering** — altar-wood supply by lottery; ongoing fuel for continual offerings.
- **Tithe-of-tithe** (v38) — Levites' portion from all tithes; Levites redirect 10% to priests.
- **"We will not forsake the house of our *Elohim*"** — summative pledge; public commitment to temple-centered piety.
