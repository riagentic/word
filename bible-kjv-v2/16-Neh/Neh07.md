## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v2 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"children" → "sons"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי, as Ezra 2. In the list it names the members of a clan or the people of a town ("the sons of Gibeon", "the sons of Jericho"); אַנְשֵׁי "the men of" (v26–33) is another word and is kept apart.
- **Figures** (throughout) — written in words, in the English order, as Ezra 2; the Hebrew prints them often in the order "seventy and two", once "thirty and five hundred" (v70), and with רִבּוֹא / רִבּוֹת "ten thousand(s)" (v66, 71, 72). Names and figures are translated as Nehemiah prints them; where the Hebrew of v6–73 is the same as Ezra 2:1–70, the wording of Ezra 2 is used. Nothing is levelled between the two lists.
- **"Now it came to pass, when the wall was built, and I had set up the doors … That I gave … charge over Jerusalem" → "And it came to pass, when the wall was built, and I set up the doors, and the gatekeepers … were appointed, and I gave … command over Jerusalem"** (v1–2) — Hebrew וַיְהִי כַּאֲשֶׁר נִבְנְתָה הַחוֹמָה followed by a chain of four wayyiqtol verbs (וָאַעֲמִיד … וַיִּפָּקְדוּ … וָאֲצַוֶּה): where the main clause begins (with "I set up", or with "I gave command") the Hebrew does not mark, and the chain of "and" is kept. הֶעֱמִיד of doors "set up" (as 3:1; 6:1); פָּקַד nifal "be appointed"; שֹׁעֲרִים "gatekeepers" (KJV "porters"). וָאֲצַוֶּה … עַל, literally "and I commanded … over": put them in command (no "charge" or "gave … charge" in the word).
- **"the ruler of the palace" → "the commander of the citadel"** (v2) — שַׂר הַבִּירָה: בִּירָה "citadel" (as 2:8); שַׂר is the one word for "prince" and "commander": of the head of a fortress "commander".
- **"a faithful man" → "as a man of truth"** (v2) — Hebrew כְּאִישׁ אֱמֶת: the כְּ "as" (= truly such a man) and the noun אֱמֶת "truth" are kept.
- **"Let not … be opened until the sun be hot; and while they stand by, let them shut the doors, and bar them: and appoint watches … every one in his watch, and every one to be over against his house" → "shall not be opened until the heat of the sun; and while they are standing, let them shut the doors, and bolt them; and set up watches … each man in his watch, and each man opposite his house"** (v3) — the qere וָאֹמַר "and I said" is translated; the ketiv ויאמר reads "and he said". לֹא יִפָּתְחוּ "shall not be opened"; חֹם הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ "the heat of the sun". וְעַד הֵם עֹמְדִים "and while they are standing": who stands (the gatekeepers at their post, or the townsmen still about) is not said; "guard" is not in the Hebrew. יָגִיפוּ "let them shut" is followed by the imperative וֶאֱחֹזוּ "and take hold" = bolt ("them" supplied); וְהַעֲמֵיד is an infinitive absolute with the force of a command, the same verb as "set up" in v1; מִשְׁמָר / מִשְׁמָרוֹת "watch / watches" (as 1 Chr 26).
- **"large and great … and the houses were not builded" → "wide on both hands and great … and there were no houses built"** (v4) — רַחֲבַת יָדַיִם, literally "wide of hands" (as Gen 34:21); וְאֵין בָּתִּים בְּנוּיִם "and there were no houses built" ("rebuilt" is not in the word).
- **"put into mine heart to gather together the nobles, and the rulers … that they might be reckoned by genealogy. And I found a register of the genealogy of them which came up" → "put it into my heart, and I gathered the nobles and the prefects … to be enrolled by genealogy. And I found the book of the genealogy of those who went up"** (v5) — the Hebrew has no object after נָתַן אֶל־לִבִּי "put into my heart" ("it" supplied) and goes on with וָאֶקְבְּצָה "and I gathered". חֹרִים "nobles"; סְגָנִים "prefects" (not "rulers", "officials"); הִתְיַחֵשׂ "be enrolled by genealogy"; סֵפֶר הַיַּחַשׂ "the book of the genealogy"; הָעוֹלִים "those who went up" (as v6).
- **"that went up out of the captivity, of those that had been carried away, whom … had carried away, and came again" → "who went up from the captivity of the exiles, whom … had carried into exile, and they returned"** (v6) — as Ezra 2:1: שְׁבִי הַגּוֹלָה "the captivity of the exiles"; הֶגְלָה "carry into exile". Nehemiah has no "And" at the head, no "to Babylon" after "carried into exile", and "and to Judah" (וְלִיהוּדָה; Ezra "and Judah").
- **"Who came with Zerubbabel … Baanah. The number, I say, of the men … was this" → "those who came with Zerubbabel … Baanah. The number of the men …"** (v7) — Hebrew הַבָּאִים "those who came" (Ezra 2:2 אֲשֶׁר־בָּאוּ "who came"); twelve names, without "and" before Baanah; Nehemiah has Azariah, Raamiah, Nahamani, Mispereth, Nehum where Ezra has Seraiah, Reelaiah, —, Mispar, Rehum. "I say … was this" is not in the Hebrew.
- **Differences from Ezra 2 in v8–60** — figures: Arah 652 (Ezra 775), Pahath-moab 2,818 (2,812), Zattu 845 (945), Binnui 648 (Bani 642), Bebai 628 (623), Azgad 2,322 (1,222), Adonikam 667 (666), Bigvai 2,067 (2,056), Adin 655 (454), Hashum 328 (223), Bezai 324 (323), Bethlehem and Netophah together 188 (Ezra 123 + 56), Bethel and the Ai 123 (223), Lod, Hadid and Ono 721 (725), Senaah 3,930 (3,630), singers 148 (128), gatekeepers 138 (139, with "in all"). Names: Hariph (Ezra Jorah), Gibeon (Gibbar), "the men of" Beth-azmaveth, Kiriath-jearim, the Ramah (Ezra "the sons of" Azmaveth, Kiriath-arim, the Ramah), "the other Nebo" (Ezra "Nebo"); Magbish (Ezra 2:30) is not in Nehemiah. Nehemiah has "and" before Joab (v11; Ezra none) and "the gatekeepers" without "the sons of" (v45). Among the temple servants Nehemiah has no Akkub, Hagab, Asnah (Ezra 2:45, 46, 50); Sia (סִיעָא; Ezra Siaha), Bazlith (בַּצְלִית; Ezra Bazluth), Sophereth without the article (סוֹפֶרֶת; Ezra Hassophereth), Perida (Ezra Peruda), Jaala (יַעְלָא; Ezra Jaalah), Amon (Ezra Ami).
- **"Ramah … Ai" → "the Ramah … the Ai"** (v30, 32) — both names have the article in Hebrew (הָרָמָה "the height"; הָעָי "the ruin"), as Ezra 2:26, 28.
- **"of the other Nebo"** (v33) — Hebrew נְבוֹ אַחֵר; the next verse has "the other Elam" (עֵילָם אַחֵר) with the same figure as v12.
- **"of Kadmiel, and of the children of Hodevah" → "of Kadmiel, of the sons of Hodevah"** (v43) — Hebrew לְקַדְמִיאֵל לִבְנֵי לְהוֹדְוָה, with no "and" (Ezra 2:40 "and Kadmiel"); the name is printed with a second לְ, לְהוֹדְוָה (Ezra הוֹדַוְיָה Hodaviah).
- **"Lebana … Hagaba"** (v48) — the Hebrew writes both names with ה, as Ezra 2:45 (לְבָנָה, חֲגָבָה); the original form follows the Hebrew.
- **"Nephishesim"** (v52) — the qere נְפִישְׁסִים "Nefishesim" is translated; the ketiv reads Nefushesim (נפושסים). Ezra 2:50 has Nefusim.
- **"Telmelah, Telharesha … but they could not shew their father's house, nor their seed" → "Tel-melah, Tel-harsha … and Immer; and they could not tell their fathers' house and their seed"** (v61) — as Ezra 2:59; Nehemiah has "and" before Immer and Addon for Ezra's Addan. הִגִּיד "tell"; זֶרַע "seed".
- **"And of the priests"** (v63) — Hebrew וּמִן־הַכֹּהֲנִים (Ezra 2:61 "of the sons of the priests"). "who took a wife from the daughters of" as Ezra.
- **"sought their register among those that were reckoned by genealogy, but it was not found: therefore were they, as polluted, put from the priesthood" → "sought their writing, those enrolled by genealogy, and it was not found; and they were defiled away from the priesthood"** (v64) — as Ezra 2:62; Nehemiah has the singular נִמְצָא "it was not found" (Ezra plural). וַיְגֹאֲלוּ מִן, a pual of גָּאַל II "be defiled" (not גָּאַל "redeem"): treated as unclean and so put out of the priesthood.
- **"the Tirshatha … the most holy things, till there stood up a priest with Urim and Thummim" → "the Tirshatha … the most holy until a priest should stand for Urim and Thummim"** (v65; also v70) — Hebrew הַתִּרְשָׁתָא, a Persian title (perhaps "the one to be revered"); a governor is meant (Nehemiah bears it, 8:9; 10:1), but it is kept apart from פֶּחָה "governor" (the earlier text had "governor"). קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים, literally "the holiness of the holinesses" ("things" is not in the Hebrew). עָמַד "stand"; לְאוּרִים וְתוּמִּים "for Urim and Thummim", one לְ (Ezra 2:63 has it twice).
- **"The whole congregation together" → "The whole assembly as one"** (v66) — קָהָל "assembly"; כְּאֶחָד "as one". The figures given (v8–62) add up to 31,089; the Hebrew does not explain the difference.
- **"their manservants and their maidservants … singing men and singing women" → "their slaves and their slave women … male singers and female singers"** (v67) — עֶבֶד "slave", אָמָה "slave woman", as Ezra 2:65; Nehemiah has 245 singers (Ezra 200).
- **"Their camels … six thousand seven hundred and twenty asses" → "camels … donkeys …"** (v69) — the Hebrew has no "their" with either (גְּמַלִּים, חֲמֹרִים; Ezra 2:67 has "their camels").
- **"And some of the chief of the fathers gave unto the work. The Tirshatha gave to the treasure a thousand drams of gold, fifty basons, five hundred and thirty priests' garments" → "And some of the heads of the fathers gave to the work. The Tirshatha gave to the treasury a thousand drachmas of gold, fifty bowls, five hundred and thirty tunics of priests"** (v70) — וּמִקְצָת "and some of" (literally "from the end of"; v71 has the plain מִן "of" for the same sense); רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת "the heads of the fathers"; אוֹצָר "treasury"; דַּרְכְּמוֹנִים "drachmas", as Ezra 2:69 (a coin; Greek drachma or Persian daric is not certain); מִזְרָק "bowl" (the vessel for dashing blood); כְּתֹנֶת "tunic". The gifts of v70–72 are not those of Ezra 2:68–69 (Ezra: 61,000 drachmas, 5,000 minas, 100 tunics, given by the heads alone); Nehemiah lists three givers.
- **"two thousand and two hundred pound of silver" → "two thousand two hundred minas of silver"** (v71, 72) — מָנֶה "mina" (a weight of silver).
- **"the rest of the people" → "the remnant of the people"** (v72) — שְׁאֵרִית "remnant" (what is left).
- **"So the priests … dwelt in their cities; and when the seventh month came" → "And the priests … dwelt in their cities. And the seventh month arrived"** (v73) — the chain of "and" is kept; the order (gatekeepers, singers, some of the people, Nethinim) and "and all Israel dwelt in their cities" are Nehemiah's (Ezra 2:70 differs). וַיִּגַּע "arrived", as Ezra 3:1, whose opening Nehemiah has here with "in their cities" (Ezra "in the cities").
- **v68 omitted** — KJV v68 ("Their horses, seven hundred thirty and six: their mules, two hundred forty and five") is not in the Leningrad Codex (and the editions that follow it): its 7:68 is our v69. The MT decides, so no verse is supplied from Ezra 2:66. Other Hebrew manuscripts and printed editions have it (from memory, not seen with the tools); it was probably lost because v67 and v68 both end in "two hundred and forty-five".

### Notes
- **Hebrew numbering** — the Leningrad Codex has no verse for our v68; our 7:69–73 are Hebrew 7:68–72.
- **Parallel to Ezra 2** — v6–73a are the same list as Ezra 2:1–70, with the differences in names and figures listed in Changes; the gifts (v70–72) differ in givers and amounts.
- **Total 42,360** — the listed figures add up to 31,089 (Ezra 2: 29,818); the total is the same in both books.
- **Hananiah** — "as a man of truth and feared *Elohim* more than many"; Nehemiah's explicit praise.
- **Gate-opening** — gates kept shut until the sun was hot (well into the morning), a security measure.
- **Empty Jerusalem** (v4) — the walls enclose mostly empty space; the resettlement follows in ch 11.
- **Register as basis for repopulation** — the link to the Ezra 2 list grounds the Persian-era community in pre-exilic continuity.
- **Seventh month** — Tishri; sets up ch 8's Torah-reading and Feast of Booths.
