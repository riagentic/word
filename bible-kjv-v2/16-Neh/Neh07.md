## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v1, 45, 73).
- **"ruler of the palace" → "commander of the citadel"** (v2) — *sar ha-birah*.
- **"basons" → "basins"** (v70).

### Notes
- **Parallel to Ezra 2** — substantially similar list. Differences: number of singers (148 here vs 128 in Ezra), various numerical shifts, extra names. Two independent copies of same underlying register.
- **Hananiah** — literally "faithful man and feared *Elohim* more than many"; Nehemiah's explicit praise.
- **Gate-opening after sunrise** — security measure; gates closed at night and opened only when daylight allowed for inspection.
- **Empty Jerusalem** (v4) — city walls enclose mostly empty space; resettlement program will follow (ch 11).
- **Register as basis for repopulation** — link to Ezra 2 list grounds Persian-era community in pre-exilic continuity.
- **Seventh month** — Tishri; sets up ch 8's Torah-reading and Feast of Booths.
