# 1 Samuel 29

[1] Now the Philistines gathered all their armies at Aphek, and the Israelites encamped by the spring that is in Jezreel.

[2] The lords of the Philistines were passing on by hundreds and by thousands, and David and his men were passing on in the rear with Achish.

[3] Then the commanders of the Philistines said, "What are these Hebrews doing here?" Achish said to the commanders of the Philistines, "Is this not David, the servant of Saul king of Israel, who has been with me these days, or rather these years, and I have found no fault in him since the day he fell to me until now?"

[4] But the commanders of the Philistines were angry with him; the commanders of the Philistines said to him, "Send the man back, that he may return to his place where you have assigned him; let him not go down with us to battle, lest in the battle he become our adversary. For how could this fellow reconcile himself to his master? With the heads of these men, would he not?

[5] Is this not the David of whom they sang in dances, saying:

'Saul has struck down his thousands,
and David his ten thousands'?"

[6] Then Achish called David and said to him, "As Yahweh lives, you have been upright, and your going out and your coming in with me in the army is good in my eyes; for I have not found evil in you from the day of your coming to me to this day. Yet the lords do not favor you.

[7] Now therefore return and go peaceably, that you not do what is evil in the eyes of the lords of the Philistines."

[8] David said to Achish, "But what have I done? What have you found in your servant, from the day I was with you until this day, that I may not go and fight against the enemies of my lord the king?"

[9] Achish answered David, "I know that you are as good in my eyes as a messenger of *Elohim*. Nevertheless the commanders of the Philistines have said, 'He shall not go up with us to the battle.'

[10] Now therefore rise early in the morning with your master's servants who came with you, and rise early in the morning, and as soon as you have light, depart."

[11] So David and his men rose early to depart in the morning, to return to the land of the Philistines. And the Philistines went up to Jezreel.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"rereward" → "rear"** (v2) — modernized.
- **"angel of God" → "messenger of *Elohim*"** (v9) — *mal'akh Elohim*.
- **"princes" → "commanders"** (v3, 4, 9).

### Notes
- **Providential delivery** — David is spared the dilemma of fighting Israel. The Philistine commanders' suspicion saves David from impossible choice.
- **David's acting** (v8) — pretends indignation. His true feelings hidden; probably relief.
- **"As good as a messenger of *Elohim*"** (v9) — Achish's high praise. The irony: David is in fact deceiving him.
- **Structural counterpoint** — while Saul seeks the dead Samuel in desperation (ch 28), David is freed from the fight by Philistine politics (ch 29). Yahweh's providence operates behind both scenes.
