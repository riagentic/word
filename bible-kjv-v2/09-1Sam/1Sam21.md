# 1 Samuel 21

[1] David came to Nob, to Ahimelech the priest. Ahimelech came trembling to meet David and said to him, "Why are you alone, and no man with you?"

[2] David said to Ahimelech the priest, "The king has charged me with a matter and has said to me, 'Let no one know anything of the matter on which I am sending you, or with which I have commissioned you.' I have appointed the young men to a certain place.

[3] Now therefore, what do you have on hand? Give me five loaves of bread, or whatever is found."

[4] The priest answered David, "I have no common bread on hand, but there is holy bread — if only the young men have kept themselves from women."

[5] David answered the priest, "Truly women have been kept from us, as always when I go on an expedition; the vessels of the young men are holy even when it is a common journey; how much more today when their vessels are sanctified?"

[6] So the priest gave him holy bread, for there was no bread there but the bread of the Presence, which had been removed from before Yahweh, to be replaced by hot bread on the day it was taken away.

[7] Now a certain man of the servants of Saul was there that day, detained before Yahweh; his name was Doeg the Edomite, the chief of the shepherds who belonged to Saul.

[8] David said to Ahimelech, "Is there not here under your hand a spear or a sword? For I did not bring my sword or my weapons with me, because the king's matter was urgent."

[9] The priest said, "The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom you struck down in the Valley of Elah — behold, it is here wrapped in a cloth behind the ephod. If you will take it, take it, for there is no other except that one here." And David said, "There is none like it; give it to me."

[10] David rose and fled that day from Saul and went to Achish the king of Gath.

[11] The servants of Achish said to him, "Is not this David the king of the land? Did they not sing to one another of him in dances, saying:

'Saul has struck down his thousands,
and David his ten thousands'?"

[12] David took these words to heart and was very much afraid of Achish the king of Gath.

[13] So he changed his behavior before them and feigned himself mad in their hands; he made marks on the doors of the gate and let his saliva run down his beard.

[14] Achish said to his servants, "Behold, you see the man is mad; why then have you brought him to me?

[15] Do I lack madmen, that you have brought this fellow to play the madman in my presence? Shall this fellow come into my house?"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"hallowed bread" → "holy bread / bread of the Presence"** (v4, 6) — *lechem ha-panim*.
- **"scrabbled" → "made marks"** (v13) — archaic.
- **"sore afraid" → "very much afraid"** (v12).

### Notes
- **David at Nob** — priestly city. Ahimelech (grandson of Eli) is priest. David deceives him about Saul's mission.
- **Bread of the Presence** (v6) — showbread; priestly food. Jesus cites this episode (Mk 2:25–26) as precedent for David eating consecrated bread. Need overrides ritual strictness.
- **Doeg the Edomite** (v7) — ominous observer. Will report to Saul (ch 22) and massacre the priests.
- **Goliath's sword** — David receives his old trophy-weapon. Symbolic re-arming.
- **David at Gath** — fleeing to the Philistines. Recognized and feared.
- **Feigned madness** — David's ploy. Ps 34 traditionally ascribed to this episode. David escapes again.
