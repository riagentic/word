# 1 Samuel 22

[1] David departed from there and escaped to the cave of Adullam. When his brothers and all his father's house heard of it, they went down to him there.

[2] Everyone who was in distress, everyone who was in debt, and everyone who was bitter of soul gathered themselves to him; he became commander over them; there were with him about four hundred men.

[3] From there David went to Mizpeh of Moab, and he said to the king of Moab, "Please let my father and my mother come to you, until I know what *Elohim* will do for me."

[4] He left them with the king of Moab, and they stayed with him all the while that David was in the stronghold.

[5] The prophet Gad said to David, "Do not stay in the stronghold; depart, and go into the land of Judah." So David departed and came into the forest of Hereth.

[6] Saul heard that David had been located, with the men who were with him. Now Saul was sitting at Gibeah under the tamarisk tree on the height, with his spear in his hand, and all his servants were standing about him.

[7] Saul said to his servants who stood about him, "Hear now, you Benjamites! Will the son of Jesse give to every one of you fields and vineyards? Will he make all of you commanders of thousands and commanders of hundreds,

[8] that all of you have conspired against me? There is no one who tells me when my son makes a covenant with the son of Jesse; none of you is sorry for me, or tells me that my son has stirred up my servant against me, to lie in wait as at this day."

[9] Then Doeg the Edomite, who was standing beside the servants of Saul, answered, "I saw the son of Jesse come to Nob, to Ahimelech son of Ahitub.

[10] He inquired of Yahweh for him, gave him provisions, and gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine."

[11] Then the king sent to summon Ahimelech son of Ahitub the priest, and all his father's house — the priests who were at Nob — and they all came to the king.

[12] Saul said, "Hear now, son of Ahitub." He answered, "Here I am, my lord."

[13] Saul said to him, "Why have you conspired against me, you and the son of Jesse, in that you have given him bread and a sword and have inquired of *Elohim* for him, so that he should rise against me, to lie in wait, as at this day?"

[14] Ahimelech answered the king, "And who among all your servants is as faithful as David — the king's son-in-law, the captain of your bodyguard, honored in your house?

[15] Did I begin today to inquire of *Elohim* for him? Far be it from me! Let not the king impute anything to his servant or to all the house of my father; for your servant has known nothing of all this, great or small."

[16] The king said, "You shall surely die, Ahimelech — you and all your father's house."

[17] The king said to the guards who stood about him, "Turn and kill the priests of Yahweh, for their hand also is with David, and because they knew he was fleeing and did not disclose it to me." But the servants of the king would not raise their hands to strike the priests of Yahweh.

[18] The king said to Doeg, "You turn and fall upon the priests." So Doeg the Edomite turned and fell upon the priests, and killed on that day eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.

[19] Nob, the city of the priests, he struck with the edge of the sword — both men and women, children and nursing infants, oxen, donkeys, and sheep — with the edge of the sword.

[20] But one of the sons of Ahimelech son of Ahitub, named Abiathar, escaped and fled after David.

[21] Abiathar told David that Saul had killed the priests of Yahweh.

[22] David said to Abiathar, "I knew on that day, when Doeg the Edomite was there, that he would surely tell Saul. I have occasioned the death of all the persons of your father's house.

[23] Stay with me; do not fear; for he who seeks my life seeks your life; with me you shall be in safekeeping."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"captain over them" → "commander over them"** (v2).
- **"hold" → "stronghold"** (v4, 5).
- **"footmen" → "guards"** (v17) — Hebrew *ratzim*.

### Notes
- **David gathers 400 men** (v1–2) — social outcasts and discontented. His kingdom-in-exile begins.
- **Adullam cave** — refuge near the Philistine border. Psalms 57, 142 traditionally tied here.
- **Parents to Moab** — David has Moabite ancestry through Ruth; appeals to that connection.
- **Gad the prophet** (v5) — David's court prophet from here forward (cf. 2 Sam 24).
- **Saul's paranoid speech** (v6–8) — addresses Benjamites (his own tribe). Accuses a conspiracy that doesn't exist.
- **Doeg's betrayal** (v9–19) — the silent witness becomes the slaughter-agent.
- **Massacre at Nob** — 85 priests + entire city. Fulfillment of Yahweh's word against Eli's house (1 Sam 2:31–36, 3:11–14), though through Saul's sin. Abiathar alone escapes.
- **Fulfillment pattern** — the oracle against Eli's line realized, but by means that make Saul responsible.
