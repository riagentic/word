## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept. **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of וַ / וְ is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v4, 6, 7, 8).
- **"fetched up … brought it into the house of Abinadab in the hill, and sanctified Eleazar his son" → "brought up … brought it to the house of Abinadab on the hill; and Eleazar his son they sanctified"** (v1) — Hebrew בַּגִּבְעָה "on the hill"; the same word is the name Gibeah, and it may be a place name here ("in the Gibeah"). The object stands first. קִדְּשׁוּ is "sanctified" (made holy, set apart). **"keep"** — Hebrew שָׁמַר.
- **"while the ark abode in Kirjathjearim, that the time was long; for it was twenty years" → "from the day that the ark dwelt in Kiriath-jearim, that the days multiplied, and they were twenty years"** (v2) — Hebrew מִיּוֹם שֶׁבֶת הָאָרוֹן, literally "from the day of the dwelling of the ark"; וַיִּרְבּוּ הַיָּמִים "and the days became many". There is no "for". **"lamented after Yahweh"** — Hebrew וַיִּנָּהוּ, a form found only here; taken from נָהָה "wail, lament". The meaning with "after" is not certain ("mourned and sought after", "followed with lamenting"); the Greek has "looked toward" (from memory).
- **"spake … saying" → "said … saying"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … לֵאמֹר. **"If ye do return unto the LORD with all your hearts" → "If with all your heart you are returning to Yahweh"** — the Hebrew order; "heart" is singular (also in "establish your heart"); שָׁבִים is a participle. **"put away the strange gods" → "remove the foreign gods"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew הָסִירוּ; אֱלֹהֵי הַנֵּכָר, literally "the gods of foreignness". **"and Ashtaroth"** stands after "from your midst", as in the Hebrew. **"prepare your hearts unto the LORD" → "establish your heart toward Yahweh"** — Hebrew הָכִינוּ: make firm, set, prepare. **"serve him only: and he will deliver you" → "serve him alone, that he may deliver you"** — וְיַצֵּל is a wish form (jussive): "and let him deliver you".
- **"Baalim and Ashtaroth" → "the Baals and the Ashtaroth"** (v4) — both are plurals with the article: the local forms of Baal and of the goddess Ashtoreth (Astarte).
- **"to Mizpeh"** (v5, 6, 7) — Hebrew הַמִּצְפָּתָה "to the Mizpah" (the name means "the watchtower" and always has the article in this chapter). **"I will pray for you" → "I will pray on your behalf"** (v5, 9) — Hebrew בַּעַד.
- **"gathered together to Mizpeh"** (v6, 7) — two forms of one verb: nifal in v6 ("gathered"), hitpael in v7 ("gathered themselves"). "It" after "poured" is supplied. The pouring out of water before Yahweh is not explained in the text.
- **"went up against Israel" → "went up to Israel"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶל "to, toward". **"heard it, they were afraid of the Philistines" → "heard, and they feared because of the Philistines"** — no object; מִפְּנֵי is literally "from the face of".
- **"Cease not to cry unto the LORD our God for us" → "Do not be silent from us, from crying out to Yahweh our Elohim"** (v8) — Hebrew אַל־תַּחֲרֵשׁ מִמֶּנּוּ מִזְּעֹק, word for word: "do not be silent (turning) away from us, away from crying out", that is, do not leave off crying out for us. **"that he will save us" → "that he may save us"** — a wish form, "and let him save us".
- **"a sucking lamb, and offered it for a burnt offering wholly" → "one suckling lamb and offered it up as a burnt offering, whole"** (v9) — Hebrew טְלֵה חָלָב אֶחָד, literally "one lamb of milk". "Offered it up" is the reading tradition (qere וַיַּעֲלֵהוּ); the written text (ketiv) has "and he offered up" without "it". כָּלִיל is "whole", an offering wholly given. **"cried unto the LORD for Israel; and the LORD heard him" → "cried out to Yahweh on behalf of Israel, and Yahweh answered him"** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲנֵהוּ "answered".
- **"as Samuel was offering" → "And Samuel was offering up"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיְהִי with a participle; the clauses are joined by "and". **"thundered with a great thunder" → "thundered with a loud voice"** — Hebrew בְּקוֹל־גָּדוֹל, literally "with a great voice"; קוֹל is "voice, sound", also of thunder. **"discomfited them" → "threw them into confusion"** — Hebrew וַיְהֻמֵּם (Exod 14:24; Josh 10:10). **"they were smitten"** — Hebrew וַיִּנָּגְפוּ, from נָגַף, another verb than "struck down" (הִכָּה) in v11.
- **"until they came under Bethcar" → "as far as below Beth-car"** (v11) — the Hebrew has no verb.
- **"a stone … between Mizpeh and Shen" → "one stone … between Mizpah and Shen"** (v12) — Hebrew אֶבֶן אַחַת; both names have the article: "the Mizpah" and "the Shen" (הַשֵּׁן "the tooth", a crag). **"Ebenezer"** — Hebrew אֶבֶן הָעָזֶר *even ha-ezer*, "the stone of the help"; the meaning is not written into the verse. A place of this name is the camp of Israel in 4:1; 5:1. **"Hitherto hath the LORD helped us" → "As far as here Yahweh has helped us"** — Hebrew עַד־הֵנָּה, "up to here", of place or of time ("until now").
- **"were subdued, and they came no more into the coast of Israel" → "were brought low, and they came no more again into the border of Israel"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּכָּנְעוּ; וְלֹא־יָסְפוּ עוֹד לָבוֹא, literally "and they did not add any more to come". גְּבוּל is "border", also the land within it (v14).
- **"were restored to Israel, from Ekron even unto Gath; and the coasts thereof did Israel deliver" → "returned to Israel, from Ekron and as far as Gath; and their border Israel delivered"** (v14) — Hebrew וַתָּשֹׁבְנָה "returned" (active). **"peace" → "*shalom*"** — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם. **"the Amorites" → "the Amorite"** — Hebrew singular with the article.
- **"he went from year to year in circuit to Bethel, and Gilgal, and Mizpeh, and judged Israel in all those places" → "he would go year by year and go around to Bethel and Gilgal and Mizpah, and he would judge Israel at all these places"** (v16) — Hebrew forms of repeated action; מִדֵּי שָׁנָה בְּשָׁנָה, literally "as often as year in year"; סָבַב "go around". Gilgal and Mizpah have the article. אֵת כָּל־הַמְּקוֹמוֹת הָאֵלֶּה stands without a preposition: "at" is supplied (the אֵת may also mark "all these places" as a second object).
- **"his return was to Ramah; for there was his house"** (v17) — Hebrew וּתְשֻׁבָתוֹ הָרָמָתָה, "and his return (was) to the Ramah".

### Notes
- **Kiriath-jearim** — the ark stays 20 years here (possibly more; ~70 years total until David moves it). Abinadab's family guardianship.
- **National repentance** — Samuel's reform: remove the foreign gods, serve Yahweh alone. Unlike the talismanic use of the ark in ch 4, this is covenantal return.
- **Water poured out** (v6) — unusual act. Symbolic: emptying self, offering like a libation.
- **Yahweh's thunder** (v10) — divine intervention parallels Deborah-Barak: Yahweh's own battle against chariot-powers. Thunderstorm / tactical disruption.
- **Ebenezer stone** (v12) — reverses the earlier Ebenezer defeat (4:1). Same name, opposite outcome. "As far as here Yahweh has helped us" — Robert Robinson's hymn (1758) "Come Thou Fount of Every Blessing" references this.
- **Samuel's judgeship** (v15–17) — judge + prophet + priest. Samuel circuits three cities yearly and returns to Ramah. Last judge; transition to monarchy impending.
