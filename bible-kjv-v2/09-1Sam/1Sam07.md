# 1 Samuel 7

[1] The men of Kiriath-jearim came and brought up the ark of Yahweh; they brought it into the house of Abinadab on the hill, and they consecrated Eleazar his son to guard the ark of Yahweh.

[2] From the day the ark remained at Kiriath-jearim, the time was long, for it was twenty years; and all the house of Israel lamented after Yahweh.

[3] Samuel spoke to all the house of Israel, saying, "If with all your heart you are returning to Yahweh, then put away the foreign gods and the Ashtaroth from among you, and direct your hearts to Yahweh, and serve him only; then he will deliver you out of the hand of the Philistines."

[4] So the children of Israel put away the Baals and the Ashtaroth, and served Yahweh alone.

[5] Samuel said, "Gather all Israel at Mizpah, and I will pray for you to Yahweh."

[6] They gathered at Mizpah and drew water and poured it out before Yahweh; they fasted on that day and said there, "We have sinned against Yahweh." And Samuel judged the children of Israel at Mizpah.

[7] When the Philistines heard that the children of Israel had gathered at Mizpah, the lords of the Philistines went up against Israel. When the children of Israel heard of it, they were afraid of the Philistines.

[8] The children of Israel said to Samuel, "Do not cease to cry out to Yahweh our *Elohim* for us, that he may save us from the hand of the Philistines."

[9] Samuel took a suckling lamb and offered it as a whole burnt offering to Yahweh; Samuel cried out to Yahweh for Israel, and Yahweh answered him.

[10] As Samuel was offering up the burnt offering, the Philistines drew near to battle against Israel; but Yahweh thundered with a loud thunder that day against the Philistines and threw them into panic, so that they were struck down before Israel.

[11] The men of Israel went out from Mizpah and pursued the Philistines, striking them down as far as below Beth-car.

[12] Then Samuel took a stone and set it between Mizpah and Shen, and called its name Ebenezer ("Stone of Help"), saying, "Up to this place Yahweh has helped us."

[13] So the Philistines were subdued, and they did not come again into the territory of Israel; the hand of Yahweh was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.

[14] The cities that the Philistines had taken from Israel were restored to Israel, from Ekron to Gath; and Israel delivered their territory out of the hand of the Philistines. There was peace also between Israel and the Amorites.

[15] Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.

[16] He went year by year on a circuit to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah, and he judged Israel in all these places.

[17] Then he would return to Ramah, for his home was there; there also he judged Israel, and there he built an altar to Yahweh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"discomfited them" → "threw them into panic"** (v10).
- **"strange gods" → "foreign gods"** (v3).
- **"coasts" → "territory"** (v13, 14).

### Notes
- **Kiriath-jearim** — the ark stays 20 years here (possibly more; ~70 years total until David moves it). Abinadab's family guardianship.
- **National repentance** — Samuel's reform: put away foreign gods, serve Yahweh alone. Unlike the talismanic use of the ark in ch 4, this is covenantal return.
- **Water poured out** (v6) — unusual act. Symbolic: emptying self, offering like a libation.
- **Yahweh's thunder** (v10) — divine intervention parallels Deborah-Barak: Yahweh's own battle against chariot-powers. Thunderstorm / tactical disruption.
- **Ebenezer stone** (v12) — reverses the earlier Ebenezer defeat (4:1). Same name, opposite outcome. "Up to this place Yahweh has helped us" — Robert Robinson's hymn (1758) "Come Thou Fount of Every Blessing" references this.
- **Samuel's judgeship** (v15–17) — judge + prophet + priest. Samuel circuits four cities yearly. Last judge; transition to monarchy impending.
