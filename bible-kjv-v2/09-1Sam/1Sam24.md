# 1 Samuel 24

[1] And it came to pass, when ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ returned from after the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, that they told him, saying, "Behold, David is in the wilderness of ⟦en-gedi|Ein Gedi|Engedi⟧."

[2] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ took three thousand chosen men out of all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he went to seek David and his men, facing the rocks of the wild goats.

[3] And he came to the folds of the flock by the way, and a cave was there, and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ went in to cover his feet. And David and his men were sitting in the innermost parts of the cave.

[4] And the men of David said to him, "Behold, the day of which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to you, 'Behold, I am giving your enemy into your hand, and you shall do to him as is good in your eyes.'" And David arose and cut off the corner of the robe that was ⟦saul|Shaul's|Saul's⟧, softly.

[5] And it came to pass afterward that the heart of David struck him, because he had cut off the corner that was ⟦saul|Shaul's|Saul's⟧.

[6] And he said to his men, "Far be it from me, from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that I should do this thing to my lord, to the ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to stretch out my hand against him, for he is the ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[7] And David tore his men apart with the words, and he did not let them rise against ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ arose from the cave and went on the way.

[8] And David arose afterward and went out from the cave, and he called after ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, saying, "My lord the king!" And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ looked behind him, and David bowed his head, face to the earth, and bowed down.

[9] And David said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "Why do you hear the words of man, saying, 'Behold, David is seeking your evil'?

[10] Behold, this day your eyes have seen that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ gave you today into my hand in the cave, and one said to kill you, but you were spared, and I said, 'I will not stretch out my hand against my lord, for he is the ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'

[11] And, my father, see, yes, see the corner of your robe in my hand. For in that I cut off the corner of your robe and did not kill you, know and see that there is no evil or transgression in my hand, and I have not sinned against you; and you are lying in wait for my soul, to take it.

[12] May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ judge between me and you, and may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ avenge me on you; and my hand shall not be on you.

[13] As the proverb of the ancient one says, 'From the wicked goes out wickedness'; and my hand shall not be on you.

[14] After whom has the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ come out? After whom are you pursuing? After a dead dog! After one flea!

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be judge, and he shall judge between me and you; and may he see and contend my case and judge me out of your hand."

[16] And it came to pass, when David finished speaking these words to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Is this your voice, my son David?" And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ lifted up his voice and wept.

[17] And he said to David, "You are more righteous than I, for you have dealt me the good, and I have dealt you the evil.

[18] And you have told today what good you have done with me: that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shut me up into your hand, and you did not kill me.

[19] And when a man finds his enemy, will he send him away on a good way? And may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ repay you good for this day, for what you have done to me.

[20] And now, behold, I know that you shall surely reign, and the kingdom of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall stand in your hand.

[21] And now, swear to me by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that you will not cut off my seed after me, and that you will not destroy my name from the house of my father."

[22] And David swore to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ went to his house, and David and his men went up to the stronghold.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"from following the Philistines" → "from after the Philistines"** (v1) — Hebrew מֵאַחֲרֵי.
- **"upon the rocks of the wild goats" → "facing the rocks of the wild goats"** (v2) — Hebrew עַל־פְּנֵי, "on the face of, in front of". "Chosen men" is singular in the Hebrew (אִישׁ בָּחוּר).
- **"sheepcotes" → "the folds of the flock"** (v3) — Hebrew גִּדְרוֹת הַצֹּאן, literally "the walls of the flock".
- **"to cover his feet" kept** (v3) — Hebrew לְהָסֵךְ אֶת־רַגְלָיו, word for word. The sense is "to relieve himself" (so also Judg 3:24): the robe covers the feet of one who crouches.
- **"in the sides of the cave" → "in the innermost parts of the cave"** (v3) — Hebrew יַרְכְּתֵי, the far end (from יָרֵךְ "thigh, flank"). **"remained" → "were sitting"** — Hebrew יֹשְׁבִים.
- **"Behold the day of which the LORD said unto thee"** (v4) — Hebrew הִנֵּה הַיּוֹם אֲשֶׁר־אָמַר יְהוָה אֵלֶיךָ; it may also be read "Behold, this is the day when Yahweh says to you". No such word of Yahweh is told before. **"I will deliver" → "I am giving"** — Hebrew נֹתֵן, a participle. **"thine enemy"** — the written text (ketiv) has the plural "your enemies"; the reading tradition (qere) has "your enemy". **"as it shall seem good unto thee" → "as is good in your eyes"**.
- **"the skirt of Saul's robe" → "the corner of the robe that was Saul's"** (v4, 5, 11) — Hebrew כְּנַף־הַמְּעִיל, literally "the wing of the robe": its edge or corner. In v5 the Hebrew has only "the corner that was Saul's"; "robe" is not repeated (some versions add it: from memory). **"privily" → "softly"** — Hebrew בַּלָּט, "softly, quietly" (Judg 4:21), at the end of the sentence.
- **"David's heart smote him" → "the heart of David struck him"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ, from הִכָּה "strike".
- **"The LORD forbid that I should do" → "Far be it from me, from Yahweh, that I should do"** (v6) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לִּי מֵיהוָה אִם־אֶעֱשֶׂה, literally "a profane thing to me, from Yahweh, if I do": the form of an oath. "From Yahweh" says before whom, or by whose will, it would be profane.
- **"the LORD's anointed" → "the *mashiach* (anointed) of Yahweh"** (v6, 10) — Hebrew מְשִׁיחַ יְהוָה, "the anointed one of Yahweh": the king anointed at Yahweh's word (10:1).
- **"to stretch forth mine hand against him" kept as "to stretch out my hand against him"** (v6, 10) — Hebrew שָׁלַח יָד בְּ.
- **"stayed his servants with these words" → "tore his men apart with the words"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיְשַׁסַּע, from שָׁסַע "tear open, cleave": a strong word, not used of speech elsewhere. It is difficult; "rebuked sharply" or "scattered" are the senses given. "Servants" is not in the Hebrew (אֲנָשָׁיו "his men"). **"suffered them not" → "did not let them"** — Hebrew לֹא נְתָנָם, "did not give them".
- **"stooped with his face to the earth, and bowed himself" → "bowed his head, face to the earth, and bowed down"** (v8) — Hebrew קָדַד "bow the head"; אַפַּיִם "nostrils, face"; הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down".
- **"men's words" → "the words of man"** (v9) — Hebrew אָדָם, "man, mankind", singular. **"seeketh thy hurt" → "is seeking your evil"** — Hebrew רָעָתֶךָ, "your evil": evil for you.
- **"some bade me kill thee: but mine eye spared thee" → "one said to kill you, but you were spared"** (v10) — the second verb has no subject in the Hebrew, so none is supplied: "you were spared" (literally "and it pitied you"). Hebrew וְאָמַר לַהֲרָגֲךָ וַתָּחָס עָלֶיךָ. The first verb is "and he said" with no subject named ("one said"; or "and he [Yahweh] said"). The second is feminine, "and she / it pitied you", with no subject: "my eye" is commonly understood, as in the phrase "let not your eye pity" (Deut 7:16), and some versions have "I spared you" (from memory). The verse keeps the Hebrew as it stands and supplies nothing.
- **"Moreover, my father, see, yea, see"** (v11) — Hebrew וְאָבִי רְאֵה גַּם רְאֵה. **"thou huntest my soul" → "you are lying in wait for my soul"** — Hebrew צֹדֶה, from צָדָה "lie in wait"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"The LORD judge … and the LORD avenge me of thee" → "May Yahweh judge … may Yahweh avenge me on you"** (v12) — the Hebrew forms can be a wish or a statement ("Yahweh will judge"); מִמֶּךָּ is "from you".
- **"the proverb of the ancients" → "the proverb of the ancient one"** (v13) — Hebrew מְשַׁל הַקַּדְמֹנִי, singular ("the ancient one", or as a collective "the ancients"). The saying has three words in the Hebrew: מֵרְשָׁעִים יֵצֵא רֶשַׁע, "from wicked ones goes out wickedness".
- **"after whom dost thou pursue?" → "After whom are you pursuing?"** (v14) — Hebrew רֹדֵף אַחֲרֵי; "after" stands four times. **"after a flea" → "After one flea"** — Hebrew פַּרְעֹשׁ אֶחָד.
- **"plead my cause, and deliver me out of thine hand" → "contend my case and judge me out of your hand"** (v15) — Hebrew וְיָרֵב אֶת־רִיבִי, verb and noun of one root; וְיִשְׁפְּטֵנִי מִיָּדֶךָ, "judge me from your hand": do me right and so free me. "Judge" (noun) is דַּיָּן, from another root than "judge" (שָׁפַט).
- **"thou hast rewarded me good, whereas I have rewarded thee evil" → "you have dealt me the good, and I have dealt you the evil"** (v17) — Hebrew גָּמַל "deal"; both nouns have the article.
- **"thou hast shewed this day" → "you have told today"** (v18) — Hebrew הִגַּדְתָּ. **"had delivered me into thine hand" → "shut me up into your hand"** — Hebrew סִגְּרַנִי, the root of "hand over" in 23:11–12, 20.
- **"will he let him go well away?" → "will he send him away on a good way?"** (v19) — Hebrew וְשִׁלְּחוֹ בְּדֶרֶךְ טוֹבָה. **"reward thee good" → "repay you good"** — Hebrew שִׁלֵּם. "For this day, for what you have done to me" is literally "in place of this day that you have done to me".
- **"thou shalt surely be king" → "you shall surely reign"** (v20) — Hebrew מָלֹךְ תִּמְלוֹךְ, "reigning you shall reign". **"shall be established" → "shall stand"** — Hebrew קָמָה, "rise, stand".
- **"that thou wilt not cut off my seed"** (v21) — Hebrew אִם־תַּכְרִית … וְאִם־תַּשְׁמִיד, literally "if you cut off … and if you destroy": the form of an oath, meaning "you will not". "Seed" is זֶרַע.
- **"went home" → "went to his house"** (v22) — Hebrew אֶל־בֵּיתוֹ.

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew Bible this chapter is 24:2–23; Hebrew 24:1 is our 23:29.
- **Saul in the cave** — at En-gedi. David's men speak of a word of Yahweh; David will not stretch out his hand against the anointed of Yahweh.
- **"The anointed of Yahweh"** — Saul was anointed by Samuel (10:1); David was anointed too (16:13).
- **The corner of the robe** — compare 1 Sam 15:27–28, where the corner (כָּנָף) of Samuel's robe is torn and Samuel speaks of the kingdom torn from Saul.
- **"A dead dog, one flea"** (v14) — David's words about himself.
- **Saul's answer** (v16–21) — acknowledges David's righteousness and that he will reign. Asks an oath for his seed and his name.
- **The stronghold** (v22) — David does not go back with Saul.
