## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"strong holds" → "strongholds"** (v14, 19, 29) — modernized spelling.

### Notes
- **David saves Keilah** — David still fights Philistines, protecting Judahite cities even while hunted.
- **Ephod oracle** (v9–12) — David consults Yahweh through the ephod. Fleeing does not end his piety; he still seeks divine guidance.
- **"God has given him into my hand"** (v7) — Saul's religious conviction is backwards. He assumes his own desire matches Yahweh's — but the reverse is true.
- **Jonathan's encouragement** (v16–18) — the last meeting between Jonathan and David. Jonathan renews the covenant and accepts David's future kingship.
- **Ziphite betrayal** (v19–28) — locals offer to hand David over. Ps 54 is traditionally linked to this episode.
- **Philistine interruption** (v27) — Providential rescue. Saul forced to abandon David's pursuit.
