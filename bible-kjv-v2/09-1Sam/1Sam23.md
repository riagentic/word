# 1 Samuel 23

[1] And they told David, saying, "Behold, the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ are fighting against ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧, and they are pillaging the threshing floors."

[2] And David asked of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "Shall I go and strike these ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to David, "Go, and you shall strike the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and save ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧."

[5] And David and his men went to ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧, and he fought against the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ and drove away their livestock and struck them a great blow. And David saved those who dwelt in ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧.

[6] And it came to pass, when ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ the son of ⟦ahimelech|Achimelech|Ahimelech⟧ fled to David, to ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧, that an ephod came down in his hand.

[8] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ summoned all the people to war, to go down to ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧, to besiege David and his men.

[9] And David knew that it was against him that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was devising the evil, and he said to ⟦abiathar|Evyatar|Abiathar⟧ the priest, "Bring the ephod near."

[10] And David said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, your servant has surely heard that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ is seeking to come to ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧, to ruin the city for my sake.

[11] Will the lords of ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧ hand me over into his hand? Will ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ come down, as your servant has heard? ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, tell, please, your servant." And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "He will come down."

[12] And David said, "Will the lords of ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧ hand over me and my men into the hand of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "They will hand over."

[13] And David arose, and his men, about six hundred men, and they went out from ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧ and went about wherever they could go about. And it was told ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ that David had escaped from ⟦keilah|Qeilah|Keilah⟧, and he ceased to go out.

[14] And David dwelt in the wilderness, in the strongholds, and he dwelt in the hill country, in the wilderness of ⟦ziph|Zif|Ziph⟧. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ sought him all the days, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not give him into his hand.

[15] And David saw that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ had come out to seek his soul. And David was in the wilderness of ⟦ziph|Zif|Ziph⟧, in ⟦horesh|Choreshah|Horesh⟧.

[16] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ arose and went to David, to ⟦horesh|Choreshah|Horesh⟧, and he made his hand strong in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[17] And he said to him, "Do not fear, for the hand of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ my father will not find you, and you shall reign over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I shall be second to you; and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ my father also knows so."

[18] And the two of them cut a covenant before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And David dwelt in ⟦horesh|Choreshah|Horesh⟧, and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ went to his house.

[19] And ⟦ziphites|Zifim|Ziphites⟧ went up to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, to ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, saying, "Is not David concealing himself with us in the strongholds in ⟦horesh|Choreshah|Horesh⟧, on the hill of ⟦hachilah|Chachilah|Hachilah⟧, which is on the south of the ⟦jeshimon|Yeshimon|Jeshimon⟧?

[20] And now, according to all the desire of your soul, O king, to come down, come down; and ours it is to hand him over into the hand of the king."

[21] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Blessed are you by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for you have had pity on me.

[23] And see and know of all the hiding places where he hides himself, and return to me with what is sure, and I will go with you. And it shall be, if he is in the land, that I will search him out among all the thousands of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧."

[24] And they arose and went to ⟦ziph|Zif|Ziph⟧ before ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧. And David and his men were in the wilderness of Maon, in the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧, to the south of the ⟦jeshimon|Yeshimon|Jeshimon⟧.

[25] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and his men went to seek, and they told David, and he went down to the crag and dwelt in the wilderness of Maon. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ heard, and he pursued after David into the wilderness of Maon.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 10, 11, 14, 16) — Hebrew *Elohim*, without the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"they rob the threshingfloors" → "they are pillaging the threshing floors"** (v1) — Hebrew שֹׁסִים, from שָׁסָה "pillage".
- **"enquired of the LORD" → "asked of Yahweh"** (v2, 4) — Hebrew שָׁאַל בַּיהוָה, the plain verb "ask" (the root of the name Saul); the questions and the answers are kept word for word. **"smite" → "strike"** (v2, 5) — Hebrew הִכָּה בְּ, "strike among".
- **"against the armies of the Philistines" → "to the battle lines of the Philistines"** (v3) — Hebrew מַעַרְכוֹת, lines drawn up for battle (from עָרַךְ "arrange"); אֶל "to".
- **"asked … yet again"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיּוֹסֶף עוֹד … לִשְׁאֹל, literally "added yet to ask". **"I will deliver" → "I am giving"** — Hebrew נֹתֵן, a participle.
- **"brought away their cattle, and smote them with a great slaughter" → "drove away their livestock and struck them a great blow"** (v5) — Hebrew נָהַג "drive"; מִקְנֶה "livestock"; מַכָּה גְדוֹלָה "a great blow" (noun of הִכָּה "strike"). The verbs are singular: David is the subject.
- **"he came down with an ephod in his hand" → "an ephod came down in his hand"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵפוֹד יָרַד בְּיָדוֹ: the ephod is the subject. The verse is difficult in its order (Abiathar fled to David before Keilah, 22:20); it is kept as it stands.
- **"God hath delivered him into mine hand" → "Elohim has estranged him into my hand"** (v7) — Hebrew נִכַּר אֹתוֹ, from the root נכר "be foreign, strange": "has made him a stranger", that is, disowned him and given him up. The word is difficult. The Septuagint has "has sold" (מָכַר, two letters in another order; from memory). The Masoretic Text is translated.
- **"a town that hath gates and bars" → "a city of double doors and a bar"** (v7) — Hebrew דְּלָתַיִם, a dual ("two doors", the two leaves of the gate), and בְּרִיחַ, singular. **"he has shut himself in"** — Hebrew נִסְגַּר, the root of "hand over" in v11, 12, 20 (הִסְגִּיר, literally "shut up into the hand of").
- **"called all the people together" → "summoned all the people"** (v8) — Hebrew וַיְשַׁמַּע, "made hear".
- **"secretly practised mischief against him" → "it was against him that Saul was devising the evil"** (v9) — Hebrew מַחֲרִישׁ הָרָעָה, literally "ploughing / working the evil"; "secretly" is not said. "Against him" stands first.
- **"hath certainly heard" → "has surely heard"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׁמֹעַ שָׁמַע, "hearing has heard". **"to destroy the city for my sake" → "to ruin the city for my sake"** — Hebrew שִׁחֵת "ruin"; בַּעֲבוּרִי "for my sake, on my account". "Your servant" (v10, 11) is עַבְדְּךָ, "your slave".
- **"the men of Keilah" → "the lords of Keilah"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew בַּעֲלֵי קְעִילָה: בַּעַל is "owner, lord"; the town's men are meant.
- **"deliver me up" → "hand me over"; "They will deliver thee up" → "They will hand over"** (v11, 12, 20) — Hebrew הִסְגִּיר. The second answer is one word, יַסְגִּירוּ, without an object, as the first is one word, יֵרֵד "He will come down". The two questions of v11 are answered in the reverse order, and the first is asked again in v12.
- **"went whithersoever they could go" → "went about wherever they could go about"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּתְהַלְּכוּ בַּאֲשֶׁר יִתְהַלָּכוּ, literally "they walked about where they walked about". **"forbare to go forth" → "ceased to go out"** — Hebrew חָדַל "cease".
- **"abode … remained" → "dwelt … dwelt"** (v14, 18, 25, 29) — Hebrew יָשַׁב each time. **"in a mountain" → "in the hill country"** (v14) — Hebrew בָּהָר, "in the mountain". **"every day" → "all the days"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים.
- **"to seek his life" → "to seek his soul"** (v15) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ. "Saw" is the Masoretic pointing (וַיַּרְא); the same letters can be read "feared".
- **"in a wood" → "in Horesh"** (v15, 16, 18, 19) — Hebrew חֹרְשָׁה, with the article in v15, 18, 19: "the wood", taken as the name of a place.
- **"strengthened his hand in God" → "made his hand strong in Elohim"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיְחַזֵּק אֶת־יָדוֹ.
- **"thou shalt be king … I shall be next unto thee" → "you shall reign … I shall be second to you"** (v17) — Hebrew תִּמְלֹךְ "reign"; מִשְׁנֶה "second".
- **"made a covenant" → "cut a covenant"** (v18) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית.
- **"the Ziphites" → "Ziphites"** (v19) — no article in the Hebrew. **"hide himself" → "concealing himself"** — Hebrew מִסְתַּתֵּר (root סתר); "hides himself" in v23 is another root (חבא). **"on the south of Jeshimon"** (v19, 24) — Hebrew מִימִין / אֶל יְמִין, literally "on the right of / toward the right of"; הַיְשִׁימוֹן, with the article, is "the desert".
- **"according to all the desire of thy soul to come down"** (v20) — Hebrew לְכָל־אַוַּת נַפְשְׁךָ, kept. **"our part shall be to deliver him" → "ours it is to hand him over"** — Hebrew וְלָנוּ "and to us".
- **"Blessed be ye of the LORD; for ye have compassion on me" → "Blessed are you by Yahweh, for you have had pity on me"** (v21) — Hebrew בְּרוּכִים אַתֶּם לַיהוָה, literally "blessed are you to Yahweh"; חָמַל "have pity, spare".
- **"prepare yet, and know and see his place where his haunt is" → "make sure yet more, and know and see his place where his foot is"** (v22) — Hebrew הָכִינוּ "make firm, make ready"; רַגְלוֹ "his foot". **"he dealeth very subtilly" → "he is surely shrewd"** — Hebrew עָרוֹם יַעְרִם, "being shrewd he is shrewd". "It has been said to me" is literally "he said to me": the speaker is not named.
- **"come ye again to me with the certainty" → "return to me with what is sure"** (v23) — Hebrew אֶל־נָכוֹן, "to what is established". **"throughout all the thousands of Judah"** — Hebrew אַלְפֵי, "thousands": the clans.
- **"in the plain" → "in the Arabah"** (v24) — Hebrew הָעֲרָבָה.
- **"he came down into a rock" → "he went down to the crag"** (v25, 28) — Hebrew סֶלַע "crag". "To seek" has no object in the Hebrew.
- **"David made haste to get away for fear of Saul" → "David was hurrying in alarm to go from the face of Saul"** (v26) — Hebrew נֶחְפָּז "hurrying away in alarm"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of". **"compassed … round about to take them" → "were closing in around … to seize them"** — Hebrew עֹטְרִים אֶל, "encircling toward" (the verb is rare; elsewhere "crown, surround"); תָּפַשׂ "seize".
- **"Haste thee, and come; for the Philistines have invaded the land" → "Hasten and go, for the Philistines have made a raid on the land"** (v27) — Hebrew פָּשַׁט עַל "spread out upon, raid".
- **"went against the Philistines" → "went to meet the Philistines"** (v28) — Hebrew לִקְרַאת "to meet".
- **"Selahammahlekoth"** (v28) — Hebrew סֶלַע הַמַּחְלְקוֹת, "the crag of the divisions" (מַחֲלֹקֶת "division", from חָלַק "divide": where the two hosts parted) or "the crag of the smooth places / of slipping away" (another חָלַק, "be smooth"). The verse gives the name; it is not explained in the Hebrew.

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — our 23:29 is 24:1 in the Hebrew Bible.
- **David saves Keilah** — David fights Philistines for a town of Judah while he is hunted.
- **Ephod** (v6, 9–12) — David asks Yahweh through the ephod that Abiathar brought.
- **"Elohim has estranged him into my hand"** (v7) — Saul's words; v14 says, "Elohim did not give him into his hand".
- **Jonathan** (v16–18) — the last meeting between Jonathan and David that is told.
- **Ziphites** (v19–28) — the heading of Ps 54 names this episode.
- **Philistine raid** (v27) — Saul must give up the pursuit.
