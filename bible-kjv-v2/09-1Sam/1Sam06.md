# 1 Samuel 6

[1] And the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was in the field of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ seven months.

[2] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ called for the priests and for the diviners, saying, "What shall we do to the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧? Make known to us with what we shall send it away to its place."

[3] And they said, "If you are sending away the ark of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, do not send it away empty, for you shall surely return to him a guilt offering. Then you will be healed, and it will be known to you why his hand does not depart from you."

[4] And they said, "What is the guilt offering that we shall return to him?" And they said, "The number of the lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧: five tumors of gold and five mice of gold; for one plague was on them all and on your lords.

[5] And you shall make images of your tumors and images of your mice that ruin the land, and you shall give glory to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; perhaps he will lighten his hand from upon you and from upon your gods and from upon your land.

[6] And why should you make your heart heavy, as the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ and ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ made their heart heavy? Was it not when he dealt ruthlessly with them that they let them go, and they went?

[7] And now, take and make one new wagon, and two nursing cows on which no yoke has gone up; and you shall bind the cows to the wagon and return their young from after them to the house.

[8] And you shall take the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and place it on the wagon; and the articles of gold that you have returned to him as a guilt offering you shall put in the box at its side; and you shall send it away, and it shall go.

[9] And you shall see: if it goes up by the way of its own border, to ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧, it is he who has done to us this great evil; and if not, then we shall know that it was not his hand that touched us; it was a chance that happened to us."

[10] And the men did so; and they took two nursing cows and bound them to the wagon, and their young they shut up in the house.

[11] And they put the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on the wagon, and the box and the mice of gold and the images of their tumors.

[12] And the cows went straight on the way, on the way to ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧; on one highway they went, going and lowing, and they did not turn aside to the right or to the left; and the lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ were going after them as far as the border of ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧.

[13] And ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧ were reaping the harvest of wheat in the valley; and they lifted up their eyes and saw the ark, and they rejoiced to see.

[14] And the wagon came to the field of ⟦joshua-bethshemite|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the ⟦beth-shemite|Beit-HaShimshi|Beth-shemite⟧ and stood there, and a great stone was there; and they split the wood of the wagon, and the cows they offered up as a burnt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] And the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ brought down the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the box that was with it, in which were the articles of gold, and they put them on the great stone; and the men of ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧ offered up burnt offerings and sacrificed sacrifices on that day to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[16] And the five lords of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ saw, and they returned to ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ on that day.

[17] And these are the tumors of gold that the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ returned as a guilt offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: for Ashdod one, for ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧ one, for ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧ one, for ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ one, for ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧ one.

[18] And the mice of gold were the number of all the cities of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ belonging to the five lords, from the fortified city to the village of the open country, and as far as the great meadow on which they set down the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — to this day in the field of ⟦joshua-bethshemite|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the ⟦beth-shemite|Beit-HaShimshi|Beth-shemite⟧.

[19] And he struck among the men of ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧, because they looked upon the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and he struck down among the people seventy men, fifty thousand men. And the people mourned, because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had struck among the people a great blow.

[20] And the men of ⟦beth-shemesh|Beit Shemesh|Beth-shemesh⟧ said, "Who is able to stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, this holy ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? And to whom shall he go up from upon us?"

[21] And they sent messengers to the inhabitants of ⟦kiriath-jearim|Qiryat Yearim|Kiriath-jearim⟧, saying, "The ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ have returned the ark of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; come down, bring it up to yourselves."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept. In v20 the Hebrew has the article: יְהוָה הָאֱלֹהִים הַקָּדוֹשׁ הַזֶּה "Yahweh, this holy Elohim". **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of וַ / וְ is kept.
- **"in the country of the Philistines" → "in the field of the Philistines"** (v1) — Hebrew בִּשְׂדֵה: שָׂדֶה is "field", here the open land, the territory.
- **"what shall we do to the ark … tell us wherewith we shall send it" → "What shall we do to the ark … Make known to us with what we shall send it away"** (v2) — Hebrew הוֹדִעֻנוּ "make us know"; בַּמֶּה "with what" (also "how"). שִׁלַּח (piel) is "send away / let go" throughout the chapter (v2, 3, 6, 8), the word of "Let my people go"; in v6 it is "let go".
- **"If ye send away" → "If you are sending away"** (v3) — a participle without a pronoun. **"in any wise return him a trespass offering" → "you shall surely return to him a guilt offering"** (v3, 4, 8, 17) — Hebrew הָשֵׁב תָּשִׁיבוּ, literally "returning you shall return": the infinitive with its verb. אָשָׁם *asham* is "guilt", and the offering that makes good a guilt (Lev 5:15); here it is a payment of gold, not a sacrifice. The verb הֵשִׁיב "return, give back" runs through the chapter (v3, 4, 7, 8, 17, 21).
- **"why his hand is not removed from you" → "why his hand does not depart from you"** (v3) — Hebrew לֹא־תָסוּר, an imperfect.
- **"emerods" → "tumors"** (v4, 5, 11, 17) — in v4, 5 the written text (ketiv) has עפלים *ofalim*, "swellings" (from עֹפֶל "a mound, a swelling"), and the reading tradition (qere) has טְחֹרִים *techorim*, the word that is itself written in v11, 17. Both are rendered by the one word "tumors" (as Deut 28:27). What sickness is meant is not certain: swellings, boils, or hemorrhoids (so the KJV "emerods").
- **"Five golden emerods, and five golden mice, according to the number of the lords" → "The number of the lords of the Philistines: five tumors of gold and five mice of gold"** (v4) — the Hebrew order; "according to" is not written. "Lords" is סְרָנִים, a word used only of the five rulers of the Philistines (not אָדוֹן). עַכְבָּר is "mouse" (Lev 11:29), any small rodent. **"one plague was on you all" → "one plague was on them all"** — the Masoretic Text has לְכֻלָּם "to them all", and then "your lords"; the old versions have "you all" (from memory). The Hebrew has no verb. מַגֵּפָה is literally "a blow".
- **"that mar the land" → "that ruin the land"** (v5) — Hebrew הַמַּשְׁחִיתִם, the "ruin" verb of Gen 6:13. **"from off you, and from off your gods" → "from upon you and from upon your gods"** — Hebrew מֵעַל three times. אֱלֹהֵיכֶם may be "your gods" or "your god" (Dagon, 5:7): the form is the same.
- **"Wherefore then do ye harden your hearts, as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened their hearts?" → "why should you make your heart heavy … made their heart heavy"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּבֵּד (piel), from כָּבֵד "be heavy", the word of Exod 8:15, 32; 9:34 and the root of "glory" (כָּבוֹד, v5); "heart" is singular both times. "The Egyptians" is מִצְרַיִם "Egypt" with a plural verb.
- **"when he had wrought wonderfully among them, did they not let the people go" → "Was it not when he dealt ruthlessly with them that they let them go"** (v6) — Hebrew הִתְעַלֵּל בְּ, the word of Exod 10:2: to deal wantonly with, make sport of. The Hebrew has "them", not "the people".
- **"make a new cart, and take two milch kine … tie the kine to the cart, and bring their calves home from them" → "take and make one new wagon, and two nursing cows … bind the cows to the wagon and return their young from after them to the house"** (v7, 10) — Hebrew קְחוּ וַעֲשׂוּ "take and make", with both objects after the two verbs. עֲגָלָה is "wagon". עָלוֹת are cows that are giving suck. בְּנֵיהֶם is literally "their sons". "No yoke has gone up" — the verb עָלָה "go up", which sounds like עָלוֹת and עֹל "yoke".
- **"lay it upon the cart" → "place it on the wagon"** (v8) — Hebrew נָתַן "give, place"; "put" in v8b, 11, 15 is שִׂים. **"the jewels of gold" → "the articles of gold"** (v8, 15) — Hebrew כְּלֵי הַזָּהָב: כְּלִי is any made article. **"in a coffer by the side thereof" → "in the box at its side"** (v8, 11, 15) — Hebrew אַרְגַּז, found only in this chapter: a box or chest (or a saddle-bag); the meaning is not certain. It has the article.
- **"if it goeth up by the way of his own coast" → "if it goes up by the way of its own border"** (v9) — Hebrew גְּבוּל "border", also the land within it. **"this great evil"** — Hebrew רָעָה. **"it was not his hand that smote us: it was a chance that happened to us" → "it was not his hand that touched us; it was a chance that happened to us"** — Hebrew נָגְעָה בָּנוּ "touched us"; the noun "plague" (נֶגַע) is from this root. מִקְרֶה is "a happening, a chance".
- **"shut up their calves at home" → "their young they shut up in the house"** (v10) — the Hebrew order.
- **"and the coffer with the mice of gold" → "and the box and the mice of gold and the images of their tumors"** (v11) — three objects, each with וְאֵת "and".
- **"took the straight way to the way of Bethshemesh, and went along the highway, lowing as they went" → "went straight on the way, on the way to Beth-shemesh; on one highway they went, going and lowing"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִשַּׁרְנָה "they were straight"; בִּמְסִלָּה אַחַת "on one highway"; הָלְכוּ הָלֹךְ וְגָעוֹ, literally "they went, going and lowing".
- **"they of Bethshemesh" → "Beth-shemesh"** (v13) — the Hebrew names the town, with a plural verb. **"rejoiced to see it" → "rejoiced to see"** — no object in the Hebrew.
- **"stood there, where there was a great stone" → "stood there, and a great stone was there"** (v14). **"clave" → "split"**. **"offered the kine a burnt offering" → "the cows they offered up as a burnt offering"** — Hebrew הֶעֱלוּ עֹלָה, literally "made go up a going-up".
- **"took down" → "brought down"** (v15) — Hebrew הוֹרִידוּ. "Them" after "put" is supplied.
- **"when the five lords … had seen it" → "the five lords … saw"** (v16) — no object.
- **"both of fenced cities, and of country villages, even unto the great stone of Abel" → "from the fortified city to the village of the open country, and as far as the great meadow"** (v18) — Hebrew מֵעִיר מִבְצָר וְעַד כֹּפֶר הַפְּרָזִי, singulars. The Masoretic Text then reads וְעַד אָבֵל הַגְּדוֹלָה, "and as far as the great *avel*": אָבֵל is known from place names (Abel-shittim, Abel-meholah) and is taken as "meadow"; the adjective is feminine, the noun elsewhere is not. The verse is difficult. The Greek and the Targum have "stone" (אֶבֶן for אָבֵל; from memory), as in v14, 15, and the KJV puts both in ("the great stone of Abel"). The Masoretic Text is translated; "stone" is not in the verse. **"which stone remaineth unto this day" → "— to this day in the field of Joshua"** — the Hebrew has no verb and no "stone".
- **"he smote the men of Bethshemesh, because they had looked into the ark" → "he struck among the men of Beth-shemesh, because they looked upon the ark"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיַּךְ בְּ "struck among"; no subject is named until the end of the verse. רָאוּ בַּאֲרוֹן is "saw / looked at the ark" (רָאָה with בְּ: to look on, to gaze at); "into" is one way of taking the בְּ, not the only one.
- **"fifty thousand and threescore and ten men" → "seventy men, fifty thousand men"** (v19) — Hebrew שִׁבְעִים אִישׁ חֲמִשִּׁים אֶלֶף אִישׁ, word for word; the two numbers stand side by side without "and". How they belong together is not said, and the verse does not explain it. Josephus has only "seventy" (from memory). **"with a great slaughter" → "a great blow"** — Hebrew מַכָּה, the noun of "strike".
- **"to whom shall he go up from us?" → "to whom shall he go up from upon us?"** (v20) — Hebrew מֵעָלֵינוּ. "He" may be Yahweh or the ark ("it"): the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"fetch it up to you" → "bring it up to yourselves"** (v21) — Hebrew הַעֲלוּ.

### Notes
- **Philistine divination** (v2) — they seek priestly/diviner counsel. Ironically more theologically astute than Israel's elders in ch 4.
- **Test of the ark** (v7–9) — unyoked cows, separated from their young. Their natural instinct would be to return to them; their straight march to Beth-shemesh thus signals Yahweh's direction.
- **Tumors and mice of gold** — plague motifs: rodent-carrier suggestion implicit. The Philistine priests recognize divine causation.
- **Beth-shemesh** — priestly city (Josh 21:16), near the Philistine border. First Israelite reception of the ark.
- **The blow at Beth-shemesh** (v19) — the men of Beth-shemesh "looked upon" the ark; death follows. Num 4:20 warned the Kohathites against seeing the holy things uncovered. The holy remains dangerous.
- **Ark ends at Kiriath-jearim** — the ark stays there for ~20 years (cf. 7:2) until David retrieves it (2 Sam 6).
