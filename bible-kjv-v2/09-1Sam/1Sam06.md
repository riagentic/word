## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"trespass offering" → "guilt offering"** (v3, 4, 8, 17) — *asham*.
- **"emerods" → "tumors"** (throughout) — as in ch 5.
- **"jewels of gold" → "objects of gold"** (v8, 15) — Hebrew *kelei zahav*.
- **"kine" → "cows"** (v7, 10, 12, 14).
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v18).
- **"fifty thousand and threescore and ten men" → "seventy men — of fifty thousand men"** (v19) — Hebrew שִׁבְעִים אִישׁ חֲמִשִּׁים אֶלֶף אִישׁ *shiv'im ish chamishim elef ish*. Awkward construction; likely glossed or corrupted. Either 70 among 50,000, or 50,070 total. Context supports small-number; 70 is more plausible given Beth-shemesh's size.

### Notes
- **Philistine divination** (v2) — they seek priestly/diviner counsel. Ironically more theologically astute than Israel's elders in ch 4.
- **Test of the ark** (v7–9) — unyoked cows, separated from calves. Their natural instinct would be to return to their calves; their straight march to Beth-shemesh thus signals Yahweh's direction.
- **Golden tumors and mice** — plague motifs: rodent-carrier suggestion implicit. The Philistine priests recognize divine causation.
- **Beth-shemesh** — priestly city (Josh 21:16), near the Philistine border. First Israelite reception of the ark.
- **Fatal curiosity** (v19) — Beth-shemesh men "looked into" the ark; fatality follows. Num 4:20 warned Kohathites against uncovered-ark sight. The holy remains dangerous.
- **Ark ends at Kiriath-jearim** — the ark stays there for ~20 years (cf. 7:2) until David retrieves it (2 Sam 6).
