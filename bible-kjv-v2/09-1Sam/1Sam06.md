# 1 Samuel 6

[1] The ark of Yahweh was in the country of the Philistines seven months.

[2] The Philistines called for the priests and the diviners, saying, "What shall we do with the ark of Yahweh? Tell us how we should send it to its place."

[3] They said, "If you send away the ark of the *Elohim* of Israel, do not send it empty; by all means return to him a guilt offering. Then you will be healed, and it will be known to you why his hand has not turned away from you."

[4] They said, "What guilt offering shall we return to him?" They answered, "Five golden tumors and five golden mice, according to the number of the lords of the Philistines, for the same plague was on all of you and on your lords.

[5] So you shall make images of your tumors and images of your mice that ravage the land, and give glory to the *Elohim* of Israel; perhaps he will lighten his hand from you, your gods, and your land.

[6] Why then do you harden your hearts as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened their hearts? When he had worked wonderfully against them, did they not let the people go, and they departed?

[7] Now therefore, prepare a new cart and take two milk cows on which no yoke has come; tie the cows to the cart, but take their calves home away from them.

[8] Take the ark of Yahweh and lay it on the cart; and put in a box beside it the objects of gold that you are returning to him as a guilt offering. Then send it off and let it go.

[9] Watch: if it goes up the way to its own territory, to Beth-shemesh, then it is he who has done us this great harm; but if not, then we will know that it was not his hand that struck us — that it happened to us by chance."

[10] The men did so: they took two milk cows and tied them to the cart, and shut up their calves at home.

[11] They put the ark of Yahweh on the cart, with the box of the gold mice and the images of their tumors.

[12] The cows went straight in the direction of Beth-shemesh, along one highway, lowing as they went; they turned neither to the right nor to the left; the lords of the Philistines went after them to the border of Beth-shemesh.

[13] The people of Beth-shemesh were reaping their wheat harvest in the valley; when they lifted up their eyes and saw the ark, they rejoiced to see it.

[14] The cart came into the field of Joshua the Beth-shemite and stopped there. A large stone was there; they split up the wood of the cart and offered the cows as a burnt offering to Yahweh.

[15] The Levites took down the ark of Yahweh and the box with it, in which were the objects of gold, and placed them on the large stone; and the men of Beth-shemesh offered burnt offerings and sacrificed sacrifices that day to Yahweh.

[16] When the five lords of the Philistines saw it, they returned that day to Ekron.

[17] These are the gold tumors that the Philistines returned as a guilt offering to Yahweh: for Ashdod, one; for Gaza, one; for Ashkelon, one; for Gath, one; for Ekron, one;

[18] and the gold mice according to the number of all the cities of the Philistines belonging to the five lords — both fortified cities and country villages — as far as the great stone at which they set down the ark of Yahweh; the stone remains to this day in the field of Joshua the Beth-shemite.

[19] He struck down some of the men of Beth-shemesh, because they had looked into the ark of Yahweh; he struck down of the people seventy men — of fifty thousand men. The people mourned because Yahweh had struck the people with a great blow.

[20] The men of Beth-shemesh said, "Who is able to stand before Yahweh, this holy *Elohim*? To whom shall he go up from us?"

[21] They sent messengers to the inhabitants of Kiriath-jearim, saying, "The Philistines have returned the ark of Yahweh; come down and bring it up to yourselves."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"trespass offering" → "guilt offering"** (v3, 4, 8, 17) — *asham*.
- **"emerods" → "tumors"** (throughout) — as in ch 5.
- **"jewels of gold" → "objects of gold"** (v8, 15) — Hebrew *kelei zahav*.
- **"kine" → "cows"** (v7, 10, 12, 14).
- **"fenced" → "fortified"** (v18).
- **"fifty thousand and threescore and ten men" → "seventy men — of fifty thousand men"** (v19) — Hebrew שִׁבְעִים אִישׁ חֲמִשִּׁים אֶלֶף אִישׁ *shiv'im ish chamishim elef ish*. Awkward construction; likely glossed or corrupted. Either 70 among 50,000, or 50,070 total. Context supports small-number; 70 is more plausible given Beth-shemesh's size.

### Notes
- **Philistine divination** (v2) — they seek priestly/diviner counsel. Ironically more theologically astute than Israel's elders in ch 4.
- **Test of the ark** (v7–9) — unyoked cows, separated from calves. Their natural instinct would be to return to their calves; their straight march to Beth-shemesh thus signals Yahweh's direction.
- **Golden tumors and mice** — plague motifs: rodent-carrier suggestion implicit. The Philistine priests recognize divine causation.
- **Beth-shemesh** — priestly city (Josh 21:16), near the Philistine border. First Israelite reception of the ark.
- **Fatal curiosity** (v19) — Beth-shemesh men "looked into" the ark; fatality follows. Num 4:20 warned Kohathites against uncovered-ark sight. The holy remains dangerous.
- **Ark ends at Kiriath-jearim** — the ark stays there for ~20 years (cf. 7:2) until David retrieves it (2 Sam 6).
