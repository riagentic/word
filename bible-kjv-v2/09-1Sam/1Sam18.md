## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"tabrets" → "tambourines"** (v6).
- **"prophesied" → "raved"** (v10) — Hebrew *hitnabbe*. Can mean prophesy or rave-prophesy (prophetic ecstasy gone dark). Context: unhinged behavior.
- **"javelin" → "spear"** (v10, 11) — Hebrew *chanit*.
- **"commune" → "speak in private"** (v22).
- **"dowry" → "bride price"** (v25) — Hebrew מֹהַר *mohar*. Customary payment from groom's family to bride's.

### Notes
- **Jonathan's love** — one of OT's great friendship narratives. Jonathan, the natural heir to Saul, freely yields to David. The robe-giving (v4) is symbolic abdication.
- **Women's song** (v7) — Saul's thousands, David's ten-thousands. Poetic parallelism (the second line intensifying the first is normal Hebrew poetry), but Saul reads it as slight.
- **Spear-throwing** (v11) — Saul's first attempt on David's life. Twice. David evades.
- **Merab then Michal** — two daughters successively offered. Michal loves David; Saul weaponizes this.
- **100 foreskins** (v25) — gruesome bride-price. Saul expects David to die attempting it. David doubles the count.
- **Saul's growing dread** — v12, v15, v29. The king's paranoia deepens as his popularity wanes relative to David's.
