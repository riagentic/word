# 1 Samuel 28

[1] In those days the Philistines gathered their armies for warfare, to fight with Israel. Achish said to David, "You know assuredly that you and your men must go out with me in the army."

[2] David said to Achish, "Very well, you shall know what your servant can do." Achish said to David, "Therefore I will make you my bodyguard for life."

[3] Now Samuel had died, and all Israel had mourned for him and buried him in Ramah, his own city. Saul had removed mediums and spiritists from the land.

[4] The Philistines gathered and came and encamped at Shunem; Saul gathered all Israel, and they encamped at Gilboa.

[5] When Saul saw the camp of the Philistines, he was afraid, and his heart trembled greatly.

[6] When Saul inquired of Yahweh, Yahweh did not answer him — neither by dreams, nor by Urim, nor by prophets.

[7] Then Saul said to his servants, "Find me a woman who is a medium, that I may go to her and inquire of her." His servants said to him, "Behold, there is a woman at En-dor who is a medium."

[8] Saul disguised himself, put on other clothes, and went with two men; they came to the woman by night. He said, "Please divine for me by the spirit, and bring up for me whomever I shall name to you."

[9] The woman said to him, "Behold, you know what Saul has done — how he has cut off the mediums and the spiritists from the land. Why then do you lay a snare for my life, to cause my death?"

[10] Saul swore to her by Yahweh, saying, "As Yahweh lives, no punishment shall come upon you for this thing."

[11] Then the woman said, "Whom shall I bring up for you?" He said, "Bring up Samuel for me."

[12] When the woman saw Samuel, she cried out with a loud voice; and the woman spoke to Saul, saying, "Why have you deceived me? For you are Saul!"

[13] The king said to her, "Do not be afraid. What do you see?" The woman said to Saul, "I see a divine being coming up out of the earth."

[14] He said to her, "What form is he of?" She said, "An old man is coming up; he is wrapped in a robe." Saul perceived that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground and did homage.

[15] Samuel said to Saul, "Why have you disturbed me by bringing me up?" Saul answered, "I am in great distress, for the Philistines are making war against me, and *Elohim* has turned away from me and answers me no more, either by prophets or by dreams. Therefore I have called you, that you may make known to me what I shall do."

[16] Samuel said, "Why then do you ask of me, since Yahweh has turned from you and has become your enemy?

[17] Yahweh has done to you as he spoke by me; Yahweh has torn the kingdom out of your hand and given it to your neighbor, to David.

[18] Because you did not obey the voice of Yahweh and did not execute his fierce wrath upon Amalek, therefore Yahweh has done this thing to you this day.

[19] Moreover, Yahweh will also give Israel with you into the hand of the Philistines; tomorrow you and your sons will be with me. Yahweh will also give the army of Israel into the hand of the Philistines."

[20] Then Saul fell at once full-length on the earth, and was greatly afraid because of the words of Samuel; there was no strength in him, for he had eaten no bread all day and all night.

[21] The woman came to Saul and saw that he was terrified. She said to him, "Behold, your maidservant has obeyed your voice; I have taken my life in my hand and have listened to the words you spoke to me.

[22] Now therefore, please listen also to the voice of your maidservant; let me set a morsel of bread before you, that you may eat; then you will have strength to go on your way."

[23] But he refused, saying, "I will not eat." However, his servants, together with the woman, urged him, and he listened to their voice. So he arose from the earth and sat on the bed.

[24] The woman had a fattened calf in the house, which she quickly slaughtered; she took flour, kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread with it.

[25] She brought it before Saul and his servants, and they ate. Then they rose and went away that night.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"familiar spirits / wizards" → "mediums / spiritists"** (v3, 7, 9) — as elsewhere.
- **"gods" → "divine being"** (v13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *elohim*. Plural form; here of a single supernatural being. Medium-language.
- **"mantle" → "robe"** (v14) — Hebrew *me'il*.
- **"sore distressed / troubled" → "in great distress / terrified"** (v15, 21) — modernized.

### Notes
- **Saul's final desperation** — denied prophetic access (v6), he turns to a medium — which he himself had outlawed (v3, 9).
- **Medium of En-dor** — often called "Witch of Endor." Consulting the dead forbidden (Lev 19:31, Deut 18:11).
- **Samuel's ghost** — the text presents as real Samuel, though some readings argue it is a necromantic deception. The content of the message is accurate; Samuel rebukes Saul and predicts his death.
- **"Tomorrow you will be with me"** (v19) — Samuel predicts Saul's death and his sons' deaths in the coming battle.
- **The medium's hospitality** (v21–25) — even the outlawed woman shows compassion. Ironic contrast: her kindness to the king who had outlawed her.
- **Saul's last supper** (v24–25) — grim parallel. The condemned man eats a final meal.
