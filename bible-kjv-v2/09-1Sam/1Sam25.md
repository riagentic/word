# 1 Samuel 25

[1] ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ died, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ gathered together and mourned for him; they buried him at his house in Ramah. Then David arose and went down to the wilderness of Paran.

[2] There was a man in Maon whose business was in ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧; the man was very great, and he had three thousand sheep and a thousand goats. He was shearing his sheep in ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧.

[3] The name of the man was ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, and the name of his wife ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧. The woman was of good understanding and beautiful in appearance; but the man was harsh and evil in his doings; he was a ⟦caleb|Kalibbi|Calebite⟧.

[4] David heard in the wilderness that ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ was shearing his sheep.

[5] David sent ten young men and said to the young men, "Go up to ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧; come to ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, and greet him in my name.

[6] Say to him: 'Peace! Long life to you, peace to you, peace to your house, and peace to all that you have.

[7] I have heard that you have shearers. Now your shepherds have been with us, and we did not harm them; nothing was missing from them all the while they were in ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧.

[8] Ask your young men, and they will tell you. Therefore let my young men find favor in your eyes, for we come on a festive day. Please give whatever you have at hand to your servants and to your son David.'"

[9] When David's young men came, they spoke to ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ all these words in the name of David, and then waited.

[10] ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ answered David's servants, "Who is David? Who is the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧? There are many servants nowadays who break away each from his master.

[11] Shall I then take my bread and my water, and my meat that I have killed for my shearers, and give it to men who come from I do not know where?"

[12] So David's young men turned back on their way, returned, and told him all these words.

[13] David said to his men, "Every man strap on his sword!" And every man strapped on his sword; David also strapped on his sword. About four hundred men went up after David, while two hundred remained with the baggage.

[14] But one of the young men told ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧, ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧'s wife, "Behold, David sent messengers out of the wilderness to greet our master, and he berated them.

[15] Yet the men were very good to us, and we were not harmed, and we did not miss anything as long as we went about with them, when we were in the fields.

[16] They were a wall to us both by night and by day, all the time we were with them keeping the sheep.

[17] Now therefore know and consider what you will do; for evil is determined against our master and against all his household. He is such a ⟦belial|son of Beliyyaal|worthless fellow⟧ that no one can speak to him."

[18] Then ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ hurried and took two hundred loaves of bread, two skins of wine, five prepared sheep, five seahs of roasted grain, a hundred clusters of raisins, and two hundred cakes of figs, and laid them on donkeys.

[19] She said to her young men, "Go on before me; behold, I am coming after you." But she did not tell her husband ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧.

[20] As she rode on the donkey and was coming down under cover of the mountain, behold, David and his men were coming down to meet her, and she met them.

[21] Now David had said, "Surely in vain have I guarded all that this fellow has in the wilderness, so that nothing was missed of all that belonged to him; and he has returned me evil for good.

[22] May ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ do so to the enemies of David, and more also, if I leave so much as one male of all that belong to him by the morning light."

[23] When ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ saw David, she hurried and got down from the donkey, fell before David on her face, and bowed to the ground.

[24] She fell at his feet and said, "On me alone, my lord, be the guilt. Please let your maidservant speak in your audience, and hear the words of your maidservant.

[25] Let not my lord regard this ⟦belial|man of Beliyyaal|worthless fellow⟧, ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧; for as his name is, so is he: ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ ("Fool") is his name, and folly is with him. But I your maidservant did not see the young men of my lord, whom you sent.

[26] Now therefore, my lord, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives and as your soul lives, since ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has restrained you from coming to bloodshed and from saving yourself with your own hand, now let your enemies and those who seek to do evil to my lord be as ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧.

[27] And now let this present that your maidservant has brought to my lord be given to the young men who follow my lord.

[28] Please forgive the transgression of your maidservant. For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will surely make my lord a sure house, because my lord is fighting the battles of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and evil shall not be found in you all your days.

[29] If a man rises to pursue you and to seek your life, the soul of my lord shall be bound in the bundle of the living in the care of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; but the souls of your enemies he shall sling out as from the hollow of a sling.

[30] When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has done to my lord according to all the good he has spoken concerning you, and has appointed you prince over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[31] this shall be no stumbling block to you or cause of grief to my lord — that you have shed blood without cause, or that my lord has saved himself. When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has dealt well with my lord, then remember your maidservant."

[32] David said to ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧, "Blessed be ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who sent you this day to meet me!

[33] Blessed be your discretion, and blessed be you, who have kept me this day from bloodshed and from saving myself with my own hand.

[34] For as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ lives, who has restrained me from harming you — unless you had hurried and come to meet me, truly by morning light there would not have been left to ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ so much as one male."

[35] So David received from her hand what she had brought him, and said to her, "Go up in peace to your house. See, I have listened to your voice and have granted your petition."

[36] ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ came to ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, and behold, he was holding a feast in his house like the feast of a king; ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧'s heart was merry within him, for he was very drunk. So she told him nothing, great or small, until the morning light.

[37] In the morning, when the wine had gone out of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, his wife told him these things; his heart died within him, and he became like a stone.

[38] About ten days later, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ struck ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, and he died.

[39] When David heard that ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ was dead, he said, "Blessed be ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who has pleaded the cause of my reproach from the hand of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ and has kept back his servant from evil! ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has returned the wickedness of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ upon his own head." David sent and spoke to ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ about taking her as his wife.

[40] When the servants of David came to ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ at ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧, they spoke to her, saying, "David sent us to you to take you to him as a wife."

[41] She rose, bowed with her face to the earth, and said, "Behold, your maidservant is a servant to wash the feet of the servants of my lord."

[42] ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ hurried and rose, and rode on a donkey; five of her young women attended her. She went after the messengers of David and became his wife.

[43] David also took ⟦ahinoam|Achinoam|Ahinoam⟧ of ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧; the two of them became his wives.

[44] But ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ had given Michal his daughter, David's wife, to Palti son of ⟦laish|Layish|Laish⟧ of Gallim.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"churlish" → "harsh"** (v3) — archaic.
- **"Belial" → "worthless fellow"** (v17, 25).
- **"Nabal" / "fool"** (v25) — Hebrew נָבָל *Naval* = "fool / scoundrel." Etymology added.
- **"bundle of life" → "bundle of the living"** (v29) — Hebrew צְרוֹר הַחַיִּים *tzeror ha-chayyim*. Living soul-protection metaphor; later used of divine preservation of the righteous.
- **"him that pisseth against the wall" → "one male"** (v22, 34) — Hebrew idiom for a male. Modernized.
- **"conversant" → "going about"** (v15) — archaic.

### Notes
- **Samuel dies** (v1) — the prophet who anointed Saul and David passes. Major transition.
- **Abigail** — one of the most positive female figures in Samuel. Intervenes to save her household and David's integrity simultaneously.
- **Nabal** — "fool" (nabal) as a type-name; prophetic of his character. Contrasts with Abigail (*av-gil* "father of joy").
- **David's restraint** (v32–35) — twice restrained: once from striking Saul (ch 24), once from striking Nabal. Yahweh works through Abigail.
- **Nabal's death** (v37–38) — struck by Yahweh, not David. Vindication without personal vengeance.
- **David's multiple wives** (v42–43) — Abigail, Ahinoam. Michal given to another by Saul. Royal polygamy emerges.
