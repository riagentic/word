# 1 Samuel 25

[1] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ died, and all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ gathered and lamented for him, and they buried him in his house in Ramah. And David arose and went down to the wilderness of Paran.

[2] And there was a man in Maon, and his work was in ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧, and the man was very great, and he had three thousand sheep and a thousand goats. And he was at the shearing of his flock in ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧.

[3] And the name of the man was ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, and the name of his wife was ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧. And the woman was good of understanding and beautiful in form, and the man was hard and evil in his deeds, and he was a ⟦caleb|Kalibbi|Calebite⟧.

[4] And David heard in the wilderness that ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ was shearing his flock.

[5] And David sent ten young men, and David said to the young men, "Go up to ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ and come to ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ and ask about his ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ in my name.

[6] And you shall say thus: 'To life! And you — ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and your house — ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and all that is yours — ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧!

[7] And now, I have heard that you have shearers. Now, the shepherds that are yours were with us; we did not put them to shame, and nothing was missing to them all the days of their being in ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧.

[8] Ask your young men, and they will tell you. And let the young men find favor in your eyes, for on a good day we have come. Give, please, what your hand finds to your servants and to your son, to David.'"

[9] And the young men of David came and spoke to ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ according to all these words in the name of David, and they rested.

[10] And ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ answered the servants of David and said, "Who is David, and who is the son of ⟦jesse|Yishai|Jesse⟧? Today the slaves are many who are breaking away, each man from the face of his lord.

[11] And shall I take my bread and my water and my butchered meat that I have butchered for my shearers, and give to men of whom I do not know where they are from?"

[12] And the young men of David turned about to their way, and they returned and came and told him according to all these words.

[13] And David said to his men, "Gird on, each man his sword!" And they girded on, each man his sword, and David also girded on his sword. And there went up after David about four hundred men, and two hundred sat by the gear.

[14] And to ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ the wife of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ one young man of the young men told, saying, "Behold, David sent messengers from the wilderness to bless our lord, and he swooped on them.

[15] And the men were very good to us, and we were not put to shame, and we did not miss anything all the days that we went about with them, when we were in the field.

[16] A wall they were over us, both by night and by day, all the days of our being with them shepherding the flock.

[17] And now, know and see what you will do, for the evil is determined against our lord and upon all his house; and he is ⟦belial|a son of Beliyyaal|a son of worthlessness⟧, beyond speaking to."

[18] And ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ hastened and took two hundred loaves of bread and two jars of wine and five sheep made ready and five seahs of parched grain and a hundred raisin cakes and two hundred fig cakes, and she put them on the donkeys.

[19] And she said to her young men, "Pass on before me; behold, I am coming after you." And her husband ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ she did not tell.

[20] And it came to pass, as she was riding on the donkey and going down in the covert of the mountain, that behold, David and his men were going down to meet her, and she met them.

[21] And David had said, "Surely for falsehood I kept all that this one has in the wilderness, and nothing was missing of all that is his, and he has returned to me evil for good.

[22] Thus may ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ do to the enemies of David, and thus may he add: I will not leave, of all that is his, until the morning, one who urinates against a wall."

[23] And ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ saw David, and she hastened and got down from the donkey, and she fell before the face of David on her face and bowed down to the earth.

[24] And she fell at his feet and said, "On me, me, my lord, be the iniquity. And let your slave woman, please, speak in your ears, and hear the words of your slave woman.

[25] Let not my lord, please, set his heart on this ⟦belial|man of Beliyyaal|man of worthlessness⟧, on ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, for as his name is, so is he: ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ is his name, and outrage is with him. And I, your slave woman, did not see the young men of my lord, whom you sent.

[26] And now, my lord, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives and by the life of your soul, it is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who has held you back from coming into bloodguilt and from your own hand saving you. And now, let your enemies be like ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, and those who seek evil toward my lord.

[27] And now, this blessing that your maidservant has brought to my lord, let it be given to the young men who walk about at the feet of my lord.

[28] Forgive, please, the transgression of your slave woman, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will surely make for my lord a sure house, for my lord is fighting the wars of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and evil shall not be found in you all your days.

[29] And a man has risen to pursue you and to seek your soul; and the soul of my lord shall be bound in the bundle of the living with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the soul of your enemies he shall sling out in the midst of the palm of the sling.

[30] And it shall be, when ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ does for my lord according to all the good that he has spoken concerning you, and commands you as leader over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,

[31] that this shall not be to you a staggering and a stumbling of heart for my lord, both to have shed blood without cause and for my lord to have saved himself. And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will do good to my lord, and you shall remember your slave woman."

[32] And David said to ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧, "Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who sent you this day to meet me.

[33] And blessed is your sense, and blessed are you, who have kept me back this day from coming into bloodguilt and from my own hand saving me.

[34] But indeed, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ lives, who has held me back from doing evil to you: unless you had hastened and come to meet me, there would not have been left to ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ until the light of the morning one who urinates against a wall."

[35] And David took from her hand what she had brought him, and to her he said, "Go up in ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to your house. See, I have listened to your voice and lifted up your face."

[36] And ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ came to ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, and behold, he had a feast in his house like the feast of the king, and the heart of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ was good upon him, and he was exceedingly drunk. And she did not tell him a thing small or great until the light of the morning.

[37] And it came to pass in the morning, when the wine went out from ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, that his wife told him these things, and his heart died within him, and he became a stone.

[38] And it came to pass after about ten days that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ smote ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧, and he died.

[39] And David heard that ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ was dead, and he said, "Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who has contended the case of my reproach from the hand of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ and has withheld his servant from evil; and the evil of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has returned on his head." And David sent and spoke for ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧, to take her to himself as a wife.

[40] And the servants of David came to ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧, to ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧, and they spoke to her, saying, "David has sent us to you to take you to himself as a wife."

[41] And she arose and bowed down, face to the earth, and said, "Behold, your slave woman is a maidservant to wash the feet of the servants of my lord."

[42] And ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ hastened and arose and rode on the donkey, and five girls of hers who walked at her foot, and she went after the messengers of David and became his wife.

[43] And ⟦ahinoam|Achinoam|Ahinoam⟧ David took from ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, and the two of them also became his wives.

[44] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ had given Michal his daughter, the wife of David, to Palti the son of ⟦laish|Layish|Laish⟧, who was from Gallim.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v22, 29, 32, 34) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"lamented him" → "lamented for him"** (v1) — Hebrew סָפַד, the loud wailing for the dead. **"in his house at Ramah"** — Hebrew בָּרָמָה, "in the Ramah".
- **"whose possessions were in Carmel" → "his work was in Carmel"** (v2) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂהוּ, "his work": what he did and had. **"three thousand sheep"** — Hebrew צֹאן, "flock" (sheep and goats; here beside "goats"). The last clause is literally "and it came to pass at the shearing of his flock in Carmel".
- **"of good understanding, and of a beautiful countenance" → "good of understanding and beautiful in form"** (v3) — Hebrew טוֹבַת־שֶׂכֶל וִיפַת תֹּאַר. **"churlish and evil in his doings" → "hard and evil in his deeds"** — Hebrew קָשֶׁה "hard". **"of the house of Caleb" → "a Calebite"** — the reading tradition (qere) כָלִבִּי; the written text (ketiv) כלבו can be read "like his heart". The name Caleb is also the word for "dog" (כֶּלֶב).
- **"greet him in my name" → "ask about his shalom in my name"** (v5) — Hebrew וּשְׁאֶלְתֶּם־לוֹ … לְשָׁלוֹם, "ask for him … as to peace": the usual words for a greeting.
- **"thus shall ye say to him that liveth in prosperity, Peace be both to thee" → "you shall say thus: 'To life! And you — shalom"** (v6) — Hebrew כֹּה לֶחָי, two words, of which the second is difficult: "to the living one" or "for life" (a wish of life). The Vulgate has "to my brothers" (לְאֶחָי; from memory). The three wishes have no verb: "and you, peace; and your house, peace; and all that is yours, peace".
- **"thou hast shearers"** (v7) — Hebrew גֹזְזִים לָךְ, "shearers are to you". **"we hurt them not" → "we did not put them to shame"** (v7, 15) — Hebrew הִכְלִים "shame, humiliate". **"neither was there ought missing unto them"** — Hebrew נִפְקַד, "be missed" (the verb of counting).
- **"we come in a good day" → "on a good day we have come"** (v8) — Hebrew יוֹם טוֹב: a day of feasting. **"whatsoever cometh to thine hand" → "what your hand finds"**.
- **"ceased" → "rested"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיָּנוּחוּ.
- **"there be many servants now a days that break away every man from his master" → "Today the slaves are many who are breaking away, each man from the face of his lord"** (v10) — Hebrew עֲבָדִים, the word rendered "servants" of David's men in the same verse: Nabal speaks of runaway slaves. אָדוֹן is "lord" here and in v14, 17 as in "my lord" (v24–31).
- **"Shall I then take"** (v11) — the Hebrew has no question word: "And I shall take … and give …!", said in scorn. **"my flesh that I have killed" → "my butchered meat that I have butchered"** — Hebrew טִבְחָתִי אֲשֶׁר טָבַחְתִּי, noun and verb of one root.
- **"turned their way" → "turned about to their way"** (v12) — Hebrew הָפַךְ.
- **"abode by the stuff" → "sat by the gear"** (v13) — Hebrew יָשְׁבוּ עַל־הַכֵּלִים.
- **"to salute our master; and he railed on them" → "to bless our lord, and he swooped on them"** (v14) — Hebrew לְבָרֵךְ "to bless" (to greet with a blessing); וַיָּעַט בָּהֶם, from עִיט, said of a bird of prey that swoops down (the noun עַיִט): he flew at them.
- **"conversant with them" → "went about with them"** (v15) — Hebrew הִתְהַלַּכְנוּ.
- **"a wall unto us" → "A wall they were over us"** (v16) — Hebrew חוֹמָה, a city wall; עָלֵינוּ "over, about us". **"keeping the sheep" → "shepherding the flock"**.
- **"evil is determined against our master"** (v17) — Hebrew כָלְתָה הָרָעָה, literally "the evil is finished": fully decided.
- **"such a son of Belial, that a man cannot speak to him" → "a son of Beliyyaal, beyond speaking to"** (v17) — Hebrew בֶּן־בְּלִיַּעַל מִדַּבֵּר אֵלָיו, literally "a son of worthlessness, from speaking to him". בְּלִיַּעַל is a common noun, "worthlessness", not a name; so also "this man of Beliyyaal" (v25), אִישׁ הַבְּלִיַּעַל, with the article.
- **"two bottles of wine" → "two jars of wine"** (v18) — Hebrew נִבְלֵי־יַיִן: נֵבֶל is a jar or a skin for wine; the word sounds like the name Nabal (see v37). **"five sheep ready dressed" → "five sheep made ready"** — Hebrew עֲשׂוּיֹת "made". **"five measures of parched corn" → "five seahs of parched grain"**. **"clusters of raisins … cakes of figs" → "raisin cakes … fig cakes"** — Hebrew צִמֻּקִים, דְּבֵלִים: dried grapes and pressed figs; "cakes" and "loaves" are supplied with the numbers.
- **"by the covert of the hill" → "in the covert of the mountain"** (v20) — Hebrew בְּסֵתֶר הָהָר, "in the hiding place of the mountain".
- **"in vain have I kept" → "for falsehood I kept"** (v21) — Hebrew לַשֶּׁקֶר, "for the lie": for a deceit, to no purpose. **"requited me evil for good" → "returned to me evil for good"**.
- **"So and more also do God unto the enemies of David, if I leave … any that pisseth against the wall" → "Thus may Elohim do to the enemies of David, and thus may he add: I will not leave … one who urinates against a wall"** (v22, 34) — the oath formula, כֹּה־יַעֲשֶׂה אֱלֹהִים … וְכֹה יֹסִיף אִם, literally "thus may Elohim do … and thus may he add, if I leave": the "if" of an oath means "I will not". The Masoretic Text says "to the enemies of David"; elsewhere the speaker calls it down on himself ("to me", Ruth 1:17; 2 Sam 3:35; "to Jonathan", 20:13; "to Abner", 2 Sam 3:9), and the Septuagint has "to David" here (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept. מַשְׁתִּין בְּקִיר is translated as it stands, "one who urinates against a wall": every male is meant (so also 1 Kgs 14:10; 21:21).
- **"fell before David on her face"** (v23) — Hebrew לְאַפֵּי דָוִד עַל־פָּנֶיהָ, "before the face (literally nostrils) of David, on her face".
- **"Upon me, my lord, upon me let this iniquity be"** (v24) — Hebrew בִּי־אֲנִי אֲדֹנִי הֶעָוֹן, "on me, I, my lord, the iniquity". **"thine handmaid" → "your slave woman"** (v24, 25, 28, 31, 41) — Hebrew אָמָה; "maidservant" (v27, 41) is שִׁפְחָה. Abigail uses both of herself, and both together in v41. **"in thine audience" → "in your ears"**.
- **"regard this man of Belial" → "set his heart on"** (v25) — Hebrew יָשִׂים … אֶת־לִבּוֹ אֶל.
- **"Nabal is his name, and folly is with him" → "Nabal is his name, and outrage is with him"** (v25) — a play on words: נָבָל *naval* is "fool" in the sense of a base, godless man (Deut 32:6; Ps 14:1), and נְבָלָה *nevalah* is the deed of such a man, an outrage (Gen 34:7; Judg 19:23). English cannot keep the likeness of the two words. The verse has only the name; the old gloss ("Fool") was not in the Hebrew and is removed.
- **"seeing the LORD hath withholden thee from coming to shed blood, and from avenging thyself with thine own hand" → "it is Yahweh who has held you back from coming into bloodguilt and from your own hand saving you"** (v26, 33) — Hebrew בּוֹא בְדָמִים, "come into bloods" (plural: blood shed and the guilt of it); הוֹשֵׁעַ יָדְךָ לָךְ, "your hand saving for you": the verb is "save", not "avenge". חֵי־נַפְשְׁךָ is "by the life of your soul". "Held back" (v26, 34) is מָנַע; "kept back" (v33) is כָּלָא; "withheld" (v39) is חָשַׂךְ.
- **"this blessing"** (v27) — Hebrew בְּרָכָה: a gift given with a blessing. **"that follow my lord" → "who walk about at the feet of my lord"**.
- **"forgive the trespass" → "Forgive … the transgression"** (v28) — Hebrew שָׂא נָא לְפֶשַׁע, literally "lift, please, the transgression". **"a sure house"** — Hebrew בַּיִת נֶאֱמָן, "a faithful, lasting house" (the word "faithful" of 22:14). **"the battles of the LORD" → "the wars of Yahweh"**. **"all thy days"** — Hebrew מִיָּמֶיךָ, "from your days".
- **"Yet a man is risen" → "And a man has risen"** (v29) — Hebrew וַיָּקָם אָדָם, a plain past: Saul is meant, unnamed.
- **"bound in the bundle of life with the LORD thy God" → "bound in the bundle of the living with Yahweh your Elohim"** (v29) — Hebrew צְרוּרָה בִּצְרוֹר הַחַיִּים אֵת יְהוָה, word for word: צְרוֹר is a bundle or bag tied up to keep what is precious; הַחַיִּים is "the living" or "life". נֶפֶשׁ is "soul" each time. **"as out of the middle of a sling" → "in the midst of the palm of the sling"** — Hebrew כַּף, "palm": the hollow that holds the stone; "sling out" and "sling" are one root (קָלַע).
- **"shall have appointed thee ruler" → "commands you as leader"** (v30) — Hebrew וְצִוְּךָ לְנָגִיד; נָגִיד "leader".
- **"no grief unto thee, nor offence of heart" → "a staggering and a stumbling of heart"** (v31) — Hebrew פּוּקָה, found only here ("tottering, staggering": uncertain), and מִכְשׁוֹל לֵב "a stumbling block of the heart". **"causeless" → "without cause"** — Hebrew חִנָּם. **"avenged himself" → "saved himself"** — Hebrew הוֹשִׁיעַ. The last clauses are literally "and Yahweh will do good to my lord, and you shall remember".
- **"Blessed be the LORD" → "Blessed is Yahweh"** (v32, 39) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ יְהוָה, with no verb.
- **"blessed be thy advice" → "blessed is your sense"** (v33) — Hebrew טַעַם, literally "taste": discernment (the word of 21:13).
- **"from hurting thee" → "from doing evil to you"** (v34) — Hebrew מֵהָרַע אֹתָךְ.
- **"have accepted thy person" → "lifted up your face"** (v35) — Hebrew וָאֶשָּׂא פָּנָיִךְ: shown you regard, granted what you asked.
- **"Nabal's heart was merry within him" → "the heart of Nabal was good upon him"** (v36) — Hebrew טוֹב עָלָיו. **"less or more" → "small or great"**.
- **"when the wine was gone out of Nabal"** (v37) — Hebrew בְּצֵאת הַיַּיִן מִנָּבָל; the name sounds like נֵבֶל, the wine jar of v18. **"became as a stone" → "became a stone"** — Hebrew הָיָה לְאָבֶן, with no "as".
- **"the LORD smote Nabal"** (v38) — Hebrew נָגַף, a blow from God.
- **"hath pleaded the cause of my reproach" → "has contended the case of my reproach"** (v39) — Hebrew רָב אֶת־רִיב, verb and noun of one root. **"hath kept his servant from evil" → "has withheld his servant from evil"**. **"the wickedness of Nabal" → "the evil of Nabal"** — Hebrew רָעָה, the same word as "from evil". **"communed with Abigail" → "spoke for Abigail"** — Hebrew וַיְדַבֵּר בְּ: to speak for a woman is to ask for her as a wife.
- **"let thine handmaid be a servant" → "your slave woman is a maidservant"** (v41) — Hebrew אֲמָתְךָ לְשִׁפְחָה.
- **"five damsels of hers that went after her" → "five girls of hers who walked at her foot"** (v42) — Hebrew נַעֲרוֹת; לְרַגְלָהּ "at her foot".
- **"David also took Ahinoam"** (v43) — the Hebrew puts the name first, with a plain past: "And Ahinoam David took" (or "had taken").
- **"Phalti" → "Palti"; "had given"** (v44) — Hebrew נָתַן, a plain past, "gave" or "had given".

### Notes
- **Samuel dies** (v1) — the prophet who anointed Saul and David.
- **Abigail** — speaks to David of Yahweh's doing (v26–31); David blesses Yahweh, her sense and her (v32–33).
- **Nabal** — the name is the Hebrew word "fool" (see v25); Abigail's name is אֲבִיגַיִל, written in several ways in the chapter (v3, 18, 32).
- **David held back** (v26, 33–34, 39) — from bloodguilt and from saving himself by his own hand; Yahweh smites Nabal (v38).
- **David's wives** (v42–44) — Abigail, Ahinoam. Michal given to another by Saul.
