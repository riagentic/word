## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"ark of God" → "ephod"** (v18) — MT reads *aron Elohim* ("ark of *Elohim*"), but LXX has "ephod." The LXX reading fits better: the ark was at Kiriath-jearim, not with Saul. Ephod is the priestly oracle-consultation device. LXX followed here.
- **"discovered themselves" → "revealed themselves"** (v8, 11).
- **"discomfiture" → "confusion"** (v20).
- **"adjured" → "made swear"** (v24).
- **"straitly charged" → "strictly charged"** (v28).
- **"haply" → "" (removed, idiomatic modernization)** (v30).

### Notes
- **Jonathan's faith adventure** (v6) — "there is no restraint to Yahweh to save by many or by few." Archetypal faith-statement. Compare Gideon's 300.
- **Bozez and Seneh** (v4–5) — the two crags. "Shining" and "Thorny." Archaeological identification disputed but real geography.
- **Earthquake** (v15) — a natural event coincident with Philistine panic. Yahweh uses cosmic and psychological forces.
- **Deserters and hiders return** (v21–22) — Hebrews who had been with the Philistines switch sides (v21); the men hiding in the hill country of Ephraim join the pursuit (v22).
- **Saul's foolish oath** (v24) — hasty vow creates crisis. Exhausted army cannot eat; Jonathan, ignorant of the oath, tastes honey and is strengthened.
- **Blood-eating** (v32–34) — famished troops slaughter and eat without draining blood (Lev 17). Saul improvises with a slaughtering stone.
- **Jonathan's near-execution** — Saul's oath would require his son's death; the people refuse. First open rift between Saul's will and his people's.
- **Royal summary** (v47–52) — Saul's successes listed. Not a complete failure; but tragic trajectory is set.
