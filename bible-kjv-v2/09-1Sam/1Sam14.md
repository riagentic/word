# 1 Samuel 14

[1] And it came to pass one day that ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ the son of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to the young man, his weapon-bearer, "Come, and let us pass over to the outpost of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ that is on the other side yonder." And he did not tell his father.

[2] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was sitting at the end of ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, under the pomegranate tree that is in Migron, and the people who were with him were about six hundred men.

[3] And ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧ the son of ⟦ahitub|Achituv|Ahitub⟧, the brother of ⟦ichabod|I-Chavod|Ichabod⟧, the son of ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧, the son of Eli, the priest of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in Shiloh, was bearing an ephod. And the people did not know that ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ had gone.

[4] And between the passes by which ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ sought to pass over to the outpost of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ there was a tooth of the crag on the one side and a tooth of the crag on the other side; and the name of the one was ⟦bozez|Botzetz|Bozez⟧, and the name of the other ⟦seneh|Senneh|Seneh⟧.

[5] The one tooth was a pillar on the north, over against ⟦michmash|Michmas|Michmash⟧, and the other on the south, over against ⟦geba|Geva|Geba⟧.

[6] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said to the young man, his weapon-bearer, "Come, and let us pass over to the outpost of these uncircumcised; perhaps ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will act for us, for there is no restraint to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to save by many or by few."

[7] And his weapon-bearer said to him, "Do all that is in your heart. Turn yourself; behold, I am with you according to your heart."

[8] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said, "Behold, we are passing over to the men, and we will reveal ourselves to them.

[9] If they say thus to us, 'Be still until we reach you,' then we will stand in our place and will not go up to them.

[10] And if they say thus, 'Come up to us,' then we will go up, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given them into our hand; and this shall be the sign for us."

[11] And the two of them revealed themselves to the outpost of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ said, "Behold, ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧ coming out of the holes where they hid themselves."

[12] And the men of the outpost answered ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ and his weapon-bearer and said, "Come up to us, and we will make you know a thing." And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said to his weapon-bearer, "Come up after me, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given them into the hand of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[13] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ went up on his hands and on his feet, and his weapon-bearer after him; and they fell before ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, and his weapon-bearer was putting them to death after him.

[14] And the first blow, which ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ and his weapon-bearer struck, was about twenty men, within about half a furrow of a yoke of field.

[15] And there was trembling in the camp, in the field, and among all the people; the outpost and the destroyer, they also trembled; and the earth quaked, and it became a trembling of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[16] And the watchmen of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ in ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ saw, and behold, the multitude melted away, and it went, and hither.

[17] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to the people who were with him, "Number, please, and see who has gone from us." And they numbered, and behold, ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ and his weapon-bearer were not there.

[18] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to ⟦ahijah|Achiyah|Ahijah⟧, "Bring forward the ark of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧." For the ark of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was, on that day, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[19] And it came to pass, while ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ spoke to the priest, that the tumult that was in the camp of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ went on, going and growing great; and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to the priest, "Withdraw your hand."

[20] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and all the people who were with him were called out, and they came to the battle; and behold, the sword of each man was against his neighbor, a very great confusion.

[21] And the ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧ had been for the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ as yesterday and the day before, who had gone up with them into the camp all around; and they also — to be with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who were with ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧.

[22] And all the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who were hiding themselves in the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧ heard that the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ had fled, and they also clung close after them in the battle.

[23] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ saved ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on that day, and the battle passed beyond ⟦beth-aven|Beit Aven|Beth-aven⟧.

[24] And the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were pressed on that day, and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ put the people under a curse, saying, "Cursed is the man who eats bread until the evening, and I am avenged on my enemies." And none of the people tasted bread.

[25] And all the land came into the forest, and there was honey on the face of the field.

[26] And the people came to the forest, and behold, a flow of honey; and no one was reaching his hand to his mouth, for the people feared the oath.

[27] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ had not heard when his father made the people swear, and he stretched out the end of the staff that was in his hand and dipped it in the honeycomb, and he brought his hand back to his mouth, and his eyes became bright.

[28] And a man of the people answered and said, "Your father surely made the people swear, saying, 'Cursed is the man who eats bread today.'" And the people were weary.

[29] And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ said, "My father has troubled the land. See, please, that my eyes have become bright, because I tasted a little of this honey.

[30] How much more, if the people had surely eaten today of the spoil of their enemies that they found! For now the blow among the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ has not been great."

[31] And they struck down among the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ on that day from ⟦michmash|Michmas|Michmash⟧ to ⟦aijalon|Ayyalon|Aijalon⟧, and the people were very weary.

[32] And the people swooped on the spoil and took flock and herd and sons of the herd and slaughtered them on the ground, and the people ate with the blood.

[33] And they told ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, saying, "Behold, the people are sinning against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, eating with the blood." And he said, "You have dealt treacherously. Roll to me a great stone today."

[34] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Scatter among the people and say to them, 'Bring forward to me each man his ox and each man his sheep, and slaughter them here and eat, and you shall not sin against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, eating with the blood.'" And all the people brought forward each man his ox in his hand that night, and they slaughtered them there.

[35] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ built an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; with it he began to build an altar to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[36] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Let us go down after the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ by night and plunder among them until the light of the morning, and let us not leave a man among them." And they said, "Do all that is good in your eyes." And the priest said, "Let us come near here to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

[37] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ asked of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, "Shall I go down after the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧? Will you give them into the hand of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?" And he did not answer him on that day.

[38] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Draw near here, all the corners of the people, and know and see in what this sin has been today.

[39] For as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, who saves ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, even if it is in ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ my son, he shall surely die." And there was no one answering him out of all the people.

[40] And he said to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "You, you shall be on one side, and I and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ my son will be on one side." And the people said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "Do what is good in your eyes."

[41] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "Give what is blameless." And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ were captured, and the people went out.

[42] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Cast between me and ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ my son." And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ was captured.

[43] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, "Tell me what you have done." And ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ told him and said, "I surely tasted, with the end of the staff that was in my hand, a little honey. Here I am; I shall die."

[44] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Thus may ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ do, and thus may he add: you shall surely die, ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧."

[45] And the people said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "Shall ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ die, who has done this great salvation in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧? Far be it! As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, not one of the hairs of his head shall fall to the ground, for with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ he has worked this day." And the people ransomed ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧, and he did not die.

[46] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ went up from after the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ went to their place.

[47] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ captured the kingship over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he fought all around against all his enemies, against ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ and against the sons of Ammon and against Edom and against the kings of ⟦zobah|Tzovah|Zobah⟧ and against the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; and wherever he turned, he condemned.

[48] And he did valiantly and struck down ⟦amalek|Amaleq|Amalek⟧ and delivered ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the hand of his pillager.

[49] And the sons of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ were ⟦jonathan|Yehonatan|Jonathan⟧ and ⟦ishvi|Yishvi|Ishvi⟧ and ⟦malchishua|Malki-Shua|Malchi-shua⟧; and the name of his two daughters: the name of the firstborn was ⟦merab|Merav|Merab⟧, and the name of the younger Michal.

[50] And the name of the wife of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was ⟦ahinoam|Achinoam|Ahinoam⟧ the daughter of ⟦ahimaaz|Achimaatz|Ahimaaz⟧; and the name of the commander of his host was ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ the son of Ner, the uncle of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧.

[51] And ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧ was the father of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and Ner the father of ⟦abner|Avner|Abner⟧ was the son of ⟦abiel|Aviel|Abiel⟧.

[52] And the war was strong against the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ all the days of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧; and when ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ saw any mighty man and any son of ability, he gathered him to himself.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v18, 36 with the article (הָאֱלֹהִים).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Now it came to pass upon a day" → "And it came to pass one day"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הַיּוֹם, literally "and the day came to be". **"the young man that bare his armour" → "the young man, his weapon-bearer"** (v1, 6, 7, 12–14, 17) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא כֵלָיו, "the bearer of his gear"; no armor is named. נַעַר is "boy", here an attendant.
- **"go over" → "pass over"** (v1, 4, 6, 8) — Hebrew עָבַר; the "passes" (v4, מַעְבְּרוֹת) are the same root. **"garrison" → "outpost"** (v1, 4, 6, 11, 12, 15) — Hebrew מַצָּב "standing place, post" (v12 מַצָּבָה). **"on the other side" → "on the other side yonder"** — Hebrew מֵעֵבֶר הַלָּז.
- **"tarried in the uttermost part of Gibeah" → "was sitting at the end of Gibeah"** (v2) — Hebrew יוֹשֵׁב בִּקְצֵה הַגִּבְעָה; the name has the article and may be read "the hill".
- **"the LORD's priest in Shiloh, wearing an ephod" → "the priest of Yahweh in Shiloh, was bearing an ephod"** (v3) — Hebrew נֹשֵׂא אֵפוֹד, the verb "bear, carry" of v1. "The priest of Yahweh in Shiloh" stands after "Eli" and most naturally belongs to him; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"a sharp rock" → "a tooth of the crag"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew שֵׁן־הַסֶּלַע, literally "the tooth of the crag". **"The forefront of the one was situate northward" → "The one tooth was a pillar on the north"** (v5) — Hebrew מָצוּק, found only here and in 2:8 ("the pillars of the earth"): uncertain. "Over against" is מוּל.
- **"it may be that the LORD will work for us" → "perhaps Yahweh will act for us"** (v6) — Hebrew אוּלַי יַעֲשֶׂה, "will do". "Jonathan" is here and in v8 the long form יְהוֹנָתָן *Yehonatan*; elsewhere in the chapter the short form.
- **"turn thee; behold, I am with thee according to thy heart" → "Turn yourself; behold, I am with you according to your heart"** (v7) — Hebrew נְטֵה לָךְ, "turn for yourself" (turn where you will), and כִּלְבָבֶךָ, the phrase of 13:14. The Septuagint has "Do all to which your heart turns" (from memory).
- **"discover ourselves" → "reveal ourselves"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew נִגְלִינוּ, the nifal of גָּלָה "uncover": let ourselves be seen.
- **"Tarry until we come to you" → "Be still until we reach you"** (v9) — Hebrew דֹּמּוּ, "be still, silent"; the word of Josh 10:12. **"this shall be a sign unto us" → "this shall be the sign for us"** (v10) — with the article.
- **"we will shew you a thing" → "we will make you know a thing"** (v12) — Hebrew וְנוֹדִיעָה אֶתְכֶם דָּבָר; דָּבָר is "word" or "thing".
- **"slew after him" → "was putting them to death after him"** (v13) — Hebrew מְמוֹתֵת, a participle of the strengthened stem of "die": dispatching those who had fallen.
- **"that first slaughter … within as it were an half acre of land, which a yoke of oxen might plow" → "the first blow … within about half a furrow of a yoke of field"** (v14) — Hebrew מַכָּה "blow", the noun of "strike". כְּבַחֲצִי מַעֲנָה צֶמֶד שָׂדֶה, literally "as in half a furrow, a yoke of field", is obscure: a "yoke of field" is taken as the land a yoke of oxen plows in a day (Isa 5:10), and מַעֲנָה as "furrow" (Ps 129:3); the Septuagint has a different text, with darts and sling stones (from memory). The meaning is uncertain.
- **"trembling in the host … the earth quaked: so it was a very great trembling" → "trembling in the camp … the earth quaked, and it became a trembling of Elohim"** (v15) — Hebrew חֲרָדָה and חָרַד "tremble" three times; "quaked" is another verb, רָגַז "be agitated, shake". חֶרְדַּת אֱלֹהִים "a trembling of Elohim" is a trembling sent by Elohim, or a very great one; the Hebrew is kept. "The destroyer" (הַמַּשְׁחִית) is the raiding force of 13:17.
- **"they went on beating down one another" → "and it went, and hither"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ וַהֲלֹם, literally "and it went and hither": the Masoretic Text is difficult and is translated as it stands. The Septuagint has "hither and thither" (from memory). "Multitude" (הָמוֹן) is the same word as "tumult" in v19.
- **"Bring hither the ark of God. For the ark of God was at that time with the children of Israel" → "Bring forward the ark of Elohim. For the ark of Elohim was, on that day, and the sons of Israel"** (v18) — Hebrew הַגִּישָׁה אֲרוֹן הָאֱלֹהִים כִּי־הָיָה אֲרוֹן הָאֱלֹהִים בַּיּוֹם הַהוּא וּבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל. The Masoretic Text says "the ark of Elohim" and is kept. Its last words, "and the sons of Israel", hang loose ("with" would be another word); the sentence is difficult. The Septuagint has "Bring the ephod; for he bore the ephod on that day before Israel" (from memory), which many follow because the ark was last named at Kiriath-jearim (7:1, 2) and because an ephod is what a priest "brings forward" to ask of Elohim (23:9; 30:7). That reading is not put into the verse.
- **"the noise … went on and increased" → "the tumult … went on, going and growing great"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ הָלוֹךְ וָרָב. **"Withdraw thine hand"** — Hebrew אֱסֹף יָדֶךָ, literally "gather your hand": stop the asking.
- **"assembled themselves" → "were called out"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּזָּעֵק, nifal of "cry out", as 13:4. **"every man's sword was against his fellow" → "the sword of each man was against his neighbor"** — Hebrew אִישׁ בְּרֵעֵהוּ. **"discomfiture" → "confusion"** — Hebrew מְהוּמָה.
- **"Moreover the Hebrews that were with the Philistines before that time … even they also turned to be with the Israelites" → "And the Hebrews had been for the Philistines as yesterday and the day before … and they also — to be with Israel"** (v21) — the Hebrew sentence has no verb for "turned": וְגַם־הֵמָּה לִהְיוֹת עִם־יִשְׂרָאֵל, "and they also, to be with Israel". The Septuagint and the Syriac have "they also turned" (סָבְבוּ for סָבִיב "all around"; from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept and nothing is supplied. "As yesterday and the day before" = as before. The name is "Israel", not "Israelites".
- **"followed hard after them" → "clung close after them"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיַּדְבְּקוּ, the verb "cling".
- **"passed over unto Beth-aven" → "passed beyond Beth-aven"** (v23) — Hebrew עָבְרָה אֶת־בֵּית אָוֶן.
- **"were distressed" → "were pressed"** (v24) — Hebrew נִגַּשׂ, as 13:6. **"had adjured the people" → "put the people under a curse"** — Hebrew וַיֹּאֶל, the hifil of אָלָה "curse": he bound them with a curse-oath (v26 "the oath", v27, 28 "made swear"). The Septuagint has a longer text before these words (from memory). **"Cursed be the man that eateth any food until evening, that I may be avenged" → "Cursed is the man who eats bread until the evening, and I am avenged on my enemies"** — Hebrew אָרוּר has no verb; לֶחֶם is "bread", food in general; וְנִקַּמְתִּי is "and I have avenged myself".
- **"all they of the land came to a wood; and there was honey upon the ground" → "all the land came into the forest, and there was honey on the face of the field"** (v25) — Hebrew וְכָל־הָאָרֶץ בָּאוּ בַיָּעַר, literally so: "the land" for its people. The verse is difficult; יַעַר is "forest", and the same letters mean "honeycomb" (v27 יַעְרַת הַדְּבָשׁ).
- **"the honey dropped" → "a flow of honey"** (v26) — Hebrew הֵלֶךְ דְּבָשׁ, literally "a going of honey". **"no man put his hand to his mouth" → "no one was reaching his hand to his mouth"** — Hebrew מַשִּׂיג, "making reach".
- **"charged the people with the oath" → "made the people swear"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew הִשְׁבִּיעַ; in v28 with the infinitive absolute, "surely made swear" (literally "making swear he made swear"). **"his eyes were enlightened" → "his eyes became bright"** (v27, 29) — the reading tradition has וַתָּאֹרְנָה "became light"; the written text has "and his eyes saw". **"an honeycomb"** — Hebrew יַעְרַת הַדְּבָשׁ.
- **"And the people were faint" → "And the people were weary"** (v28, 31) — Hebrew וַיָּעַף. In v28 the words may belong to the man's speech or to the story.
- **"hath troubled the land"** (v29) — Hebrew עָכַר, the verb of Josh 7:25 (Achan, Achor).
- **"How much more, if haply the people had eaten freely … for had there not been now a much greater slaughter" → "How much more, if the people had surely eaten … For now the blow among the Philistines has not been great"** (v30) — Hebrew אָכֹל אָכַל, infinitive absolute with its verb; the last clause is a statement in the Masoretic Text (לֹא "not"), and it may also be read as a question, "would not the blow have been greater?".
- **"flew upon the spoil" → "swooped on the spoil"** (v32) — the reading tradition has וַיַּעַט, "swooped" like a bird of prey; the written text has "and the people did / made toward spoil". **"sheep, and oxen, and calves" → "flock and herd and sons of the herd"** — Hebrew צֹאן וּבָקָר וּבְנֵי בָקָר. **"did eat them with the blood" → "ate with the blood"** (v32, 33, 34) — Hebrew עַל־הַדָּם, literally "upon the blood" (Lev 19:26); in v34 אֶל־הַדָּם.
- **"Ye have transgressed" → "You have dealt treacherously"** (v33) — Hebrew בְּגַדְתֶּם. **"roll a great stone unto me this day"** — Hebrew הַיּוֹם "today".
- **"Disperse yourselves" → "Scatter"; "Bring me hither" → "Bring forward to me"; "slay them here" → "slaughter them here"** (v34) — Hebrew פֻּצוּ; הַגִּישׁוּ; שָׁחַט, to kill by cutting the throat; בָּזֶה "here" is literally "on this". "His ox in his hand": so the Hebrew.
- **"the same was the first altar that he built" → "with it he began to build an altar"** (v35) — Hebrew אֹתוֹ הֵחֵל לִבְנוֹת מִזְבֵּחַ לַיהוָה, literally "it he began to build, an altar to Yahweh".
- **"spoil them" → "plunder among them"** (v36) — Hebrew נָבֹזָה בָהֶם. **"Let us draw near hither unto God" → "Let us come near here to Elohim"** — Hebrew נִקְרְבָה … אֶל־הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"asked counsel of God" → "asked of Elohim"** (v37) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאַל … בֵּאלֹהִים; "counsel" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"all ye chief of the people" → "all the corners of the people"** (v38) — Hebrew פִּנּוֹת הָעָם, "the corners": its chief men, as the cornerstones of a building (Judg 20:2).
- **"though it be in Jonathan my son"** (v39) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־יֶשְׁנוֹ בְּיוֹנָתָן, "if it is in Jonathan". **"he shall surely die"** (v39, 44) — infinitive absolute with its verb, מוֹת יָמוּת, literally "dying he shall die".
- **"Give a perfect lot" → "Give what is blameless"** (v41) — Hebrew הָבָה תָמִים *havah tamim*, two words: "give" and "whole, complete, blameless". What Saul asks for is not said: "give a whole (true) answer", or "show the blameless one"; "lot" is not in the Hebrew. The Septuagint has a much longer text here, in which Saul asks why Yahweh has not answered and prays that Urim be given if the guilt is in him or Jonathan, and Thummim if it is in the people (cited from memory); *tamim* and Thummim have the same letters. The Masoretic Text is kept as printed.
- **"were taken … escaped" → "were captured … went out"** (v41, 42) — Hebrew נִלְכַּד, of the one marked out by the lot (10:20, 21; Josh 7:16–18); יָצָאוּ "went out", that is, went free. **"Cast lots" → "Cast"** (v42) — Hebrew הַפִּילוּ, "make fall"; "lot" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"I did but taste … and, lo, I must die" → "I surely tasted … Here I am; I shall die"** (v43) — Hebrew טָעֹם טָעַמְתִּי, infinitive absolute with its verb; הִנְנִי אָמוּת, "behold me, I die". It may be heard as assent or as protest.
- **"God do so and more also" → "Thus may Elohim do, and thus may he add"** (v44) — Hebrew כֹּה־יַעֲשֶׂה אֱלֹהִים וְכֹה יוֹסִף, the oath formula; "to me" is not in the Hebrew here.
- **"wrought this great salvation" → "done this great salvation"; "God forbid" → "Far be it!"** (v45) — Hebrew עָשָׂה הַיְשׁוּעָה; חָלִילָה. **"there shall not one hair of his head fall" → "not one of the hairs of his head shall fall"** — the oath form אִם־יִפֹּל, "if there falls of the hair of his head". **"rescued" → "ransomed"** — Hebrew וַיִּפְדּוּ, the verb "ransom": how the people did so is not said.
- **"went up from following the Philistines" → "went up from after the Philistines"** (v46) — Hebrew מֵאַחֲרֵי.
- **"So Saul took the kingdom" → "And Saul captured the kingship"** (v47) — Hebrew לָכַד הַמְּלוּכָה: the verb of capturing a city, and of v41, 42; מְלוּכָה is "kingship". **"children of Ammon" → "sons of Ammon"**. **"whithersoever he turned himself, he vexed them" → "wherever he turned, he condemned"** — Hebrew יַרְשִׁיעַ, as printed: the causative of "be wicked, guilty", elsewhere "declare guilty, condemn" (Deut 25:1) or "act wickedly" (Dan 9:5). Here it is taken as "put in the wrong", that is, worsted; "act wickedly" is also possible. The Septuagint has "he was saved / victorious" (יִוָּשֵׁעַ; from memory). The sense is uncertain.
- **"gathered an host" → "did valiantly"** (v48) — Hebrew וַיַּעַשׂ חַיִל, literally "made ability / force" (Num 24:18); "gathered a force" is also possible. **"smote the Amalekites" → "struck down Amalek"** — the name is "Amalek". **"them that spoiled them" → "his pillager"** — Hebrew שֹׁסֵהוּ, singular.
- **"the younger"** (v49) — Hebrew הַקְּטַנָּה, literally "the small one". **"the captain of his host was Abner" → "the commander of his host was Abner"** (v50) — the name is written here אֲבִינֵר *Aviner*. "The uncle of Saul" may be Ner or Abner; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"And Kish was the father of Saul; and Ner the father of Abner was the son of Abiel"** (v51) — Hebrew בֶּן־אֲבִיאֵל, "the son of Abiel", singular; it is often read "sons of Abiel" (from memory).
- **"sore war" → "the war was strong"** (v52) — Hebrew חֲזָקָה. **"any strong man, or any valiant man, he took him unto him" → "any mighty man and any son of ability, he gathered him to himself"** — Hebrew אִישׁ גִּבּוֹר; בֶּן־חַיִל; וַיַּאַסְפֵהוּ.

### Notes
- **Jonathan's faith adventure** (v6) — "there is no restraint to Yahweh to save by many or by few." Archetypal faith-statement. Compare Gideon's 300.
- **Bozez and Seneh** (v4–5) — the two crags. "Shining" and "Thorny." Archaeological identification disputed but real geography.
- **Earthquake** (v15) — a natural event coincident with Philistine panic. Yahweh uses cosmic and psychological forces.
- **Deserters and hiders return** (v21–22) — Hebrews who had been with the Philistines are found with Israel (v21); the men hiding in the hill country of Ephraim join the pursuit (v22).
- **Saul's foolish oath** (v24) — hasty vow creates crisis. Exhausted army cannot eat; Jonathan, ignorant of the oath, tastes honey and is strengthened.
- **Blood-eating** (v32–34) — famished troops slaughter and eat without draining blood (Lev 17). Saul improvises with a slaughtering stone.
- **Jonathan's near-execution** — Saul's oath would require his son's death; the people refuse. First open rift between Saul's will and his people's.
- **Royal summary** (v47–52) — Saul's successes listed. Not a complete failure; but tragic trajectory is set.
