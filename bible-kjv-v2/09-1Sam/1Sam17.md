## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"six cubits and a span"** (v4) — ~9'9" / 3m. MT reading. 4QSamᵃ and LXX have "four cubits and a span" (~6'9"), closer to Samuel's giant but less legendary. MT numerically followed.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v5, 6, 38).
- **"coat of mail" → "coat of scale armor"** (v5) — Hebrew *shiryon qasqasim*. Scale-armor.
- **"target" → "javelin"** (v6) — Hebrew כִּידוֹן *kidon*. Curved javelin, not a shield.
- **"scrip" → "bag"** (v40) — archaic.
- **"assayed to go; for he had not proved it" → "tried to walk, for he had not tested them"** (v39).
- **"naughtiness" → "evil"** (v28) — archaic; modern "naughty" is trivialized.
- **"stripling" → "young man"** (v56) — modernized.
- **"shouted for the battle" → "shouting the war cry"** (v20).

### Notes
- **Goliath of Gath** — possibly descendant of the Anakim remnant (Josh 11:22). Elsewhere (2 Sam 21:19) "Elhanan" kills Goliath; 1 Chr 20:5 clarifies this as "Lahmi, the brother of Goliath." Textual tradition has complexity here.
- **Two David-introductions** — ch 16 (Saul's musician) and ch 17 (unknown shepherd; Saul asks "whose son?") seem to reflect two separate traditions. LXX of 1 Sam 17 is shorter than MT, omitting the passages that create the tension (v12–31, 55–58). Textual-critical issue.
- **"Five smooth stones"** (v40) — one for Goliath, four for his brothers (2 Sam 21:15–22, four Philistine giants). Possibly.
- **"Battle is Yahweh's"** (v47) — theological statement. Victory is not technological but divine.
- **David's age** — "youth" (*na'ar*), probably mid-teens. Old enough to fight; young enough to be dismissed.
- **Head brought to Jerusalem** (v54) — anachronistic (Jerusalem not yet Israelite until David captures it, 2 Sam 5). Perhaps the head was taken there later, or the text reflects later memory.
