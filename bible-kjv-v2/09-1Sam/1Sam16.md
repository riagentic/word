# 1 Samuel 16

[1] Yahweh said to Samuel, "How long will you grieve over Saul, since I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? Fill your horn with oil and go; I am sending you to Jesse the Bethlehemite, for I have provided for myself a king among his sons."

[2] Samuel said, "How can I go? If Saul hears of it, he will kill me." Yahweh said, "Take a heifer with you and say, 'I have come to sacrifice to Yahweh.'

[3] Invite Jesse to the sacrifice, and I will show you what you shall do; you shall anoint for me the one whom I name to you."

[4] Samuel did what Yahweh said and came to Bethlehem. The elders of the town came trembling to meet him and said, "Do you come peaceably?"

[5] He said, "Peaceably. I have come to sacrifice to Yahweh; consecrate yourselves and come with me to the sacrifice." He consecrated Jesse and his sons and invited them to the sacrifice.

[6] When they came, he looked on Eliab and said, "Surely the anointed of Yahweh is before him."

[7] But Yahweh said to Samuel, "Do not look on his appearance or on the height of his stature, because I have rejected him. For not as man sees; for man looks on the outward appearance, but Yahweh looks on the heart."

[8] Then Jesse called Abinadab and made him pass before Samuel. He said, "Neither has Yahweh chosen this one."

[9] Then Jesse made Shammah pass by. He said, "Neither has Yahweh chosen this one."

[10] Jesse made seven of his sons pass before Samuel. And Samuel said to Jesse, "Yahweh has not chosen these."

[11] Samuel said to Jesse, "Are all your sons here?" He said, "There remains yet the youngest, and behold, he is keeping the sheep." Samuel said to Jesse, "Send and bring him, for we will not sit down until he comes here."

[12] He sent and brought him in. Now he was ruddy, with beautiful eyes and handsome in appearance. Yahweh said, "Arise, anoint him, for this is he."

[13] Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the midst of his brothers; the spirit of Yahweh rushed upon David from that day forward. Samuel arose and went to Ramah.

[14] Now the spirit of Yahweh had departed from Saul, and an evil spirit from Yahweh tormented him.

[15] Saul's servants said to him, "Behold now, an evil spirit from *Elohim* is tormenting you.

[16] Let our lord now command your servants who are before you to seek out a man who is a skillful player on the lyre; when the evil spirit from *Elohim* is upon you, he will play with his hand, and you will be well."

[17] Saul said to his servants, "Find me a man who can play well, and bring him to me."

[18] One of the young men answered, "Behold, I have seen a son of Jesse the Bethlehemite who is skillful in playing, a man of valor, a warrior, prudent in speech, and handsome; and Yahweh is with him."

[19] Saul sent messengers to Jesse and said, "Send me David your son, who is with the sheep."

[20] Jesse took a donkey laden with bread, a skin of wine, and a young goat, and sent them by David his son to Saul.

[21] David came to Saul and stood before him; Saul loved him greatly, and he became his armor-bearer.

[22] Saul sent to Jesse, saying, "Let David, please, remain in my service, for he has found favor in my eyes."

[23] Whenever the evil spirit from *Elohim* came upon Saul, David would take the lyre and play with his hand; then Saul would be refreshed and would be well, and the evil spirit would depart from him.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"harp" → "lyre"** (v16, 23) — Hebrew *kinnor*. Lyre, not modern harp.
- **"cunning player" → "skillful player"** (v16).
- **"goodly to look to" → "handsome in appearance"** (v12).
- **"refused / have chosen not" → "rejected / have not chosen"** (v7) — Hebrew *ma'astihu*.
- **"an ass laden" → "a donkey laden"** (v20).
- **"bottle" → "skin"** (v20).
- **"prudent in matters" → "prudent in speech"** (v18) — Hebrew *nevon davar*.

### Notes
- **David's anointing** — private and hidden. Compared with Saul's public anointing, David's is familial, unexpected, with divine criterion ("Yahweh looks on the heart").
- **"Yahweh looks on the heart"** (v7) — famous verse. Divine criterion of choice opposed to human standards.
- **Seven rejected brothers** — eight total sons of Jesse. David (youngest, eighth) is the chosen one. Compare Cinderella / younger-brother motifs throughout Genesis (Jacob over Esau, Joseph over his brothers).
- **"Evil spirit from Yahweh"** (v14) — theologically challenging. Ancient Near Eastern worldview: no dualism — all spirits under Yahweh's sovereignty. Whether the spirit is personified or depression/bipolar-like affliction remains debated.
- **David's initial entry to Saul's court** — as musician and armor-bearer. Set-up for the Goliath narrative (ch 17) which complicates this narrative (two traditions of David's introduction).
- **Music as spiritual therapy** (v23) — the first biblical therapeutic music. David's psalm-tradition begins here.
