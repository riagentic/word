# 1 Samuel 10

[1] Samuel took a flask of oil and poured it on Saul's head; then he kissed him and said, "Has not Yahweh anointed you prince over his inheritance?

[2] When you depart from me today, you will find two men by Rachel's tomb at the border of Benjamin at Zelzah; they will say to you, 'The donkeys that you went to seek are found. Now behold, your father has left off worrying about the donkeys and is anxious about you, saying, "What shall I do about my son?"'

[3] Then you shall go on from there and come to the oak of Tabor; three men going up to *Elohim* at Bethel shall meet you there — one carrying three young goats, another carrying three loaves of bread, and another carrying a skin of wine.

[4] They will greet you and give you two loaves of bread, which you shall accept from their hand.

[5] After that you shall come to the hill of *Elohim*, where there is a Philistine garrison. As you come to the city, you shall meet a band of prophets coming down from the high place with harp, tambourine, flute, and lyre before them, and they will be prophesying.

[6] The spirit of Yahweh will rush upon you, and you will prophesy with them and be turned into another man.

[7] When these signs meet you, do for yourself what your hand finds to do, for *Elohim* is with you.

[8] You shall go down before me to Gilgal; behold, I am coming down to you to offer burnt offerings and sacrifice peace offerings. Seven days you shall wait, until I come to you and show you what you shall do."

[9] When he turned his back to depart from Samuel, *Elohim* gave him another heart, and all these signs came to pass that day.

[10] When they came to Gibeah, behold, a band of prophets met him, and the spirit of *Elohim* rushed upon him, and he prophesied among them.

[11] When all who knew him previously saw that he was prophesying with the prophets, the people said to one another, "What has come upon the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?"

[12] A man of the place answered, "And who is their father?" Therefore it became a proverb: "Is Saul also among the prophets?"

[13] When he had finished prophesying, he came to the high place.

[14] Saul's uncle said to him and to his servant, "Where did you go?" He said, "To seek the donkeys; when we saw that they were not to be found, we went to Samuel."

[15] Saul's uncle said, "Please tell me what Samuel said to you."

[16] Saul said to his uncle, "He told us plainly that the donkeys had been found." But about the matter of the kingship, of which Samuel had spoken, he did not tell him anything.

[17] Samuel summoned the people to Yahweh at Mizpah

[18] and said to the children of Israel, "Thus says Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel: 'I brought up Israel out of Egypt and delivered you from the hand of the Egyptians and from the hand of all the kingdoms that oppressed you.'

[19] But you have this day rejected your *Elohim*, who saves you out of all your calamities and distresses, and have said to him, 'No — set a king over us.' Now therefore present yourselves before Yahweh by your tribes and by your clans."

[20] When Samuel had brought near all the tribes of Israel, the tribe of Benjamin was chosen by lot.

[21] He brought near the tribe of Benjamin by its clans, and the clan of the Matrites was chosen by lot; and Saul son of Kish was chosen by lot. But when they sought him, he could not be found.

[22] So they inquired further of Yahweh: "Has the man come here yet?" Yahweh answered, "Behold, he has hidden himself among the baggage."

[23] Then they ran and brought him from there; and when he stood among the people, he was taller than any of the people, from his shoulders upward.

[24] Samuel said to all the people, "Do you see him whom Yahweh has chosen? There is none like him among all the people." And all the people shouted, "Long live the king!"

[25] Samuel told the people the rules of the kingship, wrote it in a book, and laid it up before Yahweh. Then Samuel sent all the people away, each to his home.

[26] Saul also went to his home at Gibeah; with him went men of valor whose hearts *Elohim* had touched.

[27] But worthless fellows said, "How shall this man save us?" They despised him and brought him no present. But he held his peace.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv3, 5, 7, 9, 10, 18, 19, 26) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"psaltery, tabret, pipe, harp" → "harp, tambourine, flute, lyre"** (v5) — Hebrew *nevel, tof, chalil, kinnor*. Modernized instrument names.
- **"among the stuff" → "among the baggage"** (v22) — Hebrew *el ha-kelim*. Among the gear / luggage.
- **"God save the king" → "Long live the king"** (v24) — Hebrew *yechi ha-melekh*. "May the king live." Standard acclamation.
- **"the children of Belial" → "worthless fellows"** (v27).

### Notes
- **Anointing** (v1) — Saul is Israel's first anointed king (*mashiach*). Private act.
- **Three signs** (v2–6) — signs of confirmation for Saul: two men about the donkeys; three men with gifts; prophetic band empowerment.
- **"Is Saul also among the prophets?"** (v11–12) — proverbial surprise. Twice again in the story (19:24). Saul's spirit-experience contrasts with his eventual self-destruction.
- **Hidden among the baggage** (v22) — Saul's diffidence. Either true humility or ominous reluctance; the ambiguity sets up his character.
- **Long live the king** (v24) — formal royal acclamation. The monarchy is established.
- **"Book of the kingship"** (v25) — Samuel writes it down. Probably the Torah-law of the king (Deut 17:14–20) plus additional regulations.
- **"How shall this man save us?"** (v27) — first opposition to Saul. Saul's initial self-restraint ("he held his peace") is exemplary.
