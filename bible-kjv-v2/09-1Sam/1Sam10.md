# 1 Samuel 10

[1] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ took the flask of oil and poured it on his head, and he kissed him and said, "Is it not that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has anointed you over his inheritance as leader?

[2] When you go today from me, you will find two men by the burial place of Rachel, in the border of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, at ⟦zelzah|Tzeltzach|Zelzah⟧; and they will say to you, 'The she-donkeys that you went to seek have been found; and behold, your father has abandoned the matters of the she-donkeys and is anxious for you, saying, "What shall I do for my son?"'

[3] And you shall pass on from there and beyond, and you shall come to the oak of ⟦tabor|Tavor|Tabor⟧; and three men will find you there, going up to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ at ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧: one carrying three kids, and one carrying three loaves of bread, and one carrying a jar of wine.

[4] And they will ask you about ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, and they will give you two of bread, and you shall take them from their hand.

[5] After that you will come to the hill of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, where the garrisons of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ are; and it shall be, as you come there to the city, that you will come upon a band of prophets coming down from the high place, and before them harp and timbrel and flute and lyre, and they will be prophesying.

[6] And the spirit of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will rush upon you, and you will prophesy with them, and you will be turned into another man.

[7] And it shall be, when these signs come to you, do for yourself what your hand finds, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is with you.

[8] And you shall go down before me to Gilgal; and behold, I am coming down to you to offer up burnt offerings, to sacrifice sacrifices of peace offerings. Seven days you shall wait, until I come to you, and I will make known to you what you shall do."

[9] And it came to pass, as he turned his shoulder to go from ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧, that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ turned for him another heart; and all these signs came on that day.

[10] And they came there, to ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧, and behold, a band of prophets to meet him; and the spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rushed upon him, and he prophesied in their midst.

[11] And it came to pass, all who knew him from yesterday and the day before — they saw, and behold, with prophets he was prophesying; and the people said, each man to his neighbor, "What is this that has happened to the son of ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧? Is ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ also among the prophets?"

[12] And a man from there answered and said, "And who is their father?" Therefore it became a proverb: "Is ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ also among the prophets?"

[13] And he finished prophesying, and he came to the high place.

[14] And the uncle of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to him and to his young man, "Where did you go?" And he said, "To seek the she-donkeys; and we saw that there was nothing, and we came to ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧."

[15] And the uncle of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "Tell me, please, what ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ said to you."

[16] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to his uncle, "He surely told us that the she-donkeys had been found." But the matter of the kingship he did not tell him, which ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ had said.

[17] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ called out the people to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧.

[18] And he said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'I, I brought up ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and I delivered you from the hand of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and from the hand of all the kingdoms that were oppressing you.'

[19] And you, today you have rejected your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who is himself a savior for you from all your evils and your distresses, and you have said to him, 'A king you shall set over us.' And now, take your stand before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ by your tribes and by your thousands."

[20] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ brought near all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ was captured.

[21] And he brought near the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ by its clans, and the clan of the ⟦matrites|Matri|Matrites⟧ was captured; and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ son of ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧ was captured, and they sought him, and he was not found.

[22] And they asked again of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "Has a man yet come here?" And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "Behold, he has hidden himself among the gear."

[23] And they ran and took him from there, and he took his stand in the midst of the people, and he was taller than all the people from his shoulder and upward.

[24] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ said to all the people, "Have you seen him whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has chosen, that there is none like him among all the people?" And all the people shouted and said, "May the king live!"

[25] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ spoke to the people the judgment of the kingship, and he wrote it in the book and laid it up before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ sent away all the people, each man to his house.

[26] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ also went to his house, to ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧; and there went with him the force whose heart ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had touched.

[27] And ⟦belial|sons of Beliyyaal|sons of worthlessness⟧ said, "How shall this one save us?" And they despised him and did not bring him an offering. And he was like one being silent.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept. In v3, 5, 7 the Hebrew has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים; in v9, 10, 26 it has none. **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of וַ / וְ is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v18).
- **"a vial of oil, and poured it upon his head" → "the flask of oil and poured it on his head"** (v1) — Hebrew אֶת־פַּךְ הַשֶּׁמֶן, with the article; "his head", the name Saul is not written. "It" is supplied. **"Is it not because the LORD hath anointed thee to be captain over his inheritance?" → "Is it not that Yahweh has anointed you over his inheritance as leader?"** — Hebrew הֲלוֹא כִּי־מְשָׁחֲךָ יְהוָה עַל־נַחֲלָתוֹ לְנָגִיד, in this order. נָגִיד *nagid* is "leader" (9:16), not "king" or "prince". The Masoretic Text is much shorter here than the Greek, which goes on: "… anointed you as ruler over his people, over Israel? And you shall reign over the people of the Lord and save them from the hand of their enemies around; and this shall be the sign to you that the Lord has anointed you over his inheritance as ruler" (from memory). Many take the Hebrew to have lost these words by a slip of the eye from one "anointed you" to the next. The Masoretic Text is translated; nothing of the longer reading is in the verse.
- **"When thou art departed from me to day, then thou shalt find two men by Rachel's sepulchre" → "When you go today from me, you will find two men by the burial place of Rachel"** (v2) — Hebrew קְבֻרַת רָחֵל (Gen 35:20). **"at Zelzah"** — Hebrew בְּצֶלְצַח, found only here; taken as a place name, but not certain. **"thy father hath left the care of the asses, and sorroweth for you" → "your father has abandoned the matters of the she-donkeys and is anxious for you"** — Hebrew נָטַשׁ "abandon"; דִּבְרֵי "the words / matters of"; "you" is plural. **"for my son"** — Hebrew לִבְנִי "for / about my son".
- **"Then shalt thou go on forward from thence" → "And you shall pass on from there and beyond"** (v3) — Hebrew וְחָלַפְתָּ מִשָּׁם וָהָלְאָה. **"the plain of Tabor" → "the oak of Tabor"** — Hebrew אֵלוֹן, a great tree. **"there shall meet thee three men" → "three men will find you there"** — Hebrew מָצָא, the verb of v2. **"going up to God to Bethel" → "going up to Elohim at Bethel"** — Hebrew אֶל־הָאֱלֹהִים בֵּית־אֵל, "to the Elohim, Bethel"; "at" is supplied. **"a bottle of wine" → "a jar of wine"** — Hebrew נֵבֶל, a skin or a jar for wine; which is not certain. The same word is the "harp" of v5.
- **"they will salute thee, and give thee two loaves of bread; which thou shalt receive of their hands" → "they will ask you about shalom, and they will give you two of bread, and you shall take them from their hand"** (v4) — Hebrew וְשָׁאֲלוּ לְךָ לְשָׁלוֹם: the greeting is to ask after one's *shalom*. שְׁתֵּי־לֶחֶם is "two of bread" (the word "loaves" of v3 is not repeated). "Them" is supplied; "hand" is singular.
- **"the hill of God, where is the garrison of the Philistines" → "the hill of Elohim, where the garrisons of the Philistines are"** (v5) — Hebrew גִּבְעַת הָאֱלֹהִים; גִּבְעָה "hill" is the same word as the name Gibeah (v10, 26), and the same place may be meant. נְצִבֵי is plural; the word (from "set up, station") is a garrison, a governor, or a pillar: which is meant is not certain. **"thou shalt meet a company of prophets" → "you will come upon a band of prophets"** (v5, 10) — Hebrew פָּגַע; חֶבֶל, literally "a cord, a line". **"with a psaltery, and a tabret, and a pipe, and a harp, before them" → "and before them harp and timbrel and flute and lyre"** — Hebrew נֵבֶל, תֹּף, חָלִיל, כִּנּוֹר: two stringed instruments, a hand drum and a wind instrument; the exact kinds are not certain. **"and they shall prophesy" → "and they will be prophesying"** — a participle. הִתְנַבֵּא (v5, 6, 10, 13) is "prophesy", to act or speak as a prophet; what they did is not described. In v11 the form is another (nifal נִבָּא) with the same English.
- **"the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee" → "the spirit of Yahweh will rush upon you"** (v6, 10) — Hebrew צָלַח עַל, the verb used of Samson (Judg 14:6): to come upon with force. רוּחַ is "spirit, wind, breath". In v10 it is "the spirit of Elohim".
- **"when these signs are come unto thee, that thou do as occasion serve thee" → "when these signs come to you, do for yourself what your hand finds"** (v7) — Hebrew עֲשֵׂה לְךָ אֲשֶׁר תִּמְצָא יָדֶךָ. "Come" follows the reading tradition (qere); the written form differs only in spelling.
- **"to sacrifice sacrifices of peace offerings"** (v8) — Hebrew לִזְבֹּחַ זִבְחֵי שְׁלָמִים. **"seven days shalt thou tarry" → "Seven days you shall wait"**. **"shew thee" → "make known to you"** — Hebrew וְהוֹדַעְתִּי.
- **"when he had turned his back to go from Samuel, God gave him another heart" → "as he turned his shoulder to go from Samuel, that Elohim turned for him another heart"** (v9) — Hebrew שִׁכְמוֹ "his shoulder"; וַיַּהֲפָךְ־לוֹ, the verb "turn" of v6 ("you will be turned into another man"): Elohim changed his heart into another. **"came to pass" → "came"** — Hebrew וַיָּבֹאוּ, as in v7.
- **"when they came thither to the hill" → "they came there, to Gibeah"** (v10) — Hebrew הַגִּבְעָתָה "to the hill / to the Gibeah", with the article; see v5. **"a company of prophets met him" → "a band of prophets to meet him"** — the Hebrew has no verb. **"among them" → "in their midst"**.
- **"when all that knew him beforetime saw that, behold, he prophesied among the prophets" → "all who knew him from yesterday and the day before — they saw, and behold, with prophets he was prophesying"** (v11) — Hebrew מֵאִתְּמוֹל שִׁלְשׁוֹם, literally "from yesterday, the third day" (= before); עִם־נְבִאִים "with prophets", without the article. **"one to another" → "each man to his neighbor"**. **"What is this that is come unto the son of Kish?" → "What is this that has happened to the son of Kish?"**
- **"Is Saul also among the prophets?"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew הֲגַם שָׁאוּל בַּנְּבִיאִים; the same words in 19:24, with the same English. **"one of the same place" → "a man from there"** (v12). **"But who is their father?" → "And who is their father?"** — Hebrew וּמִי אֲבִיהֶם; what the question means (who leads these prophets? what has parentage to do with it?) is not said. **"proverb"** — Hebrew מָשָׁל, a saying built on a likeness.
- **"when he had made an end of prophesying" → "he finished prophesying"** (v13). **"he came to the high place"** — so the Masoretic Text (הַבָּמָה); "to the house" is a common conjecture (from memory) and is not in the verse.
- **"Whither went ye?" → "Where did you go?"** (v14). **"when we saw that they were no where" → "and we saw that there was nothing"** — Hebrew כִי־אַיִן, as 9:4.
- **"He told us plainly" → "He surely told us"** (v16) — Hebrew הַגֵּד הִגִּיד, literally "telling he told": the infinitive with its verb. **"of the matter of the kingdom, whereof Samuel spake, he told him not" → "the matter of the kingship he did not tell him, which Samuel had said"** — Hebrew דְּבַר הַמְּלוּכָה "the word / matter of the kingship"; the relative clause stands last.
- **"called the people together unto the LORD to Mizpeh" → "called out the people to Yahweh, to Mizpah"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיַּצְעֵק, the causative of "cry out": to summon by a cry.
- **"I brought up Israel"** (v18) — the pronoun is written: אָנֹכִי. **"out of the hand of the Egyptians" → "from the hand of Egypt"** — Hebrew מִצְרַיִם. **"of them that oppressed you" → "that were oppressing you"** — a participle; it is masculine, though "kingdoms" is feminine.
- **"who himself saved you out of all your adversities and your tribulations" → "who is himself a savior for you from all your evils and your distresses"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־הוּא מוֹשִׁיעַ לָכֶם, a participle; רָעוֹת "evils", צָרוֹת "distresses". **"ye have said unto him, Nay, but set a king over us" → "you have said to him, 'A king you shall set over us'"** — the Masoretic Text has לוֹ "to him"; many manuscripts and the versions have לֹא "No" (from memory), as in 8:19; 12:12. The כִּי before "a king" introduces the words spoken (or, after an implied refusal, "but"). **"present yourselves" → "take your stand"** — Hebrew הִתְיַצְּבוּ. **"by your thousands"** — Hebrew אַלְפֵיכֶם: the "thousand" as a division of a tribe; in v21 the word is "clan".
- **"had caused all the tribes of Israel to come near, the tribe of Benjamin was taken" → "brought near all the tribes of Israel, and the tribe of Benjamin was captured"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew נִלְכַּד, the verb for capturing a city, used of the one marked out (Josh 7:14–18). The lot is not named in the Hebrew.
- **"by their families, the family of Matri was taken" → "by its clans, and the clan of the Matrites was captured"** (v21) — "clans" is the reading tradition (qere); the written text has the singular. הַמַּטְרִי is "the Matrite", with the article. The Greek adds that the clan of Matri was brought near man by man (from memory); the Masoretic Text goes straight to Saul. **"when they sought him, he could not be found" → "they sought him, and he was not found"**.
- **"they enquired of the LORD further, if the man should yet come thither" → "they asked again of Yahweh, 'Has a man yet come here?'"** (v22) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁאֲלוּ־עוֹד בַּיהוָה (the verb שָׁאַל "ask", the root of the name Saul); הֲבָא עוֹד הֲלֹם אִישׁ, word for word: "has there come yet here a man?" — "a man", without the article. The question is difficult ("Is there yet a man to come here?" or "Has anyone else come here?"); the Greek has "Has the man come here?" (from memory). The Masoretic Text is translated. **"among the stuff" → "among the gear"** — Hebrew אֶל־הַכֵּלִים: the baggage; כְּלִי is any made article.
- **"when he stood among the people" → "he took his stand in the midst of the people"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּתְיַצֵּב. **"he was higher than any of the people from his shoulders and upward" → "he was taller than all the people from his shoulder and upward"** — as 9:2.
- **"See ye him whom the LORD hath chosen, that there is none like him" → "Have you seen him whom Yahweh has chosen, that there is none like him"** (v24) — Hebrew הַרְּאִיתֶם, a perfect. **"God save the king" → "May the king live!"** — Hebrew יְחִי הַמֶּלֶךְ, a wish: "let the king live". The Hebrew has no "God" and no "long".
- **"told the people the manner of the kingdom, and wrote it in a book" → "spoke to the people the judgment of the kingship, and he wrote it in the book"** (v25) — Hebrew מִשְׁפַּט הַמְּלֻכָה: מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" as in "the judgment of the king" (8:9, 11), there what the king will take as his right, here the rule laid down for the kingship. בַּסֵּפֶר has the article in the pointing ("in the book", a written document). "It" is supplied twice. **"laid it up before the LORD"** — Hebrew וַיַּנַּח. **"every man to his house" → "each man to his house"**.
- **"there went with him a band of men, whose hearts God had touched" → "there went with him the force whose heart Elohim had touched"** (v26) — Hebrew הַחַיִל, "the force" (ability, strength; a body of able men); the Greek has "sons of ability" (from memory), set against the "sons of Beliyyaal" of v27. "Heart" is singular with a plural suffix.
- **"the children of Belial" → "sons of Beliyyaal"** (v27) — Hebrew בְּנֵי בְלִיַּעַל; בְּלִיַּעַל is a common noun, "worthlessness", not a name. **"How shall this man save us?" → "How shall this one save us?"** — Hebrew זֶה "this one". **"brought him no presents" → "did not bring him an offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה, a gift brought in homage; the word of the grain offering. **"But he held his peace" → "And he was like one being silent"** — Hebrew וַיְהִי כְּמַחֲרִישׁ, word for word; the Masoretic Text ends the chapter here. The Greek reads "And it came to pass after about a month" (כְּמֵחֹדֶשׁ for כְּמַחֲרִישׁ) and joins it to 11:1 (from memory). A Samuel scroll from Qumran has a longer text before 11:1, telling how Nahash king of the sons of Ammon had oppressed Gad and Reuben and gouged out every right eye, and that seven thousand men had escaped to Jabesh-gilead (from memory; Josephus tells the same). None of this is in the Masoretic Text, and none of it is in the verse.

### Notes
- **Anointing** (v1) — Saul is the first anointed one (*mashiach*) over Israel; the word here is "leader", not yet "king". Private act.
- **Three signs** (v2–6) — signs of confirmation for Saul: two men about the she-donkeys; three men with gifts; the band of prophets and the spirit.
- **"Is Saul also among the prophets?"** (v11–12) — proverbial surprise. Once again in the story (19:24). Saul's spirit-experience contrasts with his eventual self-destruction.
- **Hidden among the gear** (v22) — Saul's diffidence. Either true humility or ominous reluctance; the ambiguity sets up his character.
- **May the king live** (v24) — formal royal acclamation. The monarchy is established.
- **"The judgment of the kingship"** (v25) — Samuel writes it down. Probably the Torah-law of the king (Deut 17:14–20) plus additional regulations.
- **"How shall this one save us?"** (v27) — first opposition to Saul. Saul is "like one being silent": he does not answer it.
