# 1 Samuel 27

[1] David said in his heart, "I shall now perish one day by the hand of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧. There is nothing better for me than to escape speedily into the land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; then ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ will despair of seeking me any more within any of the territory of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and I shall escape out of his hand."

[2] So David arose and crossed over with the six hundred men who were with him to Achish son of Maoch, king of ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧.

[3] David dwelt with Achish at ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧, he and his men, every man with his household — David with his two wives, ⟦ahinoam|Achinoam|Ahinoam⟧ the ⟦jezreel|Yizreelit|Jezreelitess⟧ and ⟦abigail|Avigayil|Abigail⟧ the wife of ⟦nabal|Naval|Nabal⟧ the ⟦carmel|Karmeli|Carmelite⟧.

[4] When it was told ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ that David had fled to ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧, he sought him no more.

[5] David said to Achish, "If I have found favor in your eyes, let them give me a place in some town in the country, that I may dwell there; why should your servant dwell in the royal city with you?"

[6] So Achish gave him ⟦ziklag|Tziqlag|Ziklag⟧ that day; therefore ⟦ziklag|Tziqlag|Ziklag⟧ belongs to the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to this day.

[7] The number of days that David dwelt in the country of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ was a year and four months.

[8] David and his men went up and raided the ⟦geshurites|Geshuri|Geshurites⟧, the ⟦girzites|Girzi|Girzites⟧, and the ⟦amalekites|Amaleqim|Amalekites⟧; for those nations were the inhabitants of the land from ancient times, as you go to Shur, to the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[9] David would strike the land and leave neither man nor woman alive; he would take away the sheep, the oxen, the donkeys, the camels, and the garments, and return and come to Achish.

[10] When Achish asked, "Against whom have you made a raid today?" David would say, "Against the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧," or "Against the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ of the ⟦jerahmeelites|Yerachmeeli|Jerahmeelites⟧," or "Against the ⟦negev|Negev|Negeb⟧ of the ⟦kenites|Qenim|Kenites⟧."

[11] David would save neither man nor woman alive to bring to ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧, thinking, "Lest they should tell about us, saying, 'So David has done.'" Such was his custom all the while he dwelt in the country of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧.

[12] Achish trusted David, saying, "He has made himself utterly detestable to his people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; therefore he will be my servant forever."

## Changes

- **"coast" → "territory"** (v1).
- **"made a road" → "made a raid"** (v10) — archaic.
- **"south" → "Negev"** (v10) — as elsewhere.

### Notes
- **David in Philistine service** — morally complicated. He pretends loyalty to Achish while actually raiding the southern Canaanite/Amalekite populations. Outright deception.
- **Ziklag** — David's base. A Philistine-granted city that becomes his capital-in-waiting. Will host the historic transition (ch 30, 2 Sam 2).
- **The dark side of David** — he destroys whole populations to prevent information leakage. Moral darkening as his situation deepens.
- **"Kings of Judah"** (v6) — editorial note confirming a later-than-events composition: the writer knows of a line of Judahite kings following David.
- **16 months duration** (v7) — substantive period as a Philistine vassal. During this time the Saul–Philistine war is building.
