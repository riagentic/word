# 1 Samuel 26

[1] The Ziphites came to Saul at Gibeah, saying, "Is not David hiding on the hill of Hachilah, which is before Jeshimon?"

[2] Then Saul arose and went down to the wilderness of Ziph, with three thousand chosen men of Israel with him, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.

[3] Saul encamped on the hill of Hachilah, which is before Jeshimon, beside the road. But David remained in the wilderness, and he saw that Saul had come after him into the wilderness.

[4] David sent out spies and learned that Saul had indeed come.

[5] Then David arose and came to the place where Saul had encamped. David saw the place where Saul lay, with Abner son of Ner, the commander of his army; Saul was lying within the encampment, with the people encamped around him.

[6] Then David said to Ahimelech the Hittite and to Abishai son of Zeruiah, brother of Joab, "Who will go down with me to Saul to the camp?" Abishai said, "I will go down with you."

[7] So David and Abishai came to the people by night; behold, Saul lay sleeping within the encampment, with his spear stuck in the ground at his head; Abner and the people lay around him.

[8] Abishai said to David, "*Elohim* has given your enemy into your hand this day. Now therefore let me strike him to the ground with the spear at one stroke; I will not strike him a second time."

[9] But David said to Abishai, "Do not destroy him, for who can put out his hand against the anointed of Yahweh and be guiltless?"

[10] David said further, "As Yahweh lives, Yahweh will strike him, or his day will come to die, or he will go down into battle and perish.

[11] Yahweh forbid that I should put out my hand against the anointed of Yahweh. But take the spear that is at his head and the jug of water, and let us go."

[12] So David took the spear and the jug of water from Saul's head, and they went away. No one saw it or knew it, nor did any awaken, for they were all asleep, because a deep sleep from Yahweh had fallen upon them.

[13] Then David went over to the other side and stood on the top of the hill far off, a great distance being between them.

[14] David called to the people and to Abner son of Ner, saying, "Will you not answer, Abner?" Abner answered, "Who are you that calls to the king?"

[15] David said to Abner, "Are you not a man? Who is like you in Israel? Why then have you not guarded your lord the king? For one of the people came in to destroy the king your lord.

[16] This thing you have done is not good. As Yahweh lives, you deserve to die, because you have not guarded your lord, the anointed of Yahweh. And now see where the king's spear is, and the jug of water that was at his head!"

[17] Saul recognized David's voice and said, "Is this your voice, my son David?" David said, "It is my voice, my lord, O king."

[18] He said, "Why does my lord thus pursue his servant? What have I done? What evil is in my hand?

[19] Now therefore, please let my lord the king hear the words of his servant. If Yahweh has stirred you up against me, let him accept an offering; but if it is the sons of men, may they be cursed before Yahweh! For they have driven me out this day so that I may have no share in the inheritance of Yahweh, saying, 'Go, serve other gods.'

[20] Now therefore do not let my blood fall to the earth far from the presence of Yahweh. For the king of Israel has come out to seek a single flea, as one hunts a partridge in the mountains."

[21] Then Saul said, "I have sinned. Return, my son David, for I will no more do you harm, because my life was precious in your eyes this day. Behold, I have played the fool and have erred greatly."

[22] David answered, "Behold, the spear of the king! Let one of the young men come over and take it.

[23] May Yahweh repay every man his righteousness and his faithfulness; for Yahweh gave you today into my hand, but I would not put out my hand against the anointed of Yahweh.

[24] Behold, as your life was much esteemed this day in my eyes, so may my life be much esteemed in the eyes of Yahweh, and may he deliver me out of all distress."

[25] Then Saul said to David, "Blessed be you, my son David! You will do many things and will also prevail." So David went on his way, and Saul returned to his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"bolster" → "head"** (v7, 11, 12, 16) — Hebrew מְרַאֲשֹׁתָיו *mera'ashotav*. "At his head-side."
- **"trench" → "encampment"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew מַעְגָּל *ma'gal*. Camp-ring.
- **"cruse" → "jug"** (v11, 12).
- **"cease to abide" → "no share in"** (v19) — idiom.
- **"have played the fool" → "played the fool"** (v21) — Hebrew *hiskelti*. "Acted foolishly."

### Notes
- **Second sparing of Saul** — deliberate narrative repetition. Cave (ch 24), now camp. Both times David refuses to strike. Possibly two traditions of one event, or one of two genuine occurrences.
- **Abishai's zeal** (v8) — echoes David's own men in ch 24. David must restrain a militant nephew.
- **"Yahweh will strike him"** (v10) — three alternatives: direct divine strike, natural death, battle-death. The third fulfills at Gilboa (ch 31).
- **Deep sleep from Yahweh** (v12) — the *tardemah*, sleep-of-the-divinely-imposed type (cf. Gen 2:21 for Adam). Enables David's close approach.
- **Taunt to Abner** (v14–16) — mock-chiding the commander of the guard. David's dark humor.
- **"Serve other gods"** (v19) — Saul's pursuit has exiled David from the land / sanctuary / covenant community. Going abroad forces exposure to foreign cults.
- **Saul's "blessed be David"** (v25) — final meeting between the two. Saul acknowledges David's future blessing.
