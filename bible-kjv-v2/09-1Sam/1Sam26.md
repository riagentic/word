## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"bolster" → "head"** (v7, 11, 12, 16) — Hebrew מְרַאֲשֹׁתָיו *mera'ashotav*. "At his head-side."
- **"trench" → "encampment"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew מַעְגָּל *ma'gal*. Camp-ring.
- **"cruse" → "jug"** (v11, 12).
- **"cease to abide" → "no share in"** (v19) — idiom.
- **"have played the fool" → "played the fool"** (v21) — Hebrew *hiskelti*. "Acted foolishly."

### Notes
- **Second sparing of Saul** — deliberate narrative repetition. Cave (ch 24), now camp. Both times David refuses to strike. Possibly two traditions of one event, or one of two genuine occurrences.
- **Abishai's zeal** (v8) — echoes David's own men in ch 24. David must restrain a militant nephew.
- **"Yahweh will strike him"** (v10) — three alternatives: direct divine strike, natural death, battle-death. The third fulfills at Gilboa (ch 31).
- **Deep sleep from Yahweh** (v12) — the *tardemah*, sleep-of-the-divinely-imposed type (cf. Gen 2:21 for Adam). Enables David's close approach.
- **Taunt to Abner** (v14–16) — mock-chiding the commander of the guard. David's dark humor.
- **"Serve other gods"** (v19) — Saul's pursuit has exiled David from the land / sanctuary / covenant community. Going abroad forces exposure to foreign cults.
- **Saul's "blessed be David"** (v25) — final meeting between the two. Saul acknowledges David's future blessing.
