## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8) — Hebrew *Elohim*, here without the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Doth not David hide himself in the hill of Hachilah, which is before Jeshimon" → "Is not David hiding himself on the hill of Hachilah, facing the Jeshimon"** (v1, 3) — Hebrew עַל פְּנֵי "on the face of" = facing. הַיְשִׁימֹן has the article: the word is a common noun, "the desert / wasteland" (Deut 32:10), used here as the name of the waste east of Ziph. "which is" stands only in v3.
- **"having three thousand chosen men of Israel with him" → "and with him three thousand men, chosen ones of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew אִישׁ בְּחוּרֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"pitched" → "camped"; "abode" → "was dwelling"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew חָנָה, the verb of מַחֲנֶה "camp" (v6); יֹשֵׁב, a participle of יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"understood that Saul was come in very deed" → "knew that Saul had come for sure"** (v4) — Hebrew אֶל־נָכוֹן, literally "to what is established" (נָכוֹן, niphal of כּוּן); the same words stand in 23:23 ("with what is sure"). Some take נָכוֹן here as the name of a place ("to Nachon").
- **"captain of his host" → "the commander of his host"** (v5) — Hebrew שַׂר־צְבָאוֹ; שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander"; צָבָא "host".
- **"trench" → "the enclosure"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew מַעְגָּל *ma'gal*, from a root "round" (עֲגָלָה "wagon"); elsewhere "track". In 1 Samuel (17:20; 26:5, 7) it names some part of a camp: the round of the camp, or a ring of wagons. The exact sense is uncertain. Not the word "camp" (מַחֲנֶה, v6).
- **"Then answered David and said" kept** (v6, 14, 22) — two verbs, וַיַּעַן … וַיֹּאמֶר; in v6 also לֵאמֹר "saying".
- **"stuck in the ground at his bolster" → "pressed into the earth at his head"** (v7, 11, 12, 16) — Hebrew מְעוּכָה, "pressed" (the participle of Lev 22:24 "bruised"); אֶרֶץ "earth". מְרַאֲשֹׁתָיו *mera'ashotav* is "the place at his head", from רֹאשׁ "head". In v7, 11, 16 and in סְבִיבֹתָיו "around him" (v5, 7) the written text has a shorter spelling and the reading tradition (qere) the full one; the meaning is the same.
- **"God hath delivered" → "*Elohim* has shut up"** (v8) — Hebrew סִגַּר (piel) … בְּיָדֶךָ, "has shut up … into your hand": delivered him up as one shut in (17:46; 24:18).
- **"let me smite him … with the spear even to the earth at once, and I will not smite him the second time" → "let me strike him, please, with the spear and into the earth, one time, and I will not do it to him a second time"** (v8) — Hebrew אַכֶּנּוּ נָא בַּחֲנִית וּבָאָרֶץ פַּעַם אַחַת וְלֹא אֶשְׁנֶה לוֹ, word for word: "with the spear and into the earth", that is, a thrust that pins him to the earth. נָא "please" is kept. שָׁנָה is "do a second time"; "strike" is not repeated in the Hebrew.
- **"Destroy him not" kept** (v9, 15) — Hebrew הִשְׁחִית, the verb rendered "ruin" elsewhere in this translation (Gen 6:13). Of the killing of a man English "ruin" would say something else (to ruin a man is to bring him down, not to kill him), so "destroy" stands here.
- **"who can stretch forth his hand … and be guiltless" → "who has stretched out his hand … and been clear"** (v9) — Hebrew מִי שָׁלַח יָדוֹ … וְנִקָּה: two perfects, "who has ever done so and been held clear". נִקָּה (niphal) is "be clear, be free of guilt". No "can" in the Hebrew.
- **"the LORD'S anointed" → "the anointed of Yahweh"** (v9, 11, 16, 23) — Hebrew מְשִׁיחַ יְהוָה *meshiach Yahweh*; מָשִׁיחַ "anointed one", of the king anointed with oil (10:1).
- **"the LORD shall smite him" → "surely Yahweh will smite him"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־יְהוָה יִגָּפֶנּוּ: after the oath "As Yahweh lives" כִּי אִם brings in what is sworn ("surely", "no, but"). נָגַף "smite" is a blow from God (25:38), kept apart from הִכָּה "strike" (v8).
- **"his day shall come to die" → "his day will come and he will die"; "perish" → "be swept away"** (v10) — Hebrew וָמֵת "and he will die"; נִסְפָּה, niphal of סָפָה "sweep away" (Gen 18:23; 1 Sam 12:25; 27:1).
- **"The LORD forbid that I should stretch forth" → "Far be it from me, from Yahweh, to stretch out"** (v11) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לִּי מֵיהוָה, literally "a profane thing to me from Yahweh": "from Yahweh" says on whose side the thing is abhorred (as seen from Yahweh, before Yahweh). The same words stand in 24:6. "Forbid" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"take thou now" → "take, please"; "cruse" → "jug"** (v11, 12, 16) — Hebrew קַח־נָא; צַפַּחַת, a small vessel for water or oil (1 Kgs 17:12; 19:6).
- **"let us go" → "let us get ourselves away"; "they gat them away" → "they got themselves away"** (v11, 12) — Hebrew וְנֵלֲכָה לָּנוּ, וַיֵּלְכוּ לָהֶם: "go" with לְ + pronoun, which turns the verb back on the subject (as לֶךְ־לְךָ Gen 12:1).
- **"no man saw it, nor knew it, neither awaked" → "there was none seeing and none knowing and none awaking"** (v12) — three participles with אֵין; no object "it".
- **"a deep sleep from the LORD" → "a deep sleep of Yahweh"** (v12) — Hebrew תַּרְדֵּמַת יְהוָה, a genitive: "the deep sleep of Yahweh". תַּרְדֵּמָה as in Gen 2:21; 15:12.
- **"went over to the other side, and stood on the top of an hill afar off; a great space being between them" → "passed over to the other side and stood on the top of the mountain, far off; great was the space between them"** (v13) — Hebrew הָעֵבֶר "the side across"; הָהָר "the mountain", with the article; רַב הַמָּקוֹם "great was the place".
- **"that criest to the king" → "who have called to the king"** (v14) — Hebrew קָרָאתָ, a perfect.
- **"Art not thou a valiant man" → "Are you not a man"** (v15) — Hebrew אִישׁ only; "valiant" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"kept" → "kept watch over"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew שָׁמַר "keep", in v15 with אֶל "to", in v16 with עַל "over".
- **"ye are worthy to die" → "you are sons of death"** (v16) — Hebrew בְנֵי־מָוֶת אַתֶּם, kept word for word: men who belong to death, who have earned it (20:31; 2 Sam 12:5). The verb turns to the plural: Abner and his men.
- **"your master, the LORD'S anointed" → "over your lord, over the anointed of Yahweh"** (v16) — עַל stands twice. אֲדֹנֵיכֶם is the word "lord" of v15, 17.
- **"knew David's voice" → "recognized the voice of David"** (v17) — Hebrew הִכִּיר "recognize". **"It is my voice, my lord, O king" → "My voice, my lord the king"** — Hebrew קוֹלִי אֲדֹנִי הַמֶּלֶךְ, with no verb.
- **"Wherefore doth my lord thus pursue after his servant" → "Why is this, that my lord is pursuing after his servant"** (v18) — Hebrew לָמָּה זֶּה. **"servant"** (v18, 19) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word "slave", used by one who speaks to his superior.
- **"have stirred thee up" → "has incited you"** (v19) — Hebrew הֵסִית (2 Sam 24:1). **"let him accept an offering" → "let him smell an offering"** — Hebrew יָרַח מִנְחָה: הֵרִיחַ is "smell" (Gen 8:21); מִנְחָה is a gift, in the cult the grain offering.
- **"if they be the children of men, cursed be they" → "if the sons of man, cursed are they"** (v19) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הָאָדָם; אֲרוּרִים הֵם has no verb ("are", not "be").
- **"from abiding in the inheritance of the LORD" → "from attaching myself to the inheritance of Yahweh"** (v19) — Hebrew מֵהִסְתַּפֵּחַ בְּ, the reflexive of סָפַח "join, attach" (2:36).
- **"before the face of the LORD" → "away from the face of Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶד פְּנֵי יְהוָה: מִנֶּגֶד is "from in front of, at a distance from". The usual rendering of מִנֶּגֶד in this translation, "opposite", would say here that the blood falls in front of Yahweh; the sense is the reverse, far from his face, in a foreign land.
- **"a flea" → "one flea"; "as when one doth hunt a partridge" → "as one pursues the partridge"** (v20) — Hebrew פַּרְעֹשׁ אֶחָד (24:14); יִרְדֹּף, the verb "pursue" of v18. הַקֹּרֵא is literally "the caller", a bird named from its call, commonly taken as the partridge; the same word means "he who calls" (v14 has the verb). The Septuagint has "my soul" for "one flea" (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"I will no more do thee harm" → "I will not do evil to you any more"** (v21) — Hebrew אָרַע לְךָ, the verb of רַע "evil". **"my soul was precious"** kept — Hebrew נַפְשִׁי "my soul", that is, my life.
- **"I have played the fool, and have erred exceedingly" → "I have acted foolishly and have erred very greatly"** (v21) — Hebrew הִסְכַּלְתִּי; וָאֶשְׁגֶּה, the verb of שְׁגָגָה "error" (Lev 4:2); הַרְבֵּה מְאֹד "very much".
- **"Behold the king's spear" → "Behold, the spear of the king"** (v22) — the reading tradition (qere) חֲנִית הַמֶּלֶךְ. The written text (ketiv) has הַחֲנִית הַמֶּלֶךְ, "Behold the spear, O king".
- **"The LORD render to every man his righteousness" → "And Yahweh will return to a man his righteousness"** (v23) — Hebrew יָשִׁיב, "bring back, return"; the form may also be a wish ("may Yahweh return"). לָאִישׁ, "to the man", is said in general: to each man. **"for the LORD delivered thee into my hand" → "in that Yahweh gave you today into a hand"** — Hebrew בְּיָד, "into a hand", with no "my"; many Hebrew manuscripts and the old versions have "into my hand" (cited from memory). **"I would not" → "I was not willing"** — Hebrew לֹא אָבִיתִי.
- **"as thy life was much set by" → "as your soul was great"** (v24) — Hebrew גָּדְלָה נַפְשְׁךָ … תִּגְדַּל נַפְשִׁי, "was great … be great"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" both times. **"tribulation" → "distress"** — Hebrew צָרָה.
- **"Blessed be thou" → "Blessed are you"** (v25) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה, with no verb.
- **"thou shalt both do great things, and also shalt still prevail" → "You will both surely do and surely prevail"** (v25) — Hebrew גַּם עָשֹׂה תַעֲשֶׂה וְגַם יָכֹל תּוּכָל, twice the infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "both doing you will do and being able you will be able". No object ("great things") is named. יָכֹל is "be able", and so "prevail, overcome" (Gen 30:8; 32:28).
- **"David went on his way" → "David went his way"** (v25) — Hebrew לְדַרְכּוֹ.

### Notes
- **Second sparing of Saul** — deliberate narrative repetition. Cave (ch 24), now camp. Both times David refuses to strike. Possibly two traditions of one event, or one of two genuine occurrences.
- **Abishai's zeal** (v8) — echoes David's own men in ch 24. David must restrain a militant nephew.
- **"Yahweh will smite him"** (v10) — three alternatives: direct divine strike, natural death, battle-death. The third fulfills at Gilboa (ch 31).
- **Deep sleep of Yahweh** (v12) — the *tardemah*, sleep-of-the-divinely-imposed type (cf. Gen 2:21 for Adam). Enables David's close approach.
- **Taunt to Abner** (v14–16) — mock-chiding the commander of the guard. David's dark humor.
- **"Serve other gods"** (v19) — Saul's pursuit has exiled David from the land / sanctuary / covenant community. Going abroad forces exposure to foreign cults.
- **Saul's "blessed are you"** (v25) — final meeting between the two. Saul acknowledges David's future blessing.
