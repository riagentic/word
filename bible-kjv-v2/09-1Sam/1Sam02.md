# 1 Samuel 2

[1] And ⟦hannah|Channah|Hannah⟧ prayed and said:

"My heart is jubilant in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
my horn is high in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
My mouth is wide against my enemies,
for I rejoice in your salvation.

[2] There is none holy like ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for there is none besides you,
and there is no rock like our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] Do not multiply your talk, so high, so high;
let not arrogance go out of your mouth;
for ⟦el|an El|a God⟧ of knowledge is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and by him deeds are measured.

[4] The bow of the mighty ones is shattered,
and those who stumbled have girded on ability.

[5] The sated have hired themselves out for bread,
and the hungry have ceased;
even the barren has borne seven,
and she of many sons languishes.

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ puts to death and makes alive;
he brings down to Sheol and brings up.

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ makes poor and makes rich;
he makes low, he also raises high.

[8] He raises up the poor from the dust;
from the ash heap he raises the needy,
to make them sit with nobles,
and a throne of glory he makes them inherit.
For to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ belong the pillars of the earth,
and he has set the world upon them.

[9] The feet of his faithful ones he will keep,
and the wicked shall be made silent in the darkness;
for not by power shall a man prevail.

[10] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ — those who contend with him shall be shattered;
against him he will thunder in the heavens.
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ will judge the ends of the earth;
and may he give strength to his king
and raise the horn of his ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧."

[11] And ⟦elkanah|Elqanah|Elkanah⟧ went to Ramah, to his house; and the boy was ministering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ before the face of Eli the priest.

[12] And the sons of Eli were ⟦belial|sons of Beliyyaal|sons of worthlessness⟧; they did not know ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And the ordinance of the priests with the people: any man sacrificing a sacrifice — the young man of the priest would come when the flesh was boiling, and the fork of three teeth in his hand,

[14] and he would strike it into the basin or into the kettle or into the cauldron or into the pot; all that the fork brought up, the priest would take with it. So they would do to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who came there, in Shiloh.

[15] Also, before they turned the fat into smoke, the young man of the priest would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, "Give flesh to roast for the priest, and he will not take boiled flesh from you, but only raw."

[16] And the man said to him, "Let them surely turn the fat into smoke first, and then take for yourself as your soul desires." And he would say, "No, for you shall give it now; and if not, I take it by force."

[17] And the sin of the young men was very great before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for the men spurned the offering of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ was ministering before the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, a boy girded with a linen ephod.

[19] And a small robe his mother would make for him, and she would bring it up to him from year to year, when she came up with her husband to sacrifice the sacrifice of the days.

[20] And Eli would bless ⟦elkanah|Elqanah|Elkanah⟧ and his wife and say, "May ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ appoint for you seed from this woman in place of the asking that was asked for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧." And they would go to his place.

[21] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ visited ⟦hannah|Channah|Hannah⟧, and she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. And the boy ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ grew with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[22] And Eli was very old; and he would hear all that his sons were doing to all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and that they lay with the women who mustered at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

[23] And he said to them, "Why do you do things like these, that I am hearing of your evil matters from all this people?

[24] No, my sons; for the report is not good that I am hearing the people of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ passing on.

[25] If a man sins against a man, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will arbitrate for him; and if against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ a man sins, who will pray for him?" And they would not listen to the voice of their father, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ desired to put them to death.

[26] And the boy ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ went on growing, and was good both with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and also with men.

[27] And a man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ came to Eli and said to him, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Did I surely reveal myself to the house of your father when they were in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, belonging to the house of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧,

[28] and choose him out of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ for myself as priest, to go up on my altar, to burn incense, to bear an ephod before me? And I gave to the house of your father all the offerings by fire of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[29] Why do you kick at my sacrifice and at my offering, which I commanded for the abode, and you honor your sons more than me, to make yourselves fat from the first of every offering of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, my people?'

[30] Therefore, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'I surely said, Your house and the house of your father shall walk before me forever'; and now, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'Far be it from me; for those who honor me I will honor, and those who despise me shall be lightly esteemed.

[31] Behold, days are coming, and I will hew down your arm and the arm of the house of your father, so that there shall not be an old man in your house.

[32] And you shall look on an adversary in the abode, in all that he does good to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and there shall not be an old man in your house all the days.

[33] And a man I will not cut off for you from my altar, to wear out your eyes and to make your soul pine; and all the increase of your house shall die as men.

[34] And this is the sign for you, which shall come upon your two sons, upon ⟦hophni|Chofni|Hophni⟧ and ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧: in one day they shall die, the two of them.

[35] And I will raise up for myself a faithful priest; as is in my heart and in my soul he shall do. And I will build for him a sure house, and he shall walk before my ⟦anointed|mashiach|anointed⟧ all the days.

[36] And it shall be that everyone who remains in your house shall come to bow down to him for a payment of silver and a round of bread, and shall say, "Attach me, please, to one of the priesthoods, to eat a bit of bread."'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 25, 27, 30) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. In v13–16, 19–20 and 22 the verbs are forms of repeated action ("would come", "would say", "would bless").
- **Hannah's prayer** (v1–10) — poetry: one Hebrew line to a printed line, in the order of the Hebrew; words that stand side by side in the Hebrew are kept apart in English.
- **"My heart rejoiceth" → "My heart is jubilant"** (v1) — Hebrew עָלַץ; "rejoice" is kept for שָׂמַח in the last line. The four verbs of the verse are perfects of a state reached: "is jubilant", "is high", "is wide", "I rejoice".
- **"mine horn is exalted" → "my horn is high"** (v1) — Hebrew רָמָה קַרְנִי: the horn of an animal carried high, a picture of strength and standing. The same root in v7 ("raises high"), v8 ("raises") and v10 ("raise the horn").
- **"my mouth is enlarged over" → "My mouth is wide against"** (v1) — Hebrew רָחַב פִּי עַל, "my mouth has grown wide over / against".
- **"neither is there any rock" → "and there is no rock"** (v2) — Hebrew צוּר "rock", here a common noun in a comparison (small letter); as a name of Yahweh it is "the Rock" (Deut 32:4).
- **"Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth" → "Do not multiply your talk, so high, so high; let not arrogance go out of your mouth"** (v3) — Hebrew אַל־תַּרְבּוּ תְדַבְּרוּ גְּבֹהָה גְבֹהָה, literally "do not multiply, (that) you speak high, high": the word "high" twice. The second line has no negative of its own (יֵצֵא עָתָק מִפִּיכֶם "arrogance goes out of your mouth"); the "not" of the first line carries over. עָתָק is bold, unrestrained speech.
- **"a God of knowledge" → "an El of knowledge"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵל דֵּעוֹת *El de'ot*; the short divine name *El*, distinct from *Elohim*. דֵּעוֹת is a plural, "knowledges".
- **"by him actions are weighed" → "by him deeds are measured"** (v3) — the qere וְלוֹ "and by / for him" is translated; the written text (ketiv) has וְלֹא "and not": "and deeds are not measured" (or, as a question, "are not deeds measured?"). נִתְכְּנוּ is of the root תכן "measure, fix".
- **"The bows of the mighty men are broken" → "The bow of the mighty ones is shattered"** (v4) — Hebrew קֶשֶׁת גִּבֹּרִים חַתִּים: "bow" is singular, "shattered" is plural and agrees with "the mighty ones". The root (חתת) is "be shattered", of persons "be dismayed" (Deut 1:21); the same root in v10.
- **"are girded with strength" → "have girded on ability"** (v4) — Hebrew חָיִל: ability, the strength and standing of a capable man (also "force" of an army, "wealth"); "strength" is kept for עֹז in v10.
- **"They that were full" → "The sated"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׂבֵעִים. **"they that were hungry ceased" → "the hungry have ceased"** — Hebrew חָדֵלּוּ "have ceased", with nothing added: to hunger, or to toil.
- **"so that the barren hath born seven" → "even the barren has borne seven"** (v5) — Hebrew עַד־עֲקָרָה, literally "until / as far as the barren": the turn goes so far that the barren woman bears seven. Some join עַד to the line before ("have ceased for ever"); the Masoretic division is kept.
- **"she that hath many children is waxed feeble" → "she of many sons languishes"** (v5) — Hebrew רַבַּת בָּנִים "many of sons".
- **"killeth" → "puts to death"** (v6) — Hebrew מֵמִית, the causative of "die"; the verbs of v6–8 are participles, what Yahweh does at all times.
- **"the grave" → "Sheol"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל, the place of the dead. **"bringeth up" → "brings up"** — Hebrew וַיָּעַל, a narrative form ("and he brought up") among the participles.
- **"maketh poor"** (v7) — Hebrew מוֹרִישׁ, here of the root רוּשׁ "be poor"; the same letters are also "dispossess". **"he bringeth low, and lifteth up" → "he makes low, he also raises high"** — Hebrew מַשְׁפִּיל אַף־מְרוֹמֵם; אַף "also, even".
- **"the beggar from the dunghill" → "from the ash heap … the needy"** (v8) — Hebrew אַשְׁפֹּת, the heap of refuse and ashes outside a town; אֶבְיוֹן "needy" beside דַּל "poor".
- **"to set them among princes" → "to make them sit with nobles"** (v8) — Hebrew נְדִיבִים, "willing, generous ones", the well-born; "princes" is another word (שַׂר). "Them" is supplied.
- **"the throne of glory" → "a throne of glory"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּסֵּא כָבוֹד, without the article; כִּסֵּא is the "seat" of Eli (1:9).
- **"the pillars of the earth are the LORD's" → "to Yahweh belong the pillars of the earth"** (v8) — Hebrew מְצֻקֵי אֶרֶץ. The word מָצוּק stands only here and in 14:5; "pillars" (what is set up firm) is the usual understanding, not certain.
- **"He will keep the feet of his saints" → "The feet of his faithful ones he will keep"** (v9) — Hebrew חֲסִידָיו (qere, plural; the written text has the singular, "his faithful one"): those who keep *chesed*.
- **"shall be silent in darkness" → "shall be made silent in the darkness"** (v9) — Hebrew יִדָּמּוּ, a passive form of דָּמַם "be still, be silent".
- **"by strength shall no man prevail" → "not by power shall a man prevail"** (v9) — Hebrew כֹּחַ "power", גָּבַר "prevail".
- **"The adversaries of the LORD shall be broken to pieces" → "Yahweh — those who contend with him shall be shattered"** (v10) — Hebrew יְהוָה יֵחַתּוּ מְרִיבָיו: the name stands first, alone; מְרִיבָיו (qere, plural; the written text has the singular) is "those who contend with him", of רִיב "contend".
- **"out of heaven shall he thunder upon them" → "against him he will thunder in the heavens"** (v10) — Hebrew עָלָיו (qere), singular: "upon / against him", each one of those who contend. The written text has עלו.
- **"and he shall give strength unto his king, and exalt the horn of his anointed" → "and may he give strength to his king and raise the horn of his anointed"** (v10) — Hebrew וְיִתֶּן … וְיָרֵם: the second verb has the short form of a wish, and both follow with a plain "and"; "and he will give … and raise" is also possible, since poetry uses these forms freely. מְשִׁיחוֹ is "his anointed one", *mashiach*: the first time the word is joined with "king" in the Bible. Israel has no king when Hannah prays.
- **"went to Ramah to his house"** (v11) — Hebrew הָרָמָתָה עַל־בֵּיתוֹ, "to the Ramah, upon his house". **"did minister unto the LORD before Eli" → "was ministering to Yahweh before the face of Eli"** — Hebrew מְשָׁרֵת אֶת־יְהוָה אֶת־פְּנֵי עֵלִי.
- **"sons of Belial" → "sons of Beliyyaal"** (v12) — Hebrew בְּנֵי בְלִיָּעַל: בְּלִיַּעַל is a common noun, "worthlessness", not a name. The sentence sets the two side by side: "the sons of Eli — sons of worthlessness".
- **"the priests' custom with the people was" → "the ordinance of the priests with the people"** (v13) — Hebrew מִשְׁפַּט הַכֹּהֲנִים, the words of Deut 18:3 ("the ordinance of the priests from the people"); מִשְׁפָּט is "judgment", here what is due by rule. The sentence has no verb. It may also be joined to v12: "they did not know Yahweh, nor the ordinance of the priests with the people".
- **"the priest's servant" → "the young man of the priest"** (v13, 15) and **"the young men"** (v17) — Hebrew נַעַר, the word that is "boy" of Samuel (v11, 18, 21, 26); of an attendant it is "young man".
- **"while the flesh was in seething" → "when the flesh was boiling"** (v13) — Hebrew בָּשָׂר "flesh" (meat) throughout v13–15. **"a fleshhook of three teeth" → "the fork of three teeth"** — Hebrew הַמַּזְלֵג שְׁלֹשׁ־הַשִּׁנַּיִם.
- **"pan, or kettle, or caldron, or pot" → "the basin or … the kettle or … the cauldron or … the pot"** (v14) — four vessels: כִּיּוֹר, דּוּד, קַלַּחַת, פָּרוּר; which shape each word names is not certain. **"the priest took for himself" → "the priest would take with it"** — Hebrew בּוֹ, "with it" (the fork); the versions have "for himself" (cited from memory).
- **"all the Israelites" → "all Israel"** (v14) — Hebrew לְכָל־יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"before they burnt the fat" → "before they turned the fat into smoke"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew הִקְטִיר, to make go up in smoke on the altar; plain "burn" is another verb. The fat is Yahweh's part (Lev 3:16). In v28 the same verb has incense as its object: "burn incense".
- **"he will not have sodden flesh of thee, but raw" → "he will not take boiled flesh from you, but only raw"** (v15) — Hebrew חָי, literally "living".
- **"Let them not fail to burn the fat presently" → "Let them surely turn the fat into smoke first"** (v16) — Hebrew קַטֵּר יַקְטִירוּן כַּיּוֹם, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "burning let them burn"; כַּיּוֹם, literally "as the day", is "first, at once".
- **"then he would answer him, Nay; but thou shalt give it me now" → "And he would say, 'No, for you shall give it now'"** (v16) — the qere לֹא "No" is translated; the written text has לוֹ "to him" ("he would say to him, You shall give it now"). **"I will take it by force" → "I take it by force"** — Hebrew לָקַחְתִּי, a perfect: the thing is as good as done.
- **"before the LORD" → "before the face of Yahweh"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew אֶת־פְּנֵי יְהוָה. **"men abhorred the offering of the LORD" → "the men spurned the offering of Yahweh"** — Hebrew נִאֵץ "spurn"; הָאֲנָשִׁים "the men" has the article (the young men, or people in general). מִנְחָה is here "offering" in the wide sense, a gift brought; in the laws of sacrifice it is the grain offering.
- **"a linen ephod"** (v18) — Hebrew אֵפוֹד בָּד; **"girded"** — Hebrew חָגוּר.
- **"a little coat" → "a small robe"** (v19) — Hebrew מְעִיל קָטֹן. **"the yearly sacrifice" → "the sacrifice of the days"** — as 1:21; "from year to year" is literally "from days to days".
- **"The LORD give thee seed of this woman for the loan which is lent to the LORD" → "May Yahweh appoint for you seed from this woman in place of the asking that was asked for Yahweh"** (v20) — Hebrew יָשֵׂם "put, set". תַּחַת הַשְּׁאֵלָה אֲשֶׁר שָׁאַל לַיהוָה is literally "in place of the asking that he (one) asked for Yahweh": the root שָׁאַל "ask" of 1:17, 20, 27, 28. The verb is masculine singular, with no subject named; it is taken here as "one asked". The Septuagint and a Qumran scroll have "the loan that you lent" / "that she lent" (cited from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"they went unto their own home" → "they would go to his place"** (v20) — Hebrew לִמְקֹמוֹ, "to his place", singular.
- **"And the LORD visited Hannah" → "For Yahweh visited Hannah"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּי "for". **"grew before the LORD" → "grew with Yahweh"** — Hebrew עִם־יְהוָה.
- **"how they lay with the women that assembled at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation" → "that they lay with the women who mustered at the entrance of the tent of meeting"** (v22) — Hebrew הַנָּשִׁים הַצֹּבְאוֹת, the word of Exod 38:8: women who came in ordered service, as a host (צָבָא). The clause stands in the Masoretic Text; the oldest Greek text and a Qumran scroll do not have it (cited from memory).
- **"Why do ye such things? for I hear of your evil dealings by all this people" → "Why do you do things like these, that I am hearing of your evil matters from all this people?"** (v23) — Hebrew אֶת־דִּבְרֵיכֶם רָעִים, literally "your words / matters, evil ones"; מֵאֵת כָּל־הָעָם אֵלֶּה, "from all the people, these".
- **"ye make the LORD's people to transgress" → "the people of Yahweh passing on"** (v24) — Hebrew מַעֲבִרִים עַם־יְהוָה, a participle "making pass". The Hebrew is difficult. It is taken here with "the report": the report that the people of Yahweh pass on. The other reading supplies "you": "you make the people of Yahweh transgress (pass over)".
- **"If one man sin against another, the judge shall judge him" → "If a man sins against a man, *Elohim* will arbitrate for him"** (v25) — Hebrew וּפִלְלוֹ אֱלֹהִים. The verb פִּלֵּל is the simple form of the root of "pray" (הִתְפַּלֵּל, in the next clause): to step in between, decide. אֱלֹהִים has a singular verb; "the judges" (cf. Exod 21:6) is an interpretation.
- **"who shall intreat for him?" → "who will pray for him?"** (v25) — Hebrew יִתְפַּלֶּל־לוֹ, the usual verb "pray" (1:10, 27).
- **"because the LORD would slay them" → "for Yahweh desired to put them to death"** (v25) — Hebrew כִּי־חָפֵץ יְהוָה לַהֲמִיתָם, word for word. חָפֵץ is "delight in, be pleased to"; כִּי is "for".
- **"grew on, and was in favour both with the LORD, and also with men" → "went on growing, and was good both with Yahweh and also with men"** (v26) — Hebrew הֹלֵךְ וְגָדֵל וָטוֹב, literally "going and growing great and good"; the Hebrew has no word "favor".
- **"a man of God" → "a man of *Elohim*"** (v27) — Hebrew אִישׁ־אֱלֹהִים, the first time the title stands in Samuel.
- **"Did I plainly appear … ?" → "Did I surely reveal myself … ?"** (v27) — Hebrew הֲנִגְלֹה נִגְלֵיתִי, the infinitive absolute with its verb, literally "revealing, did I reveal myself". The Hebrew is a question; the answer expected is yes. The question runs on through v28a.
- **"in Pharaoh's house" → "belonging to the house of Pharaoh"** (v27) — Hebrew לְבֵית פַּרְעֹה, "to / of the house of Pharaoh". The Septuagint and a Qumran scroll add "slaves" (cited from memory).
- **"to be my priest, to offer upon mine altar, to burn incense, to wear an ephod" → "for myself as priest, to go up on my altar, to burn incense, to bear an ephod"** (v28) — Hebrew לַעֲלוֹת עַל־מִזְבְּחִי "to go up upon my altar"; לָשֵׂאת "lift, carry". **"all the offerings made by fire of the children of Israel" → "all the offerings by fire of the sons of Israel"** — Hebrew אִשֵּׁי; the derivation from "fire" is commonly held, not certain.
- **"Wherefore kick ye at my sacrifice and at mine offering, which I have commanded in my habitation" → "Why do you kick at my sacrifice and at my offering, which I commanded for the abode"** (v29) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר צִוִּיתִי מָעוֹן. "You" is plural. מָעוֹן "abode" stands alone, without a preposition and without "my"; the Hebrew is difficult, and "for" is supplied.
- **"to make yourselves fat with the chiefest of all the offerings of Israel my people" → "to make yourselves fat from the first of every offering of Israel, my people"** (v29) — Hebrew מֵרֵאשִׁית "from the first (part)"; לְעַמִּי is literally "to / for my people".
- **"Wherefore the LORD God of Israel saith" → "Therefore, declares Yahweh, the Elohim of Israel"** (v30) — Hebrew נְאֻם־יְהוָה, a noun: "the declaration of Yahweh", twice in the verse. **"I said indeed" → "I surely said"** — Hebrew אָמוֹר אָמַרְתִּי, literally "saying I said".
- **"Be it far from me"** (v30) — Hebrew חָלִילָה לִּי, literally "a profane thing to me". **"shall be lightly esteemed"** — Hebrew יֵקָלּוּ, "be light", set against כִּבֵּד "honor", literally "make heavy".
- **"I will cut off thine arm" → "I will hew down your arm"** (v31) — Hebrew גָּדַע "hew down"; the arm is the strength.
- **"thou shalt see an enemy in my habitation, in all the wealth which God shall give Israel" → "you shall look on an adversary in the abode, in all that he does good to Israel"** (v32) — Hebrew וְהִבַּטְתָּ צַר מָעוֹן בְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר־יֵיטִיב אֶת־יִשְׂרָאֵל. The Hebrew is difficult. צַר is "adversary" or "distress"; מָעוֹן "abode" stands without a preposition, as in v29; "he" is not named (Yahweh). Also possible: "you shall look on the distress of the abode". The Septuagint does not have the first half of the verse (cited from memory).
- **"for ever" → "all the days"** (v32, 35) — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים.
- **"And the man of thine, whom I shall not cut off from mine altar, shall be to consume thine eyes, and to grieve thine heart" → "And a man I will not cut off for you from my altar, to wear out your eyes and to make your soul pine"** (v33) — Hebrew וְאִישׁ לֹא־אַכְרִית לְךָ מֵעִם מִזְבְּחִי, kept in its order. It may say that one man will be left at the altar, to Eli's grief, or that not every man will be cut off. לַאֲדִיב "make pine" stands only here (a by-form of דאב). The Hebrew has "your soul", not "heart".
- **"shall die in the flower of their age" → "shall die as men"** (v33) — Hebrew יָמוּתוּ אֲנָשִׁים, literally "shall die, men": as grown men, not old ones (v31, 32). The Septuagint and a Qumran scroll have "by the sword of men" (cited from memory).
- **"a faithful priest … a sure house"** (v35) — one Hebrew word, נֶאֱמָן, "firm, lasting, to be relied on"; the same word in 3:20. **"that shall do according to that which is in mine heart and in my mind" → "as is in my heart and in my soul he shall do"** — Hebrew בִּלְבָבִי וּבְנַפְשִׁי.
- **"before mine anointed"** (v35) — Hebrew מְשִׁיחִי, "my anointed one", as v10: the king.
- **"crouch to him for a piece of silver and a morsel of bread" → "bow down to him for a payment of silver and a round of bread"** (v36) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down"; אֲגוֹרַת כֶּסֶף, a word that stands only here (a small payment or coin; uncertain); כִּכַּר־לֶחֶם, a round loaf.
- **"Put me, I pray thee, into one of the priests' offices, that I may eat a piece of bread" → "Attach me, please, to one of the priesthoods, to eat a bit of bread"** (v36) — Hebrew סְפָחֵנִי נָא "join me, please"; הַכְּהֻנּוֹת is the plural of "priesthood"; פַּת־לֶחֶם "a bit of bread".

### Notes
- **Hannah's Song** (v1–10) — one of the OT's great poetic praises. Mary's song (Luke 1:46–55) takes up its themes.
- **"May he give strength to his king and raise the horn of his anointed"** (v10) — sung by a woman in an Israel without a king; the text already looks toward the monarchy.
- **Corrupt priesthood** (v12–17) — Eli's sons take from the sacrifices by force, and before the fat has been turned into smoke.
- **Oracle against Eli's house** (v27–36) — predicts destruction of Eli's priestly line. Fulfillment in ch 4 (Hophni, Phinehas die), Solomon's deposition of Abiathar (1 Kgs 2:27).
- **"Faithful priest"** (v35) — commonly seen fulfilled in Zadok (1 Kgs 2:35).
- **Samuel contrasts** — v11, 18, 21, 26: deliberately interspersed in the Eli-sons narrative. Rising prophet vs failing priests.
