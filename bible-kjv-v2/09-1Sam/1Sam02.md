# 1 Samuel 2

[1] Hannah prayed and said:

"My heart exults in Yahweh;
my horn is lifted high in Yahweh.
My mouth is opened wide against my enemies,
because I rejoice in your salvation.

[2] There is none holy like Yahweh;
there is none besides you;
there is no rock like our *Elohim*.

[3] Talk no more so proudly;
let not arrogance come from your mouth;
for Yahweh is an El of knowledge,
and by him deeds are weighed.

[4] The bows of the mighty are shattered,
but the stumbling are girded with strength.

[5] Those who were full have hired themselves out for bread,
but those who were hungry have ceased to hunger.
The barren has borne seven,
but she who has many children languishes.

[6] Yahweh kills and makes alive;
he brings down to Sheol and raises up.

[7] Yahweh makes poor and makes rich;
he brings low and he lifts up.

[8] He raises up the poor from the dust;
he lifts the needy from the ash heap
to make them sit with princes
and inherit a seat of honor.
For the pillars of the earth are Yahweh's,
and on them he has set the world.

[9] He will guard the feet of his faithful ones,
but the wicked shall be silenced in darkness;
for not by might shall a man prevail.

[10] The adversaries of Yahweh shall be shattered;
against them he will thunder in the heavens.
Yahweh will judge the ends of the earth;
he will give strength to his king
and exalt the horn of his anointed."

[11] Elkanah went home to Ramah; but the boy ministered to Yahweh in the presence of Eli the priest.

[12] The sons of Eli were worthless men; they did not know Yahweh.

[13] The custom of the priests with the people was this: when any man was offering a sacrifice, the priest's servant would come while the meat was boiling, with a three-pronged fork in his hand;

[14] he would thrust it into the pan or kettle or cauldron or pot; all that the fork brought up, the priest would take for himself. This is what they did to all the Israelites who came there to Shiloh.

[15] Moreover, before the fat was burned, the priest's servant would come and say to the man who was sacrificing, "Give meat for the priest to roast; he will not accept boiled meat from you, but raw."

[16] If the man said to him, "Let the fat be burned first, and then take as much as your soul desires," he would answer, "No, give it to me now, or I will take it by force."

[17] The sin of the young men was very great in the eyes of Yahweh, for the men despised the offering of Yahweh.

[18] Samuel was ministering before Yahweh, a boy wearing a linen ephod.

[19] His mother used to make him a little robe and bring it to him from year to year, when she went up with her husband to offer the yearly sacrifice.

[20] Eli would bless Elkanah and his wife, saying, "May Yahweh give you offspring from this woman in place of the petition that was granted to Yahweh." Then they would return to their home.

[21] Indeed Yahweh visited Hannah, so that she conceived and bore three sons and two daughters. The boy Samuel grew up before Yahweh.

[22] Now Eli was very old, and he heard all that his sons were doing to all Israel, and how they lay with the women who served at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[23] He said to them, "Why do you do such things? For I hear of your evil deeds from all these people.

[24] No, my sons; it is not a good report that I hear; you are making the people of Yahweh transgress.

[25] If a man sins against a man, *Elohim* will arbitrate for him; but if a man sins against Yahweh, who can intercede for him?" Yet they did not listen to the voice of their father, for Yahweh wished to put them to death.

[26] The boy Samuel continued to grow in stature and in favor with Yahweh and also with men.

[27] A man of *Elohim* came to Eli and said to him, "Thus says Yahweh: 'Did I not reveal myself to the house of your father when they were in Egypt in the house of Pharaoh?

[28] Did I not choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be my priest, to go up to my altar, to burn incense, to wear an ephod before me? I gave to the house of your father all the offerings made by fire from the children of Israel.

[29] Why then do you despise my sacrifice and my offering that I commanded in my dwelling, and honor your sons above me, fattening yourselves on the choicest parts of every offering of my people Israel?'

[30] Therefore Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel declares: 'I did indeed say that your house and the house of your father would walk before me forever'; but now Yahweh declares: 'Far be it from me; for those who honor me I will honor, and those who despise me shall be lightly esteemed.

[31] Behold, days are coming when I will cut off your strength and the strength of your father's house, so that there will not be an old man in your house.

[32] You shall see the distress of my dwelling, amid all the good that shall be given to Israel; and there shall never be an old man in your house.

[33] The man of yours whom I do not cut off from my altar shall remain to consume your eyes and to grieve your heart; and all the increase of your house shall die in the prime of life.

[34] This shall be the sign to you that shall come upon your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas: on one day the two of them shall die.

[35] I will raise up for myself a faithful priest, who will do according to what is in my heart and my soul; I will build him a sure house, and he shall walk before my anointed forever.

[36] And everyone who is left in your house shall come to bow down to him for a piece of silver and a morsel of bread, and shall say, "Please assign me to one of the priestly offices, that I may eat a morsel of bread."'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"a God of knowledge" → "an El of knowledge"** (v3) — Hebrew אֵל דֵּעוֹת *El de'ot*; short divine name *El* preserved distinct from *Elohim*.
- **"mine horn" → "my horn"** (v1) — *qeren*. Horn = strength, dignity.
- **"enlarged over" → "opened wide against"** (v1).
- **"saints" → "faithful ones"** (v9) — Hebrew *chasidav*.
- **"grave" → "Sheol"** (v6).
- **"dunghill" → "ash heap"** (v8) — Hebrew *ashpot*. Refuse pile.
- **"seething" → "boiling"** (v13) — archaic.
- **"fleshhook" → "fork"** (v13, 14).
- **"sodden" → "boiled"** (v15).
- **"children of Belial" → "worthless men"** (v12).

### Notes
- **Hannah's Song** (v1–10) — one of the OT's great poetic praises. Magnificat of the Hebrew Bible. Mary's Magnificat (Lk 1:46–55) is modeled on it.
- **"Give strength to his king, exalt the horn of his anointed"** (v10) — prophetic anticipation of monarchy. Sung by a barren woman without a king — yet the text already looks toward David.
- **Corrupt priesthood** (v12–17) — Eli's sons defraud worshipers, stealing from sacrifices before the fat is burned. Ritual boundary violations.
- **Oracle against Eli's house** (v27–36) — predicts destruction of Eli's priestly line. Fulfillment in ch 4 (Hophni, Phinehas die), Solomon's deposition of Abiathar (1 Kgs 2:27).
- **"Faithful priest"** (v35) — prophetic promise. Partially fulfilled in Zadok (1 Kgs 2:35); ultimately in NT priesthood (Heb 5–7).
- **Samuel contrasts** — v11, 18, 21, 26: deliberately interspersed in the Eli-sons narrative. Rising prophet vs failing priests.
