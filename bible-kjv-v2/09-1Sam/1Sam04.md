And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went out to meet the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ for battle, and they camped beside ⟦ebenezer|Even-HaEzer|Ebenezer⟧, and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ camped at ⟦aphek|Afeq|Aphek⟧.

[2] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ arrayed themselves to meet ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and the battle spread, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was smitten before the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; and they struck down in the array, in the field, about four thousand men.

[10] And the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ fought, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was smitten, and they fled, each man to his tents; and the blow was very great, and there fell of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ thirty thousand men on foot.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v4, 13, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22 the Hebrew has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים "the Elohim"; in v11 it has none.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **The titles of the ark** — kept as the Hebrew varies them: "the ark of the covenant of Yahweh" (v3, 5), "the ark of the covenant of Yahweh of Hosts, who sits upon the cherubim" (v4), "the ark of the covenant of the Elohim" (v4), "the ark of Yahweh" (v6), "the ark of Elohim" (v11), "the ark of the Elohim" (v13, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22). The word אֲרוֹן is "chest".
- **"the word of Samuel came to all Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי דְבַר־שְׁמוּאֵל לְכָל־יִשְׂרָאֵל, literally "and the word of Samuel was to all Israel". In the Hebrew numbering this sentence belongs to the story of chapter 3. The Septuagint has a longer text here, in which the Philistines gather for war against Israel (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"went out against the Philistines to battle" → "went out to meet the Philistines for battle"** (v1) — Hebrew לִקְרַאת "to meet"; מִלְחָמָה is "war", of a single fight "battle" (v1, 2). **"pitched beside Eben-ezer"** — Hebrew עַל־הָאֶבֶן הָעֵזֶר, "beside the stone, the help", with the article on both words; the stone is named in 7:12.
- **"put themselves in array" → "arrayed themselves"** (v2) and **"the army" → "the array"** (v2, 12, 16) — Hebrew עָרַךְ "arrange, set in order" and its noun מַעֲרָכָה: the line of men drawn up for battle.
- **"when they joined battle" → "the battle spread"** (v2) — Hebrew וַתִּטֹּשׁ הַמִּלְחָמָה. The verb נָטַשׁ is "leave, let go"; what it says of a battle (it spread, it was let loose) is not certain.
- **"was smitten" kept** (v2, 3, 10) — Hebrew נִגַּף, נָגַף, of a blow from God: in v3 the elders say it of Yahweh. **"they slew of the army in the field" → "they struck down in the array, in the field"** — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike".
- **"Let us fetch … that, when it cometh among us, it may save us" → "Let us take to ourselves … and let it come into our midst and save us"** (v3) — Hebrew וְיָבֹא … וְיֹשִׁעֵנוּ: the subject is not named; "it" (the ark) or "he" (Yahweh). **"out of the hand" → "from the palm"** — Hebrew כַּף; "hand" (יָד) stands in v8.
- **"which dwelleth between the cherubims" → "who sits upon the cherubim"** (v4) — Hebrew יֹשֵׁב הַכְּרֻבִים, literally "the one sitting the cherubim", with no preposition; "upon" is supplied. יָשַׁב is "sit" (as a king sits) and "dwell".
- **"shouted with a great shout, so that the earth rang again" → "shouted a great shout, and the earth resounded"** (v5) — Hebrew תְּרוּעָה, the shout of battle or alarm; וַתֵּהֹם הָאָרֶץ, "the earth was thrown into commotion".
- **"the noise" → "the sound"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew קוֹל "voice, sound".
- **"God is come into the camp" → "Elohim has come into the camp"** (v7) — Hebrew בָּא אֱלֹהִים, with the verb in the singular. In v8 the Philistines speak in the plural: הָאֱלֹהִים הָאַדִּירִים הָאֵלֶּה … הַמַּכִּים, "these mighty Elohim … who struck" (plural adjective, pronoun and participle), "these mighty gods". The one word אֱלֹהִים is kept in both verses.
- **"there hath not been such a thing heretofore" → "there has not been such a thing as this yesterday or the day before"** (v7) — Hebrew אֶתְמוֹל שִׁלְשֹׁם, "yesterday, the third day": before now.
- **"that smote the Egyptians with all the plagues" → "who struck Egypt with every blow"** (v8) — Hebrew מִצְרַיִם "Egypt"; מַכָּה "a blow", of the verb "strike". "In the wilderness" is what the Philistines say.
- **"Be strong, and quit yourselves like men" → "Make yourselves strong and be men"** (v9) — Hebrew הִתְחַזְּקוּ וִהְיוּ לַאֲנָשִׁים, literally "strengthen yourselves and become men"; so again at the end of the verse. **"that ye be not servants unto the Hebrews, as they have been to you" → "lest you serve the Hebrews as they have served you"** — Hebrew עָבַד "serve", the verb of "slave".
- **"every man into his tent" → "each man to his tents"** (v10) — Hebrew לְאֹהָלָיו, plural. **"there was a very great slaughter" → "the blow was very great"** — Hebrew הַמַּכָּה, as v8. **"thirty thousand footmen" → "thirty thousand men on foot"** — Hebrew רַגְלִי.
- **"with his clothes rent, and with earth upon his head" → "his garments were torn and ground was on his head"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲדָמָה "ground, soil".
- **"sat upon a seat by the wayside watching" → "was sitting on the seat by the side of the way, watching"** (v13) — the qere יַד דֶּרֶךְ "(at) the side (literally hand) of the way" is translated; the written text has יך, which gives no clear word. הַכִּסֵּא "the seat" is the seat of 1:9.
- **"his heart trembled for the ark of God" → "his heart was trembling for the ark of Elohim"** (v13) — Hebrew חָרֵד. **"came into the city, and told it" → "came to tell it in the city"** — Hebrew בָּא לְהַגִּיד בָּעִיר.
- **"the noise of the crying … the noise of this tumult" → "the sound of the outcry … the sound of this tumult"** (v14) — Hebrew צְעָקָה "outcry"; הָמוֹן, a noisy crowd, its roar (elsewhere "multitude").
- **"ninety and eight years old"** (v15) — Hebrew בֶּן־תִּשְׁעִים וּשְׁמֹנֶה שָׁנָה, literally "a son of ninety-eight years". **"his eyes were dim" → "his eyes were set"** — Hebrew וְעֵינָיו קָמָה, literally "his eyes stood": fixed, unmoving; another word than "dim" in 3:2.
- **"I am he that came out of the army, and I fled to day out of the army" → "I am the one who came from the array, and I fled from the array today"** (v16). **"What is there done, my son?" → "What was the matter, my son?"** — Hebrew מֶה־הָיָה הַדָּבָר, "what was the word / thing".
- **"the messenger" → "the bearer of the news"** (v17) — Hebrew הַמְבַשֵּׂר, one who brings news; elsewhere mostly of good news. **"a great slaughter" → "a great smiting"** — Hebrew מַגֵּפָה, the noun of "smitten" (v2, 3, 10); elsewhere it is "plague" (literally "a blow"), which here would say a sickness.
- **"when he made mention of the ark of God" → "when he mentioned the ark of Elohim"** (v18). **"from off the seat backward by the side of the gate" → "from upon the seat backward, by the side of the gate"** — Hebrew בְּעַד יַד הַשַּׁעַר, literally "through / behind the hand of the gate"; how the seat stood to the gate is not clear. **"his neck brake"** — Hebrew מַפְרַקְתּוֹ, a word that stands only here: the joint of the neck.
- **"was with child, near to be delivered" → "was with child, about to bear"** (v19) — Hebrew הָרָה לָלַת. **"the tidings that the ark of God was taken" → "the report about the taking of the ark of Elohim"** — Hebrew אֶל־הִלָּקַח, "concerning the being taken"; so again in v21.
- **"she bowed herself and travailed; for her pains came upon her" → "she knelt and bore, for her pangs had turned upon her"** (v19) — Hebrew וַתִּכְרַע וַתֵּלֶד; נֶהֶפְכוּ עָלֶיהָ צִרֶיהָ, "her pangs were turned over upon her".
- **"the women that stood by her said unto her, Fear not" → "the women who stood by her spoke: Do not fear"** (v20) — Hebrew וַתְּדַבֵּרְנָה "spoke". **"neither did she regard it" → "she did not set her heart to it"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־שָׁתָה לִבָּהּ.
- **"she named the child I-chabod, saying, The glory is departed from Israel" → "she called the boy I-Chavod, saying, Glory has gone into exile from Israel"** (v21, 22) — Hebrew אִי־כָבוֹד *I-khavod*. The name is given without a gloss. כָּבוֹד is "glory"; אִי is either "not" ("no glory", cf. Job 22:30) or "where?" ("where is glory?"); which is not certain. גָּלָה כָבוֹד: the verb גָּלָה is "go away into exile", the word used of a people carried off; כָבוֹד has no article.
- **"because the ark of God was taken, and because of her father in law and her husband" → "concerning the taking of the ark of Elohim, and concerning her father-in-law and her husband"** (v21) — Hebrew אֶל … וְאֶל; the words are the narrator's.

### Notes
- **Battle of Aphek-Ebenezer** — devastating defeat. The ark is taken. The oracle against Eli's house (2:27–36, 3:11–14) begins to be fulfilled.
- **The elders and the ark** (v3) — they send for the ark; Yahweh is not asked, and Samuel is not named in the chapter after v1.
- **Philistines' reaction** (v7–8) — fear of "these mighty *Elohim*", spoken in the plural; the fear turns to resolve.
- **Death of Eli** — the fallen of Israel, Hophni, Phinehas, Eli, and Phinehas' wife: the deaths follow one on another.
- **"I-Chavod / Glory has gone into exile"** — the ark taken. Shiloh is not named again as a sanctuary (cf. Jer 7:12, 26:6).
- **Psalm 78:60–64** reflects on these events.
