# 1 Samuel 4

[1] And the word of Samuel came to all Israel. Now Israel went out to battle against the Philistines; they encamped at Ebenezer, and the Philistines encamped at Aphek.

[2] The Philistines drew up in battle order against Israel; when the battle was joined, Israel was defeated before the Philistines, who killed about four thousand men on the field.

[3] When the people came back to the camp, the elders of Israel said, "Why has Yahweh struck us down today before the Philistines? Let us bring the ark of the covenant of Yahweh from Shiloh to us, that it may come into our midst and save us from the hand of our enemies."

[4] So the people sent to Shiloh, and they brought from there the ark of the covenant of Yahweh of Hosts, who sits above the cherubim. The two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, were there with the ark of the covenant of *Elohim*.

[5] When the ark of the covenant of Yahweh came into the camp, all Israel gave a great shout, so that the earth resounded.

[6] When the Philistines heard the noise of the shout, they said, "What is this great shout in the camp of the Hebrews?" They realized that the ark of Yahweh had come to the camp.

[7] The Philistines were afraid; for they said, "*Elohim* has come into the camp." And they said, "Woe to us! For nothing like this has happened before.

[8] Woe to us! Who will deliver us from the hand of these mighty *Elohim*? These are the *Elohim* who struck the Egyptians with every sort of plague in the wilderness.

[9] Take courage and be men, O Philistines, lest you become slaves to the Hebrews as they have been to you; be men and fight!"

[10] So the Philistines fought, and Israel was defeated, and every man fled to his tent; there was a very great slaughter, for thirty thousand foot soldiers of Israel fell.

[11] The ark of *Elohim* was captured, and the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, died.

[12] A man of Benjamin ran from the battle line and came to Shiloh the same day, his clothes torn and earth on his head.

[13] When he arrived, behold, Eli was sitting on his seat by the wayside, watching, for his heart was trembling over the ark of *Elohim*. When the man came into the city and told the news, all the city cried out.

[14] When Eli heard the sound of the outcry, he said, "What is this uproar?" The man came in hurriedly and told Eli.

[15] Eli was ninety-eight years old; his eyes were fixed, so that he could not see.

[16] The man said to Eli, "I am he who has come from the battle; I fled from the battle today." He said, "What happened, my son?"

[17] The messenger answered, "Israel has fled before the Philistines; also there has been a great slaughter among the people. Your two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are dead; and the ark of *Elohim* has been captured."

[18] As soon as he mentioned the ark of *Elohim*, Eli fell backward off his seat by the side of the gate; his neck was broken, and he died, for he was an old man and heavy. He had judged Israel forty years.

[19] His daughter-in-law, the wife of Phinehas, was pregnant, about to give birth. When she heard the news that the ark of *Elohim* was captured, and that her father-in-law and her husband were dead, she bowed down and gave birth, for her pains came upon her.

[20] About the time of her death, the women attending her said, "Do not be afraid, for you have borne a son." But she did not answer or take it to heart.

[21] She named the child Ichabod ("Inglorious"), saying, "Glory has departed from Israel!" — because the ark of *Elohim* had been captured, and because of her father-in-law and her husband.

[22] She said, "Glory has departed from Israel, for the ark of *Elohim* has been captured."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"quit yourselves like men" → "be men"** (v9) — Hebrew וְהָיוּ לַאֲנָשִׁים *ve-hayu la-anashim*. "Become men" — steel up.

### Notes
- **Battle of Aphek-Ebenezer** — devastating defeat. The ark is taken. The oracle against Eli's house (2:27–36, 3:11–14) fulfilled.
- **Elders' theological error** — they treat the ark as a talisman, a good-luck charm to ensure victory. No consultation of Yahweh through Samuel; no repentance. Mechanical religion.
- **Philistines' reaction** (v7–8) — fear of "these mighty *Elohim*." Their plural conception betrays their polytheism. But this fear turns quickly to determination.
- **Death of Eli** — four-fold simultaneous death: 30,000 soldiers, Hophni, Phinehas, Eli, and Phinehas' wife. Complete disaster.
- **"Ichabod / Glory departed"** — theological loss. The ark captured; the sanctuary effectively desecrated. Shiloh never recovers as a sanctuary (Jer 7:12, 26:6).
- **Psalm 78:60–64** reflects on these events: "He forsook his dwelling at Shiloh ... his priests fell by the sword."
