# 1 Samuel 11

[1] And ⟦nahash|Nachash|Nahash⟧ the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧ came up and camped against ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧. And all the men of ⟦jabesh|Yavesh|Jabesh⟧ said to ⟦nahash|Nachash|Nahash⟧, "Cut a covenant for us, and we will serve you."

[2] And ⟦nahash|Nachash|Nahash⟧ the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧ said to them, "By this I will cut it for you: by gouging out for you every right eye; and I will put it as a reproach on all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[3] And the elders of ⟦jabesh|Yavesh|Jabesh⟧ said to him, "Let go of us for seven days, and let us send messengers into all the territory of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and if there is no one who saves us, we will come out to you."

[4] And the messengers came to ⟦gibeah|Givah|Gibeah⟧ of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and spoke the words in the ears of the people, and all the people lifted up their voice and wept.

[5] And behold, ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ was coming after the herd from the field. And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "What ails the people, that they weep?" And they recounted to him the words of the men of ⟦jabesh|Yavesh|Jabesh⟧.

[6] And the spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rushed upon ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ when he heard these words, and his anger burned greatly.

[7] And he took a yoke of the herd and cut it in pieces and sent throughout all the territory of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ by the hand of the messengers, saying, "Whoever does not come out after ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and after ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧, so shall it be done to his herd." And the dread of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ fell on the people, and they came out as one man.

[8] And he numbered them in ⟦bezek|Bezeq|Bezek⟧, and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were three hundred thousand, and the men of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ thirty thousand.

[9] And they said to the messengers who had come, "Thus you shall say to the men of ⟦jabesh-gilead|Yavesh Gilad|Jabesh-gilead⟧: 'Tomorrow there shall be salvation for you, at the heat of the sun.'" And the messengers came and told the men of ⟦jabesh|Yavesh|Jabesh⟧, and they rejoiced.

[10] And the men of ⟦jabesh|Yavesh|Jabesh⟧ said, "Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you shall do to us according to all that is good in your eyes."

[11] And it came to pass on the morrow that ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ put the people in three companies, and they came into the midst of the camp in the morning watch and struck down Ammon until the heat of the day. And it came to pass that those who were left were scattered, and there were not left among them two together.

[12] And the people said to ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧, "Who is he who says, 'Shall ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ reign over us?' Give the men, and we will put them to death."

[13] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said, "No man shall be put to death on this day, for today ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has worked salvation in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[14] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ said to the people, "Come, and let us go to Gilgal, and let us renew the kingship there."

[15] And all the people went to Gilgal, and they made ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ king there before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in Gilgal, and they sacrificed there sacrifices, peace offerings, before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and all the men of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ rejoiced there very greatly.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"encamped" → "camped"** (v1) — Hebrew חָנָה, the verb of מַחֲנֶה "camp" (v11).
- **"Make a covenant with us" → "Cut a covenant for us"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּרָת־לָנוּ בְרִית: the verb is "cut", the preposition לְ "for". A covenant "for" someone is one granted by the stronger side.
- **"On this condition will I make a covenant with you" → "By this I will cut it for you"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּזֹאת אֶכְרֹת לָכֶם, "by this I will cut for you": the word "covenant" is not repeated, and "condition" is not in the Hebrew. "It" is supplied because English "cut" needs an object.
- **"thrust out all your right eyes" → "gouging out for you every right eye"** (v2) — Hebrew בִּנְקוֹר לָכֶם כָּל־עֵין יָמִין; נָקַר *naqar* is "bore out, gouge".
- **"lay it for a reproach" → "put it as a reproach"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׂים "put"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach".
- **"Give us seven days' respite" → "Let go of us for seven days"** (v3) — Hebrew הֶרֶף לָנוּ, the hifil of רָפָה "sink, slacken": "slacken toward us, let us be".
- **"coasts" → "territory"** (v3, 7) — Hebrew גְּבוּל, "border", here the land within the border.
- **"if there be no man to save us" → "if there is no one who saves us"** (v3) — Hebrew מוֹשִׁיעַ, a participle: "one saving, a savior". The same root as "salvation" (v9, 13).
- **"told the tidings" → "spoke the words"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְדַבְּרוּ הַדְּבָרִים. **"in the ears of"** — Hebrew בְּאָזְנֵי, kept.
- **"after the herd"** (v5) — Hebrew אַחֲרֵי הַבָּקָר; בָּקָר is the collective word for cattle, the same in v7 ("a yoke of the herd", "his herd"): a pair of oxen is meant.
- **"What aileth the people" kept** (v5) — Hebrew מַה־לָּעָם, literally "what is to the people?". **"told him" → "recounted to him"** — Hebrew סִפֵּר.
- **"came upon" → "rushed upon"** (v6) — Hebrew וַתִּצְלַח … עַל, the verb used of the spirit on Samson (Judg 14:6, 19; 15:14) and on Saul (10:6, 10). The same letters make the verb "prosper"; the sense here is "come with force". The written text has בְּשָׁמְעוֹ "in his hearing", the reading tradition כְּשָׁמְעוֹ "as he heard": no difference in English.
- **"his anger was kindled greatly" → "his anger burned greatly"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר אַפּוֹ, literally "his nose burned".
- **"hewed them in pieces" → "cut it in pieces"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיְנַתְּחֵהוּ, "cut it into its pieces", with a singular object (the yoke). "Sent" has no object in the Hebrew.
- **"the fear of the LORD" → "the dread of Yahweh"** (v7) — Hebrew פַּחַד, "dread, terror", not יִרְאָה "fear". It may be the dread that Yahweh sends or dread before Yahweh.
- **"with one consent" → "as one man"** (v7) — Hebrew כְּאִישׁ אֶחָד.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"the men of Judah"** — Hebrew אִישׁ יְהוּדָה, a collective singular ("the man of Judah"); so also "the men of Jabesh-gilead" in v9. Israel and Judah are counted apart. The numbers are given in words.
- **"ye shall have help" → "there shall be salvation for you"** (v9) — Hebrew תְּשׁוּעָה, "salvation", as in v13. **"by that time the sun be hot" → "at the heat of the sun"** — the reading tradition has כְּחֹם "about the heat of", the written text בְּחֹם "in the heat of".
- **"all that seemeth good unto you" → "according to all that is good in your eyes"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּכָל־הַטּוֹב בְּעֵינֵיכֶם.
- **"three companies"** (v11) — Hebrew שְׁלֹשָׁה רָאשִׁים, literally "three heads" (as Judg 7:16; 9:43). **"slew the Ammonites" → "struck down Ammon"** — Hebrew וַיַּכּוּ אֶת־עַמּוֹן: the verb is "strike", the name is "Ammon".
- **"Shall Saul reign over us?"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁאוּל יִמְלֹךְ עָלֵינוּ, "Saul shall reign over us", with no question word; the tone makes it a question (or a scornful quotation). The Septuagint has "shall not reign" (from memory). **"bring the men" → "Give the men"** — Hebrew תְּנוּ.
- **"wrought salvation" → "worked salvation"** (v13) — Hebrew עָשָׂה … תְּשׁוּעָה, "did / made salvation".
- **"renew the kingdom" → "renew the kingship"** (v14) — Hebrew הַמְּלוּכָה, the office and rule of a king (10:16, 25); "kingdom" is מַמְלָכָה (13:13, 14). "Gilgal" has the article in Hebrew ("the Gilgal").
- **"sacrificed sacrifices of peace offerings" → "sacrificed there sacrifices, peace offerings"** (v15) — Hebrew זְבָחִים שְׁלָמִים, two nouns side by side. **"rejoiced greatly" → "rejoiced there very greatly"** — Hebrew עַד־מְאֹד. "There" stands three times in the verse.
- **A longer text before v1** — a Dead Sea scroll of Samuel (4QSamᵃ) and Josephus have a paragraph before v1 about Nahash's earlier oppression of the tribes beyond the Jordan (cited from memory). It is not in the Masoretic Text and is not translated here.

### Notes
- **Jabesh-gilead connection** — these were the Transjordan Israelites who had married Benjamites after Judg 21. Their connection to Benjamin (Saul's tribe) is ancient.
- **Nahash's cruelty** — eye-gouging as symbol of subjugation. Right eye makes a soldier useless (left eye was covered by the shield).
- **Saul's spirit-empowered wrath** (v6–7) — parallels the judges (Judg 14, 15) for Samson. The first test of kingship: a deliverance mission.
- **Cutting up oxen** (v7) — parallels the Levite dismembering his concubine (Judg 19:29). But here the desired response is military rally, not civil war. Inversion from chaos to deliverance.
- **Saul spares his critics** (v13) — first magnanimous act as king. Victory produces clemency.
- **Kingdom renewed at Gilgal** (v14–15) — post-victory re-coronation. Saul's kingship confirmed not just by Samuel's private anointing but by public military success.
