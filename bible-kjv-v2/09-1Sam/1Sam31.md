## Changes

- **"sore" → "greatly / badly"** (v3, 4).
- **"abuse me" → "torment me"** (v4) — Hebrew *hit'alelu*. Taunt / mistreat.
- **"tree" → "tamarisk tree"** (v13) — specification from 1 Chr 10:12.

### Notes
- **Battle of Gilboa** — Saul's predicted death (28:19) fulfilled. Israel routed; Saul and three sons killed. Jonathan, David's beloved friend, dies.
- **Saul's suicide** (v4–5) — refuses to be taken alive. Armor-bearer will not strike the king; Saul falls on his sword. Armor-bearer follows. Later 2 Sam 1 has an alternate death-tradition told by an Amalekite.
- **Philistine desecration** (v9–10) — Saul's head displayed as trophy; body hung on city wall. Armor in Ashtaroth's temple.
- **Jabesh-gilead's loyalty** (v11–13) — the city Saul rescued in ch 11 returns the favor. Night raid, body recovery, cremation (unusual for Israelites), burial, and seven-day mourning.
- **End of Saul's reign** — 40 years (per Acts 13:21). Beginning marked by rescue of Jabesh-gilead (ch 11); ending marked by Jabesh-gilead's rescue of his body.
- **Transition to 2 Samuel** — with Saul dead, David's path to kingship clears. The next book begins with David learning of Saul's death.
