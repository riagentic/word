# 1 Samuel 31

[1] The Philistines fought against Israel; the men of Israel fled from before the Philistines and fell slain on Mount Gilboa.

[2] The Philistines pressed hard upon Saul and his sons, and the Philistines struck down Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, Saul's sons.

[3] The battle went heavily against Saul; the archers overtook him, and he was badly wounded by the archers.

[4] Saul said to his armor-bearer, "Draw your sword and thrust me through with it, lest these uncircumcised come and thrust me through and torment me." But his armor-bearer would not, for he was greatly afraid. Therefore Saul took his sword and fell upon it.

[5] When his armor-bearer saw that Saul was dead, he too fell upon his sword and died with him.

[6] So Saul died, and his three sons, and his armor-bearer, and all his men, that same day together.

[7] When the men of Israel who were on the other side of the valley and those beyond the Jordan saw that the men of Israel had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned the cities and fled; the Philistines came and lived in them.

[8] The next day, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, they found Saul and his three sons fallen on Mount Gilboa.

[9] They cut off his head, stripped off his armor, and sent news throughout the land of the Philistines, to proclaim it in the house of their idols and among the people.

[10] They put his armor in the temple of Ashtaroth, and they fastened his body to the wall of Beth-shan.

[11] When the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul,

[12] all the valiant men arose and went all night, and took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons from the wall of Beth-shan; they came to Jabesh and burned them there.

[13] They took their bones and buried them under the tamarisk tree at Jabesh, and fasted seven days.

## Changes

- **"sore" → "greatly / badly"** (v3, 4).
- **"abuse me" → "torment me"** (v4) — Hebrew *hit'alelu*. Taunt / mistreat.
- **"tree" → "tamarisk tree"** (v13) — specification from 1 Chr 10:12.

### Notes
- **Battle of Gilboa** — Saul's predicted death (28:19) fulfilled. Israel routed; Saul and three sons killed. Jonathan, David's beloved friend, dies.
- **Saul's suicide** (v4–5) — refuses to be taken alive. Armor-bearer will not strike the king; Saul falls on his sword. Armor-bearer follows. Later 2 Sam 1 has an alternate death-tradition told by an Amalekite.
- **Philistine desecration** (v9–10) — Saul's head displayed as trophy; body hung on city wall. Armor in Ashtaroth's temple.
- **Jabesh-gilead's loyalty** (v11–13) — the city Saul rescued in ch 11 returns the favor. Night raid, body recovery, cremation (unusual for Israelites), burial, and seven-day mourning.
- **End of Saul's reign** — 40 years (per Acts 13:21). Beginning marked by rescue of Jabesh-gilead (ch 11); ending marked by Jabesh-gilead's rescue of his body.
- **Transition to 2 Samuel** — with Saul dead, David's path to kingship clears. The next book begins with David learning of Saul's death.

*End of 1 Samuel. 2 Samuel follows — David's rise to the throne and the turbulent reign that establishes the Davidic covenant.*
