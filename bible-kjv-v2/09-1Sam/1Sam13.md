# 1 Samuel 13

[1] Saul was [...] years old when he began to reign; and he reigned [...] and two years over Israel.

[2] Saul chose three thousand men of Israel for himself; two thousand were with Saul at Michmash and in the hill country of Bethel, and one thousand were with Jonathan at Gibeah of Benjamin. The rest of the people he sent each to his tent.

[3] Jonathan struck the garrison of the Philistines that was at Geba, and the Philistines heard of it. Saul blew the trumpet throughout the land, saying, "Let the Hebrews hear!"

[4] All Israel heard that Saul had struck the garrison of the Philistines, and also that Israel had become odious to the Philistines. The people were called together after Saul to Gilgal.

[5] The Philistines gathered to fight with Israel: thirty thousand chariots, six thousand horsemen, and people as numerous as the sand on the seashore. They came up and encamped at Michmash, east of Beth-aven.

[6] When the men of Israel saw that they were in distress (for the people were hard-pressed), the people hid themselves in caves, thickets, rocks, tombs, and pits.

[7] Some Hebrews crossed the Jordan into the land of Gad and Gilead. But Saul was still at Gilgal, and all the people followed him trembling.

[8] He waited seven days, according to the appointed time that Samuel had set; but Samuel had not come to Gilgal, and the people were scattering from him.

[9] So Saul said, "Bring to me the burnt offering and the peace offerings." And he offered the burnt offering.

[10] As soon as he had finished offering the burnt offering, behold, Samuel came; Saul went out to meet him and to greet him.

[11] Samuel said, "What have you done?" Saul said, "Because I saw that the people were scattering from me, and you did not come within the days appointed, and the Philistines were gathering at Michmash,

[12] I said, 'Now the Philistines will come down upon me at Gilgal, and I have not sought the favor of Yahweh.' So I forced myself and offered the burnt offering."

[13] Samuel said to Saul, "You have done foolishly. You have not kept the commandment of Yahweh your *Elohim*, which he commanded you. For now Yahweh would have established your kingdom over Israel forever.

[14] But now your kingdom shall not continue. Yahweh has sought out for himself a man after his own heart, and Yahweh has appointed him prince over his people, because you have not kept what Yahweh commanded you."

[15] Samuel arose and went up from Gilgal to Gibeah of Benjamin. Saul numbered the people who were present with him, about six hundred men.

[16] Saul, Jonathan his son, and the people who were present with them were staying in Geba of Benjamin; but the Philistines encamped at Michmash.

[17] Raiders came out of the camp of the Philistines in three companies: one company turned toward Ophrah, to the land of Shual;

[18] another company turned toward Beth-horon; and another company turned toward the border of the Valley of Zeboim, toward the wilderness.

[19] Now there was no smith to be found throughout all the land of Israel, for the Philistines said, "Lest the Hebrews make themselves swords or spears."

[20] All Israel had to go down to the Philistines to sharpen each man's plowshare, his mattock, his axe, or his sickle.

[21] The charge was two-thirds of a shekel for the plowshares and the mattocks, and one-third of a shekel for sharpening the axes and for setting the goads.

[22] So on the day of battle, there was neither sword nor spear found in the hand of any of the people who were with Saul and Jonathan; only Saul and his son Jonathan had them.

[23] And a detachment of the Philistines went out to the pass of Michmash.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Saul reigned one year ... two years" → "Saul was [...] years old ... [...] and two years"** (v1) — Hebrew text here is textually deficient. MT literally reads "Saul was a son of one year when he began to reign, and he reigned two years" — clearly corrupted (Saul could not have been 1 year old or reigned only 2 years). Some LXX MSS omit the verse; Acts 13:21 says 40 years. Brackets note the textual damage.
- **"strait" → "distress"** (v6).
- **"share" → "plowshare"** (v20).
- **"coulter" → "mattock"** (v20).
- **"file" (v21)** — Hebrew *pim*. An old Hebrew weight unit (~2/3 shekel); KJV translated as "file" but the actual meaning is a unit of payment. Archaeological pim-weights confirm.
- **"forks" → "axes"** / **"goads"** (v21).
- **"Geba" vs "Gibeah"** — MT variants; two nearby places. LXX preserves clearer geography.

### Notes
- **Chronology problem** (v1) — text defective; precision lost. The framework verse for Saul's reign. Acts 13:21 gives 40 years total.
- **Jonathan's initial provocation** (v3) — striking the Philistine garrison. Sets off the war.
- **Saul's impatience** (v8–14) — waits 6+ days for Samuel; on the 7th offers sacrifice himself. The act is not itself idolatrous but constitutes disobedience to Samuel's specific instruction (10:8).
- **First rejection** (v13–14) — Saul's kingdom will not continue. A "man after his own heart" (later: David) will replace him.
- **Philistine metal monopoly** (v19–22) — Philistines controlled iron smelting; Israel lacked weapon-smiths. Israel's army goes to battle nearly weaponless.
- **Transition signal** — Saul's rejection prophesied here; his replacement will appear in ch 16. His story has eight chapters of doomed reign ahead.
