## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"utterly destroy" → "devote to destruction"** (throughout) — *cherem*.
- **"it repenteth me / repent" → "I regret / change his mind"** (v11, 29, 35) — *nacham*. Translating consistently is impossible here — the Hebrew uses the same verb both of Yahweh's "regretting" making Saul king, and v29's "he will not change his mind." Apparent contradiction reflects genre: v11 is narrative (human perspective of divine action); v29 is aphoristic (divine immutability principle).
- **"witchcraft / iniquity and idolatry" → "divination / iniquity of teraphim"** (v23) — Hebrew *qesem* (divination) / *teraphim* (household gods).
- **"place" → "monument"** (v12) — Hebrew יָד *yad*. Victory monument.
- **"delicately" → "cheerfully"** (v32) — Hebrew מַעֲדַנֹּת *ma'adannot*. Debated: "hopefully" / "cheerfully" / "trembling." LXX reads "trembling."
- **"Strength of Israel" → "Glory of Israel"** (v29) — Hebrew נֵצַח יִשְׂרָאֵל *Netzach Yisrael*. "Everlasting / Glory / Enduring One of Israel."

### Notes
- **The Amalekite war** — completion of Yahweh's ancient vow to destroy Amalek (Ex 17:14–16, Deut 25:17–19). Saul's test.
- **Saul's partial obedience** — kept the king alive; spared the best livestock. Substantive disobedience despite claims of compliance.
- **"To obey is better than sacrifice"** (v22) — foundational prophetic critique of ritualism. Echoes in Hos 6:6, Mic 6:8, Mt 9:13, 12:7.
- **Second rejection** — first in ch 13, now definitive. Saul's kingdom passed on to "a neighbor better than you" — David, unnamed here but introduced in ch 16.
- **Saul's torn robe** (v27) — symbolic action. Later (24:4–5) David will cut Saul's robe; and Saul himself recognizes kingdom-loss.
- **"Yahweh will not change his mind"** (v29) — the divine vs human capacity: Yahweh's judgment against Saul stands. But the narrative says Yahweh "regretted" making Saul king (v35) — showing that the text holds these together in tension.
