## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"image" → "household idol"** (v13, 16) — *teraphim*.
- **"javelin" → "spear"** (v9, 10).
- **"standing as appointed" → "standing as their leader"** (v20).
- **"pillow of goats' hair" (v13, 16)** — Hebrew כְּבִיר הָעִזִּים *kevir ha-izzim*. Likely goat-hair netting or mat.

### Notes
- **Jonathan's intercession** (v1–7) — saves David briefly. Demonstrates Saul's still-functional rationality; but only briefly.
- **Second spear-throw** (v10) — Saul's violence recurs. David no longer returns.
- **Michal's deception** (v11–17) — saves David via window-escape. The *teraphim* in her house (indicative of household idolatry) is used as a prop.
- **"Let me go; why should I kill you?"** (v17) — Michal's claim to Saul protects herself. Clever diplomacy; whether David actually threatened her is unclear.
- **Second "Is Saul also among the prophets?"** (v24) — the proverb from ch 10 recurs. Saul's prophetic ecstasy has become uncontrolled incapacitation.
- **Prophetic protection** — Samuel's prophetic community has contagious spirit-presence. Even hostile messengers are swept into prophecy.
