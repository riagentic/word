# 1 Samuel 9

[1] And there was a man of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, and his name was ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧ son of ⟦abiel|Aviel|Abiel⟧ son of ⟦zeror|Tzeror|Zeror⟧ son of ⟦becorath|Bechorat|Becorath⟧ son of ⟦aphiah|Afiach|Aphiah⟧, son of a ⟦benjaminite|Yemini|Benjaminite⟧ man, a mighty one of ability.

[2] And he had a son, and his name was ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, a young man and good; and there was not a man of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ better than he; from his shoulder and upward he was taller than all the people.

[3] And the she-donkeys of ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧ the father of ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ were lost; and ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧ said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ his son, "Take, please, with you one of the young men, and arise, go, seek the she-donkeys."

[4] And he passed through the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧, and he passed through the land of Shalishah, and they did not find them; and they passed through the land of Shaalim, and there was nothing; and he passed through the land of ⟦benjaminite|Yemini|the Benjaminites⟧, and they did not find them.

[5] They had come into the land of ⟦zuph|Tzuf|Zuph⟧, and ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to his young man who was with him, "Come, and let us return, lest my father cease from the she-donkeys and be anxious for us."

[6] And he said to him, "Behold, please, a man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in this city, and the man is honored; all that he speaks surely comes. Now let us go there; perhaps he will tell us our way on which we have gone."

[7] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to his young man, "And behold, we go, and what shall we bring to the man? For the bread is gone from our vessels, and there is no gift to bring to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. What is with us?"

[8] And the young man answered ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ again and said, "Behold, there is found in my hand a quarter of a shekel of silver; and I will give it to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he will tell us our way."

[9] Formerly in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, thus said the man when he went to seek ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: "Come, and let us go as far as the seer"; for the prophet of today was formerly called the seer.

[10] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ said to his young man, "Your word is good; come, let us go." And they went to the city where the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was.

[11] They were going up by the ascent of the city, and they found girls going out to draw water, and they said to them, "Is the seer here?"

[12] And they answered them and said, "He is; behold, before you. Hasten now, for today he has come to the city, for the people have a sacrifice today on the high place.

[13] As you come into the city, so you will find him, before he goes up to the high place to eat; for the people will not eat until he comes, for he blesses the sacrifice; afterward the called ones eat. And now go up, for him — just now you will find him."

[14] And they went up to the city. They were coming into the midst of the city, and behold, ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ was coming out to meet them, to go up to the high place.

[15] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had uncovered the ear of ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ one day before ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ came, saying,

[16] "About this time tomorrow I will send to you a man from the land of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧, and you shall anoint him as leader over my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and he shall save my people from the hand of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧; for I have seen my people, for their outcry has come to me."

[17] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ saw ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ answered him, "Behold the man of whom I said to you, 'This one shall restrain my people.'"

[18] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ drew near to ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ in the midst of the gate and said, "Tell me, please, where is the house of the seer."

[19] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ answered ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and said, "I am the seer. Go up before me to the high place, and you shall eat with me today, and I will send you away in the morning, and all that is in your heart I will tell you.

[20] And as for the she-donkeys that were lost to you three days ago today, do not set your heart on them, for they have been found. And for whom is all the desire of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧? Is it not for you and for all the house of your father?"

[21] And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ answered and said, "Am I not a ⟦benjaminite|son of Yemini|Benjaminite⟧, from the smallest of the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and my clan the least of all the clans of the tribes of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧? And why have you spoken to me according to this word?"

[22] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ took ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ and his young man and brought them into the chamber, and he gave them a place at the head of the called ones, and they were about thirty men.

[23] And ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ said to the cook, "Give the portion that I gave you, of which I said to you, 'Put it with you.'"

[24] And the cook lifted up the leg and what was on it and put it before ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧. And he said, "Behold what is left! Put it before you, eat; for to the appointed time it has been kept for you, saying, 'I have called the people.'" And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ ate with ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ on that day.

[25] And they came down from the high place to the city, and he spoke with ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ on the roof.

[26] And they rose early; and it came to pass, about the going up of the dawn, that ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ called to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ on the roof, saying, "Arise, and I will send you away." And ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧ arose, and the two of them went out, he and ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧, outside.

[27] They were going down at the end of the city, and ⟦samuel|Shmuel|Samuel⟧ said to ⟦saul|Shaul|Saul⟧, "Say to the young man that he pass on before us" — and he passed on — "and you, stand now, and I will make you hear the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"**, **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — the Hebrew names kept. **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew chain of וַ / וְ is kept; where a clause begins without it (v5, 11, 14, 27: "They were …") the Hebrew sets a circumstance before the next event.
- **"a man of Benjamin"** (v1) — the reading tradition (qere) מִבִּנְיָמִין; the written text (ketiv) has the name in two words, מבן־ימין; the meaning is the same. **"a Benjamite" → "son of a Benjaminite man"** — Hebrew בֶּן־אִישׁ יְמִינִי "son of a man of Yemini" (so also v4 "the land of Yemini", v21 "a son of Yemini"). **"a mighty man of power" → "a mighty one of ability"** — Hebrew גִּבּוֹר חָיִל: חַיִל is strength, ability, also wealth and standing; which is meant of Kish is not said.
- **"a choice young man, and a goodly" → "a young man and good"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּחוּר וָטוֹב: בָּחוּר is a young man in his prime (literally "chosen"); טוֹב is "good", here commonly taken of his looks. **"a goodlier person" → "better"** — the same word. **"from his shoulders and upward he was higher than any of the people" → "from his shoulder and upward he was taller than all the people"** — singular.
- **"asses" → "she-donkeys"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲתֹנוֹת, the female animals. **"were lost"** (v3, 20) — Hebrew אָבַד, elsewhere "perish"; of animals, to stray, be lost. **"Take now one of the servants" → "Take, please, with you one of the young men"** — Hebrew קַח־נָא; נַעַר is a boy, a young man, an attendant (v5, 7, 8, 10, 22, 27).
- **"he passed … they passed"** (v4) — the Hebrew changes between singular and plural: "he passed … he passed … they did not find … they passed … he passed … they did not find". "Them" after "find" is supplied. **"and there they were not" → "and there was nothing"** — Hebrew וָאַיִן. **"mount Ephraim" → "the hill country of Ephraim"** — Hebrew הַר.
- **"And when they were come" → "They had come"** (v5). **"leave caring for the asses, and take thought for us" → "cease from the she-donkeys and be anxious for us"** — Hebrew יֶחְדַּל מִן "cease from"; דָּאַג "be anxious".
- **"Behold now, there is in this city a man of God" → "Behold, please, a man of Elohim is in this city"** (v6) — Hebrew הִנֵּה־נָא; אִישׁ־אֱלֹהִים, here without the article, in v7, 8, 10 with it. **"an honourable man" → "the man is honored"**. **"all that he saith cometh surely to pass" → "all that he speaks surely comes"** — Hebrew בּוֹא יָבוֹא, literally "coming it comes". **"our way that we should go" → "our way on which we have gone"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־הָלַכְנוּ עָלֶיהָ, a perfect: the way they have set out on.
- **"But, behold, if we go" → "And behold, we go"** (v7) — there is no "if". **"the bread is spent in our vessels" → "the bread is gone from our vessels"** — Hebrew כֵּלֵינוּ, any made article: their bags. **"present" → "gift"** — Hebrew תְּשׁוּרָה, found only here; the meaning ("gift") is taken from the context and is not certain. **"what have we?" → "What is with us?"**
- **"I have here at hand" → "there is found in my hand"** (v8) — Hebrew נִמְצָא בְיָדִי. The shekel is a weight. **"that will I give" → "and I will give it"** — so the Masoretic Text; the Greek has "you will give" (from memory). "It" is supplied.
- **"(Beforetime in Israel, when a man went to enquire of God, thus he spake, Come, and let us go to the seer: for he that is now called a Prophet was beforetime called a Seer.)" → "Formerly in Israel, thus said the man when he went to seek Elohim: 'Come, and let us go as far as the seer'; for the prophet of today was formerly called the seer."** (v9) — word for word: לְפָנִים בְּיִשְׂרָאֵל כֹּה־אָמַר הָאִישׁ בְּלֶכְתּוֹ לִדְרוֹשׁ אֱלֹהִים … כִּי לַנָּבִיא הַיּוֹם יִקָּרֵא לְפָנִים הָרֹאֶה, literally "for to the prophet of today it was formerly called 'the seer'". דָּרַשׁ is "seek". רֹאֶה "seer" is the participle of רָאָה "see"; נָבִיא is "prophet". The brackets of the KJV are not in the Hebrew.
- **"Well said" → "Your word is good"** (v10) — Hebrew טוֹב דְּבָרְךָ.
- **"as they went up the hill to the city, they found young maidens" → "They were going up by the ascent of the city, and they found girls"** (v11) — Hebrew מַעֲלֵה הָעִיר; נְעָרוֹת, the feminine of נַעַר.
- **"He is; behold, he is before you" → "He is; behold, before you"** (v12) — "you" is singular here and plural in "As you come" (v13). **"make haste" → "Hasten"**. **"there is a sacrifice of the people to day in the high place" → "the people have a sacrifice today on the high place"** — Hebrew בָּמָה: a raised place of worship.
- **"As soon as ye be come into the city, ye shall straightway find him" → "As you come into the city, so you will find him"** (v13) — Hebrew כְּ … כֵּן. **"they that be bidden" → "the called ones"** (v13, 22) — Hebrew הַקְּרֻאִים, from קָרָא "call" (v24 "I have called the people"): the invited guests. **"for about this time ye shall find him" → "for him — just now you will find him"** — Hebrew כִּי־אֹתוֹ כְהַיּוֹם תִּמְצְאוּן אֹתוֹ: "him" stands twice; כְּהַיּוֹם is literally "as today" (at once, this very day).
- **"when they were come into the city" → "They were coming into the midst of the city"** (v14) — participles. **"came out against them, for to go up" → "was coming out to meet them, to go up"**.
- **"the LORD had told Samuel in his ear a day before" → "Yahweh had uncovered the ear of Samuel one day before"** (v15) — Hebrew גָּלָה אֶת־אֹזֶן, kept: to disclose a thing to someone.
- **"to be captain over my people" → "as leader over my people"** (v16) — Hebrew נָגִיד *nagid*: one who stands in front or is designated; the word is kept apart from "king" (מֶלֶךְ, ch 8), "prince" (שַׂר) and "chieftain" (נָשִׂיא). **"that he may save" → "and he shall save"**. **"I have looked upon my people, because their cry is come unto me" → "I have seen my people, for their outcry has come to me"** — Hebrew רָאִיתִי אֶת־עַמִּי; the Greek has "the affliction of my people" (from memory), as Exod 3:7; the Masoretic Text is kept. צְעָקָה is "outcry"; "their" is singular in Hebrew ("its", of the people).
- **"the LORD said unto him" → "Yahweh answered him"** (v17) — Hebrew עָנָהוּ. **"Behold the man whom I spake to thee of! this same shall reign over my people" → "Behold the man of whom I said to you, 'This one shall restrain my people'"** — Hebrew זֶה יַעְצֹר בְּעַמִּי. עָצַר is "hold back, shut in, restrain" (Gen 16:2; 20:18); only here is it used of ruling a people, and "reign / rule" is an inference from this place. The plain sense of the verb is kept: he will keep the people within bounds. The Hebrew can also be read "the man of whom I spoke to you; this one shall …".
- **"Then Saul drew near to Samuel in the gate" → "And Saul drew near to Samuel in the midst of the gate"** (v18) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ הַשַּׁעַר; in v14 it was "the midst of the city".
- **"for ye shall eat with me to day" → "and you shall eat with me today"** (v19) — "you shall eat" is plural (Saul and his young man); the rest is singular. **"let thee go" → "send you away"** (v19, 26) — Hebrew שִׁלַּח.
- **"lost three days ago, set not thy mind on them" → "that were lost to you three days ago today, do not set your heart on them"** (v20) — Hebrew הַיּוֹם שְׁלֹשֶׁת הַיָּמִים, literally "today three days"; לֵב "heart". **"on whom is all the desire of Israel?" → "for whom is all the desire of Israel?"** — Hebrew וּלְמִי כָּל־חֶמְדַּת יִשְׂרָאֵל. חֶמְדָּה is "desire" or "what is desirable": either "whom does all Israel desire?" or "to whom belongs all that is desirable in Israel?" The Hebrew leaves it open.
- **"of the smallest of the tribes of Israel? and my family the least of all the families of the tribe of Benjamin" → "from the smallest of the tribes of Israel, and my clan the least of all the clans of the tribes of Benjamin"** (v21) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan"; הַצְּעִרָה is literally "the youngest, the smallest". The Masoretic Text has the plural שִׁבְטֵי בִנְיָמִן "the tribes of Benjamin"; it is translated as it stands (the versions have the singular; from memory). **"wherefore then speakest thou so to me?" → "why have you spoken to me according to this word?"** — Hebrew כַּדָּבָר הַזֶּה.
- **"parlour" → "chamber"** (v22) — Hebrew לִשְׁכָּה, a room at a place of worship. **"in the chiefest place among them that were bidden, which were about thirty persons" → "at the head of the called ones, and they were about thirty men"**.
- **"Bring the portion … Set it by thee" → "Give the portion … Put it with you"** (v23) — Hebrew תְּנָה "give"; שִׂים אֹתָהּ עִמָּךְ. "The cook" is הַטַּבָּח, literally "the slaughterer".
- **"the shoulder, and that which was upon it" → "the leg and what was on it"** (v24) — Hebrew הַשּׁוֹק, the upper hind leg, the priests' part in Lev 7:32–34. וְהֶעָלֶיהָ, "and that which was upon it", is an unusual form (the article before a preposition) and is difficult; "the fat tail" (הָאַלְיָה) is a common conjecture (from memory) and is not in the verse. **"And Samuel said" → "And he said"** — the Hebrew names no speaker. **"Behold that which is left! set it before thee, and eat: for unto this time hath it been kept for thee since I said, I have invited the people" → "Behold what is left! Put it before you, eat; for to the appointed time it has been kept for you, saying, 'I have called the people.'"** — Hebrew כִּי לַמּוֹעֵד שָׁמוּר־לְךָ לֵאמֹר הָעָם קָרָאתִי, word for word. The sentence is difficult: "saying" has nothing clear to lean on, and how "I have called the people" belongs to it is not certain. The Masoretic Text is translated as it stands.
- **"Samuel communed with Saul upon the top of the house" → "he spoke with Saul on the roof"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיְדַבֵּר עִם־שָׁאוּל עַל־הַגָּג; the speaker is not named. The Greek reads differently here and at the head of v26: "they spread a bed for Saul on the roof, and he lay down" (from memory); the earlier form of this chapter followed it. The Masoretic Text is restored.
- **"about the spring of the day, that Samuel called Saul to the top of the house" → "about the going up of the dawn, that Samuel called to Saul on the roof"** (v26) — Hebrew כַּעֲלוֹת הַשַּׁחַר. "On the roof" is the reading tradition (qere הַגָּגָה, "to the roof": called up to him there); the written text (ketiv) has הגג "the roof". **"Up, that I may send thee away" → "Arise, and I will send you away"**. **"went out both of them, he and Samuel, abroad" → "the two of them went out, he and Samuel, outside"** — Hebrew הַחוּצָה.
- **"as they were going down to the end of the city" → "They were going down at the end of the city"** (v27) — Hebrew בִּקְצֵה. **"Bid the servant pass on before us" → "Say to the young man that he pass on before us"**. **"but stand thou still a while, that I may shew thee the word of God" → "and you, stand now, and I will make you hear the word of Elohim"** — Hebrew כַּיּוֹם, literally "as the day": now, at this time; וְאַשְׁמִיעֲךָ "and I will make you hear".

### Notes
- **Saul introduced** — good to look at, tall, a Benjaminite, seeking lost she-donkeys. Unexpected beginning for a future king.
- **"Prophet" vs "seer"** (v9) — editorial note: terminology changed. Earlier: *ro'eh* (seer); later: *navi* (prophet). Shows the book's editorial date is well after Samuel's time.
- **Providential coincidence** — lost donkeys lead to Samuel. Prophetic foreknowledge meets personal quest. The private errand becomes a national turning-point.
- **Saul's humility** (v21) — appropriate self-deprecation. Benjamin had been nearly extinguished in Judg 20; Saul's clan the least of the tribe.
- **Priestly portion given to Saul** (v24) — the leg, the priests' portion (Lev 7:32–34). Samuel transfers it to Saul: foreshadowing royal office.
- **High place** (*bamah*) — local altar-shrine. Pre-Solomonic, unproblematic; post-Temple, condemned.
