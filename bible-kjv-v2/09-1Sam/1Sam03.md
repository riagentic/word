## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"precious / no open vision" → "rare / no frequent vision"** (v1) — Hebrew יָקָר *yaqar* = rare / precious. First time "word of Yahweh" defined.
- **"ere the lamp" → "had not yet gone out"** (v3).
- **"made themselves vile" → "were bringing a curse on themselves"** (v13) — Hebrew מְקַלְלִים לָהֶם *meqallelim la-hem*. MT has *la-hem* but older reading likely *Elohim* ("his sons cursed *Elohim*"). Textual variant.
- **"every whit" → "everything"** (v18) — archaic.

### Notes
- **Samuel's call** — classical vocation narrative. Paradigm for later prophets. Jesus' voice in the call narratives echoes this.
- **Revelation scarce** (v1) — prophetic silence in Israel. Samuel's emergence breaks the drought.
- **Eli accepts the oracle** (v18) — theological submission. He does not resist or try to bargain. "It is Yahweh."
- **"None of his words fall to the ground"** (v19) — Hebrew idiom: all that Samuel predicts comes true.
- **Shiloh as prophetic center** (v21) — Samuel's establishment as national prophet. Dan-to-Beersheba extent.
- **Bridge into ch 4** — Samuel's prophetic authority set; ch 4 narrates Eli's predicted judgment.
