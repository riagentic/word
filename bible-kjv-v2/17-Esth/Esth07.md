# Esther 7

[1] And the king and Haman came to drink with Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧.

[2] And the king said to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ also on the second day, at the feast of wine, "What is your request, Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧? And it shall be given you. And what is your petition? Up to half of the kingdom, and it shall be done."

[3] And Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ answered and said, "If I have found favor in your eyes, O king, and if it is good to the king, let my soul be given me at my request, and my people at my petition.

[4] For we have been sold, I and my people, to be destroyed, to be killed and to be done away with. And if we had been sold for male slaves and for maidservants, I would have kept silent, for the adversary is not worth the damage to the king."

[5] And King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ said — and he said to Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ — "Who is he, this one, and where is he, who has presumed in his heart to do so?"

[6] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ said, "A man, an adversary and an enemy: this evil Haman!" And Haman was terrified before the king and the queen.

[7] And the king arose in his fury from the feast of wine to the garden of the palace; and Haman stood to seek for his soul from Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧, for he saw that the evil had been determined against him by the king.

[8] And the king returned from the garden of the palace to the house of the feast of wine, and Haman was falling on the couch on which ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ was. And the king said, "Will he even subdue the queen with me in the house?" The word went out of the king's mouth, and they covered the face of Haman.

[9] And ⟦harbona|Charvonah|Harbona⟧, one of the eunuchs, said before the king, "Also, behold, the tree that Haman made for ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, who spoke good concerning the king, is standing in the house of Haman, fifty cubits high." And the king said, "Hang him on it."

[10] And they hanged Haman on the tree that he had prepared for ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧. And the fury of the king subsided.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"came to banquet with" → "came to drink with"** (v1) — Hebrew לִשְׁתּוֹת "to drink"; the feast (מִשְׁתֶּה) is "a drinking".
- **"the king said again unto Esther on the second day at the banquet of wine" → "the king said to Esther also on the second day, at the feast of wine"** (v2) — Hebrew גַּם "also".
- **"petition … request" → "request … petition"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew שְׁאֵלָה (from שָׁאַל "ask") is "request", בַּקָּשָׁה (from בִּקֵּשׁ "seek") "petition", the same through Esther. **"and it shall be granted thee … and it shall be performed, even to the half of the kingdom" → "And it shall be given you … Up to half of the kingdom, and it shall be done"** — וְתִנָּתֵן, וְתֵעָשׂ, each with "and".
- **"in thy sight" → "in your eyes"** (v3). **"if it please the king" → "if it is good to the king"** — Hebrew עַל־הַמֶּלֶךְ טוֹב. **"let my life be given me" → "let my soul be given me"** — נַפְשִׁי; נֶפֶשׁ is "soul" (here the life itself).
- **"to be destroyed, to be slain, and to perish" → "to be destroyed, to be killed and to be done away with"** (v4) — Hebrew לְהַשְׁמִיד לַהֲרוֹג וּלְאַבֵּד, three active infinitives ("for destroying, killing and doing away with"), the very words of the decree (3:13); אִבֵּד, piel of אָבַד "perish", is "make perish, do away with". **"bondmen and bondwomen" → "male slaves and maidservants"** — עֲבָדִים, שְׁפָחוֹת. **"I had held my tongue" → "I would have kept silent"** — הֶחֱרַשְׁתִּי.
- **"although the enemy could not countervail the king's damage" → "for the adversary is not worth the damage to the king"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי אֵין הַצָּר שֹׁוֶה בְּנֵזֶק הַמֶּלֶךְ. The sense is uncertain. הַצָּר may be "the adversary" (צַר, as in v6) or "the distress" (then: our trouble would not have been worth the damage to the king of being troubled with it); שֹׁוֶה is "equal to, worth"; נֵזֶק "damage" is found only here. The word is rendered as in v6; the other reading stands here.
- **"Then the king Ahasuerus answered and said unto Esther the queen" → "And King Ahasuerus said — and he said to Queen Esther —"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר twice. **"Who is he, and where is he" → "Who is he, this one, and where is he"** — מִי הוּא זֶה וְאֵי־זֶה הוּא. **"that durst presume in his heart to do so" → "who has presumed in his heart to do so"** — אֲשֶׁר־מְלָאוֹ לִבּוֹ, lit. "whom his heart has filled".
- **"The adversary and enemy is this wicked Haman" → "A man, an adversary and an enemy: this evil Haman!"** (v6) — Hebrew אִישׁ צַר וְאוֹיֵב הָמָן הָרָע הַזֶּה; רָע "evil". **"was afraid" → "was terrified"** — נִבְעַת.
- **"arising from the banquet of wine in his wrath went into the palace garden" → "arose in his fury from the feast of wine to the garden of the palace"** (v7) — Hebrew חֵמָה "fury" (lit. "heat"; also v10); the Hebrew has no "went". גִּנַּת הַבִּיתָן "the garden of the palace" (בִּיתָן, the inner palace, 1:5; a loan word).
- **"stood up to make request for his life to Esther" → "stood to seek for his soul from Queen Esther"** (v7) — Hebrew לְבַקֵּשׁ עַל־נַפְשׁוֹ. **"there was evil determined against him by the king" → "the evil had been determined against him by the king"** — כָלְתָה אֵלָיו הָרָעָה, lit. "the evil was completed toward him".
- **"Haman was fallen upon the bed whereon Esther was" → "Haman was falling on the couch on which Esther was"** (v8) — Hebrew נֹפֵל, a participle; מִטָּה "couch, bed". **"Will he force the queen also before me in the house?" → "Will he even subdue the queen with me in the house?"** — כָּבַשׁ "subdue" (Gen 1:28), here of force against a woman. **"As the word went out … they covered" → "The word went out … and they covered"** — two clauses; who covered the face is not named.
- **"Harbonah" (v9)** — Hebrew חַרְבוֹנָה here, חַרְבוֹנָא in 1:10; the token is kept. **"one of the chamberlains, said before the king" → "one of the eunuchs, said before the king"** — לִפְנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ may go with "said" or with "the eunuchs (who stood) before the king". **"Behold also" → "Also, behold"** — גַּם הִנֵּה.
- **"the gallows" → "the tree"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew עֵץ; see 6:4. Haman "made" it (עָשָׂה, v9) and "prepared" it (הֵכִין, v10). **"who had spoken good for the king" → "who spoke good concerning the king"** — דִּבֶּר טוֹב עַל.
- **"Then was the king's wrath pacified" → "And the fury of the king subsided"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׁכָכָה, as of the waters in Gen 8:1.

### Notes
- **"We have been sold"** (v4) — echoes the silver Haman offered (3:9); Esther takes up the word.
- **Esther names Haman** — the moment of disclosure.
- **The king leaves** for the garden; the text gives no reason.
- **Haman on the couch** — he falls before Esther to beg; the king takes it as an assault.
- **Covered face** — a sign of condemnation.
- **Harbona** — a court eunuch supplies the decisive word.
- **Hanged on his own tree** — measure for measure.
