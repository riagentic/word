# Esther 9

[1] And in the twelfth month, that is the month of Adar, on the thirteenth day of it, when the word of the king and his law came to be done, on the day that the enemies of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ hoped to have power over them — and it was turned about, so that the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ themselves had power over those who hated them —

[2] the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ assembled in their cities in all the provinces of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ to stretch out a hand against those who sought their evil; and no man stood before them, for the fear of them had fallen on all the peoples.

[3] And all the princes of the provinces and the satraps and the governors and those who did the work that belongs to the king were lifting up the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, for the fear of ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ had fallen on them.

[4] For ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ was great in the house of the king, and his report was going through all the provinces; for the man ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ went on growing greater.

[5] And the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ struck all their enemies with the blow of the sword and killing and destruction, and did to those who hated them as they willed.

[6] And in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ killed and did away with five hundred men.

[7] And ⟦parshandatha|Parshandata|Parshandatha⟧ and ⟦dalphon|Dalfon|Dalphon⟧ and ⟦aspatha|Aspata|Aspatha⟧

[8] and ⟦poratha|Porata|Poratha⟧ and ⟦adalia|Adalya|Adalia⟧ and ⟦aridatha|Aridata|Aridatha⟧

[9] and Parmashta and Arisai and Aridai and ⟦vaizatha|Vayzata|Vaizatha⟧ —

[10] the ten sons of Haman son of ⟦hammedatha|Hammedata|Hammedatha⟧, the adversary of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, they killed; but on the plunder they did not stretch out their hand.

[11] On that day the number of those killed in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel came before the king.

[12] And the king said to Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧, "In ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ have killed and done away with five hundred men and the ten sons of Haman; in the rest of the provinces of the king, what have they done? And what is your request? And it shall be given you. And what is your petition further? And it shall be done."

[13] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ said, "If it is good to the king, let it be given also tomorrow to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who are in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ to do according to the law of today, and let them hang the ten sons of Haman on the tree."

[14] And the king said for it to be done so; and a law was given in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧, and the ten sons of Haman they hanged.

[15] And the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ assembled also on the fourteenth day of the month of Adar, and they killed in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ three hundred men; but on the plunder they did not stretch out their hand.

[16] And the rest of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were in the provinces of the king assembled, and stood for their soul, and had rest from their enemies, and killed among those who hated them seventy-five thousand; but on the plunder they did not stretch out their hand.

[17] This was on the thirteenth day of the month of Adar; and they rested on the fourteenth of it and made it a day of feasting and gladness.

[18] And the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ assembled on the thirteenth of it and on the fourteenth of it, and rested on the fifteenth of it and made it a day of feasting and gladness.

[19] Therefore the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ of the open country, who dwell in the cities of the open country, make the fourteenth day of the month of Adar gladness and feasting and a good day, and a sending of portions each man to his neighbor.

[20] And ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ wrote these words and sent letters to all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were in all the provinces of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, the near and the far,

[21] to make it stand upon them that they should be holding the fourteenth day of the month of Adar and the fifteenth day of it, in every year,

[22] as the days on which the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ had rest from their enemies, and the month that was turned for them from sorrow to gladness and from mourning to a good day — to make them days of feasting and gladness, and of sending portions each man to his neighbor, and gifts to the needy.

[23] And the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ accepted what they had begun to do, and what ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ had written to them.

[24] For Haman son of ⟦hammedatha|Hammedata|Hammedatha⟧ the ⟦agagite|Agagi|Agagite⟧, the adversary of all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, had devised against the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ to do away with them, and had caused Pur — that is, the lot — to fall, to throw them into confusion and to do away with them.

[25] And when she came before the king, he said by letter that his evil design which he had devised against the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ should return on his head; and they hanged him and his sons on the tree.

[26] Therefore they called these days Purim, after the name of the Pur. Therefore, because of all the words of this letter, and what they had seen concerning this, and what had come to them,

[27] the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ made it stand and accepted it upon themselves and upon their seed and upon all who join themselves to them — and it shall not pass away — that they should be holding these two days according to their writing and according to their appointed time in every year.

[28] And these days are remembered and held in every generation, every clan, every province and every city; and these days of Purim shall not pass from the midst of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and their remembrance shall not come to an end from their seed.

[29] And Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧, daughter of ⟦abihail|Avichayil|Abihail⟧, wrote — and ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ — with all power, to make stand this second letter of Purim.

[30] And he sent letters to all the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, to the hundred and twenty-seven provinces, the kingdom of ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, words of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and truth,

[31] to make stand these days of Purim in their appointed times, as ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ and Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ had made it stand upon them, and as they had made it stand upon their soul and upon their seed: the matters of the fasts and their crying out.

[32] And the command of ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ made stand these matters of Purim, and it was written in the book.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when the king's commandment and his decree drew near to be put in execution" → "when the word of the king and his law came to be done"** (v1) — Hebrew הִגִּיעַ "reached, arrived"; דְּבַר־הַמֶּלֶךְ "the word of the king"; דָּת "law", a Persian loan word (also v13, 14). **"hoped to have power over them" kept** — שִׂבֵּר "hope", שָׁלַט "have power over". **"(though it was turned to the contrary, that the Jews had rule over them that hated them)" → "— and it was turned about, so that the Jews themselves had power over those who hated them —"** — וְנַהֲפוֹךְ הוּא, an infinitive absolute; הֵמָּה "they themselves".
- **"to lay hand on such as sought their hurt" → "to stretch out a hand against those who sought their evil"** (v2) — שָׁלַח יָד בְּ (also v10, 15, 16); רָעָתָם "their evil". **"no man could withstand them" → "no man stood before them"** — לֹא־עָמַד לִפְנֵיהֶם; no "could". **"gathered themselves together" → "assembled"** (v2, 15, 16, 18) — נִקְהֲלוּ.
- **"the rulers of the provinces, and the lieutenants, and the deputies, and officers of the king" → "the princes of the provinces and the satraps and the governors and those who did the work that belongs to the king"** (v3) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַמְּדִינוֹת, אֲחַשְׁדַּרְפְּנִים (a Persian loan word), פַּחוֹת, עֹשֵׂי הַמְּלָאכָה אֲשֶׁר לַמֶּלֶךְ. **"helped the Jews" → "were lifting up the Jews"** — מְנַשְּׂאִים, piel of נָשָׂא "lift": they supported them (or honored them).
- **"his fame went out throughout all the provinces" → "his report was going through all the provinces"** (v4) — שָׁמְעוֹ הוֹלֵךְ. **"this man Mordecai waxed greater and greater" → "the man Mordecai went on growing greater"** — הָאִישׁ; הוֹלֵךְ וְגָדוֹל, lit. "going and great".
- **"smote all their enemies with the stroke of the sword, and slaughter, and destruction" → "struck all their enemies with the blow of the sword and killing and destruction"** (v5) — Hebrew מַכַּת־חֶרֶב וְהֶרֶג וְאַבְדָן. **"did what they would unto those that hated them" → "did to those who hated them as they willed"** — כִּרְצוֹנָם.
- **"Shushan the palace" → "Shushan the citadel"** (v6, 11, 12) — Hebrew בִּירָה. **"slew and destroyed" → "killed and did away with"** (v6, 12) — הָרְגוּ … וְאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד "perish" (as 8:11).
- **"And Parshandatha … " (v7–10)** — each name has אֵת, the mark of the object: the ten names are the object of "they killed" in v10. The Hebrew order is kept; nothing is supplied. **"the enemy of the Jews" → "the adversary of the Jews"** (v10, 24) — צֹרֵר, as 8:1. **"but on the spoil laid they not their hand" → "but on the plunder they did not stretch out their hand"** (v10, 15, 16) — בַּבִּזָּה לֹא שָׁלְחוּ אֶת־יָדָם.
- **"was brought before the king" → "came before the king"** (v11) — Hebrew בָּא. **"those that were slain" → "those killed"** — הַהֲרוּגִים.
- **"what have they done in the rest of the king's provinces?"** (v12) — מֶה עָשׂוּ may be a question or an exclamation. **"petition … request" → "request … petition"** (v12) — as 7:2. **"and it shall be granted thee … and it shall be done" → "And it shall be given you … And it shall be done"**.
- **"If it please the king" → "If it is good to the king"** (v13). **"to do to morrow also according unto this day's decree" → "to do according to the law of today"** — כְּדָת הַיּוֹם. **"let Haman's ten sons be hanged upon the gallows" → "let them hang the ten sons of Haman on the tree"** — יִתְלוּ, active, with no subject named; עֵץ, see 6:4 (also v25).
- **"the king commanded it so to be done" → "the king said for it to be done so"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר … לְהֵעָשׂוֹת. **"the decree was given" → "a law was given"** — דָּת, without the article.
- **"the Jews" (v15, 18)** — the qere הַיְּהוּדִים; the ketiv היהודיים is a fuller spelling of the same word. **"slew" → "killed"** (v15, 16).
- **"stood for their lives" → "stood for their soul"** (v16) — Hebrew נַפְשָׁם. **"had rest from their enemies" kept** (v16, 22) — נוֹחַ. **"slew of their foes seventy and five thousand" → "killed among those who hated them seventy-five thousand"** — שֹׂנְאֵיהֶם "those who hated them"; the verbs of v16–18 are infinitives absolute that carry on the narrative.
- **"On the thirteenth day" → "This was on the thirteenth day"** (v17) — "This was" is supplied: the Hebrew verse begins with the date alone. **"day of feasting and gladness" kept** (v17, 18, 22) — מִשְׁתֶּה, שִׂמְחָה. **"the thirteenth day thereof" → "the thirteenth of it"** (v18) — "day" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the Jews of the villages, that dwelt in the unwalled towns" → "the Jews of the open country, who dwell in the cities of the open country"** (v19) — Hebrew הַפְּרָזִים (qere; ketiv הפרוזים, another spelling) and עָרֵי הַפְּרָזוֹת: the open, unwalled country (Ezek 38:11; Zech 2:4). **"made the fourteenth day … a day of gladness and feasting, and a good day" → "make the fourteenth day … gladness and feasting and a good day"** — עֹשִׂים, a participle: what they do now; "a day of" is not in the Hebrew. יוֹם טוֹב "a good day" (= a holiday; also v22). **"sending portions one to another" → "a sending of portions each man to his neighbor"** (v19, 22) — מִשְׁלוֹחַ מָנוֹת אִישׁ לְרֵעֵהוּ.
- **"these things" → "these words"** (v20) — הַדְּבָרִים. **"letters"** (v20, 25, 30) — סְפָרִים / הַסֵּפֶר, lit. "writing(s)"; v26 and v29 have אִגֶּרֶת, the later word for a letter. **"both nigh and far" → "the near and the far"**.
- **"To stablish this among them, that they should keep" → "to make it stand upon them that they should be holding"** (v21) — Hebrew לְקַיֵּם עֲלֵיהֶם: קִיֵּם, piel of קוּם "stand", is "make stand" = establish, confirm (also v27, 29, 31, 32; Ruth 4:7). עֹשִׂים "doing, making" a day is "holding" it (also v27, 28). **"yearly" → "in every year"** (v21, 27) — בְּכָל־שָׁנָה וְשָׁנָה, lit. "in every year and year"; so v28 "generation and generation, clan and clan, province and province, city and city".
- **"rested from their enemies" → "had rest from their enemies"** (v22). **"turned unto them from sorrow to joy, and from mourning into a good day" → "turned for them from sorrow to gladness and from mourning to a good day"** — יָגוֹן "sorrow", שִׂמְחָה "gladness", אֵבֶל "mourning". **"gifts to the poor" → "gifts to the needy"** — מַתָּנוֹת לָאֶבְיוֹנִים.
- **"undertook to do as they had begun" → "accepted what they had begun to do"** (v23) — Hebrew וְקִבֵּל, a singular verb with "the Jews".
- **"had devised against the Jews to destroy them" → "had devised against the Jews to do away with them"** (v24) — חָשַׁב; לְאַבְּדָם. **"had cast Pur, that is, the lot" → "had caused Pur — that is, the lot — to fall"** — הִפִּיל, hifil of נָפַל "fall". פּוּר is a loan word (Akkadian *pūru*, "lot"), explained in the verse itself (3:7). **"to consume them" → "to throw them into confusion"** — לְהֻמָּם, from הָמַם.
- **"But when Esther came before the king" → "And when she came before the king"** (v25) — Hebrew וּבְבֹאָהּ, "at her / its coming": Esther is not named; the feminine suffix may be Esther or "it" (the matter). **"he commanded by letters that his wicked device" → "he said by letter that his evil design"** — אָמַר עִם־הַסֵּפֶר, lit. "he said with the letter"; מַחֲשַׁבְתּוֹ הָרָעָה. **"should return upon his own head" → "should return on his head"** — עַל־רֹאשׁוֹ. **"and that he and his sons should be hanged on the gallows" → "and they hanged him and his sons on the tree"** — וְתָלוּ, a perfect with "and", which in Esther tells the past (as v23); it may also carry on the order ("and that they should hang him").
- **"Purim after the name of Pur" → "Purim, after the name of the Pur"** (v26) — פּוּרִים, the plural, "lots"; עַל־שֵׁם הַפּוּר with the article. **"this letter"** (v26, 29) — אִגֶּרֶת. **"of that which they had seen concerning this matter" → "what they had seen concerning this"** — עַל־כָּכָה, lit. "about thus".
- **"ordained, and took upon them" → "made it stand and accepted it upon themselves"** (v27) — Hebrew קִיְּמוּ וְקִבְּלוּ (qere; the ketiv וקבל is singular); "it" is supplied twice. **"all such as joined themselves unto them" → "all who join themselves to them"** — הַנִּלְוִים, a participle. **"so as it should not fail" → "— and it shall not pass away —"** — וְלֹא יַעֲבוֹר. **"according to their writing, and according to their appointed time" kept** — כִּכְתָבָם וְכִזְמַנָּם; זְמַן "appointed time" (v27, 31).
- **"remembered and kept" → "remembered and held"** (v28) — נִזְכָּרִים וְנַעֲשִׂים, participles. **"every family" → "every clan"** — מִשְׁפָּחָה. **"should not fail from among the Jews" → "shall not pass from the midst of the Jews"** — לֹא יַעַבְרוּ מִתּוֹךְ. **"nor the memorial of them perish from their seed" → "and their remembrance shall not come to an end from their seed"** — זִכְרָם … לֹא־יָסוּף.
- **"Then Esther the queen … and Mordecai the Jew, wrote with all authority" → "And Queen Esther … wrote — and Mordecai the Jew — with all power"** (v29) — Hebrew וַתִּכְתֹּב, a feminine singular verb: Esther is the subject, Mordecai is named after her. אֶת־כָּל־תֹּקֶף carries the mark of the object, lit. "wrote all power" (= with full authority; uncertain). **"to confirm this second letter of Purim" → "to make stand this second letter of Purim"** — לְקַיֵּם.
- **"And he sent the letters" → "And he sent letters"** (v30) — the subject is not named. **"an hundred twenty and seven" → "a hundred and twenty-seven"**. **"of the kingdom of Ahasuerus" → "the kingdom of Ahasuerus"** — מַלְכוּת, standing beside "the provinces". **"with words of peace and truth" → "words of shalom and truth"** — דִּבְרֵי שָׁלוֹם וֶאֱמֶת.
- **"as Mordecai the Jew and Esther the queen had enjoined them" → "as Mordecai the Jew and Queen Esther had made it stand upon them"** (v31) — קִיַּם, a singular verb. **"as they had decreed for themselves and for their seed" → "as they had made it stand upon their soul and upon their seed"** — עַל־נַפְשָׁם. **"the matters of the fastings and their cry" → "the matters of the fasts and their crying out"** — דִּבְרֵי הַצֹּמוֹת וְזַעֲקָתָם.
- **"the decree of Esther confirmed these matters of Purim" → "the command of Esther made stand these matters of Purim"** (v32) — Hebrew מַאֲמַר, lit. "saying" (1:15; 2:20); דִּבְרֵי "words / matters". **"it was written in the book" kept** — בַּסֵּפֶר.

### Notes
- **Two days of fighting** — in the provinces on the 13th of Adar, in Shushan also on the 14th; so the feast falls on the 14th in the open country and on the 15th in Shushan (v17–19).
- **The ten sons of Haman** — named one by one (v7–9).
- **"On the plunder they did not stretch out their hand"** (v10, 15, 16) — said three times, although 8:11 allowed plunder (compare 1 Sam 15:9).
- **Seventy-five thousand** (v16) — killed among "those who hated them", after they "stood for their soul".
- **"Purim"** (v26) — named from *pur*, "lot" (v24; 3:7).
- **The customs of v22** — feasting and gladness, portions sent to one another, gifts to the needy.
- **"This second letter"** (v29) — a further writing that confirms the feast.
