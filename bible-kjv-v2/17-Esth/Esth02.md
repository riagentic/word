# Esther 2

[1] After these things, when the fury of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ had subsided, he remembered Vashti, and what she had done, and what had been decreed against her.

[2] And the king's young men, his ministers, said, "Let them seek for the king girls, virgins, good in appearance.

[3] And let the king appoint overseers in all the provinces of his kingdom, and let them gather every girl, a virgin, good in appearance, to ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel, to the house of the women, into the hand of Hegai the king's eunuch, the keeper of the women; and let their cosmetics be given.

[4] And the girl who is good in the eyes of the king, let her reign in place of Vashti." And the thing was good in the eyes of the king, and he did so.

[5] A man, a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, there was in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel, and his name was ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, the son of ⟦jair|Yair|Jair⟧, the son of ⟦shimei|Shimi|Shimei⟧, the son of ⟦kish|Qish|Kish⟧, a ⟦benjaminite|Yemini|Benjaminite⟧,

[6] who had been carried into exile from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with the exiles who had been carried into exile with ⟦jeconiah|Yechonyah|Jeconiah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, whom ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ had carried into exile.

[7] And he was bringing up Hadassah, that is ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧, the daughter of his uncle, for she had neither father nor mother; and the girl was beautiful in form and good in appearance; and when her father and her mother died, ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ took her for himself as a daughter.

[8] And it came to pass, when the word of the king and his law were heard, and when many girls were gathered to ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel, into the hand of Hegai, that ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ was taken to the king's house, into the hand of Hegai, the keeper of the women.

[9] And the girl was good in his eyes, and she obtained ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ before him; and he hastened her cosmetics and her portions, to give them to her, and the seven girls who were fit to be given her from the king's house; and he moved her and her girls to the best of the house of the women.

[10] ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ had not told her people or her kindred, for ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ had commanded her that she should not tell.

[11] And every day ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ walked about before the court of the house of the women, to know the ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ of ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ and what would be done with her.

[12] And when the turn of each girl arrived to go in to King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, after she had been twelve months under the law of the women — for so the days of their rubbings were fulfilled: six months with oil of myrrh, and six months with spices and with the cosmetics of the women —

[13] then in this way the girl came to the king: all that she said was given her, to come with her from the house of the women to the king's house.

[14] In the evening she came, and in the morning she returned to the second house of the women, into the hand of Shaashgaz the king's eunuch, the keeper of the concubines. She came to the king no more, unless the king delighted in her and she was called by name.

[15] And when the turn of ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧, the daughter of ⟦abihail|Avichayil|Abihail⟧ the uncle of ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, who had taken her for himself as a daughter, arrived to go in to the king, she did not seek a thing except what Hegai the king's eunuch, the keeper of the women, said. And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ was obtaining favor in the eyes of all who saw her.

[16] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ was taken to King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, to his royal house, in the tenth month, that is the month ⟦tebet|Tevet|Tebeth⟧, in the seventh year of his reign.

[17] And the king loved ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ more than all the women, and she obtained favor and ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ before him more than all the virgins; and he set the royal crown on her head and made her queen in place of Vashti.

[18] And the king made a great feast for all his princes and his servants, the feast of ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧; and he made a release for the provinces, and gave gifts according to the hand of the king.

[19] And when virgins were gathered a second time, ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ was sitting in the king's gate.

[20] ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ was not telling her kindred or her people, as ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ had commanded her; and ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ did the command of ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, as when she was in his care.

[21] In those days, while ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ was sitting in the king's gate, ⟦bigthan|Bigtan|Bigthan⟧ and Teresh, two of the king's eunuchs, of the keepers of the threshold, were wrathful, and they sought to stretch out a hand against King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧.

[22] And the thing became known to ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, and he told it to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the queen, and ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ said it to the king in the name of ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧.

[23] And the thing was searched out and found, and the two of them were hanged on a tree; and it was written in the book of the chronicles before the king.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. The book names no god: no divine name is supplied anywhere.
- **"the wrath of king Ahasuerus was appeased" → "the fury of King Ahasuerus had subsided"** (v1) — Hebrew חֵמָה "fury", literally "heat"; שָׁכַךְ "subside", as Gen 8:1. **"was decreed"** — נִגְזַר, literally "was cut".
- **"the king's servants that ministered unto him … fair young virgins" → "the king's young men, his ministers … girls, virgins, good in appearance"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew נַעֲרֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ "the king's young men" (attendants), מְשָׁרְתָיו "his ministers"; נְעָרוֹת בְּתוּלוֹת "girls, virgins", two nouns side by side; טוֹבוֹת מַרְאֶה "good in appearance".
- **"officers" → "overseers"** (v3) — Hebrew פְּקִידִים, of the verb פָּקַד "appoint" in the same clause: men set over a task. **"unto the custody of Hege" → "into the hand of Hegai"** (v3, 8, 14) — Hebrew אֶל־יַד, literally "to the hand of". In v3 the name is written הֵגֶא, in v8, 15 הֵגַי: one man, one form.
- **"things for purification" → "cosmetics"** (v3, 9, 12) — Hebrew תַּמְרוּקִים, from מָרַק "scour, rub": what is rubbed on; the exact sense is uncertain. In v12 מְרוּקִים of the same root: "the days of their rubbings".
- **"let the maiden which pleaseth the king be queen" → "the girl who is good in the eyes of the king, let her reign"** (v4) — Hebrew תִּיטַב בְּעֵינֵי "is good in the eyes of"; תִּמְלֹךְ "reign" (a verb, not "be queen"). The same for "the thing pleased the king".
- **"a certain Jew" → "A man, a Jew, there was"** (v5) — the Hebrew order, אִישׁ יְהוּדִי הָיָה; "a Benjaminite" is אִישׁ יְמִינִי, literally "a man of Yemini".
- **"carried away … the captivity" → "carried into exile … the exiles"** (v6) — Hebrew הָגְלָה · גֹּלָה, one root three times. "Who" may refer to Mordecai or to Kish, the last name before it; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"brought up … his uncle's daughter … fair and beautiful" → "was bringing up … the daughter of his uncle … beautiful in form and good in appearance"** (v7) — Hebrew אֹמֵן, a participle, "fostering"; דֹּד "uncle"; יְפַת־תֹּאַר וְטוֹבַת מַרְאֶה. **"for his own daughter" → "for himself as a daughter"** (v7, 15) — לוֹ לְבַת.
- **"it came to pass"** (v8) — וַיְהִי kept. **"commandment and his decree" → "the word of the king and his law"** (v8; 4:3) — Hebrew דְּבַר הַמֶּלֶךְ; דָּת, a Persian loan word, "law" (also v12: "the law of the women").
- **"she obtained kindness of him" → "she obtained chesed before him"** (v9, 17) — Hebrew נָשָׂא חֶסֶד לְפָנָיו, literally "she lifted up chesed before his face"; also "obtaining favor" (v15), נֹשֵׂאת חֵן. **"speedily gave her her things for purification, with such things as belonged to her" → "hastened her cosmetics and her portions"** — Hebrew בִּהֵל "hasten"; מָנוֹת "portions". **"which were meet" → "who were fit"** — הָרְאֻיוֹת, literally "seen (fit)". **"preferred her … unto the best place" → "moved her … to the best of"** — Hebrew שִׁנָּה "change, move"; "place" is not written.
- **"shewed" → "told"** (v10, 20) — Hebrew הִגִּיד. **"kindred"** — מוֹלֶדֶת.
- **"to know how Esther did" → "to know the shalom of Esther"** (v11) — Hebrew לָדַעַת אֶת־שְׁלוֹם אֶסְתֵּר; מִתְהַלֵּךְ "walked about" (hitpael).
- **"every maid's turn was come … after that she had been twelve months, according to the manner of the women" → "the turn of each girl arrived … after she had been twelve months under the law of the women"** (v12) — Hebrew נַעֲרָה וְנַעֲרָה, literally "girl and girl"; הִגִּיעַ "arrive, reach" (v12, 15); כְּדַת הַנָּשִׁים, literally "according to the law of the women". **"sweet odours" → "spices"** — בְּשָׂמִים.
- **"Then thus came every maiden unto the king; whatsoever she desired" → "then in this way the girl came to the king: all that she said"** (v13) — Hebrew וּבָזֶה; אֵת כָּל־אֲשֶׁר תֹּאמַר "all that she would say".
- **"into the second house of the women"** (v14) — Hebrew אֶל־בֵּית הַנָּשִׁים שֵׁנִי: "second" may belong to "house" or mean "a second time"; kept as KJV. **"chamberlain" → "eunuch"** (v3, 14, 15, 21) — סָרִיס, also of a court officer. **"the concubines"** — פִּילַגְשִׁים.
- **"she required nothing but what Hegai … appointed" → "she did not seek a thing except what Hegai … said"** (v15) — Hebrew לֹא בִקְשָׁה דָּבָר; יֹאמַר "say".
- **"into his house royal … the month Tebeth" → "to his royal house … the month Tevet"** (v16) — Hebrew בֵּית מַלְכוּתוֹ, literally "the house of his kingship"; "reign" is the same word, מַלְכוּת. Likewise "the royal crown", כֶּתֶר מַלְכוּת (v17).
- **"grace and favour in his sight" → "favor and chesed before him"** (v17) — Hebrew חֵן וָחֶסֶד לְפָנָיו.
- **"princes"** (v18) — שָׂרִים, kept (not "officials"). **"Esther's feast" → "the feast of Esther"** — מִשְׁתֵּה אֶסְתֵּר. **"a release"** — הֲנָחָה, from נוּחַ "rest": a remission (of taxes, labor, or a holiday); uncertain. **"according to the state of the king" → "according to the hand of the king"** — Hebrew כְּיַד הַמֶּלֶךְ.
- **"sat in the king's gate"** (v19, 21) — יֹשֵׁב, a participle: "was sitting".
- **"the commandment of Mordecai … as when she was brought up with him" → "the command of Mordecai … as when she was in his care"** (v20) — Hebrew מַאֲמַר, literally "saying" (not מִצְוָה "commandment"); בְאָמְנָה אִתּוֹ, literally "in fostering with him", of the root of אֹמֵן in v7.
- **"which kept the door, were wroth, and sought to lay hand on" → "of the keepers of the threshold, were wrathful, and they sought to stretch out a hand against"** (v21) — Hebrew שֹׁמְרֵי הַסַּף "keepers of the threshold"; קָצַף "be wrathful"; שָׁלַח יָד בְּ.
- **"Esther certified the king thereof" → "Esther said it to the king"** (v22) — Hebrew וַתֹּאמֶר.
- **"inquisition was made of the matter, it was found out; therefore they were both hanged on a tree" → "the thing was searched out and found, and the two of them were hanged on a tree"** (v23) — Hebrew בִּקֵּשׁ (pual) "be sought"; דָּבָר "thing", as v4, 22; no "therefore". תָּלָה עַל־עֵץ "hang on a tree": עֵץ is "tree" and "wood", a stake or beam may be meant. **"the book of the chronicles"** — סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, literally "the book of the matters of the days".

### Notes
- **Mordecai** — possibly a name formed on Marduk, a Persian-court name. Kish-Shimei-Saul Benjaminite line, setting up Haman/Agag confrontation.
- **"Carried into exile with Jeconiah"** — 597 BC; Mordecai would be elderly by Xerxes's time (ca. 115+ years); "who" may refer to Kish, or "carried into exile" to his family, not himself personally.
- **Esther** = *hadassah* (myrtle) in Hebrew; *Esther* possibly from Persian *stāra* "star" or Babylonian Ishtar. Dual name suggests Persian-court assimilation.
- **Year 7** = 479 BC, after Xerxes's failed Greek campaign (480-479).
- **"The feast of Esther"** — a feast named after the new queen.
- **Bigthan and Teresh plot** — assassination attempt. Mordecai's discovery sets up later reward.
- **Hanged on a tree** — Persians impaled or displayed the condemned on stakes (Herodotus 3.159); *talah* names only the suspension, not modern "hanging" by a noose.
