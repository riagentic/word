# Esther 1

[1] And it came to pass in the days of ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ — he is the ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ who reigned from ⟦india|Hoddu|India⟧ and to ⟦cush|Kush|Ethiopia⟧, a hundred and twenty-seven provinces —

[2] in those days, when King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ sat on the throne of his kingdom, which was in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel,

[3] in the third year of his reign he made a feast for all his princes and his servants, the force of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧ and ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧, the nobles and the princes of the provinces being before him,

[4] when he showed the riches of the glory of his kingdom and the honor of the beauty of his greatness many days, a hundred and eighty days.

[5] And when these days were fulfilled, the king made for all the people who were found in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel, from great to small, a feast of seven days, in the court of the garden of the king's palace.

[6] White cotton and blue, held fast with cords of fine linen and purple on rings of silver and pillars of marble; couches of gold and silver on a pavement of porphyry and marble and mother-of-pearl and dark stone.

[7] And drink was given in vessels of gold, and the vessels differing from vessels, and royal wine in abundance, according to the hand of the king.

[8] And the drinking was according to the law: none compelling; for so the king had laid down for every great one of his house, to do according to the pleasure of each man.

[9] Also Vashti the queen made a feast of women in the royal house which belonged to King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧.

[10] On the seventh day, when the heart of the king was good with wine, he said to Mehuman, ⟦biztha|Bizzeta|Biztha⟧, ⟦harbona|Charvona|Harbona⟧, ⟦bigtha|Bigta|Bigtha⟧ and ⟦abagtha|Avagta|Abagtha⟧, ⟦zethar|Zetar|Zethar⟧ and ⟦carkas|Karkas|Carkas⟧, the seven eunuchs who ministered to the face of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧,

[11] to bring Vashti the queen before the king with the royal crown, to show the peoples and the princes her beauty; for she was good in appearance.

[12] And Queen Vashti refused to come at the word of the king that was by the hand of the eunuchs; and the king was very wrathful, and his fury burned in him.

[13] And the king said to the wise men who knew the times — for so was the matter of the king before all who knew law and judgment,

[14] and the one near to him was ⟦carshena|Karshena|Carshena⟧, ⟦shethar|Shetar|Shethar⟧, ⟦admatha|Admata|Admatha⟧, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, ⟦memucan|Memuchan|Memucan⟧, the seven princes of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧ and ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧, who saw the face of the king, who sat first in the kingdom —

[15] "According to law, what is to be done with Queen Vashti, because she has not done the command of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ by the hand of the eunuchs?"

[16] And ⟦memucan|Memuchan|Memucan⟧ said before the king and the princes, "Not against the king alone has Vashti the queen done wrong, but against all the princes and against all the peoples that are in all the provinces of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧.

[17] For the matter of the queen will go out to all the women, to make their husbands despised in their eyes, when they say, 'King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ said to bring Vashti the queen before him, and she did not come.'

[18] And this day the princesses of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧ and ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧ who have heard of the matter of the queen will say it to all the princes of the king; and there will be contempt and wrath enough.

[19] If it is good to the king, let a royal word go out from before him, and let it be written among the laws of ⟦persia|Paras|the Persians⟧ and ⟦media|Madai|the Medes⟧, and let it not pass away: that Vashti shall not come before King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧; and let the king give her royalty to her neighbor who is better than she.

[20] And when the decree of the king that he shall make is heard in all his kingdom — for it is great — all the women will give honor to their husbands, from great to small."

[21] And the word was good in the eyes of the king and the princes; and the king did according to the word of ⟦memucan|Memuchan|Memucan⟧.

[22] And he sent letters to all the provinces of the king, to each province according to its writing and to each people according to its tongue, that every man should be prince in his house and speak according to the tongue of his people.

## Changes

- **"Now it came to pass" → "And it came to pass"** (v1) — Hebrew וַיְהִי. The sentence runs on to v3; the book opens the way the narrative books do.
- **"which reigned, from India even unto Ethiopia, over an hundred and seven and twenty provinces" → "who reigned from India and to Cush, a hundred and twenty-seven provinces"** (v1) — Hebrew מֵהֹדּוּ וְעַד־כּוּשׁ; no "over". מְדִינָה "province".
- **"the throne of his kingdom"** (v2) — כִּסֵּא מַלְכוּתוֹ. **"Shushan the palace" → "Susa the citadel"** (v2, 5) — בִּירָה "citadel" (as Neh 1:1).
- **"made a feast unto all his princes" → "made a feast for all his princes"** (v3) — מִשְׁתֶּה, a drinking; שָׂרִים "princes" (so v11, 14, 16, 18, 21; "officials" of the previous text is not the word). **"the power of Persia and Media" → "the force of Persia and Media"** — חֵיל, the army. **"the nobles"** — פַּרְתְּמִים, a Persian loan word ("foremost ones").
- **"the riches of his glorious kingdom and the honour of his excellent majesty" → "the riches of the glory of his kingdom and the honor of the beauty of his greatness"** (v4) — four nouns in a chain: עֹשֶׁר כְּבוֹד מַלְכוּתוֹ … יְקָר תִּפְאֶרֶת גְּדוּלָּתוֹ. **"an hundred and fourscore days" → "a hundred and eighty days"**.
- **"were expired" → "were fulfilled"** (v5) — בִּמְלוֹאת. **"present" → "who were found"** — הַנִּמְצְאִים. **"both unto great and small" → "from great to small"** (v5, 20) — לְמִגָּדוֹל וְעַד־קָטָן. **"the king's palace"** — בִּיתַן, the inner palace (a loan word).
- **"Where were white, green, and blue hangings" → "White cotton and blue"** (v6) — the verse has no verb and no "hangings": חוּר כַּרְפַּס וּתְכֵלֶת. חוּר "white (stuff)" (8:15); כַּרְפַּס "cotton" or fine linen, a Persian loan word found only here (uncertain); תְּכֵלֶת "blue", the dye of Exod 25:4. **"fastened" → "held fast"** — אָחוּז. **"fine linen"** — בּוּץ, the later word. **"silver rings"** — גְּלִילִים, "rings" or "rods" (uncertain). **"pillars of marble"** — שֵׁשׁ, the word for fine linen, here a white stone (marble or alabaster: uncertain). **"the beds" → "couches"** — מִטּוֹת. **"red, and blue, and white, and black, marble" → "porphyry and marble and mother-of-pearl and dark stone"** — בַּהַט וָשֵׁשׁ וְדַר וְסֹחָרֶת: four stones, each found only here; בַּהַט is a loan word, perhaps Persian; all four senses are uncertain. KJV's colors are guesses as well.
- **"they gave them drink" → "drink was given"** (v7) — the infinitive הַשְׁקוֹת, no subject. **"the vessels being diverse one from another" → "the vessels differing from vessels"** — כֵלִים מִכֵּלִים שׁוֹנִים. **"according to the state of the king" → "according to the hand of the king"** — כְּיַד הַמֶּלֶךְ, his bounty.
- **"according to the law; none did compel" → "according to the law: none compelling"** (v8) — דָּת "law", a Persian loan word (so v13, 15, 19). **"appointed to all the officers" → "laid down for every great one of his house"** — יִסַּד, literally "founded"; רַב בֵּיתוֹ. **"every man's pleasure" → "the pleasure of each man"** — אִישׁ־וָאִישׁ, literally "man and man".
- **"a feast for the women" → "a feast of women"** (v9) — מִשְׁתֵּה נָשִׁים. **"the royal house"** — בֵּית הַמַּלְכוּת.
- **"merry with wine" → "good with wine"** (v10) — כְּטוֹב לֵב. **"commanded" → "said to"** — אָמַר. **"chamberlains" → "eunuchs"** (v10, 12, 15) — סָרִיסִים: also used of court officers; whether castrated is not said. **"that served in the presence of" → "who ministered to the face of"** — הַמְשָׁרְתִים אֶת־פְּנֵי. "And" before Abagtha and before Carkas, as written.
- **"the crown royal" → "the royal crown"** (v11) — כֶּתֶר מַלְכוּת. **"fair to look on" → "good in appearance"** — טוֹבַת מַרְאֶה.
- **"by his chamberlains" → "by the hand of the eunuchs"** (v12, 15). **"wroth" → "wrathful"; "his anger" → "his fury"** — קָצַף; חֵמָה.
- **"for so was the king's manner toward all that knew law and judgment" → "for so was the matter of the king before all who knew law and judgment"** (v13) — דְּבַר "matter"; לִפְנֵי "before". דִּין "judgment" (the row's "cause" is the single case; here the knowledge of judging). The words the king says begin only in v15; v13b–14 is an aside.
- **"the next unto him" → "the one near to him"** (v14) — הַקָּרֹב אֵלָיו, singular, before the seven names. **"which saw the king's face"** — רֹאֵי פְּנֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ. **"sat the first" → "sat first"** — רִאשֹׁנָה.
- **"the commandment" → "the command"** (v15) — מַאֲמַר, literally "saying".
- **"done wrong"** (v16) — עָוְתָה, the verb of עָוֹן "iniquity". **"Memucan"** — the qere מְמוּכָן; the ketiv מומכן differs only in spelling.
- **"this deed of the queen" → "the matter of the queen"** (v17, 18) — דְּבַר. **"so that they shall despise their husbands" → "to make their husbands despised"** — לְהַבְזוֹת, hifil. **"it shall be reported" → "they say"** — בְּאָמְרָם. **"commanded" → "said"** — אָמַר.
- **"Likewise shall the ladies" → "And this day the princesses"** (v18) — שָׂרוֹת, the feminine of שַׂר. **"say … unto" → "say it to"** — "it" is supplied. **"Thus shall there arise too much contempt and wrath" → "and there will be contempt and wrath enough"** — וּכְדַי בִּזָּיוֹן וָקָצֶף, literally "and as enough of contempt and wrath".
- **"If it please the king" → "If it is good to the king"** (v19) — אִם־עַל־הַמֶּלֶךְ טוֹב. **"a royal commandment" → "a royal word"** — דְּבַר־מַלְכוּת. **"that it be not altered" → "and let it not pass away"** — וְלֹא יַעֲבוֹר, literally "and it shall not pass over". **"her royal estate" → "her royalty"** — מַלְכוּתָהּ. **"unto another" → "to her neighbor"** — רְעוּתָהּ, as 1 Sam 15:28.
- **"decree"** (v20) — פִּתְגָם, a Persian loan word. **"shall be published" → "is heard"** — וְנִשְׁמַע. **"for it is great"** — כִּי רַבָּה הִיא. **"give … honour"** — יְקָר, as v4.
- **"the saying pleased" → "the word was good in the eyes of"** (v21) — וַיִּיטַב … בְּעֵינֵי.
- **"letters"** (v22) — סְפָרִים, the word for "book", here documents sent. **"into every province according to the writing thereof, and to every people after their language" → "to each province according to its writing and to each people according to its tongue"** — literally "province and province", "people and people"; כְּתָב "writing" (the script); לָשׁוֹן "tongue". **"bear rule" → "be prince"** — שֹׂרֵר, the verb of שַׂר. **"and that it should be published according to the language of every people" → "and speak according to the tongue of his people"** — וּמְדַבֵּר כִּלְשׁוֹן עַמּוֹ: a second participle with "every man" as subject; the previous text made it the publishing of the decree.

### Notes
- **Ahasuerus = Xerxes I** (486-465 BC). "Third year" = 483 BC, when Xerxes's historical Susa council planned the Greek invasion.
- **127 provinces** — the Persian Empire at its greatest extent (India's frontier to Ethiopia/Nubia).
- **Six-month display** + seven-day party — extraordinary scope matches Persian royal excess.
- **Vashti** — historical king's consort was Amestris (per Herodotus); Vashti may be throne name or variant. Tradition elaborates.
- **"Laws of Persians and Medes" unalterable** — attested principle; Daniel 6 uses same. Theme of chapter.
- **No divine name** — Esther never names God; no divine name is added in this translation. Providence works through human agency without divine-name framing.
