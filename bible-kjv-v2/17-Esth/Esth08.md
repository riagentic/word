# Esther 8

[1] On that day King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ gave the house of Haman, enemy of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, to Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧. ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ came before the king, for ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ had told what he was to her.

[2] The king took off his signet ring, which he had taken from Haman, and gave it to ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧. ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ set ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ over the house of Haman.

[3] ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ spoke again before the king, fell at his feet, and pleaded with him in tears to avert the evil plan of Haman the ⟦agagite|Agagi|Agagite⟧, the scheme that he had devised against the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[4] The king extended the golden scepter toward ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧. So ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ rose and stood before the king.

[5] She said, "If it pleases the king and if I have found favor in his sight, and if the matter seems proper to the king and I am pleasing in his eyes — let it be written to revoke the letters devised by Haman son of ⟦hammedatha|Hammedata|Hammedatha⟧ the ⟦agagite|Agagi|Agagite⟧, which he wrote to destroy the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who are in all the king's provinces.

[6] For how can I endure to see the calamity that will come upon my people? How can I endure to see the destruction of my kindred?"

[7] Then King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ said to Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ and ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, "Behold, I have given ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the house of Haman, and they have hanged him on the pole because he laid his hand on the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[8] You write for the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ as you see fit, in the king's name, and seal it with the king's signet ring; for an edict written in the king's name and sealed with the king's signet ring cannot be revoked."

[9] The king's scribes were summoned at that time in the third month, the month of Sivan, on its twenty-third day. It was written according to all that ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ commanded — to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, to the satraps, governors, and officials of the provinces from ⟦india|Hoddu|India⟧ to ⟦cush|Kush|Ethiopia⟧, 127 provinces, to each province in its own script and to each people in its own language, and to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ in their own script and language.

[10] He wrote in the name of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, sealed it with the king's signet ring, and sent letters by couriers on horseback — riders on swift royal steeds, bred of the royal stud —

[11] empowering the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were in every city to gather together and stand for their lives, to destroy, slay, and annihilate any armed force of any people or province that might attack them, with their children and women, and to plunder their goods —

[12] on a single day in all the provinces of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, the month of Adar.

[13] A copy of the writing, to be issued as a decree in every province, was published to all the peoples, so that the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ should be ready on that day to avenge themselves on their enemies.

[14] So the couriers, mounted on swift royal steeds, went out hurriedly, pressed on by the king's command. The decree was issued in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel.

[15] ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ went out from the presence of the king in royal apparel of violet and white, with a great crown of gold, and a mantle of fine linen and purple; and the city of ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ shouted and rejoiced.

[16] The ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ had light, gladness, joy, and honor.

[17] In every province and every city, wherever the king's command and his decree reached, the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ had gladness and joy — a feast and a holiday. Many of the peoples of the land became ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, for the fear of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ had fallen on them.

## Changes

- **"ring" → "signet ring"** (v2, 8, 10).
- **"lieutenants" → "satraps"** (v9).
- **"Ethiopia" → "Cush"** (v9).
- **"posts on horseback, and riders on mules, camels, and young dromedaries" → "couriers on horseback — riders on swift royal steeds, bred of the royal stud"** (v10) — Hebrew *ha-rakhashim ha-achashteranim benei ha-rammakim*; Persian loanwords for elite royal horses, not literal mules/camels.
- **"blue" → "violet"** (v15) — *tekhelet*.
- **"gallows" → "pole"** (v7) — *etz*, wood/pole; see 2:23.

### Notes
- **Estate transfer** — Haman's property to Esther; Mordecai inherits Haman's official position (signet ring = authority).
- **Unalterable law problem** — Medo-Persian legal inflexibility forces new decree rather than rescinding old one.
- **Counter-decree parallels Haman's** (v11) exactly: "destroy, slay, annihilate" — permissive for Jewish self-defense.
- **Eleven months' notice** — allows preparation; many gentiles will choose Jewish protection or defection.
- **Royal horses** — Persian postal system (Herodotus VIII.98) used relay-horses for rapid message transmission.
- **Mordecai in royal purple** — second-in-command.
- **"Fear of the Jews"** (v17) — reputation fear; some gentiles become Jews (*mityahadim*) — possibly convert, possibly align politically. First attested "becoming Jewish" outside ethnic boundaries.
