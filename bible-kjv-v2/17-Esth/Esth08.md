# Esther 8

[1] On that day King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ gave to Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the house of Haman, the adversary of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧; and ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ came before the king, for ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ had told what he was to her.

[2] And the king took off his signet ring, which he had taken away from Haman, and gave it to ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧. And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ set ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ over the house of Haman.

[3] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ spoke again before the king, and fell before his feet, and wept and pleaded with him to make the evil of Haman the ⟦agagite|Agagi|Agagite⟧ pass away, and his design that he had devised against the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[4] And the king held out to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the scepter of gold; and ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ arose and stood before the king.

[5] And she said, "If it is good to the king, and if I have found favor before him, and the thing is right before the king, and I am good in his eyes, let it be written to turn back the letters, the design of Haman son of ⟦hammedatha|Hammedata|Hammedatha⟧ the ⟦agagite|Agagi|Agagite⟧, which he wrote to do away with the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who are in all the provinces of the king.

[6] For how shall I be able to look on the evil that shall find my people? And how shall I be able to look on the destruction of my kindred?"

[7] And King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ said to Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ and to ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, "Behold, the house of Haman I have given to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧, and him they have hanged on the tree, because he stretched out his hand against the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧.

[8] And you, write concerning the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ as is good in your eyes, in the name of the king, and seal it with the signet ring of the king; for a writing that is written in the name of the king and sealed with the signet ring of the king, there is no turning it back."

[9] And the king's scribes were called at that time, in the third month, that is the month of Sivan, on the twenty-third day of it; and it was written according to all that ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ commanded, to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and to the satraps and the governors and the princes of the provinces that are from ⟦india|Hoddu|India⟧ to ⟦cush|Kush|Cush⟧, a hundred and twenty-seven provinces, to each province according to its writing and to each people according to its tongue, and to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ according to their writing and according to their tongue.

[10] And he wrote in the name of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ and sealed with the signet ring of the king, and sent letters by the hand of the runners on horses, riders of the royal steeds, sons of the mares,

[11] in which the king gave to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ who were in each city to assemble and to stand for their soul, to destroy and to kill and to do away with all the force of any people and province that harass them, little ones and women, and to plunder their spoil,

[12] on one day in all the provinces of King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, on the thirteenth of the twelfth month, that is the month of Adar.

[13] The copy of the writing, to be given as law in each province, disclosed to all the peoples, and for the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ to be ready for this day to avenge themselves on their enemies.

[14] The runners, riders of the royal steeds, went out, hurried and pressed on by the word of the king; and the law was given in ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ the citadel.

[15] And ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ went out from before the king in royal clothing of blue and white, and a great crown of gold, and a robe of fine linen and purple; and the city of ⟦susa|Shushan|Susa⟧ shouted and was glad.

[16] To the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ there was light and gladness and joy and honor.

[17] And in each province and in each city, the place where the word of the king and his law reached, there was gladness and joy to the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, a feast and a good day. And many of the peoples of the land made themselves ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, for the fear of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ had fallen on them.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the Jews' enemy" → "the adversary of the Jews"** (v1) — Hebrew צֹרֵר, participle of צָרַר "be hostile to", the root of צַר "adversary" (7:6). "the Jews" is the qere הַיְּהוּדִים; the written form (ketiv) היהודיים is a fuller spelling of the same word (also v7, 13).
- **"ring" → "signet ring"** (v2, 8, 10) — Hebrew טַבַּעַת, the ring that seals (v8, 10). **"which he had taken from Haman" → "which he had taken away from Haman"** — הֶעֱבִיר, lit. "had made pass from Haman".
- **"spake yet again" → "spoke again"** (v3) — Hebrew וַתּוֹסֶף … וַתְּדַבֵּר, lit. "added and spoke". **"fell down at his feet" → "fell before his feet"** — לִפְנֵי רַגְלָיו. **"besought him with tears" → "wept and pleaded with him"** — two verbs, וַתֵּבְךְּ וַתִּתְחַנֶּן־לוֹ; הִתְחַנֵּן is "seek favor for oneself" (חָנַן).
- **"to put away the mischief of Haman … and his device that he had devised" → "to make the evil of Haman … pass away, and his design that he had devised"** (v3) — Hebrew לְהַעֲבִיר; רָעָה "evil"; מַחֲשֶׁבֶת … חָשַׁב, one root (also v5; 9:24, 25).
- **"Then the king held out the golden sceptre toward Esther" → "And the king held out to Esther the scepter of gold"** (v4) — Hebrew שַׁרְבִיט הַזָּהָב.
- **"If it please the king" → "If it is good to the king"** (v5) — עַל־הַמֶּלֶךְ טוֹב. **"the thing seem right" → "the thing is right"** — כָּשֵׁר, no "seem". **"I be pleasing in his eyes" → "I am good in his eyes"** — טוֹבָה. **"to reverse the letters devised by Haman" → "to turn back the letters, the design of Haman"** — לְהָשִׁיב, hifil of שׁוּב "bring back" (= revoke; also v8); "the design" stands beside "the letters" (apposition). **"letters"** — סְפָרִים, lit. "writings" (סֵפֶר; also v10). **"to destroy the Jews" → "to do away with the Jews"** — לְאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד "perish" (also v11).
- **"how can I endure to see the evil that shall come unto my people" → "how shall I be able to look on the evil that shall find my people"** (v6) — Hebrew אֵיכָכָה אוּכַל וְרָאִיתִי בָּרָעָה אֲשֶׁר־יִמְצָא, lit. "how shall I be able, and I look on the evil that shall find". **"kindred" kept** — מוֹלֶדֶת. **"destruction"** — אָבְדָן, from אָבַד.
- **"because he laid his hand upon the Jews" → "because he stretched out his hand against the Jews"** (v7) — שָׁלַח יָד בְּ. **"gallows" → "tree"** (v7) — Hebrew עֵץ; see 6:4.
- **"Write ye also for the Jews, as it liketh you" → "And you, write concerning the Jews as is good in your eyes"** (v8) — Hebrew וְאַתֶּם first; כַּטּוֹב בְּעֵינֵיכֶם. **"may no man reverse" → "there is no turning it back"** — אֵין לְהָשִׁיב, lit. "there is not to turn back".
- **"on the three and twentieth day thereof" → "on the twenty-third day of it"** (v9) — numbers in ordinary English (Hebrew "on three and twenty in it"; "day" is supplied, as v12 "the thirteenth"). **"the month Sivan"** — Hebrew חֹדֶשׁ סִיוָן, a Babylonian month name.
- **"lieutenants" → "satraps"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲחַשְׁדַּרְפְּנִים, a Persian loan word (the title of the governor of a great province). **"deputies" → "governors"** — פַּחוֹת. **"rulers of the provinces" → "princes of the provinces"** — שָׂרֵי הַמְּדִינוֹת (שַׂר "prince").
- **"India … Ethiopia" → "Hoddu … Kush" (tokens)** (v9) — Hebrew הֹדּוּ, כּוּשׁ.
- **"an hundred twenty and seven provinces" → "a hundred and twenty-seven provinces"** (v9). **"unto every province according to the writing thereof, and unto every people after their language" → "to each province according to its writing and to each people according to its tongue"** — מְדִינָה וּמְדִינָה, עַם וָעָם, lit. "province and province", "people and people" (so עִיר וָעִיר "city and city", v11, 17); כְּתָב "writing", of a script and of a written text (also v8, 13); לָשׁוֹן "tongue".
- **"sent letters by posts on horseback, and riders on mules, camels, and young dromedaries" → "sent letters by the hand of the runners on horses, riders of the royal steeds, sons of the mares"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּיַד הָרָצִים בַּסּוּסִים רֹכְבֵי הָרֶכֶשׁ הָאֲחַשְׁתְּרָנִים בְּנֵי הָרַמָּכִים. רָצִים "runners" (the king's couriers); רֶכֶשׁ "steeds", a kind of swift horse (1 Kgs 4:28; uncertain). אֲחַשְׁתְּרָנִים is a Persian loan word found only here and in v14, taken as "royal" (of the realm) — uncertain. רַמָּכִים is found only here: "mares" is the usual guess — uncertain; "sons of the mares" = bred from them. Neither word names mules, camels or dromedaries.
- **"to stand for their life" → "to stand for their soul"** (v11) — Hebrew נַפְשָׁם. **"to destroy, to slay, and to cause to perish" → "to destroy and to kill and to do away with"** — the three verbs of 3:13 and 7:4. **"all the power of the people and province that would assault them" → "all the force of any people and province that harass them"** — חֵיל "force"; הַצָּרִים אֹתָם, participle of צוּר / צָרַר. **"both little ones and women"** — טַף וְנָשִׁים, with nothing to show whose: the attackers' (as the decree of 3:13 named the Jews' own) or the Jews' (as those who are attacked). Both readings are held; the verse adds nothing. **"to take the spoil of them for a prey" → "to plunder their spoil"** — שְׁלָלָם לָבוֹז.
- **"the thirteenth day of the twelfth month" → "the thirteenth of the twelfth month"** (v12) — "day" is not in the Hebrew. **"which is the month Adar" → "that is the month of Adar"** — הוּא־חֹדֶשׁ אֲדָר.
- **"The copy of the writing for a commandment to be given in every province was published unto all people" → "The copy of the writing, to be given as law in each province, disclosed to all the peoples"** (v13) — Hebrew פַּתְשֶׁגֶן, a Persian loan word, "copy" (3:14); דָּת "law", a Persian loan word, the same rendering where KJV has "decree" or "commandment" (also v14, 17); גָּלוּי "uncovered, disclosed": the Hebrew has no main verb. **"ready against that day" → "ready for this day"** — לַיּוֹם הַזֶּה; "ready" is the qere עֲתִידִים (ketiv עתודים, the same sense).
- **"So the posts that rode upon mules and camels went out, being hastened and pressed on by the king's commandment" → "The runners, riders of the royal steeds, went out, hurried and pressed on by the word of the king"** (v14) — Hebrew מְבֹהָלִים (the בָּהַל of 6:14) and דְּחוּפִים (the root of נִדְחַף, 6:12; lit. "pushed"). **"the decree was given at Shushan the palace" → "the law was given in Shushan the citadel"** — בִּירָה "citadel".
- **"royal apparel of blue and white, and with a great crown of gold, and with a garment of fine linen and purple" → "royal clothing of blue and white, and a great crown of gold, and a robe of fine linen and purple"** (v15) — Hebrew תְּכֵלֶת "blue"; חוּר "white" (white stuff, 1:6); עֲטֶרֶת "crown", another word than the כֶּתֶר of 6:8 (English has one word); תַּכְרִיךְ "robe", only here (a wrap); בּוּץ "fine linen", the later word beside שֵׁשׁ. **"rejoiced and was glad" → "shouted and was glad"** — צָהֲלָה "cried aloud".
- **"light, and gladness, and joy, and honour" kept** (v16) — Hebrew אוֹרָה, שִׂמְחָה, שָׂשׂוֹן, יְקָר.
- **"whithersoever the king's commandment and his decree came" → "the place where the word of the king and his law reached"** (v17) — Hebrew מְקוֹם אֲשֶׁר דְּבַר־הַמֶּלֶךְ וְדָתוֹ מַגִּיעַ; "the word of the king" also v14. **"a feast and a good day" kept** — יוֹם טוֹב, lit. "a good day" (= a holiday). **"became Jews" → "made themselves Jews"** — מִתְיַהֲדִים, a reflexive verb from יְהוּדִי, found only here: "became Jews" or "declared themselves Jews"; the form does not decide.

### Notes
- **Haman's house** — given to Esther; Mordecai receives the ring Haman held (3:10).
- **A written law cannot be turned back** (v8) — so a second writing is sent, not a recall of the first.
- **The counter-writing follows Haman's** (v11): "destroy, kill, do away with", the words of 3:13.
- **Eleven months' notice** — from the third month to the twelfth.
- **The royal couriers** — swift horses carry the letters (v10, 14).
- **Mordecai in royal clothing** — blue and white, purple, a great crown.
- **"The fear of the Jews"** (v17) — many of the peoples of the land "made themselves Jews".
