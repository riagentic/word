# Esther 10

[1] And King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧ laid forced labor on the land and the coastlands of the sea.

[2] And all the work of his power and his might, and the exact account of the greatness of ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧, to which the king made him great, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧ and ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧?

[3] For ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ was second to King ⟦ahasuerus|Achashverosh|Ahasuerus⟧, and great among the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧, and pleasing to the multitude of his brothers, seeking good for his people and speaking ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ to all his seed.

## Changes

- **"And"** (v1, 2) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Ahasuerus" (v1)** — the qere אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁ; the ketiv אחשרש is a shorter spelling of the same name.
- **"laid a tribute upon the land, and upon the isles of the sea" → "laid forced labor on the land and the coastlands of the sea"** (v1) — Hebrew מַס, elsewhere the forced labor of the conquered (Gen 49:15; Josh 16:10; 1 Kgs 9:21); whether a tax in money is meant here, the word does not say. אִיֵּי הַיָּם "the coastlands of the sea" — אִי is a coast or island, land reached by sea.
- **"all the acts of his power and of his might" → "all the work of his power and his might"** (v2) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה, singular; תֹּקֶף "power" (as 9:29), גְּבוּרָה "might". **"the declaration of the greatness of Mordecai, whereunto the king advanced him" → "the exact account of the greatness of Mordecai, to which the king made him great"** — פָּרָשַׁת (4:7); גִּדְּלוֹ "made him great" (גִּדֵּל, one root with גְּדֻלָּה "greatness").
- **"are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Media and Persia?" kept** (v2) — Hebrew עַל־סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים לְמַלְכֵי מָדַי וּפָרָס, lit. "upon the book of the matters of the days for the kings of", the formula of Kings.
- **"next unto king Ahasuerus" → "second to King Ahasuerus"** (v3) — Hebrew מִשְׁנֶה, "second" in rank. **"great among the Jews" kept** — לַיְּהוּדִים, lit. "to / for the Jews". **"accepted of the multitude of his brethren" → "pleasing to the multitude of his brothers"** — רָצוּי לְרֹב אֶחָיו (Deut 33:24); רֹב is "multitude", also "the greater part" (= most of his brothers): both readings are possible. **"seeking the wealth of his people" → "seeking good for his people"** — דֹּרֵשׁ טוֹב לְעַמּוֹ. **"speaking peace to all his seed" → "speaking shalom to all his seed"** — דֹבֵר שָׁלוֹם לְכָל־זַרְעוֹ.

### Notes
- **Brief closing** — three verses on the king's rule and Mordecai's standing.
- **Forced labor on the land and the coastlands** (v1) — the king's reach to the edges of the sea.
- **Mordecai second to the king** — compare Joseph (Gen 41:43, מִשְׁנֶה).
- **"Seeking good for his people, speaking shalom to all his seed"** (v3) — the last words of the book.
- **"The book of the chronicles of the kings of Media and Persia"** — a source named, as in Kings.
- **No divine name** — the book of Esther names none, here or elsewhere.
