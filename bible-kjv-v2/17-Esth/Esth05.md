# Esther 5

[1] On the third day, ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ put on her royal robes and stood in the inner court of the king's palace, opposite the king's hall; the king was sitting on his royal throne in the royal hall, facing the entrance of the hall.

[2] When the king saw Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ standing in the court, she won favor in his sight; the king extended to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the golden scepter that was in his hand, and ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ approached and touched the top of the scepter.

[3] Then the king said to her, "What is it, Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧? What is your request? Even to half of the kingdom, it shall be given you."

[4] ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ answered, "If it pleases the king, let the king and Haman come today to the banquet that I have prepared for him."

[5] Then the king said, "Bring Haman quickly, that we may do as ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ has said." So the king and Haman came to the banquet that ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ had prepared.

[6] As they were drinking wine, the king said to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧, "What is your petition? It shall be granted you. And what is your request? Even to half of the kingdom, it shall be done."

[7] ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ answered, "My petition and my request —

[8] if I have found favor in the sight of the king, and if it pleases the king to grant my petition and fulfill my request, let the king and Haman come to the banquet that I will prepare for them, and tomorrow I will do as the king has said."

[9] Haman went out that day joyful and glad of heart; but when Haman saw ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ at the king's gate — that he did not stand up or even tremble before him — Haman was filled with rage against ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧.

[10] Nevertheless, Haman restrained himself. When he came home, he sent for his friends and Zeresh his wife.

[11] Haman recounted to them the glory of his riches, the multitude of his sons, all the ways the king had promoted him, and how he had advanced him above the officials and servants of the king.

[12] Haman also said, "Even Queen ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ let no one come with the king to the banquet she had prepared except me; tomorrow also I am invited by her, together with the king.

[13] Yet all this is worth nothing to me so long as I see ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ sitting at the king's gate."

[14] Then Zeresh his wife and all his friends said to him, "Let a pole be made fifty cubits high; in the morning tell the king to have ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ hanged on it; then go in merrily with the king to the banquet." The thing pleased Haman, and he had the pole made.

## Changes

- **"indignation" → "rage"** (v9) — *chemah*.
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v11).
- **"gallows" → "pole"** (v14) — *etz*, wood/pole; see 2:23.

### Notes
- **Third day** — after the three-day fast. Esther's emergence from preparation.
- **Scepter-extension** — royal mercy-sign; Esther's life spared.
- **"Half the kingdom"** (v3, 6) — hyperbolic royal promise (cf. Mark 6:23, Herod-Herodias echo).
- **Delayed petition** — Esther's strategy: build suspense, host two banquets, increase Haman's false confidence.
- **Mordecai's continued defiance** (v9) — escalates from not bowing to not even rising.
- **Haman's boasts** — wealth, family, promotion, royal access. All undone before the book ends.
- **75-foot pole** — deliberately grotesque; public, visible from afar. Iron of the narrative: Haman himself will hang on it.
