# Esther 5

[1] And it came to pass on the third day that ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ put on royalty and stood in the inner court of the king's house, opposite the king's house; and the king was sitting on the throne of his kingdom in the royal house, opposite the entrance of the house.

[2] And it came to pass, when the king saw ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the queen standing in the court, that she obtained favor in his eyes; and the king held out to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the golden scepter that was in his hand, and ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ came near and touched the top of the scepter.

[3] And the king said to her, "What is it, ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the queen? And what is your petition? Up to half of the kingdom, and it shall be given you."

[4] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ said, "If it is good to the king, let the king and Haman come today to the feast that I have made for him."

[5] And the king said, "Make Haman hasten to do the word of ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧." And the king and Haman came to the feast that ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ had made.

[6] And the king said to ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ at the feast of wine, "What is your request? And it shall be given you. And what is your petition? Up to half of the kingdom, and it shall be done."

[7] And ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ answered and said, "My request and my petition:

[8] if I have found favor in the eyes of the king, and if it is good to the king to give my request and to do my petition, let the king and Haman come to the feast that I will make for them, and tomorrow I will do according to the word of the king."

[9] And Haman went out on that day glad and good of heart; and when Haman saw ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ in the king's gate, and he did not rise and did not stir for him, Haman was filled with fury against ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧.

[10] And Haman restrained himself and came to his house, and he sent and brought those who loved him and Zeresh his wife.

[11] And Haman recounted to them the glory of his riches, and the multitude of his sons, and all with which the king had made him great, and how he had lifted him up above the princes and the servants of the king.

[12] And Haman said, "Also ⟦esther|Ester|Esther⟧ the queen brought no one with the king to the feast that she had made except me; and also for tomorrow I am called by her with the king.

[13] And all this is not worth it to me, at every time that I see ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ sitting in the king's gate."

[14] And Zeresh his wife and all those who loved him said to him, "Let them make a tree fifty cubits high, and in the morning speak to the king, and let them hang ⟦mordecai|Mordechai|Mordecai⟧ on it; and go in with the king to the feast glad." And the thing was good before Haman, and he made the tree.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"it came to pass"** (v1, 2) — וַיְהִי kept.
- **"put on her royal apparel … over against the king's house: and the king sat upon his royal throne in the royal house, over against the gate of the house" → "put on royalty … opposite the king's house; and the king was sitting on the throne of his kingdom in the royal house, opposite the entrance of the house"** (v1) — Hebrew וַתִּלְבַּשׁ מַלְכוּת, literally "she put on kingship": "apparel" is not written. בֵּית הַמֶּלֶךְ "the king's house" (not "palace"), בֵּית הַמַּלְכוּת "the house of the kingdom" = the royal house; כִּסֵּא מַלְכוּתוֹ "the throne of his kingdom"; נֹכַח "opposite"; יוֹשֵׁב "was sitting"; פֶּתַח "entrance".
- **"she obtained favour in his sight … held out … drew near" → "she obtained favor in his eyes … held out … came near"** (v2) — Hebrew נָשְׂאָה חֵן בְּעֵינָיו, literally "she lifted up favor in his eyes" (as 2:15); הוֹשִׁיט "hold out"; שַׁרְבִיט "scepter" (as 4:11); רֹאשׁ "top", literally "head".
- **"What wilt thou, queen Esther? and what is thy request? it shall be even given thee to the half of the kingdom" → "What is it, Esther the queen? And what is your petition? Up to half of the kingdom, and it shall be given you"** (v3) — Hebrew מַה־לָּךְ, literally "what to you?" (= what do you want; the "What ails you?" of Josh 15:18 would say distress, and English "What is it to you?" would say dismissal); בַּקָּשָׁה, noun of בִּקֵּשׁ "seek": "petition" (v3, 6–8), beside שְׁאֵלָה "request" (v6–8), noun of שָׁאַל "ask"; עַד־חֲצִי הַמַּלְכוּת "up to half of the kingdom"; וְיִנָּתֵן "and it shall be given".
- **"If it seem good unto the king" → "If it is good to the king"** (v4, 8) — Hebrew אִם־עַל־הַמֶּלֶךְ טוֹב, literally "if upon the king it is good", as 3:9. **"banquet" → "feast"** (v4–14) — מִשְׁתֶּה, "a drinking"; "prepared" → "made", עָשָׂה.
- **"Cause Haman to make haste, that he may do as Esther hath said" → "Make Haman hasten to do the word of Esther"** (v5) — Hebrew מַהֲרוּ אֶת־הָמָן, "make Haman hasten"; דְּבַר אֶסְתֵּר "the word of Esther".
- **"at the banquet of wine, What is thy petition? and it shall be granted thee: and what is thy request? even to the half of the kingdom it shall be performed" → "at the feast of wine, What is your request? And it shall be given you. And what is your petition? Up to half of the kingdom, and it shall be done"** (v6) — Hebrew שְׁאֵלָה "request" (as 1 Kgs 2:16); וְיִנָּתֵן "and it shall be given"; וְתֵעָשׂ "and it shall be done". KJV and most versions reverse the two nouns; each Hebrew noun keeps one English word here (v3, 6–8).
- **"to grant my petition, and to perform my request … I will do to morrow as the king hath said" → "to give my request and to do my petition … tomorrow I will do according to the word of the king"** (v8) — Hebrew לָתֵת "to give", לַעֲשׂוֹת "to do"; כִּדְבַר הַמֶּלֶךְ "according to the word of the king". The answer in v7 breaks off: "My request and my petition:" and v8 goes on with "if".
- **"joyful and with a glad heart … that he stood not up, nor moved for him, he was full of indignation" → "glad and good of heart … and he did not rise and did not stir for him, Haman was filled with fury"** (v9) — Hebrew שָׂמֵחַ וְטוֹב לֵב, literally "glad and good of heart"; לֹא־קָם וְלֹא־זָע מִמֶּנּוּ, זוּעַ "move, stir" (or "tremble"); חֵמָה "fury", as 3:5.
- **"Nevertheless Haman refrained himself: and when he came home, he sent and called for his friends" → "And Haman restrained himself and came to his house, and he sent and brought those who loved him"** (v10, 14) — Hebrew הִתְאַפֵּק "restrain oneself"; וַיָּבֵא "brought"; אֹהֲבָיו, the participle of אָהַב "love": "those who love him" = his friends.
- **"told them of the glory of his riches … all the things wherein the king had promoted him, and how he had advanced him above the princes" → "recounted to them the glory of his riches … all with which the king had made him great, and how he had lifted him up above the princes"** (v11) — Hebrew סִפֵּר "recount"; גִּדֵּל "make great" and נִשֵּׂא "lift up", as 3:1; שָׂרִים "princes" (not "officials").
- **"Yea, Esther the queen did let no man come in … save myself; and to morrow am I invited unto her also" → "Also Esther the queen brought no one … except me; and also for tomorrow I am called by her"** (v12) — Hebrew אַף "also, even"; הֵבִיאָה "brought"; קָרוּא לָהּ "called by her" (literally "called to her").
- **"Yet all this availeth me nothing, so long as" → "And all this is not worth it to me, at every time that"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵינֶנּוּ שֹׁוֶה לִי, as 3:8; בְּכָל־עֵת אֲשֶׁר "at every time that".
- **"Let a gallows be made of fifty cubits high, and to morrow speak thou unto the king that Mordecai may be hanged thereon: then go thou in merrily … and he caused the gallows to be made" → "Let them make a tree fifty cubits high, and in the morning speak to the king, and let them hang Mordecai on it; and go in … glad … and he made the tree"** (v14) — Hebrew יַעֲשׂוּ־עֵץ "let them make a tree"; עֵץ is "tree" and "wood", here a stake or beam (as 2:23; "gallows" says a structure the word does not name); בַּבֹּקֶר "in the morning"; אֱמֹר "say, speak", no object; וְיִתְלוּ "and let them hang", a wish of its own, not "that"; שָׂמֵחַ "glad", as v9; וַיִּיטַב הַדָּבָר לִפְנֵי הָמָן "and the thing was good before Haman"; וַיַּעַשׂ "and he made".

### Notes
- **Third day** — after the three-day fast (4:16).
- **Scepter-extension** — royal sign of acceptance; Esther's life spared (4:11).
- **"Half the kingdom"** (v3, 6) — royal promise (cf. Mark 6:23, Herod-Herodias echo).
- **Delayed petition** — Esther hosts two feasts before she speaks; Haman's confidence grows.
- **Mordecai's continued defiance** (v9) — escalates from not bowing to not even rising.
- **Haman's boasts** — wealth, sons, promotion, royal access. All undone before the book ends.
- **A tree fifty cubits high** (about 25 m) — public, visible from afar. Irony of the narrative: Haman himself will hang on it (7:10).
