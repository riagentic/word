# 1 Peter 1

[1] *Kefa*, an apostle of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, to those who are elect exiles of the Dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

[2] according to the foreknowledge of *Elohim* the Father, in the sanctification of the Spirit, for obedience to *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* and for sprinkling with his blood: May grace and peace be multiplied to you.

[3] Blessed be the *Elohim* and Father of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*! According to his great mercy, he has caused us to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* from the dead,

[4] to an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven for you,

[5] who by *Elohim*'s power are being guarded through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.

[6] In this you rejoice, though now for a little while, if necessary, you have been grieved by various trials,

[7] so that the tested genuineness of your faith — more precious than gold that perishes though it is tested by fire — may be found to result in praise and glory and honor at the revelation of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.

[8] Though you have not seen him, you love him. Though you do not now see him, you believe in him and rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory,

[9] obtaining the outcome of your faith, the salvation of your souls.

[10] Concerning this salvation, the prophets who prophesied about the grace that was to be yours searched and inquired carefully,

[11] inquiring what person or time the Spirit of the *Mashiach* in them was indicating when he predicted the sufferings of the *Mashiach* and the subsequent glories.

[12] It was revealed to them that they were serving not themselves but you, in the things that have now been announced to you through those who preached the good news to you by the *Ruach HaQodesh* sent from heaven — things into which angels long to look.

[13] Therefore, preparing your minds for action, and being sober-minded, set your hope fully on the grace that will be brought to you at the revelation of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*.

[14] As obedient children, do not be conformed to the passions of your former ignorance,

[15] but as he who called you is holy, you also **be holy in all your conduct**,

[16] since it is written, "**You shall be holy, for I am holy**."

[17] And if you call on him as Father who judges impartially according to each one's deeds, conduct yourselves with fear throughout the time of your exile,

[18] knowing that you were ransomed from the futile ways inherited from your forefathers, not with perishable things such as silver or gold,

[19] but with the precious blood of the *Mashiach*, like that of a lamb without blemish or spot.

[20] He was foreknown before the foundation of the world but was made manifest in the last times for the sake of you

[21] who through him are believers in *Elohim*, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope are in *Elohim*.

[22] Having purified your souls by your obedience to the truth for a sincere brotherly love, love one another earnestly from a pure heart,

[23] since you have been born again, not of perishable seed but of imperishable, through the living and abiding word of *Elohim*;

[24] for

"**All flesh is like grass
and all its glory like the flower of grass.
The grass withers,
and the flower falls,
[25] but the word of the Lord remains forever**."

And this word is the good news that was preached to you.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 2, 3, 7, 13) — systematic.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v1) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kefa* = "rock/stone"; the name Jesus gave Simon (John 1:42); Greek Πέτρος *Petros* is a translation of the Aramaic; the original Hebrew/Aramaic name restored.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v12) — systematic.
- **"scattered" → "of the Dispersion"** (v1) — Greek ἐκλεκτοῖς παρεπιδήμοις διασπορᾶς *eklektois parepidēmois diasporas* = "elect exiles of the Dispersion"; "Dispersion" is the standard term.
- **"the trying of your faith, being much more precious than of gold that perisheth" → "the tested genuineness of your faith — more precious than gold that perishes though it is tested by fire"** (v7) — Greek τὸ δοκίμιον ὑμῶν τῆς πίστεως *to dokimion hymōn tēs pisteōs* = "the tested quality / genuineness of your faith."
- **"with joy unspeakable and full of glory" → "rejoice with joy that is inexpressible and filled with glory"** (v8) — Greek χαρᾷ ἀνεκλαλήτῳ καὶ δεδοξασμένῃ *chara aneklalētō kai dedoxasmenē*; "unspeakable" → "inexpressible."
- **"Holy Ghost sent down from heaven" → "the *Ruach HaQodesh* sent from heaven"** (v12) — systematic.
- **"gird up the loins of your mind" → "preparing your minds for action"** (v13) — Greek ἀναζωσάμενοι τὰς ὀσφύας τῆς διανοίας ὑμῶν *anazo¯samenoi tas osphyas tēs dianoias hymōn* = "girding up the loins of your mind" (an OT image of tucking up robes to run or work); rendered idiomatically as "preparing your minds for action."
- **"vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers" → "futile ways inherited from your forefathers"** (v18) — "vain conversation" in 1611 = "empty/futile way of life" (not "talk"); "inherited from your forefathers" more natural.
- **"Be ye holy; for I am holy" → "You shall be holy, for I am holy"** (v16) — OT quotation (Lev 11:44; 19:2); "Be ye" → "You shall be"; modernized.
- **"All flesh is as grass" → "All flesh is like grass"** (v24) — OT quotation (Isa 40:6-8); "as" → "like"; modernized.
- **"the word of the Lord endureth for ever" → "the word of the Lord remains forever"** (v25) — OT quotation (Isa 40:8); "endureth" → "remains"; modernized.

### Notes
- **v16** quotes Lev 11:44; 19:2.
- **v24-25** quotes Isa 40:6-8.
