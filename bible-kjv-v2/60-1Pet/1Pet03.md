# 1 Peter 3

[1] Likewise, wives, be subject to your own husbands, so that even if some do not obey the word, they may be won without a word by the conduct of their wives,

[2] when they see your respectful and pure conduct.

[3] Do not let your adorning be external — the braiding of hair and the putting on of gold jewelry, or the clothing you wear —

[4] but let your adorning be the hidden person of the heart with the imperishable beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which in ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ sight is very precious.

[5] For this is how the holy women who hoped in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ used to adorn themselves, by submitting to their own husbands,

[6] as Sarah obeyed ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, calling him lord. And you are her children, if you do good and do not fear anything that is frightening.

[7] Likewise, husbands, live with your wives in an understanding way, showing honor to the woman as the weaker vessel, since they are heirs with you of the grace of life, so that your prayers may not be hindered.

[8] Finally, all of you, have unity of mind, sympathy, brotherly love, a tender heart, and a humble mind.

[9] Do not repay evil for evil or reviling for reviling, but on the contrary, bless, for to this you were called, that you may obtain a blessing.

[10] For

"**Whoever desires to love life
and see good days,
let him keep his tongue from evil
and his lips from speaking deceit;
[11] let him turn away from evil and do good;
let him seek peace and pursue it.
[12] For the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ are on the righteous,
and his ears are open to their prayer.
But the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ is against those who do evil**."

[13] Now who is there to harm you if you are zealous for what is good?

[14] But even if you should suffer for righteousness' sake, you will be blessed. Have no fear of them, nor be troubled,

[15] but in your hearts honor ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ the Lord as holy, **always being prepared to make a defense to anyone who asks you for a reason for the hope that is in you; yet do it with gentleness and respect**,

[16] having a good conscience, so that, when you are slandered, those who revile your good behavior in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ may be put to shame.

[17] For it is better to suffer for doing good, if that should be ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ will, than for doing evil.

[18] For ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ also suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, that he might bring us to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, being put to death in the flesh but made alive in the spirit,

[19] in which he went and proclaimed to the spirits in prison,

[20] because they formerly did not obey, when ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ patience waited in the days of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, while the ark was being prepared, in which a few, that is, eight persons, were brought safely through water.

[21] ⟦immersion|Immersion|Baptism⟧, which corresponds to this, now saves you, not as a removal of dirt from the body but as an appeal to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for a good conscience, through the resurrection of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[22] who has gone into heaven and is at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, with angels, authorities, and powers having been subjected to him.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v15, 16, 18) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v21) — systematic.
- **"in like manner" → "Likewise"** (v1, 7) — modernized.
- **"putting on of apparel" → "or the clothing you wear"** (v3) — Greek ἐνδύσεως ἱματίων *endyseōs himatiōn* = "putting on of garments"; modernized.
- **"a meek and quiet spirit" → "a gentle and quiet spirit"** (v4) — Greek πνεύματος πραέος καὶ ἡσυχίου *pneumatos praeōs kai hēsychiou*; *praeos* = "meek / gentle"; "gentle" conveys the relational warmth; "meek" is archaic.
- **"in the old time the holy women" → "the holy women … used to"** (v5) — ποτε = "formerly"; expressed by "used to"; modernized.
- **"For he that will love life, and see good days" → "Whoever desires to love life and see good days"** (v10) — OT quotation (Ps 34:12-16); modernized.
- **"refrain his tongue from evil, and his lips that they speak no guile" → "let him keep his tongue from evil and his lips from speaking deceit"** (v10) — OT quotation; "guile" → "deceit"; modernized.
- **"sanctify the Lord God in your hearts" → "in your hearts honor the *Mashiach* the Lord as holy"** (v15) — Greek κύριον δὲ τὸν Χριστὸν ἁγιάσατε *kyrion de ton Christon hagiasate* = "sanctify Christ the Lord" (many MSS); "Christ" → "*Mashiach*"; "sanctify in your hearts" → "honor as holy."
- **"baptism doth also now save us" → "Immersion, which corresponds to this, now saves you"** (v21) — Greek βάπτισμα *baptisma*; rendered "Immersion" to use a literal English translation of the Greek rather than the ecclesiastical loanword "baptism," emphasizing the physical act of immersing in water.

### Notes
- **v10-12** quotes Ps 34:12-16.
