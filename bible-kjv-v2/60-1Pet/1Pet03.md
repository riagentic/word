# 1 Peter 3

[1] Likewise, wives, be subject to your own husbands, so that, even if some disobey the word, they will be gained without a word through the conduct of their wives,

[2] having observed your pure conduct in fear.

[3] Let theirs be not the outward adornment of braiding of hair and putting on of gold or wearing of garments,

[4] but the hidden person of the heart, in the incorruptibility of the meek and quiet spirit, which is very costly before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[5] For so once the holy women also, who hoped in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, used to adorn themselves, being subject to their own husbands,

[6] as Sarah obeyed ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, calling him lord; whose children you became, doing good and not fearing any terror.

[7] Husbands, likewise, dwell together according to knowledge, assigning honor as to a weaker vessel, the female one, as also to fellow heirs of the grace of life, so that your prayers may not be hindered.

[8] And finally, all be like-minded, sympathetic, loving the brothers, tenderhearted, humble-minded,

[9] not repaying evil for evil or reviling for reviling, but on the contrary blessing, because unto this you were called, that you might inherit a blessing.

[10] For

"**He who wants to love life
and to see good days,
let him stop the tongue from evil
and lips from speaking guile;
[11] and let him turn away from evil and do good;
let him seek peace and pursue it.
[12] Because the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ are upon the righteous,
and his ears unto their supplication,
but the face of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ is upon those who do evil**."

[13] And who is he who will harm you, if you become zealots of the good?

[14] But even if you should suffer because of righteousness, blessed are you. But do not fear their fear, nor be troubled,

[15] but sanctify the Lord, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, in your hearts, **ready always for a defense to everyone who asks you an account concerning the hope in you**,

[16] **but with meekness and fear**, having a good conscience, so that, in that in which you are spoken against, those who abuse your good conduct in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ may be put to shame.

[17] For it is better to suffer doing good, if the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ should so will, than doing evil.

[18] Because ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ also suffered once for sins, a righteous one for unrighteous ones, that he might bring you to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, having been put to death in flesh but made alive in spirit,

[19] in which he also went and proclaimed to the spirits in prison,

[20] who once disobeyed, when the patience of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was waiting in the days of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, while an ark was being prepared, into which a few, that is, eight souls, were saved through water.

[21] Which also now saves you, as a counterpart — ⟦immersion|immersion|baptism⟧ — not a putting off of the filth of flesh but the appeal of a good conscience unto ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, through the resurrection of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[22] who is at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, having gone into heaven, angels and authorities and powers having been subjected to him.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v15, 16, 18) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v21) — systematic.
- **"be in subjection" → "be subject"** (v1, 5) — Greek ὑποτασσόμεναι, a participle, as 2:18; also v22 ὑποταγέντων.
- **"if any obey not the word, they also may without the word be won by the conversation of the wives" → "even if some disobey the word, they will be gained without a word through the conduct of their wives"** (v1) — Greek ἀπειθοῦσιν (2:8); κερδηθήσονται, a future of κερδαίνω "gain"; ἄνευ λόγου has no article; ἀναστροφή "conduct".
- **"while they behold your chaste conversation coupled with fear" → "having observed your pure conduct in fear"** (v2) — Greek ἐποπτεύσαντες, as 2:12; τὴν ἐν φόβῳ ἁγνὴν ἀναστροφήν.
- **"whose adorning let it not be that outward adorning" → "let theirs be not the outward adornment"** (v3) — Greek ὧν ἔστω οὐχ ὁ ἔξωθεν … κόσμος: "whose" (the wives'), not "your". The three genitives are verbal nouns: "braiding", "putting around" (περιθέσεως), "putting on" (ἐνδύσεως); "gold" is plural, gold things.
- **"in that which is not corruptible, even the ornament of a meek and quiet spirit" → "in the incorruptibility of the meek and quiet spirit"** (v4) — Greek ἐν τῷ ἀφθάρτῳ τοῦ πραέως καὶ ἡσυχίου πνεύματος: a neuter adjective used as a noun; the Greek has no "ornament" or "beauty". πραΰς is "meek" (πραΰτης "meekness", v16). "Which" (ὅ, neuter) can be the spirit or the whole of what precedes.
- **"of great price" → "very costly"** (v4) — Greek πολυτελές; ἐνώπιον τοῦ θεοῦ "before *Elohim*".
- **"in the old time" → "once"** (v5) — Greek ποτε; ἐκόσμουν is an imperfect, "used to adorn".
- **"whose daughters ye are, as long as ye do well, and are not afraid with any amazement" → "whose children you became, doing good and not fearing any terror"** (v6) — Greek ἐγενήθητε τέκνα (aorist; "children"), then two participles without "if" or "as long as"; πτόησις is "terror, intimidation" (Prov 3:25 LXX).
- **"dwell with them according to knowledge, giving honour unto the wife, as unto the weaker vessel" → "dwell together according to knowledge, assigning honor as to a weaker vessel, the female one"** (v7) — Greek συνοικοῦντες, a participle with no object; τῷ γυναικείῳ is an adjective, "the female (vessel)". The printed text puts the comma after "knowledge" and joins "as to a weaker vessel" to "assigning honor"; the punctuation is the editors', and the words can also be read with "dwell together".
- **"as being heirs together" → "as also to fellow heirs"** (v7) — printed text συγκληρονόμοις, a dative: the wives are the fellow heirs. Editions: συγκληρονόμοις WH Treg NA28 ] συγκληρονόμοι RP (nominative, of the husbands: KJV).
- **"Finally, be ye all of one mind, having compassion one of another, love as brethren, be pitiful, be courteous" → "And finally, all be like-minded, sympathetic, loving the brothers, tenderhearted, humble-minded"** (v8) — five adjectives with no verb ("be" is supplied): ὁμόφρονες, συμπαθεῖς, φιλάδελφοι (φιλαδελφία, 1:22), εὔσπλαγχνοι (of good σπλάγχνα, "inward parts"; as Eph 4:32), ταπεινόφρονες (printed text; KJV's text has φιλόφρονες, "courteous").
- **"railing for railing" → "reviling for reviling"** (v9) — Greek λοιδορία, the noun of the verb in 2:23.
- **"knowing that ye are thereunto called, that ye should inherit a blessing" → "because unto this you were called, that you might inherit a blessing"** (v9) — the printed text has no "knowing"; κληρονομήσητε is "inherit".
- **"For he that will love life … let him refrain his tongue from evil, and his lips that they speak no guile" → "He who wants to love life … let him stop the tongue from evil and lips from speaking guile"** (v10) — quotation of Ps 34:12–16 (LXX 33:13–17); the Greek has no "his" with "tongue" and "lips" (γλῶσσαν, χείλη WH Treg NA28 ] + αὐτοῦ RP); παυσάτω "make cease"; δόλος "guile".
- **"Let him eschew evil … ensue it" → "and let him turn away from evil … pursue it"** (v11) — printed text ἐκκλινάτω δέ; διωξάτω "pursue".
- **"the eyes of the Lord are over the righteous, and his ears are open unto their prayers: but the face of the Lord is against them that do evil" → "upon the righteous, and his ears unto their supplication, but the face of *Yahweh* is upon those who do evil"** (v12) — Greek ἐπί both times ("upon"; in the second place the sense is hostile); δέησις "supplication"; the Greek has no "open". "The Lord" is *Yahweh* in the psalm.
- **"followers of that which is good" → "zealots of the good"** (v13) — printed text ζηλωταί; ὁ κακώσων "he who will do you harm", from κακός "evil".
- **"be not afraid of their terror" → "do not fear their fear"** (v14) — Greek τὸν φόβον αὐτῶν μὴ φοβηθῆτε (Isa 8:12): the fear they cause, or the fear they feel. μακάριοι has no verb.
- **"sanctify the Lord God in your hearts" → "sanctify the Lord, the *Mashiach*, in your hearts"** (v15) — printed text κύριον δὲ τὸν Χριστὸν ἁγιάσατε (Isa 8:13); it can also be read "sanctify the *Mashiach* as Lord". ἁγιάσατε is "sanctify, treat as holy".
- **"to give an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope" → "for a defense to everyone who asks you an account concerning the hope"** (v15) — Greek πρὸς ἀπολογίαν … λόγον: ἀπολογία is a speech in defense; λόγος here is "account", as 4:5.
- **"with meekness and fear" kept, now in v16** (v15–16) — the printed text begins v16 with ἀλλὰ μετὰ πραΰτητος καὶ φόβου, "but with meekness and fear"; KJV counts these words to v15. φόβος is "fear" (2:18; 3:2).
- **"whereas they speak evil of you, as of evildoers … that falsely accuse your good conversation" → "in that in which you are spoken against, those who abuse your good conduct"** (v16) — Greek ἐν ᾧ καταλαλεῖσθε, without "as of evildoers" (καταλαλεῖσθε WH NA28 ] καταλαλοῦσιν ὑμῶν ὡς κακοποιῶν Treg RP; two against two, NA28 decides); ἐπηρεάζοντες "treat abusively, insult".
- **"if the will of God be so" → "if the will of *Elohim* should so will"** (v17) — Greek εἰ θέλοι τὸ θέλημα τοῦ θεοῦ, verb and noun of one root, the verb in the optative ("so" is supplied).
- **"the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God" → "a righteous one for unrighteous ones, that he might bring you to *Elohim*"** (v18) — Greek δίκαιος ὑπὲρ ἀδίκων, without articles. Editions: ἔπαθεν "suffered" RP NA28 ] ἀπέθανεν "died" WH Treg NIV (two against two of the four; NA28 decides); ὑμᾶς "you" WH NA28 RP ] ἡμᾶς "us" Treg.
- **"put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit" → "put to death in flesh but made alive in spirit"** (v18) — Greek σαρκί … πνεύματι, two datives without article or preposition, set against each other (as 4:6); "in spirit" or "by the Spirit".
- **"By which also he went and preached" → "in which he also went and proclaimed"** (v19) — Greek ἐν ᾧ: "in which (spirit)", or "in which state", or "on which occasion". ἐκήρυξεν is "proclaimed" (κηρύσσω); what he proclaimed is not said.
- **"which sometime were disobedient" → "who once disobeyed"** (v20) — Greek ἀπειθήσασίν ποτε, a participle; the Greek has no "because".
- **"wherein few, that is, eight souls were saved by water" → "into which a few, that is, eight souls, were saved through water"** (v20) — Greek εἰς ἥν "into which"; ψυχαί "souls"; διεσώθησαν διʼ ὕδατος, "were saved through", with διά twice: through the water as a passage, or by means of it.
- **"The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us" → "Which also now saves you, as a counterpart — immersion"** (v21) — printed text ὃ καὶ ὑμᾶς ἀντίτυπον νῦν σῴζει βάπτισμα: the neuter "which" looks back to "water"; ἀντίτυπον "answering to the pattern (τύπος)"; "you", not "us". βάπτισμα is "immersion".
- **"not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God" → "not a putting off of the filth of flesh but the appeal of a good conscience unto *Elohim*"** (v21) — Greek ἀπόθεσις (2 Pet 1:14); ἐπερώτημα, from "to ask": an "appeal, request" or a "pledge"; the genitive "of a good conscience" can be the one who asks or the thing asked for.
- **"who is gone into heaven, and is on the right hand of God" → "who is at the right hand of *Elohim*, having gone into heaven"** (v22) — the Greek order; ἐξουσιῶν "authorities".

### Notes
- **v10-12** quotes Ps 34:12-16.
- **v14–15** takes its words from Isa 8:12–13.
- **v1, 7, 9** — "be subject", "dwell together", "not repaying … blessing" are participles in Greek, used as commands.
