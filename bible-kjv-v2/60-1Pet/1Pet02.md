# 1 Peter 2

[1] Having therefore put away all malice and all guile and hypocrisies and envies and all slanders,

[2] as newborn babes long for the rational, guileless milk, that in it you may grow unto salvation,

[3] if you tasted that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ is kind.

[4] Approaching him, a living stone, rejected indeed by men but with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ elect, held in honor,

[5] you yourselves also, as living stones, are being built up, a spiritual house, for a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices well-acceptable to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[6] Because it is contained in Scripture:

"**Behold, I lay in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ a stone,
a cornerstone, elect, held in honor,
and he who believes on him shall by no means be put to shame**."

[7] To you therefore who believe is the honor; but to those who disbelieve,

"**The stone which the builders rejected,
this became the head of the corner**,"

[8] and

"**A stone of stumbling,
and a rock of offense**";

who stumble at the word, disobeying; unto which they were also appointed.

[9] But **you are an elect race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for possession, that you may declare the virtues of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light**;

[10] who once were not a people, but now are ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ people; who had not been shown mercy, but now have been shown mercy.

[11] Beloved, I exhort you as aliens and sojourners to abstain from the fleshly desires, which war against the soul,

[12] having your conduct among the nations good, so that, in that in which they speak against you as evildoers, they may from your good works, observing them, glorify ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in the day of visitation.

[13] Be subject to every human creation because of the Lord: whether to a king as supreme,

[14] or to governors as sent through him for vengeance on evildoers and praise of those who do good

[15] (because so is the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that doing good you should silence the ignorance of the senseless men);

[16] as free, and not as having your liberty as a covering of malice, but as slaves of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[17] **Honor all. Love the brotherhood. Fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Honor the king.**

[18] Household slaves, be subject in all fear to your masters, not only to the good and gentle but also to the crooked.

[19] For this is grace, if because of conscience toward ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ someone bears sorrows, suffering unjustly.

[20] For what renown is it if, sinning and being beaten, you will endure? But if, doing good and suffering, you will endure, this is grace with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[21] For unto this you were called, because ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ also suffered for you, leaving you a model, that you might follow upon his footsteps;

[22] **who did no sin, nor was guile found in his mouth**;

[23] who, being reviled, did not revile in return; suffering, did not threaten, but kept delivering it to him who judges justly;

[24] **who himself bore our sins in his body up onto the tree, that, having died to sins, we might live to righteousness; by whose wound you were healed**.

[25] For you were being led astray like sheep, but you have now turned back to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v5) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v21) — systematic.
- **"laying aside … guile, and hypocrisies, and envies, and all evil speakings" → "having put away … guile and hypocrisies and envies and all slanders"** (v1) — Greek ἀποθέμενοι, an aorist participle, as Jas 1:21; the nouns are plural as printed. δόλος is "guile" (v22; 3:10); καταλαλιά is the noun of καταλαλέω, "speak against" (v12; 3:16).
- **"the sincere milk of the word" → "the rational, guileless milk"** (v2) — Greek τὸ λογικὸν ἄδολον γάλα. λογικός means "of reason, rational", and so "not material, spiritual"; KJV's "of the word" takes it from λόγος (1:23), which the adjective does not say directly. ἄδολον is δόλος ("guile", v1) with the negative prefix: "unadulterated". ἐπιποθήσατε "long for".
- **"grow thereby" → "grow unto salvation"** (v2) — the printed text has εἰς σωτηρίαν, which KJV's text lacks. ἐν αὐτῷ is "in it" or "by it".
- **"If so be ye have tasted that the Lord is gracious" → "if you tasted that *Yahweh* is kind"** (v3) — printed text εἰ ἐγεύσασθε (aorist), χρηστός "kind, good"; the words of Ps 34:8 (LXX 33:9), where "the Lord" is *Yahweh*; in v4 "him" is the living stone, the *Mashiach*.
- **"disallowed … chosen of God, and precious" → "rejected … with *Elohim* elect, held in honor"** (v4, 6) — Greek προσερχόμενοι "approaching" (KJV "to whom coming"); ἀποδεδοκιμασμένον (rejected after testing; v7 the same verb), παρὰ θεῷ "with, in the judgment of", ἐκλεκτόν "elect" (1:1; 2:9), ἔντιμον "held in honor", from τιμή "honor" (v7); "precious" is τίμιος (1:19).
- **"ye also … are built up a spiritual house, an holy priesthood" → "you yourselves also … are being built up, a spiritual house, for a holy priesthood"** (v5) — printed text εἰς ἱεράτευμα ἅγιον, "for, unto". οἰκοδομεῖσθε can also be an imperative, "be built up". ἀνενέγκαι "offer up" is the verb of v24; εὐπροσδέκτους "well-acceptable" (2 Cor 6:2; 8:12).
- **"it is contained in the scripture" kept** (v6) — Greek περιέχει ἐν γραφῇ.
- **"a chief corner stone, elect, precious" → "a stone, a cornerstone, elect, held in honor"** (v6) — printed text λίθον ἀκρογωνιαῖον ἐκλεκτὸν ἔντιμον. Quotation of Isa 28:16 (LXX). ἐπʼ αὐτῷ is "on him" or "on it"; οὐ μή is the strong negation.
- **"Unto you therefore which believe he is precious" → "To you therefore who believe is the honor"** (v7) — Greek ὑμῖν οὖν ἡ τιμὴ τοῖς πιστεύουσιν: the noun "honor", not an adjective said of the stone.
- **"unto them which be disobedient" → "to those who disbelieve"** (v7) — printed text ἀπιστοῦσιν; "disobeying" (ἀπειθοῦντες) comes in v8.
- **"the same is made the head of the corner" → "this became the head of the corner"** (v7) — Greek οὗτος ἐγενήθη εἰς κεφαλὴν γωνίας; quotation of Ps 118:22 (LXX 117:22).
- **"a stone of stumbling, and a rock of offence" kept** (v8) — Greek λίθος προσκόμματος καὶ πέτρα σκανδάλου (Isa 8:14). πρόσκομμα is "stumbling" (the verb προσκόπτω "stumble" follows); the second word is σκάνδαλον, everywhere else "stumbling block". English has no "rock of stumbling block", so "rock of offense" stands here and in Rom 9:33 only.
- **"which stumble at the word, being disobedient: whereunto also they were appointed" → "who stumble at the word, disobeying; unto which they were also appointed"** (v8) — Greek οἳ προσκόπτουσιν τῷ λόγῳ ἀπειθοῦντες: "the word" can go with "stumble" or with "disobeying" ("who stumble, disobeying the word"). ἐτέθησαν is the verb of "I lay" (τίθημι) in v6.
- **"a chosen generation … a peculiar people" → "an elect race … a people for possession"** (v9) — Greek γένος ἐκλεκτόν; λαὸς εἰς περιποίησιν, "a people for acquiring, for possession" (Exod 19:5; Isa 43:21; Mal 3:17); the Greek has no "his own".
- **"shew forth the praises" → "declare the virtues"** (v9) — Greek ἐξαγγείλητε "tell out"; ἀρετάς, plural of ἀρετή "virtue" (2 Pet 1:3, 5), in the Septuagint also "praiseworthy deeds" (Isa 43:21).
- **"had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy" → "had not been shown mercy, but now have been shown mercy"** (v10) — Greek οἱ οὐκ ἠλεημένοι νῦν δὲ ἐλεηθέντες, passives of ἐλεέω "have mercy"; v9–10 are one sentence.
- **"I beseech you as strangers and pilgrims" → "I exhort you as aliens and sojourners"** (v11) — Greek παρακαλῶ (also 5:1, 12; "you" is supplied, the Greek has no object); πάροικος, a resident without citizen's rights (1:17), παρεπίδημος, one staying for a time (1:1).
- **"fleshly lusts" → "the fleshly desires"** (v11) — Greek τῶν σαρκικῶν ἐπιθυμιῶν.
- **"conversation honest among the Gentiles" → "conduct among the nations good"** (v12) — Greek ἀναστροφή; ἔθνη "nations"; καλήν, καλῶν ἔργων "good".
- **"whereas they speak against you" → "in that in which they speak against you"** (v12) — Greek ἐν ᾧ.
- **"which they shall behold" → "observing them"** (v12) — Greek ἐποπτεύοντες, a present participle with no object ("them" is supplied): to look on as an eyewitness (ἐπόπτης, 2 Pet 1:16); also 3:2.
- **"every ordinance of man for the Lord's sake" → "every human creation because of the Lord"** (v13) — Greek πάσῃ ἀνθρωπίνῃ κτίσει: κτίσις is "creation, creature"; here usually taken as an institution set up among men, or "every human creature". διὰ τὸν κύριον "because of the Lord".
- **"the king" kept** (v13, 17) — Greek βασιλεύς "king", without article in v13; the word was also used of the Roman emperor.
- **"sent by him for the punishment" → "sent through him for vengeance"** (v14) — Greek διʼ αὐτοῦ "through him"; ἐκδίκησις "vengeance", as 2 Thess 1:8.
- **"put to silence" → "silence"** (v15) — Greek φιμοῦν, literally "to muzzle"; ἀφρόνων "senseless". The round brackets are those of the printed text.
- **"a cloke of maliciousness … servants of God" → "a covering of malice … slaves of *Elohim*"** (v16) — Greek κακία, the word of v1; δοῦλοι; ἐλευθερία "liberty" (Jas 1:25).
- **"Servants" → "Household slaves"** (v18) — Greek οἰκέται, the slaves of a house (οἶκος); a different word from δοῦλος (v16). "Be subject" renders a participle (ὑποτασσόμενοι), as in 3:1.
- **"the froward" → "the crooked"** (v18) — Greek σκολιοῖς, as Phil 2:15.
- **"this is thankworthy" / "this is acceptable with God" → "this is grace" / "this is grace with *Elohim*"** (v19, 20) — Greek χάρις, the word "grace, favor"; παρὰ θεῷ as in v4.
- **"for conscience toward God" kept** (v19) — Greek διὰ συνείδησιν θεοῦ, a genitive: "conscience of *Elohim*", that is, conscience toward him or consciousness of him.
- **"what glory" → "what renown"** (v20) — Greek κλέος, not δόξα.
- **"buffeted … take it patiently" → "being beaten, you will endure"** (v20) — Greek κολαφιζόμενοι (struck with the fist), ὑπομενεῖτε (future, twice). The Greek has no "for it".
- **"leaving us an example" → "leaving you a model"** (v21) — printed text ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν, ὑμῖν ("for you … you"); ὑπογραμμός is the line of writing a pupil copies; "example" is ὑπόδειγμα (2 Pet 2:6), "pattern" τύπος (5:3).
- **"Who did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth" → "who did no sin, nor was guile found in his mouth"** (v22) — quotation of Isa 53:9; δόλος "guile" (v1). The relative "who" (ὅς, v22, 23, 24) is kept.
- **"committed himself" → "kept delivering it"** (v23) — Greek παρεδίδου (παραδίδωμι "deliver"), imperfect, with no object: "it" is supplied; what he delivered (himself, his cause, those who reviled him) is not said.
- **"righteously" → "justly"** (v23) — Greek δικαίως, the opposite of ἀδίκως "unjustly" (v19); the same root as "righteousness" (v24).
- **"bare our sins in his own body on the tree" → "bore our sins in his body up onto the tree"** (v24) — Greek ἀνήνεγκεν, the verb "offer up" of v5, "bear" of sins as Heb 9:28 (Isa 53:12 LXX); ἐπὶ τὸ ξύλον with the accusative, "up onto"; ξύλον is "wood, tree" (Deut 21:22–23).
- **"being dead to sins" → "having died to sins"** (v24) — Greek ταῖς ἁμαρτίαις ἀπογενόμενοι, literally "having come to be away from the sins"; the verb is also used for dying.
- **"by whose stripes ye were healed" → "by whose wound you were healed"** (v24) — Greek τῷ μώλωπι, singular: a weal, the mark of a blow (Isa 53:5 LXX).
- **"as sheep going astray" → "being led astray like sheep"** (v25) — printed text πλανώμενοι, agreeing with "you"; πλανάω as 2 Pet 2:15.
- **"are now returned" → "have now turned back"** (v25) — Greek ἐπεστράφητε, aorist passive.
- **"Bishop of your souls" → "Overseer of your souls"** (v25) — Greek ἐπίσκοπον, "overseer"; the noun of ἐπισκοπή "visitation" (v12).

### Notes
- **v6** quotes Isa 28:16.
- **v7** quotes Ps 118:22.
- **v8** quotes Isa 8:14.
- **v9** echoes Ex 19:5-6; Isa 43:20-21.
- **v10** echoes Hos 2:23.
- **v22** quotes Isa 53:9.
- **v24** echoes Isa 53:4-5, 12.
- **v1–3, v11–12, v13–16** — each is one sentence in Greek; in v16 "as free … as slaves" depends on "be subject" (v13).
