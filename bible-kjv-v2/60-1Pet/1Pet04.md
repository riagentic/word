# 1 Peter 4

[1] Since therefore the *Mashiach* suffered in the flesh, arm yourselves with the same way of thinking, for whoever has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin,

[2] so as to live for the rest of the time in the flesh no longer for human passions but for the will of *Elohim*.

[3] For the time that is past suffices for doing what the Gentiles want to do, living in sensuality, passions, drunkenness, orgies, drinking parties, and lawless idolatry.

[4] With respect to this they are surprised when you do not join them in the same flood of debauchery, and they malign you;

[5] but they will give account to him who is ready to judge the living and the dead.

[6] For this is why the gospel was preached even to those who are dead, that though judged in the flesh the way people are, they might live in the spirit the way *Elohim* does.

[7] The end of all things is at hand; therefore be self-controlled and sober-minded for the sake of your prayers.

[8] Above all, **keep loving one another earnestly, since love covers a multitude of sins**.

[9] Show hospitality to one another without grumbling.

[10] As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of *Elohim*'s varied grace:

[11] whoever speaks, as one who speaks oracles of *Elohim*; whoever serves, as one who serves by the strength that *Elohim* supplies — in order that in everything *Elohim* may be glorified through *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*. To him belong glory and dominion forever and ever. Amen.

[12] Beloved, do not be surprised at the fiery trial when it comes upon you to test you, as though something strange were happening to you.

[13] But rejoice insofar as you share the *Mashiach*'s sufferings, that you may also rejoice and be glad when his glory is revealed.

[14] If you are insulted for the name of the *Mashiach*, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory and of *Elohim* rests upon you.

[15] But let none of you suffer as a murderer or a thief or an evildoer or as a meddler.

[16] Yet if anyone suffers as a Christian, let him not be ashamed, but let him glorify *Elohim* in that name.

[17] For it is time for judgment to begin at the household of *Elohim*; and if it begins with us, what will be the outcome for those who do not obey the gospel of *Elohim*?

[18] And

"**If the righteous is scarcely saved,
what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?**"

[19] Therefore let those who suffer according to *Elohim*'s will entrust their souls to a faithful Creator while doing good.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v1, 13, 14) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v11) — systematic.
- **"lasciviousness, lusts, excess of wine, revellings, banquetings, and abominable idolatries" → "sensuality, passions, drunkenness, orgies, drinking parties, and lawless idolatry"** (v3) — modernized list; "lasciviousness" → "sensuality"; "revellings" → "orgies"; "banquetings" → "drinking parties"; "abominable" dropped, "lawless" substituted for clarification.
- **"the same excess of riot" → "the same flood of debauchery"** (v4) — Greek τῆς ἀσωτίας ἀνάχυσιν *tēs asōtias anchysin* = "the pouring out / overflow of debauchery"; "flood of debauchery" is a strong, accurate image.
- **"If the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?" → "If the righteous is scarcely saved, what will become of the ungodly and the sinner?"** (v18) — OT quotation (Prov 11:31 LXX); "appear" → "become of"; modernized.
- **"charity shall cover the multitude of sins" → "love covers a multitude of sins"** (v8) — Greek ἀγάπη *agapē*; "charity" in KJV = "love" (the 1611 meaning); modernized to "love"; echoes Prov 10:12.

### Notes
- **v18** quotes Prov 11:31 (LXX).
