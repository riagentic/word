# 2 Kings 2

[1] When Yahweh was about to take Elijah up into heaven by a whirlwind, Elijah went with Elisha from Gilgal.

[2] Elijah said to Elisha, "Stay here, please; for Yahweh has sent me to Bethel." Elisha said, "As Yahweh lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So they went down to Bethel.

[3] The sons of the prophets who were at Bethel came out to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that Yahweh will take away your master from over your head today?" He said, "Yes, I know it; keep silent."

[4] Elijah said to him, "Elisha, stay here, please; for Yahweh has sent me to Jericho." He said, "As Yahweh lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So they came to Jericho.

[5] The sons of the prophets who were at Jericho came near to Elisha and said to him, "Do you know that Yahweh will take away your master from over your head today?" He answered, "Yes, I know it; keep silent."

[6] Elijah said to him, "Stay here, please; for Yahweh has sent me to the Jordan." He said, "As Yahweh lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So the two of them went on.

[7] Fifty men of the sons of the prophets also went and stood opposite them at a distance; the two of them stood by the Jordan.

[8] Elijah took his mantle, rolled it up, and struck the waters; they were divided to one side and the other, so that the two of them crossed over on dry ground.

[9] When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask what I shall do for you before I am taken from you." Elisha said, "Please let a double portion of your spirit be upon me."

[10] He said, "You have asked a hard thing. Yet if you see me when I am taken from you, it shall be so for you; but if not, it shall not be so."

[11] As they still went on and talked, behold, a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated the two of them; Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven.

[12] Elisha saw it and cried out, "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!" He saw him no more. He took hold of his own clothes and tore them in two pieces.

[13] He also took up the mantle of Elijah that had fallen from him, and went back, and stood on the bank of the Jordan.

[14] He took the mantle of Elijah that had fallen from him and struck the waters, saying, "Where is Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Elijah?" When he also had struck the waters, they were divided to one side and the other; and Elisha crossed over.

[15] When the sons of the prophets who were at Jericho saw him from a distance, they said, "The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha." They came to meet him and bowed themselves to the ground before him.

[16] They said to him, "Behold, there are with your servants fifty strong men; let them go, please, and seek your master — perhaps the spirit of Yahweh has taken him up and cast him upon some mountain or into some valley." He said, "You shall not send."

[17] But when they urged him until he was ashamed, he said, "Send." So they sent fifty men; they sought three days but did not find him.

[18] They came back to him while he was staying at Jericho; he said to them, "Did I not say to you, 'Do not go'?"

[19] The men of the city said to Elisha, "Behold, the situation of this city is pleasant, as my lord sees, but the water is bad and the land causes miscarriage."

[20] He said, "Bring me a new bowl, and put salt in it." They brought it to him.

[21] He went out to the spring of the waters, cast the salt in there, and said, "Thus says Yahweh: 'I have healed these waters; there shall not come from them any more death or miscarriage.'"

[22] So the waters were healed to this day, according to the word of Elisha that he spoke.

[23] He went up from there to Bethel. As he was going up on the way, young men came out of the city and jeered at him, saying to him, "Go up, baldy! Go up, baldy!"

[24] He turned around, and when he saw them, he cursed them in the name of Yahweh. Two she-bears came out of the forest and mauled forty-two of the young men.

[25] From there he went to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"barren" → "causes miscarriage"** (v19, 21) — Hebrew *meshakkelet*; specifically "making to miscarry / bereaving," not simply infertile.
- **"cruse" → "bowl"** (v20) — *tzelochit*.
- **"little children" → "young men"** (v23, 24) — Hebrew *ne'arim qetannim*; *na'ar* ranges widely, can denote teens/young men up to ~30. "Small lads" not nursery children. Context (taunting a prophet, forty-two mauled) = adolescents or young men.
- **"tare" → "mauled"** (v24) — *baqa*.
- **"chariot of Israel and the horsemen thereof"** — retained; Elisha's cry is titular, naming Elijah as Israel's true defense force.

### Notes
- **Elijah's translation** — second person in Scripture taken without death (Enoch, Gen 5:24). Becomes basis for expectation of Elijah's return (Mal 4:5, NT).
- **Gilgal → Bethel → Jericho → Jordan** — reverse-conquest itinerary; Elijah retraces Joshua's entry in reverse, exiting through the Jordan.
- **Dividing Jordan** — Elijah crosses like Moses at the sea, Joshua at the Jordan. Succession of covenant-mediators.
- **Double portion** — inheritance law (Deut 21:17): firstborn's share. Elisha asks to inherit as Elijah's firstborn prophet-son.
- **Chariots of Israel** (v12) — repeated at Elisha's death (2 Kgs 13:14) by Joash. The prophet as true military power of Israel.
- **Healing of Jericho's waters** — reverses Jericho's curse (Josh 6:26, 1 Kgs 16:34). Elisha inaugurates ministry with grace.
- **Bears at Bethel** — at site of Jeroboam's golden calf, youth of the apostate city mock the prophet. The curse / judgment frames Bethel's rebellion against Yahweh's representatives.
- **"Go up"** (v23) — likely mocking Elijah's ascension: "go up [like your master did]." Not mere bald-joke.
