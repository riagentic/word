# 2 Kings 5

[1] Naaman, commander of the army of the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, was a great man with his master and held in honor, because by him ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had given victory to ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧; he was also a mighty man of valor, but he was afflicted with skin disease.

[2] The ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ had gone out in raiding bands and had brought back as a captive from the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a young girl; she waited on Naaman's wife.

[3] She said to her mistress, "If only my lord were before the prophet who is in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧! Then he would cure him of his skin disease."

[4] He went in and told his lord, saying, "Thus and thus said the girl who is from the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[5] The king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ said, "Go now, and I will send a letter to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧." So he departed, taking with him ten talents of silver, six thousand shekels of gold, and ten changes of clothing.

[6] He brought the letter to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which read: "Now when this letter comes to you, behold, I have sent Naaman my servant to you, that you may cure him of his skin disease."

[7] When the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ read the letter, he tore his clothes and said, "Am I ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to kill and to make alive, that this man sends to me to cure a man of his skin disease? Only consider and see how he is seeking a quarrel with me."

[8] When Elisha the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ heard that the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had torn his clothes, he sent to the king, saying, "Why have you torn your clothes? Let him come now to me, and he shall know that there is a prophet in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[9] So Naaman came with his horses and his chariots, and stood at the door of Elisha's house.

[10] Elisha sent a messenger to him, saying, "Go and wash in the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧ seven times, and your flesh shall be restored to you, and you shall be clean."

[11] But Naaman became angry and went away, saying, "Behold, I thought, 'He will surely come out to me, and stand, and call on the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and wave his hand over the place, and cure the skin disease.'

[12] Are not ⟦abana|Avanah|Abana⟧ and ⟦pharpar|Parpar|Pharpar⟧, the rivers of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, better than all the waters of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧? Could I not wash in them and be clean?" So he turned and went away in a rage.

[13] His servants came near and spoke to him, saying, "My father, if the prophet had told you to do some great thing, would you not have done it? How much more, then, when he says to you, 'Wash, and be clean'?"

[14] So he went down and dipped himself seven times in the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, according to the word of the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; his flesh was restored like the flesh of a little child, and he was clean.

[15] He returned to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, he and all his company; he came and stood before him, and said, "Behold, now I know that there is no ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in all the earth, except in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; now therefore, please take a berakah — a gift — from your servant."

[16] He said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, before whom I stand, I will receive none." He urged him to take it, but he refused.

[17] Naaman said, "If not, then please let there be given to your servant two mules' load of earth; for your servant will no longer offer burnt offering or sacrifice to other gods, but only to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] In this thing may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ pardon your servant: when my master goes into the house of Rimmon to worship there, and he leans on my hand, and I bow down in the house of Rimmon — when I bow down in the house of Rimmon, may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ pardon your servant in this thing."

[19] He said to him, "Go in peace." He had gone from him a short distance

[20] when ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧, the servant of Elisha the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, said, "Behold, my master has spared this Naaman the ⟦aramean|Arammi|Syrian⟧ by not receiving from his hand what he brought. As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives, I will run after him and take something from him."

[21] So ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧ followed after Naaman. When Naaman saw someone running after him, he got down from the chariot to meet him and said, "Is all well?"

[22] He said, "All is well. My master has sent me to say, 'Behold, just now two young men of the sons of the prophets have come to me from the hill country of ⟦ephraim|Efrayim|Ephraim⟧; please give them a talent of silver and two changes of clothing.'"

[23] Naaman said, "Be willing to take two talents." He urged him, and bound two talents of silver in two bags with two changes of clothing, and laid them upon two of his servants; they carried them before him.

[24] When he came to the citadel, he took them from their hand and stored them in the house; he let the men go, and they departed.

[25] He came in and stood before his master. Elisha said to him, "Where have you been, ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧?" He said, "Your servant has not gone anywhere."

[26] He said to him, "Did not my heart go with you, when the man turned from his chariot to meet you? Is this a time to receive silver and to receive clothing, olive groves and vineyards, sheep and oxen, male and female servants?

[27] Therefore the skin disease of Naaman shall cling to you and to your offspring forever." He went out from his presence metzora — struck with skin disease — white as snow.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"captain" → "commander"** (v1) — *sar*.
- **"Syria / Syrians / Syrian" → "Aram / Arameans / Aramean"** (throughout).
- **"six thousand pieces of gold" → "six thousand shekels of gold"** (v5) — *shishshah alafim zahav*; unit likely shekel.
- **"strike his hand" → "wave his hand"** (v11) — *heniph yado*; motion of blessing/consecration.
- **"tower" → "citadel"** (v24) — *ophel*; acropolis/mound.
- **"money" → "silver"** (v26) — *kesef*.

### Notes
- **Naaman** — Aramean general, commander under Ben-hadad. Arameans Israel's chronic enemy, yet Yahweh's instrument even for them (v1).
- **Little Israelite maid** — captive girl becomes evangelist to her Gentile master. Small witness with massive consequence.
- **Rimmon** — Aramean storm-god (Hadad-Ramman); parallels Baal-Hadad.
- **Seven-fold washing** — ceremonial number; not hygiene but obedience-test. Naaman's pride almost derails him.
- **Naaman's conversion** — confesses Yahweh exclusivity (v17). Jesus cites this as Gentile inclusion precedent (Luke 4:27).
- **Two mules' load of earth** — wants Israelite soil for an altar in Damascus — physical anchor of Yahweh-worship in foreign land.
- **Gehazi's fall** — private greed, public lie. Inherits Naaman's disease as exact moral reversal: Gentile becomes clean, servant becomes *metzora*.
- **"White as snow"** — ceremonial-white is the diagnostic, the outward sign (Lev 13). Gehazi becomes visually unclean — cut off from cult and community.
