# 2 Kings 12

[1] In the seventh year of ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ began to reign; he reigned forty years in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. His mother's name was ⟦zibiah|Tzivyah|Zibiah⟧ of ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧.

[2] ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ all his days during which ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest instructed him.

[3] However, the high places were not taken away; the people still sacrificed and burned incense on the high places.

[4] ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ said to the priests, "All the silver from the sacred donations that is brought into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ — the silver from the census, the silver for each person's assessed valuation, and all the silver that anyone is moved in his heart to bring into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ —

[5] let the priests take it for themselves, each from his acquaintance; let them repair the damage of the house, wherever any damage is found."

[6] But by the twenty-third year of King ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧, the priests had not repaired the damage of the house.

[7] Then King ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ called for ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest and for the other priests, and said to them, "Why are you not repairing the damage of the house? Now therefore take no more silver from your acquaintances, but hand it over for the damage of the house."

[8] The priests consented to receive no more silver from the people and not to repair the damage of the house themselves.

[9] ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest took a chest, bored a hole in its lid, and set it beside the altar, on the right side as one comes into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; the priests who guarded the threshold put into it all the silver that was brought into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] When they saw that there was much silver in the chest, the king's scribe and the high priest came up, they tied it in bags, and counted the silver that was found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] They gave the silver that had been weighed out into the hands of those doing the work, who had oversight of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; they paid it out to the carpenters and builders who worked on the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[12] and to the masons and stonecutters, and for buying timber and hewn stone to repair the damage of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and for everything that was spent on the house for its repair.

[13] But there were not made for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ basins of silver, snuffers, bowls, ⟦shofar|shofarot|trumpets⟧, or any vessels of gold or vessels of silver, from the silver that was brought into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;

[14] for they gave that to the workmen, and with it they repaired the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] Moreover, they did not require an accounting from the men into whose hand they delivered the silver to pay out to the workmen, for they dealt faithfully.

[16] The silver from guilt offerings and from sin offerings was not brought into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it belonged to the priests.

[17] Then ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ went up and fought against ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ and captured it. ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ set his face to go up against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[19] The rest of the acts of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧, and all that he did — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?

[20] His servants arose and made a conspiracy; they struck down ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ at ⟦beth-millo|Beit Millo|Beth-millo⟧, on the way down to Silla.

[21] ⟦jozacar|Yozachar|Jozacar⟧ son of ⟦shimeath|Shimat|Shimeath⟧ and ⟦jehozabad|Yehozavad|Jehozabad⟧ son of Shomer his servants struck him down, and he died. They buried him with his fathers in the city of David; ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"money" → "silver"** (throughout, v4, 7-11, 13-15) — *kesef*.
- **"passeth the account... man is set at" → "silver from the census... for each person's assessed valuation"** (v4) — Hebrew *kesef ovēr* = census-tax (Ex 30:13); *kesef nephashot erkô* = valuation of persons (Lev 27).
- **"breaches" → "damage"** (v5, 6, 7, 12) — *bedeq*; structural fractures.
- **"bowls" → "basins"** (v13) — *sippot*.
- **"basons" → "bowls"** (v13) — *mizraqot*; sacrificial dashing-bowls.
- **"hallowed things" → "sacred things"** (v18) — *qodashim*.
- **"house of Millo" → "Beth-millo"** (v20) — location name.

### Notes
- **Jehoash = Joash** — same king, two name-forms.
- **Under Jehoiada's tutelage** — king does right only while the priest lives. After Jehoiada's death (2 Chr 24:17-25), Joash turns and kills Zechariah son of Jehoiada. Kings focuses on his early years.
- **Temple repair** — first major renovation program since Solomon. Reforms funding-mechanism: priests collected but didn't repair; king forces transparent chest-collection.
- **Chest system** — public chest, sealed accounting. Model for NT offering-boxes (Mark 12:41).
- **"Dealt faithfully"** — rare praise for administrative honesty.
- **Hazael's extortion** — temple stripped a second time (after Shishak in 1 Kgs 14). Financial pragmatism, theological loss.
- **Joash's assassination** — karmic reversal after his murder of Zechariah (2 Chr 24). Servants avenge a prophet's blood. Kings omits the reason; Chronicles supplies it.
