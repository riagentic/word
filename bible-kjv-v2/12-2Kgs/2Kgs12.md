# 2 Kings 12

[1] In the seventh year of ⟦jehu|Yehu|Jehu⟧, ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ began to reign, and forty years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦zibiah|Tzivyah|Zibiah⟧ of ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧.

[2] And ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ did what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ all his days, because ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest taught him.

[3] Only, the high places did not depart; the people were still sacrificing and making smoke on the high places.

[4] And ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ said to the priests, "All the silver of the holy things that is brought to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the silver that passes over, each man the silver of the souls of his valuation, all the silver that comes up upon the heart of a man to bring to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[5] let the priests take for themselves, each man from his acquaintance, and they shall repair the breach of the house, wherever a breach is found there."

[6] And it came to pass, in the twenty-third year of King ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧, that the priests had not repaired the breach of the house.

[7] And King ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ called ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest and the priests and said to them, "Why are you not repairing the breach of the house? And now, do not take silver from your acquaintances, for you shall give it for the breach of the house."

[8] And the priests consented not to take silver from the people and not to repair the breach of the house.

[9] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest took a chest and bored a hole in its door and placed it beside the altar, on the right as a man comes into the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and the priests, the keepers of the threshold, placed there all the silver that was brought to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] And it came to pass, when they saw that the silver in the chest was much, that the scribe of the king and the great priest came up, and they bound up and counted the silver that was found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] And they gave the measured silver into the hands of the doers of the work who were appointed over the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they brought it out to the craftsmen in wood and to the builders who were working on the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[12] and to the wall-builders and to the hewers of stone, and to buy wood and hewn stones to repair the breach of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and for all that went out upon the house to repair it.

[13] Only, there were not made for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ basins of silver, snuffers, bowls, trumpets, any vessel of gold or vessel of silver, from the silver that was brought to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[14] for they gave it to the doers of the work, and they repaired with it the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] And they did not reckon with the men into whose hand they gave the silver to give to the doers of the work, for they were acting in faithfulness.

[16] The silver of a guilt offering and the silver of sin offerings was not brought to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; they were for the priests.

[17] Then ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ went up and fought against ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ and captured it, and ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ set his face to go up against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[18] And ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ took all the holy things that ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ and ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ and ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧, his fathers, the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, had sanctified, and his own holy things, and all the gold that was found in the treasuries of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and of the house of the king, and he sent them to ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, and he went up from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[19] And the rest of the acts of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?

[20] And his servants arose and conspired a conspiracy and struck down ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ at ⟦beth-millo|Beit Millo|Beth-millo⟧, which goes down to Silla.

[21] And ⟦jozabad|Yozavad|Jozabad⟧ the son of ⟦shimeath|Shimat|Shimeath⟧ and ⟦jehozabad|Yehozavad|Jehozabad⟧ the son of Shomer, his servants, struck him down, and he died, and they buried him with his fathers in the city of David, and ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"began to reign"** (v1) — Hebrew מָלַךְ "reigned", here of the beginning; with the number of years it is "reigned". **"his mother's name was" → "the name of his mother was"** — Hebrew וְשֵׁם אִמּוֹ.
- **"wherein Jehoiada the priest instructed him" → "because Jehoiada the priest taught him"** (v2) — Hebrew כָּל־יָמָיו אֲשֶׁר הוֹרָהוּ יְהוֹיָדָע הַכֹּהֵן. The accents divide after "all his days", and אֲשֶׁר then gives the ground: "because (in that) Jehoiada the priest taught him". The words can also be read as one phrase, "all his days in which Jehoiada the priest taught him", which limits the praise to the priest's lifetime (so 2 Chr 24:2: "all the days of Jehoiada the priest"). הוֹרָה "teach" is the root of *torah*.
- **"But the high places were not taken away … sacrificed and burnt incense" → "Only, the high places did not depart … sacrificing and making smoke"** (v3) — Hebrew לֹא־סָרוּ "did not depart" (active); מְקַטְּרִים, piel of קטר with no object: "making smoke". Whether incense or the smoke of sacrifices is meant is not said.
- **"money" → "silver"** (v4–16) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף "silver"; it is weighed out, not coined. One word is kept through the passage.
- **"the money of the dedicated things" → "the silver of the holy things"** (v4) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף הַקֳּדָשִׁים.
- **"the money of every one that passeth the account, the money that every man is set at" → "the silver that passes over, each man the silver of the souls of his valuation"** (v4) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף עוֹבֵר אִישׁ כֶּסֶף נַפְשׁוֹת עֶרְכּוֹ, word for word "silver passing over, a man, silver of souls of his valuation". The words are short and their sense is disputed. כֶּסֶף עוֹבֵר is either "current silver" (Gen 23:16 "current with the trader") or the silver of each one who "passes over" to those numbered (Exod 30:13: the half shekel). "The silver of the souls of his valuation" is the sum set on persons vowed to Yahweh (Lev 27:2: "by your valuation of souls"). Uncertain.
- **"that cometh into any man's heart" → "that comes up upon the heart of a man"** (v4) — Hebrew יַעֲלֶה עַל לֶב־אִישׁ.
- **"every man of his acquaintance" → "each man from his acquaintance"** (v5, 7) — Hebrew מַכָּר, found only in these two verses. "Acquaintance" (from הִכִּיר "recognize, know") is the old understanding; others derive it from מָכַר "sell" (a dealer, or one who assesses for the temple). Uncertain.
- **"repair the breaches of the house" → "repair the breach of the house"** (v5–8, 12) — Hebrew חִזֵּק אֶת־בֶּדֶק הַבַּיִת. The verb is the piel of חָזַק "be strong", literally "make strong"; of a building it is "repair" ("make strong the breach" would say the opposite). בֶּדֶק (only here, in 22:5 and in Ezek 27:9, 27) is a rent or leak in a building; it is singular throughout. The verb "make a breach" in 14:13 is another word (פָּרַץ).
- **"it was so, that in the three and twentieth year"** (v6) — written "in the twenty-third year"; the Hebrew repeats "year".
- **"receive no more money of your acquaintance, but deliver it for the breaches of the house" → "do not take silver from your acquaintances, for you shall give it for the breach of the house"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; no "more" in the Hebrew.
- **"a chest … in the lid of it" → "a chest … in its door"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲרוֹן אֶחָד "one chest": the word used of the ark; דַּלְתּוֹ "its door": the lid, or a flap in it. **"set it" → "placed it"**, **"put therein" → "placed there"** — one verb, נָתַן (literally "give"). **"on the right side"** — the qere מִיָּמִין is translated; the ketiv בימין is "at the right". **"the priests that kept the door" → "the priests, the keepers of the threshold"** — Hebrew שֹׁמְרֵי הַסַּף.
- **"the high priest" → "the great priest"** (v10) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל. **"they put up in bags, and told the money" → "they bound up and counted the silver"** — Hebrew וַיָּצֻרוּ וַיִּמְנוּ; צוּר "bind up" (Deut 14:25); no "bags" in the Hebrew.
- **"the money, being told" → "the measured silver"** (v11) — Hebrew הַמְתֻכָּן, from תכן "measure, set the amount". **"them that did the work, that had the oversight of the house" → "the doers of the work who were appointed over the house"** (v11; "the doers of the work" also v14, 15) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂי הַמְּלָאכָה; qere הַמֻּפְקָדִים (hofal participle of פָּקַד "appoint"); the ketiv הפקדים is "the appointed / the overseers". The qere יְדֵי "hands" is translated; the ketiv is singular. **"they laid it out" → "they brought it out"** (v11), **"all that was laid out" → "all that went out"** (v12) — Hebrew הוֹצִיא / יָצָא: the silver "goes out", that is, is paid out.
- **"carpenters" → "craftsmen in wood"** (v11) — Hebrew חָרָשֵׁי הָעֵץ. **"masons, and hewers of stone" → "the wall-builders and the hewers of stone"** (v12) — Hebrew גֹּדְרִים, those who build walls (גָּדֵר "wall, fence"); חֹצְבֵי הָאֶבֶן "hewers of the stone"; אַבְנֵי מַחְצֵב "stones of hewing".
- **"bowls of silver, snuffers, basons, trumpets" → "basins of silver, snuffers, bowls, trumpets"** (v13) — Hebrew סִפּוֹת · מְזַמְּרוֹת · מִזְרָקוֹת (the bowls for dashing blood) · חֲצֹצְרוֹת (the straight metal trumpets, not the shofar). The verb is an imperfect: "were not (being) made".
- **"they reckoned not with the men"** (v15) — Hebrew וְלֹא יְחַשְּׁבוּ אֶת־הָאֲנָשִׁים. **"for they dealt faithfully" → "for they were acting in faithfulness"** — Hebrew בֶאֱמֻנָה הֵם עֹשִׂים.
- **"The trespass money and sin money" → "The silver of a guilt offering and the silver of sin offerings"** (v16) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף אָשָׁם וְכֶסֶף חַטָּאוֹת: אָשָׁם "guilt" (singular), חַטָּאוֹת "sins" (plural), the words of the two offerings. **"it was the priests'" → "they were for the priests"** — Hebrew לַכֹּהֲנִים יִהְיוּ, plural.
- **"took it" → "captured it"** (v17) — Hebrew לָכַד.
- **"the hallowed things … had dedicated … his own hallowed things" → "the holy things … had sanctified … his own holy things"** (v18) — Hebrew הַקֳּדָשִׁים אֲשֶׁר־הִקְדִּישׁוּ: noun and verb of one root. **"he went away from Jerusalem" → "he went up from Jerusalem"** — Hebrew וַיַּעַל מֵעַל, literally "went up from upon": he withdrew.
- **"the rest of the acts … the book of the chronicles"** (v19) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי twice: "the words / matters of Joash … upon the book of the matters of the days".
- **"made a conspiracy, and slew Joash in the house of Millo, which goeth down to Silla" → "conspired a conspiracy and struck down Joash at Beth-millo, which goes down to Silla"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיִּקְשְׁרוּ־קָשֶׁר, verb and noun of one root; הִכָּה "strike", here of killing. בֵּית מִלֹּא is "the house of Millo" ("the filling": Judg 9:6; 2 Sam 5:9). הַיּוֹרֵד סִלָּא "that goes down (to) Silla": Silla is not known, and the clause is obscure.
- **"Jozachar" → "Jozabad"** (v21) — the printed Hebrew text (Leningrad Codex) has יוֹזָבָד "Jozabad", beside יְהוֹזָבָד "Jehozabad"; many manuscripts and printed editions have יוֹזָכָר "Jozacar" (from memory). 2 Chr 24:26 has "Zabad the son of Shimeath the Ammonite woman, and Jehozabad the son of Shimrith the Moabite woman".

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew counts 11:21 as 12:1, so each verse of this chapter is one higher in the Hebrew (our v1 = Heb v2 … our v21 = Heb v22).
- **Jehoash = Joash** — same king, two name-forms; the Hebrew has יְהוֹאָשׁ in v1–18 and יוֹאָשׁ in v19–20.
- **Under Jehoiada's tutelage** — after Jehoiada's death (2 Chr 24:17-25), Joash turns and kills Zechariah son of Jehoiada. Kings focuses on his early years.
- **Temple repair** — first major renovation program since Solomon. Reforms funding-mechanism: priests collected but didn't repair; king forces transparent chest-collection.
- **Chest system** — public chest, counted by the king's scribe and the great priest together. Compare the offering-boxes of Mark 12:41.
- **"Acting in faithfulness"** — rare praise for administrative honesty.
- **Hazael's extortion** — temple stripped a second time (after Shishak in 1 Kgs 14). Financial pragmatism, theological loss.
- **Joash's assassination** — Kings gives no reason; 2 Chr 24:25 names "the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest".
