## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v19) — Hebrew אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים "the man of the Elohim", with the article.
- **"Syria / Syrians" → "Aram"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲרָם, the name of the people and the land; the Hebrew has "Aram" also where KJV has "the Syrians" (v5, 17, 19).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"began … to reign … and reigned seventeen years" → "began to reign … seventeen years"** (v1, 10) — one verb in the Hebrew, מָלַךְ "reigned", for the accession and the length. The Hebrew repeats "year" with the number ("in the year of twenty and three year").
- **"he did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD" → "he did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v2, 11) — Hebrew הָרַע, with the article; בְּעֵינֵי "in the eyes of".
- **"followed the sins of Jeroboam … which made Israel to sin; he departed not therefrom" → "went after the sins of Jeroboam …, with which he caused Israel to sin; he did not turn aside from it"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ אַחַר "went after"; הֶחֱטִיא hifil "caused to sin"; לֹא־סָר מִמֶּנָּה: the pronoun is singular ("from it"), though "sins" is plural.
- **"the anger of the LORD was kindled" → "the anger of Yahweh burned"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּחַר־אַף; אַף is literally "nose". **"delivered them" → "gave them"** — Hebrew נָתַן. **"all their days" → "all the days"** — Hebrew כָּל־הַיָּמִים; no "their".
- **"besought the LORD" → "besought the face of Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְחַל … אֶת־פְּנֵי יְהוָה, literally "made the face of Yahweh soft". **"hearkened unto him" → "listened to him"** — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל.
- **"a saviour"** (v5) — Hebrew מוֹשִׁיעַ, of the root "save"; who it was is not said. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי. **"as beforetime" → "as yesterday and the day before"** — Hebrew כִּתְמוֹל שִׁלְשׁוֹם, literally "as yesterday, three days ago". No brackets stand in the Hebrew around v5–6.
- **"Nevertheless they departed not … but walked therein" → "Only, they did not turn aside … in it he walked"** (v6) — Hebrew בָּהּ הָלָךְ is singular, "in it he walked" (the same words in v11): the subject is not named (Israel as one, or the king). The qere הֶחֱטִיא "he caused to sin" is translated; the ketiv החטי lacks the last letter. **"there remained the grove also" → "also the Asherah stood"** — Hebrew הָאֲשֵׁרָה עָמְדָה: the wooden object of worship that bears the goddess's name.
- **"Neither did he leave of the people to Jehoahaz but" → "For he did not leave to Jehoahaz a people, except"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; עָם "a people", here of fighting men; the subject "he" is not named (Yahweh, or the king of Aram). **"footmen" → "men on foot"** — Hebrew רַגְלִי. **"had destroyed them, and had made them like the dust by threshing" → "had done away with them and made them like the dust for threshing"** — Hebrew אִבְּדָם (piel of אָבַד "perish"); כֶּעָפָר לָדֻשׁ, literally "like the dust, to thresh / trample".
- **"the rest of the acts … the book of the chronicles"** (v8, 12) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי twice: "the words / matters of Jehoahaz … upon the book of the matters of the days". גְּבוּרָתוֹ "his might".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v9, 13) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכַּב "lay down".
- **"his might wherewith he fought against Amaziah" → "his might, how he fought with Amaziah"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר נִלְחַם עִם: "that / how he fought with".
- **"was fallen sick of his sickness whereof he died" → "had become sick with his sickness of which he would die"** (v14) — Hebrew חָלָה אֶת־חָלְיוֹ אֲשֶׁר יָמוּת בּוֹ: an imperfect, "in which he was to die". **"wept over his face" → "wept upon his face"** — Hebrew עַל־פָּנָיו. **"the chariot of Israel, and the horsemen thereof" → "The chariots of Israel and its horsemen"** — Hebrew רֶכֶב, a collective; the words of Elisha over Elijah (2:12).
- **"Put thine hand upon the bow" → "Set your hand upon the bow"** (v16) — Hebrew הַרְכֵּב יָדְךָ, literally "make your hand ride upon the bow": lay the hand to it to draw it.
- **"eastward"** (v17) — Hebrew קֵדְמָה, literally "to the front". **"The arrow of the LORD'S deliverance, and the arrow of deliverance from Syria" → "An arrow of salvation for Yahweh, and an arrow of salvation against Aram"** — Hebrew חֵץ־תְּשׁוּעָה לַיהוָה וְחֵץ תְּשׁוּעָה בַאֲרָם: no article; תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation"; לַיהוָה "for / belonging to Yahweh"; בַאֲרָם "against (in) Aram". **"smite … till thou have consumed them" → "strike … until making an end"** (v17, 19) — Hebrew עַד־כַּלֵּה, an infinitive with no object. One verb, הִכָּה "strike", stands for striking the ground and striking Aram (v17, 18, 19, 25).
- **"Smite upon the ground … and stayed" → "Strike to the ground … and stopped"** (v18) — Hebrew אַרְצָה "to the ground"; וַיַּעֲמֹד, literally "and stood".
- **"was wroth with him" → "was wrathful with him"** (v19) — Hebrew קָצַף. **"Thou shouldest have smitten" → "You should have struck"** — Hebrew לְהַכּוֹת, a bare infinitive: "(it was) to strike five or six times". **"but thrice" → "three times"** — no "but / only" in the Hebrew.
- **"the bands of the Moabites invaded the land at the coming in of the year" → "raiding bands of Moab would come into the land at the coming of the year"** (v20) — Hebrew גְּדוּדֵי מוֹאָב "raiding bands of Moab"; יָבֹאוּ, an imperfect of what happened again and again; בָּא שָׁנָה, literally "(when) a year came": commonly understood "at the coming in of the year", each year or in spring. The wording is unusual and not certain.
- **"when the man was let down, and touched the bones" → "the man went and touched the bones"** (v21) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ וַיִּגַּע הָאִישׁ, literally "and he went and touched, the man": the body, thrown in, came against the bones. **"a band of men" → "the raiding band"** — Hebrew הַגְּדוּד, with the article. **"sepulchre" → "grave"** — Hebrew קֶבֶר. **"he revived" → "he lived"** — Hebrew וַיְחִי.
- **"was gracious unto them … had respect unto them, because of his covenant" → "showed favor to them … turned to them, on account of his covenant"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיָּחָן (חָנַן "show favor") · וַיְרַחֲמֵם · וַיִּפֶן אֲלֵיהֶם · לְמַעַן. **"would not destroy them, neither cast he them from his presence as yet" → "was not willing to ruin them, and he has not thrown them from before his face until now"** — Hebrew הַשְׁחִיתָם (hifil of שָׁחַת "ruin"); הִשְׁלִיכָם מֵעַל־פָּנָיו, literally "thrown them from upon his face"; עַד־עָתָּה "until now": the writer's own time.
- **"took again … the cities" → "returned and took the cities"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיָּשָׁב … וַיִּקַּח, literally "returned and took": the Hebrew way of saying "took again". **"by war" → "in the war"** — Hebrew בַּמִּלְחָמָה. **"did Joash beat him, and recovered the cities" → "Joash struck him, and he returned the cities"** — Hebrew הִכָּהוּ; וַיָּשֶׁב hifil "brought back".

### Notes
- **Names** — the Hebrew writes the king of Israel both as יְהוֹאָשׁ (v10, 25) and יוֹאָשׁ (v9, 12–14, 25), and the king of Judah as יוֹאָשׁ (v1, 10).
- **Chronology** — v1 and v10 give the figures as the Masoretic Text prints them: seventeen years from the twenty-third year of Joash of Judah, and an accession in his thirty-seventh year.
- **Israel's nadir** — ten chariots left. Reduced to a token military, under Aramean dominance.
- **Jehoahaz's prayer answered** — Yahweh gives an unnamed savior (v5). Often identified with the Assyrian king Adad-nirari III, whose westward campaigns (ca. 805-796 BC) weakened Aram-Damascus; others think of Jehoash or Jeroboam II (14:27).
- **Elisha's final sign** — bow-and-arrow sign-act. The king strikes three times and stops; the victories are three (v25).
- **"Chariots of Israel"** — same words Elisha spoke over Elijah (2:12).
- **The bones of Elisha** — a dead man lives when he touches them (v21).
- **Covenant preservation** (v23) — the covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob as ground of Israel's continued existence, even under judgment.
- **Three victories** — Joash takes back cities from Ben-hadad. Limited but real.
