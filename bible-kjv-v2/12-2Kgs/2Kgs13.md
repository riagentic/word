# 2 Kings 13

[1] In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned seventeen years.

[2] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and followed the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat that made Israel to sin; he did not depart from them.

[3] The anger of Yahweh was kindled against Israel, and he delivered them into the hand of Hazael king of Aram, and into the hand of Ben-hadad son of Hazael, all their days.

[4] Jehoahaz entreated Yahweh, and Yahweh listened to him, for he saw the oppression of Israel, because the king of Aram oppressed them.

[5] (Yahweh gave Israel a deliverer, so that they went out from under the hand of Aram; the children of Israel lived in their tents as before.

[6] Yet they did not depart from the sins of the house of Jeroboam that made Israel to sin; they walked in them. The Asherah pole also remained in Samaria.)

[7] For he had left to Jehoahaz no more of an army than fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers; the king of Aram had destroyed them and made them like dust at threshing.

[8] The rest of the acts of Jehoahaz, and all that he did, and his might — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

[9] Jehoahaz slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; Joash his son reigned in his place.

[10] In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and he reigned sixteen years.

[11] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh; he did not depart from all the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat who made Israel to sin, but walked in them.

[12] The rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, and his might with which he fought against Amaziah king of Judah — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

[13] Joash slept with his fathers, and Jeroboam sat on his throne; Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.

[14] Now Elisha had fallen sick with the sickness of which he was to die. Joash the king of Israel came down to him, wept over his face, and said, "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!"

[15] Elisha said to him, "Take bow and arrows." He took for himself bow and arrows.

[16] He said to the king of Israel, "Draw the bow with your hand." He drew it with his hand; Elisha laid his hands upon the king's hands.

[17] He said, "Open the window eastward." He opened it. Elisha said, "Shoot." He shot. He said, "The arrow of Yahweh's victory, and the arrow of victory over Aram! You shall strike the Arameans at Aphek until you have consumed them."

[18] He said, "Take the arrows." He took them. He said to the king of Israel, "Strike the ground." He struck three times and stopped.

[19] The man of *Elohim* was angry with him and said, "You should have struck five or six times; then you would have struck Aram until you had consumed it. Now you shall strike Aram only three times."

[20] Elisha died, and they buried him. Now bands of Moabites used to raid the land at the turn of the year.

[21] As a man was being buried, behold, they spotted a raiding band. They threw the man into the tomb of Elisha; when the man touched the bones of Elisha, he revived and stood on his feet.

[22] But Hazael king of Aram oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz.

[23] Yahweh was gracious to them and had compassion on them; he turned toward them, because of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He was not willing to destroy them, and did not cast them from his presence as yet.

[24] Hazael king of Aram died, and Ben-hadad his son reigned in his place.

[25] Jehoash son of Jehoahaz took back from the hand of Ben-hadad son of Hazael the cities that he had taken in war from the hand of Jehoahaz his father. Three times Joash struck him and recovered the cities of Israel.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Syria / Syrians" → "Aram / Arameans"** (throughout).
- **"beseth his face... coming in of the year" → "used to raid the land at the turn of the year"** (v20) — *tequfat ha-shanah*; year-turn, spring.
- **"Put thine hand upon the bow" → "Draw the bow with your hand"** (v16) — *harkeb*; mount/set the bow.

### Notes
- **Israel's nadir** — ten chariots left. Reduced to a token military, under Aramean dominance.
- **Jehoahaz's prayer answered** — Yahweh sends an unnamed deliverer (v5). Identifies most likely as Assyrian king Adad-nirari III, whose westward campaigns (ca. 805-796 BC) weakened Aram-Damascus. Providence through a pagan empire.
- **Elisha's final miracle-oracle** — bow-and-arrow sign-act. King's half-hearted pounding (three strikes) limits the scope of victory. Prophetic action requires matching human vigor.
- **"Chariots of Israel"** — same title Elisha gave Elijah (2:12); now returned.
- **Posthumous miracle** — touching Elisha's bones revives a corpse. Only instance of corpse-resurrection by relic. Elisha's ministry, longer than Elijah's, closes with this sign.
- **Covenant preservation** (v23) — Abrahamic covenant as ground of Israel's continued existence, even under judgment.
- **Three victories** — Joash recovers cities from Ben-hadad III. Limited but real.
