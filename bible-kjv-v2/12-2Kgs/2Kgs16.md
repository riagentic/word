# 2 Kings 16

[1] In the seventeenth year of ⟦pekah|Peqach|Pekah⟧ the son of ⟦remaliah|Remalyahu|Remaliah⟧, ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ the son of ⟦jotham|Yotam|Jotham⟧, king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, began to reign.

[2] ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ was twenty years old when he began to reign, and sixteen years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And he did not do what was right in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, like David his father.

[3] And he walked in the way of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and also his son he made pass through the fire, according to the abominations of the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dispossessed from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[4] And he sacrificed and made smoke on the high places and on the hills and under every luxuriant tree.

[5] Then ⟦rezin|Retzin|Rezin⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and ⟦pekah|Peqach|Pekah⟧ the son of ⟦remaliah|Remalyahu|Remaliah⟧, king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, went up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ for war, and they besieged ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧, and they were not able to fight.

[6] At that time ⟦rezin|Retzin|Rezin⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ returned ⟦elath|Eilat|Elath⟧ to ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧, and he cleared away the ⟦judeans|Yehudim|Judeans⟧ from ⟦elath|Eilat|Elath⟧; and the ⟦edomites|Edomim|Edomites⟧ came to ⟦elath|Eilat|Elath⟧ and dwelt there, to this day.

[7] And ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ sent messengers to ⟦tiglath-pileser|Tiglat-Pileser|Tiglath-pileser⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, saying, "Your servant and your son am I. Come up and save me from the palm of the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ and from the palm of the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, who are rising against me."

[8] And ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ took the silver and the gold that was found in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and in the treasuries of the house of the king, and he sent to the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ a bribe.

[9] And the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ listened to him, and the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ went up to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧ and seized it and carried it into exile to ⟦kir|Qir|Kir⟧, and ⟦rezin|Retzin|Rezin⟧ he put to death.

[10] And King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ went to meet ⟦tiglath-pileser|Tiglat-Pileser|Tiglath-pileser⟧ king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ at ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and he saw the altar that was in ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧. And King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ sent to ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ the priest the likeness of the altar and its pattern, according to all its work.

[11] And ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ the priest built the altar; according to all that King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ had sent from ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, so did ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ the priest, until King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ came from ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧.

[12] And the king came from ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and the king saw the altar, and the king came near to the altar and went up upon it.

[13] And he turned into smoke his burnt offering and his grain offering, and he poured out his drink offering, and he dashed the blood of the peace offerings that were his upon the altar.

[14] And the altar of bronze that was before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, he brought it near from before the house, from between the altar and the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he placed it on the flank of the altar, northward.

[15] And King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ commanded ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ the priest, saying, "Upon the great altar turn into smoke the burnt offering of the morning and the grain offering of the evening, and the burnt offering of the king and his grain offering, and the burnt offering of all the people of the land and their grain offering and their drink offerings; and all the blood of the burnt offering and all the blood of the sacrifice you shall dash upon it. And the altar of bronze shall be for me to inquire."

[16] And ⟦uriah|Uriyah|Uriah⟧ the priest did according to all that King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ commanded.

[17] And King ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ cut off the frames of the bases and removed the basin from upon them, and the sea he brought down from upon the herd of bronze that was under it, and he placed it on a pavement of stones.

[18] And the covered way of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ that they had built in the house, and the outer entry of the king, he turned around in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, because of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧.

[19] And the rest of the acts of ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?

[20] And ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧ lay with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David, and ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v5–7) — Hebrew אֲרָם.
- **"began Ahaz … to reign"** (v1) — the verb is מָלַךְ, "reign", here of the beginning. **"Twenty years old"** (v2) — literally "a son of twenty years".
- **"did not that which was right in the sight of the LORD" → "did not do what was right in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew הַיָּשָׁר בְּעֵינֵי.
- **"made his son to pass through the fire" → "also his son he made pass through the fire"** (v3) — Hebrew הֶעֱבִיר בָּאֵשׁ, the words of Deut 18:10; the son stands first in the Hebrew. What was done is not described. **"the heathen, whom the LORD cast out" → "the nations that Yahweh dispossessed"** — Hebrew הַגּוֹיִם; הוֹרִישׁ, as 1 Kgs 14:24.
- **"burnt incense" → "made smoke"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיְקַטֵּר without an object: the verb says only that smoke was made to rise, of a sacrifice or of incense. **"under every green tree" → "under every luxuriant tree"** — Hebrew רַעֲנָן, "fresh, full of leaves", not the colour.
- **"came up to Jerusalem to war … but could not overcome him" → "went up to Jerusalem for war … and they were not able to fight"** (v5) — Hebrew לַמִּלְחָמָה, "for the war / battle"; וְלֹא יָכְלוּ לְהִלָּחֵם, "they were not able to fight": no object and no "overcome" (Isa 7:1 adds "against it").
- **"recovered Elath to Syria, and drave the Jews from Elath: and the Syrians came" → "returned Elath to Aram, and he cleared away the Judeans from Elath; and the Edomites came"** (v6) — Hebrew הֵשִׁיב, "brought back"; נִשֵּׁל, the verb of Deut 7:1; הַיְהוּדִים, the first place where the word stands in the Bible. "Edomites" is the reading tradition (qere, וַאֲדוֹמִים); the written text (ketiv) has "Arameans" (וארמים). The second "Elath" is written אֵילוֹת, "Eloth", in the Hebrew.
- **"I am thy servant and thy son … out of the hand" → "Your servant and your son am I … from the palm"** (v7) — Hebrew word order; כַּף, "palm", not יָד. "Servant" is עֶבֶד, "slave". **"which rise up against me"** — a participle, "who are rising".
- **"sent it for a present" → "sent … a bribe"** (v8) — Hebrew שֹׁחַד, the word of Exod 23:8 and 1 Kgs 15:19; "it" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"went up against Damascus, and took it, and carried the people of it captive to Kir, and slew Rezin" → "went up to Damascus and seized it and carried it into exile to Kir, and Rezin he put to death"** (v9) — Hebrew אֶל, "to"; תָּפַשׂ; וַיַּגְלֶהָ, "carried it into exile": the city stands for its people, "the people of" is not written; הֵמִית.
- **"Damascus"** (v10) — written דּוּמֶּשֶׂק the first time in this verse, דַּמֶּשֶׂק elsewhere. **"the fashion of the altar, and the pattern of it, according to all the workmanship thereof" → "the likeness of the altar and its pattern, according to all its work"** — Hebrew דְּמוּת (Gen 1:26); תַּבְנִית (Exod 25:9); לְכָל־מַעֲשֵׂהוּ, "for all its making".
- **"so Urijah the priest made it against king Ahaz came" → "so did Uriah the priest, until King Ahaz came"** (v11) — Hebrew כֵּן עָשָׂה … עַד־בּוֹא, "so he did, until the coming of": it was done by the time he came.
- **"the king approached to the altar, and offered thereon" → "the king came near to the altar and went up upon it"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּקְרַב עַל; וַיַּעַל עָלָיו, "went up upon it", as 1 Kgs 12:32, 33; the same letters can be read "and offered up upon it".
- **"he burnt his burnt offering and his meat offering" → "he turned into smoke his burnt offering and his grain offering"** (v13, 15) — Hebrew הִקְטִיר, not שָׂרַף "burn"; מִנְחָה. **"sprinkled the blood" → "dashed the blood"** — Hebrew זָרַק, thrown from a bowl. **"his peace offerings" → "the peace offerings that were his"** — Hebrew הַשְּׁלָמִים אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ.
- **"he brought also the brasen altar … from the forefront of the house … and put it on the north side of the altar" → "the altar of bronze … he brought it near from before the house … and he placed it on the flank of the altar, northward"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיַּקְרֵב, "brought near": he moved it; מֵאֵת פְּנֵי הַבַּיִת, "from the face of the house"; יֶרֶךְ, "thigh, flank", as Lev 1:11. "The altar" both times is the new one.
- **"commanded"** (v15) — the reading tradition (qere); the written text has "commanded him". **"the brasen altar shall be for me to enquire by" → "the altar of bronze shall be for me to inquire"** — Hebrew לְבַקֵּר, "to search, look into" (Lev 13:36; 27:33; Ps 27:4), without an object; "by" is not written. Whether the king means to seek an answer from God at it or to consider what is to be done with it is uncertain.
- **"cut off the borders of the bases, and removed the laver from off them; and took down the sea from off the brasen oxen … a pavement of stones" → "cut off the frames of the bases and removed the basin from upon them, and the sea he brought down from upon the herd of bronze … a pavement of stones"** (v17) — Hebrew מִסְגְּרוֹת, מְכֹנוֹת, כִּיֹּר, הַיָּם, הַבָּקָר: the words of 1 Kgs 7:23–39. The Hebrew has "the frames, the bases", both with the article. "The basin" follows the reading tradition (qere); the written text has "and the basin".
- **"the covert for the sabbath … and the king's entry without, turned he from the house of the LORD for the king of Assyria" → "the covered way of the Shabbat … and the outer entry of the king, he turned around in the house of Yahweh, because of the king of Assyria"** (v18) — Hebrew מוּסַךְ (qere; the written text has מיסך), found only here: something covered, from סָכַךְ "screen, cover"; what it was is not known. הֵסֵב בֵּית יְהוָה: "he turned around (in, to) the house of Yahweh"; the Hebrew has no preposition, and whether he removed these things from the house, moved them into it or changed them is not said. מִפְּנֵי, "from the face of": because of, or for fear of.
- **"the rest of the acts … the book of the chronicles"** (v19) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי, "the words, matters of Ahaz … the book of the matters of the days".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁכַב.

### Notes
- **Ahaz** — the first king of Judah of whom the book says that he made his son pass through the fire. Isaiah 7 belongs to this reign and this war.
- **The war with Aram and Israel** (v5) — Rezin and Pekah go up against Jerusalem (Isa 7:1–9); Ahaz turns to Assyria.
- **Tiglath-pileser** — he takes Damascus and puts Rezin to death; the exile to Kir is the word of Amos 1:5. The name is written תִּגְלַת פְּלֶסֶר in v7 and תִּגְלַת פִּלְאֶסֶר in v10.
- **The altar from Damascus** — whose altar it was (Aramean or Assyrian) is not said. Uriah the priest builds it and does all that the king commands (v11, 16); compare Isa 8:2.
- **The altar of bronze** — the altar of 1 Kgs 8:64 is moved to the north flank of the new altar, and the offerings pass to "the great altar".
- **The bases and the sea** (v17) — the works of 1 Kgs 7:23–39. Why Ahaz took them apart is not said in v17; v18 names the king of Assyria.
- **Hezekiah** — his reign follows in chapters 18–20.
