# 2 Kings 6

[1] The sons of the prophets said to Elisha, "Behold, the place where we live under you is too cramped for us.

[2] Let us go, please, to the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, and each take from there a beam, and let us make a place there where we may live." He answered, "Go."

[3] One said, "Please be willing to go with your servants." He answered, "I will go."

[4] So he went with them. When they came to the ⟦jordan|Yarden|Jordan⟧, they cut down timber.

[5] As one was felling a beam, the iron axe-head fell into the water. He cried out and said, "Alas, my master! It was borrowed."

[6] The man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Where did it fall?" He showed him the place. He cut off a stick, threw it in there, and made the iron float.

[7] He said, "Take it up for yourself." So he put out his hand and took it.

[8] The king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ was warring against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; he took counsel with his servants, saying, "At such and such a place shall be my encampment."

[9] The man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ sent to the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "Beware that you do not pass that place, for the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ are coming down there."

[10] The king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sent to the place that the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ told him and warned him about, and saved himself there — not once nor twice.

[11] The heart of the king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ was greatly troubled about this thing; he called his servants and said to them, "Will you not tell me which of us is for the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?"

[12] One of his servants said, "None, my lord, O king; but Elisha, the prophet in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, tells the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ the words that you speak in your bedchamber."

[13] He said, "Go and see where he is, that I may send and take him." It was told him, "Behold, he is in ⟦dothan|Dotan|Dothan⟧."

[14] So he sent horses and chariots and a great army there; they came by night and surrounded the city.

[15] When the attendant of the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rose early and went out, behold, an army with horses and chariots surrounded the city. His attendant said to him, "Alas, my master! What shall we do?"

[16] He answered, "Fear not; those who are with us are more than those who are with them."

[17] Elisha prayed and said, "O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, please open his eyes, that he may see." ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ opened the eyes of the young man, and he saw; behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.

[18] When they came down to him, Elisha prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying, "Please strike this people with dazzling-blindness." He struck them with dazzling-blindness according to the word of Elisha.

[19] Elisha said to them, "This is not the way, neither is this the city; follow me, and I will bring you to the man whom you seek." He led them to ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[20] When they had come into ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, Elisha said, "O ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, open the eyes of these men, that they may see." ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ opened their eyes, and they saw; behold, they were in the midst of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[21] When the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ saw them, he said to Elisha, "My father, shall I strike them? Shall I strike them?"

[22] He answered, "You shall not strike them. Would you strike those whom you have taken captive with your sword and with your bow? Set bread and water before them, that they may eat and drink, and go to their master."

[23] So he prepared a great feast for them; when they had eaten and drunk, he sent them away, and they went to their master. The raiding bands of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ came no more into the land of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[24] After this, ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ gathered all his army and went up and besieged ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧.

[25] There was a great famine in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, while they besieged it, until a donkey's head was sold for eighty shekels of silver, and a fourth of a qab of dove's dung for five shekels of silver.

[26] As the king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ was passing by on the wall, a woman cried out to him, saying, "Help, my lord the king!"

[27] He said, "If ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ does not help you, from where shall I help you? From the threshing floor, or from the winepress?"

[28] The king said to her, "What troubles you?" She answered, "This woman said to me, 'Give your son, that we may eat him today, and we will eat my son tomorrow.'

[29] So we boiled my son and ate him. I said to her on the next day, 'Give your son, that we may eat him.' But she has hidden her son."

[30] When the king heard the words of the woman, he tore his clothes — now he was passing by on the wall — and the people looked, and behold, he had sackcloth beneath on his flesh.

[31] Then he said, "⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ do so to me, and more also, if the head of Elisha son of ⟦shaphat|Shafat|Shaphat⟧ remains on him this day."

[32] Elisha was sitting in his house, and the elders were sitting with him; the king sent a man from before him. But before the messenger came to him, he said to the elders, "Do you see how this son of a murderer has sent to take away my head? Look, when the messenger comes, shut the door and hold him fast at the door; is not the sound of his master's feet behind him?"

[33] While he was still speaking with them, behold, the messenger came down to him. And he said, "Behold, this evil is from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; why should I wait for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ any longer?"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Syria / Syrians" → "Aram / Arameans"** (throughout).
- **"strait" → "cramped"** (v1) — *tzar*.
- **"blindness" → "dazzling-blindness"** (v18) — Hebrew *sanverim*; rare word (only here and Gen 19:11); not ordinary blindness but disorienting dazzle/confusion.
- **"great provision" → "great feast"** (v23) — *kerah gedolah*.
- **"bands of Syria" → "raiding bands of Aram"** (v23) — *gedudei Aram*.
- **"an ass's head" → "a donkey's head"** (v25).
- **"fourscore pieces of silver" → "eighty shekels of silver"** (v25) — *shemonim kesef*.
- **"barnfloor" → "threshing floor"** (v27) — *goren*.

### Notes
- **Floating axe-head** — smallest of Elisha's miracles, concerned with poor prophet's honor (borrowed tool). Prophet cares for ordinary loss.
- **Dothan** — near Samaria. Archaeological Tell Dotha.
- **"More with us than with them"** — invisible-army motif. Prayed-open eyes see divine reality.
- **Humane treatment of captives** — Elisha rejects execution. Prisoners of war fed and released. Practical peacemaking that stops raiding (v23).
- **Siege of Samaria** — Ben-hadad II. Famine forces cannibalism (Deut 28:57 curses). Woman's horror exposes depth of judgment.
- **"Dove's dung"** — possibly literal (as fuel or desperate famine-food) or slang for a cheap plant. Either way, expensive luxury in siege.
- **King's sackcloth** — hidden repentance-garment beneath royal robes; exposed only when he tears clothes.
- **"Son of a murderer"** — Elisha's epithet for Jehoram son of Ahab (murderer of Naboth through Jezebel).
- **Cliffhanger** — ch 6 ends unresolved; ch 7 resolves with siege's miraculous lifting.
