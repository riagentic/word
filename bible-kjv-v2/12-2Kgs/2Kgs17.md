[7] And it came to pass, because the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought them up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from under the hand of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ king of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and they feared other gods,

[10] And they set up for themselves pillars and Asherim on every high hill and under every luxuriant tree.

[11] And they made smoke there on all the high places, like the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ carried into exile from before them, and they did evil things, to vex ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] And they served the idols, of which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had said to them, "You shall not do this thing."

[13] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ warned ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ by the hand of all the prophets of every seer, saying, "Turn back from your evil ways and keep my commandments, my statutes, according to all the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ that I commanded your fathers and that I sent to you by the hand of my servants the prophets."

[14] And they did not hear, and they stiffened their neck, like the neck of their fathers, who did not believe in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[15] And they rejected his statutes and his covenant that he cut with their fathers, and his testimonies with which he warned them; and they went after the vapor and became vapor, and after the nations that were around them, of whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded them not to do like them.

[16] And they forsook all the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and made for themselves a molten image, two calves, and they made an Asherah and bowed down to all the host of the heavens and served the Baal.

[17] And they made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire, and they practiced divinations and divined, and they sold themselves to do the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to vex him.

[18] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was very angry with ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and removed them from before his face. None was left, only the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ alone.

[19] Also ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ did not keep the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they walked in the statutes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which they made.

[20] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ rejected all the seed of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and afflicted them and gave them into the hand of pillagers, until he had thrown them from before his face.

[21] For he tore ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from upon the house of David, and they made ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ the son of ⟦nebat|Nevat|Nebat⟧ king, and ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ drew ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ away from after ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and caused them to sin a great sin.

[22] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ walked in all the sins of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ that he did; they did not turn aside from it,

[23] until ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ removed ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from before his face, as he had spoken by the hand of all his servants the prophets. And ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went into exile from upon its ground to ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, to this day.

[24] And the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ brought people from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ and from ⟦cuthah|Kutah|Cuthah⟧ and from Avva and from ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ and ⟦sepharvaim|Sefarvayim|Sepharvaim⟧, and he made them dwell in the cities of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ in place of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and they took possession of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and dwelt in its cities.

[25] And it came to pass at the beginning of their dwelling there that they did not fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ sent the lions among them, and they were killing among them.

[26] And they said to the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧, saying, "The nations that you carried into exile and made dwell in the cities of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ do not know the ordinance of the god of the land, and he has sent the lions among them, and behold, they are putting them to death, because they do not know the ordinance of the god of the land."

[27] And the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ commanded, saying, "Make one of the priests whom you carried into exile from there go there, and let them go and dwell there, and let him teach them the ordinance of the god of the land."

[28] And one of the priests whom they had carried into exile from ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ came and dwelt in ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, and he was teaching them how they should fear ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[29] And they were making, nation by nation, its gods, and they set them in the house of the high places that the ⟦samaritans|Shomronim|Samaritans⟧ had made, nation by nation in their cities where they were dwelling.

[30] And the men of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ made ⟦succoth-benoth|Sukkot Benot|Succoth-benoth⟧, and the men of ⟦cuthah|Kut|Cuth⟧ made Nergal, and the men of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ made Ashima,

[31] and the ⟦avvites|Avvim|Avvites⟧ made ⟦nibhaz|Nivchaz|Nibhaz⟧ and ⟦tartak|Tartaq|Tartak⟧; and the ⟦sepharvaim|Sefarvim|Sepharvites⟧ were burning their sons in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of ⟦sepharvaim|Sefarvayim|Sepharvaim⟧.

[32] And they were fearing ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they made for themselves from their ends priests of high places, and they were offering for them in the house of the high places.

[33] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ they were fearing, and their gods they were serving, according to the ordinance of the nations from where they had carried them into exile.

[34] To this day they are doing according to the former ordinances. They are not fearing ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they are not doing according to their statutes and according to their ordinance, and according to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and according to the commandment that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded the sons of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, whose name he made ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[35] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ cut a covenant with them and commanded them, saying, "You shall not fear other gods, and you shall not bow down to them, and you shall not serve them, and you shall not sacrifice to them;

[36] but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who brought you up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ with great power and with an outstretched arm, him you shall fear, and to him you shall bow down, and to him you shall sacrifice.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 9, 14, 16, 19, 39) — Hebrew *Elohim*. In v26, 27 the settlers speak of "the god of the land" (אֱלֹהֵי הָאָרֶץ), one god among the gods of the lands: plain text, as 1 Kgs 20:23, 28. "Other gods" (v7, 35, 37, 38) and "its gods", "their gods" (v29, 31, 33) are the same word; אֱלֹהָיו in v29 can also be read "its god".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"began Hoshea … to reign"** (v1) — the verb is מָלַךְ, "reign", here of the beginning. **"he did that which was evil" → "he did the evil"** (v2, 17) — with the article.
- **"Hoshea became his servant, and gave him presents" → "became a servant to him and returned to him an offering"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew עֶבֶד, "slave"; הֵשִׁיב מִנְחָה, "brought back an offering": tribute paid again and again. The same word מִנְחָה is the grain offering.
- **"So king of Egypt"** (v4) — no king of this name is known; the Hebrew is kept as printed. **"shut him up, and bound him in prison" → "restrained him and bound him in a house of confinement"** — Hebrew עָצַר; בֵּית כֶּלֶא, as 1 Kgs 22:27.
- **"took Samaria, and carried Israel away into Assyria, and placed them" → "captured Samaria and carried Israel into exile to Assyria, and he made them dwell"** (v6) — Hebrew לָכַד; הִגְלָה, the verb of v11, 23, 26, 27, 28, 33; הוֹשִׁיב. "On the Habor" is "in Habor" in the Hebrew; "in" before "the cities" is supplied.
- **"For so it was, that the children of Israel had sinned" → "And it came to pass, because the sons of Israel sinned"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיְהִי כִּי; the sentence runs on through the chain of v7–17 to v18. **"feared other gods"** — Hebrew יָרֵא, the verb of v25–41.
- **"walked in the statutes of the heathen … and of the kings of Israel, which they had made"** (v8, 19) — Hebrew חֻקּוֹת, "statutes", as Lev 18:3; 20:23. וּמַלְכֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר עָשׂוּ is short: "and the kings of Israel, which they made"; "of" is supplied.
- **"did secretly those things that were not right" → "did in secret things that were not right"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיְחַפְּאוּ, found only here; the meaning is uncertain ("did secretly", from "cover", or "uttered"). דְּבָרִים is "words" or "things"; לֹא־כֵן, "not so". **"from the tower of the watchmen to the fenced city" → "from a tower of guards to a fortified city"** — Hebrew נוֹצְרִים, from נָצַר "guard".
- **"images and groves in every high hill, and under every green tree" → "pillars and Asherim on every high hill and under every luxuriant tree"** (v10) — Hebrew מַצֵּבוֹת, stones set up; אֲשֵׁרִים; רַעֲנָן, as 1 Kgs 14:23.
- **"burnt incense" → "made smoke"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיְקַטְּרוּ without an object. **"the heathen whom the LORD carried away" → "the nations that Yahweh carried into exile"** — Hebrew הֶגְלָה. **"wrought wicked things to provoke the LORD to anger" → "did evil things, to vex Yahweh"** (v11, 17) — Hebrew הִכְעִיס; "anger" is not a second word in the Hebrew.
- **"idols"** (v12) — Hebrew גִּלֻּלִים, a word of contempt.
- **"the LORD testified against Israel … by all the prophets, and by all the seers" → "Yahweh warned Israel … by the hand of all the prophets of every seer"** (v13) — Hebrew הֵעִיד בְּ, "bear witness against, warn solemnly". The written text (ketiv, נביאו) is "every prophet of his, every seer"; the reading tradition (qere, נְבִיאֵי) gives "all the prophets of every seer". The reading tradition is translated, as everywhere in this translation; the written form gives the easier sense. **"Turn ye" → "Turn back"** — Hebrew שׁוּבוּ. "My commandments, my statutes" stand without "and" in the Hebrew.
- **"they would not hear, but hardened their necks" → "they did not hear, and they stiffened their neck"** (v14, 40) — Hebrew וְלֹא שָׁמֵעוּ; הִקְשָׁה עֹרֶף, as Deut 10:16. **"did not believe in"** — Hebrew הֶאֱמִין בְּ, "trust, hold firm to".
- **"his covenant that he made … his testimonies which he testified against them" → "his covenant that he cut … his testimonies with which he warned them"** (v15, 35, 38) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית; עֵדְוֺתָיו אֲשֶׁר הֵעִיד בָּם: noun and verb are of one root, "his testimonies that he testified against them". **"they followed vanity, and became vain" → "they went after the vapor and became vapor"** — Hebrew הַהֶבֶל וַיֶּהְבָּלוּ, "breath, vapor": what has no substance, said of the idols (Jer 2:5).
- **"molten images, even two calves … a grove … all the host of heaven … Baal" → "a molten image, two calves … an Asherah … all the host of the heavens … the Baal"** (v16) — Hebrew מַסֵּכָה, singular; "two" follows the reading tradition (qere); אֲשֵׁירָה; הַבַּעַל with the article.
- **"used divination and enchantments" → "practiced divinations and divined"** (v17) — Hebrew קָסַם קְסָמִים and נִחֵשׁ, two verbs, as Deut 18:10; English has one word for both. **"made their sons and their daughters pass through the fire"** — as Deut 18:10.
- **"removed them out of his sight" → "removed them from before his face"** (v18, 20, 23) — Hebrew מֵעַל פָּנָיו, "from upon his face" (v18, 23); מִפָּנָיו, "from his face" (v20). **"cast them out" → "thrown them"** (v20) — Hebrew הִשְׁלִיךְ.
- **"delivered them into the hand of spoilers" → "gave them into the hand of pillagers"** (v20) — Hebrew שֹׁסִים, as Judg 2:14.
- **"For he rent Israel from the house of David" → "For he tore Israel from upon the house of David"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּי־קָרַע יִשְׂרָאֵל מֵעַל, the word of 1 Kgs 11:11, 31. The Hebrew can also be read "For Israel tore away from the house of David". **"Jeroboam drave Israel from following the LORD" → "drew Israel away from after Yahweh"** — the reading tradition (qere, וַיַּדַּח, the verb of Deut 13:5, 13); the written text (וידא) is from a verb found only here, "thrust away".
- **"they departed not from them" → "they did not turn aside from it"** (v22) — Hebrew סוּר; מִמֶּנָּה, singular, "from it".
- **"So was Israel carried away out of their own land" → "And Israel went into exile from upon its ground"** (v23) — Hebrew וַיִּגֶל, the simple stem; אַדְמָתוֹ, "its ground".
- **"brought men from Babylon … and placed them" → "brought people from Babylon … and he made them dwell"** (v24) — the Hebrew names no object for "brought" and "made dwell"; "people" and "them" are supplied. "From" is not repeated before Sepharvaim.
- **"the LORD sent lions among them, which slew some of them" → "Yahweh sent the lions among them, and they were killing among them"** (v25, 26) — Hebrew הָאֲרָיוֹת, with the article; הֹרְגִים בָּהֶם.
- **"the manner of the God of the land" → "the ordinance of the god of the land"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט, "judgment, ruling": what is laid down as right, and so also a custom. The one word is kept "ordinance" through the chapter (v26, 27, 33, 34, 37, 40), where KJV has "manner" and "ordinance".
- **"Carry thither one of the priests … and let them go and dwell there, and let him teach them"** (v27) — the Hebrew changes from "them" to "him": וְיֵלְכוּ וְיֵשְׁבוּ are plural, וְיֹרֵם singular. הֹלִיכוּ is "make go". "Teach" is הוֹרָה, the root of *torah*.
- **"every nation made gods of their own, and put them in the houses of the high places" → "they were making, nation by nation, its gods, and they set them in the house of the high places"** (v29, 32) — Hebrew גּוֹי גּוֹי, "nation, nation"; בֵּית הַבָּמוֹת, "the house of the high places", singular, as 1 Kgs 12:31. **"the Samaritans"** — Hebrew הַשֹּׁמְרֹנִים, only here: the people of Samaria, Israel before the exile.
- **"burnt their children in fire" → "were burning their sons in the fire"** (v31) — Hebrew שָׂרַף, "burn", another verb than "make pass through" (v17). "The gods of Sepharvaim" follows the reading tradition (qere); the written text has אלה ספרים.
- **"made unto themselves of the lowest of them priests … which sacrificed for them" → "made for themselves from their ends priests of high places, and they were offering for them"** (v32) — Hebrew מִקְצוֹתָם, "from their ends": from the whole of them, as 1 Kgs 12:31; nothing says "lowest". עֹשִׂים לָהֶם, "doing, making for them", has no object.
- **"after the manner of the nations whom they carried away from thence" → "according to the ordinance of the nations from where they had carried them into exile"** (v33) — the Hebrew is short: "of the nations that they carried them into exile from there".
- **"whom he named Israel" → "whose name he made Israel"** (v34) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־שָׂם שְׁמוֹ, "whose name he put (as) Israel". **"after their statutes, or after their ordinances"** — "their ordinance" is singular.
- **"him shall ye worship" → "to him you shall bow down"** (v35, 36) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה. **"with great power and a stretched out arm"** — בְּכֹחַ גָּדוֹל; elsewhere "with a strong hand".
- **"ye shall observe to do for evermore" → "you shall keep to do all the days"** (v37) — Hebrew תִּשְׁמְרוּן לַעֲשׂוֹת כָּל־הַיָּמִים. In v35–39 the speaker changes from "he wrote" to "I cut", as in the Hebrew.
- **"graven images" → "carved images"** (v41) — Hebrew פְּסִילִים. **"their children, and their children's children" → "their sons and the sons of their sons"**.

### Notes
- **Hoshea** — the last king of Israel in Samaria.
- **The king of Assyria** — v3 names Shalmaneser; v4–6 say only "the king of Assyria". The capture of Samaria is commonly dated 722 BC.
- **Halah, Habor, Gozan, the cities of the Medes** — places in the north and east of the Assyrian kingdom (18:11; 1 Chr 5:26).
- **Why Israel went into exile** (v7–23) — the charges are in the words of Deuteronomy: other gods, the statutes of the nations, high places, pillars and Asherim, the calves of Jeroboam (1 Kgs 12:28), the host of the heavens, sons and daughters passed through the fire, divination (Deut 12:2–3; 18:10).
- **The settlers** (v24–41) — peoples brought from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath and Sepharvaim fear Yahweh and serve their own gods. Of their gods only Nergal is known from elsewhere, as a god of Cuthah.
- **"To this day"** (v23, 34, 41) — the day of the writer.
