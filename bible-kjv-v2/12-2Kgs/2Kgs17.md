## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"So king of Egypt" — retained** (v4) — possibly Osorkon IV or a garbled form of "Sais." Identity debated.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v8, 11, 15, 26, 33) — *goyim*.
- **"images" → "sacred pillars"** (v10) — *matzevot*.
- **"used divination and enchantments" → "practiced divination and omens"** (v17) — *qesamim ve-nachashim*.
- **"statutes of the heathen" → "customs of the nations"** (v8, 19) — *huqqot ha-goyim*.
- **"manner of the God of the land" → "law of the *Elohim* of the land"** (v26, 27) — *mishpat elohei ha-aretz*; how Yahweh is to be rightly served in this territory.
- **"Cuthah / Cuth"** — Mesopotamian city; the Samarians later nicknamed "Cutheans" by Judeans in Second-Temple era.

### Notes
- **Hoshea** — last king of northern Israel. Reign ends 722 BC (some date 721).
- **Fall of Samaria** — traditionally attributed to Shalmaneser V (who began siege); Sargon II (successor) likely completed it and claims credit in his annals. Three-year siege.
- **Deportation sites** — Halah, Habor/Gozan, Media. Assyrian policy of ethnic mixing. Standard ANE imperial practice.
- **Theological sermon** (v7-23) — the Deuteronomic Historian's explanation of why the North fell. Chief charges: idolatry, Jeroboam's calves, child sacrifice, ignoring prophetic warnings.
- **"Lost Tribes"** — origin of the trope. The ten northern tribes dispersed into Assyrian diaspora, many assimilated; remnants survived (e.g., Anna of Asher, Luke 2:36).
- **Samaritan origins** (v24-41) — this is the biblical account. Assyrians import foreigners; mixed population develops syncretic Yahwism. Later Samaritans dispute this origin story.
- **Yahweh-priest at Bethel** — ironic; a northern-style syncretic worship teaching is installed at Jeroboam's calf-shrine site.
- **Pan-religious syncretism** (v29-33) — each deportee group imports deity: Succoth-benoth (perhaps = Sarpanit, Marduk's consort), Nergal (Mesopotamian underworld god), Ashima (possibly Asima/Ashimat-Bethel), Nibhaz and Tartak (Avite gods, unknown), Adrammelech/Anammelech (Sepharvaim sun-deities).
