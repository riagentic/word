## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"book of the covenant" → "Scroll of the Covenant"** (v2, 21).
- **"planets" → "constellations"** (v5) — *mazzalot*; zodiacal signs.
- **"chamberlain" → "eunuch"** (v11) — *saris*.
- **"suburbs" → "precincts"** (v11) — *parvarim*; rare word, temple outbuildings.
- **"mount of corruption" → "Mount of Corruption"** (v13) — Hebrew *har ha-mashchit*; punning deformation of Har ha-Mishchah ("Mount of Anointing"), the Mount of Olives.
- **"images" → "sacred pillars"** (v14) — *matzevot*.
- **"brook" → "Wadi"** (v4, 6, 12).

### Notes
- **Josiah's reform** — most thorough cultic purge in Judean history. Centralizes worship in Jerusalem (Deut 12 program).
- **Ashes carried to Bethel** (v4) — ritual defilement of the northern rival shrine; Josiah extends reform into former-Israel territory (Assyria's weakening allows).
- **Horses and chariots of the sun** — Assyrian/Babylonian astral cult imports. Archaeology confirms solar-worship elements in Judah.
- **Topheth/Gehenna** — Valley of Hinnom becomes Jerusalem's perpetual trash-fire; etymological root of NT "Gehenna" (hell).
- **Man of *Elohim*'s bones preserved** (v17-18) — fulfills 1 Kgs 13:31-32. Long-range prophecy: the man of God from Judah prophesied Josiah's name three centuries earlier (1 Kgs 13:2).
- **Slaying the priests of the high places** (v20) — Josiah enforces Deut 13's penalty for apostasy with extreme rigor; done in Israelite (not Judean) territory.
- **Passover** (v21-23) — first centralized national Passover in centuries, keeping Deut 16.
- **Post-Josianic decline** — Assyria falling (Nineveh 612 BC, Haran 610), Egypt tries to prop up Assyrian remnant. Josiah opposes Neco at Megiddo; dies. Neco installs puppet Jehoiakim, deports Jehoahaz.
- **Jehoiakim's tax** — oppressive exactions; Jer 22 condemns him.
