## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"several house" → "quarantined house"** (v5) — *bet ha-chofshit*; disputed, usually "house of quarantine / isolation." Possibly "house of freedom" euphemistically for seclusion.
- **"Azariah" / "Uzziah"** — same king, both names used in this chapter.
- **"Pul" — retained; = Tiglath-pileser III** (v19). Pul = throne name used in Babylon; Assyrian annals confirm identity.
- **"money" → "silver"** (v20).
- **"captain" → "officer"** (v25) — *shalish*.
- **"palace" → "citadel"** (v25) — *armon*.
- **"Syria" → "Aram"** (v37).

### Notes
- **Azariah = Uzziah** — 52-year reign, contemporary with Isaiah and Amos. Struck by *tzara'at*; 2 Chr 26 records why (usurping priestly incense).
- **Fourth-generation fulfillment** — Jehu's dynasty ends with Zechariah (Jehu → Jehoahaz → Joash → Jeroboam II → Zechariah). Oracle of 2 Kgs 10:30 precisely fulfilled.
- **Rapid regicides** — Zechariah (6 mo), Shallum (1 mo), Menahem, Pekahiah (2 yr), Pekah. Northern kingdom disintegrating. Five kings in roughly 30 years.
- **Menahem's brutality** — ripping open pregnant women = atrocity Hosea associates with Gilead (Hos 13:16). Assyrian-style terror.
- **Tiglath-pileser III (Pul)** — emerges 745 BC. Transforms Assyria into world empire. Menahem's 1000-talent tribute recorded in Assyrian annals ("Menihimme of Samerina").
- **Galilee and Transjordan lost** — Tiglath-pileser's 733-732 BC campaign; first wave of northern exile (2 Kgs 15:29). Set up later Galilee-of-the-Gentiles (Isa 9).
- **Syro-Ephraimite war begins** (v37) — Aram + Israel pressure Judah; backdrop for Isa 7. Drives Ahaz to Assyrian vassalage.
