## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Chaldees" → "Chaldeans"** (v2) — *Kasdim*.
- **"Syrians" → "Arameans"** (v2).
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v12, 14) — *sarim*.
- **"officers" → "eunuchs"** (v12, 15) — *sarisim*.
- **"river of Egypt" → "Wadi of Egypt"** (v7) — *nachal mitzrayim*; Wadi el-Arish, Judah's SW border.
- **"father's brother" → "uncle"** (v17).

### Notes
- **Nebuchadnezzar II** — Chaldean/Neo-Babylonian king (605-562 BC). After Carchemish (605 BC) rout of Egypt, dominates the Levant.
- **Jehoiakim's death** — terse here; Jer 22:19 specifies dishonorable burial ("buried with the burial of a donkey"). May have died mid-siege or been executed.
- **First deportation (597 BC)** — Jehoiachin + elite, 10,000 + 7,000 + 1,000 smiths. Babylonian Chronicle confirms date and event. Ezekiel among the exiles.
- **Jehoiachin (=Jeconiah, Coniah)** — held in Babylon; Babylonian ration-tablets attest his presence and royal provisions. Released under Evil-merodach (2 Kgs 25:27-30). Line of Jesus (Matt 1:11-12).
- **Zedekiah's name change** — Mattaniah ("gift of Yahweh") → Zedekiah ("Yahweh is my righteousness"). Both Yahwistic; throne-naming asserts Babylonian authority.
- **Final collapse mechanism** — Zedekiah's rebellion (under Egyptian encouragement, cf. Jer 37) triggers destruction in ch 25.
- **Wadi of Egypt to Euphrates** — Babylonian dominance of traditional Egyptian sphere.
