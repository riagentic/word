# 2 Kings 24

[1] In his days Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up; Jehoiakim became his vassal for three years, then he turned and rebelled against him.

[2] Yahweh sent against him raiding bands of Chaldeans, raiding bands of Arameans, raiding bands of Moabites, and raiding bands of the children of Ammon; he sent them against Judah to destroy it, according to the word of Yahweh that he spoke by his servants the prophets.

[3] Surely at the command of Yahweh this came upon Judah, to remove them out of his sight, for the sins of Manasseh, according to all that he did,

[4] and also for the innocent blood that he shed; for he filled Jerusalem with innocent blood, which Yahweh would not pardon.

[5] The rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and all that he did — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

[6] Jehoiakim slept with his fathers; Jehoiachin his son reigned in his place.

[7] The king of Egypt did not come out of his land again, for the king of Babylon had taken all that belonged to the king of Egypt, from the Wadi of Egypt to the River Euphrates.

[8] Jehoiachin was eighteen years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Nehushta daughter of Elnathan of Jerusalem.

[9] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that his father had done.

[10] At that time the servants of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against Jerusalem, and the city was placed under siege.

[11] Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came up against the city while his servants were besieging it.

[12] Jehoiachin the king of Judah went out to the king of Babylon, he, his mother, his servants, his officials, and his eunuchs; the king of Babylon took him captive in the eighth year of his reign.

[13] He carried out from there all the treasures of the house of Yahweh and the treasures of the king's house; he cut in pieces all the vessels of gold that Solomon king of Israel had made in the temple of Yahweh, as Yahweh had said.

[14] He carried away all Jerusalem — all the officials, all the mighty men of valor (ten thousand captives), and all the craftsmen and smiths; no one was left, except the poorest of the people of the land.

[15] He carried Jehoiachin to Babylon — the king's mother, the king's wives, his eunuchs, and the leading men of the land; he carried them into exile from Jerusalem to Babylon.

[16] All the men of might — seven thousand — and the craftsmen and the smiths — a thousand — all of them strong and fit for war; these the king of Babylon brought captive to Babylon.

[17] The king of Babylon made Mattaniah his uncle king in his place, and changed his name to Zedekiah.

[18] Zedekiah was twenty-one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hamutal daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.

[19] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, according to all that Jehoiakim had done.

[20] For because of the anger of Yahweh it came to pass in Jerusalem and Judah, until he had cast them out from his presence; and Zedekiah rebelled against the king of Babylon.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Chaldees" → "Chaldeans"** (v2) — *Kasdim*.
- **"Syrians" → "Arameans"** (v2).
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v12, 14) — *sarim*.
- **"officers" → "eunuchs"** (v12, 15) — *sarisim*.
- **"river of Egypt" → "Wadi of Egypt"** (v7) — *nachal mitzrayim*; Wadi el-Arish, Judah's SW border.
- **"father's brother" → "uncle"** (v17).

### Notes
- **Nebuchadnezzar II** — Chaldean/Neo-Babylonian king (605-562 BC). After Carchemish (605 BC) rout of Egypt, dominates the Levant.
- **Jehoiakim's death** — terse here; Jer 22:19 specifies dishonorable burial ("buried with the burial of a donkey"). May have died mid-siege or been executed.
- **First deportation (597 BC)** — Jehoiachin + elite, 10,000 + 7,000 + 1,000 smiths. Babylonian Chronicle confirms date and event. Ezekiel among the exiles.
- **Jehoiachin (=Jeconiah, Coniah)** — held in Babylon; Babylonian ration-tablets attest his presence and royal provisions. Released under Evil-merodach (2 Kgs 25:27-30). Line of Jesus (Matt 1:11-12).
- **Zedekiah's name change** — Mattaniah ("gift of Yahweh") → Zedekiah ("Yahweh is my righteousness"). Both Yahwistic; throne-naming asserts Babylonian authority.
- **Final collapse mechanism** — Zedekiah's rebellion (under Egyptian encouragement, cf. Jer 37) triggers destruction in ch 25.
- **Wadi of Egypt to Euphrates** — Babylonian dominance of traditional Egyptian sphere.
