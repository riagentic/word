## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv15, 18) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"book of the law" → "scroll of the Law"** (vv8, 11) — Hebrew סֵפֶר הַתּוֹרָה *sefer ha-Torah*; *sefer* = scroll (not codex); *Torah* = Law/Teaching.
- **"money" → "silver"** (v4, 7, 9) — *kesef*.
- **"sum" → "reckon the total"** (v4) — *ve-yattem*; alternative reading may be "melt down" (*hatem*).
- **"keepers of the door" → "keepers of the threshold"** (v4) — *shomrei ha-saph*.
- **"breaches" → "damage"** (v5) — *bedeq*.
- **"communed" → "spoke"** (v14).

### Notes
- **Josiah** — great reformer king (640-609 BC). Ascended at eight following Amon's murder.
- **Scroll found in temple** — nearly universally identified with all or part of Deuteronomy (or proto-Deuteronomy). Internal fit: Josianic reforms match Deut 12 centralization, Deut 17 kingship, Deut 28 curses that terrify the king.
- **Historical-critical note** — the "finding" has been theorized as either authentic rediscovery (scroll hidden/lost during Manasseh's reign) or pious retrieval of previously suppressed text. Either way, the narrative presents it as discovery.
- **Huldah the prophetess** — one of the few named female prophets (with Miriam, Deborah, Noadiah, Isaiah's wife). Consulted in preference to Jeremiah (who was also active). No sense of unusualness.
- **Josiah's tenderness** — immediate repentance. Contrast with Ahab's partial repentance (1 Kgs 21). Yahweh postpones judgment beyond Josiah's lifetime.
- **"In peace"** — Josiah actually dies in battle at Megiddo (2 Kgs 23:29); "peace" here likely = before the national catastrophe, without seeing Jerusalem's destruction.
- **Temple-repair reform precedes Torah-discovery** — Yahweh rewards structural care of the house with covenantal renewal.
