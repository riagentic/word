# 2 Kings 1

[1] Moab rebelled against Israel after the death of Ahab.

[2] Ahaziah fell through the lattice in his upper chamber in Samaria, and lay sick. He sent messengers, saying to them, "Go, inquire of Baal-zebub the god of Ekron, whether I shall recover from this sickness."

[3] But the Messenger of Yahweh said to Elijah the Tishbite, "Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and say to them, 'Is it because there is no *Elohim* in Israel that you go to inquire of Baal-zebub the god of Ekron?'

[4] Now therefore thus says Yahweh: 'You shall not come down from the bed to which you have gone up, but shall surely die.'" Elijah departed.

[5] When the messengers turned back to him, he said to them, "Why have you turned back?"

[6] They said to him, "A man came up to meet us, and said to us, 'Go, return to the king who sent you, and say to him: Thus says Yahweh: Is it because there is no *Elohim* in Israel that you send to inquire of Baal-zebub the god of Ekron? Therefore you shall not come down from the bed to which you have gone up, but shall surely die.'"

[7] He said to them, "What sort of man was he who came up to meet you and told you these words?"

[8] They answered him, "A hairy man, with a leather belt around his waist." He said, "It is Elijah the Tishbite."

[9] Then the king sent to him a commander of fifty with his fifty. He went up to him, and behold, he was sitting on the top of a hill. He said to him, "O man of *Elohim*, the king has said, 'Come down.'"

[10] Elijah answered and said to the commander of fifty, "If I am a man of *Elohim*, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty." Fire came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty.

[11] Again he sent to him another commander of fifty with his fifty. He spoke and said to him, "O man of *Elohim*, thus says the king, 'Come down quickly.'"

[12] Elijah answered and said to them, "If I am a man of *Elohim*, let fire come down from heaven and consume you and your fifty." The fire of *Elohim* came down from heaven and consumed him and his fifty.

[13] Again he sent a third commander of fifty with his fifty. The third commander of fifty went up, and came and fell on his knees before Elijah, and implored him, saying, "O man of *Elohim*, please let my life, and the life of these fifty your servants, be precious in your eyes.

[14] Behold, fire came down from heaven and consumed the two former commanders of fifty with their fifties; but now let my life be precious in your eyes."

[15] The Messenger of Yahweh said to Elijah, "Go down with him; do not be afraid of him." So he arose and went down with him to the king.

[16] He said to him, "Thus says Yahweh: 'Because you have sent messengers to inquire of Baal-zebub the god of Ekron — is it because there is no *Elohim* in Israel whose word to inquire? — therefore you shall not come down from the bed to which you have gone up, but shall surely die.'"

[17] So he died according to the word of Yahweh that Elijah had spoken. Jehoram reigned in his place in the second year of Jehoram son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, because he had no son.

[18] The rest of the acts of Ahaziah that he did — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v3, 15) — *mal'ak*.
- **"captain of fifty" → "commander of fifty"** (v9, 11, 13, 14) — *sar-chamishim*.
- **"hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather" → "hairy man, with a leather belt around his waist"** (v8).
- **"Baal-zebub" — retained** (v2, 3, 6, 16) — Hebrew "Lord of the Flies." Possibly intentional deformation of "Baal-zebul" ("Prince Baal"); NT "Beelzebul" (Matt 10:25, 12:24) preserves the unparodied form.

### Notes
- **Ahaziah's fall** — mundane accident leads to fatal oracular misstep. He consults Philistine oracle rather than Yahweh.
- **Moab rebels** — Mesha Stela (ca. 840 BC) records this from Moabite side: Mesha throws off Israelite yoke. 2 Kgs 3 expands.
- **Elijah's garb** — hairy-and-belted becomes the prophetic uniform. John the Baptist wears the same (Matt 3:4, Mark 1:6), explicitly marking him as Elijah redivivus.
- **Fire from heaven** — echoes Carmel. Judgment for impertinent summoning of Yahweh's prophet.
- **Third commander spared** — contrast: humility vs command. Elijah's fire is not automatic; it responds to posture.
- **Jesus and this text** (Luke 9:54–55) — disciples invoke this precedent for calling down fire on Samaritan village; Jesus rebukes them. Contrast between prophetic era and gospel.
- **Jehoram/Joram of Israel** — Ahaziah's brother (no son to succeed). Last Omride; will fall to Jehu (2 Kgs 9).
