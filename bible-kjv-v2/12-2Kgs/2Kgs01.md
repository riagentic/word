# 2 Kings 1

[1] ⟦moab|Moav|Moab⟧ rebelled against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ after the death of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧.

[2] ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ fell through the lattice in his upper chamber in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and lay sick. He sent messengers, saying to them, "Go, inquire of ⟦baal-zebub|Baal Zevuv|Baal-zebub⟧ the god of ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧, whether I shall recover from this sickness."

[13] Again he sent a third commander of fifty with his fifty. [15] The Messenger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, "Go down with him; do not be afraid of him." So he arose and went down with him to the king.

[18] The rest of the acts of ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ that he did — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"angel" → "Messenger"** (v3, 15) — *mal'ak*.
- **"captain of fifty" → "commander of fifty"** (v9, 11, 13, 14) — *sar-chamishim*.
- **"hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather" → "hairy man, with a leather belt around his waist"** (v8).
- **"Baal-zebub" — retained** (v2, 3, 6, 16) — Hebrew "Lord of the Flies." Possibly intentional deformation of "Baal-zebul" ("Prince Baal"); NT "Beelzebul" (Matt 10:25, 12:24) preserves the unparodied form.

### Notes
- **Ahaziah's fall** — mundane accident leads to fatal oracular misstep. He consults Philistine oracle rather than Yahweh.
- **Moab rebels** — Mesha Stela (ca. 840 BC) records this from Moabite side: Mesha throws off Israelite yoke. 2 Kgs 3 expands.
- **Elijah's garb** — hairy-and-belted becomes the prophetic uniform. John the Baptist wears the same (Matt 3:4, Mark 1:6), explicitly marking him as Elijah redivivus.
- **Fire from heaven** — echoes Carmel. Judgment for impertinent summoning of Yahweh's prophet.
- **Third commander spared** — contrast: humility vs command. Elijah's fire is not automatic; it responds to posture.
- **Jesus and this text** (Luke 9:54–55) — disciples invoke this precedent for calling down fire on Samaritan village; Jesus rebukes them. Contrast between prophetic era and gospel.
- **Jehoram/Joram of Israel** — Ahaziah's brother (no son to succeed). Last Omride; will fall to Jehu (2 Kgs 9).
