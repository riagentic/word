## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v4, 10, 15, 16, 19, 20) — Hebrew *Elohim*. "The gods of the nations", "their gods", "no gods" (v12, 18) and "his god" (v37) are the same word. In v10 the king of Assyria says "your Elohim" of the God of Hezekiah.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Rab-shakeh"** (v4, 8) — a title, written without the article, as in chapter 18.
- **"rent his clothes" → "tore his garments"** (v1) — Hebrew קָרַע; בְּגָדִים. **"went into" → "came into"** — Hebrew וַיָּבֹא.
- **"covered with sackcloth"** (v2) — a participle, "covering themselves with the sackcloths".
- **"a day of trouble, and of rebuke, and blasphemy: for the children are come to the birth, and there is not strength to bring forth" → "A day of distress and of reproof and of spurning … sons have come to the place of breaking forth, and there is no power to bear"** (v3) — Hebrew צָרָה; תּוֹכֵחָה, from הוֹכִיחַ "reprove" (v4); נְאָצָה, from נִאֵץ "spurn": whether the day is one on which Judah is spurned or one on which Yahweh is spurned is not said. בָנִים, "sons"; מַשְׁבֵּר, "the place of breaking": the mouth of the womb; כֹּחַ; לְלֵדָה.
- **"It may be the LORD thy God will hear … and will reprove the words" → "Perhaps Yahweh your Elohim will hear … and will reprove the words"** (v4) — Hebrew אוּלַי; וְהוֹכִיחַ בַּדְּבָרִים, "and reprove for (or with) the words". "The living Elohim" is אֱלֹהִים חַי, without the article (v4, 16). **"lift up thy prayer for the remnant that are left" → "lift up a prayer for the remnant that is found"** — Hebrew הַנִּמְצָאָה.
- **"Be not afraid of the words … with which the servants of the king of Assyria have blasphemed me" → "Do not fear because of the words … with which the young men of the king of Assyria have reviled me"** (v6) — Hebrew נַעֲרֵי, "boys, attendants": a slighting word for the king's officers; גִּדֵּף, "revile".
- **"I will send a blast upon him, and he shall hear a rumour" → "I am placing a spirit in him, and he shall hear a report"** (v7) — Hebrew נֹתֵן בּוֹ רוּחַ: "giving a spirit in him"; רוּחַ is "spirit, wind, breath"; שְׁמוּעָה, "a thing heard".
- **"warring against Libnah: for he had heard that he was departed" → "fighting against Libnah, for he had heard that he had set out"** (v8) — Hebrew נִלְחָם; נָסַע.
- **"when he heard say of Tirhakah king of Ethiopia … he sent messengers again" → "he heard concerning Tirhakah king of Cush … And he returned and sent messengers"** (v9) — Hebrew כּוּשׁ; וַיָּשָׁב וַיִּשְׁלַח, "he returned and sent": he sent again.
- **"in whom thou trustest"** (v10) — a participle, "in whom you are trusting"; the word of 18:19–30.
- **"by destroying them utterly" → "devoting them to destruction"** (v11) — Hebrew לְהַחֲרִימָם.
- **"which my fathers have destroyed" → "which my fathers ruined"** (v12) — Hebrew שִׁחֲתוּ. **"the children of Eden which were in Thelasar" → "the sons of Eden who were in Telassar"**.
- **"Where is the king of Hamath"** (v13) — the Hebrew has אַיּוֹ, "where is he". The names Hena and Ivvah stand without "of" in the Hebrew.
- **"Hezekiah received the letter … and read it … and spread it" → "took the letters … and read them … and spread it out"** (v14) — Hebrew הַסְּפָרִים, plural, "the books, written sheets"; "read them" is plural and "spread it" singular, as in the Hebrew.
- **"O LORD God of Israel, which dwellest between the cherubims, thou art the God, even thou alone, of all the kingdoms of the earth" → "Yahweh, Elohim of Israel, who sits upon the cherubim, you are Elohim, you alone, for all the kingdoms of the earth"** (v15) — the Hebrew has no "O"; יֹשֵׁב הַכְּרֻבִים, "sitting (on) the cherubim", as 1 Sam 4:4; אַתָּה־הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים, "you are he, the Elohim": the article is in the Hebrew. **"heaven and earth" → "the heavens and the earth"**.
- **"bow down thine ear" → "Turn your ear"** (v16) — Hebrew הַטֵּה, "turn, incline". **"which hath sent him to reproach" → "who has sent him to reproach"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר שְׁלָחוֹ; "him" is the messenger; it can also be read "which he has sent".
- **"have destroyed the nations and their lands" → "have laid waste the nations and their land"** (v17) — Hebrew הֶחֱרִיבוּ, the verb of "dry up" in v24; "their land" is singular.
- **"have cast their gods into the fire … therefore they have destroyed them" → "have put their gods in the fire … and they did away with them"** (v18) — Hebrew וְנָתְנוּ, "and gave, placed"; וַיְאַבְּדוּם.
- **"that all the kingdoms of the earth may know that thou art the LORD God, even thou only" → "and let all the kingdoms of the earth know that you, Yahweh, are Elohim, you alone"** (v19) — Hebrew וְיֵדְעוּ. The last words can also be read "that you are Yahweh Elohim, you alone".
- **"That which thou hast prayed to me against Sennacherib … I have heard"** (v20) — Hebrew אֶל, "to, concerning".
- **"hath despised thee, and laughed thee to scorn … hath shaken her head at thee" → "has despised you, she has derided you … behind you she has shaken her head"** (v21) — Hebrew בָּזָה; לָעַג; אַחֲרֶיךָ, "behind you": as he goes away; הֵנִיעָה, "made sway".
- **"Whom hast thou reproached and blasphemed?" → "reproached and reviled"** (v22) — Hebrew חֵרֵף; גִּדֵּף. **"exalted thy voice"** → "raised your voice" — Hebrew הֲרִימוֹתָ.
- **"the Lord"** (v23) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי, not the name Yahweh. **"With the multitude of my chariots"** — the reading tradition (qere, בְּרֹב רִכְבִּי); the written text has "with the chariotry of my chariots" (ברכב רכבי). **"I am come up to the height of the mountains, to the sides of Lebanon, and will cut down the tall cedar trees thereof, and the choice fir trees thereof: and I will enter into the lodgings of his borders, and into the forest of his Carmel" → "I, I have gone up the height of the mountains, the remote parts of Lebanon; and I will cut down the height of its cedars, the choice of its cypresses; and I will enter its farthest lodging place, the forest of its garden land"** — Hebrew אֲנִי, the pronoun set out; יַרְכְּתֵי, "the flanks": the far recesses; קוֹמַת אֲרָזָיו, "the stature of its cedars"; בְּרוֹשׁ, a conifer whose kind is not certain; מְלוֹן קִצֹּה, "the lodging place of its end"; כַּרְמֶל, "garden land, orchard", the word that is also the name Carmel.
- **"I have digged and drunk strange waters, and with the sole of my feet have I dried up all the rivers of besieged places" → "I, I have dug and drunk strange waters, and I will dry up with the sole of my feet all the Niles of Matzor"** (v24) — Hebrew קַרְתִּי; מַיִם זָרִים; וְאַחְרִב, a future beside the past; יְאֹרֵי מָצוֹר: יְאֹר is the Nile and its arms, and מָצוֹר is a name of Egypt (Isa 19:6; Mic 7:12) that sounds like "siege, fortress"; the name is kept as printed. The word מִצְרַיִם, "Egypt", is not written here.
- **"Hast thou not heard long ago how I have done it, and of ancient times that I have formed it? now have I brought it to pass, that thou shouldest be to lay waste fenced cities into ruinous heaps" → "Have you not heard? From afar I made it, from days of old, and I formed it; now I have brought it, and you are to crash fortified cities into ruined heaps"** (v25) — Hebrew לְמֵרָחוֹק, "from far off" in time; עָשִׂיתִי; יְצַרְתִּיהָ; הֲבֵיאתִיהָ, "I have made it come"; וּתְהִי לַהְשׁוֹת, "and you are (or: and it is) for crashing into ruin"; גַּלִּים נִצִּים.
- **"their inhabitants were of small power, they were dismayed and confounded; they were as the grass of the field, and as the green herb, as the grass on the house tops, and as corn blasted before it be grown up" → "those who dwell in them, short of hand, were dismayed and ashamed; they became plants of the field and green vegetation, grass of the roofs, and a blight before the standing grain"** (v26) — Hebrew קִצְרֵי־יָד; חַתּוּ וַיֵּבֹשׁוּ; עֵשֶׂב שָׂדֶה וִירַק דֶּשֶׁא; חֲצִיר גַּגּוֹת; שְׁדֵפָה לִפְנֵי קָמָה, "a blighted thing before standing grain": the last words are difficult.
- **"I know thy abode, and thy going out, and thy coming in, and thy rage against me" → "your sitting and your going out and your coming in I know, and your raging against me"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew שִׁבְתְּךָ; הִתְרַגֶּזְךָ, from רָגַז "be agitated, tremble".
- **"thy tumult is come up into mine ears" → "your ease has come up into my ears"** (v28) — Hebrew שַׁאֲנַנְךָ, "your ease": the careless pride of one who feels secure. **"I will turn thee back" → "I will bring you back"** — Hebrew וַהֲשִׁבֹתִיךָ.
- **"Ye shall eat this year such things as grow of themselves, and in the second year that which springeth of the same" → "eat this year the aftergrowth, and in the second year what springs of itself"** (v29) — "for you" is singular, to Hezekiah. אָכוֹל is a bare infinitive, "eating"; סָפִיחַ, as Lev 25:5; סָחִישׁ is found only here (Isa 37:30 has שָׁחִיס) and its exact sense is uncertain: what grows of itself in the second year.
- **"the remnant that is escaped of the house of Judah shall yet again take root downward, and bear fruit upward" → "the escape of the house of Judah that is left shall add root downward and make fruit upward"** (v30, 31) — Hebrew פְּלֵיטָה, "escape": those who have escaped; הַנִּשְׁאָרָה, "that is left"; וְיָסְפָה … שֹׁרֶשׁ, "shall add root": take root again; עָשָׂה פְרִי. In v31 שְׁאֵרִית, "remnant", stands beside פְּלֵיטָה.
- **"the zeal of the LORD of hosts"** (v31) — "of Hosts" is read (qere) but not written in the Hebrew text. קִנְאָה is "jealousy, zeal".
- **"He shall not come into this city … nor come before it with shield, nor cast a bank against it" → "He shall not come to this city … and he shall not come to meet it with a shield, and he shall not pour out a mound against it"** (v32) — Hebrew אֶל; קִדֵּם; שָׁפַךְ סֹלְלָה: the earth of a siege mound is poured out.
- **"By the way that he came" → "By the way by which he comes"** (v33) — Hebrew יָבֹא. **"saith the LORD" → "declares Yahweh"** — Hebrew נְאֻם, "the declaration of".
- **"I will defend this city, to save it, for mine own sake" → "I will shield this city, to save it, on account of myself"** (v34) — Hebrew גָּנַן, the root of מָגֵן "shield" (v32); לְמַעֲנִי.
- **"the angel of the LORD went out, and smote … they were all dead corpses" → "the angel of Yahweh went out and struck down … all of them were dead carcasses"** (v35) — Hebrew מַלְאַךְ יְהוָה: the word is "messenger"; הִכָּה; פְּגָרִים מֵתִים. Who "rose early" is not said.
- **"departed, and went and returned, and dwelt at Nineveh" → "set out and went and returned, and he dwelt in Nineveh"** (v36) — three verbs, as in the Hebrew.
- **"as he was worshipping in the house of Nisroch his god … his sons smote him … the land of Armenia" → "as he was bowing down … his sons struck him down … the land of Ararat"** (v37) — Hebrew מִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה; אֲרָרָט. "His sons" is read (qere) but not written in the Hebrew text. No god Nisroch is known from elsewhere.

### Notes
- **The parallel** — this chapter stands almost word for word in Isaiah 37.
- **Isaiah** — named here for the first time in Kings.
- **Tirhakah king of Cush** (v9) — Taharqa, of the Cushite line that ruled Egypt.
- **The prayer of Hezekiah** (v15–19) — he answers the words of 18:33–35 and 19:10–13: the gods of the nations were the work of the hands of man.
- **The word concerning Sennacherib** (v21–28) — poetry. In v23–24 the king of Assyria is quoted; in v25–28 Yahweh speaks to him.
- **The sign** (v29) — two years of what grows of itself, and sowing in the third.
- **"On that night"** (v35) — how the hundred and eighty-five thousand died is not said.
- **The death of Sennacherib** (v37) — the verse does not say how long after his return it was.
