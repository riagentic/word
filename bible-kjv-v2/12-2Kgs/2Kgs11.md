# 2 Kings 11

[1] When Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the royal seed.

[2] But Jehosheba the daughter of King Joram, sister of Ahaziah, took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him from among the king's sons who were being killed. She put him and his nurse in the bedchamber; so they hid him from Athaliah, and he was not killed.

[3] He was with her, hidden in the house of Yahweh, six years while Athaliah reigned over the land.

[4] In the seventh year Jehoiada sent for the commanders of hundreds — of the Carites and of the runners — and brought them to him into the house of Yahweh. He made a covenant with them and put them under oath in the house of Yahweh, and showed them the king's son.

[5] He commanded them, saying, "This is what you shall do: a third of you who come in on the Sabbath shall be keepers of the watch of the king's house;

[6] a third shall be at the gate of Sur, and a third at the gate behind the runners; so you shall keep the watch of the house at the barriers.

[7] The two divisions of you who go off duty on the Sabbath shall keep the watch of the house of Yahweh around the king.

[8] You shall surround the king, each with his weapons in his hand; whoever comes within the ranks shall be killed. Be with the king when he goes out and when he comes in."

[9] The commanders of hundreds did according to all that Jehoiada the priest commanded; each took his men — those who were to come on duty on the Sabbath, along with those who were to go off duty on the Sabbath — and they came to Jehoiada the priest.

[10] The priest gave to the commanders of hundreds the spears and the shields that had belonged to King David, which were in the house of Yahweh.

[11] The runners stood, each with his weapons in his hand, around the king, from the right side of the house to the left side, by the altar and the house.

[12] Then he brought out the king's son, put the crown on him, and gave him the testimony — the covenant testimony. They made him king and anointed him; they clapped their hands and said, "Long live the king!"

[13] When Athaliah heard the noise of the runners and of the people, she came to the people in the house of Yahweh.

[14] She looked, and behold, the king was standing by the pillar, as the custom was; the officers and the trumpeters were by the king, and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing the shofar. Athaliah tore her clothes and cried, "Treason! Treason!"

[15] Jehoiada the priest commanded the commanders of hundreds who were set over the army, saying, "Bring her out between the ranks; whoever follows her, kill with the sword." For the priest said, "Let her not be killed in the house of Yahweh."

[16] So they laid hands on her, and she went by the way of the horses' entrance to the king's house; there she was killed.

[17] Jehoiada made a covenant between Yahweh and the king and the people, that they should be Yahweh's people; also between the king and the people.

[18] All the people of the land went to the house of Baal and broke it down; his altars and his images they thoroughly shattered, and they killed Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars. The priest set watch officers over the house of Yahweh.

[19] He took the commanders of hundreds, the Carites, the runners, and all the people of the land; they brought down the king from the house of Yahweh, coming by way of the gate of the runners to the king's house. He sat on the throne of the kings.

[20] All the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet. Athaliah they had put to death with the sword at the king's house.

[21] Jehoash was seven years old when he began to reign.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"captains and the guard" → "Carites and of the runners"** (v4, 19) — Hebrew *ha-kari we-ha-ratzim*; Carites (likely Carian mercenaries, foreign royal bodyguard) and runners (royal couriers/guard).
- **"guard" → "runners"** (v6, 11, 13, 19) — *ratzim* throughout.
- **"ranges" → "ranks"** (v8, 15) — *sederot*; ordered rows of soldiers.
- **"pillar" — retained** (v14) — *ammud*; likely Jachin or Boaz.
- **"God save the king" → "Long live the king"** (v12) — Hebrew *yechi ha-melek*; "may the king live."
- **"brake it down... brake they in pieces thoroughly" → "broke it down... thoroughly shattered"** (v18).

### Notes
- **Athaliah** — only woman to rule Judah; Ahab's daughter, Jezebel's daughter. Imports Omride/Baalist apostasy into Davidic line.
- **Near-extinction of David's line** — critical moment for messianic promise. Jehosheba's courageous rescue preserves the dynasty; a single infant carries the line.
- **Jehosheba** — daughter of Joram of Judah, wife of Jehoiada the priest (per 2 Chr 22:11). Unique priest-royal alliance.
- **Six-year hiding** — Joash concealed in temple complex, the very place Athaliah despised.
- **Jehoiada's coup** — meticulously planned: priest + royal guard + Carite mercenaries + people of the land all coordinated.
- **"Pillar" coronation** (v14) — king's ceremonial station at the temple court. 2 Kgs 23:3 shows Josiah at the same place.
- **Three-covenant structure** (v17) — Yahweh–king, Yahweh–people, king–people. Classical covenant-renewal form.
- **Baal temple demolition** — parallel to Jehu's purge (10:27) but this time in Judah. Political + religious reset.
- **"People of the land"** (*am ha-aretz*) — landowning free citizenry, critical political actor in Judean politics.
