# 2 Kings 11

[1] And ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ the mother of ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ saw that her son was dead, and she arose and did away with all the seed of the kingdom.

[2] And ⟦jehosheba|Yehosheva|Jehosheba⟧ the daughter of King ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧, the sister of ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧, took ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧ the son of ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ and stole him from the midst of the sons of the king who were being put to death, him and his nurse, in the chamber of the beds; and they concealed him from the face of ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧, and he was not put to death.

[3] And he was with her in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, hiding himself, six years, and ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ was reigning over the land.

[4] And in the seventh year ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ sent and took the commanders of the hundreds of the ⟦carites|Karim|Carites⟧ and of the runners, and he brought them to him, to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he cut a covenant with them and made them swear in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he showed them the son of the king.

[5] And he commanded them, saying, "This is the thing that you shall do: the third of you, who come in on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ and keep the watch of the house of the king,

[6] and the third at the gate Sur, and the third at the gate behind the runners; and you shall keep the watch of the house by turns.

[7] And the two parts among you, all who go out on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, they shall keep the watch of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ toward the king.

[8] And you shall encircle the king all around, each man with his weapons in his hand, and whoever comes into the ranks shall be put to death. And be with the king in his going out and in his coming in."

[9] And the commanders of the hundreds did according to all that ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest commanded, and they took each man his men, those coming in on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ with those going out on the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, and they came to ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest.

[10] And the priest gave to the commanders of the hundreds the spear and the shields that were King David's, which were in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] And the runners stood, each man with his weapons in his hand, from the right shoulder of the house to the left shoulder of the house, toward the altar and toward the house, around the king.

[12] And he brought out the son of the king and put on him the crown and the testimony, and they made him king and anointed him, and they clapped their hands and said, "May the king live!"

[13] And ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ heard the sound of the runners, the people, and she came to the people, to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] And she saw, and behold, the king was standing by the pillar, according to the ordinance, and the commanders and the trumpets were by the king, and all the people of the land were rejoicing and blowing on trumpets. And ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ tore her garments and called, "Conspiracy! Conspiracy!"

[15] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ the priest commanded the commanders of the hundreds, those appointed over the force, and said to them, "Bring her out to within the ranks, and whoever comes after her, put to death with the sword." For the priest said, "Let her not be put to death in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[16] And they put hands on her, and she came by the way of the entrance of the horses to the house of the king, and she was put to death there.

[17] And ⟦jehoiada|Yehoyada|Jehoiada⟧ cut the covenant between ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the king and the people, to be a people for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and between the king and the people.

[18] And all the people of the land came to the house of the Baal and broke it down; his altars and his images they broke in pieces thoroughly, and Mattan the priest of the Baal they killed before the altars. And the priest set oversight over the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[19] And he took the commanders of the hundreds and the ⟦carites|Karim|Carites⟧ and the runners and all the people of the land, and they brought the king down from the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and they came by the way of the gate of the runners to the house of the king, and he sat on the throne of the kings.

[20] And all the people of the land rejoiced, and the city was quiet; and ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ they had put to death with the sword at the house of the king.

[21] ⟦jehoash|Yehoash|Jehoash⟧ was seven years old when he began to reign.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"when Athaliah … saw" → "And Athaliah … saw"** (v1) — the qere רָאֲתָה "saw" is translated; the written form (ketiv) וראתה is "and she saw". **"destroyed all the seed royal" → "did away with all the seed of the kingdom"** — Hebrew וַתְּאַבֵּד (piel of אָבַד "perish": make perish); זֶרַע הַמַּמְלָכָה "the seed of the kingdom".
- **"and they hid him, even him and his nurse, in the bedchamber" → "him and his nurse, in the chamber of the beds; and they concealed him"** (v2) — the Hebrew has no verb before "him and his nurse" (2 Chr 22:11 has "and she put"); the words hang on "stole him" and are left so. חֲדַר הַמִּטּוֹת "the chamber of the beds": a bedroom, or a room where bedding was kept. **"which were slain" → "who were being put to death"** — qere הַמּוּמָתִים (hofal participle of מוּת); the ketiv ממותתים is another form of the same sense. מִפְּנֵי "from the face of".
- **"hid" → "hiding himself"** (v3) — Hebrew מִתְחַבֵּא (hitpael of חבא); in v2 the verb is הִסְתִּיר "conceal". "In the house of Yahweh" has no preposition in the Hebrew.
- **"the rulers over hundreds, with the captains and the guard" → "the commanders of the hundreds of the Carites and of the runners"** (v4, 19) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַמֵּאוֹת לַכָּרִי וְלָרָצִים; שַׂר is "prince", of an army "commander". הַכָּרִי "the Carite" is a collective singular: a body of guards, commonly taken as men from Caria or as the Cherethites of David's guard (2 Sam 20:23, where the ketiv has the same word); not certain. הָרָצִים "the runners" are the king's guard (1 Sam 22:17; 1 Kgs 14:27). The qere הַמֵּאוֹת is translated (v4, 9, 10, 15); the ketiv המאיות is another spelling.
- **"made a covenant with them, and took an oath of them" → "cut a covenant with them and made them swear"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיִּכְרֹת לָהֶם בְּרִית, literally "cut for them a covenant"; וַיַּשְׁבַּע hifil "made swear".
- **v5–7** — the orders are short and hard to follow; the Hebrew is given as it stands. v5 has no main verb: "the third of you, those coming in on the Shabbat and keepers of the watch of the house of the king". מִשְׁמֶרֶת "charge", of guards "watch". "The gate Sur" (v6) is not known; 2 Chr 23:5 has "the gate of the foundation" (שַׁעַר הַיְסוֹד).
- **"that it be not broken down" → "by turns"** (v6) — Hebrew מַסָּח, found only here; the meaning is not known. "By turns / alternately" is one common understanding; others are "as a defence" or a name of a place or building. Uncertain.
- **"two parts of all you" → "the two parts among you"** (v7) — Hebrew שְׁתֵּי הַיָּדוֹת בָּכֶם, literally "the two hands among you". **"about the king" → "toward the king"** — Hebrew אֶל־הַמֶּלֶךְ "to, toward the king": their watch in the house of Yahweh has the king as its object.
- **"compass the king round about" → "encircle the king all around"** (v8) — Hebrew וְהִקַּפְתֶּם עַל־הַמֶּלֶךְ סָבִיב. **"weapons"** (v8, 11) — Hebrew כֵּלָיו, literally "his articles / gear". **"ranges" → "ranks"** (v8, 15) — Hebrew שְׂדֵרוֹת, found only here, in 2 Chr 23:14 and in 1 Kgs 6:9 (of woodwork: "rows"); "ranks" of men is the usual understanding, not certain.
- **"king David's spears and shields" → "the spear and the shields that were King David's"** (v10) — Hebrew הַחֲנִית is singular (2 Chr 23:9 has the plural). שְׁלָטִים "shields": the exact sense of the word is not certain (also understood as "quivers").
- **"from the right corner of the temple to the left corner" → "from the right shoulder of the house to the left shoulder of the house"** (v11) — Hebrew כֶּתֶף "shoulder", the side of a building (1 Kgs 6:8); "right" and "left" for one who faces east are south and north. לַמִּזְבֵּחַ וְלַבָּיִת "toward the altar and toward the house".
- **"gave him the testimony" → "put on him the crown and the testimony"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיִּתֵּן עָלָיו אֶת־הַנֵּזֶר וְאֶת־הָעֵדוּת: one verb, "put on him", for both. נֵזֶר is the mark of one set apart, "crown" (Exod 29:6; 2 Sam 1:10). הָעֵדוּת "the testimony" is the word used of the tablets in the ark (Exod 25:16) and of Yahweh's testimonies; what was put on the king is not said (a written copy of the covenant or of the law, Deut 17:18, is commonly thought of). Some change the word to הַצְּעָדוֹת "the bracelets" (2 Sam 1:10): a conjecture, not in the verse.
- **"they made him king"** (v12) — Hebrew וַיַּמְלִכוּ אֹתוֹ, literally "made him reign". **"clapped their hands"** — Hebrew וַיַּכּוּ־כָף, literally "struck the palm".
- **"God save the king" → "May the king live!"** (v12) — Hebrew יְחִי הַמֶּלֶךְ, a wish; no "God" and no "long".
- **"the noise of the guard and of the people" → "the sound of the runners, the people"** (v13) — Hebrew קוֹל הָרָצִין הָעָם: the two nouns stand side by side with no "and" (רָצִין has the Aramaic-like plural ending). 2 Chr 23:12 has "the sound of the people, the runners and those praising the king". קוֹל is "voice", of a movement "sound".
- **"as the manner was" → "according to the ordinance"** (v14) — Hebrew כַּמִּשְׁפָּט; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", of a ruling "ordinance": the fixed rule or custom. **"by a pillar" → "by the pillar"** — Hebrew עַל־הָעַמּוּד, with the article, literally "upon the pillar" (also understood as a raised stand); 2 Chr 23:13 has "by his pillar at the entrance", and 2 Kgs 23:3 has the king at the same place.
- **"the princes and the trumpeters" → "the commanders and the trumpets"** (v14) — Hebrew הַשָּׂרִים "the princes / commanders" (the word of v4); הַחֲצֹצְרוֹת is "the trumpets", the instruments named for those who blow them. חֲצֹצְרָה is the straight metal trumpet, not the shofar.
- **"rent her clothes, and cried, Treason, Treason" → "tore her garments and called, 'Conspiracy! Conspiracy!'"** (v14) — Hebrew קֶשֶׁר, from קָשַׁר "bind, conspire".
- **"the officers of the host" → "those appointed over the force"** (v15) — Hebrew פְּקֻדֵי הַחַיִל, literally "the appointed ones of the force". **"Have her forth without the ranges" → "Bring her out to within the ranks"** — Hebrew אֶל־מִבֵּית לַשְּׂדֵרֹת, literally "to from-the-inside to the ranks": led out between the lines of men; uncertain. **"kill" → "put to death"** (v15, 16, 20) — Hebrew הֵמִית; "put to death" is an infinitive used as a command.
- **"they laid hands on her" → "they put hands on her"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיָּשִׂמוּ לָהּ יָדַיִם, literally "they put for her hands". The words are also understood "they made room for her" (יָד as "place, side"). Uncertain.
- **"made a covenant" → "cut the covenant"** (v17) — Hebrew אֶת־הַבְּרִית, with the article. **"that they should be the LORD's people" → "to be a people for Yahweh"** — Hebrew לִהְיוֹת לְעָם לַיהוָה.
- **"the house of Baal" → "the house of the Baal"** (v18) — Hebrew הַבַּעַל, with the article. **"brake they in pieces thoroughly"** — Hebrew שִׁבְּרוּ הֵיטֵב; הֵיטֵב is "well". **"slew" → "killed"** — Hebrew הָרַג. The qere מִזְבְּחֹתָיו "his altars" is translated; the ketiv is a shorter spelling.
- **"appointed officers over the house" → "set oversight over the house"** (v18) — Hebrew פְּקֻדּוֹת, the plural of פְּקֻדָּה "oversight": posts of oversight, that is, overseers.
- **"the city was in quiet"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁקָטָה "was quiet". **"they slew Athaliah with the sword beside the king's house" → "Athaliah they had put to death with the sword at the house of the king"** — the object stands first; the qere הַמֶּלֶךְ has the article; "at" is supplied (the Hebrew has no preposition, as in v16).
- **"Seven years old was Jehoash"** (v21) — Hebrew בֶּן־שֶׁבַע שָׁנִים, literally "a son of seven years". מָלַךְ "reign" is here of the beginning: "when he began to reign".

### Notes
- **Verse numbers** — in the Hebrew, v21 is 12:1, and chapter 12 runs one verse ahead of our numbers.
- **Names** — the Hebrew writes Athaliah both as עֲתַלְיָה and עֲתַלְיָהוּ, Ahaziah as אֲחַזְיָהוּ and אֲחַזְיָה, and the young king as יוֹאָשׁ (v2) and יְהוֹאָשׁ (v21); the tokens give one form for each name.
- **Athaliah** — only woman to rule Judah; called daughter of Ahab (8:18) and daughter of Omri (8:26). Imports Omride/Baalist apostasy into Davidic line.
- **Near-extinction of David's line** — critical moment for messianic promise. Jehosheba's courageous rescue preserves the dynasty; a single infant carries the line.
- **Jehosheba** — daughter of Joram of Judah, wife of Jehoiada the priest (per 2 Chr 22:11). Unique priest-royal alliance.
- **Six-year hiding** — Joash concealed in temple complex, the very place Athaliah despised.
- **Jehoiada's coup** — meticulously planned: priest + royal guard + Carites + people of the land all coordinated.
- **"Pillar" coronation** (v14) — king's ceremonial station at the temple court. 2 Kgs 23:3 shows Josiah at the same place.
- **Three-covenant structure** (v17) — Yahweh–king, Yahweh–people, king–people. Classical covenant-renewal form.
- **Baal temple demolition** — parallel to Jehu's purge (10:27) but this time in Judah. Political + religious reset.
- **"People of the land"** (*am ha-aretz*) — landowning free citizenry, critical political actor in Judean politics.
