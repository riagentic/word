# 2 Kings 25

[1] And it came to pass in the ninth year of his reign, in the tenth month, on the tenth of the month, that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ came, he and all his force, against ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and he camped against it, and they built against it a siege wall all around.

[2] And the city came into the siege, until the eleventh year of King ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧.

[3] On the ninth of the month the famine was strong in the city, and there was no bread for the people of the land.

[4] And the city was split open, and all the men of war, by night, by the way of the gate between the two walls, which was by the garden of the king — and the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ were against the city all around — and he went by the way of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧.

[5] And the force of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ pursued after the king and overtook him in the plains of ⟦jericho|Yericho|Jericho⟧, and all his force was scattered from him.

[6] And they seized the king and brought him up to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧, and they spoke judgment with him.

[7] And the sons of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ they slaughtered before his eyes, and the eyes of ⟦zedekiah|Tzidqiyahu|Zedekiah⟧ he blinded, and he bound him in bronze fetters and brought him to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[8] And in the fifth month, on the seventh of the month — it was the nineteenth year of King ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ — ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards, a servant of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[9] And he burned the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and the house of the king; and all the houses of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and every great house, he burned with fire.

[10] And the walls of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ all around, all the force of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ that was with the chief of the guards broke down.

[11] And the rest of the people who were left in the city, and the deserters who had fallen away to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and the rest of the multitude, ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards carried into exile.

[12] And of the poor of the land the chief of the guards left some, as vinedressers and as field workers.

[13] And the pillars of bronze that were in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and the bases and the sea of bronze that were in the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ broke in pieces, and they carried their bronze to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[14] And the pots and the shovels and the snuffers and the pans, and all the utensils of bronze with which they ministered, they took.

[15] And the firepans and the bowls, what was of gold as gold, and what was of silver as silver, the chief of the guards took.

[16] The two pillars, the one sea, and the bases that ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ had made for the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: there was no weight for the bronze of all these utensils.

[17] Eighteen cubits was the height of the one pillar, and a capital of bronze was upon it, and the height of the capital was three cubits, and a lattice and pomegranates were upon the capital all around, all of it bronze; and like these the second pillar had, upon the lattice.

[18] And the chief of the guards took ⟦seraiah|Serayah|Seraiah⟧ the head priest and ⟦zephaniah|Tzefanyahu|Zephaniah⟧ the second priest and the three keepers of the threshold.

[19] And from the city he took one eunuch who was overseer over the men of war, and five men of those who saw the face of the king, who were found in the city, and the scribe of the commander of the host, who mustered the people of the land, and sixty men of the people of the land who were found in the city.

[20] And ⟦nebuzaradan|Nevuzaradan|Nebuzaradan⟧ the chief of the guards took them and led them to the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧.

[21] And the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ struck them down and put them to death at ⟦riblah|Rivlah|Riblah⟧ in the land of ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧. And ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went into exile from upon its ground.

[22] And the people who were left in the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, whom ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ had left — he appointed over them ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ the son of ⟦ahikam|Achiqam|Ahikam⟧, the son of ⟦shaphan|Shafan|Shaphan⟧.

[23] And all the commanders of the forces, they and the men, heard that the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ had appointed ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧, and they came to ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ at the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧: ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyah|Nethaniah⟧ and ⟦johanan|Yochanan|Johanan⟧ the son of ⟦kareah|Qareach|Kareah⟧ and ⟦seraiah|Serayah|Seraiah⟧ the son of ⟦tanhumeth|Tanchumet|Tanhumeth⟧ the ⟦netophathite|Netofati|Netophathite⟧ and ⟦jaazaniah|Yaazanyahu|Jaazaniah⟧ the son of the ⟦maacathite|Maachati|Maacathite⟧, they and their men.

[24] And ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧ swore to them and to their men and said to them, "Do not fear the servants of the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧; dwell in the land and serve the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and it shall go well with you."

[25] And it came to pass in the seventh month that ⟦ishmael-other|Yishmael|Ishmael⟧ the son of ⟦nethaniah|Netanyah|Nethaniah⟧, the son of Elishama, of the seed of the kingship, came, and ten men with him, and they struck ⟦gedaliah|Gedalyahu|Gedaliah⟧, and he died, and the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧ who were with him at the ⟦mizpah|Mitzpah|Mizpah⟧.

[26] And all the people arose, from small to great, and the commanders of the forces, and they came to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, for they feared the ⟦chaldeans|Kasdim|Chaldeans⟧.

[27] And it came to pass in the thirty-seventh year of the exile of ⟦jehoiachin|Yehoyachin|Jehoiachin⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, in the twelfth month, on the twenty-seventh of the month, that Evil-merodach king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, in the year that he began to reign, lifted up the head of ⟦jehoiachin|Yehoyachin|Jehoiachin⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ from the house of confinement.

[28] And he spoke good things with him and set his throne above the throne of the kings who were with him in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[29] And he changed his garments of confinement, and he ate bread continually before him all the days of his life.

[30] And his allowance, a continual allowance, was given to him from the king, the matter of a day in its day, all the days of his life.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. This chapter stands almost word for word in Jer 52 (and v1–12 in Jer 39); it is translated here as the Hebrew of Kings prints it, without filling it out from Jeremiah.
- **"in the tenth day of the month" → "on the tenth of the month"** (v1, 3, 8, 27) — the Hebrew does not write "day". "Of his reign": Hebrew לְמָלְכוֹ "of his reigning", Zedekiah's.
- **"he, and all his host … pitched against it; and they built forts against it round about" → "he and all his force … camped against it, and they built against it a siege wall all around"** (v1) — Hebrew חַיִל "force" (also v5, 10); חָנָה "camp"; דָּיֵק, a siege work (a wall or towers; the exact sense is uncertain).
- **"the city was besieged" → "the city came into the siege"** (v2) — Hebrew וַתָּבֹא הָעִיר בַּמָּצוֹר, as 24:10.
- **"on the ninth day of the fourth month the famine prevailed" → "On the ninth of the month the famine was strong"** (v3) — the Hebrew of Kings has only בְּתִשְׁעָה לַחֹדֶשׁ "on the ninth of the month"; "in the fourth month" stands in Jer 52:6 and is not added here. חָזַק "be strong"; לֶחֶם "bread".
- **"the city was broken up, and all the men of war fled by night" → "the city was split open, and all the men of war, by night"** (v4) — Hebrew וַתִּבָּקַע "was split open". The Hebrew of Kings has no verb for "the men of war": the sentence is left as it stands (Jer 52:7 has "fled and went out from the city"). הַלַּיְלָה is "by night", literally "the night".
- **"and the king went the way toward the plain" → "and he went by the way of the Arabah"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ "and he went", singular, with no subject named (the king, v5); הָעֲרָבָה, the dry steppe of the Jordan valley. "The Chaldeans were against the city": Hebrew עַל.
- **"the army of the Chaldees … all his army were scattered from him" → "the force of the Chaldeans … all his force was scattered from him"** (v5) — Hebrew חֵיל; מֵעָלָיו, literally "from upon him". "The plains of Jericho": Hebrew עַרְבוֹת, the plural of "Arabah" in v4.
- **"they took the king … and they gave judgment upon him" → "they seized the king … and they spoke judgment with him"** (v6) — Hebrew תָּפַשׂ "seize"; וַיְדַבְּרוּ אִתּוֹ מִשְׁפָּט, a plural verb. "At Riblah": Hebrew רִבְלָתָה "to Riblah".
- **"they slew the sons of Zedekiah … and put out the eyes of Zedekiah, and bound him with fetters of brass, and carried him" → "the sons of Zedekiah they slaughtered … the eyes of Zedekiah he blinded, and he bound him in bronze fetters and brought him"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׁחַט "slaughter", plural; then עִוֵּר "blinded", וַיַּאַסְרֵהוּ, וַיְבִאֵהוּ, three singulars: "he", the king of Babylon. נְחֻשְׁתַּיִם is a dual, "two bronzes": a pair of bronze fetters.
- **"captain of the guard" → "the chief of the guards"** (v8, 10, 11, 12, 15, 18, 20) — Hebrew רַב־טַבָּחִים: רַב "great one, chief"; טַבָּחִים, literally "slaughterers" (butchers or executioners), the word for the royal guard (Gen 37:36, where the title has שַׂר). "Servant": Hebrew עֶבֶד, the word for "slave", here an officer of the king.
- **"and every great man's house burnt he with fire" → "and every great house, he burned with fire"** (v9) — Hebrew כָּל־בֵּית גָּדוֹל; it can also be read "every house of a great one".
- **"that were with the captain of the guard" → "that was with the chief of the guards"** (v10) — the Hebrew of Kings has אֲשֶׁר רַב־טַבָּחִים, literally "which the chief of the guards"; "with" stands in Jer 52:14 (אֶת) and is supplied here because English cannot do without it.
- **"the fugitives that fell away to the king of Babylon, with the remnant of the multitude … carry away" → "the deserters who had fallen away to the king of Babylon, and the rest of the multitude … carried into exile"** (v11) — Hebrew הַנֹּפְלִים אֲשֶׁר נָפְלוּ, literally "the fallers who had fallen"; יֶתֶר "rest" twice; הֶהָמוֹן "the multitude" (Jer 52:15 has another word); הֶגְלָה "carry into exile".
- **"left of the poor of the land to be vinedressers and husbandmen" → "of the poor of the land … left some, as vinedressers and as field workers"** (v12) — Hebrew מִדַּלַּת "of the poor of"; "some" is supplied. יֹגְבִים is found only here and in Jer 52:16; the meaning is uncertain (workers of the fields, plowmen).
- **"the pillars of brass … the bases, and the brasen sea … did the Chaldees break in pieces" → "the pillars of bronze … the bases and the sea of bronze … the Chaldeans broke in pieces"** (v13) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת "bronze"; מְכֹנוֹת "bases", as 1 Kgs 7:27; שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces". The first "in the house" has no preposition in the Hebrew.
- **"the pots, and the shovels, and the snuffers, and the spoons, and all the vessels of brass wherewith they ministered" → "the pots and the shovels and the snuffers and the pans, and all the utensils of bronze with which they ministered"** (v14) — Hebrew סִירֹת, יָעִים, מְזַמְּרוֹת, כַּפּוֹת (literally "palms": shallow bowls), the names of 1 Kgs 7:45, 50; כֵּלִים "utensils".
- **"the firepans, and the bowls, and such things as were of gold, in gold, and of silver, in silver" → "the firepans and the bowls, what was of gold as gold, and what was of silver as silver"** (v15) — Hebrew מַחְתּוֹת, מִזְרָקוֹת; אֲשֶׁר זָהָב זָהָב וַאֲשֶׁר־כֶּסֶף כָּסֶף, literally "what was gold, gold, and what was silver, silver".
- **"the brass of all these vessels was without weight" → "there was no weight for the bronze of all these utensils"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא־הָיָה מִשְׁקָל.
- **"the chapiter upon it was brass: and the height of the chapiter three cubits; and the wreathen work, and pomegranates … and like unto these had the second pillar with wreathen work" → "a capital of bronze was upon it, and the height of the capital was three cubits, and a lattice and pomegranates … and like these the second pillar had, upon the lattice"** (v17) — Hebrew כֹּתֶרֶת "capital"; שְׂבָכָה "lattice", as 1 Kgs 7:17; עַל־הַשְּׂבָכָה "upon the lattice". "Three cubits" is the figure of Kings here; 1 Kgs 7:16 and Jer 52:22 have five cubits. The figure is kept as printed. For "cubits" the qere has the plural אַמּוֹת, the ketiv the singular אמה.
- **"the chief priest … the second priest, and the three keepers of the door" → "the head priest … the second priest and the three keepers of the threshold"** (v18) — Hebrew כֹּהֵן הָרֹאשׁ, literally "the priest of the head" (22:4 has "the great priest"); כֹּהֵן מִשְׁנֶה "priest of the second [rank]" (23:4); שֹׁמְרֵי הַסַּף.
- **"an officer that was set over the men of war, and five men of them that were in the king's presence … and the principal scribe of the host, which mustered the people" → "one eunuch who was overseer over the men of war, and five men of those who saw the face of the king … and the scribe of the commander of the host, who mustered the people"** (v19) — Hebrew סָרִיס "eunuch", also used of a court officer; פָּקִיד "overseer"; מֵרֹאֵי פְנֵי־הַמֶּלֶךְ, those admitted to the king; הַסֹּפֵר שַׂר הַצָּבָא, with the article on "scribe": it can also be read "the scribe, the commander of the host" (Jer 52:25 has no article, and "seven men" for "five"). הַמַּצְבִּא is the causative of "muster".
- **"brought them to the king of Babylon to Riblah" → "led them to the king of Babylon at Riblah"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיֹּלֶךְ, the causative of "go"; עַל, here for "to".
- **"smote them, and slew them" → "struck them down and put them to death"** (v21) — Hebrew הִכָּה; הֵמִית. **"So Judah was carried away out of their land" → "And Judah went into exile from upon its ground"** — Hebrew וַיִּגֶל, the simple stem, "went into exile"; מֵעַל אַדְמָתוֹ.
- **"over them he made Gedaliah … ruler" → "he appointed over them Gedaliah"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew הִפְקִיד "appoint"; "ruler", "governor" is not written.
- **"the captains of the armies, they and their men" → "the commanders of the forces, they and the men"** (v23, 26) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַחֲיָלִים; in v23 first הָאֲנָשִׁים "the men", then at the end וְאַנְשֵׁיהֶם "and their men". "The Mizpah" (v23, 25): the Hebrew has the article, "the Watchtower". "Nethaniah": Hebrew נְתַנְיָה.
- **"Fear not to be the servants of the Chaldees" → "Do not fear the servants of the Chaldeans"** (v24) — Hebrew אַל־תִּירְאוּ מֵעַבְדֵי הַכַּשְׂדִּים, literally "do not fear from the servants of the Chaldeans": the Chaldean officers. "To be servants of" is the reading of Jer 40:9 (מֵעֲבוֹד "from serving"), not of Kings. **"it shall be well with you" → "it shall go well with you"** — Hebrew וְיִטַב לָכֶם.
- **"of the seed royal, came, and ten men with him, and smote Gedaliah, that he died" → "of the seed of the kingship, came, and ten men with him, and they struck Gedaliah, and he died"** (v25) — Hebrew זֶרַע הַמְּלוּכָה; הִכָּה "strike"; וַיָּמֹת "and he died". "And the Jews and the Chaldeans" continues the object of "struck".
- **"both small and great … for they were afraid of the Chaldees" → "from small to great … for they feared the Chaldeans"** (v26) — Hebrew מִקָּטֹן וְעַד־גָּדוֹל; יָרְאוּ מִפְּנֵי, literally "feared from the face of".
- **"of the captivity of Jehoiachin … in the year that he began to reign did lift up the head of Jehoiachin king of Judah out of prison" → "of the exile of Jehoiachin … in the year that he began to reign, lifted up the head of Jehoiachin king of Judah from the house of confinement"** (v27) — Hebrew גָּלוּת "exile"; בִּשְׁנַת מָלְכוֹ, literally "in the year of his reigning"; נָשָׂא אֶת־רֹאשׁ "lift up the head of" (Gen 40:13), kept; בֵּית כֶּלֶא, literally "a house of keeping back". "The twenty-seventh" is the figure of Kings; Jer 52:31 has the twenty-fifth. **"Evil-merodach"** — Hebrew אֱוִיל מְרֹדַךְ, the Babylonian Amel-Marduk.
- **"he spake kindly to him, and set his throne above the throne of the kings" → "he spoke good things with him and set his throne above the throne of the kings"** (v28) — Hebrew וַיְדַבֵּר אִתּוֹ טֹבוֹת; מֵעַל כִּסֵּא, with "throne" in the singular.
- **"changed his prison garments: and he did eat bread continually before him" → "he changed his garments of confinement, and he ate bread continually before him"** (v29) — Hebrew וְשִׁנָּא "and he changed", with no subject named (Jehoiachin, or the king for him); בִּגְדֵי כִלְאוֹ; תָּמִיד "continually".
- **"his allowance was a continual allowance given him of the king, a daily rate for every day" → "his allowance, a continual allowance, was given to him from the king, the matter of a day in its day"** (v30) — Hebrew אֲרֻחָה, a fixed portion of food; דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ (Exod 5:13).

### Notes
- **Siege 588-586 BC** — from the tenth month of the ninth year (v1) to the eleventh year (v2); the breach falls in the summer of 586. The month of the breach is named in Jer 52:6, not here.
- **Zedekiah's fate** — sons killed before his eyes (last image he ever sees), then blinded. Typical Babylonian treatment of oath-breakers.
- **Riblah** — Nebuchadnezzar's forward headquarters in central Syria.
- **Nebuzaradan** — comes in the fifth month (v8), after the breach: orderly imperial dismantling.
- **Temple destruction (586 BC)** — Solomon's temple, built ca. 960 BC. Stood ~374 years.
- **Pillars Jachin and Boaz** destroyed; sea and bases broken up. Same items itemized in 1 Kgs 7. End of Solomonic era.
- **Gedaliah's administration at Mizpah** — Jer 40-41 parallel, with more detail. Jeremiah counseled submission; Ishmael's assassination ends it within months.
- **Egypt flight** — Jer 42-44: Jeremiah taken along against his will; final prophecies against Judahite community in Egypt.
- **Jehoiachin's release (561 BC)** — Babylonian ration-tablets record his provisions. Closing note of hope: Davidic line still exists, still eats at a king's table. Kings ends in exile but not in despair — the lamp still burns, however faintly.
