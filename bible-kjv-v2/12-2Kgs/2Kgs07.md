# 2 Kings 7

[1] Then Elisha said, "Hear the word of Yahweh. Thus says Yahweh: 'Tomorrow about this time a se'ah of fine flour shall be sold for a shekel, and two se'ahs of barley for a shekel, at the gate of Samaria.'"

[2] The officer on whose hand the king leaned answered the man of *Elohim* and said, "Behold, if Yahweh should make windows in heaven, could this thing be?" He said, "Behold, you shall see it with your eyes, but shall not eat of it."

[3] There were four men with skin disease at the entrance of the gate; they said to one another, "Why do we sit here until we die?

[4] If we say, 'Let us enter the city,' the famine is in the city and we shall die there; if we sit here, we also die. Now come, let us defect to the camp of the Arameans; if they spare us alive, we shall live; if they kill us, we shall but die."

[5] They rose up at twilight to go to the camp of the Arameans; when they came to the outer edge of the camp of Aram, behold, there was no one there.

[6] For the Lord had made the army of the Arameans hear a noise of chariots, a noise of horses — the noise of a great army — and they said to one another, "Behold, the king of Israel has hired against us the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Egypt to come upon us."

[7] So they arose and fled at twilight; they left their tents, their horses, and their donkeys — the camp as it was — and fled for their lives.

[8] When these men with skin disease came to the outer edge of the camp, they entered one tent, ate and drank, carried away silver, gold, and clothing, went and hid it; they returned and entered another tent, carried away from there also, went and hid it.

[9] Then they said to one another, "We are not doing right. This day is a day of good news, and we are keeping silent; if we wait until morning light, punishment will fall on us. Now come, let us go and tell the king's household."

[10] So they came and called to the gatekeepers of the city, and told them, "We came to the camp of the Arameans, and behold, there was no one there, no human voice — only horses tied up and donkeys tied up, and the tents as they were."

[11] The gatekeepers called out, and it was reported within the king's house.

[12] The king arose in the night and said to his servants, "Let me tell you what the Arameans have done to us. They know that we are hungry; so they have gone out of the camp to hide themselves in the field, saying, 'When they come out of the city, we shall catch them alive and enter the city.'"

[13] One of his servants answered, "Let some take, please, five of the horses that remain in the city — behold, they will fare like all the multitude of Israel that remain in it, like all the multitude of Israel that have perished. Let us send and see."

[14] So they took two chariots with horses; the king sent after the army of the Arameans, saying, "Go and see."

[15] They went after them as far as the Jordan; behold, all the way was full of garments and gear that the Arameans had cast away in their haste. The messengers returned and told the king.

[16] The people went out and plundered the camp of the Arameans. So a se'ah of fine flour was sold for a shekel, and two se'ahs of barley for a shekel, according to the word of Yahweh.

[17] The king appointed the officer on whose hand he leaned to have charge of the gate; the people trampled him at the gate, and he died, as the man of *Elohim* had said, who spoke when the king came down to him.

[18] It happened as the man of *Elohim* had spoken to the king, saying, "Two se'ahs of barley for a shekel and a se'ah of fine flour for a shekel shall tomorrow about this time be at the gate of Samaria";

[19] and that officer answered the man of *Elohim* and said, "Now behold, if Yahweh should make windows in heaven, could such a thing be?" He said, "Behold, you shall see it with your eyes, but shall not eat of it."

[20] So it fell out to him: the people trampled him in the gate, and he died.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"lord" → "officer"** (v2, 17, 19) — *shalish*; "third man" / high officer.
- **"porter" → "gatekeeper"** (v10, 11).
- **"Syria / Syrians" → "Aram / Arameans"** (throughout).
- **"raiment" → "clothing"** (v8).
- **"vessels" → "gear"** (v15) — *kelim*; equipment.

### Notes
- **Prophetic price forecast** — normal fluctuation to siege-crisis shekel-per-quarter-cab of dung. Elisha predicts sudden crash in prices.
- **Officer's sneer** — "windows in heaven" mocks. Gets literal judgment: dies by collapse of city-gate order he thought impossible.
- **Four lepers** — the unclean excluded from the city become its saviors. Reversal motif.
- **Sound of a great army** — Yahweh auditory-miracles Aramean rout. "Hittites and Egyptians" = northern and southern superpowers; their imagined pincer breaks Aramean nerve.
- **"Not doing right" (v9)** — missiological echo: good news must not be hoarded. Early church used this line for gospel proclamation.
- **"You shall see but not eat"** — exact inversion-fulfillment; the officer sees the plenty and is trampled to death by the rush.
