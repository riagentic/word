# 2 Kings 8

[1] And Elisha had spoken to the woman whose son he had made alive, saying, "Arise and go, you and your house, and sojourn wherever you sojourn; for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has called for the famine, and it is also coming to the land, seven years."

[2] And the woman arose and did according to the word of the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and she went, she and her house, and sojourned in the land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ seven years.

[3] And it came to pass at the end of seven years that the woman returned from the land of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and she went out to cry out to the king for her house and for her field.

[4] And the king was speaking to ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧, the young man of the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, saying, "Recount to me, please, all the great things that Elisha has done."

[5] And it came to pass, as he was recounting to the king how he had made the dead alive, that behold, the woman whose son he had made alive was crying out to the king for her house and for her field. And ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧ said, "My lord the king, this is the woman, and this is her son, whom Elisha made alive."

[6] And the king asked the woman, and she recounted to him. And the king gave her one eunuch, saying, "Return all that is hers, and all the produce of the field from the day she left the land until now."

[7] And Elisha came to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, and ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ was sick. And it was told him, saying, "The man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has come as far as here."

[8] And the king said to ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧, "Take in your hand an offering, and go to meet the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from him, saying, 'Shall I live from this sickness?'"

[9] And ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ went to meet him, and he took an offering in his hand, and all the good of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧, the load of forty camels. And he came and stood before him and said, "Your son ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ has sent me to you, saying, 'Shall I live from this sickness?'"

[10] And Elisha said to him, "Go, say to him, 'You shall surely live'; and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has shown me that he shall surely die."

[11] And he made his face stand still and set it, until he was ashamed; and the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ wept.

[12] And ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ said, "Why is my lord weeping?" And he said, "Because I know the evil that you will do to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: their fortresses you will set on fire, and their young men you will kill with the sword, and their children you will dash in pieces, and their women with child you will split open."

[13] And ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ said, "But what is your servant, the dog, that he should do this great thing?" And Elisha said, "⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has shown me you as king over ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧."

[14] And he went from Elisha and came to his lord, and he said to him, "What did Elisha say to you?" And he said, "He said to me, 'You shall surely live.'"

[15] And it came to pass on the morrow that he took the cover and dipped it in the water and spread it over his face, and he died. And ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ reigned in his place.

[16] And in the fifth year of ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — and ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ — ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ the son of ⟦jehoshaphat|Yehoshafat|Jehoshaphat⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ began to reign.

[17] He was thirty-two years old when he began to reign, and eight years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[18] And he walked in the way of the kings of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, as the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ had done, for the daughter of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ had become his wife; and he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[19] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was not willing to ruin ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, on account of David his servant, as he had said to him, to give him a lamp for his sons all the days.

[20] In his days Edom revolted from under the hand of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and they made a king reign over them.

[21] And ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ passed over to ⟦zair|Tzair|Zair⟧, and all the chariots with him. And it came to pass that he arose by night and struck Edom, who was surrounding him, and the commanders of the chariots; and the people fled to their tents.

[22] And Edom has revolted from under the hand of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to this day. Then ⟦libnah|Livnah|Libnah⟧ revolted, at that time.

[23] And the rest of the acts of ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?

[24] And ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ lay with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of David, and ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ his son reigned in his place.

[25] In the twelfth year of ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ began to reign.

[26] ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and one year he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦athaliah-queen|Atalyahu|Athaliah⟧ the daughter of Omri king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[27] And he walked in the way of the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ and did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, like the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, for he was a son-in-law of the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧.

[28] And he went with ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ to the battle with ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ at ⟦ramoth-gilead|Ramot Gilad|Ramoth-gilead⟧, and the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ struck ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧.

[29] And ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ the king returned to be healed in ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧ from the wounds with which the ⟦arameans|Arammim|Syrians⟧ had struck him at Ramah, when he fought with ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧ king of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧. And ⟦ahaziah|Achazyahu|Ahaziah⟧ the son of ⟦jehoram|Yehoram|Jehoram⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ went down to see ⟦joram|Yoram|Joram⟧ the son of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ in ⟦jezreel|Yizreel|Jezreel⟧, for he was sick.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; "the man of Elohim" has the article.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Syria" → "Aram"; "the Syrians" → "the Arameans"** (v7–13, 28, 29) — Hebrew אֲרָם; in v28, 29 the Hebrew has the plural אֲרַמִּים, "Arameans".
- **"whose son he had restored to life" → "whose son he had made alive"** (v1, 5) — Hebrew הֶחֱיָה, "make live": the verb of "keep alive" in 7:4.
- **"thou and thine household, and sojourn wheresoever thou canst sojourn" → "you and your house, and sojourn wherever you sojourn"** (v1, 2) — Hebrew בֵּיתֵךְ; וְגוּרִי בַּאֲשֶׁר תָּגוּרִי, "sojourn where you will sojourn": "can" is not written. "You" follows the reading tradition (qere); the written form is an older spelling of the same word.
- **"the LORD hath called for a famine; and it shall also come upon the land seven years" → "Yahweh has called for the famine, and it is also coming to the land, seven years"** (v1) — Hebrew קָרָא לָרָעָב, "called to the famine"; בָּא can be "has come" or "is coming".
- **"she went forth to cry unto the king for her house and for her land" → "to cry out to the king for her house and for her field"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew צָעַק אֶל; שָׂדֶה, "field".
- **"the king talked with Gehazi the servant of the man of God, saying, Tell me" → "the king was speaking to Gehazi, the young man of the man of Elohim, saying, Recount to me"** (v4–6) — Hebrew נַעַר, "boy, young man", of an attendant; סִפֵּר, "recount".
- **"how he had restored a dead body to life" → "how he had made the dead alive"** (v5) — Hebrew אֶת־הַמֵּת, "the dead one".
- **"the king appointed unto her a certain officer, saying, Restore all that was hers" → "the king gave her one eunuch, saying, Return all that is hers"** (v6) — Hebrew סָרִיס, "eunuch"; the word is also used of a court officer, whether or not he is castrated. הָשֵׁיב, "return, give back".
- **"The man of God is come hither" → "has come as far as here"** (v7) — Hebrew עַד־הֵנָּה.
- **"Take a present in thine hand … and enquire of the LORD by him, saying, Shall I recover of this disease?" → "Take in your hand an offering … and seek Yahweh from him, saying, Shall I live from this sickness?"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew מִנְחָה, the word of the grain offering, here a gift of honor; דָּרַשׁ אֶת־יְהוָה מֵאוֹתוֹ, "seek Yahweh from with him": through him (as 1 Kgs 22:7, 8); הַאֶחְיֶה מֵחֳלִי זֶה, "shall I live from this sickness": come out of it alive.
- **"even of every good thing of Damascus, forty camels' burden" → "and all the good of Damascus, the load of forty camels"** (v9) — Hebrew וְכָל־טוּב דַּמֶּשֶׂק מַשָּׂא אַרְבָּעִים גָּמָל.
- **"Go, say unto him, Thou mayest certainly recover: howbeit the LORD hath shewed me that he shall surely die" → "Go, say to him, You shall surely live; and Yahweh has shown me that he shall surely die"** (v10, 14) — the verse follows the reading tradition (qere) לוֹ, "to him". The written text (ketiv) has לֹא, "not": "Go, say, You shall surely not live", or "Go, say: Not! …". With the qere, Elisha gives Hazael an answer for the king that differs from what Yahweh has shown him; whether the words mean "of this sickness you would live" (he dies by another hand, v15) is not said. חָיֹה תִחְיֶה and מוֹת יָמוּת are each a verb with its own infinitive: literally "living you shall live", "dying he shall die". The Hebrew has "and", not "howbeit".
- **"he settled his countenance stedfastly, until he was ashamed" → "he made his face stand still and set it, until he was ashamed"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲמֵד אֶת־פָּנָיו וַיָּשֶׂם עַד־בֹּשׁ, "he made his face stand and set [it] until being ashamed" (as Judg 3:25). The Hebrew does not say who stared and who was ashamed: Elisha stares until Hazael is ashamed, or until it grows shameful.
- **"their strong holds wilt thou set on fire, and their young men wilt thou slay with the sword, and wilt dash their children, and rip up their women with child" → "their fortresses you will set on fire, and their young men you will kill with the sword, and their children you will dash in pieces, and their women with child you will split open"** (v12) — Hebrew תְּשַׁלַּח בָּאֵשׁ, "send into the fire"; רִטֵּשׁ; בִּקֵּעַ, "split". "Sons of Israel", not "children".
- **"But what, is thy servant a dog, that he should do this great thing?" → "But what is your servant, the dog, that he should do this great thing?"** (v13) — Hebrew כִּי מָה עַבְדְּךָ הַכֶּלֶב: "the dog" is what he calls himself (as 2 Sam 9:8); he is not asking whether he is one. **"that thou shalt be king over Syria" → "has shown me you as king over Aram"** — Hebrew הִרְאַנִי יְהוָה אֹתְךָ מֶלֶךְ.
- **"his master" → "his lord"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָיו.
- **"he took a thick cloth, and dipped it in water, and spread it on his face, so that he died" → "he took the cover and dipped it in the water and spread it over his face, and he died"** (v15) — Hebrew הַמַּכְבֵּר, found only here: something woven or netted, a cover; its exact meaning is uncertain. The Hebrew does not name the one who took it, and does not say "so that".
- **"Jehoshaphat being then king of Judah" → "and Jehoshaphat king of Judah"** (v16) — the Hebrew has only וִיהוֹשָׁפָט מֶלֶךְ יְהוּדָה, "and Jehoshaphat king of Judah", with no verb; the words are hard and are often held to be a copyist's slip. **"began to reign"** (v16, 25) — the Hebrew verb is מָלַךְ, "reigned", here of the beginning. In this chapter the Hebrew writes יוֹרָם (Joram) and יְהוֹרָם (Jehoram) for both kings; each is given as it is written.
- **"Thirty and two years old was he" → "He was thirty-two years old"** (v17, 26) — literally "a son of thirty-two years".
- **"for the daughter of Ahab was his wife: and he did evil in the sight of the LORD" → "for the daughter of Ahab had become his wife; and he did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v18, 27) — Hebrew הָיְתָה־לּוֹ לְאִשָּׁה; הָרַע, with the article.
- **"Yet the LORD would not destroy Judah for David his servant's sake, as he promised him to give him alway a light, and to his children" → "And Yahweh was not willing to ruin Judah, on account of David his servant, as he had said to him, to give him a lamp for his sons all the days"** (v19) — Hebrew הַשְׁחִית, "ruin"; אָמַר, "said"; נִיר, "lamp" (1 Kgs 11:36; 15:4), a word whose sense is not certain ("lamp", or "dominion"); the Hebrew has לְבָנָיו, "for his sons", with no "and" (2 Chr 21:7 has "and for his sons").
- **"Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and made a king over themselves" → "Edom revolted from under the hand of Judah, and they made a king reign over them"** (v20, 22) — Hebrew פָּשַׁע, the verb of פֶּשַׁע "transgression", as 1 Kgs 12:19: to break away from a lord ("revolted", as the KJV here); "rebel" is kept for מָרַד / מָרָה. הִמְלִיךְ מֶלֶךְ, "make a king reign".
- **"So Joram went over to Zair, and all the chariots with him: and he rose by night, and smote the Edomites which compassed him about, and the captains of the chariots: and the people fled into their tents" → "And Joram passed over to Zair … and struck Edom, who was surrounding him, and the commanders of the chariots; and the people fled to their tents"** (v21) — Hebrew אֱדוֹם, "Edom". The verse is hard: "the people" who flee to their tents seem to be Joram's own, so that he breaks out through Edom and his host goes home. Zair is not known.
- **"Then Libnah revolted at the same time" → "Then Libnah revolted, at that time"** (v22) — Hebrew אָז … בָּעֵת הַהִיא, both expressions.
- **"the rest of the acts of Joram … are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?"** (v23) — literally "the words / matters of Joram … upon the book of the matters of the days".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v24) — Hebrew שָׁכַב.
- **"In the twelfth year"** (v25) — Hebrew "in the year of twelve years".
- **"Athaliah, the daughter of Omri" — kept** (v26) — Hebrew בַּת־עָמְרִי, "daughter of Omri". By v18 she is of the house of Ahab, Omri's son; בַּת can also name a granddaughter, but the verse gives the word as written.
- **"for he was the son in law of the house of Ahab" → "a son-in-law of the house of Ahab"** (v27) — Hebrew חֲתַן, one who is joined to a family by marriage.
- **"to the war against Hazael … and the Syrians wounded Joram" → "to the battle with Hazael … and the Arameans struck Joram"** (v28, 29) — Hebrew הִכָּה, "strike"; in v29 מִן־הַמַּכִּים אֲשֶׁר יַכֻּהוּ, "from the strokes with which they struck him". **"at Ramah"** — Hebrew בָּרָמָה, "at the Ramah" ("the height"): Ramoth-gilead. **"because he was sick"** — Hebrew חֹלֶה, here of a wounded man.

### Notes
- **The woman of Shunem** — her cry reaches the king as Gehazi is telling of her (4:8–37).
- **Gehazi** — here with the king, after 5:27; the order of the stories in time is not stated.
- **"Your son" Ben-hadad** — the king of Aram speaks to the prophet as a son to a father.
- **Hazael** — 1 Kgs 19:15. The Hebrew of v15 does not say who took the cover; Hazael reigns after him.
- **Edom and Libnah** — Judah loses both.
- **Two kings named Joram / Jehoram** — one in Israel (son of Ahab), one in Judah (son of Jehoshaphat); and Ahaziah of Judah bears the name of Ahaziah of Israel (1 Kgs 22:51).
