# 2 Kings 21

[1] ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ was twelve years old when he began to reign, and fifty-five years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦hephzibah|Cheftzi-Vah|Hephzibah⟧.

[2] And he did the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, according to the abominations of the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ dispossessed from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[3] And he built again the high places that ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ his father had done away with, and he raised up altars for the Baal and made an Asherah, as ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had done, and he bowed down to all the host of the heavens and served them.

[5] And he built altars for all the host of the heavens in the two courts of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And he made his son pass through the fire, and he practiced soothsaying and divined, and he set up a ghost and knowing spirits; he did much of the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to vex him.

[7] And he put the carved image of the Asherah that he had made in the house of which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had said to David and to ⟦solomon|Shlomo|Solomon⟧ his son, "In this house and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, I will put my name forever.

[8] And I will not again make the foot of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ wander from the ground that I gave to their fathers, only if they keep to do according to all that I have commanded them, and as to all the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ that my servant ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded them."

[9] And they did not hear, and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ caused them to wander, to do the evil more than the nations that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ destroyed from before the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[10] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke by the hand of his servants the prophets, saying,

[11] "Because ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ has done these abominations — he has done evil more than all that the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ did who was before him — and has caused ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ also to sin with his idols,

[12] therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'Behold, I am bringing evil upon ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, at which both ears of everyone who hears it shall tingle.

[13] And I will stretch out over ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ the line of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and the plummet of the house of ⟦ahab|Achav|Ahab⟧, and I will wipe ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ as one wipes the dish: he has wiped it and turned it upon its face.

[14] And I will abandon the remnant of my inheritance and give them into the hand of their enemies, and they shall become plunder and pillage to all their enemies,

[15] because they have done the evil in my eyes and have been vexing me, from the day that their fathers came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and to this day.'"

[16] And also innocent blood ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ shed, very much, until he had filled ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ from end to end, besides his sin with which he caused ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ to sin, to do the evil in the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[17] And the rest of the acts of ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧, and all that he did, and his sin that he sinned, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?

[18] And ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ lay with his fathers and was buried in the garden of his house, in the garden of Uzza, and Amon his son reigned in his place.

[19] Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and two years he reigned in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. And the name of his mother was ⟦meshullemeth|Meshullemet|Meshullemeth⟧ the daughter of ⟦haruz|Charutz|Haruz⟧, from ⟦jotbah|Yotvah|Jotbah⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign"** (v1, 19) — Hebrew בֶּן־שְׁתֵּים עֶשְׂרֵה שָׁנָה … בְמָלְכוֹ, literally "a son of twelve years was Manasseh at his reigning". The verb מָלַךְ is "reign"; with the age at accession it tells the beginning of the reign.
- **"did that which was evil in the sight of the LORD" → "did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh"** (v2, 6, 15, 16, 20) — Hebrew הָרַע "the evil", with the article; בְּעֵינֵי "in the eyes of".
- **"heathen, whom the LORD cast out before the children of Israel" → "nations that Yahweh dispossessed from before the sons of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew גּוֹיִם; הוֹרִישׁ "dispossess"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of"; בְּנֵי "sons of".
- **"he built up again" → "he built again"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיָּשָׁב וַיִּבֶן, literally "and he returned and built". **"had destroyed" → "had done away with"** — Hebrew אִבַּד, literally "made perish". **"for Baal" → "for the Baal"** — the Hebrew has the article.
- **"made a grove" → "made an Asherah"** (v3, 7) — Hebrew אֲשֵׁרָה, the wooden object of worship bearing the goddess's name; in v7 פֶּסֶל הָאֲשֵׁרָה "the carved image of the Asherah". "Pole" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"the host of heaven" → "the host of the heavens"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew שָׁמַיִם is plural.
- **"And he built altars"** (v4) — Hebrew וּבָנָה, a perfect with "and" where the narrative form is expected (so also וְהֶעֱבִיר, וְעוֹנֵן, וְעָשָׂה in v6); translated as simple past.
- **"observed times, and used enchantments, and dealt with familiar spirits and wizards" → "practiced soothsaying and divined, and he set up a ghost and knowing spirits"** (v6) — Hebrew עוֹנֵן (exact sense unknown) and נִחֵשׁ "divine, seek omens", as Deut 18:10. עָשָׂה אוֹב וְיִדְּעֹנִים is literally "made a ghost and knowing spirits": the verb is "make / do", here of providing or appointing the means of asking the dead, so "set up" ("made a ghost" would say he created one); the two nouns name the spirits (Lev 20:27), and their exact sense is uncertain ("mediums and wizards", "familiar spirits" are other renderings).
- **"he wrought much wickedness in the sight of the LORD, to provoke him to anger" → "he did much of the evil in the eyes of Yahweh, to vex him"** (v6) — Hebrew הִרְבָּה לַעֲשׂוֹת הָרַע, literally "he made much the doing of the evil"; לְהַכְעִיס "to vex": "him" is supplied, and "anger" is not a second word in the Hebrew.
- **"a graven image of the grove that he had made" → "the carved image of the Asherah that he had made"** (v7) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל "carved image". **"will I put my name"** (v4, 7) — Hebrew שִׂים "put".
- **"Neither will I make the feet of Israel move any more out of the land" → "And I will not again make the foot of Israel wander from the ground"** (v8) — Hebrew לֹא אֹסִיף לְהָנִיד, literally "I will not add to make wander" (נוּד "wander", Gen 4:12); רֶגֶל "foot", singular; אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"only if they will observe to do … and according to all the law" → "only if they keep to do … and as to all the Torah"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁמַר לַעֲשׂוֹת "keep to do"; וּלְכָל־הַתּוֹרָה with לְ "to, as to", where the clause before has כְּ "according to".
- **"But they hearkened not: and Manasseh seduced them to do more evil than did the nations" → "And they did not hear, and Manasseh caused them to wander, to do the evil more than the nations"** (v9) — Hebrew שָׁמַע "hear"; הִתְעָה, the causative of "wander, go astray"; הָרָע "the evil".
- **"by his servants the prophets" → "by the hand of his servants the prophets"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּיַד.
- **"hath done wickedly above all that the Amorites did, which were before him, and hath made Judah also to sin" → "he has done evil more than all that the Amorite did who was before him — and has caused Judah also to sin"** (v11) — Hebrew הֵרַע, the verb of "evil"; הָאֱמֹרִי "the Amorite", a collective singular; הֶחֱטִיא, the causative of "sin". "Idols": Hebrew גִּלּוּלִים, a word of contempt whose derivation is uncertain (also v21).
- **"such evil … that whosoever heareth of it, both his ears shall tingle" → "evil … at which both ears of everyone who hears it shall tingle"** (v12) — Hebrew רָעָה "evil" ("such" is not written); the reading tradition (qere) has שֹׁמְעָהּ "who hears it", the written text (ketiv) שמעיו "its hearers"; the sense is the same. As 1 Sam 3:11.
- **"the line of Samaria, and the plummet of the house of Ahab" → "the line of Samaria and the plummet of the house of Ahab"** (v13) — Hebrew קָו "line", the builder's measuring line; מִשְׁקֹלֶת, a weight on a cord (a level or plummet). נָטָה "stretch out".
- **"as a man wipeth a dish, wiping it, and turning it upside down" → "as one wipes the dish: he has wiped it and turned it upon its face"** (v13) — Hebrew מָחָה "wipe", the verb of "wipe out" (Gen 6:7); the last two verbs are pointed as perfects, מָחָה וְהָפַךְ "he has wiped and turned"; עַל־פָּנֶיהָ "upon its face". "It" is supplied twice.
- **"I will forsake the remnant of mine inheritance, and deliver them … a prey and a spoil" → "I will abandon the remnant of my inheritance and give them … plunder and pillage"** (v14) — Hebrew נָטַשׁ "abandon, leave to itself"; נָתַן "give"; בַּז "plunder"; מְשִׁסָּה, from שָׁסָה "pillage".
- **"have provoked me to anger" → "have been vexing me"** (v15) — Hebrew וַיִּהְיוּ מַכְעִסִים, the participle with "be": a lasting course.
- **"till he had filled Jerusalem from one end to another" → "until he had filled Jerusalem from end to end"** (v16) — Hebrew פֶּה לָפֶה, literally "mouth to mouth" (so 10:21). "Innocent": Hebrew נָקִי "clear, free of guilt".
- **"the rest of the acts … in the book of the chronicles"** (v17, 25) — Hebrew וְיֶתֶר דִּבְרֵי … עַל־סֵפֶר דִּבְרֵי הַיָּמִים, literally "the rest of the words / matters of … upon the book of the matters of the days".
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v18) — Hebrew שָׁכַב "lie, lie down".
- **"his servants … slew the king in his own house" → "put the king to death in his house"** (v23) — Hebrew הֵמִית; "own" is not written. "Servants": Hebrew עֲבָדִים, the word for "slaves", here the king's men.
- **"slew all them that had conspired" → "struck down all who had conspired"** (v24) — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike", where killing is meant. "Made … king": Hebrew הִמְלִיךְ, literally "made reign".
- **"he was buried in his sepulchre" → "one buried him in his burial place"** (v26) — Hebrew וַיִּקְבֹּר אֹתוֹ, an active verb in the singular with no subject named: "he buried him", that is, "one buried him". Some read a plural or a passive (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. קְבֻרָה "burial place".

### Notes
- **Manasseh** — longest reign in Judah (55 years, ca. 697-642 BC). Assyrian vassal under Esarhaddon and Ashurbanipal; attested in Assyrian annals.
- **Chronicles parallel** (2 Chr 33) records Manasseh's temporary exile to Babylon and repentance; Kings omits this entirely — theological choice to emphasize Manasseh as the reason for exile.
- **Temple pollution** — altars in both courts, the carved image of the Asherah in the house itself. Maximum syncretism under royal sponsorship.
- **"Ears shall tingle"** (v12) — idiom of shocking news; echoed 1 Sam 3:11, Jer 19:3.
- **Measuring with Samaria's line** — Yahweh applies same standard of judgment to Judah that destroyed the North.
- **Innocent blood** — rabbinic tradition says Manasseh killed Isaiah (sawn in two, Heb 11:37 alludes).
- **Garden of Uzza** — likely burial grounds south/east of palace. Break with Davidic royal tombs.
- **Amon's coup** — short reign; servants' assassination probably court faction, not religious. People of the land reassert Davidic line by making Josiah king.
- **Exile foretold** — even Josiah's reform (ch 22-23) cannot undo Manasseh's guilt (v14).
