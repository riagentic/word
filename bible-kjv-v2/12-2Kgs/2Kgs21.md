# 2 Kings 21

[1] Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Hephzibah.

[2] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, according to the abominations of the nations whom Yahweh cast out before the children of Israel.

[3] He rebuilt the high places that Hezekiah his father had destroyed; he raised up altars for Baal, made an Asherah pole as Ahab king of Israel had done, bowed down to all the host of heaven, and served them.

[4] He built altars in the house of Yahweh, of which Yahweh had said, "In Jerusalem I will put my name."

[5] He built altars for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of Yahweh.

[6] He made his son pass through the fire, practiced soothsaying, used omens, and dealt with mediums and spiritists. He did much evil in the eyes of Yahweh, provoking him to anger.

[7] He set the carved image of the Asherah pole that he had made in the house of which Yahweh had said to David and to Solomon his son, "In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, I will put my name forever.

[8] I will not make the foot of Israel wander any more from the land that I gave to their fathers, if only they will take care to do according to all that I have commanded them, and according to all the Law that my servant Moses commanded them."

[9] But they did not listen; Manasseh led them astray to do more evil than the nations whom Yahweh destroyed before the children of Israel.

[10] Yahweh spoke by his servants the prophets, saying,

[11] "Because Manasseh king of Judah has done these abominations — acting more wickedly than all that the Amorites who were before him did — and has made Judah also to sin with his idols —

[12] therefore thus says Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel: 'Behold, I am bringing such evil upon Jerusalem and Judah that the ears of everyone who hears of it will tingle.

[13] I will stretch over Jerusalem the measuring line of Samaria and the plumb line of the house of Ahab; I will wipe Jerusalem as one wipes a dish, wiping it and turning it upside down.

[14] I will forsake the remnant of my inheritance and deliver them into the hand of their enemies; they shall become prey and spoil to all their enemies,

[15] because they have done what is evil in my eyes and have provoked me to anger, from the day their fathers came out of Egypt until this day.'"

[16] Moreover, Manasseh shed innocent blood in great quantity, until he had filled Jerusalem from end to end, besides the sin by which he made Judah to sin in doing what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh.

[17] The rest of the acts of Manasseh, and all that he did, and the sin that he sinned — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

[18] Manasseh slept with his fathers and was buried in the garden of his own house, the Garden of Uzza; Amon his son reigned in his place.

[19] Amon was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem. His mother's name was Meshullemeth, daughter of Haruz of Jotbah.

[20] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, as Manasseh his father had done.

[21] He walked in all the way that his father had walked, and served the idols that his father had served, and bowed down to them.

[22] He forsook Yahweh the *Elohim* of his fathers, and did not walk in the way of Yahweh.

[23] The servants of Amon conspired against him and killed the king in his own house.

[24] The people of the land struck down all who had conspired against King Amon; the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his place.

[25] The rest of the acts of Amon that he did — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah?

[26] He was buried in his tomb in the Garden of Uzza; Josiah his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v2, 9) — *goyim*.
- **"observed times, and used enchantments" → "practiced soothsaying, used omens"** (v6) — *onen ve-nichesh*.
- **"line of Samaria... plummet of the house of Ahab" → "measuring line... plumb line"** (v13) — *qav... mishqelet*; construction metaphors used for destruction.
- **"seduced" → "led astray"** (v9) — *yat'em*.

### Notes
- **Manasseh** — longest reign in Judah (55 years, ca. 697-642 BC). Assyrian vassal under Esarhaddon and Ashurbanipal; attested in Assyrian annals.
- **Chronicles parallel** (2 Chr 33) records Manasseh's temporary exile to Babylon and repentance; Kings omits this entirely — theological choice to emphasize Manasseh as the reason for exile.
- **Temple pollution** — altars in both courts, asherah image in sanctuary. Maximum syncretism under royal sponsorship.
- **"Ears shall tingle"** (v12) — idiom of shocking news; echoed 1 Sam 3:11, Jer 19:3.
- **Measuring with Samaria's line** — Yahweh applies same standard of judgment to Judah that destroyed the North.
- **Innocent blood** — rabbinic tradition says Manasseh killed Isaiah (sawn in two, Heb 11:37 alludes).
- **Garden of Uzza** — likely burial grounds south/east of palace. Break with Davidic royal tombs.
- **Amon's coup** — short reign; servants' assassination probably court faction, not religious. People of the land reassert Davidic line by making Josiah king.
- **Exile foretold** — even Josiah's reform (ch 22-23) cannot undo Manasseh's guilt (v14).
