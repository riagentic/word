## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v2, 9) — *goyim*.
- **"observed times, and used enchantments" → "practiced soothsaying, used omens"** (v6) — *onen ve-nichesh*.
- **"line of Samaria... plummet of the house of Ahab" → "measuring line... plumb line"** (v13) — *qav... mishqelet*; construction metaphors used for destruction.
- **"seduced" → "led astray"** (v9) — *yat'em*.

### Notes
- **Manasseh** — longest reign in Judah (55 years, ca. 697-642 BC). Assyrian vassal under Esarhaddon and Ashurbanipal; attested in Assyrian annals.
- **Chronicles parallel** (2 Chr 33) records Manasseh's temporary exile to Babylon and repentance; Kings omits this entirely — theological choice to emphasize Manasseh as the reason for exile.
- **Temple pollution** — altars in both courts, asherah image in sanctuary. Maximum syncretism under royal sponsorship.
- **"Ears shall tingle"** (v12) — idiom of shocking news; echoed 1 Sam 3:11, Jer 19:3.
- **Measuring with Samaria's line** — Yahweh applies same standard of judgment to Judah that destroyed the North.
- **Innocent blood** — rabbinic tradition says Manasseh killed Isaiah (sawn in two, Heb 11:37 alludes).
- **Garden of Uzza** — likely burial grounds south/east of palace. Break with Davidic royal tombs.
- **Amon's coup** — short reign; servants' assassination probably court faction, not religious. People of the land reassert Davidic line by making Josiah king.
- **Exile foretold** — even Josiah's reform (ch 22-23) cannot undo Manasseh's guilt (v14).
