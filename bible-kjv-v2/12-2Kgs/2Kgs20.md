# 2 Kings 20

[1] In those days ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ became sick, to the point of death. And ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the son of ⟦amoz|Amotz|Amoz⟧, the prophet, came to him and said to him, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: Give command to your house, for you are dying, and you shall not live."

[2] And he turned his face around to the wall and prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, saying,

[3] "Ah, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, remember, please, how I have walked before you in truth and with a whole heart, and what is good in your eyes I have done." And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ wept a great weeping.

[4] And it came to pass, ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ had not gone out of the middle court, and the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to him, saying,

[5] "Return, and you shall say to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ the leader of my people: Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of David your father: I have heard your prayer; I have seen your tears. Behold, I am healing you; on the third day you shall go up to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] And I will add to your days fifteen years, and from the palm of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ I will deliver you and this city, and I will shield this city on account of myself and on account of David my servant."

[7] And ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ said, "Take a cake of figs." And they took it and put it on the boil, and he lived.

[8] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said to ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧, "What is the sign that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will heal me, and I shall go up on the third day to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"

[9] And ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ said, "This is the sign for you from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will do the thing that he has spoken: the shadow has gone ten steps; shall it return ten steps?"

[10] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said, "It is a light thing for the shadow to decline ten steps. No, but let the shadow return backward ten steps."

[11] And ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet called to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he brought back the shadow on the steps by which it had gone down, on the steps of ⟦ahaz|Achaz|Ahaz⟧, backward ten steps.

[12] At that time Berodach-baladan the son of Baladan, king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, sent letters and an offering to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, for he had heard that ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ had been sick.

[13] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ listened to them, and he showed them all his treasure house, the silver and the gold and the spices and the good oil, and the house of his gear, and all that was found in his treasuries. There was not a thing that ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ did not show them in his house and in all his rule.

[14] And ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ the prophet came to King ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ and said to him, "What did these men say, and from where do they come to you?" And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said, "From a far land they have come, from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧."

[15] And he said, "What have they seen in your house?" And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said, "All that is in my house they have seen; there was not a thing that I did not show them in my treasuries."

[16] And ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ said to ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, "Hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

[17] Behold, days are coming, and all that is in your house, and what your fathers have treasured up to this day, shall be carried to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧; not a thing shall remain, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] And of your sons who shall come out from you, whom you shall beget, they shall take, and they shall be eunuchs in the palace of the king of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧."

[19] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ said to ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧, "Good is the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that you have spoken." And he said, "Is it not so, if there shall be ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and truth in my days?"

[20] And the rest of the acts of ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧, and all his might, and how he made the pool and the conduit and brought the water into the city, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧?

[21] And ⟦hezekiah|Chizqiyahu|Hezekiah⟧ lay with his fathers, and ⟦manasseh|Menashe|Manasseh⟧ his son reigned in his place.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"was Hezekiah sick unto death … Set thine house in order; for thou shalt die, and not live" → "became sick, to the point of death … Give command to your house, for you are dying, and you shall not live"** (v1) — Hebrew חָלָה … לָמוּת, "was sick to die"; צַו לְבֵיתֶךָ, "command for your house", as 2 Sam 17:23; מֵת אַתָּה, a participle.
- **"he turned his face to the wall" → "he turned his face around to the wall"** (v2) — Hebrew וַיַּסֵּב, as 1 Kgs 21:4.
- **"I beseech thee, O LORD, remember now how I have walked before thee in truth and with a perfect heart … And Hezekiah wept sore" → "Ah, Yahweh, remember, please, how I have walked before you in truth and with a whole heart … wept a great weeping"** (v3) — Hebrew אָנָּה; נָא; הִתְהַלַּכְתִּי, "walked about"; בֶּאֱמֶת; בְלֵבָב שָׁלֵם; בְּכִי גָדוֹל.
- **"afore Isaiah was gone out into the middle court" → "Isaiah had not gone out of the middle court"** (v4) — "court" is the reading tradition (qere, חָצֵר); the written text has "the middle city" (העיר). **"the word of the LORD came to him"** — kept.
- **"Turn again, and tell Hezekiah the captain of my people" → "Return, and you shall say to Hezekiah the leader of my people"** (v5) — Hebrew שׁוּב; נְגִיד. **"I will heal thee" → "I am healing you"** — Hebrew הִנְנִי רֹפֶא לָךְ, a participle. "Your tears" is singular in the Hebrew.
- **"out of the hand of the king of Assyria; and I will defend this city for mine own sake" → "from the palm of the king of Assyria … and I will shield this city on account of myself"** (v6) — Hebrew כַּף; גָּנַן עַל (19:34 has אֶל); לְמַעֲנִי.
- **"Take a lump of figs. And they took and laid it on the boil, and he recovered" → "Take a cake of figs. And they took it and put it on the boil, and he lived"** (v7) — Hebrew דְּבֶלֶת תְּאֵנִים, pressed figs; "take" is plural; שְׁחִין; וַיֶּחִי, the verb of v1.
- **"What shall be the sign"** (v8) — the Hebrew has no verb: "What is the sign".
- **"shall the shadow go forward ten degrees, or go back ten degrees?" → "the shadow has gone ten steps; shall it return ten steps?"** (v9) — Hebrew הָלַךְ הַצֵּל, a past form, "the shadow has gone"; אִם־יָשׁוּב, "or shall it return" (the second half of a question with two sides). The verse is commonly read as a choice, "shall the shadow go forward … or go back", and Hezekiah's answer in v10 takes it so. מַעֲלוֹת is "steps", the word of a stairway (Exod 20:26).
- **"Hezekiah answered"** (v10) — the name is written יְחִזְקִיָּהוּ here. **"to go down ten degrees: nay, but let the shadow return backward" → "to decline ten steps. No, but let the shadow return backward"** — Hebrew לִנְטוֹת, "to stretch, decline", as the day declines (Judg 19:8).
- **"cried unto the LORD: and he brought the shadow ten degrees backward, by which it had gone down in the dial of Ahaz" → "called to Yahweh, and he brought back the shadow on the steps by which it had gone down, on the steps of Ahaz, backward ten steps"** (v11) — Hebrew קָרָא; "dial" is not written: the Hebrew has מַעֲלוֹת אָחָז, "the steps of Ahaz". "It had gone down" is feminine in the Hebrew, though "shadow" is masculine; Isa 38:8 names the sun.
- **"Berodach-baladan"** (v12) — the Hebrew of Kings writes בְּרֹאדַךְ; Isa 39:1 has מְרֹדַךְ, "Merodach". The form of Kings is kept. **"letters and a present" → "letters and an offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה.
- **"Hezekiah hearkened unto them" → "listened to them"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁמַע עֲלֵיהֶם, "heard concerning them" or "listened to them"; Isa 39:2 has וַיִּשְׂמַח, "was glad". **"all the house of his precious things … the precious ointment, and all the house of his armour … in all his dominion" → "all his treasure house … the good oil, and the house of his gear … in all his rule"** — Hebrew בֵּית נְכֹתֹה, found only here and in Isaiah: "treasure house" is the best-supported sense and is uncertain; שֶׁמֶן הַטּוֹב; בֵּית כֵּלָיו, "the house of his vessels, gear, weapons": a storehouse or armory; מֶמְשַׁלְתּוֹ.
- **"from whence came they unto thee?" → "from where do they come to you?"** (v14) — Hebrew יָבֹאוּ.
- **"there is nothing among my treasures that I have not shewed them" → "there was not a thing that I did not show them in my treasuries"** (v15) — Hebrew לֹא־הָיָה דָבָר.
- **"that which thy fathers have laid up in store … nothing shall be left, saith the LORD" → "what your fathers have treasured up … not a thing shall remain, says Yahweh"** (v17) — Hebrew אָצְרוּ, the root of "treasury" (v13, 15); יִוָּתֵר; אָמַר, "says", not נְאֻם. וְנִשָּׂא is "shall be lifted, carried".
- **"thy sons that shall issue from thee … shall they take away" → "your sons who shall come out from you … they shall take"** (v18) — "they shall take" is the reading tradition (qere); the written text has "he shall take". **"eunuchs"** — Hebrew סָרִיסִים, also used of court officers.
- **"Is it not good, if peace and truth be in my days?" → "Is it not so, if there shall be shalom and truth in my days?"** (v19) — Hebrew הֲלוֹא אִם־שָׁלוֹם וֶאֱמֶת יִהְיֶה בְיָמָי: "good" is not repeated in the question; "so" is supplied. Whether he speaks in thankfulness or in unconcern is not said.
- **"the rest of the acts … the book of the chronicles"** (v20) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי, "the words, matters of Hezekiah … the book of the matters of the days". **"how he made a pool, and a conduit" → "how he made the pool and the conduit"** — with the article; the words of 18:17.
- **"slept with his fathers" → "lay with his fathers"** (v21) — Hebrew שָׁכַב.

### Notes
- **The parallel** — v1–11 stand in a shorter form in Isaiah 38 (which adds the writing of Hezekiah, Isa 38:9–20); v12–19 stand almost word for word in Isaiah 39.
- **"In those days"** (v1) — v6 still looks forward to deliverance from the king of Assyria.
- **The steps of Ahaz** (v11) — steps on which a shadow moved with the sun. How the sign came about is not said.
- **Berodach-baladan** (v12) — Marduk-apla-iddina, a king of Babylon known from Assyrian records.
- **The word of v17–18** — Babylon, not Assyria, will carry off the treasures; compare 24:13; 25:13–17; Dan 1:3.
- **The pool and the conduit** (v20) — compare 2 Chr 32:30. A rock-cut water tunnel in Jerusalem with an old Hebrew inscription is commonly held to be this work.
- **Manasseh** — his reign follows in chapter 21.
