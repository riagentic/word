# 2 Kings 4

[1] And one woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried out to Elisha, saying, "Your servant, my husband, is dead, and you know that your servant was fearing ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and the creditor has come to take my two children for himself as slaves."

[2] And Elisha said to her, "What shall I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?" And she said, "Your maidservant has not anything in the house, except a flask of oil."

[3] And he said, "Go, ask for yourself vessels from outside, from all your neighbors, empty vessels; do not make them few.

[4] And you shall go in and shut the door behind you and behind your sons, and you shall pour into all these vessels, and the full one you shall set aside."

[5] And she went from him and shut the door behind her and behind her sons; they were bringing near to her, and she was pouring.

[6] And it came to pass, when the vessels were full, that she said to her son, "Bring near to me another vessel." And he said to her, "There is not another vessel." And the oil stood.

[7] And she came and told the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he said, "Go, sell the oil and repay your debt, and you and your sons shall live on what remains."

[8] And the day came, and Elisha passed over to Shunem, and there was a great woman there, and she took hold of him to eat bread. And it came to pass, as often as he passed over, that he would turn aside there to eat bread.

[9] And she said to her husband, "Behold, please, I know that he is a holy man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who passes by us continually.

[10] Let us make, please, a small upper room with a wall, and let us put for him there a bed and a table and a seat and a lampstand, and it shall be, when he comes to us, that he shall turn aside there."

[11] And the day came, and he came there, and he turned aside to the upper room and lay down there.

[12] And he said to ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧ his young man, "Call this ⟦shunammite|Shunammit|Shunammite⟧." And he called her, and she stood before him.

[13] And he said to him, "Say, please, to her, 'Behold, you have trembled for us with all this trembling; what is to be done for you? Is there a word to speak for you to the king or to the commander of the host?'" And she said, "In the midst of my people I am dwelling."

[14] And he said, "And what is to be done for her?" And ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧ said, "Truly, she has no son, and her husband is old."

[15] And he said, "Call her." And he called her, and she stood in the entrance.

[16] And he said, "At this appointed time, about this time next year, you will be embracing a son." And she said, "No, my lord, man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not lie to your maidservant."

[17] And the woman conceived and bore a son at this appointed time, about this time next year, of which Elisha had spoken to her.

[18] And the child grew. And the day came, and he went out to his father, to the reapers.

[19] And he said to his father, "My head, my head!" And he said to the young man, "Carry him to his mother."

[20] And he carried him and brought him to his mother, and he sat on her knees until the noon, and he died.

[21] And she went up and laid him on the bed of the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and shut the door behind him and went out.

[22] And she called to her husband and said, "Send me, please, one of the young men and one of the she-donkeys, and let me run to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and return."

[23] And he said, "Why are you going to him today? It is not new moon and not ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧." And she said, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧."

[24] And she saddled the she-donkey and said to her young man, "Drive and go; do not keep me back in riding unless I say so to you."

[25] And she went and came to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to Mount ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧. And it came to pass, when the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ saw her opposite, that he said to ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧ his young man, "Behold, that ⟦shunammite|Shunammit|Shunammite⟧!

[26] Now run, please, to meet her and say to her, 'Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with you? Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with your husband? Is it ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ with the child?'" And she said, "⟦shalom|Shalom|Peace⟧."

[27] And she came to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to the mountain, and took hold of his feet. And ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧ drew near to push her away, and the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ said, "Let her be, for her soul is bitter to her, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has hidden it from me and has not told me."

[28] And she said, "Did I ask a son from my lord? Did I not say, 'Do not mislead me'?"

[29] And he said to ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧, "Gird your loins and take my staff in your hand and go. If you find a man, you shall not bless him, and if a man blesses you, you shall not answer him. And you shall put my staff on the face of the boy."

[30] And the mother of the boy said, "As ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ lives and by the life of your soul, I will not forsake you." And he arose and went after her.

[31] And ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧ had passed on before them and put the staff on the face of the boy, and there was no voice and there was no attention. And he returned to meet him and told him, saying, "The boy has not awakened."

[32] And Elisha came into the house, and behold, the boy was dead, laid on his bed.

[33] And he went in and shut the door behind the two of them and prayed to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[34] And he went up and lay upon the child and put his mouth on his mouth and his eyes on his eyes and his palms on his palms, and he crouched upon him, and the flesh of the child grew warm.

[35] And he returned and went in the house, once this way and once that way, and he went up and crouched upon him. And the boy sneezed as many as seven times, and the boy opened his eyes.

[36] And he called to ⟦gehazi|Gechazi|Gehazi⟧ and said, "Call this ⟦shunammite|Shunammit|Shunammite⟧." And he called her, and she came to him, and he said, "Take up your son."

[37] And she came and fell at his feet and bowed down to the earth, and she took up her son and went out.

[38] And Elisha returned to Gilgal, and the famine was in the land, and the sons of the prophets were sitting before him. And he said to his young man, "Set on the great pot and boil stew for the sons of the prophets."

[39] And one went out to the field to gather herbs, and he found a vine of the field and gathered from it gourds of the field, the fullness of his garment, and he came and sliced them into the pot of stew, for they did not know.

[40] And they poured for the men to eat. And it came to pass, as they were eating of the stew, that they cried out and said, "Death is in the pot, man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!" And they were not able to eat.

[41] And he said, "And take meal." And he threw it into the pot and said, "Pour for the people, and let them eat." And there was no evil thing in the pot.

[42] And a man came from ⟦baal-shalishah|Baal Shalishah|Baal-shalishah⟧ and brought to the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ bread of firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley bread, and fresh grain in his sack. And he said, "Give to the people, and let them eat."

[43] And his minister said, "How shall I set this before a hundred men?" And he said, "Give to the people, and let them eat, for thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: 'They shall eat and leave over.'"

[44] And he set it before them, and they ate and left over, according to the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*. "Man of *Elohim*" has the article everywhere in this chapter except v9 (אִישׁ אֱלֹהִים קָדוֹשׁ).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Thy servant my husband is dead … and the creditor is come to take unto him my two sons to be bondmen" → "Your servant, my husband, is dead … and the creditor has come to take my two children for himself as slaves"** (v1) — Hebrew עֶבֶד "slave", of the speaker's husband before a superior "your servant", of what the children are to become "slaves": one word. יְלָדַי "my children"; צָעַק "cry out"; הָיָה יָרֵא "was fearing".
- **"Thine handmaid hath not any thing in the house, save a pot of oil" → "Your maidservant has not anything in the house, except a flask of oil"** (v2) — Hebrew שִׁפְחָה; אָסוּךְ is found only here: a small vessel of oil for anointing (from סוּךְ "anoint oneself"), or "an anointing" of oil; uncertain.
- **"borrow thee vessels abroad of all thy neighbours … borrow not a few" → "ask for yourself vessels from outside, from all your neighbors … do not make them few"** (v3) — Hebrew שָׁאַל "ask"; אַל־תַּמְעִיטִי "do not make few".
- **"thou shalt set aside that which is full"** (v4) — Hebrew הִסִּיעַ, literally "make journey, pull away": move it off. בְּעַד "behind": the door is shut behind them, so also v5, 21, 33.
- **"who brought the vessels to her; and she poured out" → "they were bringing near to her, and she was pouring"** (v5) — two participles; "the vessels" is not written. הִגִּישׁ "bring near", so also v6.
- **"And the oil stayed" → "And the oil stood"** (v6) — Hebrew עָמַד "stand": it stopped.
- **"pay thy debt, and live thou and thy children of the rest" → "repay your debt, and you and your sons shall live on what remains"** (v7) — Hebrew שִׁלֵּם "repay"; נְשִׁי "debt" is found only here; בָּנַיִךְ "your sons"; נוֹתָר "what remains". The written text has "and you, your sons"; the reading tradition "and your sons".
- **"And it fell on a day, that Elisha passed to Shunem, where was a great woman; and she constrained him to eat bread" → "And the day came, and Elisha passed over to Shunem, and there was a great woman there, and she took hold of him to eat bread"** (v8, 11, 18) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הַיּוֹם, literally "and the day was" (1 Sam 1:4). אִשָּׁה גְדוֹלָה "a great woman": of wealth or standing (1 Sam 25:2). הֶחֱזִיק בְּ "take hold of": she pressed him. סוּר "turn aside".
- **"I perceive that this is an holy man of God, which passeth by us continually" → "I know that he is a holy man of *Elohim* who passes by us continually"** (v9) — Hebrew יָדַעְתִּי "I know"; נָא "please".
- **"a little chamber, I pray thee, on the wall; and let us set for him there a bed, and a table, and a stool, and a candlestick" → "a small upper room with a wall, and let us put for him there a bed and a table and a seat and a lampstand"** (v10) — Hebrew עֲלִיַּת־קִיר, literally "an upper room of a wall": a walled room on the roof, or a room on the wall. כִּסֵּא "seat", the word that is "throne" of a king; מְנוֹרָה "lampstand".
- **"his servant" → "his young man"** (v12, 25, 38) — Hebrew נַעַר, the word "boy", here of an attendant; so also "the young man" in v19 and "her young man" in v24. In v29–35 the same word is the child: "the boy". The child is יֶלֶד "child" in v18, 26, 34.
- **"thou hast been careful for us with all this care … wouldest thou be spoken for to the king, or to the captain of the host? … I dwell among mine own people" → "you have trembled for us with all this trembling … Is there a word to speak for you to the king or to the commander of the host? … In the midst of my people I am dwelling"** (v13) — Hebrew חָרַד "tremble" with its noun: she has taken anxious trouble. שַׂר הַצָּבָא "the commander of the host"; "own" is not written.
- **"Verily she hath no child" → "Truly, she has no son"** (v14) — Hebrew אֲבָל "truly"; בֵּן "son".
- **"in the door" → "in the entrance"** (v15) — Hebrew פֶּתַח.
- **"About this season, according to the time of life, thou shalt embrace a son" → "At this appointed time, about this time next year, you will be embracing a son"** (v16, 17) — Hebrew לַמּוֹעֵד הַזֶּה "at this appointed time"; כָּעֵת חַיָּה, literally "at the living time": when this time lives again, as Gen 18:10, 14; the sense is not certain. חֹבֶקֶת is a participle. **"do not lie unto thine handmaid"** — Hebrew כִּזֵּב; no "O" before "man of *Elohim*".
- **"that Elisha had said unto her, according to the time of life" → "about this time next year, of which Elisha had spoken to her"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־דִּבֶּר "of which he had spoken"; the words "at this appointed time" are repeated from v16 as they were spoken.
- **"Carry him to his mother … And when he had taken him"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew נָשָׂא "lift, carry"; the same verb is "take up" in v36, 37. **"till noon, and then died" → "until the noon, and he died"**.
- **"shut the door upon him" → "shut the door behind him"** (v21) — the Hebrew has only וַתִּסְגֹּר בַּעֲדוֹ "and she shut behind him"; "the door" is supplied.
- **"one of the asses, that I may run to the man of God, and come again" → "one of the she-donkeys, and let me run to the man of *Elohim* and return"** (v22, 24) — Hebrew אָתוֹן "she-donkey"; two cohortatives.
- **"Wherefore wilt thou go to him to day? it is neither new moon, nor sabbath. And she said, It shall be well" → "Why are you going to him today? It is not new moon and not Shabbat. And she said, Shalom"** (v23, 26) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם, one word: "peace". In v26 הֲשָׁלוֹם לָךְ is literally "Is there peace to you?". In v23 the written text and the reading tradition have two spellings of "you are going".
- **"Drive, and go forward; slack not thy riding for me, except I bid thee" → "Drive and go; do not keep me back in riding unless I say so to you"** (v24) — Hebrew אַל־תַּעֲצָר־לִי לִרְכֹּב, literally "do not restrain for me to ride"; עָצַר "keep back".
- **"saw her afar off … Behold, yonder is that Shunammite" → "saw her opposite … Behold, that Shunammite"** (v25) — Hebrew מִנֶּגֶד "opposite"; הַלָּז "that one yonder".
- **"she caught him by the feet: but Gehazi came near to thrust her away … Let her alone; for her soul is vexed within her" → "took hold of his feet. And Gehazi drew near to push her away … Let her be, for her soul is bitter to her"** (v27) — Hebrew הָדַף "push"; הַרְפֵּה־לָהּ, literally "slacken for her"; מָרָה "is bitter"; הֶעְלִים "hide".
- **"Did I desire a son of my lord? did I not say, Do not deceive me?" → "Did I ask a son from my lord? Did I not say, Do not mislead me?"** (v28) — Hebrew שָׁאַל "ask"; הִשְׁלָה is found only here in this form: "set at ease", give a false calm or hope. The meaning is uncertain.
- **"if thou meet any man, salute him not; and if any salute thee, answer him not again" → "If you find a man, you shall not bless him, and if a man blesses you, you shall not answer him"** (v29) — Hebrew מָצָא "find"; בֵּרַךְ "bless": the greeting was a blessing. חֲגֹר "gird".
- **"As the LORD liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee" → "As Yahweh lives and by the life of your soul, I will not forsake you"** (v30) — as 2 Kgs 2:2; the oath's "if I forsake you" means a sworn "not".
- **"there was neither voice, nor hearing" → "there was no voice and there was no attention"** (v31) — Hebrew קוֹל and קֶשֶׁב, as 1 Kgs 18:29. **"The child is not awaked" → "The boy has not awakened"**.
- **"his hands upon his hands: and he stretched himself upon the child; and the flesh of the child waxed warm" → "his palms on his palms, and he crouched upon him, and the flesh of the child grew warm"** (v34, 35) — Hebrew כַּף "palm" (the written text has "his palm", the reading tradition "his palms"); גָּהַר "crouch", as 1 Kgs 18:42: another verb than "stretched himself" in 1 Kgs 17:21 (הִתְמֹדֵד).
- **"walked in the house to and fro … and the child sneezed seven times" → "went in the house, once this way and once that way … And the boy sneezed as many as seven times"** (v35) — Hebrew אַחַת הֵנָּה וְאַחַת הֵנָּה "once here and once here"; זוֹרֵר is found only here: "sneeze"; the meaning is not certain. עַד־שֶׁבַע פְּעָמִים "up to seven times".
- **"bowed herself to the ground" → "bowed down to the earth"** (v37).
- **"there was a dearth in the land … Set on the great pot, and seethe pottage" → "the famine was in the land … Set on the great pot and boil stew"** (v38) — Hebrew הָרָעָב with the article; נָזִיד "stew".
- **"to gather herbs, and found a wild vine, and gathered thereof wild gourds his lap full, and came and shred them into the pot of pottage: for they knew them not" → "to gather herbs, and he found a vine of the field and gathered from it gourds of the field, the fullness of his garment, and he came and sliced them into the pot of stew, for they did not know"** (v39) — Hebrew אֹרֹת "herbs" and פַּקֻּעֹת "gourds" are found in this sense only here; which plants are meant is uncertain. שָׂדֶה "field" three times; מְלֹא בִגְדוֹ "the fullness of his garment". "Them" after "know" is not written.
- **"O thou man of God, there is death in the pot" → "Death is in the pot, man of *Elohim*"** (v40) — the Hebrew order; no "O".
- **"Then bring meal … And there was no harm in the pot" → "And take meal … And there was no evil thing in the pot"** (v41) — Hebrew וּקְחוּ "and take"; קֶמַח "meal"; דָּבָר רָע "an evil thing": nothing harmful.
- **"bread of the firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley, and full ears of corn in the husk thereof" → "bread of firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley bread, and fresh grain in his sack"** (v42) — Hebrew עֶשְׂרִים־לֶחֶם שְׂעֹרִים "twenty bread of barley"; כַּרְמֶל "fresh grain" (Lev 2:14); צִקְלוֹן is found only here: commonly "sack"; uncertain.
- **"his servitor said, What, should I set this before an hundred men? … They shall eat, and shall leave thereof" → "his minister said, How shall I set this before a hundred men? … They shall eat and leave over"** (v43, 44) — Hebrew מְשָׁרֵת "one who ministers"; נָתַן לִפְנֵי "set before", the verb "give" (it is "Give" in "Give to the people"); אָכֹל וְהוֹתֵר, two infinitives: "eating and leaving over".

### Notes
- **Widow's oil** — echoes Elijah-widow (1 Kgs 17). Elisha's ministry reprises Elijah's with extension.
- **Debt-slavery** — Torah-allowed (Lev 25, Deut 15), but Elisha delivers the family from it. Social-justice miracle.
- **Shunem** — Issachar-tribal town on Jezreel plain; also Abishag's hometown (1 Kgs 1:3).
- **Guest-room and staff** — ANE hospitality custom. Woman's foresight secures future blessing.
- **Parallel to Sarah** — a woman without a son is promised one "about this time next year"; echoes Gen 18.
- **Raising the child** — body-on-body revival like Elijah (1 Kgs 17). Physical contact marks real participation in the miracle, not magic staff.
- **Death in the pot** — likely colocynth (wild cucumber), purgative. Meal acts as sign, not antidote.
- **Twenty loaves for a hundred** — miracle of multiplication; direct typological precursor to Jesus feeding 5,000 (Matt 14) and 4,000 (Matt 15).
- **"Shall eat and leave over"** — prophetic formula; Jesus echoes with leftovers.
