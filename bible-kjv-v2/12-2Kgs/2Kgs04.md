# 2 Kings 4

[1] A certain woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets cried to Elisha, saying, "Your servant my husband is dead, and you know that your servant feared Yahweh; the creditor has come to take my two children to be his slaves."

[2] Elisha said to her, "What shall I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?" She said, "Your maidservant has nothing in the house except a flask of oil."

[3] Then he said, "Go, borrow vessels from outside, from all your neighbors — empty vessels; do not borrow too few.

[4] Then go in, and shut the door behind you and your sons, and pour into all those vessels; and when one is full, set it aside."

[5] So she went from him and shut the door behind her and her sons; they brought the vessels to her, and she kept pouring.

[6] When the vessels were full, she said to her son, "Bring me another vessel." He said to her, "There is not another vessel." Then the oil stopped flowing.

[7] She came and told the man of *Elohim*. He said, "Go, sell the oil and pay your debt; you and your sons can live on the rest."

[8] One day Elisha passed on to Shunem, where there was a wealthy woman; she constrained him to eat bread. So as often as he passed by, he would turn in there to eat bread.

[9] She said to her husband, "Behold, I perceive that this is a holy man of *Elohim* who passes by us continually.

[10] Let us make a small walled roof-room; let us set for him there a bed, a table, a stool, and a lampstand; when he comes to us, he shall turn in there."

[11] One day he came there, turned into the upper room, and lay down there.

[12] He said to Gehazi his servant, "Call this Shunammite." When he had called her, she stood before him.

[13] He said to him, "Say now to her, 'Behold, you have been careful for us with all this care; what is to be done for you? Would you have a word spoken for you to the king or to the commander of the army?'" She answered, "I dwell among my own people."

[14] He said, "What then is to be done for her?" Gehazi answered, "Indeed, she has no son, and her husband is old."

[15] He said, "Call her." When he had called her, she stood in the doorway.

[16] He said, "At this season, at the reviving time, you shall embrace a son." She said, "No, my lord, O man of *Elohim*, do not lie to your maidservant."

[17] The woman conceived and bore a son at that season, at the reviving time, as Elisha had said to her.

[18] When the child had grown, one day he went out to his father among the reapers.

[19] He said to his father, "My head, my head!" He said to a young man, "Carry him to his mother."

[20] When he had taken him and brought him to his mother, he sat on her knees till noon, and then died.

[21] She went up and laid him on the bed of the man of *Elohim*, and shut the door upon him, and went out.

[22] She called to her husband and said, "Send me, please, one of the young men and one of the donkeys, that I may run to the man of *Elohim* and come again."

[23] He said, "Why will you go to him today? It is neither new moon nor Sabbath." She said, "Peace — it will be well."

[24] Then she saddled the donkey and said to her servant, "Drive, and go forward; do not slacken the pace for me unless I tell you."

[25] So she went and came to the man of *Elohim* at Mount Carmel. When the man of *Elohim* saw her at a distance, he said to Gehazi his servant, "Behold, there is the Shunammite.

[26] Run now to meet her, and say to her, 'Is it well with you? Is it well with your husband? Is it well with the child?'" She answered, "It is well."

[27] When she came to the man of *Elohim* on the mountain, she caught hold of his feet. Gehazi came near to push her away, but the man of *Elohim* said, "Let her alone, for her soul is bitter within her; Yahweh has hidden it from me and has not told me."

[28] Then she said, "Did I ask for a son from my lord? Did I not say, 'Do not deceive me'?"

[29] Then he said to Gehazi, "Gird up your loins, take my staff in your hand, and go your way; if you meet anyone, do not greet him, and if anyone greets you, do not answer him; lay my staff upon the face of the child."

[30] The mother of the child said, "As Yahweh lives, and as your soul lives, I will not leave you." So he arose and followed her.

[31] Gehazi went on ahead of them and laid the staff upon the face of the child, but there was neither sound nor sign of life. Therefore he returned to meet him and told him, "The child has not awakened."

[32] When Elisha came into the house, behold, the child was dead, lying on his bed.

[33] He went in, shut the door upon the two of them, and prayed to Yahweh.

[34] He went up and lay on the child; he put his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes, his hands on his hands; he stretched himself out on him, and the flesh of the child grew warm.

[35] Then he returned and walked in the house to and fro; he went up and stretched himself out on him. The child sneezed seven times, and the child opened his eyes.

[36] He called Gehazi and said, "Call this Shunammite." So he called her. When she came to him, he said, "Take up your son."

[37] She came in, fell at his feet, bowed herself to the ground, took up her son, and went out.

[38] Elisha came again to Gilgal; there was a famine in the land. As the sons of the prophets were sitting before him, he said to his servant, "Set on the great pot and boil stew for the sons of the prophets."

[39] One went out into the field to gather herbs, and found a wild vine, and gathered wild gourds from it, his lap full; he came and shredded them into the pot of stew, for they did not know what they were.

[40] So they poured out for the men to eat. As they were eating the stew, they cried out and said, "O man of *Elohim*, there is death in the pot!" They could not eat it.

[41] He said, "Then bring flour." He threw it into the pot; he said, "Pour out for the people, that they may eat." There was no harm in the pot.

[42] A man came from Baal-shalishah and brought the man of *Elohim* bread of the firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley and fresh grain in his sack. He said, "Give to the people, that they may eat."

[43] His attendant said, "How can I set this before a hundred men?" He said, "Give the people, that they may eat; for thus says Yahweh: 'They shall eat, and shall have some left.'"

[44] So he set it before them; they ate, and had some left, according to the word of Yahweh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"pot of oil" → "flask of oil"** (v2) — Hebrew *asuk*; hapax legomenon, probably anointing-oil flask.
- **"great woman" → "wealthy woman"** (v8) — Hebrew *gedolah*; woman of substance/standing.
- **"little chamber on the wall" → "small walled roof-room"** (v10) — *aliyat-qir qetannah*; walled upper chamber on roof.
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"** (v10) — *menorah*.
- **"time of life" → "reviving time"** (v16, 17) — *ke-et chayyah*; next spring/season of renewed life (pregnancy-to-term idiom).
- **"ass" → "donkey"** (v22, 24).
- **"pottage" → "stew"** (v38, 39, 40).
- **"meal" → "flour"** (v41) — *qemach*.
- **"ears of corn in the husk" → "fresh grain in his sack"** (v42) — Hebrew *karmel be-tsiqlono*; disputed; "fresh ripe ears in his bag/garment."
- **"servitor" → "attendant"** (v43) — *meshareto*.

### Notes
- **Widow's oil** — echoes Elijah-widow (1 Kgs 17). Elisha's ministry reprises Elijah's with extension.
- **Debt-slavery** — Torah-allowed (Lev 25, Deut 15), but Elisha delivers the family from it. Social-justice miracle.
- **Shunem** — Issachar-tribal town on Jezreel plain; also Abishag's hometown (1 Kgs 1:3).
- **Guest-room and staff** — ANE hospitality custom. Woman's foresight secures future blessing.
- **Parallel to Sarah** — barren woman promised a son "at this season"; echoes Gen 18.
- **Raising the child** — body-on-body revival like Elijah (1 Kgs 17). Physical contact marks real participation in the miracle, not magic staff.
- **Death in the pot** — likely colocynth (wild cucumber), purgative. Flour acts as sign, not antidote.
- **Twenty loaves for a hundred** — miracle of multiplication; direct typological precursor to Jesus feeding 5,000 (Matt 14) and 4,000 (Matt 15).
- **"Shall eat and have some left"** — prophetic formula; Jesus echoes with leftovers.
