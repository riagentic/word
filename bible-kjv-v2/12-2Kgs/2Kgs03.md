## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"image of Baal" → "pillar of Baal"** (v2) — *matzevat ha-Baal*; standing stone.
- **"sheepmaster" → "sheep breeder"** (v4) — *noqed*; also used of Amos (1:1). Professional sheep-rearer.
- **"rendered... an hundred thousand lambs, and an hundred thousand rams" → "paid as tribute a hundred thousand lambs and the wool of a hundred thousand rams"** (v4) — Hebrew construction suggests tribute was the wool of the rams.
- **"fetched a compass" → "made a circuitous march"** (v9) — *sababu derek*.
- **"minstrel" → "musician"** (v15) — *menagen*.
- **"valley" → "wadi"** (v16, 17) — *nachal*.
- **"country" → "land"** (v20).
- **"indignation" → "wrath"** (v27) — *qetzef*.

### Notes
- **Mesha of Moab** — known from the Mesha Stela (Louvre), which records the rebellion from Moabite perspective. Stela mentions "Omri king of Israel"; confirms historicity.
- **Three-king coalition** — Israel + Judah + Edom (Edom subordinate to Judah at this point, v9).
- **Elisha's rebuke of Jehoram** — prophet has no respect for the Omride; stays only for Jehoshaphat's sake.
- **Musician as prophetic catalyst** — music aids prophetic reception; cf. Saul's prophesying with musicians (1 Sam 10), David's lyre.
- **Blood-red water** — refracted sunrise on reddish Edomite soil/sandstone. Moabites interpret as dried blood, misread situation.
- **Mesha's child sacrifice** — Moabite god Chemosh received human sacrifice. "Great wrath came upon Israel" — ambiguous: divine wrath (for having provoked this abomination)? Moabite religious fury rallying defenders? Text leaves it open.
- **Fulfilled (partially)** — Moab is devastated, but not taken. Stele claims Mesha regained independence; biblical account admits Israel withdrew. Honest report of a partial victory.
