# 2 Kings 3

[1] Jehoram son of Ahab began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, and he reigned twelve years.

[2] He did what was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, though not like his father and his mother; for he put away the pillar of Baal that his father had made.

[3] Nevertheless he clung to the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat who made Israel to sin; he did not depart from them.

[4] Mesha king of Moab was a sheep breeder; he used to pay as tribute to the king of Israel a hundred thousand lambs and the wool of a hundred thousand rams.

[5] But when Ahab died, the king of Moab rebelled against the king of Israel.

[6] King Jehoram marched out of Samaria at that time and mustered all Israel.

[7] He went and sent to Jehoshaphat king of Judah, saying, "The king of Moab has rebelled against me; will you go with me against Moab to battle?" He said, "I will go up. I am as you are, my people as your people, my horses as your horses."

[8] He said, "Which way shall we go up?" He answered, "The way through the wilderness of Edom."

[9] So the king of Israel went out, with the king of Judah and the king of Edom. They made a circuitous march of seven days; there was no water for the army or for the animals that followed them.

[10] The king of Israel said, "Alas! Yahweh has called these three kings together to deliver them into the hand of Moab."

[11] But Jehoshaphat said, "Is there no prophet of Yahweh here, that we may inquire of Yahweh by him?" One of the king of Israel's servants answered and said, "Elisha son of Shaphat is here, who poured water on the hands of Elijah."

[12] Jehoshaphat said, "The word of Yahweh is with him." So the king of Israel, Jehoshaphat, and the king of Edom went down to him.

[13] Elisha said to the king of Israel, "What have I to do with you? Go to the prophets of your father and to the prophets of your mother." The king of Israel said to him, "No; for Yahweh has called these three kings together to deliver them into the hand of Moab."

[14] Elisha said, "As Yahweh of Hosts lives, before whom I stand, surely, were it not that I regard the presence of Jehoshaphat king of Judah, I would not look at you, nor see you.

[15] But now bring me a musician." When the musician played, the hand of Yahweh came upon him.

[16] He said, "Thus says Yahweh: 'Make this wadi full of trenches.'

[17] For thus says Yahweh: 'You shall not see wind, nor shall you see rain; yet that wadi shall be filled with water, and you shall drink — you, your livestock, and your animals.'

[18] This is a light thing in the eyes of Yahweh; he will also deliver the Moabites into your hand.

[19] You shall strike every fortified city and every choice city, and shall fell every good tree, stop up all springs of water, and ruin every good piece of land with stones."

[20] In the morning, when the grain offering — was offered, behold, water came by the way of Edom; the land was filled with water.

[21] When all the Moabites heard that the kings had come up to fight against them, all who were able to put on armor, from the youngest up, were gathered and stood at the border.

[22] They rose up early in the morning; the sun shone on the water, and the Moabites saw the water opposite them red as blood.

[23] They said, "This is blood! The kings have surely fought together and struck each other down. Now then, Moab, to the spoil!"

[24] But when they came to the camp of Israel, the Israelites rose up and struck the Moabites, so that they fled before them; they went on striking Moab as they advanced.

[25] They broke down the cities, and on every good piece of land every man cast his stone and filled it; they stopped up all the springs of water and felled all the good trees, until in Kir-hareseth only its stones were left; and the slingers surrounded it and struck it.

[26] When the king of Moab saw that the battle was going against him, he took with him seven hundred swordsmen to break through to the king of Edom, but they could not.

[27] Then he took his firstborn son who was to reign in his place and offered him as a burnt offering on the wall. Great wrath came upon Israel; and they withdrew from him and returned to their own land.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"image of Baal" → "pillar of Baal"** (v2) — *matzevat ha-Baal*; standing stone.
- **"sheepmaster" → "sheep breeder"** (v4) — *noqed*; also used of Amos (1:1). Professional sheep-rearer.
- **"rendered... an hundred thousand lambs, and an hundred thousand rams" → "paid as tribute a hundred thousand lambs and the wool of a hundred thousand rams"** (v4) — Hebrew construction suggests tribute was the wool of the rams.
- **"fetched a compass" → "made a circuitous march"** (v9) — *sababu derek*.
- **"minstrel" → "musician"** (v15) — *menagen*.
- **"valley" → "wadi"** (v16, 17) — *nachal*.
- **"country" → "land"** (v20).
- **"indignation" → "wrath"** (v27) — *qetzef*.

### Notes
- **Mesha of Moab** — known from the Mesha Stela (Louvre), which records the rebellion from Moabite perspective. Stela mentions "Omri king of Israel"; confirms historicity.
- **Three-king coalition** — Israel + Judah + Edom (Edom subordinate to Judah at this point, v9).
- **Elisha's rebuke of Jehoram** — prophet has no respect for the Omride; stays only for Jehoshaphat's sake.
- **Musician as prophetic catalyst** — music aids prophetic reception; cf. Saul's prophesying with musicians (1 Sam 10), David's lyre.
- **Blood-red water** — refracted sunrise on reddish Edomite soil/sandstone. Moabites interpret as dried blood, misread situation.
- **Mesha's child sacrifice** — Moabite god Chemosh received human sacrifice. "Great wrath came upon Israel" — ambiguous: divine wrath (for having provoked this abomination)? Moabite religious fury rallying defenders? Text leaves it open.
- **Fulfilled (partially)** — Moab is devastated, but not taken. Stele claims Mesha regained independence; biblical account admits Israel withdrew. Honest report of a partial victory.
