# 2 Kings 9

[1] Elisha the prophet called one of the sons of the prophets and said to him, "Gird up your loins, take this flask of oil in your hand, and go to Ramoth-gilead.

[2] When you arrive, look out there for Jehu son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi; go in, have him rise up from among his brothers, and bring him into an inner chamber.

[3] Then take the flask of oil, pour it on his head, and say, 'Thus says Yahweh: I have anointed you king over Israel.' Then open the door and flee; do not tarry."

[4] So the young man, the young prophet, went to Ramoth-gilead.

[5] When he came, behold, the commanders of the army were sitting. He said, "I have a word for you, O commander." Jehu said, "To which of us all?" He said, "To you, O commander."

[6] He arose and went into the house; he poured the oil on his head and said to him, "Thus says Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Israel: 'I have anointed you king over the people of Yahweh, over Israel.

[7] You shall strike down the house of Ahab your master, that I may avenge the blood of my servants the prophets, and the blood of all the servants of Yahweh, at the hand of Jezebel.

[8] For the whole house of Ahab shall perish; I will cut off from Ahab every male, both bond and free in Israel.

[9] I will make the house of Ahab like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha son of Ahijah.

[10] The dogs shall eat Jezebel on the plot of ground at Jezreel, and there shall be none to bury her.'" Then he opened the door and fled.

[11] Jehu came out to the servants of his lord, and one said to him, "Is all well? Why did this madman come to you?" He said to them, "You know the sort of man and his ranting."

[12] They said, "That's a lie; tell us now." He said, "Thus and thus he spoke to me, saying, 'Thus says Yahweh: I have anointed you king over Israel.'"

[13] Then they hurried, and each took his garment and put it under him on the bare steps; they blew the shofar and said, "Jehu is king!"

[14] So Jehu son of Jehoshaphat son of Nimshi conspired against Joram. (Now Joram had been defending Ramoth-gilead, he and all Israel, against Hazael king of Aram;

[15] but King Joram had returned to Jezreel to be healed of the wounds that the Arameans had given him when he fought with Hazael king of Aram.) Jehu said, "If this is your mind, then let no one slip out of the city to go tell it in Jezreel."

[16] So Jehu rode in a chariot and went to Jezreel, for Joram was laid up there; Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to see Joram.

[17] The watchman was standing on the tower in Jezreel; he saw the company of Jehu as he came, and said, "I see a company." Joram said, "Take a horseman, and send to meet them, and let him say, 'Is it peace?'"

[18] So a horseman went to meet him and said, "Thus says the king, 'Is it peace?'" Jehu said, "What have you to do with peace? Fall in behind me." The watchman reported, "The messenger came to them, but he is not coming back."

[19] Then he sent out a second horseman, who came to them and said, "Thus says the king, 'Is it peace?'" Jehu answered, "What have you to do with peace? Fall in behind me."

[20] The watchman reported, "He came to them but is not coming back. The driving is like the driving of Jehu son of Nimshi, for he drives furiously."

[21] Joram said, "Hitch up." His chariot was hitched up. Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah went out, each in his chariot; they went out to meet Jehu, and found him at the plot of Naboth the Jezreelite.

[22] When Joram saw Jehu, he said, "Is it peace, Jehu?" He answered, "What peace, so long as the whoredoms of your mother Jezebel and her sorceries are so many?"

[23] Joram wheeled around and fled, saying to Ahaziah, "Treachery, O Ahaziah!"

[24] Jehu drew his bow with full force and struck Jehoram between his shoulders; the arrow went out through his heart, and he slumped down in his chariot.

[25] Jehu said to Bidkar his officer, "Pick him up and throw him in the plot of Naboth the Jezreelite. Remember, when you and I rode side by side behind Ahab his father, Yahweh uttered this pronouncement against him:

[26] 'Just as I saw yesterday the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons,' says Yahweh, 'I will repay you in this plot,' says Yahweh. Now therefore pick him up and throw him in the plot, according to the word of Yahweh."

[27] When Ahaziah king of Judah saw this, he fled by the way of Beth-haggan. Jehu followed after him and said, "Strike him too in the chariot." They did so at the ascent of Gur, which is by Ibleam. He fled to Megiddo and died there.

[28] His servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem, and buried him in his tomb with his fathers in the city of David.

[29] In the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab, Ahaziah began to reign over Judah.

[30] When Jehu had come to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it; she painted her eyes with kohl, dressed her hair, and looked out the window.

[31] As Jehu entered the gate, she said, "Is it peace, O Zimri, murderer of his master?"

[32] He lifted his face to the window and said, "Who is on my side? Who?" Two or three eunuchs looked out to him.

[33] He said, "Throw her down." So they threw her down; some of her blood spattered on the wall and on the horses, and he trampled her under hoof.

[34] When he had come in, he ate and drank, and said, "See now to this cursed woman, and bury her; for she is a king's daughter."

[35] They went to bury her, but they found no more of her than the skull, the feet, and the palms of her hands.

[36] They came back and told him. He said, "This is the word of Yahweh that he spoke by his servant Elijah the Tishbite, saying, 'In the plot of Jezreel the dogs shall eat the flesh of Jezebel;

[37] the corpse of Jezebel shall be like dung on the face of the field in the plot of Jezreel, so that no one can say, "This is Jezebel."'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"box of oil" → "flask of oil"** (v1, 3) — *pak shemen*.
- **"mad fellow" → "madman / his ranting"** (v11) — *meshugga*; prophets disparagingly called "mad."
- **"top of the stairs" → "bare steps"** (v13) — Hebrew *gerem ha-ma'alot*; uncertain — literal "bone/framework of the steps," possibly a stair landing serving as makeshift throne.
- **"captain" → "officer"** (v25) — *shalish*.
- **"going up to Gur" → "ascent of Gur"** (v27).
- **"garden house" → "Beth-haggan"** (v27) — Hebrew *bet-haggan* = "garden-house"; probably a place name.
- **"trode her under foot" → "trampled her under hoof"** (v33) — horses trampled her.
- **"Syria / Syrians" → "Aram / Arameans"** (v14, 15).
- **"plat of ground / plat / portion" → "plot"** (v10, 21, 25, 26, 36, 37).

### Notes
- **Jehu's anointing** — completes the Elijah-era mandate (1 Kgs 19:16); delegated to a junior prophet.
- **Hurried flight** — prophet flees before Jehu can react; strong oracular deposit, minimal witness.
- **Impromptu coronation** (v13) — garments-as-throne-cover, shofar, acclamation. Military coup-coronation.
- **"Drives furiously"** — idiomatic Hebrew *shigga'on*, same root as "madness." Jehu's driving style proverbial.
- **Naboth's vineyard as judgment-site** — Jehu executes Elijah's oracle (1 Kgs 21:19) on the very ground of Naboth's murder.
- **Ahaziah collateral** — Judahite king dies in northern purge because of Omride marriage alliance. Removes him from throne; Athaliah's seizure follows (ch 11).
- **Jezebel's defiance** — paints up, dresses hair: queenly dignity to the last. Calls Jehu "Zimri" — evoking 1 Kgs 16's short-lived regicide.
- **Eunuchs defect** — harem-keepers turn. Jezebel's household rejects her at the critical moment.
- **Bones left** — dogs eat Jezebel as Elijah foretold. Only extremities remain; identifiable but unburyable.
- **Jehu's theology** — carries out Yahweh's judgment but will himself be judged for excess (Hosea 1:4). Mixed agent.
