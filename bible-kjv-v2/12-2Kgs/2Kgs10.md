# 2 Kings 10

[1] Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria — to the rulers of Jezreel, to the elders, and to the guardians of Ahab's sons — saying,

[2] "Now as soon as this letter comes to you — since your master's sons are with you, and you have chariots and horses, a fortified city also, and weapons —

[3] select the best and most suitable of your master's sons, set him on his father's throne, and fight for your master's house."

[4] But they were exceedingly afraid and said, "Behold, two kings could not stand before him; how then shall we stand?"

[5] So the steward of the house, the governor of the city, the elders, and the guardians sent to Jehu, saying, "We are your servants, and will do all that you bid us; we will not make anyone king. Do what is good in your eyes."

[6] Then he wrote a second letter to them, saying, "If you are for me, and if you will obey my voice, take the heads of the men your master's sons, and come to me at Jezreel by this time tomorrow." Now the king's sons, seventy persons, were with the leading men of the city who had raised them.

[7] When the letter came to them, they took the king's sons and slaughtered all seventy persons; they put their heads in baskets and sent them to him at Jezreel.

[8] A messenger came and told him, saying, "They have brought the heads of the king's sons." He said, "Lay them in two heaps at the entrance of the gate until morning."

[9] In the morning he went out, stood, and said to all the people, "You are innocent. Behold, I conspired against my master and killed him — but who struck down all these?

[10] Know then that nothing shall fall to the earth of the word of Yahweh that Yahweh spoke concerning the house of Ahab; Yahweh has done what he spoke by his servant Elijah."

[11] So Jehu struck down all who remained of the house of Ahab in Jezreel, all his great men and his close associates and his priests, until he left him none remaining.

[12] He arose and departed, and came to Samaria. As he was at Beth-eked of the shepherds on the way,

[13] Jehu met the kinsmen of Ahaziah king of Judah and said, "Who are you?" They answered, "We are the kinsmen of Ahaziah; we have come down to greet the sons of the king and the sons of the queen mother."

[14] He said, "Take them alive." So they took them alive and slaughtered them at the pit of Beth-eked — forty-two men; he left none of them.

[15] When he had departed from there, he encountered Jehonadab son of Rechab coming to meet him. He greeted him and said to him, "Is your heart true, as my heart is with your heart?" Jehonadab answered, "It is." "If it is, give me your hand." He gave him his hand, and he took him up into the chariot with him.

[16] He said, "Come with me, and see my zeal for Yahweh." So he had him ride in his chariot.

[17] When he came to Samaria, he struck down all who remained to Ahab in Samaria, until he had destroyed him, according to the word of Yahweh that he spoke to Elijah.

[18] Jehu gathered all the people together and said to them, "Ahab served Baal a little; Jehu will serve him much.

[19] Now therefore call to me all the prophets of Baal, all his worshipers, and all his priests; let none be missing, for I have a great sacrifice to make to Baal. Whoever is missing shall not live." But Jehu did it with cunning, in order to destroy the worshipers of Baal.

[20] Jehu said, "Sanctify a solemn assembly for Baal." And they proclaimed it.

[21] Jehu sent through all Israel; all the worshipers of Baal came, so that not a man was left who did not come. They entered the house of Baal, and the house of Baal was full from one end to another.

[22] He said to the one in charge of the wardrobe, "Bring out the vestments for all the worshipers of Baal." So he brought out the vestments for them.

[23] Jehu and Jehonadab son of Rechab went into the house of Baal, and he said to the worshipers of Baal, "Search and see that there are none here among you of the servants of Yahweh, but only the worshipers of Baal."

[24] They went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Now Jehu had stationed eighty men outside and said, "If any of the men whom I have brought into your hands escape, the one who lets him go — his life shall be for the life of him."

[25] As soon as he had finished offering the burnt offering, Jehu said to the runners and to the officers, "Go in and strike them down; let none come out." They struck them with the edge of the sword; the runners and officers threw them out and went into the inner sanctum of the house of Baal.

[26] They brought out the pillars of the house of Baal and burned them.

[27] They broke down the pillar of Baal, broke down the house of Baal, and made it a latrine to this day.

[28] Thus Jehu wiped out Baal from Israel.

[29] However, from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat — who made Israel to sin — Jehu did not depart; namely, the golden calves that were in Bethel and in Dan.

[30] Yahweh said to Jehu, "Because you have done well in executing what is right in my eyes, and have done to the house of Ahab according to all that was in my heart, your sons to the fourth generation shall sit on the throne of Israel."

[31] But Jehu did not take heed to walk in the law of Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel with all his heart; he did not depart from the sins of Jeroboam that made Israel to sin.

[32] In those days Yahweh began to trim Israel; Hazael struck them throughout the territory of Israel:

[33] from the Jordan eastward, all the land of Gilead — the Gadites, the Reubenites, and the Manassites — from Aroer, which is by the Arnon Valley, both Gilead and Bashan.

[34] The rest of the acts of Jehu, and all that he did, and all his might — are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel?

[35] Jehu slept with his fathers, and they buried him in Samaria; Jehoahaz his son reigned in his place.

[36] The time that Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria was twenty-eight years.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"bringers up / brought up Ahab's children" → "guardians of Ahab's sons"** (v1, 5, 6) — *ha-omenim*.
- **"fenced city" → "fortified city"** (v2).
- **"armour" → "weapons"** (v2).
- **"kinsfolks" → "close associates"** (v11) — *meyudda'av*; intimates, inner circle, not literal relatives.
- **"shearing house" → "Beth-eked of the shepherds"** (v12, 14) — *bet-eked ha-ro'im*; probably place name, "binding house," a sheep-shearing station.
- **"brethren of Ahaziah" → "kinsmen of Ahaziah"** (v13).
- **"the queen" → "the queen mother"** (v13) — likely the *gebirah* Jezebel.
- **"right / true" → "true"** (v15) — *yashar*.
- **"in subtilty" → "with cunning"** (v19) — *be-oqbah*; Jacob-type deception.
- **"guard" → "runners"** (v25) — *ratzim*; royal couriers/bodyguard.
- **"the city of the house of Baal" → "the inner sanctum of the house of Baal"** (v25) — Hebrew *ir* here likely means inner citadel of the temple complex.
- **"images" → "pillars"** (v26) — *matzevot*; standing stones, not statues.
- **"draught house" → "latrine"** (v27) — *macharaot*; ceremonial defilement site.
- **"cut Israel short" → "trim Israel"** (v32) — *le-qatzot*; to clip territory.
- **"coasts" → "territory"** (v32) — *gevul*; borders/territory, not seashore.
- **"Rechab" — retained; Rechabites are a semi-nomadic Yahwist clan** (v15).

### Notes
- **Seventy sons** — extended royal family. ANE practice of large royal progeny for dynastic security; here, the very number that dies.
- **Jehu's cunning rhetoric** (v9) — pretends the Jezreel officials killed them independently, spreading blame. Political manipulation.
- **Forty-two** — same number as the Bethel youths in ch 2; narrative echo of judgment-count.
- **Rechabites** — Jehonadab/Jonadab founds the sect that rejects settled life, wine, agriculture (Jer 35). Strict Yahwists; perfect coalition partner for Jehu's purge.
- **"Come and see my zeal"** — Jehu craves witness to his cause. Pride in purge. Hosea 1:4 later condemns the bloodshed.
- **Jehu's Baal-trap** — false feast, provided vestments (for certainty of identification), witness-requirement (no Yahwists present), sealed temple, synchronized slaughter. Cold, efficient.
- **Temple made latrine** — maximal desecration.
- **Sin of Jeroboam retained** — Jehu political-religious reformer only to point of state-religious benefit. Golden calves (legitimizing his kingdom's cult) stay.
- **Four-generation dynasty** — longest in the North: Jehoahaz, Joash, Jeroboam II, Zechariah. Ends in 753 BC.
- **Hazael's conquests** — contemporaneous Assyrian pressure prevents Aram from expanding further west; Israel loses Transjordan.
