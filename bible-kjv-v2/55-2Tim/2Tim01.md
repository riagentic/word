## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v14) — Greek πνεύματος ἁγίου (no article); Hebrew *Ruach HaQodesh*, "Spirit of Holiness".
- **"by the will" → "through the will"** (v1) — Greek διὰ θελήματος.
- **"my dearly beloved son" → "a beloved child"** (v2) — Greek ἀγαπητῷ τέκνῳ; there is no "my". "Grace, mercy, peace" stand without "and".
- **"I thank God" → "I am thankful to *Elohim*"** (v3) — Greek Χάριν ἔχω, "I have gratitude".
- **"with pure conscience" → "in a pure conscience"** (v3) — Greek ἐν καθαρᾷ συνειδήσει; "from my forefathers" (ἀπὸ προγόνων) kept.
- **"prayers" → "supplications"** (v3) — Greek δεήσεσιν, requests; a different word from "prayers" (προσευχαί) in Phlm 4.
- **"night and day"** (v3–4) — Greek νυκτὸς καὶ ἡμέρας stands between "in my supplications" and "longing to see you"; it can belong to either.
- **"being mindful of" → "remembering"** (v4) — Greek μεμνημένος. Verses 3–5 are one sentence; the participles are kept.
- **"When I call to remembrance" → "having received a reminder"** (v5) — Greek ὑπόμνησιν λαβών (NA28; the later text has λαμβάνων).
- **"unfeigned" kept** (v5) — Greek ἀνυποκρίτου, "without play-acting".
- **"and I am persuaded that in thee also" kept** (v5) — Greek πέπεισμαι δὲ ὅτι καὶ ἐν σοί, without a second verb; πέπεισμαι again in v12.
- **"Wherefore" → "For which cause"** (v6, 12) — Greek δι᾽ ἣν αἰτίαν, as Titus 1:13.
- **"stir up" → "rekindle"** (v6) — Greek ἀναζωπυρεῖν, to kindle a fire up or again: "rekindle" or "keep ablaze".
- **"fear" → "cowardice"** (v7) — Greek δειλίας, lack of courage; not φόβος. πνεῦμα stands without the article: "a spirit".
- **"a sound mind" → "sober-mindedness"** (v7) — Greek σωφρονισμοῦ, of the σωφρ- words (Titus 1:8; 2:2–12). The noun can also be active: "a bringing to a sober mind" (σωφρονίζω, Titus 2:4).
- **"the testimony of our Lord" kept** (v8) — Greek τὸ μαρτύριον τοῦ κυρίου ἡμῶν, a plain genitive: the testimony about him, or his own.
- **"be thou partaker of the afflictions of the gospel" → "join in suffering hardship for the gospel"** (v8) — Greek συγκακοπάθησον τῷ εὐαγγελίῳ, "suffer evil together"; with whom is not said. The dative can be "for" or "with" the gospel. The same verb in 2:3; κακοπαθέω in 2:9; 4:5.
- **"according to the power" kept** (v8) — Greek κατὰ δύναμιν θεοῦ; κατά again three times in v9.
- **"before the world began" → "before times eternal"** (v9) — Greek πρὸ χρόνων αἰωνίων, as Titus 1:2.
- **"which was given" kept** (v9) — Greek τὴν δοθεῖσαν, passive, agreeing with "grace".
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v10) — Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ (WH, Treg, NA28); SBLGNT and RP read Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ, "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*".
- **"immortality" → "incorruption"** (v10) — Greek ἀφθαρσίαν, "not decaying".
- **"a preacher" → "a herald"** (v11) — Greek κῆρυξ; "of the Gentiles" (+ ἐθνῶν Treg, RP) is not in SBLGNT, WH, NA28.
- **"that which I have committed unto him" → "my deposit"** (v12) — Greek τὴν παραθήκην μου, a thing left in someone's keeping. It can be what Paul entrusted to him, or what was entrusted to Paul; the same noun in v14, the verb in 2:2.
- **"against that day" → "unto that day"** (v12) — Greek εἰς ἐκείνην τὴν ἡμέραν; in v18 ἐν, "in that day".
- **"Hold fast the form of sound words" → "Hold a pattern of healthy words"** (v13) — Greek ὑποτύπωσιν ἔχε ὑγιαινόντων λόγων, without the article; ὑποτύπωσις, "outline, sketch", is a compound of τύπος "pattern"; ὑγιαίνω "healthy" as in Titus.
- **"in faith and love which is in" kept** (v13) — Greek ἐν πίστει καὶ ἀγάπῃ τῇ ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ; the article τῇ is singular.
- **"That good thing which was committed unto thee" → "the good deposit"** (v14) — Greek τὴν καλὴν παραθήκην; "to you" is not in the Greek.
- **"chain" kept** (v16) — Greek ἅλυσιν, singular.
- **"when he was in Rome" → "having come to be in Rome"** (v17) — Greek γενόμενος ἐν Ῥώμῃ.
- **"very diligently" → "diligently"** (v17) — NA28 σπουδαίως (the later text has the comparative σπουδαιότερον); as Titus 3:13.
- **"ministered unto me" → "served"** (v18) — Greek διηκόνησεν; "to me" is not in NA28.
- **"very well" → "better"** (v18) — Greek βέλτιον, a comparative: "you know better" (than I can tell), or "very well".

### Notes
- **v3–5** — one sentence in Greek.
- **v8–11** — one sentence in Greek.
- **v16, 18** — δῴη, a wish (optative): "may the Lord give".
