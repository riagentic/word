# 2 Timothy 4

[1] I solemnly testify before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, who is about to judge the living and the dead, and by his appearing and his kingdom:

[2] **proclaim the word; be at hand in season, out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort, in all longsuffering and teaching**.

[3] For there will be a time when they will not bear with the healthy doctrine, but according to their own desires they will heap up teachers for themselves, itching in their hearing,

[4] and they will turn their hearing away from the truth, and will be turned aside to the myths.

[5] But you, be sober in all things, suffer hardship, do the work of an evangelist, fully accomplish your service.

[6] For I am already being poured out as a drink offering, and the time of my departure is at hand.

[7] **I have contended the good contest, I have finished the course, I have kept the faith.**

[8] Henceforth there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord will render to me in that day, the righteous judge, and not only to me but also to all who have loved his appearing.

[9] Be diligent to come to me quickly;

[10] for Demas forsook me, having loved the present age, and went to ⟦thessalonica|Thessalonike|Thessalonica⟧, Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.

[11] ⟦luke|Loukas|Luke⟧ alone is with me. Take up ⟦mark|Marcus|Mark⟧ and bring him with yourself, for he is useful to me for service,

[12] but ⟦tychicus|Tychikos|Tychicus⟧ I sent to ⟦ephesus|Ephesos|Ephesus⟧.

[13] The cloak that I left behind in Troas with ⟦carpus|Karpos|Carpus⟧, bring when you come, and the books, especially the parchments.

[14] ⟦alexander|Alexandros|Alexander⟧ the coppersmith showed me many evils — the Lord will render to him according to his works —

[15] against whom you also be on guard, for he greatly withstood our words.

[16] At my first defense no one came to my side, but all forsook me — may it not be reckoned to them —

[17] but the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, that through me the proclamation might be fully accomplished and all the nations might hear; and I was rescued out of a lion's mouth.

[18] The Lord will rescue me from every evil work and will save me into his heavenly kingdom; to whom be the glory unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

[19] Greet Prisca and Aquila and the house of ⟦onesiphorus|Onesiphoros|Onesiphorus⟧.

[20] ⟦erastus|Erastos|Erastus⟧ remained in ⟦corinth|Korinthos|Corinth⟧, but ⟦trophimus|Trophimos|Trophimus⟧ I left behind in ⟦miletus|Miletos|Miletus⟧, being sick.

[21] Be diligent to come before winter. ⟦eubulus|Euboulos|Eubulus⟧ greets you, and Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brothers.

[22] The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v1) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"I charge thee therefore" → "I solemnly testify"** (v1) — Greek Διαμαρτύρομαι, as 2:14; "you" and "therefore" are not in NA28.
- **"the Lord Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v1) — NA28 Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ, without "the Lord".
- **"who shall judge" → "who is about to judge"** (v1) — Greek τοῦ μέλλοντος κρίνειν.
- **"at his appearing and his kingdom" → "and by his appearing and his kingdom"** (v1) — NA28 καὶ τὴν ἐπιφάνειαν … καὶ τὴν βασιλείαν, accusatives of the thing sworn by; "by" is supplied for English. The later text has κατά "at".
- **"Preach" → "proclaim"** (v2) — Greek κήρυξον, to cry as a herald (κῆρυξ, 1:11; κήρυγμα "proclamation", v17).
- **"be instant" → "be at hand"** (v2) — Greek ἐπίστηθι, "stand by"; the same verb in v6, "is at hand".
- **"exhort" kept** (v2) — Greek παρακάλεσον: "exhort", "encourage" or "appeal"; here beside "reprove, rebuke", so "exhort".
- **"doctrine" → "teaching"** (v2) — Greek διδαχῇ, as Titus 1:9; "doctrine" is διδασκαλία (v3).
- **"the time will come" → "there will be a time"** (v3) — Greek ἔσται καιρός.
- **"endure sound doctrine" → "bear with the healthy doctrine"** (v3) — Greek ἀνέξονται; ὑγιαινούσης as Titus 1:9.
- **"after their own lusts" → "according to their own desires"** (v3) — Greek κατὰ τὰς ἰδίας ἐπιθυμίας.
- **"having itching ears" → "itching in their hearing"** (v3) — Greek κνηθόμενοι τὴν ἀκοήν, "being tickled as to the hearing"; ἀκοή "hearing" again in v4.
- **"turn away their ears" → "turn their hearing away"; "shall be turned unto fables" → "will be turned aside to the myths"** (v4) — Greek ἀποστρέψουσιν (the verb of 1:15), ἐκτραπήσονται (passive in form); μύθους as Titus 1:14.
- **"watch thou" → "be sober"** (v5) — Greek νῆφε.
- **"endure afflictions" → "suffer hardship"** (v5) — Greek κακοπάθησον, as 2:9.
- **"make full proof of thy ministry" → "fully accomplish your service"** (v5) — Greek πληροφόρησον, the same verb in v17; διακονία "service" (v11; 1:18).
- **"I am now ready to be offered" → "I am already being poured out as a drink offering"** (v6) — Greek ἤδη σπένδομαι, one verb, present passive: to be poured out as a libation.
- **"I have fought a good fight" → "I have contended the good contest"** (v7) — Greek τὸν καλὸν ἀγῶνα ἠγώνισμαι, noun and verb of one root, of the games as much as of battle; with the article. "Fight" is kept for μάχη (2:23–24).
- **"shall give me" → "will render to me"** (v8) — Greek ἀποδώσει, "give back, pay"; the same verb in v14.
- **"at that day" → "in that day"** (v8) — Greek ἐν ἐκείνῃ τῇ ἡμέρᾳ, as 1:18; "the righteous judge" stands after it in Greek.
- **"love his appearing" → "have loved his appearing"** (v8) — Greek ἠγαπηκόσι, a perfect.
- **"Do thy diligence to come shortly" → "Be diligent to come quickly"** (v9, 21) — Greek σπούδασον, as 2:15; ταχέως.
- **"hath forsaken me, having loved this present world" → "forsook me, having loved the present age"** (v10) — Greek ἐγκατέλιπεν (NA28, an aorist; also v16), τὸν νῦν αἰῶνα as Titus 2:12.
- **"Only Luke" → "Luke alone"** (v11) — Greek Λουκᾶς ἐστιν μόνος.
- **"Take Mark" → "Take up Mark"** (v11) — Greek ἀναλαβών, "having picked up".
- **"profitable to me for the ministry" → "useful to me for service"** (v11) — Greek εὔχρηστος (2:21; Phlm 11) εἰς διακονίαν, without the article.
- **"have I sent" → "I sent"** (v12) — Greek ἀπέστειλα, an aorist.
- **"left" → "left behind"** (v13, 20) — Greek ἀπέλιπον.
- **"did me much evil: the Lord reward him" → "showed me many evils — the Lord will render to him"** (v14) — Greek πολλά μοι κακὰ ἐνεδείξατο; NA28 ἀποδώσει, a future (a statement), where the later text has the wish ἀποδῴη.
- **"Of whom be thou ware also" → "against whom you also be on guard"** (v15) — Greek φυλάσσου, the middle of "guard" (1:12, 14).
- **"hath greatly withstood our words" → "greatly withstood our words"** (v15) — Greek ἀντέστη, an aorist, as 3:8; λόγοις "words".
- **"At my first answer no man stood with me" → "At my first defense no one came to my side"** (v16) — Greek ἀπολογίᾳ, a speech in defense; παρεγένετο, "came to be beside".
- **"I pray God that it may not be laid to their charge" → "may it not be reckoned to them"** (v16) — Greek μὴ αὐτοῖς λογισθείη, a wish; "I pray God" is not in the Greek.
- **"Notwithstanding the Lord stood with me" → "but the Lord stood by me"** (v17) — Greek ὁ δὲ κύριός μοι παρέστη.
- **"the preaching might be fully known" → "the proclamation might be fully accomplished"** (v17) — Greek τὸ κήρυγμα πληροφορηθῇ, as v5.
- **"all the Gentiles" → "all the nations"** (v17) — Greek πάντα τὰ ἔθνη.
- **"delivered out of the mouth of the lion" → "rescued out of a lion's mouth"** (v17) — Greek ἐκ στόματος λέοντος, no article; ἐρρύσθην as 3:11.
- **"And the Lord shall deliver me … and will preserve me unto" → "The Lord will rescue me … and will save me into"** (v18) — NA28 has no "And"; Greek σώσει εἰς τὴν βασιλείαν.
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v18) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων; the Greek has no verb ("to whom the glory").
- **"Salute" → "Greet"; "household" → "house"** (v19) — Greek Ἄσπασαι, the same verb as v21; οἶκον as 1:16.
- **"abode at" → "remained in"; "sick" kept** (v20) — Greek ἔμεινεν ἐν, the verb μένω ("abide" in 2:13; 3:14), here of a stay in a place: "remain"; ἀσθενοῦντα, "being weak, sick".
- **"The Lord Jesus Christ be with thy spirit" → "The Lord be with your spirit"** (v22) — NA28 Ὁ κύριος μετὰ τοῦ πνεύματός σου; "Jesus Christ" and the closing "Amen" are not in the critical text. The Greek has no verb in either sentence.
- **"you"** (v22) — "your spirit" is singular (σου), Timothy; "with you" is plural (ὑμῶν).

### Notes
- **v1** — the same word "appearing" (ἐπιφάνεια) in v8 and 1:10.
- **v14–17** — one sentence in Greek, with two asides.
