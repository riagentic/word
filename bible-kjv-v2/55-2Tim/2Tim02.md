# 2 Timothy 2

[1] You therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧,

[2] and the things which you heard from me through many witnesses, these entrust to faithful people, who will be competent to teach others also.

[3] Join in suffering hardship as a good soldier of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[4] No one serving as a soldier entangles himself in the affairs of livelihood, that he may please the one who enlisted him.

[5] And if anyone also competes in the games, he is not crowned unless he has competed lawfully.

[6] The laboring farmer must be the first to partake of the fruits.

[7] Consider what I say, for the Lord will give you understanding in all things.

[8] Remember ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, raised from the dead, of the seed of David, according to my gospel,

[9] in which I suffer hardship unto bonds as an evildoer. But **the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is not bound**.

[10] Because of this I endure all things because of the elect, that they also may obtain the salvation that is in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ with eternal glory.

[11] Faithful is the word:

For if we died together, we shall also live together;
[12] if we endure, we shall also reign together;
if we shall deny him, that one also will deny us;
[13] if we are faithless, that one abides faithful —
for he cannot deny himself.

[14] Remind them of these things, solemnly testifying before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, not to fight about words, useful for nothing, to the overthrow of the hearers.

[15] Be diligent to present yourself approved to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, a worker unashamed, cutting straight the word of truth.

[16] But the profane empty talkings shun, for they will advance to more ungodliness,

[17] and their word will eat like gangrene; of whom are ⟦hymenaeus|Hymenaios|Hymenaeus⟧ and ⟦philetus|Philetos|Philetus⟧,

[18] who have missed the mark concerning the truth, saying that a resurrection has already taken place, and they overturn the faith of some.

[19] Nevertheless the firm foundation of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ stands, having this seal: "**⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The Lord⟧ knew those who are his**," and, "Let everyone who names the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness."

[20] But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver but also of wood and of clay, and some unto honor and some unto dishonor.

[21] If therefore anyone cleanses himself from these, he will be a vessel unto honor, sanctified, useful to the master, prepared for every good work.

[22] But flee the youthful desires, and pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace with those who call upon the Lord out of a pure heart.

[23] But the foolish and untrained questionings refuse, knowing that they beget fights.

[24] And a slave of the Lord must not fight, but be mild toward all, apt to teach, bearing evil,

[25] in meekness training those who oppose, if perhaps ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may give them repentance unto full knowledge of the truth,

[26] and they may become sober again out of the snare of the devil, having been taken alive by him unto the will of that one.

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv9, 14, 15, 19, 25) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"be strong in the grace" → "be strengthened in the grace"** (v1) — Greek ἐνδυναμοῦ ἐν τῇ χάριτι, passive (or middle); ἐν "in", not "by".
- **"among many witnesses" → "through many witnesses"** (v2) — Greek διὰ πολλῶν μαρτύρων.
- **"commit" → "entrust"** (v2) — Greek παράθου, the verb of παραθήκη "deposit" (1:12, 14).
- **"faithful men, who shall be able" → "faithful people, who will be competent"** (v2) — Greek πιστοῖς ἀνθρώποις, human beings; ἱκανοί, "sufficient", not the word "able" (δυνατός) of 1:12.
- **"Thou therefore endure hardness" → "Join in suffering hardship"** (v3) — NA28 συγκακοπάθησον, as 1:8; the later text has σὺ οὖν κακοπάθησον.
- **"entangleth himself with the affairs of this life" → "entangles himself in the affairs of livelihood"** (v4) — Greek ταῖς τοῦ βίου πραγματείαις; βίος is life as livelihood, not ζωή. "This" is not in the Greek. ἐμπλέκεται can also be passive, "is entangled".
- **"strive for masteries" → "competes in the games"** (v5) — Greek ἀθλῇ, to compete as an athlete (a different word from the "contest", ἀγών, of 4:7); "lawfully" (νομίμως) kept.
- **"The husbandman that laboureth must be first partaker of the fruits" → "The laboring farmer must be the first to partake of the fruits"** (v6) — Greek τὸν κοπιῶντα γεωργὸν δεῖ πρῶτον τῶν καρπῶν μεταλαμβάνειν.
- **"and the Lord give thee" → "for the Lord will give you"** (v7) — NA28 ὃ λέγω· δώσει γάρ, a future; the later text has ἃ … δῴη, a wish.
- **"was raised" → "raised"** (v8) — Greek ἐγηγερμένον, a perfect passive participle: "having been raised". Greek order: raised from the dead, then "of the seed of David".
- **"Wherein I suffer trouble" → "in which I suffer hardship"** (v9) — Greek κακοπαθῶ, "suffer evil"; κακοῦργος "evildoer" has the same root.
- **"bonds" kept** (v9) — Greek δεσμῶν, with δέδεται "is bound"; a different word from "chain" (ἅλυσις) in 1:16.
- **"Therefore … for the elect's sakes" → "Because of this … because of the elect"** (v10) — Greek διὰ τοῦτο … διὰ τοὺς ἐκλεκτούς, the same preposition twice.
- **"It is a faithful saying" → "Faithful is the word"** (v11) — Greek πιστὸς ὁ λόγος, as Titus 3:8.
- **"if we be dead with him, we shall also live with him" → "if we died together, we shall also live together"** (v11–12) — Greek συναπεθάνομεν, συζήσομεν, συμβασιλεύσομεν: verbs with συν- "with", and no "him". With whom is not said; "that one" (v12) points to the *Mashiach* (Rom 6:8).
- **"suffer" → "endure"** (v12) — Greek ὑπομένομεν, the same verb as v10.
- **"if we deny him" → "if we shall deny him"** (v12) — NA28 ἀρνησόμεθα, a future; "him" is supplied for English, as in Titus 1:16.
- **"he" → "that one"** (v12, 13) — Greek ἐκεῖνος, as Titus 3:7.
- **"believe not" → "are faithless"** (v13) — Greek ἀπιστοῦμεν: "do not believe" or "are unfaithful"; it answers πιστός "faithful".
- **"abideth" → "abides"** (v13) — Greek μένει.
- **"he cannot deny himself" → "for he cannot deny himself"** (v13) — ἀρνήσασθαι γὰρ ἑαυτόν (WH, Treg, NA28); SBLGNT and RP have no γάρ "for".
- **"put them in remembrance" → "Remind them"** (v14) — Greek ὑπομίμνῃσκε has no object; "them" is supplied for English.
- **"charging them before the Lord" → "solemnly testifying before *Elohim*"** (v14) — Greek διαμαρτυρόμενος, the same verb as 4:1. τοῦ θεοῦ (WH, Treg, NA28); SBLGNT and RP read τοῦ κυρίου, "before the Lord".
- **"strive not about words to no profit, but to the subverting" → "not to fight about words, useful for nothing, to the overthrow"** (v14) — Greek λογομαχεῖν (μάχη "fight", v23, 24); ἐπ᾽ οὐδὲν χρήσιμον; ἐπὶ καταστροφῇ, "a turning upside down", with no "but".
- **"Study to shew thyself approved" → "Be diligent to present yourself approved"** (v15) — Greek σπούδασον (Titus 3:12) … παραστῆσαι.
- **"that needeth not to be ashamed" → "unashamed"** (v15) — Greek ἀνεπαίσχυντον: not ashamed, or with nothing to be ashamed of.
- **"rightly dividing" → "cutting straight"** (v15) — Greek ὀρθοτομοῦντα, "cutting in a straight line" (in Prov 3:6; 11:5 LXX of making a straight way).
- **"shun profane and vain babblings" → "the profane empty talkings shun"** (v16) — Greek βεβήλους κενοφωνίας, "empty sounds"; περιΐστασο "shun" as Titus 3:9.
- **"increase" → "advance"** (v16) — Greek προκόψουσιν, also 3:9, 13. "They" can be the talkings or the people who talk.
- **"will eat as doth a canker" → "will eat like gangrene"** (v17) — Greek ὡς γάγγραινα νομὴν ἕξει, literally "will have pasture", the physicians' word for a spreading sore. λόγος "word" as elsewhere.
- **"have erred" → "have missed the mark"** (v18) — Greek ἠστόχησαν.
- **"the resurrection" → "a resurrection"** (v18) — ἀνάστασιν, without the article (SBLGNT, WH, Treg); NA28 and RP read τὴν ἀνάστασιν, "the resurrection".
- **"overthrow" → "overturn"** (v18) — Greek ἀνατρέπουσιν, as Titus 1:11.
- **"knoweth" → "knew"** (v19) — Greek Ἔγνω, an aorist, as in Num 16:5 LXX.
- **"the name of Christ" → "the name of the Lord"** (v19) — NA28 κυρίου.
- **"iniquity" → "unrighteousness"** (v19) — Greek ἀδικίας.
- **"of earth" → "of clay"** (v20) — Greek ὀστράκινα, of baked clay. "Unto honor … unto dishonor": εἰς τιμήν, εἰς ἀτιμίαν.
- **"purge himself from these" → "cleanses himself from these"** (v21) — Greek ἐκκαθάρῃ ἑαυτὸν ἀπὸ τούτων, "cleanse out"; what "these" are is not said.
- **"meet for the master's use, and prepared" → "useful to the master, prepared"** (v21) — Greek εὔχρηστον τῷ δεσπότῃ (εὔχρηστος as Phlm 11); NA28 has no "and".
- **"lusts" → "desires"** (v22) — Greek ἐπιθυμίας, as Titus 2:12.
- **"foolish and unlearned questions avoid" → "the foolish and untrained questionings refuse"** (v23) — Greek ἀπαιδεύτους, "without παιδεία, training" (v25; 3:16); ζητήσεις and παραιτοῦ as Titus 3:9, 10.
- **"gender strifes" → "beget fights"** (v23) — Greek γεννῶσι μάχας; "fight" (μάχεσθαι) in v24.
- **"the servant of the Lord" → "a slave of the Lord"** (v24) — Greek δοῦλον κυρίου.
- **"gentle" → "mild"** (v24) — Greek ἤπιον; "gentle" is ἐπιεικής in Titus 3:2.
- **"patient" → "bearing evil"** (v24) — Greek ἀνεξίκακον, "holding up under evil".
- **"instructing" → "training"** (v25) — Greek παιδεύοντα, as Titus 2:12: bringing up with discipline.
- **"if God peradventure will give" → "if perhaps *Elohim* may give"** (v25) — Greek μήποτε δώῃ, a subjunctive.
- **"to the acknowledging of the truth" → "unto full knowledge of the truth"** (v25) — Greek εἰς ἐπίγνωσιν ἀληθείας; ἐπίγνωσις, not the plain γνῶσις. Also 3:7; Titus 1:1.
- **"recover themselves" → "become sober again"** (v26) — Greek ἀνανήψωσιν; νήφω "be sober" in 4:5.
- **"who are taken captive by him at his will" → "having been taken alive by him unto the will of that one"** (v26) — Greek ἐζωγρημένοι ὑπ᾽ αὐτοῦ εἰς τὸ ἐκείνου θέλημα. Two different pronouns: both can be the devil, or "him" is the devil (or the Lord's slave) and "that one" is *Elohim*. The verse leaves it open.

### Notes
- **v19** echoes Num 16:5. The Hebrew there has the name *Yahweh*; the Septuagint has ὁ θεός, and the Greek here κύριος without the article. The second saying has no single source (compare Num 16:26; Isa 26:13; 52:11).
- **v11–13** — four lines beginning with εἰ "if"; often taken as an early hymn.
