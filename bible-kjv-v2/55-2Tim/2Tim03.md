# 2 Timothy 3

[1] But understand this, that in the last days there will come times of difficulty.

[2] For people will be lovers of self, lovers of money, proud, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy,

[3] heartless, unappeasable, slanderous, without self-control, brutal, not loving good,

[4] treacherous, reckless, swollen with conceit, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[5] having the appearance of godliness, but denying its power. Avoid such people.

[6] For among them are those who creep into households and capture weak women, burdened with sins and led astray by various passions,

[7] always learning and never able to arrive at a knowledge of the truth.

[8] Just as ⟦jannes|Yannes|Jannes⟧ and ⟦jambres|Yambres|Jambres⟧ opposed ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so these men also oppose the truth, men corrupted in mind and disqualified regarding the faith.

[9] But they will not get very far, for their folly will be plain to all, as was that of those two men.

[10] You, however, have followed my teaching, my conduct, my aim in life, my faith, my patience, my love, my steadfastness,

[11] my persecutions and sufferings that happened to me at ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, at ⟦iconium|Ikonion|Iconium⟧, and at Lystra — which persecutions I endured; yet from them all the Lord rescued me.

[12] Indeed, all who desire to live a godly life in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ will be persecuted,

[13] while evil people and impostors will go on from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived.

[14] But as for you, continue in what you have learned and have firmly believed, knowing from whom you learned it

[15] and how from childhood you have been acquainted with the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[16] **All Scripture is breathed out by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness,**

**[17] that the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may be complete, equipped for every good work.**

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (vv12, 15) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 16, 17) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.

### Notes
- **v8** — *Yannes* and *Yambres* (Jannes and Jambres) are traditional Jewish names for Pharaoh's magicians (Ex 7:11).
