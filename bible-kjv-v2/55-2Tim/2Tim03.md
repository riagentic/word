# 2 Timothy 3

[1] But know this, that in the last days hard times will set in.

[2] For people will be lovers of self, lovers of money, boasters, haughty, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, ungrateful, undevout,

[3] without natural affection, implacable, slanderers, without self-control, savage, not lovers of good,

[4] betrayers, rash, puffed up, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[5] having a form of godliness but having denied its power; and from these turn away.

[6] For of these are those who creep into the houses and take captive little women heaped with sins, led by various desires,

[7] always learning and never able to come to full knowledge of the truth.

[8] And in the way that ⟦jannes|Yannes|Jannes⟧ and ⟦jambres|Yambres|Jambres⟧ withstood ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so these also withstand the truth, people corrupted in mind, unapproved concerning the faith.

[9] But they will not advance further, for their folly will be evident to all, as that of those two also came to be.

[10] But you followed closely my doctrine, conduct, purpose, faith, longsuffering, love, endurance,

[11] persecutions, sufferings, such as happened to me in ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, in ⟦iconium|Ikonion|Iconium⟧, in Lystra; what persecutions I bore — and out of them all the Lord rescued me.

[12] And indeed all who want to live godly in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ will be persecuted,

[13] but evil people and impostors will advance to the worse, deceiving and being deceived.

[14] But you, abide in the things which you learned and were assured of, knowing from whom you learned,

[15] and that from infancy you have known the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise unto salvation through faith that is in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[16] **All Scripture is ⟦elohim|Elohim-breathed|God-breathed⟧ and profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for training in righteousness,**

**[17] that the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may be fit, fitted out for every good work.**

## Changes

- **"Christ Jesus" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (vv12, 15) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv4, 16, 17) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"perilous times shall come" → "hard times will set in"** (v1) — Greek ἐνστήσονται καιροὶ χαλεποί: χαλεπός is "hard, harsh, fierce"; ἐνίστημι "to set in, be at hand".
- **"men" → "people"** (v2, 8, 13) — Greek ἄνθρωποι.
- **"lovers of their own selves, covetous" → "lovers of self, lovers of money"** (v2) — Greek φίλαυτοι, φιλάργυροι; four φιλ- words frame the list (v2, 4).
- **"proud" → "haughty"** (v2) — Greek ὑπερήφανοι. "Blasphemers" (βλάσφημοι) kept, as Titus 2:5; 3:2.
- **"unholy" → "undevout"** (v2) — Greek ἀνόσιοι, the opposite of ὅσιος "devout"; "holy" is ἅγιος.
- **"trucebreakers, false accusers, incontinent, fierce" → "implacable, slanderers, without self-control, savage"** (v3) — Greek ἄσπονδοι, "admitting no truce"; διάβολοι, as Titus 2:3; ἀκρατεῖς, the opposite of ἐγκρατής (Titus 1:8); ἀνήμεροι, "untamed".
- **"despisers of those that are good" → "not lovers of good"** (v3) — Greek ἀφιλάγαθοι, the opposite of φιλάγαθος (Titus 1:8).
- **"Traitors, heady, highminded" → "betrayers, rash, puffed up"** (v4) — Greek προδόται, προπετεῖς, τετυφωμένοι ("wrapped in smoke").
- **"denying the power thereof: from such turn away" → "having denied its power; and from these turn away"** (v5) — Greek ἠρνημένοι, a perfect; καὶ τούτους ἀποτρέπου.
- **"silly women laden with sins, led away with divers lusts" → "little women heaped with sins, led by various desires"** (v6) — Greek γυναικάρια, a diminutive of "woman", said with contempt; σεσωρευμένα, "piled up" (the verb again in 4:3); ἐπιθυμίαις ποικίλαις as Titus 3:3.
- **"lead captive" → "take captive"** (v6) — Greek αἰχμαλωτίζοντες, to take as a captive of war (Phlm 23).
- **"Now as" → "And in the way that"** (v8) — Greek ὃν τρόπον δέ.
- **"withstood … resist" → "withstood … withstand"** (v8) — one Greek verb, ἀνθίστημι; also 4:15.
- **"reprobate" → "unapproved"** (v8) — Greek ἀδόκιμοι, as Titus 1:16.
- **"proceed no further" → "not advance further"** (v9) — Greek προκόψουσιν, as 2:16; 3:13.
- **"manifest" → "evident"** (v9) — Greek ἔκδηλος; "folly" is ἄνοια, "being without mind" (νοῦς, v8). "Those two" for ἐκείνων, "of those".
- **"hast fully known" → "followed closely"** (v10) — NA28 παρηκολούθησας, an aorist: to follow at someone's side, or to trace out. The later text has the perfect.
- **"manner of life" → "conduct"; "charity, patience" → "love, endurance"** (v10) — Greek ἀγωγῇ, ἀγάπῃ, ὑπομονῇ. μου "my" stands once, before the whole list; πρόθεσις "purpose" as 1:9.
- **"afflictions" → "sufferings"** (v11) — Greek παθήμασιν.
- **"endured" → "bore"; "delivered" → "rescued"** (v11) — Greek ὑπήνεγκα, "bore up under"; ἐρρύσατο, also 4:17, 18.
- **"all that will live godly" → "all who want to live godly"** (v12) — Greek οἱ θέλοντες.
- **"seducers shall wax worse and worse, deceiving, and being deceived" → "impostors will advance to the worse, deceiving and being deceived"** (v13) — Greek γόητες, properly "sorcerers" (those who wail spells), then "swindlers"; πλανῶντες καὶ πλανώμενοι, "leading astray and being led astray" (or "going astray").
- **"the knowledge of the truth" → "full knowledge of the truth"** (v7) — Greek ἐπίγνωσιν ἀληθείας, as 2:25.
- **"continue" → "abide"; "hast been assured of" → "were assured of"** (v14) — Greek μένε; ἐπιστώθης, an aorist passive.
- **"of whom" → "from whom"** (v14) — NA28 παρὰ τίνων, plural: more than one teacher (1:5). The later text has the singular τίνος.
- **"from a child" → "from infancy"** (v15) — Greek ἀπὸ βρέφους, a baby.
- **"the holy scriptures" → "the sacred writings"** (v15) — Greek ἱερὰ γράμματα: ἱερός, not ἅγιος; γράμματα, not γραφή (v16). τὰ ἱερά, with the article (Treg, NA28, RP); SBLGNT and WH read ἱερά without it, "sacred writings".
- **"through faith which is in" kept** (v15) — Greek διὰ πίστεως τῆς ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ.
- **"is given by inspiration of God" → "is *Elohim*-breathed"** (v16) — Greek θεόπνευστος, one adjective. The Greek has no verb: "All Scripture is *Elohim*-breathed and profitable", or "Every *Elohim*-breathed Scripture is also profitable". πᾶσα γραφή is "all" or "every".
- **"instruction" → "training"** (v16) — Greek παιδείαν, as Titus 2:12. "Doctrine" (διδασκαλίαν) and "reproof" (NA28 ἐλεγμόν) kept.
- **"perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works" → "fit, fitted out for every good work"** (v17) — Greek ἄρτιος … ἐξηρτισμένος, two words of one root; πᾶν ἔργον ἀγαθόν singular.
- **"man of God" kept** (v17) — Greek ὁ τοῦ θεοῦ ἄνθρωπος; ἄνθρωπος is a human being, not specifically a male.

### Notes
- **v8** — *Yannes* and *Yambres* (Jannes and Jambres) are traditional Jewish names for Pharaoh's magicians (Ex 7:11).
- **v10–11** — "persecutions, sufferings" continue the list of v10 in Greek.
