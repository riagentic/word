# Ecclesiastes 11

[1] Send away your bread upon the face of the waters,
for in the multitude of the days you will find it.

[2] Give a portion to seven, and also to eight,
for you do not know what evil will be on the earth.

[3] If the clouds are filled with rain,
on the earth they empty it out;
and if a tree falls in the south or if in the north,
the place where the tree falls, there it will be.

[4] He who watches the wind will not sow,
and he who looks at the clouds will not reap.

[5] As you do not know what is the way of the wind,
like the bones in the belly of her who is full,
so you do not know the work of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who makes everything.

[6] In the morning sow your seed,
and at evening do not let your hand rest,
for you do not know which will succeed, this or that,
or whether both of them alike are good.

[7] And sweet is the light,
and good for the eyes to see the sun.

[8] For if man lives many years, in all of them let him be glad,
and let him remember the days of darkness, for they will be many.
All that comes is vapor.

[9] Be glad, young man, in your childhood,
and let your heart do you good in the days of your youth,
and walk in the ways of your heart
and in the sights of your eyes;
and know that for all these
⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will bring you into the judgment.

[10] And remove vexation from your heart,
and make evil pass from your flesh,
for childhood and the dawn are vapor.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v5, 9) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article (WORDS-HE: the plain token).
- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew הֶבֶל "vapor, breath" (WISDOM.md); "vanity" is the traditional rendering.
- **"Cast thy bread upon the waters: for thou shalt find it after many days" → "Send away your bread upon the face of the waters, for in the multitude of the days you will find it"** (v1) — Hebrew שַׁלַּח, piel of שָׁלַח "send away, let go" (WORDS-HE), not "cast"; עַל־פְּנֵי "upon the face of"; בְרֹב הַיָּמִים "in the multitude of the days".
- **"and also to eight; for thou knowest not what evil shall be upon the earth"** (v2) — KJV kept: Hebrew וְגַם "and also"; רָעָה "evil" (WORDS-HE; not "disaster"); חֵלֶק "portion" (WISDOM.md).
- **"If the clouds be full of rain, they empty themselves upon the earth" → "If the clouds are filled with rain, on the earth they empty it out"** (v3) — Hebrew יִמָּלְאוּ, nifal, "are filled"; עַל־הָאָרֶץ יָרִיקוּ "on the earth they empty out" (הֵרִיק, the rain is the object). **"toward the south, or toward the north … there it shall be" → "in the south or if in the north … there it will be"** — Hebrew בַּדָּרוֹם וְאִם בַּצָּפוֹן, the second "if" written; עֵץ without the article, then הָעֵץ "the tree"; יְהוּא, a rare form of יִהְיֶה "it will be" (not "lie").
- **"He that observeth the wind … he that regardeth the clouds" → "He who watches the wind … he who looks at the clouds"** (v4) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר, from שָׁמַר "keep" (WORDS-HE): here "watch" ("keeps the wind" would say he holds it); רֹאֶה בְּ "looks at". רוּחַ "wind" (the one word of v5; WORDS-HE).
- **"what is the way of the spirit, nor how the bones do grow in the womb of her that is with child" → "what is the way of the wind, like the bones in the belly of her who is full"** (v5) — Hebrew מַה־דֶּרֶךְ הָרוּחַ: רוּחַ is "wind" in v4 and "spirit, breath" of life elsewhere (12:7); the comparison with the bones allows both, and "wind" keeps the word of v4 (the other reading in this note). כַּעֲצָמִים "like the bones" (some Hebrew manuscripts read בַּעֲצָמִים "in the bones", from memory; the MT is kept); no verb "grow" is written. בְּבֶטֶן "in the belly" (WORDS-HE, not "womb"); הַמְּלֵאָה "the full one" (= the woman with child). מַעֲשֵׂה "the work", singular.
- **"in the evening withhold not thine hand … whether shall prosper … or whether they both shall be alike good" → "at evening do not let your hand rest … which will succeed … or whether both of them alike are good"** (v6) — Hebrew אַל־תַּנַּח יָדֶךָ "do not let your hand rest" (hifil of נוּחַ, the verb of 10:4); יִכְשָׁר "succeed" (כָּשֵׁר, as 10:10); כְּאֶחָד "as one, alike"; זֶרַע "seed".
- **"Truly the light is sweet, and a pleasant thing it is" → "And sweet is the light, and good"** (v7) — Hebrew וּמָתוֹק "and sweet" (no "truly"); טוֹב "good".
- **"But if a man live many years, and rejoice in them all; yet let him remember" → "For if man lives many years, in all of them let him be glad, and let him remember"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּי אִם "for if"; יִשְׂמָח, יִזְכֹּר: the second half is a wish ("let him"), joined by "and" ("yet" is not written); הָאָדָם "man". כָּל־שֶׁבָּא הָבֶל "all that comes is vapor" (or "all that has come").
- **"Rejoice, O young man, in thy youth" → "Be glad, young man, in your childhood"** (v9) — no "O" (WORDS-HE reviewer row); שְׂמַח "be glad", as v8; בָּחוּר "young man" (WORDS-HE); יַלְדוּת "childhood" (from יֶלֶד "child"; also v10), kept apart from בְּחוּרוֹת "youth" (the years of a בָּחוּר) in the next line. **"let thy heart cheer thee" → "let your heart do you good"** — Hebrew וִיטִיבְךָ, hifil of יָטַב. **"the sight of thine eyes" → "the sights of your eyes"** — Hebrew מַרְאֵי, plural. **"but know thou … into judgment" → "and know … into the judgment"** — Hebrew וְדָע "and know"; בַּמִּשְׁפָּט with the article.
- **"Therefore remove sorrow from thy heart, and put away evil from thy flesh" → "And remove vexation from your heart, and make evil pass from your flesh"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהָסֵר "and remove" (no "therefore"); כַּעַס "vexation" (WORDS-HE); הַעֲבֵר "make pass" (hifil of עָבַר); רָעָה "evil", בָּשָׂר "flesh" (the earlier "pain from your body" changed both). **"childhood and youth" → "childhood and the dawn"** — Hebrew הַשַּׁחֲרוּת, only here: from שַׁחַר "dawn" (the dawn of life) or from שָׁחֹר "black" (the black hair of youth); uncertain.
