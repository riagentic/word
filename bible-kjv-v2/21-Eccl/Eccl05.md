# Ecclesiastes 5

[1] Keep your foot when you go to the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; and to draw near to hear rather than the fools' giving of a sacrifice, for they do not know that they do evil.

[2] Do not be hasty with your mouth, and let your heart not hasten to bring out a word before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is in the heavens and you are on the earth; therefore let your words be few.

[3] For the dream comes with much occupation, and the voice of a fool with many words.

[4] When you vow a vow to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, do not delay to repay it, for there is no delight in fools. What you vow, repay.

[5] Better that you do not vow than that you vow and do not repay.

[6] Do not give your mouth to make your flesh sin, and do not say before the messenger that it is an error. Why should ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be wrathful at your voice and destroy the work of your hands?

[7] For in a multitude of dreams, and vapors, and words in plenty — but fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[8] If you see the extortion of the destitute and the robbery of judgment and righteousness in the province, do not be astonished at the matter, for a high one above a high one keeps watch, and high ones are over them.

[9] And the advantage of a land in all, it is: a king for a field that is worked.

[10] One who loves silver will not be sated with silver,
and whoever loves abundance, not with yield;
this also is vapor.

[11] When the good increases, those who eat it increase;
and what success is there for its owners
except the sight of his eyes?

[12] Sweet is the sleep of the one who works,
whether he eats little or much;
but the plenty of the rich man does not let him sleep.

[13] There is a sick evil that I have seen under the sun: riches kept for their owner to his evil.

[14] And those riches perished in an evil occupation; and he begot a son, and there is nothing in his hand.

[15] As he came out from the belly of his mother, naked he shall return, to go as he came; and he shall not carry anything for his toil that he may take away in his hand.

[16] And this also is a sick evil: exactly as he came, so he shall go; and what advantage is there for him, that he toils for the wind?

[17] Also all his days he eats in darkness, and he is much vexed, and his sickness, and wrath.

[18] Behold, what I myself have seen: it is good, which is beautiful, to eat and to drink and to see good in all his toil with which he toils under the sun, the number of the days of his life which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given him, for it is his portion.

[19] Also every man to whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given riches and wealth, and has given him power to eat of it and to take up his portion and to rejoice in his toil — this is the gift of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] For not much will he remember the days of his life, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ answers in the gladness of his heart.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 18, 19, 20) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim* (v1, 2, 6, 7, 18, 19, 20 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים; v4, 19b without).
- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v7, 10) — Hebrew הֶבֶל, literally "vapor, breath"; traditional "vanity". v7 has the plural הֲבָלִים "vapors".
- **"Keep thy foot" → "Keep your foot"** (v1) — the verse follows the qere רַגְלְךָ "your foot"; the written form (ketiv) is רגליך "your feet". שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"and be more ready to hear, than to give the sacrifice of fools" → "and to draw near to hear rather than the fools' giving of a sacrifice"** (v1) — Hebrew וְקָרוֹב לִשְׁמֹעַ מִתֵּת הַכְּסִילִים זָבַח: no "better" or "be ready" is written; מִן "rather than" carries the comparison. קָרוֹב "draw near" (or "near"); זֶבַח "sacrifice" without the article.
- **"for they consider not that they do evil" → "for they do not know that they do evil"** (v1) — Hebrew אֵינָם יוֹדְעִים לַעֲשׂוֹת רָע, literally "they do not know to do evil"; the infinitive is read as what they do not know.
- **"Be not rash with thy mouth" → "Do not be hasty with your mouth"** (v2) — Hebrew אַל־תְּבַהֵל עַל־פִּיךָ, literally "upon your mouth"; יְמַהֵר "hasten" (מִהַר); לְהוֹצִיא "to bring out"; בַּשָּׁמַיִם "in the heavens", עַל־הָאָרֶץ "on the earth".
- **"For a dream cometh through the multitude of business; and a fool's voice is known by multitude of words" → "For the dream comes with much occupation, and the voice of a fool with many words"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּרֹב עִנְיָן … בְּרֹב דְּבָרִים: רֹב "abundance" in both halves; עִנְיָן "occupation"; "is known" is not written; הַחֲלוֹם has the article.
- **"defer not to pay it; for he hath no pleasure in fools: pay that which thou hast vowed" → "do not delay to repay it, for there is no delight in fools. What you vow, repay"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵין חֵפֶץ בַּכְּסִילִים "there is no delight in fools" (no "he" written); שִׁלֵּם "repay" (of a vow; the שִׁלֵּם row); נָדַר נֶדֶר "vow a vow".
- **"Suffer not thy mouth to cause thy flesh to sin" → "Do not give your mouth to make your flesh sin"** (v6) — Hebrew אַל־תִּתֵּן אֶת־פִּיךָ לַחֲטִיא, literally "do not give"; הֶחֱטִיא "make sin".
- **"before the angel, that it was an error" → "before the messenger that it is an error"** (v6) — Hebrew הַמַּלְאָךְ "the messenger" (the same word is "angel"; who is meant — a messenger of the house of Elohim or a heavenly one — the verse does not say); שְׁגָגָה הִיא "it is an error" (nominal: present). קָצַף "be wrathful"; חִבֵּל "destroy".
- **"For in the multitude of dreams and many words there are also divers vanities: but fear thou God" → "For in a multitude of dreams, and vapors, and words in plenty — but fear *Elohim*"** (v7) — the first half has no verb (word for word: "in a multitude of dreams and vapors and words much"); the dash keeps it unfinished. "there are also divers" is not written. אֶת־הָאֱלֹהִים stands first (emphasis); יְרָא imperative "fear".
- **"the oppression of the poor, and violent perverting of judgment and justice in a province" → "the extortion of the destitute and the robbery of judgment and righteousness in the province"** (v8) — Hebrew עֹשֶׁק "extortion" (the עָשַׁק row); רָשׁ "destitute" (as Ps 82:3; beside דַּל "poor"); גֵּזֶל "robbery"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment", צֶדֶק "righteousness"; הַמְּדִינָה with the article.
- **"marvel not at the matter: for he that is higher than the highest regardeth; and there be higher than they" → "do not be astonished at the matter, for a high one above a high one keeps watch, and high ones are over them"** (v8) — Hebrew חֵפֶץ, "delight" in v4, here "matter" (as in Ecclesiastes elsewhere: the one word); גָּבֹהַּ מֵעַל גָּבֹהַ שֹׁמֵר "a high one above a high one keeping": שֹׁמֵר is the participle of שָׁמַר "keep, guard" — whether the higher one watches over the lower or watches out for himself is not said.
- **"Moreover the profit of the earth is for all: the king himself is served by the field" → "And the advantage of a land in all, it is: a king for a field that is worked"** (v9) — Hebrew וְיִתְרוֹן אֶרֶץ בַּכֹּל הוּא מֶלֶךְ לְשָׂדֶה נֶעֱבָד; the verse follows the qere הוּא, the ketiv is היא (feminine; the same sense). The meaning is uncertain: נֶעֱבָד "worked" (of ground) may go with the field ("a cultivated field") or with the king ("a king is made servant to the field", KJV "served"). יִתְרוֹן "advantage".
- **"He that loveth silver … nor he that loveth abundance with increase" → "One who loves silver … and whoever loves abundance, not with yield"** (v10) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף "silver" (also "money"); הָמוֹן "multitude, tumult", here of riches: "abundance" (another sense than the "multitude" of a crowd: the one word); תְּבוּאָה "yield"; לֹא תְבוּאָה, literally "not yield": "be sated" carries over from the first line. שָׂבַע "be sated".
- **"When goods increase … and what good is there to the owners thereof, saving the beholding of them with their eyes?" → "When the good increases … and what success is there for its owners except the sight of his eyes?"** (v11) — Hebrew הַטּוֹבָה "the good"; כִּשְׁרוֹן "success" (from כָּשֵׁר "succeed"); בְּעָלֶיהָ "its owners" (plural) beside עֵינָיו "his eyes" (singular). The verse follows the qere רְאוּת "sight"; the ketiv ראית is another spelling.
- **"The sleep of a labouring man" → "the sleep of the one who works"** (v12) — Hebrew הָעֹבֵד, participle of עָבַד "serve, work (the ground)"; הַשָּׂבָע "the plenty"; אֵינֶנּוּ מַנִּיחַ לוֹ לִישׁוֹן, literally "does not leave him to sleep".
- **"There is a sore evil … riches kept for the owners thereof to their hurt" → "There is a sick evil … riches kept for their owner to his evil"** (v13; also v16) — Hebrew רָעָה חוֹלָה "a sick evil" (KJV "sore", "grievous"); לִבְעָלָיו, plural in form ("its owners"), with לְרָעָתוֹ "to his evil" (רָעָה, the word at the head of the verse; "hurt" is the sense).
- **"But those riches perish by evil travail: and he begetteth a son" → "And those riches perished in an evil occupation; and he begot a son"** (v14) — Hebrew perfects וְאָבַד … וְהוֹלִיד; עִנְיַן רָע "an evil occupation" (bad business); whose hand is empty, the father's or the son's, the Hebrew does not say.
- **"As he came forth of his mother's womb … and shall take nothing of his labour" → "As he came out from the belly of his mother … and he shall not carry anything for his toil"** (v15) — Hebrew בֶּטֶן "belly"; עָמָל "toil" (also v16, 18, 19: "labour" in KJV); מְאוּמָה לֹא־יִשָּׂא "not anything shall he carry".
- **"in all points as he came" → "exactly as he came"** (v16) — Hebrew כָּל־עֻמַּת שֶׁ, literally "all alongside that"; יִתְרוֹן "advantage"; עָמַל לָרוּחַ "toil for the wind".
- **"and he hath much sorrow and wrath with his sickness" → "and he is much vexed, and his sickness, and wrath"** (v17) — Hebrew וְכָעַס הַרְבֵּה, pointed as a verb ("and he is vexed much"), then the two nouns וְחָלְיוֹ וָקָצֶף "and his sickness and wrath" with no verb; kept so.
- **"it is good and comely for one to eat … and to enjoy the good of all his labour … all the days of his life" → "it is good, which is beautiful, to eat … and to see good in all his toil … the number of the days of his life"** (v18) — Hebrew טוֹב אֲשֶׁר־יָפֶה "good, which is beautiful" (יָפֶה "beautiful", as 3:11); לִרְאוֹת טוֹבָה "to see good" (= enjoy); מִסְפַּר יְמֵי "the number of the days of" (= the few days); חֵלֶק "portion". The verse follows the qere חַיָּיו; the ketiv חיו is a shorter spelling of the same word.
- **"riches and wealth, and hath given him power to eat thereof, and to take his portion" → "riches and wealth, and has given him power to eat of it and to take up his portion"** (v19) — Hebrew עֹשֶׁר וּנְכָסִים "riches and wealth" (as 2 Chr 1:11); הִשְׁלִיטוֹ "has given him power" (hifil of שָׁלַט "have power over", as Esth 9:1; root of שִׁלְטוֹן "power", 8:4, 8); לָשֵׂאת "to take up, carry"; כָּל־הָאָדָם "every man"; מַתַּת "gift".
- **"because God answereth him in the joy of his heart" → "for *Elohim* answers in the gladness of his heart"** (v20) — Hebrew מַעֲנֶה, hifil participle of עָנָה, with no object written ("him" is not in the Hebrew). The root is uncertain: עָנָה "answer", or עָנָה "be occupied" (the root of עִנְיָן "occupation"), "keeps (him) occupied with the gladness of his heart"; שִׂמְחָה "gladness".

### Notes
- **The Hebrew numbering** — our 5:1 is Heb 4:17; our 5:2–20 are Heb 5:1–19. Our verse numbers are kept.
