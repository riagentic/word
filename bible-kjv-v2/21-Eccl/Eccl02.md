# Ecclesiastes 2

[1] I myself said in my heart, "Come now, let me test you with gladness; and see good." And behold, it also is vapor.

[2] Of laughter I said, "It is mad," and of gladness, "What does this do?"

[3] I explored in my heart to draw my flesh with wine — my heart leading with wisdom — and to lay hold on foolishness, until I might see what is good for the sons of man, that they should do under the heavens the number of the days of their life.

[4] I made my works great: I built houses for myself; I planted vineyards for myself.

[5] I made for myself gardens and parks, and I planted in them trees of every fruit.

[6] I made for myself pools of water, to water from them a forest sprouting trees.

[7] I acquired male slaves and maidservants, and I had sons of the house; also livestock, herd and flock, I had in abundance, more than all who were before me in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[8] I collected for myself also silver and gold, and the treasured possession of kings and of the provinces. I made for myself male singers and female singers, and the delights of the sons of man: a mistress and mistresses.

[9] And I became great and added more than all who were before me in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; also my wisdom stood by me.

[10] And all that my eyes asked I did not set aside from them; I did not hold back my heart from any gladness, for my heart was glad from all my toil, and this was my portion from all my toil.

[11] And I myself turned to all my works that my hands had done, and to the toil that I had toiled to do; and behold, all is vapor and a pursuit of wind, and there is no advantage under the sun.

[12] And I myself turned to see wisdom and madness and foolishness. For what is the man who will come after the king? That which they have already done.

[13] And I myself saw that there is an advantage to wisdom over foolishness, like the advantage of the light over the darkness.

[14] The wise one — his eyes are in his head, and the fool walks in the darkness; and I myself also knew that one event befalls them all.

[15] And I myself said in my heart, "As the event of the fool, so it will befall me also; and why then have I been wise in excess?" And I spoke in my heart that this also is vapor.

[16] For there is no remembrance of the wise with the fool forever, since already, in the days that are coming, all is forgotten. And how the wise dies with the fool!

[17] And I hated life, for evil to me was the work that is done under the sun; for all is vapor and a pursuit of wind.

[18] And I myself hated all my toil that I toil under the sun, that I must leave it to the man who will be after me.

[19] And who knows whether he will be wise or foolish? And he will have power over all my toil that I have toiled and in which I have been wise under the sun. This also is vapor.

[20] And I myself turned about to make my heart despair over all the toil that I had toiled under the sun.

[21] For there is a man whose toil is with wisdom and with knowledge and with skill, and to a man who has not toiled in it he will give it as his portion. This also is vapor and a great evil.

[22] For what comes to man in all his toil, and in the pursuit of his heart, that he toils under the sun?

[23] For all his days are pains, and his occupation is vexation; even in the night his heart does not lie down. This also is vapor.

[24] There is no good in man that he should eat and drink and make his soul see good in his toil. This also I myself saw, that it is from the hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[25] For who will eat, and who will have enjoyment, apart from me?

[26] For to a man who is good before him he has given wisdom and knowledge and gladness; and to the sinner he has given an occupation, to gather and to collect, to give to one who is good before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. This also is vapor and a pursuit of wind.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v24, 26) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article (plain `elohim` token).
- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v1, 11, 15, 17, 19, 21, 23, 26) — Hebrew הֶבֶל "vapor, breath"; traditional "vanity". **"vexation of spirit" → "a pursuit of wind"** (v11, 17, 26) — Hebrew רְעוּת רוּחַ (sense of רְעוּת uncertain, see 1:14).
- **"I said in mine heart"** (v1, 15) · **"I turned myself"** (v11, 12, 20) · **"I saw"** (v13) · **"I hated"** (v18) · **"I myself perceived"** (v14) → "I myself …" — Hebrew adds the pronoun אֲנִי after the verb (emphatic); kept each time.
- **"Go to now, I will prove thee with mirth, therefore enjoy pleasure" → "Come now, let me test you with gladness; and see good"** (v1) — Hebrew לְכָה־נָּא "go now / come now"; אֲנַסְּכָה, cohortative of נִסָּה "test", "let me test you" (he speaks to his heart); שִׂמְחָה "gladness" (one word through the chapter, v1, 2, 10, 26); וּרְאֵה בְטוֹב "and see good" (= enjoy good; kept). **"this also is vanity"** — Hebrew גַם־הוּא הָבֶל "it also is vapor" (no verb).
- **"It is mad: and of mirth, What doeth it?" → "'It is mad,' and of gladness, 'What does this do?'"** (v2) — Hebrew מְהוֹלָל "mad" (made mad; root of הוֹלֵלוֹת "madness", v12); מַה־זֹּה עֹשָׂה "what does this do".
- **"I sought in mine heart to give myself unto wine, yet acquainting mine heart with wisdom" → "I explored in my heart to draw my flesh with wine — my heart leading with wisdom"** (v3) — Hebrew תַּרְתִּי "I explored" (תּוּר, as 1:13); לִמְשׁוֹךְ בַּיַּיִן אֶת־בְּשָׂרִי "to draw my flesh with wine" (sense uncertain: "cheer", "refresh", "lead along"); וְלִבִּי נֹהֵג בַּחָכְמָה "and my heart leading (driving) with wisdom". **"all the days of their life" → "the number of the days of their life"** — Hebrew מִסְפַּר יְמֵי חַיֵּיהֶם (a counted span; "few" is not written). **"under the heaven" → "under the heavens"** — Hebrew הַשָּׁמַיִם.
- **"I made me great works" → "I made my works great"** (v4) — Hebrew הִגְדַּלְתִּי מַעֲשָׂי "I made great my works".
- **"orchards" → "parks"** (v5) — Hebrew פַּרְדֵּסִים, a Persian loan word ("enclosed park"; Neh 2:8; Song 4:13). **"trees … of all kind of fruits" → "trees of every fruit"** — Hebrew עֵץ כָּל־פֶּרִי (collective singular).
- **"the wood that bringeth forth trees" → "a forest sprouting trees"** (v6) — Hebrew יַעַר צוֹמֵחַ עֵצִים.
- **"servants and maidens" → "male slaves and maidservants"** (v7) — Hebrew עֲבָדִים "slaves"; שְׁפָחוֹת "maidservants"; קָנִיתִי "I acquired" (קָנָה "get / buy"). **"servants born in my house" → "sons of the house"** — Hebrew בְנֵי־בַיִת (= slaves born in the household; kept). **"great possessions of great and small cattle" → "livestock, herd and flock … in abundance"** — Hebrew מִקְנֶה בָקָר וָצֹאן הַרְבֵּה.
- **"I gathered me" → "I collected for myself"** (v8) — Hebrew כָּנַסְתִּי (כָּנַס "collect", beside אָסַף "gather" in v26). **"the peculiar treasure of kings" → "the treasured possession of kings"** — Hebrew סְגֻלַּת מְלָכִים. **"I gat me" → "I made for myself"** — Hebrew עָשִׂיתִי לִי. **"as musical instruments, and that of all sorts" → "a mistress and mistresses"** — Hebrew שִׁדָּה וְשִׁדּוֹת, found only here: the meaning is uncertain. Taken as "lady, mistress" (a woman of the king's household), apposition to "the delights of the sons of man"; "chest(s)" and "musical instruments" are other guesses. פִּילֶגֶשׁ "concubine" is another word.
- **"So I was great, and increased more than all" → "And I became great and added more than all"** (v9) — Hebrew וְגָדַלְתִּי וְהוֹסַפְתִּי. **"my wisdom remained with me" → "my wisdom stood by me"** — Hebrew עָמְדָה לִּי, literally "stood to me".
- **"whatsoever mine eyes desired I kept not from them" → "all that my eyes asked I did not set aside from them"** (v10) — Hebrew שָׁאֲלוּ "asked"; אָצַלְתִּי "set aside". **"I withheld not" → "I did not hold back"** — Hebrew מָנַעְתִּי. **"rejoiced in all my labour … my portion of all my labour" → "was glad from all my toil … my portion from all my toil"** — Hebrew מִכָּל־עֲמָלִי twice; חֵלֶק "portion".
- **"Then I looked on all the works" → "And I myself turned to all my works"** (v11) — Hebrew וּפָנִיתִי אֲנִי בְּ "and I turned to". **"all was vanity … there was no profit" → "all is vapor … there is no advantage"** — nominal clauses (no verb); יִתְרוֹן "advantage".
- **"for what can the man do that cometh after the king? even that which hath been already done" → "For what is the man who will come after the king? That which they have already done."** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי מֶה הָאָדָם שֶׁיָּבוֹא אַחֲרֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ אֵת אֲשֶׁר־כְּבָר עָשׂוּהוּ, word for word: "for what (is) the man who will come after the king — that which already they have done (it / him)". The sense is debated ("what can he do but what was done before?"; "whom they made king long ago"); kept as written. "do" is not in the first clause.
- **"wisdom excelleth folly, as far as light excelleth darkness" → "there is an advantage to wisdom over foolishness, like the advantage of the light over the darkness"** (v13) — Hebrew יִתְרוֹן twice. סִכְלוּת "foolishness" (v3, 12, 13; see 1:17).
- **"one event happeneth to them all" → "one event befalls them all"** (v14, 15) — Hebrew מִקְרֶה … יִקְרֶה, noun and verb of קָרָה "happen, befall". כְּסִיל "fool". **"but the fool" → "and the fool"** — Hebrew וְ.
- **"and why was I then more wise?" → "and why then have I been wise in excess?"** (v15) — Hebrew יוֹתֵר "more, to excess" (root of יִתְרוֹן). **"Then I said in my heart" → "And I spoke in my heart"** — Hebrew וְדִבַּרְתִּי (beside וְאָמַרְתִּי "I said" in the first half).
- **"no remembrance of the wise more than of the fool" → "no remembrance of the wise with the fool"** (v16) — Hebrew עִם־הַכְּסִיל "with the fool" (= as of the fool; also in the last clause). **"How dieth the wise man? as the fool" → "And how the wise dies with the fool!"** — Hebrew וְאֵיךְ יָמוּת הֶחָכָם עִם־הַכְּסִיל (exclamation or question).
- **"grievous unto me" → "evil to me"** (v17) — Hebrew רַע עָלַי, literally "evil upon me".
- **"I should leave it" → "I must leave it"** (v18) — Hebrew שֶׁאַנִּיחֶנּוּ "that I shall leave it". אָדָם "man".
- **"a wise man or a fool" → "wise or foolish"** (v19) — Hebrew סָכָל "foolish (one)", kept apart from כְּסִיל "fool". **"have rule over" → "have power over"** — Hebrew שָׁלַט בְּ. **"wherein I have shewed myself wise" → "in which I have been wise"** — Hebrew שֶׁחָכַמְתִּי.
- **"I went about to cause my heart to despair of all the labour" → "I myself turned about to make my heart despair over all the toil"** (v20) — Hebrew וְסַבּוֹתִי "and I turned about"; לְיַאֵשׁ (piel) "to make despair".
- **"in equity" → "with skill"** (v21) — Hebrew כִּשְׁרוֹן "skill, success" (Eccl 2:21; 4:4; 5:11). **"shall he leave it for his portion" → "he will give it as his portion"** — Hebrew יִתְּנֶנּוּ "he will give it"; חֶלְקוֹ "his portion".
- **"what hath man … and of the vexation of his heart" → "what comes to man … and in the pursuit of his heart"** (v22) — Hebrew מֶה־הֹוֶה לָאָדָם, literally "what is becoming to man"; רַעְיוֹן לִבּוֹ "the pursuit of his heart" (the רַעְיוֹן of "a pursuit of wind", 1:17).
- **"all his days are sorrows, and his travail grief" → "all his days are pains, and his occupation is vexation"** (v23) — Hebrew מַכְאֹבִים "pains"; כַּעַס "vexation"; עִנְיָנוֹ "his occupation". **"taketh not rest" → "does not lie down"** — Hebrew לֹא־שָׁכַב.
- **"There is nothing better for a man, than that he should eat … make his soul enjoy good" → "There is no good in man that he should eat … make his soul see good"** (v24) — the MT reads אֵין־טוֹב בָּאָדָם שֶׁיֹּאכַל, "there is no good in man that he eat"; most translations supply "better than" (מִשֶּׁ) after 3:12, 22; 8:15 (אֵין טוֹב … כִּי אִם / מֵאֲשֶׁר): not written here, so not in the verse. וְהֶרְאָה אֶת־נַפְשׁוֹ טוֹב "and make his soul see good". **"This also I saw, that it was from the hand of God" → "This also I myself saw, that it is from the hand of Elohim"** — Hebrew רָאִיתִי אָנִי; הִיא "it (is)".
- **"or who else can hasten hereunto, more than I?" → "and who will have enjoyment, apart from me?"** (v25) — Hebrew יָחוּשׁ: uncertain; "have enjoyment" (חוּשׁ "feel, sense") is followed; "hasten" (חוּשׁ "hurry") and "worry" are the other proposals. חוּץ מִמֶּנִּי "apart from me" (MT); "apart from him" is the reading of the Septuagint and Syriac (from memory): not followed.
- **"For God giveth to a man that is good in his sight" → "For to a man who is good before him he has given"** (v26) — Hebrew לְאָדָם שֶׁטּוֹב לְפָנָיו נָתַן: no subject is written in this clause (*Elohim* stands only at the end); נָתַן perfect. **"travail" → "an occupation"** — Hebrew עִנְיָן. **"to gather and to heap up" → "to gather and to collect"** — Hebrew לֶאֱסוֹף וְלִכְנוֹס (אָסַף · כָּנַס, as v8). **"good before God"** — Hebrew לִפְנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים.

### Notes
- **"Under the sun"** (v11, 17, 18, 19, 20, 22) — *Qohelet*'s characteristic phrase (29× in the book) marking the earthly/horizontal perspective.
