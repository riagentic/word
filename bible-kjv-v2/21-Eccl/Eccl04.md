# Ecclesiastes 4

[1] And I myself returned and saw all the extortions that are done under the sun; and behold, the tears of the extorted, and they have no comforter; and from the hand of their extortioners there is power, and they have no comforter.

[2] And I myself praise the dead who have already died, more than the living who are still alive.

[3] And better than both of them is he who has not yet been, who has not seen the evil work that is done under the sun.

[4] And I myself saw all toil and all skill of work, that it is a man's jealousy of his neighbor. This also is vapor and a pursuit of wind.

[5] The fool folds his hands
and eats his flesh.

[6] Better is a palm full of quiet
than two fists full of toil and a pursuit of wind.

[7] And I myself returned and saw vapor under the sun:

[8] There is one, and there is no second; also son and brother he has none; and there is no end to all his toil; also his eye is not sated with riches. "And for whom am I toiling, and making my soul lack good?" This also is vapor, and it is an evil occupation.

[9] The two are better than the one,
because they have a good wage in their toil.

[10] For if they fall, the one will raise up his companion;
but woe to him, the one who falls,
and there is no second to raise him up!

[11] Also, if two lie down, then they have warmth;
but for one, how will he be warm?

[12] And if one overpowers him,
the two will stand against him;
and the threefold thread is not quickly snapped.

[13] Better is a child poor and wise than a king old and foolish, who no longer knows how to be warned.

[14] For from the house of the bound he came out to reign, for also in his kingdom he was born destitute.

[15] I saw all the living who walk about under the sun, with the child, the second, who will stand in his place.

[16] There is no end to all the people, to all before whom he was; also the latter ones will not be glad in him. For this also is vapor and a pursuit of wind.

## Changes

- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v4, 7, 8, 16) — Hebrew הֶבֶל "vapor, breath"; traditional "vanity". **"vexation of spirit" → "a pursuit of wind"** (v4, 6, 16) — Hebrew רְעוּת רוּחַ (v4, 6), רַעְיוֹן רוּחַ (v16); see 1:14.
- **"So I returned, and considered" → "And I myself returned and saw"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew וְשַׁבְתִּי אֲנִי וָאֶרְאֶה "and I returned, I, and saw" (= I saw again); so also וְרָאִיתִי אֲנִי "and I myself saw" (v4). **"all the oppressions … such as were oppressed … their oppressors" → "all the extortions … the extorted … their extortioners"** — Hebrew הָעֲשֻׁקִים (twice: first "the extortions", with נַעֲשִׂים "that are done"; then "the extorted"), עֹשְׁקֵיהֶם: one root, עָשַׁק "extort, wrong by force" (as Lev 6:2–4; Psalms; לָחַץ is "oppress"). **"and on the side of their oppressors there was power; but they had no comforter" → "and from the hand of their extortioners there is power, and they have no comforter"** — Hebrew וּמִיַּד "and from the hand"; nominal clauses (no verb); וְ "and" (not "but"); מְנַחֵם "comforter", the same words twice.
- **"Wherefore I praised the dead" → "And I myself praise the dead"** (v2) — Hebrew וְשַׁבֵּחַ אֲנִי, an infinitive absolute standing for the verb, "and praising, I" (שִׁבַּח "praise, laud"); no tense is marked. **"which are already dead … which are yet alive" → "who have already died … who are still alive"** — Hebrew שֶׁכְּבָר מֵתוּ · אֲשֶׁר הֵמָּה חַיִּים עֲדֶנָה.
- **"Yea, better is he than both they" → "And better than both of them is he"** (v3) — Hebrew וְטוֹב מִשְּׁנֵיהֶם אֵת אֲשֶׁר "and better than the two of them, him who". **"the evil work"** — Hebrew הַמַּעֲשֶׂה הָרָע.
- **"every right work, that for this a man is envied of his neighbour" → "all skill of work, that it is a man's jealousy of his neighbor"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּשְׁרוֹן הַמַּעֲשֶׂה "the skill of the work" (as 2:21); כִּי הִיא קִנְאַת־אִישׁ מֵרֵעֵהוּ "that it is the jealousy of a man from his neighbor": rivalry, either the envy a man feels toward his neighbor or the envy he draws from him (קִנְאָה "jealousy"; "envy" in many translations).
- **"foldeth his hands together, and eateth his own flesh" → "folds his hands and eats his flesh"** (v5) — Hebrew חֹבֵק אֶת־יָדָיו, the verb of "embrace" (3:5): he clasps his hands; "own" is not written.
- **"Better is an handful with quietness, than both the hands full with travail" → "Better is a palm full of quiet than two fists full of toil"** (v6) — Hebrew מְלֹא כַף "the fullness of a palm"; חָפְנַיִם, the dual of חֹפֶן "hollow of the hand, fist"; נַחַת "quiet, rest"; עָמָל "toil".
- **"There is one alone, and there is not a second; yea, he hath neither child nor brother" → "There is one, and there is no second; also son and brother he has none"** (v8) — Hebrew בֵּן "son". **"neither is his eye satisfied with riches"** — Hebrew עֵינוֹ "his eye" (qere); the written text (ketiv) has עיניו "his eyes"; the sense is the same. תִשְׂבַּע "is sated". **"neither saith he, For whom do I labour, and bereave my soul of good?" → "'And for whom am I toiling, and making my soul lack good?'"** — the Hebrew passes to the first person with no word of saying ("he says" is not written): וּלְמִי אֲנִי עָמֵל "and for whom am I toiling"; מְחַסֵּר "making … lack" (piel of חָסֵר "lack"); טוֹבָה "good". **"yea, it is a sore travail" → "and it is an evil occupation"** — Hebrew וְעִנְיַן רָע הוּא (as 1:13).
- **"Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour" → "The two are better than the one, because they have a good wage in their toil"** (v9) — Hebrew הַשְּׁנַיִם · הָאֶחָד with the article; שָׂכָר "wage"; בַּעֲמָלָם "in their toil".
- **"the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up" → "the one will raise up his companion; but woe to him, the one who falls, and there is no second to raise him up!"** (v10) — Hebrew יָקִים · לַהֲקִימוֹ "raise up" (one verb); חָבֵר "companion"; וְאִילוֹ = אִי לוֹ "woe to him"; הָאֶחָד שֶׁיִּפּוֹל "the one who falls"; שֵׁנִי "second" (as v8).
- **"then they have heat: but how can one be warm alone?" → "then they have warmth; but for one, how will he be warm?"** (v11) — Hebrew וְחַם לָהֶם "and there is warmth to them"; וּלְאֶחָד אֵיךְ יֵחָם "and for one, how will he be warm". **"lie together" → "lie down"** — Hebrew יִשְׁכְּבוּ שְׁנַיִם "two lie down" ("together" is not written).
- **"And if one prevail against him, two shall withstand him" → "And if one overpowers him, the two will stand against him"** (v12) — Hebrew וְאִם־יִתְקְפוֹ הָאֶחָד, literally "and if he overpowers him, the one": "the one" may be the attacker or the man attacked (open); הַשְּׁנַיִם "the two"; יַעַמְדוּ נֶגְדּוֹ "stand against him". **"a threefold cord" → "the threefold thread"** — Hebrew הַחוּט הַמְשֻׁלָּשׁ (חוּט "thread", with the article); יִנָּתֵק "is snapped" (נִתַּק, of a cord torn apart, Judg 16:9; "be drawn out" of men elsewhere).
- **"Better is a poor and a wise child than an old and foolish king, who will no more be admonished" → "Better is a child poor and wise than a king old and foolish, who no longer knows how to be warned"** (v13) — Hebrew יֶלֶד "child" (also of a young man); מִסְכֵּן "poor" (only Eccl 4:13; 9:15, 16); כְּסִיל "foolish" (the "fool" of v5); אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יָדַע לְהִזָּהֵר עוֹד "who does not know any more to be warned" (nifal of זָהַר "warn").
- **"For out of prison he cometh to reign; whereas also he that is born in his kingdom becometh poor" → "For from the house of the bound he came out to reign, for also in his kingdom he was born destitute"** (v14) — Hebrew מִבֵּית הָסוּרִים "from the house of the bound" (= הָאֲסוּרִים, written without א; as Judg 16:21); יָצָא perfect; כִּי גַם בְּמַלְכוּתוֹ נוֹלַד רָשׁ, word for word: "for also in his kingdom he was born destitute" (whose kingdom, and who is born, is debated: the youth born poor in the old king's realm, or the king grown poor; kept as written). רָשׁ "destitute" (as Proverbs).
- **"I considered all the living which walk under the sun, with the second child that shall stand up in his stead" → "I saw all the living who walk about under the sun, with the child, the second, who will stand in his place"** (v15) — Hebrew רָאִיתִי "I saw"; הַמְהַלְּכִים (piel) "who walk about"; עִם הַיֶּלֶד הַשֵּׁנִי "with the child, the second" (the youth of v13 as the second after the king, or another youth: open); תַּחְתָּיו "in his place".
- **"even of all that have been before them: they also that come after shall not rejoice in him" → "to all before whom he was; also the latter ones will not be glad in him"** (v16) — Hebrew לְכֹל אֲשֶׁר־הָיָה לִפְנֵיהֶם "to all whom he was before" (= at whose head he went; or "who were before them", in time: open); הָאַחֲרוֹנִים "the latter ones" (as 1:11); יִשְׂמְחוּ־בוֹ "be glad in him". **"Surely" → "For"** — Hebrew כִּי.

### Notes
- **"Under the sun"** (v1, 3, 7, 15) — *Qohelet*'s characteristic phrase.
- **v13–16** — the parable of the old king and the youth; who is meant at each step (v14–15) is not said in the Hebrew.
