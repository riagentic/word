# Ecclesiastes 12

[1] And remember your Creator in the days of your youth,
before the days of the evil come
and years arrive of which you will say,
"I have no delight in them";

[2] before the sun is darkened, and the light,
and the moon, and the stars,
and the clouds return after the rain;

[3] in the day when the keepers of the house tremble,
and the men of ability bend themselves,
and the grinders cease because they are few,
and those who look in the windows are darkened;

[4] and doors are shut in the street,
when the sound of the mill is low,
and he rises at the voice of the bird,
and all the daughters of the song are bowed down;

[5] also of what is high they are afraid,
and terrors are in the way;
and the almond tree blossoms,
and the grasshopper loads itself,
and the caperberry fails;
for the man is going to his everlasting house,
and those who lament go around in the street;

[6] before the cord of silver is bound,
and the bowl of gold is crushed,
and the jar is broken at the fountain,
and the wheel is crushed at the pit;

[7] and the dust returns upon the earth as it was,
and the spirit returns to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who gave it.

[8] Vapor of vapors, says ⟦qohelet|the Qohelet|the Preacher⟧; all is vapor.

[9] And besides that ⟦qohelet|Qohelet|the Preacher⟧ was wise, he still taught the people knowledge; and he weighed and searched out, he set straight many proverbs.

[10] ⟦qohelet|Qohelet|The Preacher⟧ sought to find words of delight, and written in uprightness, words of truth.

[11] The words of the wise are like goads, and like nails planted are the masters of collections; they are given from one shepherd.

[12] And beyond these, my son, be warned: of making many books there is no end, and much study is a weariness of the flesh.

[13] The end of the matter; all has been heard: Fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and keep his commandments, for this is the whole of man.

[14] For every work ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will bring into judgment, concerning every hidden thing, whether good or evil.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v7, 13, 14) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article each time (WORDS-HE: the plain token).
- **Layout** (v1–7) — one Hebrew colon per line; v2 is now divided where the accents divide it ("before the sun is darkened, and the light, / and the moon, and the stars"). The imagery of v1–7 is rendered word for word; what each picture stands for is not put into the verse.
- **"Remember now thy Creator" → "And remember your Creator"** (v1) — Hebrew וּזְכֹר "and remember". בּוֹרְאֶיךָ, the participle of בָּרָא "create" (WORDS-HE), has the plural form as printed ("your Creators", word for word): a plural of majesty, as אֱלֹהִים, or a spelling of the singular; the verse has the singular. Readings that change the word ("your well", "your pit") are conjectures (from memory) and are not followed.
- **"while the evil days come not, nor the years draw nigh" → "before the days of the evil come and years arrive"** (v1, 2, 6) — Hebrew עַד אֲשֶׁר לֹא, literally "until which not" (= before), three times; יְמֵי הָרָעָה "the days of the evil" (the article kept); שָׁנִים without the article; הִגִּיעוּ "arrive, reach". חֵפֶץ "delight"; בְּחוּרוֹת "youth" (the years of a young man, as 11:9).
- **"While the sun, or the light, or the moon, or the stars, be not darkened" → "before the sun is darkened, and the light, and the moon, and the stars"** (v2) — Hebrew וְ "and" throughout; תֶחְשַׁךְ "is darkened" (the verb of v3, חָשַׁךְ); עָבִים "clouds", גֶּשֶׁם "rain".
- **"the strong men shall bow themselves" → "the men of ability bend themselves"** (v3) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי הֶחָיִל (WORDS-HE חַיִל: "men of ability"; of strength or valor here); הִתְעַוְּתוּ "bend themselves" (hitpael of עָוַת "be bent"). שֹׁמְרֵי "keepers" (שָׁמַר "keep"); יָזֻעוּ "tremble". **"the grinders … those that look out of the windows be darkened" → "the grinders … those who look in the windows are darkened"** — Hebrew הַטֹּחֲנוֹת and הָרֹאוֹת are feminine plurals ("grinding women", "women who look"; English has no gender for them); בָּטְלוּ "cease" (as the Aramaic בְּטֵל); מִעֵטוּ "they have become few"; חָשְׁכוּ "are darkened", as v2; בָּאֲרֻבּוֹת "in the windows" (the lattices; as "the windows of the heavens", Gen 7:11).
- **"And the doors shall be shut in the streets, when the sound of the grinding is low, and he shall rise up at the voice of the bird, and all the daughters of musick shall be brought low" → "and doors are shut in the street, when the sound of the mill is low, and he rises at the voice of the bird, and all the daughters of the song are bowed down"** (v4) — Hebrew דְלָתַיִם, without the article (a dual: double doors); בַּשּׁוּק "in the street" (also v5); טַחֲנָה "mill" (only here); קוֹל "sound" of the mill, "voice" of the bird (one word: WORDS-HE). וְיָקוּם has no written subject ("he rises", or "it rises to the voice of the bird"). יִשַּׁחוּ "are bowed down" (שָׁחַח, WORDS-HE); בְּנוֹת הַשִּׁיר "the daughters of the song".
- **"Also when they shall be afraid of that which is high, and fears shall be in the way" → "also of what is high they are afraid, and terrors are in the way"** (v5) — Hebrew גַּם מִגָּבֹהַּ יִרָאוּ (no "when"); חַתְחַתִּים "terrors" (only here; a doubled form of חַת "dread").
- **"and the almond tree shall flourish" → "and the almond tree blossoms"** (v5) — Hebrew וְיָנֵאץ: as pointed, the form looks like נָאַץ "spurn"; it is taken as "blossom" (נֵץ, נִצָּה) written with an א. Uncertain.
- **"and the grasshopper shall be a burden" → "and the grasshopper loads itself"** (v5) — Hebrew וְיִסְתַּבֵּל, hitpael of סָבַל "bear a load" ("drags itself along" is a guess at the sense); חָגָב "grasshopper" (WORDS-HE).
- **"and desire shall fail" → "and the caperberry fails"** (v5) — Hebrew הָאֲבִיּוֹנָה, only here: the caper (its berry was eaten to rouse appetite); "desire" (from אָבָה "be willing") is the old interpretation. וְתָפֵר, from פָּרַר "break, make void" (as הֵפֵר, WORDS-HE): "fails", or "bursts" (of the berry).
- **"because man goeth to his long home, and the mourners go about the streets" → "for the man is going to his everlasting house, and those who lament go around in the street"** (v5) — Hebrew הֹלֵךְ, a participle, "is going"; הָאָדָם "the man"; בֵּית עוֹלָמוֹ "his everlasting house" (lit. "the house of his age": WORDS-HE עוֹלָם as a genitive); סָבְבוּ "go around"; הַסֹּפְדִים "those who lament" (סָפַד, WORDS-HE).
- **"Or ever the silver cord be loosed" → "before the cord of silver is bound"** (v6) — qere יֵרָתֵק, nifal of רָתַק "bind, chain" (Nah 3:10); the written form (ketiv) ירחק "is removed, is far". The sense of the qere here is uncertain; "is snapped" (יִנָּתֵק) is a common conjecture (from memory), not followed.
- **"or the golden bowl be broken … or the wheel broken at the cistern" → "and the bowl of gold is crushed … and the wheel is crushed at the pit"** (v6) — Hebrew וְ "and" throughout (not "or"); תָרֻץ and וְנָרֹץ are one verb (רָצַץ "crush"), "crushed" both times; גֻּלָּה "bowl" (WORDS-HE); כַּד "jar"; מַבּוּעַ "fountain" (from נָבַע "gush"; עַיִן is "spring"); גַּלְגַּל "wheel"; אֶל־הַבּוֹר "at the pit" (lit. "to the pit"; בּוֹר "pit", a cistern: WORDS-HE).
- **"Then shall the dust return to the earth as it was" → "and the dust returns upon the earth as it was"** (v7) — Hebrew וְיָשֹׁב "and returns" (no "then"; still under the "before" of v6); עַל־הָאָרֶץ "upon the earth"; עָפָר "dust"; רוּחַ "spirit" (also "breath"; WORDS-HE).
- **"Vanity of vanities, saith the preacher; all is vanity" → "Vapor of vapors, says the Qohelet; all is vapor"** (v8) — Hebrew הֲבֵל הֲבָלִים "vapor of vapors" (as 1:2; WISDOM.md; "vanity of vanities" is the traditional rendering); הַקּוֹהֶלֶת has the article here (1:2 has none): a title, "the gatherer, the one who assembles"; token `qohelet`. אָמַר is a perfect ("said"), rendered "says" as 1:2.
- **"And moreover, because the preacher was wise, he still taught the people knowledge; yea, he gave good heed, and sought out, and set in order many proverbs" → "And besides that Qohelet was wise, he still taught the people knowledge; and he weighed and searched out, he set straight many proverbs"** (v9) — Hebrew וְיֹתֵר שֶׁ "and besides that" (or "and further: because"); עוֹד "still"; לִמֵּד "teach"; אִזֵּן, only here: "weigh" (as מֹאזְנַיִם "scales") or "give ear" (as אֹזֶן "ear"), uncertain; חִקֵּר "search out" (WORDS-HE חָקַר); תִּקֵּן "set straight" (as 1:15; 7:13); מְשָׁלִים "proverbs" (WORDS-HE מָשָׁל). "with great care" was not written.
- **"acceptable words: and that which was written was upright, even words of truth" → "words of delight, and written in uprightness, words of truth"** (v10) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי־חֵפֶץ "words of delight"; וְכָתוּב, a passive participle, "and written" (some versions read וְכָתַב "and he wrote", from memory; the MT is kept); יֹשֶׁר "uprightness"; אֱמֶת "truth".
- **"and as nails fastened by the masters of assemblies, which are given from one shepherd" → "and like nails planted are the masters of collections; they are given from one shepherd"** (v11) — Hebrew דָּרְבֹנוֹת "goads" (WORDS-HE דָּרְבָן); מַשְׂמְרוֹת "nails" (written with שׂ); נְטוּעִים "planted" (נָטַע; = driven in); בַּעֲלֵי אֲסֻפּוֹת "masters of collections" (בַּעַל + noun, WORDS-HE: the collected sayings, or the men of the gatherings; uncertain); נִתְּנוּ "they are given" (perfect); רֹעֶה אֶחָד "one shepherd", without a capital: the Hebrew does not say who is meant.
- **"And further, by these, my son, be admonished" → "And beyond these, my son, be warned"** (v12) — Hebrew וְיֹתֵר מֵהֵמָּה "and beyond these" (= of anything beyond these, or: further, by these); הִזָּהֵר, nifal of the root of הִזְהִיר "warn" (WORDS-HE); לַהַג "study" (only here; uncertain); יְגִעַת בָּשָׂר "a weariness of the flesh" (יָגַע "labor").
- **"Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter" → "The end of the matter; all has been heard"** (v13) — Hebrew סוֹף דָּבָר הַכֹּל נִשְׁמָע, no "let us hear". The commands put the objects first ("Elohim fear, and his commandments keep"); English has the plain order. **"for this is the whole duty of man" → "for this is the whole of man"** — Hebrew כִּי־זֶה כָּל־הָאָדָם, as printed: "duty" is not written (the sense commonly given: what man is for, or what is due from every man).
- **"For God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether it be good, or whether it be evil" → "For every work Elohim will bring into judgment, concerning every hidden thing, whether good or evil"** (v14) — Hebrew אֶת־כָּל־מַעֲשֶׂה first (the object leads, kept); מַעֲשֶׂה "work" (as 11:5); עַל כָּל־נֶעְלָם "concerning every hidden thing" (lit. "upon"; נֶעְלָם, nifal of עָלַם "be hidden"); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; טוֹב · רַע "good · evil".

### Notes

- **v1–7** — commonly read as pictures of old age and death (the body as a house, the failing senses); the text itself does not name what each picture means, and the verse keeps only the pictures.
- **v9–14** — the epilogue speaks of Qohelet in the third person.
