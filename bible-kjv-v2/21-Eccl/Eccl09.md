# Ecclesiastes 9

[1] For all this I gave to my heart, and to examine all this: that the righteous and the wise and their works are in the hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Both love and hate, man does not know; everything is before them.

[2] All is as for all: one event to the righteous and to the wicked, to the good and to the clean and to the unclean, and to him who sacrifices and to him who does not sacrifice. As the good, so the sinner; he who swears, as he who fears an oath.

[3] This is an evil in all that is done under the sun, that one event is for all; and also the heart of the sons of man is full of evil, and madness is in their heart in their life, and after it — to the dead.

[4] For whoever is joined to all the living, there is trust; for a living dog is better than the dead lion.

[5] For the living know that they will die, and the dead do not know anything, and there is no more wage for them, for their remembrance is forgotten.

[6] Also their love, also their hate, also their jealousy has already perished, and they have no more a portion forever in all that is done under the sun.

[7] Go, eat your bread with gladness, and drink your wine with a good heart, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has already been pleased with your works.

[8] At every time let your garments be white, and let oil on your head not be lacking.

[9] See life with a woman whom you love, all the days of the life of your vapor that he has given to you under the sun, all the days of your vapor; for that is your portion in life and in your toil at which you toil under the sun.

[10] All that your hand finds to do, with your power do it; for there is no work or reckoning or knowledge or wisdom in Sheol, to which you are going.

[11] I returned and saw under the sun that the race is not to the swift,
and not the battle to the mighty ones,
and also not bread to the wise,
and also not riches to the understanding,
and also not favor to those who know;
for time and occurrence befalls them all.

[12] For also man does not know his time: like the fish that are caught in an evil net, and like the birds that are caught in the trap, like them the sons of man are snared at an evil time, when it falls upon them suddenly.

[13] Also this I have seen, wisdom under the sun, and it is great to me:

[14] a little city, and few men in it; and a great king came to it and surrounded it and built against it great siegeworks.

[15] And he found in it a poor wise man, and he rescued the city by his wisdom; and no man remembered that poor man.

[16] And I said, "Wisdom is better than might, and the wisdom of the poor man is despised, and his words are not heard."

[17] The words of the wise in quiet are heard
more than the outcry of a ruler among fools.

[18] Wisdom is better than weapons of battle,
and one sinner does away with much good.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 7) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article (WORDS-HE: the plain token, no "the").
- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v9) — Hebrew הֶבֶל "vapor, breath" (WISDOM.md); "vanity" is the traditional rendering. הַבְלֶךָ "your vapor": the days of your fleeting life.
- **"I considered in my heart even to declare all this" → "I gave to my heart, and to examine all this"** (v1) — Hebrew נָתַתִּי אֶל־לִבִּי "I gave to my heart" (= I took it to heart; as 1:13, 17; 8:9, 16). וְלָבוּר, an infinitive of בּוּר, found only here: "to examine, to make clear" (uncertain).
- **"their works"** (v1) — Hebrew עֲבָדֵיהֶם, an Aramaic word for deeds (only here); not עֲבָדִים "servants".
- **"no man knoweth either love or hatred by all that is before them" → "Both love and hate, man does not know; everything is before them"** (v1) — Hebrew גַּם־אַהֲבָה גַם־שִׂנְאָה אֵין יוֹדֵעַ הָאָדָם הַכֹּל לִפְנֵיהֶם: "also love, also hate", whether Elohim's or man's is not said; לִפְנֵיהֶם "before them" (plural; the earlier "before him" was wrong). Some versions join הַכֹּל to v2 and read הֶבֶל "all before them is vapor" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"All things come alike to all" → "All is as for all"** (v2) — Hebrew הַכֹּל כַּאֲשֶׁר לַכֹּל, literally "the all, as which to all". מִקְרֶה "event", what befalls (from קָרָה "happen"; WISDOM.md); אֶחָד "one".
- **"to the good and the evil" → "to the good"** (v2) — the MT has לַטּוֹב alone; "and to the evil" (וְלָרָע) is read by the versions (from memory) and was added in the earlier text. Removed.
- **"he that feareth an oath"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׁבוּעָה יָרֵא, יָרֵא "fear" (WORDS-HE), not "shun".
- **"the sons of men" → "the sons of man"** (v3, 12) — Hebrew בְּנֵי־הָאָדָם (WORDS-HE). לֵב, בִּלְבָבָם singular: "the heart", "their heart"; הוֹלֵלוֹת "madness".
- **"while they live, and after that they go to the dead" → "in their life, and after it — to the dead"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּחַיֵּיהֶם "in their life"; וְאַחֲרָיו אֶל־הַמֵּתִים has no verb ("go" is not written); אַחֲרָיו "after it" (or "after him").
- **"For to him that is joined to all the living there is hope" → "For whoever is joined to all the living, there is trust"** (v4) — qere יְחֻבַּר "is joined"; the written form (ketiv) יבחר "chooses" ("who is there who chooses?"). בִּטָּחוֹן "trust" (as 2 Kgs 18:19), not תִּקְוָה "hope". כִּי "for", not "but". לְכֶלֶב חַי הוּא טוֹב, literally "as for a living dog, it is better"; הָאַרְיֵה הַמֵּת "the dead lion" with the article.
- **"but the dead know not any thing, neither have they any more a reward" → "and the dead do not know anything, and there is no more wage for them"** (v5) — each clause opens with "and"; שָׂכָר "wage" (WORDS-HE), playing on זִכְרָם "their remembrance" (זֵכֶר).
- **"their envy" → "their jealousy"** (v6) — Hebrew קִנְאָה (WORDS-HE "jealousy"; "envy" of a rival, 4:4). אָבָדָה singular, "has perished", of all three. חֵלֶק "portion" (WISDOM.md); לְעוֹלָם "forever".
- **"with joy … with a merry heart; for God now accepteth thy works" → "with gladness … with a good heart, for Elohim has already been pleased with your works"** (v7) — Hebrew שִׂמְחָה "gladness"; לֶב־טוֹב "a good heart"; כְבָר "already" (v6); רָצָה "be pleased with" (WORDS-HE; not "accept", "approve"); מַעֲשֶׂיךָ plural.
- **"Let thy garments be always white; and let thy head lack no ointment" → "At every time let your garments be white, and let oil on your head not be lacking"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּכָל־עֵת "at every time"; שֶׁמֶן "oil" is the subject of אַל־יֶחְסָר.
- **"Live joyfully with the wife" → "See life with a woman"** (v9) — Hebrew רְאֵה חַיִּים "see life" (= enjoy life; kept); אִשָּׁה without the article, "a woman" (the word is also "wife": WORDS-HE). The second "all the days of your vapor" stands in the MT and is kept. נָתַן has no written subject: "he has given". בַּעֲמָלְךָ אֲשֶׁר־אַתָּה עָמֵל "your toil at which you toil" (עָמָל "toil", WORDS-HE).
- **"do it with thy might … nor device … in the grave" → "with your power do it … reckoning … in Sheol"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּכֹחֲךָ "with your power" (כֹּחַ); עֲשֵׂה has no object ("it" supplied). חֶשְׁבּוֹן "reckoning" (from חָשַׁב "reckon"; as 7:25, 27). The four nouns are joined by "and"; under "there is no" English says "or". שְׁאוֹל "Sheol", not "the grave".
- **"I returned, and saw"** (v11) — Hebrew שַׁבְתִּי וְרָאֹה: שׁוּב with a second verb (= I saw again; WORDS-HE), kept. וְגַם "and also" three times, as written; גִּבּוֹרִים "mighty ones"; נְבֹנִים "the understanding" (נָבוֹן); יֹדְעִים "those who know"; חֵן "favor". **"time and chance happeneth" → "time and occurrence befalls"** — Hebrew עֵת וָפֶגַע "time and occurrence" (WORDS-HE פֶּגַע); יִקְרֶה singular, "befalls" (קָרָה).
- **"For man also knoweth not his time" → "For also man does not know his time"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי גַּם. נֶאֱחָזִים and הָאֲחֻזוֹת are one verb (אָחַז), "caught" both times; מְצוֹדָה "net"; פַּח "trap" (WORDS-HE); יוּקָשִׁים "are snared" (יָקֹשׁ); תִּפּוֹל, feminine, has "an evil time" as its subject; פִּתְאֹם "suddenly".
- **"This wisdom have I seen also under the sun, and it seemed great unto me" → "Also this I have seen, wisdom under the sun, and it is great to me"** (v13) — Hebrew גַּם־זֹה רָאִיתִי חָכְמָה … וּגְדוֹלָה הִיא אֵלָי: no verb "seem", no "example".
- **"There was a little city … and there came a great king against it, and besieged it, and built great bulwarks against it" → "a little city, and few men in it; and a great king came to it and surrounded it and built against it great siegeworks"** (v14) — the Hebrew has no "there was": the verse sets out what v13 saw. אֵלֶיהָ "to it"; סָבַב "surround" (not "besiege"). מְצוֹדִים is the word of v12 "net" (מָצוֹד, "nets" or "strongholds"); of a thing built against a city "siegeworks"; some Hebrew manuscripts read מְצוֹרִים "siege works" (from memory). The MT is kept.
- **"Now there was found in it" → "And he found in it"** (v15) — Hebrew וּמָצָא, active, no subject written ("one found", or the king). מִסְכֵּן "poor" (only Eccl 4:13; 9:15, 16); מִלֵּט "rescue"; וְאָדָם לֹא זָכַר "and no man remembered".
- **"Then said I … nevertheless the poor man's wisdom is despised" → "And I said … and the wisdom of the poor man is despised"** (v16) — Hebrew וְאָמַרְתִּי אָנִי, the pronoun written for emphasis ("I for my part"); the clauses are joined by "and" ("nevertheless", "though" are not written). גְּבוּרָה "might".
- **"The words of wise men are heard in quiet more than the cry of him that ruleth among fools"** (v17) — KJV kept in substance: Hebrew נִשְׁמָעִים … מִזַּעֲקַת, "are heard … more than" (no "better" is written; "heard in quiet are better than" is the other reading). זְעָקָה "outcry" (WORDS-HE); מוֹשֵׁל "ruler"; כְּסִילִים "fools" (WISDOM.md).
- **"weapons of war: but one sinner destroyeth much good" → "weapons of battle, and one sinner does away with much good"** (v18) — Hebrew כְּלֵי קְרָב, "articles of battle" (כְּלִי; קְרָב "battle"); "and", not "but"; יְאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד, "do away with" (WORDS-HE).
- **"under the sun"** (v3, 6, 9, 11, 13) — תַּחַת הַשֶּׁמֶשׁ, kept every time.
