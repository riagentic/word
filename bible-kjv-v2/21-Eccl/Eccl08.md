# Ecclesiastes 8

[1] Who is like the wise?
And who knows the interpretation of a thing?
The wisdom of a man makes his face shine,
and the strength of his face is changed.

[2] I — keep the mouth of a king, and that on account of the oath of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] Do not be hasty to go from his face; do not stand in an evil thing, for all that he delights in he does.

[4] Inasmuch as the word of a king is power, and who will say to him, "What are you doing?"

[5] One who keeps a commandment will not know an evil thing, and time and judgment the heart of a wise man will know.

[6] For to every matter there is a time and judgment, for the evil of man is great upon him.

[7] For he does not know what will be; for as it will be, who will tell him?

[8] There is no man who is ruler over the spirit, to keep back the spirit, and there is no power in the day of death, and there is no discharge in the war, and wickedness will not rescue its owners.

[9] All this I have seen, and I gave my heart to every work that is done under the sun: a time when man has had power over man, for evil to him.

[10] And so I saw wicked men buried, and they came; and from a holy place they would go, and they were forgotten in the city where they had done so. This also is vapor.

[11] Because the decree against the work of evil is not done quickly, therefore the heart of the sons of man is full in them to do evil.

[12] Though a sinner does evil a hundred times and lasts long for himself, yet I also know that it will be good for those who fear ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who fear before him.

[13] And it will not be good for the wicked, and he will not prolong days, like the shadow, because he does not fear before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[14] There is a vapor that is done on the earth: that there are righteous men to whom it comes according to the work of the wicked, and there are wicked men to whom it comes according to the work of the righteous. I said that this also is vapor.

[15] And I praised gladness, because there is no good for man under the sun except to eat and to drink and to be glad; and it will accompany him in his toil the days of his life which ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given him under the sun.

[16] When I gave my heart to know wisdom and to see the occupation that is done on the earth — for also by day and by night sleep with his eyes he does not see —

[17] then I saw all the work of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that man is not able to find the work that is done under the sun; for all that man toils to seek, he will not find; and even if the wise man means to know, he is not able to find.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 12, 13, 15, 17) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim* (v2, 13 without the article, v12, 15, 17 with it).
- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v10, 14) — Hebrew הֶבֶל, literally "vapor, breath"; traditional "vanity".
- **"Who is as the wise man? and who knoweth the interpretation of a thing?" → "Who is like the wise? And who knows the interpretation of a thing?"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּהֶחָכָם "like the wise" (the article kept in writing); פֵּשֶׁר "interpretation" (only here in Hebrew; the Aramaic word of Daniel, beside פִּתְרוֹן); דָּבָר "thing" (also "word").
- **"and the boldness of his face shall be changed" → "and the strength of his face is changed"** (v1) — Hebrew עֹז פָּנָיו "the strength of his face" (= hardness of look); יְשֻׁנֶּא "is changed" (pual of שָׁנָה).
- **"I counsel thee to keep the king's commandment, and that in regard of the oath of God" → "I — keep the mouth of a king, and that on account of the oath of *Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew אֲנִי פִּי־מֶלֶךְ שְׁמוֹר: "I" stands alone (a verb such as "say" is not written; KJV "I counsel thee" supplies one); פִּי "mouth" (= command); שְׁמוֹר infinitive used as a command, "keep"; מֶלֶךְ without the article, "a king"; וְעַל דִּבְרַת "and on account of"; שְׁבוּעַת אֱלֹהִים "the oath of *Elohim*" (an oath sworn by Elohim, or to him: not said).
- **"Be not hasty to go out of his sight: stand not in an evil thing" → "Do not be hasty to go from his face; do not stand in an evil thing"** (v3) — Hebrew אַל־תִּבָּהֵל מִפָּנָיו תֵּלֵךְ; the accents can also divide "Do not be dismayed; from his face you shall go"; מִפָּנָיו "from his face"; עָמַד בְּדָבָר רָע "stand in an evil thing (word, matter)"; יַחְפֹּץ "delights in" (חָפֵץ).
- **"Where the word of a king is, there is power: and who may say unto him" → "Inasmuch as the word of a king is power, and who will say to him"** (v4) — Hebrew בַּאֲשֶׁר "inasmuch as" (as 7:2); שִׁלְטוֹן "power" (also v8; root of שָׁלַט "have power", v9); וּמִי "and who".
- **"Whoso keepeth the commandment shall feel no evil thing: and a wise man's heart discerneth both time and judgment" → "One who keeps a commandment will not know an evil thing, and time and judgment the heart of a wise man will know"** (v5) — Hebrew מִצְוָה "a commandment" (no article); יֵדַע "will know" both times (KJV "feel", "discerneth"); עֵת וּמִשְׁפָּט "time and judgment" stand first.
- **"Because to every purpose there is time and judgment, therefore the misery of man is great upon him" → "For to every matter there is a time and judgment, for the evil of man is great upon him"** (v6) — Hebrew חֵפֶץ "matter" (as 5:8; "delight" in v3); כִּי … כִּי "for … for"; רָעַת הָאָדָם "the evil of man"; רַבָּה עָלָיו "great upon him" (as 6:1).
- **"for who can tell him when it shall be?" → "for as it will be, who will tell him?"** (v7) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר יִהְיֶה "as it will be" (how, or when); יַגִּיד imperfect.
- **"There is no man that hath power over the spirit to retain the spirit; neither hath he power in the day of death: and there is no discharge in that war; neither shall wickedness deliver those that are given to it" → "There is no man who is ruler over the spirit, to keep back the spirit, and there is no power in the day of death, and there is no discharge in the war, and wickedness will not rescue its owners"** (v8) — Hebrew שַׁלִּיט "ruler" (as 7:19; root of שִׁלְטוֹן "power" in the same verse); רוּחַ "spirit" (also "wind": one word; the day of death points to the breath of life); כָּלָא "keep back"; וְאֵין שִׁלְטוֹן "and there is no power" (no "he"); מִשְׁלַחַת "discharge" (a sending away); בַּמִּלְחָמָה "in the war" (not "that"); מִלֵּט "rescue"; בְּעָלָיו "its owners" (the בַּעַל row: those who have it).
- **"All this have I seen, and applied my heart unto every work … there is a time wherein one man ruleth over another to his own hurt" → "All this I have seen, and I gave my heart to every work … a time when man has had power over man, for evil to him"** (v9) — Hebrew וְנָתוֹן אֶת־לִבִּי, infinitive "and giving my heart"; שָׁלַט הָאָדָם בְּאָדָם, perfect; לְרַע לוֹ "for evil to him" (to the ruler or to the ruled: not said).
- **"And so I saw the wicked buried, who had come and gone from the place of the holy, and they were forgotten in the city" → "And so I saw wicked men buried, and they came; and from a holy place they would go, and they were forgotten in the city"** (v10) — Hebrew רְשָׁעִים "wicked men" (no article); וָבָאוּ "and they came" (to the grave, or to the holy place: not said); מִמְּקוֹם קָדוֹשׁ "from a holy place" (or "from the place of the Holy One"); יְהַלֵּכוּ imperfect "they would go". MT וְיִשְׁתַּכְּחוּ "they were forgotten" (kept); "they were praised" reads יִשְׁתַּבְּחוּ, the Greek and some Hebrew manuscripts (from memory): not taken. אֲשֶׁר כֵּן־עָשׂוּ "where (or: who) had done so".
- **"Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil" → "Because the decree against the work of evil is not done quickly, therefore the heart of the sons of man is full in them to do evil"** (v11) — Hebrew פִתְגָם "decree" (Persian loan, as Esth 1:20); נַעֲשָׂה "is done"; מַעֲשֵׂה הָרָעָה "the work of evil"; מָלֵא לֵב "the heart is full"; בְּנֵי־הָאָדָם "the sons of man".
- **"Though a sinner do evil an hundred times, and his days be prolonged" → "Though a sinner does evil a hundred times and lasts long for himself"** (v12) — Hebrew מְאַת "a hundred" ("times" supplied); וּמַאֲרִיךְ לוֹ "and lasts long for himself" (as 7:15; "his days" is not written); כִּי גַּם־יוֹדֵעַ אָנִי "yet I also know"; יִירְאוּ מִלְּפָנָיו "fear before him" (literally "from before him"; also v13).
- **"But it shall not be well with the wicked, neither shall he prolong his days, which are as a shadow" → "And it will not be good for the wicked, and he will not prolong days, like the shadow"** (v13) — Hebrew וְטוֹב לֹא־יִהְיֶה "and good will not be"; יַאֲרִיךְ יָמִים "prolong days" (no "his"); כַּצֵּל "like the shadow" (his days are like a shadow, or he does not lengthen like an evening shadow: not said; "which are" is not written).
- **"There is a vanity which is done upon the earth; that there be just men, unto whom it happeneth according to the work of the wicked" → "There is a vapor that is done on the earth: that there are righteous men to whom it comes according to the work of the wicked"** (v14) — Hebrew מַגִּיעַ אֲלֵהֶם "it reaches to them, comes to them"; צַדִּיקִים "righteous men"; מַעֲשֶׂה "work".
- **"Then I commended mirth … to eat, and to drink, and to be merry: for that shall abide with him of his labour" → "And I praised gladness … to eat and to drink and to be glad; and it will accompany him in his toil"** (v15) — Hebrew וְשִׁבַּחְתִּי "and I praised" (שִׁבַּח); שִׂמְחָה "gladness", שָׂמַח "be glad"; כִּי אִם "except"; וְהוּא יִלְוֶנּוּ "and it will accompany him" (לָוָה "join"); עָמָל "toil".
- **"When I applied mine heart … and to see the business that is done upon the earth: (for also there is that neither day nor night seeth sleep with his eyes:)" → "When I gave my heart … and to see the occupation that is done on the earth — for also by day and by night sleep with his eyes he does not see —"** (v16) — Hebrew נָתַתִּי אֶת־לִבִּי "I gave my heart"; הָעִנְיָן "the occupation"; גַם בַּיּוֹם וּבַלַּיְלָה "also by day and by night"; who "he" is, the Hebrew does not say.
- **"because though a man labour to seek it out, yet he shall not find it; yea further; though a wise man think to know it, yet shall he not be able to find it" → "for all that man toils to seek, he will not find; and even if the wise man means to know, he is not able to find"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּשֶׁל אֲשֶׁר "for all that" (uncertain; also "because"); עָמַל "toil"; "it" is not written after the verbs; אִם־יֹאמַר … לָדַעַת "if he says to know" (= means to, claims to; the אָמַר + infinitive row); וְרָאִיתִי "then I saw" (the main clause after v16).
