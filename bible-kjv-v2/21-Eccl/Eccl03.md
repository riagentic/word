# Ecclesiastes 3

[1] For everything there is a season,
and a time for every matter under the heavens:

[2] a time to give birth, and a time to die;
a time to plant, and a time to pluck up what is planted;

[3] a time to kill, and a time to heal;
a time to breach, and a time to build;

[4] a time to weep, and a time to laugh;
a time of mourning, and a time of dancing;

[5] a time to throw stones, and a time of collecting stones;
a time to embrace, and a time to be far from embracing;

[6] a time to seek, and a time to lose;
a time to keep, and a time to throw away;

[7] a time to tear, and a time to sew;
a time to be silent, and a time to speak;

[8] a time to love, and a time to hate;
a time of war, and a time of ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧.

[9] What advantage has the doer in that at which he toils?

[10] I have seen the occupation that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given to the sons of man, to be occupied with it.

[11] Everything he has made beautiful in its time; also the age he has set in their heart, yet so that man cannot find out the work that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has done from the beginning and to the end.

[12] I knew that there is no good in them except to be glad and to do good in his life.

[13] And also every man that eats and drinks and sees good in all his toil — it is a gift of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[14] I knew that all that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does, it shall be forever: to it there is nothing to add, and from it there is nothing to take away; and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has done it, that they should fear before him.

[15] What has been, already it is; and what is to be, already has been; and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ seeks what is pursued.

[16] And further I saw under the sun: the place of judgment, there was wickedness; and the place of righteousness, there was wickedness.

[17] I myself said in my heart, "The righteous and the wicked ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ will judge; for there is a time for every matter, and for every work, there."

[18] I myself said in my heart, concerning the sons of man: ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is to sift them, and to see that they are beasts, they, to themselves.

[19] For the sons of man are an event, and the beast is an event, and one event is theirs: as this one dies, so that one dies, and one spirit is to all; and the profit of man over the beast is nothing, for all is vapor.

[20] All go to one place; all came from the dust, and all return to the dust.

[21] Who knows the spirit of the sons of man, which goes up upward, and the spirit of the beast, which goes down downward to the earth?

[22] And I saw that there is nothing better than that man should be glad in his works, for that is his portion; for who will bring him to see what will be after him?

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim* (v10, 13 without the article; v11, 14, 15, 17, 18 הָאֱלֹהִים, plain `elohim` token).
- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v19) — Hebrew הֶבֶל "vapor, breath"; traditional "vanity".
- **Layout** (v1–8) — one line per pair of times, as the accents divide them (the main pause of each verse falls between its two pairs).
- **"every purpose under the heaven" → "every matter under the heavens"** (v1, 17) — Hebrew חֵפֶץ, elsewhere "delight, desire"; in Ecclesiastes (3:1, 17; 5:8; 8:6) it has the later sense "matter, affair". זְמָן "season" (a set time), עֵת "time". הַשָּׁמָיִם "the heavens".
- **"A time to be born" → "a time to give birth"** (v2) — Hebrew לָלֶדֶת, the qal infinitive of יָלַד "bear, give birth", active (not "be born").
- **"a time to break down" → "a time to breach"** (v3) — Hebrew לִפְרוֹץ (פָּרַץ "break out, breach": of a wall). **"build up" → "build"** — Hebrew לִבְנוֹת.
- **"a time to mourn, and a time to dance" → "a time of mourning, and a time of dancing"** (v4) — Hebrew סְפוֹד · רְקוֹד, infinitives without לְ (beside לִבְכּוֹת, לִשְׂחוֹק "to weep, to laugh"); so also כְּנוֹס "collecting" (v5) and מִלְחָמָה · שָׁלוֹם "of war, of shalom" (v8, nouns).
- **"cast away stones … gather stones together" → "throw stones … collecting stones"** (v5) — Hebrew לְהַשְׁלִיךְ "to throw" (as v6); כְּנוֹס (כָּנַס "collect", 2:8, 26). **"to refrain from embracing" → "to be far from embracing"** — Hebrew לִרְחֹק מֵחַבֵּק.
- **"a time to get, and a time to lose" → "a time to seek, and a time to lose"** (v6) — Hebrew לְבַקֵּשׁ "to seek"; לְאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד "perish": "to give up as lost". **"cast away" → "throw away"** — Hebrew לְהַשְׁלִיךְ.
- **"a time to rend" → "a time to tear"; "a time to keep silence" → "a time to be silent"** (v7) — Hebrew לִקְרוֹעַ · לַחֲשׁוֹת.
- **"a time of peace" → "a time of shalom"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁלוֹם (token `shalom`).
- **"What profit hath he that worketh in that wherein he laboureth?" → "What advantage has the doer in that at which he toils?"** (v9) — Hebrew יִתְרוֹן "advantage"; הָעוֹשֶׂה "the one who does / makes"; בַּאֲשֶׁר הוּא עָמֵל "in that which he toils".
- **"the travail" → "the occupation"** (v10) — Hebrew הָעִנְיָן; לַעֲנוֹת בּוֹ "to be occupied with it" (as 1:13). **"the sons of men" → "the sons of man"** (v10, 18, 19, 21) — Hebrew בְּנֵי הָאָדָם.
- **"He hath made every thing beautiful in his time: also he hath set the world in their heart, so that no man can find out the work" → "Everything he has made beautiful in its time; also the age he has set in their heart, yet so that man cannot find out the work"** (v11) — Hebrew אֶת־הַכֹּל עָשָׂה יָפֶה בְעִתּוֹ, the object first; no subject is written for עָשָׂה ("he"). אֶת־הָעֹלָם "the age" (עוֹלָם, time out of sight, past or to come: "eternity" in many translations; "the world" in later Hebrew; "darkness / ignorance" by a change of pointing, עֶלֶם: not followed). נָתַן "set", literally "gave". מִבְּלִי אֲשֶׁר לֹא־יִמְצָא, literally "without that man does not find": a double negative that says "yet so that he cannot find". **"from the beginning to the end" → "from the beginning and to the end"** — Hebrew מֵרֹאשׁ וְעַד־סוֹף.
- **"I know that there is no good in them, but for a man to rejoice" → "I knew that there is no good in them except to be glad"** (v12, 14) — Hebrew יָדַעְתִּי "I knew"; אֵין טוֹב בָּם כִּי אִם "there is no good in them except" (as 2:24; "nothing better for them" in many translations); לִשְׂמוֹחַ "to be glad"; לַעֲשׂוֹת טוֹב "to do good" (also understood "to fare well"). בְּחַיָּיו "in his life" (singular, after "them").
- **"And also that every man should eat and drink, and enjoy the good of all his labour" → "And also every man that eats and drinks and sees good in all his toil"** (v13) — Hebrew כָּל־הָאָדָם שֶׁיֹּאכַל … וְרָאָה טוֹב "every man that eats … and sees good" (as 2:1, 24); מַתַּת אֱלֹהִים הִיא "it is a gift of Elohim".
- **"whatsoever God doeth, it shall be for ever: nothing can be put to it, nor any thing taken from it: and God doeth it, that men should fear before him" → "all that Elohim does, it shall be forever: to it there is nothing to add, and from it there is nothing to take away; and Elohim has done it, that they should fear before him"** (v14) — Hebrew עָלָיו אֵין לְהוֹסִיף וּמִמֶּנּוּ אֵין לִגְרֹעַ "upon it there is no adding and from it there is no taking away" (גָּרַע "diminish"); עָשָׂה perfect; "it" is supplied for English; "men" is not written ("that they fear").
- **"That which hath been is now; and that which is to be hath already been" → "What has been, already it is; and what is to be, already has been"** (v15) — Hebrew מַה־שֶּׁהָיָה כְּבָר הוּא "what has been, already it (is)". **"God requireth that which is past" → "Elohim seeks what is pursued"** — Hebrew יְבַקֵּשׁ "seeks" (also "requires", the בִּקֵּשׁ row); נִרְדָּף "what is pursued" (nifal of רָדַף; "the persecuted" or "what has been driven away", the past: open).
- **"the place of judgment, that wickedness was there; and the place of righteousness, that iniquity was there" → "the place of judgment, there was wickedness; and the place of righteousness, there was wickedness"** (v16) — Hebrew הָרֶשַׁע both times (the second in pause); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; צֶדֶק "righteousness". **"And moreover" → "And further"** — Hebrew וְעוֹד.
- **"God shall judge the righteous and the wicked: for there is a time there for every purpose and for every work" → "The righteous and the wicked Elohim will judge; for there is a time for every matter, and for every work, there"** (v17) — the objects come first in the Hebrew; שָׁם "there" stands at the end (= with Elohim, or in the judgment; some read שָׂם "he has set": not followed). The pronoun אֲנִי "I myself" (v17, 18) as 2:1.
- **"concerning the estate of the sons of men, that God might manifest them, and that they might see that they themselves are beasts" → "concerning the sons of man: Elohim is to sift them, and to see that they are beasts, they, to themselves"** (v18) — Hebrew עַל־דִּבְרַת "concerning, on account of"; לְבָרָם "to sift them" (בָּרַר "sift, purify, set apart": uncertain); וְלִרְאוֹת "and to see" (who sees is not said; others point "to show"); שְׁהֶם־בְּהֵמָה הֵמָּה לָהֶם, word for word: "that they are a beast, they, to themselves" (בְּהֵמָה collective).
- **"For that which befalleth the sons of men befalleth beasts" → "For the sons of man are an event, and the beast is an event"** (v19) — the MT points מִקְרֶה in the absolute form both times ("an event", not "the event of", which would be מִקְרֵה, as 2:15): "the sons of man are (subject to) an event". The Septuagint is commonly cited as reading the construct (from memory): not followed. **"as the one dieth, so dieth the other" → "as this one dies, so that one dies"** — Hebrew כְּמוֹת זֶה כֵּן מוֹת זֶה. **"they have all one breath" → "one spirit is to all"** — Hebrew רוּחַ (also "breath"; the same word as v21). **"a man hath no preeminence above a beast" → "the profit of man over the beast is nothing"** — Hebrew מוֹתַר … אָיִן; מוֹתָר "profit" (as Prov 14:23), another word of the root of יִתְרוֹן.
- **"all are of the dust, and all turn to dust again" → "all came from the dust, and all return to the dust"** (v20) — Hebrew הָיָה מִן־הֶעָפָר "came to be from the dust"; שָׁב "returns".
- **"Who knoweth the spirit of man that goeth upward, and the spirit of the beast that goeth downward to the earth?" → "Who knows the spirit of the sons of man, which goes up upward, and the spirit of the beast, which goes down downward to the earth?"** (v21) — the MT points הָעֹלָה and הַיֹּרֶדֶת with the article ("the one going up", "the one going down"), not with the question marker ("whether it goes up"), which many translations follow (as the Septuagint, from memory): the MT is kept. לְמָעְלָה · לְמַטָּה "upward · downward".
- **"there is nothing better, than that a man should rejoice in his own works; for that is his portion" → "there is nothing better than that man should be glad in his works, for that is his portion"** (v22) — Hebrew אֵין טוֹב מֵאֲשֶׁר: here the comparative מִן is written ("than"). חֵלֶק "portion". **"for who shall bring him to see what shall be after him?"** — Hebrew כִּי מִי "for who"; בְּמֶה שֶׁיִּהְיֶה "into what will be".

### Notes
- **v11** — ***olam* in heart**: "the age, eternity" or "hiddenness"; Qohelet's key anthropology.
- **v1-8** — famous "Time" poem; 14 pairs of opposites.
