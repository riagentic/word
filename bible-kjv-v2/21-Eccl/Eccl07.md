# Ecclesiastes 7

[1] Better is a name than good oil,
and the day of death than the day of his birth.

[2] Better to go to the house of mourning
than to go to the house of a feast,
in that it is the end of all mankind,
and the living will give it to his heart.

[3] Better is vexation than laughter,
for in badness of face the heart is good.

[4] The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning,
and the heart of fools in the house of gladness.

[5] Better to hear the rebuke of a wise man
than for a man to hear the song of fools.

[6] For like the sound of the thorns under the pot,
so is the laughter of the fool;
and this also is vapor.

[7] For extortion makes a wise man mad,
and a gift does away with the heart.

[8] Better is the latter end of a thing than its beginning;
better is the long of spirit than the high of spirit.

[9] Do not be hasty in your spirit to be vexed,
for vexation rests in the bosom of fools.

[10] Do not say, "What has happened, that the former days were better than these?" For not from wisdom have you asked concerning this.

[11] Good is wisdom with an inheritance,
and an advantage to those who see the sun.

[12] For in the shade of wisdom, in the shade of silver;
and the advantage of knowledge is: wisdom lets its owners live.

[13] See the work of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: for who is able to make straight what he has made crooked?

[14] In the day of good be in good, and in the day of evil see: also the one alongside the other ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has made, to the end that man should not find anything after him.

[15] The whole I have seen in the days of my vapor: there is a righteous man perishing in his righteousness, and there is a wicked man lasting long in his evil.

[16] Do not be righteous overmuch, and do not make yourself overly wise; why should you make yourself desolate?

[17] Do not be wicked overmuch, and do not be foolish; why should you die when it is not your time?

[18] It is good that you take hold of this, and also from that do not let your hand rest; for one who fears ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall go out with all of them.

[19] Wisdom is strong for the wise man
more than ten rulers who are in the city.

[20] For there is not a righteous man on the earth
who does good and does not sin.

[21] Also to all the words that they speak do not give your heart, so that you do not hear your slave cursing you.

[22] For also many times your heart has known that you also have cursed others.

[23] All this I have tested by wisdom; I said, "Let me be wise," and it is far from me.

[24] Far off is what has been, and deep, deep; who will find it?

[25] I turned about, I and my heart, to know and to explore and to seek wisdom and a reckoning, and to know wickedness as stupidity, and foolishness as madness.

[26] And I find more bitter than death the woman whose heart is nets and dragnets, whose hands are bonds. One who is good before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shall escape from her, and a sinner shall be captured by her.

[27] See, this I have found, said ⟦qohelet|Qohelet|the Preacher⟧, one to one, to find a reckoning —

[28] which my soul has still sought, and I have not found: one man from a thousand I have found, and a woman among all these I have not found.

[29] Only, see, this I have found: that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ made man upright, and they have sought many devices.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13, 14, 18, 26, 29) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim* (v18 without the article, the others with it).
- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v6, 15) — Hebrew הֶבֶל, literally "vapor, breath"; traditional "vanity". v15 בִּימֵי הֶבְלִי "in the days of my vapor".
- **"A good name is better than precious ointment; and the day of death than the day of one's birth" → "Better is a name than good oil, and the day of death than the day of his birth"** (v1) — Hebrew טוֹב שֵׁם מִשֶּׁמֶן טוֹב: the first טוֹב is "better", the second goes with שֶׁמֶן "oil"; שֵׁם / שֶׁמֶן sound alike (*shem* / *shemen*). הִוָּלְדוֹ "his being born".
- **"the house of feasting: for that is the end of all men; and the living will lay it to his heart" → "the house of a feast, in that it is the end of all mankind, and the living will give it to his heart"** (v2) — Hebrew מִשְׁתֶּה "feast"; בַּאֲשֶׁר "in that"; כָּל־הָאָדָם "all mankind"; יִתֵּן אֶל־לִבּוֹ "will give to his heart" (= take to heart; "it" supplied).
- **"Sorrow is better than laughter: for by the sadness of the countenance the heart is made better" → "Better is vexation than laughter, for in badness of face the heart is good"** (v3) — Hebrew כַּעַס "vexation" (the כַּעַס row; also v9); רֹעַ פָּנִים "badness of face" (רֹעַ, root of רַע "evil": a troubled face); יִיטַב לֵב "the heart is good" (qal, not passive).
- **"but the heart of fools is in the house of mirth" → "and the heart of fools in the house of gladness"** (v4) — Hebrew וְ "and"; שִׂמְחָה "gladness".
- **"It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise, than for a man to hear the song of fools" → "Better to hear the rebuke of a wise man than for a man to hear the song of fools"** (v5) — Hebrew גַּעֲרַת חָכָם "the rebuke of a wise man" (singular); מֵאִישׁ שֹׁמֵעַ "than a man hearing".
- **"the crackling of thorns under a pot" → "the sound of the thorns under the pot"** (v6) — Hebrew קוֹל "sound"; הַסִּירִים "the thorns" and הַסִּיר "the pot" sound alike (*sirim* / *sir*); הַכְּסִיל "the fool" (singular).
- **"Surely oppression maketh a wise man mad; and a gift destroyeth the heart" → "For extortion makes a wise man mad, and a gift does away with the heart"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; הָעֹשֶׁק "extortion" (as 5:8); יְהוֹלֵל "makes mad" (root of הוֹלֵלוֹת "madness", v25); וִיאַבֵּד, piel of אָבַד, "does away with"; מַתָּנָה "gift" (a bribe).
- **"and the patient in spirit is better than the proud in spirit" → "better is the long of spirit than the high of spirit"** (v8) — Hebrew טוֹב אֶרֶךְ־רוּחַ מִגְּבַהּ־רוּחַ, literally "long of spirit" (= patient) and "high of spirit" (= proud); the second טוֹב has no "and". אַחֲרִית "latter end"; דָּבָר "thing" (also "word").
- **"Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools" → "Do not be hasty in your spirit to be vexed, for vexation rests in the bosom of fools"** (v9) — Hebrew לִכְעוֹס … כַעַס "to be vexed … vexation" (the כַּעַס row; not "anger": that is אַף); נוּחַ "rest"; חֵיק "bosom".
- **"What is the cause that the former days were better than these? for thou dost not enquire wisely concerning this" → "'What has happened, that the former days were better than these?' For not from wisdom have you asked concerning this"** (v10) — Hebrew מֶה הָיָה "what has happened"; לֹא מֵחָכְמָה שָׁאַלְתָּ "not from wisdom have you asked" (perfect).
- **"Wisdom is good with an inheritance: and by it there is profit to them that see the sun" → "Good is wisdom with an inheritance, and an advantage to those who see the sun"** (v11) — Hebrew עִם "with" (also read "as good as"); וְיֹתֵר "and an advantage" (יֹתֵר, of the root of יִתְרוֹן, v12); "by it" is not written.
- **"For wisdom is a defence, and money is a defence: but the excellency of knowledge is, that wisdom giveth life to them that have it" → "For in the shade of wisdom, in the shade of silver; and the advantage of knowledge is: wisdom lets its owners live"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי בְּצֵל הַחָכְמָה בְּצֵל הַכָּסֶף, literally "for in the shade of the wisdom, in the shade of the silver" (no verb: to be in the one shade is to be in the other; צֵל "shade" = protection); יִתְרוֹן "advantage"; תְּחַיֶּה, piel of חָיָה, "lets live" (keeps alive); בְעָלֶיהָ "its owners"; כֶּסֶף "silver" (also "money").
- **"Consider the work of God" → "See the work of *Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew רְאֵה "see" (also v14, 27, 29); תִּקֵּן "make straight", עִוֵּת "make crooked" (as 1:15).
- **"In the day of prosperity be joyful, but in the day of adversity consider: God also hath set the one over against the other, to the end that man should find nothing after him" → "In the day of good be in good, and in the day of evil see: also the one alongside the other *Elohim* has made, to the end that man should not find anything after him"** (v14) — Hebrew בְּיוֹם טוֹבָה הֱיֵה בְטוֹב "in the day of good be in good"; רָעָה "evil"; גַּם אֶת־זֶה לְעֻמַּת־זֶה, literally "also this alongside this" (לְעֻמַּת "alongside"); עָשָׂה "made"; עַל־דִּבְרַת שֶׁ "to the end that".
- **"All things have I seen in the days of my vanity … a just man … a wicked man that prolongeth his life in his wickedness" → "The whole I have seen in the days of my vapor … a righteous man … a wicked man lasting long in his evil"** (v15) — Hebrew אֶת־הַכֹּל "the whole, everything"; צַדִּיק "righteous", רָשָׁע "wicked"; מַאֲרִיךְ "lasting long" (hifil of אָרַךְ with no object: "his life" is not written; as Prov 28:2); בְּרָעָתוֹ "in his evil".
- **"Be not righteous over much; neither make thyself over wise: why shouldest thou destroy thyself?" → "Do not be righteous overmuch, and do not make yourself overly wise; why should you make yourself desolate?"** (v16) — Hebrew הַרְבֵּה "much"; יוֹתֵר "more, too much"; תִּשּׁוֹמֵם, hitpolel of שָׁמֵם "be desolate": "make yourself desolate" (= ruin yourself); also read "be appalled" (as Ps 143:4): uncertain.
- **"neither be thou foolish: why shouldest thou die before thy time?" → "and do not be foolish; why should you die when it is not your time?"** (v17) — Hebrew סָכָל "foolish (one)" (the סָכָל row; kept apart from כְּסִיל "fool"); בְּלֹא עִתֶּךָ, literally "in not your time"; רָשַׁע "be wicked".
- **"take hold of this; yea, also from this withdraw not thine hand: for he that feareth God shall come forth of them all" → "take hold of this, and also from that do not let your hand rest; for one who fears *Elohim* shall go out with all of them"** (v18) — Hebrew אָחַז "take hold"; אַל־תַּנַּח אֶת־יָדֶךָ "do not let your hand rest"; יֵצֵא אֶת־כֻּלָּם "shall go out with all of them": uncertain — "come out (safe) from them all", "go forth with both", "fulfil them all" are all defended.
- **"Wisdom strengtheneth the wise more than ten mighty men which are in the city" → "Wisdom is strong for the wise man more than ten rulers who are in the city"** (v19) — Hebrew תָּעֹז, qal of עָזַז "be strong" (not "make strong"); שַׁלִּיטִים "rulers" (root of שִׁלְטוֹן "power"); הָיוּ, perfect, "who are" (a lasting state).
- **"For there is not a just man upon earth, that doeth good, and sinneth not" → "For there is not a righteous man on the earth who does good and does not sin"** (v20) — Hebrew כִּי אָדָם אֵין צַדִּיק, literally "for man — there is none righteous": אָדָם stands first; בָּאָרֶץ "on the earth".
- **"Also take no heed unto all words that are spoken; lest thou hear thy servant curse thee" → "Also to all the words that they speak do not give your heart, so that you do not hear your slave cursing you"** (v21) — Hebrew יְדַבֵּרוּ "they speak" (no subject named); אַל־תִּתֵּן לִבֶּךָ "do not give your heart"; עַבְדְּךָ "your slave" (a household slave); מְקַלֶּלְךָ "cursing you" (קִלֵּל, also v22).
- **"For oftentimes also thine own heart knoweth that thou thyself likewise hast cursed others" → "For also many times your heart has known that you also have cursed others"** (v22) — Hebrew פְּעָמִים רַבּוֹת "many times" stands first and may go with "cursed"; יָדַע perfect. The verse follows the qere אַתָּה "you"; the ketiv את is a shorter spelling of the same word.
- **"All this have I proved by wisdom: I said, I will be wise; but it was far from me" → "All this I have tested by wisdom; I said, 'Let me be wise,' and it is far from me"** (v23) — Hebrew נִסָּה "test"; אֶחְכָּמָה cohortative "let me be wise"; וְהִיא רְחוֹקָה "and it is far" (nominal).
- **"That which is far off, and exceeding deep, who can find it out?" → "Far off is what has been, and deep, deep; who will find it?"** (v24) — Hebrew רָחוֹק מַה־שֶּׁהָיָה "far off is what has been" (KJV leaves out מַה־שֶּׁהָיָה); עָמֹק עָמֹק "deep, deep" (the doubling kept); יִמְצָאֶנּוּ "will find it".
- **"I applied mine heart to know, and to search, and to seek out wisdom, and the reason of things, and to know the wickedness of folly, even of foolishness and madness" → "I turned about, I and my heart, to know and to explore and to seek wisdom and a reckoning, and to know wickedness as stupidity, and foolishness as madness"** (v25) — Hebrew סַבּוֹתִי אֲנִי וְלִבִּי "I turned about, I and my heart"; תּוּר "explore"; חֶשְׁבּוֹן "reckoning" (the sum of a count; also v27); רֶשַׁע כֶּסֶל … הַסִּכְלוּת הוֹלֵלוֹת: two objects each ("to know wickedness [to be] stupidity, and the foolishness [to be] madness"); KJV reads "the wickedness of folly". כֶּסֶל "stupidity" (root of כְּסִיל "fool"; kept apart from אִוֶּלֶת "folly"); סִכְלוּת "foolishness", הוֹלֵלוֹת "madness" (the Eccl 1–4 row).
- **"the woman, whose heart is snares and nets, and her hands as bands: whoso pleaseth God shall escape from her" → "the woman whose heart is nets and dragnets, whose hands are bonds. One who is good before *Elohim* shall escape from her"** (v26) — Hebrew מוֹצֶא אֲנִי "I find" (participle); מְצוֹדִים "nets" (of hunting, as מְצוּדָה 9:12), חֲרָמִים "dragnets" (as Hab 1:15); אֲסוּרִים "bonds" ("as" is not written); טוֹב לִפְנֵי הָאֱלֹהִים "good before *Elohim*" (= pleasing to him); נִמְלַט "escape"; לָכַד "capture".
- **"saith the preacher, counting one by one, to find out the account" → "said *Qohelet*, one to one, to find a reckoning"** (v27) — Hebrew אָמְרָה קֹהֶלֶת: the verb is feminine (אָמְרָה "she said"), as printed; elsewhere קֹהֶלֶת takes a masculine verb (1:2; 12:8 אָמַר הַקּוֹהֶלֶת). The noun is feminine in form (an office-name, as סֹפֶרֶת Ezra 2:55); dividing the letters otherwise gives אָמַר הַקֹּהֶלֶת "said the Qohelet" (as 12:8), a common correction (from memory): not taken. English shows no gender; the Czech verb is feminine. אַחַת לְאַחַת "one to one" (feminine: thing to thing); חֶשְׁבּוֹן "reckoning".
- **"Which yet my soul seeketh, but I find not … but a woman among all those have I not found" → "which my soul has still sought, and I have not found … and a woman among all these I have not found"** (v28) — Hebrew perfects בִּקְשָׁה … מָצָאתִי; עוֹד "still"; וְ "and" both times; אָדָם "a man" (set against אִשָּׁה "a woman").
- **"Lo, this only have I found … but they have sought out many inventions" → "Only, see, this I have found … and they have sought many devices"** (v29) — Hebrew לְבַד "only" stands first; יָשָׁר "upright"; וְהֵמָּה "and they"; חִשְּׁבֹנוֹת "devices" (as 2 Chr 26:15; one root with חֶשְׁבּוֹן "reckoning", v25, 27).
