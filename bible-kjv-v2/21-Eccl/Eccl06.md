# Ecclesiastes 6

[1] There is an evil that I have seen under the sun, and it is great upon mankind:

[2] a man to whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gives riches and wealth and glory, and he lacks nothing for his soul of all that he craves, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does not give him power to eat of it, for a foreign man eats it. This is vapor, and it is an evil sickness.

[3] If a man begets a hundred, and lives many years, and many are the days of his years, and his soul is not sated from the good, and also he has had no burial, I said: better than he is the miscarriage.

[4] For in vapor it came, and in darkness it goes, and with darkness its name is covered.

[5] Also the sun it has not seen, and it has not known; there is rest for this one rather than for that one.

[6] And even if he lived a thousand years twice, and has not seen good — does not all go to one place?

[7] All the toil of man is for his mouth,
and also the soul is not filled.

[8] For what more has the wise than the fool?
What has the afflicted one who knows to walk before the living?

[9] Better is the sight of the eyes than the going of the soul.
This also is vapor and a pursuit of wind.

[10] Whatever has been, its name has already been called, and it is known that he is man; and he is not able to contend with one who is stronger than he.

[11] For there are many words that make much vapor; what more is there for man?

[12] For who knows what is good for man in life, the number of the days of the life of his vapor, and he makes them like the shadow? For who will tell man what will be after him under the sun?

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים *Elohim*, with the article, both times.
- **"vanity" → "vapor"** (v2, 4, 9, 11, 12) — Hebrew הֶבֶל, literally "vapor, breath"; traditional "vanity". v12 חַיֵּי הֶבְלוֹ "the life of his vapor" (KJV "his vain life").
- **"and it is common among men" → "and it is great upon mankind"** (v1) — Hebrew וְרַבָּה הִיא עַל־הָאָדָם: רַב "great, much" (heavy upon, or frequent among); עַל "upon". The same phrase in 8:6.
- **"riches, wealth, and honour, so that he wanteth nothing for his soul of all that he desireth, yet God giveth him not power to eat thereof, but a stranger eateth it" → "riches and wealth and glory, and he lacks nothing for his soul of all that he craves, and *Elohim* does not give him power to eat of it, for a foreign man eats it"** (v2) — Hebrew עֹשֶׁר וּנְכָסִים וְכָבוֹד (as 2 Chr 1:11); חָסֵר "lack"; יִתְאַוֶּה "crave"; יַשְׁלִיטֶנּוּ "give him power" (as 5:19); אִישׁ נָכְרִי "a foreign man"; כִּי "for".
- **"an evil disease" → "an evil sickness"** (v2) — Hebrew חֳלִי רָע (חֳלִי "sickness", as 5:17).
- **"If a man beget an hundred children" → "If a man begets a hundred"** (v3) — Hebrew מֵאָה "a hundred"; "children" is not written. וְרַב שֶׁיִּהְיוּ יְמֵי־שָׁנָיו, literally "and much that the days of his years are"; תִשְׂבַּע מִן־הַטּוֹבָה "sated from the good".
- **"and also that he have no burial" → "and also he has had no burial"** (v3) — Hebrew קְבוּרָה, the burial itself here (elsewhere "burial place"), perfect לֹא־הָיְתָה.
- **"I say, that an untimely birth is better than he" → "I said: better than he is the miscarriage"** (v3) — Hebrew אָמַרְתִּי, perfect; נֵפֶל "miscarriage" (literally "a falling", as Job 3:16); "better off" is not written.
- **"For he cometh in with vanity … and his name shall be covered with darkness" → "For in vapor it came … and with darkness its name is covered"** (v4) — the subject is the miscarriage of v3 (masculine in Hebrew; "it"); בָּא perfect "came"; כָּסָה "cover".
- **"nor known any thing: this hath more rest than the other" → "and it has not known; there is rest for this one rather than for that one"** (v5) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָדָע has no object ("any thing" is not written); נַחַת לָזֶה מִזֶּה, literally "rest to this more than this".
- **"though he live a thousand years twice told, yet hath he seen no good" → "even if he lived a thousand years twice, and has not seen good"** (v6) — Hebrew אִלּוּ "if" (unreal), פַּעֲמַיִם "twice"; הַכֹּל "all" (singular: "does not all go").
- **"and yet the appetite is not filled" → "and also the soul is not filled"** (v7) — Hebrew הַנֶּפֶשׁ "the soul" (with the article; not "his"); וְגַם "and also"; עָמָל "toil" (KJV "labour").
- **"For what hath the wise more than the fool? what hath the poor, that knoweth to walk before the living?" → "For what more has the wise than the fool? What has the afflicted one who knows to walk before the living?"** (v8) — Hebrew יוֹתֵר "more" (of the root of יִתְרוֹן "advantage"); עָנִי "afflicted" (the poor man bowed down); הָלַךְ נֶגֶד "walk before".
- **"than the wandering of the desire: this is also vanity and vexation of spirit" → "than the going of the soul. This also is vapor and a pursuit of wind"** (v9) — Hebrew מַרְאֵה עֵינַיִם "the sight of the eyes" (מַרְאֶה, the "appearance" row: what the eyes see); הֲלָךְ־נֶפֶשׁ "the going of the soul" (the roving of desire); רְעוּת רוּחַ "a pursuit of wind" (רְעוּת uncertain: "shepherding" or "desire" of wind; KJV "vexation of spirit").
- **"That which hath been is named already, and it is known that it is man: neither may he contend with him that is mightier than he" → "Whatever has been, its name has already been called, and it is known that he is man; and he is not able to contend with one who is stronger than he"** (v10) — Hebrew נִקְרָא שְׁמוֹ "its name has been called"; אֲשֶׁר־הוּא אָדָם "that he is man" (or "what man is"; אָדָם may sound the name Adam: "earthling"); דִּין עִם "contend with" (the דִּין "judge" row: a case at law). The verse follows the qere שֶׁתַּקִּיף "one who is stronger"; the ketiv שהתקיף is another spelling.
- **"Seeing there be many things that increase vanity, what is man the better?" → "For there are many words that make much vapor; what more is there for man?"** (v11) — Hebrew דְּבָרִים "words" (also "things"; after 5:2–7 words are likely, not certain); מַרְבִּים "make much"; יֹתֵר "more", as v8.
- **"in this life, all the days of his vain life which he spendeth as a shadow" → "in life, the number of the days of the life of his vapor, and he makes them like the shadow"** (v12) — Hebrew מִסְפַּר יְמֵי "the number of the days of" (as 5:18); וְיַעֲשֵׂם "and he makes them" (= spends them, as Ruth 2:19); כַּצֵּל "like the shadow".
- **"for who can tell a man" → "For who will tell man"** (v12) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר מִי־יַגִּיד, imperfect; אֲשֶׁר here "for, because".
