# Ecclesiastes 1

[1] The words of ⟦qohelet|Qohelet|the Preacher⟧, son of David, king in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] Vapor of vapors, says ⟦qohelet|Qohelet|the Preacher⟧,
vapor of vapors; all is vapor.

[3] What advantage has man
in all his toil that he toils under the sun?

[4] A generation goes, and a generation comes,
and the earth stands forever.

[5] And the sun rises, and the sun sets,
and to its place it pants, rising there.

[6] Going to the south
and circling to the north,
circling, circling goes the wind,
and on its circuits the wind returns.

[7] All the wadis go to the sea,
and the sea is not full;
to the place where the wadis go,
there they return to go.

[8] All the words are weary;
a man is not able to speak.
The eye is not sated with seeing,
and the ear is not filled from hearing.

[9] What has been is what will be,
and what has been done is what will be done,
and there is nothing new under the sun.

[10] There is a thing of which one says, "See this: it is new."
Already it has been, in the ages
that were before us.

[11] There is no remembrance of the former ones;
and also of the latter ones who will be, there will be no remembrance of them
with those who will be at the last.

[12] I, ⟦qohelet|Qohelet|the Preacher⟧, was king over ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[13] And I gave my heart to seek and to explore by wisdom concerning all that is done under the heavens. It is an evil occupation that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has given to the sons of man, to be occupied with it.

[14] I have seen all the works that are done under the sun, and behold, all is vapor and a pursuit of wind.

[15] What is crooked cannot become straight,
and what is lacking cannot be counted.

[16] I myself spoke with my heart, saying, "As for me, behold, I have made great and added wisdom above all who were before me over ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, and my heart has seen much wisdom and knowledge."

[17] And I gave my heart to know wisdom, and to know madness and foolishness; I knew that this also is a pursuit of wind.

[18] For in much wisdom is much vexation,
and he who adds knowledge adds pain.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"the Preacher" → "Qohelet"** (v1, 2, 12) — Hebrew קֹהֶלֶת *Qohelet* (a feminine participle of קָהַל "assemble": one who gathers or addresses an assembly); it stands as a name/title (token `qohelet`).
- **"Vanity of vanities … all is vanity" → "Vapor of vapors … all is vapor"** (v2, 14) — Hebrew הֲבֵל הֲבָלִים, הֶבֶל: "vapor, breath", what is fleeting and without substance; traditional "vanity" (Latin *vanitas*). One word through the book. The construct with its own plural is a superlative ("the utmost vapor"), kept word for word.
- **"What profit hath a man of all his labour which he taketh" → "What advantage has man in all his toil that he toils"** (v3) — Hebrew יִתְרוֹן "advantage" (what is left over: a gain beyond); לָאָדָם "to man"; בְּכָל־עֲמָלוֹ "in all his toil"; שֶׁיַּעֲמֹל "that he toils" (noun and verb of one root, עָמָל). The line break follows the accents (the main pause is after "man").
- **"One generation passeth away … but the earth abideth for ever" → "A generation goes … and the earth stands forever"** (v4) — Hebrew הֹלֵךְ "goes"; וְהָאָרֶץ "and the earth" (the clause sets the earth against the generations; "but" is possible); עֹמָדֶת "stands".
- **"goeth down, and hasteth to his place where he arose" → "sets, and to its place it pants, rising there"** (v5) — Hebrew וּבָא, literally "and comes / goes in" (of the sun: sets); שׁוֹאֵף "pants" (gasps, or pants after; "hastens" is an interpretation); זוֹרֵחַ הוּא שָׁם "it rising there". The line begins with "And" as the Hebrew (וְזָרַח).
- **"The wind goeth toward the south … it whirleth about continually" → "Going to the south and circling to the north, circling, circling goes the wind"** (v6) — Hebrew הוֹלֵךְ … וְסוֹבֵב are participles with no subject until הָרוּחַ in the second half: whether the first half still speaks of the sun (v5) or already of the wind is open, so no subject is supplied. סוֹבֵב סֹבֵב "circling, circling"; סְבִיבֹתָיו "its circuits" (one root). רוּחַ "wind" here and in "a pursuit of wind" (v14, 17): the same word as "spirit".
- **"All the rivers run into the sea … thither they return again" → "All the wadis go to the sea … there they return to go"** (v7) — Hebrew נְחָלִים "wadis" (stream beds); הֹלְכִים "go"; שָׁבִים לָלָכֶת "they return to go" (= go again): word for word.
- **"All things are full of labour; man cannot utter it" → "All the words are weary; a man is not able to speak"** (v8) — Hebrew כָּל־הַדְּבָרִים "all the words" (or "all the things": the next clause, לְדַבֵּר "to speak", leans to "words"; the ambiguity is the Hebrew's); יְגֵעִים "weary" (or "wearying"); "it" is not written. מִשְּׁמֹעַ "from hearing".
- **"Is there any thing whereof it may be said … it hath been already of old time" → "There is a thing of which one says, 'See this: it is new.' Already it has been, in the ages"** (v10) — the Hebrew has no question word: יֵשׁ דָּבָר "there is a thing"; שֶׁיֹּאמַר "of which he (one) says"; לְעֹלָמִים "in / for ages"; אֲשֶׁר הָיָה "that were" (the verb is singular in the Hebrew).
- **"There is no remembrance of former things; neither … of things that are to come" → "There is no remembrance of the former ones; and also of the latter ones who will be"** (v11) — Hebrew רִאשֹׁנִים · אַחֲרֹנִים are masculine plurals ("former ones, latter ones": persons or things, not said). זִכָּרוֹן "remembrance": the word that is "memorial" (a thing that reminds) in Exodus; here being remembered. לָאַחֲרֹנָה "at the last".
- **"I the Preacher was king"** (v12) — Hebrew הָיִיתִי "I was".
- **"I gave my heart to seek and search out" → "I gave my heart to seek and to explore"** (v13, 17) — Hebrew נָתַתִּי אֶת־לִבִּי "I gave my heart" (= set my mind; kept); תּוּר "explore". **"this sore travail" → "an evil occupation"** — Hebrew עִנְיַן רָע: עִנְיָן "occupation" (what one is busied with), רָע "evil" (of quality "bad" is also possible); לַעֲנוֹת בּוֹ "to be occupied with it" (verb of the same root). **"the sons of man"** — Hebrew בְּנֵי הָאָדָם. The verse begins with "And" (וְנָתַתִּי).
- **"vexation of spirit" → "a pursuit of wind"** (v14, 17) — Hebrew רְעוּת רוּחַ (v14), רַעְיוֹן רוּחַ (v17): the sense of רְעוּת / רַעְיוֹן is uncertain: "pursuit, striving after", or "shepherding" (רָעָה "shepherd") or "desire" (Aramaic רְעוּת "will"). רוּחַ "wind" (also "spirit").
- **"cannot be made straight … cannot be numbered" → "cannot become straight … cannot be counted"** (v15) — Hebrew לִתְקֹן, a qal infinitive ("to be straight"); לְהִמָּנוֹת "to be counted" (מָנָה).
- **"I communed with mine own heart, saying, Lo, I am come to great estate, and have gotten more wisdom" → "I myself spoke with my heart, saying, 'As for me, behold, I have made great and added wisdom'"** (v16) — Hebrew דִּבַּרְתִּי אֲנִי עִם־לִבִּי "I spoke, I, with my heart"; אֲנִי הִנֵּה "I, behold"; הִגְדַּלְתִּי וְהוֹסַפְתִּי חָכְמָה "I made great and added wisdom" (one act: I greatly increased wisdom). עַל־יְרוּשָׁלִָם "over Jerusalem" (of those who ruled it). לִבִּי רָאָה "my heart has seen".
- **"madness and folly" → "madness and foolishness"** (v17) — Hebrew הוֹלֵלוֹת "madness"; שִׂכְלוּת (= סִכְלוּת, written with ש) "foolishness", kept apart from אִוֶּלֶת "folly". **"I perceived that this also is vexation of spirit" → "I knew that this also is a pursuit of wind"** — Hebrew יָדַעְתִּי "I knew"; "but" is not in the Hebrew. The verse begins with "And" (וָאֶתְּנָה).
- **"much grief … increaseth sorrow" → "much vexation … adds pain"** (v18) — Hebrew כַּעַס "vexation"; יוֹסִיף "adds" (as וְהוֹסַפְתִּי, v16); מַכְאוֹב "pain".

### Notes
- **Hevel havalim** — superlative construction: "the most fleeting of vapors."
- **"Under the sun"** — Qohelet's characteristic phrase (29× in book); horizontal, earthly perspective.
