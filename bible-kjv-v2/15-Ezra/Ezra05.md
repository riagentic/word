# Ezra 5

[1] Now the prophets, Haggai the prophet and Zechariah son of Iddo, prophesied to the Jews who were in Judah and Jerusalem in the name of the *Elohim* of Israel who was over them.

[2] Then Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua son of Jozadak arose and began to rebuild the house of *Elohim* in Jerusalem; with them were the prophets of *Elohim*, supporting them.

[3] At the same time Tattenai, governor of the province Beyond the River, and Shethar-bozenai, and their associates came to them and said, "Who has commanded you to build this house and to finish this structure?"

[4] Then we said to them, "What are the names of the men who are building this building?"

[5] But the eye of their *Elohim* was on the elders of the Jews, so that they did not stop them until a report could reach Darius, and then a written reply be returned concerning this matter.

[6] A copy of the letter that Tattenai, governor of the province Beyond the River, with Shethar-bozenai and his associates the Persian commissioners who were in the province Beyond the River, sent to King Darius.

[7] They sent him a report, in which was written:

"To King Darius, all peace.

[8] Be it known to the king that we went into the province of Judah, to the house of the Great *Elohim*, which is being built with great stones and timber laid in the walls; this work is being done diligently and prospers in their hand.

[9] Then we asked these elders, saying, 'Who commanded you to build this house and to finish this structure?'

[10] We also asked their names, that we might inform you, and that we might write down the names of the men at their head.

[11] This was the answer they returned to us: 'We are servants of the *Elohim* of heaven and earth; we are rebuilding the house that was built many years ago, which a great king of Israel built and completed.

[12] But because our fathers provoked the *Elohim* of heaven to wrath, he gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, the Chaldean, who destroyed this house and exiled the people to Babylon.

[13] However, in the first year of Cyrus king of Babylon, King Cyrus issued a decree to rebuild this house of *Elohim*.

[14] Also, the gold and silver vessels of the house of *Elohim*, which Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple that was in Jerusalem and brought into the temple of Babylon — these King Cyrus took out of the temple of Babylon and delivered to one Sheshbazzar by name, whom he had made governor;

[15] he said to him, "Take these vessels; go, deposit them in the temple in Jerusalem, and let the house of *Elohim* be rebuilt on its site."

[16] Then this Sheshbazzar came and laid the foundations of the house of *Elohim* in Jerusalem; and from that time until now it has been being built, and it is not yet finished.'

[17] Now therefore, if it seems good to the king, let a search be made in the royal treasury at Babylon, whether a decree was indeed issued by King Cyrus to rebuild this house of *Elohim* in Jerusalem; and let the king send us his pleasure in this matter."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"governor on this side the river" → "governor of the province Beyond the River"** (v3, 6).
- **"house of the great God" → "house of the Great *Elohim*"** (v8) — Aramaic *elaha rabba*.
- **"Chaldean" — retained** (v12) — *Kasdaya*.

### Notes
- **Haggai and Zechariah** — contemporary prophets (ca. 520 BC). Their books record the messages that reignited the temple-rebuilding.
- **Tattenai** — attested in Persian administrative records; historical figure. Governor (*pachat*) of "Beyond the River" (the fifth Persian satrapy).
- **"Great *Elohim*"** — Aramaic-era honorific; Persian-court-appropriate divine title.
- **Sheshbazzar vs Zerubbabel** — overlap or confusion? Sheshbazzar laid the foundation originally (per this letter); Zerubbabel resumes the work. Might be the same person or two leaders in succession.
- **Appeal to archives** — Persian bureaucracy: decree must be verified via royal records. Essential plot mechanism of ch 6.
- **"Eye of their *Elohim*"** (v5) — providence metaphor; Yahweh watches over the elders, restraining opposition.
- **"Built many years ago... by a great king of Israel"** — Solomon. Rhetorical connection to pre-exilic legitimacy.
