# Ezra 7

[1] And after these things, in the reign of ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧, Ezra the son of ⟦seraiah|Serayah|Seraiah⟧, the son of ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧, the son of ⟦hilkiah|Chilqiyahu|Hilkiah⟧,

[2] the son of Shallum, the son of ⟦zadok|Tzadoq|Zadok⟧, the son of ⟦ahitub|Achituv|Ahitub⟧,

[3] the son of ⟦amariah|Amaryah|Amariah⟧, the son of ⟦azariah|Azaryah|Azariah⟧, the son of ⟦meraioth|Merayot|Meraioth⟧,

[4] the son of ⟦zerahiah|Zerachyah|Zerahiah⟧, the son of Uzzi, the son of ⟦bukki|Buqqi|Bukki⟧,

[5] the son of ⟦abishua|Avishua|Abishua⟧, the son of ⟦phinehas|Pinchas|Phinehas⟧, the son of ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧, the son of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the head priest —

[6] this Ezra went up from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧. And he was a scribe skilled in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, which ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had given; and the king gave him, according to the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ upon him, all his request.

[7] And there went up some of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and of the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ and the singers and the gatekeepers and the ⟦nethinim|Netinim|temple servants⟧ to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, in the seventh year of ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ the king.

[8] And he came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ in the fifth month; that was the seventh year of the king.

[9] For on the first of the first month, that was the founding of the going up from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, and on the first of the fifth month he came to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, according to the good hand of his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ upon him.

[10] For Ezra had established his heart to seek the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and to do it, and to teach in ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ statute and judgment.

[11] And this is the copy of the document that King ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, a scribe of the words of the commandments of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and of his statutes for ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[12] "⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens, perfect. And now:

[13] By me a decree is made that everyone in my kingdom of the people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and its priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ who offers himself willingly to go to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ may go with you,

[14] because you are sent from before the king and his seven counselors to inquire about ⟦judah|Yehud|Judah⟧ and ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ by the law of your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in your hand,

[15] and to carry the silver and gold that the king and his counselors have offered willingly to the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whose dwelling is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[16] and all the silver and gold that you find in all the province of ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧, with the willing offering of the people and of the priests, offering willingly for the house of their ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[17] Because of this, with diligence you shall buy with this silver bulls, rams, lambs, and their grain offerings and their drink offerings, and you shall bring them near on the altar of the house of your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[18] And whatever seems good to you and to your brothers to do with the rest of the silver and the gold, according to the will of your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ you shall do.

[19] And the vessels that are given to you for the service of the house of your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, deliver in full before the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[20] And the rest of the needs of the house of your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ which it falls to you to give, you shall give from the house of the treasures of the king.

[21] And by me, I, ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ the king, a decree is made to all the treasurers who are across the River, that all that Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens, asks of you shall be done with diligence,

[22] up to a hundred talents of silver, and up to a hundred kors of wheat, and up to a hundred baths of wine, and up to a hundred baths of oil, and salt without prescription.

[23] All that is by the decree of the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens, let it be done zealously for the house of the ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ of the heavens; for why should there be wrath against the kingdom of the king and his sons?

[24] And to you we make known that all the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, the singers, the gatekeepers, the ⟦nethinim|Netinim|temple servants⟧ and those who serve this house of ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧, tribute, tax and toll it is not permitted to impose on them.

[25] And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ that is in your hand, appoint judges and justices who may be judging all the people that is across the River, all who know the laws of your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧; and whoever does not know, you shall make him know.

[26] And whoever will not do the law of your ⟦elah-aram|Elah|God⟧ and the law of the king, let judgment be done on him with diligence, whether to death, or to uprooting, or to a fine of goods, or to bonds."

[27] Blessed is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of our fathers, who has put such a thing as this in the heart of the king, to beautify the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧,

[28] and has stretched out ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ to me before the king and his counselors and before all the mighty princes of the king. And I strengthened myself according to the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ upon me, and I gathered heads out of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to go up with me.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v6, 10, 11, 27, 28) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v6, 9, 27, 28) — Hebrew *Elohim*. **"God" → "*Elah*"** (v12–26) — Aramaic אֱלָהּ *Elah* (v12, 15, 19, 21, 23) and its forms אֱלָהָא *Elaha* (v24), אֱלָהָךְ "your Elah" (singular "you": v14, 19, 20, 25, 26), אֱלָהֲכֹם "your Elah" (plural "you": v17, 18), אֱלָהֲהֹם "their Elah" (v16): the Aramaic word for "God".
- **"And"** (v1, 6–8, 11, 27–28) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"the chief priest" → "the head priest"** (v5) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֵן הָרֹאשׁ, as 2 Kgs 25:18.
- **"a ready scribe" → "a scribe skilled"** (v6) — Hebrew סֹפֵר מָהִיר, מָהִיר "quick, practiced". **"granted him all his request, according to the hand of the LORD his God upon him" → "gave him, according to the hand of Yahweh his Elohim upon him, all his request"** — the Hebrew order; נָתַן "give".
- **"the porters" → "the gatekeepers"** (v7, 24) — Hebrew שֹׁעֲרִים, Aramaic תָרָעַיָּא. **"the Nethinims" → "the Netinim"** (v7, 24) — token (lit. "given ones").
- **"which was in the seventh year" → "that was the seventh year"** (v8) — Hebrew הִיא שְׁנַת הַשְּׁבִיעִית.
- **"upon the first day of the first month began he to go up" → "on the first of the first month, that was the founding of the going up"** (v9) — Hebrew הוּא יְסֻד הַמַּעֲלָה: יְסֻד as pointed is a noun, "founding, beginning"; read as a verb (יִסַּד "he fixed") it is "he set the going up" — the MT pointing is kept. "Day" is not in the Hebrew (also "the first of the fifth month").
- **"prepared his heart" → "established his heart"** (v10) — Hebrew הֵכִין לְבָבוֹ, as 2 Chr 12:14. **"to do it"** — "it" is supplied. **"statutes and judgments" → "statute and judgment"** — Hebrew חֹק וּמִשְׁפָּט, singular.
- **"the copy of the letter" → "the copy of the document"** (v11) — Hebrew פַּרְשֶׁגֶן הַנִּשְׁתְּוָן, the Aramaic loanwords of 4:7, 11. **"even a scribe of" → "a scribe of"**. **"to Israel" → "for Israel"** — Hebrew עַל־יִשְׂרָאֵל, literally "upon Israel".
- **"a scribe of the law" → "the scribe of the law"** (v12, 21) — Aramaic סָפַר דָּתָא, with the article: דָּת is a Persian loanword, "law, decree" (v14, 25, 26), not the Hebrew *torah*. **"perfect peace" → "perfect"** — Aramaic גְּמִיר, "complete, perfect"; the Aramaic has no "peace". It may stand for a shortened greeting, or describe the scribe ("a perfect scribe"); the Aramaic does not decide. **"and at such a time" → "And now"** — Aramaic וּכְעֶנֶת, as 4:10.
- **"I make a decree" → "By me a decree is made"** (v13, 21) — Aramaic מִנִּי שִׂים טְעֵם, as 4:19. **"of his priests" → "its priests"** — Aramaic וְכָהֲנוֹהִי, "his", of the people. **"which are minded of their own freewill" → "who offers himself willingly"** — Aramaic כָל־מִתְנַדַּב, singular. **"go up" → "go"** — Aramaic לִמְהָךְ.
- **"sent of the king" → "sent from before the king"** (v14) — Aramaic מִן־קֳדָם מַלְכָּא. **"to enquire concerning" → "to inquire about"** — Aramaic לְבַקָּרָא עַל, the verb of "search" (4:15, 19; 5:17; 6:1). **"Judah"** — Aramaic יְהוּד, as 5:1.
- **"freely offered" → "offered willingly"** (v15, 16) — Aramaic הִתְנַדַּבוּ, the verb of v13. **"habitation" → "dwelling"** — Aramaic מִשְׁכְּנֵהּ, as Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן. **"the freewill offering" → "the willing offering"** (v16) — Aramaic הִתְנַדָּבוּת.
- **"That thou mayest buy speedily with this money" → "Because of this, with diligence you shall buy with this silver"** (v17) — Aramaic כָּל־קֳבֵל דְּנָה אָסְפַּרְנָא תִקְנֵא בְּכַסְפָּא דְנָה. **"meat offerings" → "grain offerings"** — Aramaic מִנְחָה. **"offer them upon" → "bring them near on"** — Aramaic תְקָרֵב, as 6:10, 17.
- **"thy brethren" → "your brothers"** (v18) — qere אֶחָךְ (ketiv אחיך; also qere עֲלָךְ for ketiv עליך: spelling only). **"that do" → "you shall do"** — plural.
- **"deliver thou" → "deliver in full"** (v19) — Aramaic הַשְׁלֵם, "make complete, hand over whole".
- **"whatsoever more shall be needful … which thou shalt have occasion to bestow, bestow it out of the king's treasure house" → "the rest of the needs … which it falls to you to give, you shall give from the house of the treasures of the king"** (v20) — Aramaic חַשְׁחוּת "need", דִּי יִפֶּל־לָךְ "which falls to you", בֵּית גִּנְזֵי מַלְכָּא (as 5:17).
- **"And I, even I Artaxerxes the king" → "And by me, I, Artaxerxes the king"** (v21) — Aramaic וּמִנִּי אֲנָה. **"shall require of you" → "asks of you"** — Aramaic יִשְׁאֲלֶנְכוֹן.
- **"an hundred measures of wheat" → "a hundred kors of wheat"** (v22) — Aramaic כֹּרִין (as Hebrew כֹּר, 1 Kgs 4:22). **"salt without prescribing how much" → "salt without prescription"** — Aramaic דִּי־לָא כְתָב, literally "without writing".
- **"Whatsoever is commanded by the God of heaven" → "All that is by the decree of the Elah of the heavens"** (v23) — Aramaic מִן־טַעַם. **"diligently" → "zealously"** — Aramaic אַדְרַזְדָּא, a Persian loanword found only here, "zealously, exactly" (uncertain; another word than אָסְפַּרְנָא "with diligence"). **"the realm" → "the kingdom"** — Aramaic מַלְכוּת. **"wrath"** — Aramaic קְצַף.
- **"touching any of" → "all"** (v24) — Aramaic כָל. **"ministers of this house" → "those who serve this house"** — Aramaic פָלְחֵי, the root of פָלְחָן "service" (v19). **"it shall not be lawful" → "it is not permitted"** — Aramaic לָא שַׁלִּיט. **"to impose"** — Aramaic לְמִרְמֵא, literally "to throw".
- **"set magistrates and judges" → "appoint judges and justices"** (v25) — Aramaic מֶנִּי שָׁפְטִין וְדַיָּנִין: two words for "judge" (from שְׁפַט and from דִּין); English has no second plain word. **"which may judge" → "who may be judging"** — qere דָּאיְנִין, participle (ketiv דאנין). **"all the people that are" → "all the people that is"** — Aramaic עַמָּה, singular. **"and teach ye them that know them not" → "and whoever does not know, you shall make him know"** — Aramaic וְדִי לָא יָדַע תְּהוֹדְעוּן: the causative of "know" (as 4:14, 16), plural "you"; "him" is supplied.
- **"let judgment be executed speedily upon him" → "let judgment be done on him with diligence"** (v26) — Aramaic דִּינָה … מִתְעֲבֵד מִנֵּהּ אָסְפַּרְנָא. **"banishment" → "uprooting"** — qere לִשְׁרֹשִׁי (ketiv לשרשו), "uprooting": banishment, or another punishment (the sense is not certain). **"confiscation of goods" → "a fine of goods"** — Aramaic עֲנָשׁ נִכְסִין. **"imprisonment" → "bonds"** — Aramaic אֱסוּרִין.
- **"Blessed be" → "Blessed is"** (v27) — Hebrew בָּרוּךְ, no verb. **"in the king's heart" → "in the heart of the king"**. **"to beautify"** — Hebrew לְפָאֵר.
- **"hath extended mercy unto me" → "has stretched out steadfast love to me"** (v28) — Hebrew וְעָלַי הִטָּה־חֶסֶד, literally "and upon me stretched out *chesed*". **"the king's mighty princes" → "the mighty princes of the king"** — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַמֶּלֶךְ הַגִּבֹּרִים. **"I was strengthened as the hand … was upon me" → "I strengthened myself according to the hand … upon me"** — Hebrew הִתְחַזַּקְתִּי, reflexive; כְּיַד as v6, 9. **"chief men" → "heads"** — Hebrew רָאשִׁים.

### Notes

- **Language** — v1–11 are Hebrew. At v12 the text changes to Aramaic: the king's letter (v12–26) is given in the language in which it was written. At v27 the text changes back to Hebrew, and Ezra speaks in the first person (v27–28; ch. 8–9).
- **Ezra's descent** (v1–5) — traced through Zadok to Aaron; the list passes over several generations (cf. 1 Chr 6:3–15).
- **"The seventh year of Artaxerxes"** (v7–8) — 458 BC if Artaxerxes I is meant (398 BC if Artaxerxes II).
- **The journey** (v9) — from the first of Nisan to the first of Av, four months.
- **"Seven counselors"** (v14) — the Persian royal council (cf. Esth 1:14).
