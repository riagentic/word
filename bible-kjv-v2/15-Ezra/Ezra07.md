## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v7, 24).
- **"meat offerings" → "grain offerings"** (v17).
- **"princes" → "officials"** (v28) — *sarim*.

### Notes
- **Ezra's descent** — traced to Aaron via Zadok. Zadokite high-priestly lineage. Sixteen generations listed.
- **"Seventh year of Artaxerxes"** — likely 458 BC (Artaxerxes I) or by some chronologies 428 BC (Artaxerxes II). Most scholars favor 458 BC.
- **Four-month journey** — Nisan 1 to Ab 1, about 1,400 km (900 miles) from Babylon to Jerusalem.
- **Ezra: priest + scribe** — the first such dual-role in canonical Scripture. Scribal identity becomes dominant in Second Temple era.
- **Persian imperial charter** (v12-26) — granted wide judicial and religious authority over Beyond-the-River Jews. Historical plausible Persian policy; documentary parallels from Elephantine papyri and Behistun Inscription.
- **"Seven counselors"** — Persian royal council (cf. Esth 1:14).
- **Ezra Memoirs** begin here — v27-28 and much of ch 8-9 are first-person. Ezra's autobiographical material.
- **Aramaic section ends at v26** — returns to Hebrew at v27.
