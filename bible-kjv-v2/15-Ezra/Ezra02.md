## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v68 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"children" → "sons"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי. In the list it names the members of a clan or the people of a town ("the sons of Bethlehem"); אַנְשֵׁי "the men of" (v22, 23, 27, 28) is another word and is kept apart.
- **Figures** (throughout) — written in words, in the English order; the Hebrew gives them as printed, often in the order "seventy and two" and with רִבּוֹא "ten thousand" (v64, 69). Names and figures are translated as Ezra prints them; Neh 7 has the same list with several different names and figures, and nothing is levelled.
- **"These are the children of the province … every one unto his city; which came with Zerubbabel" → "And these are the sons of the province … each man to his city, who came with Zerubbabel"** (v1–2) — the Hebrew is one sentence: v2 begins אֲשֶׁר־בָּאוּ "who came". מְדִינָה "province" (a district under the Persian king, here Judah); שְׁבִי הַגּוֹלָה "the captivity of the exiles"; הֶגְלָה "carry into exile". The qere נְבוּכַדְנֶצַּר is the usual spelling; the ketiv writes the name with ו (no difference in sense). The list of v2 has no "and" before Baanah and names eleven men (Neh 7:7 has twelve, with Nahamani).
- **"of the children of Jeshua and Joab" → "of the sons of Jeshua, Joab"** (v6) — Ezra has no "and" (לִבְנֵי יֵשׁוּעַ יוֹאָב; Neh 7:11 has it): two houses, or a house called "Jeshua-Joab"; the Hebrew does not decide.
- **"Ramah … Ai" → "the Ramah … the Ai"** (v26, 28) — both names have the article in Hebrew (הָרָמָה, "the height"; הָעַי, "the ruin").
- **"Kirjatharim"** (v25) — so Ezra, קִרְיַת עָרִים; Neh 7:29 and elsewhere קִרְיַת יְעָרִים "Kiriath-jearim".
- **"of Elam the other" → "of the other Elam"** (v31) — Hebrew עֵילָם אַחֵר; the figure is the same as v7.
- **"the children of the porters … in all" → "the sons of the gatekeepers … in all"** (v42) — Hebrew שֹׁעֲרִים "gatekeepers"; הַכֹּל "the whole".
- **"Shalmai"** (v46) — the qere, שַׁלְמַי; the ketiv reads Shamlai (שמלי). **"Mehunim … Nephusim"** (v50) — the qere is מְעוּנִים "Meunim" and נְפוּסִים "Nefusim"; the ketiv reads Meinim and Nefisim. The qere is translated.
- **"the children of Solomon's servants"** (v55, 58) — Hebrew עַבְדֵי שְׁלֹמֹה; עֶבֶד is the one word for "slave" and "servant": of a king's men "servant". **"Hassophereth"**: the name has the article, הַסֹּפֶרֶת.
- **"Tel-melah, Tel-harsha, Cherub, Addan, and Immer: but they could not shew their father's house, and their seed" → "… Cherub, Addan, Immer; and they could not tell their fathers' house and their seed"** (v59) — the Hebrew has no "and" in the list and "and" (not "but") before the clause; הִגִּיד "tell"; זֶרַע "seed".
- **"These sought their register among those that were reckoned by genealogy … therefore were they, as polluted, put from the priesthood" → "These sought their writing, those enrolled by genealogy, and they were not found; and they were defiled away from the priesthood"** (v62) — Hebrew כְּתָבָם "their writing"; הַמִּתְיַחְשִׂים "those enrolled by genealogy" stands beside "these", not as "among"; וַיְגֹאֲלוּ מִן, a pual of גָּאַל II "be defiled" (not גָּאַל "redeem"): they were treated as unclean and so put out of the priesthood. "As polluted, put" is the sense, not the words.
- **"the Tirshatha … should not eat of the most holy things, till there stood up a priest with Urim and with Thummim" → "the Tirshatha … should not eat of the most holy until a priest should stand for Urim and for Thummim"** (v63) — Hebrew הַתִּרְשָׁתָא, a Persian title (perhaps "the one to be revered"); a governor is meant (Nehemiah bears it, Neh 8:9; 10:1), but it is kept apart from פֶּחָה "governor". קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים, literally "the holiness of the holinesses": the most holy portions of the offerings ("things" is not in the Hebrew). עָמַד "stand"; לְ "for": a priest able to give answer by Urim and Thummim.
- **"The whole congregation together" → "The whole assembly as one"** (v64) — Hebrew קָהָל "assembly"; כְּאֶחָד "as one". The sum of the figures given (v3–60) is less than 42,360; the Hebrew does not explain it.
- **"their servants and their maids … two hundred singing men and singing women" → "their slaves and their slave women … two hundred male singers and female singers"** (v65) — Hebrew עֶבֶד "slave", אָמָה "slave woman": owned persons, as in the law; מְשֹׁרְרִים וּמְשֹׁרְרוֹת, the masculine and the feminine of "singer". "These were": אֵלֶּה "these".
- **"their asses" → "donkeys"** (v67) — the Hebrew has no "their" here, though horses, mules and camels have it.
- **"of the chief of the fathers … offered freely for the house of God to set it up in his place" → "some of the heads of the fathers … offered willingly for the house of Elohim, to make it stand on its established place"** (v68) — Hebrew הִתְנַדְּבוּ "offer willingly"; לְהַעֲמִידוֹ "to make it stand"; מָכוֹן "established place" (the same word as Exod 15:17).
- **"after their ability … threescore and one thousand drams of gold … priests' garments" → "according to their strength … sixty-one thousand drachmas of gold … tunics of priests"** (v69) — Hebrew כְּכֹחָם "according to their strength"; דַּרְכְּמוֹנִים is a coin; whether the Greek drachma or the Persian daric (אֲדַרְכֹּן, 1 Chr 29:7) is meant is not certain; the word is nearer to "drachma". מָנֶה "mina" (a weight of silver). כְּתֹנֶת "tunic" (the priest's undergarment, Exod 28:39–40).
- **"So the priests … dwelt in their cities" → "And the priests … dwelt in their cities"** (v70) — the Hebrew chain of "and" is kept; וּמִן־הָעָם "and some of the people".

### Notes
- **Parallel in Neh 7** — near-identical list with minor numeric variants. Both copy from the same registry.
- **Total 42,360** — does not equal the sum of listed numbers (~29,818); the discrepancy may account for unmentioned groups or textual issues.
- **Eleven leaders with Zerubbabel** (v2) — Neh 7:7 has twelve names (Nahamani is added); the number twelve, one per tribe, is often thought to be meant. Nehemiah here is not the book's namesake (later).
- **Priest without Urim/Thummim** — post-exilic high priest lacks these divinatory gemstones (perhaps lost at 586). Suspended category preserves possibility of future adjudication.
- **Barzillai name-lineage** — rare Israelite custom: husband takes wife's name. Daughters of Barzillai inherited wealth; maternal-name preservation.
- **Netinim and Solomon's servants** — temple-dedicated labor force of non-Israelite origin. Post-exilic Jewish community includes them as accepted sub-class.
