## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v42, 70).

### Notes
- **Parallel in Neh 7** — near-identical list with minor numeric variants. Both copy from the same registry.
- **Total 42,360** — does not equal the sum of listed numbers (~29,818); the discrepancy may account for unmentioned groups or textual issues.
- **Twelve leaders with Zerubbabel** (v2) — representing twelve tribes symbolically. Nehemiah here is not the book's namesake (later).
- **Priest without Urim/Thummim** — post-exilic high priest lacks these divinatory gemstones (perhaps lost at 586). Suspended category preserves possibility of future adjudication.
- **Barzillai name-lineage** — rare Israelite custom: husband takes wife's name. Daughters of Barzillai inherited wealth; maternal-name preservation.
- **Netinim and Solomon's servants** — temple-dedicated labor force of non-Israelite origin. Post-exilic Jewish community includes them as accepted sub-class.
