# Ezra 9

[1] And when these things were finished, the princes drew near to me, saying, "The people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ have not separated themselves from the peoples of the lands, according to their abominations, of the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧, the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧, the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧, the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧, the ⟦ammonites|Ammoni|Ammonite⟧, the ⟦moabites|Moavi|Moabite⟧, the ⟦egyptian|Mitzri|Egyptian⟧ and the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧.

[2] For they have taken of their daughters for themselves and for their sons, and the holy seed has mixed itself with the peoples of the lands; and the hand of the princes and the prefects has been first in this unfaithfulness."

[3] And when I heard this word, I tore my garment and my robe, and I plucked out some of the hair of my head and of my beard, and I sat appalled.

[4] And to me were gathered everyone who trembled at the words of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, because of the unfaithfulness of the exile; and I was sitting appalled until the evening grain offering.

[5] And at the evening grain offering I rose up from my humbling, and with my garment and my robe torn, and I bowed on my knees and spread out my palms to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[6] And I said, "My ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, I am ashamed and put to shame to raise, my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, my face to you; for our iniquities have increased above the head, and our guilt has grown up to the heavens.

[7] From the days of our fathers we have been in great guilt to this day; and for our iniquities we, our kings, our priests, have been given into the hand of the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity and to plunder and to shame of face, as at this day.

[8] And now, for a little moment, there has been favor from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to leave us an escape and to give us a peg in his holy place, that our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ may give light to our eyes and give us a little preservation of life in our slavery.

[9] For slaves we are; and in our slavery our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has not forsaken us, and he has stretched out ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ to us before the kings of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧, to give us preservation of life, to raise high the house of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and to make its ruins stand, and to give us a fence in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[10] And now, what shall we say, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, after this? For we have forsaken your commandments,

[11] which you commanded by the hand of your servants the prophets, saying, 'The land that you are going in to possess is a land of impurity, through the impurity of the peoples of the lands, through their abominations, with which they have filled it from mouth to mouth with their uncleanness.

[12] And now, your daughters do not give to their sons, and their daughters do not take for your sons, and do not seek their ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ and their good forever, so that you may be strong and eat the good of the land and give it to your sons to possess forever.'

[13] And after all that has come upon us for our evil deeds and for our great guilt — for you, our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, have held back below our iniquity, and have given us an escape such as this —

[14] shall we return to break your commandments and to intermarry with the peoples of these abominations? Will you not be angry with us until making an end, so that there is no remnant and escape?

[15] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, you are righteous, for we are left an escape, as at this day. Behold, we are before you in our guilt, for there is no standing before you because of this."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; "my Elohim" (v5, 6), "our Elohim" (v8–10, 13).
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept, also where English would subordinate (v5, 9, 12).
- **"Now when these things were done, the princes came to me" → "And when these things were finished, the princes drew near to me"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּכַלּוֹת "at the finishing of"; נָגַשׁ "draw near"; שָׂרִים "princes" (as v2; 10:8, 14). **"The people of Israel … doing according to their abominations, even of the Canaanites" → "The people Israel … according to their abominations, of the Canaanite"** — Hebrew הָעָם יִשְׂרָאֵל, the two nouns side by side; "doing" is not written: כְּתוֹעֲבֹתֵיהֶם "according to their abominations", then לַ before the first name, "belonging to": the abominations are those of the peoples. The eight names are singulars with the article ("the Canaanite"), as Exod 3:8.
- **"so that the holy seed have mingled themselves with the people of those lands: yea, the hand of the princes and rulers hath been chief in this trespass" → "and the holy seed has mixed itself with the peoples of the lands; and the hand of the princes and the prefects has been first in this unfaithfulness"** (v2) — Hebrew וְהִתְעָרְבוּ "and they mixed themselves" (a plural verb with the collective "seed"); זֶרַע הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the seed of holiness"; עַמֵּי הָאֲרָצוֹת "the peoples of the lands" (as v1, 11; not "those"); סְגָנִים "prefects" (officers under a governor, a loan word; the rank is uncertain); מַעַל "unfaithfulness", a breach of trust (as Lev 5:15; 2 Chr 36:14); רִאשׁוֹנָה "first". "Wives" is not in the Hebrew of v2: "taken of their daughters", as נָשָׂא in 10:44.
- **"I rent my garment and my mantle, and plucked off the hair of my head and of my beard, and sat down astonied" → "I tore my garment and my robe, and I plucked out some of the hair of my head and of my beard, and I sat appalled"** (v3) — Hebrew בֶּגֶד "garment", מְעִיל "robe"; מִשְּׂעַר "some of the hair" (מִן "of"); מְשׁוֹמֵם, of שָׁמֵם "be desolate", of a person "be appalled" (also v4). "Word": Hebrew הַדָּבָר הַזֶּה.
- **"because of the transgression of those that had been carried away" → "because of the unfaithfulness of the exile"** (v4) — Hebrew מַעַל הַגּוֹלָה: גּוֹלָה "exile", the exiles as a body (as 8:35; 10:6, 8). חָרֵד בְּ "tremble at". **"evening sacrifice" → "evening grain offering"** (v4, 5) — Hebrew מִנְחַת הָעֶרֶב, the grain offering of the evening (Exod 29:41).
- **"I arose up from my heaviness; and having rent my garment and my mantle, I fell upon my knees, and spread out my hands" → "I rose up from my humbling, and with my garment and my robe torn, and I bowed on my knees and spread out my palms"** (v5) — Hebrew תַּעֲנִית, found only here, the noun of הִתְעַנָּה "humble oneself" (8:21): a fast; וּבְקָרְעִי, literally "and at my tearing"; כָּרַע "bow, kneel"; כַּפַּי "my palms".
- **"I am ashamed and blush to lift up my face to thee, my God" → "I am ashamed and put to shame to raise, my Elohim, my face to you"** (v6) — Hebrew בֹּשְׁתִּי וְנִכְלַמְתִּי; הֵרִים "raise"; the second "my Elohim" stands inside the clause. **"our iniquities are increased over our head, and our trespass is grown up unto the heavens" → "our iniquities have increased above the head, and our guilt has grown up to the heavens"** — Hebrew לְמַעְלָה רֹּאשׁ "above (the) head", no "our"; אַשְׁמָה "guilt" (also v7, 13, 15).
- **"have we been in a great trespass unto this day … been delivered … to a spoil, and to confusion of face, as it is this day" → "we have been in great guilt to this day … have been given … to plunder and to shame of face, as at this day"** (v7) — Hebrew נִתַּנּוּ "we were given"; "we, our kings, our priests" without "and"; בִּזָּה "plunder"; בֹּשֶׁת פָּנִים "shame of face"; כְּהַיּוֹם הַזֶּה "as at this day" (also v15).
- **"for a little space grace hath been shewed from the LORD our God, to leave us a remnant to escape, and to give us a nail in his holy place" → "for a little moment, there has been favor from Yahweh our Elohim, to leave us an escape and to give us a peg in his holy place"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּמְעַט־רֶגַע "as a little moment"; תְחִנָּה "favor" (the noun of חָנַן "show favor"); פְּלֵיטָה "escape" (the escaping, or those who have escaped: a body of survivors; also v13, 14, 15); יָתֵד "peg" (a tent peg driven in: a firm hold); מְקוֹם קָדְשׁוֹ, literally "the place of his holiness". **"that our God may lighten our eyes, and give us a little reviving in our bondage" → "that our Elohim may give light to our eyes and give us a little preservation of life in our slavery"** — Hebrew לְהָאִיר עֵינֵינוּ; מִחְיָה "preservation of life" (as Gen 45:5; also v9); עַבְדֻת "slavery", the noun of עֶבֶד "slave" (v9).
- **"For we were bondmen; yet our God hath not forsaken us … but hath extended mercy unto us in the sight of the kings of Persia" → "For slaves we are; and … our Elohim has not forsaken us, and he has stretched out chesed to us before the kings of Persia"** (v9) — Hebrew עֲבָדִים אֲנַחְנוּ, no verb: "are"; וַיַּט "and he stretched out" (as Gen 39:21); חֶסֶד; לִפְנֵי "before". **"to set up the house of our God, and to repair the desolations thereof, and to give us a wall" → "to raise high the house of our Elohim and to make its ruins stand, and to give us a fence"** — Hebrew רוֹמֵם "raise high"; הֶעֱמִיד "make stand"; חָרְבֹתָיו "its ruins"; גָּדֵר "fence, wall of stones" (not חוֹמָה "city wall": a protected place, not the city wall itself).
- **"Now, O our God, what shall we say after this?" → "And now, what shall we say, our Elohim, after this?"** (v10) — the Hebrew order; מִצְוֹת "commandments" (also v14).
- **"by thy servants the prophets … The land, unto which ye go to possess it, is an unclean land with the filthiness of the people of the lands" → "by the hand of your servants the prophets … The land that you are going in to possess is a land of impurity, through the impurity of the peoples of the lands"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּיַד "by the hand of"; עֲבָדֶיךָ, the word is "slaves": of Yahweh's men "servants"; אַתֶּם בָּאִים "you are going in" (as Deut 4:5); אֶרֶץ נִדָּה … בְּנִדַּת, the one word נִדָּה "impurity" twice (of what is cast off as unclean, Lev 15; the KJV has "unclean … filthiness"). **"which have filled it from one end to another with their uncleanness" → "with which they have filled it from mouth to mouth with their uncleanness"** — Hebrew מִפֶּה אֶל־פֶּה "from mouth to mouth" (= from end to end, as 2 Kgs 10:21; 21:16); טֻמְאָה "uncleanness". The prophets' words are not a quotation of one verse; they gather Lev 18:24–30; Deut 7:1–3.
- **"Now therefore give not your daughters … nor seek their peace or their wealth for ever: that ye may be strong … and leave it for an inheritance to your children for ever" → "And now, your daughters do not give … and do not seek their shalom and their good forever, so that you may be strong … and give it to your sons to possess forever"** (v12) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה "and now"; the objects stand first; לֹא־תִדְרְשׁוּ שְׁלֹמָם וְטוֹבָתָם, the words of Deut 23:6; לְמַעַן "so that"; וְהוֹרַשְׁתֶּם, hifil of יָרַשׁ, "you shall make (your sons) possess"; "it" is supplied; בָּנִים "sons".
- **"seeing that thou our God hast punished us less than our iniquities deserve, and hast given us such deliverance as this" → "for you, our Elohim, have held back below our iniquity, and have given us an escape such as this"** (v13) — Hebrew חָשַׂכְתָּ לְמַטָּה מֵעֲוֺנֵנוּ, literally "you have withheld downward from our iniquity": you have kept back (the stroke) below what our iniquity is. "Punished", "less", "deserve" are not written. כִּי "for"; פְּלֵיטָה "escape" as v8.
- **"Should we again break thy commandments, and join in affinity with" → "shall we return to break your commandments and to intermarry with"** (v14) — Hebrew הֲנָשׁוּב לְהָפֵר, שׁוּב "return" + infinitive (= do again: kept); הֵפֵר "break"; הִתְחַתֵּן "intermarry", literally "make oneself son-in-law". **"till thou hadst consumed us, so that there should be no remnant nor escaping" → "until making an end, so that there is no remnant and escape"** — Hebrew עַד־כַּלֵּה, an infinitive without an object: "until making an end"; אָנַף "be angry"; שְׁאֵרִית "remnant", פְּלֵיטָה "escape".
- **"O LORD God of Israel … for we remain yet escaped … in our trespasses: for we cannot stand before thee because of this" → "Yahweh, Elohim of Israel … for we are left an escape … in our guilt, for there is no standing before you because of this"** (v15) — Hebrew נִשְׁאַרְנוּ פְלֵיטָה "we are left (as) an escape"; אַשְׁמָתֵינוּ, singular in sense ("our guilt"); כִּי אֵין לַעֲמוֹד, literally "for there is none to stand": no one can stand. כִּי is "for" both times.

### Notes
- **Mixed marriages crisis** — post-exilic community's most divisive internal issue. Ezra judges wholesale intermarriage as covenant unfaithfulness.
- **"Holy seed"** (v2) — biological theology of election applied to marriage. Malachi 2, Neh 13 parallel concerns.
- **List of peoples** — some extinct by Ezra's day (Hittites, Jebusites); rhetorical: covers all unclean categories.
- **Self-mortification** — hair-tearing, tearing of robes, appalled sitting — extreme ritual grief. Rabbinic tradition sees Ezra's response as definitive.
- **Prayer pattern** — confession, historical recital, current grace, plea. Classic Second-Temple prayer form (cf. Dan 9, Neh 9).
- **"Held back below our iniquity"** (v13) — Yahweh's stroke has been less than the iniquity; restraint, not acquittal.
- **"A peg in his holy place"** (v8) — a tent peg driven in: a secure foothold at the place of the house. Precarious but real.
