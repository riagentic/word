# Ezra 3

[1] When the seventh month came, and the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ were in their cities, the people gathered themselves as one man to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[2] Then ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ son of ⟦jozadak|Yotzadaq|Jozadak⟧, with his brothers the priests, and ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, with his brothers, arose and built the altar of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, to offer burnt offerings on it as it is written in the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ the man of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] They set the altar on its foundation, for fear was upon them because of the peoples of the lands; and they offered burnt offerings on it to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, burnt offerings morning and evening.

[4] They kept the ⟦sukkot|Sukkot|Feast of Booths⟧ as it is written, and offered the daily burnt offerings by number, according to the ordinance, as each day required;

[5] and afterward the continual burnt offering, the offerings for the new moons and for all the appointed feasts of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ that are sanctified, and for everyone who offered a freewill offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[6] From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; but the foundation of the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had not yet been laid.

[7] They gave silver also to the masons and the carpenters; and food, drink, and oil to the ⟦sidonians|Tzidonim|Sidonians⟧ and the ⟦tyrians|Tzorim|Tyrians⟧, to bring cedar logs from ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ to the sea of ⟦joppa|Yafo|Joppa⟧, according to the grant that they had from ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧.

[8] In the second year of their coming to the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, in the second month, ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ son of ⟦jozadak|Yotzadaq|Jozadak⟧, the rest of their brothers the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and all who had come out of the captivity to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ began; they appointed the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, from twenty years old and upward, to oversee the work of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] ⟦jeshua|Yeshua|Jeshua⟧ with his sons and his brothers, ⟦kadmiel|Qadmiel|Kadmiel⟧ and his sons, the sons of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, stood together to oversee the workmen in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; also the sons of ⟦henadad|Chenadad|Henadad⟧, with their sons and brothers, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧.

[10] When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, they set the priests in their vestments, with trumpets, and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ the sons of ⟦asaph|Asaf|Asaph⟧ with cymbals, to praise ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ according to the ordinance of David king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[11] They sang responsively in praising and giving thanks to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For he is good;
for his steadfast love — endures forever toward ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

All the people shouted with a great shout in praising ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, because the foundation of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had been laid.

[12] But many of the priests, ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and heads of fathers' houses — old men who had seen the first house — wept with a loud voice when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes; and many shouted aloud for joy.

[13] The people could not distinguish the sound of the shout of joy from the sound of the people's weeping, for the people shouted with a great shout, and the sound was heard far away.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"set feasts" → "appointed feasts"** (v5) — *mo'adim*.
- **"money" → "silver"** (v7).
- **"meat" → "food"** (v7) — *okel*; general provisions, not just flesh.
- **"Zidon" → "Sidon"** (v7).
- **"ancient men" → "old men"** (v12) — *zeqenim*.

### Notes
- **Seventh month** — Tishri. Begins with Feast of Trumpets, Yom Kippur, and Sukkot. Appropriate month for restoration.
- **Altar first** — even before temple foundation, daily sacrifice restored. Worship prioritized over building.
- **"Fear of the peoples of the lands"** (v3) — hostile neighbors (Samarians, Arabs). Worship a defiant act.
- **Second year, second month** (v8) — builds begin roughly 536 BC. Parallels Solomon's temple-start.
- **"The sons of Judah"** (v9) — MT *bnei Yehudah*; in this Levite list likely = "the sons of Hodaviah" (2:40; Neh 7:43 Hodevah), the two names being close in Hebrew. MT kept.
- **Phoenician cedar again** — repeats Solomon's pattern with Hiram. Cyrus's grant provides the Persian state-level funding.
- **Mixed tears and shouts** (v12-13) — older generation remembers First Temple's glory; younger rejoices at any restoration. Hag 2:3 echoes this ambivalence.
- **"His chesed endures forever"** — standard liturgical refrain (Ps 136). Continuity with pre-exilic worship.
