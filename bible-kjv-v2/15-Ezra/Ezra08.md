# Ezra 8

[1] These are the heads of their fathers' houses, and this is the genealogy of those who went up with me from Babylon in the reign of King Artaxerxes:

[2] of the sons of Phinehas, Gershom; of the sons of Ithamar, Daniel; of the sons of David, Hattush;

[3] of the sons of Shecaniah, of the sons of Parosh, Zechariah, and with him were enrolled by genealogy one hundred fifty males;

[4] of the sons of Pahath-moab, Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, and with him two hundred males;

[5] of the sons of Shecaniah (Zattu), son of Jahaziel, and with him three hundred males;

[6] of the sons of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan, and with him fifty males;

[7] of the sons of Elam, Jeshaiah son of Athaliah, and with him seventy males;

[8] of the sons of Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael, and with him eighty males;

[9] of the sons of Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel, and with him 218 males;

[10] of the sons of Shelomith (Bani), son of Josiphiah, and with him 160 males;

[11] of the sons of Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai, and with him 28 males;

[12] of the sons of Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan, and with him 110 males;

[13] of the later sons of Adonikam — these are their names: Eliphelet, Jeuel, and Shemaiah — and with them 60 males;

[14] of the sons of Bigvai, Uthai and Zaccur, and with them 70 males.

[15] I gathered them at the river that flows to Ahava; we camped there three days. I surveyed the people and the priests, and found there none of the sons of Levi.

[16] Then I sent for Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam — leading men — and for Joiarib and Elnathan — teachers.

[17] I sent them to Iddo, the leader at the place Casiphia; I told them what to say to Iddo and his brothers the temple servants at the place Casiphia, to bring to us ministers for the house of our *Elohim*.

[18] By the good hand of our *Elohim* upon us, they brought us a man of understanding from the sons of Mahli son of Levi son of Israel, namely Sherebiah, with his sons and brothers, eighteen;

[19] and Hashabiah, and with him Jeshaiah of the sons of Merari, with his brothers and their sons, twenty;

[20] and from the temple servants whom David and the officials had appointed for the service of the Levites, two hundred twenty temple servants. All of them were designated by name.

[21] Then I proclaimed a fast there at the river Ahava, that we might humble ourselves before our *Elohim*, to seek from him a safe way for us, our little ones, and all our goods.

[22] For I was ashamed to ask the king for a band of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy on the way, because we had told the king, "The hand of our *Elohim* is for good upon all who seek him, but his power and wrath are against all who forsake him."

[23] So we fasted and besought our *Elohim* for this, and he was moved by our entreaty.

[24] Then I set apart twelve of the leading priests — Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their brothers with them —

[25] and weighed out to them the silver, the gold, and the vessels — the offering for the house of our *Elohim* that the king, his counselors, his officials, and all Israel who were present had offered.

[26] I weighed into their hand six hundred fifty talents of silver; silver vessels worth one hundred talents; and one hundred talents of gold;

[27] twenty gold bowls worth a thousand darics; and two vessels of fine polished bronze, as precious as gold.

[28] I said to them, "You are holy to Yahweh; the vessels are holy; the silver and the gold are a freewill offering to Yahweh the *Elohim* of your fathers.

[29] Guard them and keep them until you weigh them before the leading priests and the Levites, and the heads of fathers' houses of Israel, at Jerusalem, in the storerooms of the house of Yahweh."

[30] So the priests and the Levites received the weight of the silver, the gold, and the vessels, to bring them to Jerusalem, to the house of our *Elohim*.

[31] We departed from the river Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month, to go to Jerusalem; the hand of our *Elohim* was upon us, and he delivered us from the hand of the enemy and from ambush along the way.

[32] We came to Jerusalem and stayed there three days.

[33] On the fourth day, the silver, the gold, and the vessels were weighed in the house of our *Elohim* into the hand of Meremoth son of Uriah the priest; with him was Eleazar son of Phinehas; and with them were Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui, Levites —

[34] the whole by number and by weight, and the entire weight was recorded at that time.

[35] The children of the exiles who had come from captivity offered burnt offerings to the *Elohim* of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs; and twelve male goats as a sin offering — all a burnt offering to Yahweh.

[36] They delivered the king's edicts to the king's satraps and the governors of the province Beyond the River, and they supported the people and the house of *Elohim*.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"king's commissions" → "king's edicts"** (v36) — *datei ha-melek*.
- **"king's lieutenants" → "king's satraps"** (v36) — *achashdarpenim*; Persian term.
- **"fine copper" → "fine polished bronze"** (v27) — *nechoshet mutzhav*.
- **"chambers" → "storerooms"** (v29) — *lishkah*.
- **"men of understanding" → "teachers"** (v16) — *mevinim*; understanding-ones, likely instructors.
- **"afflict ourselves" → "humble ourselves"** (v21) — *lehit'annot*.

### Notes
- **Ezra's caravan** — second major return, ca. 458 BC. About 1,500 men + families.
- **"Hattush of the sons of David"** (v2) — Davidic descendant; further exiles include Davidic line.
- **No Levites initially** (v15) — Levites reluctant to return (temple-task less lucrative?). Special recruitment at Casiphia (a Jewish teaching center in Babylonia? Proto-academy?).
- **Faith vs military escort** — Ezra refuses armed guard; trusts divine protection based on prior boast to king.
- **Massive treasure** — 650 silver talents + 100 gold talents + vessels. Real Persian-era figures suggesting the imperial favor.
- **Three-day arrival gap** (v32) — typical rabbinic formula (compare Neh 2:11, 2 Chr 20:25).
- **Sacrificial numbers** — 12 bulls, 12 goats (tribal symbolism); 96 rams (divisible by 12?); 77 lambs. Pan-Israelite dedication.
- **"Satraps"** — authentic Persian provincial title (Aramaic *achshadarpan*, from Old Persian *xshaçapāvan*).
