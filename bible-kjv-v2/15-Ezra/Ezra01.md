# Ezra 1

[1] And in the first year of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧, that the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the mouth of ⟦jeremiah|Yirmeyahu|Jeremiah⟧ might be finished, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ stirred up the spirit of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧, and he made a voice pass through all his kingdom, and also in writing, saying:

[2] "Thus says ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧: All the kingdoms of the earth ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the heavens has given to me, and he has appointed me to build for him a house in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, which is in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[3] Whoever among you is of all his people, may his ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be with him, and let him go up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, which is in ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and let him build the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — he is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ — which is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[4] And everyone who is left, from all the places where he sojourns, let the men of his place support him with silver and with gold and with goods and with beasts, with the freewill offering for the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ which is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧."

[5] And the heads of the fathers of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧ arose, and the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ — all whose spirit ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had stirred up — to go up to build the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ which is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

[6] And all those around them strengthened their hands with utensils of silver, with gold, with goods, and with beasts, and with precious things, apart from all that was offered willingly.

[7] And King ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ brought out the utensils of the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, which ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ had brought out from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and had placed in the house of his gods.

[8] And ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧ brought them out by the hand of ⟦mithredath|Mitredat|Mithredath⟧ the treasurer, and he numbered them out to ⟦sheshbazzar|Sheshbatzar|Sheshbazzar⟧ the chieftain of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[9] And this is their number: thirty platters of gold, a thousand platters of silver, twenty-nine knives,

[10] thirty basins of gold, four hundred and ten basins of silver of a second kind, a thousand other utensils.

[11] All the utensils of gold and of silver were five thousand four hundred. All of it ⟦sheshbazzar|Sheshbatzar|Sheshbazzar⟧ brought up, with the bringing up of the exiles from ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; in v3 (second time) and v5 with the article, הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. v1–3 have the words of 2 Chr 36:22–23 and keep their wording where the Hebrew is the same.
- **"by the mouth of Jeremiah … might be fulfilled" → "from the mouth of Jeremiah might be finished"** (v1) — Hebrew מִפִּי, "from the mouth of" (2 Chr 36:22 has בְּפִי, literally "in the mouth of"); לִכְלוֹת, of כָּלָה "be finished, come to its end", not מִלֵּא "fulfill". "In the first year": literally "in year one". **"made a proclamation … and also put it in writing" → "made a voice pass through all his kingdom, and also in writing"** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבֶר־קוֹל, as Exod 36:6; the second half has no verb.
- **"The LORD God of heaven hath given me … hath charged me" → "All the kingdoms of the earth Yahweh the Elohim of the heavens has given to me … has appointed me"** (v2) — the Hebrew order; שָׁמַיִם is plural; פָּקַד עָלַי, literally "appointed upon me": laid it on me as a charge; וְהוּא "and he" is stressed.
- **"Who is there among you of all his people? his God be with him, and let him go up" → "Whoever among you is of all his people, may his Elohim be with him, and let him go up"** (v3) — Hebrew מִי "who", a question or "whoever"; יְהִי "may he be", a wish; Ezra has no "Yahweh" before "his Elohim" (2 Chr 36:23 has "Yahweh his Elohim", without a verb). וְיִבֶן "and let him build", a jussive like "let him go up". **"(he is the God,) which is in Jerusalem" → "— he is Elohim — which is in Jerusalem"** — Hebrew הוּא הָאֱלֹהִים אֲשֶׁר בִּירוּשָׁלִָם: the relative may go back to "the house" or belong to the clause, "he is the Elohim who is in Jerusalem"; the Hebrew does not decide. The article of הָאֱלֹהִים is not shown.
- **"whosoever remaineth in any place where he sojourneth, let the men of his place help him … beside the freewill offering" → "everyone who is left, from all the places where he sojourns, let the men of his place support him … with the freewill offering"** (v4) — Hebrew הַנִּשְׁאָר "who is left" (the survivors of the exile); יְנַשְּׂאוּהוּ, piel of נָשָׂא, literally "lift him up": help, provide for; עִם "with, together with", not "beside". בְּהֵמָה "beasts" (farm animals).
- **"the chief of the fathers" → "the heads of the fathers"** (v5) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת. **"with all them whose spirit God had raised" → "all whose spirit Elohim had stirred up"** — Hebrew לְכֹל, literally "as to all"; הֵעִיר as in v1. "To go up to build": two infinitives.
- **"strengthened their hands with vessels of silver … beside all that was willingly offered" → "strengthened their hands with utensils of silver … apart from all that was offered willingly"** (v6) — Hebrew חִזְּקוּ בִידֵיהֶם, literally "strengthened in their hands"; כְּלִי, any made article: "utensils" as v7, 10, 11 and 2 Chr 36:7; מִגְדָּנוֹת "precious things" (choice gifts, as Gen 24:53); לְבַד עַל "apart from"; הִתְנַדֵּב "offer willingly".
- **"which Nebuchadnezzar had brought forth … and had put them in the house of his gods" → "had brought out … and had placed"** (v7) — the same verb הוֹצִיא for Cyrus and for Nebuchadnezzar; נָתַן, literally "gave". אֱלֹהָיו "his gods": the plural form may also mean "his god".
- **"brought forth by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and numbered them unto Sheshbazzar, the prince of Judah" → "by the hand of … the treasurer, and he numbered them out to Sheshbazzar the chieftain of Judah"** (v8) — Hebrew גִּזְבָּר, a Persian word, "treasurer"; סָפַר "number"; נָשִׂיא "chieftain" (literally "one lifted up"), not שַׂר "prince"; לִיהוּדָה, literally "to / for Judah".
- **"chargers" → "platters"; "knives"** (v9) — Hebrew אֲגַרְטְלֵי, a loan word found only here; the vessel is not known (a basin or a basket has been proposed); "platters" keeps it apart from כְּפוֹר "basin" (v10). מַחֲלָפִים is found only here and is uncertain: "knives" (from חָלַף "pass through"), or "replacements" (from חָלַף "change"). The Hebrew puts the figure after the noun ("platters of gold, thirty"); here in the English order.
- **"basons … of a second sort" → "basins of silver of a second kind"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּפוֹרֵי, "basins" (uncertain, as 1 Chr 28:17); מִשְׁנִים, literally "seconds": of a second kind or rank (or "doubles"); uncertain. No "and" before "a thousand other utensils".
- **"All the vessels … were five thousand and four hundred. All these did Sheshbazzar bring up with them of the captivity" → "All the utensils … were five thousand four hundred. All of it Sheshbazzar brought up, with the bringing up of the exiles"** (v11) — Hebrew הַכֹּל "the whole"; עִם הֵעָלוֹת הַגּוֹלָה, a nifal infinitive: "with the being brought up of the exile(s)". The figures of v9–10 add up to 2,499, not 5,400: the MT is translated as printed.

### Notes
- **Direct continuation from 2 Chr 36:22-23** — Ezra-Nehemiah as one book in Hebrew. Chronicler and Ezra-Nehemiah likely same author or school.
- **Cyrus decree (539 BC)** — Hebrew translation/paraphrase of Persian imperial policy. A Persian version in Aramaic appears at Ezra 6:3-5 (slightly different). Cyrus Cylinder (British Museum) corroborates policy of restoring displaced peoples to their lands and gods.
- **"Yahweh the *Elohim* of the heavens"** — standard Persian-era divine title. Accommodates Persian dualistic-monotheism.
- **Sheshbazzar** — Babylonian-name Davidide, possibly =Shenazzar son of Jeconiah (1 Chr 3:18). First Persian-appointed governor of Yehud.
- **Zerubbabel appears later** (ch 2); possibly Sheshbazzar's successor or co-leader.
- **Temple vessels return** — Solomonic treasures, stolen by Nebuchadnezzar, returned as symbol of restored covenant.
- **5,400 total vessels** — inventory style typical of Persian administrative records; the itemized figures (v9–10) give 2,499.
- **Voluntary contribution model** — Persian subsidy + community freewill offering. No forced returnees.
