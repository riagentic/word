# Ezra 10

[12] And all the assembly answered and said with a loud voice, "So! According to your word it is upon us to do.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*; "the house of the Elohim", הָאֱלֹהִים with the article (v1, 6, 9).
- **"And" · lists** (throughout) — each "and" stands where the Hebrew has וְ, also where it joins only some names of a list (v26, 27, 29, 30, 37–41, 43) or none (v22, 28, 33); the lists have no verb. From v1 Ezra is spoken of in the third person (ch. 7–9 have "I").
- **"strange wives" → "foreign women"** (v2, 10, 11, 14, 17, 18, 44) — Hebrew נָשִׁים נָכְרִיּוֹת: נָכְרִי "foreign"; אִשָּׁה is "woman" and "wife" (one word; "wives" where they are the men's own, v19, 44).
- **"have taken strange wives" → "have made foreign women dwell"** (v2, 10, 14, 17, 18) — Hebrew הֹשִׁיב, hifil of יָשַׁב "dwell", literally "made dwell": brought into the house as wives (as Neh 13:23, 27). In v44 the Hebrew has the plain verb נָשָׂא "take".
- **"Now when Ezra had prayed, and when he had confessed" → "And while Ezra was praying and while he was confessing"** (v1) — Hebrew כְּ + infinitive twice ("as he was praying"); הִתְוַדָּה "confess"; מִתְנַפֵּל "throwing himself down". **"a very great congregation of men and women and children: for the people wept very sore" → "a very great assembly, men and women and children, for the people wept, with much weeping"** — Hebrew קָהָל "assembly" (also v8, 12, 14); הִרְבָּה־בֶכֶה, literally "making much weeping".
- **"one of the sons of Elam"** (v2) — the reading tradition (qere) is עֵילָם "Elam"; the written text (ketiv) has עולם, "olam", the letters of the word "age, forever". The qere is translated. **"We have trespassed against our God … yet now there is hope in Israel concerning this thing" → "We have acted unfaithfully against our Elohim … and now there is hope for Israel concerning this"** — Hebrew מָעַל בְּ (as 9:2, 4; also v6, 10); מֵעַמֵּי הָאָרֶץ "of the peoples of the land"; וְעַתָּה "and now"; מִקְוֶה "hope" (as 1 Chr 29:15); "thing" is not written.
- **"let us make a covenant with our God to put away all the wives, and such as are born of them" → "let us cut a covenant to our Elohim to bring out all the women and what is born of them"** (v3) — Hebrew נִכְרָת־בְּרִית "let us cut a covenant"; לֵאלֹהֵינוּ "to our Elohim"; הוֹצִיא "bring out" (= send out of the house; also v19); הַנּוֹלָד, a singular collective: "what is born". **"according to the counsel of my lord" → "by the counsel of Adonai"** — the Masoretic pointing is אֲדֹנָי, the divine title; pointed אֲדֹנִי "my lord" it would be Ezra (so the KJV). The MT is kept. **"those that tremble at the commandment of our God"** — Hebrew הַחֲרֵדִים בְּמִצְוַת, as 9:4. **"and let it be done according to the law"** — Hebrew וְכַתּוֹרָה יֵעָשֶׂה.
- **"this matter belongeth unto thee: we also will be with thee: be of good courage, and do it" → "the matter is upon you, and we are with you; be strong and do it"** (v4) — Hebrew עָלֶיךָ "upon you"; no verb in "we with you"; חֲזַק "be strong"; "it" is supplied.
- **"the chief priests, the Levites" → "the chiefs of the priests, the Levites"** (v5) — Hebrew שָׂרֵי הַכֹּהֲנִים הַלְוִיִּם, as 8:24, 29; "the Levites" stands beside "the priests" without "and" (as Deut 17:9). הִשְׁבִּיעַ "make swear".
- **"and when he came thither, he did eat no bread, nor drink water: for he mourned because of the transgression of them that had been carried away" → "and he went there: bread he did not eat and water he did not drink, for he was mourning over the unfaithfulness of the exile"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיֵּלֶךְ שָׁם "and he went there", as printed; reading וַיָּלֶן "and he spent the night there" (one letter) is a common proposal, supported by the Greek of 1 Esd 9:2 (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. The objects stand first; מִתְאַבֵּל "mourning"; גּוֹלָה "exile", the exiles as a body (also v7, 8, 16). לִשְׁכָּה "chamber".
- **"they made proclamation throughout Judah … unto all the children of the captivity, that they should gather themselves together" → "they made a voice pass through Judah … to all the sons of the exile, to be gathered"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבִירוּ קוֹל, as 2 Chr 36:22; בְּנֵי הַגּוֹלָה; לְהִקָּבֵץ, a nifal infinitive.
- **"all his substance should be forfeited, and himself separated from the congregation of those that had been carried away" → "all his goods should be devoted, and he himself should be separated from the assembly of the exile"** (v8) — Hebrew יָחֳרַם, hofal of חָרַם: given over to Yahweh beyond recall (nothing is said of destroying it: Lev 27:28); רְכוּשׁ "goods"; יִבָּדֵל "be separated" (the same root as "separate yourselves", v11). **"within three days" → "within the three days"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew לִשְׁלֹשֶׁת הַיָּמִים, with the article.
- **"gathered themselves together … sat in the street of the house of God, trembling because of this matter, and for the great rain" → "were gathered … sat in the square of the house of Elohim, trembling because of the matter and because of the rains"** (v9) — Hebrew רְחוֹב "square", the broad open place; "this" is not written; מַרְעִידִים, of רָעַד "tremble, shake" (another word than חָרֵד, v3; English has one); גְּשָׁמִים "rains", plural (also v13). "Day" is not written: "on the twentieth of the month".
- **"Ye have transgressed" → "You have acted unfaithfully"** (v10) — Hebrew מְעַלְתֶּם. **"to increase the trespass of Israel" → "to add to the guilt of Israel"** — Hebrew לְהוֹסִיף עַל־אַשְׁמַת.
- **"make confession unto the LORD God of your fathers, and do his pleasure" → "give thanksgiving to Yahweh the Elohim of your fathers, and do his will"** (v11) — Hebrew תְּנוּ תוֹדָה, literally "give thanksgiving" (as Josh 7:19: the acknowledgment that gives glory, here the confession; the word is the noun of הוֹדָה "give thanks, confess"); רְצוֹנוֹ "his will, what pleases him". הִבָּדְלוּ "separate yourselves".
- **"As thou hast said, so must we do" → "So! According to your word it is upon us to do"** (v12) — Hebrew כֵּן כִּדְבָרְךָ עָלֵינוּ לַעֲשׂוֹת; the written text (ketiv) has כדבריך "your words", the reading tradition (qere) כִּדְבָרְךָ "your word"; the qere is translated. קוֹל גָּדוֹל, literally "a great voice".
- **"But the people are many, and it is a time of much rain, and we are not able to stand without, neither is this a work of one day or two: for we are many that have transgressed in this thing" → "But the people are many, and it is a time of rains, and there is no power to stand outside, and the work is not for one day and not for two, for we have transgressed much in this matter"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲבָל "but"; הָעֵת גְּשָׁמִים, literally "the time is rains"; אֵין כֹּחַ "there is no power"; מְלָאכָה "work"; הִרְבִּינוּ לִפְשֹׁעַ, literally "we have made much to transgress": the transgression is great, not "we are many".
- **"Let now our rulers of all the congregation stand" → "Let our princes, please, stand for all the assembly"** (v14) — Hebrew יַעַמְדוּ־נָא שָׂרֵינוּ לְכָל־הַקָּהָל: נָא "please"; לְ "for, on behalf of". **"the elders of every city" → "the elders of each city"** — Hebrew זִקְנֵי־עִיר וָעִיר, literally "of city and city". **"until the fierce wrath of our God for this matter be turned from us" → "until the burning of the anger of our Elohim is turned back from us, concerning this matter"** — Hebrew עַד לְהָשִׁיב חֲרוֹן אַף, an infinitive, "until turning back the burning of the anger" (no subject named); עַד לַדָּבָר הַזֶּה, literally "until for this matter", is difficult: "as long as this matter lasts", or "on account of this matter". Uncertain.
- **"were employed about this matter … helped them" → "stood over this … helped them"** (v15) — Hebrew עָמְדוּ עַל־זֹאת, literally "stood upon this": either "stood against this" (opposed the plan), or "stood over this" (took charge of it, as KJV "were employed about"); עֲזָרֻם "helped them" fits either. The Hebrew does not decide; "stood over" keeps the words. "Jahzeiah": KJV "Jahaziah".
- **"And Ezra the priest, with certain chief of the fathers, after the house of their fathers, and all of them by their names, were separated, and sat down … to examine the matter" → "And there were set apart Ezra the priest, men, heads of the fathers, by their fathers' house, and all of them by names; and they sat down … to search out the matter"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּבָּדְלוּ, a plural nifal "and they were set apart / set themselves apart", with the names after it; אֲנָשִׁים רָאשֵׁי הָאָבוֹת "men, heads of the fathers" side by side; לְבֵית אֲבֹתָם; בְּשֵׁמוֹת "by names". Reading "and Ezra … set apart for himself men" (Greek of 1 Esd 9:16, from memory) is a common proposal; the Masoretic Text is kept. לְדַרְיוֹשׁ, found only here, an infinitive of דָּרַשׁ "seek": "to search out".
- **"they made an end with all the men" → "they finished with all, the men who"** (v17) — Hebrew וַיְכַלּוּ בַכֹּל אֲנָשִׁים, "all" and "men" side by side.
- **"among the sons of the priests there were found that had taken strange wives: namely, of the sons of Jeshua" → "there were found of the sons of the priests who had made foreign women dwell: of the sons of Jeshua"** (v18) — the Hebrew order; וַיִּמָּצֵא, singular verb. **"and his brethren; Maaseiah"** — "his brothers" are Jeshua's.
- **"they gave their hands that they would put away their wives; and being guilty, they offered a ram of the flock for their trespass" → "they gave their hand to bring out their wives, and, being guilty, a ram of the flock for their guilt"** (v19) — Hebrew וַיִּתְּנוּ יָדָם "gave their hand" (singular: a pledge); הוֹצִיא "bring out" as v3; וַאֲשֵׁמִים, pointed as the adjective "guilty"; "offered" is not written: the clause has no verb. Pointed אֲשָׁמִים it would be "and their guilt offering, a ram of the flock". The verse is left without a verb.
- **"of the sons of Binnui; Shimei" → "and Bani and Binnui, Shimei"** (v38) — so the Hebrew, וּבָנִי וּבִנּוּי שִׁמְעִי: three names, as the KJV has them; the verse had "of the sons of Binnui", the reading of the Greek (from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Of the levites … Kelaiah, (the same is Kelita)" → "he is Kelita"** (v23) — Hebrew הוּא קְלִיטָא.
- **"of the sons of Harim" → "And the sons of Harim"** (v31) — the Hebrew has וּבְנֵי, without מִן "of" (v20–30, 33, 34, 43 have it).
- **"and Ramoth"** (v29) — the written text (ketiv) is ירמות "Jeremoth", the reading tradition (qere) וְרָמוֹת "and Ramoth"; the qere is translated (the verse had the ketiv, "Jeremoth").
- **"Chelluh" → "Cheluhu"** (v35) — the written text has כלהי "Cheluhi", the qere כְּלוּהוּ "Cheluhu"; the qere is translated.
- **"Jaasau" → "Jaasai"** (v37) — the written text has ויעשו "and Jaasu", the qere וְיַעֲשָׂי "and Jaasai"; the qere is translated.
- **"Jadau" → "Jaddai"** (v43) — the written text has ידו "Jaddu", the qere יַדַּי "Jaddai"; the qere is translated.
- **"All these had taken strange wives: and some of them had wives by whom they had children" → "All these had taken foreign women; and there were among them wives, and they had set sons"** (v44) — Hebrew נָשְׂאוּ (qere; the ketiv נשאי, a participle, "takers of", says the same); וְיֵשׁ מֵהֶם נָשִׁים וַיָּשִׂימוּ בָּנִים, word for word "and there are of them women, and they set sons": שִׂים "set, put" with "sons" is found only here and is difficult (= had brought forth sons, or had established them in the house); who "they" are, the men or the women, is not clear. The words are kept, the sense is uncertain. Reading "and they sent them away with the sons" (1 Esd 9:36, from memory) is a proposal; the Masoretic Text is kept.

### Notes
- **Wholesale divorce** — the most drastic measure in post-exilic policy. Women and children "brought out". Social cost enormous.
- **"Gave their hand"** — public pledge gesture.
- **3-month investigation** (v16-17) — tenth month to first month of next year; the cases found are listed in v18-43.
- **Priestly offenders first** — seventeen priests among the foreign-marriage group. Even Jeshua's high-priestly family.
- **113 names in all** (v18-43, as the Hebrew stands: 17 priests, 10 Levites/singers/gatekeepers, 86 of Israel) — relatively small portion of population. Focus is on leadership rather than mass reform.
- **Ezra ends abruptly** (v44) — no closure on what happened to the wives and children. Nehemiah continues the story (Neh 13 has parallel mixed-marriage crisis decades later).
- **Moral tension** — Deut 23:3-6 prohibits Ammonite/Moabite citizenship; Deut 7:3 prohibits intermarriage with Canaanites. But what about widows, children? Chronicler-school resolves through separation.
- **Chapter completes Ezra-Nehemiah's first half** (Ezra = first half). Nehemiah continues the same period's story from a different angle.
