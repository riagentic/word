# Ezra 10

[1] While Ezra was praying and confessing, weeping and throwing himself down before the house of *Elohim*, a very great assembly of men, women, and children gathered to him out of Israel, for the people wept bitterly.

[2] Shecaniah son of Jehiel, of the sons of Elam, answered and said to Ezra, "We have acted unfaithfully against our *Elohim* and have married foreign women from the peoples of the land; yet now there is hope for Israel in this matter.

[3] Let us now make a covenant with our *Elohim* to put away all these wives and those born of them, according to the counsel of my lord and of those who tremble at the commandment of our *Elohim*; let it be done according to the Law.

[4] Arise, for the matter is yours, and we are with you. Be courageous, and act."

[5] Then Ezra arose and made the leading priests, the Levites, and all Israel swear to act according to this word. So they swore.

[6] Ezra withdrew from before the house of *Elohim* and went to the chamber of Jehohanan son of Eliashib. When he came there, he ate no bread and drank no water, for he was mourning because of the unfaithfulness of those who had returned from exile.

[7] They made proclamation throughout Judah and Jerusalem to all the children of the captivity that they should gather at Jerusalem;

[8] and whoever did not come within three days, according to the counsel of the officials and the elders, all his property should be forfeited, and he himself would be excluded from the assembly of those who had returned from exile.

[9] All the men of Judah and Benjamin gathered at Jerusalem within three days. It was the ninth month, on the twentieth day of the month; all the people sat in the open square before the house of *Elohim*, trembling because of this matter and because of the heavy rain.

[10] Ezra the priest stood up and said to them, "You have acted unfaithfully; you have married foreign women, adding to Israel's guilt.

[11] Now therefore make confession to Yahweh the *Elohim* of your fathers and do his pleasure: separate yourselves from the peoples of the land and from the foreign women."

[12] Then all the assembly answered with a loud voice, "As you have said, so must we do.

[13] But the people are many, and it is a time of heavy rain; we are not able to stand outside, nor is this a matter for one day or two, for we have greatly transgressed in this matter.

[14] Let our officials stand for all the assembly; all those in our cities who have married foreign women shall come at appointed times, and with them the elders of each city and its judges, until the fierce wrath of our *Elohim* over this matter is turned away from us."

[15] Only Jonathan son of Asahel and Jahzeiah son of Tikvah opposed this — with Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite supporting them.

[16] The children of the captivity did so. Ezra the priest selected men, heads of fathers' houses, all designated by name; they convened on the first day of the tenth month to examine the matter.

[17] By the first day of the first month they had dealt with all the men who had married foreign women.

[18] Among the sons of the priests who had married foreign women, there were found: of the sons of Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers — Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah;

[19] they gave their hand to send away their wives; and being guilty, they offered a ram of the flock for their guilt offering.

[20] Of the sons of Immer: Hanani and Zebadiah.

[21] Of the sons of Harim: Maaseiah, Elijah, Shemaiah, Jehiel, and Uzziah.

[22] Of the sons of Pashhur: Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethanel, Jozabad, and Elasah.

[23] Of the Levites: Jozabad, Shimei, Kelaiah (that is, Kelita), Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer.

[24] Of the singers: Eliashib. Of the gatekeepers: Shallum, Telem, and Uri.

[25] Of Israel: of the sons of Parosh — Ramiah, Izziah, Malchijah, Mijamin, Eleazar, Malchijah, and Benaiah;

[26] of the sons of Elam — Mattaniah, Zechariah, Jehiel, Abdi, Jeremoth, and Elijah;

[27] of the sons of Zattu — Elioenai, Eliashib, Mattaniah, Jeremoth, Zabad, and Aziza;

[28] of the sons of Bebai — Jehohanan, Hananiah, Zabbai, and Athlai;

[29] of the sons of Bani — Meshullam, Malluch, Adaiah, Jashub, Sheal, and Jeremoth;

[30] of the sons of Pahath-moab — Adna, Chelal, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattaniah, Bezalel, Binnui, and Manasseh;

[31] of the sons of Harim — Eliezer, Isshijah, Malchijah, Shemaiah, Shimeon,

[32] Benjamin, Malluch, and Shemariah;

[33] of the sons of Hashum — Mattenai, Mattattah, Zabad, Eliphelet, Jeremai, Manasseh, and Shimei;

[35] Benaiah, Bedeiah, Cheluhi,

[36] Vaniah, Meremoth, Eliashib,

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"strange wives" → "foreign women"** (throughout) — *nashim nakhriyyot*.
- **"trespass" → "unfaithfulness / guilt"** (v2, 6, 10).
- **"porters" → "gatekeepers"** (v24).
- **"employed about this matter" → "opposed this"** (v15) — Hebrew *amad al*; commonly read "stood against" — i.e., opposed. Reading debated: KJV "were employed" = "took charge"; most moderns "opposed." Opposition reading makes better contextual sense (explaining the need for Meshullam and Shabbethai's support).

### Notes
- **Wholesale divorce** — the most drastic measure in post-exilic policy. Women and children "put away." Social cost enormous.
- **"Gave their hand"** — public pledge gesture.
- **3-month investigation** (v16-17) — tenth month to first month of next year; 108 known cases documented.
- **Priestly offenders first** — seventeen priests among the foreign-marriage group. Even Jeshua's high-priestly family.
- **113 individuals total** — relatively small portion of population. Focus is on leadership rather than mass reform.
- **Ezra ends abruptly** (v44) — no closure on what happened to divorced wives and children. Nehemiah continues the story (Neh 13 has parallel mixed-marriage crisis decades later).
- **Moral tension** — Deut 23:3-6 prohibits Ammonite/Moabite citizenship; Deut 7:3 prohibits intermarriage with Canaanites. But what about widows, children? Chronicler-school resolves through separation.
- **Chapter completes Ezra-Nehemiah's first half** (Ezra = first half). Nehemiah continues the same period's story from a different angle.
