# Ezra 6

[1] Then King ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ issued a decree, and a search was made in the archives where the treasures were stored in ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧.

[2] There was found in ⟦ecbatana|Achmeta|Ecbatana⟧, in the fortress that is in the province of ⟦media|Madai|Media⟧, a scroll, on which was written this record:

[3] "In the first year of King ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧, King ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧ issued a decree concerning the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧: 'Let the house be rebuilt, the place where sacrifices are offered, and let its foundations be retained; its height shall be sixty cubits and its breadth sixty cubits,

[4] with three courses of great stones and one course of new timber. The expenses shall be paid from the king's house.

[5] Also, the gold and silver vessels of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that ⟦nebuchadnezzar|Nevuchadnetzar|Nebuchadnezzar⟧ took out of the temple in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ and brought to ⟦babylon|Bavel|Babylon⟧ shall be restored and brought back to their places in the temple that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧; they shall be deposited in the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.'

[6] Now therefore, Tattenai, governor of the province Beyond the River, ⟦shethar-bozenai|Shetar-Bozenai|Shethar-bozenai⟧, and your associates the ⟦persians|Parsi|Persian⟧ commissioners who are in the province Beyond the River, stay away from there.

[7] Leave the work on this house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ alone; let the governor of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and the elders of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ rebuild this house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ on its site.

[8] Moreover I make a decree regarding what you shall do for these elders of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ for the rebuilding of this house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: from the royal revenue of the tribute of the province Beyond the River, the cost shall be paid to these men promptly, so that they may not be hindered.

[9] Whatever they need — young bulls, rams, or lambs for burnt offerings to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of heaven; wheat, salt, wine, or oil as the priests in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ direct — shall be given to them day by day without fail,

[10] that they may offer pleasing sacrifices to the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of heaven, and pray for the life of the king and his sons.

[11] I also issue a decree: if anyone alters this edict, let a beam be pulled out of his house, and he shall be impaled upon it; his house shall be made a dunghill for this.

[12] May the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who has caused his name to dwell there overthrow any king or people who shall put forth a hand to alter this or to destroy this house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ that is in ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧. I, ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧, have made a decree; let it be done with all diligence."

[13] Then Tattenai, governor of the province Beyond the River, ⟦shethar-bozenai|Shetar-Bozenai|Shethar-bozenai⟧, and their associates did with all diligence what King ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ had sent.

[14] The elders of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ built and prospered through the prophesying of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet and ⟦zechariah|Zecharyah|Zechariah⟧ son of Iddo. They finished the building by the command of the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and by the decree of ⟦cyrus|Koresh|Cyrus⟧, ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧, and ⟦artaxerxes|Artachshasta|Artaxerxes⟧ king of ⟦persia|Paras|Persia⟧.

[15] This house was finished on the third day of the month Adar, in the sixth year of the reign of King ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧.

[16] The children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — the priests, the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, and the rest of the children of the captivity — celebrated the dedication of this house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with joy.

[17] They offered at the dedication of this house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ one hundred bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs; and for a sin offering for all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, twelve male goats, according to the number of the tribes of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[18] They set the priests in their divisions and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ in their classes for the service of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ at ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, as it is written in the Book of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[19] The children of the captivity kept the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ on the fourteenth day of the first month.

[20] For the priests and the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧ had purified themselves together; all of them were pure. They slaughtered the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ lamb for all the children of the captivity, for their brothers the priests, and for themselves.

[21] The children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ who had returned from exile, with all who had separated themselves from the uncleanness of the nations of the land to join them to seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, ate it.

[22] They kept the Feast of ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ seven days with joy, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had made them joyful and had turned the heart of the king of ⟦assyria|Ashur|Assyria⟧ toward them, to strengthen their hands in the work of the house of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Achmetha" → "Ecbatana"** (v2) — Median summer capital.
- **"palace" → "fortress"** (v2) — *birta*; Persian stronghold.
- **"house of the rolls" → "archives"** (v1).
- **"hanged thereon" → "impaled upon it"** (v11) — *zeqif*; Persian-style execution.
- **"sweet savours" → "pleasing sacrifices"** (v10) — *nichochin*.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v21).
- **"king of Assyria" → retained** (v22) — here used anachronistically for the Persian king (Darius) ruling the former Assyrian territory. Or possibly reflects a continuing common regional title.

### Notes
- **Temple completed 516 BC** — Adar (Feb/Mar) of Darius's sixth year. Roughly 70 years after destruction (586), fulfilling Jeremiah's prophecy.
- **Cyrus decree recovered** — archival victory. Ecbatana was summer palace; document stored from Cyrus's time there.
- **Persian state funding** — remarkable: imperial treasury funds temple reconstruction, daily sacrifices, priestly rations. Expected in return: prayer for king.
- **Twelve goats** (v17) — for all twelve tribes, not just Judah. Pan-Israelite theology: exile includes remnants of all tribes.
- **"Joined the nations of the land" converts** (v21) — non-exiles who embrace Yahwist worship welcomed into community.
- **"King of Assyria"** — possibly ironic: the Mesopotamian-Persian state blurred in popular memory. Or reflects continuity of imperial oppression turned blessing.
- **Passover and Feast of Unleavened Bread** — first cycle of proper post-exilic worship. Restoration complete.
