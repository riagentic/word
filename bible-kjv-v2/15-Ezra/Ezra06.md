# Ezra 6

[1] Then King Darius issued a decree, and a search was made in the archives where the treasures were stored in Babylon.

[2] There was found in Ecbatana, in the fortress that is in the province of Media, a scroll, on which was written this record:

[3] "In the first year of King Cyrus, King Cyrus issued a decree concerning the house of *Elohim* in Jerusalem: 'Let the house be rebuilt, the place where sacrifices are offered, and let its foundations be retained; its height shall be sixty cubits and its breadth sixty cubits,

[4] with three courses of great stones and one course of new timber. The expenses shall be paid from the king's house.

[5] Also, the gold and silver vessels of the house of *Elohim* that Nebuchadnezzar took out of the temple in Jerusalem and brought to Babylon shall be restored and brought back to their places in the temple that is in Jerusalem; they shall be deposited in the house of *Elohim*.'

[6] Now therefore, Tattenai, governor of the province Beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and your associates the Persian commissioners who are in the province Beyond the River, stay away from there.

[7] Leave the work on this house of *Elohim* alone; let the governor of the Jews and the elders of the Jews rebuild this house of *Elohim* on its site.

[8] Moreover I make a decree regarding what you shall do for these elders of the Jews for the rebuilding of this house of *Elohim*: from the royal revenue of the tribute of the province Beyond the River, the cost shall be paid to these men promptly, so that they may not be hindered.

[9] Whatever they need — young bulls, rams, or lambs for burnt offerings to the *Elohim* of heaven; wheat, salt, wine, or oil as the priests in Jerusalem direct — shall be given to them day by day without fail,

[10] that they may offer pleasing sacrifices to the *Elohim* of heaven, and pray for the life of the king and his sons.

[11] I also issue a decree: if anyone alters this edict, let a beam be pulled out of his house, and he shall be impaled upon it; his house shall be made a dunghill for this.

[12] May the *Elohim* who has caused his name to dwell there overthrow any king or people who shall put forth a hand to alter this or to destroy this house of *Elohim* that is in Jerusalem. I, Darius, have made a decree; let it be done with all diligence."

[13] Then Tattenai, governor of the province Beyond the River, Shethar-bozenai, and their associates did with all diligence what King Darius had sent.

[14] The elders of the Jews built and prospered through the prophesying of Haggai the prophet and Zechariah son of Iddo. They finished the building by the command of the *Elohim* of Israel and by the decree of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes king of Persia.

[15] This house was finished on the third day of the month Adar, in the sixth year of the reign of King Darius.

[16] The children of Israel — the priests, the Levites, and the rest of the children of the captivity — celebrated the dedication of this house of *Elohim* with joy.

[17] They offered at the dedication of this house of *Elohim* one hundred bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs; and for a sin offering for all Israel, twelve male goats, according to the number of the tribes of Israel.

[18] They set the priests in their divisions and the Levites in their classes for the service of *Elohim* at Jerusalem, as it is written in the Book of Moses.

[19] The children of the captivity kept the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

[20] For the priests and the Levites had purified themselves together; all of them were pure. They slaughtered the Passover lamb for all the children of the captivity, for their brothers the priests, and for themselves.

[21] The children of Israel who had returned from exile, with all who had separated themselves from the uncleanness of the nations of the land to join them to seek Yahweh the *Elohim* of Israel, ate it.

[22] They kept the Feast of Unleavened Bread seven days with joy, for Yahweh had made them joyful and had turned the heart of the king of Assyria toward them, to strengthen their hands in the work of the house of *Elohim*, the *Elohim* of Israel.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Achmetha" → "Ecbatana"** (v2) — Median summer capital.
- **"palace" → "fortress"** (v2) — *birta*; Persian stronghold.
- **"house of the rolls" → "archives"** (v1).
- **"hanged thereon" → "impaled upon it"** (v11) — *zeqif*; Persian-style execution.
- **"sweet savours" → "pleasing sacrifices"** (v10) — *nichochin*.
- **"heathen" → "nations"** (v21).
- **"king of Assyria" → retained** (v22) — here used anachronistically for the Persian king (Darius) ruling the former Assyrian territory. Or possibly reflects a continuing common regional title.

### Notes
- **Temple completed 516 BC** — Adar (Feb/Mar) of Darius's sixth year. Roughly 70 years after destruction (586), fulfilling Jeremiah's prophecy.
- **Cyrus decree recovered** — archival victory. Ecbatana was summer palace; document stored from Cyrus's time there.
- **Persian state funding** — remarkable: imperial treasury funds temple reconstruction, daily sacrifices, priestly rations. Expected in return: prayer for king.
- **Twelve goats** (v17) — for all twelve tribes, not just Judah. Pan-Israelite theology: exile includes remnants of all tribes.
- **"Joined the nations of the land" converts** (v21) — non-exiles who embrace Yahwist worship welcomed into community.
- **"King of Assyria"** — possibly ironic: the Mesopotamian-Persian state blurred in popular memory. Or reflects continuity of imperial oppression turned blessing.
- **Passover and Feast of Unleavened Bread** — first cycle of proper post-exilic worship. Restoration complete.
