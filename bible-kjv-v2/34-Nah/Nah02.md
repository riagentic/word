# Nahum 2

[1] The scatterer has come up against you.
Man the ramparts;
watch the road;
dress for battle;
collect all your strength.

[2] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is restoring the majesty of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
as the majesty of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
for plunderers have plundered them
and ruined their branches.

[3] The shield of his mighty men is red;
his soldiers are clothed in scarlet.
The chariots come with flashing metal
on the day he musters them;
the cypress spears are brandished.

[4] The chariots race madly through the streets;
they rush to and fro through the squares;
they gleam like torches;
they dart like lightning.

[5] He remembers his officers;
they stumble as they go,
they hasten to the wall;
the siege tower is set up.

[6] The river gates are opened;
the palace melts away;

[7] its mistress is stripped;
she is carried off,
her slave girls lamenting,
moaning like doves
and beating their breasts.

[8] ⟦nineveh|Ninveh|Nineveh⟧ is like a pool
whose waters run away.
"Halt! Halt!" they cry,
but none turns back.

[9] Plunder the silver, plunder the gold!
There is no end of the treasure
or of the wealth of all precious things.

[10] Desolate! Desolation and ruin!
Hearts melt and knees tremble;
anguish is in all loins;
all faces grow pale!

[11] Where is the lions' den,
the feeding place of the young lions,
where the lion and lioness went,
where his cubs were, with none to disturb?

[12] The lion tore enough for his cubs
and strangled prey for his lionesses;
he filled his caves with prey
and his dens with torn flesh.

[13] Behold, I am against you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
and I will burn your chariots in smoke,
and the sword shall devour your young lions.
I will cut off your prey from the earth,
and the voice of your messengers shall no longer be heard.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת; *Tzeva'ot* retained.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the entire chapter is vivid battle poetry describing Nineveh's fall.
- **"He that dasheth in pieces is come up before thy face" → "The scatterer has come up against you"** (v1) — Hebrew עָלָה הַמֵּפִיץ *alah hamepits* = the scatterer/one who dashes to pieces; "scatterer" for *mepits*.
- **"keep the munition" → "Man the ramparts"** (v1) — Hebrew נַצֹּר מִבְצָר *natzor mivtzar*; "man the ramparts" more vivid.
- **"make thy loins strong, fortify thy power mightily" → "dress for battle; collect all your strength"** (v1) — modernized.
- **"For the LORD hath turned away the excellency of Jacob … the emptiers have emptied them out, and marred their vine branches" → "For Yahweh is restoring the majesty of Jacob … for plunderers have plundered them and ruined their branches"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁב יְהוָה אֶת גְּאוֹן יַעֲקֹב *shav Yahweh et ge'on Ya'aqov*; *shav* = returning/restoring (not "turned away"); Yahweh avenges what plunderers did to Israel.
- **"the chariots shall be with flaming torches in the day of his preparation" → "The chariots come with flashing metal on the day he musters them"** (v3) — Hebrew פְּלָדוֹת הָאֵשׁ *peladot ha'esh* = flames of steel/flashing metal; "flashing metal" more vivid.
- **"the fir trees shall be terribly shaken" → "the cypress spears are brandished"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּרֹשִׁים *bereshim* = cypress trees (also cypress-wood spears); "cypress spears" captures the dual meaning.
- **"they shall justle one against another in the broad ways" → "they rush to and fro through the squares"** (v4) — "rush to and fro" more vivid.
- **"He shall recount his worthies" → "He remembers his officers"** (v5) — Hebrew יִזְכֹּר אַדִּירָיו *yizkor addirav*; "officers" for *addirim* = nobles/officers.
- **"the defence shall be prepared" → "the siege tower is set up"** (v5) — Hebrew הַסֹּכֵךְ *hassokhekhah* = the covering/siege device; "siege tower" more concrete.
- **"The gates of the rivers shall be opened, and the palace shall be dissolved" → "The river gates are opened; the palace melts away"** (v6) — historically, Nineveh was flooded when the Tigris broke through; "melts away" for *nimog*.
- **"And Huzzab shall be led away captive, she shall be brought up, and her maids shall lead her as with the voice of doves, tabering upon their breasts" → "its mistress is stripped; she is carried off, her slave girls lamenting, moaning like doves and beating their breasts"** (v7) — Hebrew הֻצַּב *hutztzav* = she is decreed/stripped; uncertain name; "its mistress" = city personification; "beating their breasts" for *metofefot*.
- **"Nineveh is of old like a pool of water: yet they shall flee away. Stand, stand, shall they cry; but none shall look back" → "Nineveh is like a pool whose waters run away. 'Halt! Halt!' they cry, but none turns back"** (v8) — modernized; "Halt!" for *imdu* = stand/halt.
- **"the store and glory out of all the pleasant furniture" → "the wealth of all precious things"** (v9) — Hebrew כְּלִי חֶמְדָּה *keli chemdah* = desirable vessels/precious things.
- **"She is empty, and void, and waste" → "Desolate! Desolation and ruin!"** (v10) — Hebrew בּוּקָה וּמְבוּקָה וּמְבֻלָּקָה *buqah umvuqah umvullaqqah* = triple sound-play; exclamatory form preserves the rhetorical force.
- **"the faces of them all gather blackness" → "all faces grow pale"** (v10) — consistent with Joel 2:6; Hebrew פָּארוּר = pallor.
- **"the lion's whelp" → "his cubs"** (v11) — Hebrew גּוּר *gur* = cub; "cubs" more natural.
- **"filled his holes with prey, and his dens with ravin" → "he filled his caves with prey and his dens with torn flesh"** (v12) — Hebrew טֶרֶף *tereph* = torn prey/torn flesh; "torn flesh" more vivid.
- **"saith the LORD of hosts" → "declares Yahweh of Hosts"** (v13) — *Tzeva'ot* retained; "declares" for *ne'um*.
- **"the voice of thy messengers shall no more be heard" → "the voice of your messengers shall no longer be heard"** (v13) — Hebrew מַלְאָכֶיךָ *mal'akhekha* = your messengers; modernized.
