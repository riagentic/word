# Nahum 2

[1] One who scatters has come up against your face.
Guard the rampart;
watch the way;
make the loins strong;
strengthen power greatly.

[2] For ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has restored the exaltation of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧
as the exaltation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
for emptiers have emptied them
and ruined their vine-branches.

[3] The shield of his mighty ones is made red;
the men of ability are clad in scarlet;
with the fire of steel are the chariots on the day of his making ready,
and the cypresses are made to quiver.

[4] In the streets the chariots rage;
they rush to and fro in the squares;
their appearance is like torches;
like lightnings they run.

[5] He remembers his nobles;
they stumble in their going;
they hasten to her wall,
and the screen is made ready.

[6] The gates of the rivers have been opened,
and the palace has melted away.

[7] And it is fixed: she is uncovered, she is carried up;
and her slave women moan as with the voice of doves,
drumming upon their hearts.

[8] And ⟦nineveh|Ninveh|Nineveh⟧ is like a pool of water from her days,
and they are fleeing.
Stand! Stand!
And there is none who turns.

[9] Plunder silver! Plunder gold!
And there is no end to the store,
glory out of all the desirable vessels.

[10] Empty, and emptied, and devastated!
And the heart melts, and the knees totter,
and anguish is in all loins,
and the faces of all of them have gathered pallor.

[11] Where is the abode of the lions,
and the pasture that was for the young lions,
where the lion walked, the lioness,
there the lion's cub, and none made them tremble?

[12] The lion tearing to pieces enough for his cubs,
and strangling for his lionesses;
and he filled his holes with prey,
and his abodes with torn things.

[13] Behold, I am against you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,
and I will burn her chariots in smoke,
and the sword shall consume your young lions;
and I will cut off your prey from the earth,
and the voice of your messengers shall be heard no more.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת, the prophets' formula.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the quotation marks of v8 are dropped (the Hebrew has none).
- **"He that dasheth in pieces is come up before thy face" → "One who scatters has come up against your face"** (v1) — Hebrew עָלָה מֵפִיץ, a perfect; מֵפִיץ, hifil participle of פּוּץ "scatter", without the article; עַל־פָּנַיִךְ "against your face" ("you" feminine: Nineveh). **"keep the munition, watch the way, make thy loins strong, fortify thy power mightily" → "Guard the rampart; watch the way; make the loins strong; strengthen power greatly"** — נָצוֹר, an infinitive used as a command (נָצַר "guard"); מְצֻרָה "rampart" (from מָצוֹר "siege"); חַזֵּק מָתְנַיִם, no "your"; אַמֵּץ כֹּחַ מְאֹד, אִמֵּץ "strengthen", מְאֹד "greatly". The former "Man the ramparts", "dress for battle", "collect all your strength" were paraphrase.
- **"For the LORD hath turned away the excellency of Jacob, as the excellency of Israel" → "For Yahweh has restored the exaltation of Jacob as the exaltation of Israel"** (v2) — Hebrew שָׁב, qal of שׁוּב with an object, "turn back" = restore (KJV "turned away" takes it the other way); a perfect. גְּאוֹן "exaltation" (as Ps 47:4 "the exaltation of Jacob"). **"for the emptiers have emptied them out, and marred their vine branches" → "for emptiers have emptied them and ruined their vine-branches"** — בְקָקוּם בֹּקְקִים, בָּקַק "empty"; זְמֹרֵיהֶם "their vine-branches"; שִׁחֵתוּ, שָׁחַת "ruin".
- **"The shield of his mighty men is made red, the valiant men are in scarlet" → "The shield of his mighty ones is made red; the men of ability are clad in scarlet"** (v3) — Hebrew גִּבֹּרֵיהוּ "his mighty ones"; מְאָדָּם, pual, "made red"; אַנְשֵׁי־חַיִל "men of ability" (חַיִל, of an army "force"); מְתֻלָּעִים, pual of תּוֹלָע "scarlet". **"the chariots shall be with flaming torches in the day of his preparation" → "with the fire of steel are the chariots on the day of his making ready"** — בְּאֵשׁ־פְּלָדוֹת: פְּלָדָה is found only here, "steel" (the flashing metal of the chariots) is the likeliest sense; KJV "torches" reads the letters as לַפִּידוֹת; uncertain. הֲכִינוֹ "his making ready" (כּוּן). **"and the fir trees shall be terribly shaken" → "and the cypresses are made to quiver"** — וְהַבְּרֹשִׁים הָרְעָלוּ: בְּרוֹשׁ "cypress", here most likely spears of cypress wood (the Septuagint, from memory, reads "the horsemen"); רָעַל hofal "be made to quiver".
- **"The chariots shall rage in the streets, they shall justle one against another in the broad ways: they shall seem like torches, they shall run like the lightnings" → "In the streets the chariots rage; they rush to and fro in the squares; their appearance is like torches; like lightnings they run"** (v4) — Hebrew order; חוּצוֹת "streets", רְחֹבוֹת "squares" (two words); מַרְאֵיהֶן "their appearance"; יְרוֹצֵצוּ, from רוּץ "run" (to and fro).
- **"He shall recount his worthies: they shall stumble in their walk; they shall make haste to the wall thereof, and the defence shall be prepared" → "He remembers his nobles; they stumble in their going; they hasten to her wall, and the screen is made ready"** (v5) — Hebrew יִזְכֹּר "remembers"; אַדִּירָיו "his nobles" (אַדִּיר "mighty", of men "nobles"); the qere בַּהֲלִיכָתָם "in their going" (written בהלכותם, plural, the same sense); חוֹמָתָהּ "her wall"; הַסֹּכֵךְ, from סָכַךְ "screen": the shelter of the attackers (or of the defenders; uncertain); וְהֻכַן "is made ready" (the root of הֲכִינוֹ, v3).
- **"The gates of the rivers shall be opened, and the palace shall be dissolved" → "The gates of the rivers have been opened, and the palace has melted away"** (v6) — Hebrew perfects; הֵיכָל "palace" (of a king; elsewhere "temple"); נָמוֹג, מוּג "melt away". The former note on the Tigris was history, not text.
- **"And Huzzab shall be led away captive, she shall be brought up, and her maids shall lead her as with the voice of doves, tabering upon their breasts" → "And it is fixed: she is uncovered, she is carried up; and her slave women moan as with the voice of doves, drumming upon their hearts"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהֻצַּב, hofal of נצב "it is set, fixed" (KJV takes it as a name, Huzzab; uncertain); גֻּלְּתָה, pual of גָּלָה "she is uncovered" (or "exiled"); הֹעֲלָתָה "she is made to go up" (carried off); אַמְהֹתֶיהָ "her slave women"; מְנַהֲגוֹת "moaning" (the same letters elsewhere "drive, lead", KJV "lead her"; uncertain); מְתֹפְפֹת, from תֹּף "timbrel", "drumming"; עַל־לִבְבֵהֶן "upon their hearts" (לֵבָב "heart"; "breasts" was interpretation).
- **"But Nineveh is of old like a pool of water: yet they shall flee away. Stand, stand, shall they cry; but none shall look back" → "And Nineveh is like a pool of water from her days, and they are fleeing. Stand! Stand! And there is none who turns"** (v8) — Hebrew מִימֵי הִיא, literally "from the days (that) she (is)" = from of old (the vowels also allow "her waters"; uncertain); וְהֵמָּה נָסִים, a participle; "they cry" is not in the Hebrew; מַפְנֶה "one who turns".
- **"Take ye the spoil of silver, take the spoil of gold: for there is none end of the store and glory out of all the pleasant furniture" → "Plunder silver! Plunder gold! And there is no end to the store, glory out of all the desirable vessels"** (v9) — Hebrew בֹּזּוּ, בָּזַז "plunder"; תְּכוּנָה "store" (what is laid up); כָּבֹד "glory" (literally "weight": wealth); כְּלִי חֶמְדָּה "vessels of desire".
- **"She is empty, and void, and waste" → "Empty, and emptied, and devastated!"** (v10) — Hebrew בּוּקָה וּמְבוּקָה וּמְבֻלָּקָה, a play of sounds (*buqah umevuqah umevullaqah*), from בָּקַק "empty" and בָּלַק "devastate"; the "and" kept. **"and the heart melteth, and the knees smite together, and much pain is in all loins" → "And the heart melts, and the knees totter, and anguish is in all loins"** — לֵב singular; נָמֵס "melt"; פִּק "tottering"; חַלְחָלָה "anguish" (root חִיל "writhe"). **"and the faces of them all gather blackness" → "and the faces of all of them have gathered pallor"** — קִבְּצוּ פָארוּר, as Joel 2:6: פָארוּר is uncertain ("pallor", or "a glow"; KJV "blackness" from פָּרוּר "pot").
- **"Where is the dwelling of the lions, and the feedingplace of the young lions, where the lion, even the old lion, walked, and the lion's whelp, and none made them afraid?" → "Where is the abode of the lions, and the pasture that was for the young lions, where the lion walked, the lioness, there the lion's cub, and none made them tremble?"** (v11) — Hebrew מְעוֹן "abode"; וּמִרְעֶה הוּא לַכְּפִרִים "and the pasture, it (was) for the young lions"; לָבִיא "lioness" (gender not certain); שָׁם "there"; מַחֲרִיד, hifil of חָרַד "make tremble".
- **"The lion did tear in pieces enough for his whelps, and strangled for his lionesses, and filled his holes with prey, and his dens with ravin" → "The lion tearing to pieces enough for his cubs, and strangling for his lionesses; and he filled his holes with prey, and his abodes with torn things"** (v12) — Hebrew טֹרֵף, מְחַנֵּק participles, then וַיְמַלֵּא, a past form; בְּדֵי "enough for"; גֹּרוֹת "cubs"; מְעֹנֹתָיו "his abodes" (as מְעוֹן v11); טְרֵפָה "a thing torn".
- **"Behold, I am against thee, saith the LORD of hosts, and I will burn her chariots in the smoke" → "Behold, I am against you, declares Yahweh of Hosts, and I will burn her chariots in smoke"** (v13) — Hebrew הִנְנִי אֵלַיִךְ "behold me against you"; נְאֻם "declares"; רִכְבָּהּ "her chariots" (third person in the middle of the address, as printed). **"and the sword shall devour thy young lions" → "and the sword shall consume your young lions"** — אָכַל "consume". **"and I will cut off thy prey from the earth, and the voice of thy messengers shall no more be heard"** — מַלְאָכֵכֵה, an unusual spelling of "your messengers" (מַלְאָךְ "messenger").

### Notes

- **Numbering** — our 2:1–13 are Hebrew 2:2–14 (our 1:15 is Hebrew 2:1).
