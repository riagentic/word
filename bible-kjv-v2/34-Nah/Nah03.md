# Nahum 3

[1] Woe to the bloody city,
all full of lies and plunder —
no end to the prey!

[2] The crack of the whip, and rumble of the wheel,
galloping horse and bounding chariot!

[3] Horsemen charging,
flashing sword and glittering spear,
hosts of slain,
heaps of corpses,
dead bodies without end —
they stumble over the bodies!

[4] And all for the countless whorings of the prostitute,
graceful and of deadly charms,
who betrays nations with her whorings,
and peoples with her charms.

[5] Behold, I am against you, declares Yahweh of Hosts,
and will lift up your skirts over your face;
and I will make nations look at your nakedness
and kingdoms at your shame.

[6] I will throw filth at you
and treat you with contempt
and make you a spectacle.

[7] And all who look at you will shrink from you and say,
"Wasted is Nineveh; who will grieve for her?"
Where shall I seek comforters for you?

[8] Are you better than Thebes
that sat by the Nile,
with water around her,
her rampart a sea,
and water her wall?

[9] Cush was her strength; Egypt too, and that without limit;
Put and the Libyans were her helpers.

[10] Yet she became an exile;
she went into captivity;
her infants were dashed in pieces
at the head of every street;
for her honored men lots were cast,
and all her great men were bound in chains.

[11] You also will be drunken;
you will go into hiding;
you will seek a refuge from the enemy.

[12] All your fortresses are like fig trees
with first-ripe figs —
if shaken they fall
into the mouth of the eater.

[13] Behold, your troops
are women in your midst.
The gates of your land are wide open to your enemies;
fire has devoured your bars.

[14] Draw water for the siege;
strengthen your forts;
go into the clay;
tread the mortar;
take hold of the brick mold!

[15] There will the fire devour you;
the sword will cut you off.
It will devour you like the locust.
Multiply yourselves like the locust;
multiply like the grasshopper!

[16] You increased your merchants more than the stars of the heavens.
The locust spreads its wings and flies away.

[17] Your princes are like grasshoppers,
your scribes like clouds of locusts
settling on the fences
in a day of cold —
when the sun rises, they fly away;
no one knows where they are.

[18] Your shepherds are asleep,
O king of Assyria;
your nobles slumber.
Your people are scattered on the mountains
with none to gather them.

[19] There is no easing your hurt;
your wound is grievous.
All who hear the news about you
clap their hands over you.
For upon whom has not come
your unceasing evil?

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"the LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (v5) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת; *Tzeva'ot* retained.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the entire chapter is battle poetry.
- **"it is all full of lies and robbery; the prey departeth not" → "all full of lies and plunder — no end to the prey"** (v1) — Hebrew מְלֵאָה כָחַשׁ פֶּרֶק *mele'ah khachash pereq*; "plunder" for *pereq* = torn prey/plunder; dash for poetic emphasis.
- **"The noise of a whip, and the noise of the rattling of the wheels, and of the pransing horses, and of the jumping chariots" → "The crack of the whip, and rumble of the wheel, galloping horse and bounding chariot!"** (v2) — modernized; "crack" for the whip sound; "galloping/bounding" for *doharer/meqappe'tz*; exclamatory for the staccato battle scene.
- **"The horseman lifteth up both the bright sword and the glittering spear" → "Horsemen charging, flashing sword and glittering spear"** (v3) — modernized; staccato style preserved.
- **"there is a multitude of slain, and a great number of carcases; and there is none end of their corpses; they stumble upon their corpses" → "hosts of slain, heaps of corpses, dead bodies without end — they stumble over the bodies!"** (v3) — modernized; exclamatory final phrase.
- **"Because of the multitude of the whoredoms of the wellfavoured harlot, the mistress of witchcrafts, that selleth nations through her whoredoms, and families through her witchcrafts" → "And all for the countless whorings of the prostitute, graceful and of deadly charms, who betrays nations with her whorings, and peoples with her charms"** (v4) — Hebrew הַנָּאוָה *hanna'vah* = graceful/beautiful; *keshafim* = sorceries/deadly charms; "prostitute" for *zonah* consistent; "betrays" for *makhorah* = selling/betraying; "peoples" for *mishpachot* = families/clans.
- **"saith the LORD of hosts" → "declares Yahweh of Hosts"** (v5) — consistent.
- **"I will discover thy skirts upon thy face" → "will lift up your skirts over your face"** (v5) — Hebrew גָּלִיתִי שׁוּלַיִךְ עַל פָּנַיִךְ *galliti shulayikh al panayikh*; the shaming of the prostitute city.
- **"I will cast abominable filth upon thee, and make thee vile, and will set thee as a gazingstock" → "I will throw filth at you and treat you with contempt and make you a spectacle"** (v6) — Hebrew שִׁקּוּצִים *shiqqutzim* = abominations/filth; "spectacle" for *re'eh* = a sight to see.
- **"all they that look upon thee shall flee from thee" → "all who look at you will shrink from you"** (v7) — Hebrew כָּל רֹאַיִךְ יִדּוֹד מִמֵּךְ *kol ro'ayikh yiddod mimekh* = will flee/shrink from you.
- **"Nineveh is laid waste: who will bemoan her? whence shall I seek comforters for thee?" → "Wasted is Nineveh; who will grieve for her? Where shall I seek comforters for you?"** (v7) — modernized.
- **"populous No, that was situate among the rivers" → "Thebes that sat by the Nile"** (v8) — Hebrew נֹא אָמוֹן *No Amon* = Thebes (No-Amon = "No" is the Egyptian word for city; Amon is the god); "Thebes" is the Greek/English name; "by the Nile" for *yeorim*.
- **"whose rampart was the sea, and her wall was from the sea?" → "her rampart a sea, and water her wall?"** (v8) — modernized; the rivers around Thebes like a sea.
- **"Yet was she carried away, she went into captivity" → "Yet she became an exile; she went into captivity"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"for her honourable men lots were cast" → "for her honored men lots were cast"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"Thou also shalt be drunken: thou shalt be hid, thou also shalt seek strength because of the enemy" → "You also will be drunken; you will go into hiding; you will seek a refuge from the enemy"** (v11) — modernized.
- **"All thy strong holds shall be like fig trees with the firstripe figs" → "All your fortresses are like fig trees with first-ripe figs"** (v12) — modernized.
- **"Behold, thy people in the midst of thee are women" → "Behold, your troops are women in your midst"** (v13) — Hebrew עַמֵּךְ נָשִׁים בְּקִרְבֵּךְ *ammekh nashim beqirbek*; "troops" more precise than "people."
- **"make strong the brickkiln" → "take hold of the brick mold"** (v14) — Hebrew חִזְקִי מַלְבֵּן *chizqi malben*; *malben* = brick mold; "take hold of" more vivid than "make strong."
- **"it shall eat thee up like the cankerworm: make thyself many as the cankerworm, make thyself many as the locusts" → "It will devour you like the locust. Multiply yourselves like the locust; multiply like the grasshopper"** (v15) — Hebrew יֶלֶק *yeleq* = hopping locust (same term as Joel 1:4); "grasshopper" for *arbeh*; consistent with Joel locust vocabulary.
- **"the cankerworm spoileth, and flieth away" → "The locust spreads its wings and flies away"** (v16) — Hebrew יָנֵץ וַיָּעֹף *yanetz vayya'of* = spreads wings and flies; "spreads its wings" more vivid.
- **"Thy crowned are as the locusts, and thy captains as the great grasshoppers" → "Your princes are like grasshoppers, your scribes like clouds of locusts"** (v17) — Hebrew מִנְזָרֶיךָ *minzarekha* = your guards/princes; טֹפְסְרַיִךְ *tofserayikh* = your scribes/marshals; "scribes" or "marshals" for *tofser*.
- **"which camp in the hedges in the cold day" → "settling on the fences in a day of cold"** (v17) — modernized.
- **"thy nobles shall dwell in the dust" → "your nobles slumber"** (v18) — Hebrew יִשְׁכְּנוּ אַדִּירֶיךָ *yishkenu addirekha* = your nobles shall dwell/rest; "slumber" parallel to "asleep" for the shepherds.
- **"There is no healing of thy bruise; thy wound is grievous" → "There is no easing your hurt; your wound is grievous"** (v19) — Hebrew אֵין כֵּהָה לְשִׁבְרֶךָ *eyn kehah leshibrekha*; *kehah* = dulling/easing; "easing" more precise.
- **"all that hear the bruit of thee shall clap the hands over thee" → "All who hear the news about you clap their hands over you"** (v19) — Hebrew שִׁמְעַת *shim'at* = report/news; "news" for *shema'*; "clap their hands" = gesture of joy at an enemy's fall.
- **"for upon whom hath not thy wickedness passed continually?" → "For upon whom has not come your unceasing evil?"** (v19) — Hebrew כִּי עַל מִי לֹא עָבְרָה רָעָתְךָ תָּמִיד; modernized.

### Notes
- **Nineveh fell 612 BC** — fulfilled with Median/Babylonian sack.
