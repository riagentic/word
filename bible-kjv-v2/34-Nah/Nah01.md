# Nahum 1

[1] An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum of Elkosh.

[2] Yahweh is a jealous and avenging *Elohim*;
Yahweh is avenging and wrathful;
Yahweh takes vengeance on his adversaries
and keeps wrath for his enemies.

[3] Yahweh is slow to anger and great in power,
and Yahweh will by no means clear the guilty.
His way is in whirlwind and storm,
and the clouds are the dust of his feet.

[4] He rebukes the sea and makes it dry;
he dries up all the rivers;
Bashan and Carmel wither;
the bloom of Lebanon withers.

[5] The mountains quake before him;
the hills melt;
the earth heaves before him,
the world and all who dwell in it.

[6] Who can stand before his indignation?
Who can endure the heat of his anger?
His wrath is poured out like fire,
and the rocks are broken into pieces by him.

[7] Yahweh is good,
a stronghold in the day of trouble;
he knows those who take refuge in him.

[8] But with an overflowing flood
he will make a complete end of the adversaries,
and will pursue his enemies into darkness.

[9] What do you plot against Yahweh?
He will make a complete end;
trouble will not rise up a second time.

[10] For they are like entangled thorns,
like drunkards as they drink;
they are consumed like stubble fully dried.

[11] From you came one
who plotted evil against Yahweh,
a worthless counselor.

[12] Thus says Yahweh:

"Though they are full of strength and many,
they will be cut down and pass away.
Though I have afflicted you,
I will afflict you no more.

[13] And now I will break his yoke from off you
and will burst your bonds apart."

[14] Yahweh has given commandment about you:

"No more shall your name be perpetuated;
from the house of your gods I will cut off
the carved image and the metal image.
I will make your grave, for you are vile."

[15] Behold, upon the mountains, the feet of him who brings good news,
who publishes peace!
Keep your feasts, O Judah;
fulfill your vows,
for never again shall the worthless pass through you;
he is utterly cut off.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"The burden of Nineveh" → "An oracle concerning Nineveh"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא נִינְוֵה *massa' Nineveh*; *massa'* = oracle/burden; "oracle" more precise than "burden."
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the chapter is an acrostic poem and then oracle.
- **"God is jealous, and the Lord revengeth; the Lord revengeth, and is furious" → "Yahweh is a jealous and avenging God; Yahweh is avenging and wrathful"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל קַנּוֹא וְנֹקֵם יְהוָה *El qanno venokhem Yahweh*; *El qanno* = jealous God; three instances of "the LORD" restored to Yahweh; "avenging" for *noqem* = taking vengeance.
- **"will not at all acquit the wicked" → "will by no means clear the guilty"** (v3) — Hebrew נַקֵּה לֹא יְנַקֶּה *naqqeh lo yenaqqeh* = will certainly not clear; "clear the guilty" more precise.
- **"Bashan languisheth, and Carmel, and the flower of Lebanon languisheth" → "Bashan and Carmel wither; the bloom of Lebanon withers"** (v4) — Hebrew וּפֶרַח לְבָנוֹן *uforah Levanon* = the bloom of Lebanon; "bloom" more vivid than "flower"; condensed.
- **"the earth is burned at his presence" → "the earth heaves before him"** (v5) — Hebrew וַתִּשָּׂא הָאָרֶץ *vatissa' ha'aretz* = the earth lifts/heaves; "heaves" captures the earthquake image better than "burned."
- **"his fury is poured out like fire, and the rocks are thrown down by him" → "His wrath is poured out like fire, and the rocks are broken into pieces by him"** (v6) — modernized.
- **"a strong hold in the day of trouble" → "a stronghold in the day of trouble"** (v7) — Hebrew מָעֹז *ma'oz* = stronghold; one word.
- **"he knoweth them that trust in him" → "he knows those who take refuge in him"** (v7) — Hebrew יֹדֵעַ חֹסֵי בוֹ *yodea' chosay vo*; *chasah* = take refuge; "take refuge" more vivid.
- **"with an overrunning flood he will make an utter end of the place thereof" → "with an overflowing flood he will make a complete end of the adversaries"** (v8) — Hebrew מְקוֹמָהּ *meqomah* = her place; "adversaries" contextual (referring to Nineveh/Assyria).
- **"and darkness shall pursue his enemies" → "and will pursue his enemies into darkness"** (v8) — modernized.
- **"he will make an utter end" → "He will make a complete end"** (v9) — modernized.
- **"affliction shall not rise up the second time" → "trouble will not rise up a second time"** (v9) — Hebrew לֹא תָקוּם פַּעֲמַיִם צָרָה *lo taqum pa'amayim tzarah*; "trouble" for *tzarah*.
- **"while they be folden together as thorns, and while they are drunken as drunkards" → "they are like entangled thorns, like drunkards as they drink"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"they shall be devoured as stubble fully dry" → "they are consumed like stubble fully dried"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"There is one come out of thee, that imagineth evil against the LORD, a wicked counsellor" → "From you came one who plotted evil against Yahweh, a worthless counselor"** (v11) — Hebrew יֹצֵא מִמֵּךְ חֹשֵׁב עַל יְהוָה רָעָה *yotze mimekh choshev al Yahweh ra'ah*; "worthless counselor" for *beliyya'al*.
- **"Though they be quiet, and likewise many, yet thus shall they be cut down, when he shall pass through" → "Though they are full of strength and many, they will be cut down and pass away"** (v12) — Hebrew שְׁלֵמִים *shelemim* = full/at peace; "full of strength" more precise.
- **"And the LORD hath given a commandment concerning thee" → "Yahweh has given commandment about you"** (v14) — modernized.
- **"that no more of thy name be sown" → "No more shall your name be perpetuated"** (v14) — Hebrew לֹא יִזָּרַע מִשִּׁמְךָ עוֹד = your name shall no longer be sown/planted.
- **"the graven image and the molten image" → "the carved image and the metal image"** (v14) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל *pesel* = carved image; מַסֵּכָה *massekah* = metal/cast image; "metal image" more precise.
- **"for thou art vile" → "for you are vile"** (v14) — modernized.
- **"keep thy solemn feasts, perform thy vows" → "Keep your feasts, O Judah; fulfill your vows"** (v15) — modernized; "feasts" for *chaggayikh*.
- **"for the wicked shall no more pass through thee" → "for never again shall the worthless pass through you"** (v15) — Hebrew בְּלִיַּעַל *beliyya'al* = worthless/Belial (as in v11); "worthless" consistent.
