# Nahum 1

[1] The burden of ⟦nineveh|Ninveh|Nineveh⟧. The book of the vision of ⟦nahum|Nachum|Nahum⟧ the ⟦elkosh|Elqoshite|Elkoshite⟧.

[2] A jealous and avenging ⟦el|El|God⟧ is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
avenging is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and furious;
avenging is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on his adversaries,
and he keeps anger for his enemies.

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ is slow to anger and great in power,
and he will surely not clear.
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ — in tempest and in storm is his way,
and cloud is the fine dust of his feet.

[4] Rebuking the sea, he dried it up,
and all the rivers he has dried;
Bashan has languished, and ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧,
and the flower of ⟦lebanon|Levanon|Lebanon⟧ has languished.

[5] Mountains have quaked because of him,
and the hills have melted away;
and the earth heaved before his face,
and the world and all who dwell in it.

[6] Before his indignation who can stand?
And who can rise up in the burning of his anger?
His fury has been poured out like fire,
and the rocks have been broken down by him.

[7] Good is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
for a strong place in the day of distress;
and he knows those who take refuge in him.

[8] And with a flood passing over
he will make a full end of her place,
and darkness shall pursue his enemies.

[9] What are you devising against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?
A full end he is making;
distress shall not rise up twice.

[10] For even as tangled thorns,
and as drunk with their drink,
they are consumed like dry stubble, fully.

[11] From you has gone out one devising evil against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
one counseling ⟦belial|Beliyyaal|worthlessness⟧.

[12] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

Though they are whole, and so many,
even so they shall be sheared off, and he shall pass over.
And I have afflicted you;
I will not afflict you any more.

[13] And now I will break his yoke-bar from upon you,
and your bonds I will snap.

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded concerning you:

No more shall any of your name be sown;
from the house of your gods I will cut off
carved image and molten image;
I will make your grave, for you are lightly esteemed.

[15] Behold, upon the mountains the feet of one who brings news,
who makes ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ heard!
Hold your feasts, ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧;
repay your vows;
for no more again shall ⟦belial|Beliyyaal|worthlessness⟧ pass through you;
all of him is cut off.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order. The quotation marks around Yahweh's words (v12–14) are dropped: the Hebrew has none.
- **"The burden of Nineveh"** (v1) — Hebrew מַשָּׂא, kept as "burden" (the word is "what is lifted up"; "oracle" is the other common rendering). **"Nahum the Elkoshite"** — הָאֶלְקֹשִׁי, a gentilic ("the man of Elqosh").
- **"God is jealous, and the LORD revengeth; the LORD revengeth, and is furious" → "A jealous and avenging El is Yahweh; avenging is Yahweh, and furious"** (v2) — Hebrew אֵל קַנּוֹא (the spelling of Josh 24:19 for קַנָּא) "a jealous El" ("zealous" is the other sense of the root); נֹקֵם, a participle, three times "avenging"; וּבַעַל חֵמָה, literally "and a lord of fury" (בַּעַל + noun names one marked by the thing, as בַּעַל שֵׂעָר "a hairy man"). **"and he reserveth wrath for his enemies" → "and he keeps anger for his enemies"** — וְנוֹטֵר הוּא, נָטַר "keep" with no object: "anger" is supplied (as Lev 19:18; Ps 103:9).
- **"and will not at all acquit the wicked" → "and he will surely not clear"** (v3) — Hebrew וְנַקֵּה לֹא יְנַקֶּה, the wording of Exod 34:7 (literally "and clearing he will not clear"); the Hebrew has no object, so "the guilty" / "the wicked" is not supplied. **"great in power"** — the qere גְּדָל־כֹּחַ "great of power" (the written form וגדול "and great" has the same sense). אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם "slow to anger", literally "long of nostrils", as Exod 34:6. **"the LORD hath his way in the whirlwind and in the storm, and the clouds are the dust of his feet" → "Yahweh — in tempest and in storm is his way, and cloud is the fine dust of his feet"** — the Hebrew order; סוּפָה "tempest", שְׂעָרָה (a spelling of סְעָרָה) "storm"; עָנָן singular; אָבָק "fine dust".
- **"He rebuketh the sea, and maketh it dry, and drieth up all the rivers" → "Rebuking the sea, he dried it up, and all the rivers he has dried"** (v4) — Hebrew גּוֹעֵר, a participle, then וַיַּבְּשֵׁהוּ (a past form) and הֶחֱרִיב (a perfect): the hymn speaks of what he has done. **"Bashan languisheth, and Carmel, and the flower of Lebanon languisheth" → "Bashan has languished, and Carmel, and the flower of Lebanon has languished"** — אֻמְלַל twice, a perfect; פֶּרַח "flower".
- **"The mountains quake at him, and the hills melt, and the earth is burned at his presence" → "Mountains have quaked because of him, and the hills have melted away; and the earth heaved before his face"** (v5) — Hebrew רָעֲשׁוּ, הִתְמֹגָגוּ (perfects; מוּג "melt away"); וַתִּשָּׂא, from נָשָׂא "lift up": the earth "lifted itself", heaved (KJV "is burned" takes it from another root; uncertain); מִפָּנָיו, literally "from his face". **"yea, the world" → "and the world"** — וְתֵבֵל.
- **"Who can stand before his indignation? and who can abide in the fierceness of his anger?" → "Before his indignation who can stand? And who can rise up in the burning of his anger?"** (v6) — Hebrew order; יָקוּם "rise up"; חֲרוֹן אַפּוֹ "the burning of his anger" (אַף "anger", literally "nose"). **"his fury is poured out like fire, and the rocks are thrown down by him" → "His fury has been poured out like fire, and the rocks have been broken down by him"** — perfects; חֵמָה "fury"; נִתְּצוּ, נָתַץ "break down".
- **"The LORD is good, a strong hold in the day of trouble; and he knoweth them that trust in him" → "Good is Yahweh, for a strong place in the day of distress; and he knows those who take refuge in him"** (v7) — Hebrew לְמָעוֹז "for a strong place"; צָרָה "distress"; חֹסֵי בוֹ "those taking refuge in him" (חָסָה, not "trust": that is בָּטַח).
- **"with an overrunning flood he will make an utter end of the place thereof" → "with a flood passing over he will make a full end of her place"** (v8) — Hebrew וּבְשֶׁטֶף עֹבֵר "and with a flood passing over"; כָּלָה יַעֲשֶׂה "he will make a full end"; מְקוֹמָהּ "her place" (feminine: Nineveh; the former "the adversaries" was not in the Hebrew). **"and darkness shall pursue his enemies"** — וְאֹיְבָיו יְרַדֶּף־חֹשֶׁךְ: "darkness" may be the subject (so the verse) or the place: "he will pursue his enemies into darkness" ("into" supplied); the Hebrew allows both.
- **"What do ye imagine against the LORD? he will make an utter end: affliction shall not rise up the second time" → "What are you devising against Yahweh? A full end he is making; distress shall not rise up twice"** (v9) — Hebrew תְּחַשְּׁבוּן, חִשֵּׁב "devise"; עֹשֶׂה a participle; פַּעֲמַיִם "twice"; צָרָה "distress" as v7.
- **"For while they be folden together as thorns, and while they are drunken as drunkards, they shall be devoured as stubble fully dry" → "For even as tangled thorns, and as drunk with their drink, they are consumed like dry stubble, fully"** (v10) — Hebrew כִּי עַד־סִירִים סְבֻכִים וּכְסָבְאָם סְבוּאִים, a hard line: עַד ("up to", "even as", "while") and the play סָבְאָם … סְבוּאִים ("their drink … drenched") are uncertain; the words are kept as near as English allows. אֻכְּלוּ, a perfect of אָכַל "consume"; מָלֵא "full", as an adverb "fully".
- **"There is one come out of thee, that imagineth evil against the LORD, a wicked counsellor" → "From you has gone out one devising evil against Yahweh, one counseling worthlessness"** (v11) — Hebrew מִמֵּךְ יָצָא (a perfect; "you" feminine, the city); יֹעֵץ בְּלִיָּעַל "one counseling *beliyyaal*" (a common noun, "worthlessness", not a name: token).
- **"Though they be quiet, and likewise many, yet thus shall they be cut down, when he shall pass through" → "Though they are whole, and so many, even so they shall be sheared off, and he shall pass over"** (v12) — Hebrew אִם־שְׁלֵמִים "if (though) whole" (at full strength); וְכֵן … וְכֵן "and so … and so"; נָגֹזּוּ, nifal of גָּזַז "shear"; וְעָבָר, singular: "he" (or "it") "shall pass over" (who is meant is not said). **"Though I have afflicted thee, I will afflict thee no more" → "And I have afflicted you; I will not afflict you any more"** — וְעִנִּתִךְ, עִנָּה "afflict"; "you" is feminine here (Judah / Zion), masculine in v14 (the king of Nineveh).
- **"For now will I break his yoke from off thee, and will burst thy bonds in sunder" → "And now I will break his yoke-bar from upon you, and your bonds I will snap"** (v13) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה "and now"; מֹטֵהוּ, מוֹט "yoke-bar"; מוֹסְרֹתַיִךְ "bonds"; נִתֵּק "snap".
- **"And the LORD hath given a commandment concerning thee, that no more of thy name be sown" → "And Yahweh has commanded concerning you: No more shall any of your name be sown"** (v14) — Hebrew וְצִוָּה; מִשִּׁמְךָ "of your name" (no seed shall be sown from it). **"the graven image and the molten image" → "carved image and molten image"** — פֶּסֶל וּמַסֵּכָה, no article. **"I will make thy grave; for thou art vile" → "I will make your grave, for you are lightly esteemed"** — קַלּוֹתָ, קָלַל "be light" (of no weight).
- **"Behold upon the mountains the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, that publisheth peace!" → "Behold, upon the mountains the feet of one who brings news, who makes shalom heard!"** (v15) — the Hebrew of Isa 52:7 and its wording: מְבַשֵּׂר "one bringing news" (that it is good is not in the word), מַשְׁמִיעַ שָׁלוֹם "making *shalom* heard". **"O Judah, keep thy solemn feasts, perform thy vows" → "Hold your feasts, Judah; repay your vows"** — חָגִּי … חַגַּיִךְ, literally "feast your feasts"; שַׁלְּמִי נְדָרָיִךְ, שִׁלֵּם "repay" a vow; no "O": the Hebrew has none. **"for the wicked shall no more pass through thee; he is utterly cut off" → "for no more again shall worthlessness pass through you; all of him is cut off"** — לֹא יוֹסִיף עוֹד "shall not again any more"; the qere לַעֲבָר "to pass" (written לעבור, the same sense); בְּלִיַּעַל as v11 (token); כֻּלֹּה "all of him".

### Notes

- **v2–8** — a partial alphabetic acrostic: the lines open with א (v2), ב (v3b), ג (v4), ה (v5), ו (v5b), ז (v6), ח (v6b), ט (v7), י (v7b), כ (v8); ד is missing at v4b (אֻמְלַל), and the scheme stops at כ. The translation does not imitate it.
- **v15** — Hebrew 2:1; our 2:1–13 are Hebrew 2:2–14.
