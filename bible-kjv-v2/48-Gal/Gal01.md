# Galatians 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, an apostle — not from men nor through a man, but through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father, who raised him from the dead —

[2] and all the brothers with me,

To the churches of Galatia:

[3] Grace to you and peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[4] who gave himself for our sins, that he might deliver us out of the present evil age, according to the will of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Father,

[5] to whom be the glory unto the ages of the ages. Amen.

[6] I marvel that you are so quickly turning away from him who called you in the grace of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ unto a different gospel,

[7] which is not another; only there are some who are troubling you and wanting to pervert the gospel of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[8] But even if we or an angel from heaven should preach a gospel to you contrary to what we preached to you, **let him be accursed**.

[9] As we have said before, also now I say again: if anyone preaches a gospel to you contrary to what you received, let him be accursed.

[10] For am I now persuading men, or ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? Or am I seeking to please men? If I were still pleasing men, I would not be a slave of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[11] For I make known to you, brothers, the gospel that was preached by me, that it is not according to man;

[12] for neither did I receive it from a man, nor was I taught it, but through a revelation of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[13] For you heard of my conduct once in Judaism, that exceedingly I was persecuting the church of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and ravaging it,

[14] and I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my own age in my race, being more abundantly a zealot for the traditions of my fathers.

[15] But when ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who set me apart from my mother's womb and called me through his grace, was pleased

[16] to reveal his Son in me, that I might preach him among the nations, immediately I did not confer with flesh and blood,

[17] nor did I go up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to those who were apostles before me, but I went away into ⟦arabia|Arav|Arabia⟧, and again I returned to ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧.

[18] Then after three years I went up to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ to visit ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧, and I remained with him fifteen days;

[19] but another of the apostles I did not see, except ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ the brother of the Lord.

[20] Now the things I write to you — behold, before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, I do not lie.

[21] Then I came into the regions of Syria and of ⟦cilicia|Kilikia|Cilicia⟧.

[22] And I was unknown by face to the churches of ⟦judea|Yehudah|Judea⟧ that are in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧;

[23] they only kept hearing, "He who once persecuted us now preaches the faith which he once ravaged,"

[24] and they glorified ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in me.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v19) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Iēsous Christos* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua* and renders Hebrew *Mashiach* = "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v18) — Greek Κηφᾶν (WH Treg NA28), Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kefa* ("rock"); the Aramaic form of the name Yeshua gave Simon. The KJV follows the reading Πέτρον (RP).
- **"from God the Father, and from our Lord" → "from *Elohim* our Father and the Lord"** (v3) — the verse follows ἡμῶν καὶ κυρίου (WH NA28); καὶ κυρίου ἡμῶν, "the Father and our Lord" (Treg RP, and the printed SBLGNT).
- **"from this present evil world" → "out of the present evil age"** (v4) — Greek ἐκ τοῦ αἰῶνος τοῦ ἐνεστῶτος πονηροῦ; αἰών is "age".
- **"for ever and ever" → "unto the ages of the ages"** (v5) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων.
- **"ye are so soon removed" → "you are so quickly turning away"** (v6) — Greek μετατίθεσθε, present tense, middle or passive: "you are transferring yourselves" or "you are being transferred". The Greek has one verb; there is no second "turning to".
- **"into the grace of Christ" → "in the grace of the *Mashiach*"** (v6) — Greek ἐν χάριτι Χριστοῦ.
- **"another gospel: which is not another" → "a different gospel, which is not another"** (v6–7) — two Greek words: ἕτερον ("different") and ἄλλο ("another").
- **"but there be some" → "only there are some"** (v7) — Greek εἰ μή, "except that".
- **"preach any other gospel unto you than that which" → "preach a gospel to you contrary to what"; "preached of me" → "preached by me"; "preach him" and "preacheth the faith" kept** (v8, 9, 11, 16, 23) — Greek εὐαγγελίζομαι, one verb, "preach the gospel" (literally "bring as good news"), the verb of the noun "gospel" (εὐαγγέλιον); also 3:8; 4:13. παρʼ ὅ is "beside", "beyond" or "contrary to that which"; the Greek has no noun "the one".
- **"accursed" kept** (v8, 9) — Greek ἀνάθεμα, a thing devoted to destruction, the Septuagint's word for Hebrew *cherem*; a different word from "cursed" (ἐπικατάρατος) in 3:10, 13.
- **"do I now persuade men, or God? or do I seek to please men?" kept** (v10) — Greek πείθω, "persuade", and ζητῶ ἀρέσκειν, "seek to please"; "men" is plural (ἀνθρώπους, ἀνθρώποις).
- **"the servant of Christ" → "a slave of the *Mashiach*"** (v10) — Greek δοῦλος.
- **"I certify you" → "I make known to you"; "not after man" → "not according to man"** (v11) — Greek γνωρίζω, κατὰ ἄνθρωπον.
- **"by the revelation of Jesus Christ" → "through a revelation of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v12) — the last clause has no verb. The genitive can mean a revelation given by *Yeshua* or a revelation that shows *Yeshua* (v16).
- **"my conversation in time past" → "my conduct once"** (v13) — Greek ἀναστροφήν ποτε.
- **"wasted it" → "ravaging it"; "destroyed" → "ravaged"** (v13, 23) — Greek ἐπόρθουν, ἐπόρθει, imperfect tense of πορθέω, "ravage, lay waste". The Greek has no "tried to".
- **"profited in the Jews' religion above many my equals in mine own nation" → "was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my own age in my race"** (v14) — Greek προέκοπτον, Ἰουδαϊσμῷ, γένει (as Phil 3:5).
- **"beyond measure" → "exceedingly"** (v13) — Greek καθʼ ὑπερβολήν.
- **"being more exceedingly zealous" → "being more abundantly a zealot"** (v14) — Greek περισσοτέρως ζηλωτὴς ὑπάρχων, a noun with a participle; the Greek has no "so … was I".
- **"when it pleased God, who separated me" → "when *Elohim*, who set me apart …, was pleased"** (v15) — the verse follows εὐδόκησεν ὁ θεός (WH Treg NA28 RP); the printed SBLGNT (Holmes, WH margin) has no ὁ θεός: "when he who set me apart … was pleased".
- **"from my mother's womb" kept** (v15) — Greek ἐκ κοιλίας μητρός μου, "out of" or "since"; not "before I was born".
- **"to reveal his Son in me" kept** (v16) — Greek ἐν ἐμοί, "in me"; also v24.
- **"among the heathen" → "among the nations"** (v16) — Greek ἔθνη, "nations", throughout the letter.
- **"immediately I conferred not with flesh and blood" kept** (v16) — Greek εὐθέως οὐ προσανεθέμην σαρκὶ καὶ αἵματι; the Greek says "flesh and blood", not "anyone". The same verb stands in 2:6 ("added").
- **"to see Peter" → "to visit *Kefa*"** (v18) — Greek ἱστορῆσαι, "to visit and get to know".
- **"save James" → "except *Yaaqov*"** (v19) — Greek εἰ μή; the wording can count *Yaaqov* among the apostles or set him beside them.
- **"behold, before God, I lie not" kept** (v20) — the Greek sentence breaks off after "the things I write to you" and continues with ὅτι, "that I do not lie"; it has no brackets.
- **"unknown by face" kept** (v22) — Greek τῷ προσώπῳ.
- **"they had heard only" → "they only kept hearing"** (v23) — Greek ἀκούοντες ἦσαν, a continuing past.
- **"they glorified God in me" kept** (v24) — Greek ἐν ἐμοί, "in me"; not "because of me".
