# Galatians 6

[1] Brothers, even if a man is overtaken in some trespass, you who are spiritual restore such a one in a spirit of meekness, looking to yourself, lest you also be tempted.

[2] **Bear one another's burdens, and so you will fulfill the law of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.**

[3] For if anyone thinks he is something, being nothing, he deludes himself;

[4] but let each one test his own work, and then he will have his boast in regard to himself alone and not in regard to the other;

[5] for each one will bear his own load.

[6] And let him who is instructed in the word share with him who instructs, in all good things.

[7] **Do not be deceived: ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is not mocked; for whatever a man sows, that he will also reap;**

[8] because he who sows unto his own flesh will from the flesh reap corruption, but he who sows unto the Spirit will from the Spirit reap eternal life.

[9] And in doing the good let us not grow weary, for in its own time we will reap, not fainting.

[10] So then, as we have time, let us work the good toward all, but most of all toward those of the household of the faith.

[11] See with what large letters I have written to you with my own hand.

[12] As many as want to make a fair show in flesh, these compel you to be circumcised, only that they may not be persecuted for the cross of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[13] For not even those who are being circumcised themselves observe ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, but they want you to be circumcised that they may boast in your flesh.

[14] But for me, may it not be to boast except in the cross of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.

[15] For neither is circumcision anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creation.

[16] And as many as will walk in line by this rule, peace upon them and mercy, and upon the ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[17] Henceforth let no one give me troubles, for I bear the marks of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ in my body.

[18] The grace of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv7, 16) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv17, 18) — Greek *Iēsous* transliterates Hebrew/Aramaic *Yeshua*.
- **"if a man be overtaken in a fault" → "even if a man is overtaken in some trespass"** (v1) — Greek ἐὰν καὶ προλημφθῇ ἄνθρωπος ἔν τινι παραπτώματι; παράπτωμα is "trespass" (Col 2:13), a different word from "transgression" (παράβασις, 3:19).
- **"in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself" → "in a spirit of meekness, looking to yourself"** (v1) — Greek ἐν πνεύματι πραΰτητος, without the article; σκοπῶν, a singular participle after the plural "you": the sentence is one, and turns to each reader alone.
- **"and so fulfil the law of Christ" → "and so you will fulfill the law of the *Mashiach*"** (v2) — the printed text has ἀναπληρώσετε, future tense, "fill up" (as Phil 2:30).
- **"if a man think himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself" → "if anyone thinks he is something, being nothing, he deludes himself"** (v3) — Greek μηδὲν ὤν, a participle; φρεναπατᾷ, "deceive the mind", a different verb from "deceive" (πλανάω) in v7.
- **"let every man prove his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another" → "let each one test his own work, and then he will have his boast in regard to himself alone and not in regard to the other"** (v4) — Greek δοκιμαζέτω; τὸ καύχημα, "the boast" (as Phil 1:26); εἰς ἑαυτὸν … εἰς τὸν ἕτερον; the Greek has no "neighbor".
- **"his own burden" → "his own load"** (v5) — Greek φορτίον, a different word from "burdens" (βάρη) in v2; βαστάσει is a plain future, without "have to".
- **"Let him that is taught in the word communicate unto him that teacheth in all good things" → "let him who is instructed in the word share with him who instructs, in all good things"** (v6) — Greek κατηχούμενος, κατηχοῦντι, "instruct by word of mouth"; κοινωνείτω, the verb of κοινωνία, "fellowship" (2:9).
- **"Be not deceived" kept** (v7) — Greek μὴ πλανᾶσθε.
- **"soweth to his flesh … soweth to the Spirit" → "sows unto his own flesh … sows unto the Spirit"** (v8) — Greek εἰς τὴν σάρκα ἑαυτοῦ, εἰς τὸ πνεῦμα.
- **"let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not" → "in doing the good let us not grow weary, for in its own time we will reap, not fainting"** (v9) — Greek ἐγκακῶμεν (as 2 Thess 3:13); καιρῷ ἰδίῳ; μὴ ἐκλυόμενοι, a participle: "if" is not in the Greek.
- **"As we have therefore opportunity" → "So then, as we have time"** (v10) — Greek καιρόν, the word of v9; ὡς is "as" or "while".
- **"let us do good unto all men, especially unto them who are of the household of faith" → "let us work the good toward all, but most of all toward those of the household of the faith"** (v10) — Greek ἐργαζώμεθα τὸ ἀγαθόν; τοὺς οἰκείους τῆς πίστεως, with the article.
- **"how large a letter I have written" → "with what large letters I have written"** (v11) — Greek πηλίκοις γράμμασιν, the size of the letters of the script; ἔγραψα is a past tense, written from the readers' point of view (as Phlm 19).
- **"they constrain you to be circumcised; only lest they should suffer persecution" → "these compel you to be circumcised, only that they may not be persecuted"** (v12) — Greek ἀναγκάζουσιν, the verb of 2:3, 14; ἐν σαρκί has no article.
- **"neither they themselves who are circumcised keep the law" → "not even those who are being circumcised themselves observe law"** (v13) — Greek οἱ περιτεμνόμενοι, present tense; νόμον without the article. φυλάσσω is elsewhere "guard"; with a law as object it means "observe".
- **"God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross" → "But for me, may it not be to boast except in the cross"** (v14) — Greek ἐμοὶ δὲ μὴ γένοιτο καυχᾶσθαι εἰ μή, the wish of 2:17; 3:21.
- **"by whom the world is crucified unto me" → "through which the world has been crucified to me"** (v14) — Greek διʼ οὗ, "through which" (the cross) or "through whom" (the *Mashiach*).
- **"For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing" → "For neither is circumcision anything"** (v15) — the printed text has οὔτε γὰρ περιτομή τί ἐστιν; "in Christ Jesus" is not in the critical text.
- **"a new creature" → "a new creation"** (v15) — Greek καινὴ κτίσις.
- **"as many as walk according to this rule, peace be on them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God" → "as many as will walk in line by this rule, peace upon them and mercy, and upon the *Yisrael* of *Elohim*"** (v16) — Greek στοιχήσουσιν, future tense, the verb of 5:25; the blessing has no verb. The last καί is "and" or "even": "the *Yisrael* of *Elohim*" can be a second group or the same people; the Greek order is kept.
- **"From henceforth let no man trouble me" → "Henceforth let no one give me troubles"** (v17) — Greek κόπους μοι μηδεὶς παρεχέτω; κόπος is elsewhere "labor"; κόπους παρέχειν is a set phrase, "cause trouble".
- **"the marks of the Lord Jesus" → "the marks of *Yeshua*"** (v17) — "Lord" is not in the critical text; στίγματα are marks branded or cut into the skin.
