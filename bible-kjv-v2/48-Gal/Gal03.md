# Galatians 3

[1] O foolish Galatians, who bewitched you, before whose eyes ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ was openly set forth, crucified?

[2] This only I want to learn from you: did you receive the Spirit from works of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, or from the hearing of faith?

[3] Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now completing by the flesh?

[4] Did you suffer so many things in vain? If indeed it was even in vain.

[5] He then who supplies the Spirit to you and works powers among you — from works of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, or from the hearing of faith?

[6] Just as ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ "**believed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and it was reckoned to him for righteousness**."

[7] Know then that those who are from faith, these are sons of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[8] And the Scripture, foreseeing that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ justifies the nations from faith, preached the gospel beforehand to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧: "**In you all the nations will be blessed**."

[9] So then those who are from faith are blessed with the faithful ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[10] For as many as are from works of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ are under a curse; for it is written, "**Cursed is everyone who does not abide in all the things written in the book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, to do them**."

[11] And that in ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ no one is justified before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is evident, because "**The righteous one will live from faith**";

[12] and the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ is not from faith, but "**He who has done them will live in them**."

[13] ⟦mashiach|The Mashiach|Christ⟧ redeemed us from the curse of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, having become a curse for us — because it is written, "**Cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree**" —

[14] that unto the nations the blessing of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ might come in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through the faith.

[15] Brothers, I speak according to man: though it is but a man's covenant, yet once it has been ratified, no one sets it aside or adds to it.

[16] Now to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ the promises were spoken, and to his seed. It does not say, "And to seeds," as of many, but as of one, "**And to your seed**," who is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[17] And this I say: the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, which came four hundred and thirty years after, does not annul a covenant previously ratified by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, so as to abolish the promise.

[18] For if the inheritance is from ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, it is no longer from promise; but to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has granted it through promise.

[19] Why then the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧? It was added for the sake of transgressions, until the seed should come to whom it has been promised, having been ordained through angels in the hand of a mediator.

[20] Now the mediator is not of one, but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is one.

[21] Is the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ then against the promises of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? May it not be! For if a law had been given that was able to make alive, righteousness would indeed have been from ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[22] But the Scripture shut up all things under sin, that the promise from faith of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ might be given to those who believe.

[23] But before the faith came, we were kept in custody under ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, being shut up unto the faith that was about to be revealed.

[24] So the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ has become our tutor unto ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, that we might be justified from faith;

[25] but the faith having come, we are no longer under a tutor.

[26] For you are all sons of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through the faith in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[27] For as many of you as were ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ into ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ have put on ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[28] **There is neither ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ nor ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female; for you are all one in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.**

[29] And if you are ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach's|Christ's⟧, then you are ⟦abraham|Avraham's|Abraham's⟧ seed, heirs according to promise.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 8, 11, 17, 18, 20, 21, 26) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v27) — Greek βαπτίζω *baptizō* = immerse/dip; rendered as the physical act rather than the transliterated ecclesiastical term.
- **"who hath bewitched you, that ye should not obey the truth" → "who bewitched you"** (v1) — the words "that ye should not obey the truth" and "among you" are not in the critical text. The verse is one question in Greek.
- **"evidently set forth, crucified" → "openly set forth, crucified"** (v1) — Greek προεγράφη ἐσταυρωμένος: προγράφω is "write before" or "post up publicly"; the Greek has no "as".
- **"by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith" → "from works of law, or from the hearing of faith"** (v2, 5) — Greek ἐξ ἔργων νόμου ἢ ἐξ ἀκοῆς πίστεως, without articles. ἀκοή is "hearing" or "the message heard"; the genitive "of faith" is left as it stands.
- **"are ye now made perfect by the flesh" → "are you now completing by the flesh"** (v3) — Greek ἐπιτελεῖσθε, "bring to an end", middle or passive ("are you being completed"); not the word "perfect" (τελειόω). πνεύματι and σαρκί have no article.
- **"if it be yet in vain" → "If indeed it was even in vain"** (v4) — Greek εἴ γε καὶ εἰκῇ, a clause without a verb. ἐπάθετε is "suffered" or "experienced".
- **"worketh miracles among you, doeth he it by" → "works powers among you —"** (v5) — Greek ἐνεργῶν δυνάμεις ἐν ὑμῖν ("among you" or "in you"); δυνάμεις is "powers", deeds of power. The question has no verb.
- **"it was accounted to him for righteousness" → "it was reckoned to him for righteousness"** (v6) — Greek ἐλογίσθη … εἰς δικαιοσύνην, as Jas 2:23. In the printed text v5 ends the question and v6 is a statement.
- **"they which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham" → "those who are from faith, these are sons of *Avraham*"** (v7) — Greek οἱ ἐκ πίστεως, οὗτοι υἱοί. Γινώσκετε can be a command ("know") or a statement ("you know").
- **"would justify the heathen through faith" → "justifies the nations from faith"** (v8) — Greek ἐκ πίστεως δικαιοῖ τὰ ἔθνη, present tense; τὰ ἔθνη is "the nations" both times in the verse.
- **"preached before the gospel" → "preached the gospel beforehand"** (v8) — Greek προευηγγελίσατο, the verb of 1:8 with προ-, "beforehand".
- **"with faithful Abraham" → "with the faithful *Avraham*"** (v9) — Greek σὺν τῷ πιστῷ Ἀβραάμ; πιστός is "faithful" or "believing". The Greek has no "man of faith".
- **"as many as are of the works of the law" → "as many as are from works of law"** (v10) — Greek ὅσοι ἐξ ἔργων νόμου εἰσίν; the Greek has no "rely on".
- **"that continueth not in all things" → "who does not abide in all the things"; "to do them" kept** (v10) — Greek ἐμμένει; τοῦ ποιῆσαι αὐτά, an infinitive of purpose.
- **"by the law" → "in law"; "The just shall live by faith" → "The righteous one will live from faith"** (v11) — Greek ἐν νόμῳ; ὁ δίκαιος ἐκ πίστεως ζήσεται. "From faith" can go with "the righteous one" or with "will live".
- **"The man that doeth them" → "He who has done them"** (v12) — Greek ὁ ποιήσας αὐτά, without "man"; ἐν αὐτοῖς is "in them".
- **"hath redeemed us" → "redeemed us"; "being made a curse" → "having become a curse"** (v13) — Greek ἐξηγόρασεν, "bought out" (also 4:5); γενόμενος.
- **"hangeth on a tree" kept** (v13) — Greek ξύλου, "wood, tree".
- **"that the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ" → "that unto the nations the blessing of *Avraham* might come in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v14) — Greek εἰς τὰ ἔθνη … ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ.
- **"the promise of the Spirit" kept** (v14) — Greek τὴν ἐπαγγελίαν τοῦ πνεύματος; not "the promised Spirit".
- **"I speak after the manner of men" → "I speak according to man"** (v15) — Greek κατὰ ἄνθρωπον λέγω, as 1:11.
- **"Though it be but a man's covenant, yet if it be confirmed, no man disannulleth, or addeth thereto" → "though it is but a man's covenant, yet once it has been ratified, no one sets it aside or adds to it"** (v15) — Greek ὅμως ἀνθρώπου κεκυρωμένην διαθήκην οὐδεὶς ἀθετεῖ ἢ ἐπιδιατάσσεται. διαθήκη is "covenant" or "will, testament"; ἐπιδιατάσσομαι is "add further provisions".
- **"to Abraham and his seed were the promises made" → "to *Avraham* the promises were spoken, and to his seed"** (v16) — Greek ἐρρέθησαν. σπέρμα is "seed" (v16, 19, 29): the argument turns on the singular. οὐ λέγει is "it does not say" or "he does not say".
- **"confirmed before of God in Christ" → "previously ratified by *Elohim*"** (v17) — "in Christ" is not in the critical text; προκεκυρωμένην, as "ratified" in v15.
- **"cannot disannul, that it should make the promise of none effect" → "does not annul …, so as to abolish the promise"** (v17) — Greek οὐκ ἀκυροῖ, εἰς τὸ καταργῆσαι: three verbs are kept apart, ἀθετέω "set aside" (v15; 2:21), ἀκυρόω "annul", καταργέω "abolish" (5:11; see 5:4).
- **"if the inheritance be of the law" → "if the inheritance is from law"; "God gave it" → "*Elohim* has granted it"** (v18) — Greek ἐκ νόμου; κεχάρισται, "has given as a favor" (as Phil 2:9).
- **"Wherefore then serveth the law?" → "Why then the law?"** (v19) — Greek Τί οὖν ὁ νόμος; "why" or "what, then, is the law?"; the question has no verb.
- **"because of transgressions" → "for the sake of transgressions"** (v19) — Greek τῶν παραβάσεων χάριν: "because of" or "for the purpose of".
- **"to whom the promise was made" → "to whom it has been promised"; "ordained by angels in the hand of a mediator" → "having been ordained through angels in the hand of a mediator"** (v19) — Greek ἐπήγγελται; διαταγεὶς διʼ ἀγγέλων ἐν χειρὶ μεσίτου.
- **"a mediator is not a mediator of one" → "the mediator is not of one"** (v20) — Greek ὁ δὲ μεσίτης ἑνὸς οὐκ ἔστιν; the Greek has no "implies more than".
- **"God forbid" → "May it not be!"** (v21) — Greek μὴ γένοιτο.
- **"which could have given life" → "that was able to make alive"** (v21) — Greek ζῳοποιῆσαι.
- **"hath concluded all under sin" → "shut up all things under sin"** (v22) — Greek συνέκλεισεν … τὰ πάντα, neuter: "all things"; the same verb in v23.
- **"the promise by faith of Jesus Christ" → "the promise from faith of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v22) — Greek ἐκ πίστεως Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ, the plain genitive of 2:16.
- **"before faith came, we were kept under the law, shut up unto the faith which should afterwards be revealed" → "before the faith came, we were kept in custody under law, being shut up unto the faith that was about to be revealed"** (v23) — Greek τὴν πίστιν, with the article; ἐφρουρούμεθα, "be guarded".
- **"the law was our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ" → "the law has become our tutor unto the *Mashiach*"** (v24) — Greek παιδαγωγός, the slave who kept watch over a boy, not a teacher; γέγονεν, "has become"; εἰς Χριστόν is "unto" or "until"; the Greek has no "came" and no "to bring us".
- **"ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus" → "you are all sons of *Elohim* through the faith in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v26) — Greek υἱοί; the Greek order is kept: "in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*" can go with "the faith" or with "you are sons".
- **"there is neither male nor female" → "there is no male and female"** (v28) — Greek οὐκ ἔνι ἄρσεν καὶ θῆλυ, the wording of Gen 1:27 in the Septuagint.
- **"ye are all one" kept** (v28) — Greek εἷς, masculine: "one person".
- **"heirs according to the promise" → "heirs according to promise"** (v29) — Greek κατʼ ἐπαγγελίαν, without the article.
