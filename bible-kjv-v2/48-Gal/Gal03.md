# Galatians 3

[1] O foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you? It was before your eyes that *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* was publicly portrayed as crucified.

[2] Let me ask you only this: Did you receive the Spirit by works of the Torah or by hearing with faith?

[3] Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now being perfected by the flesh?

[4] Did you suffer so many things in vain — if indeed it was in vain?

[5] Does he who supplies the Spirit to you and works miracles among you do so by works of the Torah, or by hearing with faith —

[6] just as Abraham "**believed *Elohim*, and it was counted to him as righteousness**"?

[7] Know then that it is those of faith who are the sons of Abraham.

[8] And the Scripture, foreseeing that *Elohim* would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham, saying, "**In you shall all the nations be blessed**."

[9] So then, those who are of faith are blessed along with Abraham, the man of faith.

[10] For all who rely on works of the Torah are under a curse; for it is written, "**Cursed be everyone who does not abide by all things written in the Book of the Torah, and do them**."

[11] Now it is evident that no one is justified before *Elohim* by the Torah, for "**The righteous shall live by faith**."

[12] But the Torah is not of faith, rather "**The one who does them shall live by them**."

[13] The *Mashiach* redeemed us from the curse of the Torah by becoming a curse for us — for it is written, "**Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree**" —

[14] so that in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua* the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles, so that we might receive the promised Spirit through faith.

[15] To give a human example, brothers: even with a man-made covenant, no one annuls it or adds to it once it has been ratified.

[16] Now the promises were made to Abraham and to his offspring. It does not say, "And to offsprings," referring to many, but referring to one, "**And to your offspring**," who is the *Mashiach*.

[17] This is what I mean: the Torah, which came 430 years afterward, does not annul a covenant previously ratified by *Elohim*, so as to make the promise void.

[18] For if the inheritance comes by the Torah, it no longer comes by promise; but *Elohim* gave it to Abraham by a promise.

[19] Why then the Torah? It was added because of transgressions, until the offspring should come to whom the promise had been made, and it was put in place through angels by an intermediary.

[20] Now an intermediary implies more than one, but *Elohim* is one.

[21] Is the Torah then contrary to the promises of *Elohim*? Certainly not! For if a law had been given that could give life, then righteousness would indeed be by the Torah.

[22] But the Scripture imprisoned everything under sin, so that the promise by faith in *Yeshua* the *Mashiach* might be given to those who believe.

[23] Now before faith came, we were held captive under the Torah, imprisoned until the coming faith would be revealed.

[24] So then, the Torah was our guardian until the *Mashiach* came, in order that we might be justified by faith.

[25] But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian,

[26] for in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua* you are all sons of *Elohim*, through faith.

[27] For as many of you as were immersed into the *Mashiach* have put on the *Mashiach*.

[28] **There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female, for you are all one in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*.**

[29] And if you are the *Mashiach*'s, then you are Abraham's offspring, heirs according to promise.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 8, 11, 17, 18, 20, 21, 26) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v27) — Greek βαπτίζω *baptizō* = immerse/dip; rendered as the physical act rather than the transliterated ecclesiastical term.
