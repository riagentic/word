# Galatians 3

[1] O foolish Galatians! Who has bewitched you? It was before your eyes that ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ was publicly portrayed as crucified.

[2] Let me ask you only this: Did you receive the Spirit by works of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ or by hearing with faith?

[3] Are you so foolish? Having begun by the Spirit, are you now being perfected by the flesh?

[4] Did you suffer so many things in vain — if indeed it was in vain?

[5] Does he who supplies the Spirit to you and works miracles among you do so by works of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, or by hearing with faith —

[6] just as ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ "**believed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and it was counted to him as righteousness**"?

[7] Know then that it is those of faith who are the sons of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧.

[8] And the Scripture, foreseeing that ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the gospel beforehand to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, saying, "**In you shall all the nations be blessed**."

[9] So then, those who are of faith are blessed along with ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, the man of faith.

[10] For all who rely on works of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ are under a curse; for it is written, "**Cursed be everyone who does not abide by all things written in the Book of the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧, and do them**."

[11] Now it is evident that no one is justified before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ by the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, for "**The righteous shall live by faith**."

[12] But the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ is not of faith, rather "**The one who does them shall live by them**."

[13] ⟦mashiach|The Mashiach|Christ⟧ redeemed us from the curse of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ by becoming a curse for us — for it is written, "**Cursed is everyone who is hanged on a tree**" —

[14] so that in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ the blessing of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ might come to the Gentiles, so that we might receive the promised Spirit through faith.

[15] To give a human example, brothers: even with a man-made covenant, no one annuls it or adds to it once it has been ratified.

[16] Now the promises were made to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ and to his offspring. It does not say, "And to offsprings," referring to many, but referring to one, "**And to your offspring**," who is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[17] This is what I mean: the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, which came 430 years afterward, does not annul a covenant previously ratified by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, so as to make the promise void.

[18] For if the inheritance comes by the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, it no longer comes by promise; but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave it to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧ by a promise.

[19] Why then the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧? It was added because of transgressions, until the offspring should come to whom the promise had been made, and it was put in place through angels by an intermediary.

[20] Now an intermediary implies more than one, but ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is one.

[21] Is the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ then contrary to the promises of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? Certainly not! For if a law had been given that could give life, then righteousness would indeed be by the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[22] But the Scripture imprisoned everything under sin, so that the promise by faith in ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ might be given to those who believe.

[23] Now before faith came, we were held captive under the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, imprisoned until the coming faith would be revealed.

[24] So then, the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ was our guardian until ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ came, in order that we might be justified by faith.

[25] But now that faith has come, we are no longer under a guardian,

[26] for in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ you are all sons of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, through faith.

[27] For as many of you as were ⟦immerse|immersed|baptized⟧ into ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ have put on ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[28] **There is neither ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ nor ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female, for you are all one in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.**

[29] And if you are ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach's|Christ's⟧, then you are ⟦abraham|Avraham's|Abraham's⟧ offspring, heirs according to promise.

## Changes

- **"Christ" / "Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 8, 11, 17, 18, 20, 21, 26) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"baptized" → "immersed"** (v27) — Greek βαπτίζω *baptizō* = immerse/dip; rendered as the physical act rather than the transliterated ecclesiastical term.
