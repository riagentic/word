# Galatians 2

[1] Then after fourteen years I went up again to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, taking Titus along with me.

[2] I went up because of a revelation and set before them (though privately before those who seemed influential) the gospel that I proclaim among the Gentiles, in order to make sure I was not running or had not run in vain.

[3] But even Titus, who was with me, was not forced to be circumcised, though he was a ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧.

[4] Yet because of false brothers secretly brought in — who slipped in to spy out our freedom that we have in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, so that they might bring us into slavery —

[5] to them we did not yield in submission even for a moment, so that the truth of the gospel might be preserved for you.

[6] And from those who seemed to be influential (what they were makes no difference to me; ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shows no partiality) — those, I say, who seemed influential added nothing to me.

[7] On the contrary, when they saw that I had been entrusted with the gospel to the uncircumcised, just as ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ had been entrusted with the gospel to the circumcised

[8] (for he who worked through ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ for his apostolic ministry to the circumcised worked also through me for mine to the Gentiles),

[9] and when ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, who seemed to be pillars, perceived the grace that was given to me, they gave the right hand of fellowship to ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ and me, that we should go to the Gentiles and they to the circumcised.

[10] Only, they asked us to remember the poor, the very thing I was eager to do.

[11] But when ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧ came to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, I opposed him to his face, because he stood condemned.

[12] For before certain men came from ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧, he was eating with the Gentiles; but when they came he drew back and separated himself, fearing the circumcision party.

[13] And the rest of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ acted hypocritically along with him, so that even ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ was led astray by their hypocrisy.

[14] But when I saw that their conduct was not in step with the truth of the gospel, I said to ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧ before them all, "If you, though a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, live like a Gentile and not like a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, how can you force the Gentiles to live like ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?"

[15] We ourselves are ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ by birth and not Gentile sinners;

[16] yet we know that **a person is not justified by works of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ but through faith in ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧**, so we also have believed in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, in order to be justified by faith in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and not by works of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, because by works of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ no one will be justified.

[17] But if, in our endeavor to be justified in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, we too were found to be sinners, is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ then a servant of sin? Certainly not!

[18] For if I rebuild what I tore down, I prove myself to be a transgressor.

[19] For through the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ I died to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, so that I might live to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] **I have been crucified with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧. It is no longer I who live, but ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ who lives in me. And the life I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who loved me and gave himself for me.**

[21] I do not nullify the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for if righteousness were through the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, then ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ died for no purpose.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v9, 12) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Iēsous Christos*; order varies with Paul's emphasis; *Yeshua* = Hebrew/Aramaic name, *Mashiach* = "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 19, 20, 21) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Cephas" → "*Kefa*"** (vv7, 8, 9, 11, 14) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kefa* ("rock"); the Aramaic name *Yeshua* gave Simon (cf. John 1:42).
- **"justified" / "justification"** (vv16, 17) — Greek δικαιόω *dikaioō* = declare/make righteous; retained as "justified" to preserve the forensic nuance.
