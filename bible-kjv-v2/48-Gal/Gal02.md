# Galatians 2

[1] Then after fourteen years I went up again to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧ with ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧, taking along Titus also.

[2] And I went up according to a revelation; and I laid before them the gospel that I proclaim among the nations, but privately before those of repute, lest somehow I should be running, or had run, in vain.

[3] But not even Titus, who was with me, being a ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧, was compelled to be circumcised;

[4] but because of the false brothers secretly brought in, who slipped in to spy out our liberty which we have in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, that they might enslave us —

[5] to whom we did not yield in subjection even for an hour, that the truth of the gospel might continue with you.

[6] But from those reputed to be something — what sort they once were makes no difference to me; ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does not receive the face of a man — for to me those of repute added nothing,

[7] but on the contrary, seeing that I had been entrusted with the gospel of the uncircumcision, just as ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ with that of the circumcision,

[8] for he who worked for ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧ unto an apostleship of the circumcision worked also for me unto the nations,

[9] and knowing the grace that was given to me, ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧ and ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧ and ⟦john|Yochanan|John⟧, those reputed to be pillars, gave to me and ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ right hands of fellowship, that we unto the nations, and they unto the circumcision;

[10] only that we should remember the poor, which very thing I was also diligent to do.

[11] But when ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧ came to ⟦antioch|Antiocheia|Antioch⟧, I withstood him to the face, because he stood condemned.

[12] For before certain ones came from ⟦james|Yaaqov|James⟧, he used to eat with the nations; but when they came, he was drawing back and separating himself, fearing those of the circumcision.

[13] And the rest of the ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ also played the hypocrite with him, so that even ⟦barnabas|Bar-Nabba|Barnabas⟧ was led away together by their hypocrisy.

[14] But when I saw that they were not walking straight according to the truth of the gospel, I said to ⟦cephas|Kefa|Cephas⟧ before all, "If you, being a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, live like a Gentile and not like a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, how do you compel the nations to live like ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧?"

[15] We, by nature ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and not sinners from the nations,

[16] yet knowing that **a man is not justified from works of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ except through faith of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧**, we also believed in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, that we might be justified from faith of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ and not from works of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, because from works of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ no flesh will be justified.

[17] But if, seeking to be justified in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, we ourselves also were found sinners, is ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ then a servant of sin? May it not be!

[18] For if I build again these things which I tore down, I show myself a transgressor.

[19] For I through ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ died to ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, that I might live to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[20] **I have been crucified with ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧; and no longer do I live, but ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ lives in me; and what I now live in the flesh, I live in faith, the faith of the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who loved me and delivered himself up for me.**

[21] I do not set aside the grace of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧; for if righteousness is through ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, then ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ died for nothing.

## Changes

- **"James" → "Jacob"** (v9, 12) — Greek Ἰάκωβος *Iakōbos* = Hebrew יַעֲקֹב *Yaaqov*, the same name as the patriarch; "James" is a later English form.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (throughout) — Greek *Iēsous Christos*; order varies with Paul's emphasis; *Yeshua* = Hebrew/Aramaic name, *Mashiach* = "Anointed One."
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv6, 19, 20, 21) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Cephas" / "Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (vv7, 8, 9, 11, 14) — Aramaic כֵּיפָא *Kefa* ("rock"); the Aramaic name *Yeshua* gave Simon (cf. John 1:42). The Greek has Κηφᾶς in v9, 11, 14 and Πέτρος in v7–8. The KJV has "Peter" in v11, 14 after the later reading.
- **"justified" / "justification"** (vv16, 17) — Greek δικαιόω *dikaioō* = declare/make righteous; retained as "justified" to preserve the forensic nuance.
- **"by revelation" → "according to a revelation"** (v2) — Greek κατὰ ἀποκάλυψιν.
- **"communicated unto them" → "laid before them"; "which I preach" → "that I proclaim"** (v2) — Greek ἀνεθέμην; κηρύσσω, "proclaim" (also 5:11), a different verb from "preach the gospel" (εὐαγγελίζομαι).
- **"them which were of reputation" → "those of repute"; "who seemed to be somewhat" → "those reputed to be something"; "who seemed to be pillars" → "those reputed to be pillars"** (v2, 6, 9) — Greek οἱ δοκοῦντες, from δοκέω, "seem, be reputed"; one word four times. The Greek has no "influential".
- **"lest by any means I should run, or had run, in vain" kept** (v2) — Greek μή πως εἰς κενὸν τρέχω ἢ ἔδραμον; the Greek has no "to make sure".
- **"being a Greek" kept** (v3) — Greek Ἕλλην ὤν, a participle; the Greek has no "though".
- **"that they might bring us into bondage" → "that they might enslave us"** (v4) — Greek καταδουλώσουσιν. The sentence of v4 breaks off and is not finished.
- **"gave place by subjection, no, not for an hour" → "did not yield in subjection even for an hour"** (v5) — Greek πρὸς ὥραν, "for an hour"; τῇ ὑποταγῇ, the noun of ὑποτάσσομαι, "be subject".
- **"God accepteth no man's person" → "*Elohim* does not receive the face of a man"** (v6) — Greek πρόσωπον θεὸς ἀνθρώπου οὐ λαμβάνει, the Hebrew idiom "to lift the face" = to show partiality (cf. Jas 2:1, 9).
- **"in conference added nothing to me" → "added nothing to me"** (v6) — Greek προσανέθεντο, the same verb as 1:16 ("confer with"); here "laid nothing further on me". The sentence begun with "from those reputed" breaks off and starts again with "for to me".
- **"the gospel of the uncircumcision … of the circumcision" kept** (v7) — Greek τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τῆς ἀκροβυστίας … τῆς περιτομῆς, plain genitives; "with that" is supplied.
- **"he that wrought effectually in Peter to the apostleship of the circumcision" → "he who worked for *Kefa* unto an apostleship of the circumcision"** (v8) — Greek ἐνεργήσας Πέτρῳ, a dative: "for" or "in"; ἀποστολή, "apostleship", has no article. The second half has only εἰς τὰ ἔθνη, "unto the nations".
- **"the right hands of fellowship" kept** (v9) — Greek δεξιὰς … κοινωνίας. The Greek names "me" before *Bar-Nabba*.
- **"that we should go unto the heathen" → "that we unto the nations"** (v9) — the Greek clause has no verb.
- **"the same which I also was forward to do" → "which very thing I was also diligent to do"** (v10) — Greek ἐσπούδασα. The Greek has no "they asked".
- **"because he was to be blamed" → "because he stood condemned"** (v11) — Greek κατεγνωσμένος ἦν, "he had been condemned".
- **"he did eat with the Gentiles" → "he used to eat with the nations"** (v12) — Greek μετὰ τῶν ἐθνῶν συνήσθιεν, imperfect tense; ἔθνη is "nations" throughout.
- **"them which were of the circumcision" → "those of the circumcision"** (v12) — Greek τοὺς ἐκ περιτομῆς; the Greek has no "party".
- **"dissembled likewise with him … carried away with their dissimulation" → "played the hypocrite with him … led away together by their hypocrisy"** (v13) — Greek συνυπεκρίθησαν, ὑποκρίσει: one root; συναπήχθη as 2 Pet 3:17.
- **"they walked not uprightly according to the truth" → "they were not walking straight according to the truth"** (v14) — Greek ὀρθοποδοῦσιν, "go with straight feet"; πρός is "toward" or "in accord with".
- **"livest after the manner of Gentiles, and not as do the Jews" → "live like a Gentile and not like a Jew"; "to live as do the Jews" → "to live like Jews"** (v14) — Greek ἐθνικῶς, Ἰουδαϊκῶς, adverbs; Ἰουδαΐζειν is one verb, "to Judaize".
- **"We who are Jews by nature, and not sinners of the Gentiles" → "We, by nature Jews and not sinners from the nations"** (v15) — Greek ἐξ ἐθνῶν ἁμαρτωλοί. v15–16 are one sentence: "We … knowing … we also believed".
- **"by the works of the law" → "from works of law"** (v16) — Greek ἐξ ἔργων νόμου, without articles, three times; ἐκ is "from" throughout the letter, διά "through".
- **"but by the faith of Jesus Christ" → "except through faith of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v16) — Greek ἐὰν μή, "except, if not": it can limit the whole statement ("is not justified … but only through faith") or only "from works of law". πίστις Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ is a plain genitive: the faith (or faithfulness) of *Yeshua*, or faith in him (as Phil 3:9); "we also believed in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*" follows with εἰς.
- **"shall no flesh be justified" kept** (v16) — Greek οὐ δικαιωθήσεται πᾶσα σάρξ (Ps 143:2).
- **"minister of sin" → "servant of sin"** (v17) — Greek διάκονος.
- **"God forbid" → "May it not be!"** (v17) — Greek μὴ γένοιτο, a wish; it does not name God. Also 3:21; 6:14.
- **"I make myself a transgressor" → "I show myself a transgressor"** (v18) — Greek συνιστάνω, "set forth, demonstrate".
- **"I through the law am dead to the law" → "I through law died to law"** (v19) — Greek διὰ νόμου νόμῳ ἀπέθανον, without articles, past tense.
- **"nevertheless I live; yet not I" → "and no longer do I live"** (v20) — Greek ζῶ δὲ οὐκέτι ἐγώ.
- **"I live by the faith of the Son of God" → "I live in faith, the faith of the Son of *Elohim*"** (v20) — Greek ἐν πίστει ζῶ τῇ τοῦ υἱοῦ τοῦ θεοῦ, the same plain genitive as v16.
- **"gave himself for me" → "delivered himself up for me"** (v20) — Greek παραδόντος, "delivered up" (the verb of Rom 4:25; 8:32); 1:4 has δόντος, "gave".
- **"I do not frustrate" → "I do not set aside"** (v21) — Greek ἀθετῶ, as 3:15.
- **"if righteousness come by the law, then Christ is dead in vain" → "if righteousness is through law, then the *Mashiach* died for nothing"** (v21) — the Greek clause has no verb; δωρεάν is "as a gift, without cause", a different word from "in vain" (εἰς κενόν, v2; εἰκῇ, 3:4).

### Notes
- **v19–20** — the critical editions count "I have been crucified with the *Mashiach*" as the end of v19; the verse numbers here are those of the KJV.
