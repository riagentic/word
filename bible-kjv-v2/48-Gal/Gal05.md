# Galatians 5

[1] **For liberty ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ set us free; stand firm therefore, and do not be held again in a yoke of slavery.**

[2] Behold, I, ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, say to you that if you are circumcised, ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ will profit you nothing.

[3] And I bear witness again to every man who is being circumcised that he is a debtor to do the whole ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[4] You were brought to nothing, away from ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, you who are being justified in ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧; you fell from grace.

[5] For we by the Spirit, from faith, eagerly await the hope of righteousness.

[6] For in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ neither circumcision avails anything, nor uncircumcision, but **faith working through love**.

[7] You were running well; who hindered you from obeying the truth?

[8] The persuasion is not from him who calls you.

[9] A little leaven leavens the whole lump.

[10] I am confident toward you in the Lord that you will think nothing else; but he who troubles you will bear the sentence, whoever he may be.

[11] But I, brothers, if I still proclaim circumcision, why am I still persecuted? Then the stumbling block of the cross has been abolished.

[12] O that those who unsettle you would even cut themselves off!

[13] For you were called for liberty, brothers; only not the liberty as an occasion for the flesh, but through love be slaves to one another.

[14] For the whole ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ has been fulfilled in one word, in this: "**You shall love your neighbor as yourself**."

[15] But if you bite and devour one another, see that you are not consumed by one another.

[16] But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you will by no means carry out the desire of the flesh.

[17] For the flesh desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; for these are opposed to one another, so that you do not do whatever things you want.

[18] But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[19] Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are: sexual immorality, uncleanness, licentiousness,

[20] idolatry, sorcery, enmities, strife, jealousy, rages, selfish ambitions, dissensions, sects,

[21] envyings, drunkennesses, revelries, and the things like these, of which I tell you beforehand, just as I told you beforehand, that those who practice such things will not inherit the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[22] But **the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith,**

**[23] meekness, self-control; against such things there is no law**.

[24] And those who are of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ crucified the flesh with the passions and the desires.

[25] If we live by the Spirit, by the Spirit let us also walk in line.

[26] Let us not become desirous of empty glory, provoking one another, envying one another.

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v21) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (vv6, 24) — in "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" phrases.
- **"Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free" → "For liberty the *Mashiach* set us free; stand firm therefore"** (v1) — Greek τῇ ἐλευθερίᾳ ἡμᾶς Χριστὸς ἠλευθέρωσεν· στήκετε οὖν (WH Treg NA28; the KJV follows RP). The dative is "for liberty" or "with liberty"; "liberty" and "set free" are one root.
- **"be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage" → "do not be held again in a yoke of slavery"** (v1) — Greek ἐνέχεσθε, "be held in, be caught in"; ζυγῷ δουλείας has no article.
- **"if ye be circumcised" → "if you are circumcised"; "every man that is circumcised" → "every man who is being circumcised"** (v2, 3) — Greek περιτέμνησθε, περιτεμνομένῳ, present tense: "let yourselves be circumcised". The Greek has no "accept".
- **"I testify" → "I bear witness"** (v3) — Greek μαρτύρομαι, a different verb from "testify" (μαρτυρέω, 4:15).
- **"he is a debtor to do the whole law" kept** (v3) — Greek ὀφειλέτης … ποιῆσαι; "do", not "keep".
- **"Christ is become of no effect unto you" → "You were brought to nothing, away from the *Mashiach*"** (v4) — Greek κατηργήθητε ἀπὸ Χριστοῦ: καταργέω, elsewhere "abolish" (3:17; v11), here in the passive of persons with ἀπό, "away from": "abolished" cannot be said of them in English, so "brought to nothing". "You" is the subject, not the *Mashiach*.
- **"whosoever of you are justified by the law" → "you who are being justified in law"** (v4) — Greek ἐν νόμῳ δικαιοῦσθε, present tense; the Greek has no "would be".
- **"ye are fallen from grace" → "you fell from grace"** (v4) — Greek ἐξεπέσατε, "fell out of".
- **"through the Spirit wait for the hope of righteousness by faith" → "by the Spirit, from faith, eagerly await the hope of righteousness"** (v5) — Greek πνεύματι ἐκ πίστεως … ἀπεκδεχόμεθα; πνεύματι has no article (also v16, 18, 25).
- **"availeth any thing" → "avails anything"** (v6) — Greek τι ἰσχύει, "has any strength"; the Greek has no "only".
- **"faith which worketh by love" → "faith working through love"** (v6) — Greek διʼ ἀγάπης ἐνεργουμένη.
- **"that ye should not obey the truth" → "from obeying the truth"; "This persuasion" → "The persuasion"** (v7, 8) — Greek πείθεσθαι, "be persuaded, obey", and πεισμονή: one root, and again in "I am confident" (πέποιθα, v10).
- **"I have confidence in you through the Lord, that ye will be none otherwise minded" → "I am confident toward you in the Lord that you will think nothing else"** (v10) — Greek πέποιθα εἰς ὑμᾶς ἐν κυρίῳ; φρονήσετε, "think" (as Phil 2:2).
- **"shall bear his judgment" → "will bear the sentence"** (v10) — Greek τὸ κρίμα.
- **"if I yet preach circumcision" → "if I still proclaim circumcision"** (v11) — Greek κηρύσσω.
- **"then is the offence of the cross ceased" → "Then the stumbling block of the cross has been abolished"** (v11) — Greek σκάνδαλον, the trigger of a trap, a cause of stumbling; κατήργηται, as 3:17.
- **"I would they were even cut off which trouble you" → "O that those who unsettle you would even cut themselves off!"** (v12) — Greek ὄφελον καὶ ἀποκόψονται, future middle: "cut themselves off" from the church, or "have themselves cut" (castrated; cf. Deut 23:1). The wording is left open.
- **"ye have been called unto liberty; only use not liberty for an occasion to the flesh" → "you were called for liberty, brothers; only not the liberty as an occasion for the flesh"** (v13) — Greek ἐπʼ ἐλευθερίᾳ ἐκλήθητε; the second clause has no verb.
- **"by love serve one another" → "through love be slaves to one another"** (v13) — Greek δουλεύετε.
- **"all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this" → "the whole law has been fulfilled in one word, in this"** (v14) — Greek πεπλήρωται, perfect tense: "has been fulfilled" or "is summed up".
- **"take heed that ye be not consumed" → "see that you are not consumed"** (v15) — Greek βλέπετε.
- **"Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh" → "walk by the Spirit, and you will by no means carry out the desire of the flesh"** (v16) — Greek ἐπιθυμίαν, singular; οὐ μὴ τελέσητε, the strong negation, a promise rather than a command; τελέω is "carry out" (as Jas 2:8), not "fulfill" (v14).
- **"the flesh lusteth against the Spirit" → "the flesh desires against the Spirit"** (v17) — Greek ἐπιθυμεῖ, the verb of "desire" (v16, 24); the second half has no verb.
- **"so that ye cannot do the things that ye would" → "so that you do not do whatever things you want"** (v17) — Greek ἵνα μὴ ἃ ἐὰν θέλητε ταῦτα ποιῆτε: purpose or result; the Greek has no "cannot".
- **"Adultery" omitted; "fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness" → "sexual immorality, uncleanness, licentiousness"** (v19) — "adultery" is not in the critical text; Greek πορνεία, ἀκαθαρσία, ἀσέλγεια.
- **"witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies" → "sorcery, enmities, strife, jealousy, rages, selfish ambitions, dissensions, sects"** (v20) — Greek φαρμακεία; ἔχθραι, plural; ἔρις, ζῆλος, singular in the printed text; θυμοί, plural of θυμός, "rage" (Col 3:8); ἐριθεῖαι (Phil 1:17; 2:3); διχοστασίαι; αἱρέσεις, parties formed by choice (the word of 2 Pet 2:1).
- **"Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings" → "envyings, drunkennesses, revelries"** (v21) — "murders" (+ φόνοι Treg RP) is not in the printed text (WH NA28); φθόνοι, μέθαι, κῶμοι are plurals.
- **"of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past" → "of which I tell you beforehand, just as I told you beforehand"** (v21) — Greek προλέγω … προεῖπον, one verb twice; "warn" is not in the Greek.
- **"they which do such things" → "those who practice such things"** (v21) — Greek πράσσοντες.
- **"longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith" → "patience, kindness, goodness, faith"** (v22) — Greek μακροθυμία, χρηστότης, ἀγαθωσύνη, πίστις; πίστις is "faith" throughout the letter and can also mean "faithfulness".
- **"Meekness, temperance" → "meekness, self-control"** (v23) — Greek πραΰτης (also 6:1), ἐγκράτεια.
- **"they that are Christ's" → "those who are of the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v24) — the verse follows τοῦ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ (WH Treg NA28); the printed SBLGNT has τοῦ Χριστοῦ alone (RP). A plain genitive; the Greek has no "belong".
- **"have crucified the flesh with the affections and lusts" → "crucified the flesh with the passions and the desires"** (v24) — Greek ἐσταύρωσαν; παθήμασιν, ἐπιθυμίαις; the Greek has no "its". πάθημα is elsewhere "suffering"; beside "desires" it is what one undergoes within, "passion".
- **"let us also walk in the Spirit" → "by the Spirit let us also walk in line"** (v25) — Greek στοιχῶμεν, "keep in line" (as Phil 3:16; also 6:16), a different verb from "walk" (περιπατέω) in v16.
- **"Let us not be desirous of vain glory" → "Let us not become desirous of empty glory"** (v26) — Greek μὴ γινώμεθα κενόδοξοι: κενός "empty" + δόξα "glory", as Phil 2:3.

### Notes
- **v14** quotes Lev 19:18.
