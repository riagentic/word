# Amos 2

[1] Thus says Yahweh:

"For three transgressions of Moab,
and for four, I will not revoke the punishment,
because he burned to lime
the bones of the king of Edom.

[2] So I will send a fire upon Moab,
and it shall devour the strongholds of Kerioth,
and Moab shall die amid uproar,
amid shouting and the sound of the trumpet;

[3] I will cut off the judge from its midst,
and will kill all its princes with him,"
says Yahweh.

[4] Thus says Yahweh:

"For three transgressions of Judah,
and for four, I will not revoke the punishment,
because they have rejected the Law of Yahweh
and have not kept his statutes,
but their lies have led them astray,
those after which their fathers walked.

[5] So I will send a fire upon Judah,
and it shall devour the strongholds of Jerusalem."

[6] Thus says Yahweh:

"For three transgressions of Israel,
and for four, I will not revoke the punishment,
because they sell the righteous for silver,
and the needy for a pair of sandals —

[7] those who trample the head of the poor into the dust of the earth
and turn aside the way of the afflicted;
a man and his father go in to the same girl,
so that my holy name is profaned;

[8] they lay themselves down beside every altar
on garments taken in pledge,
and in the house of their *Elohim* they drink
the wine of those who have been fined.

[9] "Yet it was I who destroyed the Amorite before them,
whose height was like the height of the cedars,
and who was as strong as the oaks;
I destroyed his fruit above
and his roots beneath.

[10] Also it was I who brought you up out of the land of Egypt
and led you forty years in the wilderness,
to possess the land of the Amorite.

[11] And I raised up some of your sons for prophets,
and some of your young men for Nazirites.
Is it not indeed so, O people of Israel?"
declares Yahweh.

[12] "But you made the Nazirites drink wine,
and commanded the prophets,
saying, 'You shall not prophesy.'

[13] "Behold, I will press you down in your place,
as a cart full of sheaves presses down.

[14] Flight shall perish from the swift,
and the strong shall not retain his strength,
nor shall the mighty save his soul;

[15] he who handles the bow shall not stand,
and he who is swift of foot shall not save himself,
nor shall he who rides the horse save his soul;

[16] and he who is stout of heart among the mighty
shall flee away naked in that day,"
declares Yahweh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v8) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the oracles are poetry.
- **"shall die with tumult" → "shall die amid uproar"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּשָׁאוֹן *beshon* = with a roar/uproar; modernized.
- **"the law of the LORD" → "the Law of Yahweh"** (v4) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת יְהוָה *Torat Yahweh*; *Torah* = instruction/law; retained in transliteration.
- **"have not kept his commandments" → "have not kept his statutes"** (v4) — Hebrew חֻקָּיו *chuqqav* = statutes/decrees; "statutes" more precise than "commandments."
- **"their lies caused them to err" → "their lies have led them astray"** (v4) — modernized.
- **"a pair of shoes" → "a pair of sandals"** (v6) — Hebrew נַעֲלַיִם *na'alayim* = sandals; "sandals" more precise.
- **"that pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor" → "those who trample the head of the poor into the dust of the earth"** (v7) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁאֲפִים עַל עֲפַר אֶרֶץ בְּרֹאשׁ דַּלִּים *hasha'afim al afar eretz berosh dallim*; "trample … into the dust" captures the violence.
- **"the meek" → "the afflicted"** (v7) — Hebrew עֲנָוִים *anavim* = humble/afflicted; "afflicted" in the context of judicial oppression.
- **"will go in unto the same maid" → "go in to the same girl"** (v7) — Hebrew הַנַּעֲרָה *hana'arah* = a young woman; "girl" modernized; the offense is sexual impropriety profaning Yahweh's name.
- **"clothes laid to pledge" → "garments taken in pledge"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּגָדִים חֲבֻלִים *begadim chavulim*; "taken in pledge" = pledged as collateral; the poor pledged their cloaks.
- **"the wine of the condemned" → "the wine of those who have been fined"** (v8) — Hebrew עֲנוּשִׁים *anushim* = those who have been fined/punished; wine from unjust fines drunk in the sanctuary.
- **"Behold, I am pressed under you, as a cart is pressed that is full of sheaves" → "Behold, I will press you down in your place, as a cart full of sheaves presses down"** (v13) — Hebrew הִנֵּה אָנֹכִי מֵעִיק תַּחְתֵּיכֶם *hinne anoki me'iq tachteykem*; Yahweh is the cart pressing them down; restructured for clarity.
- **"the strong shall not strengthen his force" → "the strong shall not retain his strength"** (v14) — modernized.
- **"he that is swift of foot shall not deliver himself" → "he who is swift of foot shall not save himself"** (v15) — modernized; *nephesh* implied.
- **"he that is courageous among the mighty" → "he who is stout of heart among the mighty"** (v16) — Hebrew אַמִּיץ לִבּוֹ *amitz libbo* = strong of heart; "stout of heart" retains the idiom.
