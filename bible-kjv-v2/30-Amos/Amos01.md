# Amos 1

[1] The words of Amos, who was among the shepherds of ⟦tekoa|Teqoa|Tekoa⟧, which he saw concerning ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the days of ⟦uzziah|Uzziyahu|Uzziah⟧ king of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧ and in the days of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ son of ⟦joash|Yoash|Joash⟧, king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, two years before the earthquake.

[2] And he said:
"⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ roars from ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧
and utters his voice from ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧;
the pastures of the shepherds mourn,
and the top of ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧ withers."

[3] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧,
and for four, I will not revoke the punishment,
because they have threshed ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧
with threshing sledges of iron.

[4] So I will send a fire upon the house of ⟦hazael|Chazael|Hazael⟧,
and it shall devour the strongholds of ⟦ben-hadad|Ben Hadad|Ben-hadad⟧.

[5] I will break the gate-bar of ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧,
and cut off the inhabitants from the Valley of Aven,
and him who holds the scepter from ⟦beth-eden|Beit Eden|Beth-eden⟧;
and the people of ⟦aram|Aram|Syria⟧ shall go into exile to ⟦kir|Qir|Kir⟧,
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[6] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧,
and for four, I will not revoke the punishment,
because they carried into exile a whole people
to deliver them up to Edom.

[7] So I will send a fire upon the wall of ⟦gaza|Azzah|Gaza⟧,
and it shall devour her strongholds.

[8] I will cut off the inhabitants from Ashdod,
and him who holds the scepter from ⟦ashkelon|Ashqelon|Ashkelon⟧;
I will turn my hand against ⟦ekron|Eqron|Ekron⟧,
and the remnant of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ shall perish,"
says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[9] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧,
and for four, I will not revoke the punishment,
because they delivered up a whole people to Edom,
and did not remember the covenant of brotherhood.

[10] So I will send a fire upon the wall of ⟦tyre|Tzor|Tyre⟧,
and it shall devour her strongholds."

[11] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of Edom,
and for four, I will not revoke the punishment,
because he pursued his brother with the sword
and cast off all pity,
and his anger tore perpetually,
and he kept his wrath forever.

[12] So I will send a fire upon Teman,
and it shall devour the strongholds of ⟦bozrah|Botzrah|Bozrah⟧."

[13] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"For three transgressions of the ⟦ammonites|Ammonim|Ammonites⟧,
and for four, I will not revoke the punishment,
because they have ripped open pregnant women in ⟦gilead|Gilad|Gilead⟧,
that they might enlarge their border.

[14] So I will kindle a fire in the wall of Rabbah,
and it shall devour her strongholds,
with shouting on the day of battle,
with a tempest in the day of the whirlwind;

[15] and their king shall go into exile,
he and his princes together,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה; *Adonai* = "my Lord"; combined title retained.
- **"herdmen" → "shepherds"** (v1) — Hebrew נֹקְדִים *noqedim* = sheep-breeders/shepherds; Amos was a pastoral figure.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the oracles are structured poetry.
- **"Thus saith the LORD" → "Thus says Yahweh"** (throughout) — prophetic messenger formula modernized throughout.
- **"I will not turn away the punishment thereof" → "I will not revoke the punishment"** (vv3, 6, 9, 11, 13) — Hebrew לֹא אֲשִׁיבֶנּוּ *lo ashivenu* = "I will not turn it back"; "revoke" more precise.
- **"threshing instruments of iron" → "threshing sledges of iron"** (v3) — Hebrew חֲרֻצוֹת הַבַּרְזֶל *charutzot habarzel* = iron threshing sledges; "sledges" more vivid than "instruments."
- **"the palaces of Ben-hadad" → "the strongholds of Ben-Hadad"** (v4) — Hebrew אַרְמְנוֹת *armonot* = citadels/strongholds; "strongholds" more precise (and consistent throughout the oracles) than "palaces."
- **"the bar of Damascus" → "the gate-bar of Damascus"** (v5) — Hebrew בְּרִיחַ *beriach* = bar/bolt securing a gate; "gate-bar" clarifies what is being broken.
- **"the plain of Aven" → "the Valley of Aven"** (v5) — Hebrew בִּקְעַת אָוֶן *biq'at aven* = Valley of Wickedness/Aven; "Valley" more literal than "plain."
- **"the house of Eden" → "Beth-Eden"** (v5) — Hebrew בֵּית עֵדֶן *Beit Eden* = the house/region of Eden; Hebrew place name rendered directly.
- **"carried away captive the whole captivity" → "carried into exile a whole people"** (v6) — Hebrew הַגְלוֹתָם גָּלוּת שְׁלֵמָה; "a whole people" makes the crime vivid; "exile" consistent.
- **"the brotherly covenant" → "the covenant of brotherhood"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּרִית אַחִים *berit achim*; "covenant of brotherhood" preserves the legal covenant language.
- **"did cast off all pity" → "cast off all pity"** (v11) — Hebrew שִׁחֵת רַחֲמָיו *shichet rachamav* = "destroyed his compassion"; "cast off all pity" conveys the hardness.
- **"the children of Ammon" → "the Ammonites"** (v13) — modernized.
- **"women with child of Gilead" → "pregnant women in Gilead"** (v13) — modernized phrasing.
- **"the palaces thereof" → "her strongholds"** (v14) — consistent *armonot* translation.
- **"shall go into captivity" → "shall go into exile"** (v15) — consistent *galah* translation.

### Notes
- **"Three transgressions and four"** — stylistic formula used 8× (1:3–2:6) moving inward to Israel.
- **Earthquake** (v1) — referenced Zech 14:5; archaeologically datable c. 760 BC.
