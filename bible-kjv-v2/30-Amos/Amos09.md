# Amos 9

[1] I saw ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ standing beside the altar, and he said:

"Strike the capitals until the thresholds shake,
and shatter them on the heads of all the people;
and those who are left of them I will kill with the sword;
not one of them shall flee away;
not one of them shall escape.

[2] "If they dig into Sheol,
from there shall my hand take them;
if they climb up to heaven,
from there I will bring them down.

[3] If they hide themselves on the top of ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧,
from there I will search them out and take them;
and if they hide from my sight at the bottom of the sea,
there I will command the serpent, and it shall bite them.

[4] And if they go into captivity before their enemies,
there I will command the sword, and it shall kill them;
and I will fix my eyes upon them
for evil and not for good."

[5] ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|The Lord GOD⟧ of Hosts,
he who touches the earth and it melts,
and all who dwell in it mourn,
and all of it rises like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧,
and sinks again, like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;

[6] who builds his upper chambers in the heavens
and founds his vault upon the earth;
who calls for the waters of the sea
and pours them out upon the surface of the earth —
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his name.

[7] "Are you not like the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ to me,
O people of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?" declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
"Did I not bring up ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ from ⟦caphtor|Kaftor|Caphtor⟧ and ⟦aram|Aram|the Syrians⟧ from ⟦kir|Qir|Kir⟧?

[8] Behold, the eyes of ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ are upon the sinful kingdom,
and I will destroy it from the surface of the ground,
except that I will not utterly destroy the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,"
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] "For behold, I will command,
and shake the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ among all the nations
as one shakes with a sieve,
but no pebble shall fall to the earth.

[10] All the sinners of my people shall die by the sword,
who say, 'Disaster shall not overtake or meet us.'

[11] "In that day I will raise up the booth of David that is fallen
and repair its breaches,
and raise up its ruins
and rebuild it as in the days of old,

[12] that they may possess the remnant of Edom
and all the nations who are called by my name,"
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who does this.

[13] "Behold, the days are coming," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"when the plowman shall overtake the reaper
and the treader of grapes him who sows the seed;
the mountains shall drip sweet wine,
and all the hills shall flow with it.

[14] I will restore the fortunes of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and they shall rebuild the ruined cities and inhabit them;
they shall plant vineyards and drink their wine,
and they shall make gardens and eat their fruit.

[15] I will plant them on their land,
and they shall never again be uprooted
out of the land that I have given them,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" / "Lord God of hosts" → "Lord Yahweh" / "Lord Yahweh of Hosts"** (vv5, 8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה; in v5 אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה הַצְּבָאוֹת *Adonai Yahweh hatztseva'ot*, with no אֱלֹהֵי.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֶיךָ (singular "your", addressed to Israel).
- **Poetry formatted as verse lines** (throughout) — KJV prose; the vision and oracles are poetry.
- **"Smite the lintel of the door, that the posts may shake" → "Strike the capitals until the thresholds shake"** (v1) — Hebrew הַךְ הַכַּפְתּוֹר וְיִרְעֲשׁוּ הַסִּפִּים *hakh hakkaftor veyira'ashu hassippim*; *kaftor* = capital/knob of a pillar (not "lintel"); *sippim* = thresholds; "capitals … thresholds" more architecturally precise.
- **"and cut them in the head, all of them" → "and shatter them on the heads of all the people"** (v1) — Hebrew וּבְצַּע בָּרֹאשׁ כֻּלָּם; shatter the capitals so they fall on the people's heads.
- **"he that fleeth of them shall not flee away, and he that escapeth of them shall not be delivered" → "not one of them shall flee away; not one of them shall escape"** (v1) — modernized.
- **"Though they dig into hell" → "If they dig into Sheol"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל *Sheol*; the underworld; "hell" is theological interpretation; *Sheol* retained.
- **"it is he that buildeth his stories in the heaven" → "who builds his upper chambers in the heavens"** (v6) — Hebrew בּוֹנֶה בַשָּׁמַיִם מַעֲלוֹתָיו *boneh vashamayim ma'alotav* = builds his upper chambers in the sky; "upper chambers/stories" = the divine dwelling above.
- **"and hath founded his troop in the earth" → "and founds his vault upon the earth"** (v6) — Hebrew וַאֲגֻדָּתוֹ עַל אֶרֶץ יְסָדָהּ *va'agudato al eretz yesadah*; *agudah* = bundle/vault/dome; "vault upon the earth" = the dome of the sky anchored to earth.
- **"saving that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob" → "except that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob"** (v8) — modernized.
- **"like as corn is sifted in a sieve" → "as one shakes with a sieve"** (v9) — Hebrew כַּאֲשֶׁר יִנּוֹעַ בַּכְּבָרָה *ka'asher yino'a bakhevara*; "shakes with a sieve" captures the separating motion.
- **"yet shall not the least grain fall upon the earth" → "but no pebble shall fall to the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew צְרוֹר *tzeror* = pebble/stone; the sieve metaphor: the worthless pebbles fall through; the righteous are retained; "pebble" more precise than "least grain."
- **"The evil shall not overtake nor prevent us" → "Disaster shall not overtake or meet us"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא תַגִּישׁ וְלֹא תַקְדִּים *lo taggish velo taqdem*; "disaster" for *ha-ra'ah*; "meet us" for *qaddem*.
- **"the tabernacle of David that is fallen" → "the booth of David that is fallen"** (v11) — Hebrew סֻכַּת דָּוִיד *sukkat David* = the booth/hut of David; *sukkah* = temporary shelter/booth; "booth" is more literal than "tabernacle" (which implies the Mosaic sanctuary).
- **"close up the breaches thereof" → "repair its breaches"** (v11) — Hebrew סָגַרְתִּי פִּרְצֵיהֶן *sagarthi pirtzeyhena*; "repair" more natural.
- **"of all the heathen, which are called by my name" → "and all the nations who are called by my name"** (v12) — Hebrew כָּל הַגּוֹיִם אֲשֶׁר נִקְרָא שְׁמִי עֲלֵיהֶם; "nations" for *goyim* consistently.
- **"Saith the Lord that doeth this" → "declares Yahweh who does this"** (v12) — modernized prophetic formula.
- **"and all the hills shall melt" → "and all the hills shall flow with it"** (v13) — Hebrew וְכָל הַגְּבָעוֹת תִּתְמוֹגַגְנָה *vekhol hageva'ot titmogegenah* = "all the hills will melt/flow"; "flow with it" (sweet wine) captures the abundance.
- **"I will bring again the captivity of my people of Israel" → "I will restore the fortunes of my people Israel"** (v14) — Hebrew שַׁבְתִּי אֶת שְׁבוּת עַמִּי יִשְׂרָאֵל; consistent with Joel 3:1: "restore the fortunes."
- **"they shall build the waste cities" → "they shall rebuild the ruined cities and inhabit them"** (v14) — Hebrew עָרִים נְשַׁמּוֹת *arim neshamot* = desolate/ruined cities; "ruined cities" more vivid.
- **"they shall no more be pulled up out of their land" → "they shall never again be uprooted out of the land"** (v15) — Hebrew לֹא יִנָּתְשׁוּ עוֹד *lo yinatshu od*; "uprooted" for *nasa'* = to uproot; matches the planting metaphor.

### Notes
- **v11-12** — quoted Acts 15:16-17 at the Jerusalem Council, supporting Gentile inclusion.
