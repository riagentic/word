# Amos 9

[1] I saw ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ standing on the altar, and he said:

"Strike the capital, and let the thresholds shake,
and cut them off on the head of all of them;
and the last of them I will kill with the sword;
there shall not flee of them one who flees,
and there shall not escape of them one who has escaped.

[2] If they dig into Sheol,
from there my hand shall take them;
and if they go up to the heavens,
from there I will bring them down.

[3] And if they hide themselves on the top of ⟦carmel|Karmel|Carmel⟧,
from there I will search them out and take them;
and if they conceal themselves from before my eyes on the floor of the sea,
from there I will command the serpent, and it shall bite them.

[4] And if they go into captivity before their enemies,
from there I will command the sword, and it shall kill them;
and I will set my eye upon them
for evil and not for good."

[5] And ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ of the Hosts,
he who touches the earth and it melts,
and all who dwell in it mourn,
and it rises like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧, all of it,
and sinks like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;

[6] who builds in the heavens his upper steps,
and his vault he has founded on the earth;
who calls to the waters of the sea,
and pours them out on the face of the earth:
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his name.

[7] "Are you not like the sons of the ⟦cushites|Kushim|Cushites⟧ to me,
sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?" declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.
"Did I not bring up ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧,
and the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧ from ⟦caphtor|Kaftor|Caphtor⟧, and ⟦aram|Aram|the Syrians⟧ from ⟦kir|Qir|Kir⟧?

[8] Behold, the eyes of ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ are on the sinful kingdom,
and I will destroy it from upon the face of the ground;
except that I will not utterly destroy the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,"
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] "For behold, I am commanding,
and I will shake the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ among all the nations,
as one shakes with the sieve,
and not a pebble shall fall to the earth.

[10] By the sword all the sinners of my people shall die,
who say, 'The evil shall not come near nor come to meet us.'

[11] In that day I will raise up the booth of David that is fallen,
and I will wall up their breaches,
and his ruins I will raise up,
and I will build it as in the days of old,

[12] so that they may possess the remnant of Edom
and all the nations over whom my name is called,"
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who does this.

[13] "Behold, days are coming," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
"and the plowman shall draw near to the reaper,
and the treader of grapes to him who draws the seed;
and the mountains shall drip sweet wine,
and all the hills shall melt.

[14] And I will restore the fortunes of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,
and they shall build the desolate cities and dwell in them;
and they shall plant vineyards and drink their wine,
and they shall make gardens and eat their fruit.

[15] And I will plant them on their ground,
and they shall not be uprooted again
from upon their ground that I have given them,"
says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv5, 8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words; v5 וַאדֹנָי יְהוִה הַצְּבָאוֹת "and the Lord Yahweh of the Hosts" (with the article, no אֱלֹהֵי); v1 אֲדֹנָי alone: token `adonai`.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v15) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֶיךָ (singular "your", addressed to Israel).
- **Poetic structure** — the vision and the oracles set as verse; v1a stays a prose line.
- **Vocatives without "O"** (v7) — the Hebrew has no word for it (reviewer row).
- **"I saw the Lord standing upon the altar" → "I saw the Lord standing on the altar"** (v1) — Hebrew נִצָּב עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ (נִצָּב "stand, be stationed", row; עַל "on", also "by": the old "beside" chose).
- **"Smite the lintel of the door, that the posts may shake" → "Strike the capital, and let the thresholds shake"** (v1) — Hebrew הַךְ, a singular command (who is addressed is not said); הַכַּפְתּוֹר "the capital" (singular: the knob of a pillar); וְיִרְעֲשׁוּ, a jussive; הַסִּפִּים "the thresholds".
- **"and cut them in the head, all of them; and I will slay the last of them with the sword" → "and cut them off on the head of all of them; and the last of them I will kill with the sword"** (v1) — Hebrew וּבְצַעַם בְּרֹאשׁ כֻּלָּם (בָּצַע "cut off, break off"; "them": the capitals, or the people, the verse does not say); אַחֲרִיתָם "the last of them" (as 4:2); הָרַג "kill" (row).
- **"he that fleeth of them shall not flee away, and he that escapeth of them shall not be delivered" → "there shall not flee of them one who flees, and there shall not escape of them one who has escaped"** (v1) — Hebrew לֹא־יָנוּס לָהֶם נָס וְלֹא־יִמָּלֵט לָהֶם פָּלִיט, each verb with its own noun (נוּס "flee", נִמְלַט "escape", פָּלִיט "one who has escaped": rows).
- **"Though they dig into hell … though they climb up to heaven" → "If they dig into Sheol … and if they go up to the heavens"** (v2) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (plain word, OT.md); הַשָּׁמַיִם "the heavens" (row); יַעֲלוּ "go up" (עָלָה); וְאִם "and if" (the וְ kept, vv2–4).
- **"And though they hide themselves in the top of Carmel … and though they be hid from my sight in the bottom of the sea, thence will I command the serpent" → "And if they hide themselves on the top of Carmel … and if they conceal themselves from before my eyes on the floor of the sea, from there I will command the serpent"** (v3) — Hebrew יֵחָבְאוּ "hide themselves" (נֶחְבָּא, row), יִסָּתְרוּ "conceal themselves" (נִסְתַּר: two verbs); חִפֵּשׂ "search out"; מִנֶּגֶד עֵינַי "from before my eyes"; קַרְקַע הַיָּם "the floor of the sea"; מִשָּׁם "from there" (each time, vv2–4).
- **"thence will I command the sword … and I will set mine eyes upon them for evil" → "from there I will command the sword … and I will set my eye upon them for evil and not for good"** (v4) — Hebrew מִשָּׁם; וְשַׂמְתִּי עֵינִי "and I will set my eye" (singular); לְרָעָה "for evil" (רָעָה, row).
- **"And the Lord GOD of hosts is he that toucheth the land, and it shall melt … and it shall rise up wholly like a flood; and shall be drowned, as by the flood of Egypt" → "And the Lord Yahweh of the Hosts, he who touches the earth and it melts … and it rises like the Nile, all of it, and sinks like the Nile of Egypt"** (v5) — Hebrew הַנּוֹגֵעַ "he who touches" (נָגַע, row); וַתָּמוֹג "and it melts" (מוּג); כַיְאֹר "like the Nile" (token; as 8:8); שָׁקְעָה "sinks" (as the qere of 8:8).
- **"It is he that buildeth his stories in the heaven, and hath founded his troop in the earth" → "who builds in the heavens his upper steps, and his vault he has founded on the earth"** (v6) — Hebrew qere מַעֲלוֹתָיו "his steps" (ketiv מעלותו, singular; מַעֲלָה "step", row; "upper rooms, stories" is the sense taken from the context); אֲגֻדָּתוֹ "his vault" (lit. "his bundle, band": what is bound together; uncertain); יְסָדָהּ "he has founded it", a perfect; עַל "on".
- **"he that calleth for the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth" → "who calls to the waters of the sea, and pours them out on the face of the earth"** (v6) — as 5:8, the same Hebrew line.
- **"Are ye not as children of the Ethiopians unto me, O children of Israel?" → "Are you not like the sons of the Cushites to me, sons of Israel?"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּבְנֵי כֻשִׁיִּים "like sons of Cushites" (Standing decisions: "sons of"); בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "sons of Israel". **"Have not I brought up Israel" → "Did I not bring up Israel"** — הֶעֱלֵיתִי "bring up"; פְלִשְׁתִּיִּים, אֲרָם without articles (the tokens keep their usual forms).
- **"Behold, the eyes of the Lord GOD are upon the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from off the face of the earth" → "Behold, the eyes of the Lord Yahweh are on the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from upon the face of the ground"** (v8) — Hebrew מֵעַל פְּנֵי הָאֲדָמָה "from upon the face of the ground" (אֲדָמָה, row; not "earth"); הִשְׁמִיד "destroy" (row). **"saving that I will not utterly destroy" → "except that I will not utterly destroy"** — אֶפֶס כִּי "except that"; הַשְׁמֵיד אַשְׁמִיד, literally "destroying I will destroy" (infinitive absolute; under a negation "surely" would read as "not certainly": "utterly" carries it).
- **"For, lo, I will command, and I will sift the house of Israel among all nations, like as corn is sifted in a sieve, yet shall not the least grain fall upon the earth" → "For behold, I am commanding, and I will shake the house of Israel among all the nations, as one shakes with the sieve, and not a pebble shall fall to the earth"** (v9) — Hebrew מְצַוֶּה, a participle; וַהֲנִעוֹתִי "I will make sway, shake" and יִנּוֹעַ "is shaken" (נוּעַ, row: "stagger" in 4:8; 8:12 of men walking, "shake" here of a sieve: one verb, two senses in English); בַּכְּבָרָה "with the sieve"; צְרוֹר "pebble" (or "kernel": what the sieve keeps back is debated); "corn" is not written; וְלֹא "and not".
- **"The evil shall not overtake nor prevent us" → "The evil shall not come near nor come to meet us"** (v10) — Hebrew לֹא־תַגִּישׁ וְתַקְדִּים בַּעֲדֵינוּ הָרָעָה: two hifil forms used without object ("come near", "come before"); בַּעֲדֵינוּ "about us" (also "on our account"); הָרָעָה "the evil" (רָעָה, row; "disaster" was a gloss); בַּחֶרֶב first.
- **"In that day will I raise up the tabernacle of David that is fallen, and close up the breaches thereof; and I will raise up his ruins, and I will build it as in the days of old" → "In that day I will raise up the booth of David that is fallen, and I will wall up their breaches, and his ruins I will raise up, and I will build it as in the days of old"** (v11) — Hebrew סֻכַּת דָּוִיד "the booth of David" (סֻכָּה, row); הַנֹּפֶלֶת, a participle ("that is falling" or "that is fallen"); גָּדַרְתִּי "I will wall up"; the suffixes as printed: פִּרְצֵיהֶן "their (feminine plural) breaches", הֲרִסֹתָיו "his ruins", וּבְנִיתִיהָ "I will build it (feminine)"; כִּימֵי עוֹלָם "as the days of old" (the עוֹלָם row).
- **"That they may possess the remnant of Edom, and of all the heathen, which are called by my name" → "so that they may possess the remnant of Edom and all the nations over whom my name is called"** (v12) — Hebrew לְמַעַן "so that" (row); יִירְשׁוּ "possess" (יָרַשׁ, row); שְׁאֵרִית אֱדוֹם "the remnant of Edom", as the Hebrew stands; נִקְרָא שְׁמִי עֲלֵיהֶם "my name has been called over them" (ownership). Acts 15:16–17 quotes the Greek, which reads "that the rest of men may seek (the Lord)" (אָדָם "man" for אֱדוֹם, יִדְרְשׁוּ "seek" for יִירְשׁוּ; from memory); not put into the verse. **"saith the LORD that doeth this" → "declares Yahweh who does this"** — נְאֻם־יְהוָה עֹשֶׂה זֹּאת.
- **"Behold, the days come, saith the LORD, that the plowman shall overtake the reaper, and the treader of grapes him that soweth seed; and the mountains shall drop sweet wine, and all the hills shall melt" → "Behold, days are coming, declares Yahweh, and the plowman shall draw near to the reaper, and the treader of grapes to him who draws the seed; and the mountains shall drip sweet wine, and all the hills shall melt"** (v13) — Hebrew הִנֵּה יָמִים בָּאִים, no article; וְנִגַּשׁ "and shall draw near" (נָגַשׁ, row); מֹשֵׁךְ הַזָּרַע "him who draws the seed" (the Ps 126:6 row: uncertain, the one who scatters or carries the seed); הִטִּיף "drip" (row); עָסִיס "sweet wine"; תִּתְמוֹגַגְנָה "shall melt" (מוּג, as v5; "flow with it" added an object).
- **"And I will bring again the captivity of my people of Israel" → "And I will restore the fortunes of my people Israel"** (v14) — Hebrew וְשַׁבְתִּי אֶת־שְׁבוּת, the reviewer row "restore the fortunes": literally "turn back the captivity / the turning" (שְׁבוּת from שָׁבָה or from שׁוּב); traditional "turn the captivity".
- **"and they shall build the waste cities, and inhabit them" → "and they shall build the desolate cities and dwell in them"** (v14) — Hebrew וּבָנוּ "and they shall build" ("re-" not written); נְשַׁמּוֹת "desolate" (שָׁמֵם, row); וְיָשָׁבוּ "and dwell" (יָשַׁב, row; "in them" supplied); each "and" kept.
- **"And I will plant them upon their land, and they shall no more be pulled up out of their land which I have given them" → "And I will plant them on their ground, and they shall not be uprooted again from upon their ground that I have given them"** (v15) — Hebrew אַדְמָתָם "their ground" twice (אֲדָמָה, row); לֹא יִנָּתְשׁוּ עוֹד "they shall not be uprooted again" (נָתַשׁ; "never" says more than עוֹד); מֵעַל "from upon"; אָמַר "says".

### Notes
- **vv11–12** — quoted in Acts 15:16–17 (James at Jerusalem), from the Greek; the Hebrew and the Greek differ (see Changes, v12).
