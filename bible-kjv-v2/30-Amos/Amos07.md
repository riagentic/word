# Amos 7

[1] This is what ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ showed me: behold, he was forming locusts when the latter growth was just beginning to sprout, and behold, it was the latter growth after the king's mowings.

[2] When they had finished eating the grass of the land, I said,

"O ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, please forgive!
How can ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ stand?
He is so small!"

[3] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ relented concerning this: "It shall not be," said ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] This is what ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ showed me: behold, ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ was calling for a judgment by fire, and it devoured the great deep and was eating up the land.

[5] Then I said,

"O ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|Lord GOD⟧, please cease!
How can ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ stand?
He is so small!"

[6] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ relented concerning this: "This also shall not be," said ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[7] This is what he showed me: behold, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ was standing beside a wall built with a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand.

[8] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, "Amos, what do you see?" And I said, "A plumb line." Then ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧ said,

"Behold, I am setting a plumb line
in the midst of my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
I will never again pass by them;

[9] the high places of ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ shall be made desolate,
and the sanctuaries of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall be laid waste,
and I will rise against the house of ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ with the sword."

[10] Then ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ the priest of ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ sent to ⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ king of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧. The land is not able to bear all his words.

[11] For thus Amos has said, '⟦jeroboam|Yarovam|Jeroboam⟧ shall die by the sword, and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ must go into exile away from his land.'"

[12] And ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧ said to Amos, "O seer, go, flee away to the land of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and eat bread there, and prophesy there,

[13] but never again prophesy at ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, for it is the king's sanctuary, and it is a temple of the kingdom."

[14] Then Amos answered and said to ⟦amaziah|Amatzyahu|Amaziah⟧, "I was no prophet, nor a prophet's son, but I was a herdsman and a dresser of sycamore figs.

[15] But ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ took me from following the flock, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me, 'Go, prophesy to my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.'

[16] Now therefore hear the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. You say, 'Do not prophesy against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and do not preach against the house of ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧.'

[17] "Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"'Your wife shall be a prostitute in the city,
and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword,
and your land shall be divided up with a measuring line;
you yourself shall die in an unclean land,
and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall surely go into exile away from its land.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה; used consistently for vision introductions and Amos's intercessions.
- **"Thus hath the Lord God shewed unto me" → "This is what Lord Yahweh showed me"** (vv1, 4) — modernized prophetic vision formula.
- **"he formed grasshoppers" → "he was forming locusts"** (v1) — Hebrew יוֹצֵר גֹּבַי *yotzer govay* = forming a locust swarm; "locusts" more specific than "grasshoppers."
- **"in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth" → "when the latter growth was just beginning to sprout"** (v1) — modernized.
- **"by whom shall Jacob arise? for he is small" → "How can Jacob stand? He is so small"** (v2) — Hebrew מִי יָקוּם יַעֲקֹב כִּי קָטֹן הוּא; "How can Jacob stand?" conveys the existential question; "so small" preserves the pathos.
- **"The LORD repented for this" → "Yahweh relented concerning this"** (v3, 6) — Hebrew נִחַם יְהוָה *nicham Yahweh* = Yahweh relented/changed course; "relented" avoids the theologically misleading "repented" for divine action; same as Joel 2:13.
- **"the Lord God called to contend by fire" → "Lord Yahweh was calling for a judgment by fire"** (v4) — Hebrew קֹרֵא לָרִיב בָּאֵשׁ *qore lariv ba'esh*; *riv* = legal contest/judgment; "judgment by fire" preserves the forensic metaphor.
- **"it devoured the great deep, and did eat up a part" → "it devoured the great deep and was eating up the land"** (v4) — Hebrew תֹּאכַל אֶת הַחֵלֶק *tokhel et hachelaq*; "eating up the land" — *chelaq* = portion/land.
- **"I will not again pass by them any more" → "I will never again pass by them"** (v8) — modernized.
- **"Amos hath conspired against thee" → "Amos has conspired against you"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"the land is not able to bear all his words" → "The land is not able to bear all his words"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"Israel shall surely be led away captive" → "Israel must go into exile"** (v11) — Hebrew גָּלֹה יִגְלֶה *galo yigleh*; "exile" consistent.
- **"O thou seer" → "O seer"** (v12) — archaic pronoun dropped.
- **"go, flee thee away into the land of Judah" → "go, flee away to the land of Judah"** (v12) — modernized.
- **"it is the king's chapel, and it is the king's court" → "it is the king's sanctuary, and it is a temple of the kingdom"** (v13) — Hebrew מִקְדַּשׁ מֶלֶךְ *miqdash melekh* = royal sanctuary; בֵּית מַמְלָכָה *beit mamlakhah* = house of the kingdom; "chapel" too weak for *miqdash*.
- **"I was an herdman, and a gatherer of sycomore fruit" → "I was a herdsman and a dresser of sycamore figs"** (v14) — Hebrew בּוֹלֵס שִׁקְמִים *boles shiqmim* = one who tends/dresses sycamore figs; "dresser" more precise.
- **"the Lord took me as I followed the flock" → "Yahweh took me from following the flock"** (v15) — Hebrew מֵאַחֲרֵי הַצֹּאן *me'achari hattzon*; "from following the flock" more vivid.
- **"drop not thy word against the house of Isaac" → "do not preach against the house of Isaac"** (v16) — Hebrew וְלֹא תַטִּיף עַל בֵּית יִצְחָק *velo tettif al beit Yitzhaq*; *nataph* = to drip/preach; "preach" is the standard prophetic term.
- **"Thy wife shall be an harlot in the city" → "Your wife shall be a prostitute in the city"** (v17) — modernized; "prostitute" consistent.
- **"thy land shall be divided by line" → "your land shall be divided up with a measuring line"** (v17) — Hebrew בַּחֶבֶל תְּחֻלָּק *bachevel techulaq*; "measuring line" more vivid.
- **"thou shalt die in a polluted land" → "you yourself shall die in an unclean land"** (v17) — Hebrew אֶרֶץ טְמֵאָה *eretz tema'ah* = unclean/impure land; "unclean land" preserves the ritual connotation.
