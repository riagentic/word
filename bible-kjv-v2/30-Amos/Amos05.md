# Amos 5

[1] Hear this word that I am lifting up over you, a dirge, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[2] "She has fallen; she shall not rise again,
the virgin of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
she is abandoned on her ground;
there is none to raise her up."

[3] For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

"The city that goes out a thousand
shall have a hundred left,
and that which goes out a hundred
shall have ten left,
to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[4] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

"Seek me and live;

[5] and do not seek ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧,
and to Gilgal do not go in,
and to ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧ do not pass over;
for Gilgal shall surely go into exile,
and ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ shall become wickedness."

[6] Seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and live,
lest he rush like fire on the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧,
and it devour, and there is none to quench it for ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧,

[7] those who turn judgment to wormwood,
and lay down righteousness to the earth.

[8] He who makes ⟦pleiades|Kimah|the Pleiades⟧ and ⟦orion|Kesil|Orion⟧,
and turns the shadow of death into morning,
and darkens day into night;
who calls to the waters of the sea,
and pours them out on the face of the earth:
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his name;

[9] who makes havoc flash upon the strong,
and havoc comes upon the fortress.

[10] They hate him who reproves in the gate,
and him who speaks blamelessly they abominate.

[11] Therefore, because you trample on the poor
and take from him a levy of grain,
houses of hewn stone you have built,
and you shall not dwell in them;
desirable vineyards you have planted,
and you shall not drink their wine.

[12] For I know how many are your transgressions
and how mighty are your sins:
those who harass the righteous, who take a ransom,
and turn aside the needy in the gate.

[13] Therefore the prudent shall be silent in that time,
for it is an evil time.

[14] Seek good, and not evil,
so that you may live;
and so may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, be with you,
as you have said.

[15] Hate evil, and love good,
and set up judgment in the gate;
perhaps ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts,
will show favor to the remnant of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[16] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧:

"In all squares there shall be lamenting,
and in all streets they shall say, 'Alas! Alas!'
And they shall call the farmer to mourning,
and to lamenting those who know wailing,

[17] and in all vineyards there shall be lamenting,
for I will pass through your midst," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] **Woe to those who desire the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!**
Why then is the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for you?
It is darkness, and not light,

[19] as when a man flees from the face of the lion,
and the bear meets him,
and he comes into the house and leans his hand on the wall,
and the serpent bites him.

[20] Is not the day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ darkness, and not light,
and gloom, and no brightness to it?

[21] "I hate, I reject your feasts,
and I will not smell your solemn assemblies.

[22] For even if you offer up to me burnt offerings and your grain offerings,
I will not be pleased with them;
and the peace offering of your fatlings
I will not look upon.

[23] Take away from upon me the noise of your songs;
and the music of your harps I will not hear.

[24] **And let judgment roll on like waters,
and righteousness like an ever-flowing wadi.**

[25] "Sacrifices and grain offering did you bring near to me in the wilderness forty years, house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[26] And you shall lift up ⟦sikkuth|Sikkut|Sikkuth⟧ your king, and Kiyyun your images, the star of your gods, which you made for yourselves,

[27] and I will carry you into exile beyond ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts is his name.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv14, 15, 16, 27) — Hebrew *Elohim*; v26 אֱלֹהֵיכֶם of what they made: plain "gods".
- **"the LORD, the God of hosts, the Lord" → "Yahweh, Elohim of Hosts, Adonai"** (v16) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת אֲדֹנָי, the three names in their order; "of Hosts" plain (the prophets' formula row); no article before "Elohim".
- **Poetic structure** — the dirge and the oracles set as verse; vv1, 3a, 4a, 16a and vv25–27 stay prose lines.
- **Vocatives without "O"** (vv1, 25) — the Hebrew has no word for it (reviewer row).
- **"which I take up against you, even a lamentation" → "that I am lifting up over you, a dirge"** (v1) — Hebrew אָנֹכִי נֹשֵׂא עֲלֵיכֶם קִינָה, a participle; נָשָׂא קִינָה "lift up a dirge" (the קִינָה row); עֲלֵיכֶם "over you" (also "against you").
- **"The virgin of Israel is fallen; she shall no more rise: she is forsaken upon her land" → "She has fallen; she shall not rise again, the virgin of Israel; she is abandoned on her ground"** (v2) — Hebrew נָפְלָה, the verb first; לֹא־תוֹסִיף קוּם "she shall not add to rise" (the יָסַף row); נִטְּשָׁה (נָטַשׁ "abandon", row); אַדְמָתָהּ "her ground" (אֲדָמָה, row); אֵין מְקִימָהּ "there is none raising her".
- **"The city that went out by a thousand shall leave an hundred" → "The city that goes out a thousand shall have a hundred left"** (v3) — Hebrew הַיֹּצֵאת, a participle; תַּשְׁאִיר "shall leave remaining" (hifil of שָׁאַר); לְבֵית יִשְׂרָאֵל "to (for) the house of Israel" stands at the end, as printed.
- **"Seek ye me, and ye shall live" → "Seek me and live"** (vv4, 6) — Hebrew דִּרְשׁוּנִי וִחְיוּ, two imperatives.
- **"But seek not Bethel, nor enter into Gilgal, and pass not to Beersheba" → "and do not seek Bethel, and to Gilgal do not go in, and to Beersheba do not pass over"** (v5) — Hebrew וְאַל … וְ … לֹא … וּ … לֹא, each "and" kept, the place first as printed; עָבַר "pass over" (row); הַגִּלְגָּל with the article (as 4:4).
- **"for Gilgal shall surely go into captivity, and Beth-el shall come to nought" → "for Gilgal shall surely go into exile, and Bethel shall become wickedness"** (v5) — Hebrew גָּלֹה יִגְלֶה, literally "going into exile shall go into exile" (infinitive absolute: "surely", Standing decisions); the play of sound הַגִּלְגָּל גָּלֹה cannot be kept; לְאָוֶן "for wickedness" (אָוֶן, row; also "trouble, nothing": KJV "nought"; a play on Beth-aven, Hos 4:15).
- **"lest he break out like fire in the house of Joseph, and devour it, and there be none to quench it in Beth-el" → "lest he rush like fire on the house of Joseph, and it devour, and there is none to quench it for Bethel"** (v6) — Hebrew יִצְלַח, the צָלַח "rush upon" of the spirit (row), here with no preposition: "on" supplied; וְאָכְלָה "and it (the fire) devour", feminine; לְבֵית־אֵל "for Bethel".
- **"Ye who turn judgment to wormwood, and leave off righteousness in the earth" → "those who turn judgment to wormwood, and lay down righteousness to the earth"** (v7) — Hebrew הַהֹפְכִים, a participle with the article, third person (no "you" written); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (row; was "justice"); הִנִּיחוּ "they have laid down" (הִנִּיחַ, row), לָאָרֶץ "to the earth".
- **"Seek him that maketh the seven stars and Orion, and turneth the shadow of death into the morning" → "He who makes Kimah and Kesil, and turns the shadow of death into morning"** (v8) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה, a participle ("made" was past; "Seek him" is not written); כִּימָה · כְּסִיל (tokens: the Pleiades and Orion are the usual identification, uncertain); צַלְמָוֶת "the shadow of death" (as pointed: the Job 3 row; "deep darkness" from the reading צַלְמוּת, in Changes only).
- **"and maketh the day dark with night: that calleth for the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth: The LORD is his name" → "and darkens day into night; who calls to the waters of the sea, and pours them out on the face of the earth: Yahweh is his name"** (v8) — Hebrew הַקּוֹרֵא לְ "who calls to"; וַיִּשְׁפְּכֵם, a wayyiqtol after the participle, rendered as present with the hymn; עַל־פְּנֵי הָאָרֶץ "on the face of the earth" (kept; not "surface"); יְהוָה שְׁמוֹ, the hymn line as 4:13; 9:6.
- **"That strengtheneth the spoiled against the strong, so that the spoiled shall come against the fortress" → "who makes havoc flash upon the strong, and havoc comes upon the fortress"** (v9) — Hebrew הַמַּבְלִיג (hifil of בָּלַג "shine, smile": "make flash"; uncertain); שֹׁד "havoc" (row); עָז "the strong" (row), מִבְצָר "fortress" (row); "so that" is not written.
- **"They hate him that rebuketh in the gate, and they abhor him that speaketh uprightly" → "They hate him who reproves in the gate, and him who speaks blamelessly they abominate"** (v10) — Hebrew מוֹכִיחַ (הוֹכִיחַ "reprove"); דֹּבֵר תָּמִים "one who speaks blameless(ly)" (תָּמִים, row; "the truth" was a gloss); יְתָעֵבוּ "they abominate" (תִּעֵב, row), the object first.
- **"Forasmuch therefore as your treading is upon the poor, and ye take from him burdens of wheat" → "Therefore, because you trample on the poor and take from him a levy of grain"** (v11) — Hebrew לָכֵן יַעַן בּוֹשַׁסְכֶם עַל־דָּל; בּוֹשַׁס is found only here (from בּוּס "trample", or "levy rent": uncertain); מַשְׂאַת־בַּר "a levy (lit. 'a lifting') of grain" (בָּר "grain", row).
- **"ye have built houses of hewn stone, but ye shall not dwell in them; ye have planted pleasant vineyards" → "houses of hewn stone you have built, and you shall not dwell in them; desirable vineyards you have planted"** (v11) — Hebrew the objects first; גָּזִית "hewn stone" (row); יָשַׁב "dwell" (row); כַּרְמֵי־חֶמֶד "vineyards of desire" (root of חָמַד, row); וְלֹא "and … not" (the וְ kept).
- **"how mighty are your sins: they afflict the just, they take a bribe, and they turn aside the poor in the gate from their right" → "how mighty are your sins: those who harass the righteous, who take a ransom, and turn aside the needy in the gate"** (v12) — Hebrew עֲצֻמִים "mighty" (עָצוּם, row); צֹרְרֵי צַדִּיק "harassers of the righteous" (צָרַר, row); כֹפֶר "ransom" (row: the price paid to buy off a guilty man; "bribe" is שֹׁחַד); הִטּוּ "they turn aside"; "from their right" is not written.
- **"Therefore the prudent shall keep silence in that time" → "Therefore the prudent shall be silent in that time"** (v13) — Hebrew הַמַּשְׂכִּיל (הִשְׂכִּיל "have insight", row), יִדֹּם "shall be silent"; בָּעֵת הַהִיא "in that time".
- **"that ye may live: and so the LORD, the God of hosts, shall be with you" → "so that you may live; and so may Yahweh, Elohim of Hosts, be with you"** (v14) — Hebrew לְמַעַן "so that" (row); וִיהִי־כֵן, a jussive form: a wish, not a statement.
- **"establish judgment in the gate: it may be that the LORD God of hosts will be gracious unto the remnant of Joseph" → "set up judgment in the gate; perhaps Yahweh, Elohim of Hosts, will show favor to the remnant of Joseph"** (v15) — Hebrew הַצִּיגוּ "set" (הִצִּיג); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (row); אוּלַי "perhaps" (row); יֶחֱנַן (חָנַן "show favor to", row); שְׁאֵרִית "remnant" (row).
- **"Wailing shall be in all streets; and they shall say in all the highways, Alas! alas!" → "In all squares there shall be lamenting, and in all streets they shall say, 'Alas! Alas!'"** (v16) — Hebrew רְחֹבוֹת "squares", חוּצוֹת "streets" (the רְחוֹב row), no article; מִסְפֵּד "lamenting" (noun of סָפַד "lament", row; vv16, 17 the same word); הוֹ־הוֹ "Alas! Alas!".
- **"and they shall call the husbandman to mourning, and such as are skilful of lamentation to wailing" → "And they shall call the farmer to mourning, and to lamenting those who know wailing"** (v16) — Hebrew אִכָּר "farmer" (singular); אֵבֶל "mourning"; יוֹדְעֵי נֶהִי "those who know wailing" (נְהִי, the Jer 9–12 row; "skilful" says more than "know"; the professional mourners are meant).
- **"for I will pass through thee, saith the LORD" → "for I will pass through your midst, says Yahweh"** (v17) — Hebrew אֶעֱבֹר בְּקִרְבְּךָ (עָבַר, row: "pass through" by the sense, as Exod 12:12); אָמַר יְהוָה "says Yahweh" (not נְאֻם).
- **"Woe unto you that desire the day of the LORD! to what end is it for you?" → "Woe to those who desire the day of Yahweh! Why then is the day of Yahweh for you?"** (v18) — Hebrew הוֹי הַמִּתְאַוִּים "Woe to those who desire" (the הוֹי row; third person; the hitpael of the אִוָּה "desire" row); לָמָּה־זֶּה לָכֶם יוֹם יְהוָה "why then (is) the day of Yahweh for you?" (the second "day of Yahweh" kept); הוּא "it is".
- **"As if a man did flee from a lion, and a bear met him; or went into the house, and leaned his hand on the wall, and a serpent bit him" → "as when a man flees from the face of the lion, and the bear meets him, and he comes into the house and leans his hand on the wall, and the serpent bites him"** (v19) — Hebrew מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (row); the article on each animal and on the house and the wall, as printed; וּבָא … וְסָמַךְ … וּנְשָׁכוֹ, each "and" kept (no "or"); סָמַךְ יָד "lay, lean the hand" (row).
- **"even very dark, and no brightness in it" → "and gloom, and no brightness to it"** (v20) — Hebrew וְאָפֵל "and gloom" (root of אֲפֵלָה, row), וְלֹא־נֹגַהּ לוֹ.
- **"I hate, I despise your feast days, and I will not smell in your solemn assemblies" → "I hate, I reject your feasts, and I will not smell your solemn assemblies"** (v21) — Hebrew מָאַסְתִּי (מָאַס "reject", row); לֹא אָרִיחַ בְּ "I will not smell (in)" (הֵרִיחַ "smell", row; the old "take no delight" gave a gloss for the word); עֲצֶרֶת "solemn assembly" (row; uncertain).
- **"Though ye offer me burnt offerings and your meat offerings, I will not accept them: neither will I regard the peace offerings of your fat beasts" → "For even if you offer up to me burnt offerings and your grain offerings, I will not be pleased with them; and the peace offering of your fatlings I will not look upon"** (v22) — Hebrew כִּי אִם "for even if" (not the כִּי אִם after a negative); תַּעֲלוּ "you offer up" (hifil of עָלָה); עֹלוֹת without "your", מִנְחֹתֵיכֶם with it; לֹא אֶרְצֶה (רָצָה "be pleased with", row; not "accept"); שֶׁלֶם, the singular "peace offering" (only here); מְרִיאֵיכֶם "your fatlings" (מְרִיא, row); הִבִּיט "look upon" (row).
- **"Take thou away from me the noise of thy songs; for I will not hear the melody of thy viols" → "Take away from upon me the noise of your songs; and the music of your harps I will not hear"** (v23) — Hebrew הָסֵר, singular (as are the suffixes); מֵעָלַי "from upon me"; הֲמוֹן "noise" (the הָמוֹן "multitude" row is for a crowd; here the sound: note); זִמְרַת "the music of" (זִמְרָה, row); נֵבֶל "harp" (row; kind uncertain); וְ … לֹא אֶשְׁמָע "and … I will not hear" ("for" is not written).
- **"But let judgment run down as waters, and righteousness as a mighty stream" → "And let judgment roll on like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing wadi"** (v24) — Hebrew וְיִגַּל כַּמַּיִם מִשְׁפָּט וּצְדָקָה כְּנַחַל אֵיתָן, word for word: וְיִגַּל, a jussive (גָּלַל "roll", row); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (row); נַחַל אֵיתָן "an ever-flowing wadi" (the Deut 21:4 / Amos 5:24 row: a stream bed that never runs dry; "a mighty, hard" also defended for אֵיתָן).
- **"Have ye offered unto me sacrifices and offerings in the wilderness forty years" → "Sacrifices and grain offering did you bring near to me in the wilderness forty years"** (v25) — Hebrew הַזְּבָחִים וּמִנְחָה הִגַּשְׁתֶּם־לִי, a question, the objects first; הִגִּישׁ "bring near" (the hifil of נָגַשׁ, row); מִנְחָה singular (grain offering, row). Whether the question expects "no" or "yes" the Hebrew does not say.
- **"But ye have borne the tabernacle of your Moloch and Chiun your images, the star of your god" → "And you shall lift up Sikkut your king, and Kiyyun your images, the star of your gods"** (v26) — Hebrew וּנְשָׂאתֶם אֵת סִכּוּת מַלְכְּכֶם וְאֵת כִּיּוּן צַלְמֵיכֶם כּוֹכַב אֱלֹהֵיכֶם, as pointed: סִכּוּת and כִּיּוּן are read as names (the pointing is that of שִׁקּוּץ "detestable thing"; Akkadian star names are commonly compared); סֻכַּת "the booth of" is another reading (KJV "tabernacle"); מַלְכְּכֶם "your king" (KJV "your Moloch" reads the word as a name); וּנְשָׂאתֶם, a weqatal: future ("you shall lift up", carried into exile) or past going on from v25 ("you lifted up"): the Hebrew allows both; אֱלֹהֵיכֶם "your gods" (or "your god"). Acts 7:42–43 quotes the Greek: "the tent of Moloch and the star of the god Rephan" (from memory); not put into the verse.
- **"Therefore will I cause you to go into captivity beyond Damascus, saith the LORD, whose name is The God of hosts" → "and I will carry you into exile beyond Damascus, says Yahweh; Elohim of Hosts is his name"** (v27) — Hebrew וְהִגְלֵיתִי (הִגְלָה "carry into exile", row); מֵהָלְאָה לְ "beyond"; אָמַר יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי־צְבָאוֹת שְׁמוֹ, the name line as 4:13.

### Notes
- **vv25–27** — quoted in Acts 7:42–43 (Stephen), from the Greek; Acts 7:43 has "beyond Babylon" (from memory).
