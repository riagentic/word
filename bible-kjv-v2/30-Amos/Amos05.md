# Amos 5

[1] Hear this word that I take up over you in lamentation, O house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[2] "Fallen, no more to rise,
is the virgin ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
forsaken on her land,
with none to raise her up."

[3] For thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

"The city that went out a thousand
shall have a hundred left,
and that which went out a hundred
shall have ten left
to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[4] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

"Seek me and live;

[5] but do not seek ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧,
and do not enter into Gilgal
or cross over to ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧;
for Gilgal shall surely go into exile,
and ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧ shall come to nothing."

[6] Seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and live,
lest he break out like fire in the house of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧,
and it devour, with none to quench it for ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧,

[7] O you who turn justice to wormwood
and cast down righteousness to the earth!

[8] He who made ⟦pleiades|Kimah|the Pleiades⟧ and ⟦orion|Kesil|Orion⟧,
and turns deep darkness into the morning
and darkens the day into night,
who calls for the waters of the sea
and pours them out on the surface of the earth,
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is his name;

[9] who makes destruction flash forth against the strong,
so that destruction comes upon the fortress.

[10] They hate him who reproves in the gate,
and they abhor him who speaks the truth.

[11] Therefore because you trample on the poor
and you exact taxes of grain from him,
you have built houses of hewn stone,
but you shall not dwell in them;
you have planted pleasant vineyards,
but you shall not drink their wine.

[12] For I know how many are your transgressions
and how great are your sins —
you who afflict the righteous, who take a bribe,
and turn aside the needy in the gate.

[13] Therefore he who is prudent will keep silent in such a time,
for it is an evil time.

[14] Seek good, and not evil,
that you may live;
and so ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, will be with you,
as you have said.

[15] Hate evil, and love good,
and establish justice in the gate;
it may be that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts,
will be gracious to the remnant of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[16] Therefore thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, ⟦adonai|Adonai|the Lord⟧:

"In all the squares there shall be wailing,
and in all the streets they shall say, 'Alas! Alas!'
They shall call the farmers to mourning
and to wailing those who are skilled in lamentation,

[17] and in all vineyards there shall be wailing,
for I will pass through your midst," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] **Woe to you who desire the Day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧!**
Why would you have the Day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?
It is darkness, and not light,

[19] as if a man fled from a lion,
and a bear met him,
or went into the house and leaned his hand against the wall,
and a serpent bit him.

[20] Is not the Day of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ darkness, and not light,
and gloom with no brightness in it?

[21] "I hate, I despise your feasts,
and I take no delight in your solemn assemblies.

[22] Even though you offer me your burnt offerings and grain offerings,
I will not accept them;
and the peace offerings of your fattened animals,
I will not look upon them.

[23] Take away from me the noise of your songs;
to the melody of your harps I will not listen.

[24] **But let justice roll down like waters,
and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream.**

[25] "Did you bring to me sacrifices and offerings during the forty years in the wilderness, O house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[26] You shall take up ⟦sikkuth|Sikkut|Sikkuth⟧ your king, and Kiyyun your star-god — your images that you made for yourselves,

[27] and I will send you into exile beyond ⟦damascus|Dammeseq|Damascus⟧," says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, whose name is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v3) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה.
- **"Lord, the God of hosts, the Lord" → "Yahweh, the God of Hosts, Lord"** (v16) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי הַצְּבָאוֹת אֲדֹנָי; the triple divine formula preserved.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the lament and oracles are poetry.
- **"which I take up against you, even a lamentation" → "that I take up over you in lamentation"** (v1) — Hebrew קִינָה *qinah* = funeral dirge; "over" captures the dirge-singer's posture.
- **"The virgin of Israel is fallen; she shall no more rise: she is forsaken upon her land; there is none to raise her up" → "Fallen, no more to rise, is the virgin Israel; forsaken on her land, with none to raise her up"** (v2) — restructured to open with the shocking verb "Fallen"; Hebrew word order emphasized.
- **"shall go into captivity" → "shall surely go into exile"** (v5) — Hebrew גִּלֹה יִגְלֶה *gilo yigleh* = going into exile; "exile" consistent.
- **"Beth-el shall come to nought" → "Bethel shall come to nothing"** (v5) — modernized.
- **"turneth the shadow of death into the morning" → "turns deep darkness into the morning"** (v8) — Hebrew צַלְמָוֶת *tzalmavet* = death-shadow/deep darkness; "deep darkness" more vivid.
- **"That strengtheneth the spoiled against the strong, so that the spoiled shall come against the fortress" → "who makes destruction flash forth against the strong, so that destruction comes upon the fortress"** (v9) — Hebrew הַמַּבְלִיג שֹׁד עַל עָז *hammalig shod al az*; *shod* = destruction/violence; the verb means to cause devastation to flash out; restructured.
- **"him that rebuketh in the gate" → "him who reproves in the gate"** (v10) — Hebrew מוֹכִיחַ *mokhiach* = one who reproves/corrects; "reproves" more precise.
- **"they abhor him that speaketh uprightly" → "they abhor him who speaks the truth"** (v10) — Hebrew דֹּבֵר תָּמִים *dover tamim* = one who speaks integrity; "truth" clearer.
- **"Forasmuch therefore as your treading is upon the poor" → "Therefore because you trample on the poor"** (v11) — Hebrew יַעַן בּוֹשַׁסְכֶם עַל דָּל *ya'an boshaskhem al dal*; modernized.
- **"ye take from him burdens of wheat" → "you exact taxes of grain from him"** (v11) — Hebrew מַשְׂאַת בַּר *mas'at bar* = levy of grain; "taxes of grain" captures the economic extraction.
- **"they afflict the just" → "you who afflict the righteous"** (v12) — Hebrew צַדִּיק *tzaddiq*; "righteous" more precise.
- **"they turn aside the poor in the gate from their right" → "turn aside the needy in the gate"** (v12) — Hebrew דְּרָשִׁים מֵאָבְיוֹן בַּשַּׁעַר; modernized.
- **"the prudent shall keep silence" → "he who is prudent will keep silent"** (v13) — Hebrew הַמַּשְׂכִּיל *hammaskhil* = the discerning/prudent one; modernized.
- **"establish judgment in the gate" → "establish justice in the gate"** (v15) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט *mishpat* = justice; "justice" consistent.
- **"Wailing shall be in all streets" → "In all the squares there shall be wailing"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּכָל רְחֹבוֹת *bkhol rechovot* = in all broad spaces/squares; "squares" more accurate than "streets."
- **"they shall call the husbandman to mourning, and such as are skilful of lamentation to wailing" → "They shall call the farmers to mourning and to wailing those who are skilled in lamentation"** (v16) — Hebrew אִכָּר אֶל אֵבֶל וּמִסְפֵּד *ikkar el evel umisped*; "farmers" for *ikkar*; professional mourners implied.
- **"the day of the LORD" → "the Day of Yahweh"** (vv18, 20) — consistent eschatological formula.
- **"I will not smell in your solemn assemblies" → "I take no delight in your solemn assemblies"** (v21) — Hebrew לֹא אָרִיחַ *lo ariyach* = I will not smell/savor; the sacrificial odor metaphor replaced with equivalent modern idiom.
- **"your meat offerings" → "grain offerings"** (v22) — Hebrew מִנְחוֹת *minchot*; "grain offerings" for *minchah* consistently.
- **"the peace offerings of your fat beasts" → "the peace offerings of your fattened animals"** (v22) — Hebrew שְׁלָמֵי מְרִיאֵיכֶם; "fattened animals" more precise than "fat beasts."
- **"the melody of thy viols" → "the melody of your harps"** (v23) — Hebrew נְבָלֶיךָ *nevalekha* = your harps/lyres; "harps" more natural.
- **"let judgment run down as waters" → "let justice roll down like waters"** (v24) — Hebrew יִגַּל כַּמַּיִם מִשְׁפָּט *yiggal kamayim mishpat*; "roll" more vivid than "run"; "justice" for *mishpat*.
- **"a mighty stream" → "an ever-flowing stream"** (v24) — Hebrew נַחַל אֵיתָן *nachal eitan* = a perennial/ever-flowing stream; "ever-flowing" more precise than "mighty."
- **"will I cause you to go into captivity" → "I will send you into exile"** (v27) — consistent *galah* = exile.

### Notes
- **v24** — famously invoked by Martin Luther King Jr.
- **v25-27** — quoted Acts 7:42-43 (Stephen).
