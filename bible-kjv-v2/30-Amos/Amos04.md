# Amos 4

[1] "Hear this word, you cows of Bashan,
who are on the mountain of Samaria,
who oppress the poor, who crush the needy,
who say to your husbands, 'Bring, that we may drink!'

[2] Lord Yahweh has sworn by his holiness
that, behold, the days are coming upon you,
when they shall take you away with hooks,
even the last of you with fishhooks.

[3] And you shall go out through the breaches,
each one straight ahead;
and you shall be cast out into Harmon,"
declares Yahweh.

[4] "Come to Bethel, and transgress;
to Gilgal, and multiply transgression;
bring your sacrifices every morning,
your tithes every three days;

[5] offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving of that which is leavened,
and proclaim freewill offerings, publish them;
for so you love to do, O people of Israel!"
declares Lord Yahweh.

[6] "I gave you cleanness of teeth in all your cities,
and lack of bread in all your places,
yet you did not return to me," declares Yahweh.

[7] "I also withheld the rain from you
when there were yet three months to the harvest;
I would send rain on one city,
and send no rain on another city;
one field would have rain,
and the field on which it did not rain would wither;

[8] so two or three cities would wander to another city
to drink water, and would not be satisfied;
yet you did not return to me," declares Yahweh.

[9] "I struck you with blight and mildew;
your many gardens and your vineyards, your fig trees and your olive trees
the locust devoured;
yet you did not return to me," declares Yahweh.

[10] "I sent among you a pestilence after the manner of Egypt;
I killed your young men with the sword,
and carried away your horses,
and I made the stench of your camp go up into your nostrils;
yet you did not return to me," declares Yahweh.

[11] "I overthrew some of you,
as *Elohim* overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah,
and you were as a brand plucked out of the burning;
yet you did not return to me," declares Yahweh.

[12] "Therefore thus I will do to you, O Israel;
because I will do this to you,
**prepare to meet your *Elohim*, O Israel!**"

[13] For behold, he who forms the mountains and creates the wind,
and declares to man what is his thought,
who makes the morning darkness,
and treads on the heights of the earth —
Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Hosts, is his name!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv2, 5) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv11, 12) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the entire chapter is poetry.
- **"ye kine of Bashan" → "you cows of Bashan"** (v1) — Hebrew פָּרוֹת הַבָּשָׁן *parot haBashan* = cows of Bashan; deliberately harsh metaphor for wealthy women who oppress the poor; "cows" retained.
- **"which say to their masters" → "who say to your husbands"** (v1) — Hebrew הָאֹמְרוֹת לַאֲדֹנֵיהֶם; *adoneyhem* = their lords/husbands; the women command their husbands.
- **"he will take you away with hooks, and your posterity with fishhooks" → "they shall take you away with hooks, even the last of you with fishhooks"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּצִנּוֹת *betzinnot* = hooks; בְּסִירוֹת דּוּגָה *besiroth dugah* = fishhooks; "even the last of you" more vivid.
- **"ye shall cast them into the palace" → "you shall be cast out into Harmon"** (v3) — Hebrew הַהַרְמוֹנָה *haharmona*; uncertain location; "Harmon" transliterated; KJV "palace" is speculative.
- **"your tithes after three years" → "your tithes every three days"** (v4) — Hebrew לִשְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים *lishlosheth yamim* = "every three days"; ironic hyper-religious frequency; KJV "three years" follows a different reading.
- **"offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving with leaven" → "offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving of that which is leavened"** (v5) — Hebrew מֵחָמֵץ *mechametez* = of/from leavened things; the irony is offering leavened bread as thanksgiving.
- **"proclaim and publish the free offerings" → "proclaim freewill offerings, publish them"** (v5) — Hebrew נְדָבוֹת *nedavot* = freewill offerings; restructured for clarity.
- **"for this liketh you" → "for so you love to do"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי כֵן אֲהַבְתֶּם; modernized.
- **"want of bread" → "lack of bread"** (v6) — modernized; "want" in 1611 meant "lack" but is ambiguous now.
- **"three months to the harvest" → "yet three months to the harvest"** (v7) — retained "yet" for emphasis.
- **"one piece was rained upon" → "one field would have rain"** (v7) — modernized.
- **"the palmerworm devoured them" → "the locust devoured"** (v9) — Hebrew הַגָּזָם *hagazam* = the cutting locust; one of the four locust terms from Joel 1:4; rendered as "locust" since context is blight not plague sequence.
- **"as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah" → "as *Elohim* overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah"** (v11) — Hebrew כְּמַהְפֵּכַת אֱלֹהִים; *Elohim* retained.
- **"ye were as a firebrand plucked out of the burning" → "you were as a brand plucked out of the burning"** (v11) — Hebrew כְּאוּד מֻצָּל מִשְּׂרֵפָה *ke'ud mutzal misrefah* = a burning stick snatched from fire; "brand" more vivid than "firebrand."
- **"prepare to meet thy God" → "prepare to meet your *Elohim*"** (v12) — Hebrew הִכּוֹן לִקְרַאת אֱלֹהֶיךָ; *Elohim* retained; bold formula emphasized.
- **"he that formeth the mountains, and createth the wind" → "he who forms the mountains and creates the wind"** (v13) — modernized.
- **"that maketh the morning darkness" → "who makes the morning darkness"** (v13) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה שַׁחַר עֵיפָה; "morning darkness" = turning dawn into gloom.
- **"treadeth upon the high places of the earth" → "treads on the heights of the earth"** (v13) — modernized.
- **"The LORD, The God of hosts, is his name" → "Yahweh, the God of Hosts, is his name"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת שְׁמוֹ; consistent naming formula.
