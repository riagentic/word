# Amos 4

[1] "Hear this word, cows of Bashan,
who are on the mountain of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧,
who oppress the poor, who crush the needy,
who say to their lords, 'Bring, and let us drink!'

[2] ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|The Lord GOD⟧ has sworn by his holiness
that, behold, days are coming upon you,
and he shall lift you away with hooks,
and the last of you with fishhooks.

[3] And through the breaches you shall go out,
each woman straight before her;
and you shall cast to the Harmon,"
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[4] "Come to ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧, and transgress;
at Gilgal multiply transgressing;
and bring your sacrifices every morning,
your tithes every three days;

[5] and turn into smoke a thanksgiving of what is leavened,
and call out freewill offerings, make them heard;
for so you love, sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧,"
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[6] "And I also, I gave you cleanness of teeth in all your cities,
and lack of bread in all your places;
yet you did not turn back to me," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[7] "And I also, I held back the rain from you
when there were yet three months to the harvest;
and I would make it rain on one city,
and on another city I would not make it rain;
one portion would be rained on,
and the portion on which it did not rain would dry up;

[8] and two, three cities would stagger to one city
to drink water, and would not be sated;
yet you did not turn back to me," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] "I struck you with blight and with mildew;
your many gardens and your vineyards, and your fig trees and your olive trees
the cutting locust devoured;
yet you did not turn back to me," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] "I sent among you pestilence in the way of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;
I killed your young men with the sword,
together with the captivity of your horses;
and I made the stench of your camps go up, even into your nostrils;
yet you did not turn back to me," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[11] "I overthrew among you,
like the overthrow by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧ and ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧,
and you were like a brand snatched from the burning;
yet you did not turn back to me," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] "Therefore thus I will do to you, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
because this I will do to you,
**prepare to meet your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧!**"

[13] For behold, he who forms mountains and creates wind,
and tells man what is his thought,
who makes dawn darkness,
and treads on the high places of the earth:
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts, is his name.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv2, 5) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv11, 12, 13) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **Poetic structure** — the chapter set as verse, one Hebrew line per printed line.
- **Vocatives without "O"** (vv1, 5, 12) — the Hebrew has no word for it (reviewer row).
- **"ye kine of Bashan" → "cows of Bashan"** (v1) — Hebrew פָּרוֹת הַבָּשָׁן, the women addressed as cows; the participles that follow are feminine. עָשַׁק "oppress", רָצַץ "crush"; דַּלִּים "the poor", אֶבְיוֹנִים "the needy" (rows).
- **"which say to their masters, Bring, and let us drink" → "who say to their lords, 'Bring, and let us drink!'"** (v1) — Hebrew לַאֲדֹנֵיהֶם "to their lords" (third person, as printed; the husbands are meant: the old "your husbands" changed the person and gave the sense for the word); הָבִיאָה "bring" (singular), וְנִשְׁתֶּה "and let us drink".
- **"that, lo, the days shall come upon you, that he will take you away with hooks, and your posterity with fishhooks" → "that, behold, days are coming upon you, and he shall lift you away with hooks, and the last of you with fishhooks"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי הִנֵּה יָמִים בָּאִים, no article, a participle; וְנִשָּׂא "and he shall lift (away)": the subject is not named (also read as impersonal "one shall"); צִנּוֹת "hooks" and סִירוֹת דּוּגָה "fishhooks" (lit. "thorns of fishing") are both uncertain ("shields", "baskets", "pots" are also proposed); אַחֲרִיתְכֶן "the last of you / your end" (feminine; אֶתְכֶם before it is masculine, as printed).
- **"And ye shall go out at the breaches, every cow at that which is before her; and ye shall cast them into the palace" → "And through the breaches you shall go out, each woman straight before her; and you shall cast to the Harmon"** (v3) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה נֶגְדָּהּ "a woman before her" (each one straight ahead; "woman" kept, not "cow"); וְהִשְׁלַכְתֶּנָה is active ("you shall cast"), with no object written: the passive "you shall be cast" is a repointing (hophal), named here only; הַהַרְמוֹנָה "to the Harmon", with the article: the place or thing is unknown ("Hermon", "the palace" are guesses).
- **"Come to Bethel, and transgress; at Gilgal multiply transgression" → "Come to Bethel, and transgress; at Gilgal multiply transgressing"** (v4) — Hebrew פִּשְׁעוּ "transgress" (verb of פֶּשַׁע "transgression", row); הַגִּלְגָּל with the article; הַרְבּוּ לִפְשֹׁעַ "multiply to transgress".
- **"bring your sacrifices every morning, and your tithes after three years" → "and bring your sacrifices every morning, your tithes every three days"** (v4) — Hebrew לַבֹּקֶר "for the morning", לִשְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים "for three days" (KJV "years" for יָמִים: the word is "days"); וְהָבִיאוּ "and bring" (the וְ kept). מַעֲשֵׂר "tithe" (the due of the law: row).
- **"offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving with leaven, and proclaim and publish the free offerings" → "and turn into smoke a thanksgiving of what is leavened, and call out freewill offerings, make them heard"** (v5) — Hebrew וְקַטֵּר (infinitive used as a command) "turn into smoke" (the הִקְטִיר row; "offer" names no act); תּוֹדָה "thanksgiving" (row); מֵחָמֵץ "of what is leavened" (חָמֵץ row; leaven was not to be turned into smoke, Lev 2:11); קִרְאוּ "call out"; הַשְׁמִיעוּ "make heard" (הִשְׁמִיעַ row); נְדָבוֹת "freewill offerings" (row).
- **"for this liketh you, O ye children of Israel" → "for so you love, sons of Israel"** (v5) — Hebrew כִּי כֵן אֲהַבְתֶּם, a perfect of state; בְּנֵי "sons of" (Standing decisions).
- **"And I also have given you" → "And I also, I gave you"** (v6, as v7) — Hebrew וְגַם־אֲנִי, the pronoun written.
- **"yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the LORD" → "yet you did not turn back to me, declares Yahweh"** (vv6, 8, 9, 10, 11) — Hebrew וְלֹא־שַׁבְתֶּם עָדַי נְאֻם־יְהוָה, the refrain in one wording five times; שׁוּב "turn back" (row); עָדַי "to me" (lit. "as far as me"); נְאֻם "declares" (row).
- **"want of bread" → "lack of bread"** (v6) — Hebrew חֹסֶר לֶחֶם; נִקְיוֹן שִׁנַּיִם "cleanness of teeth" (nothing to eat), kept.
- **"I have withholden the rain from you" → "I held back the rain from you"** (v7) — Hebrew מָנַעְתִּי (מָנַע "hold back", row). **"I caused it to rain upon one city" → "I would make it rain on one city"** — weqatal and yiqtol of repeated action; הִמְטִיר "cause to rain" (row). **"one piece was rained upon" → "one portion would be rained on"** — חֶלְקָה "portion" (row); תִּיבָשׁ "would dry up" (יָבֵשׁ row). עִיר אֶחָת … עִיר אַחַת "one city … one city": "another" for the second, as English needs.
- **"So two or three cities wandered unto one city … but they were not satisfied" → "and two, three cities would stagger to one city … and would not be sated"** (v8) — Hebrew וְנָעוּ (נוּעַ "sway, stagger", row); שְׁתַּיִם שָׁלֹשׁ "two, three", no "or"; שָׂבַע "be sated" (row).
- **"I have smitten you with blasting and mildew: when your gardens … increased, the palmerworm devoured them" → "I struck you with blight and with mildew; your many gardens … the cutting locust devoured"** (v9) — Hebrew שִׁדָּפוֹן "blight", יֵרָקוֹן "mildew" (the Deut 28:22 row); הַרְבּוֹת, an infinitive "making many" (the multitude of your gardens); "I laid waste" (הֶחֱרַבְתִּי) is a conjecture, not followed; הַגָּזָם "the cutting locust" (as Joel 1:4; the kind is uncertain).
- **"I have sent among you the pestilence after the manner of Egypt … and have taken away your horses; and I have made the stink of your camps to come up unto your nostrils" → "I sent among you pestilence in the way of Egypt … together with the captivity of your horses; and I made the stench of your camps go up, even into your nostrils"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּדֶרֶךְ "in the way of" (as Egypt was struck); עִם שְׁבִי סוּסֵיכֶם "with the captivity of your horses" (no verb "carried away"); מַחֲנֵיכֶם "your camps" (plural); וּבְאַפְּכֶם "and into your nose" (אַף of the body, Standing decisions).
- **"I have overthrown some of you, as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and ye were as a firebrand plucked out of the burning" → "I overthrew among you, like the overthrow by Elohim of Sodom and Gomorrah, and you were like a brand snatched from the burning"** (v11) — Hebrew הָפַכְתִּי בָכֶם "I overthrew among you" ("some" is not written); כְּמַהְפֵּכַת אֱלֹהִים "like the overthrow of Elohim", a noun (הָפַךְ · הֲפֵכָה row); אוּד "brand"; מֻצָּל "snatched"; שְׂרֵפָה "burning" (row).
- **"Therefore thus will I do unto thee, O Israel: and because I will do this unto thee" → "Therefore thus I will do to you, Israel; because this I will do to you"** (v12) — Hebrew עֵקֶב כִּי "because"; זֹאת first.
- **"For, lo, he that formeth the mountains, and createth the wind, and declareth unto man what is his thought" → "For behold, he who forms mountains and creates wind, and tells man what is his thought"** (v13) — Hebrew יוֹצֵר "forms" (יָצַר row), בֹרֵא "creates" (בָּרָא row), no articles; רוּחַ "wind" (the one word for spirit, wind, breath); מַגִּיד "tells"; שֵׂחוֹ "his thought" (found only here; whose thought, man's or God's, the Hebrew leaves open).
- **"that maketh the morning darkness, and treadeth upon the high places of the earth" → "who makes dawn darkness, and treads on the high places of the earth"** (v13) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂה שַׁחַר עֵיפָה (also read "makes dawn and darkness"); בָּמֳתֵי "high places" (row).
- **"The LORD, The God of hosts, is his name" → "Yahweh, Elohim of Hosts, is his name"** (v13) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי־צְבָאוֹת שְׁמוֹ, no article; the hymn line closes as 5:8; 9:6.
