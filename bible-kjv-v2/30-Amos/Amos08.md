# Amos 8

[1] This is what ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ showed me: behold, a basket of summer fruit.

[2] And he said, "Amos, what do you see?" And I said, "A basket of summer fruit." Then ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to me,

"The end has come upon my people ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;
I will never again pass by them.

[3] The songs of the temple shall become wailings in that day,"
declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.
"So many dead bodies! They are thrown everywhere!
Silence!"

[4] Hear this, you who trample on the needy
and bring the poor of the land to an end,

[5] saying, "When will the new moon be over,
that we may sell grain?
And the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧,
that we may offer wheat for sale,
that we may make the ephah small and the shekel great
and deal deceitfully with false balances,

[6] that we may buy the poor for silver
and the needy for a pair of sandals
and sell the chaff of the wheat?"

[7] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ has sworn by the pride of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧:
"Surely I will never forget any of their deeds."

[8] Shall not the land tremble on this account,
and everyone mourn who dwells in it,
and all of it rise like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧,
and be tossed about and sink again, like the ⟦nile|Yeor|Nile⟧ of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧?

[9] "And on that day," declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧,
"I will make the sun go down at noon
and darken the earth in broad daylight.

[10] I will turn your feasts into mourning
and all your songs into lamentation;
I will bring sackcloth on every waist
and baldness on every head;
I will make it like the mourning for an only son
and the end of it like a bitter day.

[11] "Behold, the days are coming," declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧,
"when I will send a famine on the land —
not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water,
but of hearing the words of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] They shall wander from sea to sea,
and from north to east;
they shall run to and fro, to seek the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but they shall not find it.

[13] "In that day the lovely virgins and the young men
shall faint for thirst.

[14] Those who swear by the guilt of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧,
and say, 'As your god lives, O Dan,'
and, 'As the Way of ⟦beersheba|Beer Sheva|Beersheba⟧ lives,'
they shall fall, and never rise again."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv1, 3, 9, 11) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (vv4-14) — KJV prose; vv4-14 are oracular poetry.
- **"Thus hath the Lord God shewed unto me" → "This is what Lord Yahweh showed me"** (v1) — modernized vision formula; consistent with ch. 7.
- **"the songs of the temple shall be howlings in that day" → "The songs of the temple shall become wailings in that day"** (v3) — Hebrew שִׁירוֹת הֵיכָל יְיֵלִילוּ *shirot heikhal yeylelilu*; "wailings" more precise than "howlings."
- **"there shall be many dead bodies in every place; they shall cast them forth with silence" → "So many dead bodies! They are thrown everywhere! Silence!"** (v3) — Hebrew הַרְבֵּה הַגְּוִיָּה בְּכָל מָקוֹם הִשְׁלִיךְ הָס; dramatic exclamations rendered as such; "Silence!" = *has* = command for silence.
- **"ye that swallow up the needy" → "you who trample on the needy"** (v4) — Hebrew הַשֹּׁאֲפִים אֶבְיוֹן *hasha'afim evyon* = those who pant/trample on the poor; "trample" more vivid.
- **"sell corn" → "sell grain"** (v5) — Hebrew שֶׁבֶר *shever* = grain; "grain" not "corn" (American English: corn = maize).
- **"falsifying the balances by deceit" → "deal deceitfully with false balances"** (v5) — Hebrew לְעַוֵּת מֹאזְנֵי מִרְמָה; modernized.
- **"and sell the refuse of the wheat" → "and sell the chaff of the wheat"** (v6) — Hebrew מַפַּל בַּר *mappal bar* = the fallen grain/chaff; "chaff" captures the worthlessness of what they sell.
- **"The LORD hath sworn by the excellency of Jacob" → "Yahweh has sworn by the pride of Jacob"** (v7) — Hebrew גְּאוֹן יַעֲקֹב *ge'on Ya'aqov*; consistent with Amos 6:8.
- **"it shall rise up wholly as a flood; and it shall be cast out and drowned, as by the flood of Egypt" → "and all of it rise like the Nile, and be tossed about and sink again, like the Nile of Egypt"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּיאֹר *ke'or* = like the Nile; "Nile" explicit; "tossed and sink" captures the rising and falling of the flood.
- **"I will darken the earth in the clear day" → "darken the earth in broad daylight"** (v9) — modernized.
- **"I will bring up sackcloth upon all loins" → "I will bring sackcloth on every waist"** (v10) — Hebrew שַׂק עַל כָּל מָתְנַיִם *saq al kol matnayim*; "waist" for *matnayim* = loins/hips; "every waist" more natural.
- **"as the mourning of an only son" → "like the mourning for an only son"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"Behold, the days come" → "Behold, the days are coming"** (v11) — Hebrew הִנֵּה יָמִים בָּאִים; modernized.
- **"a famine in the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the LORD" → "a famine on the land — not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of Yahweh"** (v11) — dash used to mark the extended explanation.
- **"they shall run to and fro to seek the word of the LORD, and shall not find it" → "they shall run to and fro, to seek the word of Yahweh, but they shall not find it"** (v12) — modernized.
- **"the fair virgins and young men shall faint for thirst" → "the lovely virgins and the young men shall faint for thirst"** (v13) — Hebrew הַבְּתוּלֹת הַיָּפוֹת *habbetulot hayafot* = lovely virgins; "lovely" more precise.
- **"The manner of Beer–sheba liveth" → "the Way of Beersheba lives"** (v14) — Hebrew דֶּרֶךְ בְּאֵר שֶׁבַע *derekh Be'er Sheva* = the Way of Beersheba; a pilgrimage route; "Way" more precise than "manner."
- **"they shall fall, and never rise up again" → "they shall fall, and never rise again"** (v14) — modernized.
