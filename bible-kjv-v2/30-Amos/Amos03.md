# Amos 3

[1] Hear this word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken against you, sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, against the whole clan that I brought up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, saying:

[2] "Only you have I known
of all the clans of the ground;
therefore I will visit on you
all your iniquities.

[3] Do two walk together
unless they have met by appointment?

[4] Does a lion roar in the forest
when it has no prey?
Does a young lion utter its voice from its den
unless it has captured?

[5] Does a bird fall on the trap of the earth
when there is no snare for it?
Does a trap spring up from the ground
and capture nothing at all?

[6] Is a ⟦shofar|shofar|trumpet⟧ blown in a city,
and the people do not tremble?
Is there evil in a city,
and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has not done it?

[7] For ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧ does not do a thing
except he has revealed his counsel
to his servants the prophets.

[8] A lion has roared;
who will not fear?
⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|The Lord GOD⟧ has spoken;
who will not prophesy?"

[9] Make it heard on the citadels in Ashdod and on the citadels in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and say, "Gather yourselves on the mountains of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧, and see the great tumults in its midst, and the extorted within it."

[10] "And they do not know how to do what is straight," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "those who store up violence and havoc in their citadels."

[11] Therefore thus says ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧:

"An adversary, and around the land!
And he shall bring down your strength from you,
and your citadels shall be plundered."

[12] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧:

"As the shepherd delivers from the mouth of the lion
two shanks or a piece of an ear,
so shall the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ be delivered, who sit in ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧,
on the corner of a bed and on the damask of a couch.

[13] Hear, and testify against the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧," declares ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the Hosts,

[14] "that on the day I visit the transgressions of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on him,
I will visit on the altars of ⟦bethel|Beit El|Bethel⟧,
and the horns of the altar shall be hewn off
and fall to the ground.

[15] And I will strike the winter house with the summer house,
and the houses of ivory shall perish,
and many houses shall come to an end,"
declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v7, 8, 11, 13) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words. **"the God of hosts" → "the Elohim of the Hosts"** (v13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי הַצְּבָאוֹת, with the article ("of the Hosts").
- **"O children of Israel, against the whole family which I brought up from the land of Egypt" → "sons of Israel, against the whole clan that I brought up from the land of Egypt"** (v1) — no "O"; בְּנֵי "sons of"; מִשְׁפָּחָה "clan" (also v2); עַל "against".
- **"You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities" → "Only you have I known of all the clans of the ground; therefore I will visit on you all your iniquities"** (v2) — Hebrew רַק אֶתְכֶם יָדַעְתִּי "only you have I known" (יָדַע, the plain word: the knowing that chose them, Gen 18:19); מִשְׁפְּחוֹת הָאֲדָמָה "the clans of the ground", as Gen 12:3; פָּקַד עַל "visit on" (as v14); עָוֹן "iniquity".
- **"Can two walk together, except they be agreed?" → "Do two walk together unless they have met by appointment?"** (v3) — Hebrew בִּלְתִּי אִם "unless" (also v4); נוֹעָדוּ "have met by appointment" (נוֹעַד).
- **"will a young lion cry out of his den, if he have taken nothing?" → "Does a young lion utter its voice from its den unless it has captured?"** (v4) — Hebrew כְּפִיר "young lion"; נָתַן קוֹלוֹ "utter its voice" (as 1:2); מְעוֹנָה "den"; לָכַד "capture", without object; טֶרֶף "prey".
- **"Can a bird fall in a snare upon the earth, where no gin is for him? shall one take up a snare from the earth, and have taken nothing at all?" → "Does a bird fall on the trap of the earth when there is no snare for it? Does a trap spring up from the ground and capture nothing at all?"** (v5) — Hebrew פַּח "trap" (the fowler's), מוֹקֵשׁ "snare" (here perhaps the bait or the trigger; uncertain); הָאָרֶץ "the earth", הָאֲדָמָה "the ground"; עָלָה "go up, spring up"; וְלָכוֹד לֹא יִלְכּוֹד, literally "and capturing it does not capture".
- **"Shall a trumpet be blown in the city, and the people not be afraid? shall there be evil in a city, and the LORD hath not done it?" → "Is a shofar blown in a city, and the people do not tremble? Is there evil in a city, and Yahweh has not done it?"** (v6) — Hebrew אִם … וְ, the question and its "and"; עִיר without the article; חָרַד "tremble" (Joel 2:1, 10 has רָגַז, the same English); רָעָה "evil" (the harm that comes; not "disaster").
- **"Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets" → "For Lord Yahweh does not do a thing except he has revealed his counsel to his servants the prophets"** (v7) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; לֹא … כִּי אִם "not … except" (here it limits: nothing is done without it); דָּבָר "a thing"; גָּלָה "reveal" (perfect: "has revealed"); סוֹד "counsel" (the close council and what is spoken in it, Jer 23:18, 22); עֲבָדָיו "his servants" (עֶבֶד of Yahweh).
- **"The lion hath roared, who will not fear? the Lord GOD hath spoken, who can but prophesy?" → "A lion has roared; who will not fear? Lord Yahweh has spoken; who will not prophesy?"** (v8) — Hebrew אַרְיֵה without the article; מִי לֹא יִנָּבֵא "who will not prophesy" (no "can but").
- **"Publish in the palaces at Ashdod, and in the palaces in the land of Egypt, and say, Assemble yourselves upon the mountains of Samaria, and behold the great tumults in the midst thereof, and the oppressed in the midst thereof" → "Make it heard on the citadels in Ashdod and on the citadels in the land of Egypt, and say, Gather yourselves on the mountains of Samaria, and see the great tumults in its midst, and the extorted within it"** (v9) — Hebrew הִשְׁמִיעַ "make heard"; עַל "on"; אַרְמוֹן "citadel" (also v10, 11); הֵאָסְפוּ "gather yourselves"; מְהוּמֹת "tumults"; עֲשׁוּקִים "the extorted" (passive of עָשַׁק "extort"); בְּתוֹכָהּ "in its midst", בְּקִרְבָּהּ "within it" (two words).
- **"For they know not to do right, saith the LORD, who store up violence and robbery in their palaces" → "And they do not know how to do what is straight, declares Yahweh, those who store up violence and havoc in their citadels"** (v10) — Hebrew וְלֹא "and not"; נְכֹחָה "what is straight" (straight ahead, upright); נְאֻם יְהוָה "declares Yahweh"; אָצַר "store up"; חָמָס "violence", שֹׁד "havoc".
- **"An adversary there shall be even round about the land; and he shall bring down thy strength from thee, and thy palaces shall be spoiled" → "An adversary, and around the land! And he shall bring down your strength from you, and your citadels shall be plundered"** (v11) — Hebrew צַר וּסְבִיב הָאָרֶץ, a cry without a verb: "an adversary — and all around the land"; the Greek and others read יְסוֹבֵב "shall surround" (from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. עֹז "strength"; נָבֹזּוּ "be plundered" (בָּזַז); "you" feminine singular (Samaria).
- **"As the shepherd taketh out of the mouth of the lion two legs, or a piece of an ear; so shall the children of Israel be taken out that dwell in Samaria in the corner of a bed, and in Damascus in a couch" → "As the shepherd delivers from the mouth of the lion two shanks or a piece of an ear, so shall the sons of Israel be delivered, who sit in Samaria, on the corner of a bed and on the damask of a couch"** (v12) — Hebrew הִצִּיל "deliver" (snatch away), the same verb twice; כְּרָעַיִם "shanks"; בְּדַל "piece" (only here); הַיֹּשְׁבִים "who sit" (on the furniture named; also "who dwell"); פְּאַת מִטָּה "the corner of a bed" (פֵּאָה "edge"); בִדְמֶשֶׁק is spelled and pointed unlike the city (דַּמֶּשֶׂק, 1:3, 5) and is taken as a fabric named from the city, "damask" (KJV "in Damascus"); עֶרֶשׂ "couch"; uncertain.
- **"Hear ye, and testify in the house of Jacob" → "Hear, and testify against the house of Jacob"** (v13) — Hebrew הֵעִיד בְּ "testify against" (as Ps 50:7; elsewhere "warn"); נְאֻם "declares".
- **"That in the day that I shall visit the transgressions of Israel upon him I will also visit the altars of Bethel: and the horns of the altar shall be cut off" → "that on the day I visit the transgressions of Israel on him, I will visit on the altars of Bethel, and the horns of the altar shall be hewn off"** (v14) — Hebrew פָּקַד עַל "visit on", twice (no "also"); פֶּשַׁע "transgression"; נִגְדְּעוּ "be hewn off" (גִּדַּע); בֵּית־אֵל "house of El".
- **"And I will smite the winter house with the summer house; and the houses of ivory shall perish, and the great houses shall have an end" → "And I will strike the winter house with the summer house, and the houses of ivory shall perish, and many houses shall come to an end"** (v15) — Hebrew הִכָּה "strike"; עַל "with" (literally "upon"); בָּתֵּי הַשֵּׁן "houses of ivory" (literally "of the tooth"); בָּתִּים רַבִּים "many houses" (or "great houses"); סָפוּ "come to an end" (סוּף).
