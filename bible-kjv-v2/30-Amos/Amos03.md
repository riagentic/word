# Amos 3

[1] Hear this word that Yahweh has spoken against you, O people of Israel, against the whole family that I brought up out of the land of Egypt:

[2] "You only have I known
of all the families of the earth;
therefore I will punish you
for all your iniquities.

[3] Do two walk together,
unless they have agreed to meet?

[4] Does a lion roar in the forest,
when he has no prey?
Does a young lion cry out from his den,
if he has taken nothing?

[5] Does a bird fall in a snare on the earth,
when there is no trap for it?
Does a snare spring up from the ground,
when it has taken nothing?

[6] Is a trumpet blown in a city,
and the people are not afraid?
Does disaster come to a city,
unless Yahweh has done it?

[7] "For Lord Yahweh does nothing
without revealing his secret
to his servants the prophets.

[8] The lion has roared;
who will not fear?
Lord Yahweh has spoken;
who can but prophesy?"

[9] Proclaim to the strongholds in Ashdod and to the strongholds in the land of Egypt, and say, "Assemble yourselves on the mountains of Samaria, and see the great tumults within her, and the oppressed in her midst."

[10] "They do not know how to do right," declares Yahweh, "those who store up violence and robbery in their strongholds."

[11] Therefore thus says Lord Yahweh:

"An adversary shall surround the land
and bring down your defenses from you,
and your strongholds shall be plundered."

[12] Thus says Yahweh:

"As the shepherd rescues from the mouth of the lion
two legs, or a piece of an ear,
so shall the people of Israel who dwell in Samaria be rescued,
with the corner of a couch and part of a bed.

[13] "Hear, and testify against the house of Jacob," declares Lord Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Hosts,

[14] "that on the day I punish Israel for his transgressions,
I will punish the altars of Bethel,
and the horns of the altar shall be cut off
and fall to the ground.

[15] I will strike the winter house along with the summer house,
and the houses of ivory shall perish,
and the great houses shall come to an end,"
declares Yahweh.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (vv7, 8, 11) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה; consistent throughout.
- **"Lord GOD, the God of hosts" → "Lord Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Hosts"** (v13) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי הַצְּבָאוֹת; Hebrew *Tzeva'ot* = hosts; retained.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the rhetorical questions and oracles are poetry.
- **"children of Israel" → "people of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל; "people" in place of "children" throughout (more natural).
- **"except they be agreed" → "unless they have agreed to meet"** (v3) — Hebrew נוֹעָדוּ *no'adu* = have met by appointment; "agreed to meet" more precise.
- **"where no gin is for him" → "when there is no trap for it"** (v5) — Hebrew מוֹקֵשׁ *moqesh* = snare/trap; "gin" is archaic for snare.
- **"shall there be evil in a city, and the LORD hath not done it" → "Does disaster come to a city, unless Yahweh has done it"** (v6) — Hebrew רָעָה *ra'ah* = evil/disaster; "disaster" in context of divine judgment.
- **"Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret" → "For Lord Yahweh does nothing without revealing his secret"** (v7) — Hebrew סוֹד *sod* = confidential counsel/secret; modernized syntax.
- **"Publish in the palaces at Ashdod" → "Proclaim to the strongholds in Ashdod"** (v9) — Hebrew הַשְׁמִיעוּ עַל אַרְמְנוֹת *hashmi'u al armonot*; "proclaim" more precise; "strongholds" consistent *armonot*.
- **"behold the great tumults in the midst thereof" → "see the great tumults within her"** (v9) — modernized.
- **"they know not to do right" → "They do not know how to do right"** (v10) — modernized.
- **"store up violence and robbery in their palaces" → "store up violence and robbery in their strongholds"** (v10) — consistent *armonot* = strongholds.
- **"An adversary there shall be even round about the land" → "An adversary shall surround the land"** (v11) — Hebrew צַר וּסְבִיב הָאָרֶץ *tzar usviv ha'aretz*; modernized.
- **"he shall bring down thy strength from thee, and thy palaces shall be spoiled" → "bring down your defenses from you, and your strongholds shall be plundered"** (v11) — consistent *armonot*; "defenses" for *uzzekhah*.
- **"in Damascus in a couch" → "with the corner of a couch and part of a bed"** (v12) — Hebrew בִּדְמֶשֶׁק עֶרֶשׂ *beDameseq eres*; the text is difficult; "corner of a couch and part of a bed" follows the image of pitiful remnants like ear fragments saved from a lion.
- **"Hear ye, and testify in the house of Jacob" → "Hear, and testify against the house of Jacob"** (v13) — Hebrew וְהָעִידוּ בְּבֵית יַעֲקֹב *veha'idu beveit Ya'aqov*; "against" captures the legal testimony sense.
- **"I shall visit the transgressions of Israel upon him" → "I punish Israel for his transgressions"** (v14) — modernized; "visit upon" is archaic for "punish for."
- **"I will smite the winter house with the summer house" → "I will strike the winter house along with the summer house"** (v15) — modernized.
- **"the great houses shall have an end" → "the great houses shall come to an end"** (v15) — modernized.
