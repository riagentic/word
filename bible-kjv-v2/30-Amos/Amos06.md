# Amos 6

[1] "Woe to those who are at ease in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧,
and to those who trust in the mountain of ⟦samaria|Shomron|Samaria⟧,
the marked men of the first of the nations,
and the house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ comes to them!

[2] Pass over to ⟦calneh|Kalneh|Calneh⟧, and see,
and go from there to ⟦hamath|Chamat|Hamath⟧ the great,
and go down to ⟦gath|Gat|Gath⟧ of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧.
Are they better than these kingdoms?
Or is their border greater than your border?

[3] Those who put far away the evil day,
and you bring near the seat of violence;

[4] those who lie on beds of ivory,
and sprawl on their couches,
and eat lambs from the flock
and calves from the midst of the stall;

[5] who improvise to the sound of the harp;
like David they have devised for themselves instruments of song;

[6] who drink from bowls of wine,
and anoint themselves with the first of oils,
and are not sick over the breaking of ⟦joseph|Yosef|Joseph⟧.

[7] Therefore now they shall go into exile at the head of the exiles,
and the revelry of the sprawlers shall depart."

[8] ⟦adonai-yhwh|Lord Yahweh|The Lord GOD⟧ has sworn by his soul, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of Hosts:

"I abominate the pride of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧,
and his citadels I hate;
and I will hand over the city and its fullness."

[9] And it shall be, if ten men are left in one house, they shall die.

[10] And his kinsman shall lift him up, and the one who burns him, to bring the bones out of the house; and he shall say to him who is in the remote parts of the house, "Is there yet anyone with you?" And he shall say, "None." And he shall say, "Hush! For the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is not to be mentioned."

[11] For behold, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is commanding, and he shall strike the great house into fragments, and the small house into cracks.

[12] Do horses run on the crag?
Does one plow with the cattle?
For you have turned judgment into poison,
and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood;

[13] those who rejoice in ⟦lo-debar|Lo-Devar|Lo-debar⟧,
who say, "Have we not by our strength taken for ourselves ⟦karnaim|Qarnayim ("Horns")|Karnaim⟧?"

[14] "For behold, I am raising up against you,
house of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧," declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the Hosts, "a nation;
and they shall oppress you
from ⟦lebo-hamath|Levo Chamat|Lebo-hamath⟧ to the wadi of the ⟦arabah|Aravah|Arabah⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" → "Lord Yahweh"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוִה, both words.
- **"the LORD the God of hosts" → "Yahweh, Elohim of Hosts"** (vv8, 14) — Hebrew יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת; v14 אֱלֹהֵי הַצְּבָאוֹת with the article: "of the Hosts".
- **Poetic structure** — vv1–8, 12–14 set as verse; vv9–11 stay prose.
- **Vocatives without "O"** (v14) — the Hebrew has no word for it (reviewer row).
- **"Woe to them that are at ease in Zion, and trust in the mountain of Samaria" → "Woe to those who are at ease in Zion, and to those who trust in the mountain of Samaria"** (v1) — Hebrew הוֹי הַשַּׁאֲנַנִּים … וְהַבֹּטְחִים (the הוֹי row; שַׁאֲנָן "at ease", row); בָּטַח בְּ "trust in" (not "feel secure").
- **"which are named chief of the nations, to whom the house of Israel came" → "the marked men of the first of the nations, and the house of Israel comes to them"** (v1) — Hebrew נְקֻבֵי רֵאשִׁית הַגּוֹיִם, passive participle of נָקַב "mark out, name distinctly" (row): men singled out by name; רֵאשִׁית "first" (row); וּבָאוּ לָהֶם, a weqatal "and (they) come to them" (the house of Israel comes to them for judgment or counsel).
- **"Pass ye unto Calneh … then go down to Gath" → "Pass over to Calneh, and see, and go from there to Hamath the great, and go down to Gath"** (v2) — Hebrew עִבְרוּ … וּרְאוּ וּלְכוּ … וּרְדוּ, each "and" kept; עָבַר "pass over" (row); חֲמַת רַבָּה "Hamath the great".
- **"be they better than these kingdoms? or their border greater than your border?" → "Are they better than these kingdoms? Or is their border greater than your border?"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲטוֹבִים, no subject written ("they": the cities named, or the hearers; the old "Are you" chose); גְּבוּל "border" (also the land within it: row).
- **"Ye that put far away the evil day, and cause the seat of violence to come near" → "Those who put far away the evil day, and you bring near the seat of violence"** (v3) — Hebrew הַמְנַדִּים, a participle with the article (third person), then וַתַּגִּישׁוּן, second person, as printed; יוֹם רָע "evil day" (רַע, row; "disaster" was a gloss); הִגִּישׁ "bring near" (row); שֶׁבֶת חָמָס "the seat (sitting) of violence" (חָמָס, row).
- **"That lie upon beds of ivory, and stretch themselves upon their couches" → "those who lie on beds of ivory, and sprawl on their couches"** (v4) — Hebrew שֵׁן "tooth", of ivory; סְרֻחִים "sprawling" (סָרַח; the same word in v7 "the sprawlers"); עַרְשׂוֹתָם "their couches".
- **"and eat the lambs out of the flock, and the calves out of the midst of the stall" → "and eat lambs from the flock and calves from the midst of the stall"** (v4) — Hebrew כָּרִים "lambs", מַרְבֵּק "stall" (the 1 Sam 28 row: fattened in the stall).
- **"That chant to the sound of the viol, and invent to themselves instruments of musick, like David" → "who improvise to the sound of the harp; like David they have devised for themselves instruments of song"** (v5) — Hebrew הַפֹּרְטִים, found only here (meaning uncertain: "improvise", "strum", "chant idly"); עַל־פִּי "to the mouth (sound) of"; נֵבֶל "harp" (row); כְּדָוִיד goes with the second line (the accents); חָשְׁבוּ "they have devised" (חָשַׁב of the mind, row; "invent" is close); כְּלֵי־שִׁיר "instruments of song".
- **"That drink wine in bowls, and anoint themselves with the chief ointments: but they are not grieved for the affliction of Joseph" → "who drink from bowls of wine, and anoint themselves with the first of oils, and are not sick over the breaking of Joseph"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּמִזְרְקֵי יַיִן "from bowls of wine" (מִזְרָק "bowl", row: the large altar bowl); רֵאשִׁית שְׁמָנִים "the first of oils" (רֵאשִׁית, row); יִמְשָׁחוּ "they anoint" ("themselves" supplied: no object written); נֶחְלוּ "they are made sick" (nifal of חָלָה); שֵׁבֶר "breaking" (row; as Lam 2:11).
- **"Therefore now shall they go captive with the first that go captive, and the banquet of them that stretched themselves shall be removed" → "Therefore now they shall go into exile at the head of the exiles, and the revelry of the sprawlers shall depart"** (v7) — Hebrew יִגְלוּ בְּרֹאשׁ גֹּלִים "they shall go into exile at the head of the exiles" (גָּלָה, row); מִרְזַח "revelry" (the מַרְזֵחַ row, which gives Amos 6:7 this English: one word with Jer 16:5 "mourning feast", a feast of a fellowship; meaning uncertain); וְסָר "and shall turn aside, depart" (סוּר); סְרוּחִים "sprawlers" (as v4; the sound play מִרְזַח סְרוּחִים).
- **"The Lord GOD hath sworn by himself" → "The Lord Yahweh has sworn by his soul"** (v8) — Hebrew בְּנַפְשׁוֹ "by his soul" (נֶפֶשׁ, reviewer row; "himself" is what it means).
- **"I abhor the excellency of Jacob, and hate his palaces: therefore will I deliver up the city with all that is therein" → "I abominate the pride of Jacob, and his citadels I hate; and I will hand over the city and its fullness"** (v8) — Hebrew מְתָאֵב אָנֹכִי, a participle (= מְתַעֵב: תִּעֵב "abominate", row; as 5:10); גְּאוֹן "pride" (row; of God "exaltation"); אַרְמְנוֹת "citadels" (אַרְמוֹן, row); הִסְגַּרְתִּי "I will hand over" (הִסְגִּיר, row); מְלֹאָהּ "its fullness" (row).
- **"And it shall come to pass, if there remain ten men in one house, that they shall die" → "And it shall be, if ten men are left in one house, they shall die"** (v9) — Hebrew וְהָיָה אִם (row), יִוָּתְרוּ "are left over", וָמֵתוּ.
- **"And a man's uncle shall take him up, and he that burneth him, to bring out the bones out of the house" → "And his kinsman shall lift him up, and the one who burns him, to bring the bones out of the house"** (v10) — Hebrew וּנְשָׂאוֹ דּוֹדוֹ וּמְסָרְפוֹ; דּוֹד "uncle, kinsman" (literally "beloved"); מְסָרֵף, found only here, taken as מְשָׂרֵף "one who burns" (the body, or spices for the dead: uncertain; the old "the one who anoints him for burial" is not in the word); עֲצָמִים "bones" (the dead body).
- **"and shall say unto him that is by the sides of the house, Is there yet any with thee? and he shall say, No" → "and he shall say to him who is in the remote parts of the house, 'Is there yet anyone with you?' And he shall say, 'None.'"** (v10) — Hebrew יַרְכְּתֵי הַבַּיִת "the remote parts of the house" (row); הַעוֹד עִמָּךְ "is there yet with you?" ("anyone" supplied); אֶפֶס "none" (row).
- **"Hold thy tongue: for we may not make mention of the name of the LORD" → "Hush! For the name of Yahweh is not to be mentioned"** (v10) — Hebrew הַס "Hush!" (row); כִּי לֹא לְהַזְכִּיר בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה, literally "for not to make mention by the name of Yahweh" (הִזְכִּיר, row): the infinitive with לֹא says what must not be done; the English passive carries it without supplying "we".
- **"For, behold, the LORD commandeth, and he will smite the great house with breaches, and the little house with clefts" → "For behold, Yahweh is commanding, and he shall strike the great house into fragments, and the small house into cracks"** (v11) — Hebrew מְצַוֶּה, a participle; וְהִכָּה, active ("he shall strike": the old passive changed the voice; הִכָּה, row); רְסִיסִים "fragments" (lit. "drops"); בְּקִעִים "cracks" (lit. "splits", root בָּקַע, row).
- **"Shall horses run upon the rock? will one plow there with oxen?" → "Do horses run on the crag? Does one plow with the cattle?"** (v12) — Hebrew בַּסֶּלַע "on the crag" (סֶלַע, row); אִם־יַחֲרוֹשׁ בַּבְּקָרִים, the MT as printed ("there" is not written); dividing the letters as בְּבָקָר יָם "plow the sea with cattle" is a common conjecture, not followed; בָּקָר "cattle" (the herd row).
- **"for ye have turned judgment into gall, and the fruit of righteousness into hemlock" → "For you have turned judgment into poison, and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood"** (v12) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ "poison" and לַעֲנָה "wormwood" (the רֹאשׁ וְלַעֲנָה row); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (row; was "justice"); הָפַךְ "turn" (row, as 5:7).
- **"Ye which rejoice in a thing of nought, which say, Have we not taken to us horns by our own strength?" → "those who rejoice in Lo-debar, who say, 'Have we not by our strength taken for ourselves Karnaim?'"** (v13) — Hebrew הַשְּׂמֵחִים לְלֹא דָבָר, participles with the article (third person); לֹא דָבָר is the town Lo-debar and "no thing" at once, קַרְנַיִם the town Karnaim and "horns" (tokens; the play in the tokens' forms); לָקַחְנוּ "we have taken" (לָקַח, row; not "captured"); בְחָזְקֵנוּ "by our strength" ("own" is not written).
- **"But, behold, I will raise up against you a nation, O house of Israel, saith the LORD the God of hosts; and they shall afflict you from the entering in of Hemath unto the river of the wilderness" → "For behold, I am raising up against you, house of Israel, declares Yahweh, Elohim of the Hosts, a nation; and they shall oppress you from Lebo-hamath to the wadi of the Arabah"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּי הִנְנִי מֵקִים, a participle; גּוֹי stands last, after the formula, as printed; לָחַץ "oppress" (row); נַחַל "wadi" (row); הָעֲרָבָה with the article (token).
