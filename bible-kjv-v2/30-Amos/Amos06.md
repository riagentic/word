# Amos 6

[1] "Woe to those who are at ease in Zion,
and to those who feel secure on the mountain of Samaria,
the notable men of the first of the nations,
to whom the house of Israel comes!

[2] Pass over to Calneh, and see,
and from there go to Hamath the great;
then go down to Gath of the Philistines.
Are you better than these kingdoms?
Or is their territory greater than your territory,

[3] O you who put far away the day of disaster
and bring near the seat of violence?

[4] "Those who lie on beds of ivory
and stretch themselves out on their couches,
and eat lambs from the flock
and calves from the midst of the stall,

[5] who strum the strings of the harp
and like David invent for themselves instruments of music,

[6] who drink wine in bowls
and anoint themselves with the finest oils,
but are not grieved over the ruin of Joseph!

[7] Therefore they shall now be the first of those who go into exile,
and the revelry of those who stretch themselves out shall pass away."

[8] Lord Yahweh has sworn by himself, declares Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Hosts:

"I abhor the pride of Jacob
and hate his strongholds,
and I will deliver up the city and all that is in it."

[9] And if ten men remain in one house, they shall die.

[10] And when one's relative, the one who anoints him for burial, shall take him up to bring the bones out of the house, and shall say to him who is in the innermost parts of the house, "Is there still anyone with you?" he shall say, "No"; and he shall say, "Silence! We must not mention the name of Yahweh."

[11] For behold, Yahweh commands, and the great house shall be struck down into fragments, and the little house into bits.

[12] Do horses run on rocks?
Does one plow there with oxen?
But you have turned justice into poison
and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood —

[13] you who rejoice in Lo-debar ("Nothing"),
who say, "Have we not by our own strength captured Karnaim ("Horns") for ourselves?"

[14] "For behold, I will raise up against you a nation,
O house of Israel," declares Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Hosts;
"and they shall oppress you
from Lebo-Hamath to the Brook of the Arabah."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Lord GOD" / "Lord the God of hosts" → "Lord Yahweh" / "Yahweh, the *Elohim* of Hosts"** (v8) — Hebrew אֲדֹנָי יְהוָה … יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי צְבָאוֹת (*Elohei Tzeva'ot*).
- **"the LORD the God of hosts" → "Yahweh, the God of Hosts"** (v14) — consistent.
- **Poetry formatted with blockquote indentation** (throughout) — KJV prose; the woe-oracle is poetry.
- **"trust in the mountain of Samaria" → "feel secure on the mountain of Samaria"** (v1) — Hebrew הַבֹּטְחִים *habotchim* = those who trust/feel secure; "feel secure" captures the complacency.
- **"which are named chief of the nations" → "the notable men of the first of the nations"** (v1) — Hebrew הַנְּקֻבִים רֵאשִׁית הַגּוֹיִם *hanequvim reshit hagoyim* = those named/designated as first of nations; "notable men" captures the self-styled elite.
- **"to whom the house of Israel came" → "to whom the house of Israel comes"** (v1) — present tense for the pattern of seeking their counsel.
- **"be they better than these kingdoms" → "Are you better than these kingdoms"** (v2) — Hebrew הֲטוֹבִים מֵהַמַּמְלָכוֹת הָאֵלֶּה; ironic rhetorical question.
- **"the evil day" → "the day of disaster"** (v3) — Hebrew יוֹם רָע *yom ra'* = evil day/day of calamity; "disaster" clearer.
- **"That lie upon beds of ivory" → "Those who lie on beds of ivory"** (v4) — modernized.
- **"eat the lambs out of the flock" → "eat lambs from the flock"** (v4) — modernized.
- **"That chant to the sound of the viol" → "who strum the strings of the harp"** (v5) — Hebrew הַפֹּרְטִים עַל פִּי הַנָּבֶל *haporetim al pi hanavel*; *nevel* = harp/lyre; "strum the strings of the harp" for *poret al pi*.
- **"like David invent to themselves instruments of musick" → "like David invent for themselves instruments of music"** (v5) — modernized.
- **"anoint themselves with the chief ointments" → "anoint themselves with the finest oils"** (v6) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית שְׁמָנִים *reshit shemanim* = finest/choicest oils.
- **"the affliction of Joseph" → "the ruin of Joseph"** (v6) — Hebrew שֵׁבֶר יוֹסֵף *shever Yosef* = the breach/ruin of Joseph; "ruin" more precise than "affliction."
- **"shall go captive with the first that go captive" → "shall now be the first of those who go into exile"** (v7) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ גֹּלִים *rosh golim* = head of the exiles; "first of those who go into exile" captures the irony: the first in luxury become first in exile.
- **"the banquet of them that stretched themselves shall be removed" → "the revelry of those who stretch themselves out shall pass away"** (v7) — Hebrew מִרְזַח *mirzach* = a revelry/funeral feast; "revelry" or "feast of excess" more precise.
- **"The Lord God hath sworn by himself" → "Lord Yahweh has sworn by himself"** (v8) — consistent formula.
- **"I abhor the excellency of Jacob" → "I abhor the pride of Jacob"** (v8) — Hebrew גְּאוֹן יַעֲקֹב *ge'on Ya'aqov* = the pride/majesty of Jacob; here "pride" in the negative sense.
- **"I will deliver up the city with all that is therein" → "I will deliver up the city and all that is in it"** (v8) — modernized.
- **"a man's uncle shall take him up, and he that burneth him" → "when one's relative, the one who anoints him for burial, shall take him up"** (v10) — Hebrew דּוֹדוֹ וּמְסָרְפוֹ *dodo umsarafo*; *dodo* = uncle/relative; *mesarefo* = the one who burns/prepares him; "anoints for burial" follows the context of corpse preparation.
- **"Hold thy tongue: for we may not make mention of the name of the LORD" → "Silence! We must not mention the name of Yahweh"** (v10) — Hebrew הַס כִּי לֹא לְהַזְכִּיר בְּשֵׁם יְהוָה; superstitious silence about invoking Yahweh amid the judgment.
- **"he will smite the great house with breaches, and the little house with clefts" → "the great house shall be struck down into fragments, and the little house into bits"** (v11) — Hebrew רְסִיסִים *resisim* = fragments; "fragments/bits" vivid.
- **"ye have turned judgment into gall, and the fruit of righteousness into hemlock" → "you have turned justice into poison and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood"** (v12) — Hebrew לְרֹאשׁ *lerosh* = poison/venom; לְלַעֲנָה *lela'anah* = wormwood/bitterness; "poison" and "wormwood" more vivid.
- **"from the entering in of Hemath unto the river of the wilderness" → "from Lebo-Hamath to the Brook of the Arabah"** (v14) — Hebrew מִלְּבֹוא חֲמָת עַד נַחַל הָעֲרָבָה *miLevo Chamat ad Nachal ha'Aravah*; "Lebo-Hamath" is the proper geographical term (the entrance to Hamath); "Brook of the Arabah" more precise than "river of the wilderness."
