# Romans 2

[1] Therefore you are without excuse, O man, everyone who judges; for in that in which you judge the other, you condemn yourself, for you who judge practice the same things.

[2] But we know that the sentence of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is according to truth upon those who practice such things.

[3] And do you consider this, O man who judge those who practice such things and do them, that you will escape the sentence of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[4] Or do you despise the riches of his kindness and forbearance and patience, not knowing that the kindness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ leads you unto repentance?

[5] But according to your hardness and unrepentant heart you store up for yourself wrath in the day of wrath and of revelation of the righteous judgment of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[6] who will render to each one according to his works:

[7] to those who according to endurance of good work seek glory and honor and incorruption, eternal life;

[8] but to those who are from selfish ambition and disobey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, wrath and rage,

[9] affliction and straits, upon every soul of man who works out the evil, both of the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ first and of the ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧;

[10] but glory and honor and peace to everyone who works the good, both to the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ first and to the ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧;

[11] **for there is no partiality with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**

[12] For as many as sinned without ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ will also perish without ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧; and as many as sinned in ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ will be judged through ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧;

[13] for not the hearers of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ are righteous with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but the doers of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ will be justified.

[14] For whenever nations that do not have ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ by nature do the things of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, these, not having ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, are a law to themselves,

[15] who demonstrate the work of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ written in their hearts, their conscience testifying with it, and their reasonings between one another accusing or even defending,

[16] in the day when ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ judges the hidden things of men, according to my gospel, through ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[17] But if you are named a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ and rest upon ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and boast in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧

[18] and know the will and approve the things that excel, being instructed out of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧,

[19] and are confident that you yourself are a guide of the blind, a light of those in darkness,

[20] a trainer of the senseless, a teacher of infants, having the form of knowledge and of the truth in the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ —

[21] you then who teach another, do you not teach yourself? You who proclaim not to steal, do you steal?

[22] You who say not to commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abominate the idols, do you rob temples?

[23] You who boast in ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, through the transgression of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ do you dishonor ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[24] For "**the name of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is blasphemed among the nations because of you**," just as it is written.

[25] For circumcision indeed profits if you practice ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧; but if you are a transgressor of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.

[26] If therefore the uncircumcision observes the righteous ordinances of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, will not his uncircumcision be reckoned for circumcision?

[27] And the uncircumcision that is from nature, carrying out the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, will judge you, who through letter and circumcision are a transgressor of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[28] For he is not a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ who is one in what is manifest, nor is that circumcision which is in what is manifest, in flesh;

[29] but he is a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ who is one in what is hidden, and circumcision is of the heart, in Spirit, not in letter; whose praise is not from men but from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v16) — the verse follows Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ (SBLGNT WH NA28); Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ (Treg NIV RP) is the KJV order. The editions are divided two against two, and NA28 decides.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"law" → "*Torah*"** (v12–27) — Greek νόμος. "The" stands only where the Greek has the article (v14 τὰ τοῦ νόμου, v15, 18, 20, 23, 26, 27 τὸν νόμον); elsewhere νόμος has no article (v12, 13, 14, 17, 23, 25, 27). In v14 "a law to themselves" is the common noun.
- **"thou art inexcusable, O man, whosoever thou art that judgest: for wherein thou judgest another" → "you are without excuse, O man, everyone who judges; for in that in which you judge the other"** (v1) — Greek ἀναπολόγητος, the word of 1:20; πᾶς ὁ κρίνων; ἐν ᾧ, "in that in which" or "in that"; τὸν ἕτερον, with the article. The Greek has no "you, the judge".
- **"doest the same things … them which commit such things … and doest the same" → "practice the same things … those who practice such things … and do them"** (v1, 2, 3) — Greek πράσσω, "practice", and ποιέω, "do" (v3), kept apart as in 1:32.
- **"the judgment of God is according to truth against them" → "the sentence of *Elohim* is according to truth upon those"** (v2, 3) — Greek τὸ κρίμα, the verdict given; κατὰ ἀλήθειαν, "according to truth"; ἐπί, "upon". The Greek has no "rightly falls". The sentence begins with δέ, "but" (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28).
- **"thinkest thou this" → "do you consider this"** (v3) — Greek λογίζῃ, the verb rendered "reckon" in v26 and chapter 4.
- **"the goodness of God leadeth thee to repentance" → "the kindness of *Elohim* leads you unto repentance"** (v4) — Greek χρηστότης and τὸ χρηστόν (the neuter adjective, "the kind thing"), one root; ἄγει is a plain present, "leads". The Greek has no "is meant to".
- **"after thy hardness and impenitent heart treasurest up unto thyself wrath against the day of wrath and revelation of the righteous judgment" → "according to your hardness and unrepentant heart you store up for yourself wrath in the day of wrath and of revelation of the righteous judgment"** (v5) — Greek κατά, "according to"; ἐν ἡμέρᾳ, "in the day"; ἀποκαλύψεως δικαιοκρισίας, two nouns: the Greek has no "when … will be revealed". No "and" after "revelation" (+ καί RP).
- **"by patient continuance in well doing" → "according to endurance of good work"** (v7) — Greek καθʼ ὑπομονὴν ἔργου ἀγαθοῦ, a singular genitive.
- **"immortality" → "incorruption"** (v7) — Greek ἀφθαρσία, the root of "incorruptible" in 1:23.
- **"eternal life" kept; no verb** (v7) — "eternal life" is the object of "will render" (v6); the Greek has no "he will give".
- **"unto them that are contentious, and do not obey the truth … indignation and wrath" → "to those who are from selfish ambition and disobey the truth … wrath and rage"** (v8) — Greek τοῖς ἐξ ἐριθείας, "those from selfish ambition" (as "those from faith"); ἀπειθοῦσι … πειθομένοις, one root: "disobey … be persuaded by, obey". ὀργὴ καὶ θυμός (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28; the KJV follows the order of RP) are nominatives: the sentence changes its construction, and the Greek has no "there will be".
- **"Tribulation and anguish, upon every soul of man that doeth evil" → "affliction and straits, upon every soul of man who works out the evil"** (v9) — Greek θλῖψις καὶ στενοχωρία; ψυχή, "soul", kept; κατεργαζομένου, "work out", beside ἐργαζομένῳ, "work", in v10.
- **"of the Jew first, and also of the Gentile" → "both of the *Yehudi* first and of the *Yevani*"** (v9, 10) — Greek Ἕλληνος, Ἕλληνι, "Greek", as 1:16.
- **"there is no respect of persons with God" → "there is no partiality with *Elohim*"** (v11) — Greek προσωπολημψία, "receiving of face" (Gal 2:6), as Eph 6:9; Col 3:25; παρὰ τῷ θεῷ, "with *Elohim*", as in v13.
- **"have sinned without law … in the law … by the law" → "sinned without *Torah* … in *Torah* … through *Torah*"** (v12) — Greek ἀνόμως twice, an adverb, "without law"; ἐν νόμῳ, διὰ νόμου; ἥμαρτον is a simple past.
- **"not the hearers of the law are just before God" → "not the hearers of *Torah* are righteous with *Elohim*"** (v13) — Greek νόμου without the article twice (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28; τοῦ νόμου RP); δίκαιοι, "righteous", the root of "justified".
- **"when the Gentiles, which have not the law, do by nature the things contained in the law" → "whenever nations that do not have *Torah* by nature do the things of the *Torah*"** (v14) — Greek ἔθνη without the article, "nations"; τὰ τοῦ νόμου, "the things of the *Torah*": the Greek has no "requires" and no "contained". φύσει, "by nature", stands between the two clauses and can go with "do not have" or with "do"; the English leaves it open. "Do" is plural (ποιῶσιν SBLGNT WH Treg NA28).
- **"having not the law" → "not having *Torah*"** (v14) — Greek νόμον μὴ ἔχοντες, a participle; the Greek has no "even though".
- **"Which shew the work of the law written in their hearts" → "who demonstrate the work of the *Torah* written in their hearts"** (v15) — Greek ἐνδείκνυνται; τὸ ἔργον, singular; γραπτόν agrees with "work".
- **"their conscience also bearing witness, and their thoughts the mean while accusing or else excusing one another" → "their conscience testifying with it, and their reasonings between one another accusing or even defending"** (v15) — Greek συμμαρτυρούσης, "testify together with": "it" is supplied; λογισμοί, "reasonings" (2 Cor 10:4), the noun of λογίζομαι; μεταξὺ ἀλλήλων, "between one another": between the thoughts, or between the people. The two participles have no object.
- **"In the day when God shall judge the secrets of men by Jesus Christ" → "in the day when *Elohim* judges the hidden things of men … through the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v16) — the printed κρίνει is a present (SBLGNT WH NA28); κρινεῖ, "will judge" (Treg RP), differs only in the accent. τὰ κρυπτά, "the hidden things", as "in what is hidden" in v29; διά, "through".
- **"Behold, thou art called a Jew, and restest in the law" → "But if you are named a *Yehudi* and rest upon *Torah*"** (v17) — Εἰ δέ, "but if" (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28); Ἴδε, "behold" (RP), is the KJV reading. ἐπονομάζῃ is middle or passive: "call yourself" or "are named". The "if" has no main clause: the sentence breaks off at v20 and v21 starts anew.
- **"knowest his will, and approvest the things that are more excellent" → "know the will and approve the things that excel"** (v18) — Greek τὸ θέλημα, "the will", without "his"; δοκιμάζεις τὰ διαφέροντα, as Phil 1:10: "approve the things that excel" or "test the things that differ".
- **"art confident that thou thyself art a guide of the blind" kept** (v19) — Greek πέποιθας; the Greek has no second "if".
- **"An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes, which hast the form of knowledge" → "a trainer of the senseless, a teacher of infants, having the form of knowledge"** (v20) — Greek παιδευτής (root of "train"), ἀφρόνων, νηπίων; μόρφωσις, "form" (2 Tim 3:5), not "embodiment".
- **"thou that preachest a man should not steal" → "You who proclaim not to steal"** (v21) — Greek ὁ κηρύσσων μὴ κλέπτειν.
- **"thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?" → "You who abominate the idols, do you rob temples?"** (v22) — Greek βδελυσσόμενος (root of "abomination"); ἱεροσυλεῖς, "rob temples".
- **"through breaking the law dishonourest thou God?" → "through the transgression of the *Torah* do you dishonor *Elohim*?"** (v23) — Greek διὰ τῆς παραβάσεως τοῦ νόμου, a noun; the printed text has a question mark; it can also be read as a statement.
- **"the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you, as it is written" → "the name of *Elohim* is blasphemed among the nations because of you, just as it is written"** (v24) — Greek διʼ ὑμᾶς, "because of you"; "just as it is written" stands at the end.
- **"circumcision verily profiteth, if thou keep the law: but if thou be a breaker of the law, thy circumcision is made uncircumcision" → "circumcision indeed profits if you practice *Torah*; but if you are a transgressor of *Torah*, your circumcision has become uncircumcision"** (v25) — Greek πράσσῃς, "practice"; παραβάτης νόμου, "a transgressor of law" (also v27; Gal 2:18); γέγονεν, "has become".
- **"if the uncircumcision keep the righteousness of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be counted for circumcision?" → "If therefore the uncircumcision observes the righteous ordinances of the *Torah*, will not his uncircumcision be reckoned for circumcision?"** (v26) — Greek ἡ ἀκροβυστία, the abstract noun for the uncircumcised man (also v27), as Gal 2:7; τὰ δικαιώματα, plural, as 1:32; λογισθήσεται εἰς, "be reckoned for", the wording of 4:3. φυλάσσῃ is rendered "observe" as in Gal 6:13 (elsewhere "guard"), because its object is ordinances.
- **"shall not uncircumcision which is by nature, if it fulfil the law, judge thee, who by the letter and circumcision dost transgress the law?" → "And the uncircumcision that is from nature, carrying out the *Torah*, will judge you, who through letter and circumcision are a transgressor of *Torah*"** (v27) — Greek ἡ ἐκ φύσεως ἀκροβυστία; τελοῦσα, "carry out" (Jas 2:8), a participle; διὰ γράμματος καὶ περιτομῆς, "through letter and circumcision" (with them, or by means of them), without articles; the Greek has no "written code". Printed as a statement; it can also continue the question of v26.
- **"he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly; neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh" → "he is not a *Yehudi* who is one in what is manifest, nor is that circumcision which is in what is manifest, in flesh"** (v28) — Greek ἐν τῷ φανερῷ twice, the word "manifest" of 1:19; the Greek has no "merely".
- **"he is a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, and not in the letter" → "he is a *Yehudi* who is one in what is hidden, and circumcision is of the heart, in Spirit, not in letter"** (v29) — Greek ἐν τῷ κρυπτῷ, as "the hidden things" in v16; περιτομὴ καρδίας ἐν πνεύματι οὐ γράμματι, without articles and without a verb: "in spirit" (the human spirit) is equally possible; "Spirit" is written because of the same contrast with "letter" in 7:6 and 2 Cor 3:6.
- **"whose praise is not of men, but of God" → "whose praise is not from men but from *Elohim*"** (v29) — Greek οὗ, a relative, "whose" (of the man, or of the circumcision); ἐκ, "from".

### Notes
- **v6** uses the words of Ps 62:12 and Prov 24:12.
- **v24** quotes Isa 52:5 in its Greek form: the words "because of you" and "among the nations" are not in the Hebrew text of that verse.
- **v29** — plays on *Yehudi* (from Judah, "praise").
