# Romans 2

[1] Therefore you have no excuse, O man, every one of you who judges. For in passing judgment on another you condemn yourself, because you, the judge, practice the very same things.

[2] We know that the judgment of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ rightly falls on those who practice such things.

[3] Do you suppose, O man — you who judge those who practice such things and yet do them yourself — that you will escape the judgment of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[4] Or do you presume on the riches of his kindness and forbearance and patience, not knowing that ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ kindness is meant to lead you to repentance?

[5] But because of your hard and impenitent heart you are storing up wrath for yourself on the day of wrath when ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ righteous judgment will be revealed.

[6] He will render to each one according to his works:

[7] to those who by patience in well-doing seek for glory and honor and immortality, he will give eternal life;

[8] but for those who are self-seeking and do not obey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, there will be wrath and fury.

[9] There will be tribulation and distress for every human being who does evil, the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ first and also the ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧,

[10] but glory and honor and peace for everyone who does good, the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ first and also the ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧.

[11] **For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shows no partiality.**

[12] For all who have sinned without the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ will also perish without the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, and all who have sinned under the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ will be judged by the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[13] For it is not the hearers of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ who are righteous before ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but the doers of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ who will be justified.

[14] For when Gentiles, who do not have the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, by nature do what the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ requires, they are a law to themselves, even though they do not have the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[15] They show that the work of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ is written on their hearts, while their conscience also bears witness, and their conflicting thoughts accuse or even excuse them

[16] on that day when, according to my gospel, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ judges the secrets of men by ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[17] But if you call yourself a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ and rely on the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and boast in ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧

[18] and know his will and approve what is excellent, because you are instructed from the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧;

[19] and if you are sure that you yourself are a guide to the blind, a light to those who are in darkness,

[20] an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of children, having in the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ the embodiment of knowledge and truth —

[21] you then who teach others, do you not teach yourself? While you preach against stealing, do you steal?

[22] You who say that one must not commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?

[23] You who boast in the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ dishonor ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ by breaking the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[24] For, as it is written, "**The name of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you**."

[25] For circumcision indeed is of value if you obey the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, but if you break the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, your circumcision becomes uncircumcision.

[26] So, if a man who is uncircumcised keeps the precepts of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, will not his uncircumcision be regarded as circumcision?

[27] Then he who is physically uncircumcised but keeps the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ will condemn you who have the written code and circumcision but break the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[28] For no one is a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ who is merely one outwardly, nor is circumcision outward and physical.

[29] But a ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ is one inwardly, and circumcision is a matter of the heart, by the Spirit, not by the letter. His praise is not from man but from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v16) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, 20, 23, 25, 26, 27) — Greek νόμος *nomos* here clearly refers to the Mosaic Torah throughout; retained as "Torah" to signal its Jewish identity and covenant character.
- **"For there is no respect of persons with God" → "For *Elohim* shows no partiality"** (v11) — KJV "respect of persons" is archaic for προσωποληψία *prosopolepsia* (= partiality); modernized.
- **"The name of God is blasphemed" → "The name of *Elohim* is blasphemed"** (v24) — systematic; quotation from Isa 52:5 (LXX).
- **Archaic second-person singular (thee/thy/thou) → modern second person** (throughout) — KJV uses archaic forms throughout; modernized to "you/your" consistently.
- **"contentious" → "self-seeking"** (v8) — ἐριθεία *eritheia* = selfish ambition, factional self-interest; modernized.
- **"indignation and wrath" → "wrath and fury"** (v8) — ὀργὴ καὶ θυμός; "fury" captures the intensity better than "indignation" in modern usage.
- **"circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, and not in the letter" → "circumcision is a matter of the heart, by the Spirit, not by the letter"** (v29) — modernized phrasing.

### Notes
- **v24** quotes Isa 52:5.
- **v29** — plays on *Yehudi* (Jew, from Judah = "praise").
