# Romans 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, a servant of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, called to be an apostle, set apart for the gospel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[2] which he promised beforehand through his prophets in the holy Scriptures,

[3] concerning his Son, who was descended from David according to the flesh

[4] and was declared to be the Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in power according to the Spirit of holiness by his resurrection from the dead, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ our Lord,

[5] through whom we have received grace and apostleship to bring about the obedience of faith for the sake of his name among all the nations,

[6] including you who are called to belong to ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[7] To all those in ⟦rome|Roma|Rome⟧ who are loved by ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and called to be saints:

Grace to you and peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[8] First, I thank my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ for all of you, because your faith is proclaimed in all the world.

[9] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my witness, whom I serve with my spirit in the gospel of his Son, that without ceasing I mention you

[10] always in my prayers, asking that somehow by ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ will I may now at last succeed in coming to you.

[11] For I long to see you, that I may impart to you some spiritual gift to strengthen you —

[12] that is, that we may be mutually encouraged by each other's faith, both yours and mine.

[13] I do not want you to be unaware, brothers, that I have often intended to come to you (but thus far have been prevented), in order that I may reap some harvest among you as well as among the rest of the Gentiles.

[14] I am under obligation both to ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ and to barbarians, both to the wise and to the foolish.

[15] So I am eager to preach the gospel to you also who are in ⟦rome|Roma|Rome⟧.

[16] For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for salvation to everyone who believes, to the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ first and also to the ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧.

[17] For in it the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is revealed from faith for faith, as it is written, "**The righteous shall live by faith**."

[18] For the wrath of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who by their unrighteousness suppress the truth.

[19] For what can be known about ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is plain to them, because ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has shown it to them.

[20] For his invisible attributes, namely, his eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly perceived, ever since the creation of the world, in the things that have been made. So they are without excuse.

[21] For although they knew ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, they did not honor him as ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ or give thanks to him, but they became futile in their thinking, and their foolish hearts were darkened.

[22] Claiming to be wise, they became fools,

[23] and exchanged the glory of the immortal ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for images resembling mortal man and birds and animals and creeping things.

[24] Therefore ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave them up in the lusts of their hearts to impurity, to the dishonoring of their bodies among themselves,

[25] because they exchanged the truth about ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for a lie and worshiped and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is blessed forever! Amen.

[26] For this reason ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave them up to dishonorable passions. For their women exchanged natural relations for those that are contrary to nature;

[27] and the men likewise gave up natural relations with women and were consumed with passion for one another, men committing shameless acts with men and receiving in themselves the due penalty for their error.

[28] And since they did not see fit to acknowledge ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave them up to a debased mind to do what ought not to be done.

[30] slanderers, haters of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, insolent, haughty, boastful, inventors of evil, disobedient to parents,

[31] foolish, faithless, heartless, ruthless.

[32] Though they know ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ righteous decree that those who practice such things deserve to die, they not only do them but give approval to those who practice them.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8) — systematic. Hebrew name יֵשׁוּעַ *Yeshua* ("YHWH saves") restored; "Jesus" is a Greek-Latin transliteration.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8) — systematic. Greek Χριστός *Christos* translates Hebrew מָשִׁיחַ *Mashiach* ("Anointed One"); kept in Hebrew form to retain the Jewish messianic sense.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic. See Genesis note; the divine name kept in its Hebrew form.
- **"servant" (v1)** — KJV "servant" for δοῦλος *doulos* (literally "slave"). Modern translations often say "servant" to soften; retained here as "servant" is accurate enough in context and "slave" can distract.
- **"separated unto" → "set apart for"** (v1) — KJV "separated unto" is archaic; modernized.
- **"which he had promised afore" → "which he promised beforehand"** (v2) — archaic KJV modernized.
- **"holy scriptures" → "holy Scriptures"** (v2) — capitalized as a proper designation.
- **"declared to be the Son of God" → "declared to be the Son of *Elohim*"** (v4) — systematic divine-name change.
- **"grace and apostleship … for obedience to the faith among all nations" → "grace and apostleship to bring about the obedience of faith … among all the nations"** (v5) — KJV phrasing clarified; "the obedience of faith" (ὑπακοὴν πίστεως) is a genitive of source or content.
- **"the righteousness of God is revealed from faith to faith" → "the righteousness of *Elohim* is revealed from faith for faith"** (v17) — "from faith for faith" preserves the directional sense of εἰς πίστιν; KJV "to faith" is similar but slightly less precise.
- **"The just shall live by faith" → "The righteous shall live by faith"** (v17) — OT quotation from Hab 2:4; δίκαιος *dikaios* = "righteous"; "just" is a valid rendering but "righteous" is more consistent with the righteousness (*dikaiosyne*) vocabulary of Romans.
- **"changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man" → "exchanged the glory of the immortal *Elohim* for images resembling mortal man"** (v23) — KJV archaic phrasing modernized; "uncorruptible" → "immortal."
- **"changed the truth of God into a lie" → "exchanged the truth about *Elohim* for a lie"** (v25) — same pattern; "exchanged" (ἤλλαξαν) is more precise than "changed."
- **"vile affections" → "dishonorable passions"** (v26) — KJV archaic; ἀτιμία *atimia* = dishonor.
- **"leaving the natural use of the woman" → "gave up natural relations with women"** (v27) — KJV "use" (χρῆσις) is literal but sounds clinical; modernized to "relations."
- **"reprobate mind" → "debased mind"** (v28) — KJV "reprobate" = ἀδόκιμος *adokimos* (failing the test, disqualified); "debased" captures the sense better in modern English.
- **"haters of God" → "haters of *Elohim*"** (v30) — systematic.
- **"without natural affection" → "heartless"** (v31) — KJV is a direct Latinism (ἄστοργος *astorgos* = without family affection); "heartless" conveys it in natural English.
- **"implacable" → "ruthless"** (v31) — ἄσπονδος *aspondos* = without truce; modernized.

### Notes
- **v17** quotes Hab 2:4.
