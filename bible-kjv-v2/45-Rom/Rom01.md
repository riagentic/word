# Romans 1

[1] ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, a slave of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, a called apostle, set apart unto the gospel of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[2] which he promised beforehand through his prophets in holy Scriptures,

[3] concerning his Son, who came to be from the seed of David according to the flesh,

[4] who was marked out as Son of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in power according to the Spirit of holiness from the resurrection of the dead, ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ our Lord,

[5] through whom we received grace and apostleship unto obedience of faith among all the nations, for the sake of his name,

[6] among whom are you also, called ones of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

[7] to all who are in ⟦rome|Roma|Rome⟧, beloved of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, called saints:

Grace to you and peace from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ our Father and the Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[8] First, I give thanks to my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ concerning all of you, because your faith is proclaimed in the whole world.

[9] For ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is my witness, whom I worship in my spirit in the gospel of his Son, how unceasingly I make mention of you

[10] always at my prayers, beseeching, if somehow now at last I shall be prospered in the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ to come to you.

[11] For I long to see you, that I may impart some spiritual gift to you, for you to be made stable,

[12] and this is to be encouraged together among you through the faith that is in one another, both yours and mine.

[13] But I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that many times I purposed to come to you, and was hindered until now, that I might have some fruit among you also, just as also among the rest of the nations.

[14] Both to ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ and to barbarians, both to wise and to foolish, I am a debtor;

[15] so, as far as is in me, there is readiness to preach the gospel to you also who are in ⟦rome|Roma|Rome⟧.

[16] For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ unto salvation to everyone who believes, both to the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧ first and to the ⟦greek|Yevani|Greek⟧;

[17] for the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is revealed in it from faith unto faith, as it is written, "**But the righteous one will live from faith**."

[18] For the wrath of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is revealed from heaven upon all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men who restrain the truth in unrighteousness,

[19] because what is known of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is manifest in them, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ manifested it to them.

[20] For his invisible things, from the creation of the world, being understood by the things made, are clearly seen, both his everlasting power and divinity, so that they are without excuse,

[21] because, having known ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, they did not glorify him as ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ or give thanks, but became vain in their reasonings, and their heart, without understanding, was darkened;

[22] asserting that they were wise, they became fools,

[23] and changed the glory of the incorruptible ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for the likeness of an image of corruptible man and of birds and of four-footed animals and of creeping things.

[24] Therefore ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ delivered them up in the desires of their hearts unto uncleanness, that their bodies be dishonored among them,

[25] they who exchanged the truth of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ for the lie, and revered and worshiped the creation rather than the Creator, who is blessed unto the ages. Amen.

[26] Because of this ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ delivered them up unto passions of dishonor; for both their females exchanged the natural use for that which is against nature,

[27] and likewise also the males, having left the natural use of the female, were inflamed in their craving toward one another, males with males working out the unseemliness, and receiving back in themselves the recompense of their error which was due.

[28] And just as they did not approve of having ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in full knowledge, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ delivered them up unto an unapproved mind, to do the things that are not proper,

[29] being filled with all unrighteousness, evil, covetousness, malice; full of envy, murder, strife, guile, malignity; whisperers,

[30] slanderers, haters of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, insolent, haughty, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,

[31] without understanding, covenant-breakers, without natural affection, unmerciful;

[32] they who, having fully known the righteous ordinance of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that those who practice such things are worthy of death, not only do them but also consent with those who practice them.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 4, 6, 7, 8) — systematic. v1 follows Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ (SBLGNT Treg NA28); Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ (WH RP) is the KJV order.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v1) — Greek δοῦλος *doulos*.
- **"called to be an apostle" → "a called apostle"; "called to be saints" → "called saints"; "the called of Jesus Christ" → "called ones of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 6, 7) — Greek κλητὸς ἀπόστολος, κλητοῖς ἁγίοις, κλητοὶ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ: an adjective and a plain genitive; the Greek has no "to be" and no "to belong to".
- **"separated unto the gospel" → "set apart unto the gospel"** (v1) — Greek ἀφωρισμένος εἰς εὐαγγέλιον.
- **"in the holy scriptures" → "in holy Scriptures"** (v2) — Greek ἐν γραφαῖς ἁγίαις, without the article.
- **"which was made of the seed of David" → "who came to be from the seed of David"** (v3) — Greek τοῦ γενομένου ἐκ σπέρματος Δαυίδ; γίνομαι is "become, come to be"; σπέρμα is "seed", as 4:13–18. The Greek has no "descended".
- **"declared to be the Son of God with power" → "marked out as Son of *Elohim* in power"** (v4) — Greek ὁρισθέντος, from ὁρίζω, "mark off, determine, appoint" (Heb 4:7); not a word for "declare". υἱοῦ θεοῦ has no article. "In power" can go with "marked out" or with "Son of *Elohim*".
- **"the spirit of holiness" → "the Spirit of holiness"** (v4) — Greek κατὰ πνεῦμα ἁγιωσύνης, without the article: "a spirit of holiness" is equally possible. It stands opposite "according to the flesh" (v3).
- **"by the resurrection from the dead" → "from the resurrection of the dead"** (v4) — Greek ἐξ ἀναστάσεως νεκρῶν: ἐκ is "from" (since, or on the ground of); νεκρῶν is a plain genitive, "of dead ones"; the Greek has no "his".
- **"for obedience to the faith" → "unto obedience of faith"** (v5) — Greek εἰς ὑπακοὴν πίστεως, a plain genitive without articles: the obedience that faith is, or that comes from faith. The Greek has no "to bring about".
- **"I thank my God … for you all" → "I give thanks to my *Elohim* … concerning all of you"** (v8) — Greek περί (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28); ὑπέρ, "for" (RP), is the KJV reading.
- **"is spoken of" → "is proclaimed"** (v8) — Greek καταγγέλλεται, as Phil 1:17–18.
- **"whom I serve with my spirit" → "whom I worship in my spirit"** (v9) — Greek λατρεύω, the service given to God; ἐν τῷ πνεύματί μου, "in my spirit".
- **"that without ceasing" → "how unceasingly"** (v9) — Greek ὡς ἀδιαλείπτως; ὡς is "how" (as Phil 1:8) or "that".
- **"making request, if by any means now at length I might have a prosperous journey by the will of God" → "beseeching, if somehow now at last I shall be prospered in the will of *Elohim*"** (v10) — Greek δεόμενος εἴ πως ἤδη ποτὲ εὐοδωθήσομαι ἐν τῷ θελήματι τοῦ θεοῦ: a future passive, "be led along a good way"; ἐν is "in". The printed comma puts "always at my prayers" with v9; without it, "always" goes with "beseeching".
- **"to the end ye may be established" → "for you to be made stable"** (v11) — Greek εἰς τὸ στηριχθῆναι ὑμᾶς, a passive. χάρισμα, "gift", has the root of χάρις, "grace".
- **"that I may be comforted together with you by the mutual faith both of you and me" → "to be encouraged together among you through the faith that is in one another, both yours and mine"** (v12) — Greek συμπαρακληθῆναι ἐν ὑμῖν διὰ τῆς ἐν ἀλλήλοις πίστεως; παρακαλέω is "encourage" in this letter where no request or urging is meant. The infinitive has no subject: "I" or "we".
- **"oftentimes I purposed to come unto you, (but was let hitherto,)" → "many times I purposed to come to you, and was hindered until now"** (v13) — Greek προεθέμην (the verb of 3:25), καὶ ἐκωλύθην; καί is "and". The Greek has no brackets.
- **"among other Gentiles" → "among the rest of the nations"** (v13) — Greek ἐν τοῖς λοιποῖς ἔθνεσιν; ἔθνη is "nations" throughout.
- **"to the unwise" → "to foolish"** (v14) — Greek ἀνοήτοις, as Gal 3:1; the four datives have no article.
- **"So, as much as in me is, I am ready" → "so, as far as is in me, there is readiness"** (v15) — Greek οὕτως τὸ κατʼ ἐμὲ πρόθυμον, a clause without a verb: "the readiness on my part"; the Greek has no "I am eager".
- **"the gospel of Christ" → "the gospel"** (v16) — "of Christ" (RP) is not in the critical text (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28).
- **"to the Jew first, and also to the Greek" → "both to the *Yehudi* first and to the *Yevani*"** (v16) — Greek Ἰουδαίῳ τε πρῶτον καὶ Ἕλληνι, without articles; also 2:9–10.
- **"from faith to faith" → "from faith unto faith"** (v17) — Greek ἐκ πίστεως εἰς πίστιν. δικαιοσύνη θεοῦ has no article ("a righteousness of *Elohim*"); the genitive is plain: the righteousness that is God's, or that comes from God.
- **"The just shall live by faith" → "But the righteous one will live from faith"** (v17) — Greek Ὁ δὲ δίκαιος ἐκ πίστεως ζήσεται, as Gal 3:11; the δέ, "but", is part of the words quoted. "From faith" can go with "the righteous one" or with "will live". The Hebrew of Hab 2:4 has "by his faithfulness"; the Greek as printed has no "his".
- **"against all ungodliness" → "upon all ungodliness"** (v18) — Greek ἐπί with the accusative.
- **"who hold the truth in unrighteousness" → "who restrain the truth in unrighteousness"** (v18) — Greek κατεχόντων, "hold down, restrain" (2 Thess 2:6–7) or "hold fast"; ἐν ἀδικίᾳ is "in unrighteousness", not "by their unrighteousness".
- **"that which may be known of God is manifest in them; for God hath shewed it" → "what is known of *Elohim* is manifest in them, for *Elohim* manifested it"** (v19) — Greek τὸ γνωστὸν τοῦ θεοῦ, "the known (or knowable) of God", a genitive, not "about"; φανερόν … ἐφανέρωσεν, one root; ἐν αὐτοῖς is "in them" or "among them". "It" is supplied.
- **"the invisible things of him … are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made" → "his invisible things … being understood by the things made, are clearly seen"** (v20) — Greek τὰ ἀόρατα αὐτοῦ … τοῖς ποιήμασιν νοούμενα καθορᾶται; the Greek has no "attributes", no "namely" and no "nature".
- **"his eternal power and Godhead" → "his everlasting power and divinity"** (v20) — Greek ἀΐδιος (Jude 6), a different word from αἰώνιος, "eternal"; θειότης, "divinity", a different word from θεότης, "deity" (Col 2:9).
- **"so that they are without excuse" kept** (v20) — Greek εἰς τὸ εἶναι αὐτοὺς ἀναπολογήτους, purpose or result ("that they may be"). The sentence runs on into v21.
- **"when they knew God, they glorified him not as God, neither were thankful" → "having known *Elohim*, they did not glorify him as *Elohim* or give thanks"** (v21) — Greek γνόντες, a participle; the Greek has no "although". "Him" is supplied.
- **"became vain in their imaginations, and their foolish heart was darkened" → "became vain in their reasonings, and their heart, without understanding, was darkened"** (v21) — Greek ἐματαιώθησαν (passive, root of μάταιος, "vain"); διαλογισμοί, "reasonings" (Phil 2:14 "disputings"); ἀσύνετος, "without understanding", also v31.
- **"Professing themselves to be wise" → "asserting that they were wise"** (v22) — Greek φάσκοντες εἶναι σοφοί; ἐμωράνθησαν is passive, "were made fools".
- **"changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man" → "changed the glory of the incorruptible *Elohim* for the likeness of an image of corruptible man"** (v23) — Greek ἤλλαξαν … ἐν ὁμοιώματι εἰκόνος (Ps 106:20 in Greek): two nouns, "likeness of an image"; ἄφθαρτος / φθαρτός are "incorruptible / corruptible", not "immortal / mortal". ἀλλάσσω is "change" here, μεταλλάσσω "exchange" in v25–26.
- **"gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, to dishonour their own bodies between themselves" → "delivered them up in the desires of their hearts unto uncleanness, that their bodies be dishonored among them"** (v24) — Greek παρέδωκεν, "delivered up" (also v26, 28; 4:25; 8:32), the verb of the gospels for one handed into another's power; ἐν ταῖς ἐπιθυμίαις, "in the desires"; τοῦ ἀτιμάζεσθαι, passive or middle ("to dishonor"); ἐν αὐτοῖς, "among them" (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28); ἐν ἑαυτοῖς, "among themselves" (RP). "Therefore" stands without "also" (+ καί RP).
- **"Who changed the truth of God into a lie" → "they who exchanged the truth of *Elohim* for the lie"** (v25) — Greek τὴν ἀλήθειαν τοῦ θεοῦ, a plain genitive, not "about"; ἐν τῷ ψεύδει, with the article.
- **"worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator" → "revered and worshiped the creation rather than the Creator"** (v25) — Greek ἐσεβάσθησαν καὶ ἐλάτρευσαν: λατρεύω is "worship" as in v9; κτίσις is "creation" or "creature"; παρά is "rather than" or "beyond".
- **"blessed for ever" → "blessed unto the ages"** (v25) — Greek εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας.
- **"vile affections" → "passions of dishonor"** (v26) — Greek πάθη ἀτιμίας, a genitive; the root of "dishonored" in v24.
- **"their women … the men … of the woman … men with men" → "their females … the males … of the female … males with males"** (v26, 27) — Greek θήλειαι, ἄρσενες, θηλείας: "female" and "male" (Gal 3:28), not the words for "woman" and "man". ἐν ἄρσεσιν is literally "in males".
- **"did change the natural use into that which is against nature" → "exchanged the natural use for that which is against nature"** (v26) — Greek χρῆσις, "use"; the Greek has no "relations".
- **"burned in their lust one toward another" → "were inflamed in their craving toward one another"** (v27) — Greek ἐξεκαύθησαν ἐν τῇ ὀρέξει; ὄρεξις is not the word "desire" (ἐπιθυμία, v24).
- **"working that which is unseemly, and receiving in themselves that recompence of their error which was meet" → "working out the unseemliness, and receiving back in themselves the recompense of their error which was due"** (v27) — Greek τὴν ἀσχημοσύνην κατεργαζόμενοι; ἀπολαμβάνοντες, "receive back"; ἣν ἔδει, "which was necessary". ἐν ἑαυτοῖς (SBLGNT Treg NA28 RP); αὑτοῖς (WH) has the same sense.
- **"as they did not like to retain God in their knowledge … a reprobate mind" → "just as they did not approve of having *Elohim* in full knowledge … an unapproved mind"** (v28) — Greek οὐκ ἐδοκίμασαν … ἀδόκιμον, one root: they did not test and approve, so they were delivered up to a mind that fails the test; ἐπίγνωσις is "full knowledge".
- **"those things which are not convenient" → "the things that are not proper"** (v28) — Greek τὰ μὴ καθήκοντα.
- **"Being filled with all unrighteousness, fornication, wickedness" → "being filled with all unrighteousness, evil"** (v29) — "fornication" (πορνείᾳ, RP) is not in the critical text (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28). The words of v29–31 are accusatives that still depend on "delivered them up"; the Greek has no "They were", "They are".
- **"wickedness … maliciousness … deceit, malignity" → "evil … malice … guile, malignity"** (v29) — Greek πονηρία, κακία, δόλος, κακοήθεια.
- **"backbiters, haters of God, despiteful, proud" → "slanderers, haters of *Elohim*, insolent, haughty"** (v30) — Greek καταλάλους (root of "speak against"), ὑβριστάς (1 Tim 1:13), ὑπερηφάνους, ἀλαζόνας (2 Tim 3:2). θεοστυγεῖς is "haters of God" or, as in older Greek, "hated by God".
- **"covenantbreakers, without natural affection, implacable, unmerciful" → "covenant-breakers, without natural affection, unmerciful"** (v31) — "implacable" (ἀσπόνδους, RP) is not in the critical text (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28). ἀσύνθετος is "not keeping an agreement"; ἄστοργος as 2 Tim 3:3; ἀνελεήμων has the root of ἔλεος, "mercy".
- **"knowing the judgment of God" → "having fully known the righteous ordinance of *Elohim*"** (v32) — Greek τὸ δικαίωμα … ἐπιγνόντες: δικαίωμα is what has been laid down as right (also 2:26); the Greek has no "though".
- **"which commit such things … have pleasure in them that do them" → "who practice such things … consent with those who practice them"** (v32) — Greek πράσσοντες twice, "practice"; ποιοῦσιν, "do"; συνευδοκοῦσιν, "be well pleased together with, consent".

### Notes
- **v1–7** are one sentence in Greek.
- **v17** quotes Hab 2:4.
- **v23** uses the words of Ps 106:20.
