# Romans 16

[1] I commend to you our sister Phoebe, a servant of the church at Cenchreae,

[2] that you may welcome her in the Lord in a way worthy of the saints, and help her in whatever she may need from you, for she has been a patron of many and of myself as well.

[3] Greet Priscilla and Aquila, my fellow workers in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*,

[4] who risked their necks for my life, to whom not only I give thanks but all the churches of the Gentiles give thanks as well.

[5] Greet also the church in their house. Greet my beloved Epainetos, who was the firstfruits to the *Mashiach* in Asia.

[6] Greet Mary, who has worked hard for you.

[7] Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and my fellow prisoners. They are well known to the apostles, and they were in the *Mashiach* before me.

[8] Greet Ampliatos, my beloved in the Lord.

[9] Greet Urbanus, our fellow worker in the *Mashiach*, and my beloved Stachys.

[10] Greet Apelles, who is approved in the *Mashiach*. Greet those who belong to the family of Aristobulus.

[11] Greet my kinsman Herodion. Greet those in the Lord who belong to the family of Narcissus.

[12] Greet those workers in the Lord, Tryphaina and Tryphosa. Greet the beloved Persis, who has worked hard in the Lord.

[13] Greet Rufus, chosen in the Lord; also his mother, who has been a mother to me as well.

[14] Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas, Hermas, and the brothers who are with them.

[15] Greet Philologus, Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints who are with them.

[16] Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the churches of the *Mashiach* greet you.

[17] I appeal to you, brothers, to watch out for those who cause divisions and create obstacles contrary to the doctrine that you have been taught; avoid them.

[18] For such persons do not serve our Lord the *Mashiach*, but their own appetites, and by smooth talk and flattery they deceive the hearts of the naive.

[19] For your obedience is known to all, so that I rejoice over you, but I want you to be wise as to what is good and innocent as to what is evil.

[20] **The *Elohim* of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet.** The grace of our Lord *Yeshua* be with you.

[21] Timothy, my fellow worker, greets you; so do Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my kinsmen.

[22] I Tertius, who wrote this letter, greet you in the Lord.

[23] Gaius, who is host to me and to the whole church, greets you. Erastus, the city treasurer, and our brother Quartus, greet you.

[25] Now to him who is able to strengthen you according to my gospel and the preaching of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, according to the revelation of the mystery that was kept secret for long ages

[26] but has now been disclosed and through the prophetic writings has been made known to all nations, according to the command of the eternal *Elohim*, to bring about the obedience of faith —

[27] to the only wise *Elohim* be glory forevermore through *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*! Amen.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" / "Christ Jesus" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v3, 18, 20, 25, 27) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v20, 26, 27) — systematic.
- **"church"** (v1, 4, 5, 16, 23) — ἐκκλησία *ekklesia* = assembly/congregation; KJV "church" retained; the word here means a local gathering of believers, not the later ecclesiastical institution. v4, 16 "churches" (Greek plural).
- **"Junia" (v7)** — Ἰουνίαν *Iounian*: female name Junia retained (as opposed to later masculine rendering "Junias"); strong textual and historical evidence for the feminine form. Paul calls her and Andronicus "well known to the apostles" — possibly meaning they themselves are apostles.
- **v24 absent** — "The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen." (v24 KJV) is absent from the earliest and best Greek manuscripts (א B C); omitted here. Noted.
- **"Satan" (v20)** — Σατανᾶς *Satanas*, retained as a proper name; the passage echoes Gen 3:15 (crushing the serpent's head).

### Notes
- **v20** echoes Gen 3:15.
- **v24** absent from earliest MSS.
