# Romans 8

[1] There is therefore now no condemnation for those in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧;

[2] for the law of the Spirit of life in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ set you free from the law of sin and of death.

[3] For what was impossible for the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, in that it was weak through the flesh — ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, having sent his own Son in the likeness of flesh of sin and concerning sin, condemned sin in the flesh,

[4] that the righteous ordinance of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ might be fulfilled in us, who walk not according to flesh but according to Spirit;

[5] for those who are according to flesh think the things of the flesh, but those according to Spirit the things of the Spirit.

[6] For the thinking of the flesh is death, but the thinking of the Spirit is life and peace;

[7] because the thinking of the flesh is enmity toward ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for it is not subject to the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, for neither can it be;

[8] and those who are in flesh cannot please ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[9] But you are not in flesh but in Spirit, if indeed the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ dwells in you. But if anyone does not have the Spirit of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, this one is not his.

[10] But if ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ is in you, the body indeed is dead because of sin, but the Spirit is life because of righteousness.

[11] But if the Spirit of him who raised ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ from the dead dwells in you, he who raised ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ from the dead will make alive also your mortal bodies through his Spirit dwelling in you.

[12] So then, brothers, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live according to flesh;

[13] for if you live according to flesh, you are about to die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the practices of the body, you will live.

[14] For as many as are led by the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, these are sons of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[15] For you did not receive a spirit of slavery again unto fear, but you received a Spirit of adoption as sons, in which we cry, "***Abba*, Father!**"

[16] The Spirit itself testifies with our spirit that we are children of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[17] and if children, also heirs: heirs indeed of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and fellow heirs of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, if indeed we suffer together, that we may also be glorified together.

[18] For I consider that the sufferings of the present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory that is about to be revealed unto us.

[19] For the earnest expectation of the creation eagerly awaits the revelation of the sons of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[20] for the creation was subjected to vanity, not willingly, but because of him who subjected it, upon hope

[21] that the creation itself also will be set free from the slavery of corruption unto the liberty of the glory of the children of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[22] For we know that all the creation groans together and is in birth pains together until now;

[23] and not only so, but we ourselves also, having the firstfruits of the Spirit, we ourselves also groan in ourselves, eagerly awaiting adoption as sons, the redemption of our body.

[24] For in hope we were saved; but hope that is seen is not hope; for who hopes for what he sees?

[25] But if we hope for what we do not see, through endurance we eagerly await.

[26] And in the same way the Spirit also helps our weakness; for what we should pray, as is necessary, we do not know, but the Spirit itself intercedes with unutterable groanings;

[27] and he who searches the hearts knows what the thinking of the Spirit is, because it intercedes for saints according to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[28] And we know that for those who love ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ all things work together unto good, for those who are called according to purpose.

[29] Because those whom he foreknew, he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be firstborn among many brothers;

[30] and those whom he predestined, these he also called; and those whom he called, these he also justified; and those whom he justified, these he also glorified.

[31] What then shall we say to these things? **If ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is for us, who is against us?**

[32] He who indeed did not spare his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how will he not also with him grant us all things?

[33] Who will bring a charge against ⟦elohim|Elohim's|God's⟧ elect? ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is the one who justifies;

[34] who is the one who will condemn? ⟦mashiach|The Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ is the one who died, but rather was raised, who also is at the right hand of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who also intercedes for us.

[35] Who will separate us from the love of ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧? Affliction, or straits, or persecution, or hunger, or nakedness, or danger, or sword?

[36] As it is written,

"**For your sake we are being put to death the whole day;
we were reckoned as sheep of slaughter**."

[37] But in all these things we more than overcome through him who loved us.

[38] For I am persuaded that neither death nor life, nor angels nor principalities, nor things present nor things to come, nor powers,

[39] nor height nor depth, nor any other creation will be able to separate us from the love of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ which is in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our Lord.

## Changes

- **"Jesus" / "Christ Jesus" / "Christ" → "*Yeshua*" / "*Mashiach* *Yeshua*" / "*Mashiach*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"law" → "Torah"** (v3, 4, 7) — Greek νόμος. The law of *Moshe* is "Torah" (v3, 4; v7 "the *Torah* of *Elohim*", as 7:22, 25); in v2 "the law of the Spirit of life" and "the law of sin and of death" are left as "law".
- **"Spirit" / "spirit"** (throughout) — Greek πνεῦμα; the capital letter is a decision of the translation. Without the article in Greek: v4, 5 (κατὰ πνεῦμα), v9 (ἐν πνεύματι, πνεῦμα θεοῦ, πνεῦμα Χριστοῦ), v13, 14 (πνεύματι), v15 (πνεῦμα δουλείας, πνεῦμα υἱοθεσίας). In v10 τὸ πνεῦμα stands against "the body" and can also be the human spirit ("the spirit is life").
- **"to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit" → "for those in the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v1) — Ἰησοῦ WH Treg NA28 ] + μὴ κατὰ σάρκα περιπατοῦσιν ἀλλὰ κατὰ πνεῦμα RP; the words belong to v4. The verse has no verb in Greek; κατάκριμα as 5:16, 18.
- **"hath made me free" → "set you free"** (v2) — Greek ἠλευθέρωσεν, aorist. σε "you" (singular) WH NA28 ] με "me" Treg NIV RP. "In the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*" stands between "life" and "set free" and can go with either.
- **"what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh" → "what was impossible for the *Torah*, in that it was weak through the flesh —"** (v3) — Greek τὸ γὰρ ἀδύνατον τοῦ νόμου, "the impossible thing of the law", a phrase left hanging: the sentence starts again with "*Elohim*". The Greek has no "has done". ἐν ᾧ is "in that" or "in which"; ἠσθένει, "was weak" (as ἀσθενής, 5:6).
- **"sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin" → "having sent his own Son in the likeness of flesh of sin and concerning sin"** (v3) — Greek πέμψας, aorist participle; ἐν ὁμοιώματι σαρκὸς ἁμαρτίας, a genitive (ὁμοίωμα as 5:14; 6:5); περὶ ἁμαρτίας, "concerning sin", in the Septuagint also the name of the sin offering (Lev 5:6–7). No word "offering" is in the Greek.
- **"the righteousness of the law" → "the righteous ordinance of the *Torah*"** (v4) — Greek τὸ δικαίωμα, singular (as 1:32; 2:26; see 5:16, 18).
- **"after the flesh … after the Spirit" → "according to flesh … according to Spirit"** (v4, 5, 12, 13) — Greek κατὰ σάρκα, κατὰ πνεῦμα, without articles (as Gal 4:29).
- **"they that are after the flesh do mind the things of the flesh" → "those who are according to flesh think the things of the flesh"** (v5) — Greek οἱ … ὄντες, "those who are" (not "live"); φρονοῦσιν, "think" (as Phil 2:2, 5; 3:19).
- **"to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace" → "the thinking of the flesh is death, but the thinking of the Spirit is life and peace"** (v6, 7, 27) — Greek τὸ φρόνημα τῆς σαρκός, τοῦ πνεύματος: φρόνημα is the noun of φρονέω (v5), what one thinks and is set upon; "mind" is νοῦς (7:23, 25). The verse has no verb.
- **"the carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be" → "the thinking of the flesh is enmity toward *Elohim*, for it is not subject to the *Torah* of *Elohim*, for neither can it be"** (v7) — Greek ἔχθρα, a noun (as Gal 5:20); εἰς θεόν; ὑποτάσσεται; οὐδὲ γὰρ δύναται.
- **"So then they that are in the flesh" → "and those who are in flesh"** (v8, 9) — Greek δέ; ἐν σαρκί, ἐν πνεύματι, without articles.
- **"if so be that" → "if indeed"; "he is none of his" → "this one is not his"** (v9) — Greek εἴπερ (also v17); οὗτος οὐκ ἔστιν αὐτοῦ.
- **"if Christ be in you, the body is dead because of sin" → "if the *Mashiach* is in you, the body indeed is dead because of sin"** (v10) — Greek τὸ μὲν σῶμα νεκρόν … τὸ δὲ πνεῦμα ζωή: "indeed … but", with no "although"; the verse has no verb.
- **"he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you" → "he who raised the *Mashiach* from the dead will make alive also your mortal bodies through his Spirit dwelling in you"** (v11) — the verse follows χριστὸν ἐκ νεκρῶν (Treg NA28): ἐκ νεκρῶν Χριστὸν Ἰησοῦν "from the dead the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*" WH ] χριστὸν ἐκ νεκρῶν Treg NA28; τὸν χριστὸν ἐκ νεκρῶν RP. It also follows τοῦ ἐνοικοῦντος αὐτοῦ πνεύματος, a genitive, "through his Spirit dwelling" (WH NA28) ] τὸ ἐνοικοῦν αὐτοῦ πνεῦμα, an accusative, "because of his Spirit dwelling" (Treg RP; printed in SBLGNT). ζῳοποιήσει, "make alive" (as Gal 3:21). τὸν Ἰησοῦν WH Treg NA28 ] without the article RP.
- **"ye shall die: but if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body" → "you are about to die; but if by the Spirit you put to death the practices of the body"** (v13) — Greek μέλλετε ἀποθνῄσκειν; πνεύματι, a dative; θανατοῦτε (as 7:4); τὰς πράξεις (as Col 3:9), the noun of "practice" (πράσσω, 7:15, 19). "Body" is σῶμα, "flesh" is σάρξ.
- **"they are the sons of God" → "these are sons of *Elohim*"** (v14) — Greek ὅσοι …, οὗτοι, "as many as …, these". υἱοί εἰσιν θεοῦ Treg ] υἱοὶ θεοῦ εἰσιν WH NA28; εἰσιν υἱοὶ θεοῦ RP: the verse follows the order of WH NA28, with no difference in English.
- **"the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry" → "a spirit of slavery again unto fear, but you received a Spirit of adoption as sons, in which we cry"** (v15) — Greek πνεῦμα δουλείας, πνεῦμα υἱοθεσίας, both without the article; ἐλάβετε, aorist, twice; πάλιν εἰς φόβον, with no "fall back"; υἱοθεσία, "placing as a son" (as Gal 4:5; Eph 1:5). ἐν ᾧ, "in which": the relative is neuter with πνεῦμα; the gender is grammatical.
- **"Abba, Father" kept** (v15) — Aramaic אַבָּא *Abba*, "Father", with the Greek ὁ πατήρ, "the Father", added (as Gal 4:6; Mark 14:36).
- **"The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit" → "The Spirit itself testifies with our spirit"** (v16, 26) — Greek αὐτὸ τὸ πνεῦμα, neuter; the gender is grammatical. συμμαρτυρεῖ, "testify together with".
- **"heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together" → "heirs indeed of *Elohim*, and fellow heirs of the *Mashiach*, if indeed we suffer together, that we may also be glorified together"** (v17) — Greek κληρονόμοι μὲν θεοῦ, συγκληρονόμοι δὲ Χριστοῦ, genitives; συμπάσχομεν, συνδοξασθῶμεν, "suffer with", "be glorified with": the Greek has no "him". The printed text makes v16–17 one sentence.
- **"I reckon … not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us" → "I consider … not worthy to be compared with the glory that is about to be revealed unto us"** (v18) — Greek λογίζομαι, of a judgment; οὐκ ἄξια … πρός, "not worthy in comparison with"; τὴν μέλλουσαν … ἀποκαλυφθῆναι (as Gal 3:23); εἰς ἡμᾶς, "unto us".
- **"the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God" → "the earnest expectation of the creation eagerly awaits the revelation of the sons of *Elohim*"** (v19) — Greek ἡ ἀποκαραδοκία (as Phil 1:20) is the subject; κτίσις is "creation" throughout (v19–22, 39); ἀπεκδέχεται (as Gal 5:5; also v23, 25); ἀποκάλυψις is "revelation".
- **"made subject to vanity … by reason of him who hath subjected the same in hope" → "subjected to vanity … because of him who subjected it, upon hope"** (v20) — Greek ματαιότης (as Eph 4:17); ὑπετάγη, ὑποτάξαντα; ἐφʼ ἑλπίδι (as 5:2). ἐφʼ WH NA28 ] ἐπʼ RP Treg, a spelling only.
- **"Because the creature itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the glorious liberty of the children of God" → "that the creation itself also will be set free from the slavery of corruption unto the liberty of the glory of the children of *Elohim*"** (v21) — Greek ὅτι, "that" or "because"; ἐλευθερωθήσεται; τῆς δουλείας τῆς φθορᾶς; τὴν ἐλευθερίαν τῆς δόξης, a genitive, "the liberty of the glory". The Greek has no "obtain".
- **"groaneth and travaileth in pain together" → "groans together and is in birth pains together"** (v22) — Greek συστενάζει καὶ συνωδίνει, two verbs with συν-, present tense; ὠδίνω as Gal 4:19, 27.
- **"not only they, but ourselves also, which have the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body" → "not only so, but we ourselves also, having the firstfruits of the Spirit, we ourselves also groan in ourselves, eagerly awaiting adoption as sons, the redemption of our body"** (v23) — Greek οὐ μόνον δέ (as 5:3, 11), with no "the creation"; αὐτοί twice; τοῦ σώματος, singular. καὶ αὐτοί WH Treg RP NA28 ] omitted NIV; ἡμεῖς καὶ WH Treg NA28 ] καὶ ἡμεῖς RP.
- **"we are saved by hope" → "in hope we were saved"** (v24) — Greek τῇ ἐλπίδι, a dative ("in", "by" or "for hope"); ἐσώθημεν, aorist. The Greek has no "this". τίς "who hopes" WH NA28 ] τίς τί "why does one hope" Treg; τίς τί καί RP.
- **"then do we with patience wait for it" → "through endurance we eagerly await"** (v25) — Greek διʼ ὑπομονῆς; the verb has no object.
- **"Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought" → "And in the same way the Spirit also helps our weakness; for what we should pray, as is necessary, we do not know"** (v26) — Greek συναντιλαμβάνεται, "take hold together with"; τῇ ἀσθενείᾳ, singular WH Treg NA28 ] ταῖς ἀσθενείαις RP; καθὸ δεῖ, "as is necessary". προσευξώμεθα WH Treg NA28 ] προσευξόμεθα RP.
- **"maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered" → "intercedes with unutterable groanings"** (v26) — ὑπερεντυγχάνει WH Treg NA28 ] + ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν "for us" RP. στεναγμοῖς ἀλαλήτοις: ἀλάλητος is "not spoken" or "not able to be spoken"; the Greek has no "too deep for words".

- **"all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose" → "for those who love *Elohim* all things work together unto good, for those who are called according to purpose"** (v28) — Greek πάντα συνεργεῖ εἰς ἀγαθόν: πάντα can be the subject ("all things work together") or the object ("he works all things together"). κατὰ πρόθεσιν has no "his". συνεργεῖ Treg NA28 RP ] + ὁ θεός "*Elohim* works" WH.
