# Romans 3

[1] What then is the advantage of the ⟦jew|Yehudi|Jew⟧, or what is the profit of circumcision?

[2] Much in every way. For first, that they were entrusted with the oracles of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[3] For what? If some did not believe, will their unbelief abolish the faithfulness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧?

[4] May it not be! But let ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ be true, and every man a liar, just as it is written,

"**That you may be justified in your words,
and will overcome when you are judged**."

[5] But if our unrighteousness shows the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, what shall we say? Is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ unrighteous, who brings on the wrath? I speak according to man.

[6] May it not be! Otherwise how will ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ judge the world?

[7] But if the truth of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ abounded in my lie unto his glory, why am I also still judged as a sinner?

[8] And why not, just as we are blasphemed and just as some affirm that we say, "Let us do the evil things, that the good things may come"? Their sentence is just.

[9] What then? Are we better off? Not at all; for we before charged both ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ and ⟦greeks|Yevanim|Greeks⟧ all to be under sin,

[10] just as it is written:

"**There is none righteous, not even one;
[11] there is none who understands,
there is none who seeks out ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.
[12] All turned aside, together they became useless;
there is none doing kindness, there is not so much as one.**"

[13] "**Their throat is an opened sepulchre;
with their tongues they used guile.**"

"**The venom of asps is under their lips,**"

[14] "**whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.**"

[15] "**Their feet are swift to shed blood;
[16] crushing and misery are in their ways,
[17] and the way of peace they did not know.**"

[18] "**There is no fear of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ before their eyes.**"

[19] But we know that whatever the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ says, it speaks to those in the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧, that every mouth may be stopped and all the world become answerable to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧;

[20] because from works of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ no flesh will be justified before him, for through ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ is full knowledge of sin.

[21] But now apart from ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has been manifested, being testified to by the ⟦torah|Torah|Law⟧ and the Prophets,

[22] even the righteousness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ through faith of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, unto all who believe; for there is no distinction;

[23] for **all sinned and are in lack of the glory of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,**

**[24] being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧**,

[25] whom ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ set forth as a propitiatory through faith in his blood, unto a demonstration of his righteousness because of the passing over of the sins that had happened before,

[26] in the forbearance of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, toward the demonstration of his righteousness in the present time, that he might be righteous and justifying the one who is from faith of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧.

[27] Where then is the boasting? It was shut out. Through what kind of law? Of the works? No, but through a law of faith.

[28] For we consider a man to be justified by faith apart from works of ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[29] Or is ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ only? Is he not also of nations? Yes, also of nations,

[30] if indeed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is one, who will justify circumcision from faith and uncircumcision through the faith.

[31] Do we then abolish ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ through the faith? May it not be! Rather, we establish ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

## Changes

- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*" / "the *Mashiach* *Yeshua*"** (v22, 24, 26) — systematic; v24 has the Greek order Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Greek *theos* renders Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"law" → "*Torah*"** (v19, 20, 21, 28, 31) — Greek νόμος; "the" stands only where the Greek has the article (v19 twice, v21 "by the *Torah* and the Prophets"). In v27 "law" is left as the common noun ("what kind of law", "a law of faith").
- **"What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision?" → "What then is the advantage of the *Yehudi*, or what is the profit of circumcision?"** (v1) — Greek τὸ περισσόν, "the surplus"; ὠφέλεια, the noun of "profits" in 2:25.
- **"chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God" → "For first, that they were entrusted with the oracles of *Elohim*"** (v2) — Greek πρῶτον μὲν γὰρ ὅτι ἐπιστεύθησαν; the Greek has no "the Jews". γάρ, "for" (SBLGNT WH NA28 RP; Treg omits).
- **"For what if some did not believe? shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?" → "For what? If some did not believe, will their unbelief abolish the faithfulness of *Elohim*?"** (v3) — the printed text puts the question mark after τί γάρ. ἠπίστησαν, ἀπιστία, πίστιν have the root of "entrusted" (v2): ἀπιστέω is "not believe" or "be unfaithful" (2 Tim 2:13), ἀπιστία "unbelief" (4:20) or "unfaithfulness". πίστις, elsewhere "faith", is here God's own, his faithfulness: "faith of *Elohim*" would be read as faith in him. καταργήσει, "abolish", also v31. The question begins with μή and expects "no".
- **"God forbid" → "May it not be!"** (v4, 6, 31) — Greek μὴ γένοιτο, a wish; it does not name God.
- **"yea, let God be true, but every man a liar" → "But let *Elohim* be true, and every man a liar"** (v4) — Greek γινέσθω, "let him be, let him become (be found)"; the Greek has no "though".
- **"That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged" → "That you may be justified in your words, and will overcome when you are judged"** (v4) — Greek νικήσεις, a future (SBLGNT WH NA28); νικήσῃς, "may overcome" (Treg RP). ἐν τῷ κρίνεσθαί σε is passive ("when you are judged") or middle ("when you go to law"). The Hebrew of Ps 51:4 has "be clear when you judge"; the Greek is translated as printed.
- **"commend the righteousness of God" → "shows the righteousness of *Elohim*"** (v5) — Greek συνίστησιν, "set forth, show", as Gal 2:18 ("commend" in 2 Cor, of persons); θεοῦ δικαιοσύνην has no article.
- **"Is God unrighteous who taketh vengeance?" → "Is *Elohim* unrighteous, who brings on the wrath?"** (v5) — Greek ὁ ἐπιφέρων τὴν ὀργήν; the Greek has no "on us". The question begins with μή and expects "no".
- **"(I speak as a man)" → "I speak according to man"** (v5) — Greek κατὰ ἄνθρωπον λέγω, as Gal 3:15.
- **"for then how shall God judge the world?" → "Otherwise how will *Elohim* judge the world?"** (v6) — Greek ἐπεί; κρινεῖ, a future.
- **"For if the truth of God hath more abounded through my lie" → "But if the truth of *Elohim* abounded in my lie"** (v7) — δέ, "but" (SBLGNT WH NA28); γάρ, "for" (Treg RP): the editions are divided two against two, and NA28 decides. ἐν τῷ ἐμῷ ψεύσματι, "in my lie"; ἐπερίσσευσεν, a simple past; κἀγώ, "I also".
- **"And not rather, (as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say,) Let us do evil, that good may come? whose damnation is just" → "And why not, just as we are blasphemed and just as some affirm that we say, 'Let us do the evil things, that the good things may come'? Their sentence is just"** (v8) — Greek καὶ μή, "and not": the "why" comes from v7 and is repeated here. βλασφημούμεθα, "we are blasphemed"; τὰ κακά, τὰ ἀγαθά, plural with the article; ὧν τὸ κρίμα, "whose sentence"; ἔνδικος, "just" (Heb 2:2).
- **"are we better than they? No, in no wise" → "Are we better off? Not at all"** (v9) — Greek προεχόμεθα, one word, middle or passive: "do we have an advantage?", "are we surpassed?" or "do we put up a defense?"; the Greek has no "we Jews" and no "than they". οὐ πάντως is "not at all" or "not altogether".
- **"we have before proved both Jews and Gentiles, that they are all under sin" → "we before charged both *Yehudim* and *Yevanim* all to be under sin"** (v9) — Greek προῃτιασάμεθα, "bring a charge beforehand"; Ἕλληνας, "Greeks".
- **"There is none righteous, no, not one" → "There is none righteous, not even one"** (v10) — Greek Οὐκ ἔστιν δίκαιος οὐδὲ εἷς.
- **"there is none that seeketh after God" → "there is none who seeks out *Elohim*"** (v11) — Greek ὁ ἐκζητῶν, as Heb 11:6. Both participles have the article (SBLGNT Treg NA28 RP; WH without).
- **"They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is none that doeth good, no, not one" → "All turned aside, together they became useless; there is none doing kindness, there is not so much as one"** (v12) — Greek ἐξέκλιναν, ἠχρεώθησαν, simple pasts; χρηστότητα, "kindness", the word of 2:4; οὐκ ἔστιν ἕως ἑνός. "Doing" stands without the article (SBLGNT WH Treg RP; + ὁ NA28), with no change in the translation.
- **"with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps" → "with their tongues they used guile. The venom of asps"** (v13) — Greek ἐδολιοῦσαν, an imperfect, from the root of δόλος, "guile"; τάφος ἀνεῳγμένος, "an opened sepulchre".
- **"Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness" kept** (v14) — Greek ὧν, a relative that continues v13; ἀρά, "cursing".
- **"Destruction and misery are in their ways" → "crushing and misery are in their ways"** (v16) — Greek σύντριμμα, "a breaking in pieces"; not the words for "destruction" or "ruin".
- **"have they not known" → "they did not know"** (v17) — Greek οὐκ ἔγνωσαν.
- **"it saith to them who are under the law" → "it speaks to those in the *Torah*"** (v19) — Greek λέγει … λαλεῖ, two verbs; τοῖς ἐν τῷ νόμῳ, "in", not "under".
- **"all the world may become guilty before God" → "all the world become answerable to *Elohim*"** (v19) — Greek ὑπόδικος … τῷ θεῷ, "liable to judgment, answerable to", with a dative.
- **"Therefore by the deeds of the law there shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for by the law is the knowledge of sin" → "because from works of *Torah* no flesh will be justified before him, for through *Torah* is full knowledge of sin"** (v20) — Greek διότι, "because"; ἐξ ἔργων νόμου, without articles, as Gal 2:16; πᾶσα σάρξ, "all flesh" (Ps 143:2); διὰ νόμου ἐπίγνωσις ἁμαρτίας, a clause without a verb.
- **"the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets" → "apart from *Torah* the righteousness of *Elohim* has been manifested, being testified to by the *Torah* and the Prophets"** (v21) — Greek χωρὶς νόμου, without the article; δικαιοσύνη θεοῦ, without the article, as 1:17; πεφανέρωται, a perfect; μαρτυρουμένη, a participle: the Greek has no "although".
- **"Even the righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ unto all and upon all them that believe" → "even the righteousness of *Elohim* through faith of *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*, unto all who believe"** (v22) — Greek δέ, here "even, that is"; διὰ πίστεως Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ, the plain genitive of Gal 2:16: the faith (or faithfulness) of *Yeshua*, or faith in him. "And upon all" (καὶ ἐπὶ πάντας, RP) is not in the critical text (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28).
- **"all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God" → "all sinned and are in lack of the glory of *Elohim*"** (v23) — Greek ἥμαρτον, a simple past; ὑστεροῦνται, a present, middle or passive: "come short of" or "are in lack of".
- **"Being justified freely by his grace" kept** (v24) — Greek δικαιούμενοι, a participle; δωρεάν, "as a gift, for nothing" (2 Cor 11:7; Gal 2:21 "for nothing").
- **"Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood" → "whom *Elohim* set forth as a propitiatory through faith in his blood"** (v25) — Greek προέθετο, "set forth publicly" or "purposed" (as 1:13). ἱλαστήριον is not the word "propitiation" (ἱλασμός, 1 John 2:2): it is "that which propitiates", and in the Greek Scriptures the name of the cover of the ark, the "mercy seat" (Heb 9:5; Exod 25:17); "a propitiatory" covers both. "Through faith" has no article (SBLGNT WH Treg; + τῆς NA28 RP, "through the faith"). "In his blood" can go with "faith" or with "set forth as a propitiatory"; the Greek order is kept and the verse has no comma. The Greek has no "to be received".
- **"to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God" → "unto a demonstration of his righteousness because of the passing over of the sins that had happened before, in the forbearance of *Elohim*"** (v25, 26) — Greek εἰς ἔνδειξιν; διὰ τὴν πάρεσιν, "because of the passing over", a different word from "forgiveness" (ἄφεσις); ἁμαρτημάτων, "sins", the deeds; ἐν τῇ ἀνοχῇ. The Greek has no "divine" and does not start a new sentence.
- **"To declare, I say, at this time his righteousness: that he might be just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus" → "toward the demonstration of his righteousness in the present time, that he might be righteous and justifying the one who is from faith of *Yeshua*"** (v26) — Greek πρὸς τὴν ἔνδειξιν, with the article (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28); δίκαιον καὶ δικαιοῦντα, one root, "righteous and making righteous"; τὸν ἐκ πίστεως Ἰησοῦ, "the one from faith of *Yeshua*", the plain genitive of v22.
- **"Where is boasting then? It is excluded. By what law? of works? Nay: but by the law of faith" → "Where then is the boasting? It was shut out. Through what kind of law? Of the works? No, but through a law of faith"** (v27) — Greek ἡ καύχησις, with the article and without "our"; ἐξεκλείσθη, a simple past; διὰ ποίου νόμου, "through what kind of law"; νόμου πίστεως, without articles.
- **"Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law" → "For we consider a man to be justified by faith apart from works of *Torah*"** (v28) — γάρ, "for" (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28); οὖν, "therefore" (RP), is the KJV reading. λογιζόμεθα, "consider", as 2:3; πίστει, a dative; χωρὶς ἔργων νόμου, without articles.
- **"Is he the God of the Jews only? is he not also of the Gentiles?" → "Or is *Elohim* of *Yehudim* only? Is he not also of nations?"** (v29) — Greek ἢ Ἰουδαίων ὁ θεὸς μόνον; "God" stands once; ἐθνῶν, "of nations", without the article.
- **"Seeing it is one God, which shall justify the circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith" → "if indeed *Elohim* is one, who will justify circumcision from faith and uncircumcision through the faith"** (v30) — Greek εἴπερ (SBLGNT WH Treg NA28), "if indeed, since"; ἐκ πίστεως … διὰ τῆς πίστεως, two prepositions, the second with the article.
- **"Do we then make void the law through faith? … yea, we establish the law" → "Do we then abolish *Torah* through the faith? … Rather, we establish *Torah*"** (v31) — Greek νόμον twice without the article; καταργοῦμεν, "abolish", as v3; ἱστάνομεν, "make stand".

### Notes
- **v4** quotes Ps 51:4.
- **v10–18** — a chain of quotations: Ps 14:1–3 (53:1–3); Ps 5:9; 140:3; 10:7; Isa 59:7–8; Ps 36:1, all in their Greek form. The Greek prints them as one unbroken passage; the quotation marks separate the sources.
- **v20** uses the words of Ps 143:2.
