# Romans 9

[1] I speak truth in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, I do not lie, my conscience testifying with me in the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧,

[2] that I have great sorrow and unceasing pain in my heart;

[3] for I could wish that I myself were accursed from ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ for my brothers, my kinsmen according to flesh,

[4] who are ⟦israelite|Yisraelim|Israelites⟧, whose are the adoption as sons and the glory and the covenants and the giving of the ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ and the worship and the promises,

[5] whose are the fathers, and from whom is the ⟦mashiach|Mashiach|Christ⟧ as to what is according to flesh, who is over all, ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ blessed unto the ages. Amen.

[6] But it is not as though the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has fallen. For not all those who are from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, these are ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧;

[7] nor, because they are seed of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, are all children, but "**In ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ shall seed be called for you**."

[8] That is, the children of the flesh, these are not children of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but the children of the promise are reckoned for seed;

[9] for this word is of promise: "**At this time I will come, and Sarah shall have a son**."

[10] And not only so, but also ⟦rebekah|Rivqah|Rebekah⟧, having conceived from one, ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ our father —

[11] for when they were not yet born nor had practiced anything good or bad, that the purpose of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ according to election might remain,

[12] not from works but from him who calls, it was said to her, "**The greater will be a slave to the lesser**";

[13] just as it is written, "**⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ I loved, but ⟦esau|Esav|Esau⟧ I hated**."

[14] What then shall we say? Is there unrighteousness with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? May it not be!

[15] For to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ he says, "**I will have mercy on whomever I have mercy, and I will have compassion on whomever I have compassion**."

[16] So then it is not of him who wants nor of him who runs, but of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ who has mercy.

[17] For the Scripture says to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, "**For this very thing I raised you up, that I might demonstrate in you my power, and that my name might be declared in all the earth**."

[18] So then, on whom he wants he has mercy, and whom he wants he hardens.

[19] You will say to me then, "Why then does he still find fault? For who has resisted his intention?"

[20] O man, rather, who are you who answer back to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧? Will the thing formed say to him who formed it, "Why did you make me thus?"

[21] Or does the potter not have authority over the clay, to make from the same lump one vessel unto honor and another unto dishonor?

[22] But if ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, wanting to demonstrate the wrath and to make known his power, bore in much patience vessels of wrath fitted unto destruction,

[23] and that he might make known the riches of his glory upon vessels of mercy, which he prepared beforehand unto glory,

[24] whom he also called, us, not only from ⟦jews|Yehudim|Jews⟧ but also from nations? —

[25] as he also says in ⟦hosea|Hoshea|Hosea⟧,

"**I will call what is not my people 'my people,'
and her who is not beloved 'beloved';**"

[26] "**and it shall be, in the place where it was said to them, 'You are not my people,'
there they will be called sons of the living ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.**"

[27] But ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ cries out concerning ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: "**If the number of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ be as the sand of the sea, the remnant will be saved;**

**[28] for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ will do a word upon the earth, consummating and cutting it short**."

[29] And just as ⟦isaiah|Yeshayahu|Isaiah⟧ has said before,

"**If ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧ of ⟦tzevaot|Sabaoth|hosts⟧ had not left us a seed,
we would have become as ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧
and been made like ⟦gomorrah|Amorah|Gomorrah⟧**."

[30] What then shall we say? That nations, which did not pursue righteousness, laid hold of righteousness, but the righteousness that is from faith;

[31] but ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, pursuing ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧ of righteousness, did not attain unto ⟦torah|Torah|law⟧.

[32] Why? Because it was not from faith, but as from works. They stumbled at the stone of stumbling,

[33] just as it is written,

"**Behold, I lay in ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense,
and he who believes on him will not be put to shame**."

## Changes

- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v1, 3, 5) — systematic.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v1) — systematic. Greek ἐν πνεύματι ἁγίῳ, without the article.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"my conscience also bearing me witness" → "my conscience testifying with me"** (v1) — Greek συμμαρτυρούσης μοι, "testifying together with me" (μαρτυρέω "testify").
- **"great heaviness and continual sorrow" → "great sorrow and unceasing pain"** (v2) — Greek λύπη "sorrow", ἀδιάλειπτος "unceasing", ὀδύνη "pain".
- **"I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ" kept** (v3) — Greek ηὐχόμην, an imperfect: "I was wishing / praying", the unfulfilled wish "I could wish". ἀνάθεμα εἶναι … ἀπὸ τοῦ Χριστοῦ: "to be accursed, away from the *Mashiach*"; the Greek has no "cut off". ὑπέρ "for"; κατὰ σάρκα without the article.
- **"to whom pertaineth" → "whose are"** (v4, 5) — Greek ὧν, a plain genitive without a verb; v3–5 are one sentence.
- **"adoption" → "adoption as sons"** (v4) — Greek υἱοθεσία, as Gal 4:5.
- **"giving of the law" → "giving of the *Torah*"** (v4) — νομοθεσία *nomothesia*, the Torah-giving at Sinai.
- **"the service of God" → "the worship"** (v4) — Greek ἡ λατρεία; the Greek has no "of God".
- **"of whom as concerning the flesh Christ came" → "from whom is the *Mashiach* as to what is according to flesh"** (v5) — Greek ἐξ ὧν ὁ χριστὸς τὸ κατὰ σάρκα, without a verb; τό limits the statement to "that which is according to flesh".
- **"who is over all, God blessed for ever" → "who is over all, *Elohim* blessed unto the ages"** (v5) — Greek ὁ ὢν ἐπὶ πάντων, θεὸς εὐλογητὸς εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας. The old manuscripts have no punctuation. With the printed commas the words describe the *Mashiach*; with a full stop after "flesh" they are a separate blessing: "He who is over all, *Elohim*, be blessed unto the ages." The verse follows the printed punctuation. πάντων is "all things" or "all people".
- **"hath taken none effect" → "has fallen"** (v6) — Greek ἐκπέπτωκεν, "has fallen out", that is, failed; the verb of Gal 5:4.
- **"they are not all Israel, which are of Israel" → "not all those who are from Israel, these are Israel"** (v6) — Greek οὐ γὰρ πάντες οἱ ἐξ Ἰσραήλ, οὗτοι Ἰσραήλ.
- **"In Isaac shall thy seed be called" → "In Isaac shall seed be called for you"** (v7) — Greek Ἐν Ἰσαὰκ κληθήσεταί σοι σπέρμα (Gen 21:12 in the Septuagint). σπέρμα is "seed" throughout (v7, 8, 29), as Gal 3; καλέω is "call" throughout (v7, 12, 24–26).
- **"counted for the seed" → "reckoned for seed"** (v8) — Greek λογίζεται εἰς σπέρμα, the construction of Gal 3:6.
- **"this is the word of promise" → "this word is of promise"** (v9) — Greek ἐπαγγελίας γὰρ ὁ λόγος οὗτος, the genitive put first.
- **"At this time will I come" kept** (v9) — Greek κατὰ τὸν καιρὸν τοῦτον ἐλεύσομαι; the Hebrew of Gen 18:10, 14 ("when the time revives … I will return") is not what Paul wrote.
- **"had conceived by one" → "having conceived from one"** (v10) — Greek ἐξ ἑνὸς κοίτην ἔχουσα, literally "having bed from one". The Greek has no "children" and no "forefather" (τοῦ πατρὸς ἡμῶν, "our father"). The sentence breaks off and is taken up in v12.
- **"being not yet born" kept** (v11) — Greek γεννηθέντων, from γεννάω "beget"; of the two in the womb, "born".
- **"neither having done any good or evil" → "nor had practiced anything good or bad"** (v11) — Greek πραξάντων, φαῦλον (as 2 Cor 5:10); the Byzantine text has κακόν.
- **"might stand" → "might remain"** (v11) — Greek μένῃ, the verb μένω ("abide" of persons), here of a thing: "remain".
- **"not of works, but of him that calleth" → "not from works but from him who calls"** (v12) — Greek οὐκ ἐξ ἔργων ἀλλ' ἐκ τοῦ καλοῦντος. The critical editions count these words to v12; the KJV has them in v11.
- **"The elder shall serve the younger" → "The greater will be a slave to the lesser"** (v12) — Greek ὁ μείζων δουλεύσει τῷ ἐλάσσονι (Gen 25:23 in the Septuagint).
- **"God forbid" → "May it not be!"** (v14) — Greek μὴ γένοιτο.
- **"unrighteousness" kept** (v14) — Greek ἀδικία.
- **"I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy" → "I will have mercy on whomever I have mercy"** (v15) — Greek ἐλεήσω ὃν ἂν ἐλεῶ, a future and a present (Exod 33:19 in the Septuagint).
- **"of him that willeth" → "of him who wants"** (v16, 18, 22) — Greek θέλω; "wish" is kept for βούλομαι. The Greek of v16 has no noun: "it is not of the one wanting nor of the one running".
- **"Even for this same purpose have I raised thee up" → "For this very thing I raised you up"** (v17) — Greek εἰς αὐτὸ τοῦτο ἐξήγειρά σε.
- **"shew my power in thee" → "demonstrate in you my power"; "declared" kept** (v17, 22) — Greek ἐνδείξωμαι, the verb of v22; διαγγελῇ.
- **"who hath resisted his will?" → "who has resisted his intention?"** (v19) — Greek βουλήματι, not θέλημα "will". ἀνθέστηκεν is a perfect with the force "resists". "Why then" follows NA28 (οὖν); Westcott-Hort and Tregelles omit "then".
- **"Nay but, O man, who art thou that repliest against God?" → "O man, rather, who are you who answer back to *Elohim*?"** (v20) — Greek ὦ ἄνθρωπε, μενοῦνγε σὺ τίς εἶ ὁ ἀνταποκρινόμενος.
- **"power" → "authority"** (v21) — Greek ἐξουσίαν.
- **"What if God, willing" → "But if *Elohim*, wanting"** (v22) — Greek εἰ δέ, "but if": a condition whose conclusion is never stated; the sentence of v22–24 is unfinished. θέλων can be "because he wanted" or "although he wanted".
- **"his wrath" → "the wrath"; "his power" kept** (v22) — Greek τὴν ὀργήν, without "his"; τὸ δυνατὸν αὐτοῦ, "what is powerful of him", a different form from δύναμις in v17.
- **"endured with much longsuffering" → "bore in much patience"** (v22) — Greek ἤνεγκεν ἐν πολλῇ μακροθυμίᾳ.
- **"fitted to destruction" → "fitted unto destruction"** (v22) — Greek κατηρτισμένα, a passive or middle participle: by whom they were fitted is not said. A different verb from "prepared beforehand" (προητοίμασεν, v23).
- **"on the vessels of mercy" → "upon vessels of mercy"** (v23) — Greek ἐπὶ σκεύη ἐλέους.
- **"of the Gentiles" → "from nations"** (v24, 30) — Greek ἐθνῶν, ἔθνη, without the article.
- **"I will call them my people, which were not my people" → "I will call what is not my people 'my people'"** (v25) — Greek καλέσω τὸν οὐ λαόν μου λαόν μου; Paul's wording, not that of Hos 2:23 in Hebrew.
- **"Though the number … a remnant shall be saved" → "If the number … the remnant will be saved"** (v27) — Greek ἐὰν ᾖ … τὸ ὑπόλειμμα σωθήσεται; the Greek has no "only". ὑπέρ is "for, over, concerning".
- **"he will finish the work, and cut it short in righteousness: because a short work will the Lord make upon the earth" → "*Yahweh* will do a word upon the earth, consummating and cutting it short"** (v28) — Greek λόγον γὰρ συντελῶν καὶ συντέμνων ποιήσει κύριος ἐπὶ τῆς γῆς (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the Byzantine text adds ἐν δικαιοσύνῃ ὅτι λόγον συντετμημένον. λόγος is "word", here also "account, reckoning".
- **"said before" kept; "had left us a seed" kept** (v29) — Greek προείρηκεν; ἐγκατέλιπεν, elsewhere "forsake", here "leave behind". ὡμοιώθημεν, "been made like".
- **"have attained to righteousness" → "laid hold of righteousness"** (v30) — Greek κατέλαβεν, as Phil 3:12.
- **"which followed after the law of righteousness, hath not attained to the law of righteousness" → "pursuing *Torah* of righteousness, did not attain unto *Torah*"** (v31) — Greek διώκων νόμον δικαιοσύνης εἰς νόμον οὐκ ἔφθασεν (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the Byzantine text repeats "of righteousness". νόμος has no article here: "a law of righteousness … unto law".
- **"Because they sought it not by faith, but as it were by the works of the law" → "Because it was not from faith, but as from works"** (v32) — Greek ὅτι οὐκ ἐκ πίστεως ἀλλ' ὡς ἐξ ἔργων, without a verb ("it was" is supplied); "of the law" is in the Byzantine text only.
- **"a stumblingstone and rock of offence" → "a stone of stumbling and a rock of offense"** (v33) — Greek λίθον προσκόμματος καὶ πέτραν σκανδάλου (Isa 8:14). πρόσκομμα is "stumbling" (v32); the second word is σκάνδαλον, everywhere else "stumbling block" (11:9; 14:13; 16:17). English has no "rock of stumbling block", so "rock of offense" stands here and in 1 Pet 2:8 only.
- **"whosoever believeth on him shall not be ashamed" → "he who believes on him will not be put to shame"** (v33) — Greek ὁ πιστεύων ἐπ' αὐτῷ οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται; αὐτῷ is "him" or "it" (the stone). "Whosoever" (πᾶς) is in the Byzantine text only. Worded as 1 Pet 2:6, 8.
- **Multiple OT quotations** (v7, 9, 12, 13, 15, 17, 25–29, 33) — in bold; they are translated from Paul's Greek, not from the Hebrew of the passages quoted.
