# Romans 11

[1] I say then, did ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ thrust away his people? May it not be! For I also am an ⟦israelite|Yisraeli|Israelite⟧, from the seed of ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, of the tribe of ⟦benjamin|Binyamin|Benjamin⟧.

[2] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not thrust away his people whom he foreknew. Or do you not know what the Scripture says in ⟦elijah|Eliyahu|Elijah⟧, how he intercedes with ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ against ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧?

[3] "**Lord, they killed your prophets, they dug down your altars, and I was left alone, and they seek my soul**."

[4] But what does the divine answer say to him? "**I left for myself seven thousand men, who did not bow a knee to Baal**."

[5] So then also in the present time there has come to be a remnant according to election of grace;

[6] but if by grace, it is no longer from works, otherwise grace no longer becomes grace.

[7] What then? What ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ seeks after, this it did not attain, but the election attained it; and the rest were hardened,

[8] just as it is written,

"**⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ gave them a spirit of stupor,
eyes not to see
and ears not to hear,
until this very day**."

[9] And David says,

"**Let their table become a snare and a trap
and a stumbling block and a repayment to them;
[10] let their eyes be darkened so as not to see,
and bend their back at all times**."

[11] I say then, did they stumble that they might fall? May it not be! But by their trespass salvation is to the nations, to provoke them to jealousy.

[12] But if their trespass is riches of the world, and their defeat riches of the nations, how much more their fullness!

[13] But to you I speak, the nations. Inasmuch then as I am an apostle of nations, I glorify my service,

[14] if somehow I may provoke my flesh to jealousy and save some of them.

[15] For if the throwing away of them is reconciliation of the world, what is the welcoming, if not life from the dead?

[16] And if the firstfruits is holy, so also the lump; and if the root is holy, so also the branches.

[17] But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, being a wild olive, were grafted in among them and became a fellow sharer of the root of the fatness of the olive tree,

[18] do not boast against the branches; but if you boast against them, it is not you who bear the root, but the root you.

[19] You will say then, "Branches were broken off that I might be grafted in."

[20] Well; by unbelief they were broken off, and you stand by faith. Do not think high things, but fear;

[21] for if ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ did not spare the branches that are according to nature, neither will he spare you.

[22] See then the kindness and severity of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧: upon those who fell, severity, but upon you the kindness of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, if you remain in the kindness; otherwise you also will be cut off.

[23] And those also, if they do not remain in unbelief, will be grafted in; for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is powerful to graft them in again.

[24] For if you were cut out of the olive tree that is wild according to nature, and against nature were grafted into a good olive tree, how much more will these, who are according to nature, be grafted into their own olive tree!

[25] For I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, of this mystery, that you may not be sensible in yourselves: that a hardening in part has come to ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, until the fullness of the nations has come in,

[26] and so **all ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ will be saved**; just as it is written,

"**Out of ⟦zion|Tziyon|Zion⟧ will come the one who rescues,
he will turn away ungodlinesses from ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧**;
[27] and this is the covenant from me to them,
when I take away their sins."

[28] According to the gospel they are enemies because of you, but according to the election they are beloved because of the fathers;

[29] for **the gifts and the calling of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ are not to be regretted**.

[30] For just as you once disobeyed ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, but now were shown mercy by the disobedience of these,

[31] so also these now disobeyed, that by your mercy they also may now be shown mercy;

[32] for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ shut up all unto disobedience, that he might have mercy on all.

[33] O depth of riches and wisdom and knowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧!
How unsearchable his judgments
and untraceable his ways!
[34] "**For who knew the mind of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the Lord⟧,
or who became his counselor?**"
[35] "**Or who gave to him beforehand,
and it will be repaid to him?**"
[36] Because from him and through him and unto him are all things.
To him be the glory unto the ages. Amen.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Hath God cast away his people? God forbid" → "did *Elohim* thrust away his people? May it not be!"** (v1, 2) — Greek ἀπώσατο, an aorist, as 1 Tim 1:19; μὴ γένοιτο. "Reject" is a different verb (ἀποδοκιμάζω).
- **"of the seed of Abraham" → "from the seed of Abraham"** (v1) — Greek ἐκ σπέρματος Ἀβραάμ; the Greek has no "member".
- **"what the scripture saith of Elias" → "what the Scripture says in Elijah"** (v2) — Greek ἐν Ἠλίᾳ, "in Elijah": in the passage about him.
- **"maketh intercession to God against Israel" → "intercedes with *Elohim* against Israel"** (v2) — Greek ἐντυγχάνει τῷ θεῷ κατὰ τοῦ Ἰσραήλ; the verb of 8:27, 34, "approach with a petition", here against someone.
- **"I am left alone, and they seek my life" → "I was left alone, and they seek my soul"** (v3) — Greek ὑπελείφθην, the verb of "remnant" (ὑπόλειμμα, 9:27); ψυχήν.
- **"the answer of God" → "the divine answer"** (v4) — Greek ὁ χρηματισμός, a divine pronouncement; the word "God" is not in the Greek.
- **"I have reserved to myself" → "I left for myself"** (v4) — Greek κατέλιπον, "left behind"; from it comes λεῖμμα, "remnant" (v5). "To Baal" has the feminine article in Greek (τῇ Βάαλ).
- **"a remnant according to the election of grace" kept** (v5) — Greek λεῖμμα κατ' ἐκλογὴν χάριτος γέγονεν, "has come to be"; "chosen by grace" is not what the Greek says. The article is absent.
- **"otherwise grace is no more grace" → "otherwise grace no longer becomes grace"** (v6) — Greek γίνεται. "It is" is supplied. The second half of the verse in the KJV ("But if it be of works …") is in the Byzantine text only.
- **"Israel hath not obtained that which he seeketh for" → "What Israel seeks after, this it did not attain"** (v7) — Greek ἐπιζητεῖ, a present; ἐπέτυχεν, a different verb from "attain unto" in 9:31 (φθάνω).
- **"the election hath obtained it" → "the election attained it"** (v7) — Greek ἡ ἐκλογή, the abstract noun, not "the elect".
- **"were blinded" → "were hardened"** (v7, 25) — Greek ἐπωρώθησαν, πώρωσις, as 2 Cor 3:14; Eph 4:18; a different word from "harden" in 9:18 (σκληρύνω).
- **"God hath given them the spirit of slumber" → "*Elohim* gave them a spirit of stupor"** (v8) — κατάνυξις *katanyxis*, stupor, numbness.
- **"eyes that they should not see" → "eyes not to see"** (v8, 10) — Greek τοῦ μὴ βλέπειν, an infinitive.
- **"a snare, and a trap, and a stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them" → "a snare and a trap and a stumbling block and a repayment to them"** (v9) — Greek εἰς παγίδα καὶ εἰς θήραν καὶ εἰς σκάνδαλον καὶ εἰς ἀνταπόδομα αὐτοῖς; σκάνδαλον as 14:13 (in 9:33 "a rock of offense"); ἀνταπόδομα from the verb "repay" (v35).
- **"bow down their back alway" → "bend their back at all times"** (v10) — Greek διὰ παντός, as Heb 13:15; not "forever".
- **"Have they stumbled that they should fall?" → "did they stumble that they might fall?"** (v11) — Greek ἔπταισαν, as Jas 3:2; a different verb from "stumble" in 9:32 (προσκόπτω).
- **"through their fall salvation is come unto the Gentiles" → "by their trespass salvation is to the nations"** (v11, 12) — Greek τῷ αὐτῶν παραπτώματι ἡ σωτηρία τοῖς ἔθνεσιν, without a verb. παράπτωμα "trespass" is from the verb "fall beside".
- **"for to provoke them to jealousy" → "to provoke them to jealousy"** (v11, 14) — Greek παραζηλῶσαι αὐτούς, "them"; the Greek does not name Israel here. As 10:19.
- **"the diminishing of them" → "their defeat"** (v12) — Greek ἥττημα, as 1 Cor 6:7; also "loss".
- **"the riches of the world … of the Gentiles" → "riches of the world … of the nations"** (v12) — Greek πλοῦτος κόσμου, πλοῦτος ἐθνῶν, plain genitives without a verb; the Greek has no "means" and no "inclusion" (τὸ πλήρωμα αὐτῶν, "their fullness").
- **"I speak to you Gentiles" → "to you I speak, the nations"** (v13) — Greek ὑμῖν δὲ λέγω τοῖς ἔθνεσιν.
- **"the apostle of the Gentiles, I magnify mine office" → "an apostle of nations, I glorify my service"** (v13) — Greek ἐθνῶν ἀπόστολος, without articles; τὴν διακονίαν μου δοξάζω.
- **"them which are my flesh" → "my flesh"** (v14) — Greek μου τὴν σάρκα; the Greek has no "fellow Jews".
- **"the casting away of them" → "the throwing away of them"; "the receiving of them" → "the welcoming"** (v15) — Greek ἀποβολή (the verb of Heb 10:35), πρόσλημψις (the noun of "welcome", 14:1, 3; 15:7). The Greek has no "their" with the second and no verb in either half.
- **"if the firstfruit be holy, the lump is also holy" → "if the firstfruits is holy, so also the lump"** (v16) — Greek εἰ δὲ ἡ ἀπαρχὴ ἁγία, καὶ τὸ φύραμα; the Greek has no "dough offered as".
- **"with them partakest of the root and fatness" → "became a fellow sharer of the root of the fatness"** (v17) — Greek συγκοινωνὸς τῆς ῥίζης τῆς πιότητος τῆς ἐλαίας ἐγένου (Westcott-Hort, NA28); Tregelles and the Byzantine text have "of the root and of the fatness". ἀγριέλαιος is "wild olive", the tree; ἐν αὐτοῖς "among them".
- **"Boast not against the branches" kept** (v18) — Greek μὴ κατακαυχῶ, as Jas 2:13; the Greek has no "remember".
- **"Well; because of unbelief" → "Well; by unbelief"** (v20) — Greek καλῶς· τῇ ἀπιστίᾳ … τῇ πίστει, plain datives.
- **"Be not highminded" → "Do not think high things"** (v20) — Greek μὴ ὑψηλὰ φρόνει (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); φρονέω is "think", as 12:3, 16.
- **"the natural branches" → "the branches that are according to nature"** (v21, 24) — Greek τῶν κατὰ φύσιν κλάδων; κατὰ φύσιν three times, παρὰ φύσιν "against nature" (as 1:26).
- **"take heed lest he also spare not thee" → "neither will he spare you"** (v21) — Greek οὐδὲ σοῦ φείσεται (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles); NA28 and the Byzantine text have μήπως οὐδέ, "perhaps neither".
- **"Behold therefore the goodness and severity of God" → "See then the kindness and severity of *Elohim*"** (v22) — Greek χρηστότητα καὶ ἀποτομίαν; χρηστότης is "kindness" (Gal 5:22), ἀποτομία "a cutting off short, severity" (the adverb in Titus 1:13 is "sharply").
- **"if thou continue in his goodness" → "if you remain in the kindness"** (v22, 23) — Greek ἐπιμένῃς τῇ χρηστότητι, without "his"; ἐπιμένω as Col 1:23. "Of *Elohim*" after the second "kindness" (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28).
- **"God is able" → "*Elohim* is powerful"** (v23) — Greek δυνατός.
- **"a good olive tree" kept** (v24) — Greek καλλιέλαιον, the cultivated olive; the Greek has no "back".
- **"lest ye should be wise in your own conceits" → "that you may not be sensible in yourselves"** (v25) — the verse follows ἦτε + ἐν (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles); the printed SBLGNT has no preposition ("sensible to yourselves"); NA28 and the Byzantine text have παρ', "in your own sight". φρόνιμος is "sensible", as 12:16.
- **"blindness in part is happened to Israel" → "a hardening in part has come to Israel"** (v25) — Greek πώρωσις ἀπὸ μέρους τῷ Ἰσραὴλ γέγονεν; "in part" can go with "hardening" or with "has come".
- **"the fulness of the Gentiles be come in" → "the fullness of the nations has come in"** (v25) — Greek τὸ πλήρωμα τῶν ἐθνῶν, as v12.
- **"And so all Israel shall be saved" kept** (v26) — Greek καὶ οὕτως, "and so, and in this way"; not "and then".
- **"There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness" → "Out of Zion will come the one who rescues, he will turn away ungodlinesses"** (v26) — Greek ὁ ῥυόμενος, a participle; ἀσεβείας, plural. Paul's Greek has "out of Zion"; the Hebrew of Isa 59:20 has "to Zion".
- **"this is my covenant unto them" → "this is the covenant from me to them"** (v27) — Greek αὕτη αὐτοῖς ἡ παρ' ἐμοῦ διαθήκη, without a verb.
- **"As concerning the gospel, they are enemies for your sakes" → "According to the gospel they are enemies because of you"** (v28) — Greek κατὰ μὲν τὸ εὐαγγέλιον ἐχθροὶ δι' ὑμᾶς, κατὰ δὲ τὴν ἐκλογὴν ἀγαπητοὶ διὰ τοὺς πατέρας, without a verb ("they are" is supplied twice); κατά and διά each stand twice.
- **"the gifts and calling of God are without repentance" → "the gifts and the calling of *Elohim* are not to be regretted"** (v29) — Greek ἀμεταμέλητα, as 2 Cor 7:10: not regretted, not taken back. χαρίσματα, "gifts", is from χάρις "grace".
- **"have not believed God … through their unbelief" → "disobeyed *Elohim* … by the disobedience of these"** (v30–32) — Greek ἠπειθήσατε, ἀπειθείᾳ, ἠπείθησαν, ἀπείθειαν: "disobey, disobedience"; "unbelief" (ἀπιστία) is the word of v20, 23.
- **"have now obtained mercy" → "now were shown mercy"** (v30, 31) — Greek ἠλεήθητε, ἐλεηθῶσιν, passives of "have mercy" (v32).
- **"that through your mercy they also may obtain mercy" → "that by your mercy they also may now be shown mercy"** (v31) — Greek τῷ ὑμετέρῳ ἐλέει ἵνα καὶ αὐτοὶ νῦν ἐλεηθῶσιν. "Your mercy" is the mercy shown to you. The words stand before ἵνα and can also go with what precedes: "these now disobeyed for your mercy". The second "now" is in Westcott-Hort and NA28; Tregelles and the Byzantine text omit it.
- **"God hath concluded them all in unbelief" → "*Elohim* shut up all unto disobedience"** (v32) — Greek συνέκλεισεν … εἰς ἀπείθειαν, the verb of Gal 3:22; τοὺς πάντας, "them all", twice.
- **"O the depth of the riches both of the wisdom and knowledge of God" → "O depth of riches and wisdom and knowledge of *Elohim*"** (v33) — Greek ὦ βάθος πλούτου καὶ σοφίας καὶ γνώσεως θεοῦ: three genitives side by side, or "riches of both wisdom and knowledge".
- **"his judgments" kept; "his ways past finding out" → "untraceable his ways"** (v33) — Greek κρίματα, elsewhere "sentence" (the judge's decision); in the plural "his sentences" would be read in English as sentences of speech. ἀνεξεραύνητα, from ἐραυνάω "search"; ἀνεξιχνίαστοι, "not to be tracked" (Eph 3:8).
- **"hath known … hath been" → "knew … became"** (v34) — Greek ἔγνω, ἐγένετο, aorists.
- **"who hath first given to him, and it shall be recompensed unto him again?" → "who gave to him beforehand, and it will be repaid to him?"** (v35) — Greek προέδωκεν, ἀνταποδοθήσεται; the Greek has no "gift".
- **"For of him, and through him, and to him, are all things" → "Because from him and through him and unto him are all things"** (v36) — Greek ὅτι ἐξ αὐτοῦ καὶ δι' αὐτοῦ καὶ εἰς αὐτὸν τὰ πάντα, without a verb.
- **"to whom be glory for ever" → "To him be the glory unto the ages"** (v36) — Greek αὐτῷ ἡ δόξα εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας, without a verb.
- **OT quotations** (v3-4, 8-10, 26-27, 34-35) — translated from Paul's Greek, not from the Hebrew of the passages quoted; sources in Notes. The doxology (v33-36) is formatted as poetry.

### Notes
- **v3-4** quotes 1 Kings 19:10, 14, 18.
- **v8** echoes Deut 29:4 + Isa 29:10.
- **v9-10** quotes Ps 69:22-23.
- **v26-27** quotes Isa 59:20-21 + 27:9.
- **v34** quotes Isa 40:13.
- **v35** quotes Job 41:11.
