# Romans 12

[1] I exhort you therefore, brothers, through the compassions of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, to present your bodies a sacrifice living, holy, well-pleasing to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, your rational worship;

[2] and do not conform yourselves to this age, but be transformed by the renewal of the mind, so that you may test what is the will of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, the good and well-pleasing and perfect.

[3] For I say, through the grace given to me, to everyone who is among you, not to think more highly than he ought to think, but to think so as to be sober-minded, to each as ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ apportioned a measure of faith.

[4] For just as in one body we have many members, and the members do not all have the same practice,

[5] so we, the many, are one body in ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, and individually members of one another.

[6] And having gifts that differ according to the grace given to us: whether prophecy, according to the proportion of the faith;

[7] whether service, in the service; whether he who teaches, in the teaching;

[8] whether he who exhorts, in the exhortation; he who imparts, in simplicity; he who leads, in diligence; he who has mercy, in cheerfulness.

[9] Let love be unfeigned; abhorring what is evil, being joined to what is good;

[10] in brotherly love tenderly affectionate toward one another, in honor going before one another,

[11] in diligence not slothful, in spirit fervent, being slaves to the Lord,

[12] in hope rejoicing, in affliction enduring, in prayer persevering,

[13] sharing in the needs of the saints, pursuing hospitality.

[14] Bless those who persecute you; bless and do not curse.

[15] To rejoice with those who rejoice, to weep with those who weep.

[16] Thinking the same thing toward one another, not thinking the high things but being led away with the humble. Do not become sensible in your own sight.

[17] Repaying no one evil for evil; taking forethought for things good before all men;

[18] if possible, as far as is from you, being at peace with all men;

[19] not avenging yourselves, beloved, but give place to the wrath, for it is written, "**Vengeance is mine, I will repay**, says the Lord."

[20] But "**if your enemy hungers, feed him; if he thirsts, give him drink; for doing this you will heap coals of fire upon his head**."

[21] Do not be overcome by the evil, but overcome the evil in the good.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v1, 2, 3) — systematic.
- **"Christ" → "*Mashiach*"** (v5) — systematic.
- **"I beseech you" → "I exhort you"** (v1, 8) — Greek παρακαλῶ, here an urging (as 16:17); the same verb and its noun in v8, "he who exhorts, in the exhortation" (παράκλησις, elsewhere "encouragement": 15:4–5).
- **"by the mercies of God" → "through the compassions of *Elohim*"** (v1) — Greek διὰ τῶν οἰκτιρμῶν, as Phil 2:1; "mercy" is ἔλεος.
- **"a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God" → "a sacrifice living, holy, well-pleasing to *Elohim*"** (v1, 2) — Greek θυσίαν ζῶσαν ἁγίαν εὐάρεστον τῷ θεῷ, three adjectives after the noun; εὐάρεστος is "well-pleasing".
- **"your reasonable service" → "your rational worship"** (v1) — Greek τὴν λογικὴν λατρείαν ὑμῶν, in apposition without "which is". λογικός, "of reason, of the word" (as 1 Pet 2:2), is not "spiritual" (πνευματικός); λατρεία is "worship" (9:4).
- **"be not conformed to this world" → "do not conform yourselves to this age"** (v2) — Greek συσχηματίζεσθε (as 1 Pet 1:14), middle or passive; αἰών is "age", not κόσμος "world".
- **"by the renewing of your mind" → "by the renewal of the mind"** (v2) — Greek τοῦ νοός (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); "your" is in the Byzantine text only.
- **"that ye may prove" → "so that you may test"** (v2) — Greek εἰς τὸ δοκιμάζειν, "test, approve"; one verb, not "by testing … discern".
- **"that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God" → "the will of *Elohim*, the good and well-pleasing and perfect"** (v2) — Greek τὸ ἀγαθὸν καὶ εὐάρεστον καὶ τέλειον: either describing the will, or "that which is good and well-pleasing and perfect".
- **"not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think; but to think soberly" → "not to think more highly than he ought to think, but to think so as to be sober-minded"** (v3) — Greek μὴ ὑπερφρονεῖν παρ' ὃ δεῖ φρονεῖν, ἀλλὰ φρονεῖν εἰς τὸ σωφρονεῖν: four forms of φρονέω "think"; the Greek has no "of himself".
- **"according as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith" → "to each as *Elohim* apportioned a measure of faith"** (v3) — Greek ἑκάστῳ ὡς ὁ θεὸς ἐμέρισεν μέτρον πίστεως, as 2 Cor 10:13.
- **"office" → "practice"** (v4) — Greek πρᾶξιν, as 8:13; Col 3:9.
- **"we, being many" → "we, the many"; "every one members one of another" → "individually members of one another"** (v5) — Greek οἱ πολλοί; τὸ δὲ καθ' εἷς.
- **"Having then gifts differing" → "And having gifts that differ"** (v6–8) — Greek ἔχοντες δὲ χαρίσματα … διάφορα. The sentence has no main verb; "let us use them", "let us prophesy", "let us wait" are not in the Greek. χάρισμα is from χάρις "grace".
- **"according to the proportion of faith" → "according to the proportion of the faith"** (v6) — Greek κατὰ τὴν ἀναλογίαν τῆς πίστεως, with the article; the Greek has no "our".
- **"ministry, let us wait on our ministering" → "service, in the service"; "on teaching" → "in the teaching"** (v7) — Greek διακονίαν ἐν τῇ διακονίᾳ; ἐν τῇ διδασκαλίᾳ, elsewhere "doctrine", here the act of the one who teaches.
- **"he that giveth, let him do it with simplicity" → "he who imparts, in simplicity"** (v8) — Greek ὁ μεταδιδοὺς ἐν ἁπλότητι; μεταδίδωμι as 1:11; ἁπλότης is "simplicity, singleness", not "generosity".
- **"he that ruleth, with diligence" → "he who leads, in diligence"** (v8) — Greek ὁ προϊστάμενος, "the one standing before"; σπουδή is "diligence" (also v11).
- **"he that sheweth mercy" → "he who has mercy"** (v8) — Greek ὁ ἐλεῶν, the verb of 9:15–18.
- **"Let love be without dissimulation" → "Let love be unfeigned"** (v9) — Greek ἡ ἀγάπη ἀνυπόκριτος, without a verb ("let … be" is supplied).
- **"Abhor that which is evil; cleave to that which is good" → "abhorring what is evil, being joined to what is good"** (v9–13) — Greek participles and adjectives, kept as such through v13; none of them is an imperative. "Evil" here is πονηρόν; in v17, 21 it is κακόν.
- **"Be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love; in honour preferring one another" → "in brotherly love tenderly affectionate toward one another, in honor going before one another"** (v10) — Greek τῇ φιλαδελφίᾳ … φιλόστοργοι (family affection); προηγούμενοι, "going before, leading the way", or "counting above" (ἡγέομαι "count").
- **"Not slothful in business; fervent in spirit; serving the Lord" → "in diligence not slothful, in spirit fervent, being slaves to the Lord"** (v11) — Greek τῇ σπουδῇ; ζέοντες, "boiling"; δουλεύοντες. τῷ πνεύματι is "in spirit" or "in the Spirit".
- **"patient in tribulation; continuing instant in prayer" → "in affliction enduring, in prayer persevering"** (v12) — Greek ὑπομένοντες, προσκαρτεροῦντες.
- **"Distributing to the necessity of saints; given to hospitality" → "sharing in the needs of the saints, pursuing hospitality"** (v13) — Greek κοινωνοῦντες, "having fellowship with"; διώκοντες, the same verb as "persecute" in v14.
- **"Bless them which persecute you" kept** (v14) — the verse follows τοὺς διώκοντας + ὑμᾶς (Tregelles, NA28, Byzantine); the printed SBLGNT with Westcott-Hort has no "you": "those who persecute". The Greek has no "them" after "curse".
- **"Rejoice with them that do rejoice" → "To rejoice with those who rejoice"** (v15) — Greek χαίρειν, κλαίειν, infinitives.
- **"Be of the same mind one toward another. Mind not high things, but condescend to men of low estate" → "Thinking the same thing toward one another, not thinking the high things but being led away with the humble"** (v16) — Greek φρονοῦντες twice (as Phil 2:2); συναπαγόμενοι, as Gal 2:13; τοῖς ταπεινοῖς is "the humble people" or "the humble things".
- **"Be not wise in your own conceits" → "Do not become sensible in your own sight"** (v16) — Greek μὴ γίνεσθε φρόνιμοι παρ' ἑαυτοῖς, "with yourselves"; φρόνιμος as 11:25.
- **"Recompense to no man evil for evil. Provide things honest in the sight of all men" → "Repaying no one evil for evil; taking forethought for things good before all men"** (v17) — Greek ἀποδιδόντες, προνοούμενοι καλά, participles; worded as 1 Pet 3:9; 2 Cor 8:21.
- **"as much as lieth in you, live peaceably" → "as far as is from you, being at peace"** (v18) — Greek τὸ ἐξ ὑμῶν … εἰρηνεύοντες, a participle.
- **"avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath" → "not avenging yourselves, but give place to the wrath"** (v19) — Greek μὴ ἑαυτοὺς ἐκδικοῦντες … δότε τόπον τῇ ὀργῇ; the Greek has no "of God" and no "never". "I will repay" (ἀνταποδώσω) is a fuller form of "repay" in v17 (ἀποδίδωμι).
- **"Therefore if thine enemy hunger" → "But if your enemy hungers"** (v20) — Greek ἀλλὰ ἐάν (Westcott-Hort, Tregelles, NA28); the Byzantine text has ἐὰν οὖν. ἄνθρακας πυρός, "coals of fire".
- **"Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good" → "Do not be overcome by the evil, but overcome the evil in the good"** (v21) — Greek ὑπὸ τοῦ κακοῦ … ἐν τῷ ἀγαθῷ τὸ κακόν, with the articles; ἐν is "in" or "with".
- **OT quotations** (v19 from Deut 32:35; v20 from Prov 25:21-22) — in bold; translated from Paul's Greek.

### Notes
- **v19** quotes Deut 32:35.
- **v20** quotes Prov 25:21-22.
