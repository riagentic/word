# 2 Peter 1

[1] ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, a slave and apostle of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

to those who have obtained a faith equally precious with us in the righteousness of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Savior ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧:

[2] Grace to you and peace be multiplied in the full knowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our Lord.

[3] As his divine power has bestowed on us all the things that are for life and godliness, through the full knowledge of him who called us by his own glory and virtue,

[4] through which he has bestowed on us the precious and greatest promises, that through these you may become sharers of divine nature, having escaped from the corruption that is in the world in desire.

[5] And for this very reason also, bringing in all diligence, supply in your faith virtue, and in virtue knowledge,

[6] and in knowledge self-control, and in self-control endurance, and in endurance godliness,

[7] and in godliness brotherly affection, and in brotherly affection love.

[8] For these things, being yours and increasing, make you neither idle nor unfruitful unto the full knowledge of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[9] For he with whom these things are not present is blind, being short-sighted, having forgotten the cleansing of his sins of old.

[10] Therefore, brothers, be the more diligent to make your calling and election sure; for doing these things you will never stumble.

[11] For so the entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ will be richly supplied to you.

[12] Therefore I will always be ready to remind you concerning these things, though you know them and have been made stable in the present truth.

[13] And I count it right, as long as I am in this tent, to stir you up by a reminder,

[14] knowing that the putting off of my tent is swift, as also our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ made clear to me.

[15] And I will also be diligent that at every time you may be able, after my departure, to call these things to remembrance.

[16] For it was not by following cleverly devised myths that we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, but as having become eyewitnesses of that one's majesty.

[17] For having received from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father honor and glory, a voice such as this being borne to him by the Majestic Glory: "**This is my Son, my Beloved, in whom I am well pleased**" —

[18] and this voice we ourselves heard borne out of heaven, being with him on the holy mountain.

[19] And we have the prophetic word more sure, to which you do well to pay heed, as to a lamp shining in a murky place, until day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts,

[20] knowing this first, that no prophecy of Scripture comes of one's own interpretation.

[21] For prophecy was never borne by the will of man, but **borne by the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧, men spoke from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧**.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 2, 8, 11, 14, 16) — systematic.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v1) — Aramaic name restored; see 1pe01.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v21) — systematic.
- **"Simon Peter" → "Shimon *Kefa*"** (v1) — Greek Συμεὼν Πέτρος: the Semitic form Συμεών (as Acts 15:14), not the usual Σίμων; "Peter" → "*Kefa*" (Aramaic rock).
- **"servant" → "slave"** (v1) — Greek δοῦλος *doulos*.
- **"like precious faith with us" → "a faith equally precious with us"** (v1) — Greek ἰσότιμον ἡμῖν … πίστιν, "of equal value / honor"; the same root as "precious" (τίμια, v4) and "honor" (τιμήν, v17). λαχοῦσιν "obtained" is properly "received by lot".
- **"through the righteousness" → "in the righteousness"** (v1) — Greek ἐν δικαιοσύνῃ.
- **"of God and our Saviour Jesus Christ" → "of our *Elohim* and Savior *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1) — Greek τοῦ θεοῦ ἡμῶν καὶ σωτῆρος Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ: one article for "*Elohim* and Savior", the same construction as "our Lord and Savior" in v11; the verse adds no second article.
- **"knowledge" → "full knowledge"** (v2, 3, 8; 2:20) — Greek ἐπίγνωσις *epignōsis*; "knowledge" in v5, 6 and 3:18 is the simple γνῶσις *gnōsis*. The two Greek words are kept apart; whether ἐπι- strengthens the sense ("full") or only points it at its object is debated.
- **"According as … hath given" → "As … has bestowed"** (v3, 4) — Greek δεδωρημένης, δεδώρηται, "to give as a gift" (δωρέομαι). v3–4 are a dependent clause (ὡς with a genitive absolute); the verse does not make it a main sentence. In v4 the form can also be passive: "the … promises have been bestowed on us".
- **"to glory and virtue" → "by his own glory and virtue"** (v3) — NA28 ἰδίᾳ δόξῃ καὶ ἀρετῇ, a dative: "by" (means), not "to"; KJV follows διὰ δόξης καὶ ἀρετῆς. ἀρετή is "virtue" here as in v5.
- **"exceeding great and precious promises" → "the precious and greatest promises"** (v4) — Greek τὰ τίμια καὶ μέγιστα … ἐπαγγέλματα; there is no "his".
- **"partakers of the divine nature" → "sharers of divine nature"** (v4) — Greek θείας κοινωνοὶ φύσεως, without article; κοινωνός "one who shares", the root of "fellowship" (κοινωνία).
- **"through lust" → "in desire"** (v4) — Greek ἐν ἐπιθυμίᾳ; the Greek has no word "sinful".
- **"corruption" kept** (v4) — Greek φθορά *phthora*, "decay, perishing"; the same word in 2:12, 19.
- **"giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue" → "bringing in all diligence, supply in your faith virtue"** (v5–7) — Greek παρεισενέγκαντες, "bringing in alongside"; ἐπιχορηγήσατε ἐν, "supply in": each quality is supplied *in* the one before (ἐν seven times). The same verb in v11, "will be supplied".
- **"patience" → "endurance"** (v6) — Greek ὑπομονή.
- **"brotherly kindness" / "charity" → "brotherly affection" / "love"** (v7) — Greek φιλαδελφία ("love of brothers") and ἀγάπη; two different words for love.
- **"if these things be in you … they make you that ye shall neither be barren" → "these things, being yours and increasing, make you neither idle"** (v8) — Greek participles, no "if"; πλεονάζοντα "increasing, being more and more"; ἀργούς "idle, not working" (the verb ἀργεῖ in 2:3); εἰς "unto", not "in". The Greek has no word "qualities" (also v9, 10, 12).
- **"cannot see afar off" → "being short-sighted"** (v9) — Greek τυφλός ἐστιν μυωπάζων, "is blind, being short-sighted"; no "so … that".
- **"hath forgotten that he was purged from his old sins" → "having forgotten the cleansing of his sins of old"** (v9) — Greek λήθην λαβὼν τοῦ καθαρισμοῦ, literally "having received forgetfulness of the cleansing"; a noun, not a clause.
- **"fall" → "stumble"** (v10) — Greek πταίσητε.
- **"sure" kept** (v10, 19) — Greek βεβαίαν, βεβαιότερον, the same word.
- **"I will not be negligent to put you always in remembrance" → "I will always be ready to remind you"** (v12) — NA28 μελλήσω, "I shall be about to"; KJV follows οὐκ ἀμελήσω.
- **"established" → "made stable"** (v12) — Greek ἐστηριγμένους; the same root as "unstable" (2:14; 3:16) and "stability" (3:17).
- **"the present truth" kept** (v12) — Greek ἐν τῇ παρούσῃ ἀληθείᾳ, the truth that is present (with you); the same verb as "are present" in v9.
- **"tabernacle" → "tent"** (v13, 14) — Greek σκήνωμα *skēnōma*, "tent"; the Greek does not say "body".
- **"shortly I must put off" → "the putting off … is swift"** (v14) — Greek ταχινή ἐστιν ἡ ἀπόθεσις: "swift" can mean soon or sudden; the same word in 2:1 ("swift destruction").
- **"decease" → "departure"** (v15) — Greek ἔξοδος *exodos*, "going out".
- **"his majesty" → "that one's majesty"** (v16) — Greek ἐκείνου.
- **"cunningly devised fables" → "cleverly devised myths"** (v16) — Greek σεσοφισμένοις μύθοις *sesophismenois mythois* = "cleverly crafted myths"; "fables" → "myths" (Greek μῦθος *mythos*).
- **"the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ" → "the power and coming of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v16) — systematic.
- **"when there came such a voice to him from the excellent glory" → "a voice such as this being borne to him by the Majestic Glory"** (v17) — Greek φωνῆς ἐνεχθείσης … ὑπό; φέρω "bear, carry" four times in v17–21 ("borne"). The sentence breaks off after the quotation (no main verb) and v18 goes on with "and".
- **"This is my beloved Son" → "This is my Son, my Beloved"** (v17) — NA28 ὁ υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός μου οὗτός ἐστιν, with "my" twice; KJV follows the wording of Matt 17:5. "I" (ἐγώ) is expressed in the Greek, and "am well pleased" is an aorist (εὐδόκησα).
- **"a more sure word of prophecy" → "the prophetic word more sure"** (v19) — Greek ἔχομεν βεβαιότερον τὸν προφητικὸν λόγον: "more sure" is what we have the word as; whether more sure than before or more sure than the voice is left open.
- **"ye do well that ye take heed" → "you do well to pay heed"** (v19) — Greek ποιεῖτε, present, not future.
- **"a dark place" → "a murky place"** (v19) — Greek αὐχμηρῷ, "dry, squalid, gloomy"; not the usual word for "dark".
- **"day star" → "morning star"** (v19) — Greek φωσφόρος *phōsphoros*, "light-bearer", the morning star.
- **"is of any private interpretation" → "comes of one's own interpretation"** (v20) — Greek ἰδίας ἐπιλύσεως οὐ γίνεται. "One's own" can be the prophet's own or the reader's own; the Greek does not say whose.
- **"came not in old time" / "moved by the Holy Ghost" → "was never borne" / "borne by the *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v21) — Greek ἠνέχθη … φερόμενοι, the same verb φέρω twice (and in v17, 18); used of ships driven by wind in Acts 27:15, 17. πνεύματος ἁγίου has no article.
- **"holy men of God spake" → "men spoke from *Elohim*"** (v21) — NA28 ἀπὸ θεοῦ ἄνθρωποι; KJV follows ἅγιοι θεοῦ ἄνθρωποι.
