# 2 Peter 1

[1] ⟦simeon-nt|Shimon|Simeon⟧ ⟦peter|Kefa|Peter⟧, a servant and apostle of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧,

To those who have obtained a faith of equal standing with ours by the righteousness of our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and Savior ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧:

[2] May grace and peace be multiplied to you in the knowledge of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and of ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ our Lord.

[3] His divine power has granted to us all things that pertain to life and godliness, through the knowledge of him who called us to his own glory and excellence,

[4] by which he has granted to us his precious and very great promises, so that through them you may become partakers of the divine nature, having escaped from the corruption that is in the world because of sinful desire.

[5] For this very reason, make every effort to supplement your faith with virtue, and virtue with knowledge,

[6] and knowledge with self-control, and self-control with steadfastness, and steadfastness with godliness,

[7] and godliness with brotherly affection, and brotherly affection with love.

[8] For if these qualities are yours and are increasing, they keep you from being ineffective or unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[9] For whoever lacks these qualities is so nearsighted that he is blind, having forgotten that he was cleansed from his former sins.

[10] Therefore, brothers, be all the more diligent to confirm your calling and election, for if you practice these qualities you will never fall.

[11] For in this way there will be richly provided for you an entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧.

[12] Therefore I intend always to remind you of these qualities, though you know them and are established in the truth that you have.

[13] I think it right, as long as I am in this body, to stir you up by way of reminder,

[14] since I know that the putting off of my body will be soon, as our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧ made clear to me.

[15] And I will make every effort so that after my departure you may be able at any time to recall these things.

[16] For we did not follow cleverly devised myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧, but we were eyewitnesses of his majesty.

[17] For when he received honor and glory from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ the Father, and the voice was borne to him by the Majestic Glory, "**This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased**,"

[18] we ourselves heard this very voice borne from heaven, for we were with him on the holy mountain.

[19] And we have the prophetic word more fully confirmed, to which you will do well to pay attention as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts,

[20] knowing this first of all, that no prophecy of Scripture comes from someone's own interpretation.

[21] For no prophecy was ever produced by the will of man, but **men spoke from ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ as they were carried along by the ⟦ruach-haqodesh|Ruach HaQodesh|Holy Spirit⟧**.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v1, 2, 8, 11, 14, 16) — systematic.
- **"Peter" → "*Kefa*"** (v1) — Aramaic name restored; see 1pe01.
- **"Holy Ghost" → "*Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v21) — systematic.
- **"Simon Peter" → "Simon *Kefa*"** (v1) — "Peter" → "*Kefa*" (Aramaic rock).
- **"diligence" → "effort"** (v5) — Greek σπουδὴν *spoudēn* = "eagerness / diligence / effort"; modernized.
- **"add to your faith virtue" → "supplement your faith with virtue"** (v5) — Greek ἐπιχορηγήσατε *epichorēgēsate* = "supply in addition / supplement"; the "add to" chain (v5-7) = the Greek rhetorical device *sorites* (chain argument); "supplement" captures the stacking structure.
- **"cunningly devised fables" → "cleverly devised myths"** (v16) — Greek σεσοφισμένοις μύθοις *sesophismenois mythois* = "cleverly crafted myths"; "fables" → "myths" (Greek μῦθος *mythos*).
- **"the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ" → "the power and coming of our Lord *Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v16) — systematic.
- **"moved by the Holy Ghost" → "carried along by the *Ruach HaQodesh*"** (v21) — Greek ὑπὸ Πνεύματος Ἁγίου φερόμενοι *hypo Pneumatos Hagiou pheromenoi* = "being carried / borne along by the Holy Spirit"; "moved" in KJV is accurate but "carried along" captures the wind-carrying imagery better (same root as "moved" in Acts 27:15, 17 for ships driven by wind).
