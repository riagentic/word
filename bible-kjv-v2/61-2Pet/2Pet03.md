# 2 Peter 3

[1] This is now, beloved, the second letter I write to you; in both of them I stir up your sincere mind by a reminder,

[2] to remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets, and your apostles' commandment of the Lord and Savior,

[3] knowing this first, that in the last days mockers will come with mockery, going according to their own desires

[4] and saying, "Where is the promise of his coming? For since the fathers fell asleep, all things remain thus from the beginning of creation."

[5] For this escapes them, of their own will, that there were heavens from of old, and an earth holding together out of water and through water, by the word of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[6] through which the world of that time, being flooded with water, perished;

[7] but the present heavens and the earth by the same word have been stored up for fire, being kept for the day of judgment and destruction of the ungodly men.

[8] But let this one thing not escape you, beloved, that **one day with the Lord is as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day**.

[9] The Lord is not slow concerning the promise, as some count slowness, but is patient toward you, not wishing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.

[10] But the day of the Lord will come as a thief, in which the heavens will pass away with a rushing noise, and the elements, burning with heat, will be dissolved, and the earth and the works in it will be found.

[11] All these things being thus dissolved, what sort of people ought you to be in holy ways of conduct and acts of godliness,

[12] waiting for and hastening the coming of the day of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, because of which heavens, being on fire, will be dissolved, and elements, burning with heat, melt!

[13] But new heavens and a new earth we wait for, according to his promise, in which righteousness dwells.

[14] Therefore, beloved, waiting for these things, be diligent to be found by him spotless and unblemished, in peace,

[15] and count the patience of our Lord salvation, as also our beloved brother ⟦paul|Paulus|Paul⟧, according to the wisdom given to him, wrote to you,

[16] as also in all the letters, speaking in them concerning these things; in which are some things hard to understand, which the unlearned and unstable twist, as also the rest of the Scriptures, to their own destruction.

[17] You therefore, beloved, knowing beforehand, be on your guard, lest, being led away together by the error of the unprincipled, you fall from your own stability,

[18] but grow in grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧ ⟦mashiach|the Mashiach|Christ⟧. To him the glory both now and unto the day of the age.

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — systematic.
- **"Jesus Christ" → "*Yeshua* the *Mashiach*"** (v18) — systematic.
- **"in both which I stir up your pure minds by way of remembrance" → "in both of them I stir up your sincere mind by a reminder"** (v1) — Greek ἐν αἷς, plural "in which (letters)"; εἰλικρινῆ διάνοιαν, singular "mind"; ἐν ὑπομνήσει as 1:13.
- **"the commandment of us the apostles of the Lord and Saviour" → "your apostles' commandment of the Lord and Savior"** (v2) — NA28 τῆς τῶν ἀποστόλων ὑμῶν ἐντολῆς τοῦ κυρίου καὶ σωτῆρος: "your", not "us"; two genitives side by side ("of your apostles", "of the Lord and Savior"). The Greek has no "through" and no "given by"; the verse keeps both genitives. "The words spoken beforehand" (προειρημένων ῥημάτων), as Jude 17.
- **"scoffers, walking after their own lusts" → "mockers will come with mockery, going according to their own desires"** (v3) — NA28 ἐν ἐμπαιγμονῇ ἐμπαῖκται, "mockers with mockery" (KJV's text lacks "with mockery"); κατὰ τὰς ἰδίας ἐπιθυμίας … πορευόμενοι, as Jude 16, 18. The Greek has no word "sinful". "In the last days" is literally "at the last of the days".
- **"all things continue as they were" → "all things remain thus"** (v4) — Greek πάντα οὕτως διαμένει.
- **"this they willingly are ignorant of" → "this escapes them, of their own will"** (v5) — Greek λανθάνει γὰρ αὐτοὺς τοῦτο θέλοντας, "this is hidden from them, they willing (it)"; it can also be read "it escapes them, when they maintain this, that …". The same verb λανθάνω in v8, "let … not escape you".
- **"the earth standing out of the water and in the water" → "an earth holding together out of water and through water"** (v5) — Greek γῆ ἐξ ὕδατος καὶ διʼ ὕδατος συνεστῶσα: συνίστημι "stand together, hold together, consist" (as Col 1:17); διʼ ὕδατος "through water". "By the word of *Elohim*" stands last and can go with the earth alone or with heavens and earth.
- **"Whereby" → "through which"** (v6) — Greek διʼ ὧν, plural "through which (things)": the waters, or water and word (SBLGNT WH Treg NA27 RP). The other reading, διʼ ὅν (NA28), singular: "because of which", the word of *Elohim*.
- **"the world that then was, being overflowed with water" → "the world of that time, being flooded with water"** (v6) — Greek ὁ τότε κόσμος ὕδατι κατακλυσθείς; the verb of "flood" (κατακλυσμός) in 2:5.
- **"are kept in store, reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition" → "have been stored up for fire, being kept for the day of judgment and destruction"** (v7) — Greek τεθησαυρισμένοι εἰσὶν πυρὶ τηρούμενοι: "for fire" stands between the two verbs and can go with either ("stored up, being kept for fire unto the day …"). ἀπωλείας "destruction" as 2:1, 3.
- **"be not ignorant of this one thing" → "let this one thing not escape you"** (v8) — Greek μὴ λανθανέτω ὑμᾶς, the verb of v5.
- **"is not slack concerning his promise" → "is not slow concerning the promise"** (v9) — Greek οὐ βραδύνει … τῆς ἐπαγγελίας; no "his" and no verb "fulfil".
- **"longsuffering to us-ward" → "patient toward you"** (v9) — NA28 εἰς ὑμᾶς, "you"; μακροθυμεῖ, the verb of "patience" (μακροθυμία, v15).
- **"not willing" → "not wishing"** (v9) — Greek βουλόμενος.
- **"as a thief in the night" → "as a thief"** (v10) — "in the night" is not in the critical text.
- **"with a great noise" → "with a rushing noise"** (v10) — Greek ῥοιζηδόν, the sound of something rushing or whizzing.
- **"the elements shall melt with fervent heat" → "the elements, burning with heat, will be dissolved"** (v10, 12) — Greek στοιχεῖα *stoicheia*, "elements, basic components" (also used of the heavenly bodies); the verse keeps "elements" and does not choose. καυσούμενα "being burned with heat"; λυθήσεται "will be dissolved / loosed" (λύω three times, v10–12); "melt" (τήκεται, present tense) only in v12.
- **"the works that are therein shall be burned up" → "the works in it will be found"** (v10) — Greek εὑρεθήσεται, "will be found" (SBLGNT WH Treg NA27): found, that is, laid open before the judge. NA28 prints οὐχ εὑρεθήσεται, "will not be found", an editors' emendation that no Greek manuscript has. KJV follows κατακαήσεται (RP), "will be burned up". The Greek has no "that are done".
- **"Seeing then that all these things shall be dissolved" → "All these things being thus dissolved"** (v11) — Greek λυομένων, a present participle; οὕτως "thus".
- **"in all holy conversation and godliness" → "in holy ways of conduct and acts of godliness"** (v11) — Greek ἐν ἁγίαις ἀναστροφαῖς καὶ εὐσεβείαις, both nouns plural; the Greek has no "all".
- **"Looking for and hasting unto" → "waiting for and hastening"** (v12) — Greek προσδοκῶντας (three times, v12–14) καὶ σπεύδοντας; σπεύδω with an object is "hasten" or "be eager for".
- **"wherein" → "because of which"** (v12) — Greek διʼ ἥν.
- **"we … look for new heavens" → "new heavens and a new earth we wait for"** (v13) — the Greek puts "new heavens and a new earth" first.
- **"without spot, and blameless" → "spotless and unblemished"** (v14) — Greek ἄσπιλοι καὶ ἀμώμητοι, the opposite of "spots and blemishes" (2:13). "By him" (αὐτῷ) can also be "for him" or "in his sight".
- **"account that the longsuffering of our Lord is salvation" → "count the patience of our Lord salvation"** (v15) — Greek τὴν … μακροθυμίαν σωτηρίαν ἡγεῖσθε; ἡγέομαι "count" as in v9; 1:13; 2:13.
- **"in all his epistles" → "in all the letters"** (v16) — Greek ἐν πάσαις ταῖς ἐπιστολαῖς; no "his".
- **"which they that are unlearned and unstable wrest" → "which the unlearned and unstable twist"** (v16) — Greek στρεβλοῦσιν, present (SBLGNT WH Treg NA27 RP). The other reading, στρεβλώσουσιν (NA28), future: "will twist". ἀμαθεῖς "untaught"; ἀστήρικτοι "unstable" as 2:14.
- **"seeing ye know these things before, beware" → "knowing beforehand, be on your guard"** (v17) — Greek προγινώσκοντες has no object; φυλάσσεσθε, the verb "guard" of 2:5.
- **"led away with the error of the wicked" → "led away together by the error of the unprincipled"** (v17) — Greek συναπαχθέντες, "led away along with"; ἀθέσμων as 2:7.
- **"from your own stedfastness" → "from your own stability"** (v17) — Greek ἐκπέσητε τοῦ ἰδίου στηριγμοῦ, "fall out of your own firm standing"; the root of "made stable" (1:12) and "unstable" (2:14; 3:16).
- **"in grace, and in the knowledge" → "in grace and knowledge"** (v18) — Greek ἐν χάριτι καὶ γνώσει τοῦ κυρίου; "of our Lord" can go with "knowledge" alone or with both. γνῶσις, not ἐπίγνωσις (see 1:2).
- **"both now and for ever. Amen." → "both now and unto the day of the age."** (v18) — Greek εἰς ἡμέραν αἰῶνος, "unto the day of the age (of eternity)"; the sentence has no verb. WH and NA28 end here, and the verse follows them; SBLGNT, Treg, NA27 and RP add ἀμήν, "Amen".

### Notes
- **v8** echoes Ps 90:4.
- **v16** — one of the earliest references to Paul's letters as "Scripture."
- **v4, 9, 13** — "promise" is ἐπαγγελία in v4, 9 and ἐπάγγελμα in v13 (and 1:4).
