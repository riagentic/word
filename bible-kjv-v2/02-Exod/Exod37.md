# Exodus 37

[1] And ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ made the ark of acacia wood: two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

[2] And he overlaid it with pure gold, inside and outside, and he made for it a molding of gold around.

[3] And he cast for it four rings of gold, on its four feet: two rings on its one side, and two rings on its second side.

[4] And he made poles of acacia wood, and he overlaid them with gold.

[5] And he brought the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark.

[6] And he made a ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ of pure gold: two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its width.

[7] And he made two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ of gold; of hammered work he made them, from the two ends of the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧:

[8] one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ from the end on this side, and one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ from the end on that side; from the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ he made the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, from its two ends.

[9] And the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ were spreading out wings above, screening the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ with their wings, and their faces each toward his brother; toward the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ were the faces of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[10] And he made the table of acacia wood: two cubits its length, and a cubit its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

[11] And he overlaid it with pure gold, and he made for it a molding of gold around.

[12] And he made for it a frame of a handbreadth around, and he made a molding of gold for its frame around.

[13] And he cast for it four rings of gold, and he placed the rings on the four edges that belong to its four legs.

[14] Alongside the frame were the rings, housings for the poles, to carry the table.

[15] And he made the poles of acacia wood, and he overlaid them with gold, to carry the table.

[16] And he made the utensils that were on the table: its dishes and its pans and its bowls, and the jars with which it is poured out, of pure gold.

[17] And he made the lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work he made the lampstand: its base and its shaft, its goblets, its knobs and its flowers were from it.

[18] And six branches going out from its sides: three branches of the lampstand from its one side, and three branches of the lampstand from its second side.

[19] Three goblets made like almonds on the one branch, a knob and a flower, and three goblets made like almonds on one branch, a knob and a flower: so for the six branches going out from the lampstand.

[20] And on the lampstand four goblets made like almonds, its knobs and its flowers;

[21] and a knob under the two branches from it, and a knob under the two branches from it, and a knob under the two branches from it, for the six branches going out from it.

[22] Their knobs and their branches were from it; all of it one hammered work of pure gold.

[23] And he made its lamps, seven, and its tongs and its firepans, of pure gold.

[24] Of a talent of pure gold he made it and all its utensils.

[25] And he made the altar of incense of acacia wood: a cubit its length and a cubit its width, square, and two cubits its height; its horns were from it.

[26] And he overlaid it with pure gold, its roof and its walls around and its horns, and he made for it a molding of gold around.

[27] And two rings of gold he made for it, under its molding, on its two sides, on its two flanks, for housings for poles, to carry it with them.

[28] And he made the poles of acacia wood, and he overlaid them with gold.

[29] And he made the oil of anointing, holy, and the fragrant incense, pure, the work of a perfumer.

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. Bezalel is named in v1 only; after that "he".
- **"shittim wood" → "acacia wood"** (v1, 4, 10, 15, 25, 28) — Hebrew עֲצֵי שִׁטִּים, literally "woods of acacias".
- **"the ark" kept** (v1, 5) — Hebrew אָרוֹן, a chest. The measures stand before the nouns: "two cubits and a half its length".
- **"pure gold" kept** (v2, 6, 11, 16, 17, 22–24, 26) — Hebrew זָהָב טָהוֹר; טָהוֹר is the word "clean". In v29 the same word is said of the incense. **"within and without" → "inside and outside"** (v2) — literally "from the house and from outside".
- **"a crown of gold" → "a molding of gold"** (v2, 11, 12, 26, 27) — Hebrew זֵר, found only of the ark, the table and the incense altar; uncertain: a raised border or wreath.
- **"to be set by the four corners of it" → "on its four feet"** (v3) — Hebrew פַּעֲמֹתָיו, literally "its steps". The Hebrew has "and two rings on its one side" with "and".
- **"staves" → "poles"; "put the staves into the rings" → "brought the poles into the rings"** (v4, 5, 14, 15, 27, 28) — Hebrew בַּדִּים; וַיָּבֵא "made come".
- **"mercy seat" → "*kapporet*"** (v6–9) — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת, the cover of the ark; from כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (root "cover"): "atonement cover". "Seat" is not in the word.
- **"cherubims" → "*keruvim*"** (v7–9) — Hebrew כְּרֻבִים. **"beaten out of one piece made he them, on the two ends" → "of hammered work he made them, from the two ends"** (v7) — Hebrew מִקְשָׁה, מִשְּׁנֵי קְצוֹת "from the two ends".
- **"One cherub on the end on this side … out of the mercy seat made he the cherubims on the two ends thereof" → "one *keruv* from the end on this side … from the *kapporet* he made the *keruvim*, from its two ends"** (v8) — Hebrew מִקָּצָה מִזֶּה … מִן־הַכַּפֹּרֶת: "from the *kapporet*" means of one piece with it. "Its ends" is the reading tradition (qere קְצוֹתָיו); the written form has the same sense.
- **"spread out their wings on high, and covered with their wings over the mercy seat, with their faces one to another" → "were spreading out wings above, screening the *kapporet* with their wings, and their faces each toward his brother"** (v9) — Hebrew participles (פֹּרְשֵׂי, סֹכְכִים); סָכַךְ עַל, literally "screening over", is the verb of 40:3, 21 and of מָסָךְ "screen"; אִישׁ אֶל־אָחִיו, literally "a man toward his brother". "Wings" has no "their" the first time.
- **"a border of an handbreadth" → "a frame of a handbreadth"** (v12, 14) — Hebrew מִסְגֶּרֶת, literally "what closes in": a rim or rail; uncertain.
- **"upon the four corners that were in the four feet thereof" → "on the four edges that belong to its four legs"** (v13) — Hebrew פֵּאֹת, the word for a "side" or "edge" (38:9), here the corners where the legs are; רַגְלָיו is "its feet, its legs".
- **"Over against the border" → "Alongside the frame"** (v14) — Hebrew לְעֻמַּת. **"the places for the staves" → "housings for the poles"** — Hebrew בָּתִּים, literally "houses".
- **"the vessels … his dishes, and his spoons, and his bowls, and his covers to cover withal" → "the utensils … its dishes and its pans and its bowls, and the jars with which it is poured out"** (v16) — Hebrew קְעָרֹת, כַּפֹּת (literally "palms": shallow bowls), מְנַקִּיֹּת, קְשָׂוֹת; the last two are uncertain. אֲשֶׁר יֻסַּךְ בָּהֵן is the passive of הִסִּיךְ "pour out a drink offering": "with which the drink offering is poured out". KJV "to cover withal" takes the verb as "cover". The order of the four differs from 25:29.
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"; "his shaft, and his branch, his bowls, his knops, and his flowers, were of the same" → "its base and its shaft, its goblets, its knobs and its flowers were from it"** (v17) — Hebrew יְרֵכָהּ (literally "its thigh"), קָנָהּ (literally "its reed": the same word as "branch" in v18–22), גְּבִיעִים, כַּפְתֹּרִים (a rounded swelling; uncertain), פְּרָחִים; מִמֶּנָּה הָיוּ "were from it": of one piece with it.
- **"made after the fashion of almonds" → "made like almonds"** (v19, 20) — Hebrew מְשֻׁקָּדִים, from שָׁקֵד "almond"; whether blossom or fruit is not said. **"in one branch … in another branch" → "on the one branch … on one branch"** (v19) — Hebrew בַּקָּנֶה הָאֶחָד … בְּקָנֶה אֶחָד.
- **"a knop under two branches of the same"** → "a knob under the two branches from it" (v21) — three times, as in the Hebrew.
- **"snuffers" → "tongs"; "snuffdishes" → "firepans"** (v23) — Hebrew מַלְקָחַיִם, מַחְתֹּת. **"seven lamps" → "its lamps, seven"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"the incense altar" → "the altar of incense"** (v25) — Hebrew מִזְבַּח הַקְּטֹרֶת. "Square" stands alone (רָבוּעַ). **"the horns thereof were of the same" → "its horns were from it"** — of one piece with it.
- **"the top of it, and the sides thereof round about" → "its roof and its walls around"** (v26) — Hebrew גַּגּוֹ, קִירֹתָיו: the words for the roof and the walls of a house; the "roof" is the top of the altar.
- **"by the two corners of it, upon the two sides thereof" → "on its two sides, on its two flanks"** (v27) — two Hebrew words, צַלְעֹתָיו and צִדָּיו; צַד is "side" in v18.
- **"the holy anointing oil, and the pure incense of sweet spices, according to the work of the apothecary" → "the oil of anointing, holy, and the fragrant incense, pure, the work of a perfumer"** (v29) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ is the noun "holiness", standing after "the oil of anointing"; קְטֹרֶת הַסַּמִּים "the incense of fragrant substances"; רֹקֵחַ, one who blends ointments. The command is 30:22–38.

### Notes

- **Parallels** — the ark and the *kapporet* (v1–9) are commanded in 25:10–22, the table (v10–16) in 25:23–30, the lampstand (v17–24) in 25:31–40, the altar of incense (v25–28) in 30:1–5.
- **Ark, table, lampstand, incense altar** — constructed in this chapter. The holiest furnishings, Betzalel's most sacred work.
