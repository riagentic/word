# Exodus 37

[1] ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ made the ark of acacia wood: two and a half cubits was its length, a cubit and a half its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

[2] He overlaid it with pure gold inside and outside, and made a molding of gold around it.

[3] He cast four rings of gold for it, for its four feet; two rings on one side of it and two rings on the other side of it.

[4] He made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.

[5] He put the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark.

[6] He made the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ of pure gold: two and a half cubits was its length, and a cubit and a half its width.

[7] He made two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ of gold; of hammered work he made them, on the two ends of the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ —

[8] one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ on the end from this side, and another ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ on the end from that side; of one piece with the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ he made the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ on its two ends.

[9] The ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ spread out their wings above, overshadowing the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ with their wings, with their faces toward each other; toward the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ were the faces of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[10] He made the table of acacia wood: two cubits was its length, a cubit its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

[11] He overlaid it with pure gold and made a molding of gold around it.

[12] He made a rim of a handbreadth around it, and made a molding of gold for its rim around it.

[13] He cast for it four rings of gold and put the rings on the four corners of its four feet.

[14] The rings were close to the rim, as holders for the poles to carry the table.

[15] He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold, to carry the table.

[16] He made the utensils that were on the table — its plates, its dishes, its bowls, and its pitchers for pouring libations — of pure gold.

[17] He made the lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work he made the lampstand — its base, its shaft, its cups, its capitals, and its flowers were of one piece with it.

[18] There were six branches going out of its sides — three branches of the lampstand from one side of it, and three branches of the lampstand from the other side of it.

[19] Three cups made like almond blossoms, each with a capital and a flower, were on one branch; and three cups made like almond blossoms, each with a capital and a flower, on the other branch — so for the six branches going out of the lampstand.

[20] On the lampstand itself were four cups made like almond blossoms, with their capitals and their flowers;

[21] with a capital under each pair of the six branches going out of it.

[22] Their capitals and their branches were of one piece with it; the whole of it was a single piece of hammered work of pure gold.

[23] He made its seven lamps, and its snuffers and its snuff-trays, of pure gold.

[24] Of a talent of pure gold he made it, with all its utensils.

[25] He made the altar of incense of acacia wood; a cubit was its length and a cubit its width — it was square — and two cubits was its height; its horns were of one piece with it.

[26] He overlaid it with pure gold — its top, its sides all around, and its horns. He also made a molding of gold around it.

[27] He made two rings of gold for it under its molding, on its two opposite sides, as holders for the poles to carry it.

[28] He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.

[29] He made the holy anointing oil, and the pure fragrant incense, as the work of a perfumer.

## Changes

- **Parallels ch 25, 30**. Execution of the instructions.

### Notes

- **Ark, table, lampstand, incense altar** — constructed in this chapter. The holiest furnishings, Betzalel's most sacred work.
