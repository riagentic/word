# Exodus 35

[1] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ assembled all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said to them, "These are the words that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded, to do them:

[2] Six days work shall be done, and on the seventh day it shall be holy for you, a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ of ⟦shabbat|shabbaton|Sabbath rest⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. Everyone who does work on it shall be put to death.

[3] You shall not kindle a fire in all your dwelling places on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧."

[4] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, "This is the word that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded, saying,

[5] 'Take from among you a contribution to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. Everyone willing of heart, let him bring it, the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧: gold and silver and bronze,

[6] and blue and purple and scarlet and fine linen and goats' hair,

[7] and skins of rams dyed red and skins of tachash and acacia wood,

[8] and oil for the light, and spices for the oil of anointing and for the fragrant incense,

[9] and onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.

[10] And every one wise of heart among you, let them come and make all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded:

[11] the dwelling, its tent and its covering, its clasps and its boards, its bars, its pillars and its sockets,

[12] the ark and its poles, the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧, and the veil of the screen,

[13] the table and its poles and all its utensils, and the bread of the face,

[14] and the lampstand of the light and its utensils and its lamps, and the oil of the light,

[15] and the altar of incense and its poles, and the oil of anointing, and the fragrant incense, and the screen of the entrance, for the entrance of the dwelling,

[16] the altar of the burnt offering and the grating of bronze that belongs to it, its poles and all its utensils, the basin and its stand,

[17] the hangings of the court, its pillars and its sockets, and the screen of the gate of the court,

[18] the pegs of the dwelling and the pegs of the court, and their cords,

[19] the woven garments, to minister in the Holy, the holy garments for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest, and the garments of his sons, to be priests.'"

[20] And all the congregation of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went out from before ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[21] And they came, every man whose heart lifted him up, and everyone whose spirit made him willing; they brought the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for the work of the tent of meeting and for all its service and for the holy garments.

[22] And the men came, with the women; everyone willing of heart, they brought brooch and ring and signet ring and pendant, every article of gold, and every man who waved a wave offering of gold to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[23] And every man with whom was found blue and purple and scarlet and fine linen and goats' hair and skins of rams dyed red and skins of tachash, they brought them.

[24] Everyone lifting up a contribution of silver and bronze, they brought the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and everyone with whom was found acacia wood for all the work of the service, they brought it.

[25] And every woman wise of heart spun with her hands, and they brought what was spun: the blue and the purple, the scarlet and the fine linen.

[26] And all the women whose heart lifted them up in wisdom spun the goats' hair.

[27] And the chieftains brought the onyx stones and the stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece,

[28] and the spice and the oil, for light and for the oil of anointing and for the fragrant incense.

[29] Every man and woman whose heart made them willing to bring for all the work that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded to do by the hand of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ brought a freewill offering to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[30] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, "See, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has called by name ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ the son of Uri, the son of ⟦hur|Chur|Hur⟧, of the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[31] And he has filled him with the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in wisdom, in understanding and in knowledge and in all work,

[32] and to devise designs, to work in gold and in silver and in bronze,

[33] and in the craft of stone for setting, and in the craft of wood, to work in every work of design.

[34] And to teach he has given in his heart, he and ⟦oholiab|Oholiav|Oholiab⟧ the son of ⟦ahisamach|Achisamach|Ahisamach⟧, of the tribe of Dan.

[35] He has filled them with wisdom of heart, to do all the work of a craftsman and a designer and an embroiderer in the blue and in the purple, in the scarlet and in the fine linen, and a weaver: doers of all work and devisers of designs."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v31) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses and most items of its lists with וְ "and"; where the Hebrew has none (v11–19), none is supplied.
- **"gathered all the congregation of the children of Israel together" → "assembled all the congregation of the sons of Israel"** (v1, 4, 20) — Hebrew וַיַּקְהֵל, the verb of קָהָל "assembly" (the same verb, in another stem, of the people in 32:1); עֵדָה "congregation"; בְּנֵי "sons of".
- **"These are the words which the LORD hath commanded, that ye should do them" → "These are the words that Yahweh has commanded, to do them"** (v1) — Hebrew הַדְּבָרִים "the words"; "you" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"on the seventh day there shall be to you an holy day" → "on the seventh day it shall be holy for you"** (v2) — Hebrew יִהְיֶה לָכֶם קֹדֶשׁ, with the noun "holiness": "there shall be holiness for you". "Day" is not repeated.
- **"a sabbath of rest" → "a *Shabbat* of *shabbaton*"** (v2) — Hebrew שַׁבַּת שַׁבָּתוֹן, as 31:15: שַׁבָּתוֹן is a second noun from שָׁבַת "cease", a Shabbat-keeping, a ceasing. "Rest" is another root.
- **"whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death" → "Everyone who does work on it shall be put to death"** (v2) — Hebrew יוּמָת; here without the infinitive absolute of 31:14, 15 ("surely").
- **"Ye shall kindle no fire throughout your habitations" → "You shall not kindle a fire in all your dwelling places"** (v3) — Hebrew בִּעֵר "kindle, make burn"; מוֹשָׁב "dwelling place", from יָשַׁב "dwell" (another word than מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling", v11).
- **"This is the thing" → "This is the word"** (v4) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר. The verse has לֵאמֹר "saying" twice.
- **"an offering unto the LORD" → "a contribution to Yahweh"** (v5, 21, 24) — Hebrew תְּרוּמָה, literally "what is lifted off" (from הֵרִים "lift up", the verb of v24): a part set aside for Yahweh.
- **"whosoever is of a willing heart" → "Everyone willing of heart"** (v5, 22) — Hebrew נְדִיב לִבּוֹ, literally "the willing one of his heart". נָדִיב, the verb נָדַב "make willing" (v21, 29) and נְדָבָה "freewill offering" (v29) are one root.
- **"an offering of the LORD" → "the contribution of Yahweh"** (v5, 21, 24) — Hebrew תְּרוּמַת יְהוָה. **"brass" → "bronze"** — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת.
- **"blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen, and goats' hair" kept** (v6, 23, 25, 35) — Hebrew תְּכֵלֶת, אַרְגָּמָן, תּוֹלַעַת שָׁנִי (literally "worm of scarlet"), שֵׁשׁ, עִזִּים. The first three are dyes and stand for wool dyed with them; "yarn" is not in the Hebrew. עִזִּים is "goats": "hair" is supplied.
- **"rams' skins dyed red, and badgers' skins, and shittim wood" → "skins of rams dyed red and skins of tachash and acacia wood"** (v7, 23, 24) — Hebrew מְאָדָּמִים "reddened"; תְּחָשִׁים: the animal or kind of leather is not known, and the word is kept; עֲצֵי שִׁטִּים, literally "woods of acacias".
- **"anointing oil … sweet incense" → "the oil of anointing … the fragrant incense"** (v8, 15, 28) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה; קְטֹרֶת הַסַּמִּים, literally "the incense of the fragrant substances". **"spices"** — Hebrew בְּשָׂמִים; in v28 the singular הַבֹּשֶׂם "the spice".
- **"stones to be set" → "stones for setting"** (v9, 27) — Hebrew אַבְנֵי מִלֻּאִים, literally "stones of fillings". **"onyx stones"** — Hebrew שֹׁהַם; the stone is not certainly known. **"breastplate" → "breastpiece"** — Hebrew חֹשֶׁן; the sense of the word is not certain.
- **"every wise hearted among you shall come, and make" → "every one wise of heart among you, let them come and make"** (v10, 25) — Hebrew חֲכַם־לֵב, "wise of heart": חָכְמָה "wisdom" is here the skill of the craftsman (v26, 31, 35).
- **"The tabernacle, his tent, and his covering, his taches, and his boards, his bars, his pillars, and his sockets" → "the dwelling, its tent and its covering, its clasps and its boards, its bars, its pillars and its sockets"** (v11) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling" (the inner curtains, 26:1), אֹהֶל "tent" (the curtains of goats' hair over it, 26:7), מִכְסֶה "covering" (the skins, 26:14); קֶרֶס, קֶרֶשׁ, בְּרִיחַ, עַמּוּד, אֶדֶן.
- **"staves" → "poles"** (v12, 13, 15, 16) — Hebrew בַּדִּים. **"mercy seat" → "*kapporet*"** (v12) — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת, the atonement cover of the ark. **"the vail of the covering" → "the veil of the screen"** — Hebrew פָּרֹכֶת הַמָּסָךְ.
- **"vessels" / "furniture" → "utensils"** (v13, 14, 16) — Hebrew כְּלִי, any made article; in v22 "article".
- **"the shewbread" → "the bread of the face"** (v13) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים, literally so: the bread set before the face of Yahweh (25:30). "Showbread" and "bread of the Presence" are interpretations.
- **"The candlestick also for the light" → "the lampstand of the light"** (v14) — Hebrew מְנֹרַת הַמָּאוֹר; מָאוֹר is a thing that gives light (Gen 1:14–16). **"the oil for the light" → "the oil of the light"** — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן הַמָּאוֹר.
- **"the hanging for the door at the entering in of the tabernacle" → "the screen of the entrance, for the entrance of the dwelling"** (v15) — Hebrew מָסַךְ הַפֶּתַח לְפֶתַח הַמִּשְׁכָּן; פֶּתַח "entrance" twice.
- **"his brasen grate" → "the grating of bronze that belongs to it"** (v16) — Hebrew מִכְבַּר הַנְּחֹשֶׁת אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ. **"the laver and his foot" → "the basin and its stand"** — Hebrew כִּיּוֹר, כֵּן.
- **"The hangings of the court, his pillars, and their sockets" → "the hangings of the court, its pillars and its sockets"** (v17) — Hebrew קְלָעִים. **"pins" → "pegs"** (v18) — Hebrew יְתֵדֹת.
- **"The cloths of service, to do service in the holy place" → "the woven garments, to minister in the Holy"** (v19) — Hebrew בִּגְדֵי הַשְּׂרָד לְשָׁרֵת בַּקֹּדֶשׁ. שְׂרָד is found only in this phrase and its meaning is uncertain (31:10): best supported "woven work". שֵׁרֵת is "minister"; הַקֹּדֶשׁ "the Holy" is the name of the outer room ("place" is not in the Hebrew). **"to minister in the priest's office" → "to be priests"** — Hebrew לְכַהֵן, one verb from כֹּהֵן "priest".
- **"departed from the presence of Moses" → "went out from before Moses"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיֵּצְאוּ … מִלִּפְנֵי.
- **"every one whose heart stirred him up, and every one whom his spirit made willing" → "every man whose heart lifted him up, and everyone whose spirit made him willing"** (v21, 26) — Hebrew נְשָׂאוֹ לִבּוֹ, literally "his heart lifted him"; נָדְבָה רוּחוֹ אֹתוֹ. The verse has "they came … they brought" as two verbs.
- **"for the work of the tabernacle of the congregation, and for all his service" → "for the work of the tent of meeting and for all its service"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew מְלָאכָה "work", עֲבֹדָה "service": two words.
- **"both men and women" → "the men came, with the women"** (v22) — Hebrew הָאֲנָשִׁים עַל־הַנָּשִׁים, literally "the men upon the women": the men in addition to the women, or after them.
- **"bracelets, and earrings, and rings, and tablets, all jewels of gold" → "brooch and ring and signet ring and pendant, every article of gold"** (v22) — four singular nouns in the Hebrew. חָח is a hook or clasp (the exact ornament is uncertain); נֶזֶם is a ring of the nose or the ear, the word of 32:2; טַבַּעַת is a ring, here the seal ring of the finger; כּוּמָז is found only here and in Num 31:50, and its meaning is not known. כְּלִי is "article".
- **"every man that offered offered an offering of gold" → "every man who waved a wave offering of gold"** (v22) — Hebrew הֵנִיף תְּנוּפַת זָהָב, verb and noun of one root: "wave", move to and fro before Yahweh.
- **"every man, with whom was found" kept** (v23, 24) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־נִמְצָא אִתּוֹ.
- **"Every one that did offer an offering of silver and brass" → "Everyone lifting up a contribution of silver and bronze"** (v24) — Hebrew מֵרִים תְּרוּמַת, verb and noun of one root.
- **"all the women that were wise hearted did spin with their hands, and brought that which they had spun" → "every woman wise of heart spun with her hands, and they brought what was spun"** (v25) — Hebrew singular "every woman … with her hands", then plural verbs; מַטְוֶה "what is spun", from טָוָה "spin" (both only in this passage).
- **"whose heart stirred them up in wisdom" → "whose heart lifted them up in wisdom"** (v26) — as v21.
- **"the rulers" → "the chieftains"** (v27) — Hebrew הַנְּשִׂאִם.
- **"spice, and oil for the light" → "the spice and the oil, for light"** (v28) — the Hebrew order; לְמָאוֹר has no article here.
- **"a willing offering" → "a freewill offering"** (v29) — Hebrew נְדָבָה. **"whose heart made them willing"** — Hebrew נָדַב לִבָּם אֹתָם. The Hebrew order of the verse is kept: the long subject first, then "the sons of Israel brought".
- **"the son of Uri, the son of Hur" kept** (v30) — as 31:2; מַטֶּה "tribe" is literally "staff".
- **"filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship" → "filled him with the Spirit of *Elohim*, in wisdom, in understanding and in knowledge and in all work"** (v31) — Hebrew רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים, without the article, as 31:3 and Gen 1:2; רוּחַ is "spirit, wind, breath", and the capital letter is English only. The verse repeats 31:3 in the third person; here "understanding" has no "and" before it.
- **"to devise curious works" → "to devise designs"** (v32, 35) — Hebrew לַחְשֹׁב מַחֲשָׁבֹת, verb and noun of one root (literally "to think thoughts"); in v33 מְלֶאכֶת מַחֲשָׁבֶת "work of design"; in v35 חֹשֵׁב "designer" and חֹשְׁבֵי מַחֲשָׁבֹת "devisers of designs".
- **"in the cutting of stones, to set them, and in carving of wood, to make any manner of cunning work" → "in the craft of stone for setting, and in the craft of wood, to work in every work of design"** (v33) — Hebrew חֲרֹשֶׁת twice, the work of the חָרָשׁ "craftsman" (v35); לְמַלֹּאת is literally "to fill" (the verb of "filled" in v31, 35). "To work" is the verb עָשָׂה "do, make", as in v32.
- **"he hath put in his heart that he may teach" → "to teach he has given in his heart"** (v34) — Hebrew וּלְהוֹרֹת נָתַן בְּלִבּוֹ, in this order; נָתַן is "give" (31:6). הוֹרָה "teach" is the verb from which תּוֹרָה *Torah* comes.
- **"Them hath he filled with wisdom of heart, to work all manner of work, of the engraver, and of the cunning workman, and of the embroiderer … and of the weaver" → "He has filled them with wisdom of heart, to do all the work of a craftsman and a designer and an embroiderer … and a weaver"** (v35) — Hebrew חָרָשׁ "craftsman" (a worker in stone, wood or metal), חֹשֵׁב "designer", רֹקֵם "embroiderer" (one who works in colours; needlework or coloured weaving, uncertain), אֹרֵג "weaver". The colours have the article here.

### Notes

- **Chapter recaps / parallels earlier instructions** — chs 35–39 narrate the execution of the tabernacle plans given in chs 25–31. Deliberate literary pattern: command (25–31) / obedience (35–40). v2–3 take up 31:12–17; v5–9 take up 25:2–7; v30–35 take up 31:1–6.
- **Women's contribution** (v25–26) — women are explicitly named as skilled artisans in spinning.
- **No kindling fire on Sabbath** (v3) — a specific prohibition, found only here. Later rabbinic discussion built on it.
