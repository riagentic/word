# Exodus 30

[1] "And you shall make an altar, a place for burning incense; of acacia wood you shall make it.

[2] A cubit its length and a cubit its width; it shall be square; and two cubits its height; its horns shall be from it.

[3] And you shall overlay it with pure gold, its roof and its walls around and its horns; and you shall make for it a molding of gold around.

[4] And two rings of gold you shall make for it under its molding; on its two sides you shall make them, on its two flanks; and they shall be housings for poles, to carry it with them.

[5] And you shall make the poles of acacia wood, and you shall overlay them with gold.

[6] And you shall place it before the veil that is over the ark of the testimony, before the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ that is over the testimony, where I will meet you.

[7] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall burn on it fragrant incense; morning by morning, when he dresses the lamps, he shall burn it.

[8] And when ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ sets up the lamps between the two evenings, he shall burn it: a continual incense before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ for your generations.

[9] You shall not offer up on it strange incense, or a burnt offering or a grain offering; and a drink offering you shall not pour out on it.

[10] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall make atonement on its horns once in the year; from the blood of the sin offering of atonement, once in the year he shall make atonement on it, for your generations. It is most holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[12] "When you lift up the head of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, as to those of them who are numbered, then they shall give, each man, a ransom of his soul to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ in the numbering of them, and there shall be no plague among them in the numbering of them.

[13] This they shall give, everyone who passes over to those who are numbered: half the shekel, by the holy shekel (twenty gerahs the shekel), half the shekel, a contribution to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] Everyone who passes over to those who are numbered, from twenty years old and upward, shall give the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[15] The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less, than half the shekel, in giving the contribution of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to make atonement for your souls.

[16] And you shall take the atonement money from the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and you shall give it for the service of the tent of meeting; and it shall be for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ a memorial before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to make atonement for your souls."

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[18] "And you shall make a basin of bronze, and its stand of bronze, for washing; and you shall place it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and you shall place water there.

[19] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall wash from it their hands and their feet.

[20] When they go into the tent of meeting, they shall wash with water, and they shall not die; or when they draw near to the altar to minister, to turn an offering by fire into smoke to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[21] they shall wash their hands and their feet, and they shall not die. And it shall be for them an everlasting statute, for him and for his seed, for their generations."

[22] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[23] "And you, take for yourself chief spices: of flowing myrrh five hundred, and of fragrant cinnamon half as much, two hundred and fifty, and of fragrant cane two hundred and fifty,

[24] and of cassia five hundred, by the holy shekel, and of oil of olives a hin.

[25] And you shall make it a holy oil of anointing, a perfume of blending, the work of a perfumer; a holy oil of anointing it shall be.

[26] And you shall anoint with it the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony,

[27] and the table and all its utensils, and the lampstand and its utensils, and the altar of incense,

[28] and the altar of the burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand.

[29] And you shall sanctify them, and they shall be most holy; all that touches them shall be holy.

[30] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons you shall anoint, and you shall sanctify them to be priests to me.

[31] And to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ you shall speak, saying, 'A holy oil of anointing this shall be to me for your generations.

[32] On the flesh of man it shall not be poured, and in its composition you shall not make any like it; it is holy; holy it shall be to you.

[33] A man who blends any like it, and who puts any of it on a stranger, shall be cut off from his peoples.'"

[34] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Take for yourself fragrant substances: stacte and onycha and galbanum, fragrant substances, and pure frankincense; part for part it shall be.

[35] And you shall make it incense, a perfume, the work of a perfumer, salted, pure, holy.

[36] And you shall pound some of it fine, and you shall place some of it before the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet you; most holy it shall be to you.

[37] And the incense that you shall make, in its composition you shall not make for yourselves; holy it shall be to you, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[38] A man who makes any like it, to smell it, shall be cut off from his peoples."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. v1–10 end the speech begun in 25:1; new speeches begin in v11, 17, 22, 34.
- **"an altar to burn incense upon" → "an altar, a place for burning incense"** (v1) — Hebrew מִזְבֵּחַ מִקְטַר קְטֹרֶת: מִקְטָר is a noun, "a place of making smoke", found only here; "incense" (קְטֹרֶת) is of the same root.
- **"the horns thereof shall be of the same" → "its horns shall be from it"** (v2) — Hebrew מִמֶּנּוּ: of one piece with it (27:2).
- **"pure gold"** (v3) — Hebrew זָהָב טָהוֹר; טָהוֹר is the word "clean" (v35). **"the top thereof, and the sides thereof" → "its roof and its walls"** — Hebrew גַּג and קִירֹת, the words for the roof and the walls of a house. **"a crown of gold" → "a molding of gold"** (v3, 4) — Hebrew זֵר, as 25:11; the meaning is uncertain.
- **"by the two corners thereof, upon the two sides of it" → "on its two sides … on its two flanks"** (v4) — Hebrew צַלְעֹתָיו and צִדָּיו, two words for "side". **"places for the staves" → "housings for poles"** — Hebrew בָּתִּים, literally "houses" (26:29).
- **"put" → "place"** (v6, 18, 36) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "give". **"the vail that is by the ark" → "the veil that is over the ark"** (v6) — Hebrew עַל, twice in the verse: the veil is "over" (in front of) the ark, the *kapporet* is "over" the testimony. **"mercy seat"** — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת, the "atonement cover" of the ark (25:17). **"where I will meet with thee" → "where I will meet you"** (v6, 36) — Hebrew אִוָּעֵד, as 29:42.
- **"burn thereon sweet incense" → "burn on it fragrant incense"** (v7) — Hebrew וְהִקְטִיר … קְטֹרֶת סַמִּים, literally "make smoke on it an incense of fragrant substances"; the verb is the one that is "turn into smoke" of the sacrifices on the altar (v20; 29:13); with incense as its object it is "burn incense". **"every morning" → "morning by morning"** — Hebrew בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר. **"when he dresseth the lamps" kept** — Hebrew בְּהֵיטִיבוֹ, literally "when he makes good": trims and sets them right.
- **"lighteth the lamps at even" → "sets up the lamps between the two evenings"** (v8) — Hebrew וּבְהַעֲלֹת … אֶת־הַנֵּרֹת, literally "makes the lamps go up": sets them up on the lampstand, or makes the flame go up (lights them); as 27:20. בֵּין הָעַרְבַּיִם is a dual; which hours are meant is disputed (29:39). **"a perpetual incense" → "a continual incense"** — Hebrew תָּמִיד.
- **"strange incense" kept** (v9) — Hebrew קְטֹרֶת זָרָה, the adjective of זָר "stranger" (v33; 29:33): incense other than the one prescribed in v34–38. **"offer" → "offer up"** — Hebrew הֶעֱלָה, "make go up". **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** — Hebrew מִנְחָה.
- **"once in a year"** (v10) — Hebrew אַחַת בַּשָּׁנָה. **"with the blood" → "from the blood"** — Hebrew מִדַּם. "The sin offering of atonement" is חַטַּאת הַכִּפֻּרִים (cf. Lev 16:18). **"most holy"** (v10, 29, 36) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים, literally "holiness of holinesses".
- **v11–16, word for word.** **"When thou takest the sum of the children of Israel after their number" → "When you lift up the head of the sons of Israel, as to those of them who are numbered"** (v12) — Hebrew כִּי תִשָּׂא אֶת־רֹאשׁ: "lift up the head" is the Hebrew for counting heads (a census); it is kept as in Gen 40:13. לִפְקֻדֵיהֶם is "for their numbered ones". פָּקַד is elsewhere "visit"; of men it is "muster, number".
- **"a ransom for his soul" → "a ransom of his soul"** (v12) — Hebrew כֹּפֶר נַפְשׁוֹ: כֹּפֶר is the price that covers a life (21:30), of one root with כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (v15, 16); it is not the verb פָּדָה "ransom". נֶפֶשׁ is "soul". **"when thou numberest them" → "in the numbering of them"** — Hebrew בִּפְקֹד אֹתָם, an infinitive with no subject, twice. **"plague"** — Hebrew נֶגֶף, literally "a blow" (12:13).
- **"every one that passeth among them that are numbered" → "everyone who passes over to those who are numbered"** (v13, 14) — Hebrew כָּל־הָעֹבֵר עַל־הַפְּקֻדִים: each man as he crosses over to the side of those already counted.
- **"after the shekel of the sanctuary" → "by the holy shekel"** (v13, 24) — Hebrew בְּשֶׁקֶל הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "by the shekel of the holiness": either the sacred standard of weight or the weight kept at the sanctuary ("the shekel of the Holy"). The shekel is a weight, not a coin; a gerah is a twentieth of it.
- **"an offering" → "a contribution"** (v13, 14, 15) — Hebrew תְּרוּמָה, as 25:2. In v14, 15 it is "the contribution of Yahweh".
- **"twenty years old"** (v14) — Hebrew מִבֶּן עֶשְׂרִים שָׁנָה, literally "from a son of twenty years".
- **"shall not give more … shall not give less"** (v15) — Hebrew לֹא־יַרְבֶּה … לֹא יַמְעִיט, "shall not make much … shall not make little"; "give" is supplied from the infinitive that follows (לָתֵת). **"for your souls"** (v15, 16) — Hebrew עַל־נַפְשֹׁתֵיכֶם.
- **"the atonement money"** (v16) — Hebrew כֶּסֶף הַכִּפֻּרִים, literally "the silver of the atonements". **"appoint it for" → "give it for"** — Hebrew וְנָתַתָּ אֹתוֹ עַל. **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (v16, 18, 20, 26, 36) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"a memorial"** — Hebrew זִכָּרוֹן.
- **"laver" → "basin"; "foot" → "stand"** (v18, 28) — Hebrew כִּיּוֹר and כֵּן. "Between the tent of meeting and the altar" is literally "between … and between".
- **"that they die not" → "and they shall not die"** (v20, 21) — Hebrew וְלֹא יָמֻתוּ. **"come near" → "draw near"** (v20) — Hebrew נָגַשׁ. **"to burn offering made by fire" → "to turn an offering by fire into smoke"** — Hebrew לְהַקְטִיר אִשֶּׁה, "to make an *isheh* smoke"; "burn" is another verb (שָׂרַף); the derivation of אִשֶּׁה from "fire" is not certain.
- **"a statute for ever" → "an everlasting statute"; "seed" kept** (v21) — Hebrew חָק־עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age".
- **"Take thou also unto thee principal spices" → "And you, take for yourself chief spices"** (v23) — Hebrew בְּשָׂמִים רֹאשׁ, "spices of head": the best. **"pure myrrh" → "flowing myrrh"** — Hebrew מָר־דְּרוֹר: דְּרוֹר is "freedom, release"; commonly taken as myrrh that flows of itself; uncertain. **"sweet cinnamon" → "fragrant cinnamon"; "sweet calamus" → "fragrant cane"** — Hebrew קִנְּמָן־בֶּשֶׂם, קְנֵה־בֹשֶׂם, "cinnamon of spice", "cane of spice"; what plant the cane is, is not certain. **"five hundred shekels" → "five hundred"** — "shekels" is not in the Hebrew; the weight is named in v24.
- **"an oil of holy ointment, an ointment compound after the art of the apothecary" → "a holy oil of anointing, a perfume of blending, the work of a perfumer"** (v25) — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן מִשְׁחַת־קֹדֶשׁ, "oil of anointing of holiness"; רֹקַח מִרְקַחַת מַעֲשֵׂה רֹקֵחַ, three words of the root רקח "mix spices".
- **"vessels" → "utensils"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew כְּלִי.
- **"whatsoever toucheth them" → "all that touches them"** (v29) — Hebrew כָּל־הַנֹּגֵעַ: "everyone who" or "everything that" (29:37).
- **"that they may minister unto me in the priest's office" → "to be priests to me"** (v30) — Hebrew לְכַהֵן לִי.
- **"Upon man's flesh shall it not be poured"** (v32) — Hebrew עַל־בְּשַׂר אָדָם. **"after the composition of it" → "in its composition"** (v32, 37) — Hebrew מַתְכֹּנֶת, measure, fixed proportion.
- **"Whosoever compoundeth" → "A man who blends"** (v33, 38) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֲשֶׁר. **"a stranger"** — Hebrew זָר: one who is not a priest. **"cut off from his people" → "cut off from his peoples"** — Hebrew מֵעַמָּיו, plural: his kinsfolk.
- **"sweet spices" → "fragrant substances"** (v34) — Hebrew סַמִּים, another word than בְּשָׂמִים "spices" (v23); it stands twice in the verse. **"stacte, and onycha, and galbanum" kept** — נָטָף is literally "drops", a gum that drips (which one is not known); שְׁחֵלֶת is traditionally the lid of a sea snail, uncertain; חֶלְבְּנָה is the resin galbanum. **"pure frankincense"** — Hebrew לְבֹנָה זַכָּה; זַךְ "pure, clear" is another word than טָהוֹר (v3, 35). **"of each shall there be a like weight" → "part for part it shall be"** — Hebrew בַּד בְּבַד, found only here: equal parts, as commonly understood; uncertain.
- **"a perfume, a confection after the art of the apothecary" → "incense, a perfume, the work of a perfumer"** (v35) — Hebrew קְטֹרֶת רֹקַח מַעֲשֵׂה רוֹקֵחַ. **"tempered together, pure and holy" → "salted, pure, holy"** — Hebrew מְמֻלָּח טָהוֹר קֹדֶשׁ: מְמֻלָּח is from מֶלַח "salt"; טָהוֹר is the word "clean", "pure" as of the gold (v3) and of this incense in 37:29 (זַכָּה, "pure" of the frankincense in v34, is another word); קֹדֶשׁ is the noun "holiness".
- **"beat some of it very small" → "pound some of it fine"** (v36) — Hebrew וְשָׁחַקְתָּ … הָדֵק. "To you" at the end of the verse is plural.
- **"to smell thereto" → "to smell it"** (v38) — Hebrew לְהָרִיחַ בָּהּ, "to smell of it", for one's own enjoyment.

### Notes

- **Half-shekel at the numbering** — the atonement money is given so that the numbering of Israel brings no plague (compare 2 Sam 24). Every man, rich or poor, gives the same. The temple tax of Matt 17:24–27 goes back to it.
- **Holy oil of anointing and incense** — both are reserved for the sanctuary of Yahweh; whoever makes the like for himself is cut off. The holy cannot be taken for one's own use.
- **Golden altar of incense** — inside the Holy, before the veil, smaller than the bronze altar of sacrifice. Its smoke is linked with prayer (cf. Ps 141:2, Rev 5:8, 8:3–4).
