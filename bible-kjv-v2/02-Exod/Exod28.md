# Exodus 28

[1] "And you, bring near to yourself ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ your brother, and his sons with him, from the midst of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that he may be priest to me: ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, ⟦nadab|Nadav|Nadab⟧ and ⟦abihu|Avihu|Abihu⟧, ⟦eleazar|Elazar|Eleazar⟧ and ⟦ithamar|Itamar|Ithamar⟧, the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧.

[2] And you shall make holy garments for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ your brother, for glory and for beauty.

[3] And you, you shall speak to all the wise of heart, whom I have filled with a spirit of wisdom, and they shall make the garments of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ to sanctify him, that he may be priest to me.

[4] And these are the garments that they shall make: a breastpiece and an ephod and a robe and a tunic of checkered work, a turban and a sash. And they shall make holy garments for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ your brother and for his sons, that he may be priest to me.

[5] And they, they shall take the gold and the blue and the purple and the scarlet and the fine linen.

[6] And they shall make the ephod of gold, blue and purple, scarlet and fine twined linen, the work of a designer.

[7] It shall have two shoulder pieces, joining, at its two ends, and it shall be joined.

[8] And the band of its fastening, which is on it, shall be like its work, from it: of gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen.

[9] And you shall take the two onyx stones, and you shall engrave on them the names of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[10] six of their names on the one stone, and the names of the six that remain on the second stone, according to their generations.

[11] The work of a craftsman in stone, the engravings of a seal, you shall engrave the two stones, according to the names of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; enclosed in settings of gold you shall make them.

[12] And you shall put the two stones on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, stones of memorial for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧; and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall bear their names before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ on his two shoulders for a memorial.

[13] And you shall make settings of gold,

[14] and two chains of pure gold; twisted you shall make them, the work of rope; and you shall place the chains of ropes on the settings.

[15] And you shall make a breastpiece of judgment, the work of a designer; like the work of the ephod you shall make it: of gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen you shall make it.

[16] It shall be square, doubled: a span its length and a span its width.

[17] And you shall fill in it a filling of stone, four rows of stone: a row of carnelian, topaz and emerald, the one row;

[18] and the second row: turquoise, sapphire and diamond;

[19] and the third row: jacinth, agate and amethyst;

[20] and the fourth row: beryl and onyx and jasper. They shall be set in gold in their fillings.

[21] And the stones shall be according to the names of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, twelve, according to their names; the engravings of a seal, each according to its name, they shall be for the twelve tribes.

[22] And you shall make on the breastpiece chains, twisted, the work of rope, of pure gold.

[23] And you shall make on the breastpiece two rings of gold, and you shall place the two rings on the two ends of the breastpiece.

[24] And you shall place the two ropes of gold on the two rings, at the ends of the breastpiece.

[25] And the two ends of the two ropes you shall place on the two settings, and you shall place them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, over against its face.

[26] And you shall make two rings of gold, and you shall put them on the two ends of the breastpiece, on its edge that is toward the side of the ephod, inward.

[27] And you shall make two rings of gold, and you shall place them on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, from below, on its face, alongside its joining, above the band of the ephod.

[28] And they shall bind the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue, to be on the band of the ephod, and the breastpiece shall not come loose from upon the ephod.

[29] And ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall bear the names of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in the breastpiece of judgment on his heart, when he goes into the Holy, for a memorial before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ continually.

[30] And you shall place into the breastpiece of judgment the Urim and the ⟦thummim|Tummim|Thummim⟧, and they shall be on the heart of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ when he goes in before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall bear the judgment of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ on his heart before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ continually.

[31] And you shall make the robe of the ephod wholly of blue.

[32] And the mouth of its head shall be in its midst; its mouth shall have an edge around, the work of a weaver; like the mouth of a coat of mail it shall be for it; it shall not be torn.

[33] And you shall make on its skirts pomegranates of blue and purple and scarlet, on its skirts around, and bells of gold in their midst around:

[34] a bell of gold and a pomegranate, a bell of gold and a pomegranate, on the skirts of the robe around.

[35] And it shall be on ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ to minister, and its sound shall be heard when he goes into the Holy before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ and when he comes out, and he shall not die.

[36] And you shall make a plate of pure gold, and you shall engrave on it the engravings of a seal: 'Holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.'

[37] And you shall put it on a cord of blue, and it shall be on the turban; over against the face of the turban it shall be.

[38] And it shall be on the forehead of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ shall bear the iniquity of the holy things that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ sanctify, for all their holy gifts; and it shall be on his forehead continually, for acceptance for them before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[39] And you shall weave the tunic in checkered work, of fine linen, and you shall make a turban of fine linen, and a sash you shall make, the work of an embroiderer.

[40] And for the sons of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ you shall make tunics, and you shall make for them sashes, and caps you shall make for them, for glory and for beauty.

[41] And you shall clothe with them ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ your brother, and his sons with him, and you shall anoint them and fill their hand and sanctify them, and they shall be priests to me.

[42] And make for them linen breeches to cover the flesh of nakedness; from the loins even to the thighs they shall be.

[43] And they shall be on ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and on his sons when they go into the tent of meeting, or when they draw near to the altar to minister in the Holy, and they shall not bear iniquity and die: an everlasting statute for him and for his seed after him."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. "And you" (v1, 3) and "And they" (v5) are emphatic pronouns.
- **"take thou unto thee" → "bring near to yourself"** (v1) — Hebrew הַקְרֵב, hifil of קָרַב "come near". **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי.
- **"that he may minister unto me in the priest's office" → "that he may be priest to me"** (v1, 3, 4, 41) — Hebrew לְכַהֲנוֹ־לִי, the verb of כֹּהֵן "priest", with a singular suffix ("for his being priest to me") also in v4 after "Aaron … and his sons"; in v41 the plural וְכִהֲנוּ "they shall be priests". "Minister" is another verb (שֵׁרֵת, v35, 43). The names stand in the Hebrew order: "Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, the sons of Aaron".
- **"holy garments"** (v2, 4) — Hebrew בִגְדֵי־קֹדֶשׁ, literally "garments of holiness". **"for glory and for beauty"** (v2, 40) — Hebrew לְכָבוֹד וּלְתִפְאָרֶת; תִּפְאֶרֶת is beauty, splendor, what one glories in.
- **"wise hearted … the spirit of wisdom" → "wise of heart … a spirit of wisdom"** (v3) — Hebrew חַכְמֵי־לֵב … רוּחַ חָכְמָה: the words are "wise" and "wisdom", not "skill". "Whom I have filled" is singular in the Hebrew (מִלֵּאתִיו "him whom I have filled"): each one of them. **"consecrate" → "sanctify"** (v3, 38, 41) — Hebrew קִדֵּשׁ.
- **"breastplate" → "breastpiece"** (v4, 15–30) — Hebrew חֹשֶׁן; the sense of the word itself is not certain. From the description it is a doubled square of woven work worn on the heart. **"ephod"** — Hebrew אֵפוֹד, kept as a word: a priestly garment whose exact form is not known. **"a broidered coat" → "a tunic of checkered work"** (v4, 39) — Hebrew כְּתֹנֶת תַּשְׁבֵּץ; the root שׁבץ (v39 "weave in checkered work", and the "settings" of v11) means some kind of interlaced or chequered pattern; uncertain. **"mitre" → "turban"** — Hebrew מִצְנֶפֶת, from צָנַף "wind round". **"girdle" → "sash"** — Hebrew אַבְנֵט.
- **"gold, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen"** (v5, 6, 8, 15, 33) — the dyes stand for the dyed wool, as in 26:1; in v5 each has the article. "Of" is supplied in v6, 8, 15.
- **"cunning work" → "the work of a designer"** (v6, 15) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב, see 26:1.
- **"It shall have the two shoulderpieces thereof joined at the two edges thereof; and so it shall be joined together" → "It shall have two shoulder pieces, joining, at its two ends, and it shall be joined"** (v7) — Hebrew שְׁתֵּי כְתֵפֹת חֹבְרֹת יִהְיֶה־לּוֹ אֶל־שְׁנֵי קְצוֹתָיו וְחֻבָּר, word for word "two shoulders joining it shall have, to its two ends, and it shall be joined"; חֹבְרֹת is an active participle, as in 39:4, where the preposition is עַל "on". כָּתֵף is "shoulder", here the parts of the ephod that lie on the shoulders.
- **"the curious girdle of the ephod" → "the band of its fastening"** (v8, 27, 28) — Hebrew חֵשֶׁב אֲפֻדָּתוֹ: חֵשֶׁב is used only of this band (one root with חֹשֵׁב "designer"); אֲפֻדָּה is a noun from the same root as "ephod", the putting on or binding on of it. Both are uncertain. "From it" (מִמֶּנּוּ): of one piece with the ephod.
- **"onyx stones"** (v9, 20) — Hebrew שֹׁהַם; the stone is not certainly known. **"according to their birth" → "according to their generations"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּתוֹלְדֹתָם: in the order in which they were begotten.
- **"an engraver in stone, like the engravings of a signet" → "a craftsman in stone, the engravings of a seal"** (v11, 21, 36) — Hebrew חָרַשׁ אֶבֶן … פִּתּוּחֵי חֹתָם; "like" is not in the Hebrew. **"ouches" → "settings"** (v11, 13, 14, 25) — Hebrew מִשְׁבְּצוֹת, from the root of "checkered work": plaited or filigree mounts; "enclosed" is מֻסַבֹּת "surrounded".
- **"stones of memorial"** (v12, 29) — Hebrew זִכָּרוֹן, from זָכַר "remember": what brings to remembrance.
- **"at the ends; of wreathen work" → "twisted … the work of rope"** (v14, 22, 24, 25) — Hebrew מִגְבָּלֹת (v14) and גַּבְלֻת (v22): found only of these chains; the sense is uncertain ("twisted", or "at the borders"). עֲבֹת is a rope of twisted strands; "the two ropes" (v24, 25) are the chains themselves.
- **"pure gold"** (v14, 22, 36) — Hebrew זָהָב טָהוֹר; טָהוֹר is the word "clean".
- **"breastplate of judgment"** (v15, 29, 30) — Hebrew חֹשֶׁן מִשְׁפָּט: it holds the Urim and the Thummim, by which a judgment (decision) is sought from Yahweh (Num 27:21).
- **"Foursquare it shall be being doubled" → "It shall be square, doubled"** (v16) — Hebrew רָבוּעַ יִהְיֶה כָּפוּל. A span (זֶרֶת) is the stretch of the hand, half a cubit.
- **"thou shalt set in it settings of stones" → "you shall fill in it a filling of stone"** (v17, 20) — Hebrew וּמִלֵּאתָ בוֹ מִלֻּאַת אֶבֶן: the verb and the noun are from מָלֵא "fill", the root of "fill the hand" in v41.
- **The twelve stones** (v17–20) — אֹדֶם "carnelian" (literally "red stone"), פִּטְדָה "topaz", בָּרֶקֶת "emerald" (literally "flashing stone"); נֹפֶךְ "turquoise", סַפִּיר "sapphire" (probably lapis lazuli), יָהֲלֹם "diamond" (a hard stone; not our diamond, which was not then cut); לֶשֶׁם "jacinth", שְׁבוֹ "agate", אַחְלָמָה "amethyst"; תַּרְשִׁישׁ "beryl" (the stone named from Tarshish), שֹׁהַם "onyx", יָשְׁפֵה "jasper". Only the first is fairly sure. The others are traditional identifications; the old versions do not agree with one another, and the meaning of most of the names is uncertain. KJV: "sardius, topaz, carbuncle; emerald, sapphire, diamond; ligure, agate, amethyst; beryl, onyx, jasper".
- **"every one with his name"** (v21) — Hebrew אִישׁ עַל־שְׁמוֹ, "each man according to his name". "Tribes" is שֵׁבֶט, the word "rod".
- **"over against its face"** (v25, 27, 37) — Hebrew אֶל־מוּל פָּנָיו / מִמּוּל פָּנָיו: on its front side. **"in the side of the ephod inward"** (v26) — Hebrew אֶל־עֵבֶר הָאֵפֹד בָּיְתָה, "toward the side of the ephod, houseward". **"over against the other coupling thereof" → "alongside its joining"** (v27) — Hebrew לְעֻמַּת מֶחְבַּרְתּוֹ, the noun of 26:4.
- **"by the rings thereof"** (v28) — the written text has the singular ("by its ring"), the reading tradition the plural, which is followed. **"lace" → "cord"** (v28, 37) — Hebrew פְּתִיל. **"be not loosed" → "shall not come loose"** — Hebrew יִזַּח, found only here and in 39:21.
- **"the holy place" → "the Holy"** (v29, 35, 43) — Hebrew הַקֹּדֶשׁ; "place" is not in the Hebrew (26:33).
- **"the Urim and the Thummim" kept; "in the breastplate" → "into the breastpiece"** (v30) — Hebrew אֶת־הָאוּרִים וְאֶת־הַתֻּמִּים, both with the article. What they were is not known: objects put into (אֶל) the doubled breastpiece, by which Yahweh's decision was asked (Num 27:21; 1 Sam 14:41; 28:6). The names look like plurals of אוֹר "light" and תֹּם "integrity, completeness" ("lights and perfections"); that derivation is a guess. The verse is word for word: "bear the judgment of the sons of Israel on his heart before Yahweh continually".
- **"all of blue" → "wholly of blue"** (v31) — Hebrew כְּלִיל תְּכֵלֶת.
- **"an hole in the top of it … a binding … the hole of an habergeon" → "the mouth of its head … an edge … the mouth of a coat of mail"** (v32) — Hebrew פִּי־רֹאשׁוֹ "the mouth of its head" (the opening for the head), שָׂפָה "a lip", כְּפִי תַחְרָא "like the mouth of a *tachra*". תַּחְרָא is found only here and in 39:23; "coat of mail" is the traditional sense and is uncertain. "That it be not rent" is in Hebrew a plain statement: "it shall not be torn".
- **"hem" → "skirts"** (v33, 34) — Hebrew שׁוּלַיִם, the lower edges of the robe. "In their midst": between the pomegranates.
- **"his sound shall be heard"** (v35) — Hebrew קוֹלוֹ, "its voice": of the robe with its bells (or "his sound"). "And he shall not die": Hebrew וְלֹא יָמוּת, joined with "and"; KJV "that he die not".
- **"a plate" kept** (v36) — Hebrew צִיץ, literally "blossom, flower": a shining ornament of gold; in 29:6 it is called "the holy crown". **"HOLINESS TO THE LORD" → "Holy to Yahweh"** — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה; קֹדֶשׁ is the noun "holiness, a holy thing": "a holy thing belonging to Yahweh".
- **"bear the iniquity of the holy things"** (v38) — word for word. Hebrew וְנָשָׂא אַהֲרֹן אֶת־עֲוֺן הַקֳּדָשִׁים: נָשָׂא is "bear" and "take away"; "the iniquity of the holy things" is whatever guilt clings to the holy gifts. **"which the children of Israel shall hallow in all their holy gifts" → "that the sons of Israel sanctify, for all their holy gifts"** — Hebrew לְכָל־מַתְּנֹת קָדְשֵׁיהֶם, "as to all the gifts of their holy things". **"that they may be accepted" → "for acceptance for them"** — Hebrew לְרָצוֹן לָהֶם, a noun from רָצָה "be pleased with": good pleasure, acceptance.
- **"embroider the coat of fine linen" → "weave the tunic in checkered work, of fine linen"** (v39) — Hebrew וְשִׁבַּצְתָּ, the verb of תַּשְׁבֵּץ (v4). **"needlework" → "the work of an embroiderer"** — see 26:36.
- **"bonnets" → "caps"** (v40) — Hebrew מִגְבָּעוֹת, from a root "be high, rounded": the headgear of the ordinary priests, beside the "turban" of Aaron.
- **"consecrate them" → "fill their hand"** (v41) — Hebrew וּמִלֵּאתָ אֶת־יָדָם, literally so. It is the set expression for installing a priest (29:9, 29, 33, 35; Judg 17:5, 12): "ordain" in modern versions. What the hand is filled with is not said here; in 29:22–24 the parts of "the ram of filling" are put on the palms of Aaron and his sons.
- **"linen breeches to cover their nakedness" → "linen breeches to cover the flesh of nakedness"** (v42) — Hebrew מִכְנְסֵי־בָד … בְּשַׂר עֶרְוָה; בַּד "linen" is another word than שֵׁשׁ "fine linen". "Loins" is מָתְנַיִם, "thighs" יְרֵכַיִם. The verb is an imperative: "make".
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "tent of meeting"** (v43) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"come near" → "draw near"** — Hebrew נָגַשׁ. **"that they bear not iniquity, and die" → "and they shall not bear iniquity and die"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִשְׂאוּ עָוֺן וָמֵתוּ.
- **"a statute for ever" → "an everlasting statute"; "seed" kept** (v43) — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age"; זֶרַע "seed".

### Notes

- **Aaronic priesthood established** — Aaron and his sons are set apart as the priestly line. Other Levites serve as assistants (Num 3). Non-Levites are excluded from priestly service (1 Sam 2, 1 Kgs 13).
- **Symbolic priestly garments** — the high priest bears Israel's names on shoulders (ephod stones) and on heart (breastpiece) — representing the nation before Yahweh. Typologically read as anticipating Christ as high priest (Hebrews).
- **The Urim and the Thummim** — through them the priest asks Yahweh's decision for Israel (cf. 1 Sam 14:41, 23:9–12).
