# Exodus 20

[1] ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ spoke all these words, saying:

[2] "I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, who brought you out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, out of the house of slavery.

[3] You shall have no other gods before my face.

[4] You shall not make for yourself a carved image, or any likeness of anything that is in the heavens above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the waters under the earth.

[5] You shall not bow down to them or serve them; for I, ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, am a jealous ⟦el|El|God⟧, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children to the third and fourth generation of those who hate me,

[6] but showing lovingkindness to thousands of those who love me and keep my commandments.

[7] You shall not lift up the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ in vain, for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will not hold guiltless the one who lifts up his name in vain.

[8] Remember the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day, to keep it holy.

[9] Six days you shall labor and do all your work,

[10] but the seventh day is a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. In it you shall not do any work — you, or your son, or your daughter, your male servant or your female servant, or your livestock, or the resident alien within your gates.

[11] For in six days ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and rested on the seventh day. Therefore ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ blessed the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ day and made it holy.

[12] Honor your father and your mother, that your days may be long in the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is giving you.

[13] You shall not murder.

[14] You shall not commit adultery.

[15] You shall not steal.

[16] You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor.

[17] You shall not covet your neighbor's house; you shall not covet your neighbor's wife, or his male servant, or his female servant, or his ox, or his donkey, or anything that is your neighbor's."

[18] All the people were seeing the thunders, the lightnings, the sound of the ram's horn, and the mountain smoking; and when the people saw it, they trembled and stood at a distance.

[19] They said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "You speak with us, and we will listen; but let not ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ speak with us, lest we die."

[20] ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to the people, "Do not be afraid, for ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ has come to test you, that the fear of him may be before you, so that you do not sin."

[21] And the people stood at a distance, while ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ drew near to the thick darkness where ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was.

[22] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Thus you shall say to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧: 'You have seen for yourselves that I have spoken with you from the heavens.

[23] You shall not make alongside me gods of silver, nor shall you make for yourselves gods of gold.

[24] An altar of earth you shall make for me, and on it you shall sacrifice your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, your sheep and your oxen. In every place where I cause my name to be remembered I will come to you and bless you.

[25] And if you make for me an altar of stone, you shall not build it of hewn stones; for if you use your tool on it, you profane it.

[26] You shall not go up by steps to my altar, that your nakedness not be exposed on it.'"

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"before me" → "before my face"** (v3) — Hebrew עַל־פָּנָי *al-panay*. Literally "upon my face / in my presence." Idiom: in preference to, or alongside, or in defiance of. Preserved literally.
- **"graven image" → "carved image"** (v4) — Hebrew פֶּסֶל *pesel* = cut / carved image. Not limited to sculpted idols; any cult-image-form.
- **"jealous God" → "jealous El"** (v5) — Hebrew אֵל קַנָּא *El Qanna*. *Qanna* = zealous / jealous — devoted-to-exclusivity. Not petty jealousy; covenantal zeal for exclusive relationship.
- **"mercy" → "lovingkindness"** (v6) — Hebrew חֶסֶד *chesed*, as in Gen 24 etc.
- **"take the name… in vain" → "lift up the name… in vain"** (v7) — Hebrew לֹא תִשָּׂא אֶת־שֵׁם־יְהוָה *lo tissa et-shem-Yahweh*. *Nasa* = lift up / carry. Idiom: "lift up on lips / invoke / swear by." *Shav* = emptiness / falsehood. The commandment forbids invoking Yahweh's name for false oaths, false prophecy, or empty / magical uses. Broader than only profanity in modern sense.
- **"kill" → "murder"** (v13) — Hebrew לֹא תִרְצָח *lo tirtsach*. The verb *ratsach* = murder / unlawful killing (not all killing — the OT permits killing in warfare, capital punishment, and self-defense; the Sabbath and ritual laws have their own exceptions). "Kill" is too broad; "murder" more accurate to the legal sense.
- **"covet" (v17)** — Hebrew לֹא תַחְמֹד *lo tachmod*. *Chamad* = desire / crave — likely implies more than internal attitude: the drive that leads to acquisition, scheming for another's property. "Covet" is traditional.
- **"sound of the ram's horn" (v18)** — Hebrew קוֹל הַשֹּׁפָר *qol ha-shofar*. Cf. 19:16.
- **"removed, and stood afar off" → "trembled and stood at a distance"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיָּנֻעוּ *wa-yanu'u* = trembled / shook / moved back. KJV's "removed" flattens.
- **"record my name" → "cause my name to be remembered"** (v24) — Hebrew אַזְכִּיר אֶת־שְׁמִי *azkir et-shemi*. *Zakhar* (hiphil) = cause to remember / invoke. The name is spoken liturgically; Yahweh comes to bless in response.
- **"thy nakedness be not discovered" → "nakedness not be exposed"** (v26) — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר לֹא־תִגָּלֶה עֶרְוָתְךָ. Practical-ritual reason: ancient altar design (no steps, to avoid priestly exposure when ascending — a propriety-concern in an era when loincloths / tunics were standard).

### Notes

- **Numbering of the Ten Commandments** — different traditions divide them differently:
  - Jewish: v2 = #1 (self-identification), v3–6 = #2, v7 = #3, v8–11 = #4, v12 = #5, v13 = #6, v14 = #7, v15 = #8, v16 = #9, v17 = #10.
  - Catholic / Lutheran: v3–6 = #1 (treating no-other-gods and no-images together), then v7 = #2, ... splitting "coveting wife" from "coveting house" to make #9 and #10.
  - Protestant / Orthodox / most English tradition: v3 = #1, v4–6 = #2, v7 = #3, etc., with all "coveting" in #10.
  The Hebrew text has no numbering; traditions interpret the structural unities differently.
- **"Jealous El" and transgenerational iniquity (v5–6)** — the formula is famous for its asymmetry: 3–4 generations of iniquity, thousands of lovingkindness. The ratio favors mercy heavily. Ezek 18 later modifies the transgenerational reading, insisting each person dies for their own sin.
- **"You shall not murder" distinct from "you shall not kill"** — later Jewish and Christian discussion has grappled with the precise scope of *ratsach* (murder) versus other killing-verbs (*harag*, *hikkah*, etc.). The commandment forbids unlawful killing, not all taking of life; pacifist / activist readings apply it more broadly, but translating as "murder" reflects the Hebrew specific verb.
- **Altar regulation (v24–26)** — the theology of humble / non-manipulated altars: earthen altar, undressed field-stones, no steps. Contrast to the elaborate altars Yahweh will later command for tabernacle / temple — here, simplicity of access to sacrifice is emphasized, not monumentality.
