# Exodus 26

[1] "Moreover, you shall make the dwelling of ten curtains of fine twined linen, and blue, purple, and scarlet yarns; with cherubim skillfully worked into them you shall make them.

[2] The length of each curtain shall be 28 cubits, and the width of each curtain 4 cubits; all the curtains shall be of the same size.

[3] Five curtains shall be joined to one another, and the other five curtains shall be joined to one another.

[4] You shall make loops of blue on the edge of the outermost curtain in one set, and likewise you shall make in the edge of the outermost curtain in the second set.

[5] Fifty loops you shall make in the one curtain, and fifty loops you shall make in the edge of the curtain that is in the second set; the loops shall be opposite one another.

[6] And you shall make fifty clasps of gold, and couple the curtains to one another with the clasps, so that the dwelling may be a unity.

[7] You shall make curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the dwelling — eleven curtains shall you make.

[8] The length of each curtain shall be 30 cubits, and the width of each curtain 4 cubits; the eleven curtains shall be of the same size.

[9] You shall couple five curtains by themselves and six curtains by themselves; and you shall double the sixth curtain over at the front of the tent.

[10] You shall make fifty loops on the edge of the outermost curtain in one set, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain in the second set.

[11] You shall make fifty clasps of bronze and put the clasps into the loops, and couple the tent together, so that it may be a unity.

[12] The overhanging part that remains of the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that is left over, shall hang over the back of the dwelling.

[13] The cubit on one side and the cubit on the other, of what remains in the length of the curtains of the tent, shall hang over the sides of the dwelling, on this side and on that, to cover it.

[14] You shall make a covering for the tent of tanned ram skins, and over it a covering of fine leather.

[15] You shall make the frames for the dwelling of acacia wood, standing upright.

[16] Ten cubits shall be the length of each frame, and a cubit and a half the width of each frame.

[17] There shall be two tenons in each frame, fitted to one another; thus shall you make for all the frames of the dwelling.

[18] You shall make the frames for the dwelling: twenty frames for the south side;

[19] and you shall make forty sockets of silver under the twenty frames — two sockets under one frame for its two tenons, and two sockets under another frame for its two tenons;

[20] and for the second side of the dwelling, the north side, twenty frames;

[21] and their forty sockets of silver, two sockets under one frame and two sockets under another frame;

[22] and for the rear of the dwelling, westward, you shall make six frames.

[23] You shall make two frames for the corners of the dwelling in the rear;

[24] they shall be doubled from beneath, and together they shall be finished at the top with one ring. Thus shall it be for both of them; they shall form the two corners.

[25] So there shall be eight frames, with their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets — two sockets under one frame and two sockets under another frame.

[26] You shall make bars of acacia wood: five for the frames of one side of the dwelling,

[27] and five bars for the frames of the other side of the dwelling, and five bars for the frames of the side of the dwelling at the rear westward.

[28] The middle bar, halfway up the frames, shall pass through from end to end.

[29] You shall overlay the frames with gold, and make their rings of gold as holders for the bars; and you shall overlay the bars with gold.

[30] You shall erect the dwelling according to the plan for it, which has been shown to you on the mountain.

[31] You shall make a veil of blue, purple, and scarlet yarns, and of fine twined linen; it shall be made with cherubim skillfully worked into it.

[32] You shall hang it on four pillars of acacia overlaid with gold, with hooks of gold, upon four sockets of silver.

[33] You shall hang the veil from the clasps, and shall bring the ark of the Testimony in there within the veil; the veil shall separate for you the Holy Place from the Most Holy.

[34] You shall put the mercy seat upon the ark of the Testimony in the Most Holy Place.

[35] You shall set the table outside the veil, and the lampstand opposite the table on the south side of the dwelling; and you shall put the table on the north side.

[36] You shall make for the entrance of the tent a screen of blue, purple, and scarlet yarns and fine twined linen, embroidered with needlework.

[37] You shall make for the screen five pillars of acacia and overlay them with gold; their hooks shall be of gold, and you shall cast for them five sockets of bronze."

## Changes

- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v1, 6, 7, 12, 13, 15, 17–20, 22–23, 27, 30, 33, 35) — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁכָּן *ha-mishkan*, as in 25:9.
- **"cunning work" → "skillfully worked"** (v1, 31) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב *ma'aseh choshev*. Work of a designer / artisan; skilled craftsmanship.
- **"taches" → "clasps"** (v6, 11, 33) — Hebrew קַרְסֵי *qarse*. Fastening hooks / clasps. "Taches" is archaic English.
- **"boards" → "frames"** (v15ff) — Hebrew קְרָשִׁים *qerashim*. Upright wooden frames. Translations vary; "frames" more accurate than "boards" (which suggests flat planks).
- **"rear up" → "erect"** (v30) — modernized.
- **"vail" → "veil"** (v31, 33, 35) — modernized spelling. Hebrew פָּרֹכֶת *parokhet*. The screen between Holy Place and Most Holy. Torn at Jesus' crucifixion (Matt 27:51).
- **"most holy" → "Most Holy Place"** (v33, 34) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים *qodesh ha-qodashim*. Literally "holy of holies" = the innermost chamber.
- **"door" → "entrance"** (v36) — Hebrew פֶּתַח *petach* = opening / doorway.

### Notes

- **Three-layered covering** — (1) the ornamented linen dwelling-curtains inside, (2) goat-hair tent-curtains covering those, (3) tanned ram skins, (4) fine leather outer covering. Practical weather-protection as well as symbolic gradation.
- **Layout** — 30x10 cubit structure, divided by the veil into 20x10 Holy Place and 10x10 Most Holy Place (a cube). The cube-shape of the Most Holy Place anticipates the cubic Holy of Holies in Solomon's temple (1 Kgs 6:20) and the cubic New Jerusalem of Rev 21:16.
- **North / south orientation** — Entry on the east (implied), facing the rising sun. Inside: lampstand (south), table (north), altar of incense (west-central), ark (behind veil).
