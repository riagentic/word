# Exodus 26

[1] "And the dwelling you shall make with ten curtains: fine twined linen and blue and purple and scarlet; with ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, the work of a designer, you shall make them.

[2] The length of the one curtain shall be twenty-eight cubits, and the width four cubits, the one curtain; one measure for all the curtains.

[3] Five of the curtains shall be joined one to another, and five curtains joined one to another.

[4] And you shall make loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain, at the end, in the joining; and so you shall make on the edge of the outermost curtain, in the second joining.

[5] Fifty loops you shall make on the one curtain, and fifty loops you shall make on the end of the curtain that is in the second joining, the loops being opposite one to another.

[6] And you shall make fifty clasps of gold, and you shall join the curtains one to another with the clasps, and the dwelling shall be one.

[7] And you shall make curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the dwelling; eleven curtains you shall make them.

[8] The length of the one curtain shall be thirty cubits, and the width four cubits, the one curtain; one measure for the eleven curtains.

[9] And you shall join the five curtains by themselves and the six curtains by themselves, and you shall double the sixth curtain over against the face of the tent.

[10] And you shall make fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain, the outermost in the joining, and fifty loops on the edge of the curtain of the second joining.

[11] And you shall make clasps of bronze, fifty, and you shall bring the clasps into the loops, and you shall join the tent, and it shall be one.

[12] And the overhang that is left over in the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that is left over, shall hang over the back of the dwelling.

[13] And the cubit on this side and the cubit on that side, in what is left over in the length of the curtains of the tent, shall be hanging over the sides of the dwelling, on this side and on that side, to cover it.

[14] And you shall make a covering for the tent, skins of rams dyed red, and a covering of skins of tachash above.

[15] And you shall make the boards for the dwelling, acacia wood, standing up.

[16] Ten cubits shall be the length of the board, and a cubit and a half the width of the one board.

[17] Two tenons for the one board, fitted one to another; so you shall make for all the boards of the dwelling.

[18] And you shall make the boards for the dwelling: twenty boards for the south side, southward.

[19] And forty sockets of silver you shall make under the twenty boards: two sockets under the one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under the one board for its two tenons.

[20] And for the second side of the dwelling, for the north side, twenty boards,

[21] and their forty sockets of silver: two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under the one board.

[22] And for the rear of the dwelling, westward, you shall make six boards.

[23] And two boards you shall make for the corners of the dwelling in the rear.

[24] And they shall be double from below, and together they shall be whole on its top, into the one ring; so it shall be for the two of them: they shall be for the two corners.

[25] And there shall be eight boards, and their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets: two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under the one board.

[26] And you shall make bars of acacia wood: five for the boards of the one side of the dwelling,

[27] and five bars for the boards of the second side of the dwelling, and five bars for the boards of the side of the dwelling at the rear, westward.

[28] And the middle bar, in the midst of the boards, shall pass through from end to end.

[29] And the boards you shall overlay with gold, and their rings you shall make of gold, housings for the bars; and you shall overlay the bars with gold.

[30] And you shall raise up the dwelling according to its ordinance, which you were shown on the mountain.

[31] And you shall make a veil of blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen; the work of a designer one shall make it, with ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[32] And you shall place it on four pillars of acacia overlaid with gold, their hooks of gold, on four sockets of silver.

[33] And you shall place the veil under the clasps, and you shall bring in there, within the veil, the ark of the testimony; and the veil shall separate for you the Holy from the Holy of Holies.

[34] And you shall place the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ on the ark of the testimony in the Holy of Holies.

[35] And you shall put the table outside the veil, and the lampstand opposite the table, on the side of the dwelling southward; and the table you shall place on the north side.

[36] And you shall make a screen for the entrance of the tent, of blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen, the work of an embroiderer.

[37] And you shall make for the screen five pillars of acacia, and you shall overlay them with gold, their hooks of gold; and you shall cast for them five sockets of bronze."

## Changes

- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. The speech of Yahweh to Moses runs on from 25:1.
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v1, 6, 7, 12, 13, 15, 17, 18, 20, 22, 23, 26, 27, 30, 35) — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁכָּן *ha-mishkan*, from שָׁכַן "abide": the place where he abides (25:8, 9). In this chapter "the dwelling" is first the inner covering of ten linen curtains (v1–6), and "the tent" (אֹהֶל) is the covering of goats' hair over it (v7–13).
- **"with ten curtains"** (v1) — Hebrew "the dwelling you shall make ten curtains": no preposition; "with" is supplied. יְרִיעָה is one length of tent cloth. So also "with cherubim" (v1, 31): the Hebrew sets "cherubim" beside "the work of a designer" without a preposition.
- **"fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet"** (v1, 31, 36) — Hebrew שֵׁשׁ מָשְׁזָר "linen twisted" of several threads; תְּכֵלֶת, אַרְגָּמָן, תֹּלַעַת שָׁנִי are names of dyes (the last literally "worm of scarlet") and stand for the wool dyed with them; "yarn" is not in the Hebrew. In v1 the linen stands first, in v31 and 36 last.
- **"cunning work" → "the work of a designer"** (v1, 31) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב: חֹשֵׁב is the participle of חָשַׁב "think, devise", one who devises patterns. **"wrought with needlework" → "the work of an embroiderer"** (v36) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה רֹקֵם: רָקַם is to work in several colours; whether with the needle or in the weaving is not certain.
- **"twenty-eight cubits"** (v2, 8) — Hebrew "twenty-eight by the cubit". "The one curtain" stands twice in the verse, and "one measure" (מִדָּה אַחַת) is literal.
- **"coupled together one to another" → "joined one to another"** (v3, 5, 6, 17) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה אֶל־אֲחֹתָהּ, literally "a woman to her sister": the Hebrew way of saying "one to another" of things that are feminine in grammar. חָבַר "join" is one verb in v3, 6, 9, 11.
- **"selvedge in the coupling" → "at the end, in the joining"** (v4, 5, 10) — Hebrew חֹבֶרֶת / מַחְבֶּרֶת, nouns from חָבַר: either the set of five curtains joined together or the place where the sets are joined; the Hebrew does not decide. "Edge" is שָׂפָה, literally "lip".
- **"taches" → "clasps"** (v6, 11, 33) — Hebrew קְרָסִים. **"it shall be one tabernacle" → "the dwelling shall be one"** (v6, 11) — Hebrew וְהָיָה הַמִּשְׁכָּן אֶחָד.
- **"curtains of goats' hair"** (v7) — Hebrew יְרִיעֹת עִזִּים "curtains of goats"; "hair" is supplied.
- **"in the forefront of the tabernacle" → "over against the face of the tent"** (v9) — Hebrew אֶל־מוּל פְּנֵי הָאֹהֶל; the word is "tent", not "dwelling".
- **"the remnant that remaineth" → "the overhang that is left over"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew סֶרַח הָעֹדֵף: סָרַח is "hang over freely", עָדַף "be in excess". One root in "overhang", "shall hang over" and "hanging over".
- **"rams' skins dyed red" kept** (v14) — Hebrew מְאָדָּמִים "made red"; not "tanned". **"badgers' skins" → "skins of tachash"** — Hebrew תְּחָשִׁים: the animal (or the kind of leather) is not known; the word is kept. See 25:5.
- **"boards" kept** (v15–29) — Hebrew קֶרֶשׁ. The word is found only of the dwelling and once of a ship (Ezek 27:6). Whether solid planks or open frames are meant is not certain; "frames" is a modern proposal.
- **"tenons"** (v17, 19) — Hebrew יָדוֹת, literally "hands". **"set in order" → "fitted"** — Hebrew מְשֻׁלָּבֹת, found only here and in 36:22; the sense (joined, or set parallel) is uncertain.
- **"on the south side southward"** (v18) — Hebrew לִפְאַת נֶגְבָּה תֵימָנָה, two words for south: "for the side of the Negev, toward Teman". "North" (v20, 35) is צָפוֹן; "westward" (v22, 27) is literally "toward the sea"; "southward" in v35 is again תֵּימָנָה.
- **"under one board … under another board" → "under the one board … under the one board"** (v19, 21, 25) — Hebrew תַּחַת הַקֶּרֶשׁ הָאֶחָד both times, the same words twice: under each single board.
- **"the sides of the tabernacle westward" → "the rear of the dwelling, westward"** (v22, 23, 27) — Hebrew יַרְכָתַיִם, a dual from יָרֵךְ "thigh": the two flanks, the far end.
- **"coupled together beneath … coupled together above" → "double from below … whole on its top"** (v24) — Hebrew תֹאֲמִים "twins" (as Gen 25:24) and then תַמִּים "whole, complete": two different words in the Masoretic Text, which is kept. "On its top" is עַל־רֹאשׁוֹ; 36:29 has אֶל־רֹאשׁוֹ "to its top". The Samaritan text is said to read "twins" both times (from memory). How the corner boards were built is not clear.
- **"places for the bars" → "housings for the bars"** (v29) — Hebrew בָּתִּים, literally "houses".
- **"rear up … according to the fashion thereof" → "raise up … according to its ordinance"** (v30) — Hebrew הֵקִים "make stand"; מִשְׁפָּט, elsewhere "judgment", is here the rule laid down for it. "You were shown" is a passive (הָרְאֵיתָ, "you were made to see").
- **"shall it be made" → "one shall make it"** (v31) — Hebrew יַעֲשֶׂה, third person with no subject named.
- **"hang" → "place"** (v32, 33, 34, 35) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "give"; **"set" → "put"** (v35) is שִׂים.
- **"vail" → "veil"** (v31, 33, 35) — Hebrew פָּרֹכֶת; **"hanging" → "screen"** (v36, 37) — Hebrew מָסָךְ, from סָכַךְ "screen off".
- **"divide unto you between the holy place and the most holy" → "separate for you the Holy from the Holy of Holies"** (v33, 34) — Hebrew הַקֹּדֶשׁ and קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים; "place" is not in the Hebrew. Literally "separate between the Holy and between the Holy of Holies".
- **"mercy seat"** (v34) — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת *kapporet*, from כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (literally "cover"): the cover of the ark. See 25:17.
- **"ark of the testimony"** (v33, 34) — Hebrew אֲרוֹן הָעֵדוּת; אֲרוֹן is a chest.
- **"door" → "entrance"** (v36) — Hebrew פֶּתַח, the opening.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v11, 37) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת.

### Notes

- **Layers of covering** — (1) the ornamented linen curtains, "the dwelling", inside; (2) the goat-hair curtains, "the tent", covering those; (3) ram skins dyed red; (4) the outer covering of *tachash* skins.
- **Layout** — a 30 x 10 cubit structure, divided by the veil into the Holy (20 x 10) and the Holy of Holies (10 x 10, a cube). The cube shape anticipates the inner room of Solomon's temple (1 Kgs 6:20) and the New Jerusalem of Rev 21:16.
- **North / south orientation** — the entrance is on the east (27:13–16). Inside: lampstand (south), table (north), altar of incense (before the veil, 30:6), ark (within the veil).
- **The veil** — torn at Jesus' death (Matt 27:51).
