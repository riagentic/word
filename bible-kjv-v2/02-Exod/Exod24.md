## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v10, 11, 13) — Hebrew אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "the Elohim of Israel" (v10); in v11 and v13 the word has the article, הָאֱלֹהִים "the Elohim".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept (KJV "Then", "So").
- **"And he said unto Moses" → "And to Moses he said"** (v1) — Hebrew וְאֶל־מֹשֶׁה אָמַר, with "to Moses" first: after the words for the people (20:22–23:33) comes the word for Moses himself. The speaker is not named; he speaks of Yahweh in the third person ("Come up to Yahweh").
- **"worship ye afar off" → "you shall bow down from afar"** (v1) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה "bow down" to the ground; plural.
- **"come near" → "draw near"** (v2, 14) — Hebrew נָגַשׁ (קָרַב is "come near", 22:8). **"go up" → "come up"** (v2) — Hebrew עָלָה, the verb of v1, 9, 12, 13, 15, 18.
- **"told the people" → "recounted to the people"** (v3) — Hebrew סִפֵּר. **"judgments" → "ordinances"** — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁפָּטִים, the word of 21:1: "the words of Yahweh" and "the ordinances" are named side by side.
- **"under the hill" → "under the mountain"** (v4) — Hebrew תַּחַת הָהָר: at its foot. **"twelve pillars, according to the twelve tribes" → "twelve pillars for the twelve tribes"** — Hebrew מַצֵּבָה, a stone set up (the word of 23:24, there of the pillars of the nations); the Hebrew has the singular after the number, and לְ "for".
- **"young men of the children of Israel" → "the young men of the sons of Israel"** (v5) — Hebrew נַעֲרֵי בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל; נַעַר is "boy, young man, attendant". **"offered burnt offerings" → "offered up burnt offerings"** — Hebrew וַיַּעֲלוּ עֹלֹת, literally "made go up goings-up": verb and noun of one root. **"sacrificed peace offerings of oxen" → "sacrificed sacrifices of peace offerings …: bulls"** — Hebrew וַיִּזְבְּחוּ זְבָחִים שְׁלָמִים … פָּרִים: again verb and noun of one root; "bulls" (פַּר, not שׁוֹר "ox") stands at the very end of the verse.
- **"basons" → "basins"** (v6) — Hebrew אַגָּנֹת, large bowls (another word than the "basin" of 30:18). **"sprinkled" → "dashed"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew זָרַק: to throw from a vessel in a mass; "sprinkle" is another verb (הִזָּה).
- **"read in the audience of the people" → "read in the ears of the people"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּאָזְנֵי. **"and be obedient" → "and we will hear"** — Hebrew נַעֲשֶׂה וְנִשְׁמָע "we will do and we will hear"; שָׁמַע is "hear", and so "heed". The order, doing before hearing, is the Hebrew's.
- **"the blood of the covenant, which the LORD hath made with you concerning all these words" → "the blood of the covenant that Yahweh has cut with you upon all these words"** (v8) — Hebrew דַם־הַבְּרִית; כָּרַת בְּרִית "cut a covenant"; עַל is "upon": on the ground of these words, or in accordance with them. Cited in Heb 9:20; cf. Matt 26:28; Mark 14:24.
- **"And they saw the God of Israel" → "And they saw the *Elohim* of Israel"** (v10) — Hebrew וַיִּרְאוּ אֵת אֱלֹהֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל, as it stands: the plain verb "see" (רָאָה) with a direct object. The Septuagint softens it, "they saw the place where the God of Israel stood" (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept. Only what was under his feet is described. Cf. 33:20.
- **"as it were a paved work of a sapphire stone" → "as it were a work of sapphire brick"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּמַעֲשֵׂה לִבְנַת הַסַּפִּיר, literally "like the work of a brick of the sapphire": לְבֵנָה is "brick" (1:14; 5:7), here a tile or paving slab; "stone" is not in the Hebrew. סַפִּיר is probably lapis lazuli, the deep blue stone, not the sapphire of today.
- **"as it were the body of heaven in his clearness" → "like the very heavens for purity"** (v10) — Hebrew וּכְעֶצֶם הַשָּׁמַיִם לָטֹהַר: עֶצֶם is "bone", so the very thing itself (as "that very day", 12:17); טֹהַר is "cleanness, purity" (the root of טָהוֹר "clean, pure").
- **"upon the nobles of the children of Israel he laid not his hand" → "against the nobles of the sons of Israel he did not stretch out his hand"** (v11) — Hebrew שָׁלַח יָד, as in 22:8, 11. אֲצִילֵי is found in this sense only here; "nobles", "chief men" is the usual understanding (perhaps "those set apart"); it is not certain.
- **"also they saw God" → "and they beheld *Elohim*"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיֶּחֱזוּ אֶת־הָאֱלֹהִים: a second verb, חָזָה "behold, gaze" (the verb of "seer" and "vision"), with "the Elohim".
- **"Come up to me into the mount" → "Come up to me to the mountain"** (v12) — Hebrew הָהָרָה "to the mountain". **"tables of stone, and a law, and commandments" → "the tablets of stone, and the Torah and the commandment"** — Hebrew לֻחֹת הָאֶבֶן וְהַתּוֹרָה וְהַמִּצְוָה: both nouns are singular and have the article. תּוֹרָה *torah* is "teaching, instruction, law". **"that thou mayest teach them" → "to teach them"** — Hebrew לְהוֹרֹתָם, the verb from which *torah* comes; who is to teach (Yahweh by the writing, or Moses) the Hebrew does not say. Whether "that I have written" belongs to the tablets only or also to "the Torah and the commandment" is open.
- **"his minister" kept** (v13) — Hebrew מְשָׁרְתוֹ, from שֵׁרֵת "minister to, attend on": his personal attendant. **"the mount of God" → "the mountain of *Elohim*"** — Hebrew הַר הָאֱלֹהִים.
- **"Tarry ye here for us" → "Sit here for us"** (v14) — Hebrew שְׁבוּ־לָנוּ בָזֶה; יָשַׁב "sit, remain". **"if any man have any matters to do" → "whoever has a matter"** — Hebrew מִי־בַעַל דְּבָרִים, literally "who is an owner of words": one who has a case.
- **"into the mount" → "to the mountain"** (v13, 15, 18) — Hebrew אֶל־הָהָר.
- **"abode" kept** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכֹּן, from שָׁכַן "abide": the verb from which מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling" comes (25:8, 9). **"the seventh day he called" → "he called … on the seventh day"** — the subject is not named.
- **"the sight of the glory" → "the appearance of the glory"; "devouring fire" → "consuming fire"; "in the eyes of" → "before the eyes of"** (v17) — Hebrew מַרְאֶה; אֵשׁ אֹכֶלֶת, literally "eating fire" (the verb of 22:6); לְעֵינֵי. **"top"** — Hebrew רֹאשׁ "head".
- **"went into the midst of the cloud, and gat him up into the mount" → "came into the midst of the cloud and went up to the mountain"** (v18) — Hebrew וַיָּבֹא … וַיַּעַל.

### Notes

- **The making of the covenant** — the words are told and accepted (v3), written (v4), read and accepted again (v7); the blood of the sacrifices is divided, half on the altar and half on the people (v6, 8).
- **"They saw the *Elohim* of Israel … and they ate and drank" (v10–11)** — the verse says it without softening, and adds that he did not stretch out his hand against them. Elsewhere it is said that no man can see him and live (33:20; cf. John 1:18; 1 Tim 6:16); the text does not explain the difference.
- **"The blood of the covenant" (v8)** — taken up in the words over the cup (Matt 26:28; Mark 14:24) and in Heb 9:18–20.
- **Forty days and forty nights (v18)** — cf. 34:28; Deut 9:9, 18; 1 Kgs 19:8; Matt 4:2.
