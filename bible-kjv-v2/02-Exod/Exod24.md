# Exodus 24

[1] He said to Moses, "Come up to Yahweh, you and Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel; and worship from afar.

[2] Moses alone shall come near to Yahweh, but they shall not come near; and the people shall not go up with him."

[3] Moses came and told the people all the words of Yahweh and all the ordinances, and all the people answered with one voice and said, "All the words that Yahweh has spoken we will do."

[4] Moses wrote all the words of Yahweh. He rose early in the morning and built an altar at the foot of the mountain, with twelve pillars, according to the twelve tribes of Israel.

[5] And he sent young men of the children of Israel, who offered burnt offerings and sacrificed oxen as peace offerings to Yahweh.

[6] Moses took half of the blood and put it in basins, and half of the blood he dashed against the altar.

[7] Then he took the Book of the Covenant and read it in the hearing of the people. And they said, "All that Yahweh has spoken we will do, and we will listen."

[8] Moses took the blood and dashed it on the people, saying, "Behold the blood of the covenant that Yahweh has made with you concerning all these words."

[9] Then Moses went up, with Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel.

[10] They saw the *Elohim* of Israel; and under his feet was something like a pavement of sapphire stone, like the very heavens for clearness.

[11] He did not lay his hand on the chosen ones of the children of Israel; they beheld *Elohim*, and they ate and drank.

[12] Yahweh said to Moses, "Come up to me on the mountain and remain there, and I will give you the tablets of stone, with the instruction and the commandment, which I have written for their instruction."

[13] So Moses rose with Joshua his attendant, and Moses went up to the mountain of *Elohim*.

[14] To the elders he said, "Wait here for us until we return to you. And behold, Aaron and Hur are with you; whoever has any dispute, let him go to them."

[15] Moses went up on the mountain, and the cloud covered the mountain.

[16] The glory of Yahweh dwelt on Mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it for six days; on the seventh day he called to Moses from the midst of the cloud.

[17] The appearance of the glory of Yahweh was like a consuming fire on the top of the mountain, in the sight of the children of Israel.

[18] Moses entered into the midst of the cloud and went up to the mountain; and Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"under the hill" → "at the foot of the mountain"** (v4) — Hebrew תַּחַת הָהָר *tachat ha-har*. Modernized.
- **"basons" → "basins"** (v6) — modernized spelling.
- **"sprinkled" → "dashed"** (v6, 8) — Hebrew זָרַק *zaraq* = throw / dash / splash. Not gentle sprinkling but dashing the blood on altar and people — a vivid covenant-sealing ritual.
- **"audience" → "hearing"** (v7) — modernized.
- **"be obedient" → "we will listen"** (v7) — Hebrew וְנִשְׁמָע *we-nishma*. *Shama* = hear / listen / obey. The famous covenantal commitment *na'aseh we-nishma* ("we will do and we will listen") — the order "do first, understand later" is rabbinically read as Israel's hallmark of ideal covenantal response.
- **"paved work of sapphire" → "pavement of sapphire stone"** (v10) — Hebrew לִבְנַת הַסַּפִּיר *livnat ha-sappir*. *Sappir* here may be lapis lazuli (the blue stone prized in ancient Near East) rather than modern sapphire. *Livnat* = tile / pavement / brick-work.
- **"the body of heaven" → "the very heavens"** (v10) — Hebrew כְּעֶצֶם הַשָּׁמַיִם *ke-etsem ha-shamayim*. *Etsem* = bone / essence / very-thing. "Like the essence of the heavens for clearness" — the pavement looked like the blue of the sky itself.
- **"nobles" → "chosen ones"** (v11) — Hebrew אֲצִילֵי בְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל *atsile bne yisra'el*. *Atsil* = chief / noble / setapart-one. The seventy elders.
- **"tables" → "tablets"** (v12) — Hebrew לֻחֹת הָאֶבֶן *luchot ha-even*. Modernized.
- **"law" → "instruction"** (v12) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *torah*, as elsewhere.
- **"his minister" → "his attendant"** (v13) — Hebrew מְשָׁרְתוֹ *mesharto* = his personal servant / aide. Not priestly minister yet.
- **"abode" → "dwelt"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיִּשְׁכֹּן *wa-yishkon* from *shakan* = to dwell / tabernacle. Same root as *mishkan* (tabernacle) and later *shekhinah* (divine presence). The narrative's first use of shakan-language with divine subject.

### Notes

- **Covenant ratification** — structured formally: (1) reading of the code, (2) affirmation by the people, (3) blood-sprinkling on altar (God) and people. Half the blood to each party — solemnizing the covenant between Yahweh and Israel as blood-bound.
- **"They saw the *Elohim* of Israel… and they ate and drank" (v10–11)** — extraordinary: the seventy-plus elders have a theophanic meal in Yahweh's presence. This vision is remarkable in light of other passages where seeing Yahweh is lethal (e.g., 33:20). Perhaps: the covenant-meal creates a conditional space of grace.
- **Blood-covenant language** — "blood of the covenant" (v8) is picked up in Jesus' institution of the Eucharist ("this is my blood of the covenant," Matt 26:28) and Heb 9:20.
- **Moses' forty days** (v18) — the first of several 40-day mountaintop periods (cf. Deut 9:9, 18). Echoes Jesus' 40 days in the wilderness and Elijah's 40 days to Horeb (1 Kgs 19:8).
