# Exodus 3

[1] Moses was shepherding the flock of Jethro his father-in-law, the priest of Midian; and he led the flock beyond the wilderness, and came to the mountain of *Elohim*, to Horeb.

[2] The messenger of Yahweh appeared to him in a flame of fire from the midst of a bush. He looked — and behold, the bush was burning with fire, but the bush was not consumed.

[3] Moses said, "Let me turn aside and see this great sight — why the bush is not burned up."

[4] When Yahweh saw that he turned aside to see, *Elohim* called to him from the midst of the bush and said, "Moses, Moses!" He said, "Here I am."

[5] He said, "Do not draw near here. Take your sandals off your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground."

[6] And he said, "I am the *Elohim* of your father — the *Elohim* of Abraham, the *Elohim* of Isaac, and the *Elohim* of Jacob." Moses hid his face, for he was afraid to look at *Elohim*.

[7] Yahweh said, "I have surely seen the affliction of my people who are in Egypt, and I have heard their cry because of their taskmasters; for I know their sufferings.

[8] I have come down to deliver them from the hand of the Egyptians and to bring them up out of that land to a good and broad land, to a land flowing with milk and honey, to the place of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.

[9] And now, behold, the cry of the children of Israel has come to me, and I have also seen the oppression with which the Egyptians are oppressing them.

[10] Now then, come, and I will send you to Pharaoh, that you may bring my people, the children of Israel, out of Egypt."

[11] But Moses said to *Elohim*, "Who am I, that I should go to Pharaoh, and that I should bring the children of Israel out of Egypt?"

[12] He said, "I will be with you; and this shall be the sign for you that I have sent you: when you have brought the people out of Egypt, you shall serve *Elohim* on this mountain."

[13] Moses said to *Elohim*, "Behold, when I come to the children of Israel and say to them, 'The *Elohim* of your fathers has sent me to you,' and they ask me, 'What is his name?' — what shall I say to them?"

[14] *Elohim* said to Moses, "**Ehyeh-Asher-Ehyeh (I Will Be What I Will Be).**" And he said, "Thus you shall say to the children of Israel: '**Ehyeh (I Will Be) has sent me to you.**'"

[15] And *Elohim* said further to Moses, "Thus you shall say to the children of Israel: 'Yahweh, the *Elohim* of your fathers — the *Elohim* of Abraham, the *Elohim* of Isaac, and the *Elohim* of Jacob — has sent me to you. This is my name forever, and this is my memorial from generation to generation.'

[16] Go, gather the elders of Israel together, and say to them, 'Yahweh, the *Elohim* of your fathers — the *Elohim* of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob — has appeared to me, saying: I have surely attended to you and to what has been done to you in Egypt;

[17] and I have said, I will bring you up out of the affliction of Egypt to the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, to a land flowing with milk and honey.'

[18] And they will listen to your voice. You and the elders of Israel shall go to the king of Egypt, and you shall say to him, 'Yahweh, the *Elohim* of the Hebrews, has met with us. Now please let us go a three-days' journey into the wilderness, that we may sacrifice to Yahweh our *Elohim*.'

[19] But I know that the king of Egypt will not let you go — not even by a mighty hand.

[20] So I will stretch out my hand and strike Egypt with all my wonders which I will do in its midst; and after that he will let you go.

[21] And I will give this people favor in the sight of the Egyptians, and when you go, you shall not go empty-handed.

[22] Every woman shall ask from her neighbor, and from any woman who lodges in her house, articles of silver, articles of gold, and garments; and you shall put them on your sons and on your daughters. Thus you shall plunder the Egyptians."

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"backside of the desert" → "beyond the wilderness"** (v1) — Hebrew אַחַר הַמִּדְבָּר *achar ha-midbar*. *Achar* = behind / beyond. *Midbar* = wilderness / uncultivated land. "Backside" in modern English = buttocks; the Hebrew means "beyond / far side of."
- **"Horeb" (v1)** — Hebrew חֹרֵב *Chorev*. Alternative name for Sinai (used mostly in Deuteronomy and this Elohist-type material); same mountain. Preserved.
- **"angel" → "messenger"** (v2) — as elsewhere.
- **"put off thy shoes" → "take your sandals off"** (v5) — Hebrew שַׁל־נְעָלֶיךָ *shal-ne'alekha*. *Na'al* = sandal. "Shoes" misleads toward enclosed footwear.
- **"I AM THAT I AM" → "Ehyeh-Asher-Ehyeh (I Will Be What I Will Be)"** (v14) — Hebrew אֶהְיֶה אֲשֶׁר אֶהְיֶה *Ehyeh asher Ehyeh*. The divine self-revelation, famously untranslatable:
  - The verb is 1st-person imperfect of הוה/היה (*hayah* "to be"): could mean "I am," "I will be," or "I cause to be."
  - *Asher* = "who / that / which / because."
  - Possible translations: "I AM WHO I AM" (classic, LXX ἐγώ εἰμι ὁ ὤν); "I WILL BE WHAT I WILL BE" (dynamic, future-oriented); "I AM WHO I WILL BE" (mixing tenses); "I AM BECAUSE I AM" (tautological self-definition). Original preserved in Hebrew with English gloss because no single English can carry all senses. "I Will Be" is arguably closer to Hebrew verb aspect than "I Am." The Tetragrammaton *YHWH* (v15) appears to be related 3rd-person: "he will be / he causes to be."
- **"Yahweh" (v15)** — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*. Here given explicitly as divine name; linked in Hebrew to *ehyeh* of v14. KJV renders "LORD" (small-caps). Preserved as proper name.
- **"memorial" (v15)** — Hebrew זִכְרִי *zikhri* = memorial / remembrance / how I am to be invoked. The name is both identifier and liturgical invocation.
- **"visited" → "attended to"** (v16) — Hebrew פָּקֹד פָּקַדְתִּי *paqod paqadti*. Double-intensive of *paqad* = "I have surely visited / taken account of." Fulfilling Joseph's prophecy: "*Elohim* will surely visit (*paqod yiphqod*) you" (Gen 50:24). The echo is exact.
- **"no, not by a mighty hand" → "not even by a mighty hand"** (v19) — Hebrew וְלֹא בְּיָד חֲזָקָה *we-lo be-yad chazaqah*. Hebrew ambiguous:
  1. "Not except by a mighty hand" (i.e., Pharaoh will yield only under force — NJPS).
  2. "Not even by a mighty hand" (Pharaoh will resist even under pressure — which then requires v20 "so I will stretch out my hand" — KJV tradition).
  Translation follows (2).
- **"borrow" → "ask from"** (v22) — Hebrew וְשָׁאֲלָה *we-sha'alah* from *sha'al* = ask / request. Not "borrow" (which implies return); the Hebrew is request, which in context becomes "ask and receive." The "spoiling" in 12:36 confirms that return was not expected — Yahweh gave Israel favor so the Egyptians gave the items freely as gifts / reparations. KJV "borrow" created the old charge of theft; the Hebrew does not imply promised-return.
- **"spoil" → "plunder"** (v22) — Hebrew וְנִצַּלְתֶּם *we-nitsaltem* from *natsal* = snatch / strip / plunder. The taking of Egyptian wealth is framed as plunder of a defeated oppressor, compensating for generations of unpaid forced-labor. Theological / legal logic: withheld wages after enslavement.

### Notes

- **The name of God (v14–15)** — one of the most theologically and philosophically discussed passages in Hebrew Scripture. Traditions:
  - Jewish: the name is ineffable; practices of substitution (*Adonai*, *Ha-Shem*) developed to avoid its utterance.
  - Christian (Origen, Aquinas): "I AM THAT I AM" understood ontologically — Yahweh as the self-existent, uncaused Being (*esse ipsum subsistens*).
  - Modern historical-critical: *Ehyeh / Yahweh* more dynamically understood — "I will be there (with you)" — as relational promise, not metaphysical self-definition.
  - Linguistic scholarship: *YHWH* likely from *hawah* "to be / become" or "to cause to be"; possibly the causative form "the one who causes to exist."
- **"Milk and honey" (v8, 17)** — standard Canaanite-land blessing phrase. Milk = abundant livestock (goats, sheep); honey = abundant fruit (possibly date-honey rather than bee-honey). Pastoral and horticultural plenty.
- **Moses' reluctance begins** — the first of several objections (ch 4 continues). Moses resists the commission; Yahweh progressively answers each objection.
