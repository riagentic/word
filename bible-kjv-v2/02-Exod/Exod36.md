# Exodus 36

[1] "And ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ and ⟦oholiab|Oholiav|Oholiab⟧ and every man wise of heart, in whom ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has given wisdom and understanding to know how to do all the work of the service of the Holy, shall do according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded."

[2] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ called ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ and ⟦oholiab|Oholiav|Oholiab⟧ and every man wise of heart, in whose heart ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had given wisdom, everyone whose heart lifted him up to come near to the work, to do it.

[3] And they took from before ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ all the contribution that the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had brought for the work of the service of the Holy, to do it. And they still brought to him a freewill offering morning by morning.

[4] And all the wise men who were doing all the work of the Holy came, each man from his work that they were doing,

[5] and they said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying, "The people are bringing much more than enough for the service, for the work that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has commanded to do."

[6] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ commanded, and they made a voice pass through the camp, saying, "Man and woman, let them do no more work for the contribution of the Holy." And the people were kept back from bringing.

[7] And the work was enough for them, for all the work, to do it, and there was left over.

[8] And every one wise of heart among the doers of the work made the dwelling with ten curtains: fine twined linen and blue and purple and scarlet; with ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, the work of a designer, he made them.

[9] The length of the one curtain was twenty-eight cubits, and the width four cubits, the one curtain; one measure for all the curtains.

[10] And he joined the five curtains one to one, and five curtains he joined one to one.

[11] And he made loops of blue on the edge of the one curtain, at the end, in the joining; so he made on the edge of the outermost curtain, in the second joining.

[12] Fifty loops he made on the one curtain, and fifty loops he made on the end of the curtain that was in the second joining, the loops being opposite one to one.

[13] And he made fifty clasps of gold, and he joined the curtains one to one with the clasps, and the dwelling was one.

[14] And he made curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the dwelling; eleven curtains he made them.

[15] The length of the one curtain was thirty cubits, and four cubits the width of the one curtain; one measure for the eleven curtains.

[16] And he joined the five curtains by themselves and the six curtains by themselves.

[17] And he made loops, fifty, on the edge of the curtain, the outermost in the joining, and fifty loops he made on the edge of the curtain of the second joining.

[18] And he made clasps of bronze, fifty, to join the tent, to be one.

[19] And he made a covering for the tent, skins of rams dyed red, and a covering of skins of tachash above.

[20] And he made the boards for the dwelling, acacia wood, standing up.

[21] Ten cubits was the length of the board, and a cubit and a half the width of the one board.

[22] Two tenons for the one board, fitted one to one; so he made for all the boards of the dwelling.

[23] And he made the boards for the dwelling: twenty boards for the south side, southward.

[24] And forty sockets of silver he made under the twenty boards: two sockets under the one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under the one board for its two tenons.

[25] And for the second side of the dwelling, for the north side, he made twenty boards,

[26] and their forty sockets of silver: two sockets under the one board, and two sockets under the one board.

[27] And for the rear of the dwelling, westward, he made six boards.

[28] And two boards he made for the corners of the dwelling in the rear.

[29] And they were double from below, and together they were to be whole to its top, into the one ring; so he made for the two of them, for the two corners.

[30] And there were eight boards, and their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets: two sockets, two sockets, under the one board.

[31] And he made bars of acacia wood: five for the boards of the one side of the dwelling,

[32] and five bars for the boards of the second side of the dwelling, and five bars for the boards of the dwelling at the rear, westward.

[33] And he made the middle bar, to pass through in the midst of the boards from end to end.

[34] And the boards he overlaid with gold, and their rings he made of gold, housings for the bars; and he overlaid the bars with gold.

[35] And he made the veil of blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen; the work of a designer he made it, with ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧.

[36] And he made for it four pillars of acacia, and he overlaid them with gold, their hooks of gold; and he cast for them four sockets of silver.

[37] And he made a screen for the entrance of the tent, of blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen, the work of an embroiderer,

[38] and its pillars, five, and their hooks; and he overlaid their tops and their bands with gold; and their sockets, five, of bronze.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Then wrought Bezaleel and Aholiab" → "And Bezalel and Oholiab … shall do"** (v1) — Hebrew וְעָשָׂה, a perfect with "and" that carries on the speech of Moses from 35:30–35: what they are to do. The verb stands first and is singular. KJV reads it as a narrative past.
- **"every wise hearted man, in whom the LORD put wisdom" → "every man wise of heart, in whom Yahweh has given wisdom"** (v1, 2, 8) — Hebrew חֲכַם־לֵב, literally "wise of heart"; נָתַן "give". "Wisdom" (חָכְמָה) is the word of Proverbs: here the skill of the craftsman is called by it; "skill" is not another Hebrew word. In v4 "the wise men" is הַחֲכָמִים "the wise".
- **"all manner of work for the service of the sanctuary" → "all the work of the service of the Holy"** (v1, 3, 4, 6) — Hebrew מְלֶאכֶת עֲבֹדַת הַקֹּדֶשׁ: מְלָאכָה "work", עֲבֹדָה "service", הַקֹּדֶשׁ "the Holy" (the sanctuary; "place" is not in the Hebrew, and "the holy work" is also possible).
- **"whose heart stirred him up" → "whose heart lifted him up"** (v2) — Hebrew נְשָׂאוֹ לִבּוֹ. **"to come unto the work" → "to come near to the work"** — Hebrew לְקָרְבָה.
- **"they received of Moses all the offering" → "they took from before Moses all the contribution"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיִּקְחוּ מִלִּפְנֵי; תְּרוּמָה is literally "what is lifted off", a part set aside. **"free offerings every morning" → "a freewill offering morning by morning"** — Hebrew נְדָבָה, a singular (root נָדַב "make willing"); בַּבֹּקֶר בַּבֹּקֶר, literally "in the morning, in the morning". The Hebrew stresses "they" (וְהֵם): the sons of Israel.
- **"every man from his work which they made" → "each man from his work that they were doing"** (v4) — Hebrew אִישׁ־אִישׁ, literally "man, man"; the change from "his" to "they" is in the Hebrew.
- **"The people bring much more than enough for the service of the work" → "The people are bringing much more than enough for the service, for the work"** (v5) — Hebrew מַרְבִּים … לְהָבִיא מִדֵּי הָעֲבֹדָה לַמְּלָאכָה, literally "are making much to bring, beyond the need of the service for the work".
- **"they caused it to be proclaimed throughout the camp" → "they made a voice pass through the camp"** (v6) — Hebrew וַיַּעֲבִירוּ קוֹל. **"Let neither man nor woman make any more work for the offering of the sanctuary" → "Man and woman, let them do no more work for the contribution of the Holy"** — Hebrew אִישׁ וְאִשָּׁה אַל־יַעֲשׂוּ. **"were restrained" → "were kept back"** — Hebrew וַיִּכָּלֵא.
- **"the stuff they had was sufficient for all the work to make it, and too much" → "the work was enough for them, for all the work, to do it, and there was left over"** (v7) — Hebrew מְלָאכָה "work" twice: first of what had been brought and prepared, then of the making. וְהוֹתֵר is an infinitive, "and to leave over".
- **"made ten curtains" → "made the dwelling with ten curtains"** (v8) — Hebrew אֶת־הַמִּשְׁכָּן עֶשֶׂר יְרִיעֹת, as 26:1. The verb changes from "they made" to "he made" in this verse and stays singular to the end of the chapter; no name is given. "Blue and purple and scarlet" are the dyes and stand for wool dyed with them; "yarn" is not in the Hebrew. **"cherubims of cunning work" → "*keruvim*, the work of a designer"** (v8, 35) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב.
- **"twenty and eight cubits" → "twenty-eight cubits"** (v9, 15) — literally "eight and twenty by the cubit"; "one measure" is literal.
- **"coupled" → "joined"; "selvedge … coupling" → "edge … joining"; "held one curtain to another" → "opposite one to one"; "taches" → "clasps"** (v10–18) — Hebrew חִבֵּר, שָׂפָה (literally "lip"), מַחְבֶּרֶת / חֹבֶרֶת (the set of joined curtains, or the seam: not decided), מַקְבִּילֹת, קְרָסִים. "One to one" is אַחַת אֶל־אֶחָת (v10, 12, 13, 22); the command has אִשָּׁה אֶל־אֲחֹתָהּ, "one to another" (26:3, 5, 6, 17). "Loops, fifty" (v17) is the Hebrew order; 26:10 has "fifty loops". "It became one tabernacle" → "the dwelling was one" (v13).
- **"curtains of goats' hair for the tent over the tabernacle" → "curtains of goats' hair for a tent over the dwelling"** (v14) — Hebrew עִזִּים "goats" ("hair" is supplied); לְאֹהֶל without the article.
- **"rams' skins dyed red … badgers' skins" → "skins of rams dyed red … skins of tachash"** (v19) — Hebrew מְאָדָּמִים "reddened"; what animal or kind of leather תַּחַשׁ is, is not known, and the word is kept.
- **"boards … of shittim wood, standing up" → "the boards … acacia wood, standing up"** (v20) — Hebrew קְרָשִׁים: uncertain, a plank or an open frame. **"tenons, equally distant one from another" → "tenons, fitted one to one"** (v22) — Hebrew יָדֹת, literally "hands"; מְשֻׁלָּבֹת.
- **"for the south side southward" kept** (v23) — Hebrew לִפְאַת נֶגֶב תֵּימָנָה: "Negev" and "Teman"; "north" (v25) is צָפוֹן, "westward" (v27, 32) is יָמָּה "toward the sea". **"the other side" → "the second side"** (v25, 32) — Hebrew צֶלַע … הַשֵּׁנִית.
- **"for the sides of the tabernacle westward" → "for the rear of the dwelling, westward"** (v27, 28, 32) — Hebrew יַרְכָתַיִם, literally "the two flanks" (root of יָרֵךְ "thigh").
- **"they were coupled beneath, and coupled together at the head thereof, to one ring" → "they were double from below, and together they were to be whole to its top, into the one ring"** (v29) — Hebrew תוֹאֲמִם ("twins") … תַמִּים ("whole, complete"); the second verb is an imperfect (יִהְיוּ), kept from the wording of the command (26:24). How the corner boards were made is not clear.
- **"under every board two sockets" → "two sockets, two sockets, under the one board"** (v30) — the Hebrew repeats the words: two for each board.
- **"to shoot through the boards from the one end to the other" → "to pass through in the midst of the boards from end to end"** (v33) — Hebrew לִבְרֹחַ, the verb of בְּרִיחַ "bar".
- **"places for the bars" → "housings for the bars"** (v34) — Hebrew בָּתִּים, literally "houses".
- **"a vail" → "the veil"** (v35) — the Hebrew has the article. **"with cherubims made he it of cunning work" → "the work of a designer he made it, with *keruvim*"** — the Hebrew order.
- **"four pillars of shittim wood" → "four pillars of acacia"** (v36) — Hebrew עַמּוּדֵי שִׁטִּים; "wood" is not written here.
- **"an hanging for the tabernacle door … of needlework" → "a screen for the entrance of the tent … the work of an embroiderer"** (v37) — Hebrew מָסָךְ לְפֶתַח הָאֹהֶל, מַעֲשֵׂה רֹקֵם.
- **"he overlaid their chapiters and their fillets with gold" → "he overlaid their tops and their bands with gold"** (v38) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁיהֶם, literally "their heads"; חֲשֻׁקֵיהֶם is uncertain: a ring or a rod joining the pillars. "Chapiter" is old English for the head of a column.

### Notes

- **Parallels** — v8–38 carry out 26:1–37 almost word for word; the hanging over of the curtains (26:9b, 12–13) and the placing of the veil and the furniture (26:30, 33–35) are not repeated here.
- **"Too much to give"** (v5–7) — the only occurrence in the Bible of the faithful being restrained from over-contributing. Striking: Yahweh gets too much offering, not too little. The community's outpouring exceeds even the vast needs.
- **Repetition by design** — chs 36–39 repeat ch 25–30's content almost verbatim in execution-mode. The deliberate doubling emphasizes obedient fulfillment; what was said is done exactly as said. Literary redundancy is theological affirmation.
