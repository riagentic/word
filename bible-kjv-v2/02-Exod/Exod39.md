# Exodus 39

[1] And of the blue and the purple and the scarlet they made woven garments, to minister in the Holy, and they made the holy garments that were for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[2] And he made the ephod of gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen.

[3] And they beat out the plates of gold, and he cut cords, to work in the midst of the blue and in the midst of the purple and in the midst of the scarlet and in the midst of the fine linen, the work of a designer.

[4] Shoulder pieces they made for it, joining; on its two ends it was joined.

[5] And the band of its fastening that was on it was from it, like its work: of gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[6] And they made the onyx stones, enclosed in settings of gold, engraved with the engravings of a seal, according to the names of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[7] And he put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, stones of memorial for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[8] And he made the breastpiece, the work of a designer, like the work of the ephod: of gold, blue and purple and scarlet and fine twined linen.

[9] It was square; doubled they made the breastpiece: a span its length and a span its width, doubled.

[10] And they filled in it four rows of stone: a row of carnelian, topaz and emerald, the one row.

[11] And the second row: turquoise, sapphire and diamond.

[12] And the third row: jacinth, agate and amethyst.

[13] And the fourth row: beryl, onyx and jasper; enclosed in settings of gold in their fillings.

[14] And the stones were according to the names of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, twelve, according to their names; the engravings of a seal, each according to its name, for the twelve tribes.

[15] And they made on the breastpiece chains, twisted, the work of rope, of pure gold.

[16] And they made two settings of gold and two rings of gold, and they placed the two rings on the two ends of the breastpiece.

[17] And they placed the two ropes of gold on the two rings, on the ends of the breastpiece.

[18] And the two ends of the two ropes they placed on the two settings, and they placed them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod, over against its face.

[19] And they made two rings of gold and put them on the two ends of the breastpiece, on its edge that is toward the side of the ephod, inward.

[20] And they made two rings of gold and placed them on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod, from below, on its face, alongside its joining, above the band of the ephod.

[21] And they bound the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a cord of blue, to be on the band of the ephod, and that the breastpiece should not come loose from upon the ephod, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[22] And he made the robe of the ephod, the work of a weaver, wholly of blue.

[23] And the mouth of the robe was in the midst of it, like the mouth of a coat of mail, an edge to its mouth around; it should not be torn.

[24] And they made on the skirts of the robe pomegranates of blue and purple and scarlet, twined.

[25] And they made bells of pure gold, and they placed the bells in the midst of the pomegranates, on the skirts of the robe around, in the midst of the pomegranates:

[26] a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, on the skirts of the robe around, to minister, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[27] And they made the tunics of fine linen, the work of a weaver, for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and for his sons,

[28] and the turban of fine linen, and the adornments of the caps of fine linen, and the linen breeches of fine twined linen,

[29] and the sash of fine twined linen and blue and purple and scarlet, the work of an embroiderer, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[30] And they made the plate of the holy crown of pure gold, and they wrote on it a writing, the engravings of a seal: "Holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[31] And they placed on it a cord of blue, to place it on the turban above, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[32] And all the service of the dwelling of the tent of meeting was finished. And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did; according to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so they did.

[33] And they brought the dwelling to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧: the tent and all its utensils, its clasps, its boards, its bars and its pillars and its sockets,

[34] and the covering of skins of rams dyed red, and the covering of skins of tachash, and the veil of the screen,

[35] the ark of the testimony and its poles and the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧,

[36] the table, all its utensils, and the bread of the face,

[37] the pure lampstand, its lamps, the lamps of the arrangement, and all its utensils, and the oil of the light,

[38] and the altar of gold, and the oil of anointing, and the fragrant incense, and the screen of the entrance of the tent,

[39] the altar of bronze and the grating of bronze that belongs to it, its poles and all its utensils, the basin and its stand,

[40] the hangings of the court, its pillars and its sockets, and the screen for the gate of the court, its cords and its pegs, and all the utensils of the service of the dwelling, for the tent of meeting,

[41] the woven garments, to minister in the Holy, the holy garments for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest, and the garments of his sons, to be priests.

[42] According to all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, so the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did all the service.

[43] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ saw all the work, and behold, they had done it; as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded, so they had done. And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ blessed them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Hebrew יהוה, the Tetragrammaton.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. The verbs change between "he made" and "they made" as the Hebrew has them (v2, 7, 8, 22 singular).
- **"blue, and purple, and scarlet" kept** (v1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 24, 29) — Hebrew תְּכֵלֶת, אַרְגָּמָן, תּוֹלַעַת שָׁנִי (literally "worm of scarlet", the insect that gives the dye). The three are dyes and stand for wool dyed with them; "yarn" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"cloths of service" → "woven garments"** (v1, 41) — Hebrew בִּגְדֵי שְׂרָד. The meaning of שְׂרָד is uncertain: the word is found only in this phrase (31:10; 35:19). Best supported is "plaited, woven work"; KJV "service" takes it from שֵׁרֵת "minister".
- **"to do service in the holy place" → "to minister in the Holy"** (v1, 41) — Hebrew לְשָׁרֵת בַּקֹּדֶשׁ; "place" is not in the Hebrew. "The holy garments" is literally "the garments of holiness".
- **"fine twined linen" kept** (v2, 5, 8, 28, 29) — Hebrew שֵׁשׁ מָשְׁזָר: fine linen twisted of several threads.
- **"they did beat the gold into thin plates, and cut it into wires, to work it in the blue" → "they beat out the plates of gold, and he cut cords, to work in the midst of the blue"** (v3) — Hebrew וַיְרַקְּעוּ (root of רָקִיעַ "expanse") … וְקִצֵּץ פְּתִילִם, the second verb in the singular; פָּתִיל is the "cord" of v21, 31, here a thread of gold. "It" is not in the Hebrew. This verse has no counterpart in ch 28.
- **"cunning work" → "the work of a designer"** (v3, 8) — Hebrew מַעֲשֵׂה חֹשֵׁב, from חָשַׁב "devise": work with figures in it. **"woven work" → "the work of a weaver"** (v22, 27) — אֹרֵג. **"needlework" → "the work of an embroiderer"** (v29) — רֹקֵם, one who works in colours.
- **"They made shoulderpieces for it, to couple it together: by the two edges was it coupled together" → "Shoulder pieces they made for it, joining; on its two ends it was joined"** (v4) — Hebrew חֹבְרֹת (a participle) … חֻבָּר; "on" is עַל, where the command has אֶל "at" (28:7). "Its ends" is the reading tradition (qere קְצוֹתָיו); the written form has the same sense.
- **"the curious girdle of his ephod … was of the same" → "the band of its fastening … was from it"** (v5) — Hebrew חֵשֶׁב אֲפֻדָּתוֹ; both words are uncertain (חֵשֶׁב only of this band; אֲפֻדָּה "what is bound on like an ephod"). מִמֶּנּוּ הוּא "it was from it": of one piece with it.
- **"onyx stones inclosed in ouches of gold, graven, as signets are graven, with the names" → "the onyx stones, enclosed in settings of gold, engraved with the engravings of a seal, according to the names"** (v6, 14) — Hebrew מִשְׁבְּצֹת (settings of gold thread or filigree: uncertain), פִּתּוּחֵי חוֹתָם, עַל־שְׁמוֹת "upon / according to the names". What stone שֹׁהַם is, is not certainly known.
- **"stones for a memorial to the children of Israel" → "stones of memorial for the sons of Israel"** (v7) — Hebrew אַבְנֵי זִכָּרוֹן, בְּנֵי "sons".
- **"breastplate" → "breastpiece"** (v8–21) — Hebrew חֹשֶׁן; the sense of the word is not certain.
- **"they set in it four rows of stones" → "they filled in it four rows of stone"** (v10, 13) — Hebrew וַיְמַלְאוּ־בוֹ, the verb "fill"; "in their fillings" (בְּמִלֻּאֹתָם, v13) is its noun: the places the stones fill. "The one row" (הַטּוּר הָאֶחָד) is the first row.
- **The twelve stones** (v10–13) — KJV: sardius, topaz, carbuncle; emerald, sapphire, diamond; ligure, agate, amethyst; beryl, onyx, jasper. Hebrew אֹדֶם, פִּטְדָה, בָּרֶקֶת; נֹפֶךְ, סַפִּיר, יָהֲלֹם; לֶשֶׁם, שְׁבוֹ, אַחְלָמָה; תַּרְשִׁישׁ, שֹׁהַם, יָשְׁפֵה. Only the first ("red stone") is fairly sure; the rest are traditional identifications. The סַפִּיר is probably lapis lazuli, and the יָהֲלֹם is a hard stone, not our diamond.
- **"every one with his name, according to the twelve tribes" → "each according to its name, for the twelve tribes"** (v14) — Hebrew אִישׁ עַל־שְׁמוֹ, literally "a man according to his name": each stone with the name of one son.
- **"chains at the ends, of wreathen work" → "chains, twisted, the work of rope"** (v15) — Hebrew שַׁרְשְׁרֹת גַּבְלֻת מַעֲשֵׂה עֲבֹת; גַּבְלֻת is uncertain ("twisted", or "at the borders"). In v17, 18 the chains are "the two ropes" (הָעֲבֹתֹת).
- **"pure gold" kept** (v15, 25, 30; "the pure lampstand", v37) — Hebrew טָהוֹר, the word "clean".
- **"put" → "placed"** (v16–20, 25, 31) — Hebrew נָתַן, literally "gave"; שִׂים "put" in v7, 19.
- **"before it" → "over against its face"** (v18, 20) — Hebrew אֶל־מוּל פָּנָיו, literally "toward the front of its face": on the front of the ephod; in v20 מִמּוּל פָּנָיו "from the front of its face" ("on its face").
- **"on the border of it, which was on the side of the ephod inward" → "on its edge that is toward the side of the ephod, inward"** (v19) — Hebrew שָׂפָה, literally "lip".
- **"over against the other coupling thereof" → "alongside its joining"** (v20) — Hebrew לְעֻמַּת מֶחְבַּרְתּוֹ.
- **"a lace of blue" → "a cord of blue"** (v21, 31) — Hebrew פְּתִיל תְּכֵלֶת. **"that it might be above the curious girdle" → "to be on the band"** — Hebrew לִהְיֹת עַל. **"be not loosed from the ephod" → "should not come loose from upon the ephod"** — Hebrew וְלֹא־יִזַּח … מֵעַל; רָכַס "bind" is found only here and in 28:28.
- **"all of blue" → "wholly of blue"** (v22) — Hebrew כְּלִיל תְּכֵלֶת.
- **"there was an hole in the midst of the robe, as the hole of an habergeon, with a band round about the hole, that it should not rend" → "the mouth of the robe was in the midst of it, like the mouth of a coat of mail, an edge to its mouth around; it should not be torn"** (v23) — Hebrew פֶּה "mouth" three times; שָׂפָה "lip, edge"; תַּחְרָא is found only here and in 28:32, and its meaning is uncertain. The last clause has no joining word (לֹא יִקָּרֵעַ).
- **"hems" → "skirts"** (v24–26) — Hebrew שׁוּלֵי, the lower hem. **"and twined linen" → "twined"** (v24) — the Hebrew has only מָשְׁזָר "twined"; "linen" is not there.
- **"between the pomegranates" → "in the midst of the pomegranates"** (v25) — Hebrew בְּתוֹךְ, twice.
- **"coats" → "tunics"; "mitre" → "turban"; "goodly bonnets" → "the adornments of the caps"; "linen breeches" kept; "girdle" → "sash"** (v27–29) — Hebrew כָּתְנֹת, מִצְנֶפֶת, פַּאֲרֵי הַמִּגְבָּעֹת, מִכְנְסֵי הַבָּד, אַבְנֵט. פְּאֵר is a headdress worn as an adornment (the root is "adorn"): the caps as headdresses. בַּד "linen" is another word than שֵׁשׁ "fine linen".
- **"the plate of the holy crown" kept** (v30) — Hebrew צִיץ, literally "blossom" (a shining ornament); נֵזֶר, the mark of one set apart (root of "Nazirite").
- **"a writing, like to the engravings of a signet" → "a writing, the engravings of a seal"** (v30) — the Hebrew has no "like". **"HOLINESS TO THE LORD" → "Holy to Yahweh"** — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ לַיהוָה, as in 28:36; קֹדֶשׁ is the noun: "holiness", "a holy thing", belonging to Yahweh.
- **"to fasten it on high upon the mitre" → "to place it on the turban above"** (v31) — Hebrew לָתֵת.
- **"Thus was all the work … finished" → "And all the service … was finished"** (v32, 40, 42) — Hebrew עֲבֹדָה "service" (from עָבַד "serve"); "the work" in v43 is מְלָאכָה. "Was finished" (וַתֵּכֶל) is the verb of Gen 2:1. **"did according to all" → "did; according to all … so they did"** — the Hebrew accents divide after "did".
- **"his furniture" → "its utensils"** (v33, 36, 37, 39, 40) — Hebrew כֵּלִים, any made articles. **"his taches, his boards, his bars" → "its clasps, its boards, its bars"** — "its bars" is the reading tradition (qere בְּרִיחָיו); the written form is the singular, "its bar".
- **"rams' skins dyed red" → "skins of rams dyed red"; "badgers' skins" → "skins of tachash"** (v34) — Hebrew מְאָדָּמִים "reddened"; what animal or kind of leather תַּחַשׁ is, is not known, and the word is kept. **"the vail of the covering" → "the veil of the screen"** — Hebrew פָּרֹכֶת הַמָּסָךְ.
- **"mercy seat" → "*kapporet*"** (v35) — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת, the cover of the ark; from כִּפֶּר "make atonement": "atonement cover".
- **"shewbread" → "the bread of the face"** (v36) — Hebrew לֶחֶם הַפָּנִים: the bread set before the face of Yahweh (25:30 "before my face"). "Bread of the Presence" is the same thought.
- **"the lamps to be set in order" → "the lamps of the arrangement"** (v37) — Hebrew נֵרֹת הַמַּעֲרָכָה, the noun of עָרַךְ "arrange". **"oil for light" → "the oil of the light"** — Hebrew שֶׁמֶן הַמָּאוֹר; מָאוֹר is a thing that gives light (Gen 1:14).
- **"the golden altar" → "the altar of gold"; "sweet incense" → "fragrant incense"; "the hanging for the tabernacle door" → "the screen of the entrance of the tent"** (v38) — Hebrew קְטֹרֶת הַסַּמִּים "the incense of fragrant substances"; מָסַךְ פֶּתַח הָאֹהֶל.
- **"brasen altar … grate of brass" → "the altar of bronze and the grating of bronze that belongs to it"** (v39) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת; אֲשֶׁר־לוֹ, as 35:16; "laver and his foot" → "the basin and its stand".
- **"the vessels of the service of the tabernacle, for the tent of the congregation" → "the utensils of the service of the dwelling, for the tent of meeting"** (v40) — Hebrew מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling", אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד.
- **"to minister in the priest's office" → "to be priests"** (v41) — Hebrew לְכַהֵן, the verb made from כֹּהֵן "priest".
- **"Moses did look upon all the work" → "Moses saw all the work"** (v43) — Hebrew וַיַּרְא. "Behold, they had done it; as Yahweh had commanded, so they had done": the Hebrew accents divide so. **"blessed them" (v43)** — the words of Gen 1:28 and 2:3: Moses saw the work, and behold … and blessed, as *Elohim* saw all that he had made, and behold … and blessed (Gen 1:31; 2:3).

### Notes

- **"As Yahweh commanded Moses" refrain** — repeated seven times in the making of the garments (v1, 5, 7, 21, 26, 29, 31), and again of the whole work in v32, 42, 43. Emphatic literary stress on perfect obedience.
- **Parallels ch 28** — the priestly garments are made as they were commanded there. The Urim and the Thummim (28:30) are not named in this chapter.
- **Moses' blessing (v43)** — echoes Gen 1's creation-blessing. The tabernacle is a microcosm; its completion is a mini-creation.
