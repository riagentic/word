# Exodus 39

[1] From the blue, purple, and scarlet yarns they made the finely-woven garments for serving in the Holy Place, and made the holy garments for Aaron — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[2] He made the ephod of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarns, and of fine twined linen.

[3] They beat the gold into thin plates and cut it into cords, to work it into the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and into the fine linen — in skillful work.

[4] They made shoulder-pieces for it, attached to its two edges to be joined together.

[5] The skillfully woven band on it, of the same workmanship, was of one piece with it — of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarns, and of fine twined linen — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[6] They prepared the onyx stones, enclosed in settings of gold filigree, engraved like the engravings of a signet with the names of the children of Israel.

[7] He set them on the shoulder-pieces of the ephop as memorial stones for the children of Israel — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[8] He made the breastpiece, in skillful work, like the work of the ephod — of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarns, and of fine twined linen.

[9] It was square; they made the breastpiece doubled — a span was its length and a span its width, when doubled.

[10] They set in it four rows of stones: a row of ruby, chrysolite, and beryl was the first row;

[11] the second row: turquoise, sapphire, and emerald;

[12] the third row: jacinth, agate, and amethyst;

[13] the fourth row: topaz, onyx, and jasper. They were enclosed in settings of gold filigree.

[14] The stones were according to the names of the children of Israel, twelve, according to their names; engraved like signets, each with its name, for the twelve tribes.

[15] They made for the breastpiece chains of pure gold, twisted like cords.

[16] They made two settings of gold filigree and two gold rings, and put the two rings on the two ends of the breastpiece.

[17] They put the two twisted chains of gold in the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece.

[18] They attached the other two ends of the two twisted chains to the two filigree settings, which they fastened to the shoulder-pieces of the ephod in front.

[19] They made two rings of gold and put them on the two ends of the breastpiece, at its edge that was on the inner side of the ephod.

[20] They made two more rings of gold and attached them in front to the lower part of the two shoulder-pieces of the ephod, close to where it joined, above the skillfully woven band of the ephod.

[21] They bound the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a blue cord, that it might lie on the skillfully woven band of the ephod, and that the breastpiece might not come loose from the ephod — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[22] He made the robe of the ephod of woven work, all of blue.

[23] The opening of the robe was in the middle of it, like the opening of a coat of mail, with a binding around the opening, that it should not be torn.

[24] They made on the hem of the robe pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and twined linen.

[25] They made bells of pure gold, and put the bells between the pomegranates on the hem of the robe all around, between the pomegranates —

[26] a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, around the hem of the robe for ministering — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[27] They made tunics of fine linen, woven work, for Aaron and his sons,

[28] and a turban of fine linen, and embellished headdresses of fine linen, and linen undergarments of fine twined linen;

[29] and a sash of fine twined linen — blue, purple, and scarlet, embroidered with needlework — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[30] They made the plate of the holy diadem of pure gold, and wrote on it an inscription like the engravings of a signet: "Holy to Yahweh."

[31] They fastened on it a blue cord to attach it on the turban above — as Yahweh commanded Moses.

[32] Thus all the work of the dwelling of the Tent of Meeting was finished; and the children of Israel did according to all that Yahweh had commanded Moses — so they did.

[33] Then they brought the dwelling to Moses — the tent, and all its furnishings: its clasps, its frames, its bars, its pillars, and its sockets;

[34] the covering of tanned ram skins, the covering of fine leather, and the screening veil;

[35] the ark of the Testimony with its poles, and the mercy seat;

[36] the table with all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence;

[37] the pure lampstand with its lamps — the lamps set in order — and all its utensils, and the oil for the light;

[38] the golden altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the screen for the entrance of the tent;

[39] the bronze altar with its bronze grating, its poles, and all its utensils; the basin and its stand;

[40] the hangings of the court, its pillars, its sockets, and the screen for the gate of the court, its cords, and its pegs; and all the utensils for the service of the dwelling, for the Tent of Meeting;

[41] the finely-woven garments for serving in the Holy Place — the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons, to serve as priests.

[42] According to all that Yahweh commanded Moses, so the children of Israel did all the work.

[43] Moses looked over all the work, and behold, they had done it as Yahweh had commanded; so had they done it. And Moses blessed them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Parallels ch 28**. Priestly garments constructed.
- **"Holy to Yahweh"** (v30) — as in 28:36.
- **"blessed them" (v43)** — echo of Gen 1:28 / 2:3: a creation-completion pattern. The tabernacle's completion parallels the creation of the world — both finished and blessed.

### Notes

- **"As Yahweh commanded Moses" refrain** — repeated seven times in this chapter (v1, 5, 7, 21, 26, 29, 31), plus the framing v42 — a total of eight. Emphatic literary stress on perfect obedience.
- **Moses' blessing (v43)** — echoes Gen 1's creation-blessing. The tabernacle is a microcosm; its completion is a mini-creation.
