# Exodus 25

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and let them take for me a contribution; from every man whose heart makes him willing you shall take my contribution.

[3] And this is the contribution that you shall take from them: gold and silver and bronze,

[4] and blue and purple and scarlet and fine linen and goats' hair,

[5] and skins of rams dyed red and skins of tachash and acacia wood,

[6] oil for the light, spices for the oil of anointing and for the fragrant incense,

[7] onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.

[8] And let them make me a sanctuary, and I will abide in their midst.

[9] According to all that I am showing you, the pattern of the dwelling and the pattern of all its utensils, and so you shall make it.

[10] And they shall make an ark of acacia wood: two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

[11] And you shall overlay it with pure gold; inside and outside you shall overlay it; and you shall make on it a molding of gold around.

[12] And you shall cast for it four rings of gold and place them on its four feet: two rings on its one side, and two rings on its second side.

[13] And you shall make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.

[14] And you shall bring the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark with them.

[15] In the rings of the ark the poles shall be; they shall not depart from it.

[16] And you shall place into the ark the testimony that I will give you.

[17] And you shall make a ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ of pure gold: two cubits and a half its length, and a cubit and a half its width.

[18] And you shall make two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ of gold; of hammered work you shall make them, from the two ends of the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧.

[19] And make one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ from the end on this side and one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ from the end on that side; from the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ you shall make the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧, on its two ends.

[20] And the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ shall be spreading out wings above, screening the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ with their wings, and their faces each toward his brother; toward the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ shall the faces of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ be.

[21] And you shall place the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ on the ark above, and into the ark you shall place the testimony that I will give you.

[22] And I will meet you there, and I will speak with you from above the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧, from between the two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ that are on the ark of the testimony, all that I will command you for the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[23] And you shall make a table of acacia wood: two cubits its length, and a cubit its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

[24] And you shall overlay it with pure gold, and you shall make for it a molding of gold around.

[25] And you shall make for it a frame of a handbreadth around, and you shall make a molding of gold for its frame around.

[26] And you shall make for it four rings of gold, and you shall place the rings on the four edges that belong to its four legs.

[27] Alongside the frame the rings shall be, for housings for poles, to carry the table.

[28] And you shall make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold, and the table shall be carried with them.

[29] And you shall make its dishes and its pans and its jars and its bowls, with which it is poured out; of pure gold you shall make them.

[30] And you shall place on the table the bread of the face before me continually.

[31] And you shall make a lampstand of pure gold; of hammered work the lampstand shall be made: its base and its shaft, its goblets, its knobs and its flowers shall be from it.

[32] And six branches going out from its sides: three branches of the lampstand from its one side, and three branches of the lampstand from its second side.

[33] Three goblets made like almonds on the one branch, a knob and a flower, and three goblets made like almonds on the one branch, a knob and a flower: so for the six branches going out from the lampstand.

[34] And on the lampstand four goblets made like almonds, its knobs and its flowers;

[35] and a knob under the two branches from it, and a knob under the two branches from it, and a knob under the two branches from it, for the six branches going out from the lampstand.

[36] Their knobs and their branches shall be from it; all of it one hammered work of pure gold.

[37] And you shall make its lamps, seven; and one shall set up its lamps and give light toward the side of its face.

[38] And its tongs and its firepans, of pure gold.

[39] Of a talent of pure gold one shall make it, with all these utensils.

[40] And see, and make them by their pattern, which you are being shown on the mountain."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew יְהוָה.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וְ "and"; it is kept.
- **"that they bring me an offering" → "let them take for me a contribution"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew וְיִקְחוּ־לִי תְּרוּמָה: the verb is לָקַח "take", three times in v2–3. תְּרוּמָה *terumah* is "what is lifted off" (from הֵרִים "lift up"): a part set aside for Yahweh; not a sacrifice. **"that giveth it willingly with his heart" → "whose heart makes him willing"** — Hebrew אֲשֶׁר יִדְּבֶנּוּ לִבּוֹ, "whom his heart impels". "You shall take" is plural in v2, 3.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v3) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת, copper and its alloy.
- **"blue, and purple, and scarlet" kept** (v4) — Hebrew תְּכֵלֶת, אַרְגָּמָן, תּוֹלַעַת שָׁנִי: names of dyes, standing for wool dyed with them; "yarn" is not in the Hebrew. The first two are dyes from sea snails (a blue-violet and a red-purple); the third is literally "worm of scarlet", the dye from an insect. **"fine linen"** — Hebrew שֵׁשׁ, an Egyptian word. **"goats' hair"** — Hebrew עִזִּים "goats"; "hair" is supplied.
- **"rams' skins dyed red" kept; "badgers' skins" → "skins of tachash"** (v5) — Hebrew עֹרֹת אֵילִם מְאָדָּמִים, "reddened"; עֹרֹת תְּחָשִׁים: what the תַּחַשׁ is, is not known (an animal: badger, dolphin or sea cow have been named; or a kind or colour of leather, cf. Ezek 16:10). The word is kept. **"shittim wood" → "acacia wood"** (v5, 10, 13, 23, 28) — Hebrew עֲצֵי שִׁטִּים, literally "woods of acacias".
- **"oil for the light, spices for anointing oil, and for sweet incense" → "… spices for the oil of anointing and for the fragrant incense"** (v6) — Hebrew לַמָּאֹר "for the light" (the thing that gives light, as Gen 1:14); שֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה; קְטֹרֶת הַסַּמִּים, literally "the incense of the fragrant substances" (סַמִּים, another word than בְּשָׂמִים "spices").
- **"stones to be set in the ephod, and in the breastplate" → "stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece"** (v7) — Hebrew אַבְנֵי מִלֻּאִים, literally "stones of fillings" (to fill the settings); שֹׁהַם "onyx" is a traditional identification. אֵפוֹד and חֹשֶׁן are described in ch 28; the sense of חֹשֶׁן is not certain.
- **"that I may dwell among them" → "and I will abide in their midst"** (v8) — Hebrew וְשָׁכַנְתִּי בְּתוֹכָם: שָׁכַן "abide", the verb from which מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling" (v9) is made, and "and" with a simple future, not a clause of purpose. מִקְדָּשׁ "sanctuary" is "a holy place" (root קדשׁ).
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v9) — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁכָּן *ha-mishkan*, "the place of abiding". "Tabernacle" is the Latin *tabernaculum* "tent". **"pattern"** (v9, 40) — Hebrew תַּבְנִית, from בָּנָה "build": a model or plan. **"instruments" / "vessels" → "utensils"** (v9, 39) — Hebrew כֵּלִים, made articles of any kind. **"that I shew thee" → "that I am showing you"** — a participle; "you" is singular, "you shall make" plural; "it" is supplied.
- **"ark"** (v10–22) — Hebrew אֲרוֹן, "chest" (the word of Gen 50:26; not the תֵּבָה of Noah). **"two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof" → "two cubits and a half its length"** (v10, 17, 23) — the Hebrew has no verb. The cubit (אַמָּה) is the length of the forearm, about 45 cm. "They shall make" (v10) is plural; from v11 "you" is singular.
- **"within and without" → "inside and outside"** (v11) — Hebrew מִבַּיִת וּמִחוּץ. **"a crown of gold" → "a molding of gold"** (v11, 24, 25) — Hebrew זֵר, used only of the ark, the table and the incense altar: a raised border or wreath; the exact sense is uncertain.
- **"put them in the four corners thereof" → "place them on its four feet"** (v12) — Hebrew פַּעֲמֹתָיו, literally "its steps, feet". **"place"** (v12, 16, 21, 26, 30) is נָתַן, literally "give". **"side"** — Hebrew צֵלָע. **"the other side" → "its second side"**.
- **"staves" → "poles"** (v13, 14, 15, 27, 28) — Hebrew בַּדִּים. **"they shall not be taken from it" → "they shall not depart from it"** (v15) — Hebrew לֹא יָסֻרוּ.
- **"the testimony"** (v16, 21, 22) — Hebrew הָעֵדֻת: what bears witness; the tablets (31:18).
- **"mercy seat" → "*kapporet*"** (v17–22) — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת, from כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (root "cover"): the atonement cover, a plate of gold on the ark. "Seat" is not in the word; "mercy seat" comes from Luther's *Gnadenstuhl* through Tyndale, after the Septuagint's ἱλαστήριον "place of atonement" (cited from memory; cf. Rom 3:25; Heb 9:5).
- **"cherubims" → "cherubim"** (v18–22) — Hebrew כְּרֻבִים; what they looked like the chapter does not say beyond wings and faces. **"of beaten work" → "of hammered work"** (v18, 31, 36) — Hebrew מִקְשָׁה.
- **"in the two ends" / "on the one end" / "of the mercy seat" → "from the two ends" / "from the end on this side" / "from the *kapporet*"** (v18, 19) — Hebrew מִן "from" each time: מִשְּׁנֵי קְצוֹת, מִקָּצָה מִזֶּה, מִן־הַכַּפֹּרֶת. "From the *kapporet*" means of one piece with it; so "from it" of the parts of the lampstand (v31, 35, 36). "Make" is singular at the head of v19 and plural at its end.
- **"shall stretch forth their wings on high, covering the mercy seat" → "shall be spreading out wings above, screening the *kapporet*"** (v20) — Hebrew participles; סָכַךְ "screen, cover over". **"their faces shall look one to another" → "their faces each toward his brother"** — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶל־אָחִיו.
- **"above upon the ark" → "on the ark above"** (v21) — Hebrew עַל־הָאָרֹן מִלְמָעְלָה.
- **"there I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee … of all things which I will give thee in commandment unto the children of Israel" → "I will meet you there, and I will speak with you … all that I will command you for the sons of Israel"** (v22) — Hebrew וְנוֹעַדְתִּי, niphal of יָעַד "appoint" (the root of מוֹעֵד in "tent of meeting"): meet by appointment; דִּבֶּר "speak"; אֶל־בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל "to the sons of Israel".
- **"a border of an hand breadth" → "a frame of a handbreadth"** (v25, 27) — Hebrew מִסְגֶּרֶת, "what closes in": a rim or a rail between the legs; uncertain.
- **"in the four corners that are on the four feet thereof" → "on the four edges that belong to its four legs"** (v26) — Hebrew פֵּאֹת "edges, sides"; רַגְלָיו "its legs" (literally "feet", another word than in v12).
- **"Over against the border … for places of the staves" → "Alongside the frame … for housings for poles"** (v27) — Hebrew לְעֻמַּת "close beside"; לְבָתִּים לְבַדִּים, as 30:4 and 37:27 (37:14 has "housings for the poles", בָּתִּים לַבַּדִּים); בָּתִּים, literally "houses".
- **"the dishes thereof, and spoons thereof, and covers thereof, and bowls thereof, to cover withal" → "its dishes and its pans and its jars and its bowls, with which it is poured out"** (v29) — Hebrew קְעָרֹת, כַּפֹּת (literally "palms": shallow pans), קְשׂוֹת, מְנַקִּיֹּת; the last two are uncertain. אֲשֶׁר יֻסַּךְ בָּהֵן is "with which it is poured" (נָסַךְ, the verb of the drink offering); KJV took it from סָכַךְ "cover".
- **"shewbread" → "the bread of the face"** (v30) — Hebrew לֶחֶם פָּנִים, literally "bread of face": the bread set before the face of Yahweh; the next word is לְפָנַי "before me", literally "to my face". Other renderings: "bread of the Presence", "showbread". **"alway" → "continually"** — Hebrew תָּמִיד.
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"** (v31–35) — Hebrew מְנוֹרָה *menorah*, a stand for oil lamps. **"his shaft, and his branches, his bowls, his knops, and his flowers" → "its base and its shaft, its goblets, its knobs and its flowers"** (v31) — Hebrew יְרֵכָהּ, literally "its thigh"; קָנָהּ, literally "its reed", the same word as the "branches" of v32–36 (here singular: the middle stem); גָּבִיעַ "goblet" (Gen 44:2); כַּפְתֹּר, a rounded swelling (uncertain); פֶּרַח "flower, blossom".
- **"made like unto almonds" kept** (v33, 34) — Hebrew מְשֻׁקָּדִים, from שָׁקֵד "almond": shaped like the almond, its blossom or its fruit. In v33 the Hebrew says "on the one branch" twice (the branch on this side and its fellow on that).
- **"that they may give light over against it" → "and give light toward the side of its face"** (v37) — Hebrew וְהֶעֱלָה אֶת־נֵרֹתֶיהָ וְהֵאִיר עַל־עֵבֶר פָּנֶיהָ: both verbs are third person singular with no subject named ("one"). הֶעֱלָה is "make go up": set the lamps up on the stand, or make the flame go up (light them). "The side of its face" is the space in front of it. נֵר is the lamp itself, a small bowl for oil and wick.
- **"snuffdishes" → "firepans"** (v38) — Hebrew מַחְתֹּת, pans for carrying embers (27:3). **"tongs"** — Hebrew מַלְקָחַיִם.
- **"a talent"** (v39) — Hebrew כִּכָּר, literally "a round": the largest weight, about 34 kg. **"shall he make it"** — Hebrew יַעֲשֶׂה, third person. **"look that thou make them after their pattern, which was shewed thee" → "see, and make them by their pattern, which you are being shown"** (v40) — two imperatives; מָרְאֶה is a passive participle. Cited in Heb 8:5; Acts 7:44.

### Notes

- **The pattern** (v9, 40) — Moses is shown a תַּבְנִית on the mountain and is to make everything by it; Heb 8:5 reads the earthly sanctuary as a copy of the heavenly one.
- **The order of the chapter** — the contribution (v1–9), the ark with the *kapporet* and the cherubim (v10–22), the table (v23–30), the lampstand (v31–40). The making is told in 37:1–24 in nearly the same words.
- **"Enthroned on the cherubim"** — cf. 1 Sam 4:4; Ps 80:1; Num 7:89, where the voice speaks from above the *kapporet*, from between the two cherubim.
