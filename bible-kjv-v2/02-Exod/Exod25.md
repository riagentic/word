# Exodus 25

[1] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, that they bring me a contribution. From every man whose heart moves him, you shall take my contribution.

[3] And this is the contribution that you shall take from them: gold, silver, and bronze;

[4] blue, purple, and scarlet yarns, and fine linen, and goats' hair;

[5] tanned ram skins, fine leather, and acacia wood;

[6] oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil and for the fragrant incense;

[7] onyx stones and stones for setting, for the ephod and for the breastpiece.

[8] And let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell in their midst.

[9] According to all that I show you concerning the pattern of the dwelling and the pattern of all its furnishings, so shall you make it.

[10] They shall make an ark of acacia wood; its length shall be two and a half cubits, its width a cubit and a half, and its height a cubit and a half.

[11] You shall overlay it with pure gold — inside and outside you shall overlay it — and you shall make upon it a molding of gold all around.

[12] You shall cast four rings of gold for it and put them on its four feet, two rings on one side of it and two rings on the other.

[13] You shall make poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.

[14] You shall put the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark with them.

[15] The poles shall remain in the rings of the ark; they shall not be removed from it.

[16] And into the ark you shall put the Testimony that I will give you.

[17] You shall make a ⟦mercy-seat|mercy seat (kapporet, atonement cover)|mercy seat⟧ of pure gold; its length shall be two and a half cubits, and its width a cubit and a half.

[18] You shall make two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ of gold; of hammered work you shall make them, at the two ends of the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧.

[19] Make one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ at the one end, and one ⟦cherub|keruv|cherub⟧ at the other end; of one piece with the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ shall you make the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ on its two ends.

[20] The ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ shall spread out their wings above, overshadowing the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ with their wings, with their faces toward each other; the faces of the ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ shall be toward the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧.

[21] You shall put the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ on top of the ark; and in the ark you shall put the Testimony that I will give you.

[22] There I will meet with you, and from above the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧, from between the two ⟦cherubim|keruvim|cherubim⟧ that are on the ark of the Testimony, I will speak with you about all that I give you in commandment for the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧.

[23] You shall make a table of acacia wood; two cubits shall be its length, a cubit its width, and a cubit and a half its height.

[24] You shall overlay it with pure gold, and you shall make a molding of gold for it all around.

[25] You shall make for it a rim of a handbreadth all around, and you shall make a molding of gold for its rim all around.

[26] You shall make for it four rings of gold and put the rings at the four corners that are on its four feet.

[27] Close to the rim the rings shall be, as holders for the poles to carry the table.

[28] You shall make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold; and the table shall be carried by them.

[29] You shall make its plates, its dishes, its pitchers, and its bowls for pouring libations; of pure gold you shall make them.

[30] And you shall set upon the table the bread of the Presence before me, continually.

[31] You shall make a lampstand of pure gold. The lampstand shall be made of hammered work — its base, its shaft, its cups, its capitals, and its flowers shall be of one piece with it.

[32] Six branches shall come out of its sides — three branches of the lampstand from one side, and three branches of the lampstand from the other side.

[33] Three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a capital and a flower, shall be on one branch; and three cups shaped like almond blossoms, each with a capital and a flower, on the other branch — so for the six branches coming out of the lampstand.

[34] On the lampstand itself shall be four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with their capitals and their flowers.

[35] A capital shall be under each pair of the six branches going out from it — according to the six branches coming out of the lampstand.

[36] Their capitals and their branches shall be of one piece with it; the whole of it shall be a single piece of hammered work of pure gold.

[37] You shall make its seven lamps; and its lamps shall be set up so as to give light upon the space in front of it.

[38] Its tongs and its snuff-trays shall be of pure gold.

[39] Of a talent of pure gold it shall be made, with all these utensils.

[40] And see that you make them according to the pattern for them, which is being shown you on the mountain."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"offering" → "contribution"** (v2, 3) — Hebrew תְּרוּמָה *terumah*. *Terumah* = uplifted / dedicated gift. Not a sacrificial offering but a contribution of materials for construction.
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v3) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת *nechoshet*, as in Gen 4:22.
- **"badgers' skins" → "fine leather"** (v5) — Hebrew תְּחָשִׁים *tachashim*. Uncertain identification. Traditional translations: "badger skins" (KJV), "sealskins" / "dolphin skins" (LXX / modern marine reading), "dugong skins," or a specific dyed / fine leather. "Fine leather" reflects modern scholarly uncertainty.
- **"shittim wood" → "acacia wood"** (v5, 10, 13, 23, 28) — Hebrew עֲצֵי שִׁטִּים *atse shittim*. *Shittah* = acacia (desert-hardy tree). "Shittim" is mere transliteration in KJV.
- **"dwell among them" → "dwell in their midst"** (v8) — Hebrew וְשָׁכַנְתִּי בְּתוֹכָם *we-shakhanti be-tokham*. *Shakan* = dwell / tabernacle. Same root as *mishkan* (dwelling / tabernacle) and later *shekhinah* (divine presence). The defining theological statement: Yahweh chooses to dwell among Israel through this portable sanctuary.
- **"tabernacle" → "dwelling"** (v9) — Hebrew הַמִּשְׁכָּן *ha-mishkan* = dwelling place. Literally. "Tabernacle" = Latin *tabernaculum* (little tent).
- **"crown" → "molding"** (v11, 24, 25) — Hebrew זֵר *zer* = rim / border / molding. Not a crown in the royal sense; a raised decorative edge.
- **"cherubims" → "cherubim"** (v18, 19, 20, 22) — as in Gen 3:24.
- **"commune with thee" → "speak with you"** (v22) — modernized.
- **"shewbread" → "bread of the Presence"** (v30) — Hebrew לֶחֶם פָּנִים *lechem panim*. "Bread of faces / presence" — twelve loaves continually set before Yahweh's face. "Shewbread" (KJV, showing-bread) archaic.
- **"candlestick" → "lampstand"** (v31, 32, 33, 34, 35) — Hebrew מְנוֹרָה *menorah*. Seven-branched oil lamp (not candles are medieval). Modernized.
- **"knops" → "capitals"** (v31, 33, 34, 35, 36) — Hebrew כַּפְתֹּרִים *kaphtorim*. Decorative bulbous knobs. "Knop" is archaic.
- **"snuffdishes" → "snuff-trays"** (v38) — Hebrew מַחְתֹּתֶיהָ *machtoteha*. Trays for catching burnt wick-trimmings.
- **"talent" (v39)** — Hebrew כִּכָּר *kikkar*. Weight unit, roughly 30–35 kg. A single talent (~75 lb) of pure gold for the menorah — a substantial cost.

### Notes

- **The pattern** (v9, 40) — Moses sees a heavenly model (*tavnit*). The earthly sanctuary mirrors a heavenly reality. This becomes central in Hebrews 8:5, which interprets the tabernacle as a copy and shadow of the heavenly sanctuary.
- **The three-zone sanctuary** — the tabernacle is structured as a graded holiness: outer court, Holy Place (with table, lampstand, incense altar), Most Holy Place (with the ark). Increasing restriction of access. The architecture embodies theology.
- **The ark as throne** — the *kapporet* with winged cherubim functions as the footstool / throne of the invisible Yahweh (cf. 1 Sam 4:4 "Yahweh of Hosts enthroned above the cherubim"). Ancient Near Eastern royal-throne iconography adapted to Israelite aniconic worship.
