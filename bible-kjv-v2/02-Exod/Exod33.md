# Exodus 33

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Go, go up from here, you and the people whom you brought up from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, to the land that I swore to ⟦abraham|Avraham|Abraham⟧, to ⟦isaac|Yitzchaq|Isaac⟧ and to ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧, saying, 'To your seed I will give it.'

[2] And I will send an angel before you, and I will drive out the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧, the ⟦amorite|Emori|Amorite⟧, and the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦perizzite|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧, the ⟦hivite|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusite|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧,

[3] to a land flowing with milk and honey. For I will not go up in your midst, for you are a stiff-necked people, lest I make an end of you on the way."

[4] And the people heard this evil word, and they mourned, and no man set his ornaments upon himself.

[5] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Say to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, 'You are a stiff-necked people. If for one moment I went up in your midst, I would make an end of you. And now, take your ornaments down from upon you, that I may know what I shall do to you.'"

[6] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ stripped themselves of their ornaments, from Mount ⟦horeb|Chorev|Horeb⟧.

[7] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ would take the tent and pitch it for himself outside the camp, far off from the camp, and he would call it the tent of meeting. And it would come to pass that everyone seeking ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ would go out to the tent of meeting, which was outside the camp.

[8] And it would come to pass, when ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went out to the tent, that all the people would arise and stand, each man at the entrance of his tent, and they would look after ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ until he had gone into the tent.

[9] And it would come to pass, when ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went into the tent, that the pillar of cloud would come down and stand at the entrance of the tent and speak with ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧.

[10] And all the people would see the pillar of cloud standing at the entrance of the tent, and all the people would arise and bow down, each man at the entrance of his tent.

[11] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ would speak to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ face to face, as a man speaks to his neighbor. And he would return to the camp, and his minister ⟦joshua|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ the son of Nun, a young man, would not depart from the midst of the tent.

[12] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "See, you say to me, 'Bring up this people,' and you have not made known to me whom you will send with me. And you have said, 'I know you by name, and you have also found favor in my eyes.'

[13] And now, if, please, I have found favor in your eyes, make known to me, please, your way, that I may know you, in order that I may find favor in your eyes. And see that this nation is your people."

[14] And he said, "My face shall go, and I will give you rest."

[15] And he said to him, "If your face is not going, do not bring us up from here.

[16] And by what, then, shall it be known that I have found favor in your eyes, I and your people? Is it not by your going with us, so that we are distinguished, I and your people, from every people that is on the face of the ground?"

[17] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "This word also that you have spoken I will do, for you have found favor in my eyes, and I know you by name."

[18] And he said, "Show me, please, your glory."

[19] And he said, "I myself will make all my goodness pass by before your face, and I will call out the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ before you. And I will show favor to whom I will show favor, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion."

[20] And he said, "You cannot see my face, for man shall not see me and live."

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said, "Behold, a place by me, and you shall stand on the rock.

[22] And it shall come to pass, when my glory passes by, that I will put you in the cleft of the rock, and I will screen you with my palm until I have passed by.

[23] And I will remove my palm, and you shall see my back, and my face shall not be seen."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"Depart, and go up hence" → "Go, go up from here"** (v1) — Hebrew לֵךְ עֲלֵה, two imperatives. **"seed" kept** — Hebrew זֶרַע.
- **"an angel" kept** (v2) — Hebrew מַלְאָךְ, without the article; the word is "messenger" (32:34 "my angel").
- **The six peoples** (v2) — each is a singular with the article ("the Canaanite"); the Hebrew has "and" only before the third, fourth and sixth name. The Septuagint adds "the Girgashite" (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"Unto a land flowing with milk and honey"** (v3) — the words carry on from "to the land" in v1; the Hebrew has no second "go up" here.
- **"for I will not go up in the midst of thee" kept** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי "for" twice. **"lest I consume thee" → "lest I make an end of you"** (v3, 5) — Hebrew כִּלָּה "finish, bring to an end" (32:10, 12).
- **"a stiffnecked people" kept** (v3, 5) — Hebrew עַם־קְשֵׁה־עֹרֶף, literally "a people hard of neck".
- **"these evil tidings" → "this evil word"** (v4) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר הָרָע הַזֶּה; רַע is "evil" (bad, grievous for them). **"no man did put on him his ornaments" → "no man set his ornaments upon himself"** — Hebrew שִׁית "set"; עֲדִי "ornaments" is a collective singular.
- **"For the LORD had said" → "And Yahweh said"** (v5) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר, the ordinary narrative form; it does not say "had".
- **"I will come up into the midst of thee in a moment, and consume thee" → "If for one moment I went up in your midst, I would make an end of you"** (v5) — Hebrew רֶגַע אֶחָד אֶעֱלֶה בְקִרְבְּךָ וְכִלִּיתִיךָ, two clauses side by side without "if": "one moment I go up in your midst, and I have made an end of you". "You" changes from plural ("You are") to singular in the Hebrew.
- **"put off thy ornaments from thee" → "take your ornaments down from upon you"** (v5) — Hebrew הוֹרֵד, literally "bring down". **"that I may know what to do unto thee" → "that I may know what I shall do to you"** — Hebrew וְאֵדְעָה מָה אֶעֱשֶׂה־לָּךְ.
- **"stripped themselves of their ornaments by the mount Horeb" → "stripped themselves of their ornaments, from Mount Horeb"** (v6) — Hebrew מֵהַר חוֹרֵב "from Mount Horeb": from Horeb on, or at a distance from it. "Onward" is not in the Hebrew. The verb is the "strip" of 3:22; 12:36, in the reflexive stem.
- **"took the tabernacle, and pitched it" → "would take the tent and pitch it for himself"** (v7–11) — Hebrew הָאֹהֶל "the tent" (not מִשְׁכָּן "dwelling", which is not yet made). The verbs of v7–11 are forms of repeated action: what Moses and the people used to do. וְנָטָה־לוֹ is "pitch it for himself", or "pitch it for him / for it"; who is meant by לוֹ is open.
- **"called it the Tabernacle of the congregation" → "would call it the tent of meeting"** (v7) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד, the name of the tent of chapters 25–31. **"every one which sought the LORD" → "everyone seeking Yahweh"** — Hebrew מְבַקֵּשׁ, a participle.
- **"and the LORD talked with Moses" → "and speak with Moses"** (v9) — the Hebrew names no subject: וְדִבֶּר עִם־מֹשֶׁה "and he / it spoke with Moses", after "the pillar of cloud would come down and stand". "Yahweh" is named in v11.
- **"worshipped" → "bow down"** (v10) — Hebrew הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה.
- **"face to face" kept** (v11) — Hebrew פָּנִים אֶל־פָּנִים; the same word פָּנִים as in v14, 15, 20, 23. **"as a man speaketh unto his friend" → "as a man speaks to his neighbor"** — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶל־רֵעֵהוּ; רֵעַ is the fellow man, friend, neighbor.
- **"And he turned again" → "And he would return"** (v11) — the Hebrew does not name Moses here. **"his servant Joshua" → "his minister Joshua"** — Hebrew מְשָׁרְתוֹ, the one who ministers to him (שֵׁרֵת), not עֶבֶד "slave, servant". **"a young man"** — Hebrew נַעַר, "boy, young man, attendant". **"departed not out of the tabernacle" → "would not depart from the midst of the tent"** — Hebrew מִתּוֹךְ.
- **"thou hast not let me know" → "you have not made known to me"** (v12, 13) — Hebrew הוֹדִיעַ, the causative of "know": the verb "know" stands six times in v12–17. In v12 the pronoun "you" (אַתָּה) is expressed three times.
- **"I know thee by name" kept** (v12, 17) — Hebrew יְדַעְתִּיךָ בְשֵׁם, a perfect: "I have known you by name".
- **"found grace in my sight" → "found favor in my eyes"** (v12, 13, 16, 17) — Hebrew מָצָא חֵן בְּעֵינֵי. חֵן is "favor"; its verb stands in v19.
- **"shew me now thy way" → "make known to me, please, your way"** (v13) — Hebrew אֶת־דְּרָכֶךָ, singular as written and pointed; many versions have "ways". נָא "please" stands twice in the verse. **"that I may find grace" → "in order that I may find favor"** — Hebrew לְמַעַן אֶמְצָא; no word "continue".
- **"consider that this nation is thy people" → "see that this nation is your people"** (v13) — Hebrew וּרְאֵה, the verb of "See" in v12; גּוֹי "nation", עַם "people".
- **"My presence shall go with thee" → "My face shall go"** (v14) — Hebrew פָּנַי יֵלֵכוּ, two words: "my face shall go". פָּנִים is "face"; "presence" is an interpretation, and "with you" is not in the Hebrew. The same word is in "face to face" (v11) and in "you cannot see my face" (v20). **"I will give thee rest" kept** — Hebrew וַהֲנִחֹתִי לָךְ, from נוּחַ "rest".
- **"If thy presence go not with me, carry us not up hence" → "If your face is not going, do not bring us up from here"** (v15) — Hebrew אִם־אֵין פָּנֶיךָ הֹלְכִים; "with me" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"so shall we be separated" → "so that we are distinguished"** (v16) — Hebrew וְנִפְלֵינוּ, "and we shall be distinguished, marked out" (the verb of 8:22; 9:4; 11:7). **"from all the people that are upon the face of the earth" → "from every people that is on the face of the ground"** — Hebrew הָעָם singular; אֲדָמָה "ground".
- **"I will do this thing also that thou hast spoken" → "This word also that you have spoken I will do"** (v17) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר … אֲשֶׁר דִּבַּרְתָּ, noun and verb of one root.
- **"I beseech thee, shew me thy glory" → "Show me, please, your glory"** (v18) — Hebrew הַרְאֵנִי נָא אֶת־כְּבֹדֶךָ; כָּבוֹד "glory" (weight, splendor).
- **"I will make all my goodness pass before thee" → "I myself will make all my goodness pass by before your face"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲנִי אַעֲבִיר כָּל־טוּבִי עַל־פָּנֶיךָ: the pronoun "I" is expressed; עַל־פָּנֶיךָ is literally "over / before your face". The verb is the "pass by" of v22 and 34:6.
- **"I will proclaim the name of the LORD before thee" → "I will call out the name of Yahweh before you"** (v19) — Hebrew וְקָרָאתִי בְשֵׁם יְהוָה, the same words as "call on the name of Yahweh" (Gen 4:26; 12:8), literally "call with / in the name of Yahweh". Here Yahweh himself is the one who calls, so "call out" (34:5, 6).
- **"will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and will shew mercy on whom I will shew mercy" → "I will show favor to whom I will show favor, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion"** (v19) — Hebrew וְחַנֹּתִי אֶת־אֲשֶׁר אָחֹן וְרִחַמְתִּי אֶת־אֲשֶׁר אֲרַחֵם. Each verb stands twice and is rendered the same both times. חָנַן is the verb of חֵן "favor" (v12, 13, 16, 17): Moses has "found favor", and Yahweh shows favor to whom he will. רִחַם is the verb of רַחֲמִים "compassion" (from רֶחֶם "womb"). The form of the sentence is that of 3:14 ("I will be who I will be"). Rom 9:15 cites the verse.
- **"Thou canst not see my face: for there shall no man see me, and live" → "You cannot see my face, for man shall not see me and live"** (v20) — Hebrew הָאָדָם "man, mankind".
- **"there is a place by me" → "a place by me"** (v21) — Hebrew הִנֵּה מָקוֹם אִתִּי, without a verb. **"upon a rock" → "on the rock"** — the Hebrew has the article.
- **"in a clift of the rock" → "in the cleft of the rock"** (v22) — Hebrew נִקְרַת הַצּוּר. **"will cover thee with my hand" → "I will screen you with my palm"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew וְשַׂכֹּתִי כַפִּי עָלֶיךָ, literally "I will screen my palm over you"; כַּף is the hollow of the hand, not יָד "hand".
- **"my back parts" → "my back"** (v23) — Hebrew אֲחֹרָי, a plural noun from אָחוֹר "back, hinder side": "my hinder parts", what is behind me. It is set against פָּנַי "my face" (the front), which "shall not be seen".

### Notes

- **Theological crisis** — after the golden calf, Yahweh says he will not go up in their midst (v3). Moses' mediation (v12–17) restores Yahweh's promised presence. The tabernacle's purpose (Yahweh dwelling among Israel) is restored.
- **"Face to face" (v11) and "you cannot see my face" (v20)** — both stand in one chapter with the same word פָּנִים. v11 speaks of the manner of speaking ("as a man speaks to his neighbor"), v20 of seeing. The text lets the two stand side by side.
- **Moses' boldness** — "Show me, please, your glory" (v18) is unprecedented. Yahweh grants a modified version: not face but back. The theophany at the cleft of the rock (fulfilled in ch 34) is one of the OT's great mystical moments.
- **"Man shall not see me and live" (v20)** — yet we had "they saw the *Elohim* of Israel and ate and drank" (24:10–11). The tension is preserved in the text.
