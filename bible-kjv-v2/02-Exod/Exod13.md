# Exodus 13

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[2] "Consecrate to me every firstborn — whatever opens the womb among the children of Israel, whether of man or beast; it is mine."

[3] Moses said to the people, "Remember this day in which you came out from Egypt, from the house of slavery, for by strength of hand Yahweh brought you out from this place; no leavened bread shall be eaten.

[4] Today you are going out, in the month of Aviv.

[5] When Yahweh brings you into the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, which he swore to your fathers to give you, a land flowing with milk and honey, you shall perform this service in this month.

[6] Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day there shall be a festival to Yahweh.

[7] Unleavened bread shall be eaten for seven days; no leavened bread shall be seen with you, and no leaven shall be seen with you in all your territory.

[8] And you shall tell your son on that day, saying, 'This is because of what Yahweh did for me when I came out of Egypt.'

[9] It shall be for you a sign upon your hand and a memorial between your eyes, so that the instruction of Yahweh may be in your mouth; for with a strong hand Yahweh brought you out of Egypt.

[10] You shall therefore keep this statute at its appointed time from year to year.

[11] When Yahweh brings you into the land of the Canaanites, as he swore to you and your fathers, and gives it to you,

[12] you shall dedicate to Yahweh every firstling of the womb — every firstborn of your livestock that is a male shall be Yahweh's.

[13] Every firstborn of a donkey you shall redeem with a lamb; and if you will not redeem it, you shall break its neck. And every firstborn human male among your sons you shall redeem.

[14] When your son asks you in time to come, 'What is this?', you shall say to him, 'By strength of hand Yahweh brought us out from Egypt, from the house of slavery.

[15] When Pharaoh stubbornly refused to let us go, Yahweh killed every firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of man to the firstborn of beast. Therefore I sacrifice to Yahweh every firstling of the womb that is a male, but every firstborn of my sons I redeem.'

[16] It shall be for a sign upon your hand and for frontlets between your eyes; for by strength of hand Yahweh brought us out of Egypt."

[17] When Pharaoh let the people go, *Elohim* did not lead them by way of the land of the Philistines, though that was near; for *Elohim* said, "Lest the people change their mind when they see war, and return to Egypt."

[18] So *Elohim* led the people around by way of the wilderness toward the Sea of Reeds; and the children of Israel went up in battle array from the land of Egypt.

[19] Moses took the bones of Joseph with him, for Joseph had strictly sworn the children of Israel, saying, "*Elohim* will surely visit you; and you shall carry up my bones from here with you."

[20] They set out from Succoth and encamped at Etham, on the edge of the wilderness.

[21] Yahweh went before them by day in a pillar of cloud to lead them on the way, and by night in a pillar of fire to give them light, so that they could travel by day and by night.

[22] The pillar of cloud by day and the pillar of fire by night did not depart from before the people.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"Sanctify" → "Consecrate"** (v2) — Hebrew קַדֶּשׁ־לִי *qaddesh-li* = set apart for me / declare holy to me. "Sanctify" accurate but archaic.
- **"openeth the womb" / "openeth the matrix"** (v2, 12, 15) — Hebrew פֶּטֶר רֶחֶם *peter rechem*. *Peter* = opener / firstborn; *rechem* = womb. The first to "break open" the womb.
- **"Abib" → "Aviv"** (v4) — Hebrew אָבִיב *Aviv* = new grain / spring (March–April). The first month of the Hebrew religious calendar, renamed *Nisan* in post-exilic period.
- **"law" → "instruction"** (v9) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה *torah* = instruction / teaching / guidance. "Law" is a narrow subset of Torah's meaning; the full sense is divine instruction, not merely statutes.
- **"frontlets between your eyes" (v16)** — Hebrew לְטוֹטָפֹת בֵּין עֵינֶיךָ *le-totaphot beyn enekha*. Rabbinic tradition reads this plus v9 as the basis for tefillin (phylacteries) — small scripture-boxes strapped to forehead and arm. The original sense may be metaphorical ("always before your mind"); rabbinic practice literalized it.
- **"the way of the land of the Philistines" (v17)** — the coastal route (*Via Maris* / Way of Horus) that led directly northward — heavily fortified with Egyptian garrisons and populated with Philistine-era peoples. Yahweh deliberately avoids it for Israel's sake.
- **"harnessed" → "in battle array"** (v18) — Hebrew חֲמֻשִׁים *chamushim*. Obscure: possibly "fifty / in five-units" / "in battle formation" / "armed." Military-organized departure, not panic flight.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v18) — as in 10:19.
- **"pillar of cloud… pillar of fire" (v21–22)** — likely a single theophanic presence with two modes (day / night). Classical divine-guidance imagery, echoed later in the wilderness traditions (Num 9:15–23) and the temple dedication (1 Kgs 8:10–11). Accompanies Israel throughout the wilderness until they enter Canaan.

### Notes

- **Firstborn redemption** — Yahweh's claim on every firstborn is both commemorative (remembering the Egyptian firstborn) and theological (firstborn status as token of the whole). Human firstborn redeemed (not sacrificed); unclean-animal firstborn (donkey) redeemed or killed (not offered); clean-animal firstborn given to Yahweh.
- **Memory-shaping language** — the chapter is heavy with memorial / catechetical language: "remember this day" (v3), "tell your son" (v8, 14), "for a sign upon your hand" (v9, 16). The exodus is to become a perpetual identity-shaping memory.
- **Divine presence** — the chapter transitions from deliverance (Passover) to journey (wilderness), and the pillar-theophany opens Israel's relationship with Yahweh as ever-present traveling-companion.
