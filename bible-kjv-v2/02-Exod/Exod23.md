# Exodus 23

[1] "You shall not lift up an empty report. Do not put your hand with a wicked man to be a witness of violence.

[2] You shall not be after many for evil, and you shall not answer in a case so as to turn after many, to turn aside.

[3] And to a poor man you shall not show deference in his case.

[4] When you come upon the ox of your enemy or his donkey wandering, you shall surely return it to him.

[5] When you see the donkey of him who hates you lying under its burden, you shall cease from leaving it to him; you shall surely release it with him.

[6] You shall not turn aside the judgment of your needy one in his case.

[7] From a word of falsehood keep far, and the innocent and the righteous do not kill, for I will not justify the wicked.

[8] And a bribe you shall not take, for the bribe blinds the open-eyed and overturns the words of the righteous.

[9] And a sojourner you shall not oppress; and you, you know the soul of the sojourner, for you were sojourners in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[10] And six years you shall sow your land and gather its yield,

[11] but the seventh you shall let it drop and abandon it, and the needy of your people shall eat, and what they leave the living things of the field shall eat. So you shall do with your vineyard, with your olive trees.

[12] Six days you shall do your works, and on the seventh day you shall cease, so that your ox and your donkey may rest, and the son of your slave woman and the sojourner may be refreshed.

[13] And in all that I have said to you, keep yourselves; and the name of other gods you shall not mention; it shall not be heard on your mouth.

[14] Three times in the year you shall hold a feast to me.

[15] The Feast of ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧ you shall keep: seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, as I commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of ⟦abib|Aviv|Abib⟧, for in it you came out from ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧. And they shall not appear before my face empty.

[16] And the Feast of the Harvest, the firstfruits of your works, of what you sow in the field; and the Feast of the Ingathering at the going out of the year, when you gather your works from the field.

[17] Three times in the year all your males shall appear before the face of ⟦adonai-yhwh|the Lord Yahweh|the Lord GOD⟧.

[18] You shall not sacrifice the blood of my sacrifice with what is leavened, and the fat of my feast shall not lodge until morning.

[19] The first of the firstfruits of your ground you shall bring to the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. You shall not boil a kid in its mother's milk.

[20] Behold, I am sending an angel before you to keep you on the way and to bring you to the place that I have prepared.

[21] Keep yourself before him and listen to his voice; do not rebel against him, for he will not forgive your transgression, for my name is within him.

[22] But if you surely listen to his voice and do all that I speak, then I will be an enemy to your enemies and an adversary to your adversaries.

[23] For my angel shall go before you and bring you to the ⟦amorites|Emori|Amorite⟧ and the ⟦hittites|Chitti|Hittite⟧ and the ⟦perizzites|Perizzi|Perizzite⟧ and the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧, the ⟦hivites|Chivvi|Hivite⟧ and the ⟦jebusites|Yevusi|Jebusite⟧, and I will annihilate him.

[24] You shall not bow down to their gods, and you shall not serve them, and you shall not do according to their works; but you shall surely throw them down and surely break in pieces their pillars.

[25] And you shall serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and he will bless your bread and your water, and I will remove sickness from your midst.

[26] There shall be none miscarrying or barren in your land; the number of your days I will fill.

[27] My dread I will send before you, and I will throw into confusion all the people among whom you come, and I will make all your enemies turn their neck to you.

[28] And I will send the hornet before you, and it shall drive out the ⟦hivite|Chivvi|Hivite⟧, the ⟦canaanites|Kenaani|Canaanite⟧ and the ⟦hittite|Chitti|Hittite⟧ from before you.

[29] I will not drive him out from before you in one year, lest the land become a desolation and the living things of the field multiply against you.

[30] Little by little I will drive him out from before you, until you are fruitful and inherit the land.

[31] And I will set your border from the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧ to the sea of the ⟦philistines|Pelishtim|Philistines⟧, and from the wilderness to the River; for I will give into your hand those who dwell in the land, and you shall drive them out from before you.

[32] You shall not cut a covenant with them or with their gods.

[33] They shall not dwell in your land, lest they make you sin against me; for if you serve their gods, surely it will be a snare to you."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"; "God" → "*Elohim*"** (v17, 19, 25) — Hebrew יְהוָה, אֱלֹהִים. "Their gods", "other gods" (v13, 24, 32, 33) is the same word אֱלֹהִים, of the gods of the nations.
- **"Thou shalt not raise a false report" → "You shall not lift up an empty report"** (v1) — Hebrew לֹא תִשָּׂא שֵׁמַע שָׁוְא: נָשָׂא "lift up, take up, carry" (to take it up on the lips, or to accept it); שָׁוְא is what is empty, worthless or false (the word of 20:7; Deut 5:20). **"put not thine hand with the wicked to be an unrighteous witness" → "Do not put your hand with a wicked man to be a witness of violence"** — Hebrew עֵד חָמָס: a witness whose word does violence.
- **"Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil; neither shalt thou speak in a cause to decline after many to wrest judgment" → "You shall not be after many for evil, and you shall not answer in a case so as to turn after many, to turn aside"** (v2) — Hebrew לֹא־תִהְיֶה אַחֲרֵי־רַבִּים לְרָעֹת, literally "you shall not be after many for evils"; עָנָה עַל־רִב "answer (testify) in a case". The end plays on one verb: לִנְטֹת "to turn" … לְהַטֹּת "to make turn, to turn aside". The last word has no object; "judgment" (v6) is understood. רַבִּים is "many" (also "the great").
- **"countenance a poor man in his cause" → "show deference to a poor man in his case"** (v3) — Hebrew הָדַר "adorn, honor, defer to" (Lev 19:15, 32; another word than כִּבֵּד "honor", 20:12); דָּל "poor", a different word from "needy" (אֶבְיוֹן, v6, 11). **"cause" → "case"** (v2, 3, 6) — Hebrew רִיב.
- **"If thou meet thine enemy's ox or his ass going astray" → "When you come upon the ox of your enemy or his donkey wandering"** (v4) — Hebrew פָּגַע "come upon", תֹּעֶה "wandering". **"surely return"** — infinitive absolute with its verb, הָשֵׁב תְּשִׁיבֶנּוּ, literally "returning you shall return it"; the same form in v5 (עָזֹב תַּעֲזֹב), v22 (שָׁמֹעַ תִּשְׁמַע), v24 (הָרֵס תְּהָרְסֵם, שַׁבֵּר תְּשַׁבֵּר).
- **"and wouldest forbear to help him, thou shalt surely help with him" → "you shall cease from leaving it to him; you shall surely release it with him"** (v5) — a hard verse. Hebrew וְחָדַלְתָּ מֵעֲזֹב לוֹ עָזֹב תַּעֲזֹב עִמּוֹ: the verb עָזַב, three times, is "leave, forsake", and also "let loose" (so here of freeing the animal from its load, or the load from the animal). The first clause can be the end of the condition ("and you would refrain from leaving / loosing it for him") or the beginning of the command, as rendered. Some take עָזַב here as a second verb, "arrange, help". "It" is supplied twice. רֹבֵץ is "lying" stretched out.
- **"wrest the judgment of thy poor" → "turn aside the judgment of your needy one"** (v6) — Hebrew הִטָּה מִשְׁפָּט, "turn aside (pervert) judgment".
- **"a false matter" → "a word of falsehood"** (v7) — Hebrew דְּבַר־שֶׁקֶר; שֶׁקֶר "falsehood" is another word than שָׁוְא (v1). **"I will not justify the wicked" kept** — Hebrew לֹא־אַצְדִּיק רָשָׁע: הִצְדִּיק is "declare righteous", the opposite of "condemn" (22:9). The Septuagint has a command, "you shall not justify the wicked for a gift" (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"gift" → "bribe"; "the wise" → "the open-eyed"; "perverteth" → "overturns"** (v8) — Hebrew שֹׁחַד; פִּקְחִים "those whose eyes are open"; סִלֵּף "twist, overturn". "The words of the righteous": their words, or their cause.
- **"ye know the heart of a stranger" → "you, you know the soul of the sojourner"** (v9) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ הַגֵּר; the pronoun "you" is added in the Hebrew for weight.
- **"fruits" → "yield"** (v10) — Hebrew תְּבוּאָה. **"thou shalt let it rest and lie still" → "you shall let it drop and abandon it"** (v11) — Hebrew שָׁמַט "let drop, release" (the root of the "release" of Deut 15:1) and נָטַשׁ "leave to itself". "Rest" is not in either word. Whether "it" is the land or its yield is not said. **"the beasts of the field" → "the living things of the field"** (v11, 29) — Hebrew חַיַּת הַשָּׂדֶה. **"oliveyard" → "olive trees"** — Hebrew זַיִת, singular collective; the two nouns stand without "and".
- **"thy work" → "your works"; "thou shalt rest" → "you shall cease"** (v12) — Hebrew מַעֲשֶׂיךָ, plural, and שָׁבַת "cease" (the verb of "Sabbath"); "rest" in the next clause is another verb, נוּחַ. **"may be refreshed" kept** — Hebrew וְיִנָּפֵשׁ, a verb made from נֶפֶשׁ "soul": take breath.
- **"be circumspect" → "keep yourselves"** (v13) — Hebrew תִּשָּׁמֵרוּ, niphal of שָׁמַר "keep": be on your guard. **"make no mention" → "you shall not mention"** — Hebrew הִזְכִּיר "cause to be remembered". The verse changes from "you" plural to "your mouth" singular.
- **"Three times thou shalt keep a feast unto me in the year"** (v14, 17) — two words for "times": רְגָלִים, literally "feet" (v14), and פְּעָמִים, literally "steps, beats" (v17). חָגַג is "hold a feast", the pilgrim feast (חַג, v15, 16, 18).
- **"none shall appear before me empty" → "they shall not appear before my face empty"** (v15) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יֵרָאוּ פָנַי רֵיקָם, plural, as pointed a niphal: "they shall not be seen [at] my face empty-handed". The consonants can also be read as the qal, "they shall not see my face empty". So also v17: יֵרָאֶה … אֶל־פְּנֵי "shall be seen toward the face of".
- **"the feast of harvest … the feast of ingathering, which is in the end of the year" → "the Feast of the Harvest … the Feast of the Ingathering at the going out of the year"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּצֵאת הַשָּׁנָה. **"thy labours" → "your works"** — Hebrew מַעֲשֶׂיךָ, as v12.
- **"before the Lord GOD" → "before the face of the Lord Yahweh"** (v17) — Hebrew אֶל־פְּנֵי הָאָדֹן יְהוָה. The first word is הָאָדוֹן "the lord, the master", with the article; it is not אֲדֹנָי (*Adonai*). **"all thy males"** — Hebrew כָּל־זְכוּרְךָ.
- **"Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leavened bread; neither shall the fat of my sacrifice remain" → "You shall not sacrifice the blood of my sacrifice with what is leavened, and the fat of my feast shall not lodge"** (v18) — Hebrew זָבַח … זֶבַח, verb and noun of one root; עַל־חָמֵץ "upon, beside what is leavened"; חֵלֶב־חַגִּי "the fat of my feast" (חַג, KJV "sacrifice"); לִין "lodge, stay the night".
- **"The first of the firstfruits of thy land" → "… of your ground"** (v19) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית בִּכּוּרֵי אַדְמָתְךָ; רֵאשִׁית is the first part (also the best). **"seethe a kid in his mother's milk" → "boil a kid in its mother's milk"** — Hebrew גְּדִי, a young goat. The same words in 34:26; Deut 14:21. The later Jewish rule of keeping meat and milk apart rests on them; why it was forbidden the verse does not say.
- **"an Angel" → "an angel"; "mine Angel" → "my angel"** (v20, 23) — Hebrew מַלְאָךְ, the one word for "messenger" and "angel". The one sent here bears the name of Yahweh within him and can forgive or not forgive (v21); so "angel" is kept, without a capital. "I am sending" is a participle.
- **"Beware of him, and obey his voice, provoke him not" → "Keep yourself before him and listen to his voice; do not rebel against him"** (v21) — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֶר מִפָּנָיו, literally "be on guard from his face"; שְׁמַע בְּקֹלוֹ "listen to his voice". אַל־תַּמֵּר בּוֹ is usually taken from מָרָה "rebel"; as pointed the form looks like one of מָרַר "be bitter" ("do not embitter him"). **"pardon your transgressions" → "forgive your transgression"** — Hebrew יִשָּׂא לְפִשְׁעֲכֶם, literally "lift up for your transgression"; "your" is plural here, singular in the rest of the verse. **"my name is in him" → "my name is within him"** — Hebrew בְּקִרְבּוֹ "in his inward part".
- **"an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries" kept** (v22) — Hebrew verbs made from the nouns: וְאָיַבְתִּי אֶת־אֹיְבֶיךָ וְצַרְתִּי אֶת־צֹרְרֶיךָ, "I will be enemy to your enemies and press those who press you".
- **"the Amorites, and the Hittites …" → "the Amorite and the Hittite …"; "I will cut them off" → "I will annihilate him"** (v23, 28, 29, 30) — the Hebrew names are singular, each people as one, and the pronouns follow: "him" (v23, 29, 30). הִכְחִיד is "make disappear, annihilate".
- **"thou shalt utterly overthrow them, and quite break down their images" → "you shall surely throw them down and surely break in pieces their pillars"** (v24) — Hebrew הָרַס "throw down", שִׁבֵּר "break in pieces" (piel of "break"), מַצֵּבֹת "pillars", standing stones (Gen 28:18; in 24:4 Moses himself sets up twelve). "Them" is the gods, or the nations.
- **"ye shall serve … he shall bless thy bread … I will take sickness away" → "you shall serve … he will bless your bread … I will remove sickness"** (v25) — the Hebrew changes from "you" plural to "your" singular, and from "he will bless" to "I will remove" (הֵסִיר); kept. The Septuagint has "I will bless" (cited from memory).
- **"There shall nothing cast their young, nor be barren" → "There shall be none miscarrying or barren"** (v26) — Hebrew מְשַׁכֵּלָה וַעֲקָרָה, two feminine words: "one that loses her young" (שִׁכֵּל "bereave") and "a barren one"; of women, or of beasts as well. **"I will fulfil" → "I will fill"** — Hebrew אֲמַלֵּא.
- **"my fear" → "My dread"; "destroy" → "throw into confusion"; "make all thine enemies turn their backs unto thee" → "make all your enemies turn their neck to you"** (v27) — Hebrew אֵימָה "dread"; הָמַם "confuse, rout"; וְנָתַתִּי אֶת־כָּל־אֹיְבֶיךָ אֵלֶיךָ עֹרֶף, literally "I will give all your enemies to you [as] a neck": they flee. "All the people" is singular, הָעָם.
- **"hornets" → "the hornet"** (v28) — Hebrew הַצִּרְעָה, singular with the article, found only here and in Deut 7:20; Josh 24:12. "Hornet" is the old understanding; others take the word as "dread, dejection". The meaning is uncertain.
- **"the beast of the field multiply against thee"** (v29) — Hebrew רַבָּה עָלֶיךָ "become many upon you". **"By little and little" → "Little by little"** (v30) — Hebrew מְעַט מְעַט. **"until thou be increased, and inherit the land" → "until you are fruitful and inherit the land"** — Hebrew פָּרָה "be fruitful", נָחַל "inherit".
- **"thy bounds" → "your border"** (v31) — Hebrew גְּבֻל, singular. **"the Red sea" → "the Sea of Reeds"** — Hebrew יַם־סוּף. "The sea of the Philistines" is the Mediterranean; "the River" (הַנָּהָר) is the Euphrates. **"the inhabitants of the land" → "those who dwell in the land"** — Hebrew יֹשְׁבֵי; "your hand" is plural here, "you shall drive them out" singular.
- **"make no covenant" → "not cut a covenant"** (v32) — Hebrew כָּרַת בְּרִית.
- **"for if thou serve their gods, it will surely be a snare unto thee" → "for if you serve their gods, surely it will be a snare to you"** (v33) — the Hebrew has כִּי twice: כִּי תַעֲבֹד … כִּי־יִהְיֶה, "for you will serve their gods; for it will be a snare to you", or as rendered, the first "if / when" and the second "surely".

### Notes

- **The end of the Covenant Code** — justice in court (v1–9), the seventh year and the seventh day (v10–12), the three feasts (v14–19), and the promise and warning for the way to the land (v20–33). Ch 24 follows with the making of the covenant.
- **The three feasts (v14–17)** — Unleavened Bread (with the Passover, ch 12–13), the Harvest (called the Feast of Weeks in 34:22), and the Ingathering (the Feast of Booths, Lev 23:34; Deut 16:13).
- **"Little by little" (v29–30)** — cf. Deut 7:22; Judg 1.
