# Exodus 23

[1] "You shall not spread a false report. Do not join hands with the wicked to be a malicious witness.

[2] You shall not follow a majority to do evil; nor shall you testify in a dispute so as to side with a majority and pervert justice.

[3] You shall not be partial to a poor man in his dispute.

[4] If you come upon your enemy's ox or his donkey going astray, you shall surely bring it back to him.

[5] If you see the donkey of one who hates you lying under its burden, and you would refrain from helping him — you must surely help him with it.

[6] You shall not pervert the justice due to your poor in his dispute.

[7] Keep far from a false charge; do not kill the innocent and the righteous, for I will not acquit the wicked.

[8] You shall take no bribe, for a bribe blinds those who see and subverts the cause of the righteous.

[9] You shall not oppress a resident alien; you yourselves know the life of a resident alien, for you were resident aliens in the land of Egypt.

[10] For six years you shall sow your land and gather its produce;

[11] but on the seventh you shall let it rest and lie fallow, so that the poor of your people may eat; and what they leave the wild animals may eat. You shall do the same with your vineyard and your olive orchard.

[12] Six days you shall do your work, but on the seventh day you shall rest, that your ox and your donkey may rest, and the son of your female slave and the resident alien may be refreshed.

[13] Pay attention to all that I have said to you; make no mention of the names of other gods, nor let them be heard out of your mouth.

[14] Three times a year you shall hold a festival to me.

[15] You shall keep the Feast of Unleavened Bread. (Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread, as I commanded you, at the appointed time of the month of Aviv; for in it you came out of Egypt. No one shall appear before me empty-handed.)

[16] And the Feast of Harvest, the firstfruits of your labor, of what you sow in the field. And the Feast of Ingathering at the end of the year, when you gather in your labor from the field.

[17] Three times in the year all your males shall appear before Lord Yahweh.

[18] You shall not offer the blood of my sacrifice with anything leavened; nor shall the fat of my feast remain until morning.

[19] The first of the firstfruits of your land you shall bring into the house of Yahweh your *Elohim*. You shall not boil a young goat in its mother's milk.

[20] Behold, I am sending a messenger before you to guard you on the way and to bring you to the place that I have prepared.

[21] Pay heed to him and listen to his voice; do not rebel against him, for he will not pardon your transgression, since my name is in him.

[22] But if you will indeed listen to his voice and do all that I say, then I will be an enemy to your enemies and an adversary to your adversaries.

[23] For my messenger shall go before you and bring you in to the Amorites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites, and I will wipe them out.

[24] You shall not bow down to their gods or serve them, nor shall you do after their works; but you shall utterly tear them down and smash their sacred pillars to pieces.

[25] You shall serve Yahweh your *Elohim*, and he shall bless your bread and your water. And I will take sickness away from your midst.

[26] None shall miscarry or be barren in your land; the number of your days I will make full.

[27] I will send my terror before you, and will throw into confusion all the peoples to whom you come; and I will make all your enemies turn their backs to you.

[28] And I will send the hornet before you, which shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite from before you.

[29] I will not drive them out before you in one year, lest the land become desolate and the wild animals multiply against you.

[30] Little by little I will drive them out from before you, until you have increased and taken possession of the land.

[31] I will set your borders from the Sea of Reeds to the Sea of the Philistines, and from the wilderness to the River; for I will give into your hand the inhabitants of the land, and you shall drive them out before you.

[32] You shall make no covenant with them or with their gods.

[33] They shall not dwell in your land, lest they make you sin against me; for if you serve their gods, it will surely be a snare to you."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"countenance a poor man in his cause" → "be partial to a poor man in his dispute"** (v3) — Hebrew וְדָל לֹא תֶהְדַּר בְּרִיבוֹ *we-dal lo tehdar be-rivo*. *Hadar* = honor / give preference to. Even the poor should not receive unjust favoritism — impartial justice cuts both ways.
- **"gift" → "bribe"** (v8) — Hebrew שֹׁחַד *shochad*. Specifically a bribe (not a generic gift).
- **"seethe a kid in his mother's milk" → "boil a young goat in its mother's milk"** (v19) — Hebrew לֹא־תְבַשֵּׁל גְּדִי בַּחֲלֵב אִמּוֹ *lo-tevashel gedi ba-chalev immo*. Repeated in 34:26 and Deut 14:21. Became the foundation of Jewish kashrut separation of meat and dairy. Historical-cultural origin possibly a polemic against a Canaanite fertility ritual; exact origin uncertain.
- **"Angel" → "messenger"** (v20, 23) — as elsewhere.
- **"my name is in him" (v21)** — Hebrew כִּי שְׁמִי בְּקִרְבּוֹ *ki shemi be-qirbo*. The messenger carries Yahweh's name / presence / authority. Read typologically by some Christian interpreters as proto-christological.
- **"images" → "sacred pillars"** (v24) — Hebrew מַצֵּבֹתֵיהֶם *matsevotehem*. *Matsevah* = standing stone / pillar / stele (see Gen 28:18). Canaanite cult pillars.
- **"cast their young" → "miscarry"** (v26) — Hebrew מְשַׁכֵּלָה *meshakkelah*. Modernized.
- **"hornets" → "the hornet"** (v28) — Hebrew צִרְעָה *tsir'ah*. Could be:
  1. Literal hornet / wasp (traditional).
  2. Metaphorical "terror / panic."
  3. "Depression / discouragement."
  Preserved as "hornet" per tradition; the metaphorical sense (divine-sent dread) fits context.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v31) — as throughout.
- **"Sea of the Philistines" (v31)** — the Mediterranean. "The River" (v31) = the Euphrates. The idealized boundaries of Israel's inheritance.

### Notes

- **Covenant Code's conclusion** — ch 23 ends the Sinaitic legal code, followed by the covenant-ratification ceremony of ch 24. Themes: judicial integrity, sabbath practices, pilgrimage festivals, land-warfare instructions.
- **Three pilgrimage festivals (v14–17)** — *Pesach* (Passover / Unleavened Bread), *Shavuot* (Harvest / Weeks / Pentecost), *Sukkot* (Ingathering / Booths / Tabernacles). The triple agricultural-historical cycle that will structure Israel's liturgical year.
- **"Little by little" conquest (v29–30)** — Yahweh's measured plan: gradual rather than sudden displacement of the Canaanite populations, to preserve the ecological viability of the land. Historically reflected in the slow, uneven conquest-settlement described in Joshua and Judges.
