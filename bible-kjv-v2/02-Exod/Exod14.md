# Exodus 14

[1] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[2] "Speak to the children of Israel, that they turn back and encamp before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, opposite Baal-Zephon; you shall encamp beside the sea, opposite it.

[3] For Pharaoh will say of the children of Israel, 'They are wandering in confusion in the land; the wilderness has shut them in.'

[4] And I will harden Pharaoh's heart, and he will pursue them; and I will get glory over Pharaoh and all his host, and the Egyptians shall know that I am Yahweh." And they did so.

[5] When the king of Egypt was told that the people had fled, the heart of Pharaoh and of his servants was turned against the people, and they said, "What is this we have done, that we have let Israel go from serving us?"

[6] So he made ready his chariot and took his people with him.

[7] He took six hundred picked chariots, and all the chariots of Egypt, with officers over all of them.

[8] Yahweh hardened the heart of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and he pursued the children of Israel; and the children of Israel went out with a high hand.

[9] The Egyptians pursued them, with all the horses and chariots of Pharaoh, his cavalry and his army, and overtook them as they encamped by the sea, beside Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-Zephon.

[10] As Pharaoh drew near, the children of Israel lifted up their eyes, and behold, the Egyptians were marching after them. They were very afraid, and the children of Israel cried out to Yahweh.

[11] They said to Moses, "Was it because there were no graves in Egypt that you have taken us away to die in the wilderness? What is this you have done to us, bringing us out of Egypt?

[12] Is this not what we said to you in Egypt, 'Let us alone, that we may serve the Egyptians'? For it would have been better for us to serve the Egyptians than to die in the wilderness."

[13] Moses said to the people, "Do not be afraid. Stand firm and see the salvation of Yahweh, which he will work for you today; for the Egyptians whom you see today, you shall never see again.

[14] Yahweh will fight for you, and you have only to be still."

[15] Yahweh said to Moses, "Why do you cry out to me? Speak to the children of Israel, that they set out.

[16] And you — lift up your staff, and stretch out your hand over the sea, and divide it, that the children of Israel may go into the midst of the sea on dry ground.

[17] And I, behold, I am hardening the hearts of the Egyptians, and they shall go in after them. And I will get glory over Pharaoh and all his host, over his chariots and over his cavalry.

[18] The Egyptians shall know that I am Yahweh, when I have gotten glory over Pharaoh, over his chariots, and over his cavalry."

[19] The messenger of *Elohim* who was going before the camp of Israel moved and went behind them; and the pillar of cloud moved from before them and stood behind them.

[20] It came between the camp of Egypt and the camp of Israel; and there was the cloud and the darkness, and it lit up the night. The one did not come near the other all night.

[21] Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and Yahweh drove the sea back with a strong east wind all that night and turned the sea into dry ground; and the waters were divided.

[22] The children of Israel went into the midst of the sea on dry ground; and the waters were a wall for them on their right and on their left.

[23] The Egyptians pursued and went in after them — all Pharaoh's horses, his chariots, and his cavalry — into the midst of the sea.

[24] In the morning watch, Yahweh looked down upon the host of the Egyptians through the pillar of fire and cloud, and threw the host of the Egyptians into confusion.

[25] He bound the wheels of their chariots so that they drove with difficulty; and the Egyptians said, "Let us flee from the face of Israel, for Yahweh is fighting for them against the Egyptians!"

[26] Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Stretch out your hand over the sea, that the waters may come back upon the Egyptians, upon their chariots and upon their cavalry."

[27] So Moses stretched out his hand over the sea, and the sea returned to its normal flow when the morning appeared; and the Egyptians were fleeing into it, and Yahweh shook the Egyptians off into the midst of the sea.

[28] The waters came back and covered the chariots and the cavalry — all the host of Pharaoh that had gone into the sea after them. Not one of them remained.

[29] But the children of Israel walked on dry ground in the midst of the sea; and the waters were a wall for them on their right and on their left.

[30] Thus Yahweh saved Israel that day out of the hand of the Egyptians; and Israel saw the Egyptians dead on the seashore.

[31] Israel saw the mighty hand that Yahweh had worked against the Egyptians; and the people feared Yahweh and believed in Yahweh and in Moses his servant.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"entangled in the land" → "wandering in confusion in the land"** (v3) — Hebrew נְבֻכִים הֵם בָּאָרֶץ *nevukhim hem ba-aretz*. *Nevukh* = confused / wandering in perplexity. Pharaoh reads Israel's turn-back as disorientation; it is in fact Yahweh's military trap.
- **"I will be honoured" → "I will get glory"** (v4, 17, 18) — Hebrew וְאִכָּבְדָה *we-ikkavedah* from *kaved* = be heavy / glorious. Ironic wordplay with the "heavy / hardened" heart of Pharaoh: the same root describes Yahweh's being-heavied / glorified through Pharaoh's heaviness.
- **"horsemen" → "cavalry"** (v9, 17, 18, 23, 26, 28) — Hebrew פָּרָשָׁיו *parashav*. Chariot-riders / horsemen.
- **"hold your peace" → "be still"** (v14) — Hebrew תַּחֲרִישׁוּן *tacharishun* = be silent / cease from speaking or acting. Contrast Israel's cries (v10) with the command to stillness.
- **"angel" → "messenger"** (v19) — as elsewhere.
- **"it was a cloud and darkness to them, but it gave light by night to these" → "there was the cloud and the darkness, and it lit up the night"** (v20) — Hebrew וַיְהִי הֶעָנָן וְהַחֹשֶׁךְ וַיָּאֶר אֶת־הַלָּיְלָה. Difficult phrase. LXX and some MSS give an expanded reading ("dark to the Egyptians, light to Israel"). MT is compressed. Preserving the brevity.
- **"troubled" → "threw into confusion"** (v24) — Hebrew וַיָּהָם *wa-yaham* = panic / throw into chaos. Divine-sent panic is a feature of Yahweh's holy war.
- **"took off their chariot wheels" → "bound the wheels"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיָּסַר אֵת אֹפַן מַרְכְּבֹתָיו *wa-yasar et ophan markevotav*. MT has "removed / turned aside." LXX and Samaritan: "bound." Either "removed" (chariots immobilized) or "clogged / bound" (wheels stuck). Sam / LXX makes more narrative sense.
- **"returned to his strength" → "returned to its normal flow"** (v27) — Hebrew לְאֵיתָנוֹ *le-etano*. *Ethan* = firm / enduring / constant (name "Ethan" derives from this root). The sea returned to its normal constant state — flowing normally, not the divine-arrested state.
- **"overthrew" → "shook off"** (v27) — Hebrew וַיְנַעֵר *wa-yena'er* = shake off (as a garment shakes off dust). Divine imagery: Yahweh shakes the Egyptians off into the sea.

### Notes

- **Crossing the Sea of Reeds** — the central event of Israel's national memory, celebrated in the Song of the Sea (ch 15), Psalm 78, 106, and many prophetic texts. Literary features: paired east-wind / blowback (v21, 27), pillared theophany, decisive divine intervention after human cries.
- **Historical location** — traditional identification with an arm of the Red Sea proper; modern scholarship favors shallow northern lakes (Bitter Lakes, Lake Menzaleh, or the Reed Marshes) where wind-tide combinations could plausibly expose a passable strip briefly.
- **Structural shift** — v14 ("Yahweh will fight for you, you be still") marks a theological hinge: Israel's role is passive reception of divine action. This will recur throughout the conquest narrative (Joshua 6, Jericho) and in apocalyptic texts.
