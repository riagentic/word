# Exodus 27

[1] "You shall make the altar of acacia wood, five cubits long and five cubits wide — the altar shall be square — and its height shall be three cubits.

[2] You shall make its horns on its four corners; its horns shall be of one piece with it, and you shall overlay it with bronze.

[3] You shall make its pots for removing its ashes, and its shovels, and its basins, and its flesh-hooks, and its fire-pans; all its utensils you shall make of bronze.

[4] You shall make for it a grating, a network of bronze; and on the net you shall make four bronze rings at its four corners.

[5] You shall set it below the ledge of the altar, so that the net reaches halfway up the altar.

[6] You shall make poles for the altar, poles of acacia wood, and overlay them with bronze.

[7] The poles shall be put into the rings, so that the poles are on the two sides of the altar, to carry it.

[8] You shall make it hollow, with planks; as was shown you on the mountain, so shall they make it.

[9] You shall make the court of the dwelling. On the south side southward, there shall be hangings for the court of fine twined linen, a hundred cubits long for one side.

[10] Its pillars shall be twenty, and their sockets of bronze twenty; the hooks of the pillars and their bands shall be of silver.

[11] Likewise for the north side, the length shall be a hundred cubits, with twenty pillars and their twenty sockets of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands of silver.

[12] For the width of the court on the west side shall be hangings of fifty cubits, with ten pillars and their ten sockets.

[13] The width of the court on the east side eastward shall be fifty cubits.

[14] The hangings for one side of the gate shall be fifteen cubits, with three pillars and three sockets.

[15] And on the other side the hangings shall be fifteen cubits, with three pillars and three sockets.

[16] For the gate of the court there shall be a screen twenty cubits long, of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twined linen, embroidered with needlework; it shall have four pillars and four sockets.

[17] All the pillars around the court shall be banded with silver; their hooks shall be of silver, and their sockets of bronze.

[18] The length of the court shall be a hundred cubits, and the width fifty throughout, and the height five cubits, with hangings of fine twined linen and sockets of bronze.

[19] All the utensils of the dwelling for every service, and all its pegs, and all the pegs of the court, shall be of bronze.

[20] You shall command the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ to bring you pure beaten olive oil for the light, to make a lamp burn continually.

[21] In the Tent of Meeting, outside the veil that is before the Testimony, ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and his sons shall tend it from evening until morning before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; it shall be a statute forever throughout their generations on behalf of the children of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"foursquare" → "square"** (v1) — Hebrew רָבוּעַ *ravua*. Modernized.
- **"horns" (v2)** — Hebrew קַרְנֹת *qarnot*. Projections at the four corners of the altar. Grasped for asylum (1 Kgs 1:50); smeared with blood on Yom Kippur (Lev 16:18).
- **"brass" → "bronze"** (v2, 3, 4, 6, 10, 11, 17, 18, 19) — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת, as elsewhere.
- **"fleshhooks" → "flesh-hooks"** (v3) — modernized hyphenation. Forks for handling sacrificial meat.
- **"compass" → "ledge"** (v5) — Hebrew כַּרְכֹּב *karkov*. Mid-height ledge of the altar.
- **"fillets" → "bands"** (v10, 11, 17) — Hebrew חֲשֻׁקִים *chashuqim*. Decorative bands / rods running between pillars.
- **"pins" → "pegs"** (v19) — Hebrew יְתֵדֹת *yetedot*. Tent-pegs. "Pins" is archaic.
- **"tabernacle of the congregation" → "Tent of Meeting"** (v21) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד *ohel mo'ed*. Alternative designation for the tabernacle: "tent of appointed meeting" — where Yahweh meets Moses and Israel. "Congregation" in KJV renders *mo'ed* but is less accurate.

### Notes

- **The bronze altar of sacrifice** — larger than the golden altar of incense (inside). This is the outer-court altar for burnt offerings and other public sacrifices.
- **Sanctuary gradation by material** — bronze (outer court, altar) → silver (pillar bands) → gold (inner furnishings, ark). The metals trace the gradient of increasing holiness as one moves inward.
- **Perpetual lamp** (v20–21) — the lampstand-light must burn continually; Aaron and his sons tend it. This continuous-presence light is echoed in the Second Temple and later Jewish tradition (the *ner tamid* still burns in every synagogue).
