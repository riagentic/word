## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"murrain" → "pestilence"** (v3) — Hebrew דֶּבֶר *dever* = plague / pestilence (primarily livestock disease here; can also apply to humans in other contexts). "Murrain" is archaic English.
- **"ashes of the furnace" → "soot from a furnace"** (v8, 10) — Hebrew פִּיחַ כִּבְשָׁן *piach kivshan*. *Piach* = soot. *Kivshan* = smelting furnace (as in Gen 19:28).
- **"blains" → "sores"** (v9, 10) — Hebrew אֲבַעְבֻּעֹת *ava'bu'ot* = pustules / blisters. "Blains" archaic.
- **"have raised thee up" → "have let you stand"** (v16) — Hebrew הֶעֱמַדְתִּיךָ *he'emadtikha* from *amad* = stand (hiphil: make stand / keep standing). Not "raised up" in the sense of installed; rather, preserved / kept you standing despite your wickedness, to demonstrate power. Paul cites this verse in Rom 9:17.
- **"fire ran along upon the ground" → "fire ran down to the earth"** (v23) — Hebrew וַתִּהֲלַךְ אֵשׁ אָרְצָה. *Halakh* = to go / flow. Lightning running down.
- **"fire mingled with the hail" → "fire flashing continually within the hail"** (v24) — Hebrew אֵשׁ מִתְלַקַּחַת *esh mitlaqqachat*. Hithpael of *laqach* — literally "taking itself / flashing / catching back and forth." Describing lightning within a hailstorm.
- **"rie" → "spelt"** (v32) — Hebrew כֻּסֶּמֶת *kussemet*. Ancient grain — spelt (a wheat variety) or emmer, not rye. Corrected.
- **"bolled" → "in bud"** (v31) — Hebrew גִּבְעֹל *giv'ol* = bud / stalk of flax-bloom.

### Notes

- **Selective hardening begins explicitly (v12)** — for the first time, the text says "Yahweh hardened Pharaoh's heart" (not just "Pharaoh's heart was hardened"). The active divine hardening escalates after repeated Pharaonic self-hardening; Paul in Rom 9:17–18 treats this as theological pattern of divine judicial hardening of those who have already hardened themselves.
- **Pharaoh's admission of sin (v27)** — the only time Pharaoh explicitly uses "I have sinned" during the plague cycle (repeated in 10:16). Under duress he acknowledges Yahweh's righteousness; when the pressure lifts (v34), he recants.
- **Escalation pattern** — plagues 1–6: Egyptian discomfort. Plague 7 (hail): agricultural destruction + loss of life (those left outside die). Plagues 8–10: catastrophic — locusts (food destroyed), darkness (religious / symbolic blow to Ra), firstborn (death).
