# Exodus 19

[1] In the third month of the going out of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, on this day they came to the wilderness of Sinai.

[2] And they set out from ⟦rephidim|Refidim|Rephidim⟧ and came to the wilderness of Sinai, and they camped in the wilderness; and ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ camped there in front of the mountain.

[3] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went up to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ called to him from the mountain, saying, "Thus you shall say to the house of ⟦jacob|Yaaqov|Jacob⟧ and tell the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧:

[4] 'You yourselves have seen what I did to ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and I bore you on wings of eagles and brought you to myself.

[5] And now, if you will surely listen to my voice and keep my covenant, then you shall be to me a treasured possession out of all the peoples, for all the earth is mine;

[6] and you, you shall be to me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.' These are the words that you shall speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧."

[7] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ came and called for the elders of the people and set before them all these words that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded him.

[8] And all the people answered together and said, "All that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has spoken we will do." And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ returned the words of the people to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Behold, I am coming to you in the thickness of the cloud, so that the people may hear when I speak with you, and also in you they may believe forever." And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ told the words of the people to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[10] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Go to the people, and you shall sanctify them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their cloaks,

[11] and let them be ready for the third day, for on the third day ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will come down before the eyes of all the people upon Mount Sinai.

[12] And you shall set a boundary for the people all around, saying, 'Keep yourselves from going up on the mountain and from touching its edge. Everyone who touches the mountain shall surely be put to death.

[13] No hand shall touch him, for he shall surely be stoned or surely be shot; whether beast or man, he shall not live.' At the drawing out of the ram's horn, they, they shall go up on the mountain."

[14] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went down from the mountain to the people, and he sanctified the people, and they washed their cloaks.

[15] And he said to the people, "Be ready for three days; do not draw near to a woman."

[16] And it came to pass on the third day, when it was morning, that there were thunders and lightnings and a heavy cloud upon the mountain, and the voice of ⟦shofar|a shofar|a trumpet⟧, very strong; and all the people who were in the camp trembled.

[17] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ brought the people out from the camp to meet ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and they took their stand at the lowest part of the mountain.

[18] And Mount Sinai smoked, all of it, because ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had come down upon it in fire; and its smoke went up like the smoke of a kiln, and all the mountain trembled greatly.

[19] And the voice of ⟦shofar|the shofar|the trumpet⟧ was going on and growing very strong; ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ was speaking, and ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ was answering him with a voice.

[20] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came down upon Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ called ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ to the top of the mountain, and ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went up.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Go down, warn the people, lest they break through to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ to see, and many of them fall.

[22] And also the priests, who draw near to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, let them sanctify themselves, lest ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ break out against them."

[23] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, "The people are not able to go up to Mount Sinai, for you yourself warned us, saying, 'Set a boundary for the mountain and sanctify it.'"

[24] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to him, "Go, go down, and you shall come up, you and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ with you; and the priests and the people, let them not break through to go up to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, lest he break out against them."

[25] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ went down to the people and said to them.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 17, 19) — Hebrew הָאֱלֹהִים, with the article in all three places: "the Elohim".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. Verse 1 begins without it.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v1, 3, 6) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"In the third month, when the children of Israel were gone forth" → "In the third month of the going out of the sons of Israel"** (v1) — Hebrew לְצֵאת, "of / after the going out". חֹדֶשׁ is "month" and "new moon": "on the third new moon" is also possible, and then "on this day" (בַּיּוֹם הַזֶּה, **"the same day" → "on this day"**) is the day of the new moon. The verse does not say more.
- **"were departed" → "set out"** (v2) — Hebrew נָסַע. **"pitched … camped" → "camped … camped"** — one verb, חָנָה, first in the plural, then in the singular ("Israel camped", as one). **"before the mount" → "in front of the mountain"** — Hebrew נֶגֶד.
- **"tell the children of Israel" kept** (v3) — Hebrew תַגֵּיד (הִגִּיד "tell"), beside תֹאמַר "say". The subject stands first: "And Moses — he went up".
- **"Ye have seen" → "You yourselves have seen"** (v4) — Hebrew אַתֶּם רְאִיתֶם, with the pronoun expressed. **"unto the Egyptians" → "to Egypt"** — Hebrew לְמִצְרָיִם. **"and how I bare you" → "and I bore you"** — Hebrew וָאֶשָּׂא "and I lifted, carried". **"eagles"** — Hebrew נְשָׁרִים: the נֶשֶׁר is a great bird of prey, eagle or griffon vulture.
- **"if ye will obey my voice indeed" → "if you will surely listen to my voice"** (v5) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb: שָׁמוֹעַ תִּשְׁמְעוּ בְּקֹלִי, literally "listening you will listen to my voice".
- **"a peculiar treasure unto me above all people" → "to me a treasured possession out of all the peoples"** (v5) — Hebrew סְגֻלָּה *segullah*: what a man, above all a king, owns as his own private treasure (1 Chr 29:3; Eccl 2:8). One word in Hebrew; "treasure" alone would lose that it is owned, "possession" alone that it is prized. "Peculiar" in 1611 meant "one's own". מִכָּל־הָעַמִּים is "from / out of all the peoples" or "more than all the peoples". **"for all the earth is mine"** — Hebrew כִּי־לִי כָּל־הָאָרֶץ; כִּי is "for" (it can also be heard as "though").
- **"a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation" kept** (v6) — Hebrew מַמְלֶכֶת כֹּהֲנִים וְגוֹי קָדוֹשׁ. מַמְלֶכֶת כֹּהֲנִים is two nouns bound together, "a kingdom of priests": a kingdom whose people are priests (or "a royalty of priests"). The Septuagint has βασίλειον ἱεράτευμα "a royal priesthood" (so 1 Pet 2:9; Rev 1:6 "a kingdom, priests"; cited from memory). גּוֹי is "nation" (עַם "people" in v5); קָדוֹשׁ "holy", set apart. The pronoun is expressed: "and you, you shall be".
- **"laid before their faces" → "set before them"** (v7) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׂם לִפְנֵיהֶם. **"called for the elders"** — Hebrew וַיִּקְרָא לְ.
- **"returned the words" kept** (v8) — Hebrew וַיָּשֶׁב, "brought back"; **"told"** (v9) — Hebrew וַיַּגֵּד. The same thing is said twice, with two verbs.
- **"in a thick cloud" → "in the thickness of the cloud"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּעַב הֶעָנָן, two nouns: עָב "thick cloud, thickness" and עָנָן "cloud". **"and believe thee for ever" → "and also in you they may believe forever"** — Hebrew וְגַם־בְּךָ יַאֲמִינוּ, "also in you" put first (cf. 14:31).
- **"sanctify"** (v10, 14, 22, 23) — Hebrew קִדֵּשׁ, "make holy, set apart". **"clothes" → "cloaks"** (v10, 14) — Hebrew שִׂמְלָה, the outer garment.
- **"in the sight of" → "before the eyes of"** (v11) — Hebrew לְעֵינֵי.
- **"set bounds unto the people" → "set a boundary for the people"** (v12, 23) — Hebrew הִגְבִּיל, from גְּבוּל "border". In v12 the object is the people, in v23 the mountain. **"Take heed to yourselves, that ye go not up" → "Keep yourselves from going up"** — Hebrew הִשָּׁמְרוּ לָכֶם, from שָׁמַר "keep". **"the border of it" → "its edge"** — Hebrew קָצֶה "end, edge".
- **"shall be surely put to death"** (v12) — Hebrew infinitive absolute with its verb: מוֹת יוּמָת, literally "dying he shall be made to die"; so also **"surely be stoned or surely be shot"** (v13), סָקוֹל יִסָּקֵל אוֹ־יָרֹה יִיָּרֶה.
- **"There shall not an hand touch it" → "No hand shall touch him"** (v13) — Hebrew בּוֹ, "him" or "it": the one who has touched the mountain is to be killed from a distance (or: "no hand shall touch it", the mountain). **"shot through" → "shot"** — Hebrew יָרָה "throw, shoot" (arrows or stones).
- **"when the trumpet soundeth long" → "At the drawing out of the ram's horn"** (v13) — Hebrew בִּמְשֹׁךְ הַיֹּבֵל, "at the drawing out of the *yovel*": when the horn gives a long-drawn blast. יֹבֵל *yovel* is a ram, a ram's horn (Josh 6:5), the word behind "jubilee"; it is another word than the שׁוֹפָר *shofar* of v16, 19. **"they shall come up to the mount" → "they, they shall go up on the mountain"** — Hebrew הֵמָּה יַעֲלוּ בָהָר, with the pronoun expressed. Who "they" are, and how this stands with v12, the verse does not say.
- **"Be ready against the third day" → "Be ready for three days"** (v15) — Hebrew לִשְׁלֹשֶׁת יָמִים, "for three days" (= by the third day). **"come not at your wives" → "do not draw near to a woman"** — Hebrew אַל־תִּגְּשׁוּ אֶל־אִשָּׁה; "your" is not in the Hebrew. נָגַשׁ "draw near" is also the word in v22.
- **"thunders"** (v16) — Hebrew קֹלֹת, literally "voices": the plural of קוֹל "voice, sound" is the Hebrew word for thunder. The same word stands in **"the voice of a shofar"** (v16, 19) and in **"with a voice"** (v19). **"thick cloud" → "heavy cloud"** — Hebrew כָּבֵד.
- **"the voice of the trumpet exceeding loud" → "the voice of a shofar, very strong"** (v16) — Hebrew שֹׁפָר *shofar*, a horn (of a ram or goat) blown as a signal; חָזָק "strong".
- **"at the nether part of the mount" → "at the lowest part of the mountain"** (v17) — Hebrew בְּתַחְתִּית הָהָר. **"stood" → "took their stand"** — Hebrew הִתְיַצֵּב.
- **"was altogether on a smoke" → "smoked, all of it"** (v18) — Hebrew עָשַׁן כֻּלּוֹ. **"descended" → "had come down"** — Hebrew יָרַד, as in v11, 20. **"a furnace" → "a kiln"** — Hebrew כִּבְשָׁן (Gen 19:28). **"quaked" → "trembled"** — Hebrew וַיֶּחֱרַד, the same verb as of the people in v16. Some Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint have "all the people" here (cited from memory); the Masoretic Text has "all the mountain".
- **"sounded long, and waxed louder and louder" → "was going on and growing very strong"** (v19) — Hebrew הוֹלֵךְ וְחָזֵק מְאֹד, literally "going and being strong, very". **"Moses spake, and God answered him by a voice" → "Moses was speaking, and *Elohim* was answering him with a voice"** — Hebrew imperfects, of what went on; בְקוֹל is "with a voice" or "in thunder": the word is the one of v16.
- **"charge the people" → "warn the people"** (v21), **"thou chargedst us" → "you yourself warned us"** (v23) — Hebrew הֵעִיד בְּ, "testify to, warn solemnly" (from עֵד "witness"). **"to gaze" → "to see"** (v21) — Hebrew לִרְאוֹת. **"many of them perish" → "many of them fall"** — Hebrew וְנָפַל מִמֶּנּוּ רָב.
- **"break through"** (v21, 24) — Hebrew הָרַס "tear down, break through"; **"break forth upon them" → "break out against them"** (v22, 24) — Hebrew פָּרַץ בְּ, another verb.
- **"the priests"** (v22, 24) — Hebrew הַכֹּהֲנִים. The priesthood of Aaron has not yet been set up at this point of the story (ch 28–29); who these priests are the text does not say.
- **"Away, get thee down" → "Go, go down"** (v24) — Hebrew לֶךְ־רֵד, two imperatives. The accents divide the verse after "with you": "and the priests and the people, let them not break through".
- **"and spake unto them" → "and said to them."** (v25) — Hebrew וַיֹּאמֶר אֲלֵהֶם, "and he said to them": the verb is "say", and what he said is not given; 20:1 begins anew.

### Notes

- **"Wings of eagles" (v4)** — image of Yahweh as the great eagle lifting Israel. Picked up in Deut 32:11.
- **"Kingdom of priests / holy nation" (v6)** — foundational vocation for Israel. All of Israel has priestly calling in relation to the nations. Reinterpreted for the church in 1 Pet 2:9 and Rev 1:6.
- **Sinai theophany** — one of the grandest theophanic scenes in the OT: fire, smoke, trembling, thunder, shofar-blast, the whole mountain smoking. The holiness of the mountain requires absolute boundary — touch it, die.
- **Proleptic covenant commitment (v8)** — the people commit "all that Yahweh has spoken we will do" — before they have heard the specific commandments (given in ch 20). An open-ended yes.
