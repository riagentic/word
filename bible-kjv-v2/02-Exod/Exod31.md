# Exodus 31

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ spoke to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[2] "See, I have called by name ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ the son of Uri, the son of ⟦hur|Chur|Hur⟧, of the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧.

[3] And I have filled him with the Spirit of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, in wisdom and in understanding and in knowledge and in all work,

[4] to devise designs, to work in gold and in silver and in bronze,

[5] and in the craft of stone for setting, and in the craft of wood, to work in every work.

[6] And I, behold, I have given with him ⟦oholiab|Oholiav|Oholiab⟧ the son of ⟦ahisamach|Achisamach|Ahisamach⟧, of the tribe of Dan, and in the heart of everyone wise of heart I have given wisdom, and they shall make all that I have commanded you:

[7] the tent of meeting, and the ark for the testimony, and the ⟦mercy-seat|kapporet|mercy seat⟧ that is on it, and all the utensils of the tent,

[8] and the table and its utensils, and the pure lampstand and all its utensils, and the altar of incense,

[9] and the altar of the burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand,

[10] and the woven garments, and the holy garments for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest, and the garments of his sons, to be priests,

[11] and the oil of anointing, and the fragrant incense for the Holy. According to all that I have commanded you they shall do."

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, saying,

[13] "And you, speak to the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying, 'Surely my ⟦shabbat|Shabbatot|Sabbaths⟧ you shall keep, for it is a sign between me and you for your generations, to know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ who sanctifies you.

[14] And you shall keep the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, for it is holy to you. Everyone who profanes it shall surely be put to death; for everyone who does work on it, that soul shall be cut off from the midst of its peoples.

[15] Six days work shall be done, and on the seventh day is a ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ of ⟦shabbat|shabbaton|Sabbath rest⟧, holy to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧. Everyone who does work on the day of the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ shall surely be put to death.

[16] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall keep the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧, to make the ⟦shabbat|Shabbat|Sabbath⟧ for their generations, an everlasting covenant.

[17] Between me and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ it is a sign forever, for six days ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day he ceased and was refreshed.'"

[18] And he gave to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, when he had finished speaking with him on Mount Sinai, the two tablets of the testimony, tablets of stone, written with the finger of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 18) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses and the items of the list in v7–11 with וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"saying" kept** (v1, 12, 13) — Hebrew לֵאמֹר.
- **"Bezaleel" → "Betzalel"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּצַלְאֵל *Betsal'el* = "in the shadow of El."
- **"Aholiab" → "Oholiab"** (v6) — Hebrew אָהֳלִיאָב *Oholi'av* = "the father is my tent" / "tent of the father."
- **"tribe"** (v2, 6) — Hebrew מַטֶּה, literally "staff"; of a kin group "tribe".
- **"the spirit of God" → "the Spirit of *Elohim*"** (v3) — Hebrew רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים, the same two words as Gen 1:2 and 41:38, without the article. רוּחַ is one word for "spirit", "wind" and "breath"; also possible: "a spirit of *Elohim*", "a divine spirit". The capital letter is English only. The verb has two objects, "I have filled him the Spirit of *Elohim*"; "with" is English.
- **"in wisdom, and in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship" → "in wisdom and in understanding and in knowledge and in all work"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּ "in" four times. חָכְמָה is "wisdom" (here the skill of the craftsman; the same word in v6), תְּבוּנָה "understanding", דַּעַת "knowledge", מְלָאכָה "work" (the word of v5, 14, 15). "Manner of" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"to devise cunning works" → "to devise designs"** (v4) — Hebrew לַחְשֹׁב מַחֲשָׁבֹת, verb and noun of one root: literally "to think thoughts", to plan what is to be made.
- **"to work in gold" kept** (v4, 5) — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת, the verb עָשָׂה "do, make": the same verb as "make" in v6, "do" in v11, "does" and "be done" in v14–15, "make the Shabbat" in v16 and "made the heavens" in v17. **"brass" → "bronze"** — Hebrew נְחֹשֶׁת.
- **"in cutting of stones, to set them, and in carving of timber" → "in the craft of stone for setting, and in the craft of wood"** (v5) — Hebrew חֲרֹשֶׁת twice, one word, so one rendering: the work of the חָרָשׁ "craftsman" (35:35), from חָרַשׁ "cut in, engrave". לְמַלֹּאת is literally "to fill": to set stones in their settings; the same verb as "filled" in v3.
- **"I have given with him" → kept; "in the hearts of all that are wise hearted I have put wisdom" → "in the heart of everyone wise of heart I have given wisdom"** (v6) — Hebrew נָתַתִּי "I have given" twice; "heart" is singular both times; חָכְמָה "wisdom" as in v3. "And I, behold, I": the pronoun stands first, with הִנֵּה.
- **"that they may make" → "and they shall make"** (v6) — Hebrew וְעָשׂוּ.
- **"the tabernacle of the congregation" → "the tent of meeting"** (v7) — Hebrew אֹהֶל מוֹעֵד. **"the ark of the testimony" → "the ark for the testimony"** — Hebrew הָאָרֹן לָעֵדֻת, with לְ "for": the ark that is to hold the testimony (25:16).
- **"mercy seat" → "*kapporet*"** (v7) — Hebrew כַּפֹּרֶת, from כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (literally "cover"): the atonement cover on the ark.
- **"furniture" / "vessels" → "utensils"** (v7, 8, 9) — one Hebrew word, כְּלִי: any article made for use (a vessel, a tool, a piece of equipment).
- **"the laver and his foot" → "the basin and its stand"** (v9) — Hebrew כִּיּוֹר, כֵּן.
- **"the cloths of service" → "the woven garments"** (v10) — Hebrew בִּגְדֵי הַשְּׂרָד. The meaning of שְׂרָד is uncertain: the word is found only in this phrase (35:19; 39:1, 41). Best supported is "plaited, woven work"; KJV "service" takes it from שֵׁרֵת "minister". Here an "and" separates them from the holy garments.
- **"the holy garments"** (v10) — Hebrew בִּגְדֵי הַקֹּדֶשׁ, literally "the garments of holiness". **"to minister in the priest's office" → "to be priests"** — Hebrew לְכַהֵן, one verb made from כֹּהֵן "priest"; it has neither the word "minister" (שֵׁרֵת) nor "serve" (עָבַד).
- **"sweet incense" → "the fragrant incense"** (v11) — Hebrew קְטֹרֶת הַסַּמִּים, literally "the incense of the fragrant substances" (30:34). **"for the holy place" → "for the Holy"** — Hebrew לַקֹּדֶשׁ, the noun "holiness" with the article: the name of the outer room (26:33), or "for what is holy". "Place" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"Verily" → "Surely"** (v13) — Hebrew אַךְ, a particle that affirms ("surely") or restricts ("only", "nevertheless"). With the second sense: the work on the dwelling does not set the Shabbat aside.
- **"my sabbaths" → "my *Shabbatot*"** (v13) — Hebrew שַׁבְּתֹתַי, plural; "it is a sign" (הִוא) is singular. The object stands first in the Hebrew.
- **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v13, 16, 17) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"that ye may know" → "to know"** (v13) — Hebrew לָדַעַת, an infinitive with no subject: that you may know, or that it may be known. **"that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you" → "that I am Yahweh who sanctifies you"** — Hebrew מְקַדִּשְׁכֶם, a participle: "your sanctifier".
- **"it is holy" kept** (v14, 15) — Hebrew קֹדֶשׁ, the noun: "it is holiness / a holy thing to you", "holiness to Yahweh".
- **"every one that defileth it" → "Everyone who profanes it"** (v14) — Hebrew מְחַלְלֶיהָ, a plural ("its profaners") with a singular verb: each one of them. חִלֵּל is "profane", to treat as common.
- **"shall surely be put to death" kept** (v14, 15) — Hebrew מוֹת יוּמָת, infinitive absolute with its verb: literally "dying he shall be made to die".
- **"that soul shall be cut off from among his people" → "that soul shall be cut off from the midst of its peoples"** (v14) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (the person); עַמֶּיהָ is plural, "its peoples", that is, its kinsfolk.
- **"the sabbath of rest" → "a *Shabbat* of *shabbaton*"** (v15) — Hebrew שַׁבַּת שַׁבָּתוֹן. שַׁבָּתוֹן *shabbaton* is a second noun from the same root שָׁבַת "cease" (v17): a Shabbat-keeping, a ceasing; the two together say a complete ceasing. The word is kept (16:23). "Rest" is another root (נוּחַ, 20:11); "solemn" is not in the word.
- **"to observe the sabbath" → "to make the *Shabbat*"** (v16) — Hebrew לַעֲשׂוֹת, "to do, make": to hold it. The verb of the work that is forbidden (v14, 15) and of Yahweh's making (v17).
- **"a perpetual covenant" → "an everlasting covenant"** (v16) — Hebrew בְּרִית עוֹלָם, literally "a covenant of the age"; the words stand without "for" or "as".
- **"in six days" → "six days"** (v17) — Hebrew שֵׁשֶׁת יָמִים, "six days", with no preposition (as in v15 and 20:11).
- **"he rested, and was refreshed" → "he ceased and was refreshed"** (v17) — Hebrew שָׁבַת וַיִּנָּפַשׁ. שָׁבַת is "cease, stop" (Gen 2:2, 3), the root of *Shabbat*. וַיִּנָּפַשׁ *wa-yinnafash* is the niphal of a verb made from נֶפֶשׁ *nephesh* "soul, breath": "he took breath, was refreshed". The verb is found three times (23:12 of the slave woman's son and the sojourner; 2 Sam 16:14 of David); here it is said of Yahweh.
- **"when he had made an end of communing with him" → "when he had finished speaking with him"** (v18) — Hebrew כְּכַלֹּתוֹ לְדַבֵּר אִתּוֹ. The subject of "gave" is not named; the Hebrew order is kept.
- **"two tables of testimony" → "the two tablets of the testimony"** (v18) — Hebrew לֻחֹת הָעֵדֻת, with the article.
- **"written with the finger of God" → "written with the finger of *Elohim*"** (v18) — Hebrew בְּאֶצְבַּע אֱלֹהִים; the same words in 8:19.

### Notes

- **Sabbath as covenant sign** (v13–17) — the placement of Sabbath-instruction at the close of the tabernacle plans is deliberate: the Sabbath is the temporal equivalent of the tabernacle (holy time / holy space). Even sanctuary construction must pause on Sabbath.
- **Spirit-filled craftsmanship** (v3) — the Hebrew Bible's first explicit attribution of Spirit-filling goes to an artisan, not a prophet or warrior. Theological dignity of craft and creativity.
- **Tablets "written with the finger of *Elohim*"** — definitive authorship. These will be smashed in 32:19 and rewritten in 34.
