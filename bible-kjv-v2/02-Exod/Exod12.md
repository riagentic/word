# Exodus 12

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and to ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, saying,

[2] "This month is for you the head of months; it is the first for you of the months of the year.

[3] Speak to all the congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, saying: 'On the tenth of this month they shall take for themselves, each man, a sheep for a fathers' house, a sheep for the house.

[4] And if the house is too small for a sheep, then he and his neighbor who is near to his house shall take, by the count of souls; each man according to his eating you shall count for the sheep.

[5] A sheep without blemish, a male, a year old, shall be yours; from the sheep or from the goats you shall take it.

[6] And it shall be a charge for you until the fourteenth day of this month, and all the assembly of the congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall slaughter it between the two evenings.

[7] And they shall take from the blood and put it on the two doorposts and on the lintel, on the houses in which they eat it.

[8] And they shall eat the flesh in this night, roasted with fire, and unleavened bread; with bitter herbs they shall eat it.

[9] Do not eat any of it raw, or boiled at all in water, but roasted with fire, its head with its shanks and with its entrails.

[10] And you shall not leave over any of it until morning; and what remains of it until morning you shall burn with fire.

[11] And thus you shall eat it: your loins girded, your sandals on your feet, and your rod in your hand; and you shall eat it in haste. It is a ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[12] And I will pass through the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ in this night, and I will strike every firstborn in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from man to beast; and on all the gods of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ I will do judgments. I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] And the blood shall be a sign for you on the houses where you are; and I will see the blood, and I will pass over you, and there shall be no plague among you to destroy, when I strike in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[14] And this day shall be for you a remembrance, and you shall hold it as a feast to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; for your generations, as an everlasting statute, you shall hold it as a feast.

[15] Seven days you shall eat unleavened bread; indeed, on the first day you shall make leaven cease from your houses, for whoever eats what is leavened, that soul shall be cut off from ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, from the first day until the seventh day.

[16] And on the first day a holy convocation, and on the seventh day a holy convocation shall be for you; no work shall be done on them; only what is eaten by every soul, that alone shall be done for you.

[17] And you shall keep the ⟦unleavened-bread|Matzot|Unleavened Bread⟧, for on this very day I brought your hosts out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and you shall keep this day for your generations as an everlasting statute.

[18] In the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, you shall eat unleavened bread, until the twenty-first day of the month, in the evening.

[19] Seven days leaven shall not be found in your houses; for whoever eats anything leavened, that soul shall be cut off from the congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, whether sojourner or native of the land.

[20] You shall not eat anything leavened; in all your dwelling places you shall eat unleavened bread.'"

[21] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ called all the elders of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ and said to them, "Draw out and take for yourselves from the flock according to your clans, and slaughter the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧.

[22] And you shall take a bunch of hyssop and dip it in the blood that is in the basin, and touch the lintel and the two doorposts with some of the blood that is in the basin; and you, none of you shall go out of the entrance of his house until morning.

[23] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will pass through to smite ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and he will see the blood on the lintel and on the two doorposts, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will pass over the entrance and will not allow the destroyer to come into your houses to smite.

[24] And you shall keep this word as a statute for you and for your sons forever.

[25] And it shall be, when you come to the land that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will give you, as he has spoken, that you shall keep this service.

[26] And it shall be, when your sons say to you, 'What is this service to you?',

[27] that you shall say, 'It is the sacrifice of the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, who passed over the houses of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ when he smote ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and our houses he delivered.'" And the people bowed their heads and bowed down.

[28] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ went and did; as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, so they did.

[29] And it came to pass at midnight that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ struck every firstborn in the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, from the firstborn of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ who sits on his throne to the firstborn of the captive who was in the house of the pit, and every firstborn of beast.

[30] And ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ rose in the night, he and all his servants and all ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and there was a great outcry in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, for there was no house where there was not one dead.

[31] And he called ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ by night and said, "Rise, go out from the midst of my people, both you and the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, and go, serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, as you have spoken.

[32] Both your flocks and your herds take, as you have spoken, and go; and bless me also."

[33] And ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ was strong upon the people, to hasten to send them away from the land, for they said, "We are all dead."

[34] And the people took up their dough before it was leavened, their kneading bowls bound up in their cloaks on their shoulder.

[35] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ had done according to the word of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, and they asked from the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧ vessels of silver and vessels of gold and cloaks.

[36] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had given the people favor in the eyes of the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧, and they granted them what they asked; and they stripped the ⟦egyptians|Mitzrim|Egyptians⟧.

[37] And the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ journeyed from ⟦rameses|Raamses|Rameses⟧ to ⟦succoth|Sukkot|Succoth⟧, about six hundred thousand on foot, the grown men, besides little ones.

[38] And also a mixed multitude went up with them, and flocks and herds, very much livestock.

[39] And they baked the dough that they had brought out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ into cakes of unleavened bread, for it was not leavened, because they were driven out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and were not able to linger, and also provisions they had not made for themselves.

[40] And the dwelling of the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧, which they dwelt in ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, was four hundred and thirty years.

[41] And it came to pass at the end of four hundred and thirty years, it came to pass on that very day, that all the hosts of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ went out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[42] It is a night of watching for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, to bring them out from the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; this night is for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, a watching for all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ for their generations.

[43] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, "This is the statute of the ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧: no foreigner shall eat of it.

[44] And every man's slave, bought with money: you shall circumcise him, then he shall eat of it.

[45] A settler and a hired man shall not eat of it.

[46] In one house it shall be eaten; you shall not bring out any of the flesh from the house to the outside, and you shall not break a bone in it.

[47] All the congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ shall make it.

[48] And when a sojourner sojourns with you and makes a ⟦pesach|Pesach|Passover⟧ to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, let every male of his be circumcised, and then he shall come near to make it, and he shall be as a native of the land; and no uncircumcised one shall eat of it.

[49] ⟦torah|One Torah|One law⟧ shall be for the native and for the sojourner who sojourns in your midst."

[50] And all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ did; as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ had commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧, so they did.

[51] And it came to pass on that very day that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ brought the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ out of the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ by their hosts.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"the beginning of months" → "the head of months"** (v2) — Hebrew רֹאשׁ חֳדָשִׁים; רֹאשׁ is "head". The Hebrew has no verb: "is" (or "shall be") is supplied twice.
- **"a lamb" → "a sheep"** (v3, 4, 5) — Hebrew שֶׂה *seh*: one animal of the flock, a sheep or a goat, of any age. "Lamb" is another word (כֶּבֶשׂ). Verse 5 says so itself: the *seh* may be taken "from the sheep or from the goats" (מִן־הַכְּבָשִׂים וּמִן־הָעִזִּים; כְּבָשִׂים is the plural of the word elsewhere rendered "lamb", here the sheep kind beside the goats; "or" is the Hebrew "and").
- **"according to the house of their fathers" → "for a fathers' house"** (v3) — Hebrew לְבֵית־אָבֹת, the kin group named after its fathers; then לַבָּיִת "for the house" (the household).
- **"too little for the lamb" → "too small for a sheep"** (v4) — Hebrew יִמְעַט … מִהְיֹת מִשֶּׂה, literally "is too few to be for a sheep". **"his neighbour next unto his house" → "his neighbor who is near to his house"** — Hebrew שְׁכֵנוֹ, "the one who dwells by him" (another word than רֵעַ "neighbor" of 11:2). "Shall take" has no object in the Hebrew.
- **"according to the number of the souls … shall make your count" → "by the count of souls … you shall count"** (v4) — Hebrew מִכְסַת and תָּכֹסּוּ, noun and verb of one root (כסס), found only here and Lev 27:23. נְפָשֹׁת is "souls": persons. "According to his eating" is literally "according to the mouth of his eating".
- **"without blemish" kept** (v5) — Hebrew תָמִים, "whole, complete": the same word as "blameless" of a man (Gen 6:9; 17:1). **"of the first year" → "a year old"** — Hebrew בֶּן־שָׁנָה, literally "a son of a year".
- **"ye shall keep it up" → "it shall be a charge for you"** (v6) — Hebrew וְהָיָה לָכֶם לְמִשְׁמֶרֶת, literally "it shall be to you for a keeping", from שָׁמַר "keep".
- **"kill it" → "slaughter it"** (v6, 21) — Hebrew שָׁחַט, to kill by cutting the throat.
- **"in the evening" → "between the two evenings"** (v6) — Hebrew בֵּין הָעַרְבָּיִם, literally so: the word is a dual. Which hours are meant is disputed: the dusk between sunset and dark, or the time from the sun's decline in the afternoon to sunset (the later Jewish practice).
- **"side posts" / "upper door post" → "doorposts" / "lintel"** (v7, 22, 23) — Hebrew מְזוּזֹת / מַשְׁקוֹף *mezuzot / mashqof*. *Mezuzah* = doorpost (now also used for the scripture-case on a doorpost); *mashqof* = lintel (crosspiece above door). "Put" is literally "give" (נָתַן).
- **"roast with fire, and unleavened bread; and with bitter herbs" → "roasted with fire, and unleavened bread; with bitter herbs they shall eat it"** (v8) — the Hebrew accents divide the verse so. מְרֹרִים is "bitter things"; "herbs" is supplied. עַל is "upon, with".
- **"sodden at all with water" → "boiled at all in water"** (v9) — Hebrew וּבָשֵׁל מְבֻשָּׁל בַּמָּיִם, literally "boiled, boiled in water": the word stands twice. **"raw"** — Hebrew נָא, found only here (not the particle נָא "please").
- **"his legs" / "purtenance" → "its shanks" / "its entrails"** (v9) — Hebrew כְּרָעַיִם, the lower legs, and קֶרֶב, the inward parts. The animal is roasted whole.
- **"let nothing of it remain" → "not leave over any of it"** (v10) — Hebrew הוֹתִיר "leave over" and נוֹתָר "what remains", one root.
- **"your staff" → "your rod"** (v11) — Hebrew מַקֵּל, a cut stick; "staff" is מַטֶּה (4:2; 14:16).
- **"in haste"** (v11) — Hebrew בְּחִפָּזוֹן, haste with alarm (Deut 16:3; Isa 52:12). "Hasten" in v33 is another word (מִהַר).
- **"the LORD's passover" → "a Pesach to Yahweh"** (v11) — Hebrew פֶּסַח הוּא לַיהוָה. The noun פֶּסַח *pesach* is kept as a name, for the animal (v21), the meal and the feast alike; no "lamb", "offering" or "feast" is added. It belongs to the verb פָּסַח of v13, 23, 27.
- **"smite" → "strike"** (v12, 13, 29) — Hebrew הִכָּה; here it is a killing blow. "Smite" is kept for another verb (v23, 27). **"both man and beast" → "from man to beast"** — literal. **"execute judgment" → "do judgments"** — Hebrew אֶעֱשֶׂה שְׁפָטִים, plural: acts of judgment. Whether "the gods of Egypt" are judged as beings or as images the verse does not say.
- **"I will pass over you"** (v13, 23, 27) — Hebrew פָּסַח עַל. One rendering in all three verses. The sense of the verb is not certain: (1) "pass over, spring over", so the tradition ("Passover") and the Greek of v23 ("will pass by"); (2) "spare, have compassion", so some old versions; (3) "protect, stand guard over", as in Isa 31:5, where the verb stands beside "shield" and "deliver", and so the Greek of v13 and 27 ("shelter") — in v23 Yahweh would then guard the entrance against the destroyer. The versions are cited from memory. Another verb of the same letters means "limp" (1 Kgs 18:21, 26). "Pass through" in v12, 23 is a different verb (עָבַר).
- **"the plague shall not be upon you to destroy you" → "there shall be no plague among you to destroy"** (v13) — Hebrew נֶגֶף, literally "a blow", from the verb "smite" of v23, 27 (another word than the "plague" of 11:1). לְמַשְׁחִית is literally "for a destroyer" or "for destruction": the word of v23. "You" is not in the Hebrew. "When I strike in the land" is literal.
- **"a memorial" → "a remembrance"** (v14) — Hebrew זִכָּרוֹן. **"keep it a feast" → "hold it as a feast"** (v14) — Hebrew וְחַגֹּתֶם אֹתוֹ חַג, verb and noun of one root: a pilgrim feast. "Keep" is another verb (v17, 24, 25). **"an ordinance for ever" → "an everlasting statute"** (v14, 17) — Hebrew חֻקַּת עוֹלָם, literally "a statute of the age".
- **"even the first day ye shall put away leaven" → "indeed, on the first day you shall make leaven cease"** (v15) — Hebrew אַךְ "only, indeed"; תַּשְׁבִּיתוּ is the causative of שָׁבַת "cease". **"leaven" / "leavened bread"** (v15, 19, 20) — two Hebrew words: שְׂאֹר, the leaven (sourdough) that makes dough rise, and חָמֵץ, what is leavened; מַחְמֶצֶת (v19, 20) is "anything leavened". "Bread" is not in these words. מַצּוֹת, "unleavened bread", is a plural: flat cakes baked without leaven.
- **"that soul shall be cut off"** (v15, 19) — Hebrew הַנֶּפֶשׁ הַהִוא "that soul": the person. The time, "from the first day until the seventh day", stands last in the Hebrew.
- **"an holy convocation"** (v16) — Hebrew מִקְרָא־קֹדֶשׁ, literally "a calling of holiness": a day called out as holy, or a calling together. **"no manner of work shall be done in them, save that which every man must eat" → "no work shall be done on them; only what is eaten by every soul"** — literal; "be done" is the same verb both times.
- **"ye shall observe the feast of unleavened bread" → "you shall keep the Matzot"** (v17) — Hebrew וּשְׁמַרְתֶּם אֶת־הַמַּצּוֹת, "you shall keep the unleavened bread": "feast" is not in the Hebrew. The Samaritan text and the Greek read "the commandment" (הַמִּצְוָה, the same consonants; cited from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept. **"selfsame day" → "this very day"** (v17; "that very day" v41, 51) — Hebrew בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה, literally "in the bone of this day". **"armies" → "hosts"** (v17, 41, 51) — Hebrew צְבָאוֹת.
- **"In the first month"** (v18) — Hebrew בָּרִאשֹׁן "in the first": "month" is supplied.
- **"a stranger, or born in the land" → "sojourner or native of the land"** (v19, 48, 49) — Hebrew גֵּר, one who lives among a people not his own, and אֶזְרָח, the native.
- **"habitations" → "dwelling places"** (v20) — Hebrew מוֹשְׁבֹת, from יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"Draw out and take you a lamb" → "Draw out and take for yourselves from the flock"** (v21) — Hebrew מִשְׁכוּ "draw, pull" (lead off), and צֹאן, the flock of sheep and goats; "from" is supplied. **"kill the passover" → "slaughter the Pesach"** — the animal itself is called the *pesach*.
- **"strike the lintel" → "touch the lintel"** (v22) — Hebrew וְהִגַּעְתֶּם אֶל, literally "make [it] touch to"; "with some of the blood" is "from the blood". **"bason"** — Hebrew סַף, which is also "threshold"; which is meant here is not certain. **"the door of his house" → "the entrance of his house"** (v22, 23) — Hebrew פֶּתַח, the opening.
- **"to smite the Egyptians" → "to smite Egypt"** (v23, 27) — Hebrew נָגַף, to strike with a blow from God; מִצְרַיִם is "Egypt".
- **"the destroyer" kept** (v23) — Hebrew הַמַּשְׁחִית *ha-mashchit*, the causative participle of שָׁחַת, the verb rendered "ruin" in Gen 6:13; 19:13: "the one who ruins"; "destroyer" is kept because English has no noun from "ruin". With the article it may be a being ("the destroyer", cf. 2 Sam 24:16; Heb 11:28) or the thing ("the destruction", as in v13). Yahweh himself passes through and smites (v12, 23, 29); the destroyer is named only here. "Will not allow" is literally "will not give".
- **"this thing" → "this word"** (v24) — Hebrew הַדָּבָר, "word" or "thing". "For you and for your sons" is singular in the Hebrew; the verbs around it are plural.
- **"as he hath promised" → "as he has spoken"** (v25) — Hebrew דִּבֵּר "speak".
- **"What mean ye by this service?" → "What is this service to you?"** (v26) — Hebrew מָה הָעֲבֹדָה הַזֹּאת לָכֶם, literally so. **"children" → "sons"** — Hebrew בְּנֵיכֶם.
- **"the sacrifice of the LORD's passover" → "the sacrifice of the Pesach to Yahweh"** (v27) — Hebrew זֶבַח־פֶּסַח הוּא לַיהוָה.
- **"bowed the head and worshipped" → "bowed their heads and bowed down"** (v27) — two Hebrew verbs: קָדַד, to bow the head, and הִשְׁתַּחֲוָה, to bow down to the ground.
- **"at midnight"** (v29) — Hebrew בַּחֲצִי הַלַּיְלָה "in the half of the night". **"that sat on his throne" → "who sits on his throne"** — a participle, as in 11:5. **"dungeon" → "the house of the pit"** — Hebrew בֵּית הַבּוֹר, literally so (בּוֹר "pit, cistern", as Gen 40:15).
- **"all the Egyptians" → "all Egypt"** (v30) — Hebrew וְכָל־מִצְרַיִם. **"a great cry" → "a great outcry"** — Hebrew צְעָקָה, as in 11:6.
- **"the Egyptians were urgent upon the people" → "Egypt was strong upon the people"** (v33) — Hebrew וַתֶּחֱזַק מִצְרַיִם עַל־הָעָם: the verb חָזַק "be strong" that is used of Pharaoh's heart (11:10); the verb is singular, "Egypt". **"that they might send them out of the land in haste" → "to hasten to send them away"** — Hebrew לְמַהֵר לְשַׁלְּחָם; שִׁלַּח is the verb "let go" of 11:1, 10. **"We be all dead men" → "We are all dead"** — Hebrew כֻּלָּנוּ מֵתִים, "dead" or "dying"; "men" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"their clothes" → "their cloaks"** (v34, 35) — Hebrew שִׂמְלָה, the outer wrap.
- **"borrowed … jewels" → "asked … vessels"** (v35) — as in 11:2. **"lent unto them such things as they required" → "granted them what they asked"** (v36) — Hebrew וַיַּשְׁאִלוּם, the causative of "ask": "they let them ask", that is, gave what was asked.
- **"they spoiled the Egyptians" → "they stripped the Egyptians"** (v36) — Hebrew נִצֵּל, as in 3:22.
- **"about six hundred thousand on foot that were men, beside children" → "about six hundred thousand on foot, the grown men, besides little ones"** (v37) — Hebrew כְּשֵׁשׁ־מֵאוֹת אֶלֶף רַגְלִי הַגְּבָרִים לְבַד מִטָּף, word for word. רַגְלִי is "on foot, footmen"; הַגְּבָרִים is the plural of גֶּבֶר, a grown, strong man (not the usual word for "men", אֲנָשִׁים), as in 10:11; טַף are the little children (the women are not named). The number is given as the Hebrew has it. אֶלֶף is "thousand"; that it means a "clan" or a "troop" here is a modern proposal.
- **"a mixed multitude" kept** (v38) — Hebrew עֵרֶב רַב, literally "a great mixture": people who were not Israel. **"very much cattle" → "very much livestock"** — Hebrew מִקְנֶה כָּבֵד מְאֹד, literally "livestock very heavy" (כָּבֵד "heavy", as of the famine in Gen 12:10); "heavy" would be heard of weight in English.
- **"unleavened cakes" → "cakes of unleavened bread"** (v39) — Hebrew עֻגֹת מַצּוֹת. **"thrust out" → "driven out"** — Hebrew גֹּרְשׁוּ, as 11:1. **"tarry" → "linger"**.
- **"the sojourning of the children of Israel, who dwelt in Egypt" → "the dwelling of the sons of Israel, which they dwelt in Egypt"** (v40) — Hebrew וּמוֹשַׁב … אֲשֶׁר יָשְׁבוּ, noun and verb of one root, יָשַׁב "dwell". The noun is "dwelling place" in v20; here it is the time of dwelling, so "dwelling". **"four hundred and thirty years"** (v40, 41) — the Hebrew has "thirty years and four hundred years". The Masoretic Text speaks of the dwelling in Egypt only. The Samaritan text and the Greek add the land of Canaan to the four hundred and thirty years, which shortens the time in Egypt; Gal 3:17 counts from the promise to Abraham (the two versions are cited from memory). The Masoretic Text is kept.
- **"a night to be much observed" → "a night of watching"** (v42) — Hebrew לֵיל שִׁמֻּרִים, a plural noun from שָׁמַר "keep, watch", found only in this verse (twice). Yahweh's watching over Israel, and Israel's keeping of the night, are both in the word.
- **"the ordinance of the passover" → "the statute of the Pesach"** (v43) — Hebrew חֻקַּת. **"no stranger" → "no foreigner"** — Hebrew בֶּן־נֵכָר, literally "son of foreignness".
- **"every man's servant that is bought for money" → "every man's slave, bought with money"** (v44) — Hebrew עֶבֶד "slave"; מִקְנַת־כָּסֶף is literally "a purchase of silver". "You shall circumcise" is singular.
- **"A foreigner and an hired servant" → "A settler and a hired man"** (v45) — Hebrew תּוֹשָׁב, one settled in the land without its rights, and שָׂכִיר, one who works for hire.
- **"neither shall ye break a bone thereof" → "you shall not break a bone in it"** (v46) — Hebrew וְעֶצֶם לֹא תִשְׁבְּרוּ־בוֹ. "You shall not bring out" is singular, "you shall not break" plural. Cited in John 19:36.
- **"keep it" / "keep the passover" → "make it" / "makes a Pesach"** (v47, 48) — Hebrew עָשָׂה "make, do": to prepare and hold the Pesach. "Keep" in this chapter is שָׁמַר (v17, 24, 25). **"let all his males be circumcised" → "let every male of his be circumcised"** — an infinitive used as a command.
- **"One law" → "One Torah"** (v49) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה אַחַת; תּוֹרָה is "instruction, law", here one ruling for both.
- **"by their armies" → "by their hosts"** (v51) — Hebrew עַל־צִבְאֹתָם.

### Notes

- **Passover as founding ritual** — the chapter is half narrative, half ritual instruction. The founding event (Israel's deliverance) is embedded in ongoing liturgical practice. The story shapes the ritual; the ritual makes the story perpetually present for each generation.
- **Universal access through circumcision (v48–49)** — the Passover is Israelite, but is open to any sojourner who undergoes circumcision. "One Torah for the native and for the sojourner." A remarkably open ethno-religious boundary.
- **The number in v37** — adult males on foot. With women and children, the traditional estimate reaches 2–3 million; the historical debate is intense.
- **Foreshadowing of Christ** — Christian tradition reads the sheep without blemish (v5), the blood-sign (v13), and "you shall not break a bone in it" (v46, cited in John 19:36) as typological of Jesus' death. Paul calls Christ "our Pesach, sacrificed for us" (1 Cor 5:7).
