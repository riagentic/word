[18] In the first month, from the fourteenth day of the month at evening, you shall eat unleavened bread until the twenty-first day of the month at evening.

[19] For seven days no leaven shall be found in your houses; for whoever eats what is leavened, that person shall be cut off from the congregation of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ — whether resident alien or native of the land.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"Passover"** (v11, 21, 27, 43, 48) — Hebrew פֶּסַח *Pesach*; English retained. Etymology: likely from *pasach* = "to pass over / skip / leap" (v13 *pasachti* = "I will pass over").
- **"kill it in the evening" → "slaughter it at twilight"** (v6) — Hebrew בֵּין הָעַרְבָּיִם *beyn ha-arbayim* = "between the two evenings" — dusk / twilight (the transition from afternoon to evening). Rabbinic interpretation fixes this between noon and sunset.
- **"side posts" / "upper door post" → "doorposts" / "lintel"** (v7, 22, 23) — Hebrew מְזוּזֹת / מַשְׁקוֹף *mezuzot / mashqof*. *Mezuzah* = doorpost (now also used for the scripture-case on a doorpost); *mashqof* = lintel (crosspiece above door).
- **"sodden" → "boiled"** (v9) — archaic → modern.
- **"purtenance" → "inner parts"** (v9) — Hebrew קְרָבִים *qeravim* = inward parts / entrails. The whole lamb is to be roasted, including viscera — not butchered and sold.
- **"destroyer" → "the Destroyer"** (v23) — Hebrew הַמַּשְׁחִית *ha-mashchit* = the destroyer. Personified destroying-agent, whether angelic intermediary or the destructive dimension of Yahweh's own action. The text presents as distinct from Yahweh: "Yahweh will not let the Destroyer come in." Blurring of agency common in OT theology.
- **"mixed multitude" (v38)** — Hebrew עֵרֶב רַב *erev rav*. Not only Israelites left Egypt — other slave groups and sympathizers joined. The nation forming in the wilderness is already multi-ethnic.
- **"600,000… besides children"** (v37) — Hebrew שֵׁשׁ־מֵאוֹת אֶלֶף רַגְלִי *shesh-me'ot eleph ragli*. Adult males. With women and children, traditional estimate reaches 2–3 million. Historical / numerical debates intense: some argue *eleph* here = "military unit / troop" rather than literal thousand, reducing the number substantially; others argue for miraculous multiplication as narrated. Translation literal.
- **"430 years" (v40–41)** — LXX and Samaritan Pentateuch say "430 years in Egypt and Canaan" (halving the Egypt stay). Gal 3:17 follows LXX. MT's 430 years in Egypt specifically. Textual variant with theological consequences.
- **"night of watching" (v42)** — Hebrew לֵיל שִׁמֻּרִים *leyl shimmurim*. Two meanings in the verse: Yahweh's watching (protecting Israel); Israel's watching (annual commemoration). Double vigil.

### Notes

- **Passover as founding ritual** — the chapter is half narrative, half ritual instruction. The founding event (Israel's deliverance) is embedded in ongoing liturgical practice. The story shapes the ritual; the ritual makes the story perpetually present for each generation.
- **Universal access through circumcision (v48–49)** — the Passover is Israelite, but is open to any foreigner who undergoes circumcision. "One law for native and resident alien." A remarkably open ethno-religious boundary.
- **Foreshadowing of Christ** — Christian tradition reads the lamb without blemish (v5), the blood-sign (v13), and "not a bone shall be broken" (v46, cited in John 19:36) as typological of Jesus' death. Paul calls Christ "our Pesach, sacrificed for us" (1 Cor 5:7).
