# Exodus 6

[1] Then Yahweh said to Moses, "Now you shall see what I will do to Pharaoh; for with a strong hand he shall let them go, and with a strong hand he shall drive them out of his land."

[2] *Elohim* spoke to Moses and said to him, "I am Yahweh.

[3] I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob as Almighty *Elohim*; but by my name Yahweh I did not make myself known to them.

[4] I also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their sojournings, in which they sojourned.

[5] I have also heard the groaning of the children of Israel, whom the Egyptians are holding in forced labor, and I have remembered my covenant.

[6] Therefore say to the children of Israel, 'I am Yahweh, and I will bring you out from under the forced labor of the Egyptians, and I will deliver you from their bondage, and I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and with great judgments.

[7] I will take you to myself as a people, and I will be your *Elohim*; and you shall know that I am Yahweh your *Elohim*, who brings you out from under the forced labor of the Egyptians.

[8] I will bring you into the land that I swore, with uplifted hand, to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob; and I will give it to you as a possession. I am Yahweh.'"

[9] Moses spoke thus to the children of Israel, but they did not listen to Moses because of broken spirit and harsh labor.

[10] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying,

[11] "Go in, speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he may let the children of Israel go out of his land."

[12] But Moses spoke before Yahweh, saying, "Behold, the children of Israel have not listened to me; how then will Pharaoh listen to me, since I am of uncircumcised lips?"

[13] And Yahweh spoke to Moses and to Aaron, and gave them a charge concerning the children of Israel and concerning Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt.

[14] These are the heads of their ancestral houses: the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel: Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. These are the clans of Reuben.

[15] The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanite woman. These are the clans of Simeon.

[16] These are the names of the sons of Levi, according to their generations: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. And the years of the life of Levi were 137.

[17] The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shimei, according to their clans.

[18] The sons of Kohath: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. And the years of the life of Kohath were 133.

[19] The sons of Merari: Mahli and Mushi. These are the clans of Levi according to their generations.

[20] Amram took Jochebed, his father's sister, as a wife; and she bore him Aaron and Moses. And the years of the life of Amram were 137.

[21] The sons of Izhar: Korah, Nepheg, and Zichri.

[22] The sons of Uzziel: Mishael, Elzaphan, and Sithri.

[23] Aaron took Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab, sister of Nahshon, as a wife; and she bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

[24] The sons of Korah: Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph. These are the clans of the Korahites.

[25] Eleazar, Aaron's son, took one of the daughters of Putiel as a wife; and she bore him Phinehas. These are the heads of the ancestral houses of the Levites, according to their clans.

[26] These are that Aaron and Moses to whom Yahweh said, "Bring out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt according to their hosts."

[27] These are they who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt to bring out the children of Israel from Egypt. These are that Moses and Aaron.

[28] On the day when Yahweh spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt,

[29] Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying, "I am Yahweh; speak to Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I say to you."

[30] But Moses said before Yahweh, "Behold, I am of uncircumcised lips; how then will Pharaoh listen to me?"

## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"God Almighty" → "Almighty *Elohim*"** (v3) — as in Gen 17:1.
- **"Jehovah" → "Yahweh"** (v3) — the Tetragrammaton. See Gen 2:4. The theological statement of v3 is striking: the patriarchs knew the divine name, but not in its full revelation. Textual tension: Gen 4:26 says "then people began to call on the name of Yahweh," and the patriarchs use the name Yahweh throughout Genesis. Resolution:
  1. Source-critical: different authorial traditions (Priestly says Yahweh first revealed to Moses, Yahwist has earlier patriarchal knowledge of the name). Canonically read, the two traditions stand in tension.
  2. Theological: the name was known but not *experienced* in its full covenantal-redemptive force; Moses' generation comes to know the name by what Yahweh will do for them.
- **"Jehovah"** — note on name: *Jehovah* is an English-Latin rendering, formed by superimposing the vowels of *Adonai* onto the consonants YHWH (the Masoretic "perpetual qere" signal). "Yahweh" is the probable original vocalization, though Jewish tradition has long ceased to pronounce the name.
- **"with stretched out arm" → "with an outstretched arm"** (v6) — Hebrew בִּזְרוֹעַ נְטוּיָה *bi-zroa netuyah*. Paradigmatic phrase for Yahweh's redemptive action (Deut 4:34, 5:15, etc.).
- **"with uplifted hand" (v8)** — Hebrew נָשָׂאתִי אֶת־יָדִי *nasa'ti et-yadi* = "I raised my hand" — oath gesture (swearing).
- **"armies" → "hosts"** (v26) — Hebrew עַל־צִבְאֹתָם *al tsiv'otam*. *Tsava* = host / army / organized group. Israel as organized ranks (not yet a military term here but implied).
- **"father's sister" (v20)** — Hebrew דֹּדָתוֹ *dodato*. Wife was Amram's aunt. Under the later Mosaic law, this would be an incestuous union (Lev 18:12); the patriarchs / Levitical genealogy precedes that prohibition.

### Notes

- **Renewed commission after disappointment** — ch 5 ended with failure; ch 6 reaffirms Yahweh's identity and intent with the sevenfold "I will" declaration (v6–8): "I will bring out… deliver… redeem… take… be… bring… give." A sevenfold promise anchors what happens next.
- **Insertion of genealogy (v14–25)** — interrupts the narrative to establish Moses' and Aaron's Levitical / priestly credentials. Purpose: to show that those Yahweh uses are from the chosen tribe of Levi, and specifically Aaron's line will become the priestly line (Eleazar → Phinehas → high-priestly succession).
- **Name "Yahweh" and divine self-revelation** — v3 emphasizes that this chapter marks the definitive revelation of the covenantal name in action. The plague cycle (chs 7–12) becomes the extended demonstration of what the name means.
