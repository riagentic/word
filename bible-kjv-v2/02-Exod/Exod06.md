## Changes

- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v2, 7) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*. In v2 the speaker is named *Elohim* and says "I am Yahweh".
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept.
- **"with a strong hand shall he let them go" → "with a strong hand he shall let them go"** (v1) — Hebrew בְּיָד חֲזָקָה, twice. Whose hand is meant the verse does not say: Yahweh's hand upon Pharaoh (3:19; 13:9), or Pharaoh's own hand driving them out (12:33). חָזָק "strong" is the adjective of the verb חָזַק "be strong" that is used of Pharaoh's heart (7:13). **"let them go"** — Hebrew שִׁלַּח (piel), "send away, let go": one verb for KJV "let go" and "send away". **"drive them out"** — Hebrew גֵּרֵשׁ.
- **"I am the LORD" → "I am Yahweh"** (v2, 6, 8, 29) — Hebrew אֲנִי יְהוָה, the Tetragrammaton. *Jehovah* (KJV v3) is a later mixed form: the consonants YHWH with the vowels of *Adonai*.
- **"by the name of God Almighty" → "as El Shaddai"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּאֵל שַׁדָּי, literally "in El Shaddai", that is, in the character of, as El Shaddai (Gen 17:1; 28:3; 35:11; 48:3). "The name of" is not in the Hebrew. The meaning of שַׁדַּי is not certain; "Almighty" comes from the old versions.
- **"but by my name JEHOVAH was I not known to them" → "and by my name Yahweh I was not known to them"** (v3) — Hebrew וּשְׁמִי יְהוָה לֹא נוֹדַעְתִּי לָהֶם, word for word: "and my name Yahweh — I was not known to them". There is no preposition before "my name"; "by" is supplied for English (the בְּ of "as El Shaddai" may serve for both halves). נוֹדַעְתִּי is a niphal: "I was known" or "I made myself known". The clause is a statement, and it stands so in the verse. Genesis uses the name Yahweh from 2:4 on, and the fathers speak it (Gen 15:2; 28:16); so some read the clause as a question, "and by my name Yahweh did I not make myself known to them?" (the Hebrew has no question mark and here no question particle), and others take "know the name" as knowing what the name carries, which the following verses unfold (v6–8: "you shall know that I am Yahweh"). The verse does not decide; the words are kept as they stand.
- **"I have also established" → "I also established"** (v4) — Hebrew וְגַם הֲקִמֹתִי, "make stand" (Gen 17:7). **"the land of their pilgrimage, wherein they were strangers" → "the land of their sojournings, in which they sojourned"** — Hebrew מְגֻרֵיהֶם … גָּרוּ, noun and verb of one root.
- **"And I have also heard" → "And I myself also have heard"** (v5) — Hebrew וְגַם אֲנִי שָׁמַעְתִּי; the pronoun is written out. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (throughout) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"whom the Egyptians keep in bondage" → "whom the Egyptians are making serve"** (v5) — Hebrew מַעֲבִדִים, the causative (hiphil) of עָבַד "serve", a participle. "The Egyptians" is מִצְרַיִם "Egypt" with a plural verb: the people.
- **"the burdens of the Egyptians" → "the burdens of Egypt"** (v6, 7) — Hebrew סִבְלֹת מִצְרַיִם; סְבָלוֹת are loads carried, the burdens of forced work (1:11; 5:4). **"out of their bondage" → "from their service"** — Hebrew עֲבֹדָה, the noun of עָבַד "serve"; the same word in v9.
- **Seven verbs** (v6–8) — "I will bring out" (הוֹצֵאתִי), "I will deliver" (הִצַּלְתִּי), "I will redeem" (גָּאַלְתִּי), "I will take" (לָקַחְתִּי), "I will be" (הָיִיתִי), "I will bring" (הֵבֵאתִי), "I will give" (נָתַתִּי): each has its own word. גָּאַל "redeem" is the act of the kinsman who buys back what belongs to his family; הִצִּיל "deliver" is to snatch away.
- **"with a stretched out arm" → "with an outstretched arm"** (v6) — Hebrew בִּזְרוֹעַ נְטוּיָה (Deut 4:34; 5:15). **"great judgments"** — Hebrew שְׁפָטִים, acts of judgment (7:4; 12:12), a different noun from מִשְׁפָּט.
- **"I will take you to me for a people, and I will be to you a God" → "I will take you to myself for a people, and I will be *Elohim* to you"** (v7) — Hebrew לִי לְעָם … לָכֶם לֵאלֹהִים, literally "to me for a people … to you for Elohim". **"which bringeth you out"** — a participle, הַמּוֹצִיא.
- **"concerning the which I did swear" → "that I lifted up my hand"** (v8) — Hebrew נָשָׂאתִי אֶת־יָדִי "I lifted up my hand": the gesture of an oath (Gen 14:22; Deut 32:40). The Hebrew says the gesture, not the word "swear".
- **"for an heritage" → "as a possession"** (v8) — Hebrew מוֹרָשָׁה, from יָרַשׁ "possess, take possession of": a thing possessed. It is another word than אֲחֻזָּה "possession" (Gen 17:8) and נַחֲלָה "inheritance".
- **"for anguish of spirit, and for cruel bondage" → "because of shortness of spirit and because of hard service"** (v9) — Hebrew מִקֹּצֶר רוּחַ, literally "from shortness of spirit": impatience, a spirit that cannot hold out (Num 21:4; Job 21:4; Prov 14:29). רוּחַ is also "breath", so "shortness of breath" is possible. עֲבֹדָה קָשָׁה: קָשֶׁה "hard", the adjective of the verb in 7:3.
- **"they hearkened not" → "they did not listen"** (v9, 12, 30) — Hebrew שָׁמַע אֶל "listen to". In v12 the second verb has its object directly, יִשְׁמָעֵנִי "hear me"; in v30 it is again "listen to me" (יִשְׁמַע אֵלַי).
- **"that he let the children of Israel go" → "that he let the sons of Israel go"** (v11) — Hebrew וִישַׁלַּח, "and let him let go" (a wish form after the command).
- **"who am of uncircumcised lips" → "and I am uncircumcised of lips"** (v12, 30) — Hebrew וַאֲנִי עֲרַל שְׂפָתָיִם, literally "and I — uncircumcised of lips": lips that are closed, unfit for their work, as an "uncircumcised" ear cannot hear (Jer 6:10) and an "uncircumcised" heart cannot understand (Lev 26:41). In 4:10 Moses said "heavy of mouth and heavy of tongue". The Hebrew keeps the image; so does the verse.
- **"gave them a charge unto" → "gave them a command to"** (v13) — Hebrew וַיְצַוֵּם אֶל, literally "and he commanded them to the sons of Israel and to Pharaoh".
- **"the heads of their fathers' houses" kept** (v14) — Hebrew רָאשֵׁי בֵית־אֲבֹתָם, literally "the heads of the house of their fathers"; בֵּית אָבוֹת is the plural of בֵּית אָב "father's house", the kin group below the clan. In v25 the short form stands: רָאשֵׁי אֲבוֹת "the heads of the fathers". **"families" → "clans"** (v14, 15, 17, 19, 24, 25) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּחָה.
- **"a Canaanitish woman" → "the Canaanite woman"** (v15) — the Hebrew has the article (Gen 46:10).
- **"an hundred thirty and seven years" → "one hundred and thirty-seven years"** (v16, 18, 20) — the Hebrew order is "seven and thirty and a hundred years".
- **"the families of Levi" → "the clans of the Levites"** (v19) — Hebrew הַלֵּוִי, with the article: "the Levite", the tribe as a whole; in v25 the plural הַלְוִיִּם.
- **"his father's sister" → "his aunt"** (v20) — Hebrew דֹּדָתוֹ, the feminine of דּוֹד "uncle": an aunt (in Lev 18:14 an uncle's wife). Num 26:59 calls Jochebed "a daughter of Levi", which makes her the sister of Amram's father; the word here says only "aunt". Such a marriage is forbidden in Lev 18:12. **"took him … to wife" → "took … for himself as wife"** (v20, 23, 25) — Hebrew לוֹ לְאִשָּׁה.
- **"one of the daughters of Putiel"** (v25) — Hebrew מִבְּנוֹת "from the daughters of".
- **"These are that Aaron and Moses" → "That is the Aaron and Moses"** (v26, 27) — Hebrew הוּא אַהֲרֹן וּמֹשֶׁה, literally "he is Aaron and Moses", singular; in v27 the order is "Moses and Aaron". **"according to their armies" → "by their hosts"** (v26) — Hebrew עַל־צִבְאֹתָם; צָבָא is an army, an ordered multitude.
- **"These are they which spake" → "They are the ones who spoke"** (v27) — Hebrew הֵם הַמְדַבְּרִים, a participle: "they are the speakers".
- **"And it came to pass on the day when" kept** (v28) — Hebrew וַיְהִי בְּיוֹם דִּבֶּר. The Masoretic Text closes a paragraph after v28, but the sentence runs on into v29.
- **"all that I say unto thee" → "all that I am speaking to you"** (v29) — Hebrew דֹּבֵר, a participle of דִּבֶּר "speak".

### Notes

- **Renewed commission after disappointment** — ch 5 ended with failure; ch 6 reaffirms Yahweh's identity and intent with the sevenfold "I will" declaration (v6–8): "I will bring out… deliver… redeem… take… be… bring… give." A sevenfold promise anchors what happens next.
- **Insertion of genealogy (v14–25)** — interrupts the narrative to establish Moses' and Aaron's Levitical / priestly credentials. Purpose: to show that those Yahweh uses are from the tribe of Levi, and specifically Aaron's line will become the priestly line (Eleazar → Phinehas → high-priestly succession).
- **Name "Yahweh" (v3)** — the plague cycle (chs 7–12) becomes the extended demonstration of what the name means: "you shall know that I am Yahweh" (v7; 7:5, 17; 8:22; 10:2).
