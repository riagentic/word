# Exodus 10

[1] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Go in to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, for I myself have made his heart heavy, and the heart of his servants, so that I may set these signs of mine in his midst,

[2] and so that you may recount in the ears of your son and of your son's son how I have dealt ruthlessly with ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and my signs that I have set among them, and you shall know that I am ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[3] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ went in to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and said to him, "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, the ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ of the ⟦hebrews|Ivrim|Hebrews⟧: 'How long have you refused to humble yourself before me? Let my people go, that they may serve me.

[4] For if you refuse to let my people go, behold, tomorrow I am bringing locusts into your territory.

[5] And they shall cover the eye of the land, and one shall not be able to see the land. And they shall eat the rest of what has escaped, which is left to you from the hail, and they shall eat every tree that springs up for you from the field.

[6] And they shall fill your houses and the houses of all your servants and the houses of all ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, which your fathers and your fathers' fathers have not seen, from the day they were on the ground until this day.'" And he turned and went out from ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧.

[7] And the servants of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ said to him, "How long shall this one be a snare to us? Let the men go, that they may serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Do you not yet know that ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ has perished?"

[8] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ were brought back to ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, and he said to them, "Go, serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧. Who and who are the ones going?"

[9] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said, "With our young men and with our old men we will go; with our sons and with our daughters, with our flock and with our herd we will go, for we have a feast of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[10] And he said to them, "So may ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ be with you, as I let you and your little ones go! See, for evil is before your faces.

[11] Not so! Go, now, you grown men, and serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, for that is what you are seeking." And they were driven out from the face of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧.

[12] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Stretch out your hand over the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ for the locusts, that they may come up on the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and eat every plant of the land, all that the hail has left."

[13] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ stretched out his staff over the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ drove an east wind on the land all that day and all the night. The morning came, and the east wind had carried the locusts.

[14] And the locusts came up over all the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ and rested in all the territory of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, very heavy. Before them there were no such locusts as they, and after them there shall not be such.

[15] And they covered the eye of all the land, and the land was darkened, and they ate every plant of the land and all the fruit of the trees that the hail had left over. And nothing green remained on the trees or on the plants of the field in all the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[16] And ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ hastened to call for ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and for ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ and said, "I have sinned against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ and against you.

[17] And now forgive, please, my sin only this once, and plead with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ your ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, that he remove from me only this death."

[18] And he went out from ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ and pleaded with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[19] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ turned a very strong west wind, and it lifted the locusts and thrust them into the ⟦red-sea|Sea of Reeds|Red Sea⟧. Not one locust was left in all the territory of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧.

[20] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made strong the heart of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, and he did not let the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ go.

[21] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ said to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, "Stretch out your hand toward the heavens, that there may be darkness over the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧, and let darkness be felt."

[22] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ stretched out his hand toward the heavens, and there was a darkness of gloom in all the land of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧ three days.

[23] They did not see each man his brother, and they did not rise each man from his place three days. And for all the sons of ⟦israel|Yisrael|Israel⟧ there was light in their dwelling places.

[24] And ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ called to ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ and said, "Go, serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; only your flock and your herd shall be kept in place. Your little ones also shall go with you."

[25] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said, "You yourself also shall give into our hand sacrifices and burnt offerings, and we will make them for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[26] And our livestock also shall go with us; not a hoof shall be left, for from it we shall take to serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ our ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and we ourselves do not know with what we shall serve ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ until we come there."

[27] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ made strong the heart of ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧, and he was not willing to let them go.

[28] And ⟦pharaoh|Paroh|Pharaoh⟧ said to him, "Go from me! Take heed to yourself: do not see my face again, for on the day you see my face you shall die."

[29] And ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧ said, "So you have spoken. I will not see your face again any more."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — the Tetragrammaton. **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v3, 7, 8, 16, 17, 25, 26) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים.
- **"And"** (throughout) — the Hebrew joins its clauses with וַ / וְ "and"; the chain is kept. **"children of Israel" → "sons of Israel"** (v20, 23) — Hebrew בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל.
- **"for I have hardened his heart" → "for I myself have made his heart heavy"** (v1) — Hebrew כִּי־אֲנִי הִכְבַּדְתִּי אֶת־לִבּוֹ, the causative of כָּבֵד "be heavy", the verb that had Pharaoh as its subject in 8:15, 32; 9:34; here Yahweh says "I", with the pronoun written out. In v20 and v27 the verb is another: "Yahweh made strong (וַיְחַזֵּק) the heart of Pharaoh". See 7:3.
- **"that I might shew these my signs before him" → "so that I may set these signs of mine in his midst"** (v1) — Hebrew לְמַעַן שִׁתִי … בְּקִרְבּוֹ: שִׁית "set, put"; "in his midst" is singular (Pharaoh, or Egypt as one). In v2 "set among them" is שַׂמְתִּי בָם.
- **"that thou mayest tell" → "that you may recount"** (v2) — Hebrew תְּסַפֵּר. **"thy son's son"** — Hebrew בֶּן־בִּנְךָ. **"ye may know" → "you shall know"** — Hebrew וִידַעְתֶּם, plural: Moses and the people.
- **"what things I have wrought in Egypt" → "how I have dealt ruthlessly with Egypt"** (v2) — Hebrew הִתְעַלַּלְתִּי בְּ, "deal wantonly, ruthlessly with, make sport of" (Num 22:29; Judg 19:25; 1 Sam 6:6, there also of Egypt; 31:4). KJV "wrought" leaves the force out.
- **"How long wilt thou refuse" → "How long have you refused"** (v3) — Hebrew מֵאַנְתָּ, a perfect. **"to humble thyself before me"** — Hebrew לֵעָנֹת מִפָּנָי, the root of "afflict" (1:11, 12): "to be humbled from before my face".
- **"Else" → "For"** (v4) — Hebrew כִּי. **"I will bring the locusts into thy coast" → "I am bringing locusts into your territory"** — Hebrew הִנְנִי מֵבִיא, a participle. אַרְבֶּה "locust" is a collective singular in the whole chapter ("it shall cover … it shall eat"); English uses the plural. גְּבוּל is "border", and so the land within it (v4, 14, 19).
- **"the face of the earth" → "the eye of the land"** (v5, 15) — Hebrew עֵין הָאָרֶץ, literally "the eye of the land" (again Num 22:5, 11): what the eye sees of the land, its visible surface. The Hebrew picture is kept. אֶרֶץ is here the land of Egypt.
- **"the residue of that which is escaped, which remaineth unto you" → "the rest of what has escaped, which is left to you"** (v5) — Hebrew יֶתֶר הַפְּלֵטָה הַנִּשְׁאֶרֶת: three words for what is left over. **"which groweth" → "that springs up"** — Hebrew הַצֹּמֵחַ.
- **"the houses of all the Egyptians" → "the houses of all Egypt"** (v6) — Hebrew כָל־מִצְרַיִם. **"upon the earth" → "on the ground"** — Hebrew הָאֲדָמָה.
- **"How long shall this man be a snare" → "How long shall this one be a snare"** (v7) — Hebrew זֶה "this": Moses, or the matter. **"Egypt is destroyed" → "Egypt has perished"** — Hebrew אָבְדָה.
- **"but who are they that shall go?" → "Who and who are the ones going?"** (v8) — Hebrew מִי וָמִי הַהֹלְכִים, literally "who and who": name them one by one. The Hebrew has no "but".
- **"with our young and with our old" → "with our young men and with our old men"** (v9) — Hebrew נְעָרֵינוּ, זְקֵנֵינוּ. **"we must hold a feast unto the LORD" → "we have a feast of Yahweh"** — Hebrew חַג־יְהוָה לָנוּ, literally "a feast of Yahweh is to us"; חַג is a pilgrim feast.
- **"Let the LORD be so with you, as I will let you go" → "So may Yahweh be with you, as I let you … go"** (v10) — Hebrew יְהִי כֵן יְהוָה עִמָּכֶם כַּאֲשֶׁר אֲשַׁלַּח: said in scorn. **"look to it; for evil is before you" → "See, for evil is before your faces"** — Hebrew רְאוּ כִּי רָעָה נֶגֶד פְּנֵיכֶם. Either "you have evil in view" (you intend it) or "evil is in front of you" (it awaits you); the Hebrew allows both.
- **"go now ye that are men" → "Go, now, you grown men"** (v11) — Hebrew הַגְּבָרִים, another word than הָאֲנָשִׁים "the men" (v7): גֶּבֶר is a man in his strength, here the adult males as against the little ones (v10). The word list has "mighty man" for it; that would say "warriors" here. **"for that ye did desire" → "for that is what you are seeking"** — Hebrew מְבַקְשִׁים.
- **"they were driven out from Pharaoh's presence" → "they were driven out from the face of Pharaoh"** (v11) — Hebrew וַיְגָרֶשׁ אֹתָם, literally "and one drove them out", with no subject named; the verb of 6:1.
- **"for the locusts"** (v12) — Hebrew בָּאַרְבֶּה, "with / for the locust". **"herb" → "plant"** (v12, 15) — Hebrew עֵשֶׂב.
- **"the LORD brought an east wind" → "Yahweh drove an east wind"** (v13) — Hebrew נִהַג "drive, lead". **"when it was morning, the east wind brought the locusts" → "The morning came, and the east wind had carried the locusts"** — Hebrew הַבֹּקֶר הָיָה, "the morning was"; נָשָׂא "lift, carry", the verb of v19.
- **"rested in all the coasts of Egypt: very grievous" → "rested in all the territory of Egypt, very heavy"** (v14) — Hebrew וַיָּנַח, from נוּחַ "rest"; כָּבֵד מְאֹד "very heavy", the word of 9:3, 18, 24.
- **"all that the hail had left" → "that the hail had left over"** (v15) — Hebrew הוֹתִיר; in the next clause נוֹתַר "remained", of the same root; in v12 הִשְׁאִיר "left".
- **"called for Moses and Aaron in haste" → "hastened to call for Moses and for Aaron"** (v16) — Hebrew וַיְמַהֵר … לִקְרֹא.
- **"forgive, I pray thee, my sin only this once"** (v17) — Hebrew שָׂא נָא חַטָּאתִי, literally "lift, please, my sin"; the verb is singular (to Moses), "plead" is plural (to both). **"intreat the LORD" → "plead with Yahweh"** (v17, 18) — Hebrew הֶעְתִּיר, as in 8:8. **"that he may take away" → "that he remove"** — Hebrew וְיָסֵר, as in 8:8.
- **"a mighty strong west wind" → "a very strong west wind"** (v19) — Hebrew רוּחַ־יָם חָזָק מְאֹד, literally "a sea wind": the sea is the west for one who stands in Canaan. חָזָק "strong" is the word used of Pharaoh's heart in the next verse. **"cast them into the Red sea" → "thrust them into the Sea of Reeds"** — Hebrew וַיִּתְקָעֵהוּ "thrust, drove in"; יַם־סוּף *yam suf*, "sea of reeds". "Red Sea" comes from the Septuagint.
- **"the LORD hardened Pharaoh's heart" → "Yahweh made strong the heart of Pharaoh"** (v20, 27) — Hebrew וַיְחַזֵּק יְהוָה אֶת־לֵב פַּרְעֹה, as in 9:12.
- **"even darkness which may be felt" → "and let darkness be felt"** (v21) — Hebrew וְיָמֵשׁ חֹשֶׁךְ. The verb is commonly taken from מָשַׁשׁ "feel, grope" (Gen 27:12): "and let one feel darkness". The form is unusual and the sense is not certain; others take it from מוּשׁ "depart" or think of people groping in the dark.
- **"a thick darkness" → "a darkness of gloom"** (v22) — Hebrew חֹשֶׁךְ־אֲפֵלָה, two nouns: "darkness of deep gloom".
- **"They saw not one another, neither rose any from his place" → "They did not see each man his brother, and they did not rise each man from his place"** (v23) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֶת־אָחִיו … אִישׁ מִתַּחְתָּיו, literally "each man from under him". **"in their dwellings" → "in their dwelling places"** — Hebrew מוֹשְׁבֹתָם, from יָשַׁב "dwell".
- **"let your flocks and your herds be stayed" → "your flock and your herd shall be kept in place"** (v24) — Hebrew יֻצָּג, the passive of הִצִּיג "set, place" (Gen 33:15): "shall be set", made to stay where it is.
- **"Thou must give us also sacrifices" → "You yourself also shall give into our hand sacrifices"** (v25) — Hebrew גַּם־אַתָּה תִּתֵּן בְּיָדֵנוּ. **"that we may sacrifice" → "and we will make them"** — Hebrew וְעָשִׂינוּ, the verb עָשָׂה "do, make", used of preparing an offering.
- **"there shall not an hoof be left behind"** (v26) — Hebrew לֹא תִשָּׁאֵר פַּרְסָה; "behind" is not in the Hebrew. **"with what we must serve"** — Hebrew מַה־נַּעֲבֹד אֶת־יְהוָה, literally "what we shall serve Yahweh".
- **"he would not let them go" → "he was not willing to let them go"** (v27) — Hebrew וְלֹא אָבָה.
- **"Get thee from me" → "Go from me"** (v28) — Hebrew לֵךְ מֵעָלָי. **"take heed to thyself"** — Hebrew הִשָּׁמֶר לְךָ, literally "keep yourself". **"see my face no more" → "do not see my face again"** — Hebrew אַל־תֹּסֶף רְאוֹת, literally "do not add to see"; so in v29 לֹא־אֹסִף עוֹד "I will not add any more".
- **"Thou hast spoken well" → "So you have spoken"** (v29) — Hebrew כֵּן דִּבַּרְתָּ: כֵּן is "so" or "rightly".

### Notes

- **"That you may recount" (v2)** — the signs are done not only for Pharaoh and for Egypt but to be told to the sons and the sons' sons (12:26; 13:8, 14).
- **Pharaoh's offers** — the grown men only (v11); all, but without flock and herd (v24). Moses refuses both.
- **Light for Israel** (v23) — as with the swarm (8:22), the livestock (9:4) and the hail (9:26), the sons of Israel are set apart.
- **Last meeting** (v28–29) — the words of 11:4–8 are spoken before Moses goes out (11:8).
