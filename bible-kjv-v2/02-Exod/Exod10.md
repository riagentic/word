## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"coast" → "territory"** (v4, 14, 19) — Hebrew גְּבוּל *gevul* = border / territory. "Coast" in 1611 English = border; modern English = seashore, misleading.
- **"herb" → "plant"** (v12, 15) — Hebrew עֵשֶׂב *esev*. Modernized.
- **"Red sea" → "Sea of Reeds"** (v19) — Hebrew יַם־סוּף *Yam Suph*. *Suph* = reeds / rushes. Not strictly the modern "Red Sea" (which LXX and Vulgate rendered ἐρυθρά θάλασσα "Red Sea" — the name stuck). Probable ancient reference: the Sea of Reeds = an inland reed-marsh or shallow sea in the eastern Nile delta, possibly one of the Bitter Lakes. KJV's "Red Sea" is the venerable traditional rendering; "Sea of Reeds" is more accurate to the Hebrew name.
- **"east wind" (v13)** — Hebrew רוּחַ הַקָּדִים *ruach ha-qadim*. Hot, dry desert wind (sirocco) that can bring locust plagues from Arabia.
- **"stayed" → "left behind"** (v24) — Hebrew יֻצָּג *yutsag* = placed / stationed / kept behind.
- **"serve the LORD" — Pharaoh tries to divide the release** — let men only (10:11), leave livestock (10:24). Moses insists on complete release. The theological point: Yahweh's service is total; partial concessions are not sufficient.

### Notes

- **Ninth plague — darkness** — a direct blow to Ra, the Egyptian sun god, and a foreshadowing of the firstborn-death coming in ch 12. "Darkness that can be felt" suggests a dust / sandstorm so thick that even light cannot penetrate — or a supernatural darkness beyond normal phenomena.
- **Goshen exempt** (v23) — the light in Israel's dwellings reiterates the distinction made with flies (8:22).
- **Final rupture** (v28–29) — Pharaoh's threat and Moses' acceptance mark the last face-to-face meeting before the final plague. The firstborn-plague and exodus follow.
