# Exodus 38

[1] He made the altar of burnt offering of acacia wood: five cubits was its length, five cubits its width — it was square — and three cubits its height.

[2] He made its horns on its four corners; its horns were of one piece with it, and he overlaid it with bronze.

[3] He made all the utensils of the altar — the pots, the shovels, the basins, the flesh-hooks, and the fire-pans; all its utensils he made of bronze.

[4] He made for the altar a grating of bronze network, under its ledge, reaching halfway up from beneath.

[5] He cast four rings for the four corners of the bronze grating, as holders for the poles.

[6] He made the poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with bronze.

[7] He put the poles into the rings on the sides of the altar, to carry it by them; he made it hollow, with planks.

[8] He made the basin of bronze, with its stand of bronze, from the mirrors of the ministering women who ministered at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[9] He made the court: on the south side southward, the hangings of the court were of fine twined linen, 100 cubits;

[10] their pillars were twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands were of silver.

[11] For the north side, the hangings were 100 cubits, their pillars twenty, and their sockets twenty, of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands of silver.

[12] For the west side there were hangings of 50 cubits, their pillars ten, and their sockets ten; the hooks of the pillars and their bands of silver.

[13] For the east side eastward, 50 cubits.

[14] The hangings on one side of the gate were 15 cubits, with three pillars and three sockets;

[15] and for the other side — on both sides of the gate of the court — were hangings of 15 cubits, with three pillars and three sockets.

[16] All the hangings of the court around were of fine twined linen.

[17] The sockets for the pillars were of bronze; the hooks of the pillars and their bands, of silver; the overlaying of their tops, of silver; all the pillars of the court were banded with silver.

[18] The screen for the gate of the court was embroidered with needlework of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twined linen. Its length was 20 cubits, and its height in width was 5 cubits, corresponding to the hangings of the court.

[19] Their pillars were four, and their sockets four, of bronze; their hooks were of silver, and the overlaying of their tops and their bands, of silver.

[20] All the pegs of the dwelling and of the court round about were of bronze.

[21] This is the inventory of the dwelling of the Testimony — as they were counted at the command of ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧, for the service of the ⟦levites|Leviyim|Levites⟧, by the hand of ⟦ithamar|Itamar|Ithamar⟧ son of ⟦aaron|Aharon|Aaron⟧ the priest.

[22] ⟦bezalel|Betzalel|Bezalel⟧ son of Uri son of ⟦hur|Chur|Hur⟧, of the tribe of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, made all that ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ commanded ⟦moses|Moshe|Moses⟧;

[23] and with him was ⟦oholiab|Oholiav|Oholiab⟧ son of ⟦ahisamach|Achisamach|Ahisamach⟧, of the tribe of Dan, a craftsman and designer, and an embroiderer in blue, purple, and scarlet yarns and fine linen.

[24] All the gold that was used for the work, in all the work of the Holy Place — the gold of the elevation offering — was 29 talents and 730 shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary.

[25] The silver from those of the congregation who were numbered was 100 talents and 1,775 shekels, according to the shekel of the sanctuary:

[26] ⟦beka|a beqa|a beka⟧ per head (that is, half a shekel, according to the shekel of the sanctuary), for every one who passed over to the numbered, from twenty years old and upward — for 603,550 men.

[27] The 100 talents of silver were used for casting the sockets of the sanctuary and the sockets of the veil — 100 sockets for the 100 talents, a talent for a socket.

[28] Of the 1,775 shekels he made hooks for the pillars, overlaid their tops, and banded them.

[29] The bronze of the elevation offering was 70 talents and 2,400 shekels.

[30] With it he made the sockets for the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, the bronze altar, the bronze grating for it, and all the utensils of the altar;

[31] and the sockets of the court all around, the sockets of the gate of the court, all the pegs of the dwelling, and all the pegs of the court round about.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"lookingglasses" → "mirrors"** (v8) — Hebrew מַרְאֹת *mar'ot* = mirrors (polished bronze). Women donated their personal mirrors to make the basin — a striking gesture.
- **"bekah" (v26)** — half a shekel, specific weight unit.
- Parallels chs 27, 30.

### Notes

- **Inventory (v21–31)** — the accounting of tabernacle materials. Around 30 talents of gold, 100 talents of silver, 70 talents of bronze — representing over 1 ton of gold, 3 tons of silver, 2 tons of bronze. Massive material offering.
- **Women's mirrors become the priestly washing basin** — gendered piety contribution. Later Talmudic tradition tenderizes this detail: Moses had initially resisted using mirrors (thinking them tainted by vanity), but Yahweh insisted because they had been used by Hebrew women to beautify themselves for their husbands in Egypt, sustaining family life under oppression.
