# Exodus 29

[1] "This is what you shall do to them to consecrate them, to serve me as priests: Take one young bull and two rams without blemish,

[2] and unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil; of fine wheat flour you shall make them.

[3] You shall put them in one basket, and bring them in the basket — along with the bull and the two rams.

[4] You shall bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting, and wash them with water.

[5] Then you shall take the garments, and put on Aaron the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastpiece; and you shall gird him with the skillfully woven band of the ephod.

[6] You shall set the turban on his head, and put the holy diadem on the turban.

[7] You shall take the anointing oil and pour it on his head and anoint him.

[8] Then you shall bring his sons and put tunics on them,

[9] and you shall gird them with sashes — Aaron and his sons — and tie headdresses on them. The priesthood shall be theirs by a perpetual statute. Thus you shall ordain Aaron and his sons.

[10] You shall bring the bull before the Tent of Meeting; and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the bull.

[11] You shall slaughter the bull before Yahweh at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[12] You shall take some of the blood of the bull and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger, and pour all the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.

[13] You shall take all the fat that covers the entrails, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, and burn them on the altar.

[14] But the flesh of the bull, its hide, and its dung you shall burn with fire outside the camp; it is a sin offering.

[15] "Then you shall take one of the rams, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.

[16] You shall slaughter the ram and take its blood and throw it against the altar all around.

[17] You shall cut the ram into pieces, wash its entrails and its legs, and put them with its pieces and its head.

[18] You shall burn the whole ram on the altar; it is a burnt offering to Yahweh. It is a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to Yahweh.

[19] "You shall take the other ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.

[20] You shall slaughter the ram and take some of its blood and put it on the tip of the right ear of Aaron, and on the tip of the right ear of his sons, and on the thumb of their right hand, and on the big toe of their right foot; and you shall throw the rest of the blood against the altar all around.

[21] You shall take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and on the garments of his sons with him; and he shall be consecrated, he and his garments, and his sons and his sons' garments with him.

[22] You shall also take from the ram the fat, the fat tail, the fat that covers the entrails, the lobe of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, and the right thigh; for it is a ram of ordination.

[23] Take also one loaf of bread, one cake of bread made with oil, and one wafer out of the basket of unleavened bread that is before Yahweh;

[24] and you shall put all of this in the hands of Aaron and in the hands of his sons, and you shall wave them as a wave offering before Yahweh.

[25] Then you shall take them from their hands and burn them on the altar, on top of the burnt offering, for a pleasing aroma before Yahweh; it is an offering made by fire to Yahweh.

[26] You shall take the breast of the ram of Aaron's ordination and wave it as a wave offering before Yahweh; and it shall be your portion.

[27] You shall consecrate the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution offering — what was waved and what was contributed — from the ram of ordination, from that which is for Aaron and that which is for his sons.

[28] It shall be for Aaron and for his sons as a perpetual statute from the children of Israel; for it is a contribution. It shall be a contribution from the children of Israel out of the sacrifices of their peace offerings — their contribution to Yahweh.

[29] The holy garments of Aaron shall be for his sons after him; in them they shall be anointed and ordained.

[30] Seven days shall the son who is priest in his stead put them on, when he comes into the Tent of Meeting to minister in the Holy Place.

[31] "You shall take the ram of ordination and boil its flesh in a holy place.

[32] Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram and the bread that is in the basket at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.

[33] They shall eat those things by which atonement was made, for their ordination and their consecration; but no outsider shall eat of them, because they are holy.

[34] If any of the flesh of the ordination or any of the bread remains until morning, you shall burn the remainder with fire; it shall not be eaten, because it is holy.

[35] Thus you shall do for Aaron and his sons, according to all that I have commanded you; for seven days you shall ordain them.

[36] Every day you shall offer a bull as a sin offering for atonement; and you shall purify the altar by making atonement for it, and anoint it to consecrate it.

[37] Seven days you shall make atonement for the altar and consecrate it; then the altar shall be most holy — whatever touches the altar shall be holy.

[38] "This is what you shall offer on the altar: two lambs a year old, day by day, continually.

[39] One lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the other lamb you shall offer at twilight;

[40] and with the one lamb a tenth-measure of fine flour mixed with a fourth of a hin of beaten oil, and a fourth of a hin of wine for a drink offering.

[41] The other lamb you shall offer at twilight, and shall do with it according to the grain offering of the morning and according to its drink offering, for a pleasing aroma, an offering made by fire to Yahweh.

[42] It shall be a continual burnt offering throughout your generations, at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting before Yahweh, where I will meet you, to speak with you there.

[43] There I will meet with the children of Israel, and the place shall be consecrated by my glory.

[44] I will consecrate the Tent of Meeting and the altar; I will also consecrate Aaron and his sons to serve me as priests.

[45] I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel and will be their *Elohim*.

[46] They shall know that I am Yahweh their *Elohim*, who brought them out of the land of Egypt that I might dwell in their midst. I am Yahweh their *Elohim*."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (throughout) — Hebrew *Elohim*.
- **"hallow" → "consecrate"** (v1, 36) — Hebrew לְקַדֵּשׁ *le-qaddesh*. Modernized.
- **"caul that is above the liver" → "lobe of the liver"** (v13, 22) — Hebrew יֹתֶרֶת הַכָּבֵד *yoteret ha-kaved*. The lobe / fatty appendage of the liver — a specific anatomical part prescribed for sacrifice.
- **"sin offering" (v14, 36)** — Hebrew חַטָּאת *chatta't*. Technical sacrificial category for purification / unintentional sin.
- **"sweet savour" → "pleasing aroma"** (v18, 25, 41) — Hebrew רֵיחַ־נִיחוֹחַ *reach-nichoach*. As in Gen 8:21.
- **"rump" → "fat tail"** (v22) — Hebrew הָאַלְיָה *ha-alyah*. The fat-tail of Middle Eastern sheep (*Ovis aries*) — a specific sheep breed with heavy tail-fat. "Rump" misleads.
- **"shoulder" → "thigh"** (v22, 27) — Hebrew שׁוֹק *shoq* = thigh / hind leg.
- **"consecration" → "ordination"** (v22, 26, 27, 29, 31, 34, 35) — Hebrew מִלֻּאִים *millu'im* = "fillings" (of the hand — idiom for ordination, "filling the hand" = investing with priestly office). "Ordination" is more technical; "consecration" would be *qaddesh*.
- **"heave offering" → "contribution offering"** (v27, 28) — Hebrew תְּרוּמָה *terumah*. Traditional "heave offering" reflects an old rabbinical interpretation (a raised / lifted portion); modern scholars generally read as "contribution" / "donation / dedicated gift."
- **"wave offering" (v24, 26, 27)** — Hebrew תְּנוּפָה *tenuphah*. The offering was ceremonially waved / swung before Yahweh — either horizontal motion or actual lifting.
- **"stranger" → "outsider"** (v33) — Hebrew זָר *zar*. Non-priest. Distinct from *ger* (resident alien); *zar* = one outside the priestly office.
- **"meat offering" → "grain offering"** (v41) — Hebrew מִנְחָה *minchah*. "Meat" in 1611 English = food; here specifically grain offering (cereal / flour). Corrected.

### Notes

- **Seven-day ordination** — the installation of priests takes a full week, with daily sacrifices. The seven-day pattern echoes creation / tabernacle-completion motifs.
- **Blood on ear / thumb / toe (v20)** — consecration of hearing, acting, and walking — the high priest's three organs of ministry. Same ritual for a cleansed leper (Lev 14:14).
- **Daily continual burnt offering (v38–42)** — the *tamid* sacrifice: two lambs each day, morning and evening. Sustained liturgical rhythm that will organize Israel's daily worship life.
- **"I will dwell in their midst" (v45–46)** — the covenantal climax. The entire tabernacle project's goal: Yahweh dwelling among his people. Echoes 25:8 and anticipates John 1:14 (*eskēnōsen* = "tabernacled" among us).
