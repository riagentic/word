# Proverbs 19

[1] Better is a destitute one walking in his integrity
than one crooked in his lips, and he is a fool.

[2] Also, without knowledge a soul is not good,
and he who hastens with the feet sins.

[3] The folly of a man overturns his way,
and against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ his heart rages.

[4] Wealth adds many friends,
but the poor is parted from his friend.

[5] A witness of falsehoods will not be clear,
and he who breathes out lies will not escape.

[6] Many beseech the face of a noble,
and everyone is a friend to a man of gift.

[7] All the brothers of a destitute one hate him;
how much more have his friends gone far from him!
He pursues words — they are his.

[8] He who gets heart loves his soul;
he who keeps understanding will find good.

[9] A witness of falsehoods will not be clear,
and he who breathes out lies will perish.

[10] Not fitting for a fool is luxury;
how much less for a slave to rule over princes.

[11] The insight of a man makes him slow to anger,
and his beauty is to pass over a transgression.

[12] Like the growl of a young lion is the rage of a king,
and like dew on plants is his good pleasure.

[13] Destructions to his father is a foolish son,
and a continual dripping are the strifes of a wife.

[14] House and wealth are the inheritance of fathers,
but from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a wife who acts with insight.

[15] Sluggishness makes deep sleep fall,
and a soul of slackness will hunger.

[16] He who keeps the commandment keeps his soul;
he who despises his ways will die.

[17] One who lends to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is he who shows favor to the poor,
and his dealing he will repay to him.

[18] Discipline your son, for there is hope,
and to putting him to death do not lift up your soul.

[19] One great of fury bears a fine;
for if you deliver, you will add yet again.

[20] Hear counsel and receive discipline,
so that you may be wise in your latter end.

[21] Many are the plans in the heart of a man,
but the counsel of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, it shall stand.

[22] The desire of a man is his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
and better is a destitute one than a man of a lie.

[23] The fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is to life,
and sated he will lodge; he will not be visited with evil.

[24] The sluggard has hidden his hand in the dish;
even to his mouth he will not bring it back.

[25] A scoffer you strike, and the simple one becomes shrewd;
and reprove an understanding one, he will understand knowledge.

[26] He who devastates a father, who makes a mother flee,
is a son who brings shame and brings abashment.

[27] Cease, my son, to hear discipline,
to err from the sayings of knowledge.

[28] A witness ⟦belial|of Beliyyaal|of worthlessness⟧ mocks judgment,
and the mouth of the wicked swallows wickedness.

[29] Judgments are established for scoffers,
and beatings for the back of fools.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 14, 17, 21, 23) — Hebrew יהוה.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order, each with its וְ as the Hebrew has it; v7 has three lines, as the Hebrew.
- **"Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity, than he that is perverse in his lips" → "Better is a destitute one walking in his integrity than one crooked in his lips"** (v1) — Hebrew רָשׁ "destitute" (as v7, 22; 17:5); תֻּם "integrity"; עִקֵּשׁ "crooked" (as 17:20); וְהוּא כְסִיל "and he is a fool".
- **"Also, that the soul be without knowledge, it is not good; and he that hasteth with his feet sinneth" → "Also, without knowledge a soul is not good, and he who hastens with the feet sins"** (v2) — Hebrew גַּם "also"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (also read "desire"); בְּרַגְלַיִם "with the feet" (no "his"); חוֹטֵא "sins", the verb "miss the mark" (Judg 20:16): both senses lie in the word.
- **"perverteth his way: and his heart fretteth against the LORD" → "overturns his way, and against Yahweh his heart rages"** (v3) — Hebrew תְּסַלֵּף "overturns" (סִלֵּף, Exod 23:8); Hebrew order; יִזְעַף "rages" (זָעַף of anger, as 2 Chr 26:19).
- **"Wealth maketh many friends; but the poor is separated from his neighbour" → "Wealth adds many friends, but the poor is parted from his friend"** (v4) — Hebrew הוֹן "wealth" (עֹשֶׁר is "riches"); יֹסִיף "adds"; דַּל "poor"; יִפָּרֵד "is parted" (nifal of פָּרַד); מֵרֵעֵהוּ "from his friend".
- **"A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies" → "A witness of falsehoods will not be clear, and he who breathes out lies"** (v5, 9) — Hebrew עֵד שְׁקָרִים "a witness of falsehoods" (plural of שֶׁקֶר); לֹא יִנָּקֶה "will not be clear" (as 17:5); יָפִיחַ כְּזָבִים "breathes out lies" (כָּזָב "lie"). v5 and v9 differ only in the last word, as the Hebrew: יִמָּלֵט "will escape" / יֹאבֵד "will perish".
- **"Many will intreat the favour of the prince: and every man is a friend to him that giveth gifts" → "Many beseech the face of a noble, and everyone is a friend to a man of gift"** (v6) — Hebrew יְחַלּוּ פְנֵי "beseech the face of" (lit. "make the face soft"); נָדִיב "noble" (also "generous"); כָל־הָרֵעַ "every friend", the singular with the article; לְאִישׁ מַתָּן "to a man of gift".
- **"how much more do his friends go far from him? he pursueth them with words, yet they are wanting to him" → "how much more have his friends gone far from him! He pursues words — they are his"** (v7) — Hebrew אַף כִּי "how much more"; מְרֵעֵהוּ "his friend(s)" with a plural verb רָחֲקוּ "have gone far"; מְרַדֵּף אֲמָרִים "pursuing words" (no "them", no "with"); qere לוֹ הֵמָּה "to him they are" (= they are his: words are all he has); ketiv לא הֵמָּה "they are not" (= they are gone), which KJV follows. The third line is the only three-line saying here; its sense is not certain.
- **"He that getteth wisdom loveth his own soul" → "He who gets heart loves his soul"** (v8) — Hebrew קֹנֶה־לֵּב "gets heart" (heart = mind; not "wisdom"); שֹׁמֵר תְּבוּנָה "keeps understanding"; לִמְצֹא־טוֹב "will find good", lit. "to find good".
- **"Delight is not seemly for a fool; much less for a servant to have rule over princes" → "Not fitting for a fool is luxury; how much less for a slave to rule over princes"** (v10) — Hebrew order as 17:7 (לֹא־נָאוֶה … אַף כִּי); תַּעֲנוּג "luxury"; עֶבֶד "slave"; שָׂרִים "princes".
- **"The discretion of a man deferreth his anger; and it is his glory" → "The insight of a man makes him slow to anger, and his beauty"** (v11) — Hebrew שֵׂכֶל "insight"; הֶאֱרִיךְ אַפּוֹ, lit. "makes his anger long" (אַף "anger"; "long of anger" = slow to anger: "lengthens his anger" would read the opposite in English); תִּפְאַרְתּוֹ "his beauty" (as 17:6); עֲבֹר עַל־פָּשַׁע "to pass over a transgression".
- **"The king's wrath is as the roaring of a lion; but his favour is as dew upon the grass" → "Like the growl of a young lion is the rage of a king, and like dew on plants is his good pleasure"** (v12) — Hebrew נַהַם "growl" (not שָׁאַג "roar"); כְּפִיר "young lion"; זַעַף "rage"; עֵשֶׂב "plants"; רְצוֹנוֹ "his good pleasure" (as 18:22); Hebrew order. The first line is close to 20:2.
- **"A foolish son is the calamity of his father: and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping" → "Destructions to his father is a foolish son, and a continual dripping are the strifes of a wife"** (v13) — Hebrew הַוֺּת "destructions", plural as printed; Hebrew order; דֶלֶף טֹרֵד "a continual dripping"; מִדְיְנֵי "strifes" (the מָדוֹן word); אִשָּׁה "woman / wife".
- **"and a prudent wife is from the LORD" → "but from Yahweh is a wife who acts with insight"** (v14) — Hebrew מַשְׂכָּלֶת "acting with insight" (הִשְׂכִּיל, as 17:2); נַחֲלַת אָבוֹת "the inheritance of fathers".
- **"Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger" → "Sluggishness makes deep sleep fall, and a soul of slackness will hunger"** (v15) — Hebrew עַצְלָה "sluggishness" (עָצֵל "sluggard", v24); תַּפִּיל "makes fall"; תַּרְדֵּמָה "deep sleep"; נֶפֶשׁ רְמִיָּה "a soul of slackness" (רְמִיָּה is "treachery" in the Psalms; beside "sluggishness", and as כַּף רְמִיָּה "a slack palm" 10:4, the sense here is slackness); תִרְעָב "will hunger".
- **"He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; but he that despiseth his ways shall die" → "He who keeps the commandment keeps his soul; he who despises his ways will die"** (v16) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר twice "keeps"; no "but"; qere יָמוּת "will die" (ketiv יוּמָת "will be put to death").
- **"He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD; and that which he hath given will he pay him again" → "One who lends to Yahweh is he who shows favor to the poor, and his dealing he will repay to him"** (v17) — Hebrew מַלְוֵה יְהוָה חוֹנֵן דָּל, in the Hebrew order (the lender to Yahweh is named first); חָנַן "show favor"; גְמֻלוֹ "his dealing" (what he has done; the גָּמַל "deal" root); שִׁלֵּם "repay".
- **"Chasten thy son while there is hope, and let not thy soul spare for his crying" → "Discipline your son, for there is hope, and to putting him to death do not lift up your soul"** (v18) — Hebrew יַסֵּר "discipline"; כִּי "for"; הֲמִיתוֹ "putting him to death" (hifil of מוּת; KJV "his crying" takes it from הָמָה "moan"); נָשָׂא נֶפֶשׁ אֶל "lift up the soul to" (= set one's desire on).
- **"A man of great wrath shall suffer punishment: for if thou deliver him, yet thou must do it again" → "One great of fury bears a fine; for if you deliver, you will add yet again"** (v19) — Hebrew qere גְּדָל־חֵמָה "great of fury" (ketiv גרל, unclear); חֵמָה "fury"; נֹשֵׂא עֹנֶשׁ "bears a fine"; תַּצִּיל "deliver", no object; וְעוֹד תּוֹסִף "and you will add yet again" (= you will have to do it again).
- **"Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end" → "Hear counsel and receive discipline, so that you may be wise in your latter end"** (v20) — Hebrew מוּסָר "discipline"; לְמַעַן "so that"; אַחֲרִית "latter end".
- **"There are many devices in a man's heart; nevertheless the counsel of the LORD, that shall stand" → "Many are the plans in the heart of a man, but the counsel of Yahweh, it shall stand"** (v21) — Hebrew מַחֲשָׁבוֹת "plans" (root חָשַׁב "mean, devise"); וַעֲצַת "and / but the counsel"; הִיא תָקוּם "it shall stand".
- **"The desire of a man is his kindness" → "The desire of a man is his chesed"** (v22) — Hebrew חַסְדּוֹ, token as everywhere; תַּאֲוַת "the desire of" (the noun of craving, here of a good wish). The line may mean what a man desires (in others) or what makes him desirable; also read "his shame" (the חֶסֶד of Lev 20:17: the same letters), which the sense does not need. כָּזָב "lie".
- **"The fear of the LORD tendeth to life: and he that hath it shall abide satisfied; he shall not be visited with evil" → "The fear of Yahweh is to life, and sated he will lodge; he will not be visited with evil"** (v23) — Hebrew לְחַיִּים "to life"; no "he that hath it"; יָלִין "will lodge" (spend the night); בַּל־יִפָּקֶד רָע "he will not be visited with evil" (פָּקַד "visit").
- **"A slothful man hideth his hand in his bosom, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth again" → "The sluggard has hidden his hand in the dish; even to his mouth he will not bring it back"** (v24) — Hebrew טָמַן "has hidden", perfect; בַּצַּלָּחַת "in the dish" (not "bosom"); גַּם "even". 26:15 is close.
- **"Smite a scorner, and the simple will beware: and reprove one that hath understanding" → "A scoffer you strike, and the simple one becomes shrewd; and reprove an understanding one"** (v25) — Hebrew לֵץ תַּכֶּה, imperfect, in the Hebrew order (also "she strikes"); פֶתִי "simple one"; יַעְרִם "becomes shrewd" (the root of עָרְמָה "shrewdness"); וְהוֹכִיחַ "and reprove" (infinitive as command); נָבוֹן "an understanding one"; יָבִין דָּעַת "he will understand knowledge".
- **"He that wasteth his father, and chaseth away his mother, is a son that causeth shame, and bringeth reproach" → "He who devastates a father, who makes a mother flee, is a son who brings shame and brings abashment"** (v26) — Hebrew מְשַׁדֶּד "devastates" (שָׁדַד); אָב, אֵם without "his"; יַבְרִיחַ "makes flee" (בָּרַח "flee"); מֵבִישׁ "brings shame" (as 17:2); מַחְפִּיר "brings abashment" (hifil of חָפֵר "be abashed"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach" is another word).
- **"Cease, my son, to hear the instruction that causeth to err from the words of knowledge" → "Cease, my son, to hear discipline, to err from the sayings of knowledge"** (v27) — Hebrew חֲדַל … לִשְׁמֹעַ מוּסָר לִשְׁגּוֹת: two infinitives; the saying either warns (if you stop hearing discipline, you will err) or speaks with irony; KJV makes the second a clause about the discipline. שָׁגָה "err"; אִמְרֵי "sayings".
- **"An ungodly witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity" → "A witness of Beliyyaal mocks judgment, and the mouth of the wicked swallows wickedness"** (v28) — Hebrew עֵד בְּלִיַּעַל, token as elsewhere (a common noun, "worthlessness"); יָלִיץ "mocks" (הֵלִיץ); יְבַלַּע "swallows"; אָוֶן "wickedness".
- **"Judgments are prepared for scorners, and stripes for the back of fools" → "Judgments are established for scoffers, and beatings for the back of fools"** (v29) — Hebrew נָכוֹנוּ "are established" (נָכוֹן); שְׁפָטִים "judgments" (acts of judgment); מַהֲלֻמוֹת "beatings" (as 18:6); לְגֵו "for the back".

### Notes
- **Repeated sayings** — v5 and v9 (same words but the last); v12a ≈ 20:2a; v24 ≈ 26:15.
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew numbering is the same.
