# Proverbs 31

[1] The words of Lemuel, a king:
a burden with which his mother disciplined him.

[2] What, my son? And what, son of my belly?
And what, son of my vows?

[3] Do not give to women your ability,
and your ways to the wiping out of kings.

[4] Not for kings, Lemuel,
not for kings is it to drink wine,
and for dignitaries, "Where is strong drink?" —

[5] lest he drink and forget what is decreed,
and change the cause of all the sons of affliction.

[6] Give strong drink to one who is perishing,
and wine to the bitter of soul.

[7] Let him drink and forget his poverty,
and his toil let him remember no more.

[8] Open your mouth for the mute,
for the cause of all the sons of passing away.

[9] Open your mouth, judge righteousness,
and judge the afflicted and the needy.

[10] **א** A woman of ability, who can find?
And far beyond rubies is her price.

[11] **ב** The heart of her husband trusts in her,
and spoil he does not lack.

[12] **ג** She deals him good and not evil
all the days of her life.

[13] **ד** She seeks wool and flax,
and she works with the delight of her palms.

[14] **ה** She is like the ships of a trader;
from afar she brings her bread.

[15] **ו** And she rises while it is still night,
and gives food to her house,
and a fixed portion to her girls.

[16] **ז** She plans for a field and takes it;
from the fruit of her palms she plants a vineyard.

[17] **ח** She girds her loins with strength,
and strengthens her arms.

[18] **ט** She tastes that her trade is good;
her lamp does not go out in the night.

[19] **י** Her hands she stretches out to the distaff,
and her palms hold fast the spindle.

[20] **כ** Her palm she spreads out to the afflicted,
and her hands she stretches out to the needy.

[21] **ל** She does not fear for her house from the snow,
for all her house is clothed in scarlet.

[22] **מ** Coverings she makes for herself;
fine linen and purple are her clothing.

[23] **נ** Known in the gates is her husband,
when he sits with the elders of the land.

[24] **ס** A linen wrap she makes and sells,
and a sash she gives to the merchant.

[25] **ע** Strength and splendor are her clothing,
and she laughs at the day to come.

[26] **פ** Her mouth she opens in wisdom,
and ⟦torah|torah|teaching⟧ of ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ is on her tongue.

[27] **צ** She watches over the goings of her house,
and the bread of idleness she does not eat.

[28] **ק** Her sons rise up and call her happy;
her husband, and he praises her:

[29] **ר** "Many daughters have done ably,
but you have gone up over them all."

[30] **ש** Favor is falsehood, and beauty is vapor;
a woman who fears ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, she shall be praised.

[31] **ת** Give to her from the fruit of her hands,
and let her works praise her in the gates.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v30) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v1 is now split at the main accent (athnach). The bold letters in v10–31 mark the acrostic (Notes); they are not words of the text.
- **"The words of king Lemuel, the prophecy that his mother taught him" → "The words of Lemuel, a king: a burden with which his mother disciplined him"** (v1) — Hebrew דִּבְרֵי לְמוּאֵל מֶלֶךְ מַשָּׂא אֲשֶׁר־יִסְּרַתּוּ אִמּוֹ. The main accent stands on מֶלֶךְ, so the printed text reads "Lemuel, a king" and begins a new part with מַשָּׂא "a burden" (what is lifted up: an utterance, as 30:1; "oracle", "prophecy" in English Bibles). Read across the accent, מֶלֶךְ מַשָּׂא is "king of Massa" (the people of Gen 25:14); the words allow it, the accents do not, and the name stands nowhere else in Proverbs. יִסֵּר "discipline" (WISDOM.md: the root of מוּסָר); "with" is supplied (literally "which his mother disciplined him").
- **"What, my son? and what, the son of my womb? and what, the son of my vows?" → "What, my son? And what, son of my belly? And what, son of my vows?"** (v2) — Hebrew מַה … וּמַה … וּמֶה, with no verb (no "are you doing"); בַּר "son" three times, the Aramaic word (not בֵּן); בִּטְנִי "my belly" (WORDS-HE בֶּטֶן; not "womb", רֶחֶם); נְדָרָי "my vows".
- **"Give not thy strength unto women, nor thy ways to that which destroyeth kings" → "Do not give to women your ability, and your ways to the wiping out of kings"** (v3) — Hebrew חֵילְךָ "your ability" (the חַיִל row: also "strength, wealth"; the same word as v10, 29); וּדְרָכֶיךָ "and your ways" (the negative of the first line governs both); לַמְחוֹת, from מָחָה "wipe out" (WORDS-HE): "to the wiping out of" (or "to those who wipe out"); מְלָכִין "kings", with the Aramaic plural ending.
- **"It is not for kings, O Lemuel … nor for princes strong drink" → "Not for kings, Lemuel … and for dignitaries, 'Where is strong drink?'"** (v4) — No "O" (WORDS-HE vocative). The name is spelled לְמוֹאֵל here (v1 לְמוּאֵל). אַל "not" twice, no verb; שְׁתוֹ "to drink". רוֹזְנִים "dignitaries" (WORDS-HE). The written text (ketiv) has אוֹ "or" ("or for dignitaries strong drink"); the qere אֵי "where?" is translated: they are not to ask "Where is strong drink?". The qere is uncertain in sense (some take אֵי as "not"). שֵׁכָר "strong drink".
- **"Lest they drink, and forget the law, and pervert the judgment of any of the afflicted" → "lest he drink and forget what is decreed, and change the cause of all the sons of affliction"** (v5) — Hebrew singular verbs ("he": each king); מְחֻקָּק, pual participle of חָקַק "inscribe, decree" (root of חֹק "statute"): "what is decreed"; שִׁנָּה "change"; דִּין "cause"; בְּנֵי־עֹנִי "the sons of affliction" (עֳנִי).
- **"him that is ready to perish … unto those that be of heavy hearts" → "one who is perishing … the bitter of soul"** (v6) — Hebrew אוֹבֵד, participle of אָבַד "perish"; מָרֵי נֶפֶשׁ "the bitter of soul".
- **"Let him drink, and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more" → "Let him drink and forget his poverty, and his toil let him remember no more"** (v7) — Hebrew רִישׁוֹ "his poverty" (the root of רָשׁ "destitute", 29:13; 30:8); עֲמָלוֹ "his toil" (WORDS-HE), set first.
- **"in the cause of all such as are appointed to destruction" → "for the cause of all the sons of passing away"** (v8) — Hebrew אִלֵּם "mute"; אֶל־דִּין "for the cause"; בְּנֵי חֲלוֹף, literally "sons of passing away" (from חָלַף "pass on, pass away"): those who are perishing, or those left behind and helpless; the word stands only here.
- **"judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy" → "judge righteousness, and judge the afflicted and the needy"** (v9) — Hebrew שְׁפָט־צֶדֶק "judge righteousness" (שָׁפַט with צֶדֶק as object: give righteous judgment); וְדִין, the verb דִּין "judge" (English has no second plain word: WORDS-HE); עָנִי "afflicted", אֶבְיוֹן "needy".
- **"Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies" → "A woman of ability, who can find? And far beyond rubies is her price"** (v10) — Hebrew אֵשֶׁת־חַיִל "a woman of ability" (the חַיִל row, as אַנְשֵׁי חַיִל "men of ability", Exod 18:21: capable, strong, worthy; "virtuous" and "excellent" narrow it); אִשָּׁה is "woman" and, of a married woman, "wife" (one word: v11, 23, 28 name her husband); the order is kept, with "and" (no "for"). פְּנִינִים "rubies" (WORDS-HE; uncertain, "pearls" in other Bibles); מִכְרָהּ "her price" (from מָכַר "sell").
- **"doth safely trust in her, so that he shall have no need of spoil" → "trusts in her, and spoil he does not lack"** (v11) — Hebrew בָּטַח "trust"; בַּעְלָהּ "her husband" (בַּעַל, WORDS-HE); שָׁלָל "spoil" (gain); חָסֵר "lack".
- **"She will do him good and not evil" → "She deals him good and not evil"** (v12) — Hebrew גְּמָלַתְהוּ, from גָּמַל "deal" (WORDS-HE).
- **"worketh willingly with her hands" → "she works with the delight of her palms"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּחֵפֶץ כַּפֶּיהָ "with the delight of her palms" (חֵפֶץ "delight"; כַּף "palm", beside יָד "hand", v19, 20, 31); פִּשְׁתִּים "flax", the raw stuff (the WORDS-HE row gives "linen" for cloth, Lev 13).
- **"She is like the merchants' ships; she bringeth her food from afar" → "She is like the ships of a trader; from afar she brings her bread"** (v14) — Hebrew סוֹחֵר "trader", singular; לַחְמָהּ "her bread".
- **"She riseth also while it is yet night, and giveth meat to her household, and a portion to her maidens" → "And she rises while it is still night, and gives food to her house, and a fixed portion to her girls"** (v15) — Hebrew וַתָּקָם "and she rises"; טֶרֶף "food" (literally "prey": WORDS-HE); בֵּיתָהּ "her house" (so v21, 27); חֹק "fixed portion" (as 30:8; Gen 47:22); נַעֲרֹתֶיהָ "her girls" (attendants: WORDS-HE).
- **"She considereth a field, and buyeth it" → "She plans for a field and takes it"** (v16) — Hebrew זָמְמָה, from זָמַם "devise" (as 30:32): she sets her purpose on it; "devises a field" cannot stand in English. וַתִּקָּחֵהוּ "and takes it" (לָקַח "take"; buying is not named). נָטְעָה qere "she plants"; the written form נטע can be read "he plants" or "planting". כַּפֶּיהָ "her palms".
- **"She girdeth her loins with strength" → kept** (v17) — Hebrew חָגְרָה בְעוֹז מָתְנֶיהָ; עֹז "strength"; וַתְּאַמֵּץ "and strengthens" (אִמֵּץ, another root: English has one word).
- **"She perceiveth that her merchandise is good: her candle goeth not out by night" → "She tastes that her trade is good; her lamp does not go out in the night"** (v18) — Hebrew טָעֲמָה "she tastes" (= finds by trying); סַחְרָהּ "her trade" (WORDS-HE; root of סוֹחֵר, v14); בַלַּיְלָה qere "in the night" (the written בליל is a shorter form of the same word); נֵר "lamp".
- **"She layeth her hands to the spindle, and her hands hold the distaff" → "Her hands she stretches out to the distaff, and her palms hold fast the spindle"** (v19) — Hebrew שִׁלְּחָה יָדֶיהָ "she stretches out her hands" (as v20); כִּישׁוֹר (only here): the distaff or the whorl of the spindle, uncertain; כַּפֶּיהָ "her palms"; תָּמְכוּ "hold fast" (תָּמַךְ, WORDS-HE); פֶּלֶךְ "spindle" (WORDS-HE; KJV has the two words the other way round).
- **"She stretcheth out her hand to the poor; yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy" → "Her palm she spreads out to the afflicted, and her hands she stretches out to the needy"** (v20) — Hebrew כַּפָּהּ פָּרְשָׂה "her palm she spreads out"; עָנִי "afflicted" (not "poor"); וְיָדֶיהָ שִׁלְּחָה "and her hands she stretches out", the verb of v19.
- **"She is not afraid of the snow for her household: for all her household are clothed with scarlet" → "She does not fear for her house from the snow, for all her house is clothed in scarlet"** (v21) — Hebrew לְבֵיתָהּ מִשָּׁלֶג "for her house from the snow"; שָׁנִים "scarlet" (plural: scarlet stuffs; pointed otherwise the letters give "double", שְׁנַיִם).
- **"She maketh herself coverings of tapestry; her clothing is silk and purple" → "Coverings she makes for herself; fine linen and purple are her clothing"** (v22) — Hebrew מַרְבַדִּים "coverings" (spreads, as 7:16); שֵׁשׁ "fine linen" (not "silk"); אַרְגָּמָן "purple".
- **"when he sitteth among the elders of the land" → "when he sits with the elders of the land"** (v23) — Hebrew עִם "with"; זִקְנֵי־אָרֶץ "the elders of the land"; נוֹדָע "known" first.
- **"She maketh fine linen, and selleth it; and delivereth girdles unto the merchant" → "A linen wrap she makes and sells, and a sash she gives to the merchant"** (v24) — Hebrew סָדִין "linen wrap" and חֲגוֹר "sash", both singular (each may stand for many); וַתִּמְכֹּר "and sells"; נָתְנָה "gives"; לַכְּנַעֲנִי "to the merchant" (literally "the Canaanite": the trader, as Job 41:6; Isa 23:8; WORDS-HE).
- **"Strength and honour are her clothing; and she shall rejoice in time to come" → "Strength and splendor are her clothing, and she laughs at the day to come"** (v25) — Hebrew עֹז "strength" (v17); הָדָר "splendor"; וַתִּשְׂחַק "and she laughs" (שָׂחַק, not "rejoice"); לְיוֹם אַחֲרוֹן "at a later day", "the day to come".
- **"and in her tongue is the law of kindness" → "and torah of chesed is on her tongue"** (v26) — Hebrew תּוֹרַת־חֶסֶד: the `torah` token, as of a parent's teaching (WISDOM.md; "teaching" in traditional mode), and the `steadfast-love` token for חֶסֶד (WORDS-HE, every place). Whether it is teaching that is full of chesed or teaching about chesed the construct does not say. עַל־לְשׁוֹנָהּ "on her tongue". בְחָכְמָה "in wisdom"; פִּיהָ "her mouth", first.
- **"She looketh well to the ways of her household" → "She watches over the goings of her house"** (v27) — Hebrew צוֹפִיָּה, participle of צָפָה "watch"; הֲלִיכוֹת "goings" (from הָלַךְ); עַצְלוּת "idleness".
- **"Her children arise up, and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praiseth her" → "Her sons rise up and call her happy; her husband, and he praises her"** (v28) — Hebrew בָּנֶיהָ "her sons" (WORDS-HE); וַיְאַשְּׁרוּהָ, from אִשֵּׁר "call happy" (the root of אַשְׁרֵי "happy"; not "blessed": בָּרַךְ); בַּעְלָהּ with no "also".
- **"Many daughters have done virtuously, but thou excellest them all" → "Many daughters have done ably, but you have gone up over them all"** (v29) — Hebrew עָשׂוּ חָיִל, literally "have made ability": the חַיִל of v10 (as עָשָׂה חַיִל "do valiantly", Ps 60:12, and "get wealth", Deut 8:17, 18); בָּנוֹת "daughters"; עָלִית עַל "you have gone up over" (עָלָה). The quotation marks show his words (v28 "and he praises her").
- **"Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain" → "Favor is falsehood, and beauty is vapor"** (v30) — Hebrew שֶׁקֶר הַחֵן וְהֶבֶל הַיֹּפִי, two nouns without a verb: שֶׁקֶר "falsehood", חֵן "favor" (WORDS-HE; KJV "favour"), הֶבֶל "vapor" (a breath; "vain" in KJV, "vanity" traditionally). **"but a woman that feareth the LORD, she shall be praised" → "a woman who fears Yahweh, she shall be praised"** — Hebrew אִשָּׁה יִרְאַת־יְהוָה, literally "a woman of the fear of Yahweh"; no "but"; תִתְהַלָּל, hitpael of הִלֵּל: "she shall be praised" (or "she shall glory").
- **"let her own works praise her" → "let her works praise her"** (v31) — Hebrew מַעֲשֶׂיהָ "her works" ("own" is not written); מִפְּרִי יָדֶיהָ "from the fruit of her hands".

### Notes
- **Lemuel** (v1, 4) — known only from this chapter; his mother's words (v2–9).
- **v10–31** — an alphabetic acrostic: each verse begins with the next letter of the Hebrew alphabet, א (v10 אֵשֶׁת) to ת (v31 תְּנוּ), all twenty-two in order; the bold letters show the letter of each verse. The translation does not imitate the scheme.
