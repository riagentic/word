# Proverbs 18

[1] After craving seeks one who separates himself;
against all sound wisdom he breaks out.

[2] A fool does not delight in understanding,
but rather in the uncovering of his heart.

[3] When a wicked one comes, contempt also comes,
and with disgrace, reproach.

[4] Deep waters are the words of the mouth of a man;
a gushing torrent is the fountain of wisdom.

[5] To lift up the face of a wicked one is not good,
to turn aside a righteous one in judgment.

[6] The lips of a fool come into contention,
and his mouth calls for beatings.

[7] The mouth of a fool is a ruin to him,
and his lips are the snare of his soul.

[8] The words of a murmurer are like delicacies,
and they go down into the chambers of the belly.

[9] Also one who shows himself slack in his work —
a brother is he to a destroyer.

[10] A tower of strength is the name of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
into it the righteous runs and is set on high.

[11] The wealth of a rich man is the city of his strength,
and like a wall set on high in his imagination.

[12] Before breaking the heart of a man is high,
and before glory is humility.

[13] He who returns a word before he hears —
it is folly to him, and dishonor.

[14] The spirit of a man sustains his sickness,
but a stricken spirit — who can bear it?

[15] An understanding heart gets knowledge,
and the ear of the wise seeks knowledge.

[16] The gift of a man makes room for him,
and leads him before great ones.

[17] Righteous is the first in his case,
and his neighbor comes and searches him out.

[18] The lot makes strifes cease,
and parts between mighty ones.

[19] A brother transgressed against is more than a city of strength,
and strifes are like the bar of a citadel.

[20] From the fruit of the mouth of a man his belly is sated;
with the yield of his lips he is sated.

[21] Death and life are in the hand of the tongue,
and those who love it will eat its fruit.

[22] He who has found a wife has found good,
and has obtained good pleasure from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[23] Pleas for favor the destitute speaks,
but the rich man answers harshly.

[24] A man of friends is for ruin,
but there is one who loves, clinging more than a brother.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v10, 22) — Hebrew יהוה.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order, each with its וְ as the Hebrew has it.
- **"Through desire a man, having separated himself, seeketh and intermeddleth with all wisdom" → "After craving seeks one who separates himself; against all sound wisdom he breaks out"** (v1) — Hebrew לְתַאֲוָה יְבַקֵּשׁ נִפְרָד, Hebrew order; לְ "for, after"; תַּאֲוָה "craving" (no "his"); נִפְרָד "one who separates himself" (nifal of פָּרַד); תּוּשִׁיָּה "sound wisdom" (uncertain); יִתְגַּלָּע "he breaks out" (as 17:14; 20:3). The line is hard; the versions read it differently.
- **"but that his heart may discover itself" → "but rather in the uncovering of his heart"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי אִם־בְּהִתְגַּלּוֹת לִבּוֹ, "but rather in his heart's uncovering itself" (hitpael of גָּלָה); חָפֵץ בְּ "delight in"; תְּבוּנָה "understanding".
- **"When the wicked cometh … and with ignominy reproach" → "When a wicked one comes … and with disgrace, reproach"** (v3) — Hebrew רָשָׁע without the article; בּוּז "contempt"; קָלוֹן "disgrace"; חֶרְפָּה "reproach". The line has no verb.
- **"The words of a man's mouth are as deep waters, and the wellspring of wisdom as a flowing brook" → "Deep waters are the words of the mouth of a man; a gushing torrent is the fountain of wisdom"** (v4) — Hebrew has no "as"; Hebrew order; נַחַל נֹבֵעַ "a gushing torrent" (נַחַל "wadi", here of the water itself); מְקוֹר "fountain"; no "and".
- **"to accept the person of the wicked, to overthrow the righteous in judgment" → "To lift up the face of a wicked one … to turn aside a righteous one in judgment"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׂאֵת פְּנֵי "to lift up the face of" (= to show partiality to; kept); לְהַטּוֹת "to turn aside" (as 17:23).
- **"calleth for strokes" → "calls for beatings"** (v6) — Hebrew לְמַהֲלֻמוֹת (also 19:29); בְרִיב "into contention".
- **"A fool's mouth is his destruction" → "The mouth of a fool is a ruin to him"** (v7) — Hebrew מְחִתָּה־לוֹ "a ruin to him"; מוֹקֵשׁ "snare"; נַפְשׁוֹ "his soul".
- **"The words of a talebearer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly" → "The words of a murmurer are like delicacies, and they go down into the chambers of the belly"** (v8) — Hebrew נִרְגָּן "murmurer" (the root of נִרְגַּן "murmur", Deut 1:27; one who whispers against others; not רָכִיל "talebearer"); כְּמִתְלַהֲמִים only here and 26:22: "like things swallowed greedily", "delicacies" (uncertain; KJV "wounds" is a guess); וְהֵם "and they"; חַדְרֵי־בָטֶן "the chambers of the belly" (also 20:27, 30). The same saying stands in 26:22.
- **"He also that is slothful in his work is brother to him that is a great waster" → "Also one who shows himself slack in his work — a brother is he to a destroyer"** (v9) — Hebrew גַּם "also"; מִתְרַפֶּה "shows himself slack" (hitpael of רָפָה); בַּעַל מַשְׁחִית "a destroyer", lit. "an owner of destroying" (בַּעַל + noun: one marked by it).
- **"The name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe" → "A tower of strength is the name of Yahweh; into it the righteous runs and is set on high"** (v10) — Hebrew מִגְדַּל־עֹז "a tower of strength"; Hebrew order; וְנִשְׂגָּב "and is set on high" (nifal of שָׂגַב, root of מִשְׂגָּב "secure height").
- **"his strong city, and as an high wall in his own conceit" → "The wealth of a rich man is the city of his strength, and like a wall set on high in his imagination"** (v11) — Hebrew הוֹן "wealth" (עֹשֶׁר is "riches"); קִרְיַת עֻזּוֹ "the city of his strength" (as 10:15); נִשְׂגָּבָה "set on high" (as v10); בְּמַשְׂכִּיתוֹ "in his imagination" (מַשְׂכִּית is a carved figure elsewhere, Lev 26:1; here an image of the mind).
- **"Before destruction the heart of man is haughty, and before honour is humility" → "Before breaking the heart of a man is high, and before glory is humility"** (v12) — Hebrew שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as 17:19; 16:18); יִגְבַּהּ "is high"; לֵב־אִישׁ; כָבוֹד "glory"; עֲנָוָה "humility".
- **"He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him" → "He who returns a word before he hears — it is folly to him, and dishonor"** (v13) — Hebrew מֵשִׁיב דָּבָר "returns a word" (= answers); כְּלִמָּה "dishonor" (as the Psalms).
- **"will sustain his infirmity; but a wounded spirit who can bear?" → "sustains his sickness, but a stricken spirit — who can bear it?"** (v14) — Hebrew יְכַלְכֵּל "sustains"; מַחֲלֵהוּ "his sickness"; רוּחַ נְכֵאָה "a stricken spirit" (as 17:22); יִשָּׂאֶנָּה "can bear it" (lit. "will lift it").
- **"The heart of the prudent getteth knowledge" → "An understanding heart gets knowledge"** (v15) — Hebrew לֵב נָבוֹן, adjective with "heart"; יִקְנֶה "gets".
- **"bringeth him before great men" → "leads him before great ones"** (v16) — Hebrew יַנְחֶנּוּ "leads him" (נָחָה); גְדֹלִים "great ones"; מַתָּן "gift"; יַרְחִיב לוֹ "makes room for him".
- **"He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him" → "Righteous is the first in his case, and his neighbor comes and searches him out"** (v17) — Hebrew צַדִּיק "righteous" (no "seems"); בְּרִיבוֹ "in his case"; qere וּבָא "and comes" (ketiv יבא "comes"); חָקַר "search out"; רֵעֵהוּ "his neighbor" (the other party).
- **"The lot causeth contentions to cease, and parteth between the mighty" → "The lot makes strifes cease, and parts between mighty ones"** (v18) — Hebrew מִדְיָנִים "strifes" (plural of the מָדוֹן word); יַשְׁבִּית "makes cease"; עֲצוּמִים "mighty ones"; יַפְרִיד "parts".
- **"A brother offended is harder to be won than a strong city: and their contentions are like the bars of a castle" → "A brother transgressed against is more than a city of strength, and strifes are like the bar of a citadel"** (v19) — Hebrew אָח נִפְשָׁע "a brother transgressed against" (nifal of פָּשַׁע); מִקִּרְיַת־עֹז "more than / from a city of strength": the line has no verb or adjective, and "harder to be won" is a guess at its sense; qere וּמִדְיָנִים "and strifes" (ketiv ומדונים, the same word); no "their"; כִּבְרִיחַ "like the bar", singular; אַרְמוֹן "citadel".
- **"A man's belly shall be satisfied with the fruit of his mouth; and with the increase of his lips shall he be filled" → "From the fruit of the mouth of a man his belly is sated; with the yield of his lips he is sated"** (v20) — Hebrew מִפְּרִי "from the fruit"; one verb שָׂבַע "be sated" twice; תְּבוּאַת "yield".
- **"in the power of the tongue" → "in the hand of the tongue"** (v21) — Hebrew בְּיַד־לָשׁוֹן, "hand" kept (= power).
- **"Whoso findeth a wife findeth a good thing, and obtaineth favour of the LORD" → "He who has found a wife has found good, and has obtained good pleasure from Yahweh"** (v22) — Hebrew perfects מָצָא … מָצָא and וַיָּפֶק; טוֹב "good"; רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (חֵן is "favor"). אִשָּׁה "woman / wife", one word.
- **"The poor useth intreaties; but the rich answereth roughly" → "Pleas for favor the destitute speaks, but the rich man answers harshly"** (v23) — Hebrew order; תַּחֲנוּנִים "pleas for favor"; יְדַבֶּר "speaks"; רָשׁ "destitute"; עַזּוֹת "harshly", lit. "strong things".
- **"A man that hath friends must shew himself friendly: and there is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother" → "A man of friends is for ruin, but there is one who loves, clinging more than a brother"** (v24) — Hebrew אִישׁ רֵעִים "a man of friends"; לְהִתְרֹעֵעַ "to be broken, for ruin" (from רָעַע "break"; KJV takes it from the root of רֵעַ "friend": "to keep company"; uncertain); אֹהֵב "one who loves"; דָּבֵק "clinging" (דָּבַק).

### Notes
- **Repeated sayings** — v8 = 26:22 (the same words).
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew numbering is the same.
