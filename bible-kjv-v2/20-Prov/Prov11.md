# Proverbs 11

[1] Scales of deceit are an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but a whole weight is his good pleasure.

[2] Presumption comes, and disgrace comes;
but with the modest is wisdom.

[3] The integrity of the upright leads them,
but the perverseness of the treacherous devastates them.

[4] Wealth does not profit in the day of raging,
but righteousness delivers from death.

[5] The righteousness of the blameless makes his way straight,
but by his wickedness the wicked falls.

[6] The righteousness of the upright delivers them,
but by the desire of the treacherous they are captured.

[7] In the death of a wicked man hope perishes,
and the expectation of vigor has perished.

[8] The righteous is freed from distress,
and the wicked comes in his place.

[9] With the mouth the godless one ruins his neighbor,
but by knowledge the righteous are freed.

[10] In the good of the righteous a town is jubilant,
and in the perishing of the wicked is a ringing cry.

[11] By the blessing of the upright a town rises high,
but by the mouth of the wicked it is torn down.

[12] He who despises his neighbor is lacking heart,
but a man of understandings keeps silent.

[13] He who goes about as a talebearer uncovers secret counsel,
but the faithful of spirit covers a matter.

[14] Where there is no steering, a people falls,
but salvation is in an abundance of counselors.

[15] He will surely suffer evil, for he has been surety for a stranger,
but he who hates striking hands is secure.

[16] A woman of favor holds fast glory,
and the ruthless hold fast riches.

[17] Dealing well with his soul is a man of ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
but troubling his flesh is a cruel one.

[18] The wicked makes a wage of falsehood,
but he who sows righteousness, a reward of truth.

[19] He who is honest in righteousness is for life,
but he who pursues evil, for his death.

[20] An abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are the crooked of heart,
but his good pleasure are the blameless of way.

[21] Surely, the evil one will not be clear,
but the seed of the righteous escapes.

[22] A ring of gold in the nose of a pig —
a beautiful woman, and turned aside from sense.

[23] The desire of the righteous is only good;
the hope of the wicked is raging.

[24] There is one who scatters, and is increased still,
and one who withholds more than is right — only to want.

[25] A soul of blessing will be made fat,
and he who waters, he also will be watered.

[26] He who holds back grain, the folk will curse him,
but blessing is for the head of him who sells it.

[27] He who eagerly seeks good seeks good pleasure,
but he who seeks evil, it will come to him.

[28] He who trusts in his riches, he will fall,
but like the leaf the righteous will bud.

[29] He who troubles his house will inherit wind,
and the senseless one is a slave to the wise of heart.

[30] The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life,
and he who takes souls is wise.

[31] Behold, the righteous will be repaid on the earth;
how much more the wicked and the sinner!

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 20) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Joins** (throughout) — "but" or "and" only where the Hebrew has וְ (as ch. 10); v23 has none.
- **"A false balance is abomination to the LORD" → "Scales of deceit are an abomination to Yahweh"** (v1) — Hebrew מֹאזְנֵי מִרְמָה, "scales (a pair of pans) of deceit"; תּוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה, literally "an abomination of Yahweh" (also v20; 12:22). **"a just weight is his delight" → "a whole weight is his good pleasure"** — אֶבֶן שְׁלֵמָה, "a whole stone" (a weight of full measure, as Deut 25:15); רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (also v20, 27; 12:2, 22).
- **"When pride cometh, then cometh shame" → "Presumption comes, and disgrace comes"** (v2) — Hebrew בָּא־זָדוֹן וַיָּבֹא קָלוֹן, two main verbs joined by "and" (a perfect, then the narrative form, of what always happens); no "when … then". זָדוֹן "presumption"; קָלוֹן "disgrace". **"the lowly" → "the modest"** — צְנוּעִים (only here; the verb in Mic 6:8 "walk modestly").
- **"shall guide them" → "leads them"** (v3) — Hebrew תַּנְחֵם, נָחָה "lead". **"the perverseness of transgressors shall destroy them" → "the perverseness of the treacherous devastates them"** — סֶלֶף (noun of סִלֵּף "overturn"); בֹּגְדִים "the treacherous" (בָּגַד "deal treacherously"), not "transgressors" (פֶּשַׁע). The written form ושדם and the qere יְשָׁדֵּם are the same verb שָׁדַד "devastate" in two forms; the qere is read.
- **"Riches profit not in the day of wrath" → "Wealth does not profit in the day of raging"** (v4) — Hebrew הוֹן "wealth" (עֹשֶׁר "riches" stands in v16, 28); עֶבְרָה "raging" (overflowing wrath). The second line = 10:2b.
- **"shall direct his way" → "makes his way straight"** (v5) — Hebrew תְּיַשֵּׁר דַּרְכּוֹ, piel of יָשַׁר. **"the wicked shall fall by his own wickedness" → "by his wickedness the wicked falls"** — Hebrew order; no "own".
- **"transgressors shall be taken in their own naughtiness" → "by the desire of the treacherous they are captured"** (v6) — Hebrew וּבְהַוַּת בֹּגְדִים יִלָּכֵדוּ, in this order: הַוָּה "desire" as 10:3; לָכַד nifal "be captured". "They" are the treacherous themselves.
- **"When a wicked man dieth, his expectation shall perish" → "In the death of a wicked man hope perishes"** (v7) — Hebrew בְּמוֹת אָדָם רָשָׁע תֹּאבַד תִּקְוָה, "hope" without "his"; אָדָם "man". **"the hope of unjust men perisheth" → "the expectation of vigor has perished"** — וְתוֹחֶלֶת אוֹנִים אָבָדָה, a perfect. אוֹנִים is uncertain: the plural of אוֹן "vigor, wealth" (as Ps 105:36 "vigor"), or of אָוֶן "wickedness" ("wicked men", KJV "unjust men").
- **"is delivered out of trouble" → "is freed from distress"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew נֶחֱלָץ / יֵחָלֵצוּ, חָלַץ nifal "be freed" (הִצִּיל "deliver" stands in v4, 6); צָרָה "distress". **"in his stead" → "in his place"** — תַּחְתָּיו. **"and the wicked cometh"** — וַיָּבֹא, "and comes".
- **"An hypocrite with his mouth destroyeth his neighbour" → "With the mouth the godless one ruins his neighbor"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּפֶה, "with a mouth" (no "his"); חָנֵף "godless" (literally "polluted, profane"), not "hypocrite"; יַשְׁחִית "ruins".
- **"When it goeth well with the righteous, the city rejoiceth" → "In the good of the righteous a town is jubilant"** (v10) — Hebrew בְּטוּב צַדִּיקִים, "in the good (the welfare) of the righteous"; עָלַץ "be jubilant"; קִרְיָה "town", without the article. **"there is shouting" → "is a ringing cry"** — רִנָּה.
- **"the city is exalted" → "a town rises high"** (v11) — Hebrew תָּרוּם קָרֶת, qal "be high, rise"; קֶרֶת "town" (as 9:3, 14; English has one word for קִרְיָה v10 and קֶרֶת v11). **"it is overthrown" → "it is torn down"** — תֵּהָרֵס, הָרַס "tear down"; the Hebrew order of the second line is kept.
- **"He that is void of wisdom despiseth his neighbour" → "He who despises his neighbor is lacking heart"** (v12) — Hebrew בָּז־לְרֵעֵהוּ חֲסַר־לֵב, in this order. **"a man of understanding holdeth his peace" → "a man of understandings keeps silent"** — אִישׁ תְּבוּנוֹת, plural; יַחֲרִישׁ.
- **"A talebearer revealeth secrets" → "He who goes about as a talebearer uncovers secret counsel"** (v13) — Hebrew הוֹלֵךְ רָכִיל (as Lev 19:16); מְגַלֶּה "uncovers"; סוֹד "secret counsel", singular. **"he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter" → "the faithful of spirit covers a matter"** — נֶאֱמַן־רוּחַ; מְכַסֶּה "covers"; דָבָר without the article.
- **"Where no counsel is, the people fall" → "Where there is no steering, a people falls"** (v14) — Hebrew תַּחְבֻּלוֹת, literally "steering" (guidance), not עֵצָה "counsel"; עָם without the article. **"in the multitude of counsellors there is safety" → "salvation is in an abundance of counselors"** — תְּשׁוּעָה "salvation"; יוֹעֵץ is singular in the Hebrew, a collective ("counselor").
- **"He that is surety for a stranger shall smart for it" → "He will surely suffer evil, for he has been surety for a stranger"** (v15) — Hebrew רַע־יֵרוֹעַ כִּי־עָרַב זָר: the noun רַע "evil" with the nifal of the same root, literally "evil he will suffer evil" ("surely" carries the doubling); כִּי "for"; עָרַב "be surety for". **"he that hateth suretiship is sure" → "he who hates striking hands is secure"** — שֹׂנֵא תֹקְעִים, literally "who hates those striking": the striking of hands that seals a pledge (6:1); "hands" supplied. בּוֹטֵחַ "trusting, secure".
- **"A gracious woman retaineth honour: and strong men retain riches" → "A woman of favor holds fast glory, and the ruthless hold fast riches"** (v16) — Hebrew אֵשֶׁת־חֵן, חֵן "favor"; תָּמַךְ "hold fast" in both lines; כָּבוֹד "glory"; עָרִיצִים "the ruthless". The Septuagint has two more lines here (from memory): the MT is kept.
- **"The merciful man doeth good to his own soul: but he that is cruel troubleth his own flesh" → "Dealing well with his soul is a man of chesed, but troubling his flesh is a cruel one"** (v17) — Hebrew גֹּמֵל נַפְשׁוֹ אִישׁ חָסֶד, in this order: the participle first, then "a man of chesed". גָּמַל "deal (well)", "well" is supplied by the sense of the line; עֹכֵר "troubling"; שְׁאֵר "flesh" (one's own body; elsewhere "near kin"); אַכְזָרִי "cruel". No "own" in the Hebrew.
- **"The wicked worketh a deceitful work: but to him that soweth righteousness shall be a sure reward" → "The wicked makes a wage of falsehood, but he who sows righteousness, a reward of truth"** (v18) — Hebrew פְּעֻלַּת־שָׁקֶר, "a wage of falsehood" (פְּעֻלָּה as 10:16); the second line has no verb: "makes" carries on. שֶׂכֶר "reward"; אֱמֶת "truth". The Hebrew plays on שֶׁקֶר / שֶׂכֶר.
- **"As righteousness tendeth to life" → "He who is honest in righteousness is for life"** (v19) — Hebrew כֵּן־צְדָקָה לְחַיִּים. כֵּן is taken as the adjective "honest" (as Gen 42:11) in a bond with צְדָקָה; it may also be the adverb "so" (KJV "as"). **"so he that pursueth evil pursueth it to his own death" → "but he who pursues evil, for his death"** — וּמְרַדֵּף רָעָה לְמוֹתוֹ; the second "pursueth" and "own" are supplied in KJV.
- **"They that are of a froward heart are abomination to the LORD" → "An abomination to Yahweh are the crooked of heart"** (v20) — Hebrew order; עִקְּשֵׁי־לֵב "the crooked of heart". **"such as are upright in their way" → "the blameless of way"** — תְּמִימֵי דָרֶךְ, תָּמִים "blameless".
- **"Though hand join in hand, the wicked shall not be unpunished" → "Surely, the evil one will not be clear"** (v21) — Hebrew יָד לְיָד, literally "hand to hand": a pledge given hand in hand, "be sure of it" (in English "hand to hand" would say close fighting). לֹא־יִנָּקֶה "will not be clear"; רָע "the evil one" (not רָשָׁע "wicked"). **"shall be delivered" → "escapes"** — נִמְלָט, "escapes" (a nifal participle or perfect); זֶרַע "seed".
- **"As a jewel of gold in a swine's snout, so is a fair woman which is without discretion" → "A ring of gold in the nose of a pig — a beautiful woman, and turned aside from sense"** (v22) — Hebrew נֶזֶם "ring" (as Gen 24:47); אַף "nose"; no "as … so": the two lines stand side by side. וְסָרַת טָעַם, "and turned aside from sense" (טַעַם "sense", literally "taste").
- **"the expectation of the wicked is wrath" → "the hope of the wicked is raging"** (v23) — Hebrew תִּקְוַת "hope"; עֶבְרָה "raging" as v4. תַּאֲוָה "desire" (of a good wish; elsewhere "craving"). No "but" (no וְ).
- **"There is that scattereth, and yet increaseth" → "There is one who scatters, and is increased still"** (v24) — Hebrew וְנוֹסָף עוֹד, nifal of יָסַף "be added to". **"withholdeth more than is meet, but it tendeth to poverty" → "one who withholds more than is right — only to want"** — מִיֹּשֶׁר, literally "from uprightness" (more than is right); אַךְ־לְמַחְסוֹר "only to want", no verb.
- **"The liberal soul shall be made fat" → "A soul of blessing will be made fat"** (v25) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ־בְּרָכָה, "a soul of blessing". **"shall be watered also himself" → "he also will be watered"** — יוֹרֶא is written with א, as from יָרָא; it is taken as hofal of רָוָה "be watered" (the root of מַרְוֶה "one who waters"). The form is uncertain.
- **"He that withholdeth corn, the people shall curse him" → "He who holds back grain, the folk will curse him"** (v26) — Hebrew מֹנֵעַ "holds back"; בָּר "grain" (another word than דָּגָן); לְאוֹם "folk", singular; יִקְּבֻהוּ "will curse him" (from נָקַב or קָבַב: Num 23:8). **"blessing shall be upon the head of him that selleth it" → "blessing is for the head of him who sells it"** — לְרֹאשׁ as 10:6; מַשְׁבִּיר "sells grain" ("it").
- **"He that diligently seeketh good procureth favour" → "He who eagerly seeks good seeks good pleasure"** (v27) — Hebrew שֹׁחֵר "seek eagerly" (from שַׁחַר "dawn"); יְבַקֵּשׁ "seeks"; רָצוֹן "good pleasure". **"he that seeketh mischief" → "he who seeks evil"** — דֹּרֵשׁ רָעָה. Three verbs of seeking stand here (שִׁחֵר, בִּקֵּשׁ, דָּרַשׁ); English has "seek" for the last two.
- **"He that trusteth in his riches shall fall" → "He who trusts in his riches, he will fall"** (v28) — Hebrew הוּא "he" kept. **"shall flourish as a branch" → "like the leaf … will bud"** — Hebrew כֶּעָלֶה, "like the leaf" (with the article); פָּרַח "bud".
- **"the fool shall be servant to the wise of heart" → "the senseless one is a slave to the wise of heart"** (v29) — Hebrew עֶבֶד אֱוִיל, no verb; אֱוִיל "senseless one"; עֶבֶד "slave". רוּחַ "wind", no article.
- **"he that winneth souls" → "he who takes souls"** (v30) — Hebrew לֹקֵחַ נְפָשׁוֹת, לָקַח "take" (to take souls is elsewhere to take lives, 1:19; here said of the wise). The Septuagint reads "the souls of the lawless are taken away" (from memory); the MT is kept.
- **"shall be recompensed in the earth: much more the wicked and the sinner" → "will be repaid on the earth; how much more the wicked and the sinner!"** (v31) — Hebrew הֵן "Behold"; יְשֻׁלָּם, pual of שִׁלֵּם "repay"; אַף כִּי "how much more". 1 Pet 4:18 quotes the Septuagint form of this verse.

### Notes
- **v30** — "takes *nefashot*" (souls / lives): MT retained (see Changes).
