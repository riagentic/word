# Proverbs 8

[1] Does not wisdom call,
and understanding give her voice?

[2] At the head of the heights, beside the way,
at the house of the tracks she stands;

[3] beside the gates, at the mouth of the town,
at the coming in of the entrances she cries aloud:

[4] "To you, men, I call,
and my voice is to the sons of man.

[5] Understand shrewdness, simple ones,
and fools, understand heart.

[6] Hear, for foremost things I will speak,
and the opening of my lips is uprightness,

[7] for truth my palate softly recites,
and an abomination of my lips is wickedness.

[8] In righteousness are all the sayings of my mouth;
there is nothing twisted or crooked in them.

[9] All of them are straight to the one who understands,
and right to those who find knowledge.

[10] Take my discipline and not silver,
and knowledge rather than choice gold,

[11] for wisdom is better than rubies,
and all delights do not equal her.

[12] I, wisdom, abide with shrewdness,
and knowledge of devisings I find.

[13] The fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is to hate evil;
pride and exaltation and the way of evil
and a mouth of perversities I hate.

[14] Mine are counsel and sound wisdom;
I am understanding; mine is might.

[15] By me kings reign,
and dignitaries decree righteousness;

[16] by me princes rule,
and nobles, all the judges of righteousness.

[17] I love those who love me,
and those who seek me eagerly find me.

[18] Riches and glory are with me,
enduring wealth and righteousness.

[19] Better is my fruit than gold, and than fine gold,
and my yield than choice silver.

[20] In the path of righteousness I walk,
in the midst of the tracks of judgment,

[21] to make those who love me inherit substance,
and their treasuries I fill.

[22] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ acquired me, the beginning of his way,
before his works, from long ago.

[23] From of old I was set, from the head,
from the former times of the earth.

[24] When there were no deeps I was brought forth,
when there were no springs heavy with water.

[25] Before mountains were sunk,
before hills I was brought forth,

[26] while he had not yet made earth and open places,
and the head of the dusts of the world.

[27] When he established the heavens, there was I;
when he inscribed a circle on the face of the deep,

[28] when he made firm the skies above,
when the springs of the deep grew strong,

[29] when he set for the sea its limit,
and the waters do not pass over his mouth,
when he inscribed the foundations of the earth,

[30] and I was beside him, a master workman,
and I was delights day by day,
playing before him at every time,

[31] playing in the world of his earth,
and my delights were with the sons of man.

[32] And now, sons, listen to me,
and happy are those who keep my ways.

[33] Hear discipline and be wise,
and do not let it go.

[34] Happy is the man who listens to me,
to keep awake at my doors day by day,
to keep the doorposts of my entrances.

[35] For he who finds me has found life,
and has obtained good pleasure from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;

[36] but he who sins against me does violence to his soul;
all who hate me love death."

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v13, 22, 35) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v13, 29, 30 and 34 have three lines. v4–36 are Wisdom's words.
- **"Doth not wisdom cry? and understanding put forth her voice?" → "Does not wisdom call, and understanding give her voice?"** (v1) — Hebrew הֲלֹא "does not" governs both lines; תִּקְרָא "call" (also v4); תִּתֵּן קוֹלָהּ "give her voice" (as 1:20). תְּבוּנָה "understanding" (WISDOM.md; also בִּינָה, v5, 14: English has one word).
- **"She standeth in the top of high places, by the way in the places of the paths" → "At the head of the heights, beside the way, at the house of the tracks she stands"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּרֹאשׁ־מְרוֹמִים "at the head of the heights"; עֲלֵי־דָרֶךְ "beside (literally 'upon') the way"; בֵּית נְתִיבוֹת "the house of the tracks" (= where the tracks meet; or בֵּית "between"); נִצָּבָה "she stands" (נִצָּב, stationed).
- **"She crieth at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors" → "beside the gates, at the mouth of the town, at the coming in of the entrances she cries aloud"** (v3) — Hebrew לְיַד "beside" (literally "at the hand of"); לְפִי־קָרֶת "at the mouth of the town" (קֶרֶת, a poetic word for "town"); מְבוֹא פְתָחִים "the coming in of the entrances" (פֶּתַח "entrance"; דֶּלֶת is "door", v34); תָּרֹנָּה "she cries aloud" (רָנַן, a ringing cry; as 1:20).
- **"Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man" → "To you, men, I call, and my voice is to the sons of man"** (v4) — Hebrew אִישִׁים, a rare plural of אִישׁ "man"; בְּנֵי אָדָם "the sons of man" (reviewer row); no "O".
- **"O ye simple, understand wisdom: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart" → "Understand shrewdness, simple ones, and fools, understand heart"** (v5) — Hebrew הָבִינוּ twice ("understand", hifil of בִּין: also "gain understanding"); עָרְמָה "shrewdness" (WISDOM.md; good here, as 1:4); פְּתָאיִם "simple ones", כְּסִילִים "fools"; לֵב "heart" (the seat of the mind: "sense").
- **"I will speak of excellent things; and the opening of my lips shall be right things" → "foremost things I will speak, and the opening of my lips is uprightness"** (v6) — Hebrew נְגִידִים, the plural of נָגִיד "leader" (literally "what is in front"): "foremost, princely things" (uncertain; not נְדִיבִים "nobles", v16); מֵישָׁרִים "uprightness" (as 1:3; the line has no verb).
- **"For my mouth shall speak truth; and wickedness is an abomination to my lips" → "for truth my palate softly recites, and an abomination of my lips is wickedness"** (v7) — Hebrew חִכִּי "my palate" (as 5:3), not "mouth"; יֶהְגֶּה, הָגָה "softly recite" (reviewer row; as Ps 1:2); רֶשַׁע "wickedness" (noun of רָשָׁע "wicked"); the Hebrew order of both lines is kept.
- **"All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing froward or perverse in them" → "In righteousness are all the sayings of my mouth; there is nothing twisted or crooked in them"** (v8) — Hebrew אִמְרֵי־פִי "the sayings of my mouth"; נִפְתָּל "twisted", עִקֵּשׁ "crooked" (Deut 32:5); וְ "and", "or" after the negative.
- **"They are all plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge" → "All of them are straight to the one who understands, and right to those who find knowledge"** (v9) — Hebrew נְכֹחִים "straight" (what is in front, straight ahead); יְשָׁרִים "right" (of words; "upright" is said of persons).
- **"Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold" → "Take my discipline and not silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold"** (v10) — Hebrew קְחוּ "take" (WORDS-HE: "receive" only with a note); מוּסָר "discipline"; חָרוּץ "gold" (a poetic word; also v19 beside פָּז "fine gold"); נִבְחָר "chosen, choice": it may also belong to "knowledge" ("and knowledge is to be chosen rather than gold").
- **"For wisdom is better than rubies; and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it" → "for wisdom is better than rubies, and all delights do not equal her"** (v11) — Hebrew פְּנִינִים "rubies" (uncertain; "pearls", "corals" in some; as Job 28:18 row); כָּל־חֲפָצִים "all delights" (חֵפֶץ "delight"); לֹא יִשְׁווּ־בָהּ "do not equal her".
- **"I wisdom dwell with prudence, and find out knowledge of witty inventions" → "I, wisdom, abide with shrewdness, and knowledge of devisings I find"** (v12) — Hebrew שָׁכַנְתִּי עָרְמָה, literally "I abide (in) shrewdness", with no preposition ("with" supplied); שָׁכַן "abide"; דַּעַת מְזִמּוֹת "knowledge of devisings" (מְזִמָּה "devising", WISDOM.md), one phrase, not "knowledge and discretion".
- **"The fear of the LORD is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate" → "The fear of Yahweh is to hate evil; pride and exaltation and the way of evil and a mouth of perversities I hate"** (v13) — Hebrew שְׂנֹאת רָע "to hate evil" (an infinitive); גֵּאָה "pride", גָּאוֹן "exaltation" (the rising up of the proud; two words of one root); דֶּרֶךְ רָע "the way of evil"; פִּי תַהְפֻּכוֹת "a mouth of perversities" (as 6:14).
- **"Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am understanding; I have strength" → "Mine are counsel and sound wisdom; I am understanding; mine is might"** (v14) — Hebrew לִי "to me / mine" twice; תּוּשִׁיָּה "sound wisdom" (uncertain: sound counsel, success); אֲנִי בִינָה "I am understanding" (Wisdom says she is understanding: not "I have insight"); גְּבוּרָה "might".
- **"By me kings reign, and princes decree justice" → "By me kings reign, and dignitaries decree righteousness"** (v15) — Hebrew רוֹזְנִים "dignitaries" (as Ps 2:2); יְחֹקְקוּ "decree", literally "inscribe" (the verb of v27, 29: חָקַק); צֶדֶק "righteousness".
- **"By me princes rule, and nobles, even all the judges of the earth" → "by me princes rule, and nobles, all the judges of righteousness"** (v16) — Hebrew שָׂרִים יָשֹׂרוּ, noun and verb of one root ("princes act as princes"); נְדִיבִים "nobles" (literally "willing ones"); כָּל־שֹׁפְטֵי צֶדֶק "all the judges of righteousness": the Masoretic text; "of the earth" (KJV) is the reading of other Hebrew manuscripts and versions (from memory).
- **"those that seek me early shall find me" → "those who seek me eagerly find me"** (v17) — Hebrew מְשַׁחֲרַי, שִׁחֵר "seek eagerly" ("early" in KJV, from שַׁחַר "dawn"). The written text has אהביה "those who love her"; the qere אֹהֲבַי "those who love me" is translated.
- **"Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness" → "Riches and glory are with me, enduring wealth and righteousness"** (v18) — Hebrew כָּבוֹד "glory" (WORDS-HE: not "honor"); הוֹן עָתֵק "enduring wealth" (עָתֵק "old, lasting", Isa 23:18; not the עָתָק "arrogance" of 1 Sam 2:3); no "yea".
- **"My fruit is better than gold, yea, than fine gold; and my revenue than choice silver" → "Better is my fruit than gold, and than fine gold, and my yield than choice silver"** (v19) — Hebrew מֵחָרוּץ וּמִפָּז "than gold and than fine gold" (two words for gold: חָרוּץ, פָּז); תְּבוּאָה "yield"; Hebrew order kept.
- **"I lead in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment" → "In the path of righteousness I walk, in the midst of the tracks of judgment"** (v20) — Hebrew אֹרַח "path", נְתִיבוֹת "tracks"; אֲהַלֵּךְ "I walk" (piel of הָלַךְ: walk about); מִשְׁפָּט "judgment".
- **"That I may cause those that love me to inherit substance; and I will fill their treasures" → "to make those who love me inherit substance, and their treasuries I fill"** (v21) — Hebrew לְהַנְחִיל "to make inherit" (הִנְחִיל); יֵשׁ "substance", literally "there is" (what exists, what one has); אוֹצָרוֹת "treasuries".
- **"The LORD possessed me in the beginning of his way, before his works of old" → "Yahweh acquired me, the beginning of his way, before his works, from long ago"** (v22) — Hebrew יְהוָה קָנָנִי: קָנָה "get, acquire" (WISDOM.md; WORDS-HE קָנָה row: Gen 4:1; 14:19, 22; 4:5, 7 "get wisdom"); "possessed" (KJV) and "created" (with Gen 14:19, 22; Deut 32:6) are the other ways of taking it; the verse keeps the word's own sense and decides nothing more. רֵאשִׁית דַּרְכּוֹ stands without a preposition ("in" / "at" are supplied by others): "the beginning (or 'first') of his way", in apposition to "me" or as a time; קֶדֶם "before"; מִפְעָלָיו "his works" (מִפְעָל, from פָּעַל "work"); מֵאָז "from long ago" (literally "from then"; Ps 93:2).
- **"I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, or ever the earth was" → "From of old I was set, from the head, from the former times of the earth"** (v23) — Hebrew מֵעוֹלָם "from of old"; נִסַּכְתִּי, nifal of נָסַךְ "pour out" and "set, install" (Ps 2:6 "I have set my king"): "I was set" (uncertain; also read from סָכַךְ "weave", as Ps 139:13); מֵרֹאשׁ "from the head" (= from the first, as Isa 40:21); מִקַּדְמֵי־אָרֶץ "from the former (times) of the earth": "times" is supplied.
- **"When there were no depths, I was brought forth; when there were no fountains abounding with water" → "When there were no deeps I was brought forth, when there were no springs heavy with water"** (v24, 25) — Hebrew חוֹלָלְתִּי, polal of חִיל "writhe": "I was brought forth" (in labor; as Ps 90:2; Deut 32:18 of God "be in labor with"); תְּהֹמוֹת "deeps" (תְּהוֹם, Gen 1:2); מַעְיָנוֹת "springs"; נִכְבַּדֵּי־מָיִם, literally "heavy with water" (כָּבֵד "heavy").
- **"Before the mountains were settled" → "Before mountains were sunk"** (v25) — Hebrew הָטְבָּעוּ, hofal of טָבַע "sink" (as Exod 15:4 "sunk"): the mountains sunk into their bases; no article ("mountains", "hills"); לִפְנֵי "before".
- **"While as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields, nor the highest part of the dust of the world" → "while he had not yet made earth and open places, and the head of the dusts of the world"** (v26) — Hebrew עַד־לֹא "while not yet"; חוּצוֹת "open places" (what is outside: the open country; elsewhere "streets"); רֹאשׁ עָפְרוֹת "the head (= first or chief part) of the dusts" (עָפָר "dust", plural here); תֵּבֵל "world". Each member has "and" (no "nor").
- **"When he prepared the heavens, I was there: when he set a compass upon the face of the depth" → "When he established the heavens, there was I; when he inscribed a circle on the face of the deep"** (v27) — Hebrew בַּהֲכִינוֹ "when he established" (הֵכִין, also "prepare"); שָׁם אָנִי "there (was) I", Hebrew order; בְּחוּקוֹ "when he inscribed" (חָקַק, "decree" in v15; English has two words for the one verb); חוּג "circle" (the horizon or the vault).
- **"When he established the clouds above: when he strengthened the fountains of the deep" → "when he made firm the skies above, when the springs of the deep grew strong"** (v28) — Hebrew בְּאַמְּצוֹ "when he made firm" (אִמֵּץ); שְׁחָקִים "skies"; בַּעֲזוֹז עִינוֹת תְּהוֹם, an intransitive infinitive (עָזַז qal "be strong"): the springs are the subject, "when they grew strong"; it is not "he strengthened" (that would need another stem). עַיִן of water "spring" (beside מַעְיָן "spring", v24: English has one word).
- **"When he gave to the sea his decree, that the waters should not pass his commandment: when he appointed the foundations of the earth" → "when he set for the sea its limit, and the waters do not pass over his mouth, when he inscribed the foundations of the earth"** (v29) — Hebrew בְּשׂוּמוֹ "when he set"; חֻקּוֹ "its limit" (חֹק of the sea's bound, as Job 38:10); וּמַיִם לֹא יַעַבְרוּ־פִיו "and the waters do not pass over his mouth": פִּיו "his mouth" (= his command) or "its mouth" (the sea's shore); kept word for word; עָבַר "pass over"; בְּחוּקוֹ "when he inscribed" (as v27); מוֹסְדֵי אָרֶץ "the foundations of the earth".
- **"Then I was by him, as one brought up with him: and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him" → "and I was beside him, a master workman, and I was delights day by day, playing before him at every time"** (v30) — Hebrew וָאֶהְיֶה "and I was" (wayyiqtol, twice); אֶצְלוֹ "beside him"; אָמוֹן is uncertain: pointed as the noun "master workman, artisan" (Jer 52:15; Song 7:1 אָמָּן); also taken as "nursling, one brought up" (KJV), or read "faithful" or "constantly". The verse keeps "a master workman" and decides nothing more. שַׁעֲשֻׁעִים "delights" (plural, no "his": she was delight itself, or his delight); יוֹם יוֹם "day by day"; מְשַׂחֶקֶת "playing" (שִׂחֵק "play", Judg 16:25; 2 Sam 6:5); בְּכָל־עֵת "at every time".
- **"Rejoicing in the habitable part of his earth; and my delights were with the sons of men" → "playing in the world of his earth, and my delights were with the sons of man"** (v31) — Hebrew מְשַׂחֶקֶת as v30; תֵּבֵל אַרְצוֹ "the world of his earth"; וְשַׁעֲשֻׁעַי אֶת־בְּנֵי אָדָם, with no verb: "were" is supplied for the narrative of v30; אֶת "with"; בְּנֵי אָדָם "the sons of man".
- **"Now therefore hearken unto me, O ye children: for blessed are they that keep my ways" → "And now, sons, listen to me, and happy are those who keep my ways"** (v32) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה בָנִים: no "O"; וְאַשְׁרֵי "and happy" (WISDOM.md: אַשְׁרֵי "Happy"; "for" is not written).
- **"Hear instruction, and be wise, and refuse it not" → "Hear discipline and be wise, and do not let it go"** (v33) — Hebrew מוּסָר "discipline"; אַל־תִּפְרָעוּ, פָּרַע "let loose, let go" (as 1:25); "it" is supplied.
- **"Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors" → "Happy is the man who listens to me, to keep awake at my doors day by day, to keep the doorposts of my entrances"** (v34) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי אָדָם "Happy is the man"; לִשְׁקֹד "to keep awake" (שָׁקַד, as Ps 127:1: wakeful watching); דַּלְתֹתַי "my doors" (דֶּלֶת; not "gates"); לִשְׁמֹר "to keep, guard"; מְזוּזֹת "doorposts"; פְּתָחַי "my entrances" (פֶּתַח).
- **"For whoso findeth me findeth life, and shall obtain favour of the LORD" → "For he who finds me has found life, and has obtained good pleasure from Yahweh"** (v35) — The written text has מצאי (מֹצְאֵי "those who find me"); the qere מָצָא "he has found" is translated: "he who finds me has found life". וַיָּפֶק "and he has obtained" (הֵפִיק, "bring out, get"); רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (WORDS-HE: חֵן is "favor").
- **"But he that sinneth against me wrongeth his own soul: all they that hate me love death" → "but he who sins against me does violence to his soul; all who hate me love death"** (v36) — Hebrew וְחֹטְאִי, חָטָא "sin" (also "miss": "he who misses me", set against "he who finds me", v35); חֹמֵס "does violence to" (verb of חָמָס "violence"); נַפְשׁוֹ "his soul"; מְשַׂנְאַי "those who hate me"; אָהֲבוּ, a perfect of what is always so.

### Notes
- **Hebrew verse numbers** — the same as ours in this chapter.
- **v22–31** — rendered word for word; the sense of קָנָנִי (v22), נִסַּכְתִּי (v23) and אָמוֹן (v30) is not decided in the verse. The passage was read in the early church debates on the Son (Prov 8:22 in the Arian controversy); the Hebrew words themselves are given above.
