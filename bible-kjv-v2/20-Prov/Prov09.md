# Proverbs 9

[1] Wisdom has built her house;
she has hewn her seven pillars.

[2] She has butchered her meat; she has mixed her wine;
also she has arranged her table.

[3] She has sent out her girls; she calls
on the tops of the heights of the town:

[4] "Whoever is simple, let him turn aside here!"
As for one lacking heart, she says to him,

[5] "Come, eat of my bread,
and drink of the wine I have mixed.

[6] Forsake the simple, and live,
and walk straight in the way of understanding."

[7] He who disciplines a scoffer takes disgrace for himself,
and he who reproves the wicked, his blemish.

[8] Do not reprove a scoffer, lest he hate you;
reprove the wise, and he will love you.

[9] Give to the wise, and he will be wiser still;
make known to the righteous, and he will add teaching.

[10] The beginning of wisdom is the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and the knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.

[11] For by me your days will multiply,
and they will add to you years of life.

[12] If you are wise, you are wise for yourself;
and if you scoff, you alone will bear it.

[13] The woman of foolishness is boisterous;
she is simpleness, and she does not know anything.

[14] And she sits at the entrance of her house,
on a seat at the heights of the town,

[15] to call to those who pass by on the way,
who make their paths straight:

[16] "Whoever is simple, let him turn aside here!"
And as for one lacking heart, she says to him,

[17] "Stolen waters grow sweet,
and bread of hiding places is pleasant."

[18] And he does not know that the shades are there,
in the depths of Sheol her called ones.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order (the existing breaks already follow the accents).
- **"Wisdom"** (v1) — Hebrew חָכְמוֹת, a plural form of חָכְמָה (as 1:20), with a singular verb: one figure, "Wisdom". **"she hath hewn out her seven pillars"** — literally "she has hewn her pillars, seven".
- **"She hath killed her beasts" → "She has butchered her meat"** (v2) — Hebrew טָבְחָה טִבְחָהּ, the verb טָבַח "butcher" with its own noun, literally "she has butchered her butchering": טֶבַח is what is butchered for the meal. No "beasts" (בְּהֵמָה) stands here.
- **"she hath also furnished her table" → "also she has arranged her table"** (v2) — Hebrew אַף עָרְכָה, the verb עָרַךְ "arrange" (as Gen 22:9; Exod 40:4), Hebrew order kept.
- **"her maidens" → "her girls"** (v3) — Hebrew נַעֲרֹתֶיהָ, the נַעֲרָה of attendants ("her girls", as Gen 24:61).
- **"she crieth upon the highest places of the city" → "she calls on the tops of the heights of the town"** (v3) — Hebrew תִקְרָא, imperfect: she herself calls (the accents set it in the first line). עַל־גַּפֵּי מְרֹמֵי קָרֶת: גַּף is uncertain ("back, top, height"); מְרֹמִים "heights"; קֶרֶת "town", a poetic word (קִרְיָה has the same English, 11:10–11: note). The same phrase without גַּפֵּי stands in v14.
- **"let him turn in hither" → "let him turn aside here"** (v4, 16) — Hebrew יָסֻר, the verb סוּר "turn aside".
- **"him that wanteth understanding" → "one lacking heart"** (v4, 16) — Hebrew חֲסַר־לֵב, "lacking heart"; the heart is here the seat of the mind. The line opens with the person ("one lacking heart") and goes on "she says to him": kept. In v16 both words have וְ (וַחֲסַר־לֵב וְאָמְרָה): the first is "And"; the second, which marks the main clause after "as for", has no English word.
- **"eat of my bread … drink of the wine"** (v5) — Hebrew לַחֲמוּ בְלַחֲמִי … וּשְׁתוּ בְּיַיִן, literally "eat in my bread … drink in the wine".
- **"Forsake the foolish" → "Forsake the simple"** (v6) — Hebrew פְתָאיִם, the plural of פֶּתִי "simple one" (v4). It may also be taken as an abstract plural, "simple ways" (the current wording): the form is the plural of the person.
- **"go in the way of understanding" → "walk straight in the way of understanding"** (v6) — Hebrew אִשְׁרוּ, piel of אָשַׁר "go straight, go on" (as 4:14; 23:19); another sense of the root than אִשֵּׁר "call happy". בִּינָה "understanding".
- **"He that reproveth a scorner getteth to himself shame" → "He who disciplines a scoffer takes disgrace for himself"** (v7) — Hebrew יֹסֵר, the verb of מוּסָר "discipline"; לֹקֵחַ לוֹ "takes for himself"; קָלוֹן "disgrace". The verb הוֹכִיחַ "reprove" stands in the second line and in v8.
- **"getteth himself a blot" → "his blemish"** (v7) — Hebrew מוּמוֹ, "his blemish" (the מוּם of a sacrifice), with no verb: the line leans on "takes" of the first line. Whose blemish (the reprover's, or the wicked one's found on him) the Hebrew does not say.
- **"a wise man" → "the wise"; "a just man" → "the righteous"** (v8, 9) — Hebrew חָכָם, צַדִּיק, singular adjectives used as nouns; no "man" in the Hebrew. The singular shows in "he".
- **"Give instruction to a wise man" → "Give to the wise"** (v9) — Hebrew תֵּן לְחָכָם, no object; "instruction" is supplied in KJV. **"teach" → "make known"** — הוֹדַע. **"increase in learning" → "add teaching"** — וְיוֹסֶף לֶקַח, the verb יָסַף "add" and לֶקַח "teaching" (literally "what is received").
- **"The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom" → "The beginning of wisdom is the fear of Yahweh"** (v10) — Hebrew תְּחִלַּת חָכְמָה יִרְאַת יְהוָה, in this order; "the fear of Yahweh" is worded as in 1:7, where the order is the other way and the word for "beginning" is another (רֵאשִׁית; here תְּחִלָּה).
- **"the knowledge of the holy" → "the knowledge of the Holy One"** (v10) — Hebrew קְדֹשִׁים, a plural without the article, parallel to יְהוָה (the same plural 30:3; Hos 11:12): read as a plural of majesty, "the Holy One". Literally "holy ones"; whether holy beings or holy things are meant is a reading of others.
- **"thy days shall be multiplied" → "your days will multiply"** (v11) — Hebrew יִרְבּוּ, the active verb "become many". **"the years of thy life shall be increased" → "they will add to you years of life"** — וְיוֹסִיפוּ לְךָ, an active plural without a named subject (= years will be added); kept as it stands.
- **"but if thou scornest" → "and if you scoff"** (v12) — Hebrew וְלַצְתָּ, "and you scoff", the אִם of the first line carried on.
- **"A foolish woman is clamorous" → "The woman of foolishness is boisterous"** (v13) — Hebrew אֵשֶׁת כְּסִילוּת, "a woman of foolishness": כְּסִילוּת (only here) is the abstract of כְּסִיל "fool", kept apart from אִוֶּלֶת "folly". הֹמִיָּה "boisterous, turbulent" (from הָמָה "roar", as 7:11).
- **"she is simple, and knoweth nothing" → "she is simpleness, and she does not know anything"** (v13) — Hebrew פְּתַיּוּת, the abstract of פֶּתִי, "simpleness" ("she is" supplied); וּבַל־יָדְעָה מָּה, literally "and she does not know what".
- **"For she sitteth at the door of her house" → "And she sits at the entrance of her house"** (v14) — Hebrew וְיָשְׁבָה, "And she sits" (not "For"); פֶּתַח "entrance". **"on a seat in the high places of the city" → "on a seat at the heights of the town"** — כִּסֵּא "seat"; "at" is supplied for the place.
- **"To call passengers who go right on their ways" → "to call to those who pass by on the way, who make their paths straight"** (v15) — Hebrew לְעֹבְרֵי־דָרֶךְ "to the passers of the way"; הַמְיַשְּׁרִים אֹרְחוֹתָם, piel "who make straight their paths" (= keep straight on).
- **"Stolen waters are sweet" → "Stolen waters grow sweet"** (v17) — Hebrew יִמְתָּקוּ, "become sweet". **"bread eaten in secret" → "bread of hiding places"** — Hebrew לֶחֶם סְתָרִים, literally "bread of hiding places" (סֵתֶר "hiding place"); "eaten" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"But he knoweth not" → "And he does not know"** (v18) — Hebrew וְלֹא־יָדַע.
- **"the dead" → "the shades"** (v18) — Hebrew רְפָאִים, the shades of the dead in Sheol (as 2:18; Ps 88:10; Job 26:5); the same word names the ancient people Rephaim (token there only).
- **"and that her guests are in the depths of hell" → "in the depths of Sheol her called ones"** (v18) — Hebrew בְּעִמְקֵי שְׁאוֹל קְרֻאֶיהָ, in this order, no "and that"; קְרֻאִים "the called ones" (the invited, as 1 Sam 9:13); שְׁאוֹל *She'ol*.

### Notes
- **Two invitations**: Wisdom (v1-6) and the woman of foolishness (v13-18) call with the same words (v4 = v16).
- **Closes Part I** (chapters 1-9).
