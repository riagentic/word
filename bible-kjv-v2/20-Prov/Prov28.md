# Proverbs 28

[1] The wicked flee, and there is none pursuing,
and the righteous are like a young lion, confident.

[2] By the transgression of a land many are its princes,
and by a man who understands, who knows, so it shall last long.

[3] A man who is destitute and extorts from the poor —
a sweeping rain, and there is no bread.

[4] Those who forsake ⟦torah|torah|the law⟧ praise the wicked,
and those who keep ⟦torah|torah|the law⟧ provoke them.

[5] Men of evil do not understand judgment,
and those who seek ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ understand all.

[6] Better is the destitute walking in his integrity
than the crooked of two ways while he is rich.

[7] One who guards ⟦torah|torah|the law⟧ is a son who understands,
and one who keeps company with gluttons puts his father to shame.

[8] One who increases his wealth by interest and increase
gathers it for one who shows favor to the poor.

[9] One who turns away his ear from hearing ⟦torah|torah|the law⟧,
even his prayer is an abomination.

[10] One who leads the upright astray into an evil way,
into his own pit he himself will fall,
and the blameless will inherit good.

[11] Wise in his eyes is a rich man,
and a poor one who understands searches him out.

[12] When the righteous are jubilant, great is the beauty,
and when the wicked rise, a man must be searched for.

[13] One who covers his transgressions will not prosper,
and one who confesses and forsakes will be shown compassion.

[14] Happy is the man who is in dread continually,
and one who makes his heart hard will fall into evil.

[15] A growling lion and a rushing bear —
a wicked ruler over a poor people.

[16] A leader lacking understanding and great in extortions —
one who hates unjust gain will prolong days.

[17] A man pressed by the blood of a soul —
to the pit he will flee;
let them not support him.

[18] One who walks blameless will be saved,
and one crooked of two ways will fall at once.

[19] One who works his ground will be sated with bread,
and one who pursues empty things will be sated with poverty.

[20] A man of faithfulness is great in blessings,
and one who hastens to be rich will not be clear.

[21] To recognize faces is not good,
and for a morsel of bread a man will transgress.

[22] A man evil of eye hastens after wealth,
and does not know that want will come upon him.

[23] One who reproves a man will afterward find favor
more than one who makes the tongue smooth.

[24] One who robs his father and his mother
and says, "It is no transgression,"
is a companion to a man who destroys.

[25] One wide of soul stirs up strife,
and one who trusts in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will be made fat.

[26] One who trusts in his heart, he is a fool,
and one who walks in wisdom, he will escape.

[27] One who gives to the destitute has no lack,
and one who hides his eyes is great in curses.

[28] When the wicked rise, a man hides himself,
and when they perish, the righteous multiply.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v5, 25) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v10, 17, 24 keep three lines, where one Hebrew colon has a strong inner pause.
- **"The wicked flee when no man pursueth: but the righteous are bold as a lion" → "The wicked flee, and there is none pursuing, and the righteous are like a young lion, confident"** (v1) — Hebrew נָסוּ (plural) … רָשָׁע (singular) and צַדִּיקִים (plural) … יִבְטָח (singular): the number changes inside each line; English keeps the plural. וְאֵין רֹדֵף "and there is none pursuing"; כְּפִיר "young lion" (WORDS-HE; beside אֲרִי "lion", v15); יִבְטָח "is confident", the verb of "trusts" (v25, 26), here with no object. וְ "and".
- **"For the transgression of a land many are the princes thereof: but by a man of understanding and knowledge the state thereof shall be prolonged" → "By the transgression of a land many are its princes, and by a man who understands, who knows, so it shall last long"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּפֶשַׁע "by the transgression" (פֶּשַׁע, WORDS-HE); שָׂרֶיהָ "its princes" (שַׂר); מֵבִין יֹדֵעַ "understanding, knowing" (two participles); כֵּן "so" (or a noun, "what is right, stability", KJV "the state thereof": uncertain); יַאֲרִיךְ "it shall last long" (the hifil of v16 "prolong").
- **"A poor man that oppresseth the poor is like a sweeping rain which leaveth no food" → "A man who is destitute and extorts from the poor — a sweeping rain, and there is no bread"** (v3) — Hebrew גֶּבֶר "man" (WORDS-HE standing decision "mighty man"; it stands alone here and in v21, so "man", as the Job ruling; named here once for the chapter); רָשׁ "destitute" and דַּל "poor" (two words, as Ps 82:3: רָשׁ in v3, 6, 27; דַּל in v3, 8, 11, 15); עֹשֵׁק "extorts from" (WORDS-HE עָשַׁק); מָטָר סֹחֵף "a sweeping rain"; וְאֵין לָחֶם "and there is no bread" (לֶחֶם "bread"; no "like").
- **"They that forsake the law praise the wicked: but such as keep the law contend with them" → "Those who forsake torah praise the wicked, and those who keep torah provoke them"** (v4, 7, 9) — Hebrew תּוֹרָה with no article: the `torah` token, "the word as written" (WISDOM.md); whether the torah of Yahweh or the teaching of the wise is meant is not said ("the law" in traditional mode, "teaching" the other sense). עָזַב "forsake", שָׁמַר "keep"; יִתְגָּרוּ בָם "provoke them, stir up strife with them" (הִתְגָּרָה, as Deut 2:5).
- **"Evil men understand not judgment: but they that seek the LORD understand all things" → "Men of evil do not understand judgment, and those who seek Yahweh understand all"** (v5) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי־רָע "men of evil"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment"; בִּקֵּשׁ "seek"; כֹל "all" ("things", "it completely" are not written).
- **"Better is the poor that walketh in his uprightness, than he that is perverse in his ways, though he be rich" → "Better is the destitute walking in his integrity than the crooked of two ways while he is rich"** (v6) — Hebrew רָשׁ "destitute"; תֹּם "integrity" (WORDS-HE); עִקֵּשׁ דְּרָכַיִם "crooked of two ways" (דְּרָכַיִם is a dual, also v18: one who goes two ways at once); וְהוּא עָשִׁיר "and he rich".
- **"Whoso keepeth the law is a wise son: but he that is a companion of riotous men shameth his father" → "One who guards torah is a son who understands, and one who keeps company with gluttons puts his father to shame"** (v7) — Hebrew נוֹצֵר "guards" (נָצַר, beside שָׁמַר "keep", v4); בֵּן מֵבִין "a son who understands" (not "wise": חָכָם); רֹעֶה, here "one who keeps company with" (רָעָה II, the root of רֵעַ; the same letters as "shepherd": the sense "who feeds gluttons" is also possible); זוֹלְלִים "gluttons" (WORDS-HE); הִכְלִים "put to shame".
- **"He that by usury and unjust gain increaseth his substance, he shall gather it for him that will pity the poor" → "One who increases his wealth by interest and increase gathers it for one who shows favor to the poor"** (v8) — Hebrew נֶשֶׁךְ "interest" (literally "a bite"), תַּרְבִּית "increase" (WORDS-HE, Lev 25:36, which names this verse); qere וְתַרְבִּית "and increase", the written form ובתרבית "and by increase": the sense is the same. הוֹן "wealth"; חוֹנֵן "one who shows favor to" (חָנַן).
- **"He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer shall be abomination" → "One who turns away his ear from hearing torah, even his prayer is an abomination"** (v9) — Hebrew מֵסִיר "turns away" (hifil of סוּר, WORDS-HE "remove"); תְּפִלָּה "prayer"; תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" (no verb: "is").
- **"Whoso causeth the righteous to go astray in an evil way, he shall fall himself into his own pit: but the upright shall have good things in possession" → "One who leads the upright astray into an evil way, into his own pit he himself will fall, and the blameless will inherit good"** (v10) — Hebrew מַשְׁגֶּה "leads astray" (hifil of שָׁגָה "err"); יְשָׁרִים "the upright", תְּמִימִים "the blameless" (WORDS-HE; KJV "the righteous … the upright"); שְׁחוּת "pit" (only here; another word than בּוֹר, v17, and שַׁחַת, 26:27); הוּא "he himself" (emphatic); נָחַל "inherit"; טוֹב "good".
- **"The rich man is wise in his own conceit; but the poor that hath understanding searcheth him out" → "Wise in his eyes is a rich man, and a poor one who understands searches him out"** (v11) — Hebrew order kept; בְּעֵינָיו "in his eyes"; דַּל "poor"; חָקַר "search out" (25:2).
- **"When righteous men do rejoice, there is great glory: but when the wicked rise, a man is hidden" → "When the righteous are jubilant, great is the beauty, and when the wicked rise, a man must be searched for"** (v12) — Hebrew עָלַץ "be jubilant" (WORDS-HE); תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (WORDS-HE; here the splendor of the land, "glory" in KJV); יְחֻפַּשׂ אָדָם, pual of חָפַשׂ "search for": "a man is (must be) searched for", because men hide (v28). KJV "is hidden" gives the sense of v28, not the verb here.
- **"He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy" → "One who covers his transgressions will not prosper, and one who confesses and forsakes will be shown compassion"** (v13) — Hebrew פְּשָׁעָיו "his transgressions" (פֶּשַׁע, not חַטָּאת "sin"); הִצְלִיחַ "prosper"; מוֹדֶה "confesses" (הוֹדָה, WORDS-HE: "give thanks", "confess" of sin); עֹזֵב "forsakes" ("them" is not written); יְרֻחָם "will be shown compassion" (pual of רִחַם).
- **"Happy is the man that feareth alway: but he that hardeneth his heart shall fall into mischief" → "Happy is the man who is in dread continually, and one who makes his heart hard will fall into evil"** (v14) — Hebrew אַשְׁרֵי "Happy" (WISDOM.md); מְפַחֵד "is in dread" (piel of פָּחַד; not יָרֵא "fear"); תָּמִיד "continually" (WORDS-HE); מַקְשֶׁה לִבּוֹ "makes his heart hard" (WORDS-HE, as Exod 7:3); רָעָה "evil" (here what befalls him).
- **"As a roaring lion, and a ranging bear; so is a wicked ruler over the poor people" → "A growling lion and a rushing bear — a wicked ruler over a poor people"** (v15) — Hebrew order kept, no "as … so"; נֹהֵם "growling"; שׁוֹקֵק "rushing" (uncertain: also "ranging", "hungry", from a root "be eager"); מֹשֵׁל "ruler" (מָשַׁל "rule"); עַם־דָּל "a poor people".
- **"The prince that wanteth understanding is also a great oppressor: but he that hateth covetousness shall prolong his days" → "A leader lacking understanding and great in extortions — one who hates unjust gain will prolong days"** (v16) — Hebrew נָגִיד "leader" (WORDS-HE); חֲסַר תְּבוּנוֹת "lacking understanding" (plural, WISDOM.md "understanding"); וְרַב מַעֲשַׁקּוֹת "and great in extortions" (noun of עָשַׁק, v3); the first line has no verb ("is", "also" are not written): one leader, lacking and extorting, set against the man of the second line; qere שֹׂנֵא "one who hates" (singular), the written form שנאי "those who hate" (plural); בֶּצַע "unjust gain" (WORDS-HE); יַאֲרִיךְ יָמִים "will prolong days" ("his" is not written). No "but".
- **"A man that doeth violence to the blood of any person shall flee to the pit; let no man stay him" → "A man pressed by the blood of a soul — to the pit he will flee; let them not support him"** (v17) — Hebrew עָשֻׁק, passive participle of עָשַׁק "extort, oppress": "pressed, burdened" (by the guilt of blood shed); דַּם־נָפֶשׁ "the blood of a soul" (נֶפֶשׁ "soul"); עַד־בּוֹר "to the pit" (literally "until the pit": = death; "fugitive until death" in the existing text adds "fugitive"); אַל־יִתְמְכוּ־בוֹ "let them not support him" (תָּמַךְ, WORDS-HE; no "no one").
- **"Whoso walketh uprightly shall be saved: but he that is perverse in his ways shall fall at once" → "One who walks blameless will be saved, and one crooked of two ways will fall at once"** (v18) — Hebrew תָּמִים "blameless" (WORDS-HE); יִוָּשֵׁעַ "will be saved"; נֶעְקַשׁ דְּרָכַיִם "crooked of two ways" (as v6); בְּאֶחָת "at once", or "in one (of the two ways)": both readings are defended.
- **"He that tilleth his land shall have plenty of bread: but he that followeth after vain persons shall have poverty enough" → "One who works his ground will be sated with bread, and one who pursues empty things will be sated with poverty"** (v19) — Hebrew עָבַד "work" (of the ground, WORDS-HE); אַדְמָתוֹ "his ground"; שָׂבַע "be sated" both times (the one verb: "plenty of poverty"); רֵקִים "empty things" (or "empty men", KJV "vain persons": the word allows both); רִישׁ "poverty" (noun of רָשׁ "destitute"). Compare 12:11.
- **"A faithful man shall abound with blessings: but he that maketh haste to be rich shall not be innocent" → "A man of faithfulness is great in blessings, and one who hastens to be rich will not be clear"** (v20) — Hebrew אִישׁ אֱמוּנוֹת "a man of faithfulness" (plural; אֱמוּנָה, WORDS-HE); רַב־בְּרָכוֹת "great in blessings" (as v27 "great in curses"); אָץ "hastens"; לֹא יִנָּקֶה "will not be clear" (נָקָה nifal, WORDS-HE: free of guilt, = unpunished).
- **"To have respect of persons is not good: for for a piece of bread that man will transgress" → "To recognize faces is not good, and for a morsel of bread a man will transgress"** (v21) — Hebrew הַכֵּר־פָּנִים "recognize faces" (= show partiality in judgment; as Deut 1:17); וְ "and"; פַּת־לֶחֶם "a morsel of bread"; פָּשַׁע "transgress" (verb of פֶּשַׁע); גָּבֶר "man" (see v3).
- **"He that hasteth to be rich hath an evil eye, and considereth not that poverty shall come upon him" → "A man evil of eye hastens after wealth, and does not know that want will come upon him"** (v22) — Hebrew נִבְהָל "hastens" (nifal of בָּהַל: "be dismayed" of fear, Gen 45:3; "hasten" with לְ, as here, "after"); אִישׁ רַע עָיִן "a man evil of eye" (= grudging; kept); הוֹן "wealth"; יֵדַע "knows"; חֶסֶר "want" (beside רִישׁ "poverty", v19).
- **"He that rebuketh a man afterwards shall find more favour than he that flattereth with the tongue" → "One who reproves a man will afterward find favor more than one who makes the tongue smooth"** (v23) — Hebrew מוֹכִיחַ "one who reproves" (25:12); אַחֲרַי as pointed is "after me"; "afterward" is the usual reading (an old adverb form): uncertain. חֵן "favor" (WORDS-HE); מַחֲלִיק לָשׁוֹן "makes the tongue smooth" (hifil of חָלַק; 26:28 "smooth").
- **"Whoso robbeth his father or his mother, and saith, It is no transgression; the same is the companion of a destroyer" → "One who robs his father and his mother and says, 'It is no transgression,' is a companion to a man who destroys"** (v24) — Hebrew גּוֹזֵל "robs" (WORDS-HE); וְאִמּוֹ "and his mother"; אֵין־פָּשַׁע "there is no transgression"; חָבֵר "companion"; אִישׁ מַשְׁחִית "a man who destroys" (hifil participle of שָׁחַת "ruin"; "the destroyer", 2 Kgs 23:13).
- **"He that is of a proud heart stirreth up strife: but he that putteth his trust in the LORD shall be made fat" → "One wide of soul stirs up strife, and one who trusts in Yahweh will be made fat"** (v25) — Hebrew רְחַב־נֶפֶשׁ "wide of soul" (= greedy, of the appetite; KJV "proud heart" takes it of arrogance: both are defended; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"); גֵּרָה "stir up"; מָדוֹן "strife"; בּוֹטֵחַ עַל "trusts in"; יְדֻשָּׁן "will be made fat" (pual of דָּשֵׁן, WORDS-HE "grow fat"; = will prosper; kept).
- **"He that trusteth in his own heart is a fool: but whoso walketh wisely, he shall be delivered" → "One who trusts in his heart, he is a fool, and one who walks in wisdom, he will escape"** (v26) — Hebrew בְּלִבּוֹ "in his heart" (not "mind"; "own" not written); הוּא … הוּא "he … he", kept; בְּחָכְמָה "in wisdom"; יִמָּלֵט "will escape" (WORDS-HE נִמְלַט).
- **"He that giveth unto the poor shall not lack: but he that hideth his eyes shall have many a curse" → "One who gives to the destitute has no lack, and one who hides his eyes is great in curses"** (v27) — Hebrew רָשׁ "destitute"; אֵין מַחְסוֹר "there is no lack"; מַעְלִים עֵינָיו "hides his eyes"; רַב־מְאֵרוֹת "great in curses" (מְאֵרָה, noun of אָרַר "curse"; another word than קְלָלָה, 26:2; 27:14; English has one word).
- **"When the wicked rise, men hide themselves: but when they perish, the righteous increase" → "When the wicked rise, a man hides himself, and when they perish, the righteous multiply"** (v28) — Hebrew יִסָּתֵר אָדָם "a man hides himself" (singular; the verb of 27:12); וּבְאָבְדָם "and when they perish" (אָבַד); יִרְבּוּ "multiply" (רָבָה). v12 and v28 have the same first half: "when the wicked rise".

### Notes
- **Torah (v4, 7, 9)** — three sayings on keeping or forsaking torah; the word may name the torah of Yahweh or the teaching of the wise, as in 1:8; 3:1.
