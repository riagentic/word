# Proverbs 14

[1] The wise among women builds her house,
but folly with her hands throws it down.

[2] He who walks in his uprightness fears ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but the one devious in his ways despises him.

[3] In the mouth of a senseless one is a rod of pride,
but the lips of the wise keep them.

[4] Where there are no cattle, the crib is clean,
but much yield is by the power of an ox.

[5] A witness of faithfulness does not lie,
but a false witness breathes out lies.

[6] A scoffer seeks wisdom, and there is none,
but knowledge to the understanding is a light thing.

[7] Go from before a man who is a fool,
and you have not known lips of knowledge.

[8] The wisdom of the shrewd is to understand his way,
but the folly of fools is deceit.

[9] Guilt mocks the senseless,
but among the upright is good pleasure.

[10] The heart knows the bitterness of its soul,
and in its gladness a stranger does not mingle.

[11] The house of the wicked will be destroyed,
but the tent of the upright will blossom.

[12] There is a way that is right before a man,
but its end is ways of death.

[13] Even in laughter a heart is in pain,
and the end of it, of gladness, is grief.

[14] From his ways he who turns back in heart will be sated,
and a good man from upon himself.

[15] The simple one believes every word,
but the shrewd understands his step.

[16] The wise fears and turns aside from evil,
but a fool is enraged and trusts.

[17] One quick to anger does folly,
and a man of devisings is hated.

[18] The simple inherit folly,
but the shrewd crown themselves with knowledge.

[19] The evil are bowed down before the good,
and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.

[20] Even by his neighbor the destitute is hated,
but the lovers of the rich are many.

[21] He who despises his neighbor sins,
but he who shows favor to the humble, happy is he.

[22] Do not those who devise evil go astray?
But ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth — those who devise good.

[23] In all pain there will be profit,
but a word of the lips is only to want.

[24] The crown of the wise is their riches;
the folly of fools is folly.

[25] A deliverer of souls is a witness of truth,
but he who breathes out lies is deceit.

[26] In the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a trust of strength,
and for his sons there will be a refuge.

[27] The fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a fountain of life,
to turn aside from the snares of death.

[28] In the multitude of a people is the splendor of a king,
but in the lack of folk is the ruin of a dignitary.

[29] The slow to anger is great of understanding,
but the short of spirit raises folly.

[30] The life of the flesh is a heart of healing,
but rot of the bones is jealousy.

[31] He who extorts from the poor reproaches his Maker,
but he honors him who shows favor to the needy.

[32] In his evil the wicked is thrust down,
but the righteous takes refuge in his death.

[33] In the heart of the understanding wisdom rests,
and in the midst of fools it is made known.

[34] Righteousness exalts a nation,
but a disgrace to folks is sin.

[35] The good pleasure of a king is toward a servant who acts with insight,
but his raging will be on one who brings shame.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 26, 27) — Hebrew יהוה.
- **Layout** (throughout) — two lines (cola) to a saying, in the Hebrew order, as the accents divide them. וְ at the head of the second line is "but" where the lines are set against each other, "and" where they run together (v7, 10, 13, 14, 17, 19, 26, 33); v24 and v27 have no וְ.
- **Words of the chapter** — as Prov 13: כְּסִיל "fool", אֱוִיל "senseless one" (v3, 9), אִוֶּלֶת "folly", פֶּתִי "simple one" (v15, 18), עָרוּם "shrewd" (v8, 15, 18), נָבוֹן "understanding" (v6, 33), תְּבוּנָה "understanding" (v29), לֵץ "scoffer", רָשׁ "destitute" (v20; beside דַּל "poor", v31), נֶפֶשׁ "soul". Hebrew perfects of general truth (v1, 6, 18, 19, 31) are English presents.
- **"Every wise woman buildeth her house: but the foolish plucketh it down with her hands" → "The wise among women builds her house, but folly with her hands throws it down"** (v1) — Hebrew חַכְמוֹת נָשִׁים, "the wise ones of women", with a singular verb (בָּנְתָה); the same letters pointed חָכְמוֹת are "Wisdom" (9:1; 1:20), and many read so here: MT pointing kept. אִוֶּלֶת "folly" (not "the foolish woman"); הָרַס "throw down"; no "own".
- **"He that walketh in his uprightness feareth the LORD: but he that is perverse in his ways despiseth him" → "… but the one devious in his ways despises him"** (v2) — Hebrew נְלוֹז דְּרָכָיו, nifal of לוּז "turn aside": "devious of his ways"; בּוֹזֵהוּ "despises him" (Yahweh).
- **"In the mouth of the foolish is a rod of pride: but the lips of the wise shall preserve them" → "In the mouth of a senseless one is a rod of pride, but the lips of the wise keep them"** (v3) — Hebrew אֱוִיל "senseless one"; חֹטֶר "rod" (a shoot or stick, Isa 11:1; not the שֵׁבֶט "rod" of 13:24); the common change to גֵּוָה "back" ("a rod for his back") is not followed. תִּשְׁמוּרֵם "keep them".
- **"Where no oxen are, the crib is clean: but much increase is by the strength of the ox" → "Where there are no cattle, the crib is clean, but much yield is by the power of an ox"** (v4) — Hebrew אֲלָפִים "cattle" (as Deut 7:13), שׁוֹר "ox" (two words); בָּר "clean" (empty; בַּר "grain" has the same letters, and some read "there is no grain"); תְּבוּאוֹת "yield", כֹּחַ "power".
- **"A faithful witness will not lie: but a false witness will utter lies" → "A witness of faithfulness does not lie, but a false witness breathes out lies"** (v5) — Hebrew עֵד אֱמוּנִים "a witness of faithfulness"; וְיָפִיחַ כְּזָבִים עֵד שָׁקֶר, word for word "and breathes out lies a witness of falsehood" (the order "breathes out lies" first); יָפִיחַ hifil of פּוּחַ "breathe, blow" (also taken as a noun "witness": so v25).
- **"A scorner seeketh wisdom, and findeth it not" → "A scoffer seeks wisdom, and there is none"** (v6) — Hebrew וָאָיִן "and there is not". **"knowledge is easy unto him that understandeth" → "knowledge to the understanding is a light thing"** — Hebrew לְנָבוֹן נָקָל, the order kept; נָקֵל "a light thing".
- **"Go from the presence of a foolish man, when thou perceivest not in him the lips of knowledge" → "Go from before a man who is a fool, and you have not known lips of knowledge"** (v7) — Hebrew לֵךְ מִנֶּגֶד לְאִישׁ כְּסִיל, "go from in front of a man, a fool" (מִנֶּגֶד here "from before", not "opposite" as Gen 21:16); וּבַל־יָדַעְתָּ, a perfect with בַּל: "and you have not known" (there you will have found none); "when" and "in him" are not written.
- **"The wisdom of the prudent is to understand his way" → "The wisdom of the shrewd is to understand his way"** (v8) — Hebrew עָרוּם "shrewd"; הָבִין "understand"; מִרְמָה "deceit".
- **"Fools make a mock at sin: but among the righteous there is favour" → "Guilt mocks the senseless, but among the upright is good pleasure"** (v9) — Hebrew אֱוִלִים יָלִיץ אָשָׁם: the verb is singular, so אָשָׁם "guilt" (also "guilt offering") is its subject and the plural "senseless ones", standing first, its object; the common "fools mock at guilt" reads the verb against its number. Uncertain. יְשָׁרִים "upright" (not "righteous"); רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (as Deut 33:16).
- **"The heart knoweth his own bitterness; and a stranger doth not intermeddle with his joy" → "The heart knows the bitterness of its soul, and in its gladness a stranger does not mingle"** (v10) — Hebrew מָרַּת נַפְשׁוֹ "the bitterness of its soul"; בְּשִׂמְחָתוֹ "in its gladness" first; הִתְעָרַב "mingle" (Ps 106:35); זָר "stranger".
- **"shall be overthrown … the tabernacle of the upright shall flourish" → "will be destroyed … the tent of the upright will blossom"** (v11) — Hebrew יִשָּׁמֵד "be destroyed"; אֹהֶל "tent"; יַפְרִיחַ hifil of פָּרַח "blossom".
- **"There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death" → "There is a way that is right before a man, but its end is ways of death"** (v12 = 16:25, the same Hebrew and the same English) — Hebrew יָשָׁר לִפְנֵי־אִישׁ, literally "right before (the face of) a man": right as he sees it; "seemeth" is not written. דַּרְכֵי־מָוֶת "ways of death", no article.
- **"Even in laughter the heart is sorrowful; and the end of that mirth is heaviness" → "Even in laughter a heart is in pain, and the end of it, of gladness, is grief"** (v13) — Hebrew יִכְאַב־לֵב "a heart is in pain"; וְאַחֲרִיתָהּ שִׂמְחָה תוּגָה, literally "and its end, gladness, grief": the suffix "its" points ahead to "gladness"; תּוּגָה "grief".
- **"The backslider in heart shall be filled with his own ways: and a good man shall be satisfied from himself" → "From his ways he who turns back in heart will be sated, and a good man from upon himself"** (v14) — Hebrew מִדְּרָכָיו "from his ways" first; סוּג לֵב "one turned back in heart" (סוּג "turn back", Ps 44:18); וּמֵעָלָיו "and from upon him": obscure (from what is his own, or from what is above him); the change to מִמַּעֲלָלָיו "from his deeds" is not followed. יִשְׂבַּע "will be sated" serves both lines.
- **"The simple believeth every word: but the prudent man looketh well to his going" → "The simple one believes every word, but the shrewd understands his step"** (v15) — Hebrew יַאֲמִין לְ "believes"; יָבִין לַאֲשֻׁרוֹ "understands his step" (בִּין, as v8).
- **"A wise man feareth, and departeth from evil: but the fool rageth, and is confident" → "The wise fears and turns aside from evil, but a fool is enraged and trusts"** (v16) — Hebrew חָכָם "the wise" (no "man"); סָר מֵרָע "turns aside from evil" (as Job 1:1); מִתְעַבֵּר "is enraged" (hitpael, as Deut 3:26; also "oversteps"); בוֹטֵחַ "trusting", without an object.
- **"He that is soon angry dealeth foolishly: and a man of wicked devices is hated" → "One quick to anger does folly, and a man of devisings is hated"** (v17) — Hebrew קְצַר־אַפַּיִם, literally "short of nostrils" (the opposite of אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם "slow to anger", v29); יַעֲשֶׂה אִוֶּלֶת "does folly"; אִישׁ מְזִמּוֹת "a man of devisings" ("wicked" is not written; מְזִמָּה is said of good and bad devising).
- **"the prudent are crowned with knowledge" → "the shrewd crown themselves with knowledge"** (v18) — Hebrew יַכְתִּרוּ, hifil of כָּתַר (noun כֶּתֶר "crown"; piel "encircle", Ps 142:7): an active verb, "make a crown of" (or "encircle") knowledge; "themselves" is supplied. נָחֲלוּ "inherit".
- **"The evil bow before the good" → "The evil are bowed down before the good"** (v19) — Hebrew שַׁחוּ, qal of שָׁחַח "be bowed down" (Ps 42:5); the second line has no verb.
- **"The poor is hated even of his own neighbour: but the rich hath many friends" → "Even by his neighbor the destitute is hated, but the lovers of the rich are many"** (v20) — Hebrew גַּם־לְרֵעֵהוּ first; רֵעַ "neighbor" (also "friend"); רָשׁ "destitute"; אֹהֲבֵי עָשִׁיר "the lovers of the rich", not "friends".
- **"He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: but he that hath mercy on the poor, happy is he" → "… but he who shows favor to the humble, happy is he"** (v21) — Hebrew מְחוֹנֵן, polel of חָנַן "show favor"; the qere עֲנָוִים "humble"; the written form (ketiv) עניים "afflicted". אַשְׁרָיו "happy is he".
- **"Do they not err that devise evil? but mercy and truth shall be to them that devise good" → "Do not those who devise evil go astray? But chesed and truth — those who devise good"** (v22) — Hebrew חֹרְשֵׁי רָע / טוֹב "devisers of evil / of good" (as 3:29; 6:14; 12:20; חָרַשׁ is also "plow", Job 4:8, and "engrave, work as a craftsman": the image is not certain). The second line has no verb and no "to": the dash keeps the gap ("are with / are for"). חֶסֶד carries its token (as the plain-text חֶסֶד "disgrace" of v34 does not).
- **"In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury" → "In all pain there will be profit, but a word of the lips is only to want"** (v23) — Hebrew עֶצֶב "pain" (toil that hurts, as Gen 3:16); יִהְיֶה "there will be"; מוֹתָר "profit"; דְּבַר־שְׂפָתַיִם "a word of the lips"; מַחְסוֹר "want".
- **"but the foolishness of fools is folly" → "the folly of fools is folly"** (v24) — Hebrew אִוֶּלֶת כְּסִילִים אִוֶּלֶת, the one word twice, no "but" (no וְ).
- **"A true witness delivereth souls: but a deceitful witness speaketh lies" → "A deliverer of souls is a witness of truth, but he who breathes out lies is deceit"** (v25) — Hebrew מַצִּיל נְפָשׁוֹת עֵד אֱמֶת, "delivering souls — a witness of truth", the Hebrew order; וְיָפִחַ כְּזָבִים מִרְמָה "and one breathing out lies — deceit" (as v5; no "witness" in the line).
- **"In the fear of the LORD is strong confidence: and his children shall have a place of refuge" → "In the fear of Yahweh is a trust of strength, and for his sons there will be a refuge"** (v26) — Hebrew מִבְטַח־עֹז "a trust of strength"; לְבָנָיו "for his sons" (whose sons, the fearer's or Yahweh's, is not said); מַחְסֶה "refuge".
- **"The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death" → "… to turn aside from the snares of death"** (v27) — the second line is 13:14b, the same Hebrew and the same English.
- **"In the multitude of people is the king's honour: but in the want of people is the destruction of the prince" → "In the multitude of a people is the splendor of a king, but in the lack of folk is the ruin of a dignitary"** (v28) — Hebrew עָם "people", לְאֹם "folk" (two words); הַדְרַת "splendor"; אֶפֶס "lack, end"; מְחִתָּה "ruin"; רָזוֹן "dignitary" (from the root of רוֹזְנִים, Ps 2:2; the same letters are "leanness" in Ps 106:15: some read so here).
- **"He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly" → "The slow to anger is great of understanding, but the short of spirit raises folly"** (v29) — Hebrew אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם, literally "long of nostrils"; רַב־תְּבוּנָה "great of understanding"; קְצַר־רוּחַ "short of spirit"; מֵרִים "raises" (lifts high).
- **"A sound heart is the life of the flesh: but envy the rottenness of the bones" → "The life of the flesh is a heart of healing, but rot of the bones is jealousy"** (v30) — Hebrew חַיֵּי בְשָׂרִים לֵב מַרְפֵּא, the Hebrew order; בְּשָׂרִים "flesh" is plural (the body); לֵב מַרְפֵּא "a heart of healing" (מַרְפֵּא also "calm", 15:4); רְקַב "rot"; קִנְאָה "jealousy" (here of the envy of a rival).
- **"He that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker: but he that honoureth him hath mercy on the poor" → "He who extorts from the poor reproaches his Maker, but he honors him who shows favor to the needy"** (v31) — Hebrew עֹשֵׁק "extorts from" (as Lev 6:2); דָּל "poor"; חֵרֵף "reproaches"; עֹשֵׂהוּ "his Maker" (עָשָׂה "make"). וּמְכַבְּדוֹ חֹנֵן אֶבְיוֹן, literally "and his honorer is one showing favor to the needy": English needs the order "he honors him who …". אֶבְיוֹן "needy".
- **"The wicked is driven away in his wickedness: but the righteous hath hope in his death" → "In his evil the wicked is thrust down, but the righteous takes refuge in his death"** (v32) — Hebrew בְּרָעָתוֹ "in his evil" (or "in his calamity") first; יִדָּחֶה nifal of דָּחָה "thrust down" (Ps 36:12); חֹסֶה "takes refuge" (not "hope"). The Greek has "in his integrity" (בְּתֻמּוֹ, a change of letters; from memory): not followed, MT kept.
- **"but that which is in the midst of fools is made known" → "and in the midst of fools it is made known"** (v33) — Hebrew וּבְקֶרֶב כְּסִילִים תִּוָּדֵעַ: the subject is wisdom (feminine verb). The Greek and Syriac add "not" ("it is not known"; from memory): not followed. תָּנוּחַ "rests".
- **"Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people" → "… but a disgrace to folks is sin"** (v34) — Hebrew תְרוֹמֵם "exalts"; גּוֹי "nation"; וְחֶסֶד לְאֻמִּים חַטָּאת in the Hebrew order. חֶסֶד here is a second word with the letters of *chesed*: "disgrace" (Lev 20:17), plain text, no token (not the קָלוֹן "disgrace" of 13:18). לְאֻמִּים "folks", no "any".
- **"The king's favour is toward a wise servant: but his wrath is against him that causeth shame" → "The good pleasure of a king is toward a servant who acts with insight, but his raging will be on one who brings shame"** (v35) — Hebrew רְצוֹן "good pleasure" (as v9); עֶבֶד "servant" (the word is "slave": of a king's men "servant"); מַשְׂכִּיל "who acts with insight"; עֶבְרָה "raging" (Ps 7:6); תִּהְיֶה מֵבִישׁ, literally "will be one who brings shame" (the hifil of בּוֹשׁ; "on" is supplied).

### Notes
- **v12** — repeated word for word at 16:25.
- **v26–27** — the fear of Yahweh in two sayings side by side: a trust and a fountain.
