# Proverbs 30

[1] The words of Agur son of ⟦jakeh|Yaqeh|Jakeh⟧, the burden.
The declaration of the mighty man to ⟦ithiel|Itiel|Ithiel⟧,
to ⟦ithiel|Itiel|Ithiel⟧ and ⟦ucal|Uchal|Ucal⟧:

[2] For I am more brutish than a man,
and the understanding of mankind is not mine.

[3] And I have not learned wisdom,
and the knowledge of holy ones I know.

[4] Who has gone up to the heavens and come down?
Who has gathered wind in his fists?
Who has bound up waters in the cloak?
Who has raised up all the ends of the earth?
What is his name, and what is the name of his son, for you know?

[5] Every saying of ⟦eloah|Eloah|God⟧ is refined;
a shield is he to those who take refuge in him.

[6] Do not add to his words,
lest he reprove you, and you be shown a liar.

[7] Two things I have asked from you;
do not hold them back from me before I die.

[8] Emptiness and a word of lie put far from me;
poverty and riches do not give me;
feed me the bread of my fixed portion,

[9] lest I be sated and deny
and say, "Who is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧?"
and lest I be made poor and steal
and seize the name of my ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧.

[10] Do not slander a slave to his master,
lest he curse you, and you be guilty.

[11] A generation that curses its father,
and its mother it does not bless;

[12] a generation clean in its eyes,
and from its filth it is not washed;

[13] a generation — how high are its eyes,
and its eyelids are lifted up;

[14] a generation whose teeth are swords,
and whose jaw teeth are knives,
to devour the afflicted from the earth
and the needy from mankind.

[15] The leech has two daughters: "Give! Give!"
Three are these that will not be sated,
four that have not said, "Enough":

[16] Sheol, and the closing up of the womb,
earth not sated with water,
and fire that has not said, "Enough."

[17] An eye that mocks a father
and despises obedience to a mother,
the ravens of the wadi will pick it out,
and the sons of the eagle will eat it.

[18] Three are they that are too wonderful for me,
and four I do not know:

[19] the way of the eagle in the heavens,
the way of a serpent upon a rock,
the way of a ship in the heart of the sea,
and the way of a man with a young woman.

[20] Thus is the way of a woman who commits adultery:
she eats and wipes her mouth
and says, "I have not worked wickedness."

[21] Under three the earth trembles,
and under four it is not able to bear:

[22] under a slave when he reigns,
and a base fool when he is sated with bread;

[23] under a hated woman when she is married,
and a maidservant when she dispossesses her mistress.

[24] Four are they, the small ones of the earth,
and they are wise, made wise:

[25] the ants are a people not strong,
and they prepare their bread in the summer;

[26] hyraxes are a people not mighty,
and they put their house in the crag;

[27] a king there is none for the locusts,
and they go out dividing in ranks, all of them;

[28] a lizard takes hold with the hands,
and it is in the palaces of a king.

[29] Three are they that step well,
and four that go well:

[30] an old lion, mighty among beasts,
and it does not turn back from the face of any;

[31] one girded at the loins, or a he-goat,
and a king with whom there is no rising up.

[32] If you have been a base fool in lifting yourself up,
and if you have devised,
hand to mouth!

[33] For the pressing of milk brings out curds,
and the pressing of the nose brings out blood,
and the pressing of anger brings out contention.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v9) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (v9) — Hebrew אֱלֹהָי "my Elohim"; v5 has אֱלוֹהַּ *Eloah*.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v1 is now split where the accents divide it. The numerical sayings (v15–31) stand as printed: "three … four", the things listed, without "things" or "there are" added.
- **"The words of Agur the son of Jakeh, even the prophecy" → "The words of Agur son of Jakeh, the burden"** (v1) — Hebrew הַמַּשָּׂא, with the article: "the burden" (what is lifted up: an utterance laid on the speaker; "oracle", "prophecy" in English Bibles; as 2 Kgs 9:25 and the prophets). Some take it as the place or people Massa (Gen 25:14; Prov 31:1), "Agur son of Jakeh of Massa"; that needs another form of the word (הַמַּשָּׂאִי) and is not the text.
- **"the man spake unto Ithiel, even unto Ithiel and Ucal" → "The declaration of the mighty man to Ithiel, to Ithiel and Ucal"** (v1) — Hebrew נְאֻם הַגֶּבֶר, as Num 24:3 and 2 Sam 23:1 ("the declaration of the mighty man"; גֶּבֶר, beside אִישׁ and אָדָם in v2). לְאִיתִיאֵל לְאִיתִיאֵל וְאֻכָל is printed with the vowels of two names, Ithiel (the name of Neh 11:7) and Ucal, and is translated so. The same letters, divided and pointed otherwise (לָאִיתִי אֵל … וָאֵכֶל), read "I have wearied myself, El, I have wearied myself, El, and I am consumed"; that reading changes no letter, fits v2–3, and is often proposed, but it is not the printed text.
- **"Surely I am more brutish than any man, and have not the understanding of a man" → "For I am more brutish than a man, and the understanding of mankind is not mine"** (v2) — Hebrew כִּי "for" (or "surely"); בַּעַר "brutish" (WISDOM.md); מֵאִישׁ "than a man" (or "too brutish to be a man"); בִּינַת אָדָם "the understanding of mankind": אִישׁ and אָדָם are kept apart (WORDS-HE).
- **"I neither learned wisdom, nor have the knowledge of the holy" → "And I have not learned wisdom, and the knowledge of holy ones I know"** (v3) — Hebrew וְלֹא־לָמַדְתִּי חָכְמָה וְדַעַת קְדֹשִׁים אֵדָע: the second line has no "not"; the negative of the first line may carry over ("nor do I know"), or the line says what he does know; the Hebrew is kept. קְדֹשִׁים is plural, as 9:10: taken of God ("the Holy One", a plural as אֱלֹהִים קְדֹשִׁים Josh 24:19) or of holy beings; lower case leaves it open.
- **"Who hath ascended up into heaven, or descended? … in a garment? who hath established … if thou canst tell?" → "Who has gone up to the heavens and come down? … in the cloak? Who has raised up … for you know?"** (v4) — word for word: עָלָה "go up", וַיֵּרַד "and came down" (no "or"); שָׁמַיִם "heavens"; רוּחַ "wind" (also "spirit"); בְּחָפְנָיו "in his fists" (two hollow hands); צָרַר "bind up"; בַּשִּׂמְלָה "in the cloak" (with the article); הֵקִים "raise up" (make stand); אַפְסֵי־אָרֶץ "the ends of the earth"; מַה־שֶּׁם־בְּנוֹ "what is the name of his son"; כִּי תֵדָע "for you know", ironic (as Job 38:5).
- **"Every word of God is pure" → "Every saying of Eloah is refined"** (v5) — Hebrew אִמְרַת אֱלוֹהַּ "the saying of Eloah"; צְרוּפָה "refined" (as metal; Ps 18:30). חָסָה "take refuge".
- **"thou be found a liar" → "you be shown a liar"** (v6) — Hebrew נִכְזַבְתָּ, nifal of כָּזַב "lie": be proved a liar. הוֹכִיחַ "reprove".
- **"Two things have I required of thee; deny me them not" → "Two things I have asked from you; do not hold them back from me"** (v7) — Hebrew שָׁאַל "ask"; מָנַע "hold back" (WORDS-HE). "Things" and "them" are not written (שְׁתַּיִם "two", feminine).
- **"vanity and lies … feed me with food convenient for me" → "Emptiness and a word of lie … feed me the bread of my fixed portion"** (v8) — Hebrew שָׁוְא "emptiness" (as the "empty" row); דְּבַר־כָּזָב "a word of lie"; רֵאשׁ "poverty" (one root with רָשׁ "destitute", 29:13); הַטְרִיפֵנִי, from טֶרֶף "food" (literally "prey"); לֶחֶם חֻקִּי "the bread of my fixed portion": חֹק is the word for "statute" (WORDS-HE); of food it is the share laid down, as Gen 47:22 ("fixed portion") and 31:15.
- **"Lest I be full, and deny thee … or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain" → "lest I be sated and deny … and lest I be made poor and steal and seize the name of my Elohim"** (v9) — Hebrew אֶשְׂבַּע "be sated"; כִּחַשְׁתִּי "deny" (act falsely), with no object ("you" is not written); וּפֶן "and lest" (not "or"); אִוָּרֵשׁ, nifal of יָרַשׁ "be dispossessed": be made poor; תָּפַשְׂתִּי "seize" (lay hold of the name: make free with it, profane it).
- **"Accuse not a servant unto his master … thou be found guilty" → "Do not slander a slave to his master … you be guilty"** (v10) — Hebrew הִלְשִׁין "slander" (from לָשׁוֹן "tongue"); עֶבֶד "slave"; אֲדֹנָיו qere "his master" (the written form אדנו is a singular spelling; the sense is the same); קִלֵּל "curse" (literally "make light of"); אָשַׁם "be guilty". "He" who curses is not named (the slave, most take it).
- **"There is a generation that curseth their father" → "A generation that curses its father"** (v11–14) — Hebrew דּוֹר "generation" four times, each line beginning with it and no verb "there is"; the pronouns are singular ("its father, its mother, its eyes"). v11 קִלֵּל "curse" (as v10); v12 טָהוֹר "clean", בְּעֵינָיו "in its eyes" ("own" is not written), צֹאָה "filth" (excrement); v13 מָה־רָמוּ "how high are"; v14 מְתַלְּעוֹת "jaw teeth", מַאֲכָלוֹת "knives" (literally "eaters", from אָכַל, as לֶאֱכֹל "to devour"), עֲנִיִּים "the afflicted", אֶבְיוֹנִים "the needy", מֵאָדָם "from mankind".
- **"The horseleach hath two daughters, crying, Give, give. There are three things that are never satisfied, yea, four things say not, It is enough" → "The leech has two daughters: 'Give! Give!' Three are these that will not be sated, four that have not said, 'Enough'"** (v15) — Hebrew עֲלוּקָה (only here) "leech"; הַב הַב "Give! Give!" (no "crying"); שָׁלוֹשׁ הֵנָּה "three are these"; לֹא אָמְרוּ is a perfect, "have not said"; הוֹן, the word for "wealth" (29:3), here "enough".
- **"The grave; and the barren womb; the earth that is not filled with water" → "Sheol, and the closing up of the womb, earth not sated with water"** (v16) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" (not "the grave"); עֹצֶר רָחַם "the closing up of the womb" (עָצַר "close up", as Gen 20:18); שָׂבַע "be sated" (as v15); no articles.
- **"The eye that mocketh at his father, and despiseth to obey his mother, the ravens of the valley … the young eagles" → "An eye that mocks a father and despises obedience to a mother, the ravens of the wadi … the sons of the eagle"** (v17) — Hebrew עַיִן without the article; לְאָב, לִיקֲּהַת־אֵם without "his"; יְקָּהָה "obedience" (as Gen 49:10; uncertain here); נַחַל "wadi"; בְּנֵי־נֶשֶׁר "sons of the eagle" (the young; נֶשֶׁר also covers the great vultures).
- **"There be three things which are too wonderful for me, yea, four which I know not" → "Three are they that are too wonderful for me, and four I do not know"** (v18) — Hebrew נִפְלָא מִן "be too wonderful for" (WORDS-HE); וְאַרְבָּעָה qere, the written form וארבע is the other gender of the numeral (no change of sense).
- **"The way of an eagle in the air … in the midst of the sea … with a maid" → "the way of the eagle in the heavens … in the heart of the sea … with a young woman"** (v19) — Hebrew הַנֶּשֶׁר "the eagle" (with the article); בַּשָּׁמַיִם "in the heavens"; בְלֶב־יָם "in the heart of the sea"; גֶּבֶר "man" (alone here; the "mighty man" of v1 is the set formula); עַלְמָה "young woman" (WORDS-HE: the word does not say "virgin").
- **"Such is the way of an adulterous woman … I have done no wickedness" → "Thus is the way of a woman who commits adultery … I have not worked wickedness"** (v20) — Hebrew מְנָאָפֶת, participle of נָאַף "commit adultery"; מָחָה "wipe"; פָּעַל אָוֶן "work wickedness" (as פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness"). The verbs are perfects of what she always does: English present.
- **"For three things the earth is disquieted, and for four which it cannot bear" → "Under three the earth trembles, and under four it is not able to bear"** (v21) — Hebrew תַּחַת "under" (four times, v21–23); רָגַז "tremble" (the verb of 29:9 "be agitated"); נָשָׂא "bear".
- **"a fool when he is filled with meat" → "a base fool when he is sated with bread"** (v22) — Hebrew נָבָל "base fool" (WISDOM.md; not כְּסִיל "fool"); לֶחֶם "bread".
- **"an odious woman when she is married; and an handmaid that is heir to her mistress" → "a hated woman when she is married, and a maidservant when she dispossesses her mistress"** (v23) — Hebrew שְׂנוּאָה "hated" (as Deut 21:15; WORDS-HE); תִּבָּעֵל, nifal of בָּעַל "become the husband of" (literally "is owned": taken as wife); שִׁפְחָה "maidservant"; תִּירַשׁ גְּבִרְתָּהּ, יָרַשׁ with a person as object: "dispossess" (WORDS-HE; she takes her mistress's place; "be heir to" in KJV).
- **"There be four things which are little upon the earth, but they are exceeding wise" → "Four are they, the small ones of the earth, and they are wise, made wise"** (v24) — Hebrew קְטַנֵּי־אָרֶץ "the small ones of the earth"; חֲכָמִים מְחֻכָּמִים "wise, made wise" (the pual participle of the same root: wise beyond the ordinary; "exceedingly wise" is the sense).
- **"yet they prepare their meat in the summer" → "and they prepare their bread in the summer"** (v25) — Hebrew וַיָּכִינוּ "and they prepare" (וְ, not "yet"; so v26, 27); לֶחֶם "bread"; עַם לֹא־עָז "a people not strong".
- **"The conies are but a feeble folk, yet make they their houses in the rocks" → "hyraxes are a people not mighty, and they put their house in the crag"** (v26) — Hebrew שְׁפַנִּים "hyraxes" (as Lev 11:5), without the article; עַם לֹא־עָצוּם "a people not mighty" (the same words as v25 but עָצוּם); שִׂים "put"; בֵּיתָם "their house", singular; סֶלַע "crag".
- **"yet go they forth all of them by bands" → "and they go out dividing in ranks, all of them"** (v27) — Hebrew אַרְבֶּה "locusts" (a collective singular); חֹצֵץ "dividing" (from חָצַץ "divide"): in divisions; כֻּלּוֹ "all of it".
- **"The spider taketh hold with her hands, and is in kings' palaces" → "a lizard takes hold with the hands, and it is in the palaces of a king"** (v28) — Hebrew שְׂמָמִית (only here): a lizard (the gecko is commonly meant), uncertain (KJV "spider"); בְּיָדַיִם "with the hands" (no "her"); תְּתַפֵּשׂ may also be "you can seize (it)" with the hands; בְּהֵיכְלֵי מֶלֶךְ "in the palaces of a king".
- **"There be three things which go well, yea, four are comely in going" → "Three are they that step well, and four that go well"** (v29) — Hebrew מֵיטִיבֵי צָעַד and מֵיטִבֵי לָכֶת, the hifil of יָטַב "do well" with "step" and "go": the same verb in both lines.
- **"A lion which is strongest among beasts, and turneth not away for any" → "an old lion, mighty among beasts, and it does not turn back from the face of any"** (v30) — Hebrew לַיִשׁ "old lion" (as Job 4:11, beside אַרְיֵה "lion"; the kind is uncertain); גִּבּוֹר "mighty"; בְּהֵמָה "beast"; מִפְּנֵי "from the face of" (WORDS-HE).
- **"A greyhound; an he goat also; and a king, against whom there is no rising up" → "one girded at the loins, or a he-goat, and a king with whom there is no rising up"** (v31) — Hebrew זַרְזִיר מָתְנַיִם (only here), literally "girded (?) of loins": the animal is not known (KJV "greyhound"; also taken as a cock or a war horse); מָתְנַיִם "loins"; אוֹ "or"; תַּיִשׁ "he-goat". אַלְקוּם (only here): read as אַל "no" + קוּם "rising up", "no rising up with (= against) him"; others take it as a word for a band of men ("whose troops are with him"). Both are uncertain.
- **"If thou hast done foolishly in lifting up thyself, or if thou hast thought evil, lay thine hand upon thy mouth" → "If you have been a base fool in lifting yourself up, and if you have devised, hand to mouth!"** (v32) — Hebrew נָבַלְתָּ, verb of נָבָל "base fool" (v22); הִתְנַשֵּׂא "lift oneself up"; וְאִם "and if" (not "or"); זַמּוֹתָ "you have devised" (זָמַם, with no object: "evil" is not written); יָד לְפֶה "hand to mouth", with no verb.
- **"Surely the churning of milk bringeth forth butter … so the forcing of wrath bringeth forth strife" → "For the pressing of milk brings out curds … and the pressing of anger brings out contention"** (v33) — Hebrew כִּי "for"; מִיץ "pressing" three times (only here; one word); יוֹצִיא "brings out" three times; חֶמְאָה "curds"; אַף "nose" in the second line and אַפַּיִם "anger" (literally "two nostrils") in the third: one word, set side by side; רִיב "contention" (WORDS-HE); "so" is "and".

### Notes
- **Agur** — unknown otherwise; the Hebrew says no more than "son of Jakeh". The chapter's form: a confession (v2–4), a word on the sayings of Eloah (v5–6), a prayer (v7–9), and numerical sayings ("three … four", v15–31).
