# Proverbs 21

[1] Streams of water — the heart of a king in the hand of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
wherever he delights, he turns it.

[2] Every way of a man is right in his eyes,
but the weigher of hearts is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] To do righteousness and judgment
is chosen by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ rather than sacrifice.

[4] Height of eyes and breadth of heart —
the lamp of the wicked is sin.

[5] The plans of the diligent are surely to profit,
and everyone who hastens, surely to lack.

[6] The getting of treasures by a tongue of falsehood
is a driven vapor — those who seek death.

[7] The havoc of the wicked drags them away,
for they refuse to do judgment.

[8] Twisted is the way of a man, and strange;
but the pure — his work is upright.

[9] Better to dwell on a corner of a roof
than a woman of strifes and a shared house.

[10] The soul of the wicked desires evil;
his neighbor is not favored in his eyes.

[11] When a scoffer is punished, a simple one becomes wise;
and when insight is given to the wise, he takes knowledge.

[12] A righteous one acts with insight toward the house of the wicked;
he overturns the wicked to evil.

[13] Whoever stops his ear at the cry of the poor,
he too will call and not be answered.

[14] A gift in secret subdues anger,
and a bribe in the bosom, strong fury.

[15] It is gladness to the righteous to do judgment,
but ruin to the workers of wickedness.

[16] A man who wanders from the way of acting with insight
will rest in the assembly of the shades.

[17] A man of lack — one who loves gladness;
one who loves wine and oil will not become rich.

[18] A ransom for the righteous is the wicked,
and in the place of the upright, the treacherous.

[19] Better to dwell in a land of wilderness
than a woman of strifes and vexation.

[20] Desirable treasure and oil are in the habitation of the wise,
but a fool of a man swallows it.

[21] Whoever pursues righteousness and ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧
will find life, righteousness and glory.

[22] The city of the mighty a wise man went up,
and brought down the strength of its trust.

[23] Whoever keeps his mouth and his tongue
keeps his soul from distresses.

[24] Presumptuous, haughty — scoffer is his name;
he acts in the raging of presumption.

[25] The craving of the sluggard puts him to death,
for his hands refuse to do.

[26] All the day he craves a craving,
but the righteous gives and does not withhold.

[27] The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination;
how much more when he brings it with depravity.

[28] A witness of lies will perish,
but a man who hears will speak forever.

[29] A wicked man makes his face strong,
but the upright — he understands his way.

[30] There is no wisdom and there is no understanding,
and there is no counsel, over against ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[31] The horse is made ready for the day of battle,
but to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the salvation.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 30, 31) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **"The king's heart is in the hand of the LORD, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will" → "Streams of water — the heart of a king in the hand of Yahweh; wherever he delights, he turns it"** (v1) — Hebrew פַּלְגֵי־מַיִם stands first, with no "as" and no verb: the two nouns are set side by side (WORDS-HE פֶּלֶג "stream"); לֶב־מֶלֶךְ "the heart of a king" (no article); יַחְפֹּץ "he delights" (חָפֵץ); יַטֶּנּוּ hifil of נָטָה "turn, incline". Who "he" is (Yahweh or the king) the Hebrew leaves open.
- **"right in his own eyes: but the LORD pondereth the hearts" → "right in his eyes, but the weigher of hearts is Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew בְּעֵינָיו "in his eyes" ("own" not written); יָשָׁר of a way "right" (WORDS-HE reviewer row); תֹכֵן לִבּוֹת, participle of תָּכַן "measure, weigh", with "hearts" plural.
- **"To do justice and judgment is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice" → "To do righteousness and judgment is chosen by Yahweh rather than sacrifice"** (v3) — Hebrew צְדָקָה "righteousness", מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (= justice: once per chapter); נִבְחָר "chosen" (nifal participle of בָּחַר, as 22:1), לַיהוָה "to / by Yahweh", מִן "rather than".
- **"An high look, and a proud heart, and the plowing of the wicked, is sin" → "Height of eyes and breadth of heart — the lamp of the wicked is sin"** (v4) — Hebrew רוּם־עֵינַיִם וּרְחַב־לֵב, word for word (the proud: as Ps 101:5 "high of eyes and broad of heart"). נִר is pointed as the word for "lamp" (נֵר / נִיר, 1 Kgs 11:36; 24:20) and also as the word for "tillage, newly broken ground" (Jer 4:3; Prov 13:23, KJV "plowing"): uncertain. How the two lines join (the proud look is the lamp, or a list of three) is not said.
- **"The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want" → "The plans of the diligent are surely to profit, and everyone who hastens, surely to lack"** (v5) — Hebrew מַחְשְׁבוֹת "plans" (as Ps 33:10); אַךְ twice, "surely"; מוֹתָר "profit" (what is left over); אָץ "one who hastens"; מַחְסוֹר "lack" (as v17; 22:16).
- **"is a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death" → "is a driven vapor — those who seek death"** (v6) — Hebrew הֶבֶל נִדָּף: הֶבֶל "vapor" ("vanity" in traditional versions), נִדָּף "driven away" (by wind). מְבַקְשֵׁי־מָוֶת "seekers of death" stands loose at the end (the treasure-getters are meant); the MT is kept. Some Hebrew manuscripts and the Septuagint are cited for מוֹקְשֵׁי "snares of death" (from memory; not followed). פֹּעַל "getting", literally "work, doing"; בִּלְשׁוֹן שָׁקֶר "by a tongue of falsehood".
- **"The robbery of the wicked shall destroy them" → "The havoc of the wicked drags them away"** (v7) — Hebrew שֹׁד "havoc, devastation" (as 24:2); יְגוֹרֵם from גָּרַר "drag away". מֵאֲנוּ "they refuse" (a perfect of what is always so).
- **"The way of man is froward and strange" → "Twisted is the way of a man, and strange"** (v8) — Hebrew הֲפַכְפַּךְ "twisted" (only here; from הָפַךְ "turn"); וָזָר read as "and strange" (זָר, the word of the "strange woman"); it is also taken as a word found only here, "guilty" (laden): uncertain. זַךְ "pure", פָּעֳלוֹ "his work" (as 24:12, 29).
- **"It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman in a wide house" → "Better to dwell on a corner of a roof than a woman of strifes and a shared house"** (v9) — Hebrew עַל־פִּנַּת־גָּג "on a corner of a roof"; מֵאֵשֶׁת מִדְיָנִים "than a woman of strifes" (מִדְיָנִים, plural of the kind of מָדוֹן "strife"; "with" not written); וּבֵית חָבֶר "and a house of association" (חֶבֶר "fellowship, company": a house shared with her; not "wide"). The same saying is 25:24: one wording where the Hebrew is the same.
- **"his neighbour findeth no favour in his eyes" → "his neighbor is not favored in his eyes"** (v10) — Hebrew אִוְּתָה "desires" (piel of אָוָה); לֹא־יֻחַן, passive of חָנַן "show favor": "is not shown favor". רֵעֵהוּ "his neighbor" (the same word is "friend" in 22:11).
- **"When the scorner is punished, the simple is made wise: and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge" → "When a scoffer is punished, a simple one becomes wise; and when insight is given to the wise, he takes knowledge"** (v11) — Hebrew בַּעְנָשׁ "in punishing" (עָנַשׁ, elsewhere "fine" of a money penalty, Exod 21:22; here no money is named); וּבְהַשְׂכִּיל לְחָכָם "and in giving insight to a wise one" (הִשְׂכִּיל; or "when a wise one acts with insight"); יִקַּח "he takes" (לָקַח). Whether the wise man or the simple one takes knowledge is not said.
- **"The righteous man wisely considereth the house of the wicked: but God overthroweth the wicked for their wickedness" → "A righteous one acts with insight toward the house of the wicked; he overturns the wicked to evil"** (v12) — Hebrew מַשְׂכִּיל צַדִּיק: "a righteous one" (no article) "acting with insight" (הִשְׂכִּיל, WISDOM.md); "God" is not written. Whether the Righteous One is God (who then overturns the wicked) or a righteous man (and the overturning is God's or his) is not said: kept open. מְסַלֵּף "overturning" (סִלֵּף); לָרָע "to evil" (ruin).
- **"Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard" → "Whoever stops his ear at the cry of the poor, he too will call and not be answered"** (v13) — Hebrew אָזְנוֹ "his ear" (singular); זַעֲקַת "cry"; דָּל "poor"; יִקְרָא "call", יֵעָנֶה "be answered".
- **"A gift in secret pacifieth anger: and a reward in the bosom strong wrath" → "A gift in secret subdues anger, and a bribe in the bosom, strong fury"** (v14) — Hebrew יִכְפֶּה, only here: "subdue, avert" (uncertain); אַף "anger" (literally "nose"); שֹׁחַד "bribe"; בַּחֵק "in the bosom" (the fold of the garment); חֵמָה עַזָּה "strong fury".
- **"It is joy to the just to do judgment: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity" → "It is gladness to the righteous to do judgment, but ruin to the workers of wickedness"** (v15) — Hebrew שִׂמְחָה "gladness"; מְחִתָּה "ruin" (the Psalms row; "terror" is the other sense of the word); פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן "workers of wickedness" (as Ps 5:5; 6:8).
- **"The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead" → "A man who wanders from the way of acting with insight will rest in the assembly of the shades"** (v16) — Hebrew הַשְׂכֵּל, the infinitive of הִשְׂכִּיל (as 1:3); קָהָל "assembly"; יָנוּחַ "will rest" (נוּחַ). רְפָאִים "the shades" of the dead, plain, no token (reviewer ruling; as Job 26:5; Ps 88:10; Prov 9:18; not the ancient people of that name).
- **"He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man" → "A man of lack — one who loves gladness"** (v17) — Hebrew אִישׁ מַחְסוֹר "a man of lack" (no verb; "shall be" not written); שִׂמְחָה "gladness" (as v15); יַעֲשִׁיר "become rich".
- **"the transgressor for the upright" → "in the place of the upright, the treacherous"** (v18) — Hebrew כֹּפֶר "ransom"; וְתַחַת "and in the place of" (תַּחַת); בּוֹגֵד "one who deals treacherously" (בָּגַד; not פֶּשַׁע "transgression"). The Hebrew order is kept.
- **"It is better to dwell in the wilderness, than with a contentious and an angry woman" → "Better to dwell in a land of wilderness than a woman of strifes and vexation"** (v19) — Hebrew בְּאֶרֶץ־מִדְבָּר "in a land of wilderness"; מִדְיָנִים is the qere; the written form מדונים is the plural of מָדוֹן "strife" (same sense). כַּעַס "vexation".
- **"There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise; but a foolish man spendeth it up" → "Desirable treasure and oil are in the habitation of the wise, but a fool of a man swallows it"** (v20) — Hebrew אוֹצָר נֶחְמָד "desirable treasure" (אוֹצָר also "treasury, store"); נְוֵה "habitation"; כְּסִיל אָדָם "a fool of a man" (WISDOM.md: כְּסִיל "fool"); יְבַלְּעֶנּוּ "swallows it" (בִּלַּע).
- **"He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, righteousness, and honour" → "Whoever pursues righteousness and chesed will find life, righteousness and glory"** (v21) — Hebrew רֹדֵף "pursuing" (רָדַף); חֶסֶד carries its token (reviewer row; the verse had plain "steadfast love"); כָּבוֹד "glory"; יִמְצָא "will find".
- **"A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof" → "The city of the mighty a wise man went up, and brought down the strength of its trust"** (v22) — Hebrew עִיר גִּבֹּרִים stands first; עָלָה "went up" (with the city as direct object: went up on it), then וַיֹּרֶד "and brought down", a perfect and a narrative form: a deed told as done (the present is the usual rendering). עֹז "strength"; מִבְטֶחָה "its trust" (what it trusted in).
- **"keepeth his soul from troubles" → "keeps his soul from distresses"** (v23) — Hebrew צָרוֹת "distresses" (plural of צָרָה); שֹׁמֵר twice, "keeps".
- **"Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath" → "Presumptuous, haughty — scoffer is his name; he acts in the raging of presumption"** (v24) — Hebrew זֵד "presumptuous" and זָדוֹן "presumption" (one root, זִיד); יָהִיר "haughty" (also Hab 2:5); לֵץ "scoffer"; עֶבְרָה "raging" (lit. "overflowing").
- **"The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour" → "The craving of the sluggard puts him to death, for his hands refuse to do"** (v25) — Hebrew תַּאֲוָה "craving" (as v26); תְּמִיתֶנּוּ hifil of מוּת "put to death"; לַעֲשׂוֹת "to do" (no object: work is meant).
- **"He coveteth greedily all the day long: but the righteous giveth and spareth not" → "All the day he craves a craving, but the righteous gives and does not withhold"** (v26) — Hebrew הִתְאַוָּה תַאֲוָה, verb and noun of one root (as Num 11:4); the subject is not named (the sluggard of v25, or anyone); חָשַׂךְ "withhold".
- **"how much more, when he bringeth it with a wicked mind?" → "how much more when he brings it with depravity"** (v27) — Hebrew אַף כִּי "how much more"; בְזִמָּה "with depravity" (זִמָּה, a shameful design: WORDS-HE).
- **"A false witness shall perish: but the man that heareth speaketh constantly" → "A witness of lies will perish, but a man who hears will speak forever"** (v28) — Hebrew עֵד־כְּזָבִים "a witness of lies" (כָּזָב); שׁוֹמֵעַ "hearing"; לָנֶצַח יְדַבֵּר "will speak forever": what is meant (his word stands, he speaks unchallenged, or he speaks to the end) is not said.
- **"A wicked man hardeneth his face: but as for the upright, he directeth his way" → "A wicked man makes his face strong, but the upright — he understands his way"** (v29) — Hebrew הֵעֵז … בְּפָנָיו "makes strong with his face" (עָזַז; = shows a bold, defiant face). יָבִין "he understands" and דַּרְכּוֹ "his way" are the qere; the written forms יכין דרכיו read "he establishes his ways".
- **"There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the LORD" → "There is no wisdom and there is no understanding, and there is no counsel, over against Yahweh"** (v30) — Hebrew אֵין three times; תְּבוּנָה "understanding" (WISDOM.md); לְנֶגֶד "over against, before" (both "against" and "in the sight of" are read).
- **"The horse is prepared against the day of battle: but safety is of the LORD" → "The horse is made ready for the day of battle, but to Yahweh is the salvation"** (v31) — Hebrew מוּכָן hofal of כּוּן "made ready"; מִלְחָמָה "battle"; הַתְּשׁוּעָה "the salvation" (with the article; "victory" is the sense).
