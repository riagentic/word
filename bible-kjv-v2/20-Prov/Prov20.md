# Proverbs 20

[1] A scoffer is wine, strong drink is in uproar,
and everyone who reels by it will not be wise.

[2] Like the growl of a young lion is the dread of a king;
he who provokes him to fury sins against his soul.

[3] It is glory to a man to sit away from contention,
but every senseless one breaks out.

[4] Because of winter the sluggard does not plow;
and he asks in the harvest, and there is nothing.

[5] Deep waters is counsel in the heart of a man,
but a man of understanding draws it out.

[6] Many a man proclaims, each one, his ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧,
but a man of faithfulness, who can find?

[7] One who walks about in his integrity is righteous;
happy are his sons after him!

[8] A king who sits on the throne of judgment
winnows with his eyes all evil.

[9] Who can say, "I have made my heart pure;
I am clean from my sin"?

[10] A weight and a weight, an ephah and an ephah —
an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, even both of them.

[11] Even by his deeds a boy makes himself known,
whether pure and whether upright is his work.

[12] The hearing ear and the seeing eye —
⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has made even both of them.

[13] Do not love sleep, lest you be dispossessed;
open your eyes, be sated with bread.

[14] "Bad, bad," says the buyer,
and he goes off, then he boasts.

[15] There is gold and abundance of rubies,
but a precious vessel are lips of knowledge.

[16] Take his garment, for he has become surety for a stranger,
and on behalf of a foreign woman take him in pledge.

[17] Sweet to a man is bread of falsehood,
but afterward his mouth will be filled with gravel.

[18] Plans by counsel are established,
and with steering make war.

[19] He who reveals secret counsel goes about as a talebearer;
and with one who opens wide his lips do not mix.

[20] He who curses his father and his mother —
his lamp will be put out in the pupil of darkness.

[21] An inheritance hurried at the first —
and its latter end will not be blessed.

[22] Do not say, "Let me repay evil";
wait for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and he will save you.

[23] An abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is a weight and a weight,
and scales of deceit are not good.

[24] From ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are the steps of a mighty man,
and man — how can he understand his way?

[25] It is a snare to a man to say rashly, "Holy,"
and after vows to inquire.

[26] A wise king winnows the wicked
and brings back the wheel over them.

[27] The lamp of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the ⟦nishmat-adam|nishmat adam — breath of man —|spirit of man⟧,
searching all the chambers of the belly.

[28] ⟦steadfast-love|Chesed|Steadfast love⟧ and truth guard a king,
and he upholds by ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ his throne.

[29] The beauty of young men is their power,
and the splendor of old men is gray hair.

[30] The stripes of a wound are a scouring against evil,
and blows, the chambers of the belly.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v10, 12, 22, 23, 24, 27) — Hebrew יהוה.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order, each with its וְ as the Hebrew has it.
- **"Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging: and whosoever is deceived thereby is not wise" → "A scoffer is wine, strong drink is in uproar, and everyone who reels by it will not be wise"** (v1) — Hebrew לֵץ "scoffer"; Hebrew order; הֹמֶה "in uproar" (הָמָה "roar, murmur"); שֹׁגֶה בּוֹ "reels by it" (שָׁגָה of wine, as 5:19, 20, 23; Isa 28:7; "err" in 19:27); לֹא יֶחְכָּם "will not be wise".
- **"The fear of a king is as the roaring of a lion: whoso provoketh him to anger sinneth against his own soul" → "Like the growl of a young lion is the dread of a king; he who provokes him to fury sins against his soul"** (v2) — Hebrew נַהַם כַּכְּפִיר "like the growl of a young lion" (as 19:12a); אֵימָה "dread"; מִתְעַבְּרוֹ hitpael of עָבַר with a suffix, "who makes himself furious with him / provokes him to fury" (עֶבְרָה "fury"); חוֹטֵא נַפְשׁוֹ "sins against his soul" (or "misses / forfeits his soul": the חָטָא of 19:2).
- **"It is an honour for a man to cease from strife: but every fool will be meddling" → "It is glory to a man to sit away from contention, but every senseless one breaks out"** (v3) — Hebrew כָּבוֹד "glory"; שֶׁבֶת "to sit / dwell" (infinitive of יָשַׁב; "cease" would be שָׁבַת); מֵרִיב "from contention"; אֱוִיל "senseless one"; יִתְגַּלָּע "breaks out" (as 17:14; 18:1).
- **"The sluggard will not plow by reason of the cold; therefore shall he beg in harvest, and have nothing" → "Because of winter the sluggard does not plow; and he asks in the harvest, and there is nothing"** (v4) — Hebrew מֵחֹרֶף, lit. "from winter" (the season of rains and plowing; "from the autumn on" is also read); qere וְשָׁאַל "and he asks" (ketiv ישאל "he asks"); no "therefore"; וָאָיִן "and there is nothing".
- **"Counsel in the heart of man is like deep water" → "Deep waters is counsel in the heart of a man"** (v5) — Hebrew מַיִם עֲמֻקִּים, no "like", Hebrew order (as 18:4); יִדְלֶנָּה "draws it out" (דָּלָה, of water); אִישׁ תְּבוּנָה "a man of understanding".
- **"Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but a faithful man who can find?" → "Many a man proclaims, each one, his chesed, but a man of faithfulness, who can find?"** (v6) — Hebrew רָב־אָדָם "many of mankind"; יִקְרָא אִישׁ חַסְדּוֹ "proclaims, each, his chesed" (token as everywhere); אִישׁ אֱמוּנִים "a man of faithfulness" (the plural noun of אֱמוּנָה).
- **"The just man walketh in his integrity: his children are blessed after him" → "One who walks about in his integrity is righteous; happy are his sons after him!"** (v7) — Hebrew מִתְהַלֵּךְ "walks about" (hitpael); the line has no verb: "the righteous walks about in his integrity" is also read; אַשְׁרֵי "happy" (not "blessed": that is בָּרוּךְ); בָּנָיו "his sons".
- **"A king that sitteth in the throne of judgment scattereth away all evil with his eyes" → "A king who sits on the throne of judgment winnows with his eyes all evil"** (v8) — Hebrew כִּסֵּא־דִין "the throne of judgment" (דִּין "cause, judging", not מִשְׁפָּט); מְזָרֶה "winnows" (זָרָה of grain, as v26); Hebrew order.
- **"I have made my heart clean, I am pure from my sin" → "I have made my heart pure; I am clean from my sin"** (v9) — Hebrew זִכִּיתִי "I have made pure" (זַךְ "pure", v11); טָהַרְתִּי "I am / have become clean".
- **"Divers weights, and divers measures, both of them are alike abomination to the LORD" → "A weight and a weight, an ephah and an ephah — an abomination to Yahweh, even both of them"** (v10) — Hebrew אֶבֶן וָאֶבֶן "a weight and a weight" (lit. "a stone and a stone": two weights for one; as Deut 25:13); אֵיפָה "ephah" (a dry measure); תּוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה גַּם־שְׁנֵיהֶם as 17:15. v23 has the same words for the same Hebrew.
- **"Even a child is known by his doings, whether his work be pure, and whether it be right" → "Even by his deeds a boy makes himself known, whether pure and whether upright is his work"** (v11) — Hebrew גַּם "even"; מַעֲלָלִים "deeds"; נַעַר "boy"; יִתְנַכֶּר "makes himself known" (hitpael of נכר: in Gen 42:7 the same form is "made himself a stranger", and "dissembles" is also read here); זַךְ "pure", יָשָׁר "upright"; פָּעֳלוֹ "his work".
- **"the LORD hath made even both of them" → "Yahweh has made even both of them"** (v12) — Hebrew גַם־שְׁנֵיהֶם "even both of them" (as v10).
- **"Love not sleep, lest thou come to poverty; open thine eyes, and thou shalt be satisfied with bread" → "Do not love sleep, lest you be dispossessed; open your eyes, be sated with bread"** (v13) — Hebrew תִּוָּרֵשׁ "be dispossessed" (nifal of יָרַשׁ; = come to poverty); שְׂבַע־לָחֶם, imperative "be sated".
- **"It is naught, it is naught, saith the buyer: but when he is gone his way, then he boasteth" → "'Bad, bad,' says the buyer, and he goes off, then he boasts"** (v14) — Hebrew רַע רַע "bad, bad"; הַקּוֹנֶה "the buyer" (קָנָה "buy"); וְאֹזֵל לוֹ "and he goes off"; יִתְהַלָּל "boasts".
- **"There is gold, and a multitude of rubies: but the lips of knowledge are a precious jewel" → "There is gold and abundance of rubies, but a precious vessel are lips of knowledge"** (v15) — Hebrew פְּנִינִים "rubies" (uncertain: "corals, pearls"); כְּלִי יְקָר "a precious vessel" (כְּלִי "vessel, article", not "jewel"); Hebrew order.
- **"Take his garment that is surety for a stranger: and take a pledge of him for a strange woman" → "Take his garment, for he has become surety for a stranger, and on behalf of a foreign woman take him in pledge"** (v16) — Hebrew כִּי־עָרַב זָר "for he has become surety for a stranger"; qere נָכְרִיָּה "a foreign woman" (ketiv נכרים "foreigners"); חַבְלֵהוּ "take him in pledge". 27:13 has nearly the same words.
- **"Bread of deceit is sweet to a man" → "Sweet to a man is bread of falsehood"** (v17) — Hebrew עָרֵב "sweet" (another root than עָרַב "be surety", v16); לֶחֶם שָׁקֶר "bread of falsehood"; חָצָץ "gravel".
- **"Every purpose is established by counsel: and with good advice make war" → "Plans by counsel are established, and with steering make war"** (v18) — Hebrew מַחֲשָׁבוֹת "plans" (as 19:21; no "every"); תִּכּוֹן "are established"; תַּחְבֻּלוֹת "steering" (lit.; "guidance").
- **"He that goeth about as a talebearer revealeth secrets: therefore meddle not with him that flattereth with his lips" → "He who reveals secret counsel goes about as a talebearer; and with one who opens wide his lips do not mix"** (v19) — Hebrew גּוֹלֶה־סּוֹד הוֹלֵךְ רָכִיל, in this order (also "a talebearer reveals secret counsel", as 11:13); סוֹד "secret counsel"; וּלְ "and with", no "therefore"; פֹתֶה שְׂפָתָיו "one who opens wide his lips" (פָּתָה "be open, simple"; KJV "flattereth"); לֹא תִתְעָרָב "do not mix" (hitpael of עָרַב: one root with v16).
- **"Whoso curseth his father or his mother, his lamp shall be put out in obscure darkness" → "He who curses his father and his mother — his lamp will be put out in the pupil of darkness"** (v20) — Hebrew מְקַלֵּל "curses" (קִלֵּל, lit. "makes light of"); וְאִמּוֹ "and his mother"; qere בֶּאֱשׁוּן "in the pupil" (ketiv באישון, the common spelling of the same word, as 7:9 "in the pupil of night": the black center).
- **"An inheritance may be gotten hastily at the beginning; but the end thereof shall not be blessed" → "An inheritance hurried at the first — and its latter end will not be blessed"** (v21) — Hebrew qere מְבֹהֶלֶת "hurried" (ketiv מבחלת "loathed"); בָּרִאשֹׁנָה "at the first"; וְאַחֲרִיתָהּ "and its latter end".
- **"Say not thou, I will recompense evil; but wait on the LORD, and he shall save thee" → "Do not say, 'Let me repay evil'; wait for Yahweh, and he will save you"** (v22) — Hebrew אֲשַׁלְּמָה "let me repay" (cohortative of שִׁלֵּם); קַוֵּה "wait for"; no "but"; וְיֹשַׁע לָךְ "and he will save you" (short form, also "that he may save you").
- **"Divers weights are an abomination unto the LORD; and a false balance is not good" → "An abomination to Yahweh is a weight and a weight, and scales of deceit are not good"** (v23) — Hebrew as v10 in its first words; מֹאזְנֵי מִרְמָה "scales of deceit".
- **"Man's goings are of the LORD; how can a man then understand his own way?" → "From Yahweh are the steps of a mighty man, and man — how can he understand his way?"** (v24) — Hebrew מִצְעֲדֵי־גָבֶר "the steps of a mighty man" (גֶּבֶר beside אָדָם in the same verse: kept apart); וְאָדָם מַה־יָּבִין "and man, what / how can he understand"; no "then", no "own".
- **"It is a snare to the man who devoureth that which is holy, and after vows to make enquiry" → "It is a snare to a man to say rashly, 'Holy,' and after vows to inquire"** (v25) — Hebrew מוֹקֵשׁ אָדָם "a snare of a man"; יָלַע קֹדֶשׁ "to say rashly, 'Holy'" (לָעַע / יָלַע "speak rashly", Job 6:3; KJV "devour", from לוּעַ "swallow"; uncertain); לְבַקֵּר "to inquire" (בִּקֵּר, as the reviewer's חָקַר row has it; חָפַשׂ "searching" is v27).
- **"A wise king scattereth the wicked, and bringeth the wheel over them" → "A wise king winnows the wicked and brings back the wheel over them"** (v26) — Hebrew מְזָרֶה "winnows" (as v8); וַיָּשֶׁב "and brings back / turns" (the threshing wheel); אוֹפָן "wheel".
- **"The spirit of man is the candle of the LORD, searching all the inward parts of the belly" → "The lamp of Yahweh is the nishmat adam, searching all the chambers of the belly"** (v27) — Hebrew נֵר יְהוָה נִשְׁמַת אָדָם, in the Hebrew order (either may be the subject); נֵר "lamp"; נִשְׁמָה "breath" (token kept); חֹפֵשׂ "searching"; חַדְרֵי־בָטֶן "the chambers of the belly" (as 18:8; v30).
- **"Mercy and truth preserve the king: and his throne is upholden by mercy" → "Chesed and truth guard a king, and he upholds by chesed his throne"** (v28) — Hebrew חֶסֶד twice, token; אֱמֶת "truth"; יִצְּרוּ "guard" (נָצַר); מֶלֶךְ without the article; וְסָעַד "and he upholds", active (the king, or chesed taken as the agent).
- **"The glory of young men is their strength: and the beauty of old men is the grey head" → "The beauty of young men is their power, and the splendor of old men is gray hair"** (v29) — Hebrew תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty" (as 17:6); בַּחוּרִים "young men"; כֹּחַ "power"; הֲדַר "splendor"; שֵׂיבָה "gray hair".
- **"The blueness of a wound cleanseth away evil: so do stripes the inward parts of the belly" → "The stripes of a wound are a scouring against evil, and blows, the chambers of the belly"** (v30) — Hebrew חַבֻּרוֹת "stripes"; פֶּצַע "wound"; qere תַּמְרוּק "a scouring" (noun; ketiv תמריק "scours"; the root of the "rubbings" of Esth 2:12); בְּרָע "against / in evil" (or "the evil one"); מַכּוֹת "blows"; the second line has no verb ("are a scouring" carries over).

### Notes
- **Repeated sayings** — v10 and v23 (the same words for the same Hebrew); v2a ≈ 19:12a; v16 ≈ 27:13.
- **Verse numbers** — the Hebrew numbering is the same.
