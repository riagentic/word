# Proverbs 5

[1] My son, be attentive to my wisdom;
incline your ear to my understanding,

[2] to keep devisings,
and your lips shall guard knowledge.

[3] For the lips of a strange woman drip honey,
and smoother than oil is her palate,

[4] but her latter end is bitter as wormwood,
sharp as a two-edged sword.

[5] Her feet go down to death;
her steps hold fast to Sheol.

[6] Lest she make level the path of life,
her courses wander; she does not know.

[7] And now, sons, listen to me,
and do not turn aside from the sayings of my mouth.

[8] Make your way far from her,
and do not come near to the entrance of her house,

[9] lest you give your majesty to others,
and your years to a cruel one,

[10] lest strangers be sated with your power,
and your pains be in the house of a foreigner,

[11] and you groan at your latter end,
when your flesh and your body come to an end,

[12] and you say, "How I hated discipline,
and my heart spurned reproof!

[13] And I did not listen to the voice of my teachers,
and to those who taught me I did not incline my ear.

[14] I was almost in all evil
in the midst of the assembly and the congregation."

[15] Drink water from your cistern,
and streams from the midst of your well.

[16] Shall your springs be scattered outside,
streams of water in the squares?

[17] Let them be for you alone,
and not for strangers with you.

[18] Let your fountain be blessed,
and rejoice in the wife of your youth,

[19] a doe of loves and an ibex of favor;
let her breasts drench you at every time;
in her love may you reel continually.

[20] And why should you reel, my son, with a strange woman,
and embrace the bosom of a foreign woman?

[21] For before the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are the ways of a man,
and all his courses he makes level.

[22] His iniquities shall capture him, the wicked one,
and in the cords of his sin he shall be held fast.

[23] He shall die for lack of discipline,
and in the greatness of his folly he shall reel.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v21) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v19 has three cola.
- **"understanding"** (v1) — Hebrew תְּבוּנָה (WISDOM.md: בִּינָה and תְּבוּנָה are both "understanding").
- **"That thou mayest regard discretion" → "to keep devisings"** (v2) — Hebrew לִשְׁמֹר מְזִמּוֹת, an infinitive ("to keep"), and מְזִמּוֹת plural of מְזִמָּה "devising" (a purpose thought out; good here as 1:4). The second line is its own clause, a yiqtol: "your lips shall guard" (נָצַר "guard", beside שָׁמַר "keep").
- **"a strange woman"** (v3, 20) — Hebrew זָרָה, the feminine of זָר "stranger", standing alone; "woman" is carried by the feminine form (as אִשָּׁה זָרָה, 2:16; 7:5, WISDOM.md). Whether she is another man's wife (6:24–35; 7:19) or an outsider is not said by the word: "forbidden woman" and "adulteress" interpreted it. **"drop as an honeycomb" → "drip honey"** — Hebrew נֹפֶת, honey that drips from the comb (Ps 19:10 "the drippings of the honeycombs"); not דְּבַשׁ.
- **"her mouth" → "her palate"** (v3) — Hebrew חִכָּהּ, the roof of the mouth (as Job 12:11); the organ of speech here. The Hebrew order "and smoother than oil is her palate" is kept.
- **"But her end" → "but her latter end"** (v4) — Hebrew אַחֲרִיתָהּ (as Deut 32:20, 29). **"a two-edged sword"** — Hebrew חֶרֶב פִּיּוֹת, literally "a sword of mouths" (edges, as Judg 3:16).
- **"her steps take hold on hell" → "her steps hold fast to Sheol"** (v5) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל, the plain name of the place of the dead, not "hell"; יִתְמֹכוּ "hold fast" (תָּמַךְ). מָוֶת "death" stands without a preposition: "to" is supplied.
- **"Lest thou shouldest ponder the path of life, her ways are moveable, that thou canst not know them" → "Lest she make level the path of life, her courses wander; she does not know"** (v6) — Hebrew אֹרַח חַיִּים פֶּן־תְּפַלֵּס: תְּפַלֵּס can be "she" or "you" (KJV); with "she" the line says that her courses wander so that she will not level the path of life; פִּלֵּס is "make level" (as 4:26; also "weigh": v21). נָעוּ "wander, totter". לֹא תֵדָע has no object ("it" / "them" is not written) and can again be "she" or "you". אֹרַח "path", מַעְגָּל "course" (the track of a wheel), kept apart as in 2:9, 15; 4:11, 26 (reviewer ruling 2026-10-16; overrides the Ps 139–141 row "path").
- **"Hear me now therefore, O ye children" → "And now, sons, listen to me"** (v7) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה בָנִים: "sons", no "O" (reviewer: no vocative "O"). **"depart from the words of my mouth" → "turn aside from the sayings of my mouth"** — סוּר "turn aside"; אֵמֶר "saying" (beside דָּבָר "word").
- **"Remove thy way far from her" → "Make your way far from her"** (v8) — Hebrew הַרְחֵק מֵעָלֶיהָ, literally "from upon her". **"door" → "entrance"** — Hebrew פֶּתַח (דֶּלֶת is "door", 8:34).
- **"thine honour" → "your majesty"** (v9) — Hebrew הוֹדֶךָ, הוֹד "majesty, splendor" (WORDS-HE: not "honor"); here a man's vigor and bloom. **"unto the cruel" → "to a cruel one"** — Hebrew לְאַכְזָרִי, singular, no article.
- **"lest strangers be filled with thy wealth" → "lest strangers be sated with your power"** (v10) — Hebrew כֹּחֶךָ "your power" (כֹּחַ); שָׂבַע "be sated". **"thy labours" → "your pains"** — Hebrew עֲצָבֶיךָ, plural of עֶצֶב "pain" (toil that hurts; Gen 3:16, 17): what was won with pain. The line has no verb: "be" is supplied from the first line. נָכְרִי "foreigner" beside זָר "stranger".
- **"And thou mourn at the last" → "and you groan at your latter end"** (v11) — Hebrew וְנָהַמְתָּ בְאַחֲרִיתֶךָ, a weqatal that continues "lest". **"when thy flesh and thy body are consumed" → "when your flesh and your body come to an end"** — בִּכְלוֹת "when … come to an end"; שְׁאֵרֶךָ is literally "your flesh" (the near-kin word, Lev 18:6, used of the body itself): two words for flesh, "body" for the second.
- **"despised reproof" → "spurned reproof"** (v12) — Hebrew נָאַץ (נִאֵץ "spurn"); the Hebrew order "and reproof my heart spurned".
- **"obeyed the voice" → "listen to the voice"** (v13) — Hebrew שָׁמַעְתִּי בְּקוֹל (WORDS-HE: no "obey"). **"them that instructed me" → "those who taught me"** — מְלַמְּדַי, the participle of לִמֵּד "teach"; מוֹרָי "my teachers" is another word (the root of הוֹרָה, 6:13). Each line has its "and".
- **"I was almost in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly" → "I was almost in all evil in the midst of the assembly and the congregation"** (v14) — Hebrew כִּמְעַט הָיִיתִי בְכָל־רָע: "all evil" may be every wrong or every misfortune (ruin); both are defended. קָהָל "assembly", עֵדָה "congregation", in the Hebrew order.
- **"out of thine own cistern" → "from your cistern"** (v15) — Hebrew מִבּוֹרֶךָ: בּוֹר is the dug pit (WORDS-HE "pit"); of water drunk, a cistern. "own" is not written. **"running waters" → "streams"** — Hebrew נֹזְלִים, literally "flowing ones" (Exod 15:8). בְּאֵר "well".
- **"Let thy fountains be dispersed abroad, and rivers of waters in the streets" → "Shall your springs be scattered outside, streams of water in the squares?"** (v16) — Hebrew יָפוּצוּ, "be scattered" (פּוּץ). The Hebrew has no question word: read as a question (against v17) or as a statement / wish ("your springs shall be scattered", of offspring); the choice is not certain. פַּלְגֵי־מָיִם "streams of water" (reviewer row); רְחֹבוֹת "squares" (open places).
- **"rejoice with the wife of thy youth" → "rejoice in the wife of your youth"** (v18) — Hebrew מֵאֵשֶׁת, literally "from the wife". מָקוֹר "fountain".
- **"Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe" → "a doe of loves and an ibex of favor"** (v19) — Hebrew אַיֶּלֶת אֲהָבִים וְיַעֲלַת־חֵן: no verb; אֲהָבִים is plural ("loves", as 7:18), חֵן "favor"; יַעֲלָה, feminine of יָעֵל "ibex". **"let her breasts satisfy thee at all times" → "let her breasts drench you at every time"** — יְרַוֻּךָ, piel of רָוָה "drink one's fill" (7:18): "saturate, drench". **"be thou ravished always with her love" → "in her love may you reel continually"** — Hebrew תִּשְׁגֶּה, from שָׁגָה "stray, reel" (as with wine: Isa 28:7); the same verb in v19, 20 and 23 ("he shall reel"), so one English word in all three; תָּמִיד "continually".
- **"embrace the bosom of a stranger" → "embrace the bosom of a foreign woman"** (v20) — Hebrew נָכְרִיָּה, feminine of נָכְרִי "foreigner" (WISDOM.md); beside זָרָה "a strange woman" in the first line. חֵיק "bosom".
- **"For the ways of man are before the eyes of the LORD, and he pondereth all his goings" → "For before the eyes of Yahweh are the ways of a man, and all his courses he makes level"** (v21) — Hebrew נֹכַח "before, in front of"; אִישׁ "a man"; מְפַלֵּס, the participle of פִּלֵּס as in v6 ("make level"); "weighs", "watches" is the sense taken by many. מַעְגָּל "course" (as v6).
- **"His own iniquities shall take the wicked himself" → "His iniquities shall capture him, the wicked one"** (v22) — Hebrew יִלְכְּדֻנוֹ אֶת־הָרָשָׁע: the object is named twice ("him, the wicked one"); לָכַד "capture"; "own", "himself" are not written. **"he shall be holden with the cords of his sins" → "in the cords of his sin he shall be held fast"** — חַטָּאתוֹ singular; תָּמַךְ "hold fast" (as v5).
- **"He shall die without instruction; and in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray" → "He shall die for lack of discipline, and in the greatness of his folly he shall reel"** (v23) — Hebrew בְּאֵין מוּסָר, "in the absence of discipline"; אִוֶּלֶת "folly"; יִשְׁגֶּה as v19, 20.

### Notes
- **Hebrew verse numbers** — the same as ours in this chapter.
- **The strange woman** (v3–14, 20) — the father's warning; the water and spring images of v15–19 speak of the man's own wife (v18).
