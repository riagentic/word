# Proverbs 12

[1] He who loves discipline loves knowledge,
but he who hates reproof is brutish.

[2] The good one obtains good pleasure from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but a man of devisings he condemns.

[3] A man is not established by wickedness,
but the root of the righteous will not be shaken.

[4] A woman of ability is the crown of her husband,
but like rottenness in his bones is she who causes shame.

[5] The thoughts of the righteous are judgment;
the steering of the wicked is deceit.

[6] The words of the wicked are a lying in ambush for blood,
but the mouth of the upright delivers them.

[7] The wicked are overthrown, and they are no more,
but the house of the righteous will stand.

[8] According to his insight a man is praised,
but the twisted of heart will be for contempt.

[9] Better is one lightly esteemed who has a slave
than one who honors himself and lacks bread.

[10] The righteous knows the soul of his beast,
but the compassion of the wicked is cruel.

[11] He who works his ground will be sated with bread,
but he who pursues empty things is lacking heart.

[12] The wicked has coveted the net of evil ones,
but the root of the righteous gives.

[13] In the transgression of the lips is an evil snare,
but the righteous goes out from distress.

[14] From the fruit of a man's mouth he is sated with good,
and the dealing of a man's hands he will bring back to him.

[15] The way of a senseless one is right in his eyes,
but he who listens to counsel is wise.

[16] A senseless one — in the day his vexation is known,
but he who covers disgrace is shrewd.

[17] He who breathes out faithfulness declares righteousness,
but a false witness, deceit.

[18] There is one who speaks rashly like the piercings of a sword,
but the tongue of the wise is healing.

[19] The lip of truth will be established perpetually,
but for a moment, the tongue of falsehood.

[20] Deceit is in the heart of those who devise evil,
but to those who counsel ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧ is gladness.

[21] No trouble will befall the righteous,
but the wicked are full of evil.

[22] An abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are lips of falsehood,
but those who do faithfulness are his good pleasure.

[23] A shrewd man covers knowledge,
but the heart of fools calls out folly.

[24] The hand of the diligent will rule,
but slackness will be for forced labor.

[25] Anxiety in the heart of a man bows it down,
but a good word makes it glad.

[26] The righteous is more excellent than his neighbor,
but the way of the wicked makes them wander.

[27] Slackness does not roast its game,
but the precious wealth of a man is the diligent.

[28] In the path of righteousness is life,
and the way of the pathway is no death.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 22) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Joins** (throughout) — "but" or "and" only where the Hebrew has וְ (as ch. 10); v5 has none.
- **"Whoso loveth instruction" → "He who loves discipline"** (v1) — Hebrew מוּסָר "discipline"; בַּעַר "brutish".
- **"A good man obtaineth favour of the LORD" → "The good one obtains good pleasure from Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew טוֹב, no "man"; יָפִיק "obtains" (literally "brings out"); רָצוֹן "good pleasure" as 11:1. **"a man of wicked devices will he condemn" → "a man of devisings he condemns"** — אִישׁ מְזִמּוֹת, מְזִמָּה "devising" (good or bad, as 1:4; "wicked" is not in the word). יַרְשִׁיעַ "he condemns" (Yahweh); it may also be read of the man, "acts wickedly".
- **"A man shall not be established by wickedness" → "A man is not established by wickedness"** (v3) — Hebrew אָדָם "man" (also v23, 27; אִישׁ v2, 8, 25: English has one word, see v14). **"shall not be moved" → "will not be shaken"** — בַּל־יִמּוֹט, as 10:30.
- **"A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband" → "A woman of ability is the crown of her husband"** (v4) — Hebrew אֵשֶׁת־חַיִל, as Ruth 3:11 ("a woman of ability"; 31:10); חַיִל is strength and worth, "virtuous" is too narrow. אִשָּׁה "woman" is also "wife"; בַּעַל "owner", of a wife "husband". **"she that maketh ashamed is as rottenness in his bones" → "like rottenness in his bones is she who causes shame"** — Hebrew order; מְבִישָׁה "causes shame" (as 10:5).
- **"The thoughts of the righteous are right: but the counsels of the wicked are deceit" → "The thoughts of the righteous are judgment; the steering of the wicked is deceit"** (v5) — Hebrew מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (what is just); תַּחְבֻּלוֹת "steering", not עֵצָה "counsel" (as 11:14); מִרְמָה "deceit". No "but" (no וְ).
- **"The words of the wicked are to lie in wait for blood" → "are a lying in ambush for blood"** (v6) — Hebrew אֱרָב־דָּם, the infinitive of אָרַב "lie in ambush for". **"shall deliver them" → "delivers them"** — "them" is the upright, or those lain in wait for: not said.
- **"The wicked are overthrown, and are not"** (v7) — Hebrew הָפוֹךְ רְשָׁעִים וְאֵינָם, an infinitive absolute (הָפַךְ "overthrow") with no person and no voice: "(one) overthrows the wicked" or "the wicked are overthrown"; the passive is kept as in KJV.
- **"A man shall be commended according to his wisdom" → "According to his insight a man is praised"** (v8) — Hebrew לְפִי־שִׂכְלוֹ, literally "to the mouth of his insight" (according to); שֵׂכֶל "insight"; Hebrew order. **"he that is of a perverse heart shall be despised" → "the twisted of heart will be for contempt"** — נַעֲוֵה־לֵב, nifal of עָוָה "twist"; לָבוּז "for contempt".
- **"He that is despised, and hath a servant, is better" → "Better is one lightly esteemed who has a slave"** (v9) — Hebrew טוֹב נִקְלֶה וְעֶבֶד לוֹ, "better" first; נִקְלֶה "lightly esteemed"; וְעֶבֶד לוֹ "and a slave is his" (also read "and is a slave to himself", working for himself); עֶבֶד "slave". **"he that honoureth himself" → "one who honors himself"** — מִתְכַּבֵּד, hitpael of כִּבֵּד "honor"; חֲסַר־לָחֶם "lacking bread".
- **"A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast" → "The righteous knows the soul of his beast"** (v10) — Hebrew יוֹדֵעַ "knows"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul"; בְּהֵמָה "beast". **"the tender mercies of the wicked" → "the compassion of the wicked"** — רַחֲמִים "compassion" (plural form).
- **"He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread" → "He who works his ground will be sated with bread"** (v11) — Hebrew עֹבֵד אַדְמָתוֹ, עָבַד "work" of the ground, אֲדָמָה "ground"; שָׂבַע "be sated". **"he that followeth vain persons" → "he who pursues empty things"** — מְרַדֵּף רֵיקִים, "pursues" (רָדַף); רֵיקִים "empty (ones, things)": persons or things, not said. **"is void of understanding" → "is lacking heart"** — חֲסַר־לֵב.
- **"The wicked desireth the net of evil men: but the root of the righteous yieldeth fruit" → "The wicked has coveted the net of evil ones, but the root of the righteous gives"** (v12) — Hebrew חָמַד "covet", a perfect; מְצוֹד "net" (or "stronghold": uncertain); רָעִים "evil ones" ("men" not in the Hebrew); יִתֵּן "gives", no object ("fruit" is supplied in KJV). The verse is obscure and much emended; the MT is kept.
- **"The wicked is snared by the transgression of his lips" → "In the transgression of the lips is an evil snare"** (v13) — Hebrew בְּפֶשַׁע שְׂפָתַיִם מוֹקֵשׁ רָע, word for word; "the wicked" (רָשָׁע) does not stand here: רָע is "evil", the adjective of "snare" or "the evil one" ("the snare of the evil one"). **"shall come out of trouble" → "goes out from distress"** — וַיֵּצֵא; צָרָה "distress".
- **"A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth" → "From the fruit of a man's mouth he is sated with good"** (v14) — Hebrew order; מִן "from". **"the recompence of a man's hands shall be rendered unto him" → "the dealing of a man's hands he will bring back to him"** — גְּמוּל "dealing" (what one has done, Ps 28:4); the qere יָשִׁיב, hifil "he will bring back" (the subject is not named); the written form ישוב, qal "will return to him". The first line has אִישׁ, the second אָדָם: both "man".
- **"is right in his own eyes" → "is right in his eyes"** (v15) — Hebrew יָשָׁר בְּעֵינָיו; no "own". **"he that hearkeneth unto counsel" → "he who listens to counsel"** — שֹׁמֵעַ לְ; עֵצָה "counsel". אֱוִיל "senseless one" (also v16).
- **"A fool's wrath is presently known" → "A senseless one — in the day his vexation is known"** (v16) — Hebrew אֱוִיל בַּיּוֹם יִוָּדַע כַּעְסוֹ, in this order: בַּיּוֹם "in the day" (= at once, the same day); כַּעַס "vexation". **"a prudent man covereth shame" → "he who covers disgrace is shrewd"** — כֹּסֶה קָלוֹן עָרוּם: עָרוּם "shrewd" (in a good sense here; not "prudent"), the predicate; קָלוֹן "disgrace".
- **"He that speaketh truth sheweth forth righteousness" → "He who breathes out faithfulness declares righteousness"** (v17) — Hebrew יָפִיחַ, hifil of פוּחַ "breathe out" (utter, 6:19); אֱמוּנָה "faithfulness" (not אֱמֶת "truth"); יַגִּיד "declares". **"a false witness deceit"** — עֵד שְׁקָרִים, literally "a witness of falsehoods" (plural); מִרְמָה "deceit"; no verb.
- **"There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword" → "There is one who speaks rashly like the piercings of a sword"** (v18) — Hebrew בּוֹטֶה, "speaks rashly" (the root of מִבְטָא, Num 30:6). **"is health" → "is healing"** — מַרְפֵּא.
- **"shall be established for ever" → "will be established perpetually"** (v19) — Hebrew לָעַד "perpetually" (beside לְעוֹלָם "forever"). **"a lying tongue is but for a moment" → "but for a moment, the tongue of falsehood"** — וְעַד־אַרְגִּיעָה, literally "and until I make a moment" (the blink of an eye); לְשׁוֹן שָׁקֶר "the tongue of falsehood"; no verb.
- **"them that imagine evil" → "those who devise evil"** (v20) — Hebrew חֹרְשֵׁי רָע, חָרַשׁ "plow, devise". **"to the counsellors of peace is joy" → "to those who counsel shalom is gladness"** — לְיֹעֲצֵי שָׁלוֹם, יָעַץ "counsel"; שָׁלוֹם *shalom*; שִׂמְחָה "gladness".
- **"There shall no evil happen to the just" → "No trouble will befall the righteous"** (v21) — Hebrew לֹא־יְאֻנֶּה … כָּל־אָוֶן, אָוֶן "trouble" (of what befalls; "wickedness" 10:29: one word); רָע "evil" stands in the second line. **"shall be filled with mischief" → "are full of evil"** — מָלְאוּ רָע, a perfect of state.
- **"they that deal truly are his delight" → "those who do faithfulness are his good pleasure"** (v22) — Hebrew עֹשֵׂי אֱמוּנָה, "doers of faithfulness"; רָצוֹן as v2. שִׂפְתֵי־שָׁקֶר "lips of falsehood"; Hebrew order.
- **"A prudent man concealeth knowledge" → "A shrewd man covers knowledge"** (v23) — Hebrew אָדָם עָרוּם; כֹּסֶה "covers" (as v16). **"proclaimeth foolishness" → "calls out folly"** — יִקְרָא אִוֶּלֶת, קָרָא "call", אִוֶּלֶת "folly"; כְּסִילִים "fools".
- **"shall bear rule" → "will rule"** (v24) — Hebrew תִּמְשׁוֹל. **"but the slothful shall be under tribute" → "but slackness will be for forced labor"** — וּרְמִיָּה, "slackness" as 10:4 (the noun, not "the slothful"); לָמַס "for forced labor" (not "tribute").
- **"Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop" → "Anxiety in the heart of a man bows it down"** (v25) — Hebrew דְּאָגָה "anxiety"; יַשְׁחֶנָּה "bows it down". "It" (feminine in both lines) is the heart or the man's soul; the Hebrew suffix does not agree with לֵב, and the reference is not certain. שִׂמַּח "make glad".
- **"The righteous is more excellent than his neighbour"** (v26) — Hebrew יָתֵר מֵרֵעֵהוּ צַדִּיק: יָתֵר taken as "excelling" (יֶתֶר "excess, excellence") with מִן "than". It is also read as a verb from תּוּר "search out, guide" ("the righteous guides his neighbor"): uncertain. **"seduceth them" → "makes them wander"** — תַּתְעֵם, hifil of תָּעָה "wander".
- **"The slothful man roasteth not that which he took in hunting" → "Slackness does not roast its game"** (v27) — Hebrew לֹא־יַחֲרֹךְ רְמִיָּה צֵידוֹ: רְמִיָּה "slackness" as v24; חָרַךְ (only here) "roast" is uncertain ("catch, start up" in other readings); צַיִד "game". **"but the substance of a diligent man is precious" → "but the precious wealth of a man is the diligent"** — וְהוֹן־אָדָם יָקָר חָרוּץ, word for word "and wealth of a man, precious, diligent": the order is hard, and many rearrange it ("a diligent man gets precious wealth"); the MT order is kept.
- **"In the way of righteousness is life" → "In the path of righteousness is life"** (v28) — Hebrew אֹרַח "path". **"and in the pathway thereof there is no death" → "and the way of the pathway is no death"** — וְדֶרֶךְ נְתִיבָה אַל־מָוֶת: no "in", no "thereof" in the MT pointing; אַל־מָוֶת "no death" (אַל, otherwise "not" with verbs, here with a noun: "not-death"). Some repoint אֶל־מָוֶת "to death" (from memory): the MT is kept.
