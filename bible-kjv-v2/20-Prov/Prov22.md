# Proverbs 22

[1] A name is chosen rather than great riches;
than silver and than gold, favor is better.

[2] The rich and the destitute meet;
the maker of them all is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] The shrewd one sees evil and hides himself,
but the simple pass on and are punished.

[4] The reward of humility, the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
is riches and glory and life.

[5] Thorns, traps are in the way of the crooked;
whoever keeps his soul will be far from them.

[6] Train the boy according to his way;
even when he grows old he will not turn aside from it.

[7] The rich rules over the destitute,
and the borrower is a slave to the man who lends.

[8] Whoever sows injustice will reap trouble,
and the rod of his raging will come to an end.

[9] One good of eye — he will be blessed,
for he gives of his bread to the poor.

[10] Drive out a scoffer, and strife will go out,
and cause and disgrace will cease.

[11] Whoever loves the pure of heart,
the favor of his lips — his friend is the king.

[12] The eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ guard knowledge,
and he overturns the words of the treacherous.

[13] The sluggard says, "A lion is in the street!
In the midst of the squares I shall be murdered!"

[14] A deep pit is the mouth of strange women;
one denounced by ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will fall there.

[15] Folly is bound up in the heart of a boy;
the rod of discipline will put it far from him.

[16] Whoever extorts from the poor to make much for him,
whoever gives to the rich — surely to lack.

[17] Incline your ear and hear the words of the wise,
and your heart you shall set to my knowledge,

[18] for it is pleasant if you keep them in your belly;
they shall be ready together on your lips.

[19] That your trust may be in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
I have made you know today, even you.

[20] Have I not written for you excellent things,
with counsels and knowledge,

[21] to make you know the certainty of the sayings of truth,
to bring back sayings, truth, to those who send you?

[22] Do not rob the poor because he is poor,
and do not crush the afflicted in the gate,

[23] for ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ will contend their case
and despoil of soul those who despoil them.

[24] Do not make friends with a master of anger,
and with a man of furies you shall not go,

[25] lest you learn his paths
and take a snare for your soul.

[26] Do not be among those who strike the palm,
among those who are surety for loans.

[27] If you have nothing to repay,
why should he take your bed from under you?

[28] Do not move back the ancient border
that your fathers made.

[29] Have you beheld a man skilled in his work?
Before kings he will station himself;
he will not station himself before obscure men.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v2, 4, 12, 14, 19, 23) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Layout** (v29) — the verse is split into three lines where the accents divide it (the athnach falls after "he will station himself").
- **"A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches, and loving favour rather than silver and gold" → "A name is chosen rather than great riches; than silver and than gold, favor is better"** (v1) — Hebrew נִבְחָר שֵׁם "a name is chosen" (as 21:3; "good" is not written with "name"); מִכֶּסֶף וּמִזָּהָב חֵן טוֹב "than silver and than gold, favor is good" (מִן with טוֹב: "better"); חֵן "favor" (not "loving favour").
- **"The rich and poor meet together" → "The rich and the destitute meet"** (v2) — Hebrew עָשִׁיר וָרָשׁ; רָשׁ "destitute" (one in want, from רוּשׁ; WORDS-HE, as Ps 82:3; beside דַּל "poor", v9, 16, 22: two words); נִפְגָּשׁוּ "meet" (a perfect of what is always so); עֹשֵׂה "maker".
- **"A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself" → "The shrewd one sees evil and hides himself"** (v3) — Hebrew עָרוּם "shrewd" (WISDOM.md; "man" not written); רָעָה "evil" (not "danger"); וְנִסְתָּר "and hides himself" is the qere; the written form ויסתר is the narrative form of the same verb ("and hid himself"). נֶעֱנָשׁוּ "are punished" (as 21:11). The same saying is 27:12: one wording where the Hebrew is the same.
- **"By humility and the fear of the LORD are riches, and honour, and life" → "The reward of humility, the fear of Yahweh, is riches and glory and life"** (v4) — Hebrew עֵקֶב, literally "heel": what follows on a thing, its outcome (as Ps 19:11 "reward"). "the fear of Yahweh" stands beside "humility" with no "and": it names humility, or it is itself the reward, or a second subject; not decided. כָּבוֹד "glory".
- **"Thorns and snares are in the way of the froward" → "Thorns, traps are in the way of the crooked"** (v5) — Hebrew צִנִּים פַּחִים, two nouns with no "and" (צִנִּים "thorns", uncertain: also "hooks"); פַּח "trap" (מוֹקֵשׁ is "snare", v25); עִקֵּשׁ "crooked"; שׁוֹמֵר נַפְשׁוֹ "whoever keeps his soul".
- **"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it" → "Train the boy according to his way; even when he grows old he will not turn aside from it"** (v6) — Hebrew חֲנֹךְ, the verb of "dedicate" a house (Deut 20:5): of a boy, to start him, train him (the one verb named here); לַנַּעַר "the boy" (נַעַר); עַל־פִּי דַרְכּוֹ "according to his way" (literally "upon the mouth of his way": the way that fits him, or the way he should go; not decided); גַּם כִּי "even when"; סוּר "turn aside".
- **"the borrower is servant to the lender" → "the borrower is a slave to the man who lends"** (v7) — Hebrew רָשִׁים "destitute" (plural of רָשׁ, as v2); עֶבֶד "slave"; לְאִישׁ מַלְוֶה "to the man who lends".
- **"He that soweth iniquity shall reap vanity: and the rod of his anger shall fail" → "Whoever sows injustice will reap trouble, and the rod of his raging will come to an end"** (v8) — Hebrew עַוְלָה "injustice"; אָוֶן here "trouble" (the word is also "wickedness", 21:15: one word, two senses); יִקְצָר "will reap" is the qere, the written form יקצור the same verb; עֶבְרָה "raging" (as 21:24); יִכְלֶה "will come to an end". Whether it is the rod he wields or the rod that strikes him is not said.
- **"He that hath a bountiful eye" → "One good of eye — he will be blessed"** (v9) — Hebrew טוֹב־עַיִן "good of eye" (= generous; set against רַע עָיִן "evil of eye", 23:6); הוּא "he"; נָתַן "gives" (a perfect); מִלַּחְמוֹ "of his bread".
- **"Cast out the scorner, and contention shall go out; yea, strife and reproach shall cease" → "Drive out a scoffer, and strife will go out, and cause and disgrace will cease"** (v10) — Hebrew גָּרֵשׁ "drive out"; מָדוֹן "strife"; דִּין "cause" (a matter brought to judgment, a lawsuit); קָלוֹן "disgrace"; יִשְׁבֹּת "will cease" (שָׁבַת).
- **"He that loveth pureness of heart, for the grace of his lips the king shall be his friend" → "Whoever loves the pure of heart, the favor of his lips — his friend is the king"** (v11) — Hebrew טְהָר־לֵב "pure of heart" is the qere; the written form טהור has the same sense. Whether he loves the pure of heart (persons) or loves being pure of heart is not said. חֵן שְׂפָתָיו "the favor of his lips" stands loose, with no "for"; רֵעֵהוּ "his friend" (the same word is "neighbor", 21:10).
- **"preserve knowledge, and he overthroweth the words of the transgressor" → "guard knowledge, and he overturns the words of the treacherous"** (v12) — Hebrew נָצְרוּ "guard" (נָצַר); וַיְסַלֵּף "and he overturns" (סִלֵּף, as 21:12); בֹגֵד "one who deals treacherously" (as 21:18).
- **"There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets" → "A lion is in the street! In the midst of the squares I shall be murdered!"** (v13) — Hebrew בַחוּץ "in the street" (as 1:20); בְּתוֹךְ רְחֹבוֹת "in the midst of the squares"; אֵרָצֵחַ "I shall be murdered" (רָצַח, the word of Exod 20:13, said of a lion: the sluggard's own overstatement, kept).
- **"strange women … he that is abhorred of the LORD" → "strange women … one denounced by Yahweh"** (v14) — Hebrew זָרוֹת "strange women" (feminine plural of זָר; WISDOM.md; not "forbidden"); שׁוּחָה "pit" (as 23:27; another word than בּוֹר, 1:12: English has one word); זְעוּם "denounced" (passive of זָעַם, as 24:24); יִפָּל is the qere, the written form יפול the same verb.
- **"Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child; but the rod of correction" → "Folly is bound up in the heart of a boy; the rod of discipline"** (v15) — Hebrew אִוֶּלֶת "folly"; נַעַר "boy"; שֵׁבֶט מוּסָר "the rod of discipline"; no "but".
- **"He that oppresseth the poor to increase his riches, and he that giveth to the rich, shall surely come to want" → "Whoever extorts from the poor to make much for him, whoever gives to the rich — surely to lack"** (v16) — Hebrew עֹשֵׁק "extorting from" (עָשַׁק); לְהַרְבּוֹת לוֹ "to make much for him" (for himself, or for the poor man: not decided, so the line is read either as one who gains by oppression and loses by bribing the rich, or as oppression that in the end enriches the poor); אַךְ "surely" (as 21:5), לְמַחְסוֹר "to lack"; no verb in the second line.
- **"Bow down thine ear … and apply thine heart unto my knowledge" → "Incline your ear … and your heart you shall set to my knowledge"** (v17) — Hebrew הַט "incline"; וְלִבְּךָ תָּשִׁית, an imperfect after two imperatives, the object first ("set the heart to", as Exod 7:23). The words דִּבְרֵי חֲכָמִים "the words of the wise" stand here inside the verse, as the start of a new part of the book (22:17–24:22); no heading is added.
- **"For it is a pleasant thing if thou keep them within thee; they shall withal be fitted in thy lips" → "for it is pleasant if you keep them in your belly; they shall be ready together on your lips"** (v18) — Hebrew נָעִים "pleasant"; בְּבִטְנֶךָ "in your belly" (בֶּטֶן, the inner man); יִכֹּנוּ nifal of כּוּן "be ready, be fixed" (or a wish: "let them be ready"); יַחְדָּו "together".
- **"I have made known to thee this day, even to thee" → "I have made you know today, even you"** (v19) — Hebrew הוֹדַעְתִּיךָ "I have made you know" (the object is "you"; "them" is not written); אַף־אָתָּה "even you"; מִבְטַחֶךָ "your trust" (as 21:22).
- **"Have not I written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge" → "Have I not written for you excellent things, with counsels and knowledge"** (v20) — Hebrew שָׁלִישִׁים is the qere, literally "third ones", the word for an officer of the third rank (Exod 14:7): here taken as "things of the first rank, excellent things" (uncertain). The written form שלשום is שִׁלְשׁוֹם "formerly" (literally "the day before yesterday": "Have I not written for you before?"). The same consonants pointed שְׁלוֹשִׁים give "thirty" ("thirty sayings"), a common reading that links the section with an Egyptian teaching in thirty chapters (from memory): it is not the MT's pointing and is not followed. בְּמוֹעֵצֹת "with counsels" (as 1:31).
- **"That I might make thee know the certainty of the words of truth; that thou mightest answer the words of truth to them that send unto thee" → "to make you know the certainty of the sayings of truth, to bring back sayings, truth, to those who send you"** (v21) — Hebrew קֹשְׁטְ "certainty" (an Aramaic word, also Ps 60:4); אִמְרֵי אֱמֶת "sayings of truth", then אֲמָרִים אֱמֶת "sayings, truth" (the two nouns side by side); לְהָשִׁיב "to bring back" (an answer: as "bring back word", Num 13:26); לְשֹׁלְחֶיךָ "to those who send you".
- **"neither oppress the afflicted in the gate" → "and do not crush the afflicted in the gate"** (v22) — Hebrew גָּזַל "rob"; דַּכָּא "crush"; עָנִי "afflicted".
- **"For the LORD will plead their cause, and spoil the soul of those that spoiled them" → "for Yahweh will contend their case and despoil of soul those who despoil them"** (v23) — Hebrew יָרִיב רִיבָם, verb and noun of one root (רִיב "contend", "case" in law); קָבַע "despoil" (also Mal 3:8, 9; another verb than גָּזַל "rob", v22; uncertain: "rob"); נָפֶשׁ "of soul" (= of life).
- **"Make no friendship with an angry man; and with a furious man thou shalt not go" → "Do not make friends with a master of anger, and with a man of furies you shall not go"** (v24) — Hebrew תִּתְרַע, from רֵעַ "friend"; בַּעַל אַף "master of anger" (בַּעַל + noun: one marked by the thing, literally "owner"; as 24:8; אַף "anger"); אִישׁ חֵמוֹת "a man of furies" (plural); אַל in the first line, לֹא in the second ("you shall not"): kept.
- **"Lest thou learn his ways, and get a snare to thy soul" → "lest you learn his paths and take a snare for your soul"** (v25) — Hebrew אֹרְחֹתָיו "his paths" is the qere (the written form ארחתו is singular); אָלַף "learn"; לָקַחְתָּ "take"; מוֹקֵשׁ "snare".
- **"Be not thou one of them that strike hands, or of them that are sureties for debts" → "Do not be among those who strike the palm, among those who are surety for loans"** (v26) — Hebrew תֹקְעֵי־כָף "those who strike the palm" (the gesture that seals a pledge); עֹרְבִים "those who are surety" (עָרַב); מַשָּׁאוֹת "loans" (as Neh 5:7, 10); no "or".
- **"If thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy bed" → "If you have nothing to repay, why should he take your bed"** (v27) — Hebrew לְשַׁלֵּם "to repay" (שִׁלֵּם); יִקַּח "he take" (the creditor; not named); מִשְׁכָּב "bed".
- **"Remove not the ancient landmark, which thy fathers have set" → "Do not move back the ancient border that your fathers made"** (v28) — Hebrew הִסִּיג גְּבוּל "move back the border" (WORDS-HE: no stone is named); עוֹלָם as a genitive, literally "the border of the age" (of time out of sight: here "ancient"); עָשׂוּ "made". As 23:10.
- **"Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men" → "Have you beheld a man skilled in his work? Before kings he will station himself; he will not station himself before obscure men"** (v29) — Hebrew חָזִיתָ "you have beheld" (חָזָה), with no question word: a question or a statement ("You have beheld a man …"); מָהִיר "skilled" (literally "quick", as Ezra 7:6; Ps 45:1); מְלָאכָה "work"; יִתְיַצֵּב "station himself" (to serve); חֲשֻׁכִּים "obscure men" (literally "dark ones", only here).

### Notes
- **v17–21** — the opening of "the words of the wise" (22:17–24:22); 24:23 begins a second short collection ("These also are of the wise").
