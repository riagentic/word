# Proverbs 29

[1] A man of reproofs who makes his neck hard
suddenly shall be broken, and there is no healing.

[2] When the righteous increase, the people rejoice,
but when a wicked one rules, a people groans.

[3] A man who loves wisdom makes his father rejoice,
but a companion of prostitutes does away with wealth.

[4] A king by judgment makes a land stand,
but a man of levies throws it down.

[5] A man who flatters his neighbor
is spreading a net upon his feet.

[6] In the transgression of an evil man is a snare,
but a righteous one gives a ringing cry and rejoices.

[7] A righteous one knows the cause of the poor;
a wicked one does not understand knowledge.

[8] Men of scoffing fan a town into flame,
but the wise turn back anger.

[9] A wise man enters into judgment with a senseless man,
and he is agitated and laughs, and there is no rest.

[10] Men of blood hate the blameless one,
and the upright seek his soul.

[11] All his spirit a fool brings out,
but a wise one stills it back.

[12] A ruler who gives heed to a word of falsehood —
all his ministers are wicked.

[13] The destitute man and the man of oppressions meet together;
the one who gives light to the eyes of both of them is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[14] A king who judges the poor in truth —
his throne shall be established perpetually.

[15] Rod and reproof give wisdom,
but a boy let go puts his mother to shame.

[16] When the wicked increase, transgression increases,
but the righteous shall look on their fall.

[17] Discipline your son, and he will give you rest,
and he will give delights to your soul.

[18] Where there is no vision, a people is let loose,
but the one who keeps ⟦torah|torah|the law⟧, happy is he.

[19] By words a slave is not disciplined,
for he understands, but there is no answer.

[20] You have beheld a man hasty in his words:
there is more hope for a fool than for him.

[21] One who pampers his slave from youth —
and his end shall be an heir.

[22] A man of anger stirs up strife,
and a furious man abounds in transgression.

[23] The pride of a man will bring him low,
but the lowly of spirit will hold fast glory.

[24] One who shares with a thief hates his soul;
a curse he hears, and he does not tell.

[25] The trembling of man gives a snare,
but one who trusts in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ shall be set on high.

[26] Many seek the face of a ruler,
but from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the judgment of a man.

[27] An abomination to the righteous is a man of injustice,
and an abomination to the wicked is one upright of way.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v13, 25, 26) — Hebrew יהוה *YHWH*.
- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; the existing breaks stand. וְ at the head of the second line is "but" where the line sets one person against another, otherwise "and".
- **"He, that being often reproved hardeneth his neck, shall suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy" → "A man of reproofs who makes his neck hard suddenly shall be broken, and there is no healing"** (v1) — Hebrew אִישׁ תּוֹכָחוֹת, "a man of reproofs" (one who has been reproved often; תּוֹכַחַת "reproof", WISDOM.md); מַקְשֶׁה עֹרֶף, hifil of קָשָׁה "make hard" with עֹרֶף "neck" (as Deut 10:16); יִשָּׁבֵר "shall be broken"; וְאֵין מַרְפֵּא "and there is no healing" (as 6:15).
- **"When the righteous are in authority … when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn" → "When the righteous increase … when a wicked one rules, a people groans"** (v2) — Hebrew בִּרְבוֹת "when (they) increase" (רָבָה, as v16); רָשָׁע is singular; the first line has הָעָם "the people", the second עָם without the article ("a people"); נֶאֱנַח "groan".
- **"rejoiceth his father … he that keepeth company with harlots spendeth his substance" → "makes his father rejoice … a companion of prostitutes does away with wealth"** (v3) — Hebrew יְשַׂמַּח, piel of שָׂמַח "rejoice" (v2); רֹעֶה זוֹנוֹת, literally "one who pastures with prostitutes", a companion (as 13:20; 28:7); זוֹנָה "prostitute"; אִבֵּד "do away with" (as 1:32); הוֹן "wealth", without "his".
- **"establisheth the land … he that receiveth gifts overthroweth it" → "makes a land stand … a man of levies throws it down"** (v4) — Hebrew יַעֲמִיד, hifil of עָמַד "stand"; אֶרֶץ without the article. אִישׁ תְּרוּמוֹת: תְּרוּמָה is the word for the "contribution" lifted off for Yahweh (Exod 25:2), so not the WORDS-HE "contribution" here; of a king's man it is what he lifts off the people: levies (taxes), or gifts taken (bribes); the word itself does not decide. הָרַס "throw down".
- **"flattereth … for his feet" → "flatters … upon his feet"** (v5) — Hebrew גֶּבֶר מַחֲלִיק עַל־רֵעֵהוּ, literally "makes smooth upon his neighbor" (smooth words: flatters); גֶּבֶר "man" (alone here, as the Job row; "mighty man" would say a warrior). פְּעָמָיו "his feet": whose feet (his own or the neighbor's) the Hebrew does not say. פּוֹרֵשׂ is a participle, "is spreading".
- **"there is a snare: but the righteous doth sing and rejoice" → "is a snare, but a righteous one gives a ringing cry and rejoices"** (v6) — Hebrew יָרוּן, from רָנַן "give a ringing cry" (as 1:20); צַדִּיק singular without the article.
- **"considereth the cause of the poor: but the wicked regardeth not to know it" → "knows the cause of the poor; a wicked one does not understand knowledge"** (v7) — Hebrew יֹדֵעַ "knows"; דִּין "cause" (WORDS-HE, which names this verse); דַּלִּים "the poor"; לֹא־יָבִין דָּעַת "does not understand knowledge". The second line has no "but".
- **"Scornful men bring a city into a snare" → "Men of scoffing fan a town into flame"** (v8) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי לָצוֹן "men of scoffing" (לָצוֹן, WISDOM.md); יָפִיחוּ, hifil of פּוּחַ "blow": blow on a fire, fan into flame (KJV "bring into a snare" takes it from פַּח "trap"); קִרְיָה "town" (beside עִיר "city"). **"turn away wrath" → "turn back anger"** — Hebrew יָשִׁיבוּ אָף; אַף "anger" (literally "nose").
- **"If a wise man contendeth with a foolish man, whether he rage or laugh" → "A wise man enters into judgment with a senseless man, and he is agitated and laughs"** (v9) — Hebrew נִשְׁפָּט אֶת "enter into judgment with" (as 2 Chr 22:8); there is no "if" and no "whether … or". אֱוִיל "senseless" (WISDOM.md). Who is agitated and laughs ("he") is not said: most take it of the senseless one. רָגַז "be agitated" (in anger or fear); נַחַת "rest".
- **"The bloodthirsty hate the upright: but the just seek his soul" → "Men of blood hate the blameless one, and the upright seek his soul"** (v10) — Hebrew אַנְשֵׁי דָמִים "men of blood"; תָּם "blameless" (WORDS-HE). "Seek his soul" is said of those who seek a man's life to kill him; with "the upright" as subject it is taken as caring for the blameless one's life; others read the line "and as for the upright, they (the men of blood) seek his soul". The Hebrew is kept, the sense is open.
- **"A fool uttereth all his mind: but a wise man keepeth it in till afterwards" → "All his spirit a fool brings out, but a wise one stills it back"** (v11) — Hebrew כָּל־רוּחוֹ "all his spirit" (רוּחַ, also "wind, breath": here his temper), first; הוֹצִיא "bring out"; שִׁבַּח "still" (of the sea, Ps 89:9); בְּאָחוֹר "behind, back": he holds it back.
- **"If a ruler hearken to lies, all his servants are wicked" → "A ruler who gives heed to a word of falsehood — all his ministers are wicked"** (v12) — Hebrew מֹשֵׁל מַקְשִׁיב, a participle, no "if"; הִקְשִׁיב "give heed" (1:24); דְּבַר־שָׁקֶר "a word of falsehood"; מְשָׁרְתָיו "his ministers" (שֵׁרֵת, WORDS-HE).
- **"The poor and the deceitful man meet together: the LORD lighteneth both their eyes" → "The destitute man and the man of oppressions meet together; the one who gives light to the eyes of both of them is Yahweh"** (v13) — Hebrew רָשׁ "destitute" (WORDS-HE; another word than דַּל "poor", v7, 14); אִישׁ תְּכָכִים "a man of oppressions" (תֹּךְ, Ps 10:7; 55:11); נִפְגָּשׁוּ "meet" (פָּגַשׁ). The second line ends with the name: "giving light … is Yahweh".
- **"faithfully … for ever" → "in truth … perpetually"** (v14) — Hebrew בֶּאֱמֶת "in truth"; לָעַד "perpetually" (WORDS-HE); יִכּוֹן "shall be established". The verse has no "if": the king is named first and taken up by "his throne".
- **"The rod … a child left to himself bringeth his mother to shame" → "Rod and reproof … a boy let go puts his mother to shame"** (v15) — Hebrew שֵׁבֶט וְתוֹכַחַת without articles, with one verb in the singular (יִתֵּן); נַעַר "boy"; מְשֻׁלָּח, pual of שִׁלַּח "let go, send away"; מֵבִישׁ "puts to shame".
- **"When the wicked are multiplied … shall see their fall" → "When the wicked increase … shall look on their fall"** (v16) — Hebrew בִּרְבוֹת … יִרְבֶּה, one root twice ("increase"); רָאָה בְּ "look on".
- **"Correct thy son … yea, he shall give delight" → "Discipline your son … and he will give delights"** (v17) — Hebrew יַסֵּר "discipline" (WISDOM.md); וִינִיחֶךָ "and he will give you rest"; מַעֲדַנִּים "delights", plural; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"Where there is no vision, the people perish" → "Where there is no vision, a people is let loose"** (v18) — Hebrew חָזוֹן "vision" (WORDS-HE), the seer's vision (root חָזָה, v20); "prophetic" is not in the word. יִפָּרַע, nifal of פָּרַע "let loose" (as Exod 32:25: the people "let loose"); KJV "perish" is a guess. עָם without the article.
- **"he that keepeth the law, happy is he" → "the one who keeps torah, happy is he"** (v18) — Hebrew שֹׁמֵר תּוֹרָה, without the article: the `torah` token as written (WISDOM.md); whether the Torah of Yahweh or the teaching of the wise is meant the verse does not say ("the law" in traditional mode). אַשְׁרֵהוּ "his happiness": "happy" (WISDOM.md).
- **"A servant will not be corrected by words: for though he understand he will not answer" → "By words a slave is not disciplined, for he understands, but there is no answer"** (v19) — Hebrew עֶבֶד "slave" (WORDS-HE); נוֹסַר, nifal of יָסַר "be disciplined"; כִּי "for" (also "though": the sense is that he understands and still gives no answer); מַעֲנֶה "answer" (noun).
- **"Seest thou a man that is hasty in his words?" → "You have beheld a man hasty in his words:"** (v20) — Hebrew חָזִיתָ, a perfect of חָזָה "behold" (the root of חָזוֹן, v18), with no question word (as 22:29; 26:12); it is often read as a question. אָץ "hasty" (אוּץ "press").
- **"He that delicately bringeth up his servant from a child shall have him become his son at the length" → "One who pampers his slave from youth — and his end shall be an heir"** (v21) — Hebrew מְפַנֵּק (only here) "pampers"; מִנֹּעַר "from youth"; וְאַחֲרִיתוֹ "and his end". מָנוֹן is found only here and its meaning is uncertain: "heir" or "offspring" (as from נִין "offspring", Gen 21:23; so KJV "son"), others "grief" or "insolence". Whose end (the master's or the slave's) is not said.
- **"An angry man … a furious man aboundeth in transgression" → "A man of anger … a furious man abounds in transgression"** (v22) — Hebrew אִישׁ־אַף "a man of anger" (אַף, literally "nose"); גֵּרָה "stir up"; מָדוֹן "strife"; בַּעַל חֵמָה, literally "owner of fury" (בַּעַל + noun: one marked by the thing, WORDS-HE): "a furious man"; רַב־פָּשַׁע "great of transgression".
- **"A man's pride … but honour shall uphold the humble in spirit" → "The pride of a man … but the lowly of spirit will hold fast glory"** (v23) — Hebrew גַּאֲוַת אָדָם; תַּשְׁפִּילֶנּוּ and שְׁפַל־רוּחַ, one root ("bring low" / "lowly"); תָּמַךְ "hold fast" (as 11:16, where the gracious woman holds fast glory); כָּבוֹד "glory". KJV makes "honour" the subject; the Hebrew allows both, and 11:16 favors the lowly one as subject.
- **"Whoso is partner with a thief hateth his own soul: he heareth cursing, and bewrayeth it not" → "One who shares with a thief hates his soul; a curse he hears, and he does not tell"** (v24) — Hebrew חוֹלֵק "shares, divides" (the spoil); נַפְשׁוֹ "his soul" ("own" is not written); אָלָה "curse" (the curse laid on a witness who keeps silent, Lev 5:1; "oath" in WORDS-HE: it is an adjuration); הִגִּיד "tell, declare". The object comes first.
- **"The fear of man bringeth a snare: but whoso putteth his trust in the LORD shall be safe" → "The trembling of man gives a snare, but one who trusts in Yahweh shall be set on high"** (v25) — Hebrew חֶרְדַּת אָדָם, noun of חָרַד "tremble" (not יִרְאָה "fear"); יִתֵּן "gives"; מוֹקֵשׁ "snare"; יְשֻׂגָּב, pual of שִׂגֵּב "set on high" (out of reach: safe).
- **"Many seek the ruler's favour; but every man's judgment cometh from the LORD" → "Many seek the face of a ruler, but from Yahweh is the judgment of a man"** (v26) — Hebrew פְּנֵי־מוֹשֵׁל "the face of a ruler"; מִשְׁפַּט־אִישׁ "the judgment of a man" (what is judged for him); no verb.
- **"An unjust man is an abomination to the just: and he that is upright in the way" → "An abomination to the righteous is a man of injustice, and … one upright of way"** (v27) — Hebrew תּוֹעֲבַת צַדִּיקִים, literally "the abomination of the righteous" (= to them, as Gen 43:32); אִישׁ עָוֶל "a man of injustice"; יְשַׁר־דָּרֶךְ "upright of way"; רָשָׁע singular. The Hebrew order is kept.
