# Proverbs 4

[1] Hear, sons, the discipline of a father,
and give heed to know understanding,

[2] for good teaching I have given you;
⟦torah|my torah|my teaching⟧ do not forsake.

[3] For a son I was to my father,
tender and an only one before my mother,

[4] and he taught me and said to me,
"Let your heart grasp my words;
keep my commandments and live.

[5] Get wisdom, get understanding;
do not forget, and do not turn from the sayings of my mouth.

[6] Do not forsake her, and she will keep you;
love her, and she will guard you.

[7] The beginning of wisdom: get wisdom,
and with all your property get understanding.

[8] Prize her highly, and she will raise you high;
she will honor you when you embrace her.

[9] She will give to your head a wreath of favor;
a crown of beauty she will deliver to you."

[10] Hear, my son, and take my sayings,
and the years of life will be many for you.

[11] In the way of wisdom I have taught you;
I have made you tread in the courses of uprightness.

[12] When you walk, your step will not be narrowed,
and if you run, you will not stumble.

[13] Hold fast to discipline, do not let go;
guard her, for she is your life.

[14] Into the path of the wicked do not come,
and do not go straight on in the way of the evil.

[15] Let it go, do not pass through it;
turn off from it and pass on.

[16] For they do not sleep unless they have done evil,
and their sleep is robbed unless they cause stumbling.

[17] For they eat the bread of wickedness,
and the wine of violences they drink.

[18] But the path of the righteous is like the light of brightness,
going on and shining until the day is established.

[19] The way of the wicked is like gloom;
they do not know over what they stumble.

[20] My son, to my words give heed;
to my sayings turn your ear.

[21] Let them not depart from your eyes;
keep them in the midst of your heart.

[22] For life they are to those who find them,
and to all his flesh healing.

[23] More than all keeping, guard your heart,
for from it are the outgoings of life.

[24] Remove from you crookedness of mouth,
and deviousness of lips put far from you.

[25] Let your eyes look straight ahead,
and your eyelids be straight before you.

[26] Make level the course of your foot,
and all your ways will be established.

[27] Do not turn to the right or to the left;
remove your foot from evil.

## Changes

- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v4 has three cola. The quotation marks of v4–9 (the grandfather's words) are editorial: the Hebrew has none, and where his words end is not marked.
- **"Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding" → "Hear, sons, the discipline of a father, and give heed to know understanding"** (v1) — Hebrew בָנִים "sons" (not "children"); no "O" (the Hebrew has none); מוּסָר "discipline" (as v13; 1:2); הִקְשִׁיב "give heed"; לָדַעַת בִּינָה "to know understanding" (בִּינָה, not שֵׂכֶל "insight").
- **"For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law" → "for good teaching I have given you; my torah do not forsake"** (v2) — Hebrew לֶקַח טוֹב "good teaching" (as 1:5) first, as kept; נָתַתִּי "I have given" (a perfect: "I give" is also possible); תּוֹרָתִי "my torah" (token `torah`, the father's teaching, WISDOM.md); עָזַב "forsake".
- **"For I was my father's son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother" → "For a son I was to my father, tender and an only one before my mother"** (v3) — Hebrew כִּי־בֵן הָיִיתִי לְאָבִי, "son" first; רַךְ "tender"; יָחִיד "only one" ("beloved" is not in the word); לִפְנֵי "before" (in the sight of).
- **"He taught me also … Let thine heart retain my words" → "and he taught me … Let your heart grasp my words"** (v4) — Hebrew וַיֹּרֵנִי "and he taught me" (הוֹרָה, the root of תּוֹרָה); יִתְמָךְ "let … grasp" (תָּמַךְ, as 3:18); שְׁמֹר "keep"; וֶחְיֵה "and live".
- **"forget it not; neither decline from the words of my mouth" → "do not forget, and do not turn from the sayings of my mouth"** (v5) — Hebrew קְנֵה "get" (קָנָה, as 1:5; v7); בִּינָה "understanding"; אַל־תִּשְׁכַּח has no object ("it" is not written); אַל־תֵּט "do not turn" (נָטָה, as v27); אִמְרֵי־פִי "the sayings of my mouth".
- **"she shall preserve thee … she shall keep thee" → "she will keep you … she will guard you"** (v6) — Hebrew שָׁמַר "keep", נָצַר "guard" (as 2:11). "Her" is wisdom (feminine).
- **"Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding" → "The beginning of wisdom: get wisdom, and with all your property get understanding"** (v7) — Hebrew רֵאשִׁית חָכְמָה קְנֵה חָכְמָה, word for word "the beginning of wisdom: get wisdom" (as 1:7, רֵאשִׁית also "first, chief thing"; no "therefore"); בְּכָל־קִנְיָנְךָ "with all your property" (קִנְיָן, what one has got: at the price of all of it, or among all of it); "get" three times, קָנָה and קִנְיָן one root.
- **"Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour" → "Prize her highly, and she will raise you high; she will honor you"** (v8) — Hebrew סַלְסְלֶהָ, only here: "prize highly" (uncertain: "lift up, heap up", or "embrace"); וּתְרוֹמְמֶךָּ "and she will raise you high" (רוֹמֵם); תְּכַבֵּדְךָ "she will honor you" (כִּבֵּד); חִבֵּק "embrace".
- **"an ornament of grace: a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee" → "a wreath of favor; a crown of beauty she will deliver to you"** (v9) — Hebrew לִוְיַת־חֵן "a wreath of favor" (as 1:9); עֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת "a crown of beauty" (תִּפְאֶרֶת "beauty"; כָּבוֹד "glory" does not stand here); תְּמַגְּנֶךָּ, from מִגֵּן "deliver up" (Gen 14:20); taken from מָגֵן "shield", "she will shield you (with a crown)" is the other reading.
- **"Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings; and the years of thy life shall be many" → "Hear, my son, and take my sayings, and the years of life will be many for you"** (v10) — Hebrew קַח "take" (לָקַח, as 2:1); וְיִרְבּוּ לְךָ שְׁנוֹת חַיִּים "and the years of life will be many for you" (no "your").
- **"I have taught thee in the way of wisdom; I have led thee in right paths" → "In the way of wisdom I have taught you; I have made you tread in the courses of uprightness"** (v11) — Hebrew הֹרֵתִיךָ "I have taught you" (הוֹרָה, as v4); הִדְרַכְתִּיךָ "I have made you tread" (hifil of דָּרַךְ "tread"; "lead" is נָחָה); מַעְגְּלֵי־יֹשֶׁר "courses of uprightness" (as 2:9, 13; מַעְגָּל "course", kept apart from אֹרַח "path", v14, 18, as in Prov 2).
- **"thy steps shall not be straitened" → "your step will not be narrowed"** (v12) — Hebrew יֵצַר, from צָרַר "be narrow, cramped"; צַעַד "step" (singular); כָּשַׁל "stumble".
- **"Take fast hold of instruction; let her not go: keep her" → "Hold fast to discipline, do not let go; guard her"** (v13) — Hebrew הַחֲזֵק בְּ "hold fast to" (as 3:18); אַל־תֶּרֶף "do not let go" (הִרְפָּה, "let sink"); נִצְּרֶהָ "guard her": מוּסָר is masculine, "her" and "she" are feminine (wisdom, or discipline as she is spoken of; not decided).
- **"Enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the way of evil men" → "Into the path of the wicked do not come, and do not go straight on in the way of the evil"** (v14) — Hebrew אֹרַח "path", first, as kept; בּוֹא "come"; אַל־תְּאַשֵּׁר "do not go straight on" (piel of אָשַׁר "go straight, advance", as 9:6; the same letters as אִשֵּׁר "call happy", 3:18); רָעִים "the evil" ("men" not written).
- **"Avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away" → "Let it go, do not pass through it; turn off from it and pass on"** (v15) — Hebrew פְּרָעֵהוּ "let it go" (פָּרַע, as 1:25: leave it alone); עָבַר בְּ "pass through"; שְׂטֵה מֵעָלָיו "turn off from it" (שָׂטָה, the word of the wife who strays, Num 5:12; here of leaving a road: "stray" would say the opposite); וַעֲבוֹר "and pass on" (עָבַר).
- **"For they sleep not, except they have done mischief … unless they cause some to fall" → "For they do not sleep unless they have done evil, and their sleep is robbed unless they cause stumbling"** (v16) — Hebrew לֹא יִשְׁנוּ "they do not sleep" (not "cannot"); אִם־לֹא יָרֵעוּ "unless they have done evil" (הֵרַע); נִגְזְלָה "is robbed" (גָּזַל); יַכְשִׁילוּ qere "they cause stumbling" (hifil of כָּשַׁל; no object written); the written form (ketiv) יכשולו is the qal "they stumble".
- **"and drink the wine of violence" → "and the wine of violences they drink"** (v17) — Hebrew לָחֲמוּ "they eat" (לָחַם "eat bread", the verb of לֶחֶם); רֶשַׁע "wickedness"; יֵין חֲמָסִים "the wine of violences" (plural, as 2 Sam 22:49), object first, as kept.
- **"But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day" → "But the path of the righteous is like the light of brightness, going on and shining until the day is established"** (v18) — Hebrew אוֹר נֹגַהּ "the light of brightness" ("dawn" is the sense commonly given); הוֹלֵךְ וָאוֹר "going on and shining" (= ever brighter); עַד־נְכוֹן הַיּוֹם "until the day is established" (full day, noon); צַדִּיקִים "the righteous".
- **"The way of the wicked is as darkness" → "The way of the wicked is like gloom"** (v19) — Hebrew אֲפֵלָה "gloom" (not חֹשֶׁךְ "darkness", 2:13); בַּמֶּה "over what"; כָּשַׁל "stumble".
- **"attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings" → "to my words give heed; to my sayings turn your ear"** (v20) — Hebrew לִדְבָרַי … לַאֲמָרַי first, as kept; הַקְשִׁיבָה "give heed"; הַט "turn" (נָטָה hifil; "incline" in English idiom; as 2:2).
- **"keep them in the midst of thine heart"** (v21) — Hebrew אַל־יַלִּיזוּ "let them not depart" (hifil of לוּז, as the qal in 3:21); שָׁמַר "keep"; לְבָבֶךָ (the form לֵבָב of "heart").
- **"For they are life unto those that find them, and health to all their flesh" → "For life they are to those who find them, and to all his flesh healing"** (v22) — Hebrew חַיִּים first, as kept; בְּשָׂרוֹ "his flesh" (singular: each one who finds them); מַרְפֵּא "healing".
- **"Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life" → "More than all keeping, guard your heart, for from it are the outgoings of life"** (v23) — Hebrew מִכָּל־מִשְׁמָר "more than all keeping" (מִשְׁמָר "a keeping, a watch": guard it above everything else that is guarded; "with all diligence" takes מִן otherwise); נְצֹר "guard"; תּוֹצְאוֹת חַיִּים "the outgoings of life" (where life goes out from, its sources or its ends; the word of a border's outgoings, Josh 15).
- **"Put away from thee a froward mouth, and perverse lips put far from thee" → "Remove from you crookedness of mouth, and deviousness of lips put far from you"** (v24) — Hebrew הָסֵר "remove" (הֵסִיר, as v27); עִקְּשׁוּת פֶּה "crookedness of mouth" (from עִקֵּשׁ "crooked", 2:15); לְזוּת שְׂפָתַיִם "deviousness of lips" (from לוּז, as נָלוֹז "devious", 2:15; 3:32); הִרְחִיק "put far".
- **"Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee" → "Let your eyes look straight ahead, and your eyelids be straight before you"** (v25) — Hebrew לְנֹכַח "straight ahead" (in front); הִבִּיט "look"; עַפְעַפַּיִם "eyelids"; יַיְשִׁרוּ "be straight, go straight" (hifil of יָשַׁר, as 3:6 piel "make straight"); נֶגְדֶּךָ "before you".
- **"Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established" → "Make level the course of your foot, and all your ways will be established"** (v26) — Hebrew פַּלֵּס "make level" (or "weigh": פֶּלֶס "balance", 16:11; both are defended); מַעְגַּל רַגְלֶךָ "the course of your foot" (singular; מַעְגָּל as v11); יִכֹּנוּ "will be established" (nifal of כּוּן; or a wish, "let … be established").
- **"Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil" → "Do not turn to the right or to the left; remove your foot from evil"** (v27) — Hebrew אַל־תֵּט "do not turn" (נָטָה, as v5); יָמִין וּשְׂמֹאול "right and left" ("hand" not written; "or" after the negative); הָסֵר "remove"; רָע "evil".

### Notes
- **"My son" / "sons"** (v1, 10, 20) — the father's address of Prov 1–9; in v3–9 he passes on his own father's teaching.
- **v23** — the heart (the seat of thought and will in Hebrew) is to be guarded above all.
