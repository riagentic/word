# Proverbs 16

[1] To man belong the arrangements of the heart,
but from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the answer of the tongue.

[2] All the ways of a man are pure in his eyes,
but one who measures spirits is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[3] Roll your works upon ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
and your plans will be established.

[4] Everything ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ has worked for its own end,
and even the wicked for the day of evil.

[5] An abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is everyone high of heart;
surely, he will not be clear.

[6] By ⟦steadfast-love|chesed|steadfast love⟧ and truth iniquity is atoned for,
and by the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is turning aside from evil.

[7] When ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is pleased with the ways of a man,
even his enemies he makes to be at peace with him.

[8] Better is a little with righteousness
than a multitude of yields without judgment.

[9] The heart of a man devises his way,
but ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ establishes his step.

[10] Divination is on the lips of a king;
in judgment his mouth does not act unfaithfully.

[11] A balance and scales of judgment are ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧;
his work are all the weights of the bag.

[12] An abomination to kings is doing wickedness,
for by righteousness a throne is established.

[13] The good pleasure of kings is lips of righteousness,
and him who speaks upright things he loves.

[14] The fury of a king is messengers of death,
but a wise man will appease it.

[15] In the light of the face of a king is life,
and his good pleasure is like a thick cloud of the latter rain.

[16] To get wisdom — how much better than gold!
And to get understanding is to be chosen more than silver.

[17] The highway of the upright is to turn aside from evil;
he who keeps his soul guards his way.

[18] Before breaking is exaltation,
and before stumbling, height of spirit.

[19] Better is being low of spirit with the humble
than dividing spoil with the proud.

[20] He who acts with insight upon a word will find good,
and he who trusts in ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, happy is he.

[21] The wise of heart will be called understanding,
and sweetness of lips adds teaching.

[22] A fountain of life is insight to its owners,
but the discipline of the senseless is folly.

[23] The heart of the wise makes his mouth act with insight,
and upon his lips it adds teaching.

[24] A honeycomb of honey are sayings of pleasantness,
sweet to the soul and healing to the bone.

[25] There is a way that is right before a man,
but its end is ways of death.

[26] The soul of a toiler toils for him,
for his mouth presses upon him.

[27] ⟦belial|A man of Beliyyaal|A man of worthlessness⟧ digs evil,
and upon his lip is as a scorching fire.

[28] A man of perversities lets loose strife,
and a murmurer separates a close friend.

[29] A man of violence entices his neighbor
and makes him walk in a way that is not good.

[30] Shutting his eyes to devise perversities,
pressing his lips, he has finished evil.

[31] A crown of beauty is gray hair;
in the way of righteousness it is found.

[32] Better is the slow to anger than a mighty one,
and he who rules over his spirit than he who captures a city.

[33] Into the bosom the lot is hurled,
but from ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is all its judgment.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 20, 33) — Hebrew יהוה.
- **Layout** (throughout) — two lines (cola) to a saying, in the Hebrew order, as the accents divide them. וְ at the head of the second line is "but" where the lines are set against each other, "and" where they run together (v3, 4, 6, 13, 15, 16, 18, 20, 21, 23, 27, 28, 29, 32); v5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 17, 19, 24, 30, 31 have no וְ (v12 and v26 have כִּי "for").
- **Words of the chapter** — as Prov 13–15: מַחֲשָׁבוֹת "plans" (v3), רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (v13, 15), תּוֹעֵבָה "abomination" (v5, 12; "to" for the construct, as 15:8), שֵׂכֶל "insight" (v22), הִשְׂכִּיל "act with insight" (v20, 23), בִּינָה "understanding" (v16), נָבוֹן "understanding" (v21), אֱוִיל "senseless one" (v22), לֶקַח "teaching" (v21, 23; lit. "what is received", as Deut 32:2), מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (v8, 10, 11, 33).
- **"The preparations of the heart in man, and the answer of the tongue, is from the LORD" → "To man belong the arrangements of the heart, but from Yahweh is the answer of the tongue"** (v1) — Hebrew לְאָדָם מַעַרְכֵי־לֵב, literally "to man the arrangements of the heart" ("belong" for the לְ; מַעֲרָךְ, noun of עָרַךְ "arrange"); וּמֵיְהוָה מַעֲנֵה לָשׁוֹן: the two lines are set against each other, so "but"; KJV joins them under one verb.
- **"All the ways of a man are clean in his own eyes; but the LORD weigheth the spirits" → "All the ways of a man are pure in his eyes, but one who measures spirits is Yahweh"** (v2) — Hebrew זַךְ "pure"; בְּעֵינָיו "in his eyes" (no "own"); וְתֹכֵן רוּחוֹת יְהוָה, "and a measurer of spirits — Yahweh": the participle of תָּכַן "measure, weigh" (noun תֹּכֶן "measure", Exod 5:18), in the Hebrew order.
- **"Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established" → "Roll your works upon Yahweh, and your plans will be established"** (v3) — Hebrew גֹּל אֶל־יְהוָה "roll upon Yahweh" (as Ps 37:5; "commit" in Changes only); מַעֲשֶׂיךָ "your works"; וְיִכֹּנוּ "will be established".
- **"The LORD hath made all things for himself: yea, even the wicked for the day of evil" → "Everything Yahweh has worked for its own end, and even the wicked for the day of evil"** (v4) — Hebrew כֹּל פָּעַל יְהוָה, "everything" first; פָּעַל "work" (not עָשָׂה "make"); לַמַּעֲנֵהוּ, from לְמַעַן "on account of" with a suffix: "for its own end" (or "for himself"; it sounds like מַעֲנֶה "answer", v1: some read "for its answer", what fits it); רָעָה "evil".
- **"Every one that is proud in heart is an abomination to the LORD: though hand join in hand, he shall not be unpunished" → "An abomination to Yahweh is everyone high of heart; surely, he will not be clear"** (v5) — Hebrew גְּבַהּ־לֵב "high of heart" (as "his heart was high", 2 Chr 26:16); יָד לְיָד, literally "hand to hand": a pledge, "surely" (as 11:21; English "hand to hand" says close combat; "though … join" is not written); לֹא יִנָּקֶה "he will not be clear" (נָקָה nifal, as Exod 20:7 piel).
- **"By mercy and truth iniquity is purged: and by the fear of the LORD men depart from evil" → "By chesed and truth iniquity is atoned for, and by the fear of Yahweh is turning aside from evil"** (v6) — Hebrew חֶסֶד carries its token; יְכֻפַּר, pual of כִּפֶּר "make atonement" (root sense "cover"); עָוֺן "iniquity"; סוּר מֵרָע, an infinitive: "turning aside from evil" ("men" is not written).
- **"When a man's ways please the LORD, he maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him" → "When Yahweh is pleased with the ways of a man, even his enemies he makes to be at peace with him"** (v7) — Hebrew בִּרְצוֹת יְהוָה "when Yahweh is pleased with" (רָצָה, Yahweh is the subject); יַשְׁלִם, hifil of the root of שָׁלוֹם "make to be at peace" (no token: the noun is not written); "his enemies" first in the line. Who "he" is (Yahweh, or the man's ways) is not said.
- **"Better is a little with righteousness than great revenues without right" → "… than a multitude of yields without judgment"** (v8) — Hebrew רֹב תְּבוּאוֹת "a multitude of yields"; בְּלֹא מִשְׁפָּט "without judgment" (as 13:23).
- **"A man's heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps" → "The heart of a man devises his way, but Yahweh establishes his step"** (v9) — Hebrew יְחַשֵּׁב, piel of חָשַׁב "devise"; יָכִין "establishes" (the verb of v3, 12); צַעֲדוֹ "his step" (singular; צַעַד, as 2 Sam 22:37, beside אֲשֻׁר "step" of 14:15: English has one word).
- **"A divine sentence is in the lips of the king: his mouth transgresseth not in judgment" → "Divination is on the lips of a king; in judgment his mouth does not act unfaithfully"** (v10) — Hebrew קֶסֶם "divination" (the word forbidden in Deut 18:10; here said of the king's word, as if it were a sure oracle); לֹא יִמְעַל "does not act unfaithfully" (מָעַל, Lev 5:15).
- **"A just weight and balance are the LORD's: all the weights of the bag are his work" → "A balance and scales of judgment are Yahweh's; his work are all the weights of the bag"** (v11) — Hebrew פֶּלֶס "balance" (a beam or steelyard, Isa 40:12), מֹאזְנַיִם "scales"; מִשְׁפָּט "judgment" (= just); מַעֲשֵׂהוּ "his work" first; אַבְנֵי־כִיס "the weights of the bag" (literally "the stones": as Deut 25:13).
- **"It is an abomination to kings to commit wickedness" → "An abomination to kings is doing wickedness"** (v12) — Hebrew עֲשׂוֹת רֶשַׁע "doing wickedness"; כִּי "for"; יִכּוֹן "is established".
- **"Righteous lips are the delight of kings; and they love him that speaketh right" → "The good pleasure of kings is lips of righteousness, and him who speaks upright things he loves"** (v13) — Hebrew רְצוֹן מְלָכִים "the good pleasure of kings"; שִׂפְתֵי־צֶדֶק "lips of righteousness"; יְשָׁרִים "upright things" (plural); יֶאֱהָב "he loves" (singular: each king).
- **"The wrath of a king is as messengers of death: but a wise man will pacify it" → "The fury of a king is messengers of death, but a wise man will appease it"** (v14) — Hebrew חֲמַת "fury" (חֵמָה); מַלְאֲכֵי־מָוֶת "messengers of death", no "as"; יְכַפְּרֶנָּה, piel of כִּפֶּר with the fury as object: "appease" (as Gen 32:20; literally "cover it", the verb of "make atonement", v6).
- **"In the light of the king's countenance is life; and his favour is as a cloud of the latter rain" → "In the light of the face of a king is life, and his good pleasure is like a thick cloud of the latter rain"** (v15) — Hebrew פְּנֵי־מֶלֶךְ "the face of a king"; רְצוֹנוֹ "his good pleasure"; עָב "thick cloud" (beside עָנָן "cloud"); מַלְקוֹשׁ "latter rain" (the last rain of spring, Deut 11:14).
- **"How much better is it to get wisdom than gold! and to get understanding rather to be chosen than silver!" → "To get wisdom — how much better than gold! And to get understanding is to be chosen more than silver"** (v16) — Hebrew קְנֹה, an infinitive of קָנָה "get" first; חָרוּץ "gold" (a poetic word, Ps 68:13; the same letters as "diligent", 13:4); נִבְחָר "to be chosen".
- **"The highway of the upright is to depart from evil: he that keepeth his way preserveth his soul" → "The highway of the upright is to turn aside from evil; he who keeps his soul guards his way"** (v17) — Hebrew מְסִלַּת "highway" (a raised road); סוּר מֵרָע "to turn aside from evil"; שֹׁמֵר נַפְשׁוֹ נֹצֵר דַּרְכּוֹ in the Hebrew order (as 13:3: שָׁמַר "keep", נָצַר "guard").
- **"Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall" → "Before breaking is exaltation, and before stumbling, height of spirit"** (v18) — Hebrew לִפְנֵי־שֶׁבֶר גָּאוֹן, no verb ("goeth" is not written); שֶׁבֶר "breaking" (as 15:4); גָּאוֹן "exaltation" (Exod 15:7: here a man's lifting of himself, "pride"); כִּשָּׁלוֹן "stumbling" (only here); גֹּבַהּ רוּחַ "height of spirit".
- **"Better it is to be of an humble spirit with the lowly, than to divide the spoil with the proud" → "Better is being low of spirit with the humble than dividing spoil with the proud"** (v19) — Hebrew שְׁפַל־רוּחַ "low of spirit"; the qere עֲנָוִים "humble"; the written form (ketiv) עניים "afflicted" (as 14:21); חַלֵּק שָׁלָל "dividing spoil"; גֵּאִים "the proud" (15:25).
- **"He that handleth a matter wisely shall find good: and whoso trusteth in the LORD, happy is he" → "He who acts with insight upon a word will find good, and he who trusts in Yahweh, happy is he"** (v20) — Hebrew מַשְׂכִּיל עַל־דָּבָר "one who acts with insight upon a word" (דָּבָר "word", or "matter", without the article; "the word" of 13:13 has none either); אַשְׁרָיו "happy is he".
- **"The wise in heart shall be called prudent: and the sweetness of the lips increaseth learning" → "The wise of heart will be called understanding, and sweetness of lips adds teaching"** (v21) — Hebrew לַחֲכַם־לֵב יִקָּרֵא נָבוֹן, literally "to the wise of heart, 'understanding' is called"; מֶתֶק "sweetness"; יֹסִיף "adds"; לֶקַח "teaching" (lit. "what is received").
- **"Understanding is a wellspring of life unto him that hath it: but the instruction of fools is folly" → "A fountain of life is insight to its owners, but the discipline of the senseless is folly"** (v22) — Hebrew מְקוֹר חַיִּים "a fountain of life" first; שֵׂכֶל "insight"; בְּעָלָיו "its owners" (בַּעַל, plural); מוּסַר אֱוִלִים "the discipline of the senseless" (the discipline they get is their folly, or the discipline they give).
- **"The heart of the wise teacheth his mouth, and addeth learning to his lips" → "The heart of the wise makes his mouth act with insight, and upon his lips it adds teaching"** (v23) — Hebrew יַשְׂכִּיל פִּיהוּ, hifil with an object: "makes his mouth act with insight"; וְעַל־שְׂפָתָיו יֹסִיף לֶקַח "and upon his lips it adds teaching" (as v21).
- **"Pleasant words are as an honeycomb, sweet to the soul, and health to the bones" → "A honeycomb of honey are sayings of pleasantness, sweet to the soul and healing to the bone"** (v24) — Hebrew צוּף־דְּבַשׁ "a honeycomb of honey" (as Ps 19:10), no "as"; אִמְרֵי־נֹעַם "sayings of pleasantness" (as 15:26); מַרְפֵּא "healing"; עֶצֶם "bone" (singular).
- **"There is a way that seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death" → "There is a way that is right before a man, but its end is ways of death"** (v25 = 14:12, the same Hebrew and the same English).
- **"He that laboureth laboureth for himself; for his mouth craveth it of him" → "The soul of a toiler toils for him, for his mouth presses upon him"** (v26) — Hebrew נֶפֶשׁ עָמֵל עָמְלָה לּוֹ: נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (here the appetite: hunger works for the worker) is written and is the subject; עָמֵל "toiler", עָמַל "toil" (root of עָמָל "toil"); אָכַף עָלָיו "presses upon him" (only here).
- **"An ungodly man diggeth up evil: and in his lips there is as a burning fire" → "A man of Beliyyaal digs evil, and upon his lip is as a scorching fire"** (v27) — Hebrew אִישׁ בְּלִיַּעַל (token: "a man of worthlessness", as 1 Sam 25:25); כֹּרֶה "digs" (כָּרָה, as a pit is dug, Ps 7:15); the qere שְׂפָתוֹ "his lip"; the written form (ketiv) שפתיו "his lips"; צָרָבֶת "scorching".
- **"A froward man soweth strife: and a whisperer separateth chief friends" → "A man of perversities lets loose strife, and a murmurer separates a close friend"** (v28) — Hebrew אִישׁ תַּהְפֻּכוֹת "a man of perversities" (Deut 32:20); יְשַׁלַּח "lets loose" (piel of שָׁלַח); מָדוֹן "strife"; נִרְגָּן "murmurer" (נֵרְגַּן "murmur", Deut 1:27: one who whispers against others; uncertain); אַלּוּף "close friend" (as 2:17; 17:9; Ps 55:13 "companion"; the word of a "chief" of Edom, Gen 36, is another sense), singular.
- **"A violent man enticeth his neighbour, and leadeth him into the way that is not good" → "A man of violence entices his neighbor and makes him walk in a way that is not good"** (v29) — Hebrew אִישׁ חָמָס "a man of violence"; יְפַתֶּה "entices"; וְהוֹלִיכוֹ "and makes him walk" (hifil of הָלַךְ, as Deut 8:2); בְּדֶרֶךְ "in a way", no article.
- **"He shutteth his eyes to devise froward things: moving his lips he bringeth evil to pass" → "Shutting his eyes to devise perversities, pressing his lips, he has finished evil"** (v30) — Hebrew עֹצֶה עֵינָיו (עָצָה "shut", only here), קֹרֵץ שְׂפָתָיו "pressing his lips" (קָרַץ, of the eye "wink", 6:13; 10:10): two participles; כִּלָּה רָעָה "he has finished evil" (a perfect: the evil is as good as done).
- **"The hoary head is a crown of glory, if it be found in the way of righteousness" → "A crown of beauty is gray hair; in the way of righteousness it is found"** (v31) — Hebrew עֲטֶרֶת תִּפְאֶרֶת "a crown of beauty" first; שֵׂיבָה "gray hair" (elsewhere "old age"); the second line is a plain statement, not "if" (בְּדֶרֶךְ צְדָקָה תִּמָּצֵא).
- **"He that is slow to anger is better than the mighty; and he that ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city" → "Better is the slow to anger than a mighty one, and he who rules over his spirit than he who captures a city"** (v32) — Hebrew טוֹב first; אֶרֶךְ אַפַּיִם "slow to anger" (literally "long of nostrils", as 14:29); גִּבּוֹר "mighty one"; מֹשֵׁל בְּרוּחוֹ "he who rules over his spirit"; לֹכֵד "he who captures".
- **"The lot is cast into the lap; but the whole disposing thereof is of the LORD" → "Into the bosom the lot is hurled, but from Yahweh is all its judgment"** (v33) — Hebrew בַּחֵיק "into the bosom" (the fold of a garment) first; יוּטַל, hofal of טוּל "hurl"; וּמֵיְהוָה כָּל־מִשְׁפָּטוֹ "and from Yahweh is all its judgment" (the decision it gives).

### Notes
- **v1–7, 9, 11, 20, 33** — sayings about Yahweh gather at the head of the chapter and close it (v33).
- **v10–15** — sayings about the king.
- **v25** — repeats 14:12 word for word.
