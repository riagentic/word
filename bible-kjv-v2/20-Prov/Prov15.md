# Proverbs 15

[1] A tender answer turns back fury,
but a word of pain makes anger go up.

[2] The tongue of the wise makes knowledge good,
but the mouth of fools pours forth folly.

[3] In every place are the eyes of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
keeping watch on the evil and the good.

[4] Healing of the tongue is a tree of life,
but perverseness in it is a breaking in the spirit.

[5] A senseless one spurns the discipline of his father,
but he who keeps reproof acts shrewdly.

[6] The house of the righteous is much store,
but in the yield of the wicked is trouble.

[7] The lips of the wise strew knowledge,
but the heart of fools is not so.

[8] The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,
but the prayer of the upright is his good pleasure.

[9] An abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the way of the wicked,
but him who pursues righteousness he loves.

[10] Discipline is evil to him who forsakes the path;
he who hates reproof will die.

[11] Sheol and ⟦abaddon|Avaddon|Abaddon⟧ are before ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧;
how much more the hearts of the sons of man!

[12] A scoffer does not love being reproved;
to the wise he will not go.

[13] A glad heart makes a face good,
but in pain of heart the spirit is stricken.

[14] The heart of the understanding seeks knowledge,
but the mouth of fools feeds on folly.

[15] All the days of the afflicted are evil,
but the good of heart has a feast continually.

[16] Better is a little with the fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧
than a great treasury and confusion in it.

[17] Better is an allowance of greens and love there
than a crib-fed ox and hatred with it.

[18] A man of fury stirs up strife,
but the slow to anger quiets contention.

[19] The way of a sluggard is like a hedge of thorn,
but the path of the upright is built up.

[20] A wise son makes a father glad,
but a fool of a man despises his mother.

[21] Folly is gladness to one lacking heart,
but a man of understanding makes his walk straight.

[22] Plans are made void where there is no secret counsel,
but in a multitude of counselors they stand.

[23] A man has gladness in the answer of his mouth,
and a word in its time, how good it is!

[24] The path of life is upward for one who acts with insight,
in order to turn aside from Sheol beneath.

[25] ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|The LORD⟧ tears away the house of the proud,
but he sets up the border of the widow.

[26] An abomination to ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ are plans of evil,
but clean are sayings of pleasantness.

[27] He who troubles his house is he who gains unjust gain,
but he who hates gifts will live.

[28] The heart of the righteous softly recites how to answer,
but the mouth of the wicked pours forth evil things.

[29] Far is ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ from the wicked,
but the prayer of the righteous he hears.

[30] The light of the eyes makes the heart glad;
a good report makes the bone fat.

[31] The ear that hears the reproof of life
will lodge in the midst of the wise.

[32] He who lets discipline go rejects his soul,
but he who hears reproof gets heart.

[33] The fear of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ is the discipline of wisdom,
and before glory is humility.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (v3, 8, 9, 11, 16, 25, 26, 29, 33) — Hebrew יהוה.
- **Layout** (throughout) — two lines (cola) to a saying, in the Hebrew order, as the accents divide them; v16–17 and v31 are one sentence running over both lines. וְ at the head of the second line is "but" where the lines are set against each other, "and" where they run together (v23, 33); v3, 10, 11, 12, 24, 30 have no וְ.
- **Words of the chapter** — as Prov 13–14: מוּסָר "discipline" (v5, 10, 32, 33), תּוֹכַחַת "reproof" (v5, 10, 31, 32), הוֹכִיחַ "reprove" (v12), כְּסִיל "fool", אֱוִיל "senseless one" (v5), אִוֶּלֶת "folly", נָבוֹן "understanding" (v14), תְּבוּנָה "understanding" (v21), דַּעַת "knowledge", לֵץ "scoffer", רָצוֹן "good pleasure" (v8), נֶפֶשׁ "soul" (v32), מַחֲשָׁבוֹת "plans" (v22, 26; as Ps 33:10, 11).
- **"A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger" → "A tender answer turns back fury, but a word of pain makes anger go up"** (v1) — Hebrew מַעֲנֶה־רַּךְ "a tender answer"; יָשִׁיב "turns back"; חֵמָה "fury" (beside אַף "anger"); דְּבַר־עֶצֶב "a word of pain" (a word that hurts; עֶצֶב as 14:23), singular; יַעֲלֶה hifil of עָלָה "make go up".
- **"The tongue of the wise useth knowledge aright: but the mouth of fools poureth out foolishness" → "The tongue of the wise makes knowledge good, but the mouth of fools pours forth folly"** (v2) — Hebrew תֵּיטִיב, hifil of יָטַב "make good"; יַבִּיעַ "pours forth" (bubbles out, as a spring), so v28.
- **"The eyes of the LORD are in every place, beholding the evil and the good" → "In every place are the eyes of Yahweh, keeping watch on the evil and the good"** (v3) — Hebrew בְּכָל־מָקוֹם first; צֹפוֹת "keeping watch" (צָפָה, the verb of צֹפֶה "watchman").
- **"A wholesome tongue is a tree of life: but perverseness therein is a breach in the spirit" → "Healing of the tongue is a tree of life, but perverseness in it is a breaking in the spirit"** (v4) — Hebrew מַרְפֵּא לָשׁוֹן "healing of the tongue" (מַרְפֵּא "healing", also "calm": 14:30; the one word kept); סֶלֶף "perverseness" (as 11:3; noun of סִלֵּף "overturn"); שֶׁבֶר בְּרוּחַ "a breaking in the spirit".
- **"A fool despiseth his father's instruction: but he that regardeth reproof is prudent" → "A senseless one spurns the discipline of his father, but he who keeps reproof acts shrewdly"** (v5) — Hebrew אֱוִיל "senseless one"; יִנְאַץ "spurns" (נִאֵץ, beside בָּזָה "despise"); שֹׁמֵר "keeps"; יַעְרִם, hifil of the root of עָרוּם "shrewd": "acts shrewdly" (1 Sam 23:22).
- **"In the house of the righteous is much treasure: but in the revenues of the wicked is trouble" → "The house of the righteous is much store, but in the yield of the wicked is trouble"** (v6) — Hebrew בֵּית צַדִּיק חֹסֶן רָב: no "in" before "house" (the house may also be a place-word without the preposition, "in the house"); חֹסֶן "store" (laid-up wealth; not אוֹצָר "treasury", v16); תְּבוּאָה "yield"; נֶעְכָּרֶת, nifal participle of עָכַר "trouble": "a thing troubled".
- **"The lips of the wise disperse knowledge: but the heart of the foolish doeth not so" → "The lips of the wise strew knowledge, but the heart of fools is not so"** (v7) — Hebrew יְזָרוּ "strew" (זָרָה, as Exod 32:20); לֹא־כֵן "not so" (no "doeth"): either "does not do so" or "is not right" (כֵּן "honest, right", Gen 42:11); both are in the words.
- **"The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD: but the prayer of the upright is his delight" → "… but the prayer of the upright is his good pleasure"** (v8) — Hebrew תּוֹעֲבַת יְהוָה, literally "the abomination of Yahweh" (as Gen 43:32), so v9, 26; רְצוֹנוֹ "his good pleasure".
- **"The way of the wicked is an abomination unto the LORD: but he loveth him that followeth after righteousness" → "An abomination to Yahweh is the way of the wicked, but him who pursues righteousness he loves"** (v9) — the Hebrew order of both lines; מְרַדֵּף, piel of רָדַף "pursue".
- **"Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way: and he that hateth reproof shall die" → "Discipline is evil to him who forsakes the path; he who hates reproof will die"** (v10) — Hebrew מוּסָר רָע לְעֹזֵב אֹרַח: "evil" (רָע), or "evil discipline is for him who forsakes"; אֹרַח "path" (beside דֶּרֶךְ "way"); the second line has no וְ.
- **"Hell and destruction are before the LORD: how much more then the hearts of the children of men?" → "Sheol and Abaddon are before Yahweh; how much more the hearts of the sons of man!"** (v11) — Hebrew שְׁאוֹל "Sheol" and אֲבַדּוֹן *Avaddon*, "destruction" (a name of the place of the dead beside Sheol, Job 26:6; token); נֶגֶד "before"; אַף כִּי "how much more" (literally "also that"); בְּנֵי־אָדָם "sons of man".
- **"A scorner loveth not one that reproveth him: neither will he go unto the wise" → "A scoffer does not love being reproved; to the wise he will not go"** (v12) — Hebrew הוֹכֵחַ לוֹ, an infinitive, literally "reproving to him"; "neither" is not written: אֶל־חֲכָמִים "to the wise" first.
- **"A merry heart maketh a cheerful countenance: but by sorrow of the heart the spirit is broken" → "A glad heart makes a face good, but in pain of heart the spirit is stricken"** (v13) — Hebrew יֵיטִב פָּנִים "makes a face good" (as v2); עַצְּבַת־לֵב "pain of heart" (עַצֶּבֶת, a noun of the root of עֶצֶב "pain", Ps 147:3); רוּחַ נְכֵאָה "a stricken spirit" (the line has no verb: "is" supplied).
- **"The heart of him that hath understanding seeketh knowledge: but the mouth of fools feedeth on foolishness" → "The heart of the understanding seeks knowledge, but the mouth of fools feeds on folly"** (v14) — the qere וּפִי "and the mouth"; the written form (ketiv) ופני "and the face". יִרְעֶה "feeds" (רָעָה, of a flock grazing).
- **"he that is of a merry heart hath a continual feast" → "the good of heart has a feast continually"** (v15) — Hebrew וְטוֹב־לֵב מִשְׁתֶּה תָמִיד, literally "and the good of heart — a feast continually": "has" is supplied; עָנִי "afflicted".
- **"Better is little with the fear of the LORD than great treasure and trouble therewith" → "… than a great treasury and confusion in it"** (v16) — Hebrew טוֹב … מִן, "good … more than" = "better … than"; בְּיִרְאַת "with (lit. in) the fear"; אוֹצָר "treasury" (a store-house and what is in it, Deut 28:12); מְהוּמָה "confusion" (Deut 7:23); בוֹ "in it".
- **"Better is a dinner of herbs where love is, than a stalled ox and hatred therewith" → "Better is an allowance of greens and love there than a crib-fed ox and hatred with it"** (v17) — Hebrew אֲרֻחַת יָרָק "an allowance of greens" (אֲרֻחָה, a portion of food, as 2 Kgs 25:30; יָרָק "greens", Deut 11:10); וְאַהֲבָה־שָׁם "and love there"; שׁוֹר אָבוּס, an ox fed at the crib (אֵבוּס, 14:4).
- **"A wrathful man stirreth up strife: but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife" → "A man of fury stirs up strife, but the slow to anger quiets contention"** (v18) — Hebrew אִישׁ חֵמָה "a man of fury"; מָדוֹן "strife", רִיב "contention" (two words); יַשְׁקִיט "quiets".
- **"The way of the slothful man is as an hedge of thorns: but the way of the righteous is made plain" → "The way of a sluggard is like a hedge of thorn, but the path of the upright is built up"** (v19) — Hebrew עָצֵל "sluggard"; מְשֻׂכַת חָדֶק "a hedge of thorn"; אֹרַח "path" (beside דֶּרֶךְ "way"); יְשָׁרִים "upright"; סְלֻלָה "built up" (a raised road: the verb of מְסִלָּה "highway").
- **"A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish man despiseth his mother" → "A wise son makes a father glad, but a fool of a man despises his mother"** (v20) — Hebrew כְּסִיל אָדָם "a fool of a man" (אָדָם, a man of mankind).
- **"Folly is joy to him that is destitute of wisdom: but a man of understanding walketh uprightly" → "Folly is gladness to one lacking heart, but a man of understanding makes his walk straight"** (v21) — Hebrew חֲסַר־לֵב "lacking heart" (the heart as the seat of the mind; "wisdom" is not written); יְיַשֶּׁר־לָכֶת, literally "makes straight to walk" ("his" supplied).
- **"Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established" → "Plans are made void where there is no secret counsel, but in a multitude of counselors they stand"** (v22) — Hebrew הָפֵר, an infinitive of הֵפֵר "make void" (Ps 33:10), used as the verb; בְּאֵין סוֹד "where there is no secret counsel" (סוֹד, the close circle and what is said in it); יוֹעֲצִים "counselors"; תָּקוּם "it stands" (singular: each plan).
- **"A man hath joy by the answer of his mouth: and a word spoken in due season, how good is it!" → "A man has gladness in the answer of his mouth, and a word in its time, how good it is!"** (v23) — Hebrew שִׂמְחָה לָאִישׁ, literally "gladness to a man" ("has" for the לְ); בְּעִתּוֹ "in its time" ("spoken" is not written).
- **"The way of life is above to the wise, that he may depart from hell beneath" → "The path of life is upward for one who acts with insight, in order to turn aside from Sheol beneath"** (v24) — Hebrew אֹרַח "path"; לְמַעְלָה "upward"; מַשְׂכִּיל "one who acts with insight"; לְמַעַן סוּר "in order to turn aside"; שְׁאוֹל מָטָּה "Sheol beneath".
- **"The LORD will destroy the house of the proud: but he will establish the border of the widow" → "Yahweh tears away the house of the proud, but he sets up the border of the widow"** (v25) — Hebrew בֵּית גֵּאִים יִסַּח יְהוָה, the object first ("the house of the proud Yahweh tears away": English needs the name first); נָסַח "tear away" (Deut 28:63 nifal); וְיַצֵּב "sets up" (הִצִּיב, the form of a wish: "and may he set up" is also possible); גְּבוּל "border".
- **"The thoughts of the wicked are an abomination to the LORD: but the words of the pure are pleasant words" → "An abomination to Yahweh are plans of evil, but clean are sayings of pleasantness"** (v26) — Hebrew מַחְשְׁבוֹת רָע "plans of evil" (or "of an evil man"; "the wicked" is רָשָׁע, not written); וּטְהֹרִים אִמְרֵי־נֹעַם "and clean — sayings of pleasantness" (טָהוֹר "clean", as of what is fit for Yahweh; זַךְ "pure" is 16:2) (אֵמֶר "saying", נֹעַם "pleasantness"); "the words of the pure" reads the line otherwise.
- **"He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house; but he that hateth gifts shall live" → "He who troubles his house is he who gains unjust gain, but he who hates gifts will live"** (v27) — Hebrew עֹכֵר בֵּיתוֹ בּוֹצֵעַ בָּצַע, the Hebrew order; בָּצַע בֶּצַע "gain unjust gain" (verb and noun of one root); מַתָּנֹת "gifts" (given to bend judgment).
- **"The heart of the righteous studieth to answer: but the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things" → "The heart of the righteous softly recites how to answer, but the mouth of the wicked pours forth evil things"** (v28) — Hebrew יֶהְגֶּה "softly recites" (הָגָה, as Ps 1:2; 37:30; of the heart: it goes over its words before it speaks); לַעֲנוֹת "to answer" ("how" supplied); יַבִּיעַ "pours forth" (v2); רָעוֹת "evil things".
- **"The LORD is far from the wicked: but he heareth the prayer of the righteous" → "Far is Yahweh from the wicked, but the prayer of the righteous he hears"** (v29) — the Hebrew order of both lines.
- **"The light of the eyes rejoiceth the heart: and a good report maketh the bones fat" → "The light of the eyes makes the heart glad; a good report makes the bone fat"** (v30) — Hebrew מְאוֹר־עֵינַיִם "the light of the eyes" (מָאוֹר, a thing that gives light, Gen 1:16); שְׁמוּעָה "report"; תְּדַשֶּׁן "makes fat"; עָצֶם "bone" (singular); no "and" in the Hebrew.
- **"The ear that heareth the reproof of life abideth among the wise" → "… will lodge in the midst of the wise"** (v31) — Hebrew תָּלִין "will lodge" (לוּן, stay the night); בְּקֶרֶב "in the midst of".
- **"He that refuseth instruction despiseth his own soul: but he that heareth reproof getteth understanding" → "He who lets discipline go rejects his soul, but he who hears reproof gets heart"** (v32) — Hebrew פּוֹרֵעַ "lets go" (as 13:18); מוֹאֵס "rejects"; קוֹנֶה לֵּב "gets heart" (the mind, as v21; "understanding" is not written).
- **"The fear of the LORD is the instruction of wisdom; and before honour is humility" → "The fear of Yahweh is the discipline of wisdom, and before glory is humility"** (v33) — Hebrew מוּסַר חָכְמָה "the discipline of wisdom"; כָבוֹד "glory"; עֲנָוָה "humility" (= 18:12b).

### Notes
- **v11** — Sheol and Abaddon side by side also Job 26:6; Prov 27:20.
- **v16–17** — two "better … than" sayings side by side.
