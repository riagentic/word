# Proverbs 7

[1] My son, keep my sayings,
and my commandments treasure up with you;

[2] keep my commandments and live,
and my ⟦torah|torah|teaching⟧ as the pupil of your eyes;

[3] bind them on your fingers;
write them on the tablet of your heart.

[4] Say to wisdom, "You are my sister,"
and call understanding a relative,

[5] to keep you from a strange woman,
from a foreign woman who has made her sayings smooth.

[6] For at the window of my house,
through my lattice I looked down,

[7] and I saw among the simple,
I discerned among the sons,
a young man lacking heart,

[8] passing along in the street beside her corner,
and the way to her house he steps along,

[9] in the twilight, in the evening of the day,
in the pupil of night and gloom.

[10] And behold, a woman to meet him,
in the attire of a prostitute, and guarded of heart.

[11] Loud she is, and stubborn;
in her house her feet do not abide;

[12] now in the street, now in the squares,
and beside every corner she lies in ambush.

[13] And she held fast to him and kissed him;
she made her face strong and said to him,

[14] "Sacrifices of peace offerings are upon me;
today I have repaid my vows.

[15] Therefore I have come out to meet you,
to seek your face eagerly, and I have found you.

[16] With coverings I have covered my couch,
striped cloths of the yarn of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧;

[17] I have sprinkled my bed
with myrrh, aloes and cinnamon.

[18] Come, let us drink our fill of love until the morning;
let us delight ourselves with loves.

[19] For the man is not in his house;
he has gone on a way far off;

[20] the bag of silver he has taken in his hand;
on the day of the full moon he will come to his house."

[21] She turned him aside with the greatness of her teaching;
with the smoothness of her lips she drew him away.

[22] He goes after her suddenly,
as an ox comes to the slaughter,
and as a fetter to the discipline of a senseless one,

[23] until an arrow cleaves his liver;
as a bird hastens to a trap,
and he does not know that it is for his soul.

[24] And now, sons, listen to me,
and be attentive to the sayings of my mouth.

[25] Let your heart not stray to her ways;
do not wander in her tracks,

[26] for many are the slain she has made fall,
and mighty are all those she has killed.

[27] Ways of Sheol is her house,
going down to the chambers of death.

## Changes

- **Layout** (throughout) — one Hebrew line (colon) per printed line, in the Hebrew order; v7, 22 and 23 have three lines (v23 is divided at the pause inside its first half).
- **"keep my words, and lay up my commandments with thee" → "keep my sayings, and my commandments treasure up with you"** (v1) — Hebrew אֲמָרָי, אֵמֶר "saying" (beside דָּבָר "word"; also v5, 24); תִּצְפֹּן "treasure up" (צָפַן "hide, store"), in the Hebrew order.
- **"and my law as the apple of thine eye" → "and my torah as the pupil of your eyes"** (v2) — Hebrew וְתוֹרָתִי כְּאִישׁוֹן עֵינֶיךָ: the line has no verb ("keep" carries over); תּוֹרָה keeps the `torah` token (WISDOM.md; a parent's teaching, "teaching" in traditional mode); אִישׁוֹן "pupil" (literally "little man", as Deut 32:10); עֵינֶיךָ plural "eyes". וֶחְיֵה is an imperative, "and live".
- **"and call understanding thy kinswoman" → "and call understanding a relative"** (v4) — Hebrew וּמֹדָע לַבִּינָה תִקְרָא: בִּינָה "understanding" (WISDOM.md; not "insight", which is שֵׂכֶל); מֹדָע "relative", literally "one known" (as Ruth 2:1), masculine; "your" is not written.
- **"From the strange woman, from the stranger which flattereth with her words" → "from a strange woman, from a foreign woman who has made her sayings smooth"** (v5) — Hebrew אִשָּׁה זָרָה "a strange woman" and נָכְרִיָּה "a foreign woman" (WISDOM.md; no article); אֲמָרֶיהָ הֶחֱלִיקָה, a perfect, "she has made her sayings smooth" (as 2:16). "forbidden woman", "adulteress" interpreted; whose wife she is, v19 says.
- **"I looked through my casement" → "through my lattice I looked down"** (v6) — Hebrew בְּעַד אֶשְׁנַבִּי נִשְׁקָפְתִּי: אֶשְׁנָב "lattice" (Judg 5:28); נִשְׁקַף "look down" (as 2 Sam 6:16).
- **"And beheld among the simple ones, I discerned among the youths, a young man void of understanding" → "and I saw among the simple, I discerned among the sons, a young man lacking heart"** (v7) — Hebrew וָאֵרֶא "and I saw" (wayyiqtol); פְּתָאיִם "the simple" (WISDOM.md); בָּנִים "sons" (not "youths"); נַעַר "young man"; חֲסַר־לֵב "lacking heart" (WISDOM.md; the heart as the seat of the mind).
- **"Passing through the street near her corner; and he went the way to her house" → "passing along in the street beside her corner, and the way to her house he steps along"** (v8) — Hebrew עֹבֵר "passing" (עָבַר); אֵצֶל "beside"; יִצְעָד, צָעַד "step" (literally; Judg 5:4 "march"); the Hebrew order kept.
- **"In the twilight, in the evening, in the black and dark night" → "in the twilight, in the evening of the day, in the pupil of night and gloom"** (v9) — Hebrew בְּעֶרֶב יוֹם "in the evening of the day"; בְּאִישׁוֹן לַיְלָה, literally "in the pupil of night" (the same word as v2: the black middle of the night); אֲפֵלָה "gloom". נֶשֶׁף "twilight" (dusk here).
- **"there met him a woman with the attire of an harlot, and subtil of heart" → "a woman to meet him, in the attire of a prostitute, and guarded of heart"** (v10) — Hebrew וְהִנֵּה אִשָּׁה לִקְרָאתוֹ, with no verb ("behold" kept); שִׁית "attire"; זוֹנָה "prostitute"; וּנְצֻרַת לֵב, literally "guarded of heart" (נָצַר "guard": she keeps her purpose hidden; "wily" is the sense).
- **"She is loud and stubborn; her feet abide not in her house" → "Loud she is, and stubborn; in her house her feet do not abide"** (v11) — Hebrew הֹמִיָּה, from הָמָה "roar, be in uproar" (of a person "moan", Ps 42:5): "loud, restless"; סוֹרֶרֶת "stubborn" (as סוֹרֵר, Deut 21:18); שָׁכַן "abide". Hebrew order kept.
- **"Now is she without, now in the streets, and lieth in wait at every corner" → "now in the street, now in the squares, and beside every corner she lies in ambush"** (v12) — Hebrew פַּעַם … פַּעַם "now … now" (literally "a time … a time"); חוּץ "the outside, the street"; רְחֹבוֹת "squares"; תֶאֱרֹב "she lies in ambush" (אָרַב, as 1:11).
- **"So she caught him, and kissed him, and with an impudent face said unto him" → "And she held fast to him and kissed him; she made her face strong and said to him"** (v13) — Hebrew perfects and וַתֹּאמַר: the scene is told in the past (from "I looked down", v6); הֶחֱזִיקָה בּוֹ "held fast to him"; הֵעֵזָה פָנֶיהָ, literally "she made her face strong" (עָזַז: = brazen).
- **"I have peace offerings with me; this day have I payed my vows" → "Sacrifices of peace offerings are upon me; today I have repaid my vows"** (v14) — Hebrew זִבְחֵי שְׁלָמִים עָלָי, "upon me" (an obligation she had; the meat of such sacrifices was eaten on the day: Lev 7:15–16); שִׁלַּמְתִּי נְדָרָי "I have repaid my vows" (שִׁלֵּם נֶדֶר).
- **"to seek thy face diligently" → "to seek your face eagerly"** (v15) — Hebrew לְשַׁחֵר (שִׁחֵר "seek eagerly", root of שַׁחַר "dawn").
- **"I have decked my bed with coverings of tapestry, with carved works, with fine linen of Egypt" → "With coverings I have covered my couch, striped cloths of the yarn of Egypt"** (v16) — Hebrew מַרְבַדִּים רָבַדְתִּי, noun and verb of one root ("coverings … covered"); עֶרֶשׂ "couch"; חֲטֻבוֹת "striped" or "colored" cloths (uncertain); אֵטוּן is found only here: "yarn, thread" (uncertain; "linen" in KJV). מִצְרַיִם keeps its token. The current "cotton" is not in the Hebrew.
- **"I have perfumed my bed" → "I have sprinkled my bed"** (v17) — Hebrew נַפְתִּי, נוּף "sprinkle, wave"; מִשְׁכָּב "bed"; "with" is supplied before the three spices (מֹר "myrrh", אֲהָלִים "aloes", קִנָּמוֹן "cinnamon").
- **"let us take our fill of love until the morning: let us solace ourselves with loves" → "let us drink our fill of love until the morning; let us delight ourselves with loves"** (v18) — Hebrew לְכָה "Come"; נִרְוֶה, רָוָה "drink one's fill" (as 5:19 "drench"); דֹּדִים "love" (literally "loves", plural, as Song 1:2); אֳהָבִים "loves" (as 5:19), a second word; נִתְעַלְּסָה "let us delight ourselves" (cohortative).
- **"For the goodman is not at home, he is gone a long journey" → "For the man is not in his house; he has gone on a way far off"** (v19) — Hebrew הָאִישׁ "the man" (she does not say "my husband"); בְּבֵיתוֹ "in his house"; בְּדֶרֶךְ מֵרָחוֹק "on a way from far" (far off).
- **"He hath taken a bag of money with him, and will come home at the day appointed" → "the bag of silver he has taken in his hand; on the day of the full moon he will come to his house"** (v20) — Hebrew צְרוֹר־הַכֶּסֶף "the bag of silver" (כֶּסֶף: silver as money); בְּיָדוֹ "in his hand"; הַכֵּסֶא "the full moon" (as Ps 81:3 כֶּסֶה); בֵיתוֹ "his house".
- **"With her much fair speech she caused him to yield, with the flattering of her lips she forced him" → "She turned him aside with the greatness of her teaching; with the smoothness of her lips she drew him away"** (v21) — Hebrew הִטַּתּוּ "she turned him aside" (perfect; הִטָּה); בְּרֹב לִקְחָהּ, לֶקַח "teaching", literally "what is received" (1:5; 4:2): her "much teaching", said with irony ("persuasion" is the sense); חֵלֶק "smoothness" (from חָלָק "smooth", 5:3); תַּדִּיחֶנּוּ "she drew him away" (הִדִּיחַ, Deut 13:5, 10).
- **"He goeth after her straightway, as an ox goeth to the slaughter, or as a fool to the correction of the stocks" → "He goes after her suddenly, as an ox comes to the slaughter, and as a fetter to the discipline of a senseless one"** (v22) — Hebrew הוֹלֵךְ "goes" (participle); פִּתְאֹם "suddenly"; שׁוֹר "ox"; טֶבַח "slaughter". The third line, וּכְעֶכֶס אֶל־מוּסַר אֱוִיל, is obscure: עֶכֶס is an anklet (Isa 3:18), here taken as a fetter; מוּסָר "discipline"; אֱוִיל "senseless one" (WISDOM.md). KJV turns the words round ("a fool to the correction of the stocks"); many change the Hebrew letters to get "as a stag to a noose": the Masoretic words are kept, word for word, and the meaning is uncertain.
- **"Till a dart strike through his liver; as a bird hasteth to the snare, and knoweth not that it is for his life" → "until an arrow cleaves his liver; as a bird hastens to a trap, and he does not know that it is for his soul"** (v23) — Hebrew יְפַלַּח "cleaves" (פִּלַּח "split"); חֵץ "arrow"; פַּח "trap" (the fowler's; מוֹקֵשׁ is "snare"); כִּי־בְנַפְשׁוֹ הוּא, literally "that it is in (= at the cost of) his soul"; נֶפֶשׁ "soul".
- **"Hearken unto me now therefore, O ye children" → "And now, sons, listen to me"** (v24) — Hebrew וְעַתָּה בָנִים: no "O" (reviewer row); אִמְרֵי־פִי "the sayings of my mouth".
- **"Let not thine heart decline to her ways, go not astray in her paths" → "Let your heart not stray to her ways; do not wander in her tracks"** (v25) — Hebrew אַל־יֵשְׂטְ, שָׂטָה "stray" (as Num 5:12; 4:15); אַל־תֵּתַע, תָּעָה "wander"; נְתִיבוֹת "tracks" (נְתִיבָה).
- **"For she hath cast down many wounded: yea, many strong men have been slain by her" → "for many are the slain she has made fall, and mighty are all those she has killed"** (v26) — Hebrew רַבִּים חֲלָלִים הִפִּילָה "many are the slain she has made fall"; וַעֲצֻמִים כָּל־הֲרֻגֶיהָ, "and mighty (or 'numerous') are all her killed ones": עָצוּם is both "mighty" and "numerous" (Gen 18:18); the word is kept with its first sense.
- **"Her house is the way to hell, going down to the chambers of death" → "Ways of Sheol is her house, going down to the chambers of death"** (v27) — Hebrew דַּרְכֵי שְׁאוֹל בֵּיתָהּ: "ways" is plural, "of Sheol" a genitive, and the Hebrew order is kept; שְׁאוֹל is the plain name of the place of the dead, not "hell". חַדְרֵי־מָוֶת "the chambers of death".

### Notes
- **Hebrew verse numbers** — the same as ours in this chapter.
- **The scene** (v6–23) — told by the father from his window; v14–20 are the woman's words.
