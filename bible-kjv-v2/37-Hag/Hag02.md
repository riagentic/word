# Haggai 2

[1] In the seventh, on the twenty-first of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came by the hand of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet, saying:

[2] Say, please, to ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, governor of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and to ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the great priest, and to the remnant of the people, saying:

[3] Who is left among you who saw this house in its first glory? And how do you see it now? Is not the like of it as nothing in your eyes?

[4] And now be strong, ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and be strong, ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the great priest; and be strong, all the people of the land, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧; and work, for I am with you, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts,

[5] the word that I cut with you when you came out of ⟦egypt|Mitzrayim|Egypt⟧; and my spirit is standing in your midst. Do not fear.

[6] For thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Yet once, it is a little while, and I am shaking the heavens and the earth and the sea and the dry ground;

[7] and I will shake all the nations, and the desirable things of all the nations shall come, and I will fill this house with glory, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[8] Mine is the silver, and mine is the gold, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[9] Great shall be the glory of this house, the latter, more than the former, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts; and in this place I will give ⟦shalom|shalom|peace⟧, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

[10] On the twenty-fourth of the ninth, in the second year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came to ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet, saying:

[11] Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Ask, please, the priests for ⟦torah|torah|instruction⟧, saying:

[12] Behold, a man carries holy flesh in the corner of his garment, and with his corner touches the bread, or the stew, or the wine, or oil, or any food: will it become holy? And the priests answered and said, No.

[13] And ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ said, If one unclean by a soul touches any of these, will it become unclean? And the priests answered and said, It will become unclean.

[14] And ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ answered and said, So is this people, and so is this nation before me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and so is all the work of their hands; and what they bring near there is unclean.

[15] And now, set, please, your heart from this day and upward: before stone was set on stone in the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧,

[16] since they were, one came to a heap of twenty, and there were ten; one came to the winepress to draw off fifty from the trough, and there were twenty.

[17] I struck you with blight and with mildew and with hail, all the work of your hands; and you were not toward me, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[18] Set, please, your heart from this day and upward, from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth, from the day that the temple of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ was founded: set your heart.

[19] Is the seed yet in the granary? And even the vine and the fig tree and the pomegranate and the olive tree has not borne. From this day I will bless.

[20] And the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came a second time to ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ on the twenty-fourth of the month, saying:

[21] Say to ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧, governor of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, saying: I am shaking the heavens and the earth;

[22] and I will overthrow the throne of kingdoms, and I will destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the nations; and I will overthrow chariot and its riders; and horses and their riders shall go down, each by the sword of his brother.

[23] In that day, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, I will take you, ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, my servant, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, and I will set you as the signet ring; for you I have chosen, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton preserved.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת.
- **Layout** (throughout) — prose, as the Hebrew; the quotation marks are dropped (the Hebrew has none).
- **"In the seventh month, in the one and twentieth day of the month, came the word of the LORD by the prophet Haggai, saying" → "In the seventh, on the twenty-first of the month, the word of Yahweh came by the hand of Haggai the prophet, saying"** (v1) — Hebrew בַּשְּׁבִיעִי "in the seventh", without "month" (dates as printed, also vv10, 18, 20); בְּיַד "by the hand of"; לֵאמֹר "saying".
- **"Speak now to … the high priest, and to the residue of the people" → "Say, please, to … the great priest, and to the remnant of the people"** (v2) — Hebrew אֱמָר־נָא "say, please"; הַכֹּהֵן הַגָּדוֹל "the great priest" (as Num 35:25; "high priest", also v4); שְׁאֵרִית "remnant".
- **"Who is left among you that saw this house in her first glory? … is it not in your eyes in comparison of it as nothing?" → "Who is left among you who saw this house in its first glory? … Is not the like of it as nothing in your eyes?"** (v3) — Hebrew הַנִּשְׁאָר "who is left"; בִּכְבוֹדוֹ הָרִאשׁוֹן "in its first glory"; כָמֹהוּ כְּאַיִן "the like of it as nothing".
- **"Yet now be strong, O Zerubbabel … and be strong, O Joshua … all ye people of the land" → "And now be strong, Zerubbabel … and be strong, Joshua … all the people of the land"** (v4) — Hebrew חֲזַק "be strong" (three times); the vocatives without "O"; וַעֲשׂוּ "and work"; נְאֻם "declares".
- **"According to the word that I covenanted with you when ye came out of Egypt, so my spirit remaineth among you: fear ye not" → "the word that I cut with you when you came out of Egypt; and my spirit is standing in your midst. Do not fear"** (v5) — Hebrew אֶת־הַדָּבָר "the word", with the object marker and no verb ("according to" is not in the Hebrew; the phrase hangs on v4); כָּרַתִּי "I cut" (the verb of cutting a covenant, here with "word"); עֹמֶדֶת "is standing"; בְּתוֹכְכֶם "in your midst". The Septuagint lacks the first half (from memory).
- **"Yet once, it is a little while, and I will shake the heavens, and the earth, and the sea, and the dry land" → "Yet once, it is a little while, and I am shaking the heavens and the earth and the sea and the dry ground"** (v6) — Hebrew עוֹד אַחַת מְעַט הִיא; מַרְעִישׁ "am shaking" (participle; also v21); חָרָבָה "dry ground". Heb 12:26 quotes the Greek ἔτι ἅπαξ "yet once more" (from memory).
- **"And I will shake all nations, and the desire of all nations shall come" → "and I will shake all the nations, and the desirable things of all the nations shall come"** (v7) — Hebrew חֶמְדַּת כָּל־הַגּוֹיִם: a singular noun "desirable thing" (as 1 Sam 9:20; 2 Chr 32:27; 36:10) with a plural verb וּבָאוּ "shall come", so the treasures of the nations (with v8); no capital, no title. KJV "the desire of all nations" reads one desired person.
- **"The silver is mine, and the gold is mine" → "Mine is the silver, and mine is the gold"** (v8) — Hebrew order לִי הַכֶּסֶף וְלִי הַזָּהָב.
- **"The glory of this latter house shall be greater than of the former … and in this place will I give peace" → "Great shall be the glory of this house, the latter, more than the former … and in this place I will give shalom"** (v9) — Hebrew כְּבוֹד הַבַּיִת הַזֶּה הָאַחֲרוֹן: "the latter" may belong to "the glory" ("the latter glory of this house") or to "this house"; the word order is kept; שָׁלוֹם token.
- **"In the four and twentieth day of the ninth month, in the second year of Darius, came the word of the LORD by Haggai the prophet" → "On the twenty-fourth of the ninth, in the second year of Darius, the word of Yahweh came to Haggai the prophet"** (v10) — Hebrew לַתְּשִׁיעִי "of the ninth" (no "month"); אֶל־חַגַּי "to Haggai" (not "by", here).
- **"Ask now the priests concerning the law, saying" → "Ask, please, the priests for instruction, saying"** (v11) — Hebrew שְׁאַל־נָא "ask, please"; תּוֹרָה, a priest's ruling (as Mal 2:7).
- **"If one bear holy flesh in the skirt of his garment, and with his skirt do touch bread, or pottage, or wine, or oil, or any meat, shall it be holy?" → "Behold, a man carries holy flesh in the corner of his garment, and with his corner touches the bread, or the stew, or the wine, or oil, or any food: will it become holy?"** (v12) — Hebrew הֵן "behold"; בְּשַׂר־קֹדֶשׁ "holy flesh"; כָּנָף "corner" (literally "wing"); נָזִיד "stew"; מַאֲכָל "food"; יִקְדָּשׁ "will it become holy" (qal, as Deut 22:9).
- **"If one that is unclean by a dead body touch any of these" → "If one unclean by a soul touches any of these"** (v13) — Hebrew טְמֵא־נֶפֶשׁ "unclean by a soul" (a dead person, as Num 5:2; 9:6; "dead body" is not in the Hebrew); יִטְמָא "become unclean".
- **"and that which they offer there is unclean" → "and what they bring near there is unclean"** (v14) — Hebrew יַקְרִיבוּ "bring near" (of an offering).
- **"consider from this day and upward, from before a stone was laid upon a stone in the temple of the LORD" → "set, please, your heart from this day and upward: before stone was set on stone in the temple of Yahweh"** (v15) — Hebrew שִׂימוּ־נָא לְבַבְכֶם (also v18); וָמָעְלָה "and upward" (onward or backward in time: unclear); מִטֶּרֶם שׂוּם "before setting"; הֵיכַל "temple".
- **"Since those days were, when one came to an heap of twenty measures, there were but ten: when one came to the pressfat for to draw out fifty vessels out of the press, there were but twenty" → "since they were, one came to a heap of twenty, and there were ten; one came to the winepress to draw off fifty from the trough, and there were twenty"** (v16) — Hebrew מִהְיוֹתָם "since they were" (sense unclear; the Septuagint reads otherwise, from memory); עֲרֵמָה "heap"; "measures" and "vessels" are not in the Hebrew; יֶקֶב "winepress"; לַחְשֹׂף "to draw off" (literally "to strip"); פּוּרָה "trough" (as Isa 63:3).
- **"I smote you with blasting and with mildew and with hail in all the labours of your hands; yet ye turned not to me" → "I struck you with blight and with mildew and with hail, all the work of your hands; and you were not toward me"** (v17) — Hebrew שִׁדָּפוֹן "blight"; יֵרָקוֹן "mildew"; אֵת כָּל־מַעֲשֵׂה יְדֵיכֶם "all the work of your hands" (object, no "in"); וְאֵין־אֶתְכֶם אֵלַי, literally "and there was not you toward me" (the form is strange); the line follows Amos 4:9 וְלֹא־שַׁבְתֶּם עָדַי "yet you did not return to me", which the Septuagint reads here (from memory): the MT stands.
- **"even from the day that the foundation of the LORD'S temple was laid, consider it" → "from the day that the temple of Yahweh was founded: set your heart"** (v18) — Hebrew יֻסַּד "was founded"; שִׂימוּ לְבַבְכֶם "set your heart" (no "it").
- **"Is the seed yet in the barn? yea, as yet the vine … hath not brought forth: from this day will I bless you" → "Is the seed yet in the granary? And even the vine … has not borne. From this day I will bless"** (v19) — Hebrew מְגוּרָה "granary" (a homonym of the Ps 34:4 word "terror"; as מַמְּגֻרוֹת Joel 1:17); וְעַד "and even"; לֹא נָשָׂא "has not borne" (literally "lifted"); אֲבָרֵךְ "I will bless" (no "you").
- **"And again the word of the LORD came" → "And the word of Yahweh came a second time"** (v20) — Hebrew שֵׁנִית "a second time".
- **"I will shake the heavens and the earth" → "I am shaking the heavens and the earth"** (v21) — Hebrew מַרְעִישׁ (participle, as v6).
- **"and I will destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the heathen; and I will overthrow the chariots, and those that ride in them; and the horses and their riders shall come down" → "and I will destroy the strength of the kingdoms of the nations; and I will overthrow chariot and its riders; and horses and their riders shall go down"** (v22) — Hebrew הָפַךְ "overthrow" (twice); הִשְׁמִיד "destroy"; הַגּוֹיִם "the nations"; מֶרְכָּבָה "chariot" (singular); יָרְדוּ "go down".
- **"will I take thee, O Zerubbabel, my servant, the son of Shealtiel … and will make thee as a signet: for I have chosen thee" → "I will take you, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, my servant … and I will set you as the signet ring; for you I have chosen"** (v23) — Hebrew order "Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, my servant"; no "O"; שַׂמְתִּיךָ "I will set you"; כַּחוֹתָם "as the signet ring" (with the article); בְךָ בָחַרְתִּי "you I have chosen" (the object first).

### Notes
- **Dates** — the second year of Darius I, 520 BC: the seventh month, day twenty-one (v1); the ninth month, day twenty-four (vv10, 18, 20).
- **Signet ring** (v23) — Jer 22:24 tore Coniah off as a signet ring; here the word is turned to his grandson.
- **v6–7** — "Yet once" is quoted in Heb 12:26–27.
