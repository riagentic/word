# Haggai 1

[1] In the second year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king, in the sixth month, on the first day of the month, the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came by the hand of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet to ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, governor of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and to ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the high priest:

[2] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: These people say the time has not yet come to rebuild the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[3] Then the word of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ came by the hand of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet,

[4] "Is it a time for you yourselves to dwell in your paneled houses, while this house lies in ruins?

[5] Now, therefore, thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Consider your ways.

[6] You have sown much, and harvested little;
you eat, but you never have enough;
you drink, but you never have your fill;
you clothe yourselves, but no one is warm;
and he who earns wages
does so to put them into a bag with holes.

[7] "Thus says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts: Consider your ways.

[8] Go up to the hills and bring wood and build the house, that I may take pleasure in it and that I may be glorified, says ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[9] You looked for much, and behold, it came to little. And when you brought it home, I blew it away. Why? declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts. Because of my house that lies in ruins, while each of you busies himself with his own house.

[10] Therefore the heavens above you have withheld the dew, and the earth has withheld its produce.

[11] And I have called for a drought on the land and the hills, on the grain, the new wine, the oil, on what the ground brings forth, on man and beast, and on all their labors."

[12] Then ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, and ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the high priest, with all the remnant of the people, obeyed the voice of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, and the words of ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧ the prophet, as ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ had sent him. And the people feared ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧.

[13] Then ⟦haggai|Chaggai|Haggai⟧, the messenger of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧, spoke to the people with ⟦yhwh|Yahweh's|the LORD's⟧ message: "**I am with you**, declares ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧."

[14] And ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ stirred up the spirit of ⟦zerubbabel|Zerubbavel|Zerubbabel⟧ son of Shealtiel, governor of ⟦judah|Yehudah|Judah⟧, and the spirit of ⟦joshua-priest|Yehoshua|Joshua⟧ son of ⟦jehozadak|Yehotzadaq|Jehozadak⟧, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people. And they came and worked on the house of ⟦yhwh|Yahweh|the LORD⟧ of Hosts, their ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧,

[15] on the twenty-fourth day of the month, in the sixth month, in the second year of ⟦darius|Daryavesh|Darius⟧ the king.

## Changes

- **"LORD" → "Yahweh"** (throughout) — Tetragrammaton יְהוָה rendered directly.
- **"LORD of hosts" → "Yahweh of Hosts"** (throughout) — Hebrew יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת *Yahweh Tzeva'ot*; the military-covenantal divine title preserved.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv12, 14) — Hebrew אֱלֹהִים *Elohim*; "Yahweh their *Elohim*."

### Notes
- **520 BC** — rebuilding resumed under Haggai's prodding, 18 years after return.
