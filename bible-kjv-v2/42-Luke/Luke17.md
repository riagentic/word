# Luke 17

[1] And he said to his disciples, "It is impossible that the stumbling blocks should not come, yet woe to the one through whom they come!

[2] It is to his gain if a millstone lies around his neck and he has been thrown into the sea, rather than that he should make one of these little ones stumble.

[3] Give heed to yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him, and if he repents, forgive him;

[4] and if seven times in the day he sins against you and seven times turns back to you, saying, 'I repent,' you shall forgive him."

[5] And the apostles said to the Lord, "Add faith to us."

[6] But the Lord said, "If you have faith as a grain of mustard, you would say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted and be planted in the sea,' and it would have obeyed you.

[7] "But who of you, having a slave plowing or shepherding, will say to him when he has come in out of the field, 'Immediately come forward and recline,'

[8] but will not say to him, 'Make ready what I may have for supper, and having girded yourself serve me until I have eaten and drunk, and after these things you yourself shall eat and drink'?

[9] Does he have thanks for the slave because he did the things ordered?

[10] So also you, when you have done all the things ordered you, say, 'We are useless slaves; what we owed to do we have done.'"

[11] And it came to pass, in the going to ⟦jerusalem|Yerushalayim|Jerusalem⟧, that he was passing through the midst of ⟦samaria-nt|Shomron|Samaria⟧ and ⟦galilee|Galil|Galilee⟧.

[12] And as he was entering into a certain village, ten leprous men met him, who stood far off,

[13] and they lifted a voice, saying, "⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, Chief, have mercy on us."

[14] And seeing them, he said to them, "Go and show yourselves to the priests." And it came to pass, as they were going, that they were cleansed.

[15] But one of them, seeing that he was healed, returned, glorifying ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ with a loud voice,

[16] and he fell upon his face at his feet, giving thanks to him; and he was a ⟦samaritans|Shomroni|Samaritan⟧.

[17] And ⟦yeshua|Yeshua|Jesus⟧, answering, said, "Were not the ten cleansed? But the nine — where are they?

[18] Were they not found returning to give glory to ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧, except this foreigner?"

[19] And he said to him, "Rise and go; your faith has saved you."

[20] And having been asked by the ⟦pharisees|Perushim|Pharisees⟧ when the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ comes, he answered them and said, "The kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ does not come with close watching,

[21] nor will they say, 'Behold, here!' or, 'There!' For behold, the kingdom of ⟦elohim|Elohim|God⟧ is within you."

[22] And he said to the disciples, "Days will come when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it.

[23] And they will say to you, 'Behold, there!' or, 'Behold, here!' Do not go away, nor pursue.

[24] For just as the lightning, flashing out of the one part under heaven, shines unto the other part under heaven, so will the Son of Man be in his day.

[25] But first he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.

[26] And even as it came to pass in the days of ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧, so will it be also in the days of the Son of Man:

[27] they were eating, they were drinking, they were marrying, they were being given in marriage, until the day on which ⟦noah|Noach|Noah⟧ entered into the ark, and the flood came and destroyed all.

[28] Likewise, even as it came to pass in the days of Lot: they were eating, they were drinking, they were buying, they were selling, they were planting, they were building;

[29] but on the day on which Lot went out from ⟦sodom|Sedom|Sodom⟧, it rained fire and sulfur from heaven and destroyed all.

[30] According to the same things will it be on the day on which the Son of Man is revealed.

[31] In that day, he who will be on the housetop, and his goods in the house, let him not go down to take them; and the one in the field likewise, let him not turn back.

[32] **Remember Lot's wife.**

[33] Whoever seeks to obtain his soul will lose it, but whoever loses it will keep it alive.

[34] I say to you, in this night there will be two on one bed: the one will be taken along and the other will be left;

[35] there will be two women grinding together: the one will be taken along, but the other will be left."

[37] And answering, they say to him, "Where, Lord?" And he said to them, "**Where the body is, there also the eagles will be gathered together.**"

## Changes

- **"Jesus" → "*Yeshua*"** (v13, 17) — Hebrew name preserved. In v16 the Greek has only αὐτοῦ, "his feet": the name is not repeated.
- **"God" → "*Elohim*"** (vv15, 18, 20, 21) — divine name retained.
- **"v36 absent"** — Luke 17:36 ("Two men shall be in the field; the one shall be taken, and the other left") is absent from earliest MSS; noted in ### Notes.
- **"It is impossible but that offences will come: but woe unto him" → "It is impossible that the stumbling blocks should not come, yet woe to the one"** (v1) — Greek ἀνένδεκτόν ἐστιν τοῦ τὰ σκάνδαλα μὴ ἐλθεῖν, "it is not admissible that the stumbling blocks should not come" (Matt 18:7 has ἀνάγκη, "a necessity"). σκάνδαλον is "stumbling block", the noun of σκανδαλίζω "make stumble" (v2): a trap, a cause of falling; "sin" is not in the word. πλὴν οὐαὶ δι' οὗ ἔρχεται has no "to him": "the one" is supplied.
- **"It were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he cast into the sea, than that he should offend" → "It is to his gain if a millstone lies around his neck and he has been thrown into the sea, rather than that he should make … stumble"** (v2) — Greek λυσιτελεῖ αὐτῷ, "it pays him, it is gain to him", a present (Matt 18:6 συμφέρει, Mark 9:42 καλόν ἐστιν … μᾶλλον); εἰ … περίκειται … καὶ ἔρριπται, "if … lies around … and has been thrown" (ῥίπτω; Mark has βέβληται, "has been cast"); λίθος μυλικός, "a stone of a mill" (Mark and Matthew: μύλος ὀνικός, "a donkey's millstone"); σκανδαλίσῃ, "make stumble".
- **"Take heed to yourselves: If thy brother trespass against thee" → "Give heed to yourselves. If your brother sins"** (v3) — Greek προσέχετε ἑαυτοῖς, as Acts 5:35; 20:28. ἁμάρτῃ, "sins"; "against thee" (RP εἰς σέ) is not in the critical text.
- **"seven times in a day turn again to thee … thou shalt forgive him" → "seven times turns back to you … you shall forgive him"** (v4) — the second "in a day" (RP) is not in the critical text. ἐπιστρέψῃ, "turns, turns back"; ἀφήσεις, a future.
- **"Increase our faith" → "Add faith to us"** (v5) — Greek πρόσθες ἡμῖν πίστιν, "add to us faith" (προστίθημι as Acts 2:41, 47): "add faith to what we have" or "give us faith besides".
- **"If ye had faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye might say … and it should obey you" → "If you have faith as a grain of mustard, you would say … and it would have obeyed you"** (v6) — Greek εἰ ἔχετε, a present, "if you have"; then ἐλέγετε ἄν, "you would be saying", and ὑπήκουσεν ἄν, "it would have obeyed" (a past form). συκάμινος, "sycamine", is the mulberry tree (a different word from συκομορέα, the sycamore of 19:4).
- **"having a servant plowing or feeding cattle, will say unto him by and by, when he is come from the field, Go and sit down to meat" → "having a slave plowing or shepherding, will say to him when he has come in out of the field, 'Immediately come forward and recline'"** (v7) — Greek δοῦλος, "slave"; ποιμαίνοντα, "shepherding"; εὐθέως, "immediately", which may also be read with "will say" (so the KJV: "by and by"); παρελθὼν ἀνάπεσε, "having come forward, recline" (lit. "fall back", the posture at a meal). Verses 7–8 are one question in the printed text.
- **"And will not rather say unto him, Make ready wherewith I may sup, and gird thyself … and afterward thou shalt eat and drink" → "but will not say to him, 'Make ready what I may have for supper, and having girded yourself … and after these things you yourself shall eat and drink'"** (v8) — Greek ἀλλ' οὐχὶ ἐρεῖ, without "rather"; ἑτοίμασον τί δειπνήσω (δειπνέω, from δεῖπνον "supper"); περιζωσάμενος; διακόνει, "serve" (the root of διάκονος); μετὰ ταῦτα; σύ, an emphatic "you".
- **"Doth he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded him? I trow not" → "Does he have thanks for the slave because he did the things ordered?"** (v9) — Greek μὴ ἔχει χάριν τῷ δούλῳ, "does he have thanks (χάρις, grace) for the slave?" — a question with μή, which expects the answer "no". "that", "him" and "I trow not" (RP) are not in the critical text. τὰ διαταχθέντα, "the things ordered" (v9, 10), from διατάσσω "give orders" (as 8:55), the word for a standing rule in Gal 3:19 ("ordain").
- **"We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do" → "We are useless slaves; what we owed to do we have done"** (v10) — Greek δοῦλοι ἀχρεῖοι, "useless slaves" (as Matt 25:30): of no gain to their lord beyond what is owed; ὃ ὠφείλομεν ποιῆσαι, "what we owed (were bound) to do". No "only" stands in the Greek.
- **"as he went to Jerusalem, that he passed through the midst of Samaria and Galilee" → "in the going to *Yerushalayim*, that he was passing through the midst of *Shomron* and *Galil*"** (v11) — Greek ἐν τῷ πορεύεσθαι, without a subject (WH NA28; Treg RP add αὐτόν, "as he was going"); καὶ αὐτὸς διήρχετο, an imperfect. διὰ μέσον Σαμαρείας καὶ Γαλιλαίας: "through the midst of" or "through between" Samaria and Galilee, that is, along the border; the Greek allows both.
- **"there met him ten men that were lepers, which stood afar off" → "ten leprous men met him, who stood far off"** (v12) — Greek ἀπήντησαν αὐτῷ δέκα λεπροὶ ἄνδρες. Editions: ἀπήντησαν WH (and the printed SBLGNT) ] + αὐτῷ Treg NA28 RP — "met him" (the majority); without it, "there met ten leprous men". πόρρωθεν, "from far off".
- **"they lifted up their voices, and said, Jesus, Master" → "they lifted a voice, saying, '*Yeshua*, Chief'"** (v13) — Greek ἦραν φωνήν, "a voice", singular (as Acts 4:24). ἐπιστάτα: ἐπιστάτης, "one set over" others, a word only Luke has (5:5; 8:24, 45; 9:33, 49); it is neither διδάσκαλος "Teacher" nor κύριος "Lord" nor δεσπότης "master".
- **"Go shew yourselves … as they went" → "Go and show yourselves … as they were going"** (v14) — Greek πορευθέντες ἐπιδείξατε, "having gone, show"; ἐν τῷ ὑπάγειν αὐτούς, a different verb of going (ὑπάγω, "go off"). ἰδών has no object: "them" is supplied.
- **"turned back, and with a loud voice glorified God" → "returned, glorifying *Elohim* with a loud voice"** (v15) — Greek ὑπέστρεψεν, "returned" (as v18); δοξάζων, "glorifying" (δόξα, v18). ἰάθη, "was healed" (ἰάομαι); in v14, 17 the word is ἐκαθαρίσθησαν, "were cleansed".
- **"Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine?" → "Were not the ten cleansed? But the nine — where are they?"** (v17) — Greek οἱ δέκα, "the ten", with the article; οἱ δὲ ἐννέα ποῦ; without a verb.
- **"There are not found that returned to give glory to God, save this stranger" → "Were they not found returning to give glory to *Elohim*, except this foreigner?"** (v18) — Greek οὐχ εὑρέθησαν ὑποστρέψαντες, printed as a question; ἀλλογενής, "of another race" (γένος), a foreigner: the word that stood on the temple barrier forbidding entry to those who were not Jews.
- **"Arise, go thy way: thy faith hath made thee whole" → "Rise and go; your faith has saved you"** (v19) — Greek ἀναστὰς πορεύου, "rising, go"; ἡ πίστις σου σέσωκέν σε: σῴζω is "save", here said to a man who has been healed of leprosy (as Mark 5:34; 10:52). All ten were cleansed; this is said to the one who returned. The Greek has one word for both "save" and "make well".
- **"when he was demanded of the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God should come … cometh not with observation" → "having been asked by the *Perushim* when the kingdom of *Elohim* comes … does not come with close watching"** (v20) — Greek πότε ἔρχεται, a present; μετὰ παρατηρήσεως, "with close watching": παρατήρησις, the noun of παρατηρέω, "watch closely" (6:7; 14:1; 20:20). It does not come in such a way that it can be watched for; "signs" is not in the Greek.
- **"Lo here! or, lo there! … the kingdom of God is within you" → "'Behold, here!' or, 'There!' … the kingdom of *Elohim* is within you"** (v21) — the second "lo" (Treg RP ἰδοὺ ἐκεῖ) is not in WH NA28. Greek ἐντὸς ὑμῶν ἐστιν: ἐντός is "inside, within" (Matt 23:26, "the inside of the cup"), so "within you"; with a plural "you" it can also mean "in your midst, among you" — and the words are spoken to the Pharisees. The Greek allows both. Luke's usual words for "in the midst of" are ἐν μέσῳ (2:46; 22:27; 24:36).
- **"The days will come … and ye shall not see it" → "Days will come … and you will not see it"** (v22) — Greek ἐλεύσονται ἡμέραι, without the article; οὐκ ὄψεσθε has no object: "it" is supplied.
- **"See here; or, see there: go not after them, nor follow them" → "'Behold, there!' or, 'Behold, here!' Do not go away, nor pursue"** (v23) — Greek ἰδοὺ ἐκεῖ, ἤ· ἰδοὺ ὧδε. Editions: ἐκεῖ· Ἰδοὺ ὧδε Treg (and the printed SBLGNT), without "or" ] ἐκεῖ, ἤ· Ἰδοὺ ὧδε WH NA28; ὧδε, ἤ· Ἰδοὺ ἐκεῖ RP — the verse follows WH NA28. μὴ ἀπέλθητε μηδὲ διώξητε, "do not go away nor pursue", without an object.
- **"as the lightning, that lighteneth out of the one part under heaven, shineth unto the other part under heaven" → "just as the lightning, flashing out of the one part under heaven, shines unto the other part under heaven"** (v24) — Greek ὥσπερ, "just as" (as Matt 24:27); ἀστράπτουσα ἐκ τῆς ὑπὸ τὸν οὐρανὸν εἰς τὴν ὑπ' οὐρανόν, literally "out of the (one) under heaven unto the (other) under heaven": the noun (region, part) is left unsaid and "one part … other part" is supplied. "in his day" (Treg NA28 RP) is not in WH. "also" (KJV) is not in the Greek.
- **"be rejected of this generation" → "be rejected by this generation"** (v25) — Greek ἀποδοκιμασθῆναι ἀπὸ τῆς γενεᾶς ταύτης, "rejected from (on the part of) this generation"; Mark 8:31 has ὑπό.
- **"as it was in the days of Noe" → "even as it came to pass in the days of *Noach*"** (v26, 28) — Greek καθὼς ἐγένετο.
- **"They did eat, they drank, they married wives, they were given in marriage … destroyed them all" → "they were eating, they were drinking, they were marrying, they were being given in marriage … destroyed all"** (v27) — four Greek imperfects without "and" (so also the six of v28); ἐγαμίζοντο, a passive. ἀπώλεσεν πάντας, "destroyed all" (v27, 29).
- **"it rained fire and brimstone from heaven" → "it rained fire and sulfur from heaven"** (v29) — Greek ἔβρεξεν πῦρ καὶ θεῖον: the verb has no expressed subject, "it rained" or "he rained" (Gen 19:24: Yahweh rained).
- **"Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed" → "According to the same things will it be on the day on which the Son of Man is revealed"** (v30) — Greek κατὰ τὰ αὐτά (WH Treg NA28; RP ταῦτα, "according to these things"); ἀποκαλύπτεται, a present passive.
- **"his stuff in the house, let him not come down to take it away … let him likewise not return back" → "his goods in the house, let him not go down to take them … likewise, let him not turn back"** (v31) — Greek τὰ σκεύη αὐτοῦ, "his goods, vessels" (as Mark 3:27); μὴ ἐπιστρεψάτω εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω, "let him not turn to the things behind" (as Mark 13:16).
- **"Whosoever shall seek to save his life shall lose it; and whosoever shall lose his life shall preserve it" → "Whoever seeks to obtain his soul will lose it, but whoever loses it will keep it alive"** (v33) — Greek ψυχή, "soul" (also "life": the one word). περιποιήσασθαι (WH Treg NA28), "obtain, gain for oneself" (as Acts 20:28); RP has σῶσαι, "save". ὃς δ' ἂν ἀπολέσῃ has no object (RP adds αὐτήν): "it" is supplied; "his life" (KJV) is not repeated in the Greek. ζῳογονήσει, "will keep alive, bring forth alive" (as Acts 7:19).
- **"I tell you, in that night there shall be two men in one bed; the one shall be taken" → "I say to you, in this night there will be two on one bed: the one will be taken along"** (v34) — Greek ταύτῃ τῇ νυκτί, "this night"; δύο … ὁ εἷς … ὁ ἕτερος are masculine forms, the form Greek also uses for two persons in general: "men" is not in the Greek. παραλημφθήσεται, "will be taken along" (παραλαμβάνω, as Matt 24:40); ἀφεθήσεται, "will be left".
- **"Two women shall be grinding together" → "there will be two women grinding together"** (v35) — Greek δύο ἀλήθουσαι, a feminine participle: "women" is in the form of the word. ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό, "at the same place, together".
- **"Wheresoever the body is, thither will the eagles be gathered together" → "Where the body is, there also the eagles will be gathered together"** (v37) — Greek σῶμα, "body" (Matt 24:28 has πτῶμα, "corpse"); καί, "also"; ἀετοί, "eagles": the word also covers vultures, the birds that gather at a dead body. λέγουσιν, "they say", a present.

### Notes
- **v36** absent from earliest MSS.
- **v26–29** refer to Gen 6–7 and Gen 19.
